## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (J Dent Res 2014, University of Iowa): meta-analysis of RCTs comparing alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) vs. extraction alone in non-molar teeth to quantify dimensional outcomes.

ARP significantly preserves buccolingual width (+1.89 mm, p<.001) and midbuccal height (+2.07 mm, p<.001); only distal height failed to reach significance; optimal protocol is flap elevation + barrier membrane + xenograft/allograft.

This study is partially superseded by Avila-Ortiz 2019, which expanded to 22 RCTs and a 9-modality classification; the 2014 first-quantification anchor values for ARP-vs-natural-healing retain standalone reference value.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis, SR+MA, J Dent Res 2014, Iowa 대학교): 비대구치 발치 후 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) vs 자연치유 무작위 대조 시험 (RCT) 메타분석.

ARP가 협설폭 +1.89 mm (p<.001)·협측높이 +2.07 mm (p<.001) 유의 보존; 원심 높이만 NS; 최우수 프로토콜: 판막 거상 + 차폐막 + 이종골/동종골.

Avila-Ortiz 2019 (22 RCT, 9가지 술식 분류)에 의해 부분 대체 — "ARP vs 자연치유" 1차 정량 기준값 (협설폭 +1.89 mm·협측높이 +2.07 mm)은 독립 참고가치 유지.

## Summary
Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs comparing alveolar ridge preservation (socket grafting) to extraction alone in non-molar teeth (University of Iowa, J Dent Res 2014). ARP is effective across all dimensional outcomes except distal height. Optimal protocol: flap elevation + barrier membrane + xenograft or allograft.

## Key Contributions
- ARP significantly preserves ridge dimensions vs extraction alone
- Buccolingual width: +1.89mm (p<.001) — most clinically relevant gain
- Midbuccal height: +2.07mm (p<.001) — largest absolute benefit
- Midlingual height: +1.18mm (p=.022)
- Mesial height: +0.48mm (p=.002)
- Distal height: +0.24mm (NS, p=.102) — only non-significant dimension
- Subgroup superiority: flap elevation + membrane + xenograft/allograft for midbuccal/lingual height

## Results Table
| Dimension | ARP benefit (mm) | 95% CI | p-value |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|---------|
| Buccolingual width | +1.89 | 1.41–2.36 | <.001 |
| Midbuccal height | +2.07 | 1.03–3.12 | <.001 |
| Midlingual height | +1.18 | 0.17–2.19 | .022 |
| Mesial height | +0.48 | 0.18–0.79 | .002 |
| Distal height | +0.24 | −0.05–0.53 | .102 (NS) |

## Optimal Protocol (from subgroup analysis)
- **Flap elevation**: allows better access and graft adaptation
- **Barrier membrane**: superior height outcomes
- **Xenograft or allograft**: outperforms other materials for midbuccal/midlingual height
