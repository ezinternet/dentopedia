## Three-line Summary

Narrative review with an embedded overview-of-systematic-reviews (Journal of Dentistry 2024) on post-extraction alveolar socket healing — screened 459 records, included 11 systematic reviews (3 with meta-analysis, 1 network meta-analysis) on unassisted-healing dimensional change, all AMSTAR-2 "critically low" quality, plus narrative synthesis of histology, sinus pneumatisation, clinical/procedural/site factors, and alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) efficacy/technique/cost-effectiveness.

Horizontal ridge width reduction (29–63%) exceeds vertical reduction (11–22%) over 3–7 months with most modelling in the first 3 months; Couso-Queiruga (2021, 28 studies) pooled non-molar horizontal reduction 2.54 mm (95% CI 1.97–3.11) vs molar 3.61 mm (95% CI 3.24–3.98); ARP with xenograft/allograft attenuates but does not eliminate dimensional change (Cochrane: width MD 1.18 mm, height MD 1.35 mm vs extraction alone), and flapless technique is not inferior to flap-and-primary-closure for hard-tissue outcomes.

Clinical implication: ARP decisions should be individualised and prosthetically driven rather than routine, since evidence quality for ARP-efficacy reviews is uniformly critically low (AMSTAR-2) and dimensional change is only a surrogate outcome — the authors call for standardised core outcome sets and flag AI as an unexplored future aid to healing prediction.

## 세줄요약

발치 후 치조와 치유(post-extraction alveolar socket healing)에 관한 서술적 리뷰(narrative review) + 삽입된 체계적 문헌고찰들의 개관(overview of SRs) (Journal of Dentistry 2024) — 459편 중 11편의 SR(3편은 메타분석 포함, 1편은 네트워크 메타분석)을 선정했고, 비보조 치유(unassisted healing)의 치수변화 근거는 AMSTAR-2 기준 전부 "critically low" 품질이었으며, 조직학·상악동 함기화(sinus pneumatisation) 논쟁·임상/시술/부위 요인·치조제보존술(Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 효능·기술변수·비용효과까지 종합했다.

수평 치조제 폭 감소(29–63%)가 수직 감소(11–22%)보다 3–7개월 동안 더 크며 대부분의 개조는 첫 3개월 내 발생; Couso-Queiruga(2021, 28편)는 비구치부(non-molar) 수평 감소 2.54mm(95% CI 1.97–3.11) vs 구치부(molar) 3.61mm(95% CI 3.24–3.98)로 보고; ARP(이종골/동종골 이식재 사용)는 치수변화를 완화하지만 완전히 막지는 못함(Cochrane: 폭 MD 1.18mm, 높이 MD 1.35mm, 발치단독 대비), 무판막(flapless) 술식이 판막거상+1차봉합 술식에 비해 경조직 결과에서 열등하지 않음.

임상적 의미: ARP 효능 근거의 AMSTAR-2 품질이 전부 critically low이고 치수변화는 대리결과(surrogate outcome)에 불과하므로, ARP는 일률적 시행이 아닌 개별화·보철 주도(prosthetically-driven) 결정이어야 하며, 저자들은 표준화된 core outcome set 정립과 향후 인공지능(AI)의 치유예측 활용 가능성을 제언한다.

## Summary

This 2024 Journal of Dentistry review by Fok and Jin (University of Hong Kong) synthesizes the full arc of post-extraction alveolar socket healing knowledge: histological/molecular phases, dimensional ridge change, sinus pneumatisation, patient/procedural/site factors, and alveolar ridge preservation (ARP). For the dimensional-change question specifically, the authors conducted a formal overview of systematic reviews (Feb 2024, 4 databases, 459 records screened, 11 SRs included, all AMSTAR-2 "critically low"), confirming horizontal reduction (29–63%) exceeds vertical (11–22%) over 3–7 months and that molar sites lose more width than non-molar sites (3.61 vs 2.54 mm). ARP with xenograft/allograft attenuates but does not eliminate this loss, and flapless technique is non-inferior to flap-with-primary-closure for hard-tissue outcomes while favoring soft-tissue/patient-reported outcomes. The paper's clinical takeaway is that ARP should be individualised and prosthetically driven rather than applied routinely, given uniformly low-certainty evidence and the surrogate nature of the dimensional-change outcome.

## Key Contributions

- Formal overview-of-SRs (with AMSTAR-2 appraisal) specifically for the unassisted post-extraction dimensional-change question — cross-validates the quality ceiling ("critically low") of the individual SR/MA pages already in this wiki category.
- Consolidated four-phase histological/molecular model of socket healing (haemostasis/coagulation → inflammation → proliferation → bone modelling & remodelling).
- Synthesis of patient-, procedure-, and site-related factors (facial bone plate thickness, flap vs flapless, socket integrity) linked directly to ARP decision-making guidance.
- Rare inclusion of a cost-effectiveness angle (Barootchi et al. analysis) and an explicit call for a standardised core outcome set for ARP research.
- Flags AI-assisted healing prediction as a future direction (aspirational, not evaluated within this paper).

## Methodology

Narrative literature review with an embedded overview-of-systematic-reviews component limited to the dimensional-change objective. Databases: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Library (2004–2024, searched February 2024) plus manual search; 459 records screened → 11 SRs included (7 SR-only, 3 SR+MA, 1 network meta-analysis); Cohen's kappa 0.93 for reviewer agreement. AMSTAR-2 applied to all 11 included SRs. Other objectives (histology, sinus pneumatisation, clinical factors, ARP efficacy/technique/cost-effectiveness) were synthesized narratively, not via a second systematic search.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Unassisted healing, horizontal vs vertical reduction (3–7 mo) | 29–63% vs 11–22%; most change in first 3 months |
| Non-molar horizontal reduction (Couso-Queiruga 2021, 28 studies) | 2.54 mm (95% CI 1.97–3.11) |
| Molar horizontal reduction | 3.61 mm (95% CI 3.24–3.98) |
| ARP (xenograft) vs extraction alone, width/height loss reduction (Cochrane) | MD 1.18 mm / MD 1.35 mm |
| ARP (allograft), height loss reduction | MD 3.73 mm (1 study) |
| Flapless vs flap ARP, hard-tissue change (Atieh, 149 sockets/5 RCTs) | No significant difference; flapless favors PROMs, keratinized tissue |
| AMSTAR-2 quality of all 11 included SRs | Critically low (uniform) |
