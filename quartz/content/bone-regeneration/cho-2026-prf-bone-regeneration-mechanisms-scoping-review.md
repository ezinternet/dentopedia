## Three-line Summary

Narrative-structured scoping review (Seoul National University, JKAOMS 2026) synthesizing platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) biological mechanisms — fibrin matrix architecture, growth-factor release kinetics, and osteoimmunological/cytokine crosstalk — and their clinical applications in oral/maxillofacial bone regeneration (GBR, ARP, sinus lift, MRONJ/ORN, TMD).

PRF's 3D fibrin scaffold entraps ~97% of platelets and >50% of leukocytes, releasing >9,000 ng/mL of growth factors (PDGF-AA dominant) over 10 days with peak activity in the first postoperative week; it mechanistically couples angiogenesis-osteogenesis (VEGF/ERK/Notch), drives MSC osteogenic differentiation (BMP/SMAD, ERK/MAPK), inhibits osteoclastogenesis (↓NFATc1/TRAP/cathepsin K), and shifts macrophage polarization M1→M2 (↓IL-1β/NLRP3, ↑IL-4/IL-10).

Frames PRF as a biologically active adjunct (not a stand-alone structural graft) across GBR/ARP/sinus-lift/MRONJ-ORN/TMD; the review itself lacks a formal systematic search protocol (no PRISMA-ScR/databases/dates described despite the "scoping review" title), and flags unstandardized centrifugation reporting as the field's main reproducibility barrier.

## 세줄요약

서술형으로 구성된 스코핑 리뷰(서울대학교, 대한구강악안면외과학회지 2026) — 혈소판풍부피브린(Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)의 생물학적 기전(섬유소 매트릭스 구조·성장인자 방출동역학·골면역학/사이토카인 크로스토크)과 구강악안면 골재생 임상 적용(골유도재생술, 발치와보존, 상악동거상술, 약물관련턱뼈괴사/방사선골괴사, 턱관절장애)을 종합.

PRF의 3차원 섬유소 지지체는 혈소판 ~97%·백혈구 >50%를 포집해 10일간 9,000ng/mL 이상의 성장인자(PDGF-AA 우세)를 방출하며 술후 첫 주 최고 활성; 혈관신생-골형성 결합(VEGF/ERK/Notch), MSC 골형성분화 촉진(BMP/SMAD, ERK/MAPK), 파골세포형성 억제(NFATc1/TRAP/cathepsin K↓), 대식세포 M1→M2 극성전환(IL-1β/NLRP3↓, IL-4/IL-10↑)을 매개.

PRF를 독립 구조 이식재가 아닌 생물학적 보조제로 자리매김 — GBR/ARP/상악동거상술/MRONJ-ORN/TMD 전반 적용; 논문 자체는 정식 체계적 검색 프로토콜(PRISMA-ScR/데이터베이스/기간) 기술 없이 "스코핑 리뷰" 명칭만 사용했고, 원심분리 프로토콜 표준화 부재를 재현성의 핵심 걸림돌로 지적.

## Summary

This is a mechanism-focused, narrative-structured review synthesizing what is currently known about *why* platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) works as a regenerative biomaterial in oral and maxillofacial surgery, rather than reporting new clinical outcomes. It traces the evolution of platelet concentrates (PRP → PRF → variants H-/T-/A-/i-PRF/Ly-PRF → CGF), describes PRF's microstructure (a heterogeneous platelet-rich zone and fibrin-rich zone formed during single-spin, anticoagulant-free centrifugation), and quantifies its growth-factor release kinetics. The core of the paper organizes PRF's biological effects into six mechanistic pathways — angiogenesis-osteogenesis coupling, MSC osteogenic differentiation, osteoblast/ECM function, osteoclastogenesis inhibition, fibroblast/soft-tissue regulation, and immunomodulation (macrophage M1→M2 polarization, T-cell/Treg modulation) — then maps each pathway onto six clinical application domains (GBR, ARP, sinus lift, soft-tissue healing, MRONJ/ORN, TMD). The review closes by identifying centrifugation-protocol non-standardization (RPM vs. actual RCF) as the field's central reproducibility problem and proposes future directions (AR2T3 reporting framework, Alb-PRF/cross-linked membranes, biofunctionalization, exosome/3D-biofabrication integration).

Because the retrieved PMC full text contains no standalone Methods section (no explicit search databases, date ranges, or PRISMA-ScR inclusion/exclusion criteria), this page is labeled `confidence: narrative-review` rather than `sr`, despite "scoping review" appearing in the title — a deliberate deviation logged per the content actually observed (see `logs/ingest-deviations.md`).

## Key Contributions

- Consolidates the platelet-concentrate generational lineage (PRP → PRF → H-/T-/A-/i-PRF/Ly-PRF → CGF) and the biological rationale for each step (anticoagulant-free single-spin protocol, 3D fibrin entrapment, sustained vs. bimodal release).
- Quantifies PRF's growth-factor release kinetics: >9,000 ng/mL total protein over 10 days, PDGF-AA dominant, single TGF-β1 release peak ~day 7 (vs. PRP's bimodal pattern), growth factors/MMPs peaking within 6h and cytokines at 6–24h.
- Provides a structured six-pathway mechanistic map: (1) angiogenesis-osteogenesis coupling via VEGF/PDGF/EGF and ERK/EGFR/Notch signaling; (2) MSC osteogenic differentiation via BMP/SMAD and ERK/MAPK; (3) osteoblast function/ECM formation (HSP47, LOX upregulation, OPG/RANKL ratio increase); (4) osteoclastogenesis inhibition (↓NFATc1/TRAP/cathepsin K/DCSTAMP/OSCAR, apoptosis via intrinsic mitochondrial pathway); (5) fibroblast/soft-tissue regulation; (6) immunomodulation (macrophage M1→M2 shift, Treg induction via GARP mechanism, pyroptosis suppression via ↓NLRP3/caspase-11/IL-18).
- Surveys six clinical domains and grounds outcomes with mechanistic explanation rather than isolated effect sizes: GBR, ARP, sinus floor elevation (including Schneiderian membrane perforation repair), soft-tissue/periodontal healing, MRONJ/ORN, and TMD (intra-articular i-PRF + arthrocentesis).
- Identifies the field's core translational barrier: RPM-only protocol reporting is insufficient because actual relative centrifugal force (RCF) depends on rotor radius/design/tube angulation — proposes the AR2T3 acronym (Herrera-Vizcaino) as a standardized reporting fix.

## Results

Key mechanistic findings/synthesis (not a single-study clinical-outcomes trial):

- **Composition**: 10 mL whole blood → ~2–3 mL PRF, retaining ~97% of platelets and >50% of leukocytes; two-zone histological architecture (platelet-rich zone + fibrin-rich zone).
- **Release kinetics**: >9,000 ng/mL cumulative protein release over 10 days; PDGF-AA most abundant, followed by TGF-β1, PDGF-BB, VEGF, EGF, IGF-1; highest biological activity in postoperative week 1.
- **Angiogenesis-osteogenesis coupling**: VEGF/PDGF/EGF drive endothelial proliferation/tube formation via ERK/EGFR/Notch; medium-force centrifugation outperforms high-force protocols for vessel density/maturation.
- **Osteogenic signaling**: PRF activates BMP2/SMAD1/5/8 phosphorylation and ERK1/2/PI3K-Akt in MSCs and osteoblasts, upregulating RUNX2/ALP/osteocalcin; increases OPG/RANKL ratio, net favoring bone formation over resorption.
- **Immunomodulation**: PRF shifts macrophage polarization M1→M2 (↓TNF-α/IL-1β, ↑arginase-1/CD206), reduces LPS-induced pyroptosis (↓NLRP3/caspase-11/IL-18), and may induce regulatory CD4+ T cells via a GARP-related mechanism.
- **Clinical translation examples cited**: canine sinus lift DBBM+PRF osseointegration ≈43.5% vs. tissue-glue+DBBM ≈30–31% at 6 months; PRF-alone transcrestal sinus elevation 95.65% 1-year implant survival with ≈6.72 mm mean lift; MRONJ RCT showed no statistically significant added benefit of L-PRF over surgery alone despite numerically higher 6-month resolution (92% vs. 83%).
