## Three-line Summary

Case series (n=3) of a modified multistaged alveolar ridge-splitting (ARS) technique using piezoelectric osteotomies and titanium miniplate/miniscrew fixation for horizontal augmentation of narrow alveolar ridges with simultaneous implant placement.

All three cases achieved successful ridge expansion and osseointegration at up to 24-month follow-up; miniplate fixation provided controlled expansion and prevented buccal plate fracture; no implant failures or complications reported.

The technique extends conventional ARS by adding intermittent horizontal cuts and rigid fixation, offering predictable expansion but requiring hardware removal surgery and careful case selection.

## Summary

Oh & Oh (2026) report a modified alveolar ridge-splitting (ARS, 치조제 분할술) protocol that adds intermittent horizontal osteotomies and titanium miniplate/miniscrew fixation to standard sagittal–vertical ridge splitting. The modification is aimed at narrow ridges (<4 mm buccolingual width) that carry fracture risk with conventional single-plane expansion. Three cases — two maxillary posterior (one with bidirectional buccal + palatal expansion) and one mandibular posterior — were treated with simultaneous implant placement and autogenous GBR. All achieved osseointegration and no complications at up to 24 months.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces intermittent horizontal osteotomies (every 2–3 mm) as a modification to conventional ARS, allowing incremental controlled expansion without catastrophic buccal plate fracture.
- Adds titanium miniplate fixation (buccal surface) to hold the expanded segment during bone consolidation — converts a one-attempt expansion into a staged, hardware-stabilized process.
- Demonstrates bidirectional expansion (buccal + palatal) for maxillary narrow ridges, extending the technique's indication beyond unidirectional mandibular cases.
- Autogenous bone chips from osteotomy trimming are used for GBR defect fill, minimizing the need for a second donor site.

## Methodology

Piezoelectric surgery (Surgystar X) for all osteotomies. Osteotomy sequence: sagittal crestal cut → vertical releasing cuts → intermittent horizontal cuts. Titanium miniplate and miniscrew placed buccally after expansion; hardware removed after osseous consolidation. Simultaneous implant placement; bone defects filled with autogenous chips ± GBR membrane. CBCT evaluation at baseline, post-expansion, and follow-up.

## Results

| Case | Site | Pre-expansion width | Implant diameter | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mandibular posterior | 3.4–5.7 mm | 4.0–5.0 mm | Osseointegration at 24 mo, no complications |
| 2 | Maxillary posterior | Narrow (bidirectional) | Not specified | Osseointegration, successful expansion |
| 3 | Maxillary multiple | Narrow (staged) | Not specified | All implants osseointegrated |

No implant failures or complications across all three cases.
