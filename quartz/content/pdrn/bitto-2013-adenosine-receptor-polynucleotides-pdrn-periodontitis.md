## Three-line Summary

Animal study (rat experimental periodontitis induced by lower-left first-molar cervical ligation; groups: sham, vehicle, PDRN gel, PDRN gel + DMPX A2A antagonist) demonstrating that topically applied PDRN reduces periodontal inflammation and apoptosis via A2A adenosine receptor stimulation.

PDRN gel restored normal histological architecture of gingiva/PDL, blunted inflammatory proteins (p-JNK, p-ERK, TNF-α, IL-6, HMGB-1), reduced pro-apoptotic BAX, and preserved anti-apoptotic Bcl-2 vs vehicle; co-administration of the A2A antagonist DMPX abolished all PDRN effects, proving the mechanism is receptor-mediated rather than direct scavenging.

This is the foundational mechanistic anchor for PDRN's anti-inflammatory action in dental contexts — A2A agonism — that subsequent peri-implant, sinus, and socket-healing PDRN studies cite; limitation is that the rat ligature model generalizes to human peri-implant/sinus contexts only inferentially.

## 세줄요약

동물 연구 (하악 제1대구치 경부 결찰로 유도한 랫 실험적 치주염; 군: sham·vehicle·PDRN 젤·PDRN 젤+DMPX A2A 길항제): 국소 적용 PDRN이 A2A 아데노신 수용체(Adenosine A2A Receptor) 자극을 통해 치주 염증과 세포자멸을 감소시킴을 증명.

PDRN 젤은 치은/치주인대(PDL) 조직학적 구조 회복, 염증 단백질(p-JNK·p-ERK·TNF-α·IL-6·HMGB-1) 억제, 세포자멸촉진 BAX 감소, 항세포자멸 Bcl-2 보존; A2A 길항제 DMPX 공투여 시 PDRN 효과 완전 소거 → 수용체 매개 기전 확인.

이후 임플란트 주위·상악동·발치와 PDRN 연구들이 인용하는 PDRN 항염 기전의 원전 근거(A2A 작용제); 한계는 랫 결찰 모델의 임상/인간 peri-implant·상악동 적용은 추론적.

## Summary
Rat experimental periodontitis study demonstrating that PDRN reduces periodontal inflammation and apoptosis through A2A adenosine receptor stimulation; receptor blockade (DMPX) abolishes the effect, proving the mechanism is receptor-mediated.

## Key Contributions
The mechanistic anchor for PDRN's anti-inflammatory action: A2A adenosine receptor agonism. Later dental PDRN literature (bone/soft-tissue regeneration, peri-implant adjunct) repeatedly cites this A2A mechanism.

## Methodology
Experimental periodontitis induced by ligating the lower-left first molar cervix in rats (sham: no ligature). After 7 days: PDRN gel vs vehicle ± DMPX (A2A antagonist). Histology and inflammatory/apoptotic protein expression.

## Results
- Vehicle EPD: severe gingival/PDL infiltrate; ↑ p-JNK, p-ERK, TNF-α, IL-6, HMGB-1, BAX; ↓ Bcl-2.
- PDRN gel: restored histology, blunted inflammatory/apoptotic proteins, preserved Bcl-2.
- DMPX abrogated PDRN's effects → A2A-receptor mediated.
- Limitation: rat periodontitis model; mechanism generalization to peri-implant/sinus contexts is inferential.
