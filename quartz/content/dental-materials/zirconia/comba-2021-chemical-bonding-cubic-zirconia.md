## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (Materials 2021) investigating the bond strength stability of chemical-based bonding techniques to cubic (5Y-PSZ) high-translucency zirconia, comparing MDP primer alone, CoJet tribochemical silica coating alone, and their combination, with aging (6 months).

CoJet + silane + MDP primer produced the best long-term bond stability at 6 months; MDP alone also significantly improved bond strength vs control; single-mechanism groups showed greater bond degradation after aging than combined-treatment groups.

Cubic 5Y zirconia responds to MDP bonding similarly to standard 3Y-TZP, confirming that the same MDP-based protocol is applicable regardless of zirconia translucency grade.

## 세줄요약
인비트로(Materials 2021): 큐빅(Cubic, 5Y-PSZ) 고투명 지르코니아에 대한 화학적 결합 기법(MDP 프라이머 단독, CoJet 마찰화학 실리카 코팅 단독, 병용)의 접착강도 안정성을 6개월 노화 후 비교.

CoJet + 실란(Silane) + MDP 프라이머 병용이 6개월 최고 장기 결합 안정성; MDP 단독도 대조군보다 유의한 향상; 단일 기전 군은 노화 후 결합 저하가 병용 군보다 더 컸음.

큐빅 5Y 지르코니아(Zirconia)는 표준 3Y-TZP와 MDP 결합에 유사하게 반응 → 투명도 등급에 무관하게 동일한 MDP 기반 프로토콜 적용 가능.

## Summary
In vitro investigation of chemical-based bonding techniques on cubic (5Y-PSZ) high-translucency zirconia bond strength stability. Confirms that MDP chemical bonding principles applicable to standard Y-TZP extend to cubic zirconia; tribochemical silica coating (CoJet) provides additional micromechanical interlocking for optimal long-term stability.

## Key Contributions
- First confirmation that cubic 5Y zirconia responds to MDP bonding similarly to 3Y-TZP
- CoJet + silane + MDP: optimal protocol for long-term bond stability
- Bond degradation after aging: greater in single-mechanism groups; combined treatments more durable
- Clinical implication: same MDP-based protocol applicable regardless of zirconia translucency grade
