## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (J Adhes Dent 2025; n=105 specimens; 5,000 thermocycles) comparing 6 cleaning protocols for saliva-contaminated monolithic zirconia before self-adhesive resin cement bonding, measuring shear bond strength (SBS) after thermocycling.

Sandblasting + Ivoclean achieved the highest SBS (9.3 MPa), followed by sandblasting alone (8.59 MPa) and Ivoclean alone (7.21 MPa); isopropyl alcohol produced the lowest SBS (3.04 MPa), even lower than the contaminated untreated control (3.65 MPa).

Isopropyl alcohol is clinically contraindicated for zirconia cleaning; pumice and air-water spray are ineffective; sandblasting combined with Ivoclean represents the optimal decontamination protocol.

## 세줄요약
인비트로(J Adhes Dent 2025; n=105개 시편; 열순환 5,000회): 타액 오염된 단일체 지르코니아(Monolithic Zirconia)의 6가지 세정 프로토콜을 자기접착형 레진 시멘트 접착 후 전단접착강도(SBS)로 평가.

샌드블라스팅+Ivoclean 최고 SBS(9.3 MPa); 샌드블라스팅 단독(8.59 MPa)·Ivoclean 단독(7.21 MPa) 순; 이소프로필알코올(Isopropyl Alcohol) 최저(3.04 MPa), 오염 무처리 대조군(3.65 MPa)보다도 낮음.

이소프로필알코올은 지르코니아(Zirconia) 세정에 임상 금기; 퍼미스·물분사는 비효과적; 샌드블라스팅+Ivoclean 병용이 최적 오염제거 프로토콜.

## Summary
In vitro study comparing 6 cleaning protocols for saliva-contaminated monolithic zirconia before self-adhesive resin cement bonding (5,000 thermocycles). Sandblasting + Ivoclean combination produced the highest shear bond strength (9.3 MPa), significantly better than sandblasting alone (8.59 MPa) or Ivoclean alone (7.21 MPa). Isopropyl alcohol was the worst performer, yielding lower SBS than untreated control — a critical clinical warning against routine alcohol wipe.

## Key Contributions
- Sandblasting + Ivoclean synergy: each step independently contributes to decontamination
- Isopropyl alcohol contra-indicated: worsens bond strength below control
- Pumice and air-water spray: equivalent to untreated control — ineffective
- Ivoclean mechanism: zirconia oxide particles absorb phosphate contaminants from saliva
