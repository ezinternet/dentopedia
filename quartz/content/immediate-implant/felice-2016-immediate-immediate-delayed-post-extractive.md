## Three-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (n=210, 70 per arm) randomising single post-extraction implant sites to immediate placement, immediate-delayed placement (6 weeks), or delayed placement (4 months), reporting outcomes at 4 months post-loading (crown delivery).

Implant failure: 4/70 (6.0%) immediate, 4/70 (6.2%) immediate-delayed, 1/70 (1.6%) delayed (P=.369, NS); marginal bone loss significantly greater with more delay (-0.17 vs -0.20 vs -0.24mm, P=.006); Pink Esthetic Score significantly better for immediate/immediate-delayed vs delayed (12.48 vs 12.38 vs 11.71, P<.001).

At 4 months post-loading, immediate and immediate-delayed (6-week) placement show a numerically (non-significantly) higher failure rate than delayed placement, but significantly better peri-implant bone preservation and pink esthetics — an early trade-off signal the 1-year companion report re-examines.

## 세줄요약
3군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)(n=210, 군당 70명) — 발치 후 단일임플란트를 즉시식립·즉시지연식립(6주)·지연식립(4개월) 3군에 무작위배정, 부하 후 4개월 시점 결과 보고.

임플란트 실패: 즉시 4/70(6.0%), 즉시지연 4/70(6.2%), 지연 1/70(1.6%)(P=.369, 무의미); 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 지연 정도가 클수록 유의하게 큼(-0.17 vs -0.20 vs -0.24mm, P=.006); 핑크심미지수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES)는 즉시·즉시지연군이 지연군보다 유의하게 우수(12.48 vs 12.38 vs 11.71, P<.001).

4개월 시점에서 즉시·즉시지연(6주) 식립은 지연식립 대비 실패율이 수치상(비유의) 높으나 변연골 보존·핑크심미는 유의하게 우수 — 1년 후속보고에서 재검토되는 초기 트레이드오프 신호.

## Summary
This is the 4-month post-loading (interim) report of a large 3-arm RCT (n=210) that randomised single post-extraction implant sites to immediate placement, immediate-delayed placement (6 weeks after extraction), or conventional delayed placement (4 months after extraction). At this early timepoint, failure rates trended higher (non-significantly) for the two earlier-placement arms (6.0% and 6.2%) versus delayed (1.6%), while marginal bone loss and pink esthetics were both significantly better for immediate/immediate-delayed placement than for delayed placement. This creates an early, seemingly paradoxical pattern — earlier placement costs a little in failure risk but buys better bone and soft-tissue esthetics — that the 1-year companion report re-examines with a longer observation window on the same cohort.

## Key Contributions
- Large (n=210) 3-arm RCT directly comparing three placement-timing protocols (immediate/6-week/4-month) rather than the usual 2-arm immediate-vs-delayed design
- Isolates an early esthetic-vs-failure-risk trade-off at the 4-month timepoint: immediate/immediate-delayed placement wins on bone level and PES but shows a numerically (non-significantly) higher failure rate

## Methodology
Randomised parallel-group trial, 210 patients requiring single-tooth replacement, randomised 70/70/70 to immediate, immediate-delayed (6-week), or delayed (4-month) placement. Immediate/immediate-delayed sockets grafted with bone substitute + resorbable membrane when needed; delayed sockets grafted similarly if poorly preserved or in esthetic zones. Implants with ≥25 Ncm insertion torque healed unloaded 4 months; <25 Ncm healed 6 months. Temporary then definitive crowns. Outcomes (blinded assessors): implant/crown failure, complications, marginal bone level change, PES, patient satisfaction. This report covers 4-month post-loading data.

## Results
| Outcome | Immediate (n=70) | Immediate-delayed 6wk (n=70) | Delayed 4mo (n=70) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failures | 4 (6.0%) | 4 (6.2%) | 1 (1.6%) | .369 |
| Complications (patients) | 5 | 6 | 3 | .601 |
| Marginal bone loss (4mo) | -0.17 ± 0.11mm | -0.20 ± 0.09mm | -0.24 ± 0.12mm | .006 |
| PES at crown delivery | 12.48 | 12.38 | 11.71 | <.001 |
