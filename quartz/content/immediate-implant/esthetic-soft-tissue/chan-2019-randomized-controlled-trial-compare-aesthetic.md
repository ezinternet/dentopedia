## Three-line Summary

Randomized controlled trial (RCT), n=40 subjects with single immediately placed implants in maxillary anterior/premolar teeth, comparing temporary non-occluding crown (test) vs. healing abutment (control) over 12 months.

Mid-facial mucosal marginal level and papilla height showed minimal changes within both groups with no significant intergroup differences; marginal bone remodeling was modest and similar between groups, except for vertical crestal bone resorption.

Immediate provisionalization did not improve aesthetic outcomes over healing abutment restoration; both protocols achieved stable soft tissue levels at 12 months, suggesting the choice between them may depend on factors beyond pure esthetics.

## 세줄요약

무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT), n=40명, 상악 전치부·소구치 발치 후 즉시식립 단일임플란트에서 비교합 임시크라운을 이용한 즉시 임시보철 (Immediate Provisionalization)군과 치유지대주 (Healing Abutment)군을 12개월간 비교.

협측 점막변연레벨 (Mid-facial Mucosal Marginal Level)과 유두 높이 (Papilla Height)는 양 군 모두 최소 변화·군간 유의차 없음; 변연골개조 (Marginal Bone Remodeling, MBR)도 소폭·유사했으나 수직 치조정골흡수 (Vertical Crestal Bone Resorption)만 대조군(치유지대주)이 유의하게 적었음.

즉시 임시보철은 치유지대주 대비 추가적인 심미적 이득을 제공하지 못했고, 두 프로토콜 모두 12개월째 안정적 연조직 수준을 유지 — 술식 선택은 순수 심미 요인보다 다른 임상적 고려에 좌우될 수 있음을 시사.

## Summary

This randomized controlled trial compared immediate provisionalization with a non-occluding temporary crown versus a healing abutment in 40 patients receiving single immediately placed implants in the maxillary anterior or premolar region, following patients for 12 months after permanent restoration was delivered at 4 months. Both groups showed minimal, statistically indistinguishable changes in mid-facial mucosal marginal level and papilla height, and marginal bone remodeling was modest and similar between groups. The one significant difference was greater vertical crestal bone resorption in the immediate-provisionalization group compared with the healing-abutment group, though this radiographic difference did not translate into a clinically meaningful esthetic divergence. Implant survival at 12 months was numerically lower in the test group (90% vs 100%) but not statistically significant given the small sample. The findings suggest immediate provisionalization confers no added esthetic benefit over a healing abutment for immediately placed single implants, so the choice between the two protocols may reasonably rest on factors such as chairside efficiency, number of visits, or patient preference rather than expected soft-tissue outcome.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT with extended (12-month) follow-up directly comparing non-occluding immediate provisional crowns vs. healing abutments for immediately placed single implants in the esthetic zone
- Minimal, statistically indistinguishable mid-facial mucosal marginal level and papilla height changes regardless of provisionalization approach
- Similar marginal bone remodeling between the two protocols, with the single exception of greater vertical crestal bone resorption in the immediate-provisionalization group
- RCT-level evidence that immediate provisionalization does not confer additional esthetic benefit over a healing abutment in immediate implant placement

## Methodology

Randomized controlled trial, n=40 subjects with hopeless maxillary anterior or premolar teeth requiring extraction and single-implant replacement. After implant placement, subjects were randomly assigned to test (immediate non-occluding provisional crown) or control (healing abutment); permanent restoration was delivered at 4 months, with follow-up continuing to 12 months post-restoration. Primary outcomes were facial mucosal marginal level, papilla height, and radiographic crestal bone change; secondary outcomes were implant survival, clinical bone remodeling, and esthetic parameters.

## Results

| Outcome | Test (Provisional Crown) | Control (Healing Abutment) | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant survival at 12 mo | 90% (9/10) | 100% (10/10) | Not significant |
| Mid-facial mucosal marginal level change | Minimal | Minimal | No difference |
| Papilla height change | Minimal | Minimal | No difference |
| Marginal bone remodeling | Modest | Modest | No difference |
| Vertical crestal bone resorption | Higher | Lower | Significant, favors control |

Both protocols achieved stable soft tissue levels at 12 months; the radiographic difference in vertical crestal resorption did not correspond to a clinically meaningful esthetic divergence between groups.
