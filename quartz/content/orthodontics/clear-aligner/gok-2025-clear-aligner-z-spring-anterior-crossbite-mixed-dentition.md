## Three-line Summary

RCT, n=30 children (7–12y, Angle Class I molar occlusion + isolated pseudo-Class III anterior crossbite), clear aligners (Group A, n=15) vs Z-spring appliance (Group B, n=15).

Anterior crossbite corrected in all patients; treatment duration significantly shorter with Z-spring (48.4±27 days) than clear aligners (96.3±22.7 days, p<.05); Group A showed significant arch-depth increases (incisal 2.6mm, gingival 1.17mm, p<.001) not seen in Group B; OHRQoL (COHIP-SF-19) comparable between groups.

Clinical implication: both appliances effectively correct dental anterior crossbite in the mixed dentition, but movement mechanism differs (Z-spring favors tipping, clear aligners enable tipping + alignment + bodily movement) — abstract-only ingest, so methodological detail (randomization/blinding/attrition) is unconfirmed pending full text.

## 세줄요약

RCT, n=30 (혼합치열기 7–12세, Angle Class I 구치관계 + 고립성 위-Class III 전치부 반대교합), 투명교정(A군, n=15) vs Z-spring(B군, n=15).

전치부 반대교합은 전원 성공적으로 교정; 치료기간은 Z-spring군이 유의하게 짧음(48.4±27일 vs 96.3±22.7일, p<.05); A군은 절치·치은부 치열궁 깊이 유의 증가(각 2.6mm/1.17mm, p<.001)한 반면 B군은 변화 없음; 삶의 질(COHIP-SF-19)은 양군 유사.

임상적 함의: 두 장치 모두 혼합치열기 전치부 반대교합 교정에 효과적이나 이동기전이 다름(Z-spring=경사이동 위주, 투명교정=경사+정렬+치체이동) — abstract-only 인제스트로 무작위배정·눈가림·이탈률 등 방법론 상세는 본문 확보 전까지 불확실.

## Summary

**Abstract-only — full text not retrieved** (PMC12746677 returned an empty body; this page is built from the PubMed structured abstract only). This RCT randomized 30 children (7–12 years, Angle Class I molar occlusion, isolated dental pseudo-Class III anterior crossbite) 1:1 to clear aligners (Group A) or a removable Z-spring appliance (Group B), and compared treatment duration, cephalometric change, dental-cast arch-depth change, and oral health-related quality of life (COHIP-SF-19).

## Key Contributions

- Direct head-to-head RCT of clear aligners vs a conventional removable interceptive appliance (Z-spring) for dental anterior crossbite in the mixed dentition — the wiki's first RCT-level evidence on this growing-patient axis (previously only [[ye-2025-combined-use-of-miniscrews]], an n=1 case report on a different presentation).
- Quantifies a clinically meaningful treatment-duration trade-off: Z-spring corrects crossbite roughly twice as fast as clear aligners in this cohort.
- Distinguishes movement quality between appliances via model analysis: clear aligners produced measurable arch-depth (dentoalveolar) changes that Z-spring did not, consistent with clear aligners enabling more controlled/bodily movement vs the tipping-dominant mechanics of a spring appliance.
- Adds validated pediatric OHRQoL data (COHIP-SF-19) showing no quality-of-life difference between the two treatment modalities.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial (parallel, 2-arm), n=30.
- **Population**: 7–12 year-old children, Angle Class I molar relationship, isolated pseudo-Class III (dental, not skeletal) anterior crossbite.
- **Groups**: Group A — clear aligners (n=15); Group B — Z-spring appliance (n=15).
- **Outcomes**: treatment duration; cephalometric change (U1–NA angle, overjet); dental model analysis (incisal/gingival arch depth); OHRQoL via COHIP-SF-19.
- **Not available from abstract**: randomization/allocation-concealment procedure, blinding, exact statistical models, attrition/dropout, follow-up duration beyond active treatment.

## Results

| Outcome | Group A (clear aligner) | Group B (Z-spring) | Comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crossbite corrected | All patients | All patients | — |
| Treatment duration | 96.3 ± 22.7 days | 48.4 ± 27 days | B significantly shorter (p<.05) |
| U1–NA angle change | +5.9° | +7.7° | Both within-group p<.01; between-group Δ NS (p>.05) |
| Overjet change | +4 mm | +4.2 mm | Both within-group p<.01; between-group Δ NS (p>.05) |
| Incisal arch depth | +2.6 mm (p<.001) | No significant change | A only |
| Gingival arch depth | +1.17 mm (p<.001) | No significant change | A only |
| COHIP-SF-19 (OHRQoL) | Comparable | Comparable | NS between groups (p>.05) |
