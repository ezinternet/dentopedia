## Three-line Summary
In-vitro high-throughput scratch-assay screen of 39 lactic acid bacteria (LAB) strains on the Ca9-22 gingival epithelial cell line, using automated fluorescence microscopy and a modified Gompertz kinetic model to quantify re-epithelialization.
Streptococcus salivarius MS-oral-D6 was the strongest stimulator (~2.5-fold vs untreated control), and its effect was traced to a secreted >180 kDa serine protease (not lactic/acetic acid) whose activity was abolished by the protease inhibitor PMSF; P. gingivalis W83 significantly inhibited re-epithelialization, serving as a negative-control benchmark.
Purely in-vitro mechanistic evidence (no animal or human data) — clinical translation requires confirming the effector protein's mechanism and testing purified protein or high-producing strains in vivo.

## 세줄요약
잇몸상피세포주(Ca9-22)를 이용한 고처리량 스크래치 분석(scratch assay)으로 유산균(Lactic Acid Bacteria, LAB) 39종을 스크리닝한 시험관내(in-vitro) 연구, 자동형광현미경과 변형 곰페르츠(Gompertz) 모델로 재상피화(re-epithelialization) 동역학을 정량화했다.
살리바리우스연쇄상구균(Streptococcus salivarius) MS-oral-D6 균주가 가장 강력한 촉진 효과(무처치 대비 약 2.5배)를 보였고, 그 효과는 유산·초산이 아닌 분비형 세린단백분해효소(secreted serine protease, >180 kDa)에 기인했으며 단백분해효소 억제제(PMSF)로 효과가 소실됐다; 치은포르피로모나스균(P. gingivalis) W83은 대조적으로 재상피화를 유의하게 억제했다.
순수 시험관내(in-vitro) 기전 연구로 동물·인체 데이터가 없어, 임상 적용 전 정제 단백질 또는 고생산 균주의 생체내(in-vivo) 검증이 필요하다.

## Summary
This study developed a high-throughput scratch-assay platform (96-well HTSScratcher plus automated CellProfiler image segmentation) to quantify gingival epithelial re-epithelialization kinetics on the Ca9-22 cell line, fitting cell-infiltration curves with a modified Gompertz function. Screening 39 lactic acid bacteria (LAB) identified Streptococcus salivarius strain MS-oral-D6 as the strongest stimulator of re-epithelialization, accelerating repair kinetics by roughly 2.5-fold relative to untreated controls in a dose-dependent manner, while P. gingivalis W83 significantly suppressed repair, confirming the assay against known literature benchmarks. Mechanistic follow-up showed that MS-oral-D6's effect was not attributable to lactic or acetic acid fermentation products (unlike two other stimulatory S. salivarius strains) but instead depended on a secreted, proteinase K-sensitive, >180 kDa protein subsequently identified by LC-MS/MS as most consistent with a secreted serine protease; the effect was abolished by the serine-protease inhibitor PMSF. The findings suggest a probiotic bacterium can accelerate gingival wound healing through a specific secreted enzyme rather than through nonspecific metabolic byproducts, offering a candidate molecular mechanism for probiotic effects on gingival tissue health, though entirely in vitro.

## Key Contributions
- High-throughput, automated scratch-assay platform for gingival re-epithelialization kinetics (improvement on manual low-throughput scratch assays)
- Screen of 39 LAB strains identifying S. salivarius MS-oral-D6 as the top stimulator of gingival epithelial repair
- Identification of a secreted serine protease (not fermentation acids) as the likely effector molecule, via desalting/proteinase K sensitivity/SDS-PAGE/LC-MS/MS and PMSF inhibition

## Methodology
In-vitro experimental study using the Ca9-22 gingival epithelial cell line. Initial screen of 39 LAB strains at MOI 50 (duplicate); dose-response confirmation of 6 selected strains at MOI 10/50/250 (n=3, ≥2 independent experiments), with hTGFα as positive control and p38/MEK1/2 inhibitors as negative control, plus P. gingivalis W83 and L. rhamnosus GG as literature-benchmark reference strains. Mechanistic characterization used conditioned-medium transfer, 7 kDa desalting, proteinase K digestion, PMSF inhibition, SDS-PAGE/silver staining, and LC-MS/MS peptide mapping against S. salivarius genome sequences (n=37).

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| hTGFα (positive control) | >5-fold increase in re-epithelialization vs untreated |
| p38+MEK1/2 inhibitors (negative control) | ~2-fold suppression vs untreated |
| S. salivarius MS-oral-D6 (top stimulator) | ~2.5-fold increase; dose-dependent (P=0.039 at MOI 50, P<0.0001 at MOI 250) |
| P. gingivalis W83 | Significant inhibition (P=0.037) at high dose; monolayer deterioration |
| MS-oral-D6 conditioned medium + proteinase K | Stimulatory activity abolished |
| MS-oral-D6 conditioned medium + PMSF (serine-protease inhibitor) | Stimulatory activity abolished |
| Candidate effector protein | Secreted serine protease, >180 kDa, gene present in 12/37 sequenced S. salivarius genomes |
