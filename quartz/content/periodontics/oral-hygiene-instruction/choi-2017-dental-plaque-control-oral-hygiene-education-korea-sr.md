## Three-line Summary

This systematic review of 22 Korean studies (1990–2015) evaluated the effectiveness of professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR) combined with oral hygiene education (OHE) or oral hygiene instruction (OHI) across children, adults, and elderly populations. The optimal regimen was 4–5 visits at 1–2 week intervals, with disclosing agent-based instruction showing greater efficacy than verbal or model-based education alone.

Plaque index reductions ranged from 38% to 9.4-fold decreases depending on intervention frequency and method; periodontal probing depth decreased by 0.1–2.25 mm in 3-year maintenance programs (Shin et al., 2001), and bleeding on probing improved significantly with repeated education (Jo and Min, 2010: 42.52% improvement).

Regular OHE combined with PMPR reduces tooth loss and improves periodontal health, but the lack of randomized controlled trials (RCTs) in Korean literature limits the strength of evidence; future research should adopt rigorous RCT designs and develop standardized outcome measures.

치면세균막 지수는 중재 방법과 횟수에 따라 38%에서 9.4배까지 감소하였고, 3년 유지관리에서 치주낭 깊이는 0.1~2.25 mm 감소하였으며(Shin 등, 2001), 반복교육 시 탐침 출혈이 유의하게 개선되었다(Jo와 Min, 2010: 42.52% 개선).

## Summary

This systematic review synthesized evidence from 22 Korean studies published between 1990 and 2015 to evaluate the effectiveness of professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR) combined with oral health education (OHE) or oral hygiene instruction (OHI) on periodontal outcomes across children, adults, and elderly populations. The review identified optimal PMPR regimens as 4–5 visits at 1–2 week intervals, with disclosing agent-based education demonstrating superior efficacy compared to verbal or model-based instruction alone. Key findings included plaque index reductions ranging from 38% to 9.4-fold depending on intervention frequency, significant probing depth reductions (0.1–2.25 mm) in 3-year maintenance programs, and improved bleeding on probing with repeated education. The authors concluded that routine OHE/OHI combined with PMPR reduces tooth loss and improves periodontal health; however, the absence of randomized controlled trials in the Korean literature limits the strength of evidence, highlighting the need for rigorous RCT designs and standardized outcome measures.

## Key Contributions

- Identified optimal PMPR regimen: 4–5 visits at 1–2 week intervals with disclosing agent and repeated instruction
- Demonstrated plaque index reductions of 38–94% with professional mechanical plaque removal and education
- Showed 3-year maintenance programs reduced probing depth by up to 2.25 mm and bleeding on probing by 42.52%
- Established disclosing agent-based instruction as more effective than verbal or model-based education
- Highlighted critical evidence gap: no RCTs in Korean literature on this topic

## Methodology

This systematic review searched five core Korean databases (KoreaMed, KMbase, KISS, NDSL, KISTI) and five Korean journals for articles published between 1990 and 2015 on plaque control interventions including OHE or OHI. Of 307 initial records, 232 duplicates were removed; 45 records were screened, and 22 studies met inclusion criteria. Included studies comprised experimental-control group designs (5), within-subject comparisons (10), and management frequency comparisons (7). Target populations included children/youth (4 studies), adults (15), elderly (2), and other groups (1). Outcome measures included O'Leary plaque index (12 studies), Patient Hygiene Performance (PHP) index (5), and Simplified Oral Hygiene Index (S-OHI, 4 studies). No risk of bias assessment or meta-analysis was performed due to heterogeneity in study populations, interventions, and outcome measures.

Additional findings: 3-year maintenance care increased the proportion of sites with probing depth ≤3 mm from 86.6% to 93.88% (Chae et al., 2009). Bleeding on probing decreased with repeated education, with 1-year maintenance showing 15%→15.9% (0.8% increase) and 3-year showing 12.8%→12.4% (0.3% decrease) (Shin et al., 2001). Optimal visit frequency was 1–2 week intervals with 4–5 sessions; disclosing agent-based instruction showed 24.1-point improvement in PHP versus 2.8 points for model-based instruction and 11.6 points for direct practice instruction (An et al., 2012). The review emphasized that PMPR without OHE had limited effectiveness, and that personalized, repeated instruction according to patient characteristics was essential for sustained improvement.
