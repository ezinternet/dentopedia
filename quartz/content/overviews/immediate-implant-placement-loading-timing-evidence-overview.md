> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 즉시식립·즉시부하(Type 1A)는 **과학적·임상적으로 검증됨(SCV)** — 10,456개 임플란트 가중생존율 98.0% (Gallucci 2026 SR)
> - 9가지 식립시기×부하 조합 모두 가중생존율 91.6–100% 범위; **조합(combination)으로 계획** — 식립시기와 부하시기를 독립적으로 결정하지 않는다 (Gallucci 2018/2026)
> - **Type 1B (즉시식립+조기부하)**: 생존율 수치(91.6%)가 1B 조합 중 최저 — "임상적으로만 문서화(CD)" 등급, 근거 가장 취약; 1B는 피할 수 있으면 피한다
> - **즉시식립 vs 지연식립** (Felice 2016, Esposito 2017, 3군 RCT n=210): 실패율 비유의(6% vs 6.2% vs 1.6%), 즉시군이 골수준·핑크심미점수(PES) 유의하게 우월
> - **즉시부하 vs 통상부하** (Shibly 2010 RCT, 치주질환 이력 n=60): 생존율·골재생 차이 없음; 즉시부하가 점막치은접합부(MGJ) 이동 현저히 적음
> - **구치부 광폭경 즉시식립** (Checchi 2017 RCT n=100): 즉시군 골소실 多·PES 열등; 능선보존+지연식립이 구치부에서는 유리 — 전치부와 결론 방향이 다름
> - **PROMs·환자 만족도**: 식립·부하 시기가 만족도·불편감에 미치는 영향 미미 (Gotfredsen 2021 SR; Huynh-Ba 2018 SR) — 임상결과 차이가 환자경험 차이로 이어지지 않음
> - **즉시식립 조기식립 비교** (Puisys 2022 RCT, 전치부 n=50): 1년 PES 동등(12.8 vs 12.5), 즉시군이 내원 횟수 ~50% 절감; 적절한 증례 선택 전제
> - **간격부 이식재 (Gap Grafting)**: 자가골+이종골 1:1 혼합물이 무이식 대비 PES 유의 개선(13 vs 11.5, El-Ebiary 2023 RCT)
> - **소켓실드 기법 (Socket Shield)**: 협측 연조직 볼륨 유지 및 PES 개선, 전통적 기법 대비 유리 (Venkatraman 2023 RCT)
> - **임상 결정 원칙**: 1A·1C·2-3A·4A–C 모두 SCV → 선택 기준은 생존율이 아니라 **연조직·해부학·골벽 온전성**

## Three-line Summary

The 9 implant placement (Type 1–4) × loading (A/B/C) protocol combinations are all clinically viable, with weighted survival rates of 91.6–100% across 10,456 implants in 140 studies (Gallucci 2026 SR); the lowest-performing combination is Type 1B (immediate placement + early loading, 91.6%), while Type 1A (immediate placement + immediate loading) is now fully scientifically and clinically validated (SCV) at 98.0% — upgraded from the 2018 predecessor where it was merely "clinically documented."

RCT-level evidence shows that immediate placement does not disadvantage survival vs delayed (felice-2016 / esposito-2017: 6% vs 1.6% immediate vs delayed, NS), while immediate placement delivers significantly better bone levels and PES, and immediate loading equivalence in survival/bone-regeneration holds even in periodontally compromised patients (Shibly 2010) — however, molar wide-diameter immediate placement underperforms ridge-preservation + delayed in esthetics and marginal bone loss (Checchi 2017), highlighting a site-specific reversal of the anterior esthetic zone finding.

Placement and loading timing independently have minimal effect on patient-reported outcomes and satisfaction (Gotfredsen 2021, Huynh-Ba 2018); esthetic outcomes are further improvable via gap grafting (El-Ebiary 2023: PES 13 vs 11.5) and socket shield technique (Venkatraman 2023), making soft-tissue management — not timing selection alone — the primary lever for esthetic excellence.

## 세줄요약

식립시기(Type 1–4) × 부하시기(A/B/C) 9가지 조합은 모두 임상적으로 허용 가능한 생존율(91.6–100%, 임플란트 10,456개, Gallucci 2026 SR); 최저 성과 조합은 Type 1B(즉시식립+조기부하, 91.6%), Type 1A(즉시식립+즉시부하)는 98.0%로 완전한 과학적·임상적 검증(SCV) 등급 달성.

RCT 근거에서 즉시식립은 지연 대비 생존율 비열등(Felice 2016/Esposito 2017: 6% vs 1.6%, NS)이나 골수준·PES 유의 우월; 즉시부하는 치주질환 이력 환자에서도 골재생·생존율 동등(Shibly 2010); 단 구치부 광폭경 즉시식립은 능선보존+지연 대비 PES·골소실 모두 열등(Checchi 2017) — 전치부와 방향 다름.

식립·부하 시기 자체가 환자만족도·불편감에 미치는 영향은 미미(Gotfredsen 2021, Huynh-Ba 2018); 심미 결과 향상의 실질 레버는 간격부 이식재(El-Ebiary 2023: PES 13 vs 11.5)와 소켓실드(Venkatraman 2023) 등 연조직 관리다.

## 2. The 9-Protocol Matrix (Gallucci 2026)

Placement timing and loading protocol must be **planned as a pair**, not two independent decisions.

| Protocol | Survival | Validation | Key note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type 1A** — Immediate + Immediate | 98.0% | **SCV** | Upgraded from CD (2018); preferred for esthetic cases |
| **Type 1B** — Immediate + Early | 91.6% | CD | Lowest survival in the matrix; avoid unless justified |
| **Type 1C** — Immediate + Conventional | 95.0% | **SCV** | Safe when primary stability uncertain for immediate loading |
| **Type 2-3A** — Early + Immediate | 97.8% | **SCV** | Newly validated in 2026 (was CID in 2018) |
| **Type 2-3B** — Early + Early | 100% | CD | 100% on only 82 implants — very limited data |
| **Type 2-3C** — Early + Conventional | 94.0% | **SCV** | Reliable option when soft-tissue healing time needed |
| **Type 4A** — Late + Immediate | 97.2% | **SCV** | Standard late-placement with immediate loading |
| **Type 4B** — Late + Early | 97.9% | **SCV** | Well-validated |
| **Type 4C** — Late + Conventional | 97.5% | **SCV** | Classic protocol, maximum evidence base |

*SCV = Scientifically and Clinically Validated. CD = Clinically Documented (less evidence). Source: Gallucci 2026.*

**Clinical implication of the matrix**: survival differences between SCV protocols are small (94–98%). Protocol selection should be driven by soft-tissue/esthetic goals, bone wall integrity, primary stability, and site anatomy — not by expectation of a survival advantage.

## 3. Placement Timing: Immediate vs Delayed

### Anterior site (Felice 2016 / Esposito 2017 — 3-arm RCT, n=210)

| Outcome | Immediate | 6-week | 4-month | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Failure rate (1-year) | 6.0% | 6.2% | 1.6% | P=.369 (NS) |
| Marginal bone loss | −0.25 mm | −0.29 mm | −0.31 mm | P=.015 ✓ immediate best |
| PES (1-year) | 12.52 | 12.49 | 11.78 | P<.001 ✓ immediate/6-week best |

Immediate and 6-week (immediate-delayed) placement are statistically equivalent in failure rate to delayed, while significantly preserving more bone and peri-implant esthetics. The numerically higher (but NS) failure rate in immediate groups is worth watching at longer follow-up.

### Anterior site (Puisys 2022 — RCT, n=50, 1-year)

- Immediate (Type 1A) vs early (Type 2-3C) placement, maxillary anterior, intact bone walls
- No failures in either group
- PES: 12.8 ± 1.19 vs 12.5 ± 1.36 (NS, p=.362)
- Chair time: immediate group ~50% fewer appointments
- Mid-buccal mucosal level: immediate group +0.2 mm (slight coronal gain)

**Conclusion**: Esthetic equivalence at 1 year with significant efficiency advantage for immediate placement in well-selected cases.

### Molar site (Checchi 2017 — RCT, n=100)

Wide-diameter (6–8 mm) immediate implants vs ridge preservation + delayed conventional implants in molar sockets:

- Failure rate: 10.6% vs 4.6% (NS)
- Marginal bone loss: **1.06 mm vs 0.63 mm (P<.0001)** — immediate significantly worse
- PES: **9.71 vs 10.86 (P=.02, 1-year)** — delayed significantly better
- Clinical visits: 7.48 vs 10.30 (P<.001) — immediate fewer

**Site-specific reversal**: unlike anterior esthetic sites, molar immediate placement with wide-diameter implants yields inferior esthetic and bone outcomes. Efficiency gain does not compensate.

## 4. Loading Timing: Immediate vs Conventional

### Periodontally compromised patients (Shibly 2010 RCT ×2, n=60, 1–2 year)

All patients received allograft + collagen membrane at immediate placement:

| Outcome | Immediate loading | Conventional loading | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-year survival | 96.6% | 93.3% | NS (p>0.5) |
| 2-year bone gain | +1.19 mm | +1.00 mm | NS |
| MGJ coronal displacement | 15% of sites | 65% of sites | Significant |

**Key finding**: immediate loading achieves equivalent survival and bone regeneration to conventional loading even in periodontally compromised patients — and preserves mucogingival junction position far better.

## 5. Patient-Reported Outcomes (PROMs)

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| Huynh-Ba 2018 SR (Type 1, loading timing) | Patient satisfaction uniformly high regardless of loading timing; minimal PROM differences between immediate and conventional loading |
| Gotfredsen 2021 SR (all timing combinations) | No strong evidence that placement or loading timing alone influences discomfort, satisfaction, or esthetics from patient perspective; weak signal for transient preference for immediate loading in full-arch edentulous cases |

**Clinical implication**: timing protocol selection does not meaningfully differentiate patient experience. Communicate the timing difference to patients in terms of number of visits and surgical steps — not expected satisfaction outcomes.

## 6. Esthetic Enhancement Strategies

### Gap Grafting (El-Ebiary 2023 — RCT, n=24, esthetic zone)

Computer-guided immediate implant + digital immediate provisionalization, gap grafted (autogenous:xenograft 1:1) vs ungrafted:

- PES at 6 months: **13.0 vs 11.5 (p=0.048)** — grafted significantly better
- Intra-group trajectory: grafted improved (11.58→12.42, p=0.039); ungrafted declined (11.75→11.17, p=0.035)

**Conclusion**: grafting the 2–3 mm jumping gap in guided immediate placement actively improves and protects esthetic outcomes; leaving it empty leads to measurable esthetic decline.

### Socket Shield Technique (Venkatraman 2023 — RCT, n=22, 12-month)

Socket shield (SST) vs conventional technique, single maxillary incisor immediate implant + immediate loading:

- Buccal soft-tissue volumetric change: SST superior (less volume loss)
- PES: SST superior at 12 months

**Conclusion**: retaining the buccal root fragment as a biological shield preserves peri-implant soft-tissue volume and PES over 12 months.

## 7. Decision Framework

```
Step 1 — Site assessment
  Anterior esthetic zone, intact buccal wall:
    → Type 1A (immediate+immediate) if primary stability achieved
    → Type 1C (immediate+conventional) if primary stability borderline
    → Type 2-3A/C (early) if soft-tissue augmentation needed first
  Molar site:
    → ARP + Type 4A/C (delayed) preferred for esthetics
    → Wide-diameter immediate: only if patient explicitly accepts esthetic compromise

Step 2 — Loading window
  Avoid Type 1B (immediate + early loading) — lowest evidence, lowest survival
  Type 1A: ISQ ≥60 or insertion torque ≥30 N·cm threshold typically required
  Type 1C: safe fallback when primary stability criteria borderline

Step 3 — Esthetic augmentation (anterior only)
  Gap ≥2 mm → graft (auto:xeno 1:1 or DBBM) [El-Ebiary 2023]
  Thin buccal plate → consider socket shield technique [Venkatraman 2023]

Step 4 — Patient counseling
  Timing protocol does not meaningfully affect patient satisfaction [Gotfredsen 2021]
  Communicate in terms of visit count and total treatment duration
```

---

## 8. Evidence Gaps

- **Type 1B**: highest clinical interest (immediate+early), lowest evidence quality and worst pooled survival — needs well-powered RCTs
- **Molar immediate placement long-term data**: Checchi 2017 shows 1-year disadvantage; 5-year MBL/failure trajectory unknown
- **Socket shield RCT scale**: Venkatraman 2023 n=22 only; finding directionally consistent with histological rationale but underpowered
- **PROMs at >1 year**: both SR (Gotfredsen, Huynh-Ba) note very limited long-term patient-reported data
