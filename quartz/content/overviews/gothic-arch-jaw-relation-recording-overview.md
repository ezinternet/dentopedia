> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **주제**: 고딕아치 트레이싱(Gothic Arch Tracing, GAT = arrow-point tracing)을 중심으로 한 **수평 악간관계 = 중심위(Centric Relation, CR) 기록** 술식 11편(complete-denture 10 + occlusion Kwak)의 재현성·정확도·디지털화 종합. 수직 차원(OVD)은 [[complete-denture-ovd-determination-overview]]가 담당 — 이 페이지는 그 **수평 짝**.
> - **3대 술식 계열** (de Moraes Melo Neto 2021): ① 유도법(guided: 양손조작 Bimanual·턱끝유도 Chin-point) ② 도해법(graphic: 구내/구외 고딕아치) ③ 생리법(physiologic: 삼킴 Swallowing·혀 위치). 임상 선택은 이 3계열 안에서 이뤄진다.
> - **⭐ 2026-07 추가 — 그 SR이 스스로 지목한 공백을 같은 연구그룹이 메웠다**: de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 SR은 자기 한계를 "도해법끼리 정면 비교 없음 + 무치악 환자 없음"이라 적었는데, **de Sousa Ervolino 2023**(같은 UNESP 그룹, Goiato·de Moraes Melo Neto 공저)이 **무치악 12명에서 3계열 4기법을 한 번에** 비교했다 — 구외/구내 고딕아치(도해) vs 연하(생리) vs 혀거상 전방도수조작 FMTE(유도).
> - **⭐ 생리법(삼킴/연하)이 최약이라는 데 독립 연구가 수렴한다**: Bhagat 2025는 삼킴 재현성 0.62로 최하위, de Sousa Ervolino 2023은 연하법(D)이 **유일하게 유의하게 열등**(전후변위 3.78/3.45mm vs 나머지 2.8~3.1mm). 지표도 표본도 다른 두 연구가 같은 결론 → **삼킴/연하법은 1차 선택에서 빼라**는 근거가 두꺼워졌다.
> - **⭐ 반대로 '혀'를 쓰는 유도법은 잘 나온다**: de Sousa Ervolino의 FMTE(2.81/2.82)가 **수치상 최저**이고, Jász 2024도 혀끝(경구개·연구개 경계)·Dawson 양손조작을 최근접으로 꼽는다. **주의**: de Sousa Ervolino 저자의 결론("그래픽법이 우수")은 FMTE를 이기지 못하므로 자기 데이터와 어긋난다 — 결론이 아니라 수치를 보라. (또 EOGA가 양측 3.00±0.00, 즉 12명×3회에서 SD=0이라 측정 바닥/반올림 인공물 의심 → 이 논문의 도해법 수치는 신뢰도가 낮다.)
> - **재현성(reproducibility) 위계**: 디지털 고딕아치(~0.98, 체위 무관) > 턱끝유도(~0.79, 앙와위 신뢰) > 삼킴(~0.62, 직립 한정) (Bhagat 2025). 유도법 내에서는 **양손조작 ≥ 턱끝유도** (de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 SR: 3편 우세·2편 동등·1편 열세).
> - **정확도(reference 대비 일치) 위계는 reference에 따라 뒤집힌다**: 무치악 split-cast 검증에서는 **nick-and-notch(0.15/0.23mm) < 구내 고딕아치(0.42/0.51) < 구외 고딕아치(0.74/0.86)** 로 정적(static) 왁스기록이 최소 오차 (Singh 2026). 즉 GAT은 **재현성은 최고지만 정적 기준 대비 오차는 더 큼** — "재현성 ≠ 정확도"의 핵심 모순.
> - **오차의 방향(direction)이 크기만큼 중요**: 디지털 운동분석기(MIP 0점) 기준 Dawson 양손조작·혀끝(경구개·연구개 경계)이 최근접(전 축 유의차 無), 고딕아치 정점은 **전방+하방**(개구 OVD 인공물), 턱 후퇴법은 **후방 압박** — 다만 전부 ±1mm 이내 (Jász 2024). 후방 압박(능동/수동 후퇴)만 피하면 임상 허용.
> - **구내 > 구외 고딕아치**: 추적판이 **횡적 회전축(hinge axis)에 가까울수록** 각→선형 오차증폭이 적고 혀·근육 간섭이 적다 — Singh 2026 일관 + 생역학적 근거.
> - **구외 GAT vs 교합기 프로그래밍**(반조절성, 수평과로각 HCG): SR+MA(Potdukhe 2023, n=325)에서 cephalogram(+10°)·OPG(+5°)가 GAT보다 유의하게 큰 HCG 산출, CBCT·전방위 왁스는 차이 無 → 저자는 **OPG를 GAT의 실용적 대체**로 제시. 단 "기준이 GAT 자체"라 높은 값=더 정확이 아님 + 인도 표본 편중·이질성 큼.
> - **디지털화**: CAD/CAM 간이 구내 고딕아치 장치가 전통 구내장치와 CR 일치(편차 <0.5mm)하며 의자시간 57.5→41.9분 단축 (Zhou 2023, abstract-only). 자체제작 디지털 트레이서도 재현성 벤치마크 (Bhagat 2025).
> - **⭐ 2026-07 추가 — 디지털화는 한 갈래가 아니고, 성적이 갈린다**: ① **추적장치를 디지털화**하는 길(Zhou 2023 CAD/CAM 구내 GAT)은 <0.5mm로 **성공**. ② **턱운동추적으로 대체**하는 길(Li 2022, 자체제작 시스템, 무치악 10명)은 전체 악궁 편차 **0.76–1.35mm(p<0.001)**로 저자 스스로 "정확도 개선 필요" — **아직 임상 미달**. 같은 팀 벤치 연구(Li 2021)는 112–133μm였으니 **벤치→구강에서 약 6–10배 열화**한 셈이다. "디지털이니 정확하겠지"가 아니라 **어느 갈래인지**를 물어야 한다.
> - **⭐ 근거 자체가 얇다**: 디지털 총의치 악간관계 기록법 scoping review(Ribeiro 2024)는 4779편을 훑어 **단 10편**만 포함했다 — 상용 시스템이 활발히 팔리는 것에 비해 근거가 희박하다는 것 자체가 소견. 상용 4종·혁신 3종(2–4회 내원), 다만 **정확도 순위는 매기지 않으므로** 비교정확도 근거로 인용 금지.
> - **장기·기능 정의 확장**: GAT을 단발 기록이 아니라 **정상화된 저작위(CM-H)=치료위** 설정 도구로 쓴 10년 추적(Kwak 2025, n=16) — CM-H↔MICP 일치 63% 유지, 전체 회복 81%. Gysi의 "최후방 인대유도 경계위" CR 정의에서 **신경근 조화 기능위**로의 재정의.
> - **원장 결정 요지**: 무치악 정밀·표준 케이스 → nick-and-notch/정적기록이 오차 최소; **신경근 제어 저하·습관성 전방폐구·심한 흡수릉·전악 기능재건**에는 고딕아치(특히 구내·디지털)가 시각적 정점확인·기능위 포착으로 유리. 후방 압박 술식(턱 후퇴)은 회피.

## Three-line Summary

11-paper synthesis (10 complete-denture + Kwak 2025 occlusion) on Gothic arch (arrow-point) tracing and centric-relation recording: reproducibility hierarchy is digital Gothic arch ≈ 0.98 (posture-independent) > chin-point 0.79 (supine-dependable) > swallowing 0.62 (upright only; Bhagat 2025), with bimanual ≥ chin-point for guided methods (de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 SR) — and de Sousa Ervolino 2023, from the same group as that SR, closed its two declared gaps by comparing all three families head-to-head in edentulous patients, independently confirming that the physiologic (deglutition) family is the weakest arm.

Accuracy against a static split-cast reference runs opposite: nick-and-notch (0.15/0.23 mm) < intraoral Gothic arch (0.42/0.51) < extraoral Gothic arch (0.74/0.86), because the apex is recorded at an opened OVD (Singh 2026); error direction matters — Gothic arch biases anterior, retrusion methods seat posteriorly, all within ±1 mm (Jász 2024), intraoral beats extraoral, and OPG substitutes for extraoral GAT in condylar-guidance programming (Potdukhe 2023 SR+MA).

Clinically, standard edentulous cases favor a well-made static record (smallest discrepancy); Gothic arch tracing is preferred for impaired neuromuscular control, severely resorbed ridges, or functional-position definition, where a CAD/CAM intraoral device preserves accuracy (<0.5 mm) while saving ~16 minutes of chair time (Zhou 2023) — but "digital" splits into two bets with different records: digitizing the tracing device works, whereas replacing tracing with jaw-motion tracking still reaches only 0.76–1.35 mm in patients despite 112–133 µm on the bench (Li 2022, Li 2021), and the whole digital-MMR field rests on 10 studies out of 4,779 screened (Ribeiro 2024).

## 세줄요약

고딕아치(arrow-point) 트레이싱과 수평 악간관계(중심위) 기록 11편 종합 — **재현성 위계**: 디지털 고딕아치 ~0.98·체위무관 > 턱끝유도 0.79·앙와위 신뢰 > 삼킴 0.62·직립 한정(Bhagat 2025); 양손조작 ≥ 턱끝유도(de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 SR). 그 SR이 스스로 지목한 공백(도해법 정면비교·무치악 부재)을 같은 그룹의 de Sousa Ervolino 2023이 무치악 12명·3계열 4기법으로 메웠고, **연하법이 최약**이라는 결론이 독립적으로 수렴했다.

**기준 대비 정확도 위계**는 반전: nick-and-notch(0.15/0.23 mm) < 구내 고딕아치(0.42/0.51) < 구외 고딕아치(0.74/0.86)로 정적 왁스기록이 split-cast 오차 최소(Singh 2026); 오차 방향은 고딕아치=전방, 턱후퇴=후방, 모두 ±1 mm 내(Jász 2024); 구내>구외 우위이며 OPG가 교합기 수평과로각 프로그래밍 대체 가능(Potdukhe 2023 SR+MA).

임상 결정: 무치악 표준 케이스는 잘 만든 정적 기록(오차 최소); 신경근 제어 저하·흡수릉·전악재건에는 고딕아치(특히 구내·디지털, <0.5 mm 정확도 유지·의자시간 ~16분 단축; Zhou 2023); 턱 후방 압박 술식과 **연하법**은 회피. "디지털"은 두 갈래로 성적이 갈린다 — 추적장치 디지털화는 검증됐으나(<0.5mm), 턱운동추적 대체는 환자에서 0.76~1.35mm로 아직 미달(벤치는 112~133μm; Li 2022·Li 2021)이고, 디지털 악간관계 근거 자체가 4779편 중 10편뿐(Ribeiro 2024).

## Summary

Determining the **horizontal jaw relation — centric relation (CR)** is the most error-sensitive step of complete-denture and full-mouth occlusal reconstruction: edentulism removes periodontal proprioception, leaving mandibular position to the TMJ and musculature. **Gothic arch (arrow-point) tracing (GAT)** — a stylus on a mandibular plate scribing an arrowhead whose apex marks CR — is the classical *graphic* method. This synthesis assembles the current evidence on how GAT compares with the other recording families, and resolves a recurring confusion: **reproducibility and accuracy are different axes and they rank GAT differently.**

Following de Moraes Melo Neto 2021, CR techniques fall in three families — **guided** (bimanual manipulation, chin-point guidance), **graphic** (intraoral/extraoral GAT), and **physiologic** (swallowing, tongue position). GAT is prized because a patient *re-traces the same apex repeatedly* (high reproducibility), but the apex is recorded at an opened vertical dimension and reflects a functional border, not a static habitual closure — so against a static or MIP reference it can sit slightly off. The clinically useful questions are therefore: which method re-finds the same point (reproducibility), which point is "right" and against what reference (accuracy), in which *direction* do methods err, intraoral vs extraoral, and how does digitization change the trade-off.

## Results

### Axis 1 — The three CR-recording families and the evidence map

de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 ([[complete-denture/de-moraes-melo-neto-2021-centric-relation-condylar-reproducibility-sr]]; PRISMA SR, 6 low-quality studies, **all dentate, none edentulous**) is the framework anchor. Critically, the within-method evidence is almost entirely **guided**: every one of the 6 studies compared only **bimanual manipulation vs chin-point guidance** — none compared the two *graphic* (Gothic arch) techniques head-to-head, and none enrolled edentulous patients. Tally: bimanual superior in 3, equivalent in 2, inferior in 1 → **bimanual ≥ chin-point** for condylar-position reproducibility in dentate non-TMD subjects. The take-home is twofold: a technique-level anchor (two-handed stabilization beats one-handed guidance) **and an evidence gap** — graphic and physiologic methods, and the edentulous population, were essentially unstudied at SR level. The papers below fill exactly those gaps.

**The same group then filled its own gaps.** de Sousa Ervolino 2023 ([[complete-denture/de-sousa-ervolino-2023-centric-relation-recording-techniques-edentulous]]; observational cross-sectional, n=12 **edentulous**, Araçatuba/UNESP — Goiato and de Moraes Melo Neto are co-authors on both papers) put all three families in one design and measured them the way the SR framed the question: tri-dimensional displacement of the condylar housing against the condylar guide wall on a semi-adjustable articulator, each technique repeated three times per subject. The four arms are **extraoral Gothic arch (EOGA)** and **intraoral Gothic arch (IOGA)** — the graphic head-to-head the SR said did not exist — plus **deglutition (D)** for the physiologic family and **frontal manipulation with tongue elevation (FMTE)** for the guided family. It is the closest thing this literature has to a within-subject, all-families, edentulous comparison, and its arrival is why Axes 2 and 3 below can now be read across families rather than within one.

Read it with two cautions. First, **the authors' own conclusion overreaches their data**: they conclude "graphic recordings showed better reproducibility and accuracy," yet FMTE (2.81/2.82 mm) is numerically the *lowest* anteroposterior displacement in their table, and the only statistically clean separation is that deglutition is worst (ANOVA F=11.396, p<0.001). Their data support "physiologic is worst," not "graphic is best." Second, **their graphic arm is numerically suspect**: EOGA is reported as 3.00 ± 0.00 mm on *both* sides — a zero standard deviation across 12 subjects × 3 repeats is not a plausible biological measurement and points to a measurement floor or rounding artefact. Treat this paper's family-level *ranking of the graphic arm* as unusable, and its physiologic finding as its durable contribution.

### Axis 2 — Reproducibility: digital Gothic arch leads, swallowing trails

Bhagat 2025 (in-vivo, n=30 edentulous/overdenture, custom digital intraoral tracer) ranks reproducibility cleanly: **digital Gothic arch tracing 0.98 (≈99%, posture-independent) > light chin-point guidance 0.79 (≈88%, dependable supine) > swallowing 0.62 (≈77%, reliable only upright)** (all p≈0.000). Two clinical messages: GAT is the reproducibility benchmark and *tolerates posture change*, and among bedside guided/physiologic methods **chin-point guidance is preferable to swallowing** (more reproducible and posture-robust). This complements de Moraes Melo Neto 2021's guided-method finding by adding the graphic and physiologic arms it lacked.

**The physiologic family's weakness is now a convergence, not a single finding.** de Sousa Ervolino 2023 reaches the same verdict from a different direction: deglutition was the **only** technique to differ significantly from the others in anteroposterior condylar displacement (3.78 mm right / 3.45 mm left vs 2.81–3.12 mm for EOGA, IOGA, and FMTE). Two independent groups, two different populations, two incommensurable metrics — internal reproducibility coefficients in Bhagat, articulator condylar displacement in de Sousa Ervolino — land on the same clinical instruction: **do not make swallowing/deglutition your primary record.** Nothing in this overview is corroborated as robustly across studies as this one negative, which is worth stating plainly because the swallowing method's appeal is precisely that it is fast: de Sousa Ervolino times it at **86 s versus 340 s for IOGA and 286 s for EOGA**, so the trade being offered is roughly four minutes of chair time against the reproducibility of the record. Take the four minutes.

**And the tongue keeps showing up on the good side.** FMTE — frontal manipulation *with tongue elevation* — was numerically the lowest-displacement arm in de Sousa Ervolino (2.81/2.82 mm) at 101 s, and Jász 2024 independently found tongue-tip positioning at the hard/soft-palate border among the closest methods to the motion-analyser reference, alongside Dawson bimanual. This is a weak convergence (neither result is a clean significance win, and the two studies measure different things), but the direction is consistent and cheap to act on: when guiding manually, having the patient elevate the tongue costs nothing and both datasets favour it.

### Axis 3 — Accuracy is reference-dependent, and it inverts the ranking

Here the two non-GAT-friendly results live, and they are not contradictions but different *reference standards*:

- **Singh 2026** (within-subject, n=20 edentulous, **split-cast on a semi-adjustable articulator** as the objective check) found a discrepancy hierarchy **Nick and Notch 0.15 mm (centric) / 0.23 mm (protrusive) < intraoral Gothic arch 0.42 / 0.51 < extraoral Gothic arch 0.74 / 0.86** (all pairwise p<0.001; protrusive error > centric for all). Against a static split-cast reference, the **static** wax registration (nick-and-notch) is most consistent and GAT carries more discrepancy.
- **Jász 2024** (cross-sectional, n=34 dentate, **KaVo Arcus Digma 2 motion analyser, MIP = 0 point**) found that **Dawson bimanual** (0/0.01 mm sagittal, ns all axes) and **tongue-tip at the hard/soft-palate border** deviated least, while the **gothic-arch apex sat anterior + downward** (0.28–0.30 mm sagittal, −0.47 to −0.5 mm vertical) and retrusion methods sat posterior — but **all seven methods stayed within ±1 mm**.

The reconciliation: GAT is highly *reproducible* (Bhagat 2025) yet shows larger *discrepancy versus a static/MIP reference* (Singh 2026, Jász 2024) because its apex is a functional border captured at an **opened OVD** (Jász 2024 explicitly flags the open-registration confounder for GAT's anterior-inferior bias). "Reproducible" ≠ "coincident with habitual closure." Reading these three papers on one axis is the single most important clinical insight of this overview.

### Axis 4 — Direction of error, and intraoral > extraoral

Two convergent sub-themes:

- **Direction matters more than magnitude** (Jász 2024, [[complete-denture/jasz-2024-centric-relation-methods-digital-mandibular-motion]]): the methods to *avoid* are the **posterior-seating** ones (active retrusion, passive 20 N chin push) that force a retruded condyle; GAT's bias is anterior and partly an OVD artifact, so it is clinically safer in direction even when larger in magnitude. Non-retrusive methods (Dawson, tongue-at-border, GAT, adduction field) are all acceptable.
- **Intraoral beats extraoral GAT** (Singh 2026, mechanistic, and consistent with biomechanics): a tracing plate **near the transverse hinge axis** suffers less angular-to-linear magnification than a forward-projecting extraoral platform, and the smaller intraoral device disturbs tongue/musculature less. When GAT is chosen, prefer the **intraoral** form.

### Axis 5 — Extraoral GAT vs articulator programming (condylar guidance)

Potdukhe 2023 (SR+MA, 20 of 22 studies, n=325 edentulous, PROSPERO CRD42022283974) addresses a *different* use of GAT — programming the **horizontal condylar guidance (HCG)** angle of a semi-adjustable articulator. Versus extraoral GAT as the reference: **cephalogram (+10.2–10.7°) and panoramic OPG (+5.1°)** gave significantly higher HCG angles (p<0.05 bilaterally; cephalogram pool tightest at I²=0%), while **CBCT and protrusive interocclusal wax record showed no significant difference** (but with I²=95–99%, so "no difference" is weakly supported). The authors name **OPG the best practical accurate alternative**. Two caveats anchor the interpretation: "accuracy" is agreement *against GAT itself* (a higher reading is not automatically more anatomically correct), and 19/22 studies were from India (generalizability). Net: a radiograph can substitute for the technique-sensitive extraoral tracer for *articulator programming* specifically.

### Axis 6 — Digitization and the function-defined therapeutic position

- **Digitization narrows the cost of GAT** (Zhou 2023, prospective paired n=10, abstract-only): a CAD/CAM **simplified intraoral Gothic arch device** reproduced the conventional intraoral tracer's CR to **<0.5 mm** (midline + molar, vertical + horizontal by STL best-fit) while cutting chair time **57.5 → 41.9 min** (P<0.01). Combined with Bhagat 2025's custom digital tracer, the trajectory is clear: digital intraoral GAT keeps the reproducibility advantage and removes much of the chairside-time penalty.
- **But "digital" is two different bets, and only one has paid off.** The Zhou/Bhagat route *digitizes the tracing device* — the patient still traces an arrow point, the apparatus is just CAD/CAM and the readout is an STL. The rival route *replaces tracing altogether* with jaw-motion tracking, and it has not reached clinical accuracy. Li 2022 (prospective, n=10 edentulous, Peking University, in-house optical jaw-tracking system) derived both dimensions computationally — the vertical from a plane 2 mm above mandibular rest position, the horizontal from the highest-density intersection of the habitual opening-closing trajectory through that plane — and landed at a whole-arch 3D deviation of **0.76–1.35 mm (95% CI, p<0.001)** against new complete dentures in intercuspation, with the authors concluding "the accuracy needs to be further improved." Tellingly, the anterior reference point showed *no* significant difference while the whole arch did: a single point agreeing while the arch does not is the signature of rotational rather than translational error. The same team's bench study (Li 2021) recorded eccentric jaw relations on an articulator at **112–133 µm**, so the identical hardware degrades roughly **6–10× from articulator to mouth** — a rare, quantified demonstration of how far an in-vitro accuracy claim travels. For calibration, Singh 2026 treats 0.74–0.86 mm as clinically perceptible; Zhou's device sits under 0.5 mm, Li's method above it. The lesson for the reader is not "digital is better" but **ask which digital**: digitizing a validated analogue technique is a small, safe step; replacing the patient's traced border with an inferred one from habitual movement is a different and unfinished project — and it rests on a substantive assumption, that habitual closure *is* the target, which is exactly what long-term edentulism erodes.
- **The evidence base for digital jaw relation is thinner than the marketing.** Ribeiro 2024 (PRISMA-ScR scoping review, UFRN + Zurich) screened **4,779 records and included only 10 studies** on maxillomandibular relationship recording for computer-engineered complete dentures. It maps 4 commercially available CAD-CAM denture systems and 3 innovative techniques (2–4 visits; the Baltic System requires a trial denture; three techniques including WholeYouNexteeth rated lower-difficulty regardless of operator skill), and concludes the techniques are effective but depend on the learning curve and the patient's clinical condition. Two boundaries matter: a scoping review **maps and does not rank**, so this page must never be cited for comparative accuracy; and "difficulty level" is the reviewers' descriptive appraisal, not a validated patient-centred outcome. The 10-of-4,779 yield is itself the finding — the systems are sold faster than they are studied.
- **From an instant to a position** (Kwak 2025 — page stem [[occlusion/kwak-2024-masticatory-positioning-occlusal-stability-full-mouth]], indexed under 2024; prospective 10-year follow-up, n=16, occlusion): a Gothic arch tracer (N-GAT) was used not to capture a single CR but to set the **normalized hard-food chewing position (CM-H) as the therapeutic position** for full-mouth rehabilitation. At ~10 years, **CM-H↔MICP concordance held at 63%**, overall stomatognathic recovery reached **81% (37% maintained + 44% progressed)**, and recovery was strongest when occlusal adjustment accompanied reconstruction. This reframes the classical Gysi CR (most-posterior, ligament-guided border) toward a **neuromuscular function-harmonized position**, and supplies the rare long-term human validation that a GAT-defined occlusion is durable.

## Clinical Decision Threads [미검증 — Claude 해석]

- **Default standard edentulous case (good ridges, cooperative patient)** → a well-made **static record (nick-and-notch / careful retruded wax)** gives the smallest discrepancy against split-cast (Singh 2026); guided **bimanual** is the reproducibility-validated manual adjunct (de Moraes Melo Neto 2021, Jász 2024).
- **Impaired neuromuscular control, habitual protrusive closure, or severely resorbed ridges** → choose **Gothic arch tracing** for its visual apex confirmation and high reproducibility (Singh 2026 author caveat; Bhagat 2025). Prefer the **intraoral** form over extraoral (less error magnification).
- **Chairside method choice when not using GAT** → **chin-point guidance over swallowing** (Bhagat 2025); chin-point is dependable supine. **Avoid forced posterior retrusion** methods (Jász 2024). Two independent datasets now agree that the **swallowing/deglutition family is the weakest** (Bhagat 2025 reproducibility 0.62; de Sousa Ervolino 2023 the only significantly worse arm) — its ~4-minute time saving (86 s vs 286–340 s for tracing) is the whole of its case. When manipulating manually, **have the patient elevate the tongue**: FMTE was numerically best in de Sousa Ervolino and tongue-tip positioning was among the closest in Jász 2024, at no cost.
- **Programming a semi-adjustable articulator's condylar guidance** → extraoral GAT works but is technique-sensitive; **OPG (panoramic) is an acceptable, more practical alternative** for the HCG angle (Potdukhe 2023).
- **Going digital** → a CAD/CAM intraoral Gothic arch device preserves accuracy (<0.5 mm) and saves ~15 min of chair time (Zhou 2023); reasonable to adopt where the workflow exists. **But distinguish the two digital routes**: digitizing the *tracing device* is validated (<0.5 mm); replacing tracing with *jaw-motion tracking* is not yet (Li 2022: 0.76–1.35 mm whole-arch, authors' own verdict "accuracy needs to be further improved"; the same hardware reads 112–133 µm on an articulator, so bench numbers do not transfer). And the field's evidence base is thin overall — 10 studies out of 4,779 screened (Ribeiro 2024).
- **Full-mouth reconstruction / defining the therapeutic position** → consider the GAT-derived **chewing-centered functional position** (Kwak 2025), and pair reconstruction with **occlusal adjustment** for best long-term stomatognathic recovery.

## Cross-cutting caveat — references are not comparable across studies

Each study judges "accuracy" against a *different* benchmark: split-cast/habitual retruded closure (Singh 2026), MIP via motion analyser (Jász 2024), GAT-as-reference (Potdukhe 2023), or internal reproducibility with no external truth (Bhagat 2025, de Moraes Melo Neto 2021). There is **no anatomic CR gold standard** in this literature. Therefore cross-study magnitude comparisons are illegitimate; the durable conclusions are the *within-study rankings and directions* synthesized above, not the absolute millimetre values.

## 원장 메모 체크리스트

- 재현성과 정확도를 분리해 말하라 — "고딕아치가 제일 재현성 좋다"는 맞지만 "제일 정확하다"는 기준에 따라 틀린다.
- 무치악 표준 케이스: 잘 만든 정적 왁스(nick-and-notch)가 split-cast 오차 최소.
- 고딕아치를 쓸 거면 **구내** 장치로 (구외보다 오차증폭·간섭 적음).
- 턱끝유도 > 삼킴 (의자 옆에서). **턱 후퇴 압박은 금지** (후방 변위).
- **삼킴/연하법은 1차 선택에서 빼라** — 독립된 두 연구가 수렴(Bhagat 재현성 0.62 최하; de Sousa Ervolino 유일하게 유의 열등). 유일한 장점은 시간(86초 vs 트레이싱 286~340초)이니, 그 4분을 아끼지 마라.
- 손으로 유도할 땐 **혀를 올리게 하라** — 공짜인데 두 연구가 다 좋게 나온다(FMTE 수치상 최저, Jász의 혀끝법 최근접).
- 교합기 수평과로각만 필요하면 OPG로 대체 가능.
- 디지털 구내 고딕아치 = 정확도 유지 + 의자시간 단축. 단 **"디지털"이 두 갈래**임을 구분하라 — 추적장치를 디지털화한 것(<0.5mm, 검증됨) vs 턱운동추적으로 대체한 것(0.76~1.35mm, 아직 임상 미달).
- 전악재건 치료위는 저작위 기반 기능위 + 교합조정 동반 시 장기 안정.
