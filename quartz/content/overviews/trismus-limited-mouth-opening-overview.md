## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: 개구장애 (Trismus)는 **진단명이 아니라 징후**다. 치과에서 가장 위험한 실수는 이를 곧바로 턱관절장애 (Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD)로 등치시키는 것 — 파상풍·중추신경계 병변·구강하악 근긴장이상 (Oromandibular Dystonia)·방사선 후 섬유화가 같은 징후로 나타난다. 논문 2편(서술적 리뷰 1·증례보고 1) 종합. [확인]
> - **정의 수치**: 개구장애 = 절치간 거리 (Interincisal Distance) **< 35 mm** (정상 40–60 mm). 이 한 숫자가 기록과 추적의 기준선이며, 회복 판정도 이 축에서 한다(Falletti 2026). [확인]
> - **4분면 병인 분류(Falletti 2026의 핵심 기여)**: 신경학적 vs 비신경학적 × 구조적 vs 비구조적. 이 2×2가 유용한 이유는 **치료 방향이 분면마다 갈리기 때문**이다 — 구조적이면 물리적 해제(수술·물리치료), 신경학적·비구조적이면 약물(보툴리눔 독소·바클로펜)로 간다.
> - 분면별 대표 원인: 중추·말초신경 병변, **파상풍 (Tetanus)**, 구강하악 근긴장이상(신경학적) / 턱관절장애, **구강안면 감염**, **방사선 후 섬유화 (Post-radiation Fibrosis)**, 대사 장애(비신경학적). 치과 일상에서 흔한 것은 감염·TMD·시술 후지만, **놓치면 치명적인 것은 파상풍과 중추 병변**이다. [확인]
> - **진단은 red-flag 우선 단계적 알고리즘**: 흔한 원인부터 훑지 말고 위험 신호(전신 증상·발열·진행성 신경학적 결손·외상력·예방접종력·악성종양력·방사선 조사력)를 먼저 배제한 뒤 국소 원인으로 내려간다(Falletti 2026). [확인]
> - **치료는 원인 지향**: 급성·비합병성 → 비스테로이드소염제 (NSAIDs)·근이완제; 신경학적·경직형 → **보툴리눔 독소 A형 (BoNT-A)** 또는 **바클로펜 (Baclofen)**; **물리치료는 모든 분면에 공통**; 관절외 제한이 불응성이면 **오훼돌기절제술 (Coronoidectomy)**. [확인]
> - **치과에서 가장 흔한 의원성 원인 — 하치조신경 전달마취 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 후 개구장애**: 59세 여성이 IANB 2회 후 **12시간 이내에 개구 18 mm**까지 악화되었고, 구조화된 프로그램(악운동 운동·NSAIDs·근이완제·스테로이드 주사)으로 **50일째 42 mm 완전 회복**(Khalifeh 2024). [확인, 증례]
> - **IANB 개구장애의 6가지 기전**: ① 내측익돌근 (Medial Pterygoid) 관통 ② 혈종 ③ 감염 ④ 근독성 (Myotoxicity) ⑤ 갈고리형 바늘 (Barbed Needle) 손상 ⑥ 용적에 의한 압력. 여러 기전이 겹칠 수 있어 단일 원인 규명보다 **조기 개입**이 중요. [확인]
> - **회복의 실제 동력은 물리치료였다**: 이 증례에서 12일 이후 **단계적으로 강화한 악운동 운동이 회복의 주 동인**이었고, 약물은 보조였다. 치료 지연은 **반흔 형성과 만성 제한**으로 이어진다 — 즉 "좀 지켜보자"가 가장 나쁜 선택. [확인, 증례]
> - 임상 규칙: ① 개구장애를 보면 **먼저 mm를 재서 기록**하고(<35 mm), ② TMD로 단정하기 전에 red flag(발열·전신증상·신경학적 결손·방사선력·외상·예방접종력)를 훑고, ③ 시술 후 발생이면 IANB 6기전을 떠올려 **조기에 악운동 운동을 시작**하고, ④ 원인 분면에 맞는 약물을 붙이되 **물리치료는 어느 분면이든 공통**으로 유지하며, ⑤ 불응성 관절외 제한은 오훼돌기절제술 상담 대상.
> - 근거 한계(중대): **이 페이지는 서술적 리뷰 1편 + 단일 증례 1편**에 기반한다. 4분면 분류와 단계적 알고리즘은 **전문가 제안이지 검증된 진단 도구가 아니며**, IANB 회복 경과(50일·42 mm)와 "물리치료가 주 동인"이라는 귀인은 **n=1**이다. 수치를 예후 상담에 쓸 때는 이 점을 명시해야 한다. [확인]
> - **턱관절장애 자체의 치료 사다리 → [[overviews/tmd-management-evidence-ladder]]·[[overviews/conservative-physical-therapy-myofascial-tmd-ladder]] 참조** — 이 페이지는 "개구 제한이라는 징후의 감별"만 다루고 TMD 관리 알고리즘은 중복하지 않는다.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of two papers (1 narrative review, 1 case report) treating trismus as a **sign requiring differential diagnosis rather than a diagnosis in itself** — defined as an interincisal distance below 35 mm against a normal range of 40–60 mm. Falletti 2026 proposes a four-quadrant etiological classification (neurological versus non-neurological × structural versus non-structural) whose value is that treatment direction differs by quadrant: structural causes call for physical release (surgery, physiotherapy) while neurological non-structural causes call for pharmacological management. Etiologies span CNS and PNS lesions, tetanus, and oromandibular dystonia on one side, and TMJ disorders, orofacial infections, post-radiation fibrosis, and metabolic disturbances on the other.

Diagnosis is organized as a **red-flag-first stepwise algorithm** rather than a survey of common causes — systemic symptoms, fever, progressive neurological deficit, trauma history, immunization status, malignancy, and prior irradiation are excluded before descending to local causes. Management is cause-directed: NSAIDs and muscle relaxants for acute uncomplicated presentations, botulinum toxin A or baclofen for neurological and spastic variants, physiotherapy across all quadrants, and coronoidectomy for refractory extra-articular restriction.

The most common iatrogenic cause in dental practice is inferior alveolar nerve block (IANB). Khalifeh 2024 documents a 59-year-old woman whose opening fell to 18 mm within 12 hours of two IANB injections and who recovered fully to 42 mm by day 50; the report catalogues six mechanisms by which IANB produces trismus — medial pterygoid penetration, hematoma, infection, myotoxicity, barbed needle injury, and volume-induced pressure — and identifies escalating jaw exercise after day 12 as the primary driver of recovery, with delayed treatment risking scar formation and chronic restriction.

## 세줄요약

논문 2편(서술적 리뷰 1·증례보고 1) 종합 — 개구장애 (Trismus)를 **진단명이 아니라 감별이 필요한 징후**로 다룬다. 정의는 절치간 거리 (Interincisal Distance) **35 mm 미만**(정상 40–60 mm). Falletti 2026은 **신경학적 vs 비신경학적 × 구조적 vs 비구조적**의 4분면 병인 분류를 제안하며, 그 가치는 **분면마다 치료 방향이 갈린다**는 데 있다 — 구조적이면 물리적 해제(수술·물리치료), 신경학적·비구조적이면 약물. 원인은 중추·말초신경 병변, 파상풍 (Tetanus), 구강하악 근긴장이상 (Oromandibular Dystonia)에서 턱관절장애, 구강안면 감염, 방사선 후 섬유화 (Post-radiation Fibrosis), 대사 장애까지 걸친다.

진단은 흔한 원인부터 훑는 방식이 아니라 **위험신호 우선 단계적 알고리즘**이다 — 전신 증상·발열·진행성 신경학적 결손·외상력·예방접종력·악성종양력·방사선 조사력을 먼저 배제한 뒤 국소 원인으로 내려간다. 치료는 원인 지향: 급성·비합병성엔 비스테로이드소염제 (NSAIDs)·근이완제, 신경학적·경직형엔 보툴리눔 독소 A형 (BoNT-A) 또는 바클로펜 (Baclofen), **물리치료는 전 분면 공통**, 불응성 관절외 제한엔 오훼돌기절제술 (Coronoidectomy).

치과 진료에서 가장 흔한 의원성 원인은 하치조신경 전달마취 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB)다. Khalifeh 2024의 59세 여성은 IANB 2회 후 **12시간 이내 개구 18 mm**로 악화되었다가 **50일째 42 mm**로 완전 회복했으며, 보고는 IANB가 개구장애를 만드는 **6가지 기전**(내측익돌근 관통·혈종·감염·근독성·갈고리형 바늘 손상·용적 압력)을 정리하고 **12일 이후 강화한 악운동 운동이 회복의 주 동인**이었음을 지목한다 — 치료 지연은 반흔 형성과 만성 제한 위험.

## Summary

Limited mouth opening is a chairside problem with an unusually wide differential and an unusually narrow default. The default is to call it TMD; the differential includes conditions that are neurological, infectious, oncological, and in the case of tetanus, immediately life-threatening. These two papers supply a structure for resisting the default and a worked example of the commonest iatrogenic cause.

Key propositions:

1. **Trismus is a sign, not a diagnosis, and it has a numeric definition.** [확인] Interincisal distance below 35 mm, against a normal 40–60 mm (Falletti 2026). Measuring and recording the number at first presentation establishes the baseline against which every subsequent judgement — worsening, response, recovery — is made.
2. **The four-quadrant classification is a treatment-routing device, not a taxonomy for its own sake.** [확인] Neurological versus non-neurological crossed with structural versus non-structural. Structural restriction needs physical release; neurological non-structural restriction needs pharmacological suppression of tone. Placing a case in a quadrant is therefore equivalent to choosing a treatment family.
3. **Diagnosis must lead with red flags rather than with prevalence.** [확인] The stepwise algorithm excludes systemic and neurological danger first — fever, systemic symptoms, progressive deficit, trauma, immunization status, malignancy, prior irradiation — before working down to local causes. This ordering exists precisely because the dangerous causes are rarer than the benign ones and would otherwise be reached last.
4. **Physiotherapy is the one modality common to every quadrant.** [확인] Whatever the cause, mobilization is part of management (Falletti 2026), and in the IANB case it was the decisive component (Khalifeh 2024).
5. **Pharmacology is quadrant-specific.** [확인] NSAIDs and muscle relaxants for acute uncomplicated presentations; BoNT-A or baclofen where the mechanism is neurological or spastic. Using the acute-case drugs for a dystonic presentation would be a quadrant error.
6. **Refractory extra-articular restriction has a surgical answer.** [확인] Coronoidectomy, reserved for cases that do not resolve with cause-directed conservative management.
7. **IANB causes trismus by at least six distinct mechanisms.** [확인] Medial pterygoid penetration, hematoma, infection, myotoxicity, barbed needle injury, and volume-induced pressure (Khalifeh 2024). Because several can coexist, management should not wait on identifying which one operated.
8. **Early, escalating jaw exercise is the intervention that changes the trajectory.** [확인, 증례] In the reported case, opening fell to 18 mm within 12 hours and returned to 42 mm by day 50, with escalation of physical therapy after day 12 named as the primary driver; delay risks scar tissue and permanent restriction. The clinical corollary is that watchful waiting is the worst available option.

## Clinical Decision Rules

1. **Measure and record interincisal distance before anything else.** Below 35 mm defines the problem; the number is the baseline for every later judgement.
2. **Run the red flags before reaching for a TMD label** — fever, systemic symptoms, progressive neurological deficit, trauma, immunization status, malignancy history, prior head and neck irradiation.
3. **Place the case in a quadrant, then pick the treatment family.** Structural restriction implies physical release; neurological non-structural restriction implies pharmacological tone reduction.
4. **After a procedure, assume IANB-related trismus is multifactorial and start jaw exercise early.** Do not wait to establish which of the six mechanisms operated; delay is what converts a recoverable restriction into a scarred one.
5. **Keep physiotherapy in the plan regardless of quadrant**, and escalate it rather than holding it steady if opening is not improving.
6. **Escalate to coronoidectomy consultation only after cause-directed conservative management has failed** in extra-articular restriction.

## Evidence Limits

- **The evidence base here is two papers, one of which is a single case.** This page maps a clinical framework; it does not carry the pooled-evidence weight of most other overviews in this wiki.
- **The four-quadrant classification is a proposal, not a validated instrument.** Falletti 2026 offers it as a novel organizing scheme; no study has tested its inter-rater reliability, diagnostic yield, or effect on outcomes.
- **The red-flag algorithm is expert-derived.** Its ordering is clinically sensible but has not been prospectively evaluated against missed-diagnosis rates.
- **All IANB numbers come from n=1.** The 18 mm nadir, the 42 mm recovery, the 50-day timeline, and the attribution of recovery to post-day-12 physiotherapy are single-case observations. They should not be quoted to patients as expected values. [미검증 as prognostic figures]
- **Treatment recommendations are not compared head-to-head.** BoNT-A versus baclofen, physiotherapy intensity, and coronoidectomy timing are described rather than trialled within these sources.
- **No Korean or Asian population data** appear in either source; whether presentation or recovery differs is unaddressed.
