## Three-line Summary

Prospective 1-year study (50 patients, 80 implants) simultaneously measuring ISQ at placement, 3, 6, and 12 months with both Osstell ISQ and Osseo 100 RFA devices.

Both devices produced equivalent readings (r>0.95), ISQ increased gradually throughout healing, and dense bone (D1–D2) yielded significantly higher ISQ than soft bone (D3–D4) at every timepoint.

Osstell and Osseo 100 are interchangeable for clinical stability monitoring; bone density is a persistent discriminator of ISQ throughout the entire healing period.

## 세줄요약

전향적 1년 연구(50명, 80개 임플란트)에서 Osstell ISQ와 Osseo 100 RFA 기기를 식립 당일·3·6·12개월에 동시 측정하여 두 기기의 일치도 및 ISQ 변화를 평가하였다.

두 기기는 동등한 수치를 나타냈으며(r>0.95), ISQ는 시간 경과에 따라 점진적으로 상승했고, 치밀골(D1–D2)이 연조직(D3–D4)보다 모든 시점에서 유의하게 높은 ISQ를 보였다.

Osstell과 Osseo 100은 임상 안정성 모니터링에서 상호 교환 가능하며, 골밀도는 전체 치유 기간에 걸쳐 ISQ의 지속적인 결정인자임이 확인되었다.

## Summary
Prospective 1-year study comparing Osstell ISQ and Osseo 100 RFA devices simultaneously in 80 implants. Result: **both devices are equivalent** (r>0.95 correlation). ISQ gradually increases through healing. Bone type (D1-D2 vs D3-D4) significantly influences ISQ at all timepoints.

## Results

| Timepoint | Osstell vs Osseo 100 | Bone D1-D2 vs D3-D4 |
|---|---|---|
| T0 (placement) | r>0.95, equivalent | D1-D2 >> D3-D4 (p<0.05) |
| T1 (3mo/loading) | Equivalent | D1-D2 >> D3-D4 (p<0.05) |
| T2 (6mo) | Equivalent | D1-D2 >> D3-D4 (p<0.05) |
| T3 (12mo) | Equivalent | D1-D2 >> D3-D4 (p<0.05) |

- ISQ trajectory: gradual increase from T0 to T3 in both bone density groups
- No device-related complications; no implant failures

## Clinical Takeaway
- **Osstell and Osseo 100 are interchangeable** — use whichever is available
- Bone density (D1/D2 vs D3/D4) is a persistent ISQ discriminator throughout healing
- ISQ monitoring over time confirms osseointegration trajectory regardless of device
