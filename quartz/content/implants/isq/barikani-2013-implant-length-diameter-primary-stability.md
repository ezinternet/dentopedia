## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (60 Nobel Biocare Replace Select TiUnit Tapered implants, 2 lengths × 3 diameters, placed into artificial bone blocks analogous to D1 and D3 bone types) measuring primary stability by resonance frequency analysis (RFA/ISQ, Osstell mentor).

D1 bone gave significantly higher ISQ than D3 (p≤0.001); implant length had no effect on ISQ in D1 bone but significantly increased ISQ in D3 bone (WP 13mm vs 10mm mean ISQ difference 9.60, p=0.005 in D3); narrow-platform (3.4mm) implants showed significantly lower ISQ than regular (4.3mm) or wide (5mm) platform implants (p≤0.007), with no difference between regular and wide platforms (p≥0.215).

Clinical implication: in low-quality (D3) bone, increasing implant length and avoiding narrow-diameter implants both meaningfully raise primary stability; in vitro artificial-bone-block design (no biological remodeling) is the key limitation.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 연구 (Nobel Biocare Replace Select TiUnit Tapered 임플란트 60개, 길이 2종×직경 3종, D1/D3 유사 인공 골블록에 식립) 후 공진주파수분석(Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA)으로 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 측정.

D1 골이 D3 골보다 ISQ 유의하게 높음(p≤0.001); 임플란트 길이는 D1 골에서는 영향 없었으나 D3 골에서는 유의하게 ISQ 증가(WP 13mm vs 10mm 평균차 9.60, D3에서 p=0.005); 협폭(Narrow Platform, NP, 3.4mm) 임플란트는 일반형(Regular Platform, RP)·광폭형(Wide Platform, WP)보다 유의하게 낮은 ISQ(p≤0.007), RP-WP 간 차이는 없음(p≥0.215).

임상적 의미: 저밀도(D3) 골에서는 임플란트 길이 증가와 협폭(NP) 임플란트 회피 모두 1차 안정성 향상에 유의미; 인공 골블록 기반 in-vitro 설계(생물학적 골 리모델링 미반영)가 핵심 한계.

## Summary

This in vitro study placed 60 Nobel Biocare Replace Select TiUnit Tapered implants (10mm or 13mm length; 3.4mm/NP, 4.3mm/RP, or 5mm/WP diameter) into artificial bone blocks analogous to dense (D1) and soft (D3) bone types, then measured primary stability via resonance frequency analysis (Osstell mentor, ISQ). D1 bone produced significantly higher ISQ than D3 bone across all implant configurations. Critically, the effect of implant length on ISQ was bone-quality-dependent: length made no significant difference in dense D1 bone, but longer (13mm) implants produced significantly higher ISQ than shorter (10mm) implants in soft D3 bone across all three diameter classes. Narrow-platform (3.4mm) implants consistently underperformed regular- and wide-platform implants on ISQ, with no significant RP-vs-WP difference. The authors conclude that in low bone quality, both increasing implant length and avoiding narrow-diameter implants are effective strategies to raise primary stability — informing immediate-loading site selection.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates a bone-quality × implant-length interaction effect on ISQ that prior single-factor studies (Bischof et al., Degidi et al.) missed by not analyzing the interaction term.
- Length matters only in soft (D3) bone — in dense (D1) bone, length is not a lever for stability.
- Diameter effect plateaus above narrow-platform: RP and WP perform equivalently: the clinically actionable threshold is "avoid NP," not "maximize diameter."

## Methodology

In vitro experimental design. 60 Nobel Biocare Replace Select TiUnit Tapered implants divided into 12 groups (n=5) crossing 2 lengths (10mm, 13mm) × 3 diameters (3.4mm NP, 4.3mm RP, 5mm WP) × 2 bone-type analog blocks (D1, D3). ISQ measured immediately after placement via Osstell mentor RFA device. Univariate ANOVA tested variable interactions; T-test with Bonferroni correction assessed bone type × length; Tukey's HSD post-hoc assessed diameter effect. α = 0.05.

## Results

| Comparison | Finding | p-value |
|---|---|---|
| D1 vs D3 bone | D1 ISQ significantly higher | p ≤ 0.001 |
| WP 13mm vs 10mm, D1 bone | ISQ higher for longer, mean diff 2.60 | p = 0.000 |
| WP 13mm vs 10mm, D3 bone | ISQ higher for longer, mean diff 9.60 | p = 0.005 |
| RP 13mm vs 10mm, D3 bone | ISQ higher for longer | p = 0.011 |
| RP 13mm vs 10mm, D1 bone | No significant difference | p = 0.785 |
| NP 13mm vs 10mm, D3 bone | ISQ higher for longer | p = 0.001 |
| NP 13mm vs 10mm, D1 bone | No significant difference | p = 0.065 |
| NP vs RP/WP (diameter) | NP significantly lower ISQ | p ≤ 0.007 |
| RP vs WP (diameter) | No significant difference | p ≥ 0.215 |

Mean ISQ (±SD) by group — D1 (dense) bone: NP/RP/WP at 10mm = 70.8±0.83 / 75.2±2.58 / 74.6±0.54; at 13mm = 69.0±1.58 / 75.6±1.81 / 77.2±0.44. D3 (soft) bone: NP/RP/WP at 10mm = 37.6±4.77 / 51.4±4.66 / 49.6±3.91; at 13mm = 49.8±1.09 / 60.4±0.89 / 59.2±4.02.
