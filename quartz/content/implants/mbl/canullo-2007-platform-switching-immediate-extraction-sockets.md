## Three-line Summary

Proof-of-concept case series (9 patients, 10 immediately placed and immediately loaded maxillary implants in extraction sockets, 18–36 month follow-up) testing platform switching (PS) at a 1.0mm per-side offset (6mm implant platform, 4mm transmucosal abutment) — the largest offset magnitude reported anywhere in this wiki's PS cluster.

Radiographic bone resorption was 0.78 ± 0.36mm (aggregate; significantly below a 1.7mm reference value, P ≤ .005); soft tissue outcomes were favorable — mean buccal margin gain 0.2mm, mean papilla height gain 0.25mm, PPD averaging 2.8mm.

Small, uncontrolled case series (no platform-matched comparator) combining immediate placement + immediate loading + PS in one intervention, so the PS-specific bone-preservation effect cannot be isolated from the other two variables; abstract-only ingest limits verification of table-level detail (e.g., mesial/distal split) reported secondhand elsewhere.

Proof-of-concept 증례군 연구(환자 9명, 발치 후 즉시 상악 임플란트 10개, 즉시부하, 추적 18–36개월) — 6mm 임플란트 플랫폼에 4mm 임시 지대주를 연결해 편측 1.0mm 오프셋의 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS)을 시험 — 이 위키 PS 클러스터 중 가장 큰 오프셋 값.

## Summary

This 2007 proof-of-concept case series by Canullo and Rasperini evaluated 10 implants (9 patients) placed immediately into fresh maxillary extraction sockets and immediately loaded with provisional crowns, using a platform-switched abutment configuration — a 6mm implant platform paired with a 4mm transmucosal abutment, i.e. a 1.0mm per-side horizontal mismatch. This is the largest offset magnitude reported in any platform-switching study held in this wiki, roughly double the 0.35–0.45mm range typical of sibling studies. Over 18–36 months of follow-up, radiographic bone resorption averaged 0.78 ± 0.36mm, significantly lower than a 1.7mm reference value (P ≤ .005), while soft tissue measures were favorable: no net recession (mean buccal margin gain 0.2mm), papilla height gain (0.25mm), and shallow pocket depths (average 2.8mm, none exceeding 3mm). The study design confounds three variables — immediate placement, immediate loading, and platform switching — in a single uncontrolled case series, so it cannot isolate how much of the tissue preservation is attributable to PS specifically. It is cited secondhand by [[implants/mbl/gupta-2019-platform-switching-crestal-bone-loss-sr]]'s comparative table with a mesial/distal bone-loss split (0.57mm / 1.01mm) not verifiable from the abstract alone, since no PMC full text was available for this article.

## Key Contributions
- Provides an independently-sourced upper-bound data point on the platform-switching offset-magnitude question: 1.0mm per-side, roughly double the 0.45mm threshold Hürzeler et al. 2007 proposed as "sufficient."
- Demonstrates that even at this larger offset, bone resorption (0.78mm aggregate) stayed well below the 1.7mm reference value used for comparison, with favorable soft-tissue (papilla, buccal margin) outcomes.
- One of the earliest studies to combine immediate placement + immediate loading + platform switching in the same protocol, rather than testing PS in isolation on healed sites.

## Methodology
Prospective, uncontrolled case series (proof-of-concept design; no platform-matched comparator arm). 9 patients, 10 implant sites, maxillae without compromised bone. Hopeless teeth underwent periodontal infection-control therapy before extraction; implants (6mm platform) placed immediately into fresh sockets with a 4mm provisional transmucosal abutment and immediate non-functional provisional crown. Definitive prosthesis at 3 months. Radiographic (mesial/distal crestal bone height via image-analysis software), PPD, recession, and papilla-height measurements taken at prosthesis insertion (baseline) and every 6 months; mean follow-up 22 months (range 18–36).

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Per-side horizontal offset | 1.0mm (6mm implant platform, 4mm abutment) |
| Bone resorption (aggregate) | 0.78 ± 0.36mm (vs 1.7mm reference, P ≤ .005) |
| PPD | Average 2.8mm; none exceeding 3mm |
| Buccal margin | Mean gain 0.2mm (no net recession) |
| Papilla height | Mean gain 0.25mm |
| Osseointegration | 10/10 implants clinically osseointegrated |
| Mesial/distal bone-loss split | Not available from abstract (secondhand only, via Gupta 2019: M 0.57mm / D 1.01mm — unverified here) |
