## Three-line Summary

Meta-analysis of 5 prospective studies (525 implants) evaluated marginal bone level (변연골 수준, MBL) changes at 1 year after implant loading across diverse loading protocols.

Pooled mean marginal bone loss (변연골 소실, MBL) was 0.56 mm (Z=7.48, p=0.01); platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭) implants showed substantially less MBL (0.01–0.95 mm) compared to conventional controls (~1.67 mm); significant heterogeneity was present (I²=99.8%).

The 0.56 mm pooled 1-year MBL falls within Albrektsson's accepted success threshold (<1.5 mm), but extreme heterogeneity (I²=99.8%) severely limits the generalizability of this estimate.

## Summary

Kumar et al. (2021) conducted a systematic review and meta-analysis of 5 prospective studies (525 implants) examining marginal bone level (변연골 수준, MBL) changes at dental implants over a 1-year post-loading follow-up. MEDLINE was searched from 1980 to 2018. A random-effects model was applied.

The pooled mean MBL was **0.56 mm** at 1 year (Z=7.48, p=0.01). Significant heterogeneity was found across studies (I²=99.8%, tau=0.03, Cochran Q p=0.041). The authors noted that most marginal bone resorption occurs during the first year after loading. Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭) was highlighted as reducing MBL substantially (0.01–0.95 mm vs ~1.67 mm with conventional connection designs).

The Albrektsson success criteria — <1.5 mm bone loss in the first year, <0.2 mm/year thereafter — serve as the reference threshold. The pooled 0.56 mm estimate falls within this range, suggesting that across a broad range of implant systems and loading approaches, 1-year MBL remains clinically acceptable. However, the extreme heterogeneity (I²=99.8%) means the pooled estimate must be interpreted with caution.

## Key Contributions

- Pooled 1-year MBL benchmark: **0.56 mm** across diverse implant systems and loading protocols.
- Quantified platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭) benefit: 0.01–0.95 mm MBL for PS implants vs ~1.67 mm for conventional.
- Established that 1-year MBL within Albrektsson criteria is achievable broadly, not only in controlled settings.
- Highlighted the persistent problem of heterogeneity in MBL reporting, calling for standardized methodology.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (체계적 고찰 + 메타분석).
- **Database**: MEDLINE, 1980–2018.
- **Inclusion**: RCT or prospective design; ≥1-year follow-up; MBL data reported.
- **Studies included**: 5 prospective studies; n=525 implants.
- **Statistical model**: Random-effects meta-analysis; Z-test; heterogeneity quantified by I², tau², Cochran Q.
