## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Nanomaterials 2020) of cold atmospheric plasma (CAP) in implantology, consolidating the dual clinical rationale: surface activation for osseointegration and decontamination for peri-implantitis management.

CAP improves surface wettability, removes hydrocarbon contamination (biological aging), enhances osteoblast adhesion and preclinical osseointegration, and exerts antimicrobial activity against peri-implantitis-associated pathogens — proposed for chairside use immediately before implant placement.

Evidence at time of publication was predominantly preclinical; clinical data were limited and no standard protocol was established, making this a framing review rather than a practice-change document.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 서술적 리뷰(Nanomaterials 2020): 임플란트학에서 CAP의 이중 임상 근거 통합 — 골유착을 위한 표면 활성화와 임플란트주위염 관리를 위한 제염.

줄2: CAP는 젖음성 개선·탄화수소 오염(생물학적 노화) 제거·골모세포 부착·전임상 골유착 향상·임플란트주위염 병원균 항균 → 식립 직전 chairside 사용 제안.

줄3: 발표 당시 근거는 대부분 전임상이며 임상 데이터 미흡·표준 프로토콜 미확립 — 실무 변화 문서보다 개념 정립 리뷰 성격.

## Summary

Lays out CAP's dual clinical rationale that organizes this whole cluster: surface activation (→ osseointegration) and decontamination (→ peri-implantitis). Its key practical claim is chairside use — activating an implant surface in the operatory immediately before placement to reverse hydrocarbon "biological aging."

## Key Contributions

- Consolidates the surface-activation vs decontamination dual rationale.
- Emphasizes chairside, low-temperature applicability.

## Methodology

- Narrative literature review.

## Results

- Improves wettability; removes hydrocarbon contamination.
- Enhances osteoblast adhesion and osseointegration (preclinical).
- Antimicrobial against peri-implantitis-associated pathogens.
- Narrative — no pooled estimates.

**Boundary (living-document):** narrative; clinical data limited.
