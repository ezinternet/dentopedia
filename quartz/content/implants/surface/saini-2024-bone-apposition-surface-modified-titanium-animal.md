## Three-line Summary
SR+MA (PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD42020204907) of 37 preclinical animal studies (1891 implants, 513 animals — rabbits, dogs, pigs, sheep) comparing machined (turned) vs surface-modified titanium implants in healthy healed sites, 20 of which entered meta-analysis (BIC%, RTV, ISQ) for follow-up 1–12 weeks.

Pooled BIC% was significantly higher than machined for SLA (SMD 15.62, 95% CI 10.32–20.91, p<0.01), laser-modified (SMD 15.64, p<0.01), acid-etched (SMD 13.51, p<0.01) and anodized (SMD 14.23, p<0.01) surfaces, but NOT for sandblasted-alone (SMD 9.44, p=0.65); for RTV only laser-modified reached significance (SMD 15.41, p<0.01), and no subgroup reached significance for ISQ.

Clinically, subtractive and additive surface modification generally promote greater early-stage bone apposition than machined surfaces, but the effect is heterogeneous by surface type and outcome measure — plain sandblasting alone did not separate from machined on BIC or RTV in this animal-model evidence, unlike combined SLA/laser/acid-etch/anodized treatments.

## 세줄요약
PRISMA/PROSPERO(CRD42020204907) 기반 SR+MA — 동물실험 37편(임플란트 1891개, 동물 513마리: 토끼·개·돼지·양), 이 중 20편이 메타분석(골-임플란트 접촉률(Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)%·제거토크값(Removal Torque Value, RTV)·임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ), 추적 1–12주)에 포함, 머신드(기계가공) 표면 vs 표면처리 티타늄 임플란트 비교.

풀링된 BIC%는 샌드블라스팅+산부식(Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched, SLA, SMD 15.62, p<0.01)·레이저처리(SMD 15.64, p<0.01)·산부식(SMD 13.51, p<0.01)·양극산화(Anodized, SMD 14.23, p<0.01)에서 머신드 대비 유의하게 높았으나, 단독 모래분사(Sandblasted alone)만으로는 유의차 없음(SMD 9.44, p=0.65); RTV는 레이저처리만 유의(p<0.01), ISQ는 모든 하위군에서 유의차 없음.

임상적으로 감산(subtractive)·부가(additive) 표면처리는 대체로 머신드보다 초기 골형성을 촉진하나 표면 종류·결과지표별 이질성이 큼 — 단독 모래분사만으로는 이 동물모델 근거에서 BIC·RTV 이점이 뚜렷하지 않아, SLA·레이저·산부식·양극산화 복합처리와는 대비된다.

## Summary
This systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA-compliant, PROSPERO CRD42020204907) synthesized 37 preclinical animal studies (2000–2020; 1891 implants in 513 healthy animals — rabbits/guinea pigs most common, then dogs, pigs, foxhounds, sheep) comparing machined (turned/smooth) titanium implants against surface-modified implants (subtractive: sandblasted, acid-etched, SLA, laser-modified, anodized; additive: HA, calcium-phosphate, collagen, and other coatings) for bone apposition. Twenty studies with ≥2 per subgroup entered random-effects meta-analysis for BIC%, RTV, and ISQ. Surface-modified implants generally showed significantly higher BIC% than machined surfaces (SLA, laser-modified, acid-etched, anodized subgroups all p<0.01), but sandblasted-alone did not reach significance for BIC or RTV, and no surface subgroup reached significance for ISQ. Quality appraisal used the SYRCLE risk-of-bias tool and modified ARRIVE guidelines; heterogeneity was considerable (I²=74–94%) in most significant BIC subgroups. The authors conclude both subtractive and additive surface modifications generally promote greater early osseointegration than machined surfaces, while flagging substantial cross-study heterogeneity from animal species, implant site, and follow-up duration.

## Key Contributions
- First SR+MA restricted to healthy-animal-model preclinical evidence (2000–2020) on surface-modified vs machined titanium implant bone apposition, pooling 37 studies (20 meta-analyzed, 1891 implants/513 animals)
- Subgroup-resolved BIC%, RTV, and ISQ effect sizes by specific surface-modification type, rather than a single pooled "any modification vs machined" estimate
- Identifies that plain sandblasting alone does not separate from machined surfaces on BIC% (p=0.65) or RTV (p=0.51) in animal-model histomorphometry, distinguishing it from SLA/laser/acid-etch/anodized surfaces which do
- Dual quality appraisal (SYRCLE RoB + modified ARRIVE) across all 37 included animal studies

## Methodology
PRISMA-compliant SR+MA; PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, EMBASE searched 2001–2020 plus manual search (2720 records → 37 included qualitative synthesis → 20 meta-analyzed). Outcomes: BIC% (28 studies), RTV/N-cm (14 studies), ISQ (10 studies). Random-effects model, SMD with 95% CI (R Metapackage), I²/Q-statistic heterogeneity, Begg's test for publication bias.

## Results

### BIC% (bone-to-implant contact) vs machined
| Surface | Studies | SMD | 95% CI | I² | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SLA (sandblasted+acid-etched) | 8 | 15.62 | 10.32–20.91 | 89% | <0.01 |
| Laser-modified | 4 | 15.64 | 7.64–23.51 | 89% | <0.01 |
| Acid-etched | 4 | 13.51 | 6.24–20.78 | 74% | <0.01 |
| Anodized | 4 | 14.23 | 2.98–25.49 | 94% | <0.01 |
| Sandblasted (alone) | 2 | 9.44 | 6.16–12.72 | 0% | 0.65 (NS) |

### Removal Torque Value (RTV, N-cm) vs machined
| Surface | Studies | SMD | 95% CI | I² | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sandblasted (alone) | 2 | 6.95 | 3.25–10.65 | 0% | 0.51 (NS) |
| Acid-etched | 2 | 20.36 | 14.60–26.12 | 0% | 0.95 (NS) |
| SLA | 2 | 9.79 | 7.72–11.86 | 0% | 0.53 (NS) |
| Laser-modified | 5 | 15.41 | 9.31–21.51 | 82% | <0.01 |

### ISQ vs machined
| Surface | Studies | SMD | 95% CI | I² | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SLA (vs turned) | 3 | 1.66 | 0.92–2.39 | 0% | 0.81 (NS) |
| Sandblasted (alone) | 2 | 0.88 | −0.26–2.02 | 0% | 0.65 (NS) |
| Anodized | 2 | 1.98 | 0.85–3.11 | 0% | 0.42 (NS) |

Additive-coating findings were descriptive only (heterogeneity + <2 studies/coating type precluded pooling): e.g. HA-coating raised BIC% in most studies (Faeda 2012: 73% vs 46.3% machined; Zechner 2003: 39% vs 19.3%) but not in London 2002 (22.5% vs 31.0%). Time-window sensitivity was notable — e.g. SLA lost significance vs machined after >12 weeks follow-up.
