## Three-line Summary
Prospective multicenter case series from three Chinese centers followed 40 subjects (92 implants; OsseoSpeed TX, fluoride-modified nanostructure surface) with early loading (6–8 weeks) of splinted screw-retained fixed prostheses in the posterior mandible over 36 months.
Implant survival was 100% at 3 years, and mean marginal bone level changed by +0.23 ± 0.48 mm (gain, p=0.00061 vs. loading baseline), with statistically significant bone gain confirmed at 12, 24, and 36 months.
Early loading of splinted fluoride-modified implants in posterior mandible appears durable at 3 years, but the absence of a control group and single-brand restriction limit generalizability beyond this specific system.

## One-line Summary
Prospective multicenter (n=40 subjects, 92 implants, 3-year): 100% survival; mean MBL gain of +0.23 mm at 36 months confirming long-term safety of early loading protocol for fluoride-modified splinted implants in posterior mandible.

## Summary
This 3-year prospective multicenter follow-up enrolled 45 subjects (107 implants) and evaluated 40 (92 implants) at 36 months. Using OsseoSpeed TX implants (fluoride-modified nanostructure surface) with an early loading protocol (6–8 weeks post-surgery), 100% survival was achieved. At subject level, mean marginal bone change was +0.23 ± 0.48 mm (bone gain, p=0.00061), with statistically significant bone changes at 12, 24, and 36 months compared to loading baseline. The study extends the 1-year Zhou 2016 data to confirm durable bone gain with splinted implants in posterior mandible under controlled occlusal load.

## Key Contributions
- 3-year durability of early loading protocol for splinted posterior mandibular implants
- Marginal bone gain (not loss) at all three timepoints — unique finding possibly related to favorable surface and splinting load distribution
- Multicenter design across three Chinese centers reduces single-center bias
- Fluoride-modified nanostructure surface (OsseoSpeed TX) appears to support stable long-term osseointegration

## Methodology
Prospective multicenter (3 Chinese centers), no control arm. n=45 enrolled (40 completed 36 months, 92 implants). OsseoSpeed TX implants (Dentsply), 2–3 implants per edentulous region (Kennedy Class I or II mandible), one-stage. Prosthesis (screw-retained splinted fixed) at 6–8 weeks. MBL measured at 6, 12, 24, 36 months. Statistical unit: both subject and implant level.

## Results
- Survival: 100% (92/92 at 36 months)
- MBL (subject level, 36 months): +0.23 ± 0.48 mm (p=0.00061 vs. loading)
- MBL (implant level): significant at 12 (p=0.039), 24 (p=0.015), 36 months (p<0.001)
- Soft tissue: healthy throughout; no significant probing depth changes
