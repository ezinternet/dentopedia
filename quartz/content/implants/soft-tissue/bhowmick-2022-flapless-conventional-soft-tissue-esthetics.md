## Three-line Summary

Line 1: Prospective split-mouth study (10 patients, 20 Osstem SSIII titanium collar implants) comparing soft tissue health indices and esthetic outcomes between flapless and conventional open-flap implant placement over ~12 months.
Line 2: Probing depth significantly lower in flapless group at 3 months (p<0.05); VAS esthetic score higher (7.62 vs 6.85, p=0.05); GI, PI, and mSBI showed no significant between-group differences.
Line 3: Flapless technique offers better soft tissue adaptation and esthetic outcomes; however, the small sample (n=10) and short follow-up limit clinical inference.

## Summary

This prospective split-mouth study enrolled 10 partially edentulous patients receiving 20 two-piece one-stage titanium collar implants (Osstem SSIII). Each patient received one implant placed via flapless technique and one via conventional open-flap surgery on contralateral sides. Peri-implant soft tissue outcomes (GI, PI, mSBI, probing depth) and esthetics (VAS) were recorded at 6 weeks, 3 months, and 1 and 3 months post-prosthesis delivery.

The flapless group demonstrated significantly lower probing depth at 3 months (p<0.05) and a higher VAS esthetic score (7.62 vs 6.85, p=0.05), with better soft tissue contour and emergence profile (출현 윤곽). Conventional inflammatory indices (GI, PI, mSBI) showed no significant between-group differences, indicating equivalent soft tissue health by standard periodontal measures.

The results suggest flapless implant placement preserves the gingival architecture more effectively, reduces trauma, and improves patient-perceived esthetics in the short term. Both surgical approaches produced acceptable peri-implant soft tissue health.

## Key Contributions

- Provides split-mouth level evidence isolating the effect of surgical access (flapless vs open flap) on soft tissue esthetics with titanium collar implants
- Quantifies esthetic advantage using VAS scores — a patient-reported outcome rarely reported alongside clinical indices in this specific comparison
- Confirms conventional peri-implant health indices (GI, PI, mSBI) are not significantly different between approaches, directing attention to esthetic and geometric soft tissue differences as the distinguishing outcomes

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective split-mouth (within-subject control)
- **n**: 10 patients, 20 implants (Osstem SSIII, two-piece, one-stage)
- **Implant**: Titanium collar (transmucosal collar) design
- **Groups**: Flapless (test) vs conventional open-flap (control), one per patient on contralateral sides
- **Outcomes**: GI, PI, mSBI, probing depth, VAS esthetic score, soft tissue contour and emergence profile
- **Follow-up**: 6 weeks, 3 months, 1 month post-prosthesis, 3 months post-prosthesis (~12 months total)
- **Setting**: Multi-center, India
