## Three-line Summary

Animal (mouse) study: partial infraorbital nerve transection (pIONT) trigeminal neuropathic pain model plus cross-species (human/mouse) single-nucleus RNA-seq of trigeminal ganglion (TG) cell atlases and in vitro mechanistic assays.

CBLN2 is a marker gene predominantly expressed in Aδ-LTMR (and, in mice, also Aβ/cLTMR) neurons of the trigeminal ganglion; after nerve injury, TET3-mediated promoter demethylation drives sustained CBLN2 upregulation, and CBLN2 knockdown alleviates both initiation and maintenance of mechanical allodynia, while exogenous CBLN2 drives ERK-dependent neuronal hyperexcitability and proinflammatory cytokine induction.

Clinical implication: identifies a novel TET3-CBLN2-ERK epigenetic axis as a candidate peripheral-sensitization drug target for trigeminal neuropathic pain, but the evidence is confined to a rodent model with unconfirmed human translational relevance (cross-species divergence in CBLN2 fiber-type distribution).

## 세줄요약

동물(마우스) 연구: 삼차신경병증통증(Trigeminal Neuropathic Pain, TNP) 부분 안와하신경 절단(pIONT) 모델 및 사람·마우스 교차종 단일세포핵 RNA-seq(snRNA-seq) 삼차신경절(Trigeminal Ganglion, TG) 세포지도 분석·시험관내 기전 실험.

세레벨린 2 (Cerebellin 2, CBLN2)는 삼차신경절 내 Aδ 저역치 기계수용체(Aδ-LTMR) 우세 발현 마커 유전자(마우스에서는 Aβ/cLTMR에도 발현)이며, 신경손상 후 텐-일레븐 전좌효소 3 (Ten-Eleven Translocation 3, TET3) 매개 프로모터 탈메틸화가 CBLN2의 지속적 상승을 유도, CBLN2 넉다운은 기계적 이질통(Mechanical Allodynia)의 개시·유지 단계 모두를 완화시키며, 외인성 CBLN2는 ERK 의존적 뉴런 과흥분성·염증성 사이토카인 발현을 유도.

임상적 의미: 삼차신경병증통증 말초감작에 대한 새로운 TET3-CBLN2-ERK 후성유전 축을 치료표적 후보로 제시하나, 근거는 설치류 모델에 국한되며 사람으로의 번역 가능성은 미확인(CBLN2의 섬유형 분포에 종간 차이 존재).

## Summary

This paper answers a question the wiki's existing peripheral trigeminal-nerve-injury cascade could not: which molecular marker sits on the Aδ/Aβ/C-fiber LTMRs that [[orofacial-pain/korczeniewska-2022-pathophysiology-post-traumatic-trigeminal-neuropathic]] describes as undergoing ectopic firing and phenotypic switching after trigeminal nerve injury? Using cross-species single-nucleus RNA-seq of trigeminal ganglion (TG) cell atlases, the authors identify cerebellin 2 (CBLN2) — previously studied only as a CNS synaptic organizer (cerebellum, hippocampus, spinal dorsal horn, central amygdala) — as a cell-type marker gene predominantly expressed in Aδ-LTMR neurons in both human and mouse TG, with broader expression extending into Aβ-LTMR and c-fiber LTMR populations in mice specifically.

In a mouse partial infraorbital nerve transection (pIONT) model of trigeminal neuropathic pain (TNP), CBLN2 mRNA and protein rise rapidly after injury (day 1) and stay elevated through day 21, tracking the time course of established mechanical allodynia. The mechanistic novelty is upstream: this rise is driven by TET3-mediated active demethylation of the Cbln2 promoter's CpG island, demonstrated with methylation-specific PCR and bisulfite sequencing, and confirmed causal by Tet3 knockdown (which both lowers Cbln2 expression and relieves allodynia). This is, functionally, an epigenetic layer sitting on top of the ion-channel and chemokine cascade already documented in the wiki's peripheral-sensitization literature — a different evidence class from [[orofacial-pain/ke-2025-trpc4-trigeminal-neuropathic-pain-mice]]'s TRPC4 (a channel-level, not transcriptional-level, mechanism), but methodologically similar in offering true bidirectional causal evidence: siRNA knockdown of Cbln2 relieves allodynia in both the initiation phase (concurrent with pIONT) and the already-established maintenance phase (day-7 delayed knockdown), while exogenous recombinant CBLN2 (rCBLN2) injected into naive TG reproduces facial mechanical allodynia and directly increases TG neuron excitability (lower rheobase, higher AP firing frequency).

Downstream, CBLN2 acts through ERK/MAPK signaling: rCBLN2 rapidly (within 15 minutes) activates ERK phosphorylation in TG neurons, and the MEK inhibitor PD98059 blocks both the electrophysiological hyperexcitability and a broad panel of CBLN2-induced pro-inflammatory chemokines/cytokines (Cxcl1, Cxcr2, Cxcl9, Cxcl10, Il-6), reducing cytokine induction by 21–42%. This closes a TET3 → CBLN2 → ERK → neuroinflammation loop entirely within the trigeminal ganglion, with no central (spinal trigeminal nucleus) component examined — consistent with the wiki's existing peripheral/central literature split.

The key caveat for extrapolating this to humans is a cross-species divergence the authors flag explicitly: human CBLN2 is more narrowly restricted to Aδ-LTMR (NF3, 56.3%) than mouse CBLN2, which shows substantial additional expression in Aβ-LTMR (NF2, 48.0%) and c-fiber LTMR (39.1%) populations. This means murine Cbln2-knockout phenotypes involving Aβ/cLTMR pathways may not translate directly to human Aδ-predominant biology, and the authors recommend prioritizing Aδ-LTMR-selective validation for any human-directed CBLN2 therapeutic.

## Key Contributions

1. First identification of a cerebellin family member (CBLN2) expressed in a peripheral sensory ganglion (trigeminal ganglion), extending cerebellin biology beyond its established CNS roles.
2. Cross-species TG cell atlas mapping links CBLN2 specifically to Aδ-LTMR (human) and Aδ/Aβ/cLTMR (mouse) — the same fiber classes implicated in the peripheral sensitization cascade of [[orofacial-pain/korczeniewska-2022-pathophysiology-post-traumatic-trigeminal-neuropathic]].
3. Establishes a causal TET3-mediated promoter demethylation mechanism for CBLN2 upregulation after nerve injury (MSP/BSP + Tet3 knockdown).
4. Demonstrates CBLN2 is functionally necessary for both initiation and maintenance phases of neuropathic mechanical allodynia (bidirectional causal evidence: knockdown relieves, exogenous protein induces).
5. Identifies the downstream ERK/MAPK pathway as the effector linking CBLN2 to neuronal hyperexcitability and a proinflammatory cytokine cascade (Cxcl1/9/10, Cxcr2, Il-6), pharmacologically reversible with a MEK inhibitor.

## Methodology

- Mouse (ICR, male, 6–8 weeks) partial infraorbital nerve transection (pIONT) TNP model vs sham.
- Cross-species (human + mouse) single-nucleus RNA-seq (snRNA-seq) analysis of TG cell atlases resolving 15 transcriptional clusters (NF1/NF2/NF3/cLTMR, etc.); comparison against inflammatory-soup (IS) and cortical-spreading-depression (CSD) migraine models.
- Von Frey filament mechanical allodynia testing (blinded); qRT-PCR, Western blot, ELISA, immunofluorescence for CBLN2/pERK.
- Methylation-specific PCR (MSP) and bisulfite sequencing PCR (BSP) of the Cbln2 promoter CpG island; parallel Tet1/2/3 expression profiling.
- Intra-TG siRNA knockdown of Cbln2 and Tet3 (concurrent-with-injury and delayed/established-pain timepoints); intra-TG recombinant CBLN2 (rCBLN2) gain-of-function injection.
- Whole-cell current-clamp electrophysiology on acutely isolated TG neurons; MEK inhibitor (PD98059) pathway-dissection experiments; key findings replicated in female mice.

## Results

- CBLN2 marks Aδ-LTMR in human (NF3, 56.3% expressing) and mouse (~70% of Aδ-LTMR), with additional Aβ-LTMR (48.0%) and cLTMR (39.1%) expression in mice specifically.
- Cbln2 mRNA rises 2.6–3.6-fold in TG by day 1–10 post-pIONT (p<0.001) and remains elevated (2.0-fold) at day 21; protein confirmed elevated by Western blot and ELISA.
- Cbln2 promoter shows significantly reduced CpG methylation post-pIONT (MSP p<0.05, BSP p<0.01); of Tet1/Tet2/Tet3, only Tet3 is significantly upregulated post-pIONT.
- Cbln2 siRNA knockdown (both concurrent-with-injury and delayed day-7) significantly relieves mechanical allodynia; Tet3 knockdown (day 10) relieves allodynia and reduces both Tet3 and Cbln2 expression (p<0.01–0.001), confirming the causal TET3→CBLN2 link.
- Exogenous rCBLN2 induces facial mechanical allodynia, reduces neuronal rheobase, and increases AP firing frequency; activates ERK phosphorylation within 15 minutes in vitro and in vivo.
- MEK inhibitor PD98059 blocks CBLN2-induced hyperexcitability and reduces CBLN2-induced proinflammatory cytokine/chemokine induction (Cxcl1, Cxcr2, Cxcl9, Cxcl10, Il-6; 2.4–358-fold induction by rCBLN2 alone) by 21–42%.
- Core CBLN2/ERK/allodynia findings replicated in female mice with no significant sex difference reported.
