## Three-line Summary

Radiographic caries detection has high specificity (0.87–0.97) but poor sensitivity for initial enamel lesions (Se ~0.24–0.42), improving for dentin lesions (Se 0.36–0.56), and only reaching acceptable sensitivity (>0.60) for cavitated proximal lesions. This SR+MA of 117 studies (13,375 teeth, 19,108 surfaces) confirms that bitewing/periapical radiography reliably detects caries only after lesions are cavitated or well into dentin. In high-caries-risk populations, supplementary non-radiographic detection methods are indicated for initial lesion management.

## 세줄요약

방사선 사진을 이용한 우식 검출은 특이도 (specificity)가 0.87–0.97로 높지만, 초기 우식 (initial caries) 및 법랑질 우식의 민감도 (sensitivity)는 0.24–0.42에 불과하여 대부분을 놓친다. 상아질 우식 (dentin caries)은 민감도 0.36–0.56으로 개선되지만 여전히 불충분하며, 와동형성 인접면 우식 (cavitated proximal caries)에서 비로소 >0.60으로 임상적 신뢰성을 확보한다. 117개 연구를 포괄한 이 메타분석은 "방사선 검출은 우식이 상당히 진행된 후에야 신뢰할 수 있다"는 임상 원칙에 대한 최대 규모의 정량적 근거를 제공한다.

## Summary

Schwendicke et al. (2015) conducted the largest systematic review and meta-analysis of radiographic caries detection accuracy, pooling 117 studies comprising 13,375 teeth and 19,108 surfaces. The study stratified pooled accuracy estimates by both lesion location (occlusal vs. proximal) and lesion depth (initial/enamel, dentin, cavitated) to provide clinically specific guidance.

The core finding: **specificity is consistently high across all categories, but sensitivity scales dramatically with lesion depth.** Enamel/initial lesions are largely invisible to radiography (Se 0.24–0.42); dentin lesions are partially detected (Se 0.36–0.56); cavitated proximal lesions reach clinically acceptable detection (Se >0.60). This quantifies the long-taught clinical principle that radiographs detect caries late.

Importantly, risk of bias was high in most included studies (QUADAS-2 assessment), which tempers confidence in the exact point estimates. Nevertheless, the direction and ordering of findings are robust and consistent with the clinical literature.

## Key Contributions

1. **Scale**: 117 studies, 13,375 teeth, 19,108 surfaces — largest radiographic caries SR+MA at time of publication
2. **Stratified accuracy**: First SR+MA to systematically separate sensitivity/specificity by both location AND depth simultaneously
3. **Quantified detection threshold**: Provides the specific lesion stage (cavitated proximal) at which radiography becomes reliably useful (Se >0.60, Sp >0.90)
4. **Clinical guidance**: Directly supports supplementary non-radiographic methods for initial/enamel lesion detection in high-risk patients
5. **Evidence quality assessment**: QUADAS-2 applied — most studies high risk of bias, reducing certainty of estimates

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Systematic review + random-effects meta-analysis |
| Databases | Medline, Embase, Cochrane Central, grey literature |
| Records screened | 947 identified; 442 full-text reviewed; 117 included |
| Primary outcome | Sensitivity and specificity of radiographic caries detection |
| Summary statistics | Pooled sensitivity, specificity, diagnostic odds ratios (DORs) |
| Risk of bias tool | QUADAS-2 |
| Stratification | Location (proximal vs. occlusal) × Depth (initial, dentin, cavitated) |
| Teeth included | Mainly permanent; primary dentition underrepresented |

## Results

### Pooled Detection Accuracy by Depth and Location

| Lesion depth | Location | Pooled sensitivity | Pooled specificity | DOR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Initial (enamel) | Proximal | ~0.24 | 0.70–0.84 | Low |
| Initial (enamel) | Occlusal | ~0.42 | ~0.97 | Low |
| Dentin | Proximal | 0.36 (95% CI 0.24–0.49) | 0.87–0.89 | Moderate |
| Dentin | Occlusal | 0.56 (95% CI 0.53–0.59) | 0.87–0.95 | Moderate |
| Cavitated | Proximal | >0.60 | >0.90 | High |
| Cavitated | Occlusal | No studies available | — | — |

### Clinical Interpretation by Stage

| Stage | Detection rate | Clinical implication |
|---|---|---|
| Enamel/initial proximal | ~24% | ~76% of lesions missed — radiograph unreliable for primary detection |
| Dentin proximal | ~36–49% | Still suboptimal — significant lesion burden missed |
| Cavitated proximal | >60% | Radiography reliable — cavity already formed |
| All stages (specificity) | 0.87–0.97 | Positive radiographic finding = very likely true caries; low false-positive rate |

### Key Quantitative Thresholds

- **Se 0.24**: Lower bound for proximal enamel detection — the worst-performing category
- **Se >0.60**: First stage where radiographic detection can be considered clinically adequate (cavitated proximal)
- **Sp 0.97**: Upper bound (occlusal initial) — radiograph nearly never false-positive for caries
- **DOR shift**: Low (enamel) → Moderate (dentin) → High (cavitated) — lesion depth is the primary driver of diagnostic accuracy
