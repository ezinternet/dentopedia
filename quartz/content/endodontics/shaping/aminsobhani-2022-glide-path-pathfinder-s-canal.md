## Three-line Summary
In vitro pre/post photo superimposition study (n=100 S-shaped resin blocks, 5 groups × 20) comparing canal transportation at 10 cross-section levels among five glide-path systems: Scout RaCe, One G, PathFile, GPS rotary, and manual K-file as control.

Manual K-file produced significantly greater transportation in the apical and middle thirds versus all rotary systems (p<0.001), and in the coronal third vs Scout RaCe (p<0.05); the four rotary systems were statistically equivalent to each other across all thirds.

The homogeneity across four modern rotary systems and the consistent manual K-file inferiority reinforce using rotary glide-path instrumentation in complex anatomy, extending the natural-dentin findings of Aflaki 2020.

## 세줄요약
5가지 글라이드패스 기구(Scout RaCe, One G, PathFile, GPS 회전형, 수기 K-file 대조군) 비교 in vitro 연구 — S형 레진 블록 100개(5군 × 20개) 전후 사진 중첩(Photoshop), 10개 단면 이동량 측정(Digimizer).

수기 K-file은 치근단·중간 1/3에서 모든 회전형 대비 유의하게 큰 근관 이동 유발(p<0.001); 치관 1/3에서도 Scout RaCe보다 큼(p<0.05); 4종 회전형 기구 간에는 통계적 유의차 없음.

4가지 현대 회전형 글라이드패스 시스템의 동질성 및 수기 K-file 열세는 복잡한 근관 형태에서 회전형 기구 선택의 근거 — Aflaki 2020의 자연 상아질 결론을 더 넓은 기구 비교로 보강.

## Summary
Five-arm comparison of glide-path systems in S-shaped blocks: four rotary NiTi systems behaved equivalently in producing canal transportation, while manual stainless-steel K-files caused significantly more deviation, particularly in the apical and middle thirds. Reinforces the rotary-over-manual conclusion of Aflaki 2020 with a broader system comparison.

## Key Contributions
- Five-system head-to-head transportation comparison
- Confirms manual K-file inferiority across all canal thirds
- Demonstrates homogeneity among four current rotary glide-path systems

## Methodology
- Design: in vitro
- Sample: 100 S-shaped resin blocks (5 × 20)
- Groups: Scout RaCe, One G, PathFile, GPS, K-file (control)
- Procedure: pre/post photo superimposition (Photoshop CC 2019); 10 cross-section transportation measurements (Digimizer)
- Statistics: one-way ANOVA, repeated-measures ANOVA; α=0.05

## Results
- Manual K-file: significantly higher transportation in apical and middle thirds (p<0.001)
- Coronal third: K-file > Scout RaCe (p<0.05)
- Four rotary systems: not statistically different from each other

## Limitations
- Resin substrate (not dentin)
- Single operator
- No biological or time-cost outcome
