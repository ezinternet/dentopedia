## Three-line Summary
Umbrella review synthesizing 4 descriptive systematic reviews and 9 meta-analyses on irrigation protocol effectiveness in endodontic therapy — the highest-level synthesis currently available for this topic, with 8 of 13 included articles rated high methodological quality and low primary-study overlap between meta-analyses confirming independent evidence.

Passive ultrasonic irrigation (PUI) emerged as the most evidence-supported technique, with significantly better outcomes vs conventional irrigation for both microbial reduction (RD −0.15, 95% CI −0.28 to −0.01, p=0.03, I²=60%) and apical healing (RD −0.09, 95% CI −0.16 to −0.02, p=0.01, I²=0%).

High heterogeneity in primary studies (irrigant type, concentration, volume, activation parameters) and sparse long-term outcome data limit the strength of recommendations; results confirm and extend the Kumar 2023 SR+MA at a higher synthesis level.

## 세줄요약
근관치료 세정 프로토콜 효과를 평가한 SR 4편+MA 9편의 우산 리뷰 — 현재 이 주제에서 가장 높은 수준의 합성 근거(포함 논문 13편 중 8편 고방법론 품질, MA 간 1차 연구 중복 낮음으로 독립 근거 확인).

수동초음파세정(PUI)이 기존 세정 대비 미생물 감소(RD −0.15, 95% CI −0.28~−0.01, p=0.03)와 치근단 치유(RD −0.09, 95% CI −0.16~−0.02, p=0.01) 양쪽에서 가장 강한 근거로 뒷받침.

1차 연구 간 높은 이질성(세정제 종류·농도·용량·활성 파라미터)과 장기 결과 데이터 부족이 한계 — Kumar 2023 SR+MA를 더 높은 합성 수준에서 확인.

## Summary
Umbrella review (UR) synthesizing 4 descriptive SRs and 9 meta-analyses on irrigation protocol effectiveness in endodontic therapy. PUI (passive ultrasonic irrigation) emerges as the most evidence-supported technique for both microbial reduction and apical healing. High heterogeneity across irrigant protocols observed throughout the evidence base.

## Key Quantitative Findings (PUI vs Conventional Irrigation)

| Outcome | Risk Difference | 95% CI | p-value | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Microbial reduction | **RD −0.15** | −0.28, −0.01 | **0.03** | 60% |
| Apical healing | **RD −0.09** | −0.16, −0.02 | **0.01** | 0% |

- 8 of 13 included articles rated high methodological quality
- Low overlap of primary studies across MAs (confirms independence of evidence)

## Evidence Hierarchy Context
- This is the highest-level synthesis available: umbrella review of SRs/MAs
- Confirms findings of Kumar 2023 (SR+MA) and Tonini 2022 (SR) at a higher synthesis level

## Limitations
- Dependent on quality of included SRs/MAs
- High heterogeneity in primary studies (irrigant type, concentration, volume, activation parameters)
- Long-term outcome data sparse
