## Three-line Summary

5-year RCT (n=50, University of Copenhagen) comparing zirconia-based (abutment + crown) versus metal-based (metal-ceramic abutment + ceramic crown) implant-supported single premolar restorations.

5-year survival was identical (96% both groups), marginal bone loss was comparable (~1.1 mm), and the zirconia group had zero chipping events vs one in the metal-ceramic group and zero abutment fractures.

Zirconia-based restorations are non-inferior to metal-based for premolar implant crowns at 5 years, with a chipping-free profile supporting monolithic zirconia for posterior single implant restoration.

## 세줄요약

소구치 임플란트 단관 수복에서 지르코니아 기반(지대주+크라운) vs 금속 기반(금속-세라믹 지대주+크라운) 5년 RCT(n=50, 코펜하겐대).

5년 생존율 동등(양군 96%), 변연골 소실 비슷(~1.1 mm), 지르코니아군 칩핑 0건 vs 금속군 1건, 지대주 파절 없음.

지르코니아 기반 수복은 소구치 임플란트 단관에서 금속 기반과 비열등하며 칩핑 없는 장점 — 구치부 단일 임플란트 수복에서 모노리식 지르코니아를 지지한다.

## Summary
5-year RCT comparing zirconia-based abutment+crown vs metal-based abutment+ceramic crown for premolar implant-supported restorations.

## Key Contributions
- 5-year survival: zirconia 96% vs metal-based 96% — no significant difference
- MBL comparable between groups (-1.1mm at 5 years)
- Zirconia group: zero chipping events vs one in metal-ceramic group

## Methodology
RCT; n=50; 5-year follow-up; University of Copenhagen.

## Results
Zirconia-based restorations non-inferior to metal-based for premolar implant crowns at 5 years. Abutment fractures: zero in zirconia group.
