Title: Immediate placement or immediate restoration/loading of single implants for molar tooth replacement: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Atieh MA, Payne AGT, Duncan WJ, de Silva RK, Cullinan MP
Year: 2010
Journal: International Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Implants, 25(2):401-15

ABSTRACT:

This systematic review and meta-analysis assessed the survival of immediately placed single implants in fresh molar extraction sites and immediately restored/loaded single molar implants in healed molar sites.

Search of main electronic databases including Cochrane Oral Health Group's Trials Register conducted up to November 1, 2008. Meta-analysis prepared in accordance with Academy of Osseointegration Workshop guidelines.

For immediately placed molar implants:
- 9 studies, 1,013 implants included
- Survival rate: 99.0%
- No significant differences between immediate and delayed loading/restoration in molar sites (RR 0.30, 95% CI: 0.05-1.61; P=0.16)

For immediate restoration/loading of single implants in healed molar sites:
- 7 studies, 188 single implants
- Implant survival rate: 97.9%
- No difference between immediate and delayed loading (RR 3.0, 95% CI: 0.33-27.16; P=0.33)
- Favorable marginal bone level changes in immediate loading group at 12 months (mean difference -0.31, 95% CI: -0.53 to -0.096; P=0.005)

Conclusions: Protocols of immediate placement and immediate restoration/loading of single implants in mandibular molar regions showed encouraging results.

Note: Study focuses on molar regions (posterior/posterior mandible) — distinct scope from anterior esthetic zone protocols.
