A New Classification System to Determine Posterior Mandible Morphology for Implant Therapy

Authors: Faisal Alqaood, Jahanzeb Chaudhry, Amar Hassan Khamis, Keyvan Moharamzadeh, Moosa Abdulla Abuzayeda
Journal: International Journal of Dentistry, 2026, Vol 2026, Pages 3244223
Date: 2026-02-01

--- FULL TEXT EXCERPT ---

195 CBCT volumes, retrospective cross-sectional, Dubai Health, IRB MBRU IRB-2021-52.
Sirona Galileos CBCT, 15x15x15 cm FOV, 0.15 mm voxel, 85 kVp.

Measurements: Wc (crest width), Wb (base width), Vcb (alveolar ridge height), Vb (height below P point), Vc (height above P point), lingual concavity depth and angle.
Line A = 2 mm coronal to superior IAC border. Point A = intersection Line A + lingual cortical plate. Point P = most prominent lingual cortical plate point.

Ridge type distribution:
- U-type (undercut): 54.4% left, 52.1% right [most common]
- P-type (parallel): 27.2% left, 33.5% right
- C-type (convergent): 18.5% left, 14.4% right

Sex differences: Males > females in alveolar ridge height and height above P point (p<0.001 left; p=0.047 right).
Age <40 vs >=40: Greater crest width (p=0.023 L, p<0.001 R) and ridge height (p=0.025 L, p=0.008 R) in younger.
First molar presence: Significantly greater crest width (p<0.001), ridge height (p<0.001), concavity depth (right p<0.001) in those with first molar.
Undercut presence: Greater height above P point (p=0.006), deeper concavity depth (p=0.001), but lower height below P point (p=0.002).

Ridge type morphometrics:
- C-type: lowest crest width (6.75/6.66 mm), lowest ridge height (12.59/12.64 mm)
- P-type: intermediate
- U-type: highest height above P point, deepest concavity depth

Lingual concavity depth:
- With undercut: 1.72 mm (L), 1.93 mm (R) vs without 0.61 mm, 0.71 mm (p<0.001)
- U-type: 1.89 mm (L), 2.27 mm (R) vs C-type 1.19/1.16 mm

CPDU Classification System:
Step 1 - Ridge shape:
  C (convergent): wide base, narrow crest
  P (parallel): equal width crest and base
  D (divergent): narrow base, wide crest, inherent undercut
Step 2 - Undercut (for C and P only):
  +U if undercut present; step 2 not applicable for D (undercut inherent)
Step 3 - Influential status:
  I = influential (above IAC, poses LPP risk)
  N = non-influential

Examples: P-U-I, D-I, C-U-I, C, P, P-U-N

Key recommendation: CBCT mandatory for preoperative posterior mandibular implant assessment.
D-type ridges have highest lingual perforation risk.
