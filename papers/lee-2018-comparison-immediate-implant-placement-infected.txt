Comparison of immediate implant placement in infected and non-infected extraction sockets: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Lee J, Park D, Koo KT, Seol YJ, Lee YM
Acta Odontologica Scandinavica 2018;76(5):338-345
DOI: 10.1080/00016357.2018.1453084
PMID: 29611763
Institution: Department of Periodontology and Dental Research Institute, Seoul National University, School of Dentistry, Seoul, Republic of Korea

[ABSTRACT ONLY — full text not retrieved via PMC]

OBJECTIVE
To investigate the feasibility of immediate implant placement in infected extraction sockets.

METHODS
Electronic and manual searches up to March 2017.
Study types: randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and nonrandomized controlled clinical trials (CCTs).
Meta-analysis model: fixed-effects model for survival rate (primary outcome).
Outcomes: survival rate (risk difference), MBL, PD, mBI, MGL, WKG (weighted mean differences).

5 studies included in systematic review; 3 studies in meta-analysis.

RESULTS
Risk difference for immediate implant placement in infected extraction socket vs non-infected socket: -0.02

Weighted mean differences (WMDs) at baseline vs latest follow-up:
- MBL: 0.32 mm
- PD: 0.12 mm
- mBI: 0.07
- MGL: -0.06 mm
- WKG: 0.20 mm (initial) and 0.51 mm (later)

No statistical differences observed between groups, EXCEPT for change in WKG.

CONCLUSIONS
Implants can be placed in infected extraction sockets after thorough socket debridement. For aesthetics, WKG should be considered when performing immediate implant placement in infected sites.
