Immediate versus early loading of immediately placed bone-level tapered dental implants with hydrophilic surface in full arch maxillary rehabilitation: A pilot randomized clinical trial with 2-year follow-up.

Authors: Marković A, Todorović A, Glišić M, Marković J, Ilić B, Janjić B, Mišić T, Trifković B, Vučić U, Šćepanović M, Dard MM
Year: 2024
Journal: Clinical Implant Dentistry and Related Research, 26(4):688-703
DOI: 10.1111/cid.13334
PMID: 38693759

Abstract:
This study compared implant stabilities between immediately and early loaded, immediately placed bone-level tapered dental implants in the maxilla and evaluated marginal bone loss (MBL), OHRQoL, and patient satisfaction at 2-year follow-up. Prospective RCT on 24 maxillary failing dentition patients. 12 patients: implants immediately loaded with temporary restorations; 12 patients: no temporization (early loading at week 7). Final implant-supported screw-retained complete PFM prostheses delivered at week 7. Insertion torque: immediate 33.0±4.87 Ncm, early 29.26±8.31 Ncm. ISQ dynamics similar for both groups (Penguin p=0.268; Ostell p=0.552). MBL at submillimeter level. Cumulative survival rate: 91.80% immediate vs 97.22% early (p=0.162 ns). OHIP total score significantly decreased over time in both groups (p<0.001). High patient satisfaction at 2 years regardless of loading protocol. Both immediate and early loading protocols for 6 immediately placed bone-level tapered implants are adequate for fixed maxillary rehabilitation.
