Fracture incidence of WaveOne Gold files: a prospective clinical study
Authors: Clóvis Stephano Pereira Bueno, Daniel Pinto de Oliveira, Rina Andréa Pelegrine, et al.
Year: 2020
DOI: 10.1111/iej.13349
Journal: International Endodontic Journal
PMID: 32573799

Abstract:
Aim: This prospective clinical study evaluated the incidence of instrument fracture observed after single-file root canal treatment of molars using WaveOne Gold instruments.

Methodology: Three standardized, experienced and calibrated specialists treated 750 maxillary and mandibular molars with curvatures less than 45° (2691 root canals) over a 12-month period. All the treatments were performed in a single session. A total of 1104 WaveOne Gold instruments were used, including 38 small, 750 primary, 228 medium and 88 large instruments.

Results: The fracture incidence data is reported in the full text. This prospective study by experienced specialists using WaveOne Gold single-file system for molar treatment.

Keywords: WaveOne Gold, reciprocating, single-file, fracture incidence, prospective clinical study, molar

Methods:
Prospective clinical study. 3 experienced, calibrated specialists. 750 maxillary and mandibular molars. Curvatures <45° (Schneider method). 2691 root canals. 12-month period. Single-session treatments. 1104 WaveOne Gold instruments used:
