Oral health-related quality of life of conventional removable partial dentures, unilateral nonmetal
clasp dentures, and shortened dental arch with 2- or 3-tooth unilateral distal extension tooth loss in
the mandible: A randomized, crossover, clinical trial.
Nakai N, Kurogi T, Murata H.

STATEMENT OF PROBLEM: Conventional RPDs with metal clasps are still common for distal-extension tooth
loss; unilateral nonmetal clasp dentures (thermoplastic denture base resin) are increasingly used; the
shortened dental arch (SDA) concept remains controversial. These options remain controversial,
particularly with loss of a few distal teeth unilaterally.
PURPOSE: Investigate OHRQoL of participants requesting a new dental prosthesis at a private practice.
METHODS: 3-period randomized crossover, within-subject controlled clinical trial. 24 participants
(mean age 59.0 y; 7 men 62.6 [45-85] y; 17 women 57.5 [24-75] y). All fully dentate in the maxilla,
unilateral distal-extension loss of 2 or 3 teeth in the mandible. Allocated to 1 of 6 sequence groups,
used a prosthesis or nothing for 2-week periods. At end of each intervention participants answered the
Oral Health Impact Profile questionnaire. Mixed-effects model (alpha=.05).
RESULTS:
- Total OHIP scores were HIGHER for unilateral nonmetal clasp dentures (P=.002) and for SDA (P=.049)
  than for conventional RPDs, with medium and small effects respectively.
- Unilateral nonmetal clasp dentures had a similar effect compared with SDA (P>.05).
- Nonmetal clasp dentures (P=.011) and SDA (P=.005) demonstrated medium effects in the ORAL FUNCTION
  dimension compared with conventional RPDs.
- Nonmetal clasp dentures exhibited large and medium effects in the OROFACIAL APPEARANCE dimension
  compared with conventional RPDs (P=.001) and SDA (P=.006).
- OROFACIAL PAIN dimension: SDA showed effects similar to conventional RPDs and nonmetal clasp
  dentures (P>.05).
- PSYCHOSOCIAL IMPACT dimension: no significant differences (P>.05), but the nonmetal clasp denture
  score was lower than the conventional RPD score with a small effect.
- Men had significantly lower total OHIP scores for SDA than women (P=.033).
CONCLUSIONS: Unilateral nonmetal clasp dentures and shortened dental arch were BETTER than conventional
removable partial dentures for the oral health-related quality of life of individuals with unilateral
distal-extension tooth loss in the mandible, and SEX had a significant effect on removable prosthetic
planning.

*** SCORING-DIRECTION NOTE (added at ingest): the abstract reports higher total OHIP scores for the
NMCD and SDA arms while concluding those arms were BETTER. In the standard OHIP scoring convention a
LOWER score denotes better OHRQoL, so the direction reported here is inverted relative to that
convention. The instrument variant/scoring used is not recoverable from the abstract. The CONCLUSION
statement (NMCD and SDA better than conventional RPD) is the interpretation carried forward. ***
