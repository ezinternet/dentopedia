Title: The effect of mandibular advancement with clear aligners compared with activator therapy on maxillary and mandibular volumes: a randomized controlled trial using CBCT
Authors: Kavasoğlu N, Naiboğlu E
Journal: BMC Oral Health, 2026;26(1)
DOI: 10.1186/s12903-026-07924-4
PMID: 41709259
PMC: PMC12964786

ABSTRACT:
Objective: Evaluate effects of Mandibular Advancement Appliance (MAA) on mandibular and maxillary volumes in growing individuals via 3D CBCT, compared with Activator and control.
Methods: Single-center RCT, n=55, skeletal Class II, CVM stages 2-3. MAA (n=20), Activator (n=20), Control (n=15). Volumetric analysis via CBCT + Mimics 15.0.
Results: Volumetric increases in both treatment groups but NOT statistically significant (p>0.05). No significant differences vs control.
Conclusions: MAA and Activator showed similar volumetric effects; Class II correction predominantly positional, not volumetric. MAA may be effective alternative for Class II.
Trial registration: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07056829 (registered June 29, 2025; retrospectively).

KEY RESULTS:
- Mandibular volume change: Activator 419.23 mm3 vs MAA 263.48 mm3 vs Control 874.50 mm3; H=0.128, p=0.938
- Maxillary volume change: Activator 809.49 mm3 vs MAA 996.59 mm3 vs Control 388.92 mm3; H=1.176, p=0.556
- ANCOVA adjusted mandibular: F=0.131, p=0.877
- ANCOVA adjusted maxillary: F=0.049, p=0.952
- Intragroup MAA maxillary: z=-2.613, p=0.009 (significant within-group increase, but no intergroup difference)
- Mean treatment duration: MAA 8.15 months, Activator 7.15 months
- Mean age: 12.5y (MAA), 12.6y (Activator), 12.3y (Control)
- ICC for segmentation: >0.90 (excellent)
- Baseline mandibular volume significantly different between groups (p=0.001) — ANCOVA used to adjust

LIMITATIONS:
- Small n (n=20 per treatment group), high inter-individual growth variability → type II error possible
- Short observation period (6-8 months); long-term skeletal remodeling requires longer follow-up
- No objective wear-time monitoring; compliance based on patient/parent report
- No complementary cephalometric measurements (condylar growth, SNB/ANB)
- Baseline mandibular volume and inter-scan intervals differed significantly between groups
- CBCT retrospectively analyzed (clinically indicated scans)

METHODS:
- CBCT system: i-CAT 3D; segmentation: Mimics 15.0; HU threshold 226-3071
- MAA: Invisalign MAA, 2mm increments every 8 weeks, follow-up every 5-6 weeks
- Activator: Projet Bite Wafer, prescribed full-time (24h except meals/sport)
- Outcome: mandibular and maxillary volumetric changes (primary), overjet and skeletal age (secondary)
- Stats: Kruskal-Wallis H, Wilcoxon signed-rank, ANCOVA
