Author information:
(1)Department of Pediatric Dentistry, Kornberg School of Dentistry, Temple 
University, Philadelphia, Pa., USA.

Neurocardiogenic syncope is a condition that frequently occurs in children. The 
purpose of this case report was to describe the clinical manifestations of a 
7-year old child with neurocardiogenic syncope and the remedial management of 
loss of consciousness during dental treatment.
