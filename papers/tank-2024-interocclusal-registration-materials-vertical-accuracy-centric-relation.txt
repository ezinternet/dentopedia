ABSTRACT

Background: The precise recording and transfer of maxillo-mandibular relationships are pivotal in prosthetic treatments.

Aims and Objectives: To evaluate the efficacy of four bite registration materials: alu wax, hard wax, polyvinylsiloxane, and bis-acrylic resin, in accurately recording, maintaining, and reproducing the vertical interocclusal relationship on either side of the dentition.

Materials and Methods: Thirty subjects within the age group of 20–45 years were selected. The difference in the reading of the vertical distance with the material and at maximum intercuspation is calculated individually for each record on the left and right sides.

Results: On the right side, the lowest mean was observed in MIP and the highest in hard wax. On the left side, the lowest mean was observed in MIP, while the highest was in alu wax. Among the four bite registration materials evaluated, Luxabite and Jetbite exhibited the highest mean accuracy scores.

Conclusions: Closure on interocclusal record material and bite release produce vertical discrepancies. Waxes showed the most, and bis-acrylic Luxabite and addition silicones showed the least dimensional distortion vertically.

I

The accurate recording and subsequent transfer of the maxillo-mandibular relationship to an articulator are crucial for the success of any prosthetic treatment.[] A crucial factor in this evaluation is the dimensional stability of the chosen material. Minimizing this deformation becomes paramount, as excessive dimensional changes can compromise the accuracy of mounted casts in the articulator.[] This investigation assessed the efficacy of four distinct bite registration materials (alu wax, hard wax, polyvinylsiloxane, and bis-acrylic resin) in accurately recording, maintaining, and reproducing the vertical interocclusal relationship on either side of the dentition.[]

M

Study design: The presentstudy was conducted in the Department of Prosthodontics, Govt Dental College and Hospital, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. The ethical clearance for the study was obtained from the Institutional Ethical Committee (Government Dental College and Hospital IEC GDCH/PROS.2/2021). Undergraduate and postgraduate dental students volunteered for the study. Thirty subjects within the age group 20–45 years were selected without any gender bias. Each subject underwent interocclusal registrations in centric relation with all four materials. Maxillary and mandibular casts were articulated with maximum intercuspation on the Hanau Wide Vue articulator. Each subject underwent interocclusal registrations in a centric relationship with all four materials. The interocclusal records were meticulously positioned on the occlusal surface of the maxillary cast, with the Lucia jig firmly in place, ensuring precise alignment before seating the mandibular cast over the records. The parameter selected was the vertical distance between the two predefined points, measured using a digital vernier caliper. The difference in reading of the vertical distance with the two materials and at maximum intercuspation is calculated individually for each record on the left and right sides.

Recording centric relation using alu wax (Group 1): A double thickness of alu wax was then positioned in the mouth alongside the jig, capturing the occlusal contact at centric relation. Once the alu wax hardened completely, it was carefully removed and stored in a container of water for preservation.

Bite registration using hard wax (Group 2): Upon complete hardening of the wax, the bite registration was gently extracted from the oral cavity and preserved in a suitable storage environment to maintain its integrity until subsequent analysis.

Centric relation registration with polyvinylsiloxane (Jetbite) (Group 3): The Jetbite® system utilizes premixed polyvinylsiloxane (PVS) in auto-mix cartridges equipped with a dedicated spiral mixing tip and a spreader tip for optimized material manipulation.

Centric relation registration with Luxabite bis-acrylic resin (Group 4): The Luxabite® bis-acrylic resin is dispensed through a dedicated 4:1 ratio dual-cartridge system into an auto-mixing gun provided by the manufacturer.

D

In this study, comparison between four different materials was done on the right and left sides. Among the four bite registration materials evaluated, Luxabite and Jetbite exhibited the highest mean accuracy scores, while hard wax yielded the lowest. This disparity might be attributed to the high viscosity of waxes, hindering their ability to faithfully capture intricate surface details. This observation aligns with previous research conducted.[] The high accuracy of polyvinylsiloxane is attributed to its addition polymerization reaction, which minimizes dimensional changes due to the absence of by-products. This finding aligns with numerous studies.[] Among the evaluated materials, Jetbite emerged as the most accurate and dimensionally stable, while hard wax exhibited the lowest performance.

C

Selecting the optimal interocclusal recording material necessitates careful consideration of both dimensional stability and minimal deformation. Clinicians must weigh the inherent properties of each material as per the patient. Closure on interocclusal record material and bite release produce vertical discrepancies. Waxes showed the most, and bis-acrylic Luxabite and addition silicones showed the least dimensional distortion vertically.
