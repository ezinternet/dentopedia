Title: Clinician- and patient-reported outcomes following the surgical treatment of single gingival recession defects: A systematic review.
Authors: Cairo F, Couso-Queiruga E, Barbato L, Rupe C, Stuhr S, Chambrone L, Avila-Ortiz G.
Journal: Periodontology 2000. 2025.
DOI: 10.1111/prd.12641
PMID: 40693671
PROSPERO: CRD517050

OBJECTIVE: To analyze the effect of root coverage surgical therapy for the treatment of single gingival recession defects (GRD) in terms of clinician- and patient-reported outcomes (CROs and PROs), with emphasis on esthetic perception.

METHODS: PRISMA 2020-compliant systematic review. RCTs reporting outcomes of root coverage for single GRDs. Primary outcomes: root coverage esthetic scores (RES) and patient-reported esthetic perception/satisfaction (VAS). Data extracted and categorized; risk of bias assessed.

RESULTS:
- 58 articles from 50 different RCTs selected
- 1820 subjects with 2219 single GRDs treated

Key findings:
- Root coverage and gingival phenotype modification therapy positively influenced both RES and patient-reported esthetic values
- CAF + sCTG (bilaminar) > CAF alone (monolaminar) for both RES and VAS values
- CAF + soft tissue substitutes: beneficial effect on VAS only (not RES)
- Lateral flap displacement > coronal flap displacement for RES values
- Meta-regression: statistically significant positive association between mean root coverage percentage and RES (greater %RC → higher RES)
- Association between patient-reported esthetic perception and MRC was NOT statistically significant
- Dentinal hypersensitivity substantially reduced with surgical root coverage regardless of modality

CONCLUSIONS: Surgical therapy for single GRDs had a positive effect on both clinician-reported esthetic scores and patient-reported esthetic perception and satisfaction. Bilaminar techniques (CAF+sCTG) are generally associated with superior results.
