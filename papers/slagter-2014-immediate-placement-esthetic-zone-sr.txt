Title: Immediate placement of dental implants in the esthetic zone: a systematic review and pooled analysis.
Authors: Slagter KW, den Hartog L, Bakker NA, Vissink A, Meijer HJA, Raghoebar GM
Year: 2014
Journal: Journal of Periodontology, 85(7):e241-50

ABSTRACT:

Research interest on immediate placement has shifted from implant survival toward optimal preservation of soft and hard tissues. This study systematically assesses implant survival, peri-implant hard and soft tissue changes, esthetic outcome, and patient satisfaction of immediately placed single-tooth implants in the esthetic zone.

MEDLINE, EMBASE, and CENTRAL searched for publications up to June 2013. Pooled analysis performed to identify factors associated with survival and peri-implant tissue changes after immediate implant placement.

Thirty-four studies were considered eligible. Results:
- 1-year implant survival: 97.1% (95% CI: 0.958 to 0.980)
- Mean marginal peri-implant bone loss: 0.81 ± 0.48 mm
- Mean loss of interproximal peri-implant mucosa level: 0.38 ± 0.23 mm
- Mean loss of peri-implant midfacial mucosa level: 0.54 ± 0.39 mm

Regression analysis revealed that delayed provisionalization (OR 58.03, 95% CI: 8.05-418.41, P<0.000), use of a flap (OR 19.87, 95% CI: 10.21-38.66, P<0.000), and use of a connective tissue graft (OR 4.56, 95% CI: 1.72-12.08, P=0.002) were associated with marginal peri-implant bone-level change >0.50 mm.

Conclusions: Immediate placement with immediate provisionalization of dental implants in the esthetic zone results in excellent short-term treatment outcome in terms of implant survival and minimal change of peri-implant soft and hard tissue dimensions.

Keywords: dental implantation, dental restoration permanent, esthetics dental
