"Contemporary Sterilization Protocols of Healing Abutments for Reusability: A Systematic Review."
JDR Clin Trans Res. 2022;7(4):352-359. (e-pub 2021-10-07)

== ABSTRACT (full) ==
Background: Although healing abutments (HAs) are designated for SINGLE USE by most implant manufacturers, clinicians commonly reuse them. There is a lack of adequate references describing detailed sterilization protocols for reuse.

Purpose: Compile, organize, and describe the most common techniques for HA sterilization and their efficiency in eliminating traces of microorganisms.

Methods: Electronic search of 5 databases (MEDLINE/PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science, Google Scholar), Jan 2000–Dec 2020. Search variables: dental implant, healing abutment, contaminate, contamination, reuse, sterilization. Included studies: minimum sample size 10 HAs (5 per group), English. Risk of bias assessed.

Results:
- 812 articles identified → 8 included.
- STEAM AUTOCLAVE was the most widely used form of resterilization.
- NOT A SINGLE protocol was able to achieve 100% virgin surface of the healing abutments.

Conclusions:
- Although reuse of HAs is a cost-effective measure, thorough surface decontamination followed by resterilization is highly recommended before reuse.
- Results useful for knowing sterilization protocols to inform more appropriate therapeutic decisions (with consideration of cost and patient preference).

Study type: Systematic Review (JDR Clin Trans Res). MeSH: Dental Abutments, Dental Implants, Equipment Reuse, Steam, Sterilization, Surface Properties.
Keywords: dental implant(s), disinfection, healing abutment, resterilization, reuse, sterilization.
