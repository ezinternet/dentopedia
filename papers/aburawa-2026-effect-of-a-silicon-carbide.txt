Effect of a silicon carbide bur on shear bond strength between resin cement and zirconia

Authors: Aburawa et al.
Journal: Biomater Investig Dent. 2026;13:46767
PMID: 42668905
PMC: PMC13525429
DOI: 10.2340/biid.v13.46767

ABSTRACT

Purpose: This in-vitro pilot study aimed to investigate the effect of a silicon carbide grinding bur on the shear bond strength (SBS) between zirconia and 10-MDP-containing cement.

Materials and methods: Thirty zirconia specimens were prepared from Copra Supreme Symphony zirconia (4Y-PSZ, Whitepeaks Dental Solutions) and randomly assigned to three groups: airborne-particle abrasion (APA) with 110-μm aluminum oxide particles, treatment with a silicon carbide grinding bur (SiC bur, Cimara, VOCO GmbH), and APA followed by SiC bur treatment (APA + SiC bur). All specimens were bonded with PANAVIA V5 adhesive resin cement (Kuraray), aged with 5,000 thermocycles (5-55°C), and subjected to SBS test. Statistical analysis: one-way ANOVA and Tukey's post hoc (α=0.05). Fracture modes assessed by digital microscopy at ×35.

Results: APA: 37.00 MPa (min 32.70, max 41.10). SiC bur: 20.77 MPa (min 9.98, max 24.75). APA + SiC bur: 24.86 MPa (min 18.28, max 33.56). APA group showed significantly higher SBS than SiC bur and APA + SiC bur groups (p<0.001). APA + SiC bur showed higher mean than SiC bur alone but difference not statistically significant (p>0.05). All groups showed mixed fracture modes (10-90% residual cement coverage). No visible cracks in zirconia at ×35.

Conclusion: Silicon carbide bur treatment did not have a beneficial effect on SBS after 5,000 thermocycles. APA resulted in significantly higher SBS than SiC bur treatment alone or APA followed by SiC bur treatment.

Clinical relevance: Under the tested conditions, the silicon carbide bur cannot be recommended as an alternative or adjunct to APA for improving zirconia-resin cement bonding.

KEY MESSAGES

1. APA resulted in higher SBS after 5,000 thermocycles than SiC bur treatment alone or sequential APA + SiC bur treatment
2. SiC bur did not provide a beneficial effect and cannot be recommended as an alternative or adjunct to APA
3. Untreated zirconia was not included; the effect of SiC bur relative to no surface treatment could not be determined

INTRODUCTION AND METHODS

Zirconia lacks a glass phase and has a crystalline structure that impairs bonding. APA with aluminum oxide particles is the most commonly used and effective surface treatment for zirconia, increasing surface roughness and surface energy. A notable drawback is microdefects formation at restoration margins.

The VOCO Cimara Repair System includes a silicon carbide bur (SiC Grinding Bur, REF 1201). In a previous multi-system comparison, VOCO produced unexpectedly high SBS. This study isolated the SiC bur contribution.

- Materials: Copra Supreme Symphony zirconia (4Y-PSZ, ~4 mol% yttria), monolithic, milled + sintered at 1630°C/120 min
- Groups: n=10 per group
  - APA: 110-μm Al₂O₃, 2 bar, 10 mm, 10 s, perpendicular
  - SiC bur: Cimara SiC Grinding Bur, 7,000 rpm, dry, 10 strokes
  - APA + SiC bur: APA first, then SiC
- Cementation: Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus + PANAVIA V5 (MDP-containing)
- Aging: 5,000 thermocycles, 5±2°C to 55±2°C
- Testing: SBS, Instron 4465, 1 mm/min, knife-edge blade

RESULTS

| Group | n | Mean SBS (MPa) | Min | Max |
|-------|---|----------------|-----|-----|
| APA | 10 | 37.00 | 32.70 | 41.10 |
| SiC bur | 10 | 20.77 | 9.98 | 24.75 |
| APA + SiC bur | 10 | 24.86 | 18.28 | 33.56 |

APA vs SiC bur: p<0.001
APA vs APA+SiC: p<0.001
SiC bur vs APA+SiC: p>0.05 (not significant)
Overall effect: η²=0.762, ω²=0.737 (large effect)
All groups: mixed failure mode (10-90% cement remnants)

LIMITATIONS

- No untreated control group (cannot assess SiC vs no treatment)
- Small pilot sample (n=10 per group)
- Zirconia-zirconia assembly (not tooth-zirconia configuration)
- Cement layer thickness not measured
- Surface roughness not quantified

DISCUSSION

SiC bur treatment may provide adequate short-term bond strength in some repair protocols but less durable after aging (Libecki et al. found SiC group declined significantly after 150 days/37,500 thermocycles while APA maintained). Current results are post-aging and show APA superiority. Sequential APA + SiC numerically higher than SiC alone but significantly lower than APA alone — SiC may alter APA-created topography unfavorably.
