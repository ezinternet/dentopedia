Title: Impact of Clear Aligners versus Fixed Appliances on Periodontal Status of Patients Undergoing Orthodontic Treatment: A Systematic Review of Systematic Reviews
Authors: Federica Di Spirito, Francesco D'Ambrosio, Davide Cannatà, Vincenzo D'Antò, Francesco Giordano, Stefano Martina
Journal: Healthcare (Basel)
Year: 2023
DOI: 10.3390/healthcare11091340
PMID: 37174882
PMCID: PMC10178428
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10178428/

DESIGN: Umbrella review (systematic review of systematic reviews with meta-analyses)
N SRs included: 4
N total patients: 2,042 (clear aligner: 978; fixed: 1,064; ages 10–51)
Outcomes: Plaque Index (PI), Gingival Index (GI), BOP/SBI, PPD, Gingival Recession

KEY NUMERICAL FINDINGS:
Plaque Index:
- Short-term (2–3 months): CA better (MD −0.35 to −0.69)
- Mid-term (6–9 months): CA superior (MD −0.91 to −1.10)
- Long-term (≥12 months): no significant difference

Gingival Index:
- Short-term: CA better (MD −0.24 to −0.37)
- Mid-term: small advantage CA (MD −0.14)
- Long-term: no significant difference

PPD:
- Short/mid-term: CA modest improvement (MD −0.26 to −0.42)
- Long-term: CA limited worsening (MD −0.45 to −0.93)

Sulcus Bleeding Index:
- Short-term: CA superior (SMD −0.44 to −0.49)
- Mid-term: CA favored (SMD −0.91)

Gingival Recession:
- CA stable at 3 months; fixed showed significant recession increase

OVERALL CONCLUSION:
"The impact of orthodontic treatment with clear aligners and fixed appliances on periodontal health should be considered comparable"
Differences "practically negligible in a clinical setting" (< 1 mm PPD; < 1 point for ordinal indices)

AMSTAR 2:
- High quality: 1 (Crego-Ruiz 2023)
- Moderate quality: 1 (Jiang 2018)
- Low quality: 2 (Lu 2018, Wu 2022)

LIMITATIONS:
- High heterogeneity across SRs
- Overlapping primary studies across SRs
- Heterogeneous follow-up timing
- No CAL data
- Recession evidence limited to short-term single study
