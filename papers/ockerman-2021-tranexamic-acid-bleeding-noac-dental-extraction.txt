Source: PubMed/PMC (PMC8128271), DOI 10.1371/journal.pmed.1003601. Condensed working copy.
Ockerman A, Miclotte I, Vanhaverbeke M, Vanassche T, Belmans A, Vanhove J, Meyns J, Nadjmi N, Van Hemelen G, Winderickx P, Jacobs R, Politis C, Verhamme P. PLoS Med 2021;18(5):e1003601.

ABSTRACT: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter trial; 10% TXA mouthwash vs placebo; used once before extraction then 3x/day for 3 days. Primary: patients with any oral bleeding up to day 7. 222 randomized, 218 in full analysis set (106 TXA, 112 placebo). Any bleeding 28 (26.4%) vs 32 (28.6%), RR 0.92 (0.60-1.42), P=0.72. Bleeds 46 vs 85, rate ratio 0.57 (0.31-1.05), P=0.07. Periprocedural VAS 4+-1.78 vs 4+-1.82 (P=0.80). Early bleeding RR 0.76 (0.42-1.37). Delayed bleeding rate ratio 0.32 (0.12-0.89). Multiple extractions rate ratio 0.40 (0.20-0.78). One TIA in placebo arm while interrupting NOAC. Limits: premature stop after futility analysis; self-reported compliance.

METHODS: Prospective RCT, 4 Belgian hospitals (UZ Leuven, Heilig Hart Leuven, St-Jan Genk, AZ Monica Antwerp). Adults on rivaroxaban/apixaban/edoxaban/dabigatran scheduled for extraction. All skipped morning NOAC dose (optionally evening before; 18-24 h window) per EHRA. 1 g/10 mL (10%) TXA mouthwash vs taste/color-matched placebo; computer-generated block randomization, not stratified; blinded. Mouthwash 1 min, spit. Wound management at physician discretion. NOAC resumed day after extraction unless hemostasis issues. Blinded phone follow-up day 2 and day 7. Primary: number of patients with any oral bleeding. Secondary: bleeding categories (minor/clinically relevant/major; early day 0-1 vs delayed day>=2), VAS bleeding score, unplanned medical contacts, reinterventions, unplanned NOAC interruption. Safety: thrombotic events, allergy, non-oral bleeding. Sample size 236 (detect 15% difference, control 30%, 80% power, alpha 5%, chi-squared). Full analysis set excluded 3 lost to follow-up and 1 control patient who used off-trial TXA. Unplanned DSMB interim analysis May 2020 when 222/236 randomized; trial suspended (SARS-CoV-2), DSMB recommended not to reinitiate based on bleeding numbers (futility). Subgroup analysis prespecified; post hoc analysis of oral bleeds + facial hematomas.

BASELINE (TXA n=106 / placebo n=112): age 74.8+-8.8 / 72.7+-10.7; male 81 (76.4%) / 64 (57.1%); CHA2DS2-VASc 3.7 / 3.7; rivaroxaban 38/40, apixaban 29/33, edoxaban 21/19, dabigatran 18/20; AF 88 (83.0%) / 88 (78.6%); VTE 11/14. Groups balanced except sex.

RESULTS Table 2 (TXA vs placebo; RR for patients, rate ratio for number of bleeds):

Oral bleeding events: 46 vs 85, rate ratio 0.57 (0.31-1.05) [table/abstract; text 1.04]
Clinically relevant: 4 (3.8%) vs 10 (8.9%), RR 0.42 (0.14-1.31); events 5 vs 13
Minor: 27 (25.5%) vs 29 (25.9%), RR 0.98; events 41 vs 72
Delayed: 7 (6.6%) vs 17 (15.2%), RR 0.44 (0.19-1.01); events 11 vs 36, rate ratio 0.32 (0.12-0.89)
Early: 25 (23.6%) vs 27 (24.1%), RR 0.98; events 35 vs 49, rate ratio 0.76 (0.42-1.37)
Facial hematomas: 6 (5.7%) vs 14 (12.5%), RR 0.45 (0.18-1.14)
Total bleeding (oral + hematoma): 31 (29.2%) vs 40 (35.7%), RR 0.82 (0.56-1.21); events 52 vs 99, rate ratio 0.56 (0.32-0.96)
Table 3: unplanned medical contact 7 (6.6%) vs 18 (16.1%), RR 0.41 (0.18-0.94); reintervention after oral bleeding 4 (3.8%) vs 10 (8.9%), RR 0.42 (0.13-1.31); unplanned NOAC interruption 6 (5.7%) vs 9 (8.0%), RR 0.70 (0.26-1.91). Thrombotic events 0 vs 1 (TIA after NOAC interruption 2 days before extraction); allergic reactions 2 (1.9%) vs 2 (1.8%) (tingling in mouth); epistaxis 2 vs 4; eye bleeding 1 vs 2; bruises 2 vs 4; hematuria 1 vs 1.
60 patients had oral bleeding; 28 had >1 bleed. No TXA patient had >=4 oral bleeds vs 10 placebo patients. All oral bleeds managed locally except one major bleed in placebo arm (transfusion, 2-day hospitalization). TXA no effect on day 0-1 bleeding; fewer bleeds on days 2, 3, 4.
Subgroups (exploratory): >=2 teeth extracted: 33 bleeds in 19/61 (31.2%) TXA vs 77 bleeds in 24/57 (42.2%) placebo, rate ratio 0.40 (0.20-0.78), significant interaction. Age >=75: 24 bleeds in 13/59 (22.0%) vs 55 bleeds in 16/51 (31.4%), rate ratio 0.35 (0.15-0.79), interaction P=0.07 (not significant). Early NOAC restart (<24 h) suggested benefit, not significant.

DISCUSSION/LIMITATIONS: First RCT of TXA for post-extraction bleeding in NOAC patients. Event rate >1 in 4 patients. Main limitation: premature stop at 222/236 after futility analysis. Self-reported compliance by phone. No fixed NOAC restart time. Subgroup findings exploratory. Authors: TXA well tolerated; affordable; future studies in high-risk populations.
