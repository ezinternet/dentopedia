Title: Botulinum Toxin Type A for Myogenous Temporomandibular Disorders, Focusing on the Duration of Clinical Effects and Dosage Parameters: A Systematic Review
Authors: de Santana IHG, Pereira KC, Leite SEM, de Carvalho Batista FR, Ferreira AJ, Costa BSL, Guimaraes CC, Rodrigues TLC, Paiva MDEB. Head Face Med 2026;22(1). Published 2026-06-20.

ABSTRACT: Ten RCTs included; qualitative synthesis (no meta-analysis) due to heterogeneity. PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO (CRD420251178977). Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, LILACS (search 22 Dec 2025). RoB-2. In placebo-controlled trials BoNT-A did not consistently show superiority over saline. Greater pain reduction in refractory myofascial pain. Function improved in both BoNT-A and control groups. Effects generally persisted 8-24 weeks. Evidence insufficient for dose-response. AEs mostly mild-moderate, transient. Not first-line; consider in selected refractory cases after failed conservative therapy.

METHODS: PICOS - adults/any age with myogenous TMD by RDC/TMD or DC/TMD; intramuscular BoNT-A into masticatory muscles; comparators placebo, conservative therapy or none; primary outcomes pain (VAS/NRS) and duration of clinical effect (time to relapse); secondary MMO, tenderness, AEs; RCTs only. Two reviewers independently (Rayyan). RoB-2. GRADE by outcome (pain, function, duration, dose-response, AEs); pooling not appropriate; GRADE judgments on direction/consistency; full GRADE and SoF in Supplementary Material 2 (not in text).

RESULTS: 179 records (Embase 36, MEDLINE 31, Scopus 77, WoS 29, LILACS 6); 24 duplicates removed; 155 screened; 119 excluded; 36 full texts; 26 excluded; 10 RCTs included.

Risk of bias (RoB-2): most low for missing data (D3); some concerns in D2, D4, D5 (blinding, self-reported outcomes, no preregistered protocols); most trials overall "some concerns", fewer low; none high.

SUMMARY OF RESULTS
Pain: all trials measured pain (VAS, CPI, NRS). Reduced in both BoNT-A and control arms (saline, splint, acupuncture, biofeedback). Placebo-controlled trials: no significant BoNT-A vs saline difference "across all included studies" (per Results text); refractory myofascial pain populations showed greater reduction with BoNT-A, maintained through follow-up. Versus splint/acupuncture/biofeedback: pain reduced in both without consistent differences.
Function: MMO, excursions, JFLS. Improved in BoNT-A and control arms; similar to saline in placebo trials.
Duration: effects described over 8 to 24 weeks; crossover differences mainly weeks 8-16; up to six-month follow-up trials reported outcomes through final time point. Peak effects 8-16 weeks then gradual decline (Discussion). Longer sustained effects only in observational/case reports (not extrapolable).
Dose-response: total doses 40-100 U across regimens; similar improvements, no consistent superiority of higher doses; insufficient evidence; units formulation-specific, not interchangeable.
AEs: transient muscle weakness, smile asymmetry, headache, fatigue, reduced EMG activity; isolated mild dysphagia and temporary mimetic paralysis; no serious or permanent AEs. Related to total dose, number of muscles, repeated application.

CONCLUSION: does not support routine first-line use; adjunct/second-line in selected refractory patients within multimodal care; calls for standardized, preregistered RCTs with long follow-up.
