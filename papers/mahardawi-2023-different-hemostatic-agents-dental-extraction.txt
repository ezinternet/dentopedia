Source: PubMed / PMC10397210 (PMID 37532770), DOI 10.1038/s41598-023-39023-7. Condensed working copy.
Mahardawi B, Jiaranuchart S, Arunjaroensuk S, Tompkins KA, Somboonsavatdee A, Pimkhaokham A. The effect of different hemostatic agents following dental extraction in patients under oral antithrombotic therapy: a network meta-analysis. Sci Rep 2023;13(1):12519. Published 2023-08-02.

ABSTRACT: NMA of hemostatic agents for dental extraction in patients on oral antithrombotic therapy (OAT). 23 RCTs included. Cyanoacrylate tissue adhesive: reduced odds of postoperative bleeding vs conventional (gauze/cotton pressure, sutures), tendency toward significance (OR 0.03, P=0.051). Tranexamic acid only agent significantly lower bleeding (OR 0.27, P=0.007). Chitosan dressing and collagen plug shortest time to hemostasis but ranked last for bleeding events, higher odds than conventional.

METHODS: PRISMA-NMA; PROSPERO CRD42023408207. Databases PubMed/Medline, CENTRAL, Scopus + grey literature; search ended March 20, 2023. Two independent reviewers (Rayyan). Excluded trials where control group stopped/modified drug regimen and hemostasis by other methods (e.g. electrocautery). RoB 2. Frequentist NMA in R 4.2.2 (netmeta, dmetar), random-effects; reference = "conventional" (gauze/cotton pressure +/- sutures). Time to hemostasis: SMD; bleeding events: OR. Heterogeneity I2; inconsistency net-split/Cochran Q. P-score ranking. GRADE via CINeMA.

RESULTS: 1655 identified; 82 full-text; 59 excluded; 23 included, 22 in MA. 13 parallel-group, 9 split-mouth, 1 mixed. Patients: 3 studies antithrombotic unspecified, 14 anticoagulants, 6 antiplatelets. Extraction simple in 19, surgical in 1, both in 2, unspecified 1. Agents: chitosan dental dressing, oxidized regenerated cellulose, tranexamic acid (TXA), fibrin sponge/adhesive, collagen plug, bismuth subgallate, gelatin sponge, feracrylum, epsilon aminocaproic acid, Ankaferd blood stopper, cyanoacrylate tissue adhesive. 16 studies had conventional group. Time to hemostasis reported in 11 studies; bleeding events in 16 (follow-up up to 10 days); in direct comparisons no significant difference hemostatic vs conventional in all studies; cyanoacrylate fewer events than gelatin sponge; gelatin sponge significant vs chitosan; fibrin and bismuth subgallate better than collagen.
RoB2: 8 low, 14 some concerns, 1 high.
Time to hemostasis (9 studies, 5 methods, all simple extraction): I2=98.3%, Q=288.97, P<0.0001. Chitosan SMD -9.78 (95% CI -12.78 to -6.78) direct; collagen plug SMD -10.13 (-15.53 to -4.73) indirect vs conventional. Ankaferd longer than chitosan SMD 7.39 (0.33 to 14.45) indirect. Ranking: chitosan and collagen best.
Bleeding events (16 studies, 12 methods): I2=12.5%, Q=3.10 P=0.69. TXA vs conventional OR 0.27 (0.10 to 0.69) only significant; cyanoacrylate OR 0.03 (0.0008 to 1.02) P=0.051 indirect. Direct: bismuth subgallate and fibrin lower odds than collagen plug. Indirect: chitosan higher odds than cyanoacrylate and TXA; collagen plug higher than cyanoacrylate and TXA. Ranking: cyanoacrylate best; TXA narrowest CI; chitosan and collagen last.
Bleeding events in anticoagulated patients (11 studies, 11 treatments): I2=32.3%, Q=1.33 P=0.7217. TXA OR 0.24 (0.08 to 0.77) vs conventional. Bismuth subgallate lower odds than collagen plug; chitosan higher odds than cyanoacrylate and TXA; collagen higher than TXA (indirect). Gelatin sponge performed worse than conventional measures in anticoagulated analysis (discussion).
Simple extraction subgroup (12 studies, 11 treatments): I2=34.3%, Q=6.13 P=0.11. TXA OR 0.12 (0.02 to 0.71). Bismuth subgallate and fibrin lower than collagen (direct); TXA lower than collagen (indirect). 7-study anticoagulated simple-extraction analysis similar trend.
GRADE/CINeMA: time to hemostasis all low/very low. Bleeding events: TXA efficacy downgraded one level to moderate (heterogeneity). Agent-vs-agent significant comparisons moderate to low. TXA vs collagen indirect: high overall, moderate in anticoagulated (imprecision), low in simple-extraction only (bias, imprecision).

DISCUSSION: TXA approx 70% decrease in odds; should be among first options in OAT extractions. Chitosan/collagen faster hemostasis but more later bleeding; proposed reasons poor mechanical properties, dislodgement, unpredictable biodegradation. Gelatin sponge may expand in small sockets, impair clot formation. Cyanoacrylate also studied vs sutures in healthy third-molar surgery.
LIMITATIONS: too few antiplatelet trials for antiplatelet-only MA; only one analysis for time to hemostasis; extracted teeth not specified; most studies potential risk of bias; lower certainty.
CONCLUSION: cyanoacrylate and TXA favorable for reducing postoperative bleeding; chitosan and collagen faster hemostasis but more bleeding events, ranked last.
