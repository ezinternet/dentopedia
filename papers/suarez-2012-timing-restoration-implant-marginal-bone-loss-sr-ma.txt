Title: Effect of the timing of restoration on implant marginal bone loss: a systematic review.
Authors: Suarez F, Chan HL, Monje A, Galindo-Moreno P, Wang HL
Year: 2012
Journal: Journal of Periodontology, 84(2):159-69

ABSTRACT:

The aim of this study is to compare marginal bone loss (MBL) between implants restored with: 1) immediate restoration/loading (IR/L); 2) early loading (EL); and 3) conventional loading (CL).

Electronic literature search from three databases (until November 2011) and hand search. Clinical human studies reporting comparison of MBL between IR/L, EL, or CL with ≥12-month follow-up. Minimal 10 implants per group. Both immediate placement (IP) and delayed placement (DP) included and analyzed separately.

Initial search: 1,640 articles; 27 full texts evaluated; 11 studies qualified and classified into 4 groups:
1) IR/L + DP versus CL + DP (n=6 articles)
2) IR + DP versus EL + DP (n=2 articles)
3) EL + DP versus CL + DP (n=1 article)
4) IL + IP versus CL + IP (n=2 articles)

Meta-analysis for group 1: 0.09 mm (95% CI = -0.27 to 0.09 mm) difference in mean MBL, favoring IR/L but without significant difference (P=0.33). No significant difference in MBL found for groups 2 through 4 after adjusting for implant placement level. Eight RCTs determined to be at moderate-to-high risk of publication bias.

Conclusions: This meta-analysis does not show an effect of the timing of restorations on implant MBL. Selection of restoration protocols should be based on factors other than MBL.

Study design: SR + MA; 11 studies included (8 RCTs, 2 CCTs, 1 retrospective)
