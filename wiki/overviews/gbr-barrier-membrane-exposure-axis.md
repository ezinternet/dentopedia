## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심: 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) 차폐막 17편을 "막노출 (Membrane Exposure)"이라는 공통 실패 모드 중심으로 4축(재료·가교, 막 구성, 판막·절개 설계, 티타늄메쉬 맞춤화)으로 통합.
> - 통합 명제: GBR 성공은 차폐막을 묻힌 상태로(submerged) 혈류 공급되게 유지하는 데 좌우 — 노출은 막 브랜드보다 연조직·판막 관리가 결정.
> - 축1(재료·가교): 화학적 가교 (Cross-linking) 막은 골 증대 이득 없이 노출 ~30% 더 많음(Wessing 2018 SR+MA, 20편) — 비가교 또는 물리적 가교 막 선호.
> - 가교 "방법"이 문제이지 가교 자체가 문제 아님 — 탈수열 가교 (Dehydrothermal Cross-linking, DHT) 막은 노출 신호 없이 분해만 느림(Lee 2015 RCT).
> - 합성 젤라틴/폴리락트산 (Polylactic Acid, PLA) 막은 상용 PLA 매트릭스에 비열등(6개월 골흡수·골생성·노출·감염 동등) — 저비용 대안(Wang 2025 RCT, 단일치 결손).
> - 축2(막 구성): 콜라겐 막 이중층은 단일층 대비 이득 없음(Choi 2017); 골이식 + 흡수성 막은 자연치유 대비 수평 −2.19mm·수직 −1.72mm 흡수 감소(Troiano 2018 SR+MA+TSA — **단, 이 논문은 위키에서 Canullo 2021 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA)에 의해 supersede 표시됨**; 방향은 뒤집히지 않고 재료 순위가 추가된 것).
> - 축2 확장 — "막을 한 겹 더" 논쟁은 메쉬 위에서도 반복된다: 천공형 티타늄 강화 dPTFE 메쉬 위 콜라겐막 추가는 SR에서 수직골증가·합병증 차이 없음(Yousefi-Koma 2025, RCT 3편)이나, 정작 핵심 RCT는 콜라겐막 **생략**의 비열등성 입증에 실패(Urban 2025, 4.1 vs 4.5mm) — 현 근거로 생략 지지 불가.
> - 축3(판막·절개, 노출의 핵심 레버): 절개 위치와 "잔존 판막 길이 vs 인접 각화치은 폭"이 조기 노출 예측; 1개월 내 재피개 안 되면 노출 지속(Park 2007).
> - 판막 거상은 각화치은을 3.21mm 더 잃게 함 — 얇은 연조직 자체가 노출 위험인자(Lee 2018 SR+MA).
> - **노출의 재생 비용이 이 페이지에서 가장 큰 수치다**: 확장형 폴리테트라플루오로에틸렌 (expanded Polytetrafluoroethylene, e-PTFE) 노출 시 골재생률 96.6% → 46.5%, 비노출 부위 대비 골증대 약 6배 차이(Plonka 2017 SR). 무장력 1차 봉합은 구호가 아니라 정량화된 술기 — 골막이완절개 단독으로 +5.5mm 전진(수직절개 1차 +1.1mm, 2차 +1.9mm).
> - 축4(티타늄메쉬): 맞춤형 CAD/CAM 메쉬(CTM)는 수직 증대는 메쉬-무관(NS)이나 수평 증대 우위(6.38 vs 3.85mm)·노출 경향 낮음(Ragucci 2025); 노출은 수직 골 yield를 깎음(Tay 2022, ratio-of-means 0.62~0.65).
> - 축4 근거등급 상향: Ragucci 2025는 후향연구 위주 SR+MA이나, **치유합병증을 1차 결과변수로 둔 이중맹검 비열등성 RCT**가 존재 — CAD/CAM 티타늄 메쉬는 맞춤형 강화 dPTFE 메쉬 대비 합병증 비열등(8.3% vs 12.5%), 수직골증대도 차이 없음(5.18 vs 5.79mm, NS)이나 재생률은 PTFE 우위(99.5% vs 87.0%, p=0.013) (Cucchi 2024).
> - 노출은 all-or-none이 아니라 **용량-반응**이다: 노출된 메쉬 1cm²당 신생골 용적 16.3% 감소(Cucchi 2017 RCT) — Tay 2022의 ratio-of-means를 면적 단위로 환산한 형태.
> - 임상 결론: 막 화학이 노출 내성 baseline을 정하지만(비가교 > 화학가교), 통제 가능한 외과 변수(절개 위치·판막 생존 길이·무장력 1차 봉합)가 노출 결과를 지배하며, 노출 면적이 커질수록 재생량이 비례해 깎인다.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 17 GBR barrier-membrane papers organized around membrane exposure as the dominant failure mode across four axes — membrane material/cross-linking, membrane configuration, flap/incision design, and titanium-mesh customization.

Chemical cross-linking raises exposure ~30% over non-cross-linked membranes without a bone-gain payoff (Wessing 2018 SR+MA); flap design and residual keratinized tissue predict exposure better than membrane brand (Park 2007 prospective; Lee 2018 SR+MA), and the regenerative cost of exposure is large and quantified — e-PTFE exposure drops regeneration from 96.6% to 46.5%, roughly a 6-fold bone-gain difference vs non-exposed sites (Plonka 2017 SR).

Exposure is dose-dependent rather than all-or-none (16.3% new-bone-volume deficit per cm² of exposed mesh, Cucchi 2017 RCT; ratio-of-means 0.62–0.65, Tay 2022 SR+MA), and among customized devices CAD/CAM titanium mesh is non-inferior to reinforced d-PTFE mesh for healing complications at RCT level (8.3% vs 12.5%, Cucchi 2024).

## 세줄요약

GBR 차폐막 17편을 "막노출(membrane exposure)"이라는 공통 실패 모드 중심으로 4축(재료·가교, 막 구성, 판막·절개, 티타늄메쉬 맞춤화)으로 통합 — 노출은 막 브랜드보다 연조직·판막 관리가 좌우한다.

화학가교막은 비가교막 대비 노출 ~30% 더 많고 골이득은 없으며(Wessing 2018 SR+MA), 판막 절개 위치·각화치은 폭이 노출을 예측하고(Park 2007 전향), 판막 거상 자체가 각화치은 3.21 mm 손실을 초래하며(Lee 2018 SR+MA), 노출 시 e-PTFE 골재생률은 96.6%에서 46.5%로 떨어진다(Plonka 2017 SR).

노출은 전부-아니면-전무가 아니라 용량-반응이며(노출 메쉬 1 cm²당 신생골 16.3% 감소, Cucchi 2017 RCT; ratio-of-means 0.62–0.65, Tay 2022 SR+MA), 맞춤형 기기 간에는 CAD/CAM 티타늄 메쉬가 강화 d-PTFE 메쉬 대비 치유합병증 비열등이다(8.3% vs 12.5%, Cucchi 2024 RCT).

## Summary

Across the collagen-membrane, synthetic-membrane, and titanium-mesh literature, the single recurring endpoint that predicts GBR outcome is **membrane exposure**. This page consolidates 17 papers into four decision axes. The body is English; confidence is tagged per proposition.

### Axis 1 — Membrane material & cross-linking

1. **Chemical cross-linking raises exposure risk without a clear bone-gain payoff.** Wessing 2018 SR+MA (20 controlled studies) found cross-linked membranes exposed ~30% more often than non-cross-linked, while collagen-membrane + particulate lateral GBR was effective overall and simultaneous vs subsequent implant placement gave similar survival. [확인 — SR+MA]
2. **Non-cross-linked resorbable membranes trend to higher bone gain + lower exposure.** Wessing 2016 multicenter RCT (creos xenoprotect/CXP vs reference) showed a non-significant trend favoring CXP on both bone gain and exposure at dehisced single-implant sites — directionally consistent with Axis-1 proposition 1, but interim. [확인 RCT, but interim/NS]
3. **Cross-linking by dehydrothermal (physical) route behaves benignly.** Lee 2015 RCT (n=28 dehiscence) found dehydrothermally cross-linked (DCM) vs non-cross-linked (NCM) collagen both improved clinical/CBCT parameters at 16 weeks; DCM showed slower degradation (residual leaflets) **without** the elevated exposure signal attributed to chemically cross-linked membranes — i.e. cross-linking *method* matters, not cross-linking per se. [확인 RCT]
4. **A synthetic gelatin/PLA membrane is non-inferior to a commercial PLA matrix.** Wang 2025 RCT (n=32) found GT/PLA vs Guidor® equal on bone resorption, osteogenic efficacy, exposure, and infection at 6 months — a viable low-cost alternative where collagen is the default. [확인 RCT, in single-tooth defects]

### Axis 2 — Membrane configuration

5. **Doubling the collagen layer does not improve preservation.** Choi 2017 randomized pilot (n=11 molars, open-healing ARP) found single- vs double-layer collagen membrane equivalent (width −1.7 vs −1.8 mm) — more membrane ≠ more protection. [확인, small pilot]
6. **Graft + resorbable membrane substantially beats natural healing.** Troiano 2018 SR+MA+TSA (7 studies) quantified allograft/xenograft + resorbable membrane vs natural healing at horizontal −2.19 mm and vertical −1.72 mm less resorption, TSA-confirmed. **Supersession note:** this wiki marks Troiano 2018 as `superseded_by` Canullo 2021 (SR + network meta-analysis, 88 RCTs / 2805 patients / 3073 sockets). The supersession is an *extension, not a reversal* for the proposition used here — Canullo 2021 confirms that all grafting materials significantly reduce horizontal and vertical shrinkage vs spontaneous healing, and adds what Troiano could not provide: a material ranking (xenograft and allograft ± bioactive agents best dimensionally) plus a dimensional-vs-histomorphometric trade-off. Troiano's −2.19 / −1.72 mm magnitudes therefore still stand as the pooled effect size, but material *choice* should be read off Canullo 2021, not this page. [확인 SR+MA+TSA; superseded-for-material-choice by SR+NMA]
7. **The "add another barrier layer" question recurs over titanium mesh — and the two evidence tiers disagree.** Mesh and Ti-reinforced d-PTFE are perforated to aid graft revascularization, which sacrifices cell occlusion; an overlying collagen membrane (CM) is meant to restore it. Yousefi-Koma 2025 SR (3 RCTs + 1 case series, 119 patients) found **no significant difference** in vertical bone gain or surgical/healing complication rate with vs without the CM. But Urban 2025 RCT (n=30), the central trial that SR pools, **failed to demonstrate non-inferiority of omitting the CM** (absolute vertical bone gain 4.1 ± 2.7 mm PTFE-alone vs 4.5 ± 2.1 mm PTFE+CM; complications 6.7% in both arms), and found more type-1 pseudo-periosteum with the CM. Read together with Choi 2017, the configuration axis is not a clean "more membrane ≠ more protection" rule: doubling a *collagen* layer over a socket buys nothing, but removing the CM from a *perforated* mesh is not yet supported. [확인 SR + RCT, conflicting; small evidence base]

### Axis 3 — Flap & incision design (the dominant exposure lever)

8. **Exposure is a flap-design problem, not only a membrane-material one.** Park 2007 prospective human study (25 patients, 29 dehiscence defects) showed incision location relative to the mucogingival junction and "surviving flap length" (vs adjacent keratinized-gingiva width) predicted early exposure; sites not re-covered by 1 month stayed exposed, and graft-alone flaps survived 1.6 mm farther beyond adjacent KG width than membrane-covered flaps (P=0.002) — the stiff/avascular membrane compromises overlying flap perfusion. [확인 prospective]
9. **Flap elevation costs keratinized tissue.** Lee 2018 SR+MA (11 studies) found flap vs flapless ARP equivalent on bone width/height, but flap elevation reduced keratinized gingiva width by 3.21 mm more — relevant because thin/reduced soft tissue is itself an exposure risk factor (Park 2007). [확인 SR+MA]
10. **Tension-free primary closure is a quantified technique, and the price of failing it is the largest number on this page.** Plonka 2017 SR (English literature 1990–2015) stratifies GBR flap designs by the advancement required for passive closure — minor (<3 mm), moderate (3–6 mm), major (≥7 mm) — and quantifies each maneuver's contribution: first vertical releasing incision +1.1 mm, second +1.9 mm, **periosteal releasing incision +5.5 mm** (the single largest gain). It also supplies the sharpest statement of this page's thesis: reported exposure rates 6.95–13.1%, roughly **6-fold more bone gain at non-exposed than exposed sites**, and **e-PTFE regeneration falling from 96.6% to 46.5% once exposed**. Vertical GBR complication rates (0–45.5%) far exceed horizontal (0–24%), and immediate implants open far more often (80%) than delayed (17–32%). This is the operative "how" behind the Axis-3 conclusion that Park 2007 and Lee 2018 establish descriptively. [확인 SR, narrative synthesis — no meta-analysis]

### Axis 4 — Titanium mesh & vertical augmentation

11. **For vertical gain, mesh customization buys fit, not height.** Ragucci 2025 SR+MA (22 studies, 608 patients, 1,318 implants) found customized CAD/CAM mesh (CTM) vs conventional mesh (TM) equal on vertical gain (5.14 vs 6.24 mm, NS), but CTM superior on horizontal gain (6.38 vs 3.85 mm, P=0.004) with an exposure trend favoring CTM (20.3% vs 30.9%, NS). [확인 SR+MA, mostly retrospective]
12. **At RCT level the customized-device choice is settled for complications, not for regeneration rate.** Ragucci 2025 is SR+MA but retrospective-heavy; Cucchi 2024 supplies the randomized evidence on the same clinical question with **healing complication rate as the primary endpoint** — precisely this page's failure mode. In this double-blind non-inferiority RCT (50 randomized, 48 treated, vertical defects >3 mm, both arms collagen-membrane-covered with 50:50 autogenous+DBBM), CAD/CAM Ti-mesh was non-inferior to customized Ti-reinforced d-PTFE mesh for healing complications (8.3% vs 12.5%), vertical bone gain did not differ (5.18 vs 5.79 mm, p=0.233), and osseointegration was equivalent (~98% both). PTFE was significantly better only on regeneration rate (99.5% vs 87.0%, p=0.013). [확인 RCT, double-blind non-inferiority]
13. **Exposure is dose-dependent, not all-or-none.** Cucchi 2017 pilot RCT (n=40, 108 simultaneous implants, posterior mandible) compared Ti-reinforced d-PTFE vs Ti mesh + cross-linked collagen membrane: vertical bone gain equivalent (4.2 vs 4.1 mm, p=0.58), healing complications comparable (15.0% vs 21.1%, p=0.69), but it quantified the exposure gradient — **a 16.3% deficit in new bone volume per cm² of exposed mesh**. d-PTFE had 3× fewer surgical complications (5.0% vs 15.8%, all transient paresthesia from Ti-mesh adaptation), while Ti mesh retains the revisability advantage (removable on late exposure + infection without losing the implant). This converts Tay 2022's ratio-of-means into an area-dose form and is the mechanistic bridge between Axis 3 and Axis 4. [확인 RCT, pilot]
14. **Mesh/membrane exposure directly cuts vertical bone yield.** Tay 2022 SR+MA (31 publications) found healing complications reduced vertical bone gain (ratio-of-means 0.62–0.65) though uncommon (~11% site-level) — exposure is not just a soft-tissue nuisance, it degrades the regenerative result. [확인 SR+MA]
15. **Customized Ti-mesh is the practical state of the art for non-space-maintaining vertical defects.** Chiapasco 2021 documented CAD/CAM customized titanium mesh GBR workflow; this is the substrate Ragucci 2025 later pooled. [확인 / technique]

### Decision synthesis (clinical bottom line)

| Question | Evidence-based default | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-linked vs non-cross-linked collagen at dehiscence? | Prefer non-cross-linked (or physically/DHT-cross-linked); avoid chemically cross-linked | Lee 2015: DHT route safe; chemical cross-link = Wessing 2018 exposure penalty |
| Single vs double membrane layer? | Single layer sufficient | Choi 2017 small pilot |
| Collagen membrane over a perforated mesh / Ti-reinforced d-PTFE? | Keep it — omission is not yet supported | Yousefi-Koma 2025 SR found no difference, but Urban 2025 RCT failed to show non-inferiority of omitting it |
| Biggest exposure lever? | Flap/incision design + keratinized tissue, not membrane brand | Park 2007, Lee 2018; Plonka 2017 quantifies the maneuvers |
| How much advancement does each maneuver buy? | Periosteal releasing incision +5.5 mm; vertical incisions +1.1 / +1.9 mm | Plonka 2017; stratify by required advancement (<3 / 3–6 / ≥7 mm) |
| Synthetic vs collagen membrane? | Synthetic GT/PLA acceptable alternative | Wang 2025, single-tooth defects only |
| ARP material choice (not just graft-vs-none)? | Read off the NMA, not Troiano | Canullo 2021 supersedes Troiano 2018 for material ranking; graft-vs-natural-healing direction unchanged |
| Vertical defect mesh choice? | CAD/CAM Ti-mesh non-inferior to reinforced d-PTFE for complications; CTM better horizontally; vertical gain mesh-agnostic | Cucchi 2024 RCT upgrades Ragucci 2025's retrospective-heavy pooling; PTFE better on regeneration rate only |
| How bad is an exposure, quantitatively? | Proportional to exposed area, and large | 16.3% new-bone deficit per cm² exposed mesh (Cucchi 2017); RoM 0.62–0.65 (Tay 2022); e-PTFE regeneration 96.6% → 46.5% (Plonka 2017) |

**The unifying thesis:** GBR success is gated by keeping the barrier *submerged and perfused*. Membrane chemistry sets a baseline exposure tolerance (non-cross-linked > chemically cross-linked), but the controllable surgical variables — incision location, flap survival length vs keratinized-gingiva width, and tension-free primary closure achieved through a stratified advancement technique (Plonka 2017) — dominate the exposure outcome. Exposure in turn caps the achievable bone gain, and it does so *proportionally to exposed area* rather than as a binary event: 16.3% less new bone per cm² of exposed mesh (Cucchi 2017), ratio-of-means 0.62–0.65 for complicated vs uncomplicated vertical GBR (Tay 2022), and regeneration collapsing from 96.6% to 46.5% for exposed e-PTFE (Plonka 2017). That dose-response is why partial early re-coverage is worth pursuing rather than writing the site off (Park 2007: sites not re-covered by 1 month stay exposed).
