## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 이 페이지는 턱관절장애(Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD) 통증을 두 축 — (a) 병인 연관(수면·치아상실·발치)과 (b) 치료 modality(저출력레이저 LLLT·경피신경자극 TENS·증식치료·인공관절치환) — 으로 8편을 종합한 근거 허브다. 단계별 결정은 [[overviews/tmd-management-evidence-ladder]]로 link-out.
> - **수면–TMD 연관이 가장 확고**: Albadi 2026 — TMD 환자 poor sleep ~90% vs 비TMD ~15%; Jiang 2024 — UK Biobank + 멘델무작위화(MR)로 **긴 수면시간이 만성 TMD 통증과 인과적 연관**(OR 6.77, 95% CI 1.73–26.55, p=.006). 단 급성이 아닌 **만성** 통증 특이적이고, 단기수면/불면 연관은 BMI·당뇨 보정 후 소실 → "poor sleep이 TMD 유발"은 실재하나 좁다.
> - **치아상실 vs TMD 역할 분리**: Jeon 2024(n=392) — 저작곤란 **중증도**는 치아상실/보철(후방결손 6.5개 vs 1.5개)이 좌우, TMD군은 오히려 **스트레스·불면 부담**이 큼(ISI≥15 13.6% vs 9.9%, 여성 77.4%). TMD는 기계적 교합문제라기보다 심리·수면 동반질환.
> - **발치–TMD, 가장 약하고 방향 충돌**: Aggarwal 2026 SR+MA — pooled RR 0.49(발치군 통증 51%↓)는 **치료적 발치**(간섭치 제거) 효과가 견인; 반대로 관찰코호트는 발치가 TMD 위험↑(Huang RR 1.6), 예방적 제3대구치 수술은 단기 이환(1주 개구제한 92%·근막통 69%). GRADE low-moderate → "발치로 턱통증 치료"는 특정 간섭치 있을 때만.
> - **치료 근거 최강 = 보존적 전기·광 치료**: Nemani 2024 RCT(12개월) — TMJ VAS TENS 7.76→1.05, LLL 7.61→0.87 vs 연성스플린트 7.53→4.14(TENS≈LLL, 둘 다 스플린트·대조 우월; 스플린트 = 무치료 수준). Desai 2021 이중맹검 RCT도 LLLT > sham 재확인.
> - **연성 스플린트 단독은 신뢰 낮음**: Nemani에서 스플린트 vs 대조 유의차 없음 — 단독요법 과신 경계.
> - **턱↔목 통증 분리**: Nemani — TMJ 치료가 경부통 해결 안 함(군간 차 없음, r −0.45~0.34). 목은 따로 봐야.
> - **후순위 modality**: 증식치료(Park, 후향 n=19: NRS 5.7→1.7, 연조직·골관절염형 모두 개선, 저위험)와 인공관절치환(Guzman, n=6: VAS 7→2, 16.7% 재수술)은 대규모 대조 없음 — 각각 난치성·말기 관절용 후순위 rung.
> - **임상 함의**: 보존 우선(LLLT/TENS 최강 근거·안전), 스플린트 단독 과신 금지, 만성·통증 TMD는 수면·스트레스 스크리닝(특히 여성), 증식치료는 난치성, 인공관절은 말기 — 발치는 특정 간섭치에 한정.

## Three-line Summary

Evidence hub over 8 papers organizing TMD along two axes — etiologic associations and treatment modalities: the **sleep–TMD association is best-supported**, corroborated from prevalence SR (Albadi: ~90% poor sleep in TMD vs ~15% non-TMD) to Mendelian-randomization causal evidence (Jiang: long sleep OR 6.77, 95% CI 1.73–26.55, p=.006, specific to *chronic* TMD pain, with short-sleep/insomnia signals dissolving after BMI/diabetes adjustment); tooth loss drives chewing-discomfort *severity* while TMD drives the *stress/insomnia* burden (Jeon, n=392); and the extraction–TMD link is weakest and direction-conflicted (Aggarwal SR+MA pooled RR 0.49 favoring extraction, but driven by therapeutic removal of interfering teeth, with observational cohorts showing extraction increasing risk; GRADE low-to-moderate).

For treatment, the strongest evidence is conservative electro-/photo-therapy: Nemani's 12-month RCT (n=48) shows TENS and LLLT roughly equivalent and clearly superior to soft splints (TMJ VAS TENS 7.76→1.05, LLL 7.61→0.87 vs soft splint 7.53→4.14; splint ≈ control), and Desai's double-blind RCT independently confirms LLLT beats sham; soft splint as sole therapy is unreliable.

Prolotherapy (Park, retrospective n=19: NRS 5.7→1.7, benefits both soft-tissue and osteoarthritic subtypes, low-risk) and customized total TMJ replacement (Guzman, n=6: VAS 7→2, 16.7% revision) show large pre/post gains but sit at the bottom of the evidence hierarchy — later rungs for refractory or end-stage disease; jaw treatment does not resolve concurrent cervical pain (Nemani: no between-group neck-pain difference, r −0.45 to 0.34).

## 세줄요약

TMD 통증을 병인연관(수면·치아상실·발치)과 치료modality(LLLT·TENS·증식치료·인공관절)로 종합한 8편 허브: 수면–TMD 연관이 가장 확고(Albadi poor sleep ~90% vs ~15%; Jiang MR 긴 수면 OR 6.77, 만성 통증 특이적), 치아상실은 저작곤란 중증도를·TMD는 스트레스/불면 부담을 좌우(Jeon), 발치–TMD는 가장 약하고 방향충돌(Aggarwal pooled RR 0.49지만 치료적 발치 효과가 견인, 관찰코호트는 위험↑, GRADE low-moderate).

치료 근거 최강은 보존적 전기·광 치료 — Nemani RCT(12개월) TMJ VAS TENS 7.76→1.05·LLL 7.61→0.87 vs 연성스플린트 7.53→4.14(TENS≈LLL 우월, 스플린트≈대조), Desai 이중맹검도 LLLT > sham; 연성 스플린트 단독은 신뢰 낮음.

증식치료(Park n=19 NRS 5.7→1.7, 저위험)·인공관절치환(Guzman n=6 VAS 7→2, 재수술 16.7%)은 대규모 대조 없어 난치성·말기용 후순위; 턱 치료가 경부통 해결 안 함(군간 무차이). 단계 결정은 tmd-management-evidence-ladder 참조.

## Summary

TMD care fails in two predictable ways: chasing occlusion when the driver is sleep/stress, and over-relying on a single appliance. This page synthesizes 8 previously-unsynthesized papers to separate **what is associated with TMD** (sleep, tooth loss, extraction) from **what actually treats it** (conservative electro/photo-therapy, then prolotherapy, then joint replacement). It is a companion hub to the stepwise [[overviews/tmd-management-evidence-ladder]] and the conservative-physiotherapy ladder — it supplies the etiologic screening logic and the modality head-to-heads.

The two load-bearing conclusions: **screen every chronic/painful TMD patient for sleep and stress** (the sleep link is causal, not just correlational, but specific to chronic pain), and **lead treatment with LLLT or TENS, not soft splints** (which performed no better than control in a 12-month RCT).

## Key Contributions

### A. Etiologic associations — ranked by evidence strength

| Association | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Sleep ↔ TMD** | Albadi SR (~90% vs ~15% poor sleep); Jiang MR long-sleep OR **6.77** (1.73–26.55, p=.006, FDR .024), chronic-pain-specific | **Best-supported**; causal for *chronic* TMD pain; short-sleep/insomnia signals vanish after BMI/DM adjustment; bruxism–/OSA–TMD inconsistent |
| **Tooth loss / chewing** | Jeon n=392: severe discomfort prosthetic 36.3% vs TMD 12.1%; missing posterior 6.5 vs 1.5 teeth (p<.001) | Tooth loss drives discomfort *severity*; TMD drives *stress/insomnia* burden (ISI≥15 13.6% vs 9.9%), 77.4% female |
| **Extraction ↔ TMD** | Aggarwal SR+MA: pooled RR **0.49** (0.31–0.66) favoring extraction, but therapeutic-extraction-driven; observational cohorts show extraction ↑ risk (Huang RR 1.6); prophylactic surgery 1-wk trismus 92%, myofascial pain 69% | **Weakest, direction-conflicted**; GRADE low-to-moderate; "extract to cure the jaw" justified only for a specific interfering/supraerupted tooth |

The clinical reframe: TMD is a psychophysiological/sleep-linked condition more than a mechanical-occlusion one (Jeon, Albadi, Jiang), and extraction is not a TMD therapy except in the narrow therapeutic case.

### B. Treatment modalities — ranked by evidence strength

| Modality | Study | Effect | Evidence tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LLLT** | Nemani RCT (12 mo); Desai double-blind sham RCT | TMJ VAS 7.61→0.87 (Nemani); LLLT > sham (Desai) | **Strongest** (2 RCTs, one blinded) |
| **TENS** | Nemani RCT | TMJ VAS 7.76→1.05; **TENS ≈ LLLT**, both > splint & control | **Strong** |
| **Soft splint** | Nemani RCT | 7.53→4.14; **NS vs control** | Unreliable as sole therapy |
| **Dextrose prolotherapy** | Park retrospective, n=19 | NRS 5.7→1.7 (dose-response); both soft-tissue & OA subtypes; low-risk | Low (uncontrolled) — refractory cases |
| **Total TMJ replacement** | Guzman case series, n=6 | VAS 7→2; function LTPS 6.5→3.5; 16.7% revision | Low (descriptive) — end-stage joints |

Two structural cautions from Nemani: soft splints performed no better than control (temper routine splint reliance), and TMJ-directed therapy did **not** resolve concurrent cervical pain (no between-group difference; TMJ↔cervical r −0.45 to 0.34) — the neck must be addressed separately.

## Methodology

Narrative synthesis (no new pooling). Base: 1 SR+MA (Aggarwal), 1 SR (Albadi), 1 biobank + Mendelian-randomization study (Jiang), 1 cross-sectional (Jeon), 2 RCTs (Desai abstract-only, Nemani), 1 retrospective series (Park), 1 case series (Guzman). Numbers quoted verbatim from source pages; Desai is abstract-only (direction captured, effect sizes not recoverable).

## Results

**Chairside TMD read:**

1. **Screen sleep and stress** in every chronic/painful TMD patient (PSQI/STOP-Bang, ISI) — the sleep link is causal for chronic pain; refer for sleep medicine / psychology when indicated. Especially in women.
2. **Lead with LLLT or TENS** — strongest, safest head-to-head evidence; roughly equivalent to each other.
3. **Do not rely on a soft splint as sole therapy** — no better than control at 12 months.
4. **Treat the neck separately** — jaw therapy does not fix cervical pain.
5. **Reserve dextrose prolotherapy** for conservative-refractory TMD (low-cost, low-risk, benefits OA and soft-tissue subtypes).
6. **Reserve total alloplastic joint replacement** for end-stage joints after conservative/minimally-invasive tiers fail.
7. **Extraction is not a TMD treatment** — justified only to remove a specific interfering/supraerupted tooth, never prophylactically for jaw pain.
