## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **논지(bottom line)**: 힐링 어버트먼트 (Healing Abutment, HA) 재사용 논쟁은 **두 개의 독립된 축**으로 나뉜다 — ①**청결도 축(cleanliness)**: 사용된 HA를 미사용 표면 (virgin surface)만큼 깨끗하게 만들 수 있는가? ②**생물학적 반응 축(biologic inertness)**: 깨끗하게 만들어도 숙주 염증을 유발하지 않는가? 위키 보유 8편으로 이 두 축과 임상 현황을 종합한다.
> - **제조사 입장**: 거의 모든 임플란트 제조사가 HA를 **단일사용(single-use, disposable)** 부품으로 지정한다. 근거로 든 것은 미세나사산 오염 잔존과 반복 처리에 의한 티타늄 표면 변화. 그러나 제조사 지침을 실제로 받은 임상의는 **4.7%뿐**(95.3% 미수령, Paganotto 2022).
> - **청결도 축 — SR 2편**: 어떤 통상적 프로토콜도 **100% 미사용 표면을 복원하지 못한다** (Eswaramurthy 2021, 8편 SR). 통상 세척(기계·화학·증기)은 종종 오염을 완전히 제거하지 못하나, 보조 요법을 더하면 충분해질 수 있고, **재사용에 의한 임상적 부작용(감염·골소실·실패) 보고는 0건** (Bidra 2019, 6편 관찰연구 SR).
> - **청결도 축 — 잔류물 정량**: 멸균은 세균을 완전 박멸하나(배양 음성) **표면 단백질은 남는다** — 재사용 HA 18.76 vs 신품 9.35 µg/mL(평균차 9.41, 95% CI 4.28–14.54; Burioni 2024). 선행연구 Wadhwani에서 표준 세척·멸균 후에도 **99%**가 단백/펩타이드 잔존.
> - **청결도 축 — 효과적 보조법**: 세 논문이 "특정 프로토콜이면 거의 미사용 표면"을 보인다 — ①**1% 차아염소산나트륨 (Sodium Hypochlorite, NaOCl)+초음파**로 성숙 바이오필름 **99.7%** 제거, SEM/EDX상 신품과 동등(Çetinsoy 2026); ②**NaOCl 또는 air polishing** 추가 시 body 표면 오염 최저(1.7–2.4% vs 5–6%, p<0.05; Naghsh 2024); ③**화학+전기화학** 병용 시 micro-gap·microleakage 없이 다회 재사용 가능(Kyaw 2023, RCT).
> - **잔존부위 패턴**: 오염은 **나사산(thread)·드라이버홀(driver hole/ADH)·커넥터 등 깊은 요철부**에 집중되고 occlusal 상면은 기하구조상 접근이 어려워 프로토콜 간 차이가 없다(Naghsh 2026 occlusal p=0.26). 즉 "닦기 쉬운 곳"이 아니라 "닦기 어려운 곳"이 재사용 안전성의 관건.
> - **생물학적 반응 축 — 핵심 반전**: 가장 잘 세정한 HA조차(Abreu 2024, D/E군) **대식세포 염증성 사이토카인(9종, 최대 5일)을 대조 대비 높게 유발** → 청결도와 생물학적 불활성은 **분리(decoupled)** 된다. 저자 결론은 이 세트에서 가장 강경한 "**재멸균·재세정해도 재사용하지 말라**".
> - **축 간 충돌(contradiction)**: Kyaw(청결도 축, RCT) "엄격 프로토콜이면 다회 재사용 OK" ↔ Abreu(생물학 축, in vitro) "깨끗해도 염증 유발 → 재사용 불가". 둘 다 옳을 수 있다 — 서로 **다른 종말점(endpoint)** 을 측정하기 때문. 이 충돌이 논쟁의 미해결 핵심.
> - **임상 현황(practice reality)**: 임플란트 전문의 **98.1%가 재사용**(비용 71.2%·편의성 26%), 94.5%는 환자에게 고지하지 않음, 53.3%는 한계를 못 느끼고 교차감염 위험 인식은 8.9%뿐(Paganotto 2022). 재사용은 이미 표준 관행이며 논쟁은 "할지 말지"가 아니라 "어떻게 안전하게"에 가깝다.
> - **근거 강도 주의**: 이 논쟁 전체가 **in vitro·survey·SR(of observational)** 로만 구성 — **임상 결과 연구(RCT of peri-implant outcomes)는 0건**. 잔류 오염↔임플란트주위질환의 인과는 미입증. "부작용 보고 없음"은 "안전 입증"과 다르다(Bidra의 경고).
> - **정책 결론(clinic SOP 관점)**: 재사용을 채택한다면 — (a) **다단계 프로토콜**(기계적 debridement → 1% NaOCl+초음파 ±air polishing 또는 전기화학 → autoclave), (b) **나사산·드라이버홀 육안+확대 검사**, (c) **재사용 횟수 제한**과 동일 환자 우선, (d) 심미부·중요 증례는 **신품 사용**, (e) 손상·마모·변색 HA 폐기, (f) **고지·동의(informed consent)** 고려. 궁극 안전성 판단은 향후 in vivo 염증·임상 결과 연구를 기다려야 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 8 papers framing the healing-abutment (HA) reuse debate as two independent axes: a cleanliness axis (can a used HA be restored to a virgin surface?) and a biologic-inertness axis (even if clean, does it avoid provoking host inflammation?); two SRs agree no routine protocol achieves 100% virgin surface; residual protein persists after sterilization (reused 18.76 vs new 9.35 µg/mL; Burioni 2024) and concentrates in thread/driver-hole recesses.

Specific adjuncts approach the virgin surface on the cleanliness axis: 1% NaOCl + ultrasonic removes 99.7% of mature biofilm (Çetinsoy 2026), air polishing reduces body-surface contamination to 1.7–2.4% (Naghsh 2024), combined chemical + electrochemical enables multiple cycles without micro-gap change (Kyaw 2023 RCT) — but Abreu 2024 decouples the axes: even the best-cleaned groups provoke macrophage cytokine secretion for up to 5 days, yielding the strictest conclusion ("do not reuse").

Manufacturers label HAs single-use yet 98.1% of implantologists reuse them (cost the primary driver, 71.2%) without informing patients (94.5%); crucially, zero clinical-outcome studies link reuse to peri-implant infection, bone loss, or failure, so the entire debate rests on in vitro and survey data — and the practically useful question is less "whether to reuse" than "under which protocol and with what disclosure."

## 세줄요약

힐링 어버트먼트 재사용 논쟁을 청결도 축(미사용 표면 복원 가능?)과 생물학적 반응 축(깨끗해도 염증 안 내나?)으로 분리한 8편 종합: 2편 SR은 어떤 통상 프로토콜도 100% 미사용 표면을 복원 못 하고, 멸균 후 잔류 단백질이 나사산·드라이버홀 요철부에 집중됨을 일치시킨다.

1% NaOCl + 초음파(바이오필름 99.7% 제거, Çetinsoy 2026), air polishing(Naghsh 2024), 화학+전기화학 병용(Kyaw 2023 RCT)은 청결도 축에서 거의 미사용 표면에 근접하나, Abreu 2024는 가장 잘 닦은 군조차 대식세포 염증성 사이토카인(9종, 최대 5일)을 유발해 두 축을 분리하며("재사용 불가") — Kyaw(청결도, 다회 재사용 가능)와 충돌하는 핵심 미해결 모순이다.

제조사는 단일사용 지정이지만 임상의 98.1%가 재사용하고(비용 71.2%), 94.5%는 환자에게 미고지; 임플란트주위 감염·골소실과 재사용을 연결한 임상 결과 연구는 0건 — 근거 전체가 in vitro·survey·관찰 SR이므로 "할지 말지"보다 "어떤 프로토콜·고지로"가 실질 질문이다.

## Thesis

**The reuse debate is not one argument but two, running on different endpoints, and most disagreements dissolve once the axes are separated.**

- **Axis 1 — Cleanliness (surface):** *Can a retrieved HA be decontaminated back to a virgin-like surface?* The two systematic reviews converge on "not completely with routine methods": Eswaramurthy 2021 found **no protocol achieved a 100% virgin surface**, and Bidra 2019 found 3 of 6 observational studies where routine mechanical/chemical/steam methods left contaminants. In vitro work quantifies what persists — Burioni 2024 shows sterilization eradicates *bacteria* (culture-negative) but not *protein* (reused 18.76 vs new 9.35 µg/mL), echoing the classic Wadhwani finding that 99% of standard-processed HAs retained protein. Critically, the residue concentrates in **threads, driver holes, and connectors**, while the occlusal top shows no between-protocol difference (Naghsh 2024, occlusal p=0.26) — the safety-limiting sites are the ones hardest to clean. But three studies show *specific adjuncts approach a virgin surface*: 1% NaOCl + ultrasonic removes **99.7%** of mature biofilm with SEM/EDX indistinguishable from unused (Çetinsoy 2026); NaOCl or glycine air-polishing minimize body-surface contamination (Naghsh 2024); and combined chemical + electrochemical cleaning permits multiple reuse cycles without micro-gap/microleakage change (Kyaw 2023, the highest-design study).

- **Axis 2 — Biologic inertness (host response):** *Even a well-cleaned HA — does it stay biologically silent?* Abreu 2024 is the pivot: the strategies that removed the most debris/protein (groups D/E) were **not** the ones that avoided inflammation — *every* cleaned group still drove high macrophage cytokine secretion (9 cytokines, up to 5 days) vs new controls. Cleanliness and inertness are **decoupled**. This yields the batch's most restrictive conclusion: do not reuse even after decontamination + sterilization.

- **The core unresolved contradiction:** Kyaw (cleanliness axis, RCT) concludes reuse is acceptable with a rigorous protocol; Abreu (biologic axis, in vitro) concludes reuse is not acceptable because cleanliness ≠ inertness. Both can be internally valid because they measure different endpoints. Neither is a clinical outcome.

- **The evidence ceiling:** the entire debate rests on in vitro, survey, and SR-of-observational data. **No study reports a clinical consequence** (peri-implant infection, bone loss, mechanical failure, implant loss) attributable to reuse (Bidra 2019). Absence of reported harm is not proof of safety — but it does bound how strongly a single-use mandate can be asserted from current evidence.

- **Practice reality vs manufacturer label:** manufacturers designate HAs single-use, yet **98.1%** of implantologists reuse them (cost 71.2%), **94.5%** do not tell patients, and **95.3%** never received manufacturer reuse guidance (Paganotto 2022). The clinically useful question is therefore less "reuse or not" and more "if reused, under what protocol and disclosure."

## Clinical Decision Points

**1. Which axis governs the decision?**
- If your concern is *cross-contamination / infection*: current evidence is reassuring — sterilization is reliably bactericidal (Burioni: culture-negative; Bidra: no reported infections). A rigorous multi-step protocol addresses this axis.
- If your concern is *residual denatured protein driving peri-implant inflammation*: evidence is unresolved and leans cautionary (Abreu: inflammation persists even when clean). This axis, not sterility, is the real basis of the single-use recommendation.

**2. If reusing, which protocol has the best cleanliness evidence?**
- Preferred adjuncts (each shown near-virgin on the cleanliness axis): **1% NaOCl + ultrasonic** (Çetinsoy, 99.7% biofilm removal) · **glycine air-polishing** (Naghsh) · **combined chemical + electrochemical** (Kyaw, RCT, best for repeated cycles).
- Baseline of mechanical debridement + ultrasonic + autoclave *alone* is repeatedly shown insufficient (Naghsh control 6.1%; Çetinsoy autoclave-only 54.4% debridement).
- Inspect the **thread, driver hole, and connector** recesses under magnification — the occlusal surface is not the limiting site.

**3. How many reuse cycles / which cases?**
- NaOCl-only degrades the implant–HA interface with repeated use (Kyaw: worsening micro-gap/microleakage) — limit cycles unless using a surface-preserving protocol (electrochemical).
- Prefer **same-patient** reuse; use **new HAs in esthetic/critical cases**; discard any HA with visible wear, corrosion, or thread damage.

**4. Ethics / disclosure.**
- 94.5% of clinicians do not inform patients (Paganotto). Given the single-use labeling and unresolved biologic-response data, consider **informed consent / disclosure** as a defensible default.

## Gaps & Future Research

- **No clinical-outcome study exists** linking reused HAs to peri-implant infection, marginal bone loss, soft-tissue inflammation, or implant failure. This is the single highest-value gap — every current recommendation is extrapolated from bench/survey data.
- **Abreu's in vitro inflammation finding needs in vivo confirmation**: does macrophage cytokine secretion translate to clinically meaningful peri-implant mucositis/soft-tissue outcomes?
- **Protocol standardization**: adjuncts that work (NaOCl, air-polishing, electrochemical) are studied in isolation; no head-to-head consensus protocol or dose/time/cycle-limit standard exists (residency programs each use a distinct approach).
- **Surface durability across cycles**: how many decontamination cycles before titanium surface oxidation/porosity meaningfully increases biofilm retention (Paganotto's mechanism) remains unquantified.
- **Material scope**: nearly all data are titanium; zirconia/PEEK healing abutment reuse is essentially unstudied.
