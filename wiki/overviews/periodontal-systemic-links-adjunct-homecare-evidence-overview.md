> - **치주염–당뇨 연관은 고확신·양방향**: Arbildo-Vega 2024(umbrella, SR 18편) — pooled RR ~1.26–1.86, OR ~1.69–2.59; 혈당조절 불량 당뇨는 치주염 위험 **86%↑**. 당뇨환자는 치태·치은·부착소실(CAL)·치주낭·출혈(BOP) 모두 악화. AMSTAR-2 18편 중 13편 high.
> - **구강-전신의 중심 노드 = 치주염**: Botelho 2022(umbrella, MA 293개, Nature Comm) — 855개 비교 중 강한근거 66개, 그중 치주염이 암(MA 9)·심혈관(5)·당뇨(3)·임신부작용·신경퇴행 등과 연관. FSN 97.5%가 향후 뒤집힐 가능성 낮음. **단 66개 중 중재수준은 단 2개** — 연관이지 인과 아님.
> - **근거질의 공통 한계**: 전신 연관은 압도적으로 관찰연구, AMSTAR-2 낮음(Botelho high/moderate 10.3%뿐, 71.4% critically-low). 강하지만 associational로 읽어야.
> - **보조요법은 근거 약함·효과 작음**: 프로바이오틱스(Mendonça 2024 umbrella SR 30편) — 단기(≤3개월) PPD/CAL/BOP 소폭 개선(CAL +0.20mm, PPD -0.31mm, BOP -3%)이나 6개월엔 소실, 임플란트 주위엔 무효, 경성결과(골소실·생존) 데이터 없음 → "권고 확정 불가". *L. reuteri*가 가장 일관.
> - **레스베라트롤(Bandiaky 2026 SR 22편)**: 전임상 골보호 신호 강함(치조골소실 -7~60%)이나 인간 RCT는 소규모·이질·메타분석 불가·**인간 골소실 데이터 0**. NSPT 보조로만, 단독 아님. 최대효과는 침습성 치주염(Stage III–IV grade C).
> - **치약은 유일하게 실행적**: Suvan 2025(umbrella 72편) — "모든 치약이 동등"은 오해; 19개 활성성분 중 6개만 표적조건에 유의효과, 불소 6종도 효능 편차. **환자 상태별 맞춤 선택**. 단 초록전용.

## Three-line Summary

Roundup hub over 5 papers (3 umbrella reviews + 2 SRs) on periodontal care's two axes — systemic links and adjunct/home-care: the **periodontitis–diabetes link is high-confidence and bidirectional** (Arbildo-Vega 2024, 18 SRs: pooled RR ~1.26–1.86, OR ~1.69–2.59; 86% higher periodontitis risk in poorly-controlled diabetes; worse plaque/gingival/CAL/PD/BOP), and periodontitis is the central node of oral-systemic disease (Botelho 2022, 293 MAs / 855 comparisons: 66 strong associations spanning cancer/CVD/diabetes/pregnancy/neurodegeneration, robust to future nulls — but **only 2 of 66 are intervention-level**, so associational not causal).

Adjunct/home-care therapies carry weaker, smaller-effect evidence: probiotics (Mendonça 2024, 30 SRs) give only short-term (≤3 mo) sub-clinical gains that vanish by 6 months (CAL +0.20 mm, PPD −0.31 mm, BOP −3%), no peri-implant benefit and no hard-outcome data (explicit "no definitive recommendation"; *L. reuteri* most consistent); resveratrol (Bandiaky 2026, 22 studies) has a strong preclinical bone-protection signal (ABL −7 to −60%) but small, un-poolable human RCTs and zero human bone-loss data, useful only as a host-modulatory NSPT adjunct.

Toothpaste is the one clearly actionable home-care message (Suvan 2025, 72 reviews): "all toothpastes are equivalent" is a misconception — only 6 of 19 active ingredients show significant benefit for their targeted condition (with efficacy variability even among 6 fluoride types), so product selection should be condition-tailored; evidence quality is the shared caveat throughout (AMSTAR-2 weak — Botelho only 10.3% high/moderate, 71.4% critically-low — and adjuncts limited by heterogeneity and absent hard outcomes). Specialized adjunct decisions live in the linked host-modulation and probiotics/PDT overviews.

## Summary

Two questions dominate periodontal counseling: *how much does this patient's systemic health matter, and do the adjuncts on the shelf actually work?* This page synthesizes 5 previously-unsynthesized papers to answer both, and to keep the answers calibrated to evidence quality. It links out to the dedicated adjunct overviews rather than restating them.

The pattern is a mismatch between strength and actionability: the **systemic links are strong but associational** (you cannot yet claim treating gums fixes systemic disease — only 2 of 66 strong findings were intervention-level), while the **adjuncts are actionable but weak** (probiotics and resveratrol are supportive-only, short-term, and lack hard outcomes). The one crisp, do-this-tomorrow message is toothpaste: match the active ingredient to the condition rather than assuming equivalence.

## Key Contributions

### A. Periodontal–systemic links (strong, associational)

| Paper | Scope | Magnitude | Quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arbildo-Vega 2024 | Umbrella, 18 SRs / 296 primaries (PD↔DM) | RR 1.26–1.86, OR 1.69–2.59; poorly-controlled DM +86% periodontitis risk; worse plaque/GI/CAL/PD/BOP | AMSTAR-2 13/18 high, but 17.26% overlap; bidirectional asserted not quantified |
| Botelho 2022 | Umbrella, 293 MAs / 855 comparisons (oral-systemic) | 66 strong associations (cancer 9, CVD 5, DM 3, pregnancy 2, neurodegeneration 3); FSN 97.5% robust | **Only 2/66 intervention-level**; AMSTAR-2 10.3% high/moderate, 71.4% critically-low; observational |

Read together: periodontitis is the hub of oral-systemic disease and the diabetes edge is quantified and robust — but the causal/interventional claim is not yet earned. Justifiable to screen periodontitis patients for systemic risk and to co-manage diabetics; not justifiable to promise systemic cure via periodontal therapy.

### B. Adjunct / home-care therapies (actionable, weaker)

| Adjunct | Paper | Effect | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Probiotics | Mendonça 2024 (30 SRs) | Short-term only: CAL +0.20 mm, PPD −0.31 mm, BOP −3% at 3 mo, NS at 6 mo; no peri-implant benefit; no hard outcomes | Conflicting; NSPT adjunct at best; *L. reuteri* most consistent |
| Resveratrol | Bandiaky 2026 (16 animal + 6 RCT) | Preclinical ABL −7 to −60%; RCTs positive but un-poolable; **zero human bone-loss data** | Supportive host-modulatory adjunct; best in aggressive (Stage III–IV C) PD |
| Toothpaste | Suvan 2025 (72 reviews) | 6 of 19 active ingredients significant for targeted condition; fluoride efficacy varies across 6 types | **Actionable**: individualize product to the condition |

Neither probiotics nor resveratrol replaces non-surgical periodontal therapy (NSPT); both are supportive and short-horizon. Toothpaste selection is the one home-care lever with a clear, individualized recommendation.

## Methodology

Narrative synthesis (no new pooling). Base: 3 umbrella reviews (Arbildo-Vega, Botelho, Mendonça), 2 SRs (Bandiaky narrative, Suvan abstract-only). Effect sizes quoted verbatim; AMSTAR-2/JBI grades reported to expose certainty limits. Suvan is abstract-only (ingredient counts captured, effect sizes not).

## Results

**Chairside periodontal read:**

1. **Diabetics** — recognize elevated periodontitis risk (poorly-controlled +86%); co-manage bidirectionally (periodontal therapy is one of the 2 intervention-level oral-systemic effects, reducing systemic inflammation).
2. **Periodontitis patients** — systemic screening/awareness is evidence-based (cancer/CVD/diabetes/pregnancy/neurodegeneration associations), but frame it as association, not proven causation.
3. **Probiotics** — optional short-term NSPT adjunct (*L. reuteri*); do not expect sustained or peri-implant benefit.
4. **Resveratrol** — investigational host-modulatory adjunct, strongest rationale in aggressive periodontitis; not standalone.
5. **Toothpaste** — do not treat as interchangeable; match the active ingredient (fluoride type, desensitizer, antimicrobial) to the patient's dominant condition.
6. **Weight all systemic-link numbers as robust-but-associational, and all adjunct numbers as small-effect/short-term.**
