## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 투명교정(Clear Aligner, CA)은 치근 흡수(External Apical Root Resorption, EARR)를 유발하지만, 평균 −0.56 mm로 일상적 증례에서는 임상적 의미가 없다; 고정식 교정장치(Fixed Appliance, FA) 대비 유의하게 적다(MD −0.47~−0.50 mm, p<0.001).
> - **절대 EARR 정량**: Butsabul 2024 SR+MA (CBCT 9편, 638명, 6524치) — 전체 절치 평균 −0.56 mm (95% CI −0.73~−0.38, p<0.00001); 상악 중절치 최대 −0.74 mm; 상악 절치 치근부피 −13.34 mm³; 심각 흡수(>20%) 단 3.7–6.3% 치아.
> - **CA vs FA 비교 1**: Yildirim 2026 SR+MA (CBCT 6편, 392명) — CA가 FA보다 EARR 유의하게 적음 (MD −0.50 mm, 95% CI −0.79~−0.21, p<0.001, I²=60.8%, GRADE 낮음).
> - **CA vs FA 비교 2**: Samulak 2026 SR+MA (CBCT 코호트 4편, 1204 전치) — CA WMD −0.47 mm vs FA −0.54 mm; 군간 차이 유의 (p<0.01); 비발치 상악 중절치에서 CA 우위 가장 두드러짐.
> - **고위험 치아**: 상악 중절치 ≥ 상악 측절치 ≥ 하악 중절치 — 이 3군에서만 통계적 유의 흡수; 견치·하악 측절치는 흡수 추세이나 비유의.
> - **고위험 이동**: 압하(intrusion)·정출(extrusion)·발치(extraction)·중증 총생·치근단-구개피질골 근접 → EARR 증폭인자.
> - **진단 도구**: CBCT가 2D 방사선보다 정확 — 2D는 약 +0.2 mm 과대추정; CBCT 없이 정확한 흡수량 정량화 불가.
> - **임상 임계값**: 절대 −0.56 mm는 임상적으로 비의미; 그러나 ≥2 mm이면 모니터링·치료계획 재검토 권고.
> - **치료 선택 함의**: EARR 위험 증가 환자(가늘거나 짧은 치근, 과거 흡수력, 파이펫형 치근) → CA 선호; 평균 위험 환자는 치료 유형으로 치료 결정 불필요.
> - **근거 한계**: 3개 SR+MA 모두 GRADE 낮음; 비무작위 연구 지배적; 발치 아군은 단일 연구(탐색적만); 장기 추적(≥5년) RCT 없음.
> - **임상 takeaway**: "CA는 FA보다 치근흡수 적지만 0이 아니다 — 중절치 집중 모니터링 + 압하·발치 계획 시 CBCT 베이스라인 + 심각 흡수 환자는 FA보다 CA 선호".

## Three-line Summary

Three CBCT-based SR+MAs (Butsabul 2024: k=9, n=638; Yildirim 2026: k=6, n=392; Samulak 2026: k=4, 1204 anterior teeth) converge on the finding that clear aligner (CA) therapy causes statistically and clinically minimal root resorption (−0.56 mm overall; maxillary central incisor worst at −0.74 mm; severe >20% loss affects only 3.7–6.3% of teeth) and is associated with significantly less EARR than fixed appliances (MD −0.47 to −0.50 mm, p<0.001).

The absolute CA EARR is not clinically meaningful in routine cases, but specific risk amplifiers — vertical movements (intrusion/extrusion), extraction protocols, severe crowding correction, and root-apex proximity to palatal cortex — elevate individual tooth risk above the population mean and warrant CBCT monitoring.

EARR risk at treatment planning is actionable: patients with thin/short roots, pipette root morphology, or prior resorption history should prefer CA over fixed appliances; CBCT provides the only accurate quantification tool (2D radiography overestimates by ~0.2 mm).

## 세줄요약

CBCT 기반 SR+MA 3편 수렴: CA 유발 EARR 평균 −0.56 mm (상악 중절치 −0.74 mm; 심각 흡수 3.7–6.3%만), FA 대비 유의하게 적음 (MD −0.47~−0.50 mm, p<0.001); 임상적으로 일상 증례에선 비의미.

고위험 인자(압하·발치·중증 총생·피질골 근접)는 개별 치아 위험을 집단 평균 이상으로 증폭; 이 경우 CBCT 베이스라인 기록 필요.

치료 선택: 고위험 치근형태(세거나 짧은, 파이펫형, 과거 흡수력) → CA 선호; 평균 위험에서는 EARR 만을 이유로 치료 유형 변경 불필요; CBCT만이 정확한 정량화 도구.

## 1. Rationale for This Synthesis

Root resorption is one of the most commonly cited orthodontic risks in patient counselling. With three independent SR+MAs published between 2024–2026 using CBCT-standardized measurements, sufficient evidence now exists to synthesize the absolute magnitude of CA-induced EARR, the CA-vs-FA comparative advantage, and the tooth-type and movement-specific risk hierarchy into a single clinical reference.

This synthesis extends [[clear-aligner-adverse-effects-overview]] which covers adverse effects broadly; this page focuses specifically on the quantified root resorption evidence and its clinical risk-stratification implications.

## 3. Absolute EARR in Clear Aligner Therapy (Butsabul 2024)

The critical methodological contribution of Butsabul 2024 is isolation of the **absolute** EARR caused by CA alone (not a relative comparison to FA), addressing the confound that prior reviews faced when comparing two techniques with different indications and treatment durations.

**Tooth-level findings:**
| Tooth | Root length change | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Overall incisors | −0.56 mm (95% CI −0.73~−0.38) | p<0.00001 |
| Maxillary central incisor | −0.74 mm | Significant |
| Maxillary lateral incisor | Significant | Significant |
| Mandibular central incisor | Significant | Significant |
| Canines | Trend only | NS |
| Mandibular lateral incisor | Trend only | NS |

**Root volume loss (3D sub-analysis, 3 studies):**
- Upper incisor volume: −13.34 mm³ (95% CI −16.57~−10.10; p<0.00001)
- Volume confirms central > lateral incisor pattern

**Severe resorption threshold:** Only 3.7–6.3% of teeth exceed the 20% root-length-loss criterion — confirming that individual severe events exist but are uncommon in the treated population.

**Clinical interpretation:** The 0.56 mm mean loss is not clinically meaningful by established thresholds. However, every patient had at least one detectable resorption event, supporting pre-treatment CBCT baseline and periodic monitoring for high-risk individuals.

## 4. Clear Aligner vs Fixed Appliance Comparison

Two independent SR+MAs (Yildirim 2026, Samulak 2026) converge on the same directional finding despite using slightly different study populations:

**Convergence across SR+MAs:**
- Yildirim 2026: MD −0.50 mm (CA vs FA, in favor of CA), p<0.001
- Samulak 2026: WMD difference ~−0.07 mm (CA −0.47 vs FA −0.54), p<0.01
- Both maintain significance after sensitivity analyses (Yildirim: leave-one-out range −0.41 to −0.60 mm)

**Note on discordant RCT:** A smaller RCT (Bespalez-Neto 2026) found no CA-vs-FA EARR difference. Contextual interpretation: smaller RCT power, heterogeneous case selection, and AI-3D volumetric vs linear CBCT landmark measurement explain discordance without true biological contradiction.

**Subgroup signal:** Yildirim 2026 extraction-protocol subgroup (single study only): MD −0.95 mm — substantially larger than non-extraction, hypothesis-generating for high-biomechanical-risk protocols.

## 5. Risk Factor Hierarchy

Based on convergent evidence across all three SR+MAs:

**Tooth-level risk (highest to lowest):**
1. Maxillary central incisor (highest EARR in every study)
2. Maxillary lateral incisor
3. Mandibular central incisor
4. Mandibular lateral incisor / canines (below statistical threshold)

**Treatment-level risk amplifiers:**
- **Intrusion/extrusion** — vertical root displacement increases PDL stress and hyalinization
- **Extraction space closure** — root apex movement over longer distance through bone
- **Severe crowding correction** — larger planned tooth displacement
- **Root-apex proximity to palatal cortex** — mechanical compression risk
- **Long treatment duration** — cumulative force exposure

**Mitigating factors (inconsistent evidence across studies):**
- Age (inconsistent across studies)
- Sex (inconsistent)
- Treatment duration (duration inconsistent; magnitude more important than time)

## 6. Clinical Decision Framework

### 6.1 When is EARR a treatment-selection factor?

**CA preferred over FA when:**
- Patient has elevated EARR risk profile: thin/short roots, pipette (blunt) root morphology, prior resorption history, genetic predisposition
- Anterior vertical movement (intrusion) is in the treatment plan
- Patient values reduced iatrogenic risk and EARR data is a primary concern

**CA and FA equivalent for EARR when:**
- Standard risk profile (no prior resorption, normal root morphology)
- Non-extraction, non-intrusion treatment plan
- Average treatment duration

**EARR should NOT be the sole factor driving treatment-type selection** in average-risk patients — the 0.07–0.50 mm absolute difference between CA and FA is statistically real but clinically small.

### 6.2 Monitoring Protocol

| Risk level | Baseline imaging | Mid-treatment | Post-treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | Periapical OK | Periapical at 12–18 months | Final periapical |
| High risk (root morphology, vertical movements) | CBCT | CBCT at 12 months | CBCT final |
| Extraction / intrusion protocol | CBCT | CBCT at 9–12 months | CBCT + consider pausing |

### 6.3 Imaging Standard

CBCT is the only method that accurately quantifies root resorption:
- 2D radiography overestimates EARR by approximately +0.2 mm (Butsabul 2024)
- Panoramic/periapical are acceptable for screening but not quantification
- AI-based 3D root volume measurement (Bespalez-Neto 2026) represents an emerging alternative but lacks SR-level comparative validation

## 7. Research Gaps

1. **No long-term (≥5 yr) RCT**: All included studies are non-randomized cohorts; GRADE certainty limited to low for all SR+MAs
2. **Extraction protocol EARR**: single-study signal (MD −0.95 mm) — needs confirmatory multi-study analysis
3. **EARR during refinement phases**: does additional aligner series compound EARR? No data
4. **Severe EARR management**: no systematic review on clinical management protocols when >20% root loss is detected mid-treatment
5. **AI-CBCT concordance with linear CBCT**: discordance between Bespalez-Neto (3D volumetric AI) and linear-CBCT SR+MAs needs methodological reconciliation
