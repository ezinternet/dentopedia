## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 문제**: 유치(primary tooth) 발수술(pulpectomy) 3가지 임상 결정 — ① 치근관내 약제(intracanal medicament) 선택, ② 충전재(obturation material) 선택, ③ 치수 상태가 애매할 때(equivocal pulp status) 절단술(pulpotomy) vs 발수술 중 무엇을 할 것인가.
> - **축 1 — 매복제(medicament)**: 2% 염화클로르헥시딘(Chlorhexidine, CHX) 겔 ≈ RG-Dent(메트로니다졸·요오드포름·덱사메타손)  >  수산화칼슘(Calcium Hydroxide, Ca(OH)₂). CHX 4.94 logCFU 감소, RG-Dent 4.77 logCFU — 차이 0.17로 임상적으로 동등. Ca(OH)₂ 3.69 logCFU로 유의하게 열등(효과크기 d>4). 핵심: **7일 교체 관행은 근거 없음 — 2일로 충분**.
> - **축 2 — 충전재(obturation)**: 전체 분석(SR+MA 15편, 1,669치)에서는 Ca(OH)₂-요오드포름(Vitapex/Metapex) vs ZOE 계열 차이 없음. 그러나 **고품질 연구 2편만 보면 ZOE 계열이 12개월 이상에서 유의하게 우월** (임상 OR 0.21, 방사선 OR 0.10). 재흡수 예정 치아 → Vitapex, 장기 잔존 치아 → ZOE.
> - **축 3 — 진단 불명확 치수(equivocal pulp)**: 5–10분 지속 출혈 또는 방사선 소견 불명확 → 치수절단술(iRoot BP Plus, 86.4%) > 발수술(ZOE-요오드포름, 67.1%) at 24개월(p<0.001). **단, 무증상(asymptomatic)일 때만** (HR=0.42); 유증상에서는 차이 없음(HR=0.88, p=0.41).
> - **실패 감시**: 절단술 후 → 내부 흡수(internal resorption) 주시; 발수술 후 → 외부 흡수(external resorption) 주시. 불완전한 기성금속관(Stainless Steel Crown, SSC) 봉합이 독립적 최대 실패 위험(HR=3.12).
> - **증거 등급 주의**: 충전재 SR+MA는 고품질 연구 2편뿐; 절단술·발수술 비교는 단일기관 후향적 코호트; 매복제는 in-vitro. 임상 적용 시 등급 한계 인지 필수.
> - **기존 의사결정 지도와 연계**: [[overviews/primary-molar-caries-pulp-restoration-decision-tree]]는 가역·비가역 분기를 다루나, 매복제 선택·충전재 선택·진단 불명확 치수 처치는 다루지 않음 — 이 페이지가 그 세 영역을 보완.
> - **실천 요약**: 애매한 유구치 → 무증상이면 bioceramic(아이루트 BP 플러스) 절단술 + SSC 완전 봉합 우선; 발수술 선택 시 장기 잔존 치아는 ZOE, 탈락 임박 치아는 Vitapex; 치근관 약제는 CHX 또는 RG-Dent 2일, Ca(OH)₂ 단독 회피.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of three complementary evidence sources (in-vitro factorial study, SR+MA, retrospective propensity-matched cohort) addressing three under-resolved decision axes in primary tooth pulp management: intracanal medicament choice against E. faecalis, obturation material selection for pulpectomy, and treatment modality for equivocal (diagnostically indeterminate) pulp status.

CHX and RG-Dent both substantially outperform Ca(OH)₂ as intracanal medicaments (4.94 and 4.77 vs 3.69 logCFU reduction; d>4; 2-day dwell sufficient); ZOE-based obturating materials outperform Ca(OH)₂/iodoform paste only in high-quality studies at ≥12 months (clinical OR 0.21, radiographic OR 0.10); and pulpotomy (iRoot BP Plus) significantly outperforms pulpectomy (ZOE-iodoform) for equivocal asymptomatic primary molars (86.4% vs 67.1% at 24 months, p<0.001) but not for symptomatic ones.

The overarching clinical principle is symptom-driven de-escalation: asymptomatic uncertainty favors tooth-preserving pulpotomy over pulpectomy; when pulpectomy is performed, ZOE outperforms Vitapex for teeth expected to function long-term; Ca(OH)₂ should not be used as the sole intracanal medicament regardless of dwell duration.

## 세줄요약

유치 치수 처치에서 해결되지 않은 세 가지 결정 축(치근관내 약제 선택, 발수 충전재 선택, 진단 불명확 치수의 처치 방법)을 in-vitro 이원 요인 연구·SR+MA·후향적 성향점수매칭 코호트 세 편으로 종합한 합성 페이지다.

CHX·RG-Dent가 Ca(OH)₂보다 현저히 우수한 매복제이며(4.94·4.77 vs 3.69 logCFU 감소, d>4, 2일 충분); ZOE 계열 충전재는 고품질 연구에서만 12개월 이상 Ca(OH)₂-요오드포름보다 유의하게 우월(임상 OR 0.21)하고; 진단 불명확 무증상 유구치에서는 치수절단술(86.4%)이 발수술(67.1%)을 유의하게 앞선다(p<0.001).

과도 진단과 과도 처치를 모두 피하는 핵심은 증상 상태이며, 무증상 불명확 치아는 생체세라믹 절단술로 보존하고, 발수가 필요할 때는 탈락 시기 기반으로 재료를 선택하며, Ca(OH)₂ 단독 매복은 회피한다.

## Summary

Three papers converge on a unified picture of primary tooth root canal management across three decision nodes that the existing overview ([[overviews/primary-molar-caries-pulp-restoration-decision-tree]]) does not address in depth: which intracanal medicament to use, which obturating material to choose for pulpectomy, and what to do when the pulp status is genuinely ambiguous.

**The starting premise**: Standard AAPD guidelines set a ≤5-minute hemostasis criterion for pulpotomy, but clinical reality often produces cases that fall outside this boundary — prolonged bleeding that stops before 10 minutes, radiographic findings that could represent physiologic or pathologic change, or a child whose history and clinical signs are discordant. These equivocal cases previously had no high-quality evidence base.

**The three axes are clinically interdependent**: the decision to perform pulpotomy vs pulpectomy determines whether obturation material matters; the choice of obturation material determines whether intracanal medicament plays a role. Together they form a nested decision hierarchy — Axis 3 upstream, Axis 2 mid-stream, Axis 1 downstream.

## Axis 1 — Intracanal Medicament: CHX vs RG-Dent vs Ca(OH)₂

Source: [[endodontics/hasan-2026-rgdent-chx-caoh2-efaecalis-invitro]] (in-vitro, n=60 premolars, 21-day E. faecalis infection model)

### Background

*Enterococcus faecalis* (E. 파에칼리스) is the dominant persistent pathogen in root-canal-treated teeth. Ca(OH)₂ is the historical standard intracanal medicament despite known limitations against this organism. RG-Dent (metronidazole/iodoform/dexamethasone) is widely used in pediatric dentistry but had lacked rigorous comparative evidence.

### Comparison Table — logCFU Reduction (pooled across dwell durations)

| Medicament | logCFU Reduction | vs Ca(OH)₂ (Δ) | Cohen's d | vs CHX (Δ) | Cohen's d |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2% CHX gel | 4.94 ± 0.23 | +1.25 | 5.08 (very large) | — | — |
| RG-Dent | 4.77 ± 0.26 | +1.09 | 4.13 (very large) | −0.17 | 0.69 (medium) |
| Ca(OH)₂ | 3.69 ± 0.27 | (reference) | — | −1.25 | 5.08 |

Two-way ANOVA: medicament type explains 87% of variance (η²=0.869, p<0.001). Dwell-time extension 2→7 days: no significant effect (p=0.78, η²=0.001, no interaction).

### Key Messages

- CHX and RG-Dent are statistically equivalent for practical purposes: the CHX advantage is real but small (p=.036, d=0.69, post-hoc power only 56%).
- Ca(OH)₂ is clearly inferior against mature E. faecalis biofilm (d>4 for both comparisons).
- The 7-day dwell convention has no bactericidal justification — 2-day protocol is adequate, supporting shorter inter-appointment intervals.
- RG-Dent's advantage over Ca(OH)₂ is clinically important: it lacks minocycline (no tooth discoloration risk) and is widely stocked in pediatric practices.

### Limitation

In-vitro model (premolars, not primary teeth); mature 21-day biofilm may not replicate every clinical scenario; n=10 per subgroup limits precision of CHX vs RG-Dent sub-comparison.

## Axis 2 — Obturation Material: Ca(OH)₂/Iodoform (Vitapex/Metapex) vs ZOE

Source: [[endodontics/najjar-2019-caoh2-iodoform-vitapex-vs-zoe-pulpectomy-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 15 studies, 1,669 primary teeth)

### Background

Two material families dominate primary tooth pulpectomy: Ca(OH)₂/iodoform (상품명: Vitapex, Metapex) — resorbable, popular for its presumed physiologic exfoliation compatibility — and ZOE (Zinc Oxide Eugenol, 산화아연유지놀) or ZOE/iodoform composites. The overall pooled evidence showed no difference, masking a quality-dependent signal.

### Comparison Table — Clinical and Radiographic Success by Study Quality

| Time-point | Study tier | n (teeth) | Clinical OR | p-value | Radiographic OR | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 months | Overall | pooled | 1.84 (0.51–6.61) | 0.35 | 0.71 (0.34–1.51) | 0.38 |
| 12 months | Overall | pooled | 0.78 (0.21–2.88) | 0.07 | 0.39 (0.07–2.35) | 0.31 |
| 12 months | High-quality only | 2 studies | significantly favors ZOE | p<0.05 | significantly favors ZOE | p<0.05 |
| ≥18 months | Overall | pooled | 0.52 (0.17–1.59) | 0.25 | 0.13 (0.06–1.61) | 0.16 |
| ≥18 months | High-quality only | 2 studies | 0.21 (0.07–0.69) | **0.010** | 0.10 (0.02–0.45) | **0.003** |

OR is expressed as Ca(OH)₂/iodoform vs ZOE — OR <1 favors ZOE (lower failure odds with ZOE).

### Exfoliation-Timing Decision Rule

| Clinical scenario | Recommended material | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Primary tooth nearing physiologic exfoliation | Ca(OH)₂/iodoform (Vitapex/Metapex) | Resorbable — does not interfere with successor eruption |
| Primary tooth expected to remain ≥12 months | ZOE or ZOE/iodoform+Ca(OH)₂ | High-quality evidence supports superior long-term success |

### Key Messages

- Pooling all studies (mostly moderate-quality) hides a real signal: high-quality studies consistently favor ZOE beyond 12 months.
- The magnitude is clinically meaningful: radiographic OR 0.10 at ≥18 months means Ca(OH)₂/iodoform has roughly 10× the odds of radiographic failure compared to ZOE in high-quality studies.
- Heterogeneity is substantial at 12+ months (I²=45–62%), indicating important clinical variation not fully captured.
- Publication bias detected for radiographic success at ≥18 months (Egger p=0.02) — pooled estimates may slightly overstate Ca(OH)₂/iodoform performance.

### Limitation

Only 2 high-quality studies (modified CONSORT ≥19/27 points) underlie the key quality-stratified finding; definition of "success" varied across studies; follow-up beyond 24 months was sparse.

## Axis 3 — Equivocal Pulp Status: Pulpotomy vs Pulpectomy

Source: [[endodontics/xiao-2026-pulpotomy-vs-pulpectomy-equivocal-primary-molars]] (retrospective propensity-matched cohort, 312 primary molars, 156 matched pairs)

### Background

Standard AAPD criteria define hemostasis within ≤5 minutes as the threshold for pulpotomy eligibility. A substantial proportion of clinical cases fall in the gray zone: bleeding that stops at 5–10 minutes, or radiographic findings (slight periapical widening, early furcal opacity) that are neither clearly normal nor pathologic. This study provides the first large propensity-matched cohort addressing this exact population.

### Survival Table — 24-month Kaplan-Meier Outcomes

| Subgroup | Pulpotomy (iRoot BP Plus) | Pulpectomy (ZOE-iodoform) | HR (pulpotomy vs pulpectomy) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All equivocal teeth | 86.4% (CI 81.2–91.6%) | 67.1% (CI 60.3–73.9%) | 0.783 | 0.047 |
| Asymptomatic subgroup | 91.2% | 74.5% | 0.42 (CI 0.24–0.73) | **0.002** |
| Symptomatic subgroup | — | — | 0.88 (CI 0.49–1.58) | 0.41 (NS) |

HR <1 favors pulpotomy (lower hazard of failure).

### Failure Mode Table

| Failure type | Pulpotomy group | Pulpectomy group | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internal resorption (내부 흡수) | 32.4% | 5.9% | <0.01 |
| External resorption (외부 흡수) | 8.8% | 33.3% | <0.01 |
| Periapical radiolucency | 14.7% | 23.5% | 0.21 (NS) |
| Clinical failure rate | 44.1% | 37.3% | 0.49 (NS) |

### Independent Failure Risk Factors (multivariate Cox)

| Factor | HR | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Incomplete SSC sealing | 3.12 | Restoration quality dominates treatment choice |
| ZOE usage (vs bioceramic) | 1.68 | Material type matters independently |
| Pulpectomy vs pulpotomy | 0.783 | Treatment modality significant only after controlling above |

### Key Messages

- In asymptomatic equivocal cases, pulpotomy offers a large, clinically significant survival advantage (HR=0.42) — this is a strong argument for attempting pulpotomy first rather than defaulting to pulpectomy when the diagnosis is uncertain.
- Symptomatic presentation erases the advantage: when the child reports preoperative pain, pulpotomy and pulpectomy perform equivalently, consistent with the hypothesis that pain signals irreversible pulp change even when bleeding duration is equivocal.
- Failure type, not just failure rate, differs — internal resorption is a pulpotomy-specific failure mode; external resorption is a pulpectomy-specific failure mode. Follow-up radiograph interpretation should be failure-type-aware.
- SSC sealing quality is the single largest modifiable risk factor (HR=3.12) — restoration quality matters more than treatment choice. A technically sound SSC is non-negotiable regardless of which pulp therapy is selected.

### Limitation

Single-center, single-operator retrospective cohort (Hefei, China); iRoot BP Plus was the sole bioceramic evaluated; equivocal definition (5–10 min bleeding, inconclusive radiograph) may not map exactly to other centers' clinical judgment; follow-up mean 24.6 months with 36-month maximum.
