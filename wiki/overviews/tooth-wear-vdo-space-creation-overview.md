## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 마모된 치열의 첫 질문은 "고경(Vertical Dimension of Occlusion, VDO/OVD)을 얼마나 올릴까"가 아니라 **"고경이 애초에 상실되긴 했는가"**다. 마모 ≠ 고경 상실 — 치조돌기 보상으로 고경이 유지된 채 치관만 짧아진 경우가 통례이며, 이때 거상은 불필요할 뿐 아니라 관절에 청구서를 남긴다.
> - **수복공간은 네 개의 축에서 얻는다**: ① 절단연 방향(Dahl 개념 — 상대적 축성 이동), ② 전악(반부가형 semi-additive VDO 거상), ③ 근단 방향(치관연장술), ④ 교정적 압하. **근거 경사가 직관을 뒤집는다** — 이 위키에서 유일한 SR+MA를 가진 축은 **고경을 건드리지 않는 ①번**이고, ④번은 논문이 0편이다.
> - **축 ① Dahl (근거 최강)**: 첫 SR+MA(11편·비뚤림 낮음 8/중간 3·GRADE 낮음~중간) — 후방 교합 **완전 재확립 86.9%**(부분 10.0%·없음 0.6%, 1–25.4개월), 환자 나이 무관. **간접 연간실패율 0–0.7% vs 직접 0–8.7%(P<.05)**. 단 추적기간이 교란(간접 1.7–2.2년 vs 직접 1.3–7년) — 같은 기간을 비교한 게 아니다.
> - **축 ② 전악 반부가형 거상**: DSD→Kois→구치부 VDO→전방유도→심미 순서로 5년 생존율 **90.1%**(212수복물). 그러나 **교합면 비니어만 79.1%**(구개측 100%·순측 94.1%), **후방 위치가 유일한 유의 실패인자**(HR 0.143, p=0.008). 재료는 무의미(복합레진 86.3% vs 세라믹 93.1%, p=0.107). 장기 확인: 최소침습 유리세라믹 전악 5년 수복물 생존 96.6%(n=20, 최대 12.4년).
> - **축 ③ 치관연장술 (근거 최약)**: 위키가 가진 건 SCTA(초치조상부 조직부착) **침범 교정 증례 1건**(n=1 + 서술고찰)이지 마모 전치의 심미적 치관연장 근거가 아니다. 가져올 수 있는 건 원칙뿐 — SCTA ≈ 결합조직 1mm + 접합상피 1mm + 열구 1mm, **변연–치조정 <2mm면 침범**(골탐침).
> - **축 ④ 교정적 압하: 논문 0편.** Rule #4 — 이 축은 위키가 답할 수 없다.
> - **고경 상실 판정 도구**: 자유공극(Freeway Space) **2–4mm**, 최근접 발음공간 **1–2mm**; 감소 시 하안면 1/3 단축·이순구 심화·구각 처짐·이근 긴장. **⚠️ 단 이 수치는 총의치(무치악) 맥락의 서술고찰에서 왔다** — 유치악 마모 환자 적용은 외삽이고, 저자 스스로 "단일 우월 방법 없음·Willis 게이지 전제는 일부 집단에서 실패(필리핀 표본 상안면이 하안면보다 ~3cm 큼)·Boos bimeter는 과대추정"이라 적었다.
> - **거상은 공짜가 아니다 — 그러나 근거가 갈린다**: 동물(쥐 n=15·8주)에서 **OVD 거상이 교합상실보다 과두연골 손상이 더 빠르고 심했고 연골하골 회복도 지연**. 반대편엔 임상 CBCT(후향 n=12): VD **약 +2mm에서 1년 TMJ 형태 변화 없음·TMD 부작용 0**·후방 관절강 유의 증가. **널리 인용되는 "Abduo ≤5mm 안전역"의 원논문은 이 위키에 없다** — 인용의 인용이므로 그 숫자에 기대지 말 것.
> - **⚠️ 가장 중요한 경계 — "고경 올리면 턱관절이 좋아진다"는 정당화는 근거가 정면으로 반대한다**: Cochrane SR이 **비가역적 교합 처치(삭제·크라운)를 TMD 목적으로 쓰는 데 강한 반대 권고**를 냈고 교합조정은 효과 없음으로 결론. 마모 수복의 적응증은 **심미·기능·구조 보존**이지 TMD 치료가 아니다. 이 경계가 흐려지면 근거 없이 비가역적 처치를 하게 된다.
> - **역설적 안심 재료**: 8년 코호트(Milosevic 2016, n=164)에서 **이갈이와 고경 거상은 실패와 연관되지 않았고**, 오히려 **"충분한 구치부 지지"가 실패까지의 시간을 늘렸다**. 전악 증례군에서도 이갈이·TWES 등급·나이트가드 모두 무의미(NS). 즉 위험인자는 우리가 두려워하는 것(거상·이갈이)이 아니라 **수복물의 위치(후방/교합면)**일 수 있다.
> - **거상 후 검증: 접촉 "개수"를 세지 말 것**. OVD를 2/5mm 올렸을 때 **접촉 개수는 어떤 조건에서도 안 변했고(Friedman p>.05, 편차 크기와 상관도 없음) 접촉 위치는 이동했다**(Y·Z 유의, 5mm 구강내 Z에서 d=0.57–1.20). **가상 안궁이전은 편차를 못 줄였다.** 5mm 거상은 교합기 기반이 구강내보다 우수. 단 정직하게 — 관찰 편차는 대체로 AAO 허용치(<0.5mm) 안에 있었고, 모든 값은 ground truth가 아니라 Group D 대비 상대값이다.
> - **기준위**: 전악수복(기존 MIP를 잃거나 바꿔야 함) → 재현 가능한 CR/CO; 건강 유치악+활주<2mm+무증상 → 기존 MIP 수용; 과두 불안정·TMD 징후 → 근육 유도 ICP; 과두개산형(hyperdivergent) 골격 → flag. 교합채득재는 왁스 회피(엘라스토머 대비 ~6배 편차, 단 이 근거 자체가 약함).
> - **병인 통제는 원칙이지 근거가 아니다**: 구개측 마모 패턴 → 내인성 산(GERD) 시사 → 의과 의뢰. 그러나 Hardan SR은 **"병인별로 결과를 비교한 연구가 하나도 없다"**고 명시한다. 우리는 병인을 통제해야 한다고 믿지만, 그 믿음의 임상 결과 근거는 없다.
> - **확신도**: 축 ① [확인 — SR+MA]. 축 ② [확인-하 — 후향 n=8·전향 n=20 소표본]. 축 ③ [참고수준 — n=1]. 축 ④ [근거 없음]. TMJ 안전역 [상충 — 동물 vs 소규모 임상]. 재료 선택 [확인 — 무의미차 수렴].

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 10 papers on how restorative space is created in the worn dentition, organized around a diagnostic inversion: the first question is not *how much* to raise the occlusal vertical dimension (OVD) but *whether the OVD was ever lost* — wear does not equal loss of vertical dimension, and space can be won on four distinct axes (incisal via the Dahl concept, whole-arch via semi-additive VDO increase, apical via crown lengthening, and orthodontic intrusion), whose evidence gradient inverts clinical intuition.

The only axis carrying a systematic review with meta-analysis is the one that never changes the OVD: fixed Dahl restorations re-establish complete posterior occlusal contact in 86.9% of patients (partial 10.0%, none 0.6%; 1–25.4 months; age non-significant) with indirect annual failure rates of 0–0.7% versus direct 0–8.7% (P<.05, Fan 2026, 11 studies, GRADE low-to-moderate) — though the indirect advantage is confounded by shorter follow-up (1.7–2.2 y vs 1.3–7 y); whole-arch semi-additive VDO increase achieves 90.1% five-year survival but concentrates failure in posterior occlusal veneers (79.1% vs palatal 100%, posterior location HR 0.143, p=0.008, Ferrando-Cascales 2023); crown lengthening rests on a single case report; and orthodontic intrusion has no paper in this wiki at all.

Raising the OVD is not free but the evidence is split — OVD elevation damaged condylar cartilage faster and more severely than occlusal loss in rats (Wang 2024) while a clinical CBCT cohort found no TMJ morphological change and zero TMD events at ~+2 mm one year on (Zhang 2024, n=12), and the widely cited "Abduo ≤5 mm" safe threshold has no source paper in this wiki; the decisive boundary is that a Cochrane review issues a strong recommendation *against* irreversible occlusal modification for TMD (Singh 2024), so wear rehabilitation must be justified by esthetics, function, and structural preservation — never by a claim that raising the bite treats the joint.

## 세줄요약

마모 치열에서 수복공간을 어떻게 얻는가에 대한 10편 종합 — 진단의 전환이 핵심: 첫 질문은 고경(OVD)을 *얼마나* 올릴까가 아니라 고경이 *상실되긴 했는가*다. 마모 ≠ 고경 상실이며, 공간은 네 축(절단연=Dahl, 전악=반부가형 거상, 근단=치관연장술, 교정적 압하)에서 얻을 수 있고 그 근거 경사는 임상 직관을 뒤집는다.

SR+MA를 가진 유일한 축은 **고경을 건드리지 않는 Dahl**이다 — 후방 교합 완전 재확립 86.9%(부분 10.0%·없음 0.6%, 1–25.4개월, 나이 무관), 간접 연간실패율 0–0.7% vs 직접 0–8.7%(P<.05, Fan 2026, 11편, GRADE 낮음~중간). 단 간접 우위는 추적기간 교란(1.7–2.2년 vs 1.3–7년). 전악 반부가형 거상은 5년 생존 90.1%이나 실패가 구치부 교합면 비니어에 집중(79.1% vs 구개측 100%, 후방 위치 HR 0.143 p=0.008). 치관연장술은 증례 1건, 교정적 압하는 논문 0편.

거상은 공짜가 아니나 근거가 갈린다 — 쥐에서 OVD 거상이 교합상실보다 과두연골 손상이 빠르고 심했으나(Wang 2024), 임상 CBCT는 +2mm·1년에 TMJ 변화 0·TMD 0(Zhang 2024, n=12); 널리 인용되는 "Abduo ≤5mm"의 원논문은 이 위키에 없다. 결정적 경계는 Cochrane이 **TMD 목적의 비가역적 교합 처치에 강한 반대 권고**를 냈다는 것 — 마모 수복의 정당화는 심미·기능·구조 보존이지 "고경을 올리면 관절이 좋아진다"가 아니다.

## Summary

The patient says the teeth are too short. The reflex is to reach for the vertical dimension. This synthesis argues that reflex is the error, and assembles the wiki's evidence into the diagnostic sequence that should precede it.

Ten papers span the space: one SR+MA on the Dahl concept ([[occlusion/fan-2026-fixed-dahl-restorations-localized-tooth-wear-sr-ma]]), one narrative review on OVD determination ([[complete-denture/fayad-2025-vertical-dimension-determination-prosthodontics-review]]), one SR on direct-versus-indirect wear restoration ([[prosthetic-materials/hardan-2022-treatment-tooth-wear-using-direct]]), two clinical cohorts on whole-arch adhesive rehabilitation ([[veneers/ferrando-cascales-2023-total-rehabilitation-adhesive-restorations-severe]], [[prosthetic-materials/hjerppe-2025-minimally-invasive-glassceramic-fullmouth-12year]]), two on the TMJ consequences of raising the bite ([[tmj/wang-2024-tmj-ovd-elevation-occlusal-loss-rats]], [[tmj/zhang-2024-tmj-morphology-ipilffdp-cbct]]), one Cochrane review that draws the indication boundary ([[tmj/singh-2024-occlusal-interventions-tmd-cochrane-sr]]), one factorial experiment on verifying the result ([[occlusion/biren-2025-virtual-articulator-occlusal-contacts-increased-vertical-dimension]]), and one case report standing in for an entire missing axis ([[periodontics/alam-2024-supracrestal-tissue-attachment-biologic-width]]).

Four questions organize them. **(1) Was the OVD lost at all?** Usually not — and the tools that answer this come from edentulous prosthodontics, which is an extrapolation this page names rather than hides. **(2) Is the wear localized or generalized?** This is the fork between Dahl and whole-arch. **(3) What is the etiology?** Palatal patterns point intrinsic; but no study has compared outcomes by etiology. **(4) What reference position do I build to?** Answered by [[overviews/cr-co-micp-reference-position-debate]], not re-derived here.

## Thesis

**The evidence gradient runs opposite to the invasiveness gradient, and that is the finding.**

A worn mandibular incisor can be lengthened along four axes. Move the incisal edge coronally and let the rest of the dentition adapt (Dahl). Move the whole occlusion apart (semi-additive VDO increase). Move the gingiva and bone apically (crown lengthening). Or move the tooth itself into its socket (orthodontic intrusion). These are not four flavors of the same treatment — they are four different biological bargains, and the wiki's evidence is distributed across them in an order that should discipline the reflex.

The axis with the **strongest evidence — the field's only SR+MA — is the one that does not touch the OVD at all.** Fan 2026 pools 11 studies and reports that 86.9% of Dahl patients re-establish complete posterior occlusal contact, without removing sound tooth structure and without a planned vertical increase. The axis with the **second-strongest evidence** (whole-arch semi-additive raise) rests on a retrospective case series of 8 patients and a prospective cohort of 20 — real data, but small, single-operator, and without controls. The **third axis** (crown lengthening) is represented in this wiki by a single case report that is not even about tooth wear; it is about correcting a supracrestal tissue attachment violation caused by a deep crown margin. The **fourth axis** (orthodontic intrusion) has no paper here at all.

So the honest ranking is: the least invasive option has the best evidence, the most definitive option has the weakest, and one option cannot be discussed from this wiki. That is an unusually clean alignment — and it means the burden of proof sits on whoever proposes to raise the vertical dimension, not on whoever proposes to leave it alone.

**Two guardrails hold the whole structure up.** First, an indication boundary: Singh 2024's Cochrane review issues a **strong recommendation against irreversible occlusal modification for TMD**, and finds occlusal adjustment without benefit. Nothing in this page licenses "raising the OVD to treat the joint." Wear rehabilitation is justified by esthetics, function, and structural preservation — three things the patient can see and the clinician can photograph — and by nothing else. Second, a verification boundary: Biren 2025 shows that the metric most clinicians reach for to confirm the delivered occlusion (**contact count**) is precisely the metric that does not move when the occlusion does. The count was statistically unaffected by OVD amount, method, or virtual facebow, and did not correlate with deviation magnitude in any direction, while contact *positions* deviated significantly in Y and Z. You cannot verify a raise by counting.

**And the risk factors are not the ones we fear.** Milosevic 2016 (n=164, 8 years, via Hardan's SR) found that **bruxism and increased OVD were not associated with failure**, while adequate posterior support *lengthened* time-to-failure. Ferrando-Cascales found parafunction, TWES grade, and bite-guard use all non-significant — while posterior/occlusal location alone predicted failure (HR 0.143, p=0.008). The profession worries about the parafunctional patient and the raised bite; the data, such as it is, worries about the posterior occlusal veneer.

## 핵심 명제 (Evidence Propositions)

1. **Localized wear can be restored without changing the OVD, and this is the best-evidenced path.** Fan 2026 (SR+MA; 11 studies — RCTs, cohorts, case series; 8 low / 3 moderate risk of bias; search to June 2025; GRADE low-to-moderate) pooled occlusal re-establishment after fixed Dahl restorations: **complete 86.9%, partial 10.0%, none 0.6%**, occurring over 1–25.4 months, with patient age a non-significant moderator. The mechanism — intrusion of the loaded teeth plus over-eruption of the temporarily separated posteriors — gains restorative space **without removing sound tooth structure**. [확인 — SR+MA]

2. **Within the Dahl axis, indirect beats direct — but the contrast is confounded by follow-up.** Annual failure rate: indirect **0–0.7%** vs direct **0–8.7%** (P<.05); annual complication rate: indirect 5.6–7.2% vs direct 5.2–30.2%. Predominant failures are fracture, wear, and loss. **The indirect groups were followed 1.7–2.2 years and the direct groups 1.3–7 years** — a shorter observation window mechanically produces a lower annual failure estimate for a failure mode that accumulates. Read the direction, discount the magnitude. [확인 — 저자 명시 한계]

3. **Wear does not equal loss of vertical dimension, and the tools that test this are borrowed from edentulous prosthodontics.** Fayad 2025 (narrative review, 79 studies) gives freeway space ≈ **2–4 mm** and closest speaking space ≈ **1–2 mm**, with reduced-VD signs being a shortened lower facial third, deepened mentolabial fold, drooping commissures, mentalis strain, and loss of lip support; excessive-VD signs are muscle fatigue, speech difficulty, teeth clicking during speech, and accelerated ridge resorption. **This is a complete-denture-context review and applying it to a dentate wear patient is extrapolation.** The authors themselves report no universally superior method, that the Willis-gauge equal-facial-thirds assumption fails in some populations (a Filipino sample's upper third exceeded the lower by ~3 cm), and that the Boos bimeter overestimates. [확인 — 단, 외삽]

4. **Whole-arch semi-additive VDO increase works at five years, but failure concentrates in one place.** Ferrando-Cascales 2023 (retrospective case series; 8 patients, mean age 46.6 y, TWES 2.0 grade 2–4, 212 restorations, 60 months, single expert operator, Spain) used a DSD → Kois analyzer → posterior VDO → anterior guidance → esthetic veneer sequence: overall survival **90.1%** (mean survival 57.6 months). By type: palatal veneers **100%**, vestibular **94.1%**, **occlusal 79.1%**. Cox regression: **posterior location the only significant predictor (HR 0.143, 95% CI 0.034–0.601, p=0.008)**; material non-significant (composite 86.3% vs ceramic 93.1%, p=0.107); TWES grade, parafunction, and bite guard all non-significant. Failures peaked at year 2 (n=10) and year 5 (n=6). [확인 — 후향·n=8·단일술자]

5. **The additive philosophy's real dividend is reparability, not survival.** Of Ferrando-Cascales's 21 complications, **18 were minor chipping repaired directly** without replacing the restoration (1 debonding recemented, 1 tooth fracture requiring extraction + implant, 2 secondary caries); zero marginal microleakage and zero postoperative sensitivity across 60 months. Hardan 2022 records the contrast that makes this matter: **resin composites fail by fracture that is often repairable, while ceramo-metal crowns fail by complete loss often requiring endodontics or extraction** (Smales 2007). The choice is not which material survives longer — it is which failure mode you want to inherit. [확인]

6. **No restorative technique or material is proven superior for tooth wear.** Hardan 2022 (PRISMA 2020 SR; 2776 records → 16 clinical studies; follow-up to 10 years) could **not perform a meta-analysis** because of heterogeneity in design, materials, follow-up, and outcome criteria, and found findings on direct-versus-indirect **contradictory**, concluding no evidence for superiority of any technique or material. Its practice bottom line is a philosophy, not a ranking: prefer conservative, reversible, additive approaches. Long-term corroboration for the additive route: Hjerppe 2025 (prospective, n=20, 439 restorations, mean 4.5 y / max 12.4 y) reports **5-year restoration survival 96.6%** and patient-level 86.4%, with 79.7% lithium disilicate, technical complications 3% (chipping n=5, retention loss n=7), and patient VAS esthetics 9.5 / function 9.3. [확인 — SR + 전향 코호트 수렴]

7. **The risk factors are not the intuitive ones.** Via Hardan's SR: **Milosevic 2016** (n=164, composite, 8 years) found year-1 failure 5.4%, longer time-to-failure with older age **and adequate posterior support**, and — pointedly — **bruxism and increased OVD were not associated with failure**; **Taubóck 2021** (n=13, 11 years) found direct composite at raised VDO performs well long-term in severe wear; **Mehta 2021** found higher failure risk for molars, posterior-mandibular, and multi-session anterior restorations. Ferrando-Cascales independently found parafunction and bite guard non-significant while posterior location dominated. **Two independent datasets converge on location over parafunction.** [확인 — 단, 개별 연구는 SR 경유]

8. **Raising the OVD is not biologically free, and the animal and clinical evidence point opposite ways.** Wang 2024 (adult female rats, n=15, three groups — control / OVD-elevated / occlusal-loss; micro-CT, H&E, toluidine blue, IHC at 3 d, 1, 2, 4, 8 weeks) found **OVD elevation caused faster and more severe condylar cartilage damage than occlusal loss, with delayed subchondral bone repair**; both experimental groups showed partial repair, controls were unaffected. Against this, Zhang 2024 (retrospective CBCT, n=12 TMJ-symptom-free patients, 18 measurement items in Mimics 21.0, 1 year post-loading) found **no significant TMJ morphological change, no TMD adverse events**, and a significantly increased posterior joint space, with a mean VD increase of ~2 mm correlating with increased medial joint space. **Species, dose, and time all differ — this is not a resolved contradiction but an unbridged gap.** [상충 — 동물 vs 후향 n=12]

9. **The "Abduo ≤5 mm safe threshold" is a citation without a source in this wiki.** Both [[tmj/wang-2024-tmj-ovd-elevation-occlusal-loss-rats]] and [[tmj/zhang-2024-tmj-morphology-ipilffdp-cbct]] invoke it (Zhang controlled VD increase ≤5 mm on that basis), but the Abduo paper itself is not held here and a QMD search returns nothing. **We can report that two papers rely on this number; we cannot verify it.** Do not let a threshold's ubiquity substitute for its provenance. [확인 — 검색 0건 / 원논문 미보유]

10. **Nothing here licenses raising the bite to treat the joint.** Singh 2024 (Cochrane SR; CENTRAL/MEDLINE/Embase/registries; GRADE) found stabilization splints show very low certainty of small pain benefit versus no treatment and **no meaningful advantage over non-occluding placebo splints**; **occlusal adjustment shows no benefit**; and **irreversible occlusal modifications (grinding, crowns for TMD) lack evidence of benefit and carry risk of harm — a strong recommendation against was issued.** This is the indication boundary for every axis on this page: wear rehabilitation is justified by esthetics, function, and structural preservation, not by a therapeutic claim on the temporomandibular joint. [확인 — Cochrane]

11. **You cannot verify a raised occlusion by counting contacts.** Biren 2025 (prospective within-subject, 18 dentate Angle Class I adults, 8 splints each in a full factorial of method × amount × facebow, Group D as reference baseline) found the **number of occlusal contacts was statistically unaffected by OVD amount, method, or virtual facebow use (Friedman, p>.05), with no correlation to deviation magnitude in any direction** — while contact *positions* deviated significantly in Y (anteroposterior) and Z (superoinferior), most at 5 mm intraorally (Z effect sizes d = 0.57–1.20; Group G Z-deviation 0.466 mm, Group H 0.438 mm). Two workflow findings follow: at 5 mm, **articulator-based increase produced fewer deviations than intraoral**, and **the virtual facebow did not substantially reduce deviations in any comparison**. Honest caveats the authors state themselves: observed deviations mostly fell within the cited AAO acceptable threshold (<0.5 mm), and every deviation is **relative to Group D — an unvalidated baseline, not ground truth**, so this quantifies inter-method disagreement, not accuracy. [확인 — 저자 명시 한계]

12. **The apical axis is a stub, and the orthodontic axis is a hole.** Alam 2024 is a **single case report (47-year-old female) plus narrative review** about correcting a supracrestal tissue attachment (SCTA) violation caused by deeply placed subgingival crown margins — **not about lengthening worn teeth**. What transfers is principle only: SCTA ≈ 1 mm connective tissue + 1 mm junctional epithelium + 1 mm sulcus, with **margin-to-crest <2 mm on bone sounding** as the violation threshold, and correction options tabulated against keratinized tissue and bone-to-CEJ relationship (gingivectomy, apically positioned flap ± osteotomy, surgical/orthodontic extrusion). **Orthodontic intrusion for restorative space has no paper in this wiki** — Rule #4 applies; this axis cannot be advised from here. [참고수준 / 근거 없음]

## Clinical Decision Points

**진단 순서 (the sequence that must precede any raise):**

1. **Was the OVD actually lost?** Measure freeway space (normal 2–4 mm) and closest speaking space (1–2 mm); read the face for overclosure signs (shortened lower third, deepened mentolabial fold, drooping commissures, mentalis strain). Remember the reference values come from edentulous prosthodontics. **Most worn dentitions have compensated and retain their OVD — in which case axis ① is the answer and the rest of this page is moot.**

2. **Localized or generalized?** This is the fork. Localized (typically anterior) → Dahl. Generalized with demonstrated VDO loss → whole-arch semi-additive. Check the antagonist: an isolated single-arch problem is uncommon, since the opposing surface wore against something.

3. **What is the etiology?** Palatal/lingual pattern → intrinsic acid (GERD, vomiting) → **refer to medicine** (see [[overviews/dental-erosion-etiology-prevention-synthesis]]). Incisal pattern → attrition/bruxism. **But note Hardan 2022: no study has compared restorative outcomes by etiology** — controlling the cause is a principle, not an evidence-based predictor of restorative success.

4. **How much posterior support is there?** Milosevic's 8-year cohort found adequate posterior support lengthened time-to-failure — one of the few protective factors in this literature.

5. **Which reference position?** Defer to [[overviews/cr-co-micp-reference-position-debate]] — full-mouth reconstruction → reproducible CR/CO; healthy dentate with slide <2 mm and asymptomatic → accept existing MIP; deformed/unstable condyle or TMD signs → muscle-guided premature-contact-free ICP; hyperdivergent skeletal pattern → flag for functional and condylar assessment first.

## Gaps & Future Research

- **The central diagnostic claim of this page has no paper behind it.** "Wear does not equal OVD loss because of dentoalveolar compensation" is the premise the Dahl concept operationally depends on, yet this wiki holds **no study measuring how often the OVD is actually preserved in worn dentitions**, nor a validated method for detecting loss in a *dentate* patient. Everything we use to answer question 1 is extrapolated from complete-denture prosthodontics. This is the biggest hole on the page and it sits at the very first decision node.
- **Orthodontic intrusion is entirely absent.** One of four legitimate axes cannot be discussed. Acquiring even one paper here would change what this wiki can advise.
- **Crown lengthening for worn teeth is unrepresented.** [[periodontics/alam-2024-supracrestal-tissue-attachment-biologic-width]] is a crown-margin violation case standing in for an axis it was never about. Mandibular anterior crown lengthening specifically — where root length and bone thickness constrain the option — has no source.
- **The TMJ contradiction is unbridged, not resolved.** Wang 2024 (rat, damage) and Zhang 2024 (human, no change) differ in species, dose, duration, and outcome measure. **No study raises the OVD in humans by a randomized amount and images the joint.** The "≤5 mm" threshold circulating through both papers has no retrievable primary source here.
- **The Dahl direct-vs-indirect comparison needs equal follow-up.** The P<.05 favoring indirect is read across 1.7–2.2 y versus 1.3–7 y. A properly matched comparison does not exist, and Fan 2026 notes long-term data beyond 7 years remain scarce.
- **Etiology has never been tested as an outcome modifier.** Hardan 2022 states plainly that **no study compared outcomes by etiology or by anterior versus posterior region**. The whole clinical convention of "control the cause before restoring" is, restoratively, untested.
- **Whole-arch evidence is small and operator-bound.** Ferrando-Cascales: 8 patients, one expert operator, retrospective, no control. Hjerppe: 20 patients. The five-year numbers are encouraging and the sample cannot support generalization.
- **Verification lacks ground truth.** Biren 2025's deviations are relative to an unvalidated baseline group. No study of OVD-increase workflows uses an independent reference standard (compare [[overviews/cr-co-micp-reference-position-debate]], where only Mei 2022's industrial-CT design manages this).
