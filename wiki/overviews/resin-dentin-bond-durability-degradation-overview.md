## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: 레진-상아질 결합은 시간이 지나며 **열화**한다. 두 기전 — ① 친수성 수지의 **가수분해(hydrolysis)**, ② 물이 많고 수지가 부족한 콜라겐 기질의 **효소분해(MMP·cysteine cathepsin)**. **공통 원흉은 잔류 물(water)** 이며 형태학적으로 nanoleakage·water-tree로 나타난다. 위키 19편을 7축으로 합성.
> - **Umbrella-level 검증(El Alaoui 2026)**: 9편 SR(7편 MA)을 종합해 상아질 biomodifier 20종을 천연·물리·화학 3범주로 taxonomy화 — 비열대기압플라즈마(Non-Thermal Atmospheric Plasma, NTAP)가 물리적 agent 중 장기 결합강도 향상 근거 최다(SMD 최대 3.28)이나 두 NTAP SR 간 중복도(CCA)가 57.9%(very high)로 과대평가 가능성 존재; NaOCl·HOCl은 §4의 CHX와 반대로 결합강도를 저하시키는 화학적 탈단백제로 재확인.
> - **혼성층(hybrid layer)의 양면성**: 결합의 핵심이자 가장 약한 고리. 노화 시 콜라겐 노출·수지 용출로 붕괴 (Breschi 2018, Tjäderhane 2015).
> - **Water-tree 개념**: Tay & Pashley가 전기케이블 절연체 열화 개념을 차용 — 혼성층 위/내부에 수지상(dendritic) 수분채널 형성, 노화 시 nanoleakage가 reticular→water-tree로 이행 (Tay 2003 ×2); 세관 폐쇄 시 소실 = **경상아질 수분 flux가 원인** (Tay 2005).
> - **5대 내구성 전략(랜드마크 통합)**: ①전환율↑·esterase 저항 ②MMP 억제제 ③콜라겐 가교 ④에탄올 습윤접착 ⑤생체모방 재광화 (Liu 2011, J Dent Res).
> - **MMP 억제 — 실험실 근거 있으나 임상 전이 실패**: 클로르헥시딘(Chlorhexidine, CHX)은 실험실 SR+MA(Kiuru 2021, 740→43편)에서 6/12/24개월 노화 결합강도 유의 보존 — 그러나 임상 SR+MA(Bourgi 2026, RCT/CCT 11편, 최대 4년)에서는 유지율·술후 과민·2차우식 모두 무차이(RD=0.01/0.00, I²=0%). 현대 MDP 접착제가 이미 탄탄한 혼성층(Hybrid Layer)을 형성해 CHX 혜택을 상쇄하는 것으로 해석 — 일상적 CHX 불필요; 고위험 우식·항균 목적에만 선택적.
> - **콜라겐 가교(Collagen Cross-Linking) 5종 분류(Liao 2026, SR+MA 63편 in vitro)**: 알데히드·리보플라빈·폴리페놀·카르보디이미드·키토산계가 특정 조건에서 접착력 향상; 산부식(Etch-and-Rinse, E&R) 전처리에서 가장 일관된 효과; 키토산 근거 불확실; GRADE 낮음~매우 낮음, 출판 비뚤림 주의 — 임상 적용 시기 아직 아님. Neves 2026(PAC 기전)·Zhou 2025(NaOCl 상호작용)와 함께 가교 3편 보완 논문 축을 이룸.
> - **MDP 화학결합 기전**: 10-MDP가 hydroxyapatite와 이온결합해 **자기조립 nanolayer(MDP-Ca)** 형성 + 산-염기 저항층 (Yoshihara 2011, Carrilho 2019). **단 반전**: 상용 접착제는 nanolayering이 희소(15% 실험용서만, Tian 2015)하고, in-vitro 우월성(Fehrenbach 2021)에도 **NCCL RCT선 임상 이점 없음(p=0.05, de Oliveira 2021)**.
> - **습윤접착 도그마 재검토**: 에탄올 습윤+CHX는 in-vitro 내구성↑(Talungchit 2014)이나, etch-and-rinse 건조 vs 습윤 NCCL RCT 5편서 **유지율·과민 차이 없음**(Forville 2024) → "촉촉한 상아질" 절대원칙 약화.
> - **에탄올 습윤접착술 (Ethanol-Wet Bonding Technique, EWBT) — 6년 RCT로 임상 판정 종결(Souza 2026)**: NCCL 148개 6년 유지율 — 수분습윤 대조(NE) 88.9%, EWBT+유니버설(EU) 81.1%, EWBT+3단계 E&R(EMP) 77.8%(대조와 유의차 없음), **EWBT+소수성 본드 단독(EB) 48.7%**(log-rank p=0.008, 6개월부터 모든 군보다 열등). → 친수성 접착제엔 **수명 이점 없음**, 소수성 레진 단독과 조합은 **임상적으로 불가**. 12개월 "동등" 결론(Mortazavi 2012)은 **부분 대체(partially superseded)**. 한계: 군집 미보정·LOCF·회수율 72.9%.
> - **에어어브레이전 (Airborne-Particle Abrasion, APA) 전처치 — 임상 이점 없음(Forville 2026)**: 경화 상아질 NCCL에 50 μm Al₂O₃ 10초 → 18개월 유지율 90.3% vs 대조 96.7%(ns), HR=3.03(ns, 검정력 부족·해로운 방향 경향). El Alaoui 2026 umbrella의 in-vitro "air abrasion 결합강도↑"가 임상으로 번역되지 않음.
> - **보조 전략**: 산성 상아질 conditioner가 장기 결합 개선·선택적 extrafibrillar 탈회로 건조접착 보조 (Zheng 2024). **콜라겐 가교(collagen crosslinking)**: 프로안토시아니딘(proanthocyanidin, PAC) 프라이머가 혼성층·하부 상아질의 복합탄성계수↑·미세투과도↓(1년 유지), 삼량체(DP3)>사량체(DP4) — 층별 내구성 기전으로 Zhou 2025 가교 효과를 보강 (Neves 2026, in-vitro).
> - **임상 한 줄**: 결합 수명을 늘리려면 **물을 줄이고(용매 충분 휘발·습윤 과하지 않게)·효소를 막고(CHX)·콜라겐을 보존**하라. MDP/nanolayer·에탄올 습윤접착·에어어브레이전의 in-vitro 우월성은 임상서 희석되거나 사라지므로 술식 규율(전환율·오염차단·CHX)이 더 결정적. **EWBT는 일상 NCCL 접착에 추가하지 말고, 프라이머 생략한 소수성 본드 단독 사용은 금지.**
> - **확신도**: 열화 기전·water-tree·MDP nanolayering = in-vitro 강함; MMP-CHX = SR+MA 중등도; EWBT 무효·EB 실패 = 6년 RCT 중등도; 습윤접착·MDP·APA 임상효과 = RCT/SR이 in-vitro와 괴리(낮음~중등도).

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 19 resin-bonding wiki papers across 7 axes of bond-degradation research: bonds fail via two parallel mechanisms — hydrolysis of hydrophilic resin (solvent-phase, thermodynamically driven) and MMP/cysteine-cathepsin collagenolysis of the water-rich, resin-sparse hybrid layer — with residual water as the shared culprit, visualized by nanoleakage silver-tracer and Tay–Pashley water-tree morphology (Tay 2003 ×2, Tay 2005).

CHX inhibits MMPs and preserves aged bond strength in laboratory SR+MA (Kiuru 2021, 740→43 studies, effect increasing through 24 months), but a 2026 clinical SR+MA of 11 RCTs (Bourgi 2026) found no benefit in restoration retention, sensitivity, or secondary caries (all RD ≤0.01, I²=0%) — modern MDP adhesives form hybrid layers robust enough to absorb the enzyme-inhibition gain, reversing the prior routine-CHX recommendation; collagen cross-linker SR+MA (Liao 2026, 63 in-vitro) finds all five classes (aldehyde, riboflavin, polyphenol, carbodiimide, chitosan) context-dependent with GRADE low–very low and no universal agent; 10-MDP nanolayering, EWBT, and air abrasion show in-vitro superiority but no NCCL RCT advantage (de Oliveira 2021; Forville 2024·2026).

A 6-year RCT settles EWBT: no longevity gain with hydrophilic adhesives (81.1%/77.8% vs 88.9% water-wet, NS) and clinically unacceptable 48.7% retention paired with hydrophobic bond alone (log-rank p=0.008; Souza 2026), partially superseding Mortazavi 2012.

Clinical rule: reduce water (full solvent evaporation, do not over-wet), rely on MDP adhesives' inherent hybrid-layer stability rather than CHX as the enzyme block (CHX not required routinely), and preserve collagen (crosslinkers as evidence matures) — do not add EWBT or air abrasion routinely, and never use a hydrophobic bond without its primer.

## 세줄요약

위키 19편 7축 종합: 레진-상아질 결합 열화의 두 기전은 친수성 수지의 가수분해(hydrolysis)와 물이 많고 수지가 적은 혼성층(hybrid layer) 콜라겐의 기질금속단백분해효소(Matrix Metalloproteinase, MMP)/카텝신(cathepsin) 분해이며, 공통 원흉은 잔류 물(residual water) — 형태학적으로 나노누출(nanoleakage)·수분수(water-tree, Tay 2003·2005)로 나타남.

클로르헥시딘(CHX)은 실험실 SR+MA(Kiuru 2021)에서 24개월까지 노화 결합강도 유의 보존 — 그러나 임상 SR+MA(Bourgi 2026, RCT 11편, 최대 4년)에서 유지율·술후 과민·2차우식 모두 무차이(RD=0.01/0.00); 현대 MDP 접착제가 CHX 혜택을 상쇄하므로 일상적 CHX 불필요; 콜라겐 가교 5종 분류(Liao 2026, in vitro 63편)는 E&R 전처리에서 가장 일관되나 GRADE 낮음~매우 낮음으로 임상 전용 시기 아님.

10-MDP 나노층·에탄올 습윤접착술(EWBT)·에어어브레이전은 실험실 우월성에도 임상(NCCL) RCT 이점 없음(de Oliveira 2021, Forville 2024·2026); 6년 RCT(Souza 2026)에서 EWBT는 친수성 접착제 병용 시 수명 이점 없고, 소수성 본드 단독 병용 시 유지율 48.7%로 임상 부적합.

임상 원칙: 결합 수명은 술식 규율(용매 충분 휘발·과습윤 금지)이 재료 교체보다 결정적 — CHX는 일상 불필요, EWBT·에어어브레이전은 추가하지 않고, 프라이머 없는 소수성 본드 단독 사용 금지.

## Synthesis

According to the 19 resin-dentin papers held in this wiki (all in `resin-bonding`), the clinical bottom line is: **the resin-dentin bond is intrinsically time-limited, and every durability strategy is ultimately a way to remove water, inhibit collagenolytic enzymes, or preserve/replace the collagen matrix.** Seven axes organize the evidence.

### 1. The hybrid layer: essential yet the weak link

The hybrid layer (resin-infiltrated demineralized collagen) is both the bonding mechanism and the primary failure site. Reviews establish that it degrades over time via collagen exposure and resin leaching ([[resin-bonding/breschi-2018-dentin-bonding-collagen-structure-bond-preservation-review]], [[resin-bonding/tjaderhane-2015-dentin-bonding-can-we-make-it-last-review]]).

### 2. Two degradation mechanisms + the role of water

The landmark unifying review [[resin-bonding/liu-2011-limitations-bonding-dentin-bond-degradation-review]] (J Dent Res) frames it: bonds fail through (1) **hydrolysis** of suboptimally polymerized hydrophilic resin and (2) **collagenolysis** of the water-rich, resin-sparse matrix by matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs) and cysteine cathepsins — and proposes five experimental strategies (below). Water is the shared culprit, made visible by silver-tracer nanoleakage.

### 3. Water-treeing & nanoleakage (the Tay–Pashley axis)

Borrowing from high-voltage cable-insulation science, [[resin-bonding/tay-2003-water-treeing-degradation-dentin-adhesives]] showed dendritic water-filled channels ("water trees") forming within/above the hybrid layer; [[resin-bonding/tay-2003-aging-two-modes-nanoleakage-bonded-dentin]] defined two nanoleakage modes (reticular within the hybrid layer vs water-trees in the adhesive) and showed aging shifts expression toward water-trees; [[resin-bonding/tay-2005-tubular-occlusion-prevents-water-treeing-self-etch]] proved transdentinal water flux (not just residual solvent) drives it — occluding tubules eliminates water-treeing.

### 4. MMP inhibition — laboratory vs clinical disconnect

Chlorhexidine (CHX) is the most-studied MMP inhibitor: [[resin-bonding/kiuru-2021-mmp-inhibitors-dentin-bonding-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 740→43 studies) found CHX has **no effect on immediate** bond strength but **significantly preserves aged** bond strength at 6, 12, and 24 months, with the advantage growing over time — appearing to support CHX application.

**The clinical verdict — no benefit (Bourgi 2026).** [[resin-bonding/bourgi-2026-chx-pretreatment-clinical-adhesive-restorations-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 11 RCTs/CCTs, follow-up 1 week–4 years) is the clinical counterpart: CHX dentin pretreatment (0.2–2%, 15–60 s) showed **no significant effect** on restoration retention (RD 0.01, I²=0%), postoperative sensitivity (RD 0.01, I²=0%), or secondary caries (RD 0.00, I²=0%), with GRADE moderate (retention/sensitivity) to low (caries). The most likely explanation: modern adhesives with functional monomers (10-MDP) form hybrid layers mechanically and chemically robust enough that the MMP-inhibitory contribution of CHX is redundant — the bond degrades more slowly from the outset, leaving no room for CHX to add. **Clinical conclusion: do not routinely apply CHX; reserve for antimicrobial/disinfection purposes in high-caries-risk patients.** The Kiuru 2021→Bourgi 2026 gap is the most striking lab-to-clinical translation failure on this page.

### 5. MDP chemical bonding & nanolayering — strong in vitro, diluted clinically

10-MDP chemically bonds to hydroxyapatite via ionic interaction, self-assembling into a "nanolayer" (the adhesion-decalcification concept): [[resin-bonding/yoshihara-2011-nanolayering-mdp-enamel-dentin]] (greater on dentin than enamel, concentration- and scrubbing-dependent) and [[resin-bonding/carrilho-2019-mdp-adhesive-interface-stability-sr]] (nanolayer + acid-base resistant zone + MDP-Ca salt). **But the clinical caveats are real**: commercial adhesives show *sparse* nanolayering (only the 15% experimental primer was profuse — [[resin-bonding/tian-2015-paucity-nanolayering-mdp-resin-dentin]]); MDP self-etch adhesives are superior in vitro ([[resin-bonding/fehrenbach-2021-10-mdp-bonding-performance-sr-ma]]) yet show **no clinical NCCL advantage** vs non-MDP (p=0.05, [[resin-bonding/de-oliveira-2021-10-mdp-self-etch-adhesive-dentin-sr]]).

### 6. Wet bonding, conditioners, and the moisture dogma re-examined

Ethanol-wet bonding (replacing water with ethanol before hydrophobic resin) plus CHX improves in-vitro durability ([[resin-bonding/talungchit-2014-ethanol-wet-bonding-chlorhexidine-resin-dentin-durability]]).

**Ethanol-wet bonding — the 6-year clinical verdict.** An early 12-month NCCL RCT (36 lesions, EWBT + hydrophobic bond only) reported "equal performance" to conventional bonding ([[resin-bonding/mortazavi-2012-ethanol-wet-bonding-clinical-trial]]). [[resin-bonding/souza-2026-ewbt-adhesive-systems-6year-rct]] — a double-blind RCT of 148 NCCLs in 67 adults followed 6 years (72–83 months), the longest clinical EWBT follow-up to date — overturns that optimism and separates the EWBT effect by adhesive hydrophilicity. 6-year retention: water-wet 3-step E&R control (NE) **88.9%**; EWBT + universal adhesive (EU) **81.1%**; EWBT + 3-step E&R with primer (EMP) **77.8%**; EWBT + undiluted hydrophobic bond only (EB) **48.7%** (overall log-rank p=0.008). EB was inferior to every other group (vs NE p=0.030, vs EU p=0.005, vs EMP p=0.015), with the curve separating from 6 months (76.3% retention at 6 mo), while NE/EU/EMP did not differ. Two conclusions follow: (1) **EWBT adds no longevity benefit with hydrophilic adhesives** — the extra 60 s ethanol step buys nothing; (2) **EWBT paired with a hydrophobic-only resin is clinically unacceptable** — the purest clinical expression of the concept with commercial materials lost over half its restorations. Mortazavi 2012 is therefore **partially superseded** (same concept; the 12-month window was too short to show failure). The in-vitro EWBT durability gains (Talungchit 2014) did not translate — the starkest example of this overview's in-vitro/clinical gap. Caveats: restoration-level analysis without cluster adjustment, LOCF imputation (may underestimate failure), 72.9% recall, unblinded operator, and ethanol-solvated experimental hydrophobic primers (the original EWBT concept) were not tested.

Beyond ethanol replacement, the "moist dentin" dogma is weakening clinically: across 5 split-mouth NCCL RCTs, **dry vs wet etch-and-rinse bonding showed no difference** in retention or sensitivity ([[resin-bonding/forville-2024-moist-dentin-adhesive-systems-reevaluation]], moderate GRADE). Acid-based dentin conditioners can improve long-term durability and aid dry bonding via selective extrafibrillar demineralization ([[resin-bonding/zheng-2024-dentin-conditioners-bond-strength-sr]]).

**Five-class crosslinker survey (Liao 2026).** [[resin-bonding/liao-2026-collagen-crosslinkers-resin-dentin-interface-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 63 in-vitro studies, 2015–2025) provides the broadest cross-linker taxonomy to date, covering all five main classes — aldehyde (glutaraldehyde, PAC), polyphenol (grape seed extract, tannic acid), riboflavin ± UVA, carbodiimide (EDC), and chitosan. All classes produce some bond-strength enhancement under specific conditions; the most consistent effect emerges when used as a pretreatment with etch-and-rinse (E&R) adhesive systems. Chitosan and siloxane evidence is limited and unconfirmed. Critically: GRADE is **low to very low** throughout, publication bias is significant in aged subgroups of aldehyde and polyphenol treatments, and no single agent performs universally — **clinical translation is not yet warranted.** Together with Neves 2026 (PAC mechanism, nano-DMA) and Zhou 2025 (NaOCl interaction), this forms the crosslinking axis: mechanism → class survey → complication alert.

**Collagen crosslinking — the layer-resolved mechanism (a third strategy beyond enzyme inhibition and chemical bonding).** Where CHX (§4) blocks collagenolysis and MDP (§5) adds chemical anchorage, proanthocyanidin (PAC) crosslinkers stabilize the bond by stiffening the collagen matrix itself. Neves (2026), an NIH-funded nano-DMA + micro-permeability in-vitro study (5 groups, 1-year simulated-body-fluid aging) from the Bedran-Russo group, dissects *how* this works rather than reporting a single bond-strength figure: rinse-out AB-type PAC primers (DESIGNER-standardized DP3/DP4 oligomers) **raised the complex modulus (E\*) of the hybrid layer and the underlying mineralized dentin and reduced interfacial micropermeability vs unprimed control (p<0.001)**, with the reinforcement acting strongest on the hybrid layer and underlying dentin (not the adhesive layer) and persisting at 1 year. Structure–activity: **trimeric (DP3) > tetrameric (DP4)** PACs (better fit into interfibrillar collagen spaces), with native viscoelastic damping preserved. Mechanistically it unifies the two failure modes of §2 — crosslinked collagen resists enzymatic cleavage *and* displaces unbound water — giving a layer-specific account that extends the bulk crosslinker bond-strength gains synthesized by Zhou (2025) and reinforces the Tjäderhane hybrid-layer-durability frame. ### 7. The umbrella-level taxonomy — where NTAP and NaOCl fit

[[resin-bonding/el-alaoui-2026-dentin-biomodification-resin-composite-umbrella]] is the first umbrella review to sit above axes 4–6: it synthesizes 9 SRs (7 meta-analyses, 175 primary studies) into a 3-way taxonomy of 20 dentin biomodifiers — natural, physical, chemical — evaluated across 51 experimental setups. It confirms, at the umbrella level, the same water/enzyme/collagen logic this synthesis is built on: chemical deproteinizers **NaOCl and HOCl both significantly reduce bond strength** (consistent with, and reinforcing, [[resin-bonding/zhou-2025-collagen-crosslinkers-naocl-dentin-bond-strength-sr-ma]]'s NaOCl-damage finding from §6's collagen-crosslinking discussion), while natural collagen-active agents (grape seed extract, chitosan, riboflavin) are uniformly beneficial-or-neutral, extending [[resin-bonding/kiuru-2021-mmp-inhibitors-dentin-bonding-sr-ma]]'s CHX-specific MMP-inhibition finding (§4) into a broader agent class.

The umbrella review's most novel contribution is a **physical-agent axis largely absent from this synthesis's original six**: non-thermal atmospheric plasma (NTAP) shows the largest pooled long-term effect sizes of any biomodifier surveyed (SMD up to 3.28, helium-based > argon-based), and simple mechanical **scrubbing** and **air abrasion** both improve bond strength and are — unlike NTAP — already chairside-affordable. A methodological caveat travels with the NTAP result: the two NTAP-focused SRs it draws on overlap at CCA = 57.9% (very high, vs 1.52% overall), so the apparent NTAP superiority may be partly an artifact of shared primary-study inclusion rather than independent replication — a caution worth carrying into clinical uptake decisions.

**Air abrasion — the umbrella's in-vitro gain does not survive the clinic.** The umbrella review's "air abrasion improves bond strength" is a laboratory finding. [[resin-bonding/forville-2026-air-abrasion-nccl-18m-rct]], a double-blind split-mouth RCT (63 patients, 126 NCCL restorations, universal adhesive + bulk-fill composite), tested airborne-particle abrasion (APA; 50 μm Al₂O₃, 10 s, 5 bar) as a pretreatment to remove the sclerotic surface layer: 18-month retention was **90.3% (APA) vs 96.7% (control)**, not significant, with HR = 3.03 (95% CI 0.74–12.37, p=0.122) — no benefit on any secondary outcome and a numerically *adverse*, underpowered trend. Air abrasion of sclerotic NCCL dentin should therefore not be added routinely; the 18-month horizon and low power leave longer-term harm neither confirmed nor excluded.

### Clinical bottom line

To extend bond longevity: (1) **maximize degree of conversion** and evaporate solvents fully; (2) avoid both over-wet and over-dry extremes (the moisture window is wider than the dogma implied); (3) **do not routinely apply CHX** — laboratory SR+MA shows MMP inhibition (Kiuru 2021) but clinical SR+MA (11 RCTs, Bourgi 2026) shows no benefit in retention, sensitivity, or caries; reserve CHX for antimicrobial/disinfection purposes in high-caries-risk patients; (4) use **MDP-containing** adhesives for their chemical bond and acid-base-resistant zone — but don't expect nanolayering alone to guarantee longevity; (5) preserve collagen (crosslinkers/conditioners await clinical-grade evidence; Liao 2026 shows all 5 classes context-dependent) and consider biomimetic remineralization as it matures; (6) among physical dentin pretreatments, **scrubbing is the chairside-ready option** — air abrasion improves bond strength in vitro but showed no NCCL retention benefit at 18 months (Forville 2026), and NTAP is promising but equipment-limited with a high-overlap caveat; (7) **avoid NaOCl/HOCl as a routine dentin pretreatment before bonding** — both reduce bond strength; (8) **do not add ethanol-wet bonding routinely, and never apply a hydrophobic bond resin without its primer** — EWBT gave no 6-year benefit with hydrophilic adhesives and 48.7% retention with hydrophobic-only resin (Souza 2026). In-vitro superiority of MDP nanolayering, ethanol-wet bonding, air abrasion, and CHX consistently exceeds their measured clinical benefit, so **technique discipline (conversion, contamination control, MDP adhesive selection) matters more than any single adjunct**.
