## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 원칙**: 구강위생교육(OHI)은 치주질환 예방·유지관리의 기초이며, 반복적·개별화된 교육이 효과적임. [확인]
> - **칫솔 선택**: 진동회전형(oscillating-rotating) 전동칫솔이 치면세균막·치은염 감소에서 가장 효과적 (SUCRA 89.2%, 86.5%); 모든 전동칫솔이 수동보다 우수 (Luo 2026 NMA). [확인]
> - **수동 기법**: 특정 수동 칫솔질 기법의 우월성 미확인; 개별 환자에 맞춘 기법 선택 필요 (Rajwani 2020 SR). [확인]
> - **구강세정제**: CPC는 양치 시 CHX와 동등 (Windhorst 2025); 비양치 시 CHX가 우수; 수술 후에는 CHX 선호. [확인]
> - **치약**: 표준 불소 치약은 기계적 치태 제거에 부가효과 없음; 트리클로산·주석불화물이 항치태·항치은염 효과 (Valkenburg 2019). [확인]
> - **착색제 활용 교육**: 착색제를 이용한 교육이 구두·모형 교육보다 효과적 (Choi 2017 한국 SR; Choi 2013은 착색제 개별 칫솔질 교육 PHP +24.0점 vs 강의·모형 +2.8점). [확인]
> - **칫솔질 교육 (Toothbrushing Instruction, TBI) 전달방식 — 기술보다 대조군이 결과를 정한다**: 약한 대조군(구두 1회·표준 조언)과 비교하면 강화 교육이 이기고(Lin 2025, Holloway 2022, Baumann 2025의 PCR), 능동적 위생사 교육과 비교하면 디지털 도구는 대부분 동등 — 스마트칫솔·거울(Smart Toothbrush and Mirror, STM) = 구두 TBI (40.5% vs 40.57%, Jeong 2022), 만화 도구 = 모형 (P=0.965, Saraf 2024). [확인]
> - **유일한 예외 가상현실 (Virtual Reality, VR)**: 착색제 1:1 교육 대비 2주 치면세균막 지수 (Plaque Index, PI) 감소 0.44 vs 0.07 (p=0.012, n=30) — 그러나 검사자 비눈가림·사후 등록·2주 추적이라 예비 근거 (Kwon 2026). [확인]
> - **공통 유효성분은 "자기 치태를 보여주는 피드백"**: 착색제 실습(Lin)·구강내 카메라 (Intra-Oral Camera, IOC) 영상(Holloway)·VR 치태 게이지(Kwon)·STM 음성 피드백(Jeong) 모두 같은 원리 — Choi 2013/2017의 착색제 교육 우위와 같은 방향. [확인]
> - **치태는 움직이지만 치은 지표는 잘 안 움직임**: 탐침 시 출혈 (BOP) 변화 없음(Kwon), 치은출혈지수 (GBI) 군간 차이 없음(Baumann, p=.70), 치은·출혈 유의 경계(Holloway) — 치은지수 차이를 보인 Lin 2025는 접촉시간 약 6배(180 vs 30분) 교란. [확인]
> - **개별화 표적화**: 탐침깊이 4 mm 이상 부위만 세는 agPCR 기반 교육이 치주염증표면적 (PISA)을 더 낮춤(Mitani 2024, 초록만·수치 없음); 고령자(65+) 건강행동과정접근법 (HAPA) 단계맞춤 교육은 PCR만 개선(62.27→57.00%, p=.026). [확인]
> - **칫솔질 시간**: 지도받지 않은 성인 수동칫솔 87.3초 vs 전동칫솔 151.1초 — 수동은 2분 권고 미달 (Thomassen 2026 SR, 관찰연구 12편, GRADE 수동 중등도). [확인]
> - **심리 요인**: 자기효능감 등은 치태 결과와 연관되나 비일관적 (Valeriani 2026 SR, 전향연구 10편, 초록만) — 위험평가 참고용이지 확립된 예측인자 아님. [확인]
> - **근거 등급**: TBI 전달방식 우월성은 전반적으로 낮음~매우 낮음 (소표본 n=22–62, 추적 8일~3개월, 초록만 4편). 임상 기본값은 착색제 기반 개별 교육 + 반복 강화, 도구 교체보다 피드백·표적화 추가가 합리적. [확인]
> - **교정 환자**: 전동칫솔(진동회전형)이 교정기에서 특히 효과적 (Luo 2026, Liu 2026). [확인]
> - **노인**: 전동칫솔 효과 유의하지 않음 (악력·손재주 한계); 개별화된 접근 필요. [확인]

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 35 papers on oral hygiene instruction: electric toothbrushes outperform manual for plaque and gingivitis, with oscillating-rotating technology ranking first (SUCRA 89.2% for plaque, 86.5% for gingivitis; Luo 2026 NMA, 19 RCTs); no specific manual toothbrushing technique has proven superior (Rajwani 2020 SR, 13 studies).

Chemical agents in dentifrices drive antiplaque/antigingivitis benefit — triclosan-copolymer and stannous fluoride have strong evidence, chlorhexidine weak evidence, while the dentifrice vehicle itself adds no mechanical plaque-removal benefit (Valkenburg 2019 meta-review); CPC mouthwash is non-inferior to CHX in brushing situations but CHX remains superior for non-brushing plaque control (Windhorst 2025 SR+MA).

Oral hygiene education is most effective when repeated, personalized, and supplemented with disclosing agents (Choi 2017 Korean SR); the optimal PMPR regimen is 4-5 visits at 1-2 week intervals with disclosing-based instruction; electric toothbrushes are especially effective in orthodontic patients and children, but benefit is non-significant in the elderly. Newer toothbrushing-instruction delivery trials (VR, smart mirror, intra-oral camera, hands-on multimedia, HAPA, cartoon aid) beat weak verbal-only controls but are mostly equivalent to active hygienist instruction, move plaque more than gingival bleeding, and are small and short (low certainty).

## 세줄요약

구강위생교육 35편 종합: 전동칫솔이 수동보다 치면세균막·치은염 감소에서 우수하며, 진동회전형이 1위 (SUCRA 89.2%, 86.5%; Luo 2026 NMA, 19 RCT); 특정 수동 칫솔질 기법의 우월성 미확인 (Rajwani 2020 SR).

치약의 화학적 활성 성분이 항치태·항치은염 효과를 이끔 — 트리클로산·주석불화물은 강한 근거, 클로르헥시딘은 약한 근거; 치약 자체는 기계적 치태 제거에 부가효과 없음 (Valkenburg 2019); CPC 구강세정제는 양치 시 CHX와 동등하나, 비양치 시 CHX가 우수 (Windhorst 2025 SR+MA).

구강위생교육은 반복적·개별화·착색제 활용 시 효과적 (Choi 2017); 최적 PMPR 요법은 1-2주 간격 4-5회 방문; 전동칫솔은 교정 환자·소아에서 특히 효과적이나, 노인에서는 유의하지 않음. 새 칫솔질 교육 전달방식(VR·스마트거울·구강내 카메라·실습+멀티미디어·HAPA·만화 도구)은 구두 단독 대조군보다는 낫지만 능동적 위생사 교육과는 대체로 동등하고, 치은 출혈보다 치태를 주로 개선하며, 소표본·단기라 확실성이 낮음.

## 1. 칫솔 기술 — 전동 vs 수동

[확인] Luo 2026 NMA(19 RCT): 5종 칫솔 기술 순위화.

| 순위 | 기술 | SUCRA (치태) | SUCRA (치은염) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **진동회전형 (O-R)** | **89.2%** | **86.5%** |
| 2 | 음파 (Sonic) | 72.4% | 70.1% |
| 3 | 초음파 (Ultrasonic) | 54.1% | 52.8% |
| 4 | 기타 전동 | 45.3% | 43.9% |
| 5 | 수동 | 18.3% | 16.7% |

**효과 크기**:
- O-R vs 수동: SMD −1.24 (치태), MD −0.32 (치은염 MGI 0-4)
- 절대적 치태 감소: O-R이 수동보다 약 18% 더 많이 감소

**하위 군 분석**:
- **교정 환자**: 효과 가장 큼 (O-R이 교정기·호선 사이를 효과적으로 세정)
- **소아**: 효과 큼 ("스킬 이퀄라이저" — 손재주 부족 보완)
- **노인**: 효과 유의하지 않음 (악력·관절염·인지/시각 결함)

## 5b. TBI delivery: digital, behavioral and individualized targeting (added 2026-09-30)

**Question.** The held baseline is that individual, repeated toothbrushing instruction (TBI) with a plaque-disclosing agent beats lectures or model demonstration (Choi 2013: PHP improvement +24.0 vs +2.8 points; Choi 2017: 4-5 visits at 1-2 week intervals), and that no single manual technique is superior (Rajwani 2020). Nine newer papers ask whether *how* the instruction is delivered — VR, smart mirror, intra-oral camera, app plus hands-on practice, behavior-theory staging, cartoon aids, site-targeted plaque charts — changes outcomes beyond that baseline.

| [[behavioral-dentistry/motivational-interviewing/valeriani-2026-influence-psychological-factors-biofilm-related]] | SR, 10 prospective studies, adults | Psychological exposures vs later biofilm-related outcomes | Self-efficacy predicted hygiene behaviour and, after motivation + OHI, plaque control, but inconsistently; perceived benefits and TRA variables associated with gingival/plaque outcomes | Abstract-only; no meta-analysis; associations not causal |

### Synthesis

1. **The comparator, not the technology, predicts the result.** Enriched instruction beat *weak* controls — a single verbal session plus pamphlet (Lin 2025), standard advice without images (Holloway 2022), standard instruction without stage-tailored planning for plaque (Baumann 2025). Against *active* hands-on instruction, the technology was equivalent: STM = verbal chairside TBI in children (Jeong 2022), cartoon aid = acrylic model in preschoolers (Saraf 2024). This mirrors the older Korean finding that individual disclosing-agent TBI outperforms lectures and models (Choi 2013, Choi 2017): what separates arms is active, individual, feedback-rich instruction, not the medium.

2. **Kwon 2026 is the only trial where a digital medium beat an active disclosing-agent comparator** (PI reduction 0.44 vs 0.07 at 2 weeks). It is a single 30-person trial with unblinded examiners, retrospective registration and a 2-week horizon, and both arms rebounded from the immediate post-education PI by week 2 (control 2.87 vs baseline 2.94; VR 2.47 vs 2.92). Read it as "interactive VR is at least not worse than disclosing-agent TBI in the short term," not as a demonstrated upgrade.

3. **The shared active ingredient appears to be showing patients their own plaque with feedback.** Disclosing-agent typodont practice (Lin), intra-oral-camera images of the patient's own mouth (Holloway), a VR plaque gauge that falls only with correct 45-degree Bass technique (Kwon) and real-time STM voice feedback (Jeong) are all feedback loops. This is a synthesis inference across trials that did not isolate the component; none of the held trials dismantles feedback from other elements.

4. **Plaque moves; gingival bleeding mostly does not.** BOP was unchanged in both Kwon arms, GBI fell equally with standard and HAPA instruction (Baumann), and Holloway's gingival and bleeding scores only approached significance. Lin 2025 did show a GI difference (0.9 vs 1.5), but it carries the ~6x contact-time confound. Mitani 2024 reports lower PISA with deep-pocket-targeted instruction but without numbers. A delivery-mode upgrade therefore has no held, unconfounded evidence of added gingival benefit over standard instruction plus professional cleaning.

5. **Individualization is shifting from "which technique" to "which target."** Rajwani 2020 found no superior technique; the newer trials instead individualize *where* (Mitani: plaque at PD >=4 mm sites rather than whole-mouth O'Leary PCR, to avoid instruction drifting to stained areas that are not deep pockets), *whom by readiness* (Baumann: HAPA stages, written goal and coping planning), and *what to show* (Holloway: the patient's own images). Valeriani 2026 gives prospective, though inconsistent, support that self-efficacy relates to later plaque control after motivation and OHI — a plausible mechanism for staged instruction, not proof.

6. **Duration is an instruction target the device literature under-reads.** Uninstructed adults brush ~87 s with a manual brush versus ~151 s with a powered brush (Thomassen 2026). No held page tests whether part of the powered-brush plaque advantage (Luo 2026) travels with longer brushing time; that remains an open question, not a finding. Practically, timing the patient's actual brushing is a cheap chairside check against the 2-minute guideline.

### Confounds and evidence grade

| Confound | Where |
|---|---|
| Contact time / dose unequal between arms | Lin 2025 (~180 vs 30 min) |
| Small samples | Holloway n=22, Kwon n=30, Jeong n=42, Saraf n=43, Baumann n=62; Mitani n not reported |
| Short follow-up | Saraf 8 d, Kwon 2 wk, Holloway 4 wk, Jeong 1 mo, Baumann 12 wk, Lin 3 mo, Mitani 5-6 mo |
| Retrospective trial registration | Kwon 2026, Jeong 2022 (Lin 2025 gives no registration number) |
| Unblinded outcome assessment | Kwon 2026 (examiners unblinded) |
| Behavior outcome in one arm / self-report | Baumann 2025 (interdental-brush use measured in HAPA arm only); Holloway 2022 (self-reported confidence) |
| Abstract-only evidence | Mitani 2024, Saraf 2024, Holloway 2022, Valeriani 2026 |

**Grade.** Low to very low certainty that any delivery mode is superior to individual disclosing-agent TBI. Moderate-certainty only for the descriptive duration figure for manual brushing (Thomassen 2026 GRADE). There is no held SR/MA pooling TBI delivery modes.

**Clinical reading.** Keep individual, disclosing-agent-based TBI with repeated reinforcement (Choi 2013/2017) as the default. Low-cost add-ons consistent with the held evidence: show the patient images of their own plaque, direct instruction to sites with PD >=4 mm when such pockets are present, add a stage-appropriate written goal and obstacle planning for older adults, and check actual brushing time. Do not expect VR or smart-mirror systems to outperform a hygienist doing active chairside instruction, and do not promise added gingival benefit from the delivery upgrade itself.
