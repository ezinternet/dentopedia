> - **피질골 (Cortical Bone)**: 뼈 외층, 공극률 (Porosity) 5–10%, 하버시안 시스템 (Haversian System, Osteon) 구조, 굽힘·비틀림 저항; 임플란트 1차 안정성 (Primary Stability) 기여
> - **해면골 (Trabecular Bone)**: 뼈 내부, 공극률 50–90%, 소주 망상 (Trabecular Network) 구조, 압축 분산; 대사 활성 높고 리모델링 빠름
> - **섬유골 (Woven Bone)**: 미성숙, 콜라겐 배열 무질서, 빠른 형성; 발치 후 소켓·골절 치유 초기·임플란트 BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 초기에 등장
> - **층판골 (Lamellar Bone)**: 성숙, 콜라겐 평행 층 구조, 기계적으로 우수; 리모델링 완료 후 피질골·해면골 모두 층판 구조로 교체됨
> - **임상 핵심**: 초기 BIC = 섬유골(Woven) → 수개월 리모델링 후 층판골(Lamellar)로 교체; 최종 골유착 (Osseointegration) 상태의 BIC는 층판골
> - **골밀도 분류 (Misch D1–D4)**: D1·D2 = 피질골 우세(>1250 HU) → 1차 안정성 우수; D3·D4 = 해면골 우세 → 2차 안정성 (Secondary Stability)에 의존
> - **안정성 강하 (Stability Dip, ~3–4주)**: 섬유골 흡수·층판골 형성 전환 구간; 피질골:해면골 비율이 낮을수록 dip 심화
> - **병적 섬유골**: Paget 병에서 비정상 파골세포 과활성 → woven bone 재등장 (병적 골재건)
> - **스트레스 차폐 (Stress Shielding)**: 임플란트 주위 나사산 간 골은 낮은 shear strain → 리모델링 시 흡수; 피질골 비율이 최종 골부피 결정에 critical

## Three-line Summary

Two independent classification axes govern bone tissue types: maturity (woven ↔ lamellar) and location/density (cortical ↔ trabecular). Woven bone is rapidly deposited, disorganized collagen matrix — the universal first response to healing (extraction socket, fracture, early BIC) — subsequently replaced by organized lamellar bone through remodeling. Cortical vs trabecular reflects architectural density and spatial position; both mature into lamellar structure, but differ in porosity (5–10% vs 50–90%), mechanical role, and metabolic turnover rate.

## Thesis

The four bone tissue types represent two orthogonal classification dimensions. The maturity axis (woven → lamellar) describes the temporal sequence of bone formation and remodeling: woven bone is the rapid, mechanically inferior scaffold deposited in any healing context, which remodeling progressively converts to organized lamellar bone. The architecture axis (cortical ↔ trabecular) describes spatial and density organization independent of maturity: both cortical and trabecular bone are ultimately lamellar in mature skeleton. For implant dentistry, understanding this two-axis framework explains why early BIC readings capture woven bone and why final osseointegration quality depends on lamellar replacement, and why bone density classification (Misch D1–D4) maps onto the cortical:trabecular ratio to predict primary vs secondary stability.

## Clinical Decision Points

### 1. Interpreting BIC at Different Healing Timepoints

Early BIC (≤4 weeks) = woven bone contact — mechanically weaker, not yet osseointegrated in the lamellar sense. Final BIC (≥12 weeks conventional, ≥8 weeks with enhanced surfaces) = lamellar bone contact — functional osseointegration. Do not equate early histological bone contact with mature osseointegration quality.

### 2. Bone Density Classification → Expected Healing Trajectory

| Misch Class | HU | Dominant Type | Primary Stability | Stability Dip | Loading Timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | >1250 | Cortical | High | Mild | Conventional |
| D2 | 850–1250 | Cortical + coarse trabecular | Good | Moderate | Conventional–Early |
| D3 | 350–850 | Thin cortical + fine trabecular | Moderate | Pronounced | Delayed preferred |
| D4 | <350 | Trabecular only | Low | Severe | Delayed / augment first |

### 3. Woven Bone as a Healing Marker

Histological presence of woven bone (disorganized collagen, active osteoblasts) = active healing, not mature bone. In ridge preservation biopsies, woven bone at 8 weeks indicates ongoing remodeling — implant placement should await lamellar conversion. Paget disease is the pathological re-emergence of woven bone in adults — a diagnostic red flag on biopsy.

### 4. Stress Shielding and Long-term Bone Volume

Irandoust-2020 computational data: thread-region bone (lowest shear stimulus) is resorbed during remodeling regardless of initial woven bone volume. Implication: peri-implant trabecular bone visible on early CBCT will undergo stress-shielding-driven resorption; long-term bone volume is governed by load distribution at the cortical collar, not the initial woven/trabecular infill.

## Gaps & Future Research

- **Human histological data on woven-to-lamellar transition timeline**: most quantified timelines are animal model or computational; human socket histology (Trombelli 2008) is 12–24 week biopsy, not implant-specific.
- **HU thresholds for trabecular vs cortical dominance**: existing D1–D4 boundaries are consensus-based (Misch 1990s); CBCT-based HU-to-bone-type mapping needs prospective validation.
- **Stress shielding quantification in vivo**: Irandoust-2020 findings are computational (FEM) — no RCT or prospective histology validates the 35% thread-region resorption prediction at 15,000 days.
