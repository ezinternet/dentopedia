## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **명제**: 임플란트 보철은 두 층으로 나눠 설계한다 — **지대주 (Abutment)는 생물학적 봉쇄 (Biologic Seal)와 변연골 안정**을, **크라운 (Crown)은 치태 조절 (Plaque Control)과 점막 건강**을 맡는다. 이 페이지는 강의 슬라이드의 8개 항목을 위키가 가진 논문 33편에 하나씩 대조한 **근거 점검표**다. 항목마다 근거 강도가 다르다.
> - **지대주 ① 연조직 수직 두께 ≥3 mm** — 🟢 근거 강함. 골정상 약 2 mm 식립에서 얇은 점막(≤2 mm)은 1년 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 1.35 mm, 두꺼운 점막(≥3.1 mm)은 0.12 mm였다(Linkevicius 2009). 얇은 점막을 막(membrane)으로 두껍게 하면 1.22 → 0.24 mm로 줄어 원래 두꺼운 점막과 차이가 없었다(Puisys 2015). 얇은 점막에서 **골하 2 mm 식립 + 3 mm 지대주**는 0.12 mm, **골정 식립 + 1 mm 지대주**는 0.95 mm였다(Pico 2019 RCT). 다만 높이와 깊이를 함께 바꾼 설계다. [확인]
> - **① 의 단서 — 두께의 영향은 조건부다 (Spinato RCT 2편)**: 골정상에 식립한 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS) 골수준 임플란트에서는 **점막 두께와 무관하게 지대주 높이만** 변연골소실을 갈랐다. 12개월 MBL은 짧은 지대주 0.59–0.80 mm vs 긴 지대주 0.28–0.37 mm였다(Spinato 2019). 6개월 MBL은 1 mm 지대주 0.61(얇은)/0.64(두꺼운) mm vs 3 mm 지대주 0.32/0.26 mm였다(Spinato 2020). 즉 이 조건에서는 **3 mm 지대주가 얇은 점막의 불이익을 상쇄**한다. 두께의 영향은 골정상 위 식립(Linkevicius 2009)이나 막 증대 비교(Puisys 2015)에서 나타났다. [확인]
> - **조직수준 (Tissue-level) 임플란트**: 점막 두께에 맞춰 식립 깊이를 조절했다(얇음 2 mm 골하, 중간 1 mm 골하, 두꺼움 골정). 그래도 식립~18개월 골흡수는 얇은 점막 1.05 mm, 두꺼운 점막 0.25 mm로 얇은 점막이 예측인자였다(p=0.007). 크라운 장착 후에는 0.14 mm로 안정적이었다(Spinato 2025). 깊게 식립하면 처리 표면의 조기 노출이 줄었다(비노출 100% vs 53.3%, Spinato 2022). 골흡수는 **보철 전 생물학적 폭 (Biological Width) 형성 단계**에서 대부분 끝난다. [확인]
> - 단, 위키의 두께 증대 연구는 **동종 막·무세포 진피기질 (Acellular Dermal Matrix, ADM)**을 썼다. 슬라이드가 말하는 **결합조직이식 (Connective Tissue Graft, CTG)**을 이 질문에 직접 시험한 논문은 위키에 없다. [확인]
> - **지대주 ② 지대주 높이 ≥2 mm** — 🟢 근거 강함. 계통적 고찰·메타분석 (SR+MA)에서 MD 0.26 mm(95% CI −0.02~0.53, GRADE 중등도)였고 탐침깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD)·탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)과는 무관했다(Tajti 2024). 짧은 지대주는 18개월 근심 MBL 0.565 mm, 긴 지대주는 0.295 mm였다(Galindo-Moreno 2014). 지대주 높이가 1 mm 늘 때마다 MBL 발생 odds가 37% 줄었다(OR 0.63, Misch 2025). 회귀식에서 **MBL이 0이 되는 지대주 높이는 PS 2.5 mm, 비-PS 3.0 mm**였다(Spinato 2018, 후향·초록만). 따라서 "≥2 mm"는 최소선이고, 가능하면 2.5–3 mm를 목표로 한다. [확인]
> - **지대주 ③ 골정 가까운 2 mm는 완만하고 오목하게** — 🟢 근거 강함. 오목형 지대주는 원통형보다 연조직 높이 3.57 vs 2.95 mm, 장벽상피 2.46 vs 1.89 mm였고 염증도 적었다(Camacho-Alonso 2024 RCT, 조직학). 6개월 MBL은 0.4 vs 0.6 mm였다(Pérez-Sayans 2022 RCT). 각도–MBL 상관은 **접합부 0–2 mm에서만** 유의했다(Han 2025). 그래서 "2 mm" 구간이 이 위치에 놓인다. [확인]
> - **지대주 ④ 최종 지대주 조기 체결 (One-Abutment-One-Time, OAOT)** — 🟡 효과는 일관되나 작다. 무작위 대조시험 (RCT) 7편의 SR+MA에서 MD −0.23 mm(I² 77%, Abayov 2026)였다. 개별 RCT는 0.48 mm 차이(Grandi 2014)부터 차이 없음(Moreira 2021)까지 갈린다. 유병률 신호(교체군 임플란트주위염 55.6% vs 5.6%, Krebs 2024)는 후향 연구라 크기를 그대로 믿기 어렵다. [확인]
> - **크라운 ① 과도하게 볼록한 윤곽 회피** — 🟢 근거 강함(연관 근거). 골수준 임플란트에서 출현각 (Emergence Angle, EA)이 30°를 넘으면 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 31.3% vs 15.1%, **볼록 윤곽 + >30°는 37.8%**였다(Katafuchi 2018). 조직수준 임플란트에서는 연관이 없었다. 전치부 RCT에서 볼록 윤곽의 순측 퇴축은 64.3%, 오목 윤곽은 14.3%였다(OR 12.69, Siegenthaler 2022). [확인]
> - **크라운 ② 치경부 치간공극 (Cervical Embrasure) 개방** — ⚪ **위키에 직접 근거 없음**. 공극 크기나 청소 가능성을 독립 변수로 측정한 논문이 없다. 가장 가까운 근거는 "윤곽·각도가 위생 접근을 바꾼다"는 계통적 고찰의 해석뿐이다(Soulami 2022). [확인]
> - **크라운 ③ 인접면 접촉 (Proximal Contact) 길이 조절** — ⚪/🟡. 접촉 **길이·높이**를 다룬 논문은 없다. 위키가 가진 것은 **접촉 상실** 근거다. 개방 접촉은 임플란트주위점막염 (Peri-implant Mucositis) RR 1.74였고 골소실·주위염과는 유의하지 않았다(Atieh 2026). 통합 근심 접촉 상실은 44.2%였다(Sheba 2023). [확인]
> - **크라운 ④ 가능하면 치은연상 마진** — 🟡 간접 근거. 마진이 깊어질수록 잔존 시멘트가 늘었고(시험관 연구), 저자는 인접·설측 마진 깊이 ≤1.5 mm를 권한다(Gehrke 2019). 임상 연구 65명에서는 언더컷이 클수록 잔존 시멘트가 많았다(연조직 p=0.004, Vindasiute 2015). **시멘트 유지형 보철에만** 해당한다. [확인]
> - **임상 takeaway**: 우선순위는 **두께 확보(또는 PS 골정상 식립이면 3 mm 지대주로 상쇄) → 높이 ≥2 mm(목표 2.5–3 mm) → 0–2 mm 구간 오목 → 볼록·>30° 금지(골수준)**다. OAOT는 비용이 작을 때 하는 보조 수단이다. 치간공극과 접촉 길이는 생물학적 원칙으로는 타당하지만, 위키 근거로는 아직 숫자를 말할 수 없다.

## Three-line Summary

A checklist-style synthesis that tests each of eight lecture take-home points — four for the abutment (biologic seal, bone stability) and four for the crown (plaque control, mucosal health) — against the 33 papers the wiki holds, assigning each point an evidence grade.
The abutment-side points (≥3 mm vertical soft tissue, abutment height ≥2 mm, a gentle concave profile in the first 2 mm above the platform, early definitive abutment) are all supported, the first three strongly and OAOT modestly (pooled −0.23 mm).
On the crown side, avoiding over-convex contours is well supported in bone-level implants, the supragingival-margin point rests on cement-remnant evidence, and the "open cervical embrasure" and "proximal contact length" points have no direct quantitative evidence in the wiki.

## 세줄요약

강의 슬라이드의 8개 항목(지대주 4·크라운 4)을 위키가 가진 논문 33편에 하나씩 대조해 근거 등급을 매긴 점검표형 종합이다.
지대주 쪽 네 항목(연조직 ≥3 mm, 지대주 높이 ≥2 mm, 접합부 위 2 mm 오목, 최종 지대주 조기 체결)은 모두 지지된다. 앞의 셋은 강하게, OAOT는 약하게(통합 −0.23 mm) 지지된다.
크라운 쪽은 '과볼록 회피'가 골수준 임플란트에서 잘 지지되고, '치은연상 마진'은 잔존 시멘트 근거로 간접 지지된다. '치간공극 개방'과 '접촉 길이 조절'은 위키에 직접적인 정량 근거가 없다.

## Summary

This page sits under [[overviews/abutment-emergence-profile-peri-implant-tissue-overview]] (the lever hierarchy) and next to [[overviews/emergence-angle-threshold-context-dependence-overview]] (why the 30° cut-off is conditional). Those two pages are organized by *mechanism*. This one is organized the way a clinician uses the material: as a **delivery checklist**. It splits the transmucosal restoration into two jobs. The **abutment** builds a stable biologic seal and protects the crestal bone. The **crown** keeps the mucosa cleansable and uninflamed. Each claim below is graded against held evidence only: 🟢 consistent RCT/SR or several concordant cohorts; 🟡 direction consistent but effect small, indirect or observational; ⚪ no direct evidence in the wiki.

### Abutment — biologic seal and marginal-bone stability

**A1. Secure ≥3 mm of vertical soft tissue; if thin, go subcrestal with a tall abutment, or thicken the tissue (🟢).**
The thickness effect is the best-replicated finding in the set. In [[implants/mbl/linkevicius-2009-reaction-crestal-bone-around-implants]], implants placed about 2 mm supracrestally lost 1.35 ± 0.33 mm (thin, ≤2 mm), 0.32 mm (medium) and 0.12 ± 0.16 mm (thick, ≥3.1 mm) at 1 year. The companion cohort [[implants/mbl/linkevicius-2009-influence-soft-tissue-thickness-crestal-bone]] shows the same direction (thin 1.61 vs thick 0.26 mm mesially). Thickening reverses it. In [[implants/mbl/puisys-2015-influence-mucosal-tissue-thickening-crestal-bone]], untreated thin mucosa lost 1.22 mm mesially, versus 0.24 mm after allogenic-membrane thickening and 0.22 mm in naturally thick mucosa (thickened vs thick, P = 0.909). [[implants/soft-tissue/puisys-2015-use-acellular-dermal-matrix-membrane]] shows ADM raises vertical thickness from 1.54 to 3.75 mm, a gain that crosses the ~3 mm line. The surgical alternative is in [[implants/mbl/pico-2019-abutment-height-implant-depth-thin-mucosa]]: in thin mucosa, 3-mm abutments on implants placed 2 mm subcrestally lost 0.12 mm at 12 months versus 0.95 mm for 1-mm abutments on equicrestal implants. Height and depth were co-varied, so the effect belongs to the *combination*.
**Thickness is conditional, not universal (Spinato).** Two RCTs on equicrestal, platform-switched bone-level implants found abutment height, *not* mucosal thickness, decided MBL. [[implants/mbl/spinato-2019-biological-width-establishment-around-dental]] (cluster RCT, 70 implants, ≤2 vs >2 mm mucosa × 1 vs 3 mm abutments) reported 12-month MBL of 0.59–0.80 mm with short versus 0.28–0.37 mm with long abutments, irrespective of thickness. [[implants/mbl/spinato-2020-influence-of-abutment-height-and]] (80 implants) reported 6-month MBL of 0.61/0.64 mm (thin/thick) with 1-mm versus 0.32/0.26 mm with 3-mm abutments. Read with Linkevicius (thickness mattered only for *supracrestal* placement) and Puisys, this means the thin-mucosa penalty is real, but **a tall abutment on an equicrestal PS implant largely absorbs it**. Thickening is therefore one route, not the only one. For **tissue-level implants**, [[implants/mbl/spinato-2022-influence-of-apico-coronal-positioning]] placed implants 2 mm subcrestal (thin), 1 mm (medium) or equicrestal (thick). Five-month bone loss was still 0.66 / 0.32 / 0.22 mm (p = 0.025), but deeper placement kept the treated surface unexposed (100% vs 53.3%). Its follow-up, [[implants/mbl/spinato-2025-marginal-bone-changes-around-tissue]], shows placement-to-18-month loss of 1.05 / 0.37 / 0.25 mm (thin predicts, p = 0.007; smoking, p = 0.028), yet only 0.14 ± 0.56 mm after crown delivery. Most loss is biologic-width establishment that finishes **before** the prosthesis.

*Caveat on the slide wording*: the held thickening studies used allogenic membrane/ADM. No held paper tests **CTG** for this crestal-bone question, so "CTG" on the slide is an extrapolation by graft type, not a tested claim.

**A2. Abutment height ≥2 mm (🟢).**
The SR+MA [[implants/mbl/tajti-2024-less-marginal-bone-loss]] (bone-level, platform-switched, OAOT) found less MBL with ≥2 mm abutments at 1 year (MD 0.26 mm, 95% CI −0.02 to 0.53, moderate certainty), with no effect on PPD or BOP. [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]] adds the time course: short abutments lost 0.565 mm versus 0.295 mm mesially at 18 months, and most of the loss happened in the first 6 months. [[implants/soft-tissue/misch-2025-abutment-height-emergence-angle-peri-implant-bone-loss]] shows height *gates* angle. Each extra millimetre cut the odds of any MBL by 37% (OR 0.63). The short-abutment/wide-angle group carried peri-implantitis OR 4.19. A tall abutment largely neutralized a ≥30° angle. [[implants/mbl/spinato-2018-minimum-abutment-height-to-eliminate]] (retrospective, maxilla, abstract-only) estimated the abutment height at which MBL reaches zero: **2.5 mm with platform switching, 3.0 mm without**. It also found no difference between native and grafted bone. Read as a floor, ≥2 mm is the minimum; 2.5–3 mm is the target. Height is a **hard-tissue** lever; it does not change soft-tissue inflammation markers.

**A3. The first ~2 mm above the platform: gentle and concave, not convex (🟢).**
[[implants/camacho-alonso-2024-histological-and-immunohistochemical-soft-tissue]] supplies the mechanism (split-mouth RCT with histology, thick mucosa). Concave 3-mm abutments carried more soft tissue (3.57 vs 2.95 mm) and a longer barrier epithelium (2.46 vs 1.89 mm). They also had more perpendicular collagen (23.8 vs 15.7%) and less inflammation (all p<0.001). [[implants/mbl/perezsayans-2022-impact-abutment-geometry-early-marginal]] translates this to bone: 6-month MBL was 0.4 mm concave versus 0.6 mm cylindrical (p = 0.030). It also found a negative height × shape interaction, so the A2 height rule should not be transferred unchanged across shapes. Why "2 mm": [[implants/mbl/han-2025-cad-cam-abutment-profile-angle-mbl]] found profile-angle–MBL correlations only 0–1 and 1–2 mm from the junction, not 2–3 mm. [[implants/mbl/nam-2025-the-link-between-abutment-configuration]] shows the coupling extends to 2–3 mm for subcrestal implants measured from the platform. So **for subcrestal placements the "critical" band is deeper than 2 mm**, and the slide's number is a crestal/equicrestal default.

**A4. Connect the definitive abutment early; minimize disconnections (OAOT) (🟡).**
The direction is consistent; the size is small. Pooled: [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]] (7 RCTs, 645 implants) gave MD −0.23 mm (95% CI −0.44 to −0.02, I² 77%), with no PPD difference. [[implants/mbl/wang-2017-one-time-versus-repeated-abutment]] also favours OAOT, but its random-effects SMDs are implausibly large; trust its direction, not its magnitude. Individual trials span the range. [[implants/mbl/grandi-2014-one-abutment-one-time-versus]] (post-extractive immediate loading) found 0.11 vs 0.58 mm, a difference of 0.48 mm that the authors themselves doubt is clinically meaningful. [[implants/mbl/hamudi-2022-the-association-of-one-abutment]] found 0.35 vs 0.57 mm at 3 years ("minimal"). [[implants/mbl/moreira-2021-one-abutment-one-time-effect]] was equivalent (p = 0.330). [[implants/mbl/degidi-2011-one-abutment-at-one-time]] found no vertical difference, only less horizontal remodelling. The disease signal comes from [[implants/peri-implantitis/krebs-2024-influence-of-repeated-implant]]: 55.6% versus 5.6% implant-level peri-implantitis, OR 6.13 per extra replacement. It is retrospective, small and single-system, so it is a hypothesis, not an effect size. **Verdict**: OAOT is a low-cost add-on, not a substitute for A1–A3.

### Crown — plaque control and mucosal health

**C1. Avoid over-convex crown contours (🟢 for bone-level implants; association).**
[[implants/peri-implantitis/katafuchi-2018-restoration-contour-is-a-risk]] (168 implants, 10.9 years): peri-implantitis 31.3% when EA >30° versus 15.1% when ≤30°. Convex + >30° reached 37.8% (interaction p = .003), and tissue-level implants showed no association. [[implants/peri-implantitis/yi-2020-emergence-angle-peri-implantitis-cross-sectional]] reproduces the bone-level-only pattern (EA ≥30°, convex profile, bilaterally splinted middle implants). Soft-tissue RCTs: [[implants/soft-tissue/siegenthaler-2022-convex-emergence-profile-mucosal-recession-rct]] found mid-facial recession 64.3% convex vs 14.3% concave (OR 12.69). [[implants/peri-implantitis/wang-2022-buccal-emergence-profile-peri-implant-rct]] found 0.13 vs 0.63 mm recession when the molar buccal profile targeted ~31° instead of 40°. [[implants/peri-implantitis/dixon-2019-restorative-design-and-associated-risks-peri]] frames restorative design as a modifiable risk factor against a background prevalence of mucositis "up to" 40–65% and peri-implantitis 20–47%. It gives no design thresholds. Context limits for the 30° number are in [[overviews/emergence-angle-threshold-context-dependence-overview]].

**C2. Open the cervical embrasure to leave a cleansable space (⚪ — no direct evidence held).**
No held paper measures embrasure dimension or brush/floss access as an exposure. The nearest support is interpretive. [[implants/peri-implantitis/soulami-2022-implant-abutment-emergence-angle-peri-implantitis-sr]] reads the angle/contour association as partly a **hygiene-access** effect. [[implants/peri-implantitis/bishti-2025-interproximal-devices-peri-implant-mucositis-sr-ma]] concerns interproximal cleaning devices, not crown shape. The point is biologically coherent with C1, but the wiki cannot give it a threshold.

**C3. Adjust the proximal contact length (⚪/🟡 — contact *loss* held, contact *length* not).**
No held paper tests contact length or contact height. What the wiki holds is the consequence of a **failed** contact. [[food-impaction/atieh-2026-open-proximal-contacts-peri-implant-diseases]] (SR+MA, 4,882 implants) found open contact raised mucositis risk (RR 1.74) but not MBL or peri-implantitis (low to very low certainty). [[food-impaction/sheba-2023-interproximal-contact-loss-systematic-meta]] pools mesial contact loss at 44.2% and distal at 27.5%. The management side (surveillance, re-contouring, MM-DD occlusal adjustment) is synthesized in [[overviews/food-impaction-proximal-contact-loss-overview]]. Practical reading: design a contact that stays closed and is checked over time. How long it should be is outside held evidence.

**C4. Supragingival (supramucosal) crown margin when possible (🟡 — indirect, cement-retained only).**
[[prosthetic-materials/tooth-preparation/gehrke-2019-influence-margin-location-luting]] (in-vitro, 0–3 mm margin depths) found cement remnants at every depth, increasing as the margin went deeper. The authors advise margins as coronal as possible and ≤1.5 mm proximally/orally. [[prosthetic-materials/vindasiute-2015-clinical-factors-influencing-removal-cement-excess]] (65 patients) found larger restoration–tissue undercuts left more cement (soft tissue p = 0.004), while implant location did not matter. Neither study measures peri-implant disease directly. The supporting chain runs from margin depth to residual cement to inflammation, and its last link is not tested here. For screw-retained crowns this point largely does not apply.

### Priority ladder (what to protect first)

1. **Tissue thickness**: measure it. If ≤2 mm, either thicken it (A1) or plan subcrestal placement with a tall abutment.
2. **Abutment height ≥2 mm** (A2). This is the strongest single prosthetic lever on bone, and it buffers the angle.
3. **0–2 mm above the platform concave** (A3); in subcrestal placements, extend the concave zone deeper.
4. **No convex/>30° crown contour on bone-level implants** (C1). Do not transfer this rule to tissue-level implants.
5. **OAOT** when it costs little (A4); **coronal margins** if cement-retained (C4).
6. **Embrasure and contact design** (C2, C3): apply clinical judgement, then keep the contact under maintenance surveillance.

### Evidence gaps

- **CTG versus allogenic/ADM thickening** for crestal-bone protection: not held.
- **Cervical embrasure geometry** and **proximal contact length/height** as exposures for mucositis or papilla: not held.
- Spinato 2018–2025 were ingested 2026-11-25 and folded into A1–A2. Three of the five are abstract-only (2018, 2019, 2020); 2019 reports MBL only as ranges across thickness groups.
- No held RCT tests **thin mucosa + tall abutment + supracrestal/tissue-level** head-to-head against thickening; the A1 reconciliation is inferential across designs.
- Most abutment-side numbers come from abstract-only ingests; effect sizes are transcribed, but methods could not be checked against full text.
