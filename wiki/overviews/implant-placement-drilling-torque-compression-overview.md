## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **골 압박 괴사 (Compression Necrosis)**: 과압박에 의한 무균성 골괴사가 조기 실패의 독립 기전으로 조직학적 확인됨 — 골아세포 테두리 부재·생존골 전무·골세포 빈 소공; 35–50 Ncm에서도 D2 골에 프리태핑 없이 언더사이즈 드릴링 시 발생 가능 (Ramesh 2024, n=1 증례+문헌고찰).
> - **드릴링 프로토콜이 삽입토크 (Insertion Torque, IT) 결정**: 골밀도별 프로토콜 효과 고도 유의 (D1-D4 모두 p<0.001); 방향은 언더사이즈 > 표준 > 오버사이즈로 일관. **D1에서는 오버사이즈 준비**가 옳고 언더사이즈는 80 Ncm 초과 유발; D4(10 PCF)에서는 어떤 조합도 25-30 Ncm 도달 불가 — 드릴링 조절의 한계 (Stoilov 2025 in vitro, 2340 osteotomies).
> - **치유챔버 거시형태 (Healing Chamber Macrogeometry)**: 동일 골절개에서 치유챔버 디자인이 IT를 29% 낮춤(5.70 vs 8.01 Ncm)에도 21일 후 중간·심부 골-임플란트 접촉률 (BIC)이 유의하게 높음 — C2 59.30% vs 40.30%, C3 42.10% vs 17.90% (Gehrke 2026 토끼 경골). **낮은 IT = 나쁜 결과'라는 직관을 반박** — IT와 BIC가 해리될 수 있음.
> - **토크 렌치 정밀도 — 제조사가 결정**: 스프링식 토크제한장치 (Spring-Style Mechanical Torque-Limiting Device, MTLD): Astra Tech 3.33% vs DIO 16.67% 중앙 편차; 40사이클 후 DIO 95%가 ±10% 초과 (Aydin 2026 in vitro). 마찰식 MTLD: BioHorizons 최대 25.83% 편차·76% 측정치 >10% 초과, Astra Tech·Dr. Idhe는 상대적으로 양호 (Fayaz 2014, 100 멸균 사이클). 모든 기기가 **체계적 과소전달 (under-delivery)** 경향.
> - **임상 프로토콜 위험**: 처방된 식립 토크(예: 35 Ncm)가 실제로 전달되지 않아 골유착 실패 위험 — 토크 렌치는 주기적으로 검증해야 하며, 제조사별로 교정 주기가 다름.
> - **단일치 치료계획 — 4부위 결정 분지**: 상악 전치(D3·심미 우선·플랫폼 3-4 mm 치은 하방)·상악 후치(D4·최종드릴 ~0.5 mm 언더사이즈·>4-6개월 치유)·하악 전치(D1-D2·열괴사 경계·3-4개월)·하악 후치(D2·하악관 2 mm·mental foramen 5 mm·1:1 C/I ratio); 치간 협측 ≥1.5 mm·근원심 6-7 mm·교합 간 5-7 mm 보편기준 (Srikanth 2025 내러티브 리뷰).
> - **GLP-1RA와 임플란트**: 세마글루타이드·리라글루타이드 사용 T2DM 환자에서 임플란트 주위 변연골 소실이 인슐린·메트포르민군보다 유의하게 적음 (p<0.01; Ahmad 2025 내러티브 리뷰). Wnt/β-catenin·MAPK 경로를 통한 혈당 독립적 골형성 효과. **RCT 없음 — 가설 수준이나 임상적으로 매우 중요한 신약 효과**.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 7 papers (1 case report+review, 2 in vitro, 1 in vivo preclinical, 2 narrative reviews, 1 retrospective-based narrative review) on the quality determinants of dental implant placement: the papers converge on the theme that three interrelated variables — bone compression intensity, drilling protocol choice, and torque-delivery accuracy — jointly determine whether the insertion torque prescribed by the treatment plan is actually delivered at the bone interface, and a fourth paper (Srikanth 2025) provides the region-specific planning framework in which these variables are applied.

The core tension is that insertion torque is simultaneously a goal (sufficient primary stability for osseointegration) and a hazard (overcompression necrosis), and Gehrke 2026 shows the tension can be partially resolved through macrogeometry: a healing-chamber design that seated 29% lower than standard (5.70 vs 8.01 Ncm) still achieved superior bone-to-implant contact (BIC 59.30% vs 40.30% at C2, p=0.0002), while Stoilov 2025 demonstrates that protocol choice shifts torque highly significantly across all four bone densities but is exhausted as a strategy in D4 bone.

Torque-wrench accuracy adds a hidden error at the delivery end: spring-style MTLDs deviate 3.33–16.67% by manufacturer (Aydin 2026), friction-style MTLDs reach 25.83% deviation after 100 sterilization cycles (Fayaz 2014), both with systematic under-delivery as the failure mode — so the torque value dialed by the surgeon may differ substantially from the torque actually reaching the implant-bone interface.

## 세줄요약

7편(증례+문헌고찰 1·in vitro 2·전임상 in vivo 1·내러티브 리뷰 3) 종합: 골 압박 괴사는 35-50 Ncm에서도 D2 골+프리태핑 없음+언더사이즈 드릴링 조합에서 발생 가능하며 조직학적으로 확인(Ramesh 2024); 드릴링 프로토콜이 IT를 골밀도별로 고도 유의하게 변화시키나 D4에서는 효과 소진(Stoilov 2025); 치유챔버 거시형태는 IT 29% 낮추면서도 BIC 59.30% vs 40.30% 달성 — IT≠BIC 해리 중요 (Gehrke 2026).

토크 렌치는 제조사·사용횟수에 따라 3.33-25.83% 편차로 체계적 과소전달(Aydin 2026, Fayaz 2014); 단일치 치료계획은 4부위별 골밀도-치수-해부학 기준으로 분기(Srikanth 2025); GLP-1RA 사용 T2DM 환자에서 임플란트 주위 골 소실 유의 감소(Ahmad 2025, 후향 1편 — 가설 수준).

임상 결론: 골밀도 확인→프로토콜 선택(D1 오버사이즈/D2-D3 언더사이즈/D4 보조 전략)→토크 렌치 주기적 교정→프리태핑 고려→치유챔버 임플란트 선택 가능; GLP-1RA 복용 T2DM 환자는 유리한 조건으로 간주.

## Summary

This overview addresses the quality determinants of the implant placement act itself — from the diagnostic planning stage through the surgical technique and tool calibration — by synthesising papers scattered across the implants, implants/osteotomy-thermal, implants/survival, and prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw categories.

### Axis 1 — Bone compression necrosis: the failure mode at the extreme

Ramesh's 2024 case report (n=1, 33-year-old non-smoking female, mandibular molar, D2-D3) provides the most direct human evidence in this wiki for compression necrosis as an independent early-failure mechanism. The implant failed at 6 weeks with 50% bone loss; histology of the retrieved sequestrum showed bony trabeculae with absent osteoblastic riming, no viable bone anywhere in the specimen, and lacunae devoid of osteocytes — the hallmark triad of aseptic necrosis. Overheating was excluded (copious saline irrigation documented), and infection was excluded (CBC, ESR, viral markers all normal). What remained was overcompression: a 3.5 mm implant inserted with a 3.2 mm final drill, no pre-tapping, at 35–50 Ncm in dense crestal bone. The clinically important number is that this failure occurred **within the 35–50 Ncm range** widely considered safe — the risk appears when torque is generated by dense cortical bone resisting lateral compression rather than by controlled axial tapping against cancellous bone. The paper's prevention list: precise surgical technique, avoid torque beyond manufacturer's limit, pre-tapping in dense bone, and reversing the implant a quarter turn after seating.

### Axis 2 — Drilling protocol as the primary control variable

Stoilov's 2025 in vitro study (390 protocol groups × 6 sites = 2340 osteotomies per density, four polyurethane foam densities at 35/25/15/10 PCF) is the most comprehensive systematic manipulation of drilling protocol in this folder. Key findings:

**Direction is consistent across all densities**: undersized > standard > oversized (all p<0.001). The direction reversal in D1 is the major clinical surprise: in **dense bone (35 PCF/D1)** the conventional recommendation to under-prepare is wrong — undersized preparation in D1 risks IT exceeding 80 Ncm and incomplete seating, while **overpreparation reduces IT to the safe window**. In D2-D3 the undersized protocol moves torque into the target range for small-diameter implants. In D4 (10 PCF), nothing works — 0/10 target-window attainment across all designs and protocols, meaning soft bone requires entirely different strategies (wider implants, osseodensification, tilted placement) rather than drilling adjustment.

**Macro-geometry matters less than protocol** in this data: parallel-walled implants equalled or beat tapered designs in most conditions, and the tapered vs parallel difference was non-significant in D3. This demotes the instinct to always choose tapered implants in soft bone.

### Axis 3 — Macrogeometry and the IT/BIC dissociation

Gehrke's 2026 study (40 prototype implants, 5 rabbits, 21-day intra-animal design) introduces a critical conceptual correction to the IT-centric mindset: a macrogeometry with healing chambers (small holes between threads) seated at 29% lower IT than standard cylindrical (5.70 ± 0.85 vs 8.01 ± 0.82 Ncm, p<0.0001) yet achieved statistically superior BIC at the middle and apical cervical levels — C2 59.30% vs 40.30%, C3 42.10% vs 17.90% (p=0.0002). The improvement localizes to trabecular bone; at the coronal cortical level (C1) both designs were equivalent (74.50% vs 76.00%, NS), meaning crestal integrity is not sacrificed.

The mechanistic explanation is multi-factorial: reduced cortical compression preserves osteocyte viability; additionally, the healing chambers themselves provide blood clot stabilization, haptotaxis, and contact guidance for osteoprogenitor cells. The authors decline to isolate a single cause, which is scientifically honest. The key take-away is that the goal of insertion-torque optimization should shift from maximization to adequacy — sufficient for primary stability, low enough to avoid compressive necrosis — and macrogeometry is a tool that can uncouple these two objectives.

### Axis 4 — Torque wrench accuracy: the hidden delivery error

Two papers bracket the torque-delivery error from different angles.

**Spring-style MTLDs (Aydin 2026):** 9 devices (3 each from Implance, Astra Tech, DIO), 40 cycles of mechanical loading + steam sterilization. Astra Tech stayed within ±10% on 95% of measurements (median 3.33% deviation); Implance exceeded ±10% on 60%; DIO exceeded ±10% on 95% (median 16.67% deviation). χ²=99.241, p<0.001. The cycle × manufacturer interaction was significant (p<0.001): Astra Tech's error actually *improved* with cycling, while DIO deteriorated rapidly. No device underwent plastic deformation (pre-defined exclusion criterion). The failure mode is entirely manufacturer-dependent, not a property of spring-style design.

**Friction-style MTLDs (Fayaz 2014):** 15 devices (5 each from Astra Tech 25 Ncm, BioHorizons 30 Ncm, Dr. Idhe 30 Ncm), 100 sterilization cycles. All started within ±10%; BioHorizons degraded to 25.83% maximum deviation with 76% of readings >10% tolerance across all five devices; Astra Tech reached 13.4% and Dr. Idhe 12.5%. The failure direction in all cases was **under-delivery** (lower preload than targeted), which raises the risk of loose abutment screws rather than overloaded bone. The lubrication protocol (multi-point Astra Tech and Dr. Idhe vs. single-point BioHorizons) was proposed as the mechanism.

Combined clinical reading: **a torque wrench is not a passive instrument** — it is a consumable requiring periodic verification and brand-specific maintenance. A 35 Ncm prescription may deliver as little as 26–28 Ncm with a degraded DIO spring-style wrench, or 22 Ncm with a worn BioHorizons friction-style device, both in the range that compromises primary stability in softer bone.

### Axis 5 — Single-tooth treatment planning: the 4-region framework

Srikanth's 2025 narrative review (J Maxillofac Oral Surg, 65 citations) compiles the regional decision criteria that translate bone-quality and anatomic data into an operative plan. The four regions map to distinct priorities:

| Region | Bone density | Priority | Key surgical adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maxillary anterior | D3 | Esthetics | Platform 3-4 mm apical to gingival margin; ≥2 mm facial bone; palatal placement |
| Maxillary posterior | D4 | Primary stability | Under-drill ~0.5 mm; >4-6 months healing; sinus elevation if <10 mm vertical |
| Mandibular anterior | D1-D2 | Heat necrosis avoidance | Copious irrigation; bone tapping; 3-4 months healing |
| Mandibular posterior | D2 | Nerve safety + load | 2 mm IAN margin; 5 mm from mental foramen; 1:1 C/I ratio |

Universal minimums: mesiodistal 6-7 mm, buccolingual ≥6 mm (≥1.5 mm each side), interocclusal 5-7 mm, ≥1.5 mm to adjacent root, ≥3 mm between implants, papilla crest-to-contact ≤5 mm. The "triangle of bone" concept places the fixture to bisect the buccolingual bone volume, not to follow the socket trajectory. Immediate loading threshold: >30-35 Ncm; >35 Ncm for maxillary posterior.

### Axis 6 — Systemic context: GLP-1RAs and peri-implant bone

Ahmad's 2025 narrative review (20 studies: 10 in vitro, 9 animal, 1 retrospective clinical) synthesizes the emerging evidence that GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 수용체 작용제, GLP-1 Receptor Agonist, GLP-1RA) — semaglutide, liraglutide, exenatide — have pleiotropic benefits on peri-implant bone beyond glycemic control. Mechanism: Wnt/β-catenin + MAPK pathway activation → osteogenic differentiation of periodontal ligament stem cells even under high-glucose inflammatory conditions. The single clinical study (retrospective) found significantly less peri-implant marginal bone loss in GLP-1RA-treated T2DM patients vs insulin and metformin groups (p<0.01). Evidence level is preclinical-dominant — no RCT exists — but the frequency of GLP-1RA prescribing makes this a growing clinical reality. **Patients on semaglutide/liraglutide presenting for implant placement in T2DM may have favorable peri-implant bone conditions** relative to comparable patients on older antidiabetics; however, the companion paper (mawardi-2023, related) notes semaglutide-associated hyposalivation as a countervailing concern.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **식립 전 골밀도 분류**: CBCT Hounsfield 단위 확인 → Misch D 등급 배정 → 프로토콜 선택. D1은 오버준비, D2-D3는 언더준비, D4는 보조전략(넓은 임플란트, 오세오덴시피케이션).
2. **프리태핑 결정**: D2 이상 치밀골 + 언더사이즈 드릴링 조합 시 프리태핑 필수 고려 — Ramesh 증례는 프리태핑 없이 발생. 프리태핑 없는 고토크 식립이 진정한 위험 지점.
3. **토크 렌치 관리 프로토콜**: 최소 25-50 사이클마다(또는 분기마다) 디지털 게이지로 교정 확인. 제조사별 윤활 지침 준수. DIO 스프링식·BioHorizons 마찰식 기기에서 허용 편차 초과 가능성 높음 — 사용 기기 구체적 검증 필요.
4. **IT가 낮아도 거시형태가 BIC 보상 가능**: 치유챔버 임플란트 선택 시 식립 토크가 표준보다 낮게 나오더라도 즉시 실패로 해석하지 말 것 (Gehrke 2026). IT와 RFA/ISQ 병행 측정으로 실제 안정성 판단.
5. **GLP-1RA 복용 T2DM 환자**: 인슐린·메트포르민군보다 유리한 임플란트 환경 가능. 단, 타액 감소(semaglutide) 부작용 확인 — 구강 위생 지도 및 불소 관리 병행.
6. **4부위별 안전거리 준수**: 상악 전치 협측 ≥2 mm, 하악 후치 하악관 2 mm + mental foramen 5 mm; 이 기준은 수술 계획의 절대 마진.

## Gaps & Future Research

- 압박 괴사 발생 최소 토크 역치: 35 Ncm에서 발생 가능하다면 실제 위험 구간은? 전향 연구 또는 동물 모델 필요.
- 치유챔버 임플란트 임상 RCT: 21일 토끼 데이터를 사람·장기 생존율로 번역.
- 토크 렌치 교정 주기에 대한 표준화: 현재 제조사 및 병원마다 상이.
- GLP-1RA RCT for implant outcomes: 후향 1편 신호를 검증하는 무작위연구 급요.
