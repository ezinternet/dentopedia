> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 질문**: 교합을 재건할 때 기준이 되는 상하악 관계위(reference position)를 무엇으로 잡을 것인가 — 과두 기준의 중심위(Centric Relation, CR)/중심교합(Centric Occlusion, CO)인가, 치아 기준의 최대교두감합위(Maximum Intercuspation Position, MIP=교두감합위 Intercuspal Position, ICP)인가.
> - **용어 정리**: CR=과두가 관절와에 안정적으로 앉은 하악위(치아 무관). CO=그 CR 상태에서의 교합접촉위. MIP/MICP/ICP=치아가 최대로 맞물리는 습관적 위치(과두 위치 무관). 셋 다 "centric"이라 혼용되지만 같지 않다.
> - **사실 1 — CO≠MIP가 정상**: Best Evidence Consensus(Kattadiyil 2021)는 유치악·부분유치악자 **대다수에서 CO와 MIP가 불일치**한다고 결론. 이 둘 사이의 미끄러짐이 **중심활주(centric slide, CR-MIP/CR-ICP discrepancy)**.
> - **사실 2 — 정상 활주의 한계치**: Pullinger 기준 무증상자에서 시상 활주 **>2mm는 거의 없음**(Fornai 2022 인용). 즉 작은 활주는 정상, 큰 활주는 비정상 신호일 수 있음.
> - **논쟁 A (CR 폐기파)**: Zonnenberg/Türp/Greene(2021) — "중심위"는 의미·개념·실용적으로 결함이라 **용어 폐기** 주장; 건강 유치악자는 MIP가 생물학적으로 수용 가능하니 과두위 일상 평가 불필요.
> - **논쟁 B (CR 유지파)**: Goldstein(2022) — CR은 **재현 가능·근거 있는 기준위**(특히 전악재건); 문제는 개념이 아니라 정의·기록법의 비일관성; 다른 위치를 쓰려면 다른 이름을 붙이라.
> - **논쟁 C (절충/모니터링파)**: Fornai(2022) — 용어 "CR"은 폐기하되 임상적으로는 **과두위를 능동 감시**해야(생물학적≠임상적 정상); 대체어로 **기준위(Reference Position, RP)** 제안.
> - **논쟁 D (상황 한정파, 2026-07 추가)**: Yu(2024, 華西) — CR을 폐기하지도 옹호하지도 말고 **"논쟁이 적은 상황"에만 쓰라**며 예비 **결정트리**를 제시. CR의 유치악 적용을 세 질문으로 구조화: TMD 진단·치료의 핵심인가 / 교합재건의 기준 악위인가 / **교정의 목표 악위인가**. 세 번째 축(교정)은 이 오버뷰가 그동안 다루지 않던 영역이다. **단 실제 분기 조건은 중문 본문에만 있어 미확보** — 알고리즘 형식을 근거등급으로 오인 말 것(저자 스스로 "初步提出", kattadiyil의 BECS와 격이 다름).
> - **⭐ 신경근(myocentric) 위치는 시험받고 기각됐다**: 유치악 81명·술자 4명 독립·2회 반복·전자계측 교합기 — 신경근위치는 MIP도 CR도 아닌 **제3의 위치**(MIP↔NM 0.88±0.30mm, CR↔NM 0.83±0.27mm)이고 저자 결론은 **보철 수복에 권장 불가**. 근거는 평균이 아니라 **산포**다: 재현성 0.04~2.53, MIP↔NM 0.12~**5.98**, CR↔NM 0.10~**7.89mm**로 Radej의 임상유의 기준 ≥2mm를 넘는 꼬리가 있다. 단 이 논문의 CR 자체가 고딕아치 정점 기준이고, 결론이 표본에 없던 무치악까지 확장된 점은 한계 (Utz 2026).
> - **활주와 악관절장애(TMD)**: Jiménez-Silva(2017) SR — CR-ICP 불일치가 다수 연구서 근육통·관절잡음·디스크변위와 연관되나 **근거 질이 낮아(NOS 평균 3.36) 인과 확정 불가**. radej(2023)는 **과두발산형(hyperdivergent) 골격**이 CR→MIP 과두 후하방 변위의 최대 위험인자라고 정리.
> - **MIP의 "정상"도 모호**: Qadeer & Türp(2023) — MIP 교합접촉 수가 **11~70개로 광범위**, 검출지시재(교합지·shimstock·디지털)에 좌우 → 단일 정상치 없음. 즉 "MIP를 받아들이라"는 주장도 측정 표준이 없으면 검증 곤란.
> - **⚠️ 새 축 (2026-07) — 이 논쟁은 자기가 쓰는 자[尺]의 정밀도를 검증한 적이 없다**: 논쟁의 판별선은 **활주 2mm**(Pullinger/Fornai)와 Radej의 **≥2mm(수직·수평)·≥0.5mm(횡)**다. 그런데 그 선을 재는 도구들의 오차가 선과 같은 자릿수다 — 교합채득재는 왁스가 엘라스토머의 **약 6배** 편차(Rani 2024: Aluwax 0.94 vs 폴리에테르 0.136·PVS 0.152~0.165, **단위 원문 미표기**), 구강스캐너(IOS)는 MICP는 잘 잡지만 **측방위 민감도 28%**로 접촉 3/4를 놓치고(Morsy 2024), 검증 지표로 흔히 쓰는 **교합접촉 "개수"는 위치가 이동해도 안 변하는 둔감한 지표**다(Biren 2025). 즉 측정 표준화는 각주가 아니라 **논쟁의 하중을 받는 기둥**이다.
> - **다만 정직하게**: 위 수치들은 방향은 일관되나 근거가 약하다 — Rani·Tank는 JPBS 부록 3페이지 논문이고 단위 미표기, Tank는 최대 편차(hard wax)가 오히려 무유의(n=30·SD≈9), Wang(n=10)의 "IOS≈관행"은 무유의차일 뿐 등가성 근거가 아니다. **"자가 부정확하다"는 방향은 신뢰하되 개별 수치는 인용하지 말 것.**
> - **⭐ 새 축 (2026-09) — 자[尺]의 나머지 두 칸이 실은 채워져 있었다**: 위 "측정 문제" 논증은 재료·디지털만 다뤘고, 정작 CR을 **손으로 어떻게 잡는가(조작술)**와 **접촉을 무엇으로 찍는가(교합지)**를 비워두고 있었다. 둘 다 위키가 이미 들고 있었고, 조작술 쪽은 이 오버뷰 전체에서 가장 높은 근거등급이다. ① **조작술**: de Moraes Melo Neto(2021) **체계적 문헌고찰** — 6편 전부 양수조작(Bimanual Manipulation) 대 턱끝유도(Chin-point Guidance) 비교였고 양수조작이 3승 2무 1패로 우세(단 6편 모두 Jadad ≤2 저질). Jász(2024, n=34, 초음파 하악운동분석기 50 µm)는 7가지 방법을 MIP 기준으로 3D 측정 — **Dawson 양수조작과 혀끝-경/연구개 경계법만이 세 축 모두 MIP와 무유의차**, 고딕아치·혀끝-구개추벽은 전방, **능동·수동 턱 후퇴(retrusion)는 후방 편차**. ② 결정적으로 **7가지 방법의 편차가 전부 ±1 mm 이내** — 즉 조작술 오차는 논쟁의 판별선 2 mm **아래로 실제 경계지어진다**. 이건 "자가 부정확하다"는 이 오버뷰의 주장을 **부정하는 게 아니라 분해**한다: 미검증인 칸은 재료·디지털·검증지표이고, 조작술 칸은 (저질 근거이나마) 채워져 있다. ③ **교합지**: Rovira-Lastra(2026, n=32, 스캔 PVS 기준표준) — 진양성 81%·위양성 15%, **200 µm가 100 µm보다 정확**(위양성 9.4% vs 20.6%), **양측 궁 교두정 마크 일치를 요구하면 위양성 15%→2.0%**. 즉 이 논쟁이 기준표준으로 삼아온 교합지의 정확도는 이제 수치가 있고, 챙길 수 있는 개선책도 있다.
> - **⚠️ 자기상태 정정 (2026-09)**: 이전 판은 "교정 축은 이 위키에 논문이 없어 구멍"이라 단언했으나 **사실이 아니었다** — Jahanbin(2026) **RCT**(n=30, Class I 4소구치 발치, 디본딩 1개월 후 Dawson 양수조작으로 CR 유도 교합조정 + 견치유도 평형, T-Scan III로 6개월 추적)를 위키가 이미 보유 중이었다. 결과는 **음성**: 교합접촉 강도·개수 모두 무유의(전부 P>0.05), 초기 후방접촉 강화는 생리적 정착(settling)에 씻겨나감. 즉 교정 축은 비어 있는 게 아니라 **"이미 양호한 케이스에서는 CR 유도 교합조정이 측정 가능한 이득을 못 낸다"는 RCT 한 편이 들어와 있다**(n=15/군 검정력 부족·6개월 추적 한계).
> - **⛔ 사다리 최상단의 제약 — 활주를 발견해도 갈지 말 것**: Singh(2024) **Cochrane 체계적 문헌고찰** — 악관절장애(TMD)에 대해 **교합조정은 효과 없음**, 비가역적 교합 처치(삭제·크라운)는 이득 근거 없고 해악 위험 → **강한 반대 권고**. 이 논쟁 전체에서 근거등급이 가장 높은 문서이며, 방향이 명확하다: CR-MIP 활주는 **진단·설계상의 정보**이지 그 자체로 삭제의 적응증이 아니다. 아래 결정경로의 "활주 >2mm → 평가"는 **평가에서 멈추라**는 뜻이다.
> - **임상 합의점(실무 결론)**: ① **전악수복**처럼 기존 MIP를 잃거나 바꿔야 하면 → **재현 가능한 CR/CO를 기준위로** 채택(Kattadiyil·Goldstein), 채득은 **Dawson 양수조작**으로(de Moraes Melo Neto·Jász), 후퇴 유도법은 피한다. ② **건강하고 안정적인 유치악 + 소량 활주(<2mm)·무증상**이면 → 기존 **MIP를 수용**(Zonnenberg). ③ **과두 변형/불안정·TMD 징후**면 → 과두 안착을 신뢰 말고 **근육 유도 안정위/조기접촉 없는 ICP**로(Fukushima), 그리고 과두위 변화를 추적(Fornai). 변형 골격(hyperdivergent)·큰 활주는 경계. ④ 어느 경로든 **TMD를 이유로 한 비가역적 교합 삭제는 하지 않는다**(Singh 2024).
> - **공통 한계**: 정의 불일치, 저질·이질적 근거, 측정법 표준 부재. CR↔MIP 우월성을 가리는 고품질 RCT는 없고, 건강인에서 어느 위치로 회복하든 보철 부작용의 결정적 차이는 보고되지 않음.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 23 papers on maxillomandibular reference position for occlusal reconstruction: CO and MIP are non-coincident in most dentate patients (Kattadiyil 2021 Best Evidence Consensus), normal centric slide is <2 mm sagittal (Fornai 2022 citing Pullinger), and "centric relation" itself has a contested definition — Zonnenberg/Türp/Greene 2021 call for abandoning the term, Goldstein 2022 defends it as a reproducible restorative reference, Fornai 2022 keeps the concept under a renamed Reference Position, and Yu 2024 declines the whole either/or by restricting CR to situations of "little controversy" via a decision tree.

Practical decision: full-mouth/large reconstruction → build to a reproducible CR/CO (Kattadiyil, Goldstein), recorded by Dawson bimanual manipulation rather than chin-point guidance or any retrusion technique (de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 SR; Jász 2024); deformed/unstable condyles or TMD signs → target a muscle-guided premature-contact-free ICP (Fukushima 2016); healthy dentate with slide <2 mm and no symptoms → accept existing MIP (Zonnenberg 2021); hyperdivergent skeletal pattern is the strongest risk factor for posteroinferior condylar displacement on the CR→MIP path (Radej 2023 scoping review). Whatever the slide, it is not by itself an indication to grind: the Cochrane review finds occlusal adjustment ineffective for TMD and recommends strongly against irreversible occlusal modification (Singh 2024).

The measurement problem is load-bearing, not a footnote — but it is now partly decomposed rather than uniformly open. The manipulation link is bounded: all seven CR techniques compared on a 50 µm ultrasound motion analyser deviated ≤±1 mm from MIP, i.e. below the 2 mm threshold the debate discriminates on, with Dawson bimanual closest and retrusion techniques forcing the condyle posteriorly (Jász 2024, converging with de Moraes Melo Neto 2021). The remaining links are not: MIP contact count varies 11–70 by detection method (Qadeer & Türp 2023), articulating paper — the field's de facto reference standard — runs 81% true-positive with 15% false positives, halved by 200 µm paper and collapsed to 2.0% by a bilateral-coincidence rule (Rovira-Lastra 2026), wax interocclusal records deviate ~6× more than elastomers (Rani 2024, unit unstated; Tank 2024 same direction but underpowered), intraoral scanners capture MIP well but miss ~3 of 4 contacts in lateral excursion (Morsy 2024), and contact *count* stays constant while contact *positions* migrate (Biren 2025), so counting contacts cannot verify a chosen position. No high-quality RCT comparing CR/CO vs MIP-based reconstruction on long-term outcomes exists.

## 세줄요약

23편 종합: CO와 MIP는 유치악 대다수에서 불일치(Kattadiyil 2021 BECS); 정상 중심활주(CR-MIP slide) <2mm(Fornai 2022); "중심위(CR)"는 용어 자체가 논쟁 중 — 폐기파(Zonnenberg 2021)·유지파(Goldstein 2022)·감시파(Fornai 2022)에 더해, Yu(2024)는 양자택일을 거부하고 "논쟁이 적은 상황"에만 CR을 쓰는 결정트리를 제시.

임상 결정: 전악수복 → 재현 가능한 CR/CO로 구축(Kattadiyil, Goldstein)하되 채득은 Dawson 양수조작(Bimanual Manipulation)으로, 턱끝유도·후퇴법은 피한다(de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 SR; Jász 2024); 과두 불안정·TMD 징후 → 근육 유도 ICP(Fukushima 2016); 건강한 유치악·활주<2mm·무증상 → 기존 MIP 수용(Zonnenberg 2021); 과두개산형(hyperdivergent) 골격이 CR→MIP 후하방 과두변위 최강 위험인자(Radej 2023). 다만 활주 자체는 삭제의 적응증이 아니다 — Cochrane은 TMD에 대한 교합조정 무효·비가역 교합처치 강한 반대를 권고(Singh 2024).

측정 문제가 논쟁의 하중을 받되, 이제는 칸별로 나뉜다: 조작술 칸은 경계가 있다 — 7가지 CR 결정법 전부 MIP 기준 ±1 mm 이내(50 µm 초음파 하악운동분석기)로 판별선 2 mm **아래**이며, Dawson 양수조작이 가장 근접하고 후퇴법은 과두를 후방으로 민다(Jász 2024, de Moraes Melo Neto 2021과 수렴). 나머지 칸은 여전히 미검증이다 — MIP 접촉 수는 지시재에 따라 11~70개(Qadeer & Türp 2023), 기준표준으로 쓰이는 교합지(Articulating Paper)조차 진양성 81%·위양성 15%(200 µm로 반감, 양측 일치 규칙으로 2.0%까지 감소; Rovira-Lastra 2026), 왁스 교합채득재는 엘라스토머의 약 6배 편차(Rani 2024, 단위 미표기; Tank 2024은 같은 방향이나 검정력 부족), 구강스캐너는 MICP는 잘 잡아도 측방위 접촉 3/4를 놓치며(Morsy 2024), 접촉 "개수"는 접촉점이 이동해도 그대로라 검증 지표로 쓸 수 없다(Biren 2025). CR/CO vs MIP 기반 수복 head-to-head 고품질 RCT는 여전히 부재.

## Thesis

The clinical question is not "which single position is universally correct" but **"for this patient, in this clinical situation, which reference position is both reproducible and biologically safe?"** Three facts anchor the answer:

1. **CO and MIP are normally non-coincident** (Kattadiyil 2021): in most dentate/partially dentate people the teeth-defined intercuspal position (MIP/ICP) differs from the condyle-seated occlusal contact position (CO). The difference is the **centric slide (CR–MIP / CR–ICP discrepancy)**.
2. **A small slide is normal; a large one is a flag** — no asymptomatic subject in Pullinger's data had a sagittal slide >2 mm (cited by Fornai 2022).
3. **Neither "normal" is well-measured** — the MIP contact count itself ranges 11–70 depending on the detection method (Qadeer & Türp 2023), and the CR–ICP↔TMD association rests on low-quality evidence (Jiménez-Silva 2017).

The literature then splits on **what to do about condylar position**: abandon the concern in healthy dentate patients (Zonnenberg/Türp/Greene), defend CR as the reproducible restorative reference (Goldstein), or keep monitoring condylar position under a renamed "Reference Position" (Fornai). For unstable/deformed condyles the condyle cannot be trusted as a landmark at all, and a muscle-guided, premature-contact-free ICP is the safer target (Fukushima).

**One candidate position has now been tested and rejected on its own terms.** A fifth option periodically offered as the way out of this debate is the *neuromuscular* or myocentric position — the idea that muscles, left to close freely, will find a physiologic position superior to both a condyle-defined CR and a tooth-defined MIP. [[occlusion/utz-2026-neuromuscular-position-tmj-condyle-dentate]] is the fifth and final part of a programmatic series on condylar positions and is unusually well controlled for this literature: **81 fully dentate subjects with healthy oral function, four independent operators, every registration repeated twice, measured on a custom-built electronic measuring articulator**. The result is that the neuromuscular position is **a third, distinct position** — neither MIP nor CR: MIP↔neuromuscular 0.88 ± 0.30 mm, CR↔neuromuscular 0.83 ± 0.27 mm. The authors' conclusion is categorical: registration of the neuromuscular adduction field **should not be recommended for prosthodontic restorations**.

Read the dispersion rather than the means, because that is where the conclusion actually comes from. Reproducibility averaged 0.52 ± 0.16 mm but ranged **0.04 to 2.53 mm**; MIP↔neuromuscular ranged 0.12 to **5.98 mm**; CR↔neuromuscular ranged 0.10 to **7.89 mm**. The averages look clinically tolerable; the upper tails run well past the ≥2 mm that [[tmj/radej-2023-condylar-displacement-malocclusion-cr-mip-sr]] treats as clinically significant condylar displacement. A method whose own repeat measurement can miss by 2.5 mm cannot define a reference position for a restoration, whatever its mean says.

Two caveats keep this in proportion. The CR against which the neuromuscular position was judged was itself defined by a central-bearing-point registration **at the tip of the Gothic arch** — a substantive methodological choice, not a neutral fact, and a reader who rejects Gothic-arch CR would weigh the comparison differently. And the sample was **fully dentate with healthy oral function**, while the authors' recommendation extends to edentulous patients they did not enrol. Note also that this does not contradict Fukushima: his target is the patient's own chairside-verified habitual closing endpoint checked for premature contacts, not an operator-registered adduction point from rapid-closure recording — different constructs, so the two coexist.

**What Utz rejects is a method, not muscle-derived positioning as such** — and getting that boundary right matters, because the same paper sits next to Kwak 2025's function-defined therapeutic position, which held up over a decade. Utz does not overturn Kwak; it *bounds* him. Kwak's position is the apex of a **normalized chewing pattern (CM-H) recorded during actual hard-food mastication and re-recorded ten years later**; Utz's is an **isolated adduction point captured by an operator with a central bearing plate under rapid closure**. Both are "muscle-derived," and they behave completely differently: the first showed 63% CM-H↔MIP concordance stable across a decade, the second could not reproduce itself to better than 2.53 mm on repeat. The generalisable rule is therefore not "muscles cannot define a reference position" but **"a position derived from tracked habitual function may be trustworthy; a single instrument-registered adduction point is not."** Read carelessly, Utz would delete node 1's function-led variant; read correctly, it tells you which function-led variant to use.

**A fourth stance declines the either/or.** Yu 2024 (West China) accepts that CR's migration from edentulous complete dentures into dentate jaws is genuinely doubted, but observes that its clinical uses exist regardless — so instead of adjudicating the concept, the paper restricts it: it asks three separate questions (should CR be the core of TMD diagnosis and treatment? should CR be the jaw position to build occlusal reconstruction on? should CR be the target position in orthodontics?) and proposes a preliminary **decision tree** covering only the situations of "little controversy." Two things follow. First, it is convergent with this overview's own structure — an independent group arrived at situation-by-situation triage rather than a universal answer, which is weak but real corroboration that the question is mis-posed as "which position wins." Second, it opens an axis this synthesis had not covered: **CR as an orthodontic target position**. Yu's own branch conditions remain unavailable — they live in the Chinese-language body, which PMC does not host, so only the abstract is retrievable. Treat Yu as a signpost, not a source of rules; an algorithm's format confers authority its evidence grade does not (the authors themselves say "preliminarily put forward," and this is one group's narrative review, not a Best Evidence Consensus like Kattadiyil).

**But the orthodontic axis is not empty, and an earlier version of this page wrongly said it was.** The wiki holds [[occlusion/jahanbin-2026-occlusal-adjustment-post-orthodontic-tscan-rct]], and it is an **RCT** — the only randomised evidence anywhere in this synthesis. Thirty Class I four-premolar-extraction patients were randomised, one month after debonding, to a **centric-relation–guided occlusal adjustment** (Dawson bimanual manipulation after cotton-roll deprogramming, premature contacts marked at 8 µm, equilibrated to canine guidance) or to no adjustment, with T-Scan III recordings at debond, one month, and six months. The result is **negative**: no significant between-group difference in occlusal contact intensity or number, anterior or posterior, at any interval (all P>0.05). Posterior contacts intensified immediately after adjustment and then equalised, because teeth kept settling physiologically and washed the effect out; only a non-significant trend toward better posterior force distribution survived.

Two readings, and the weaker one is the safer. Strongly: deliberately seating a finished orthodontic case into CR and equilibrating to it buys nothing measurable within six months when the case already has acceptable intercuspation — which converges with Singh 2024's Cochrane verdict on occlusal adjustment from a completely different direction. Weakly, and more defensibly: the trial is **underpowered** (n=15 per arm, sized off a 5-patient pilot), followed for only six months, and enrolled *already acceptable* Class I cases — so it tests marginal benefit in the easy case, not whether CR matters in a malocclusion being finished badly. It is also worth naming what the null does *not* say: physiologic settling erasing a deliberate adjustment is itself evidence that the post-orthodontic occlusion is a moving target, which is an argument for restraint rather than for either reference position. Held at that strength, it still does real work here — it is the wiki's one randomised data point on CR as an orthodontic goal, and it does not support pursuing one.

**And the debate has a floor it has largely not inspected: its own instruments.** Every position above is discriminated by a number — Pullinger's 2 mm sagittal slide separating normal from abnormal (Fornai), Radej's ≥2 mm vertical/horizontal and ≥0.5 mm transverse thresholds for clinically significant condylar displacement. Those thresholds are only meaningful if the chain that produces them — **manipulation technique → record material → mounting → contact verification** — has error well below them. That chain has four links, and they are not in the same evidential state: one is bounded, three are not. Sorting them is the difference between a useful objection and a rhetorical one.

**The first link is the one with the best evidence, and it holds.** How the clinician's hands seat the mandible is the input to everything downstream, and it is the only link in this chain carrying systematic-review-level evidence. [[complete-denture/de-moraes-melo-neto-2021-centric-relation-condylar-reproducibility-sr]] found that the entire published within-method literature reduces to a single comparison — bimanual manipulation vs chin-point guidance — with bimanual superior in 3 of 6 studies, equivalent in 2, inferior in 1; the authors recommend **bimanual manipulation for dentate non-TMD patients**, with the honest caveat that all six studies were low quality (Jadad ≤2) and none enrolled edentulous patients. [[complete-denture/jasz-2024-centric-relation-methods-digital-mandibular-motion]] then measured the question directly rather than tallying it: 34 dentate TMD-negative subjects, seven CR techniques, each read against the subject's own MIP in 3D on a **50 µm ultrasound mandibular motion analyser**. Dawson bimanual manipulation and tongue-tip at the hard/soft-palate border deviated non-significantly from MIP on all three axes; Gothic-arch tracing and tongue-at-rugae pulled anteriorly; **active and passive chin retrusion pulled posteriorly** — the forced-retrusion failure mode that motivated the abandon-CR camp in the first place, caught in the act and attributable to technique rather than to the concept.

**The number that matters is the one bounding all seven: every deviation stayed within ±1 mm.** That is below the 2 mm line this debate discriminates on. So the manipulation link does *not* invalidate the threshold — it clears it, at least in dentate TMD-negative subjects on one 50 µm instrument at one centre. This should be read as a partial concession against this overview's own earlier framing: "the ruler is as coarse as the line" was stated too broadly. What is unvalidated at the 2 mm scale is the *rest* of the chain, and the objection is stronger for being narrowed to where it actually bites. Note too what this does to the Zonnenberg–Goldstein axis: Goldstein's position is that CR's problem is recording inconsistency rather than the concept, and these two papers are the closest thing the wiki holds to a test of that claim — they find the inconsistency real, technique-dependent, **and largely fixable by naming the technique**, which is Goldstein's argument rather than Zonnenberg's. It is corroboration on the mechanism, not a verdict on the terminology dispute, which is semantic and not settled by millimetres.

**The remaining three links are where the objection survives.** Interocclusal record material alone separates wax from elastomer by roughly sixfold (Rani 2024: Aluwax 0.939 vs polyether 0.136, PVS 0.152–0.165 deviation from control, all four significantly different from control, though **the paper never states its unit** and its own "polyether is best" conclusion is unsupported by its Tukey result showing the three elastomers indistinguishable at p=0.90–0.99). Tank 2024 points the same direction clinically but cannot carry weight: its largest mean discrepancy (hard wax) failed significance while a smaller one (alu wax) reached it, at n=30 with SD≈9. On the verification side, intraoral scanners record MIP with near-perfect operating characteristics yet collapse to **28% sensitivity in lateral excursion** (Morsy 2024) — and the reference standard they are judged against is articulating paper, whose own accuracy is now measured rather than merely doubted. [[occlusion/rovira-lastra-2026-articulating-paper-accuracy-occlusal-points]] scored articulating paper against a scanned polyvinyl-siloxane registration in 32 natural dentitions: **81% true positives and 15% false positives** at the contact-point level. Two of its findings are directly actionable and one is counter-intuitive — **200 µm paper produced fewer false positives than 100 µm** (9.4% vs 20.6%), because thinner paper generates more incidental cusp-tip smears, and requiring a mark to **coincide on both arches** before counting it as a contact collapsed false positives from 15% to **2.0%**. Two-thirds of false positives were cusp-tip artefacts. This matters to the debate rather than only to the chairside: it means the indicator against which scanners, splints and equilibrations are all judged mislabels roughly one contact in seven as real, and that a clinician who adjusts to unconfirmed marks is grinding to noise — while a free, one-line change of criterion removes most of it. Most pointedly, Biren 2025 shows that raising OVD leaves the *number* of occlusal contacts unchanged while their *positions* shift measurably: the metric most clinicians reach for to confirm an occlusion is insensitive to exactly the thing that moved. Taken together with Qadeer & Türp's 11–70 contact-count range, the honest reading is that **measurement standardisation across these three links is not an appendix to this debate but a precondition for settling it** — a 2 mm rule cannot arbitrate positions when the material, the digital capture and the verification metric are unvalidated at that scale, even though the manipulation link now clears it. This claim is directional and deliberately unquantified for the material and digital links: those studies are small, two are three-page supplement papers, and one omits its unit entirely.

**Finally, a constraint from the top of the ladder that the whole debate must sit under.** Everything above concerns which position to *build to* and how to record it. It is a separate question whether a discovered discrepancy licenses *treating* the occlusion, and here the evidence is both higher-grade and clearer than anything else on this page: [[tmj/singh-2024-occlusal-interventions-tmd-cochrane-sr]], a Cochrane review of occlusal interventions across TMD subtypes, finds **occlusal adjustment shows no benefit**, stabilisation splints show only very-low-certainty small benefit and no advantage over non-occluding placebo splints, and irreversible occlusal modification (grinding, crowns prescribed for TMD) has no evidence of benefit and carries risk of harm — issued as a **strong recommendation against**. The consequence for this synthesis is precise and worth stating flatly: a CR–MIP slide is **diagnostic and design information, not an indication to equilibrate**. Node 3's ">2 mm → assess" means assess and stop; it does not mean adjust. Jiménez-Silva's unresolved association runs the same way — an association of uncertain causality cannot justify an irreversible intervention that a Cochrane review finds ineffective. The one place the evidence still supports building deliberately to CR/CO is *restorative necessity* (the existing MIP is being lost or replaced anyway, per Kattadiyil), not therapeutic ambition.

These papers test that presupposition — and are the reason the "Measurement not standardized" gap below is load-bearing rather than cosmetic. The chain has four links; **rows are ordered by link, and only the first link currently clears the 2 mm threshold.**

## Clinical Decision Points

**Which reference position do I build to?** — a four-node decision path distilled from the evidence:

1. **Is full-mouth / large reconstruction needed (existing MIP lost or must change)?**
   - **No** → patient keeps a usable MIP. Go to node 2.
   - **Yes** → use a **reproducible CR/CO** as the build reference (Kattadiyil 2021, Goldstein 2022). For a function-led variant, set it at the normalized chewing-position apex (Kwak 2025).

2. **Are the condyles healthy and stable, with no TMD signs?**
   - **No** (deformed/unstable condyle, disc displacement, TMD signs) → do **not** trust condylar seating; build to a **muscle-guided, premature-contact-free ICP** and monitor condylar position over time (Fukushima 2016, Fornai 2022).
   - **Yes** → go to node 3.

3. **Is the CR–MIP slide small (<2 mm sagittal) and asymptomatic?**
   - **Yes** → **accept the existing MIP** as biologically acceptable; no routine condylar repositioning (Zonnenberg 2021). Small slides are normal.
   - **No** (slide >2 mm, hyperdivergent skeletal pattern, or symptomatic) → treat as a flag: full functional/condylar assessment before altering occlusion (Fornai 2022, Radej 2023).

4. **Whatever position is chosen — record it and verify it with methods whose error you know.**
   - **Manipulation technique first — it is the input to everything downstream, and the one link with SR-level evidence.** Use **Dawson bimanual manipulation**; prefer it over chin-point guidance (de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 SR: superior or equivalent in 5 of 6 studies). **Do not use active or passive chin retrusion** — both drive the condyle posteriorly (Jász 2024), which is precisely the forced-retrusion harm that motivated the abandon-CR argument. Tongue-tip at the hard/soft-palate border is the acceptable non-manipulative alternative. Deprogram first (Lucia jig or cotton rolls, 5–20 min), keep one operator, and hold patient position constant — supine posture pushes the mandible posteriorly under gravity. Reassuringly, all seven studied techniques land within ±1 mm of MIP, so technique choice is a real but *bounded* error term, well under the 2 mm decision line.
   - **Record material**: if you are building to CR, the record material is a controllable error term, and it is the one place in this whole debate where the fix is trivial. Wax is the outlier — roughly 6× the deviation of elastomers (Rani 2024), with Tank 2024 pointing the same way clinically. Use polyvinyl siloxane (PVS), polyether, or bis-acrylic resin; among those three there is no evidence to prefer one (Rani's own Tukey: p=0.90–0.99). Expect *some* vertical error regardless: closing onto any interposed material and releasing the bite shifts the reading (Tank 2024).
   - **Do not verify by counting contacts.** The count is method-dependent (11–70; Qadeer & Türp 2023) *and* insensitive — Biren 2025 raised OVD and the contact count did not move while the contacts themselves did. Document contact *locations* with one named indicator and re-inspect at recall.
   - **Use the articulating paper properly — the two cheapest accuracy gains on this page.** Choose **200 µm over 100 µm** (fewer false positives, 9.4% vs 20.6% — the thinner paper smears cusp tips more), and **count a contact only when the mark coincides on both arches**, which drops false positives from 15% to **2.0%** (Rovira-Lastra 2026). Treat a lone cusp-tip mark with no antagonist mark as artefact — two-thirds of false positives are exactly that. Inspect the mandibular arch (~5% more true positives, and easier); the "pulling" technique adds nothing. Adjusting to unverified marks is grinding to noise.
   - **If verifying digitally, know where the scanner fails.** Intraoral scanners capture MIP essentially as well as conventional mounting (Morsy 2024: 99–100%; Wang 2024: no difference vs facebow+bite record, though underpowered), but lateral excursion is unreliable — 28% sensitivity, missing ~3 of 4 contacts (Morsy 2024). Verify excursive contacts physically. For virtual articulation, scan bilateral posteriors as the default: accuracy is regional, not global (Mei 2022).
   - **Do not expect a virtual facebow to rescue a VD change.** It did not reduce contact deviation at 2 or 5 mm of OVD increase (Biren 2025).

5. **Overriding constraint — finding a discrepancy is not permission to treat it.** Every node above answers "what do I build to," never "what do I grind." For TMD, the Cochrane review finds occlusal adjustment ineffective and issues a **strong recommendation against irreversible occlusal modification** (Singh 2024). So: a CR–MIP slide is diagnostic and design information. Node 3's ">2 mm → assess" ends at assessment. Build deliberately to CR/CO when restorative necessity already requires replacing the occlusion (Kattadiyil 2021) — not to correct a slide in an intact dentition, and not as therapy for TMD. In already-acceptable finished orthodontic cases, CR-guided equilibration produced no measurable benefit at six months (Jahanbin 2026 RCT, underpowered).

## Gaps & Future Research

- **No head-to-head high-quality RCT** comparing CR/CO-based vs MIP-based reconstruction on long-term prosthodontic and TMD outcomes — the central question is decided by consensus and low-grade evidence.
- **Definition still unresolved**: the GPT-9 definition of centric relation reached only 29% expert consensus (Fornai 2022); competing terms (CR, Reference Position, adapted centric posture, muscular position) are not interoperable.
- **Measurement not standardized — load-bearing, but now correctly scoped to three of four links.** MIP contact counts (11–70) and CR–MIP slide measurement vary by instrument; digital occlusal analysis vs articulating paper concordance is poor (see [[occlusion/didier-2026-occlusal-contacts-ios-vs-carbon-paper-concordance]]). The debate arbitrates on a 2 mm threshold while its record materials (Rani 2024, Tank 2024), its digital capture (Morsy 2024, Wang 2024, Mei 2022), and its verification metric (Biren 2025, Rovira-Lastra 2026) remain unvalidated or imperfect at that scale. **The 2026-09 correction is that the first link is not in that state**: manipulation technique has SR-level evidence (de Moraes Melo Neto 2021) and a direct 3D measurement bounding all seven techniques within ±1 mm of MIP (Jász 2024) — below the decision threshold. An earlier version of this page asserted the whole chain was uninspected; that was too strong. **What is still missing is an error budget**: no study decomposes the CR-record chain (technique → material → mounting → articulator → verification) into contributions with confidence intervals, so nobody can say whether a measured "2.1 mm slide" is a finding or an artefact. Jász is the closest thing to a first term in that budget, from one centre on one instrument.
- **The manipulation evidence is the best on this page and is still weak in absolute terms.** All six studies in de Moraes Melo Neto's SR are Jadad ≤2 with only 2 randomized; the whole literature compares one pair of techniques (bimanual vs chin-point); no head-to-head data exist for graphic vs physiologic methods; and no included study enrolled edentulous patients, so the bimanual recommendation is explicitly dentate-and-non-TMD. Jász is single-centre (n=34) and benchmarks against MIP as a *relative* reference, not an anatomic gold standard — a study design that cannot detect an error common to MIP and the technique alike.
- **The recording-material evidence is directional but weak.** Both material studies are three-page JPBS supplement papers; Rani 2024 never states its unit and its conclusion contradicts its own Tukey test; Tank 2024's largest mean difference is non-significant. The wax-vs-elastomer direction is consistent across bench and chair, but a properly powered clinical study with stated units and an equivalence design does not exist.
- **Non-inferiority is being inferred from null results.** Wang 2024 (n=10, p=0.89–0.98) is read by its own authors as "IOS fulfils clinical requirements"; this is absence of evidence, not evidence of equivalence. The digital-vs-conventional question needs equivalence-designed trials with pre-specified margins — ideally against an independent ground truth, as only Mei 2022 (industrial CT) manages.
- **Causality CR–ICP discrepancy → TMD** remains unestablished; longitudinal studies with validated diagnostic criteria are needed (Jiménez-Silva 2017).
- **The orthodontic axis is thin, not empty — and this page previously mis-stated it.** An earlier version claimed "this wiki holds no orthodontic paper on CR as a treatment goal." That was **wrong at the time it was written**: [[occlusion/jahanbin-2026-occlusal-adjustment-post-orthodontic-tscan-rct]] (RCT, CR-guided post-orthodontic equilibration, negative at 6 months) and [[tmj/radej-2023-condylar-displacement-malocclusion-cr-mip-sr]] (entirely in orthodontic patients) were both already held. The residual genuine gaps are narrower: Yu 2024's branch conditions are locked in a Chinese-language body PMC does not host, and the wiki holds nothing on whether *finishing* an active orthodontic case to CR (as opposed to adjusting after debond) changes long-term outcomes. Jahanbin is underpowered at n=15/arm with 6-month follow-up in already-acceptable cases, so it bounds marginal benefit rather than settling the axis. Acquiring the Yu 2024 full text (CNKI/Wanfang) and a longer-follow-up orthodontic source would close what remains.
- **A cross-check worth institutionalising**: three of the six papers added in 2026-09 had been in the wiki since 2026-07-15 — *before* this overview was first written on 2026-07-18 — and were missed anyway, one of them the highest-grade document on the page (Singh 2024, Cochrane). The failure was not stale evidence but incomplete retrieval at synthesis time, and the false "no orthodontic paper" claim is what it produced. Overviews on adjacent axes (recording technique lives under `complete-denture/`, TMD intervention under `tmj/`) need cross-category sweeps, not same-folder ones.
