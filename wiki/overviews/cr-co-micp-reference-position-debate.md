> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 질문**: 교합을 재건할 때 기준이 되는 상하악 관계위(reference position)를 무엇으로 잡을 것인가 — 과두 기준의 중심위(Centric Relation, CR)/중심교합(Centric Occlusion, CO)인가, 치아 기준의 최대교두감합위(Maximum Intercuspation Position, MIP=교두감합위 Intercuspal Position, ICP)인가.
> - **용어 정리**: CR=과두가 관절와에 안정적으로 앉은 하악위(치아 무관). CO=그 CR 상태에서의 교합접촉위. MIP/MICP/ICP=치아가 최대로 맞물리는 습관적 위치(과두 위치 무관). 셋 다 "centric"이라 혼용되지만 같지 않다.
> - **사실 1 — CO≠MIP가 정상**: Best Evidence Consensus(Kattadiyil 2021)는 유치악·부분유치악자 **대다수에서 CO와 MIP가 불일치**한다고 결론. 이 둘 사이의 미끄러짐이 **중심활주(centric slide, CR-MIP/CR-ICP discrepancy)**.
> - **사실 2 — 정상 활주의 한계치**: Pullinger 기준 무증상자에서 시상 활주 **>2mm는 거의 없음**(Fornai 2022 인용). 즉 작은 활주는 정상, 큰 활주는 비정상 신호일 수 있음.
> - **논쟁 A (CR 폐기파)**: Zonnenberg/Türp/Greene(2021) — "중심위"는 의미·개념·실용적으로 결함이라 **용어 폐기** 주장; 건강 유치악자는 MIP가 생물학적으로 수용 가능하니 과두위 일상 평가 불필요.
> - **논쟁 B (CR 유지파)**: Goldstein(2022) — CR은 **재현 가능·근거 있는 기준위**(특히 전악재건); 문제는 개념이 아니라 정의·기록법의 비일관성; 다른 위치를 쓰려면 다른 이름을 붙이라.
> - **논쟁 C (절충/모니터링파)**: Fornai(2022) — 용어 "CR"은 폐기하되 임상적으로는 **과두위를 능동 감시**해야(생물학적≠임상적 정상); 대체어로 **기준위(Reference Position, RP)** 제안.
> - **논쟁 D (상황 한정파, 2026-07 추가)**: Yu(2024, 華西) — CR을 폐기하지도 옹호하지도 말고 **"논쟁이 적은 상황"에만 쓰라**며 예비 **결정트리**를 제시. CR의 유치악 적용을 세 질문으로 구조화: TMD 진단·치료의 핵심인가 / 교합재건의 기준 악위인가 / **교정의 목표 악위인가**. 세 번째 축(교정)은 이 오버뷰가 그동안 다루지 않던 영역이다. **단 실제 분기 조건은 중문 본문에만 있어 미확보** — 알고리즘 형식을 근거등급으로 오인 말 것(저자 스스로 "初步提出", kattadiyil의 BECS와 격이 다름).
> - **활주와 악관절장애(TMD)**: Jiménez-Silva(2017) SR — CR-ICP 불일치가 다수 연구서 근육통·관절잡음·디스크변위와 연관되나 **근거 질이 낮아(NOS 평균 3.36) 인과 확정 불가**. radej(2023)는 **과두발산형(hyperdivergent) 골격**이 CR→MIP 과두 후하방 변위의 최대 위험인자라고 정리.
> - **MIP의 "정상"도 모호**: Qadeer & Türp(2023) — MIP 교합접촉 수가 **11~70개로 광범위**, 검출지시재(교합지·shimstock·디지털)에 좌우 → 단일 정상치 없음. 즉 "MIP를 받아들이라"는 주장도 측정 표준이 없으면 검증 곤란.
> - **⚠️ 새 축 (2026-07) — 이 논쟁은 자기가 쓰는 자[尺]의 정밀도를 검증한 적이 없다**: 논쟁의 판별선은 **활주 2mm**(Pullinger/Fornai)와 Radej의 **≥2mm(수직·수평)·≥0.5mm(횡)**다. 그런데 그 선을 재는 도구들의 오차가 선과 같은 자릿수다 — 교합채득재는 왁스가 엘라스토머의 **약 6배** 편차(Rani 2024: Aluwax 0.94 vs 폴리에테르 0.136·PVS 0.152~0.165, **단위 원문 미표기**), 구강스캐너(IOS)는 MICP는 잘 잡지만 **측방위 민감도 28%**로 접촉 3/4를 놓치고(Morsy 2024), 검증 지표로 흔히 쓰는 **교합접촉 "개수"는 위치가 이동해도 안 변하는 둔감한 지표**다(Biren 2025). 즉 측정 표준화는 각주가 아니라 **논쟁의 하중을 받는 기둥**이다.
> - **다만 정직하게**: 위 수치들은 방향은 일관되나 근거가 약하다 — Rani·Tank는 JPBS 부록 3페이지 논문이고 단위 미표기, Tank는 최대 편차(hard wax)가 오히려 무유의(n=30·SD≈9), Wang(n=10)의 "IOS≈관행"은 무유의차일 뿐 등가성 근거가 아니다. **"자가 부정확하다"는 방향은 신뢰하되 개별 수치는 인용하지 말 것.**
> - **임상 합의점(실무 결론)**: ① **전악수복**처럼 기존 MIP를 잃거나 바꿔야 하면 → **재현 가능한 CR/CO를 기준위로** 채택(Kattadiyil·Goldstein). ② **건강하고 안정적인 유치악 + 소량 활주(<2mm)·무증상**이면 → 기존 **MIP를 수용**(Zonnenberg). ③ **과두 변형/불안정·TMD 징후**면 → 과두 안착을 신뢰 말고 **근육 유도 안정위/조기접촉 없는 ICP**로(Fukushima), 그리고 과두위 변화를 추적(Fornai). 변형 골격(hyperdivergent)·큰 활주는 경계.
> - **공통 한계**: 정의 불일치, 저질·이질적 근거, 측정법 표준 부재. CR↔MIP 우월성을 가리는 고품질 RCT는 없고, 건강인에서 어느 위치로 회복하든 보철 부작용의 결정적 차이는 보고되지 않음.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 16 papers on maxillomandibular reference position for occlusal reconstruction: CO and MIP are non-coincident in most dentate patients (Kattadiyil 2021 Best Evidence Consensus), normal centric slide is <2 mm sagittal (Fornai 2022 citing Pullinger), and "centric relation" itself has a contested definition — Zonnenberg/Türp/Greene 2021 call for abandoning the term, Goldstein 2022 defends it as a reproducible restorative reference, Fornai 2022 keeps the concept under a renamed Reference Position, and Yu 2024 declines the whole either/or by restricting CR to situations of "little controversy" via a decision tree.

Practical decision: full-mouth/large reconstruction → build to a reproducible CR/CO (Kattadiyil, Goldstein); deformed/unstable condyles or TMD signs → target a muscle-guided premature-contact-free ICP (Fukushima 2016); healthy dentate with slide <2 mm and no symptoms → accept existing MIP (Zonnenberg 2021); hyperdivergent skeletal pattern is the strongest risk factor for posteroinferior condylar displacement on the CR→MIP path (Radej 2023 scoping review).

The measurement problem is load-bearing, not a footnote: the debate discriminates on a 2 mm slide threshold, yet the instruments that read it carry error of the same order — MIP contact count varies 11–70 by detection method (Qadeer & Türp 2023), wax interocclusal records deviate ~6× more than elastomers (Rani 2024, unit unstated; Tank 2024 same direction but underpowered), intraoral scanners capture MIP well but miss ~3 of 4 contacts in lateral excursion (Morsy 2024), and contact *count* stays constant while contact *positions* migrate (Biren 2025), so counting contacts cannot verify a chosen position. No high-quality RCT comparing CR/CO vs MIP-based reconstruction on long-term outcomes exists.

## 세줄요약

16편 종합: CO와 MIP는 유치악 대다수에서 불일치(Kattadiyil 2021 BECS); 정상 중심활주(CR-MIP slide) <2mm(Fornai 2022); "중심위(CR)"는 용어 자체가 논쟁 중 — 폐기파(Zonnenberg 2021)·유지파(Goldstein 2022)·감시파(Fornai 2022)에 더해, Yu(2024)는 양자택일을 거부하고 "논쟁이 적은 상황"에만 CR을 쓰는 결정트리를 제시.

임상 결정: 전악수복 → 재현 가능한 CR/CO로 구축(Kattadiyil, Goldstein); 과두 불안정·TMD 징후 → 근육 유도 ICP(Fukushima 2016); 건강한 유치악·활주<2mm·무증상 → 기존 MIP 수용(Zonnenberg 2021); 과두개산형(hyperdivergent) 골격이 CR→MIP 후하방 과두변위 최강 위험인자(Radej 2023).

측정 문제가 논쟁의 하중을 받는다: 판별선은 활주 2mm인데 그 선을 재는 도구의 오차가 같은 자릿수다 — MIP 접촉 수는 지시재에 따라 11~70개(Qadeer & Türp 2023), 왁스 교합채득재는 엘라스토머의 약 6배 편차(Rani 2024, 단위 미표기; Tank 2024은 같은 방향이나 검정력 부족), 구강스캐너는 MICP는 잘 잡아도 측방위 접촉 3/4를 놓치며(Morsy 2024), 접촉 "개수"는 접촉점이 이동해도 그대로라 검증 지표로 쓸 수 없다(Biren 2025). CR/CO vs MIP 기반 수복 head-to-head 고품질 RCT는 여전히 부재.

## Thesis

The clinical question is not "which single position is universally correct" but **"for this patient, in this clinical situation, which reference position is both reproducible and biologically safe?"** Three facts anchor the answer:

1. **CO and MIP are normally non-coincident** (Kattadiyil 2021): in most dentate/partially dentate people the teeth-defined intercuspal position (MIP/ICP) differs from the condyle-seated occlusal contact position (CO). The difference is the **centric slide (CR–MIP / CR–ICP discrepancy)**.
2. **A small slide is normal; a large one is a flag** — no asymptomatic subject in Pullinger's data had a sagittal slide >2 mm (cited by Fornai 2022).
3. **Neither "normal" is well-measured** — the MIP contact count itself ranges 11–70 depending on the detection method (Qadeer & Türp 2023), and the CR–ICP↔TMD association rests on low-quality evidence (Jiménez-Silva 2017).

The literature then splits on **what to do about condylar position**: abandon the concern in healthy dentate patients (Zonnenberg/Türp/Greene), defend CR as the reproducible restorative reference (Goldstein), or keep monitoring condylar position under a renamed "Reference Position" (Fornai). For unstable/deformed condyles the condyle cannot be trusted as a landmark at all, and a muscle-guided, premature-contact-free ICP is the safer target (Fukushima).

**A fourth stance declines the either/or.** Yu 2024 (West China) accepts that CR's migration from edentulous complete dentures into dentate jaws is genuinely doubted, but observes that its clinical uses exist regardless — so instead of adjudicating the concept, the paper restricts it: it asks three separate questions (should CR be the core of TMD diagnosis and treatment? should CR be the jaw position to build occlusal reconstruction on? should CR be the target position in orthodontics?) and proposes a preliminary **decision tree** covering only the situations of "little controversy." Two things follow. First, it is convergent with this overview's own structure — an independent group arrived at situation-by-situation triage rather than a universal answer, which is weak but real corroboration that the question is mis-posed as "which position wins." Second, it opens an axis this synthesis had not covered at all: **CR as an orthodontic target position**. That axis remains a hole here, because Yu's actual branch conditions live in the Chinese-language body, which PMC does not host — only the abstract is retrievable. Treat Yu as a signpost, not a source of rules; an algorithm's format confers authority its evidence grade does not (the authors themselves say "preliminarily put forward," and this is one group's narrative review, not a Best Evidence Consensus like Kattadiyil).

**And the debate has a floor it has not inspected: its own instruments.** Every position above is discriminated by a number — Pullinger's 2 mm sagittal slide separating normal from abnormal (Fornai), Radej's ≥2 mm vertical/horizontal and ≥0.5 mm transverse thresholds for clinically significant condylar displacement. Those thresholds are only meaningful if the chain that produces them — record material → mounting → contact verification — has error well below them. The evidence says it may not. Interocclusal record material alone separates wax from elastomer by roughly sixfold (Rani 2024: Aluwax 0.939 vs polyether 0.136, PVS 0.152–0.165 deviation from control, all four significantly different from control, though **the paper never states its unit** and its own "polyether is best" conclusion is unsupported by its Tukey result showing the three elastomers indistinguishable at p=0.90–0.99). Tank 2024 points the same direction clinically but cannot carry weight: its largest mean discrepancy (hard wax) failed significance while a smaller one (alu wax) reached it, at n=30 with SD≈9. On the verification side, intraoral scanners record MIP with near-perfect operating characteristics yet collapse to **28% sensitivity in lateral excursion** (Morsy 2024) — and the reference standard they are judged against is articulating paper, itself of contested accuracy. Most pointedly, Biren 2025 shows that raising OVD leaves the *number* of occlusal contacts unchanged while their *positions* shift measurably: the metric most clinicians reach for to confirm an occlusion is insensitive to exactly the thing that moved. Taken together with Qadeer & Türp's 11–70 contact-count range, the honest reading is that **measurement standardisation is not an appendix to this debate but a precondition for settling it** — a 2 mm rule cannot arbitrate positions when the tools reading it are unvalidated at that scale. This claim is directional and deliberately unquantified: the underlying studies are small, two are three-page supplement papers, and one omits its unit entirely.

### Can we actually record and verify the position we chose?

The debate's thresholds (2 mm slide; Radej's ≥2 mm / ≥0.5 mm) presuppose a measurement chain accurate well below them. These papers test that presupposition — and are the reason the "Measurement not standardized" gap below is load-bearing rather than cosmetic.

| Paper | Design | n / scope | Link in the chain | Key finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rani 2024 | In vitro (JPBS 3-pp supplement) | 10 records/material, mock prep 46-48 | record **material** | Aluwax 0.939 vs polyether (Ramitec) 0.136, PVS 0.152–0.165 deviation from control — wax ≈6× worse; F(4,45)=261.5, p<0.001; **but** the 3 elastomers are indistinguishable (p=0.90–0.99), contradicting the paper's own "polyether is best"; **unit never stated** |
| Tank 2024 | Cross-sectional (JPBS 3-pp supplement) | 30 dentate, 20–45 y | record **material**, clinically | Same direction (waxes worst, PVS/bis-acrylic best) but **does not establish it**: only alu wax differed significantly from MIP; hard wax had the largest mean discrepancy yet was non-significant (n=30, SD≈9). Durable point is conceptual — closing onto any material and releasing the bite introduces vertical error at all |
| Morsy 2024 | In vitro | master casts, articulator | **digital capture / verification** | IOS multi-occlusion: MIP sensitivity/NPV 100%, specificity/PPV 99%; protrusive acceptable; **lateral sensitivity 28%, NPV 56%** — misses ~3 of 4 true contacts. Reference standard is articulating paper, not ground truth |
| Wang 2024 | Cross-sectional (abstract only — PMC lacks the Chinese body) | 10, ideal occlusion | **digital capture** | 3 IOS vs facebow+bite-registration mounting for MIP inter-arch distance: no significant difference (p=0.89–0.98). **Null ≠ equivalence** — SD ±6.4 mm dwarfs the 0.6–1.4 mm group differences; all three read consistently smaller than control (hypothesis-generating only) |
| Mei 2022 | In vitro (**industrial-CT reference standard**) | 9 scan strategies, 1 volunteer's casts | **virtual articulation** | VIR accuracy is *regional*, not global: bilateral-posterior (BP) scanning is the best full-arch default; the region you scan is the region that is accurate. Methodologically the strongest of the digital set — its reference is true ground truth, not another imperfect indicator |
| Biren 2025 | Cross-sectional | 18, 8 splints each | **verification metric itself** | Raising OVD 2/5 mm left the *number* of occlusal contacts unchanged while contact *positions* deviated (Z-direction clinically relevant at 5 mm intraoral); virtual facebow did not reduce deviation. Contact count is insensitive to what actually moved. Deviations are relative to a reference group, not to truth |

## Clinical Decision Points

**Which reference position do I build to?** — a four-node decision path distilled from the evidence:

1. **Is full-mouth / large reconstruction needed (existing MIP lost or must change)?**
   - **No** → patient keeps a usable MIP. Go to node 2.
   - **Yes** → use a **reproducible CR/CO** as the build reference (Kattadiyil 2021, Goldstein 2022). For a function-led variant, set it at the normalized chewing-position apex (Kwak 2025).

2. **Are the condyles healthy and stable, with no TMD signs?**
   - **No** (deformed/unstable condyle, disc displacement, TMD signs) → do **not** trust condylar seating; build to a **muscle-guided, premature-contact-free ICP** and monitor condylar position over time (Fukushima 2016, Fornai 2022).
   - **Yes** → go to node 3.

3. **Is the CR–MIP slide small (<2 mm sagittal) and asymptomatic?**
   - **Yes** → **accept the existing MIP** as biologically acceptable; no routine condylar repositioning (Zonnenberg 2021). Small slides are normal.
   - **No** (slide >2 mm, hyperdivergent skeletal pattern, or symptomatic) → treat as a flag: full functional/condylar assessment before altering occlusion (Fornai 2022, Radej 2023).

4. **Whatever position is chosen — record it and verify it with methods whose error you know.**
   - **Record material**: if you are building to CR, the record material is a controllable error term, and it is the one place in this whole debate where the fix is trivial. Wax is the outlier — roughly 6× the deviation of elastomers (Rani 2024), with Tank 2024 pointing the same way clinically. Use polyvinyl siloxane (PVS), polyether, or bis-acrylic resin; among those three there is no evidence to prefer one (Rani's own Tukey: p=0.90–0.99). Expect *some* vertical error regardless: closing onto any interposed material and releasing the bite shifts the reading (Tank 2024).
   - **Do not verify by counting contacts.** The count is method-dependent (11–70; Qadeer & Türp 2023) *and* insensitive — Biren 2025 raised OVD and the contact count did not move while the contacts themselves did. Document contact *locations* with one named indicator and re-inspect at recall.
   - **If verifying digitally, know where the scanner fails.** Intraoral scanners capture MIP essentially as well as conventional mounting (Morsy 2024: 99–100%; Wang 2024: no difference vs facebow+bite record, though underpowered), but lateral excursion is unreliable — 28% sensitivity, missing ~3 of 4 contacts (Morsy 2024). Verify excursive contacts physically. For virtual articulation, scan bilateral posteriors as the default: accuracy is regional, not global (Mei 2022).
   - **Do not expect a virtual facebow to rescue a VD change.** It did not reduce contact deviation at 2 or 5 mm of OVD increase (Biren 2025).

## Gaps & Future Research

- **No head-to-head high-quality RCT** comparing CR/CO-based vs MIP-based reconstruction on long-term prosthodontic and TMD outcomes — the central question is decided by consensus and low-grade evidence.
- **Definition still unresolved**: the GPT-9 definition of centric relation reached only 29% expert consensus (Fornai 2022); competing terms (CR, Reference Position, adapted centric posture, muscular position) are not interoperable.
- **Measurement not standardized — and this is now the load-bearing gap, not a footnote.** MIP contact counts (11–70) and CR–MIP slide measurement vary by instrument; digital occlusal analysis vs articulating paper concordance is poor (see [[occlusion/didier-2026-occlusal-contacts-ios-vs-carbon-paper-concordance]]). The 2026-07 additions sharpen this from a complaint into a structural objection: the debate arbitrates on a 2 mm threshold while its record materials (Rani 2024, Tank 2024), its digital capture (Morsy 2024, Wang 2024, Mei 2022), and its verification metric (Biren 2025) are unvalidated at that scale. **What is missing is an error budget**: no study decomposes the CR-record chain (material → mounting → articulator → verification) into contributions with confidence intervals, so nobody can say whether a measured "2.1 mm slide" is a finding or an artefact.
- **The recording-material evidence is directional but weak.** Both material studies are three-page JPBS supplement papers; Rani 2024 never states its unit and its conclusion contradicts its own Tukey test; Tank 2024's largest mean difference is non-significant. The wax-vs-elastomer direction is consistent across bench and chair, but a properly powered clinical study with stated units and an equivalence design does not exist.
- **Non-inferiority is being inferred from null results.** Wang 2024 (n=10, p=0.89–0.98) is read by its own authors as "IOS fulfils clinical requirements"; this is absence of evidence, not evidence of equivalence. The digital-vs-conventional question needs equivalence-designed trials with pre-specified margins — ideally against an independent ground truth, as only Mei 2022 (industrial CT) manages.
- **Causality CR–ICP discrepancy → TMD** remains unestablished; longitudinal studies with validated diagnostic criteria are needed (Jiménez-Silva 2017).
- **The orthodontic axis is unaddressed here.** Yu 2024 names "should CR be the target position in orthodontics?" as one of its three questions, but the branch conditions are locked in a Chinese-language body PMC does not host, and this wiki holds no orthodontic paper on CR as a treatment goal. Acquiring the Yu 2024 full text (CNKI/Wanfang) or an orthodontic-side source would close a real hole.
