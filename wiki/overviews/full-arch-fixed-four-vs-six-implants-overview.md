## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 무치악 고정성 풀아치 수복에서 **임플란트 4개 vs 6개** 결정을 임상 근거(생존·변연골·비용·합병증)와 생체역학 근거(유한요소분석, FEA)의 *상반된* 두 축으로 종합한 5편 페이지. 핵심 긴장: **임상 성적은 동등, 생체역학은 6개 우위** — 이 괴리가 결정 규칙을 만든다.
> - **임상 축 — 생존율·변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 은 4개 = 6개**: Toia 3년 다기관 RCT (n=56, 280 임플란트) — 생존 4개 100% vs 6개 99%, 3년 MBL 군간 유의차 없음; Toia 5년 RCT (n=47) — 생존 4개 100% vs 6개 99.3%, MBL 유의차 없음, 양군 임플란트주위염 0. [RCT 근거]
> - **대규모 후향 재확인**: Caramés 2025 (943명·5,989 즉시부하 임플란트·2,431 사분악, 평균 5년) — 2년 생존 98.6% vs 98.8% (p=0.362), 5년 98.4% vs 98.7% (p=0.343). 임플란트 개수·치조골위축 분류가 아니라 **악궁(상악 위험비 HR 1.815)·연령(HR 1.026/년)이 실패 예측인자**. [후향 대규모]
> - **4개의 실제 이득 = 비용·무증대**: 골증대 회피 + 초기·총 치료비 낮음 (Toia). **대가 = 기술적 합병증↑** (Toia 5년: 4개 16.6% vs 6개 0%) — 주로 보철 chipping/파절. 참고로 풀아치 보철 합병증은 개수 무관하게 흔함 (Toia 3년 FCD 47.2%가 chipping/fracture 경험).
> - **생체역학 축 — FEA는 일관되게 6개 우위**: Pandey 2023 (하악 FEA) — All-on-Six가 피질골·임플란트 최대 주응력 현저히 낮음 (경사하중 시 피질골 5.47 vs 139.85 MPa, 임플란트 35.51 vs 244.43 MPa); All-on-Four는 원심 캔틸레버가 응력 집중 증폭.
> - **프레임워크 재료가 응력을 좌우 (Cabbarova 2026 FEA, 6개 재료 비교)**: All-on-Six + 강성 프레임(티타늄·지르코니아)이 생체역학 최적; **PEEK/PEKK는 저탄성계수→변형↑→하중전달↑라 풀아치 프레임 금기**; 섬유강화복합 (Trilor/Trinia) 은 심미·경량 필요 시 허용 가능한 중간 성능. All-on-Four의 원심 캔틸레버는 재료 무관하게 응력 집중.
> - **괴리의 해소 (결정 규칙)**: 임상 생존이 동등한 이유는 표준 증례에서 4개도 생리적 한계 안에 있기 때문 — FEA의 응력차는 *안전여유 (safety margin)* 의 차이지 실패율의 차이가 아니다. 따라서 **표준 증례 = 4개 (비용효율·무증대), 생체역학 위험 증례 = 6개**.
> - **6개를 고려할 증례**: 이갈이 (bruxism)·저골질·긴 캔틸레버·대합치가 자연치/임플란트로 교합력 큰 경우·상악(Toia가 상악 특정 연구; Caramés에서 상악이 실패 예측인자). 4개를 쓸 땐 **강성 프레임(Ti/Zr) + 캔틸레버 최소화**로 생체역학 불리를 보상.
> - **악궁 차이 주의**: Toia RCT는 **상악** FCD, Pandey/Cabbarova FEA는 **하악** 모델 — 근거를 악궁 넘어 외삽할 때 신중. 상악은 골질이 낮아 개수·강성 프레임의 이득이 더 클 수 있다.
> - **결론**: 4개는 표준 무치악 풀아치에서 6개에 **비열등 (생존·MBL)** 하며 비용·무증대 이점이 명확 — 단 기술적 합병증을 예견하고 강성 프레임·캔틸레버 관리를 병행. 6개는 "더 안전"이 아니라 "생체역학 위험을 흡수하는 여유" — 위험 증례에 표적 사용.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 5 papers on the four-vs-six implant decision for edentulous fixed full-arch rehabilitation, deliberately contrasting a clinical-outcomes axis (2 RCTs + 1 large retrospective) against a biomechanical axis (2 FEA studies) that disagree.

Clinically, 4 implants are non-inferior to 6 for survival and marginal bone level (Toia 3-yr and 5-yr RCTs: survival ~100% both, no significant MBL difference; Caramés 2025 retrospective, 943 patients/5,989 implants: 5-yr survival 98.4% vs 98.7%, p=0.343, with jaw type and age — not implant number — predicting loss), 4-I being cheaper and augmentation-avoiding but incurring more technical complications; biomechanically, FEA consistently favors 6 (All-on-Six markedly lowers peri-implant/cortical stress — Pandey oblique load cortical bone 5.47 vs 139.85 MPa; All-on-Four's distal cantilever concentrates stress regardless of framework, and rigid Ti/Zr frameworks are optimal while PEEK/PEKK are contraindicated — Cabbarova 2026).

Resolution: equivalent clinical survival means 4 implants stay within physiological limits in standard cases (the FEA stress gap is a safety-margin difference, not a failure-rate difference), so use 4 as the cost-effective default and reserve 6 for biomechanical-risk cases (bruxism, low bone quality, long cantilever, maxilla), compensating a 4-implant plan with a rigid framework and minimized cantilever.

## 세줄요약

임상적으로 4개는 생존·변연골소실에서 6개에 비열등(Toia 3년·5년 RCT: 생존 양군 ~100%, MBL 유의차 없음; Caramés 2025 후향 943명/5,989 임플란트: 5년 생존 98.4% vs 98.7%, p=0.343, 개수가 아니라 악궁·연령이 실패 예측), 4개는 저비용·무증대이나 기술적 합병증↑; 생체역학적으로 FEA는 일관되게 6개 우위(All-on-Six가 피질골·주위골 응력 현저히 낮춤 — Pandey 경사하중 피질골 5.47 vs 139.85 MPa; All-on-Four 원심 캔틸레버는 재료 무관 응력집중, 강성 Ti/Zr 프레임 최적·PEEK/PEKK 금기 — Cabbarova 2026).

해소: 임상 생존 동등은 표준 증례에서 4개가 생리적 한계 내에 있음을 뜻함(FEA 응력차는 안전여유 차이지 실패율 차이 아님) → 4개를 비용효율 기본값으로, 6개는 생체역학 위험 증례(이갈이·저골질·긴 캔틸레버·상악)에 표적 사용하고, 4개 계획은 강성 프레임+캔틸레버 최소화로 보상.

## Summary

For the edentulous jaw restored with an implant-supported fixed complete denture (FCD, "All-on-X"), one of the most consequential planning choices is **how many implants** to place. Marketing and convention offer both "All-on-Four" (cost, no augmentation) and "All-on-Six" (more support). This overview reads the evidence as **two axes that genuinely disagree** — and the disagreement is the useful part.

---

## Axis 1 — Clinical outcomes: 4 ≈ 6

### Toia 2021 & 2025 (multicenter RCTs, maxilla)
The strongest design in the cluster — the same trial reported at 3 and 5 years:
- **Toia 2021 (3-yr, n=56 patients / 280 implants):** survival 100% (4-I) vs 99% (6-I); **no significant between-group MBL change** (4-I 0.30→0.24 mm; 6-I 0.14→0.12 mm); low technical/biological complications, but **prosthetic complications (chipping/fracture) affected 47.2% of FCDs regardless of number**.
- **Toia 2025 (5-yr, n=47 / 233 implants):** survival 100% (4-I) vs 99.3% (6-I); **no significant MBL difference at 5 years**; **no peri-implantitis in either group**; 4-I showed **more technical complications (16.6% vs 0%)** but **lower initial and total cost**. Conclusion: 4-I is **non-inferior** to 6-I radiographically.

### Caramés 2025 (large retrospective, both jaws)
- **943 patients, 5,989 immediately loaded implants (2,431 quadrants), mean 5.0 yr** under a standardized anatomy-driven decision-support system.
- **2-yr survival 98.6% vs 98.8% (p=0.362); 5-yr 98.4% vs 98.7% (p=0.343)** — comparably high.
- Multilevel Cox: **jaw type (maxilla HR 1.815, p=0.037) and age (HR 1.026/yr, p=0.030) predict loss — not implant number or ridge-atrophy class.**
- Caveat: prosthetic/technical complications not assessed.

**Axis-1 verdict:** for survival and marginal bone, **four implants are non-inferior to six**; the real cost of choosing four is *more prosthetic/technical maintenance*, and its benefit is *lower cost and augmentation avoidance*. The dominant survival driver is the jaw (maxilla worse), not the count.

---

## Axis 2 — Biomechanics (FEA): 6 > 4

### Pandey 2023 (FEA, mandible)
All-on-Four (2 axial + 2 tilted 17°) vs All-on-Six (6 axial), loaded 100 N vertical/horizontal and 141 N oblique:
- **All-on-Six markedly lowers peak principal stress** on cortical bone and implants across all loads (oblique: cortical bone **5.47 vs 139.85 MPa**; all implants **35.51 vs 244.43 MPa**).
- Slightly higher trabecular stress under vertical load (2.0 vs 1.28 MPa) — a minor trade-off.
- All-on-Four remains viable **with a rigid framework** in atrophic ridges; the distal cantilever is the stress amplifier.

### Cabbarova 2026 (FEA, mandible, 6 framework materials)
All-on-Four vs All-on-Six across titanium, zirconia, PEEK, PEKK, Trilor, Trinia (150 N bilateral):
- **All-on-Six → more homogeneous, lower peri-implant bone/screw stress across every material.**
- **PEEK/PEKK transmit the highest stress** (low elastic modulus → more deformation → more load to bone/implants) → **contraindicated for full-arch frameworks**.
- **Titanium/zirconia distribute stress most favorably**; FRC composites (Trilor/Trinia) are intermediate, clinically acceptable if esthetics/weight demand.
- **All-on-Four's distal cantilever amplifies stress regardless of material.**

**Axis-2 verdict:** more implants and a **rigid framework** both reduce stress; six implants provide a larger biomechanical safety margin, and the cantilever is the recurring villain.

---

## Reconciling the two axes (the decision rule)

The axes are not contradictory once framed correctly: **equivalent clinical survival means four implants stay within physiological bone-loading limits in standard cases.** The FEA stress difference is a **safety-margin difference, not a failure-rate difference** — six implants "waste" capacity that a standard case never needs, but that a high-load case does.

| | Four implants | Six implants |
|---|---|---|
| Survival / MBL (standard case) | Non-inferior (RCT + retrospective) | Reference |
| Cost / augmentation | Lower cost, augmentation-avoiding | Higher |
| Technical complications | Higher (Toia: 16.6% vs 0%) | Lower |
| Biomechanical safety margin | Smaller; cantilever-sensitive | Larger (FEA) |
| Best fit | Standard-risk case, cost-sensitive | Bruxism, low bone quality, long cantilever, maxilla |

**Decision rule:**
- **Default to four** for the standard edentulous case wanting a cost-effective, augmentation-free fixed full-arch — but **counsel that prosthetic maintenance is likely**, use a **rigid framework (Ti/Zr, never PEEK/PEKK)**, and **minimize the distal cantilever**.
- **Escalate to six** when biomechanical risk is elevated: **bruxism/parafunction, low bone quality, a long required cantilever, a heavily loaded opposing dentition, or the maxilla** (worse survival predictor in Caramés; Toia's data are maxilla-specific).
- **Watch the jaw, not just the number:** maxilla is the consistent risk signal.

**Cross-arch caveat:** Toia's RCTs are **maxillary**; Pandey/Cabbarova FEA are **mandibular**. Do not extrapolate a number across arches without noting that the maxilla's lower bone quality likely widens the benefit of both extra implants and rigid frameworks.
