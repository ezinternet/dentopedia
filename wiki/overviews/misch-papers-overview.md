## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 범위: Carl E. Misch 명의 위키 수록 논문 15편(1993–2025)을 한데 묶은 종합. 대부분 narrative review·합의회의 보고·증례 보고이며 무작위배정 임상시험(RCT)은 없다. [확인]
> - 골이식재: 자가골은 골형성·골유도·골전도, 동종골은 골유도·골전도, 합성골은 골전도만 가진다는 분류가 기준점이다. 벽이 3개 이상 소실된 결손은 자가골 또는 소공 막이 필요하다(1993). [확인]
> - 하악 결합부 블록골(symphysis block)은 접근성·골량·낮은 이환율을 장점으로 제시하나 비교 자료는 없다(1995). [확인]
> - 골질 기반 설계: 4단계 골밀도별 설계의 예비 다기관 보고에서 364개 임플란트의 수술 생존율은 98.9%다. 비교군과 장기 추적이 없어 장기 기준으로 쓰지 않는다(1998). [확인]
> - 구치부 설계 논리: 직경 중심 설계는 표면적을 약 30%만 늘리는 반면 구치부 힘은 300% 이상 높다. 이 수치는 설계 논증이지 결과 데이터가 아니다(1999). [확인]
> - 증례 수준 조직학: 2례 1년 하중에서 계면 골은 층판골, 회전율 5 µm/day 미만으로 원격 골과 같았다. 표본이 너무 작아 설계 효과의 증거가 아니다(2001). [확인]
> - 교합: 임플란트 보호 교합(implant-protected occlusion, 1994)은 생체역학적 근거 체계이며, 과부하가 변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)을 높일 수 있다는 2005년 서술 고찰은 정성적 결론이다. 과부하 인과는 위키에서 여전히 논쟁 중이다. [미검증]
> - 단축 임플란트(<10 mm 구치부): 크라운 높이·교합력·낮은 골밀도 때문에 하중 관리가 필요하며 측방 접촉·캔틸레버 제거, 임플란트 수 증가, 고정이 감소 수단이다(2005). 초록의 99% 생존율은 인용 연구 수치다. [확인]
> - 크라운 높이 공간(Crown Height Space, CHS): ICOI 합의회의 두 부(2005·2006)는 대부분 쟁점에서 단일 의견에 도달하지 못했고 수치 기준을 주지 않는다. 전문가 의견으로만 인용한다. [확인]
> - 지대주 높이와 출현각(2025, 후향 방사선, 임플란트 192개·119명·평균 7.4년): 짧은 지대주(<2 mm)+넓은 출현각(≥30°) 조합이 MBL 최대(+0.48 mm)·임플란트주위염 약 4배(OR 4.19). 지대주 높이 1 mm 증가마다 MBL odds 37% 감소(OR 0.63). 이 논문만 위키 Misch 묶음에서 대표본 정량 자료다. [확인]
> - 생존·성공 정의: 1998 품질 척도(통증·동요도 중심)와 2008 ICOI Pisa 합의(보철 지지를 성공 첫 기준, 심미적 만족 필수)는 결과 연구를 비교할 때 정의 앵커로 쓴다. 2008 초록에는 생존율 수치가 없다. [확인]
> - 신경 합병증(2010): 하치조신경(IAN) 손상 발생률은 0–44%다. 수술 중 → 1주 → 12주 4단계 관리와 의뢰 기준을 일반 치과의사용 프로토콜로 제시한다. 설문에서 치과의사 73%가 신경 감각 이상을 경험했다고 답했다. [확인]
> - 즉시부하(2004 2부): 임시 수복물의 응력 조절이 과부하 위험을 줄이는 핵심이나, 초록에 임상 결과가 없어 즉시부하 안전성의 증거로 인용하지 않는다. [확인]
> - 종합 한 줄: Misch 묶음은 설계·골질·교합·보철 높이를 "응력을 어떻게 나눌 것인가"라는 하나의 생체역학 질문으로 묶는다. 그러나 정량 근거는 2025 후향 연구 한 편에 집중되어 있어, 나머지는 가설·원칙·전문가 합의로 읽어야 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Fifteen Misch papers (1993–2025) spanning graft classification, bone-quality implant design, occlusal schema, crown-height guidelines, success definitions and nerve management, almost all narrative reviews, consensus statements or case reports.
Quantitative anchors are sparse: a 364-implant preliminary survival of 98.9% (1998), a 192-implant retrospective analysis in which a short abutment with a wide emergence angle carried +0.48 mm MBL and OR 4.19 for peri-implantitis (2025), and a 0–44% neurosensory-impairment range (2010).
Treat the bone-quality survival figure and the CHS and occlusal positions as expert rationale rather than outcome evidence, and use the 2025 abutment study as the only data-bearing anchor in this set.

## 세줄요약

15편의 Misch 논문(1993–2025)은 골이식재 분류, 골질 기반 설계, 교합 체계, 크라운 높이 지침, 생존 정의, 신경 관리를 아우르며, 거의 전부 서술 고찰·합의 보고·증례 보고다.
정량 기준은 드물다: 예비 보고의 364개 임플란트 생존율 98.9%(1998), 짧은 지대주+넓은 출현각이 MBL +0.48 mm·임플란트주위염 OR 4.19를 보인 192개 후향 분석(2025), 신경 감각 이상 발생률 0–44%(2010).
골질 생존율, CHS, 교합 입장은 결과 근거가 아니라 전문가 근거로 취급하고, 이 묶음에서 자료를 가진 근거는 2025 지대주 연구 하나로 본다.

## Summary

**Thesis.** Across four decades Misch's papers repeatedly ask one question: how is load distributed through an implant, its bone and its restoration? The answers cluster into five positions, each stated with a different evidence grade.

**1. Grafting (1993, 1995).** The 1993 review gives the mode-of-action taxonomy (osteogenic, osteoinductive, osteoconductive) and a defect-morphology rule: walls lost in three or more directions need autogenous bone or a small-pore membrane. The 1995 review champions the mandibular symphysis block for severe localized ridge defects, but the advantages are asserted without comparative data. See [[bone-regeneration/misch-1993-bone-grafting-materials-implant-dentistry]] and [[bone-regeneration/misch-1995-mandibular-symphysis-graft-severe-ridge-defects]].

**2. Bone-quality-based design (1998, 1999, 2001).** The 1998 preliminary report (364 implants, 98.9% surgical survival, five centres) argues that initial survival may be similar across bone densities, but it has no comparator arm. The 1999 posterior-design argument uses a design-level contrast: conventional diameter-based designs add about 30% surface area while posterior forces are up to more than 300% higher. The 2001 bioengineered case (two implants, one year) shows lamellar bone at the interface with turnover below 5 µm/day, a preliminary histology that cannot prove a design effect. See [[implants/misch-1998-bone-quality-based-implant-system-stage-1-2]], [[implants/misch-1999-implant-design-posterior-regions]] and [[implants/misch-2001-bioengineered-implant-predetermined-bone-response]].

**3. Occlusion and load management (1994, 2004 Part II, 2005 occlusal trauma, 2005 short implants).** The 1994 "implant-protected occlusion" schema combines implant orientation, load direction, surface area, occlusal table width and protection of the weakest area. The 2005 selective review argues that occlusal overload can raise peri-implant MBL, but its conclusion is qualitative. The 2004 Part II rationale locates overload risk in the stresses on the transitional restoration (patient factors, implant position, cantilevers, load direction, contact intensity, diet) and gives no outcome data for immediate loading. The 2005 short-implant review (<10 mm posterior) recommends load-reduction measures: removing lateral contacts and cantilevers, adding implants, enlarging diameter or surface, and splinting. Its 99% survival figure is quoted from an earlier study. See [[occlusion/misch-1994-implant-protected-occlusion-biomechanical-rationale]], [[occlusion/misch-2005-occlusal-trauma-peri-implant-bone-loss-literature]], [[immediate-implant/loading-protocol/misch-2004-rationale-immediate-load-implant-part2]] and [[implants/short-narrow/misch-2005-short-dental-implants-literature-review]].

**4. Crown height and abutment geometry (2005, 2006, 2025).** The two ICOI consensus parts on crown-height space (CHS) summarize general guidelines but reach a single opinion on few issues and give no numeric thresholds, so they are cited as expert opinion. The 2025 retrospective radiographic study (192 bone-level implants, 119 patients, mean 7.4 years, University of Michigan) cross-classifies transmucosal abutment height (TmAH, cutoff 2 mm) with restoration emergence angle (REA, cutoff 30°). The short-abutment, wide-angle group has the greatest MBL (+0.48 mm vs long/narrow, p<0.001) and about four times the peri-implantitis odds (OR 4.19, p=0.013). Each 1 mm increase in TmAH reduces the odds of any MBL by 37% (OR 0.63, p=0.003). Abutment height is the dominant modifiable protective factor: at TmAH >2 mm the emergence-angle penalty is largely neutralized, and a wide angle is hazardous mainly on a short abutment. See [[implants/misch-2005-crown-height-space-guidelines-part1]], [[implants/misch-2006-crown-height-space-guidelines-part2]] and [[implants/soft-tissue/misch-2025-abutment-height-emergence-angle-peri-implant-bone-loss]].

**5. Definitions of success and complications (1998 scale, 2008 ICOI Pisa, 2010 nerve).** The 1998 health-to-disease scale uses pain and mobility as primary criteria, with removal indicated for pain, vertical mobility, uncontrolled bone loss or generalized periradiolucency. The 2008 ICOI Pisa statement defines implant success, survival and failure, puts prosthetic support first, and makes aesthetic patient satisfaction a necessary element; it reports no survival percentage. The 2010 review covers inferior alveolar nerve (IAN) injury: incidence 0–44%, and a four-period protocol (intraoperative, 1 week, 12 weeks, referral criteria at each stage). See [[implants/survival/misch-1998-implant-quality-scale-health-disease-continuum]], [[implants/survival/misch-2008-implant-success-survival-failure-icoi-pisa]] and [[oral-surgery/nerve-injury/misch-2010-mandibular-nerve-neurosensory-implant-management]].

**Where the positions meet.** Load management recurs in 1994, 1999, 2004, 2005 (both), 2008 (definition only) and 2025. The shared premise is that overload is manageable by design, length, abutment and occlusion choices. The strongest data for that premise is the 2025 abutment study, and it measures radiographic bone level, not survival.

**Tensions and caveats.**
- Overload-to-bone-loss causation is argued in the 2005 occlusal review but remains qualitative; the 2025 study shows association with abutment geometry, not a load mechanism.
- The 1994 schema and the 2005 overload argument are compatible rationales, not tested comparisons. Read them as positions, not as evidence for each other.
- The 1998 98.9% survival figure cannot be compared with later survival studies because it is preliminary, has no comparator and no long-term follow-up.
- Crown-height guidance is consensus without numbers. The 2025 TmAH cutoff of 2 mm is the only numeric threshold in this set, and it comes from a single centre.

**Gaps in this corpus.** No RCT, no meta-analysis, and no long-term survival dataset from Misch's own papers. Most design and occlusal claims need outside sources before they can carry clinical weight.

**Clinical takeaways (as the held papers support them).**
1. Choose abutment height first: keep TmAH above 2 mm where the aesthetic and occlusal plan allows, especially when the emergence angle is 30° or more. [확인] (2025, retrospective)
2. Plan the transitional restoration as the main overload control in immediate loading; the abutment and implant position matter, but the 2004 rationale gives no outcome proof. [미검증]
3. For short posterior implants (<10 mm), reduce load through contact and cantilever control, added implants, larger diameter or surface, and splinting, as recommended in the 2005 review. [미검증] (expert rationale)
4. Use the 1998 quality scale and the 2008 ICOI definition as the checklist when comparing outcome studies. [확인] (definitional)
5. For IAN injury, follow the time-based protocol from the 2010 review and refer at the stated criteria. [확인] (expert protocol)
