> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **2개 환자 트랙이 완전히 다른 근거를 가진다** — 건강 성인(1차 의료) vs 치주염 치료 완료 환자(SPT 트랙)는 리콜 간격 권고의 근거 구조가 다르다.
> - **건강 성인(고확실성)**: 위험 기반 리콜 (Risk-based Recall) = 6개월 = 24개월 — 우식, 치은출혈, 구강건강 관련 삶의 질 (OHRQoL) 모두 차이 없음(Cochrane 고확실성, n=1736). 6개월 의무 간격은 근거 없음.
> - **건강 성인 — 그러나 전신 건강 이득은 별도**: 정기 스케일링을 안 하면 치아상실 위험비 (HR) 1.87, 급성심근경색 (AMI) 발생률 약 2배 → 임상 지표와 장기 전신 지표가 해리.
> - **치주염 치료 완료 환자(EFP Grade A)**: 3–12개월 개인화 지지 치주치료 (Supportive Periodontal Therapy, SPT) — 고위험(흡연·당뇨·비순응·잔존포켓) 3개월, 저위험 6–12개월. 17편 전향 코호트에서 치아상실·CAL 진행 유의 감소.
> - **SPT 안정성 실측**: 14개 연구 중 10개에서 CAL 손실 ≤0.5 mm(5–15년) — SPT 순응이 장기 치아 보존의 결정 변수.
> - **재발 주예측인자 2가지**: 비순응 + 잔존 포켓 (Residual Pocket) ≥5–6 mm — 이 두 인자가 있으면 3개월 간격으로 강화.
> - **프로토콜 선택**: 유도 바이오필름 요법 (Guided Biofilm Therapy, GBT) = 전통 SRP(임상적 동등); 기기 선택보다 개인화 구강위생교육(OHI) 이 임상 결과에 더 중요.
> - **임플란트 유지관리(SPIC)**: 동일 위험 기반 원칙(PRA/IDRA 알고리즘) — 정기 관리 미참여 시 치주위임플란트염 발생률 37% vs 정기 관리군 11%.
> - **RCT 공백**: SPT 간격 비교·SPT vs 모니터링 단독 RCT는 현재까지 전무(Manresa 2018 코크란). 현 3–6개월 권고는 관찰 연구 합의 + 적극 치료기 외삽.
> - **임상 결론**: 건강 성인은 위험도 기반 간격 연장 가능(장기 시스템 이득 유지 목적 유지); 치주염 환자는 EFP S3 Grade A 프로토콜 준수 + 비순응·잔존포켓 집중 관리.

## Three-line Summary

Across seven studies (2 Cochrane SRs, 1 rapid review, 2 SRs, 1 RCT, 1 narrative review), evidence bifurcates sharply by patient type: healthy adults in primary care show no clinically meaningful difference between 6-month, risk-based, and 24-month recall intervals (high certainty), whereas treated periodontitis patients benefit significantly from structured SPT every 3–12 months (EFP Grade A), with long-term cohort data demonstrating CAL stability ≤0.5 mm in over 70% of compliant patients over 5–15 years.

The primary predictors of SPT failure are non-compliance and residual pockets ≥5–6 mm after active therapy; SPT protocol (GBM vs conventional SRP) makes no significant difference to clinical outcomes in well-maintained patients.

A critical RCT gap persists for SPT-specific interval comparisons, making the current 3–6 month periodontal maintenance standard evidence-based by cohort consensus and EFP guideline synthesis rather than direct RCT proof.

## 세줄요약

7편 연구 종합(코크란 SR 2편·급속 리뷰 1편·SR 2편·RCT 1편·서술 리뷰 1편): 건강 성인은 치과 검진 간격 (Dental Recall Interval) 6개월 = 위험 기반 = 24개월(고확실성), 치주염 치료 완료 환자는 지지 치주치료 (Supportive Periodontal Therapy, SPT) 3–12개월이 EFP Grade A 강력권고이며 5–15년 전향 코호트에서 70% 이상이 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 손실 ≤0.5mm.

비순응과 잔존 포켓 (Residual Pocket) ≥5–6 mm가 SPT 실패의 주예측인자이며, 유도 바이오필름 요법 (Guided Biofilm Therapy, GBT) vs 전통 치근활택술 (Scaling and Root Planing, SRP)은 임상적으로 동등(RCT 근거).

SPT 간격 비교 RCT가 전무하여 현행 3–6개월 권고는 관찰 연구 합의 + 전향 코호트 외삽 기반이며, 직접 간격 비교 RCT가 가장 절실한 연구 공백이다.

## The Two-Track Model of Dental Recall

The evidence base for recall management divides cleanly into two populations with fundamentally different risk profiles, evidence quality, and clinical recommendations.

### Track A — Healthy Adults: Primary Care Recall

For adults without active or treated periodontitis who attend general dental practice regularly, the highest-quality evidence (high-certainty Cochrane) shows:

- **6-month = risk-based = 24-month recall** for caries surface increment, gingival bleeding, and OHRQoL over 4 years ([[periodontics/fee-2020-recall-intervals-oral-health-primary-care]])
- Routine scale-and-polish at any fixed interval produces **no clinically significant benefit** for gingivitis, probing depth, or OHRQoL in low-risk adults ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/lamont-2018-routine-scale-and-polish-periodontal-health]])
- The universal 6-month recall tradition is **not evidence-based** for periodontal or caries health in this population

**However, systemic outcomes diverge from short-term clinical indicators**:
- Not scaling for 3 years → tooth loss HR 1.87 (95%CI 1.03–3.38) (Lee 2019 Korean cohort, n=557)
- No regular scaling → AMI incidence 3.5% vs 1.9%, HR 0.543 (Kao 2021 Taiwan claims, n=14,328)
- Regular SPT in diabetes patients → €8.04/quarter reduction in healthcare costs (Smits 2020, Netherlands)

([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/matthews-2024-dental-scaling-polishing-adults-rapid-review]])

**Practical implication**: Interval extension is clinically safe for short-term periodontal/caries outcomes; the argument for maintaining regular attendance rests on long-term tooth loss prevention and systemic health cost reduction rather than short-term clinical metrics.

### Track B — Treated Periodontitis Patients: SPT

For adults who have completed active periodontal therapy, evidence converges from multiple sources:

#### Interval Recommendations

| Risk Profile | Recall Interval | Evidence Base |
|---|---|---|
| High risk (smoking, DM, residual pockets, non-compliance) | 3 months | EFP S3 Grade A; Farooqi 2015; Wang 2026 |
| Moderate risk | 3–6 months | EFP S3 Grade A; Wang 2026 |
| Low risk | 6–12 months | EFP S3 Grade A; Wang 2026 |
| Approaching 12 months | Significant tooth loss increase | Farooqi 2015 |

The **EFP S3 Clinical Practice Guideline** (evidence from 17 prospective cohorts) issues a **Grade A strong recommendation** for structured risk-based SPT in Stage I–IV periodontitis patients following active therapy ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/matthews-2024-dental-scaling-polishing-adults-rapid-review]]).

#### Long-Term Stability Under SPT

Jung et al. (2019) synthesized 14 independent investigations (n=964, 5–15 year follow-up):
- **10/14 studies**: mean CAL loss ≤0.5 mm — clinically stable
- 3–20% of sites experience CAL loss ≥2 mm (the high-risk subset)
- Independent predictors of progression: **poor oral hygiene, smoking, poor SPT compliance**

([[periodontics/jung-2019-periodontitis-spt-disease-progression-sr]])

#### The RCT Gap in SPT

Manresa et al. (2018) — the Cochrane SR specifically on SPT — found **zero eligible RCTs** comparing:
- SPT versus monitoring only
- SPT at different time intervals
- Different mechanical debridement approaches

The current 3–6 month periodontal maintenance standard rests on observational cohort consensus and EFP guideline synthesis from indirect evidence — not direct RCT comparison of intervals ([[periodontics/manresa-2018-supportive-periodontal-therapy-cochrane-sr]]).

## Predictors of SPT Failure

The two modifiable factors that most reliably predict recurrence and progression:

1. **Non-compliance** with scheduled maintenance visits — the dominant risk factor across all long-term studies
2. **Residual pockets ≥5–6 mm** after active therapy — independently associated with disease recurrence, tooth loss, and implant loss

Both factors are also linked: non-compliant patients are more likely to retain residual pockets due to insufficient professional biofilm disruption between visits.

([[periodontics/wang-2026-long-term-periodontal-peri-implant-tissue-stability]])

## SPT Protocol: Modality Equivalence

The choice of instrument for periodontal maintenance does not significantly affect clinical outcomes in well-maintained SPT patients:

| Protocol | BoP reduction | PPD, CAL | Treatment time | Pain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conventional SRP | Significant (14.7%→7.9%, p=0.004) | Comparable | ~40 min | Slightly lower |
| GBM (erythritol + slim ultrasonic) | Non-significant (12.2%→9.0%, p=0.191) | Comparable | ~42 min | Slightly higher |
| Final outcomes at 12 months | Equivalent | Equivalent | Equivalent | Marginal SRP advantage |

GBM showed fewer furcation-involved multirooted teeth at 12 months (p=0.015) — a signal requiring longer follow-up confirmation.

(**Clinical implication**: Select modality based on patient anatomy, furcation involvement, and preference — not expected superiority of one protocol.) 

([[periodontics/stahli-2024-clinical-evaluation-novel-protocol-supportive]])

Wang et al. (2026) confirm across the broader literature: ultrasonic scaling, air-polishing, and guided biofilm therapy are **all effective**; no single protocol demonstrates superior long-term results ([[periodontics/wang-2026-long-term-periodontal-peri-implant-tissue-stability]]).

## Peri-Implant Maintenance (SPIC)

The same risk-stratified principles apply to implant maintenance:

- Regular supportive peri-implant care (SPIC) markedly reduces peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis progression
- Risk-stratified intervals: 3–6 months (moderate-to-high risk) / up to 12 months (low risk)
- Risk assessment tools: **PRA** (Personalized Risk Assessment) and **IDRA** (Implant Disease Risk Assessment) algorithms

([[periodontics/wang-2026-long-term-periodontal-peri-implant-tissue-stability]])

## Emerging Technologies

Chair-side biomarker testing (GCF/saliva inflammatory markers) and AI-supported radiographic image analysis offer early detection potential before clinical attachment loss occurs. Clinical integration currently requires expert interpretation; long-term prospective validation is pending. Not yet a standard-of-care recommendation.

## Key Gaps and Future Research

1. **SPT interval RCT**: No RCT compares 3-month vs 6-month vs 12-month SPT in treated periodontitis — the most glaring evidence gap in periodontal maintenance
2. **Pediatric recall**: Very low certainty evidence for optimal recall intervals in children/adolescents; dedicated RCTs needed
3. **Underserved populations**: No studies on scaling effectiveness or appropriate intervals for low-income or inequitably-accessed populations (identified by Matthews 2024 for the Canadian Dental Care Plan)
4. **Long-term GBT/GBM validation**: Furcation-related GBM signal from Stahli 2024 requires confirmation in multi-year trials
5. **AI/biomarker clinical integration**: Promising but not validated as standalone diagnostic or recall-interval determinants
