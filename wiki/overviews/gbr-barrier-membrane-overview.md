## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) 차폐막을 17편 (서술적 고찰 6 + 동물실험 6 + 임상 전향 1 + 체계적 고찰 3 + 벤치 1) 으로 종합 — 흡수성/비흡수성이라는 단일 상류 변수가 1차 임상 분기점이다.
> - Benic 2014의 결손 분류(Class 1–5)가 이 페이지 전체 축의 상위 라우팅 표다: Class 1–2(소형·골벽 지지)는 흡수성 이식재+막, Class 3(골벽 지지 없음)는 DBBM+티타늄강화 e-PTFE, Class 5(수직 결손)는 자가골 블록. Esposito 2009 Cochrane SR(13 RCT)은 이 프레임을 임플란트 레벨 근거로 뒷받침: 위축 하악에서 수직증대보다 단축 임플란트가 실패(OR=5.74)·합병증(OR=4.97, p<0.05) 모두 적고, 골대체재가 자가골보다 환자 선호도 우위(장골 자가골 대비 OR=0.03) — "막을 잘 고르는 것"보다 "애초에 증대를 피할 수 있는가"가 상위 결정임을 시사.
> - **한 단계 뒤의 질문은 다르다** — Esposito 2009이 "증대를 할 것인가"(시술 선택)라면, Gurbanov 2024는 "이미 이식했다면 그 자리의 임플란트가 10년 뒤에도 버티는가"(장기 결과 안심)를 묻는다: 임플란트 생존율 (Implant Survival Rate, ISR) 이식부 96–100% vs 비이식부 91.6–94%로 열등하지 않으나, **10년 이상 기준을 통과한 연구가 전 세계 3편뿐**이고 메타분석 불가·전부 비무작위 (OCEBM Level 3–4) ·검정력 산정 없음 — 방향성 안심이지 동등성 입증이 아니다. [확인]
> - GBR은 끊김 없는 16–24주 차단 기능이 필요 — 치주재생술 (GTR) 의 4–6주보다 3~6배 길며, 가교화 안 된 천연 콜라겐막은 2–4주에 흡수되어 critically 부족하다 (Sasaki 2021). 이 시간 격차가 가교화 (crosslinking) 전략의 핵심 동기.
> - 가교화 화학이 콜라겐막 수명을 결정: 글루타르알데히드 (GA) 는 염증·세포독성 부담, 자외선 (UV) 은 조직통합 불량, EDC (카르보디이미드) 는 효소 저항성 + 낮은 세포독성으로 현재 선호되며, 리보스 (ribose) 가교 (Ossix Plus) 는 임상 사용 가능. [확인]
> - 차폐막 단독으로는 공간 유지 불가 — Park 2015에서 이식재 없는 막 단독군은 2주에 중앙 함몰. 위계: 막 + 이식재 > 이식재 단독 > 막 단독 > sham.
> - PASS 원칙이 막 종류와 무관하게 GBR 성공을 좌우: Primary closure (무장력 1차 봉합) · Angiogenesis (혈관신생) · Space maintenance (공간 유지) · Stability (안정성) — 이 중 막 노출 방지 (P) 가 단일 최대 결정인자.
> - 결손 유형별 표준: 수평 증대 (낮은 연조직 압력) → 흡수성 콜라겐막 + 복합 이식재 (자가골편 + DBBM), 수직 능선 증대 (VRA, 높은 압력) → 비흡수성 막 (e-PTFE·Ti강화 PTFE·티타늄 메쉬). (Buser 2023)
> - 이개열 (dehiscence) 결손에서 막의 역할은 골형성 촉진이 아니라 이식재 봉쇄 (containment) 일 수 있음 — Darby 2024: 5×3 mm 협측 이개열에서 막+DBBM vs DBBM 단독 신생골 차이 없음 (NS), 골은 측벽에서 구심성 (centripetal) 으로 자라 들어온다.
> - 동물실험 근거: EDC 막은 8주에 구조 유지 + 막관통 혈관신생 (favorable 신호, Park 2015), 리보스 가교막은 핀 고정 없이도 천연 콜라겐막 (Bio-Gide) + 4핀 고정보다 신생골 많음 (p=0.001, Friedmann 2022).
> - 차세대 막은 "프로그래머블 면역조절 인터페이스"로 패러다임 전환 (Liu 2026) — 대식세포 M1→M2 편극화가 핵심 표적이며, 동기는 GBR 합병증률 ~26% (100편) 와 발치 후 12개월 내 능선 폭 최대 50% 소실.
> - 차세대 재료군 4종 (고분자 복합·생분해성 Mg/Zn 합금·MXene·시트르산 기반 고분자) + 구조 4종 (이중층·야누스 Janus·구배 gradient·4D 프린팅) — 모두 연구 단계로 표준 임상 미적용. Zhou 2026의 커큐민-스트론튬 금속-페놀 전기방사막 (Cur-Sr/SF/PCL) 이 Liu 2026의 "프로그래머블 인터페이스" 명제를 골형성 (Wnt/Ca/CaN) ·혈관신생 (VEGF/HIF-1) ·M1→M2 면역재프로그래밍을 동시에 구현한 구체 설계로 입증 (벤치 + 쥐 치조골).
> - 합성 흡수성 막의 임상 진입: 3D 예성형 PLGA막 (Son 2026, 전향 20명) 이 티타늄 메쉬급 수평 골증대 (~3 mm, 획득률 87.2%) 를 막 노출 0건·2차 제거 불필요로 달성 — Ti-mesh의 15–50% 노출 부담을 회피. 그러나 흡수성 cage scaffold 전반은 근거가 희박 (Seiler 2026 SR: 7편·45부위, RCT 1편, 모든 연구가 분해속도 미보고, 비뚤림 위험 높음) 하여 Ti-mesh의 검증된 예측가능성을 아직 못 따라잡는다.
> - 어류유래 (잉어피부) EDC/NHS 콜라겐막 (Bujda 2026, 쥐 대퇴골) 은 단독 사용 시 우세 경향 + 비자극성 (생체적합성 우수) 이나 비critical-size 모델 한계로 통계 비유의 — 콜라겐 원료 다변화 (zoonotic/종교·윤리/비용 회피) 방향을 제시하나 graft 병용 시 Bio-Oss+Bio-Gide에 열등.
> - 임상 takeaway: 1차 결정은 가교화 종류가 아니라 결손 유형이며, 어떤 흡수성 막도 공간 유지가 필요한 결손에는 내부 이식재 (DBBM 선호) 가 필수다. 막 노출을 막는 무장력 봉합이 막 재료 선택보다 결과에 더 큰 영향을 준다.
> - Mizraji 2023 (Periodontology 2000, 2023) 이 Sasaki 2021의 흡수성/비흡수성 분류를 독립적으로 재확인하며, 정량적 노출위험 위계를 추가: 티타늄 메쉬 (7–66%) > 가교화 콜라겐 (native 대비 ~30% 높음) > native 콜라겐 > d-PTFE (~15%, open-healing 허용). 두 개의 독립적 Tier-1 리뷰가 같은 분류에 수렴 — 분류 신뢰도 강화.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 17 papers (6 narrative reviews, 6 animal studies, 1 prospective trial, 3 SRs, 1 bench study): GBR barrier membranes divide by a single upstream variable — resorbability — with crosslinking chemistry determining whether a collagen membrane meets the 16–24-week GBR barrier-function threshold (native collagen only lasts 2–4 weeks; EDC and ribose crosslinking extend this adequately).

Space maintenance requires graft support in all cases (membrane-alone collapses at 2 weeks; Park 2015), defect type governs membrane resorbability choice (horizontal → crosslinked collagen; vertical → non-resorbable), and for dehiscence defects the membrane's primary role may be graft containment rather than osteogenesis induction.

Next-generation "programmable interface" membranes targeting macrophage M1→M2 polarization (concretely realized by curcumin-strontium metal-phenolic SF/PCL electrospun membranes in vitro) and clinical-stage 3D-preformed PLGA membranes (zero exposure, ~3 mm augmentation) represent the active frontier but remain research-stage or early clinical.

## 세줄요약

17편(서술적 고찰 6·동물 6·임상 전향 1·SR 3·벤치 1) 종합: 골유도재생술(Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) 차폐막은 흡수성/비흡수성이라는 단일 상류 변수로 분기하며, 가교화(crosslinking) 전략이 콜라겐막의 16–24주 차단기능 충족 여부를 결정한다(천연 콜라겐은 2–4주에 흡수되어 부족, EDC·리보스 가교화가 해법).

공간 유지는 모든 경우에 이식재가 필수(막 단독군은 2주에 함몰; Park 2015), 결손 유형이 막 선택을 결정하며(수평 → 가교화 콜라겐, 수직 → 비흡수성), 작은 이개열 결손에서 막의 역할은 골형성 촉진이 아닌 이식재 봉쇄일 수 있다.

차세대 '프로그래머블 인터페이스' 막(대식세포 M1→M2 편극화 표적; 커큐민-스트론튬 금속-페놀 전기방사막으로 구현)과 임상 진입한 3D 예성형 PLGA막(노출 0건·~3 mm 수평 증대)이 최전선이나 아직 연구 단계 또는 초기 임상이다.

## Summary

### Core Thesis

A GBR barrier membrane is classically defined as a passive physical barrier that excludes faster-migrating non-osteogenic cells — fibroblasts and epithelial cells — while allowing slower osteoprogenitor cells and vasculature to repopulate a bony defect (Buser 2023). This passive occlusion model, established by Dahlin's 1988 rabbit experiment, remains the clinical foundation today. However, seven papers across the knowledge base collectively reveal an evolution in three layers: (1) membrane taxonomy by component, (2) crosslinking chemistry as the mechanism determining whether a biodegradable collagen membrane can actually sustain barrier function long enough for GBR, and (3) an emerging paradigm in which the membrane is a programmable immunomodulatory interface rather than an inert wall.

### Core Propositions

1. **GBR requires 16–24 weeks of uninterrupted barrier function** — nearly 3–6× longer than GTR's 4–6-week requirement (Sasaki 2021). Uncrosslinked native collagen membranes typically resorb within 2–4 weeks (Buser 2023), which falls critically short; this temporal gap is the primary design problem driving crosslinking strategies.

2. **The resorbability binary remains the first clinical decision fork** — non-biodegradable membranes (e-PTFE, d-PTFE, Ti-reinforced PTFE, Ti-mesh) and biodegradable membranes (collagen, aliphatic polyesters) differ not only in degradation kinetics but in surgical management implications. No evidence-based guidelines beyond this binary exist for clinical membrane selection (Sasaki 2021).

3. **Crosslinking chemistry determines collagen membrane longevity but carries trade-offs** — glutaraldehyde (GA) crosslinking produces inflammatory reactions and cytotoxicity at high concentration; UV crosslinking yields poor tissue integration; EDC (carbodiimide) crosslinking offers enzymatic resistance with low cytotoxicity (Sasaki 2021). Animal data confirm that EDC-crosslinked membranes remain structurally intact at 8 weeks (Park 2015) and ribose-crosslinked membranes outperform native collagen membranes for new bone formation at 3 months (Friedmann 2022).

4. **The PASS principle governs clinical GBR success regardless of membrane type** — Primary wound closure (tension-free), Angiogenesis, Space maintenance, and Stability are the four essential conditions identified across 35 years of GBR development (Buser 2023). Membrane selection interacts with PASS: non-resorbable membranes provide the space maintenance PASS element for vertical augmentation, while crosslinked collagen membranes provide enough barrier duration for horizontal augmentation when supported by a graft.

5. **Membrane alone cannot maintain space without graft support** — the EDC membrane-only group in Park 2015 showed central membrane collapse at 2 weeks. Graft material (BCP, DBBM) acts as the internal scaffold preventing membrane tent-collapse. The hierarchy: membrane + graft > graft alone > membrane alone > sham.

6. **Membrane's primary function in dehiscence defects may be containment rather than bone formation** — Darby 2024 found no significant difference in new bone formation between DBBM + resorbable membrane vs DBBM alone in 5×3 mm buccal dehiscence defects at 6 weeks, but the membrane tended to retain more bone substitute at central sections (p=0.052). Bone ingrowth occurred centripetally from lateral defect walls, not from the graft core. This suggests the membrane's role in self-contained dehiscence defects is primarily to retain particulate graft material, not to independently drive osteogenesis. The same principle extends to ARP: Khehra 2025 (Harvard RCT, n=20, 6 months) found that in post-extraction ARP sites with thick buccal bone (~1 mm), collagenated bovine xenograft alone equaled graft + non-crosslinked collagen membrane on all hard- and soft-tissue endpoints (horizontal bone width, vertical bone change, KTW, histomorphometric new bone; all p>0.244). In sites where the graft itself contains an integrated collagen component (DBBM-C), the inherent collagen matrix may function as an adequate biologic barrier — rendering a separate resorbable membrane redundant for the containment role. This applies specifically to thick-buccal-bone ARP; thin-wall/compromised sockets retain the standard indication for membrane coverage.

7. **Next-generation membranes reframe the membrane as a programmable immunomodulatory interface** — Liu 2026 identifies macrophage M1→M2 polarization as the central biological target; a pooled complication rate of ~26% across 100 GBR studies and up to 50% alveolar ridge width loss within 12 months post-extraction motivate this redesign. Current collagen and PTFE membranes are biologically inert — they do not modulate the immune microenvironment, stimulate angiogenesis, or provide osteogenic signals. Four next-generation material families (polymer composites, biodegradable Mg/Zn alloys, MXene-based systems, citrate-based polymers) and four structural architectures (bilayer, Janus, gradient, 4D-printed) address these gaps in research-stage platforms.

## Results

### Axis 1 — Membrane Classification: Non-biodegradable vs Biodegradable (Sasaki 2021)

Membranes are split into non-biodegradable and biodegradable classes by function, with 16–24 weeks of barrier function required for GBR (4–6 weeks for GTR). EDC crosslinking is now preferred over glutaraldehyde, ribose is clinically available, and beyond this binary no evidence-based selection guideline exists (Sasaki 2021; Mizraji 2023). Full synthesis: [[overviews/gbr-membrane-classification-crosslinking-overview]].

### Axis 2 — Crosslinking Strategy: GA vs EDC vs Ribose (Park 2015, Veríssimo 2014, Friedmann 2022)

Animal evidence maps the crosslinking decision: EDC (mammalian and fish collagen) gives a biocompatible barrier lasting at least 8 weeks, glutaraldehyde lasts longer but is fibrously encapsulated, ribose improves new bone over native collagen with pins, and a membrane alone collapses without graft support (Park 2015; Friedmann 2022; Bujda 2026). Full synthesis: [[overviews/gbr-membrane-classification-crosslinking-overview]].

### Axis 3 — GBR Clinical Application: PASS Principles, Horizontal vs Vertical (Buser 2023)

Buser 2023's PASS principles (primary closure, angiogenesis, space maintenance, stability) organize GBR. Horizontal augmentation uses a collagen membrane with a composite autograft–DBBM graft, and vertical augmentation uses a non-resorbable framework. Full synthesis: [[overviews/gbr-clinical-application-additive-value-overview]].

### Axis 4 — Membrane Alone vs Membrane + Graft: Additive Value in Dehiscence (Darby 2024)

In a small self-contained dehiscence defect a collagen membrane added no bone over DBBM alone and mainly contained the graft (Darby 2024), so a membrane's value depends on defect geometry. Full synthesis: [[overviews/gbr-clinical-application-additive-value-overview]].

### Axis 5 — Next-Generation Membranes: Programmable Interface Concept (Liu 2026)

Next-generation membranes aim to program the tissue interface, chiefly by shifting macrophages from M1 to M2, through polymer composites, biodegradable Mg/Zn alloys, MXenes, and citrate-based polymers in bilayer, Janus, gradient, or 4D-printed architectures. A silk fibroin–PCL membrane loaded with curcumin–strontium nanoparticles is the working preclinical example (Liu 2026; Zhou 2026). Full synthesis: [[overviews/gbr-next-generation-synthetic-membranes-overview]].

### Axis 6 — Novel Membrane Materials: Mineralized and Reticulated Collagen (Veríssimo 2014)

Mineralized, glutaraldehyde-crosslinked polyanionic collagen kept its structure at 12 weeks, but the hydroxyapatite mineral added nothing beyond the crosslinking and the membrane was fibrously encapsulated (Veríssimo 2014). Full synthesis: [[overviews/gbr-next-generation-synthetic-membranes-overview]].

### Axis 7 — Synthetic-Resorbable Membranes/Scaffolds as the Titanium-Mesh Alternative (Son 2026, Seiler 2026)

Rigid resorbable synthetic membranes aim to match titanium-mesh space maintenance without removal surgery. A 3D-preformed PLGA membrane gave 87.2% hard-tissue gain with zero exposure in a single-arm study (Son 2026), but the whole category rests on seven small studies with unreported degradation (Seiler 2026). Full synthesis: [[overviews/gbr-next-generation-synthetic-membranes-overview]].

### Axis 8 — Foundational Reviews and Adjacent Evidence: Defect Classification, Cochrane-Level Comparison, Long-Term Survival in Grafted Sites, Collagen Biomaterials Science, and Membrane Fixation (Benic 2014, Esposito 2009, Gurbanov 2024, Li 2025, Park 2022)

Foundational and adjacent evidence: the Class 1–5 defect taxonomy (Benic 2014); the Cochrane finding that short implants can beat vertical augmentation on complications (Esposito 2009); directional but thin ten-year survival reassurance for grafted sites (Gurbanov 2024); the collagen biomaterials layer (Li 2025); and membrane fixation, which helps only some membranes (Park 2022). Full synthesis: [[overviews/gbr-clinical-application-additive-value-overview]].

## Clinical Decision Threads [미검증]

**Decision Thread 1 — Defect type → membrane resorbability**

The primary clinical decision is not crosslinking type but defect type:
- Horizontal augmentation (low soft-tissue pressure, contained) → biodegradable collagen membrane is adequate; crosslinking determines whether it lasts long enough
- Vertical ridge augmentation (high soft-tissue pressure, open) → non-resorbable membrane (Ti-reinforced PTFE, Ti-mesh, ePTFE) required for space maintenance against gravity and muscle pull. An emerging alternative — rigid synthetic-resorbable membranes/scaffolds (3D-PLGA, PCL cage scaffolds) — aims to deliver this space maintenance without second-stage removal and at lower exposure risk: Son 2026 shows ~3 mm horizontal augmentation with zero exposure clinically, but Seiler 2026's SR cautions the broader evidence is 7 small heterogeneous studies with unreported degradation kinetics, so Ti-mesh remains the validated default for demanding vertical cases (see Axis 7).

**Decision Thread 2 — Crosslinking choice for biodegradable membranes**

If a biodegradable membrane is chosen for horizontal augmentation:
- Native collagen (no crosslink): barrier function 2–4 weeks; insufficient for GBR's 16–24-week requirement; only appropriate when tension-free closure + composite graft provides all space maintenance and the membrane serves primarily as a wound-covering containment layer (as in Darby 2024's small dehiscence model)
- Crosslinked collagen (EDC or ribose): 8–24+ weeks confirmed barrier function; preferred for defects where membrane degradation timing matters. Friedmann 2022 provides direct evidence that ribose-crosslinked (Ossix Plus) produces more new bone than native collagen (Bio-Gide) + pin fixation. EDC crosslinking (Park 2015) confirmed sustained barrier and transmembraneous angiogenesis — a favorable vascular integration signal absent from GA-crosslinked membranes

**Decision Thread 3 — Graft support is mandatory when membrane alone is placed**

Park 2015 demonstrated membrane collapse without graft at 2 weeks. In clinical practice: any biodegradable membrane placed over a defect requiring space maintenance (i.e., non-self-contained defects) requires an internal graft scaffold. DBBM is the preferred volume-maintaining graft material; composite graft (DBBM + autogenous chips) is the current gold standard for horizontal GBR (Buser 2023). For small, self-contained buccal dehiscence defects, DBBM alone may provide sufficient bone formation without the membrane driving incremental bone gain (Darby 2024) — though the membrane contributes graft containment.

**Decision Thread 4 — Membrane exposure is the dominant complication driver**

The PASS principle's "P" (primary tension-free closure) is the outcome-determining factor, not membrane material selection alone. A PTFE membrane under a closed wound outperforms a premium crosslinked collagen membrane over an exposed wound. Surgical technique for tension-free closure — periosteal releasing incisions, coronally advanced flaps — represents the highest-leverage intervention for GBR success. [See [[overviews/suture-wound-closure-decision-ladder]] for the closure evidence base in augmentation contexts.]

**Decision Thread 5 — When to consider next-generation membranes (research context)**

Conventional membranes have a ~26% pooled complication rate (Liu 2026). Current next-generation platforms (Mg/Zn alloys, MXene, citrate polymers, 4D-printed) address immunomodulation and angiogenesis — gaps that crosslinking alone cannot fill. These are not yet clinically available in standard practice. The most actionable near-term signal from Liu 2026 is the macrophage M1→M2 framing: when evaluating new membrane products, the key biological question is whether they modulate the immune microenvironment, not merely extend degradation duration.

- [[bone-regeneration/darby-2024-resorbable-membrane-dbbm-gbr-dehiscence]] — Animal study (9 dogs, 6w, split-mouth, 5×3 mm buccal dehiscence); membrane + DBBM vs DBBM alone: no new bone advantage (NS); membrane contains graft (p=0.052 trend); bone ingrowth centripetal from lateral walls
- [[bone-regeneration/bujda-2026-carp-collagen-membrane-gbr-rat-femoral]] — Animal study (21 rats, 4/12/16w); European-carp-skin EDC/NHS-crosslinked collagen membrane non-irritant + trends best used alone (non-significant, large effect sizes); Bio-Oss pairing underperforms Bio-Oss+Bio-Gide. - [[bone-regeneration/zhou-2026-metal-phenolic-electrospun-membrane-alveolar-gbr]] — In-vitro + rat alveolar defect; curcumin–strontium metal-phenolic SF/PCL electrospun membrane (Cur-Sr/SF/PCL) synergistically drives osteogenesis (Wnt/Ca/CaN), angiogenesis (VEGF/HIF-1), and M1→M2 reprogramming. - [[bone-regeneration/son-2026-plga-membrane-horizontal-ridge-gbr-prospective]] — Prospective single-arm (20 pts/21 sites); 3D-preformed resorbable PLGA membrane achieves ~3 mm horizontal augmentation, 87.2% retention, 0 exposure at 5 mo. - [[bone-regeneration/seiler-2026-resorbable-scaffolds-alveolar-ridge-augmentation-sr]] — Systematic review (7 studies, 39 pts, 1 RCT); resorbable scaffolds (PLA/PCL/PLGA/β-TCP) a viable second-surgery-avoiding alternative to Ti-mesh, but evidence sparse/heterogeneous (no meta-analysis), degradation kinetics unreported in all 7. The evidence-base reality check for Axis 7
- [[bone-regeneration/benic-2014-horizontal-bone-augmentation-guided-regeneration]] — Narrative review (Periodontology 2000); Class 1–5 defect classification and material-routing table; four-origin bone graft taxonomy. The master clinical framework Axis 1–2 material choices route through (Axis 8)
