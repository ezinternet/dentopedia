## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 발치된 치아 뿌리 형태를 그대로 복제해 즉시 식립하는 **근관유사 임플란트 (Root-Analogue Implant, RAI)** — CAD-CAM 밀링 또는 적층제조 (DMLS/SLM) 로 제작 — 5편을 근거·재료·적응증으로 종합. 결론 미리: **심미·연조직은 유망하나 아직 실험적이며 루틴 사용은 정당화되지 않음.**
> - **최고 근거 (SR) — Alqutaibi 2026 (28편·432 RAI, 메타분석 없음)**: 대부분 생존 71–100%·성공 64.5–100%. **재료가 예후를 가름** — 티타늄·하이브리드(Ti-Zr)가 가장 예측가능 (생존 최대 100%, 1년 MBL 최저 0.7 mm), **지르코니아는 33.3–100%로 산포 크고 매크로유지·표면처리 부족 시 파절/실패↑**. 근거는 단일환자 증례·소규모 시리즈 지배, RCT 단 1편, 대부분 추적 ≤2년.
> - **최장 추적 (10년) 이나 n=1 — Figliuzzi 2022 증례보고**: Ti-6Al-4V DMLS RAI, 파절된 상악 제2소구치 즉시식립, **10년 치조정 골소실 0 mm·PPD 2.5 mm·BoP 없음**. RAI 문헌 중 최장 추적이나 단일 증례라 일반화 불가 — "best case"의 상한을 보여줄 뿐.
> - **최대 단일기관 시리즈 — Bose 2020 (107 REPLICATE RAI, 추적 18.9개월)**: 생존 94.4%, 평균 MBL 1.20 mm (부하 후 진행 유의하게 둔화). 그러나 **4-도메인 복합 성공률은 64.5%에 그침** — 임플란트 수준(80.8%)·보철 변연적합(83.3%)이 발목. 연조직(96.8%)·환자만족(90%)은 우수. 저자 결론: **기존 나사형 즉시임플란트 대비 MBL 우위 없고 변연적합 약하며 ~15% 고비용 → 루틴 사용 아직 부정당.**
> - **자연치근 보존이 RAI를 이긴다 — Bose 2023 (RAI 20 vs 강제교정정출 FOE 20)**: 양군 생존 100%이나 **FOE가 MBL(p<0.01)·인접치간 유두/접촉·점막질에서 RAI보다 유의하게 우수**; 복합 FIPS FOE ~9.0 vs RAI ~7.5. 함의: 가능하면 **강제교정정출로 자연치근/치조골을 보존**하는 편이 즉시 RAI보다 조직 보존적 — RAI는 대안이지 우월책이 아님. (contradicts RAI 우위 가정)
> - **지르코니아 RAI의 경고 — Debeus 2025 (전향 n=30, 맞춤 2-piece ZrO2, 5년)**: **생존 75.8%·성공 71.0%** — 지르코니아 임플란트 문헌 벤치마크(1년 ~95.6%)를 크게 하회. 9/30 실패 = 조기 골유착 4 + **수평 기계적 파절 5**. 저자 명시적 결론: **"임상 권장 불가"** — 원인은 지르코니아 재료 자체가 아니라 구개측(off-axis) 식립 + 측방 교합 과부하로 지목.
> - **재료 위계 (교차 수렴)**: 티타늄·하이브리드 RAI > 지르코니아 RAI. 지르코니아는 심미 이점에도 **파절 취약** — 매크로유지 설계·표면처리·축상(on-axis) 식립·측방 과부하 회피가 전제. 이는 일반 지르코니아 임플란트의 파절/취성 우려와 동일 선상 (→ [[overviews/zirconia-implant-clinical-outcomes]]).
> - **RAI의 진짜 매력과 한계**: 매력 = 무증대·발치와 정밀적합·심미/연조직·1회 수술. 한계 = 변연적합 편차·정밀 제작 의존·고비용·장기근거 부재(≤2년, RCT 1편)·지르코니아 파절. **핵심 전제 = 발치 시 치근 손상 없이 정밀 복제 가능해야 하고(파절치·만곡근 난이도↑), 즉시 축상 안정 확보.**
> - **적응증 결정**: ① 자연치근 보존 가능하면 (FOE/socket-shield 등) 먼저 고려, ② RAI 선택 시 **티타늄/하이브리드** 우선·강성 축상 식립·측방 과부하 회피, ③ 지르코니아 RAI는 파절위험 상담 필수·off-axis 금물, ④ 환자에게 **실험적·장기근거 제한**임을 고지. 루틴 1차 선택은 아직 표준 나사형 즉시임플란트.
> - **연구 공백**: 다기관 RCT·장기(>5년) 추적·변연적합 표준화·지르코니아 파절 저감 설계가 채워지기 전엔 RAI는 선택적·연구적 술식으로 남는다.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 5 papers on root-analogue implants (RAI) — CAD-CAM/additively manufactured implants replicating the extracted tooth's root morphology for immediate single-tooth replacement — across evidence, material, and indication.

The best evidence (Alqutaibi 2026 SR, 28 studies/432 RAIs) shows survival 71–100% but material-dependent predictability (titanium/hybrid most reliable, MBL as low as 0.7 mm; zirconia 33.3–100% with higher fracture risk), on a base dominated by single-case reports with ≤2-year follow-up and only 1 RCT; the largest series (Bose 2020, 107 RAIs) reports 94.4% survival but only 64.5% composite success (weak marginal fit, ~15% higher cost, "not yet justified for routine use"), a custom-zirconia prospective trial (Debeus 2025, n=30) reports just 75.8% 5-year survival ("cannot be recommended," fractures from off-axis placement/overload), and a comparative study (Bose 2023) found forced orthodontic extrusion of the natural root outperforms RAI on bone and soft tissue — while a single 10-year case (Figliuzzi 2022) shows 0 mm bone loss as a best-case ceiling.

Clinically, RAI is esthetically/soft-tissue promising but experimental: prefer preserving the natural root (e.g., forced orthodontic extrusion) when possible, choose titanium/hybrid over fracture-prone zirconia, place on-axis avoiding lateral overload, disclose the limited long-term evidence, and keep conventional screw-type immediate implants as the routine first choice.

## Summary

A root-analogue implant (RAI, 근관유사 임플란트) is a patient-specific implant whose body replicates the three-dimensional morphology of the just-extracted tooth root, produced by CAD-CAM milling or additive manufacturing (DMLS/SLM) and placed immediately into the fresh socket — the appeal being a press-fit that avoids augmentation, preserves the socket, and optimizes esthetics with a single surgery. This overview asks the practical question: **is RAI ready for routine use, and if used, how?** The five papers converge on a cautious answer.

---

## The evidence base and its ceiling

### Alqutaibi 2026 — the systematic review (best available synthesis)
- **28 clinical studies, 432 RAIs** (titanium, zirconia, hybrid Ti-Zr; milled or additively manufactured), no meta-analysis.
- Survival mostly **71–100%**, success **64.5–100%**.
- **Material stratifies predictability:** titanium and **hybrid Ti-Zr are most predictable** (survival up to 100%, MBL as low as 0.7 mm at 1 year); **zirconia ranges 33.3–100%** with higher failure/fracture risk when macroretentive design or surface treatment is inadequate.
- **The crucial limitation:** evidence is **dominated by single-patient reports and small case series**, mostly **≤2-year follow-up**, heterogeneous protocols, and **only 1 RCT** → not yet a basis for routine practice; well-designed multicenter RCTs with longer follow-up are needed.

### Figliuzzi 2022 — the 10-year ceiling (n=1)
A single Ti-6Al-4V DMLS RAI (fractured maxillary 2nd premolar, immediate placement) followed **10 years**: **crestal bone resorption 0 mm, PPD 2.5 mm, no BoP, no recession, full satisfaction.** It is the **longest-documented RAI follow-up** and shows what a *best case* can achieve — but as n=1 it defines a ceiling, not an expectation.

---

## Where RAI falls short (and against what)

### Bose 2020 — the largest single-center series
- **107 REPLICATE RAIs; 31 with full follow-up (18.9 months).**
- Survival **94.4%**, mean **MBL 1.20 mm** (progression significantly slower after loading).
- **Composite 4-domain success only 64.5%**, dragged down by implant-level (80.8%) and **prosthetic/marginal-fit (83.3%)** shortfalls — despite strong soft-tissue (96.8%) and satisfaction (90%).
- Authors' verdict: RAI **did not outperform conventional screw-shaped immediate implants** on MBL, had **weaker marginal fit at ~15% higher cost**, and is **not yet justified for routine use** (esthetics/soft-tissue favorable). This series is the source of the hybrid-implant MBL benchmark cited by later reviews.

### Bose 2023 — the natural root wins (RAI vs forced orthodontic extrusion)
- **20 RAI vs 20 forced orthodontic extrusion (FOE)**-preserved natural-root restorations, scored by the Functional Implant Prosthodontic Score (FIPS).
- **Both 100% survival**, but **FOE significantly better on MBL (p<0.01), interproximal papillae/contacts, and mucosa quality**; composite FIPS **FOE ~9.0 vs RAI ~7.5**.
- Implication: **preserving the natural root (via FOE) is more bone/soft-tissue-conserving than immediate RAI** — RAI is an alternative, not a superior option. (Small sample, unequal follow-up, single-operator — but directionally important.)

### Debeus 2025 — the zirconia warning
- Prospective single-arm, **n=30 custom two-piece ZrO2 (ZV3) implants**, single maxillary premolar, up to 5 years.
- **5-year survival 75.8%, success 71.0%** — far below the zirconia-implant benchmark (~95.6% at 1 year).
- **9/30 lost: 4 early osseointegration failures + 5 horizontal fractures.**
- Authors explicitly conclude the protocol **"cannot be recommended for clinical practice,"** attributing fractures to **off-axis (palatal) placement and lateral occlusal overload — not to zirconia as a material per se.**

## Synthesis & Clinical Bottom Line

| Question | Answer | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Is RAI ready for routine use? | No — experimental; evidence is case-report-dominated, ≤2 yr, 1 RCT | SR (Alqutaibi 2026) |
| Which material is safest? | Titanium/hybrid Ti-Zr > zirconia (fracture-prone) | SR + prospective |
| Does RAI beat conventional immediate implants? | No MBL advantage; weaker marginal fit, higher cost | largest series (Bose 2020) |
| Is there a better tissue-preserving option? | Yes — preserve the natural root (forced orthodontic extrusion) | comparative (Bose 2023) |
| What kills zirconia RAI? | Off-axis placement + lateral overload → fracture | prospective (Debeus 2025) |
| Best documented long-term result? | 0 mm bone loss at 10 yr — but n=1 | case report (Figliuzzi 2022) |

**Decision rule:**
1. **Prefer preserving the natural root** (forced orthodontic extrusion, or socket-shield where indicated) before defaulting to any immediate implant — it out-preserves bone and soft tissue vs RAI.
2. If placing an RAI, **choose titanium or hybrid Ti-Zr** over zirconia; ensure **macroretentive design, on-axis placement, and no lateral overload**.
3. **Zirconia RAI carries a real fracture risk** — mandatory consent, avoid off-axis/palatal placement and heavy lateral contacts.
4. **Disclose that RAI is experimental** with limited long-term (>5 yr) and RCT evidence; keep **conventional screw-type immediate implants as the routine first choice**.

**Prerequisite reality check:** RAI requires an **atraumatic, morphologically faithful extraction** (harder with fractured or curved roots) and **immediate on-axis primary stability** — conditions that are not always met, which is part of why routine use remains unjustified.

**Research gaps:** multicenter RCTs, >5-year follow-up, standardized marginal fit, and fracture-reducing zirconia designs must land before RAI moves from selective/experimental to routine.
