> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **결론 (thesis)**: 스타틴 (Statin)–치과 골치유 문헌은 두 축으로 갈린다 — ⓐ 고지혈증 (Hyperlipidemia)은 골유착 (Osseointegration, OI)을 저해하는 **숙주 위험인자**, ⓑ 국소 스타틴은 골치유를 높이는 **치료 보조제** 후보. 두 축 모두 생물학적 타당성은 강하지만 인체 근거는 약하거나 상충한다.
> - **축 A 핵심 수치**: 2년 추적 연구 1편에서 고콜레스테롤혈증이 임플란트 주위염 (Peri-implantitis)·실패와 독립적으로 연관 (OR 5.1, P = 0.046, Sun 2023) — 그러나 다른 후향 코호트는 영향 없음. 즉 **임상 신호 자체가 상충**한다.
> - **축 A 기전**: 고지혈증은 Wnt/β-catenin 억제·PPARγ 과활성으로 조골세포 (Osteoblast) 분화를 억제하고, RANKL로 파골세포 (Osteoclast)를 촉진하며, 티타늄 산화막 (TiO₂)이 LDL을 산화 LDL (oxLDL)로 바꿔 국소 활성산소 (Reactive Oxygen Species, ROS)를 높인다 (Sun 2023).
> - **축 B 전임상**: 동물 임플란트 연구의 ~95%에서 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 향상 (Tahamtan 2020); 발치와 동물 SR 9편(403마리) **전부 양성** — 잔존 치조제 높이 0.960 vs 0.922 (12주), TGF-β1 (P<0.05)·골형성단백질-2 (Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2, BMP-2) (P<0.01) mRNA 상향 (Kommuri 2025).
> - **축 B 인체 RCT는 상충**: Degala 2018 (n=30, 분할구강 (split-mouth)) 심바스타틴 (Simvastatin) **10 mg** + Gelfoam → 1·4·8·12주 모든 시점 방사선 골밀도 유의 증가 (P = .001). Katanec 2026 (n=9 파일럿) 심바스타틴 **1 mg** + cerabone® → 신생골 비율 (New Bone/Total Volume, NB/TV) 31.0% vs 26.2% (p=0.45), **유의차 없음**.
> - **왜 상충하나 (분기점)**: 용량(10 mg vs 1 mg), 전달체(Gelfoam 단독 vs 천천히 흡수되는 이종골 (Xenograft)에 식염수 단순 혼합), 결과지표(방사선 gray-level vs 생검 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry)), 검정력 (n=9, NB/TV 95% CI −8.7~+18.4)이 모두 다르다.
> - **역설**: Katanec의 1 mg은 쥐 SR에서 효과를 낸 용량(Wu 2008 PLGA 1 mg)과 **절대량이 같다** — 용량보다 **서방형 전달 (Controlled-Release)** 여부(PLGA·하이드로겔 vs 단순 혼합)가 더 결정적일 가능성.
> - **스타틴 종류**: 지질친화성 (Lipophilic) 스타틴(심바·로바·아토르바스타틴)이 BMP-2/Smad·OPG/RANKL·NF-κB 경로로 작용 (Rallis 2025); 반면 치주 국소 투여에선 로수바스타틴이 가장 효과적 (Tahamtan 2020) — 최적 약제는 미확정.
> - **전신 투여 한계**: 경구 생체이용률 10–20%(초회통과효과 (First-pass effect)), 쥐 전신 항흡수 효과엔 인체 콜레스테롤 용량의 ≥10배 필요 (Tahamtan 2020) → 골 목적이면 국소 투여가 원칙.
> - **임상 takeaway**: ① 고지혈증 환자는 금기가 아니라 **위험 인지 + 보수적 프로토콜**(술전 지질 조절, 고초기고정, 치유기간 연장 — Sun 2023 저자 권고, 근거 수준 낮음); ② 발치와 보존 시 국소 심바스타틴은 **안전하지만 루틴 권고 불가** — 특히 이종골 단순 혼합은 효과 기대 금지 (Katanec 2026); ③ 쓰더라도 서방형 전달체 + 충분 용량이 전제.

## Three-line Summary

Six studies (three narrative reviews, one animal systematic review, two human split-mouth RCTs) address statins and dental bone healing from two clinical angles: hyperlipidemia as a host risk for osseointegration, and local statin application as a bone-healing adjunct.
The evidence splits: hyperlipidemia impairs osseointegration in preclinical models and reviews (with one conflicting clinical signal, OR 5.1 vs a null cohort), while local statin in extraction sockets is uniformly positive in animals but conflicting in humans — Degala 2018 (simvastatin 10 mg, Gelfoam, n=30) positive vs Katanec 2026 (simvastatin 1 mg admixed with xenograft, n=9) negative.
Clinically, hyperlipidemia warrants risk-aware, conservative implant protocols rather than contraindication, and local statin is a safe but unproven adjunct whose benefit likely depends on dose and controlled-release delivery — not suitable as routine socket-preservation practice yet.

## 세줄요약

6편(서사적 리뷰 3편·동물 체계적 문헌고찰 1편·인체 분할구강 RCT 2편)이 스타틴 (Statin)과 치과 골치유를 두 축 — 고지혈증 (Hyperlipidemia)이라는 숙주 위험, 국소 스타틴이라는 치유 보조 치료 — 에서 다룬다.
고지혈증은 전임상·리뷰에서 골유착 (Osseointegration, OI)을 저해하지만 임상 신호는 상충(OR 5.1 vs 무관 코호트)하고, 발치와 국소 스타틴은 동물에선 일관되게 양성이나 인체에선 Degala 2018 (10 mg, Gelfoam, n=30) 양성 vs Katanec 2026 (1 mg, 이종골 혼합, n=9) 음성으로 갈린다.
임상적으로 고지혈증은 금기가 아닌 위험 인지·보수적 프로토콜의 대상이고, 국소 스타틴은 안전하나 효과 미입증 보조제로 용량·서방형 전달 (Controlled-Release)에 좌우될 가능성이 커 아직 루틴 발치와 보존 (Socket Preservation)에 쓰기 이르다.

## Clinical Angles

The literature on statins in dental bone surgery is easy to misread because two different clinical questions share the same drug name. They must be kept apart.

### Axis A — Hyperlipidemia as host risk

**Question**: A patient presents with hyperlipidemia (often already taking a systemic statin). Does this systemic context change implant risk or planning?

- [[implants/survival/sun-2023-effects-of-hyperlipidemia-on-osseointegration]] is the main Axis-A source: it details how elevated LDL/oxLDL and reduced HDL suppress osteoblastogenesis (Wnt/β-catenin inhibition, PPARγ upregulation, adipocyte shift of BMSCs), drive osteoclastogenesis (RANKL, TNF-α, IL-1, IL-6), and amplify inflammation (NF-κB, M1 macrophage polarization, impaired neutrophils). It adds a titanium-specific hazard: the TiO₂ surface oxidizes circulating LDL to oxLDL.
- [[implants/survival/rallis-2025-statins-dental-implant-osseointegration]] (abstract-only) restates the triad — reduced osteoblastic function, increased osteoclast activity, compromised collagen turnover — and flags obesity and lipid metabolism as confounders requiring patient stratification.
- **Clinical signal is contested**: one two-year study links hypercholesterolemia to peri-implantitis/failure (OR 5.1, P = 0.046); a separate retrospective cohort found no influence (both reported via Sun 2023).
- **Systemic statin as a possible offset**: Rallis 2025 notes modest BMD gains with systemic statin use but no confirmed fracture-risk reduction and no trial showing improved implant osseointegration. The existing [[overviews/drug-systemic-disease-dental-management-overview]] separately records an observational statin HR of 0.16 for implant failure (Chidiac 2026) — an observational signal outside this six-paper set, not a trial result.

### Axis B — Local statin application as therapy

**Question**: Should a statin be placed locally into an extraction socket, graft, or implant bed to enhance bone healing?

- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/kommuri-2025-efficacy-of-statins-in-tissue]] — animal SR, extraction sockets; all 9 studies positive.
- [[drug/systemic-disease/tahamtan-2020-the-effects-of-statins-on]] — broad review: ~95% of animal implant studies positive for BIC; alveolar-bone-preservation human RCTs showed only non-significant trends.
- [[implants/survival/sun-2023-effects-of-hyperlipidemia-on-osseointegration]] also bridges into Axis B: it catalogs local delivery routes (peri-implant injection, implant surface coating, bone-graft modification) specifically as a countermeasure in hyperlipidemic hosts.
- [[oral-surgery/third-molar/degala-2018-evaluation-efficacy-simvastatin-bone-regeneration-third-molars]] — human RCT, positive.
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/katanec-2026-histomorphometric-evaluation-of-socket-preservation]] — human RCT, negative.

The axes interact: Sun 2023 frames local statin delivery as the remedy for the Axis-A deficit, but no dental-implant–specific clinical trial under hyperlipidemic conditions exists.

## Mechanistic Framework

### Anabolic: BMP-2/Smad

- Statins (HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors) upregulate BMP-2 and activate Smad transcription factors, driving osteogenic commitment; a parallel Ras-PI3K-Akt-Erk cascade supports osteoblast survival and matrix synthesis (Rallis 2025, Tahamtan 2020).
- In vivo confirmation in sockets: Liu 2009 (in Kommuri 2025) showed simvastatin–PLGA raised TGF-β1 (P<0.05) and BMP-2 (P<0.01) mRNA at weeks 1–4, and VEGF at weeks 1–2, temporally aligned with bone formation.

### Anti-resorptive: OPG/RANKL and NF-κB

- Statins shift the OPG/RANKL ratio toward osteoclast suppression and inhibit NF-κB and p38 MAPK (Rallis 2025).
- The same OPG↑/RANKL↓ mechanism explains reduced orthodontic relapse in Tahamtan 2020 — a cross-domain consistency check.
- Mirror image of Axis A: hyperlipidemia *activates* NF-κB and RANKL (Sun 2023). Statins therefore push on the same nodes that hyperlipidemia disturbs, which is the biological rationale for Sun 2023's statin-coating countermeasures.

### Angiogenic / early wound phase

- VEGF upregulation (Kommuri 2025) and VEGF/eNOS-driven early fibrovascular activity are proposed to explain Katanec 2026's non-significant +5% CT/TV in the statin arm — interpreted by the authors as a possibly earlier phase rather than impaired osteogenesis. This is untested (no molecular markers were measured).

### Lipophilic vs hydrophilic statins

- Rallis 2025 attributes the pro-osteoblastic effect to **lipophilic** statins — simvastatin, lovastatin, atorvastatin.
- Yet the held clinical data do not map cleanly onto that list: in periodontal local delivery, **rosuvastatin** was the most efficacious agent (Tahamtan 2020), and four of nine animal socket studies used **fluvastatin** (Kommuri 2025). Neither appears on Rallis's lipophilic list. The wiki does not hold a head-to-head comparison of statin types for socket or implant healing.

### Concentration dependency

- Katanec 2026 argues that BMP-2/Ras/PI3K/Akt/ERK signaling requires **sustained low-micromolar** concentrations that a bolus cannot maintain.
- Concentration cuts both ways: simvastatin 1 mmol/L *suppressed* dental pulp stem cell proliferation while increasing mineralization (Tahamtan 2020), and one implant-coating study impaired osseointegration (Tahamtan 2020). More drug is not monotonically better.
- Systemic route is inefficient: oral bioavailability 10–20% after first-pass metabolism; rodent systemic anti-resorptive effects need ≥10× the human cholesterol-lowering dose (Tahamtan 2020). Sun 2023 likewise argues systemic statins need large doses to reach bone levels, increasing side effects.

### Delivery is the bottleneck

| Delivery | Where used | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| PLGA scaffold / microspheres | Animal sockets (Wu 2008, Liu 2009, Yasunami 2015 in Kommuri 2025); periodontal pockets (Tahamtan 2020) | Positive |
| Hydrogel microspheres (0.01 mg) | Animal socket (Li 2019 in Kommuri 2025) | Positive |
| Implant surface coating (MAO-Ti, mesoporous TiO₂, CaP) | Hyperlipidemic animals (Sun 2023) | Positive, but low loading efficiency; one coating study negative (Tahamtan 2020) |
| Gelfoam carrier, 10 mg | Human third-molar sockets (Degala 2018) | Positive (radiographic) |
| Saline admixture into slowly resorbing bovine xenograft, 1 mg | Human sockets (Katanec 2026) | Null (histologic) |

Katanec 2026's interpretation: simple admixture gives no controlled release — the drug likely washes out or adsorbs to the graft surface, and the dense cerabone® pore architecture limits diffusion to osteogenic cells.

## Key Tensions

### 1. Uniformly positive animal SR vs a negative human pilot

Kommuri 2025 reports 9/9 positive animal studies; Katanec 2026 finds no histomorphometric benefit in humans. Candidate explanations from the held papers:

- **Bias in the animal base**: allocation concealment HIGH risk in 9/9 and detection blinding HIGH in 8/9 (Kommuri 2025). A uniformly positive literature with this bias profile should be read as an upper bound.
- **Model mismatch**: young growing rats (4–8 weeks) in most animal studies (Kommuri 2025); tibia ≠ alveolar bone and supratherapeutic doses (Tahamtan 2020).
- **Delivery vehicle**: the positive animal studies used PLGA, hydrogel microspheres, or HA carriers; Katanec used saline admixture with xenograft.
- **Power**: n=9; NB/TV 95% CI −8.7 to +18.4 — compatible with no effect, a small benefit, or a small harm. The point estimate actually favored the control (31.0% vs 26.2%).

Katanec 2026 is not isolated: Tahamtan 2020 reports that two earlier human ridge-preservation RCTs showed only non-significant trends, and a PLGA/HA/simvastatin construct failed to form bone at 3 months. The human pattern is mixed-to-null, not a single outlier.

### 2. Degala 2018 (10 mg, positive) vs Katanec 2026 (1 mg, negative)

Dose is the most visible difference (10×), but it is confounded with nearly everything else:

| Factor | Degala 2018 | Katanec 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Simvastatin dose | 10 mg | 1 mg |
| Carrier | Gelfoam (no graft) | Saline admixture into cerabone® plus + collagen membrane |
| Site / indication | Impacted mandibular third molars, age 18–40 | Bilateral non-adjacent extractions for socket preservation, mean age 56 (29–79) |
| Outcome measure | Gray-level radiographic density (periapical), CBCT in ~1/5 | 3-month trephine biopsy histomorphometry |
| n | 30 | 9 |
| Full text held | No (abstract-only) | Yes |

Two cautions:
- **Absolute dose alone does not explain it**: Wu 2008 achieved positive ridge preservation in rats with simvastatin **1 mg** in PLGA (Kommuri 2025) — the same absolute dose Katanec used in a far larger human socket. This points to *delivery and local concentration over time* rather than milligrams alone.
- **Outcome measures differ in kind**: Degala measured radiographic gray-level in an ungrafted socket; Katanec measured new-bone fraction in biopsy tissue within a graft. Neither study's endpoint was implant outcome, and Degala did not assess implant-site suitability.

### 3. Preclinical strength vs human translation gap

All three reviews converge on the same verdict: mechanistic plausibility is established, clinical translation is not (Rallis 2025, Tahamtan 2020, Sun 2023). Specifically:
- No human RCT of statins for implant osseointegration exists in the held literature (Tahamtan 2020, Rallis 2025).
- No dental-implant–specific clinical trial in hyperlipidemic patients exists (Sun 2023).
- Optimal statin type, dose, and delivery remain undefined (Tahamtan 2020, Kommuri 2025, Katanec 2026).

### 4. Axis A's own clinical signal is split

The OR 5.1 for hypercholesterolemia (P = 0.046, just under threshold) is countered by a retrospective cohort showing no effect (Sun 2023). Hyperlipidemia should be treated as a plausible risk modifier, not an established independent failure predictor.

## Clinical Decision Points

### CDP #1 — Patient with hyperlipidemia: should this modify implant planning?

- **Not a contraindication.** No held paper supports withholding implants for hyperlipidemia.
- **Treat as a risk modifier.** Mechanistic evidence for impaired OI is consistent (Sun 2023, Rallis 2025); clinical evidence is split (OR 5.1 vs null cohort).
- **Author-recommended conservative measures** (Sun 2023 — expert opinion in a narrative review, not trial-tested): pre-surgical lipid normalization; high-compression insertion with bicortical anchorage where feasible; post-surgical antibiotic prophylaxis; delayed second-stage surgery; extended healing before loading.
- **Statin users**: an ongoing systemic statin is not a reason for concern on the held evidence; observational data elsewhere in the wiki point toward lower, not higher, failure (see [[overviews/drug-systemic-disease-dental-management-overview]]). Do not start or change a systemic statin for implant purposes — systemic dosing is pharmacologically inefficient for bone (Tahamtan 2020) and outside dental scope.
- **Stratify the patient**: obesity and broader metabolic disease confound the hyperlipidemia signal (Rallis 2025); assess them together.

### CDP #2 — Socket preservation: should local simvastatin be used?

- **Routine use: no.** The only human histomorphometric RCT is null (Katanec 2026), earlier human ridge-preservation RCTs were non-significant (Tahamtan 2020), and the single positive RCT is abstract-only with a radiographic surrogate endpoint (Degala 2018).
- **Specifically, do not admix simvastatin into a slowly resorbing xenograft and expect benefit** — this exact protocol was safe but histologically neutral at 3 months (Katanec 2026).
- **Safety is reassuring**: no adverse events or graft complications in Katanec 2026; Degala 2018 reported none in the abstract.
- **Where it may be reasonable** (clinician judgment, with consent framed as off-label/experimental): ungrafted socket healing where a low-cost adjunct is acceptable and radiographic acceleration is the goal, mirroring Degala 2018's design. Expected benefit is uncertain.
- **MRONJ-risk extractions**: fluvastatin reduced necrotic bone exposure in two rat MRONJ models (Kommuri 2025). This is a hypothesis for future trials, not a clinical protocol — no held human data.

### CDP #3 — Which statin type, dose, and delivery?

- **Route**: local, not systemic (Tahamtan 2020, Sun 2023).
- **Agent**: simvastatin has the most dental bone data (both human RCTs, 5/9 animal socket studies). Fluvastatin has animal socket and MRONJ data; rosuvastatin leads in periodontal pocket delivery. No held head-to-head comparison exists for sockets or implants.
- **Dose**: held human doses are 1 mg (null, with xenograft) and 10 mg (positive, with Gelfoam); animal socket doses span simvastatin 0.01–2.2 mg (Kommuri 2025). No dose–response study exists, and higher local concentrations can suppress cell proliferation (Tahamtan 2020).
- **Delivery**: this is the most likely determinant. The positive preclinical work used controlled-release carriers (PLGA, hydrogel microspheres, HA); Katanec 2026 explicitly calls for hydrogels, polymeric microspheres, or electrospun membranes before clinical adoption. Implant-surface statin coatings remain preclinical, with low loading efficiency (Sun 2023) and one negative coating study (Tahamtan 2020).
- **Bottom line**: no evidence-based protocol exists (Rallis 2025). Any use today is experimental.
