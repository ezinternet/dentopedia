## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 위키 내 27편 종합(리뷰·SR/MA 4 + 동물 in vivo 8 + 사람 임상 3 + in vitro 11 + 안전성 1) — 2026-08-07 갱신으로 동물 2편(Cho·Berger) 추가.
> - 핵심 명제: 저온/비열 플라즈마(Cold Atmospheric Plasma, CAP)는 새 임플란트 종류가 아니라 체어사이드 표면 재활성화 도구다.
> - 작동 기전: 티타늄 표면은 생물학적으로 노화(탄화수소 축적·습윤성 저하)하는데, 플라즈마가 이를 역전 — 탄소↓·친수성/표면에너지↑(형태·조성은 불변); 동일한 반응성 산소/질소종이 세균도 죽여 제염이라는 2차 근거 제공.
> - 두 역할: ① 골유착 촉진 ② 표면 제염(치주염 peri-implantitis).
> - 사람 근거(신규): 작지만 부분적으로 유의한 초기 안정성 이득 — Stacchi RCT는 60/90일에만 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ) 유의(절대차 작음), Kwon RCT는 안정성 음성(변연골 변화만 양성), Kim 코호트는 대조군 없음.
> - 전 근거단계 공통 패턴: 최종치를 올리기보다 초기 치유를 앞당김 — 무처리 대조군이 8주경 따라잡는다(Jang·Danna). 더 높은 최종 plateau는 미입증.
> - 용량 비단조성: 60초는 좋으나 300–600초는 오히려 세균 부착 증가(Lee); 항균효과는 ~24h 지속·72h엔 소실(Yang) → 짧은 프로토콜 + 처리 직후 즉시 식립.
> - 정량 MA는 플라즈마가 아니라 UV에 기반(Pesce, I²>75%) — 플라즈마 특이 통합 효과크기는 아직 없음.
> - 한계: 보철 적용은 노화(thermocycling) 후 이득 소실(Kim-2021); 표면 의존적(Danna: textured Ti엔 도움, CaP엔 무효); 장기/하중/생존 데이터 전무(최장 추적 90일).
> - 임상 frame: 노화 fixture의 식립 직전 재활성화·연조직(D3/D4)·하악·짧은 fixture에서 가장 타당; SLActive 대비 비열등(Long, 동물)이나 우월성은 아님; "더 오래 처리 = 더 좋음"은 틀림(과처리 역효과).
> - **근거 갱신(2026-08)**: 플라즈마 단독의 효과가 두 가지 조건에 의존한다는 것이 드러남 — ① **코팅 담체(Coating Carrier) 역할**: Cho 2025(비글)는 진공 플라즈마 단독은 Kahm 2024처럼 비유의였으나, **저용량 rhBMP-2와 병용**했을 때만 BIC 83.91%·나사산간골밀도(ITBD) 81.32%로 유의 향상 — 플라즈마의 진짜 가치는 "치료" 자체보다 생체활성 코팅의 표면 결합을 돕는 **담체(carrier)** 기능일 수 있음. ② **숙주 성별(Sex) 의존성**: Berger 2022(토끼, DBD 30초)는 **수컷에서만** BIC·골량·토크 유의 향상, 암컷은 기저 골유착이 이미 높아 플라즈마 효과가 가려짐(masked) — 동물모델의 "효과 있음/없음" 혼재 결과 일부가 **성별 층화 여부**로 설명될 수 있음을 시사.
> - **근거 갱신(2026-08) — 오염의 화학적 정체**: Wagstaffe 2019(XPS/STM, UV 자매 오버뷰와 공유하는 기전 논문)가 처음으로 "탄화수소"의 실체를 특정 — 기름·유분이 아니라 **폼산·아세트산 같은 소형 유기산**이 카르복실기-양이온 브리징 결합으로 화학흡착된 부착탄소오염 (Adventitious Carbon)층. 플라즈마의 반응성 산소/질소종이 실제로 끊는 대상이 바로 이 카르복실기-Ti 결합이라는 뜻 — "탄화수소 제거"라는 뭉뚱그린 표현이 이제 구체적 화학반응으로 특정됨. SLActive는 이 층이 SLA보다 얇아 RGD 펩타이드 흡수량이 전 pH에서 더 높음 — 흡착을 결정하는 건 표면친수성 자체가 아니라 이 층의 **치환 용이성**.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 27 wiki papers (4 reviews/SR/MA, 8 animal in vivo, 3 human clinical, 11 in vitro, 1 mouse safety) on cold/non-thermal atmospheric plasma (CAP) for dental implants: CAP reverses biological aging of titanium surfaces (carbon ↓, wettability/surface energy ↑, morphology/composition unchanged), serving two jobs — osseointegration promotion and surface decontamination.

Human evidence (2 RCTs + 1 cohort) shows a small, partly significant *early* stability benefit (Stacchi RCT: ISQ significant only at 60/90 days; Kwon RCT: stability NS, marginal bone only); the universal pattern across every evidence tier is early healing acceleration that narrows by 8 weeks as controls catch up — not a higher final endpoint; dose is non-monotonic (60 s optimal, 300–600 s backfires), antimicrobial effect decays by 72 h, prosthetic benefit does not survive thermocycling aging, and no long-term/survival data exist (longest human follow-up 90 days).

Plasma is most rationale as a chairside pre-placement re-activation tool for aged fixtures or in soft-bone/short-implant/mandibular scenarios, or as a coating-carrier enhancer for bioactive agents like rhBMP-2 (Cho 2025); its effect appears to depend on host bone-forming capacity (Berger 2022: significant only in males) and "treat longer = better" is incorrect and must be avoided.

## 세줄요약

위키 27편(리뷰·SR/MA 4 + 동물 8 + 사람 임상 3 + in vitro 11 + 안전성 1) 종합: 저온/비열 대기압 플라즈마(Cold Atmospheric Plasma, CAP)는 티타늄 표면 생물학적 노화를 역전(탄소↓·친수성/표면에너지↑·형태/조성 불변)하여 ① 골유착 촉진 ② 제염 두 역할을 수행.

사람 근거(RCT 2 + 코호트 1)는 작고 부분적으로 유의한 *초기* 안정성 이득(Stacchi RCT: 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ) 60/90일에만 유의; Kwon RCT: 안정성 음성); 전 근거계층 공통 패턴은 초기 치유 가속이나 8주 무렵 대조군이 따라잡음 — 최종치 상승 미입증; 용량 비단조성(60초 최적, 300–600초 역효과), 항균효과 72h 내 소실, 보철 이득은 열순환 노화 후 소실, 장기/생존 데이터 전무(최장 추적 90일).

체어사이드 식립 직전 노화 fixture 재활성화·연조직(D3/D4)·하악·단폭 fixture에서 가장 합리적, 또는 rhBMP-2 같은 생체활성 코팅의 담체(Cho 2025); 효과는 숙주 골형성능에 의존(Berger 2022: 수컷에서만 유의); "오래 처리할수록 좋다"는 틀림.

## Thesis

Plasma is a chairside surface re-activation tool, not a new implant class. Titanium surfaces age biologically (hydrocarbon accumulation, falling wettability); plasma reverses that — carbon down, hydrophilicity/surface energy up, morphology and elemental composition unchanged — which improves early cell response and, in animals, early bone-to-implant contact. The same reactive oxygen/nitrogen species kill bacteria, giving a second rationale (decontamination / peri-implantitis). The 2nd-batch human studies upgrade the evidence from "pre-clinical only" to "early human signal present, but small": the controlled RCT (Stacchi) reached significance only at 60/90 days with a limited absolute ISQ gap, the other RCT (Kwon) was negative on stability, and the cohort (Kim) lacks a control. The honest through-line — visible in rat, dog, and human alike — is that plasma buys *speed* in the early healing window; the untreated control tends to catch up, and a higher final plateau is not demonstrated.

**2026-08 addition — plasma-alone efficacy is conditional.** Two new animal studies sharpen *when* plasma alone works and when it doesn't. Cho 2025 (beagle) found plasma alone statistically indistinguishable from rhBMP-2 alone — consistent with Kahm's earlier null — but the *combination* (plasma + low-dose rhBMP-2) reached significance on BIC and inter-thread bone density. This reframes plasma's most defensible mechanism as surface-energy-mediated *coating retention/uniformity*, not necessarily a standalone osteogenic stimulus. Berger 2022 (rabbit) found DBD plasma significantly improved BIC and mechanical anchorage only in males; female rabbits' baseline osseointegration was already high enough that plasma's local wettability effect was masked by a more osteogenic systemic (estrogen-driven) environment. Read together, these two papers suggest the field's mixed positive/null animal results may partly reflect **unmeasured co-factors** — whether a bioactive coating is co-applied, and the host's baseline bone-forming capacity — rather than plasma protocol differences alone.

**2026-08 addition — what the "hydrocarbon" plasma removes chemically *is*.** This wiki cluster (and its sibling UV-photofunctionalization cluster) had established only that hydrocarbon accumulates and that plasma/UV removes it, without specifying the chemistry. Wagstaffe 2019 (XPS/STM, a general Ti-surface-chemistry paper shared with the UV overview) resolves this: the adventitious contamination on air-exposed titania is predominantly small organic acids (e.g. formic, acetic acid) that chemisorb via deprotonated carboxylate groups bridging to under-coordinated surface Ti cations — not a generic oil or grease film. Plasma's reactive oxygen/nitrogen species most plausibly act by dissociating this specific carboxylate-titanium bond, giving the "carbon ↓" surface-physics finding (Zheng, Lee-H, Ho, Wagner, Zeng, Jeon, Yang above) a molecular mechanism rather than a black-box description. The same paper shows SLActive (etched/stored to minimize this layer) adsorbs substantially more RGD peptide than SLA at every pH tested — direct surface-chemistry evidence that it is the *displaceability* of this organic-acid layer, not surface hydrophilicity per se, that governs biomolecule uptake. This has a practical corollary already visible in the sibling UV cluster (Houshmand 2023): a contact-angle improvement alone does not prove the layer was actually removed — one UV wavelength (UVB) improved wettability without significantly reducing surface carbon/nitrogen on the same disk batch that a shorter wavelength (UVC) did clear. Plasma protocols validated mainly by contact-angle or qualitative wettability change (rather than XPS carbon quantification) should be read with the same caution.

## Where the evidence converges (direction)

- **Surface physics.** Carbon ↓ and wettability/surface energy ↑ without topography/composition change — Zheng (contact angle ↓~100%, carbon ↓~80%), Lee-H (DBD, carbon ↓~60%), Ho (carbon p<0.001, hydrophilicity p<0.0001), Wagner (composition unchanged), Zeng (nanostructure preserved), Jeon, Yang (O/C↑).
- **The "carbon" being removed is chemically small organic acids, not oil (2026-08).** Wagstaffe 2019 (XPS/STM) identifies the adventitious layer as mainly formic/acetic-acid-type carboxylates chemisorbed via cation bridging to the oxidized Ti surface — the specific bond reactive plasma species most plausibly dissociate; SLActive's thinner version of this layer (vs SLA) tracks with higher RGD peptide uptake at every pH, independently supporting that displaceability of this layer — not hydrophilicity alone — governs biomolecule adsorption.
- **Cells.** ↑ early osteoblast and fibroblast adhesion/proliferation/differentiation in vitro — Wagner, Zheng, Zeng, Hadzik, Ho (with Apln/Crabp2 upregulation), Lee-H, Jeon.
- **Bone (animal).** ↑ early BIC/bone volume — rat (Zheng, BIC ↑25–40%, osteoclasts ↓~50%), pig (Naujokat 90.4 vs 86.5%), foxhound (Nevins, BIC sig at 4 wk), dog plasma-jet (Jang, BV/BIC sig at 4 wk), beagle (Danna, textured-Ti BIC sig at 6 wk), beagle vs SLActive (Long, comparable to commercial hydrophilic); rabbit males only (Berger 2022, DBD 30s, BIC/torque sig); beagle plasma+rhBMP-2 combination (Cho 2025, BIC 83.91% sig vs plasma-alone/BMP-alone).
- **Human (new).** Early stability signal — Stacchi RCT (ISQ sig higher at 60/90 d), Kim cohort (ISQ 78.97→83.74 at 8 wk, no dip); Kwon RCT (greater marginal bone change, stability NS).
- **Antimicrobial.** Broad in-vitro pathogen/biofilm reduction — Jungbauer SR (55 studies, time-dependent), Alqutaibi SR (15 studies, implant surfaces), Lee (zirconia), Zeng (S. aureus), Yang (S. mutans).

## Where the evidence draws boundaries (magnitude / conditions)

- **Benefit is early and transient, not a higher endpoint.** Jang and Danna: control catches up by 8 wk / advantage only at one timepoint. Nevins: significance timepoint-specific. Ho: cell-attachment difference gone by 12 h.
- **Human effect is small and mixed.** Stacchi: significant only at 60/90 d, absolute ISQ gap limited. Kwon: stability/defect NS (only marginal-bone-change positive). Kim: uncontrolled single-arm.
- **Effect can be non-significant.** Naujokat ("slight"); Kahm (rabbit vacuum plasma — BA/bone volume/ISQ higher but NS); Cho (plasma-alone group statistically indistinguishable from rhBMP-2-alone — a second independent plasma-alone null).
- **Effect is conditional on co-factors, not just protocol.** Cho 2025: plasma alone is null, but plasma **as a coating carrier** for low-dose rhBMP-2 reaches significance on BIC/ITBD — the mechanism may be improved bioactive-agent surface retention (lower burst release, ELISA) rather than a standalone osteogenic signal. Berger 2022: DBD plasma significantly improves BIC/mechanical anchorage in **male** rabbits only; female rabbits' already-high baseline osseointegration masks the local wettability effect — a host-sex/bone-forming-capacity moderator not previously captured in this synthesis.
- **Quantitative MA rests on UV, not plasma.** Pesce pooled 4 UV-rabbit studies (plasma stayed qualitative), I²>75%.
- **Wettability alone does not prove decontamination (cross-technology caution, 2026-08).** In the sibling UV cluster, Houshmand 2023 (EDS, same SLA batch) found UVB significantly improved contact angle (129.9°→58.9°) without significantly reducing surface carbon/nitrogen versus control (p=0.171/0.785), while UVC did both. No plasma paper in this cluster has run the equivalent head-to-head (contact-angle-positive vs XPS-carbon-negative) check — protocols validated only by qualitative wettability change should be read with the same caution until one does.
- **Dose is non-monotonic / time-decaying.** Lee: 60 s good, 300–600 s *increases* bacterial adhesion. Yang: antibacterial effect lasts ~24 h, gone by 72 h. → short protocol, placement promptly after treatment.
- **Prosthetic benefit does not survive aging.** Kim-2021: NTP zirconia retention gain lost after thermocycling; NTP+silane/primer worse after aging.
- **Surface-dependent.** Danna: APP helped textured Ti but not calcium-phosphate surfaces.
- **No long-term/survival data.** Longest human follow-up is 90 days (Stacchi); no loaded/MBL/survival endpoints.

## Clinical decision frame

| Scenario | Plasma rationale | Evidence basis | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chairside re-activation immediately before placement (aged fixture) | Reasonable | Stacchi RCT, Kim cohort; animal (Nevins/Jang/Naujokat/Zheng) | small human effect; place promptly after treatment |
| Soft (D3/D4) / low-initial-stability sites where early stability matters | Most plausible | Kim subgroup (low-ISQ gains most); animal BIC↑ | short-term ISQ only |
| Mandible, shorter fixtures, higher insertion torque | Greater relative gain | Kim subgroup | uncontrolled cohort |
| Faster osseointegration / earlier loading ambition | Speed only, not higher final | Jang/Danna convergence; Stacchi late-only | no loading/survival data |
| Decontaminating contaminated/peri-implantitis surface | Plausible adjunct | Alqutaibi, Jungbauer, Lee, Zeng, Yang | in-vitro; short protocol; effect time-decays |
| Plasma as cheaper alternative to SLActive | Defensible (non-inferiority) | Long (beagle, comparable) | animal; not superiority |
| Transgingival/soft-tissue collar | Emerging | Hadzik (fibroblast↑) | preliminary in vitro |
| Zirconia crown cement retention | Not supported after aging | Kim-2021 | benefit lost on thermocycling; +primer worse |
| Longer treatment = better | No | Lee (≥300 s↑ adhesion), Yang (24 h decay) | over-treatment backfires |
| Mucosal safety of chairside CAP | Screened pre-clinically | Jablonowski (mouse) | device/dose-specific; short-term |
| **Plasma as bioactive-coating carrier (2026-08)** | Most defensible use of plasma-alone protocols so far | Cho 2025 (beagle, plasma+rhBMP-2 sig, plasma-alone NS) | animal only; single low rhBMP-2 dose; ISQ did not detect the BIC gain |
| **Host sex / baseline bone-forming capacity (2026-08)** | Effect modifier, not yet actionable clinically | Berger 2022 (rabbit, males sig, females NS) | animal model only; human sex-stratified data absent |

## Open questions / verification needed

- Larger, longer human RCTs with loading, MBL, and survival endpoints (current max follow-up 90 d).
- Reconcile the mixed human stability result (Stacchi positive-late vs Kwon negative).
- Plasma-specific (not UV) pooled effect size for osseointegration.
- Standardized device/gas/time protocol and a defined treatment→placement window (given 24-h antimicrobial decay).
- Clinical peri-implantitis decontamination outcomes (vs laser / air-abrasion / CHX).
- **(2026-08)** Test the plasma-as-coating-carrier hypothesis (Cho 2025) with other bioactive agents and in human trials — is plasma's main value as a reactivation tool or as a delivery enhancer?
- **(2026-08)** Sex/host bone-quality stratification in future human plasma RCTs — none of the 3 existing human studies (Stacchi, Kwon, Kim) report sex-stratified results, so Berger's animal-model sex effect is currently untestable in the human evidence tier.

## Sibling overview — UV photofunctionalization

UV photofunctionalization is the **sibling re-activation technology** to cold plasma: same biological-aging premise (hydrocarbon removal → superhydrophilic, high-energy surface; topography unchanged), but a separate and now larger clinical base. The quantitative MA referenced above (Pesce 2020) is in fact UV-driven; UV-specific clinical evidence has since strengthened — a photofunctionalization-specific SR+MA (Lang 2022: OSI MD=2.13, p=0.007; 2-month ISQ MD=3.48, p=0.04), a split-mouth RCT in controlled diabetics (Krishna 2025: higher 3-month ISQ/OSI, less 9-month crestal bone loss), a fractal-analysis split-mouth RCT (Farsiani 2025: peri-implant trabecular FD ↑), and a 2026 systematic review reframing UV as a *surface-agnostic, positive-sum* strategy (Komatsu 2026). For the consolidated UV evidence see **[[overviews/uv-photofunctionalization-dental-implants]]** (15 papers). The plasma vs UV through-line is identical: both buy early-healing *speed*, neither has demonstrated a higher final endpoint or long-term loaded-survival advantage, and (2026-08) both target the same chemically-identified organic-acid contamination layer (Wagstaffe 2019) — with the UV cluster's Houshmand 2023 finding (wettability ≠ decontamination proof) applying as a methodological caution to plasma protocol validation as well.
