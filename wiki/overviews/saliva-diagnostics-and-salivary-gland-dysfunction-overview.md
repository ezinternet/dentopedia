> - **핵심 논지**: 침(saliva)은 임상적으로 두 얼굴을 가진다 — ① **내용물(content)**을 읽는 *진단 매체*(무엇이 들어있나)와 ② **유량(flow)**이 고장 나는 *분비 기능*(얼마나 나오나). 이 종합은 두 축을 하나의 지도로 묶는다.
> - **축 A — 침 진단(saliva diagnostics)**: 혈장 단백질체의 **20~30%가 침과 중첩**되어 침은 전신 상태의 '거울'. 3-pillar = 살리바오믹스(salivaomics)·침 엑소좀학(exosomics)·침 액체생검(liquid biopsy) — [[nonaka-2023-saliva-diagnostics-salivaomics-exosomics-liquid-biopsy]].
> - **검출 기술**: EFIRM 전기화학 센서가 추출·증폭 없이(~30분, 40~50 µL) 폐암 EGFR ctDNA와 SARS-CoV-2를 침에서 검출; 바이오센서·마이크로플루이딕·**웨어러블 구강기기**로 point-of-care 이동 — [[li-2024-salivary-diagnostics-opportunities-challenges]].
> - **암 바이오마커 수치**: 침 MMP-9가 구강편평세포암(OSCC) 수술 후 **588→132 ng/mL** 감소, 전기화학 PSA 센서 민감도 **40 fg/mL**, MMP-1 컷오프 ~199 pg/mL.
> - **축 B — 침샘 기능 기전**: 침 분비 = 부교감 신경전달물질 → **GPCR(M3 무스카린수용체) → IP3R → SOCE → 아쿠아포린5(AQP5)** Ca²⁺ 신호축; AQP5-KO 시 분비 60%↓ — [[kim-2023-xerostomia-cellular-targets]].
> - **구강건조 역학**: 지속성 xerostomia 유병률 **10~50%**(여성 ~30%, 고령 ~50%), 원인 1위는 **약물성**(>400종). FDA 승인 완치약은 없음 — 필로카르핀/세비멜린(sialagogue)은 대증적.
> - **약물 축의 신규 위험**: **세마글루타이드(Semaglutide/Ozempic, GLP-1 작용제)**가 hyposalivation을 일으킨 첫 보고(3례) — 수정 Schirmer 평균 9 mL/3분, 중단 시 가역 — [[mawardi-2023-semaglutide-associated-hyposalivation-case-series]]. 체중감량 처방 급증 → 진료 시 **GLP-1 복용력 문진** 필요.
> - **감별의 축**: 구강건조 호소 시 약물성(항우울·항고혈압·GLP-1) vs 쇼그렌(Sjögren) vs 방사선 vs 노화를 나눠야 함 — 쇼그렌은 항-SSA/Ro 등 자가항체 패널로 배제([[shiboski-2016-acr-eular-sjogren-classification-criteria]]), 항고혈압제는 유량 감소 근거([[ramirez-martinez-acitores-2020-antihypertensive-xerostomia-salivary-flow-sr]]).
> - **두 축의 연결점**: 침샘 기능(유량)이 무너지면 진단 매체(내용)의 질도 떨어진다 — hyposalivation은 시료 표준화를 방해. 표준화(수집·보존·기준치 부재)가 침 진단 임상화의 최대 장벽.
> - **임상 takeaway**: (1) 구강건조 = 약물력부터 확인(특히 다약제·GLP-1), (2) 필요 시 쇼그렌 패널로 감별, (3) 관리는 원인약 조정·sialagogue·타액대체제(항균성 미흡, ≤4h)로 대증, (4) 침 진단은 유망하나 표준화 전까지 보조적.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-paper synthesis of 10 papers framing saliva along two clinical axes — (A) content: saliva as a non-invasive diagnostic medium (salivaomics, exosomics, EFIRM liquid biopsy, OSCC/PSA/diabetes biomarkers, wearable sensors) and (B) flow: salivary-gland secretory function and its failure in xerostomia/hyposalivation via the GPCR→IP3R→SOCE→AQP5 calcium-signaling cascade.

Salivary MMP-9 drops 588→132 ng/mL after OSCC surgery; EFIRM electrochemistry detects EGFR ctDNA and SARS-CoV-2 in ~30 min on 40–50 µL; persistent xerostomia affects 10–50% of the population with no FDA-approved permanent cure; semaglutide (GLP-1 agonist) is a newly reported hyposalivation cause (3 cases, reversible); and 20–30% salivary–plasma proteome overlap positions saliva as a systemic health mirror.

The axes are coupled: reduced flow degrades the diagnostic medium — hyposalivation disrupts sample standardization, which remains the central barrier to clinical translation of salivary diagnostics; differential diagnosis must separate drug-induced (>400 agents, now GLP-1 agonists) from Sjögren's (ACR-EULAR anti-SSA/Ro panel) from radiation and aging, with management limited to cause modification and sialagogues.

## 세줄요약

10편 종합 — 침을 두 임상 축으로 정리: ① 내용물 축(살리바오믹스·엑소좀·EFIRM 액체생검, OSCC/PSA/당뇨 바이오마커, 웨어러블 구강센서), ② 유량 축(GPCR→IP3R→SOCE→아쿠아포린5(AQP5) Ca²⁺ 신호축과 그 고장인 구강건조증/타액저하).

침 MMP-9는 구강편평세포암(OSCC) 수술 후 588→132 ng/mL 감소; EFIRM 전기화학 센서는 40–50 µL에서 ~30분 내 EGFR ctDNA 검출; 지속성 구강건조 유병률 10–50%이며 완치약 없음; 세마글루타이드(Semaglutide, GLP-1 작용제)가 타액저하 신규 원인으로 보고됨(3례, 중단 시 가역).

두 축은 기계적으로 연결 — 유량 감소는 진단 매체의 질을 저하시켜 표준화를 방해하며(임상화 최대 장벽), 감별진단은 약물성(>400종, 이제 GLP-1 포함) vs 쇼그렌(Sjögren, 항-SSA/Ro 패널) vs 방사선·노화를 나눠야 하고 관리는 원인 교정·사이알로그(sialagogue)·타액대체제로 대증.

## Summary

Saliva occupies two distinct but coupled clinical roles. As a **diagnostic medium**, it is a non-invasive "mirror" of systemic health — 20–30% of the salivary proteome overlaps the plasma proteome, and modern salivary diagnostics is organized into salivaomics, saliva exosomics, and saliva liquid biopsy, with electrochemical platforms (EFIRM) and wearable intraoral devices pushing toward point-of-care detection of cancer ctDNA and viral RNA. As a **secretory function**, saliva is produced by polarized acinar cells through a tightly ordered GPCR → IP3R → SOCE → AQP5 calcium-signaling cascade; when that flow fails — most often through xerogenic drugs (>400 agents, now including the GLP-1 agonist semaglutide), Sjögren's syndrome, head-and-neck radiation, or aging — the result is xerostomia/hyposalivation affecting 10–50% of the population with no permanent cure. The two axes are mechanistically linked: reduced flow degrades the very medium diagnostics depend on, and standardization of collection remains the central obstacle to clinical translation. This overview maps the four newly ingested saliva papers against the wiki's existing xerostomia, Sjögren, salivary-marker, and COVID-saliva pages to give a single decision-oriented reference.

## The Two Axes

### Axis A — Saliva as a diagnostic medium (content)

- **Why saliva works**: salivary glands secrete plasma-derived fluid, so saliva reflects systemic state; 20–30% proteome overlap with plasma ([[nonaka-2023-saliva-diagnostics-salivaomics-exosomics-liquid-biopsy]]).
- **Three pillars**: salivaomics (DNA/RNA/protein/metabolite/microbiome), saliva exosomics (30–100 nm exosome cargo protected by a lipid bilayer), saliva liquid biopsy (ctDNA/CTC/EV).
- **Detection engineering**: EFIRM electrochemical assay (<100 molecules, ~30 min, 40–50 µL, CLIA-certified for EGFR mutations in plasma and saliva and for SARS-CoV-2 IgG); trend toward biosensors, microfluidics, and wearable intraoral sensors ([[li-2024-salivary-diagnostics-opportunities-challenges]]).
- **Representative biomarker numbers**: MMP-9 falls 588→132 ng/mL after OSCC surgery; MMP-1 cutoff ~199 pg/mL; electrochemical PSA sensitivity 40 fg/mL; HPV ctDNA for HNSCC; salivary BRCA1/2.
- **Precedent in wiki**: saliva already appears as a *disease readout* in COVID-19 oral sequelae ([[tsuchiya-2023-covid-19-oral-sequelae-gustatory-saliva]]) and as diabetes markers linked to HbA1c ([[maftei-2021-salivary-markers-hba1c-type2-diabetes-extraction]]).
- **Periodontitis application (untargeted proteomics)**: an SR of 13 discovery-proteomics studies finds a reproducible salivary signature — complement C3, profilin-1, S100A8, fibrinogen ↑ and cystatin-SN, leukocyte elastase inhibitor ↑ in health — yet only S100A8 is meta-analyzable (AUC 0.71) and multi-protein ML panels reaching AUC ~0.97 lack external validation; whole saliva trades GCF's site-specificity for whole-mouth convenience ([[corana-2024-salivary-proteomics-novel-biomarkers-periodontitis-sr]]).

### Axis B — Salivary-gland flow and its failure (function)

- **Secretory mechanism**: parasympathetic ACh → M3 muscarinic GPCR → PLC/IP3 → ER Ca²⁺ release (IP3R2/3) → store-operated Ca²⁺ entry (STIM1 + Orai1/TRPC1) → K⁺/Cl⁻ channels + AQP5 apical trafficking → water secretion. Knockouts abolish or halve salivation (M1/M3 double-KO lost; AQP5-KO −60%) ([[kim-2023-xerostomia-cellular-targets]]).
- **Epidemiology & causes**: persistent xerostomia 10–50% (women ≤30%, elderly ≤50%); leading cause is drugs (>400 xerogenic agents), then Sjögren's, radiation, aging; no permanent FDA-approved cure — sialagogues (pilocarpine, cevimeline) are palliative with systemic side effects.
- **New drug entrant**: semaglutide (GLP-1 receptor agonist) — first reported hyposalivation (3 cases, mean modified Schirmer 9 mL/3 min, reversible on discontinuation or managed with pilocarpine) ([[mawardi-2023-semaglutide-associated-hyposalivation-case-series]]).
- **Clinical outcomes & modifiers in wiki**: xerostomia dental-treatment outcomes ([[poudel-2026-xerostomia-dental-treatment-outcomes-sr]]); antihypertensive drugs reduce salivary flow ([[ramirez-martinez-acitores-2020-antihypertensive-xerostomia-salivary-flow-sr]]); Sjögren chemosensory/oral disorders ([[sijan-gobeljic-2020-sjogren-chemosensory-oral-disorders-cross-sectional]]).

## Clinical Decision Path — the patient complaining of dry mouth

1. **Medication history first** — antidepressants, antipsychotics, antihistamines, antihypertensives, and now **GLP-1 agonists (semaglutide/Ozempic/Wegovy)**; risk rises with total drug count (polypharmacy). Onset of semaglutide hyposalivation ~4 weeks.
2. **Quantify** — unstimulated flow (modified Schirmer / sialometry; reference unstimulated 0.3–0.4 mL/min, stimulated 1.5–2 mL/min) to separate subjective xerostomia from objective hyposalivation.
3. **Exclude Sjögren's** — anti-SSA/Ro, anti-SSB/La, RF, ANA + labial-gland biopsy/focus score; 2016 ACR-EULAR criteria ([[shiboski-2016-acr-eular-sjogren-classification-criteria]]).
4. **Exclude radiation / aging / systemic** — head-and-neck radiation history; diabetes (HbA1c).
5. **Manage** — adjust/withdraw the causative drug where possible (reversible), sialagogues for retained gland function, saliva substitutes/rinses for symptomatic relief (poor antibacterial properties, ≤4 h), and caries/candidiasis prophylaxis for chronic hyposalivation.

## Open Questions / Gaps

- **Standardization** is the shared bottleneck for Axis A — collection, preservation, and reference ranges are unstandardized across labs; wiki has no dedicated methodology page yet.
- **Class-effect of GLP-1 agonists**: only semaglutide is reported; whether liraglutide/dulaglutide/exenatide share the hyposalivation signal is unknown (n=3, hypothesis-generating).
- **Regenerative rescue** of irradiated/aged glands (acinar-specific GPCR agonists, organoids) is preclinical — no clinical page held.
- **Bridging axes**: no held paper quantifies how hyposalivation degrades salivary biomarker assay performance — a concrete link between the two axes worth watching.
