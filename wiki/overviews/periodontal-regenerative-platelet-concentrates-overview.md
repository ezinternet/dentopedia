## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 결론: 자가혈소판농축물(Autologous Platelet Concentrate, APC — PRF/PRP/CGF)의 치주재생 3대 적응증(치근이개부·골내결손·근면피복) SR/MA·NMA 3편(합계 185 RCT)을 통합 — **같은 Miron/Barootchi 저자 그룹**이 만든 자매 논문으로 결손 유형별 APC 위치를 지도화.
> - 공통 결론: **PRF를 결손 술식(OFD 등)에 추가하면 항상 유의한 개선** — 치근이개부(OFD vs OFD/PRF: PPD 1.73mm, VCAL 1.42mm), 골내결손(OFD vs OFD/PRF: PPD 1.27mm, CAL 1.22mm, 골충전 1.59mm), 근면피복(CAF vs CAF+PRF: 평균근피복 +6.12%, p=0.02) 세 곳 모두에서 일관.
> - 반대로 **PRF를 다른 확립된 재생재료와 head-to-head 비교하면 대부분 무차이(동등)** — 골내결손에서 PRF ≈ EMD ≈ PRP ≈ 차폐막(barrier membrane); 치근이개부에서 PRF ≈ 골이식·콜라겐막·PRP·rhBMP2. 즉 PRF는 "더 나은" 재료가 아니라 "저비용 자가 대체재"로 자리매김.
> - 예외 1: 근면피복에서는 **PRP·CGF는 유의한 이득 없음**(PRP −0.61%, CGF +7.47% 둘 다 NS) — PRF만 유의. 세 결손 유형 중 유일하게 "PRF 특이적" 효과가 확인된 곳.
> - 예외 2: 골내결손 Group III에서 **골이식(BG) > PRF (CAL −0.40mm 열위, p=0.004)** — 공간유지가 중요한 지표에서는 그래도 이식재가 근소 우위.
> - PRF를 "약물 전달 매트릭스"로 쓰는 패턴이 치근이개부·골내결손 두 곳에서 **재현**됨: 메트포르민·비스포스포네이트·스타틴을 PRF에 얹으면 추가 유의 개선(반면 항생제·LLLT는 무효, 골내결손에서 확인).
> - 근면피복만의 독자적 발견: PRF 효과가 **baseline 각화조직폭(Keratinized Tissue Width, KTW)에 비례**(교호작용 +4.98, p=0.03) — KTW 있는 부위일수록 PRF가 잘 듣는다. 반면 어떤 APC도 KTW·치은두께(Gingival Thickness, GT) 자체는 늘리지 못함 — 결합조직이식(Connective Tissue Graft, CTG)의 고유 강점은 대체 안 됨.
> - 근거 등급 차이 주의: 치근이개부·골내결손은 **직접 pairwise MA**(치근이개부 21 RCT, 골내결손 55 RCT)인 반면, 근면피복은 **NMA**(109 RCT, CAF 공통비교군)로 간접비교 포함 — 셋 다 SR+MA급이지만 통계 구조가 다르다.
> - 공통 한계: 세 논문 모두 **이질성이 매우 높음**(I²≈85–94%), PRF 제조 프로토콜(원심분리 rpm·시간·기기) 과소보고(치근이개부 4/21, 골내결손 19/55 미보고), human histology로 진짜 재생(치주인대·백악질 신생) 증명한 논문 전무.
> - 임상 결정 프레임: (1) 결손 술식에 PRF 추가 = 거의 항상 이득, 저비용·저위험이므로 추가를 기본값으로 고려. (2) PRF로 골이식·차폐막·CTG를 "대체"하려는 결정은 결손 유형별로 다르다 — 골내결손은 동등(대체 가능성 있음), 근면피복은 CTG가 여전히 근피복률 우위(단 KTW 충분 시 PRF가 형태학적 morbidity 감소 대안). (3) 흡연자·중증 결손·KTW 결핍 부위는 근거 공백 — 표준 재료 우선.
> - **2026-07-10 업데이트 (아시아저널 PMC 9편 추가)**: 골내결손·근면피복 두 지표에 신선한 단일-RCT가 추가돼 트리오 SR/MA 결론을 재확인 — Parihar 2026(골내결손, DFDBA+PRF PPD/CAL 유의개선)·Corekci 2025(근면피복, DGG+L-PRF는 피복률 동등이나 **통증만** 유의감소 — 이미 이식재가 있으면 PRF의 이득이 "결과"에서 "이환율"로 이동한다는 새로운 뉘앙스).
> - **6번째 적응증 등장 — 임플란트 주위염**: Shetty 2023 SR(12편 중 OFD 직접비교 1편뿐)이 처음으로 다뤘으나 근거는 "promising하나 결정적이지 않음" — 트리오 수준의 확립된 적응증은 아직 아님.
> - **상악동거상술은 이번에도 예외 패턴 유지**: Sivakumar 2022 SR+MA(6 RCT)가 PRP 첨가는 임플란트 생존율에 이득 없음(OR 0.84, NS)을 재확인 — 앞서 실린 Idiri 2023(DBBM+PRF 신생골 무이득)과 함께 "PRF가 항상 돕는다"는 트리오 원칙이 상악동 부위에서는 재현되지 않음을 거듭 보여줌.
> - **발치와/치조제보존**: 신규 RCT 3편(Nainoor 2024·Manlerd 2025·Razi 2024)이 모두 "부분적 이득, split-decision" 패턴 — PRF가 연조직·초기치유는 개선해도 창상폐쇄 면적·경조직 보존까지 항상 이기지는 못함. 기존 al-Maawi 2021 SR의 "6개월이면 이득 소실" 결론과 일관.
> - **GBR 기전 배경지식**: Cho 2026 스코핑리뷰가 "왜 PRF 효과가 부위별로 들쭉날쭉한가"의 세포·분자적 설명을 제공(섬유소 매트릭스 방출동역학·골면역학) — Assiri 2026(염소 모델)의 결손유형별(장골 vs 치조골) 상반된 효과와 함께 읽으면 트리오의 "결손 술식에 추가하면 대개 돕는다"는 원칙이 결손 해부학적 맥락에 크게 의존함을 시사.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 3 sibling SR/MA(NMA) papers from Periodontology 2000 2024 mapping autologous platelet concentrate (APC: PRF/PRP/CGF) evidence across furcation defects (21 RCTs), intrabony defects (55 RCTs), and root coverage (109-RCT NMA).

Consistent finding: adding PRF to a surgical procedure always improves outcomes (furcation OFD+PRF vs OFD: PPD +1.73 mm; intrabony: PPD +1.27 mm, CAL +1.22 mm; root coverage: mean root coverage +6.12%), while PRF vs established regenerative materials is equivalent — making PRF a low-cost autologous substitute, not a superior material.

Root coverage is the one indication where PRF alone (PRP/CGF both NS) shows a unique, baseline-KTW-dependent benefit; furcation and intrabony share a reproducible "PRF as drug-delivery matrix" pattern (metformin/bisphosphonate/statin loading adds further benefit); all three suffer I²≈85–95% heterogeneity and no human histology proving true regeneration.

## 세줄요약

Periodontology 2000 2024년 자매 SR/MA(NMA) 3편 종합 — 자가혈소판농축물(Autologous Platelet Concentrate, APC: PRF/PRP/CGF)을 치근이개부(21 RCT)·골내결손(55 RCT)·근면피복(NMA, 109 RCT) 3대 치주재생 적응증에 걸쳐 지도화.

공통 패턴: PRF를 술식에 추가하면 항상 유의 개선(치근이개부 PPD +1.73mm·골내결손 PPD +1.27mm·근면피복 +6.12%); 확립된 재료와 head-to-head는 대등 — PRF는 저비용 자가 대체재이지 우월한 재료가 아님.

근면피복만 PRF 특이적·기저 각화조직폭(Keratinized Tissue Width, KTW) 의존적 효과(PRP/CGF는 무효); 치근이개부·골내결손에서 PRF를 소분자 약물전달 매트릭스로 쓰는 패턴 재현; 세 논문 모두 I²≈85–95% 이질성·조직학적 진성 재생 증명 전무.

## Summary

Three Periodontology 2000 systematic reviews published in 2024 — all substantially overlapping in authorship (Miron, Moraschini, Barootchi, Wang, and colleagues) and explicitly cross-referencing each other as a "sibling set" — collectively map where **autologous platelet concentrates (APCs: PRF, PRP, CGF)** add value across the three classic periodontal regeneration indications: **class II furcation defects** ([[periodontics/miron-2024-prf-furcation-defects-sr-ma]], 21 RCTs, 786 patients), **intrabony defects** ([[periodontics/miron-2024-autogenous-platelet-concentrates-intrabony-defects]], 55 RCTs — the largest PRF synthesis to date), and **RT1 gingival recession / root coverage** ([[periodontics/barootchi-2024-autologous-platelet-concentrates-root-coverage]], 109-RCT network meta-analysis). Reading them side by side turns three separate indication-specific summaries into a single evidence map of what PRF (and, less consistently, PRP/CGF) can and cannot do in periodontal regenerative surgery.

## What Is Consistent Across All Three Indications

1. **Adding PRF to a surgical/access procedure always helps, and by a similar order of magnitude.** Furcation (PPD 1.73mm), intrabony (PPD 1.27mm), and root coverage (mRC +6.12%) all show PRF significantly outperforming the "bare" procedure. This is the single most reproducible finding in the platelet-concentrate literature and the strongest basis for recommending PRF as a low-cost adjunct essentially regardless of defect type. [확인] (Miron 2024a, Miron 2024b, Barootchi 2024)
2. **PRF is repeatedly "equivalent," never clearly superior, to established regenerative materials it is compared against head-to-head.** In furcations and intrabony defects, PRF ≈ bone graft ≈ collagen/barrier membrane ≈ PRP ≈ EMD ≈ rhBMP2 on the primary outcomes. This equivalence — not superiority — is the actual evidentiary basis for using PRF: it is a cheap, autologous, harvest-free substitute, not a better material. [확인] (Miron 2024a Group 2; Miron 2024b Group III)
3. **Very high between-study heterogeneity (I²≈85–95%) and systematic underreporting of PRF preparation protocols (centrifuge system, rpm, spin time)** afflict both pairwise MAs, meaning pooled point estimates should be read as directional, not precise. Root coverage's NMA structure sidesteps I² reporting but shares the same underlying protocol-reporting gap. [명확한 한계]
4. **No paper in the set has human histological confirmation of true periodontal regeneration** (new cementum, PDL, bone in continuity) attributable to PRF — all three rely on clinical/radiographic surrogates (PPD, CAL, RBF, mRC).

## What Differs Across Indications

- **Only root coverage shows a PRF-specific (not APC-class) effect.** In furcation and intrabony defects, the tested APC is essentially PRF (with T-PRF/L-PRF variants). In root coverage, three different APCs were compared on the same CAF comparator, and **only PRF cleared significance** — PRP (−0.61%, p=0.92) and CGF (+7.47%, p=0.33) did not. This is the one place in the trio where a real cross-APC ranking exists, and it favors PRF's longer growth-factor release kinetics over PRP's short-lived, anticoagulant-dependent clot.
- **Only root coverage identifies a positive effect modifier (baseline KTW)** that predicts *how much* PRF will help — a finding with no furcation or intrabony analogue, because those defect types don't have an equivalent "soft-tissue band" prerequisite.
- **The "PRF as drug-delivery matrix" pattern (biomolecule loading) is reproduced in furcation and intrabony defects but was not studied for root coverage** — an open question given PRF's role there is soft-tissue/vascular rather than hard-tissue.
- **Directionally opposite finding on adding a bone graft to PRF**: in furcations, adding BG to PRF gave a non-significant PPD result (0.20mm, p=0.42) while VCAL/HCAL still improved; in intrabony defects, adding PRF *to* a bone graft (the reverse combination — BG base + PRF add-on) robustly improved all three outcomes (PPD/CAL/RBF). The direction of combination (which material is the "base" and which is "added") appears to matter and is not always symmetric — a subtlety easy to miss when only one paper is read in isolation.
- **Evidence architecture differs**: furcation and intrabony are direct pairwise meta-analyses; root coverage is a network meta-analysis using CAF as an anchor, enabling indirect PRF-vs-PRP-vs-CGF ranking that a pairwise design could not have produced with the sparse head-to-head trial base actually available.

## Clinical Decision Framework

1. **Adding PRF to standard surgical treatment (OFD, CAF, ± bone graft) is a low-risk, evidence-consistent decision across all three defect types.** The adjunctive benefit is one of the most reproducible signals in periodontal regeneration research and carries no added donor-site morbidity (autologous, chairside).
2. **Do not expect PRF to outperform bone graft, membrane, EMD, or rhBMP2 in furcation or intrabony defects — expect equivalence.** The decision to substitute PRF for one of these materials should be driven by cost, availability, and morbidity avoidance, not by an expectation of superior regeneration.
3. **In root coverage, PRF is the only APC worth using as a CAF adjunct — PRP and CGF lack supporting evidence for mean root coverage.** But CTG remains the benchmark for magnitude of root coverage; PRF is the reasonable second-line choice specifically to avoid palatal harvest morbidity, and its benefit is conditional: **it works best where adequate baseline KTW already exists.** In KTW-deficient sites, prefer CTG.
4. **No APC (in any of the three defect types) is a KTW- or gingival-thickness-generating intervention.** Where soft-tissue volume/thickness gain is the actual goal (not just coverage %), APCs alone are insufficient — pair with a matrix/scaffold or default to autogenous grafting.
5. **PRF's value as a biomolecule carrier (metformin, bisphosphonates, statins) is a reproducible secondary finding in furcation and intrabony defects** — useful context if a clinician is considering adjunctive local pharmacotherapy, though this remains investigational and not yet guideline-endorsed.
6. **Evidence gaps to weigh before over-committing to PRF**: no human histology proving true regeneration anywhere in the set; near-total exclusion of smokers (0 in furcation set, 3/55 in intrabony set); mean follow-up in furcation studies (9.42 months) below the EFP-recommended 12 months; and a disclosed PRF-related intellectual-property conflict of interest from the senior author of the intrabony paper, which does not invalidate the pooled estimates but warrants noting.

## Adjacent Evidence — PRF/APC Beyond the Periodontal Trio

The three sibling papers' estimates sit inside a wider evidence context that reinforces, extends, or in one case contradicts the "PRF as adjunct" thesis:

- **Furcation prognosis backdrop**: the furcation defects Miron 2024a treats with OFD±PRF are not prognostically neutral to begin with. [[periodontics/nibali-2016-tooth-loss-furcation-involvement-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 21 longitudinal studies) found furcation involvement borderline-doubles 10-year tooth-loss risk (RR 1.46, p=0.06) and significantly doubles it at 10–15 years (RR 2.21, p<0.0001) — yet most furcation-involved molars, including grade III, still respond well to periodontal therapy. This is the prognostic case for regenerative adjuncts like PRF over early extraction.
- **Intrabony regeneration is itself a crowded field**: [[periodontics/stavropoulos-2021-periodontal-regeneration-intrabony-nma]] (NMA, 30 RCTs) found all regenerative approaches beat OFD, but combination therapies (GTR+graft, EMD+graft) were the most efficacious overall — a broader hierarchy that Miron 2024b's "PRF ≈ EMD ≈ graft ≈ membrane" equivalence sits inside. PRF's equivalence is against individual materials that are themselves not the ceiling; combination protocols outperform any single regenerative material, PRF included.
- **Root coverage's technique hierarchy**: [[periodontics/mucogingival-surgery-apf-fgg-ctg]] frames CTG as the gold standard for Miller I–II/Cairo RT1–RT2 root coverage on a double-blood-supply/predictability basis, consistent with Barootchi 2024's own finding that CTG generally exceeds PRF — PRF is a second-line, lower-morbidity substitute, not a challenger to CTG's primacy.
- **PRF's benefit doesn't generalize to non-surgical therapy**: [[periodontics/sherif-2025-iprf-vitamin-c-nonsurgical-periodontitis]] (3-arm RCT, n=45) tested i-PRF±vitamin C as an adjunct to non-surgical PMPR in stage-II periodontitis and found a clean null on BOP/PD/CAL at 6 months — the only durable benefit was reduced post-operative pain (days 2–3, p≤0.021). A floor-effect counterpoint to the trio's positive surgical-adjunct data: PRF's value looks tied to surgical/access-flap contexts, not moderate non-surgical disease.
- **Extraction sockets show a time-limited PRF benefit**: [[oral-surgery/al-maawi-2021-prf-extraction-socket-healing-sr]] (SR, 20 studies) found PRF improves early extraction-socket healing (pain reduced in 66.6% of studies, soft-tissue healing in 75%, socket fill in 85%) but the advantage vanishes by 6 months (no difference in dimensional bone loss), with zero long-term implant-outcome data — a fourth indication where PRF's benefit window is narrower than the chronic surgical-adjunct pattern in the trio. Three fresh Asian-journal RCTs (2026-07 ingest) reinforce this narrower, split-decision picture at head-to-head resolution: [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/manlerd-2025-lprf-membrane-socket-sealing-healing]] (n=45) found L-PRF sealing improved early soft-tissue healing index and pain but gave **no benefit in wound-closure area** vs collagen sponge or spontaneous healing; [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/razi-2024-prf-fdba-peri-implant-ridge-preservation]] (n=60) found PRF won on soft-tissue thickness while FDBA won on hard-tissue/bone-density preservation — a genuine split-decision, not a clean PRF win; and [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/nainoor-2024-enha-dfdba-prf-socket-preservation]] (n=40, both arms PRF-covered) found no significant difference between an eggshell-nanohydroxyapatite and a DFDBA graft core, meaning the PRF membrane itself was not the discriminating variable in that trial.
- **Ridge preservation reproduces the "equivalent-to-materials, better-than-nothing" pattern**: [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/siawasch-2025-autologous-platelet-concentrates-alveolar-ridge]] (SR+MA, 35 papers/33 RCTs) found APC alone (PRP/PRGF/L-PRF) consistently beat unassisted healing (faster soft-tissue healing, less pain, less resorption, more bone fill) in alveolar ridge preservation, but comparisons against other biomaterials remain too sparse to conclude — mirroring the trio's "low-cost autologous substitute, not superior material" framing in a fifth context.
- **The one place PRF adjunction did not help**: [[bone-regeneration/idiri-2023-prf-xenograft-sinus-ridge-augmentation-sr]] (SR, 7 RCTs) found PRF/L-PRF added to DBBM xenograft in sinus lift produced no significant improvement in new bone formation, residual graft %, or soft-tissue content versus DBBM alone — a genuine exception to the trio's "adding PRF always helps" thesis, possibly reflecting DBBM's slow-resorbing scaffold diluting PRF's short growth-factor release window. By contrast, [[implants/isq/ragher-2025-iprf-venous-blood-synthetic-graft-isq]] (4-arm RCT, n=64) found i-PRF alone matched a synthetic graft (NovaBone) for 6-month implant stability (ISQ >70 in both vs ~55–57 for no-graft/venous blood) in indirect sinus augmentation. PRF's benefit therefore looks inconsistent depending on whether it is added to an existing graft (Idiri 2023: no added value) or used as the primary graft substitute itself (Ragher 2025: matches a synthetic scaffold); venous blood alone showed no advantage over no graft in either study, confirming that unconcentrated blood lacks PRF's structured fibrin matrix. This sinus-lift exception now has SR+MA-level confirmation at the clinical-endpoint level: [[sinus-lift/lateral/sivakumar-2022-prp-sinus-augmentation-implant-survival-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 6 RCTs, 781 implants) found PRP added to sinus augmentation gives **no significant implant-survival benefit** (pooled OR 0.84, 95% CI 0.37–1.91, I²=0%) — three independent evidence streams (Idiri's new-bone-formation null, Ragher's stability-equivalence, Sivakumar's survival null) now converge on sinus augmentation being the one anatomical site where platelet concentrates consistently fail to clear the "adding PRF helps" bar that holds everywhere else in this evidence map.
- **Bone regeneration outside the alveolus is defect-geometry-dependent, not uniformly positive**: [[bone-regeneration/assiri-2026-iprf-prf-beta-tcp-bone-regeneration-goat]] (animal, goat critical-size metacarpal defects, micro-CT) found i-PRF + β-TCP significantly outperformed PRF + β-TCP and β-TCP alone on new bone volume/density at 8 weeks — a positive PRF-class signal, but in a long-bone critical-size model that contradicts [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/park-2022-prf-gbr-damaged-socket-yonsei]]'s finding that PRF-based sticky bone was only non-inferior (not superior) to conventional GBR in a beagle 2-wall alveolar defect. Read together, the two studies suggest PRF/i-PRF's regenerative ceiling depends heavily on defect geometry (long-bone critical-size vs alveolar multi-wall) rather than being a fixed, transferable effect size — a mechanistic explanation is offered by [[bone-regeneration/cho-2026-prf-bone-regeneration-mechanisms-scoping-review]] (scoping/narrative review), which frames PRF's regenerative contribution as depending on the interplay between fibrin-matrix architecture, growth-factor release kinetics, and local osteoimmunology (macrophage M1→M2 polarization) rather than a constant biological "dose."
- **Two fresh single-RCTs reinforce (and one nuances) the trio's core intrabony/root-coverage conclusions.** [[periodontics/parihar-2026-dfdba-prf-membrane-intrabony-defects-rct]] (split-mouth RCT, n=15 defect pairs) found DFDBA+PRF significantly outperformed DFDBA alone on PPD (p=0.01) and CAL (p=0.04) — a small but directionally consistent replication of Miron 2024b's "adding PRF to a graft helps" pattern. [[periodontics/corekci-2025-deepithelialized-gingival-graft-lprf-recession-rct]] (split-mouth RCT, n=15) adds an important nuance to Barootchi 2024's root-coverage finding: when L-PRF is added to a procedure that *already includes a graft* (de-epithelialized gingival graft, not bare CAF), root-coverage % is statistically equivalent (93.30% vs 95.28%, NS) — the PRF benefit shifts entirely to reduced early postoperative pain/discomfort rather than coverage magnitude. This suggests the trio's "PRF always improves the clinical outcome" thesis holds most cleanly against a *bare* procedure (OFD, CAF alone); once a graft is already doing the regenerative work, PRF's marginal contribution looks more like morbidity reduction than outcome improvement.
- **A sixth indication is emerging — peri-implantitis — but the evidence is still preliminary.** [[implants/peri-implantitis/shetty-2023-platelet-concentrates-peri-implantitis-treatment-sr]] (SR, 12 candidate articles → 4 eligible) found only one study directly compared platelet concentrates against open flap debridement; the rest combined platelet concentrates with graft materials against heterogeneous comparators, blocking meta-analysis. The author's own conclusion — "preliminary, promising, but not conclusive" — means peri-implantitis cannot yet be added to the furcation/intrabony/root-coverage trio as a fourth *established* PRF indication; it is a research direction, not a clinical decision point.
