## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 결론: 자가혈소판농축물(Autologous Platelet Concentrate, APC — PRF/PRP/CGF)의 치주재생 3대 적응증(치근이개부·골내결손·근면피복) SR/MA·NMA 3편(합계 185 RCT)을 통합 — **같은 Miron/Barootchi 저자 그룹**이 만든 자매 논문으로 결손 유형별 APC 위치를 지도화.
> - 공통 결론: **PRF를 결손 술식(OFD 등)에 추가하면 항상 유의한 개선** — 치근이개부(OFD vs OFD/PRF: PPD 1.73mm, VCAL 1.42mm), 골내결손(OFD vs OFD/PRF: PPD 1.27mm, CAL 1.22mm, 골충전 1.59mm), 근면피복(CAF vs CAF+PRF: 평균근피복 +6.12%, p=0.02) 세 곳 모두에서 일관.
> - 반대로 **PRF를 다른 확립된 재생재료와 head-to-head 비교하면 대부분 무차이(동등)** — 골내결손에서 PRF ≈ EMD ≈ PRP ≈ 차폐막(barrier membrane); 치근이개부에서 PRF ≈ 골이식·콜라겐막·PRP·rhBMP2. 즉 PRF는 "더 나은" 재료가 아니라 "저비용 자가 대체재"로 자리매김.
> - 예외 1: 근면피복에서는 **PRP·CGF는 유의한 이득 없음**(PRP −0.61%, CGF +7.47% 둘 다 NS) — PRF만 유의. 세 결손 유형 중 유일하게 "PRF 특이적" 효과가 확인된 곳.
> - 예외 2: 골내결손 Group III에서 **골이식(BG) > PRF (CAL −0.40mm 열위, p=0.004)** — 공간유지가 중요한 지표에서는 그래도 이식재가 근소 우위.
> - PRF를 "약물 전달 매트릭스"로 쓰는 패턴이 치근이개부·골내결손 두 곳에서 **재현**됨: 메트포르민·비스포스포네이트·스타틴을 PRF에 얹으면 추가 유의 개선(반면 항생제·LLLT는 무효, 골내결손에서 확인).
> - 근면피복만의 독자적 발견: PRF 효과가 **baseline 각화조직폭(Keratinized Tissue Width, KTW)에 비례**(교호작용 +4.98, p=0.03) — KTW 있는 부위일수록 PRF가 잘 듣는다. 반면 어떤 APC도 KTW·치은두께(Gingival Thickness, GT) 자체는 늘리지 못함 — 결합조직이식(Connective Tissue Graft, CTG)의 고유 강점은 대체 안 됨.
> - 근거 등급 차이 주의: 치근이개부·골내결손은 **직접 pairwise MA**(치근이개부 21 RCT, 골내결손 55 RCT)인 반면, 근면피복은 **NMA**(109 RCT, CAF 공통비교군)로 간접비교 포함 — 셋 다 SR+MA급이지만 통계 구조가 다르다.
> - 공통 한계: 세 논문 모두 **이질성이 매우 높음**(I²≈85–94%), PRF 제조 프로토콜(원심분리 rpm·시간·기기) 과소보고(치근이개부 4/21, 골내결손 19/55 미보고), human histology로 진짜 재생(치주인대·백악질 신생) 증명한 논문 전무.
> - 임상 결정 프레임: (1) 결손 술식에 PRF 추가 = 거의 항상 이득, 저비용·저위험이므로 추가를 기본값으로 고려. (2) PRF로 골이식·차폐막·CTG를 "대체"하려는 결정은 결손 유형별로 다르다 — 골내결손은 동등(대체 가능성 있음), 근면피복은 CTG가 여전히 근피복률 우위(단 KTW 충분 시 PRF가 형태학적 morbidity 감소 대안). (3) 흡연자·중증 결손·KTW 결핍 부위는 근거 공백 — 표준 재료 우선.

## One-line Summary

Synthesis of three 2024 Periodontology 2000 SR/MA(NMA) papers on autologous platelet concentrates (APC: PRF/PRP/CGF) across the three main periodontal regeneration indications — furcation defects (21 RCTs), intrabony defects (55 RCTs), and root coverage (109-RCT NMA) — showing a consistent "PRF-as-adjunct-improves-outcomes, PRF-vs-established-material-is-equivalent" pattern, with root coverage as the one indication where PRF alone (not PRP/CGF) shows a unique, KTW-dependent significant benefit, and intrabony/furcation sharing a reproducible "PRF as drug-delivery matrix" finding for small biomolecules.

## 한줄요약

2024년 Periodontology 2000에 실린 자가혈소판농축물(PRF/PRP/CGF) SR/MA(NMA) 3편(치근이개부 21 RCT, 골내결손 55 RCT, 근면피복 NMA 109 RCT)을 종합 — 세 적응증 모두에서 "PRF를 술식에 추가하면 유의 개선, 확립된 재료와 head-to-head는 대등"이라는 일관 패턴을 확인했고, 근면피복만 PRF(PRP·CGF 아님)의 독자적·KTW 의존적 효과가 있으며, 치근이개부·골내결손 두 곳에서는 PRF를 소분자 약물전달 매트릭스로 쓰는 패턴이 재현됨.

## Summary

Three Periodontology 2000 systematic reviews published in 2024 — all substantially overlapping in authorship (Miron, Moraschini, Barootchi, Wang, and colleagues) and explicitly cross-referencing each other as a "sibling set" — collectively map where **autologous platelet concentrates (APCs: PRF, PRP, CGF)** add value across the three classic periodontal regeneration indications: **class II furcation defects** ([[periodontics/miron-2024-prf-furcation-defects-sr-ma]], 21 RCTs, 786 patients), **intrabony defects** ([[periodontics/miron-2024-autogenous-platelet-concentrates-intrabony-defects]], 55 RCTs — the largest PRF synthesis to date), and **RT1 gingival recession / root coverage** ([[periodontics/barootchi-2024-autologous-platelet-concentrates-root-coverage]], 109-RCT network meta-analysis). Reading them side by side turns three separate indication-specific summaries into a single evidence map of what PRF (and, less consistently, PRP/CGF) can and cannot do in periodontal regenerative surgery.

## What Is Consistent Across All Three Indications

1. **Adding PRF to a surgical/access procedure always helps, and by a similar order of magnitude.** Furcation (PPD 1.73mm), intrabony (PPD 1.27mm), and root coverage (mRC +6.12%) all show PRF significantly outperforming the "bare" procedure. This is the single most reproducible finding in the platelet-concentrate literature and the strongest basis for recommending PRF as a low-cost adjunct essentially regardless of defect type. [근거강함] (Miron 2024a, Miron 2024b, Barootchi 2024)
2. **PRF is repeatedly "equivalent," never clearly superior, to established regenerative materials it is compared against head-to-head.** In furcations and intrabony defects, PRF ≈ bone graft ≈ collagen/barrier membrane ≈ PRP ≈ EMD ≈ rhBMP2 on the primary outcomes. This equivalence — not superiority — is the actual evidentiary basis for using PRF: it is a cheap, autologous, harvest-free substitute, not a better material. [합의수준] (Miron 2024a Group 2; Miron 2024b Group III)
3. **Very high between-study heterogeneity (I²≈85–95%) and systematic underreporting of PRF preparation protocols (centrifuge system, rpm, spin time)** afflict both pairwise MAs, meaning pooled point estimates should be read as directional, not precise. Root coverage's NMA structure sidesteps I² reporting but shares the same underlying protocol-reporting gap. [명확한 한계]
4. **No paper in the set has human histological confirmation of true periodontal regeneration** (new cementum, PDL, bone in continuity) attributable to PRF — all three rely on clinical/radiographic surrogates (PPD, CAL, RBF, mRC).

## What Differs Across Indications

- **Only root coverage shows a PRF-specific (not APC-class) effect.** In furcation and intrabony defects, the tested APC is essentially PRF (with T-PRF/L-PRF variants). In root coverage, three different APCs were compared on the same CAF comparator, and **only PRF cleared significance** — PRP (−0.61%, p=0.92) and CGF (+7.47%, p=0.33) did not. This is the one place in the trio where a real cross-APC ranking exists, and it favors PRF's longer growth-factor release kinetics over PRP's short-lived, anticoagulant-dependent clot.
- **Only root coverage identifies a positive effect modifier (baseline KTW)** that predicts *how much* PRF will help — a finding with no furcation or intrabony analogue, because those defect types don't have an equivalent "soft-tissue band" prerequisite.
- **The "PRF as drug-delivery matrix" pattern (biomolecule loading) is reproduced in furcation and intrabony defects but was not studied for root coverage** — an open question given PRF's role there is soft-tissue/vascular rather than hard-tissue.
- **Directionally opposite finding on adding a bone graft to PRF**: in furcations, adding BG to PRF gave a non-significant PPD result (0.20mm, p=0.42) while VCAL/HCAL still improved; in intrabony defects, adding PRF *to* a bone graft (the reverse combination — BG base + PRF add-on) robustly improved all three outcomes (PPD/CAL/RBF). The direction of combination (which material is the "base" and which is "added") appears to matter and is not always symmetric — a subtlety easy to miss when only one paper is read in isolation.
- **Evidence architecture differs**: furcation and intrabony are direct pairwise meta-analyses; root coverage is a network meta-analysis using CAF as an anchor, enabling indirect PRF-vs-PRP-vs-CGF ranking that a pairwise design could not have produced with the sparse head-to-head trial base actually available.

## Clinical Decision Framework

1. **Adding PRF to standard surgical treatment (OFD, CAF, ± bone graft) is a low-risk, evidence-consistent decision across all three defect types.** The adjunctive benefit is one of the most reproducible signals in periodontal regeneration research and carries no added donor-site morbidity (autologous, chairside).
2. **Do not expect PRF to outperform bone graft, membrane, EMD, or rhBMP2 in furcation or intrabony defects — expect equivalence.** The decision to substitute PRF for one of these materials should be driven by cost, availability, and morbidity avoidance, not by an expectation of superior regeneration.
3. **In root coverage, PRF is the only APC worth using as a CAF adjunct — PRP and CGF lack supporting evidence for mean root coverage.** But CTG remains the benchmark for magnitude of root coverage; PRF is the reasonable second-line choice specifically to avoid palatal harvest morbidity, and its benefit is conditional: **it works best where adequate baseline KTW already exists.** In KTW-deficient sites, prefer CTG.
4. **No APC (in any of the three defect types) is a KTW- or gingival-thickness-generating intervention.** Where soft-tissue volume/thickness gain is the actual goal (not just coverage %), APCs alone are insufficient — pair with a matrix/scaffold or default to autogenous grafting.
5. **PRF's value as a biomolecule carrier (metformin, bisphosphonates, statins) is a reproducible secondary finding in furcation and intrabony defects** — useful context if a clinician is considering adjunctive local pharmacotherapy, though this remains investigational and not yet guideline-endorsed.
6. **Evidence gaps to weigh before over-committing to PRF**: no human histology proving true regeneration anywhere in the set; near-total exclusion of smokers (0 in furcation set, 3/55 in intrabony set); mean follow-up in furcation studies (9.42 months) below the EFP-recommended 12 months; and a disclosed PRF-related intellectual-property conflict of interest from the senior author of the intrabony paper, which does not invalidate the pooled estimates but warrants noting.
