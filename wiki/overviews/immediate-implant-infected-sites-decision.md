## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 감염 부위 즉시식립(Immediate Implant Placement, IIP)의 안전성은 **감염 유형**에 따라 결정된다 — 만성 치근단 병변(chronic periapical lesion)은 프로토콜 준수 시 IIP 가능; 급성 농양(acute purulent abscess)은 8–12주 조기식립(Early placement)이 더 나은 변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 결과; 수술 후 감염(postoperative infection)은 70.6% 실패라는 별개의 고위험 사건.
> - **증거 구조**: Pranckeviciene 2024 SR+MA (22편, 앵커)·Saijeva 2020 SR+MA (9편, n=2,281)·AlMugeiren 2024 MA (RCT 10편, n=849)·Amato 2025 후향적 (n=143, 2–12년 추적) 포함 초기 10편에, 2026-09-29 갱신에서 Elaskary 2024를 포함해 총 21편이 되도록 10편(상충 MA 1·후향/비교 코호트 4·증례/증례군 5)을 추가.
> - **만성 병변 군**: 생존율 비감염 대비 유의차 없음 (RR=0.99, Saijeva 2020; OR=2.08 유의하나 92–100% 범위, AlMugeiren 2024). 단, 각화치은폭(Keratinized Gingiva Width, KGW)은 감염 군에서 미세하게 감소 — 모니터링 필요.
> - **급성 농양 분기**: Prati 2017 (n=131)에서 급성 농양 → 8–12주 조기식립 → MBL 24개월 0.48 mm (최선), IIP 군 0.78 mm, 지연식립(Delayed) 군 1.02 mm. 이 결과는 Muñoz-Cámara 2020 (급성 감염 군 100% 생존)와 외관상 충돌하지만 대상 정의가 다름 — 자세한 논의는 '충돌 해석' 섹션 참조.
> - **수술 후 감염**: 감염 발생 시 70.6% 실패 (Tabrizi 2020, n=34 감염 환자/980명). 항생제 투여 임계일: 비흡연자 9일, 흡연자 6일 초과 시 HR 기하급수 증가. 예방이 핵심.
> - **공통 프로토콜 요소**: ① atraumatic 발치, ② 철저한 소파(curettage)·세척(saline·CHX), ③ 예방적 항생제(아목시실린 500 mg tid 5–7일 또는 아목시실린/클라불란산 625 mg bid), ④ 초기 안정성(Initial Stability) 확보 (삽입토크(Insertion Torque, IT) ≥35 N·cm 권장).
> - **절대 금기**: 화농성 삼출(purulent exudate) 활동성 누공·봉와직염(cellulitis)·상악동 천공(sinus perforation) — Pranckeviciene 2024 합의. 단 이 금기의 이유("오염이 소파 능력을 넘는다")는 임상 원칙이지 직접 시험된 기전이 아니며, 발치 시점 pus 유무로 층화해 즉시식립 결과를 보고한 논문은 위키에 없다.
> - **상충 근거 (갱신 2026-09-29)**: de Oliveira-Neto 2019 MA(8편·임플란트 935개)는 감염 부위 즉시식립의 **실패 RR 2.99**(95% CI 1.04–8.56, p=0.04)로 위 "차이 없음"과 반대다. 신뢰구간 하한이 1.04이고 전 연구가 비무작위 2군·비뚤림 위험 불명확~높음이며 감염 유형(급성/만성, 근관성/치주성)을 나누지 않아, 이 오버뷰의 핵심 명제(감염 **유형**이 변수)를 검증하지도 반박하지도 못한다 — 그 논문 저자도 유형별 층화를 향후 과제로 제시.
> - **급성(Tier B) 판정의 근거는 얇다**: "급성 농양은 조기식립 권장"은 Prati 2017 단일 전향 연구(적응증이 다른 군 비교)에 의존한다. 반면 Amato 2025 후향(급성=농양 또는 누공 56개, 평균 7년, 생존 96.4%)·Blus 2015(급성 36개, 1년 94.4%)·Muñoz-Cámara 2020(급성 100%)은 "급성" 군에 즉시식립을 하고도 생존이 비감염과 차이 없었다. 이 논문들이 발치 시점 pus를 기록했는지는 위키에 없어, 조기식립은 "입증된 우월"이 아니라 **보수적 기본값**으로 읽는 것이 정확하다.
> - **신규 논문은 전부 만성/정의 미상**: da Silva 2023(후향, 423개; 만성 치근단 치주염 생존 88.8% vs 93.3%, 보정 OR 2.36, 95% CI 0.70–7.97, 유의하지 않음; 흡연 >20개비/일 OR 7.66·짧은 임플란트 OR 14.06이 독립 실패인자)·Crippa 2022(레이저 소독 감염 발치와 90개, 실패 1건)·Kakar 2020(105/110, 95.45%, 초록만)·Pal 2018(PRGF 1회 vs 2회, 무작위 방법 미기재)·Narad 2016(초록만)·증례 5건 중 어느 것도 급성 화농을 문서화하지 않았다. 누공이 있는 증례(Rathi 2025: 만성 치근단 농양+협측 누공, 소파+항생제+이종골로 즉시식립, n=1)도 급성 근거가 아니다.
> - **Elaskary 2024 재해석**: 감염군의 징후는 누공 57.9%·종창 15.8%·방사선투과상 15.8%·fistula 10.5%이고 화농은 기록되지 않았으며, 프로토콜은 발치 후 치근 재식립 6일 뒤 식립하는 단계적 방식이다 — "발치 당일 즉시식립"의 근거가 아니라 **6일 지연 식립**의 근거다.
> - **심미 주의**: 감염 군에서 협측골 소실 폭이 더 크거나 KGW 감소 가능성 있음 → 전치부 감염 IIP는 추가 연조직 이식(Connective Tissue Graft, CTG) 고려.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 21 papers (4 SR+MA, 1 SR, 1 MA, 5 prospective/comparative cohorts, 5 retrospective, 5 case reports/series; 2015–2026) on IIP in infected extraction sockets: the key clinical variable is infection type, not infection per se — chronic periapical lesion with proper debridement and antibiotic prophylaxis yields survival comparable to non-infected sites, while acute purulent abscess favors an 8–12 week Early-placement protocol for optimal marginal bone preservation.

Postoperative infection after IIP is a distinct high-risk event (70.6% failure, Tabrizi 2020) with antibiotic timing as the critical modifiable variable (critical threshold day 9 non-smokers / day 6 smokers); infected-site IIP survival from the primary SR+MA evidence base shows no statistically significant difference versus non-infected sites when protocol criteria are met (RR=0.99, Saijeva 2020; no difference, Pranckeviciene 2024).

Absolute contraindications remain active purulence, fistula, cellulitis, and sinus perforation (a consensus based on clinical principle — no held paper reports IIP outcomes stratified by documented pus at surgery, and one 2019 MA reports a 3-fold failure risk that later syntheses do not reproduce); keratinized gingiva width is the one clinical parameter consistently reduced in infected-site IIP and warrants monitoring.

## 세줄요약

21편(SR+MA 4·SR 1·MA 1·전향/비교 5·후향 5·증례/증례군 5; 2015–2026) 합성: 핵심 변수는 '감염 자체'가 아닌 **감염 유형** — 만성 치근단 병변(chronic periapical lesion)은 철저한 소파+항생제 예방 시 비감염 부위와 생존율 동등(RR=0.99, Saijeva 2020; Pranckeviciene 2024 SR+MA); 급성 화농성 농양(acute purulent abscess)은 8–12주 조기식립(Early placement)이 24개월 변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 최선(0.48 mm, Prati 2017).

수술 후 감염은 별개 고위험 사건(70.6% 실패, Tabrizi 2020): 항생제 임계일(비흡연 9일·흡연 6일) 이후 급격히 위험도 상승 — 예방이 치료보다 훨씬 효율적.

절대 금기는 활동성 화농성 삼출·봉와직염·상악동 천공(임상 원칙에 근거한 합의이며, 발치 시점 pus로 층화한 즉시식립 결과는 위키에 없고, 실패 위험 3배를 보고한 2019 MA는 이후 종합에서 재현되지 않음); 각화치은폭(Keratinized Gingiva Width, KGW)은 감염 군에서 지속적으로 감소 → 수술 후 모니터링 필수.

## Summary

감염 부위에서의 IIP는 오랫동안 "절대 금기"로 인식되어 왔으나, 2015년 이후 축적된 prospective/SR+MA 증거들은 이 통념을 임상 유형별로 세분화하고 있다. 이 오버뷰는 `immediate-implant/` 카테고리의 감염 관련 논문 21편(2026-09-29 갱신: 10편 추가)을 **감염 유형 × 치료 타이밍 × 결과**의 축으로 통합한다.

## 2. Three-Tier Clinical Framework

### 2.1 Tier A — Chronic Periapical Lesion (비화농성 만성 병변)

**Definition**: Periapical pathology without active pus, no sinus tract, no cellulitis. Includes endodontically failed teeth with periapical granuloma or cyst, and chronic low-grade abscess.

**Verdict: IIP is viable** [확인]

- Pranckeviciene 2024 SR+MA: survival equivalent to non-infected sites when debridement + antibiotics applied
- Saijeva 2020 SR+MA (n=2,281): RR=0.99 for survival; MBL, PD, mBI, MGL all NS
- AlMugeiren 2024 MA (10 RCTs, n=849): survival OR=2.08 in favor of non-infected but individual RCT survival 92–100%; plaque index and bleeding index favorable in infected group (RR=0.59, 0.77)
- Amato 2025 (n=143, 7-yr mean): chronic infection group 95% survival vs non-infected 97.8% (p=0.8 NS)
- Blus 2015 (n=168, 1-yr): chronic 100% vs non-infected 98.8%
- da Silva 2023 (retrospective, 423 implants): chronic apical periodontitis 88.8% vs 93.3%, NS (adjusted OR 2.36, 95% CI 0.70–7.97) — but note the lower point estimate; independent failure predictors were smoking >20 cigarettes/day (OR 7.66) and short implants 6.0–8.5 mm (OR 14.06, from 5 failures of 26 short implants)
- Crippa 2022 (90 laser-decontaminated infected sockets, 1.7-yr mean): 1 failure (1%); antibiotics and chlorhexidine were co-interventions and the control was a healed site, so the laser effect cannot be isolated
- Kakar 2020 (abstract only): 105/110 (95.45%) with laser decontamination + in-situ hardening alloplast; Pal 2018 (chronic periapical infection, PRGF twice vs once) and Narad 2016 (abstract only, 24/24 stable) add low-grade support — none has a non-infected control adequate for a survival comparison

**Contradicting evidence — de Oliveira-Neto 2019 [확인].** This MA (8 non-randomized two-group studies, 935 implants, all with antibiotics) reports a failure RR of 2.99 (95% CI 1.04–8.56) for infected vs non-infected sites, the only held synthesis with a significant survival penalty; AlMugeiren 2024 (OR=2.08) leans the same way. Saijeva 2020 (9 cohorts, 2,281 sockets, RR=0.99) and Pranckeviciene 2024 do not reproduce it. It does not overturn Tier A: the lower CI bound is 1.04, the risk of bias was unclear-to-high, and infected sites were pooled without an acute/chronic or endodontic/periodontal split — precisely the distinction Tier A and Tier B rest on. Read it as a reminder that Tier A survival is high but not equal in every dataset, not as a finding about any one infection type.

**Required protocol elements** [확인]:
1. Atraumatic extraction (piezo or periotome preferred)
2. Complete soft-tissue curettage of all granulation tissue
3. Socket irrigation: saline ± CHX 0.12%
4. Socket culture/debridement verification (visible clean bone)
5. Prophylactic antibiotics: amoxicillin 500 mg tid × 5–7 days (or amoxicillin/clavulanate 625 mg bid where resistance concern)
6. Primary stability IT ≥35 N·cm

**Monitoring**: KGW is the one parameter consistently reduced in infected IIP (Saijeva 2020, Lee 2018). If initial KGW <2 mm, consider simultaneous free gingival graft (Free Gingival Graft, FGG) or plan secondary vestibuloplasty.

**VST + 6-day decontamination variant (Elaskary 2024)**: Note that this variant does not fit the Tier A definition above (no sinus tract): 11 of its 19 infected sites had a sinus tract (57.9%), 3 swelling, 3 radiolucency only, 2 fistula, and pus was not documented. It is also not same-day placement — the implant went in on day 6 after the extracted root was disinfected and splinted back. For Type II sockets (deficient labial plate) with signs of infection (periapical radiolucency, fistulae, sinus tracts), the 6-day protocol — socket debridement/metronidazole irrigation + temporary tooth reimplantation — followed by Vestibular Socket Therapy (VST) achieved 100% 1-year survival with bone gain (apical 2.4 mm, mid 1.6 mm, crestal 0.85 mm) and minimal mucosal recession (0.29–0.51 mm), comparable to non-infected controls. This protocol addresses the specific challenge of Type II infected sockets in the esthetic zone where conventional curettage alone may be insufficient for decontamination.

### 2.2 Tier B — Acute Periapical Abscess (급성 화농성 농양)

**Definition**: Active pus, fluctuant swelling, or sinus tract with purulent exudate. Prati 2017 protocol criterion: "acute periapical abscess" requiring drainage.

**Verdict: Early placement (8–12 weeks) is the conservative default; immediate placement is not disproven** [확인 for the data, 미검증 for the weighting]

The key data point is Prati 2017 (n=131 flapless transmucosal implants, prospective):

| Timing group | Indication | 24-mo MBL |
|---|---|---|
| Early (8–12 wk post-abscess) | Acute abscess | **0.48 ± 0.72 mm** |
| Immediate | Chronic lesion | 0.78 ± 0.70 mm |
| Delayed (standard) | Healthy | 1.02 ± 1.01 mm |

Caveat on this table: the three groups differ in indication (abscess vs chronic vs healthy), so it is not a randomized comparison of timing, and it is the only held study that compares timing in acute abscess sites.

**Immediate placement in "acute" groups (held cohorts) [확인]**:
- Amato 2025 (retrospective, esthetic zone, mean 7 yr, range 2–12): acute group (perio/endo abscess or fistula) n=56, survival 96.4% vs non-infected 97.8%, chronic 95% (p=0.8) — the largest and longest held dataset on immediate placement in abscess/fistula sites
- Blus 2015 (prospective, 1 yr): acute n=36, 94.4% vs non-infected 98.8%, NS
- Muñoz-Cámara 2020 (prospective): 100% survival in "acute periapical infection"
- None of the held pages states whether purulence was present at the time of surgery, so these data show that immediate placement after debridement + antibiotics can work in sites called "acute", not that it works with active pus. Case reports/series (Rathi 2025: chronic abscess with a buccal draining sinus, Crippa 2020, Sotomayor Julio 2026, Jurado 2025) are chronic or undefined and add nothing for true acute purulence.

Prati's MBL hierarchy (Early < Immediate < Delayed) is counterintuitive but consistent: abscess sites, once cleared, provide a biologically active socket that osseointegrates favorably. Early placement captures this window. Pranckeviciene 2024 also cites acute infection with purulent exudate as a contraindication to immediate placement.

**Practical path** [미검증]:
1. Drain abscess / extract tooth (or RCT if salvageable)
2. 8–12 week healing interval
3. If socket heals cleanly → implant placement (now a healed/healing site, not "infected")
4. Standard protocol from that point

Note: Muñoz-Cámara 2020 achieved 100% survival in "acute periapical infection" with IIP — but their cohort included patients with periapical pathology managed with antibiotics prior to extraction, not necessarily active purulence at time of surgery. The definitional difference explains the apparent conflict (see Section 4 below).

### 2.3 Tier C — Postoperative Infection after IIP (시술 후 발생 감염)

**Definition**: Acute infection developing days after implant placement — distinct from pre-existing socket pathology.

**Verdict: High-risk event — 70.6% implant failure** [확인]

Tabrizi 2020 (n=980; 34 developed post-op infection, 3.46% incidence):

- Failure rate among infected implants: **70.6% (24/34)**
- Critical antibiotic threshold: **Day 9** for non-smokers; **Day 6** for smokers
  (beyond these days, HR for failure increases sharply even with antibiotic treatment)
- Independent predictors of failure: smoking, immediate placement, bone graft presence, late infection diagnosis

**Prevention is the primary strategy** — once postoperative infection is established, salvage rate is only 29.4%. Key prevention levers:
1. Smoking cessation (highest modifiable risk)
2. Sterile technique, socket irrigation
3. Antibiotic prophylaxis started preoperatively
4. Early infection recognition and treatment initiation within Day 4 of symptoms

## 3. Absolute Contraindications to IIP in Infected Sites

Consensus from Pranckeviciene 2024 + Chrcanovic 2015 + clinical principle:

| Condition | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Active purulent exudate at surgery | Contamination overwhelms debridement capacity |
| Fluctuant cellulitis / Ludwig's angina | Systemic spread risk |
| Sinus perforation + infection | IIP into maxillary sinus contraindicated |
| Uncontrolled systemic infection | Host defense compromised |
| Inadequate primary stability achievable | Infection undermines bone anchor |

> The rationales above are clinical principle, not tested mechanisms: no held paper compares immediate placement with vs without documented pus at surgery. The evidence that actually exists is indirect — Prati 2017 favoring early placement in abscess sites, and the acute-group cohorts in Section 2.2 showing acceptable survival with immediate placement.

## 4. Conflict Resolution — Muñoz-Cámara vs Prati

**Apparent contradiction**: Muñoz-Cámara 2020 shows 100% survival placing implants immediately in "acute periapical infection" sites. Prati 2017 recommends 8–12 weeks for acute abscess.

**Resolution** [미검증]:

The two studies classify "acute" differently:
- **Muñoz-Cámara 2020**: "Acute periapical infection" = teeth with symptomatic periapical pathology, confirmed radiographically. Antibiotics given 7 days pre-op. No mention of purulent exudate at extraction — likely chronic/sub-acute presentation managed medically before surgery.
- **Prati 2017**: "Acute periapical abscess" = clinical abscess requiring drainage, with intraoral/extraoral swelling. This is the true acute purulent state.

**Clinical implication**: The "acute periapical infection" umbrella in the literature conflates sub-acute/chronic presentations with true purulent abscess. The clinical decision hinge is **whether purulent exudate is present at the time of extraction**:
- No active pus at time of extraction (even if diagnosed as "acute infection" and pre-treated with antibiotics) → IIP viable [확인]
- Active pus/fluctuant abscess at extraction → Early placement 8–12 weeks [확인]

**Limit of this resolution [미검증]:** the definitional explanation is plausible for Muñoz-Cámara, but Amato 2025 explicitly grouped abscess or fistula sites as "acute" and still reported 96.4% survival with immediate placement, and Blus 2015 reported 94.4%. Unless those papers' clinical criteria are re-read against the full text for purulence at surgery, the hinge "pus at extraction" remains a working rule, not a demonstrated threshold. Elaskary 2024 (sinus tract 57.9%, staged day-6 placement) and Rathi 2025 (draining sinus, immediate placement, n=1) show that sinus-tract cases are handled both ways in the literature.

## 7. Evidence Gaps and Cautions

- **RCT evidence is limited**: Most positive data comes from prospective cohorts and SR+MAs of heterogeneous studies. AlMugeiren 2024 (10 RCTs, n=849) is the strongest RCT-level synthesis but still reports OR=2.08 marginal survival difference, just within wide CIs.
- **No pus-stratified outcome data**: no held paper reports immediate-placement outcomes separately for documented purulence at surgery; the acute/chronic labels in Amato 2025, Blus 2015 and Muñoz-Cámara 2020 are not mapped to that criterion in the wiki. A 2020 retrospective cohort (Peñarrocha-Oltra; 8,110 implants, early apical peri-implantitis higher with immediate vs delayed placement and with an apical lesion) surfaced in surveillance but has no open-access full text and is not held.
- **Abstract-only sources**: Kakar 2020 and Narad 2016 were ingested from abstracts (empty PMC body); their infection definitions and follow-up lengths are unverified. Elaskary 2024's page cites Kakar with "48.75 months", which the abstract does not contain.
- **Follow-up**: Most studies ≤2 years. Amato 2025 (2–12 yr mean 7 yr) is the longest, showing durable equivalence.
- **Protocol standardization**: CHX concentration, antibiotic choice/duration, debridement method (piezo vs conventional), and timing definitions vary widely.
- **Esthetic zone caution**: All major infected-site IIP studies involve mixed arch locations. Esthetic-zone infected IIP carries additional dimensional risk → CTG should be considered more liberally than in posterior sites.
