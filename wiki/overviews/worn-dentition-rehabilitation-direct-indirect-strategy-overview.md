## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 마모 치열(worn dentition) 수복에서 직접 수복(direct composite)과 간접 수복(indirect ceramic)의 생존율에는 통계적 유의차가 없으며, 직접 복합레진의 수리 가능성(repairability)이 임상 우선 선택 근거가 된다.
> - **최고 근거 SR+MA**: Chantler 2024 (14편, 직접 vs 간접 최소침습) — 생존율 무차이 (OR 1.79, 95% CI 0.64–5.05, p=0.270); 전향 통합 생존율 직접 99% vs 간접 100%; 리튬 디실리케이트(lithium disilicate) 최저 실패율 1.8%.
> - **재료 간 수명 (구치부 복합레진)**: Josic 2023 SR+MA — 단기(RR 1.54, p=0.27)·중기(RR 1.87, p=0.27)·장기(RR 0.95, p=0.86) 모두 직접 vs 간접 유의차 없음; 마모 치열 아군도 NS.
> - **국소 마모 Dahl 수복**: Fan 2026 SR+MA (11편) — 간접 연간 실패율(AFR) 0–0.7% vs 직접 0–8.7% (p<0.05); 후방 교합접촉 재형성(occlusal re-establishment) 86.9%; 단 추적기간 불일치(간접 1.7–2.2년 vs 직접 최대 7년)로 장기 우월성 결론 주의.
> - **전악 중증 마모 재활**: Ferrando-Cascales 2023 (5년 후향, 8명, 212개 수복) — 전체 생존율 90.1%(60개월); 후방 비니어 실패 위험 HR 0.143 (p=0.008); 21개 합병증 중 18개를 직접 복합레진 수리로 해결.
> - **합병증 유형 차이**: 직접 복합레진 → 파절(수리 가능); 금속도재관(ceramo-metal crown) → 완전 탈락(비가역적) — 수리 가능성이 직접의 핵심 임상 우위.
> - **수직교합고경(VDO) 증대 가역성**: 직접 복합레진은 성형 가능 + 수리 가능 → 단계적 VDO 거상의 진단·가역적 매체로 적합; 간접은 확정 후 수정 불가.
> - **임상 선택 기준**: ①경도~중등도 마모 → 직접 복합레진 우선; ②국소 전치부 마모 → 고정성 Dahl(직접·간접 모두 가능); ③중증 전악 마모(TWES 2–4) → 직접 또는 간접 동등, 후방 가능하면 레진 리스크 관리; ④장기 확정 보철 → 리튬 디실리케이트.
> - **연간 합병증률(ACR)**: 직접 5.2–30.2%, 간접 5.6–7.2% — 재료 무관하게 마모 치열은 합병증률 높음; 정기 유지 관리 필수.
> - **근거 한계**: 대부분 SR에서 메타분석 불가(높은 이질성), 장기 RCT 부족; GRADE 근거 수준 대부분 낮음–매우 낮음.
> - **임상 takeaway**: "직접 복합레진 먼저, 결과 좋으면 그대로" — 생존율 차이 없고 수리 가능하며 치질 보존적. 실패 시 간접으로 전환 가능. 후방부는 어떤 재료든 합병증률 높으므로 위치 기반 위험 상담 필수.

## Three-line Summary

Worn dentition rehabilitation using minimally invasive direct vs indirect restorations has been evaluated in two SR+MAs (Chantler 2024: 14 studies; Hardan 2022: 16 studies) — neither demonstrates survival superiority for indirect over direct composite; direct composites fail reparably (fracture) while irreversible ceramo-metal failures argue for conservative first-line approaches.

For localized Dahl restorations, Fan 2026 SR+MA (11 studies) shows indirect AFR significantly lower short-term (0–0.7% vs 0–8.7%, p<0.05) with 86.9% posterior occlusal re-establishment, but follow-up confound limits long-term superiority claims; lithium disilicate carries the lowest failure rate (1.8%) when indirect is chosen.

The shared weight of evidence favors direct resin composite as reversible, repairable first-line treatment for mild–moderate wear, with escalation to indirect ceramics (especially lithium disilicate) justified by biological tooth destruction severity and long-term durability goals.

## 세줄요약

두 편의 SR+MA (Chantler 2024: 14편, Hardan 2022: 16편) — 마모 치열에서 직접 vs 간접 수복의 생존율 우열 없음; 직접 복합레진은 수리 가능 파절, 금속도재관은 비가역적 탈락 — 수리 가능성이 직접 우선 선택 핵심 근거.

국소 Dahl 수복(Fan 2026 SR+MA, 11편): 간접 AFR 0–0.7% vs 직접 0–8.7% (p<0.05), 후방 교합 재형성 86.9%; 단기 간접 우위가 추적기간 불일치로 장기 결론 불가; 리튬 디실리케이트 최저 실패율(1.8%).

근거 합산 임상 지침: 경도~중등도 마모는 직접 복합레진 먼저(가역·수리), 중증/장기 확정은 간접(특히 리튬 디실리케이트); 후방 위치는 어떤 재료든 실패 위험인자.

## 3. Clinical Decision Points

### 3.1 First-Line Choice: Direct Composite

Across four independent systematic reviews, no evidence supports indirect restoration superiority in terms of survival rates. Direct composite is the rational first-line treatment because:
- Equivalent survival to indirect in mild–moderate wear (Chantler 2024, Josic 2023, Hardan 2022)
- Failure mode = repairable fracture (vs irreversible catastrophic failure of ceramo-metal crowns)
- Reversible: no irreversible tooth preparation; can be removed if VDO proves poorly tolerated
- Conservative: preserves tooth structure for future escalation
- Medium-term proven: prospective data 99% survival, up to 10-year clinical series (Hardan 2022)

### 3.2 When to Escalate to Indirect

Indirect restorations (especially lithium disilicate) are justified when:
1. **Biological failure threshold**: tooth structure destruction prevents adequate direct bonding surface
2. **Long-term durability goal**: lithium disilicate 1.8% failure rate is the lowest in any class (Chantler 2024)
3. **Localized anterior Dahl**: indirect short-term AFR significantly better (0–0.7% vs 0–8.7%, p<0.05)
4. **Patient compliance**: high attrition parafunctional patient who cannot commit to regular composite repair
5. **Severe generalized wear (TWES 2–4)**: direct and indirect equivalent (90.1% survival composite vs 93.1% ceramic, p=0.107 NS; Ferrando-Cascales 2023)

### 3.3 Dahl Concept Decision

Fixed Dahl restorations achieve posterior occlusal re-establishment in 86.9% of patients (Fan 2026). This is age-independent. Key decision:
- Localized anterior wear with posterior clearance → Dahl is first choice over full-mouth rehabilitation
- Material for Dahl: both direct and indirect work; indirect carries lower AFR short-term but follow-up confound limits 10-year extrapolation
- Complication management: annual complication rate 5–30% regardless of material; plan for maintenance

### 3.4 Location Risk Stratification

Posterior veneer placement is the single strongest significant risk factor for failure (HR 0.143, p=0.008, Ferrando-Cascales 2023). This means:
- Posterior table-tops and veneers carry higher failure risk regardless of material
- Anterior veneers (palatal 100% survival, vestibular 94.1% at 60 months) are the safest location
- Patient should be counselled about location-specific complication rates before treatment

### 3.5 VDO Management Integration

The minimally invasive philosophy couples direct composite with VDO monitoring:
- Semi-additive protocol: posterior occlusal composite first → anterior guidance → esthetic veneers (Ferrando-Cascales 2023, Lee 2019)
- Short-term composite provisionals allow freeway space verification before irreversible commitment
- Digital workflow eliminates chairside occlusal adjustment at final delivery (Lee 2019: PMMA double-scan, no adjustment needed)

## 5. Research Gaps

1. **No head-to-head long-term RCT** directly comparing direct vs indirect with equal follow-up and standardized outcomes — existing SR+MAs carry GRADE very-low certainty (Josic 2023, Chantler 2024)
2. **Etiology-stratified data absent**: no study compares outcomes by wear etiology (chemical erosion vs mechanical attrition vs bruxism); different etiologies may favor different approaches
3. **Dahl long-term (>5 yr)**: Fan 2026 MA limited to 1.7–2.2 yr for indirect; long-term indirect Dahl survival unknown
4. **Implant-assisted rehabilitation**: full-mouth wear + tooth loss combination lacks dedicated SR
5. **Bruxism modification**: whether occlusal splint or botulinum toxin co-management changes survival not studied in wear-specific RCTs
