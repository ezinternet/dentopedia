## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심: 임플란트 출현윤곽(Emergence Profile) 설계와 맞춤 연조직 컨디셔닝 6편 종합 — 개념틀(gomez-meda) → 실행 장치(ruales-carrera·azpiazu-flores) → 캡처 근거(li) → 주변 임상(han·lim).
> - 개념틀 (gomez-meda 2021, narrative): 심미생물학적 윤곽(Esthetic Biological Contour, EBC) **4-zone** — Zone1 임플란트-지대주 계면, Zone2 준임계윤곽(Sub-critical), Zone3 임계윤곽(Critical), Zone4 치은연상.
> - 결정적 원칙: **임계윤곽(Zone3) 볼록도↑ → 치은연 근단측 이동(퇴축 위험), 오목도↑ → 치은연 치관측 상승**; **준임계윤곽(Zone2)은 오목~중립이 치조정골 안정**, 볼록하면 골소실·염증↑.
> - 인용 벤치마크: 골수준(Bone-level) 임플란트 **1~3mm 치조정하(sub-crestal)** 식립, 생물학적 폭(Biologic Width) **~3~4mm**, 협측 연조직 **≥2mm** → 치조정골 안정(Linkevicius), 지대주 높이 **≥3mm** → 골소실 방지(Galindo-Moreno).
> - 캡처의 물리학 (li 2019, prospective, n=12): 임시보철(Interim Restoration) 제거 **0초 즉시** 출현윤곽 붕괴 시작, 시간의 **로그함수(ln)**로 회복 — 반면 유두·치은연은 20분간 **≤0.27mm** 변화(임상적 무의미) → 인상은 임시보철 유지 상태 또는 윤곽 복제 scan body로 떠야 함.
> - 실행 장치 1 (ruales-carrera 2019, case series): 즉시식립 당일 chairside **맞춤 치유지대주(Customized Healing Abutment, CHA)** — PEEK/PMMA+티타늄 인서트로 발치치아 형태 복제, 임계+준임계윤곽 동시 재현 → 임시보철·재개방수술 불필요.
> - 실행 장치 2 (azpiazu-flores 2025, case report): **3D 프린팅 출현윤곽 성형체(EPF)**를 규격 티타늄 치유지대주에 장착 — 연조직 성형 + 구강스캐너(IOS) 등록을 동시 수행(scan body 대체), 임시지대주 나사 불필요, 수평골결손 대구치를 협측 이식 없이 심미 수복.
> - 공유 어휘: 임계/준임계윤곽(Su 2010), dual-zone(Chu 2012)이 core 3편의 공통 언어 — **최종 크라운이 아니라 임시보철·치유지대주가 1차 연조직 성형 도구**라는 합의.
> - 주변 근거 (han 2025, prospective n=20): dual-thread 상악 심미대 즉시식립 — 성공 85%/생존 100%, ISQ 70일에 >70, **중안면 퇴축 1.86→1.21 개선**, 1년 협측골 두께 −0.34~−0.48mm; 출현윤곽 설계가 아니라 macrodesign·1차안정성 축.
> - 주변 근거 (lim 2022, animal, 개 5마리): 즉시식립 협측 gap에서 **결합조직이식(SCTG) 단독이 치관측 점막 최우수**(MM-IS 1.99mm), GBR은 견부 하방에만 작용, **GBR+SCTG 병용은 오히려 최저(1.24mm, 비가산적)** — 단 모두 NS(underpowered).
> - 임상 bottom line: 견부 상방 연조직 두께·출현윤곽은 능동적으로 지지·복제해야 하는 **labile 차원**이고 유두·치은연은 상대적으로 안정 → **osseous가 아니라 connective/맞춤윤곽이 심미대 value-add**.
> - **신규 SR+MA (rios-osorio 2025, 17편 RCT)**: 이종 콜라겐 매트릭스(XCM)를 비가교/가교로 분리 분석 — **비가교 XCM*은 CTG 대비 점막두께(MT) 열위**(MD −0.27mm, P=0.01)이나 **가교형 VCMX는 CTG와 동등**(MD −0.02mm, P=0.83); 각화점막폭(KMW)은 **FGG가 XCM* 대비 우위**(MD +1.47mm, P=0.0006) — 이전까지 case-level 근거만 있던 "연조직 성형 재료" 축에 최초로 근거강함 등급 자료 추가.
> - 재료 선택의 임상적 의미: 준임계윤곽(Zone2, gomez-meda) 부위 볼륨 확보에 자가이식(CTG)이 여전히 최우선이나, **가교형 XCM(VCMX)은 공여부 이환 없이 동등한 볼륨 결과**를 낼 수 있어 맞춤 치유지대주·EPF와 병용 시 자가이식 대체 옵션이 됨.
> - **배경 검토 (blaskovic 2021, narrative)**: FGG·CTG·유경이식편·근단변위판(APF)·롤술식·터널링·이종 콜라겐 매트릭스를 아우르는 술식 기준서 — KM ≥2mm, 연조직 두께 ≥2mm 임계값과 이식편 생존 3조건(고정·밀착·혈류)을 재확인, rios-osorio의 재료 비교가 놓인 술식 지형도 제공.
> - **심미 윤곽 이식 (le 2022, narrative)**: "골이 연조직을 이끈다" 원리 — GBR로 순측골을 증대하면 상부 점막이 자연히 두꺼워져 별도 CTG 없이도 출현윤곽 개선 가능(8년 안정 사례), 폰틱 부위에도 적용; 텐팅 스크류로 협측 치조정 볼륨 유지.
> - **경조직 확장 (lee 2025, animal, 개 4마리)**: 열구형 골결손 GBR에서 연조직 블록형 골대체재(Soft BBS)의 L-형 느슨 적용 vs 압축 적용은 골폭 차이 없음 — **막 고정 없이는 두 군 모두 견부 부위 골유지 실패**, 순수 연조직 논의를 넘어 협측 열구결손의 hard+soft 복합 관리로 범위 확장, 막 고정(핀·택)의 필요성을 시사.
> - **디지털 캡처 선례 (monaco 2016, technique)**: li(2019)의 "0초 붕괴" 문제를 2016년에 이미 인지 — 스캔바디(STL1) + 임시보철 구강내·외 이중 스캔(STL2)을 중첩(STL3)해 인상 없이 맞춤 CAD/CAM 지대주 제작; li·azpiazu-flores 캡처 논의의 초기/토대적 선례.
> - 공백 갱신: **재료 비교 공백은 rios-osorio(2025)가 부분 해결** — XCM 하위유형(가교/비가교)별 CTG 대비 우열은 이제 SR+MA 근거 확보. 단 core 3편(gomez-meda·ruales-carrera·azpiazu-flores)의 정량 심미결과(PES/WES) 부재, 준임계 오목 vs 중립 head-to-head, 디지털 캡처 3방식 정확도 비교는 여전히 미해결.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 11 papers establishing the peri-implant emergence-profile arc: the 4-zone critical/subcritical (EBC) framework (Gomez-Meda 2021) governs gingival-margin position and crestal-bone stability; the provisional/healing abutment — not the definitive crown — is the primary tissue-shaping instrument; the profile collapses the instant the interim restoration is removed (Li 2019, logarithmic recovery).

Material-choice: SR+MA of 17 RCTs (Ríos-Osorio 2025) shows crosslinked XCM (VCMX) equals CTG for mucosal thickness while non-crosslinked XCM is inferior and FGG retains superiority for keratinized-mucosa width; the "bone drives soft tissue" counter-strategy (Le 2022) and combined hard+soft dehiscence GBR (Lee 2025) extend the arc into complex management.

Clinical capture rule: scan or impress with the interim restoration in place or via a customized scan body (Monaco 2016 precedent, Azpiazu-Flores 2025 EPF) — bare stock scan body records a collapsed contour.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 출현윤곽(Emergence Profile) 설계·맞춤 연조직 컨디셔닝 11편 종합 — 임계윤곽(Critical Contour, Zone3)이 치은연 위치를, 준임계윤곽(Sub-critical Contour, Zone2)이 치조정골 안정을 결정하며(Gomez-Meda 2021), 임시보철·맞춤 치유지대주(Customized Healing Abutment)가 1차 성형 도구.

임시보철 제거 0초부터 윤곽 붕괴 시작·로그함수 회복(Li 2019) → 임시보철 유지 상태 또는 윤곽 복제 scan body로 캡처 필수(Monaco 2016 선례; Azpiazu-Flores 2025 3D-프린팅 성형체).

재료 선택은 SR+MA(Ríos-Osorio 2025, 17 RCT)로 가교형 XCM(VCMX)=결합조직이식(CTG) 동등·비가교 XCM 열위·유리치은이식(FGG)>XCM* 각화폭 확립; 골 우선 전략(Le 2022)·열구결손 GBR(Lee 2025)이 hard+soft 복합관리로 확장.

## Summary

Peri-implant esthetics in the anterior (and increasingly posterior) zone is decided less by the definitive crown than by how the transmucosal **emergence profile** is shaped, held, and recorded during healing. This synthesis draws on six papers to trace a single clinical arc — from the **conceptual framework** that classifies the transmucosal contour into zones, through the **physical devices** that execute that framework chairside or digitally, to the **capture problem** created by the emergence profile's own instability — plus two adjacent papers that supply the stability and augmentation context in which conditioning happens.

The tight core is three papers. Gomez-Meda et al. (2021) provides the vocabulary: the **Esthetic Biological Contour (EBC)** organizes the emergence profile into four zones, of which the **critical contour** (Zone 3, at the junctional epithelium) governs the gingival margin position and the **subcritical contour** (Zone 2) governs crestal bone stability. Ruales-Carrera et al. (2019) and Azpiazu-Flores et al. (2025) then execute that framework — the former with a chairside **customized healing abutment (CHA)** placed at immediate surgery, the latter with a **3D-printed emergence-profile former (EPF)** that doubles as a scan-body surrogate. Li et al. (2019) supplies the reason the whole workflow must be disciplined: the emergence profile collapses the instant the interim restoration is removed, so the contour must be captured *before* it distorts.

The two looser-fitting papers frame the surgical context. Han et al. (2025) tracks a dual-thread implant macrodesign for esthetic-zone immediate placement — its value here is quantitative recession and buccal-bone data, not contour design. Lim et al. (2022) is a canine histologic comparison of GBR vs connective tissue graft vs their combination, informing which augmentation actually adds tissue *coronal to the implant shoulder* — the exact dimension the emergence profile then sculpts.

Five further papers extend this arc along three lines. On the **material-choice axis**, Ríos-Osorio et al. (2025) contribute the first SR+MA-tier evidence comparing xenogeneic collagen matrices against autogenous grafts, splitting the XCM class into crosslinked and non-crosslinked variants — a resolution the earlier case-level core papers could not offer. Blašković & Blašković (2021) supply the surrounding procedural taxonomy (FGG, CTG, pedicle grafts, APF, roll technique, tunneling, xenogeneic substitutes) that frames where the Ríos-Osorio comparison sits. Le & Hayashi (2022) add a technique-level counterpoint — the **Aesthetic Contour Graft** — arguing that many soft-tissue deficiencies are secondarily bone-driven, so GBR-based labial bone augmentation can substitute for a separate CTG. On the **hard-tissue-complement axis**, Lee & Lee (2025) show, in a dog dehiscence-GBR model, that soft block bone substitute packing technique (loose L-shaped vs compact) makes no difference to bone width and that non-fixed membranes fail coronally regardless — extending the synthesis's soft-tissue focus into combined hard+soft dehiscence management and reinforcing that mechanical membrane stabilization, not graft-handling technique, is the binding constraint. On the **digital-capture axis**, Monaco et al. (2016) is an early precedent for the same capture problem Li (2019) later quantifies: a two-scan (scanbody + provisional, in situ then subgingival) STL-superimposition technique that predates and foreshadows the customized-scan-body logic used by Azpiazu-Flores (2025).

**핵심 명제 (Evidence Propositions)**

1. **The transmucosal emergence profile divides into a critical and a subcritical contour with opposite biological jobs.** Gomez-Meda et al. (2021) formalize this as the EBC 4-zone model: the critical contour (Zone 3, immediately supra-junctional) controls the free gingival margin — added convexity displaces the margin apically (recession risk), while concavity elevates it coronally — whereas the subcritical contour (Zone 2) governs crestal bone, where a concave-to-neutral shape maintains bone and an over-convex shape raises bone-loss and inflammation risk. This is the design grammar every subsequent device obeys. [확인 — 개념 합의]

2. **The provisional/healing abutment — not the definitive crown — is the primary tissue-shaping instrument.** All three core papers (Gomez-Meda 2021, Ruales-Carrera 2019, Azpiazu-Flores 2025) treat contour development as a healing-phase task executed by an interim device, with the final restoration merely copying the matured profile. [확인]

3. **The emergence profile is a labile dimension that collapses within seconds of interim-restoration removal, while papilla and gingival margin are stable.** Li et al. (2019), a prospective study of 12 esthetic-zone sites in 10 patients with serial intraoral scans, found the emergence profile discrepancy was already significant at 0 seconds and recovered as a logarithmic function of time (∝ ln[time]), whereas mesial papilla, distal papilla, and gingival margin all changed ≤0.27 mm over 20 minutes. Practical consequence: **impress/scan with the interim restoration in place or with a customized scan body replicating the profile** — a bare stock scan body records a collapsed contour. [확인 — prospective, 소표본]

4. **A chairside customized healing abutment reproduces both critical and subcritical contours at immediate placement, eliminating provisionals and reopening surgery.** Ruales-Carrera et al. (2019) fabricated CHAs from a PEEK/PMMA block plus a titanium insert, matched to the extracted tooth's emergence geometry, and used the dual-zone (Chu 2012) and critical/subcritical (Su 2010) frameworks to shape papilla-supporting subcritical and margin-defining critical zones. Indicated for Type 1 (immediate) placement when primary stability is insufficient for immediate loading. Evidence is case-series/qualitative — no PES/WES or recession numbers. [사례수준]

5. **A 3D-printed emergence-profile former can unify soft-tissue conditioning and digital registration on a stock titanium healing abutment.** Azpiazu-Flores et al. (2025) designed an EPF in free software (Meshmixer, 0.02 mm Boolean offset) that seats on a stock healing abutment collar, requires no temporary abutment screw, and serves simultaneously as tissue conditioner and scan-body surrogate — delivering an esthetic mandibular-molar crown despite horizontal buccal deficiency and only 1 mm attached gingiva, without buccal grafting. Single case. [사례수준]

6. **Connective tissue graft, not bone augmentation, builds mucosa coronal to the implant shoulder — and combining them is not additive.** Lim et al. (2022), a randomized canine histomorphometric study (5 dogs, 4 sites each, immediate narrow implants, 4-month non-submerged healing), ranked mucosal height as SCTG 1.99 > GBR 1.45 > control 1.40 > GBR+SCTG 1.24 mm, with GBR producing mineralized tissue only apical to the shoulder and the combination performing worst. All differences were non-significant (underpowered, 2 implant losses) — a mechanistic signal, not proof, that soft-tissue grafting is the coronal value-add. [동물·NS — 신호 수준]

7. **Macrodesign-driven primary stability can improve midfacial recession in esthetic-zone immediate placement, but this is design-specific evidence.** Han et al. (2025), a single-arm prospective study of a dual-thread 11° internal-hex implant (n=20, 17 analyzed), reported 85% success / 100% survival, ISQ exceeding 70 by ~70 days, midfacial recession improving 1.86 → 1.21 mm, and 1-year buccal-bone thickness loss of −0.34 to −0.48 mm (gap grafted with bovine bone mineral + collagen membrane). Single-manufacturer, single-arm, 1-year — generalizability open. [prospective 단일군]

8. **Crosslinked xenogeneic collagen matrix (VCMX) matches CTG for mucosal thickness, but non-crosslinked XCM and FGG remain the gold standards for thickness and keratinized width respectively.** Ríos-Osorio et al. (2025), an SR+MA of 17 RCTs (14 meta-analyzed), found non-crosslinked XCM* significantly inferior to CTG for mucosal thickness (MD −0.27 mm, P=0.01) while crosslinked VCMX showed no significant difference from CTG (MD −0.02 mm, P=0.83); FGG remained superior to XCM* for keratinized mucosa width (MD +1.47 mm, P=0.0006). This is the first SR+MA-tier resolution of the graft-material-choice question this synthesis's core papers left open. [확인 — SR+MA, 17 RCT]

9. **The full soft-tissue-augmentation taxonomy — and the "bone drives soft tissue" counter-principle — frame where graft-material choice sits.** Blašković & Blašković (2021), a narrative procedural review, catalogs FGG, CTG/subepithelial CTG, pedicle grafts, apically-positioned flap, roll technique, tunneling, and xenogeneic substitutes against KM ≥2 mm and soft-tissue-thickness ≥2 mm thresholds and three graft-survival conditions (immobilization, bed contact, vascularity). Le & Hayashi (2022), a clinical technique article, argues the converse emphasis: most peri-implant soft-tissue deficiency has an underlying osseous cause, so labial GBR ("Aesthetic Contour Graft") with particulate allograft + collagen membrane can thicken the overlying mucosa directly — in staged or simultaneous protocols, with tenting screws to preserve crestal space — sometimes obviating a separate CTG, with contours reported stable to 8 years in illustrative cases. [narrative-review — 술식 수준]

10. **Soft block bone substitute packing technique does not affect GBR outcomes for peri-implant dehiscence, but membrane fixation does.** Lee & Lee (2025), a dog study (4 dogs, 32 implants, 8 weeks), compared L-shaped loosely-packed vs compactly-packed soft collagen-block bone substitute in non-fixed-membrane GBR for buccal dehiscence defects: no significant bone-width difference between packing techniques at any level, but both groups showed near-zero bone at the implant shoulder — confirming that without membrane fixation (pins/tacks), coronal bone regeneration fails regardless of graft-handling technique. This extends the synthesis beyond pure soft-tissue conditioning into combined hard+soft dehiscence management, complementing Lim (2022)'s finding that GBR alone does not build tissue coronal to the shoulder. Micro-CT correlated strongly with histomorphometry (r=0.971, bias +0.31 mm), validating radiographic surrogate assessment for this class of pre-clinical study. [animal — 신호 수준]

11. **A two-scan digital-impression technique for capturing provisional-conditioned emergence profile predates and foreshadows the customized scan-body/EPF logic.** Monaco et al. (2016), a technique short communication, described the Fully Digital Technique (FDT): a scanbody scan (STL1) and a provisional-restoration scan taken both in situ and immediately after removal to record the subgingival contour (STL2), superimposed via best-fit algorithm into a single merged file (STL3) that drives CAD/CAM abutment design without a conventional impression. No quantitative accuracy or PES/MBL data were reported. This is an early precedent for the capture problem Li (2019) later quantifies kinetically, and for the unified conditioning-plus-registration logic Azpiazu-Flores (2025) executes with a 3D-printed EPF. [narrative/technique — 사례 수준]

| 연조직 증대 재료 선택 | Ríos-Osorio 2025 | 볼륨(MT)은 CTG 우선, 단 가교형 XCM(VCMX)은 동등 대체 가능; 각화폭(KMW)은 FGG가 XCM* 대비 우위 유지 |
| 골 우선 vs 연조직 우선 전략 | Le 2022 · Blašković 2021 | 순측골 결손이 원인이면 GBR(Aesthetic Contour Graft)로 골 우선 교정 시 별도 CTG 불필요할 수 있음; 폰틱 부위도 동일 원리 적용 |
| 열구결손 GBR 골대체재 적용법 | Lee 2025 | Soft BBS의 L형 느슨 vs 압축 적용은 무차이 — 막 고정(핀·택) 없이는 두 방식 모두 견부 골유지 실패 |
| 디지털 캡처 초기 선례 | Monaco 2016 | 스캔바디+임시보철 이중스캔 STL 중첩(FDT) — Li의 붕괴 문제·Azpiazu-Flores의 통합 캡처 로직의 토대 |

### 공백·미해결 (Open Questions)

- **정량 심미결과 부재**: 맞춤 지대주 core(Ruales-Carrera, Azpiazu-Flores)는 전부 case/qualitative — PES/WES·퇴축 정량치 없음.
- **준임계 오목 vs 중립** head-to-head 미검증 — 인용된 연구에서 주장될 뿐 이 페이지들 안에서 직접 비교 없음.
- **디지털 캡처 3방식**(임시보철 유지 / 맞춤 scan body / 3D EPF) 등록 정확도 비교 부재 — Monaco(2016)가 초기 선례를, Li(2019)는 "왜", Azpiazu-Flores(2025)는 하나의 "어떻게"를 제시하나 상호 정량 비교는 여전히 없음.
- **GBR+SCTG 비가산성**(Lim)은 underpowered·NS — 기전 신호이지 증명 아님; 출현윤곽 컨디셔닝과의 상호작용 미검증.
- **재료 비교 공백은 부분 해결**: Ríos-Osorio(2025) SR+MA가 XCM 하위유형(가교/비가교) vs CTG/FGG 우열을 근거강함 수준으로 확립. 남은 공백은 이 재료 비교가 gomez-meda의 4-zone 윤곽설계·맞춤 치유지대주 프로토콜과 어떻게 상호작용하는지(예: 준임계윤곽 재현에 VCMX가 CTG를 대체할 수 있는지) — 아직 직접 검증 없음.
- **골 우선(Le/Blašković) vs 연조직 우선(gomez-meda/rios-osorio) 전략 간 head-to-head 부재**: "골이 연조직을 이끈다" 원리와 core 3편의 "임시보철·치유지대주가 1차 성형 도구" 원리가 상호배타적인지, 상호보완적인지 직접 비교 연구 없음.
