## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 투명교정의 치아이동 정확도는 이동 유형에 따라 체계적으로 다르며, 수직 이동(압하·정출)은 어떤 브랜드에서도 신뢰할 수 없다; 리파인먼트(refinement)는 실패가 아니라 예정된 치료 단계다.
> - **이동 유형별 정확도 서열 (일관)**: 협설 경사(bucco-lingual tipping) > 근원심 경사(mesiodistal tipping) > 회전(rotation) > 압하/정출(intrusion/extrusion) ≥ 확장(expansion) — 낮을수록 과교정(over-correction) 필요.
> - **회전 정확도**: AlBaqshi 2025 SR — Invisalign Progress Assessment 도구가 실제 이동을 과대추정; Algarni 2026 (n=80) — Eon Aligner가 Invisalign보다 근원심 회전 우수 (차이 1.9–4.2°, 단 양쪽 모두 유의한 편향 존재).
> - **수직 이동 신뢰불가**: Algarni 2026 — Invisalign 수직이동 "신뢰불가 (unreliable)", Eon Aligner 중간이나 변동성 있음 — **두 시스템 모두 수직 이동을 얼라이너 단독으로 계획해선 안 됨**.
> - **횡적 폭경 변화 신뢰**: 양 브랜드 모두 횡적 폭경(소구치간·견치간) 평균차 0.06–0.34 mm — Camardella 임상적 유의성 기준(0.4 mm) 이내; 브랜드 간 차이 없음.
> - **리파인먼트 드라이버 6가지** (Nogueira-Matos 2026, 20편 SR): ①증례 복잡도 ②발치 치료 ③IPR 전략 ④심한 이격(severe spacing) ⑤큰 교정량 ⑥이동 유형 한계 (회전·압하·수직·확장).
> - **원격 모니터링의 한계**: 내원 횟수 감소 → 리파인먼트 필요성 감소시키지 못함 (Nogueira-Matos 2026).
> - **순응도 예측인자**: AlBaqshi 2025 — 남성·교정 경험 없는 환자가 순응도 높음; 착용 시간 부족이 1위 실패 원인; Invisalign Progress Assessment 도구가 이동 과대추정.
> - **실용적 과교정 원칙**: 소프트웨어 계획에서 회전은 3–5° 이상, 압하는 대부분 추가 보조장치(어태치먼트·엘라스틱)와 병행; 수직 이동 단독 계획 금지.
> - **근거 한계**: AlBaqshi 2025는 6편(Cureus 발행, 낮은 심사 기준 주의); Nogueira-Matos 2026은 리파인먼트 정의가 20개 연구에서 모두 다름; Algarni 2026은 단일 기관 후향 연구 n=80.
> - **임상 takeaway**: "투명교정 계획 시 수직·회전 이동은 과교정+보조장치를 초기부터 반영, 리파인먼트를 전제로 환자 상담 — 원격 모니터링은 편의지 리파인먼트 예방책이 아니다".

## Three-line Summary

Clear aligner tooth movement accuracy follows a consistent hierarchy — bucco-lingual tipping (most accurate) > mesiodistal tipping > rotation/intrusion/expansion (least accurate) — with vertical movements rated unreliable for both Invisalign and Eon Aligner, while transverse arch-width changes remain within clinical relevance thresholds for both systems (mean difference 0.06–0.34 mm; Algarni 2026 n=80).

Refinement after the first aligner series is a multifactorial but predictable outcome, not a treatment failure: six independent drivers — case complexity, extraction, IPR strategy, severe spacing, correction magnitude, and movement limitations — account for refinement burden, while remote monitoring reduces appointment frequency but does not reduce refinement need (Nogueira-Matos 2026 SR, 20 studies).

Compliance is the primary modifier of all outcomes, driven by wear time (primary failure driver), patient sex (males more compliant), and absence of prior orthodontic experience; systematic over-correction of rotation, intrusion, and expansion movements from initial planning is the evidence-supported response to low accuracy in these movement types.

## 세줄요약

이동 유형별 정확도 서열 일관 — 협설 경사(최고) > 근원심 경사 > 회전·압하·확장(최저); 수직 이동은 두 브랜드 모두 신뢰불가; 횡적 폭경은 임상적 유의성 기준 이내 (Algarni 2026, n=80).

리파인먼트는 실패가 아니라 예측 가능한 단계: 6가지 독립 드라이버로 설명되며, 원격 모니터링은 내원 줄이지만 리파인먼트 필요성은 줄이지 않음 (Nogueira-Matos 2026 SR, 20편).

순응도는 모든 결과의 1차 수정인자 — 착용 시간 부족이 1위 실패 원인; 회전·압하·확장의 초기 과교정 반영이 근거 기반 계획 원칙.

## 3. Tooth Movement Accuracy Hierarchy

All three studies converge on the same movement-type hierarchy, consistent with the established biomechanical understanding:

### 3.1 Most Accurate — Bucco-lingual tipping
- Soft tissue-guided, aligner material most effective
- Both Invisalign and Eon Aligner achieve good agreement with predicted values
- Clinical implication: can be planned without systematic over-correction

### 3.2 Moderate Accuracy — Mesiodistal tipping
- Moderate agreement; slightly more variance than bucco-lingual
- Clinical implication: over-correction of 1–2° recommended in complex cases

### 3.3 Low Accuracy — Rotation
- Significant systematic bias between predicted and achieved (both brands, all studies)
- Algarni 2026: Eon Aligner outperforms Invisalign for mesiodistal rotation (differences 1.9–4.2°), though both show statistically significant prediction overestimation
- AlBaqshi 2025: rotation listed as lowest accuracy movement class
- Clinical implication: systematic over-correction of 3–5° or more; attachments are typically required

### 3.4 Low Accuracy — Intrusion / Extrusion
- **Vertical movement is unreliable for both Invisalign and Eon Aligner (Algarni 2026)**
- Invisalign: described as "unreliable" for vertical crown movement
- Eon Aligner: moderate but highly variable (except first premolars)
- Butsabul 2024 (EARR context): intrusion/extrusion is the primary risk amplifier for root resorption
- Clinical implication: **do not plan vertical movements with aligners alone — adjuncts mandatory (attachments, vertical elastics, overcorrection series)**

### 3.5 Low Accuracy — Expansion
- Both transverse expansion and rotation share lowest accuracy tier
- Transverse arch-width is measured differently from pure expansion: see 3.6 below

### 3.6 Note on Transverse Arch Width vs Expansion
An important distinction exists between **arch width measurement** (interpremolar/intercanine width) and planned **skeletal/dentoalveolar expansion**:

- **Arch width agreement**: Algarni 2026 — both brands show excellent agreement: mean differences 0.06–0.34 mm, within Camardella's 0.4 mm clinical relevance threshold, no significant between-brand difference. This is the reassuring finding.
- **Large-scale expansion** (>4–5 mm planned): evidence from other studies (not in this SR synthesis) shows lower success rates and higher relapse risk
- Clinical implication: modest arch-width changes are reliably delivered; large-scale arch development requires caution and should reference the growing-children evidence ([[mathew-2026-clear-aligners-growing-children-umbrella-review]])

## 4. Refinement: Drivers and What Cannot Prevent It

### 4.1 Six Key Refinement Drivers (Nogueira-Matos 2026)
1. **Malocclusion complexity**: higher severity score → higher refinement burden
2. **Extraction treatment**: space closure creates complex tooth movement trajectories
3. **IPR strategy**: both amount and sequencing affect residual space management
4. **Severe spacing**: large gaps require extensive mesiodistal movement
5. **Large correction sequence**: more planned steps → more accumulated prediction error
6. **Movement type limitations**: rotation, intrusion, vertical, transverse expansion

### 4.2 What Remote Monitoring Does NOT Do
Remote monitoring (asynchronous digital check-in) reduces **appointment frequency** and **chair time** — it does NOT reduce the need for refinement scans or additional aligner sets. Patients and clinicians should not expect remote monitoring to improve prediction accuracy; it is a convenience tool, not a clinical outcome modifier.

### 4.3 Refinement as Treatment Planning Component
The key clinical reframe from Nogueira-Matos 2026: refinement should be **built into the consent and scheduling** from day one, not presented as a complication or failure. For complex cases (extraction, rotations >10°, vertical corrections), plan for at least one refinement series.

## 5. Compliance: Primary Outcome Modifier

### 5.1 Compliance Predictors (AlBaqshi 2025 SR)
- **Higher compliance**: male gender, patients without prior orthodontic experience
- **Primary failure cause**: inadequate wear time (not achieving 20–22 hours/day)
- Comfort, esthetics, and shorter duration compared to fixed appliances positively influence compliance

### 5.2 Digital Monitoring Tool Bias
The **Invisalign Progress Assessment** tool overestimates actual tooth movement — this means that software-reported "on track" assessments may not reflect true clinical progress. Clinicians should not rely solely on the digital tracker; periodic intraoral comparison against physical ITero models or physical aligner seating tests is necessary.

## 6. Brand Comparison: Invisalign vs Eon Aligner

Algarni 2026 is the first study to directly compare the two brands in a clinical (not just virtual-setup) context:

| Movement | Invisalign | Eon Aligner | Between-brand significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interpremolar width | 0.20–0.34 mm deviation | 0.06–0.20 mm deviation | NS |
| Intercanine width | Comparable | Comparable | NS |
| Mesiodistal rotation (incisors) | Significant bias (predicted > achieved) | Significant bias, but smaller (1.9–4.2° better) | Eon better |
| Horizontal movement (canines/premolars) | Mean 0.206–0.341 mm difference | Mean 0.015–0.24 mm difference | Eon better |
| Vertical movement | Unreliable | Moderate, fluctuating (1st PM: moderate) | No clear winner |

**Clinical interpretation:** The brand difference in rotation and horizontal movement (Eon advantage) exists at a statistically significant level but with absolute differences in the 2–4° range for rotation. Whether this is clinically meaningful for a given patient depends on the specific movements planned; neither brand should be considered universally superior for all movement types.

## 7. Planning Principles: Overcorrection Guidelines

Based on synthesized accuracy data:

| Movement | Over-correction recommendation |
|---|---|
| Bucco-lingual tipping | None required for routine cases |
| Mesiodistal tipping | 1–2° in complex cases |
| Rotation | ≥3–5° systematically; attachments typically required |
| Intrusion / extrusion | Do not plan with aligners alone; always use attachments + vertical elastics |
| Arch expansion (>3 mm) | Plan 20–30% excess; consider combined approach in adults |

## 8. Research Gaps

1. **Standardized refinement definition**: "refinement" defined differently across all 20 studies in Nogueira-Matos 2026 — no single accepted clinical or research definition exists
2. **Eon Aligner RCT**: Algarni 2026 is retrospective, single-center, n=80 — needs RCT-level confirmation of rotational advantage
3. **AI/ML refinement prediction**: mentioned as preliminary in Nogueira-Matos 2026 — no SR-level validation exists
4. **Compliance interventions**: AlBaqshi 2025 notes wear time is #1 failure driver, but evidence on effective compliance-improvement strategies is thin; audio-visual reminders studied in single RCTs only
5. **Complex movement combinations**: no study isolates combined rotation + intrusion (the most difficult predicted scenario); existing data addresses single-movement-type accuracy
