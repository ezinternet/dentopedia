## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **위치**: 이 페이지는 새 사다리(ladder)가 아니라 **업데이트 층**이다. 기존 [[overviews/conservative-physical-therapy-myofascial-tmd-ladder]]·[[overviews/tmd-management-evidence-ladder]]·[[overviews/btx-orofacial-applications-overview]]·[[overviews/occlusal-splint-tmd-value-adjunct-prediction-overview]] 위에 근육형 턱관절장애(Myogenous Temporomandibular Disorder, M-TMD) 관련 SR/MA/NMA 24편(전문 6편·**초록만 18편**)을 얹어 확인·정밀화·충돌을 판정한다.
> - **결론(thesis)**: 24편은 사다리 순서를 뒤집지 않는다 — 능동치료(도수치료(Manual Therapy, MT)·운동·심리행동) 우선, 장치·전기물리는 보조, 보툴리눔독소 A(Botulinum Toxin Type A, BoNT-A)는 불응 시. 바뀌는 것은 **확실성 수준과 개별 모달리티의 등급**이다.
> - **도수치료 — 확인, 단 확실성 하향**: 네트워크 메타분석(Network Meta-Analysis, NMA) 3편 중 2편에서 1위(Al-Moraissi 2021 단기 83.5%, Menéndez-Torre 2023 SUCRA 0.932), Zhang 2026에선 2위(79.9%). 그러나 근막이완(Myofascial Release, MFR) 전용 SR은 GRADE 낮음·최소임상중요차(Minimal Clinically Important Difference, MCID 15–22 mm) 넘은 시험은 일부뿐 (Ferrillo 2026, 7 RCT).
> - **NMA 1위 충돌**: Al-Moraissi 2021(52 RCT, 2018년까지, 위약 대조) = MT 1위 vs Zhang 2026(41 RCT, 2025년까지, 통상치료 대조) = 광생체조절치료(Photobiomodulation, PBM) 1위(SUCRA 88.9%). 비교군·검색시기·노드 구성이 달라 **직접 모순이 아님** — MT는 두 NMA 모두 상위 2위 안이라 견고, PBM 1위는 2018년 이후 시험에 의존.
> - **레이저/PBM — 정밀화(하향)**: Candido-do-Prado 2025 통증 SMD −0.84(18 RCT, 초록만)는 긍정이나, Wu 2021 갈륨알루미늄비소(GaAlAs) 레이저는 종료 시 WMD −0.76(95% CI −1.51~0.01, P=.046 — **CI가 0에 닿는 내부 불일치**)이고 3–4주 후 소실·개구량(MMO) 변화 없음; Ahmad 2021은 37 RCT 중 18편 유효·12편 위약 동등. → "통증 완화엔 PBM 우선"은 과대 — **단기 보조**로 읽을 것.
> - **건침(Dry Needling, DN)·침술 — 충돌, 하향**: Al-Moraissi 2022 NMA에서 침술·DN 모두 통증·압통역치(Pressure Pain Threshold, PPT)가 위약과 차이 없음(DN은 MMO만 ↑, 매우 낮음). Tesch 2019의 PPT +44.6% vs −5.5%는 **n=5 예비시험**. 기존 사다리 3단계의 "DN↔분사신장 상호교환"은 유지하되 DN 통증효과는 대부분 비특이적(위약)으로 표기해야 한다.
> - **BoNT-A — 충돌의 해소**: De Santana 2026(10 RCT, 2025.12까지, 전문)은 위약 대비 **일관된 우월성 없음**, 불응성 근막통 시험에서만 ~50% 감소; 효과 8–24주(정점 8–16주), 총 40–100 U에서 용량-반응 없음. 단 본문("전 연구 무차이")과 Table 2(불응군 우월)가 **내부 불일치**. Al-Moraissi 2021의 ≥6개월 1위(85.8%)는 매우 낮음, Menéndez-Torre에선 최하위(0.103). → 선택된 불응 환자 한정의 마지막 단계로 재확인.
> - **교합안정장치(Stabilization Splint) — 확인**: Honnef 2022(매우 낮음, 대안과 동등)·Boulatar 2026(낮음, 기저치 대비 호전뿐)·Orzeszek 2023(13 RCT 전부 고위험 비뚤림, 우월성 근거 불충분) 세 SR 모두 같은 2010–2022 시험 풀에서 나와 **독립 확증 3건이 아니라 사실상 1건**. 교육·상담이 자발통에서 장치보다 나았던 RCT 1편(p=0.034).
> - **심리·교육 — 확인·확장**: 심리치료 ≈ 표준치료, 심리+표준+도수 병용 > 상담+표준(매우 낮음; Christidis 2024); 인지행동치료(Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, CBT)·통증신경과학교육(Pain Neuroscience Education, PNE)+운동·MT가 통증 파국화(Pain Catastrophizing)를, PNE·MT가 운동공포(Kinesiophobia)를 줄일 수 있음, 장치·최면은 무효(Dinsdale 2025). 근막통 아형에서 불안·우울이 더 흔함(p=.001; Reis 2022).
> - **기타 — 임상 밖**: 오존 7 시험 중 3편 고위험(Machado 2025); 미세전류(Microcurrent Electrical Nerve Stimulation, MENS) −0.57점, I² 83.7%, 3편(Bavarian 2021); 국소 칸나비디올(Cannabidiol, CBD) 근막통 긍정 신호만(Grossman 2021); 마그네슘은 TMD 시험 0편·성인 경련 MD −0.42/주 NS(Patil 2026).
> - **진단·위험 층(치료가 아니라 선별을 바꿈)**: 근막성 구강안면통 유병률 20.60%(5편, CI 9.71–38.50)·근막+TMJ 11.95%(31편; Zieliński 2026); 교근 강성 Hedges g 1.485·+6.16 kPa(매우 낮음, 역치 없음; Naralan 2026); 유전 다형성 OR 2.46(유전자검사 근거 아님; Alshahrani 2024); 운동선수 vs 비운동선수 차이 없음·마우스가드 사용과 증상 감소 연관(Saini 2024).
> - **임상 결론**: 일반 치과의사의 기본값은 교육+자가관리+운동+MT(가능 시)+심리 선별 → 빠른 통증 완화가 필요하면 스플린트 또는 PBM을 *단기 보조*로 → 불응·선택된 환자에서만 BoNT-A(효과 ~2–6개월, 재주사·근위축 동의). DN·오존·MENS·CBD·마그네슘은 기본 경로에 넣지 않는다.

## Three-line Summary

This page is an update layer, not a new ladder: 24 newly ingested SR/MA/NMA papers (6 full-text, 18 abstract-only) on myogenous/myofascial masticatory muscle pain are judged against the four existing TMD overviews. They confirm the active-first order (manual therapy ranks top-2 in all three treatment NMAs, but MFR-specific GRADE is only low), while pooled splint, laser, needling and BoNT-A evidence is downgraded or qualified.

Key conflicts resolve on comparator and population rather than true contradiction. Al-Moraissi 2021 (MT first, placebo comparator, search to 2018) and Zhang 2026 (PBM first, conventional-care comparator, search to 2025) used different networks. Acupuncture and dry needling equal placebo for pain (Al-Moraissi 2022), with only a 5-patient PPT signal on the other side (Tesch 2019). BoNT-A shows no consistent superiority over saline except in refractory myofascial pain (De Santana 2026; 8–24 weeks, 40–100 U, no dose-response). GaAlAs laser gives a borderline, transient effect (Wu 2021: WMD −0.76, CI touching 0, gone at 3–4 weeks), while the broader PBM pool is positive (Candido-do-Prado 2025: SMD −0.84). The three muscle-subtype splint SRs (Honnef/Boulatar/Orzeszek) draw on one overlapping trial pool, all at low to very-low certainty.

Epidemiology and risk papers (myofascial prevalence 20.6%, masseter stiffness g 1.485, polymorphism OR 2.46, athletes not at higher risk, more anxiety/depression in myofascial pain) change screening, not treatment. Concrete edits are proposed for the existing overviews: downgrade dry needling, soften "PBM first for pain", add De Santana to the BoNT-A rung, and add low-GRADE/MCID caveats to manual therapy.

## 세줄요약

새 사다리가 아닌 업데이트 층: 근육형 저작근 통증 SR/MA/NMA 24편(전문 6·초록만 18)을 기존 TMD overview 4편과 대조 — 능동치료 우선 순서는 확인(MT는 치료 NMA 3편 모두 상위 2위)되나, MFR 전용 GRADE는 낮음이고 스플린트·레이저·건침·BoNT-A는 하향 또는 단서 추가.

핵심 충돌은 진짜 모순이 아니라 비교군·대상군 차이: MT 1위(Al-Moraissi 2021, 위약 대조, ~2018) vs PBM 1위(Zhang 2026, 통상치료 대조, ~2025); 침술·DN 통증은 위약 수준(Al-Moraissi 2022) vs n=5 PPT 신호(Tesch 2019); BoNT-A는 불응 근막통에서만 우월(De Santana 2026, 8–24주, 40–100 U 용량-반응 없음); GaAlAs 레이저 경계적·일시적(Wu 2021, CI 0 접촉) vs PBM 통합 SMD −0.84(Candido-do-Prado 2025); 스플린트 SR 3편은 같은 시험 풀(낮음~매우 낮음).

역학·위험 논문(근막통 유병률 20.6%, 교근 강성 g 1.485, 유전 다형성 OR 2.46, 운동선수 위험 증가 없음, 근막통 아형에서 불안·우울 증가)은 치료가 아니라 선별을 바꿈 — 기존 overview 수정 제안: DN 하향, "통증엔 PBM 우선" 완화, BoNT-A 단계에 De Santana 추가, MT에 낮은 GRADE·MCID 단서.

## Explicit conflicts and how to read them

**1. Which NMA's #1 is right — MT (Al-Moraissi 2021) or PBM (Zhang 2026)?** They are not testing the same thing.
- Al-Moraissi compares against placebo or no treatment, includes injections, ozone and hypnosis, and searched to 2018.
- Zhang compares against conventional care, covers nonpharmacological care only, and searched to October 2025.
- Menéndez-Torre has no PBM node and puts MT first (0.932).
- MT is therefore top-2 in all three myogenous-TMD networks compared here (Al-Moraissi 2021, Menéndez-Torre 2023, Zhang 2026), and Yao's top-3 includes MT-type nodes. That is robust. PBM's first place appears only in the newest network and depends on post-2018 trials. It also faces Wu 2021's transient GaAlAs result, Brochado's "PBM+MT not better than MT", and Zhang's own publication-bias signal (Egger p<0.05, recorded in the master ladder).
- Reading: when the target is pain, PBM is a defensible *short-term* adjunct, but SUCRA rank is not certainty. Prefer MT, which also wins on MMO (SUCRA 92.9%).

**2. BoNT-A: "no consistent superiority over placebo" (De Santana 2026) vs older positive reviews (Zhu 2020, Saini 2024, Ramos-Herrada 2022).** Three factors reconcile these.
- **Population.** The positive signal concentrates in refractory myofascial pain, about 50% reduction with higher PPT in the refractory trial.
- **Placebo response.** Both arms improve in saline-controlled trials.
- **Date.** De Santana includes null and pilot trials that post-date the older SRs, such as Minston 2024, which was null at 2 months.
- De Santana itself is internally inconsistent. Its text says no difference "across all included studies", while Table 2 reports refractory superiority. Yurttutan 2019 is described differently in the table and in the text.
- The rankings disagree outright: Al-Moraissi 2021 puts BoNT-A first at ≥6 months (very low), and Menéndez-Torre puts it last (0.103).
- Reading: every source agrees on the *placement*, which is last-line after conservative failure in selected refractory patients. They disagree only on effect size, so the placement is what to act on.

**3. Dry needling: placebo-level (Al-Moraissi 2022) vs positive pilot (Tesch 2019).** Tesch is a 2-trial review plus a 5-patient pilot with a surrogate outcome (PPT), so it cannot outweigh a placebo-anchored NMA. Menéndez-Torre's "DN ≈ MT" is an indirect comparison and does not show DN beats sham. Reading: DN's pain effect is mostly non-specific. If DN is used, the defensible goal is mobility (MMO), and patients should be told that the evidence is very low.

**4. Laser: Wu 2021 borderline vs Candido-do-Prado 2025 SMD −0.84.**
- The metrics differ. Wu reports WMD on VAS; the page does not state the scale. Candido-do-Prado reports SMD, so the magnitudes are not comparable.
- The scope differs. Wu covers one laser class (GaAlAs) in myofascial pain only. Candido-do-Prado covers all wavelengths, with articular trials the most homogeneous.
- Wu has internal inconsistencies: a CI upper bound of 0.01 with P=.046, and an abstract that says "insufficient evidence" against conclusions that say "moderate evidence".
- Reading: laser benefit is sensitive to parameters and trial selection. Candido-do-Prado's strongest subgroup is articular, not muscular TMD, so the muscle-pain case for PBM is weaker than its headline SMD suggests.

**5. Splints: three SRs, one signal.** Honnef rates the evidence very low, Boulatar low, and Orzeszek finds every trial at high RoB. They overlap in time and trial pool. They agree with each other and with Singh 2024 Cochrane and Cimino 2025. The only "moderate" splint label in the new set is Al-Moraissi 2021's appliance vs placebo comparison at short term. That answers "better than nothing", not "better than alternatives".

**6. Other internal inconsistencies recorded on the pages.**
- Patil 2026's pooled pregnancy RR 1.35 has no stated direction convention; a ratio above 1 normally means more events. Use it only as "a pregnancy signal of uncertain magnitude".
- Al-Moraissi 2021's printed ranking is not in descending order (71.7 after 71.2), and its conclusion names local anaesthetic (LA), which is not in the listed top four.
- Ferrillo 2026's trial table has arm sizes that do not sum (Ferreira).
- Orzeszek 2023 states two different search end dates.

## Diagnosis / epidemiology / risk layer — changes screening, not treatment

- **How common:**
  - Myofascial orofacial pain is 20.60% (CI 9.71–38.50), but rests on only 5 studies.
  - The better-supported figure is myofascial + TMJ pain at 11.95% (31 studies, 522,056 observations), consistent with Valesan 2021's painful-TMD estimate of about 11–13% (Zieliński 2026).
  - Practical meaning: roughly one in ten adults in a general practice has painful TMD, and muscle pain is the main share. Routine brief screening is justified. This matches the master ladder's axis 1.
- **Objective muscle marker:**
  - Masseter shear-wave stiffness is higher in bruxism and myofascial TMD (g 1.485; +6.16 kPa; Naralan 2026).
  - Certainty is very low, I² is about 84%, and there are no reference values. Shear-wave elastography (SWE) is a research or adjunct tool, not a diagnostic test, and palpation-based DC/TMD diagnosis stays the standard.
  - It complements post-BoNT-A elastography follow-up (Yan 2025).
- **Genetics:** polymorphism–TMD OR 2.46 (Alshahrani 2024). This is an association, not a predictor. It supports the biopsychosocial susceptibility model in Kapos 2020 and does not justify testing.
- **Sport:**
  - Athletes do not carry more TMD symptoms than non-athletes (pooled NS).
  - Mouthguard use is associated with fewer symptoms (Saini 2024, observational, no numbers). Do not read this as a mouthguard *treatment* effect for TMD.
  - Mouthguards are justified on injury grounds anyway (dental-trauma pages).
- **Psychological burden:**
  - Anxiety and depression are more frequent and more severe in myofascial pain than in other subtypes (Reis 2022).
  - Catastrophizing and kinesiophobia are modifiable targets (Dinsdale 2025).
  - Screening change: in muscle-dominant TMD, add a brief validated anxiety/depression screen and ask about fear of jaw movement and catastrophizing before choosing therapy. This is where CBT/PNE-type care has its best-supported role.

## Practical clinical reasoning for a general dentist

This section is reasoning built from held pages. It contains no new factual claims.

1. **Confirm the subtype.** Use DC/TMD palpation to identify myalgia or myofascial pain with referral. Separate it from arthralgia and disc conditions, where PBM's articular signal (Candido-do-Prado), splint-for-disc logic (Gu 2025) and arthrocentesis apply instead.
2. **Screen the psychosocial layer at the first visit.** Muscle-subtype patients carry more anxiety and depression (Reis). Catastrophizing and kinesiophobia respond to education, CBT and MT (Dinsdale).
3. **Default first line:** education and self-management, therapeutic jaw exercise, and manual therapy where available (in-house or by referral). The order is stable across Yao, Al-Moraissi 2021, Menéndez-Torre and Zhang, but expect modest effect sizes. Several MFR trials did not reach MCID (Ferrillo 2026), so set expectations as "improvement", not "cure".
4. **If the patient needs fast pain relief,** a stabilization splint or a PBM course is a reasonable *short-term* adjunct.
   - Neither has been shown to beat active alternatives in the long run (Salloum, Honnef, Orzeszek; Wu's effect gone at 3–4 weeks).
   - Choose by cost, access and patient preference.
   - Do not present either as the definitive treatment.
5. **Do not escalate to BoNT-A early.**
   - Reserve it for refractory myofascial pain after an adequate conservative trial.
   - Consent should cover these points: the effect lasts about 8–24 weeks, so re-injection is likely; no consistent dose-response has been shown within 40–100 U; muscle and bone adverse effects are documented in the umbrella review and the BTX overview.
6. **Do not offer as routine care:** dry needling or acupuncture for pain (placebo-level), ozone, MENS alone, topical CBD, or magnesium supplements. These can be discussed as investigational if the patient asks.
7. **Re-assess at 4–6 weeks,** per the master ladder. Persistent pain with high distress points to co-management with psychology or pain medicine rather than another device.

## What we still do not know (evidence gaps)

- Head-to-head trials of PBM vs MT, and of PBM added to MT beyond Brochado. Durability of PBM beyond 3–4 weeks in muscle pain.
- Isolated-MFR RCTs with sham control and MCID-anchored outcomes (Ferrillo 2026's explicit gap).
- Large sham-controlled DN trials in masticatory muscles with pain as the primary outcome. The base was 2 controlled trials in 2019.
- BoNT-A dose-finding in clearly defined refractory populations, and long-term (>6 months) RCT follow-up.
- Any RCT of electrolytes or magnesium in TMD myalgia, including the local masseter Mg injection signal.
- Reference values and diagnostic thresholds for masseter elastography.
- Splint trials with blinding-resistant designs, such as a placebo palatal plate, restricted to myogenous TMD.
- Full-text verification of the 18 abstract-only pages. Pooled numbers, heterogeneity and trial lists are missing for most.

## What would change this page

- A low-RoB RCT or NMA showing durable (≥3 months) PBM superiority over MT or exercise would justify a "PBM first for pain" rung.
- A large sham-controlled DN trial in masticatory muscles beating placebo on pain would restore DN as a core trigger-point option.
- A pooled RCT meta-analysis restricted to refractory myofascial pain with a dose arm would let BoNT-A carry an effect size and dose.
- Full-text retrieval of Candido-do-Prado 2025, Al-Moraissi 2021/2022 or Honnef 2022 that changes their abstract-level conclusions.
