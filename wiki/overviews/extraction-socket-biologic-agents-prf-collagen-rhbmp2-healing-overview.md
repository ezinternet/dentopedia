## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 발치와 생물학적 제제 선택은 목표에 따라 다르다 — 연조직 조기치유·통증관리엔 PRF·콜라겐, 수평·수직 골 보존엔 rhBMP-2; L-PRF는 자연치유 대비 6개월 이후 골 차원 우위가 없다.
> - **rhBMP-2 우월 근거**: Alavi 2024 SR+MA (29 RCT, 1,068명) — rhBMP-2 수평 흡수 유의 감소(p=0.01), 수직 흡수 유의 감소(p<0.0008); L-PRF 대비 수평 보존 우위(p<0.0001).
> - **L-PRF의 한계**: Alavi 2024 — L-PRF vs 자연치유 수평·수직 차이 **모두 NS**; 자가채혈·저비용 대안이지만 차원보존 우월 입증 없음.
> - **PRF 조기치유 근거**: Al-Maawi 2021 SR (20연구) — 통증 66.6% 연구에서 Day 1–3 유의 감소; 1주 연조직 치유 75% 개선; 골충전(bone fill) 8–15주 85% 향상 — **그러나 6개월에서 우위 없음**. PRF는 조기 2–3개월 특이 효과.
> - **흡연자에서 PRF**: Alrayyes 2022 RCT (4군, 18명 중증흡연자, 40 소켓) — A-PRF > 콜라겐 플러그 단독 연조직 폐합(p=0.012), 치유지수(p<0.0001); 흡연 유발 치유장애를 PRF가 부분 보완.
> - **콜라겐 vs Bio-Oss 창상치유**: Saliba 2020 (분할구강, n=13) — 콜라겐 협설 창상 폐합 우위(p=0.045); Bio-Oss 군에서 5일차 통증 더 높음(p=0.004); 콜라겐이 단기 창상치유와 통증관리에 유리.
> - **Bio-Oss collagen 박벽 소켓**: Fan 2021 (개 실험, 12주) — Bio-Oss collagen이 얇은 협측골 벽 소켓에서도 치조제 폭 보존; 조직학적 이종골 입자 잔류 확인.
> - **임상 목표별 제제 선택**: ①통증/연조직 조기치유 → 콜라겐·PRF; ②골충전 조기(2–3개월) → PRF; ③수평·수직 차원 장기보존 → rhBMP-2(비용), 콜라겐+이종골 병용; ④박벽 소켓 → Bio-Oss collagen; ⑤흡연자 → PRF > 콜라겐 플러그.
> - **혈액유래 제제(non-L-PRF)**: Alavi 2024 — 수직 보존 유의(p=0.001), 수평은 NS; 세부 조성별 효과 불명확.
> - **근거 한계**: Alavi 2024에서 15/29편 high RoB; Al-Maawi 2021 메타분석 불가(이질성); Fan 2021은 동물실험; Saliba 2020 소규모(n=13). GRADE 근거수준 낮음~중간.
> - **임상 takeaway**: "PRF와 콜라겐은 조기 치유·통증 관리 도구, rhBMP-2는 차원 보존 도구" — 임플란트 예정 부위 수평 차원 보존이 주목표이면 rhBMP-2가 현재 최고 근거; PRF는 비용효과적 조기치유 보조 (특히 흡연자, 고통증 예상 부위).

## Three-line Summary

In extraction socket management, biologic agents serve distinct mechanistic goals: PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin) and collagen optimize early soft tissue healing and pain (days 1–3 significant; 1-week tissue closure 75% improved), while rhBMP-2 alone demonstrates significant dimensional preservation at both horizontal (p=0.01) and vertical (p<0.0008) dimensions — L-PRF shows no significant difference from natural healing at either dimension (Alavi 2024 SR+MA, 29 RCTs, n=1,068).

Collagen plug outperforms xenograft (Bio-Oss) for wound closure (p=0.045) and early pain (day 5, p=0.004; Saliba 2020 split-mouth), while Bio-Oss collagen composite preserves ridge width even in thin-walled buccal bone sockets (Fan 2021 dog study); in heavy smokers, A-PRF surpasses collagen plug alone for soft tissue closure (p=0.012, p<0.0001; Alrayyes 2022 RCT).

The optimal socket management strategy is target-dependent: collagen or PRF for short-term comfort and soft tissue, rhBMP-2 or Bio-Oss collagen for dimensional ridge preservation, with PRF providing an autologous, cost-effective adjunct in high-risk healing contexts (smoking, expected high pain).

## 세줄요약

발치와 생물제제의 기전 목표는 다르다: PRF·콜라겐은 조기 연조직(1–3일 통증, 1주 창상 75% 개선)에 특이; rhBMP-2는 수평·수직 골 차원 보존 모두 유의(p=0.01·p<0.0008) — L-PRF는 자연치유 대비 차원 우위 없음 (Alavi 2024 SR+MA, 29 RCT, n=1,068).

콜라겐이 Bio-Oss보다 창상 폐합 우위(p=0.045)·통증 낮음(p=0.004; Saliba 2020); Bio-Oss collagen은 박벽 소켓에서도 치조제 폭 보존(Fan 2021); 흡연자에서 A-PRF > 콜라겐 단독(p=0.012; Alrayyes 2022).

임상 전략: 단기 통증·연조직 → 콜라겐·PRF; 장기 골 차원 보존 → rhBMP-2·Bio-Oss collagen; 흡연자 고위험 → PRF 우선.

This overview synthesizes the comparative evidence for PRF, collagen, xenograft collagen composites, and rhBMP-2 specifically in the fresh extraction socket context.

## 3. Agent-by-Agent Summary

### 3.1 Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF / A-PRF / L-PRF)

PRF is an autologous concentrate derived from the patient's own blood. Its biologic role is primarily **early phase** (days to 2–3 months):

- **Pain**: reduced in 66.6% of studies, most pronounced days 1–3 (Al-Maawi 2021)
- **Soft tissue healing**: 75% of studies show improved closure at 1 week
- **Early bone fill**: 85% of studies show higher socket fill at 8–15 weeks — but this advantage **disappears at 6 months** (no difference vs spontaneous healing)
- **Dimensional preservation**: L-PRF shows NO significant advantage vs natural healing for horizontal or vertical bone dimensions (Alavi 2024 SR+MA)
- **Smoker adaptation**: PRF compensates partially for smoking-impaired healing — A-PRF significantly superior to collagen plug alone in heavy smokers (Alrayyes 2022)
- **Conclusion**: PRF is a **comfort and early tissue adjunct**, not a dimensional preservation agent. Ideal for: high-pain-risk patients, smokers, early implant placement scheduling, or where quality of life post-extraction is a priority

### 3.2 rhBMP-2 (Recombinant Human Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2)

The only biologic agent with significant dimensional evidence in this comparison:

- Significant horizontal bone resorption reduction: p=0.01 (Alavi 2024)
- Significant vertical bone resorption reduction: p<0.0008 (Alavi 2024)
- Superior to L-PRF for horizontal preservation: p<0.0001 (Alavi 2024)
- Outperforms all blood-derived biologic agents tested
- **Limitation**: cost (commercial carrier + growth factor), off-label use in socket context, 15/29 RCTs in the SR had high risk of bias

### 3.3 Collagen (Absorbable Collagen Plug)

Collagen plugs (e.g., Collaplug) serve as scaffold and hemostatic agents:

- Better buccolingual wound closure than xenograft (p=0.045; Saliba 2020 split-mouth)
- Lower pain vs Bio-Oss + Surgicel on day 5 (p=0.004; Saliba 2020)
- Performs worse than A-PRF for soft tissue in heavy smokers (Alrayyes 2022: collagen < PRF groups)
- **Conclusion**: Collagen is the best passive material for wound healing and patient comfort when PRF preparation is not feasible (no centrifuge). It is inferior to PRF in high-risk healing contexts.

### 3.4 Bio-Oss Collagen Composite (Xenograft + Collagen Carrier)

Combining Bio-Oss particles with a collagen carrier addresses the biocompatibility tradeoff:

- Preserves ridge width even in challenging thin buccal wall sockets (Fan 2021 dog model, 12 weeks)
- Histology confirms xenograft particle retention at 12 weeks — scaffold role maintained
- More pain than collagen alone in the early phase (Saliba 2020 — Bio-Oss + Surgicel group)
- **Conclusion**: Bio-Oss collagen is indicated when the primary goal is **ridge width preservation** (thin buccal wall, immediate implant staging, aesthetic zone maintenance)

### 3.5 Blood-Derived Preparations (Non-L-PRF)

A heterogeneous class (CGF, PRP, combinations):
- Significant vertical preservation vs control: p=0.001 (Alavi 2024)
- Horizontal: NS
- Clinical interpretation limited by class heterogeneity in the SR

## 4. Clinical Decision Matrix

| Clinical Scenario | Recommended Agent | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Routine extraction, patient comfort primary | Collagen plug | Superior wound closure vs xenograft, low pain |
| Routine extraction, bone volume preservation | rhBMP-2 or Bio-Oss collagen | Only agents with dimensional evidence |
| Heavy smoker, soft tissue closure priority | A-PRF | PRF > collagen in smokers (p=0.012) |
| Thin buccal wall, ridge preservation | Bio-Oss collagen | Proven ridge width benefit even in thin-wall model |
| Early implant placement (8 wk), healing optimization | PRF | Best early tissue quality, cost-effective autologous |
| Long-term dimensional control, implant pre-ARP | rhBMP-2 | Only significant horizontal + vertical preservation |
| No centrifuge available, basic comfort | Collagen plug | Better than xenograft for wound healing/pain |

## 6. Research Gaps

1. **Long-term dimensional data for PRF**: no RCT follows PRF-treated sockets beyond 6 months for ridge dimensions; implant success in PRF sockets unstudied
2. **rhBMP-2 cost-effectiveness**: no published cost-utility analysis comparing rhBMP-2 to L-PRF or Bio-Oss for socket preservation in routine clinical settings
3. **Smoker long-term data**: Alrayyes 2022 is the only 4-arm smoker RCT; follow-up is 28 days only — no bone fill or implant data in heavy smokers with PRF
4. **Collagen vs Bio-Oss collagen head-to-head bone outcomes**: Saliba 2020 reports soft tissue and pain only; no radiographic bone fill comparison between collagen plug and Bio-Oss collagen at 6–12 months
5. **Fan 2021 translation**: animal model (dog); no human RCT data on Bio-Oss collagen in thin-wall buccal socket specifically
