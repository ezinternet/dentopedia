> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF)** = 보철 로딩 *전* 골유착 실패. 후기 실패(주위염·과부하)와 기전·위험인자가 다르다. 발생률: 임플란트 단위 약 1~4%, 환자 단위 약 3~7% (Yari 코호트 4.0%/7.0%; all-on-four 1년 0.4~1.1%, Uesugi).
> - **숙주 가변 위험인자 — 흡연**: 가장 일관된 레버. 풀링 교차비 (Odds Ratio, OR) 2.59 (Fan SR+MA 59,246개), 용량 의존적(Naseri), 다변량 보정 후에도 유의(Yari OR 1.84·Uesugi OR 2.92·Zhang 2025 OR 2.148). 단 Wåhlberg 2025(스웨덴 다기관, 실패 63건)에서는 **조기 실패에 비유의** (OR 1.61, p=0.12) — 조기 합병증에서만 유의(OR 2.30). 흡연 효과를 "실패"만으로 단정 말고 합병증까지 포함해 상담.
> - **부위별 절대 조기 임플란트 실패율 (Fouda 2020 SR+MA, 78,230개)**: 전방 상악 (anterior maxilla) **6.2%** (최고) → 후방 하악 (posterior mandible) ~4.2% → 후방 상악 (posterior maxilla) ~1.9% → 전방 하악 (anterior mandible) **2.5%** (최저). **결정적 발견**: 후방 상악 vs 후방 하악은 **유의차 없음** (RR 1.07, p=0.68) — 상악·하악 격차는 전방 상악이 만든다. "하악은 안전하다"는 후방 하악 실제 위험(~4.2%)을 가린다.
> - **해부·골질 인자**: 후방 상악·4형 골 (type IV bone)이 반복 검출 — Yari OR 2.96/2.88, Uesugi 상악 OR 3.12. 즉시식립 환경에서 전방 상악 위험 더욱 두드러짐(Yang 2026 HR 3.15).
> - **전신 인자**: 당뇨 상대위험 (Relative Risk, RR) 2.75·두경부 방사선 RR 2.73 (Moy); 골다공증 경향(Uesugi p=0.051). **항고혈압제 복용은 오히려 보호적** — 복용군 EIF 2.29% vs 정상혈압 4.33% (Masri), 시간당 위험비 (Hazard Ratio, HR) 0.23 (Chidiac); 스타틴도 HR 0.16 보호적. **SSRI는 위험 증가 HR 2.17** — 수술 전 약 복용 여부 확인 필수.
> - **부위 이력 인자**: 치주문제로 발치한 부위 (OR 2.53)·기존 골증대 부위 (OR 2.24)·부비동 증대 후 (Bonsmann nested case-control). 전용 SR+MA (Clauser 2022, 코호트 10편 풀링): 이식부(Bone-Grafted Site) 전체 OR **1.50** (95% CI 1.06–2.13) — 이식부 EIF 위험 증가의 첫 풀링 근거.
> - **술식 인자**: 즉시 잠정보철/즉시로딩 (immediate provisional) — Yari에서 가장 강한 단일 예측인자 (OR 3.42); 매몰형 치유가 조기 실패를 줄인다는 신호(Yang HR 0.53·Guarnieri)가 있지만 삽입 토크·임플란트 디자인 교란 배제 불가 — 흡연자에서의 프로토콜 차등 효과는 근거 없음.
> - **기전 근거 (Shibli 2024)**: 흡연자의 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) ~50% 낮음(p<0.05) — 골유착 *지연*의 직접 조직학적 관찰. 니코틴 혈관 수축 경로는 추론.
> - **예방 — 항생제는 거의 무효**: 위약대조 이중맹검 RCT SR+MA(Momand): RR 0.66 비유의, 치료필요수 (Number Needed to Treat, NNT) 143, GRADE 중간. 즉시 발치·식립에서만 예외 신호.
> - **표면 보강**: 광기능화 (Photofunctionalisation, UV 표면처리) OR 0.51 보호 경향, 비유의(p=0.25, Uesugi) — 근거 불충분.
> - **삽입 토크 (Insertional Torque)는 양극단이 모두 위험 — U자형 곡선**: 저토크 <30 N·cm 조기 실패 위험 약 14배(HR 13.909, 1.835–105.416, P=.0108, Carr 2022 n=214); **고토크** ≥60 N·cm 위험 9배(OR 9.00, 1.06–76.18, P=.043, Spresser 2025 n=207, 대조군 30–59 N·cm) → **함의된 안전창은 30–59 N·cm**. 단 두 연구의 대조군 구간이 정렬되지 않아(Carr 대조군 상한 없음) 단일 임계값으로 읽지 말 것.
> - **유전 소인 축이 새로 생겼다 (Ferrer 2021)**: MMP 1 G-1607GG·MMP 8 C-799T·MMP 13 -77 A>G(모두 프로모터부, 전사활성 변화 → 염증·세포외기질 분해). PRISMA SR이나 **풀링 없음·GRADE 없음**, 포함 10편 중 연관 보고 5편 — 확립된 예측인자가 아니라 **정밀의학 토대**. 이전에는 위키에 임플란트 유전 페이지가 전무했음.
> - **문헌 간 방향 불일치가 누적되고 있다 (반증 신호)**: 전신질환·약물 무관(Carr 2019, 8,540개 — 나이·성별·시대 보정 후), 약물은 단변량만 유의(Basson 2023), 수술 시각 비유의(Tattan 2021), **고령이 오히려 보호적**(Nyland 2024 HRR 0.86/10세), 상·하악 동일·여성 우세(Grigoras 2024 — 주류와 정면 충돌). 위험인자 목록은 **합의가 아니라 경합 중**으로 읽어야 한다.
> - **정의 불일치가 이 분야의 구조적 한계 (Scoffier 2026)**: scoping review가 1,838편→**최종 8편**만 포함, "조기 실패"의 정의·평가가 연구마다 달라 **합산 자체가 불가**하다고 명시. 3영역 분류(국소=골치유장애·감염 / 전신=숙주 취약성·유전 / 술기=수술 외상·초기 안정성)를 제안.
> - **임상 결론**: EIF는 *숙주(흡연·전신질환·약물)×해부/골질(전방상악·후방하악·4형골)×술식(즉시로딩·토크)* 다변량 문제. 레버: 금연 상담·혈당·혈압약 복용 확인·부위별 위험 인지·보수적 로딩·**토크는 30–59 N·cm 목표**. 항생제 예방은 비복잡 케이스에서 지양.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 37 papers on early implant failure (EIF — loss of osseointegration before prosthetic loading): incidence is low (≈1–4% implant-level; 4.0%/7.0% patient-level, Yari cohort n=1,323/738; all-on-four 0.4–1.1% at 1 year, Uesugi n=561) but multifactorial; smoking is the most consistent modifiable risk factor (pooled OR 2.59, Fan SR+MA 32 studies, 59,246 implants; dose-dependent, Naseri SR+MA; survives multivariate adjustment Yari OR 1.84, Uesugi OR 2.92) — though not in every cohort (Nyland 2024, Wåhlberg 2025); insertional torque shows a **U-shaped** relationship (low <30 N·cm HR 13.909, Carr 2022; high ≥60 N·cm OR 9.00, Spresser 2025), implying a safe mid-window of 30–59 N·cm.

Site matters — but not uniformly: absolute EIF by quadrant (Fouda 2020 SR+MA, 78,230 implants): anterior maxilla 6.2% (highest) vs anterior mandible 2.5% (lowest, RR 2.44); critically, posterior maxilla vs posterior mandible is NOT significantly different (1.9% vs 1.46%, RR 1.07, p=0.68), and posterior mandible (~4.2%) exceeds posterior maxilla within-jaw — "mandible is safe" oversimplifies; type IV bone OR 2.88 and immediate loading OR 3.42 (Yari) compound site risk; surgical complications dominate procedure-related failure (HR 15.84, Carr 2019; OR 28.35, da Rocha Coelho 2021); genetic susceptibility has an emerging but unpooled basis (MMP-1/8/13 promoter polymorphisms, Ferrer 2021, 10 studies screened → 5 reporting associations).

Routine preoperative antibiotic prophylaxis does NOT meaningfully reduce EIF: RR 0.66 (non-significant), NNT 143 (Momand SR+MA); postoperative antibiotic omission is a signal in one cohort (OR 2.77, da Rocha Coelho 2021 — timing-complementary, not contradictory); antihypertensive medication compliance is independently protective (Rat 2.29% vs 4.33%, Masri; AHT HR 0.23, statin HR 0.16, Chidiac) while SSRIs raise risk (HR 2.17); and the literature's direction is unstable — Carr 2019 found no systemic/medication risk after adjustment, Nyland 2024 found *older* age protective, Basson 2023 found medications significant only univariately, and Grigoras 2024 reported equal maxilla–mandible loss with female predominance (contradicting the received literature); the biggest structural limit is definitional heterogeneity (Scoffier 2026: 1,838 screened → only 8 included; "early failure" defined inconsistently across studies, making pooling unreliable).

## 세줄요약

조기 임플란트 실패(Early Implant Failure, EIF; 보철 로딩 전 골유착 실패) 37편 통합: 발생률은 낮지만(임플란트 단위 ≈1~4%; Yari 코호트 n=1,323) 다변량 사건 — 흡연이 가장 일관된 가변 위험인자(풀링 교차비(Odds Ratio, OR) 2.59, Fan SR+MA 59,246개; 다변량 보정 후 유의, Yari OR 1.84·Uesugi OR 2.92; 단 Nyland 2024·Wåhlberg 2025에선 비유의); **삽입 토크는 U자형** — 저토크 <30 N·cm 시간당 위험비(Hazard Ratio, HR) 13.909(Carr 2022), 고토크 ≥60 N·cm OR 9.00(Spresser 2025) → **안전창 30–59 N·cm**; 당뇨 상대위험(Relative Risk, RR) 2.75·두경부 방사선 RR 2.73(Moy 21년 코호트).

부위 위험은 사분면으로 봐야 한다 — Fouda 2020 SR+MA(78,230개): 전방 상악 (anterior maxilla) **6.2%** 최고 vs 전방 하악 (anterior mandible) **2.5%** 최저(RR 2.44); 후방 상악 vs 후방 하악은 **유의차 없음**(RR 1.07, p=0.68) — 후방 하악(~4.2%)이 후방 상악(~1.9%)보다 높음; 4형 골 (type IV bone) OR 2.88·즉시 잠정보철/즉시 로딩(Immediate Provisional/Immediate Loading) OR 3.42(Yari)가 중첩되면 고위험; 상악동 증대·치주 발치 부위도 독립 위험.

술전 예방적 항생제는 EIF를 유의하게 줄이지 못함(RR 0.66 비유의, 치료필요수(Number Needed to Treat, NNT) 143, Momand 7 RCT); 단 술후 항생제 **미사용**은 독립 위험인자로 검출됨(OR 2.77, da Rocha Coelho 2021 — 시점상 보완, 상충 아님); 항고혈압제 복용 환자는 보호적(혈압약 복용군 EIF 2.29% vs 정상혈압 4.33%, Masri; 시간당 위험비(Hazard Ratio, HR) 0.23, Chidiac); SSRI 복용은 위험 증가(HR 2.17); **술기 합병증이 가장 강력한 조작 가능 예측인자**(HR 15.84, Carr 2019; OR 28.35, da Rocha Coelho 2021); 핵심 레버는 금연·혈당 조절·부위인지 계획·보수적 로딩·**토크 30–59 N·cm 유지**이며 일반적 항생제 예방은 항균제 내성(Antimicrobial Resistance, AMR) 스튜어드십 관점에서 지양. **단 전신질환·약물의 위험 신호는 문헌 간 불일치**(Carr 2019 무관·Nyland 2024 고령 보호·Grigoras 2024 상하악 동일·여성 우세)이므로, 위험인자 목록을 합의가 아닌 **경합 중인 가설집합**으로 읽어야 한다.

## Thesis

**Clinical question:** What predicts early implant failure (EIF, before prosthetic loading), and what — if anything — prevents it?

EIF is mechanistically distinct from late failure: it reflects a **failure to achieve osseointegration**, not its later breakdown by peri-implantitis or overload. Across a general-placement cohort ([[implants/survival/yari-2023-risk-factors-early-implant-failure]]), an immediate-loading full-arch cohort ([[implants/full-arch/uesugi-2024-risk-factors-early-failure-all-on-four]]), pooled smoking data ([[implants/survival/fan-2024-smoking-early-implant-failure-sr-ma]], [[implants/survival/naseri-2020-smoking-levels-implant-failure-sr-ma]]), a grafted/sinus nested case-control ([[implants/survival/bonsmann-2025-risk-factors-early-implant-failure-sinus-nested]]), a dedicated SR+MA on early failure in grafted sites ([[implants/survival/clauser-2022-early-implant-failure-grafted]], OR 1.50 pooled, 10 cohort studies), and a classic long-term cohort ([[implants/survival/moy-2005-dental-implant-failure-rates-risk]]), the same determinants recur: **smoking, diabetes/radiation, posterior maxilla, low-density (type IV) bone, compromised/grafted sites, and immediate loading.** A large SR+MA ([[implants/survival/fouda-2020-impact-alveolar-bone-sites]], 78,230 implants) now provides the first pooled **absolute EIF rates by quadrant**: anterior maxilla 6.2% (highest), anterior mandible 2.5% (lowest, RR 2.44 vs anterior maxilla); crucially, **posterior maxilla vs posterior mandible is not significantly different** (1.9% vs 1.46%, RR 1.07, p=0.68) — the entire jaw-level gap is driven by the anterior maxilla alone, and the posterior mandible (~4.2%) is not a "safe" site. On the prevention side, the highest-quality synthesis ([[drug/antibiotics/momand-2024-antibiotic-prophylaxis-early-implant-failure]]) shows routine antibiotic prophylaxis barely moves EIF (RR 0.66, NS; NNT 143), while antihypertensive and statin use appear independently protective ([[drug/systemic-disease/masri-2024-early-implant-failure-antihypertensive-medications]], [[drug/systemic-disease/chidiac-2026-antihypertensive-antihyperlipidemic-antidepressant-implant-survival]]). The actionable levers are **host optimization (smoking cessation, glycemic control, medication review), site/bone-aware planning using quadrant-level absolute rates, torque kept in a mid-window, and conservative loading** — not antibiotics.

**What the 2026 batch added — and what it destabilized.** Ten papers added since the last revision (2026-10-21) sharpen three things and unsettle one. (1) **Insertional torque is now a biphasic curve, not a threshold**: [[implants/survival/carr-2022-low-insertional-torque-early]] found <30 N·cm carries a ~14-fold early-failure hazard (HR 13.909, 95% CI 1.835–105.416, P=.0108; 214 implants, VA North Texas), while [[implants/survival/spresser-2025-high-insertional-torque-early]] found ≥60 N·cm carries a 9-fold odds (OR 9.00, 95% CI 1.06–76.18, P=.043; 207 implants, same center) against an active 30–59 N·cm control — jointly implying a **safe mid-window of 30–59 N·cm**. The two comparators are *not* aligned (Carr's is unbounded ≥30), so this is a bracketing, not a single validated cutoff. (2) **Procedure-related risk is dominated by complications and adjuncts, not by demographics**: [[implants/survival/carr-2019-early-implant-failure-associated-patient]] (8,540 implants, Mayo Clinic) found surgical complication HR 15.84 (11.10–22.61) with *no* systemic disease or medication increasing risk after age/sex/era adjustment, and [[implants/survival/da-rocha-coelho-2021-evaluation-of-the-association]] (2,537 implants) found postoperative complications OR 28.35 and graft augmentation OR 1.83, again with diabetes/hypertension/age/sex null. (3) **A genetic-susceptibility limb now exists**: [[implants/survival/ferrer-2021-genetic-polymorphisms-associated-with]] maps MMP-1 G-1607GG, MMP-8 C-799T and MMP-13 -77 A>G promoter polymorphisms to altered transcriptional activity, inflammation and matrix degradation — but with no pooled estimate and only 5 of 10 screened studies reporting associations, it is a precision-medicine foundation, not a predictor (it fills what had been an entirely empty genetic axis in the wiki).

**What it unsettled:** the direction of several classic risk factors is now contested within our own corpus. [[implants/survival/carr-2019-early-implant-failure-associated-patient]] found no systemic/medication signal, [[implants/survival/basson-2023-correlates-of-early-dental]] found PPI/IBD significant only univariately, [[implants/survival/tattan-2021-surgery-start-time-and-early]] found surgery start time non-significant (HR 1.9, p=.105), [[implants/survival/nyland-2024-risk-factors-for-early-dental]] found *older* age protective (HRR 0.86 per decade, P=.037), and [[implants/survival/grigoras-2024-early-implant-failure-a-meta]] reported equal maxilla/mandible failure with female predominance — all contradicting one or more held pages. The structural reason is now explicit: [[implants/survival/scoffier-2026-aetiology-of-early-implant-failure]] screened 1,838 records and could include only **8**, because "early implant failure" is defined so inconsistently across studies that results cannot be pooled. Any risk-factor table in this overview — including the ones below — should be read as a **contested hypothesis set**, not a consensus.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **Host optimization first.** Smoking is the most consistent *modifiable* driver (Fan OR 2.59; dose-dependent in Naseri; survives adjustment in Yari/Uesugi and, in abstract-level data, Zhang 2025 OR 2.148) — but not in every cohort: Wåhlberg 2025 found smoking non-significant for early *failure* (OR 1.61, 0.88–2.94) and significant only for early *complications* (OR 2.30). Counsel smokers on complication risk as well as failure. Smoking-cessation counseling and glycemic control (diabetes RR 2.75, Moy) precede surgical decisions.
2. **Site/bone-aware planning.** Posterior maxilla and type IV bone repeatedly elevate EIF (Yari OR ~2.9-3.0; Uesugi maxilla OR 3.12). Absolute rates by quadrant (Fouda 2020 SR+MA, 78,230 implants): **anterior maxilla 6.2%** (highest), posterior mandible ~4.2%, posterior maxilla ~1.9%, **anterior mandible 2.5%** (lowest). Key nuance: posterior maxilla vs posterior mandible is NOT significantly different (RR 1.07, p=0.68) — the maxilla–mandible gap is driven entirely by the anterior maxilla. Do not assume posterior mandible is "safe." In low-density bone, favor protocols that protect primary stability and defer loading.
3. **Loading protocol is a choice, not a constant.** Immediate provisional/loading was the strongest single predictor in Yari (OR 3.42) and frames the high-risk all-on-four scenario (Uesugi). Reserve immediate loading for favorable host/bone/torque profiles.
4. **Compromised/grafted sites carry extra risk.** Periodontal-extraction sites, previously augmented bone, and sinus-augmented sites raise EIF (Yari; Bonsmann). Dedicated SR+MA pooling 10 cohort studies (Clauser 2022) quantifies the grafted-site penalty: OR 1.50 (95% CI 1.06–2.13) for early failure before loading, confirming the signal is real even across heterogeneous graft types and sites. Informed consent, delayed loading consideration, and ISQ monitoring are warranted.
5. **Do NOT default to antibiotic prophylaxis for prevention.** In healthy patients undergoing uncomplicated surgery, routine prophylaxis does not meaningfully reduce EIF (Momand NNT 143); reserve antibiotics for immediate post-extraction/infected sockets, where the signal exists and it is better framed as treatment.
6. **Surface conditioning (photofunctionalisation) is not yet evidence-backed** for EIF reduction (Uesugi OR 0.51, p=0.25) — promising trend, underpowered.
7. **Antihypertensive medication compliance is a host-factor predictor.** Hypertensive patients *on* antihypertensives achieve EIF rates *below* normotensive controls (Masri 2024: 2.29% vs 4.33%, p=.01; Chidiac 2026: HR 0.23, p=0.013). Hypertensive patients *not* on medication are the highest-risk group (patient-level EIF 28.6%, Masri 2024). Preoperative question: "Are you taking your blood pressure medications regularly?" Statins are similarly protective (HR 0.16). SSRIs increase failure risk (HR 2.17, Chidiac 2026) — flag for enhanced post-operative monitoring.
8. **Healing protocol (submerged vs non-submerged) is a confounded variable — do not use it to individualise advice for smokers.** Yang 2026 (HR 0.53 for submerged) and Guarnieri 2025 point the same way, and the baseline SR (Troiano 2018) is not held because it has no open access, but in Yang the protocol was chosen by insertion torque and in Guarnieri it is entangled with implant design. No held paper stratifies smokers by protocol (De Bruyn 2025 has no submerged arm and 3 events), so whether smokers benefit more or less from a buried protocol is unanswered. Mechanistic reasoning that an exposed healing abutment is worse in smokers (impaired mucosal healing, Apatzidou 2022; more early complications, Wåhlberg 2025 OR 2.30) is a hypothesis, not a finding. [미검증]
9. **Target a mid-window of insertion torque (30–59 N·cm) — both extremes raise early-failure risk.** Low torque <30 N·cm carried a ~14-fold hazard (HR 13.909, 95% CI 1.835–105.416, P=.0108; [[implants/survival/carr-2022-low-insertional-torque-early]]) and high torque ≥60 N·cm a 9-fold odds (OR 9.00, 95% CI 1.06–76.18, P=.043; [[implants/survival/spresser-2025-high-insertional-torque-early]], control 30–59 N·cm) — the two ends bracket a **biphasic (U-shaped) curve** whose implied safe band is Spresser's own control range. Caution: Carr's comparator is unbounded (≥30), so the 30–59 window is *inferred*, not a validated cutoff; both CIs are very wide at the extremes (upper bounds >100 and >76) reflecting sparse events. Practical reading: avoid both a spinning/low-torque implant and deliberate high-torque over-compression — do not chase maximal torque for its own sake. Note the ISQ/primary-stability literature lives in [[implants/isq]]; this decision point is about the early-*failure* endpoint, not stability measurements.
10. **Do not treat demographic/systemic risk factors as settled.** In our own corpus the direction is contested: no systemic/medication risk after adjustment (Carr 2019, n=8,540), medications significant only univariately (Basson 2023), surgery-time non-significant (Tattan 2021), *older* age protective (Nyland 2024), and equal maxilla–mandible loss with female predominance (Grigoras 2024). Counselling should therefore be framed around the levers with the most consistent direction (smoking, glycemic control, procedure quality, torque window, conservative loading) rather than around any single demographic claim.
11. **Surgical complications and adjunctive procedures are the strongest controllable procedural signals.** Any surgical complication was associated with early failure at HR 15.84 (11.10–22.61) in Carr 2019 and OR 28.35 in da Rocha Coelho 2021; adjuncts cluster too — socket preservation HR 2.67 and xenogenic material HR 2.12 (Carr 2019), pre-placement ridge augmentation HR 7.5 and simultaneous bone/soft-tissue grafting HR 3.7/5.03 (Tattan 2021). Conversely simple alveoloplasty at placement was *protective* (HR 0.33, Carr 2019). This argues for minimising avoidable intra-operative complexity and treating complication-avoidance (not antibiotic prophylaxis) as the primary prevention lever in uncomplicated cases.
12. **Genetic susceptibility is not yet actionable.** MMP-1/8/13 promoter polymorphisms ([[implants/survival/ferrer-2021-genetic-polymorphisms-associated-with]]) give a mechanistic rationale for why some patients fail without local or systemic contraindications, but with no pooled estimate and only 5 of 10 screened studies reporting associations this cannot inform patient selection. Mention it as a research direction, not a consent conversation.

## Gaps & Future Research

- Most risk-factor evidence is **retrospective** with low event counts; prospective multivariable models with 95% CIs are scarce.
- **Smoking's effect on EIF is not uniform across datasets**: pooled OR 2.59 (Fan) and cohort ORs 1.84–2.92 (Yari, Uesugi, Zhang) vs non-significant OR 1.61 (Wåhlberg, 63 failure patients). No held paper explains the gap; differences in early-failure definition (Wåhlberg: any event within the first year), covariates and event counts are candidate explanations, not established ones.
- **Protocol × host interactions are untested**: healing mode (submerged/non-submerged), flap design and immediate provisionalization are analysed as main effects in retrospective cohorts (Yang, Guarnieri, De Bruyn, Yari), never as modifiers of smoking. A prospective or stratified analysis with smoking status recorded is missing.
- **Mechanism is thin**: the only human-tissue evidence is Shibli 2024 (one group, micro-implants, 8 weeks, no survival outcome); the vasoconstriction/hypoxia pathway is extrapolated. Cessation-timing evidence for implants is not held beyond Caggiano 2022 (see [[overviews/smoking-tobacco-periodontal-implant-overview]]).
- Definition of "early failure" is inconsistent (before abutment vs before loading vs within 1 year of loading) — harmonization needed for pooling. The widely quoted "2–6% of implants" EIF range (e.g., Krisam 2019 introduction) is itself a span across such heterogeneous definitions and populations, not a pooled estimate. Scoffier 2026 makes this the field's headline problem: screening 1,838 records yielded only 8 includable studies, and the authors state results cannot be pooled across definitions — **a CONSENSUS definition of "early implant failure" is now the single highest-value methodological target in this field.**
- **Insertional torque needs a prospective dose-response study.** Carr 2022 (low <30 N·cm) and Spresser 2025 (high ≥60 N·cm) together imply a U-shaped curve with an optimal mid-window, but neither study used aligned comparator bands (Carr's control is unbounded ≥30) and both CIs are extremely wide (upper bounds 105 and 76). No held study measures torque continuously against EIF with a pre-specified window.
- **The genetic axis is a single SR with no pooling.** Ferrer 2021 (MMP-1/8/13 promoter polymorphisms) is the wiki's only implant-genetics page; it reports 5 of 10 screened studies with associations and provides no pooled effect estimate or GRADE rating. Replication cohorts and a meta-analysis with heterogeneity assessment are missing.
- **Contradictory directions are unresolved, not synthesised.** Carr 2019 (no systemic/medication risk, n=8,540), Basson 2023 (medications univariate only), Tattan 2021 (surgery time NS), Nyland 2024 (older age protective), and Grigoras 2024 (equal maxilla–mandible, female predominance) each conflict with one or more held pages. What is needed is not another single-centre cohort but a **harmonised multi-centre analysis with a shared early-failure definition** — precisely what Scoffier 2026 says the field lacks.
- Immediate-socket/grafted scenarios are exactly where prophylaxis *might* help but are under-powered in current RCTs.
- Photofunctionalisation and other surface/host adjuncts (e.g., vitamin D status — see [[overviews/vitamin-d-osseointegration-implant-overview]]) need adequately powered trials with EIF as a primary endpoint.

## Clinical Quiz
<!-- quiz_spec -->

**Q1.** 임플란트 식립 예정 환자가 흡연자입니다. "식립 며칠 전 담배 끊으면 된다"고 합니다. 이 오버뷰에 근거해 위험을 정량화하고 상담 접근을 설명하세요. *(근거: Fan 2024 SR+MA + Yari 2023 + Naseri 2020)*

> **모범답안**: 흡연은 조기 임플란트 실패(EIF)의 **가장 일관된 가변 위험인자**다. Fan 2024 SR+MA(32편, 59,246개 임플란트): 풀링 OR **2.59**. 단기 금연으로 완전히 해결되지 않는다 — Naseri 2020에서 용량 의존적으로 위험이 증가하며(heavy smoker↑), Yari 2023에서 다변량 보정 후에도 OR 1.84, Uesugi에서 OR 2.92로 유의하다. 상담 원칙: ① 식립 **최소 4주 전** 금연 시작 + 장기 유지가 목표임을 설명 ② "며칠 금연"으로는 조직 산소화·혈관 반응이 충분히 회복되지 않는다 ③ 금연 상담·보조제(varenicline/NRT) 연계. 이 오버뷰는 금연 상담을 "통상적 항생제 예방"보다 훨씬 실질적인 EIF 예방 레버로 제시한다.

**Q2.** 단순 발치창에 임플란트 식립 예정인 일반 환자에게 "예방적 항생제를 드시면 실패 위험이 낮아집니다"라고 안내해도 됩니까? *(근거: Momand 2024 SR+MA — NNT 143, GRADE 중간)*

> **모범답안**: **안 된다.** 이 오버뷰의 핵심 소견: Momand 2024 SR+MA(위약대조 이중맹검 RCT만 7편, n=3,014개 임플란트, GRADE 중간)에서 술전 예방적 항생제가 EIF를 **유의하게 줄이지 못했다** — RR 0.66, 비유의, NNT 143. NNT 143은 1명의 실패를 막으려면 143명에게 항생제를 줘야 한다는 의미로 임상적 효용이 매우 낮다. 항균제 내성(AMR) 스튜어드십 관점에서 비복잡 증례에서 통상적 항생제 예방은 이 오버뷰가 명시적으로 **지양**을 권고한다. 단, 즉시 발치와 식립에서는 예외 신호가 있으므로 그 경우는 별도 판단한다.

**Q3.** 후방 상악에 전반적 골이식 없이 Type IV 골에 즉시 임시보철과 함께 임플란트를 식립하는 계획입니다. 이 오버뷰에서 이 계획의 위험도를 어떻게 평가합니까? *(근거: Yari 2023 + Uesugi 2024 + 다변량 위험 누적)*

> **모범답안**: **이 계획은 EIF 위험인자 3개가 동시 겹치는 고위험 시나리오**다. Yari 2023 다변량 분석에서: ① 후방 상악 OR 2.96 ② Type IV 골 OR 2.88 ③ 즉시 임시보철/즉시 로딩 OR 3.42 — 단일 예측인자 중 가장 강하다. 이 오버뷰는 위험인자가 누적될 때 각 OR이 단순 가산이 아니라 상승작용으로 작동할 수 있음을 경고한다. 권장 조정: ① 즉시 로딩을 포기하고 보수적 로딩(8–12주)으로 변경 ② Type IV 골에서는 CA/SLActive 친수성 표면 또는 OD(골밀도화) 기법 병용(surface 오버뷰 연계) ③ 흡연·당뇨 등 추가 위험인자 유무 재확인. 이 계획을 그대로 진행하면 EIF 확률이 단일 부위 평균(1–4%)을 크게 상회할 것이다.

**Q4.** 술자가 "초기 안정성을 최대한 확보하겠다"며 모든 임플란트를 70 N·cm 이상 강한 토크로 식립하고 있습니다. 또한 토크가 20 N·cm로 낮게 측정된 한 부위는 "어차피 약하니 그냥 진행"하려 합니다. 이 두 판단을 이 오버뷰의 토크 근거로 평가하세요. *(근거: Carr 2022 + Spresser 2025 — 삽입 토크 U자 곡선)*

> **모범답안**: **두 판단 모두 위험하다.** 삽입 토크는 임계값이 아니라 **U자형(양극단) 곡선**이다. ① 고토크 ≥60 N·cm는 30–59 N·cm 대조군 대비 조기 실패 교차비(Odds Ratio, OR) **9.00**(95% CI 1.06–76.18, P=.043; Spresser 2025, 207개) — 과압축·골 미세손상 경로로 해석된다. "최대 토크=최선"은 틀렸다. ② 저토크 <30 N·cm는 ≥30 N·cm 대비 시간당 위험비(Hazard Ratio, HR) **13.909**(95% CI 1.835–105.416, P=.0108; Carr 2022, 214개) — 스피닝/불충분한 일차안정성은 실패로 직결된다. **함의된 안전창은 30–59 N·cm**(Spresser의 대조군 구간). 주의: Carr의 대조군은 상한이 없어(≥30) 30–59가 *검증된* 컷오프가 아니라 **추론된 창**이고, 두 CI 모두 상한이 매우 넓다(사건 수 희소). 저토크 부위는 그대로 진행하지 말고 골밀도화(OD)·더 넓은 직경·연장된 치유·하중 연기 등 일차안정성 보강 후 판단한다.

**Q5.** 같은 오버뷰를 읽은 동료가 "임플란트 조기 실패는 전신질환·약물 때문이고, 상악이 항상 하악보다 위험하다"고 단정합니다. 이 오버뷰의 최신 근거(2026 배치)에 비추어 이 주장을 반박 또는 수정하세요. *(근거: Carr 2019 + Nyland 2024 + Grigoras 2024 + Scoffier 2026 — 방향 불일치)*

> **모범답안**: **두 주장 모두 "합의"가 아니라 "경합 중"이다.** ① 전신질환·약물: Carr 2019(임플란트 8,540개, Mayo)에서 나이·성별·시대 보정 후 **어떤 전신질환·약물도 위험을 높이지 않았고**, Basson 2023은 약물(PPI/IBD)이 **단변량에서만** 유의(다변량 소실), da Rocha Coelho 2021도 당뇨·고혈압 무관(P≥.12). 반대로 Masri 2024·Chidiac 2026은 항고혈압제·스타틴의 보호 효과를 보고 — 신호가 일관되지 않다. ② 상악 vs 하악: Fouda 2020 SR+MA(78,230개)에서 격차는 **전방 상악(6.2%)이 만든 것**이고 후방 상악 vs 후방 하악은 유의차가 없으며(1.9% vs 1.46%, RR 1.07, p=0.68), Grigoras 2024는 아예 상·하악 실패가 **동일**하고 여성 우세라고 보고(주류와 정면 충돌). ③ 구조적 원인: Scoffier 2026 scoping review가 1,838편을 스크리닝하고 **최종 8편**만 포함 — "조기 실패"의 정의 자체가 연구마다 달라 **합산이 불가**하다. 따라서 상담은 방향이 가장 일관된 레버(흡연·혈당·술기 품질·토크 창·보수적 로딩) 중심으로 하고, 단일 인구학적·전신적 주장을 확정된 사실처럼 제시하지 않는다.
