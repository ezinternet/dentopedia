## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **테제**: 슈나이더막 천공은 흔한 합병증이지만(평균 발생률 **29.42%**, 보고범위 7–56%), 적절히 수복하면 임플란트 생존을 깎지 않는다 — 발견 즉시 수술을 중단하는 것은 근거가 없는 결정이다. 예후는 "천공 여부"가 아니라 "수복의 질"과 "예방"이 결정한다.
> - **결과(SR+MA, schiavo-2024)**: 천공(수복)군 생존 97.1% vs 비천공군 97.7% — **유의차 없음** (OR 0.78, 95% CI 0.49–2.23, p=0.28, I²=0%). 1,666명·2,229 상악동·5,052 임플란트, 측방창 접근법만 포함, 보철 후 ≥6개월.
> - **식립 시점 무차별**: 천공군에서 동시 vs 지연 식립 생존 차이 없음 (RR=1.00, CI 0.94–1.07, p=0.95) — 첫 하위분석 [schiavo-2024]. 천공이 발견돼도 원래의 식립 시점 계획을 유지할 수 있다.
> - **수복 재료**: 콜라겐막이 가장 보편적(10편 중 7편), 그 외 봉합·PRF·Bichat 지방패드·산화셀룰로스·블록이식·결합조직·탈회동종골 [schiavo-2024]. 이질성 0%라는 점에서 "수복 자체"가 재료에 우선한다.
> - **예측(두께, SR+MA ke-2024)**: 천공 증례의 막이 연속형 분석에서 유의하게 얇음 (MD **−0.91 mm**, 95% CI −1.48~−0.33, I²=94%, 7/7 연구 방향 일치). 그러나 2 / 1.5 / 1 mm 어떤 절단값도 유의한 위험 오즈를 내지 못함 → **선별용 절단값 없음**.
> - **U자형 해석**: 두꺼운 막이 오히려 천공 위험을 높일 수 있어(낮은 위험대 ~1–1.5 mm 추정) 두께-위험은 단조가 아닐 수 있다 — ke-2024의 가설. 두께는 위험 신호 하나일 뿐 선별 도구가 아니다.
> - **예방(transcrestal, kang-2022)**: RBH<5 mm 부위의 막 천공 문헌 발생률 ~28% 대비, 돔형 큐렛(DASK)으로 막 긴장을 **둔성 박리로 전방향 해소**(인접 임플란트 사이 포함)한 수정 OSFE에서 **천공 0건** — 6개월 생존 100%, 이식골 흡수는 RBH≥5 mm군과 유의차 없음.
> - **기전 연결**: ke-2024의 "얇은 막" 위험은 측방창, kang-2022의 긴장해소 예방은 경치조골 — 두 접근의 공통 메시지는 **천공은 막의 재료적 결함이 아니라 술식-막 상호작용(긴장 집중)이 결정**한다는 것이다.
> - **중요한 경계**: schiavo-2024·ke-2024는 **측방창(lateral window)만** 포함, kang-2022는 경치조골(transcrestal) — 결론을 반대쪽 접근법으로 일반화할 때는 연구설계 차이를 명시해야 한다.
> - **임상 takeaway**: ① 삽입물이 천공돼도 수복 후 계획대로 진행(식립 시점 유지), ② 수복은 콜라겐막이 표준, ③ 술전 CBCT 두께로 수술을 거부하거나 "안전"하다고 선별하지 말 것, ④ 경치조골 RBH<5 mm에서는 둔성 박리·긴장해소가 천공 방지의 핵심 기법 단계.

## Three-line Summary

Schneiderian membrane perforation is common (mean incidence 29.42%; reported 7–56%) but repaired perforation does not reduce implant survival: 97.1% (perforated/repaired, 480 sinuses) vs 97.7% (non-perforated, 1,224 sinuses), OR 0.78 (95% CI 0.49–2.23, p=0.28, I²=0%; 5,052 implants, lateral window, ≥6-month follow-up); one-stage vs two-stage placement showed no survival difference in perforated sinuses (RR=1.00) [schiavo-di-flaviano-2024].

Membrane thickness predicts perforation only on the continuous scale, not as a clinical cutoff: perforation cases had thinner membranes (MD −0.91 mm, 95% CI −1.48 to −0.33, I²=94%), yet 2/1.5/1 mm thresholds all yielded non-significant ORs — likely a U-shaped relationship, so preoperative CBCT thickness should not triage cases [ke-2024].

Prevention is technique-driven: a modified transcrestal OSFE releasing membrane tension by blunt curette dissection in all directions (including between adjacent implants) achieved 0 perforations at RBH<5 mm versus ~28% reported rates, with 100% 6-month survival [kang-2022].

## 세줄요약

슈나이더막 천공은 흔하고(평균 발생률 29.42%, 범위 7–56%) 수복하면 생존을 깎지 않는다: 천공(수복)군 97.1% vs 비천공군 97.7%, OR 0.78 (CI 0.49–2.23, p=0.28, I²=0%, 5,052 임플란트, 측방창·보철 후 ≥6개월); 천공군에서 동시 vs 지연 식립 생존 차이 없음 (RR=1.00) [schiavo-2024].

막 두께는 연속형에서만 위험 신호: 천공 증례가 유의하게 얇음 (MD −0.91 mm, CI −1.48~−0.33, I²=94%) 그러나 2/1.5/1 mm 절단값 모두 비유의 — U자형 관계 추정, 술전 CBCT 두께로 선별하지 말 것 [ke-2024].

예방은 술식이 결정: 돔형 큐렛 둔성 박리로 막 긴장을 전방향(인접 임플란트 사이 포함) 해소한 수정 경치조골(OSFE)은 RBH<5 mm에서 천공 0건(문헌 ~28% vs), 6개월 생존 100% [kang-2022].

## Thesis

막 천공의 예후는 발생 여부가 아니라 **수복 실행**과 **술식 설계**가 결정한다. 결과 축에서 수복은 생존 차이를 0.6%p로, 이질성 0%로 압축시키고, 예측 축에서 두께는 연속 신호일 뿐 선별 불가능하며, 예방 축에서 긴장해소는 천공률을 ~28%에서 0으로 보낸다. 세 축이 한 방향을 가리킨다: **천공을 두려워해 계획을 바꾸지 말고, 수복 프로토콜을 갖추고, 긴장해소로 예방하라.**

## Three Axes — Occurrence, Prediction, Outcome

**Axis 1 — Occurrence (schiavo-2024 · kang-2022)**: Perforation is a routine surgical event, not a rare disaster — mean 29.42% across included lateral-window studies, rising steeply as residual bone height falls (≈28% reported at RBH<5 mm). Transcrestal data suggest this "expected" rate is technique-dependent: blunt blunt curette dissection releasing membrane tension in all directions, including the space between adjacent implants, drove the rate to 0/60 at the same RBH range [kang-2022]. Occurrence is therefore best managed preemptively (tension release) rather than accepted as random.

**Axis 2 — Prediction (ke-2024)**: The only quantified, meta-analysed predictor is membrane thickness on CBCT, and it is clinically unusable as a dichotomous rule: continuous analysis shows thinner membranes in perforation cases (MD −0.91 mm, I²=94%), yet no cutoff (2/1.5/1 mm) reaches significance, and the probable U-shape (very thick membranes also risky) actively cautions against treating "thick membrane = safe". Thickness is a continuous, confounded signal among many (window size, septa, instruments, operator experience); it should inform caution, not select or refuse surgery.

**Axis 3 — Outcome (schiavo-2024)**: Repair converts perforation into a non-event for survival: 0.6%p difference, OR 0.78 with CI crossing 1, I²=0%, and a first subgroup analysis showing one-stage vs two-stage placement are equally viable in perforated sinuses (RR=1.00). Repair materials varied (collagen membrane dominant, 7/10) with zero heterogeneity — the act of sealing (not the specific material) is what matters. The prior Diaz-Olivares 2021 SR+MA reached the same conclusion, corroborating across independent datasets.

## Clinical Decision Points

- **D1 (intraoperative)**: Perforation detected during lateral sinus lift → repair with collagen membrane (standard), continue grafting, keep the planned placement timing. Aborting the procedure or deferring to a second stage is not supported by survival evidence (RR=1.00).
- **D2 (preoperative)**: CBCT shows a very thin (<1 mm) or very thick (>2 mm) membrane → neither excludes nor guarantees surgery; adjust technique (smaller window, gentler elevation, expect difficulty) rather than cancel. Do not cite thickness cutoffs for triage.
- **D3 (RBH<5 mm, transcrestal)**: Prefer techniques that release membrane tension by blunt dissection (dome curettes, all directions incl. inter-implant space) — the modified OSFE bench-marks 0 perforations at this RBH where ~28% is literature-expected [kang-2022].
- **D4 (planning)**: One-stage vs two-stage is decided by the usual criteria (RBH, membrane quality, graft volume) — a perforation alone is not a reason to switch timing, since outcomes are equivalent in perforated sinuses [schiavo-2024].
- **D5 (narrative)**: Explain to the patient that perforation is a common, manageable event — repaired perforation shows no measurable survival penalty; the decision to proceed after repair is evidence-based, not a judgment call that requires risk-signing.

## Gaps & Future Research

- Both SR+MAs pool lateral-window studies only; transcrestal perforation outcome data are absent from meta-analyses — the kang-2022 prevention result lacks an outcome-axis counterpart for the crestal approach.
- No head-to-head trial of repair materials (collagen vs PRF vs block graft) — current evidence only shows "any sealed repair ≈ no perforation", with zero heterogeneity masking material effects.
- The U-shaped thickness hypothesis is untested prospectively; a large single-arm registry correlating CBCT thickness × perforation with repair outcomes is missing.
- No study measures perforation size/position as modifiers of the survival effect; the 0.6%p difference is a mean that could hide size-dependent effects (small central vs large marginal tears).
