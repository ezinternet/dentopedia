## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **결론**: 초기고정력(삽입토크 <10 N·cm, 초기 ISQ <55, 수술 중 회전 포함) 부재는 성공의 절대 금기가 아니다. 2차 생물학적 안정성이 1차 기계적 고정을 대체하며, 보유 근거상 성공률 95.9~100%.
> - **핵심 수치 1**: 초기 고정 없이 식립된 임플란트 92개 중 상실 3개(3.26%) — 전부 "저항 없는 회전"(C등급) 군. 저항 있는 회전·측방동요는 0% 상실 (Cobo-Vázquez 2018).
> - **핵심 수치 2**: IT ≤20 N·cm 코호트(n=30, 스피너 <5 N·cm 3개 포함) 전례 골유착, ISQ 67→80 상승 (Norton 2017) — IT는 회전마찰, ISQ는 축방향 강성.
> - **핵심 수치 3**: IT <10 N·cm/회전 97개: 성공 95.9%, 즉시식립 100% (23/23), 실패 4건 모두 후방 지연식립 (Galen 2025).
> - **핵심 수치 4**: ITV <10 N·cm + 초기 ISQ <55(6개는 측정 불가) 즉시식립 16개 전례 100% 생존(평균 65.2개월), ISQ 42.2→68.7 (Jang 2025).
> - **비결 3축**: ① 회전을 허용하지 않는 술식(언더드릴링 1단계·골이식·잠복/2단계 부하), ② 6개월 이상 지연부하(즉시부하 필요시 IT ≥35 N·cm, ≤20 N·cm는 연결형 보철), ③ 절대 초기값이 아닌 **ISQ 상승 궤적** 모니터링.
> - **토크를 억지로 올리지 말 것**: 언더밀링 증가로 IT 25→63 N·cm여도 ISQ는 불변(비유의, Rosas-Díaz 2024); 과도한 압축은 골세포 사멸 → "과잉 골밀도화 = 골파괴" 동물 근거(Coyac 2019).
> - **언더드릴링·골밀도화(OD) 근거는 갈림**: 즉시식립 상악 전치부 RCT는 언더드릴링이 ISQ를 못 살림(모든 시점 통상군 우위); 저밀도 골 OD SR+MA도 1차/2차 안정성 비유의. In vitro에서는 OD가 언더드릴링 대비 IT 2.4배 우위.
> - **반대 신호**: 전향 멀티센터에서 초기고정 부재 HR 23.54 (실패의 지배적 축), 후향에서 제거 사유 2위(22.4%) — 완전 공회전 spinner·후방 지연식립은 관리·선별 주의.
> - **임상 takeaway**: "안정을 사기 위해 토크를 쫓지 말고, 회전을 막고 기다리는 프로토콜로 ISQ가 오르는 것을 신뢰하라."

## Three-line Summary

Low or absent primary stability (insertion torque <10 N·cm, initial ISQ <55, intraoperative spinning) is a manageable condition, not an absolute contraindication: across the held retrospective cohorts, success runs 95.9–100% with 100% survival even in a strict low-stability immediate cohort ([[immediate-implant/primary-stability/jang-2025-flapless-immediate-implant-low-primary-stability]], n=16, IT<10 N·cm AND ISQ<55, mean 65.2 months).

The clinical levers are three: (1) surgical — prevent rotation and preserve the bed (single-step undersized drilling, near-universal socket/ridge grafting, two-stage submerged protocol); (2) loading — delayed loading ≥6 months by default, or immediate loading only under a specific floor (IT ≥35 N·cm per Bavetta; ≤20 N·cm tolerable only as splinted prostheses per Darriba 2023); (3) monitoring — trust the rising ISQ trajectory over the absolute initial value (Norton 2017: ISQ 67→80 at loading despite IT ≤20 N·cm).

Chasing torque does not buy stability: under-milling escalation raises IT 25→63 N·cm while ISQ stays flat (Rosas-Díaz 2024), and undersized drilling failed to improve ISQ in an immediate-maxilla RCT (Sierra-Rebolledo 2021). The failure axis is real — absence of primary stability is HR 23.54 in a prospective multicenter cohort and the second most documented removal reason (22.4%) in a failure case series — so the strategies here are for selected cases under a conservative protocol, not a license to abandon primary stability as a goal.

## 세줄요약

낮거나 없는 초기고정력(삽입토크 <10 N·cm, 초기 ISQ <55, 수술 중 회전)은 절대 금기가 아닌 관리 가능한 상태다: 후향 코호트에서 성공률 95.9~100%, 엄격한 저안정성 즉시식립 코호트([[immediate-implant/primary-stability/jang-2025-flapless-immediate-implant-low-primary-stability]], n=16, IT<10 N·cm AND ISQ<55, 평균 65.2개월)에서도 생존율 100%.

임상 지렛대는 셋: ① 술식 — 회전을 막고 골상을 보존(언더드릴링 1단계, 거의 전례 골이식, 잠복 2단계); ② 부하 — 기본은 6개월 이상 지연부하, 즉시부하는 특정 하한에서만(IT ≥35 N·cm, Bavetta; ≤20 N·cm은 연결형 보철로만, Darriba 2023); ③ 모니터링 — 절대 초기값보다 ISQ 상승 궤적을 신뢰(Norton 2017: IT ≤20 N·cm인데 부하 시 ISQ 67→80).

토크를 쫓는 것은 안정성을 사지 않는다: 언더밀링으로 IT 25→63 N·cm여도 ISQ 불변(Rosas-Díaz 2024), 즉시식립 상악 RCT도 언더드릴링이 ISQ를 못 살림(Sierra-Rebolledo 2021). 실패 축은 실재한다 — 전향 멀티센터에서 초기고정 부재 HR 23.54, 실패 증례 시리즈에서 제거 사유 2위(22.4%) — 따라서 아래 전략은 보수적 프로토콜 하의 선별된 증례를 위한 것이지, 초기고정을 포기해도 된다는 허가가 아니다.

## Summary

The classic teaching — *primary stability is mandatory for osseointegration* — survives in this evidence base only as a **default goal, not a gate**. Five held studies now document that implants placed with low or absent primary stability integrate at rates indistinguishable from routine placement, provided three conditions hold: the implant is not free-rotating in a hopeless site, the surgical protocol protects the bed and prevents further motion, and loading waits for secondary (biological) stability to arrive.

This page synthesizes the clinical side of the "third regime" — the cases where the operator reads no torque and no ISQ — that the [[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]] approaches from the torque side. Its core reframing applies here too, inverted: *if insertion torque is a reading of the bone that resisted the drill, then a low or absent reading is a property of the bone, not a verdict on the implant.* The held evidence says that bone properties can be compensated by biology (secondary stability) when the mechanical challenge is contained. It also says — loudly, in the HR 23.54 cohort — that compensation has limits.

## Why low primary stability succeeds — three axes

### Axis 1 — Concept: the initial reading is not the verdict

- **IT measures rotational friction, ISQ measures axial stiffness** — different constructs, confirmed by the held IT–ISQ decoupling literature (SR+MA r=0.44, I²>90%; [[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]] §6). A low initial reading predicts neither failure nor poor secondary stability: [[implants/isq/norton-2017-low-insertion-torque-primary-stability]] (ISQ 67.2→80.0 by loading, Spearman IT-ISQ NS) is the cleanest demonstration.
- **Secondary stability rescues what primary did not provide**: the primary→secondary transition is exactly the axis Jang's 100%-survival cohort documents (ISQ 42.2→68.7, 95% CI of gain 17.2–32.9, p<0.01). The clinically meaningful signal is the **rising trajectory**, not the absolute initial value.
- **Grade matters more than absence**: Cobo-Vázquez's only losses (3/38, 7.89%) were in grade C (free rotation without resistance); grades B (rotation with resistance) and D lost nothing. A spinner that still meets resistance behaves differently from one that spins freely — the free rotation is the red flag, not the low torque per se.

### Axis 2 — Surgery: prevent rotation, protect the bed

The success protocols converge on the same moves regardless of placement timing:

- **Single-step undersized drilling** (stop one drill short) — used universally in Jang's immediate cohort; under-preparation of low-density bone in Galen's.
- **Near-universal grafting** — 15/16 cases in Jang (socket bone graft), socket grafting/sealing for immediate sites in Galen, gap management (graft/membrane) in Kim. The closed wound matters: it converts the low-stability site into a protected healing chamber.
- **Two-stage / submerged protocol** — Galen's entire cohort was two-stage loaded at ~4 months; Kim requires ≥6 months delayed loading; only 1 of Jang's 16 was submerged — flapless immediate non-submerged also succeeded, but with extended healing where ISQ lagged.
- **Caveat — do not overshoot**: the undersizing/compression dose is a lever with a ceiling. [[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]] §6 (Rosas-Díaz 2024: IT 25→63 N·cm by under-milling, ISQ 67.6→65.7 NS) and Coyac 2019 (excessive osseo-densification → micro-fracture, osteocyte death, osseo-destruction) bound how far compression can rescue a soft site.

### Axis 3 — Loading: wait, or load only under a proven floor

- **Default: delayed loading.** Kim mandates ≥6 months and calls it essential; Norton loaded at ~3 months (ISQ≥55 temporization gate); Galen used ~4 months (two-stage with ≥6 months post-loading success assessment); Sierra-Rebolledo's RCT never reached mean ISQ 70 → wait ~12 weeks.
- **Immediate loading, if the case demands it**: the floor is **IT ≥35 N·cm** (Bavetta 2019 via [[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]] Rule 5: fresh-socket implants with placement-day ISQ 57.6 — below the conventional ≥70 — survived 100% loaded on the sole criterion IT ≥35 N·cm).
- **Below the floor, change the prosthesis geometry**: Darriba 2023 — ≤20 N·cm implants survive immediate loading as **splinted prostheses**; >20 N·cm as single crowns. This is a prosthesis-design gate, not a survival line.

## What the marketing gets wrong — undersized drilling and OD have weaker evidence than claimed

Two interventions marketed as the answer to low primary stability have contested evidence in this wiki:

- **Undersized drilling**: the only RCT on immediate implants ([[immediate-implant/primary-stability/sierra-rebolledo-2021-undersized-drilling-immediate-tapered-implants-maxilla]], n=30, anterior maxilla) found IT non-significantly higher (41.36 vs 38.44 N·cm, p=0.654) and **ISQ consistently higher in the conventional group at every timepoint**. The authors read the IT–ISQ dissociation as compression/micro-damage from higher torque eroding RFA stability.
- **Osseodensification (골밀도화, OD)**: in vitro in type IV bone OD beats conventional under-drilling (IT 21.7 vs 8.9 N·cm, ISQ 69.8 vs 65.2 — [[implants/osseodensification/barbera-millan-2021-primary-stability-low-density-osseodensification]]), but the low-density-bone SR+MA ([[implants/osseodensification/mohammadi-2025-osseodensification-conventional-low-density-bone-sr-ma]]) found no significant ISQ advantage: primary MD 4.13 (p=0.13), secondary MD 1.78 (p=0.11); OD's only significant benefits were 12-month probing depth and palatal crestal bone level.

Practical read: undersized drilling as bed protection (stop one drill short, keep the socket walls) is defensible and is what the successful cohorts did; undersizing/escalating compression **to chase torque** is not supported.

## ⚠️ Gray zones and the counter-evidence

- **Free rotation (spinner) is the danger zone**: Cobo-Vázquez's only losses (grade C 7.89%); in Galen, all four failures were posterior delayed placements — the sites where a free-spinning implant gets loaded anyway. The multicenter cohort's HR 23.54 for absent primary stability ([[implants/survival/multicenter-2024-survival-analysis-private-practice-prospective]]) and Alrowis's 22.4% of removal reasons ([[implants/survival/alrowis-2025-factors-affecting-dental-implant-failure-retrospective]]) are the counterweight: the same condition that succeeds at 96% in selected hands is the dominant failure axis in unselected practice. Selection is the difference.
- **Eight of the success numbers are retrospective or case-level**: no held RCT has randomized *to low primary stability*; the interventional evidence (Darriba) is a subgroup/meta-level read on torque thresholds, and Bavetta's ≥35 N·cm floor is a retrospective threshold. Grade as moderate at best.
- **The 3-year horizon is unwritten**: no held study reports 3-year crestal bone in immediately loaded low-IT implants (named gap in [[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]] Gap #4 remains open for the low-IT arm specifically).
- **OD's claims outrun its ISQ evidence** in low-density bone — expect a marketing vs. evidence gap, and use OD for its tissue-preservation rationale, not for a predicted ISQ gain.

## Chairside decision rules

1. **Read the grade, not just the number.** Resistance on rotation (B) and even oscillation (D) lost nothing in Cobo-Vázquez; free rotation (C) carries the real early-loss signal. A site that spins freely gets a salvage protocol or a different plan — not a business-as-usual protocol.
2. **Contain the mechanical challenge, then wait.** Single-step undersized drilling + graft + submerged/two-stage is the reproducible recipe across four cohorts. Extended healing (6–14 extra weeks, Jang) is a tool, not a complication.
3. **Delayed loading by default; if immediate loading is demanded, gate on IT ≥35 N·cm** — and below 20 N·cm, only splint.
4. **Monitor the trajectory, not the snapshot.** Serial ISQ trending (placement → ~4–13 weeks) is the decision instrument; Norton and Jang both show the absolute initial value is misleading. ISQ ≥55 permits temporization even at very low IT (Norton).
5. **Never chase torque into the crest.** Compression-driven torque buys no ISQ (Rosas-Díaz) and can destroy the bed (Coyac). If the site reads low, accept the low reading and run the waiting protocol.

## Named gaps — deliberately not filled in

- No held study randomizes *to* the absence of primary stability; every success number is observational.
- The IT ≥35 N·cm immediate-loading floor rests on one retrospective cohort (Bavetta) bracketed by one SR+MA (Darriba) — concordant but thin.
- No held 3-year data on crestal bone for immediately loaded low-IT implants.
- No held head-to-head of OD vs conventional under-drilling in **human** low-density bone with ISQ/IT endpoints satisfactory to a meta-analysis (the two low-density SR+MAs disagree in strength of conclusion).
