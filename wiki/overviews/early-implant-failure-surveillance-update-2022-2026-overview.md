## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 이 페이지는 2026-11 서베일런스로 추가한 조기실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF) 논문 25편(2019–2026)을 기존 [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]에 덧붙여 읽는 갱신판이다. 대부분 단일기관 후향 연구이고 12편은 초록만 확보했다.
> - **가장 큰 문제는 정의의 불일치**: 같은 "조기실패"가 부하 전 소실(Iványi), 식립 후 3–4개월(Abrishami), 6개월 이내(Gong, Griffin), 부하 후 12개월 이내(Masri, Chaushu), 1년 미만(Frumkin)으로 쓰인다. 비율을 논문 간에 직접 비교할 수 없다.
> - **조기실패율은 정의 따라 1.55–14.56%로 흩어진다**: Yuyuen 1.55% (114/7,368), Kang 3.4% (35/1,031), Abrishami 4.3% (42/983), Gong의 66–80세 군 14.56%, 측방 상악동 거상 후 Jonas 13.2% (21/159). 상악동 거상 후가 높은 쪽이다.
> - **임플란트 단위와 환자 단위 결과가 갈린다**: 항응고제는 임플란트 단위 OR 2.64이지만 환자 단위 보정 후 p=0.87, PPI도 임플란트 OR 1.91이지만 환자 단위 p=0.11. 한 환자의 여러 임플란트를 독립으로 취급하면 약물 효과가 부풀 수 있다 (군집 보정 필요).
> - **재현되는 인자**: 흡연 (Sezer OR 2.140, Jonas 2024 OR 6.09, Jonas 2026 OR 8.52, Sobhani OR 2.70), 짧거나 좁은 임플란트 (Yuyuen 직경 <3.5 mm OR 6.21·길이 <10 mm OR 2.06, Sezer ≤8 mm OR 2.089), 고령 (Gong 66–80세 OR 9.235, Griffin 70세 이상 +257%).
> - **술자·부위**: Kang 2019 (단국대)에서 수련의 식립 aHR 2.86, 하악 aHR 2.31 — 하악은 단변량 p=0.117로 보정 후에만 유의했다.
> - **약물**: 항생제는 방향이 엇갈린다. 페니실린 알레르기 환자의 실패율 17.1% vs 8.4% (Zahra, 보정 OR 2.22)와 달리 Fetner는 알레르기 자체를 독립 요인으로 보지 않았고, Gong은 술후 항생제 OR 0.481(보호)이지만 기존 RCT 메타분석(Mehrabanian, RR 0.66, 비유의)은 예방투여 이득이 없다고 했다. 대상이 술후 투여 vs 술전 예방으로 달라 단순 모순은 아니다.
> - **상악동 거상**: 측방 거상 후 골 중격이 있으면 OR 0.13(보호), 경치조 거상 동시 식립에서 치성 막 비후군의 천공률 30.4% vs 정상 9.5%였으나 초기 실패는 0이었다 (Li 2026, 초록만). 베타차단제 OR 4.05 (Jonas 2026)는 초록 수준이라 확정할 수 없다.
> - **비타민 D**: 결핍 시 EIF 약 4배 (Wiedemann, 리뷰 초록)와 통합 RR 1.87 (95% CI 1.01–3.45; Sodnom-Ish)은 크기가 다르고 후자의 신뢰구간 하한이 1에 가깝다. 보충의 인과 효과는 확정되지 않았다.
> - **예측 도구의 한계**: 프랙탈 분석은 군간 차이가 없었고 사후 검정력이 5.1–42.2%였다 (Iványi). 감쇠능 분석 장비 비교 (Hong)는 기준 장비와의 일치도일 뿐 조기실패 예측 타당도가 아니다.
> - **임상 takeaway**: 흡연·고령·좁고 짧은 임플란트·수련의 식립이 겹치면 보철 전 조기 소실 위험이 높다는 방향은 일치한다. 효과 크기는 정의와 설계에 의존하므로 수치를 상담에 쓸 때는 정의를 같이 말해야 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Surveillance update of 25 papers (2019–2026; 13 full text, 12 abstract-only) on early implant failure (EIF), mostly single-centre retrospective cohorts plus sinus-augmentation, drug, vitamin D and diagnostic-tool studies.

EIF rates ranged from 1.55% to 14.56% depending on the definition (before loading, 3–4 months, 6 months, 12 months after loading) and population; smoking, older age, narrow/short implants and trainee-placed implants recurred as risk factors, while drug effects (anticoagulants, PPIs) were significant at implant level but lost significance at patient level.

Because EIF windows, units of analysis and abstract-only evidence differ, the pooled message is directional rather than quantitative: report the definition alongside any rate, adjust for clustering, and treat single-study effect sizes (e.g. ORs above 6) as hypotheses.

## 세줄요약

조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF) 서베일런스 25편(2019–2026; 전문 13편, 초록만 12편) 갱신: 단일기관 후향 코호트가 대부분이며 상악동 거상, 약물, 비타민 D, 진단 도구 연구가 포함된다.

EIF 비율은 정의(부하 전, 3–4개월, 6개월, 부하 후 12개월)와 집단에 따라 1.55–14.56%로 흩어졌고, 흡연·고령·좁거나 짧은 임플란트·수련의 식립은 반복해서 위험인자로 나온 반면 약물(항응고제, PPI)의 효과는 임플란트 단위에서만 유의하고 환자 단위에서는 사라졌다.

정의·분석 단위·초록 수준 근거가 달라 종합 메시지는 방향성이지 정량이 아니다 — 비율을 말할 때 정의를 함께 밝히고, 군집 보정을 확인하며, OR 6 이상 같은 단일 연구 수치는 가설로 취급한다.

## Summary

The existing EIF overview ([[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]) already frames EIF as a failure to achieve osseointegration and maps risk factors and prevention. This update adds what the 2022–2026 surveillance sweep changes about *how to read* that evidence, rather than re-listing every factor.

## Key Contributions

### 1. EIF has no common definition, so rates are not comparable

| Paper | Window / definition used | Reported EIF |
|---|---|---|
| [[implants/survival/ivnyi-2025-fractal-analysis-as-a-predictor]] | loss before loading (no time window) | 24 cases vs 24 controls |
| [[implants/survival/abrishami-2023-analyzing-the-causes-and-frequency]] | about 3–4 months (text also says 3–5) | 4.3% (42/983 implants) |
| [[implants/survival/gong-2025-impact-of-age-related-bone]] | within 6 months | 66–80 y 14.56%; 51–65 y 0.64% |
| [[implants/survival/griffin-2025-insights-into-early-and-late]] | within 6 months (EHR) | abstract-level percentages only |
| [[implants/survival/chaushu-2023-the-effect-of-anticoagulants-on]] and [[implants/survival/masri-2023-the-effect-of-proton-pump]] | removal up to 12 months after loading (Introduction uses abutment connection) | 3.8% overall; PPI 5.4% vs 3.5% |
| [[implants/survival/frumkin-2024-effect-of-osteopenia-and-osteoporosis]] | under 1 year | 134 failures of 1,229 implants (all failures) |
| [[implants/survival/kang-2019-early-implant-failure-a-retrospective]] (Korea, Dankook) | early period, single SLA system | 35/1,031 (3.4% by table counts; text states 4.4%) |
| [[implants/survival/yuyuen-2026-the-prevalence-of-early-implant]] (Thailand) | nested case-control within one hospital | 114/7,368 (1.55%) |
| [[sinus-lift/lateral/jonas-2024-the-impact-of-sinus-anatomy]] | lateral sinus augmentation | 13.2% (21/159) |

Two of these (Masri/Chaushu, Frumkin) define "early" from loading or as under one year, which is broader than pre-prosthetic failure; [[implants/survival/golobdeeb-2024-effect-of-implant-maintenance-on]] reports early failure within 1–7 years of follow-up. The headline rates therefore measure different events.

### 2. Implant-level vs patient-level analysis changes the drug story

In the same Israeli cohort (687 patients, 2,971 implants), anticoagulant use was associated with EIF at implant level (OR 2.64, p = 0.01) but not after patient-level adjustment (p = 0.87); PPI use showed OR 1.91 at implant level but no patient-level effect (p = 0.11). [[implants/survival/sezer-2023-covid-19-as-a-factor]] likewise reports 3.1% at implant level vs 10.4% at patient level. Analyses that treat multiple implants per patient as independent can overstate drug effects; this refines the drug entries in [[overviews/systemic-host-risk-factors-implant-survival-overview]].

### 3. Factors that recur across settings

- **Smoking:** OR 2.140 ([[implants/survival/sezer-2023-covid-19-as-a-factor]]), 6.09 (lateral sinus, Jonas 2024), 8.52 (sinus augmentation, [[sinus-lift/jonas-2026-impact-of-hypertension-and-antihypertensive]], abstract), 2.70 ([[implants/survival/sobhani-2025-periodontitis-type-2-diabetes-and]], all failures, abstract). Direction agrees with [[implants/survival/fan-2024-smoking-early-implant-failure-sr-ma]]; magnitudes in the small sinus cohorts are likely inflated.
- **Implant dimension:** diameter below 3.5 mm OR 6.21 and length below 10 mm OR 2.06 (Yuyuen multivariable); length of 8 mm or less OR 2.089 (Sezer); length of 10 mm or less (abstract-level, [[implants/survival/ankaya-2025-evaluation-of-factors-affecting-implant]]).
- **Age:** 66–80 y OR 9.235 (Gong) and 70 y or older +257% (Griffin, abstract). Gong's 20–35 y band (4.99%) was higher than the 36–50 and 51–65 bands without explanation.
- **Operator and site:** trainee placement aHR 2.86 and mandible aHR 2.31 in a Korean single-system cohort; the mandible effect appeared only after adjustment (univariate p = 0.117).

### 4. Drugs and antibiotics: conflicting directions with different exposures

[[drug/antibiotics/zahra-2022-dental-implant-failure-rates-in]] (abstract only) reported 17.1% failure with self-reported penicillin allergy vs 8.4% in non-allergic patients given amoxicillin (adjusted OR 2.22), with clindamycin at 19.9%. [[drug/antibiotics/fetner-2026-self-reported-penicillin-allergy-alternative]] (abstract only, 179 patients, 374 implants, EIF 6.1%) found allergy itself was not an independent factor, and alternative antibiotics were associated with more surgical complications in exploratory analysis. This fits the earlier systematic review [[drug/antibiotics/salgado-peralvo-2021-penicillin-allergy-risk-factor-early-implant-failure]], which concluded that allergy per se cannot be declared a risk factor because the allergy and the substitute drug are confounded. Postoperative antibiotics were protective in Gong (OR 0.481), whereas the RCT meta-analysis [[implants/survival/mehrabanian-2024-routine-antibiotic-prophylaxis-and]] found no significant benefit of prophylaxis (RR 0.66, 95% CI 0.30–1.47); exposure (postoperative vs preoperative prophylaxis) and design differ, so these are refinements rather than contradictions.

### 5. Sinus augmentation raises the baseline

EIF after lateral augmentation was 13.2% in Jonas 2024, with a bony septum protective (OR 0.13), smoking harmful (OR 6.09) and lateral wall thickness borderline (OR 0.67 per mm, p = .054, although the paper's conclusion calls it significant). In transcrestal augmentation with simultaneous placement ([[sinus-lift/transcrestal/li-2026-impact-of-schneiderian-membrane-pathological]], abstract only), 123 implants had no early failures despite membrane perforation of 30.4% in the odontogenic thickening group vs 9.5% in normal membranes. [[sinus-lift/lateral/nguyen-2022-analysis-of-acute-sinusitis-related]] adds three retrieved implants with acute sinusitis after augmentation in which SEM-EDS and TEM showed organic coating and unusual elements but no bacteria (hypothesis-generating only), and [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/zhang-2024-risk-factors-for-implant-failure]] describes sudden mobility at 6 weeks in a transcrestal case.

### 6. Host factors and nutrition

[[implants/survival/frumkin-2024-effect-of-osteopenia-and-osteoporosis]] linked osteopenia/osteoporosis with first, second and under-1-year failures (p-values 0.0065, 0.044, 0.0044; no ORs reported). Vitamin D: [[implants/vitamin-d/sodnomish-2026-the-effect-of-dietary-factors]] pooled low vitamin D with EIF at RR 1.87 (95% CI 1.01–3.45), mostly from observational studies, while [[implants/vitamin-d/wiedemann-2025-vitamin-d-screening-and-supplementation]] (abstract only) states about a fourfold increase and recommends pre-operative D3 supplementation. The two magnitudes differ and neither establishes a causal effect of supplementation. [[implants/survival/golobdeeb-2024-effect-of-implant-maintenance-on]] found peri-implantitis in 9% without maintenance vs 4% with it, and 25% of implants with peri-implantitis had failed early vs 4% — an association measured over 1–7 years, not a pre-prosthetic effect.

### 7. Predictors and tools

Fractal dimension and lacunarity showed no group difference (post-hoc power 5.1–42.2%) in [[implants/survival/ivnyi-2025-fractal-analysis-as-a-predictor]]. [[implants/isq/hong-2025-comparative-analysis-of-novel-damping]] compared damping-capacity devices before loading (37 patients, 58 implants) and found strong agreement with the reference device for one device (r = -0.870) and moderate for the other (r = -0.473); this is device agreement, not validated prediction of early failure. [[implants/survival/munakata-2024-risk-factors-for-early-implant]] reviews graft-related risk with figures cited from other studies, and [[implants/survival/rosen-2024-multiple-dental-implant-failures-a]] describes 371 patients with repeated failure (abstract only).

## Methodology

Papers came from the PubMed surveillance sweep of 2026-11-08 for early implant failure in the 2022–2026 window plus Korean-affiliated earlier papers. Each paper passed the DOI dedup and retraction gate and was summarised in its own wiki page; this synthesis reads those pages, not new sources. Thirteen have PMC full text and twelve are abstract-only (flagged above). No pooling was attempted; rates and effect sizes are quoted as reported and differ in definition, unit of analysis and adjustment. Several source texts lacked tables, so some numbers come from narrative text.

## Results

1. EIF rates cannot be compared without the definition.
2. Report implant- and patient-level estimates separately; drug effects are the most sensitive to this choice.
3. Smoking, older age, narrow or short implants and trainee placement are the factors that appear across independent cohorts.
4. Antibiotic findings differ because the exposure (allergy, substitute drug, postoperative vs preoperative course) differs.
5. After sinus augmentation the baseline is higher, and sinus-specific anatomy matters.
6. Vitamin D deficiency is associated with EIF, but magnitudes (RR 1.87 vs about 4-fold) conflict and supplementation benefit is unproven.
7. No predictor in this set (fractal analysis, damping devices) has demonstrated predictive validity for EIF.

**Clinical reasoning drawn from the held pages (not new facts).** For a patient who smokes, is older, and needs a narrow or short implant, the combination points the same way across several cohorts, so plan for staged loading and closer early follow-up. Record the EIF definition used in the clinic's own audit so internal rates can be compared with the literature. Do not read an implant-level drug association as evidence that the drug itself causes failure until patient-level adjustment is checked. Final judgement rests with the treating clinician.

**Gaps.** A shared EIF definition, patient-level (clustered) analyses in all cohorts, prospective Korean data (only Kang 2019 and the earlier Korean cohorts), and trials of vitamin D supplementation with EIF as the outcome are missing from the wiki.
