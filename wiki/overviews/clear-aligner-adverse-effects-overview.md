> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **부작용(AE) 지형**: 가역적 투명교정(removable aligner)의 부작용은 통증·치근흡수가 가장 자주 연구되고, 치주변화·백색병소(White Spot Lesion, WSL)·발음변화·구취·TMD 증상·수면 중 악물기까지 폭넓게 보고됨 — 34편 종합 SR(Ronchi Lemos 2026)
> - **통증은 예측 가능한 곡선**: 삽입/교체 후 **24시간 내 정점 → 3일째 감소 → 1주째 최소화**. 임상가가 "1~3일은 참았다가 지나간다"고 미리 안내할 수 있는 신뢰할 해석 (Ronchi Lemos 2026)
> - **치근흡수(EARR)는 네 가지 이상 측정 렌즈로 수렴**: ① SR-MA 절대값 CBCT 9편 — 절치 치근길이 −0.56mm, 상악 중절치 최대 −0.74mm (Butsabul 2024) ② RCT AI 3D 표면 — 전체 중앙값 −0.72mm (CA 고정교정 유의차 없음) (Bespalez-Neto 2026) ③ SR-MA — 선형 −0.33mm, 체적 −4.37mm³ (Ronchi Lemos 2026) ④ **비교 CBCT SR-MA 2편(독립)** — Yıldırım 2026 (6편/392명, MD −0.50mm CA 우위) 및 Samulak 2026 (4편/1,204 전치, WMD −0.47mm CA vs −0.54mm FA; p<0.01) — CA가 FA보다 치근흡수 유의하게 적다는 신호를 독립적으로 재현. **수치 상의 차이는 측정 방법(절대/비교, 선형/체적, CBCT 3D/2D) 때문이지 근거 충돌이 아님**
> - **임상적으로 미미한 수준**: 심각 흡수(>20% 치근 손실)는 전체 치아의 **약 3.7–6.3%**에만 해당 (Butsabul 2024)
> - **가장 취약한 치아**: 상악 측절치가 가장 큰 EARR (p=0.002 vs 하악, p<0.001 vs 상악 중절치, Bespalez-Neto 2026); Butsabul은 상악 중절치를 최대(−0.74mm)로 봄 — **상악 전치부(중·측절치)를 함께 집중 모니터링**
> - **예측인자 없음**: 나이·성별·혼잡도·치료기간은 EARR 크기의 유의한 예측인자 아님 (Bespalez-Neto 2026) → 선제적 위험 계층화가 어렵다는 뜻
> - **압하/정출·발치·치근단-구개피질골 근접 시** 모니터링 강화 권고 (Butsabul 2024, 개별 치아 요인)
> - **치주·백색병소는 고정교정보다 유리**: CA에서 플라크 축적 낮음(SMD −1.58), WSL 발생률·심도·타액 우식균 수준도 정성적으로 낮음 (Raghavan 2023, 중등도 근거)
> - **플라크 제거는 칫솔 종류보다 표면 접근성**: 수동/전동 칫솔 간 플라크 제거 유의차 없음 → 원심·치은연 표면은 어느 칫솔이든 잔존 높음 (Arslan Çarpar 2026, RCT)
> - **동의서(informed consent) 실무**: 환자에게 반드시 안내할 항목 = ① 삽입 직후 1~3일 통증 ② 상악 전치부 미미하지만 불가피한 치근흡수(임상적으로 유의미하지 않은 수준) ③ 발음 일시적 변화·구취 가능성
> - **측정 방법 차이를 인지**: 치근흡수 "수치"를 인용할 때 반드시 측정 렌즈(절대 vs 비교, CBCT 3D vs 2D 방사선)를 함께 명시 — 2D 방사선은 3D CBCT에 비해 흡수를 과소평가

## Three-line Summary

This synthesis aggregates the held evidence on adverse effects of removable clear-aligner therapy. Pain follows a predictable 24h-peak → day-3-decline → 1-week-minimum curve. External apical root resorption (EARR) is consistently small across three complementary measurement approaches (CBCT SR-MA −0.56 mm absolute; RCT AI-3D median −0.72 mm, no CA-vs-fixed difference; SR-MA −0.33 mm linear), with severe resorption (>20% root loss) in only ~3.7–6.3% of teeth. Aligners appear more favorable than fixed appliances for plaque and white-spot lesions, while plaque removal is governed more by surface accessibility than toothbrush type. The synthesis closes with an informed-consent checklist and a note on how differing measurement lenses reconcile the disparate root-resorption numbers.

## 세줄요약

투명교정(가역적 장치) 부작용에 대해 보유한 근거를 종합. 통증은 "24시간 내 정점 → 3일째 감소 → 1주째 최소화"의 예측 가능한 곡선. 외부 유치단 흡수(EARR)는 세 가지 보완적 측정 접근에서 모두 미미 — CBCT SR-MA −0.56mm(절대), RCT AI-3D 중앙값 −0.72mm(CA vs 고정교정 무차이), SR-MA −0.33mm(선형); 심각 흡수(>20% 치근 손실)는 치아의 약 3.7–6.3%만. 플라크·백색병소는 고정교정보다 유리, 플라크 제거는 칫솔 종류보다 표면 접근성이 좌우. 치근흡수 "수치" 인용 시 측정 렌즈(절대/비교, 3D/2D)를 반드시 함께 명시. 동의서 항목과 임상 체크리스트로 마무리.

## 1. Scope and Evidence Base

### 1-1. What counts as an "adverse effect" of clear aligners

The adverse-effect profile of removable orthodontic aligners spans far more than pain. The largest held synthesis, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/ronchi-lemos-2026-adverse-effects-orthodontic-aligners-sr]] (34 studies: 10 RCTs, 6 non-randomized, 18 cohort), catalogs the full landscape — pain, apical root resorption, periodontal changes, white spot lesions, speech alterations, halitosis, TMD symptoms, and nocturnal clenching. Of these, pain and root resorption dominate the quantitative literature; many others are reported descriptively.

## 2. Pain — the Predictable Curve

Pain is the most frequently studied aligner adverse effect and the one with the clearest clinical translation. In [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/ronchi-lemos-2026-adverse-effects-orthodontic-aligners-sr]], pain peaked **within the first 24 hours** after tray insertion or change, declined substantially by day 3, and became minimal by 1 week.

**Clinical translation**: because the curve is reproducible, the clinician can set expectations before treatment — "expect the worst soreness in the first 1–3 days, then a rapid taper" — rather than reacting to a patient's unexpected discomfort. [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/caldas-2024-pain-ohrqol-satisfaction-aligner]] frames this within patient-reported outcomes (pain/OHRQoL as the most-studied patient-experience dimension).

## 3. Root Resorption — Four Measurement Lenses, One Conclusion

External apical root resorption (EARR) is where the held numbers look superficially different but actually converge. The apparent spread comes from **five different measurement lenses**, not from conflicting evidence:

| Lens | Paper | Metric | Finding | Why it differs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Absolute, CBCT, linear | [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/butsabul-2024-clear-aligner-root-resorption-cbct-sr-ma]] | incisor root length change | −0.56 mm overall; max. central −0.74 mm | Isolates aligner effect (absolute pre/post), not a vs-fixed contrast |
| Comparative, AI-3D CBCT | [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/bespalez-neto-2026-root-resorption-clear-aligner-ai-cbct]] | median overall EARR | −0.72 mm; CA −0.71 vs fixed −0.72 (NS) | RCT head-to-head; volumetric 3D surface; small n |
| Comparative, CBCT-only SR+MA (1) | [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/yildirim-2026-cbct-earr-clear-aligner-vs-fixed-sr-ma]] | pooled MD CA − FA | −0.50 mm (95% CI −0.79 to −0.21; p<0.001; I²=60.8%) | 6 non-RCT studies, 392 pts; CBCT-linear only; GRADE low |
| Comparative, CBCT anterior SR+MA (2) | [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/samulak-2026-cbct-anterior-root-resorption-ca-vs-fa-sr-ma]] | WMD CA vs FA, anterior teeth | CA −0.47 mm (95% CI −0.55 to −0.40) vs FA −0.54 mm (95% CI −0.75 to −0.33); p<0.01 | 4 CBCT cohort studies, 1,204 anterior teeth; anterior-specific OIIRR; independent replication of CA-vs-FA advantage |
| Comparative, SR single-arm MA | [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/ronchi-lemos-2026-adverse-effects-orthodontic-aligners-sr]] | MA linear / volumetric | −0.33 mm linear; −4.37 mm³ volumetric | Pooled across heterogeneous designs including 2D |

**Reconciliation**: differences in the point estimates are an artifact of methodology (absolute vs comparative baseline; 2D radiograph vs 3D CBCT surface model; linear vs volumetric outcome; RCT vs observational cohort). The **direction is now consistent across all five lenses** — EARR is real, measurable, but small. Importantly, two independent CBCT SR+MAs (Yıldırım 2026: MD −0.50 mm; Samulak 2026: CA WMD −0.47 mm vs FA −0.54 mm) now corroborate that CA produces significantly less anterior EARR than fixed appliances, while the Bespalez-Neto 2026 RCT found no significant CA-vs-fixed difference — the apparent discordance is explained by heterogeneous case selection and measurement modality differences (3D volumetric surface vs. linear CBCT), not true biological contradiction.

**Key thresholds to quote in consent**:
- Severe resorption (>20% root loss) affects only ~**3.7–6.3%** of teeth [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/butsabul-2024-clear-aligner-root-resorption-cbct-sr-ma]]
- No appliance-type, age, sex, crowding, or duration effect on EARR magnitude [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/bespalez-neto-2026-root-resorption-clear-aligner-ai-cbct]]
- Susceptible teeth: **maxillary lateral incisors** (most, per RCT 3D) and **maxillary central incisors** (worst absolute, per CBCT SR-MA) — monitor the maxillary anterior complex

## 4. Periodontal Health, Plaque, and White Spot Lesions

- **Plaque (PI)**: clear aligners show significantly lower plaque accumulation than fixed appliances (SMD −1.58; 95% CI −2.57, −0.58) [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/raghavan-2023-wsls-plaque-clear-aligners-fixed]]; confirmed by the largest multidomain SR+MA (MD = −0.437; 95% CI −0.507 to −0.367; p<0.001; I²=0% for RCTs; GRADE moderate) [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/kaur-2026-ca-vs-fa-periodontal-pain-qol-sr-ma]]
- **Gingival inflammation (GI)**: CA associated with lower gingival index than FA (MD = −0.233; 95% CI −0.296 to −0.170; p<0.001; **I²=0%; GRADE high**) — the most consistent periodontal finding across the held evidence [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/kaur-2026-ca-vs-fa-periodontal-pain-qol-sr-ma]]
- **Probing depth (PD)**: CA associated with lower probing depth (MD = −0.332; 95% CI −0.431 to −0.234; p<0.001; **I²=0%; GRADE high**) — note: 0.332 mm reduction is below established periodontal thresholds (≤3 mm healthy; ≥6 mm severe), representing a population-level trend rather than individual clinical significance [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/kaur-2026-ca-vs-fa-periodontal-pain-qol-sr-ma]]
- **WSL / caries bacteria**: qualitatively lower white-spot incidence, severity, and salivary caries-associated bacteria in aligner patients (moderate-quality evidence)
- **Plaque-removal mechanics**: manual vs powered toothbrush show **no significant difference** across any region or surface — residual plaque is highest on distal and gingival surfaces regardless of brush type, implying **surface accessibility** governs cleaning more than the device [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/arslan-carpar-2026-plaque-removal-powered-manual-toothbrush]]

**Clinical takeaway**: aligners have a built-in oral-hygiene advantage over fixed appliances across plaque (I²=0% in RCTs), gingival inflammation (I²=0%, GRADE high), and probing depth (I²=0%, GRADE high), but the absolute PD/GI reductions are modest. The obligation to target distal/gingival surfaces remains.

## 5. Other Reported Effects (Descriptive)

Beyond pain and root resorption, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/ronchi-lemos-2026-adverse-effects-orthodontic-aligners-sr]] reports — largely descriptively rather than quantified — speech alterations, halitosis, TMD symptoms, and nocturnal clenching. These should be part of routine anticipatory counseling (temporary lisping, transient halitosis around tray wear), though quantitative evidence is thinner than for pain and EARR.

## 6. Informed-Consent Checklist

Borrowed directly from the held evidence, the consent conversation should cover:

1. **Pain** — peaks within 24h of insertion/change, largely resolves by day 3, minimal by 1 week.
2. **Root resorption** — small, measurable, clinically insignificant in routine cases; ~3.7–6.3% of teeth exceed 20% loss; most likely on maxillary anterior incisors; not predicted by age/sex/crowding/duration.
3. **Oral-hygiene** — lower WSL/plaque risk than fixed, but distal/gingival surfaces still demand deliberate cleaning.
4. **Transient effects** — possible lisping/speech change and halitosis.
5. **Measurement caveat** — when quoting resorption figures, state the measurement lens (CBCT 3D vs 2D radiograph; absolute vs comparative).
