> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **결론**: 봉와직염(Cellulitis)은 **연조직에 퍼진 확산성 염증**이라 뺄 농이 없고, 농양(Abscess)은 **고여서 구획된 농**이다. 처치 분기는 단순하다 — 봉와직염은 원인 제거와 항생제, 농양은 절개배농(Incision and Drainage, I&D). 어려운 것은 둘을 가르는 일이다.
> - **용어 정리**: 구강내소염술(구강내 I&D)은 **시술**이고 봉와직염은 **진단명**이라 같은 층위가 아니다. 봉와직염이 농양으로 국소화됐을 때 구강내소염술이 적응증이 된다.
> - **스펙트럼**: 초음파 에코 패턴 분류로 부종(등에코) → 봉와직염(고에코) → 농양 전 단계(혼합에코) → 농양(무에코)로 나눈다 (Ghali 2021이 선행연구에서 채택한 분류로, 시간에 따른 진행을 직접 보인 것은 아님).
> - **임상 소견만으로는 한계**: 금표준 진단검사가 없고(Rrapi 2021), 봉와직염은 경계 불분명한 일측 홍반·열감·압통, 농양은 그 안의 압통 있는 파동성 부위다(Lewis 2014). 진찰만의 정확도는 Ghali가 인용한 선행연구에서 약 68%였다.
> - **초음파 감별 정확도(치성·소아·휴대용)**: 민감도 72.97%, 특이도 73.58%, 음성예측도(Negative Predictive Value, NPV) 79.59% — 초음파 초심 레지던트(약 10시간 교육)가 시행한 **중등도** 성적, 진찰의 보조수단이지 단독 판단 도구가 아님 (Frid 2025, n=90).
> - **초음파 한계**: 표재 공간(협·견치·교근하·악하)은 MRI와 일치했으나 심부 공간(설하·저작·인두)은 초음파가 전부 놓침 (Ghali 2021, n=20). 일반 연부조직 SR/MA 4편의 근거요약(Jeffers 2024, 초록)은 "신뢰성 있게 구분"이라 했으나 치성 근거는 이보다 낮다.
> - **처치 실측(정의상 성립하는 수치)**: Frid 2025는 농양 = I&D에서 농 확인, 봉와직염 = 7일 내 항생제만으로 해결로 **결과로 군을 정의**했으므로 53/53·37/37은 분기가 옳다는 증거가 아니다. Ghali 2021은 영상으로 분류해 봉와직염 16명 항생제, 농양 4명 배농 (소표본, 분기를 다른 방법과 비교하지 않음). 일부 저자는 둘 다 배농하라고도 제안한다(Ghali 서론).
> - **항생제 반응이 없을 때**: 봉와직염 항생제 시험의 실패율은 6–37%로 흩어지고 통합 18%(Obaitan 2016, 유사질환 혼동 시사). 작은 초기 농양이 항생제에 일시 호전돼 봉와직염으로 오인될 수 있어(Frid 2025) **재평가·재스캔 기준**이 필요하다.
> - **병리 — 치성**: 다균성·혐기균 우세 감염, 치근단 감염이 가장 흔하고 심부 근막공간으로 확산 가능(Ogle 2017); 확산성 감염 25명 파일럿에서 흡연 80%·취약계층 72%, 절대혐기균 우세, **배농 없는 선행 항생제가 흔함**(Bakathir 2009).
> - **에스컬레이션**: 발열 ≥38°C·개구장애·림프절병증·연하곤란·확산성 봉와직염이 있으면 원인 처치에 항생제를 더하거나 입원을 고려하고, **구강저 부종·호흡곤란은 즉시 상급병원 이송**(루드비히 앙기나 위험; 항생제 오버뷰). 안와·비강 주변 위험 삼각부는 농양을 놓쳤을 때의 대가가 커서 CT 음성만으로 배제하지 않는다는 주의 신호다(Lewis 2014, 증례 1건·비치과).
> - **근거 강도 유의**: 치성 감별의 직접 근거는 소아 전향 코호트 1편과 소표본 1편뿐이고, 14편 중 9편은 초록만 확보했다. 파동성·경결감 같은 **임상 징후 자체의 정확도를 시험한 치성 논문은 위키에 없다.**

## Three-line Summary

In odontogenic infection, cellulitis is diffuse soft-tissue inflammation with no collection to drain, whereas an abscess is a loculated collection; the treatment fork is source removal plus antibiotics for cellulitis versus incision and drainage for abscess, and in the one dental cohort that applied it, the groups were defined by outcome (pus at I&D vs resolution on antibiotics within 7 days), so its 53/53 and 37/37 are true by definition rather than proof the fork works, and some authors suggest draining both.

The hard part is the call itself: there is no gold-standard test, exam alone is limited, and handheld ultrasound in children with odontogenic infection reached only moderate accuracy (sensitivity 72.97%, specificity 73.58%, NPV 79.59%), with superficial spaces seen as well as by MRI but deep spaces missed entirely.

Pathogenesis in dental infection is polymicrobial and anaerobe-dominant from a tooth source, and antibiotics given without drainage are a recurring pre-admission pattern, so, as an untested inference, lack of response should prompt re-examination for an undrained collection, an unremoved source and deep-space spread; the evidence base is thin (two dental diagnostic studies, nine of 14 papers abstract-only, no study of clinical-sign accuracy).

## 세줄요약

치성감염에서 봉와직염은 뺄 농이 없는 확산성 연조직 염증이고 농양은 구획된 농이다; 처치 분기는 봉와직염 = 원인 제거+항생제, 농양 = 절개배농이며, 이를 적용한 치과 코호트(Frid)는 결과로 군을 정의했기 때문에 53/53·37/37은 분기의 검증이 아니라 정의상 수치이고, 일부 저자는 둘 다 배농하라고도 제안한다.

어려운 것은 감별 자체다: 금표준 검사가 없고 진찰만으로는 한계가 있으며, 소아 치성감염 휴대용 초음파는 중등도(민감도 72.97%, 특이도 73.58%, NPV 79.59%)였고 표재 공간은 MRI만큼 보였으나 심부 공간은 전부 놓쳤다.

치성 병리는 치아 기원의 다균성·혐기균 우세 감염이고 배농 없는 항생제 선행이 반복적 패턴이므로, 항생제에 반응이 없으면(추론) 미배농 농양·미제거 원인·심부 확산을 다시 점검해야 한다; 근거는 얇다(치과 감별 연구 2편, 14편 중 9편 초록만, 임상 징후 정확도 연구 없음).

## Summary

The question this page answers is the one that precedes every drainage decision in dental infection: is this cellulitis or an abscess? The wiki previously held the treatment half (drainage and source removal first, antibiotics only for systemic or spreading disease) but not the diagnostic half or the "what is cellulitis" half. This synthesis adds both.

**What the two states are.** Cellulitis is a diffuse inflammation of soft tissue; on CT it is poorly defined swelling with no discrete collection, whereas an abscess is a well-defined low-attenuation collection (Frid 2025, introduction). On exam, cellulitis shows erythema, edema and warmth, and an abscess is suspected where a tender, fluctuant area is palpated within it (Lewis 2014). On ultrasound the progression is staged by echogenicity: edematous change (isoechoic), cellulitis (hyperechoic), pre-abscess (mixed), abscess (anechoic) (Ghali 2021). Read this as an image-grading scheme Ghali adopts from earlier work (its reference 4), not as observed progression over time; it fits the idea that cellulitis comes before pus collects, but no paper held here shows how or when a cellulitis becomes an abscess.

**Why it is hard to call.** No gold-standard test exists (Rrapi 2021), and mimickers exist that look like cellulitis (Marchionne 2019 describes one in critically ill patients; Cranendonk 2017 lists others). Antibiotic trials of cellulitis show failure rates of 6–37% (pooled 18%), a spread the authors suggest may reflect mimic confusion (Obaitan 2016). In dental infection the direct evidence is two studies: a prospective pediatric cohort of handheld point-of-care ultrasound (Frid 2025) and a 20-patient comparison with MRI (Ghali 2021).

**What decides treatment.** Drainage and source removal are the primary determinants of success in dentoalveolar infection, and one study found no-antibiotic outcomes equal to broad-spectrum when drainage was established (Teoh 2021). Systemic antibiotics are reserved for fever ≥38°C, trismus, lymphadenopathy, spreading cellulitis, or difficulty swallowing or breathing, otherwise definitive dental treatment comes first (see the antibiotic overview linked below).

## 1. What Cellulitis Is — and Why There Is No Pus to Drain

Cellulitis is infection and inflammation spreading through soft tissue; there is no walled-off collection, which is why incision yields nothing and antibiotics plus source removal are the treatment (Frid 2025; Lewis 2014). The echogenicity grading Ghali 2021 adopts from earlier work describes a continuum: tissue that is only edematous, then cellulitis, then a mixed "pre-abscess" picture as pus begins to collect, then an anechoic abscess. Treat "pre-abscess" as the point where reassessment matters most — a small early abscess can improve temporarily on antibiotics and be mislabelled cellulitis, which the Frid 2025 authors name as a source of verification bias.

## 2. Telling Them Apart

**Exam.** Cellulitis: erythema, edema, warmth. Abscess: a tender, fluctuant area within it (Lewis 2014). History and exam separate the two in many cases; ultrasound helps in unclear ones (Long 2021, abstract). Exam alone is limited: Ghali 2021 cites earlier work with 68% correct by exam alone and 70% with ultrasound added (a citation inside that paper, not a result of it).

**Ultrasound.** In children with odontogenic infection, handheld POCUS by residents with ~10 hours of training gave moderate accuracy (Frid 2025). About one-third of positive scans had no pus, and some small or deep abscesses were missed under edema or because the child could not tolerate probe pressure. The authors position it as an adjunct to the exam, and the NPV near 80% as a way to support ruling out abscess in lower-risk cases together with the clinical exam. Ghali 2021 found ultrasonography equal to MRI for superficial spaces and unable to see the deep ones.

**Contrast with the soft-tissue literature.** The general skin and soft-tissue summary (Jeffers 2024, abstract only) says POCUS reliably differentiates; the Frid 2025 authors explain their lower figures by novice operators, a handheld device and complex facial anatomy. Do not transfer the soft-tissue conclusion to odontogenic infection without the dental data.

**CT/MRI.** CT or MRI show the collection and the extent of spread and are the tools for deep or complex infection (Frid 2025, introduction; Ghali 2021). One case report shows contrast CT reading preseptal cellulitis while bedside ultrasound found an abscess (Lewis 2014); with n=1 this is a caution, not a ranking of modalities.

## 3. The Treatment Fork

| Finding | Action | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Diffuse swelling, no collection, no systemic signs | Remove the source, antibiotic if criteria met, reassess | Frid 2025; Teoh 2021; antibiotic overview |
| Fluctuance, or a sonographic collection that agrees with the exam | Incision and drainage plus source removal; a scan alone is not enough (PPV 65.85%, about one-third of positive scans had no pus) | Frid 2025; Ghali 2021; Teoh 2021 |
| Fever ≥38°C, trismus, lymphadenopathy, dysphagia, spreading cellulitis | Add antibiotic (amoxicillin-based per the antibiotic overview) to source control and drainage, or hospital escalation | Antibiotic overview |
| Floor-of-mouth swelling or breathing difficulty (Ludwig's angina risk) | Immediate hospital transfer; beyond intraoral I&D | Antibiotic overview; Gargava 2022 documents Ludwig's angina as the most common space (24.66%) and I&D in 38%, not these criteria |

Observed outcomes in the dental studies that applied the fork: in Frid 2025 the groups were **defined by outcome** — abscess = pus at I&D, cellulitis = complete resolution on antibiotics alone at 7 days, with ambiguous or indeterminate cases excluded (14 + 6) — so 37 drained abscesses and 53 antibiotic-resolved cellulitis cases are true by definition, not evidence the fork works (and the authors note small abscesses can improve temporarily on antibiotics). Ghali 2021 sorted by imaging and then drained 4 abscesses and treated 16 cellulitis cases with antibiotics. Neither compares the fork against an alternative; Ghali's own introduction notes that some authors drain both.

## 4. Pathogenesis

**General cellulitis** occurs when the skin barrier, the immune system and/or circulation are impaired; diabetes, obesity and age are predisposing, and relapse follows persistent risk factors and lymphatic damage (Cranendonk 2017, abstract). This is a model built largely on lower-limb disease.

**Odontogenic cellulitis and abscess** start from a tooth: a polymicrobial, anaerobe-dominant infection, most often periapical, usually confined near the alveolar ridge but able to enter deep fascial spaces (Ogle 2017, abstract). In 25 patients with severe spreading infection, 80% smoked, 72% came from deprived areas, cultures were dominated by strict anaerobes (anaerobic streptococci, Prevotella, Fusobacterium), and inappropriate prior antibiotics without surgical drainage were common (Bakathir 2009, abstract). These are associations in a small pilot without a comparison group. Not all cultures are anaerobe-dominant: Ghali 2021 reports more aerobic (65.7%) than anaerobic growth in its 20 patients, polymicrobial overall. The same authors state that whether bacterial factors convert a localized abscess into a spreading infection remains open. How a diffuse dental cellulitis turns into a collection is **not explained by any paper held here.**

## 5. When Antibiotics Do Not Work

Cellulitis antibiotic trials fail in 6–37% (pooled 18% intention-to-treat), a wide variation the authors suggest "may be due to" confusion of cellulitis with its mimics and a doubtful diagnostic label (Obaitan 2016, abstract). Non-infectious lookalikes exist (Marchionne 2019). For dental infection the held evidence points to a different, more specific explanation worth checking first: an undrained abscess or an unremoved source — prior antibiotics without drainage were common in spreading infections (Bakathir 2009), and small abscesses can masquerade as responding cellulitis (Frid 2025). That reading is an inference from these pieces, not a tested finding. [미검증]

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **Source first.** Identify and plan removal of the dental source in every case; drainage and source removal drive success (Teoh 2021).
2. **Look for a collection.** Fluctuance on exam; ultrasound for superficial facial and cheek spaces if available, accepting moderate accuracy and no value for deep spaces. Correlate a positive scan with the exam before intervening (Frid 2025).
3. **Do not rule out by a negative scan alone.** NPV near 80% leaves roughly one in five negatives wrong in the pediatric cohort; pair with exam and reassess (Frid 2025). Adults and experienced operators are not covered by that figure.
4. **Antibiotic only on criteria.** Localized abscess with no systemic signs: drain; spreading cellulitis, fever ≥38°C, trismus, lymphadenopathy, dysphagia: antibiotic added (antibiotic overview).
5. **Set a reassessment trigger.** If swelling persists or returns on antibiotics, look again for pus, for the unremoved source and for deep-space spread; a negative repeat ultrasound does not exclude deep spaces (Ghali 2021: none of the 8 deep spaces seen), so use CT/MRI if deep spaces are suspected. [미검증 — inference]
6. **Escalate by location and signs.** Floor-of-mouth swelling or breathing difficulty means immediate hospital transfer (antibiotic overview). A danger-zone (periorbital/nasal) infection raises the cost of a missed abscess and a negative CT does not rule one out (Lewis 2014, n=1, non-dental).

## Bottom Line (한 줄 결론)

봉와직염은 농이 아직 모이지 않은 확산성 염증이라 배농할 것이 없고, 농양은 모인 농이라 절개배농이 답이다 — 그리고 그 둘을 가르는 일에는 금표준이 없어, 진찰과 초음파(치성 소아 근거는 중등도)를 함께 쓰고 항생제에 반응이 없으면(추론, 미검증) 미배농 농양·미제거 원인·심부 공간 확산을 다시 점검한다 — 초음파가 심부를 못 보므로 음성이라도 배제되지 않고 필요하면 CT/MRI.
