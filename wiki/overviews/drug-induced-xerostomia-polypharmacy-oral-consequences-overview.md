## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 결론**: 5종 이상 다약제복용 (Polypharmacy) → 구강건조증 (Xerostomia) OR 2.05 (95% CI 1.31-3.22, I2=0%; Srisanoi 2026 SR+MA 23편). 개별 약제 중 아미트립틸린 (Amitriptyline) 항콜린 작용이 가장 강력한 단일약제 신호 (dry mouth OR 11.60, NNH 2.89; Brueckle 2023 SR+MA 23 RCT).
> - **항콜린 약물 클래스 효과**: 항콜린 부담 (Anticholinergic Burden) 높을수록 구강건조 연관이 뚜렷, 중간·낮음은 불명확; 4편 연구에서 유의 연관; 유병률 10.6-59.2% (Prado-Mel 2022 SR 10편).
> - **다약제복용 → 치아 소실 선형 관계**: 0→5종 복용 시 잔존 자연치아 16 → 12개(SD 9→8), 치과 합병증 1→2개 (Cannon 2023 SR 9편, 37,459명).
> - **구강건조 → 구강안면통증**: 약물 유발 구강건조증과 구강안면통증 (Orofacial Pain) 사이에 양의 연관 보고 (설문 점수 7.14 vs 5.04; Kohli 2023 SR 7편 1,029명). 단 타액 분비량 측정치와 약물 사용은 연관 없음 — 주관적 증상과 객관적 유량의 해리(dissociation).
> - **구강건조 → MRONJ 경로 가설**: 타액 분비량 감소가 약물관련 악골괴사증 (MRONJ)과 유의하게 연관된 연구 4편 중 3편 (Suryani 2023 SR 10편 272례); 타액 IL-6·MMP9·RBMS3 등 바이오마커 상승도 단일연구에서 보고. 인과 관계는 미확립 — 가설 수준.
> - **다약제복용 → 구강 전반 악화**: 구강건강평가도구 (OHAT) 점수 일관된 악화, 구강 칸디다증 위험 증가 (3종 이상 질환 + 6종 이상 약물; Srisanoi 2026).
> - **우울증 동반**: 구강건조증을 가진 65세 이상 노인에서 우울증 유병률 최대 64%; 자의식·긴장·집중 저하 등 6가지 심리적 결과도 보고 (Cannon 2023). **치과 진료가 우울증·고립의 간접 지표가 될 수 있음**.
> - **항콜린 감량의 한계**: ADS 2점 감소 중재를 실시한 단 1편의 RCT에서 4주 후 타액 분비 변화 없음 (Prado-Mel 2022) — 단기 deprescribing만으로는 구강건조가 즉각 회복되지 않는다.
> - **임상 전략 순서**: ① 약물 검토·deprescribing 시도(예방 우선); ② 항콜린 부담 측정도구(ARS/ACB/DBI)로 처방 평가; ③ 항콜린 약물 대체 가능 여부 주치의와 협의; ④ 타액 대체제·불소·구강 수분 보조; ⑤ 정기 DMFT·OHAT 모니터링; ⑥ 7종 이상 약물 복용 환자는 MRONJ 처치 전 타액 분비 평가.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 6 papers (2 SR+MA, 3 SR, 1 SR) on drug-induced xerostomia across mechanisms, drug classes, and consequences in older adults: pooled evidence establishes polypharmacy (>5 medications) as independently associated with dry mouth (OR = 2.05, 95% CI 1.31–3.22, I2=0%; Srisanoi 2026 SR+MA), while amitriptyline shows the largest single-drug effect size — dry mouth OR 11.60 (95% CI 6.42–20.98), NNH 2.89 — in a placebo-controlled 23-RCT SR+MA (Brueckle 2023).

Consequences compound: Cannon's 2023 SR (9 studies, 37,459 elderly) shows natural teeth fall linearly as medicine count rises (16 to 12 as drugs go from 0 to 5) and depression prevalence reaches 64% in dry-mouth older adults; Kohli's 2023 SR identifies a positive association between medication-induced xerostomia and orofacial pain (pain inventory 7.14 vs 5.04) with the dissociation that salivary flow measurements show no association with medication use; and Suryani's 2023 SR raises a hypothesis that drug-induced salivary reduction may contribute to MRONJ pathogenesis through reduced first-line mucosal defense.

Anticholinergic burden is the drug-class signal with the clearest evidence (Prado-Mel 2022 SR, 10 studies, significant in 4) but the only RCT (ADS reduced by 2 units) found no improvement in salivary flow at 4 weeks, limiting simple deprescribing as an immediate solution and shifting the clinical priority from cure to prevention and monitoring.

임상 교훈: 단기 deprescribing만으로 타액이 즉각 회복되지 않는다(유일 RCT, 4주 NS); 처방 최적화는 예방 단계에서 필요; 항콜린 부담 측정→주치의 협의→타액 대체제·불소·OHAT 모니터링·MRONJ 고위험 시 타액 평가가 임상 흐름.

## Summary

This overview synthesizes 6 papers on medication-induced xerostomia — scattered across geriatric-dentistry, oral-medicine/salivary-chemosensory, and drug categories — that had not been linked to an existing overview. Together they address the full clinical pipeline: which drug classes cause xerostomia, how the burden scales with polypharmacy count, what downstream consequences develop, and how far pharmacological intervention can reverse the process.

### Axis 1 — Quantifying the drug-count effect

**Polypharmacy (>5 drugs) → dry mouth OR 2.05.** Srisanoi's 2026 SR+MA (23 studies, 75+ age cohort) pooled two self-report dry-mouth studies and found a significant, zero-heterogeneity association. The polypharmacy signal tracks across outcomes: poorer OHAT scores (two studies consistently), loop diuretics, proton pump inhibitors and cardiac agents all individually over-represented in the dry-mouth group. The multimorbidity exposure, by contrast, produced non-significant heterogeneous dry-mouth ORs (OR 1.87, 95% CI 0.50–7.00, I2=85%), suggesting it is the drug count rather than the disease count per se that drives the oral harm.

Cannon's 2023 SR (9 studies, 37,459 adults aged 65+) adds a teeth-loss trajectory: remaining natural teeth 16 (SD 9) at 0 drugs falling linearly to 12 (SD 8) at 5 drugs; dental complications rising from 1 to 2 per patient. Psychological burden is layered on top — depression prevalence up to 64%, plus self-consciousness, irritability and difficulty completing daily tasks reported in two studies. This positions xerostomia as a gateway to broader systemic harm that a tooth count alone does not capture.

### Axis 2 — Drug-class hierarchy

**Anticholinergic drugs are the clearest class-level signal** (Prado-Mel 2022 SR, 10 studies including 1 RCT). High anticholinergic burden showed a positive association with xerostomia in 4 of the 10 studies, and the association was consistently stronger at high burden than at low or intermediate levels. The scales used (ARS, ACB, ADS, DBI) vary between studies, which is the main methodological obstacle to pooling. Xerophthalmia data were too sparse to conclude.

**Amitriptyline is the highest-signal single drug.** Brueckle's 2023 SR+MA (23 placebo-controlled RCTs, 4217 adults, multiple indications) provides the only within-class placebo-controlled quantification: anticholinergic ADRs pooled OR 7.41 (NNH 2.89), with dry mouth the dominant symptom — individual dry mouth OR 11.60 (95% CI 6.42–20.98, 15 studies) and aggregated dry-mouth category OR 11.10. Critically, the effect was **dose-independent** (meta-regression p=0.40), meaning there is no safe low-dose window — any amitriptyline prescription carries near-certain xerostomia risk. Non-anticholinergic ADRs were not elevated (OR 1.18), which validates the mechanistic specificity of the dry-mouth signal.

A dental-practice implication: antidepressants appeared in all four Kohli 2023 studies that named a drug class, placing this drug category at the interface of psychiatric and orofacial pain management.

### Axis 3 — Downstream consequences

**Orofacial pain.** Kohli's 2023 SR (7 studies, 1029 patients) finds a positive association between medication-induced xerostomia and orofacial pain across multiple study designs (xerostomia inventory 7.14 vs 5.04, p<0.001; OR 1.04 in dementia residents). The notable dissociation: **salivary flow measurements showed no association with medication use**, only the subjective symptom. This means that measuring salivary flow alone will not identify patients at orofacial pain risk — the patient's self-reported symptom is the relevant signal, not the volume measurement.

**MRONJ pathway hypothesis.** Suryani's 2023 SR (10 observational studies, 272 MRONJ cases) asks whether medication-induced salivary changes contribute to MRONJ pathogenesis. Reduced salivary flow was significant in 3 of 4 studies measuring it (Liao p=0.017, Kuo p=0.0024, Stockmann p=0.039). Single studies identified elevated IL-6, IL-1 family, MMP9, hypotaurine, and RBMS3 in the saliva of MRONJ patients. The evidence is hypothesis-generating — small, heterogeneous, non-standardised samples — but it opens a new clinical action point: in patients taking xerostomia-inducing drugs who are planned for invasive bone surgery, pre-procedural salivary assessment may have prognostic value.

### Axis 4 — Can deprescribing reverse xerostomia?

The answer, from the only RCT in Prado-Mel 2022, is no — at least not rapidly. When anticholinergic drug scores (ADS) were reduced by a median of 2 units, salivary flow was unchanged at 4 weeks. This is an important clinical reframe: **medication optimisation should occur before xerostomia develops, not as a cure for established dry mouth.** The glandular hypofunction likely involves acinar atrophy that does not reverse on the 4-week timescale of the RCT.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **처방 검토 시점**: 신규 5종 이상 약물 시작, 신규 항콜린 계열(항우울제·항히스타민·과민성방광제) 추가, 또는 구강건조 호소 시 즉시 약물 목록 검토. 항콜린 부담 측정도구(ACB/ARS) 사용.
2. **아미트립틸린 복용 환자**: 처방 시작과 동시에 구강건조증 고위험으로 간주(OR 11.60). 정기 DMFT·타액 점도 평가. 불소 바니시 주기 단축. 복용 중단 불가 시 타액 대체제 선제 처방.
3. **오랄페인 호소**: 약물 복용 환자의 구강안면통증은 타액 유량 측정보다 자가보고 구강건조 설문지(예: XI, OHIP 건조 문항)가 진단 신호로 우월. 설문 시행 후 통증 원인 감별.
4. **MRONJ 고위험 처치 전**: 5종 이상 약물 + 비스포스포네이트/데노수맙 병용 환자에서 타액 분비량 및 타액 내 염증 바이오마커 평가를 고려(근거는 관찰 수준이나 위험 대비 비용 낮음).
5. **항콜린 감량 기대치**: 감량이 가능해도 타액이 4주 이내 회복된다고 환자에게 안내하지 말 것 — 단일 RCT에서 즉각 타액 개선 없음. 장기적 효과는 아직 미확립.
6. **고령 환자 통합 케어**: 구강건조·우울증·영양 섭취 불량(malnutrition)이 삼각 연결됨(Cannon 2023). 치과 진료에서 우울 증상 확인 시 노인전문의·영양사로 연결.

## Gaps & Future Research

- 항콜린 감량 RCT: 장기(>6개월) 효과와 타액 회복 궤적 불명확.
- 타액 바이오마커(IL-6, MMP9)의 MRONJ 예측 전향 연구: 현재 관찰·후향 데이터만.
- 비항콜린 다약제복용(혈압약·이뇨제·PPI 혼합) 환자에서 타액 유량 감소의 독립 기여도.
- 구강건조 → 구강안면통증 경로의 기전: 중추성 감각화인지, 윤활 소실에 의한 마찰 통증인지 불명확.
