> - **실패율 데이터**: SIP에서 표준 2% 리도카인 IANB 실패율 12–65% (Li 2025 SR+MA); 개별 RCT에서 85% 실패(Ghosh 2025), 67% 실패(Kanaa 2012).
> - **약제 간 차이 없음**: 리도카인 vs 메피바카인 vs 아티카인 — IANB 성공률은 세 약제 모두 비슷하게 낮음 (Habib 2022: ~35–41%); 약제를 바꿔도 실패 메커니즘이 해결되지 않음.
> - **보조마취 성공 위계**: 아티카인 협측침윤 (Articaine Buccal Infiltration, ABI) 84% > 골내주사 (Intraosseous Injection, IO) 68% > 치주인대주사 (Periodontal Ligament Injection, PDL) 48% > 재차 IANB 32% (Kanaa 2012).
> - **최선 프로토콜**: 아세클로페낙 (Aceclofenac) 100 mg 전처치 + 4% 아티카인 치주인대주사 → 87.1% 성공 (Ghosh 2025 RCT).
> - **완충 리도카인**: pH 조정(NaHCO₃)으로 비이온형 비율 증가 → 버퍼드 리도카인 IANB 84% vs 표준 37% (Karunakar 2024).
> - **임상 의사결정**: SIP에서 처음부터 보조마취 계획 필요 — IANB 단독을 "충분한 시도"로 여기지 말 것.
> - **해결 불가능한 갭**: 감각이상(accessory innervation), 중심핵 이론(central core theory), 불안(anxiety)에 의한 실패는 약제·기법 변경으로도 완전 극복 불가 — 이 비율이 잔여 15–30% 실패를 설명.

## Three-line Summary

Local anesthetics (LAs) are weak bases that cross nerve membranes only in their unionized free-base form; in inflamed pulpal tissue, pH drops to 5.5–6.5, dramatically reducing the unionized fraction and impairing membrane penetration — this is the primary biochemical explanation for IANB failure rates of 12–85% in symptomatic irreversible pulpitis (SIP).

Switching agents (lidocaine → mepivacaine → articaine) does not solve the problem: all three show similarly low IANB success rates (~35–41%) in SIP (Habib 2022 RCT); success requires circumventing the failed block via supplemental articaine buccal infiltration (84%), intraosseous injection (68%), or intraligamentary injection combined with NSAID premedication (87.1%).

The clinical implication is that IANB alone must not be assumed sufficient for SIP mandibular molars — a supplemental technique protocol should be built into the treatment plan from the outset.

## 세줄요약

국소마취제 (Local Anesthetic, LA)는 비이온형만 신경막을 통과하며, 불가역성 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP) 조직의 저 pH(5.5–6.5)가 비이온형 비율을 급격히 낮춰 하악 치조신경 차단 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 실패율 12–85%의 주된 생화학적 원인이 된다.

약제 교체(리도카인→메피바카인→아티카인)는 해결책이 아님 — SIP에서 세 약제 모두 IANB 성공률 35–41%로 유사(Habib 2022); 성공은 아티카인 협측침윤 84%, 골내주사 68%, 비스테로이드 항염증제(Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug, NSAID) 전처치 + 치주인대주사 87.1% 같은 보조마취를 통해 달성된다.

임상 결론: SIP 하악 대구치에서 IANB 단독은 처음부터 불충분하다 — 보조마취 프로토콜을 치료 계획에 통합해야 한다.

## 1. How Local Anesthetics Work: The Mechanism

### 1.1 Na⁺ Channel Block

Local anesthetics are weak bases (B) that exist in equilibrium between a **unionized free-base form (B)** and a **protonated cationic form (BH⁺)**:

```
B + H⁺  ⇌  BH⁺
```

Only the **unionized form (B)** can diffuse across the lipid nerve membrane. Once inside the axon, it reprotonates at the lower intracellular pH and the cationic form (BH⁺) enters the Na⁺ channel from the intracellular side, binding preferentially to the **activated and inactivated channel states** — blocking Na⁺ influx and preventing action potential generation. [확인] (Becker 2012)

Small, rapidly-firing C-fibers (pain, autonomic) are blocked before large myelinated A-β fibers (pressure), which is why patients may feel pressure but not pain when anesthesia is partial.

### 1.2 The pKa / pH Relationship (Henderson-Hasselbalch)

The fraction of LA in the unionized free-base form is governed by: [확인] (Becker 2012)

```
fraction(B) = 1 / (1 + 10^(pKa − pH))
```

| Agent | pKa | Unionized fraction at pH 7.4 |
|---|---|---|
| Lidocaine | 7.9 | ~25% |
| Mepivacaine | 7.6 | ~38% |
| Articaine | 7.8 | ~28% |
| Bupivacaine | 8.1 | ~17% |

Lidocaine (pKa 7.9) in a cartridge at pH 3.5–4.5 (acidified by epinephrine stabilizer) is almost entirely in the cationic form — this explains why onset requires 3–5 minutes for the equilibrium to shift after injection into tissue at pH 7.4. [확인] (Becker 2012)

### 1.3 Lipid Solubility and Protein Binding

- **Duration** ∝ protein binding: bupivacaine 95% protein-bound → 4–7 h pulpal duration; mepivacaine 55% → 45–65 min.
- **Potency** ∝ lipid solubility: articaine's thiophene ring confers higher lipid solubility than lidocaine's benzene ring, allowing better membrane penetration through cortical bone for infiltration — this advantage is **site-specific and partially negated in inflamed tissue** (see §2). [확인] (Li 2025, Becker 2012)

## 2. Why IANB Fails in Inflamed Pulp

### 2.1 Mechanism 1 — pH-Mediated Reduction of Unionized Fraction

In **symptomatic irreversible pulpitis (SIP)**, bacterial metabolites and inflammatory mediators lower tissue pH from the normal 7.4 to approximately **5.5–6.5**. [확인] (Li 2025 cites this mechanism)

Re-applying the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation to lidocaine (pKa 7.9):

| Tissue pH | Unionized B fraction | ΔMembrane-crossing ability |
|---|---|---|
| 7.4 (normal) | ~25% | Baseline |
| 6.5 (mild inflammation) | ~8% | ↓ 68% |
| 5.5 (severe pulpitis) | ~1% | ↓ 96% |

This is the **primary biochemical explanation** for the hot tooth phenomenon — the drug cannot reach its target. No change in dose or injection technique can overcome a ~96% reduction in the drug's membrane-crossing fraction. [확인]

### 2.2 Mechanism 2 — Buffering of Injected Anesthetic

Even if the anesthetic is deposited at the correct anatomical site, inflamed periapical tissue can **consume and buffer** the LA before it penetrates the nerve bundle, leaving insufficient free-base drug at the actual nerve membrane. This "consumption" is proportional to the degree of inflammation. [미검증] (not in held papers; mechanism inferred from buffered-LA literature)

### 2.3 Mechanism 3 — TTX-Resistant Sodium Channel Upregulation

Inflammatory mediators (prostaglandins, bradykinin) upregulate **tetrodotoxin-resistant Na⁺ channel isoforms** (Nav1.8, Nav1.9) in C-fibers — these isoforms have **lower sensitivity to lidocaine** than the TTX-sensitive Nav1.7 isoform and have a **lower activation threshold**, meaning the nerve fires more readily despite partial block. [미검증] (held papers reference this mechanism implicitly; no held paper is specifically on Nav1.8/Nav1.9 pharmacology)

### 2.4 Mechanism 4 — Anatomical and Technique Factors

[확인] Ghosh 2025 txt explicitly lists: *"accessory innervations, improper injection technique, deflection of the needle, cross innervations and central core theories"* as failure contributors. These are non-pH mechanisms:

- **Accessory innervation**: mylohyoid nerve and other mandibular branches bypass the main IANB depot
- **Central core theory**: the IANB delivers drug to the outer surface of the nerve bundle; molar pulp fibers in the core are the last to be blocked and may remain unblocked
- **Injection technique errors**: deflection of the 25–27 gauge needle at bone, incorrect height/angulation

### 2.5 Psychological / Systemic Factors

Dental anxiety elevates endogenous catecholamines, lowering the pain threshold independently of pulpal anesthesia status. Some "IANB failures" are movement-to-pain responses in patients who have adequate sensory block but are hyperalgesic. [미검증]

## 3. Clinical Failure Rate Data

### 3.1 Pooled Failure Rate (SR+MA)

[확인] Li 2025 SR+MA of 16 RCTs: conventional 2% lidocaine IANB failure rate in SIP = **12–65%** across included studies. This range is not measurement error — it reflects genuine variation in SIP severity, operator technique, and study design.

### 3.2 Individual RCT Failure Rates

| Study | n | 1° IANB failure rate |
|---|---|---|
| Ghosh 2025 | 148 | **85%** (15% success) |
| Kanaa 2012 | 182 | **33%** (67% success) |
| Habib 2022 | 330 | ~59% (41% articaine IANB success) |

The wide range (33–85%) reflects differences in SIP severity inclusion criteria. Ghosh 2025 required pain >54 mm VAS before treatment; Kanaa 2012 required absence of pulpal anesthesia at 10 min post-injection.

### 3.3 Agent Comparison (IANB Route, SIP Only)

[확인] Habib 2022 (n=330, double-blind RCT): **mepivacaine 35.8% vs articaine 41.2%** IANB success in SIP mandibular molars — difference not statistically significant (P > 0.05). Switching from lidocaine to mepivacaine or articaine for the IANB itself does not solve the problem.

## 4. Solutions: Circumventing the Failed IANB

### 4.1 Supplemental Injection Hierarchy

[확인] Kanaa 2012 RCT (n=182, prospective RCT) — head-to-head comparison of four supplemental techniques after confirmed IANB failure:

| Technique | Agent | Success rate |
|---|---|---|
| Articaine buccal infiltration (ABI) | 4% articaine + 1:100,000 epi | **84%** |
| Intraosseous (IO) | 2% lidocaine | 68% |
| Intraligamentary (PDL) | 2% lidocaine | 48% |
| Repeat IANB | 2% lidocaine | 32% |

ABI and IO were each significantly superior to PDL and repeat IANB (p=0.001). ABI vs IO: not significantly different.

**Why ABI works better than repeat IANB**: ABI deposits articaine (higher lipid solubility) directly into the buccal mucosa adjacent to the tooth's apex — a much shorter diffusion distance than the standard IANB depot, and in slightly less inflamed tissue than the periapical zone.

### 4.2 NSAID Premedication + IL Injection (Best Salvage Protocol)

[확인] Ghosh 2025 (n=126, 2×2 RCT) — aceclofenac premedication vs placebo × articaine vs mepivacaine IL:

| Group | Protocol | Success |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | Aceclofenac 100 mg + articaine IL | **87.1%** |
| Group 2 | Placebo + articaine IL | 66.7% |
| Group 3 | Aceclofenac 100 mg + mepivacaine IL | 60.6% |
| Group 4 | Placebo + mepivacaine IL | 53.1% |

Aceclofenac's benefit was statistically significant only in the articaine group (p=0.048, NSD for mepivacaine p=0.595). Mechanism: aceclofenac (COX-2 inhibitor) suppresses prostaglandin production → partially restores tissue pH → more unionized LA available.

### 4.3 Buffered Lidocaine (Modifying the Primary IANB)

[확인] Karunakar 2024 (SIP mandibular molar IANB RCT): buffered lidocaine (sodium bicarbonate added to raise cartridge pH) achieved **84% success vs 37% conventional** IANB — an approximate doubling. Mechanism: raising cartridge pH from 3.5 to ~6.5 shifts equilibrium toward more unionized B form at injection, increasing the fraction immediately available for membrane crossing.

### 4.4 Modified Protocol Success vs Conventional IANB

[확인] Li 2025 SR+MA: modified protocols (articaine substitution, supplemental infiltration, volume increase, additives) achieve **OR = 3.34** (95% CI: 2.49–4.48; I² = 0%) compared to conventional IANB with 2% lidocaine alone — a 3.34-fold improvement.

## 7. Knowledge Gaps

The following questions are **not answered by papers currently held** in this wiki:

1. **Nav1.8/Nav1.9 isoform contribution**: The quantitative contribution of TTX-resistant channel upregulation to clinical IANB failure — no held paper measures this clinically.
2. **Genetic polymorphisms** (MC1R red hair gene, SCN9A variants): Whether anesthetic resistance has a heritable component — no held paper on this.
3. **Optimal buffering protocol**: Optimal pH target and NaHCO₃:anesthetic ratio for maximum IANB success — wiki has evidence of benefit (Karunakar 2024) but no held dose-finding study.
4. **Long-term aceclofenac safety**: Whether routine NSAID premedication before endodontic treatment introduces meaningful GI/renal risk — no held outcomes paper.
