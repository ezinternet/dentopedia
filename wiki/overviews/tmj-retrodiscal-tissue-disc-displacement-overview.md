## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **다루는 것**: 관절원판 후방조직 (retrodiscal tissue / 이중판대 (bilaminar zone))을 역학·생화학·세포·신경·영상 5개 축에서 본 논문 5편 종합. 위키의 기존 TMJ 오버뷰들은 **관리 사다리**(보존→중재→수술)를 덮지만, "원판 변위 때 실제로 무슨 조직에서 무슨 일이 벌어지는가"라는 **조직 축**은 비어 있었다.
> - **핵심 테제 — 주인공이 바뀐다**: 교과서는 관절원판 (articular disc)을 주역, 후방조직을 원판을 붙잡아 두는 수동적 "후방 제동 (posterior brake)"으로 그린다. 5편은 정반대로 수렴한다 — **관절원판이야말로 불활성 구획**(무신경·무혈관·치밀 콜라겐)이고, **후방조직이 적응하고·아프고·영상에 먼저 나타나는 활성 구획**이다.
> - **① 역학 — 제동장치가 아니다**: 돼지 후방조직 영률 (Young's modulus)은 생리적 변형(5–20%)에서 **<1 MPa**, 50% 변형에서야 최대 2.9 MPa (Coombs 2017). 병적 전방 변위를 물리적으로 저지할 강성이 아니다.
> - **② 그러나 균질한 한 덩어리도 아니다**: 후하방 부착부 (posterior inferior attachment, PIA)는 후상방 부착부 (posterior superior attachment, PSA)보다 강성 **2.13배**·강도 2.30배·콜라겐 1.93배·가교 (crosslink) 1.78배 (Donahue 2023). 뻣뻣한 PIA = 원판을 과두 (condyle)에 붙드는 닻, 무른 PSA = 관절융기 (articular eminence) 활주 허용.
> - **⚠️ 두 편이 부위 방향에서 어긋난다**: Coombs는 **측두골측**이 더 뻣뻣하다 하고(p<0.001), Donahue는 **과두측(PIA)**이 더 뻣뻣하다 한다. 둘 다 돼지·체외·초록만 확보(abstract-only)라 절대값 비교가 불가능하며, 프로토콜(점증적 응력완화 vs 인장)과 부위 정의가 달라 미해결로 남는다.
> - **③ 세포 — 약하니까 바뀐다**: 전방 원판 변위 (anterior disc displacement, ADD) 후 후방조직이 하중 견디는 섬유연골 (fibrocartilage)로 전환된다. 전구세포 유사 섬유아세포 FB2 증식 + 혈관주위세포 (pericyte) 유래 벽세포 (mural cell) 아군집 MC4 출현 → **MC4→FB2 FGF2·BMP5 곁분비** → ERK1/2(~6h) → SMAD1/5/9(~24h) (Yuan 2026). 즉 **원판을 되돌리지 못해도 조직이 스스로 원판처럼 되어간다**.
> - **④ 신경 — 아픈 건 원판이 아니라 후방조직**: 관절원판은 **신경이 아예 없다**(pgp9.5 음성). 후방조직만 비펩타이드성 통각수용기 (non-peptidergic nociceptor, MrgprD/A3/C11) ~20%를 가지며 CGRP+ 75% 배경 위에 얹혀 있다. 모든 TMJ 조직에 저역치기계수용기 (A-LTMR)가 없어 **기계적 자극은 고유감각이 아니라 통각으로 처리된다** (Alfaro 2025).
> - **⑤ 영상 — 사람에서도 후방조직이 먼저 움직인다**: 후방조직 T2 이완시간 (T2 relaxation time) 정상 34.4 ± 3.2 → 편측 ADD 반대측 37.8 ± 5.8 → 환측 41.6 ± 7.1 ms (p<0.001); 비정복성 (ADDwoR) 환측 43.3, 정복성 (ADDwR)은 35.8로 사실상 정상. 원판 자체 T2는 환측에서만 변했다 (Kakimoto 2024).
> - **🔗 가장 강한 수렴 — "편측"은 편측이 아니다**: 사람 T2(무증상 반대측 상승)와 돼지 scRNA-seq(무처치 반대측도 재형성, ECM 신호는 오히려 더 강함)가 **다른 종·다른 방법으로 같은 결론**에 도달한다. 편측 원판 변위 환자에서 반대측 관절을 정상으로 가정하지 말 것.
> - **⚠️ 남는 긴장 — 적응과 퇴행을 영상이 구분 못 한다**: Kakimoto에서 삼출·골관절염·골수부종 (JE/OA/BE)이 없는 변위 단독군(n=8)은 T2가 정상과 무차이였는데, Yuan에서는 염증 없이도 재형성이 뚜렷했다. **T2 상승은 퇴행(수분↑·콜라겐 붕괴)을 읽고, 적응성 치밀화는 오히려 반대 방향일 수 있다** — 현재 영상으로 둘을 분리할 수단이 없다 (가설, 미검증).
> - **임상 takeaway**: ①원판 재위치를 치료 성공의 정의로 삼지 말 것 — 통증원은 후방조직이고 자연경과의 회복 경로도 후방조직 적응이다 ②편측 변위여도 반대측을 본다 ③ADDwoR이 퇴행 위험 표현형(T2 최고치) ④후방조직 장력에 의존하는 원판 주름술·재위치술은 역학적 근거가 약하다(<1 MPa) ⑤그러나 이 5편 중 사람 연구는 단면연구 1편뿐 — **임상 결정 근거로는 낮은 등급**.

## Three-line Summary

Five-paper synthesis on the retrodiscal tissue (bilaminar zone) across five independent axes — tensile mechanics, regional biochemistry, single-cell biology, sensory innervation, and in-vivo quantitative MRI — assembling a tissue-level account of temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disc displacement that none of the wiki's existing TMD/TMJ overviews provides, because those are organized around management ladders rather than around what the tissue is doing.

The papers invert the textbook assignment of roles: the articular disc, classically the protagonist, is the inert compartment (aneural, avascular, densely collagenous, most anisotropic, highest glycosaminoglycan, and the least responsive on T2 mapping), while the retrodiscal tissue is too compliant to restrain displacement (Young's modulus <1 MPa at physiological strain) yet is precisely the compartment that remodels into load-bearing fibrocartilage after displacement (FB2 progenitor fibroblasts + pericyte-derived MC4 mural cells signalling through FGF2 and BMP5), carries the joint's only diverse nociceptor population (~20% non-peptidergic MrgprD/A3/C11 on a 75% CGRP+ background), and registers the earliest quantitative imaging change in patients (retrodiscal T2 34.4 → 37.8 contralateral → 41.6 ms affected).

The strongest cross-species convergence is that nominally unilateral displacement is a bilateral tissue event — human T2 elevation in the clinically unaffected joint and porcine remodeling of the untreated contralateral retrodisc agree by wholly different methods — while the sharpest unresolved tensions are a directional disagreement about which lamina is stiffer (temporal in Coombs, condylar in Donahue) and the inability of current imaging to distinguish adaptive densification from degeneration.

## 세줄요약

후방조직 (retrodiscal tissue, 이중판대 (bilaminar zone))을 인장 역학·부위별 생화학·단일세포 생물학·감각신경 분포·생체 정량 MRI의 5개 독립 축에서 다룬 논문 5편 종합으로, 기존 TMD/TMJ 오버뷰들이 관리 사다리 중심이라 비어 있던 **조직 축**을 채운다.

이 논문들은 교과서의 배역을 뒤집는다 — 주인공이던 관절원판 (articular disc)이 오히려 불활성 구획(무신경·무혈관·치밀 콜라겐·최고 글리코사미노글리칸 (GAG)·T2 최저 반응)이고, 후방조직은 변위를 저지하기엔 너무 무르지만(생리적 변형에서 영률 <1 MPa) 변위 후 하중 견디는 섬유연골로 재형성되고(FB2 전구 섬유아세포 + 혈관주위세포 유래 MC4 벽세포의 FGF2·BMP5 신호), 관절에서 유일하게 다양한 통각수용기 집단(비펩타이드성 ~20% + CGRP+ 75%)을 가지며, 환자에서 가장 먼저 정량 영상 변화를 보이는(후방조직 T2 34.4 → 반대측 37.8 → 환측 41.6 ms) 활성 구획이다.

가장 강한 종간 수렴은 "편측" 변위가 실은 양측 조직 사건이라는 것이고(사람 T2의 무증상 반대측 상승과 돼지의 무처치 반대측 재형성이 전혀 다른 방법으로 일치), 가장 날카로운 미해결 긴장은 어느 판이 더 뻣뻣한가에 대한 방향 불일치(Coombs 측두골측 vs Donahue 과두측)와 적응성 치밀화와 퇴행을 현재 영상이 구분하지 못한다는 점이다.

## Thesis

Standard teaching gives the articular disc the leading role in temporomandibular disorder (TMD) with disc displacement: the disc slips forward, the disc is what imaging is read for, and recapturing the disc is what splints, arthrocentesis, and repositioning surgery are implicitly aiming at. The retrodiscal tissue appears in that account as scenery — a posterior "brake" that should have held the disc back and failed.

**Five papers, using five methods that share no technique, converge on the opposite casting.** The disc is the inert compartment. The retrodiscal tissue is where the disease actually lives.

### 1. The retrodisc cannot be the brake — it is far too soft

[[tmj/coombs-2017-structure-function-relationships-temporomandibular-retrodiscal]] measured it directly: 20 porcine retrodiscal specimens under incremental stress-relaxation from 5% to 50% strain. Young's modulus stayed **below 1 MPa across the entire physiological strain range**, stiffening only at an extreme 50% strain to about **2.9 MPa**. Second-harmonic-generation microscopy showed why: collagen is well organized only immediately adjacent to the disc and becomes progressively disorganized posteriorly, with elastin at the disc boundary and mid-body. The authors' own conclusion is that the tissue is not stiff enough to resist pathologic anterior displacement, and that the classical posterior-restraint model needs replacing.

That single result carries a surgical implication the paper does not make but its own page notes: **disc repositioning and plication procedures that rely on posterior tissue tension are working against a substrate measured in fractions of a megapascal.** [확인 — Coombs 수치; 술식 함의는 미검증 (Claude 해석, cross-paper synthesis — 위키에 이 술식의 역학적 검증 논문 없음)]

### 2. But it is not one tissue, and the difference reassigns the tether

[[tmj/donahue-2023-characterization-temporomandibular-joint-disc-complex]] refuses to treat the "bilaminar zone" as a single structure. In the Yucatan minipig it separated the **posterior superior attachment (PSA)** from the **posterior inferior attachment (PIA)** and found them quantitatively different: the PIA is **2.13× stiffer**, **2.30× stronger**, **1.93× more collagenous** per dry weight, and **1.78× more crosslinked**; the PSA is the softest, fattiest, most heterogeneous region of the three sampled. The lateral disc stands apart from both, with **1.48–5.39× the glycosaminoglycan (GAG)** of the attachments and the most anisotropic collagen.

Donahue reads this as a division of labour: the stiff inferior attachment tethers the disc to the condyle so the two move together, while the compliant superior attachment stretches to let the disc translate over the articular eminence and recoils to help return it.

**This does not contradict Coombs so much as partition the claim.** Tethering the disc to the condyle through the normal, low-strain arc of articulation is a different mechanical job from restraining a pathologic anterior displacement, and a structure can do the first while failing the second. Reading the two together, the honest statement is: *there is a tether, it is the inferior lamina, and it is not a safety catch.* [미검증 — 이 조화(reconciliation)는 Claude의 cross-paper 해석이며 두 논문 어느 쪽도 이렇게 쓰지 않았다]

**⚠️ Where they genuinely disagree.** Coombs reports stiffness **higher in the temporal region** (p < 0.001); Donahue reports the **condylar-side attachment (PIA)** as the stiffer one. If Coombs's temporal-versus-condylar sampling maps onto the superior-versus-inferior laminae, the two results point in opposite directions. Nothing in what we hold resolves it: both papers are **abstract-only** in this wiki, so Donahue's absolute moduli are unavailable and only ratios can be compared; the protocols differ (incremental stress-relaxation to 50% strain versus tensile characterization); and the region definitions may not be the same anatomy under two names. This is flagged, not settled.

### 3. Because it is weak, it converts — and now we know how

If the native retrodisc is mechanically feeble, then the histologically familiar "pseudo-disc" — dense fibrous tissue with chondrocytes and increased GAG, appearing in long-standing displacement without reduction — is not an incidental scar. It is the joint restoring the load-bearing function the disc no longer provides in place. [[tmj/yuan-2026-distinct-mural-cells-fibroblasts-drive]] is the first cellular-resolution account of that conversion.

In a porcine unilateral anterior disc displacement (ADD) model with single-cell RNA sequencing at 5 weeks, **the retrodisc — not the disc — is the responsive compartment**: fibroblasts expand markedly after ADD while the disc barely changes. A progenitor-like fibroblast subcluster (**FB2**, EFEMP1/APOE, top pseudotime potential) expands and feeds both matrix-producing (FB1, FB3 — the latter with a fibrocartilage signature) and modulatory (FB4–6) fates. In parallel a mural-cell subcluster essentially **absent from healthy retrodiscal tissue — MC4, pericyte-derived, ECM-remodeling** — emerges and becomes the dominant signalling hub, driving fibroblasts through **FGF2–FGFR1 and BMP5–BMPR1A/BMPR2**, activating ERK1/2 at ~6 h and SMAD1/5/9 at ~24 h. Histology matches: loose, fatty connective tissue is replaced by dense tissue and **Alcian-blue-positive cartilaginous masses where healthy tissue had almost none**.

Note what that last detail means when read against Donahue. Healthy PSA is the **fattiest, most heterogeneous, lowest-GAG** region of the posterior complex; the adapted retrodisc gains exactly the GAG-rich cartilaginous character that Donahue measured as the *disc's* distinguishing feature. **Adaptive remodeling is, biochemically, the retrodisc becoming more disc-like.** [미검증 — Donahue(건강 기준선)와 Yuan(변위 후 조직학)을 이어 붙인 Claude의 종합; 두 논문은 서로를 측정하지 않았다]

The therapeutic move Yuan derives from this is deliberately unconventional: rather than reposition the disc, **pharmacologically amplify the tissue's own adaptation**. Computational screening (P-NET + Seurat → ConnectivityMap/DGIdb) nominated **zaprinast** (PDE5 inhibitor / GPR35 agonist), which raised fibroblast ECM and chondrogenic markers in vitro through the same ERK1/2 + SMAD1/5/9 axis and, in a rat ADD model, improved collagen organization in the posterior band and retrodisc and partly protected condylar cartilage and subchondral bone.

### 4. And it is the compartment that hurts

[[tmj/alfaro-2025-sensory-fiber-types-mouse-tmj]] answers the question Coombs left open — if the retrodisc is not a mechanical restraint, what is it for? — with the sensory half of the answer, and sharpens it in a way worth stating precisely.

**The articular disc is completely devoid of nerve fibres (pgp9.5-negative).** Disc displacement per se therefore cannot be a direct pain generator. The retrodisc, by contrast, carries the only dual nociceptor architecture in the joint: **~20% non-peptidergic nociceptors (MrgprD, MrgprA3, MrgprC11)**, absent from the capsule and anterior disc, layered on a **75% CGRP+ peptidergic** background (versus ~96–99% CGRP+ and near-total Htr3a+ coverage in capsule and anterior disc). And across **every** TMJ tissue there are **no low-threshold mechanoreceptors** — no parvalbumin+ fibres, no NFH+CGRP− myelinated afferents.

That last absence changes the interpretation of "the retrodisc is a sensory organ." It is not a proprioceptive organ. **Mechanical input to this joint has no non-nociceptive channel to travel in.** Compression of the retrodisc by a displaced disc is routed, at the level of fibre identity, as pain.

### 5. In living patients, the retrodisc moves first — and on both sides

[[tmj/kakimoto-2024-comparison-t2-values-displaced-unilateral]] is the only human study in this set: 65 unilateral ADD patients (15 with reduction, 50 without) against 17 volunteers, 1.5T T2 mapping with separate regions of interest for the disc, the bilaminar zone, and the superior and inferior attachments — an ROI scheme whose anatomical justification is exactly Donahue's PSA/PIA separation.

Retrodiscal T2 rose from **34.4 ± 3.2 ms** (volunteers) to **37.8 ± 5.8** on the clinically unaffected contralateral side to **41.6 ± 7.1** on the affected side (p < 0.001), driven almost entirely by the without-reduction subgroup (**ADDwoR affected 43.3 ± 6.8**; **ADDwR affected 35.8 ± 4.4**, essentially normal). The **disc itself changed only on the affected side** (31.5 vs 28.9 contralateral) — which Kakimoto attributes to the disc being avascular and densely collagenous, and therefore insensitive to the water-content shifts T2 reads.

**Four independent methods now say the same thing about the disc.** It is aneural (Alfaro), avascular and T2-unresponsive (Kakimoto), the most collagen-anisotropic and highest-GAG structure (Donahue), and the compartment that barely changes transcriptionally after displacement (Yuan). The disc is where the *displacement* is; it is not where the *disease* is.

### The strongest convergence: "unilateral" is not unilateral

Kakimoto's contralateral finding (37.8 vs 34.4 ms in clinically silent joints) and Yuan's contralateral finding (the untreated CADD retrodisc remodels too, with **even stronger ECM-reorganization gene signatures than the loaded side**) were produced by nothing in common — human 1.5T MRI in a retrospective cross-section versus porcine single-cell transcriptomics in an experimental model. They agree. Given the near-total methodological independence, this is the most robust proposition on the page: **in nominally unilateral disc displacement, the contralateral retrodiscal tissue is already involved.**

### The sharpest unresolved tension: adaptation and degeneration look the same

Kakimoto's subgroup with displacement but **no joint effusion, osteoarthritis, or bone-marrow edema (n = 8)** was **indistinguishable from volunteers** on T2 — the authors' conclusion being that displacement alone is a weak degenerative driver and the concomitant inflammatory findings dominate the signal. Yuan's model, by contrast, produced unmistakable remodeling from a purely mechanical insult with no overt condylar degeneration at 5 weeks.

These are reconcilable, and the reconciliation is clinically interesting. T2 elongation indexes **more mobile water and disrupted collagen alignment** — the signature of degeneration. Adaptive fibrocartilaginous remodeling is **densification**, which should move water content in the opposite direction. If so, **the two processes may be biochemically opposed while being invisible to each other on the same sequence**, and a normal retrodiscal T2 in a displaced joint could mean either "nothing has happened" or "successful adaptation." No study in this wiki has tested that. [미검증 — 가설; Kakimoto·Yuan 어느 쪽도 이 대비를 다루지 않는다]

Timing compounds it: Yuan's endpoint is **5 weeks** (early, adaptive), while Kakimoto's patients carry long-standing displacement, and Yuan's own page flags that adaptation can also run toward maladaptive fibrosis with stiffness and pain. Nothing here tells us where the fork is.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **Do not define treatment success as disc recapture.** The disc is aneural (Alfaro), so displacement itself does not generate pain; the pain generator is the compressed, densely and diversely innervated retrodisc. The natural recovery route in displacement without reduction is retrodiscal fibrocartilaginous adaptation (Yuan), not repositioning. This is the tissue-level explanation for why the wiki's clinical evidence — [[tmj/sa-2024-conservative-invasive-tmd-disc-displacement-sr]], 38 RCTs — finds conservative treatment matching or approaching surgical outcomes with no universally superior modality.
2. **In "unilateral" disc displacement, treat the other joint as involved until shown otherwise.** Elevated contralateral retrodiscal T2 in clinically silent joints (Kakimoto) and contralateral remodeling in untreated joints (Yuan) agree across species and methods. Examine and, where imaging is being obtained anyway, image both sides.
3. **Reduction status is the phenotype that matters.** Displacement *with* reduction looked essentially normal on retrodiscal T2 (35.8 vs 34.4 ms); displacement *without* reduction carried the whole signal (43.3 ms). Treat ADDwoR as the degeneration-risk group, not disc displacement generically.
4. **A quantitative imaging change is mostly telling you about inflammation, not about displacement.** With effusion, osteoarthritis, and bone-marrow edema excluded, displaced joints were indistinguishable from normal on T2 (n = 8). Do not read a T2 or signal change as a measure of how displaced the disc is.
5. **Be sceptical of procedures that load the posterior attachment.** At physiological strain the retrodiscal tissue is under 1 MPa. If any part of the complex is a mechanical anchor it is the inferior lamina (PIA, 2.13× stiffer). This is a mechanical inference from two animal ex-vivo studies — the wiki holds **no** surgical study testing it. [미검증]
6. **Grade all of the above low.** Four of the five papers are animal (two of them abstract-only in this wiki), the fifth is a single-centre retrospective cross-section at 1.5T with no histological correlation. There is no clinical outcome, no randomization, and no longitudinal human data anywhere in this synthesis. It reorganizes how to *think* about disc displacement; it does not by itself justify changing a treatment protocol.

## Gaps & Future Research

- **No human retrodiscal mechanics exist in this wiki.** Both mechanical papers are porcine ex-vivo, and both are held **abstract-only** — Donahue's absolute moduli are unavailable, so its ratios cannot be placed on Coombs's MPa scale. The regional-stiffness disagreement (temporal-stiffer versus condylar-stiffer) could be settled by one study testing PSA and PIA under a single protocol; nobody has.
- **Only tensile behaviour has been measured.** Shear, compression, and fatigue of the retrodisc are untested, and disc displacement is a fatigue problem as much as a tensile one.
- **Yuan's sample is tiny and one-sided**: 2 ADD and 2 sham pigs, female only, a single 5-week endpoint, no human tissue validation, surgically induced displacement, and the rat model **did not reproduce fibrocartilage formation** — a species discrepancy that is unexplained and that the drug validation nonetheless rests on.
- **Zaprinast has no human data of any kind** — no dose, no local-delivery formulation, no safety data in this indication. It is a mechanistic candidate, not a treatment.
- **The adaptive/maladaptive fork is undefined.** Yuan's page flags that the same programme can run toward stiffness and pain; nothing identifies when, in whom, or by what signal. This is the single most clinically consequential gap on the page.
- **Nothing images adaptation.** No study has T2-mapped a histologically confirmed pseudo-disc, so the hypothesis that adaptation and degeneration move T2 in opposite directions is untested — and until it is, quantitative retrodiscal imaging cannot be used to say whether a joint is coping.
- **No outcome link.** Neither retrodiscal T2 nor adaptation status has been related to pain, function, or treatment response in any longitudinal study. Every clinical inference on this page is mechanism-to-practice reasoning, not outcome evidence.
- **Alfaro's map is murine, 2D, and sex-imbalanced**, and it maps healthy joints only — how the retrodiscal nociceptor population changes *after* displacement (sprouting, sensitization, or neuronal input into the MC4/FB2 programme, which Yuan explicitly leaves open) is unstudied in both papers.
