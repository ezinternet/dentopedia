## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: "출현각도 (Emergence Angle, EA) 30°"는 보편 상수가 아니라 **조건부 역치**다. 같은 각도가 측정 깊이·기준점·지대주 높이·식립 깊이·임플란트 형태·보철 유형에 따라 다르게 읽힌다. 모체 오버뷰([[overviews/abutment-emergence-profile-peri-implant-tissue-overview]])의 "좁은 각도·높은 지대주" 결론을 유지하되, **어디서·무엇을 기준으로·어떤 임플란트에서 재는가**를 명시하지 않으면 30°라는 숫자는 임상 의사결정을 오도한다.
> - 축 1 — 깊이 의존성: 후방부 단일치 260개(206명)에서 프로파일 각도와 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)의 상관은 임플란트-지대주 접합부 **0–1·1–2 mm에서만 유의**하고 2–3 mm는 미유의(P>.05). 역치도 0–1 mm에서 근심 34°·원심 28°, 1–2 mm에서 33°·20°로 깊이와 근원심에 따라 달라진다(Han 2025, 초록만). [확인]
> - 축 2 — 기준점·식립 깊이 의존성: 골하 >1 mm 식립 84개·골정 83개에서, 골정군(플랫폼 기준)과 골하군(골정 기준)은 0–1·1–2 mm에서만 유의했으나 **골하 식립을 플랫폼 기준으로 잰 경우에는 0–1·1–2·2–3 mm 모두 유의**하고 역치도 더 작았다(Nam 2025, 초록만 — 실제 역치값은 초록에 없음). [확인]
> - 두 연구를 함께 읽으면 "2–3 mm 무의미"는 보편 규칙이 아니라 **골정·플랫폼 기준 특유의 소견**이다. 골하 식립에서는 골정 기준과 플랫폼 기준이 서로 다른 깊이 창을 보므로, 방사선 사진의 각도 측정 프로토콜을 먼저 고정해야 한다.
> - 축 3 — 높이가 각도를 게이트한다: 골수준 임플란트 192개(119명, 평균 7.43년)에서 **짧은 지대주(<2 mm)+넓은 각도(≥30°) 집합(SW)**이 MBL 0.79 ± 1.02 mm로 가장 컸고(긴/좁음 0.26, 긴/넓음 0.27, 짧은/좁음 0.46), SW vs 긴/넓음 +0.43 mm(p=0.001). 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis, PI)은 SW 25.6% vs 긴/넓음 9.1%, OR 4.19(95% CI 1.35–13.0, p=0.013). [확인]
> - 같은 연구의 결정적 역설: "MBL >0 mm"의 가능성은 지대주 높이 1 mm 증가마다 odds 37% 감소(OR 0.63, 95% CI 0.46–0.85, p=0.003)했고 **출현각도 단독은 유의하지 않았다**(OR 0.99, p=0.991; 교호작용 p=0.276). 즉 "각도가 해롭다"와 "각도는 무의미하다"는 서로 다른 결과 변수(MBL *정도* vs MBL *발생*)와 층화 조건에서 나온다. [확인]
> - 축 4 — 임플란트 형태·윤곽과의 상호작용: 골수준 임플란트 168개(평균 10.9년)에서 EA >30°의 주위염 31.3% vs ≤30° 15.1%(p=.04), **볼록 윤곽 + >30°는 37.8%로 각도×윤곽 상호작용 유의(p=.003)** — 그러나 조직수준 임플란트에서는 연관 없음(Katafuchi 2018, 초록만). [확인]
> - SR+MA 수준에서는 각도 단독이 약하고 윤곽이 강하다: Atieh 2023은 전체 MBL 유의차 없음(MD 0.80 mm, p=0.09)이지만 볼록 윤곽의 주위염 RR 2.32(57.8% vs 21.3%, p=0.02); Liu 2025는 전체 MBL p=0.082, EA >30° 서브그룹 MBL +0.190 mm(p<0.001), 볼록/넓음의 치조정골 재형성 (Crestal Bone Remodeling, CBR) +0.560 mm(p=0.001). [확인]
> - 인과·기전 축: 개 모델 20/40/60/80°에서 24주 MBL 0.07/0.11/0.23/0.38 mm(p<0.001)로 용량반응, ≥60°에서 접합상피 연속성 붕괴(Strauss 2024); 스플린트 중간 임플란트에서 넓은 각도의 봉쇄 실패가 집중(Lee 2025). 반면 5년 후향 155명에서는 단일(p=0.369)·연결(p=0.176) 크라운에서 EA–MBL 무연관, 브릿지에서만 약한 신호(p=0.042, R²=7.9%; Wach 2026). [확인]
> - 모순의 해석: Wach의 평균 EA는 31.8° ± 10.4°로 **매우 넓은 각도(60–80°)가 거의 없고**, 2D 근원심 측정·다변량 미보정·탐색적 분석이다. 전임상 용량반응과 상충하는 게 아니라 **임상 각도 범위 안에서 각도 단독의 신호가 약하다**는 뜻이다. 17편 scoping review도 "<30°는 그럴듯하나 불확실, 오목 윤곽은 일관되게 유리"라고 같은 방식으로 정리한다(Prpic 2026). [확인]
> - 임상 takeaway: ① 30°를 단일 합격선으로 쓰지 말고 **측정 깊이(접합부 0–2 mm)·기준점(플랫폼 vs 골정)·식립 깊이**를 먼저 명시 ② 뼈수준 임플란트·짧은 지대주(<2 mm)·볼록 윤곽이 겹치면 최우선 위험 — 지대주 높이 ≥2 mm 확보와 오목 윤곽이 각도 수정보다 큰 레버 ③ 조직수준 임플란트에는 이 30° 규칙을 그대로 전가하지 않기 ④ 연조직 퇴축 endpoint(Wang 2022: 0.13 vs 0.63 mm, p=.006; Siegenthaler 2022: 볼록 64.3% vs 오목 14.3%, OR 12.69)는 골 endpoint와 별도로 본다. [확인]

## Three-line Summary

A focused synthesis of eleven papers asking why the same "30° emergence-angle rule" produces significant, null, and even contradictory results across the literature: the threshold is not one number but an interaction of measurement depth, reference point, vertical implant position, abutment height, implant design and prosthesis type.

The angle–bone coupling is confined to the first 0–2 mm from the implant–abutment junction in equicrestal, platform-referenced measurements (Han 2025: cut-offs 34°/28° then 33°/20°;Nam 2025: significant only at 0–1 and 1–2 mm), but extends through 2–3 mm when a subcrestal implant is measured from the platform; the clinical danger cluster is a short (<2 mm) abutment plus a wide (≥30°) angle plus a convex contour in a bone-level implant (Misch 2025 SW: MBL 0.79 mm, peri-implantitis OR 4.19;Katafuchi 2018: convex + >30° 37.8%, interaction p=.003), while height itself, not angle, predicted the *incidence* of any bone loss (OR 0.63 per mm, p=0.003;angle OR 0.99).

Read before applying a numeric threshold: stratify by depth/reference/position, choose abutment height and contour before micro-managing the angle, restrict the rule to bone-level implants, and treat null 5-year single-crown data as evidence of a weak isolated signal within a narrow angle range — not as refutation of the preclinical dose-response or of contour-driven risk.

## 세줄요약

"출현각도 30°"가 왜 연구마다 유의·무의미·상반으로 갈리는지를 11편으로 좁혀 본 종합 — 역치는 단일 숫자가 아니라 **측정 깊이·기준점·식립 깊이·지대주 높이·임플란트 형태·보철 유형의 상호작용**이다.

골정·플랫폼 기준에서는 접합부 0–2 mm에서만 각도–골소실 상관이 유의하고(Han 2025: 34°/28°→33°/20°;Nam 2025: 0–1·1–2 mm만), 골하 식립을 플랫폼 기준으로 재면 2–3 mm까지 확장된다. 실제 위험 집합은 **골수준 임플란트 + 짧은 지대주(<2 mm) + 넓은 각도(≥30°) + 볼록 윤곽**(Misch 2025 SW: MBL 0.79 mm·주위염 OR 4.19;Katafuchi 2018: 볼록+>30° 37.8%, 상호작용 p=.003), 그리고 "골소실 발생"을 예측한 것은 각도(OR 0.99)가 아니라 높이(1 mm당 OR 0.63)였다.

임상에서는 30°를 합격선으로 쓰기 전에 깊이·기준점·식립 깊이를 명시하고, 각도 미세조정보다 지대주 높이와 오목 윤곽을 먼저 확보하며, 조직수준 임플란트에 규칙을 그대로 적용하지 말 것. 5년 단일크라운 무연관(Wach 2026)은 좁은 각도 범위에서 각도 단독 신호가 약하다는 뜻이지 전임상 용량반응이나 윤곽 위험의 반박이 아니다.

## Summary

> Scope note: this is a sub-overview of [[overviews/abutment-emergence-profile-peri-implant-tissue-overview]]. The mother page covers seven abutment/restoration levers across the whole tissue axis; this page takes only the emergence-angle threshold and asks why a single numeric rule fails to replicate. It does not re-litigate surface treatment, disconnection policy or zenith design.

The literature appears to disagree about whether emergence angle predicts peri-implant bone loss. Atieh 2023 found no overall between-angle MBL difference (MD 0.80 mm, p=0.09);Wach 2026 found no EA–MBL relationship in single or splinted crowns;Han 2025 found a correlation only near the junction;Strauss 2024 found a clean dose–response in dogs. These are not four estimates of the same quantity. They differ in **where the angle is measured, from what reference, at what implant depth, stacked on what abutment height and contour, in which implant design, and for which outcome**. The synthesizable claim is therefore not a revised cut-off but a **set of conditions attached to any cut-off**.

### Axis 1 — Depth: the harmful angle lives near the junction

Han 2025 (retrospective, 206 patients / 260 posterior single-tooth restorations, internal conical connection with platform switching, CAD-CAM abutments; abstract-only) measured the profile angle at 0–1, 1–2 and 2–3 mm below the implant–abutment junction rather than as one global emergence angle. Convex profiles carried greater MBL at every follow-up (P<.001), but angle–MBL correlation was significant **only in the 0–1 and 1–2 mm zones**, not at 2–3 mm (P>.05). C-index cut-offs were depth- and side-specific: 34° mesial / 28° distal at 0–1 mm, and 33° / 20° at 1–2 mm. In other words, the "30°" number is an average over a zone where the real threshold varies by roughly a dozen degrees depending on how deep and on which side you measure. Effect sizes and adjusted estimates are unavailable in the abstract.

### Axis 2 — Reference point and vertical implant position

Nam 2025 (retrospective, 167 posterior single-tooth restorations: 84 implants >1 mm subcrestal in 76 patients, 83 equicrestal in 73; same Yonsei group; abstract-only) added the missing design variable: which line the angle is measured from. Three schemes were compared — equicrestal/platform-referenced (EI), subcrestal/platform-referenced (SI), and subcrestal/adjacent-bone-crest-referenced (SB). EI and SB showed significant angle–MBL correlation at 0–1 and 1–2 mm only; **SI was significant at all three ranges including 2–3 mm**, with generally smaller cut-off values.

This is the direct qualification of Axis 1: "correlation dies beyond 2 mm" is a property of equicrestal platform-referenced measurement, not of the angle itself. In a subcrestal implant the platform sits apical to the bone crest, so a platform-referenced angle describes a different piece of transmucosal geometry than the same number referenced to bone. The abstract does not report the actual cut-off values, follow-up duration or adjustment variables, so the finding is a **direction of modification**, not a usable new threshold.

### Axis 3 — Abutment height gates the angle, and the outcome decides the verdict

Misch 2025 (retrospective radiographic cohort, 192 bone-level implants / 119 patients, 7.43 ± 2.73 yr; PMC full text) cross-classified trans-mucosal abutment height (TmAH; cut-off 2 mm) × restoration emergence angle (REA; cut-off 30°) into long/narrow (LN), long/wide (LW), short/narrow (SN) and short/wide (SW). Mean MBL was 0.26, 0.27, 0.46 and 0.79 mm respectively; SW exceeded LN by +0.48 mm (95% CI 0.25–0.71, p<0.001), LW by +0.43 (0.18–0.68, p=0.001) and SN by +0.25 (0.00–0.45, p=0.013), while **LW and LN did not differ** (+0.08, p=0.444). Peri-implantitis prevalence was 13.3% / 9.1% / 13.2% / 25.6%, with SW vs LW OR 4.19 (95% CI 1.35–13.0, p=0.013);all five failures were in SW sites.

The apparent contradiction inside this paper is the most useful part of the synthesis. In the logistic model for **any** MBL > 0 mm, TmAH was the only significant covariate (OR 0.63 per 1 mm, 95% CI 0.46–0.85, p=0.003) and REA was null (OR 0.99, p=0.991;interaction p=0.276). In the linear model for **amount** of MBL, the angle mattered — but only in the short-abutment stratum. So "angle is harmless" is a statement about incidence in a cohort where tall abutments dominate the protective variance; "angle is harmful" is a statement about severity when height is insufficient. The authors' own reading is that shallow placement forces both a short abutment and a wide crown angle, which makes the two prosthetic variables hard to separate observationally — the causal direction remains unproven.

### Axis 4 — Implant design and contour: who the 30° rule applies to

Katafuchi 2018 (cross-sectional radiographic analysis, 168 analysed implants, mean 10.9 yr; abstract-only) is the observational origin of the rule: in **bone-level** implants, peri-implantitis prevalence was 31.3% above 30° vs 15.1% at or below (p=.04), and convex contour plus >30° reached 37.8% with a significant angle × profile interaction (p=.003). In **tissue-level** implants there was no association. Misch 2025 independently notes that tissue-level implants, or bone-level implants with a long transmucosal abutment, may protect through the same mechanism — more supracrestal tissue-adhesion space and a larger distance from the inflammatory lesion to the bone crest.

The two SR+MAs split the risk the same way. Atieh 2023 (4 studies, 912 implants, 3.8–10.9 yr; abstract-only) found angle alone non-significant for MBL overall but significantly better MBL with ≤30° in **platform-matched** (non-platform-switched) implants, and convex profile carried peri-implantitis RR 2.32 (57.8% vs 21.3%, p=0.02). Liu 2025 (7 RCTs, 283 implants; abstract-only) found overall shape NS on early MBL (p=0.082), a significant EA >30° subgroup effect (MD +0.190 mm, p<0.001), and convex/wide abutments driving **crestal bone remodeling** above the platform (MD +0.560 mm, p=0.001). Platform design, contour shape and angle are therefore separate stratifiers, not interchangeable proxies.

The soft-tissue endpoint must be read in parallel rather than substituted: Wang 2022 (RCT, 21 mandibular molars with ≥2 mm supracrestal mucosa; abstract-only) cut 12-month recession to 0.13 vs 0.63 mm (p=.006) with a W/H-ratio-derived profile whose initial angle was 31.4° vs 40.0° (p=.025), and Siegenthaler 2022 (3-arm RCT, anterior) found mid-facial recession in 64.3% convex vs 14.3% concave (OR 12.69, 95% CI 1.89–88.48, p=.01) with no MBL difference (p=.599). A contour can protect the margin and leave bone unchanged, or raise peri-implantitis risk without changing mean MBL.

### Axis 5 — Prosthesis type and evidence range explain the null studies

Wach 2026 (5-year retrospective, 155 patients / MIS implants; full text) measured EA on 2D periapical films at mesial/distal sites only, in patients without diabetes, osteoporosis, radiotherapy, active bisphosphonates or diagnosed bruxism. Mean EA was 31.8° ± 10.4°; EA–MBL was null for single (p=0.369) and splinted (p=0.176) crowns, with a weak bridge-only association (p=0.042, R²=7.9%). It was exploratory, used simple rather than multivariable regression, and did not assess buccal/lingual aspects — so it speaks to the narrow clinical angle range it actually contained, not to 60–80° angles.

The preclinical evidence occupies that extreme range: Strauss 2024 (dog RCT, 48 sites, 20°/40°/60°/80°, 24 wk; full text) showed monotonic MBL 0.07 / 0.11 / 0.23 / 0.38 mm (p<0.001), plaque reaching 100% and disrupted junctional-epithelium continuity at ≥60°, with baseline soft-tissue thickness non-significant (p=0.915). Lee 2025 (dog split-mouth, 30° vs 60° in three-implant splints; abstract-only) localized the failed soft-tissue seal to splinted/middle implants. Prpic 2026 (PRISMA-ScR, 17 studies; full text, no RoB assessment or meta-analysis) reaches the same cautious formulation: EA <30° is plausible but inconclusive for MBL/peri-implantitis, while a concave profile is consistently associated with margin stability. Null and dose-response results are thus **range- and endpoint-specific**, not a single disagreement to be averaged.

### Decision ladder

1. **Before measuring an angle — fix the protocol.** State implant depth (equicrestal vs subcrestal), reference line (platform vs adjacent bone crest), distance band from the junction, and mesial/distal side. A number without these four fields is not comparable to the 30° literature (Han 2025;Nam 2025).
2. **Choose the abutment height first.** In bone-level implants, target trans-mucosal height >2 mm: it is the only significant predictor of any MBL in Misch 2025 (OR 0.63/mm), and a wide angle in a tall-abutment group was indistinguishable from narrow (+0.08 mm, p=0.444).
3. **Then choose the contour.** Prefer concave/flat over convex, especially when the angle cannot be kept low; convexity independently raises peri-implantitis risk (Atieh RR 2.32) and crestal remodeling (Liu +0.560 mm) and drives recession (Siegenthaler OR 12.69).
4. **Only then tune the angle.** Treat ≤30° as a conservative design target within the 0–2 mm zone, not a pass/fail threshold — the measured cut-offs near the junction range from 20° to 34° by side and depth (Han 2025), and small RCTs do not detect an isolated angle effect at ordinary clinical angles (Wach 2026).
5. **Apply the design boundary.** The >30°/convex rule is supported for bone-level implants (Katafuchi 2018) and is not transferable to tissue-level implants; platform-matched/non-switched designs showed the clearest angle benefit in Atieh 2023.
6. **Name the endpoint.** If the concern is recession, use the soft-tissue RCTs; if it is MBL amount, use the height × angle strata; if it is peri-implantitis, use the contour and design studies; if it is crests above the platform, use Liu 2025's CBR. Do not let a null in one endpoint erase a signal in another.
7. **Escalate on stacked risk, not on one number.** Short abutment + wide angle + convex contour + shallow bone-level placement is the configuration carrying the highest observed burden (Misch SW;Katafuchi convex × >30°;Prpic 2026 consolidation). If all four are present, change the prosthetic geometry rather than debating the degree.

### Evidence gaps

- **Three of the four threshold-defining clinical studies are abstract-only** (Han 2025, Nam 2025, Katafuchi 2018). Depth-specific cut-offs, adjustment models and effect sizes for the depth/reference axis cannot be re-checked from the held artifacts; Nam's actual angles are not reported at all.
- **No study crosses all modifiers in one design.** Misch 2025 tests height × angle but not measurement depth or reference point; Han/Nam test depth and reference but not height; Katafuchi tests angle × contour but reports no height. The stacked-risk ladder in step 7 is a synthesis across studies, not a fitted interaction.
- **Misch 2025's REA × TmAH interaction term was not significant (p=0.276)**, and its initial crown emergence level and transmucosal contour were not documented. The stratification is clinically coherent but the statistical evidence for the interaction itself is weak; the retrospective design cannot separate shallow placement from short abutment.
- **Outcome definitions differ**: MBL amount vs any MBL >0 mm vs peri-implantitis vs recession vs crestal bone remodeling. Null and positive results often refer to different endpoints, so a pooled "angle effect" would be misleading.
- **Wach 2026's angle range clusters near 32°** and lacks buccal/lingual assessment and multivariable adjustment — it cannot adjudicate the 60–80° range where the preclinical seal failure occurs.
- **No prospective clinical trial randomizes angle with abutment height held constant.** Prpic 2026 calls the <30° rule inconclusive pending RCTs; that remains the limiting gap for turning conditional thresholds into a single design number.
