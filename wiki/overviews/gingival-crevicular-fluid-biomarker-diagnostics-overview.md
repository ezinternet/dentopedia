> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 논지**: 치은열구액(Gingival Crevicular Fluid, GCF)은 치은열구에서 수동적으로 스며 나오는 **부위특이·비침습 삼출액**으로, 그 안의 분자를 읽으면 국소 숙주반응을 실시간으로 감시할 수 있다. 이 종합은 5편을 묶어 GCF 바이오마커를 **① 기능 계층(무엇을 재나)** 과 **② 임상 목적(왜 재나)** 의 두 축으로 지도화한다.
> - **축 A — 기능 계층 4층**: (1) **염증 사이토카인**(IL-1β, TNF-α, IL-6, IL-10, PGE2), (2) **효소·기질금속단백분해효소**(Matrix Metalloproteinase, MMP-1/2/3/**8**/**9**/13, ALP), (3) **골대사 조절축**(RANKL/OPG 비, 스클레로스틴(Sclerostin), 오스테오칼신(Osteocalcin, OC), N-telopeptide(NTx)), (4) **후성유전 층**(miRNA: miR-146a·200b·223·23a).
> - **축 B — 임상 목적 3종**: (1) **치주 진단·중증도·치료반응**(donertas, fadli), (2) **교정 치아이동(Orthodontic Tooth Movement, OTM) 모니터링**(bud), (3) **골대사·전신질환 read-out**(fadli, foroughi).
> - **가장 견고한 단일 마커 = IL-1β**: 5편 전부에 등장 — fadli에서 GCF 최다연구 마커, bud에서 초기 치아이동 구동, donertas RCT에서 가이드 바이오필름 치료(Guided Biofilm Therapy, GBT)로 유의 감소(1·3개월, p<0.05), foroughi에서 활성염증 고민감 마커.
> - **골재형성의 마스터 스위치 = RANKL/OPG 비**: 압박측 → RANKL↑·OPG↓(파골세포 형성, 흡수), 긴장측 → OPG↑(형성). foroughi는 RANKL:OPG 비를 **골흡수에 특이적**인 마커로 규정.
> - **치주 진단 수치**: cosin-villanueva 체계적리뷰(16편)는 **AUC > 0.8**을 진단 타당성 기준으로 miR-146a·miR-200b(-3p)·miR-223·miR-23a는 합격, **miR-203은 불합격**(오히려 VEGFA 표적 보호인자). fadli(37편)의 GCF 유의 패널 = IL-1β·스클레로스틴·OPG·IL-34.
> - **치료반응 감시 실증**: donertas RCT(n=80, Stage 1–2)에서 GBT가 기존 큐렛+초음파 대비 GI·PI·**GCF IL-1β**를 더 감소 — 단, TNF-α·IL-10·MMP-8은 군간 차이 없음(마커 선택이 결과를 좌우함을 보여주는 사례).
> - **교정 모니터링 신호**: bud(19편)에서 **MMP-9는 4시간 내 검출되는 활성 치아이동 초기마커**, ALP는 형성(긴장측·14일), NTx/OC는 후기 골대사. 채취는 흡수지 스트립 30–60초로 무손상.
> - **GCF vs 타액 vs 혈액**: GCF = 부위특이(단일 치아·부위), 타액 = 전악 평균(편리하나 희석·비특이), 혈액 = 전신부하(CRP·IL-6). fadli는 "GCF는 타액의 일부라 마커가 대거 중첩"이라 지적. 자매 종합 [[saliva-diagnostics-and-salivary-gland-dysfunction-overview]]와 대비축을 이룸.
> - **공통 한계**: (1) **구강강 특이 마커가 없음** — 모두 전신 유래라 단일마커 질병특이 진단 불가, (2) 표준 컷오프·수집 표준화 부재, (3) 이질성으로 메타분석 불가(cosin), (4) **MRONJ의 인체 GCF 연구 전무**(fadli), (5) 효소면역측정법(ELISA) 비용·시간이 임상화 병목.
> - **전진 방향**: 단일 파라미터 labside(ELISA·PCR) → **멀티플렉스 chairside**(측방유동검사(Lateral Flow Assay, LFA)·바이오센서·미세유체 lab-on-a-chip) + AI 통합. 사이토카인+미생물 DNA+골흡수 마커를 한 휴대 플랫폼에서 동시 검출하는 것이 foroughi의 제안.
> - **임상 takeaway**: 오늘의 GCF는 **연구·모니터링 도구**(치료 전후 IL-1β/MMP 추세, OTM 진행)이지 아직 독립 진단검사가 아니다. 부위특이가 필요하면 GCF, 전악 스크리닝이면 타액을 택하고, 표준화·chairside 검사·규제승인이 임상 진입의 관문이다.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-paper synthesis of 5 papers (1 RCT, 3 narrative/scoping/SR reviews, 1 SR) mapping gingival crevicular fluid (GCF) as a passive, site-specific diagnostic biomarker medium across two axes: functional layers (inflammatory cytokines → enzymes/MMPs → bone-turnover regulators RANKL/OPG/sclerostin → epigenetic miRNAs; diagnostic miRNAs include miR-146a/200b/223/23a with AUC > 0.8) and clinical purposes (periodontal diagnosis and treatment response, orthodontic tooth-movement monitoring, bone-turnover/systemic read-out).

IL-1β is the single most robust marker across all five papers; the RANKL:OPG ratio is the master bone-resorption switch; MMP-9 is detectable within 4 hours of orthodontic force; GBT reduced GCF IL-1β significantly while TNF-α/MMP-8 showed no group difference (Donertas 2026 RCT, n=80).

The shared barriers are the absence of any oral-cavity-exclusive marker, missing standardized cut-offs, and ELISA cost/complexity; the forward path is multiplex AI-integrated chairside platforms (lateral flow/microfluidic/biosensor), with today's GCF serving as a research and monitoring tool rather than a stand-alone diagnostic test.

## 세줄요약

치은열구액(GCF)을 부위특이·비침습 진단 매체로 보고 5편을 두 축 — **기능 계층**(염증 사이토카인 → 효소/MMP → 골대사 조절축 RANKL/OPG·스클레로스틴 → 후성유전 miRNA; 진단 miRNA miR-146a/200b/223/23a AUC > 0.8) 과 **임상 목적**(치주 진단·치료반응, 교정 치아이동 모니터링, 골대사·전신 read-out) — 으로 종합.

IL-1β가 5편 전부를 관통하는 최견고 마커이고 RANKL:OPG 비가 골흡수 마스터 스위치이며, MMP-9는 교정력 4시간 내 검출되는 초기 치아이동 마커이고, GBT는 GCF IL-1β를 유의하게 줄이나 TNF-α/MMP-8은 군간 차이 없었다(Donertas 2026 RCT, n=80).

구강강 특이 마커 부재·표준 컷오프 부재·ELISA 비용이 공통 장벽이라 멀티플렉스 AI chairside 플랫폼(측방유동검사·미세유체·바이오센서)이 전진 방향으로 제시되며, 현재 GCF는 독립 진단검사가 아닌 연구·모니터링 도구다.

## Summary

Gingival crevicular fluid (GCF) is the fluid that seeps passively from the gingival blood-vessel plexus into the gingival sulcus, changing in volume and composition with local inflammatory status. That makes it a **minimally invasive, repeatable, site-specific** sample — the biofluid most directly relevant to the periodontium, distinct from whole saliva (a diluted, whole-mouth average) and blood (systemic load). Five papers in this wiki converge on GCF as a biomarker medium: a periodontal-diagnostic miRNA systematic review (Cosín-Villanueva 2024), a jawbone-turnover scoping review across GCF and saliva (Fadli 2024), an orthodontic tooth-movement narrative review (Bud 2024), an oral–systemic biomarker/diagnostic-technology narrative review (Foroughi 2025), and a clinical RCT reading GCF cytokines as a treatment-response outcome (Donertas 2026).

Read together, they show that GCF biomarkers organize into **functional layers** and serve **distinct clinical purposes**, that a small set of markers (led by IL-1β and the RANKL:OPG ratio) recurs across every application, and that the same limitations — no oral-exclusive marker, no standardized cut-offs, assay cost — block routine diagnostic use everywhere. The forward path all five imply is a move from single-parameter labside assays (ELISA, PCR) to multiplex, AI-integrated chairside platforms.

## Axis A — Functional biomarker layers (what is measured)

GCF markers stratify by biological function; each layer reports on a different part of the host response.

1. **Inflammatory cytokines** — IL-1β, TNF-α, IL-6, IL-10, PGE2, IL-34, IL-37. These report *active inflammation*. IL-1β is the workhorse: the most-studied GCF marker (Fadli), the driver of the initial inflammatory phase of tooth movement (Bud), and the cytokine that fell most under guided biofilm therapy (Donertas). Salivary/GCF IL-1β and TNF-α are high-sensitivity but lose specificity under systemic inflammation (Foroughi).
2. **Enzymes / matrix metalloproteinases (MMPs)** — MMP-1/2/3/8/9/13, alkaline phosphatase (ALP). These report *tissue remodeling and turnover*. MMP-8 and MMP-9 recur as periodontal-inflammation markers (Foroughi, Donertas); MMP-9 is detectable within 4 h of orthodontic force and is proposed as an early marker of active tooth movement (Bud). ALP is a bone-*formation* enzyme, rising on the tension side by day 14 (Bud).
3. **Bone-turnover regulators** — RANKL, OPG (and the **RANKL:OPG ratio**), sclerostin, osteocalcin (OC), N-telopeptide (NTx), TRAcP-5b, DPD. The RANKL:OPG ratio is the master switch for resorption vs formation (Bud, Foroughi) and is presented as reliably *specific* for bone resorption (Foroughi). Sclerostin, OPG, and IL-34 are the significant GCF panel for jawbone-turnover disease (Fadli).
4. **Epigenetic layer (miRNAs)** — miR-146a, miR-200b (miR-200b-3p), miR-223, miR-23a (diagnostic candidates), and miR-203 (a protective factor, *not* a diagnostic marker). This is the newest layer, adding post-transcriptional regulation to the protein/cytokine picture (Cosín-Villanueva).

## Axis B — Clinical purposes (why it is measured)

- **Periodontal diagnosis, severity, and treatment response.** GCF cytokines/MMPs scale with disease stage (healthy < gingivitis < periodontitis) and normalize after therapy. Donertas 2026 (RCT, n=80) used GCF IL-1β as an outcome and found guided biofilm therapy (GBT) beat conventional curette+ultrasonic debridement on GI, PI, and IL-1β — but *not* on TNF-α, IL-10, or MMP-8, showing that which marker you pick decides what you can conclude. Cosín-Villanueva ranks miRNA candidates by AUC > 0.8 for distinguishing disease from health.
- **Orthodontic tooth-movement (OTM) monitoring.** Bud 2024 maps biomarkers by pressure vs tension side and by timing: early inflammatory phase (IL-1β, TNF-α, PGE2, MMP-9) → later bone-turnover phase (ALP, OC, NTx). The vision is chairside GCF sampling to confirm active movement, shorten treatment, and flag adverse effects (root resorption, bone thinning).
- **Bone-turnover and systemic read-out.** Fadli 2024 positions GCF (with saliva) as a non-invasive window on jawbone turnover across periodontitis, osteoporosis, and MRONJ; Foroughi 2025 extends the biomarker logic to systemic-disease risk stratification (CVD, diabetes, RA, etc.), where GCF RANKL/OPG and MMP-8/-9 complement salivary and blood markers.

## GCF vs saliva vs blood — choosing the compartment

| Compartment | Scope | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GCF** | Single site / tooth | Site-specific, reflects local periodontal/bone response directly | Tiny volume, laborious per-site sampling, contamination (blood/saliva) |
| **Saliva** | Whole mouth | Convenient, larger volume, good for screening | Diluted whole-mouth average, non-specific; GCF markers overlap into it (Fadli) |
| **Blood/serum** | Systemic | Standard bone-turnover & inflammatory panels (CRP, IL-6) | Invasive; reflects systemic, not site, status |

No biomarker is oral-cavity-exclusive — all are systemic (Fadli), so oral fluids mirror overall status but cannot yet make a precise single-marker, disease-specific diagnosis. This overview is the site-specific counterpart to the whole-mouth [[saliva-diagnostics-and-salivary-gland-dysfunction-overview]].

## Diagnostic technology — labside → chairside

- **Labside (accuracy gold standard, but slow/centralized/single-parameter):** ELISA (antibody–antigen, high specificity/reproducibility) and PCR (pathogen-DNA: Pg, Td, Fn). ELISA cost/complexity is the explicit barrier to routine GCF use (Bud, Foroughi).
- **Chairside (speed/portability/multiplex, some specificity traded):** lateral flow assays (minutes, inexpensive), microfluidics / lab-on-a-chip (multiplex, microliter volumes), and biosensors (real-time, miniaturizable/wireless).
- **The proposed endpoint (Foroughi):** an AI-integrated, smartphone-linked multiplex device detecting cytokines + microbial DNA + bone-resorption markers simultaneously, for early detection and personalized risk stratification.
- **Sampling note (Bud):** absorbent paper strips (30–60 s, no tissue damage) are the practical GCF harvesting method; volume read by electronic transducer, markers assayed by ELISA in current studies.

## Cross-cutting limitations & evidence gaps

- **No oral-cavity-exclusive marker** — every marker is systemic; single-marker disease-specific diagnosis is not yet possible (Fadli).
- **No standardized cut-offs; heterogeneous sampling** — blocks clinical translation and, in Cosín-Villanueva, blocks meta-analysis (only qualitative synthesis possible).
- **MRONJ GCF gap** — no human GCF bone-turnover study exists for MRONJ; only an animal model, with saliva (MMP-9/TNF-α/IL-6) the current oral-fluid signal (Fadli).
- **Assay cost/complexity** — ELISA is laborious and expensive, the main obstacle to chairside adoption (Bud, Foroughi).
- **Confounders** — gingival inflammation, occlusal load, and poor hygiene must be controlled before a GCF signal can be attributed to the intended process (e.g. OTM) (Bud).
- **Study quality** — reviews report high risk of bias for confounders and assessor blinding (Fadli); associations without established causation (Foroughi).

## Clinical bottom line

Today, GCF is a **research and monitoring** medium, not a stand-alone diagnostic test. It excels where a *site-specific* signal is needed — tracking IL-1β/MMP trends before and after periodontal therapy, or confirming active orthodontic tooth movement — while whole saliva suits whole-mouth screening and blood suits systemic load. The single most reliable GCF signals are **IL-1β** (active inflammation) and the **RANKL:OPG ratio** (bone resorption). Routine chairside use awaits standardized cut-offs, validated multiplex point-of-care platforms, and regulatory clearance.
