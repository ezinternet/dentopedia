> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 코르티코스테로이드 (Corticosteroid, CS)의 치과 주위수술기 투여 효과는 **결과 지표별로 비대칭**이다: 부종(Edema)·개구제한(Trismus)은 강력한 근거로 감소, 통증(Pain)은 근거 일관성 낮음
> - 사랑니(Third Molar, M3) SR+MA 3편(Markiewicz 2008, Almeida 2019, Dan 2010) 모두 부종·개구제한 감소 유의(P<.001), 통증은 2편에서 비유의
> - **약물 간 위계**: 개구제한 조절에 덱사메타손 (Dexamethasone) > 메틸프레드니솔론 (Methylprednisolone) (Singh 2023 SR+MA, SMD −0.69mm, p<0.0001; Libório 2025 SR+MA 확인)
> - **투여 타이밍**: 개구제한 조절에는 술전(Preoperative) > 술후(Postoperative) 투여 (Almeida 2019)
> - **투여 경로**: 경로별 차이 없음 — 단, 점막하(Submucosal) 경로만 예외적으로 삶의 질 (OHRQoL) 우월 (Almeida 2019; Ibikunle 2016 확인)
> - **용량·스케줄**: 3일 체감용량 > 단회 고용량(부종·개구제한 기준, Bakri 2024); 용량-반응 기울기 없음 (Larsen 2021, 20/30/40 mg)
> - **메틸프레드니솔론 단독 근주 RCT 2편** (Larsen 2021, Gholami 2021): 통증·부종·개구제한 전부 비유의 → SR+MA 긍정결과와 대비, 경로·용량·이질성 문제
> - **코르티코스테로이드+NSAID 병용**: 코르티코스테로이드 단독 대비 통증·개구제한·부종 모두 추가 이점 (Buyukkurt 2006)
> - **안전성**: 단회 주위수술기 투여 → 감염 위험 증가 없음 (RR 1.004, Dan 2010); 악교정수술(Orthognathic Surgery) 고용량 반복은 무혈성 골괴사·부신억제 위험 다름
> - **부신위기 프로토콜**: 장기 스테로이드 복용환자(≥5 mg/일 >2주) 대수술 시 하이드로코르티손 (Hydrocortisone) 25–50 mg IV/IM 보충 (Satpathi 2024)

## Three-line Summary

Perioperative corticosteroids in dentistry reduce edema and trismus after third-molar and oral surgery with strong, consistent meta-analytic evidence (SMD 1.1–1.4 for edema; WMD 2.7–4.1 mm mouth-opening improvement; Markiewicz 2008, Almeida 2019, Dan 2010), but their effect on pain is inconsistent across the evidence base — two SR+MAs report null pooled pain effects while one reports significant reduction.

Within corticosteroids, dexamethasone outperforms methylprednisolone specifically for trismus reduction (Singh 2023 SR+MA SMD −0.69 mm, p<0.0001; confirmed Libório 2025), whereas methylprednisolone RCTs (Larsen 2021, Gholami 2021) produce null results for all three outcomes — suggesting that the positive SR+MA signal may be driven by dexamethasone trials.

Clinical prescribing refinements with evidence support: preoperative timing over postoperative (trismus), 3-day taper over single-dose (swelling/trismus; Bakri 2024), submucosal route for QoL (Ibikunle 2016), and corticosteroid+NSAID combination for additive benefit on pain and trismus (Buyukkurt 2006); infection risk from single perioperative doses is pooled-null (Dan 2010 RR 1.004, p=.89).

## 세줄요약

주위수술기 코르티코스테로이드 (Corticosteroid, CS)는 사랑니·구강외과 수술 후 부종(Edema)·개구제한(Trismus)을 강력한 메타분석 근거로 감소시키나(부종 표준화평균차 SMD 1.1–1.4, 개구량 가중평균차 WMD 2.7–4.1 mm; Markiewicz 2008·Almeida 2019·Dan 2010), 통증(Pain)에 대한 효과는 SR+MA 간 불일치.

약물 간 위계에서 덱사메타손 (Dexamethasone)은 개구제한 조절에서 메틸프레드니솔론 (Methylprednisolone)에 유의하게 우월(Singh 2023 SR+MA SMD −0.69 mm, p<0.0001; Libório 2025 확인)하며, 메틸프레드니솔론 단독 RCT 2편(Larsen 2021·Gholami 2021)은 전 결과 지표에서 비유의 — SR+MA 긍정결과가 덱사메타손 임상시험 주도 가능성.

임상 처방 최적화 근거: 술전 투여(개구제한)·3일 체감용량(부종·개구제한)·점막하 경로(삶의질)·CS+NSAID 병용(통증·개구제한 추가 이점); 단회 주위수술기 투여의 감염 위험 증가는 pooled RR 1.004로 비유의.

## 2. Outcome-by-Outcome Summary

### 2.1 Edema / Swelling

The most robust and consistent benefit domain:

- **Markiewicz 2008**: edema SMD 1.4 (95% CI 0.6–2.2) at early (1–3d), SMD 1.1 (0.1–2.0) at late (>3d); both p<.05
- **Dan 2010**: CS significantly reduced oral-surgery edema (P<.0001); local methylprednisolone (Methylprednisolone) ≥25 mg sufficient threshold
- **Bakri 2024**: 3-day oral prednisolone taper produced significantly less facial swelling at days 1, 3, and 7 vs single 25 mg dose (p<0.05 all timepoints)
- **Buyukkurt 2006**: prednisolone alone reduced swelling at day 2 vs control; prednisolone + diclofenac (Diclofenac) reduced swelling at day 7 additionally

**Direction**: uniformly positive across all studies. Effect magnitude: mild to moderate (SMD ~1.1–1.4).

### 2.2 Trismus / Mouth Opening

Second most consistent benefit:

- **Markiewicz 2008**: WMD 4.1 mm at early, 2.7 mm at late; both p<.005
- **Almeida 2019**: pooled MD 6.10 mm improvement (95% CI 3.42–8.77, p<.00001); preoperative timing specifically superior to postoperative
- **Libório 2025**: methylprednisolone improved interincisal distance at 48h (I²=0%, p<0.01) and 7d (I²=0%, p<0.05) — zero heterogeneity
- **Singh 2023**: dexamethasone > methylprednisolone at day 2 (SMD −0.69 mm, 95% CI −1.01 to −0.38, p<0.0001)
- **Ibikunle 2016**: submucosal prednisolone route superior to oral for QoL (which correlates with trismus recovery)
- **Bakri 2024**: tapered prednisolone produced significantly greater mouth opening days 1/3/7 vs single dose
- **Buyukkurt 2006**: prednisolone + diclofenac > prednisolone alone and control at days 2 and 7
- **Larsen 2021 / Gholami 2021**: both null for trismus — outliers at individual trial level

**Drug hierarchy for trismus**: Dexamethasone > Methylprednisolone (head-to-head SR+MA evidence). Preoperative > postoperative timing. Taper > single dose.

### 2.3 Pain

The most contested outcome:

| Source | Pain result |
|---|---|
| Markiewicz 2008 SR+MA | NS (early WMD 0.4 VAS, p=.07; late p=.4) |
| Almeida 2019 SR+MA | **Significant** (MD −17.38, p=0.002 vs placebo) |
| Dan 2010 SR+MA | **Significant** (P<.0001 in oral surgery arm) |
| Libório 2025 SR+MA (methylprednisolone-only) | NS at 24h (I²=47%, p=0.85) and 7d |
| Singh 2023 SR+MA (dex vs MP) | NS — dexamethasone = methylprednisolone |
| Larsen 2021 RCT | NS (all MP doses) |
| Gholami 2021 RCT | NS (MP masseter or gluteal) |
| Buyukkurt 2006 RCT | Prednisolone + diclofenac > control at 6h; prednisolone alone NS at 6h |

**Interpretation**: Pooled positive pain signals (Almeida 2019, Dan 2010) are likely driven by corticosteroid trials that include dexamethasone or higher-dose agents. Methylprednisolone-specific evidence (Libório 2025) and RCTs of methylprednisolone alone (Larsen, Gholami) consistently null for pain. Combination with NSAID recovers some pain benefit even when steroid-alone fails.

### 2.4 Quality of Life (OHRQoL / OHIP-14)

- **Ibikunle 2016** (n=186): prednisolone (oral or submucosal) significantly better QoL vs no prednisolone (p<0.05); submucosal > oral (p=0.001)
- **Larsen 2021**: methylprednisolone null for OHIP-14 vs placebo
- Drug difference (prednisolone vs methylprednisolone) or route difference (submucosal) may explain the divergence

## 3. Drug-Level Decision Map

```
Corticosteroid selection for perioperative M3 surgery:

Primary concern: Trismus + Edema
  → Dexamethasone 4–8 mg PO/IM preoperatively  [Singh 2023; Almeida 2019]
  → If using methylprednisolone: expect trismus benefit, not pain benefit [Libório 2025]

Primary concern: Pain
  → Corticosteroid alone insufficient [Markiewicz 2008; Libório 2025]
  → Add NSAID (diclofenac or ibuprofen) for additive pain reduction [Buyukkurt 2006]

Oral route preferred (no injection):
  → Prednisolone 5 mg TID→BID→QD over 3 days (tapered) > single 25 mg dose [Bakri 2024]

QoL / patient-reported recovery:
  → Prednisolone submucosal route > oral [Ibikunle 2016]

Orthognathic surgery (extended/high-dose):
  → IV methylprednisolone ≥85 mg for edema; risk profile different — avascular
     osteonecrosis and adrenal suppression risk must be weighed [Dan 2010]
```

## 4. Safety

- **Infection risk** (single perioperative dose): pooled RR 1.0041, P=.89 — not significant across oral surgery trials (Dan 2010). Do not withhold perioperative CS for fear of infection.
- **Adverse effects in RCTs**: None reported across all included RCTs in Singh 2023, Libório 2025, Larsen 2021, Gholami 2021, Ibikunle 2016, Buyukkurt 2006.
- **Wound healing**: No reports of decreased healing in either oral or orthognathic settings (Dan 2010).
- **Adrenal crisis risk**: Patients on chronic systemic corticosteroids (≥5 mg prednisolone/day >2 weeks) — supplement with hydrocortisone (Hydrocortisone) 25–50 mg IV/IM before major dental surgery (Satpathi 2024).
- **Orthognathic surgery high-dose**: Distinct risk profile — avascular osteonecrosis, steroid-induced psychosis, adrenal suppression (Dan 2010); not applicable to single-dose perioperative M3 context.
- **Long-term topical CS**: Oral candidiasis risk — concurrent antifungal recommended (Satpathi 2024).

## 6. Evidence Gaps

1. **Methylprednisolone pain discrepancy**: SR+MA signal vs individual RCT null. Need head-to-head RCTs with dexamethasone comparator and pain as primary endpoint.
2. **Dose-response for trismus**: Larsen 2021 (null, 20/30/40 mg IM methylprednisolone) argues no dose-response, but optimal dexamethasone dose not well-defined above 8 mg.
3. **Oral prednisolone taper evidence**: Bakri 2024 (n=15) is severely underpowered; finding directionally plausible but needs replication.
4. **Orthognathic surgery**: Dan 2010 covers this but with limited full-text access; dedicated evidence synthesis needed for larger jaw surgeries.
