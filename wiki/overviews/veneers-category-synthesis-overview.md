## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **범주 구조**: 비니어(Veneer) 28편은 3개 주제군으로 정리된다 — ① 재료 선택·생존율·프로토콜, ② 삭제 디자인·최소침습, ③ 교합면 비니어(마모·침식 수복). 각 주제군에 별도 overview가 있으며, 본 페이지는 그 overview들의 상위 지도이자 3개 축을 관통하는 비협상 원칙을 정리한다.
> - **핵심 반직관**: 모든 세라믹 비니어 재료의 장기 생존율은 통계적으로 유사(93–97%)하나 합병증 부담이 재료를 가른다 — 10년 기술적 합병증 장석(Feldspathic) 41.48% vs 리튬디실리케이트(Lithium Disilicate, LDS) 6.1%(약 7배; Klein 2025 SR+MA, 29편·7,753 비니어). "어떤 재료가 오래 가냐"가 아니라 "어떤 재료가 덜 망가지냐"가 임상 선택의 실질 기준.
> - **비협상 원칙 1 — 법랑질 한정 변연**: 상아질 변연 노출 시 실패율 ~10배(El-Mowafy 2018). 모든 최소침습·무삭제 전략의 목적이 이것 — 삭제량 자체가 아니라 법랑질 보존.
> - **비협상 원칙 2 — 삭제량은 형태가 결정**: "얼마나 깎을까"는 치아 상태가 아니라 최종 보철 형태·색 목표로 결정(P = LT − EV, Gürel/Coachman). 추가부피가 충분하면 P = 0(진정한 무삭제 가능).
> - **생존율 vs 합병증 분리**: 생존(비니어가 치아에 붙어 있음)과 합병증(파절·탈락·변색 등 이차 사건)은 별개 지표다. Klein 2025 기준 생존은 모두 93–97%지만 10년 합병증 누적이 장석 41%인 반면 LDS는 6% — 장기 환자에게 LDS를 선택하는 이유.
> - **실패 위계**: 파절(Fracture) > 탈락(Debonding) > 색변화(Color Change) (Alghazzawi 2024 narrative). 대부분 실패는 수리 가능한 chipping — additive 접착 철학의 실질 강점.
> - **절단연 피개 자유**: 절단연 피개(Incisal Coverage) 여부는 생존율에 무의미(OR 1.25 NS; Albanesi 2016 SR+MA) → 심미·교합 기준으로 자유롭게 선택.
> - **교합면 비니어 특이점**(→ `occlusal-veneer-tooth-wear-erosion-overview`): 구치부 교합면에서는 세라믹과 CAD/CAM 복합레진 임상 생존율 차이 없음(p=.124~.107 NS; Schlichting 2022 RCT · Ferrando-Cascales 2023). 후방 위치가 유일 유의 실패 예측인자(HR 0.143, p=0.008).
> - **접착 강도 위계**: LDS 25.4 MPa > 투명 지르코니아(Translucent Zirconia, TZ) 12.1 MPa; 법랑질 기질 21.1 MPa > 복합레진 코어 11.3 MPa(Hajeer 2026 SR+MA, in-vitro, I²=98%). LDS는 HF 에칭+실란화, 지르코니아는 실리카 네트워크 없어 MDP 프라이머 의존.
> - **파노라마 설계 방법론 진화**: 아날로그 APT(Gürel 2007) → 색 수식(Coachman 2014) → 디지털 3D-DSP·CAD-CAM PMMA 목업(Cattoni 2016) → 모두 동일한 "설계 후 삭제" 원칙.
> - **근거 한계**: 최장 추적 SR+MA도 비교 대상이 heterogeneous(기기·술자·프로토콜), 지르코니아 장기(≥5년) 임상 데이터 없음, 교합면 비니어 RCT는 소표본(n=11).
> - **근관치료 치아(Endodontically Treated Tooth, ETT) 라미네이트**: 현대 접착 프로토콜(IDS 등)에서는 ETT와 생활치 간 생존율 유의차 없음 — Gresnigt 2019 전향적 11년(88.1% vs 95.6%, NS) · Etienne 2025 후향적 15년(95% vs 96%, p=0.289, OR=1.68). 단, 구세대 장석계(1987–2009)로 진행한 Beier 2012(20년)에서는 ETT 유의한 실패 위험(p=0.0012) — 프로토콜 진보 전·후의 해석 분기점. **가장 그럴듯한 해석은 "ETT 효과"의 실체가 기질(substrate) 효과라는 것** — 같은 Etienne 데이터에서 상아질 노출 >30%의 OR=4.67로 ETT의 OR=1.68보다 약 3배 크며, pre-IDS 시대엔 그 기질을 구제할 수단이 없었다. 또한 **유의하지 않음 ≠ 위험 없음**: 두 현대 연구가 같은 방향(생존율 수치적 열세·OR>1)을 가리키되 ETT 하위군 표본이 작아(n=43·38) 검정력이 부족 — 잔여 위험 신호는 남아 있다. 임상 핵심: ETT 금기가 아니나 ① 내부 표백으로 색조 선제 관리(Gresnigt에서 ETT 색조 불일치는 유의 — 접착 실패가 아닌 차폐 문제) ② 치수 상태가 아니라 **에나멜 잔존량**으로 판단 ③ OR=1.68 감안해 신중한 증례 선택 필수. 상세 종합은 본문 "ETT — An Era-Dependent, Not Absolute, Risk" 절.

## Three-line Summary

The 28-paper veneers category clusters into three thematic groups — material selection/survival/protocol, preparation design/minimal-invasion, and posterior occlusal veneers for tooth wear — each with its own topic overview; the unifying finding across all three is the **enamel-confinement principle**: dentin margin exposure multiplies failure risk ~10× regardless of material, preparation depth, or design, making enamel preservation the purpose of minimal preparation, not reduced thickness per se (El-Mowafy 2018; confirmed across Klein 2025, Reis 2025, Ali 2023, Chandode 2026).

Material survival is counterintuitively similar across ceramic types (feldspathic 96.13%, LDS 96.81%, LRGC 93.70%; Klein 2025 SR+MA, 29 studies, 7,753 veneers, up to 20.7 yr), but complication burden differentiates them sharply — 10-year technical complications for LDS are 6.1% vs feldspathic 41.48% — making LDS the preferred choice for long-term planning; LDS bond strength exceeds translucent zirconia (25.4 vs 12.1 MPa; Hajeer 2026 SR+MA) and LDS's silica network enables HF etching + silanization, while zirconia relies on MDP primer alone.

For tooth-wear rehabilitation, ultrathin occlusal veneers (table-top) add the posterior dimension: ceramic and CAD/CAM composite resin are clinically equivalent at 3–5 years (NS across two independent studies), failures are predominantly repairable minor chipping, and posterior location is the only significant failure predictor (HR 0.143, p=0.008; Ferrando-Cascales 2023 retrospective, 5-yr), with minimum safe thickness dependent on substrate, material, and parafunction (0.5–1.0 mm range, unresolved).

## The Three Non-Negotiable Principles

These emerge consistently across all 28 papers regardless of material, technique, or indication.

### Principle 1: Enamel-Confined Margins

The single strongest predictor of failure across all veneer types is **dentin margin exposure**.

- El-Mowafy 2018 (narrative): dentin-exposed margins fail at ~10× the rate of enamel-confined margins — supported by Gürel's 580-veneer 12-year series
- All survival statistics (Klein 2025 SR+MA) implicitly assume predominantly enamel bonding
- Immediate dentin sealing (IDS with OptiBond FL) is the standard protocol when dentin is inevitably exposed (Schlichting 2022)
- No-prep veneers achieve 97.4% survival precisely because they maximize enamel bonding (Chandode 2026)

**Clinical rule**: If a preparation will expose substantial dentin, reassess indication. Conventional crown may be more appropriate than a veneer with poor bond substrate.

### Principle 2: Survival and Complications Are Separate Metrics

Klein 2025 (SR+MA, 29 studies, 7,753 veneers, up to 20.7 yr):

| Material | 10-yr Survival | 10-yr Technical Complications | 10-yr Biological Complications |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feldspathic | 96.13% | 41.48% | 6.51% |
| LRGC | 93.70% | 29.87% | 4.40% |
| LDS | 96.81% | 6.10% | 0.45% |
| Zirconia | 100% (at 2.6 yr) | — | — |

A veneer "surviving" (still attached) at 10 years is not the same as a veneer without complication events. The compelling reason for LDS over feldspathic is the ~7× reduction in technical complication burden — not the tiny (non-significant) survival difference. Zirconia's 100% survival is at 2.6 years — long-term data absent.

Komine 2024 (55-study narrative review) adds a time axis: 10-yr survival drops to 72.6–95.0% from ≤5 yr levels of 95.1–100%, and annual intervention rate rises from 0.6 at 3 yr to 1.78 at 10 yr — IDS and night-guard use strongly modulate this trajectory (IDS: 11-yr survival 96.4% vs 81.8% without; night guard: 8-yr survival 89.1% vs 63.9%).

### Principle 3: Failure Mode Is Predominantly Repairable

Alghazzawi 2024 (narrative review) confirms the failure hierarchy: **fracture > debonding > color change**. Crucially, most fractures are partial chipping:
- Schlichting 2022 (RCT): zero complete failures; all 5 failures were restorable chipping in the composite group
- Ferrando-Cascales 2023 (retrospective): 18 of 21 complications repaired directly with composite
- This reparability is the clinical argument for the additive-adhesive philosophy — treatments are not "all or nothing"

## ETT (Endodontically Treated Teeth) — An Era-Dependent, Not Absolute, Risk

Three papers in this category answer the same question — *does prior endodontic treatment of the abutment compromise laminate veneer survival?* — and appear to disagree. The disagreement is real in the data, but it is best read as an **era/protocol effect**, not a live clinical controversy.

### The apparent contradiction

- **Beier 2012** (Innsbruck, 1987–2009, 318 silicate glass-ceramic PLVs): nonvital teeth carried a **significantly higher** failure risk, p=0.0012. [확인]
- **Gresnigt 2019** (Groningen, prospective, 384 feldspathic LVs with IDS): ETT 88.1% vs vital 95.6% — **not significant**. [확인]
- **Etienne 2025** (Strasbourg, retrospective, 672 LDS LVs): ETT 95.0% vs vital 96.0%, p=0.289 — **not significant**, OR=1.68. [확인]

### Study comparison

| Study | Design / follow-up | Cohort | Material and era | ETT n | ETT vs vital survival | Statistical result | Adhesive protocol |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[veneers/beier-2012-porcelain-veneers-nonvital-bruxism-20year]] | Retrospective, up to 20 yr | 84 pts / 318 PLVs, Innsbruck 1987–2009 | Silicate glass-ceramic (Empress era) | not reported separately | ETT significantly worse | **p=0.0012 (significant)** | Pre-IDS; 1987–2009 dentin management |
| [[veneers/gresnigt-2019-ceramic-laminate-veneers-ids-11year]] | Prospective, 11 yr | 104 pts / 384 LVs, Groningen | Feldspathic, modern era | 43 | 88.1% vs 95.6% | Not significant | IDS applied whenever >50% dentin exposed (that subgroup: 96.4% vs 81.8%, significant) |
| [[veneers/etienne-2025-ceramic-veneers-dentin-vitality-15year]] | Retrospective, 1–15 yr, single operator + single ceramist | 189 pts / 672 LVs, Strasbourg | Lithium disilicate (IPS e.max Press) | 38 | 95.0% vs 96.0% | p=0.289 (NS); OR=1.68 | Variolink II / ExciTE F DSC (2001–2015) → G-CEM LinkForce + G-Premio BOND (2015–2023) |

### Resolution: the divide is chronological, not clinical

Beier's veneers were placed between 1987 and 2009 on silicate glass-ceramic, using adhesive protocols that predate immediate dentin sealing (IDS) and modern dentin management. Both dissenting studies are modern-era and, crucially, **agree with each other across independent axes** — different centres, different materials (feldspathic vs LDS), different designs (prospective vs retrospective) — yet land on the same non-significant result. That is why the typed relation between Gresnigt and Etienne is `reinforces`, while both carry `contradicts` toward Beier.

The most parsimonious reading is that what Beier detected as an "ETT effect" was substantially a **substrate effect**. ETT abutments are, on average, the more heavily restored and more dentin-exposed teeth in any cohort — and dentin exposure is precisely the variable Etienne isolated, at OR=4.67 for >30% exposure (p=0.033), roughly three times the magnitude of the ETT odds ratio in the same dataset. Pre-IDS protocols had no way to rescue a dentin-dominated substrate; modern ones do. Read this way the ETT finding does not compete with Principle 1 — it is a consequence of it.

### "Not significant" is not "no risk"

Both modern papers point the same direction even where they fail to reach significance:

- Gresnigt: ETT survival is **numerically lower** — 88.1% vs 95.6%, a 7.5-point gap that does not reach significance at n=43.
- Etienne: **OR=1.68** for failure in ETT, despite p=0.289 at n=38.

Two independent modern cohorts producing a consistent, same-direction, non-significant elevation is a weak signal, not a demonstrated null — both are underpowered for the ETT subgroup specifically. The honest synthesis: **ETT is not a strong independent risk factor for laminate veneer survival in the modern adhesive era, but a residual, unquantified elevated-risk signal persists across both modern studies.** Neither "ETT is contraindicated" nor "ETT is equivalent to vital" is supported by this evidence set.

### What is not in dispute: color, not bonding

Survival equivalence must not be allowed to erase a separate, well-documented problem. All three papers treat esthetics as its own axis:

- Gresnigt 2019 found **significantly greater color mismatch** in ETT versus adjacent vital teeth — with survival non-significant, this was the dominant clinical concern in that subgroup. [확인]
- Etienne 2025 attributed ETT esthetic complications to **underlying discoloration (a masking challenge), not adhesive failure**; 3 of 11 total failures were classified as esthetic. [확인]
- Beier 2012's leading failure mode was ceramic fracture (44.83%) — a mechanical axis independent of shade. [확인]

ETT therefore shifts the question from *"will it stay on?"* to *"will it match?"* — a planning and opacity problem (internal bleaching, higher-opacity cement, ceramic thickness for the required shade shift → Axis 1, P = LT − EV), not a bonding problem.

### Clinical takeaway [미검증]

1. **Prior endodontic treatment is not a contraindication** to a ceramic laminate veneer. The prohibition traces to pre-IDS-era data and does not survive replication in the modern adhesive era.
2. **Judge the tooth by its substrate, not its pulp status.** Quantify remaining enamel and dentin exposure first. A vital tooth with >30% dentin exposure (OR=4.67) is the higher-risk restoration than an enamel-confined ETT (OR=1.68).
3. **Apply IDS wherever dentin is exposed.** The measured benefit is largest exactly where ETT cases tend to land (>50% dentin: 96.4% vs 81.8%, significant).
4. **Settle shade before preparation.** Manage ETT discoloration up front — internal bleaching, cement opacity, thickness allowance — rather than meeting mismatch as a post-cementation surprise.
5. **Combined severe discoloration plus dentin-dominated substrate** is where the crown threshold below applies; residual OR=1.68 stacked on a ≥3-tone shade shift is a different risk profile from an isolated, enamel-confined ETT.
6. **Beier's other finding crosses eras untouched**: bruxism carried 7.7× failure risk (p=0.0004) and is not corrected by adhesive advances. Parafunction screening stays mandatory regardless of vitality status.

## Material Selection Guide

### Anterior Veneers (Esthetic Indication)

| Clinical need | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Best long-term complication profile | LDS (e.max Press/CAD) | 10-yr complication 6.1% vs feldspathic 41.5% |
| Budget / traditionalist | Feldspathic | Comparable survival; higher secondary event burden |
| Posterior/high-force zone | LDS or leucite-reinforced ceramic | Higher fracture resistance vs feldspathic |
| Young patient, reversibility priority | Composite resin veneer | 88% 5-yr survival (Lim 2023 SR); direct repairability |
| Zirconia | Do not use until ≥5-yr clinical data available | Bond strength 12.1 MPa vs LDS 25.4 MPa (Hajeer 2026); no long-term data |

### Posterior Occlusal Veneers (Tooth-Wear Indication)

| Factor | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Material choice | Ceramic (LDS) or CAD/CAM composite — clinically equivalent at 3–5 yr |
| Minimum thickness | 0.5–1.0 mm; 0.5 mm viable with adequate conditioning, not recommended in parafunction |
| Surface conditioning | Monobond Etch & Prime or HF; APF contraindicated at ≤0.5 mm + parafunction |
| Substrate management | Dentin → IDS; composite → airborne particle abrasion + silane |
| Parafunction | Night guard; 8-yr survival 89.1% vs 63.9% without (Komine 2024) |

## Bonding Protocol Summary

Bond strength data from Hajeer 2026 (SR+MA, in-vitro, I²=98.2% — exploratory):

| Combination | Pooled bond strength |
|---|---|
| LDS + enamel substrate | ~25.4 MPa (LDS) × 21.1 MPa (enamel) — highest combination |
| LDS + composite core | ~25.4 MPa (LDS) × 11.3 MPa (comp. core) |
| Translucent zirconia + enamel | ~12.1 MPa (TZ) × 21.1 MPa (enamel) |

**Protocol by material:**
- **LDS / feldspathic / LRGC**: HF etching (9.6% HF) → silane coupling agent → adhesive resin cement. HF etching time is fabrication-technique-dependent: platinum foil 120 s, refractory die 90 s (Gonzalez-Martin 2024 SEM); 150 s adds no benefit.
- **Zirconia**: No silica network → HF etching ineffective. Use MDP-containing primer (e.g., Z-Prime Plus) + sandblasting. Bond strength significantly lower; clinical justification remains unclear.
- **Substrate**: Enamel is the ideal substrate; when dentin exposed, IDS immediately after preparation (before impressioning) preserves the bonding layer.

## Preparation and Planning Axes

### Axis 1: How much reduction?

**P = LT − EV** (Coachman "Do the Math")
- LT = final ceramic thickness required for the target color shift
- EV = added volume from additive wax-up
- If additive volume equals ceramic thickness → P = 0 (true no-prep)

Color shift rules: 1–2 shade tones → ~0.3 mm thin laminate; ≥3 tones or intrinsic stain → more invasive preparation + intrasulcular margin extension.

### Axis 2: No-prep vs conventional

Chandode 2026 — both yield 91–100% survival (no-prep ~97% vs conventional ~92%); enamel bonding is the key, not the amount removed.

**No-prep contraindications**: intrinsic discoloration (>3 tone shift needs opacity; no-prep veneers are too translucent), severe misalignment, large form modification (overcontour risk).

### Axis 3: Design and planning tool

| Generation | Tool | Clinical role |
|---|---|---|
| Analog (Gürel 2007) | APT/mock-up with lab-fabricated provisional | Patient approval before preparation |
| Semi-digital (Coachman 2014) | 2D photo-based smile design + color math | Quantified reduction depth per zone |
| Full digital (Cattoni 2016) | 3D-DSP: 2D photo + intraoral STL → CAD/CAM PMMA | Same-day mock-up as prep guide; 108 PLVs, 2-yr follow-up |

## Veneers vs Full Crowns — Indication Threshold

The evidence is consistent: enamel-bonded veneers outperform dentin-bonded veneers. The threshold for switching from veneer to crown:
- Preparation will require ≥50% dentin exposure at margins
- Severe structural compromise requiring axial wall coverage
- Posterior molar (especially ≥2nd molar) in heavy parafunction
- ETT with severe discoloration that cannot be managed with internal bleaching + high-opacity cement

When in doubt: Beuer 2025 consensus (SSRD/SEPES/PROSEC) recommends a "minimality hierarchy" — no prep > butt-joint incisal > palatal chamfer > conventional veneer prep > partial crown > full crown.
