## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 위험도 *평가·예측*에 초점을 둔 5편 종합 — 기존 [[overviews/peri-implantitis-management-overview]]가 "발생 후 치료"라면 이 페이지는 "발생 전 누가 고위험인가"를 다루는 짝 페이지.
> - **가장 견고한 두 위험인자 (엄브렐라 수렴)**: 41개 메타분석 엄브렐라 (Giok 2024, SR+MA) — 치주염 과거력 (오즈비 OR 3.84, 95% CI 2.58–5.72) 과 흡연 (상대위험 RR 2.07, 95% CI 1.41–3.04) 만 "highly suggestive" 등급. 24개 유의 연관 중 어느 것도 최고 등급 "convincing"엔 도달 못 함. [확인]
> - **"suggestive" 2차 위험인자 8종** (Giok 2024): 당뇨 (DM)·고혈당·구강위생/정기관리 부재·만성 치주질환력·전치부 임플란트 위치·골보호단백 (OPG) 유전자 다형성·각화점막 (Keratinized Mucosa) 부족. → 절대적이지 않으나 상담·모니터링에 반영.
> - **PiRA Part 1 (Tarce 2025, 엄브렐라 49 SR)**: 위험도 평가도구의 근거 기반. 10개 환자관련 인자가 ≥1개 리뷰에서 메타분석 수준 유의 — 치주염력·임플란트 위치·흡연·상실치 수·정기내원 순응도·전신질환(특히 당뇨). 치료관련 인자로는 임플란트 표면거칠기·보철 상부구조 특성(치관-임플란트비·수복물 종류). **핵심 한계: 거의 모든 SR이 인자를 다변량 보정 없이 단독 분석 → 인자별 상대 가중치는 이 리뷰만으론 산출 불가.**
> - **PiRA Part 2 (Quirynen 2025, 후향 460명/1,432 임플란트)**: Part 1 인자를 예측모델로 조작화. 주위염 유병률 17% (부분무치악 13.1%, 완전무치악 20%). 부분무치악에서 유의 인자 — 치주염 감수성·PPD ≥5mm 부위 ≥3개·흡연. 8인자 모델 민감도 90.2%/특이도 55.0% (양성예측도 22.7%, **음성예측도 97.5%**).
> - **PiRA의 실제 임상 값어치 = 배제 (rule-out)**: 높은 음성예측도(97.5%)·낮은 양성예측도(22.7%) → "저위험 확정"엔 강하나 "고위험 확진"엔 약함. 무료 온라인 도구 (pira.gbiomed.kuleuven.be) 부분무치악용 배포. 완전무치악 모델(n=50)은 검정력 부족으로 임상 사용 부적합, 전향 다기관 검증 예정. [후향·모델개발 단계]
> - **전자담배는 "안전한 대안"이 아니다 (Fathi 2024, 엄브렐라 5편)**: 전통담배 대비 임상 염증징후(탐침시출혈·탐침깊이·주위 골소실)를 줄이거나 변화 없을 수 있으나, 여전히 니코틴 함유 + 비흡연자 대비 광범위 구강 유해; 장기 만성 주위염 위험은 정량화 부족. 근거는 질적/서술적. [근거제한]
> - **수정 가능한 예방 창(窓) = 점막염 (Peri-implant Mucositis) 관리, 그러나 마법 보조제는 없음 (Chuachamsai 2022, 엄브렐라 12 SR)**: 비외과 기계적 청결술 (NSMD) 에 항생제·소독제·프로바이오틱스·에어폴리싱·레이저/광역학치료를 더해도 탐침깊이·출혈·부착수준·치면세균막지수에 유의 추가 이득 없음. 포함 SR의 AMSTAR-2 신뢰도는 critically low~low. → **위험 관리의 핵심은 보조 약제가 아니라 NSMD + 정기 지지치료 순응도.**
> - **결정 framework (술전)**: ① 치주염력·흡연 = 1순위 평가 (가장 견고), ② PiRA 도구로 저위험 배제 활용(음성예측도 강점), ③ 당뇨·각화점막 부족·전치부 위치·정기관리 부재는 조정 가능 인자로 상담/모니터링 강화, ④ 전자담배 흡연자를 "안전군"으로 분류 금지, ⑤ 식립 후 점막염 조기 발견·NSMD + SPT 순응도가 진행 억제의 실질 레버.
> - **한계 요약**: 근거의 대부분이 다변량 보정 부재 관찰연구 엄브렐라 — 인자의 존재는 견고하나 *상대 가중치·상호작용*은 미확립; PiRA는 유망하나 아직 단일기관 후향 개발 단계.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 5 papers on peri-implantitis risk *assessment and prediction* (as opposed to management), spanning two umbrella reviews of risk factors (Giok 2024, Tarce 2025 = PiRA Part 1), a retrospective predictive-model build (Quirynen 2025 = PiRA Part 2), an e-cigarette umbrella review (Fathi 2024), and a mucositis-adjunct umbrella review (Chuachamsai 2022).

Across umbrella reviews, only history of periodontitis (OR 3.84) and smoking (RR 2.07) reach "highly suggestive" evidence (Giok 2024, 41 meta-analyses), with diabetes/hyperglycaemia, lack of prophylaxis, anterior-jaw location, and lack of keratinized mucosa "suggestive"; PiRA operationalizes these into an 8-factor model (Quirynen 2025) whose clinical value is rule-out (sensitivity 90.2%, NPV 97.5%) rather than rule-in (specificity 55%, PPV 22.7%) — while e-cigarettes are not a "safe" alternative (Fathi 2024) and no adjunct beats non-surgical mechanical debridement for the modifiable precursor lesion, mucositis (Chuachamsai 2022).

Clinically: assess periodontitis history and smoking first, use PiRA to confirm low risk, intensify monitoring for suggestive factors (diabetes, missing keratinized mucosa, anterior location, poor recall compliance), never classify vapers as safe, and rely on debridement + supportive therapy compliance — not adjunctive agents — to keep mucositis from progressing.

## 세줄요약

임플란트주위염 위험도 *평가·예측*에 초점을 둔 5편 종합(치료가 아닌 예측) — 위험인자 엄브렐라 2편(Giok 2024, Tarce 2025=PiRA Part 1), 후향 예측모델 구축(Quirynen 2025=PiRA Part 2), 전자담배 엄브렐라(Fathi 2024), 점막염 보조요법 엄브렐라(Chuachamsai 2022).

엄브렐라 전반에서 치주염 과거력(OR 3.84)과 흡연(RR 2.07)만 "highly suggestive"(Giok 2024, 41개 메타분석), 당뇨/고혈당·정기관리 부재·전치부 위치·각화점막 부족은 "suggestive"; PiRA는 이를 8인자 모델로 조작화(Quirynen 2025)하며 그 임상 값어치는 확진(특이도 55%, PPV 22.7%)보다 배제(민감도 90.2%, NPV 97.5%); 전자담배는 "안전한 대안"이 아니고(Fathi 2024), 수정 가능한 전구병소인 점막염에는 비외과 기계적 청결술을 능가하는 보조제가 없다(Chuachamsai 2022).

임상: 치주염력·흡연을 1순위로 평가, PiRA로 저위험을 확정, suggestive 인자(당뇨·각화점막 부족·전치부·정기내원 저순응)엔 모니터링 강화, 전자담배 흡연자를 안전군으로 분류 금지, 점막염 진행 억제는 보조 약제가 아니라 청결술+지지치료 순응도에 의존.

## Summary

Peri-implantitis (임플란트주위염) is the leading long-term threat to implant survival, and much of the wiki's coverage is *reactive* — how to treat an established lesion (see [[overviews/peri-implantitis-management-overview]]). This page is the companion *predictive* view: **before placement, who is at elevated risk, and how confidently can we say so?** The five papers here are dominated by umbrella reviews (evidence about the evidence) plus the two-part PiRA (Peri-implantitis Risk Assessment) program that turns that evidence into a chairside tool.

---

## What the umbrella reviews converge on (the risk-factor hierarchy)

### Giok 2024 — the cleanest hierarchy (umbrella of 41 meta-analyses)
Umbrella review of meta-analyses of observational studies (PROSPERO CRD42024512408; 12 articles, 41 unique meta-analyses). Of **41 associations, 24 significant**, graded by an evidence-strength ladder:
- **"Highly suggestive" (top tier reached):** history of **periodontitis (OR 3.84, 95% CI 2.58–5.72)** and **cigarette smoking (RR 2.07, 95% CI 1.41–3.04)**.
- **"Suggestive" (8 factors):** diabetes mellitus, hyperglycaemia, lack of prophylaxis/maintenance, chronic periodontal disease history, **anterior-jaw implant location**, OPG gene polymorphisms, and **lack of keratinized mucosa**.
- **None reached "convincing"** — the strongest empirical statement the field can currently make is "highly suggestive," not proven causation.

### Tarce 2025 (PiRA Part 1) — the evidence base for a tool (umbrella of 49 SRs)
Screened 4,724 records → 49 SRs (30 patient-related, 19 treatment-related). **Ten patient-related risk factors** reached meta-analysis-level significance in ≥1 review — history of periodontitis, implant location, smoking, number of teeth lost, recall compliance, and systemic disease (notably diabetes). **Treatment-related** factors reaching significance: implant surface roughness and prosthetic suprastructure characteristics (crown-to-implant ratio, restoration type).
- **The pivotal limitation:** nearly all included SRs analyzed factors **in isolation, without multifactorial adjustment** — so the *relative weight* of each factor is not derivable from the review. Tarce explicitly built Part 1 as groundwork for the multifactorial model in Part 2.

**Convergence:** across two independent umbrella reviews, **periodontitis history and smoking are the two most robustly evidenced factors** — they should anchor preoperative consent and monitoring.

---

## From evidence to a tool: PiRA Part 2 (Quirynen 2025)

Retrospective single-center cohort (Leuven; **460 patients, 1,432 implants**; 350 partially edentulous, 50 fully edentulous) building a preoperative prediction model from the Part 1 factors.
- **Baseline prevalence:** 17% overall (13.1% partially edentulous, 20% fully edentulous).
- **Significant factors (partially edentulous):** susceptibility to periodontitis, ≥3 sites with PPD ≥5 mm, and smoking.
- **8-factor model performance:** sensitivity **90.2%**, specificity **55.0%**, PPV **22.7%**, **NPV 97.5%**.

### How to read those numbers (the key clinical nuance)
The model is a **rule-out instrument, not a rule-in instrument**:
- **High sensitivity + very high NPV (97.5%)** → a "low-risk" result is trustworthy; it reliably identifies patients unlikely to develop peri-implantitis.
- **Low specificity + low PPV (22.7%)** → a "high-risk" flag is weak; most flagged patients will *not* develop disease. Do not treat a positive PiRA as a contraindication — treat it as a prompt for intensified maintenance and consent.
- A **free online tool (pira.gbiomed.kuleuven.be)** is deployed for partially edentulous candidates. The **fully-edentulous model (n=50, sensitivity 100%/specificity 51.3%) is underpowered** and not for clinical use; a prospective multicenter trial is planned.

## Two modifiable-behavior questions the risk picture raises

### E-cigarettes are not a "safe" swap (Fathi 2024, umbrella of 5 SRs)
Compared with traditional tobacco, e-cigarettes **may reduce or not change** clinical inflammatory signs (bleeding on probing, probing depth, peri-implant bone loss) — but they **still deliver nicotine** and are linked to broad adverse oral-health effects vs non-smokers, and **long-term chronic peri-implantitis risk from vaping is not quantified**. Evidence is qualitative/narrative (heterogeneous SRs of mostly cross-sectional studies). **Clinical message: do not reclassify a vaping patient into the low-risk (non-smoker) bucket.**

### The modifiable precursor window — mucositis, but no magic adjunct (Chuachamsai 2022, umbrella of 12 SRs)
Peri-implant mucositis is the reversible precursor to peri-implantitis, making it the main *modifiable* window. But adding antiseptics, probiotics, air-polishing, laser/photodynamic therapy, or antibiotics to **non-surgical mechanical debridement (NSMD)** produced **no significant difference** in probing depth, bleeding on probing, attachment level, or plaque index (6 meta-analyzing SRs; vote-counting confirmed no benefit for antiseptics/probiotics). Included-SR confidence was **critically low to low (AMSTAR 2)**, ~two-thirds of primary studies at high risk of bias. **Clinical message: the lever for arresting the precursor lesion is NSMD + supportive-therapy compliance, not adjunctive agents.**

## Clinical Bottom Line

| Question | Answer | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Which risk factors are most robust? | Periodontitis history (OR 3.84) + smoking (RR 2.07) | "highly suggestive" (umbrella) |
| Which are secondary but worth flagging? | Diabetes/hyperglycaemia, no prophylaxis, anterior location, no keratinized mucosa | "suggestive" (umbrella) |
| Can a tool predict who gets peri-implantitis? | PiRA rules *out* well (NPV 97.5%), rules *in* poorly (PPV 22.7%) | retrospective, single-center |
| Are e-cigarettes a safe alternative? | No — still nicotine; long-term risk unquantified | umbrella, qualitative |
| Does any adjunct beat debridement for mucositis? | No — NSMD + SPT compliance is the lever | umbrella, low-confidence SRs |

**Decision rule (pre-placement):** Score periodontitis history and smoking first (the anchors). Use PiRA primarily to *confirm low risk*; a high-risk flag intensifies maintenance and consent, not exclusion. Flag suggestive factors (diabetes, missing keratinized mucosa, anterior-jaw site, poor recall compliance) for closer monitoring. Never file a vaper as a non-smoker. After placement, the real protective lever is early mucositis detection with NSMD and supportive peri-implant therapy compliance.

**Key limitation across the cluster:** the evidence is overwhelmingly umbrella reviews of **unadjusted observational studies** — factor *existence* is robust, but factor *weighting and interaction* remain unestablished, and PiRA is still a single-center retrospective development awaiting prospective validation.
