## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 위험도 *평가·예측*에 초점을 둔 종합 — 기존 [[overviews/peri-implantitis-management-overview]]가 "발생 후 치료"라면 이 페이지는 "발생 전 누가 고위험인가"를 다루는 짝 페이지.
> - **가장 견고한 두 위험인자 (엄브렐라 수렴)**: 41개 메타분석 엄브렐라 (Giok 2024, SR+MA) — 치주염 과거력 (오즈비 OR 3.84, 95% CI 2.58–5.72) 과 흡연 (상대위험 RR 2.07, 95% CI 1.41–3.04) 만 "highly suggestive" 등급. 24개 유의 연관 중 어느 것도 최고 등급 "convincing"엔 도달 못 함. [확인]
> - **"suggestive" 2차 위험인자 8종** (Giok 2024): 당뇨 (DM)·고혈당·구강위생/정기관리 부재·만성 치주질환력·전치부 임플란트 위치·골보호단백 (OPG) 유전자 다형성·각화점막 (Keratinized Mucosa) 부족. → 절대적이지 않으나 상담·모니터링에 반영.
> - **PiRA Part 1 (Tarce 2025, 엄브렐라 49 SR)**: 위험도 평가도구의 근거 기반. 10개 환자관련 인자가 ≥1개 리뷰에서 메타분석 수준 유의 — 치주염력·임플란트 위치·흡연·상실치 수·정기내원 순응도·전신질환(특히 당뇨). 치료관련 인자로는 임플란트 표면거칠기·보철 상부구조 특성(치관-임플란트비·수복물 종류). **핵심 한계: 거의 모든 SR이 인자를 다변량 보정 없이 단독 분석 → 인자별 상대 가중치는 이 리뷰만으론 산출 불가.**
> - **PiRA Part 2 (Quirynen 2025, 후향 460명/1,432 임플란트)**: Part 1 인자를 예측모델로 조작화. 주위염 유병률 17% (부분무치악 13.1%, 완전무치악 20%). 부분무치악에서 유의 인자 — 치주염 감수성·PPD ≥5mm 부위 ≥3개·흡연. 8인자 모델 민감도 90.2%/특이도 55.0% (양성예측도 22.7%, **음성예측도 97.5%**).
> - **PiRA의 실제 임상 값어치 = 배제 (rule-out)**: 높은 음성예측도(97.5%)·낮은 양성예측도(22.7%) → "저위험 확정"엔 강하나 "고위험 확진"엔 약함. 무료 온라인 도구 (pira.gbiomed.kuleuven.be) 부분무치악용 배포. 완전무치악 모델(n=50)은 검정력 부족으로 임상 사용 부적합, 전향 다기관 검증 예정. [후향·모델개발 단계]
> - **전자담배는 "안전한 대안"이 아니다 (Fathi 2024, 엄브렐라 5편)**: 전통담배 대비 임상 염증징후(탐침시출혈·탐침깊이·주위 골소실)를 줄이거나 변화 없을 수 있으나, 여전히 니코틴 함유 + 비흡연자 대비 광범위 구강 유해; 장기 만성 주위염 위험은 정량화 부족. 근거는 질적/서술적. [근거제한]
> - **수정 가능한 예방 창(窓) = 점막염 (Peri-implant Mucositis) 관리, 그러나 마법 보조제는 없음 (Chuachamsai 2022, 엄브렐라 12 SR)**: 비외과 기계적 청결술 (NSMD) 에 항생제·소독제·프로바이오틱스·에어폴리싱·레이저/광역학치료를 더해도 탐침깊이·출혈·부착수준·치면세균막지수에 유의 추가 이득 없음. 포함 SR의 AMSTAR-2 신뢰도는 critically low~low. → **위험 관리의 핵심은 보조 약제가 아니라 NSMD + 정기 지지치료 순응도.**
> - **신규 (2026-10) — 치주염 과거력을 "식립 시점 잔존 치주낭"으로 구체화 (Ferrarotti 2026, 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR), 코호트 4편)**: 활성 치주치료 후 남은 잔존 치주낭 (Residual Periodontal Pocket) 이 많거나 깊을수록 이후 임플란트주위염이 많았다 — 4편 모두 같은 방향 (Kumar 2018 OR 2.73; Vagia 2021 PPD >3 mm 잔존 부위당 HR 1.06; Zhang 2018 PPD ≥6 mm 부위 >10% 시 HR 3.62 또는 6.71 — 원문 내 수치 불일치; Pjetursson 2012 잔존 치주낭 4.1 vs 1.9개). 인접 치아의 치은염·치주염도 1편에서 연관 (OR 8.0). 메타분석 불가, 분지부 병변 (Furcation Involvement) 평가 연구 0편, 치주낭 깊이 임계값은 도출 불가. → "치주염 과거력 있음"보다 **식립 전 치주 재평가로 잔존 치주낭을 줄이는 것**이 수정 가능한 레버. [확인]
> - **신규 (2026-10) — 재료: 지르코니아 임플란트 (Zirconia Implant, ZI) 유병률 (Moraschini 2026, 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 (SR+MA), 19편·ZI 891개)**: 임플란트 수준 임플란트주위염 유병률 ≤3년 2%, 3–5년 2%, >5년 8% (95% CI 0–23%, 예측구간 0–67%) — 시간이 갈수록 증가. RCT 5편의 ZI 대 티타늄 비교는 유의차 없음 (RR 1.66, 95% CI 0.48–5.71) 이나 GRADE 낮음~매우 낮음이라 **동등성 근거가 아니다**. 단기 저유병률은 짧은 추적·느슨한 진단 정의 탓일 수 있어 "지르코니아라서 저위험"으로 분류하면 안 된다. [확인]
> - **결정 framework (술전)**: ① 치주염력·흡연 = 1순위 평가 (가장 견고) — 치주염력은 식립 전 잔존 치주낭 재평가로 구체화, ② PiRA 도구로 저위험 배제 활용(음성예측도 강점), ③ 당뇨·각화점막 부족·전치부 위치·정기관리 부재는 조정 가능 인자로 상담/모니터링 강화, ④ 전자담배 흡연자를 "안전군"으로 분류 금지, ⑤ 식립 후 점막염 조기 발견·NSMD + SPT 순응도가 진행 억제의 실질 레버.
> - **한계 요약**: 근거의 대부분이 다변량 보정 부재 관찰연구 엄브렐라 — 인자의 존재는 견고하나 *상대 가중치·상호작용*은 미확립; PiRA는 유망하나 아직 단일기관 후향 개발 단계.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis on peri-implantitis risk *assessment and prediction* (as opposed to management), built on two umbrella reviews of risk factors (Giok 2024, Tarce 2025 = PiRA Part 1), a retrospective predictive-model build (Quirynen 2025 = PiRA Part 2), an e-cigarette umbrella review (Fathi 2024), a mucositis-adjunct umbrella review (Chuachamsai 2022), a residual-pocket systematic review (Ferrarotti 2026) and a zirconia-implant prevalence meta-analysis (Moraschini 2026).

Across umbrella reviews, only history of periodontitis (OR 3.84) and smoking (RR 2.07) reach "highly suggestive" evidence (Giok 2024, 41 meta-analyses), and residual periodontal pockets left at implant placement narrow the periodontitis anchor to a measurable state (4 cohorts, all in the same direction, no threshold derivable); PiRA operationalizes the factors into an 8-factor model (Quirynen 2025) whose clinical value is rule-out (sensitivity 90.2%, NPV 97.5%) rather than rule-in (PPV 22.7%), while e-cigarettes are not a "safe" alternative, zirconia implants show low short-term but rising >5-year prevalence (8%) with no proven difference from titanium, and no adjunct beats non-surgical mechanical debridement for mucositis.

Clinically: assess periodontitis history and smoking first and re-evaluate residual pockets before placement, use PiRA to confirm low risk, intensify monitoring for suggestive factors, never classify vapers or zirconia-implant patients as low-risk by default, and rely on debridement + supportive therapy compliance to keep mucositis from progressing.

## 세줄요약

임플란트주위염 위험도 *평가·예측*에 초점을 둔 종합(치료가 아닌 예측) — 위험인자 엄브렐라 2편(Giok 2024, Tarce 2025=PiRA Part 1), 후향 예측모델 구축(Quirynen 2025=PiRA Part 2), 전자담배 엄브렐라(Fathi 2024), 점막염 보조요법 엄브렐라(Chuachamsai 2022), 잔존 치주낭 체계적 문헌고찰(Ferrarotti 2026), 지르코니아 임플란트 유병률 메타분석(Moraschini 2026).

엄브렐라 전반에서 치주염 과거력(OR 3.84)과 흡연(RR 2.07)만 "highly suggestive"(Giok 2024, 41개 메타분석)이고, 식립 시점의 잔존 치주낭이 치주염 기준을 측정 가능한 상태로 좁힌다(코호트 4편 모두 같은 방향, 임계값 도출 불가); PiRA는 인자를 8인자 모델로 조작화(Quirynen 2025)하며 그 임상 값어치는 확진(PPV 22.7%)보다 배제(민감도 90.2%, NPV 97.5%); 전자담배는 "안전한 대안"이 아니고, 지르코니아 임플란트는 단기 유병률이 낮으나 5년 초과 시 8%로 오르며 티타늄과의 차이는 입증되지 않았고, 점막염에는 비외과 기계적 청결술을 능가하는 보조제가 없다.

임상: 치주염력·흡연을 1순위로 평가하고 식립 전 잔존 치주낭을 재평가, PiRA로 저위험을 확정, suggestive 인자엔 모니터링 강화, 전자담배 흡연자나 지르코니아 임플란트 환자를 기본값으로 저위험군에 넣지 않으며, 점막염 진행 억제는 청결술+지지치료 순응도에 의존.

## Summary

Peri-implantitis (임플란트주위염) is the leading long-term threat to implant survival, and much of the wiki's coverage is *reactive* — how to treat an established lesion (see [[overviews/peri-implantitis-management-overview]]). This page is the companion *predictive* view: **before placement, who is at elevated risk, and how confidently can we say so?** The core papers here are umbrella reviews (evidence about the evidence) plus the two-part PiRA (Peri-implantitis Risk Assessment) program that turns that evidence into a chairside tool.

## What the umbrella reviews converge on (the risk-factor hierarchy)

### Giok 2024 — the cleanest hierarchy (umbrella of 41 meta-analyses)
Umbrella review of meta-analyses of observational studies (PROSPERO CRD42024512408; 12 articles, 41 unique meta-analyses). Of **41 associations, 24 significant**, graded by an evidence-strength ladder:
- **"Highly suggestive" (top tier reached):** history of **periodontitis (OR 3.84, 95% CI 2.58–5.72)** and **cigarette smoking (RR 2.07, 95% CI 1.41–3.04)**.
- **"Suggestive" (8 factors):** diabetes mellitus, hyperglycaemia, lack of prophylaxis/maintenance, chronic periodontal disease history, **anterior-jaw implant location**, OPG gene polymorphisms, and **lack of keratinized mucosa**.
- **None reached "convincing"** — the strongest empirical statement the field can currently make is "highly suggestive," not proven causation.

### Tarce 2025 (PiRA Part 1) — the evidence base for a tool (umbrella of 49 SRs)
Screened 4,724 records → 49 SRs (30 patient-related, 19 treatment-related). **Ten patient-related risk factors** reached meta-analysis-level significance in ≥1 review — history of periodontitis, implant location, smoking, number of teeth lost, recall compliance, and systemic disease (notably diabetes). **Treatment-related** factors reaching significance: implant surface roughness and prosthetic suprastructure characteristics (crown-to-implant ratio, restoration type).
- **The pivotal limitation:** nearly all included SRs analyzed factors **in isolation, without multifactorial adjustment** — so the *relative weight* of each factor is not derivable from the review. Tarce explicitly built Part 1 as groundwork for the multifactorial model in Part 2.

**Convergence:** across two independent umbrella reviews, **periodontitis history and smoking are the two most robustly evidenced factors** — they should anchor preoperative consent and monitoring.

### Refining the first anchor — residual pockets at placement (Ferrarotti 2026)
[[implants/peri-implantitis/ferrarotti-2026-residual-periodontal-pockets-at-implant]] — systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42024567630; search to December 2025; 3870 records → 4 cohorts: 3 retrospective, 1 prospective; 70–100 patients and 165–260 implants each) asking whether residual pockets left after active periodontal therapy, at the time of implant placement, predict later peri-implantitis in treated periodontitis patients.
- **All four cohorts point the same way:** Kumar 2018 residual PPD at placement OR 2.73 (1.45–5.16); Vagia 2021 HR 1.06 (1.02–1.10) per residual site with PPD >3 mm; Zhang 2018 >10% of sites with PPD ≥6 mm significant (HR 3.62 in the table but 6.71 in the text — the authors do not reconcile them, so treat the size as uncertain and the direction as stable); Pjetursson 2012 4.1 vs 1.9 residual pockets (PPD ≥5 mm) in patients with vs without peri-implantitis.
- One cohort also linked gingivitis/periodontitis on adjacent teeth at implant restoration to peri-implantitis (OR 8.0, 95% CI 1.2–70.1) — a hypothesis-generating site-level signal.
- **Limits:** no meta-analysis (exposure and case definitions, follow-up and effect measures differ), moderate risk of bias in the three retrospective studies, no study of furcation involvement, maintenance quality not separable from the residual-pocket effect, and **no pocket-depth threshold can be set**.
- **What it changes here:** Giok's binary "history of periodontitis" (OR 3.84) becomes partly a modifiable pre-placement state — the residual pocket burden after therapy. It supports reaching periodontal stability before placement and tightening supportive care when pockets cannot be eliminated; this is compatible with [[implants/survival/lee-2026-supportive-periodontal-therapy-improves-implant]] (regular SPT offsets much of the survival penalty at periodontitis-related sites), though Lee measured implant removal rather than peri-implantitis.
- **Do not confuse with residual peri-implant pockets after treatment.** Monje 2025 ([[implants/peri-implantitis/monje-2025-long-term-surgical-treatment-outcomes]]) found that residual *peri-implant* pockets ≥6 mm after surgery carry about 8× higher recurrence odds — a post-treatment signal on the implant, whereas Ferrarotti's exposure is residual *periodontal* pockets on teeth before placement. Both point to residual pocket depth as the screenable variable at two different stages.

## From evidence to a tool: PiRA Part 2 (Quirynen 2025)

Retrospective single-center cohort (Leuven; **460 patients, 1,432 implants**; 350 partially edentulous, 50 fully edentulous) building a preoperative prediction model from the Part 1 factors.
- **Baseline prevalence:** 17% overall (13.1% partially edentulous, 20% fully edentulous).
- **Significant factors (partially edentulous):** susceptibility to periodontitis, ≥3 sites with PPD ≥5 mm, and smoking.
- **8-factor model performance:** sensitivity **90.2%**, specificity **55.0%**, PPV **22.7%**, **NPV 97.5%**.

### How to read those numbers (the key clinical nuance)
The model is a **rule-out instrument, not a rule-in instrument**:
- **High sensitivity + very high NPV (97.5%)** → a "low-risk" result is trustworthy; it reliably identifies patients unlikely to develop peri-implantitis.
- **Low specificity + low PPV (22.7%)** → a "high-risk" flag is weak; most flagged patients will *not* develop disease. Do not treat a positive PiRA as a contraindication — treat it as a prompt for intensified maintenance and consent.
- A **free online tool (pira.gbiomed.kuleuven.be)** is deployed for partially edentulous candidates. The **fully-edentulous model (n=50, sensitivity 100%/specificity 51.3%) is underpowered** and not for clinical use; a prospective multicenter trial is planned.

## Two modifiable-behavior questions the risk picture raises

### E-cigarettes are not a "safe" swap (Fathi 2024, umbrella of 5 SRs)
Compared with traditional tobacco, e-cigarettes **may reduce or not change** clinical inflammatory signs (bleeding on probing, probing depth, peri-implant bone loss) — but they **still deliver nicotine** and are linked to broad adverse oral-health effects vs non-smokers, and **long-term chronic peri-implantitis risk from vaping is not quantified**. Evidence is qualitative/narrative (heterogeneous SRs of mostly cross-sectional studies). **Clinical message: do not reclassify a vaping patient into the low-risk (non-smoker) bucket.**

### The modifiable precursor window — mucositis, but no magic adjunct (Chuachamsai 2022, umbrella of 12 SRs)
Peri-implant mucositis is the reversible precursor to peri-implantitis, making it the main *modifiable* window. But adding antiseptics, probiotics, air-polishing, laser/photodynamic therapy, or antibiotics to **non-surgical mechanical debridement (NSMD)** produced **no significant difference** in probing depth, bleeding on probing, attachment level, or plaque index (6 meta-analyzing SRs; vote-counting confirmed no benefit for antiseptics/probiotics). Included-SR confidence was **critically low to low (AMSTAR 2)**, ~two-thirds of primary studies at high risk of bias. **Clinical message: the lever for arresting the precursor lesion is NSMD + supportive-therapy compliance, not adjunctive agents.**

## Does implant material change the risk? Zirconia (Moraschini 2026)

[[implants/peri-implantitis/moraschini-2026-prevalence-of-peri-implantitis-around]] — SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD420251273821; search to February 2026) of 19 studies (687 patients, 995 implants: 891 zirconia, 104 titanium; mean follow-up about 49 months), the first to take peri-implantitis prevalence around zirconia implants as the primary outcome.
- **Implant-level prevalence rises with time:** 2% (95% CI 0–6%) at ≤3 years, 2% (0–8%) at >3–5 years, 8% (0–23%) beyond 5 years, with a study-level range of 0–33.3% and a >5-year prediction interval of 0–67%.
- **Zirconia vs titanium (5 RCTs, 109 vs 106 implants):** RR 1.66 (95% CI 0.48–5.71; p=0.42) — not significant, but with low to very low GRADE certainty and few events; the authors warn against reading it as equivalence.
- The low short-term figures come from short follow-up, mostly one-piece implants with single crowns, and heterogeneous case definitions; the >5-year estimate overlaps titanium-dominated implant-level figures (Diaz 2022 12.53%; Reis 2025 18.0%).
- **Risk-assessment message:** implant material does not appear decisive, consistent with Monje 2025's finding that implant design, surface and system show no consistent superiority ([[implants/peri-implantitis/monje-2025-surgical-implant-factors-peri-implant-diseases]]). Do not move a patient into a lower-risk bucket because a zirconia implant is planned; patient factors (periodontitis, smoking, residual pockets) still govern.

## Clinical Bottom Line

| Question | Answer | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Which risk factors are most robust? | Periodontitis history (OR 3.84) + smoking (RR 2.07) | "highly suggestive" (umbrella) |
| Which are secondary but worth flagging? | Diabetes/hyperglycaemia, no prophylaxis, anterior location, no keratinized mucosa | "suggestive" (umbrella) |
| Can a tool predict who gets peri-implantitis? | PiRA rules *out* well (NPV 97.5%), rules *in* poorly (PPV 22.7%) | retrospective, single-center |
| Are e-cigarettes a safe alternative? | No — still nicotine; long-term risk unquantified | umbrella, qualitative |
| Does any adjunct beat debridement for mucositis? | No — NSMD + SPT compliance is the lever | umbrella, low-confidence SRs |
| Do residual periodontal pockets at placement matter? | Yes, directionally in all 4 cohorts; no threshold derivable | SR, 4 observational cohorts, no meta-analysis |
| Is a zirconia implant lower-risk? | Not shown — >5-year prevalence 8%, RCT comparison vs titanium NS but not equivalence | SR+MA, GRADE low to very low |

**Decision rule (pre-placement):** Score periodontitis history and smoking first (the anchors), and for treated periodontitis patients re-evaluate residual pockets before placement (Ferrarotti 2026). Use PiRA primarily to *confirm low risk*; a high-risk flag intensifies maintenance and consent, not exclusion. Flag suggestive factors (diabetes, missing keratinized mucosa, anterior-jaw site, poor recall compliance) for closer monitoring. Never file a vaper as a non-smoker. After placement, the real protective lever is early mucositis detection with NSMD and supportive peri-implant therapy compliance.

**Key limitation across the cluster:** the evidence is overwhelmingly umbrella reviews of **unadjusted observational studies** — factor *existence* is robust, but factor *weighting and interaction* remain unestablished, and PiRA is still a single-center retrospective development awaiting prospective validation.
