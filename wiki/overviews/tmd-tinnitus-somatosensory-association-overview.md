## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 측두하악장애(Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)와 이명(Tinnitus)은 실재하는 연관성이 있으나, 그 크기는 근거 수준에 따라 크게 갈린다 — 서술적 리뷰는 대담한 수치(OR 7.3, 8배 유병률)를 제시하지만, 유일한 정량적 메타분석(Bousema 2018)은 전체 근거를 GRADE **낮음(low)**으로 판정한다. 6편(narrative-review 3·sr 1·sr+ma 2)을 기전·연관성·진단·치료 4축으로 통합.
> - **기전 (Ralli 2017, Saczuk 2024 6-이론 모델)**: 체성감각 이명 (Somatosensory Tinnitus, ST)은 삼차신경(V)·안면신경(VII)·경추(C2-C3) 등 체성감각 구심로가 배측 와우핵(Dorsal Cochlear Nucleus, DCN)에서 청각 구심로와 교차 수렴 — 탈구심 후 신경가소성 (Neuroplasticity)으로 DCN 자발발화가 증가해 이명을 유발/변조. TMJ 조작(악물기·최대개구)이 대규모 코호트(n=310)에서 이명 크기를 **94.3%**까지 변조시켜 TMJ가 가장 강력한 체성 변조 부위임을 확인.
> - **연관성 — 근거 등급에 따라 수치가 갈림**: Bousema 2018(SR+MA, 22편 독립연구)은 TMD-이명 양방향 OR **2.3–6.7**(subtype별)을 보고하되, **출판편향으로 미분류 TMD의 OR이 6.7→4.5로 하향**되고 전체 근거는 GRADE 낮음(눈가림 2/22, 무작위 3/22)이라 명시. 반면 Dipalma 2025(SR, 12편)는 통증성 TMD-이명 **OR 7.3**, Saczuk 2024(narrative, 77편)는 TMD 환자 이명 유병률 **2–59%**(일반인구 대비 ~8배), 이명 환자 중 TMJ장애 동반율 **19%**(중증 이명군 36%)를 보고 — **방향은 일치하나 크기는 근거 수준이 낮을수록 과장될 위험**.
> - **진단 — 수렴하는 스크리닝 로직**: Michiels 2023의 4기준 의사결정나무(정확도 82.2%·민감도 82.5%·특이도 79%), Saczuk의 다학제적 진단 알고리즘, Ralli의 19-조작 체성 검사 배터리가 공통적으로 "이비인후과·청력검사 정상 + 체성 조작 시 이명 변조 + 편측 일치"를 ST 후보 기준으로 삼는다.
> - **동측성(Laterality)이 핵심 임상 단서**: 편측 TMD와 편측 이명이 **같은 쪽(ipsilateral)**이면 연관 지지(Ralli 76.2% 일치, Saczuk ~54% 상관), **반대쪽(discordant)**이면 개별 환자에서 TMD-이명 연관을 배제하는 근거로 활용 — 침습적 치료 전 확인할 값싼 bedside 체크.
> - **치료 — 가장 견고한 근거는 다학제적 비침습 병용**: Dapuzzo 2024(SR+MA, RCT만 5편, n=329)가 유일하게 인과적 치료-효과를 정량화 — 도수치료+운동+환자교육 병용이 풀링 OR **0.26**[0.19–0.37]으로 이명 증상을 유의하게 개선. LLLT(레이저)는 소규모 1편(n=31)뿐으로 예비적.
> - **개선율은 크지만 완전 소실은 드묾**: TMD 치료 후 이명 개선/소실 43–86%(Saczuk) · 16편 역사적 종합 평균 69%(Ralli, 32%는 무변화) · Michiels: 완전 관해는 드묾 — "치료하면 대개 좋아지나 대부분 완전히 사라지진 않는다"가 환자 상담 문구.
> - **반응 예측인자**: 기저 이명 중증도·이명 관련 장애(handicap)가 **높을수록** 오히려 호전 폭이 큼(Dapuzzo, Dipalma-Plaza-Manzano — 천장효과 반영일 가능성); 기저 저작근(특히 측두근) 압통 역치가 낮을수록(더 민감할수록) 반응이 나쁨 — 상담 시 "중증 이명은 개선 여지가 크지만, 근육 과민성이 심하면 반응이 더딜 수 있다"로 설명 가능.
> - **인과관계는 아직 미확정**: Saczuk·Bousema 모두 TMD와 이명이 독립적으로 공존하는지, 인과적인지, 스트레스 같은 공통 상위 교란변수를 공유하는지 명시적으로 미해결 과제로 남김 — "치료하면 이명이 좋아진다"는 관찰적 근거이지 기전적 확증은 아님.
> - **관련 논문**: [[tmj/ma-2025-tmd-tinnitus-chewing-side-preference-correlation]] (편측저작-이명-TMD 동측성 상관, n=285)이 본 overview의 동측성 원칙을 구체적 임상 변수(저작측)로 확장.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 6 papers (3 narrative reviews, 1 systematic review, 2 systematic reviews with meta-analysis) on the association, mechanism, diagnosis, and treatment of TMD-associated (somatosensory) tinnitus — from the foundational mechanistic review (Ralli 2017) through quantitative association evidence (Bousema 2018 SR+MA, Dipalma 2025 SR, Saczuk 2024 narrative) to RCT-level treatment evidence (Dapuzzo 2024 SR+MA) and a validated diagnostic decision tree (Michiels 2023).

TMD and tinnitus are consistently, bidirectionally associated (odds ratios 2.3–7.3 depending on subtype and study design) via cross-modal auditory-somatosensory convergence at the dorsal cochlear nucleus, but the only rigorous quantitative meta-analysis (Bousema 2018) grades the overall evidence "low" and shows publication bias inflates several pooled estimates; the only RCT-restricted meta-analysis (Dapuzzo 2024, 5 RCTs, n=329) confirms that multidisciplinary non-invasive TMD therapy (manual therapy + exercise + education) significantly reduces tinnitus symptoms (OR 0.26), with 43–86% of patients improving but total remission remaining rare.

For practice: screen otologically-unremarkable tinnitus patients for TMD using a somatic maneuver history and check laterality concordance (ipsilateral TMD-tinnitus supports the link, discordant argues against it), then route confirmed cases to multidisciplinary TMD-directed therapy rather than otologic treatment alone — but counsel patients that improvement, not cure, is the realistic expectation and that causality remains scientifically unproven.

## 세줄요약

체성감각 이명 (Somatosensory Tinnitus, ST)/측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 연관성·기전·진단·치료를 다룬 논문 6편(서술적 리뷰 3·SR 1·SR+MA 2) 종합 — 기전 기초(Ralli 2017)부터 정량적 연관성 근거(Bousema 2018 SR+MA·Dipalma 2025 SR·Saczuk 2024 narrative), RCT 수준 치료근거(Dapuzzo 2024 SR+MA), 검증된 진단 의사결정나무(Michiels 2023)까지.

TMD와 이명은 배측 와우핵(Dorsal Cochlear Nucleus)의 청각-체성감각 교차수렴을 통해 일관되게 양방향 연관되나(subtype별 OR 2.3–7.3), 유일한 정량적 메타분석(Bousema 2018)은 전체 근거를 GRADE **낮음**으로 판정하고 출판편향으로 일부 통합추정치가 부풀려졌음을 명시; RCT만 포함한 유일한 메타분석(Dapuzzo 2024, RCT 5편·329명)은 다학제적 비침습 TMD 치료(도수치료+운동+교육)가 이명 증상을 유의하게 개선(OR 0.26)함을 확인, 43–86% 개선하나 완전 소실은 드묾.

임상 적용: 이비인후과적으로 원인불명인 이명 환자는 체성 조작 병력으로 TMD를 선별하고 동측성(편측 TMD=편측 이명이면 지지, 반대측이면 배제)을 확인한 뒤, 확진 시 이비인후과 단독치료 대신 다학제적 TMD 치료로 연결하되 — "치료" 아닌 "개선" 기대치를 환자에게 설명하고 인과관계는 아직 과학적으로 미확정임을 전제할 것.

## Summary

Tinnitus is traditionally an otolaryngology problem, but a recurring cross-specialty observation is that patients with temporomandibular disorders (TMD) frequently report tinnitus, and that TMD-directed treatment often improves it. This overview integrates six papers spanning that literature's full arc: **mechanism** (Ralli 2017's foundational somatosensory-tinnitus model, deepened by Saczuk 2024's six-theory anatomical/physiological framework), **association strength** (Bousema 2018's rigorous odds-ratio meta-analysis, contrasted against the larger headline numbers in Dipalma 2025's systematic review and Saczuk 2024's narrative prevalence figures), **diagnosis** (Michiels 2023's validated 4-criterion decision tree, Saczuk's multidisciplinary diagnostic algorithm, and Ralli's somatic-maneuver screening battery), and **treatment** (Dapuzzo 2024's RCT-restricted meta-analysis, the only paper in this set to quantify a causal treatment effect rather than a cross-sectional association).

The central tension this synthesis surfaces is an **evidence-quality gradient**: narrative reviews and even systematic reviews without meta-analytic pooling report striking headline associations (OR 7.3 for painful TMD-tinnitus, 8-fold prevalence multipliers), while the one paper that pooled odds ratios with formal quality appraisal and publication-bias correction (Bousema 2018) grades the entire literature "low" quality and shows trim-and-fill correction meaningfully shrinks several estimates. This does not mean the association is spurious — the direction is consistent across every study design — but it means clinicians should treat the specific magnitude claims (especially "8x" or "OR 7") as upper-bound, narrative-review-driven figures rather than settled quantitative fact.

The treatment picture is more reassuring: Dapuzzo 2024's pooled analysis of 5 RCTs (the only treatment-focused meta-analysis, restricted to RCT designs rather than observational cross-sectional data) confirms a statistically robust, consistent benefit of multidisciplinary non-invasive TMD therapy (manual therapy + exercise + patient education) on tinnitus symptoms across three independent effect measures (OR, RR, RD), converging with Ralli's historical pooled 69% improvement/resolution rate and Saczuk's 43–86% range. Total tinnitus remission remains uncommon; the realistic clinical promise is improvement, not cure.

## Results

### Axis 1 — Mechanism: Somatosensory Convergence at the Dorsal Cochlear Nucleus

**Ralli 2017 (narrative review)** establishes the foundational model: auditory and trigeminal/dorsal-column somatosensory afferents converge at the dorsal and ventral cochlear nucleus (DCN/VCN). After cochlear deafferentation, animal electrophysiology shows upregulated glutamatergic (VGLUT2+) somatosensory input and increased DCN/VCN spontaneous firing — a neuroplastic mechanism for somatically-modulated tinnitus. Clinically, TMJ maneuvers (clenching, maximal opening, protrusion) modulated tinnitus loudness in **94.31%** of a 310-patient cohort — the single most reliable somatic-modulation trigger, ahead of head/neck maneuvers (59.1–68%) and eye-gaze maneuvers (common mainly post-acoustic-neuroma-surgery).

**Saczuk 2024 (narrative review, 77 publications)** extends this into a **six-theory mechanistic framework**: (1) shared first-pharyngeal-arch embryology (medial pterygoid, tensor veli palatini, tensor tympani); (2) topographical proximity (discomalleolar ligament, condylar pressure on the auriculotemporal nerve, posterior condylar displacement injuring the chorda tympani/stapes); (3) shared sensory innervation (trigeminal V, facial VII, glossopharyngeal IX, vagus X, C2–C3, sympathetic carotid plexus); (4) shared motor innervation (trigeminal mandibular branch co-innervates masseter and tensor tympani); (5) DCN cross-modal neuroplasticity (same mechanism as Ralli); (6) stress as a shared predisposing/aggravating factor for both TMD and tinnitus via muscle hyperexcitability and neurotransmitter imbalance.

### Axis 2 — Association Strength: A Quality-Stratified Odds-Ratio Ladder

| Study | Design | n / scope | Key association metric | Evidence grading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bousema 2018 | sr+ma | 22 independent studies (2,139 screened) | Tinnitus-CSD OR 2.6 [1.1–6.4]; Tinnitus-TMDa OR 2.3 [1.5–3.6]; Tinnitus-TMDm OR 4.1 [2.1–8.1]; Tinnitus-TMD unspecified OR 6.7→**4.5 after trim-and-fill** [2.4–18.8 raw] | **GRADE low** — only 2/22 blinded, 3/22 randomized, publication bias confirmed |
| Dipalma 2025 | sr | 12 studies (2014–2024, 976 screened) | Painful TMD–tinnitus OR **7.3** [3.50–15.39] (Fernandes 2014); TMD in 97.8% of one ST cohort (Didier) | Methodologically limited — small samples, weak/absent controls, cross-sectional |
| Saczuk 2024 | narrative-review | 77 publications (2009–2024, 30,621 screened) | Tinnitus prevalence in TMD 2–59% (~8× general population); TMJ-disorder frequency in tinnitus patients 19% overall, 36% severe tinnitus | Narrative synthesis — causality explicitly unresolved |
| Ralli 2017 | narrative-review | ≥9 clinical cohorts + own 310-patient cohort | TMJ-disorder–tinnitus risk ratios 2.4–8× across epidemiological cohorts (Bernhardt RR 2.4, Buergers 8.3×, Lee 3.22×, Manfredini ~30% prevalence) | Narrative — no formal quality appraisal |

**Reading the ladder**: every design finds the same *direction* of association, but the *magnitude* shrinks as methodological rigor increases. Bousema's own trim-and-fill correction (unspecified-TMD OR 6.7→4.5, TMDm OR 4.1→3.3) is the clearest internal demonstration that publication bias inflates the headline numbers reported uncritically in the narrative-review literature. Clinicians citing "8× more likely" or "OR 7.3" to patients should understand these are narrative-review/single-study figures, not GRADE-appraised pooled estimates — the more conservative, quality-appraised range is closer to OR 2–4.5.

### Axis 3 — Diagnosis: Converging Screening Logic

Three independent diagnostic frameworks converge on the same clinical logic despite being developed separately:

- **Michiels 2023**: a validated 4-criterion clinical decision tree diagnoses somatosensory tinnitus with **82.2% accuracy** (82.5% sensitivity, 79% specificity) — the only quantified diagnostic-accuracy figure in this literature (abstract-only; underlying criteria not detailed in the retrievable text).
- **Saczuk 2024**: a multidisciplinary diagnostic algorithm — when standard otolaryngological workup for tinnitus (audiometry, imaging) is unremarkable, extend evaluation to the stomatognathic system/TMJ (dentist), cervical spine/musculoskeletal structures (physiotherapist), and psychological status (psychiatrist, given stress's shared etiologic role). ST-suggestive features: tinnitus modulated by voluntary head/jaw/neck/eye movement or myofascial trigger-point pressure, concurrent cervical/head/shoulder pain, trauma-preceded onset, symmetric audiometry with asymmetric tinnitus, coexisting TMD/bruxism/clenching.
- **Ralli 2017**: a 19-maneuver somatic examination battery (TMJ, head/neck, eye-gaze maneuvers) operationalizes the same screening idea at the bedside.

**The shared laterality rule**: all three converge on using laterality concordance as a bedside discriminator. Ralli's cohort found 76.2% concordance between a positive somatic (TMJ) history and TMJ modulation in unilateral tinnitus (p<.001); Saczuk reports ~54% correlation between tinnitus laterality and body-pain side, and explicitly states discordant TMD/tinnitus laterality (opposite sides) argues *against* a link in that individual patient. This is a zero-cost bedside heuristic: **ipsilateral TMD-tinnitus supports somatic workup; contralateral argues for otologic-only management.**

### Axis 4 — Treatment: The RCT-Level Evidence

**Dapuzzo 2024 (SR+MA, PROSPERO CRD42022314972)** is the only paper in this set restricted to RCTs (5 trials, n=329, 2017–2020) rather than cross-sectional association data, making it the strongest causal-treatment evidence available:

| RCT | Country | n | Comparison | Key finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delgado de la Serna 2020 | Spain | 61 | Manual therapy + exercise + education vs. exercise + education alone | MT arm superior on TMD pain, tinnitus severity (VAS, p<.001, η²=0.233), THI (p<.001, η²=0.501), depression, mandibular ROM |
| Van der Wal/Michiels 2020 | Belgium | 80 | Early- vs. delayed-start orofacial PT | 34–46% achieved clinically relevant tinnitus-questionnaire improvement |
| Demirkol 2017 | Turkey | 31 | Nd:YAG vs. 810 nm diode LLLT | Both improved from baseline; Nd:YAG 100% vs. diode 30% median VAS improvement |

Pooled across all 5 RCTs: **OR 0.26** [0.19, 0.37], **RR 0.49** [0.40, 0.59], **RD −0.31** [−0.38, −0.24] — all highly significant (p<.00001), with moderate-to-substantial heterogeneity (I² 22–78% depending on effect measure). The authors conclude a **multidisciplinary non-invasive approach** (manual therapy + exercise + education) is the most efficacious identified treatment. Predictors of better outcome: higher baseline tinnitus severity/handicap (12–42% of outcome variance) and — counterintuitively — higher initial impairment predicting *more* room to improve; predictors of worse outcome: higher baseline pressure-pain hypersensitivity over temporalis (10–41% of variance).

This RCT-level finding is corroborated by two lower-rigor but consistent sources: **Ralli 2017**'s historical pooled treatment-outcome data (16 studies, 1964–2014) showing an average **69%** improvement/resolution (32% no change), and **Saczuk 2024**'s narrative synthesis reporting **43–86%** improvement across the reviewed intervention studies. **Michiels 2023** frames the first-line treatment identically — musculoskeletal physical therapy (counseling + exercise + manual technique) directed at the cervical spine and/or TMJ — noting total remission is rare even with treatment.

### Clinical Decision Synthesis

1. **Screen** otologically-unremarkable tinnitus patients (normal audiometry, negative imaging) for somatic/TMD contribution using history + a somatic maneuver battery or the Michiels 4-criterion decision tree.
2. **Check laterality concordance** — ipsilateral TMD/tinnitus supports a somatic link and justifies dental/PT referral; discordant (opposite-side) laterality argues against it in that patient.
3. **If ST-suggestive**, route to multidisciplinary TMD-directed therapy — manual therapy + exercise + patient education (± stabilization splint) — the only bundle with RCT-level causal evidence (Dapuzzo 2024, OR 0.26). Avoid over-relying on otologic treatment alone.
4. **Set realistic expectations**: expect improvement in 43–86% of patients (or ~69% by historical pooled data), not necessarily complete resolution — total remission is uncommon (Michiels 2023).
5. **Counsel on evidence quality**: the epidemiological headline figures (OR 7.3, "8× more common") come from narrative reviews and SRs without meta-analytic pooling; the only quality-appraised quantitative synthesis (Bousema 2018) grades the whole evidence base "low" and shows publication bias inflates several estimates — communicate the association as real but of uncertain exact magnitude, and causality as still scientifically unresolved (Saczuk 2024, Bousema 2018 both explicit on this point).
