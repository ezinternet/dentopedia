## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> **범위** — 한국 기관(대학병원·치과대학병원)과 한국 건강보험(NHIS) 자료로 만든 연구 29편만 썼다. 외국·다국가 연구는 뺐다. 기관 논문은 환자 국적을 따로 적지 않아 "한국 기관 = 한국 환자"로 본다. 건강보험 3편은 한국 가입자 전체라 가장 직접적이다.
>
> **수명** — 장기 임플란트 단위 누적생존율 (Cumulative Survival Rate, CSR)은 대체로 **95–98%**다. 예: 연세대 10년 98.2%, 서울대 마이크로스레드 10년 초과 97.9%.
>
> **예외** — RBM 표면 임플란트는 10–15년 **92.5%**이고 실패 17개 중 11개가 고정체·나사 파절이다.
>
> **단위** — 환자 단위가 더 낮다. 연세대 12.3년 코호트는 임플란트 95.2%, 환자 88.4%.
>
> **조기실패** (보철 전) — 표준 직경 대형 코호트 **0.8–3.4%**, 협폭 임플란트 **4.4–9.4%**. 정의가 논문마다 달라 서술 수준으로만 비교한다.
>
> **건강보험 자료** — 조기실패를 분리하지 못하는 청구 기반이다. 현재 흡연 HR 1.59, RBM 표면 HR 1.841 (SA 대비), 병원급 치과 HR 0.35 (개인의원 대비; 증례 선택 차이 가능).
>
> **기관 코호트의 위험인자** — 수련의 식립 aHR 2.86, 하악 2.31, 치주염 관련 치아 상실 3.57, 정기 유지관리 불량 2.35. 이벤트 수가 6–48개라 방향성만 본다.
>
> **재식립** — 1년 생존 88.1%, 5년 생존은 치주염 병력 있으면 90.2%, 없으면 97.0%.
>
> **공백** — 한국 자료로 임플란트 중앙 수명을 낸 연구는 없다. 16기관 연구의 중앙 생존 16년은 보철물 기준이다.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 29 studies (2011–2026) done in Korean institutions or on Korean National Health Insurance Service (NHIS) data: 3 nationwide claims cohorts, one 16-institution prosthesis-lifespan study and about 25 single- or two-centre cohorts and trials; patient nationality is rarely stated and is inferred from the Korean setting.

Long-term implant-level survival clustered at 95–98% (92.5% for a fracture-prone RBM design; patient-level 88.4–95.7%), while pre-prosthetic early failure ranged from 0.8% in large standard-diameter cohorts to 4.4–9.4% in narrow-diameter implants; claims-based NHIS studies cannot separate early failure.

Risk signals (smoking HR 1.59, trainee operator aHR 2.86, periodontitis-related tooth loss aHR 3.57, RBM surface HR 1.841) come from small-event single-centre data or claims without clinical covariates, so they are directional; no Korean study reports a median implant lifespan.

## 세줄요약

한국 기관 또는 한국 건강보험(NHIS) 자료로 만든 29편(2011–2026) 종합: 건강보험 전국 청구자료 3편, 16기관 보철 수명 연구 1편, 나머지는 단일·2기관 코호트와 시험이며 환자 국적은 거의 명시되지 않아 한국 기관이라는 점으로 한국 환자로 본다.

장기 임플란트 단위 누적생존율은 95–98%에 몰려 있고(고정체 파절이 많은 RBM 설계 92.5%, 환자 단위 88.4–95.7%), 보철 전 조기실패는 표준 직경 대형 코호트 0.8%에서 협폭 임플란트 4.4–9.4%까지 벌어지며 건강보험 청구 연구는 조기실패를 분리하지 못한다.

위험 신호(흡연 HR 1.59, 수련의 식립 aHR 2.86, 치주염 관련 치아 상실 aHR 3.57, RBM 표면 HR 1.841)는 이벤트가 적은 단일기관 자료이거나 임상 공변량이 없는 청구 자료에서 나와 방향성이고, 임플란트 중앙 수명을 낸 한국 연구는 없다.

## Summary

This page answers one question using only Korean-setting evidence held in the wiki: what survival and early-failure experience should be expected for implants placed in Korea, and which factors are linked to loss? It narrows and updates [[overviews/korean-university-hospital-implant-survival-cohorts-overview]] and complements [[overviews/early-implant-failure-surveillance-update-2022-2026-overview]], which mixes international data. Inclusion rule: the study was done at a Korean institution or on Korean NHIS data; international reviews and multinational cohorts are excluded. Table labels below are short; the linked pages are listed under *Evidence key*.

## Key Contributions

### 1. Longevity (implant-level cumulative survival)

| Study | Implants | Follow-up | Survival |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kim 2018 · Yonsei (Straumann, abstract only) | 1,692 | 10 y | 98.23% (patient 95.70%) |
| Nam 2023 · SNUDH (micro-thread) | 490 | >10 y | 97.9% |
| Ryoo 2023 · SNUDH (RBM) | 247 | 10–15 y | 92.5%; 10 fixture + 1 screw fractures, 6 peri-implantitis |
| Song 2024 · Yonsei | 872 | mean 12.3 y | 95.2% (patient 88.4%); 78.4% of failures late |
| Yum 2023 · SNUDH (SLA) | 782 | ≥5 y | 96.8%; 17 of 25 failures peri-implantitis |
| Kim 2025 TST · SNUDH (early failures excluded) | 316 | ≥5 y | 98.1% |
| Lee 2026 SPT · SNUDH + Pusan | 3,555 | 5 y | 98.8% (patient 99.0%) |
| Kim 2026 disability · Dankook | 465 | up to 10 y | 98.3% |
| Kim 2025 mandible · Wonkwang | 604 | mean 44.4 mo | 97.68% |
| Jang 2011 · Seoul Veterans | 6,385 | mean 45.7 mo | 96.33% as reported (crude 98.3%) |
| Kim 2024 one-piece NDI · SNU Bundang | 40 | mean 10.5 y | 95.1% (patient 96.0%) |

Patient-level survival is lower than implant-level survival wherever both are given.

**Nationwide NHIS.** Among 2,354,706 implants in people aged 65 or older (mean 2.84 years), late removal was 0.53%; relative to SA surfaces the adjusted HR for removal was 1.841 for RBM and 1.424 for HA, and abutment type was unrelated. **Prosthesis, not implant:** a 16-institution study (841 prostheses, 485 patients) estimated a median prosthesis survival of 16 years.

### 2. Early (pre-prosthetic) failure

| Study | Early failures | Rate | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kim 2018 · Yonsei | 13 / 1,692 | 0.77% | abstract only |
| Lee 2026 SPT · SNUDH + Pusan | 32 / 3,555 | 0.90% | 67% of 48 removals |
| Kim 2026 disability · Dankook | 5 / 465 | 1.1% | 5 of 7 failures before prosthesis |
| Lee 2026 long neck · Daegu | 2 / 117 | 1.7% | 1-year follow-up |
| Kim 2024 one-piece NDI · SNU Bundang | 1 / 40 | 2.5% | 3 months after immediate loading |
| Kang 2019 · Dankook | 35 / 1,031 | 3.4% | text states 4.4%; table counts used |
| Kim 2023 NDI · Kyung Hee | 12 / 274 | 4.4% | 12 of 19 failures |
| Kwon 2026 · Yonsei (immediate RCT) | 3 / 54 | 5.6% | socket anatomy |
| Aung 2024 · SNUDH (immediate) | 3 / 49 | 6.1% | all sinus-related |
| Hwang 2023 3.0 mm · SNUDH | 6 / 64 | 9.4% | all 0–4 months; 5 osseointegration failures |
| Han 2025 · SNU (immediate, esthetic) | 3 / 20 | 15% | small series |
| Song 2026 · Yonsei (immediate loading) | 2 tapered vs 5 straight | — | counts only |

Large standard-diameter cohorts sit at about 0.8–3.4%; narrow-diameter and small immediate series reach 4–15%. The three NHIS papers use re-implantation or removal claims, exclude failure before restoration or report no early/late split, so they say nothing about pre-prosthetic failure.

### 3. Risk factors (Korean data only)

- **Smoking:** current smokers HR 1.59 (95% CI 1.20–2.09) in 23,573 adults; ex-smokers 1.16 (not significant); dose-response above 10 cigarettes/day, 10 years or 10 pack-years; women 3.39 vs men 1.48. Narrow-diameter implants: smoking OR 4.758, osteoporosis OR 3.420. The SNUDH SLA cohort found smoking not significant (HR 2.050) with very few smokers.
- **Operator and site:** trainee surgeon aHR 2.86 and mandible aHR 2.31 for early failure (Dankook); the mandible effect appeared only after adjustment.
- **Periodontitis and maintenance:** periodontitis-related tooth loss aHR 3.57, poor supportive periodontal therapy (SPT) compliance aHR 2.35; regular SPT offsets the penalty (98.8% vs 96.3%).
- **Age:** HR 1.17 per year in the 3.0 mm series (6 events).
- **Care setting:** hospital-based clinics HR 0.35 vs private practices; the top 10% and 5% volume private practices HR 1.23 and 1.38. Residual case-mix confounding is likely.
- **Surface and system:** RBM HR 1.841 vs SA in NHIS data; double-threaded tapered vs dual-thread HR 2.507 in one SLA cohort.
- **Osteoporosis:** OR 3.420 in narrow-diameter implants, but a one-year postmenopausal prospective series stratified by bone density had no failures; too short and small to reconcile.

### 4. After failure, and late mechanical failure

- **Re-implanted implants:** 1-year survival 88.1% (80 implants, Gangneung-Wonju); 5-year survival 90.2% with vs 97.0% without a periodontitis history, periodontitis history aHR 3.417 (464 implants, SNUDH). Both abstract only.
- **Late mechanical failure:** 1.24-fold odds per 1,000 mm³ of masseter volume (23 cases vs 92 controls, Ewha; exploratory, abstract only).
- **Stability testing:** two damping-capacity devices agreed with the reference device (r = -0.870 and -0.473) before loading, but no failure was analysed, so this is device agreement, not prediction.

## Methodology

Papers are the Korean-setting pages in the wiki as of 2026-11-08, collected by PubMed surveillance and summarised as source and wiki pages; this page reads those pages only. Early-failure percentages are count over stated denominator, rounded. No pooling was attempted because follow-up, failure definition and unit (implant, patient, prosthesis, claim) differ. The international short-implant overdenture SR+MA was excluded as not Korean-patient evidence.

## Results

1. Korean institution cohorts report long-term implant-level survival of about 95–98%, with patient-level values lower.
2. The 92.5% RBM result reflects fixture fracture, and NHIS data point the same way for the RBM surface.
3. Pre-prosthetic early failure is about 0.8–3.4% in large standard-diameter cohorts and higher in narrow-diameter and small immediate series.
4. NHIS claims cannot separate early failure and lack clinical covariates, so their effect sizes are population-level associations.
5. Trainee placement, periodontitis-related tooth loss, poor maintenance and prior failure recur in institution cohorts.
6. HRs and ORs above 4 rest on 6–48 events and are hypotheses.

**Clinical reasoning drawn from the held pages (not new facts).** Quote 95–98% long-term survival and say whether it is per implant or per patient; add that early-failure risk is higher for narrow-diameter or immediate protocols. Treat maintenance (SPT) and periodontitis control as the modifiable levers and give smokers a dose-response message. Do not read hospital-versus-private differences as proof that either setting causes failure. Final judgement rests with the treating clinician.

**Gaps.** No Korean prospective multicentre early-failure study, no NHIS analysis separating pre-prosthetic failure, no Korean data on anticoagulant or PPI effects, no median implant lifespan, and most cohorts give no ethnicity variable.
