## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 범위: 술식 변형과 시점(모체 클러스터 3)과 부비동염 예방·관리(클러스터 4)를 묶은 하위 종합. 절 번호는 모체 기준을 유지한다. [확인]
> - 측방과 치조정 접근의 3년 흡수는 같다(0.95 vs 0.98 mm). 측방은 초기 이득이 크지만(12.0 vs 8.07 mm) 대부분 1년 안에 흡수되므로, 잔존 골 높이가 결정 변수다(Park 2023, n=103). [확인]
> - 발치 전 변형 측방 거상(Yue 2022, 3년 증례): 잔존 골 1–2 mm가 12–14 mm로 늘었고, 13개월 뒤 발치와 즉시 식립을 했다. 순서를 뒤집는 전략이지만 1례 보고다. [확인]
> - 바닥 결손이 있는 상동에서 막 우선 측방 거상은 1단계 완료율 97.2%였다. 1–7년 누적 생존은 96.5%였지만 8년에는 64.3%로 떨어져, 후기 꼬리를 추적해야 한다(Wen 2023, n=35/36). [확인]
> - 부비동염 빈도(Rocha 2023, SR+MA 27편): 측방창 1.35%, 치조정 0.00%, 관골 임플란트는 sinus-slot 21.62% vs 상동 외 0.00%. 술식의 범주보다 세부 기법이 위험을 더 많이 결정한다. [확인]
> - 술 전 만성 부비동염은 감염과 임플란트 손실의 1순위 예측인자다(Kozuma 2017, n=109, 121 상동, p=0.007). 모든 합병증 8건(6.6%)이 이 군에서 나왔다. 프로토콜은 비과 평가 → ENT 의뢰 → 클래리스로마이신 ≥12주 또는 FESS → 해소 확인 후 거상이다. [확인]
> - 가역·비가역 금기 분류(Pignataro 2008): 전문가 의견이라 수치는 인용할 수 없고, 후속 연구가 수치로 채우는 틀로 쓴다. [확인]
> - 증례 수준 예방: 비강 스테로이드와 LTRA를 2개월 쓴 1례에서 울혈이 해소된 뒤 양측 거상을 했고 7개월 동안 부비동염이 없었다(Suzuki-Yamazaki 2020, n=1). [확인]
> - 거상 직후 상악동 입구(MSO)가 일시적으로 좁아지는 것은 생리적 반응이다. 그 자체로 항생제를 늘릴 근거는 아니다(Sakuma 2020). [확인]
> - 종합 한 줄: 부비동염 예방은 선별 → 해부 층위 평가 → 술식 상담 순서이며, 기법 선택은 환자의 부비동 상태를 반영해야 한다. [미검증]

## Three-line Summary

This sub-overview holds the procedural-variant and timing cluster and the sinusitis-prevention cluster of the parent lateral-window sinus-lift synthesis.
Lateral and crestal approaches resorb equally by three years, and procedure-specific technique (such as the sinus-slot zygomatic approach) drives sinusitis risk more than the broad category does.
Preoperative chronic sinusitis is the dominant modifiable infection risk, so the sequence is screen, stratify ENT anatomy, and then counsel on technique.

## 세줄요약

이 하위 종합은 모체 측방창 상악동 거상 종합의 술식 변형·시점 절과 부비동염 예방 절을 담는다.

## Cluster 3 — Procedural variants and timing

- **Lateral vs crestal at 3 years.** Park 2023 (n=103 Korean, retrospective longitudinal) — lateral SFE gives larger initial graft gain (12.0 mm vs 8.07 mm crestal) but 3-year resorption is identical (0.95 vs 0.98 mm), concentrated in year 1. Residual bone height (RBH) is the decision variable; long-term outcomes converge.
- **Pre-extraction LSFE.** Yue 2022 (3-year case) — for periodontally hopeless upper molars, a modified LSFE *before* extraction grew residual 1–2 mm to 12–14 mm; extraction + immediate placement followed 13 months later; <2 mm bone loss at 3 years. Sequencing flips the usual order.
- **Sinus floor defects.** Wen 2023 (n=35/36 sinuses, 1–9 yr) — collagen-membrane-first LSFE in patients with pre-existing sinus floor defects yielded 97.2% one-stage completion despite a 27.8% intraoperative SMP rate; cumulative survival 96.5% at 1–7 yr but dropping to 64.3% at 8 yr — late-failure tail to watch.
- **Sinusitis rate by technique.** Rocha 2023 (SR+MA, 27 studies: 18 SFE, 9 zygomatic implant) provides the first direct comparison of postoperative sinusitis prevalence: overall SFE 1.11% vs zygomatic implants 3.76%. Within SFE: **lateral window 1.35% vs transcrestal 0.00%** (95% CI for transcrestal 0.00–3.18%). Within zygomatic implants: sinus-slot technique 21.62% vs extrasinus technique 0.00%. Technique choice dominates sinusitis risk more than procedure category. The sinus-slot zygomatic approach carries unacceptably high sinusitis risk and should be avoided where extrasinus or intrasinus approaches are feasible.

## Cluster 4 — Sinusitis prevention and management in the lateral sinus lift context

This cluster addresses the downstream complication — postoperative sinusitis — rather than intraoperative membrane management.

### Preoperative chronic sinusitis: the #1 risk factor for infection and implant loss

Kozuma 2017 (retrospective cohort, 109 patients, 121 sinuses, 252 implants) established the strongest multivariate signal for postoperative sinus complications after lateral augmentation: **preoperative chronic sinusitis was the #1 independent predictor of both postoperative infection (p=0.007) and implant loss (p=0.007)** by logistic regression. All 8 complicated cases (6.6%) occurred in the chronic-sinusitis-positive subset. The risk hierarchy from Kozuma 2017:

- *Infection axis*: chronic sinusitis > timing of implant insertion > membrane perforation (NS) > diabetes (NS)
- *Implant loss axis*: chronic sinusitis > sex (male) > diabetes > postoperative denture use > membrane perforation

The clinical protocol Kozuma proposes: mandatory preoperative rhinologic workup (SNOT-20 + nasal endoscopy + CT paranasal sinuses) → ENT referral if chronic sinusitis diagnosed → macrolide therapy (clarithromycin ≥12 weeks) or FESS to achieve clearance → confirm resolution → only then proceed to augmentation. Removable denture pressure over the grafted sinus should be avoided postoperatively.

### The Pignataro framework: when ENT involvement is mandatory, and what it can and cannot fix

Pignataro 2008 (Italian position paper, expert opinion — no original clinical data) is the foundational source for the ENT-collaboration model this cluster's other papers build on and reference throughout as "the Pignataro taxonomy." It proposes a 3-step ENT management sequence for sinus-lift candidates: **preventive-diagnostic** (nasal endoscopy + CT when sinus pathology is suspected), **preventive-therapeutic** (FESS to correct reversible contraindications *before* SFE), and **diagnostic-therapeutic** (postoperative sinusitis surveillance and management). Its central clinical contribution is a binary contraindication taxonomy that still organizes later consensus papers in this cluster:

| Type | Conditions | Path forward |
|---|---|---|
| Reversible (FESS-correctable) | OMC anatomical variants, chronic rhinosinusitis, nasal polyposis, oro-antral fistula | Correct via FESS (or, per Suzuki-Yamazaki 2020 below, medical management), then proceed to SFE |
| Irreversible (absolute contraindication) | Cystic fibrosis, Kartagener's syndrome, primary immunodeficiency, sinonasal malignancy | SFE precluded |

As expert opinion with no original incidence data, Pignataro 2008's specific numbers cannot be cited — but the *framework* is what later papers operationalize with data: Kozuma 2017 supplies the multivariate evidence that preoperative chronic sinusitis (a "reversible" condition in Pignataro's schema) is the dominant risk factor once actually measured, and Gulbey 2026 (Cluster 2) supplies the CBCT-based OMC-variant detection that the "preventive-diagnostic" step calls for. Read this way, Pignataro 2008 is the organizing skeleton the rest of Cluster 4 fills in with numbers.

### High-risk patients: ENT stratification before sinus lift

Suzuki-Yamazaki 2020 (case report, n=1, 48M with septal deviation + concha bullosa + Haller cell + allergic rhinitis — the Pignataro "reversible contraindication" classification) describes a noninvasive medical prevention protocol for patients who decline FESS:

- **Topical nasal steroid** (fluticasone propionate nasal spray): 2 months preoperative
- **LTRA** (pranlukast hydrate): 2 months preoperative, for allergic-rhinitis-driven mucosal swelling
- **Perioperative antibiotic** (amoxicillin): standard prophylaxis

After 2 months of this protocol, nasal congestion resolved. Bilateral sinus lift was performed uneventfully; no sinusitis at 2-month panoramic or 7-month clinical follow-up. This case illustrates the Pignataro taxonomy: irreversible contraindications (Wegener's, malignant tumors) absolutely preclude sinus lift; reversible contraindications (anatomic drainage impairments, allergic rhinitis, chronic rhinosinusitis) require ENT-directed pretreatment, not automatic cancellation.

### Post-operative MSO dynamics: transient obstruction is the norm, not the exception

Sakuma 2020 (detailed above in Cluster 2 anatomy) is equally relevant to the sinusitis axis: 19.4% of sinuses had transient infundibulum occlusion at 1 week post-LSFE, and 11.1% had edema anatomically reaching the MSO — yet zero sinusitis developed across 72 sinuses at 9 months. This documents that transient post-LSFE MSO narrowing is an expected physiological response, not a complication in itself, and should not trigger antibiotic escalation unless clinical signs of infection emerge.

### Quantitative sinusitis benchmarks by technique

Rocha 2023 (detailed in Cluster 3) provides the key numbers: lateral-window SFE sinusitis 1.35%, transcrestal 0.00%, overall SFE 1.11%. These figures allow procedure-specific risk communication and informed consent quantification for the first time in a pooled framework.

**Synthesis [미검증]:** the sinusitis prevention chain has three links: (1) **screen** for preoperative chronic sinusitis — it is the dominant modifiable systemic risk, outranking even membrane perforation in Kozuma's model; (2) **stratify ENT anatomy** — OMC variations (Gulbey 2026), Haller cells, concha bullosa, and septal deviation require a structured ENT consultation pathway; (3) **counsel on technique** — lateral window carries 1.35% sinusitis risk, transcrestal effectively zero; technique choice should incorporate the patient's baseline sinus health and anatomy when both approaches are feasible.
