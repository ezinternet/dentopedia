## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: 비타민 D(Vitamin D, 25(OH)D)와 골유착(Osseointegration)의 관계는 "기전·동물에서는 강하지만 사람 임상에서는 갈린다" — 결핍을 *위험 축*으로 보고 **중증 결핍만 교정**하는 것이 방어 가능한 임상 입장이고, 충분군에 대한 *루틴 보충*은 입증되지 않았다.
> - 근거 사다리: 우산형 종설 1편(Tallon 2024) ▸ 체계적 고찰 3편(Miron 2025·43편, Werny 2022·18편, Buzatu 2024·7편 human) ▸ RCT 1편(Ayyad 2025, 국소도포) ▸ 전향 코호트 2편(Mohsen 2024, Francis 2024) ▸ 후향 1편(Dulinska 2025) ▸ 서사적 종설 1편(Duarte 2025).
> - **용량-반응은 단조가 아니라 U자형** (Duarte 2025 서사적 종설): 결핍만이 아니라 **과잉도 해롭다** — 혈청 >70 ng/mL(과잉, Hypervitaminosis D)에서 임플란트 주위 골소실 **21.1배** 증가(Duarte가 인용한 Cheng 2024 후향 연구). 즉 "많을수록 안전"이 아니라 **위쪽 경계가 존재**한다.
> - 동물·기전 근거는 일관되게 양성: 결핍은 신생골·골-임플란트 접촉(Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)을 낮추고 보충은 골유착을 높임 — 특히 당뇨·골다공증·만성신장질환(CKD) 동반 시(Werny 2022).
> - 사람 근거가 갈리는 지점 = **결핍의 중증도**. 양성 신호는 거의 전부 **중증 결핍(<10~20 ng/mL)** + 동반위험(흡연·치주염·당뇨)에 몰려 있다.
> - 위험 쪽 정량: Mohsen 2024(전향, 143부위) — 조기실패 전체 7%이나 **<10 ng/mL에서 46.2%** vs >30 ng/mL 4.5%·10~30 ng/mL 2.3%; Miron 2025 — 결핍 시 조기실패 **최대 4배**; Dulinska 2025 — 결핍군 임플란트 주위 골소실 유의 증가.
> - 무효 쪽: Francis 2024(전향, 174 임플란트) — **상관 없음**, 실패한 8건이 오히려 비타민 D가 더 높음(42.5 vs 31.9 ng/mL); Buzatu 2024(7편 human·4450 임플란트) — 전반적 유의 연관 없음(평균 소실 8.37%).
> - 충돌 해석: 무효 연구는 대체로 **비타민 D 충분한 집단**이라 결핍 신호가 희석됨 — "결핍이 위험"과 "충분군에서 무차이"는 모순이 아니라 *용량-반응의 양 끝*.
> - 국소도포라는 다른 경로: Ayyad 2025 RCT(즉시식립 24개, 국소 칼시트리올 겔) — 6개월 탐침깊이·출혈지수·통증·ISQ(80 vs 75)·골밀도(+18.1% vs +6.4%) 모두 유의 우수 (소규모·단일센터, 전신 vs 국소는 별개 질문).
> - 근거 한계(Tallon 2024 우산형): 메타분석 부재, 선택적 보고, 기준치·용량·결과지표 불일치 — 그래서 "보충하면 좋아진다"는 인과는 아직 약함.
> - 임상 takeaway: ① 고위험(흡연·당뇨·치주염·재발 실패·중증 골다공증) 환자는 **술전 25(OH)D 선별** ② **중증 결핍(<10~20 ng/mL) 교정 후 식립** ③ 충분군에 루틴 보충은 근거 없음 — 게다가 과잉(>70 ng/mL)은 *적극적 위험*이라 보충의 목표는 정상범위 복귀이지 최대화가 아니다 ④ 비타민 D는 골질·1차 안정성을 대체하지 못함 — [[overviews/bone-quality-implant-risk-modification-overview]]의 위험 축에 *부가* 레버로 본다.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 9 papers (1 umbrella review, 3 SRs, 1 RCT, 2 prospective cohorts, 1 retrospective, 1 narrative review) on vitamin D [25(OH)D] and implant osseointegration spanning mechanism, animal models, and human clinical data; the umbrella review (Tallon 2024) identifies no pooled meta-analysis and heterogeneous thresholds/outcomes across the literature.

Animal and mechanistic evidence consistently supports a pro-osseointegration role for vitamin D; the human evidence splits by deficiency severity — positive signals concentrate in severe deficiency (<10–20 ng/mL) with co-risks (Mohsen 2024: failure rate 46.2% vs 4.5% for <10 vs >30 ng/mL; Miron 2025 SR: up to 4× early failure risk), while replete-population studies find no association (Francis 2024, n=174: NS, failed implants actually had higher vitamin D; Buzatu 2024 SR, 7 studies/4450 implants: no significant overall association); topical calcitriol gel at immediate implant placement improved 6-month ISQ (80 vs 75) and bone density (+18.1% vs +6.4%) in a small single-center RCT (Ayyad 2025, n=24).

Pre-surgical 25(OH)D screening and correction of severe deficiency (<10–20 ng/mL) before implant placement is defensible in high-risk patients (smokers, diabetes, periodontitis, recurrent failure, severe osteoporosis); routine supplementation of replete patients is not supported by current evidence, and the dose–response is U-shaped rather than monotonic — a narrative review (Duarte 2025) reports hypervitaminosis D (>70 ng/mL) carrying a 21.1-fold increase in peri-implant bone loss, so the target of correction is a normal range, not a maximum.

## 세줄요약

비타민 D[25(OH)D]와 임플란트 골유착(Osseointegration) 9편(우산형 1·SR 3·RCT 1·전향 2·후향 1·서사적 종설 1) 종합 — 기전/동물·SR/MA·사람 임상 근거 사다리 전반; Tallon 2024 우산형 고찰은 메타분석 부재·기준치·용량·결과지표 불일치를 명시.

동물·기전 근거는 일관되게 양성; 사람 근거는 결핍 중증도에 따라 갈림 — 양성 신호가 중증 결핍(<10~20 ng/mL)+동반위험에 몰림(Mohsen 2024: 실패율 <10 ng/mL 46.2% vs >30 ng/mL 4.5%; Miron 2025 SR: 조기 실패 최대 4배), 충분군 연구는 무차이(Francis 2024 n=174 NS·Buzatu 2024 SR 7편/4450개 전반 유의 연관 없음); 국소 칼시트리올 겔을 즉시식립에 적용한 소규모 RCT(Ayyad 2025, n=24)는 6개월 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ) 80 vs 75·골밀도 +18.1% vs +6.4% 모두 유의 우위.

임상 권고: 고위험군(흡연·당뇨·치주염·재발 실패·중증 골다공증)에서 술전 25(OH)D 선별 + 중증 결핍(<10~20 ng/mL)만 교정 후 식립이 방어 가능; 충분군 루틴 보충은 현재 근거 없으며, 용량-반응이 U자형이라 과잉(>70 ng/mL)에서 임플란트 주위 골소실 21.1배 증가가 보고됨(Duarte 2025 서사적 종설) — 교정의 목표는 정상범위 복귀이지 최대화가 아니다.

## Summary

Vitamin D is biologically tied to bone metabolism (calcium homeostasis, osteoblast differentiation, immunomodulation), so a pro-osseointegration role is mechanistically plausible. The wiki holds 9 papers spanning the full evidence ladder, and they do **not** all agree. This page resolves the apparent contradiction.

**The signal is real but conditional.** Across the human studies, the association between low vitamin D and implant failure appears almost exclusively at the **severe-deficiency end** and is amplified by co-morbid risk (smoking, periodontitis, diabetes). Where populations are vitamin-D-replete, no association is detectable. The two "negative" studies and the "positive" studies are therefore best read as opposite ends of a dose–response curve, not as a true conflict.

**The curve also has an upper bound.** Duarte 2025 (narrative review) frames the relationship as U-shaped rather than monotonic: excess is its own risk, with hypervitaminosis D (>70 ng/mL) reported at a 21.1-fold increase in peri-implant bone loss. This does not change the deficiency-end reading above, but it does retire the implicit assumption that higher 25(OH)D is simply safer, and it converts "do not routinely supplement" from a null-benefit argument into one with a harm ceiling behind it.

### Why the studies disagree — reconciliation

- **Severity stratification is everything.** Mohsen 2024 shows a clean dose–response (46.2% → 4.5% → 2.3% as 25(OH)D rises). The null studies (Francis 2024, Buzatu 2024) sampled largely replete cohorts, so the high-risk <10 ng/mL stratum was thin — the curve's steep end was simply under-sampled.
- **Outcome timing.** The risk signal is strongest for *early* implant failure / osseointegration phase (Miron 2025, Mohsen 2024), where bone formation is rate-limiting — consistent with the animal BIC data (Werny 2022).
- **Co-risk amplification.** The worst loss rates (Buzatu 11.1%) require deficiency *plus* smoking/periodontitis — vitamin D is an effect modifier, not an isolated lever.
- **Local ≠ systemic.** Ayyad 2025 (topical calcitriol at the osteotomy) is a different mechanism question than serum status; promising but small/single-centre and not generalisable to systemic supplementation.
- **The curve is U-shaped, not monotonic.** Duarte 2025 adds an upper bound the deficiency literature does not test for: >70 ng/mL → 21.1× peri-implant bone loss. Note the honest limit of this as an explanation — Francis 2024's failed implants averaged 42.5 ng/mL, well *below* that 70 ng/mL threshold, so hypervitaminosis does **not** account for the Francis anomaly; the replete-cohort/under-sampling reading above remains the better explanation for that specific study. What the U-curve does change is the shape of the recommendation, not the reading of Francis.
- **Genetic effect modification is unresolved.** Buzatu 2024 reports a mixed picture on vitamin D receptor (Vitamin D Receptor, VDR) polymorphisms — TaqI showed no association with implant loss (Alvim-Pereira 2008), while rs3782905 allele G was significantly associated with poor osseointegration (Pereira). Serum status may therefore not be the whole exposure, but the wiki holds no paper that resolves this.
- **Evidence quality caps causality.** Tallon 2024 (umbrella) and Werny 2022 flag no meta-analysis, selective reporting, and inconsistent reference levels — so "correct deficiency and outcomes improve" remains association-level for systemic vitamin D in humans. Duarte 2025's upper-bound signal inherits a weaker pedigree still: it is a narrative review relaying a single retrospective study, so the 21.1× figure should be read as a flag to avoid over-supplementation, not as a calibrated risk estimate.

### Clinical decision

1. **Screen selectively, not universally.** Order serum 25(OH)D pre-operatively in high-risk patients: smokers, diabetics, periodontitis, prior/recurrent early implant failure, severe osteoporosis, malabsorption.
2. **Correct severe deficiency before placement.** The defensible threshold for action is severe deficiency (**<10–20 ng/mL**); this is where the failure curve is steep and supplementation is biologically rational.
3. **Do not routinely supplement replete patients** (>30 ng/mL) for implant outcomes — no human evidence of benefit, and failures have occurred at normal/high levels (Francis 2024). Where a patient is already on high-dose supplementation, the target is return to a normal range rather than maximisation: excess (>70 ng/mL) is reported as an active risk, not a neutral one (Duarte 2025).
4. **Vitamin D is additive, not substitutive.** It does not replace primary stability, bone quality management, or co-risk control — layer it onto, not instead of, the [[overviews/bone-quality-implant-risk-modification-overview]] risk axis.
5. **Topical vitamin D** is an emerging adjunct (Ayyad 2025) — interesting but not yet a standard-of-care recommendation.
