## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 결론**: 아스피린 단독·직접경구항응고제 (Direct Oral Anticoagulant, DOAC)·단일 VKA(INR ≤3.5) 복용 환자는 단순 발치·임플란트 식립 시 **약을 중단하지 않는다** — 혈전색전증 위험이 출혈 위험보다 크고, 지속 군의 술후 출혈은 중단 군과 유의하게 다르지 않다 (Calcia 2021 SR+MA).
> - **이중항혈소판요법 (Dual Antiplatelet Therapy, DAPT) 예외**: 즉시 출혈 RR 10.3 (건강 대조군 대비; Alagil 2023), 지연 출혈 RR 7.72 — 단독 아스피린과 달리 처치 계획 시 심장내과 협진 필수.
> - **헤파린 브릿징 (Heparin Bridging) 반대**: ACCP 2022 (Douketis) 강한 권고 — 심방세동 환자 브릿징은 출혈만 늘리고 혈전색전증은 줄이지 않음.
> - **DOAC 스케줄링**: 혈중 농도 최저점에 시술 예약; DOAC 유지 + 국소 지혈로 충분 (Yefet 2025 SR).
> - **VKA 2일 중단**: 연속 full-dose 대비 출혈 OR 0.30 (95% CI 0.09-0.97); LMWH 브릿징은 차이 없음 (Boccatonda 2023 NMA, VKA-전용).
> - **임플란트**: 경구 항응고제 유지 시 출혈 위험 유의하게 증가하지 않음 (RR 1.81, 95% CI 0.70-4.63; Zou 2022 SR+MA) — VKA 경계 신호(RR 3.42, P=.05), DOAC는 안전 (RR 1.67).
> - **1차 지혈제 — 트라넥삼산 (Tranexamic Acid, TXA)**: 출혈 사건 유의 감소 유일한 약제 (OR 0.27, GRADE 중등도; Mahardawi 2023 NMA 23편). 단 NOAC 환자 조기 출혈에는 효과 없음 (EXTRACT-NOAC; Ockerman 2021); 지연 출혈·다발 발치에는 유효.
> - **혈소판풍부피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)**: 항응고 환자에서 봉합 단독·마른 거즈보다 우수; TXA·키토산·젤라틴 스펀지와 출혈 사건 유의차 없음 (Katz 2024 SR); Xa 억제제에서 젤라틴 스펀지와 동등 (Kyyak 2023 RCT). 지혈 시간은 키토산보다 느림.
> - **키토산(Chitosan) 역설**: 지혈 시간 최단(NMA 최상위)이나 출혈 사건 수는 가장 많음 — 속도와 재출혈 위험이 해리됨 (Mahardawi 2023).
> - **스페인 SECIB CPG**: 단순 발치·임플란트에서 항응고제 유지 + 국소 지혈; INR ≤2.2면 출혈 위험 건강인과 유사; 근거는 대부분 등급 D (Valenzuela-Mencia 2024).

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 11 papers (3 SR+MA, 2 NMA, 2 RCT, 2 SR, 2 consensus guidelines) on perioperative management of anticoagulant/antiplatelet therapy for dental procedures: the evidence from DOAC-era studies confirms that continuation is safe for simple extractions and implant placement when local hemostatic measures are applied — postoperative bleeding does not differ significantly between continued and interrupted single-drug therapy (Calcia 2021), whereas DAPT carries an immediate-bleeding risk of RR 10.3 vs healthy controls (Alagil 2023) that places it in a separate decision category.

Among local hemostatic agents, the 2023 network meta-analysis of 23 RCTs (Mahardawi) identifies TXA as the only agent with a statistically significant reduction in bleeding events (OR 0.27, GRADE moderate), while PRF is non-inferior to TXA, chitosan and gelatin sponge for bleeding events but achieves hemostasis more slowly than chitosan; paradoxically, chitosan achieves the fastest hemostasis but the highest later bleeding odds — the speed/rebleeding tradeoff must guide agent selection.

Guideline hierarchy: ACCP 2022 (Douketis) gives strong recommendations against heparin bridging in atrial fibrillation and for VKA continuation during device implantation; the SECIB CPG (Valenzuela-Mencia 2024) endorses continuing anticoagulants for all routine oral surgery with local hemostasis, noting that evidence remains grade D for most indications.

## 세줄요약

11편(SR+MA 3, NMA 2, RCT 2, SR 2, 지침 2) 종합: 단일 항응고제·항혈소판제 복용 환자는 단순 발치·임플란트 시 약 유지가 원칙 — 술후 출혈은 중단군과 유의차 없고(Calcia 2021), 아스피린 단독은 건강인 대비 출혈 위험 없으나 DAPT는 즉시 출혈 RR 10.3(Alagil 2023)으로 별도 결정 범주.

국소 지혈제 NMA(23편, Mahardawi 2023): 출혈 사건을 유의하게 줄이는 유일한 약제는 TXA(OR 0.27, GRADE 중등도); PRF는 TXA·키토산·젤라틴 스펀지와 동등하고 단독봉합보다 우수; 키토산은 가장 빠른 지혈이나 출혈 사건이 가장 많음(속도-재출혈 해리).

지침 계층: ACCP 2022는 심방세동 헤파린 브릿징 강하게 반대; SECIB CPG는 모든 처치에서 항응고 유지 + 국소 지혈 지지(근거 D 위주); DOAC는 혈중 최저점 예약이 실용적 추가 전략(Yefet 2025).

## Summary

This overview synthesizes the 11 papers in the `drug/anticoagulants` folder that were not covered by the companion perioperative overview (see [[overviews/drug-anticoagulant-antiplatelet-perioperative-overview]]). They address three intersecting clinical questions: (1) whether to continue or interrupt anticoagulant/antiplatelet therapy for extractions and implants; (2) which local hemostatic agent to choose; and (3) what the updated guideline landscape says.

### Axis 1 — Continuation vs interruption: the evidence base

**Single-drug therapy is safe to continue.** The 2021 SR+MA by Calcia (13 RCTs) showed that continuing versus stopping a single anticoagulant or antiplatelet drug was associated with more intraoperative bleeding (RR ~2.57), but *not* more postoperative bleeding (RR 0.81, P=0.42). This dissociation — more oozing during surgery but not after — is the mechanical basis for the "continue and use local hemostasis" strategy. Yefet's 2025 SR of 17 DOAC-specific oral surgery studies confirms that DOACs are safe with local measures and adds the practical refinement of scheduling at the pharmacokinetic trough.

**The DAPT exception is quantitatively large.** Alagil's 2023 SR+MA distinguishes regimen classes with a precision earlier reviews lacked: aspirin alone produced no significant bleeding versus healthy controls (RR 1.26 immediate, 2.17 delayed, both non-significant), whereas dual antiplatelet therapy produced RR 10.3 immediate and RR 7.72 delayed — a signal that is both large and statistically certain. Continuing versus interrupting DAPT gave RR 2.13 for immediate bleeding (P=0.07, non-significant), which is consistent with the "don't stop DAPT" advice on thromboembolic grounds but acknowledges an unresolved practical risk.

**VKA-specific: 2-day suspension reduces bleeding in one NMA.** Boccatonda's 2023 NMA (8 VKA-specific RCTs) found a 2-day coumarin suspension had OR 0.30 (95% CI 0.09-0.97) for bleeding versus continuous full-dose, while reduced-dose coumarin and LMWH bridging did not differ from continuous. This sits in tension with the "never bridge" message because it uses 2-day interruption rather than bridging — the clinical lesson is that modest VKA reduction is feasible and does not require substituting heparin.

**Implant placement: OAC continuation is safe.** Zou's 2022 SR+MA (7 studies, implant-specific) pooled RR 1.81 (0.70-4.63) for continued OAC versus controls — non-significant at P=0.22. The VKA subgroup showed a borderline tendency (RR 3.42, 95% CI 1.00-11.67, P=.05); the DOAC subgroup did not (RR 1.67). This extends the extraction evidence to implant surgery and supports continuation with local measures.

### Axis 2 — Local hemostatic agents: the NMA hierarchy

Mahardawi's 2023 NMA of 23 RCTs is the most powerful ranking to date. Using conventional gauze/cotton as reference:

| Agent | Bleeding events | Time to hemostasis |
|---|---|---|
| Cyanoacrylate | OR 0.03 (P=0.051, borderline) | — |
| **TXA** | **OR 0.27 (95% CI 0.10–0.69), P-score top** | mid-ranking |
| PRF | not significantly different from TXA | not reported in NMA |
| Chitosan | not significantly different from TXA | fastest (SMD −9.78) |
| Collagen plug | not significantly different from TXA | fastest (SMD −10.13) |
| Gelatin sponge | *worse* than conventional in anticoagulated subgroup | — |

Key dissociation: chitosan and collagen achieve the fastest hemostasis but the highest later bleeding odds (worse than conventional for bleeding events), attributed to low mechanical strength and dislodgement after initial clot formation.

Two PRF papers fill in the DOAC-specific gap. Katz's 2024 SR (11 studies, 864 patients) found PRF outperforms dry gauze and stitches alone, and does not differ significantly from TXA, chitosan or gelatin sponge for bleeding events. Kyyak's 2023 RCT (21 patients on FXa inhibitors, split-mouth vs gelatin sponge) found no difference between PRF and gelatin sponge — both acceptable, both slower than chitosan. PRF's advantage is being fully autologous (no pharmaceutical cost or allergy risk) but it requires centrifuge availability.

**TXA mouthwash and NOACs: a negative primary RCT.** The EXTRACT-NOAC trial (Ockerman 2021, 218 patients, 4 Belgian hospitals, double-blind) found TXA mouthwash did not reduce primary any-bleeding at 7 days (26.4% vs 28.6%, RR 0.92, P=0.72) or early bleeding in NOAC patients whose morning dose was skipped. Exploratory findings showed a reduction in delayed bleeds (rate ratio 0.32) and in multi-tooth extractions (rate ratio 0.40). The trial stopped early for futility. Practical implication: TXA mouthwash is not a simple add-on fix for NOAC patients; its role is focused on delayed and multi-tooth situations.

### Axis 3 — Guideline hierarchy

**ACCP 2022 (Douketis)** is the international reference. Two strong recommendations: (1) against heparin bridging in atrial fibrillation — the largest body of evidence shows bridging increases bleeding without reducing thromboembolism; (2) continue VKA during pacemaker/ICD implantation. Separate minor-procedure recommendations exist for dental, dermatologic and endoscopic settings, but are not detailed in the available abstract.

**SECIB CPG 2024 (Valenzuela-Mencia)** is the dental-specialty reference. Fifteen PICO questions, grade C for two (coumarin at INR ≤2.2 has similar bleeding to healthy patients; INR >3 or multiple extractions needs physician input) and grade D (expert opinion) for the remainder. The overall message mirrors ACCP: continue the anticoagulant, use local hemostasis. Hereditary coagulopathies need haematologist input.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **단순 발치 프로토콜**: DOAC·아스피린·단일 VKA(INR ≤3.5) — 유지. 국소 지혈: TXA 4.8% 양치액(OR 0.27) 또는 PRF 또는 봉합+압박. 당일 아침 복용을 생략하거나 최저점 시술을 조율하면 DOAC 환자에서 추가 안전마진.
2. **DAPT 환자**: 반드시 심장내과 협진 후 결정. 발치 후 즉시·지연 출혈 고위험(RR 10.3·7.72) — 완전히 피하거나 스텐트 시점·약제 조율 후 진행. 이 결정은 치과의사가 단독으로 내릴 수 없다.
3. **지혈제 선택 알고리즘**: TXA 우선(유일하게 출혈 사건 유의 감소); 자가채혈 가능하면 PRF (TXA와 동등, 완전 자가); 키토산은 빠른 지혈이 필요할 때만(재출혈 위험 감수); 젤라틴 스펀지는 항응고 환자에서 오히려 재출혈 증가 경향.
4. **TXA 양치액 한계**: NOAC 환자 조기 출혈에는 효과 없음(EXTRACT-NOAC). 지연 출혈(day 2–7) 예방 및 다수 발치(2개 이상)에서 탐색적 이점 — 만능 해법이 아님.
5. **임플란트**: 경구 항응고 유지로 식립 가능; VKA는 경계 신호(P=.05)이므로 INR 확인 및 충분한 국소 지혈 준비.

## Gaps & Future Research

- DAPT 지속 vs 중단 RCT: P=.07 결과를 확정하는 대규모 데이터 부재.
- PRF의 표준화된 제조 프로토콜 및 DOAC-특이적 대조 RCT 부족 (kyyak 2023: n=21, 단일 기관).
- 다수 발치·복잡 임플란트 처치에서 DOAC 수술 전후 전략 RCT.
- 키토산 dislodgement 기전 연구 및 장기 결과.
