## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 결론: 총의치 수직고경(Occlusal Vertical Dimension, OVD)을 정확히 잡는 **단일 신뢰 기법은 없다** — 여러 방법을 교차 검증해 합의점을 찾는 것이 표준 (Alhajj 2017, Fayad 2025).
> - 방법 분류: **발치 전(pre-extraction)** — 기존 기록·측면 윤곽·두부계측(cephalometric) / **발치 후(post-extraction)** — 안정위+자유공극(rest position + freeway space), 안모·심미, 발음(phonetics), 연하(swallowing), 안면계측(anthropometric), 두부계측.
> - 안정위(rest position) 단독은 불안정: 두부계측 50명 연구에서 **안면 연조직이 실제 하악 위치 변화를 가리고**, 안정위가 변동하며 의치 장착 시 이동 → 무치악 안정위만으로 OVD 결정은 부적합 (Sheppard 1975).
> - 안면계측(anthropometric) 타당도 SR+MA(13편/3275명/11기법): **엄지 길이(thumb length)**가 전체 최강 상관(r≈0.63), 남성은 외안각–구열(distal canthus–rima oris ≈0.57), 여성은 **새끼손가락 길이(little-finger ≈0.46)** — 단 I²≈99%, 비뚤림 위험 높아 보조 지표로만 (Goyal 2026).
> - OVD 오류는 실제 결과를 부른다: 즉각 **+5 mm 증가** 시 뇌파(EEG) 부정 심리지표 상승, **−3 mm 감소** 시 교합력 저하 (Matsuda 2014) — 잘못된 VD는 심리·기능에 측정 가능한 악영향.
> - **수직고경 과다(증가, over-opening) 징후**: 긴 하안면, 입술 다물기 곤란·긴장, 치아 부딪힘(clicking), 연하·발음 곤란, 잔존치조제 외상, 근피로 (Fayad 2025).
> - **수직고경 과소(감소, over-closing) 징후**: 짧은 하안면, 구각부 처짐·구각구순염(angular cheilitis), **이순구(mentolabial fold) 깊어짐**, 턱끝 돌출, **이근(mentalis muscle) 과활성으로 턱끝이 오톨도톨·뭉치는 모양**, 볼·혀 깨물기 (Fayad 2025).
> - 질문의 "하악 턱끝 복숭아 형태"는 위 **과소 VD(over-closing)에서 이근 과활성으로 턱끝 피부가 자갈/뭉친 복숭아씨처럼 보이는 소견**에 해당할 가능성이 높음 — 즉 VD를 너무 낮게 잡았다는 신호로 읽고, 안정위·자유공극·발음·안모를 재평가해 VD를 적정 회복하는 방향 (단, 본 위키 논문은 "복숭아"라는 용어 자체를 명시하진 않음 — Fayad의 mentalis strain 기술에 근거한 해석).
> - 임상 워크플로: 한 가지에 의존하지 말고 ①안정위−자유공극(2–4 mm) ②발음(/s/ closest speaking space, /m/) ③안모·입술지지 ④필요시 안면계측·두부계측을 **교차 확인**, 환자 적응 고려해 점진 조정.
> - 용어: 자유공극(Freeway Space, 안정위-교합위 차 ~2–4 mm), 안정위(rest position), 발음공간(closest speaking space).

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 7 papers (Fayad 2025 comprehensive review, Alhajj 2017 method classification, Goyal 2026 SR+MA of anthropometric validity, Khan 2023 anthropometric RCT, Matsuda 2014 EEG consequence study, Sheppard 1975 cephalometric rest-position study, Satin 2023 OVD-transfer accuracy): no single method reliably establishes complete-denture OVD — the physiologic rest position alone is unstable (soft tissue masks skeletal change; shifts on denture insertion, Sheppard 1975), and anthropometric adjuncts are corroborating only (thumb length r ≈ 0.63 best correlate, I² = 99%, Goyal 2026), a verdict Khan 2023's index-finger RCT sits inside rather than overturns.

A wrong VD produces measurable harm: acute +5 mm increase raises an EEG negative-mood indicator, and −3 mm decrease lowers occlusal force (Matsuda 2014); reduced VD presents clinically as shortened lower face, drooping commissures, angular cheilitis, and mentalis hyperactivity ("bunched chin"), while increased VD shows elongated face, strained incompetent lips, and clicking teeth during speech.

Clinical bottom line: cross-check at least three methods in sequence — rest position minus freeway space (2–4 mm), phonetics (/s/ closest speaking space), and esthetic/lip-support assessment — seek convergence rather than relying on any single technique, and adjust gradually allowing patient adaptation (Fayad 2025). Then remember that determining the vertical is not keeping it: digital workflows carry OVD imperfectly and **in one direction — 98.9% of transfer deviations increase it** (Satin 2023, duplicate denture 0.45 mm vs occlusion rim 0.72 mm), so expect a slightly over-opened try-in rather than a random one.

## 세줄요약

7편 종합(Fayad 2025 종합리뷰, Alhajj 2017 방법 분류, Goyal 2026 안면계측 SR+MA, Khan 2023 검지 RCT, Matsuda 2014 EEG 결과, Sheppard 1975 두부계측 안정위, Satin 2023 OVD 전달 정확도): 총의치 OVD를 정확히 잡는 단일 신뢰 기법 없음 — 안정위는 불안정(연조직이 골격 변화를 가리고, 의치 장착 시 이동, Sheppard 1975); 안면계측은 보조지표(엄지 길이 r≈0.63 최강, I²=99%, Goyal 2026)이며, 검지법을 의치 제작까지 밀고 간 RCT(Khan 2023, 여성 r=0.966·1주 만족 97%)도 이 판정을 **뒤집지 못한다**(상관≠개별환자 정확도, 1주는 너무 짧음).

VD 오류는 실제 결과: +5mm 즉각 증가 시 EEG 부정 심리지표 상승, -3mm 감소 시 교합력 저하(Matsuda 2014); 과소 VD는 짧은 하안면·구각 처짐·구각구순염·이근 과활성("턱끝 뭉침"), 과다 VD는 긴 하안면·긴장된 입술·치아 부딪힘 소견(Fayad 2025).

임상 결론: 안정위-자유공극(2–4mm) → 발음(/s/ 근접 발화 공간) → 안모·입술 지지 등 최소 3가지 방법을 교차 검증해 합의점을 찾고, 단계적으로 조정하며 적응 허용(Fayad 2025). 그리고 **정하는 것과 지키는 것은 다르다** — 디지털 워크플로는 OVD를 한 방향으로 흘린다: 측정치 98.9%가 양(+)편차로 **OVD를 체계적으로 증가**시키며(복제의치 0.45mm < 교합제 0.72mm; Satin 2023), 계통 편향이라 무작위 산포와 달리 시적 단계에서 **예측·보상 가능**하다. 단 복제의치법은 기존 의치가 있어야 하므로 초회 무치악 환자는 교합제법이 기본값이다.

## Thesis

Determining the occlusal vertical dimension (OVD) in an edentulous patient is the step with the least intrinsic landmark — there are no teeth to dictate it — and the five papers here triangulate the problem from four angles.

**1. There is no single accurate method (Alhajj 2017, Fayad 2025).** Alhajj classifies methods into *pre-extraction* (pre-extraction records, profile/silhouette tracing, cephalometry) and *post-extraction* (physiologic rest position minus freeway space, esthetics/facial appearance, swallowing, craniofacial/anthropometric landmarks, cephalometry, phonetics, pre-existing dentures). Fayad's comprehensive review extends this from classical to digital methods (3-D scanning, CBCT, CAD/CAM, AI) and reaches the same verdict: all are empirical with error, so **combine techniques and seek convergence**.

**2. The physiologic rest position — the workhorse method — is itself unstable (Sheppard 1975).** On cephalometric radiographs of 50 denture wearers, facial soft tissue *masked* skeletal mandibular movement, the edentulous rest position varied over time and usually *shifted on denture insertion*, and its range widened with denture age/experience. Conclusion: the edentulous rest position is "not particularly suitable" for OVD, least of all via facial measurements. This is the historical anchor for the modern "don't rely on rest position alone" caution.

**3. Anthropometric (facial-measurement) methods are usable but only as adjuncts (Goyal 2026).** This SR+MA (13 cross-sectional studies, 3275 participants, 11 facial methods) found *thumb length* the strongest overall correlate of OVD (r ≈ 0.63 common-effect), with sex-specific best methods (men: distal outer-canthus–rima-oris ≈ 0.57; women: little-finger length ≈ 0.46). But heterogeneity was extreme (I² up to 99%) and risk of bias high — so these are corroborating, not standalone, references.

**4. Getting VD wrong has measurable consequences (Matsuda 2014).** In 21 denture wearers given duplicate dentures at −3 mm and +5 mm VDO, an acute **+5 mm increase raised an EEG negative-mood indicator** after chewing, and a **−3 mm decrease lowered occlusal force** — objective evidence that VD error perturbs both psychology and function, justifying the care the determination methods demand.

**5. An anthropometric method taken all the way to a denture still ends as an adjunct (Khan 2023).** The anthropometric literature almost universally stops at a correlation coefficient; this RCT (80 edentulous patients randomized, 71 analysed, Dow University, Pakistan) is unusual in going further — it *built the dentures* on the index-finger-derived OVD and then measured patient satisfaction. Correlation between right index-finger (2D) length and the Sn–Me facial distance was strong (females r = 0.966, p < 0.001), and 97.0% of the experimental group reported satisfaction at one week versus 84.2% of controls. The authors' own conclusion is nonetheless "an **adjunct** method," and three limits explain why that restraint is right: a group-level correlation does not establish that the method predicts an *individual* patient's OVD within clinical tolerance (no Bland–Altman or limits-of-agreement analysis is reported, only correlation); a **one-week** endpoint is far too short for OVD-related complications, which surface over months, and near-ceiling satisfaction in new denture wearers is routine; and the sample is single-centre and single-population, while anthropometric ratios vary by ethnicity — the paper itself notes its values diverge from Sudanese and other comparator cohorts. Randomization was by coin toss. Khan 2023 therefore sits *inside* Goyal 2026's scope and does not overturn it: the SR+MA's "adjunct only, I² ≈ 99%" verdict remains the governing statement, and this trial is best read as showing that an anthropometric start does not obviously *harm* short-term satisfaction — not that it establishes the right vertical.

**6. Determining the OVD is not the same as keeping it — and the workflow leaks in one direction (Satin 2023).** Every paper above answers "what vertical should this patient have?" None answers "did that vertical survive into the finished denture?" This in-vitro study (mounted edentulous casts standing in for a patient; n = 15 fabrications per arm, not 15 patients) compared the two common digital routes for carrying OVD into a CAD-CAM denture — **duplicate dentures (DD)** versus **occlusion rims (OR)** — against a scanned articulated-cast control. DD deviated less (0.45 ± 0.23 mm vs OR 0.72 ± 0.13 mm; F = 46.00, p < 0.0001), but the more useful finding is the *direction*: **98.9% of all measurements deviated positively — both workflows systematically increased the OVD.** A one-directional bias is clinically different from random scatter because it is predictable and therefore compensable, whereas noise is not. Two caveats keep this in proportion: the authors judge the magnitude "may not be clinically significant," and a vertical error is partly absorbed by the freeway space in a way a horizontal error is not — so do not equate 0.7 mm here with the 0.74–0.86 mm horizontal discrepancy that [[complete-denture/singh-2026-nick-notch-intraoral-gothic-arch-jaw-relation]] calls clinically perceptible. And DD's advantage is unavailable to the patient who most needs care: duplicating a well-fitting denture presupposes one exists, so a first-time edentulous patient gets the occlusion-rim route by default.

## Method matrix

| Method | Class | Evidence here | Practical note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-extraction records / profile / cephalometry | pre-extraction | Alhajj 2017, Fayad 2025 | Best when available; rarely is in fully edentulous |
| Physiologic rest position − freeway space (~2–4 mm) | post-extraction | Alhajj 2017; **caution** Sheppard 1975 | Workhorse but rest position is variable/maskable |
| Esthetics / facial appearance / lip support | post-extraction | Fayad 2025 | Judges over- vs under-opening (see signs below) |
| Phonetics (closest speaking space, /s/, /m/) | post-extraction | Alhajj 2017, Fayad 2025 | Functional cross-check |
| Swallowing | post-extraction | Alhajj 2017 | Adjunct |
| Anthropometric / facial measurements (thumb, finger, canthus–rima) | post-extraction | **Goyal 2026 SR+MA**; primary RCT **Khan 2023** | Adjunct only; high heterogeneity. Khan built dentures on index-finger OVD (97% satisfied at 1 wk) but reports correlation, not agreement — and 1 wk is too short to judge a vertical |
| Cephalometric | both | Alhajj 2017, Sheppard 1975 | Skeletal > facial markers |

**And one step the matrix above does not cover — transferring the OVD you decided on:**

| Transfer route | Class | Evidence here | Practical note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duplicate denture (DD) | digital workflow | **Satin 2023** (in vitro) | Less deviation (0.45 ± 0.23 mm) — but requires an existing well-fitting denture, so unavailable first-time |
| Occlusion rim (OR) | digital workflow | **Satin 2023** (in vitro) | More deviation (0.72 ± 0.13 mm, p < 0.0001); the default route for first-time edentulous patients |
| *Either route* | — | Satin 2023 | **98.9% of deviations increase OVD** — the error is systematic and one-directional, therefore anticipatable at the try-in rather than random |

## Clinical signs of incorrect VD (answering the "chin" question)

Grounded in the Fayad 2025 review of incorrect-VD signs:

- **Excessive / increased VD (over-opening)**: elongated lower face; strained, parted, or incompetent lips; clicking of teeth during speech; difficulty swallowing; trauma/soreness of residual ridges; muscle fatigue; reduced freeway space.
- **Reduced / decreased VD (over-closing)**: shortened lower face; drooping oral commissures with angular cheilitis; **deepened mentolabial (chin) fold**; prominent ("witch's") chin; **mentalis muscle strain/hyperactivity producing a bunched, dimpled chin**; cheek/tongue biting; excessive freeway space.

The clinical picture of a **"peach-pit / bunched" appearance over the mandibular chin** corresponds to **mentalis hyperactivity from a reduced (over-closed) VD** — i.e. a signal that the vertical dimension has been set too low. The corrective reading is to re-evaluate VD with the multi-method cross-check above (rest position + freeway space, phonetics, lip support/esthetics) and restore an appropriate dimension. (Note: the source papers describe mentalis strain / deepened mentolabial fold but do not use the word "peach" — this is the literature-grounded interpretation of that descriptive sign.)
