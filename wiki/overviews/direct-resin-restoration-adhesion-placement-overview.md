## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: 직접 복합레진(composite) 수복의 두 축(접착방식·충전방식) 모두 "재료 선택"보다 "프로토콜 실행"이 결과를 좌우한다.
> - 축 1 접착 — 법랑질(enamel) 가장자리가 있으면 선택적 법랑질 산부식 (Selective Enamel Etching) 또는 3-step 산부식-수세 (Etch-and-Rinse, EAR)이 SR+MA·RCT에서 일관 우세 (Hong 2021, Assis 2023).
> - 유니버설 접착제 자가부식 (Self-Etch, SE) 단독은 법랑질 가장자리 존재 시 회피 — 비우식성 치경부 병소 (Noncarious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 24개월 RCT에서 임상 부적합(Clearfil SE mode 6개 탈락, Oza 2022).
> - **장기 RCT 2편이 이 규칙을 더 날카롭게 만든다**: 갈림길은 "E&R이냐 SE냐"가 아니라 **"법랑질에 인산을 대느냐 마느냐"**다. 3년 RCT는 E&R과 선택적 법랑질 산부식 (Selective Enamel Etching, SEE)을 모든 FDI 항목에서 동등하다고 봤고(성공률 82.6% vs 83.8%, Peumans 2023), 4년 RCT는 산부식 없는 유니버설 단독군만 기저치 아래로 유지율이 떨어졌다(Omoto 2025). 즉 법랑질만 산부식하면 두 전략은 3–4년까지 수렴한다.
> - 순수 상아질(dentin) 표적(NCCL·깊은 상아질 변연)은 두 전략 모두 임상 허용, SE가 술후 민감 더 낮음(Assis 2023).
> - 유니버설 접착제 결합강도 향상: 매트릭스 금속단백분해효소 (Matrix Metalloproteinase, MMP) 억제(클로르헥시딘 2% 세척)·능동 도포·선택적 상아질 산부식·다층 도포·소수성 레이어·에탄올 습윤 접착(Hardan 2021, 모두 p≤0.02) — 단축 도포·접착 직전 탈감작제는 결합강도 저하.
> - 즉시 상아질 봉쇄 (Immediate Dentin Sealing, IDS)는 간접 수복 한정(3-step EAR·2-step SE에서만 효과), 직접 수복엔 비적용 — 임상 전환(술후 민감·수명 개선)은 미입증(Reis 2024 비판).
> - 축 2 충전 — 벌크필(bulk-fill)과 적층충전(incremental)은 9 RCT MA·12 RCT 네트워크 메타분석(NMA)·우산형 리뷰 모두에서 ≥24개월 임상 동등(Zailai 2025 실패 RR 0.82, Chaple-Gil 2026 차이 없음).
> - **단, 이 동등성엔 조건이 붙는다** — 비피복 벌크필은 나노하이브리드의 2–4배로 마모된다(59–118 µm vs 31 µm/200k회, SDR은 임시재 수준; Osiewicz 2022 in-vitro). 0.5–1.0 mm 통상 레진으로 덮으면 마모가 나노하이브리드 단독과 같아진다(monoblock). 즉 **피복은 유동성 벌크필만의 각주가 아니라 모든 벌크필에 걸리는 조건**이며, 동등성 RCT가 24개월에서 멈춘다는 점과 함께 읽어야 한다.
> - 근관치료 (Endodontically Treated Tooth, ETT) 대구치도 벌크필 적용 범위 안 — 24개월 RCT(n=240)에서 폴리에틸렌 섬유강화는 일반 벌크필 대비 추가 이득 0(Metwaly 2024).
> - "저수축 = 임상 우월" 명제는 21 RCT MA로 부정 — 유일한 차이는 12개월 변연적합에서 통상 메타크릴레이트가 우세(Kruly 2018), silorane도 동등(Magno 2016).
> - 신규 메커니즘(He 2026, in-vitro): 중합성 억제제 PBHT로 겔포인트(gel point)를 지연시켜 수축응력을 유의 저감(47.5→13.1×10⁻³ MPa·s)하고 기존 BHT 대비 용출도 4배 감소 — 기전은 유망하나 세포독성·노화·임상 데이터 없는 단일 bench 연구로, silorane과 같은 "lab 결과 ≠ 임상 우월" 경계선 위에 있다.
> - 복합레진 vs 아말감: 5년 생존 동등(Worthington 2021 Cochrane), 후방 복합레진 기저 실패율 1.55/100 수복-년(Astvaldsdottir 2015), 주 실패원인은 2차 우식(보통 3년 후).
> - 수리(repair)가 교체보다 우월 — 15년 후방 복합레진 SR은 없으나 전치부 데이터 강력(van de Sande 2019, 수리를 비실패로 보면 15년 생존 69%) — 국소 결함은 수리가 1차 선택.
> - 간접 수복으로의 escalation은 근거가 아니라 해부(교두 피개·교두간 거리 ½ 접근·근관치료 후 후방치 변연융선 소실+과중 교합)로 결정 — 일상적 Class II에 간접 수복은 강제되지 않는다. SR+MA 3편(Angeletaki 2016 11년, Josic 2023, Tennert 2024) 중 **간접이 우월하다고 본 것은 하나도 없다**.
> - 독일 S3 가이드라인(AWMF 083-028, 20개 학회)이 두 축 모두에 착지한다 — Part 1(Wolff 2024): 후방부는 간접 레진 인레이보다 **직접 레진 우선**, 적응증에 교두 수복 포함. Part 2(Sekundo 2024): 치수 근접 시 1단계 선택적 우식제거, Class II는 sectional matrix+링·쐐기, 법랑질 인산 산부식, 벌크필 4 mm는 충분 출력 조사 조건부. 단 consensus는 근거 사다리 **최하위** — SR+MA를 이기는 게 아니라 수렴하는 것.
> - **러버댐 근거는 통념보다 약하다** — Cochrane(RCT 6편, 전부 high RoB)은 6개월 NCCL 생존에만 낮은 확신도의 이득(OR 2.29)을 보고했고 12·18개월엔 효과 불명(Miao 2021). 쓰는 게 맞지만 근거는 "생물학적 타당성 + 저비용"이지 입증된 장기 이득이 아니다 — 정설처럼 말하지 말 것.
> - 상시 라이너(liner)는 근거 없음 — Cochrane SR+MA(RCT 8편)에서 술후 과민 이득 불일치·1–2년 실패율 동일(Schenkel 2019). 깊은 와동·상아질 봉쇄·언더컷 차단 같은 개별 적응증에서만.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of Cochrane SR, umbrella reviews, multiple SR+MA/NMA, large RCTs, and the German S3 guideline on two decisions in every direct composite restoration — adhesion strategy and placement technique; the discriminating variable is not etch-and-rinse (EAR) versus self-etch (SE) but **whether enamel receives phosphoric acid at all** (Hong 2021 SR+MA; Oza 2022 RCT SE-mode failure at 24 mo; Peumans 2023 3-yr RCT EAR ≈ selective-etch; Omoto 2025 4-yr RCT, un-etched universal alone falls below baseline retention).

Bulk-fill and incremental placement are clinically equivalent through ≥24 months (9-RCT MA RR 0.82 NS; 12-RCT NMA no significant difference; umbrella review; Zailai 2025, Chaple-Gil 2026) — conditional on a conventional occlusal cover layer, since uncovered bulk-fills wear 2–4× a nanohybrid (Osiewicz 2022) — and the claim "low-shrinkage = clinically superior" is refuted by 21-RCT MA (Kruly 2018).

Protocol execution, not material generation, drives outcome; repair outperforms replacement for localized defects (15-year anterior survival 69% with repair as non-failure; Van de Sande 2019); no SR+MA at any follow-up length finds indirect composite superior to direct (Angeletaki 2016, Josic 2023, Tennert 2024), and the German S3 guideline positively prefers direct in the posterior — so escalation to indirect is an anatomic judgment about remaining structure and load, not an evidence-mandated step.

## 세줄요약

Cochrane SR·우산형 리뷰·SR+MA/NMA·대규모 RCT·독일 S3 가이드라인을 아우르는 직접 복합레진 수복의 두 축 종합; 갈림길은 EAR이냐 SE냐가 아니라 **법랑질에 인산을 대느냐**다(Hong 2021 SR+MA; Oza 2022 SE 단독 24개월 부적합; Peumans 2023 3년 RCT E&R≈SEE 동등; Omoto 2025 4년 RCT 무산부식군만 기저치 이하).

벌크필과 적층충전은 ≥24개월 임상 동등(9-RCT MA RR 0.82 NS·12-RCT NMA 차이 없음; Zailai 2025·Chaple-Gil 2026) — 단 통상 레진 피복층이 전제(비피복 벌크필 마모 2–4배, Osiewicz 2022); "저수축=임상 우월" 명제는 21-RCT MA로 부정(Kruly 2018).

"재료 선택"보다 "프로토콜 실행"이 결과를 좌우; 국소 결함은 교체보다 수리 우선(15년 전치 생존 69%, Van de Sande 2019); SR+MA 3편 어디서도 간접이 우월하지 않고 독일 S3는 후방부 직접을 적극 선호하므로, 간접 에스컬레이션은 근거가 강제하는 단계가 아니라 잔존 치질·교합하중에 대한 해부학적 판단이다.

## Scope and Question

This overview synthesizes wiki evidence on two decisions every direct resin restoration requires: **how to bond** (adhesion strategy) and **how to place the material** (incremental vs. bulk-fill, low-shrinkage chemistry, repair). It draws on Cochrane SR, umbrella review, multiple SR+MA, NMA, and large RCTs already ingested under `wiki/resin-bonding/` and `wiki/resin/`. Indirect restorations are touched on only where direct vs. indirect comparison is informative; see [[inlay/angeletaki-2016-direct-vs-indirect-inlay-onlay-sr-ma]] for inlay/onlay focus.

The clinical question this page should answer: *given a posterior or cervical lesion in a permanent tooth, which adhesion and placement choices have the strongest evidence behind them, and where is the evidence base genuinely silent?*

## Part 1 — Adhesion Strategy

### 1.1 Classification (contemporary)

Reject "generation" terminology ([[resin-bonding/breschi-2025-adhesive-dentistry-evolution-review]]). Classify by smear-layer interaction:

| Class | Variants | Smear layer | Enamel bond | Dentin bond | Technique sensitivity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Etch-and-rinse (EAR) | 3-step, 2-step | Removed by H3PO4 | Strongest | Strong (3-step best) | High (wet/dry balance) |
| Self-etch (SE) | 2-step, 1-step | Modified, incorporated | Weaker | Acceptable–strong | Lower |
| Universal | E&R / selective-etch / SE mode | Operator choice | Mode-dependent | 10-MDP chemical bond to HAp | Mode-dependent |

3-step EAR remains the **gold standard for long-term performance**; universal adhesives are not "universal" in outcome — the chosen mode dominates.

### 1.2 Etching mode for universal adhesives — the central clinical decision

**Clinical RCT meta-evidence** consistently favors enamel etching when enamel margins exist:

| Source | n RCTs | Population | Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[resin-bonding/hong-2021-universal-adhesive-etching-modes-sr-ma]] | 13 | mixed direct restorations | E&R > SE — retention OR 0.35, marginal adaptation OR 0.49, marginal staining OR 0.49 | [확인] sr+ma |
| [[resin-bonding/assis-2023-acid-etching-universal-adhesive-nccl-sr-ma]] | 20 | NCCL | E&R better medium-term retention/adaptation/staining/secondary caries; SE lower post-op sensitivity | [확인] sr+ma |
| [[resin-bonding/doshi-2023-universal-adhesive-nccl-strategy-sr-ma]] | 17 (13 MA) | NCCL | No significant TE vs SE difference (I²=0%); publication bias suspected at 18mo retention | [확인] sr+ma |
| [[resin-bonding/oza-2022-universal-adhesive-nccl-rct-2year]] | 1 (5-arm RCT, 234 NCCL) | NCCL | Clearfil Universal Bond Quick **SE mode clinically unacceptable** (6 lost); SLE and E&R modes acceptable | [확인] rct |
| [[resin-bonding/peumans-2023-universal-adhesive-nccl-3year-rct]] | 1 (RCT, 251 NCCL, 90% recall) | NCCL | Same adhesive (CUBQ) at **3 years**: E&R 82.6% vs SEE 83.8% success — no difference on any FDI parameter | [확인] rct |
| [[resin-bonding/omoto-2025-nccl-adhesion-strategies-4year-rct]] | 1 (split-mouth RCT, 200 NCCL) | NCCL | **4 years**: survival curves NS (p=0.315), but Scotchbond Universal *alone* fell below baseline retention while the selective-etch arm did not | [확인] rct |
| [[resin-bonding/sharifian-2023-tbs-er-se-modes-superficial-deep-dentin]] | in vitro | extracted molars | Scotchbond Universal E&R > SE μTBS; superficial > deep dentin (p=0.005) | [확인] in-vitro |

**Reading the disagreement**: Hong 2021 (general restorations) and Assis 2023 (NCCL) favor E&R; Doshi 2023 (NCCL, more recent search) finds no difference. The Oza 2022 RCT — which Doshi/Assis postdate — is the cleanest signal: **universal adhesive in pure SE mode failed at 24 months on cervical lesions**, while selective-enamel-etch (SLE) and full E&R modes did not. The mechanism is the established hydrophilic / under-etched-enamel problem ([[resin-bonding/tay-2003-dentin-adhesives-hydrophilic]]) — 1-step acidic primers do not adequately demineralize enamel even when 10-MDP secures dentin.

**Two longer trials sharpen this into a cleaner rule.** Peumans 2023 followed the *same* adhesive as Oza (Clearfil Universal Bond Quick) for 3 years and found **E&R and selective-enamel-etch self-etch clinically indistinguishable** (success 82.6% vs 83.8%; retention 87.2% vs 86.3%; no FDI parameter separated them). Omoto 2025 followed Scotchbond Universal for 4 years and found the four adhesion strategies statistically equivalent on survival (p=0.315) — *but* the un-etched universal arm dropped below its own baseline retention by year 4 while the selective-etch arm held. Read together with Oza, the discriminating variable is not **E&R vs self-etch** — it is **whether enamel gets phosphoric acid at all**. Once selective enamel etching is in the protocol, the two strategies converge and stay converged out to 3–4 years; it is *pure* SE with untouched enamel that decays. This `refines` rather than reverses the bottom line below, and it extends the evidence horizon from 24 months to 3–4 years.

**Clinical bottom line**:
- Enamel margins present → **selective enamel etching (32–37% H3PO4, 15 s, rinse) + universal on dentin in SE mode**, OR full 3-step EAR. [확인]
- Pure dentinal target (NCCL, deep dentinal margin) → either strategy clinically acceptable; **SE has lower post-op sensitivity** ([[resin-bonding/assis-2023-acid-etching-universal-adhesive-nccl-sr-ma]]). [확인]
- Universal adhesive **SE mode alone with enamel margins** → avoid; the penalty is retention loss that accrues over years, visible by year 4 ([[resin-bonding/omoto-2025-nccl-adhesion-strategies-4year-rct]]) rather than at delivery. [확인]
- Once enamel *is* etched, **E&R vs SEE is not a decision worth agonizing over** — 3-year RCT equivalence on every FDI parameter ([[resin-bonding/peumans-2023-universal-adhesive-nccl-3year-rct]]). Spend the attention on etching the enamel, not on choosing between the two etched protocols. [확인]

### 1.3 Hybrid-layer durability and the universal-adhesive checklist

[[resin-bonding/hardan-2021-universal-adhesive-dentin-bond-sr-ma]] (61 in vitro studies) quantifies what improves and degrades universal-adhesive dentin bond strength. The actionable list:

**Increase bond strength (all p ≤ 0.02)**
- Prior MMP-inhibitor application (chlorhexidine 2% rinse, EDC primer)
- Scrubbing (active) application — supports manufacturer instructions
- Selective dentin etching
- Multiple-layer application
- Hydrophobic-layer overlay (especially with 1-step systems)
- Ethanol-wet bonding ([[resin-bonding/mortazavi-2012-ethanol-wet-bonding-clinical-trial]])
- Adequate (not shortened) application time
- Adequate air-blowing and light-curing time

**Decrease bond strength**
- Shortened application time
- Dentin desensitizers applied immediately before bonding

[[resin-bonding/carrilho-2019-mdp-adhesive-interface-stability-sr]] (SR, 72 studies) supplies the interfacial *why* behind three items on that list. 10-MDP self-assembles into nano-layered structures and builds an acid-base-resistant zone of water-insoluble MDP-Ca salts, which is what keeps the interface stable through a year of water storage — but the benefit is conditional: **active scrubbing with adequate dwell time**, **selective enamel etching for the higher-pH one-bottle systems**, and **HEMA-free formulation** are the conditions under which it materialises, while excess ethanol/HEMA co-solvent and under-concentrated MDP dissolve it. This is the mechanistic complement to Hardan 2021's statistical one, and it is why §1.2's "etch the enamel" rule and this section's "scrub, don't dab" rule are the same rule seen from two angles.

Mechanistically the hybrid-layer (HL) degrades via water sorption + hydrolysis + endogenous MMP-2/9 and cysteine cathepsins activated by acid etching ([[resin-bonding/breschi-2025-adhesive-dentistry-evolution-review]]; full treatment in [[overviews/resin-dentin-bond-durability-degradation-overview]]). The chlorhexidine 2% rinse before bonding is the single most validated MMP-inhibition step that bench data supports; clinical longevity proof is weaker — Reis 2024 ([[overviews/reis-2024-biomimetic-restorative-myths-narrative]]) flags this gap. [미검증] On the cost-benefit balance the CHX rinse is cheap and well-tolerated; absent clinical harm signal, in vitro consistency justifies routine use even while waiting for RCTs.

### 1.4 Immediate Dentin Sealing (IDS) — indirect only, with caveats

For indirect restorations (inlay/onlay/overlay), [[resin-bonding/hardan-2022-immediate-dentin-sealing-sr-ma]] (21 in vitro) shows IDS > DDS bond strength, **but only for 3-step EAR and 2-step SE — not for 2-step EAR or 1-step SE**. Adding a flowable resin layer over the adhesive amplifies aged bond strength.

The clinical-translation question is unresolved: [[overviews/reis-2024-biomimetic-restorative-myths-narrative]] argues clinical RCTs do not show IDS reduces post-op sensitivity or improves longevity ([[resin-bonding/samartzi-2021-immediate-dentin-sealing-review]] is the BRD-positive narrative counterpart). For *direct* restorations IDS is not applicable — this section exists to mark the boundary; the full in-vitro-vs-clinical argument lives in [[overviews/immediate-dentin-sealing-evidence-synthesis]].

## Part 2 — Placement Technique

### 2.1 Material baseline — composite vs. amalgam

[[resin/worthington-2021-composite-vs-amalgam-cochrane-sr]] (Cochrane, 2021 update): 5-year survival of direct composite ≈ amalgam in posterior permanent teeth, with a non-significant trend toward higher secondary-caries risk for composite. Amalgam phase-out is driven by Minamata + esthetics, not by survival inferiority — so composite restoration is the default, but the marginal-seal advantage one might expect from adhesion is not large at 5 years. [확인]

Posterior composite **baseline failure rate**: **1.55 lost restorations per 100 restoration-years** ([[resin/astvaldsdottir-2015-posterior-resin-composite-longevity-sr]], 8 prospective trials, 62,030 restoration-months). Dominant failure mode: secondary caries, typically after year 3. [확인 — SR, "low quality" GRADE]

### 2.2 Bulk-fill vs. incremental — converged evidence of equivalence

| Source | Design | n | Outcome | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[resin/boaro-2019-bulk-fill-composites-clinical-properties-sr-ma]] | SR+MA, lab + RCT 1–10 yr | 103 articles | BF: less shrinkage/stress/cusp deflection; lower microhardness ≤2mm; **clinical equivalent** | [확인] sr+ma |
| [[resin/silva-2023-bulk-fill-vs-conventional-resins-umbrella]] | Umbrella review (AMSTAR-2 low–moderate) | multiple SRs | Overall pooled trend toward conventional ~5× (NS); abrasion/microleakage/elastic modulus/shrinkage — **similar** | [확인] |
| [[resin/zailai-2025-bulk-fill-vs-conventional-composites-clinical-sr-ma]] | SR+MA, RCTs ≥12 mo | 9 RCT / 632 restorations | Failure RR 0.82 (0.33–2.01), p=0.67; equivalent <24mo and ≥24mo | [확인] sr+ma |
| [[resin/chaple-gil-2026-bulk-fill-incremental-class-ii-sr-nma]] | SR+NMA Class II | 12 RCT, 6/12/24 mo | No significant difference at any timepoint; ormocer-based / overlay BF non-significantly top-ranked | [확인] sr+nma |

**Bottom line**: For Class I/II direct posterior restorations, **bulk-fill and incremental are clinically equivalent through ≥24 months**. The choice is operator/cavity preference — bulk-fill saves chair time, incremental gives layer-shade control. [확인]

**Three caveats remain**:
1. **Bulk-fill needs a conventional occlusal cover layer — and not only the flowables.** [[resin/osiewicz-2022-wear-of-bulk-fill-resin]] (in-vitro ACTA three-body wear, ISO/TS 14569) tested four bulk-fills *uncovered* against a nanohybrid control: **59–118 µm/200k cycles vs 31 µm**, with SDR reaching the wear level of a temporary material (~118 µm vs ~100 µm for Protemp), and Vickers hardness 40–70 vs 124. Crucially, a **0.5–1.0 mm conventional cover layer made the stack wear like the nanohybrid alone** (monoblock behaviour) — the low-modulus substrate underneath does not compromise it. So the capping rule is not a flowable-only footnote to the IFU; it is the condition under which the clinical-equivalence finding above should be read, since the RCTs pooled in Zailai 2025 / Chaple-Gil 2026 stop at 24 months, which is short relative to how wear accumulates. Uncovered bulk-fill on a load-bearing occlusal surface — and especially in a bruxer — is where the equivalence claim stops applying. [확인 — in-vitro]
2. **Cavity geometry**: in very wide MOD cavities with high C-factor, incremental oblique 2 mm layers reduce bonded:free surface ratio better than a single 4 mm increment. Direct head-to-head evidence is in [[resin/chaple-gil-2026-bulk-fill-incremental-class-ii-sr-nma]] — equivalent — but mechanistic prudence still applies for the most extreme geometries.
3. **Structurally compromised endodontically treated molars are inside the envelope, and don't need reinforcing.** [[resin/metwaly-2024-clinical-performance-polyethylene-fiber-reinforced-resin]] (24-month double-blind RCT, n=240 ETT lower molars, Class II) found **no difference on any modified USPHS parameter** between polyethylene-fiber-reinforced bulk-fill and plain bulk-fill, both holding Alpha for retention, gross fracture, and secondary caries throughout. Two readings matter here: plain bulk-fill performs acceptably in the high-risk ETT indication, and the fiber-reinforcement step buys nothing measurable at 2 years. (Both arms are bulk-fill — this is not an incremental comparison.) [확인] rct

### 2.3 Polymerization shrinkage and "low-shrinkage" composites

[[resin/meereis-2018-polymerization-shrinkage-stress-resin-sr-ma]] (62 in vitro): the largest stress-reduction contribution comes from **resin matrix modification**, not photoinitiator change (p=0.29 NS for photoinitiator subgroup). The clinically deleterious quantity is not volumetric shrinkage itself but **stress at the bonded interface** in a confined cavity — manifesting as cuspal deflection, microleakage, debonding, post-op hypersensitivity. (Monomer chemistry, bulk-fill kinetics, and fiber reinforcement are treated at length in [[overviews/resin-polymerization-shrinkage-stress-overview]]; whether the resin polymerizes adequately at all given light-tip geometry is a separate axis, in [[overviews/resin-light-curing-degree-of-conversion-overview]].)

[[resin/kruly-2018-low-shrinkage-vs-methacrylate-resin-ma]] (21 RCT, up to 60 months) pooled silorane + ormocer + bulk-fill as "low-shrinkage" composites vs. conventional Bis-GMA/methacrylate. **Only finding**: 12-month marginal adaptation **favored conventional methacrylate**. All other outcomes (retention, recurrent caries, sensitivity, discoloration) — no difference. The often-repeated claim "low shrinkage = clinically superior" is **not supported by RCT MA**. [확인]

[[resin/magno-2016-silorane-composite-not-better-conventional-ma]] reaches the same verdict for silorane specifically. Silorane is now largely discontinued — the chemistry didn't deliver clinical superiority justifying the workflow constraints.

[미검증] Practical reading: stress management is real and matters, but contemporary methacrylate composites + sound technique (proper increment thickness, adequate light intensity/duration, soft-start curing, flowable liner where indicated) match anything the modified-monomer families deliver. Money spent on a high-CRI curing light and a radiometer outperforms money spent chasing exotic monomer chemistry.

**A newer mechanistic lever, still bench-only**: [[resin/he-2026-polymerizable-inhibitor-shrinkage-stress]] (in vitro, UDMA/Bis-EMA/TEGDMA experimental composite) targets shrinkage stress through a route Meereis 2018 did not catalogue in these terms — **delaying the gel point** with a polymerizable radical inhibitor (PBHT, a methacrylate-functional BHT analog). At 3 wt% PBHT, max shrinkage-stress rate fell from 47.5 to 13.1 ×10⁻³ MPa·s, while volumetric shrinkage barely moved — reinforcing the same principle Meereis 2018 already established, that **interfacial stress, not raw volumetric shrinkage, is the target**, and that resin-matrix-level intervention (here, an inhibitor bound into the network) is what moves the needle, not photoinitiator chemistry. Notably, plain non-polymerizable BHT (the incumbent commercial inhibitor) **failed to reduce stress** in this UDMA-rich matrix — only the copolymerizing PBHT worked, and it also leached ~4× less inhibitor than BHT (132 vs 549 µg/g at 1 wt%), addressing a real cytotoxicity/elution liability of the current approach. Mechanical properties (FS/FM/fracture toughness) were preserved at the recommended 1 wt% dose.

[미검증] Read this with the same caution as silorane and the other "low-shrinkage" families in this section: it is a **single in-vitro synthesis-and-characterization study** ([근거약함] in-vitro), with no cytotoxicity testing, no aging/wear data, and no clinical or even ex-vivo tooth-restoration performance measured — the authors themselves flag this. The Kruly 2018 RCT MA lesson above is exactly the caution this result needs: 21 pooled RCTs of "low-shrinkage" chemistry (silorane/ormocer/bulk-fill) found **no clinical superiority** despite favorable lab-stress profiles. PBHT's mechanism is more targeted (gel-point delay validated directly on shrinkage stress, not just volumetric shrinkage) and it solves a genuine incumbent problem (BHT leaching/cytotoxicity) rather than chasing a new monomer backbone — so it is mechanistically more promising than another silorane-style reformulation. But until it survives aging, biocompatibility screening, and ideally an RCT, it sits in the same "lab result ≠ clinical superiority" bucket as everything else in this section. File it as a research lead, not a practice change.

### 2.4 Direct vs. indirect composite — for the borderline case

Three SR+MA sit on this question, and none of them favors indirect:

| Source | Studies | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| [[inlay/angeletaki-2016-direct-vs-indirect-inlay-onlay-sr-ma]] | 3 RCT (248 inlays, 176 onlays; to 11 yr) | No failure-risk difference at 5 yr (RR 1.54, p=0.52) or 11 yr (RR 0.95, p=0.92); only 11-yr marginal discoloration favored **direct** (RR 0.41, p=0.04); onlay 5-yr survival 87% |
| [[resin/josic-2023-direct-indirect-resin-longevity-sr-ma]] | 23 included / 8 MA | No significant longevity difference at short/medium/long term; **GRADE very low** |
| [[resin/tennert-2024-direct-vs-indirect-composite-longevity-sr-ma]] | 5 RCT, 627 restorations | Direct RR 0.61 (0.47–0.79) — **direct fails less**; all studies high RoB; evidence certainty very low |

The 2024 Tennert MA is methodologically more stringent (RCT only, ≥3-yr follow-up). Angeletaki 2016 is the longest-horizon entry (11 years) and the only one where the single significant outcome — marginal discoloration — points the same direction as Tennert. All three agree evidence certainty is very low, and the tie-breaker is that **no analysis at any follow-up length has found indirect composite superior**. Note that the "evidence base is thin" here is thin on *both* sides: Angeletaki pooled 3 RCTs total, which is why it declined to recommend either material.

The German S3 guideline reaches the same place as a formal recommendation: [[resin/wolff-2024-direct-composite-restorations-permanent-teeth]] (AWMF 083-028 Part 1, GRADE, 20-society panel) states that in the posterior region **direct composite should be preferred over indirect composite inlays**, and recognizes direct composite for Classes I–V *including cusp-replacement restorations*. That last clause matters: it moves cusp coverage from "automatic indirect indication" to "a case where direct is a defensible option" — narrower than §3.4's anatomy list below implies. [확인] consensus — but note consensus sits at the *bottom* of the evidence ladder; it does not outrank the SR+MA above, it converges with them.

[미검증] For typical Class I/II cavities the direct route is sound and probably superior; the indirect adhesive overlay holds its place for cuspal coverage, large cavities approaching cusp replacement, occlusal vertical dimension rehabilitation, and worn dentition — none of which the meta-analyses can resolve with current data.

### 2.5 Repair beats replacement

[[resin/van-de-sande-2019-composite-repair-anterior-15year]] — 15-year retrospective, 634 anterior restorations. Treating repair as non-failure: **15-yr survival 69%, AFR 2.4%** (Class III/IV); direct veneers 10-yr survival jumps from 38% (repair counted as failure) to **74%** (repair as non-failure). Repair preserves tooth structure, reduces chair time and patient anxiety. [확인 — retrospective, single practice]

The wiki has no posterior repair longevity SR yet — when you have a localized marginal staining, fracture, or secondary caries on an otherwise sound restoration, repair (rebevel/freshen/etch/bond/composite over) is the evidence-supported first choice.

## Part 3 — Integrated Decision Framework

### 3.1 Posterior Class I/II caries — default protocol

This sequence is the wiki's own synthesis, but it can now be checked against a formally voted one: [[resin/sekundo-2024-direct-composite-restorations-permanent-teeth]] (AWMF 083-028 Part 2) put essentially this same sequence through six PICO questions and a NIH Type 1 consensus conference, and every recommendation passed by strong consensus (16–17/0/0). Where it agrees with what follows, that is independent convergence; where it is more cautious, it is flagged inline.

1. **Anesthesia + isolation.** Rubber dam is often described as the one bonding-interface variable nobody disputes. The evidence we hold does not support that framing: [[resin-bonding/miao-2021-rubber-dam-isolation-restorative-treatment]] (Cochrane SR+MA, 6 RCTs, 1342 participants, **all at high risk of bias**) finds rubber dam *may* improve direct composite NCCL survival at 6 months (OR 2.29, 95% CI 1.05–4.99, **low certainty**) with little or no effect detectable at 12 and 18 months (very-low certainty). Sekundo 2024 lands in the same place — both relative and absolute isolation are usable, rubber dam *may* benefit long-term longevity, and most reviews found no rubber-dam-vs-cotton-roll difference. Use rubber dam: the biological rationale is sound, the cost is low, and moisture control is not seriously arguable chairside. But the recommendation rests on plausibility and cheapness, not on demonstrated long-term benefit — state it that way rather than as settled evidence. [확인]
2. **Conservative caries removal**, leaving affected dentin if pulp risk; selective excavation accepted in current cariology consensus — and specifically, Sekundo 2024 recommends **one-stage selective removal** over stepwise/non-selective for dentin lesions close to the pulp.
3. **Pulp protection** only when within ~0.5 mm of pulp (calcium silicate, e.g. Biodentine — see [[resin-bonding/tavangar-2022-calcium-silicate-cement-caries-dentin]]). Do **not** generalize this into routine lining: [[dental-materials/schenkel-2019-dental-cavity-liners-composite-restorations-sr]] (Cochrane SR+MA, 8 RCTs) found no consistent post-op-hypersensitivity benefit and identical 1–2-year failure rates with vs without a liner under Class I/II composite. A liner is justified by a case-specific indication (deep cavity, dentin sealing, blocking undercuts) — not by default. [확인] sr+ma
4. **Matrix** — for Class II, an anatomically preformed sectional matrix + ring + wedge is the guideline-preferred system for proximal contact and marginal ridge (Sekundo 2024 Rec 4).
5. **Adhesion** — selective enamel etch (32–37% H3PO4, 15 s, rinse 10 s) + universal adhesive on dentin (scrubbing 20 s, gentle air-thin, light-cure 10 s).
   - Optional: 2% CHX 60 s before adhesive application (MMP inhibition; in vitro support, clinical neutral).
6. **Placement** — either incremental (2 mm oblique layers, light-cure each) or bulk-fill (≤4 mm in single increment per IFU). Both supported; Sekundo 2024 Statement 2 likewise holds bulk-fill safe to 4 mm **conditional on an adequate-power curing unit**. Cap any bulk-fill with a conventional sculptable layer on the load-bearing occlusal surface — see §2.2 caveat 1, which applies to sculptable bulk-fills too, not only flowables.
7. **Light-cure** — adequate intensity (≥800 mW/cm², radiometer-verified), full IFU time (typically 20 s per increment per shade), through opaquer/dentin layer where deepest. Sekundo 2024 could not grade this formally (evidence too poor) but states curing is decisive, governed by handling, delivered energy (power × time), and shade/opacity — the geometry side of which is worked out in [[overviews/resin-light-curing-degree-of-conversion-overview]].
8. **Occlusion + polish** — articulating paper static + excursive, polish per manufacturer system. Polishing every restoration is a strong-consensus guideline statement (Sekundo 2024 Statement 3), though it too rests on only two studies rather than trial evidence.

### 3.2 NCCL (cervical erosion/abfraction/abrasion)

- Lesion entirely in dentin or with thin/sclerotic enamel.
- **Universal adhesive in either selective-enamel-etch or full E&R mode** — both validated.
- **Pure SE mode → avoid** when enamel margin exists ([[resin-bonding/oza-2022-universal-adhesive-nccl-rct-2year]]); at 4 years the un-etched arm falls below its own baseline retention ([[resin-bonding/omoto-2025-nccl-adhesion-strategies-4year-rct]]). Between the two *etched* protocols there is nothing to choose at 3 years ([[resin-bonding/peumans-2023-universal-adhesive-nccl-3year-rct]]).
- Flowable composite acceptable for thin lesion; sculptable for bulk.
- Sclerotic dentin is harder to bond — longer etch (or extended adhesive application) reasonable.

### 3.3 Existing restoration with localized defect

Repair, don't replace, unless the defect compromises the bulk of the restoration. Refresh margin with diamond, microair-abrade or roughen, etch (silane if old composite filler exposed and ceramic-containing), bond, layer fresh composite ([[resin/van-de-sande-2019-composite-repair-anterior-15year]]).

### 3.4 When to escalate to indirect

Not from evidence — from anatomy:
- Cusp coverage required (cracked tooth, undermined cusp).
- Cavity area approaching ½ intercuspal distance.
- Endo-treated posterior tooth with marginal-ridge loss + heavy occlusal load.
- Aesthetic anterior cases (veneer indication).

The direct/indirect SR+MA evidence ([[inlay/angeletaki-2016-direct-vs-indirect-inlay-onlay-sr-ma]], [[resin/josic-2023-direct-indirect-resin-longevity-sr-ma]], [[resin/tennert-2024-direct-vs-indirect-composite-longevity-sr-ma]]) does **not** mandate indirect for routine Class II.

[미검증] Two of these anatomic triggers are softer than the list makes them look. [[resin/wolff-2024-direct-composite-restorations-permanent-teeth]] explicitly recognizes direct composite **for cusp-replacement restorations**, so "cusp coverage required" is an argument for considering indirect, not a boundary that rules direct out. And for the endo-treated posterior molar, [[resin/metwaly-2024-clinical-performance-polyethylene-fiber-reinforced-resin]] kept 240 ETT lower molars at Alpha for retention and gross fracture on plain bulk-fill through 24 months — short follow-up, moderate remaining structure, so it does not license direct restoration of a gutted molar, but it does mean "endo-treated" alone is not the trigger. The real trigger in both rows is remaining tooth structure and occlusal load, which no meta-analysis in our wiki has operationalized into a threshold.

## Evidence Gaps (open questions in our wiki)

- **Clinical RCT for CHX/MMP inhibition** improving 5+ year composite longevity — in vitro consistent, clinical absent.
- **Posterior composite repair longevity SR** — anterior data strong, posterior data fragmentary in our wiki.
- **Universal adhesive head-to-head products** at 5+ yr. *(Partially closed — the wiki now holds a 3-yr RCT ([[resin-bonding/peumans-2023-universal-adhesive-nccl-3year-rct]]) and a 4-yr RCT ([[resin-bonding/omoto-2025-nccl-adhesion-strategies-4year-rct]]); both are single-product, NCCL-only, and stop short of 5 years. What is still missing is a multi-product head-to-head at 5+ yr on **carious** cavities rather than cervical lesions.)*
- **Bulk-fill wear at clinical time-scales** — the equivalence RCTs stop at 24 months, while [[resin/osiewicz-2022-wear-of-bulk-fill-resin]] shows the wear liability is intrinsic and only masked by a cover layer. No clinical wear study ≥5 yr in our wiki resolves whether capped bulk-fill stays equivalent.
- **Curing-mode interaction with placement** beyond the dual-cure note in [[resin-bonding/li-2025-curing-modes-dual-cure-dentin]] and the geometry work in [[overviews/resin-light-curing-degree-of-conversion-overview]].
- **Deep margin elevation (DME) / cervical margin relocation (CMR)** — *no longer a gap*: [[resin-bonding/elhaddad-2026-cervical-margin-relocation-periodontal-restorative]] (SR+MA, 2026) pools periodontal and restorative outcomes and finds CMR does not harm periodontal health (probing depth MD −0.96 mm, I²=0%; BOP MD −0.52), with marginal adaptation driven by bonding strategy and material. Applied context is in [[overviews/restorative-margin-periodontal-interface-overview]]. The residual gap is DME **under a direct** restoration specifically — the pooled data lean on adhesively bonded glass-ceramic.

When a clinical decision falls into one of these gaps, the wiki cannot answer — say so and bring the PDF.
