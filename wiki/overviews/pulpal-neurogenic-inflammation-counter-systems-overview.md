## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: 치수에는 **자기 자신의 신경성 염증을 끄는 내인성 제동장치**가 두 벌 있다 — 교감신경 유래 신경펩타이드 Y (Neuropeptide Y, NPY)와 내인성 오피오이드계. 치수의 운명은 흥분계(물질 P (Substance P, SP)·칼시토닌유전자관련펩타이드 (Calcitonin Gene-Related Peptide, CGRP))와 이 제동계의 **균형**이 결정한다. 논문 6편(SR 1·서술적 리뷰 4·in vitro 1) 종합. [확인]
> - **가장 임상적으로 중요한 귀결: 브레이크는 조용히 고장난다.** 두 가지 서로 다른 경로로 — ① 진행된 우식에서 교감신경 섬유 자체가 파괴되면서 NPY/Y1 발현이 **붕괴**하고(Caviedes-Bucheli 2026, 92편), ② 과도한 교정력이 오피오이드 항침해 용량을 **압도**한다(Caviedes-Bucheli 2025, 38편). 둘 다 결과가 **무증상 비가역성 치수염 또는 괴사**다. 통증이 없다는 것이 치수가 건강하다는 뜻이 아니다. [확인]
> - 치수 신경펩타이드 5종 지도(토대): SP·CGRP·뉴로키닌 A (Neurokinin A, NKA)·NPY·혈관작용장펩타이드 (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide, VIP)가 각자 수용체를 통해 신경성 염증·혈관 조절·면역세포 crosstalk를 지배한다. 시술과 광범위 치수/상아질 손상이 이들을 방출시키고, 연쇄 작용이 치수 괴사와 치근단 병소 확대로 이어진다 — NK1·NK2·CGRP 수용체 길항제가 약리 표적(Caviedes-Bucheli 2008). [확인]
> - **NPY = 억제 축의 주역**: 교감신경 혈관주위 섬유에서 방출되어 Gi/Go 연결 Y1/Y2 수용체를 통해 SP/CGRP 구동 신경성 염증을 억제하고, 통증수용체 TRPV1을 억제하며, 골형성단백질-2 (Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2, BMP-2)를 통해 **3차 상아질 형성**을 유도한다. 즉 NPY는 염증을 끄면서 동시에 방어벽을 쌓는다. [확인]
> - **NPY의 시간 곡선이 결정적**: NPY/Y1 발현은 경도–중등도 우식에서 **정점**을 찍고 진행 우식에서 교감신경 섬유가 파괴되며 **붕괴**한다. 제동장치가 가장 필요한 시점에 사라진다 — 이것이 진행 우식에서 치수 예후가 급격히 나빠지는 신경생물학적 설명이다(Caviedes-Bucheli 2026). [확인]
> - **오피오이드 = 두 번째 제동 축**: β-엔도르핀·엔케팔린·다이놀핀·소마토스타틴 (Somatostatin, SST)·엔도카나비노이드가 MOR/DOR/KOR 수용체와 항염증 사이토카인(IL-10, TGF-β)을 통해 SP/CGRP를 억누른다. **중등도 교정력(56–180 g)은 보상되지만 >180 g는 항침해 용량을 압도한다**(Caviedes-Bucheli 2025). [확인]
> - **교정 임상 수치(바로 쓸 수 있음)**: 재활성화 간격 **약 40–50일**이 오피오이드계 회복을 허용하고, **180 g 초과 힘은 무증상 비가역성 치수염·괴사 위험**을 만든다. 교정 중 치수 사고가 "왜 아프다는 말이 없었는데"로 끝나는 이유. [확인]
> - **감작(sensitization) 축 — 비신경세포도 증폭한다**: 말초에서 삼차신경절 위성교세포 (Satellite Glial Cells, SGC)가 코넥신 매개 간극결합·PGE2/Nav1.7 상향·IL-1β로 뉴런 흥분성을 증폭하고, 중추에서 미세아교세포·별아교세포가 글루타민합성효소·p38MAPK·TNF-α로 감작을 유지한다(Ye 2021). 교세포 표적 치료(코넥신43 억제·플루오로시트르산·토나베르사트)는 전임상 유망, 단 **이부딜라스트는 만성 편두통 임상시험에서 실패**. [확인]
> - **감작의 임상 얼굴 — 열 이질통 (Thermal Allodynia)**: 실외 한랭 → 실내 온난의 반복적 온도 순환이 상아세관액을 외향 이동시키고(냉→온 순서에서 유속 140% 증가), C-섬유 표현형을 바꾸고, 신경성장인자 (Nerve Growth Factor, NGF) 상향으로 **TRPV1 역치를 무해한 온난 범위까지 낮춰** 지연성·미만성·정중선 교차 통증을 만든다. 여성 3명(49–55세) 증례이며 **표준 상아질 지각과민 치료에 반응하지 않았다** — 관리는 세관 폐쇄가 아니라 **치수 열 절연**(비니어·마우스가드)(Le Fur-Bonnabesse 2017). [확인, 증례 수준]
> - **약리 표적 하나가 구체화됨**: 로즈마리·세이지 유래 디테르페노이드 **카르노솔 (Carnosol)**이 LPS 자극 사람 치수세포에서 IL-1β·IL-6·TNF-α를 용량의존적으로 억제하고 흰쥐 치수염 모델에서 조직학적 염증을 줄인다. 기전은 **RAGE/NF-κB 축** — RAGE 전사 하향, NF-κB p65 인산화·핵이동 감소, **RAGE siRNA 넉다운 시 효과 소실**(= RAGE가 필수 상류 매개체)(Liu 2025). 생활치수치료의 약리 보조 후보. [확인, in vitro + 동물]

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of six papers (1 systematic synthesis of 92 studies, 4 narrative reviews, 1 in vitro/animal study) organized around a single mechanism: the dental pulp carries **two endogenous brake systems on its own neurogenic inflammation** — sympathetically-derived neuropeptide Y (NPY) acting through Gi/Go-coupled Y1/Y2 receptors, and the endogenous opioid system (β-endorphin, enkephalins, dynorphin, somatostatin, endocannabinoids) acting through MOR/DOR/KOR plus IL-10 and TGF-β — and pulpal fate is set by the balance between these brakes and the excitatory axis of substance P (SP) and calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP), first mapped alongside neurokinin A and vasoactive intestinal peptide by Caviedes-Bucheli 2008.

The clinically decisive property is that **both brakes fail silently, by two different routes**. NPY suppresses SP/CGRP-driven inflammation, inhibits TRPV1 nociceptors, and drives tertiary dentinogenesis via BMP-2 — but NPY/Y1 expression peaks in mild-to-moderate caries and then collapses in advanced lesions as the sympathetic fibres carrying it are destroyed, removing the brake precisely when it is most needed (Caviedes-Bucheli 2026, 92 studies). In parallel, moderate orthodontic forces of 56–180 g are compensated by the opioid counter-system while forces above 180 g overwhelm antinociceptive capacity, with reactivation intervals of roughly 40–50 days allowing recovery; exceeding that threshold risks **asymptomatic irreversible pulpitis or necrosis** (Caviedes-Bucheli 2025, 38 studies). Absence of pain is therefore not evidence of pulpal health.

Sensitization is amplified by non-neuronal cells and has a recognizable clinical face. Satellite glial cells in the trigeminal ganglion raise neuronal excitability through connexin-mediated gap junctions, PGE2/Nav1.7 upregulation, and IL-1β, while central microglia and astrocytes sustain it via glutamine synthetase, p38MAPK, and TNF-α (Ye 2021). Clinically, repeated cold-to-warm ambient cycling can shift dentinal fluid outward (140% greater velocity in cold→warm sequences), alter C-fibre phenotype, and lower the TRPV1 threshold into the innocuous warm range, producing delayed, diffuse, midline-crossing pain unresponsive to standard desensitizing therapy and manageable only by pulpal thermal insulation rather than tubule occlusion (Le Fur-Bonnabesse 2017, 3 cases). One druggable node has been characterized: carnosol suppresses IL-1β/IL-6/TNF-α in LPS-stimulated human dental pulp cells through the RAGE/NF-κB axis, with RAGE siRNA knockdown abolishing the effect (Liu 2025).

## 세줄요약

논문 6편(92편 종합 1·서술적 리뷰 4·in vitro/동물 1) 종합 — 하나의 기전을 축으로: 치수는 **자기 신경성 염증을 끄는 내인성 제동장치를 두 벌** 가진다. 교감신경 유래 신경펩타이드 Y (Neuropeptide Y, NPY)가 Gi/Go 연결 Y1/Y2 수용체로 작동하고, 내인성 오피오이드계(β-엔도르핀·엔케팔린·다이놀핀·소마토스타틴·엔도카나비노이드)가 MOR/DOR/KOR 수용체와 IL-10·TGF-β로 작동하며, 치수의 운명은 이 제동계와 흥분 축(물질 P (Substance P, SP)·칼시토닌유전자관련펩타이드 (Calcitonin Gene-Related Peptide, CGRP))의 균형이 정한다 — 뉴로키닌 A·혈관작용장펩타이드를 포함한 5종 지도는 Caviedes-Bucheli 2008이 토대를 놓았다.

임상적으로 결정적인 성질은 **두 제동장치가 서로 다른 경로로 조용히 고장난다**는 것이다. NPY는 SP/CGRP 염증을 억제하고 통증수용체 TRPV1을 억제하며 골형성단백질-2 (BMP-2)로 3차 상아질을 만들지만, NPY/Y1 발현은 경도–중등도 우식에서 정점을 찍은 뒤 **진행 우식에서 교감신경 섬유가 파괴되며 붕괴**한다 — 가장 필요한 시점에 브레이크가 사라진다(Caviedes-Bucheli 2026, 92편). 한편 교정력 56–180 g은 오피오이드 대응계가 보상하지만 **180 g 초과는 항침해 용량을 압도**하고, 재활성화 간격 약 40–50일이 회복을 허용하며, 역치를 넘기면 **무증상 비가역성 치수염·괴사** 위험이 생긴다(Caviedes-Bucheli 2025, 38편). 통증이 없다는 것은 치수가 건강하다는 근거가 아니다.

감작은 비신경세포가 증폭하며 알아볼 수 있는 임상 얼굴을 가진다: 삼차신경절 위성교세포 (Satellite Glial Cells)가 코넥신 간극결합·PGE2/Nav1.7·IL-1β로 흥분성을 올리고 중추 미세아교세포·별아교세포가 글루타민합성효소·p38MAPK·TNF-α로 이를 유지한다(Ye 2021). 임상적으로는 한랭→온난 반복 순환이 상아세관액을 외향 이동시키고(냉→온에서 유속 140% 증가) C-섬유 표현형을 바꿔 **TRPV1 역치를 무해한 온난 범위로 낮춰** 지연성·미만성·정중선 교차 통증을 만들며, 표준 지각과민 치료에 반응하지 않고 **세관 폐쇄가 아니라 치수 열 절연**으로만 관리된다(Le Fur-Bonnabesse 2017, 3증례). 약리 표적으로는 카르노솔 (Carnosol)이 RAGE/NF-κB 축을 통해 사람 치수세포의 IL-1β·IL-6·TNF-α를 억제하며 RAGE siRNA 넉다운 시 효과가 소실된다(Liu 2025).

## Summary

Pulpal inflammation is usually taught as a bacterial and immunological story. These six papers tell the neurogenic half: the pulp's own sensory and autonomic nerves are not passive reporters of damage but active participants that can both drive and suppress inflammation, and the direction of that balance determines whether a pulp survives.

Key propositions:

1. **Five neuropeptides constitute the pulp's neurogenic machinery.** [확인] SP, CGRP, NKA, NPY, and VIP govern neurogenic inflammation, vascular regulation, and immune-cell crosstalk through specific receptors. Release during dental procedures and extensive pulp/dentin injury drives plasticity changes whose cascading action can produce pulp necrosis and periapical lesion growth. NK1, NK2, and CGRP-receptor antagonists are the pharmacologic targets this framework predicts (Caviedes-Bucheli 2008).
2. **NPY is the pulp's principal inhibitory neuropeptide, and it is anabolic as well as anti-inflammatory.** [확인] Released from sympathetic perivascular fibres, NPY acts via Gi/Go-coupled Y1/Y2 receptors to suppress SP/CGRP-driven neurogenic inflammation, inhibit TRPV1 nociceptors, and drive tertiary dentinogenesis through BMP-2 (Caviedes-Bucheli 2026). It simultaneously dampens the fire and builds the wall.
3. **The NPY brake collapses exactly when it is most needed.** [확인] NPY/Y1 expression peaks in mild-to-moderate caries and then falls away in advanced lesions as the sympathetic fibres are destroyed. This supplies a neurobiological account of why pulpal prognosis deteriorates sharply with lesion progression, and why the biological case for vital pulp therapy is strongest in the earlier window.
4. **A second, independent brake is the endogenous opioid counter-system.** [확인] β-endorphin, enkephalins, dynorphin, somatostatin, and endocannabinoids act through MOR/DOR/KOR receptors and anti-inflammatory cytokines (IL-10, TGF-β) to suppress SP/CGRP-driven neurogenic inflammation (Caviedes-Bucheli 2025, 38 of 819 screened studies).
5. **Orthodontic force has a quantified threshold at which the opioid brake fails.** [확인] Moderate forces of 56–180 g are compensated; forces above 180 g overwhelm antinociceptive capacity. Reactivation intervals of approximately 40–50 days allow the opioid system to recover. Exceeding these parameters risks asymptomatic irreversible pulpitis or necrosis.
6. **Both failure modes are silent — which is the single most actionable finding here.** [확인] In advanced caries the reporting fibres themselves are destroyed; under excessive orthodontic force the antinociceptive system is saturated rather than the injury being absent. In both cases the pulp can be undergoing irreversible change without generating symptoms. Symptom absence must not be used as evidence of pulpal health in these two contexts.
7. **Non-neuronal glia amplify and maintain orofacial sensitization.** [확인] Peripherally, satellite glial cells in the trigeminal ganglion amplify neuronal excitability via connexin-mediated gap junctions, PGE2/Nav1.7 upregulation, and IL-1β release; centrally, microglia and astrocytes sustain sensitization in the trigeminal nucleus through glutamine synthetase, p38MAPK, and TNF-α (Ye 2021). Glial-targeted agents (connexin 43 inhibition, fluorocitrate, tonabersat) are preclinically promising, but ibudilast failed in chronic migraine — the translational record is not yet positive.
8. **Thermal allodynia is a distinct clinical entity from dentin hypersensitivity and requires a different treatment logic.** [확인, 증례 수준] Repeated ambient cold-to-warm cycling shifts dentinal fluid outward (140% greater velocity in cold→warm sequences), induces C-fibre phenotypic change, lowers the TRPV1 activation threshold into the innocuous warm range via peripheral sensitization and NGF upregulation, and drives central sensitization producing diffuse, midline-crossing pain. The three reported patients (women aged 49–55) were unresponsive to standard DH treatment. Management targets pulpal thermal insulation — veneers, mouthguards — rather than tubule occlusion (Le Fur-Bonnabesse 2017).
9. **RAGE/NF-κB is a characterized, druggable node in pulpitis.** [확인, in vitro + 동물] Carnosol dose-dependently suppressed IL-1β, IL-6, and TNF-α in LPS-stimulated human dental pulp cells and reduced histological inflammation in a rat pulpitis model, acting by downregulating RAGE transcriptionally and reducing NF-κB p65 phosphorylation and nuclear translocation; RAGE siRNA knockdown abolished the benefit, establishing RAGE as the obligate upstream mediator (Liu 2025).

The through-line: excitation (SP/CGRP), inhibition (NPY, opioids), amplification (glia), and a druggable inflammatory node (RAGE/NF-κB) describe one regulatory system rather than four topics. Its clinical signature is that failure presents as silence.

## Clinical Decision Rules

1. **Do not treat symptom absence as evidence of pulpal health in advanced caries or under orthodontic load.** Both contexts have a documented mechanism for silent irreversible change — destroyed sympathetic fibres in one, saturated antinociception in the other.
2. **Keep orthodontic forces at or below 180 g and reactivation intervals near 40–50 days.** These are the specific numbers the opioid counter-system literature supplies; treat them as mechanistically-derived guidance, not as validated clinical thresholds.
3. **Treat the early-caries window as the biologically favourable moment for vital pulp therapy.** NPY-mediated inhibition and BMP-2-driven tertiary dentinogenesis are intact in mild-to-moderate lesions and lost in advanced ones.
4. **When thermal pain does not respond to desensitizing therapy, stop repeating tubule occlusion.** Reframe as thermal allodynia with peripheral and central sensitization, and manage by insulating the pulp thermally.
5. **Expect the pharmacological future in this area to target receptors and transcription, not the bacteria** — NK1/NK2/CGRP-R antagonism, RAGE/NF-κB inhibition, and glial modulation are the three named avenues, none of which is clinically available for pulpal indications today.

## Evidence Limits

- **No clinical trials.** None of the six papers is an RCT in the pulpal indication. The NPY synthesis, despite covering 92 studies, is built largely on immunohistochemistry with no human in vivo functional validation; the opioid and glial papers are narrative reviews.
- **The orthodontic numbers deserve caution.** The 56–180 g compensation range, the >180 g threshold, and the 40–50 day reactivation interval come from a narrative review of 38 studies, not from a trial designed to test them. They are the best available guidance but carry [미검증] status as clinical protocol values.
- **The thermal allodynia entity rests on three patients.** The proposed four-step mechanism is coherent and the treatment implication is actionable, but the evidence base is a case series.
- **Carnosol is preclinical.** In vitro human pulp cells plus a rat model, with no human data on dosing, delivery, or safety in a vital pulp therapy context.
- **The glial translational record is mixed, not positive.** Preclinical promise for connexin 43 inhibition and tonabersat sits alongside ibudilast's clinical failure in chronic migraine — a caution against assuming glial targets will translate.
- **Combining the two brake systems into one framework is this page's synthesis, not a claim any single paper makes.** The NPY and opioid literatures are separate; reading them as parallel counter-systems is an inference [미검증], though both papers share an author group and a common SP/CGRP reference axis.
