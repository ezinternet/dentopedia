## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **결론**: 방사선 사진 (Radiograph, X-ray) **한 장만으로는** 세 가지 실패 기전(① 드릴링 과열에 의한 열괴사 (Thermal Necrosis), ② 과도한 골압박 (Overcompression), ③ 임플란트 주위염 (Peri-implantitis))을 감별할 수 **없다**. 위키가 가진 근거로 쓸 수 있는 감별 도구는 **시점 (Timing)** + **임상 탐침 소견 (탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)·화농 (Suppuration)·탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD))** + **기준 방사선 사진 (Baseline Radiograph)과의 비교**다.
> - **① 과열 — X-ray 특이 소견 없음**: 보유 논문 중 임플란트 열괴사의 방사선 소견을 기술한 논문은 **한 편도 없다**. Möhlhenrich 2015 체계적 고찰 (Systematic Review, SR)은 드릴링 중 온도 28.4–64.4°C를 정리했지만 대부분 in vitro 비생체 소·돼지 골이고 임상 방사선 데이터가 없다. 열괴사 임계값 47°C/1분 (Bertollo 2011).
> - **과열의 생물학적 결과는 확인됨 (영상은 아님)**: 미니피그 in vivo에서 골내 최고온도 46.5°C vs 35.4°C → 2주 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 31.3% vs 44.9% (Heuzeroth 2021) — 조직형태계측이지 방사선 소견이 아니다.
> - **② 과압박 — 동물 µCT에서 "분화구 모양 병소 (crater-like lesion)"**: 쥐 발치와 고미스핏 (misfit) 모델에서 미세골절 → 광범위 골세포 (Osteocyte) 사멸 → 흡수만 있고 형성 없음 (TRAP·카텝신 K 양성, ALP·제1형 교원질 음성) → 섬유성·염증성 육아조직으로 찬 분화구형 조기 실패 (Coyac 2019). **단 쥐·초록 전용·수치 전무**이며, 영상은 임상 치근단 사진이 아니라 마이크로 CT (µCT)다.
> - **"고토크 = 괴사"는 아니다**: 양 하악 110 Ncm에서 골괴사 없음·골형성 증가 (Trisi 2011); 사람 최대 176 Ncm에서도 1년 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 유의차 없음 (Khayat 2011); SR+메타분석 (SR+MA)에서 ≥50 Ncm이 생존율 (RR 0.51, NS)·MBL (MD 0.15 mm, NS)에 영향 없음 (Lemos 2020). 과압박 손상은 **토크 수치가 아니라 골상 (bone bed)·미스핏·임플란트 형상**에 달려 있다 (기계가공 표면이 인공골 균열 유발, Nascimento 2024).
> - **반대편 신호**: 3년 RCT에서 ≥50 Ncm → MBL↑·협측 점막 퇴축↑, 누적 성공률 91.3% vs 98.2%, 하악에서 두드러짐 (Marconcini 2018); 주제범위 고찰 (Scoping Review)도 하악 MBL 1.53 vs 1.03 mm, 실패는 D1·D4 골에 집중 (Manfredini 2025 — 수치는 사실상 단일 RCT 유래). 즉 사람에서 과압박 의심 소견은 "조기 탈락"이 아니라 **수년에 걸친 변연골 (crestal) 소실**로도 나타날 수 있다.
> - **③ 임플란트 주위염 — X-ray 단독 진단 불가 (정의상)**: 2017 World Workshop 합의 (Berglundh 2018)는 **BOP ± 화농 + PD 증가 + 진행성 골소실**을 모두 요구한다. 방사선 골소실만 있고 BOP/화농이 없으면 주위염 진단이 성립하지 않는다. 보철 장착 시 기준 방사선 사진·탐침 기록을 권고.
> - **기준 사진이 없을 때**: 보유 임상연구들이 쓰는 조작적 기준은 BOP/화농 + PD ≥6 mm + 골소실 ≥3 mm (Galarraga-Vinueza 2020, Cafferata 2025, Sbricoli 2026 — EFP 기준 인용).
> - **생리적 리모델링 구분**: 초기 골 리모델링 최대 1.5 mm는 생리적일 수 있고, 안정 기준은 PD <5 mm + BOP/화농 없음 + 기준 대비 골소실 <2 mm (Ramseier 2024). 식립 7일째 안정성 저하는 병리가 아닌 리모델링이며 ISQ로도 안 잡힌다 (Trisi 2011).
> - **형태 중복 함정**: 과압박 동물 모델의 "분화구형 병소"와 임플란트 주위염 수술 RCT의 "분화구형/환상형 골내결손 (crater-like/circumferential intrabony defect)" (Monje 2025, Park 2025)은 **같은 모양 단어**다. 결손 모양으로 기전을 가르면 안 된다.
> - **임상 감별 순서 (위키 근거 조합, 직접 검증된 알고리즘 아님)**: (1) 실패 시점 — 보철 로딩 전이면 조기 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF; 임플란트 단위 약 1–4%) → ①/② 영역, 로딩 후 진행성이면 ③ 우선 의심; (2) BOP/화농/PD 확인 — 양성이면 ③ 요건 충족 가능; (3) 기준 사진과 비교 — 진행성인지; (4) 수술 기록 (드릴 마모·관주·가이드 슬리브·골질·최종 토크·골삭제 직경) — ①과 ②를 가르는 유일한 근거는 **영상이 아니라 수술 기록**.
> - **①과 ②는 영상으로 서로 구별 불가**: 둘 다 조기·골유착 실패이며, 위키에 ①의 방사선 특이 징후는 없다. 단 조직학적으로는 구별 가능: ① 과열 = 골 격리편·재생 없음·혈병 미조직화 (Piattelli 1998); ② 기계적 지연 = 섬유성 피막 + 세균 없음 (Piattelli 2003). 둘이 한 수술에서 겹칠 수 있다 (치밀골 D1은 발열도 크고 압박도 크다).
> - **새 논문 — 열괴사 인간 조직학 지문 (Piattelli 1998, n=8)**: 6개 소견 전 증례 일치 — 골 격리편 (Bone Sequestra), 재생 없음, 염증 간극 (Inflammatory Gap), 혈병 미조직화 (No Clot Organization), 치밀 성숙골 (Compact Mature Bone), 2차 세균 (Secondary Bacterial Colonization). 세균은 결과이지 원인 아님. 치밀 성숙골은 방열 어려운 고밀도 부위임을 시사.
> - **새 논문 — 지연 기계적 실패 인간 조직학 종착점 (Piattelli 2003, n=51)**: 동요도 (Mobility)로 제거된 최대 인간 회수임플란트 시리즈 — 전 증례에서 섬유성 피막 (Fibrous Encapsulation) 600–1,100 µm + 치밀 성숙 주위골 + 골개조 희박 + **세균 없음**. ①열괴사 (골격리편·재생 없음)·③임플란트주위염 (골용해·세균·탐침 깊어짐)과 조직학적으로 구별됨 — 기계적 과부하의 독립적 종착점.
> - **새 논문 — PIBN 두 경로의 조직상 수렴 (Abukraa 2025, n=5명 10개)**: 과압박 조기 3건 + 임플란트주위염 지연 2건. 조직검사 2건(각 경로 1건) → 두 기전 모두 **동일 패턴**: 빈 골세포소강 (Empty Osteocyte Lacunae) + 비생존 골편 + 급·만성 염증세포 침윤. **조직학으로도 감별 불가 — 시점이 유일한 감별 변수** (1개월 이내 = 과압박, 1년 이후 = 임플란트주위염 속발).
> - **Gap (업데이트)**: 세 기전을 동시에 비교한 논문 0편. 과열의 방사선 징후 0편. 과압박의 사람 조직학 — **부분 해소**: Abukraa 2025가 첫 인간 조직검사 확인 증례 제공(과압박 1건 조직학), 단 n=1 조직검사·초록 전용. 이 페이지는 여전히 추론적 조합이다.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 20 held papers/overviews across three failure-mechanism threads — drilling heat (Möhlhenrich 2015 SR, Heuzeroth 2021 minipig, Bertollo 2011, Kosior 2025 SR, Piattelli 1998 human histology), mechanical overcompression (Coyac 2019 rat, Trisi 2011 sheep, Nascimento 2024 bench, Khayat 2011 prospective, Lemos 2020 SR+MA, Marconcini 2018 RCT, Manfredini 2025 scoping review, Piattelli 2003 human retrieved-implant histology, Abukraa 2025 human PIBN case series) and peri-implantitis case definition (Berglundh 2018 consensus, Ramseier 2024) — asking whether X-ray findings alone separate the three.

They do not: no held paper reports a radiographic sign specific to thermal necrosis (heat evidence is in vitro temperature 28.4–64.4°C and animal BIC 31.3% vs 44.9%; the Piattelli 1998 human histological fingerprint of 6 features has no radiographic equivalent), overcompression's "crater-like lesion" is rat µCT from an abstract with no numbers and shares its shape vocabulary with peri-implantitis intrabony defects, and the first human biopsy data (Abukraa 2025) confirm that overcompression and peri-implantitis produce identical histological endpoints — empty osteocyte lacunae + nonvital bone + inflammation — making timing the only clinical differentiator.

The usable differential is timing (pre-loading early failure ≈1–4% implant-level vs post-loading progression) plus clinical probing plus a baseline radiograph, with the surgical record as the only discriminator between heat and compression; no held paper compares all three mechanisms, so this framework is reasoned combination, not a validated diagnostic test.

## 세줄요약

드릴링 발열 (Möhlhenrich 2015 체계적 고찰 (Systematic Review, SR)·Heuzeroth 2021 미니피그·Bertollo 2011·Kosior 2025 SR·Piattelli 1998 인간 조직학), 기계적 과압박 (Overcompression; Coyac 2019 쥐·Trisi 2011 양·Nascimento 2024 벤치·Khayat 2011 전향적·Lemos 2020 SR+메타분석 (SR+MA)·Marconcini 2018 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)·Manfredini 2025 주제범위 고찰·Piattelli 2003 인간 회수임플란트·Abukraa 2025 임플란트 주변 골 괴사 (Peri-Implant Bone Necrosis, PIBN) 증례군), 임플란트 주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 증례 정의 (Berglundh 2018 합의·Ramseier 2024)의 세 갈래 근거 20편으로 방사선 사진만으로 세 기전 감별이 가능한지 검토.

불가능하다: 열괴사 (Thermal Necrosis)의 방사선 특이 징후를 보고한 보유 논문은 없고(근거는 in vitro 온도 28.4–64.4°C와 동물 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 31.3% vs 44.9%뿐), 과압박의 "분화구형 병소"는 수치 없는 쥐 마이크로 CT (µCT) 초록이며 주위염 골내결손과 같은 형태 용어를 공유하고, 주위염은 정의상 진행성 골소실 외에 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP) ± 화농 (Suppuration) + 탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 증가를 요구한다.

실제 감별 도구는 시점(로딩 전 조기 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF) 임플란트 단위 ≈1–4% vs 로딩 후 진행) + 임상 탐침 + 기준 방사선 사진 (Baseline Radiograph)이고, 발열과 압박을 가르는 유일한 근거는 수술 기록이다 — 세 기전을 함께 비교한 논문이 없어 이 틀은 검증된 진단검사가 아닌 추론적 조합이다.

## Overview Scope and Question

**Clinical question:** When a dental implant fails, can the radiograph (periapical or panoramic, as routinely available chairside) distinguish between three proposed mechanisms?

1. **Drilling overheating** — thermal necrosis of the osteotomy wall from heat generated during site preparation.
2. **Excessive bone compression (overcompression)** — mechanical injury of the peri-implant bone from insertion strain, misfit, or undersized osteotomy (high insertion torque as its common proxy).
3. **Peri-implantitis** — plaque-associated inflammatory destruction of supporting bone after osseointegration.

**Scope boundaries.** Mechanisms 1 and 2 are *osteotomy/insertion-mediated* and act at surgery; mechanism 3 is *biofilm-mediated* and acts after the implant is in function. Other failure causes (overload, systemic host factors, smoking, infection of the socket, malposition) are outside the question and are covered in [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]] and [[overviews/implant-failure-mbl-risk-factors-overview]]. "Radiographic finding" here means what a clinician can read on a clinical radiograph — µCT and histology from animal models are reported as mechanism evidence, not as clinical imaging signs.

**Answer in one sentence.** The radiograph documents *that* bone was lost and *how much*, but in the held evidence it does not encode *why*; the mechanism is assigned by timing, clinical probing, and the surgical record.

## Mechanism 1: Drilling Overheating (Thermal Necrosis)

**What the wiki holds.**

- **Thresholds.** The classical time–temperature thresholds — 47 °C for 1 min reduces bone regenerative capacity, 50 °C for 30 s causes cellular necrosis, 56 °C frank osteonecrosis; uncooled cortical drilling routinely exceeds 100 °C — come from an orthopaedic engineering chapter synthesizing mostly non-dental drilling data ([[oral-surgery/bertollo-2011-drilling-bone-practicality-limitations-complications]]).
- **Measured temperatures.** The earliest held SR on implant osteotomy heat reports 28.4–64.4 °C across 27 studies, with drill wear after 50 sites raising temperature significantly; nearly all included work is in vitro in non-vital bovine/porcine bone, meta-analysis was not possible, and **no clinical outcome or radiographic data are reported** ([[implants/osteotomy-thermal/mohlhenrich-2015-heat-generation-drill-wear-dental]]).
- **Biological consequence in vivo.** In minipig mandible, a control drilling protocol whose peak-temperature CI crossed 47 °C (46.5 °C) produced lower BIC than an optimized protocol (35.4 °C): 31.3% vs 44.9% at 2 weeks (p<.0001), 66.2% vs 73.7% at 8 weeks (p=.0455); industry-affiliated authorship ([[implants/osteotomy-thermal/heuzeroth-2021-thermal-exposure-osteotomy-osseointegration]]). This shows heat reduces the quality of osseointegration — histomorphometrically, not radiographically, and without implant loss as the endpoint.
- **Histology across techniques.** Rotary drilling produced the most microcracks, osteocyte damage and thermal injury and the lowest %BIC among five bed-preparation families, but the gap is largely protocol-mediated (speed, cooling, load) ([[implants/osteotomy-thermal/kosior-2025-histological-histomorphometric-implant-bed-preparation]]).
- **Context.** Dense D1 bone amplifies drilling heat; the wiki's dense-bone overview frames thermal injury as the D1-specific failure mechanism ([[overviews/dense-bone-osteotomy-heat-reimplantation-overview]]).
- **Human histological fingerprint (Piattelli 1998).** The first systematic human histological series of thermally failed implants (8 cases, University of Chieti) established a 6-feature pattern present in **all** specimens: (1) bone sequestra (devitalized bone fragments); (2) absence of peri-implant bone regeneration; (3) inflammatory infiltrate occupying the bone–implant gap; (4) failure of the peri-implant blood clot to organize; (5) compact and mature surrounding bone; (6) bacterial colonization of the implant surface and necrotic bone. Bacteria were interpreted as **secondary** post-necrotic colonizers, not the primary cause; the compact mature surrounding bone is consistent with placement into dense cortical bone where heat dissipation is reduced ([[implants/osteotomy-thermal/piattelli-1998-histologic-evaluation-eight-cases-failed]]). This 6-feature pattern is the histological reference standard for thermal necrosis — and it has no established radiographic correlate.

**Radiographic pattern — what X-ray CAN and CANNOT show.**

- **Cannot (in held evidence):** No held paper describes a radiographic appearance specific to thermal necrosis around a dental implant — no sign, no time course of radiolucency, no sensitivity or specificity. Every heat datapoint in the wiki is a temperature, a histologic score, or a BIC percentage.
- **Can (by inference only):** If heat prevents osseointegration, the endpoint is an early failure of integration; any peri-implant radiolucency that appears would be the generic picture of non-integration, which is not distinguishable from other causes of early failure on the held evidence.

**Timing.** Heat acts during surgery; its measured biological effect in vivo is in the first weeks of healing (2 and 8 weeks, Heuzeroth). Clinically this places it within the **early (pre-loading) failure** window.

**Clinical signs.** No held paper reports a clinical symptom or sign profile specific to thermally induced implant failure.

## Mechanism 2: Excessive Bone Compression (Overcompression)

**The positive mechanism evidence.**

- **Rat model — crater-like lesion.** In rat maxillary extraction sockets, high implant–osteotomy misfit that osseo-densified the bone produced micro-fractures, an extensive zone of dying osteocytes, a pro-resorptive interface (TRAP activity, cathepsin K) with **no** formation program (no ALP activity, no collagen I), and early implant failure presenting on µCT/histology as a **crater-like lesion filled with fibrous, inflamed granulation tissue** ([[implants/coyac-2019-preclinical-model-links-osseo-densification-misfit]]). The abstract reports no n, torque, misfit magnitude, BIC percentage, timepoint or p-value. It proves a destructive regime exists without locating its threshold, and transfer is bounded by rat ≠ human, fresh socket ≠ healed ridge, misfit ≠ bur-mediated osseodensification.
- **Bench — mechanical precursor.** In D2-equivalent polyurethane blocks, strain-gauge bone deformation rose with insertion turns; trapezoidal threads and conical bodies raised torque without damaging the bed, whereas **machined-surface implants exceeded the block's mechanical resistance and produced visible cracks** ([[implants/isq/nascimento-2024-measurement-bone-deformation-insertion-torque]]). Harm tracked design and surface, not the torque number.

- **Human case series — first biopsy-confirmed PIBN from overcompression (Abukraa 2025).** In 5 patients (10 total failed implants), 3 early cases presented immediately after placement (overcompression as likely cause) and 2 late cases arose as peri-implantitis sequelae. Two cases underwent biopsy (one early, one late) — both confirmed bone necrosis with the **same** pattern: empty osteocyte lacunae + nonvital bone fragments + acute and chronic inflammatory infiltrate. All patients were treated by implant and necrotic bone removal with no recurrence. The critical finding: the histological pattern was **convergent** across the two mechanisms — confirming that **timing, not tissue appearance, is the differentiating variable** ([[implants/peri-implantitis/abukraa-2025-peri-implant-bone-necrosis-clinical]]).

**High torque that does NOT produce necrosis.** These papers prevent the reading "high torque = compression necrosis":

- Sheep mandible, 110 Ncm vs 10 Ncm (assigned exposure): **no bone necrosis** (no osteocyte lacunar emptying, no avascular zones), greater bone apposition and removal torque at every timepoint; a transient day-7 stability dip that ISQ did not detect ([[implants/isq/trisi-2011-high-low-implant-torque-histology-sheep]]).
- Human prospective, MIT >70 Ncm (mean 110.6, up to 176 Ncm) vs 30–50 Ncm: all implants stable at 2–3 months; MBL 0.72 vs 1.03 mm at loading and 1.24 vs 1.09 mm at 1 year, not significantly different — but control n=9, post-hoc torque groups, 1-year horizon, and the high-torque group's loading→1-year trajectory (~0.52 mm vs ~0.06 mm, derived, untested) points toward later crestal loss ([[implants/isq/khayat-2011-clinical-outcome-dental-implants-high]]).
- SR+MA, 6 studies, 651 implants, ≥50 vs <50 Ncm: survival RR 0.51 (95% CI 0.06–4.06, NS), MBL MD 0.15 mm (−0.14 to 0.44, NS) ([[implants/isq/lemos-2020-high-insertion-torque-dental-implants-sr-ma]]).

**The human harm signal — late crestal loss, not early loss.**

- RCT, 116 single implants in healed sites, 3 years: ≥50 Ncm → significantly greater MBL and facial recession, most pronounced in the mandible; cumulative success 91.3% vs 98.2%; buccal bone thickness ≥1 mm attenuated recession ([[implants/isq/marconcini-2018-high-insertion-torque-bone-resorption-rct]]).
- Scoping review, 11 studies: mandibular MBL 1.53 vs 1.03 mm and mandibular facial recession −1.40 vs −0.26 mm at 3 years; failures cluster in D1 and D4 bone; headline numbers appear to derive from a single RCT, no pooling, and it does not overturn the Lemos null ([[implants/isq/manfredini-2025-high-insertion-torque-clinical-implications]]).

**Radiographic pattern.**

- **Animal (µCT, not clinical radiograph):** crater-like peri-implant lesion with early failure (Coyac).
- **Human:** the only held radiographic endpoint is **crestal MBL measured over 1–3 years** (Khayat, Marconcini, Manfredini). No held human study shows an early crater-like radiolucency attributable to compression, and none has histology to confirm compression necrosis as the cause of the MBL. The wiki's torque overview reads high torque as partly a readout of dense crestal bone rather than an independent dose ([[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]]).

**Timing.** Two possible presentations in the held evidence: (a) early integration failure (rat model); (b) slow crestal bone loss over years after loading (human RCT). Presentation (b) overlaps in time with peri-implantitis — which is why clinical probing is decisive there.

## Mechanism 3: Peri-implantitis

**Case definition — clinical signs are mandatory.** The 2017 World Workshop consensus defines peri-implantitis as **BOP ± suppuration + increased PD + progressive bone loss**; mucositis is BOP without progressive bone loss; baseline radiographs and probing at prosthesis delivery are recommended ([[implants/peri-implantitis/berglundh-2018-peri-implant-diseases-conditions-consensus]]). Because inflammation on probing is part of the definition, **a radiograph alone cannot diagnose peri-implantitis** — radiographic bone loss without BOP/suppuration does not meet the case definition.

**When no baseline radiograph exists.** Held clinical studies operationalize the EFP criteria as BOP/suppuration + PD ≥6 mm + bone loss ≥3 mm ([[implants/peri-implantitis/galarraga-vinueza-2020-macrophage-polarization-peri-implantitis-lesions]], [[implants/peri-implantitis/cafferata-2025-impaired-treg-mediated-immune-regulation]], [[implants/peri-implantitis/sbricoli-2026-peri-implant-disease-prevalence-type2-diabetes]]).

**Separating disease from physiological remodeling.** Peri-implant stability is PPD <5 mm, absence of BOP/suppuration and <2 mm bone loss from baseline; initial remodeling up to 1.5 mm may be physiological; probing force ≤0.15 N avoids false-positive BOP around implants ([[implants/peri-implantitis/ramseier-2024-diagnostic-measures-monitoring-follow-up]]).

**Radiographic pattern.** Progressive marginal bone loss relative to baseline. Held surgical-treatment RCTs describe the defects as **crater-like/circumferential intrabony defects** ([[implants/peri-implantitis/monje-2025-surface-decontamination-reconstructive-peri-implantitis]], [[implants/peri-implantitis/park-2025-implantoplasty-vs-titanium-brushes-peri-implantitis-rct]]); defects are also classed intraosseous (class I) vs supra-alveolar (class II) ([[implants/peri-implantitis/smeets-2014-peri-implantitis-definition-etiology-review]]). This is the same shape vocabulary as Coyac's overcompression lesion — defect morphology therefore does not discriminate the mechanism.

**Late osseointegration failure — fibrous encapsulation endpoint (Piattelli 2003).** The largest human histological series of retrieved mobility-failed implants (n=51 over 5 years, University of Chieti) showed a universal 600–1,100 µm **fibrous connective tissue layer** between the implant and surrounding bone in every case. The surrounding bone was compact, highly mineralized, with well-structured Haversian canals and few remodeling areas. No bacteria were found at the coronal implant surface; inflammatory infiltrate was scarce. This fibrous encapsulation pattern — compact mature bone + fibrous interface + no infection — is mechanistically consistent with osseointegration that was established but disrupted by mechanical overload. It is histologically **distinct** from thermal necrosis (bone sequestra + absent regeneration + organized blood clot failure, Piattelli 1998) and from peri-implantitis (osteolytic pocket + bacterial plaque + BOP) ([[implants/peri-implantitis/piattelli-2003-clinical-histologic-aspects-dental-implants]]). This is the histological endpoint of late mechanical failure, not of early overcompression necrosis.

**Timing.** Late: after osseointegration and prosthesis delivery, detected as progression against the baseline taken at loading.

## Differential Diagnosis — Practical Framework

| Mechanism | Radiographic pattern | Timing | Clinical requirement | Differentiating factor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ① Drilling overheating (thermal necrosis) | **No specific sign in held evidence**; at most the generic picture of non-integration | Surgery → early healing weeks (pre-loading) | None specific held; failure of integration / mobility | **Surgical record**: worn drill, inadequate irrigation, guided sleeve blocking irrigation, dense D1 bone, long drilling time |
| ② Overcompression | Animal µCT: crater-like lesion with early failure; human: crestal MBL over 1–3 years (mechanism unconfirmed) | Early (animal) **or** gradual post-loading crestal loss (human) | None specific held; must exclude peri-implantitis by probing when loss is late | **Surgical record**: very high final torque *in dense crestal cortex* (esp. mandible), undersized osteotomy / large misfit, thin buccal bone <1 mm, implant surface/design |
| ③ Peri-implantitis | Progressive bone loss vs baseline; crater-like/circumferential or supra-alveolar defects | Post-loading, progressive | **BOP ± suppuration + increased PD mandatory** (Berglundh 2018); without baseline: PD ≥6 mm + bone loss ≥3 mm | **Clinical probing** + progression on serial radiographs |

**How to use it (reasoned combination, not a validated algorithm):**

1. **Date the failure.** Before prosthetic loading → early implant failure, mechanistically a failure *to achieve* osseointegration (incidence ≈1–4% implant-level; [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]); peri-implantitis is essentially excluded by definition because there is no post-loading baseline to progress from. After loading with progressive loss → peri-implantitis is the leading candidate and must be tested clinically.
2. **Probe.** BOP/suppuration with increased PD satisfies the inflammatory criteria for peri-implantitis. Late crestal loss *without* BOP/suppuration does not meet the case definition; in a dense mandibular crest placed at very high torque, the held RCT signal (Marconcini) makes compression-associated remodeling one candidate — but no held paper validates this as a diagnosis.
3. **Compare with the baseline radiograph.** Distinguish progression from the up-to-1.5 mm initial remodeling that may be physiological (Ramseier).
4. **Read the surgical record, not the film, to separate ① from ②.** Both are early, both act at surgery, both can end in the same radiographic picture, and both are amplified by dense D1 bone — so they can coexist in one failure. The held evidence gives no X-ray sign that assigns an early failure to heat versus compression.
5. **Do not assign a mechanism from defect shape.** "Crater-like" appears in both the overcompression animal model and peri-implantitis surgical RCTs.
6. **Do not assign overcompression from the torque number alone.** 110 Ncm produced no necrosis in sheep (Trisi), up to 176 Ncm did not prevent human osseointegration at 1 year (Khayat), and pooled data show no survival/MBL difference at ≥50 Ncm (Lemos); bench harm tracked surface and design (Nascimento).

## Key Uncertainties

- **No held paper compares all three mechanisms**, radiographically or otherwise. The framework above combines three separate literatures.
- **No held radiographic sign for thermal necrosis.** Heat evidence is in vitro temperature (Möhlhenrich), thresholds from orthopaedic data (Bertollo), and animal histomorphometry (Heuzeroth, Kosior). Whether overheated sites fail with any distinctive film appearance is unknown to this wiki.
- **Human histology for overcompression — partially resolved.** Abukraa 2025 provides the first biopsy-confirmed case of overcompression-driven peri-implant bone necrosis (PIBN): 1 histologically confirmed case among 3 early-onset PIBN patients showing empty osteocyte lacunae + nonvital bone + inflammatory infiltrate. However, the histological pattern is **identical** to the peri-implantitis PIBN pattern (1 late-onset biopsy confirmed the same picture) — confirming that histology alone cannot assign the mechanism. The rat-only pathway evidence (Coyac) is now supplemented by human case data, but the series is small (n=1 histologically confirmed overcompression case) and abstract-only ingest limits detail. The human harm signal (Marconcini, Manfredini) remains crestal MBL whose mechanism is inferred.
- **Coyac 2019 is rat and abstract-only** — no n, torque, misfit magnitude, timepoint, or BIC number; "excessive" is not defined; its crater-like lesion is µCT/histology, not a clinical radiograph.
- **Heat and compression may coexist.** Dense D1 bone amplifies both drilling heat and insertion strain, so one early failure may carry both mechanisms, and no held evidence separates their contributions.
- **Late non-inflammatory crestal loss is unresolved.** For a post-loading bone loss without BOP/suppuration, the wiki holds no validated way to attribute it to compression versus other remodeling causes.
- **Diagnostic accuracy is untested.** No sensitivity/specificity data exist for any of the discriminators (timing, probing, surgical record) as a mechanism test.
