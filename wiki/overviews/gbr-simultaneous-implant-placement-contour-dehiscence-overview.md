## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 임플란트 **동시 골유도재생술 (Simultaneous Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)** — 식립과 같은 세션에 이개열 (dehiscence) · 천공 (fenestration) 결손을 메우고 협측 윤곽을 증대하는 시나리오 — 10편으로 종합. 기존 GBR 오버뷰(차폐막 분류·막노출·이식재 클래스·치조제보존술)가 다루지 않던 **단일 술식 축**이다.
> - **핵심 명제: 동시 GBR의 율속 단계는 골형성이 아니라 이식재 부피의 생존이다.** 신생골은 결손 측벽에서 구심성 (centripetal) 으로 알아서 자라 들어온다 — 문제는 그때까지 이식재가 남아 있느냐다.
> - **막은 골형성 촉진자가 아니라 격납 장치 (containment device)**: 개 표준화 5×3 mm 협측 이개열에서 DBBM+콜라겐막 vs DBBM 단독의 신생골·최초 골-임플란트 접촉 (first Bone-to-Implant Contact, fBIC) ·협측 골두께 모두 차이 없음 (NS); 막은 중앙부 이식재 보존 경향만 보였다 (p=0.052). 같은 실험에서 측방 절편이 중앙 절편보다 신생골 유의하게 많음 — 구심성 유입의 직접 증거 (Darby 2024).
> - **재료는 이제 레버가 아니다 (동등성으로 수렴)**: 합성골 (이상성 인산칼슘, Biphasic Calcium Phosphate) 이 이종골 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 에 비열등 — 결손고 감소 74.5% vs 63.4%, p=0.216 (Cha 2024, 다기관 비열등성 RCT n=49). 막도 마찬가지: 열탈수가교막 (Dehydrothermal Crosslinked Membrane, DCM) = 비가교막 (n=28, Lee 2015), 신규 비가교막 vs 기준막은 경향뿐 (Wessing 2016).
> - **진짜 레버는 봉합 순간의 부피 방어다.** 이식재는 일차봉합 과정에서 치조정 (crestal) 부위에서 무너진다 — 돼지 사체 수평증대에서 임플란트 플랫폼 수준 이식재 변위 (Graft Material Displacement, GMD) 는 결손 함입 여부와 무관하게 약 −28% (Raabe 2025).
> - **막 고정의 효과는 조건부다 — 이 오버뷰가 밝히는 긴장 지점**: 함입/부분함입 결손에서 **4-핀** 고정은 치조정 변위를 −38.1% → −15.8% 로 절반 이하로 줄였으나 (p<0.001, Raabe 2025), 미함입 오목 (concavity) 결손에서 **2-핀** 고정은 어느 레벨에서도 무효였고 (p≥0.19) 대신 **낮은 장력의 큰 판막 전진**이 지배 인자였다 (전진 9.20 mm·장력 0.02 N → 치조정 변위 −35.7%, vs 4.42 mm·0.09 N → −45.0%, p<0.001; Zhou 2025). 결손 함입도와 핀 개수가 고정 효과를 가른다.
> - **고정을 아예 안 하면 치관부는 비어버린다**: 비고정 콜라겐막 사용 시 임플란트 견부 골폭 0 mm, 1 mm 하방 약 0.1 mm — 이식재 충전 기법 (느슨 vs 압축) 을 바꿔도 보완 불가 (Lee 2025, 개 32 임플란트).
> - **증례 선택이 흡수를 예측한다**: 전방 상악 동시 GBR 6개월 이식재 흡수율 평균 **49.78%**; 나이·성별·임플란트 브랜드·재료는 예측력 없고 오직 술전 CBCT 골형태 — 골오목 깊이 (Bone Concavity Depth, BCD) ≥1.03 mm (r=−0.872, p<0.001) 와 골오목 각도 (Bone Concavity Angulation, BCA) <155.3° (p<0.001) 가 흡수 적은 증례를 가려낸다 (Gan 2023). 깊고 좁은 오목 = 텐트 효과 = 유리한 증례.
> - **살아남은 부피는 10년 간다**: 심미부 조기식립 (Type 2) + 2층 복합이식 (자가골편 + 저치환 이종골) 10년 전향 추적에서 성공률 95%, 협측 골벽 0 → **1.67 mm**, 6~10년 수직 골소실 **0.02 mm**, 심미지수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES) 중앙값 8, 협측 퇴축 0.17 mm (Chappuis 2018). 즉 초기 1년을 통과한 부피는 이후 사실상 정지 상태다.
> - **세 개의 손실 수치를 곱하지 마라**: −28~45% (봉합, 돼지 사체) · −49.78% (6개월, 인체 후향) · +1.67 mm 잔존 (10년, 인체 전향) 은 서로 다른 모델·시점·측정법에서 나온 값이라 **누적 곱셈은 불가**하다. 방향만 공유한다 — 손실은 앞쪽(봉합~6개월)에 몰려 있고 뒤쪽은 안정적이다.
> - **임상 takeaway**: 재료 고민 대신 ① 술전 CBCT로 BCD/BCA 보고 증례 난이도를 판정하고 ② 예상 흡수분만큼 과충전 (overcontour) 하고 ③ 함입 결손이면 4-핀 고정, 미함입 오목이면 골막이완절개로 장력을 먼저 없애고 ④ 무장력 일차봉합으로 막을 묻어라. 이 넷이 막 브랜드보다 10년 협측 윤곽을 더 크게 좌우한다.
> - **미해결**: 핀 고정 근거는 전부 사체·동물이며 인체 RCT가 없다. Cha 2024는 6개월 재진입·1년 변연골까지만이고, Chappuis 2018은 단일기관 n=20이다. 동시 vs 단계적 (staged) 접근을 장기 윤곽 유지로 비교한 RCT도 아직 없다.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 10 papers (2 human RCTs, 1 multicenter RCT interim, 1 10-year prospective series, 1 retrospective CBCT study, 3 animal/ex vivo studies, 1 narrative review) on guided bone regeneration performed **simultaneously with implant placement** for peri-implant dehiscence/fenestration defects and buccal contour augmentation — the one GBR axis not covered by the existing membrane-classification, membrane-exposure, graft-material, and ridge-preservation overviews.

The unifying thesis is that new bone formation is not the rate-limiting step — bone grows centripetally from the lateral defect walls regardless of membrane presence (Darby 2024), and graft materials and membranes have converged on non-inferiority (Cha 2024; Lee 2015; Wessing 2016) — whereas **graft volume survival** is: roughly 28–45% of graft displaces during primary closure (Raabe 2025; Zhou 2025), ~50% resorbs by 6 months (Gan 2023), and non-fixed membranes leave essentially zero bone at the implant shoulder (Lee 2025).

Volume that survives the first year is durable — 1.67 mm of facial bone wall regenerated from zero, with only 0.02 mm of vertical loss between years 6 and 10 and 95% success (Chappuis 2018) — so the controllable levers are pre-operative case selection by CBCT concavity morphology (Gan 2023), over-contouring, defect-appropriate membrane stabilization, and tension-free primary closure (Buser 2023).

첫 1년을 통과한 부피는 10년간 사실상 정지하므로 (협측골 1.67 mm, 6~10년 소실 0.02 mm, 성공률 95%; Chappuis 2018), 조절 가능한 레버는 술전 CBCT 골오목 형태에 의한 증례 선택 (Gan 2023) · 과충전 · 결손 유형에 맞춘 막 고정 · 무장력 일차봉합 (Buser 2023) 이다.

## Thesis

Ask what limits the outcome of GBR done at the same session as implant placement, and the literature answers with an inversion of the usual framing. The intuitive model is that a barrier membrane *induces* bone, and that picking the right membrane and the right graft therefore determines how much bone appears. The evidence assembled here does not support that model.

Darby's split-mouth dog study created standardized 5×3 mm buccal dehiscences at early implant placement and grafted them with DBBM either covered or uncovered by a resorbable collagen membrane. At 6 weeks the membrane produced **no significant difference** in new bone, first bone-to-implant contact, buccal bone thickness, or regenerated area. It only tended to retain more graft at central sections (p=0.052). Meanwhile lateral sections contained significantly more new bone than central sections — bone was marching in *centripetally from the residual walls*, not being conjured out of the graft core. The membrane, in that model, is a containment device.

Read alongside the material comparisons, this reframes the whole procedure. Cha's multicenter non-inferiority RCT (n=49, Seoul + Madrid) found a synthetic biphasic calcium phosphate statistically non-inferior to DBBM at peri-implant dehiscences (74.5% vs 63.4% defect-height reduction, p=0.216). Lee's 2015 single-blinded RCT found dehydrothermally cross-linked and non-cross-linked collagen membranes equivalent on every clinical and CBCT parameter at 16 weeks. Wessing's multicenter RCT found a new non-cross-linked membrane only *trending* better than the reference. Three separate comparisons, three convergences on equivalence. If the material choices no longer separate outcomes, the variance must live somewhere else.

It lives in volume. The graft that the surgeon places is not the graft that heals. Raabe's ex vivo study measured graft displacement during primary wound closure and found ~28% loss at the implant platform *regardless of defect containment*. Gan's retrospective CBCT series measured 49.78% mean graft resorption at 6 months in the anterior maxilla. Lee's 2025 dog study showed that with a non-fixed collagen membrane, bone width at the implant shoulder was **0 mm** and at 1 mm apical **~0.1 mm** — the coronal region, precisely the region that determines the esthetic result, simply collapsed.

And yet the procedure works, durably, when the volume survives. Chappuis's 10-year prospective series of early placement with a two-layer composite graft rebuilt the facial bone wall from 0 mm to 1.67 mm, lost only 0.02 mm of vertical facial bone between years 6 and 10, and reported 95% success with a median PES of 8. The regenerated wall, once established, behaves like bone rather than like a graft.

So the thesis: **at simultaneous GBR, treat bone formation as a given and graft volume as the scarce resource.** Every clinical decision should be evaluated by how much volume it delivers to the 1-year mark.

## Clinical Decision Points

### 1. Is this case favorable? — read the concavity before opening the flap

Gan's finding is the most directly actionable item in this set because it is pre-operative and free. Mean graft resorption in anterior maxillary simultaneous GBR was 49.78% at 6 months, and none of the variables clinicians usually discuss — age, sex, implant brand, implant angulation, graft material — predicted it. Only two CBCT measurements did:

| Morphology | Threshold | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Bone concavity depth (BCD) | **≥1.03 mm** → less resorption (r=−0.872, p<0.001) | A deeper concavity gives mechanical "tent" support and resists membrane collapse |
| Bone concavity angulation (BCA) | **<155.3°** → less resorption (p<0.001) | A narrower, more contained concavity holds graft against soft-tissue pressure |

A flat, shallow, wide-angled ridge is the unfavorable phenotype: nothing supports the membrane, and soft-tissue pressure wins. That is the case to either over-contour aggressively, stabilize mechanically, or stage.

### 2. Simultaneous or staged? — defect geometry, per PASS

Buser's review frames the decision through the PASS conditions and post-extraction timing types 1–4, with composite graft (autogenous chips for osteogenesis + DBBM for volume maintenance) as the horizontal-augmentation standard. Simultaneous GBR is appropriate when the residual walls can both stabilize the implant and supply the centripetal bone ingrowth that Darby demonstrated. When the defect has too few walls to do either, the space-maintenance leg of PASS fails and the case belongs to a staged or vertical protocol — delegated to the vertical ridge augmentation and protocol-ladder overviews.

### 3. How to defend the volume at closure — and why the fixation evidence disagrees

This is the axis where the held papers genuinely conflict, and the conflict is informative rather than noise:

| Study | Defect model | Fixation tested | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raabe 2025 | Contained + partially contained | **4 pins** | Crestal displacement −15.8% vs −38.1% without pins (**p<0.001**) |
| Zhou 2025 | Uncontained (bone concavity) | **2 pins** | **No effect** at any level (p≥0.19); flap advancement/tension was the only significant predictor |
| Lee 2025 | 5×5 mm dehiscence | **None** (non-fixed) | 0 mm bone at shoulder regardless of graft packing technique |

Two variables differ between Raabe and Zhou — defect containment and pin count — so the honest reading is a boundary condition, not a contradiction: **fixation earns its keep where the membrane can be tensioned against contained walls with enough pins; in an uncontained concavity, two pins are not enough and the dominant lever moves to the flap.** Zhou quantified that lever directly: major advancement (9.20 mm) at one-quarter the tension (0.02 vs 0.09 N) reduced crestal graft loss from −45.0% to −35.7% (p<0.001). Lee 2025 supplies the floor case — with no fixation at all, no amount of graft-packing skill recovers the coronal region.

Practical ordering: **release the flap until it is genuinely tension-free first, then fix the membrane, then over-contour** — because Raabe's mechanistic observation was that graft compression is essentially unavoidable (pinned sites compress during pinning, unpinned sites compress during closure, converging on similar final thickness). The benefit of pinning is *predictability and control of where* the collapse happens, not elimination of it.

### 4. Which materials? — choose on cost, ethics, and availability, not efficacy

Given Cha (synthetic ≈ DBBM), Lee 2015 (DCM ≈ NCM), and Wessing 2016 (trend only), material selection at this indication should be driven by non-efficacy considerations. Cha's own framing is the useful one: a synthetic biphasic calcium phosphate is the option for patients with religious, ethical, or disease-transmission concerns about xenografts, and it costs nothing in defect resolution to choose it. Membrane selection likewise reduces to handling preference and exposure tolerance — an axis owned by the membrane-exposure overview rather than this one.

## Gaps & Future Research

- **No human RCT on membrane fixation at this indication.** The entire fixation argument (Raabe, Zhou, Lee 2025) rests on ex vivo pig mandibles and dog models with no healing period or postoperative loading. The pin-count × containment interaction inferred above is a hypothesis generated by cross-study comparison, not a tested one.
- **Short human follow-up on the material question.** Cha 2024 reports 6-month re-entry and 1-year marginal bone; whether the synthetic's higher complete-resolution trend (62.5% vs 44%) is real or an underpowered artifact is unresolved, and the trial was registered retrospectively.
- **The long-term benchmark rests on n=20, single center.** Chappuis 2018 is the strongest durability evidence held here but is a case series from the group that developed the protocol.
- **The three volume-loss figures are not commensurable.** −28 to −45% (ex vivo pig, closure), −49.78% (human retrospective, 6 months), and +1.67 mm residual (human prospective, 10 years) come from different species, models, timepoints, and measurement methods. They agree on direction — loss is front-loaded, survivors are stable — but must not be multiplied into a cumulative estimate.
- **Simultaneous vs staged has no head-to-head long-term contour trial.** The choice is currently made on defect geometry and expert framework (Buser 2023) rather than comparative outcome data.
- **Darby's null is a 6-week dog endpoint.** Concluding from it that membranes are dispensable clinically would over-read a short-term animal model whose own graft-retention trend (p=0.052) points the other way over longer horizons.
