> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 구강점막 정상 변이 병소(포다이스 그래뉼, 교흔/morsicatio, 마찰성 각화증, 담배쌈지각화증)는 흔하고(외래 환자 3.9–14%) 대부분 양성이지만, OLP·OPMD와 임상적으로 혼동되어 불필요한 조직검사·스테로이드 치료로 이어지는 주요 함정이 있다.
> - **유병률 스펙트럼**: 대규모 네팔 연구(n=16,572)에서 정상 변이 전체 13.96%; 포다이스 그래뉼 8.84%, 마찰성 각화증+교흔 우세. 인도 연구(n=144,056)에서 각화성 백색 병소 3.89% — 마찰성 각화증 71.65% > 담배쌈지각화증 27.38% > morsicatio 0.9%.
> - **성별·부위**: 3가지 각화성 병소 모두 남성 우세(p=0.01); 정상 변이의 80%가 협점막(buccal mucosa) 집중.
> - **최대 함정**: morsicatio ↔ OLP 혼동 — 2년 오진 증례(baklouti-2023); 더모스코피(구강경)로 Wickham striae 부재 확인 시 비침습적으로 감별 가능.
> - **감별 핵심**: 더모스코피에서 백황색 무구조 영역 + 느슨한 인설 + Wickham striae 없음 → morsicatio/마찰성 병소. 레이스 모양 Wickham striae → OLP 방향.
> - **처치 원칙**: 원인 제거 우선(불량 수복물·날카로운 치아 조정, 담배 습관 중단, 볼 물기 행동 교정) → 불응 시 조직검사. 정상 변이를 조직검사 없이 경과 관찰 후 해소 확인이 표준.
> - **포다이스 그래뉼** 유병률은 연구 간 1.2–82.8%로 극변동 — 단일 글로벌 기준치보다 병소 패턴 인식이 임상적으로 더 중요.
> - **임상 시사점**: 정상 변이 병소는 잠재악성질환(OPMD)이 아니므로 암 공포를 유발하지 않도록 환자 설명이 중요; 단, 담배쌈지각화증은 무연담배 노출의 OPMD 전환 위험 마커로 기억할 것.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 3 studies (total screening exposure n>160,000) on benign oral mucosal normal variants — Fordyce granules, linea alba/morsicatio, frictional keratosis, tobacco pouch keratosis.

Prevalence: ~14% of dental outpatients have at least one normal variant; all are predominantly buccal-mucosa-dominant and male-predominant; the clinically critical risk is morsicatio being misdiagnosed as OLP and treated with topical steroids for years (resolved by dermoscopy — absence of Wickham striae).

Management is cause-elimination first and biopsy only if unresponsive; tobacco pouch keratosis is the one entity that warrants vigilance as a smokeless-tobacco exposure marker for OPMD risk.

## 세줄요약

3편 연구(총 스크리닝 n>160,000) — 포다이스 그래뉼, 교흔(linea alba/morsicatio), 마찰성 각화증(frictional keratosis), 담배쌈지각화증(tobacco pouch keratosis) 양성 구강점막 정상 변이 종합.

유병률 약 14%(치과 외래); 전체적으로 협점막 집중·남성 우세; 임상적 함정은 morsicatio의 OLP 오진 — 더모스코피에서 Wickham striae 부재 확인으로 비침습 감별.

처치 원칙: 원인 제거 우선, 불응 시 조직검사; 담배쌈지각화증만 무연담배 노출의 OPMD 마커로 예외적 주의.

## Summary

Benign white keratotic and sebaceous normal variants of the oral mucosa are collectively common — present in roughly 14% of dental outpatients in large population-based records (Gupta 2023, Nepal, n=16,572; Nautiyal 2024, India, n=144,056 screened). Despite their benign nature, they generate clinical risk through misdiagnosis: the most dangerous confusion is **morsicatio (교흔) ↔ oral lichen planus (OLP)**, documented in a case where incorrect OLP treatment with topical steroids continued for 2 years (Baklouti 2023). Dermoscopy (mucoscopy) resolves this non-invasively.

This synthesis covers four entities across three axes: (1) prevalence and demography, (2) the misdiagnosis pitfall, and (3) management algorithm.

## Axis 1 — Prevalence and Demography

| Entity | Prevalence (context) | Key demographic |
|---|---|---|
| Fordyce granules (포다이스 그래뉼) | 8.84% overall (Nepal); ranges 1.2–82.8% globally | Peak in 11–20y; normal variant, no treatment needed |
| Linea alba / morsicatio | Subsumed under frictional keratosis; morsicatio 0.9% of keratotic whites (India) | Male predominant; often unaware of habit |
| Frictional keratosis (마찰성 각화증) | 71.65% of keratotic white lesions; 3.89% of all patients (India) | Male, mean age 26.3y |
| Tobacco pouch keratosis (담배쌈지각화증) | 27.38% of keratotic whites (India) | Male, mean age 44.5y; cumulative habit marker |

Across all three studies, **80% of normal variants were located on the buccal mucosa**, and all keratotic entities showed male predominance (chi-square p=0.01 in Nautiyal 2024). Global Fordyce granule prevalence varies by 60-fold across populations — pattern recognition is more reliable than a memorized prevalence number.

## Axis 2 — The OLP Misdiagnosis Pitfall

Morsicatio labiorum/buccarum is the entity most likely to be misdiagnosed as OLP, because both present as chronic white plaques on the buccal mucosa or lips. Baklouti 2023 reports a 2-year diagnostic error: the patient received topical steroids for "OLP" with no improvement before dermoscopy provided the correct diagnosis.

**Dermoscopy (mucoscopy) differentiates:**

| Feature | Morsicatio / frictional | OLP |
|---|---|---|
| Surface | Structureless whitish/yellowish areas | Reticular / lace pattern |
| Wickham striae | **ABSENT** ← key | **PRESENT** (pathognomonic) |
| Scale | Loose, easily removable | Adherent, not removable |
| Vessels | Dotted-vessel erosions possible | Linear/branching on background |

Key behavioral clue: patients with morsicatio are often **unaware of their habitual biting** — anxious temperament, particularly at night. Direct questioning about parafunctional habits should be routine.

Frictional keratosis from sharp tooth/restoration cusps can similarly be confused with early leukoplakia (OPMD). Management test: eliminate the irritant → if the lesion resolves within 2–4 weeks, it was frictional. Persistent lesions warrant biopsy.

## Axis 3 — Management Algorithm

```
White oral lesion observed
        │
        ├─ Obvious irritant (sharp tooth/restoration, biting habit, smokeless tobacco)?
        │   YES → eliminate cause + 2-4 week review
        │           ├─ Resolved → frictional/behavioral → no biopsy, reassure
        │           └─ Persistent → biopsy
        │
        └─ No clear irritant / Wickham-striae-like pattern?
            → dermoscopy first (if available)
                ├─ Wickham striae present → OLP workup
                └─ Structureless, no striae → morsicatio/frictional → habit counseling
```

**Tobacco pouch keratosis** is the one entity requiring heightened vigilance: it marks chronic smokeless-tobacco exposure, which is an OPMD risk factor. Unlike pure frictional keratosis, its resolution may be incomplete if the tobacco habit is entrenched, and it carries a non-negligible risk of progressing to erythroleukoplakia if the habit continues.

## Clinical Takeaways

1. **Fordyce granules, linea alba, morsicatio, frictional keratosis** — inform patients these are benign. Avoid the word "lesion" if possible; say "normal variation."
2. **When OLP is suspected but unresponsive to topical steroids**, consider morsicatio in the differential and ask about biting habits — or use dermoscopy to look for Wickham striae.
3. **Biopsy is not routinely needed** for classic presentations; reserve it for lesions unresponsive to cause-elimination within 2–4 weeks.
4. **Tobacco pouch keratosis** in a smokeless-tobacco user is a behavioral marker; cessation counseling is primary management, with biopsy if white areas persist post-cessation.
