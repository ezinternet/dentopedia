## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: **재발성 아프타성 구내염 (Recurrent Aphthous Stomatitis, RAS)** 8편을 세 축으로 종합 — ① 분류·병태생리, ② 국소→비약물→전신으로 올라가는 치료 사다리, ③ 특발성 RAS와 구분되는 **증후군성 아프타 궤양 중첩** (PFAPA·베체트). 결론 미리: 흔한 특발성 RAS는 **국소 치료가 여전히 1차**이며, 증후군 중첩이 바꾸는 것은 1차 치료가 아니라 **감별·의뢰(red flag)** 다.
> - **역학·분류**: 성인 유병률 **~20%**, 소아·청소년기 발병, 가족력 흔함 (Mills 2017). 세 형태 — 소형 (minor, <1 cm, 반흔 없이 1–2주 치유), 대형 (major, >1 cm, 반흔 남기며 느리게 치유), 포진형 (herpetiform, 다발 1–2 mm) (Manfredini 2021). 선진국에서 유병률↑, 여성 약우세(포진형에서 더 뚜렷) (Gasmi 2021).
> - **병인은 다인성**: 면역·유전·영양·스트레스; 유발인자 = 국소 외상·정서적 스트레스·철/비타민B12/엽산 결핍·글루텐 감수성 (Mills 2017). 스펙트럼은 국소 외상·구강미생물 불균형부터 전신질환(베체트·염증성 장질환(IBD)·조혈인자 결핍)까지 (Gasmi 2021).
> - **1차 = 국소 코르티코스테로이드 (Topical Corticosteroid)**: 트리암시놀론 (Triamcinolone)·클로베타솔 (Clobetasol)이 통증·치유시간 단축에 가장 강한 근거 — 네 편의 리뷰가 일치하는 표준 치료 (Belenguer 2014·Manfredini 2021·Gasmi 2021·Mills 2017). 히알루론산 (Hyaluronic Acid) 병용, 클로르헥시딘 (Chlorhexidine)은 궤양 빈도 소폭 감소 (Belenguer 2014).
> - **비약물 전환 (D'Amario 2025, 45개 무작위대조시험(RCT))**: **저출력레이저치료 (Low-Level Laser Therapy, LLLT)** 가 즉각적(1일째) 통증 완화에 단일 최고 modality — 다이오드 1일째 통증척도(VAS) **2.04 vs 약물 4.85**, 이산화탄소(CO₂) 레이저 **8.48→0.68** 즉시. 오메가-3 (Omega-3)는 6개월 RCT 3편에서 위약 우월(한 군 VAS 6개월째 1.48 vs 위약 ~6.6)·재발 감소. 천연 점막부착젤 — 알로에 76.6%·몰약 76.7% 6일째 치유/무통, 커큐민 (Curcumin) = 트리암시놀론과 차이 없음. 저자 결론: 비약물이 스테로이드에 **버금가거나 더 나은 위험-이득비**.
> - **전신 단계 상승**: 중증·빈발 RAS → **콜히친 (Colchicine) 0.5 mg 1일 2회**에 양호한 근거; 난치성 대형 → 답손 (Dapsone)·탈리도마이드 (Thalidomide) (Manfredini 2021).
> - **탈리도마이드 = 중증·난치 전용 예비약 (Jian 2024 SR+MA, 21 RCT n=1,668)**: 완전반응 위약 대비 **상대위험도(RR) 6.95**, 타약물 대비 2.18, 재발간격 **+26.91일**, 치유시간·궤양 수·크기 감소. **그러나 통증(VAS) 개선 없음 (MD −0.64, p=0.26)**, 부작용 **RR 1.87**(말초신경병증·혈전). **25 mg/일**이 효능-안전 최적점, 신경병증 증상 시 즉시 중단, **임신 절대금기**. 근거 한계 — 21편 중 17편 중국, 이질성 I² 최대 99%.
> - **합성 통찰 — 통증 vs 질병조절의 분리**: 탈리도마이드는 재발·치유·궤양 크기 등 **질병 경과**는 개선하나 **개별 궤양의 통증은 못 줄이고**, LLLT는 정반대로 **통증은 즉시 줄이나** 근본 경과는 못 바꾼다. 두 축을 목표에 맞춰 조합.
> - **증후군 중첩 ①: PFAPA (주기열·아프타·인두염·경부림프절염 증후군, Periodic Fever, Aphthous stomatitis, Pharyngitis, Adenitis)** — 소아기 가장 흔한 주기발열증후군, 원인 희귀돌연변이 없음 → 다유전자성 자기염증질환; **감수성 유전좌위가 베체트·재발성 아프타와 공유**; 진단기준 미충족 환자에서도 **편도절제술이 효과적** (Manthiram 2023).
> - **증후군 중첩 ②: 베체트병 (Behçet's Disease, BD)** — 만성 다발성 혈관염, 재발성 구강·생식기 궤양이 가장 흔한 초기 발현. 구강 건강이 단순 발현 부위가 아니라 **병인적 동인** — 미생물 불균형(*S. sanguinis/mutans*)·선천면역 저하(만노스결합렉틴↓)·치주 상태 불량이 (특히 남성에서) 중증도와 연관; **1개월 치주치료 → 초기 외상성 악화 후 6개월째 재발 궤양 빈도 감소**; **활동기에는 치과치료 연기**(기계적 외상이 악화 유발) (Mumcu 2021). 흥미로운 대비 — BD 궤양 부위 미생물총은 **RAS 궤양 부위와 구별**된다(중첩된 소인, 구별되는 국소 발현).
> - **중첩이 바꾸는 것 = 1차 치료가 아니라 감별**: D'Amario 2025의 치료 근거는 베체트·크론 이차성 RAS를 **명시적으로 배제**했다 → 치료 사다리는 특발성 RAS용이고, 증후군성은 증후군 표적치료(베체트=콜히친/아프레밀라스트, PFAPA=편도절제)를 받는다. 실무 payoff는 **"언제 특발성이 아니라 전신·증후군을 의심하나"** — 소아 주기열+인두염+경부림프절염(PFAPA), 생식기·안구 궤양·실크로드 혈통(베체트), 위장증상(IBD), **성인 신규 발병**(RAS는 통상 소아기 발병), 국소치료 불응, 지속적 조혈인자 결핍.
> - **근거 등급**: 대부분 narrative review; 치료 사다리의 정량 근거는 **탈리도마이드 SR+MA(Jian 2024)** 와 **45-RCT PRISMA 리뷰(D'Amario 2025)** 두 축이 지탱, 증후군 두 편은 서술적 리뷰. 전반적으로 완치가 아닌 **증상 완화·재발 감소** 지향.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 8 papers on recurrent aphthous stomatitis (RAS) — recurring painful oral mucosal ulcers affecting ~20% of adults — across three axes: classification/pathophysiology, a topical→non-pharmacological→systemic treatment ladder, and the syndromic aphthous-ulceration overlap (PFAPA, Behçet) that must be distinguished from idiopathic RAS. The organizing thesis: for the common idiopathic case the topical-first ladder is unchanged, and what the syndromic overlap changes is not first-line therapy but the differential diagnosis and referral pathway.

Topical corticosteroids (triamcinolone, clobetasol) remain the agreed first-line for pain and healing across four reviews (Belenguer 2014, Manfredini 2021, Gasmi 2021, Mills 2017); the most systematic treatment synthesis (D'Amario 2025, 45 RCTs) argues non-pharmacological options give the best risk–benefit ratio, with LLLT the single best modality for immediate pain (diode day-1 VAS 2.04 vs 4.85 pharmacological; CO₂ 8.48→0.68) and omega-3 reducing recurrence over 6 months; step-up runs colchicine 0.5 mg bid (Manfredini) to thalidomide, which for major/topical-refractory disease raises complete response (RR 6.95 vs placebo; recurrence interval +26.91 days) but does NOT reduce pain (MD −0.64, p=0.26) and significantly increases adverse events (RR 1.87, peripheral neuropathy/thrombosis), best at 25 mg/day and absolutely contraindicated in pregnancy (Jian 2024 SR+MA, 21 RCTs, n=1,668). PFAPA is a polygenic childhood autoinflammatory periodic-fever syndrome whose susceptibility loci are shared with Behçet and recurrent aphthous ulcers, with tonsillectomy effective even beyond strict criteria (Manthiram 2023); in Behçet, oral dysbiosis and periodontal disease are aetiological drivers of severity and a 1-month periodontal intervention reduced ulcer recurrence at 6 months, but elective dental treatment should be deferred during active flares (Mumcu 2021).

Clinically: confirm idiopathic RAS by excluding secondary/syndromic causes, treat first-line with a topical corticosteroid (adding LLLT for fast pain relief and correcting any hematinic deficiency), reserve colchicine then thalidomide for severe/refractory disease with full counseling on thalidomide's no-pain-benefit and neurotoxicity/pregnancy risks, and apply a red-flag rule — periodic fever with pharyngitis and cervical adenitis (PFAPA), genital/ocular ulcers or Silk-Route ancestry (Behçet), GI symptoms (IBD), adult-onset, refractory disease, or persistent deficiency — to trigger workup and referral rather than escalating topical therapy blindly.

국소 코르티코스테로이드(트리암시놀론·클로베타솔)가 네 편의 리뷰에서 일치하는 1차 치료이나, 가장 체계적인 치료 종합(D'Amario 2025, 45 RCT)은 비약물(LLLT·오메가-3·천연젤)이 더 나은 위험-이득비를 가지며 LLLT가 즉각 통증완화에 최고(다이오드 1일째 VAS 2.04 vs 4.85)라 논증; 단계 상승은 콜히친 0.5 mg bid → 탈리도마이드이고, 탈리도마이드는 중증·난치 대형 RAS에서 완전반응 RR 6.95·재발간격 +26.91일이나 통증(VAS)은 개선 못 하고(MD −0.64, p=0.26) 부작용 RR 1.87(말초신경병증·혈전), 25 mg/일 최적·임신 절대금기(Jian 2024 SR+MA, 21 RCT n=1,668); PFAPA는 감수성 유전좌위를 베체트·RAS와 공유하는 소아 자기염증 주기발열증후군으로 편도절제가 효과적(Manthiram 2023), 베체트는 구강 미생물불균형·치주질환이 중증도의 병인적 동인이며 1개월 치주치료가 6개월째 궤양 재발을 줄이나 활동기 선택적 치과치료는 연기(Mumcu 2021).

임상: 이차성·증후군성 원인을 배제해 특발성 RAS를 확진하고, 국소 스테로이드로 1차 치료(빠른 통증완화엔 LLLT 병용, 조혈인자 결핍 교정), 중증·난치엔 콜히친 후 탈리도마이드를 통증 무개선·신경독성·임신금기 상담과 함께 예비 사용하며, red-flag 규칙(주기열+인두염+경부림프절염=PFAPA, 생식기·안구 궤양·실크로드 혈통=베체트, 위장증상=IBD, 성인 신규발병, 난치, 지속 결핍)으로 국소치료 맹목적 상승 대신 검사·의뢰를 촉발.

## Summary

A patient sitting in the chair with recurring mouth ulcers poses two nested questions the clinician must answer in order: (1) **is this idiopathic recurrent aphthous stomatitis, and how far up the treatment ladder should I go?** and (2) **is this actually a window onto a systemic or syndromic disease masquerading as ordinary canker sores?** This overview synthesizes 8 wiki papers to answer both. Six papers map idiopathic RAS — its classification, multifactorial pathophysiology, and a topical-to-systemic treatment ladder whose two quantitative anchors are a thalidomide systematic review + meta-analysis and a 45-RCT treatment review. Two more papers (PFAPA, Behçet) were deliberately clustered here because they share the aphthous-ulceration phenotype and overlapping susceptibility biology with idiopathic RAS — **not because they are the same disease** — and they define when question (2) should override question (1). The practical payoff of holding all eight together is a red-flag rule for pivoting from "escalate topical therapy" to "work up and refer."

## RAS: classification & pathophysiology

RAS is common and early-onset: **~20% adult prevalence**, typically beginning in childhood or adolescence, with a frequent family history (Mills 2017). Gasmi 2021 notes higher prevalence in developed countries and a slight female predominance that is more marked in the herpetiform type.

Three morphological types organize management (Manfredini 2021; Gasmi 2021):
- **Minor** — <1 cm, heals within **1–2 weeks without scarring** (the large majority of presentations).
- **Major** — >1 cm, heals slowly and **may scar**.
- **Herpetiform** — crops of multiple small **1–2 mm** ulcers.

Pathogenesis is **multifactorial** — immunological, genetic, nutritional, and stress-related (Mills 2017). The recognized triggers are local trauma, emotional stress, **iron / vitamin B12 / folate deficiency**, and gluten sensitivity (Mills 2017); Gasmi 2021 frames the etiologic spectrum as running from local trauma and oral-microbiota imbalance all the way to systemic disease (Behçet, inflammatory bowel disease, hematinic deficiency). The single most important diagnostic message across the pathophysiology papers is that a thorough history and workup are needed to **separate truly idiopathic RAS from ulcers secondary to systemic disease** before settling on the idiopathic label (Gasmi 2021) — the hinge on which the syndromic-overlap section below turns.

## The treatment ladder: topical → non-pharmacological → systemic

Every treatment paper agrees RAS management is **symptom-directed and recurrence-reducing, not curative** — no held paper claims any therapy achieves complete long-term prevention or corrects the underlying etiology (Belenguer 2014; Manfredini 2021).

### First-line: topical corticosteroids (and adjuncts)

Four reviews converge: **topical corticosteroids (triamcinolone acetonide, clobetasol)** carry the strongest evidence for reducing pain and shortening healing time and are the **gold-standard first-line** (Belenguer 2014; Manfredini 2021; Gasmi 2021; Mills 2017). **Hyaluronic acid** is a first-line adjunct (Manfredini 2021); **chlorhexidine mouthwash** gives a modest reduction in ulcer frequency (Belenguer 2014).

### The non-pharmacological turn (D'Amario 2025)

The most systematic treatment synthesis held — D'Amario 2025, a PRISMA review of **45 RCTs (2013–2023)** using pain (VAS) as the common endpoint — argues **non-pharmacological therapies offer the best risk–benefit ratio, frequently matching or surpassing corticosteroids with fewer adverse effects**. This is an *evolution in emphasis*, not a contradiction of the corticosteroid-first reviews (corticosteroids still work; the claim is about risk–benefit). Key quantified signals:
- **LLLT — the single most promising modality for immediate pain relief.** Diode laser **day-1 VAS 2.04 vs 4.85** for pharmacological comparators (converging near zero by day 7); CO₂ single session **VAS 8.48 → 0.68** immediately post-treatment (p<0.001); 809 nm LLLT **84.7 → 31.5 by day 2** vs near-flat placebo; effective in children with minor RAS (VAS 1 vs 3, p=0.0001).
- **Omega-3** — three 6-month RCTs all beat placebo with no side effects; one test group's VAS fell to **1.48 by 6 months vs ~6.6** for placebo, with reduced recurrence.
- **Natural mucoadhesive gels** — Aloe vera **76.6% complete healing by day 6**; myrrh **76.7% near-painless by day 6**; **curcumin showed no significant difference vs triamcinolone**; silver nitrate gave the fastest symptom resolution (mean 1.16 days).

Belenguer 2014 already flagged LLLT as a safe, effective adjunct especially for frequent recurrences; D'Amario 2025 elevates it from adjunct to leading option for the pain dimension. The main brake is translational: study heterogeneity and a lack of validated commercial formulations for many natural agents.

### Systemic step-up: colchicine, dapsone

For **severe or frequent RAS**, **colchicine 0.5 mg twice daily** has good evidence (Manfredini 2021). Refractory major disease escalates to **dapsone** or **thalidomide** (Manfredini 2021). D'Amario 2025 additionally reports levamisole ± low-dose prednisolone reducing ulcer number, size, pain, and frequency.

### Thalidomide: the severe-refractory reserve (Jian 2024)

The quantitative anchor for systemic therapy is Jian 2024 — the **first SR + meta-analysis** of thalidomide for RAS, pooling **21 RCTs (n=1,668)**:
- **Complete response:** vs placebo **RR 6.95** (3.04–15.92); vs other drug **RR 2.18** (1.38–3.45); combination vs other drug alone **RR 2.44**.
- **Secondary benefits:** recurrence interval **+26.91 days**; healing time **−1.31 days**; ulcer number **−0.98**; ulcer diameter **−0.85**; TNF-α reduced in 3 of 4 studies.
- **The two critical caveats:** overall response vs other drug was **NOT significant** (RR 1.18, p=0.07), and **VAS pain did not improve** (MD −0.64, p=0.26). Thalidomide controls the disease course but not per-episode pain.
- **Harm:** adverse events **RR 1.87** (1.17–2.99) — peripheral neurotoxicity, thrombosis/embolism, constipation, somnolence; withdrawals-for-AE 12 (thalidomide) vs 1 (control).
- **Dosing / safety:** **25 mg/day** is the efficacy–safety sweet spot (200 mg/day → high AEs; 50 mg/day not superior to 25 as maintenance); stop immediately on peripheral-neuropathy symptoms; **absolute contraindication in pregnancy / women of childbearing potential**.
- **Evidence limits:** 17/21 trials from China, mostly high risk of bias, heterogeneity I² up to 99% → reserve agent for **major or topical-refractory RAS**, not first-line.

### The pain vs disease-control split (synthetic insight)

Reading Jian 2024 against D'Amario 2025 exposes a clean inversion the individual papers do not state: **thalidomide improves disease course (recurrence interval, healing, ulcer size) but not pain, while LLLT improves pain immediately but not disease course.** The two therapeutic axes are dissociable, so the choice should follow the patient's dominant complaint — acute pain relief points to topical/LLLT, whereas frequency and severity of a disabling recurrent course point (eventually, and cautiously) toward systemic control.

## Trigger correction & the correctable subset

Cutting across the ladder, every trigger review stresses that **correcting nutritional deficiency resolves a subset of patients** (Mills 2017), making a hematinic screen (iron, B12, folate) and attention to stress and local trauma **integral rather than optional** (Manfredini 2021; Mills 2017). This is the one place idiopathic RAS is potentially *fixable* rather than merely *managed*, and it overlaps with the diagnostic workup that also screens for the systemic causes below.

## Syndromic aphthous-ulceration overlap (distinct from idiopathic RAS)

The two syndromic papers share the aphthous-ulceration **phenotype** and overlapping **susceptibility biology** with idiopathic RAS, but are distinct diseases requiring syndrome-directed care. Holding them beside idiopathic RAS is what makes the red-flag rule possible.

### PFAPA (Manthiram 2023)

**PFAPA — periodic fever, aphthous stomatitis, pharyngitis, cervical adenitis** — is the **most common childhood periodic-fever syndrome**, presenting with regularly timed fevers plus oral aphthae, pharyngitis, or cervical adenitis. Recent genetics found **no causative rare mutations**, reframing it as a **complex polygenic autoinflammatory disorder** — and, decisively for this cluster, its **susceptibility loci are shared with Behçet disease and recurrent aphthous ulcers**. **Tonsillectomy is effective even in patients not meeting full diagnostic criteria**, with a distinct tonsillar immune profile, suggesting the strict diagnostic definition may be too narrow for clinical decision-making. The shared genetics blur the nosological boundary between PFAPA, Behçet, and idiopathic RAS.

### Behçet disease (Mumcu 2021)

**Behçet disease (BD)** is a chronic multisystem inflammatory vasculitis whose most common and usually **initial manifestation is recurrent oro-genital ulceration**; it is most prevalent along the ancient Silk Route (Turkey highest, milder and less prevalent in Korean and Japanese populations). Mumcu 2021 argues oral health is an **active aetiological driver of BD**, not merely a manifestation site:
- **Mechanism:** oral dysbiosis (*Streptococcus sanguinis* / *S. mutans* overgrowth), impaired innate immunity (low serum mannose-binding lectin, neutrophil extracellular traps, TLR2/4 activation), and dysregulated adaptive responses (γδ T cells, IL-1/TNF-α polymorphisms) interacting with periodontal infective foci.
- **Severity link:** poor periodontal status and plaque accumulation predict a more severe course, **especially in males** (5-year mediation analysis).
- **Interventional evidence:** a structured **1-month dental/periodontal treatment → transient trauma-triggered flare in the first 1–2 days → significantly reduced recurrent oral ulcer frequency at 6 months** vs untreated controls, with systemic BD therapy held constant.
- **Chairside rule:** **defer elective dental treatment during active flares** (mechanical trauma from treatment can itself trigger exacerbation via epithelial breach), but treat periodontal disease and reinforce hygiene during quiescence.
- **Pharmacologic ulcer control** (narrative hierarchy): BD-specific Triorasol mouthwash > betamethasone mouthwash; colchicine + prophylactic benzathine penicillin > colchicine alone; azithromycin/minocycline as immunomodulatory adjuncts; apremilast (PDE4 inhibitor) reducing ulcers within the first treatment week.

A subtle counterpoint to the PFAPA "shared loci" theme: Mumcu 2021 reports **site-specific taxa that distinguish BD ulcer sites from RAS ulcer sites** — so predisposition may overlap while the *local* disease expression is microbiologically distinct.

### What the overlap does — and does not — change about first-line RAS care

It **does not** change first-line therapy for the common idiopathic case: the topical-first ladder stands. Tellingly, D'Amario 2025 **explicitly excluded RAS secondary to Behçet's and Crohn's** from its treatment evidence — so the quantified ladder is validated *for idiopathic RAS*, and syndromic disease is treated by **syndrome-directed** means (e.g., colchicine/apremilast and periodontal care in Behçet; tonsillectomy in PFAPA). What the overlap **does** change is the **diagnostic threshold**: the shared phenotype means a fraction of "recurrent canker sores" are the presenting face of a systemic syndrome, and the clinician's job is to recognize the red flags and pivot from escalation to workup/referral.

## Synthesis & Clinical Bottom Line

| Question | Answer | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Is topical corticosteroid still first-line for idiopathic RAS? | Yes — strongest evidence for pain and healing; the agreed gold standard | 4 narrative reviews (Belenguer 2014, Manfredini 2021, Gasmi 2021, Mills 2017) |
| Best option for immediate pain relief? | LLLT — day-1 VAS 2.04 vs 4.85; best risk–benefit of the non-drug options | 45-RCT PRISMA review (D'Amario 2025) |
| How to reduce recurrence without escalating drugs? | Correct hematinic deficiency (iron/B12/folate); consider omega-3 (6-month RCTs) | narrative + 45-RCT review (Mills 2017, D'Amario 2025) |
| When does thalidomide belong? | Major or topical-refractory only; complete-response RR 6.95 but NO pain benefit, AE RR 1.87, 25 mg/day, pregnancy absolutely contraindicated | SR+MA, 21 RCTs (Jian 2024) |
| Does the syndromic overlap change first-line idiopathic RAS therapy? | No — topical-first ladder unchanged; syndromic cases get syndrome-directed care | synthesis (D'Amario excluded Behçet/Crohn) |
| When to suspect a systemic/syndromic cause instead of idiopathic RAS? | Periodic fever + pharyngitis + adenitis (PFAPA); genital/ocular ulcers or Silk-Route ancestry (Behçet); GI symptoms (IBD); adult-onset; refractory; persistent deficiency | narrative (Manthiram 2023, Mumcu 2021, Gasmi 2021, Mills 2017) |
| Dental treatment timing in known Behçet? | Defer elective treatment during active flares (trauma triggers exacerbation); treat periodontal disease during quiescence | narrative review (Mumcu 2021) |

**Decision rule:**
1. **Confirm idiopathic RAS by excluding secondary/syndromic causes first** — history (onset age, periodicity, systemic features) plus a hematinic screen (iron, B12, folate). RAS classically begins in childhood/adolescence, so **adult new-onset aphthae warrant extra scrutiny**.
2. **First-line: a topical corticosteroid** (triamcinolone or clobetasol) ± hyaluronic acid. For fast pain relief, add **LLLT** (day-1 benefit, minimal adverse effects); chlorhexidine gives a modest frequency reduction.
3. **Reduce recurrence:** correct any nutritional deficiency (potentially resolves a subset); consider omega-3 over months for frequency reduction.
4. **Frequent/severe RAS not controlled topically:** step up to **colchicine 0.5 mg bid** (or dapsone).
5. **Major or topical-refractory RAS only:** consider **thalidomide 25 mg/day** — but counsel that it **does not reduce pain**, carries peripheral-neuropathy/thrombosis risk (RR 1.87), must be **stopped immediately on neuropathy symptoms**, and is **absolutely contraindicated in pregnancy / women of childbearing potential**.
6. **RED FLAG — suspect a systemic/syndromic cause and work up / refer (rather than escalating topical therapy) when:** a child has **regularly periodic fever + pharyngitis + cervical adenitis** (PFAPA → pediatrics/rheumatology; tonsillectomy may be curative even short of full criteria); there are **recurrent genital/ocular/skin/vascular lesions or Silk-Route ancestry** (Behçet → rheumatology); there are **GI symptoms** (IBD); the disease is **adult-onset, unusually severe, or topical-refractory**; or there is **persistent hematinic deficiency**.
7. **In diagnosed Behçet:** **defer elective dental treatment during active flares** (mechanical trauma can trigger exacerbation), but **treat periodontal disease and reinforce oral hygiene during quiescence** — a 1-month periodontal intervention reduced recurrent oral ulcer frequency at 6 months.

**Evidence reality check:** the field is dominated by narrative reviews; the ladder's two quantitative pillars are one thalidomide SR+MA (high heterogeneity, China-weighted) and one 45-RCT treatment review (heterogeneous, many agents lacking commercial formulations), and the syndromic papers are narrative reviews. Management remains symptom-directed and recurrence-reducing — not curative.
