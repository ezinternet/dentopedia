## Three-line Summary

Hub over 6 papers on the implant abutment/restoration interface across 4 axes: on **material**, zirconia and titanium abutments are clinically equivalent (Davoudi 2025 umbrella, 14 SRs / 10,063 implants: survival Zr 98.6–98.8% vs Ti 98.62–99.4%, MBL/mechanical complications mostly NS) despite titanium's bench superiority (fracture resistance 1454 N vs 443.6 N), so material choice is esthetics/phenotype-driven (IAO consensus agrees) — but a critical caveat: this parity is about *abutments*, and two-piece zirconia *implant bodies* underperformed in Sterzenbach 2025's RCT (survival 89.3% vs 100% titanium, non-inferiority not confirmed, osseointegration failures), a gap the non-significant p=0.068 obscures.

On **morphology**, transmucosal abutment macro-shape (linear divergent vs concave) had no measurable 1-year peri-implant effect (Barwacz 2024 RCT, all outcomes p>0.1) — the literature's actual risk factor is convex over-contour (Siegenthaler OR 12.6 for recession), and the real soft-tissue levers are keratinized mucosa ≥2 mm and tissue thickness (IAO consensus: FGG for width, CTG for thickness; thickness–MBL link unresolved). On **fit**, crown type/material/fabrication are interchangeable for marginal gap (Dudley 2025 umbrella: CAD-CAM 80.3 vs conventional 88.0 µm, all within the 120 µm limit); the variability clinicians see is a measurement artifact (impression-replica reads +37.86 µm high vs microscopy, p<.001).

On **field trends**, the literature has pivoted titanium → zirconia → hybrid and toward digital workflows (Zhang 2026 bibliometric, 2,051 papers), but the zirconia/digital keyword surge reflects unresolved uncertainty, not consensus. Practical read: for the restorative interface (abutment material, crown fit) the evidence supports flexible, esthetics-led choices, but confidence drops sharply moving from abutment to zirconia implant body and from short- to long-term data. Specialized decisions live in the linked emergence-profile and abutment overviews.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 지대주·보철 계면 4축(재료·형태/emergence·적합도·동향)을 6편으로 종합: 지대주 재료는 지르코니아 ≈ 티타늄(Davoudi umbrella 생존 Zr 98.6–98.8% vs Ti 98.62–99.4%, 대부분 무차이)이나 벤치 강도는 Ti 압도(1454 vs 443.6N), 선택은 심미 주도 — 단 이 동등성은 지대주 한정이고 2편 지르코니아 임플란트체는 열등(Sterzenbach RCT 89.3% vs 100%, p=0.068이 격차 가림).

형태(linear vs concave)는 1년 결과 무차이(Barwacz, 전 지표 p>0.1) — 진짜 위험은 볼록 과윤곽(Siegenthaler OR 12.6), 진짜 레버는 KM ≥2mm·연조직 두께(IAO: FGG 폭·CTG 두께, 두께–MBL 미해결); 보철 적합도는 크라운 종류·재료·제작법 무차이(Dudley CAD-CAM 80.3 vs 통상 88.0µm, 120µm 내), 변동은 측정 artifact(impression replica +37.86µm 과대).

연구동향은 티타늄→지르코니아→하이브리드·디지털 급증(Zhang bibliometric 2,051편)이나 미해결 불확실성 반영. 재료·적합도는 유연·심미 주도 선택 가능하나 지르코니아 임플란트체·장기로 넘어가면 확신 급락. 특화 결정은 emergence-profile·abutment 오버뷰 link-out.

## Summary

The implant restorative interface generates recurring "which is better?" questions — zirconia or titanium abutment, concave or straight emergence, CAD-CAM or conventional crown. This page synthesizes 6 papers to answer them, and the answer is unusually consistent: **at the abutment/crown level, the choices are largely equivalent and should be made on esthetics, phenotype, and workflow — not on a survival or fit advantage that does not exist.** It links out to the specialized emergence-profile and abutment overviews.

Two caveats keep this from being "anything goes": the equivalence is about abutments, not zirconia implant *bodies* (which underperformed in an RCT), and the soft-tissue outcome is driven by keratinized mucosa and tissue thickness, not abutment contour subtleties (though gross convex over-contour is a real risk).

## Key Contributions

### Axis 1 — Abutment material: zirconia ≈ titanium (clinically)

- **Davoudi 2025** (umbrella, 14 SRs, 6,456 patients / 10,063 implants): survival Zr 98.6–98.8% vs Ti 98.62–99.4%; MBL 6/10 reviews no difference; mechanical complication RRs 0.87/0.52 (NS, prevalence <7.9%). Bench gap large (fracture resistance Ti 1454 N vs Zr 443.6 N; flexural 2000 vs 900–1200 MPa) → limit Zr to controlled force/angulation (stock ≤15–20°, CAD/CAM ≤30°).
- **Bressan 2024 (IAO consensus)**: Ti vs Zr no consistent soft-tissue or recession difference; abutment material choice should be **esthetics/phenotype-driven, not evidence-driven**; Ti-vs-Zr crestal bone effect "controversial."
- **Critical caveat — Sterzenbach 2025** (RCT, n=60, 3 yr): hybrid abutment/crown performed comparably (zero crown-attributable failures), but two-piece **zirconia implant bodies** survived 89.3% vs 100% titanium (non-inferiority NOT confirmed; failures were osseointegration, not prosthetic). Do not blur "zirconia abutment ≈ titanium" into "zirconia implant ≈ titanium implant."

### Axis 2 — Morphology / emergence profile & soft tissue

- **Barwacz 2024** (RCT, n=54, anterior maxilla): linear-divergent vs concave transmucosal profile → **no significant 1-year difference** in any outcome (zenith p=0.853, thickness p=0.674, KMW p=0.106, MBL p≈0.77, BOP p=0.917); survival 100%. Morphologic change did not correlate with mucosal thickness/zenith. Implicates **convexity** (not concave-vs-linear) as the real risk (contrast Siegenthaler OR 12.6 for recession).
- **Bressan 2024 (IAO)**: the real soft-tissue levers — KM ≥2 mm (less inflammation/plaque/recession, but no effect on failure/PPD/bone) and tissue thickness (FGG best for width, CTG for thickness; matrices lower morbidity). Thickness–MBL relationship underpowered, **no consensus reached**.

### Axis 3 — Restoration fit / marginal gap (Dudley 2025)

Umbrella, 17 SRs / 301 in-vitro crowns: mean marginal gap 79.4 ± 22.8 µm; **no effect** of crown type (p=.775), material (p=.784), or fabrication (CAD-CAM 80.3 vs conventional 88.0 µm, p=.319) — all within the 120 µm limit. The dominant variable is the **measurement instrument**: impression-replica overvalues gaps by +37.86 µm vs microscopy (p<.001). Reported "fit differences" are largely methodological artifacts.

### Axis 4 — Research-field trends (Zhang 2026)

Bibliometric, 2,051 papers (2005–2024): quadratic growth; material narrative shift titanium (2005–2010) → platform switching/FEA (2011–2019) → zirconia/microbial leakage/stability (2020–2024); digital-workflow surge (CAD-CAM, IOS, additive). The zirconia/digital keyword bursts reflect **unresolved uncertainty**, not established consensus — mapping activity, not effectiveness.

## Methodology

Narrative synthesis (no new pooling). Base: 2 umbrella reviews (Davoudi, Dudley), 1 consensus report (Bressan/IAO), 2 RCTs (Sterzenbach, Barwacz), 1 bibliometric analysis (Zhang). Numbers quoted verbatim from source pages.

## Results

**Chairside interface read:**

1. **Abutment material** — choose zirconia vs titanium on esthetics/phenotype, not survival; limit zirconia to controlled occlusal load/angulation.
2. **Do not extrapolate to zirconia implant bodies** — an RCT showed a clinically meaningful osseointegration disadvantage; a non-significant p-value can hide it.
3. **Emergence profile** — do not over-invest in concave-vs-straight tuning; avoid convex over-contour; invest instead in KM ≥2 mm and soft-tissue thickness (FGG width / CTG thickness).
4. **Crown fit** — crown type/material/CAD-CAM-vs-conventional are interchangeable for marginal gap; distrust cross-study fit comparisons that mix impression-replica and microscopy methods.
5. **Field trends** — zirconia/digital prominence signals open questions and thin long-term data, not settled superiority.
