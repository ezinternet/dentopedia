## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - bone-regeneration·골이식재 관련 논문 총 **29편**(narrative review 8, SR/SR+MA/NMA 15, 동물 2, 전향 1, 후향 1, in-vitro 1, scoping review 1)을 "골이식재 클래스 선택" 단일 축으로 종합. *(2026-07-13 초판 19편; 2026-10-21 골이식재 서베일런스 10편 추가)* 결손 유형별 재료·보조제 선택 프레임.
> - **4대 생물학적 특성 매트릭스가 선택의 뼈대**: 자가골(autograft)만이 골형성(osteogenic)+골유도(osteoinductive)+골전도(osteoconductive)+골유착(osseointegration) 4특성을 모두 가진 유일한 재료이며 복잡 증대의 gold standard — 시판 동종/이종/합성 대체재는 사실상 골전도성만 충족(Zhao 2021, DePace 2025, Bubalo 2026). **Haugen 2026 종설**이 전 스펙트럼(자가골/동종골/이종골/합성골/복합체/CAD-CAM)을 골형성/골유도/골전도 삼각 구도로 재정리하고 임상 적용(치조제 보존·상악동 거상·GBR·치주재생·임플란트주위염)별 권장 재료 매핑 제공.
> - 자가골의 대가는 **공여부 이환율과 흡수**: 하악 상행지 온레이 블록이 12개월에 43.7% 흡수(상악 60.2% > 하악 37.6%, Stricker 2021), narrative review는 자가골 6개월 내 최대 55% 부피 소실 보고(Bubalo 2026) — "gold standard이지만 부피가 사라진다"는 핵심 trade-off.
> - **결손 유형별 알고리즘**(Bubalo 2026 제안): 수평결손 → 이종골/동종골 + 흡수성 막 ± PRF, 수직결손 → 자가골 + 합성골/이종골, 복잡결손 → 3D 프린팅 스캐폴드. 최고 신생골형성은 이종골:자가골 1:1 혼합(65.8%) 보고.
> - **자가골 첨가는 동종골 단독 대비 추가 이득 없음**: 수평 GBR 4 RCT(n=109) SR+MA에서 동종골+자가골 vs 동종골 단독 골획득 차이 무의미(치조정 0mm MD −0.46mm, 4–5mm MD +0.17mm, GRADE very low) — 공여부 이환만 더할 뿐(Meza-Mauricio 2022).
> - **합성골 = 이종골 등가**: 토끼 두개골서 이상성 HA/β-TCP(Straumann Bone Ceramic)와 Bio-Oss 골충전량 유의차 없음, 오히려 대입자 세라믹이 염증·이물거대세포 반응↑(Rokn 2011) — 흡수율↑이 신생골↑로 이어진다는 가설 미확인.
> - **치아유래 자가이식재가 공여부 없는 자가골 대안으로 부상**: 자가치아골이식(Autogenous Tooth Bone Graft, AUTO-BG)은 피질골 유사 특성(BMP·type I 콜라겐·인산칼슘), 치조제폭 +5mm·상악동후 임플란트 생존 96%·GBR 골획득 87% 보고(Janjua 2022); 건전치=근관치료치 동등(4개월 VB% 20.78 vs 22.89, Minetti 2020); 개 실험서 치근유래가 이종골보다 잔존입자 거의 없는 생활골 재생 우세(총골 65.6% vs 57.8%, 잔존 0.5% vs 19.7%), 단 이종골이 협측윤곽 안정엔 근소 우위(Nakajima 2026).
> - **약물·생물학적 보조제는 기껏해야 modest**: 국소 심바스타틴은 치조정골소실↓·골폭↑·골밀도↑·BIC↑ 보고하나 근거 5편·메타분석 불가·ISQ 개선 비일관(Tale 2026); 히알루론산은 하악 제3대구치 발치 7일 통증만 소폭↓(ES 0.32), 골형성 이득은 3개월 BV/TV 메타분석서 미확인(p=0.42, I²=90%), 한 연구는 오히려 수평골소실↑(Domic 2023); 혈소판농축물(APC)은 CGF가 성장인자 방출 최장(28일)이나 근거 narrative·ISQ 효과 상반(Giannotti 2023). **Yang 2025 스티키 본 종설**이 AFG/i-PRF+골 이식재 복합체를 새로운 클래스로 정리: 피브린 네트워크가 부착력·성장인자 지속방출(PDGF/TGF-β/VEGF/IGF 7–14일)·M2 극성화·MSC 모집 제공, 임상 적용(치조제 보존·상악동 거상·주위염 GBR·치주 골내결손) 정리했으나 인체 RCT 제한적.
> - **막은 수동 차단벽이 아니라 능동 조직**: GBR 막이 CD68+ 대식세포·periostin+ 골전구세포를 모집하고 FGF-2/BMP-2 발현 — 하부 결손 골형성 유전자 발현과 상관(Elgali 2017, 저자 자신 rat/인체 연구 기반). GBR은 수평·수직 증대 후 임플란트 생존 ~95%, 골유착 임플란트 최대 40%가 GBR 필요.
> - **이식재는 봉합하는 순간 15~40% 무너진다**: 돼지 사체 수평증대 60건서 이식재 변위가 결손 형태와 무관하게 컸고(−28%대), 4-핀 막고정이 변위를 유의하게 줄임(−15.8% vs −38.1%, p<0.001) — 일차봉합 시 치은변연부 과충전(overcontouring) + 핀고정이 저비용 예측성 향상법(Raabe 2025).
> - **디지털 맞춤화는 유망하나 아직 예비적**: CAD/CAM 3D 프린팅(CBCT→STL→설계→출력) 워크플로우서 티타늄 메쉬 지배적(23편 중 16편), 맞춤형 골이식재/동종골 블록이 기존 블록 대비 골부피↑·흡수↓·수술시간↓ 보고하나 RCT 근거 소수·이질적(Elrefaei 2025); 3D 스캐폴드 설계는 다공성↔강도 상충 균형이 관건이고 혈관화·하중지지·제조표준화는 미해결(Sun 2025).
> - **즉시식립 peri-implant gap(jumping gap)**: RCT 11편서 이종골·합성골이 PRF·자가골보다 협측/변연골 유지 우수, 소켓실드·즉시임시수복은 연조직·심미에 유리, ISQ는 이식재 무관 — 이질성 커 메타분석 불가(Ahamed 2025).
> - **ARP 구성요소 네트워크 메타분석** (Component Network Meta-Analysis, CNMA; Zhang 2026, 31 RCT, 853 부위): 수평 치조제폭 구성요소 효과 — 막 −1.31mm(유의), 동종골 −0.77mm(유의), 혈소판풍부섬유소 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF) −0.40mm(유의); 이종골 −0.57mm·콜라겐 −0.42mm·합성골 +0.01mm는 비유의. 이종골≈합성골 교체가능성 명제를 정밀화 — ARP에서는 동종골이 이종골·합성골보다 수평폭 구성요소 효과 유의. 전반적 비일관성 유의(Q=19.90, p=0.006), 확실성 낮음~매우 낮음.
> - **새로운 흡수성 합성골 클래스 탄산칼슘아파타이트** (Carbonate Apatite, CO3Ap; Nunn 2024, SR 43편): 동물실험서 HA·β-TCP보다 골형성 우세; 인체 상악동 신생골 33.8%(7개월), 3년 임플란트 생존 100%(n=13); 흡수율 14.2% vs 탈단백우골 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 24.2%(소규모 비교). osteoinductive 주장은 동물 데이터만 — RCT 없음. "합성골 = 골전도성만" 명제를 잠재적으로 교란하는 재료로 감시 필요.
> - **동종골 블록이 수평증대의 자가골 블록 대안**: 실패 2.4%, 수평획득 4.79mm, 임플란트 생존 96.9%(Monje 2014, SR 15편); 블록 생존 94.52%, 임플란트 97.36%, 주요 합병증은 노출(Pérez-González 2020, SR 7편, 323 블록, 501 임플란트). 공여부 없이 자가골 블록에 근접하는 임상 결과.
> - **맞춤형 CAD/CAM 스캐폴드 수직증대 결과** (Saleh 2026, SR+MA 13편, 241 환자): 6–9개월에 수직 5.64mm, 수평 4.96mm, 용적 1,307mm³. 합병증 21.7%(조기 24.3%, 후기 16.5%). 경흡연 −2.4mm, 숙련 술자 +2.42mm. GRADE 수직·수평 moderate, 용적·합병증 low. 대부분(9/13편) 맞춤형 티타늄 메쉬.
> - **임상 bottom line**: 재료 선택은 대개 효능이 아니라 부피안정성 목표·비용·가용성·공여부 이환·잔존입자 허용도로 결정된다. 생활골 최대화가 목표면 자가골/치아유래, 부피장기유지가 목표면 저흡수 이종골, 공여부 회피면 동종골/합성골(또는 동종골 블록) — 그리고 어떤 재료든 봉합 시 붕괴를 과충전·막고정으로 방어하라.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 29 bone-regeneration and graft-material papers (8 narrative reviews, 15 SR/SR+MA/NMA, 2 animal, 1 prospective, 1 retrospective, 1 ex-vivo, 1 scoping review; initial 19 from 2026-07-13, 10 added 2026-10-21 bone-graft-material surveillance) organized as a graft-material selection matrix: the four-property biological framework (osteogenic + osteoinductive + osteoconductive + osseointegrative) shows autograft is the only material possessing all four and the gold standard, while marketed allograft/xenograft/alloplast substitutes satisfy essentially only osteoconductivity (Zhao 2021, DePace 2025, Bubalo 2026). **Haugen 2026** provides a comprehensive evidence-based mapping of all graft classes (autograft, allograft, xenograft, alloplast: β-TCP/HA/bioglass, composites, CAD-CAM) to clinical indications (socket preservation, sinus lift, GBR, periodontal, peri-implantitis). **Yang 2025** introduces "sticky bone" (AFG/i-PRF + bone substitutes) as a fibrin-based composite class with sustained growth-factor release and defect adhesion.

Head-to-head evidence collapses many perceived material differences — adding autogenous bone to allograft gives no extra horizontal bone gain (GRADE very low, Meza-Mauricio 2022), biphasic HA/β-TCP ceramic equals bovine xenograft on bone fill (Rokn 2011), and xenograft/alloplast are broadly interchangeable so choice is driven by cost, volume-stability target, residual-particle tolerance, and donor-site morbidity; autologous tooth-derived grafts emerge as a donor-site-free autograft alternative producing near-complete vital bone (Janjua 2022, Minetti 2020, Nakajima 2026), while pharmacologic/biologic adjuncts (simvastatin, hyaluronic acid, platelet concentrates) are modest and inconsistent (Tale 2026, Domic 2023, Giannotti 2023). Sticky bone extends the composite concept with fibrin-matrix handling advantages but human RCT evidence remains limited.

Independent of material, the graft physically collapses 15–40% at primary closure and four-pin membrane fixation significantly reduces displacement (Raabe 2025), the GBR membrane is a biologically active osteoprogenitor-recruiting layer rather than a passive barrier (Elgali 2017), and digital CAD/CAM 3D-printed customization is promising but still preliminary (Elrefaei 2025, Sun 2025).

## 세줄요약

bone-regeneration·골이식재 논문 총 29편(narrative review 8, SR/SR+MA/NMA 15, 동물 2, 전향 1, 후향 1, 사체 1, scoping review 1)을 골이식재 선택 매트릭스로 종합 *(초판 19편 2026-07-13, 서베일런스 10편 추가 2026-10-21)*: 4대 생물학적 특성(골형성+골유도+골전도+골유착) 프레임에서 자가골만이 4특성을 모두 갖춘 유일 재료이자 gold standard이고, 시판 동종/이종/합성 대체재는 사실상 골전도성만 충족(Zhao 2021, DePace 2025, Bubalo 2026). **Haugen 2026 종설**이 전 스펙트럼(자가골/동종골/이종골/합성골/복합체/CAD-CAM)을 골형성/골유도/골전도 삼각 구도로 재정리하고 임상 적용별(치조제 보존·상악동 거상·GBR·치주재생·임플란트주위염) 권장 재료 매핑 제공. **Yang 2025 스티키 본 종설**이 AFG/i-PRF+골 이식재 복합체(피브린 네트워크 부착력·성장인자 지속방출·M2 극성화·MSC 모집)를 새로운 클래스로 정리.

Head-to-head 근거는 재료 간 차이를 상당 부분 무너뜨린다 — 동종골에 자가골 첨가해도 수평 골획득 추가 이득 없고(GRADE very low, Meza-Mauricio 2022), 이상성 HA/β-TCP 세라믹이 우골 이종골과 골충전 동등(Rokn 2011), 이종/합성골이 대체로 호환되므로 선택은 비용·부피안정성 목표·잔존입자 허용도·공여부 이환으로 결정; 치아유래 자가이식재가 공여부 없는 자가골 대안으로 거의 완전한 생활골 형성(Janjua 2022, Minetti 2020, Nakajima 2026), 반면 약물·생물학적 보조제(심바스타틴·히알루론산·혈소판농축물)는 효과가 modest·비일관(Tale 2026, Domic 2023, Giannotti 2023). 스티키 본은 복합체 개념을 피브린 매트릭스 조작성으로 확장하나 인체 RCT 증거 제한적.

재료와 무관하게 이식재는 일차봉합 시 15~40% 물리적으로 붕괴하며 4-핀 막고정이 변위를 유의하게 감소(Raabe 2025), GBR 막은 수동 차단벽이 아니라 골전구세포를 능동 모집하는 생물학적 층(Elgali 2017), 디지털 CAD/CAM 3D 프린팅 맞춤화는 유망하나 아직 예비적(Elrefaei 2025, Sun 2025).

## Summary

This page synthesizes the 19 bone-regeneration papers not yet referenced by any overview into a single decision axis: **how to select a bone graft material**. It is intentionally complementary to three existing pages. [[overviews/bone-regeneration-protocol-ladder]] builds the socket-wall-count decision tree for alveolar ridge preservation; [[overviews/dbbm-bone-substitute-overview]] goes deep on one material (DBBM) and its platelet-concentrate adjuncts; [[overviews/gbr-barrier-membrane-overview]] covers the barrier-membrane axis. This page instead lays out the **material-class taxonomy and the biological-property matrix that drives selection**, then routes it by defect type.

The organizing insight from the material-science reviews is a four-property matrix. Autograft is the only material that is simultaneously osteogenic (contains living osteoblasts/progenitors), osteoinductive (BMPs recruit host cells), osteoconductive (scaffold for ingrowth), and osseointegrative; every marketed allograft, xenograft, and alloplast satisfies essentially only osteoconductivity (Zhao 2021; DePace 2025; Bubalo 2026). **Haugen 2026** extends this framework with a comprehensive evidence-based mapping of all graft classes to specific clinical indications, and **Yang 2025** adds a fibrin-based composite class ("sticky bone") that integrates platelet-concentrate growth-factor kinetics with graft handling. This is why autograft remains the clinical gold standard for complex/large augmentation — and simultaneously why it is used less routinely than its biology would suggest, because harvesting adds donor-site morbidity (>10% donor-site complication rate in the orthopedic literature, DePace 2025) and the harvested block resorbs substantially.

## Key Contributions

### Axis 1 — The four-property biological matrix (the selection backbone)

| Property | Autograft | Allograft (incl. DBM) | Xenograft (DBBM) | Alloplast (HA/β-TCP, bioglass) | Tooth-derived (AUTO-BG) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Osteogenic (living cells) | ✅ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ (acellular, but autologous matrix) |
| Osteoinductive (BMPs) | ✅ | partial (DBM) | ✗ | ✗ | ✅ (retains BMPs, type I collagen) |
| Osteoconductive | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Osseointegrative | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Donor-site morbidity | high | none | none | none | none (uses extracted tooth) |
| Volume stability | poor (resorbs) | moderate | high (slow resorption) | variable | high (near-complete vital replacement) |

Autograft is the only complete-profile material (Zhao 2021, DePace 2025). Deproteinized bovine xenograft reaches ~39% new bone at 6 months vs ~40% for autograft in maxillary sinus grafting, with 95% implant survival at 14 years reported in one longitudinal cohort (Zhao 2021) — i.e. the osteoconductive-only substitutes are clinically adequate for many indications despite the incomplete biological profile.

The cost of autograft's biology is resorption. A retrospective CBCT-volumetric cohort of mandibular ascending-ramus onlay blocks found **43.7% ± 19.0% resorption at 12 months**, significantly worse in the maxilla (60.2%) than the mandible (37.6%, p=0.01); age had no effect and no implants were lost (Stricker 2021). The narrative-review figure of "up to 55% volume loss within 6 months" for autogenous bone (Bubalo 2026) is consistent. This resorption variability (Stricker's 44% vs some prior reports near 12%) is itself a planning hazard.

### Axis 2 — Defect-type-specific selection algorithm

Bubalo 2026 proposes a defect-driven algorithm that this page adopts as the routing frame:

- **Horizontal defect** → xenograft/allograft + resorbable membrane ± PRF.
- **Vertical defect** → autograft + alloplast/xenograft.
- **Complex/large defect** → 3D-printed scaffold.
- Highest reported new-bone formation came from a **1:1 xenograft:autograft mix (65.8%)** (Bubalo 2026) — the mix buys autograft's biology while the xenograft fraction defends volume.

The horizontal-defect branch is directly tested: a SR+MA of 4 RCTs (n=109) found **adding autogenous bone to an allograft gave no additional horizontal bone gain** (crest 0 mm MD −0.46 mm, 95%CI −1.03 to 0.11; 4–5 mm MD +0.17 mm; GRADE very low), with similar resorption, histology, and complications (Meza-Mauricio 2022). Clinically: for horizontal GBR, the autograft addition mostly adds donor-site morbidity without measurable benefit.

### Axis 3 — Tooth-derived autografts: donor-site-free autologous matrix

A distinct emerging class uses the patient's own extracted tooth (ground/demineralized dentin ± enamel) as an autologous graft, sidestepping the donor-site problem while retaining BMPs, type I collagen, and calcium phosphates resembling autogenous cortical bone (Janjua 2022). Reported outcomes: ridge-width gain ~5 mm, ~96% implant survival after sinus augmentation, ~87% bone gain in GBR, pooled implant failure ~2.3–3.9% (Janjua 2022). A prospective multicenter pilot (28 patients, 34 sockets) showed **healthy-tooth vs endodontically-treated-tooth grafts perform equivalently** at 4 months (vital bone 20.78% vs 22.89%, NS; no residual endodontic filling or inflammation) — so root-canal-treated teeth are viable donors (Minetti 2020).

A within-animal Beagle study (23 sockets) sharpened the trade-off vs xenograft: **tooth-root graft gave near-complete vital-bone replacement** (65.6% total bone, only 0.5% residual graft) versus collagenated equine xenograft (57.8% total bone, 19.7% persistent residual particles, p=0.032); the xenograft retained a marginally taller buccal crest (12.3 vs 11.8 mm, p=0.031) and greater peripheral bone extension (Nakajima 2026). Interpretation: tooth-derived grafts favor vital-bone quality with minimal leftover biomaterial; xenografts favor buccal-contour volume stability but leave persistent hybrid particles — the same "vital bone vs volume" tension seen in [[overviews/bone-regeneration-socket-biology-and-arp-critique]]. Limits: no implants placed, single 3-month timepoint, and the chairside grinder/preparation burden plus limited graft yield per tooth keep AUTO-BG a niche option (Janjua 2022).

### Axis 4 — Substitute head-to-heads collapse perceived differences

An animal calvarial study (13 rabbits, 52 defects) found **no significant bone-fill difference** between large/small biphasic HA/β-TCP ceramic (Straumann Bone Ceramic), Bio-Oss xenograft, and empty control at 4/8 weeks — and the large-particle ceramic caused significantly more inflammation and foreign-body giant-cell reaction (Rokn 2011). The hypothesis that a ceramic's faster resorption yields more new bone was not confirmed. Combined with the xenograft≈allograft interchangeability documented in [[overviews/dbbm-bone-substitute-overview]], the practical message is that substitute-class choice is rarely an efficacy decision.

**Within-xenograft class: porcine ≈ bovine (SR+MA, 2025).** A dedicated SR+MA (Wang 2025, 10 studies, 452 sites, PROSPERO CRD42024628683) specifically comparing porcine bone xenograft (PBX) vs bovine bone xenograft (BBX) across MSFA and ARP found no significant difference in NFB (WMD=1.5, 95% CI −1.46–4.46, p=0.321, I²=44.6%), nor in any secondary histomorphometric or radiographic outcome (RBG, CT, VHR, HWR, VR). This SR+MA-level evidence closes the last major within-substitute-class gap: not only is xenograft ≈ allograft ≈ alloplast across classes, but within the xenograft class, the animal origin (bovine vs porcine) is also clinically inconsequential for bone formation. Clinical implication: PBX is a valid BBX alternative — relevant when bovine-derived materials raise cultural or religious concerns. Evidence gap remains: no implant survival or long-term graft resorption data directly comparing PBX vs BBX. See [[bone-regeneration/wang-2025-porcine-bovine-xenograft-msfa-arp-sr-ma]].

**Zhang 2026 CNMA: within alveolar ridge preservation (ARP), membrane and allograft are the dominant components; xenograft and alloplast are non-significant.** A component network meta-analysis (CNMA) of 31 ARP RCTs (853 sites, 21 interventions) decomposed horizontal width preservation into independent component effects: collagen/barrier membrane −1.31 mm (95% CI −2.51 to −0.11, significant); allograft −0.77 mm (−1.42 to −0.11, significant); PRF/platelet concentrate −0.40 mm (−0.78 to −0.02, significant); xenograft −0.57 mm (−1.26 to +0.13, non-significant); collagen alone −0.42 mm (non-significant); alloplast +0.01 mm (null). Network-level inconsistency was significant (global Q=19.90, p=0.006) and certainty was low to very low throughout. This refines the "xeno≈allo≈alloplast interchangeable" head-to-head claim: while the three class substitutability holds in many individual studies, at the CNMA level **only allograft carries a statistically significant horizontal preservation component in ARP**, with xenograft and alloplast falling short of significance. The significant inconsistency means this ordering is not stable across all ARP protocols, and the membrane component is the clearest, largest effect regardless of fill material. See [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/zhang-2026-component-network-meta-analysis-scaffold]].

**Carbonate apatite (CO3Ap) as an emerging third resorbable alloplast profile.** A SR (Nunn 2024, 43 publications: 5 in vitro, 28 animal, 7 human) evaluated CO3Ap (Cytrans Granules). Animal data: more bone formation than HA and β-TCP at equivalent sites. Human sinus data (n=13–27): sinus height loss 16.4–27.8%; new bone 33.8% at 7 months; 3-year implant survival 100% (n=13); resorption 14.2% vs DBBM 24.2% (n=6 vs 8, underpowered). No RCTs. Authors attribute the favourable outcomes to CO3Ap's rapid resorption allowing complete replacement by vital bone — the osteoinductive claim from animal data does not have human confirmation. Clinically: CO3Ap represents a fast-resorbing alloplast profile that may minimise residual-graft burden relative to DBBM, distinct from the slow-resorb (DBBM/HA) and intermediate-resorb (β-TCP/BCP) profiles already in the matrix. It does not conclusively overturn "alloplast = osteoconductivity only," but is the strongest candidate to date for a more nuanced alloplast classification. See [[bone-regeneration/nunn-2024-novel-alloplast-carbonate-apatite-granules]].

**Allogeneic blocks for major horizontal/vertical augmentation: a credible donor-site-free block alternative.** Two systematic reviews consolidate the evidence base for cortical/cancellous allograft block placement. Monje 2014 (15 prospective studies, 361 blocks, mostly atrophic maxilla) found block failure 2.4% (predominantly early exposure), horizontal gain 4.79 mm, implant survival 96.9% (228 implants, 1.4–5.2 years follow-up). Pérez-González 2020 (7 non-randomised studies, 323 blocks, 501 implants) reported block survival 94.52%, implant survival 97.36%; exposure was the most common complication; new bone 31–42%, residual graft 14–40%. These outcomes are comparable to autogenous-block benchmarks without donor-site morbidity, extending substitute interchangeability to the block form factor. β-TCP in intrabony periodontal defects (Jasser 2021, 5 studies, n=small) also demonstrates substitute-class reach into periodontal applications: better than debridement alone (PD 6.20 vs 2.95 mm, CAL 5.80 vs 2.30 mm) but only one randomised trial — evidence very weak. Xenograft with collagen membrane after mandibular third-molar removal (Toledano-Serrabona 2021, 3 split-mouth RCTs) produced PPD MD 2.36 mm and CAL MD 2.52 mm at the distal second molar, with high heterogeneity (I² 95–97%) and no bone-level difference. See [[bone-regeneration/monje-2014-feasibility-utilizing-allogeneic-bone-blocks]], [[bone-regeneration/perez-gonzalez-2020-assessment-clinical-outcomes-histomorphometric-findings]], [[periodontics/jasser-2021-effectiveness-beta-tricalcium-phosphate-comparison]], [[oral-surgery/third-molar/toledano-serrabona-2021-xenograft-periodontal-defects-mandibular-third]].

### Axis 5 — Pharmacologic / biologic adjuncts are modest and inconsistent

- **Local simvastatin** (SR, 5 studies, no meta-analysis): reduced peri-implant crestal bone loss (0.975 vs 1.356 mm, P<0.0001), increased bone width (5.55 vs 3.36 mm) and density, and raised BIC in animals (84.3% vs 59.5%) — low-cost and biologically plausible, but 5 small heterogeneous studies and no consistent ISQ benefit (Tale 2026).
- **Hyaluronic acid** (SR+MA, 5 preclinical + 22 clinical, n=1062): significantly reduced pain 7 days after lower-third-molar surgery (ES 0.32, 95%CI 0.12–0.51) but no effect on early pain/swelling/trismus, and the preclinical bone-formation benefit was **not** confirmed at 3-month BV/TV meta-analysis (p=0.42, I²=89.9%); one study reported MORE coronal horizontal bone loss with HyA (Domic 2023). HyA is a soft-tissue/analgesia adjunct, not a ridge-preservation material.
- **Autologous platelet concentrates** (narrative review): growth-factor concentration and release duration rise across generations (PRP releases within 1 h; PRF 7–10 d; A-PRF up to 14 d; **CGF up to 28 d**, with CD34+/CD45+/CD105+ progenitor-like cells); CGF is framed as the most effective current APC, but the evidence is narrative and CGF implant-stability findings conflict (Giannotti 2023). **Yang 2025** extends the APC concept into a fibrin-matrix composite class ("sticky bone"): AFG or i-PRF mixed with particulate grafts yields a viscoelastic scaffold with defect adhesion, sustained growth-factor release (PDGF, TGF-β, VEGF, IGF over 7–14 days), M2 macrophage polarization, and MSC recruitment — clinical applications span socket preservation, sinus elevation, peri-implant GBR, and periodontal defects, but human RCT evidence remains limited. This aligns with the [[overviews/dbbm-bone-substitute-overview]] finding that platelet concentrates improve histomorphometry but not ISQ/survival, and with [[overviews/periodontal-regenerative-platelet-concentrates-overview]].

### Axis 6 — Material-independent mechanics: the graft collapses, and the membrane is active

Two papers show that graft outcome depends on physics and membrane biology as much as material chemistry:

- **Graft displacement at closure**: an ex-vivo study (60 horizontal augmentations on 30 pig hemimandibles) found graft-material displacement at the implant platform was large regardless of defect morphology (partially-contained −28.3% vs contained −28.6%, NS), but **four-pin membrane fixation cut displacement roughly in half** (−15.8% with pins vs −38.1% without, p<0.001), worst in the central-crestal zone (Raabe 2025). Practical rule: expect ~15–40% crestal collapse during primary closure, over-contour to compensate, and pin-fix the membrane.
- **The active membrane**: GBR membranes are not passive barriers — they actively recruit CD68+ monocytes/macrophages and periostin+ osteoprogenitors, express FGF-2/BMP-2, and their molecular activity correlates with coupled bone-formation genes (osteocalcin, cathepsin K, RANKL, CXCR4, MCP-1) in the underlying defect; GBR achieves ~95% implant survival after horizontal/vertical augmentation, and up to 40% of osseointegrated implants need GBR (Elgali 2017). Optimal membrane porosity remains unresolved. See [[overviews/gbr-barrier-membrane-overview]] and [[overviews/gbr-barrier-membrane-exposure-axis]].

### Axis 7 — Digital / 3D-printed customization: promising, still preliminary

A PRISMA-ScR scoping review (23 clinical studies, 9 RCTs) mapped the CAD/CAM workflow (CBCT→DICOM→STL→CAD→3D print). Titanium mesh dominates (16/23 studies, ~0.5 mm, DMLS/SLM/milling); one RCT (Kim, n=60) showed higher % bone volume with a 3D-printed customized graft vs conventional block at 5 months, and another (Wang, n=24) showed customized allogeneic blocks gave greater horizontal gain, less resorption, and shorter operative time than autogenous blocks — but the RCT base is small and heterogeneous, no meta-analysis possible (Elrefaei 2025). The general orthopedic scaffold literature (narrative) frames the core design tension: large pores/high porosity favor cell ingrowth and angiogenesis but reduce mechanical strength (target ~7–30 GPa modulus, 50–200 MPa compressive to approximate cortical bone), and composite/coated scaffolds (Ti+SiHA+VEGF, PCL/β-TCP+BMP-2) outperform single-material ones preclinically — vascularization, load-bearing stability, and manufacturing scalability remain unresolved (Sun 2025). Treat specific numeric benefits as preliminary pending protocol standardization; this connects to [[overviews/vertical-ridge-augmentation-overview]].

The most recent outcome data for CAD/CAM customized scaffolds in clinical use comes from Saleh 2026 (SR+MA, 13 studies including 6 RCTs, 241 patients, horizontal and/or vertical ridge augmentation). At 6–9 months: horizontal gain 4.96 mm (95% CI 3.88–6.05), vertical gain 5.64 mm (95% CI 3.93–7.36), volumetric gain 1,307 mm³. Overall complication rate 21.7% (early membrane exposure / wound dehiscence 24.3%; late implant/scaffold failure 16.5%). Key effect modifiers: light smoking reduced gain approximately −2.4 mm; expert surgeon status added +2.42 mm. GRADE: moderate confidence for linear dimensional gain, low for volumetric gain and complications. Notably 9 of 13 studies used customized titanium mesh as the "scaffold," not a resorbable printed structure — so these outcomes primarily represent the titanium-mesh-dominated subset of the CAD/CAM space. The Elrefaei 2025 scoping review data (23 studies, 9 RCTs) and Saleh 2026 SR+MA together anchor the best current dimensional estimates for this approach. See [[implants/vertical-ridge-augmentation/saleh-2026-efficacy-engineered-scaffolds-horizontal-vertical]].

### Axis 8 — Special case: the immediate-implant peri-implant gap

For the "jumping gap" after immediate placement, a SR of 11 RCTs found xenografts and alloplastic grafts consistently outperformed PRF and autogenous bone in preserving buccal/marginal bone; socket-shield and immediate provisionalization favored soft-tissue/esthetic preservation; implant stability (ISQ) generally did not differ by graft material; allograft was essentially untested (1/11 trials). High heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis/GRADE (Ahamed 2025). This is the gap-grafting counterpart to [[overviews/immediate-implant-soft-tissue-gap-grafting-material-choice]].

## Methodology

Selection axis chosen because all 29 papers share the underlying question "which graft material/adjunct, for which defect." Papers were grouped by evidence weight (SR/SR+MA for efficacy claims; narrative reviews for the biological-property framework and taxonomy; animal/ex-vivo for mechanism and displacement physics; scoping review for the digital workflow map; comprehensive reviews for full-spectrum mapping). Where numbers are cited, effect sizes and confidence intervals are carried through from each source page's Three-line Summary. Evidence caveats: the four-property matrix and defect algorithm rest on narrative reviews (selection-bias risk, no pooled effects); the two strongest quantitative signals are the null autograft-addition SR+MA (Meza-Mauricio 2022) and the HyA SR+MA (Domic 2023). Per Rule #1, all claims are grounded in wiki pages we hold; no web search was used.

## Clinical Bottom Line

Graft-material selection is, for most routine indications, **not an efficacy decision** — substitutes are broadly interchangeable and adjuncts are modest. Decide by four practical levers:

1. **Goal = maximal vital bone / minimal residual particle** → autograft or tooth-derived graft (near-complete vital replacement, Nakajima 2026; Minetti 2020).
2. **Goal = long-term volume/contour stability** → slow-resorbing xenograft (accepting persistent particles and the long-term-safety caveat in [[overviews/dbbm-bone-substitute-overview]]).
3. **Goal = avoid donor-site morbidity** → allograft/alloplast (equivalent to xenograft on efficacy, Rokn 2011; Meza-Mauricio 2022) or tooth-derived autograft when a suitable extracted tooth exists.
4. **Any material** → defend against the 15–40% closure collapse: over-contour + four-pin membrane fixation (Raabe 2025), and remember the membrane is an active osteogenic participant, not just a wall (Elgali 2017).

Adjuncts (simvastatin, hyaluronic acid, platelet concentrates) may be added at low cost but should not be relied on to change the hard-tissue outcome, and none reliably improves implant stability. Final clinical judgment rests with the operator.
