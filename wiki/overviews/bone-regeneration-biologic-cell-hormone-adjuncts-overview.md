## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 결론: 이식재에 무언가를 더하는 보조제 중 사람에서 정량 이득이 확인된 것은 생물학적 제제(혈소판 농축물, 골형성단백질-2 (recombinant human Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2, rhBMP-2))뿐이고, 그 크기도 1 mm 미만이다. 나머지는 전임상이거나 근거가 약하다. [확인]
> - 기준점(Monje 2026 SR+MA, RCT 23편, 1,078명): 치조제 증대에서 폭 +0.30 mm(I² 0%), 발치와 보존에서 폭 +0.48 mm·높이 +0.40 mm였다. rhBMP-2는 발치와 폭 +1.08 mm로 가장 컸지만 I² 84.6%다. [확인]
> - 상악동 거상에서는 높이 이득이 없었다(+0.32 mm, p=0.878). 대신 잔존 이식재가 줄었다(WMD −5.54). 즉 상악동에서 보조제는 "더 많은 골"이 아니라 "더 빠른 치환"이다. [확인]
> - 세포 치료: 협부 지방체 유래 줄기세포 (Buccal Fat Pad-derived Stem Cells, BFPSCs)는 6편·49명에서 모두 좋은 결과를 보고했지만 5/6이 심각한 비뚤림 위험이다(De 2026). Monje 2026도 세포 치료는 메타분석이 불가능해 우월성을 검증하지 못했다. [확인]
> - 자가골 대체 축: 소아 구순구개열 치조열 재건에서 rhBMP-2는 장골 이식과 골형성이 비슷하거나 낫고(12편 중 4편 우위, 7편 차이 없음), 수술 시간·입원이 짧았다(Chou 2026). P-15 펩타이드 이식재는 치주 결손 충전 72.3% vs 탈회동결건조골 (Demineralized Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft, DFDBA) 51.4%였으나 제조사 후원 비중이 크다(Spanninga 2025). [확인]
> - L-PRF 블록(Dhondt 2026, 분할구강 RCT 6명): 수직 골증대에서 자가골+DBBM 혼합과 수직 높이 증가가 차이 없었다(부하 1년 3.0 vs 3.8 mm, P=.96). 6명이라 동등성 증명은 아니다. [확인]
> - 호르몬·멜라토닌(Ahmadpour 2025 SR 2편): 국소 성장호르몬 87.5%, 부갑상선호르몬 (Parathyroid Hormone, PTH) 66.7%, 칼시토닌 60%, 멜라토닌 91.7%의 연구가 대조보다 좋았다고 했지만, 효과 크기 없는 투표 집계이고 호르몬 SR은 전부 전임상이다. [확인]
> - 물리·재료 자극: 광생물조절 (Photobiomodulation, PBM)은 660–1064 nm, 약 2–6 J/cm²에서 줄기세포 골분화를 일관되게 올렸지만 시험관 연구만 있다(Hakimiha 2025). 주입형 나노섬유 젤은 토끼 5주 BIC 58.7 vs 50.5%였다(Yaylaci 2026). [확인]
> - 남은 장벽(Huang 2024): 1 cm 이상 결손에서 지지체의 혈관화 실패, 환자 맞춤 3D 프린팅의 규제 경로 미성숙. [확인]
> - 임상 takeaway: 보조제는 결손이 어려워서가 아니라 공여부 이환을 피하거나 치환을 앞당기려 할 때 고려한다. 1 mm 미만 이득을 위해 비용·시간을 크게 쓸 이유는 약하고, 상악동 높이를 위해 쓸 근거는 없다. [미검증]

## Three-line Summary

Among adjuncts added to a bone graft, only biologics (platelet concentrates and rhBMP-2) have pooled human evidence of a gain, and that gain is under one millimetre in ridge augmentation and ridge preservation and absent for sinus height.
Cell therapy, local hormones, melatonin, photobiomodulation and new injectable materials are supported by uncontrolled series, vote counts or preclinical work only.
The clearer case for some adjuncts is replacing autograft to avoid donor-site morbidity, where rhBMP-2, P-15 peptide graft and an L-PRF block perform comparably in small or industry-heavy evidence.

## 세줄요약

이식재에 더하는 보조제 가운데 사람에서 통합된 이득 근거가 있는 것은 생물학적 제제(혈소판 농축물, rhBMP-2)뿐이고, 그 이득은 치조제 증대와 발치와 보존에서 1 mm 미만이며 상악동 높이에서는 없다.
세포 치료, 국소 호르몬, 멜라토닌, 광생물조절, 새 주입형 재료는 대조 없는 증례군, 투표 집계, 전임상 연구로만 뒷받침된다.
일부 보조제의 더 분명한 쓰임은 공여부 이환을 피하려 자가골을 대체하는 것이고, 여기서 rhBMP-2, P-15 펩타이드 이식재, L-PRF 블록은 소규모이거나 제조사 비중이 큰 근거에서 비슷한 성적을 보인다.

## 1. The anchor — Monje 2026 pooled effect sizes

[[bone-regeneration/monje-2026-use-of-cells-and-or]] is the only held SR+MA that pools biologic and cell adjuncts across all three implant-site procedures (56 studies qualitative, 23 RCTs / 1,078 patients meta-analysed). Every other paper in this overview should be read against its numbers.

| Procedure | Outcome | Adjunct vs scaffold alone | Heterogeneity | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ridge augmentation (6 RCTs, n=172) | Width | WMD +0.30 mm, p<0.001 | I² 0% | Small, consistent |
| | Width, PRP subset | +0.52 mm, p=0.015 | I² 11% | Small, consistent |
| | Height | +0.39 mm, p=0.155 | — | Not significant |
| Ridge preservation (9 RCTs, n=455) | Width | +0.48 mm, p=0.035 | I² 74% | Small, inconsistent |
| | Height | +0.40 mm, p<0.001 | I² 5% | Small, consistent; Egger p=0.010 (publication bias) |
| | Width, rhBMP-2 | +1.08 mm, p=0.02 | I² 84.6% | Largest single effect, least consistent |
| | Non-mineralized tissue | Less, p=0.009 | — | Quality of fill improves |
| Sinus floor elevation (8 RCTs, n=451) | Height | +0.32 mm, p=0.878 | I² 87% | **No dimensional benefit** |
| | Residual graft | WMD −5.54, p<0.001 | — | Faster replacement |

Three readings follow:
- **The effect is sub-millimetre everywhere it exists.** Width and height gains of 0.3–0.5 mm are statistically real but close to CBCT measurement error.
- **The sinus is different.** Adjuncts do not make the graft taller; they make it turn over faster. This matches the platelet-concentrate finding in [[overviews/dbbm-platelet-adjuncts-bcp-bmp2-overview]] that histomorphometric gains do not carry to ISQ or survival.
- **Cell therapy is untested at this level.** Monje could not meta-analyse it, so no pooled claim about stem cells exists in this wiki.

All participants were systemically healthy, so these numbers say nothing about diabetic, irradiated or bisphosphonate patients, the groups where an adjunct might matter most.

## 2. Cell therapy — favourable but uncontrolled

[[bone-regeneration/de-2026-are-buccal-fat-pad-derived]] is the only held SR on a specific cell source. Six human studies (49 patients; 30 BFPSC, 19 conventional graft) all reported favourable clinical and radiographic results at a mean 15 months, and four added histology showing new bone. But ROBINS-I rated 5 of 6 at serious risk of bias, and the Oxford CEBM grades were 2b ×1, 3b ×3 and 4 ×2.

The argument for buccal fat pad cells is mostly about the harvest, not the bone: fat-pad harvest is intraoral and appears to cause less pain, a shorter stay and less donor morbidity than a bone harvest. That is a reasonable hypothesis, but no held trial randomises it.

The lab side is consistent but does not get us further: [[bone-biology/hakimiha-2025-photobiomodulation-enhances-osteogenesis-in-oral]] shows that stem cells from the PDL, pulp and fat respond to light with osteogenic differentiation, which tells us these cells are responsive, not that implanting them works.

## 3. Autograft replacement — comparable, with caveats

Several adjuncts are best understood as ways to avoid a second surgical site rather than to grow more bone.

| Adjunct | Study | Setting | Result vs autograft or standard | Main caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| rhBMP-2 + allograft | [[bone-regeneration/chou-2026-recombinant-human-bone-morphogenetic-protein]] (SR, 21 studies) | Pediatric craniofacial, mostly alveolar cleft | Bone formation: 4/12 favour rhBMP-2, 1 favours iliac crest, 7 no difference; shorter surgery in 5/5 and stay in 7/8; complications favour iliac crest in 5/12 | 18 of 21 studies are one procedure; no long-term pediatric safety data |
| ABM/P-15 peptide graft | [[bone-regeneration/spanninga-2025-clinical-efficacy-and-safety-of]] (SR, 28 studies) | Periodontal defects, spine, trauma | Periodontal fill 72.3% vs DFDBA 51.4% vs debridement 40.3%; cervical fusion non-inferior to autograft; no product-related adverse events | Most studies part-funded by the manufacturer; cervical data from one trial |
| L-PRF bone block | [[implants/vertical-ridge-augmentation/dhondt-2026-vertical-guided-bone-regeneration-leukocyte]] (split-mouth RCT, 6 pts) | Vertical GBR with d-PTFE | Vertical gain 3.0 vs 3.8 mm one year after loading (P=.96) vs 50:50 autograft + DBBM; one implant lost to infection in the L-PRF arm | Six patients — feasibility, not equivalence |
| BFPSCs | [[bone-regeneration/de-2026-are-buccal-fat-pad-derived]] | Adult maxillary defects | All favourable | Serious risk of bias in 5/6 |

The pattern is the same in each row: "no worse than autograft" with less donor morbidity, from evidence that is small, single-procedure or industry-heavy. None of these shows more bone than autograft.

## 4. Local hormones and melatonin — vote counts only

[[bone-regeneration/ahmadpour-2025-local-delivery-of-various-hormonal]] (22 studies, all animal or in vitro) and [[bone-regeneration/ahmadpour-2025-local-delivery-of-melatonin-along]] (24 studies, animal, human and in vitro) report the share of studies that beat their controls:

| Agent | Share of studies favouring it | Human data |
|---|---|---|
| Melatonin | 91.7% | Some, not separated in the abstract |
| Growth hormone | 87.5% | None |
| PTH | 66.7% | None |
| Calcitonin | 60% | None |

Vote counting ignores effect size and study quality, and positive studies are more likely to be published, so a high percentage is weak evidence. The proposed mechanisms (Wnt, BMP/Smad, PI3K/AKT and others) are the same pathways cited for photobiomodulation in §2, which makes them plausible but not specific. No clinical dose, carrier or protocol can be taken from these reviews.

## 5. Scaffolds and new materials — the engineering ceiling

[[bone-regeneration/huang-2024-biomaterial-scaffolds-maxillofacial-bone-te]] maps scaffold design along two axes: physical design (pores of 200–500 µm, interconnectivity, matching stiffness) and biological loading (BMP-2, VEGF, marrow and fat-derived stem cells). It names two unsolved problems: scaffolds fail in defects larger than about 1 cm without pre-vascularisation, and patient-specific 3D printing has no mature regulatory path.

[[implants/surface/yaylaci-2026-injectable-nanofiber-gel-enhances-osseointegration]] is an example of the newest layer: a self-assembling peptide gel injected into the osteotomy raised 5-week BIC (58.7% vs 50.5%), BV/TV (51.8% vs 42.6%) and reverse torque (53.2 vs 41.8 N·cm) over plain SLA in rabbits. Its claimed repair of drilling microdamage was a design goal, not a measured outcome.

## Clinical decision points

1. **Ridge augmentation or ridge preservation in a healthy patient**: an adjunct may add about 0.3–0.5 mm. Use it if it is cheap and easy (for example, chairside PRF); do not plan a case around it.
2. **Sinus floor elevation**: do not expect extra height from an adjunct. If the reason is faster graft replacement before loading, Monje 2026 supports that, but faster replacement has not been shown to improve implant outcomes.
3. **Avoiding a harvest**: rhBMP-2 (strongest in pediatric clefts), P-15 graft or an L-PRF block are options when donor morbidity is the main concern. Tell the patient the evidence shows "comparable", not "better".
4. **Stem cells, hormones, melatonin, light therapy, new gels**: research use only. No held paper gives a clinical protocol.
5. **Medically compromised patients**: no held pooled data. This is the largest gap: the groups where adjuncts might matter most were excluded from Monje 2026.

## Gaps and update triggers

- An RCT of an adjunct in diabetic, irradiated or antiresorptive-treated patients.
- A controlled trial of stem-cell therapy with a pooled effect size.
- Any adjunct trial reporting implant survival or marginal bone level, not only graft dimensions or histology.
- An independent (non-manufacturer) RCT of P-15 in alveolar ridge procedures.
