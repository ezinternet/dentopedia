## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **범주 구조**: 방사선학(Radiology) 53편은 5개 주제군으로 정리된다 — ① 선량·최적화, ② 콘빔CT(Cone-Beam CT, CBCT) 진단 성능, ③ 이미지 아티팩트·품질, ④ 해부학적 변이(하악관·영양관·비구개관), ⑤ 방사선 감별진단. 각 주제군에 별도 overview가 있으며, 본 페이지는 그 overview들을 위한 한 단계 위의 지도다.
> - **선량 의사결정 핵심**(→ `dental-imaging-radiation-dose-optimization-overview`): Modality 위계(구내 ~1.3 µSv ≪ 파노라마 ~18 µSv ≪ CBCT ~121 µSv)가 단일 최대 선량 절감 레버. 이후 FOV·kVp/mAs·복셀·빔높이 4개 조절 레버. 갑상선·생식선 접촉 차폐 중단 권고(Benavides 2023 합의).
> - **CBCT 적응증 결정 원칙**(→ `cbct-diagnostic-performance-overview`): ALADAIP — "2D로 충분하면 2D". 외흡수 진단 민감도 78.1%·특이도 79.3%(Baena 2022 SR+MA, 17편), 근관치료 계획 변경 24–56%(Tay 2022 SR). routine follow-up CBCT는 불필요.
> - **아티팩트 인식**(→ `dental-radiographic-artifacts-taxonomy-overview`): 해석적 광학 아티팩트(삼각형 방사선투과상 Triangular-Shaped Radiolucency, TSR·마하밴드·치경부 소각)가 위양성 우식 ~13%(Dioguardi 2025 SR+MA); 파노라마 오류율 77.2%(Lingam 2023, n=2,629).
> - **해부학 변이 — 이분하악관**(본 페이지 신규 종합): CT/CBCT 촬영 환자 약 20.7%에서 이분 하악관(Bifid Mandibular Canal, BMC) 관찰(Aung 2023 SR+MA, 40편, n=17,714) — 임플란트 계획·하치조신경차단(Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 마취 실패의 해부학적 원인.
> - **해부학 변이 — 영양관**(본 페이지 신규 종합): 하악 전치부 치근단방사선(IOPAR) 영양관(Nutrient Canal, NC) 출현이 당뇨병(DM 84%), 고혈압(HTN 66%), 치주염(52%) 군에서 건강 대조군(20%)보다 유의하게 높음(Hasan 2022) — 미진단 전신질환 스크리닝 단서.
> - **해부학 변이 — 비구개관**(본 페이지 신규 종합): 비구개관(NasopalatineCanal, NPC) 길이 평균 9.64 mm, 남성에서 더 길고 넓음; 나이 들수록 내외측 직경 증가(Yadav 2024, n=102 CBCT). 전치부 임플란트 계획 시 필수 파악.
> - **PDL 확장 감별진단**(본 페이지 신규 종합): 정상 치주인대(PDL) 폭 ~0.15–0.21 mm. 전반적·대칭 확장 = 전신질환(피부경화증 Scleroderma)·기능 원인, 국소 확장+골파괴 = 악성종양(골육종 Osteosarcoma·림프종·전이) 의심 → 즉시 의뢰(Mortazavi 2016).
> - **파노라마 품질**: 진단 불가 영상 15%로 권고 기준(≤10%) 초과, 오류의 원인은 기기가 아닌 위치 교육(positioning training) 부재(Lingam 2023). 아동 유령상(Ghost Image)의 가장 흔한 원인 = 갑상선 보호대가 X선원과 회전 중심 사이 위치.
> - **핵심 임상 결정 지도**: 하악 수술·임플란트 계획 전 CBCT → BMC·NPC·하악관 변이 필수 확인. 구내방사선 전치부에서 NC 증가 → 혈당·혈압 검사 의뢰 고려. 상악 대구치 TSR → 마스킹 테스트 후 우식 가능성 감별.
> - **근거 한계**: 해부학 변이 연구 대부분 단면(cross-sectional)·단일기관, BMC 이질성 I²≈99%. 선량 최적화에 SR+MA 부재 — 레버 효과 크기는 주로 phantom/몬테카를로 기반.

## Three-line Summary

The 53-paper radiology category spans five thematic clusters — dose optimization, CBCT diagnostic performance, image artifacts, anatomical variants, and radiographic differential diagnosis — each with its own topic overview; bifid mandibular canal (BMC) prevalence is 20.7% on CT/CBCT imaging (Aung 2023 SR+MA, 40 studies, 17,714 patients), nutrient canal frequency on intraoral periapical radiographs rises sharply in diabetes (84%), hypertension (66%), and periodontitis (52%) vs healthy controls (20%) (Hasan 2022), and nasopalatine canal dimensions are sex- and age-dependent with clinically relevant variation (Yadav 2024 CBCT, n=102).

Panoramic image quality audits reveal a 15% diagnostically unacceptable rate far exceeding the ≤10% benchmark (Lingam 2023, n=2,629), driven by positioning rather than hardware; interpretive artifacts (triangular-shaped radiolucencies, Mach band, cervical burnout) cause ~13% false-positive caries readings (Dioguardi 2025 SR+MA); and dose optimization favors the modality hierarchy (intraoral ≪ panoramic ≪ CBCT) as the dominant lever, with the paradigm now shifting away from thyroid/gonad contact shielding (Benavides 2023 consensus) and toward non-ionizing alternatives for pediatric cases (0.55T low-field MRI, Willershausen 2025).

Across all clusters the unifying principle is justification-first imaging: periodontal ligament widening requires mandatory malignancy exclusion before attributing to occlusal trauma (Mortazavi 2016); mandibular canal anatomy variants (BMC, nutrient canals) demand pre-surgical CBCT to avoid hemorrhage and anesthesia failure; and ALADAIP governs modality escalation from 2D to 3D.

## 세줄요약

방사선학 53편은 5개 주제군 — 선량 최적화, CBCT 진단 성능, 이미지 아티팩트, 해부학적 변이, 방사선 감별진단 — 으로 구성되며 각 주제군에 별도 overview가 있다; 이분하악관(Bifid Mandibular Canal, BMC) 유병률은 CT/CBCT 환자 20.7%(Aung 2023 SR+MA, 40편, n=17,714), 영양관(Nutrient Canal, NC)은 당뇨(84%)·고혈압(66%)·치주염(52%) 군에서 건강 대조군(20%) 대비 유의하게 증가(Hasan 2022), 비구개관(Nasopalatine Canal, NPC) 치수는 성별·연령 의존(Yadav 2024, n=102).

파노라마 진단 불가율 15%(권고 ≤10% 초과; Lingam 2023, n=2,629) — 원인은 위치 교육 부재; 해석적 아티팩트(삼각형 방사선투과상·마하밴드·치경부 소각)가 위양성 우식 ~13%(Dioguardi 2025 SR+MA); 선량 최적화는 modality 위계가 최대 레버이며 갑상선·생식선 접촉 차폐 중단 권고(Benavides 2023 합의).

통합 원칙 = 정당화 우선 영상: PDL 확장은 악성종양부터 배제(Mortazavi 2016), 하악 수술 전 CBCT로 BMC·변이 확인, ALADAIP로 2D→3D 단계적 격상.

## Anatomical Variants — New Synthesis

### 1. Mandibular Canal Variants

The mandibular canal (MC) houses the inferior alveolar neurovascular bundle (IANVB). Variants that split or diverge create practical surgical risk, anesthesia failure, and implant placement hazard. The wiki holds papers covering three variant types plus nutrient/accessory canals in the adjacent region.

**Bifid Mandibular Canal (BMC)** — [[radiology/aung-2023-bifid-mandibular-canal-proportional]]

Aung 2023 (SR+MA, 40 CT/CBCT studies, 17,714 patients / 31,973 hemi-mandibles) is the largest pooled analysis:

| Metric | Pooled value |
|---|---|
| Patient-level BMC prevalence | 20.7% (95% CI 15.9–26%) |
| Hemi-mandible-level prevalence | 14.3% (95% CI 10.7–18.3%) |
| Bilateral in BMC-positive patients | 22.8% |
| Mean accessory-canal length | 12.14 mm |
| Mean accessory-canal diameter | 1.54 mm |

Prevalence is significantly higher in **males** and on the **right side**. Classification system strongly affects prevalence: Naitoh's (includes buccofacial/lingual variant) yields 16.9% vs Langlais's 1.6% at hemi-mandible level — much literature variance is definitional, not population variation. Methodological quality is moderate (JBI 54.69%) and publication bias is detected.

[[radiology/menditti-2025-real-anatomy-mandibular-canal]] provides a narrative taxonomy: BMC, trifid MC (TMC), intramandibular accessory canals, and dental canals are true MC variants; extramandibular "nutrient function" canals (prevalence up to ~95% in one cadaver study) are distinct and not connected to the IAN. CBCT — not panoramic radiography — is essential for pre-surgical mapping. [[radiology/kuribayashi-2010-bifid-mandibular-canals-cone]], [[radiology/eshak-2014-cone-beam-ct-evaluation-presence]], [[radiology/wamasing-2018-imaging-bifid-mandibular-canal]], [[radiology/cuozzo-2024-prevalence-anatomical-characteristics-bifid]] provide additional prevalence and CBCT characterization data.

**Clinical implication**: IANB failure in a patient with lip numbness but persistent tooth pain may reflect accessory innervation through a BMC branch. Pre-implant/surgery CBCT in the posterior mandible should explicitly assess for BMC.

### 2. Nutrient Canals (Mandibular Anterior)

Nutrient canals (NCs) are radiolucent neurovascular channels visible on mandibular anterior IOPARs. Unlike MC variants, they carry vessels to alveolar bone rather than the IAN. [[radiology/hasan-2022-prevalence-nutrient-canals-mandibular]] (cross-sectional, n=200, India) compared four groups:

| Group | NC prevalence | vs control p-value |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy controls | 20% | — |
| Diabetes mellitus (DM) | 84% | 0.000004 |
| Hypertension (HTN) | 66% | 0.000003 |
| Chronic periodontitis | 52% | 0.00085 |

Disease duration — not age or sex — was the significant within-group NC correlate. Most NCs (72–74%) were located periapically (beyond the root apex). [[radiology/kumar-2014-incidence-nutrient-canals-hypertensive]], [[radiology/kaur-2019-evaluation-mandibular-anterior-nutrient]], [[radiology/abdar-esfahani-2013-mandibular-anterior-nutrient-canals]] add single-disease and anatomical location data.

**Clinical implication**: Elevated NC frequency on a routine anterior IOPAR is a potential screening indicator for undiagnosed DM, HTN, or periodontitis. Single-center design limits generalizability; use as an ancillary prompt, not a diagnostic test.

### 3. Nasopalatine Canal (NPC) Morphometry

The nasopalatine canal (NPC) is a key anterior maxillary landmark for implant placement, local anesthetic injection, and craniofacial surgery. [[radiology/yadav-2024-nasopalatine-canal-morphology-cbct]] (CBCT cross-sectional, n=102, India, Planmeca 8×8 cm FOV, 0.16 mm):

| Parameter | Finding |
|---|---|
| Mean NPC length | 9.64 mm (males 10.19, females 9.16; P=0.012) |
| Palatal AP diameter | Males significantly wider (P<0.001) |
| Mediolateral lower diameter | Increases with age (≥61 yr: 4.2 vs ≤20 yr: 2.8 mm; P=0.027) |
| Canal length shortest in | ≥61 yr group (7.15 mm) |
| Most common sagittal shape | Cylindrical (25.5%), Funnel (23.5%), Hourglass (18.6%) |

**Clinical implication**: An enlarged NPC (particularly in older males) may encroach on implant space in the anterior maxilla. CBCT morphometry is required before implant planning in this region to determine whether the canal will compromise osseointegration or produce post-surgical neurosensory disturbance.

## Panoramic Image Quality and Positioning Errors

[[radiology/lingam-2023-common-errors-subjective-quality-panoramic]] (n=2,629 digital OPGs, Riyadh) is the largest single-institution audit in the wiki:

- Only **32.8%** were error-free; **77.2%** had ≥1 positioning error
- **15% diagnostically unacceptable** vs ≤10% benchmark
- Only 32.1% rated excellent vs ≥70% benchmark
- Ghost/reflected images (Error 10): rare overall (~2.2%) but significantly concentrated in **5–12-year-old children** (P=0.001) — most commonly caused by thyroid collars or lead aprons positioned between the X-ray source and the rotation center

[[radiology/devlin-2013-object-position-magnification-panoramic-radiography]] characterizes object-plane magnification as a function of lateral distance from the image layer. [[radiology/martins-2022-multilayer-panoramic-radiography-device-mapping]] describes multilayer panoramic device mapping. Ghost image classification and differentiation from real anatomy is systematically addressed in [[radiology/kim-2024-real-ghost-pseudo-ghost-images-panoramic]] and [[radiology/ramos-2016-ghost-images-metal-objects-panoramic]].

**Practical upshot**: Poor panoramic quality is a training and protocol problem. The same thyroid collars that older shielding protocols required are now the primary source of ghost images in children — consistent with the Benavides 2023 shift away from contact shielding.

## Radiographic Differential Diagnosis

### Periodontal Ligament (PDL) Widening

[[radiology/mortazavi-2016-lesions-associated-with-periodontal-ligament]] (narrative review) catalogues the full differential for PDL widening (normal width ~0.15–0.21 mm):

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Functional / benign | Occlusal trauma, orthodontic force, bruxism |
| Inflammatory | Periodontitis, periapical inflammation |
| Systemic disease | Scleroderma (symmetric generalized widening) |
| **Malignancy** | **Osteosarcoma, non-Hodgkin lymphoma, metastasis** |

**Clinical alarm pattern**: Localized PDL widening + cortical bone destruction → malignancy referral before attributing to occlusal trauma. "Widened PDL = trauma" is the dangerous default; the Mortazavi review functions as a safety net.

### Interpretive Artifacts Mimicking Caries

[[radiology/dioguardi-2025-radiographic-artifacts-caries-diagnosis-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 5 observational studies, PROSPERO-registered) quantified optical interpretive artifacts:

| Artifact | Pooled prevalence / false-positive rate |
|---|---|
| Non-carious TSR on maxillary molars | 0.308 pooled (raw ~26%), I²≈98% |
| TSR in primary dentition | ~51% (vs ~27% permanent) |
| Optical effects misread as caries/fracture | ~13% (pooled 0.133, I²=89%) |

Defense strategies: masking test (cover enamel margin — if radiolucency disappears, it was a Mach band), digital contrast adjustment, beam-angle control, clinical examination correlation. GRADE certainty low to very low.

## Cross-Cutting Themes and Clinical Decision Hierarchy

### Hierarchy for mandibular surgical/implant planning

1. Panoramic radiograph first — establish global anatomy, identify obvious MC position
2. If BMC risk or complex anatomy: CBCT mandatory before implant placement or third-molar surgery
3. Confirm MC course, detect BMC branches (Aung 2023: 1 in 5 patients affected), identify retromolar canal if planning in that region

### Hierarchy for anterior maxillary implant planning

1. Assess NPC dimensions on CBCT before placement in the central incisor zone
2. Males and older patients have larger NPCs — adjust residual bone assessment accordingly

### Hierarchy for imaging selection (ALADAIP)

1. Is a 2D radiograph sufficient for the clinical question? → Use it
2. Is 3D required? → Select minimum FOV that answers the diagnostic question; for endodontics: small FOV + ≤0.2 mm voxel; for implants/prosthetics: larger FOV + 0.2–0.4 mm voxel
3. Pediatric patients: justify CBCT with particular rigor (children account for 70% of collective dental radiation dose; Hedesiu 2018)

### Artifact awareness checklist for interpretation

1. Upper molar suspicious radiolucency → apply masking test before diagnosing proximal caries
2. Ghost image on panoramic → check whether lead apron/thyroid collar was worn (especially in children)
3. Widened PDL → confirm or exclude malignancy before comfort diagnosis of occlusal trauma
