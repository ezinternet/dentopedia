## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **범위**: 모놀리식 지르코니아 (Monolithic Zirconia) 보철물을 체어사이드에서 교합조정 (occlusal adjustment)·연삭 (grinding)한 뒤 어떻게 마무리할지에 관한 보유 근거 35편 종합. 33편이 실험실 연구 (in-vitro), 1편 서술 리뷰, 1편 전향 임상 연구. **임상 결과(파절·생존)를 직접 측정한 연구는 없다.** [확인]
> - **결론(테제)**: 조정 후 **"연마는 필수, 재글레이징 (reglazing)은 선택 — 단, 강도와 색은 서로 다른 답을 낸다."** 표면조도 (surface roughness, Ra)와 굴곡강도 (flexural strength)는 연마 쪽이, 색안정성 (color stability)은 글레이즈 쪽이 우세하다. [확인 — 아래 축별 근거]
> - **조도**: 다이아몬드 버 연삭 후 Ra ≈1.1–1.9 µm → 지르코니아 전용 연마키트로 0.2–0.5 µm까지 회복, 다수 연구에서 **글레이즈 수준 이하**(Caglar 0.23–0.28 vs 포셀린 키트 0.78 µm; Gaonkar 0.47 vs 글레이즈 0.53 µm; Pott Ra <0.20 µm). 세균부착 임계 0.2 µm 이하 도달 보고(Kulvarangkun, Phatphutthitham). [확인]
> - **키트 선택**: **포셀린 (feldspathic) 연마키트는 지르코니아에 부적합** — 지르코니아·리튬디실리케이트 전용 키트가 유의하게 매끈(Caglar, Kulvarangkun). 술자 경력은 결과에 무관(Kozmacs, 학생~기공사 동등). [확인]
> - **강도**: 연삭만 하고 끝내면 강도↓(Lee, Ozkan, Elraggal). **연삭 후 연마 시 최고 강도**(Elmamooz 999 vs 대조 689 MPa; Ozkan; Phuntusuntorn 3Y 1214→1704 MPa). **재글레이징은 최저 강도**(Elmamooz 529 MPa) — 강도 관점에선 조정 후 재소성 글레이즈는 피한다. [확인]
> - **버 선택**: **텅스텐카바이드 (tungsten carbide) 버는 가장 매끈하지만 강도를 붕괴**시킴(Kheur 978→496 MPa @1.5 mm, 196 MPa @1.0 mm). 다이아몬드 버는 거칠지만 1.5 mm 잔여두께에서 강도 유지. 저속·무주수 스톤 버는 Lava Esthetic에서 강도↓(Phatphutthitham 508→424 MPa). [확인]
> - **상변태 (t→m phase transformation)**: 결과가 갈린다 — 미검출(Caglar, Chen, Kheur, Shin, Phuntusuntorn) vs 검출·연마로 감소(Lee, Regulska, Yahyazadehfar, Yoo) vs 연마로 오히려 소실(Wertz). 공통 결론: **연마는 상변태층을 늘리지 않고 줄이거나 그대로 둔다.** 측정법 차이(Wertz: ISO 표준식이 2단계 변태를 놓침)가 불일치 원인 후보. [확인]
> - **색·투광성**: 연삭 단독은 적포도주 침지 후 임상 허용치 초과(Soares ΔE00 >1.8); **글레이즈가 색안정성 최우수**(Soares, Agar, Sayed ChG, Mazzir 리뷰 9편), 연마는 그 중간. 투광성 (translucency parameter, TP)은 연마로 유지(Monteiro 4종 모두). >100 µm 조정은 외부착색 (extrinsic stain) 손상의 최대 요인(Mazzir). [확인]
> - **재료별 예외**: 같은 프로토콜이 재료에 따라 반대로 작동 — Monteiro에서 Cercon hT +60%(576→856 MPa) vs **Lava Plus −60%(578→229 MPa)**. 5Y·6Y 고투광 지르코니아는 연마 강화 효과가 작거나 없음(Phuntusuntorn 6Y 무변화). [확인]
> - **안전·운영**: 20,000 rpm 구내 연마는 치수 손상 임계 5.5 °C 초과(Liu, ≥8 °C/2분) → **≤10,000 rpm 또는 간헐 연마**. 연마키트는 **flash 멸균 10회까지 재사용** 가능(Lunkad, Sayed). [확인]
> - **임상 순서 제안**: 다이아몬드 버(주수, 미세입자)로 조정 → 지르코니아 전용 2–3단계 연마(단계당 40–60 s, 저속) → 전치부·착색 보철은 조정을 **모두 끝낸 뒤** 글레이즈 고려, 구치부 고하중은 연마로 종료. [미검증 — 이 순서는 위 근거의 종합 추론이며 임상 RCT로 검증된 적 없음]
> - **한계**: 전부 평판 시편 실험실 연구, 제품 1–4종 단위, 임상 파절·대합치 마모 결과 미연결. 대합치 법랑질 마모 (enamel wear) 근거(연마 > 글레이즈)는 [[overviews/zirconia-types-clinical-selection]]에 이미 종합되어 있다.

## Three-line Summary

This overview synthesizes 35 held papers (33 in-vitro, 1 narrative review, 1 prospective clinical) on how to finish monolithic zirconia after chairside occlusal adjustment, across five outcome axes: surface roughness, flexural strength, tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation, color/translucency stability, and practical protocol (kit, speed, heat, sterilization).
Polishing with a zirconia-specific kit after diamond-bur grinding consistently restores roughness to glaze level or below (Ra ~0.2–0.5 µm) and yields the highest flexural strength, whereas reglazing after adjustment gives the lowest strength (Elmamooz 529 vs 999 MPa), tungsten-carbide burs collapse strength despite a smooth surface (Kheur 978→196 MPa at 1.0 mm), and porcelain polishing kits are inadequate.
Glaze remains the most color-stable surface under staining challenge, protocol effects are material-specific (Lava Plus lost ~60% strength where Cercon hT gained ~60%), and no study links any finishing protocol to clinical fracture or survival — the decision rule is therefore "always polish, glaze only for esthetic/stain cases and only after all adjustment is complete."

## 세줄요약

이 종합 페이지는 모놀리식 지르코니아 (Monolithic Zirconia) 체어사이드 교합조정 후 마무리 방법에 관한 보유 35편(실험실 33·서술 리뷰 1·전향 임상 1)을 표면조도 (Ra)·굴곡강도·상변태 (t→m)·색/투광성·실무 프로토콜(키트·회전속도·발열·멸균) 5축으로 정리한다.
다이아몬드 버 연삭 후 지르코니아 전용 키트 연마는 조도를 글레이즈 수준 이하(Ra ~0.2–0.5 µm)로 되돌리고 강도도 가장 높은 반면, 조정 후 재글레이징은 강도 최저(529 vs 999 MPa), 텅스텐카바이드 버는 표면은 매끈해도 강도 붕괴(978→196 MPa), 포셀린 연마키트는 부적합하다.
색안정성은 글레이즈가 가장 우수하고 효과는 재료마다 다르며(Lava Plus −60% vs Cercon hT +60%), 어떤 프로토콜도 임상 파절·생존과 연결된 근거가 없다 — 규칙은 "항상 연마, 글레이즈는 심미·착색 케이스에서 조정 완료 후에만".

## Axis 1 — Surface Roughness: Polishing Reaches or Beats Glaze

**Grinding baseline.** Water-cooled diamond-bur adjustment raises Ra to roughly 1.1–1.9 µm: 1.77 µm (Caglar), 1.24 µm (Gaonkar), 1.87 µm (Yahyazadehfar), 1.08 µm for translucent zirconia vs 2.00–2.59 µm for glass-ceramics (Ergul — zirconia's hardness limits groove depth).

**Polishing restores it.**

| Study | After polishing | Comparator |
|---|---|---|
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/caglar-2018-the-effect-of-various-polishing]] | 0.23 µm (EVE Diacera), 0.28 µm (Meisinger) | Porcelain kit 0.78 µm |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/gaonkar-2020-an-in-vitro-study-to]] | 0.47 µm (OptraFine), 0.49 µm (eZr) | Glazed control 0.53 µm |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/kheur-2022-a-comparative-study-on-simulated]] | 0.24 µm (diamond paste), 0.32 µm (ceramic kit) | Control 0.27 µm; novel zirconia slurry failed (2.81 µm) |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/pott-2018-polish-of-interface-areas-between]] | <0.20 µm, all 3 intraoral systems | Statistically equal to glaze firing |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/kulvarangkun-2022-experimental-study-of-surface-roughness]] | Below 0.2 µm (LiDi/zirconia set) | Feldspathic set and glaze rougher |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/phatphutthitham-2025-effect-of-grinding-and-polishing]] | <0.20 µm, both protocols (5Y-PSZ) | High-speed diamond + silicone smoother than low-speed stone |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/shin-2021-comparison-of-surface-topography-and]] | Fine polish < layering glaze, 3Y/4Y/5Y alike | No yttria-content effect on Ra |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/hmaidouch-2014-surface-roughness-of-zirconia-for]] | Full-contour: polish ≤ glaze; veneered: polish stays rougher | Veneered zirconia retains grooves/voids |

**Kit specificity matters more than operator.** Porcelain/feldspathic kits are consistently worse on zirconia (Caglar, Kulvarangkun). A multipurpose diamond kit can serve zirconia, ZLS, and lithium disilicate alike without changing hardness ([[dental-materials/zirconia/limpuangthip-2023-surface-roughness-and-hardness-of]]). Operator experience did not change the result — students, junior and senior dentists, and technicians were statistically equivalent with a 2-step kit ([[dental-materials/zirconia/kozmacs-2017-polishing-of-monolithic-zirconia-crowns]]).

**Time per step.** In [[dental-materials/zirconia/chen-2022-effects-of-different-polishing-systems]], 20 s per step was significantly rougher than 40–60 s; 60 s was the effective ceiling and still rougher than glaze for that system.

**Mixed-material margins.** Where zirconia meets veneering ceramic or composite, polish zirconia first with its own polisher, then the veneering ceramic, then composite (Pott 2018).

**Novel chairside material.** A fully-sintered (Y, Nb)-TZP chairside block started rougher and with more monoclinic phase than lab 3Y-TZP, but a conventional 2-step zirconia kit equalized roughness ([[dental-materials/zirconia/yoo-2022-analysis-of-surface-characteristics-of]]).

## Axis 2 — Flexural Strength: Grind → Polish Wins, Reglaze Loses

**Polishing after grinding gives the highest strength across independent groups.**

- [[dental-materials/zirconia/elmamooz-2025-evaluation-of-the-effect-of]] — grind + polish 999.2 ± 81.1 MPa vs control 688.8 MPa vs **grind + reglaze 528.9 ± 17.5 MPa (lowest)**; treatment and adjustment extent both significant (p<0.0001). All groups stayed >500 MPa.
- [[dental-materials/zirconia/ozkan-2025-effects-of-surface-treatments-ltd]] — grind + polish highest strength and lowest Ra in 4Y and 6Y-TZP; grinding alone worst.
- [[dental-materials/zirconia/phuntusuntorn-2026-effect-of-surface-adjustment-and]] — finishing/polishing raised 3Y-TZP 1213.7→1704.3 MPa (~40%), 5Y-PSZ 694.2→993.7 MPa, 6Y-PSZ no significant change.
- [[dental-materials/zirconia/lee-2019-the-effects-of-surface-grinding]] — coarse (150 µm) diamond grinding without polishing gave the most t→m and lowest strength; polishing removed the transformed layer. All groups >500 MPa (ISO 6872).
- [[dental-materials/zirconia/elraggal-2022-effect-of-surface-treatments-on]] — grinding consistently reduced strength and fracture toughness in both LT (3Y) and HT (4Y) zirconia; low-pressure APA raised strength.

**The "grinding strengthens" exception is temporary.** [[dental-materials/zirconia/desouza-2020-flexural-strength-and-crystalline-stability]] found grinding *raised* biaxial strength (1078 vs as-sintered 775 MPa) via transformation toughening, but autoclave aging and 200,000 thermocycles erased the gain equally across groups. Read together with Lee and Ozkan: compressive-stress toughening from grinding is real but not durable, while the surface flaws it introduces are — so the gain should not be relied on.

**Bur type is a strength decision, not just a roughness one.** [[dental-materials/zirconia/kheur-2022-a-comparative-study-on-simulated]]: tungsten-carbide burs gave the smoothest surface (Ra ≈0.40 µm) yet cut strength 978→496 MPa at 1.5 mm residual thickness and to 196 MPa at 1.0 mm, with grinding-direction cracks on SEM; diamond burs roughened (Ra ≈0.81 µm) without significant strength loss at 1.5 mm. Low-speed uncooled stone burs reduced Lava Esthetic strength 508→424 MPa (Phatphutthitham). Zirconia needed ~5× longer grinding than composite for equal removal, and diamond grain rounding/loss is the dominant bur-wear mechanism ([[dental-materials/zirconia/rosentritt-2024-cutting-efficiency-of-diamond-grinders]]) — worn burs are a plausible source of extra heat and flaws [미검증 — inference, not measured].

**Material-specific reversal.** [[dental-materials/zirconia/monteiro-2026-effect-of-occlusal-adjustment-and]] applied one adjust-and-polish protocol to four materials: Cercon hT +~60% (576→856 MPa), Lava Plus −~60% (578→229 MPa), Prettau 4 Anterior and Cercon xT unchanged. The protocol is therefore not material-agnostic; brand-level data should override the general rule where available.

**Composition and sintering context.** Higher yttria (5Y) gives lower absolute strength (508–568 MPa vs 891–1081 MPa for 3Y), and speed sintering did not change strength or translucency ([[dental-materials/zirconia/gulmez-2025-impact-of-sintering-speed-and]]). For veneered zirconia, regenerative heat treatment (900 °C/60 min or 1000 °C/30 min) reset monoclinic content to 0 vol% without hurting veneer adhesion but did not restore smoothness ([[dental-materials/zirconia/candido-2022-effect-of-grinding-and-regenerative]]).

## Axis 3 — Phase Transformation: Contradictory Detection, Consistent Direction

| Finding | Papers |
|---|---|
| No t→m detected after grinding/polishing | Caglar 2018, [[dental-materials/zirconia/chen-2022-effects-of-different-polishing-systems]], Kheur 2022, Shin 2021, Phuntusuntorn 2026 (also after aging, qualitative XRD) |
| t→m after grinding, reduced by polishing | Lee 2019, [[dental-materials/zirconia/regulska-2021-analysis-of-the-surface-condition]] (subsurface gradient on grazing-incidence XRD; grit size and wet/dry cooling no effect), [[dental-materials/zirconia/yahyazadehfar-2021-effect-of-different-surface-treatments]] (grind 4.7% monoclinic, lowest Weibull m 12.2), Yoo 2022 |
| Polishing/chewing *eliminated* monoclinic phase | [[dental-materials/zirconia/wertz-2021-the-influence-of-surface-preparation]] — Rietveld refinement detected a two-stage transformation the ISO 13356 Garvie-Nicholson method missed |
| Comparative surface treatments | [[dental-materials/zirconia/mielak-2024-alternative-treatments-for-zirconium-oxide]] — sandblasting 20.8% monoclinic (highest), HF etching 1.8% (lowest but non-wettable), laser 7.9%, plasma 4.8% |
| Aging × pH | Ozkan 2025 — LTD + pH 3 raised t→m to 22% and cut strength up to 30% (pH 9: 15%, 18%); 6Y resisted transformation better than 4Y |

**Cross-paper reading.** Detection depends on method (conventional XRD vs grazing-incidence vs Rietveld) and grit, so "no transformation" results should not be read as "no surface damage." The direction is consistent in every study that compared polishing with grinding alone: **polishing never increased monoclinic content relative to the ground surface.** Yahyazadehfar also found no correlation between roughness, monoclinic fraction, and strength — monoclinic fraction is a weak proxy for clinical risk. Chen's conclusion that the post-adjustment concern is "residual stress and roughness rather than t→m" fits this reading.

## Axis 4 — Color and Translucency: Here Glaze Wins

| Study | Challenge | Result |
|---|---|---|
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/soares-2024-optical-properties-and-color-stability]] | 18-day red wine | Grind-only ΔE00 >1.8 (unacceptable); glaze most stable; polish in between; TP/OP unaffected |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/agar-2025-effect-of-finishing-protocol-treatments]] | 10,000 brushing cycles | Stain+glaze most color-stable (ΔE00 ≈0.33) but lowest translucency; polished least stable (ΔEab 1.02); all below perceptibility |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/sayed-2024-influence-of-chairside-simulated-adjustment]] | 14-day chlorhexidine | Adjusted multilayer zirconia showed perceptible ΔE 1.0–3.3 and translucency loss; glazed specimens most resistant → finish adjustments before final glaze |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/mazzir-2026-color-stability-of-extrinsically-colored]] | Narrative review, 9 in-vitro studies | >100 µm occlusal reduction is the most critical factor for stain loss; glaze > polish for stain integrity |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/monteiro-2026-effect-of-occlusal-adjustment-and]] | — | Polishing preserved TP in all 4 materials; increased contact angle |
| [[dental-materials/zirconia/ergul-2026-optimizing-cad-cam-ceramics-a]] | 10,000 coffee thermocycles | Polishing best for translucent zirconia (roughness), glaze better for glass-ceramics; all within clinical thresholds |

Baseline shade can differ between brands with the same Vita designation because of yttria content and manufacturer shade mapping (Sayed 2024a) — a matching issue independent of finishing.

**Reconciling Axes 2 and 4.** Strength favors polish and penalizes reglazing; color favors glaze. These are not contradictory findings but different endpoints. The practical resolution is site-based: posterior high-load restorations end with polish; esthetic or extrinsically stained restorations may be reglazed, but only after all adjustment is complete, accepting the strength cost shown in Elmamooz.

## Axis 5 — Protocol and Operations

- **Pulpal heat** — [[dental-materials/zirconia/liu-2023-the-impact-of-different-polishing]]: Meisinger polishers at 20,000 rpm on Lava Esthetic crowns exceeded the 5.5 °C pulpal threshold (≥8 °C within 2 min) in a perfused model; 10,000 rpm stayed mostly below. Use low speed or intermittent polishing on vital teeth.
- **Kit sterilization and reuse** — [[dental-materials/zirconia/lunkad-2025-evaluation-of-surface-roughness-of]]: three kits stayed clinically acceptable across 10 flash cycles. [[dental-materials/zirconia/sayed-2024-the-impact-of-repeated-steam]]: both standard and flash steam degraded EVE Diacera progressively; flash was better; recommend ≤10 flash cycles to keep Ra ≤0.25 µm.
- **Reduce the need to adjust** — [[dental-materials/zirconia/kim-2026-trueness-and-the-amount-of]]: DLP-printed crowns at 0° build angle needed the least occlusal (25.7 µm) and intaglio (20.6 µm) adjustment; milled crowns needed the most intaglio adjustment (56.5 µm). Fit-optimized fabrication reduces the amount of zirconia that must later be ground and re-polished.

## Clinical Decision Thread

1. **Adjust with fine-grit diamond, water-cooled** — not tungsten carbide (Kheur), not uncooled low-speed stone on 5Y (Phatphutthitham). Keep residual thickness ≥1.5 mm where possible (Kheur's collapse was at 1.0 mm).
2. **Always polish after any adjustment** with a zirconia-specific multi-step kit, 40–60 s per step (Chen), at ≤10,000 rpm on vital teeth (Liu). Do not use a porcelain kit (Caglar, Kulvarangkun).
3. **Decide glaze by site**: posterior/high-load → stop at polish (strongest; Elmamooz, Ozkan, Phuntusuntorn). Anterior or stained → glaze is the most color-stable option (Soares, Agar, Mazzir), but only after all adjustment (Sayed) and knowing reglazing gave the lowest strength in Elmamooz.
4. **Check brand-level data** for the material in use — a protocol that strengthens one 3Y product weakened another (Monteiro).
5. **Kit maintenance**: flash sterilization, retire after ~10 cycles (Lunkad, Sayed).

[미검증 — this sequence is a synthesis of in-vitro findings; no clinical trial has tested it as a protocol.]

## Gaps and Future Research

- No clinical study links any finishing protocol to fracture, chipping, or survival; strength differences are specimen-level only.
- Antagonist enamel wear by finishing protocol is synthesized separately in [[overviews/zirconia-types-clinical-selection]] (polish > glaze); none of the 35 papers here measured wear and strength together.
- Material-specific reversals (Monteiro) mean results for one brand cannot be generalized; most studies tested 1–4 products.
- Phase-transformation evidence is method-dependent (Wertz); a standardized measurement would resolve the detection conflict.
- Glaze-vs-polish color trade-off has no clinical-perception study in vivo.
