> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 치경부(치경부 병소, Class V/NCCL) 레진 충전에서 결정 변수는 *치은압배코드(retraction cord)* 자체가 아니라 **오염·수분 차단(dry field)** 이다. 코드는 열구액(치은열구액, Gingival Crevicular Fluid, GCF)을 막는 여러 수단 중 하나일 뿐, 그 자체가 목적이 아니다.
> - **격리가 생존율을 실제로 바꾼다 (최고등급)**: Cochrane 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(Miao 2021)은 러버댐(rubber dam)이 코튼롤 대비 6개월 NCCL 직접 레진 생존율을 높임 — **OR 2.29 (95% CI 1.05–4.99, 낮은 근거수준)**. "수분·미생물 통제는 접착 수복 성공의 전제"가 이 리뷰의 핵심 문장.
> - **근거의 한계**: 그 6개월 결과가 유일하게 low-certainty이고, 12·18개월 이후는 매우 불확실(OR 1.38→1.00), 포함 RCT 6편 전부 비뚤림 위험 높음. 즉 "격리 원칙"은 강하지만 "코드 vs 무코드"를 직접 비교한 논문은 위키에 **없다**(Rule #4 부분 공백).
> - **마진 위치가 코드 필요성을 가른다**: 치은연상/치은연(supra·equi-gingival) 마진이면 코튼롤+석션 또는 러버댐으로 충분 → 코드 불필요. 치은연하(subgingival) 마진이라 열구액이 새는 경우에만 기계적 압배(코드, 또는 치경부용 클램프 B4/212)로 **마진 노출 + GCF 차단**이 의미 있다.
> - **성공은 재료가 아니라 접착제·술자**: Peumans (2020)는 복합레진 *종류*가 아니라 **접착 시스템과 올바른 술식(operator technique)** 이 NCCL 수복 성공을 좌우한다고 정리 — 격리는 그 "operator" 통제 요소의 핵심.
> - **경화상아질(sclerotic dentin)일수록 격리 여유가 더 중요**: Goodacre (2022)는 경화상아질 표면 texturing + **30초 연장 산부식** + 20초 가벼운 scrubbing 도포를 권고. 접착이 원래 약한 표면이므로 오염 통제 실패의 대가가 크다.
> - **코드를 무리하게 깊이 넣지 말 것 (역방향 위험)**: Schätzle (2001, 26년 prospective)은 치은연하 충전 마진이 plaque·치은지수(Gingival Index, GI, 7회 조사 전부 p<0.001)·부착소실을 증가시킴을 보고. 격리를 위해 마진을 억지로 치은연하로 만드는 것은 장기 치주 손해가 될 수 있다 — "격리 위해 깊게"와 "치주 위해 얕게"의 상충.
> - **이미 치은연하로 깊은 경우의 대안**: ElHaddad (2026, CMR 첫 SR+MA)의 치경부 변연 거상(Cervical Margin Relocation, deep margin elevation)은 마진을 청소 가능한 치은연상으로 끌어올리는 술기로, 건강·안정 치주에서 치주지표를 악화시키지 않음(probing depth MD −0.96 mm, I²=0%; BOP MD −0.52).
> - **지혈제 주의**: 코드에 지혈제(FeSO₄·염화알루미늄)를 쓰면 접착면에 잔류 시 접착을 저해 → 광중합 전 충분히 세척. 비함침(non-impregnated) 코드 건식 압배로 열구를 잠깐 벌려 접착·중합까지만 유지하는 것이 실용적.
> - **임상 결론(통합)**: 목표는 *코드*가 아니라 *dry field* — ① 치은연상 마진이면 러버댐/코튼롤로 충분, ② 열구액이 새는 치은연하 마진에서만 코드(또는 치경부 클램프)로 노출·차단, ③ 마진을 격리 목적으로 무리하게 치은연하로 만들지 말 것, ④ 이미 깊으면 CMR로 올려라, ⑤ 지혈제 잔류는 세척.
> - **[2026-11-26 추가] 탈락의 기준선**: 치경부 수복 전향 임상시험 81편 메타분석(Mahn 2015, 초록만)에서 **5년 탈락 12.3%**, 임상지수 실패 5년 17.4%·8년 32.3%. 즉 "레진이 빠졌다"는 드문 사고가 아니라 예상되는 실패 양식이고, 무엇을 바꾸면 그 비율이 움직이는지가 질문이다.
> - **격리 근거는 엇갈린다 (논쟁)**: Mahn 2015는 러버댐 + 상아질 조면화(dentin roughening) 조합에서 유지율이 유의하게 높다고 했지만 이는 **시험 간 비교**다. 같은 질문을 직접 무작위 비교한 Loguercio 2015 split-mouth RCT는 러버댐 vs 코튼롤/압배코드 **18개월 유지 73% vs 73%** 로 차이 없음. Cochrane(Miao 2021)도 6개월만 low-certainty. → 격리는 원칙으로 유지하되, "러버댐을 안 써서 빠졌다"고 단정할 근거는 약하다.
> - **상아질을 '세정만' 하면 가장 많이 빠진다**: Lührs 2020 RCT(7.7년, 전문)에서 세정만 한 군 탈락 **27.8%** 로 최고(전처치 3군 합산 대비 p=0.041). 버 조면화·그루브·플로어블 라이너 사이엔 차이 없음(p=0.328) — 조면화 자체가 레버이고, 그루브·플로어블은 취향 문제. Mahn 2015의 조면화 효과와 같은 방향.
> - **효과 없는 전처리**: 산부식 후 10% 차아염소산나트륨 (NaOCl) 단백질 제거는 유지력을 올리지 못함(Favetti 2022 RCT 3년, 연간 실패율 대조 9% vs NaOCl 17.8%, p=0.077, 초록만) — 시간만 늘리는 단계.
> - **접착 전략 > 재료 종류**: 1단계 자가부식(Self-Etch, SE)이 2단계 SE(p=0.026)·3단계 산부식-수세(Etch-and-Rinse, E&R, p=0.002)보다 임상지수 불량(Mahn 2015). 후향 460개(Saengnil 2022)에서도 접착 전략이 실패 핵심 인자였고 선택적 법랑질 산부식(Selective Enamel Etching) 군 실패가 가장 적었다(단 술자 수준과 교락).
> - **환자·치아 위험인자 — 하악이 일관되게 불리**: 하악치는 Göstemeyer 2021(OR 2.89, 1.00–8.31)과 Favetti 2022(p=0.039) 모두에서 생존 불량, 소구치도 불량(Favetti p=0.013). **교합 마모면(occlusal wear facet)** 은 탈락 위험인자(Saengnil 2022, p=0.041). 나이는 엇갈림 — Göstemeyer는 고령일수록 위험↓(OR 0.90/년), Saengnil은 연령 무관.
> - **재료 대안**: 와동형성 없이 수복한 경화 NCCL에서 글래스하이브리드 (Glass Hybrid, GH) vs 레진은 3년 완전 탈락 17 vs 19개로 생존 차이 없음(p=0.396), GH가 시술시간(8.6 vs 11.7분)·총비용(41.72 vs 51.60€) 모두 유리(Göstemeyer/Schwendicke 2021, 같은 시험, 초록만). 우산 리뷰(Fernández 2026, 초록만)는 레진강화형 글래스아이오노머 (Resin-Modified Glass Ionomer, RMGIC) 샌드위치가 NCCL 유지력을 개선한다고 요약.
> - **탈락 재수복 체크리스트**: ① 상아질 조면화했나(세정만 X), ② 1단계 SE 대신 다단계/선택적 법랑질 산부식, ③ 교합 마모면 있으면 위험 고지(교합 원인 평가), ④ 하악·소구치는 예후 설명 시 반영, ⑤ 반복 탈락 + 경화 상아질이면 GH/RMGIC를 대안으로 고려, ⑥ NaOCl 전처리는 추가하지 말 것, ⑦ 법랑질 베벨은 유지력 목적이면 생략 가능.
> - **[2026-11-26 2차] 법랑질 베벨 (enamel bevel) — 유지력 이득 없음, 근거 일치**: RCT 한정 SR+MA(Schroeder 2015, RCT 4편)에서 1년 유지율 위험차 **0.0 (95% CI −0.04~0.04, p=0.91)**, 변연변색도 무차이. 메타분석 2편(Heintze 2010 임상연구 50편, Mahn 2015 81편) 모두 "베벨 무영향". 개별 시험: Da Costa 2013 12개월 91% vs 88%, Baratieri 2003은 6개월 100% vs 66%였으나 **2·3년엔 차이 소실**(초기 이득이 있어도 지속되지 않음). 결합강도 시험관 연구(Melo 2026)도 베벨 무효(p=0.177). Goodacre 2022의 베벨 권고는 서술형 리뷰 수준 — 임상 비교 근거로는 지지되지 않는다. 단, 최장 추적이 3년이고 RCT 수가 적어 "이득 없음 ≠ 완전 동등". 심미적 경계 이행 목적의 베벨 효과는 위키에 근거 없음.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 5 papers arguing that isolation (dry field), not the retraction cord per se, is the evidence-backed determinant of cervical composite success: the highest-tier evidence (Miao 2021 Cochrane SR+MA) shows rubber dam vs cotton-roll moisture/microbial control raises 6-month NCCL composite survival (OR 2.29, 95% CI 1.05–4.99, low certainty), while the cord is only one of several tools to achieve a dry field when a subgingival margin allows GCF infiltration.

The decision to use a cord is governed by margin geometry — supragingival/equigingival margins need no cord; only subgingival margins warrant retraction — and this must be weighed against the long-term periodontal cost of subgingival restorative margins (higher plaque, GI, and attachment loss at all 7 survey points, Schätzle 2001 26-year prospective), with deep margin elevation/CMR as the alternative when the margin is already subgingival (ElHaddad 2026 SR+MA: PD MD −0.96 mm, BOP MD −0.52).

Clinical takeaway: goal is dry field, not cord placement; (1) supragingival margin → rubber dam/cotton rolls, no cord needed; (2) subgingival margin with GCF seeping → non-impregnated cord or cervical clamp (B4/212), rinse any hemostatic before bonding; (3) margin already deep → consider CMR rather than deeper retraction; "cord vs no cord" has no direct RCT in this wiki. Update 2026-11-26 (+8 papers on retention loss): baseline 5-year loss is 12.3% (Mahn 2015 MA); the direct isolation RCT shows no difference (Loguercio 2015, 73% vs 73%), whereas dentin roughening (Lührs 2020: cleaning-only loss 27.8%, p=0.041), multi-step or selective-enamel-etch adhesion, and patient/tooth factors (mandible, premolar, occlusal wear facets) are the more consistent retention levers.

## 세줄요약

5편 종합: 치경부 레진 성공의 결정 변수는 치은압배코드(retraction cord)가 아닌 오염·수분 차단(dry field) — Cochrane SR+MA(Miao 2021)에서 러버댐 수분통제가 6개월 NCCL 레진 생존율 향상(OR 2.29, 낮은 근거수준); 코드는 치은연하 마진에서 열구액(GCF)을 막는 여러 수단 중 하나이며 접착 원칙이 목적.

마진 위치가 코드 필요성을 결정: 치은연상 마진 → 코드 불필요; 치은연하 마진에서 GCF가 새는 경우에만 기계적 압배가 의미 있음; 치은연하 충전 마진은 플라크·치은지수(GI)·부착소실을 증가시킴(Schätzle 2001, 26년 전향, 7회 조사 전부 p<0.001) — "격리 위해 깊게"와 "치주 위해 얕게"의 상충.

임상 결론: ①치은연상 마진 → 러버댐/코튼롤, 코드 불필요; ②GCF 세는 치은연하 마진 → 비함침 코드(#000/#00) 또는 치경부 클램프(B4/212), 지혈제는 접착 전 충분히 세척; ③이미 깊은 마진 → 치경부 변연 거상(Cervical Margin Relocation, CMR; ElHaddad 2026 SR+MA) 고려; "코드 vs 무코드" 직접 RCT는 위키 없음. 2026-11-26 갱신(탈락 관련 8편 추가): 5년 탈락 기준선 12.3%(Mahn 2015 MA); 격리 직접비교 RCT는 무차이(Loguercio 2015, 73% vs 73%)인 반면 상아질 조면화(Lührs 2020, 세정만 탈락 27.8%)·다단계/선택적 법랑질 산부식·하악·소구치·교합 마모면이 더 일관된 탈락 변수.

## Summary

The clinical question — "치경부 레진 충전에 열구액 때문에 코드를 꽂아야 하나?" — is best answered by separating the *goal* from the *tool*. The goal is contamination control (a dry, GCF-free bonding field). The cord is one tool for that goal, useful only in a specific geometry.

**Axis 1 — Isolation is a real survival determinant.** The only direct high-grade evidence on isolation method for cervical composites is a Cochrane review, [[resin-bonding/miao-2021-rubber-dam-isolation-restorative-treatment]] (sr+ma): rubber dam vs cotton rolls raised NCCL direct-composite survival at 6 months, OR 2.29 (95% CI 1.05–4.99, low-certainty). Its framing sentence — "effective moisture and microbial control is a prerequisite for successful adhesive restoration" — is the load-bearing principle. Caveat: only that 6-month endpoint reached low-certainty; 12/18-month evidence is very uncertain (OR 1.38 → 1.00) and all 6 included RCTs are at high risk of bias. There is **no wiki paper directly comparing retraction cord vs no cord** for cervical resin — the cord recommendation is an extrapolation from the isolation principle, not a tested variable.

**Axis 2 — Technique, not material, and the operator's control of the field.** [[nccl/peumans-2020-nccl-treatment-when-why-how]] (narrative-review) concludes NCCL restoration success is driven by the adhesive system and correct operative technique rather than composite type — and isolation is the central operator-controlled variable. [[nccl/goodacre-2022-nccl-morphology-etiology-restoration-guidelines]] adds that sclerotic dentin (etch-resistant) needs surface texturing, 30-s extended etch, and a light 20-s scrubbing application; because bonding to this substrate is already weak, the penalty for contamination is higher — a stronger reason to secure the dry field where the margin is subgingival.

**Axis 3 — Margin geometry decides whether a cord is even needed.** For supragingival/equigingival margins, cotton rolls + good evacuation or a rubber dam suffice, and no cord is required. Only when the margin is subgingival and GCF seeps does mechanical retraction (cord, or a cervical rubber-dam clamp such as B4/212) earn its place — to expose the margin and physically block GCF through bonding and light-cure.

**Axis 4 — The periodontal counterweight.** Do not create a subgingival margin merely to place a cord. [[periodontics/schatzle-2001-restoration-margins-periodontal-tissues-26year]] (26-year prospective) found subgingival filling margins associated with higher plaque, higher gingival inflammation (GI elevated at all 7 surveys, p<0.001), and greater attachment loss; [[overviews/restorative-margin-periodontal-interface-overview]] reconciles this with Ercoli's "intracrevicular margins are compatible with health *under maintained hygiene*." When a lesion is already subgingival/deep, [[resin-bonding/elhaddad-2026-cervical-margin-relocation-periodontal-restorative]] (sr+ma) shows cervical margin relocation / deep margin elevation raises the margin to a cleansable level without harming periodontal health in healthy/stabilized patients (PD MD −0.96 mm, I²=0%; BOP MD −0.52).

**Axis 5 — Retention loss: the baseline and the contested isolation effect (added 2026-11-26).** [[resin-bonding/mahn-2015-bonding-parameters-cervical-restorations-ma]] (sr+ma, 81 prospective trials / 185 experiments, abstract-only) sets the baseline: 12.3% of cervical restorations lost at 5 years, clinical-index failure 17.4% at 5 years and 32.3% at 8 years. It reported higher retention when rubber dam was used on roughened dentin (p<0.05), but this is a between-experiment association. The randomized head-to-head trial, [[resin-bonding/loguercio-2015-isolation-method-operative-field-gingival]] (split-mouth RCT, 60 restorations, one-step self-etch), found identical 18-month retention with rubber dam versus cotton roll/retraction cord (73% vs 73%). Read together with Miao 2021 (only the 6-month endpoint reaches low certainty), the isolation principle stands but its measurable effect on retention is uncertain; it should not be the default explanation for a lost cervical restoration.

**Axis 6 — Retention levers with more consistent support.** (a) *Dentin preparation*: [[resin-bonding/luhrs-2020-composite-restorations-nccl-cavity-preparation]] (RCT, 7.7 years, full text) found cleaning-only restorations had the highest loss (27.8%; vs pooled prepared groups p=0.041), with no difference between bur roughening, a cervical groove, or a flowable liner (p=0.328) — consistent with Mahn's roughening finding. (b) *Adhesive strategy*: one-step self-etch underperformed two-step self-etch (p=0.026) and three-step etch-and-rinse (p=0.002) in Mahn 2015; in [[resin-bonding/saengnil-2022-retrospective-factors-failure-nccl-restorations]] (retrospective, 460 restorations), adhesive strategy was a main failure factor and selective enamel etching failed least, although strategy was confounded with operator level. (c) *Ineffective add-on*: [[resin-bonding/favetti-2022-naocl-acid-etching-cervical-retention-3year-rct]] (RCT, 3 years, abstract-only) found 10% NaOCl deproteinization after etching gave no retention benefit (AFR 9% control vs 17.8% NaOCl, p=0.077). (d) *Patient and tooth factors*: mandibular teeth had worse survival in two independent trials (Göstemeyer 2021 OR 2.89, 95% CI 1.00–8.31; Favetti 2022 p=0.039), premolars in one (Favetti p=0.013), and occlusal wear facets predicted restoration loss (Saengnil p=0.041). Age is inconsistent: older patients had lower risk in Göstemeyer (OR 0.90 per year) but age was not associated in Saengnil.

**Axis 7 — Material alternatives when retention keeps failing.** In sclerotic NCCLs restored without mechanical preparation, [[resin-bonding/gostemeyer-2021-glass-hybrid-nanocomposite-sclerotic-nccl-18m-rct]] (18-month interim) and its 3-year report [[resin-bonding/schwendicke-2021-glass-hybrid-composite-nccl-3year-rct]] (both abstract-only) found no survival difference between a glass hybrid and an etch-and-rinse composite (total loss 17 vs 19 restorations, p=0.396), with retention loss the dominant failure mode in both arms; the glass hybrid was faster (8.6 vs 11.7 min) and cheaper (total 41.72 vs 51.60 EUR, p<0.001). The umbrella review [[dental-materials/fernandez-2026-direct-restorations-longevity-umbrella-review]] (abstract-only) summarizes that an RMGIC sandwich improves NCCL retention and that adhesive protocol is the key prognostic factor across materials. Note that the GH trial arms had no cavity preparation, whereas Lührs shows preparation matters for composite; the two findings are not directly comparable.

**Axis 8 — Enamel bevel: no retention benefit (added 2026-11-26, second batch).** Every clinical source we hold points the same way. [[resin-bonding/schroeder-2015-enamel-bevel-cervical-composite-retention-sr-ma]] (RCT-only SR+MA, 4 RCTs, 2 at low risk of bias; abstract-only) found no difference in 1-year retention (risk difference 0.0, 95% CI −0.04 to 0.04, p=0.91) or marginal discoloration (0.05, −0.02 to 0.13). Both broad cervical meta-analyses from the Heintze group — [[resin-bonding/heintze-2010-clinical-performance-cervical-restorations-ma]] (50 studies; 3-year loss 10%) and Mahn 2015 (81 trials) — found bevelling had no influence, while dentin roughening did improve retention in both. Individual trials: [[resin-bonding/da-costa-2013-enamel-bevel-nccl-composite-rct]] (prospective within-patient trial, randomization not stated in the abstract; 84 restorations) reported 12-month retention 91% beveled vs 88% non-beveled; [[resin-bonding/baratieri-2003-resin-viscosity-enamel-bevel-class-v-3year-rct]] (RCT, 105 Class V) showed a 6-month advantage (100% vs 66%) that disappeared at 2 and 3 years. At the bench, [[resin-bonding/melo-2026-class-v-bevel-universal-adhesive-bond-strength]] (in-vitro push-out, full text) found no bevel effect (p=0.177) but did find etch-and-rinse above self-etch (p=0.042). The bevel recommendation in Goodacre 2022 is therefore a narrative-review position not supported by comparative data. Limits: longest follow-up is 3 years, the RCT count is small, and absence of benefit is not proof of equivalence; no held paper tests bevelling for esthetic margin blending.

**Practical caveat — hemostatic residue.** Impregnated cords (FeSO₄, aluminium chloride) left on the bonding surface impair adhesion; rinse thoroughly before adhesive/light-cure. A non-impregnated cord under dry pressure, retracting the sulcus only through bonding, is the pragmatic default.

## Decision tree (chairside)

1. **Margin supra-/equigingival, field stays dry?** → No cord. Cotton rolls + evacuation or rubber dam. (Isolation grade 🟢; cord unnecessary.)
2. **Margin subgingival, GCF encroaching?** → Retract: non-impregnated cord (#000/#00) or cervical clamp (B4/212) to expose margin + block GCF until cure. Rinse any hemostatic before bonding.
3. **Lesion already deep/subgingival (indirect or large direct)?** → Consider cervical margin relocation to lift the margin supragingival before finishing (ElHaddad 2026).
4. **Never** deepen a margin subgingivally just to place a cord — weigh Schätzle's long-term periodontal cost.
5. **Re-treating a debonded cervical restoration?** → Roughen the dentin (do not just clean; Lührs 2020); use a multi-step or selective-enamel-etch protocol rather than one-step self-etch (Mahn 2015, Saengnil 2022); skip NaOCl deproteinization (Favetti 2022); check for occlusal wear facets and record mandibular/premolar site as higher risk. An enamel bevel is optional — it does not improve retention (Schroeder 2015, Heintze 2010, Mahn 2015).
6. **Repeated loss on sclerotic dentin, or chairtime/cost is limiting?** → A glass hybrid or RMGIC approach is a reasonable alternative (Göstemeyer/Schwendicke 2021, no survival difference; Fernández 2026).
