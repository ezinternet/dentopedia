## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 19편(동물 형태계측 5·기법·해부 7·플랩리스 전환 근거 7)을 **혈행 축** 하나로 묶은 spine — [[flapless-vs-flapped-implant-surgery-overview]]가 다룬 임상결과(실패율·치조정골·통증)에 *왜 그 결과가 나오는지*의 혈관 기전을 잇는다. 테제: **골막 (Periosteum) 박리의 혈행 페널티는 실재하고 측정 가능하지만, 자가 제한적이며(3–28일 시간창) 뼈 차원의 이득으로는 환원되지 않는다.**
> - **혈관 손상의 직접 증거**: 형광혈관조영술 (Fluorescein Angiography) 개 4마리에서 전층 판막 거상 자체만으로 판막 혈류가 기저치 대비 유의 감소 — 최소 3일, 치간부는 7일 지속; 혈관외 확산은 모세관내 순환보다 먼저·더 넓게 회복 (McLean 1995).
> - **연결 단절**: 점막골막판 (Mucoperiosteal Flap) 거상 시 치은-골막 혈관신과 치주인대 (Periodontal Ligament, PDL) 혈관신이 치조골을 관통하는 순환로와의 연결이 단절됨 (Nobuto 2003).
> - **재혈관화 타임라인 (Nobuto 시리즈, 비글)**: 3일차 싹돋움 (Sprouting)·가교 (Bridging)·내감식 (Intussusception) 신생혈관 + 혈액섬 (Blood Island) 유사 구조 → 5–7일차 정맥상 (Sinusoid) 형태 성숙·볼크만관 (Volkmann's Canal) 개구부 주변 골흡수 시작 → 7–14일차 신생혈관 주변 파골·조골 활성 병행(혈관신생-골 리모델링 연쇄) → 21–28일차 퇴행 후 성긴 망상 재구성·골표면 평탄화.
> - **일과성 기능 저하 제안**: PDL 강이 술 후 14일경 최대 확장되며 골흡수가 동반되어 치아 지지 기능이 일시 감소·술 후 동요를 초래할 수 있다는 해부학적 제안 (Nobuto 2003, 동물 — 사람 직접 검증 없음).
> - **혈행 페널티가 실제 청구되는 곳은 막 피개**: 레이저 도플러 유량측정 (Laser Doppler Flowmetry, LDF) 개 연구에서 막 쪽 판막 관류가 무막 대조측보다 4개 시점 모두 낮았고, 2주 내 막 노출이 생긴 3/4마리 모두 막 쪽 관류가 더 낮았음 (Zanetta-Barbosa 1993) — 막 노출률 6.95–13.1%·노출 시 골형성률 96.6%→46.5% (~6배 붕괴, Plonka 2017)과 연결되는 관류-노출-골형성 사슬.
> - **골막이완절개 (Periosteal Releasing Incision, PRI)의 트레이드오프**: 단일 기법 중 최대 이동량 (+5.5mm, Plonka 2017)이지만 하악 후방부에서 하순동맥 (Inferior Labial Artery, ILA, 평균 직경 2.72±0.26mm)·안면동맥 분지가 골막에 인접 (Iwanaga 2019) — 부종·출혈·이상감각 위험 (Hur 2025). 대안: 이중판절개 (Double Flap Incision, DFI) 9.64 vs 7.13mm 이동·통증·부종 유의 낮음 (Ogata 2013 RCT), 구개측 이완절개로 협측 PRI 완전 회피 (Kim 2017, 상악 한정), 변형 골막이완절개 (Modified PRI, MPRI)·다이오드 레이저 PRI·콜라겐매트릭스 개방치유로 PRI 생략 (Hur 2025 / Moslemi 2016 / Stankovic 2018).
> - **사람에서 골막 보존이 뼈를 사지는 않는다**: 치조제보존 (ARP) 골 폭·높이 — SR+MA (Lee 2018, 11연구)·RCT (Siu 2023, n=24)·동물 (Araujo 2009) 모두 군 간 차이 없음; 임플란트 변연골소실 (MBL) MD −0.07mm 비유의 (Chrcanovic 2014 MA).
> - **골막 보존이 사는 것은 연조직**: 각화치은 (Keratinized Gingiva, KG) 폭 — 절개군이 무절개 대비 3.21mm 더 소실 (WMD −3.21mm, 95% CI −4.10~−2.33, p<0.00001, Lee 2018); 교합면 연조직 두께 (Siu 2023 p<0.05); 임플란트 주위 점막 혈관 수 51.4 vs 38.2·염증 소견 0/12 vs 5/12 (Kim 2009, 개); 술후 통증 VAS 3.1 vs 5.7 (Tarpara 2025).
> - **종 간 균열**: 동물은 무절개 (Flapless) 임상지수 우위 (탐침깊이 1.0 vs 1.7mm, Llamas-Monteagudo 2017 SR), 사람은 우세 경향만으로 결론 미합치 — 동물의 우위가 점막 혈관·연조직 축(측정 가능한 것)이지 골 차원이 아님과 정합.
> - **임상 takeaway**: ① 무절개를 "뼈 보존"으로 팔지 말 것 — 근거는 연조직·편의성 축에 있음 ② 막을 덮어야 하는 판막 수술에서 진짜 적은 관류-노출 사슬 — 필요 이동량만큼만 이완하고 PRI 깊이·부위를 골라 대안 기법 활용 ③ 골막 박리 후 치유는 시간창이 있는 생리 (3–7일 관류 저하 → ~14일 흡수 피크 → 21–28일 재조직화)이므로 단기 소견을 과잉 해석하지 말 것.

## Three-line Summary

Nineteen held papers (5 animal morphometry, 7 technique/anatomy, 7 flapless-transition evidence) synthesize the vascular axis of mucoperiosteal flap elevation: elevating a full-thickness flap causes a direct, measurable perfusion deficit (significant circulation reduction lasting ≥3 days, 7 days interproximally, McLean 1995) and severs the transcortical gingivo-periosteal–PDL connections (Nobuto 2003), which triggers a potent, stereotyped angiogenic recovery coupled to a transient osteoclastic remodeling wave (sprouting/bridging/intussusception by day 3, sinusoidal vessels with resorption by days 7–14, mesh reorganization and smooth bone surface by days 21–28, Nobuto 2005 ×2).

The penalty is paid clinically where a flap must cover a membrane: flap perfusion over membranes is measurably lower and lower perfusion tracks with membrane exposure (Zanetta-Barbosa 1993), and exposure collapses regeneration ~6-fold (96.6%→46.5%, Plonka 2017) — making PRI (+5.5 mm advancement) a vascular/anatomic trade-off instrument with anatomy-defined risk (facial artery/ILA adjacent to buccal periosteum, Iwanaga 2019) and lower-morbidity alternatives (DFI, palatal release, MPRI, laser PRI, collagen-matrix open healing).

Sparing the periosteum (flapless) does not convert into bone: ARP ridge width/height null across an SR+MA (Lee 2018), RCT (Siu 2023) and animal histology (Araujo 2009), implant MBL null (Chrcanovic 2014); what it buys is soft tissue — keratinized gingiva (+3.21 mm, Lee 2018), mucosal vascularity (Kim 2009), occlusal tissue thickness (Siu 2023) and early comfort (Tarpara 2025) — complementing the outcome-axis synthesis in [[flapless-vs-flapped-implant-surgery-overview]].

## 세줄요약

19편(동물 형태계측 5·기법·해부 7·플랩리스 전환 7)을 혈행 축으로 종합: 점막골막판 (Mucoperiosteal Flap) 거상은 직접 측정 가능한 관류 손상(최소 3일, 치간부 7일 지속)과 치조골 관통 순환로의 단절을 유발하고, 3–28일에 걸친 강력한 혈관신생 (Angiogenesis)-골 리모델링 연쇄(3일 싹돋움·내감식 → 7–14일 정맥상 혈관+파골/조골 병행 → 21–28일 망상 재구성)로 회복된다.

혈행 페널티가 실제로 청구되는 지점은 막 피개 판막 — 관류 감소가 막 노출과 연관되고 노출 시 골형성률이 ~6배 붕괴(96.6%→46.5%)되므로, 골막이완절개 (PRI, +5.5mm 이동)는 혈관·해부학적 위험(하순동맥 (ILA) 직경 2.72mm가 골막 인접)과 맞바꾸는 도구이며 이중판절개 (DFI)·구개측 이완절개·MPRI 등 저손상 대안이 존재한다.

사람에서 골막 보존(무절개)은 치조제보존 (ARP) 골 폭·높이·변연골소실 (MBL)에서 전부 귀무이고(SR+MA·RCT·동물 일치), 실제 이득은 각화치은 폭 +3.21mm·점막 혈관 분포·연조직 두께·초기 편의성 — 임상결과 축 종합은 [[flapless-vs-flapped-implant-surgery-overview]] 참조.

## Synthesis

**The vascular penalty is real, direct, and time-bounded.** The chain runs: (1) elevation alone — before any resection or grafting — drops flap circulation significantly below baseline for at least 3 days (7 days at interproximal sites), with extravascular diffusion recovering faster than intracapillary perfusion (McLean 1995); (2) the elevated flap's gingivo-periosteal plexus loses its transcortical connections to the PDL plexus (Nobuto 2003); (3) the tissue mounts a potent, stereotyped angiogenic response — sprouting, bridging and intussusception already by day 3, blood-island-like endothelial clusters, maturing sinusoidal vessels by days 5–7 (Nobuto 2005 3-D); (4) this angiogenesis is *coupled to bone remodeling*: osteoclastic resorption begins around Volkmann's canal openings and around the new sinusoidal vessels (days 5–14), glomerulus-like vessel aggregates sit inside resorption cavities with activated osteoblasts around them (day 14), and by days 21–28 the plexus regresses into a coarse mesh as the bone surface re-smooths (Nobuto 2003, 2005 ×2). Two clinically usable consequences fall out of this timeline. First, the penalty is a *physiologic window*, not a scar — perfusion dip days 0–7, resorption peak around day 14, reorganization days 21–28 — so short-term findings (early swelling, transient radiolucency, transient mobility where PDL is involved) should be interpreted against this schedule, not as complications. Second, the resorption is vascular-mediated: new vessels *carry* the osteoclast wave, which is presumably why a fully reflected, well-reapproximated flap still shows a remodeling cost at the crest.

**Where the penalty is actually billed: membrane coverage.** The one setting in our holdings where measured flap hypoperfusion maps onto a hard clinical endpoint is GTR/GBR: membrane-side flaps showed lower LDF than control sides at every measured period, and every animal whose membrane exposed within 2 weeks (3/4) was one with lower membrane-side perfusion (Zanetta-Barbosa 1993). Exposure is the outcome that converts a perfusion problem into a bone-gain problem — 6.95–13.1% exposure rates, with exposed ePTFE sites regenerating 46.5% vs 96.6% at covered sites (~6-fold, Plonka 2017). The synthesis: the vascular cost of flap elevation is usually absorbed silently, but a flap that must seal a membrane under tension is the scenario where absorption fails.

**The PRI is a trade-off instrument, not a free lever.** Plonka's framework quantifies the upside — the periosteal releasing incision is the single largest advancement gain (+5.5 mm) — and defines when it is needed (moderate 3–6 mm, major ≥7 mm advancement strata). The cost side is vascular and anatomic: the ILA runs adjacent to the buccal periosteum in every examined side (mean Ø 2.72±0.26 mm at the PRI midpoint region, Iwanaga 2019), and the PRI literature itself lists edema, bleeding, paresthesia and discomfort as its morbidity (Hur 2025). The held alternatives all attack this trade-off from different directions: DFI buys *more* advancement than PRI (9.64 vs 7.13 mm, P<.001) with significantly less pain and swelling (Ogata 2013 RCT); the palatal releasing incision eliminates the buccal PRI entirely in maxillary cases (Kim 2017); MPRI (Hur 2025) and diode-laser PRI (Moslemi 2016) reduce the trauma of the release itself; and collagen-matrix open healing skips the release altogether (Stankovic 2018). Note the evidence asymmetry: the +5.5 mm PRI figure comes from a systematic review, the alternatives from single RCT/case-series/technique papers — the direction is consistent, the magnitudes are not interchangeable.

**Sparing the periosteum does not buy bone in humans.** Across every hard-tissue endpoint we hold, flapless vs flapped is null: ARP ridge width and height (SR+MA of 11 studies, Lee 2018; single-blind RCT n=24, Siu 2023; 6-month dog histology, Araujo 2009) and implant marginal bone loss (MD −0.07 mm, Chrcanovic 2014). The animal-versus-human split (Llamas-Monteagudo 2017) is consistent with this reading: animals show flapless superiority in *clinical parameters* — probing depth 1.0 vs 1.7 mm, inflammation 0/12 vs 5/12 — which are mucosal/soft-tissue readouts, exactly the axis where Kim 2009 found the flapless advantage (vessel number 51.4 vs 38.2, area fraction 1.7% vs 1.2%, inflammation 0/12 vs 5/12). The species gap dissolves once you ask what each species' endpoint measures.

**What sparing the periosteum does buy is soft tissue — and it is mechanistically the same claim.** Keratinized gingiva is the largest quantified benefit: flapped ARP lost 3.21 mm more KG width than flapless (WMD, 95% CI −4.10 to −2.33, p<0.00001, Lee 2018) — an order of magnitude larger than any bone-dimension difference in the same comparisons. Siu 2023 adds significantly thicker occlusal soft tissue at re-entry (p<0.05) and numerically more vital mineralized tissue (44±10%, NS). Tarpara 2025 adds halved early pain (VAS d3 3.1 vs 5.7). The perfusion→mucosa chain (McLean's 3–7 day dip; Kim's vessel counts) explains why the soft-tissue axis is where flaplessness shows: the periosteum's blood supply feeds the mucosa the flap is made of, more than it feeds the ridge underneath it.

**Boundary with the outcome-axis overview.** Implant failure rates (Chrcanovic's bias-caveated RR 1.75) and the live Surendra-vs-Tarpara crestal controversy are synthesized on the outcome axis in [[flapless-vs-flapped-implant-surgery-overview]]; this page adds the vascular mechanism and the ARP dimensional null, and does not re-litigate those endpoints.

## Conflicts and open points

- **Animal superiority vs human null** (Llamas-Monteagudo 2017): resolved here as an *endpoint-axis* artifact (mucosal readouts favor flapless in both species; bone readouts null in humans) — but this resolution is cross-paper inference, not a tested hypothesis.
- **Chrcanovic's RR 1.75** is driven by high-risk-of-bias studies and pre-guided-era protocols (see [[flapless-vs-flapped-implant-surgery-overview]]); no modern guided-flapless failure meta-analysis is held.
- **Mechanism tier fragility**: the entire morphometric timeline rests on one research group (Nobuto series, 2003–2005) plus McLean 1995 — all dog studies, n=4–12, and 5 of the 6 animal pages are abstract-only. The timeline is a consistent story, not a replicated one.
- **No human perfusion measurement exists in our holdings** — LDF over membranes was dog-only (Zanetta-Barbosa); the human link from elevation to perfusion deficit is inferred, not measured.
- **No RCT of PRI-sparing vs PRI on vascularity or bone outcomes** — the alternatives (DFI, palatal release, MPRI, laser PRI, collagen matrix) are supported by technique papers and small series, and DFI's RCT endpoint was advancement+morbidity, not bone.
- **Nobuto 2003's transient-mobility proposition** is an animal suggestion with no human verification; do not counsel patients on it as established.

## Clinical takeaways for chairside

1. **Do not justify flapless by bone preservation.** The ARP bone-dimension null spans SR+MA, RCT and animal histology (Lee 2018, Siu 2023, Araujo 2009) and implant MBL is null (Chrcanovic 2014). Justify flapless by soft tissue (KG width, tissue thickness, vascularity) and early comfort — and require CBCT/guided planning to compensate for lost visualization (decision ladder on [[flapless-vs-flapped-implant-surgery-overview]]).
2. **When a flap must seal a membrane, perfusion is the currency.** Exposure collapses regeneration ~6-fold (Plonka 2017) and hypoperfusion tracks exposure (Zanetta-Barbosa 1993) — advance only as much as coverage requires (PASS), not maximally.
3. **Choose the release instrument by anatomy and needed millimeters.** Moderate-to-major advancement in the maxilla: consider palatal release (Kim 2017) or DFI (Ogata 2013 — more millimeters, less morbidity). Posterior mandible: the ILA/facial-artery branches sit adjacent to the buccal periosteum around the first/second molar (Iwanaga 2019) — control depth, consider MPRI/laser variants (Hur 2025, Moslemi 2016), or open-healing collagen matrix when the defect allows skipping the release (Stankovic 2018).
4. **Read early post-flap findings against the vascular schedule.** Perfusion dip days 0–7, resorptive peak around day 14, reorganization days 21–28 — early crestal changes or transient findings inside this window are expected physiology of the angiogenic-remodeling cascade (Nobuto series), not automatic failure signals.
5. **Frame flapless vs flapped for patients on the soft-tissue axis**: what the evidence supports is less keratinized-tissue loss, thicker occlusal tissue and less early pain — with equivalent bone outcomes.
