> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **결론**: 골내결손 (intrabony defect) 재생술은 개방 판막 소파술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD)보다 **1년 시점에서 확실히 낫다**. 그러나 "무엇을 더 얹을수록 좋다"는 직관과 "장기적으로도 OFD보다 낫다"는 주장은 보유 근거로 지지되지 않는다.
> - **법랑기질유도체 (Enamel Matrix Derivative, EMD) 단독 vs OFD**: RCT 67편 SR+MA에서 골내결손 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) +1.14 mm, 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) +1.00 mm 추가 개선 (Miron 2026 전문: 이 비교 20 RCT, I² 87%, GRADE 매우 낮음). [확인]
> - **EMD + 골이식 = 임상 이득 없거나 미미**: Hasuike 2024(12개 연구, 12개월)는 EMD 단독 대비 CAL +0.05 mm, PD +0.27 mm로 모두 비유의, **방사선 결손 충만만** +0.80 mm 유의. Miron 2026 전문(12 RCT)은 CAL +0.33 mm(95% CI 0.02–0.64)로 겨우 유의했으나 PD는 비유의(+0.26 mm)이고 **출판 편향 강하게 의심·GRADE 매우 낮음**. 초록의 "이종골 병용 CAL +0.79 mm"는 본문 결과에 없고, 고찰은 오히려 이종골 병용이 EMD 단독보다 CAL이 **적었다**고 써서 이종골 방향은 논문 안에서도 불일치. [확인] → 기존 Stavropoulos 2021 NMA·치주 치료 ladder의 "병용 > 단독"을 **EMD 축에서는 좁혀야** 한다(이득은 많아야 0.3 mm 수준).
> - **장기 내구성 (≥5년)**: RCT 17편·501 결손, 평균 추적 9.3년. 조직유도재생술 (Guided Tissue Regeneration, GTR)은 기저 대비 CAL 3.27 mm 획득이 유지되지만, **OFD도 기저 대비 CAL 2.49 mm**를 얻어 GTR의 OFD 대비 우위 1.52 mm(95% CI −0.06~3.10)는 p=0.06으로 비유의, 확실성 낮음 (Wang 2025 전문). 이전에 지적한 CI–p값 불일치는 초록이 부호를 빠뜨린 탓으로, 전문에서 해소됨. 재생술 중 **골이식 단독**군은 생물학제제군보다 CAL이 0.94 mm 적었다. [확인]
> - **차폐막 vs EMD**: 전체로는 GTR과 EMD 차이가 비유의(PPD +0.51 mm, CAL +0.19 mm; 10 RCT)이고, **비흡수성 차폐막 하위군만** EMD보다 PPD 1.08 mm 더 감소 (Miron 2026). Miron은 다른 SR(Rojas)을 인용해 GTR 결손의 28.8%에서 차폐막 노출을 보고하지만 이는 2차 인용이고 원 SR은 위키에 없다. Wang 2025 메타회귀에서도 비흡수성 차폐막이 장기 CAL·PD 개선과 연관. [확인]
> - **생물학제제 간 교환 가능성**: 히알루론산 (Hyaluronic Acid, HA)은 CAL에서 EMD에 비열등 (WMD 0.51 mm, 3 RCT, Mahintach 2026). 혈소판 풍부 섬유소 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)는 골내결손에서 EMD와 임상적으로 동등 (Miron 2024, 55 RCT). 선택 기준은 우월성이 아니라 비용·취급성. [확인]
> - **칼슘포스페이트 + GTR vs OFD**: 골내결손 CAL 1.54 mm, 치근이개부 CAL 1.81 mm (Kouhi 2024, 7편). 초록 CI가 0을 포함하는데도 "유의"로 보고되어 **인용 시 주의**. [확인]
> - **치근이개부 (furcation)**: EMD 근거는 "제한적" (Miron 2026 RCT 4편). 2급 치근이개부는 PRF·골이식 근거가 더 두텁다 (Miron 2024, 21 RCT). 치근이개부 침범 자체는 10–15년 치아 상실 위험 RR 2.21 (Nibali 2016). [확인]
> - **비외과 보조로서의 EMD**: PPD +0.30 mm만 추가되고 CAL +0.21 mm는 비유의 (Miron 2026, 9 RCT) → 비외과 치료에서는 일상 권고 근거가 약하다. [확인]
> - **술전 부위 준비가 재료만큼 중요할 수 있다**: MIST + EMD + 이종골 전 2주 독시사이클린 국소도포 시 CAL 4.1 vs 3.2 mm, 성공률 83.3% vs 46.7% (NNT 2.73, Aimetti 2025, 비흡연자). [확인]
> - **임상 takeaway**: 재평가 후 잔존 깊은 치주낭 + 골내결손 ≥3 mm → 재생술 적응. 기본은 **EMD(또는 PRF/HA) 단독 + 판막 설계·부위 준비 최적화**. 골이식 추가는 비지지형 결손의 공간 유지나 방사선 충만이 목표일 때로 한정. 환자에게는 "5년 이상 OFD 대비 우위는 아직 확정되지 않았다"고 설명한다.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of the wiki's intrabony and furcation regeneration evidence centred on GTR and EMD: three SR+MAs ingested on 2026-11-22 (Miron 2026, Hasuike 2024, Wang 2025) plus a Kouhi 2024 SR+MA are read against the earlier Stavropoulos 2021 network meta-analysis and the biologic-alternative literature (HA, PRF, bioactive glass).

At 12 months, regeneration reliably beats OFD (EMD alone +1.14 mm CAL), but adding a bone graft to EMD adds little or no clinical attachment (CAL +0.05 mm, ns, Hasuike 2024; +0.33 mm, very-low certainty with suspected publication bias, Miron 2026) beyond radiographic fill, and at ≥5 years the GTR-over-OFD margin (CAL 1.52 mm) is no longer statistically secure.

Clinically, the default is EMD (or an equivalent biologic) alone with optimized flap design and site preparation. The "combination beats monotherapy" message of earlier syntheses holds at best for GTR + graft and for xenograft add-ons, not as a general rule.

## 세줄요약

치주 골내결손·치근이개부 재생 근거를 조직유도재생술 (Guided Tissue Regeneration, GTR)과 법랑기질유도체 (Enamel Matrix Derivative, EMD) 중심으로 종합했다. 2026-11-22 신규 SR+MA 3편(Miron 2026·Hasuike 2024·Wang 2025)과 Kouhi 2024를 Stavropoulos 2021 NMA 및 히알루론산 (HA)·혈소판 풍부 섬유소 (PRF)·생체활성유리 대안 근거와 대조했다.

12개월 시점에서 재생술은 개방 판막 소파술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD)보다 확실히 낫다(EMD 단독 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) +1.14 mm). 그러나 EMD에 골이식을 더해도 CAL 이득은 없고(+0.05 mm, 비유의) 방사선 충만만 늘며, 5년 이상에서는 GTR의 OFD 대비 우위(CAL 1.52 mm)가 통계적으로 확정되지 않는다.

임상 기본값은 EMD(또는 동등한 생물학제제) 단독에 판막 설계·부위 준비를 최적화하는 것이다. 기존 종합의 "병용 > 단독"은 GTR+이식재와 이종골 병용에서만 제한적으로 성립하며, 일반 원칙으로 쓸 수 없다.

## Summary

Periodontal regeneration aims to rebuild the attachment apparatus — new cementum, a functionally oriented periodontal ligament (PDL) and alveolar bone — on a previously diseased root surface. Two strategies dominate the clinical literature: **GTR**, which uses a barrier membrane to exclude gingival epithelium and connective tissue so that PDL-derived cells can repopulate the defect, and **EMD**, which applies amelogenin-rich enamel-matrix proteins to a conditioned root surface to mimic root development. Miron 2026 notes that preclinical work shows EMD inducing new cementum, PDL and bone, especially on dry, conditioned root surfaces with minimal blood contamination, and that EMD is one of the few biologic agents with human histologic evidence of regeneration. Sculean 2015 likewise identifies GTR and EMD as the modalities with the broadest combined animal and human evidence.

The newly ingested evidence changes three parts of the wiki's earlier picture, which rested mainly on the Stavropoulos 2021 NMA:

1. **Short-term efficacy over OFD is confirmed.** Miron 2026 (67 RCTs, 41 of them intrabony) finds EMD adds 1.14 mm CAL and 1.00 mm PPD reduction over OFD, consistent with Stavropoulos's CAL range of +1.26 to +2.66 mm for regenerative approaches versus OFD.
2. **"Combination beats monotherapy" does not hold for EMD + graft.** Hasuike 2024 isolates the comparison (EMD + any graft vs EMD alone, RCTs, 12 months) and finds no CAL or PD benefit, only greater radiographic defect fill. Miron 2026's full text finds a small pooled CAL gain (+0.33 mm, 95% CI 0.02–0.64; PD +0.26 mm, ns) at very low certainty with publication bias strongly suspected. Its abstract's "xenograft CAL +0.79 mm" exception does not appear in the Results, and its Discussion states the opposite (EMD + xenograft gained less CAL than EMD alone), so no graft type can be singled out. Conversely, adding EMD to a bone graft does help (CAL +0.84 mm over graft alone, 5 RCTs). Stavropoulos's ranking of combinations as "most efficacious" came from an indirect network and is narrowed, not overturned: GTR + graft is not tested by either new review.
3. **Long-term superiority over OFD is uncertain.** Wang 2025 (17 RCTs, 5–20 years) shows that regenerative gains over baseline persist (GTR CAL +3.27 mm), but OFD also gains 2.49 mm CAL from baseline, and the GTR-versus-OFD difference (CAL 1.52 mm, 95% CI −0.06 to 3.10; p = 0.06) does not reach significance, with low certainty. Stavropoulos's "all regenerative approaches outperform OFD at medium-to-long term" is therefore qualified on the time axis.

## Where the Evidence Agrees

- **Regeneration beats OFD at 6–12 months.** Every held comparison (Miron 2026, Stavropoulos 2021, Kouhi 2024, Miron 2024 for PRF) points the same way, with CAL advantages in the 1–1.5 mm range for single modalities.
- **Biologics are broadly interchangeable.** EMD, PRF and HA have no shown hierarchy: PRF is clinically equivalent to EMD (Miron 2024; Estrin 2026), and HA is non-inferior for CAL (Mahintach 2026). The choice is a cost, availability and handling decision, not an efficacy one.
- **Radiographic fill and clinical attachment can diverge.** Adding a graft raises radiographic defect fill (Hasuike 2024: +0.80 mm; alloplastic subgroup +0.88 mm) without raising CAL. Radiographic bone fill is therefore not a substitute endpoint for attachment gain.

## Where the Evidence Disagrees or Is Unresolved

| Tension | Side A | Side B | Current reading |
|---|---|---|---|

## Clinical Decision Framework

1. **Indication.** Use regeneration after step-2 re-evaluation leaves a residual deep pocket with an intrabony component of at least 3 mm. That is the inclusion threshold in Wang 2025 and the regenerative branch in the Liang 2025 cemental-tear consensus.
2. **Prepare the site.** Control inflammation before surgery. In one RCT, pre-surgical local doxycycline cut the composite-outcome failure rate from 53.3% to 16.7% (success 83.3% vs 46.7%; Aimetti 2025). It also lowered bleeding at surgery (30% vs 66.7%).
3. **Default material: a biologic alone.** EMD is the most evidenced (human histology, 41 intrabony RCTs). PRF and HA are acceptable equivalents where cost or handling matter.
4. **Add a graft only with a specific goal.** Valid goals are space maintenance in a non-contained defect, or radiographic fill. Do not expect added attachment gain (Hasuike 2024). If a graft is added to EMD, expect at most ~0.3 mm extra CAL (Miron 2026, very low certainty). No graft type is reliably better: Miron's xenograft signal is internally inconsistent, and in Hasuike 2024 the xenograft benefit did not appear with minimally invasive flaps.
5. **Choosing membrane-based GTR.** It may give more pocket reduction (Miron 2026). It is the approach with long-term data (Wang 2025; Liu 2019 cites 120-month CAL-V 3.5 mm with absorbable barriers). Weigh it against technique sensitivity, for which the wiki holds no comparative data.
6. **Furcation.** EMD evidence is thin. For class II furcations, PRF, grafts and membranes have the larger RCT base (Miron 2024). Furcation involvement predicts tooth loss over 10–15 years (Nibali 2016, RR 2.21), so a regenerative attempt is reasonable before extraction.
7. **Non-surgical use.** EMD as an adjunct to subgingival instrumentation adds only 0.30 mm PPD and a non-significant 0.21 mm CAL (Miron 2026). It is not a routine recommendation.
8. **Counselling.** Tell patients that the 1-year gain is well supported, that the gain over baseline is maintained beyond 5 years, and that superiority over simple flap surgery at 5+ years is not yet established.

## Gaps (what the wiki does not hold)

- A primary-source comparison of GTR versus EMD complications. Membrane exposure (28.8%) is known here only as Miron 2026's citation of another SR.
- Newer head-to-head evidence on GTR + graft versus GTR alone.
- Defect-morphology-stratified results (wall number, defect angle). Hasuike 2024 flags under-reporting. Wang 2025's meta-regression found no significant association with defect wall number; baseline CAL, follow-up time and non-resorbable membranes were the retained CAL predictors.
- Smokers. They are nearly absent from the PRF literature (3/55 RCTs; Miron 2024), and Aimetti 2025 enrolled non-smokers only.
- Full texts. Miron 2026 and Wang 2025 were re-ingested from full text on 2026-11-22. Kouhi 2024 remains abstract-only, so its subgroup and risk-of-bias detail is not verified here.

## Adjacent Applications

- **Root coverage.** EMD gave modest, non-significant root-coverage gains with substantial heterogeneity (Miron 2026). In multiple recessions, mCAF + EMD is promising but less evidenced than mCAF + connective tissue graft (Gualtieri 2025). See [[overviews/root-coverage-surgery-graft-adjunct-outcome-overview]].
