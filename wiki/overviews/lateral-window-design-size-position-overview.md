## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **범위**: 측방창 상악동거상술 (Lateral Window Sinus Floor Elevation, LSFE)에서 골창(antrostomy) 크기·수직 위치·수평 위치 계획에 관한 보유 근거 종합. 술전 콘빔CT (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT) 기반 개인화 계획의 세 축(크기·높이·전후)을 각각 지배하는 인자를 정리한다. [확인]
> - **크기: 천공 위험인자 아님** — Alalshaikh 2024 SR+MA는 창 크기가 슈나이더막 천공 (Schneiderian Membrane Perforation, SMP) 에 영향을 주지 않음을 확인했다. [확인] 그러나 작은 창은 생물학적 이점(조직학적 골성숙↑)이 있고(Sun 2026 Avila-Ortiz 인용), 임상 트렌드도 최소화 방향이다(Scaini 2025). [확인]
> - **수직 위치: PSAA가 결정인자** — 창 높이 >11 mm에서 골내형 (intraosseous) 후상치조동맥 (Posterior Superior Alveolar Artery, PSAA) 비율이 유의하게 증가한다(χ²=19.11, p=0.004; Urechescu 2026). 치조정 기준 PSAA 평균 위치 ~16 mm(Gulec 2025). [확인]
> - **수평 위치: 중격이 결정인자** — 상악동 중격 (Maxillary Sinus Septa) 유병률 33.2%(Henriques 2022, 22,460 상악동 SR+MA). 86%가 횡방향으로 표준 창 절개선을 가로지른다. 중격 위에 창을 걸면 천공 위험 OR 4.03(Yang 2024). [확인]
> - **측벽 두께: 기구 선택 기준** — 측방창 측벽 두께 (Lateral Wall Thickness, LWT) ≥1.25 mm는 리머 드릴 접근의 안전 임계값(Monje 2016). 얇은 측벽에서는 피에조전기 기구 (piezoelectric device) 로 전환한다. [확인]
> - **실용 순서**: CBCT에서 ① 중격 유무·위치(수평 창 디자인), ② PSAA 위치·유형·직경(수직 창 높이 계획), ③ 측벽 두께(기구 선택) 순으로 판독한다. [미검증 — 이 순서 자체는 추론]
> - **한계**: 창 치수의 최적값을 비교한 RCT 없음; 모든 수직 위험 데이터는 후향적 CBCT; 창 크기–골성숙 연관은 단일 소규모 비교 증례군.

## Three-line Summary

This overview synthesizes the available evidence on lateral window (antrostomy) size and position planning for sinus floor elevation, drawing on data from Alalshaikh 2024 (perforation risk SR+MA), Sun 2026 (microsurgical review), Scaini 2025 (state-of-the-art narrative review), Urechescu 2026 (PSAA localization, 4-class height framework), Gulec 2025 (PSAA CBCT anatomy), Henriques 2022 (septa SR+MA), Yang 2024 (septa and perforation SR+MA), and Monje 2016 (lateral wall thickness threshold).
Window size is not a perforation risk factor, but smaller windows have a histologic graft-maturation advantage; vertical position is governed by PSAA risk (window height >11 mm significantly elevates intraosseous PSAA encounter), and horizontal position by septa (33% sinus prevalence, 86% transverse, OR 4.03 for perforation).
Individualized preoperative CBCT reading — septum location first, then PSAA position, then lateral wall thickness — drives the window design decision; no single universal window dimension exists.

## 세줄요약

이 종합 페이지는 측방창 상악동거상술 (Lateral Window Sinus Floor Elevation, LSFE) 에서 골창(antrostomy) 크기·수직 위치·수평 위치 계획에 관한 보유 근거를 Alalshaikh 2024, Sun 2026, Scaini 2025, Urechescu 2026, Gulec 2025, Henriques 2022, Yang 2024, Monje 2016 에서 종합한다.
창 크기는 슈나이더막 천공 (Schneiderian Membrane Perforation, SMP) 위험인자가 아니나 작은 창은 조직학적 골성숙 이점이 있고; 수직 위치는 후상치조동맥 (Posterior Superior Alveolar Artery, PSAA) 위험(창 높이 >11 mm → 골내형 PSAA 유의 증가, p=0.004)이, 수평 위치는 중격 (Maxillary Sinus Septa, 33%, OR 4.03) 이 결정한다.
단일 최적 창 치수는 없으며; 개인화된 CBCT 판독(중격 → PSAA → 측벽 두께 순)이 창 디자인의 근거 프레임이다.

## §1. Window Size — Not a Perforation Risk Factor, But Biology Favors Smaller

### 1.1 Window size does not affect perforation likelihood

**Alalshaikh 2024 (SR+MA)** ranked the lateral-window SMP predictors and confirmed that **window size did not affect perforation likelihood** — it was not a significant predictor in the multi-factor analysis. Number of missing teeth was also non-significant. The dominant predictors were thin membrane (<1.5 mm), sinus septa (~3× risk), and smoking (~1.6× risk). [확인]

This is a critical "what does NOT matter" anchor: surgeons optimizing window design for perforation prevention should focus on septa location and membrane thickness, not on window dimensions per se.

### 1.2 Smaller windows: biological advantage and clinical trend

Despite the null perforation finding, evidence suggests a **histologic graft-maturation advantage for smaller windows**:

Sun 2026 cites Avila-Ortiz (24 lateral procedures, allograft, 6-month core biopsy): larger window area correlated negatively with vital bone (VB) and positively with residual allograft (RA). The proposed mechanism: a smaller bony window preserves more osteogenic lateral-wall cells participating in graft remodeling. [확인]

Actual window dimensions in microsurgical case series:
- **~5 mm** circular (MGES, Shakibaie, 20 sinuses)
- **5 × 8 mm = 31.4 mm²** ellipse (EBI Sinus Kit, Pariente, 15 procedures)
- **5–6 mm** circular piezo (Merli, 147 sinuses; perforation 6%, vertical gain 7.44 mm)
[확인 — Sun 2026에서 인용된 수치]

Scaini 2025 notes the broader clinical trend toward smaller lateral windows for morbidity reduction, with CBCT + digital surgical planning (DSP) enabling more precise window placement that enables this miniaturization. [확인]

**Clinical bottom line on size**: minimize for morbidity and graft maturation within the envelope that provides adequate access; do not make size decisions based on perforation prevention. [미검증 — 이 임상 결론은 추론]

## §2. Window Vertical Position — PSAA as the Determinant

### 2.1 Inferior border: sinus floor relationship

Standard positioning: **inferior window margin 2–3 mm above the sinus floor** (동저 기준 상방 2–3 mm), to allow elevator access from below the membrane-floor junction. [미검증 — 위키에 이 임계값을 직접 지지한 비교 연구 없음; 술기 관습]

### 2.2 Superior border: 4-class PSAA risk framework (Urechescu 2026)

Urechescu 2026 introduced the evidence basis for the **vertical position decision**: window height above the sinus floor correlates with the probability of encountering an intraosseous PSAA:

| Window class | Height above sinus floor | Mean PSAA-floor distance | Intraosseous PSAA frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Class 1 | <3 mm | 2.0 ± 0.7 mm | 30.4% |
| Class 2 (small) | 3–8 mm | 5.9 ± 2.9 mm | 20.9% |
| Class 3 (medium) | 8–11 mm | 9.4 ± 0.9 mm | 25.9% |
| **Class 4 (large)** | **>11 mm** | **14.2 ± 2.9 mm** | **35.0% ↑** |

χ²(6)=19.11, **p=0.004**; Cramer's V=0.111. Large windows (>11 mm) carry significantly overrepresented intraosseous PSAA (standardized residual +2.3). [확인]

### 2.3 PSAA position from alveolar crest — the absolute ceiling

Gulec 2025 (Turkish cohort, 510 sinuses): mean PSAA-to-alveolar-crest distance **15.93 mm (R) / 17.24 mm (L)**, i.e., ~16 mm average. [확인]

This provides the absolute upper reference: the superior osteotomy line approaching 16 mm above the alveolar crest significantly increases PSAA encounter probability. Combined with Urechescu's >11 mm height rule (from the sinus floor), both thresholds converge toward the same clinical caution zone.

### 2.4 Practical vertical planning rule

```
Inferior line:  동저 (sinus floor) + 2–3 mm   [술기 관습, 미검증]
Superior line:  CBCT에서 PSAA 위치 확인 후 창 높이 결정
                - 11 mm 이하 가능: Class 2–3, 비교적 안전
                - 11 mm 초과 불가피: CBCT에서 PSAA 경로·유형·직경 필수 확인
                  → 대직경 혈관 확인 시 피에조 en-bloc 거상 전환
                    (psaa-hemorrhage-management-lateral-sinus-lift-overview 참조)
```

## §3. Window Horizontal Position — Septa as the Determinant

### 3.1 Septa prevalence and orientation

Henriques 2022 (SR+MA, 62 studies, 22,460 sinuses): pooled per-sinus septa prevalence **33.2%** (95% CI 27.8–38.5%); per-patient prevalence **41.0%**. Orientation is **86% transverse** — exactly the morphology that bisects a standard lateral window osteotomy line. Sex is not a predictor (OR 0.785, p=0.098). [확인]

Practical implication: approximately **1 in 3 sinuses** requires a modified window approach due to septa.

### 3.2 Septa and perforation risk

Yang 2024 (SR+MA, 10 studies, 2,168 lateral SFEs): septa presence carries **OR 4.03 (95% CI 1.77–9.19)** for SMP. Nemati 2023 multivariate Cox model gives **HR 8.07 (95% CI 2.93–22.29, p<0.001)** — the second-strongest anatomical predictor in that model. [확인]

### 3.3 Horizontal planning strategy

| Scenario | Strategy |
|---|---|
| No septa on CBCT | Standard window, anterior-posterior positioning by implant site |
| Septa anterior to window | Extend window anterior to septum |
| Septa within planned window | **Split into two windows** (anterior and posterior to septum), OR relocate window to clear septum entirely |
| Septa posterior to window | No modification needed |

## §4. Lateral Wall Thickness — Instrument Selection Gate

Monje 2016 (reamer-drill technique, dual-center retrospective): **≥1.25 mm** lateral wall thickness is the safe-zone threshold for the reamer approach (12.5% overall SMP). [확인]

Yang 2024 also confirmed lateral wall thickness as a significant perforation predictor (limited evidence, 3 studies). [확인]

Abullais 2024 (n=150 sinuses): females had significantly greater lateral wall thickness in both premolar and molar regions (p=0.018/0.032); facial index type had no significant correlation — confirming CBCT measurement is mandatory regardless of gross facial morphology. [확인]

Practical rule: measure lateral wall thickness on CBCT at the planned window site. [미검증 — 이 "측정 필수" 권고는 추론]
- **≥1.25 mm**: reamer or rotary approach acceptable
- **<1.25 mm**: piezoelectric device preferred (soft-tissue sparing, less wall-erosion risk)

## §5. Integrated Preoperative CBCT Checklist for Window Design

Based on the held papers, a prioritized reading sequence:

| Priority | CBCT finding | Decision it drives |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Septa: present? location? orientation? | Horizontal window placement / split window vs single |
| **2** | PSAA: visible? type (intraosseous/submucosal)? diameter? distance from sinus floor? | Vertical window height ceiling; en-bloc piezo strategy if large-diameter |
| **3** | Lateral wall thickness at planned site | Instrument selection (reamer vs piezo) |
| **4** | Membrane thickness | Graded risk modifier (no clean cutoff) |
| **5** | Mucous retention cyst | Pre-surgical management; HR 27.75 for perforation |
| **6** | OMC patency and UPSA type | ENT consultation trigger (UPSA 3/4/6) |

**What does NOT need to drive window design decisions**: window SIZE itself (not a perforation predictor per Alalshaikh 2024). [확인]
