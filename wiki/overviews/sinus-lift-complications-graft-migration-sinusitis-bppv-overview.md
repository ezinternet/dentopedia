## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 이 페이지는 상악동거상술(Maxillary Sinus Floor Augmentation, MSFA) 합병증을 3축 — 이식재(소뼈 이종골) 이동·이물 부비동염 / 배출구(ostium) 폐쇄 / 양성돌발두위현훈(BPPV) — 으로 6편을 종합한다. 해부·술기 결정은 [[overviews/sinus-lift-anatomy-ostium-septa-perforation-oms-overview]]로 link-out.
> - **소뼈 이종골(bovine xenograft, ABB/Bio-Oss)은 인체에서 사실상 비흡수**: 입자가 10–20년 뒤에도 그대로 남아 **이동/표면화**한다. Nowzari 2022 — 7/7 증례 모두 임플란트 주위 열구로 입자 이동(2개월~6년), 대부분 무증상이나 만성염증·세균저장소 위험.
> - **후기(수년 뒤) 합병증**: Rodriguez 2019(n=5, 의뢰편향) — 잠복기 2–13년; 13년째 상악동염(CT 80% 혼탁), 7년째 소뼈 관련 낭종(첫 보고). 스펙트럼: 이물반응·구강상악동교통·낭종·임플란트 실패. **치료는 이식재 외과적 제거**(재이종골 아닌 자가골 재건), 입자 산란으로 기술적으로 까다로움.
> - **이물 부비동염은 항생제 불응·재발 가능**: Felisati 2012(증례) — 이식재 감염+변위로 항생제 저항성 사골-상악동염, 1차 수술 후 재발, CT로 잔존 확인 후 2차 정밀 내시경 → 6시간 뒤 자연배출·회복, 6개월 뒤 재증대 성공.
> - **배출구(ostium) 보존이 예방의 핵심**: Vaddi 2021(CBCT n=200) — 상악동저–ostium 높이 유치악 31.4mm/무치악 29.5mm, 점막비후 차감 시 **안전증대높이 ≈27mm(유치악)/≈23mm(무치악)**. Ostium은 47%가 제1대구치 부위·직경 ~2.4mm. 이 높이 초과 시 배출구 폐쇄→부비동염 성립. 무치악은 점막비후 더 큼(6.40 vs 4.05mm, p<0.05).
> - **BPPV — 골절도(osteotome)/malleting 술기 위험**: Akcay 2016(증례) — OSFE 직후 BPPV, 사전 미고지 현훈력 있던 환자서 치료불응·10일 소요. Damsaz 2023(전향 n=112) — 폐쇄식 3.1%(2명) vs 능선분할 0%, 전체 1.8%, **p=0.509(유의 아님)**. 기전=malleting 진동+두부 과신전으로 이석 변위.
> - **예방·인지 요점**: (이식재) 소뼈 비흡수성 인지, 부비동 증상 항생제 불응 시 CT/CBCT 추적·외과적 제거; (배출구) 술전 CBCT로 안전높이 계산, ostium 폐쇄 회피; (BPPV) 술전 현훈력 스크리닝, malleting 힘·두부 과신전 최소화, Epley 정복술.
> - **근거 한계**: 6편 중 case report/series 4편(3편 초록전용)·전향코호트 1(사건 2건)·CBCT 형태계측 1 — 발생률 확정 불가, 방향성·인지 신호로 활용.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 6 papers on maxillary sinus-floor-augmentation complications across 3 axes — graft (bovine xenograft) migration/foreign-body sinusitis, ostium obstruction, and BPPV: bovine xenograft (ABB/Bio-Oss) is **effectively non-biodegradable in humans** (particles intact at 10–20+ years), so it migrates — into the peri-implant sulcus in all 7/7 of Nowzari 2022's cases (2 mo–6 yr, mostly subclinical) or into the sinus, causing late complications years out (Rodriguez 2019: latency 2–13 yr; 13-yr sinusitis with 80% opacification, first reported bovine-bone cyst) whose definitive treatment is surgical removal, not repeat xenograft.

Displaced/infected xenograft can produce antibiotic-refractory sinusitis that first-attempt surgery may not clear (Felisati 2012: relapse then CT-guided second endoscopy → fragment expulsion at 6 h, recovery); prevention hinges on preserving ostium drainage — Vaddi 2021 (CBCT, n=200) gives a safe augmentation height of floor-to-ostium minus mucosal thickening (≈27 mm dentate / ≈23 mm edentulous; ostium in first-molar region in 47%, ~2.4 mm diameter; edentulous mucosal thickening greater, 6.40 vs 4.05 mm, p<0.05).

BPPV after osteotome/closed (malleting) sinus lift is attributed to vibration plus head hyperextension displacing otoliths — Akcay 2016 (n=1, refractory course in a patient with prior vertigo) and Damsaz 2023 (prospective n=112: closed sinus lift 3.1% vs ridge split 0%, overall 1.8%, but p=0.509, not significant, only 2 events); screen for prior vertigo, minimize malleting force/head extension, treat with Epley. Evidence is largely case reports/series (4 of 6, three abstract-only) plus one underpowered cohort — directional and recognition-oriented, not incidence-defining. Anatomy/technique decisions live in the linked sinus-anatomy overview.

## 세줄요약

상악동거상술(MSFA) 합병증 3축(이식재 이동·이물 부비동염 / 배출구 폐쇄 / BPPV)을 6편으로 종합: 소뼈 이종골(Bio-Oss)은 인체서 사실상 비흡수라 10–20년 뒤에도 이동 — Nowzari 7/7 임플란트 주위 열구 이동(대부분 무증상), Rodriguez 후기(2–13년) 합병증(13년째 부비동염 CT 80% 혼탁·소뼈 낭종 첫 보고), 치료는 외과적 제거(재이종골 아닌 자가골).

이물 부비동염은 항생제 불응·재발 가능(Felisati, CT로 잔존확인 후 2차 내시경→6시간 뒤 배출); 예방핵심은 배출구 보존 — Vaddi(CBCT n=200) 안전증대높이 ≈27mm(유치악)/≈23mm(무치악), ostium 47% 제1대구치부·직경 ~2.4mm, 초과 시 폐쇄→부비동염.

BPPV는 골절도/malleting 진동+두부 과신전 이석변위 — Akcay(현훈력 환자 불응) + Damsaz(전향 n=112 폐쇄식 3.1% vs 능선분할 0%, 전체 1.8%, p=0.509 유의 아님). 예방: 술전 현훈력 스크리닝·malleting 최소화·Epley. 근거 대부분 증례/소코호트라 방향성·인지 신호. 해부·술기는 sinus-anatomy 오버뷰 link-out.

## Summary

Sinus-lift complications cluster into three mechanisms a clinician should recognize distinctly: a **material** problem (bovine xenograft never resorbs, so it migrates and can inflame years later), a **drainage** problem (block the ostium and you convert a graft into a sinusitis), and a **vestibular** problem (malleting an osteotome can dislodge otoliths → BPPV). This page synthesizes 6 papers to make each mechanism, its prevention, and its recognition explicit; anatomy/technique selection lives in the linked sinus-anatomy overview.

The unifying prevention theme is **preserve sinus physiology**: keep grafts below the ostium's safe height so the sinus can clear itself, know that bovine particles are permanent (and surface-migrate), and reserve percussion techniques' vestibular risk for patients screened for vertigo.

## Key Contributions

### Axis 1 — Bovine xenograft: non-biodegradable → migration & late complications

| Paper | Design / n | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Nowzari 2022 | Case series, n=7 | **7/7** intact bovine particles migrated into peri-implant sulcus (2 mo–6 yr); no biodegradation; mostly asymptomatic but a chronic-inflammation/bacterial reservoir |
| Rodriguez 2019 | Retrospective series, n=5 (referral-biased) | Complication latency **2–13 yr**; 13-yr sinusitis (80% opacification), first bovine-bone cyst; spectrum: foreign-body reaction, oroantral communication, implant failure; **surgical removal curative** |
| Felisati 2012 | Case report, n=1 | Infected/displaced graft → antibiotic-refractory ethmoidomaxillary sinusitis; relapse after first surgery; CT-guided second endoscopy → fragment expulsion at 6 h; re-augmentation at 6 mo |

Bovine anorganic bone (ABB/Bio-Oss) is effectively permanent in humans (particles unchanged at 10–20+ yr per cited literature). The practical consequences: particle surfacing in the sulcus is common and subclinical but persistent; displaced particles in the sinus cause late, sometimes antibiotic-refractory complications; the definitive cure is surgical removal with autogenous (not repeat-xenograft) reconstruction; CT/CBCT follow-up is warranted when post-graft sinus symptoms resist antibiotics.

### Axis 2 — Ostium obstruction: preserve drainage (Vaddi 2021)

CBCT morphometry (n=200 sinonasal complexes): sinus-floor-to-ostium height 31.40 ± 5.04 mm (dentate) / 29.50 ± 4.41 mm (edentulous); **safe augmentation height ≈ 27 mm (dentate) / ≈ 23 mm (edentulous)** after subtracting mean mucosal thickening. Ostium in the first-molar region in 47% of sinuses, mean diameter ~2.4 mm; mucosal thickening more prevalent and larger in edentulous (6.40 vs 4.05 mm, p<0.05). Elevating graft above the safe height risks blocking the only ~2.4 mm drainage port — the anatomic precondition that lets foreign-body sinusitis establish. Preoperative CBCT measurement of floor-to-ostium height minus mucosal thickening is the preventive step. (Limitation: 2D linear proxy of a 3D structure; safe-height formula not surgically validated.)

### Axis 3 — BPPV after osteotome/closed (malleting) sinus lift

- **Akcay 2016** (n=1): BPPV immediately after osteotome sinus floor elevation in a patient with undisclosed prior vertigo (2 episodes/9 yr); standard same-night Epley failed, resolved only over a 10-day escalated course. Prior vertigo history proposed as a predisposer to protracted/refractory BPPV.
- **Damsaz 2023** (prospective, n=112): closed (transcrestal) sinus lift BPPV 3.1% (2 patients) vs ridge split 0%, overall 1.8%; **between-group p=0.509 (NS)** — the raw signal is not statistically confirmed (only 2 events). Mechanism: malleting vibration + head hyperextension displacing otoliths.

Recognition/prevention: screen for prior vertigo before osteotome/mallet-based transcrestal lift; minimize malleting force and extreme head extension; manage with canalith repositioning (Epley), escalating for a prolonged course.

## Methodology

Narrative synthesis (no pooling; heterogeneous designs). Base: 3 case reports/series (Nowzari, Rodriguez, Felisati), 1 CBCT morphometric study (Vaddi), 1 case report (Akcay), 1 prospective cohort (Damsaz). Felisati, Akcay, Damsaz are abstract-only. Several comparator rates in the bovine papers (e.g. 4.3% acute sinusitis) are cited literature, not primary data. Case reports/series carry no incidence estimate.

## Results

**Chairside sinus-lift complication read:**

1. **Bovine xenograft is permanent** — counsel that particles do not resorb and may surface in the sulcus; keep peri-implant hygiene tight and consider removal if a particle drives local inflammation.
2. **Post-graft sinus symptoms that resist antibiotics** → image with CT/CBCT for residual/displaced fragments; definitive treatment is surgical removal (autogenous reconstruction), not another xenograft.
3. **Measure ostium-to-floor height on CBCT** and keep graft elevation below the safe height (~27 mm dentate / ~23 mm edentulous) to preserve drainage; expect larger mucosal thickening in edentulous sinuses.
4. **Before osteotome/malleting transcrestal lift, screen for vertigo history**; minimize percussion force and head hyperextension; know BPPV can occur (~1.8–3.1%) and treat with Epley.
5. **Read complication rates as directional** — the evidence is case-report/series-heavy and the one cohort is underpowered.
