## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **범주 구조**: 국소마취(Local Anesthesia) 37편은 6개 주제군으로 정리된다 — ① 하악 마취 효율 ladder, ② 완충·변형 마취제, ③ IANB 실패 해부학, ④ 컴퓨터제어·무침주사 전달 장치, ⑤ 전처치·표면마취, ⑥ 안전·합병증. 각 주제군에 별도 overview가 있으며, 본 페이지는 그 overview들의 상위 지도이자 ⑤⑥을 신규 종합한다.
> - **핵심 임상 명제**: 하악대구치, 특히 증상성 비가역 치수염(Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP)에서 표준 하치조신경전달마취(Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB)는 단독으로는 자주 부족하다. 4% articaine 협측침윤(Buccal Infiltration, BI)은 lidocaine IANB와 성공률 동등(RR 1.06; Saatchi 2025 SR+MA, 5 RCT). IANB 단독 실패 시 보충주사(RR 2.02)·병용주사(RR 1.86) 모두 IANB 단독보다 유의 우월(Rujirawan 2025 Network MA, 28 RCT).
> - **완충의 최대 효과 지점**: 체어사이드 8.4% 중탄산나트륨(NaHCO₃) 완충은 SIP 하악대구치 IANB 성공률을 ~37% → ~84%로 거의 2배(Karunakar 2024). 건강 조직 발치에서는 발현만 빠르게, 성공률은 변하지 않음(Kalra 2024). → 완충이 가장 필요한 시나리오가 "hot tooth 하악대구치"임을 기억.
> - **전처치 효과**(본 페이지 신규 종합): SIP 하악대구치에서 IANB 전 이부프로펜(Ibuprofen) >400 mg 경구 전처치가 성공률을 위약 대비 유의하게 증가(4편 SR 합의; 최고근거 SR: de Geus — 위약 성공률 ~20% → 전처치군 ~79%; Khademi 2023 umbrella review). 전처치+완충 병용이 이론적으로 상승 효과이나 직접 비교 데이터 부재.
> - **표면마취 전 주사**(본 페이지 신규 종합): 소아에서 8% lidocaine·20% benzocaine·5% EMLA 2분 도포 후 IANB — 세 군 간 통증·행동·맥박 유의차 없음(Karkoutly 2024 RCT, n=45, underpowered). EMLA 2분은 불충분(권장 ~5분). 어떤 표면마취 제제가 우월한지에 대한 데이터 불충분.
> - **IANB 실패 해부학**: 실패는 다인성 — 피질골 두께, 바늘 편향, 신경위치 파악 어려움, 부신경지배. 부신경지배 경로 3가지: 이설골근신경(Mylohyoid Nerve), 경부신경총(Cervical Plexus, C2–C3), 후구치관(Retromolar Canal, RMC, 유병률 16.12%). "입술 마비 있는데 치아 아프면" → 반복 IANB 아닌 부신경 의심 + 보충 전략.
> - **CCLAD·STA 장치 편익 재조정**: 가장 엄격한 5군 RCT(Küçükkurt 2026, n=200)에서 5개 시스템 간 주사 통증강도 유의차 없음(p=0.380). 장치의 실질 이득은 "덜 아프다"가 아니라 "덜 무섭다(공포 감소)·보충마취 필요율 감소·소아 협조도 개선"에 있다.
> - **신경학적 합병증**(본 페이지 신규 종합): 복시·안검하수·일과성 실명(중경막동맥→안동맥 우발 혈관내 확산), 4% articaine IANB의 감각이상(Paresthesia) 위험 7.3배(vs 2% lidocaine, Garisto 등), 안면신경마비(Bell's Palsy)가 IANB 후 가장 흔한 신경학적 합병증(Ghafoor 2023 서술적 리뷰). → 실제 발생률 추정은 불가; 기전·인식 수준으로 활용.
> - **임신·특수 환자**: 표준 2% lidocaine + 1:200,000 에피네프린(Epinephrine). 확진된 amide 알레르기는 드물며 diphenhydramine 기반 대체 가능. 진성 amide 알레르기 감별에서 보조제(방부제) 과민이 실제 원인인 경우 많음(Bina 2018).
> - **한국 현황**: 2023년 기준 한국에는 치과 국소마취 임상진료지침이 공식적으로 부재 — 동일 JKDA 호에서 진정 가이드라인은 있으나 마취 가이드라인은 없음이 지적됨(Hwang 2023 position paper).
> - **근거 한계**: 대부분 소규모 단일기관 RCT(n=28–200), hot tooth에 특화된 결과가 일반 처치에 적용 안 됨, 표면마취 3군 비교는 underpowered, 신경 합병증 발생률은 미정량.

## Three-line Summary

The 37-paper local anesthesia category clusters into six thematic groups — mandibular anesthesia efficacy, buffered/modified agents, anatomic IANB failure, computerized/needle-free delivery, pre-injection topical/premedication, and safety/complications — each with its own topic overview; the dominant clinical principle across all clusters is that standard IANB alone frequently fails in symptomatic irreversible pulpitis, and the evidence-based response is either 4% articaine buccal infiltration as an equivalent primary alternative (RR 1.06; Saatchi 2025 SR+MA) or systematic escalation (preoperative ibuprofen >400 mg raises success from ~20% to ~79%; Khademi 2023 umbrella), supplementary/combined injection after failure (RR 2.02/1.86 vs IANB alone; Rujirawan 2025 Network MA), or intraosseous delivery (100% success; Park 2024).

Computerized/pressure-controlled delivery systems (CCLAD, The Wand, STA) do not reduce primary injection pain vs conventional syringe in the most rigorous trial (Küçükkurt 2026, n=200, p=0.380); their defensible benefits are dental anxiety reduction, decreased need for supplementary injections, and pediatric behavior management; topical pre-injection agents (benzocaine, lidocaine, EMLA) show no superiority over one another in the 2-minute contact window studied (Karkoutly 2024 RCT), while neurological complications (diplopia, Bell's palsy, paresthesia) are mechanism-level awareness points without reliable incidence estimates (Ghafoor 2023 narrative).

The unifying pharmacological foundation is acid–base chemistry: charged BH⁺ ions cannot cross nerve membranes; buffering elevates free-base fraction and is the single most effective chemical intervention for inflamed tissue; 4% articaine IANB carries a 7.3× paresthesia risk vs 2% lidocaine and is therefore preferred for infiltration, not nerve block delivery.

## 세줄요약

국소마취 37편은 ① 하악 마취 효율 ladder, ② 완충·변형 마취제, ③ IANB 실패 해부학, ④ CCLAD·무침주사, ⑤ 전처치·표면마취, ⑥ 안전·합병증 6군으로 정리; 표준 IANB는 증상성 비가역 치수염(SIP)에서 자주 실패 → 4% articaine 협측침윤(RR 1.06, Saatchi 2025 SR+MA), 이부프로펜 >400 mg 전처치(위약 ~20% → ~79%; Khademi 2023 umbrella), 보충주사(RR 2.02, Rujirawan 2025 Network MA) 순으로 단계적 대응.

CCLAD·STA는 주사 통증강도 무차이(Küçükkurt 2026, n=200, p=0.380) — 실질 이득은 공포 감소·보충마취 회피·소아 협조도; 표면마취 제제 간 2분 도포 시 차이 없음(Karkoutly 2024, underpowered); 신경 합병증(복시·Bell's palsy·감각이상)은 기전 수준 인식 — 발생률 미정량(Ghafoor 2023 서술적 리뷰).

약리 기반 = 산-염기 화학: 완충이 SIP 하악 IANB 성공률을 2배로 올리는 유일한 화학 레버; 4% articaine IANB는 감각이상 7.3배 위험 → 침윤용으로 제한, 전달마취에는 2% lidocaine 권장.

## Pharmacology Foundations

[[local-anesthesia/becker-2012-local-anesthetics-pharmacological-considerations]] establishes the mechanism framework underlying every other cluster:

- Amide local anesthetics (lidocaine, articaine, mepivacaine, bupivacaine) work as free-base species (B) that diffuse across lipid nerve membranes; once inside, the charged cation (BH⁺) blocks the sodium channel.
- Cartridge pH ~3.5–4.5 heavily favors the BH⁺ form → slow onset; buffering raises free-base proportion before injection.
- In inflamed tissue (SIP), local acidosis further depletes free-base → explains why normal solutions fail and why buffering helps disproportionately in hot teeth.
- Articaine is unique among amide agents in containing a thiophene ring (higher lipid solubility, faster tissue diffusion) — rationale for its infiltration advantage.
- 4% articaine IANB carries a 7.3× paresthesia risk vs 2% lidocaine; use articaine for buccal infiltration, not for block delivery.
- Epinephrine (1:100,000–1:200,000) reduces systemic absorption, prolongs duration, and improves depth — standard for most procedures; 1:200,000 preferred in pregnancy (Zhou 2023).

## Pre-Injection Topical Anesthesia (New Synthesis)

Pre-injection topical agents aim to blunt needle-insertion pain. The wiki holds three papers examining this in pediatric contexts.

[[local-anesthesia/karkoutly-2024-topical-anesthetics-lidocaine-benzocaine-emla-ianb]] (triple-blind RCT, n=45 schoolchildren aged 6–10, 15/arm, 2-min application):
- Compared 8% lidocaine gel, 20% benzocaine gel (standard), and 5% EMLA cream before IANB
- **No significant differences** on FLACC behavioral scale (p=0.806), Wong-Baker FACES (p=0.593), or post-IANB pulse (p=0.351)
- Major limitation: 15/arm is underpowered; 2-min EMLA application is suboptimal (EMLA needs ~5 min)

[[local-anesthesia/subramanian-2023-comparative-two-topical-anesthetic-agents-pediatric]] provides a contrasting result in a different comparison (lidocaine vs benzocaine in a different pediatric age group) — the two papers are tagged `contradicts` in the wiki; the most likely explanation is methodological heterogeneity rather than a true effect.

[[local-anesthesia/wambier-2017-intrapocket-topical-versus-injected-anesthetic-srp]] shows that for scaling and root planing (SRP), intrapocket topical anesthetic is as effective as injectable for pain control in single-session short-duration procedures — expanding the scope of topical LA beyond pre-injection use to standalone anesthesia in periodontal contexts.

**Current evidence bottom line**: No topical pre-injection agent is clearly superior to 20% benzocaine in the standard 2-minute application window. Longer EMLA contact times may yield benefits not captured by current data.

## Premedication Before IANB (New Synthesis)

[[local-anesthesia/khademi-2023-premedication-inferior-alveolar-nerve-block-pulpitis-umbrella]] (umbrella review: 4 SR, 3 with MA; primary RCTs n=7–35 per SR):

All four included SRs converged: preoperative **ibuprofen >400 mg** significantly increases IANB success in SIP molar patients.

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| de Geus SR (best evidence) | IANB success: ~20% placebo → ~79% ibuprofen pre-treatment |
| Khademi umbrella overall | Ibuprofen/NSAIDs > acetaminophen; opioid/ketamine limited evidence |
| AMSTAR 2 quality | 2 of 4 SRs "low quality" |

**Mechanism**: NSAIDs suppress the prostaglandin-driven inflammatory cascade that lowers local tissue pH — directly counteracting the acid–base mechanism that impairs IANB in inflamed pulps. The pre-treatment window matters: ibuprofen should be taken 30–60 minutes before injection.

**Combination strategy**: Premedication (ibuprofen) + buffering (NaHCO₃) + supplementary injection (if IANB still fails) represents the maximum-escalation protocol for SIP hot tooth, though the three modalities have not been tested in combination in a single trial.

## Neurological Complications of Local Anesthesia (New Synthesis)

[[local-anesthesia/ghafoor-2023-neurological-complications-local-anesthesia-dentistry]] (narrative review, Cureus; no systematic search) organizes complications by nerve:

| Nerve | Complication | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| CN V1 (Ophthalmic) | Diplopia, ptosis, transient/permanent blindness | Intravascular injection → middle meningeal → ophthalmic artery retrograde spread |
| CN V3 (Mandibular) | Paresthesia (prolonged numbness/dysesthesia) | 4% articaine IANB: 7.3× risk vs 2% lidocaine (Garisto et al.); direct nerve trauma |
| CN VII (Facial) | Bell's palsy | Immediate: parotid gland variant anatomy; Delayed: sympathetic vasospasm or viral reactivation |

**Evidence level caveat**: Ghafoor 2023 is a non-systematic Cureus narrative review with no formal search protocol or PRISMA methodology. Prevalence estimates (particularly the 7.3× paresthesia figure) are from cited primary sources, not meta-analyzed here. Use as mechanism-awareness, not for informed-consent quantification.

**Clinical take-home**:
1. Aspiration before every injection — intravascular injection is the gateway to ocular complications.
2. 4% articaine IANB: evidence of elevated paresthesia risk → prefer articaine buccal infiltration over block.
3. Diplopia/visual disturbance after IANB → emergency evaluation immediately.
4. Bell's palsy: usually resolves spontaneously (immediate type within hours); delayed type may take weeks — neurological follow-up required.

## Clinical Decision Framework — The Four Axes

### Axis 1: Which injection for which tooth?

| Tooth / indication | Recommended primary approach |
|---|---|
| Maxillary teeth | Infiltration (± palatal); IANB not required |
| Mandibular incisors | Bilateral IANB or bilateral lingual infiltration |
| Mandibular molars (healthy pulp) | IANB or articaine buccal infiltration |
| Mandibular molars (SIP / hot tooth) | Premedication → buffered IANB or articaine BI → supplementary if failure |

### Axis 2: When IANB "worked" but tooth still hurts

"Lip numb, tooth hurts" = classic accessory innervation pattern, not technique failure.

Check:
- Mylohyoid nerve accessory sensory contribution → Gow-Gates or supplementary mylohyoid injection
- Cervical plexus (C2–C3) contribution to posterior mandible → supplementary inferior cervical injection
- Retromolar canal (BMC/RMC, prevalence 16.12%; Filo 2015) → supplementary retromolar injection

Alternatives: Gow-Gates block (higher trunk, covers more branches) or Akinosi-Vazirani closed-mouth block (useful when limited opening) — [[local-anesthesia/haas-2011-alternative-mandibular-nerve-block-techniques]].

### Axis 3: Special populations

| Population | Key principle |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy | 2% lidocaine + 1:200,000 epinephrine; prefer 14–20 weeks for elective; avoid nitrous oxide in 1st trimester (Zhou 2023) |
| Pediatric | Articaine buccal infiltration as IANB alternative (Yu 2021 SR+MA); CCLAD for cooperation; no dose advantage of 8% vs 20% topical benzocaine |
| Suspected amide allergy | True allergy rare; confirm vs preservative sensitivity; diphenhydramine alternative (Bina 2018) |
| Needle phobia | Needle-free mucoadhesive patch (Adami 2021, 90% procedure completion without injection) |

### Axis 4: Safety floor

- Aspirate before every injection
- Limit epinephrine in patients with severe uncontrolled cardiovascular disease
- 4% articaine → infiltration only (not IANB), avoid repeated block delivery
- Maximum safe doses: lidocaine 4.4 mg/kg (7 mg/kg with epinephrine); articaine 7 mg/kg

## Guideline Gap and Standardization Status

[[local-anesthesia/hwang-2023-proposal-clinical-guidelines-local-anesthesia-dentistry]] documents that as of 2023, Korea lacks a formal clinical practice guideline for dental local anesthesia — despite LA being the most frequently performed clinical dental procedure. Sedation has a contemporaneous guideline (Karm 2023 in the same JKDA issue); local anesthesia does not.

This gap means Korean practitioners must integrate:
- Individual drug pharmacology (Becker 2012 framework)
- International evidence from the RCT clusters above
- Drug-specific Korean regulatory information (cartridge concentration availability differs from Western markets)
