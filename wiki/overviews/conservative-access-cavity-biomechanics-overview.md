## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: 보존적 접근와동 (Conservative Endodontic Cavity, CEC)의 파절저항 이득은 **조건부**이고, 그 대가(미형성 근관면·충전 공극·근관 미발견)는 **무조건부**다. 이 비대칭이 이 페이지의 결론이다. 논문 9편(SR+NMA 1·SR+MA 2·SR 3·in vitro 3) 종합. [확인]
> - 겉보기 모순: 풀링 메타분석은 이득을 보고하고(Motiwala 2022 NMA — CEC SUCRA 51.4% vs 전통 접근와동 TEC 15.3%, 약 562 N 차이; Mrinalini 2024 MA — 표준화평균차 (Standardized Mean Difference, SMD) 2.61, p<0.001), 가장 잘 통제된 개별 체외연구 3편은 **파절저항 차이 없음**을 보고한다(Barbosa 2020·Lima 2021·Rover 2020, 모두 P>0.05). [확인]
> - 모순 해소 ①: 변연능선 (Marginal Ridge)이 실제 조절변수다. 능선이 모두 온전할 때만 CEC·트러스가 TEC보다 유의하게 강하고, **능선이 1개라도 소실되면 이득이 소멸한다**(Ballester 2021, 33편 MA). 능선 상태를 모형에 넣지 않고 풀링하면 조절된 효과의 평균이 나온다 — Mrinalini의 I²=92%가 바로 그 서명이다. [확인(1차 소견)/미검증(이질성 귀인)]
> - 모순 해소 ②: 무효과를 보고한 3편은 모두 **수복 후** 하중을 걸었다(Barbosa는 임시수복 후, Lima는 열기계적 순환 후). 수복만으로 건전치 강도의 약 72%가 회복된다(Saeed 2021) — 수복이 접근구 크기 차이를 덮어버린다. [확인(수치)/미검증(인과 귀인)]
> - 모순 해소 ③: 치아 위치도 갈린다 — 하악 대구치에서는 CEC 이득이 보이고 상악에서는 안 보인 연구가 있다(Saeed 2021, 4/10편만 양성). [확인]
> - 대가는 재현적이다: CEC·트러스·초보존 (UltraAC) 모두 **미형성 근관 표면적↑·치수강 잔여 충전재↑**(Barbosa 2020, P<0.05), **충전 공극↑**(Rover 2020 하악 전치, Lima 2021 하악 대구치) — 세 연구가 다른 치아·다른 기구로 같은 방향을 가리킨다. [확인]
> - 근관 미발견 위험: CEC를 **치과용 수술현미경 (Dental Operating Microscope, DOM) + 초음파 트로핑 (Ultrasonic Troughing) 없이** 쓰면 근관 미발견률이 유의하게 증가한다(Ballester 2021, P<0.05). 장비를 갖추면 TEC와 동등 — 즉 확대는 옵션이 아니라 전제조건. [확인]
> - 기구가 일부 구제한다: 초보존 와동에서 왕복 (Reciproc)은 근관편이 (Canal Transportation)를 유의하게 늘렸지만 XP-endo Shaper(연속회전·가변테이퍼)는 늘리지 않았다(Lima 2021) — 접근을 좁힐수록 적응형 기구의 값어치가 올라간다. [확인]
> - 더 큰 레버는 수복이다: 섬유보강 복합레진 (Fiber-Reinforced Composite, FRC) 코어가 재래식 하이브리드/나노하이브리드보다 16편 중 15편에서 우월했고(Selvaraj 2023), 폴리에틸렌·단섬유 FRC가 유리섬유 FRC보다 낫고 유지홈·교합면 1/3 배치가 효과를 더한다(Shah 2021). 접근구를 몇 mm 줄일지보다 **무엇으로 어떻게 수복할지**가 파절저항에 더 크게 작동할 개연성이 높다. [확인(개별 소견)/미검증(효과크기 직접 비교)]
> - FRC의 부수 이득: 파절 양상이 대부분 **호의적(수복 가능)** 이어서 재앙적 치근 파절 위험을 낮춘다(Shah 2021) — 파절저항 수치(N)만 보면 놓치는 축. 단 FRC도 건전치 수준에는 못 미친다. [확인]
> - 임상 규칙: ① 변연능선 2개 온전 + DOM·초음파 보유 + 단순 해부 → CEC 고려 가능, ② 능선이 이미 하나라도 깨졌으면 **접근구를 아끼는 생역학적 명분이 사라진다** — TEC로 시야를 확보하는 편이 낫다, ③ CEC를 택했으면 충전 단계에 추가 검증(방사선 2각도·공극 확인)을 붙이고 적응형 기구를 쓴다, ④ 파절저항이 목표라면 접근구보다 FRC 코어·유지홈·교합면 피개 설계에 자원을 쓴다.
> - 근거 한계 (중요): 9편 중 **임상 연구는 0편**이다. 전부 발치치 체외실험이거나 그 풀링이다 — 치주인대 없음, 정적 하중, 실제 저작 피로 미반영. 따라서 절대 수치(562 N, SMD 2.61)는 임상 예후로 옮길 수 없고, 방향성과 조절변수만 취한다. [확인]

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 12 papers resolving an apparent contradiction in conservative access cavity (CEC) biomechanics: pooled analyses report a significant fracture-resistance advantage for CEC over traditional endodontic cavities (TEC) — SUCRA 51.4% vs 15.3%, ~562 N difference (Motiwala 2022, 10 molar studies, n=456); SMD 2.61, 95% CI 1.47–3.74, p<0.001 (Mrinalini 2024, 14 studies) — while the three best-controlled individual in vitro studies find no fracture-resistance difference whatsoever (Barbosa 2020, Lima 2021, Rover 2020, all P>0.05).

The contradiction resolves on three moderators that the pooled models did not stratify: (1) marginal ridge status — CEC and truss designs beat TEC only when all marginal ridges are intact, and the advantage vanishes once one or more ridges are lost (Ballester 2021, 33 studies), which is precisely the signature that Mrinalini's I²=92% heterogeneity would produce; (2) restoration state — all three null studies load-tested after restoration, and restoration alone recovers ~72% of intact fracture strength (Saeed 2021), swamping the access-outline difference; (3) tooth position — the CEC advantage appeared in mandibular but not maxillary molars in at least one study (Saeed 2021, only 4/10 studies positive).

The costs, unlike the benefit, are unconditional and reproduce across tooth types and instrument systems: conservative, truss, and ultraconservative designs all leave significantly more unprepared canal surface and more residual filling material in the pulp chamber (Barbosa 2020, P<0.05) and produce more filling voids (Rover 2020 in mandibular incisors; Lima 2021 in mandibular molars); CEC without a dental operating microscope plus ultrasonic troughing significantly raises the missed-canal rate (Ballester 2021, P<0.05), making magnification a prerequisite rather than an option; and the larger lever on fracture resistance is plausibly the restoration itself — fiber-reinforced composite (FRC) cores outperformed conventional hybrid/nanohybrid composites in 15 of 16 studies (Selvaraj 2023) with predominantly favorable, repairable failure patterns (Shah 2021).

## 세줄요약

논문 9편(네트워크 메타분석 1·쌍대 메타분석 2·체계적 고찰 3·통제 체외연구 3) 종합 — 보존적 접근와동 (Conservative Endodontic Cavity, CEC) 생역학의 겉보기 모순을 해소: 풀링 분석은 CEC가 전통 접근와동 (Traditional Endodontic Cavity, TEC)보다 파절저항이 유의하게 높다고 보고하는 반면(Motiwala 2022 — 누적순위곡선하면적 (Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking, SUCRA) 51.4% vs 15.3%, 약 562 N 차이, 대구치 10편 n=456; Mrinalini 2024 — 표준화평균차 (Standardized Mean Difference, SMD) 2.61, 95% CI 1.47–3.74, p<0.001, 14편), 가장 잘 통제된 개별 체외연구 3편은 파절저항 차이를 전혀 찾지 못했다(Barbosa 2020·Lima 2021·Rover 2020, 모두 P>0.05).

모순은 풀링 모형이 층화하지 않은 조절변수 3개에서 풀린다: ① 변연능선 (Marginal Ridge) 상태 — CEC·트러스 (Truss)는 **능선이 모두 온전할 때만** TEC보다 유의하게 강하고 1개라도 소실되면 이득이 소멸(Ballester 2021, 33편), Mrinalini의 이질성 (Heterogeneity) I²=92%가 바로 이 미모형화 조절변수의 서명; ② 수복 상태 — 무효과 3편은 모두 수복 후 하중을 걸었고 수복만으로 건전치 강도의 약 72%가 회복되어(Saeed 2021) 접근구 차이를 덮음; ③ 치아 위치 — 하악 대구치에서는 이득이 보이고 상악에서는 안 보임(Saeed 2021, 10편 중 4편만 양성).

이득과 달리 **대가는 무조건적이고 치아 종류·기구를 바꿔도 재현된다**: 보존적·트러스·초보존 (Ultraconservative Access Cavity, UltraAC) 설계 모두 미형성 근관 표면적과 치수강 잔여 충전재가 유의하게 증가하고(Barbosa 2020, P<0.05) 충전 공극 (Void)이 늘어남(Rover 2020 하악 전치, Lima 2021 하악 대구치); 치과용 수술현미경 (Dental Operating Microscope, DOM)과 초음파 트로핑 (Ultrasonic Troughing) 없이 CEC를 쓰면 근관 미발견률이 유의하게 증가(Ballester 2021, P<0.05) — 확대는 옵션이 아니라 전제조건; 그리고 파절저항의 더 큰 레버는 수복 자체일 개연성이 높다 — 섬유보강 복합레진 (Fiber-Reinforced Composite, FRC) 코어가 재래식 하이브리드/나노하이브리드 대비 16편 중 15편에서 우월(Selvaraj 2023)했고 파절 양상도 대부분 호의적·수복 가능(Shah 2021).

## Summary

Minimally invasive endodontics rests on a biomechanical promise: preserve pericervical dentin and the marginal ridges, and the endodontically treated tooth will fracture less. This page tests that promise against nine papers that measure it directly, and finds the promise is **real but heavily conditional** — while the price paid for it is **not conditional at all**.

The headline numbers look decisive. Motiwala 2022's network meta-analysis ranked four designs by fracture resistance and placed conservative cavities second (SUCRA 51.4%) against traditional cavities last (15.3%), a gap of roughly 562 N in preserved fracture load; notably, TEC's reduction versus intact teeth was statistically significant (MD −927.52 N, 95% CI −1304.80 to −550.24) while CEC's was not (MD −365.59 N, 95% CI −759.02 to 27.83) — CEC teeth were, statistically speaking, not distinguishable from intact ones. Mrinalini 2024 pooled 14 studies of mandibular molars and found conservative access superior to conventional at SMD 2.61 (95% CI 1.47–3.74, p<0.001), with truss designs also superior (SMD −1.26, p<0.001).

Yet the three studies in this set that controlled tooth anatomy most tightly — matching specimens by micro-CT before allocation and imaging each tooth before and after preparation and filling — all report the same null. Barbosa 2020 compared TEC, CEC, and truss in 30 matched mandibular molars and found fracture resistance equivalent (P>0.05). Lima 2021 compared ultraconservative versus traditional access across two instrument systems in 40 mandibular molars, applied thermomechanical cycling, and found load capacity equivalent across all four groups. Rover 2020 compared minimally invasive versus traditional access in 40 mandibular incisors and found fracture resistance equivalent. Three studies, two tooth types, four instrument systems, one answer: no difference.

Key propositions:

1. **The fracture-resistance advantage of conservative access is conditional on marginal ridge integrity, and disappears without it.** [확인] Ballester 2021's meta-analysis of 33 studies stratified explicitly on ridge status: with all marginal ridges intact, both ConsAC and TrussAC significantly outperformed TradAC (P<0.05); with one or more ridges lost, neither did (P>0.05). This is the single most clinically actionable finding in the set, because it converts a design preference into a case-selection rule.
2. **The pooled meta-analyses most likely averaged over this moderator rather than contradicting it.** [미검증] Mrinalini 2024 reported I²=92% for the conservative-versus-conventional comparison — extreme heterogeneity of exactly the kind an unmodeled binary moderator produces. Neither Motiwala 2022 nor Mrinalini 2024 stratified on ridge status. This is an inference about why the numbers diverge, not a finding either paper reports.
3. **Restoration state plausibly explains the null results.** [확인(수치)/미검증(귀인)] Saeed 2021 flagged that restoration alone restores approximately 72% of intact fracture strength, and catalogued testing-without-restoration as a primary confounder inflating apparent access-design effects. Barbosa 2020 load-tested after temporary restoration; Lima 2021 after thermomechanical cycling. If restoration recovers most of the lost strength, the residual access-outline effect falls below detection in a group of ten.
4. **Tooth position modulates the effect.** [확인] Saeed 2021 found only 4 of 10 included studies positive for conservative access, with a mandibular-favoring, maxillary-null pattern in at least one. The pooled molar estimates blend both.
5. **The shaping and obturation penalties of conservative access are unconditional and reproducible.** [확인] Barbosa 2020: conservative and truss cavities left significantly larger unprepared canal surface areas and significantly more residual filling material in the pulp chamber (P<0.05), with no compensating gain in microbial reduction, transportation, centering, or voids. Lima 2021: ultraconservative access increased unprepared canal wall percentage and pulp-chamber voids regardless of instrument. Rover 2020: minimally invasive access produced significantly more filling voids (P<0.05) and this was the *only* significant difference found. Different teeth, different files, same direction.
6. **Magnification is a prerequisite for conservative access, not an accessory.** [확인] Ballester 2021: ConsAC without DOM plus ultrasonic troughing significantly increased the missed-canal rate (P<0.05); with both, detection was comparable to TradAC. A missed canal is a treatment failure mode of a different order than a few hundred newtons of laboratory fracture load.
7. **Adaptive instrumentation partially rescues restricted access.** [확인] Lima 2021 found canal transportation significantly higher with ultraconservative access when using Reciproc (reciprocating), but not when using XP-endo Shaper (continuous rotation, variable taper) — the instrument choice interacts with the access choice, and the narrower the access, the more the adaptive file earns its cost.
8. **The restoration is plausibly a larger lever on fracture resistance than the access outline.** [확인(개별 소견)/미검증(직접 비교)] Selvaraj 2023: FRC outperformed conventional hybrid and nanohybrid composites in 15 of 16 studies reporting p<0.05. Shah 2021: polyethylene and short-fiber FRC outperformed glass FRC, and retention slots plus occlusal-third fiber placement further amplified resistance. No study in this set compared "conservative access + conventional core" against "traditional access + FRC core," so the relative magnitude is an inference, not a measurement.
9. **FRC changes failure mode, not just failure load.** [확인] Shah 2021 reports failures under FRC are predominantly favorable — at the enamel-dentin level and therefore repairable — reducing catastrophic, extraction-triggering root fractures. Fracture resistance measured only in newtons misses this axis. FRC still does not reach intact-tooth fracture resistance.

The asymmetry is the point. The benefit requires intact marginal ridges, a microscope, ultrasonic troughing, favorable tooth position, and possibly an unrestored tooth to even be measurable. The cost — unprepared canal wall, filling voids, residual chamber material, elevated missed-canal risk — appears in every study that looked for it, under every condition tested.

## Clinical Decision Rules

These follow from the propositions above; each carries the evidence caveat that no clinical study exists in this set.

1. **Check the marginal ridges first, not the access outline.** Two intact ridges is the gate. With a ridge already lost to caries or a prior restoration, the biomechanical case for a contracted access has no supporting evidence — take the visibility instead (Ballester 2021).
2. **Do not attempt conservative access without a microscope and ultrasonic troughing.** The missed-canal penalty without them is statistically significant and clinically far more consequential than the fracture-load difference being chased (Ballester 2021).
3. **If conservative access is chosen, add obturation verification.** Every in vitro study that measured voids found more of them under contracted access (Rover 2020, Lima 2021) or more residual chamber material (Barbosa 2020, Lima 2021). Two-angle radiographic verification and deliberate chamber cleanout are the countermeasures.
4. **Match the file to the access.** Restricted access plus reciprocating motion produced measurable canal transportation; restricted access plus a continuous-rotation variable-taper file did not (Lima 2021).
5. **Spend the fracture-resistance budget on the restoration.** FRC core material, retention slots, occlusal-third fiber placement, and cuspal coverage have direct supporting evidence (Shah 2021, Selvaraj 2023); shaving millimetres off the access outline has conditional evidence that evaporates under the conditions most restored molars are actually in.
6. **Expect conservative access to be a single-rooted, simple-anatomy, experienced-operator choice** — consistent with the strategy algorithm in [[overviews/endodontic-access-cavity-decision-tree]].

## Evidence Limits

- **No clinical studies.** All nine papers are in vitro or syntheses of in vitro work. There is no randomized trial, no cohort, and no survival data on access cavity design. Absolute values (562 N, SMD 2.61) describe extracted teeth under laboratory loading and must not be carried into prognosis.
- **No periodontal ligament, mostly static loading.** Extracted-tooth models omit the PDL's load damping. Only Lima 2021 applied thermomechanical cycling; Selvaraj 2023 explicitly names static in vitro loading as its main translation limit.
- **Extreme heterogeneity where it matters most.** Mrinalini 2024's I²=92% on the primary comparison means the pooled SMD describes no particular study well. Motiwala 2022's CINeMA confidence was low to moderate throughout, with publication bias noted.
- **Unstandardized cavity definitions.** "Conservative," "contracted," "ultraconservative," "truss," and "ninja" are not dimensionally standardized across studies — Saeed 2021 names this as a leading source of the mixed picture, and it means the exposure variable itself differs between pooled studies.
- **The restoration-lever comparison is inferred, not measured.** No study in this set factorially crossed access design against core material, so proposition 8 remains [미검증] as a magnitude claim even though each component finding is [확인].
- **Ridge-status stratification exists in only one paper.** The central resolving moderator rests on Ballester 2021 alone; independent replication of the ridge-conditional effect would materially strengthen this page's thesis.
