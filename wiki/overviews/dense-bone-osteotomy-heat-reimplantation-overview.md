> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 질문**: 골경화성(dense/sclerotic, Misch **D1**) 하악구치부에서 임플란트가 여러 번 실패한 자리에 재식립할 때 무엇을 주의해야 하는가.
> - **기전 재구성**: D1 치밀골 실패는 통념(1차 안정성 부족)과 달리 실제로는 **드릴링 발열에 의한 열괴사(thermal osteonecrosis)**가 핵심 위험기전 — D1은 오히려 전체 생존율이 가장 높은 골질(97.6%, Khaohoen)이지만 발열 관리 실패 시에만 위험이 커지는 구조.
> - **임계 온도**: 47°C/1분(치과) ~ 50°C(정형외과 교차검증, Timon) — **외부 관주(irrigation)가 단일 가장 중요한 완화변수**.
> - **발열 결정인자는 다인자적**: drill 형상·마모·간헐(peck) drilling·관주 온도·골밀도·속도·하중이 상호작용(Chauhan). D1 치밀골에서 모든 요인이 증폭됨.
> - **재질보다 마모관리**: drill **재질**(세라믹 vs 금속) 효과는 결론 불가(Chakraborty)이나 **drill 마모**는 발열의 일관된 예측인자 — 교체주기 준수가 재질 선택보다 우선. 지르코니아 drill이 스테인리스보다 발열 낮다는 특정 재질쌍 신호도 있음(Bento, in vitro 한정).
> - **기법 간 비교**: 전통 회전 drilling이 laser·피에조·osteotome·osseodensification(OD)보다 미세균열·발열·%BIC 모두 열등하나, **회전수·냉각·하중이 적절하면 drilling도 대안과 동등**(Kosior) — 기법 교체보다 프로토콜 관리가 우선.
> - **피에조 trade-off**: 술후 통증은 줄이나 골내 온도는 회전 drilling보다 **더 높음**(Jain) — 치밀골·구치부 같은 고열부하 상황엔 오히려 전통 회전(신품 drill+철저 냉각+간헐)이 안전할 수 있음.
> - **가이드수술 주의**: 슬리브가 외부 관주를 막아 발열↑ — full-guided라면 냉각(~10°C) 식염수 + 800–1200 rpm + peck drilling, **특히 D1 치밀골·깊은 구치부에서 위험 최대**(Saxena).
> - **반복 실패 부위 재식립**: 생존율이 시도 횟수에 따라 하락 — 1회 실패 후 재식립 88.7%, 2회 실패 후 85%; **2차 시도 71–100%, 3차 시도 50–100%**(Solderer). 동시 다발 실패("cluster effect")는 전신/유전 요인을 시사 — 재수술 전 통제 필요.
> - **재식립 조건**: 즉시 vs 지연 재식립은 골량 충분·감염 없음 조건에서 결과 동등; 협측판 결손 등 확장 결손은 GBR 선행. Large-diameter·rough-surface 임플란트 선호.
> - **임상 결론**: (1) 발열 관리를 프로토콜의 중심에 둔다(냉각·간헐드릴링·drill 교체), (2) 반복 실패 이력 자체가 예후 인자이므로 재수술 전 전신·국소 원인을 재평가한다, (3) 위키에 "골경화성+반복실패"를 직접 다룬 SR/RCT는 없음 — 두 인접 근거군의 조합.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 9 papers spanning two connected problems in dense/sclerotic (Misch D1) bone implant surgery: osteotomy heat generation/thermal osteonecrosis mechanics (7 papers: Chauhan 2018 SR 34 studies, Timon 2019 orthopedic cross-specialty review, Kosior 2025 PRISMA SR 29 studies comparing 5 bed-preparation methods, Chakraborty 2024 SR on drill material, Saxena 2024 SR on guided drilling, Jain 2024 SR on piezo-vs-rotary pain/heat trade-off, Bento 2023 SR+MA zirconia-vs-steel drills) and repeated-failure-site reimplantation protocol (Solderer 2019 narrative Q&A review, 34 studies), anchored by bone-density survival context (Khaohoen 2023 narrative review, 119 studies).

D1 dense bone generates more osteotomy heat than lower-density bone despite having the highest overall implant survival (97.6%, Khaohoen) — the failure mechanism in dense bone is thermal injury (exceeding the 47°C/1-min necrosis threshold), not lack of primary stability, and is best mitigated by external irrigation, intermittent (peck) drilling, and drill-replacement protocols rather than switching preparation technique alone (Kosior: drilling penalty is largely protocol-mediated); reimplantation at a previously-failed site carries progressively lower survival with each repeat attempt (88.7% after 1 failure, 85% after 2, 2nd-attempt range 71–100%, 3rd-attempt range 50–100%, Solderer), and simultaneous multi-implant failure ("cluster effect") should prompt a systemic/genetic workup before re-surgery.

No paper in this wiki directly studies "sclerotic posterior mandible with repeated implant failure" as a single cohort — this overview combines the D1-bone thermal-mechanism evidence with the general reimplantation-protocol evidence, so the synthesis carries lower certainty than either thread alone (Rule #4 boundary, stated explicitly).

## 세줄요약

골경화성(치밀골, Misch D1) 임플란트 수술의 두 연결 문제 9편 통합: 골절제 발열/열괴사 기전(7편 — Chauhan 2018 SR 34편, Timon 2019 정형외과 교차검증, Kosior 2025 PRISMA SR 29편 5기법 비교, Chakraborty 2024 drill재질 SR, Saxena 2024 가이드drilling SR, Jain 2024 피에조 vs 회전 통증/발열 trade-off SR, Bento 2023 지르코니아 vs 스틸 drill SR+MA)와 반복실패부위 재식립 프로토콜(Solderer 2019 narrative Q&A, 34편), 골밀도-생존율 배경(Khaohoen 2023, 119편)을 축으로 종합.

D1 치밀골은 전체 생존율이 가장 높음에도(97.6%, Khaohoen) 골절제 발열이 저밀도골보다 큼 — 치밀골 실패 기전은 "1차 안정성 부족"이 아니라 **47°C/1분 열괴사 임계값을 넘는 발열 손상**이며, 냉각 관주·간헐(peck) drilling·drill 교체 프로토콜이 핵심 완화책(Kosior: drilling의 단점은 기법 자체보다 프로토콜 의존적); 실패부위 재식립은 시도 횟수에 따라 생존율이 하락(1회 실패 후 88.7%, 2회 실패 후 85%, 2차 시도 71–100%, 3차 시도 50–100%, Solderer), 동시 다발 실패("cluster effect")는 재수술 전 전신/유전 요인 평가를 시사.

위키에 "골경화성 하악구치부 + 반복실패"를 단일 코호트로 직접 다룬 논문은 없음 — 본 overview는 D1 골 발열기전 근거군과 일반 재식립 프로토콜 근거군을 조합한 것으로, 각 근거 단독보다 확실성이 낮음(Rule #4 경계 명시).

## Thesis

**Clinical question:** In dense/sclerotic (D1) posterior mandibular bone where an implant has failed at the same site multiple times, how should re-placement be approached to avoid another failure?

The evidence splits into two threads that this wiki has not previously connected. The **first** is the biomechanics of osteotomy heat generation, established across seven papers spanning general drilling factors ([[implants/chauhan-2018-biomechanical-factors-heat-generation-osteotomy]]), cross-specialty thermal-necrosis thresholds ([[implants/timon-2019-thermal-osteonecrosis-bone-drilling-orthopedic]]), a five-technique histological comparison ([[implants/kosior-2025-histological-histomorphometric-implant-bed-preparation]]), drill-material questions ([[implants/chakraborty-2024-implant-drill-materials-heat-osteotomy]], [[implants/bento-2023-steel-versus-zirconia-drills-heat]]), guided-surgery-specific heat penalties ([[implants/saxena-2024-guided-implant-drilling-bone-temperature]]), and a piezo-vs-rotary pain/heat trade-off ([[implants/jain-2024-heat-generation-pain-piezosurgery-drilling]]). Together they establish that **dense (D1) bone amplifies drilling heat** relative to soft bone, that the classical 47°C/1-min necrosis threshold is repeatedly breached under suboptimal conditions (worn drills, high RPM, guided-sleeve irrigation blockage), and that **protocol discipline — external/chilled irrigation, intermittent drilling, drill replacement — matters more than which preparation technique is chosen** (Kosior's central finding: drilling can match laser/piezo/osteotome/osseodensification outcomes when rotation speed, cooling, and load are optimized). This reframes D1 bone risk: it is not a primary-stability problem (D1 in fact has the highest reported survival, 97.6%, [[implants/khaohoen-2023-biomaterials-dental-implants-bone-density-narrative-review]]) but a thermal-management problem.

The **second** thread addresses what "repeated failure at the same site" itself implies. [[implants/solderer-2019-removal-failed-dental-implants-revisited]] is the wiki's only paper quantifying reimplantation-survival decay with repeat attempts (88.7% → 85% → 71–100% at 2nd attempt → 50–100% at 3rd attempt) and flags a "cluster effect" — simultaneous or repeated multi-implant failure — as a signal to investigate systemic or genetic host factors before re-surgery, independent of local bone quality. It also establishes that immediate and delayed reimplantation are equivalent **given adequate bone and no infection**, and that extended buccal-plate defects require GBR staging first.

No single paper in this wiki tests the intersection — dense posterior-mandibular bone with a history of repeated failure at one site — so this overview's clinical recommendation is a **reasoned combination, not a directly-evidenced protocol**: manage osteotomy heat aggressively (irrigation, intermittent drilling, fresh drills, cautious guided-surgery use) because that is the dominant D1-specific failure mechanism, and treat the repeated-failure history as an independent prognostic signal requiring host-factor workup before re-drilling the same site again.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **Reframe D1 dense bone risk as thermal, not mechanical.** D1 bone has the highest reported overall survival (97.6%, Khaohoen) — a prior failure there is more consistent with drilling heat injury than inadequate primary stability. Plan the osteotomy protocol accordingly rather than over-torquing for stability.
2. **Make irrigation and drill freshness non-negotiable.** External (and internal, where available) chilled irrigation is the single most consistent heat mitigator across both dental and cross-specialty literature (Timon, Saxena); drill wear — not material — is the consistent driver of temperature rise (Chakraborty), so replace drills on a fixed-use schedule rather than by material choice alone.
3. **Use intermittent (peck) drilling and conservative speed**, especially if the case is fully guided — sleeves block irrigation and elevate peak temperature specifically in D1 bone and deep posterior osteotomies (Saxena: chilled ~10°C saline + 800–1200 rpm + peck drilling).
4. **Weigh piezo vs rotary by priority, not by default.** Piezosurgery reduces postoperative pain but generates higher intraosseous temperature than rotary drilling (Jain) — in a thermally hostile site (dense bone, deep posterior mandible), a well-cooled, intermittent, fresh-drill rotary protocol may be the safer default over piezo.
5. **Do not assume technique-switching alone solves the heat problem.** Kosior's histological SR found rotary drilling can match laser/piezo/osteotome/osseodensification outcomes when speed, cooling, and load are optimized — protocol discipline outweighs the choice of preparation method.
6. **Treat a repeated-failure site as its own prognostic category.** Reimplantation survival declines with each prior failure (88.7% → 85% → 2nd-attempt 71–100% → 3rd-attempt 50–100%, Solderer) — set expectations accordingly and do not treat a 3rd attempt as routine.
7. **Investigate host factors before re-drilling.** A history of repeated failure at the same site — especially if paired with failures elsewhere — matches Solderer's "cluster effect," a signal to screen for systemic or genetic contributors (glycemic control, smoking, bone metabolic disease, parafunction/occlusal overload) before another surgical attempt.
8. **Confirm bone quality and rule out infection before choosing immediate vs. delayed reimplantation.** The two are equivalent in outcome only when bone is adequate and infection is absent (Solderer); extended buccal-plate defects need GBR staging first.
9. **Favor larger-diameter, rough-surface implants for the replacement fixture** (Solderer) — but do not compensate for a difficult prior thermal history by over-drilling or over-torquing, which recreates the original risk.

## Gaps & Future Research

- No paper in this wiki (or, per the constituent SRs, in the broader literature they screened) directly studies dense/sclerotic bone **specifically in the posterior mandible with a repeated-failure history** — this overview is a reasoned combination of adjacent evidence, not a directly-tested protocol.
- Heat-generation evidence is dominated by in vitro/ex vivo studies (Chakraborty, Bento, much of Chauhan's 34 studies) — clinical (in vivo) thermal outcome data in dense human mandibular bone specifically is sparse.
- Solderer's reimplantation-survival figures (71–100% / 50–100%) come from small, heterogeneous case series — no controlled comparison exists between reimplantation approaches (immediate vs delayed, technique-matched drilling protocols) at previously-failed dense-bone sites.
- The "cluster effect" host-factor hypothesis is descriptive, not tested — no paper in this wiki quantifies which systemic/genetic workup actually changes reimplantation outcomes.
- Osseodensification's compaction mechanism is well-evidenced for **low-density (D3–D4)** bone primary-stability gain but has no D1-specific evidence in this wiki for thermal-injury reduction — do not extrapolate OD's low-density benefits to the dense-bone thermal problem without direct evidence.
