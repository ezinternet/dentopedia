## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: 치태착색제(Plaque-Disclosing Agent, PDA)의 **추가 효과는 "누가 치태를 제거하는가"와 대상 숙련도에 따라 갈린다** — 숙련된 본인 칫솔질 교육에서는 무효, 술자가 제거하는 전문가 예방처치(prophylaxis)와 비숙련 집단에서는 개선이 보고된다. 8편 종합(RCT 2 + 후향 비교 1 + 전향 단일군 1 + 증례 1 + 단면 1 + in-vitro 2).
> - **대조 RCT 2편은 방향이 갈린다**: ① 치대생 124명, Modified Bass 교육에 PDA(Mira 2-tone) 추가 vs 교육 단독 — 기저선→1주 치태지수(PI) 변화량 군간 차이 0.09 (95% CI −0.05~0.24), 치은지수(GI) 0.04 (−0.14~0.21), 모두 비유의 (Ballı Akgöl 2026; 저자 해석은 천장효과·호손 효과). ② 18–36개월 영유아 25명 교차 RCT에서 전문가 예방처치 전 1% 말라카이트 그린 착색 → 협·순면과 교합면 치태 제거가 유의하게 우수, 처치 시간 391초 vs 437초로 단축 (de Alencar 2019; 설측·구개면은 차이 없음).
> - **두 RCT는 같은 질문이 아니다**: Ballı Akgöl은 *환자 본인의 칫솔질 교육* 효과, de Alencar는 *술자의 제거 효율*을 봤다. 착색이 "보이게 해서 술자가 놓친 치태를 지운다"는 직접 효과는 대조 근거가 있고, "교육 효과를 키운다"는 효과는 숙련자에서 대조 근거로 부정된 셈이다 (제 판단).
> - **개선을 보인 나머지 연구는 설계가 약함**: 만성 치주염 205명 후향 비교에서 PDA 기반 가이드 바이오필름 치료(Guided Biofilm Therapy, GBT) 구강위생지도 3개월 후 PI 31.8 vs 44.3, 탐침 출혈(BOP) 23.4 vs 28.8, PD≥4mm 부위 19.2 vs 26.1 (모두 p≤0.002) — 단 GBT 묶음 개입이라 PDA 단독 효과 분리 불가, 설계 표기도 불일치 (Lei 2025). 아동양육시설 9명 전후비교 PCR 66.1%→38.7% (Nagashima 2018), 17세 1례 PI 2.0→0.8 (Kamra 2024) — 대조군 없음.
> - **숙련도 가설은 여전히 미검증**: "숙련도가 낮을수록 이득"은 이 8편이 직접 검증한 것이 아니다 (제 판단).
> - **치태 호발 부위는 집단별로 다르다**: 588명 학령기 아동 단면연구에서 연령·SES·BMI·DMFT가 검출 부위와 유의하게 연관(예: 중증 우식 DMFT 6–10에서 상악 후방 구개면·하악 후방 협면 p<0.0001), 성별만 무관. 혀 치태는 우식 없는 아동에서도 91.5% (Fasoulas 2019). → 교육 시 부위 지정 근거.
> - **안전성 쟁점**: 에리트로신계 2-Tone 용액은 치은 상피세포에서 50× 희석 시 apoptosis(Sub G1↑, TUNEL+)·창상치유 지연(p<.001), 100×·200×에서는 경미하거나 없음. 시험한 음료·세정액 10종 모두 증류수보다 낫지 않게 착색 제거 (Jung 2020, in-vitro).
> - **수복물 변색 쟁점**: 레진 복합체 3종 + 자이오머 1종에 1회 도포 직후 CIEDE2000 측정 — Mira-2-Ton 용액은 4재료 모두 임상 허용 한계 초과(ΔE00 > 1.8; Charisma Diamond 7.18, Z250 2.98), 에리트로신 겔(ProPind)은 1.35–1.63으로 허용 범위 (Meneke 2026, in-vitro; 타액·연마 없음).
> - **한계 — in-vitro를 임상 위험으로 직접 환산할 수 없다**: 실제 구강 내 접촉 농도·시간·타액 세척은 두 in-vitro 연구 모두 다루지 않으며, 저자 권고는 염증·구강건조·불량 위생 환자에서 신중 사용.
> - **기계적 한계**: 8편 중 어느 것도 PDA vs 다른 시각 피드백(디지털 영상 등)을 직접 비교하지 않았다.
> - **임상 takeaway**: 전문가 예방처치 전 착색은 제거 효율을 높인다는 대조 근거가 있음(영유아); 비숙련 환자·아동의 자가점검 교육에는 합리적 도구이나 효과 크기는 확정 불가; 숙련 환자의 칫솔질 교육에는 추가 이득 근거 없음; 전치부 레진 수복물이 많으면 용액보다 겔 제형 고려; 염증·건조 구강에서는 사용 신중.
> - **확신도**: 효능은 중-약 (대조 RCT 2편이 서로 다른 질문에서 반대 방향, 나머지는 비대조·후향); 안전성은 약 (in-vitro 2편).

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 8 papers (2 RCTs, 1 retrospective comparison, 1 prospective single-arm study, 1 case report, 1 cross-sectional study, 2 in-vitro studies) on plaque-disclosing agents (PDAs): the two RCTs answer different questions and point in opposite directions — adding a PDA to brushing instruction gave no benefit in proficient dental students (n=124; ΔPI difference 0.09, 95% CI −0.05 to 0.24), whereas disclosing before professional prophylaxis in infants (crossover, n=25) improved smooth- and occlusal-surface plaque removal and shortened the procedure.

The weaker designs (a 205-patient retrospective GBT comparison, a 9-child single-arm study, one case report) all report improvement, but none isolates the PDA from co-delivered instruction; the proficiency hypothesis remains untested, and a cross-sectional study (n=588) shows plaque location varies with age, socioeconomic status, BMI and caries.

Safety: in-vitro, an erythrosine-based solution induced gingival epithelial apoptosis at 1:50 dilution, and a two-tone solution discolored resin composites and a giomer beyond the clinical acceptability threshold after one application while an erythrosine gel did not; clinical size of both risks is unquantified.

약한 설계(GBT 후향 비교 205명, 아동 단일군 9명, 증례 1례)는 모두 개선을 보고하나 PDA를 동반 교육과 분리하지 못함; 숙련도 가설은 미검증이며, 단면연구(n=588)는 치태 부위가 연령·SES·BMI·우식에 따라 달라짐을 보임.

안전성: in-vitro에서 에리트로신계 용액이 1:50 희석에서 치은 상피 apoptosis를 유발하고, 2색 용액은 1회 도포로 레진·자이오머를 임상 허용 한계 이상 변색시킨 반면 에리트로신 겔은 허용 범위; 두 위험 모두 임상적 크기는 미정량.

## Summary

Plaque-disclosing agents make biofilm visible so that patients and clinicians can target removal. The eight held papers address three separate questions — does adding a PDA improve outcomes, where does disclosed plaque sit in a population, and is the dye safe — and they differ sharply in evidence level, so they are read below by axis rather than pooled.

### Axis 1 — Does adding a PDA improve outcomes?

The two controlled trials split by who removes the plaque. For patient self-care, the evidence is negative: Ballı Akgöl 2026 (single-center RCT, NCT06080672, n=124 fourth-year dental students, 1:1) gave all participants identical Modified Bass instruction and added Mira 2-tone staining with mirror review to one arm. The prespecified primary outcome, ΔPI from baseline to week 1, did not differ between groups (0.09, 95% CI −0.05 to 0.24); ΔGI at week 1 also did not (0.04, 95% CI −0.14 to 0.21), and no secondary ΔPI/ΔGI comparison was significant, although both arms improved within-group (Friedman p<0.001). In the ANCOVA (adjusted for sex and baseline gum bleeding, which was imbalanced between arms) the test arm had lower adjusted GI at week 1 (p=0.009) but not at month 1 (p=0.142), and no adjusted PI difference was significant; baseline GI was also lower in the test arm (p=0.047), so this signal is hard to attribute to the PDA. Authors attribute the null to a ceiling effect and note PI drifted back toward baseline by month 1.

For operator removal, the evidence is positive: de Alencar 2019 (randomized crossover trial, n=25 children aged 18–36 months in an early-childhood-caries prevention program, Bauru, Brazil, 1-month washout) compared professional prophylaxis preceded by 1% malachite green disclosing against prophylaxis without it. Disclosing gave significantly better plaque removal on labial/buccal and occlusal surfaces (Wilcoxon), but not on lingual/palatal surfaces (p>0.05), and shortened the procedure (391.27 ± 142.2 s vs 436.51 ± 128.4 s); an inverse plaque-index–duration correlation appeared only in the disclosed arm. Limits: small sample, low baseline plaque (PI 1.49 smooth, 1.77 occlusal), and a single operator setting, so it says nothing about patient learning.

The remaining positive reports cannot isolate the dye. Lei 2025 (retrospective comparison, n=220 enrolled / 205 analyzed, chronic periodontitis, Xi'an Jiaotong University) compared guided-biofilm-therapy (GBT) oral hygiene guidance built around disclosing against traditional chairside education over 3 months: PI 31.80 ± 14.06 vs 44.27 ± 15.74 (p<0.001), BOP 23.39 ± 18.53 vs 28.76 ± 16.12 (p<0.001), PD≥4 mm sites 19.18 ± 16.56 vs 26.12 ± 14.91 (p=0.002), with higher self-care efficacy on all three SESS dimensions (p<0.001). The PDA is one part of a GBT bundle, the Methods label the design inconsistently (retrospective cohort vs case-control) and sample sizes are reported inconsistently, so the effect cannot be attributed to the PDA. The GBT bundle itself is covered in [[overviews/professional-biofilm-management-gbt-air-polishing-overview]]. Nagashima 2018 (prospective single-arm, n=9 children in a children's home, Japan) paired group and individual instruction with 3 months of weekly self-checking using disclosing solution: PCR fell from 66.1% (60.7% at 1 month) to 38.7% (P=0.009 and P=0.007) and PHP from 2.8 (2.7) to 1.4 (P=0.012 and P=0.018). Improvement ratio was 0.40 ± 0.35 and did not correlate with grade (r=0.63, P=0.070). Kamra 2024 (case report, n=1, 17-year-old with chronic localized gingivitis) reports PI 2.0→1.8→1.5→1.3→0.8 and GI 2.5→2.3→2.0→1.9→1.0 at baseline, 12 h, 24 h, 1 week and 4 weeks, with twice-daily brushing maintained at a 6-month phone call.

Reading across them (my judgment, not a finding of any single paper): the two RCTs do not conflict, because they test different mechanisms — disclosing helps an operator see and remove plaque they would otherwise miss (de Alencar), but did not add to brushing instruction in already-proficient learners (Ballı Akgöl). The self-care results are consistent with benefit that depends on baseline skill, but cannot establish it — the positive self-care studies have no randomized comparator and instruction co-occurred with the dye, so the effect of the PDA itself is not separable there. The Ballı Akgöl page also records the repeated-PDA-instruction signal from Choi 2013 (PHP +24.0 vs +2.8) as context; that paper is not re-evaluated here.

### Axis 2 — Where does disclosed plaque sit? (targeting instruction)

Fasoulas 2019 (cross-sectional, n=588 schoolchildren aged 4–18, northern Greece, Mira-2-Ton tablets, presence/absence only) mapped detection across 11 tooth surfaces plus tongue. Age/education level, nationality (a socioeconomic proxy), BMI and DMFT were each significantly associated with detection sites — for example preschool children on upper posterior palatal and lower posterior buccal surfaces (both p<0.0001), overweight/obese children on upper posterior buccal (p=0.0014), and severe caries (DMFT 6–10) on upper posterior palatal and lower posterior buccal (both p<0.0001). Sex was the only variable with no effect on any surface (all p>0.05). Tongue biofilm was 91.5% even at DMFT=0 and did not differ across DMFT (p=0.6018). Design limits: convenience sample, binary plaque recording, association only. Practical use: a disclosed map can direct which surfaces to coach for a given patient profile, not predict outcomes.

### Axis 3 — Safety and removability

Jung 2020 (in-vitro; cultured human gingival epithelial cells, mouse head/neck sections, porcine skin) tested an erythrosine-based 2-Tone (Blue-Violet) solution at 200×, 100× and 50× dilution. At 50× it reduced viability on MTT (p<.001), increased the Sub G1 apoptotic fraction, gave TUNEL-positive staining (p<.001) with loss of intercellular adhesion, and caused wound-healing failure; effects were milder at 100× and absent or minimal at 200×. The stain was broad (nuclei, cytoplasm, soft tissue, cortical bone, similar to H&E) and none of ten tested drinks/cleansers outperformed distilled water for decolorization (p>.05). The authors advise safer alternatives and caution in patients with oral inflammation, dryness or poor hygiene. Boundary: n=3 experimental repeats, one product, and no data on actual intraoral contact concentration or duration, so the size of clinical risk is unknown. The dye-substitute question (D&C Red No. 33 vs erythrosine) is covered by [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/sooampon-2026-dc-red-33-erythrosine-plaque-disclosure-rct]], which is outside this synthesis.

Restoration staining is a separate risk. Meneke 2026 (in-vitro, 80 discs of three resin composites and one giomer, n=20 per material, 10 per agent) measured color change with CIEDE2000 immediately after one application. Mira-2-Ton two-tone solution exceeded the clinical acceptability threshold (ΔE00 > 1.8) on all four materials — highest on Charisma Diamond (7.18 ± 2.65), lowest on Z250 (2.98 ± 1.13) — while the erythrosine-based ProPind gel stayed perceptible but acceptable (ΔE00 1.35–1.63) with no difference between materials (p>0.05). Boundary: single application, no saliva and no polishing, so persistence in the mouth is untested. Read with Jung 2020, the two in-vitro studies pull in different directions on erythrosine: gel delivery spared restorations, while the erythrosine solution tested by Jung was cytotoxic at high concentration — formulation, not dye alone, appears to matter (my judgment).

### Hygienist checklist

1. Confirm the patient's baseline brushing proficiency before adding a PDA; for already-proficient patients this synthesis finds no evidence of added benefit.
2. For children or low-literacy patients, use PDA as a self-check tool with weekly or visit-to-visit reinforcement, and record PCR/PHP (or PI) each time.
3. Use the disclosed map to coach specific surfaces; do not assume sites by sex.
4. Before professional prophylaxis, disclose to find missed plaque — expect the gain on buccal and occlusal surfaces, not lingual/palatal.
5. Check for gingival inflammation, dry mouth or poor hygiene before staining; if present, limit use or choose an alternative agent.
6. In patients with visible anterior composite or giomer restorations, prefer a gel formulation over a two-tone solution.
7. Warn patients that the stain is hard to remove and persists on soft tissue.

## Key Contributions

- Separates three questions (efficacy of adding PDA, localization, safety) that single papers conflate, and ranks the evidence behind each.
- Separates operator removal (positive crossover RCT) from patient self-care instruction (null RCT in proficient learners), so the two trials are read as answering different questions rather than conflicting.
- Keeps the population-proficiency hypothesis for self-care, flagged as untested.
- Quantifies the null primary outcome with CIs and the improvement figures in the weaker studies, with design limits stated.
- Surfaces two in-vitro risks — epithelial cytotoxicity and restoration discoloration — with their boundaries, without converting either to a clinical rate.
