## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 이 페이지는 한국 대학병원·치과대학병원 소속 후향 코호트 등 핵심 14편(2011–2026, 이후 10편 추가 — 맨 아래 항목)을 모아 임플란트 수명(생존율, implant survival)을 종합한 것이다. 대부분 단일기관 후향 연구라 근거 수준은 낮고, 국내 임상 수치의 기준점 용도로 읽어야 한다.
> - **임플란트 단위 누적생존율 (Cumulative Survival Rate, CSR)은 대체로 95–98%**: 서울대 SLA 96.8% (≥5년), 마이크로스레드 97.9% (>10년), tapered-straight-tapered 98.1% (≥5년), 연세대 Straumann tissue-level 98.23% (10년), 단국대 장애 환자 98.3% (최대 10년), 고령 환자 95.39% (2–17년).
> - **가장 낮은 값은 설계 특이적 기계적 실패**: RESTORE RBM 임플란트는 10–15년 CSR 92.5%, 실패 17개 중 고정체 파절 10개 + 나사 파절 1개(11/17). 생존율만 보면 같은 "10년"이라도 기종에 따라 5%p 이상 벌어진다.
> - **실패 양상은 시기에 따라 다르다**: 장기 추적에서는 임플란트주위염 (peri-implantitis)이 주 원인(SLA 코호트 실패 25개 중 17개), 장애 환자 코호트에서는 실패 7개 중 5개가 보철 전 조기 실패 — 치유기와 유지기를 나눠 봐야 한다.
> - **위험인자는 일관되지 않다**: 당뇨 (Diabetes Mellitus, DM)는 HR 4.670 (p=0.059, 6 events)부터 비유의까지 흩어지고, 2차 수술(2-stage surgery) HR 4.769, 흡연 OR 4.758·골다공증 OR 3.420 (좁은직경 임플란트)은 각각 단일 코호트 소견이다. 이벤트가 6–32개라 Cox 추정이 불안정하다.
> - **덧붙여 눈에 띄는 교차 소견은 피개의치 (overdenture)**: Ryoo(HR 109.345, 임플란트 2개 근거)와 Hwang denosumab 코호트(유의한 실패 위험)에서 독립적으로 위험 신호가 나왔고, 메타분석(Pachiou 2026)은 짧은 임플란트 지지 피개의치 환자의 45.7%에서 유지관리 사건을 보고했다. 생존율 지표 밖의 비용이 있다.
> - **골흡수억제제 (Anti-resorptive Drug, ARD)**: 폐경 후 여성 806명에서 비스포스포네이트 99.13%·데노수맙 98.08%·대조군 98.24%로 생존율 차이가 없었다 (초록만 확보).
> - **짧은·좁은 임플란트**: 7 mm 임플란트 95.74% (평균 15개월, 실패 2개는 구강암 재건 환자), 좁은직경 3년 92.4% — 추적 기간이 짧아 장기 수명의 근거로는 약하다.
> - **방법론 주의**: 핵심 14편 중 다변량 분석에서 Cox 모형이 불안정(사건 수 부족)한 연구가 다수이며, 4편(Jang, Ryoo, Yum, Nam)은 본문 내 수치 불일치가 인제스트 시 확인됐다. 기계학습은 이벤트가 적은 자료에서 Cox를 이기지 못했다 (C-index 0.72 vs 0.81).
> - **공백**: 전국 단위 건강보험 청구자료 기반 생존율, 전향 연구, 비교군이 있는 장기(≥10년) 연구가 위키에 없다. 상담 시 "10년 95–98%"는 단일기관 후향 수치임을 같이 말해야 한다.
> - **분당서울대병원 계열 10편 추가(2026-11-10)**: 생존율 자체보다 **'생존은 높은데 성공은 낮다'**는 간극이 반복된다 — 초광폭 92.3% vs 83.3%(Kim 2023), 경사 식립 고정성 하이브리드 94.9% vs 60.6%(Seok 2023), 수평 증대 95.9% vs 89.8%(Choi 2023). 예측 인자는 설계가 아니라 상태: 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)<60이면 성공 61%, 임플란트 주위염 수술 시 골소실 >50%면 생존 21.3%(Hwang 2023). 표면·합금 RCT 3편은 1년 100%로 군간 차이 없음. 가이드 수술은 42.7%에서 중도 포기(Ku 2022). 고정체 파절은 평균 61.7개월에 경고 징후 후 발생(Yu 2020), 인접 치근 손상 치아는 90.6% 기능 유지(Yi 2021).

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 24 papers (2011–2026) from Korean university hospitals and dental schools — a core set of 14 plus 10 Bundang papers on complications, technique and materials added 2026-11-10. The core 14: 8 long-term implant-survival cohorts, 2 narrow/short-implant cohorts, 1 one-year long-neck cohort, 1 SR+MA on short-implant overdentures, 1 antiresorptive-drug cohort and 1 failure-prediction study; almost all single-centre retrospective designs.

Implant-level cumulative survival clustered at 95–98% for 5–10+ years (range 92.5% for a fracture-prone RBM implant to 98.3% in a disability cohort); late failures were mostly peri-implantitis or mechanical fracture, and no risk factor replicated across cohorts except a recurring, sparsely supported overdenture signal.

These figures are a local benchmark rather than causal evidence: event counts were 6–108, Cox models were unstable, and nationwide, prospective and comparator-controlled long-term Korean data are not in the wiki.

## 세줄요약

한국 대학병원·치과대학병원 24편(2011–2026) 종합 — 핵심 14편 + 2026-11-10 추가된 분당서울대병원 계열 10편(합병증·술식·재료). 핵심 14편: 장기 생존율 코호트 8편, 좁은·짧은 임플란트 코호트 2편, 1년 롱넥 코호트 1편, 짧은 임플란트 지지 피개의치 SR+MA 1편, 골흡수억제제 코호트 1편, 실패 예측 1편이며 대부분 단일기관 후향 설계.

임플란트 단위 누적생존율은 5–10년 이상에서 95–98%에 몰려 있고(고정체 파절 취약 RBM 임플란트 92.5% ~ 장애 환자 코호트 98.3%), 후기 실패는 임플란트주위염 또는 기계적 파절이 주였으며 코호트 간 재현된 위험인자는 피개의치 신호 정도뿐이었다.

이 수치는 인과 근거가 아닌 국내 기준점이다 — 이벤트가 6–108개로 적고 Cox 모형이 불안정하며, 전국 단위·전향·비교군 설계의 장기 한국 자료는 위키에 없다.

## Summary

This overview asks a practical question: *what survival and failure pattern should be expected for implants placed in Korean university-hospital settings, and which factors are associated with loss?* It deliberately complements, rather than repeats, [[overviews/systemic-host-risk-factors-implant-survival-overview]] (umbrella-level systemic risk tiers) and [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]] (early-failure prevention) by anchoring on **local single-cohort data**.

Three points carry the synthesis:

1. **Survival level.** Where follow-up was ≥5 years, implant-level cumulative survival rate (CSR) fell between 96.8% and 98.3% for most contemporary systems. The two lower values come from different mechanisms — a fracture-prone design (92.5%) and a mixed elderly/older-era cohort (95.39%, 96.33% reported).
2. **Failure mode depends on the window.** Early (pre-prosthetic) loss dominated the disability cohort; late biological loss (peri-implantitis) dominated the SLA cohort; mechanical fracture dominated the RBM cohort.
3. **Risk-factor evidence is thin and heterogeneous.** Each factor appears in one or two cohorts with wide confidence intervals; overdenture support is the only signal appearing independently in two Korean cohorts, and even there one estimate rests on 2 implants.

### Failure modes by cohort

- **Biological, late:** 17 of 25 failures in the SLA cohort were peri-implantitis ([[implants/survival/yum-2023-the-cumulative-survival-rate-of]]); 7 of 19 in the narrow-implant cohort ([[implants/short-narrow/kim-2023-clinical-outcome-of-narrow-diameter]]).
- **Mechanical:** 11 of 17 failures in the RBM cohort were fixture (10) or screw (1) fracture ([[implants/survival/ryoo-2023-a-retrospective-study-of-the]]) — a design-specific hazard that a pooled "10-year survival" figure would hide.
- **Early, pre-prosthetic:** 5 of 7 failures in the disability cohort, and 12 of 19 in the narrow-implant cohort, occurred before or at osseointegration; [[implants/survival/kim-2018-retrospective-radiographic-observational-study-of]] likewise reports 13 implants removed early vs 8 late.
- **Crown-level complications are separate from survival:** decementation affected 9.33% of implants in the disability cohort despite 99.7% functional survival.

### Risk-factor signals and how well they hold

- **Diabetes:** HR 4.670 (p = 0.059, 6 events, 94.3% vs 98.9%) in [[implants/survival/kim-2025-clinical-evaluation-of-tapered-straight]], with greater mesial marginal bone loss (MBL); not significant for survival in [[implants/survival/yum-2023-the-cumulative-survival-rate-of]] (HR 1.962, p = 0.131) but significant for failure in the post-menopausal ARD cohort. Direction is consistent, precision is not.
- **Smoking / osteoporosis:** OR 4.758 and 3.420 in narrow-diameter implants; smoking not significant in the SLA cohort (HR 2.050, p = 0.333, small smoker group).
- **Two-stage surgery:** HR 4.769 (95% CI 1.081–21.033) for survival in the micro-thread cohort with only 10 events; associated with more mesial MBL in the TST cohort.
- **Overdenture support:** HR 109.345 (95% CI 9.245–1,293.229) in the RBM cohort, resting on 2 implants in 1 patient; significant failure risk in the ARD cohort; 45.7% of short-implant overdenture patients needed at least one maintenance event in the SR+MA.
- **Anterior placement and periodontitis-related tooth loss:** the main risk factors in both Cox and RSF models in [[implants/survival/hwang-2026-prediction-and-risk-factor-analysis]]; machine learning did not beat the best Cox model (C-index 0.81 vs RSF 0.72, AUC 0.79).
- **Age:** significant in [[implants/survival/jang-2011-a-retrospective-study-on-related]] (p = .048; lowest survival 66.67% in patients older than 79), whereas [[implants/survival/park-2016-long-term-outcomes-of-dental]] concludes age alone does not appear to affect survival in patients aged 65–89.
- **Antiresorptive drugs:** no difference in failure or MBL across bisphosphonate, denosumab and control groups.

## Methodology

Selection came from a PubMed surveillance sweep (2026-11-08) for implant survival/longevity papers with Korean university or hospital affiliations. Papers were ingested through the standard pipeline (dedup and retraction gate, full text from PMC where available, otherwise abstract-only) and are summarised here from their wiki pages. No external sources were used. Where a study defined survival at implant level vs patient level, both are shown. Cross-paper comparison is narrative; no pooling was attempted because follow-up definitions, implant systems and censoring rules differ.

## Results

**Pattern.** Long-term implant-level CSR in modern single-centre Korean cohorts is 96.8–98.3%; the lower values (92.5%, 92.4% at 3 years) correspond to a fracture-prone implant design and to narrow-diameter implants, respectively. These figures are descriptive of the cohorts, not interchangeable across implant systems.

**Reading cautions recorded during ingest.**
- [[implants/survival/jang-2011-a-retrospective-study-on-related]]: reported CSR 96.33% vs crude 98.3% (108/6,385); one system name differs between sections; tables were missing from the available text.
- [[implants/survival/ryoo-2023-a-retrospective-study-of-the]]: failure-cause percentages differ between Results and Discussion; Table 2 counts were used.
- [[implants/survival/yum-2023-the-cumulative-survival-rate-of]]: implant count differs between a figure caption and Results; several "significant" descriptions with p > 0.05.
- [[implants/survival/nam-2023-the-cumulative-survival-rate-of]]: external vs internal connection survival figures swapped between text and Table 1.
- Five pages (Hwang ×2, Park 2016, Kim 2018, Pachiou) are abstract-only; effect sizes for risk factors are unavailable there.

**Clinical reasoning (derived from the held papers, not new facts).**
- When counselling, quote a range (about 95–98% at 5–10 years) and state that it comes from single-centre retrospective data.
- Separate the healing window (early loss) from the maintenance window (peri-implantitis, mechanical events) when planning recall.
- Treat diabetes and smoking as "monitor more closely" factors for MBL and peri-implantitis rather than as proven survival determinants in this data.
- Plan overdenture cases with maintenance burden in mind; the survival number alone understates it.
- Confirm the fracture history of a specific implant design before assuming a generic 10-year figure applies.

**Gaps.** No nationwide Korean survival analysis, no prospective Korean long-term cohort, and no head-to-head comparison between systems are in the wiki. [[implants/park-2024-dental-implant-utilization-nhis-korea]] covers utilization, not survival.

**Three patterns across the set.**

1. *Survival overstates how well implants are doing.* Wherever both are reported, success sits well below survival: 83.3% against 92.3% for ultra-wide implants, 60.6% against 94.9% under fixed hybrids, 89.8% against 95.9% in augmented sites. The long-term survival figures in the main body of this page should be read with that discount in mind. An implant that is present is not necessarily an implant without bone loss or complications.
2. *Condition predicts outcome; design does not.* In the cohorts that looked for predictors, the significant variable was the state of the site: primary stability (Kim 2023), bone loss at the time of surgery (Hwang), graft status (Seok, Choi). Diameter, length, surgical technique and membrane use were not. The three randomised trials make the same point from the other side: surface chemistry, surface roughness and alloy made no difference to one-year outcome in patients selected for favourable conditions.
3. *The complications are slow and signalled.* Fixture fracture arrived about five years after loading with warning signs first; adjacent-root injury mostly settled without treatment over ten years; peri-implantitis survival fell with the stage at which surgery was done. The common implication is for recall: these cohorts reward surveillance and early intervention more than they reward device selection.

**Reading the set as a whole.** These are single-centre, mostly retrospective studies from one referral hospital, several with overlapping authors, and the randomised trials are small and one year long. Their value to this page is local applicability and long follow-up, not statistical power. The success figures depend on each paper's criteria and are not comparable across papers.
