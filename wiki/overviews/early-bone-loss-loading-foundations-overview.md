## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 범위: 1992–2005년 Misch 공저 고찰 다섯 편(힘 전달 1992, 역토크 2000, 초기 골소실 원인 2002, 설계 2003, 교합 가이드라인 2005)을 묶은 기초 종합. 다섯 편 모두 서술적 고찰이며 **초록만 확보된 상태**(전문 미확보)다. [확인]
> - 힘 전달(1992): 임플란트 생체역학의 어휘(힘 성분·모멘트·응력-변형률)를 세운 입문서이며 정량 자료가 없다. 이후 설계·과부하 논문들이 전제하는 기초다. [확인]
> - 역토크 검사(2000): 초기 부하 실패를 거르려는 역토크 검사 자체가 저밀도 골에서 전단 하중으로 초기 골소실을 일으킬 수 있다고 비판한다. 골밀도·미세회전·크기/설계는 평가되지 않았다. [확인]
> - 초기 골소실 원인(2002): 여섯 가설(수술 외상·교합 과부하·주위염·미세간극·생물학적 폭·크레스트 모듈) 중 생물학적 폭 재형성, 골능선 이하 미세간극, 교합 과부하, 크레스트 모듈을 가장 유력하다고 보았다. 원인은 미해결이며 효과 크기는 없다. [확인]
> - 기준선: 초기 골소실 이후 연간 0.2 mm 이하라는 관행적 기준은 2002년 리뷰에 그대로 등장한다. 같은 기준을 현대 메타분석(Kumar 2021, 하중 후 1년 pooled MBL 0.56 mm, I²=99.8%)과 비교할 때 기간·측정 기준이 다르다는 점을 먼저 확인할 것. [확인]
> - 설계(2003): 단일 최적 설계는 없고, 형상·재료·표면·나사산이 서로 얽힌 공학적 절충이라는 틀을 제시한다. 나사산 설계의 성공 영향은 자료가 부족하다고 명시한다. [확인]
> - 교합(2005): 과부하 요인은 큰 캔틸레버·이갈이·부적절한 교합 설계·조기 접촉이며, 임플란트 교합을 생리적 범위 안에 두라고 권고한다. 그러나 저자들도 임플란트 전용 근거 기반 교합 개념이 없다고 인정한다. [확인]
> - 교합 과부하와 골소실: 연관은 시사되지만 인과는 미해결이다. 과부하 연관 SR(Di Fiore 2022, 7편)은 근거 질이 낮고, 80편 SR(Mojaver 2025)은 과부하를 위험 조절인자(risk modifier)로 읽는다. [확인] (overview 본문 재확인)
> - 즉시 임시 보철 하중(Yari 2023, 조기 실패 overview 경유): 즉시 임시 보철/하중이 즉시 임시 보철/하중이 조기 실패의 가장 강한 단일 예측인자였다(OR 3.42). 역토크 비판이 제기한 "초기 부하 실패" 문제가 여기서 임상 결정 지점으로 이어진다. [확인] (overview 본문 재확인)
> - **공저 주의**: 다섯 편 모두 Misch가 공저자이고 Oh·Wang이 여러 편에 겹친다. 이 다섯 편이 서로 같은 틀로 말하는 것은 독립 확인이 아니다 (`reinforces` 순환 규칙). [확인]
> - 종합 한 줄: 1992–2005의 기초 고찰은 "무엇이 초기 골소실을 일으키는가"라는 질문과 그 검증 도구(역토크)의 한계를 열어 두었고, 이후 메타분석이 그 질문에 기준선과 기술 레버를 주었다. 원인 우선순위에 대한 정량 답은 여전히 없다. [미검증]

## Three-line Summary

Five Misch co-authored narrative reviews (1992–2005) form the mechanical and early-bone-loss foundation of the implant overviews: force-transfer vocabulary, a critique of reverse torque testing, six hypothesized causes of early crestal bone loss, design as interrelated engineering trade-offs, and overload guidelines for implant occlusion.
None of the five reports pooled outcome data, and all five are abstract-only in this wiki, so they set the questions and the vocabulary rather than the answers.
Read them as positions to test against later evidence: the 0.2 mm/year benchmark, the microgap and overload hypotheses, and the early-loading risk are now answered in part by meta-analyses, while causal ranking and reverse-torque validity remain open.

## 세줄요약

1992–2005년 Misch 공저 서술적 고찰 다섯 편이 현대 임플란트 overview의 기계·초기 골소실 기초를 이룬다. 내용은 힘 전달 어휘, 역토크 검사 비판, 초기 치조정 골소실 여섯 가설, 설계를 얽힌 공학적 절충으로 보는 틀, 임플란트 교합 과부하 가이드라인이다.
다섯 편 모두 결과 자료를 합산하지 않았고 이 위키에서는 초록만 확보되어 있으므로, 답이 아니라 질문과 어휘를 세운 자료다.
이들을 현대 근거와 맞춰 볼 입장으로 읽는다. 연간 0.2 mm 기준, 미세간극·과부하 가설, 초기 부하 위험은 메타분석이 일부 답했고, 원인의 정량 순위와 역토크의 타당성은 아직 열려 있다.

## Summary

These five reviews share one authorship core (Misch with Oh and Wang), one period (1992–2005), and one method (narrative argument from mechanics). They are useful because they name the questions that later evidence was built to answer.

**1. Force transfer as vocabulary (Bidez & Misch, 1992).** The primer defines force components, moments, transfer mechanisms, impact and stress-strain relations for implant support. It contains no numbers. Its role is to fix the terms that the design and overload papers below assume. See [[implants/bidez-1992-force-transfer-implant-dentistry-basic-concepts]].

**2. A diagnostic test under question (Jividen & Misch, 2000).** Reverse torque testing is criticized as a screen for early loading failure. The argument is that its shear load may itself cause early crestal bone loss and failure in low-density bone, and that bone density, micromotion, size and design were not evaluated. The review also proposes a failure nomenclature. No quantitative failure data appear in the abstract. See [[implants/jividen-2000-reverse-torque-testing-early-loading-failures]].

**3. Six hypotheses for early bone loss (Oh, Yoon, Misch & Wang, 2002).** The six candidates are surgical trauma, occlusal overload, peri-implantitis, microgap, biologic width and crest module. The authors judge biologic-width reformation, a microgap at or below the crest, occlusal overload and crest module most likely, with surgical trauma and peri-implantitis as contributors. Typical loss after the first year is stated as ≤0.2 mm per year. They call for randomized trials and give no effect sizes. See [[implants/mbl/oh-2002-causes-early-implant-bone-loss]].

**4. Design as trade-offs (Steigenga et al., 2003).** Implant design (geometry, material, surface, thread) is framed as interrelated engineering choices that govern strength, interfacial stability and load transfer. There is no single optimal design, and the authors flag limited evidence on how thread design affects overall success. See [[implants/steigenga-2003-dental-implant-design-long-term-success]].

**5. Occlusion guidelines with an evidence gap (Kim, Oh, Misch & Wang, 2005).** Overload factors are large cantilevers, parafunction, improper occlusal design and premature contacts. The advice is to keep implant occlusion physiologic. The authors state that no evidence-based implant-specific occlusal concept exists, so the recommendations rest on biomechanical rationale. See [[occlusion/kim-2005-occlusal-considerations-implant-therapy-guidelines]].

## Where the positions meet

- **Occlusal overload** appears as a cause in 2002 and as a guideline factor in 2005. Both papers treat it as plausible and unquantified. Later reviews keep it as an associated risk, not a proven cause (see below).
- **Loading protocol** is the subject of the 2000 critique, but the critique concerns the test, not the loading decision. The wider early-loading question is picked up by [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]].
- **Microgap and biologic width** appear only in the 2002 review. No paper in this set tests them directly.
- **Design** appears only as an engineering framing (2003). The design levers that later pages argue for are in [[overviews/bone-quality-implant-risk-modification-overview]].

## What later evidence did with these questions

- **The 0.2 mm/year benchmark needs its denominator.** Kumar 2021 (SR+MA, 5 prospective studies, 525 implants) gives a pooled 1-year post-loading MBL of 0.56 mm with I² = 99.8%, which the MBL risk-factor overview reads against the Albrektsson criteria (<1.5 mm in year 1, <0.2 mm/year after). The 2002 review's 0.2 mm figure is the later-year criterion; it is not a first-year norm. See [[overviews/implant-failure-mbl-risk-factors-overview]] and [[implants/mbl/kumar-2021-marginal-bone-level-changes-meta-analysis]]. [확인]
- **The microgap hypothesis now has a technique lever.** Within Kumar 2021, platform-switched implants showed 0.01–0.95 mm MBL against about 1.67 mm for conventional connections. This is an association across mixed studies, not a test of microgap as the cause. [확인]
- **Occlusal overload has moved from cause to modifier.** Di Fiore 2022 (SR, 7 studies) reports an association with peri-implant bone loss but low evidence quality. Mojaver 2025 (SR, 80 studies) reports MBL 0.65–3.0 mm and peri-implantitis 20–50%, and reads overload as a risk modifier that interacts with biofilm inflammation. See [[overviews/implant-occlusion-loading-biomechanics-overview]] and [[occlusion/mojaver-2025-occlusal-overload-peri-implant-health-sr]]. [확인]
- **Early loading risk has a number.** Yari 2023 found immediate provisional loading to be the strongest single predictor of early failure in its series (OR 3.42). See [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]. [확인]
- **The reverse-torque critique is not resolved in this wiki.** A search for reverse-torque mentions returned pages on implant removal, abutment contamination, surfaces and stability methods, none of which tests the screen itself. This is a gap in the held evidence, not a finding that the test is valid or invalid. [확인]

## Reading rules for this set

- **All five are narrative reviews.** None is a systematic review, and all five are abstract-only in this wiki. Quote them as positions, not as outcome data.
- **Shared authorship is not independent confirmation.** Every paper in the set has Misch as co-author, and Oh and Wang recur. Where the five agree, the agreement is internal to one research group's framing (see the `reinforces` circularity rule in INGEST.md).
- **Keep the 2002 list as a checklist, not a ranking.** The "most likely" judgement was made without effect sizes and the authors asked for trials to settle it.

## Clinical takeaways (as the held papers support them)

1. Use the 2002 six-cause list as a checklist when early crestal loss is found, and record which factors were present, rather than assigning one cause. [확인] (Oh 2002)
2. Compare early bone loss against the first-year pooled value (0.56 mm, Kumar 2021) and the later-year ≤0.2 mm/year criterion separately, and do not apply the later-year figure to year one. [확인]
3. Do not treat reverse torque alone as clearance for early loading; the 2000 critique stands and no held paper validates the screen. [확인] (Jividen 2000; gap search)
4. Treat occlusal overload as a modifier worth correcting (cantilevers, premature contacts, parafunction), not as a proven single cause of bone loss. [확인] (Kim 2005; Di Fiore 2022; Mojaver 2025)
5. Reserve immediate provisional loading for favorable host, bone and torque profiles, since it was the strongest single early-failure predictor in Yari 2023. [확인] (via the early-failure overview)
