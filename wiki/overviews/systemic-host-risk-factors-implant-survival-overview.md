## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 이 페이지는 "어떤 전신질환·숙주인자가 임플란트 생존을 실제로 낮추는가"를 8편(umbrella review 6 + 전향 코호트 1 + 방법론 risk-of-bias 1)으로 종합해 **위험 계층화(risk stratification)** 로 정리한 cross-cutting 허브다.
> - **가장 확고한 위험인자 = 흡연**: 17개 SR 중 15개가 유의한 흡연↔실패/변연골소실(MBL) 연관 보고, 메타분석 RR/OR 대략 **1.9–2.9배** (Banerjee 2024 umbrella). 용량반응: ≥10개비/일에서 유의(p=0.001).
> - **두 번째로 확고 = 두경부 방사선치료(radiotherapy)**: 성공률 86.2%(조사) vs 95.2%(비조사), 절대 ~9%p 감소 (Zarzar 2023; 더 큰 Pacheco 2025는 81.5% vs 94.6%). 금기는 아니나 명확한 성공률 저하.
> - **당뇨(diabetes)는 통념보다 약함**: 모든 pooled 메타분석에서 DM vs non-DM 임플란트 실패 **유의차 없음** (Meza-Mauricio 2019; D'Ambrosio 2023). 단 주위염(peri-implantitis) 위험은 증가 경향, 혈당조절 불량(HbA1c 7–9%)은 예외. → 금기 아닌 감시(monitor) 인자.
> - **항흡수제(antiresorptive)/MRONJ**: pooled 실패효과 유의하지 않고 MRONJ는 소수 실패의 2–17%만 차지. 위험은 **정맥(IV) bisphosphonate·≥45–60개월·암 적응증**에 집중 (Dahiya 2024). 경구 단기는 저위험.
> - **쇼그렌증후군(Sjögren, SS)은 오히려 양호**: SR 통합 성공률 ~95.2% (Barros 2021), 5년 전향연구에서 생존·성공 **100%**, MBL·PPD 대조군과 무차이 (Hosseini 2024). 삶의 질 부담은 임플란트가 아니라 구강건조(hyposalivation) 자체에서 옴.
> - **약물 신호(D'Ambrosio 2023)**: SSRI(+7.5%)·양성자펌프억제제(PPI, +4.5%)는 일관된 실패 증가 신호(예비적); 항고혈압제는 오히려 보호 신호(0.6% vs 4.1%).
> - **근거의 질이 거의 전반적으로 약함**: umbrella review 대부분 AMSTAR-2 low/critically-low, "high"는 0–1편뿐(흡연 1/17, SS 3/4 critically-low, 방사선 1/15, 항흡수제 0/10). Oliveira-Neto 2018(ROBIS)은 흡연 메타분석 6편 중 5편이 high/unclear risk임을 명시 — 수치를 확정이 아닌 **잠정치**로 취급하라.
> - **실무 계층화**: (강한 근거) 흡연·두경부 방사선 → 사전동의·금연상담·프로토콜 조정. (감시 인자) 당뇨·항흡수제·SSRI/PPI·쇼그렌 → 금기 아님, 밀착 관찰(특히 IV/장기 항흡수제, 혈당불량). 절대 금기는 사실상 장기 글루코코르티코이드뿐.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-cutting risk-stratification synthesis of 8 papers (6 umbrella reviews + 1 prospective cohort + 1 ROBIS methods appraisal) on which systemic/host factors actually lower implant survival: **smoking is the firmest risk factor** (15/17 SRs significant; meta-analytic RR/OR ≈ 1.9–2.9, dose-response threshold ≥10 cig/day; Banerjee 2024), and **head-and-neck radiotherapy is next** (success 86.2% irradiated vs 95.2% non-irradiated; Zarzar 2023) — both are elevate-and-consent factors, not contraindications.

By contrast, factors clinicians often over-weight are weak or null: **diabetes shows no significant failure difference in every pooled meta-analysis** (Meza-Mauricio 2019; D'Ambrosio 2023) though peri-implantitis and poor glycemic control (HbA1c 7–9%) trend worse; **antiresorptives** show no significant pooled failure effect with MRONJ risk concentrated in IV bisphosphonate ≥45–60 months (Dahiya 2024); and **Sjögren syndrome performs well** (≈95% SR success; 100% 5-year survival with MBL/PPD equal to controls — Barros 2021, Hosseini 2024), its burden driven by hyposalivation not implant failure.

The evidence base is methodologically weak almost everywhere — modal AMSTAR-2 is low/critically-low with typically 0–1 "high" SRs (1/17 smoking, 3/4 SS critically-low, 1/15 radiotherapy, 0/10 antiresorptive) — and Oliveira-Neto 2018 makes this explicit for the smoking literature (5/6 meta-analyses high/unclear ROBIS risk); treat every estimate as provisional, stratify aggressively on smoking and prior radiation, and monitor-not-contraindicate diabetes, antiresorptives, SSRI/PPI, and Sjögren.

## 세줄요약

전신질환·숙주인자가 임플란트 생존을 실제 낮추는지 종합한 8편(umbrella 6 + 전향 코호트 1 + 방법론 1): **흡연이 가장 확고한 위험인자**(17개 SR 중 15개 유의, 메타 RR/OR ≈ 1.9–2.9, ≥10개비/일 용량반응; Banerjee 2024), **두경부 방사선치료가 다음**(성공 86.2% vs 95.2%; Zarzar 2023) — 둘 다 금기가 아닌 사전동의·프로토콜 조정 대상.

반면 통념보다 약하거나 무의: **당뇨는 모든 pooled 메타분석에서 실패 유의차 없음**(주위염·HbA1c 7–9%만 예외), **항흡수제**도 pooled 실패효과 무의(MRONJ 위험은 IV·≥45–60개월에 집중), **쇼그렌은 오히려 양호**(SR 성공 ~95%, 5년 생존 100%, MBL 대조군 무차이) — 부담은 구강건조에서 옴.

근거의 질이 거의 전반적으로 약함(AMSTAR-2 low/critically-low 다수, high 0–1편)이며 Oliveira-Neto 2018은 흡연 메타분석 6편 중 5편이 high/unclear risk임을 명시 — 모든 추정치를 잠정치로 취급, 흡연·방사선은 강하게 계층화하고 당뇨·항흡수제·SSRI/PPI·쇼그렌은 금기 아닌 감시 인자로.

## Summary

Clinicians carry an intuitive ranking of "which patients are risky for implants," but that ranking is often out of step with the pooled evidence. This page assembles 8 previously-unsynthesized papers into one **risk-stratification map**: it separates factors with *firm* survival-lowering evidence (smoking, radiotherapy) from those that are *weak, null, or over-weighted* (diabetes, antiresorptives, Sjögren) and from *preliminary drug signals* (SSRI, PPI) — and it foregrounds a uniform caveat: the underlying evidence is methodologically weak almost everywhere, so these are directions, not decrees.

This is a cross-cutting hub. Disease-specific depth lives in the dedicated overviews (smoking, systemic-disease, drug-management, early-failure); this page is the connecting tissue that ranks them against each other and flags the shared quality problem.

### The methodological through-line (why every number is provisional)

Across these umbrella reviews the modal AMSTAR-2 grade is low or critically-low, and "high" is rare: 1/17 (smoking), 3/4 critically-low (Sjögren), 50% critically-low (diabetes SRs), 1/15 (radiotherapy), 0/10 high (antiresorptive). **Oliveira-Neto 2018 is the dedicated appraisal that makes this explicit**: applying ROBIS to 6 smoking-implant meta-analyses, it found only 1/6 low risk, 3/6 unclear, 2/6 high — i.e. 5/6 high or unclear — with the most-failed domains being study eligibility criteria and study identification/selection (κ=0.90). The lesson generalizes: a large pooled effect built on high-risk-of-bias inputs is a hypothesis with numbers attached, not a settled fact.

### Diabetes: the clearest "over-weighted" factor

D'Ambrosio and Meza-Mauricio converge: **no pooled meta-analysis finds a significant DM failure signal.** The nuance is that (a) primary studies lump all diabetics together rather than stratifying by HbA1c/duration, and (b) peri-implantitis (not osseointegration failure) is where the diabetic signal actually sits — D'Ambrosio cites Ferreira OR 1.9 for peri-implantitis and Tawil's HbA1c ≤7% → 0 peri-implant disease vs 7–9% → 6/141 implants affected. So the correct frame is glycemic-control-stratified surveillance, not blanket caution. This *refines* the peri-implantitis emphasis of [[implants/survival/meza-mauricio-2019-diabetes-implant-failure-peri-implant]].

### Sjögren: reassuring, from two independent angles

Barros (SR pooling, 722 implants / 189 SS patients, 95.22% success) and Hosseini (prospective 5-year, 23 pSS vs 24 matched controls, 100% survival in both, MBL −0.04 vs −0.23 mm p=.685) agree that implant *performance* is preserved in Sjögren. What differs is host burden — DMFT, salivary flow, gingival index, OHIP-49 QoL — which tracks the dry mouth, not the implant. The practical message flips a common assumption: Sjögren is a QoL-management challenge, not an implant-survival contraindication.

## Methodology

Narrative cross-cutting synthesis (no new pooling; the sources are themselves umbrella reviews of heterogeneous primaries). Evidence base: 6 umbrella reviews (Banerjee, Barros, Meza-Mauricio, Zarzar, D'Ambrosio, Dahiya), 1 prospective 5-year cohort (Hosseini), 1 ROBIS methods appraisal (Oliveira-Neto). Four sources are abstract-only (Barros, Meza-Mauricio, Oliveira-Neto, Zarzar) — directionality and headline rates captured, some effect sizes not recoverable. All figures quoted verbatim from source pages; AMSTAR-2/ROBIS grades reported to expose certainty limits.

## Results

**Chairside risk-stratification algorithm:**

1. **Smoker?** → Established ~2–3× failure risk (dose-dependent). Document, counsel cessation, obtain informed consent, consider protocol modification. Firmest single risk factor.
2. **Prior head-and-neck radiotherapy?** → ~9%p absolute success reduction. Valid but higher-risk; consent and adapted planning.
3. **Diabetic?** → Not a contraindication; stratify by glycemic control (HbA1c ≤7% ≈ healthy; 7–9% → closer peri-implant surveillance). Watch peri-implantitis, not osseointegration failure.
4. **On antiresorptives?** → Individualize: oral short-duration low-risk; IV bisphosphonate ≥45–60 months / cancer indication is the real MRONJ concern. Consider antibiotic prophylaxis.
5. **Sjögren / other systemic (HIV, hypothyroid, cardiovascular)?** → Reassure on survival; manage hyposalivation/QoL. SSRI/PPI users → monitor (preliminary signal). Long-term glucocorticoids remain the one near-absolute caution.
6. **Always** → State the evidence is low-certainty; avoid over-promising and over-restricting.
