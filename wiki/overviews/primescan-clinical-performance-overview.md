## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **전악 임상 정확도 최상위**: 프라임스캔(Primescan)은 현재까지의 임상 연구(ender-2026, in vivo 10명)에서 전악 진도성(trueness) **41 µm**로 6개 IOS 중 최우수. 전통 폴리에테르 인상(21 µm)은 정밀도에서 여전히 우위이나 전악 임상 적용 역치(< 100 µm) 이내.
> - **프라임스캔 2(Primescan 2)**: 1세대와 통계적 차이 없음(ender-2026). 임상 성능 동등 — 기기 교체 시 재학습 최소화.
> - **조명 조건**: 치과 진료실 조명(2500 lx/4100 K)에서 최적(guo-2026). 어두운 환경(0 lx)에서도 TRIOS 4보다 색조 측정 견고성 우위. **진료실 조명을 프라임스캔의 색온도(4100 K)에 맞출 것**.
> - **포스트 준비 공간(post preparation)**: TRIOS 4보다 유의하게 높은 진도성·정밀도(wildhagen-2026). IOS 포스트 디지털 인상 첫 타당성 증거. 깊이 10 mm는 양호, 12 mm에서 정확도 감소 — 임상 적용 시 깊이 10 mm 이하 목표 권장.
> - **삭제 형태(preparation design) 의존성**: 단순 교합 비니어(partial occlusal veneer)에서 가장 양호(sanioglu-2026). **전부 비니어(buccal + palatal) 준비에서 최대 오차(62.3 µm)** — 이 디자인에서는 TRIOS 5가 우위. 단면 삭제는 프라임스캔 유리.
> - **전악 in vitro 벤치마크**: buhl-2025에서 trueness 50–100 µm, 구치부 연장 시 오차 증가. vitai-2023 SR+NMA에서 TRIOS 3와 함께 전악 상위 클러스터.
> - **고정식 교정 장치 환경**: schlenz-2022에서 최속 스캔(92–115 s), 전통 알지네이트 대비 정확도 우위.
> - **임상 의사결정 분기**: ① 단순 단일치 왕관 → 프라임스캔 우선 선택 ② 전부 비니어 구개측 준비 → TRIOS 계열 고려 ③ 포스트 준비 → 프라임스캔 가능(**8 mm 이하**; 10 mm 이상 주의) ④ 완전궁(유치열) → 프라임스캔 2위(42 µm, borbola-2024); EmeraldS·Mediti700도 동등 ⑤ **무치악 경간 길이 증가 → TRIOS 3 유리** (mayer-2025: R²=0.892 프라임스캔 더 민감); 완전 무치악 → SPG 우선.
> - **완전궁 7-way 비교(가상적합법, borbola-2024)**: 프라임스캔 변연 간격 2위(42 µm); MAE 정밀도 2그룹; EmeraldS·Mediti700이 新 경쟁자로 등장 — 단일 IOS에 의존 말 것.
> - **인레이·온레이 디지털 인상**: 프라임스캔 인레이 진도성 **12.29 µm** — 비교 IOS 중 최우수(revilla-leon-2025 SR+MA). 디지털·전통 인상 간 변연 불일치 유의차 없음; 비니어는 근거 불충분.
> - **근관치료 접근와 스캔(신규)**: 프라임스캔으로 근관구 위치·개수 판독 가능(현미경·CBCT 동등); 접근와 결함 87.5% 진단 정확도; 석회화 근관구에서는 중등도(79.2%) — 그 경우 CBCT 보완 필요(grazziotin-2026).
> - **환자 경험**: IOS는 전통 인상 대비 의자옆시간(chair-side time) 대폭 단축(12 vs 75.5 min; ramos-morro-2026), 환자 편의 개선 — 프라임스캔은 이 편익에서 타 IOS와 동등.

## Three-line Summary
Primescan (Dentsply Sirona CEREC) consistently ranks among the top-tier IOS for complete-arch and single-unit scanning accuracy across in vivo and in vitro studies. Its active-triangulation acquisition yields advantages in speed and robustness under dark conditions, but shows specific limitations with palatal concavities and deep narrow preparations. The 2026 evidence wave adds post-space feasibility data and lighting optimization guidelines that directly inform daily clinical decisions.

## 세줄요약
프라임스캔(Dentsply Sirona CEREC)은 임상 및 in vitro 연구 전반에서 전악·단일치 스캔 정확도 최상위권을 유지한다. 능동 삼각법(active triangulation) 방식은 속도·저조도 견고성에서 유리하나, 구개측 오목면과 좁고 깊은 준비에서 한계가 있다. 2026년 신규 근거는 포스트 준비 타당성과 조명 최적화 지침을 추가하여 일상 임상 결정에 직접 적용된다.

## Thesis
**Is Primescan the preferred IOS for general practice?**

For the majority of single- and multi-unit restorative scenarios, Primescan delivers top-tier trueness (< 50 µm single-unit, < 100 µm full-arch in vivo) and the fastest scan speeds. The 2026 literature confirms this advantage extends to novel indications (post spaces, various preparation designs) with scenario-specific caveats: anterior veneers with palatal reduction favor TRIOS-class confocal technology, and very deep post preparations (> 10 mm) challenge both IOS classes. For fully edentulous full-arch implant cases, neither IOS currently equals stereophotogrammetry.

## Clinical Decision Points

### 1. Standard single- and multi-unit crown/bridge (anterior + posterior)
- **Decision**: Primescan is a primary choice; comparable to MEDIT i900 (no significant difference, ender-2026)
- **Caveat**: Anterior veneer with palatal reduction — TRIOS confocal technology may outperform (sanioglu-2026, interaction p < 0.001)
- **Action**: For anterior veneers involving palatal margin, consider whether TRIOS access is available; otherwise verify fit carefully

### 2. Complete arch (full-arch impressions for fixed prosthetics)
- **Decision**: Primescan acceptable for dentate complete-arch (< 100 µm trueness in vivo, ender-2026; 42 µm marginal gap 2nd-best in virtual-fit 7-way, borbola-2024)
- **Caveat 1**: Error accumulates in posterior segments; conventional impression still superior in precision (21 µm, ender-2026)
- **Caveat 2 (new — edentulous spans)**: When edentulous spans are present, TRIOS 3 significantly outperforms Primescan (RMS 0.296 vs 0.338 mm; 9/12 scenarios, mayer-2025); Primescan accuracy declines more steeply with span length (R²=0.892)
- **Action**: For fully dentate complete-arch, Primescan clinically sufficient. For cases with ≥1 long edentulous span, prefer TRIOS-class confocal IOS or validate with try-in; EmeraldS and Mediti700 are emerging competitors now meeting fit criteria

### 3. Post-and-core (endodontically treated teeth)
- **Decision**: Primescan feasible for digital post impression (wildhagen-2026 µ-CT; almalki-2024 typodont — converging evidence)
- **Benchmarks**: 6 mm: 58 µm, 8 mm: 81 µm, 10 mm: 101 µm (almalki-2024); both studies agree accuracy declines with depth; almalki-2024 adds that 6 and 8 mm yield acceptable clinical accuracy vs PVS
- **Caveat**: 10 mm apical third reaches 163 µm — clinical fit risk; > 10 mm (up to 12 mm per wildhagen) shows further significant decline; no clinical threshold RCT yet
- **Action**: Target post preparation depth **≤ 8 mm** for Primescan IOS impression (supported by almalki-2024); at 10 mm use caution; at ≥ 10 mm prefer conventional PVS impression

### 4. Environmental setup
- **Decision**: Set operatory illuminance to 2500 lx at 4100 K (warm white) for Primescan (guo-2026)
- **Caveat**: TRIOS-based workflows require different setup (1000 lx/5500 K); do not apply Primescan lighting guideline to other scanners
- **Action**: If clinic has mixed IOS, standardize to Primescan's optimum (dental lamp at 2500 lx) unless TRIOS is primary scanner

### 5. Fully edentulous full-arch implant impressions
- **Evidence gap**: No Primescan-specific data in this scenario; vankos-2026 SR+MA shows IOS ≈ conventional (no significant difference in RMS) but accuracy still debated
- **Decision**: Both techniques clinically acceptable per current SR; stereophotogrammetry (SPG) superior in accuracy per pozzi-2025 NMA
- **Action**: For precision full-arch fixed implant prosthetics, SPG preferred; IOS acceptable for diagnostics or simpler cases

## Acquisition Technology: Why Active Triangulation Matters

Primescan uses **능동 삼각법 (active triangulation, AT)**: projects structured light onto the target and calculates depth from reflection geometry. TRIOS uses **공초점 스캐닝 (confocal scanning)**: measures depth by focus point.

Clinical implications:
- **AT advantage**: faster frame rate, better performance under ambient light, more robust to patient movement — consistent with schlenz-2022 (fastest scan) and guo-2026 (robust at 0 lx)
- **AT limitation**: structured-light geometry is disrupted by concave surfaces at steep angles — explains the sanioglu-2026 interaction (anterior palatal veneer) and wildhagen-2026 deep-channel finding

This mechanistic distinction allows prediction of when to prefer TRIOS: any preparation with steep-walled concavities inaccessible to the light pattern (deep palatal box, deep post channel) favors confocal scanning.

## Gaps and Future Research
1. **Clinical acceptance threshold for post space**: almalki-2024 provides RMS benchmarks (58–101 µm) and identifies 8 mm as the practical upper limit vs PVS; wildhagen-2026 extends to 12 mm with µ-CT validation. Neither defines prosthetic fit failure threshold — clinical RCT needed
2. **Edentulous span threshold**: mayer-2025 establishes Primescan's greater span-length sensitivity (R²=0.892) but does not define the specific span length at which Primescan becomes clinically unacceptable vs TRIOS 3 — a threshold RCT is needed
3. **EmeraldS and Mediti700 independent validation**: borbola-2024 is the first study to show these newer scanners match Primescan/Trios5; independent multi-center replication needed before clinical preference shift
4. **Primescan 2 expanded evidence**: ender-2026 is the only study to date — further direct comparisons in complex scenarios (anterior palatal veneers, post spaces, edentulous spans) needed
5. **Longitudinal fit outcomes**: No study links IOS trueness values to crown marginal gap, prosthetic survival, or patient outcomes — the accuracy literature assumes 100 µm threshold without clinical fit validation
6. **Mixed lighting field validation**: guo-2026 tested only at manufacturer-recommended color temperatures — cross-temperature testing (Primescan under 5500 K, TRIOS under 4100 K) not evaluated
