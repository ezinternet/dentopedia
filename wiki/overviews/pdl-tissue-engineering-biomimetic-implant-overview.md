## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 30 years (1989–2025) of in vivo attempts to engineer functional periodontal ligament (PDL) around dental implants — drawing on a scoping review (Mathew 2020, n=13 studies), a ligaplant narrative review (Bajaj 2023, n=328 relevant articles), and a comprehensive PDLSC mechanobiology review (Zhao 2025) — to map the current state and translation barriers of biomimetic implants.

The evidence arc progresses from earliest models using retained tooth roots through exogenous scaffolds (PLG, ePTFE, Dacron) and cultured PDL cells to the current frontier of bio-hybrid implants using embryonic dental follicle stem cells, which reproducibly form Sharpey's-fiber-inserting PDL, enable orthodontic-force-mediated bone remodeling, and support nerve innervation with measurable nociception in animal models; the sole human trial (Gault 2010 ligaplant) confirmed firm integration without bone contact and tooth-equivalent mobility — but complete functional sensory recovery has not been demonstrated in any model and autologous cell sourcing remains the unresolved prerequisite for clinical translation.

PDLSC biology (Zhao 2025) reveals the cellular mechanism that makes this feasible: PDLSCs at the compression side drive osteoclastogenesis via M-CSF/RANKL while tension-side PDLSCs drive bone formation via BMP pathways, with TRPV4 and Piezo1 as mechanosensors and exosomal miR-155-5p/PGE2 as immune modulators — establishing PDLSCs as the central orchestrators of the biological processes biomimetic implants must replicate.

## 세줄요약

PDL 주위 조직공학 30년(1989–2025)을 3편(Mathew 2020 스코핑 고찰·Bajaj 2023 리가플란트 서술 고찰·Zhao 2025 PDLSCs 기계생물학 리뷰)으로 종합 — 잔존 치근 → 외래 스캐폴드 → 배양 PDL 세포 → 배아성 치낭 줄기세포 기반 바이오하이브리드 임플란트의 4세대 진화를 추적.

동물 모델에서 Sharpey 섬유 삽입·교정력 매개 골 리모델링·신경분포·통증 감지가 재현되었고, 유일한 인체 시험(Gault 2010)은 직접 골 접촉 없이 견고 통합과 자연치 동등 이동성을 확인했으나 완전한 감각 회복은 아직 미입증; 자가세포 확보·배양 복잡성·숙주 반응 예측 불가·고비용이 이식 장벽이다.

PDLSCs는 압박측 M-CSF/RANKL(파골세포 유도)과 장력측 BMP(조골세포 유도)를 기계수용 채널 TRPV4·Piezo1로 연결하고 엑소좀으로 면역을 조절하는 중심 조절자(Zhao 2025) — 이 세포 오케스트라를 복제하는 것이 임상 이식의 핵심 과제다.

## Thesis

Osseointegrated implants are the gold standard of tooth replacement — predictable survival exceeding 95% at 10 years, load-bearing capacity, and bone-level stability. What they cannot reproduce is the periodontal ligament: a 0.15–0.38 mm fibrous tissue layer that transforms a tooth from a rigid post into a sensate, shock-absorbing, biologically adaptive structure. This synthesis examines whether 30 years of tissue-engineering research has closed that gap — and what it will take to do so.

The core deficits of osseointegrated implants are three and biologically precise:
1. **Shock absorption**: natural teeth deflect 25–100 μm under mastication; osseointegrated implants are rigid to bone, transmitting full impact force to the crestal cortex.
2. **Proprioception**: the PDL carries mechanoreceptors (Ruffini endings, free nerve endings) sensitive to loads as small as 100 μN, enabling jaw position sense and chewing refinement; implants have no equivalent sensor.
3. **Orthodontic tooth movement**: OTM depends on PDL cell-mediated bone remodeling at the pressure and tension sides; osseointegrated implants are ankylosed and will not move under orthodontic force.

These deficits are not cosmetic. They translate into altered chewing patterns, higher impact forces on opposing natural teeth, and the categorical inability to incorporate implants into orthodontic arches. Biomimetic implants attempt to address all three by engineering a functional PDL layer around the titanium surface.

## The Four-Generation Taxonomy (Mathew 2020)

Mathew 2020 (scoping review, MEDLINE 1989–2018, n=1,395 screened → 12 animal studies + 1 human trial) provides the most structured evidence map of in vivo attempts. Four generations are discernible by PDL source:

| Generation | PDL Source | Representative Approach | Key Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Retained/adjacent natural roots | Tooth root preserved adjacent to implant to maintain PDL blood supply | Requires sacrifice of viable root structure; patient-specific |
| 2nd | Exogenous scaffolds | PLG, ePTFE, Dacron membranes around HA-coated titanium | Fibrous tissue forms but poorly organized; no true Sharpey fibers |
| 3rd | Cultured PDL cells from extracted teeth | PDL cell suspension seeded onto implant surface | Cellular organization inconsistent; allogenic source needed |
| 4th | Bio-hybrid implants (stem cells) | ED18.5 dental follicle (DF) cells + HA-coated titanium | Most reproducible PDL formation, bone remodeling, and nociception |

**4th-generation outcomes (Oshima 2014, Nakajima 2016, cited in Mathew 2020):**
- Organized PDL fiber formation with perpendicular Sharpey-fiber insertion into cementum-like layer
- Orthodontic-force-mediated osteoclast + osteoblast activity confirming functional bone remodeling
- Antineurofilament-immunoreactive nerve fiber ingrowth into newly formed PDL
- C-Fos-immunoreactive neuron response at 2 h post-orthodontic force (nociceptive confirmation)

**Critical gap:** No model has demonstrated complete functional sensory recovery with absolute sensory perception. Nerve fiber presence ≠ functional proprioception.

## The Ligaplant Clinical Pipeline (Bajaj 2023)

Bajaj 2023 (narrative review, PubMed/Google Scholar/CENTRAL to Oct 2022, n=328 relevant articles reviewed) describes the engineering pipeline that has reached human application:

1. PDL cell isolation from mid-root of freshly extracted teeth
2. Culture on temperature-responsive dishes (≥37°C proliferating; <32°C detachment without enzyme)
3. Seeding onto HA-coated titanium pins in a bioreactor
4. Transplantation into extraction socket

The resulting implant achieves **firm integration without direct bone contact** — maintaining a PDL-like fibrous interface analogous to the natural tooth suspension system. The advantages documented in animal models include: micro-movements consistent with shock absorption, proprioceptive nerve supply, support for bone remodeling under applied loads, and elimination of bone graft requirements in some models.

**Gault 2010 human trial** (sole human data point, cited in both Mathew 2020 and Bajaj 2023):
- HA-coated titanium ligaplant placed in extraction socket
- Follow-up: firm integration without direct bone contact confirmed
- Mobility scores: equivalent to natural teeth
- Limitations: basic mobility endpoint only; no sensory testing; single case; no histologic PDL confirmation in humans

**Translation barriers (Bajaj 2023):**
- Culture requirements are complex — temperature and timing sensitivity, risk of non-PDL cell contamination from pulp/gingival cells
- Autologous cells require a freshly extracted tooth as the source — not available in all clinical scenarios
- Host acceptance is unpredictable
- Cost is substantially higher than conventional implant placement

## PDLSC Biology: The Cellular Engine (Zhao 2025)

Zhao 2025 (Stem Cell Research & Therapy, Peking University) reveals how PDLSCs transduce mechanical and inflammatory signals into the tissue-remodeling events biomimetic implants must replicate. This is the cellular biology underlying why 4th-generation bio-hybrid implants work better — and what "functional PDL" actually requires.

### Mechanical Force Response (OTM Model)

| Force Side | Cell Event | Key Mediators | Tissue Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compression | M1 macrophage polarization, osteoclastogenesis | M-CSF, RANKL, TNF-α, IL-1β | Bone resorption → tooth displacement |
| Tension | Osteoblast differentiation, matrix synthesis | BMP, OIF, COL-I, OCN, ALP | New bone formation |
| Both | Sterile inflammation (initiation) | PGE2, IL-6, MMPs | Remodeling cascade entry |

PDLSC proliferation peaks at days 3–7 post-force (CD146⁺Ki67⁺); apoptosis increases under heavy force (Bax↑, BCL-2↓, caspase-3). Light continuous force is the biological optimum — heavy force shifts PDLSCs from remodeling toward destruction.

### Mechanotransduction Channels

| Mechanosensor | Mechanism | Relevance to Biomimetic Implants |
|---|---|---|
| TRPV4 | Ca²⁺ influx under compressive load | Modulates stemness and osteogenic fate; critical for OTM signal transduction |
| Piezo1 | Stretch-activated ion channel | Cytoskeletal reorganization; force propagation |
| TRPC6 | Ca²⁺ channel, activated by mechanical stretch | Inflammatory signaling amplification |
| Integrins (αvβ3, αvβ5) | ECM-cytoskeleton force coupling | Sharpey fiber anchoring; scaffold attachment for bio-hybrid implants |

These channels explain why scaffold surface properties matter: HA coating (vs uncoated titanium) produces better-organized fibrous tissue with perpendicularly oriented collagen (Mathew 2020) — HA surface chemistry promotes integrin-mediated cell attachment and correct fiber orientation.

### Immunomodulation

PDLSCs do not just remodel bone — they actively regulate the inflammatory microenvironment:
- PGE2: suppresses T cell proliferation, promotes M2 macrophage polarization
- Exosomal **miR-155-5p**: regulates macrophage function; therapeutic exosome delivery proposed
- IL-10 pathway: anti-inflammatory; modulates macrophage polarization at OTM sites

In the context of biomimetic implants: any engineered PDL placed into an extraction socket enters an inflammatory microenvironment. PDLSCs' immunomodulatory capacity may be as critical as their mechanical-force-sensing capacity for achieving stable integration without excessive inflammation-driven tissue destruction.

## Functional Benchmarks for Clinical Translation

To claim a "functional" biomimetic implant, three independent endpoints must be met (none has been fully satisfied in humans):

1. **Shock absorption**: quantified micro-movement under standardized masticatory force (measured PDL mobility ≥ 25 μm)
2. **Proprioception**: measurable force-detection threshold (≤ 1 g two-point discrimination) via tactile-acuity testing or neurophysiologic recording from implant-adjacent nerves
3. **OTM capability**: radiographic bone remodeling with measurable implant displacement (≥ 1 mm over 3 months) under standardized orthodontic force in a controlled trial

Gault 2010 documented benchmark 1 (mobility equivalent to natural teeth) only. Benchmarks 2 and 3 remain undemonstrated in humans.

## Translation Gap Summary

| Barrier | Current Status | Required Breakthrough |
|---|---|---|
| Cell source | Autologous only (extracted tooth PDL) | iPSC-derived PDLSCs or cryopreserved allogeneic banks |
| Culture scalability | Manual bioreactor; 2–4 week timeline | GMP-compatible closed-system manufacturing |
| Host acceptance | Unpredictable; immunomodulation proposed but untested clinically | Phase I/II safety + engraftment data |
| Scaffold design | HA-coated titanium is current best; fiber orientation inconsistent | Biomimetic nanostructured surfaces or decellularized ECM |
| Regulatory pathway | No established precedent for cell-seeded dental implant | Classification and clinical trial framework needed |
| Sensory recovery | Not demonstrated | Functional nerve regeneration protocol |

## Therapeutic Frontier (Zhao 2025)

- **Exosome-based delivery**: PDLSC-derived exosomes (miR-155-5p, IL-10 pathway) may promote periodontal regeneration without cell transplantation — lower regulatory burden, shelf-stable
- **miRNA therapeutics**: local delivery of miR-155-5p inhibitors or BMP-pathway agonists to guide PDLSC osteogenic vs adipogenic fate
- **TRPV4/Piezo1 agonists**: pharmacological mechanosensor activation to promote PDL-like tissue organization in scaffold models

These approaches may eventually provide a "scaffold + drug" alternative to whole-cell transplantation — bypassing the culture complexity and autologous sourcing barriers.

## Clinical Implications (Current Practice)

Ligaplants/biomimetic implants are **experimental** — not available outside research settings. The clinical value of this literature for current practice is:

1. **Benchmarking osseointegration**: knowing precisely what conventional implants lack (shock absorption, proprioception, OTM) enables honest patient communication — "this implant functions like a tooth but does not feel like a tooth."
2. **Patient selection for implant vs other options**: in young orthodontic patients where OTM is anticipated, implant placement before orthodontic completion remains problematic precisely because of ankylosed bone integration.
3. **Planning around proprioception deficits**: implant patients may overload opposing teeth due to reduced tactile feedback — occlusal design and parafunctional risk assessment are more critical, not less.

## Open Questions

- When will the second human ligaplant trial be reported? (Gault 2010 remains the only human case after 16+ years)
- Can iPSC-derived PDLSCs replace autologous tooth-derived cells?
- Is exosome delivery sufficient to induce functional PDL regeneration without live cell transplantation?
- What is the minimum scaffold geometry + surface chemistry to induce Sharpey-fiber insertion reproducibly?
- Can functional proprioception (not just nerve fiber presence) be demonstrated in a large-animal model?
