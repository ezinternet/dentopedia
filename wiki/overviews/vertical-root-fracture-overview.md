## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **정의·위치**: 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)은 유럽근관치료학회 (European Society of Endodontology, ESE) 입장문(Patel 2025)이 정한 종축 균열·파절 스펙트럼의 말단 — 비근관충전치 VRF (VRF in Non-Root-Filled Teeth, VRF-NRFT)와 근관충전치 VRF (VRF in Root-Filled Teeth, VRF-RFT)를 **역학·병인·관리가 다른 별개 실체**로 분리한다. 단근치는 발치, 다근치는 치근절제 (Root Resection)·절단이 표준 분기.
> - **역학 — 분모가 다르면 숫자도 다르다**: 환자 단위 유병률은 대학 1.80%·개원 2.62%·합산 2.01% (Ek 2023)로 낮지만, 근관충전치 단위로는 4–32% (Patel 2022/2025)·2–25% (Liao 2021), 근관충전치 발치 원인의 13–21% (Patel 2025). 수술 의뢰된 실패치 코호트에선 18.7% (Hsiao 2020) — 선택편향된 분모. 서로 비교 불가.
> - **"VRF 유행"은 과장**: COVID-19 기간 합산 유병률 1.72%→3.82% 상승분의 대부분은 **의심 (suspected)** 사례 (0.99%→2.31%)이고 확진은 0.65%→1.01% (Ek 2023) — 발치 권고 전 **육안 확인**이 필요.
> - **위험인자**: 50세 초과 (보정 OR 3.20)·대구치 (OR 4.31)·소구치 (OR 2.61) (Hsiao 2020); 재근관치료 (Retreatment) 1–5년 OR 12.19 (García-Guerrero 2021)·2회 이상 OR 최대 87.9 (Lee 2026); 근단부 상아질 과삭제 (Excessive Apical Dentine Loss) OR 12.9 (Lee 2026, 대구치) — 반면 치경부 주위 상아질 (Pericervical Dentine) 손실은 보정 후 비유의.
> - **포스트 (Post)는 합의 불가 지점**: 포스트가 보호 (OR 0.22/0.26, García-Guerrero), 무관 (Hsiao p=0.630; Lee 대구치 OR 1.19), 금속 포스트는 실패 시 치근파절 양상 (Van Landuyt 2025 15년 RCT) — 층화(치종·시점·결과지표)가 달라 억지 합의 금지.
> - **임상·방사선 징후**: 고립된 좁고 깊은 치주낭 64–93%, 치관측 누공 (Sinus Tract) 35–42%, 방사선 병소 없음 13–14% (Liao 2021); 콘빔CT (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT)상 **J형 골결손·피질골 소실·탐침 >6 mm = VRF**, 각형 결손·탐침 <6 mm = 균열치 (Alaugaily 2022).
> - **영상 정확도 사다리**: 치근단방사선 (Periapical Radiography, PA) 민감도 0.518 vs CBCT 0.708, 특이도 차이 없음 (Shokri 2024 SR+MA, 대부분 in vitro); 근관충전치 CBCT 민감도 0.758 (비충전 0.911); 불완전 파절 0.522·금속 포스트 0.560 (de Lima 2025, **GRADE very low**); in vivo 민감도 58.5% (Habibzadeh 2023). 한국 영상지침 (Kim 2024): **PA 우선 (Grade A), 불충분 시 소촬영범위 (small Field of View, FOV) CBCT (Grade B)**. Baageel 2016의 "압도적 근거"는 부분 대체됨.
> - **CBCT 음성 이후**: 치주내시경 (Periodontal Endoscopy) 민감도 84.3%·특이도 100%·음성예측도 79.25% (Yang 2025) — 양성은 거의 확진, 음성은 배제 불가. 자기공명영상 (MRI)·광자계수 CT (Photon-Counting CT, PCCT)·광간섭단층촬영 (Optical Coherence Tomography, OCT)은 전부 ex vivo/in vitro 연구 단계.
> - **인공지능 (Artificial Intelligence, AI)**: CBCT 기반 AI 정확도 91.4–97.8% (Alshahrani 2026 SR, GRADE moderate)이나 그 SR이 Hu 2022·Yang 2023·Mun 2024를 포함 → **독립 근거 아님 (순환)**. 파노라마 AI는 민감도 90%대·특이도 52.63–60.77%로 CBCT 의뢰용 선별 (triage) 수준.
> - **처치**: 발치가 가장 예측 가능 (Patel 2022); 개원 설문에서도 CBCT 후 발치 59.6% (Scardini 2025). 자연치 보존은 **증례 시리즈 수준** — 4-META/MMA-TBB 접착 + 의도적 재식립 (Intentional Replantation) 신호가 최량 (Okaguchi 6/6; Hayashi 12개월 88.5%→60개월 59.3%), 레진-아이오노머·은-글라스아이오노머 (Glass Ionomer Cement, GIC)는 전부 실패 (Corbella 2025 SR). Salihu 2024의 "유망" 평가는 Corbella로 부분 대체.
> - **감별**: 시멘트질 열개 (Cemental Tear)는 VRF로 오진되기 쉬우나 완전 제거 시 1년 기능 보존 94% (Liang 2025) — 잘못된 VRF 진단은 보존 가능한 치아의 발치로 이어진다.
> - **임상 takeaway**: 고위험 프로필(고령·대구치/소구치·재근관치료력·근단 과삭제) + 고립 깊은 치주낭이면 의심 → 다각도 PA → 소FOV CBCT로 골결손 패턴 판독 → 음성이면 내시경/탐색 수술로 **육안 확인 후** 발치 결정. 예방은 근단 삭제 최소화·재치료 최소화·조기 교두피개.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 37 held papers on vertical root fracture (VRF): the ESE position statement (Patel 2025) places VRF at the terminal end of the longitudinal crack spectrum and splits it into VRF-NRFT and VRF-RFT; prevalence figures are not interchangeable because denominators differ — 2.01% per patient (Ek 2023) versus 4–32% per root-filled tooth (Patel 2022/2025) and 2–25% (Liao 2021), with an 18.7% per-tooth rate in surgery-referred failing teeth (Hsiao 2020).

Risk concentrates in older patients (>50 y, aOR 3.20), molars/premolars, retreated teeth (OR 12.19 at 1–5 y; up to 87.9 for ≥2 reRCTs) and excessive apical — not pericervical — dentine loss in molars (aOR 12.9), while post findings conflict across strata; diagnosis climbs a ladder from multi-angle PA (pooled sensitivity 0.518) to small-FOV CBCT (0.708; 0.522 for incomplete fractures, 0.560 with metal posts, GRADE very low) to periodontal endoscopy or surgical exploration, with MRI, PCCT, OCT and AI still ex vivo or small-sample.

Extraction remains the most predictable treatment and root resection is the multi-rooted alternative; tooth-sparing repair (4-META/MMA-TBB bonding with intentional replantation) is case-series evidence only (Corbella 2025 SR), so the chairside priorities are visual confirmation before extraction, ruling out look-alikes such as cemental tear, and prevention through conservative apical preparation, fewer retreatments and prompt cuspal coverage.

## 세줄요약

보유 논문 37편 종합: ESE 입장문(Patel 2025)은 수직치근파절 (VRF)을 종축 균열 스펙트럼의 말단에 두고 비근관충전치/근관충전치 VRF로 분리; 유병률은 분모가 달라 서로 비교 불가 — 환자 단위 2.01% (Ek 2023) vs 근관충전치 단위 4–32% (Patel 2022/2025)·2–25% (Liao 2021), 수술 의뢰 실패치 18.7% (Hsiao 2020).

위험은 50세 초과 (aOR 3.20)·대구치/소구치·재근관치료 (1–5년 OR 12.19; 2회 이상 최대 87.9)·대구치의 근단부(치경부 아님) 상아질 과삭제 (aOR 12.9)에 집중되고 포스트 결과는 층화별로 상충; 진단은 다각도 치근단방사선 (민감도 0.518) → 소FOV CBCT (0.708; 불완전 파절 0.522, 금속 포스트 0.560, GRADE very low) → 치주내시경·탐색 수술의 사다리이며, MRI·PCCT·OCT·AI는 아직 ex vivo 또는 소표본.

발치가 가장 예측 가능하고 다근치는 치근절제가 대안; 자연치 보존 (4-META/MMA-TBB 접착 + 의도적 재식립)은 증례 시리즈 근거뿐 (Corbella 2025 SR) — 체어사이드 우선순위는 발치 전 육안 확인, 시멘트질 열개 등 유사질환 감별, 그리고 근단 보존적 형성·재치료 최소화·조기 교두피개를 통한 예방.

This page synthesizes every held paper whose primary subject is vertical root fracture: definition and classification, epidemiology, risk factors and prevention (endodontic, restorative, occlusal), clinical and radiographic signs, the imaging-accuracy ladder (PA, CBCT, PCCT, MRI, periodontal endoscopy, OCT), AI detection, and management (extraction, root resection, bonded repair with intentional replantation). Evidence character: almost no RCTs. The base is one society consensus, narrative reviews, a handful of retrospective cohort/case-control studies, diagnostic SR+MAs dominated by ex vivo teeth with induced fractures, bench studies and case series. ## 1. Definition and place in the longitudinal-fracture spectrum

[[cracked-tooth/patel-2025-position-statement-longitudinal-cracks-fractures]] (ESE consensus, IEJ 2025) standardizes the spectrum: craze line → cracked tooth → fractured cusp → split tooth → VRF → split root, and recommends abandoning "cracked tooth syndrome". Two features matter for VRF specifically:

- **VRF-NRFT and VRF-RFT are separate entities.** VRF-NRFT: male, >50 y, first molars (maxillary MB root, mandibular mesial root), mostly reported in Chinese populations and linked to certain Asian dietary patterns; incidence quoted as 6–37%. VRF-RFT: extraction prevalence 4–32%, causing 13–21% of root-filled-tooth extractions, with a cyclical-fatigue pathogenesis (initiation → propagation → separation).
- **Management is keyed to root number.** Single-rooted → extraction; multi-rooted → root resection/amputation (no VRF-specific survival data; extrapolated root-resection survival 90.6% at 10 y, 96.8% at 15 y). [확인]

[[cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]] (narrative review, King's College London) supplies the working classification behind this: incomplete VRF (one side of the root) vs complete VRF (proximal to opposite side), with split tooth reserved for visible separation. It frames fracture toughness as probably the dominant pathogenic factor and cites micro-CT/cadaver data (De-Deus 2019: no new microcracks in 178 cadaver teeth / 65,530 micro-CT images) showing canal preparation does not create new microcracks — sectioning studies are judged invalid. [확인]

[[cracked-tooth/liao-2021-vertical-root-fracture-non-endodontically-treated]] (narrative review, 106 articles) is the detailed source for the NRFT/RFT split: VRFNETT patients are older (mean 69 vs 56 y in one series), more often male and Chinese, with severely attrited first molars and links to betel-quid and hard-food chewing; VRFETT clusters in maxillary premolars and mesial roots of mandibular molars. Its treatment section is **partially superseded** by Corbella 2025; its epidemiology, signs and etiology sections remain current. [확인]

**Differential look-alike.** [[periodontics/liang-2025-cemental-tear-diagnosis-treatment-consensus]] describes cemental tear as a cementum detachment at the cemento-dentinal junction that is easily misdiagnosed as VRF; with complete fragment removal, 94% of teeth are functionally retained at 1 year. [[cracked-tooth/gao-2021-diagnosis-of-cracked-teeth-using]] adds lateral canals and external root resorption as CBCT look-alikes of root fracture. Synthesis: a wrong VRF label converts a retainable tooth into an extraction, so differential diagnosis is not optional. [미검증]

## 2. Epidemiology — the denominator problem

| Figure | Denominator | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2.01% combined (1.80% university, 2.62% private) | Patients seen in endodontic settings | Ek 2023 |
| 4–32% | Root-filled teeth (heterogeneous diagnosis) | Patel 2022; Patel 2025; Salihu 2024 |
| 2–25% (3.69–25% in several studies) | Endodontically treated teeth | Liao 2021 |
| 3.7–30.8% (cited) | After root canal treatment | Baageel 2016 |
| 13–21% | Root-filled-tooth **extractions** | Patel 2025 |
| 86–97% | Extracted VRF teeth that were root filled | Patel 2022 (Yoshino 93.6%, Sugaya 97%, Liao 86%) |
| 18.7% per tooth / 22.5% per patient | Symptomatic root-filled teeth referred for microsurgery | Hsiao 2020 |
| 72% of root fractures were vertical | Extraction-confirmed root fractures in root-filled teeth | Mizuhashi 2022 |

These numbers answer different questions and must not be pooled or compared head-to-head. [[cracked-tooth/ek-2023-prevalence-of-vertical-root-fractures]] itself notes that its per-patient rate is not comparable with the per-tooth 4–32% range. Hsiao's 18.7% sits inside the per-tooth range only because the sample is already filtered to failing teeth undergoing surgery. [확인]

**Time and the "epidemic" claim.** Patel 2025 reports rising CT/VRF incidence including a post-COVID-19 increase (without numbers). Ek 2023 supplies the numbers and qualifies them: combined prevalence rose 1.72% → 3.82% (p < 0.0001), but suspected cases drove it (0.99% → 2.31%) whereas confirmed-positive cases rose only 0.65% → 1.01% (p = 0.0052; private practice alone not significant, p = 0.0708). The authors conclude VRF is uncommon per patient and "not an epidemic", and that crown-originating cracks and split teeth were often mislabeled VRF. [확인]

**Timing after treatment.** Patel 2022: typically 2–5 years after RCT. [[cracked-tooth/garcaguerrero-2021-vertical-root-fractures-a-time]]: 53.3% of cases within 5 years, 28.8% at 6–10 y, 17.7% at ≥11 y. Salihu 2024 cites 80% detected within 2 years of first symptoms. [확인]

## 3. Risk factors and prevention

### 3a. Patient and tooth factors

[[cracked-tooth/hsiao-2020-analysis-of-clinical-associated-factors]] (359 microsurgery-confirmed root-filled teeth): age ≥51 (31.9% vs 12.6%; aOR 3.20, 1.81–5.64), molars (aOR 4.31) and premolars (aOR 2.61) vs incisors were independent; gender, location and RCT history were not. Fractured molar roots were almost always maxillary MB (15/16) and mandibular mesial (9/13). [[endodontics/shaping/lee-2026-residual-pericervical-apical-dentine-vertical]] (Tokyo molar case-control, 44 cases vs 92 controls) agrees on the mesial root of mandibular first molars (69.6%) and finds mandibular first/second molars aOR 19.2/18.9 vs maxillary first molar. [[cracked-tooth/mizuhashi-2022-diagnosis-of-vertical-root-fractures]] shows premolar predominance with a bucco-lingual bias (premolars 76.2% bucco-lingual), explained by thin mesio-distal dimension and oval canals prepared round. [확인]

### 3b. Endodontic treatment factors

| Factor | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Retreatment | OR 12.19 (1–5 y), OR 4.28 (6–10 y) | García-Guerrero 2021 |
| Repeated retreatment | aOR 9.89 (1 reRCT) to 87.9 (≥2 reRCTs) | Lee 2026 |
| Time since primary RCT ≥15 y | aOR 20.7 | Lee 2026 |
| Excessive apical dentine loss (RCF/root-width ratio >0.21) | aOR 12.9 (2.92–57.5); 61.4% of cases vs 10.9% of controls | Lee 2026 |
| Excessive pericervical dentine loss | Not significant after adjustment (81.8% vs 65.2%) | Lee 2026 |
| Minimal canal shaping (#40/.04 vs ISO #80) | No reduction in VRF or cracks ex vivo; +MIE/cement sealer below untreated controls (p=0.020) | Rathke 2024 |
| Intracanal calcium hydroxide | OR 8.55 only after >10 y | García-Guerrero 2021 |
| Obturation system / technique (bench NMA) | GP/ZOE weakest (SUCRA 12.3%); no technique differed significantly (single cone SUCRA 70.2%) | Li 2024 |
| Microcracks from preparation | None in 65,530 micro-CT images (cited) | Patel 2022 |

Reading across: two independent case-control designs (Colombia, Tokyo) converge on **retreatment history** as a strong VRF marker, while Hsiao's null for "RCT history" used a coarse, underpowered variable (non-surgical only vs non-surgical plus surgical) and is not a true contradiction. Lee refines the ESE "preserve pericervical dentine" advice: in molars, the apical end of the preparation carried the signal. [[endodontics/shaping/rathke-2024-ex-vivo-minimally-invasive-endodontic]] then cautions that minimal shaping per se did not buy fracture resistance in incisors ex vivo — so the defensible message is "avoid over-enlargement apically", not "smaller is always stronger". [미검증]

[[endodontics/obturation/li-2024-comparative-assessment-of-vertical-fracture]] (in vitro NMA, 48 studies, 2,724 teeth) ranks Resilon/Epiphany first (SUCRA 80.8%, off the market) and finds calcium-silicate sealers not statistically different from resin sealers; all filled roots stayed below untreated controls. Bench load-to-fracture only — the page's own interpretation is that obturation choice is at most a weak tie-breaker (avoid ZOE and heavy spreader pressure). [[cracked-tooth/luo-2026-vertical-root-fracture-in-endodontically]] (abstract-only narrative review) frames risk as treatment-related vs occlusal axes and states that occlusal evidence is far thinner because force-measurement methods are limited. [확인]

### 3c. Restorative factors — posts, ferrule, cuspal coverage, contacts

**Posts — findings differ by stratum; do not force a consensus.**

| Study (design, population) | Post finding |
|---|---|
| García-Guerrero 2021 (case-control, mixed teeth) | Protective: OR 0.22 (1–5 y), 0.26 (6–10 y) |
| Hsiao 2020 (cohort, surgery-referred) | Null: 17.7% vs 19.7%, p=0.630; screw posts 7/21 (33.3%), p=0.079 |
| Lee 2026 (case-control, molars only) | Null: OR 1.19, p=0.672 |
| Mizuhashi 2022 (fractured teeth only) | No difference among metal/composite/no core, p=0.558 (no at-risk denominator) |
| Van Landuyt 2025 (15-y RCT, 182 restorations) | Survival NS (fiber 68.5% vs metal 53.6%); metal failures more often by non-restorable root fracture |
| Oulghazi 2024 (scoping review, mostly FEA/bench) | High-modulus alloys transmit stress to dentin; cited RCT (Sarkis-Onofre) no root-fracture difference cast vs fiber |
| Patel 2022 / Patel 2025 / Liao 2021 (reviews, consensus) | Advise passive/adhesive posts; Liao lists excessive post-space preparation as iatrogenic |

These do not measure the same thing: incidence among all root-filled teeth (García-Guerrero), prevalence among failing teeth (Hsiao), molar-only association (Lee), distribution among already-fractured teeth (Mizuhashi), and failure **mode** in a randomized trial (Van Landuyt). A protective association in a case-control sample may reflect indication (posts placed in teeth judged restorable). The consistent element is the failure-mode signal — rigid metal posts that fail tend to fail by root fracture — not an incidence signal. [미검증]

**Ferrule and post geometry.** [[post-and-core/oulghazi-2024-factors-influencing-vertical-radicular-fractures]]: a 2 mm ferrule significantly raises fracture resistance (FEA) and can neutralize post length/material effects; maxillary premolars and mandibular molars are most predisposed; "danger zones" are the mesial root of mandibular molars and buccal root of maxillary premolars. Hypothesis-grade (no quality appraisal). [확인]

**Cuspal coverage and contacts.** Patel 2022 cites 10-year survival 91.3% crowned vs 76% not (Boren 2015), ~3× extraction risk when the crown is placed >4 months after RCT (Pratt 2016), and 58% of extractions due to fracture in terminal teeth without proximal contacts vs 38% with contacts (Ng 2011). Hsiao 2020 found the opposite crude direction (cuspal coverage 20.8% vs direct 13.0%, crude OR 1.76, p=0.090, NS) — in a failing-tooth sample this most plausibly reflects confounding by indication (more destroyed teeth get crowns). [확인 (수치), 해석은 미검증]

### 3d. Prevention bundle (consensus level)

Patel 2025 lists: conservative endodontics preserving pericervical dentine, adhesive/passive post technique, avoid over-preparation, immediate composite access restoration, prompt cuspal coverage, maintain proximal contacts, bioactive/adhesive intracanal material beyond a pre-existing crack, and universal occlusal/parafunction management. Patel 2022 states openly that high-level evidence for these is lacking. With Lee 2026 added, the held evidence most specifically supports: minimize apical enlargement and avoid repeated retreatment in molars. [미검증]

## 5. Imaging accuracy ladder

### 5a. PA vs CBCT for the fracture line

| Evidence | Sensitivity | Specificity | Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[cracked-tooth/shokri-2024-is-cone-beam-computed-tomography]] SR+MA (23 studies, 16 pooled) | CBCT 0.708 vs PA 0.518 (P=0.011) | CBCT 0.841 vs PA 0.876 (P=0.46) | Mostly in vitro; I² 70–87%; Egger P<0.05 for 3 estimates |
| Shokri subgroup | CBCT root-filled 0.758 vs non-root-filled 0.911 | 0.774 vs 0.937 | Artifact penalty |
| [[cracked-tooth/de-2025-exploring-diagnostic-reliability-of-cbct]] SR+MA (100 in vitro studies, 46 pooled) | Complete 0.749 vs incomplete 0.522; empty canal 0.763 vs metal post 0.560 | 0.842 vs 0.786; 0.841 vs 0.719 | **GRADE very low** for all comparisons; fiberglass posts no penalty |
| de Lima, metal-post teeth | Voxel ≤0.16 mm 0.632 vs 0.482 | unchanged | No voxel benefit in empty/GP canals; MAR unreliable; small, centered FOV better |
| [[cracked-tooth/habibzadeh-2023-diagnostic-efficacy-of-cone-beam]] SR (20 studies, unpooled) | No post 71.50%; post 72.76%; **in vivo 58.5%**, ex vivo 66.4% | No post 75.64%; ex vivo 46.8% | 100% sensitivity only in vitro |
| [[cracked-tooth/mizuhashi-2022-diagnosis-of-vertical-root-fractures]] (36 in vivo VRFs) | CBCT 91.7% (B-L) / 100% (M-D); intraoral 25% / 0% | not estimable | Verification-biased, fractured teeth only, consensus read |
| [[cracked-tooth/alaugaily-2022-cbct-patterns-bone-loss-clinical]] | CBCT 4.4× odds vs PA of detecting bone defects | — | Secondary signs, not the line |

Reading the ladder: CBCT is more sensitive than PA, and its specificity is similar, but it still misses roughly 3 in 10 VRFs even in the pooled, mostly in vitro data, falls further in root-filled teeth, incomplete fractures and metal-post teeth, and the in vivo figure (58.5%) is lower again. Mizuhashi's 91.7–100% is not a contradiction of these numbers but a consequence of including only confirmed fractures. Patel 2022/2025 therefore position CBCT as a tool for the **periradicular bone-loss pattern**, not for proving the fracture line; Gao 2021 adds that some narrow VRF lines are invisible on CBCT while some wide crown cracks are visible, and names device-, patient- and evaluator-related modifiers. [확인 (수치), 종합은 미검증]

**Partial supersession.** [[cracked-tooth/baageel-2016-vertical-root-fracture-biological-effects]] (SR, vote count 12 CBCT-better / 5 no-difference / 5 non-comparative) called the evidence for CBCT "overwhelming"; Shokri 2024 pools the same comparison and confirms the direction but with quantified imperfection and publication bias, and Habibzadeh 2023 could not confirm optimal efficacy. Baageel's stepwise rule (clinical tests and 2D first, CBCT when inconclusive, before exploratory surgery) survives. [확인]

### 5b. Guideline sequence

[[cracked-tooth/kim-2024-developing-evidence-based-clinical-imaging]] (Korean Clinical Imaging Guidelines adaptation; 3 source guidelines, AGREE II rigor 90/33/31; 6-member Delphi): **Recommendation 1 — periapical radiography first (Grade A, level II, mean agreement 8.0)**; **Recommendation 2 — small-FOV CBCT only when intraoral radiographs are insufficient (Grade B, level II, mean 7.6)**. It cites CBCT sensitivity in filled teeth 72.76% and notes tooth-specific advice could not be given. (The held page notes that the pairing of Grade A with the PA recommendation and Grade B with the CBCT recommendation is inferred from the abstract and conclusion.) Shokri and Mizuhashi both recommend multiple PA angulations because a VRF line is visible only when the beam runs nearly parallel to it, and intraoral radiography detected none of the mesio-distal VRFs in Mizuhashi. [확인]

### 5c. Beyond CBCT

| Modality | Key result | Evidence stage |
|---|---|---|
| Photon-counting CT — [[cracked-tooth/leal-2026-photon-counting-ct-for-diagnosing]] | With Ni-Cr post, PCCT sensitivity 0.65 vs Accuitomo 0.22, Veraview 0.10; AUC 0.48–0.76; metal-post penalty is **device-dependent** | Ex vivo, 18 teeth (8 fractured); authors propose incidental read only |
| MRI 4.0 T — [[cracked-tooth/groenke-2023-sensitivity-and-specificity-of-mri]] | MRI 0.66/0.72 vs CBCT 0.58/0.87 (sens/spec), AUC 0.74 vs 0.75, no significant difference | Ex vivo roots, microCT reference, fair rater agreement |
| Periodontal endoscopy — [[cracked-tooth/yang-2025-role-of-periodontal-endoscopy-in]] | In CBCT-negative suspected VRF: sensitivity 84.3%, specificity/PPV 100%, NPV 79.25%, AUC 0.92; kappa 0.444 → 0.842 with endoscopy; avoided planned flap surgery in 36/56 | Prospective single-centre, 112 teeth, 62.5% prevalence; misses mainly premolars |
| OCT — [[endodontics/diagnosis/alkhani-2026-optical-coherence-tomography-detection-dental]] | Sensitivity 83–98%, specificity 63–100%; catheter TD-OCT 0.93/0.95 but needs canal prep to ISO 50–90; penetration 1–3 mm | Scoping review, 10 in vitro studies |
| Multimodal crack diagnosis — [[cracked-tooth/hernando-2026-diagnostic-methods-dental-cracks-systematic-review]] | OCT best single method; combinations beat single tools | SR, 12 studies (cracks broadly, abstract-level numbers unavailable) |

Of these, only periodontal endoscopy has in vivo diagnostic data in held evidence, and its positive result is near-confirmatory while a negative does not exclude VRF (11 of 70 VRFs missed). [확인]

### 5d. Real-world practice

[[cracked-tooth/scardini-2025-diagnosis-approaches-and-clinical-management]] (517 Brazilian dentists): 85.7% request CBCT for suspected VRF; median auxiliary exams 4 (endodontists) vs 3 (GPs) vs 2 (other specialists). [[cracked-tooth/gioti-2026-symptomatic-cracked-tooth-management-survey]] (427 European dentists, cracked teeth broadly): periodontal burden was the strongest extraction trigger and CBCT use did not independently change decisions. Practice is CBCT-heavy relative to the guideline's PA-first sequence. [확인]

## 7. Management

### 7a. Default: extraction or root resection

Patel 2022 and Patel 2025: extraction is the most predictable treatment; root resection/amputation for selected multi-rooted teeth. Salihu 2024 agrees on extraction for complete fractures, intraosseous fractures with periodontal pain and deep dehiscence. In practice, extraction was the most cited management after CBCT (59.6%) vs surgical exposure (17.2%) (Scardini 2025), with endodontists more likely to explore surgically first. [확인]

### 7b. Tooth-sparing repair — case-series level only

[[resin-bonding/corbella-2025-surgical-techniques-vertical-root-fractures-sr]] (first SR; 8 articles = 6 low-quality case series):

| Technique | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 4-META/MMA-TBB + intentional replantation (Okaguchi 2019) | 6/6 successful, 33–74 months |
| 4-META/MMA-TBB extraoral bonding + replant (Sugaya 2001) | 9/12 good prognosis; PD 7.4 → 4.6 mm |
| 4-META/MMA-TBB extraoral & intraoral (Hayashi 2002/2004) | 8/26 failed; worse in posterior teeth and fractures >2/3 from cervical |
| MTA seal + calcium sulfate (Taschieri) | 5/7 at 33 months; teeth with PD >4 mm excluded |
| Resin-ionomer (Anderegg 2018) | 0/5 — all failed within 6 months |
| 4-META + silver GIC + e-PTFE (Selden 1996) | All failed by 12 months |

Liao 2021 records the time course for the Hayashi 2004 series: survival 88.5% / 69.2% / 59.3% at 12 / 36 / 60 months. Corbella's conclusion: no technique can be recommended as standard; repair is feasible only for correctly diagnosed **incomplete** VRF; RCTs are needed. [[cracked-tooth/salihu-2024-review-of-approaches-to-the]] (narrative, "promising" framing; also covers CO2 laser fusion, hemisection, calcium hydroxide) is **partially superseded** by Corbella on this point; its treat-vs-extract criteria (incomplete fracture, intact periodontium, cooperative patient vs complete fracture, severe bone loss, chronic infection, medically compromised) remain usable. [확인]

Below the case series: [[cracked-tooth/sun-2024-a-novel-surgical-treatment-approach]] is a single premolar case (resin + iRoot BP Plus dual layer, retention forms, ~15 min extraoral, asymptomatic with ~3 mm probing at 24 months; first-layer material described inconsistently). Bench support for material choice: [[resin-bonding/li-2025-laboratory-study-on-reattachment-of]] — 4-META/MMA-TBB restored fracture resistance to 220.9 N (79.3% of sound roots), shortest dye penetration (0.63 mm), biocompatible like iRoot BP Plus, whereas Fuji IX GIC reached only 53.2 N and Clearfil SA Luting was most cytotoxic; [[resin-bonding/shimizu-2021-evaluation-of-restorative-techniques-for]] — a composite system with 10-3 pretreatment exceeded Super-Bond in microtensile bond strength (46.43 vs 39.36 MPa) in bovine models. Both are bench-only and cannot establish clinical success; they do align with the clinical failure of GIC-type repairs. [확인]

### 7c. Decision pathway (wiki synthesis)

```
Suspected VRF (isolated narrow deep pocket ± sinus tract, root-filled,
age >50, premolar/molar, retreatment history, 2–5 y after RCT)
        │
        ├─ Rule out look-alikes: cemental tear, lateral canal, ERR,
        │   endodontic failure / periodontitis, coronal crack mislabeled VRF
        │
        ▼
Multi-angle PA (Grade A first step)
        │ inconclusive
        ▼
Small-FOV CBCT (Grade B) — read bone-loss pattern:
  J-shaped defect, cortical loss, PD >6 mm → favours VRF
  angular defect, PD <6 mm → favours cracked tooth
  metal post: small centred FOV, voxel ≤0.16 mm; negative ≠ excluded
        │ still uncertain
        ▼
Periodontal endoscopy (positive ≈ confirms) or exploratory flap
with methylene blue + microscope (visual gold standard)
        │ VISUALLY CONFIRMED
        ▼
Single-rooted → extraction (default)
Multi-rooted, one root fractured → root resection/amputation
Incomplete VRF, limited periodontal breakdown, motivated + informed patient
  → 4-META/MMA-TBB bonding ± intentional replantation as an
    experimental option (case-series evidence; avoid GIC/resin-ionomer)
```

The sequence is assembled from Kim 2024, Patel 2025, Alaugaily 2022, de Lima 2025, Yang 2025, Liao 2021, Ek 2023, Liang 2025 and Corbella 2025; no single held paper validates the whole pathway. [미검증]

## 8. Controversies & Evidence Gaps

### Contradictions and tensions found

1. **Posts: protective vs null vs failure-mode harm.** García-Guerrero (protective, OR 0.22/0.26) vs Hsiao and Lee (null) vs Van Landuyt/Oulghazi (metal posts fail by root fracture) vs consensus caution (Patel, Liao). Different denominators, tooth strata and endpoints; unresolved. [확인]
2. **Pericervical vs apical dentine.** ESE consensus emphasizes pericervical preservation; Lee 2026 finds only apical loss significant in molars; Rathke 2024 finds minimal shaping gives no fracture protection ex vivo. Partial tension — tooth group, design and endpoint differ. [확인]
3. **Can CBCT see the fracture line?** Patel 2022/2025: not reliably. Mizuhashi 2022: 91.7–100% detection (verification-biased). Hu 2022 radiologist sensitivity 96.4% (selected visible fractures). Pooled/in vivo figures 0.52–0.71. The high figures come from samples that include only visible or confirmed fractures. [확인]
4. **Strength of CBCT superiority.** Baageel 2016 "overwhelming" vs Habibzadeh 2023 "could not confirm optimal efficacy" vs Shokri 2024 "more sensitive, still imperfect, publication bias". The later, pooled evidence governs. [확인]
5. **Fiber posts and CBCT artifact.** De Lima 2025: fiberglass posts no accuracy penalty; Habibzadeh 2023 cites one study with lower sensitivity under fiberglass (30–50%) than metal (70–80%). Single-study divergence. [확인]
6. **Calcium hydroxide.** García-Guerrero: OR 8.55 after >10 y (causation rests on cited in vitro work); Salihu cites 23–43.9% dentin weakening (Rosenberg 2006) against newer studies finding no increased fracture risk. [확인]
7. **"VRF epidemic".** Patel 2025 reports rising incidence including post-COVID-19; Ek 2023 shows the rise is mostly suspected cases and calls VRF not an epidemic. [확인]
8. **Cuspal coverage.** Patel 2022 cites strong protective survival data; Hsiao's crude association runs the other way (NS) in a failing-tooth sample. Likely confounding by indication, not a true reversal. [미검증]
9. **Sex.** Liao links VRFNETT to male patients; Hsiao finds gender non-significant in root-filled teeth; Yang 2025's discussion claim of female predominance is unsupported by its own Table 1 (p=0.367). [확인]
10. **AI evidence circularity.** Alshahrani 2026 includes Hu, Yang and Mun — not independent confirmation. [확인]

### Evidence gaps

- No RCT on any VRF prevention measure (cuspal coverage timing, post type for VRF incidence, apical preparation size) — Patel 2022 calls for them.
- Imaging accuracy is dominated by ex vivo teeth with induced, often wide fractures; GRADE very low (de Lima); in vivo data are scarce (Habibzadeh: 2 in vivo studies).
- No VRF-specific survival data for root resection (Patel 2025 extrapolates from general root-resection series).
- Tooth-sparing repair rests on 6 low-quality case series plus single cases and bench work; no comparative or long-term (>5 y) controlled data.
- Occlusal risk factors are under-measured (Luo 2026).
- Lee 2026's AUC 0.940 model and Yang 2025's endoscopy protocol are single-centre without external validation.
- No held paper on VRF prevalence or risk in a Korean population; no held paper on outcomes of implants placed after VRF extraction versus repair. [미검증]

## 9. Chairside takeaways

1. **Think VRF** in a root-filled premolar/molar of a patient over 50, especially after retreatment, 2–5 years post-RCT, with an isolated narrow deep pocket and/or a coronally placed sinus tract. [확인]
2. **Take multiple PA angulations first**; a single film misses about half of VRFs (pooled PA sensitivity 0.518) and all mesio-distal ones in Mizuhashi's series. [확인]
3. **Order small-FOV CBCT only if inconclusive, and read the bone, not the line**: J-shaped defect, lost cortical plate and probing >6 mm point to VRF. A negative CBCT does not exclude VRF, particularly with metal posts or incomplete fractures. [확인]
4. **Confirm visually before recommending extraction** (endoscopy or exploratory flap with methylene blue); suspected cases outnumber confirmed ones, and cemental tear or crown-originating cracks can masquerade as VRF. [확인]
5. **Default treatment is extraction (single-rooted) or root resection (multi-rooted).** Offer bonded repair/intentional replantation only for incomplete VRF in a motivated patient, framed as case-series evidence with declining survival over 5 years; never use GIC or resin-ionomer for the repair. [확인]
6. **Prevent at the endodontic and restorative stage**: avoid apical over-enlargement in molars, avoid unnecessary repeated retreatment, place cuspal coverage promptly (within about 4 months), keep proximal contacts and a ferrule, and manage parafunction. [미검증 (근거 수준이 consensus·observational)]
7. **Treat AI output as triage**: panoramic AI flags candidates for CBCT; it is not a diagnosis. [확인]
