> - **두 공간 분리**: 근관(무혈관, 밀봉 가능) vs PDL(혈관·신경·면역세포 풍부) — 근관을 완벽히 밀봉해도 PDL은 독립적으로 염증 상태를 유지하며 별도 해소 시간이 필요하다.
> - **기전 1 — 기존 염증 매개체 잔존**: 치수 괴사·치근단 치주염(Periapical Periodontitis)이 있던 치아는 PDL 공간에 IL-8(인터루킨-8)·Substance P(물질 P)·PGE₂(프로스타글란딘 E₂)가 이미 축적되어 있다 — 채워진 근관이 아니라 **혈류를 통한 청소**가 해소 경로다. IL-8과 Substance P는 치근단 삼출액에서 직접 측정됨 [확인 — Otaify 2026 RCT].
> - **기전 2 — 근단 밖 압출(Apical Extrusion)**: 시술 중 기구·세정액·debris가 근단공(Apical Foramen) 밖 PDL로 밀려나오는 것은 완전히 막을 수 없다; 이것이 술후 통증 강도를 가르는 가장 실질적인 시술 변수다 [확인 — 다중 RCT/SR 종합].
> - **기전 3 — PDL 통각수용체 민감화**: A-δ 섬유(날카로운 타진통)·C 섬유(둔한 자발통) 모두 염증 상태에서 역치(threshold)가 낮아져 정상 자극에도 통증 발생 — 타진통·교합통의 해부학적 기반 [미검증 — 훈련지식].
> - **통증 예측 최강 인자**: 술전 자발통(Spontaneous Pain, 비교위험도 Relative Risk RR = 5.83)·타진통(Percussion Pain, RR = 3.45) — PDL 염증 부하가 컸던 치아일수록 술후에도 더 아프다 [확인 — Ohshima 2026 SR, 24,320증례].
> - **해소 시간**: 모든 술식·기구에서 공통 — 통증 정점 6~24시간, 7~14일이면 최소 [확인 — Singh 2026·Usman Sultan 2026·Zarei 2026].
> - **임상 함의**: 술후 통증 = 치료 실패가 아니다; 술전 증상이 심할수록 선제 항염 계획(코르티코스테로이드, 적절한 진통제)이 시술 변수 최적화보다 더 큰 영향을 미친다.

## Three-line Summary

When root canal disinfection and obturation are correctly executed, residual post-operative pain originates in the periodontal ligament (PDL) and periapical tissues — not the canal itself — through three concurrent mechanisms: persistence of pre-existing inflammatory mediators (IL-8, Substance P directly measured in periapical exudate; Otaify 2026 RCT), unavoidable apical extrusion of irrigants/debris beyond the apical foramen during instrumentation (the single operative mechanistic thread linking all procedural pain axes; endodontic-postoperative-pain-overview synthesis), and sensitization of PDL nociceptors (A-δ and C fibers) whose pain threshold drops under the local inflammatory milieu.

Preoperative symptomatic status is the dominant predictor of post-treatment PDL pain intensity: spontaneous pain (RR 5.83) and percussion pain (RR 3.45) concentrate flare-up risk (Ohshima 2026, 24,320 cases); these reflect pre-existing PDL inflammatory load that persists after obturation because resolution depends on vascular clearance — a process that takes 7–14 days regardless of technique.

The clinical corollary is that successful obturation eliminates the infection source but does not acutely resolve periapical PDL inflammation; preemptive anti-inflammatory planning (corticosteroids, preemptive analgesia) for symptomatic teeth targets the actual pain substrate more effectively than optimizing any single procedural variable.

## 세줄요약

근관소독·충전이 정확히 됐을 때도 남는 술후 통증은 근관 내부가 아닌 **치주인대(PDL)·치근단 주위 조직**에서 기원한다 — 세 기전: ① 기존 염증 매개체 잔존(IL-8·Substance P 치근단 삼출액 직접 측정; Otaify 2026 RCT), ② 시술 중 불가피한 근단 밖 압출(Apical Extrusion)에 의한 PDL 자극(다중 RCT/SR 공통 기전), ③ 염증 상태에서 역치가 낮아진 PDL 통각수용체(A-δ·C 섬유) 민감화.

술전 증상 상태가 술후 PDL 통증 강도의 최강 예측인자다: 자발통(RR 5.83)·타진통(RR 3.45) — 이는 PDL 염증 부하가 컸음을 반영하며, 충전 후에도 혈관성 청소에 7~14일이 소요되므로 술전 상태가 심할수록 술후에도 더 아프다(Ohshima 2026, 24,320증례).

임상 함의: 충전 성공 = PDL 염증 즉시 해소가 아니다; 증상성 치아는 선제 항염 계획(코르티코스테로이드·진통제)이 시술 변수 최적화보다 통증 감소에 더 효과적이다.

## Summary

Root canal treatment operates on the canal space — removing infected pulp tissue, disinfecting with irrigants, and sealing with gutta-percha and sealer. When this is done correctly, the infection source is eliminated. Yet post-operative pain frequently follows, and its substrate is the **periodontal ligament (PDL) and periapical bone**, not the canal itself.

### Why PDL and canal are separate compartments

The canal is an avascular space after pulp removal: it can be disinfected and sealed. The PDL, by contrast, is a vascularized, innervated connective tissue anchoring the tooth to alveolar bone. It contains fibroblasts, osteoclasts, immune cells (macrophages, mast cells, lymphocytes), and a dense network of sensory nerve fibers. Inflammatory resolution in PDL is vascular — it depends on blood-borne clearance of mediators and cellular debris, a process that cannot be sealed away.

### Mechanism 1 — Persistence of pre-existing inflammatory mediators

In teeth with necrotic pulp and periapical periodontitis, the PDL space has accumulated inflammatory mediators before treatment even begins. **IL-8** (interleukin-8; neutrophil chemotactic factor) and **Substance P** (neuropeptide; direct pain signal) have been directly measured in periapical exudate in a clinical RCT comparing LLLT vs calcium hydroxide dressing — both mediators were detectable and both fell over 48 hours in both groups regardless of intervention [확인 — Otaify 2026]. This demonstrates that the periapical inflammatory state exists as a measurable, time-dependent entity that persists after canal sealing and resolves on its own timeline.

Other mediators implicated by general physiology include PGE₂ (prostaglandin E₂; sensitizes nociceptors), bradykinin (vasodilator, direct nociceptor activator), and histamine (mast-cell derived; vasodilation + nociceptor sensitization) [미검증 — 훈련지식; 본 위키에서 직접 측정된 데이터 없음].

### Mechanism 2 — Apical extrusion (Apical Extrusion)

Even technically correct canal instrumentation pushes small amounts of debris, irrigant, and bacteria beyond the apical foramen into the PDL space. This is the **single cross-cutting mechanistic thread** linking all procedural axes of post-endodontic pain:

- Hand files cause more early pain than NiTi rotary/reciprocating — implicated mechanism is greater apical extrusion [확인 — Singh 2026 SR, 5 RCTs]
- Manual dynamic irrigation (MDI) ranked worst (SUCRA 1.5%) among 7 activation methods — likely raises apical hydraulic pressure [확인 — Hatipoğlu 2025 Bayesian NMA, 31 studies, n=2,595]
- Side-vented needle (SNI) and passive ultrasonic irrigation (PUI) both outperformed open-ended conventional needle at 24–72h — lateral flow reduces apical pressure [확인 — Zarei 2026 RCT, necrotic mandibular molars]

Any procedural choice that reduces what is extruded beyond the foramen — accurate working length, avoiding MDI, avoiding hand files, side-vented delivery — moves pain in the reducing direction.

### Mechanism 3 — PDL nociceptor sensitization

PDL contains two classes of nociceptive fibers [미검증 — 훈련지식]:

| Fiber | Response | Pain character |
|---|---|---|
| A-δ (myelinated) | Mechanical stimulus, acute | Sharp percussion pain, bite pain |
| C (unmyelinated) | Chemical/thermal/mechanical | Dull aching, spontaneous pain |

Under inflammatory conditions, both fiber types undergo **peripheral sensitization**: their activation threshold falls, so stimuli normally below pain threshold (biting, percussion) now trigger pain. This is the mechanistic basis for post-treatment percussion tenderness and bite pain — the PDL is mechanically compressed by occlusal force, and sensitized nociceptors fire at lower load.

### Pain timeline — resolution across all techniques [확인]

Across all procedural axes (instrumentation kinematics, irrigation delivery, working-length method), the pain time-course is reproducible:

```
Pain intensity
    │
    │      ▲ peak (6–24 h)
    │     / \
    │    /   \
    │   /     \___________→ minimal (7–14 days)
    └──────────────────────
        24h   72h    7–14d
```

Technique modulates peak height, not curve shape. The curve reflects PDL inflammatory clearance kinetics.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **Reframe "pain after good treatment" for the patient.** Successful obturation eliminates the infection source; PDL inflammatory resolution is a separate, time-dependent biological process. Pain does not equal treatment failure — it reflects pre-existing PDL inflammatory load being cleared through the vasculature.

2. **Stratify by pre-treatment PDL load, not by technique.** Spontaneous pain (RR 5.83) and percussion pain (RR 3.45) are the best available proxies for how inflamed the PDL already is. A symptomatic tooth warrants a preemptive pharmacological plan targeting PDL inflammation directly — see [[overviews/endodontic-flare-up-management-overview]] for the full corticosteroid/analgesic protocol.

3. **Minimize apical extrusion as the procedural lever that most directly delivers material to PDL.** Prefer side-vented needle irrigation, avoid MDI and hand files, maintain accurate working length. These choices reduce what is introduced into the PDL space during treatment.

4. **Cover the 12-hour pain peak preemptively.** PDL nociceptor sensitization is maximal in the early post-treatment window; analgesic cover should be established before the peak, not initiated when the patient calls.

5. **Percussion tenderness resolving by 7–14 days = expected healing trajectory.** A-δ fiber sensitization in PDL is reversible as local inflammation clears; persistent percussion pain beyond 2–3 weeks warrants reassessment (canal missed, crack, vertical fracture, apical cyst requiring surgery).

## Bottom Line (한 줄 결론)

근관이 잘 됐는데도 아픈 이유는 통증의 무대가 **근관 밖 PDL**이기 때문 — 기존 염증 매개체 잔존 + 불가피한 근단 밖 압출 + PDL 통각수용체 민감화가 삼위일체로 작동하며, 이를 좌우하는 최강 변수는 시술 술식이 아니라 **치료 전 PDL 염증 부하(술전 자발통·타진통)**다.
