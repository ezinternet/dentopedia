## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 결론**: 치아침식마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW)는 전연령대 흔한 병태 — 성인 최대 80%(FDI 2024), 청소년 37.6%(Juarez-Lopez 2026), 유치열 35.6%(Marschner 2024 SR+MA 16,777명). 역학이 확립됐으므로 이제 임상 전략은 **위험인자 screening → 원인 제거 → 예방 → 복원** 순서.
> - **고위험군 유병률**: 섭식장애 (Eating Disorder) 65%, 위식도역류질환 (Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease, GERD) 54.1%, 특수 식이요법 65.9%, 산성음료 상습 40%, 직업적 노출 51%, 약물 30% (Dallavilla 2024 SR+MA 148편).
> - **성인 정량 위험인자** (Marschner 2024 아남네시스 SR+MA 87편): 산성식품 OR 2.40, 역류 OR 2.27, 소화기 장애 OR 1.81, 탄산음료 OR 1.43, 남성 OR 1.30.
> - **유치열 위험인자** (Marschner 2024 유병률 SR+MA 23편): 산성음료 OR 6.90, 산성식품 OR 5.14, GERD OR 1.98, 음료 입에 물기 OR 1.82; 빨대 보호 OR 0.58.
> - **청소년+탄산음료**: 탄산음료 매일 섭취 OR 1.98; 취침 전 섭취 OR 7.8(단일 연구) — 침 세정 소실이 야간 고위험 기제 (Juarez-Lopez 2026).
> - **GERD-침식 양방향**: GERD/인후두역류 (Laryngopharyngeal Reflux, LPR) 환자에서 ETW 유병률 증가, 역으로 ETW 환자에서 GERD 유병률 증가; 치과와 소화기과 양방향 스크리닝 필요 (Wang 2025).
> - **섭식장애 스크리닝**: 섭식장애 환자에서 ETW 유병률 유의 증가 + 타액분비율·pH 감소; 치과의사가 섭식장애 첫 발견자가 될 수 있음 (Chan 2024 SR+MA 33편).
> - **예방 — 칼슘 강화**: 칼슘 강화 산성음료(오렌지주스 등)의 법랑질 용해 2.6배 감소 (Chatzidimitriou 2024 in situ SR+MA 21편); 우유·카세인 인산 펩타이드-무정형 인산칼슘 (Casein Phosphopeptide-Amorphous Calcium Phosphate, CPP-ACP) 검은 결과 불일치.
> - **예방 — 스타나스 불소**: 기존 etiology overview (gomez-2026)에서 불소 제제 중 스타나스 불소 (Stannous Fluoride) 우위 확인됨. Ne 2022 SR(4편, in-vitro)에서 CPP-ACP +13.27% 미세경도 증가; 불소 결과는 비일관적.
> - **임상 프로토콜**: FDI 2024 정책 — 복원 전 원인 제거 우선; BEWE(Basic Erosive Wear Examination) 스코어 활용; 최소 침습 원칙.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 9 papers (5 SR+MA, 1 SR, 1 in situ SR+MA, 1 policy statement, 1 GERD-specific SR) on the epidemiology, risk factors, and prevention/management of erosive tooth wear (ETW): population prevalence spans 35.6% in primary dentition to a reported 80% in some adult cohorts, and risk-group prevalence is dramatically elevated — 65% in eating disorders, 54.1% in GERD, and 65.9% in patients on special diets — establishing ETW as a widespread condition requiring systematic risk stratification rather than opportunistic management.

Quantitative risk-factor evidence from paired Marschner 2024 meta-analyses identifies the major anamnestic risk signals for adult (OR 2.40 acidic food, OR 2.27 regurgitation, OR 1.81 digestive disorders, OR 1.43 carbonated drinks) and primary dentition (OR 6.90 acidic drinks, OR 5.14 acidic food, OR 1.98 GERD, OR 1.82 holding drinks) cohorts, with straw use protective at OR 0.58; GERD and LPR show a bidirectional association with ETW (Wang 2025) and eating disorders are detectably linked through lower salivary flow and pH (Chan 2024), making the dental appointment a unique screening opportunity.

Prevention evidence is modest in quality: calcium-enriched acidic beverages reduce enamel dissolution 2.6-fold compared to plain acidic drinks in in situ trials (Chatzidimitriou 2024), and CPP-ACP raises in vitro microhardness +13.27% (Ne 2022), but CPP-ACP in chewing gum is non-significant in in situ evidence and the overall calcium/CPP-ACP literature is inconsistent; stannous fluoride remains the best-supported topical agent (per companion etiology overview).

## Summary

This overview synthesizes the 9 papers in the `dental-erosion` folder that were not covered by the companion etiology/prevention-chemistry overview ([[dental-erosion-etiology-prevention-synthesis]]). That overview focused on the in vitro and in situ chemistry of erosion (kanzow-2016 erosive wear mechanisms, west-2014 saliva protection, barac-2015 in vitro, souza-2020 CPP-ACP, gomez-2026 prevention umbrella). The current overview addresses the population-level and clinical dimensions: who is affected, by how much, why, and what can be done.

### Axis 1 — Population prevalence

The 2024 FDI policy statement (Dallavilla and Marschner data quoted within) captures the scale: erosive tooth wear is common enough to warrant a dedicated policy statement, with prevalence approaching 80% in some adult surveys. The population-level evidence comes from two Marschner 2024 meta-analyses and one Juarez-Lopez 2026 SR+MA.

**Primary dentition (Marschner 2024 prevalence SR+MA, 23 studies, 16,777 children)**: pooled prevalence 35.6%. Significant predictors: acidic drinks OR 6.90 (95% CI 2.13-22.38), acidic food OR 5.14 (95% CI 1.99-13.25), GERD OR 1.98 (95% CI 1.40-2.79), holding drinks in mouth OR 1.82 (95% CI 1.07-3.09). Straw use protective: OR 0.58 (95% CI 0.39-0.86). These effect sizes indicate that acidic drink habits are the dominant modifiable factor in children; straw use is a simple, evidence-based protective behavior.

**Adolescents (Juarez-Lopez 2026 SR+MA, 24 studies, 21,541)**: pooled prevalence 37.6%. Daily carbonated drink consumption OR 1.98. Bedtime intake OR 7.8 in one high-quality study (salivary clearance loss during sleep). Study heterogeneity was high, and the bedtime OR is a single-study estimate, but the biological mechanism (reduced nocturnal salivary flow) is robust.

**Adults**: The paired Marschner 2024 anamnestic SR+MA (87 studies) provides the most comprehensive adult risk quantification to date. Significant anamnestic risk factors: acidic foods OR 2.40 (95% CI 1.83-3.14), regurgitation OR 2.27 (95% CI 1.86-2.77), digestive disorders OR 1.81 (95% CI 1.43-2.28), carbonated drinks OR 1.43 (95% CI 1.08-1.89), male sex OR 1.30 (95% CI 1.14-1.49). The OR hierarchy is consistent with the pediatric data — acid exposure is the dominant driver, with GERD/regurgitation as the most clinically important systemic pathway.

### Axis 2 — High-risk groups

Dallavilla's 2024 SR+MA (148 studies, 7 predefined risk groups) provides the clearest risk-group prevalence estimates:

| Risk group | ETW prevalence |
|---|---|
| Special diets | 65.9% |
| Eating disorders | 65% |
| GERD | 54.1% |
| Occupational acid exposure | 51% |
| Acidic beverages (habitual) | 40% |
| Medications (acidic) | 30% |

These figures define the priority screening order in a dental practice: a patient with an eating disorder or GERD is at roughly twice the population prevalence risk.

**Eating disorders**: Chan's 2024 SR+MA (33 studies) adds mechanistic evidence — patients with eating disorders show not only higher ETW prevalence but also significantly lower unstimulated salivary flow rate and lower salivary pH. The dual insult (regurgitated acid + depleted salivary buffering) explains the elevated prevalence. Importantly, the oral manifestations may be the presenting sign of an undiagnosed eating disorder: Chan notes that dental professionals are often the first healthcare contact where ETW patterns are recognized.

**GERD and LPR**: Wang's 2025 SR (22 studies, adults) establishes the bidirectional association. ETW prevalence is elevated in GERD/LPR patients AND GERD/LPR prevalence is elevated in ETW patients. Neither association is fully confounded by common dietary habits — the acid reflux pathway is independent. Clinical implication: dentists should ask about reflux; gastroenterologists should ask about ETW; each can detect the other's disease.

### Axis 3 — Prevention: calcium and CPP-ACP

The companion etiology overview (gomez-2026 umbrella) establishes stannous fluoride as the best-supported topical preventive agent, with no other product consistently outperforming it. The current papers address two additional preventive strategies.

**Calcium-enriched acidic beverages**: Chatzidimitriou's 2024 SR+MA (21 in situ RCTs) found calcium-enriched orange juice caused 2.6-fold less enamel surface loss than plain acidic equivalent. Calcium-enriched products effectively neutralize the proton-driven dissolution by saturating the oral fluid with calcium ions near the enamel surface. CPP-ACP chewing gum showed no statistically significant protection in in situ evidence. Results for milk and CPP-ACP paste were contradictory across studies.

**CPP-ACP in vitro**: Ne's 2022 SR (4 in vitro studies) found CPP-ACP treatment raised enamel microhardness +13.27% versus artificial saliva control. However, the 4-study base is small, and the translation to clinical ETW protection is not established — in vitro microhardness change does not directly predict clinical surface loss protection.

**Practical hierarchy**: (1) Eliminate/reduce acid exposure — address the underlying cause (dietary modification, reflux management, eating disorder referral); (2) Stannous fluoride toothpaste — highest-quality clinical evidence from the etiology overview; (3) Calcium-enriched variants of acidic beverages if the patient cannot avoid them; (4) CPP-ACP products — biologically plausible but in situ and clinical evidence inconsistent; discuss as adjunct, not primary prevention.

### Axis 4 — Clinical framework (FDI 2024)

The FDI 2024 policy statement formalizes the umbrella definition: tooth wear includes erosive tooth wear, attrition, abrasion, and abfraction — chemical and mechanical causes may coexist, and management must address all contributing factors. The statement endorses: (1) address etiology before restoring; (2) use validated indices (BEWE) for monitoring; (3) minimum-invasive restorative approach when intervention is required; (4) intercept early with preventive therapies.

The FDI statement's prevalence claim (up to 80% of adults in some populations) drives the urgency for integrating ETW screening into routine examination rather than treating it as a specialist referral concern.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **스크리닝 우선순위**: 모든 성인 환자 BEWE 기록. 섭식장애·GERD/LPR·직업적 산 노출·산성음료 일상 복용 이력은 **고위험** — 즉시 원인 파악.
2. **GERD/LPR 연동**: ETW 환자에서 역류 증상 확인 → 소화기과 의뢰. 역으로 GERD 처방 중인 환자는 구강 내 산 노출 유의.
3. **섭식장애 접근**: 구개측 침식 패턴(특히 전치부)은 self-induced vomiting 신호. 비심판적 진단 → 적절한 정신건강 의뢰. 타액 분비율 측정 권장.
4. **소아·청소년 예방 메시지**: 탄산음료 줄이기, 빨대 사용(OR 0.58 보호), 취침 전 산성음료 절대 금지. GERD 진단 아동은 불소 바니시 강화.
5. **칼슘 강화 음료 권고**: 산성음료(오렌지주스 등)를 완전히 중단 못 하는 환자에서 칼슘 강화 제품 선택 권고 — 법랑질 용해 2.6배 감소.
6. **복원 타이밍**: FDI 원칙 — 원인 제거 및 진행 안정화 확인 후 복원. 활성 진행 중 복원은 소모 가속.

## Gaps & Future Research

- 성인 코호트 RCT: 식이 개입(산성음료 감소, 빨대 도입) 실제 ETW 진행 억제 여부.
- CPP-ACP 임상 RCT: 현재 in vitro/in situ 증거를 실제 환자 ETW 감소 데이터로 번역 필요.
- GERD 치료(PPI) → ETW 진행 감소 여부: 약물 개입의 치과 outcomes.
- 섭식장애 조기 발견 프로토콜: 치과적 스크리닝 도구의 민감도·특이도.
