## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 결론**: 치아침식마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW)는 전연령대 흔한 병태 — 유치열 35.6%(Marschner 2024 SR+MA 16,777명), 청소년 37.6%(Juarez-Lopez 2026). 임상 전략은 **위험인자 선별 (screening) → 원인 제거 → 예방 → 진행 모니터링 → 복원** 순서.
> - **일반인구 치아마모 (Tooth Wear, TW) 40.8%** (Salari 2026 SR+MA, 단면연구 133편, 95% CI 36.7-45.0): 교모 (Attrition)·마모 (Abrasion)·치경부 쐐기결손 (Abfraction)까지 포함한 **전체 마모** 수치이지 ETW 전용이 아님. 이질성 (I²) 99.1%, 개별 연구 약 1-98% — 질병 부담의 대략적 추정치로만 사용.
> - **FDI "최대 80%" 재해석 (이전 판 교정)**: FDI 2024의 80%는 **치아마모 전체**에 대해 "지역에 따라" 보고된 **상한값**이지, 성인 ETW 유병률이나 통합 추정치가 아니다. 통합 평균(40.8%)과 개별 보고 상한(80%)은 서로 다른 통계량 — 어느 쪽도 ETW 35.6-37.6%와 직접 비교 불가.
> - **고위험군 유병률**: 섭식장애 (Eating Disorder) 65%, 위식도역류질환 (Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease, GERD) 54.1%, 특수 식이요법 65.9%, 산성음료 상습 40%, 직업적 노출 51%, 약물 30% (Dallavilla 2024 SR+MA 148편).
> - **성인 정량 위험인자** (Marschner 2024 아남네시스 SR+MA 87편): 산성식품 OR 2.40, 역류 OR 2.27, 소화기 장애 OR 1.81, 탄산음료 OR 1.43, 남성 OR 1.30.
> - **유치열 위험인자** (Marschner 2024 유병률 SR+MA 23편): 산성음료 OR 6.90, 산성식품 OR 5.14, GERD OR 1.98, 음료 입에 물기 OR 1.82; 빨대 보호 OR 0.58.
> - **청소년+탄산음료**: 탄산음료 매일 섭취 OR 1.98; 취침 전 섭취 OR 7.8(단일 연구) — 침 세정 소실이 야간 고위험 기제 (Juarez-Lopez 2026).
> - **GERD-침식 양방향**: GERD/인후두역류 (Laryngopharyngeal Reflux, LPR) 환자에서 ETW 유병률 증가, 역으로 ETW 환자에서 GERD 유병률 증가 (Wang 2025). 섭식장애는 ETW 증가 + 타액분비율·pH 감소 — 치과의사가 첫 발견자가 될 수 있음 (Chan 2024 SR+MA 33편).
> - **예방 — 칼슘·CPP-ACP**: 칼슘 강화 산성음료 법랑질 용해 2.6배 감소 (Chatzidimitriou 2024 in situ SR+MA 21편); 카세인 인산 펩타이드-무정형 인산칼슘 (Casein Phosphopeptide-Amorphous Calcium Phosphate, CPP-ACP) in vitro 미세경도 +13.27% (Ne 2022)이나 in situ 결과 불일치.
> - **예방 — 불소 제제 (단서 추가)**: 스타나스 불소 (Stannous Fluoride, SnF₂) 우위(gomez-2026 umbrella, 중등도 확실성)는 유지하되, Haleon 후원 in situ 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-analysis, NMA; Creeth 2024, 14편)에서는 표면미세경도 회복률 (Surface Microhardness Recovery, SMHR) 기준 SnF₂-아연이 NaF보다 8.8% 낮고 순보호 이득 없음 — **평가지표 차이**(표면소실 vs 재광화)이지 번복 아님. 확실한 것: 유리불소 ≥1000 ppm.
> - **진행 모니터링 (신규 축)**: Murchie 2026 SR(종단 코호트 5편) — 구강내 스캐너 (Intraoral Scanner, IOS)는 12개월에 10-150 µm 손실을 검출하는데 기초 침식마모 검사 (Basic Erosive Wear Examination, BEWE) 등 서열 지수는 변화 없음으로 나오는 경우가 많음; 일치도 약-중등도 (AUC 0.80); **검증된 진행 기준값 없음** (>30 µm ~ ≥200 µm 제각각). BEWE=선별, IOS=고위험군 선택적 보조.
> - **임상 프로토콜**: FDI 2024 — 원인 제거 후 복원, 최소 침습. 단 확보한 FDI 본문엔 특정 지수 권고가 없다 (이전 판의 "FDI가 BEWE 권고" 귀속은 교정). "진행 안정화 확인 후 복원"의 **안정화 판정 기준은 검증되지 않음** (Murchie 2026).
## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 11 papers (6 SR+MA, 2 SR, 1 in situ SR+MA, 1 policy statement, 1 GERD-specific SR) on the epidemiology, risk factors, monitoring and prevention/management of erosive tooth wear (ETW): ETW-specific prevalence is 35.6% in primary dentition and 37.6% in adolescents, all-mechanism tooth wear in the general population pools at 40.8% (I² 99.1%; Salari 2026), and the FDI's "as high as 80%" is an upper-bound single report for all tooth wear rather than a pooled adult ETW rate; risk-group prevalence is dramatically elevated — 65% in eating disorders, 54.1% in GERD, and 65.9% in patients on special diets — establishing ETW as a widespread condition requiring systematic risk stratification rather than opportunistic management.

Quantitative risk-factor evidence from paired Marschner 2024 meta-analyses identifies the major anamnestic risk signals for adult (OR 2.40 acidic food, OR 2.27 regurgitation, OR 1.81 digestive disorders, OR 1.43 carbonated drinks) and primary dentition (OR 6.90 acidic drinks, OR 5.14 acidic food, OR 1.98 GERD, OR 1.82 holding drinks) cohorts, with straw use protective at OR 0.58; GERD and LPR show a bidirectional association with ETW (Wang 2025) and eating disorders are detectably linked through lower salivary flow and pH (Chan 2024), making the dental appointment a unique screening opportunity.

Prevention evidence is modest in quality: calcium-enriched acidic beverages reduce enamel dissolution 2.6-fold compared to plain acidic drinks in in situ trials (Chatzidimitriou 2024), and CPP-ACP raises in vitro microhardness +13.27% (Ne 2022), but CPP-ACP in chewing gum is non-significant in in situ evidence and the overall calcium/CPP-ACP literature is inconsistent; stannous fluoride remains the best-supported topical agent on surface-loss endpoints (per companion etiology overview), qualified by an industry-funded in situ NMA showing no stannous-zinc advantage over NaF on a remineralisation endpoint; progression monitoring has no validated threshold — intraoral scanning detects micron-level loss that BEWE misses, but the two are not interchangeable (Murchie 2026).

## Summary

This overview synthesizes 11 papers in the `dental-erosion` folder that were not covered by the companion etiology/prevention-chemistry overview ([[dental-erosion-etiology-prevention-synthesis]]). That overview focused on the in vitro and in situ chemistry of erosion (kanzow-2016 erosive wear mechanisms, west-2014 saliva protection, barac-2015 in vitro, souza-2020 CPP-ACP, gomez-2026 prevention umbrella). The current overview addresses the population-level and clinical dimensions: who is affected, by how much, why, how progression is monitored, and what can be done.

### Axis 1 — Population prevalence

The 2024 FDI policy statement captures the scale only loosely: it reports that the prevalence of **tooth wear** (the umbrella term — erosion, attrition, abrasion, possibly abfraction) has been reported "as high as 80%", described as geographically related, and the held text gives no pooled estimate or age group ([[dental-erosion/fdi-2024-tooth-wear-policy-statement]]). *Revision note (2026-11-24): earlier versions of this page read that figure as "up to 80% of adults" with erosive tooth wear; the held FDI page supports neither the adult nor the erosion-specific reading.* The population-level ETW evidence comes from two Marschner 2024 meta-analyses and one Juarez-Lopez 2026 SR+MA, plus one general-population all-wear meta-analysis (Salari 2026).

**General population, all tooth wear (Salari 2026 SR+MA, 133 cross-sectional studies)**: pooled prevalence 40.8% (95% CI 36.7-45.0), I² = 99.1%, individual study prevalences roughly 1.4-97.9%, Egger p = 0.346 ([[dental-erosion/salari-2026-global-prevalence-tooth-wear-general-population]]). Three cautions keep this figure from being conflated with the others on this page: (1) the outcome is **all tooth wear**, not ETW, although most included studies are labelled as measuring erosion; (2) no pooled subgroup by wear type, age, region or diagnostic index is reported, so it cannot be compared like-for-like with the 35.6% primary-dentition or 37.6% adolescent ETW estimates; (3) near-total heterogeneity means it is a burden statement, not an expected rate for any clinic. The authors' claim that prevalence is "increasing annually" conflicts with their own meta-regression (later publication year → lower prevalence). **Salari vs FDI**: both describe all tooth wear, so they share a construct, but 40.8% is a pooled mean of 133 studies while 80% is a single upper-bound figure — the two are not in contradiction, and 80% should not be quoted as a typical adult rate.

**Primary dentition (Marschner 2024 prevalence SR+MA, 23 studies, 16,777 children)**: pooled prevalence 35.6%. Significant predictors: acidic drinks OR 6.90 (95% CI 2.13-22.38), acidic food OR 5.14 (95% CI 1.99-13.25), GERD OR 1.98 (95% CI 1.40-2.79), holding drinks in mouth OR 1.82 (95% CI 1.07-3.09). Straw use protective: OR 0.58 (95% CI 0.39-0.86). These effect sizes indicate that acidic drink habits are the dominant modifiable factor in children; straw use is a simple, evidence-based protective behavior.

**Adolescents (Juarez-Lopez 2026 SR+MA, 24 studies, 21,541)**: pooled prevalence 37.6%. Daily carbonated drink consumption OR 1.98. Bedtime intake OR 7.8 in one high-quality study (salivary clearance loss during sleep). Study heterogeneity was high, and the bedtime OR is a single-study estimate, but the biological mechanism (reduced nocturnal salivary flow) is robust.

**Adults**: The paired Marschner 2024 anamnestic SR+MA (87 studies) provides the most comprehensive adult risk quantification to date. Significant anamnestic risk factors: acidic foods OR 2.40 (95% CI 1.83-3.14), regurgitation OR 2.27 (95% CI 1.86-2.77), digestive disorders OR 1.81 (95% CI 1.43-2.28), carbonated drinks OR 1.43 (95% CI 1.08-1.89), male sex OR 1.30 (95% CI 1.14-1.49). The OR hierarchy is consistent with the pediatric data — acid exposure is the dominant driver, with GERD/regurgitation as the most clinically important systemic pathway.

### Axis 2 — High-risk groups

Dallavilla's 2024 SR+MA (148 studies, 7 predefined risk groups) provides the clearest risk-group prevalence estimates:

| Risk group | ETW prevalence |
|---|---|
| Special diets | 65.9% |
| Eating disorders | 65% |
| GERD | 54.1% |
| Occupational acid exposure | 51% |
| Acidic beverages (habitual) | 40% |
| Medications (acidic) | 30% |

These figures define the priority screening order in a dental practice: a patient with an eating disorder or GERD is at roughly twice the population prevalence risk.

**Eating disorders**: Chan's 2024 SR+MA (33 studies) adds mechanistic evidence — patients with eating disorders show not only higher ETW prevalence but also significantly lower unstimulated salivary flow rate and lower salivary pH. The dual insult (regurgitated acid + depleted salivary buffering) explains the elevated prevalence. Importantly, the oral manifestations may be the presenting sign of an undiagnosed eating disorder: Chan notes that dental professionals are often the first healthcare contact where ETW patterns are recognized.

**GERD and LPR**: Wang's 2025 SR (22 studies, adults) establishes the bidirectional association. ETW prevalence is elevated in GERD/LPR patients AND GERD/LPR prevalence is elevated in ETW patients. Neither association is fully confounded by common dietary habits — the acid reflux pathway is independent. Clinical implication: dentists should ask about reflux; gastroenterologists should ask about ETW; each can detect the other's disease.

### Axis 3 — Prevention: calcium and CPP-ACP

The companion etiology overview (gomez-2026 umbrella) establishes stannous fluoride as the best-supported topical preventive agent, with no other product consistently outperforming it (moderate certainty, mostly in vitro/in situ surface-loss models). That ranking is now qualified by [[dental-erosion/creeth-2024-daily-fluoride-remineralisation-enamel-erosion-nma-in-situ]], an in situ NMA of 14 Haleon-funded single-protocol studies: on a remineralisation-weighted microhardness endpoint, SnF₂-zinc paste gave SMHR 8.8% lower than 1150 ppm NaF (p = 0.001) with equivalent acid resistance and no net protection gain, while fluoride itself showed a clear dose-response (acid resistance plateau near 1000 ppm F; MFP inferior to free-fluoride NaF). This is an endpoint difference, not a supersession — see the companion overview for detail. The robust common ground is a free-fluoride dentifrice at ≥1000 ppm F. The current papers address two additional preventive strategies.

**Calcium-enriched acidic beverages**: Chatzidimitriou's 2024 SR+MA (21 in situ RCTs) found calcium-enriched orange juice caused 2.6-fold less enamel surface loss than plain acidic equivalent. Calcium-enriched products effectively neutralize the proton-driven dissolution by saturating the oral fluid with calcium ions near the enamel surface. CPP-ACP chewing gum showed no statistically significant protection in in situ evidence. Results for milk and CPP-ACP paste were contradictory across studies.

**CPP-ACP in vitro**: Ne's 2022 SR (4 in vitro studies) found CPP-ACP treatment raised enamel microhardness +13.27% versus artificial saliva control. However, the 4-study base is small, and the translation to clinical ETW protection is not established — in vitro microhardness change does not directly predict clinical surface loss protection.

**Practical hierarchy**: (1) Eliminate/reduce acid exposure — address the underlying cause (dietary modification, reflux management, eating disorder referral); (2) Fluoride toothpaste at ≥1000 ppm free fluoride, with stannous formulations preferred on surface-loss evidence (gomez-2026, moderate certainty) — but not shown superior to well-formulated NaF on remineralisation endpoints (creeth-2024, industry-funded in situ); (3) Calcium-enriched variants of acidic beverages if the patient cannot avoid them; (4) CPP-ACP products — biologically plausible but in situ and clinical evidence inconsistent; discuss as adjunct, not primary prevention.

### Axis 4 — Clinical framework (FDI 2024)

The FDI 2024 policy statement formalizes the umbrella definition: tooth wear includes erosive tooth wear, attrition, abrasion, and abfraction — chemical and mechanical causes may coexist, and management must address all contributing factors. The held statement sequences management as: (1) recognise and address the aetiology (behaviour change, medical collaboration for GERD and eating disorders) before restoring; (2) restore once the underlying cause has been addressed. *Revision note (2026-11-24): earlier versions attributed a BEWE-monitoring recommendation and an explicit minimum-invasive clause to FDI; the retrieved FDI text names no index and lacks the formal recommendation list, so those are no longer attributed to it.* BEWE is the commonest index in the held prevalence literature (8 of the adolescent studies in Juarez-Lopez 2026).

**Monitoring progression — the threshold problem (Murchie 2026 SR, 5 longitudinal cohorts, n = 24-517, 1-25 years)** ([[dental-erosion/murchie-2026-defining-progression-in-erosive-tooth]]). The FDI sequence "stabilise, then restore" presupposes that progression can be measured. Murchie shows it currently cannot be defined in a validated way:
- Intraoral scanner (IOS) superimposition detected surface loss of about 10-150 µm over roughly 12 months, ~137 µm at 10 years and >300-400 µm at 25 years; over ≤12 months the categorical indices (BEWE, Smith and Knight) often recorded no change where IOS did.
- Agreement between IOS and BEWE was weak to moderate (volumetric loss vs BEWE-defined progression AUC 0.80; -1.22 mm³ cut-off, sensitivity 79% / specificity 61% — with BEWE as an imperfect reference).
- Progression thresholds ranged from >30 µm to ≥200 µm, or were undefined; no study validated IOS against a high-resolution reference such as laser profilometry; index thresholds themselves are consensus-based.
- Interpretation: IOS (continuous µm/mm³) and ordinal indices measure different constructs and are not interchangeable. Practical split: indices for screening and risk stratification, IOS selectively for high-risk patients, for judging a preventive intervention, and for patient communication. Caveat: 5 heterogeneous studies, qualitative synthesis only, no publication-bias assessment.

This refines how the prevalence data above should be read: index-based cross-sectional prevalence (BEWE and others) captures established, categorical wear and is insensitive to short-term change. The case for integrating ETW screening into routine examination rests on the ETW-specific and risk-group figures (35.6-37.6% in children and adolescents, 30-66% in risk groups), not on the FDI 80% upper bound.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **스크리닝 우선순위**: 모든 성인 환자 BEWE 기록 (선별·위험 계층화 용도 — 단기 진행 검출엔 둔감, Murchie 2026). 섭식장애·GERD/LPR·직업적 산 노출·산성음료 일상 복용 이력은 **고위험** — 즉시 원인 파악.
2. **GERD/LPR 연동**: ETW 환자에서 역류 증상 확인 → 소화기과 의뢰. 역으로 GERD 처방 중인 환자는 구강 내 산 노출 유의.
3. **섭식장애 접근**: 구개측 침식 패턴(특히 전치부)은 self-induced vomiting 신호. 비심판적 진단 → 적절한 정신건강 의뢰. 타액 분비율 측정 권장.
4. **소아·청소년 예방 메시지**: 탄산음료 줄이기, 빨대 사용(OR 0.58 보호), 취침 전 산성음료 절대 금지. GERD 진단 아동은 불소 바니시 강화.
5. **칼슘 강화 음료 권고**: 산성음료(오렌지주스 등)를 완전히 중단 못 하는 환자에서 칼슘 강화 제품 선택 권고 — 법랑질 용해 2.6배 감소.
6. **복원 타이밍**: FDI 원칙 — 원인 제거 후 복원. "진행 안정화" 판정에 검증된 기준값은 없음 (IOS 진행 기준 >30~≥200 µm 제각각, Murchie 2026) → 고위험·경계 케이스는 같은 조건의 IOS 스캔을 기준선으로 남겨 비교하되, µm 변화를 단독 복원 근거로 쓰지 말 것.
7. **유병률 숫자 인용 주의**: 환자·스탭 교육에서 "성인 80%"로 말하지 말 것 — FDI 80%는 전체 마모의 지역별 상한 보고; 일반인구 전체 마모 통합치는 40.8%(이질성 99%), ETW 전용은 유치열 35.6%·청소년 37.6%.

## Gaps & Future Research

- 성인 코호트 RCT: 식이 개입(산성음료 감소, 빨대 도입) 실제 ETW 진행 억제 여부.
- **성인 ETW 전용 통합 유병률 부재**: Salari 2026은 전체 마모(40.8%)이고 유형·연령·지역 하위분석이 없음 — 성인 일반인구 ETW 수치는 여전히 미보유.
- **진행 기준값 검증** (Murchie 2026으로 부분 채워짐 — 문제는 정의됐으나 미해결): µm/mm³ 손실과 임상적 의미 있는 진행을 잇는 검증 기준, 레이저 프로파일로메트리 대비 IOS 검증, 법랑질/상아질 분리 측정.
- CPP-ACP 임상 RCT: 현재 in vitro/in situ 증거를 실제 환자 ETW 감소 데이터로 번역 필요.
- GERD 치료(PPI) → ETW 진행 감소 여부: 약물 개입의 치과 outcomes.
- 섭식장애 조기 발견 프로토콜: 치과적 스크리닝 도구의 민감도·특이도.
