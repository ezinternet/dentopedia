## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 전제: 발치 후 **즉시 임플란트 식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP, Type 1)** 의 성패는 발치 당일이 아니라 **발치 전 증례 선택(case selection)** 에서 결정된다. 생존율은 프로토콜 전반에서 높지만(검증 조합 기준 90%대 후반; [[overviews/immediate-implant-placement-loading-timing-evidence-overview]]), 심미·연조직 실패는 부적절한 선택에서 주로 발생한다([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chen-2014-esthetic-outcomes-immediate-early-implant]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/hamilton-2023-type1a-immediate-implant-immediate-loading-esthetic-sr-ma]]).
> - **발치 전 게이트(콘빔CT, Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT + 임상)**: 협측골벽 두께·완전성, 협측 결손 범위, 치근 시상위치(Sagittal Root Position, SRP), 치근단/치주 감염 상태, 연조직 표현형(gingival phenotype), 1차 안정성 확보 가능 골량(치근단·구개측), 보철 주도(3차원) 식립 가능성을 함께 평가한다([[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-conditions]]).
> - **증례선택 인자의 근거 지도(Dias 2025, 범위고찰 28편; ORF IEM 2025 컨센서스 근거)**: IIP/즉시수복 증례선택 인자를 **해부학적(협측벽 온전성·치조돌기 크기·협측 간극 폭·치은 표현형)/술적/보철적** 축으로 정리. 근거가 전방 단일치 심미부에 집중되고 **구치부·다장치 고정성 보철물(Fixed Dental Prosthesis, FDP)은 얇음** — 이 위키의 구치부 중격·전악 페이지가 부분적으로 보완. 초록전용이라 정량 합성 없음. [[immediate-implant/dias-2025-immediate-implant-placement-and-restorations]]
> - **분류의 맹점 = 환자 수준 인자(Sabri 2022, 체계적 고찰 13개 분류 + 신규 제안)**: 기존 발치와 분류는 협측 골결손·인접면골·치은 퇴축·연조직 표현형 같은 형태 인자를 반복하되 **전신질환·흡연 등 환자 관련 인자는 거의 누락**. 신규 분류는 형태+환자 인자를 결합 — 해부학 중심 SRP 분류의 blind spot을 보완하라는 직접 시사. 초록전용, 분류 범주는 전문 미확보. [[immediate-implant/anatomic-assessment/sabri-2022-single-rooted-extraction-socket-classification]]
> - **동시 충족을 요구하는 4조건(보수적 프로토콜)**: ① 감염이 조절/관리 가능할 것 ② 소켓 형태에 끌리지 않는 이상적 **3차원 위치(3-dimensional, 3D)·보철 주도 위치** 확보 ③ 치근단·구개/설측 골에서 충분한 **1차 안정성(Primary Stability)** ④ **도약거리(Jumping Distance)**/협측 갭, 협측벽, 연조직, (필요 시) 임시보철을 포함한 통합 계획. 하나라도 결여되면 조기(Type 2/3)·지연(Type 4) 또는 발치와 보존술(Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP)로 전환을 고려한다.
> - **협측골벽이 관문**: 다발골(Bundle Bone)은 치아 의존적이라 발치 후 흡수되며 즉시식립이 이를 막지 못한다([[immediate-implant/araujo-2005-ridge-alterations-immediate-implant-dog]]). 얇은 벽(≤1 mm)은 큰 수직 흡수와 연관되고([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2013-ridge-alterations-post-extraction-esthetic]]) 연조직 비후가 골소실을 가릴 수 있어([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2015-soft-tissue-alterations]]) 연조직 외형만으로 판정하면 안 된다. 협측에서 **≥2 mm 구개측 위치**가 협측 치조정 보존에 유리하다([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/staas-2026-fiipp-palatal-position-cbct-3year]]).
> - **도약거리(Jumping Distance)/협측 갭**: 보철 주도·구개측 위치의 결과로 생기는 간극은 예견된 소견이다. 갭/결손 크기·협측벽 두께·심미 위험에 따라 관찰/골대체재 이식/골유도재생(Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)으로 구분하며, 협측 열개·벽 결손은 단순 갭 충전이 아니라 GBR 결손으로 다뤄야 한다([[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/seyssens-2022-immediate-implant-socket-grafting-sr-ma]], [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/sanz-2017-bone-graft-gap-immediate-implant-rct]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-soft-tissue-gap-grafting-material-choice]]).
> - **즉시 임시보철(Immediate Provisionalization)** 은 즉시식립과 별개 결정: 충분한 1차 안정성, 비접촉 교합, 양호한 3D 위치, 연조직 형성에 유리한 보철 외형이 전제될 때 고려한다([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/pitman-2022-immediate-implant-provisionalization-sr-ma]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]]). 전제 미충족 시 치유지대주/맞춤형 치유지대주/지연 보철이 더 안전하다.
> - **부위 특이성**: 전치부의 유리한 심미 결과를 구치부 광경(wide-diameter) 즉시식립에 일반화하면 안 된다 — 구치부에서는 능선보존+지연/통상 직경이 심미·변연골에서 유리할 수 있다([[immediate-implant/checchi-2017-wide-diameter-immediate-post-extractive]] vs [[immediate-implant/puisys-2022-immediate-implant-placement-vs-early]]). 5년 뒤 변연·유두·방사선상을 방어할 자신이 없으면 단계적 접근이 가장 예측 가능한 선택이다.
> - **구치부 6범주 분류(Liu 2026, 델파이 컨센서스 + 후향적 64건)**: Smith–Tarnow 중격 A/B/C에 치근와 정착 D(원위)/M(근심)/P(구개)를 더한 해부 기반 증례선택 틀. Type A(완전 중격 봉입)가 최선(삽입토크(Insertion Torque, IT) >30 N/cm·생존 >90%), Type P는 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) ≥60·초기 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 0.11–0.19mm로 상악동 회피 옵션. 유형별 금기 역치(예: 구개골 <4mm면 Type P 금기) 포함 — **의사결정 보조이며 전향 검증 필요**. [[immediate-implant/molar-septum/liu-2026-expanded-classification-for-immediate-molar]]
> - **인구 수준 위험은 형태 분류에 없는 축(Findler 2026, 국가 코호트 158,824개)**: 즉시식립은 조기/지연 대비 독립 실패 위험인자(3.08% vs 2.07%, 오즈비(Odds Ratio, OR) 1.487); 남성·상악·낮은 사회경제적 지위(Socioeconomic Status, SES)도 위험. 형태·해부 게이트 합격만으로 충분하지 않음을 실세계 등록자료가 시사 — 단 행정 코딩 설계로 교란 통제 제한. [[immediate-implant/findler-2026-clinical-success-rates-of-dental]]

## Three-line Summary

Immediate implant placement (Type 1) should be treated as a **case-selection protocol**, not a default timing shortcut: a pre-extraction gate (CBCT + clinical exam) must confirm a manageable infection status, the possibility of a **restorative-driven 3D position** independent of the socket, sufficient **primary stability** from apical/palatal-lingual bone, and an explicit plan for the buccal gap/jumping distance and soft tissue ([[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-conditions]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/hamilton-2023-type1a-immediate-implant-immediate-loading-esthetic-sr-ma]]).

The facial/buccal wall is the dominant esthetic constraint — bundle bone loss is biologically inevitable after extraction and not prevented by implant placement ([[immediate-implant/araujo-2005-ridge-alterations-immediate-implant-dog]]), thin walls carry high resorption/recession risk ([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2013-ridge-alterations-post-extraction-esthetic]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chen-2014-esthetic-outcomes-immediate-early-implant]]), and palatal positioning (≥2 mm from the buccal crest) plus situation-appropriate gap grafting/GBR and soft-tissue management are the main technical safeguards ([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/staas-2026-fiipp-palatal-position-cbct-3year]], [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/seyssens-2022-immediate-implant-socket-grafting-sr-ma]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-soft-tissue-gap-grafting-material-choice]]).

Immediate provisionalization is a separate decision from immediate placement, and site-specific evidence can reverse anterior-zone optimism (e.g. molar wide-diameter immediate placement underperforms ridge-preservation + delayed on PES and marginal bone; [[immediate-implant/checchi-2017-wide-diameter-immediate-post-extractive]]), so staging (Type 2/3/4 or ARP) remains the predictable choice when any prerequisite is uncertain.

## 세줄요약

즉시 임플란트 식립(Immediate Implant Placement, IIP, Type 1)은 시술 속도가 아니라 **증례 선택 프로토콜**로 다뤄야 하며, 발치 전 콘빔CT(CBCT)·임상 평가에서 감염 관리 가능성, 소켓 비의존적 **보철 주도 3차원 위치**, 치근단·구개/설측 기반 **1차 안정성(Primary Stability)**, 협측 갭/**도약거리(Jumping Distance)**·연조직 계획이 동시에 성립하는지 확인해야 한다([[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-conditions]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/hamilton-2023-type1a-immediate-implant-immediate-loading-esthetic-sr-ma]]).

협측골벽은 심미의 관문으로, 다발골(Bundle Bone) 소실은 발치 후 불가피하고 즉시식립이 이를 막지 못하며([[immediate-implant/araujo-2005-ridge-alterations-immediate-implant-dog]]) 얇은 벽·불리한 위치는 후퇴 위험을 높인다([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2013-ridge-alterations-post-extraction-esthetic]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chen-2014-esthetic-outcomes-immediate-early-implant]]). 협측에서 ≥2 mm 구개측 식립([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/staas-2026-fiipp-palatal-position-cbct-3year]])과 상황 맞춤 갭 이식/골유도재생(GBR)·연조직 관리([[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/seyssens-2022-immediate-implant-socket-grafting-sr-ma]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-soft-tissue-gap-grafting-material-choice]])가 핵심 안전장치다.

**즉시 임시보철(Immediate Provisionalization)** 은 식립과 별개의 결정이고, 전치부 소견을 구치부 광경 즉시식립에 일반화할 수 없으므로([[immediate-implant/checchi-2017-wide-diameter-immediate-post-extractive]] vs [[immediate-implant/puisys-2022-immediate-implant-placement-vs-early]]) 전제 조건이 불확실하면 조기/지연 식립 또는 발치와 보존술(ARP)이 더 예측 가능한 경로다.

## Summary

This overview synthesizes ingested wiki evidence into a **pre-extraction case-selection protocol** for Type 1 immediate implant placement. Its scope is the decision to place *today* versus stage (Type 2/3/4 or ridge preservation): infection control, socket/buccal-wall anatomy, restoratively driven 3D position, primary stability, jumping-gap/defect management, molar-specific constraints, and the separation of placement from immediate provisionalization. It does not replace surgical/prosthetic training or CBCT planning, and it does not claim universal superiority of immediate timing over staged protocols (see [[overviews/implant-placement-timing-immediate-early-delayed]]).

## 1. Pre-extraction Gate (CBCT + Clinical)

The decision is made **before** extraction, not after the socket is visible. A minimum assessment bundle from the ingested evidence:

| Domain | What to determine | Why it gates Type 1 | Key sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facial/buccal plate | Thickness, full-height integrity, fenestration/dehiscence | Thin/deficient plate is the dominant esthetic risk; bundle bone loss is extraction-driven and unavoidable | [[immediate-implant/araujo-2005-ridge-alterations-immediate-implant-dog]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2013-ridge-alterations-post-extraction-esthetic]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chen-2014-esthetic-outcomes-immediate-early-implant]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-conditions]] |
| Root/socket anatomy | Sagittal root position (SRP), apical bone, palatal/lingual anchorage, septum (molars) | Dictates whether an ideal 3D position and primary stability are achievable without socket-driven malposition or perforation | [[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]] (SRP/anatomic spokes), [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/staas-2026-fiipp-palatal-position-cbct-3year]] |
| Infection/pathology | Acute uncontrolled vs chronic/manageable lesion, sinus involvement | Controlled debridement can permit IIP in selected pathology; uncontrolled/acute purulence shifts risk and staging | [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-infected-sites-decision]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-evidence-survival-timing-infected-loading-overview]] |
| Soft tissue | Phenotype/thickness, keratinized tissue, baseline margin level | Thin phenotype and limited baseline reserve amplify recession risk; soft-tissue plan is part of surgery | [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chen-2014-esthetic-outcomes-immediate-early-implant]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-esthetic-soft-tissue-overview]] |
| Restorative plan | Prosthetically driven axis/position, emergence profile, loading intent | Implant must not be socket-driven; loading/provisionalization is a separate, conditional decision | [[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/pitman-2022-immediate-implant-provisionalization-sr-ma]] |

**Interpretation caveat:** soft-tissue contours can mask underlying hard-tissue loss (thick-wall vs thin-wall remodeling dynamics; [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2015-soft-tissue-alterations]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2013-ridge-alterations-post-extraction-esthetic]]), so a favorable gingival outline does not by itself clear the buccal-wall gate.

## 2. Four Preconditions for Same-Day Placement (Conservative Bundle)

Type 1 is reasonable only when these are satisfied together; absence of any one should bias toward staging (early/delayed placement, ARP, or staged GBR) rather than "heroic" same-day execution ([[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-conditions]]).

1. **Controlled infection.** Acute uncontrolled infection is not a Type 1 candidate; selected chronic periapical pathology may be considered with thorough debridement and protocolized management, with survival comparable in pooled evidence under those conditions ([[immediate-implant/infected-socket/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-infected-sites-decision]]).
2. **Ideal 3D, prosthetically driven position (not socket-driven).** The socket must permit the planned restorative axis/position; if the native socket anatomy would force malposition, the case is not a straightforward Type 1 ([[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]]).
3. **Primary stability from stable bone (typically apical/palatal-lingual), not the fragile facial plate.** Achievable stability governs both feasibility and any loading decision; loading thresholds and timing are separate axes ([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/hamilton-2023-type1a-immediate-implant-immediate-loading-esthetic-sr-ma]], [[immediate-implant/loading-protocol/wittneben-2023-immediate-implant-loaded-single-crown-sr]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-placement-loading-timing-evidence-overview]]).
4. **Explicit gap/defect and soft-tissue plan.** Jumping distance, buccal wall/phenotype, and (if considered) provisional contour must be planned *with* the surgery, not after ([[overviews/immediate-implant-soft-tissue-gap-grafting-material-choice]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-esthetic-soft-tissue-overview]]).

## 3. Buccal Wall as the Gatekeeper

- **Biology:** extraction removes the tooth-dependent blood supply to bundle bone; ridge modeling follows and immediate placement does not prevent this remodeling ([[immediate-implant/araujo-2005-ridge-alterations-immediate-implant-dog]]).
- **Risk concentration:** thin facial bone/thin phenotype and unfavorable position are repeatedly linked to recession/esthetic compromise ([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chen-2014-esthetic-outcomes-immediate-early-implant]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2013-ridge-alterations-post-extraction-esthetic]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-conditions]]).
- **Position safeguard:** placing the implant ≥2 mm palatal from the buccal crest supports buccal hard-tissue preservation ([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/staas-2026-fiipp-palatal-position-cbct-3year]]).

**Practical gate:** intact, favorably thick walls with adequate apical/palatal bone support the Type 1 pathway; missing, fenestrated, or very thin walls with high esthetic demand push toward early/delayed placement and/or staged augmentation ([[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]], [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/meijer-2024-immediate-implant-bony-defect-10year-rct]] as an expert, protocol-specific exception rather than a default).

## 4. Jumping Distance, Gap Grafting, and When It Becomes GBR

The **jumping distance (horizontal gap)** between implant and socket wall is an expected consequence of a correct palatal/restorative position — the gap must not dictate implant position. Management depends on gap/defect morphology, wall thickness, and esthetic risk ([[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-soft-tissue-gap-grafting-material-choice]]):

| Situation (synthesized) | Direction of management | Evidence anchors |
|---|---|---|
| Small, contained gap behind a favorable/thick wall | Case-dependent: monitor or graft according to esthetic risk/material plan | [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/seyssens-2022-immediate-implant-socket-grafting-sr-ma]] |
| Gap in esthetic zone / contour support needed | Bone substitute (often considered when gap ≥~2 mm) to limit buccal resorption and support contour; collagen-containing grafts may add benefit (heterogeneous) | [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/seyssens-2022-immediate-implant-socket-grafting-sr-ma]], [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/sanz-2017-bone-graft-gap-immediate-implant-rct]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-soft-tissue-gap-grafting-material-choice]] |
| Buccal dehiscence / missing facial wall | Treat as defect reconstruction (GBR), not a simple gap fill; staging may be preferable | [[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-conditions]], [[overviews/immediate-implant-soft-tissue-gap-grafting-material-choice]] |

Material choice (hard-tissue gap vs soft-tissue augmentation) must be separated anatomically; autogenous connective tissue graft (CTG) remains the strongest soft-tissue augmenter in pooled esthetic-zone evidence, with substitutes as alternatives under specific trade-offs ([[overviews/immediate-implant-soft-tissue-gap-grafting-material-choice]]).

## 5. Site-Specific Modifiers: Molar / Wide-Diameter and Anatomy

Molar sockets add traps (septum morphology, wide/divergent sockets, sinus floor/canal proximity, prosthetic-center control). Septal bone may aid stability but can also divert the fixture from the prosthetic center; inadequate apical/septal engagement argues for socket grafting and delayed placement. Critically, anterior-zone timing/esthetic advantages do **not** generalize to molar wide-diameter immediate placement:

- [[immediate-implant/puisys-2022-immediate-implant-placement-vs-early]] (anterior single-tooth, RCT): comparable PES with operational advantages for immediate in a strictly selected scenario.
- [[immediate-implant/checchi-2017-wide-diameter-immediate-post-extractive]] (molar, wide-diameter, RCT): ridge preservation + delayed conventional-diameter favored on PES and marginal bone vs wide-diameter immediate.

Anatomic assessment (e.g. SRP, premolar perforation risk, septum classification) and technique selection are therefore site-specific ([[overviews/immediate-implant-decision-ladder]]). Liu 2026 (concept development + Delphi consensus, 64 molars) formalizes this with a **six-category anatomy-based classification** — septal types A (complete containment; IT >30 N/cm, survival >90%), B (partial support), C (peripheral engagement) plus root-socket types D (distal), M (mesial), P (palatal) — with a treatment hierarchy (prioritize A, then P in the maxilla / D–M when root anatomy favors) and explicit contraindication thresholds (e.g. no Type P if palatal bone height <4 mm or sinus contact <1 mm; no Type C if buccolingual width <6 mm). It extends the Smith–Tarnow A–C system ([[immediate-implant/molar-septum/smith-2013-classification-molar-extraction-immediate-implant]]) but is explicitly **decision support, not outcome validation** — prospective multicenter validation is pending ([[immediate-implant/molar-septum/liu-2026-expanded-classification-for-immediate-molar]]; see also [[overviews/maxillary-molar-palatal-root-socket-iip]]).

## 6. Immediate Provisionalization Is a Separate Decision

Placement timing and restoration/loading timing are distinct variables ([[overviews/immediate-implant-placement-loading-timing-evidence-overview]]). Immediate provisionalization may support soft-tissue contour in selected esthetic cases but requires, at minimum, adequate primary stability, a non-loading occlusal scheme, and a correct 3D position/restoration contour; survival can be comparable while soft-tissue/patient-centered outcomes depend on indication and execution ([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/pitman-2022-immediate-implant-provisionalization-sr-ma]], [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/hamilton-2023-type1a-immediate-implant-immediate-loading-esthetic-sr-ma]], [[immediate-implant/loading-protocol/wittneben-2023-immediate-implant-loaded-single-crown-sr]]). When prerequisites are unmet, a healing/customized healing abutment or delayed provisional is the safer route.

## 7. Chairside Decision Flow (Protocol Summary)

1. **Gate (pre-extraction):** CBCT/clinical — buccal wall, defect, SRP/anatomy, infection, phenotype, apical/palatal bone for stability, restorative axis.
2. **Qualify:** all four preconditions met? if no → stage (Type 2/3/4, ARP, or staged GBR).
3. **Execute position first:** restorative-driven, palatal/lingual engagement (≥2 mm palatal safeguard where indicated), avoid thin buccal plate as primary purchase.
4. **Manage gap/defect by morphology:** contained gap vs GBR-level dehiscence/missing wall; plan soft-tissue augmentation when phenotype/esthetics demand it.
5. **Decide provisional separately:** only if stability/occlusion/contour criteria are satisfied.
6. **Site check:** do not transfer anterior-zone conclusions to molar/wide-diameter sockets without site-specific evidence.
