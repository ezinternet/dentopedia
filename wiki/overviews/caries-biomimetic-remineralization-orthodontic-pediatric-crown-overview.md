> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 교정 처치(고정식 교정·클리어 얼라이너)와 소아 전치부 수복은 새로운 우식 위험 부위를 만든다. 바이오마임틱 히드록시아파타이트(nHAp, Zn-CHA) 제제와 수복재 선택(지르코니아 vs 복합레진 스트립 크라운)이 그 위험을 관리한다.
> - **Zn-CHA (아연탄산 히드록시아파타이트, Zinc-Carbonate Hydroxyapatite)**: 에나멜 산저항성·재광화·침식 방지 및 상아세관 폐쇄 효과 확인. 일부 임상시험에서 불소 치약 비열등 또는 우월. 그러나 in-vitro 중심(21/30편), RCT 부족 — 불소 대체 임상 우월성 미확정.
> - **교정 브라켓 주변 탈광화 예방 접착제**: 바이오액티브 필러(BAG, n-HAP, n-ACP, nCaF2) 함유 접착제가 모든 in-vitro/in-situ 연구(24편)에서 대조군 대비 유의한 재광화. 그러나 15/24편 높은 비뚤림 위험, 메타분석 불가, 28일 이상 장기 임상 데이터 없음.
> - **클리어 얼라이너 부착물(Attachment) 주변**: 수동 불소 치약 단독은 부착물 주변 에나멜 탈광화를 악화(DIAGNOdent +4.35 단위). nHAp 집중 프로토콜이 최고 재광화(−5.36), 불소 니스(−4.45), CPP-ACP(−4.02) 순. 얼라이너 치료 시작 시점부터 능동적 재광화 프로토콜 필요.
> - **소아 전치부 크라운 (PZC vs RCSC)**: 9 RCT SR+MA — PZC가 2차 우식(RR=0.41), 크라운 탈락(RR=0.15), 수복 실패(RR=0.07), 치태 축적(RR=0.08) 모두 우월. 단 길항치 마모 7.58배 (지르코니아 경도 1000–1400 HV vs 에나멜 300–400 HV). 우식 위험↑·미용 요구↑ → PZC; 이갈이·길항치 보호 우선 → RCSC.
> - **기존 오버뷰와의 관계**: `non-restorative-caries-management-topical-fluoride-synthesis`가 SDF·불소 니스·고농도 불소 치약을 다루고 있음. 본 오버뷰는 **HA 바이오마임틱 제제·교정 처치 위험 부위·소아 전치부 수복재 선택**이라는 기존 미포함 축을 추가.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 4 papers on caries prevention at iatrogenic-risk sites: biomimetic zinc-carbonate hydroxyapatite (Zn-CHA, butera-2023 narrative review), bioactive-filler orthodontic adhesives (hamdi-2024 SR, 24 in vitro/in situ studies), nano-hydroxyapatite (nHAp) remineralization around clear aligner attachments (unal-2025 prospective trial n=40), and prefabricated zirconia crowns vs composite strip crowns for primary anterior teeth (chen-2026 SR+MA, 9 RCTs).

Hydroxyapatite-based agents (Zn-CHA, nHAp) consistently outperform controls in enamel remineralization but lack large RCT evidence against fluoride; nHAp ranked first (DIAGNOdent −5.36) over fluoride varnish and CPP-ACP around aligner attachments, while passive fluoride toothpaste alone worsened demineralization; prefabricated zirconia crowns (PZC) dominate composite strip crowns (RCSC) on caries prevention (RR=0.41) and retention but cause 7.58× more antagonist wear.

Clinical bottom line: start active remineralization (nHAp or CPP-ACP) from the beginning of aligner/bracket treatment; select PZC when caries risk or esthetic demand is high and no parafunctional concern, RCSC when antagonist wear is the priority.

## 세줄요약

4편 종합: Zn-CHA 바이오마임틱 히드록시아파타이트(butera-2023), 바이오액티브 필러 교정 접착제(hamdi-2024, 24편 SR), 클리어 얼라이너 부착물 주변 nHAp 재광화(unal-2025, n=40), 소아 전치부 지르코니아 vs 복합레진 스트립 크라운(chen-2026, 9 RCT SR+MA).

히드록시아파타이트 계열(Zn-CHA, nHAp)이 재광화에서 대조군 대비 일관 우월이나 불소 대비 대규모 RCT 부족; 얼라이너 부착물 주변에서 nHAp 1위(−5.36), 수동 불소 치약 단독은 오히려 악화; 지르코니아 크라운이 우식 예방·유지 모두 우월하나 길항치 마모 7.58배.

임상 적용: 교정 치료 시작부터 nHAp/CPP-ACP 능동 프로토콜 시행; 소아 전치부 크라운은 우식 위험·심미 요구↑ → PZC, 이갈이·길항치 보호 우선 → RCSC.

## Summary

Orthodontic appliances (fixed brackets, clear aligner attachments) and pediatric anterior restorations create localised environments of elevated caries risk. This synthesis organises the latest evidence around three axes: (1) biomimetic hydroxyapatite as a fluoride-alternative/supplement, (2) remineralization at orthodontic-appliance surfaces, and (3) crown material choice for secondary caries prevention in primary anterior teeth.

## Axis 1 — Biomimetic Hydroxyapatite: Zn-CHA as Fluoride Alternative

Zinc-carbonate hydroxyapatite (Zn-CHA, 아연탄산 히드록시아파타이트) mimics the mineral composition of biological enamel and integrates Zn²⁺ for antimicrobial and acid-resistance enhancement. Butera 2023 reviewed 30 studies (2003–2023; predominantly in vitro):

| Outcome | Zn-CHA finding |
|---|---|
| Enamel acid-resistance | Consistently improved vs control |
| Remineralization (in vitro) | Comparable or superior to NaF toothpaste in most models |
| Erosion protection | Positive in vitro; limited clinical data |
| Dentinal tubule occlusion (hypersensitivity) | Effective in multiple models |
| Clinical non-inferiority to NaF | Shown in some trials; superiority not conclusively established |

**Evidence ceiling**: 21/30 studies are in vitro; RCT count is small. Zn-CHA is a clinically credible fluoride alternative or adjunct, not a proven replacement. This pairs with the broader HA picture in unal-2025 and hamdi-2024 (see Axis 2).

## Axis 2 — Remineralization at Orthodontic Appliance Sites

### 2a. Fixed-bracket adhesives (hamdi-2024)

Bracket-adjacent white spot lesions (WSL, 백반증) are a well-documented complication of fixed orthodontic treatment, where biofilm accumulates at the adhesive margin. Hamdi 2024 reviewed 24 in vitro/in situ studies of experimental orthodontic adhesives incorporating bioactive fillers:

| Filler type | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bioactive glass (BAG, 바이오액티브 글라스) | Remineralization + alkaline buffer |
| Nano-hydroxyapatite (n-HAP) | Mineral replenishment |
| Nano-amorphous calcium phosphate (n-ACP) | Controlled ion release |
| Nano-calcium fluoride (nCaF2) | Sustained fluoride delivery |

All 24 included studies showed significant remineralization vs non-releasing adhesive controls (by hardness, micro-CT, or polarized light microscopy). **Caveat**: 15/24 studies rated high risk of bias; heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis; no study exceeded 28 days of pH cycling. The clinical evidence base is promising but immature — adoption should be selective for patients with very high WSL risk.

### 2b. Clear aligner attachment sites (unal-2025)

Composite attachments on clear aligners create analogous plaque-retention zones. Unal 2025 (prospective, n=40, 4 arms, 6 months, DIAGNOdent measurement) compared protocols:

| Protocol | DIAGNOdent change at 6 months |
|---|---|
| Control (1450 ppm NaF toothpaste only) | **+4.35** (worsened) |
| Nightly CPP-ACP | −4.02 |
| Quarterly 5% NaF varnish | −4.45 |
| Intensive nHAp | **−5.36** (best) |

**Key clinical message**: passive fluoride toothpaste alone is insufficient to prevent demineralization at aligner attachment sites. An active remineralization protocol should begin at aligner delivery, not reactively after WSL appear. nHAp was numerically superior, though the non-randomized design (patient preference), n=10/group, and more intensive nHAp dosing protocol limit conclusive ranking.

## Axis 3 — Primary Anterior Crown Selection: PZC vs RCSC

Chen 2026 (SR+MA, 9 RCTs, n=1,746 primary anterior teeth, children 3–6y, PROSPERO CRD42024625706) provides the strongest comparative evidence:

| Outcome | RR (PZC vs RCSC) | p | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secondary caries | 0.41 | 0.030 | PZC strongly protective |
| Crown retention failure | 0.15 | <0.001 | PZC far superior |
| Restoration failure | 0.07 | <0.001 | PZC far superior |
| Plaque accumulation | 0.08 | <0.001 | PZC far superior |
| Antagonist tooth wear | **7.58** | <0.001 | RCSC strongly favoured |

The zirconia hardness mismatch is the decisive trade-off: PZC (zirconia hardness 1000–1400 HV) vs natural enamel (300–400 HV). When PZC opposes a primary or permanent tooth, accelerated antagonist wear is a real risk.

**Case-selection algorithm:**

```
Child with primary anterior tooth requiring full-coverage restoration
        │
        ├─ High caries risk / poor hygiene / multiple teeth involved?
        │   AND no parafunctional concern (bruxism, heavy occlusal loading)?
        │   → PZC preferred (retention, caries prevention, plaque control)
        │
        ├─ Opposing permanent tooth present + bruxism / parafunctional history?
        │   → RCSC preferred (antagonist wear protection)
        │
        └─ Esthetic demand primary concern, low caries risk, compliant family?
            → Either; discuss antagonist wear risk with parents for PZC
```

8/9 RCTs rated low risk of bias (RoB 2) — evidence quality is relatively high for a pediatric intervention.
