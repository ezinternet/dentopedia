## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 핵심 명제: 플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching, PLS — 임플란트보다 좁은 지대주를 얹는 방식)은 변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)을 **일관되게, 반복적으로** 줄인다 — 총 22편(SR+MA 3편·RCT 5편·합의보고서 1편·후향연구 2편·기전연구 4편·서술리뷰/원저 다수). **방향은 수렴하지만 만장일치는 아니다** — null RCT 1편(Rossi 2015) 존재, 아래 참조.
> - **기전은 3층**: ①염증성 미세간극 이격(Desai 2013, Salimi 2011 — 임플란트-지대주 접합부(IAJ)를 내측·치관측으로 이동시켜 생물학적 폭경(biologic width) 재형성 자극 감소) ②생역학적 응력 재분산(유한요소분석(FEA) 원저 Sahabi 2013·Yadav 2025) ③부식·금속이온 방출 감소(Alrabeah 2016). **①은 이 위키가 가진 유일한 직접 측정(Chien 2016)이 오히려 반대 방향** — 확정된 사실로 서술하지 말 것.
> - **크기(magnitude)는 연구마다 다르나 방향은 대체로 일치**: Strietzel 2015(SR+MA, 22편)는 PS 0.49mm vs PM 1.01mm(가중평균차 약 0.5mm); Di Girolamo 2016(SR+MA, 15 RCT/1,439개)은 "포함연구 중 PS가 더 나쁜 결과를 보인 연구 0편"; Juan-Montesinos 2022(SR+MA, 9편/937개)는 MD 0.255mm(p<0.05); RCT는 Enkling 2013 3년·Rocha 2016 3년(0.28 vs 0.68mm)·Messias 2019 5년(0.23mm 차이)·Bhatt 2025 12개월(0.32 vs 0.81mm, p<0.001)이 유의하게 PS 우위, Rossi 2015 6개월만 유의차 없음.
> - **생존율은 무차이**: 모든 연구가 PS와 PM 간 임플란트 생존율에 유의차 없음을 일관되게 보고 — PS의 이득은 골 보존이지 생존 자체가 아니다.
> - **탐침깊이(probing depth)는 PS에서 소폭 증가 경향**: Juan-Montesinos 2022에서 MD 0.082mm(비유의)이나 sensitivity 분석(1편 제외)에서 유의(0.190mm)로 전환 — 골 보존 이득이 약간의 탐침깊이 증가를 동반할 수 있음, 방향은 아직 완전히 확정적이지 않음.
> - **역사적 흐름**: **플랫폼 스위칭은 애초부터 설계된 기법이 아니었다** — Lazzara & Porter 2006이 origin point: 1991년 광경 임플란트 출시 당시 매칭 지대주 재고 부족으로 우연히 협경 지대주가 얹힌 케이스들에서 골소실이 더 적다는 사후 방사선 관찰이 출발점(우연한 발견 → 사후 명명·가설화). Salimi 2011·Desai 2013(서술리뷰, 기전 정교화 단계) → Enkling 2013·Rocha 2016(개별 RCT로 검증) → Strietzel 2015·Di Girolamo 2016(첫 SR+MA로 정량 확정) → Messias 2019(5년 장기 확증) → Juan-Montesinos 2022(최신 SR+MA로 재확인). 20년에 걸쳐 우연한 관찰→명명→기전화→검증→정량화→장기확증의 흐름.
> - **임상 적용**: 심미구역·협착 치조제(<3mm)·짧은 임플란트·골높이 제한 상황에서 PS 우선 고려(Salimi 2011의 적응증 제안이 이후 문헌으로 뒷받침됨). 즉시*식립*은 Attia 2025로 근거 일부 확보, 즉시*부하*와의 조합은 여전히 gap.
> - **근거 한계**: 절대적 MBL 감소치(0.2~0.5mm)는 통계적으로 유의하나 임상적으로 작다 — Albrektsson 성공기준(1년 <1.5mm) 안에서의 미세조정에 가깝다. 이질성(heterogeneity)이 SR+MA마다 보고방식이 달라 직접 비교가 어렵다.
> - **[2026-08-15 추가·중요] 만장일치가 깨졌다 — null RCT 1편**: Rossi 2015(파일럿 RCT, 환자 23명, PS 21개 vs 표준 18개, 6개월)는 변연골 수준·탐침깊이·부착고 **어느 지표에서도 군간 유의차를 찾지 못했고**, 두 군 모두 임플란트 숄더 약 1mm 하방에서 골이 안정화됐다. 저자 결론은 "이미 최적화된 표준 임플란트를 PS로 더 개선하기는 어려워 보인다". 이 위키가 이전에 "PM이 더 나은 연구 0편"이라 적을 수 있었던 것은 **이 논문을 아직 갖고 있지 않았기 때문**이다. 단 6개월·파일럿·소표본이라 SR+MA 3편을 뒤집지는 못한다 — 방향은 여전히 PS 우위지만 **"만장일치"라는 표현은 이제 쓸 수 없다**.
> - **[2026-08-15 추가] 기전은 2층이 아니라 3층 — 부식(corrosion) 축 신규**: Alrabeah 2016(in vitro, ICP-MS, 42개 시편)에서 플랫폼 매칭군이 스위칭군보다 금속이온을 **약 2배 더 방출**했고(V·Al·Co·Cr·Mo, p<0.05), Co·Cr·Mo는 미스매치 크기에 비례해 감소했다. 즉 PS의 골보존 효과에는 응력 재분산·미세간극 이격 외에 **부식 계면을 조직에서 멀리 떼어놓는 제3의 경로**가 있을 수 있다.
> - **[2026-08-15 추가] 기전 이야기가 단순하지 않다**: Chien 2016(분할구강, 14명, 임플란트주위 열구액 사이토카인)에서 초기 치유기 연조직 반응은 대체로 두 군이 비슷했고, **종양괴사인자-α (TNF-α)는 1주차에 PS군이 오히려 약 3배 높았다**(17±7 vs 6±1 pg, p=0.005). "PS가 염증을 줄인다"는 서술은 최소한 초기 치유기에는 그대로 적용되지 않는다.
> - **[2026-08-15 추가] 유한요소분석(FEA) 원저 2편 — 골 응력은 내려가지만 부품 응력은 올라간다**: Sahabi 2013(2개 시스템)·Yadav 2025(D2·D3 골질, 200N 축·30° 사면하중) 모두 PS가 치조정 피질골·해면골의 최대 von Mises 응력을 낮추는 대신, **임플란트·지대주·지대주 나사로 응력을 옮긴다**고 보고. 사면하중에서 특히 두드러진다. 골보존의 대가가 보철 부품 쪽 기계적 부담일 수 있다 — 탐침깊이 신호에 이은 **두 번째 trade-off**다.
> - **[2026-08-15 추가] 즉시식립 근거 일부 확보**: Attia 2025(후향, 37명, 최장 23년)에서 즉시식립 임플란트도 **근심 변연골소실이 PS에서 유의하게 적었다**(0.26 vs 0.75mm, p=0.044; 회귀 β=2.11, p=0.039). 단 **원심은 유의차 없음** — 근심/원심 비대칭은 기존 문헌이 다루지 않던 관찰이다.
> - **[2026-08-15 추가] 미세간극 수직 위치라는 별개 축**: Schwarz 2013(Camlog 재단 합의보고서)은 PS와 별도로 **기계가공 칼라를 치조정 상방(supracrestal)에 두면 하방(subcrestal)보다 골소실이 적고**, 치조정 하방 1mm 이상이면 리모델링은 늘지만 거친 표면의 골 피개는 더 잘 유지된다고 정리. 연결부 형태(내측/외측·원추/버트조인트)의 영향은 "근거 부족". PS는 *수평* 오프셋, 미세간극 위치는 *수직* 축 — 혼동 금지.
> - **[2026-08-10 추가] 얼마나 벌려야 하나(offset magnitude)**: 기존 9편은 전부 "PS 했다/안 했다"만 비교했지 오프셋 크기 자체는 다루지 않았음 — 신규 4편(Hürzeler 2007·Canullo & Rasperini 2007·Rodríguez-Ciurana 2009·이들을 엮은 Gupta 2019 리뷰)이 실제 편측 미스매치 수치를 공급. Hürzeler 2007: **편측 0.45mm 축소가 골소실 방지에 "충분"**하다는, 이 위키에서 가장 구체적인 임계값 주장(단, 소규모·비무작위·단일 오프셋 시험). Canullo 2007은 1.0mm(가장 큰 값)도 효과 있었음을 보고(대조군 없는 증례군). 그러나 어느 연구도 오프셋 크기를 독립변수로 통제한 용량-반응 시험은 아니므로, **검증된 최소 임계값이나 dose-response 곡선은 여전히 확립되지 않음**. Rodríguez-Ciurana 2009는 별개로 임플란트 간 거리(<3mm 근접배치에서도 PS가 골첨부 보존) 데이터도 제공 — 오프셋 크기 질문과 혼동 금지.

## Three-line Summary

Synthesis of 22 papers on platform switching (PLS) and marginal bone loss (MBL), including the 2006 origin paper that coined the term: three SR+MA (Strietzel 2015, 22 studies: PS 0.49mm vs PM 1.01mm weighted mean; Di Girolamo 2016, 15 RCTs/1,439 implants: zero included study favored PM; Juan-Montesinos 2022, 9 studies/937 implants: MD 0.255mm, p<0.05), a 2013 expert consensus report (Schwarz 2013, Camlog Foundation), and five RCTs (Enkling 2013, 3yr; Rocha 2016, 3yr, PS 0.28 vs PM 0.68mm, p=0.002; Messias 2019, 5yr equivalence trial, PS 0.23mm lower, p=0.025; Bhatt 2025, 12mo, PS 0.32 vs PM 0.81mm, p<0.001) converge on direction — PLS reduces MBL with no survival penalty — with one dissent: Rossi 2015, a 6-month pilot RCT (n=39 implants), found no significant difference on any measured parameter.

The mechanism is now three-layered rather than two: a coronal-and-inward shift of the implant-abutment junction's inflammatory microgap away from crestal bone (Desai 2013, Salimi 2011), biomechanical stress redistribution confirmed by two primary FEA studies (Sahabi 2013, Yadav 2025 — bone stress down, but implant/abutment/screw stress up), and roughly twofold lower metal-ion release from the platform-switched interface in accelerated corrosion testing (Alrabeah 2016). The biologic layer is not clean: Chien 2016 found peri-implant crevicular cytokine profiles broadly similar between PS and PM during early healing, with TNF-α paradoxically ~3x *higher* in PS at week 1.

Platform switching was not designed from the outset for bone preservation — it originated as an accidental clinical observation (Lazzara & Porter 2006): a 1991 prosthetic-component inventory gap forced many wide-diameter implants to be restored with narrower "standard" abutments, and retrospective radiographic follow-up of these unintentionally platform-switched cases showed less-than-expected crestal bone loss. The evidence accumulated textbook-style over the following two decades — accidental observation and naming (2006) → mechanism proposed (2011-2013) → individual RCTs (2013, 2016) → first meta-analyses (2015-2016) → long-term equivalence trial (2019) → confirmatory meta-analysis (2022) — with one soft caveat: probing depth showed a small, borderline-significant increase with PLS in the most recent meta-analysis, suggesting the bone-preservation benefit may not be entirely free.

## 세줄요약

플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching, PLS)과 변연골 소실(MBL) 관련 22편 종합(용어를 최초 명명한 2006년 origin 논문 포함) — SR+MA 3편(Strietzel 2015, 22편: PS 0.49mm vs PM 1.01mm; Di Girolamo 2016, 15 RCT/1,439개: 포함연구 중 PM이 더 나은 연구 0편; Juan-Montesinos 2022, 9편/937개: MD 0.255mm, p<0.05), 전문가 합의보고서 1편(Schwarz 2013, Camlog 재단), RCT 5편(Enkling 2013 3년; Rocha 2016 3년, PS 0.28 vs PM 0.68mm, p=0.002; Messias 2019 5년 동등성시험, PS 0.23mm 적음, p=0.025; Bhatt 2025 12개월, PS 0.32 vs PM 0.81mm, p<0.001)이 방향에서 수렴: PLS는 MBL을 줄이며 생존율 손해는 없음. 단 **반대 1편** — Rossi 2015(6개월 파일럿 RCT, 임플란트 39개)는 측정한 모든 지표에서 유의차를 찾지 못했다.

기전은 이제 2층이 아니라 3층이다 — ①임플란트-지대주 접합부의 염증성 미세간극을 치조정에서 치관측·내측으로 이동(Desai 2013, Salimi 2011), ②생역학적 응력 재분산(유한요소분석 원저 2편 Sahabi 2013·Yadav 2025로 확인 — 골 응력은 내려가나 임플란트·지대주·나사 응력은 올라감), ③가속부식 시험에서 스위칭 계면의 금속이온 방출이 약 2배 적음(Alrabeah 2016). 생물학적 층은 깔끔하지 않다 — Chien 2016은 초기 치유기 열구액 사이토카인 양상이 두 군에서 대체로 비슷했고 종양괴사인자-α (TNF-α)는 1주차에 PS군이 오히려 약 3배 높았다고 보고.

플랫폼 스위칭은 처음부터 골보존을 노리고 설계된 기법이 아니었다 — 1991년 부품 재고 부족으로 우연히 협경 지대주가 얹힌 케이스들의 사후 방사선 관찰(Lazzara & Porter 2006)에서 출발한 우연한 발견이다. 근거는 이후 20년에 걸쳐 교과서적으로 축적됐다 — 우연한 관찰·명명(2006) → 기전 제안(2011-2013) → 개별 RCT(2013·2016) → 첫 메타분석(2015-2016) → 장기 동등성시험(2019) → 재확증 메타분석(2022) — 단, 최신 메타분석에서 탐침깊이가 PLS에서 소폭·경계적 유의 증가를 보여 골보존 이득이 완전히 "공짜"는 아닐 수 있음을 시사.

## Thesis

Platform switching is one of the more strongly convergent implant design questions in this wiki — but **[revised 2026-08-15]** it is convergent in *direction*, not unanimous. Twenty-two papers spanning 2006–2025, three meta-analyses, an expert consensus report, and five independent RCTs with follow-up out to 5 years point the same way: placing a narrower abutment on a wider implant platform (PLS) reduces marginal bone loss relative to platform-matched (PM) restorations, without a survival penalty. One RCT dissents ([[implants/mbl/rossi-2015-randomized-clinical-investigation-titanium-implants]], 6-month pilot, n=39 implants: no significant difference on marginal bone level, pocket depth, or attachment height), and the mechanistic picture has become more complicated rather than less as primary-source evidence replaced secondhand citation.

**The mechanism is three-layered, and only two layers are clean.** Biologically, the implant-abutment junction (IAJ) — where the peri-implant inflammatory infiltrate concentrates — is shifted inward and coronally, away from the crestal bone that would otherwise remodel to re-establish biologic width around it (Desai 2013, Salimi 2011). Biomechanically, two primary finite-element analyses now held in this wiki ([[implants/mbl/sahabi-2013-biomechanical-effects-platform-switching-two]], [[implants/mbl/yadav-2025-finite-element-analysis-platform-switching]]) confirm PLS lowers peak von Mises stress in crestal cortical and cancellous bone — previously this wiki cited FEA only secondhand through narrative reviews. Third and newly added, [[implants/mbl/alrabeah-2016-effect-platform-switching-metal-ion]] shows platform-matched couples release roughly twice the metal ions of switched couples under accelerated corrosion (V, Al from titanium abutments; Co, Cr, Mo from cobalt-chrome, the latter three decreasing proportionally with mismatch magnitude) — suggesting a corrosion-distancing pathway independent of stress and microgap-inflammation arguments.

**The biologic layer, however, does not behave as the standard story predicts.** [[implants/mbl/chien-2016-effect-platform-switching-periimplant-crevicular]] sampled peri-implant crevicular fluid cytokines in a split-mouth design over the first six weeks after abutment connection and found the two configurations broadly similar — with tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α) paradoxically about three times *higher* in PS at week 1 (17±7 vs 6±1 pg, p=0.005), the only significant between-group cytokine difference. No paper this wiki holds confirms the microgap-inflammation mechanism histologically; the direct biomarker measurement we do hold points the other way in early healing. The mechanism should therefore be presented as plausible and multiply-supported at the biomechanical and corrosion layers, but **not** as established at the inflammatory layer.

**The clinical numbers agree in direction, less precisely in magnitude.** Strietzel 2015 (SR+MA, 22 studies — 15 RCTs + 7 prospective cohorts, meta-analysis of 13 RCTs) found a weighted mean MBL of 0.49 mm (95% CI 0.38–0.60) for PS versus 1.01 mm (95% CI 0.62–1.40) for PM, p<0.0001 — roughly half the bone loss. Di Girolamo 2016 (SR+MA, 15 RCT publications, 1,439 implants) reported the cleanest directional signal in this cluster: *no* included study found worse outcomes with PLS, though implant failure rates did not differ. Juan-Montesinos 2022 (SR+MA, 9 studies, 937 implants — the most recent and possibly best-conducted synthesis) found a more modest but still significant MD of 0.255 mm (p<0.05). The three individual RCTs bracket this range: Enkling 2013 (3-year multicenter RCT) found PS significantly better maintained throughout follow-up; Rocha 2016 (3-year multicenter RCT, posterior mandible, n=135 implants) found PS 0.28 ± 0.56 mm vs PM 0.68 ± 0.64 mm (mean advantage 0.39 mm, 95% CI 0.15–0.64, p=0.002); Messias 2019 (5-year equivalence RCT, n=128 implants) found PS 0.23 mm lower (95% CI 0.03–0.43, p=0.025) and explicitly rejected the equivalence hypothesis in PS's favor. No RCT or meta-analysis in this cluster found a survival difference between PS and PM.

**Three caveats now temper the picture, where there was previously one.** *First*, Juan-Montesinos 2022 — the newest SR+MA — found probing depth increased slightly more with PLS (MD 0.082 mm, not significant overall, but reaching significance at MD 0.190 mm in a one-study-removed sensitivity analysis), the first hint of a soft-tissue trade-off. *Second* **[added 2026-08-15]**, both primary FEA studies report that the crestal bone stress PLS removes does not vanish — it moves onto the implant, abutment, and abutment screw, most pronounced under oblique (non-axial) loading. Sahabi 2013 notes the elevated interface stress stayed below titanium and cobalt-chrome yield strength, so it is not an immediate failure risk, but neither paper tested fatigue or long-term component survival; Yadav 2025 explicitly calls for more durable abutment materials and designs under oblique function. *Third* **[added 2026-08-15]**, the null RCT (Rossi 2015) sets a floor on how large the effect can be claimed to be: in a base implant design whose standard configuration already stabilized bone ~1 mm below the shoulder, adding platform switching produced no measurable improvement over six months. Its authors' framing is worth carrying forward — PLS may add less where the underlying implant design is already performing well, which the meta-analytic averages cannot show because they pool across designs.

**Historical arc.** Platform switching was not conceived from the start as a bone-preservation technique — it was discovered by accident. Lazzara & Porter 2006, the origin paper for this entire cluster, describes how a 1991 prosthetic-component inventory gap at Implant Innovations forced many early wide-diameter (5.0/6.0 mm) implants to be restored with "standard" (4.1 mm) healing abutments and prosthetic components purely because matching-diameter parts were not yet available. Retrospective radiographic follow-up of these unintentionally platform-switched cases showed smaller-than-expected crestal bone loss compared to conventionally matched-diameter restorations — the observation that gave the technique its name and its first mechanistic hypothesis (IAJ repositioned inward and away from crestal bone). Everything downstream of that accidental finding is a textbook sequence rare enough to be worth naming explicitly: naming and hypothesis (Lazzara & Porter 2006) → mechanism elaborated on narrative/FEA grounds (Salimi 2011, Desai 2013) → individually tested in RCTs (Enkling 2013, Rocha 2016) → quantitatively confirmed by the first meta-analyses (Strietzel 2015, Di Girolamo 2016) → confirmed at long-term follow-up by an equivalence trial (Messias 2019) → reconfirmed by a newer, better-designed meta-analysis (Juan-Montesinos 2022). Each stage tested and did not overturn the prior stage's conclusion — a genuine convergence, not merely a repeated claim, but one that started as a byproduct of supply-chain constraints rather than deliberate design.

**Bottom line:** platform switching is a well-evidenced, low-risk technique choice for marginal bone preservation, with the strongest indications being esthetic-zone implants, narrow ridges (<3 mm), limited bone height, and short implants (Salimi 2011's original indication list, not contradicted by later literature). The absolute magnitude of benefit (0.2–0.5 mm) is statistically robust but clinically modest — within Albrektsson's <1.5 mm/year-1 success threshold either way — so PLS should be framed as an incremental bone-preservation refinement, not a decision that changes whether an implant succeeds.

## Offset Magnitude — How Much Mismatch Is Needed?

**[Added 2026-08-10, 4 papers]** Every study above compares PS (any offset) vs PM (zero offset) — none stratifies by *how much* offset. Four newly ingested papers close part of that gap by reporting the actual per-side horizontal mismatch (half the implant-minus-abutment diameter difference) tested in individual primary studies, rather than only the PS-vs-PM outcome number.

| Study | Per-side offset | Design | Bone outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[implants/mbl/hurzeler-2007-peri-implant-bone-level-platform-switched]] | 0.45 mm (5mm implant/4.1mm abutment) | prospective, non-randomized, n=14 vs 8 | PS -0.22mm vs non-PS -2.02mm at 1yr (P≤.0001); authors conclude 0.45mm/side "seems sufficient" |
| [[implants/mbl/rodriguez-ciurana-2009-interimplant-distance-platform-switched-bone-crest]] | 0.35–0.45 mm (4 diameter pairs) | prospective, n=37 patients | M 0.7±0.57mm, D 0.55±0.52mm (offset dataset, per Gupta 2019's table) |
| [[implants/mbl/canullo-2007-platform-switching-immediate-extraction-sockets]] | 1.0 mm (6mm implant/4mm abutment) — largest in this cluster | case series, n=9, uncontrolled | 0.78±0.36mm aggregate vs 1.7mm reference (P≤.005) |
| [[implants/mbl/gupta-2019-platform-switching-crestal-bone-loss-sr]] | compiles 9 studies, mostly 0.25–0.45mm/side | narrative review | all 9 favored PS; no dose-response analysis performed by the review itself |

**What this does and does not establish.** Hürzeler 2007's 0.45 mm/side "sufficient" conclusion is the closest thing in this wiki's evidence base to a stated minimum-effective threshold — but it is a single small (n=14 vs 8) non-randomized, single-offset, 1-year study; the claim was never dose-response tested against smaller offsets within that trial. Canullo & Rasperini 2007 shows an offset roughly double that (1.0mm/side) also worked, in an uncontrolled case series that confounds PS with immediate placement and immediate loading. Across the compiled studies, offsets from 0.15mm to 1.0mm all reportedly reduced bone loss relative to platform matching — but because no single study varied offset magnitude as its own independent variable, **the wiki still cannot state a validated minimum-effective mismatch or a dose-response curve.** The practical range actually used across implant systems and primary studies clusters at 0.25–0.45mm/side; 1.0mm is an outlier, not a typical design choice.

**A distinct, easy-to-conflate "distance" question**: [[implants/mbl/rodriguez-ciurana-2009-interimplant-distance-platform-switched-bone-crest]]'s primary finding is not abutment-offset magnitude but *inter-implant* spacing — 41 pairs of adjacent PS implants placed <3mm apart (closer than Tarnow et al. 2000's classic 3mm rule for non-PS implants) still preserved a measurable interproximal bone peak (0.24mm coronal to the imaginary IAJ-to-IAJ line), extending [[implants/mbl/tarnow-2000-inter-implant-distance-crestal-bone-height]]'s rule to platform-switched implants. This is a separate clinical question from "how much narrower should one abutment be" and should not be conflated with it.

## Mechanism — What Primary Sources Actually Show

**[Added 2026-08-15, 4 papers]** Until now this overview's mechanism account rested entirely on narrative reviews citing FEA and radiographic correlation secondhand. Four primary mechanistic studies now sit in the wiki, and they do not all support the standard story equally.

| Study | Type | Layer tested | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[implants/mbl/sahabi-2013-biomechanical-effects-platform-switching-two]] | in-vitro FEA (2 implant systems, 100N axial / 100N at 15°) | biomechanical | PS lowers peak von Mises stress in crestal cortical and cancellous bone in both systems; high-stress zone migrates from implant shoulder periphery toward implant center; **implant-abutment interface stress rises** but stays below yield strength |
| [[implants/mbl/yadav-2025-finite-element-analysis-platform-switching]] | in-vitro FEA (D2 mandible / D3 maxilla, 200N axial / 200N at 30°) | biomechanical | Same direction, wider conditions: PS reduces crestal bone stress especially under oblique loading, while **raising stress on implant, abutment, and abutment screw** — worst in maxilla under axial and mandible under oblique load |
| [[implants/mbl/alrabeah-2016-effect-platform-switching-metal-ion]] | in-vitro accelerated corrosion (42 specimens, ICP-MS, ISO 10271) | corrosion (new layer) | Platform-matched couples released ~2x more V, Al (Ti abutments) and Co, Cr, Mo (CoCr abutments), p<0.05; Co/Cr/Mo reduction **proportional to mismatch magnitude**; SEM showed pitting at all interfaces, worst at platform outer borders |
| [[implants/mbl/chien-2016-effect-platform-switching-periimplant-crevicular]] | prospective split-mouth, 14 patients, PICF cytokines at 1/2/4/6 wk | inflammatory | **Does not support the standard story**: profiles broadly similar; PD deeper in PM only at week 1; TNF-α ~3x *higher* in PS at week 1 (p=0.005), left unexplained by the authors |

**Reading these together.** The biomechanical layer is now the best-supported: two independent FEA studies, different implant systems, different bone qualities, different load angles, same directional result. The corrosion layer is new, single-study, in vitro, and mechanistically appealing precisely because its effect scales with mismatch magnitude — the one dose-response signal anywhere in this cluster, though it is a *corrosion* dose-response, not a bone-loss one. The inflammatory layer — historically the headline explanation, repeated in every narrative review here — is the layer with the only direct human biomarker measurement, and that measurement does not confirm it. This does not mean PLS fails to preserve bone; the clinical data are clear enough. It means **the wiki should stop stating the microgap-inflammation mechanism as settled fact** and present it as the least-verified of the three proposed pathways.

**The trade-off both FEA studies agree on.** Stress removed from crestal bone reappears on the prosthetic components. Neither study tested cyclic fatigue, screw loosening, or long-term component fracture, so the clinical significance is unknown — but it is a concrete, replicated prediction that this wiki holds no clinical data against. See Gaps.

## Clinical Decision Points

1. **Default to platform switching when the restorative system allows it** — the bone-preservation benefit is well-replicated (3 SR+MA, 4 RCTs favoring PS, 1 consensus report, 2 retrospective studies) and carries no survival penalty in any study this wiki holds. **[revised 2026-08-15]** One 6-month pilot RCT (Rossi 2015) found no benefit; this qualifies the strength of the recommendation but does not reverse it, given its short follow-up and small sample against three meta-analyses.
2. **Highest-value indications**: esthetic-zone implants (soft-tissue volume preservation), narrow ridges (<3 mm buccolingual), limited bone height, and short implants (Salimi 2011) — situations where every fraction of a millimeter of crestal bone matters most.
3. **Do not oversell the magnitude to patients** — the absolute MBL reduction (0.2–0.5 mm) is statistically robust but clinically modest; it is a refinement within already-successful outcomes (Albrektsson <1.5 mm/yr threshold), not a difference between success and failure.
4. **Watch the probing-depth signal** — Juan-Montesinos 2022's borderline PD increase is not yet a reason to avoid PLS, but is worth tracking as more evidence accumulates; do not treat it as settled either way.
5. **Immediate loading + PLS combination** — Salimi 2011 (2011 vintage) found evidence insufficient to recommend routinely; this wiki does not yet hold a dedicated synthesis of more recent immediate-loading-specific PLS evidence (see Gaps).
6. **[Added 2026-08-15] Do not assume the removed bone stress disappeared** — both FEA studies here show it transfers to the implant, abutment, and abutment screw, worst under oblique loading. In high-load posterior sites, steep cusp inclines, or parafunction, treat component selection (abutment material, screw specification, retrievability) as part of the platform-switching decision rather than an unrelated choice. No clinical data in this wiki quantifies this risk — this is a precaution derived from replicated in-vitro prediction, not a demonstrated failure mode.
7. **[Added 2026-08-15] Keep the vertical axis separate from the horizontal one** — Schwarz 2013's consensus concerns *where the microgap sits relative to the crest* (supracrestal machined collar showed less bone loss than subcrestal; a subcrestal microgap ≥1mm caused more remodeling but better rough-surface bone coverage). Platform switching is a horizontal-offset decision. The two interact but are separately decided, and evidence for one should not be cited for the other.
8. **[Added 2026-08-15] Expect less benefit where the baseline design is already good** — Rossi 2015's null result came from an implant whose standard configuration already stabilized bone ~1mm below the shoulder. Meta-analytic averages pool across implant designs and cannot show this; the marginal gain from PS is plausibly smaller on already-optimized platforms.

## Gaps & Future Research

- **[Partially closed 2026-08-10]** How much horizontal mismatch is needed was previously an unaddressed question — 4 newly ingested papers ([[implants/mbl/hurzeler-2007-peri-implant-bone-level-platform-switched]], [[implants/mbl/canullo-2007-platform-switching-immediate-extraction-sockets]], [[implants/mbl/rodriguez-ciurana-2009-interimplant-distance-platform-switched-bone-crest]], [[implants/mbl/gupta-2019-platform-switching-crestal-bone-loss-sr]]) now supply per-study offset dimensions (see § Offset Magnitude above) and one specific threshold claim (0.45mm/side, Hürzeler 2007). Still open: no study within this wiki varies offset magnitude as its own controlled independent variable, so a true dose-response curve or validated minimum-effective threshold remains unestablished.
- **[Partially closed 2026-08-15]** PLS combined with immediate *placement* now has one controlled data point: [[implants/mbl/attia-2025-influence-platform-switching-marginal-bone]] (retrospective, 37 patients, follow-up to 23 years) found significantly less mesial MBL with PS (0.26 vs 0.75mm, p=0.044) in immediately inserted implants. Still open: immediate *loading* specifically remains untested with adequate power — Salimi 2011 flagged this in 2011 and nothing here closes it. (Canullo & Rasperini 2007 combines PS + immediate placement + immediate loading in one uncontrolled case series, but cannot isolate PS's independent contribution.)
- **[New 2026-08-15] The mesial/distal asymmetry in Attia 2025 is unexplained** — PS advantage reached significance mesially but not distally, in a study small enough that this could be noise. No other paper in this cluster reports MBL split by surface, so it can be neither corroborated nor dismissed from what the wiki holds.
- **[New 2026-08-15] No clinical data tests the FEA-predicted component-stress trade-off.** Sahabi 2013 and Yadav 2025 both predict higher implant/abutment/screw stress under PS; neither tested fatigue, and no clinical study in this wiki reports screw loosening, abutment fracture, or component survival stratified by PS vs PM. This is the largest untested prediction in the cluster.
- **[New 2026-08-15] The inflammatory mechanism is contradicted by the only direct measurement held here.** Chien 2016's week-1 TNF-α elevation in PS (3x, p=0.005) is a single small split-mouth study and its authors offer no explanation, but no other paper in this wiki measures peri-implant cytokines under PS at all. Replication is needed before either accepting or discarding the microgap-inflammation account.
- **[New 2026-08-15] Rossi 2015's null result has not been reconciled.** No later paper in this wiki addresses why a well-conducted (if small, 6-month) RCT found nothing where meta-analyses find 0.25–0.5mm. The authors' own hypothesis — that an already-optimized baseline implant leaves no headroom — is untested.
- The probing-depth trade-off signal (Juan-Montesinos 2022) rests on one meta-analysis with a non-significant primary estimate; needs independent replication before treating it as a real cost of PLS.
- Mechanism is inferred from computational, corrosion, and radiographic evidence, not directly confirmed by histology in any paper this wiki holds; the corrosion pathway (Alrabeah 2016) is single-study and in vitro, and no study links measured ion release to measured bone loss in the same subjects.
- Heterogeneous MBL magnitude reporting across meta-analyses (0.255 mm to ~0.5 mm difference) makes it hard to state a single "expected benefit" number for patient counseling; use ranges, not point estimates.
