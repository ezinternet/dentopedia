## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 이 페이지는 즉시식립(immediate/Type 1 placement)의 4개 결정축 — 생존율·식립타이밍·감염소켓·부하/보철 — 을 미연결 8편으로 종합한 근거 허브다. 특화 결정은 기존 [[overviews/implant-placement-timing-immediate-early-delayed]]·[[overviews/immediate-implant-infected-sites-decision]]로 link-out.
> - **즉시 vs 지연 생존, 근거가 갈림 — 갈림의 원인은 연구설계**: Mello 2017(관찰연구 포함 30편, 3049 임플란트)은 즉시 95.21% vs 지연 98.38%로 **유의한 ~3%p 열세**(p=.001); García-Sánchez 2022(RCT 6편 head-to-head)는 **생존 유의차 없음** + 즉시가 1년 변연골·심미(PES)에서 오히려 우세. 약한 관찰연구를 넣으면 즉시가 불리해 보인다.
> - **절대 생존은 높음**: Lang 2012(전향 46편) 연간 실패율 0.82%, 2년 생존 98.4%. 즉시식립 자체는 고생존 술식.
> - **타이밍 프로토콜 우열 없음**: Pommer 2021(상악 단일치, ≥3년) — 식립×부하 타이밍 9조합 어디서도 장기 생존·변연골 유의차 없음(IPIL vs IPDL p=.306 등). 타이밍보다 **증례 선택·술기**가 결정적.
> - **변연골소실(MBL) 패턴**: 초기 리모델링 후 안정화. Zhang 2024 — 식립→보철 -0.74mm(p<0.05), 보철→5년 추가 -0.21mm(NS). 진행성이 아니라 조기집중형.
> - **감염소켓 즉시식립 — 실행 가능성 시사, 프로토콜급 아님**: Zhang 2024(만성 근단감염 구치, n=41) 5년 생존 97.6%; 그러나 Espada-Salgado 2026 scoping review는 근거가 증례보고 중심·비교연구 결여·감염분류/소독 프로토콜 미표준화라 **cautious**. 공통 필수요소는 무외상 발치·철저한 debridement·1차 안정성 확보.
> - **부하**: Azarias 2025(전악 즉시부하 리뷰 15편) 생존 >93%, MBL <2mm; 전악 즉시부하는 **중등도 1차안정성(토크 30–44 N·cm 또는 ISQ ≥60)** 으로 충분(단일치 즉시부하는 45 N·cm 권장). Bergkvist ISQ 50.6서도 98.2% — ISQ≥60 임계는 전악에선 보수적일 수 있음.
> - **보철재료는 저위험 변수**: Aldebes 2022(RCT, root-analogue zirconia) PFM vs 복합레진 크라운 간 변연골소실 무차이(전부 p>0.05).
> - **근거 한계**: 8편 중 5편이 초록전용/narrative — pooled 수치는 확정이 아닌 방향성. 가장 약한 지점이 하필 임상적으로 가장 중요한 곳(즉시 vs 지연 effect size, 감염소켓 프로토콜, 장기 데이터).
> - **임상 함의**: 즉시식립은 고생존이나 즉시-지연 격차는 작고 설계민감. 감염소켓은 무외상·debridement·1차안정성 3요소 하에 선택적 시행. 전악 즉시부하는 중등도 안정성으로 가능, 보철재료 선택은 골결과에 영향 없음.

## Three-line Summary

Evidence hub over 8 papers spanning immediate (Type 1) placement's four decision axes — survival, placement/loading timing, infected sockets, restoration material: **absolute immediate survival is high** (Lang 2012, 46 prospective studies: annual failure 0.82%, 2-yr survival 98.4%) but the **immediate-vs-delayed comparison is design-dependent** — Mello 2017 (30 studies incl. observational, 3,049 implants) finds a significant ~3-point survival disadvantage (95.21% vs 98.38%, p=.001) whereas García-Sánchez 2022 (6 head-to-head RCTs) finds no survival difference and immediate even favored on 1-yr MBL/PES (at ~2–3× complication rate).

Timing protocol shows no superior combination (Pommer 2021, maxillary single-tooth, all placement×loading comparisons NS), and marginal bone loss is early-then-stabilizing rather than progressive (Zhang 2024: placement→crown −0.74 mm p<0.05, crown→5 yr −0.21 mm NS); immediate placement into **infected sockets is feasibility-suggestive but not protocol-grade** — Zhang shows 97.6% 5-yr survival in chronic apically infected posterior sites, but Espada-Salgado 2026's scoping review cautions the base is case-report-dominated, non-comparative, and lacks any standardized infection-classification or decontamination protocol (shared essentials: atraumatic extraction, meticulous debridement, adequate primary stability).

On loading/restoration, full-arch immediate loading is high-survival (Azarias 2025: >93%, MBL <2 mm) with **moderate primary stability sufficing** (torque 30–44 N·cm or ISQ ≥60; single-implant immediate loading still wants ≥45 N·cm), and restoration material is a low-stakes variable (Aldebes 2022 RCT: PFM vs composite crowns, no marginal-bone-resorption difference, all p>0.05); five of eight sources are abstract-only or narrative, so read the pooled numbers as directional. Specialized decisions live in the linked timing and infected-site overviews.

## 세줄요약

즉시식립(Type 1)의 4개 결정축(생존·타이밍·감염소켓·부하/보철)을 8편으로 종합한 허브: 절대 생존은 높으나(Lang 2012 연간실패 0.82%, 2년 98.4%) 즉시 vs 지연 비교는 설계의존적 — Mello 2017(관찰포함 30편)은 즉시 95.21% vs 지연 98.38% 유의 열세(p=.001), García-Sánchez 2022(RCT 6편)는 생존 무차이 + 즉시가 1년 변연골·심미 우세.

타이밍 프로토콜 우열 없음(Pommer 2021 전 조합 NS), MBL은 조기집중 후 안정화(Zhang 식립→보철 -0.74mm, 이후 -0.21mm NS); 감염소켓 즉시식립은 실행가능성 시사(Zhang 5년 97.6%)이나 Espada-Salgado scoping은 증례중심·비교연구 결여·프로토콜 미표준화로 cautious — 공통 필수는 무외상발치·debridement·1차안정성.

전악 즉시부하 고생존(Azarias >93%, MBL<2mm)·중등도 안정성(토크 30–44 또는 ISQ≥60)으로 충분, 보철재료(PFM vs 복합레진)는 골결과 무차이(Aldebes RCT, p>0.05); 8편 중 5편 초록/narrative라 pooled 수치는 방향성으로 해석. 특화 결정은 링크된 타이밍·감염부위 오버뷰 참조.

## Summary

Immediate (Type 1) implant placement is one of the most litigated timing decisions in implantology. This page pulls 8 previously-unsynthesized papers into a single hub organized around the four questions a clinician actually asks: *Does immediate placement survive as well as delayed? Does the timing protocol matter? Can I place into an infected socket? How stable do I need primary stability to be, and does the crown material matter?* It links out to the specialized decision overviews rather than restating them.

The unifying insight: **immediate placement is a high-survival procedure in absolute terms, but the *comparative* immediate-vs-delayed gap is small and highly sensitive to which studies are pooled** — RCT-only reviews wash out the difference, broad observational-inclusive reviews reveal a modest survival penalty. Everything downstream (timing protocol, restoration material) is low-stakes; the genuinely under-evidenced frontier is infected-socket protocolization.

## Key Contributions

### 1. Survival: high absolute, design-dependent comparison

| Study | Design / n | Immediate survival | Comparison finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lang 2012 | SR, 46 prospective studies | 98.4% at 2 yr (annual failure 0.82%) | Non-comparative; antibiotics the one factor tied to ↓ failure |
| Mello 2017 | SR+MA, 30 studies / 3,049 implants (obs-inclusive) | 95.21% | **vs delayed 98.38%, p=.001** (significant penalty); MBL/ISQ/PPD all NS |
| García-Sánchez 2022 | SR+MA, 6 head-to-head RCTs | ~97% (single-arm pool) | **No survival difference**; immediate favored on 1-yr MBL & PES; ~2–3× complications |

The Mello↔García-Sánchez conflict is not a contradiction about biology — it is a difference in inclusion criteria. Restrict to RCTs and the gap vanishes; add weaker observational cohorts and a ~3-point penalty appears. Practically: immediate survival is high, the immediate-vs-delayed decision should be driven by site/soft-tissue and complication tolerance, not a survival cliff.

### 2. Timing protocol: no superior combination

Pommer 2021 cross-tabulated placement × loading timing for maxillary single-tooth esthetic-zone implants (≥3-yr) and found **no significant long-term survival or bone-remodeling difference across any tested combination** (IPIL vs IPDL p=.306; DPIL vs DPDL p=1.000; EPDL vs DPDL p=.600), nor any effect of one-abutment-one-time, flap design, or simultaneous augmentation. Case selection and technique outrank timing — though several comparisons were underpowered (only 3 of 9 combinations had ≥2 controlled trials).

### 3. Marginal bone loss: early-then-stabilizing

Zhang 2024 (5-yr, chronic apically infected posterior sites) shows the characteristic pattern: significant early remodeling placement→crown (−0.74 ± 1.03 mm, p<0.05) then a non-significant crawl crown→5 yr (−0.21 ± 0.70 mm, NS). Lang 2012 concurs: MBL mostly <1 mm, predominantly year 1; soft-tissue change concentrated in the first 3 months. Bone loss is not progressive — it is a front-loaded remodeling event.

### 4. Infected sockets: feasibility-suggestive, not protocol-grade

Two sources, deliberately different in tone:
- **Zhang 2024** — 5-yr clinical observation, 41 implants into chronic apically infected posterior sockets after thorough debridement: 97.6% survival, 1 peri-implantitis, 0 mechanical complications. Outcomes stable.
- **Espada-Salgado 2026** — PRISMA-ScR scoping review of 24 studies: outcomes "generally favorable" but the evidence is **case-report/series-dominated, non-comparative, with no standardized infection classification or decontamination protocol** across studies. It explicitly adopts a more cautious stance than earlier SR+MAs that called infected-socket IIP "safe with debridement + antibiotics."

Shared, consistently-reported essentials: atraumatic extraction, meticulous debridement, adequate primary stability. Catalogued adjuncts (antibiotics, CHX, grafts, membranes, PRF, laser decontamination) are descriptive, not comparatively validated.

### 5. Loading & restoration: moderate stability suffices, material is low-stakes

- **Azarias 2025** (narrative review, 15 full-arch immediate-loading studies): survival >93%, MBL <2 mm at 1 yr. Synthesized thresholds — immediate full-arch loading works with **moderate** primary stability (insertion torque 30–44 N·cm or ISQ ≥60), lower than the ≥45 N·cm typically asked for single immediately-loaded implants. Bergkvist 2009 loaded at mean ISQ 50.6 yet achieved 98.2% at 3 yr — the ISQ≥60 threshold may be conservative for full-arch splinted cases.
- **Aldebes 2022** (RCT, root-analogue zirconia, immediate flapless): crown material (PFM vs indirect composite) makes **no difference to marginal bone resorption** (all p>0.05; MBR 0.2–1.2 mm). Material choice is not a bone-level lever.

## Methodology

Narrative synthesis (no new pooling; heterogeneous designs). Base: 3 SR+MA (García-Sánchez, Mello, Pommer), 1 SR (Lang), 1 scoping review (Espada-Salgado), 1 narrative review (Azarias), 1 RCT (Aldebes), 1 5-yr clinical observation (Zhang). Five are abstract-only or narrative — directionality captured, some effect sizes not recoverable. All figures quoted verbatim from source pages.

## Results

**Chairside read:**

1. **Immediate survival is high** (~97–98% short-term); do not expect a large survival penalty vs delayed in well-selected cases — the penalty appears mainly when weaker observational data are pooled.
2. **No timing protocol is demonstrably superior** — choose by soft-tissue/esthetic and biological-width considerations, not a survival ranking.
3. **Expect front-loaded bone remodeling** (~0.5–0.75 mm to crown delivery), then stability; progressive loss is a red flag, not the norm.
4. **Infected sockets**: permissible in experienced hands with atraumatic extraction + meticulous debridement + adequate primary stability, but there is **no standardized protocol** — treat as case-by-case, higher-caution. See the infected-sites decision overview.
5. **Full-arch immediate loading**: moderate primary stability (torque 30–44 N·cm or ISQ ≥60) is adequate; **restoration material does not affect crestal bone** — decide it on esthetics/economics.
6. **Read the pooled numbers as directional** — 5/8 sources are abstract-only or narrative.
