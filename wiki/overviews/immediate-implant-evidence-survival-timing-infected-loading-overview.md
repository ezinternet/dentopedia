## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 이 페이지는 즉시식립(immediate/Type 1 placement)의 5개 결정축 — 생존율·식립타이밍·감염소켓·부하/보철·환자체감(PROM) — 을 27편으로 종합한 근거 허브다. 특화 결정은 기존 [[overviews/implant-placement-timing-immediate-early-delayed]]·[[overviews/immediate-implant-infected-sites-decision]]로 link-out.
> - **생존율의 새 기준점 = Gallucci 2026**(PROSPERO 갱신 SR, 140편·임플란트 10,456개, 2024.1까지): 식립×부하 9조합 전부의 가중누적생존율 산출 — Type 1A(즉시식립+즉시부하) 98.0%(SCV, 검증등급 최상), Type 1C(즉시식립+통상부하) 95.0%(SCV) 인 반면 **Type 1B(즉시식립+조기부하)는 91.6%(CD, 검증 미흡)** — 같은 즉시식립이라도 부하 타이밍에 따라 손실률이 **거의 4배** 갈린다(1A 2.0% vs 1B 8.4% 손실). 전신 Gallucci 2018(69편, 이 논문의 전신·직접 supersede 대상)이 확립한 Type1–4×A–C 분류체계 위에서 갱신된 수치.
> - **즉시 vs 지연 생존 — 갈림은 해소됨(Mello 2017 superseded)**: Mello 2017은 **García-Sánchez 2022에 의해 완전 대체(full supersession, 2026-08-11)**. García-Sánchez 2022(RCT 6편 head-to-head)가 현행 기준 — **생존 유의차 없음** + 즉시가 1년 변연골·PES 우세. Mello 2017(관찰포함 30편)의 ~3%p 열세(p=.001)는 약한 관찰연구의 설계 편향이었으며, 방법론적 교훈으로만 남음.
> - **해소는 독립 SR+MA 2편이 받친다**: Patel 2023(비교연구 10편 = RCT 6 + 비무작위 4, 즉시 341 vs 지연 359 임플란트)이 별도 검색·별도 저자진으로 **위험비 0.99(95% CI 0.96–1.02, p=0.44), I²=0%**, 총생존 즉시 97.4% vs 지연 97.5%를 냈다 — **이질성 0%**라 "무차이"가 통계적 검출력 부족이 아니라 연구 간 일관된 동등임을 뜻한다. Mello 2017의 열세를 재현하지 못한 SR+MA가 이제 둘이며, 두 SR+MA 모두 "생존율은 즉시 vs 지연의 결정 인자가 아니다"로 수렴한다.
> - **같은 갈등이 단일 코호트 내부에서도 재현된다**: Felice 2016/Esposito 2017(동일 210명 3군 RCT, 4개월/1년 보고)은 즉시·즉시지연(6주) 배치가 지연(4개월) 대비 변연골·PES **유의 우위**를 보이면서 동시에 실패율은 **비유의하게 더 높은** 경향(6.0%/6.2% vs 1.6%, P=.369, 4개월→1년 신규 실패 0건으로 안정) — "즉시식립은 골·심미는 이기고 생존은 공짜가 아닐 수 있다"는 트레이드오프가 관찰연구 간 비교가 아니라 **하나의 RCT 안에서도** 나타난다.
> - **절대 생존은 높음** *(Lang 2012, Gallucci 2026으로 superseded — 역사적 기준선)*: Lang 2012(전향 46편) 연간 실패율 0.82%, 2년 생존 98.4%. 즉시식립 자체는 고생존 술식. 현행 생존율 기준은 Gallucci 2026(140편, 10,456개)의 가중 누적생존율로 대체됨.
> - **부위·직경이 방향을 뒤집을 수 있다**: Checchi 2017(구치부 RCT, n=100, 광경직경 6.0–8.0mm 즉시 vs 치조제보존+지연 통상직경)은 **지연군이 심미(PES 9.71 vs 10.86, P=.02)·변연골(1.06 vs 0.63mm, P<.0001) 모두 유의 우위** — 전치부 단일치 RCT(Puisys 2022: PES 12.8 vs 12.5 NS, 협측점막 즉시군 소폭 우세 P=.047)와 정반대 방향. 같은 "즉시식립" 문헌이라도 전치 표준직경과 구치 광경직경은 다른 결론.
> - **타이밍 프로토콜 우열 없음**: Pommer 2021(상악 단일치, ≥3년) — 식립×부하 타이밍 9조합 어디서도 장기 생존·변연골 유의차 없음(IPIL vs IPDL p=.306 등). Puisys 2022(전치부 RCT, n=50)도 즉시 vs 조기 배치 간 PES·치조정골 무차이 재확인, 대신 즉시군 의자시간 ~50% 단축(127 vs 259분, P<.001). 타이밍보다 **증례 선택·술기**가 결정적.
> - **변연골소실(MBL) 패턴**: 초기 리모델링 후 안정화. Zhang 2024 — 식립→보철 -0.74mm(p<0.05), 보철→5년 추가 -0.21mm(NS). 진행성이 아니라 조기집중형.
> - **감염소켓 즉시식립 — 실행 가능성 시사, 프로토콜급 아님**: Zhang 2024(만성 근단감염 구치, n=41) 5년 생존 97.6%; Elaskary 2024(VST + 6-day 소독 프로토콜, 감염·비감염 type II 상악 소켓 n=41임플란트) **100% 1년 생존, 양군 협측골·점막 동등** — 감염 소켓도 적절히 소독하면 비감염 대비 비열등. Pranckeviciene 2024 SR+MA(109편 검색, 22편 포함)도 동일 결론: 항생제+소파 동반 시 치근단 병변 부위 생존율이 비감염 부위와 동등, 화농성 급성 감염·동 천공은 금기 유지. **Amato 2025**(Tarnow group, 후향적 코호트 n=127명·143 임플란트, 심미부위 단일치 즉시식립+즉시 프로비저널, 평균 7년·최대 12년 추적) — 비감염 97.8%·급성감염 96.4%·만성감염 95%, p=0.8; **현행 감염소켓 단일치 비교 연구 중 최장기 추적** — 감염 여부가 생존을 예측하지 않는다는 결론을 최대 12년까지 연장함. Ickroth 2025 SR(20연구, 525 임플란트)은 비온전 소켓도 협측골 두께·PES·생존율이 온전 소켓과 유사함을 확인하나 근거수준 매우 낮음(비온전 RCT 2편, 고위험 비뚤림). 그러나 Espada-Salgado 2026 scoping review는 증례보고 중심·비교연구 결여·감염분류/소독 프로토콜 미표준화라 **cautious**. 공통 필수요소: 무외상 발치·철저한 debridement·1차 안정성 확보.
> - **부하**: Azarias 2025(전악 즉시부하 리뷰 15편) 생존 >93%, MBL <2mm; 전악 즉시부하는 **중등도 1차안정성(토크 30–44 N·cm 또는 ISQ ≥60)** 으로 충분(단일치 즉시부하는 45 N·cm 권장). Bergkvist ISQ 50.6서도 98.2% — ISQ≥60 임계는 전악에선 보수적일 수 있음. Shibly 2010 짝논문 2편(치주질환 병력 환자, n=60, RCT)은 **즉시부하 vs 관행(3개월지연)부하** 자체를 무작위배정해 생존·골신득 무유의차(각 p>0.5) + 즉시부하군이 상피부착부(MGJ) 관상변위 유의하게 적음(15% vs 65%) — 부하 타이밍은 배치 타이밍과 별개 변수이며 최소한 이환 환자군에선 즉시부하가 연조직 위치보존에 유리.
> - **PROM — SR 기반 "무차이"와 RCT 기반 "즉시 우위" 공존**: Gotfredsen 2021(SR, 35–40편)·Huynh-Ba 2018(SR, 9편) — 단일치·단축결손에서는 배치·부하 타이밍이 불편감·만족도에 강한 영향 없음; 전악 무치악에서만 즉시부하에 대한 **약한·일시적**(≤1년) 선호 신호, 12개월엔 희미해짐. **Pannuti 2026 SR**(5개 전향 연구, 무치악 상악, 121명·724 임플란트, 2014–2024): VAS가 80% 연구에서 7개 PRO를 측정하는 지배적 도구, 12개 임상가보고결과(Clinician-Reported Outcome, ClinRO)는 연구 간 표준화 전무 — **부하 타이밍 간 PROM 비교는 현재 신뢰할 수 없음을 방법론적으로 확인**; "무차이" 결론이 진정한 동등인지 측정 이질성의 산물인지 구분 불가. 그러나 Yang 2026 RCT(n=220, 1년)는 즉시 임플란트군이 OHIP-14(3.20 vs 4.15, P<0.001)·PES(12.18 vs 11.34, P<0.001)·WES(7.78 vs 7.23, P<0.001)·환자 만족도(부착·색조·저작 전 영역) 모두에서 지연군보다 유의하게 우수 — 단일기관(중국)·1년 추적·MBL 미보고 한계 있음. SR 수준의 "타이밍 무차이" 결론은 유효하나, 충분히 좋은 후보에서 즉시식립이 환자 체감 삶의 질을 실제로 개선할 가능성을 이 RCT가 시사한다. 임상 근거(골·생존)가 타이밍 결정의 주 동력이어야 하며 환자 선호가 결정을 주도해서는 안 됨은 변함없다.
> - **보철재료는 저위험 변수**: Aldebes 2022(RCT, root-analogue zirconia) PFM vs 복합레진 크라운 간 변연골소실 무차이(전부 p>0.05).
> - **치주질환 환자 즉시식립(Li 2025, 후향적 n=95, 234 임플란트, 12개월)**: 전체 생존 97.86%; Stage IV·Grade C에서 생존율 유의 저하(Kaplan-Meier, p<0.05) — 치주염 중증도 자체가 즉시식립 예후에 영향을 미침. 치근단 감염과 구분되는 치주 감염 특이 데이터.
> - **구치부 즉시식립 후 early vs conventional 부하(Botros 2025, RCT, n=30 임플란트)**: 완전 가이드 플랩리스 즉시 구치부 임플란트에서 6주 early vs 3개월 conventional 부하 — 양군 생존율 100%, ISQ/DCA 무차이(p>0.05); 구치부에서도 early loading이 안전함을 RCT로 확인.
> - **당뇨 환자 즉시식립(Li 2026, SR+MA, 10편, 1,350명)**: 혈당 조절 여부와 무관하게 즉시식립 생존율 무차이(well-controlled RR=1.00, poorly-controlled RR=0.96, 양쪽 NS); 단 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)·탐침 출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP)은 당뇨군에서 유의하게 높고 혈당 조절 불량할수록 악화(MBL MD=0.39mm, p<0.00001) — 즉시식립 가능하나 철저한 혈당 관리와 장기 monitoring 필수.
> - **근거 한계**: 20편 중 다수가 초록전용/narrative(특히 이탈리아 컨소시엄 RCT 3편은 abstract-only) — pooled 수치는 확정이 아닌 방향성. 가장 약한 지점이 하필 임상적으로 가장 중요한 곳(즉시 vs 지연 effect size, 감염소켓 프로토콜, 장기 데이터).
> - **임상 함의**: 생존율 논쟁의 실무 기준은 이제 Gallucci 2026 — Type 1A/1C는 검증된 고생존, **Type 1B(즉시식립+조기부하)는 피하거나 신중히**. 부위 특이성 확인(전치부 표준직경 vs 구치부 광경직경은 반대 결론). 감염소켓은 무외상·debridement·1차안정성 3요소 하에 선택적 시행. 전악 즉시부하는 중등도 안정성으로 가능, 부하 프로토콜은 배치 타이밍과 별개로 판단, 보철재료 선택은 골결과에 영향 없음, 환자 선호는 결정을 주도하지 않는다.

## Three-line Summary

Evidence hub over 27 papers spanning immediate (Type 1) placement's five decision axes — survival, placement/loading timing, infected sockets, loading/restoration, and patient-reported experience: the current reference for the survival question is Gallucci 2026 (PROSPERO-updated SR, 140 studies, 10,456 implants), whose 9-combination weighted-survival/validation-tier table shows Type 1A (immediate placement + immediate loading) at a validated 98.0% but **Type 1B (immediate placement + early loading) at only 91.6%, still under-validated** — roughly a 4-fold difference in implant loss hiding inside "immediate placement" depending on loading timing alone.

The immediate-vs-delayed survival conflict is now resolved, and by two independent SR+MAs rather than one: Mello 2017 (30-study, observational-inclusive, ~3-point penalty) has been **fully superseded by García-Sánchez 2022** (August 2026) — restrict to RCTs and the penalty disappears — while Patel 2023 reaches the same verdict from a separate search and author group (10 comparative studies, RR 0.99, 95% CI 0.96–1.02, **I²=0%**, 97.4% vs 97.5%), the zero heterogeneity meaning this is consistent equivalence rather than an underpowered null; Mello 2017 survives only as a design-sensitivity lesson, and the same trade-off shape reappears **within a single 210-patient 3-arm RCT** (Felice 2016 / Esposito 2017): immediate and immediate-delayed (6-week) placement win significantly on bone level and PES over delayed (4-month) placement, while showing a non-significant but persistent higher failure trend (6.0–6.2% vs 1.6%, stable from 4 months to 1 year). Site and diameter can flip the esthetic direction entirely — Checchi 2017's molar, wide-diameter immediate RCT loses decisively on PES and marginal bone to delayed ridge-preservation placement, the mirror image of Puisys 2022's anterior single-tooth RCT (comparable PES, small mucosal gain for immediate, ~50% less chair time).

Loading protocol is a variable distinct from placement timing — Shibly 2010's paired RCTs (periodontally compromised patients) randomize immediate vs conventional *loading* of already-immediately-placed implants and find equivalent survival/bone with better mucogingival preservation for immediate loading — while patients themselves barely notice timing at all (Gotfredsen 2021, Huynh-Ba 2018: no strong PROM difference for single-tooth cases, only a weak transient full-arch preference for immediate loading that fades by 12 months); restoration material remains low-stakes (Aldebes 2022 RCT, all p>0.05). A large share of the newer RCT evidence (the Italian-consortium trials) is abstract-only, so read pooled effect sizes as directional.

## 세줄요약

즉시식립(Type 1)의 5개 결정축(생존·타이밍·감염소켓·부하/보철·환자체감)을 27편으로 종합한 허브: 생존율의 새 기준은 Gallucci 2026(PROSPERO 갱신 SR, 140편·10,456임플란트) — 9조합 가중생존율에서 Type 1A(즉시+즉시부하) 98.0%(검증됨) 대비 **Type 1B(즉시+조기부하) 91.6%(미검증)**로 손실률 약 4배 차이.

즉시 vs 지연 생존 갈등은 해소: Mello 2017(관찰포함 30편 ~3%p 열세)은 **García-Sánchez 2022에 의해 완전 superseded**(2026-08) — RCT만 보면 생존 무차이이고 설계 편향이 원인이며, 독립 SR+MA인 Patel 2023(비교연구 10편, 위험비 0.99, I²=0%, 97.4% vs 97.5%)이 이질성 0%로 같은 결론을 재확인한다. 동일한 트레이드오프(골·PES 우세, 실패율 비유의 증가)가 **하나의 210명 3군 RCT 내부**(Felice 2016/Esposito 2017)에서도 재현 — 즉시·즉시지연이 골·PES는 유의 우위이나 실패율은 비유의하게 더 높은 경향(4개월→1년 안정). 부위·직경이 방향을 뒤집기도 함 — Checchi 2017(구치·광경직경)은 지연군이 PES·변연골 모두 우위(전치부 Puisys 2022와 정반대).

부하 프로토콜은 배치 타이밍과 별개 변수(Shibly 2010 짝RCT: 즉시부하 vs 관행부하 생존·골 무차이+MGJ 보존 우위); 환자는 타이밍을 거의 못 느낌(Gotfredsen 2021·Huynh-Ba 2018 PROM SR, 전악만 약한 일시적 즉시부하 선호); 보철재료(PFM vs 복합레진)는 골결과 무차이. 이탈리아 컨소시엄 RCT 다수가 초록전용이라 pooled 수치는 방향성으로 해석. 특화 결정은 링크된 타이밍·감염부위 오버뷰 참조.

## Summary

Immediate (Type 1) implant placement is one of the most litigated timing decisions in implantology. This page pulls 20 papers into a single hub organized around the five questions a clinician actually asks: *Does immediate placement survive as well as delayed? Does the timing protocol — or the loading protocol, which is a separate variable — matter? Can I place into an infected socket? How stable do I need primary stability to be, and does the crown material matter? Does any of this matter to the patient?* It links out to the specialized decision overviews rather than restating them.

The unifying insight has sharpened since this page was first written: **immediate placement is a high-survival procedure in absolute terms, but the comparative gap is not one number — it depends on which loading protocol is paired with it, which site/diameter is involved, and which studies are pooled.** Gallucci 2026's 9-combination validated survival table now supplies the reference point: Type 1A (immediate placement + immediate loading) is a validated 98.0%, but Type 1B (immediate placement + *early* loading) sits at only 91.6% and remains under-validated — a nearly 4-fold difference in implant loss hiding inside the single label "immediate placement." The same trade-off shape (better bone/esthetics, non-significantly worse survival) that made the Mello-vs-García-Sánchez comparison look like a genuine conflict reappears **inside a single well-designed 3-arm RCT** (Felice 2016/Esposito 2017), which is the strongest evidence yet that this is a real, reproducible tension and not a pooling artifact — though it never reaches significance at n=210. Site and implant diameter can override everything else: Checchi 2017's molar/wide-diameter trial reverses the esthetic advantage that anterior-zone trials (Puisys 2022) find for immediate placement. Everything downstream of survival (restoration material, patient perception) is genuinely low-stakes; the remaining under-evidenced frontier is infected-socket protocolization.

Gallucci 2026 reframes the whole axis: it is no longer useful to ask "does immediate placement survive as well as delayed" as a single question, because within "immediate placement" the loading protocol alone produces a difference (Type 1A 98.0% vs Type 1B 91.6%) comparable in size to the immediate-vs-delayed gap Mello 2017 found. The Mello↔García-Sánchez conflict is now **resolved**: Mello 2017 was fully superseded by García-Sánchez 2022 (August 2026) — the ~3-point penalty was observational inclusion bias, and Mello 2017 survives only as a design-sensitivity lesson. The resolution does not rest on a single review: Patel 2023, a separate SR+MA with its own search window (2014–2022) and author group, pools 10 comparative studies to RR 0.99 (95% CI 0.96–1.02) at **I²=0%** — and because it deliberately includes non-randomised comparative studies alongside RCTs, it is not vulnerable to the objection that García-Sánchez's null is an artifact of RCT-only restriction. Two independent reviews failing to reproduce Mello's penalty, one of them with zero between-study heterogeneity, is what moves this from "contested" to "settled for practical purposes." Felice 2016/Esposito 2017 shows the same trade-off *shape* — better bone/esthetics, a non-significant excess of failures — reproducing **inside a single 210-patient RCT**, which is stronger evidence that the trade-off is real (even if this trial alone is underpowered to confirm it statistically) than a cross-study pooling artifact would be. Practically: immediate survival is high in the validated protocols (1A, 1C), the loading protocol chosen alongside placement matters as much as the placement decision itself (avoid Type 1B/early loading of immediately-placed implants without strong justification), and site/diameter can override the whole picture — Checchi 2017's molar/wide-diameter reversal is the clearest evidence that this isn't a single-axis decision.

### 2. Timing protocol: no superior combination, confirmed again in the esthetic zone

Pommer 2021 cross-tabulated placement × loading timing for maxillary single-tooth esthetic-zone implants (≥3-yr) and found **no significant long-term survival or bone-remodeling difference across any tested combination** (IPIL vs IPDL p=.306; DPIL vs DPDL p=1.000; EPDL vs DPDL p=.600), nor any effect of one-abutment-one-time, flap design, or simultaneous augmentation. Case selection and technique outrank timing — though several comparisons were underpowered (only 3 of 9 combinations had ≥2 controlled trials).

**Qian 2024** (SR+NMA, Semmelweis group; PRISMA-NMA; Bayesian/frequentist, SUCRA ranking) specifically addressed the anterior maxillary single-tooth context — the highest-stakes esthetic zone — and found that **Type 1A and Type 1B both achieve equivalent survival, MBL, PES, and patient satisfaction to conventional protocols when cases are appropriately selected**, but Type 1A carries higher operator- and case-selection dependency. For clinicians who want the most conservative default, Type 2/3 + conventional or early loading is the safest general recommendation, and **Type 1A should be reserved for cases fully meeting the Hamilton 2023 ITI selection criteria**. This NMA adds resolution to the Pommer 2021 "no superior combination" finding: it is not that timing doesn't matter, but that the correct protocol matches the socket/patient profile — with immediate placement being justified and equivalent only when prerequisites are met.

Puisys 2022 (RCT, n=50, maxillary anterior, intact walls) independently confirms the same null-difference pattern at the individual-trial level: immediate placement + immediate provisionalization vs early placement + GBR + delayed loading produced statistically equivalent 1-year PES (12.8 vs 12.5, p=.362) and equivalent crestal bone change at both mesial and distal sites (p>.05); the only significant difference was a small mid-buccal mucosal *gain* for immediate placement (+0.2 mm, p=.047) — directional, not a recession risk. The clinically decisive difference in this trial was operational, not biological: immediate placement cut total chair time roughly in half (127 vs 259 min, p<.001). Read together with Checchi 2017 (Key Contribution 1), the pattern is that anterior single-tooth, intact-wall cases show timing-agnostic esthetics with an efficiency edge for immediate, while molar/wide-diameter cases show a real esthetic and bone-level cost to immediate placement — the site and implant selection, not a universal timing rule, determines which pattern applies.

### 3. Marginal bone loss: early-then-stabilizing

Zhang 2024 (5-yr, chronic apically infected posterior sites) shows the characteristic pattern: significant early remodeling placement→crown (−0.74 ± 1.03 mm, p<0.05) then a non-significant crawl crown→5 yr (−0.21 ± 0.70 mm, NS). Lang 2012 concurs: MBL mostly <1 mm, predominantly year 1; soft-tissue change concentrated in the first 3 months. Bone loss is not progressive — it is a front-loaded remodeling event.

### 4. Infected sockets: feasibility-suggestive, not protocol-grade

Five sources, spanning a range of designs:
- **Zhang 2024** — 5-yr clinical observation, 41 implants into chronic apically infected posterior sockets after thorough debridement: 97.6% survival, 1 peri-implantitis, 0 mechanical complications. Outcomes stable.
- **Elaskary 2024** — 1-yr prospective cohort (n=41 implants): VST (Vestibular Socket Therapy) + 6-day decontamination protocol (metronidazole irrigation + temporary root reimplantation) in infected vs non-infected Elian type II maxillary sockets. **100% survival in both groups**; facial bone thickness gain significant in both; no between-group difference in bone or soft-tissue levels. Provides a structured presurgical protocol (the 6-day window) rather than post-extraction-immediate debridement alone. Single-operator, small sample (n=26), 1-year follow-up limit.
- **Pranckeviciene 2024** (SR+MA, 109 publications screened, 22 included) — compares IIP into sockets **with vs without periapical pathology**: with meticulous debridement + antibiotic prophylaxis, survival in the pathology group was **comparable to the non-infected group**. Acute infection with purulent exudate or sinus perforation remains a contraindication. The traditional view of periapical pathology as an *absolute* contraindication is not supported when careful protocols are followed; the conclusion mirrors Zhang 2024 and Elaskary 2024 but is now backed by a pooled SR+MA. Methodological heterogeneity across included studies limits certainty.
- **Amato 2025** (Retrospective cohort; Amato/Tarnow group; JOMI 2025; n=127 patients, 143 single-tooth implants; mean 7-year follow-up, range 2–12 years; esthetic zone IIP + immediate provisionalization; 3 groups: non-infected n=47, acute infected n=56, chronic infected n=40): Survival — non-infected 97.8%, acute 96.4%, chronic 95.0%; overall 96.5% (5 failures); **no significant between-group difference (p=0.8)**. The longest-follow-up dataset currently available for the direct acute/chronic infected vs non-infected single-tooth comparison in the esthetic zone; reinforces Pranckeviciene 2024's SR-level finding that infection status does not independently predict failure with appropriate protocols, now over a 12-year horizon.
- **Ickroth 2025** (SR, 20 studies, 525 implants) — compares IIP into **intact vs non-intact alveolar sockets** (i.e., socket with buccal wall defects, not just apical pathology): buccal bone thickness (1.10–3.18 mm intact vs 1.18–3.00 mm non-intact), soft-tissue changes, PES, and survival (>90–100% both groups) were all **comparable**. Only 2 RCTs available for non-intact sockets, both at high risk of bias — meta-analysis not possible. **Evidence quality is very low; extends cautious optimism to compromised socket anatomy but urgently needs high-quality RCTs.**
- **Espada-Salgado 2026** — PRISMA-ScR scoping review of 24 studies: outcomes "generally favorable" but the evidence is **case-report/series-dominated, non-comparative, with no standardized infection classification or decontamination protocol** across studies. It explicitly adopts a more cautious stance than earlier SR+MAs that called infected-socket IIP "safe with debridement + antibiotics."

The convergence across these five sources — two prospective cohorts, one SR+MA, one SR, one scoping review — provides a consistent feasibility signal, but the evidence tier (low quality, non-standardized protocols) is firm. Shared, consistently-reported essentials: atraumatic extraction, meticulous debridement, adequate primary stability. Catalogued adjuncts (antibiotics, CHX, grafts, membranes, PRF, laser decontamination) are descriptive, not comparatively validated.

### 5. Loading & restoration: moderate stability suffices, loading protocol is separable from placement timing, material is low-stakes

- **Azarias 2025** (narrative review, 15 full-arch immediate-loading studies): survival >93%, MBL <2 mm at 1 yr. Synthesized thresholds — immediate full-arch loading works with **moderate** primary stability (insertion torque 30–44 N·cm or ISQ ≥60), lower than the ≥45 N·cm typically asked for single immediately-loaded implants. Bergkvist 2009 loaded at mean ISQ 50.6 yet achieved 98.2% at 3 yr — the ISQ≥60 threshold may be conservative for full-arch splinted cases.
- **Shibly 2010 (paired RCTs, n=60, periodontally compromised patients)** isolate *loading* protocol as a variable independent of *placement* timing — both papers start from implants already immediately placed into extraction sockets (with allograft + collagen membrane) and randomize only the loading protocol: immediate provisional loading vs conventional loading after 3 months submerged healing. At 1 year: survival 96.6% vs 93.3% (NS, p>0.5), bone gain 0.99 vs 0.75 mm (NS); at 2 years (companion paper, same/overlapping cohort): survival 96.7% vs 93.3%, bone gain 1.19 vs 1.00 mm (NS), papilla index equivalent. The one significant difference across both papers is soft-tissue: mucogingival junction coronal displacement was far more common with conventional (submerged) loading than immediate (transmucosal) loading (65% vs 15% of sites) — plausibly because the submerged protocol's flap/primary-closure step, absent from the transmucosal immediate protocol, drags the MGJ coronally. This is independent evidence, in a comorbid population, that immediate loading is not merely non-inferior but soft-tissue-favorable when the surgeon has already committed to immediate placement — a distinct decision layer from "should I place immediately at all."
- **Aldebes 2022** (RCT, root-analogue zirconia, immediate flapless): crown material (PFM vs indirect composite) makes **no difference to marginal bone resorption** (all p>0.05; MBR 0.2–1.2 mm). Material choice is not a bone-level lever.

### 6. Patient-reported outcomes: timing barely registers, except a fading full-arch signal

Every axis above is a clinician-measured endpoint (survival, MBL, PES). Two systematic reviews ask the question none of the others do: does the patient notice or care about any of this?

- **Huynh-Ba 2018** (SR, PROSPERO #49604, 9 studies of Type 1 single-tooth implants; only 3 with comparative data): patient satisfaction was **uniformly high regardless of loading protocol**, with little difference between immediate and early/conventional loading; qualitative evidence pointed to a positive OHRQoL impact from immediate placement/loading, but instrument heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis or a firm protocol preference.
- **Gotfredsen 2021** (SR, 35–40 RCTs/prospective cohorts, broader population from single-tooth through full-arch edentulous) reaches the same null for single-tooth/short-span cases — no strong evidence that placement or loading timing affects discomfort or satisfaction — but isolates a population-specific signal Huynh-Ba's narrower scope could not detect: in **full-arch edentulous** reconstructions, patients show a modest preference for immediate loading over early/conventional loading at ≤1-year follow-up, which becomes statistically unclear by 12 months.

**Pannuti 2026** (SR, PROSPERO, PRISMA 2020; 5 prospective studies = 4 RCTs + 1 non-RCT; 121 patients, 724 implants; edentulous maxilla; search Jan 2014–May 2024) does not evaluate what patients *feel* but rather maps the **instruments** used to compare loading protocols — raising a prior methodological question. VAS dominated patient-reported outcomes (80% of studies), measuring 7 PROs (pain, satisfaction, comfort, speech, mastication, esthetics, self-esteem); supplemented by OHIP-19/20 and McGill Pain Questionnaire. Twelve clinician-reported outcomes were identified across 4 studies with no inter-study consensus on instruments or assessor calibration. The take-away: **cross-study comparison of loading protocols on patient-centered grounds is currently unreliable** — the "no strong PROM difference" signal in Gotfredsen 2021 and Huynh-Ba 2018 may partly reflect measurement heterogeneity rather than true patient-level equivalence. Standardized PRO/CRO measures for maxillary loading-timing trials are needed before pooled PROM conclusions can be trusted.

**Yang 2026** (RCT, n=220, China, 1-year) introduces a discordant note: unlike the SR findings above, this single parallel-group RCT found that **immediate placement was significantly superior across all patient-reported measures** — OHIP-14 (3.20 vs 4.15, P<.001), PES (12.18 vs 11.34, P<.001), WES (7.78 vs 7.23, P<.001), and VAS satisfaction across attachment level, colour, and chewing function — alongside better peri-implant health (PD, mPLI, mSBI; all P<.05). Implant survival was equivalent (95.45% vs 92.73%, P=.391). The divergence from the SR-level null result may reflect a genuine near-term patient-experience advantage for immediate placement in well-selected anterior cases, or may reflect single-site design and 1-year follow-up limitations (MBL not reported). Either way, the conclusion that timing *cannot* influence patient experience is harder to hold than it was before Yang 2026.

Read together, the PROM evidence converges with the clinical evidence on where timing decisions should be made: for single-tooth and short-span cases (the majority of this overview's scope), neither survival, bone level, nor patient experience differs meaningfully enough across timing protocols to make timing the primary decision driver in the SR literature, though Yang 2026 raises the possibility that in favorable anterior cases immediate placement confers a real patient-experience advantage — site anatomy, soft-tissue phenotype, and infection status (Key Contributions 1 and 4) should drive the choice instead. The one population where patients do notice — full-arch immediate loading — is also the one population where Azarias 2025 (Key Contribution 5) independently shows a favorable, high-survival clinical profile, so patient preference and clinical evidence point the same direction there rather than conflicting.

## Methodology

Narrative synthesis (no new pooling; heterogeneous designs). Base (27): 1 NMA (Qian 2024 — anterior maxillary placement × loading), 8 SR/SR+MA/SRs on survival, timing, and infected sockets (García-Sánchez, Patel, Mello [superseded], Pommer, Lang [superseded], Gallucci 2026, Gallucci 2018, Pranckeviciene 2024, Ickroth 2025), 2 further SRs on patient-reported outcomes (Gotfredsen, Huynh-Ba), 1 scoping review (Espada-Salgado), 2 narrative reviews (Azarias, Thoma 2026), 8 RCTs (Aldebes, Puisys, Checchi, Felice/Esposito — same cohort at two timepoints — Shibly ×2, Yang 2026), 2 prospective clinical cohorts (Zhang, Elaskary). A substantial share — particularly the Italian-consortium RCTs (Felice, Esposito, Checchi) — are abstract-only; directionality is captured but some effect sizes are not independently recoverable. ## Results

**Chairside read:**

1. **Immediate survival is high in the validated protocols** (Type 1A/1C, Gallucci 2026) — do not expect a large survival penalty vs delayed in well-selected cases; but **Type 1B (immediate placement + early loading) is the weak point** (91.6%, under-validated) — avoid pairing immediate placement with early loading unless there is a specific reason to.
2. **No timing protocol is demonstrably superior in anterior single-tooth cases** — choose by soft-tissue/esthetic and biological-width considerations, not a survival ranking; Puisys 2022 confirms this at the individual-RCT level and adds a ~50% chair-time argument for immediate.
3. **Site and diameter can override the timing decision entirely** — Checchi 2017's molar/wide-diameter trial shows delayed placement winning on both esthetics and bone; do not generalize anterior single-tooth timing conclusions to molar/wide-diameter cases.
4. **Expect front-loaded bone remodeling** (~0.5–0.75 mm to crown delivery), then stability; progressive loss is a red flag, not the norm.
5. **Infected sockets**: permissible in experienced hands with atraumatic extraction + meticulous debridement + adequate primary stability, but there is **no standardized protocol** — treat as case-by-case, higher-caution. See the infected-sites decision overview.
6. **Loading protocol is a separate decision from placement timing** — once immediate placement is chosen, immediate loading (vs conventional) does not cost survival or bone and preserves mucogingival position better in at least one comorbid (periodontally compromised) population (Shibly 2010).
7. **Full-arch immediate loading**: moderate primary stability (torque 30–44 N·cm or ISQ ≥60) is adequate; **restoration material does not affect crestal bone** — decide it on esthetics/economics.
8. **Patients rarely drive the timing decision** — PROM evidence shows little difference across protocols for single-tooth cases; only full-arch edentulous patients show a mild, fading preference for immediate loading, and it happens to align with where the clinical evidence also favors immediate loading.
9. **Read the pooled numbers as directional** — a substantial share of sources, especially the newer Italian-consortium RCTs, are abstract-only or narrative.
