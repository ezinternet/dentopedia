## Three-line Summary

In-vivo method-comparison study in 14 healthy adults (7 men, 7 women; mean age 26.61 +/- 4.20 years) comparing the repeatability of three centric relation (CR) recording methods — bimanual manipulation with a jig, chin-point guidance with a jig, and Gothic arch tracing — using a mechanical 3D mandibular position indicator on a Denar D4A articulator.

Within-subject variability spanned 0.03 mm (left z axis, bimanual) to 1.6 mm (left y axis, Gothic arch); by F test bimanual was the most consistent (reported as 10.11, p = 1, to 0.438, p = 0.005 times less variation than Gothic arch) and chin-point guidance fell between the two.

For CR jaw-relation records in dentate, non-TMD subjects, bimanual manipulation gave the most repeatable condylar positioning and Gothic arch the least — but this is an abstract-only report of a small healthy-volunteer sample, with no per-axis data tables available for verification.

## 세줄요약

생체내(in-vivo) 술식비교 연구, 건강한 성인 14명(남 7명·여 7명, 평균 연령 26.61 +/- 4.20세)에서 중심위(Centric Relation, CR) 기록법 3가지 — 지그(Jig)를 이용한 양수조작(Bimanual Manipulation), 지그를 이용한 턱끝유도(Chin-Point Guidance), 고딕아치 추적(Gothic Arch Tracing) — 의 재현성을 교합기(Articulator, Denar D4A)에 장착한 기계적 3차원(3-Dimensional, 3D) 하악위치 지시기(Mandibular Position Indicator)로 비교.

피험자 내 변동성은 0.03 mm(양수조작법 좌측 z축)에서 1.6 mm(고딕아치법 좌측 y축)까지였고, F검정(F-test)에서 양수조작법이 가장 일관적이었다(고딕아치법보다 10.11배, p = 1 ~ 0.438배, p = 0.005 적은 변동으로 기재), 턱끝유도법은 두 술식의 중간.

치아가 있고 측두하악장애(Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD)가 없는 성인에서 중심위 기록 시 양수조작법이 가장 재현성이 높고 고딕아치법이 가장 낮았으나, 초록 기반 소표본 건강 지원자 연구이며 축별 원자료 표가 없어 검증이 제한된다.

## Summary

Keshvad and Winstanley (2003) asked which of three routinely used centric relation (CR) registration techniques most repeatably positions the mandible: bimanual manipulation with a jig, chin-point guidance with a jig, and Gothic arch tracing. Fourteen healthy adults (7 men, 7 women; mean age 26.61 +/- 4.20 years) with no extraction, temporomandibular dysfunction, or orthodontic history were recorded four times per method — at baseline, 1 hour, 1 day, and 1 week at approximately the same time of day. Accurate casts were mounted on a Denar D4A articulator via facebow and a maximum intercuspation silicone registration record, and a purpose-built mechanical 3D mandibular position indicator resolved positions on the x (anteroposterior), y (superoinferior), and z (mediolateral) axes, read with a modified stereomicroscope. Within-subject variability ranged from 0.03 mm (bimanual, left z) to 1.6 mm (Gothic arch, left y); the F test identified bimanual manipulation as the most consistent and Gothic arch as the least, with chin-point guidance intermediate. This is the original head-to-head dataset for a study later listed among the included studies of the de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 SR, but only the structured abstract is available here.

## Key Contributions

- Head-to-head repeatability comparison of three CR methods in the same 14 subjects, spanning guided (bimanual, chin-point) and graphic (Gothic arch) families.
- Mechanical 3D mandibular position indicator giving per-axis (x anteroposterior, y superoinferior, z mediolateral) mandibular-position data on an articulator.
- Four repeated records per subject over baseline, 1 hour, 1 day, and 1 week at approximately the same time of day.
- Explicit repeatability ranking: bimanual most consistent, Gothic arch least, chin-point guidance intermediate.

## Methodology

- **Design**: In-vivo method-comparison / repeatability study in healthy volunteers.
- **Subjects**: 14 healthy adults (7 male, 7 female), mean age 26.61 +/- 4.20 years; no history of extractions, temporomandibular joint dysfunction, or orthodontic treatment.
- **Mounting**: casts on a Denar D4A articulator via facebow plus maximum intercuspation silicone registration record.
- **Measurement**: mechanical 3D mandibular position indicator on the articulator; axes x (anteroposterior), y (superoinferior), z (mediolateral shift); stereomicroscope modified to accept the indicator for data extraction.
- **Schedule**: each CR method recorded four times per subject — baseline, 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, approximately the same time of day.
- **Analysis**: F test comparing within-method variability to identify the least-variable (most repeatable) method.

## Results

| Outcome | Result (as reported in the abstract) |
|---|---|
| Within-subject variability range | 0.03 mm (left z axis, bimanual) to 1.6 mm (left y axis, Gothic arch) |
| Most repeatable method | Bimanual manipulation with a jig |
| Least repeatable method | Gothic arch tracing |
| Intermediate method | Chin-point guidance with a jig ("somewhere between the other 2 methods") |
| F-test comparison | Bimanual showed between 10.11 (p = 1) and 0.438 (p = 0.005) times less variation than Gothic arch |
| Authors' conclusion | Bimanual manipulation positioned the condyles in the temporomandibular joint with more consistent repeatability than the other two methods |

The ratio/p-value pairings are reproduced verbatim from the abstract; the larger ratio attached to p = 1 and the smaller to p = 0.005 is internally counterintuitive and cannot be resolved without the full text.
