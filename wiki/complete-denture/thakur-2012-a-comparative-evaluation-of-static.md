## Three-line Summary

Clinical comparative study (n=28 completely edentulous patients) comparing static interocclusal wax with functional extraoral Gothic arch tracing for centric relation (CR) and horizontal condylar guidance (HCG), with subjective evaluation of the dentures produced.

CR coincided by both methods in only 7.14%; interocclusal-wax CR was anterior to Gothic CR in 71.42% and posterior in 21.43%, and Gothic arch gave higher mean HCG on both sides but not significantly (P>0.05).

Woelfel subjective evaluation found no significant difference in any parameter — Gothic records CR more posteriorly without changing complete-denture clinical performance, and HCG angle was approximately similar by both methods (abstract-only).

완전 무치악 환자 28명 임상 비교연구 — 중심위(Centric Relation, CR)와 수평과로각(Horizontal Condylar Guidance, HCG)을 교합간 왁스(정적)와 구외 고딕아치 트레이싱(기능적)으로 기록하고 Woelfel 주관 평가로 완전의치를 평가.

두 방법의 CR 일치는 7.14%뿐이며, 왁스 CR은 고딕 CR보다 전방 71.42%·후방 21.43%; 고딕아치 HCG는 양측 모두 평균적으로 더 높았으나 유의하지 않음(P>0.05).

Woelfel 주관 평가는 모든 항목에서 유의차 없음 — 고딕아치가 CR을 더 후방에 기록하지만 완전의치 임상 성과에는 영향 없고 HCG 각도는 양 방법이 대체로 유사(초록만 확보).

## Summary

This abstract-only clinical comparative study (PMC3416942 has no full-text body) enrolled 28 completely edentulous patients and recorded centric relation (CR) and horizontal condylar guidance (HCG) by two methods — a static interocclusal wax record and a functional extraoral Gothic arch tracing. The records were transferred to an articulator, the difference between the two values was recorded, and patients were divided into two groups by recording method to achieve balanced occlusion; the resulting dentures were scored with the Woelfel subjective evaluation criteria. CR coincided between methods in only 7.14% of patients, with wax CR anterior to Gothic CR in 71.42% and posterior in 21.43% — i.e. the Gothic arch recorded CR more posteriorly. Gothic tracings yielded higher mean HCG values bilaterally than protrusive wax records, but the difference was statistically insignificant (P>0.05), and subjective denture evaluation showed no significant difference on any parameter. The authors conclude that the more posterior Gothic CR does not alter clinical performance of the complete denture and that HCG angle is approximately similar by both methods.

## Key Contributions

- Head-to-head, within-patient comparison of a **static** wax record and a **functional** Gothic arch tracing for *both* CR and HCG in 28 completely edentulous patients.
- Geometric quantification of CR disagreement: coincidence **7.14%**, wax anterior **71.42%**, wax posterior **21.43%**.
- HCG comparison showing Gothic arch higher on both sides but **not significantly** (P>0.05), so the two methods give approximately similar guidance angles.
- Links the recording method to a **denture-level subjective outcome** (Woelfel criteria), finding no clinical difference despite the positional CR difference.
- Early (2012) primary evidence later pooled in [[complete-denture/potdukhe-2023-extraoral-gothic-arch-condylar-guidance-sr-ma]] for the wax-vs-Gothic HCG comparison.

## Methodology

- **Design**: clinical comparative study (cross-sectional), n=28 completely edentulous patients.
- **Interventions**: centric relation and horizontal condylar guidance recorded by interocclusal wax and by extraoral Gothic arch tracing.
- **Transfer**: records moved to an articulator; the difference between the two values recorded.
- **Groups**: two groups defined by the CR/HCG recording method used to establish balanced occlusion.
- **Outcome measure**: Woelfel subjective evaluation criteria of the fabricated dentures; significance threshold P>0.05.
- **Abstract-only caveat**: allocation, blinding, articulator type, named statistical tests and follow-up are not available.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| CR coincidence (wax vs Gothic) | 7.14% of patients |
| Interocclusal-wax CR anterior to Gothic CR | 71.42% of patients |
| Interocclusal-wax CR posterior to Gothic CR | 21.43% of patients |
| Mean HCG (Gothic vs protrusive wax) | Gothic higher on both sides in all subjects; **P>0.05** (not significant) |
| Woelfel subjective evaluation | No significant difference in any parameter, both groups |
| HCG angle overall | Approximately similar by both methods |

- The CR percentages account for essentially all patients (7.14 + 71.42 + 21.43 ≈ 100%).
- Geometric non-coincidence of the two CR records was **not** accompanied by a detectable subjective denture difference.
- This paper's own reported direction — Gothic CR more **posterior** than the static wax record — is reference-specific: it is not the same comparison as a Gothic apex measured against a motion-analyser MIP reference.
