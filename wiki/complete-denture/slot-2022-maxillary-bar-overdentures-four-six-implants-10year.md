## One-line Summary
10-year RCT (n=46 completers, augmented posterior maxilla): bar overdentures on 4 vs. 6 implants showed comparable survival, marginal bone loss, and patient satisfaction; 4 implants sufficient.

## 한줄요약
10년 RCT(n=46, 골증대 상악): 4임플란트 바 오버덴처 = 6임플란트 오버덴처 — 생존율·변연골소실·환자만족도 동등; 4임플란트로 충분.

## Summary
This 10-year RCT enrolled 66 edentulous patients with atrophic maxillae requiring bone augmentation (iliac crest graft). After augmentation and healing, patients were randomized to four or six posterior implants supporting bar overdentures. Forty-six patients completed 10-year follow-up. Implant survival was 100% in the 4-implant group and 96.7% in the 6-implant group. Marginal bone loss was 0.41 mm (4-implant) versus 0.70 mm (6-implant), not significantly different. Patient satisfaction improved significantly from baseline in both groups with no between-group difference. The 6-implant group required more new overdentures (12 vs. 7) due to wear. The study concluded that four posterior implants are sufficient for maxillary bar overdentures in augmented bone.

## Key Contributions
- Longest RCT (10-year) for maxillary bar overdentures comparing implant number
- 4-implant configuration shown non-inferior to 6-implant across all primary outcomes
- High survival rates despite augmented bone foundation (atrophic maxilla + iliac crest graft)
- 6-implant group had higher overdenture replacement rate due to prosthetic wear
- Supports minimizing implant number to reduce surgical burden without compromising outcomes

## Methodology
RCT (parallel two-arm). n=66 enrolled (46 completed). Bone augmentation (iliac crest) → randomization post-healing → 4 or 6 Straumann implants in posterior maxilla → 3 months osseointegration → bar overdenture. Follow-up: 10 years. Outcomes: implant survival, overdenture survival, peri-implant clinical scores, radiographic bone height changes, patient satisfaction (VAS). Funded by Straumann (COI).

## Results
- Implant survival: 4-implant 100%, 6-implant 96.7%
- MBL from baseline: 4-implant 0.41 ± 0.37 mm; 6-implant 0.70 ± 1.07 mm (p=NS)
- New overdentures made: 4-implant=7, 6-implant=12 (both due to excessive wear)
- Clinical parameters: no significant differences between groups
- Patient satisfaction: significantly improved vs. baseline; no between-group difference
