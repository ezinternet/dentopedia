> - **핵심 명제**: 교합 수직 고경 (Occlusal Vertical Dimension, OVD) 평가법은 단일 표준이 없고, 임상 적용을 위해 **전임상 단계 (교합기에서 정량 결정) → 구내 검증 단계 (임시 보철로 확인)** 의 2단계 워크플로로 재분류해야 함
> - **가변성 논쟁 정리**: 생리적 수직 고경은 고정점이 아닌 **범위**; 치조골 보상 기전 (dentoalveolar compensatory mechanism)이 마모를 일부 상쇄하나 (Crothers & Sandham: 8.3 mm 소실 중 5.1 mm 보상, 순 3.2 mm 안면고경 감소) 상아질은 법랑질보다 마모 속도 약 6배 — 고령·급격한 소실 시 보상 실패
> - **변경의 임상 근거**: 수직 고경 감소 자체는 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD)의 원인이라는 증거 없음 (구치부 상실이 위험인자); 거상 시 초기 증상은 대부분 1–2주 내 자기제한적, 3–11년 추적에서도 적응 유지 (Ormianer & Palty 2009)
> - **거상 한계**: 중심위 (Centric Relation, CR)에서 순수 회전 운동 범위 약 20 mm가 이론적 상한; **전치부 기준 ≤5 mm 거상은 안전·예측 가능**; 악간 공간 약 4 mm 확보 시 포괄 수복 가능
> - **평가법 5분류**: ① 생리적 안정위 + 자유공극 (Freeway Space) ② 정량적 계측·기록 (안면 비율·두부방사선·전정간 거리) ③ 발음 ④ 연하 ⑤ 환자 주관적 평가
> - **한국인 참고값**: 전정간 거리 상악 20.8 / 하악 17.3 / 총 35.0 mm (Park 2005; Fayz 서양인 21.3/16.7/34.0 mm) · 전치부 평균 치관 길이 상악 11.0/9.8/10.5 mm, 하악 8.7/9.1/10.5 mm (중절치/측절치/견치)
> - **임상 takeaway**: 교합기에서 진단모형 분석 + 전정간 거리 + 재채득한 중심위 (Lucia jig/Leaf gauge/Gothic arch tracer)로 잠정 거상량을 정하고, 진단 왁스업 → 임시 보철 장착 후 자유공극·발음·연하·심미·증상 감시로 검증 — 최근접 발음 공간 (Closest Speaking Space)은 임시 보철 장착 상태에서만 의미 있음

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (J Korean Acad Prosthodont 2022;60:301-12, ~50 references from 1928–2021) synthesizing the OVD immutability-vs-variability debate, the clinical evidence for changing occlusal vertical dimension (OVD), and five families of OVD evaluation methods, reclassified into a two-stage clinical workflow for full-mouth rehabilitation.

Physiologic OVD is a range, not a fixed point; the stomatognathic system adapts to increases within physiologic limits (initial symptoms self-limited within 1–2 weeks in the cited series), OVD reduction per se is not a proven TMD cause, and a ≤5 mm anterior increase is judged safe and predictable (theoretical ceiling ≈20 mm of pure hinge rotation from centric relation); Korean reference values are supplied (vestibular distances 20.8/17.3/35.0 mm; anterior crown lengths 11.0/9.8/10.5 mm maxillary, 8.7/9.1/10.5 mm mandibular).

Because no single method is a gold standard, the authors propose determining the provisional increase preclinically on the articulator (vestibular-distance values + re-recorded centric relation) and verifying it intraorally with provisionals (freeway space, speech, swallowing, esthetics, symptom surveillance); the main limitations are the narrative design and the small, uncontrolled, short-follow-up clinical series it cites.

## 세줄요약

대한치과보철학회지 2022 서사 리뷰 (참고문헌 약 50편, 1928–2021)로, 교합 수직 고경 (Occlusal Vertical Dimension, OVD)의 불변성 vs 가변성 논쟁·수직 고경 변경의 임상 근거·5가지 평가법군을 정리하고 전악 구강 회복을 위한 2단계 임상 워크플로로 재분류.

생리적 수직 고경은 고정점이 아닌 범위이며 악구강계 (stomatognathic system)는 생리적 한계 내 거상에 적응함 (인용 연구들에서 초기 증상은 1–2주 내 자기제한적); 수직 고경 감소 자체가 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD)의 원인이라는 증거는 없고, 전치부 기준 ≤5 mm 거상은 안전·예측 가능 (이론적 상한은 중심위 (Centric Relation, CR)에서 순수 회전 약 20 mm); 한국인 참고값 제시 (전정간 거리 20.8/17.3/35.0 mm; 전치부 치관 길이 상악 11.0/9.8/10.5 mm, 하악 8.7/9.1/10.5 mm).

어느 평가법도 표준이 없으므로 교합기에서 전정간 거리와 재채득한 중심위로 잠정 거상량을 정하고 (전임상 단계), 임시 보철 (provisional prosthesis) 장착 후 자유공극 (Freeway Space)·발음·연하·심미·증상 감시로 구내 검증하도록 제안; 서사 리뷰 설계와 소규모·비대조·단기 추적 연구 의존이 한계.

## Summary

This review addresses the question every full-mouth rehabilitation poses — how much to open the bite, and how to know it is right. It first resolves the conceptual dispute: OVD is not a lifelong constant, because the dentoalveolar compensatory mechanism (secondary dentin, continuous eruption, muscle adaptation) can only partly offset wear and aging, so the modern position treats physiologic OVD as a range. It then appraises the clinical evidence on changing OVD: reduced OVD is not an established cause of TMD (posterior tooth loss is the risk factor), and increases within physiologic limits produce only transient, self-limiting symptoms, with long-term adaptation documented up to 11 years. Five families of evaluation methods are catalogued (physiologic rest position + freeway space; quantitative measurements and records; phonetics; swallowing; patient subjective evaluation), each criticized for empiricism and population dependence. The paper's practical contribution is the reclassification into a two-stage workflow: a preclinical articulator stage where the provisional increase is set from vestibular-distance reference values (Fayz 21.3/16.7/34.0 mm; Korean Park 20.8/17.3/35.0 mm) with a re-recorded centric relation, and an intraoral verification stage where diagnostic wax-up provisionals are checked for freeway-space encroachment, speech, swallowing, esthetics, and symptoms. It concludes that ≤5 mm anterior increases are safe and predictable in appropriate indications, and calls for ethnicity-, age-, and sex-specific normative data.

## Key Contributions

- Reframes the OVD immutability-vs-variability debate with quantitative compensation data (8.3 mm loss vs 5.1 mm compensation → net 3.2 mm facial-height loss; dentin wears ~6× faster than enamel), supporting physiologic OVD as a range.
- Consolidates the adaptation evidence for OVD increase across six decades of clinical series (Christensen 1970 → Ormianer & Palty 2009), and explains the Christensen-vs-Carlsson discrepancy by occlusal instability rather than vertical change per se.
- Supplies a five-category taxonomy of OVD evaluation methods with reliability appraisal, including the freeway-space measurement-inconsistency problem (individual range <1–10 mm; rest position drifts with age).
- Delivers the clinically actionable two-stage workflow (preclinical articulator determination → intraoral verification with provisionals) that the existing complete-denture-centric OVD reviews lack for dentate/partially edentulous patients.
- Provides Korean-population reference values (vestibular distances; anterior crown lengths) and states the clinical boundaries: ≤5 mm anterior increase, ~20 mm hinge-rotation ceiling, ~4 mm interarch space threshold.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Narrative (non-systematic) review; no PRISMA flow, database list, or risk-of-bias appraisal.
- **Evidence base**: ~50 references, 1928–2021, English and Korean prosthodontic literature (including Goldstein 2021 OVD Best Evidence Consensus and Park 2005 Korean anthropometric data).
- **Structure**: definitions → immutability/variability debate → clinical evidence for OVD change → five-category evaluation-method classification → clinical reclassification (preclinical vs intraoral) → conclusion.
- **Focus**: full-mouth rehabilitation of worn dentition, partially edentulous, and edentulous patients.

## Results

**OVD variability and compensation**
| Evidence | Value |
|---|---|
| Tooth-structure loss (Crothers & Sandham 1993) | 8.3 mm mean, compensated 5.1 mm → net 3.2 mm facial-height loss |
| Dentin vs enamel wear rate (Burak 1999, cited) | ~6× faster in dentin |
| Reduced OVD → TMD | No established causal link; posterior tooth loss is the risk factor |
| Physiologic rest position | A range, not a point; drifts with age; VDR decreases parallel to OVD loss |

**Adaptation to OVD increase (cited series)**
| Study | n / design | Increase | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Christensen 1970 | 20 dentate + 22 edentulous, molar overlays, 3–7 d | molar region | Symptoms (bruxism, muscle pain), more frequent in dentate — attributed to occlusal instability |
| Carlsson 1979 | 6 adults, balanced canine-to-molar splints, 7 d | full-arch splint | Symptoms resolved 1–2 d; all adapted with new rest position |
| Dahl & Krogstad 1982/1985 | 20 worn dentition, Dahl appliance → crowns | partial bite-raising | Transient discomfort; adapted; no TMD symptoms at 3-yr crown follow-up |
| Gough & Setchell 1999 | 45 patients, Dahl-type appliance | localized | 94% no discomfort |
| Gross & Ormianer 1994 / Ormianer & Gross 1998 | 6 severe-wear, fixed provisionals → final | fixed prosthodontic | Speech/muscle discomfort resolved 1–2 wk; stable at 2 yr |
| Ormianer & Palty 2009 | 30 tooth- and implant-supported | 3–5 mm (premolar) | All adapted over 3–11 yr; no muscle/speech problems |

**Freeway space and rest position**
- Niswonger 1934: 200 dentate subjects — 87% ~3 mm freeway space.
- Pleasure 1951: VDR − 3 mm = OVD.
- Goldstein 2021 consensus: measurement methods inconsistent; individual freeway space <1 mm to ~10 mm (means mostly 2–4 mm); rest + 3 mm unreliable in pathologic occlusion (Abduo & Lyons 2012).

**Vestibular distance norms (mm)**
| Source | Maxillary vestibule→incisal | Mandibular vestibule→incisal | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| McGrane 1949 (100 dentate) | 22 | 18 | 40 |
| Ellinger 1968 (radiographic) | 20 | 16.33 | — |
| Fayz 1987 (overbite considered) | 21.3 | 16.7 | 34.0 |
| Park 2005 (Korean adults) | 20.8 | 17.3 | 35.0 (deepest points) |

**Facial / cephalometric / phonetic references**
- Willis 1930: outer canthus→labial commissure = subnasale→chin; McGee 1947: pupil→stomion = glabella→subnasale = subnasale→gnathion.
- Hull & Junghans 1968: upper:lower facial height 43:57; Orthlieb 2000: OVD formula from gonial angle.
- Silverman 1953: closest speaking space (S) constant over life, individual-specific; Pound 1977: S-space 1–2 mm; M (bilabial) guides to rest position but hides tooth relation; F/V reflect maxillary incisal length; Swerdlow 1964 judged phonetics most reliable.

**Clinical boundaries and Korean crown lengths**
- Pure hinge rotation from CR ≈ 20 mm → theoretical maximum increase.
- ≤5 mm anterior increase: safe and predictable; interarch space ~4 mm: comprehensive restoration possible.
- Korean anterior crown length (mm): maxilla 11.0 / 9.8 / 10.5; mandible 8.7 / 9.1 / 10.5 (central / lateral / canine).
