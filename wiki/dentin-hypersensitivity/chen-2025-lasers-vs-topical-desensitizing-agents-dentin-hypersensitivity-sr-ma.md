## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA; six databases; RCTs only; random-effects model throughout because I² > 50% in all subgroups) comparing lasers with topical desensitizing agents for dentin hypersensitivity (abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

Lasers were superior only in the long-term air-blast subgroup; other subgroups showed no difference, several were not robust to sensitivity analysis, one showed publication bias, and GRADE certainty was very low.

Evidence is insufficient to declare lasers or topical agents superior; laser is a feasible option but the head-to-head advantage is not established.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA(PRISMA, 6개 DB, RCT만, 모든 하위군 I² > 50%로 랜덤효과모형): 상아질 과민증에서 레이저 vs 국소 탈감작제 비교 (초록만 확보).

레이저는 장기 공기분사 하위군에서만 우월했고 나머지는 차이 없음; 일부 하위군은 민감도 분석에서 불안정, 중기 촉각 군은 출판 편향, GRADE 확실성 매우 낮음.

레이저 우위를 단정할 근거는 불충분하며 레이저는 실행 가능한 선택지일 뿐 직접비교 우월성은 확립되지 않음.

## Summary

abstract-only — full text not retrieved. This review compared lasers (diode lasers most common) with topical desensitizing agents (fluoride compounds most common) in RCTs of dentin hypersensitivity, analysed by stimulus (air blast, tactile) and follow-up (short, medium, long term). Only the long-term air-blast comparison favoured lasers. The medium-term tactile subgroup showed publication bias, and the medium-term air-blast and short-/medium-term tactile subgroups lacked robustness in sensitivity analysis. GRADE certainty was very low, so the authors conclude it is insufficient to say which modality is more effective while regarding laser as feasible and reliable.

## Key Contributions
- Direct laser vs topical-agent comparison in a meta-analysis, stratified by stimulus and follow-up.
- Transparent about fragility: sensitivity analysis, publication bias, GRADE very low.

## Methodology
PRISMA; MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane, Web of Science, ClinicalTrials.gov, Scopus; RCTs only; random-effects model (I² > 50% in all subgroups). Pooled effect sizes, number of trials and participants are not stated in the abstract. The abstract's flow count ("4,480 of 4,455 studies were excluded") is internally inconsistent.

## Results
Long-term air blast: lasers superior. Other subgroups: no difference. Medium-term tactile: publication bias. Medium-term air blast and short-/medium-term tactile: not robust. GRADE: very low.
