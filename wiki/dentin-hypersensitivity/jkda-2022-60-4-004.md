## Three-line Summary
Narrative review (Kyung Hee Univ. Dept. of Conservative Dentistry, Jang, JKDA 2022) providing an outpatient DH decision framework from pain assessment to a stepwise treatment ladder.

First-line at-home agents (KNO₃, NovaMin, arginine toothpaste) are followed by in-office sealing agents (varnish, oxalate, glutaraldehyde, laser), with restorative composite reserved for deep lesions unresponsive to conservative measures.

Success requires both accurate diagnosis (excluding pulpal pathology) and patient adherence to behavioral modification (brushing technique, acid-diet avoidance).

## 세줄요약
Narrative review(경희대 보존과 장지현, JKDA 2022): 외래 DH 의사결정 — 통증 평가에서 단계별 치료 ladder까지 실무 중심.

1차(가정): KNO₃·NovaMin·arginine 치약 → 2차(원내 세관폐쇄): 바니시·옥살레이트·글루타르알데히드·레이저 → 3차(수복): 깊은 병소에 복합레진.

정확한 진단(치수병변 감별)과 환자 순응도(칫솔질 교정·산성식품 회피)가 성공의 핵심 결정 요인.

## Summary
JKDA DH 특집 2번. 임상 management 실무 집중. 외래에서 시린이 호소 환자 만났을 때 의사결정 ladder 제공.

## Treatment Ladder

| 단계 | 옵션 | 적응 |
|---|---|---|
| 1차 (at-home) | KNO3·NovaMin·arginine toothpaste | 경증, conservative |
| 2차 (in-office, sealing) | Varnish, oxalate, glutaraldehyde, laser | 중등도, 자가요법 무효 |
| 3차 (restorative) | Composite restoration | 깊은 lesion, 보존적 무효 |

## Patient Education
- 브러싱 기법 수정 (압력·방향)
- Acidic diet 회피 (citrus, vinegar)
- 정기 평가·재평가
