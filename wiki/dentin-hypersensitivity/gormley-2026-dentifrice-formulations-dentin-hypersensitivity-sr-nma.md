## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and frequentist network meta-analysis (93 RCTs, 9548 adults with dentin hypersensitivity; 47 studies/4636 participants in the 2-week network) comparing self-applied dentifrices against a benchmark fluoride dentifrice (RoB 2, CINeMA certainty; abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

Stannous fluoride and arginine dentifrices gave the largest reductions: Schiff MD −0.85 (95% CI −1.08 to −0.62; high certainty) and −0.78 (−1.02 to −0.54; low); Yeaple tactile MD +14.87 g (10.24–19.50; moderate) and +9.78 g (4.69–14.86; low); other formulations showed small-to-moderate effects, generally low certainty.

Stannous fluoride or arginine dentifrices are proposed as first-line self-care for dentin hypersensitivity, chosen by patient preference, tolerability and availability rather than expected superiority; only the stannous evaporative-cold result is high certainty.

## 세줄요약

성인 상아질 과민증(Dentin Hypersensitivity, DH) 대상 RCT 93편(9,548명; 2주 네트워크 메타분석 47편·4,636명)을 벤치마크 불소 치약과 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 네트워크 메타분석(Network Meta-Analysis, NMA; RoB 2, CINeMA 확실성 평가; 초록만 확인 — 전문 미확보).

불화주석(Stannous Fluoride, SnF₂)과 아르기닌 치약의 감소폭이 가장 컸다: Schiff 평균차 −0.85 (95% CI −1.08~−0.62; 높은 확실성)·−0.78 (−1.02~−0.54; 낮음), Yeaple 촉각역치 +14.87 g (10.24~19.50; 중등도)·+9.78 g (4.69~14.86; 낮음); 그 외 제형은 소~중등도 효과이며 대체로 확실성 낮음.

SnF₂ 또는 아르기닌 치약을 1차 자가관리로 권고하되 우월성 기대보다 환자 선호·내약성·구입성으로 선택; 높은 확실성은 SnF₂의 증발냉각(Schiff) 결과뿐.

## Summary

Abstract-only — full text not retrieved. This systematic review and frequentist network meta-analysis (Periodontology 2000, 2026) updates a previous review of self-applied dentifrices for dentin hypersensitivity (DH). It pooled RCTs in adults with diagnosed DH (93 studies, 9548 participants; 47 studies, 4636 participants in the 2-week networks) and ranked dentifrices against a benchmark fluoride dentifrice. Stannous fluoride and arginine consistently gave the most clinically meaningful reductions; the authors recommend them as first-line self-care, selected by patient preference, tolerability and availability.

## Key Contributions

- First comparative ranking in the wiki of dentifrice actives (previously only a formulation map and single RCTs).
- Only stannous fluoride's evaporative-cold effect is high certainty; arginine shows comparable effect sizes at lower certainty.
- Other formulations: small-to-moderate effects, generally low certainty.

## Methodology

Medline, EMBASE, Cochrane Library searched Nov 2022–Jul 2025, plus records from a previous review and citation searching. Random-effects frequentist NMA at 2 weeks; RoB 2; CINeMA. Outcomes: evaporative cold (Schiff), tactile (Yeaple), thermal cold. Details beyond the abstract (protocol registration, node sizes, heterogeneity) not retrieved.

## Results

| Outcome (2 wk vs benchmark fluoride) | Stannous fluoride | Arginine |
|---|---|---|
| Schiff MD | −0.85 (95% CI −1.08, −0.62); high | −0.78 (−1.02, −0.54); low |
| Yeaple MD | 14.87 (10.24, 19.50); moderate | 9.78 (4.69, 14.86); low |

Per-formulation estimates for other actives are not available from the abstract.
