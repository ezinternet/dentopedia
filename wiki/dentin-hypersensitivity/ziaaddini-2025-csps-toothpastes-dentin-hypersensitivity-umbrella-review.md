## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42024617640; searched to Feb 2025) of 9 systematic reviews (5 with meta-analysis; 2014–2021) on calcium sodium phosphosilicate (CSPS) toothpastes vs placebo/negative control for dentin hypersensitivity (DH), covering 29 unique primary RCTs (13 placebo/negative-control).

Reviews generally favored CSPS, but there was no new pooling; overlap was very high (CCA 21.55% all trials; 31.73% placebo-controlled), only 1 of 9 reviews was AMSTAR 2 high quality (8 low/critically low), and heterogeneity was present in all.

CSPS toothpaste is probably effective for DH, but the nine concordant reviews largely recount the same ~13 placebo-controlled trials, so certainty is lower than the review count implies.

## 세줄요약

우산 리뷰(PROSPERO CRD42024617640; 2025년 2월까지 검색): 2014–2021년 체계적 문헌고찰 9편(메타분석 5편)에서 칼슘소듐포스포실리케이트(Calcium Sodium Phosphosilicate, CSPS) 치약 vs 위약·음성대조의 상아질 과민증(Dentin Hypersensitivity, DH) 효과를 평가; 고유 1차 RCT 29편(위약/음성대조 13편).

리뷰들은 대체로 CSPS 우월을 보고했으나 새 통합분석은 없었고, 중복도가 매우 높았으며(CCA 전체 21.55%, 위약대조 31.73%), AMSTAR 2 고품질은 1편뿐(8편 저/극저품질), 전 리뷰에 이질성이 있었다.

CSPS 치약은 DH에 효과적일 가능성이 높지만, 일치하는 9편은 사실상 같은 위약대조 RCT 약 13편의 반복 집계이므로 확실성은 리뷰 편수가 시사하는 것보다 낮다.

## Summary

First umbrella review of CSPS (NovaMin, bioactive glass) toothpastes for DH. It collected 9 SRs/MAs (Talioti 2014, West 2015, Bae 2015, Zhu 2015, Hu 2018, Marto 2019, Hu 2019, Martins 2020, de Freitas 2021), assessed quality with AMSTAR 2 and risk of bias with ROBIS, and measured how much the reviews share the same primary trials. The authors conclude that CSPS toothpastes outperform placebo and negative-control pastes, with cautious interpretation advised.

Appraisal (wiki reading): this is a tally of review conclusions, not a new estimate. It adds value mainly as a certainty check: the evidence base for CSPS vs placebo is about 13 trials re-used across 9 reviews, and most of those reviews are low quality. Its own clinical-implication sentence ("routine use") is stronger than its data support.

## Key Contributions

- Overlap quantification: CCA 21.55% (all 29 CSPS trials) and 31.73% (13 placebo/negative-control trials), both "very high" (>15).
- Quality map: AMSTAR 2 high = 1 (Zhu 2015); low/critically low = 8. ROBIS synthesis domain low only for Zhu 2015 and Hu 2018.
- Dose picture left unresolved: 7.5% best in Talioti 2014 (2.5% ≈ placebo at 8 weeks), 5% better than control in Zhu 2015 and de Freitas 2021.

## Methodology

PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane to Feb 2025 (English). 5681 records, 85 full texts, 9 SRs. Duplicate reviewers, third arbiter. Overlap by citation matrix (% overlap, CA, CCA; Pieper thresholds). No GRADE, no re-pooling (heterogeneity precluded subgroup analysis). Comparators: inert placebo or regular fluoride toothpaste (e.g. 1450 ppm F) as negative control.

## Results

- Overlap Phase I: 79 instances / 29 trials / 9 reviews, 65.51% overlap, CA 30.26%, CCA 21.55%. Phase II: 46 / 13 / 9, 61.53%, CA 39.31%, CCA 31.73%.
- Quality: 1 high, 8 low or critically low (no registered protocol, incomplete searches, excluded-study list missing, RoB not used in interpretation).
- Largest quantified review result: Bae 2015 SMD −2.36 (95% CI −3.72 to −1.00; I²=92%) vs placebo, an extremely heterogeneous estimate. Zhu 2015 (the only high-quality review) rated 5% CSPS better than negative control with moderate-strength evidence.
- Hu 2019 NMA: CSPS ≈ potassium ≈ strontium; better than fluoride and placebo at 4 weeks. Martins 2020 NMA: CSPS among the best against tactile, air and cold stimuli.
- Caveats: negative-control pastes contain fluoride (not inert); no included review after 2021 despite a 2025 search; 5 of 9 reviews are broad DH reviews with only 2–13 CSPS trials; the text's ROBIS statement for domain 4 ("nine reviews unclear/high" vs "only two low") is internally inconsistent.
