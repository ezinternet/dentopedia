## Three-line Summary

Three-arm RCT (52 randomized, 48 analyzed, Stage III/IV periodontitis) comparing Gluma, Er,Cr:YSGG laser, and Gluma+laser for dentin hypersensitivity after non-surgical periodontal therapy (NSPT), with VAS and Schiff scale to 6 months.

All three arms significantly reduced VAS and Schiff scores (P<0.05), with the largest gain by month 2 and slight recurrence at 6 months; no significant intergroup difference at any time point (P>0.05).

Chemical and laser desensitization are comparable for post-NSPT DH, and combining them adds no measurable benefit; patients should be warned of symptom recurrence and need for maintenance or retreatment (abstract-only; n=16/group is small).

## 세줄요약

3군 RCT(무작위 배정 52명·분석 48명, Stage III/IV 치주염)로 비수술 치주치료(NSPT) 후 상아질 과민증에 대해 Gluma, Er,Cr:YSGG 레이저, 병용을 비교하고 VAS·Schiff를 6개월까지 평가.

세 군 모두 VAS·Schiff가 유의하게 감소(P<0.05), 2개월까지 개선이 가장 크고 6개월에 약간 재발; 모든 시점에서 군간 유의 차이 없음(P>0.05).

NSPT 후 DH에서 화학제와 레이저는 동등하고 병용의 추가 이득도 없음; 재발 가능성과 유지·재치료 필요성을 환자에게 설명해야 함(초록 기반, 군당 n=16으로 소규모).

## Summary

In Stage III/IV periodontitis patients with DH after NSPT (VAS >4, Schiff 2-3), 52 were randomized to Gluma (n=18), Er,Cr:YSGG laser (n=17), or both (n=17); 48 were analyzed. Every arm improved significantly, mostly within two months, with slight recurrence at six months and no between-arm differences. This supports using either a chemical or a laser desensitizer after NSPT without expecting added benefit from combining them.

## Key Contributions

- Population-specific evidence: post-NSPT DH in advanced periodontitis.
- Combination arm shows no additive effect over either single modality.
- Documents recurrence at 6 months, which argues for maintenance planning.

## Methodology

Three-arm parallel RCT, Trakya University. VAS and Schiff scale at baseline, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 2 months, 6 months. 48 analyzed (16 per group), α=0.05. Details beyond the abstract (laser settings, blinding, controls) were not retrieved.

## Results

Significant VAS and Schiff reductions in all arms (P<0.05); peak improvement through month 2; slight recurrence at 6 months, still better than baseline; no significant intergroup difference at any point (P>0.05). With 16 per group, absence of a difference is weak evidence of equivalence.
