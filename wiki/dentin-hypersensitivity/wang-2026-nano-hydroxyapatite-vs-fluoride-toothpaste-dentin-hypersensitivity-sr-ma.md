## Three-line Summary

PROSPERO-registered (CRD420251272119) PRISMA 2020 systematic review and meta-analysis of 13 RCTs (609 adults with dentin hypersensitivity; n-HAP 296 vs fluoride 313) comparing nano-hydroxyapatite (n-HAP) toothpaste with fluoride toothpaste, 7 databases to December 2025.

n-HAP beat fluoride early (VAS at 2 weeks, 4 studies, n=232; tactile threshold SMD -1.31 at 2 weeks per abstract), but fluoride was better for cold air sensitivity at 8 weeks (SMD 0.35, 95% CI 0.05-0.65, I²=0%, 2 studies); 15-20% n-HAP pooled SMD -0.77 (95% CI -1.16 to -0.39, I²=11%) although the subgroup-difference test was not significant (p=0.41).

n-HAP looks like a faster-acting option for mechanical/tactile sensitivity and fluoride may suit cold-triggered, long-term maintenance, but RoB 1 raised "some concerns" in most trials, follow-up was only 1-12 weeks, several outcomes had I² 80-97%, and the 15-20% optimum is exploratory.

## 세줄요약

PROSPERO 사전등록(CRD420251272119)·PRISMA 2020 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석: 상아질 과민증(Dentin Hypersensitivity, DH) 성인 609명(나노수산화인회석 296 vs 불소 313)을 대상으로 한 RCT 13편에서 나노수산화인회석(nano-hydroxyapatite, n-HAP) 치약과 불소 치약을 비교(7개 DB, 2025년 12월까지).

n-HAP가 초기에 우세(시각통증척도 VAS 2주 4편 n=232; 초록 기준 촉각역치 2주 SMD -1.31)했으나 냉공기 민감도는 8주에 불소가 우세(SMD 0.35, 95% CI 0.05-0.65, I²=0%, 2편); 15-20% n-HAP 합산 SMD -0.77(95% CI -1.16~-0.39, I²=11%)이나 하위군 간 차이 검정은 비유의(p=0.41).

n-HAP는 기계적·촉각 자극 과민에 빠른 효과, 불소는 냉자극 과민의 장기 유지에 적합할 수 있으나, 대부분 RoB 1에서 "some concerns", 추적 1-12주, 일부 결과 I² 80-97%, 15-20% 최적 농도는 탐색적 결과.

## Summary
This PROSPERO-registered systematic review and meta-analysis pooled 13 RCTs (609 adults) comparing nano-hydroxyapatite (n-HAP) toothpaste with fluoride toothpaste for dentin hypersensitivity. n-HAP produced greater early relief on VAS and tactile threshold, whereas fluoride was better for cold air sensitivity at 8 weeks. The authors suggest n-HAP for rapid relief of mechanical sensitivity and fluoride for cold-triggered, long-term maintenance, with 15-20% n-HAP the most consistent concentration. Interpretation is limited by short follow-up, RoB "some concerns", and very high heterogeneity in several outcomes.

## Key Contributions
- First pooled RCT comparison of n-HAP vs fluoride toothpaste for DH.
- Time-dependent pattern of effect (n-HAP early, fluoride later for cold air).
- Exploratory concentration subgroup (15-20%).

## Methodology
7 databases to December 2025 (incl. CNKI, Wanfang, SinoMed); PRISMA 2020; RoB 1; GRADE; SMD with RevMan/Stata/R; subgroups by follow-up and n-HAP concentration; leave-one-out, fixed-effect and high-RoB sensitivity analyses.

## Results
- 13 RCTs, 609 participants (n-HAP 296 / fluoride 313); n-HAP 5-30%; fluoride comparators varied; no adverse effects reported.
- VAS at 2 weeks (4 studies, n=232): favoured n-HAP, reported SMD 8.81 (95% CI 0.31-17.30), I²=80.2% — implausibly large for an SMD and the p-value (0.0017) does not match the CI, so treat the direction, not the magnitude, as the finding. At 4 weeks I²=97.1%; by 8-12 weeks the gap approached non-significance.
- 15-20% n-HAP: SMD -0.77 (95% CI -1.16 to -0.39), I²=11%; overall VAS-by-concentration SMD -0.56 (95% CI -0.86 to -0.27); subgroup-difference p=0.41 (no confirmed dose-response).
- Tactile threshold: overall SMD -1.13 (95% CI -1.66 to -0.61) favouring n-HAP; 2-week SMD -1.31 (95% CI -1.80 to -0.82, abstract).
- Cold air sensitivity: no difference through 6 weeks; fluoride better at 8 weeks, SMD 0.35 (95% CI 0.05-0.65), I²=0%, only 2 studies.
- Air blast sensitivity: SMD -0.77 (95% CI -1.38 to -0.15), I²=92%; no conclusion drawn.
- Egger p=0.198 (VAS); VAS robust to leave-one-out. Stated GRADE certainty high for some outcomes despite I² of 92-97% on others, so the grading deserves caution.
