## Three-line Summary

Randomized, double-blind, parallel-group RCT (60 enrolled, 54 completed; 120 non-carious cervical lesion teeth) comparing 8% arginine toothpaste, 660 nm photobiomodulation (PBM), their combination, and a fluoride + sham-laser control for dentin hypersensitivity (DH) over 8 weeks.

All four arms showed a large, significant DH reduction over time (Schiff 5.0→1.9; VAS pre-intervention 6.3→1.8; both p<0.001), but no significant between-group differences at any time point (p>0.05) and no additive effect of combining laser with arginine.

Neither 660 nm PBM nor arginine outperformed a fluoride-toothpaste + oral-hygiene control, and the strong control-arm improvement highlights large placebo/Hawthorne and hygiene-standardization effects in DH trials.

## 세줄요약

무작위 이중맹검 평행군 RCT(60명 등록·54명 완료, 비우식성 치경부병소 120치아)로 8% 아르기닌 치약, 660 nm 광생물조절(PBM/저출력레이저), 병용, 불소+가짜레이저 대조군을 8주간 비교.

네 군 모두 치아과민증이 유의하게 크게 감소(Schiff 5.0→1.9, 시술 전 VAS 6.3→1.8, 둘 다 p<0.001)했으나 모든 시점에서 군간 차이가 없었고(p>0.05) 레이저+아르기닌 병용의 상가효과도 없었다.

660 nm PBM도 아르기닌도 불소치약+구강위생 대조군을 능가하지 못했고, 대조군의 뚜렷한 개선은 DH 시험에서 위약·호손 효과와 위생표준화 영향이 크다는 점을 부각한다.

## Summary

This Brazilian university RCT tested the common clinical assumption that adding low-level laser (photobiomodulation, PBM) on top of an 8% arginine desensitizing toothpaste yields extra benefit for dentin hypersensitivity (DH). Patients with non-carious cervical lesions and confirmed DH (Schiff 2–3 to air) were randomized into four parallel arms — laser only (LG), arginine only (AG), laser + arginine (LAG), and a fluoride-toothpaste + sham-laser control (CG) — and followed for 8 weeks with evaporative (Schiff), tactile, and VAS assessments.

The null hypothesis held. Every arm improved substantially and significantly over time, but the arms were statistically indistinguishable at every time point, and the combination gave no additive or synergistic advantage. Notably, the control arm improved as much as the active treatments, which the authors attribute to placebo response, the Hawthorne effect, and standardized oral-hygiene instruction — a reminder that uncontrolled DH studies systematically overstate specific-treatment efficacy.

## Key Contributions

- **Direct 4-arm test** of additivity: 8% arginine + 660 nm PBM is not superior to either modality alone for DH over 8 weeks.
- **Fluoride + sham-laser control** quantifies the large non-specific improvement (placebo/Hawthorne/hygiene) that confounds single-arm DH claims.
- **Cross-validated outcomes**: evaporative (Schiff), tactile (binary), and pre/post VAS; tactile-positive teeth carried ~1.46 higher VAS units early, linking the two clinical stimulus tests.

## Methodology

- **Design**: randomized, double-blind, parallel-group RCT, CONSORT-reported; ReBEC RBR-2vxzvkz.
- **Eligibility**: adults with non-carious cervical lesions + DH (Schiff 2–3) in ≥2 non-adjacent teeth; periodontally healthy; strict exclusions (endo lesions, extensive restorations, prior perio therapy/bleaching, current desensitizers, pregnancy, chemo/radiotherapy).
- **Groups (baseline n)**: LG 16, AG 17, LAG 11, CG 16 (60 enrolled; 54 completed; 120 teeth, ~69% premolars; mean age 51.4 y, 76.6% female).
- **PBM**: Photon Lase III diode, 660 nm continuous, 100 mW, spot 0.028 cm², 0.5 cm standoff; 3 points × 28 s = 84 s/tooth; 8.4 J; nominal 300 J/cm².
- **Arginine**: 1.14% sodium monofluorophosphate + 8% arginine + calcium carbonate (Colgate Sensitive Pro Relief); ~0.25 g finger-applied 1 min; also daily toothpaste.
- **Control**: sham laser + 1,450 ppm fluoride toothpaste.
- **Blinding**: block randomization by independent researcher; opaque sealed envelopes; participants + outcome assessor blinded; toothpastes in identical opaque containers.
- **Outcomes**: baseline, 2, 4, 8 weeks — Schiff (0–3), binary tactile (#5 explorer), VAS (0–10) pre/post.
- **Analysis**: linear mixed-effects models (group, time, group×time; random intercepts) for Schiff/VAS; GEE for tactile; R 4.3.1; α=0.05. A priori sample size 44 (effect size 0.53, power 80%).

## Results

- **Overall time effect** (pooled): Schiff 5.0(±0.8)→3.5→2.9→1.9(±1.3), Δ −3.1, p<0.001; VAS pre-intervention 6.3→1.8, Δ −3.5, p<0.001; VAS post-intervention 5.1→1.6.
- **Schiff by group** (baseline / 8 wk / Δ): LG 4.8/1.9/−3.0 · AG 5.1/2.1/−2.9 · LAG 5.1/2.0/−2.9 · CG 5.1/1.6/−3.5. Group p-values 0.573–0.695; Δ p=0.686.
- **VAS Δ by group**: LG −3.7 · AG −3.7 · LAG −3.4 · CG −3.1 (p=0.966). No significant group×time interaction.
- **No additive effect**: LAG did not beat AG or LG.
- **Tactile ↔ VAS (GEE)**: tactile-positive teeth higher VAS at baseline (6.6 vs 5.1, p=0.037) and 4 wk (4.4 vs 2.4, p=0.009); borderline 2 wk (p=0.053); NS at 8 wk; ~1.46 VAS-unit increase early.
- **Control-arm improvement**: as large as active arms — attributed to placebo, Hawthorne, and hygiene standardization.
- **Safety**: no adverse effects.
- **Key caveat**: attrition left unbalanced groups (LAG n=11); the null between-group finding may be underpowered and should be read cautiously.
