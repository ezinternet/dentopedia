## Three-line Summary
Narrative review (Yonsei Univ. Dept. of Conservative Dentistry, Kim, JKDA 2022) on preventing postoperative hypersensitivity in Class II direct composite restorations, attributing the problem primarily to polymerization-shrinkage–driven microleakage.

The recommended protocol integrates selective etching with adequate primer, incremental thin-layer filling, slow-start curing, pre-wedging, and a sectional matrix system to restore tight proximal contacts.

Postoperative sensitivity after Class II composites is largely preventable through technique optimization; bulk-fill advances supplement but do not fully replace the sectional-matrix and incremental-filling principles.

## Prevention Protocol

| 단계 | 권장 |
|---|---|
| Adhesive technique | Selective etching, adequate primer 적용 |
| Filling | Incremental (얇은 layer로 분할) |
| Curing | Slow-start (낮은 출력 시작 → 증가) |
| Proximal box | Pre-wedging, sectional matrix system, proper contact restoration |
