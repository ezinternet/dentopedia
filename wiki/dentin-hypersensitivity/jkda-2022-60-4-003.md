## Three-line Summary
Narrative review (Gangnam Severance + SNU Dept. of Conservative Dentistry, JKDA 2022 special issue) covering DH prevalence, hydrodynamic theory, diagnosis, and treatment options for Korean clinicians.

DH prevalence ranges 8–57% across studies; diagnosis suffers from self-report vs clinical-assessment inconsistency; treatment spans at-home desensitizing toothpaste to in-office laser and restorative options.

This introductory review provides a diagnostic and treatment framework for Korean practitioners, with future directions pointing toward nanomaterials and biomimetic approaches.

## 세줄요약
Narrative review(강남세브란스 + 서울대 보존과, JKDA 2022 특집): 한국 임상의를 위한 DH 유병률·수역학설·진단·치료 종합 프레임워크.

유병률 8–57%(연구별 다양); 자가 보고와 임상 평가 간 진단 불일치; 치료는 가정용 치약에서 레이저·수복치료까지.

나노소재·생체모방 방향의 미래 동향을 제시하며 JKDA DH 특집 3편의 도입부 역할.

## Summary

## Mechanism
- **Hydrodynamic theory (Brännström)**: 노출된 상아질 세관 내 유체 이동 → odontoblast layer 자극 → 통증

## Treatment Options
1. **At-home**: desensitizing toothpaste (KNO3, NovaMin, arginine)
2. **In-office (occlusion)**: oxalate, glutaraldehyde, varnish
3. **In-office (sealing)**: laser (Nd:YAG, diode), resin sealant
4. **Restorative**: deep lesion → composite restoration
