## Three-line Summary

Retrospective patient-level data linkage of 5 years of patient complaints and staff incident reports at a large multi-site London hospital, testing whether complaints add independent patient-safety information.

Only 7.6% of complaints overlapped with an incident report, but those 446 overlapping cases were disproportionately serious incidents; complaints reported greater harm than staff reports in 60% of linked cases and surfaced 582 surrounding safety events staff had not recorded.

Complaints function as a complementary patient-safety surveillance source, capturing events invisible to staff reporting, and should be integrated with incident-report analysis rather than treated as a separate administrative function.

## Summary
Van Dael and colleagues linked five years of patient complaints with staff incident reports at a large London hospital to test whether complaints add safety information. Although only 7.6% of complaints overlapped with an incident report, those overlapping cases were disproportionately serious. Complaints reported greater harm than staff reports in 60% of linked cases, surfaced 582 adjacent safety events staff had not recorded, and frequently offered contesting causal accounts. The study reframes complaints as a complementary patient-safety surveillance source rather than mere dissatisfaction.

## Key Contributions
- Demonstrates patient-level data linkage between complaints and staff incident reports.
- Shows complaints capture surrounding safety events (n=582) that staff reports omit.
- Finds complaints describe greater harm in 60% of linked cases and contesting accounts in 46%.
- Argues for triangulating heterogeneous viewpoints to understand unsafe care.

## Methodology
Five years of patient complaints and staff incident reports from a large multi-site London hospital. Retrospective patient-level data linkage identified overlapping reports. Mixed quantitative coding + in-depth qualitative comparison of harm level, missed adjacent events, and combined narratives.

## Results
Overlapping incidents n=446 (7.6% of complaints, 0.6% of incident reports) but disproportionately Serious Incidents and severe harm. Linked complaints reported greater harm in 60% of cases, surfaced 582 surrounding safety events missed by staff, gave contesting causal accounts in 46% and complementary accounts in 26%.
