## Three-line Summary

Biomechanical rationale article (Compendium, 1994) for an implant-specific occlusal schema called "implant-protected occlusion".

The scheme combines implant orientation, load direction, implant surface area, occlusal table width and protection of the weakest area.

It is a rationale for a schema; no outcome data appear in the abstract, so weigh it against the later controversy sources.

## 세줄요약

생체역학 근거 논문 (Compendium, 1994) — "임플란트 보호 교합"이라는 임플란트 전용 교합 체계.

임플란트 방향, 부하 방향, 표면적, 교합면 폭, 가장 약한 부위 보호를 하나의 교합 철학으로 묶음.

체계의 근거 논문이며 초록에 결과 자료가 없으므로 이후 논쟁 자료와 대조할 것.

## Summary

This paper is the origin of "implant-protected occlusion" as a named occlusal philosophy. It reads as a rationale, and the controversy in the occlusion folder is where its claims get tested.
