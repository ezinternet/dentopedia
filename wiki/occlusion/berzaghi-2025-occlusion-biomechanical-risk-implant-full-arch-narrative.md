## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Berzaghi, Testori, Scaini, Bortolini; J Pers Med 2025) synthesizing evidence on occlusal and biomechanical risk factors in implant-supported full-arch fixed dental prostheses, covering cantilever length, prosthetic material choice, occlusal scheme selection, and parafunction.

Cantilever length, prosthetic material properties, occlusal scheme, and parafunctional habits all modulate biomechanical risk in full-arch implant prostheses; evidence-based risk-reduction design recommendations are provided, though no quantitative meta-analysis is performed.

As a narrative review, this paper provides a clinically useful synthesis but consensus-level rather than quantitatively graded guidance; clinicians managing full-arch cases with bruxism or extended cantilevers benefit from its risk-stratified design checklist.
