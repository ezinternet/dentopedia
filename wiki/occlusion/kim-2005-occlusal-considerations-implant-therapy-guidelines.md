## Three-line Summary

Guideline-oriented review (Clinical Oral Implants Research, 2005) on implant occlusion with a biomechanical rationale.

Overload factors are large cantilevers, parafunction, improper occlusal design and premature contacts; the advice is to keep implant occlusion within physiologic limits.

The authors state that no evidence-based implant-specific occlusal concept exists, so these are rationale-based recommendations.

## Summary

This paper gives the clinician a checklist of occlusal overload causes around implants and a guideline stance: keep implant occlusion physiologic. Its honest limitation (no implant-specific evidence) is as important as its advice.

## Key Contributions

- Checklist of overload factors for implant occlusion.
- Explicit statement of the implant-occlusion evidence gap.

## Methodology

Narrative review with biomechanical rationale.

## Results

Qualitative guidelines; no numeric thresholds.
