## Three-line Summary

A fully digital occlusal analysis protocol combining Trios 3 intraoral scanning with 3Shape Patient Specific Motion (PSM) was evaluated in 37 patients receiving 52 metal-free restorations.
Only 17.3% required subtractive and 7.7% additive occlusal finishing, demonstrating high accuracy of the digital workflow when scan fitting is verified against articulating paper contacts.
The study highlights that PSM reduces excursive-movement errors but cannot correct scan-fitting errors; clinical verification of scan fit against articulating paper is essential for minimal post-delivery adjustment.

구강 내 스캐너(Intraoral Scanner, IOS) Trios 3와 환자 맞춤 운동(Patient Specific Motion, PSM)을 결합한 완전 디지털 교합 분석 프로토콜을 37명 환자, 52개 보철물에 적용하여 평가하였다.
소삭 조정(Subtractive Adjustment) 17.3%, 첨가 조정(Additive Adjustment) 7.7%로 교합지 임상 검증을 병행한 디지털 워크플로우(Digital Workflow)의 높은 정확도를 확인하였다.
PSM은 측방 운동(Excursive Movement) 오류를 줄이나 스캔 피팅(Scan Fitting) 오류는 보정하지 못하므로 교합지 대조 검증이 임상 필수 단계이다.

## Summary

This pilot clinical audit evaluated a fully digital protocol for occlusal analysis in prosthodontic rehabilitation using the Trios 3 intraoral scanner and 3Shape Patient Specific Motion (PSM) technology. Thirty-seven patients received 52 metal-free restorations (primarily monolithic zirconia) across single and multi-unit cases on both natural teeth and implants. Scan fitting was verified against 21 µm articulating paper clinical contact marks before CAD design; MI contacts were reinforced with 25 µm additive morphing in CAD to counteract periodontal ligament compressibility artefacts. The result was 77% of restorations requiring no adjustment, with only 17.3% subtractive (chairside) and 7.7% additive (lab) finishing. The key insight distinguishing this study from prior PSM literature is that PSM controls dynamic/excursive occlusion errors but does not resolve intermaxillary scan-fitting inaccuracies; explicit verification of scan fit against clinical articulating paper contacts is indispensable.

## Key Contributions

- Step-by-step fully digital occlusal analysis protocol (IOS + PSM + CAD) for single/multi-unit metal-free restorations
- 77% of restorations required no occlusal adjustment; 17.3% subtractive (chairside only); 7.7% additive (lab only)
- Scan fit verification against 21 µm Accufilm II contacts is identified as the critical quality gate PSM alone cannot replace
- 25 µm additive MI contact morphing in CAD counters periodontal ligament compressibility artefacts in virtual articulation
- Monolithic zirconia with 0.2 mm milling resolution demonstrated clinically acceptable fit without master models

## Methodology

**Study design**: Prospective clinical audit / pilot case series; January–May 2023; single center.

**Patients (n=37, restorations n=52)**: Needed single or multi-unit (up to 3-unit) metal-free restorations on natural teeth or implants; excluded if complex occlusal therapy required (CR re-organization, VDO change).

**Digital workflow sequence**:
1. Initial IOS of prepared teeth (Trios 3, 3Shape A/S)
2. Temporaries placed and functionalized 4–6 weeks
3. Second scan session: working arch + temporaries, abutment/scan body (double-cord or scan body technique), antagonist, bilateral scan bites, PSM recording
4. Contacts marked intraorally with 21 µm red (excursive) / black (MI) Accufilm II during scanning
5. CAD (Dental System): sagittal view scan-fit verification; AFG wax-up; 25 µm/0.48 mm MI contact morphing
6. Milling: monolithic zirconia 850 (0.2 mm cutters); lithium disilicate from CAD/CAM-milled wax
7. Delivery: 8 µm Shimstock + Accufilm II verification before cementation/screw tightening

## Results

| Outcome | n | % |
|---|---|---|
| Restorations with no adjustment | ~40 | ~77% |
| Subtractive adjustment (chairside) | 9 | 17.3% |
| Additive adjustment (lab) | 4 | 7.7% |
| Restorations in monolithic zirconia | 33 patients | ~89% |
| Single crowns | 40 | 77% |
| Multi-unit restorations | 12 | 23% |
| Implant-supported single crowns | 8 | 15% |

All subtractive adjustments performed chairside; all additive adjustments returned to lab. No remakes reported.
