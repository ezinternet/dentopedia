## Three-line Summary

Selective narrative review (Implant Dentistry, 2005) arguing that occlusal overload on implants may increase peri-implant marginal bone loss.

The argument integrates cellular biomechanics, bone mechanics, animal work and clinical reports, and favours a force-management occlusal plan.

Qualitative conclusion without effect sizes; the question remains contested in the wiki.

## 세줄요약

선택적 서술 고찰 (Implant Dentistry, 2005) — 임플란트에 대한 교합 과부하가 주위 변연골 소실을 높일 수 있다고 주장.

생체역학, 골역학, 동물 및 임상 보고를 통합하고 힘 관리 중심의 교합 계획을 지지.

정량 효과 없이 정성적 결론; 이 질문은 위키에서 여전히 논쟁 중.

## Summary

This review is the implant-side case that occlusal overload contributes to marginal bone loss. It supports a force-management occlusal approach, but it is narrative and does not settle the controversy on its own.

## Key Contributions

- Force-management argument for occlusion around implants.
- Integration of mechanics, bone physiology and clinical sources.

## Methodology

Selective MEDLINE-assisted and hand-searched narrative review.

## Results

Qualitative: overload "may increase" marginal bone loss. No effect sizes in the abstract.
