## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 16 systematic reviews/meta-analyses (2012–2025; >15,000 restorations; follow-up 6–120 months) on direct restorations (conventional composite, bulk-fill, ormocer, GIC/RMGIC) in posterior teeth and non-carious cervical lesions (NCCLs), appraised with AMSTAR 2 and GRADE.

Conventional composites showed >90% 5-year survival (especially with multi-step adhesives); bulk-fill failure <7% in short-medium term; ormocer composites failed more (12.1%, mainly marginal degradation); GICs had lower survival; the RMGIC sandwich technique improved NCCL retention.

Longevity depends on material class, bonding strategy and patient risk; the adhesive protocol was a key prognostic factor across all materials. Only the abstract was available, so per-review numbers, AMSTAR 2/GRADE ratings and overlap handling could not be checked.

## 세줄요약

우산 리뷰 (Umbrella review): 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 16편 (2012–2025, 수복물 >15,000개, 추적 6–120개월)을 AMSTAR 2와 GRADE로 평가해 구치부 및 비우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesion, NCCL)의 직접수복 재료 (복합레진·벌크필·오모서·글래스아이오노머)의 임상 수명을 종합했다.

기존 복합레진은 5년 생존율 90% 초과 (특히 다단계 접착제), 벌크필은 단·중기 실패율 7% 미만, 오모서 (Ormocer) 복합레진은 실패율 12.1%로 유의하게 높았고 (주로 변연 열화), 글래스아이오노머 (GIC)는 생존율이 낮았으며, 레진강화형 글래스아이오노머 (RMGIC) 샌드위치 술식이 NCCL 유지력을 개선했다.

수명은 재료 종류·접착 전략·환자 위험도에 좌우되며 접착 프로토콜이 전 재료에 걸친 핵심 예후인자다; 초록만 확보되어 개별 리뷰 수치·AMSTAR 2/GRADE 등급·중복 처리는 확인하지 못했다.

## Summary

This umbrella review (J Esthet Restor Dent 2026) synthesized 16 systematic reviews and meta-analyses published 2012–2025, covering more than 15,000 direct restorations followed for 6 to 120 months. It compared conventional composites, bulk-fill resins, ormocer-based composites and glass ionomer cements (GIC/RMGIC) in posterior teeth and non-carious cervical lesions, using AMSTAR 2 for review quality and GRADE for certainty. Conventional and bulk-fill composites were the most reliable options, ormocers failed more often, GICs had lower survival but remain useful under high caries risk or moisture challenge, and an RMGIC sandwich base improved NCCL retention. The adhesive protocol emerged as a key prognostic factor across materials. This page is built from the abstract only.

## Key Contributions
- One synthesis spanning posterior restorations and NCCLs across composite, bulk-fill, ormocer and GIC/RMGIC materials.
- Names adhesive protocol (multi-step adhesives for composites) as a cross-material prognostic factor.
- States that the RMGIC sandwich technique improved retention in NCCLs, relevant to cervical resin retention loss.

## Methodology
Umbrella review of 16 SR/MAs (2012–2025); >15,000 restorations; follow-up 6–120 months; AMSTAR 2 and GRADE; outcomes were survival rates, failure types and comparative risk estimates (RR, OR). Search strategy, registration and overlap handling are not reported in the abstract.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Conventional composite | >90% 5-year survival, especially with multi-step adhesives |
| Bulk-fill resin | Similar short-medium-term outcomes; failure <7%; long-term data limited |
| Ormocer-based composite | Failure 12.1% (significantly higher), mainly marginal degradation |
| GIC | Lower survival; useful in high caries risk / moisture-challenged conditions |
| RMGIC sandwich technique | Improved retention in NCCLs |
| Key prognostic factor | Adhesive protocol, across all materials |
