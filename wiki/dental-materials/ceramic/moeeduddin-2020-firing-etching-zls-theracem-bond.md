## Three-line Summary

A controlled in-vitro study assessed how firing cycle number and HF etching parameters affect tensile bond strength (인장결합강도, Tensile Bond Strength, TBS) of TheraCem self-adhesive composite cement to Vita Suprinity ZLS (지르코니아 강화 리튬실리케이트, Zirconia-reinforced Lithium Silicate) ceramic. Etching duration (45 s optimal, 4.83 MPa peak) and firing cycles (1 cycle highest at 4.00 MPa) significantly affected TBS, while etchant concentration did not (p = 0.31). Clinicians should prioritize adequate etching duration and single firing cycle over concentration variation when cementing ZLS restorations with TheraCem.

## 세줄요약

Vita Suprinity ZLS 세라믹에 대한 TheraCem 자가접착 복합레진시멘트의 TBS를 소성 횟수·불산 에칭 조건별로 정량 비교한 시험관 연구. 에칭 시간(45초 최고 4.83 MPa, p < 0.001)과 소성 횟수(1회 최고 4.00 ± 0.71 MPa, 비소성 최저 3.29 ± 0.53 MPa; p < 0.001)는 TBS에 유의미하였으나, 에칭 농도(p = 0.31)는 유의미하지 않았다. ZLS 수복물 접착 시 에칭 시간이 농도보다 더 중요한 변수이며, 소성 1회가 최적임을 시사한다.

## Summary

This in-vitro study evaluated how two bonding variables — the number of crystallization firing cycles and hydrofluoric acid (HF) etching protocol (duration and concentration) — affect the TBS of TheraCem self-adhesive composite resin cement to Vita Suprinity (ZLS ceramic, Dentsply Sirona).

Fifty-three ZLS specimens (12.5 × 14 × 2 mm) were subjected to different combinations of firing cycles and etching conditions, then cemented to titanium rods with TheraCem. TBS was measured and analyzed with least-squares means linear regression and Tukey HSD post-hoc test.

Key finding: etching duration and firing cycle count were the decisive variables; etchant concentration was not statistically significant.

## Key Contributions

1. Etching duration, not concentration, is the controlling variable for TBS of TheraCem to Vita Suprinity
2. 45-second HF etching yields the highest TBS peak (4.83 MPa)
3. A single crystallization firing cycle produces higher TBS than multiple cycles or no firing
4. As-machined (unfired) ZLS specimens yielded the lowest TBS (3.29 ± 0.53 MPa)

## Methodology

- **Material**: Vita Suprinity PC (ZLS; 12.5 × 14 × 2 mm discs; n = 53)
- **Cement**: TheraCem (self-adhesive composite resin cement)
- **Bonded to**: titanium rods (tensile loading fixture)
- **Independent variables**:
  - Firing cycles: 0 (as-machined), 1, 2, 3
  - HF etching duration: 20, 45, 60 s
  - HF etchant concentration: multiple levels
- **Statistical analysis**: Least-square means linear regression + Tukey HSD (α = 0.05)

## Results

### Firing Cycle vs TBS

| Firing condition | TBS (MPa) |
|---|---|
| 1 cycle (highest) | 4.00 ± 0.71 |
| As-machined / 0 cycles (lowest) | 3.29 ± 0.53 |
| 2–3 cycles | Intermediate (p < 0.001 overall) |

### Etching Duration vs TBS

| HF etching duration | TBS (MPa) |
|---|---|
| 45 s (peak) | 4.83 |
| 20 s (lowest) | 3.02 ± 0.28 |
| 60 s | Intermediate |

### Etchant Concentration

- Effect on TBS: **not significant** (p = 0.31)
