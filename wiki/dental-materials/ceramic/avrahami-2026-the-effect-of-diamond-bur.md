## Three-line Summary

In vitro SEM study (Dentistry Journal 2026): 32 zirconia-reinforced lithium silicate (ZLS, Celtra Duo) single crowns ground consecutively on one CEREC MC XL 4-axis unit with a single diamond bur set, divided into 4 groups of n=8 by grinding sequence so bur life fell from 73% remaining to 3%; marginal gap measured at 4 sites × 3 measurements per crown at ×250 after temporary cementation under 50 N.

Total mean marginal gap showed no significant difference across wear groups — 47.20 ± 5.16, 44.62 ± 7.23, 54.14 ± 10.02 and 48.53 ± 7.87 µm for Groups 1–4 (F(3,28) = 2.143, p = 0.117) — and no site-specific effect (distal p = 0.277, mesial p = 0.732, palatal p = 0.251, buccal p = 0.084); every value stayed well under the 120 µm clinical limit.

A single bur set is therefore safe for at least 32 ZLS units on a 4-axis mill, but the non-monotonic pattern (the most worn group fitted better than Group 3) with n=8 per group means the study proves "no detectable harm within the manufacturer's 32-unit interval", not that bur wear is harmless beyond it.

## Summary

In vitro bench study testing whether cumulative diamond-bur wear degrades the marginal adaptation of chairside CAD/CAM ZLS single crowns. A single machine-prepared typodont maxillary canine (1.2 mm shoulder, 6° convergence, 2 mm incisal reduction) was scanned with a Primescan IOS and given one master design in CEREC SW 5.1.3 (90 µm radial/incisal spacer, 25 µm contacts, 1000/1500 µm minimum thickness, 50 µm margin). Thirty-two Celtra Duo crowns were then ground back-to-back on a CEREC MC XL 4-axis wet grinding unit using one fresh Step Bur 12S + Cylinder Pointed Bur 12S set, and grouped by sequence (1–8, 9–16, 17–24, 25–32) to represent progressive wear — the machine's wear readout put the burs at 73%, 57%, 30% and 3% remaining after crowns 8, 16, 24 and 32 respectively. Each crown was temporarily cemented (Temp-Bond NE) under 50 N static load and the marginal gap measured by SEM at ×250 at mid-buccal, mid-palatal, mid-mesial and mid-distal, three measurements per site.

One-way ANOVA found no significant group difference in total mean marginal gap (p = 0.117), nor any site-specific difference, and all means (44.62–54.14 µm) fell far below the 120 µm clinical acceptability threshold. The authors conclude clinicians can use a standard bur set for at least 32 ZLS single crowns without marginal compromise on a 4-axis unit, and attribute the absence of linear deterioration (unlike the Tan & Dudley lithium disilicate data) to the CEREC electronic bur measurement (EBM) wear-compensation routine.

## Key Contributions

- Isolates **cumulative bur wear** as the independent variable for **marginal gap** rather than for 3D trueness/precision — the same group's earlier ZLS work measured global superimposition accuracy.
- Ties each group to the machine's own **wear readout** (73% → 57% → 30% → 3% remaining life), so the result maps directly onto the Dentsply Sirona replacement interval of ~32 ZLS units for 4-axis units.
- Clinical validation of that interval: every group mean stayed under 55 µm, i.e. roughly half the 120 µm limit, after 32 units of wear.
- Documents a **non-linear** wear–fit relationship, arguing against extrapolating linear degradation curves from lithium disilicate to ZLS.

## Methodology

- **Design/n**: in vitro, 32 ZLS (CELTRA DUO, Dentsply Sirona) single crowns on one typodont abutment; 4 groups × n=8 by grinding sequence; G*Power: n=8/group detects Cohen's d = 0.626 at 80% power.
- **Abutment**: Nissin UR31C Jacket plastic maxillary right canine, machine-prepared — 1.2 mm circumferential shoulder finish line, 6° axial convergence, 2 mm incisal reduction.
- **Digital chain**: Primescan IOS → STL → CEREC SW 5.1.3 master design (radial spacer 90 µm, incisal spacer 90 µm, proximal/incisal/dynamic contact 25 µm, radial minimum thickness 1000 µm, incisal minimum thickness 1500 µm, margin thickness 50 µm, ramp width 50 µm, ramp angle 60°).
- **Grinding**: CEREC MC XL 4-axis wet grinding, fresh Step Bur 12S + Cylinder Pointed Bur 12S at start, 32 crowns consecutively; wear percentage logged from the software until the system alerted failure/replacement.
- **Measurement**: temporary cementation (Temp-Bond NE Unidose KaVo Kerr) with 50 N constant axial load (Lutron FG-20KG); SEM JSM-IT100 at ×250; mid-buccal, mid-palatal, mid-mesial, mid-distal × 3 measurements = 12 per crown; crown removed with GC pliers and abutment cleaned with 96% ethanol between specimens.
- **Statistics**: Kolmogorov–Smirnov normality, one-way ANOVA, α = 0.05.

## Results

| Group (crowns) | Bur life after group | Total mean marginal gap |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 (1–8) | 73% | 47.20 ± 5.16 µm |
| Group 2 (9–16) | 57% | 44.62 ± 7.23 µm (lowest) |
| Group 3 (17–24) | 30% | 54.14 ± 10.02 µm (highest) |
| Group 4 (25–32) | 3% | 48.53 ± 7.87 µm |

- TMMG across groups: F(3,28) = 2.143, **p = 0.117** → no significant wear effect; null hypothesis accepted.
- Per-surface ANOVA: distal p = 0.277, mesial p = 0.732, palatal p = 0.251, buccal p = 0.084 (distal/palatal numerically higher, not significant — discussed as possible eccentric loading).
- Kolmogorov–Smirnov: normal distribution for all variables (p > 0.05).
- All 32 crowns within the McLean–von Fraunhofer 120 µm limit; range 44.62–54.14 µm is comparable to SEM-measured lithium disilicate crowns at 42–43 µm in the cited literature.
- Mechanism offered for the missing linear trend: Dentsply Sirona's electronic bur measurement (EBM) touch-trigger probing of axial length and radial diameter before each cycle.
- Contrast: Tan & Dudley, lithium disilicate, 12 crowns/group — 4-axis 100.40 µm vs 5-axis 101.08 µm (no unit difference) but gap increased steadily with bur age; differences attributed to material (LDS vs ZLS), scanner (Trios 3 vs Primescan), cement (removable adhesive vs Temp-Bond) and microscope (stereo vs SEM).
- Limitations: in vitro; one typodont, one material, one IOS, one grinding system; wear read only from the machine (no visual/dimensional bur inspection); n=8 underpowered for small effects.
