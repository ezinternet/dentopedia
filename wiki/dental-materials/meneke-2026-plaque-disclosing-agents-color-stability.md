## Three-line Summary

In vitro bench study of 80 disc-shaped specimens (4 restorative materials — 3 resin composites + 1 giomer, n=20 each, 10 per disclosing agent) measuring immediate color change after a single plaque-disclosing-agent application with CIEDE2000.

Mira-2-Ton two-tone solution produced clinically unacceptable (ΔE00 > 1.8) color change in all four materials — highest on Charisma Diamond (7.18 ± 2.65), lowest on Z250 (2.98 ± 1.13) — while the erythrosine-based ProPind gel stayed perceptible-but-acceptable (ΔE00 < 1.8; 1.35–1.63) with no material differences (p>0.05).

Delivering plaque disclosure as an erythrosine gel rather than a low-molecular-weight two-tone solution protects restoration aesthetics, but the single-application, no-saliva, no-polishing protocol limits clinical extrapolation.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 실험실 연구 — 4종 수복재료(레진 복합체 3종 + 자이오머 1종), 재료당 20개·착색제당 10개의 원반 시편(n=80)에 단회 착색제를 도포한 직후 CIEDE2000로 색변화 측정.

Mira-2-Ton two-tone 용액은 4종 모두에서 임상적 허용 불가(ΔE00 > 1.8) 색변화를 유발 — Charisma Diamond 최대 7.18 ± 2.65, Z250 최소 2.98 ± 1.13; 반면 에리스로신 기반 ProPind 젤은 전 군에서 인지 가능하나 임상적 허용(ΔE00 < 1.8, 1.35–1.63) 수준이었고 재료 간 차이 유의성 없음(p>0.05).

임상적으로는 저분자량 two-tone 용액보다 젤/에리스로신 제형 착색제를 선택하는 것이 수복물의 심미 수명 유지에 유리하나, 단회 도포·타액 부재·폴리싱 없음 프로토콜이라 일반화에는 한계가 있다.

## Summary

This in vitro laboratory study evaluated whether two plaque-disclosing agents discolor four restorative materials. Eighty disc-shaped specimens (8 mm × 2 mm), 20 per material — three resin composites (Z250, Charisma Diamond, Estelite Universal Flow Medium) and one giomer (Beautifil II), all A2 shade — were polished, stored in distilled water at 37°C for 24 h, and measured with a VITA Easyshade Compact spectrophotometer. Each material received 10 specimens of the two-tone solution Mira-2-Ton and 10 of the erythrosine-based ProPind gel; color change (ΔE00, CIEDE2000) was computed after a 10 s application, 30 s rinse and air-dry. All groups exceeded the perceptibility threshold (ΔE00 > 0.8). Mira-2-Ton caused clinically unacceptable change (ΔE00 > 1.8) in every material — Charisma Diamond highest (7.18 ± 2.65), Z250 lowest (2.98 ± 1.13), with a significant difference (p<0.05) — whereas ProPind remained clinically acceptable (ΔE00 < 1.8) in all groups with no material differences (p>0.05). The authors conclude that delivery form and dye chemistry (molecular weight) drive staining, and advise clinicians to select disclosing agents with attention to their composition.

## Key Contributions

- Compares two disclosing-agent *delivery forms/chemistries* (two-tone solution vs erythrosine gel) against restoration color stability using CIEDE2000 with standard perceptibility (0.8) and acceptability (1.8) thresholds.
- Shows the two-tone solution Mira-2-Ton produced clinically unacceptable discoloration in all four materials while the erythrosine gel ProPind stayed clinically acceptable — a difference driven by delivery form and dye molecular weight, not by material alone.
- Links material rankings to resin-matrix monomers (TCD-DI-HEA in Charisma Diamond; TEGDMA in Beautifil II; hydrophobic Bis-EMA in Z250; Bis-MPEPP in Estelite) and to colorant molecular weight (erythrosine 879.9 g/mol vs CI 45410 785.7 and CI 42090 797.9 g/mol).

## Methodology

In vitro bench design; n=80 specimens (G*Power: effect size 0.45, α 0.05, power 0.80), 20 per material × 4 materials, split 10/10 between the two disclosing agents. Materials: Z250 (micro-hybrid), Charisma Diamond (nanohybrid), Estelite Universal Flow Medium (supra-nano flowable), Beautifil II (giomer). Specimens were mold-cured (LED-B, 20 s, ≥1,200 mW/cm²), polished with Sof-Lex discs, and stored 24 h in distilled water at 37°C before baseline. Mira-2-Ton (fluid; C.I. 45410 + C.I. 42090) or ProPind gel (erythrosine) was applied 10 s, rinsed 30 s, air-dried. Color measured with VITA Easyshade Compact (3 readings, white background, calibrated); ΔE00 by CIEDE2000. Statistics: SPSS v27 — Kolmogorov-Smirnov, One-way ANOVA + Tukey, Kruskal-Wallis, Mann-Whitney U (p<0.05).

## Results

ΔE00 (mean ± SD) after each disclosing agent:

| Material | Mira-2-Ton ΔE00 | ProPind ΔE00 |
|---|---|---|
| Z250 | 2.98 ± 1.13 ᵃ | 1.42 ± 0.21 ᴬ |
| Charisma Diamond | 7.18 ± 2.65 ᵇᶜ | 1.35 ± 0.38 ᴬ |
| Estelite Universal Flow Medium | 3.26 ± 1.87 ᵃ | 1.63 ± 0.46 ᴬ |
| Beautifil II | 5.36 ± 3.03 ᵃᶜ | 1.49 ± 0.48 ᴬ |

- Thresholds: perceptible ΔE00 > 0.8, clinically unacceptable ΔE00 > 1.8. All groups were perceptible.
- Mira-2-Ton: unacceptable in all groups; Charisma Diamond highest (7.18 ± 2.65) vs Z250 lowest (2.98 ± 1.13), p<0.05; Z250 / Estelite / Beautifil II not significantly different from each other (p>0.05).
- ProPind: acceptable (ΔE00 < 1.8) in all groups, no significant material differences (p>0.05).
- Mann-Whitney U: Mira-2-Ton ΔE00 significantly higher than ProPind (p<0.05).
