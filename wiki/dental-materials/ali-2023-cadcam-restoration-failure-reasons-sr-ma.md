## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PubMed/Embase systematic review) identifying and quantifying the clinical failure modes of CAD/CAM restorations across ceramic material types.

Fracture was the dominant failure mode (55% of failures), followed by debonding/detachment (20%); restoration thickness <1 mm significantly increased fracture risk, and adequate occlusal thickness (≥1.5 mm) combined with strict adhesive protocols markedly reduced failure risk.

Posterior zirconia and nanoceramic restorations showed the highest fracture prevalence; adhesive protocol deviations were the primary driver of debonding failures.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA(PubMed/Embase): 세라믹 재료 유형별 CAD/CAM 수복물의 임상 실패 원인과 빈도를 정량화.

파절(Fracture)이 주요 실패 원인(전체의 55%), 이어서 탈락·탈접착(Debonding, 20%); 두께 <1 mm에서 파절 위험 유의하게 증가, 교합면 두께 ≥1.5 mm + 접착 프로토콜 준수 시 실패 위험 현저히 감소.

구치부 지르코니아·나노세라믹에서 파절 빈도 최고; 접착 프로토콜 이탈이 탈접착의 주요 원인.

## Summary
SR+MA identifying and quantifying failure modes of CAD/CAM restorations in clinical studies.

## Key Contributions
- Fracture: 55% of failures; most common in posterior zirconia and nanoceramic
- Debonding/detachment: 20% of failures; adhesive protocol deviations major cause
- Restoration thickness <1mm significantly increases fracture risk

## Methodology
PubMed/Embase systematic review + meta-analysis.

## Results
Fracture dominant failure mode. Adequate thickness (≥1.5mm occlusal) and strict adhesive protocol reduce failure risk significantly.
