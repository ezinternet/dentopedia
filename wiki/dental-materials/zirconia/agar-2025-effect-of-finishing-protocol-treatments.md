## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (60 super translucent zirconia plates, Vita YZ ST A1, 7×11×2 mm; n=20 per group — Polished, Glazed, Chroma+Glaze) measuring color difference (ΔEab, ΔE00) and translucency parameter (TPab, TP00) before vs after 10,000 brushing cycles (200 g, 1.5 Hz; ~1 year of oral hygiene).

Finishing protocol significantly changed translucency (TP00 H=46.79/21.09, both p<0.001; Chroma+Glaze lowest TPab 4.71–4.8) and color stability (ΔEab H=13.21, p=0.0014; ΔE00 H=9.14, p=0.0104), with Chroma+Glaze most stable (median ΔE00 ≈ 0.33) and Polished least stable (ΔEab = 1.02), while brushing itself produced no significant within-group TP change (all p>0.64).

Clinical implication: after occlusal adjustment, stain+glaze buys color stability and masking at the cost of translucency, and all protocols stayed under the perceptibility threshold (1.8–2.6) — but this is one brand, in vitro, without thermocycling or surface-roughness measurement.

## 세줄요약

In-vitro 연구: 초투명 지르코니아 (super translucent zirconia) 60개(Vita YZ ST A1, 7×11×2 mm), 마무리 프로토콜 3군(연마/글레이징/Chroma+Glaze) 각 20개 — 10,000회 칫솔질(200 g, 1.5 Hz, 약 1년 분량) 전후 색차(ΔEab, ΔE00)와 반투명도 (translucency parameter, TP) 비교.

마무리 프로토콜이 반투명도(TP00 H=46.79/21.09, 모두 p<0.001; Chroma+Glaze 최저 TPab 4.71–4.8)와 색 안정성(ΔEab H=13.21, p=0.0014; ΔE00 H=9.14, p=0.0104)에 유의한 영향 — 색차 최소 Chroma+Glaze(ΔE00 중앙값 ≈0.33), 최대 연마군(ΔEab = 1.02); 칫솔질 자체의 군내 TP 변화는 유의하지 않음(모두 p>0.64).

임상 의의: 교합조정 후 스테인+글레이즈는 색 안정성·차폐를 얻는 대신 반투명도를 잃고, 모든 군이 지각역치(perceptibility threshold, 1.8–2.6) 이내 — 단 단일 브랜드·in vitro·열순환 없음·표면거칠기 (surface roughness, Ra) 미측정.

## Summary

Žagar et al. (2025) asked a question that sits at the end of every occlusal adjustment appointment: does the finishing protocol chosen for a super translucent zirconia restoration still look right after a year of toothbrushing? Sixty plates of Vita YZ ST (A1) were finished three ways — polished, glazed, or stained plus glazed — and measured spectrophotometrically against white and black backgrounds before and after 10,000 brushing cycles (200 g, 1.5 Hz).

The finishing protocol mattered for **both** optical outcomes, but brushing itself did not measurably move translucency (all within-group p>0.64). Chroma + Glaze produced the lowest translucency (TPab 4.71–4.8; significantly below both Glazed and Polished) and the smallest color change (median ΔE00 ≈ 0.33, significantly better than Polished only). The polished surface was the least optically stable (ΔEab = 1.02) and gained the most translucency after wear, which the authors attribute to uncontrolled surface roughening. All means stayed below the perceptibility threshold (1.8–2.6), so the practical trade is not "acceptable vs unacceptable" but **masking and color stability versus light transmission**.

## Key Contributions

1. **Optical cost of the finishing choice is quantified** — the axis most adjustment/grinding studies (strength, phase transformation, antagonist wear) leave unmeasured
2. **Stain + glaze = most color-stable** (median ΔE00 ≈ 0.33; vs Polished adj p=0.00059 for ΔEab / p=0.0076 for ΔE00), but not significantly better than glaze alone (p=0.359 / p=0.717)
3. **Stain + glaze = least translucent** (TPab 4.71–4.8; lower than Glazed p<0.0001 and Polished p=0.0147–0.0152 for TP00) — clinically usable as intentional masking in the anterior zone
4. **Polishing alone is optically the weakest option** (highest ΔEab 1.02, greatest TP increase after brushing) despite being the simplest and most wear-friendly finish mechanically
5. **Wear does not shift TP significantly** over 1 simulated year — translucency is set by the surface protocol more than by brushing abrasion

## Methodology

- 60 plates 7×11×2 mm milled from Vita YZ ST shade A1 (CAD/CAM), sintered 1530 °C, thickness verified ±0.01 mm
- Three groups (n=20): **P** polisher (Komet ZrO2) + polishing paste 2 min; **G** Vita Lumex Unique 370 glaze fired 750 °C/1 min under vacuum; **CG** Vita Lumex Unique 330 Red-Brown Chroma A + Vita Lumex AC 370 glaze, no polishing
- Color: VITA Easyshade V, recalibrated per set, 3 readings/specimen, white + black backgrounds, single operator
- Outcomes: ΔEab (CIELAB), ΔE00 (CIEDE2000), TPab, TP00
- Wear: mechanical brushing simulator, 10,000 cycles, medium bristles, standard toothpaste, 200 g, 1.5 Hz (≈1 year)
- G*Power a priori (f=0.25, α=0.05, power 0.80 → ≥18); Shapiro–Wilk → non-parametric: Kruskal–Wallis, Mann–Whitney U or Dunn (Bonferroni), Wilcoxon signed-rank; α=0.05

## Results

| Outcome | Statistic |
|---|---|
| TPab before / after | H=43.48 / H=18.67, both p<0.001 |
| TP00 before / after | H=46.79 / H=21.09, both p<0.001 |
| ΔEab among groups | H=13.21, p=0.0014 |
| ΔE00 among groups | H=9.14, p=0.0104 |

- CG translucency lowest: vs G p<0.0001 (before), p<0.001 (after); vs P p<0.0001 and p=0.0147 (TP00) / p=0.0152 (TPab); G vs P p>0.20
- CG TPab 4.71–4.8 (most opaque); Polished/Glazed higher and not different from each other
- No within-group before/after TP change (all p>0.64)
- Color: CG vs P significant (ΔEab adj p=0.00059; ΔE00 adj p=0.0076); CG vs G (p=0.359 / p=0.717) and G vs P (p=0.215 / p=0.257) not significant
- CG median ΔE00 ≈ 0.33 (imperceptible); P ΔEab = 1.02 (highest, most variable); all means below perceptibility threshold 1.8–2.6
