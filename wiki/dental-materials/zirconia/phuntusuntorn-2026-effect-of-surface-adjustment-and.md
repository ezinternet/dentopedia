## Three-line Summary

Finishing and polishing (FP) substantially increased biaxial flexural strength in 3Y-TZP (1213.7 → 1704.3 MPa, ~40%) and moderately in 5Y-PSZ (694.2 → 993.7 MPa), while 6Y-PSZ showed no significant change (1015.6 → 1065.0 MPa). Simulated hydrothermal aging (134°C, 0.2 MPa, 5 h autoclave ≈ 15–20 years in vivo) did not significantly affect strength, hardness, or surface roughness in any of the three zirconia materials, and no monoclinic phase was detected by qualitative XRD. SEM revealed localized grain-boundary microcracking in aged 3Y-TZP, indicating early low-temperature degradation (LTD) onset without measurable mechanical consequences under the applied conditions.

## 세줄요약

마무리·연마(FP)는 3Y-TZP의 이축 굴곡강도를 1213.7→1704.3 MPa(~40%)로 크게 향상시키고 5Y-PSZ에는 중등도(694.2→993.7 MPa) 개선 효과를 보였으며, 6Y-PSZ는 변화가 미미했다. 모의 열수 노화(134°C·0.2 MPa·5시간 ≈ 임상 15~20년)는 세 재료 모두에서 강도·경도·표면 거칠기에 유의한 영향을 주지 않았고, 정성적 X선 회절에서 단사정상(monoclinic)도 검출되지 않았다. SEM에서 노화 후 3Y-TZP에 국소적 결정립 경계 미세균열이 관찰되어 초기 저온열화(LTD) 징후가 나타났으나 기계적 성능 저하로는 이어지지 않았다.

## Summary

This in vitro study from Chiang Mai University compared three CAD/CAM zirconia types — conventional 3Y-TZP (DD BioZX2), high-translucency 5Y-PSZ (DD cubeX2), and 6Y-PSZ (Nacera Pearl Natural) — under four experimental conditions: control (no surface adjustment), surface adjustment (grinding with 100 µm diamond bur + three-step ZiLMaster polishing), hydrothermal aging alone, and surface adjustment followed by aging. Disc-shaped specimens (12 mm diameter × 1.2 mm thickness, n = 10/group, N = 120 total) were tested per ISO 6872:2015 for biaxial flexural strength (piston-on-three-ball), surface roughness (profilometer), surface hardness (nanoindentation), grain size (SEM + ImageJ), and phase composition (qualitative XRD).

## Key Contributions

1. **Polishing substantially improves 3Y-TZP strength**: FP increased biaxial flexural strength from 1213.7 MPa (control) to 1704.3 MPa (~40%), attributable to surface flaw removal and possible compressive residual stress generation.
2. **Moderate benefit for 5Y-PSZ, none for 6Y-PSZ**: FP raised 5Y-PSZ from 694.2 to 993.7 MPa (moderate); 6Y-PSZ remained at ~1015–1065 MPa regardless of surface treatment, reflecting its predominantly cubic microstructure.
3. **Hydrothermal aging did not compromise bulk mechanical properties**: Under the applied 5 h autoclave protocol, no significant changes in biaxial flexural strength, surface hardness, or surface roughness were observed in any group.
4. **No monoclinic phase detected by XRD**: Neither FP nor aging induced detectable tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation within qualitative XRD limits, though slight peak broadening in 3Y-TZP after grinding suggests residual lattice strain.
5. **Early LTD signs in aged 3Y-TZP by SEM**: Grain-boundary microcracking and localized grain pull-out were visible in aged 3Y-TZP, indicating subcritical degradation at the surface not yet reflected in bulk strength.
6. **Surface roughness after FP remained ≤ 0.20 µm** (the clinically accepted bacterial adhesion threshold) in all materials, even after subsequent aging.

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Study type | In vitro |
| Materials | 3Y-TZP (DD BioZX2), 5Y-PSZ (DD cubeX2), 6Y-PSZ (Nacera Pearl Natural); all Dental Direkt |
| Specimen geometry | Disc 12 mm diameter × 1.2 mm thickness; n = 10/group, N = 120 |
| Milling | 5-axis milling (Xmill 500 Plus); sintering 1500°C/120 min (3/5Y), 1450°C/90 min (6Y) |
| Surface adjustment (G2) | 100 µm diamond bur at 200,000 rpm × 20 s, then 3-step ZiLMaster polishing (Shofu), 1 min/step |
| Hydrothermal aging | Autoclave 134°C, 0.2 MPa, 5 h (ISO 13356; ≈ 15–20 years in vivo per Chevalier et al.) |
| Flexural strength | Piston-on-three-ball biaxial test (ISO 6872:2015); crosshead speed 0.5 mm/min |
| Surface roughness | Contact profilometer (Surftest SJ-301), Ra in µm |
| Hardness | Nanoindentation (DUH-211, Shimadzu), Berkovich indenter, 2940 mN max load |
| Phase analysis | Qualitative XRD (D8 ADVANCE, Bruker), Cu-Kα, 20–80° 2θ |
| Grain size | SEM × 10,000 + ImageJ (≥ 300 grains/specimen), thermal etch 1400°C/30 min |
| Statistics | Three-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD, α = 0.05 |

## Results

**Biaxial Flexural Strength (MPa):**

| Material | Control | Control + Aging | FP | FP + Aging |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3Y-TZP | 1213.7 | 1153.5 | **1704.3** | 1674.6 |
| 5Y-PSZ | 694.2 | 651.3 | **993.7** | 871.5 |
| 6Y-PSZ | 1015.6 | 986.3 | 1065.0 | 1019.0 |

FP × Zirconia type interaction was significant (p < 0.05, ηp² = 0.23). Aging had no significant effect (p > 0.05, ηp² = 0.02).

**Surface Roughness (Ra, µm):** FP significantly reduced Ra in all materials (p < 0.05). Post-FP values for 3Y-TZP reached Ra ≈ 0.13 µm, well below the 0.20 µm clinical threshold. Aging caused non-significant slight increases.

**Surface Hardness:** Zirconia type was the only significant factor (p < 0.05). FP and aging had no significant effect on hardness.

**XRD Phase Analysis:** No monoclinic peaks at 28.2° or 31.5° (2θ) in any group. Slight peak broadening in 3Y-TZP after FP, consistent with residual lattice strain.

**SEM:** Aged 3Y-TZP showed grain-boundary microcracking and localized grain pull-out (early LTD). Aged 5Y-PSZ and 6Y-PSZ showed no significant surface morphology changes.
