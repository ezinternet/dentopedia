## Three-line Summary

Speed sintering does not significantly affect flexural strength (p = 0.245) or translucency (p = 0.478) in Katana UTML (5Y-PSZ), Katana STML (4Y-PSZ), or DD Cube Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP) zirconia. Speed sintering reduces grain size in all materials and produces a smoother surface topography in 3Y-TZP, while glazed surfaces are smoother than polished in 4Y-PSZ and 5Y-PSZ after aging. Higher yttria content increases translucency and cubic phase ratio but reduces flexural strength (508–568 MPa for 5Y-PSZ vs. 891–1081 MPa for 3Y-TZP) and monoclinic phase stability under aging.

## 세줄요약

고속 소결은 5Y-PSZ(카타나 UTML), 4Y-PSZ(카타나 STML), 3Y-TZP(DD Cube Bio ZX2) 모두에서 굴곡강도(p=0.245)와 반투명도(p=0.478)에 유의한 영향을 미치지 않았다. 고속 소결은 모든 재료에서 입자 크기를 감소시켰으며 3Y-TZP에서는 균일한 표면을 형성했고, 에이징 후 4Y-PSZ/5Y-PSZ에서는 광택(글레이징)이 연마보다 더 매끄러운 표면을 나타냈다. 이트리아 함량 증가는 반투명도·큐빅상을 높이고 굴곡강도(3Y-TZP: 891–1081 MPa, 5Y-PSZ: 508–568 MPa)와 에이징 후 단사정상 형성을 감소시켰다.

## Summary

This in-vitro study evaluated the combined effects of sintering speed and surface finishing on optical, mechanical, microstructural, and surface properties of three commercially available monolithic zirconia ceramics differing in yttria content. 180 specimens (12×12×1.2 mm) were fabricated, sintered either conventionally or with a speed protocol, then glazed or polished with a clinical polishing system, and subsequently subjected to hydrothermal aging (134°C, 5h). Translucency parameter, biaxial flexural strength (ISO 6872), XRD phase composition, SEM grain size, and AFM surface roughness were measured before and after aging.

The principal finding is that speed sintering is clinically equivalent to conventional sintering in terms of flexural strength and translucency across all three zirconia generations. This validates single-visit chairside delivery workflows for CAD/CAM monolithic zirconia restorations. The yttria content—rather than sintering speed or surface treatment—is the dominant variable governing optical and mechanical properties and aging susceptibility.

## Key Contributions

1. **Speed sintering validation across three zirconia generations**: No significant effect on flexural strength or translucency for 3Y-TZP, 4Y-PSZ, or 5Y-PSZ
2. **Grain size reduction**: Speed sintering consistently reduced average grain size across all materials (SEM, ×10000)
3. **Material-dependent phase stability**: 5Y-PSZ fully stable; 4Y-PSZ develops monoclinic phase only post-aging (especially in speed+polished: 8.9% vs. 4% conventional); 3Y-TZP shows monoclinic phase before and after aging
4. **Surface finishing trade-offs**: Glazed groups smoother than polished in 4Y-PSZ and 5Y-PSZ post-aging; speed-sintered 3Y-TZP shows more homogeneous surface topography
5. **Clinical implication**: Zirconia polishing kits can be safely used after speed sintering for intraoral adjustments

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Study design** | In vitro, 3-factor full factorial |
| **Materials** | Katana UTML (5Y-PSZ), Katana STML (4Y-PSZ), DD Cube Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP) |
| **Sample size** | 180 specimens, n=15/group |
| **Sintering** | Conventional vs. speed (In Fire HTC speed; Dentsply Sirona) |
| **Surface treatment** | Grinding+polishing (EVE Diacera RA 322) vs. glazing (Ivoclar Vivadent, 840°C/1 min) |
| **Aging** | Autoclave, 134°C, 0.2 MPa, 5h |
| **Flexural strength** | Piston-on-3-ball (P3B), ISO 6872 (Instron 4467, 1 mm/min) |
| **Translucency (TP)** | Vita Easyshade V spectrophotometer (CIE L*a*b*) |
| **Phase analysis** | XRD (Empyrean, CuKα, 2θ 5–90°) + Rietveld refinement (TOPAS V3.0) |
| **Grain size** | FE-SEM (Merlin, Carl Zeiss) ×10000 + thermal etching 1200°C/20 min |
| **Surface roughness** | AFM (Hitachi 5100N, tapping mode, 20 µm² area) |
| **Statistics** | 3-way ANOVA, 1-way ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, Bonferroni/Tukey |

## Results

### Flexural Strength

No significant effect of sintering speed (p < 0.05 only for material difference; speed sintering p < 0.05 overall but not within-material), surface finishing (p = 0.189), or their interactions (p > 0.05) within each material.

| Material | Speed+Polished | Speed+Glazed | Conv+Polished | Conv+Glazed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Katana UTML (5Y-PSZ) | 508 ± 32 MPa | 515 ± 43 MPa | 569 ± 50 MPa | 548 ± 42 MPa |
| Katana STML (4Y-PSZ) | 669 ± 57 MPa | 660 ± 74 MPa | 707 ± 53 MPa | 695 ± 43 MPa |
| DD Cube Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP) | 983 ± 122 MPa | 891 ± 101 MPa | 1081 ± 209 MPa | 1081 ± 83 MPa |

### Translucency

Only material type significantly affected translucency (p < 0.05). Sintering speed, surface treatment, and aging did not produce significant changes (p > 0.05). Ranking: Katana UTML (TP ≈ 22–25) > Katana STML (TP ≈ 21–23) > DD Cube Bio ZX2 (TP ≈ 7–11).

### Phase Composition (XRD, Rietveld refinement)

| Material | Condition | Tetragonal % | Cubic % | Monoclinic % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UTML (5Y-PSZ) | Before aging | 44–48 | 52–56 | 0 |
| UTML (5Y-PSZ) | After aging | 47–49 | 51–53 | 0 |
| STML (4Y-PSZ) | Before aging | 48 | 52 | 0 |
| STML (4Y-PSZ) | Speed+Polished, after aging | 47.5 | 43.6 | **8.9** |
| STML (4Y-PSZ) | Conv+Polished, after aging | 48 | 48 | **4.0** |
| DD Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP) | Speed+Polished, before aging | 69.7 | 25.3 | 5.0 |
| DD Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP) | Speed+Polished, after aging | 62 | 22 | **16.0** |
| DD Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP) | Conv+Polished, before aging | 60.4 | 22.8 | 15.8 |
| DD Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP) | Conv+Polished, after aging | 64.4 | 15.9 | **19.8** |

### Grain Size (SEM, after aging)

| Material | Speed sintered (µm) | Conventional (µm) |
|---|---|---|
| Katana UTML | 1.60 | 1.95 |
| Katana STML | 1.04 | 1.15 |
| DD Cube Bio ZX2 | 0.50 | 0.78 |

Speed sintering consistently reduced grain size across all materials.

### Surface Topography (AFM)

- **4Y-PSZ and 5Y-PSZ**: Glazed groups smoother than polished groups after aging (AFM)
- **3Y-TZP**: Speed-sintered groups showed more homogeneous (smoother) surface than conventionally sintered
