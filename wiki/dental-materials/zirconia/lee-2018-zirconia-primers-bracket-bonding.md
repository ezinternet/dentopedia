## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (Restor Dent Endod 2018) comparing shear bond strength (SBS) and Adhesive Remnant Index (ARI) of ceramic brackets bonded to zirconia using four surface treatment protocols — Scotchbond Universal, Z-Prime Plus, Zirconia Liner, Metal/Zirconia Primer, and untreated control.

Scotchbond Universal (MDP-containing, self-priming) achieved the highest SBS and ARI score 3 (cohesive failure within adhesive), outperforming all dedicated zirconia primers; untreated control showed the lowest SBS.

Scotchbond Universal eliminates the need for a separate zirconia primer and provides a clinically ideal failure mode (bracket debonds through adhesive, not ceramic fracture), making it the preferred choice for orthodontic bracket bonding to zirconia restorations.

## Summary
In vitro study comparing SBS of ceramic brackets bonded to zirconia using four different primers/adhesives plus control. Scotchbond Universal (MDP-containing universal adhesive, no separate primer) achieved highest SBS and cohesive-type failure, outperforming dedicated zirconia primers. Practical finding for orthodontic treatment of patients with zirconia restorations.

## Key Contributions
- Scotchbond Universal self-primes zirconia — embedded MDP sufficient without separate zirconia primer
- ARI 3 (cohesive failure in adhesive): clinically ideal — bracket debonds through adhesive, not ceramic
- Dedicated zirconia primers (Z-Prime, Zirconia Liner): lower SBS than universal adhesive with integrated MDP
- Clinical implication: for orthodontic bracket bonding to zirconia, use MDP-containing universal adhesive
