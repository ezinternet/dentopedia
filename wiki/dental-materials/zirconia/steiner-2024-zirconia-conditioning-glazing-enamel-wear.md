## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (Clin Oral Investig 2024) tested 3Y-TZP zirconia under 6 surface conditions (polished, sintered, rough ± glaze) against natural enamel over 10,000 cycles using 3D volumetric scanning.

Polishing alone produced the least antagonist enamel wear (0.4 mm³); glazing over a rough surface did not reduce wear to the same level as polishing, demonstrating that the surface condition beneath the glaze is the primary determinant of antagonist wear.

Clinically, if zirconia requires chairside adjustment, polishing the adjusted area is essential — glazing over the roughened surface does not adequately protect the opposing enamel.

## Summary
2024 study demonstrating that the surface condition BENEATH the glaze determines antagonist enamel wear. Glazing over a rough surface does not achieve the same protection as polishing alone — only polishing as a solo treatment minimizes wear.

## Key Contributions
- 6 groups; 10,000 cycles; 3D volumetric scan
- Volume loss order (most→least): rough Zr > glazed-rough > glazed-sintered > 46μm-finish ≈ control > glazed-polished > **polished alone (0.4mm³)**
- Key message: **glazing does not cancel out underlying roughness**
- Polish → glaze: better than rough → glaze, but still worse than polish alone
- Sub-surface condition is the key determinant of antagonist wear
