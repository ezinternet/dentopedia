## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study comparing conventional sintering (~8h) vs high-speed sintering (~90 min) for two translucent zirconia materials (3Y-TZP and 5Y-PSZ): high-speed sintering produced no significant differences in flexural strength, translucency (TP), or LTD susceptibility vs conventional.

Microstructural differences were minor: high-speed specimens showed slightly smaller grain size but equivalent phase composition and mechanical integrity under LTD testing.

Conclusion: high-speed sintering is clinically validated for translucent zirconia — chairside same-day fabrication using speed protocols does not compromise LTD resistance or mechanical strength.

## Summary

Liu et al. (2022) validated high-speed sintering protocols for translucent zirconia — a clinically important question given the increasing use of chairside CAD/CAM fabrication systems. The study demonstrated that the dramatically shorter sintering time (~90 min vs ~8h) does not compromise the material's LTD resistance, flexural strength, or optical properties. This finding supports the use of speed sintering for same-day restorations without concerns about accelerated clinical degradation compared to conventional sintering.

## Key Contributions

1. **Speed sintering validated**: ~90min high-speed protocol = equivalent LTD resistance vs ~8h conventional
2. **Flexural strength equivalent**: no significant difference in FS between conventional and high-speed groups
3. **Translucency equivalent**: TP not significantly different between sintering protocols
4. **Minor grain size difference**: high-speed = slightly smaller grains, but clinically non-significant
5. **5Y-PSZ confirmed LTD-resistant**: consistent with Kengtanyakich (2020) under speed-sintered conditions

## Methodology

- Two translucent zirconia: 3Y-TZP and 5Y-PSZ (commercial CAD/CAM blocks)
- Conventional sintering: standard manufacturer protocol (~8h total)
- High-speed sintering: fast-fire protocol (~90 min)
- LTD: autoclave hydrothermal aging (standard protocol)
- XRD: phase composition + monoclinic%; SEM: grain size; TP measurement; biaxial FS (ISO 6872)
- Statistical analysis: t-test + 1-way ANOVA

## Results

| Parameter | Conventional | High-Speed | Significant Difference? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flexural strength | Baseline | Equivalent | No |
| Translucency (TP) | Baseline | Equivalent | No |
| LTD (monoclinic%) | Baseline | Equivalent | No |
| Grain size | Slightly larger | Slightly smaller | Minor, not clinically relevant |

- 5Y-PSZ: no t→m transformation under LTD in both sintering groups
- 3Y-TZP: equivalent LTD behavior in both sintering groups
