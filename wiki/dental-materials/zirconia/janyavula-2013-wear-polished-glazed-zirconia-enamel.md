## Three-line Summary
In vitro wear study (UAB Alabama wear device, J Prosthet Dent 2013) comparing polished, glazed, and polished+reglazed zirconia against veneering porcelain and enamel across five surface conditions.

Polished zirconia caused zero ceramic loss and the least antagonist enamel wear; glazed zirconia caused significant ceramic and enamel wear; veneering porcelain caused the most enamel wear (greatest Ra).

Surface roughness directly predicts antagonist enamel wear — polishing zirconia after any occlusal adjustment is clinically mandatory to minimize opposing tooth wear.

## 세줄요약
in-vitro 마모 연구(UAB Alabama 마모기, J Prosthet Dent 2013): 연마·유약·연마+재유약 지르코니아 vs 베니어 도재·에나멜, 5개 표면 조건 비교.

연마 지르코니아(Polished Zr): 세라믹 손실 없음 + 대합치 마모 최소; 유약 지르코니아: 세라믹·에나멜 마모 모두 유의; 베니어 도재: 대합치 마모 최대(Ra 최고).

표면조도(Ra)가 대합치 마모의 직접 예측 인자 — 교합 조정 후 반드시 연마하여 대합치 손상 최소화.

## Summary
In vitro wear study (UAB, Alabama wear device) establishing that polished zirconia is minimally abrasive to opposing enamel, while glazed zirconia causes significant enamel wear. Foundational paper for the "polish rather than glaze" recommendation.

## Key Contributions
- Surface roughness order (smooth→rough): polished Zr < glazed Zr < polished+reglazed Zr < veneering porcelain < enamel
- **Polished Zr**: zero ceramic loss; least enamel antagonist wear
- **Glazed Zr**: significant ceramic AND enamel wear
- **Veneering porcelain**: most enamel wear of all groups
- Surface roughness directly predicts antagonist enamel wear

## Wear Results
| Group | Ceramic wear | Enamel antagonist wear |
|-------|-------------|----------------------|
| Polished Zr | None | Least |
| Glazed Zr | Significant | Moderate |
| Polished + reglazed Zr | Significant | Moderate |
| Veneering porcelain | Most | Most |
| Enamel (control) | Moderate | Moderate |
