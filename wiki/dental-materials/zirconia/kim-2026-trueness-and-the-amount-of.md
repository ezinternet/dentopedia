## Three-line Summary

A within-patient clinical study compared trueness and chairside adjustment of DLP-printed zirconia crowns at three build angles (0°, 45°, 90°) versus conventionally milled crowns across 10 patients (40 crowns total). Crowns printed at 0° showed the best trueness (RMS 49.0 µm) and required the least occlusal (25.7 µm) and intaglio (20.6 µm) adjustment; milled crowns required the most adjustment, particularly at the intaglio surface (56.5 µm). Although all deviations fell within accepted clinical tolerances, build angle is a critical process parameter for optimizing DLP zirconia restoration fit and minimizing chairside adjustment burden.

## 세줄요약

DLP 프린팅 지르코니아 크라운을 0°·45°·90° 빌드 앵글과 밀링 방식으로 제작해 환자 내 비교(10명, 40크라운)한 임상연구. 0° 빌드 앵글이 적합도 (RMS 49.0 µm)와 교합면(25.7 µm)·인타글리오(20.6 µm) 조정량 모두 최소였으며, 밀링 크라운이 인타글리오 조정량 가장 큼(56.5 µm). 모든 편차가 임상 허용범위 내였으나, DLP 지르코니아에서 빌드 앵글 최적화(0°)가 의장 조정 부담 감소와 워크플로 효율 향상에 핵심 인자.

## Summary

This prospective within-patient clinical study evaluated how build angle in digital light processing (DLP, 디지털 광조형) additive manufacturing affects the trueness (적합도) and chairside adjustment of zirconia crowns (지르코니아 크라운) compared with conventionally milled crowns.

Ten patients each received four single-unit molar zirconia crowns: one milled and three DLP-printed at 0°, 45°, and 90° build angles (40 crowns total). Trueness was quantified as root mean square (RMS, 평균제곱근) surface deviation between intraoral scans of seated crowns and their CAD designs. Chairside adjustment was quantified as the RMS deviation between pre- and post-adjustment scans, separately for occlusal and intaglio surfaces.

The principal finding was that the 0° build angle produced the best trueness and required the least adjustment at both surfaces. Milled crowns showed trueness comparable to the 90° DLP group but required substantially more intaglio adjustment, reflecting geometric constraints of subtractive manufacturing. All measured deviations remained within commonly accepted clinical tolerances for single-unit fixed restorations.

## Key Contributions

1. **빌드 앵글 0°가 DLP 지르코니아 적합도 최우수**: 0°(49.0 µm) > 45°(63.3 µm) > 밀링(66.2 µm) > 90°(70.5 µm) 순; 45°·90°·밀링 간 차이는 비유의
2. **인타글리오 적합은 DLP 전 그룹이 밀링보다 우수**: 밀링(56.5 µm) vs DLP 0°(20.6 µm)/45°(25.4 µm)/90°(21.2 µm) — 절삭가공의 기하학적 한계(버 직경·공구경로 제약) 반영
3. **교합면 조정량도 0° DLP 최소**: 밀링 63.8 µm vs 0° 25.7 µm (Δ38.1 µm, p=0.011)
4. **0°와 45°는 등가성 검증 통과**: ±30 µm 및 ±50 µm 마진 내 등가(both p<0.001) — 해부학적 제약 시 45° 유효 대안
5. **Within-patient 설계로 개인 간 변동 통제**: 선형혼합효과모델(ICC 0.96–0.99)로 측정 반복성 확인

## Methodology

- **Study design**: Prospective within-patient; each patient = 1 milled + 3 DLP (0°/45°/90°); 40 crowns total
- **Restoration type**: Single-unit molar zirconia crowns
- **Trueness**: RMS of intraoral scan (post-cementation) vs CAD design file
- **Adjustment**: RMS of intraoral scan pre-adjustment vs post-adjustment (quantifies chairside material removal)
- **Analysis**: Linear mixed-effects model (fixed: manufacturing method; random intercept: patient); Tukey post-hoc; equivalence testing at ±30 µm and ±50 µm
- **Repeatability**: ICC [2,1] 0.96–0.99 (high)

## Results

| Manufacturing Method | Trueness RMS µm (SD) | Occlusal Adj µm (SD) | Intaglio Adj µm (SD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| DLP 0° | 49.0 (11.6) | 25.7 (7.8) | 20.6 (9.8) |
| DLP 45° | 63.3 (11.4) | NR | 25.4 (10.7) |
| DLP 90° | 70.5 (11.5) | NR | 21.2 (7.0) |
| Milled | 66.2 (13.4) | 63.8 (15.0) | 56.5 (26.2) |

NR = not separately reported in paired comparison text; overall method effect significant (p=0.003 occlusal, p<0.001 intaglio)

**Key statistical comparisons (Tukey-adjusted)**:
- Trueness: 0° better than all others (p=0.002–0.033); 45°/90°/milled not different from each other (p≥0.359)
- Intaglio adjustment: milled > all DLP groups (Δ31.1–35.9 µm, p≤0.004); DLP groups not different from each other (p≥0.853)
- Occlusal adjustment: milled > 0° (Δ38.1 µm, p=0.011)
