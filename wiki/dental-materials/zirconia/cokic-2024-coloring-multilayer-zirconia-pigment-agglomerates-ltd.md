## Three-line Summary

In-vitro microstructural study on Lava Esthetic multilayer zirconia (3M): coloring via liquid staining introduces Er₂O₃-HfO₂-Al₂O₃ pigment agglomerates (0.38–0.57µm grain size, locally reduced Y₂O₃) that act as selective LTD initiation sites — surrounding tetragonal grains undergo t→m transformation preferentially at these sites.

Pigment agglomerates show smaller local grain size (0.38–0.57µm vs bulk ~0.5µm) and lower yttria content — both factors increase LTD susceptibility at those microstructural defects.

Clinically, colored multilayer zirconia restorations may be more vulnerable to focal LTD initiation than uncolored counterparts, particularly at the high-monoclinic dentin-layer where yttria is lowest.

## Summary

Cokic et al. (2024) investigated why colored multilayer zirconia formulations may show different LTD behavior than uncolored versions. Through microstructural analysis, they identified that colorant pigments (Er₂O₃-HfO₂-Al₂O₃) form agglomerates with locally smaller grain size and lower yttria content — creating microstructural hot spots for LTD initiation. This work provides the mechanistic explanation for color-layer-specific LTD patterns in multilayer zirconia, particularly in the high-opacity dentin layer where colorant concentration and reduced Y₂O₃ are highest.

## Key Contributions

1. **Pigment agglomerates as LTD initiation sites**: Er₂O₃-HfO₂-Al₂O₃ agglomerates locally reduce yttria and grain size → preferential t→m transformation
2. **Microstructural mechanism identified**: not bulk composition but local microstructural heterogeneity drives focal LTD
3. **Color-layer stratification**: LTD susceptibility varies by layer in multilayer zirconia — not uniform
4. **Clinical implication**: colored restorations may accumulate localized LTD faster than spectrophotometric or XRD bulk measurements suggest

## Methodology

- Lava Esthetic multilayer zirconia (3M), colored and uncolored specimens
- Autoclave hydrothermal aging (standard LTD protocol)
- SEM/STEM-EDX: pigment agglomerate identification, grain size mapping, local yttria distribution
- XRD: bulk t→m monoclinic quantification before and after aging
- TEM-EDX for elemental mapping at agglomerate sites

## Results

- Pigment agglomerate composition: Er₂O₃ + HfO₂ + Al₂O₃
- Local grain size at agglomerates: 0.38–0.57µm (vs bulk average)
- Local Y₂O₃: significantly reduced at agglomerate sites
- t→m transformation preferentially at agglomerate periphery
- Bulk XRD monoclinic underestimates localized LTD — focal hotspots not captured by bulk measurement
