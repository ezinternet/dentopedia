## Three-line Summary
In vitro pull-out retention study (IJOD 2026) measured retention of CAD/CAM monolithic zirconia crowns at two abutment heights (2 mm vs 4 mm) cemented with TheraCem (calcium silicate self-adhesive resin) or glass ionomer cement.

Abutment height was the primary retention determinant (r=0.95 positive correlation), and TheraCem significantly outperformed glass ionomer at both 2 mm and 4 mm heights.

Even at the challenging 2 mm abutment height, TheraCem with monolithic zirconia achieved acceptable retention, supporting this combination as the preferred protocol for short clinical crown scenarios.

## 세줄요약
in-vitro(IJOD 2026): CAD/CAM 단일체 지르코니아 크라운을 지대치 높이 2mm vs 4mm에서 TheraCem(칼슘실리케이트 자가접착 레진) 또는 글라스아이오노머로 합착 후 인장 유지력 측정.

지대치 높이가 유지력의 주요 결정인자(r=0.95); TheraCem이 2mm·4mm 모두에서 글라스아이오노머보다 유의하게 우수.

2mm의 짧은 지대치에서도 TheraCem+단일체 지르코니아 조합이 허용 가능한 유지력을 제공하여 짧은 임상 크라운의 표준 선택으로 지지된다.

## Summary
In vitro study establishing the relationship between abutment height (2 vs 4 mm) and pull-out retention force of monolithic zirconia crowns cemented with TheraCem (calcium silicate self-adhesive resin) vs glass ionomer (GI). Strong positive correlation between abutment height and retention. TheraCem significantly outperforms GI cement at all heights, supporting its use for short clinical crown scenarios.

## Key Contributions
- Abutment height: primary retention determinant (r=0.95) — preparation design critical
- TheraCem: significantly superior to glass ionomer at both 2mm and 4mm heights
- Short clinical crown management: TheraCem + monolithic zirconia = reliable option
- Conservative preparation guidance: even 2mm abutment with TheraCem achieves acceptable retention
