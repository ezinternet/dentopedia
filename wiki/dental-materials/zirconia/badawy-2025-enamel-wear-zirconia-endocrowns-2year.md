## Three-line Summary
2-year prospective clinical study (J Esthet Restor Dent 2025; n=20 endocrowns; glazed vs polished CAD/CAM zirconia on endodontically treated mandibular first molars) measuring antagonist enamel wear with intraoral scanner + Geomagic software at 6, 12, 18, and 24 months.

Enamel wear was 0.422±0.130 mm (polished) vs 0.491±0.105 mm (glazed) at 2 years — no significant difference (p=0.108); 100% survival with no ceramic fractures in either group.

Both glazing and chairside polishing are clinically acceptable finishing methods for zirconia endocrowns; the in vitro finding of polished < glazed wear does not reach significance in this 2-year clinical context, possibly due to in vivo glaze wear leveling over time.

## 세줄요약
2년 전향적 임상연구(J Esthet Restor Dent 2025; n=20 엔도크라운; 유약 vs 연마 CAD/CAM 지르코니아, 근관치료된 하악 제1대구치): 구강 스캐너+Geomagic으로 6·12·18·24개월에 대합치 에나멜 마모 측정.

2년 에나멜 마모: 연마(Polished) 0.422±0.130 mm vs 유약(Glazed) 0.491±0.105 mm — 유의한 차이 없음(p=0.108); 두 군 모두 파절 없이 100% 생존.

유약과 체어사이드 연마 모두 지르코니아(Zirconia) 엔도크라운에 임상적으로 수용 가능한 마무리 방법; 연마 < 유약 마모라는 인비트로 결과는 이 2년 임상에서 유의하지 않았으며, 시간 경과에 따른 구강 내 유약 소실로 마모 패턴이 수렴했을 가능성 있음.

## Summary
2-year prospective clinical study comparing glazed vs polished monolithic zirconia endocrowns for antagonist enamel wear. Intraoral scanner + Geomagic software used for digital wear measurement. Both finishing methods produce clinically acceptable enamel wear with no significant difference.

## Key Contributions
- 20 endocrowns; CAD/CAM zirconia; endodontically treated lower first molars
- ZG (glazed, n=10) vs ZP (adjusted+polished, n=10)
- 100% survival at 2 years — no failures
- Enamel wear ZP: 0.422±0.130mm vs ZG: 0.491±0.105mm (NS, p=0.108)
- Digital measurement with intraoral scanner at 6, 12, 18, 24 months
- Clinical significance: glazing or chairside polishing both acceptable for endocrowns

## Note on In Vitro Discrepancy
In vitro studies (Janyavula, Park, Lawson) consistently show polished < glazed. This 2yr clinical study shows NO significant difference — possibly due to in vivo glaze wear smoothing out over time.
