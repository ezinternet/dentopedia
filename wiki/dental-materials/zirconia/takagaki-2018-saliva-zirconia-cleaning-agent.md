## Three-line Summary
In vitro XPS and bond strength study (Dent Mater J 2018) examined saliva-contaminated Y-PSZ (high-translucency yttria-partially stabilized zirconia) after four cleaning protocols (air abrasion, Ivoclean, phosphoric acid, no cleaning) using XPS surface elemental analysis plus resin cement SBS testing.

Air abrasion achieved the highest bond strength with lowest organic contamination; phosphoric acid cleaning left phosphorous residues confirmed by XPS that chemically blocked MDP adhesion to zirconia, reducing bond strength; Ivoclean was effective with no phosphorous residue.

Phosphoric acid must not be used for zirconia cleaning before MDP-based cementation; air abrasion is the protocol of choice, with Ivoclean as the chemical alternative when abrasion is unavailable.

## 세줄요약
in-vitro XPS + 접착강도 연구(Dent Mater J 2018): 타액 오염 Y-PSZ 고투명 지르코니아에 4가지 세정법(에어어브레이전·Ivoclean·인산·무처리) 적용 후 XPS 원소분석 및 전단결합강도 측정.

에어어브레이전 최고 접착강도·최저 유기 오염; 인산 세정 시 XPS로 확인된 인산 잔류물이 MDP 화학결합을 방해하여 접착강도 저하; Ivoclean은 인산 잔류 없이 효과적.

MDP 기반 시멘트 사용 시 지르코니아 세정에 인산 사용 금지; 에어어브레이전이 1차 프로토콜, 불가 시 Ivoclean이 화학적 대안이다.

## Summary
In vitro XPS analysis + bond strength study on Y-PSZ (high-translucency yttria-partially stabilized zirconia) after saliva contamination and cleaning. Key finding: phosphoric acid cleaning leaves phosphorous residues that chemically interfere with MDP bonding — a mechanism-level warning against using phosphoric acid for Y-PSZ cleaning.

## Key Contributions
- XPS evidence that phosphoric acid leaves P residues on zirconia → blocks MDP chemical adhesion
- Y-PSZ (more yttria = higher translucency, but same surface chemistry) — findings applicable to all translucency grades
- Air abrasion: most thorough mechanical decontamination
- Clinical implication: avoid phosphoric acid for zirconia cleaning; prefer air abrasion or Ivoclean

## Results
| Cleaning method | XPS contamination | Bond strength |
|----------------|------------------|--------------|
| Air abrasion | Lowest organic | Highest |
| Ivoclean | Low, no P residue | High |
| Phosphoric acid | P residue present | Reduced (MDP blocked) |
| Control (no clean) | High organic | Lowest |
