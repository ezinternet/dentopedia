## Three-line Summary
In vitro retention strength study (BMC Oral Health 2022) compared three complete primer-cement systems (Panavia V5 + Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus; Multilink Speed + Monobond N; Duo-Link + Z-Prime Plus) for monolithic zirconia crowns on standardized molar preparations after 10,000 thermocycles.

Panavia V5 + Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus achieved the highest retention force through MDP-to-MDP chemical synergy within a matched same-manufacturer system, outperforming cross-brand primer-cement combinations.

Matching primer and cement from the same manufacturer's system is the key clinical principle, as it maximizes chemical compatibility and retention; cross-brand mixing compromises performance even when both components contain MDP.

## Summary
In vitro retention strength study comparing three complete primer-cement systems for monolithic zirconia crowns on standardized molar preparations after 10,000 thermocycles. Confirms that matched primer-cement systems (from same manufacturer) optimized for chemical compatibility outperform cross-brand combinations.

## Key Contributions
- Panavia V5 + Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus: highest retention (MDP-MDP chemical synergy)
- System matching within manufacturer: improved cement-primer chemical compatibility
- 10,000 thermocycles: all groups tested for aging resistance
- Clinical protocol: use primer and cement from same matched system

## Results
| System | Retention (relative) |
|--------|---------------------|
| Panavia V5 + Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus | Highest |
| Multilink Speed + Monobond N | Intermediate |
| Duo-Link + Z-Prime Plus | Lower |
