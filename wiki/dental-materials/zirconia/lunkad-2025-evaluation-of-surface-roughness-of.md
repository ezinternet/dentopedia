## Three-line Summary

This in vitro study compared the effect of up to 10 flash sterilization cycles on the surface roughness of multilayered monolithic zirconia (MMLZ) specimens polished with three commercial polishing kits: EVE Diacera, SS White, and Ultradent Jiffy. All three systems produced clinically acceptable surface roughness (Ra ≤ 0.0325 μm) after 10 autoclave cycles, with no statistically significant differences within any single polishing system across cycles. The study supports reuse of polishing kits through the manufacturer-recommended maximum of 10 flash sterilization cycles without meaningful loss of polishing efficiency.

## Summary

Repeated chairside grinding of multilayered monolithic zirconia (MMLZ) restorations followed by polishing is routine clinical practice. Concern exists that multiple sterilization cycles degrade polishing instrument efficacy (rubber matrix hardening, thermal degradation). This study directly tested whether EVE Diacera, SS White (both manufacturer-recommended for zirconia), and Ultradent Jiffy (generic/universal polishing kit) maintain clinically acceptable polishing performance through 10 flash sterilization cycles.

One hundred disc-shaped MMLZ specimens (Zolid Gen-X, 10 mm × 3 mm) were milled, assigned to polishing groups and sterilization-cycle subgroups (n=10 each), then surface roughness (Ra) was measured by profilometer and SEM. The glazed control group (no polishing) served as comparator. All three polishing systems produced surfaces with Ra well below the 0.5 μm clinical threshold throughout all sterilization cycles. Polished surfaces were consistently smoother than the glazed control (Ra 0.0325 μm). No statistically significant within-system change across 1, 5, or 10 cycles was detected for any polishing kit after Bonferroni correction.

## Key Contributions

1. **First comparative study on sterilization-cycle effects across three MMLZ polishing kits**, including both manufacturer-recommended and generic systems.
2. **Clinically acceptable Ra maintained through 10 flash autoclave cycles** — maximum Ra 0.0325 μm, all groups far below 0.5 μm threshold.
3. **Polished MMLZ surfaces were smoother than glazed controls** — glazed group Ra 0.0325 μm (highest); polished groups 0.022–0.025 μm.
4. **Generic system (Ultradent Jiffy) equivalent to manufacturer-recommended kits** in polishing performance across all sterilization cycles.
5. **EVE Diacera: statistically significant Ra increase at cycle 5 (P=0.0009), partial recovery at cycle 10** — the only within-system significant change, though still clinically acceptable.

## Methodology

**Design**: In vitro, single-operator (blinded subgroup allocation), comparative  
**Material**: Zolid Gen-X multilayer zirconia (CAD/CAM; ISO 6872 Type II Class 5; shade A2; 3Y-TZP cervical / 5Y-TZP incisal)  
**Polishing systems**:
- EVE Diacera: diamond-impregnated, manufacturer-recommended; 4,000–8,000 RPM × 30 sec
- SS White: manufacturer-recommended; 7,000–10,000 RPM × 30 sec (coarse + fine)
- Ultradent Jiffy: universal generic; 7,000–10,000 RPM × 30 sec

**Sterilization**: Flash autoclave (134°C, 2 bar, 3–5 min; total cycle 15 min)  
**Measurement**:
- Profilometer (Talysurf Intra): Ra (arithmetic mean roughness), 3 measurements per specimen averaged
- Confocal optical microscope (Bruker GT-K 3D): Vertical Scan Interferometry, 5× Michelson lens, 1×1 mm view
- SEM (JSM-6360LV): 1000×, gold-palladium sputter coating

**Statistics**: Shapiro-Wilk normality → Kruskal-Wallis one-way ANOVA → Dunn post-hoc with Bonferroni correction; significance P ≤ 0.05 (corrected P ≤ 0.0011 for post-hoc)  
**Sample size**: n=100 total (n=10 control glazed; n=30 per polishing system; n=10 per cycle subgroup); G-power calculated for 80% power, effect size Δ²=2, α=0.05

## Results

**Surface roughness values after 10 autoclave cycles:**

| Group | Sterilization Cycles | Ra Mdn (IQR) μm |
|---|---|---|
| Control (glazed) | — | 0.0325 (0.00) |
| EVE Diacera (10E) | 10 | 0.0245 (0.009) |
| Ultradent Jiffy (10U) | 10 | 0.023 (0.003) |
| SS White (10W) | 10 | 0.025 (0.002) |

**Statistically significant vs. glazed control** (polished smoother): EVE Diacera at cycles 1 (P=0.0002) and 10 (P=0.0010); Ultradent Jiffy at cycles 5 (P=0.0003) and 10 (P=0.0001); SS White at cycles 1 (P<0.0001) and 5 (P<0.0001).

**Within-system across cycles**: No significant differences within EVE Diacera (1E/5E/10E), Ultradent Jiffy (1U/5U/10U), or SS White (1W/5W/10W) — all P > 0.0011.

**Cross-system comparisons**: Most pairwise comparisons between different polishing systems at same cycle were not statistically significant.

**SEM findings**: Ultradent Jiffy and SS White produced more homogeneous, smoother surfaces. EVE Diacera surface characteristics resembled glazed control (wavy, slight irregularity). No morphological degradation of polishing efficacy noted after 10 cycles.

**Clinical interpretation**: All Ra values remain below 0.5 μm clinical threshold and below the 0.2 μm tongue-perception threshold. Flash sterilization per manufacturer protocol (maximum 10 cycles) does not impair polishing performance.
