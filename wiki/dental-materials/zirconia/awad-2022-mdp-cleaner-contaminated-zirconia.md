## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (Materials 2022) testing an MDP-containing cleaner (Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus) against water and alcohol cleaning on contaminated monolithic zirconia prior to resin cement bonding.

Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus (MDP-based) achieved significantly higher shear bond strength than water or alcohol cleaning in all contamination scenarios, functioning both as a decontaminant and as a chemical bonding agent via P–O–Zr phosphate bonds.

A single MDP-containing cleaner replaces separate cleaning + priming steps, offering a clinically convenient one-step protocol for contaminated zirconia cementation.

## 세줄요약
인비트로(Materials 2022): 오염된 단일체 지르코니아(Monolithic Zirconia) 접착 전 MDP 함유 세정제(Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus)를 수세·알코올 세정과 비교.

Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus(MDP계)는 모든 오염 시나리오에서 수세·알코올보다 유의하게 높은 전단접착강도(SBS) 달성 — P–O–Zr 인산 결합으로 오염물 제거와 화학적 결합을 동시 수행.

MDP 함유 세정제 한 가지로 세정·프라이밍 단계를 대체하는 1단계 임상 프로토콜 가능; 오염 지르코니아 접착에 편의성 높은 선택지.

## Summary
In vitro study demonstrating that MDP-containing cleaner (acting simultaneously as cleaning and priming agent) significantly improves shear bond strength to contaminated monolithic zirconia compared to water or alcohol cleaning alone. MDP's phosphate functional group displaces organic contaminants while forming chemical bond with zirconia.

## Key Contributions
- Dual-function MDP cleaner: removes contaminants + establishes chemical bond in one step
- Superior to water and alcohol cleaning in all contamination scenarios
- Clinical convenience: single agent replaces separate cleaning + priming steps
- Mechanism: phosphate-zirconia chemical bonding (P–O–Zr) remains even after cleaning
