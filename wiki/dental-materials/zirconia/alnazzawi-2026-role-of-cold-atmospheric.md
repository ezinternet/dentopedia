## One-line Summary
In vitro study (n=200, 3Y-TZP + 5YSZ to dentin, 10,000 thermocycles): CAP and APA produce equivalent SBS; zirconia type does not affect bonding; combination treatments (APA+CAP, HF+CAP) reduce SBS; MDP primer is the dominant variable.

## 한줄요약
인비트로 (n=200, 3Y-TZP + 5YSZ 상아질 접착, 10,000회 열순환): 냉온 대기압 플라즈마 (Cold Atmospheric Plasma, CAP)와 공기분사연마 (Airborne Particle Abrasion, APA) 전단결합강도 (Shear Bond Strength, SBS) 동등; 지르코니아 종류(3Y vs 5Y) 영향 없음; APA+CAP, HF+CAP 병용은 오히려 SBS 감소; 10-MDP 프라이머가 결정 변수.

## Summary
This study compared CAP, APA, HF acid, and their combinations for bonding 3Y-TZP and 5YSZ zirconia to dentin using self-adhesive resin cement (Maxcem Elite Chroma). Two hundred specimens were tested with and without MDP primer (Z-prime plus) after 10,000 thermocycles. CAP and APA produced statistically equivalent SBS (~17 MPa without primer, ~26-27 MPa with primer). Zirconia material type had no significant effect. Combinations (APA+CAP, HF+CAP) produced lower SBS than individual treatments, possibly because rough APA topography interferes with CAP surface activation. HF etching alone had no effect on zirconia SBS. The primer variable had the largest effect size (η²=0.86), confirming 10-MDP as the critical bonding factor.

## Key Contributions
- First study comparing CAP vs APA for zirconia-to-dentin bonding (previous work focused on composite adherend)
- Demonstrates 3Y-TZP and 5YSZ produce equivalent SBS — generation/yttria content does not drive bonding protocol choice
- Critical finding: combinations reduce SBS — overtreatment is counterproductive
- Confirms 10-MDP primer as dominant factor (η²=0.86 vs surface treatment η²=0.94) — primer non-negotiable for all surface treatments
- HF ineffective for zirconia bonding even combined with CAP — confirms exclusion from zirconia protocols
- CAP provides non-abrasive, phase-preservation benefit while matching APA clinically

## Methodology
- Design: In vitro, Taibah University (ethics: TUCDREC/210323)
- Zirconia: 100 3Y-TZP (ceraMotionZ HT, Dentaurum) + 100 5YSZ (ceraMotionZ Cubic, Dentaurum)
- Dentin: 100 extracted permanent molars, 3×4×3 mm dentin blocks
- 5 surface treatments × 2 primer conditions × 2 materials = 10 subgroups (n=10 each)
- Surface treatments: Control | APA (50 μm Al₂O₃, 2 bar, 20 s) | CAP (dielectric barrier discharge, 20 kV, 20 kHz) | APA+CAP | HF+CAP (9.5% HF gel, 1 h)
- Primer: Z-prime plus (Bisco, MDP-containing)
- Dentin prep: 37% phosphoric acid + Ambar universal adhesive
- Cement: Maxcem Elite Chroma (self-adhesive resin cement, Kerr)
- Aging: 10,000 thermocycles, 5-55°C (30 s dwell, 10 s transfer)
- SBS: Instron, 0.5 mm/min, 12 mm² bonding area; failure mode optical microscopy ×30

## Results
SBS (MPa), 3Y-TZP / 5YSZ:
| Treatment | No primer (3Y/5Y) | With primer (3Y/5Y) |
|---|---|---|
| Control | 4.83 / 4.49 | 7.56 / 7.21 |
| APA | 17.01 / 16.75 | 25.94 / 26.27 |
| CAP | 17.43 / 17.52 | 26.99 / 26.89 |
| APA+CAP | 15.42 / 16.47 | 24.40 / 24.97 |
| HF+CAP | 15.14 / 14.94 | 20.96 / 22.26 |

Multiple-way ANOVA partial η²:
- Surface treatment: 0.94 (p<0.0001)
- Primer: 0.86 (p<0.0001)
- Zirconia type: 0.00 (p=0.354, NOT significant)

CAP vs APA: statistically comparable within all groups (p>0.05 pairwise)
