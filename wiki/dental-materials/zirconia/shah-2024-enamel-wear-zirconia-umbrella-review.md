## Three-line Summary
Umbrella review (J Indian Prosthodont Soc 2024) synthesized 4 systematic reviews (2013–2023) evaluating antagonist enamel wear opposing full-coverage zirconia crowns versus other ceramics and natural enamel.

Polished monolithic zirconia produced antagonist enamel wear ≤ natural enamel and was less damaging than glazed zirconia, metal-ceramics, feldspathic porcelain, and lithium disilicate; meta-analysis was not possible due to study heterogeneity.

The clinical guideline from the highest level of evidence available is to polish monolithic zirconia rather than glaze it when enamel preservation of opposing teeth is the priority.

## Summary
Umbrella review (4 systematic reviews, 2013–2023) providing the highest level of synthesis on antagonist enamel wear opposing zirconia crowns. Concludes polished monolithic zirconia is the most enamel-friendly of tested ceramic materials.

## Key Contributions
- Polished monolithic Zr ≤ natural enamel wear over time
- Polished < glazed for antagonist enamel wear
- Polished Zr < metal ceramics, feldspathic porcelains, lithium disilicate
- Meta-analysis not possible due to heterogeneous study designs and outcome variables
- Clinical guideline: polish monolithic zirconia; do not routinely glaze if enamel preservation is priority
