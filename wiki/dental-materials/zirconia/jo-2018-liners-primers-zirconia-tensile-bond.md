## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (J Adv Prosthodont 2018) comparing phosphate-containing and phosphate-free silica glass-ceramic liners versus dedicated MDP primers (Monobond Plus, Z-Prime Plus) for tensile bond strength between zirconia and a resin luting agent.

Phosphate-containing liner achieved tensile bond strength equivalent to MDP primers through dual bonding (silica micromechanical interlocking + phosphate–zirconia chemical bond); the phosphate-free liner performed significantly lower, confirming phosphate groups are critical.

All pretreated groups significantly outperformed untreated control, and the liner system offers a laboratory-integrated alternative to chairside MDP priming when a glass-ceramic liner has already been applied.

## 세줄요약
in-vitro(J Adv Prosthodont 2018): 인산기 포함/미포함 실리카 글라스세라믹 라이너 vs MDP 프라이머(Monobond Plus, Z-Prime Plus) 인장결합강도 비교.

인산기 함유 라이너 = MDP 프라이머 수준의 인장결합강도(이중 결합: 실리카 미세기계적 맞물림 + 인산기-지르코니아 화학 결합); 인산기 미포함 라이너는 유의하게 낮아 인산기 필수 확인.

전처리 전군이 무처리보다 유의하게 우수 — 라이너가 이미 적용된 경우 별도 MDP 프라이머 없이 동등한 접착 기대 가능.

## Summary
In vitro study comparing silica-based glass-ceramic liners (P-containing and P-free) with MDP-containing primers (Monobond Plus, Z-Prime Plus) for tensile bond strength between zirconia abutments and crowns. P-containing liner achieves bond strength equivalent to MDP primers, suggesting dual (micromechanical + chemical) bonding mechanism via phosphate and silicate groups.

## Key Contributions
- Glass-ceramic liner (phosphate-containing) achieves TBS comparable to dedicated MDP primers
- Dual bonding mechanism: silica interlocking + phosphate-Zr chemical bonding
- P-free liner: lower than P-containing, confirming phosphate groups critical for chemical adhesion
- Clinical alternative to MDP primers when liner already applied in laboratory
