## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 4종 단일구조 지르코니아(3Y-TZP: Lava Plus·Cercon hT; 5Y-PSZ: Prettau 4 Anterior·Cercon xT)를 유약 처리 vs 교합조정+연마(GAP) 조건으로 비교
> - 투명도 (Translucency Parameter, TP): 연마 후 모든 군에서 유약 처리와 통계적으로 유의한 차이 없이 유지(임상적으로 허용 가능)
> - 접촉각: GAP 조건에서 모든 군 유의하게 증가(소수성↑) — 유약 처리가 소수성 더 낮아 생물막 위험 더 적음
> - 굴곡강도 (Flexural Strength): 소재에 따라 결과 상이 — Cercon hT 576→856 MPa(+60%), Lava Plus 578→229 MPa(감소), PA·xT 보존
> - Cercon hT 강도 증가 원인: 3Y-TZP의 마르텐사이트 변태(Martensitic Transformation)로 압축응력 형성 → 결함 전파 억제
> - Lava Plus 강도 감소 원인: 연마 중 도입된 표면 결함이 압축응력 구역보다 커 강도 저하
> - 임상 takeaway: 4종 중 3종(PA·hT·xT)은 구내 연마로 재유약 처리 대체 가능 → 같은 날 합착 가능, 기공소 재내원 불필요
> - 단, Lava Plus는 GAP 후 굴곡강도 유의 감소 → 재유약 고려 필요
> - 연마 후 접촉각 증가(소수성↑)는 균일하므로 모든 소재에서 생물막 위험 소폭 상승 주의
> - 한계: 기하학적 디스크 시편, 열순환·기계적 노화 미포함, 단일 유약 재료 사용

## Three-line Summary (EN)

This in-vitro study compared glazed versus polished-after-occlusal-adjustment (GAP) surface conditions across four monolithic zirconia types (two 3Y-TZP: Lava Plus, Cercon hT; two 5Y-PSZ: Prettau 4 Anterior, Cercon xT) for translucency parameter (TP), contact angle, and biaxial flexural strength (n = 10/group, piston-on-three-balls). Polishing maintained TP for all materials (no statistically significant difference from glazed), increased contact angle (reduced wettability) universally, and produced material-specific flexural outcomes: Cercon hT increased ~60% (576→856 MPa), Lava Plus decreased (578→229 MPa), while Prettau 4 Anterior and Cercon xT preserved strength. Intraoral polishing after occlusal adjustment is clinically viable for three of four materials tested, eliminating laboratory reglazing in most cases.

## 세줄요약 (KO)

4종 단일구조 지르코니아(3Y-TZP 2종·5Y-PSZ 2종)를 유약 처리 상태와 교합조정 후 연마 (Glazed + Adjustment + Polishing, GAP) 상태로 비교하여 투명도 (Translucency Parameter, TP), 접촉각, 굴곡강도 (Flexural Strength)를 평가한 체외 실험이다. 연마 후 TP는 모든 군에서 유약 처리와 통계적으로 유의한 차이 없이 유지되었고, 접촉각은 모든 군에서 증가(소수성↑)하였으며, 굴곡강도는 Cercon hT에서 약 60% 상승(576→856 MPa), Lava Plus에서 감소(578→229 MPa), Prettau 4 Anterior와 Cercon xT에서 보존되었다. 임상적으로 대부분의 단일구조 지르코니아 수복물은 재유약 처리 없이 구내 연마로 충분하며, 같은 날 합착이 가능하다.

## Summary

This study directly addresses the clinical question of whether to reglaze or intraorally polish after occlusal adjustment of monolithic zirconia restorations. Four zirconia types — Prettau 4 Anterior (PA, 5Y-PSZ), Lava Plus (LP, 3Y-TZP), Cercon hT (hT, 3Y-TZP), and Cercon xT (xT, 5Y-PSZ) — were fabricated as discs (12 × 1 mm, n = 10/group) and glazed uniformly. Half underwent simulated occlusal adjustment (medium then fine diamond burs) followed by intraoral rubber polishing kit (Zir Gloss/Jota). Three outcomes were measured: translucency parameter (spectrophotometry, CIEDE2000), contact angle (goniometry, sessile drop), and biaxial flexural strength (piston-on-three-balls, ISO 6872). Statistical analysis used two-way ANOVA with Tukey post-hoc.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates polishing maintains translucency across all four zirconia types (TP statistically equivalent to glazed state)
- Quantifies the universal contact angle increase with polishing (all materials: glazed ~13–22° → polished ~31–44°)
- Shows material-specific flexural strength response to polishing: cannot generalize across zirconia types
- Provides practical clinical evidence supporting same-appointment polishing instead of laboratory reglazing for PA, hT, and xT
- Highlights mechanical risk of polishing for specific 3Y-TZP formulations (Lava Plus: 60% strength reduction)

## Methodology

**Study design**: Factorial in-vitro experiment (2 surface conditions × 4 materials = 8 groups, n = 10/group, N = 80).

**Materials**:
| Code | Product | Manufacturer | Type | Y₂O₃ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LP | Lava Plus | 3M ESPE | 3Y-TZP | ≤6% |
| hT | Cercon hT | Dentsply Sirona | 3Y-TZP | 5% |
| PA | Prettau 4 Anterior | Zirkonzahn | 5Y-PSZ | <12% |
| xT | Cercon xT | Dentsply Sirona | 5Y-PSZ | 9% |

**Surface conditions**:
- Glazed (G): Prettau Plus glaze, 80 ± 20 μm, fired at 750°C
- Glazed + Adjustment + Polishing (GAP): simulated occlusal adjustment with medium (90–160 μm) and fine (38–45 μm) diamond burs, then Zir Gloss rubber polishing kit; glaze reduced 10–20 μm

**Outcome measures**:
- Translucency parameter (TP): CM 3700d spectrophotometer, CIEDE2000, black/white background method
- Contact angle: DSA25 goniometer, sessile drop, 1 μL distilled water, Young–Laplace
- Flexural strength: piston-on-three-balls (ISO 6872), artificial saliva at 37°C, 0.5 mm/min

## Results

### Translucency Parameter

| Material | Glazed | Polished | Sig. |
|---|---|---|---|
| PA (5Y-PSZ) | 4.2 | 3.9 | ns |
| LP (3Y-TZP) | 3.8 | 5.0 | ns |
| hT (3Y-TZP) | 6.4 | 6.3 | ns |
| xT (5Y-PSZ) | 6.9 | 6.6 | ns |

- xT and hT showed highest TP (cubic phase dominance, non-birefringent)
- Surface condition did not significantly affect TP (main effect p = 0.723); interaction significant (p = 0.042) but no pair differed within material
- Polishing effectively preserved translucency for all materials

### Contact Angle

| Material | Glazed (°) | Polished (°) |
|---|---|---|
| PA | 21.8 | 31.1 |
| LP | 22.0 | 37.6 |
| hT | 13.4 | 39.6 |
| xT | 19.7 | 43.7 |

- Surface condition main effect p < 0.001; polishing universally increased contact angle (reduced wettability)
- Glazed surfaces have lower contact angles → more hydrophilic → potentially less biofilm risk

### Flexural Strength

| Material | Glazed (MPa) | Polished (MPa) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| PA (5Y-PSZ) | 410.4 | 333.1 | Preserved (ns) |
| LP (3Y-TZP) | 577.5 | 228.8 | Decreased ↓ |
| hT (3Y-TZP) | 576.5 | 856.0 | Increased ↑ (+60%) |
| xT (5Y-PSZ) | 384.5 | 287.1 | Preserved (ns) |

- Interaction material × surface condition p < 0.001
- hT increase attributed to martensitic transformation toughening (tetragonal → monoclinic phase transformation under mechanical stress)
- LP decrease attributed to surface defects exceeding the compression zone created by toughening
- 5Y-PSZ materials (PA, xT) have reduced tetragonal phase → weaker transformation toughening → strength preserved but not boosted
