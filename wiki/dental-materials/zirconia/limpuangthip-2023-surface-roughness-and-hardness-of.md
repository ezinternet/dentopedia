## Three-line Summary

A multipurpose diamond polishing kit (EVE Diacera) reduced surface roughness (Ra) of three CAD/CAM ceramics — VITA YZ zirconia, Celtra Duo ZLS, and IPS e.max CAD — to levels comparable with enamel (mean Ra 0.573 µm) and lab as-received specimens after 60 s coarse + 60 s fine polishing. VITA YZ showed the greatest total Ra reduction (ΔRa 1.243 µm) due to its homogenous fine-grained crystalline structure, while IPS e.max CAD showed the least (ΔRa 0.793 µm); Vickers hardness was unchanged across all materials post-polishing (VITA YZ ~1,236 HV, Celtra Duo ~641 HV, IPS e.max CAD ~558 HV). Coarse polishing bur weight loss correlated with material hardness, while fine polishing bur showed no significant wear regardless of material.

## 세줄요약

범용 다이아몬드 연마 키트(EVE Diacera)로 세 가지 CAD/CAM 세라믹을 60초 거친 연마 + 60초 미세 연마한 결과, 모든 소재의 표면조도 (Surface Roughness, Ra)가 법랑질 수준(평균 0.573 µm) 및 실험실 완성품과 유사해졌다. 균일한 미세 결정 구조를 가진 VITA YZ 지르코니아가 가장 큰 Ra 감소(ΔRa 1.243 µm)를 보였고, IPS e.max CAD가 가장 작은 감소(ΔRa 0.793 µm)를 나타냈으며, 연마 후 모든 소재의 비커스 경도 (Vickers Hardness, HV)는 변하지 않았다. 거친 연마 버의 중량 감소는 소재 경도 순서(VITA YZ > Celtra Duo > IPS e.max CAD)와 일치했으나, 미세 연마 버는 세 소재 모두 유의한 중량 감소가 없었다.

## Summary

This in vitro study evaluated the effect of a multipurpose ceramic polishing kit (EVE Diacera HP) on surface roughness (Ra), Vickers hardness, and polishing bur weight across three CAD/CAM ceramic types: translucent zirconia (VITA YZ XT), zirconia-reinforced lithium disilicate/ZLS (Celtra Duo), and lithium disilicate glass ceramic (IPS e.max CAD).

Specimens were ground with a fine diamond bur, then sequentially polished with coarse (25–35 µm diamond, 60 s) and fine (3–6 µm diamond, 60 s) polishing burs under standardized 1 N pressure and 10,000 rpm, using a custom pressure-control device. Ra was measured every 15 s via non-contact optical profilometry; hardness was assessed by Vickers indentation before and after final polishing.

All three materials reached Ra values not significantly different from enamel (0.573 µm) or lab as-received specimens after complete polishing, confirming that a single multipurpose kit suffices for all three CAD/CAM ceramic classes. The polishing efficacy was driven by the relationship between polishing bur grain size and ceramic crystalline structure rather than material hardness.

## Key Contributions

1. **Multipurpose kit sufficiency**: EVE Diacera achieves equivalent final Ra across all three CAD/CAM ceramic classes — eliminating the need for material-specific polishing kits.
2. **Polishing duration plateau**: 60 s coarse + 60 s fine polishing is sufficient; VITA YZ plateaus even earlier (after 30 s fine polishing).
3. **Grain-size mechanism**: Polishing efficacy correlates with the ratio of bur grain size to ceramic crystalline structure size, not with material hardness.
4. **Hardness preservation**: Vickers hardness of all materials was unchanged after grinding and polishing — no surface damage in terms of hardness.
5. **Bur wear pattern**: Coarse bur abrasion correlates with material hardness; fine bur shows negligible wear across all materials.

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | In vitro, repeated-measures ANOVA |
| Materials | IPS e.max CAD (LT A3), VITA YZ (XT A3), Celtra Duo (LT A3) |
| Specimen geometry | 7 × 5 × 4 mm rectangular blocks |
| Grinding | Fine diamond bur (27–76 µm), 1 N, 200,000 rpm, 15 s |
| Coarse polishing | EVE H2DCmf (25–35 µm), 1 N, 10,000 rpm, 60 s |
| Fine polishing | EVE H2DC (3–6 µm), 1 N, 10,000 rpm, 60 s |
| Ra measurement | Alicona InfiniteFocusSL profilometer, 50×, 5 areas/specimen, every 15 s |
| Hardness | Vickers FM-810, 100 g/15 s, n = 4/group |
| Bur weight | Radwag analytical balance, n = 6 burs/group |
| SEM | JSM-6400 at 300×, 1,000×, 5,000×, 10,000× |
| Comparators | Lab as-received specimens, human enamel (n = 30 teeth) |
| Statistics | Two-way repeated-measures ANOVA, one-way ANOVA, Tukey post hoc, α = 0.05 |

## Results

### Surface Roughness (Ra, µm)

| Material | Lab as-received | Post-grind | Post-coarse 60 s | Post-fine 60 s | Total ΔRa |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IPS e.max CAD | 0.698 | 1.440 | 0.864 | 0.647 | 0.793 |
| VITA YZ | 0.668 | 1.838 | 0.803 | 0.595 | 1.243 |
| Celtra Duo | 0.637 | 1.663 | 0.768 | 0.593 | 1.070 |
| Human enamel | 0.573 | — | — | — | — |

- Final Ra values of all three materials were not significantly different from each other or from enamel.
- Coarse polishing accounted for the majority of Ra reduction.
- VITA YZ: greatest Ra reduction, largest post-grind Ra (1.838 µm) due to homogenous fine-grained structure responsive to coarse bur grain size.
- IPS e.max CAD: smallest Ra reduction due to larger, heterogeneous needle-like crystal structure relative to bur grain size.

### Vickers Hardness (HV)

| Material | Pre-polish | Post-polish | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| IPS e.max CAD | 557.9 ± 30.7 | 554.7 ± 4.2 | NS |
| VITA YZ | 1235.9 ± 20.3 | 1191.6 ± 20.1 | NS |
| Celtra Duo | 640.7 ± 35.6 | 632.4 ± 5.1 | NS |

Hardness order: VITA YZ > Celtra Duo > IPS e.max CAD (significant between-group differences; no within-material change).

### Polishing Bur Weight Loss

- Coarse bur: significant weight loss after VITA YZ > Celtra Duo > IPS e.max CAD polishing
- Fine bur: no significant weight loss in any material
