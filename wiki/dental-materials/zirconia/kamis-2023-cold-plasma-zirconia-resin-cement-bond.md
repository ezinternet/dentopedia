## Three-line Summary

Cold atmospheric pressure plasma (CAPP) alone does not significantly improve shear bond strength (SBS) of resin cement to zirconia, but CAPP combined with 10-MDP primer (PPR) yields SBS significantly greater than untreated controls. Sandblasting + primer (SPR) and laser + plasma + primer (LPPR) similarly outperformed untreated zirconia. CAPP increases surface wettability via contact angle reduction, which facilitates MDP primer penetration — the chemical bond, not roughness alone, drives improved SBS.

## 세줄요약

냉각 대기압 플라즈마 (Cold Atmospheric Pressure Plasma, CAPP) 단독은 지르코니아-레진시멘트 전단결합강도 (Shear Bond Strength, SBS)를 유의하게 향상시키지 않으나, CAPP + 10-메타크릴로일옥시데실 디하이드로겐 포스페이트 (10-Methacryloyloxydecyl Dihydrogen Phosphate, 10-MDP) 프라이머 조합(PPR)은 대조군 대비 유의하게 높은 SBS를 나타냈다. 샌드블라스팅+프라이머(SPR)와 레이저+플라즈마+프라이머(LPPR) 군도 대조군을 유의하게 상회했다. CAPP는 접촉각 (Contact Angle)을 낮춰 표면 습윤성을 개선하고, MDP 프라이머의 화학적 결합을 촉진하는 기전으로 작용한다.

## Summary

This in vitro study compared nine surface treatment protocols for CAD-CAM zirconia (Vita YZ-HT, 3Y-TZP) prior to cementation with two resin cements: RelyX Ultimate Clicker (adhesive) and TheraCem (self-adhesive). Cold atmospheric pressure plasma (CAPP) was evaluated alongside sandblasting, laser, and 10-MDP primer combinations.

Key finding: CAPP alone improves wettability but does not significantly improve SBS. However, when CAPP precedes MDP primer application (PPR group), SBS is significantly greater than untreated controls — indicating that plasma's benefit operates through enhanced primer penetration rather than direct surface roughening. Sandblasting + primer (SPR) and laser + plasma + primer (LPPR) also achieved significant SBS improvements, but adding laser to the PPR protocol (LPPR) provided no meaningful additive benefit.

The study clarifies that increased surface roughness (Ra) and improved wettability are distinct mechanisms: plasma primarily affects wettability (contact angle), while sandblasting primarily affects Ra. Only the combination of a wettability-enhancing treatment with chemical primer bonding yields clinically relevant SBS gains.

## Key Contributions

- **Mechanism clarification:** CAPP's benefit is wettability-mediated, not roughness-mediated — plasma + primer (PPR) works even without significant Ra increase.
- **Clinical hierarchy:** SPR ≥ PPR > LPPR ≈ LPR > U for SBS; laser addition to PPR offers no additive gain.
- **Self-adhesive cement data:** TheraCem response patterns differ from adhesive RelyX cement across surface treatment groups.
- **Primer necessity confirmed:** Neither CAPP nor laser alone provides significant SBS improvement without MDP primer.

## Methodology

- **n = 189** Vita YZ-HT zirconia discs (sintered, polished), 21 per group × 9 groups
- **Resin cements:** RelyX Ultimate Clicker (adhesive) and TheraCem (self-adhesive)
- **Nine groups:**
  1. Untreated (U)
  2. Sandblasting only (S)
  3. Laser only (L)
  4. CAPP only (P)
  5. 10-MDP Primer only (PR)
  6. Sandblasting + Primer (SPR)
  7. Laser + Primer (LPR)
  8. Plasma + Primer (PPR)
  9. Laser + Plasma + Primer (LPPR)
- **Measurements:** 표면조도 (Surface Roughness, Ra), 접촉각 (Contact Angle), 전단결합강도 (Shear Bond Strength, SBS)

## Results

| Group | Ra vs U | Contact Angle vs U | SBS vs U |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | Significantly ↑ | Significantly ↓ | Not significant |
| L | Not significant | Significantly ↓ | Not significant |
| P (CAPP) | Not significant | Significantly ↓ | Not significant |
| PR | Significantly ↑ | Not tested | Not significant |
| SPR | Significantly ↑ | — | **Significantly ↑** |
| LPR | — | — | Not significant |
| PPR | Significantly ↑ | — | **Significantly ↑** |
| LPPR | Significantly ↑ | — | **Significantly ↑** |

**Interpretation:** Groups showing significant SBS improvement (SPR, PPR, LPPR) all combined surface modification with 10-MDP primer. CAPP alone (P) improved wettability but not SBS, confirming that wettability improvement is a prerequisite for, not a substitute for, chemical primer bonding.
