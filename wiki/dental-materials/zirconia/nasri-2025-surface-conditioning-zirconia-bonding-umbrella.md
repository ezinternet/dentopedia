## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD420251028077) of 8 systematic reviews (5 qualitative, 3 with meta-analysis) covering 233 primary studies (1998–2025; the paper's own abstract states 389 — an internal inconsistency, reported as found) on surface conditioning methods for resin bonding to zirconia restorations.

Airborne-particle abrasion (APA) combined with 10-MDP primers was identified as the most validated and clinically reliable protocol; inter-review overlap of primary studies was weak (corrected covered area, CCA = 0.038); 6/8 included SRs had low overall risk of bias by ROBIS.

Emerging techniques — nanostructured alumina coating (NAC), selective infiltration etching (SIE), silica nanofilm/plasma deposition, and femtosecond laser conditioning — show promising but not yet clinically mature bond-strength and durability data; substantial heterogeneity in aging protocols and bond-strength test methods across primary studies limits pooled interpretation.

## 세줄요약

우산 리뷰(PROSPERO CRD420251028077)로 지르코니아 레진 접착을 위한 표면처리(surface conditioning) 방법을 다룬 체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review, SR) 8편(정성적 5편, 메타분석 포함 3편)을 종합 — 원저 연구 233편(1998~2025년, 초록에는 389편으로 기재되어 본문과 불일치, 원문 그대로 기록).

에어본 입자 연마(Airborne-Particle Abrasion, APA) + 10-메타크릴로일옥시데실 이수소인산염(10-MDP) 프라이머 병용이 가장 검증되고 임상적으로 신뢰할 수 있는 프로토콜로 확인; SR 간 원저 중복도는 약함(교정중복면적, Corrected Covered Area, CCA = 0.038); 포함 SR 8편 중 6편이 ROBIS 기준 전체 편향위험 낮음.

나노구조 알루미나 코팅(Nanostructured Alumina Coating, NAC), 선택적 침투 에칭(Selective Infiltration Etching, SIE), 실리카 나노필름/플라즈마 증착, 펨토초 레이저 처리는 유망하나 아직 임상 적용 성숙도가 낮음; 노화 프로토콜과 결합강도 측정법의 이질성이 커서 풀링 해석에 주의가 필요.

## Summary

This umbrella review synthesized 8 systematic reviews (published 2014–2025, covering primary studies from 1998–2025) evaluating surface conditioning methods to improve resin bonding to zirconia restorations, following CRD/PRISMA methodology and pre-registered on PROSPERO (CRD420251028077). Airborne-particle abrasion (APA) combined with 10-MDP primer emerged as the most extensively validated and clinically reliable protocol, while newer approaches — nanostructured alumina coating (NAC), selective infiltration etching (SIE), silica nanofilm/plasma deposition, and femtosecond laser conditioning — showed promising but less mature evidence. Inter-review overlap of primary studies was weak (CCA = 0.038), and 6 of 8 included SRs had low overall risk of bias by ROBIS. Clinically, the review reinforces APA+MDP as the current standard of care for zirconia bonding while flagging heterogeneous aging/testing protocols across the underlying primary literature as a barrier to firmer pooled conclusions.

## Key Contributions
- Umbrella-level (SR-of-SRs) confirmation that APA + 10-MDP primer is the best-validated zirconia bonding protocol, consolidating findings previously scattered across single in-vitro studies and individual SRs.
- CCA overlap analysis (0.038) showing the 8 included SRs draw on a largely non-redundant primary-study base, strengthening confidence that the synthesis is not double-counting the same evidence.
- ROBIS-graded quality map of the 8 SRs (6 low, 1 high, 1 moderate risk of bias), letting readers weight each SR's contribution.
- Evidence-maturity ranking of emerging alternatives to APA: NAC and SIE described as promising with growing support; femtosecond laser and plasma/silica-nanofilm deposition as high-potential but early-stage; hot chemical etching flagged as effective but clinically impractical/hazardous.

## Methodology
Umbrella review per Centre for Reviews and Dissemination methods + PRISMA, searching MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Database of SRs, and Scopus (search window 21 Nov 2024–20 June 2025) plus PROSPERO and manual search, no publication-year restriction (included SRs range 2014–2025). PICO: zirconia crowns/bridges (P), surface treatments (I) vs no treatment (C), bond strength/durability (O). Of 138 records identified (134 database + 4 other), 8 SRs were ultimately included after PRISMA screening. Overlap was quantified via corrected covered area (CCA); methodological quality via ROBIS; findings summarized narratively and in a GRADE-style summary-of-findings table, since pooling across the heterogeneous SRs was not feasible.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| SRs included | 8 (5 qualitative-only, 3 with meta-analysis per body text — Table 1 as extracted shows 4/8 "Yes" for meta-analysis, an internal inconsistency) |
| Primary studies covered | 233 (body text; abstract states 389 — reported as found, not reconciled) |
| CCA (inter-review overlap) | 0.038 — weak/slight overlap |
| ROBIS overall risk of bias | Low: 6/8 SRs; High: 1/8 (Habibzadeh et al. 2024); Moderate: 1/8 (Papia et al. 2014) |
| Best-validated protocol | Airborne-particle abrasion (APA) + 10-MDP primer |
| APA parameters (synthesized) | 25–250 µm particle size (studies here: 25–150 µm), 0.05–0.45 MPa pressure, 10–20 s duration, 5–20 mm standoff |
| APA risk | Surface micro-cracks; tetragonal→monoclinic (t/m) phase transformation |
| NAC vs APA (cited RCT) | Statistically equivalent clinical retention (see malgaj-2026) despite NAC being non-damaging |
| Synthetic-diamond APA bond strength (Papia et al.) | 22.7–31.0 MPa |
| Gas plasma | Only treatment with bond strength ≈ untreated control (Li et al.'s analysis); may improve bioactivity/cytocompatibility instead |
| Femtosecond laser | Superior bond strength, minimal collateral damage, but costly / not widely clinically available |
