## Three-line Summary

In-vitro 4-group study (40 HT 3Y-PSZ discs: G=glaze, G-A=glaze+age, DG-A=deglaze+age, NG-A=no-glaze+age; combined aging: hydrothermal → pH cycling → load cycling): deglazed (1338.22 MPa) and non-glazed (1320.44 MPa) groups showed significantly higher flexural strength than glazed groups (P<0.005).

Glazing did NOT protect against aging (G=1092.78 vs G-A=1141.11 MPa, P=0.872) and did NOT enhance strength vs deglazed/non-glazed; glaze blocks surface LTD (G-A: 0% monoclinic) but removing the glaze does not weaken the ceramic.

Clinical implication: intraoral wear of the glaze layer (deglazing) is not a strength concern for HT 3Y-PSZ; the exposed polished surface is actually mechanically superior to the glazed surface.

## 세줄요약

HT 3Y-PSZ 40개, 4군(G/G-A/DG-A/NG-A), 복합 에이징(수열+pH+부하): 탈글레이징(1338.22 MPa)·비글레이징(1320.44 MPa)이 글레이징군(1092.78 MPa)보다 굴곡강도 유의하게 높음(P<0.005).

글레이징: 에이징 후 강도 변화 없음(P=0.872); LTD는 차단(G-A 단사정 ~0%)하나 기계적 강도 향상 없음.

임상 의의: 구강 내 글레이즈 마모(탈글레이징)는 HT 3Y-PSZ의 강도 우려 없음; 노출된 연마면이 오히려 우수.

## Summary

Aflatoonian et al. (2025) addressed the clinical scenario of intraoral glaze wear in monolithic zirconia restorations. The key finding was that losing the glaze layer (deglazing, as occurs clinically from occlusal wear) does NOT weaken high-translucency 3Y-PSZ. In fact, the deglazed and non-glazed groups showed significantly higher flexural strength. The glaze layer blocks surface LTD (maintaining near-zero monoclinic content in glazed groups) but provides no mechanical benefit — and in fact reduces flexural strength relative to polished surfaces.

## Key Contributions

1. **Deglazed zirconia is NOT weakened** — 1338 MPa vs glazed 1093 MPa; clinically, glaze wear is not a strength risk
2. **Glazing reduces FS** relative to polished/non-glazed: consistent with Sarabi (2024) and Kosmac (1999)
3. **Glaze blocks surface LTD** (monoclinic ~0% in G-A vs 4–8% in DG-A/NG-A) but this benefit is offset by reduced FS
4. Combined aging protocol (HT + pH + load) more clinically representative than single-stressor protocols

## Methodology

- 40 discs (12mm × 1.2mm), HT 3Y-PSZ (Upcera HT), all initially sintered and glazed
- 4 groups: G, G-A, DG-A (glaze removed), NG-A (glaze layer milled off before aging)
- Aging: hydrothermal (134°C) → pH cycling → load cycling
- Biaxial FS (ISO 6872); XRD t→m quantification; 1-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD (α=0.05)

## Results

| Group | FS (MPa) | Monoclinic |
|---|---|---|
| G (glaze) | 1092.78 ± 175.40 | ~0% |
| G-A (glaze + aged) | 1141.11 ± 117.43 | ~0% |
| DG-A (deglaze + aged) | 1338.22 ± 175.13 | 4.23% |
| NG-A (no glaze + aged) | 1320.44 ± 77.44 | 7.63% |

- DG-A/NG-A >> G/G-A in FS (P<0.005)
- G vs G-A: P=0.872 (non-significant) — aging does not reduce glazed FS
