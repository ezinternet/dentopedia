## Three-line Summary

In-vitro comparative study (3Y-TZP/2 types, ATZ alumina-toughened zirconia, 12Ce-TZP ceria-stabilized; autoclave aging at increasing intervals up to 500h/134°C): 3Y-TZP showed catastrophic LTD — strength dropped from 1740 to 1169 MPa (↓33%); ATZ unexpectedly showed strength INCREASE (1093→1378 MPa, ↑26%).

12Ce-TZP was completely immune to LTD — maintained ~500 MPa throughout all aging intervals with no monoclinic transformation.

Establishes the ATZ paradox (aging-induced transformation toughening from residual tetragonal ZrO₂ phase) and the Ce-TZP stability ceiling, providing the material-class framework that later studies on 4Y/5Y-PSZ validated with yttria-cubic zirconia.

## Summary

Kohorst et al. (2012) provided the first systematic comparison of LTD resistance across four fundamentally different zirconia ceramic classes. The study revealed three distinct LTD behavior patterns: susceptible (3Y-TZP, strength loss), paradoxical (ATZ, strength gain from transformation toughening), and immune (12Ce-TZP, no phase transformation). These patterns established the material-class framework that subsequent studies on cubic-phase yttria zirconia (4Y-PSZ, 5Y-PSZ) confirmed and extended. The 3Y-TZP data (1740→1169 MPa, ↓33%) remains the most-cited baseline for quantifying LTD severity.

## Key Contributions

1. **3Y-TZP ↓33% FS after 500h**: 1740→1169 MPa — landmark severity baseline for LTD
2. **ATZ paradox**: ↑26% FS (1093→1378 MPa) — residual t-ZrO₂ in alumina matrix undergoes protective transformation toughening
3. **Ce-TZP immunity**: 12Ce-TZP (ceria-stabilized) completely LTD-resistant but limited to ~500 MPa (too low for posterior restorations)
4. **Material-class framework**: three LTD behavior archetypes defined for the first time

## Methodology

- 4 materials: 3Y-TZP (2 types), ATZ (alumina + ZrO₂ composite), 12Ce-TZP (12 mol% ceria)
- Autoclave aging: 134°C/2bar, multiple intervals up to 500h
- XRD: monoclinic fraction; biaxial flexural strength (ISO 6872); Weibull statistics

## Results

| Material | Baseline FS | Post-500h FS | Change | LTD | Monoclinic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3Y-TZP | 1740 MPa | 1169 MPa | ↓33% | Severe | High |
| ATZ | 1093 MPa | 1378 MPa | ↑26% | Paradoxical | Moderate |
| 12Ce-TZP | ~500 MPa | ~500 MPa | 0% | Immune | None |

- 3Y-TZP: strength loss proportional to aging time
- ATZ: initial decrease, then recovery via transformation toughening
- 12Ce-TZP: stable across all intervals
