## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (Materials 2022) comparing five different cleaning methods applied to contaminated zirconia, measuring shear bond strength (SBS) to resin cement after each cleaning protocol.

All five cleaning methods produced significantly higher SBS than the contaminated-uncleaned control group, confirming that any structured cleaning approach restores bond strength after contamination.

Cleaning before cementation of contaminated zirconia is universally essential; this study contributes general confirmation to the evidence base but does not rank specific cleaning methods against each other.

## 세줄요약
인비트로(Materials 2022): 오염된 지르코니아(Zirconia)에 5가지 세정법을 적용한 후 레진 시멘트 전단접착강도(Shear Bond Strength, SBS)를 비교.

5가지 세정법 모두 오염 무처리 대조군보다 유의하게 높은 SBS 달성 → 어떤 구조화된 세정 방식이든 오염 후 접착강도를 회복시킴.

오염 지르코니아(Zirconia) 접착 전 세정은 보편적으로 필수; 이 연구는 세정 필요성에 대한 일반적 확인을 제공하지만 특정 세정법 간 순위는 제시하지 않음.

## Summary
In vitro investigation of five different cleaning methods on contaminated zirconia shear bond strength to resin cement. Confirms the universal principle that any cleaning method improves bond strength compared to contaminated-uncleaned control; contributes to growing evidence base for zirconia cleaning protocols.
