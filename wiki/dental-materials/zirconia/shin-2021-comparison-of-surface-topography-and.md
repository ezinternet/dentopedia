## Three-line Summary

A laboratory study compared surface roughness (Ra), phase transformation, and topography of 3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, and 5Y-TZP dental zirconia (IPS e.max ZirCAD LT/MT/MT multi) after standardized grinding and 3-step clinical polishing. No significant differences in Ra were found between yttrium oxide compositions; fine polishing (P3) yielded lower Ra than layering-technique glazing in all groups. XRD confirmed no phase transformation under study conditions; FE-SEM showed grain size increases with yttria content.

## 세줄요약

3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, 5Y-TZP 지르코니아(IPS e.max ZirCAD LT/MT/MT multi)를 동일 조건(2 N, 15,000 rpm)으로 연삭 후 3단계 연마 버로 순차 연마하여 표면 거칠기(Ra)를 비교한 실험실 연구. 연마 후 세 군 간 Ra에 유의한 차이 없음; 미세 연마(P3)는 모든 군에서 유약 처리보다 낮은 Ra 달성. XRD에서 상변환 없음 확인, FE-SEM에서 이트리아 함량 증가에 따른 결정립 크기 증가 관찰.

## Summary

Shin and Lee (2021) addressed whether yttrium oxide composition affects surface roughness outcomes after chairside grinding and polishing — a clinically relevant question since 3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, and 5Y-TZP zirconia have different microstructures yet are routinely polished with the same kit. Using a custom standardized polishing device (2 N, 15,000 rpm, air-cooled), three IPS e.max ZirCAD compositions underwent identical grinding and 3-step polishing. The null hypothesis — that yttria composition does not affect surface roughness — was accepted.

Fine polishing (P3) produced Ra values below the 0.2 µm clinical threshold for plaque accumulation in all three groups, and was significantly smoother than layering-technique glazing. XRD confirmed no tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation under these conditions. FE-SEM revealed that grain size increases with yttria content (3Y < 4Y < 5Y), potentially explaining why composition-dependent differences in phase transformation susceptibility did not translate into roughness differences.

## Key Contributions

1. **Composition-agnostic polishing**: Same clinical bur kit yields equivalent Ra across 3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, 5Y-TZP
2. **Fine polish > glazing**: P3 significantly lower Ra than layering-technique glazing in all compositions
3. **No phase transformation**: 2 N / 15,000 rpm / air-cooled conditions insufficient to drive tetragonal → monoclinic conversion (XRD)
4. **Grain size gradient**: FE-SEM confirms 3Y < 4Y < 5Y grain size progression
5. **Clinical threshold met**: P2 (medium polish) achieves Ra < 0.2 µm (plaque threshold) and < 0.5 µm (tongue detection threshold)

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | In-vitro controlled laboratory |
| Materials | IPS e.max ZirCAD LT (3Y), MT (4Y), MT multi-incisal (5Y), Ivoclar Vivadent |
| Specimen | 12.0 × 6.0 × 4.0 mm bars; n = 23/group; sintered 1500°C |
| Polishing | Custom device: 2 N, 15,000 rpm, 0.1 mm/s, 10 strokes/step, air-cooled |
| Burs | Pre-Zirpol Dia HP (grinding); Zirpol Dia HP 3-step (P1/P2/P3), Neobiotech |
| Ra measurement | Contact profilometer (Tencor D-300); 3 mm scan, 0.1 mm/s, 2 µm tip |
| Phase analysis | XRD (Smartlab Rigaku): 40 kV, 30 mA, 10–70° 2θ |
| Morphology | FE-SEM 30,000× after thermal etching 1,300°C |
| Statistics | Kruskal-Wallis + Mann-Whitney-U post hoc (α = .05) |

## Results

**Surface roughness (Ra µm) — key values**:

| Stage | LT (3Y) | MT (4Y) | MM (5Y) | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C (glazed, control) | ~0.17 | 0.19 ± 0.05 | — | — |
| P2 (medium polish) | 0.18 ± 0.03 | 0.19 ± 0.04 | 0.13 ± 0.04 | LT vs MM: P<.001; MT vs MM: P<.001 |
| P3 (fine polish) | lower than C | lower than C | lower than C | All vs C: P<.05 |

- Between-composition comparison: no significant differences overall
- P3 < C in all groups (P < .05)

**XRD**: Tetragonal peak pattern preserved; no monoclinic peaks at any stage in any group

**FE-SEM**: Grain size increases with yttria (3Y < 4Y < 5Y); grinding introduces scratches; P3 reduces scratch depth
