## Three-line Summary
In vitro 7-arm comparison (J Adv Prosthodont 2015) testing cleaning methods for saliva-contaminated, air-abraded zirconia surfaces: no cleaning, water, methanol, phosphoric acid, Ivoclean alone, air abrasion alone, and air abrasion + Ivoclean.

Air abrasion + Ivoclean achieved the highest bond strength; air abrasion alone nearly matched it; Ivoclean alone outperformed phosphoric acid and methanol; water washing alone was insufficient.

For chairside re-cementation of a saliva-contaminated zirconia restoration, air abrasion is the minimal effective step, with Ivoclean adding marginal but measurable benefit.

## 세줄요약
in-vitro 7군 비교(J Adv Prosthodont 2015): 타액오염 에어어브레이전 처리 지르코니아의 세정법 — 무세정·수세·메탄올·인산·Ivoclean 단독·에어어브레이전 단독·에어어브레이전+Ivoclean.

에어어브레이전+Ivoclean: 최고 결합강도; 에어어브레이전 단독도 거의 동등; Ivoclean 단독 > 인산·메탄올; 수세 단독: 불충분.

임상적으로 타액오염 지르코니아 재접착 시 에어어브레이전이 최소 필수 단계, Ivoclean 병용 시 소폭 추가 이득.

## Summary
In vitro study comparing 7 cleaning methods for saliva-contaminated air-abraded zirconia surfaces to restore resin bond strength. Comprehensive comparison including water, methanol, phosphoric acid, Ivoclean, air abrasion, and combined air abrasion + Ivoclean protocols.

## Key Contributions
- 7-method head-to-head comparison with resin bond strength endpoint
- Air abrasion alone restores bond strength to near-uncontaminated levels
- Ivoclean alone: significantly better than phosphoric acid or methanol
- Air abrasion + Ivoclean: marginal additional benefit over air abrasion alone

## Results
| Method | Bond strength (relative) |
|--------|------------------------|
| Air abrasion + Ivoclean | Highest |
| Air abrasion alone | High |
| Ivoclean alone | Intermediate-high |
| Phosphoric acid | Intermediate |
| Methanol | Intermediate |
| Water | Low |
| No cleaning | Lowest |
