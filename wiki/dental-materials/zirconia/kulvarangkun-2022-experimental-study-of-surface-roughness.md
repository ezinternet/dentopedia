## Three-line Summary

Lithium disilicate and zirconia extraoral polishing sets (Jota) achieved significantly lower surface roughness than the feldspathic porcelain set and glazing across all three ceramic types (feldspathic porcelain, lithium disilicate, zirconia). Ra values after LiDi/Zirconia polishing were below the 0.2 μm bacterial-adhesion threshold and below the 0.25–0.5 μm tongue-detection range, meeting clinical acceptability. The feldspathic polishing set, with larger diamond abrasive particles, produced the roughest surfaces and is not recommended as a sole finishing step after occlusal adjustment.

## 세줄요약

리튬 디실리케이트 및 지르코니아 연마 세트(Jota)는 세 종류 세라믹(장석질 도재, 리튬 디실리케이트, 지르코니아) 모두에서 장석질 세라믹 연마 세트 및 글레이징보다 유의하게 낮은 표면 거칠기(Ra)를 달성하였다. 연마 후 Ra 값은 세균 부착 임계치(<0.2 μm) 및 혀 감지 범위(0.25–0.5 μm)를 하회하여 임상적으로 허용 가능하였다. 장석질 연마 세트는 연마 입자가 가장 커 모든 세라믹에서 최고 Ra를 보였으며 단독 마무리 단계로는 권장되지 않는다.

## Summary

This in-vitro study examined the surface roughness (Ra) of 120 ceramic specimens across three materials — feldspathic porcelain (장석질 도재, Vintage PRO), lithium disilicate (리튬 디실리케이트, IPS e.max CAD), and zirconia (지르코니아, Prettau Zirconia) — after polishing with three extraoral ceramic polishing sets and glazing (control). Each ceramic was divided into four subgroups (n = 10): glazed, or ground with a 46 μm diamond bur then sequentially polished by two rubber wheels from the feldspathic (Jota 1429), lithium disilicate (Jota 1433), or zirconia (Jota 1434) polishing sets at 8,000 rpm with 1 N pressure. Ra was measured by profilometer; surface morphology by SEM; wheel composition by XRD and EDS.

Both the LiDi and Zirconia polishing sets produced significantly lower Ra than glazing and the feldspathic polishing set, with no significant difference between the two sets (p <0.05 threshold). Polishing outperformed glazing — an important clinical finding. The feldspathic polishing set performed worst, correlating with its largest abrasive particle size visible in SEM and confirmed by XRD (diamond dominant; TiO₂, Fe₂O₃ in feldspathic set; additionally SiC exclusive to zirconia set). All ceramics polished with LiDi or Zirconia sets achieved Ra <0.2 μm (bacterial adhesion threshold per Bollen et al.) and Ra <0.25 μm (tongue detection threshold).

## Key Contributions

1. First cross-material comparison including a lithium disilicate–specific polishing set alongside feldspathic and zirconia sets.
2. LiDi and Zirconia polishing sets functionally equivalent in Ra across all three ceramic substrates — either set is clinically appropriate regardless of ceramic type.
3. XRD/EDS evidence: polishing outcome correlates more with abrasive particle size than with elemental composition of wheels.
4. Polishing with LiDi/Zirconia sets outperformed glazing — reframing the assumption that glazing is the gold standard for smooth surfaces.
5. Ra <0.2 μm (clinically acceptable for bacterial adhesion) achieved by LiDi and Zirconia sets on all ceramics; feldspathic set failed this threshold for feldspathic porcelain (Ra 0.6319 μm).

## Methodology

- **Specimens**: 15 × 20 × 2 mm, sintered per manufacturer instructions; n = 10 per subgroup, 40 per ceramic, 120 total
- **Grinding**: Fine diamond bur 46 μm, 10,000 rpm, 30 s, 1 bur per 10 specimens
- **Polishing**: 8,000 rpm, 1 N, 10 passes per wheel, one direction, standardized custom device; sequential two-wheel protocol per set
- **Ra measurement**: Talysurf Intra 50 profilometer; 4 mm traversal, 3 points per specimen; 1 mN stylus force, 0.5 mm/s
- **Analysis**: SEM (JSM-6610, ×1,000 and ×500 magnification); XRD (10–100°, step 0.01°); EDS for wheel morphology
- **Statistics**: One-way ANOVA + Dunnett's T3 post hoc, p <0.05

## Results

| Ceramic | Glaze | Feldspathic set | LiDi set | Zirconia set |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Feldspathic porcelain | 0.3481 ± 0.2505 μm | 0.6319 ± 0.1145 μm | **0.0476 ± 0.0307 μm** | 0.0590 ± 0.0187 μm |
| Lithium disilicate | 0.1630 ± 0.0601 μm | 0.1217 ± 0.0481 μm | **0.0162 ± 0.0084 μm** | 0.0177 ± 0.0601 μm |
| Zirconia | 0.0981 ± 0.0170 μm | 0.2750 ± 0.0756 μm | **0.0185 ± 0.0044 μm** | 0.0230 ± 0.0189 μm |

- LiDi set and Zirconia set: **not significantly different** on any ceramic
- Feldspathic set and glaze: **not significantly different** from each other (both inferior to LiDi/Zirconia sets)
- Feldspathic set exceeded 0.5 μm threshold for feldspathic porcelain only → tongue-detectable roughness
- XRD: all wheels diamond-dominant; zirconia set uniquely contains SiC (Hv 1,800–2,000 kg/mm²) + TiO₂
- SEM: feldspathic set showed largest abrasive grains → deepest parallel scratches in lithium disilicate specimens

**Clinical thresholds**:
- Ra <0.2 μm: prevents bacterial adhesion (Bollen et al.) — met by LiDi/Zirconia sets on all three ceramics
- Ra 0.25–0.5 μm: tongue-detectable roughness — exceeded only by feldspathic ceramic + feldspathic set combination
