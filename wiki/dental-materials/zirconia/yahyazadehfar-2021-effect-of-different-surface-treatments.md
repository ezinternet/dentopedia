## Three-line Summary

Er,Cr:YSGG laser treatment of graded dental zirconia (Y-TZP) produced the lowest monoclinic phase transformation (mean Vm = 1.2%, comparable to untreated control) and the highest Weibull reliability modulus (m = 20.5) among control, sandblasting (SA), diamond-bur grinding (GB), and laser (LS) groups. Grinding with a diamond bur generated the greatest surface roughness (Ra 1.87 µm) and monoclinic phase increase (4.7%), with the lowest Weibull modulus (12.2), indicating unreliable flaw distribution. Sandblasting yielded the numerically highest flexural strength (1023 MPa) despite significant phase transformation, while no significant correlation existed between surface roughness or monoclinic phase and biaxial flexural strength.

## 세줄요약

Er,Cr:YSGG 레이저 처리는 모노클리닉상 변환율(Vm = 1.2%)이 대조군 수준으로 가장 낮고 Weibull 계수(m = 20.5)가 가장 높아 굴곡강도의 신뢰성이 우수하다. 버 그라인딩(GB)은 표면조도 (Ra 1.87 µm)와 모노클리닉상 비율(4.7%)이 가장 높고 Weibull 계수(12.2)가 가장 낮아 임상적으로 가장 불리한 처리법이다. 샌드블라스팅은 상변환이 유의하게 증가함에도 굴곡강도가 가장 높았으며(1023 MPa), 표면조도·모노클리닉 비율과 굴곡강도 사이에는 유의한 상관관계가 없었다.

## Summary

This in-vitro study compared four surface treatment methods for graded sintered dental zirconia (지르코니아 (Zirconia, Y-TZP)) to identify which approach best roughens the bonding surface without compromising mechanical integrity. Forty disk-shaped specimens (12 mm diameter × 1.2 mm) were divided into four groups (n = 10): untreated control, grinding with a diamond bur (GB), sandblasting with 110-µm Al₂O₃ at 2.5 bar (SA), and Er,Cr:YSGG laser irradiation at 1.5 W / 15 Hz (LS).

Three outcomes were assessed: (1) 표면 조도 (Surface Roughness, Ra/Rz) via optical profilometry (ISO 25178), (2) 모노클리닉상 변환율 (Monoclinic Phase Fraction, Vm) via X-ray diffraction (XRD), and (3) 이축굴곡강도 (Biaxial Flexural Strength, BFS) with 2-parameter 와이블 분석 (Weibull Analysis) per ISO 6872:2008.

The key finding was that laser treatment maintained phase composition close to the unmodified control while achieving the most uniform flaw distribution (highest Weibull modulus), making it the most mechanically reliable option. Diamond bur grinding was the most damaging: highest roughness, highest phase conversion, most variable strength. Sandblasting offered the highest mean BFS despite phase transformation, possibly due to surface compressive residual stresses induced by particle impact.

## Key Contributions

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| Laser lowest monoclinic phase | LS: Vm = 1.2% (vs SA 2.9%, GB 4.7%, control 1.4%); no significant difference from control |
| Laser highest Weibull modulus | m = 20.5 (LS) vs 16.5 (control), 16.3 (SA), 12.2 (GB); σ₀ = 1020.2 MPa |
| GB highest roughness | Ra = 1.87 µm (GB) vs 0.29 (LS), 0.23 (SA), 0.19 (control); P < 0.005 |
| SA highest mean BFS | 1023.0 ± 74.8 MPa, significantly > control (P = 0.02) and GB (P < 0.01) |
| No roughness–strength correlation | Pearson r non-significant in all groups (P > 0.33) |
| No monoclinic–strength correlation | Pearson r non-significant in all groups (P > 0.39) |
| Laser power threshold | ≤ 1.5 W avoids microcracks and discoloration (SEM pilot, 4 extra disks) |

## Methodology

**Design**: In-vitro comparative (4-group parallel), one-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc.

**Material**: Graded zirconia (Upcera, Shenzhen; yttrium partially-stabilised; >98% ZrO₂, sintered at 1450 °C). Disks pre-polished to 1200-grit SiC baseline.

**Treatments**:
- **Control**: No surface modification after baseline polish.
- **GB**: Diamond bur (108–120 µm), 200 kPa, 20 s, back-and-forth under water coolant.
- **SA**: 110-µm Al₂O₃ particles, 2.5 bar, 30 mm distance, 30 s.
- **LS**: Er,Cr:YSGG 2940 nm, 1.5 W, 15 Hz, 74 µs pulse, 1 mm non-contact, 80% water/air.

**Measurement tools**: Keyence VHX2000 (roughness); Rigaku Ultimate IV XRD (phase); Zwick Roell universal frame, 1 mm/min (BFS).

**XRD formula**: Vm = 1.311 Xm / (1 + 0.311 Xm) where Xm = (Im-111 + Im111) / (Im-111 + Im111 + It101).

**Key statistical findings**:
- GB roughness significantly > all others (P < 0.005); control / LS / SA roughness not significantly different (P > 0.7).
- SA and GB Vm significantly > control and LS (P < 0.001).
- SA BFS significantly > control (P = 0.02) and GB (P < 0.01); LS BFS not significantly different from any group.
- No significant Pearson correlation between roughness or Vm and BFS (all P > 0.33).
