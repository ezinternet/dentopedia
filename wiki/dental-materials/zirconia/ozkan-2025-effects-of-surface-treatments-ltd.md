## Three-line Summary

Grinding followed by polishing produced the highest biaxial flexural strength and lowest surface roughness in both 4Y-TZP and 6Y-TZP, while grinding alone caused the greatest mechanical deterioration. LTD aging combined with pH 3 exposure accelerated tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation to 22% and reduced flexural strength by up to 30%, versus 15% transformation and 18% strength loss at pH 9. 6Y-TZP exhibited lower absolute strength than 4Y-TZP but greater resistance to LTD-induced phase transformation due to its higher cubic phase content.

## 세줄요약

연마(그라인딩)+폴리싱이 4Y-TZP 및 6Y-TZP 모두에서 최고 이축굴곡강도 (Biaxial Flexural Strength, BFS)와 최저 표면조도 (Surface Roughness, Ra)를 나타냈으며, 연마만 단독 시행 시 기계적 성능이 가장 크게 저하되었다. 저온열화 (Low-Temperature Degradation, LTD) 에이징+pH 3 노출 조합은 단사정 변태 (Tetragonal-to-Monoclinic Phase Transformation)를 22%까지 가속하고 굴곡강도를 최대 30% 감소시킨 반면, pH 9에서는 단사정 변태 15%, 강도 18% 손실로 비교적 양호했다. 6Y-TZP는 4Y-TZP보다 절대 강도는 낮지만 고큐빅상 함량 덕분에 LTD 저항성이 우수하여, 고부하 후방부 적용 시 표면처리 선택이 중요하다.

## Summary

This in vitro study investigated the combined effects of surface treatment type, hydrothermal aging (LTD), and pH environment on the mechanical properties of two high-translucent zirconia materials: 4Y-TZP and 6Y-TZP. A total of 336 disc-shaped specimens were divided into four surface treatment groups (glazed, grinding alone, grinding+polishing, grinding+glazing) and three conditions (control, LTD+pH3, LTD+pH9). Grinding+polishing provided the most favorable combination of flexural strength and surface roughness. The combined insult of LTD and acidic pH (3) produced the most severe degradation, with up to 22% monoclinic phase and 30% strength reduction. 6Y-TZP, despite lower baseline strength, showed greater LTD resistance than 4Y-TZP, underscoring the trade-off between translucency and durability.

## Key Contributions

1. First study to evaluate combined effects of LTD aging and prolonged pH exposure (14 days) on high-translucent zirconia surface treatments
2. Demonstrates that post-grinding polishing is the most effective surface treatment for preserving mechanical properties after LTD
3. Quantifies differential degradation under acidic (pH 3) vs alkaline (pH 9) conditions after LTD
4. Provides Weibull reliability analysis for all 24 experimental groups
5. Characterizes 4Y-TZP vs 6Y-TZP trade-off: strength vs LTD resistance

## Methodology

- 336 disc specimens (12 mm diameter, 1.2 mm thickness) sintered at 1450°C
- Surface treatments: Glazed / Grinding alone (350,000 rpm, 46-μm diamond bur) / Grinding+Polishing (3-step Meisinger→Optrafine→Signum) / Grinding+Glazing
- LTD: Autoclave 134°C, 0.2 MPa, 30 h (≈10 years clinical aging)
- Post-LTD: 14 days at pH 3 (citric acid) or pH 9 (ammonium buffer), 37°C
- BFS: Piston-on-three-balls (ISO 6872), Instron 5965, 1 mm/min
- XRD (Empyrean): Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya method for monoclinic fraction
- SEM (Quanta 250 FEG), Profilometry (Talysurf CCI HD)

## Results

| Group | BFS (MPa) | Ra (μm) | Monoclinic % after LTD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glazed control | 1171 ± 113 | 0.71 | — |
| Grinding control | 839 ± 73 | highest | — |
| Grinding+Polish control | 1112 ± 118 | low | 12% (best) |
| LTD + pH 3 (grinding) | 760 ± 80 | 2.57 | 22% |
| LTD + pH 9 (grinding) | 631 ± 66 | — | 15% |

- 6Y-TZP: lower BFS but ~34% cubic content → greater LTD resistance vs 4Y-TZP (~22% cubic)
- Grinding+polishing recommended; grinding alone is contraindicated
