## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (3 commercial 3Y-TZP zirconia: Zpex, Prettau, ZirTough; tested at 134°C/2bar/200h and 180°C/9bar/16h): ALL Prettau specimens fractured catastrophically in both aging protocols — no other brands fractured.

ZirTough was most resistant (31.4% monoclinic at 200h/134°C); Zpex showed intermediate degradation; correlation between monoclinic content and flexural strength was strongly negative (ρ=-0.71 to -1.0).

The first study to show catastrophic fracture during LTD testing for a commercial dental zirconia — directly predicts clinical risk for restorations in high-moisture, high-stress environments.

## Summary

Flinn et al. (2014) tested three commercial 3Y-TZP materials across two aging protocols and discovered that one brand (Prettau) underwent catastrophic fracture during LTD testing. This was the first study to demonstrate that some commercial dental zirconias can fracture under LTD conditions alone — without any mechanical loading — highlighting that not all 3Y-TZP formulations have equivalent LTD resistance. The strong negative correlation between monoclinic content and residual flexural strength directly validates the LTD mechanism as a clinically relevant failure pathway.

## Key Contributions

1. **Catastrophic fracture documented** in a commercial dental zirconia (Prettau) under LTD alone — no other brands fractured
2. **Brand-to-brand variability** in LTD resistance within the same 3Y-TZP class — cannot assume equivalence
3. **Dose-response quantified**: ρ=-0.71 to -1.0 between monoclinic% and FS — strongest empirical validation of LTD mechanism
4. **Two temperature protocols compared**: 134°C/200h equivalent to 180°C/16h, confirming accelerated testing validity

## Methodology

- 3 commercial 3Y-TZP materials: Zpex (Tosoh), Prettau (Zirkonzahn), ZirTough
- Bars: 1.5 × 4 × 45mm, standard polish finish
- Protocol A: 134°C/2bar/200h; Protocol B: 180°C/9bar/16h
- XRD monoclinic quantification; 4-point flexural strength (ISO 6872); Spearman correlation

## Results

| Material | Monoclinic (200h/134°C) | Survived? | FS Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zpex | Moderate increase | ✅ Yes | ↓ moderate |
| Prettau | — | ❌ ALL fractured | — |
| ZirTough | 31.4% | ✅ Yes | ↓ mild |

- Spearman ρ = -0.71 to -1.0 (monoclinic vs FS)
- Prettau fracture at 134°C/200h AND 180°C/16h
