## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (3 Y-TZP materials — Prettau, Zirprime and a new formulation, ZirTough; thin bars 22 × 3 × 0.2 mm, n = 55/brand; aging at 134 °C/0.2 MPa for 5–200 h and at 180 °C/1.0 MPa for 8–48 h; 4-point bend, ASTM 1161-B).

After 200 h at 134 °C flexural strength fell from 1328 ± 89.9 MPa to all specimens fractured for Prettau, 1041 ± 130 → 779 ± 137 MPa for Zirprime and 1436 ± 136 → 1243 ± 101 MPa for ZirTough, with monoclinic fractions of 78.8%, 74.8% and 31.4%; at 180 °C all Prettau bars fractured spontaneously by 16 h, while Zirprime (595 ± 88.4 MPa) and ZirTough (1068 ± 76.8 MPa) survived 48 h.

Strength fell as monoclinic phase rose in every group (Spearman ρ −0.71 to −1.0), and LTD resistance differed sharply between Y-TZP brands — thin 0.2 mm bars exaggerate the effect relative to clinical restoration thickness.

## Summary

Flinn et al. (2014, Journal of Prosthetic Dentistry; University of Washington) compared the accelerated-aging behaviour of a new Y-TZP (ZirTough) with two commercial Y-TZPs (Prettau, Zirprime), using very thin bars cut and ground from manufacturer-sintered blocks. Two aging regimes were used: standard autoclave sterilization conditions (134 °C, 0.2 MPa, up to 200 h) and industrial ceramic aging conditions (180 °C, 1.0 MPa, up to 48 h). Prettau degraded catastrophically under both regimes — every specimen fractured during aging. Zirprime transformed almost as much as Prettau (74.8% monoclinic at 200 h) but survived with reduced strength. ZirTough transformed least and retained the most strength. Across all groups, flexural strength correlated inversely with monoclinic content, and SEM showed a transformed surface layer on fracture surfaces, tying the strength loss to the tetragonal→monoclinic transformation.

## Key Contributions

1. **Catastrophic LTD in a commercial Y-TZP**: Prettau thin bars all fractured during aging at 134 °C (by 200 h) and at 180 °C (by 16 h)
2. **Large between-brand variability within Y-TZP**: monoclinic at 200 h/134 °C ranged 31.4% (ZirTough) to 78.8% (Prettau)
3. **Strength–phase link**: Spearman ρ −0.71 to −1.0 between monoclinic fraction and flexural strength
4. **Two aging regimes**: clinical sterilization-type (134 °C) and industrial (180 °C) conditions reported side by side

## Methodology

- Materials: Prettau, Zirprime (commercial Y-TZP) and ZirTough (new Y-TZP); n = 55 bars per brand
- Specimens: thin bars 22 × 3 × 0.2 mm, cut and ground from blocks sintered per manufacturer; non-aged controls; composition by energy dispersive spectroscopy
- Aging A: 134 °C, 0.2 MPa — 5, 50, 100, 150, 200 h (n = 5 per time point)
- Aging B: 180 °C, 1.0 MPa — 8, 16, 24, 48 h (n = 5 per time point)
- Outcomes: monoclinic fraction by XRD; 4-point bend flexural strength (ASTM 1161-B); SEM of fracture surfaces
- Statistics: 1-way ANOVA with heteroscedasticity-consistent standard errors; ANOVA for incomplete block design (monoclinic ratio); Spearman rank correlation
- Source artifact: PubMed structured abstract only (no full text)

## Results

**134 °C / 0.2 MPa, 200 h**

| Material | Flexural strength, baseline → 200 h (MPa) | Monoclinic, baseline → 200 h |
|---|---|---|
| Prettau | 1328 ± 89.9 → all fractured during aging (P < .001) | 3.08% → 78.8% |
| Zirprime | 1041 ± 130 → 779 ± 137 (P < .001) | 1.95% → 74.8% |
| ZirTough | 1436 ± 136 → 1243 ± 101 (P = .017) | 12.4% → 31.4% |

**180 °C / 1.0 MPa**

| Material | Outcome | Monoclinic |
|---|---|---|
| Prettau | All specimens fractured spontaneously by 16 h | 79.0% |
| Zirprime | 1041 → 595 ± 88.4 MPa at 48 h | 68.1% |
| ZirTough | 1436 → 1068 ± 76.8 MPa at 48 h | 39.5% |

- Spearman ρ between flexural strength and monoclinic fraction: −0.71 to −1.0 in all groups
- SEM: transformed layer visible on fracture surfaces
