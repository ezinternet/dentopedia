## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (N = 153 discs; three zirconia generations — 1st-gen VITA YZ T, 2nd-gen INCORIS TZI, 3rd-gen KATANA UTML; protocols CF = fatigue only, AF = 134 °C/20 h hydrothermal aging then fatigue, AFA = 4 × 5 h aging intercalated with fatigue; step-stress biaxial fatigue to failure, n = 15/group).

Continuous aging + fatigue lowered VITA YZ T fatigue strength (YAF 516 ± 38 MPa) while the alternating protocol raised it (YAFA 730 ± 58 MPa); INCORIS TZI after AF had the highest fatigue strength (810 ± 76 MPa) and cycles to failure (147,000); KATANA UTML showed no difference between treatments and no t→m transformation, although it was the less fatigue-resistant material.

Clinical reading: the shape of the aging protocol (continuous vs alternating) changes the measured fatigue outcome of 3Y-TZP, so in-vitro LTD/fatigue numbers from different protocols are not interchangeable; the 5Y-PSZ trades strength for chemical stability — abstract-only, in-vitro evidence.

## 세줄요약

시험관 연구(원판 총 153개; 지르코니아 3세대 — 1세대 VITA YZ T, 2세대 INCORIS TZI, 3세대 KATANA UTML; CF=피로만, AF=134 °C/20시간 수열노화 후 피로, AFA=5시간 노화 4회와 피로를 교대; 단계응력 이축 피로를 파절까지, 군당 n=15).

연속 노화+피로는 VITA YZ T 피로강도를 낮췄고(YAF 516 ± 38 MPa) 교대 프로토콜은 오히려 높였다(YAFA 730 ± 58 MPa); INCORIS TZI는 AF 후 피로강도(810 ± 76 MPa)·파절까지 사이클(147,000) 모두 최고; KATANA UTML은 처리 간 차이 없고 정방정→단사정 상전이(t→m)도 없었으나 피로 저항은 더 낮았다.

임상적 의미: 노화 프로토콜 형태(연속 vs 교대)가 3Y-TZP의 피로 결과를 바꾸므로 프로토콜이 다른 시험관 LTD·피로 수치는 서로 바꿔 쓸 수 없다; 5Y-PSZ는 강도를 내주고 화학적 안정성을 얻는다 — 초록 기반 시험관 근거.

## Summary

Prado et al. (2022, Dental Materials) asked how hydrothermal aging and mechanical fatigue interact across three generations of dental zirconia, and introduced an intercalated aging/fatigue protocol (AFA) alongside fatigue-only (CF) and aging-then-fatigue (AF) arms. Fatigue was run with a step-stress approach to failure and analysed as survival data (Kaplan-Meier, Mantel-Cox) plus Weibull statistics. The 1st-generation 3Y-TZP (VITA YZ T) behaved opposite ways depending on protocol: continuous aging + fatigue reduced its fatigue strength, alternating aging/fatigue increased it. The 2nd-generation 3Y-TZP (INCORIS TZI) was the best performer after aging. The 3rd-generation high-translucency zirconia (KATANA UTML) did not undergo t→m transformation and was unaffected by the aging treatments, but its fatigue strength was lower. The abstract does not report a mechanism for the strength gain under the alternating protocol; any explanation (e.g., transformation-induced compressive stress) is interpretation, not a finding of this study.

## Key Contributions

1. **Intercalated aging/fatigue protocol (AFA)**: a novel protocol alternating 5 h hydrothermal steps with fatigue cycling, compared directly with continuous aging (AF) and fatigue-only control (CF)
2. **Protocol-dependent outcome in 1st-gen 3Y-TZP**: YAF 516 ± 38 MPa (decrease) vs YAFA 730 ± 58 MPa (increase) — the same material moves in opposite directions
3. **2nd-gen 3Y-TZP best after aging**: INCORIS TZI AF 810 ± 76 MPa and 147,000 cycles to failure — highest fatigue strength and survival
4. **3rd-gen 5Y-PSZ chemically stable but weaker**: KATANA UTML — no t→m, no treatment effect, lower fatigue resistance
5. **Failure origin**: all fractures started on the tensile side at pre-existing defects

## Methodology

- Materials: VITA YZ T (VITA; 1st gen), INCORIS TZI (Dentsply Sirona; 2nd gen), KATANA UTML (Kuraray Noritake; 3rd gen) discs, N = 153
- Groups: 6 groups (n = 3) for monotonic testing; 9 groups (n = 15) for fatigue testing — 3 treatments × 3 materials
- Treatments: CF (fatigue only); AF (134 °C, 2 bar, 20 h in hydrothermal reactor, then fatigue); AFA (4 × 5 h hydrothermal aging intercalated with fatigue)
- Fatigue: step-stress, biaxial piston-on-3-balls; initial 100 MPa, +50 MPa per 10,000 cycles, 20 Hz, to failure
- Statistics: Kaplan-Meier and Mantel-Cox (α = 0.05); Weibull analysis
- Fractography: stereomicroscope, SEM; phase analysis by XRD
- Source artifact: PubMed structured abstract only (no full text)

## Results

| Material (generation) | Protocol | Fatigue strength (MPa) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| VITA YZ T (1st) | AF — continuous | 516 ± 38 | decreased |
| VITA YZ T (1st) | AFA — alternating | 730 ± 58 | increased |
| INCORIS TZI (2nd) | AF | 810 ± 76 | highest; 147,000 cycles to failure |
| KATANA UTML (3rd) | AF / AFA | not reported in abstract | no difference between treatments; no t→m; less resistant overall |

- Control (CF) values are not given in the abstract; "decreased/increased" follows the authors' wording
- Fracture origin in all specimens: tensile side, pre-existing defects
- Authors' conclusion: INCORIS TZI had higher fatigue strength and survival after hydrothermal + mechanical aging; KATANA UTML, although less resistant, did not suffer chemical degradation
