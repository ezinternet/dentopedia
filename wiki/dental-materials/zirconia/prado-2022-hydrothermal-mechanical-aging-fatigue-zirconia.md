## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (4 commercial zirconia: VITA YZ HT 3Y-TZP, KATANA UTML 5Y-PSZ, INCORIS TZI 3Y-TZP, Ceramill ZI 3Y-TZP; alternating HT aging + mechanical fatigue protocol): alternating HT+fatigue INCREASED flexural strength in VITA YZ (730→754→808 MPa) — consistent with transformation toughening under sequential stressors.

KATANA UTML showed NO t→m transformation after hydrothermal aging — confirming 5Y-PSZ LTD resistance; INCORIS TZI maintained highest combined performance (810±76 MPa, 147k fatigue cycles before failure).

The alternating dual-stressor protocol is more clinically representative than single-stressor approaches and reveals that aging can paradoxically protect some 3Y-TZP formulations through sequential compressive stress induction.

## Summary

Prado et al. (2022) introduced an alternating hydrothermal + mechanical fatigue protocol to simulate the combined stressors in the oral environment more realistically than single-stressor approaches. The key finding was that KATANA UTML (5Y-PSZ, high cubic content) showed no t→m transformation under LTD conditions, confirming the cubic-phase LTD resistance established by Kengtanyakich (2020) in a different multi-brand context. The paradoxical strength increase in VITA YZ HT under alternating stress is consistent with the transformation toughening mechanism documented across multiple studies in this surveillance.

## Key Contributions

1. **Alternating dual-stressor protocol**: more clinically representative than sequential or single-stressor; reveals interaction effects
2. **KATANA UTML (5Y-PSZ): NO LTD** — independent confirmation of cubic-phase LTD resistance
3. **INCORIS TZI best combined performance**: 810±76 MPa + 147k cycles — highest combined mechanical and LTD resistance
4. **Paradoxical FS increase**: VITA YZ HT strength increased under alternating protocol — transformation toughening from sequential compressive stress

## Methodology

- 4 commercial materials: VITA YZ HT (3Y-TZP), KATANA UTML (5Y-PSZ), INCORIS TZI (3Y-TZP), Ceramill ZI (3Y-TZP)
- Alternating protocol: hydrothermal aging (134°C) + mechanical fatigue cycling; several cycles
- XRD: t→m monoclinic; biaxial flexural strength; fatigue failure load (N cycles)
- Statistical comparison: 1-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD

## Results

|---|---|---|---|---|
| VITA YZ HT | 3Y-TZP | 808±85 MPa (↑ from 730) | Yes | Moderate |

| INCORIS TZI | 3Y-TZP | 810±76 MPa | Minimal | 147k (highest) |
