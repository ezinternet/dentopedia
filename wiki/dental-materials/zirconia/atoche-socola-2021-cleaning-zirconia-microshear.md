## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (J Adv Prosthodont 2021) comparing various cleaning techniques on contaminated CAD/CAM zirconia (Ivoclar Vivadent) using microshear bond strength (µSBS) of dual-cure resin cement as the primary outcome.

All cleaning groups showed significantly higher µSBS compared to the contaminated-uncleaned control group, confirming that any form of cleaning restores bond strength after contamination.

Cleaning before cementation is mandatory for contaminated zirconia; the study contributes to the growing evidence base but does not identify an optimal single cleaning method.

## 세줄요약
인비트로(J Adv Prosthodont 2021): 오염된 CAD/CAM 지르코니아(Ivoclar Vivadent)에서 다양한 세정 기법의 이중경화형 레진 시멘트 미세전단접착강도(Microshear Bond Strength, µSBS)를 비교.

모든 세정군이 오염 무처리 대조군보다 µSBS 유의하게 향상 → 오염 후 어떤 방식으로든 세정하면 접착강도 회복 가능.

오염된 지르코니아(Zirconia)는 접착 전 세정이 필수이나, 이 연구 단독으로는 최적 세정법을 특정하지 못하며 추가 비교 연구가 필요.

## Summary
In vitro comparison of various cleaning techniques on contaminated CAD/CAM zirconia (Ivoclar Vivadent) using microshear bond strength of dual-cure resin cement as the outcome. Confirms that cleaning is mandatory before cementation of contaminated zirconia.
