## Three-line Summary

This in vitro study evaluated the effect of two steam sterilization techniques — standard (121°C, 15 min) and flash/immediate (134°C, 3 min) — on the polishing efficacy of the EVE Diacera chairside adjustment kit (CAK) on highly translucent monolithic multilayered zirconia (HTMLZ, Zolid Gen-X). One hundred disc specimens were polished after each of 5 cycle milestones (1, 5, 10, 15, 20 cycles) with surface roughness measured by profilometry (Ra, Rq) and SEM. Both techniques caused progressive roughness degradation; flash sterilization was superior, and the study recommends ≤10 flash sterilization cycles to maintain clinically acceptable Ra (≤0.25 μm) on HTMLZ.

## 세줄요약

이 시험관 연구는 EVE Diacera 의자옆 조정 키트(CAK)를 표준(121°C, 15분)과 즉시/플래시(134°C, 3분) 두 가지 증기 멸균 방식으로 반복 멸균했을 때 고투명도 단일체 다층 지르코니아(HTMLZ, Zolid Gen-X)의 연마 표면 거칠기에 미치는 영향을 평가하였다. 100개 디스크 시편에 대해 멸균 1·5·10·15·20회 시점에서 연마 후 프로파일로미터(Ra, Rq)와 SEM으로 측정한 결과, 두 방식 모두 거칠기가 증가하였으며 플래시 멸균이 우수하였다. 임상 허용 임계값(Ra 0.25 μm)은 플래시 10회·표준 15회에서 초과되어, EVE Diacera 키트는 플래시 멸균 최대 10회 이내 사용을 권장한다.

## Summary

Steam sterilization of chairside adjustment kits (CAK) is clinically necessary before intraoral use, but repeated heat/moisture cycles degrade the elastomeric binder matrix of polishing instruments, causing loss of abrasive particles and progressive surface roughening of treated restorations. This study is the **first to quantify this degradation specifically for a zirconia-dedicated CAK** (EVE Diacera) on highly translucent monolithic layered zirconia (HTMLZ).

Key finding: **flash sterilization (134°C, 3 min)** preserves polishing efficacy significantly better than **standard sterilization (121°C, 15 min)**. The difference is primarily attributed to the shorter exposure duration to steam (3 vs. 15 min) rather than the higher temperature, since moisture-driven hydrolysis and oxidative degradation of the elastomeric matrix is time-dependent. The clinically unacceptable Ra threshold of 0.25 μm was crossed at 10 flash cycles and 15 standard cycles.

Clinical translation: When using EVE Diacera for HTMLZ adjustment, limit flash sterilization to ≤10 cycles (manufacturer's recommendation validated with caveats). If standard sterilization is used, degrade the kit after ≤5 cycles. Surface roughness beyond 0.25 μm compromises HTMLZ translucency, promotes biofilm accumulation, and increases antagonist tooth wear.

## Key Contributions

1. **First study** to investigate steam sterilization impact on a zirconia-specific polishing kit (EVE Diacera).
2. Flash sterilization preserves Ra ≤0.25 μm through cycle 5; standard sterilization through cycle 10 — but with 10 specimens per cycle milestone in this study (clinical reality is 1–4 restorations per cycle), actual clinical performance likely exceeds these thresholds.
3. Identifies **sterilization duration** as the dominant degradation variable (not temperature/pressure).
4. Rq values provide additional clinical guidance: high Rq after standard sterilization >10 cycles indicates glazing may be preferable to further polishing with that degraded kit.
5. SEM confirms progressive surface defect spread correlated with elastomeric binder hardening and abrasive loss.

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Specimen | Zolid Gen-X (ZGX) HTMLZ, 10 mm × 3 mm discs, shade A2, n=100 |
| CAD/CAM | Ceramill Mind DRS + vhf 5-axis mill, 60,000 rpm dry milling |
| Sintering | Program P7 (manufacturer), standard heat-up/dwell/cooling |
| Polishing kit | EVE Diacera — 8 tools (2 coarse rigid + 3 medium + 3 fine flexible, diamond/ZrO₂/SiO₂ abrasives, elastomeric binder) |
| Grinding | NTI-Kahla diamond bur, 40 μm grain, 160,000 rpm, pressure-controlled |
| Polishing | 90 s unidirectional per specimen, motor-driven handpiece |
| Group S (standard) | 121°C, 15 min, 1 bar, pre-vacuum |
| Group F (flash) | 134°C, 3 min, 2 bar, pre-vacuum |
| Cycle milestones | 1 (control), 5, 10, 15, 20 cycles; n=10 per subgroup |
| Roughness measurement | JEOL profilometer + Vision 32, stylus ~0.75 mm, 3 measurements/specimen, 20× |
| SEM | JSM-6360 LV JEOL, 5000×, Au-Pd sputter coating |
| Statistics | One-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD, P ≤ 0.05 |

## Results

| Group | Cycle 1 Ra (μm) | Cycle 5 Ra (μm) | Cycle 10 Ra (μm) | Cycle 15 Ra (μm) | Cycle 20 Ra (μm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gp F (flash) | 0.208 (acceptable) | ≤0.25 (acceptable) | >0.25 (unacceptable) | — | 0.413 |
| Gp S (standard) | 0.240 (acceptable) | 0.238 (acceptable) | ≤0.25 (acceptable) | >0.25 (unacceptable) | 0.612 |

- Statistically significant differences across all 5 cycle subgroups in both groups (P ≤ 0.05, ANOVA).
- Tukey HSD: Gp S showed significant pairwise differences from cycle 15 onward; Gp F from cycle 10 onward.
- Rq: fewer significant pairs in Gp F vs Gp S — flash sterilization produces more consistent surface quality.
- SEM: control cycle = smooth surfaces; cycle 10 and 20 = increasing surface defects; Gp F defects smaller/sparser than Gp S.
