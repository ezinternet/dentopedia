## Three-line Summary

Mechanical surface processing (grinding, polishing) and chewing simulation caused the monoclinic phase fraction of 3Y/4Y/5Y-TZP dental zirconia to decrease — not increase — with polishing and chewing completely eliminating the monoclinic phase and converting it to tetragonal phases. The preferred tetragonal phase differed by yttria content: phase t for 3 mol% and phase t″ for 5 mol%. Rietveld refinement proved substantially more accurate than the Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya formula (DIN EN ISO 13356 standard), detecting a two-stage transformation that the standard method entirely missed.

## 세줄요약

기계적 표면 처리(연삭·연마)와 저작 시뮬레이션은 3Y/4Y/5Y-TZP 치과용 지르코니아 (Y-TZP, Yttria-stabilized Tetragonal Zirconia Polycrystalline)의 단사정상 (Monoclinic Phase) 비율을 감소시키며, 연마·저작은 단사정상을 완전히 소멸시켜 정방정상 (Tetragonal Phase)으로 전환한다. 선호되는 정방정상은 이트리아 함량에 따라 달라지며 3 mol%에서는 t상, 5 mol%에서는 t″상이 주를 이룬다. 리트벨트 정밀화 (Rietveld Refinement)는 DIN EN ISO 13356 표준의 가르비-니콜슨 공식보다 훨씬 정확하게 2단계 상전이를 검출하였다.

## Summary

This in-vitro study investigated how dental laboratory surface tools — grinding, polishing — along with chewing simulation and thermocycling affect the crystallographic phase composition of seven commercially available 3Y-, 4Y-, and 5Y-TZP zirconia plates from two manufacturers. All specimens were sintered and milled, then subjected to six distinct treatment positions. Surface roughness was measured by confocal laser scanning microscopy (CLSM). Phase composition was analyzed by XRD using both the Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya formula (DIN EN ISO 13356) and Rietveld refinement.

The null hypothesis — that monoclinic fraction and roughness would increase with mechanical and hydrothermal stress — was rejected. Instead, grinding halved the monoclinic fraction in 3Y-TZP, and polishing/chewing completely eliminated it, driving conversion to tetragonal phases. Thermocycling produced a slight increase in monoclinic fraction, consistent with low-temperature degradation (LTD). Rietveld refinement revealed a two-stage transformation that Garvie-Nicholson entirely missed, and also identified which tetragonal sub-phase (t vs. t″) was preferred based on yttria content.

## Key Contributions

1. Grinding reduces (halves) monoclinic phase in 3Y-TZP; polishing/chewing eliminates monoclinic phase entirely across all 3Y/4Y/5Y-TZP — the reverse of LTD degradation
2. Thermocycling causes slight monoclinic phase increase (consistent with LTD); mechanical processing does not trigger LTD
3. Preferred tetragonal phase is yttria-content-dependent: t for 3 mol%, t″ for 5 mol%
4. Rietveld refinement resolves all five phases (m, t, t′, t″, c); Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya misses the intermediate reduction (A→B) and shows absolute monoclinic fractions up to 3× lower than Rietveld
5. Surface roughness (Sa) increases with grinding and decreases with polishing; chewing simulation does not significantly change Sa

## Methodology

- **Materials**: 7 zirconia types (3Y, 4Y, 5Y-TZP; 2 manufacturers), sintered at 1450°C/120 min, 5-axis milled
- **Treatment positions (6)**:
  - A: Untreated reference
  - B: Grinding (Panther Flame 055M → 055XF at 15,000 rpm)
  - C: Grinding + polishing
  - D: Polishing (goat hair bur + diamond paste)
  - E: Grinding + polishing + chewing simulation + thermocycling
- **Surface roughness**: CLSM (Keyence VK-X1000), Sa per ISO 25178-2; Kruskal-Wallis + Dunn/Bonferroni statistics
- **XRD**: Bruker D8 Discover, CuKα, 25–75°, VÅNTEC-500 detector; (a) Garvie-Nicholson + Toraya (DIN EN ISO 13356); (b) Rietveld via TOPAS 4.2 (Rwp 1.5–2.2)
- **Sample size**: 2–3 specimens per yttria content; exploratory design

## Results

| Phase analysis | Grinding (B) | Polishing (C/D) | Chewing+TC (E) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monoclinic fraction | Halved (3Y-TZP) | Eliminated entirely | Eliminated + slight TC ↑ |
| Garvie-Nicholson detects? | No (missed) | Yes (disappearance only) | Yes |
| Rietveld detects? | Yes (two stages) | Yes | Yes |

- Sa: grinding ↑, polishing ↓, chewing neutral; positions C/D/E not significantly different
- Preferred tetragonal: t for 3Y-TZP, t″ for 5Y-PSZ; manufacturer differences within measurement accuracy
- Thermocycling-induced monoclinic increase: slight, consistent with LTD literature
