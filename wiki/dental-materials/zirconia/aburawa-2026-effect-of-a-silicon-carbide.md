## One-line Summary
In vitro pilot (n=30 4Y-PSZ specimens, 5,000 thermocycles): APA (37.0 MPa) significantly outperformed SiC bur alone (20.8 MPa) and APA + SiC bur (24.9 MPa); SiC bur cannot be recommended as an alternative or adjunct to APA for zirconia cementation.

## 한줄요약
인비트로 파일럿 (n=30, 4Y-PSZ, 5,000회 열순환): 공기분사연마 (Airborne Particle Abrasion, APA, 37.0 MPa)가 탄화규소 버(SiC bur, 20.8 MPa)와 APA+SiC(24.9 MPa)보다 유의하게 높은 전단결합강도(Shear Bond Strength, SBS); SiC 버는 대안·보조수단으로 권장 불가.

## Summary
The Cimara ceramic repair system (VOCO) includes a silicon carbide grinding bur, and an earlier study showed its complete system produced unexpectedly high SBS. This in vitro pilot isolated the SiC bur contribution vs established APA for primary zirconia cementation. After 5,000 thermocycles, APA (37.0 MPa) was significantly superior to SiC bur (20.8 MPa, p<0.001) and sequential APA + SiC bur (24.9 MPa, p<0.001). Adding SiC bur after APA worsened bond strength vs APA alone, possibly by disrupting the surface topography created by APA. The study used 4Y-PSZ clinically relevant monolithic zirconia with PANAVIA V5 (MDP-containing) cement.

## Key Contributions
- Negative result: SiC bur does not provide benefit vs APA for 4Y-PSZ zirconia cementation after aging
- Shows that adding SiC bur to APA actually reduces SBS (24.9 vs 37.0 MPa) — sequential combination counterproductive
- Uses clinical-grade multilayer 4Y-PSZ with standard MDP-containing system (PANAVIA V5)
- Large effect size (η²=0.762) despite small pilot study confirms robust APA superiority
- Consistent with prior data: Libecki et al. showed SiC group declined to near zero after 37,500 thermocycles
- Key limitation: no untreated control; cannot assess SiC vs no treatment

## Methodology
- Design: In vitro pilot, 3-group
- Zirconia: Copra Supreme Symphony 4Y-PSZ (Whitepeaks); milled + sintered 1630°C/120 min
- n=30 (10/group); sample size: SD=7 MPa, Δ=10 MPa, 80% power, α=0.05
- Groups: APA (110 μm Al₂O₃, 2 bar, 10 mm, 10 s) | SiC bur (Cimara REF 1201, 7,000 rpm, dry, 10 strokes) | APA + SiC bur
- Cementation: Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus + PANAVIA V5 (MDP)
- Configuration: zirconia–cement–zirconia symmetric; 5,000 thermocycles (5-55°C)
- SBS: Instron 4465, 1 mm/min; failure mode: digital microscopy ×35

## Results
| Group | Mean SBS (MPa) | vs APA |
|---|---|---|
| APA | 37.00 | — |
| APA + SiC bur | 24.86 | p<0.001 |
| SiC bur | 20.77 | p<0.001 |

- SiC bur vs APA + SiC bur: p>0.05 (ns)
- Effect size: η²=0.762, ω²=0.737 (large)
- All groups: mixed failure modes (10-90% residual cement); no macroscopic zirconia fracture
