## Three-line Summary
In vitro shear bond strength study (Indian J Dent Res 2017) evaluated 4 cleaning protocols (air abrasion, Ivoclean, water, phosphoric acid) on saliva-contaminated zirconia specimens bonded with two resin cements (conventional vs self-adhesive MDP).

Air abrasion yielded the highest SBS and Ivoclean outperformed water rinsing; the self-adhesive MDP-containing cement exceeded conventional resin cement across all four cleaning conditions.

Optimal zirconia cementation after intraoral try-in involves air abrasion decontamination combined with a self-adhesive MDP cement, a protocol supported by both mechanical and chemical cleaning evidence.

## Summary
In vitro comparative evaluation of four cleaning protocols on saliva-contaminated zirconia bonded with two cement types. Confirms that mechanical decontamination (air abrasion) outperforms chemical methods alone, and that self-adhesive MDP-containing cement provides higher bond strength regardless of cleaning protocol.

## Key Contributions
- Air abrasion: most effective mechanical decontamination
- Ivoclean: chemical superiority over water cleaning
- MDP-containing self-adhesive cement: higher SBS across all cleaning groups vs conventional cement
- Clinical protocol: air abrasion + self-adhesive MDP cement = optimal combination
