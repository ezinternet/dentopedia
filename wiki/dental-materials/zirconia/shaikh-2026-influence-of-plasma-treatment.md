## One-line Summary
Systematic review and meta-analysis of 14 in vitro studies (Jan 2026): plasma surface treatment significantly improves zirconia shear bond strength vs untreated (SMD 3.04; 95% CI 1.76-4.32) and is comparable to sandblasting; combined CAP + 10-MDP primer achieves best results.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (14개 연구, 검색일 2026-01): 플라즈마 처리가 지르코니아 전단결합강도를 미처리 대비 유의하게 향상(SMD 3.04; 95% CI 1.76-4.32); 공기분사연마 (Airborne Particle Abrasion, APA)와 동등; 냉온 대기압 플라즈마 (Cold Atmospheric Plasma, CAP) + 10-MDP 프라이머 병용이 최고 효과.

## Summary
Plasma treatment of zirconia surfaces modifies the outermost atomic layer, increasing surface energy and wettability without bulk structural changes. This SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD42025640563) searched 5 databases through January 2026 and included 14 in vitro studies (2016-2024). Meta-analysis showed plasma significantly improves shear bond strength vs untreated zirconia (SMD 3.04; 95% CI 1.76-4.32; I²=89%; p<0.00001). Plasma vs sandblasting was not significant in primary analysis (SMD 0.89, ns) but reached significance in sensitivity analysis excluding one outlier (SMD 1.49, p=0.01). Combined CAP + 10-MDP primer produced the best results across multiple studies, and one long-term study (Bunz 2021) showed plasma-associated protocol had the best 250-day aging durability.

## Key Contributions
- First PROSPERO-registered SR+MA quantifying plasma treatment for zirconia bonding (14 studies, 2016-2024, 10 countries)
- Confirms plasma ≈ APA for shear bond strength; plasma does not damage zirconia microstructure or induce phase transformation
- Identifies CAP + 10-MDP primer as synergistic protocol with superior immediate and aged bond strength
- Documents that combination protocols (plasma + APA) can outperform either modality alone for long-term durability (Bunz 2021: 250 days/37,500 cycles)
- Highlights outlier: Kim 2024 showed plasma alone reduced SBS in 3Y-TZP but improved it in (Y,Nb)-TZP — zirconia composition matters
- High heterogeneity (I²=89-90%) reflects lack of standardized plasma protocols — key gap identified

## Methodology
- Design: SR+MA, PRISMA 2020, QUIN risk-of-bias tool; 5 databases + grey literature
- 14 in vitro studies; 2016-2024; countries: Japan (5), China, Iran, Brazil, Egypt, Taiwan, Germany, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Finland
- Plasma types: CAP, APPJ, atmospheric low-temperature plasma, oxygen/argon/nitrogen/multi-gas plasma
- Meta-analysis: RevMan 5.4, random-effects model, SMD + 95% CI
- Risk of bias: majority low-risk by QUIN; 5 studies moderate (incomplete reporting of blinding/sample size justification)

## Results
| Comparison | n studies | Total n/group | SMD | 95% CI | p | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plasma vs untreated | 9 | 130 | 3.04 | 1.76-4.32 | <0.00001 | 89% |
| Plasma vs sandblasting (primary) | 6 | 80 | 0.89 | −0.41 to 2.19 | 0.18 | 90% |
| Plasma vs sandblasting (sensitivity) | 5 | — | 1.49 | 0.34-2.63 | 0.01 | 87% |

Key individual study findings:
- Bunz 2021: Plasma + APA + universal adhesive = best long-term durability (250 days/37,500 cycles)
- Ye 2022: CAP + MDP primer → highest SBS for high-translucency zirconia (immediate and aged)
- Kim 2024 (outlier): plasma alone ↓ SBS in 3Y-TZP; ↑ SBS in (Y,Nb)-TZP when combined with APA
- Liu 2016: 5-min argon-O₂ plasma increased SBS by 88.6% vs untreated
