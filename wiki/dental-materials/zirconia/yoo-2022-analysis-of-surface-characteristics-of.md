## Three-line Summary

A chairside (Y, Nb)-TZP fully-sintered zirconia block showed significantly higher surface roughness and monoclinic phase content than conventional 3Y-TZP in as-delivered and ground states, but polishing with a conventional 2-step zirconia kit reduced roughness to equivalent levels. Phase transformation occurred at every surface treatment step in the chairside material, absent in lab-side 3Y-TZP, yet polishing significantly reduced the monoclinic phase content. Conventional sequential polishing with diamond bur then 2-step polisher is recommended before delivering (Y, Nb)-TZP restorations.

## 세줄요약

(Y, Nb)-TZP 완전소결 체어사이드 블록은 연삭 후 표면조도(Ra)와 단사정(monoclinic) 상 함량이 기존 3Y-TZP보다 유의하게 높았으나, 2단계 지르코니아 연마킷 적용 후 양 군 간 조도 차이가 소실되었다. 체어사이드 군에서는 연삭 단계마다 상전이(tetragonal→monoclinic)가 관찰된 반면 랩사이드 군은 나타나지 않았으며, 연마 후 M상 함량이 유의하게 감소하였다. 임상 적용 시 고속 버 삭제 후 반드시 순차 연마를 시행할 것을 권고한다.

## Summary

This in vitro study compared surface roughness (Ra) and phase transformation behavior of a chairside (CS) fully-sintered (Y, Nb)-TZP zirconia block (Perfit-FS, Vatech MCIS) against a conventional lab-side (LS) 3Y-TZP (Perfit-HT) through a sequential 5-step grinding-and-polishing protocol. Forty monolithic anterior crowns (n=20/group) were analyzed after each treatment step by confocal laser scanning microscopy (CLSM), X-ray diffraction (XRD), and FE-SEM.

The CS group exhibited significantly higher Ra and monoclinic (M) phase content in as-delivered and ground states due to the machining characteristics of the fully-sintered niobium-containing block. After completing the 2-step polishing sequence (J1/J2), Ra values became comparable to the polished LS group. While the LS group showed no detectable phase transformation at any step, the CS group showed M phase at every step — including the as-delivered state — with significant reduction after polishing (P < .001).

The study concludes that delivering (Y, Nb)-TZP restorations in as-milled or ground states is clinically unacceptable. Full sequential polishing restores surface quality to the clinically accepted standard set by conventional 3Y-TZP.

## Key Contributions

1. **First surface characterization of (Y, Nb)-TZP fully-sintered chairside blocks** — filling a documentation gap for this material (Perfit-FS, Vatech MCIS)
2. **Phase transformation at every step in CS group** — including as-delivered milled state, absent in 3Y-TZP; demonstrates inherent metastability of this formulation under mechanical stress
3. **Polishing normalizes Ra** — conventional 2-step kit achieves Ra equivalent to polished 3Y-TZP, validating the same clinical protocol
4. **Clinical recommendation** — delivering without full polishing is not recommended; grain exposure and elevated Ra present until J2 step

## Methodology

- **Groups**: CS = (Y, Nb)-TZP fully-sintered block (Perfit-FS), 4-axis mill; LS = 3Y-TZP (Perfit-HT), 5-axis mill + post-milling sintering
- **Treatment steps**: AD (as-delivered) → blue diamond bur → red bur → yellow bur → J1 polisher → J2 polisher
- **Measurements per step**:
  - CLSM Ra (ISO 4287, Gaussian filter 25 µm, 3 locations × 3 scans)
  - XRD monoclinic phase Vm (Bruker D8, 27–32°, Toraya method)
  - FE-SEM representative specimens (×1,000 and ×10,000)
- **Statistics**: RM-ANOVA (Greenhouse-Geisser), Dunnett's post-hoc, independent t-test; n=20/group

## Results

| State | CS Ra (µm) | LS Ra (µm) | CS Vm (M%) | LS Vm (M%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| As-delivered (AD) | Higher* | Lower* | Detectable | None |
| Ground (blue/red/yellow) | Higher* | Lower* | Present each step | None |
| Polished (J1/J2) | Comparable | Comparable | Significantly reduced | None |

\* P < .05 between groups at AD and ground states; no significant difference after polishing

- RM-ANOVA for Ra: F=795.22, df=3.56, P < .001
- RM-ANOVA for Vm: F=282.75, df=5, P < .001
- FE-SEM: 5–10 µm grain exposure in CS group through grinding; markedly less after J2
