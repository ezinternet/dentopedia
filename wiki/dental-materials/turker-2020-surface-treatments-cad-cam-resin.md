## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (J Adv Prosthodont 2020) comparing 5 surface treatments on two polymer-containing CAD/CAM materials (Lava Ultimate resin-ceramic; Vita Enamic polymer-infiltrated ceramic) bonded with two dual-cure resin cements (TheraCem, RelyX U200).

CoJet tribochemical silica coating + Z-Prime primer produced the highest shear bond strength universally; HF etching was effective only for Vita Enamic (ceramic-rich); laser offered no consistent advantage.

Material-specific surface treatment selection is essential — HF etching is contraindicated for Lava Ultimate (resin-rich) and MDP primer (Z-Prime) is required for polymer-containing CAD/CAM materials.

## 세줄요약
인비트로(J Adv Prosthodont 2020): 두 가지 폴리머 함유 CAD/CAM 수복재(Lava Ultimate 레진-세라믹; Vita Enamic 폴리머 침윤형 세라믹) × 5가지 표면처리 × 2종 이중경화 시멘트(TheraCem, RelyX U200) 전단접착강도(SBS) 비교.

CoJet 마찰화학 실리카 코팅+Z-Prime 프라이머가 전 조건에서 최고 SBS; HF 산부식은 Vita Enamic(세라믹 다량)에서만 효과적; 레이저는 일관된 이점 없음.

재료별 표면처리 선택이 필수 — HF 산부식은 Lava Ultimate(레진 풍부)에 금기이며, 폴리머 함유 CAD/CAM 수복재에는 MDP 프라이머(Z-Prime) 적용 필요.

## Summary
In vitro comparison of five surface treatments on two polymer-containing CAD/CAM restorative materials (Lava Ultimate: resin-ceramic; Vita Enamic: polymer-infiltrated ceramic) bonded with two dual-cure resin cements (TheraCem, RelyX U200). Material-specific surface treatment protocols required — HF etching only appropriate for materials with ceramic content.

## Key Contributions
- CoJet tribochemical silica coating + Z-Prime: universally highest SBS regardless of material
- HF acid: effective only for Vita Enamic (ceramic-rich); ineffective/damaging for Lava Ultimate
- Z-Prime (MDP primer): essential chemical bonding component for resin-based CAD/CAM materials
- Laser: no consistent advantage
