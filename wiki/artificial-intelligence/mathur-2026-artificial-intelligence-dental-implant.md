## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (10 systematic reviews from 261 records, 5 databases inception–April 2025, AMSTAR-2 appraised) of AI models for dental implant type identification, osseointegration prediction, and treatment-success forecasting on periapical/CBCT/panoramic radiographs; abstract-only.

CNN-based AI models exceeded 90% accuracy for implant treatment and success prediction, outperforming other AI architectures; only 5/10 reviews addressed osseointegration prediction; deep learning consistently outperformed traditional machine learning; AMSTAR-2 confidence was moderate-to-high.

AI (especially deep learning/CNN) shows strong potential for implant identification, planning, and success prediction, but limited datasets, lack of multi-implant analyses, and standardization gaps remain barriers to clinical adoption; abstract-only — full text not retrieved.

## Summary

This umbrella review (systematic review of systematic reviews) consolidated evidence from 10 systematic reviews (out of 261 records screened across Science Direct, PubMed-MEDLINE, Scopus, and Embase, from database inception to April 2025) evaluating artificial intelligence (AI) models applied to periapical, CBCT, or panoramic radiographs of dental-implant-eligible patients. Included reviews assessed AI accuracy for three tasks: determining appropriate implant type, predicting osseointegration, and forecasting treatment success. An overlap analysis of primary studies across the included reviews was performed to reduce double-counting, and AI intervention characteristics were analyzed separately for their influence on treatment-planning accuracy. Methodological quality of the 10 contributing reviews was appraised with AMSTAR 2. Convolutional neural networks (CNN) were the standout performer, exceeding 90% accuracy for implant treatment and success prediction and outperforming other AI model types; deep learning models generally outperformed traditional machine learning approaches. Only 5 of the 10 reviews addressed AI-driven osseointegration prediction, marking it as an emerging rather than mature application. AMSTAR-2 appraisal indicated moderate-to-high overall confidence in the review base. The authors conclude AI — especially deep learning — has real potential to enhance implant identification, planning, and success prediction, but limited datasets, a lack of studies analyzing multiple implants, and unresolved standardization issues remain key barriers before broader clinical adoption. **This page is abstract-only** — full text was not retrieved (no PMC ID; Elsevier subscription journal), so per-review breakdowns, exact overlap metrics, and a PRISMA flow diagram are not available here.

## Key Contributions

- Focuses specifically on AI performance across three concrete implant-dentistry decision points (type identification, osseointegration prediction, success/treatment-outcome forecasting), narrower in scope than broader prosthodontics-wide umbrella reviews.
- Performs a primary-study overlap analysis across the 10 contributing reviews to reduce evidence double-counting — a meta-epidemiological safeguard increasingly standard in umbrella reviews.
- Separately characterizes AI model type (CNN vs other architectures; deep learning vs traditional machine learning) as a moderator of treatment-planning accuracy, rather than reporting a single pooled figure.
- Applies AMSTAR 2 to grade the 10 included reviews, reporting moderate-to-high overall confidence — a comparatively more favorable quality profile than some sibling AI umbrella reviews in this wiki's `artificial-intelligence` category.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Umbrella review (overview of systematic reviews, with or without meta-analysis).
- **Databases**: Science Direct, PubMed-MEDLINE, Scopus, Embase; searched from inception to April 2025.
- **Population/scope**: Studies evaluating AI models on periapical, CBCT, or panoramic radiographs of patients eligible for dental implants.
- **Outcomes assessed**: (1) accuracy of AI-determined implant type, (2) AI-predicted osseointegration, (3) AI-forecasted treatment success.
- **Selection**: 261 records identified → 10 systematic reviews met inclusion criteria.
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR 2.
- **Overlap handling**: Overlap analysis performed on primary studies across the 10 included reviews (method/metric not specified in the abstract).
- **Note**: Abstract-only ingestion — search strategy detail, PRISMA numbers at each stage, and per-review AMSTAR-2 scores are unavailable pending full-text access.
