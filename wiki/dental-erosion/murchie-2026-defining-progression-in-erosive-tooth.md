## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA 2020, qualitative critical synthesis, no meta-analysis; MEDLINE/Embase/Scopus/WoS to January 2026, updated August 2026; 1,074 records, 5 longitudinal cohort studies, n = 24-517, follow-up 1-25 years) asking whether intraoral scanner (IOS) quantification and clinical indices (BEWE, Smith and Knight) measure the same thing as erosive tooth wear (ETW) "progression".

IOS detected measurable surface loss (about 10-150 um at roughly 12 months; about 137 um at 10 years; over 300-400 um at 25 years) in short intervals where categorical indices often showed no change; agreement with BEWE was weak to moderate (one study: volumetric loss vs BEWE-defined progression AUC 0.80, -1.22 mm3 cut-off, 79% sensitivity / 61% specificity), and progression thresholds ranged from more than 30 um to 200 um or more, or were undefined.

IOS and ordinal indices are not interchangeable progression measures, no validated link between micron-level loss and clinically meaningful progression exists, and no included study validated IOS against a high-resolution reference (e.g. laser profilometry); use indices for screening and IOS selectively as an adjunct in high-risk patients; evidence base is small (5 studies) and heterogeneous.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (PRISMA 2020, 정성적 비판적 종합·메타분석 없음; 2026년 1월까지 검색 후 8월 갱신; 1,074건 중 종단 코호트 5편, n = 24-517, 추적 1-25년) — 구강내 스캐너 (Intraoral Scanner, IOS) 정량 측정과 임상 지수 (BEWE, Smith and Knight)가 치아침식 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW) "진행"을 같은 의미로 측정하는지 평가.

IOS는 범주형 지수가 변화를 못 잡는 짧은 기간에도 표면 손실을 검출했다 (약 12개월 10-150 um, 10년 약 137 um, 25년 300-400 um 초과); BEWE와의 일치도는 약-중등도 (한 연구에서 부피 손실 vs BEWE 진행 AUC 0.80, 기준 -1.22 mm3, 민감도 79%/특이도 61%)이고 진행 기준값은 30 um 초과부터 200 um 이상까지 제각각이거나 미정의.

IOS와 서열 지수는 진행의 호환 척도가 아니며, 마이크론 단위 손실을 임상적으로 의미 있는 진행과 연결하는 검증된 기준이 없고 고해상도 기준법 (레이저 프로파일로메트리 등)에 대한 IOS 검증도 포함 연구 전무; 지수는 선별, IOS는 고위험 환자에서 선택적 보조로 쓰는 것이 현실적이나 근거는 5편·이질적.

## Summary

This systematic review asks how ETW progression is defined across quantitative IOS monitoring and conventional ordinal indices. Its answer is that the two measure different constructs: IOS yields continuous dimensional loss (um or mm3), BEWE and Smith and Knight yield ordinal severity categories. Differences in detection are therefore partly construct differences, not only sensitivity differences.

## Key Contributions

- Construct-mismatch framing: IOS and indices are not interchangeable progression measures.
- No validated framework links micron-level loss to clinical relevance; thresholds in index systems are consensus-based and also unvalidated against quantified loss.
- Pragmatic use: indices for screening and risk stratification; IOS selectively (high-risk patients, evaluating preventive interventions, patient communication).

## Methodology

PRISMA 2020 SR, four databases plus grey literature to January 2026 (update August 2026); 5 longitudinal cohort studies of 1,074 records; NOS appraisal; qualitative synthesis only; English-language restriction. Full text read.

## Results

- IOS loss: about 10-150 um over about 12 months; about 137 um at 10 years; over 300-400 um at 25 years.
- At up to 12 months categorical indices often showed no change where IOS did.
- IOS-vs-BEWE agreement weak to moderate; volumetric loss discriminated BEWE progression with AUC 0.80 (-1.22 mm3: sensitivity 79%, specificity 61%), with BEWE as the imperfect reference.
- Thresholds: more than 30 um to 200 um or more, or none.
- No study validated IOS against laser profilometry; enamel vs dentine rarely separated; index inter-examiner kappa 0.65-0.87.
- Limits: 5 studies, heterogeneous workflows, no publication-bias assessment.
