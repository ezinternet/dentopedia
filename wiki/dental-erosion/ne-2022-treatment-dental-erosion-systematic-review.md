## Three-line Summary

Qualitative systematic review (PRISMA, OSF-registered; 6 databases + grey literature; no meta-analysis) of in vitro studies on human permanent teeth treated with topical anti-erosion agents vs an artificial-saliva comparator; 522 records screened, only 4 studies included (n = 35, 150, 40, 36 specimens), 3 at low and 1 at high risk of bias.

Two fluoride-toothpaste studies showed no clear anti-erosion benefit over artificial saliva (in the Brazilian study, Sensodyne Pronamel and Elmex Erosion Protection had the lowest surface loss but did not differ from the control, p > 0.05), whereas the two CPP-ACP (Tooth Mousse) studies reported benefit: microhardness +13.27% vs +2.53% with artificial saliva, and significantly reduced enamel wear (p < 0.001).

Evidence is four heterogeneous laboratory studies without a water-only negative control and without acquired pellicle, so the CPP-ACP signal is hypothesis-level and the 'fluoride does not work' reading should not be generalized; it does not override the higher-level umbrella review favoring stannous fluoride dentifrices.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR; 메타분석 없음): 인간 영구치에 국소 항침식제를 적용하고 인공타액 (artificial saliva) 대조군과 비교한 실험실 연구 (in vitro) 4편만 포함 (표본 n = 35, 150, 40, 36; 522편 검색), 비뚤림 위험 (risk of bias) 3편 낮음·1편 높음.

불소 치약 2편은 인공타액 대비 뚜렷한 항침식 효과가 없었고 (브라질 연구에서 Sensodyne Pronamel과 Elmex Erosion Protection이 표면 손실 최저였으나 대조군과 차이 없음, p > 0.05), 카제인 포스포펩타이드-비정질 인산칼슘 (Casein Phosphopeptide-Amorphous Calcium Phosphate, CPP-ACP) 2편은 미세경도 +13.27% (인공타액 +2.53%), 법랑질 마모 유의 감소 (p < 0.001)를 보고.

포함 연구가 4편이고 방법이 이질적이며 물만 쓴 음성대조군과 획득피막 (acquired pellicle)이 없어 CPP-ACP 신호는 가설 수준이고, '불소는 효과 없음'으로 일반화하면 안 된다. 스태너스불화물 치약을 우위로 본 상위 수준 우산 리뷰 (umbrella review)를 뒤집지 못한다.

## Summary

Source: PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.13864)), full text via PMC. This PRISMA systematic review (PeerJ 2022) asked which topical treatments best remineralize or protect eroded human enamel. After screening 522 records, four studies fulfilled the criteria, and all four are laboratory (in vitro) studies on extracted human teeth, so it is an in vitro-only evidence base despite the clinical question; no clinical trials were included. No meta-analysis was done. Two studies tested commercial anti-erosion toothpastes and two tested CPP-ACP tooth mousse, each against artificial saliva.

## Key Contributions

- Qualitative synthesis restricted to human-enamel in vitro studies with an artificial-saliva comparator.
- CPP-ACP was the only agent with significant microhardness gain and wear reduction; fluoride toothpastes showed no clear benefit over control.
- Toothpaste performance tracked abrasivity, calcium/phosphate content and deposit formation rather than fluoride alone.

## Methodology

- PICO: eroded human permanent teeth; any topical agent; artificial-saliva comparator; enamel outcome. Bovine, dentine-only and primary teeth excluded.
- PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane, Scopus, Lilacs plus Google Scholar and OpenGrey; two independent reviewers.
- Cochrane risk-of-bias tool adapted for in vitro work (key domains: allocation concealment, participant blinding, outcome blinding).
- Flow: 522 records, 305 duplicates, 217 screened, 13 full-text, 4 included.

## Results

| Study (country) | n | Outcome | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Czech Republic | 35 | microhardness | Enamel Care formed compact deposit layer; Elmex Erosion and SensiShield abrasive, low anti-erosion action |
| Brazil | 150 | microhardness | Pronamel and Elmex Erosion lowest surface loss, not different from control (p > 0.05) |
| Thailand | 40 | microhardness | CPP-ACP +13.27% vs +2.53% artificial saliva |
| United Kingdom | 36 | profilometry | Tooth Mousse significantly reduced enamel wear (p < 0.001) |

Risk of bias: 3 low, 1 high (lack of quantitative data). Limitations: 4 heterogeneous studies, no water-only negative control, no acquired pellicle, high heterogeneity of erosive protocols. The Results text also names APF gel among best-performing topical agents although APF is not in the Table 1 agent list, which conflicts with the abstract's statement that CPP-ACP was the only effective product.
