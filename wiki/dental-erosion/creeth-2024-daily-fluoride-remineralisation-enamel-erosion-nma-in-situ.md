## Three-line Summary

Network meta-analysis (NMA) of 14 single-protocol, crossover in situ studies (22 treatments; subject-level data; mixed model; healthy adults wearing palatal appliances with eroded bovine enamel, single product use, 4 h intra-oral remineralisation, then a second acid challenge; outcomes surface microhardness recovery, SMHR, and acid resistance ratio, ARR). All 14 studies were sponsored and funded by Haleon (formerly GSK Consumer Healthcare), and three of five authors are affiliated with Haleon; the set is the sponsor's own complete in situ series, not a systematic review.

Fluoride showed a clear dose-response: 1150 ppm F as NaF vs fluoride-free placebo gave SMHR +8.8% (SE 0.6%; 33.0% vs 24.2%) and ARR +0.27 (SE 0.03; 0.43 vs 0.15) across 9 mutual studies (both p < 0.001); remineralisation rose linearly (+0.7% SMHR per 100 ppm F, 95% CI 0.6-0.9) whereas acid resistance plateaued near 1000 ppm F; a fluoride mouthwash after toothpaste added SMHR +2.4% (SE 1.1%, p = 0.043, 3 studies) but ARR +0.08 (p = 0.164, not significant); monofluorophosphate (MFP) toothpaste was inferior to free-fluoride NaF (SMHR -8.9%, p < 0.001).

Polyvalent metal ions (stannous, zinc) and short-chain polyphosphates sharply reduced fluoride-induced remineralisation (SnF2-zinc paste SMHR 8.8% lower than the NaF benchmark, p = 0.001, ARR equivalent), and the stannous-zinc system gave no net gain in overall protection (RER) over well-formulated NaF; findings are single-sponsor, single-model, single-exposure surface-hardness data and do not establish clinical erosive tooth wear (ETW) outcomes.

## 세줄요약

14개 단일 프로토콜 교차설계 in situ 연구 (22개 처치, 대상자 수준 데이터, 혼합모형; 구개 장치에 부착한 침식 소 법랑질을 건강한 성인이 착용, 제품 1회 사용 후 4시간 구강내 재광화, 이후 2차 산 공격; 결과변수 표면미세경도 회복률 SMHR, 산저항비 ARR)의 네트워크 메타분석(NMA)이다. 14편 전부 Haleon (전 GSK Consumer Healthcare)이 후원·재정 지원했고 저자 5명 중 3명이 Haleon 소속이며, 이 연구 세트는 후원사 자체의 in situ 시리즈 전체로 체계적 문헌고찰이 아니다.

불소는 명확한 용량-반응을 보였다: 1150 ppm F (NaF) 대 무불소 위약은 SMHR +8.8% (SE 0.6%; 33.0% 대 24.2%), ARR +0.27 (SE 0.03; 0.43 대 0.15) (9편 공통, 모두 p < 0.001); 재광화는 100 ppm F당 SMHR +0.7% (95% CI 0.6-0.9)로 선형 증가한 반면 산저항은 약 1000 ppm F에서 정체; 불소 가글은 치약 후 추가 시 SMHR +2.4% (SE 1.1%, p = 0.043, 3편) 증가했으나 ARR +0.08은 유의하지 않았고 (p = 0.164), 모노플루오로포스페이트 (MFP) 치약은 유리불소 NaF보다 열등했다 (SMHR -8.9%, p < 0.001).

다가 금속이온 (주석, 아연)과 단쇄 폴리인산은 불소 유도 재광화를 크게 저해했고 (SnF2-아연 치약은 NaF 기준 대비 SMHR 8.8% 낮음, p = 0.001; ARR은 동등), 주석-아연 시스템은 잘 설계된 NaF 대비 전체 보호효과 (RER)에서 순이득이 없었다; 단일 후원사·단일 모델·1회 노출 표면경도 데이터이므로 임상 침식성 치아마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW) 결과를 입증하지 않는다.

## Summary

Daily-use fluoride products are first-line protection against dietary-acid enamel wear, but the effects of fluoride concentration, salt, vehicle and co-formulated ingredients are poorly separated in single studies. This network meta-analysis pools subject-level data from 14 crossover in situ studies run on one protocol (22 treatments), measuring remineralisation (surface microhardness recovery, SMHR) after a single treatment and 4 h of intra-oral remineralisation, and protection against a second acid challenge (acid resistance ratio, ARR).

**Funding and independence.** All 14 studies were sponsored and funded by Haleon (formerly GSK Consumer Healthcare), three of five authors are Haleon employees by listed affiliation (the other two are at Indiana University, where 13 studies ran), and most tested the sponsor's marketed products. This is an industry-funded, single-protocol analysis, not an independent systematic review. No separate conflict-of-interest statement appeared in the retrieved text.

Main findings: fluoride acts dose-dependently; remineralisation is linear to at least 1426 ppm F whereas acid resistance plateaus near 1000 ppm F. MFP toothpaste underperforms free-fluoride NaF in this clean-enamel model, a fluoride rinse after brushing adds a modest remineralisation gain, and stannous, zinc and short-chain polyphosphates suppress remineralisation. For stannous-zinc toothpaste, strong acid resistance did not offset reduced remineralisation, so net protection (RER) was not better than NaF.

## Key Contributions

- Whole-series pooling of a single in situ erosion protocol, with a reproducibility anchor (NaF 1150 ppm vs placebo in 9 studies; SMHR increment 6-12%).
- Distinguishes remineralisation (linear in F) from acid resistance (plateau about 1000 ppm F).
- Ingredient-level interference estimates for metal ions, polyphosphates and SLS.

## Methodology

- Design: network meta-analysis of subject-level data, mixed model with random effects for study, period and subject; 14 in situ crossover studies (13 Indiana University, 1 UK); healthy adults; bovine enamel in palatal appliances; single treatment, 4 h remineralisation, second acid challenge.
- Outcomes: SMHR, ARR, RER (Knoop microhardness).
- Not a systematic review; no risk-of-bias tool; all studies from one sponsor.

## Results

- 1150 ppm F as NaF vs placebo: SMHR +8.8% (SE 0.6%), ARR +0.27 (SE 0.03), RER +36.2% (SE 3.1), all p < 0.001.
- SMHR +0.7% per 100 ppm F (95% CI 0.6-0.9; linear, p = 0.427 for non-linearity); ARR plateau about 1007 ppm F (p = 0.029).
- MFP/SLS vs NaF non-SLS at 1450 ppm: SMHR -8.9% (p < 0.001); ARR not significantly different.
- Fluoride mouthwash after toothpaste: SMHR +2.4% (p = 0.043), ARR +0.08 (p = 0.164). Mouthwash-only ranked above toothpaste-only.
- Short-chain polyphosphates: SMHR 9.0-12.1% lower than NaF non-SLS (p < 0.001).
- SnF2-zinc: SMHR 8.8% lower (p = 0.001), ARR equivalent (p = 0.831); no net RER advantage per the authors.
- SLS-containing paste: modest SMHR decrement (3.3%, p = 0.006 in one comparison; direct SLS contrast underpowered).

## Clinical Insights

- The data support about 1000 ppm F or more and free-fluoride salts for erosive-lesion protection in this model; they do not show that higher concentrations add acid resistance beyond about 1000 ppm F.
- A fluoride rinse after brushing may add a small remineralisation benefit; the demineralisation-resistance gain was not significant.
- Treat the stannous and polyphosphate findings as protocol-specific: the authors note profilometry-based in situ studies where stannous outperformed NaF. Surface microhardness after a single use is a surrogate, and no clinical ETW endpoint was measured.
- Industry sponsorship and a single-protocol set limit generalisability and independence.
