## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis of observational studies (87 publications / 71 studies; searched Feb 2023; PROSPERO CRD4202339776) mapping 80 anamnestic risk factors for erosive tooth wear in permanent dentition.

Pooled random-effects ORs were significant for acidic foods (2.40), regurgitation (2.27), digestive disorders (1.81), nutritional supplements (1.73), carbonated drinks (1.43), male gender (1.30) and seasoning sauces (1.28); most studies had low risk of bias.

Screen primarily for GERD symptoms and dietary factors; validated questionnaires are needed. Abstract-level only.

## 세줄요약

관찰연구 87편(연구 71건)을 대상으로 한 체계적 고찰 및 메타분석으로, 영구치열의 침식성 치아마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW) 문진 위험인자 80개를 열 가지 영역으로 지도화했다.

합산 OR은 산성 식품 2.40, 역류 2.27, 소화기 질환 1.81, 영양보충제 1.73, 탄산음료 1.43, 남성 1.30, 양념 소스 1.28로 유의했고 대부분 낮은 편향위험이었다.

위식도역류질환 (Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease, GERD) 증상과 식이요인을 우선 선별하라는 것이 저자 권고이며, 초록 수준 정보만 확보했다.

## Summary

Source: PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jdent.2024.104962), PMID 38552999); J Dent 2024;144:104962. The review asks which anamnestic (history-based) factors are associated with erosive tooth wear (ETW) in permanent dentition. Eighty factors were grouped into ten domains (socio-demographics, socio-economics, general health, oral diseases, medication, oral hygiene, food, beverages, dietary habits, leisure-related). The authors conclude that clinicians should concentrate primarily on gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) symptoms and dietary factors when screening patients at risk.

## Key Contributions

- Quantifies, by meta-analysis, history-based ETW risk factors that earlier narrative work ([[dental-erosion/kanzow-2016-etiology-pathogenesis-dental-erosion]]) described qualitatively.
- Heatmap mapping of 80 factors across ten domains.
- Recommends validated questionnaires focused on the most important factors.

## Methodology

Observational studies reporting anamnestic risk factors for ETW; MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science plus manual search (Feb 2023); PROSPERO CRD4202339776. Quality/risk of bias with the modified Newcastle-Ottawa scale for cross-sectional studies; heatmap plus random-effects meta-analysis of ORs where possible.

## Results

87 publications (71 studies). Significant pooled associations:

| Factor | OR | 95% CI | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acidic foods | 2.40 | 1.44-4.00 | 0.033 |
| Regurgitation | 2.27 | 1.41-3.65 | 0.033 |
| Digestive disorders | 1.81 | 1.48-2.21 | <0.001 |
| Nutritional supplements | 1.73 | 1.28-2.35 | 0.019 |
| Carbonated drinks | 1.43 | 1.17-1.75 | 0.019 |
| Male gender | 1.30 | 1.16-1.44 | <0.001 |
| Seasoning sauces | 1.28 | 1.13-1.44 | 0.003 |

Most included studies had low risk of bias. Heterogeneity and per-factor study counts are not available from the abstract.
