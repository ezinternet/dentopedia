## Summary
Grazziotin et al. scanned the pulp chambers of 24 extracted molars with Primescan after standardized endodontic access preparations and compared the resulting 3D models to clinical microscope examination and CBCT imaging. For primary orifice identification (number and location), scanner agreement was non-significant against both comparators, indicating Primescan performs equivalently to the gold-standard modalities for routine orifice mapping. Diagnostic accuracy for chamber floor features — bur abrasion defects and anatomical structures such as isthmuses — reached 87.5% (95% CI: 67.6–97.3) versus the microscope. However, for calcified orifices, accuracy dropped to a moderate level: 79.2% (95% CI: 57.8–92.9) vs microscope and 70.8% (95% CI: 48.9–87.4) vs CBCT. The study establishes IOS as a novel endodontic documentation tool but highlights calcification as the key performance limitation.

## Key Contributions
- First demonstration of Primescan feasibility for endodontic access opening imaging — expands IOS indications beyond restorative
- Accurate orifice number/location detection vs both microscope and CBCT (the two current standards)
- High diagnostic accuracy for access defects (87.5% vs microscope) — supports intraoperative chamber floor documentation
- Moderate accuracy for calcified orifices — critical limitation; calcified cases still require CBCT supplementation
- Extends the evidence base for Primescan in narrow/deep intraoral cavity scanning alongside wildhagen-2026 (post space) and almalki-2024 (post length)

## Methodology
Comparative in vitro study. N=24 extracted molars; coronal substance loss and endodontic access preparations performed under standardized conditions. Primescan 3D scan of each access cavity. Comparators: clinical microscope (primary reference), CBCT. Outcome documentation: orifice number and location; chamber floor features (calcifications, bur-induced defects, isthmus, anatomical variations). Statistical analysis: agreement testing (non-significant = accurate); diagnostic accuracy (% agreement) with 95% CI.

## Results

- vs microscope: non-significant agreement → accurate
- vs CBCT: non-significant agreement → accurate

**Access defects and anatomical features:**
