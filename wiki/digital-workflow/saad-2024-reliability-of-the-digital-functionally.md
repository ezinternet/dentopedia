## Three-line Summary

A within-patient RCT (n=13, maxillary first premolar) compared monolithic CAD-CAM zirconia crowns adjusted using buccal interocclusal record alone (Group I) versus buccal plus lateral IOS-recorded interocclusal records via Patient Specific Motion (Group II). Group II showed significantly lower lateral excursion pressure (20.62 ± 3.38% vs 26.00 ± 4.95%, p<0.0001) and MI pressure (7.23 vs 8.08%, p=0.001), with smaller crown volume confirming effective pre-milling material removal. The digital functionally generated path technique is a reliable method to identify and correct eccentric occlusal interferences in CAD-CAM zirconia crowns before milling, reducing chairside adjustment and protecting zirconia surface integrity.

## Summary

This randomized clinical trial (NCT06272474) evaluated a novel digital workflow integrating lateral mandibular excursion recording via intraoral scanner (IOS) into CAD software to pre-emptively eliminate occlusal interferences in monolithic zirconia crowns before milling. Each of 13 patients received both a reference crown (Group I, adjusted for static occlusion only via buccal bite record) and a test crown (Group II, adjusted for both static and dynamic occlusion via buccal + lateral IOS records using the Exocad intersection cutting tool). Outcomes were measured with the Occlusense digital occlusal analyzer (60-micron pressure sensor). Group II crowns demonstrated significantly fewer lateral excursion interferences and reduced MI pressure, confirming the null hypothesis was refuted. The smaller crown volume in Group II reflected successful material removal during the virtual design phase, which is clinically preferable to post-milling diamond bur grinding — avoiding tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation that weakens zirconia. This study provides in vivo confirmation of findings from earlier in vitro digital FGP/PSM studies.

## Key Contributions

- First in vivo RCT validating IOS-based Patient Specific Motion (PSM)/digital FGP for pre-milling occlusal interference removal in monolithic zirconia crowns
- Lateral excursion pressure reduced by 20.7% (Group II vs Group I, p<0.0001) and MI pressure by 10.5% (p=0.001) with the new technique
- Crown volume statistically significantly smaller in Group II (157.63 vs 160.36 mm³, p=0.001) — demonstrates effective virtual material removal before milling rather than chairside grinding
- Occlusense validated as reliable digital occlusal analysis tool comparable to T-Scan for this application
- Workflow: IOS lateral excursion scan → Exocad intersection cutting → virtual interference removal → milling without intraoral occlusal adjustment

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Within-patient RCT; blind protocol (patients unaware of crown type); NCT06272474 |
| Sample | 13 participants (8F/5M, 18–25 y), maxillary first premolar, Class I, canine-guided occlusion |
| Group I (Reference) | Crown adjusted for static occlusion using buccal bite registration only (BBR) |
| Group II (Test) | Crown adjusted for MI + lateral excursion; lateral IOS recording (Medit i500 v2.2, working side, endpoint at canine contact) imported into Exocad v3.1 Rejeka; intersection cutting tool for overlap removal |
| Milling | Zenostar zirconia (Ivoclar Vivadent), Ceramill (Amann Girrbach); identical blank/machine/sintering for all |
| Cementation | Zinc oxide temporary cement; silicon putty index for standardized seating pressure; 5-minute set time; 2-minute rest between recordings |
| Outcome | Occlusense (Dr. Bausch, 60 μm sensor, 60 Hz): max pressure during lateral excursion (working side) and MI; crown volume via Meshmixer |
| Statistics | Shapiro-Wilk normality; paired t-test; Wilcoxon Sign Rank test; p ≤ 0.05; SPSS v23 |

## Results

| Outcome | Group I (buccal only) n=13 | Group II (buccal+lateral) n=13 | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max pressure, lateral excursion (%, mean ± SD) | 26.00 ± 4.95 | 20.62 ± 3.38 | <0.0001* |
| 95% CI | 23.01–28.99 | 18.57–22.66 | |
| Pressure at MI (%, mean ± SD) | 8.08 ± 1.50 | 7.23 ± 1.59 | 0.001* |
| Crown volume (mm³, mean ± SD) | 160.36 ± 15.94 | 157.63 ± 14.45 | 0.001* |

*Statistically significant, paired t-test, p ≤ 0.05.

Null hypothesis ("no significant difference between groups") was **refuted**.
