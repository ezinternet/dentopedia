## Three-line Summary
Clinical study (10 volunteers, 4 illuminance levels, 2 IOS): ambient lighting significantly affects both IOS scanning trueness and digital shade accuracy. Primescan performed best at 2500 lx/4100 K (standard dental operatory light); TRIOS 4 peaked at 1000 lx/5500 K. Practical takeaway: match the clinical lighting to the specific scanner's recommended color temperature.

## 세줄요약
임상연구(10명, 4개 조도 조건, 2 IOS): 주변 조명이 스캔 진도성 및 디지털 색조 정확도에 유의한 영향. 프라임스캔은 2500 lx/4100 K(치과 조명)에서 최적, TRIOS 4는 1000 lx/5500 K에서 최적. 임상 팁: 사용 스캐너의 색온도에 맞춰 조명 조건을 선택할 것.

## Summary
Guo et al. tested two IOS (Primescan and TRIOS 4 wireless) under four illuminance levels (0, 500, 1000, 2500 lx) matched to each scanner's native light source color temperature (Primescan: 4100 K warm white; TRIOS 4: 5500 K cool daylight) in 10 volunteers. Scanning trueness was quantified against stone models digitized with an InEos X5 reference scanner; digital shade was compared to visual consensus using Vita Classical and 3D-Master guides. Both scanning accuracy and shade matching were significantly affected by illuminance. Primescan achieved optimal trueness (100.78 µm) and shade accuracy (96.7%) at the highest tested illuminance (2500 lx), consistent with standard dental operatory lighting. TRIOS 4 optimized at 1000 lx but degraded significantly at 0 lx. Primescan showed no significant trueness difference across the illuminance range, suggesting greater robustness under low-light conditions.

## Key Contributions
- First systematic evaluation of IOS accuracy and shade matching across matched illuminance/color-temperature conditions in vivo
- Scanner-specific optimal lighting: Primescan → 2500 lx; TRIOS 4 → 1000 lx — different workflows require different environments
- Primescan more robust to darkness (0 lx) for shade determination; TRIOS 4 showed significant degradation
- Clinical guideline: standard dental operatory lighting (2000–3000 lx) benefits Primescan but may slightly degrade TRIOS 4
- Demonstrates that shade matching and scanning accuracy track together across lighting conditions

## Methodology
In vivo clinical study (randomized crossover). N=10 volunteers, maxillary arches. IOS: Primescan (Dentsply Sirona, 4100 K), TRIOS 4 wireless (3Shape, 5500 K). Illuminance levels: 0 lx (dark room), 500 lx, 1000 lx, 2500 lx. Shade matching: digital vs. visual consensus (Vita Classical and 3D-Master, middle third of #11). Trueness: IOS vs. InEos X5-digitized stone model (best-fit superimposition, RMS). Statistics: one-way ANOVA, α=0.05.

## Results
**Scanning trueness (µm, mean ±SD):** PR at 2500 lx: **100.78 ±19.36** (best); TR at 1000 lx: **104.04 ±20.88** (best). Both significantly affected by illuminance (p < 0.05). PR across all illuminances: not significantly different from each other (p > 0.05). TR at 0 lx: significantly lower accuracy (p < 0.05 vs. optimal).
**Shade accuracy (Vita Classical):** PR best 2500 lx: 96.7 ±10.5%; TR best 1000 lx: 93.3 ±14.1%. TR at 0 lx: significantly lower (p < 0.05).
