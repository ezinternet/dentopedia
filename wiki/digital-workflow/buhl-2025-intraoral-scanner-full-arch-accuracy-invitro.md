## Three-line Summary
In-vitro head-to-head comparison (J Dent 2025, standardized arch models, industrial CT reference standard) of multiple current IOS devices under identical conditions for full-arch impression accuracy.

Current IOS trueness ranged 50–200 µm with significant inter-device differences; error propagation increased in posterior extensions, making scan strategy critical for complex full-arch cases.

All devices performed adequately for single crowns and short-span FDPs, but for full-arch prosthetics the accumulating error must be factored into clinical decision-making for passivity requirements.

## 세줄요약
in-vitro 직접 비교 (J Dent 2025, 표준화 악궁 모델, 산업용 CT 기준): 동일 조건에서 현세대 IOS 여러 기기의 전악 인상 정확도 측정.

현세대 IOS 진실도(trueness) 범위 50–200μm, 기기 간 유의한 차이가 있으며, 구치부 연장 시 오차가 누적되어 스캔 전략이 복잡 증례에서 중요하다.

단관 및 단범위 브릿지에서는 모든 기기가 적합하나, 전악 보철에서는 누적 오차가 수동 적합(passivity) 요건에 영향을 미치므로 임상 의사결정에 고려해야 한다.

## Summary
Head-to-head in vitro comparison of current IOS accuracy for full-arch impressions using industrial CT as reference standard.

## Key Contributions
- Direct comparison of 4–6 current IOS devices under identical conditions
- Trueness range: 50–200μm; significant inter-device differences
- Error propagation increases in posterior extensions; scan strategy critical

## Methodology
In vitro; standardized arch models; industrial CT reference standard.

## Results
Current IOS trueness 50–200μm range. All devices adequate for single crowns/short-span FDPs. For complex full-arch prosthetics, error must be considered in clinical decision-making.
