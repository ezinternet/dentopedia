## Three-line Summary

Case report of a 78-year-old Korean female with reduced occlusal vertical dimension (OVD) from multiple tooth loss and severe attrition, treated with full-arch digital workflow at Seoul National University Prosthodontics (2019).

OVD raised 2.5 mm (freeway space 7 mm → phonetics/Willis-confirmed target); 10 implants placed with CBCT-planned, fully guided OneGuide templates; CAD/CAM PMMA provisionals delivered with no chairside occlusal adjustment; final full-arch zirconia restorations replicated provisional occlusal morphology via double-scan merge.

Demonstrates that digital workflow (scan merge + condylar parameter transfer from Arcus Digma) can eliminate intraoral adjustment at final delivery, but evidence is limited to n=1 with unspecified follow-up; long-term OVD stability and implant MBL not reported.

## 세줄요약

다수의 치아 상실과 전반적 치아 마모로 수직고경 (Occlusal Vertical Dimension, OVD)이 감소된 78세 여성에 대한 증례보고로, 서울대학교 치과보철학교실에서 완전 디지털 워크플로우 기반 전악 보철 수복을 시행하였다 (2019).

자유공극 (Freeway Space) 7 mm·Willis 안면 계측·발음 평가를 통해 수직고경 2.5 mm 거상 결정; CBCT 기반 수술용 가이드 (OneGuide, Osstem)로 임플란트 10개 정밀 식립; 진단 왁스업 스캔+지대치 스캔 합성(double-scan) 워크플로우로 CAD/CAM 지르코니아 (Zirconia) 최종 수복물 장착 시 교합 조정 불필요.

디지털 스캔 합성 기법과 아쿠스 디그마 2 (Arcus Digma 2) 전자 하악운동 측정 파라미터 이전이 임상 내 교합 조정을 제거할 수 있음을 보여주지만, n=1 증례보고·추시 기간 미기술의 한계로 일반화 불가.

## Summary

This Seoul National University case report describes the full mouth rehabilitation of a 78-year-old female with significantly reduced OVD caused by multiple posterior tooth loss and severe dentition attrition. The treatment protocol integrated multiple digital technologies: CBCT with radiographic stent for implant planning (Implant Studio, 3Shape), fully guided surgery (OneGuide, Osstem), electronic jaw movement recording (Arcus Digma 2, KaVo) for articulator condylar parameters, and CAD/CAM PMMA provisional restorations fabricated via wax-up double-scan merge. OVD was raised 2.5 mm at the canine zenith reference point, confirmed by freeway space measurement (~7 mm before treatment), Willis anthropometric measurement, and phonetic evaluation. Gothic arch tracing was used to locate centric relation. Final full-arch zirconia restorations replicated the validated provisional morphology through the double-scan technique, achieving the target occlusal scheme — mutual protection on the natural-tooth canine side and group function on the implant-supported canine side — without intraoral adjustment. The case illustrates that careful conventional clinical judgment (OVD assessment, CR recording, provisional monitoring) remains essential even within a fully digital workflow.

## Key Contributions

- **OVD determination protocol**: Multi-method approach combining freeway space measurement, Willis anthropometric measurement, and phonetic assessment; provisional dental monitoring at new OVD for 2 months prior to final prostheses
- **CBCT-guided surgery with IOS scan merge**: Radiographic stent CBCT + intraoral scan of provisional model merged in Implant Studio for implant position planning; surgical guide produced and used for all 10 implants
- **Articular parameter transfer from electronic jaw movement recording**: Arcus Digma 2 sagittal condylar guidance angle and lateral condylar guidance angle transferred to KaVo semi-adjustable articulator, enabling N.A.T. + N.F.R. wax-up with anatomically generated wear facets
- **Double-scan CAD/CAM**: Wax-up model scan + abutment model scan merged, PMMA milled — resulting in provisional delivery with zero chairside occlusal adjustment; same principle applied to transfer provisional morphology to final zirconia
- **Hybrid occlusal scheme**: Canine guidance (right, natural tooth) + group function (left, implant canine) chosen to protect implants from lateral cantilever forces while maintaining functional guidance

## Methodology

Single case report. Patient: 78-year-old Korean female, multiple posterior tooth loss, generalized severe attrition, previous ill-fitting removable partial denture, mild osteoporosis (non-medicated). Treatment: 10 implants (#14,15,16,23,27,35,36,37,46,47), full-arch fixed zirconia restorations on implants and natural tooth abutments, CAD/CAM workflow throughout provisional and final phases. Digital tools: 3Shape Implant Studio, OneGuide (Osstem), Arcus Digma 2 (KaVo), KaVo PROTAR Evo 7 articulator, CAD/CAM PMMA milling (Monolayer PMMA, Huge), T-Scan occlusal analysis, Imprint II Garant impression material (3M).

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| OVD increase | 2.5 mm (at canine zenith) |
| Freeway space at baseline | ~7 mm |
| Freeway space confirmed at new OVD | Adequate (specific mm not restated) |
| Implants placed | 10 (fully guided) |
| PMMA provisional adjustment at delivery | None required |
| Provisional monitoring period | 2 months (stable occlusion) |
| Occlusal scheme — right | Mutual protection / canine guidance |
| Occlusal scheme — left | Group function |
| Final restoration material — posterior | Full zirconia, FujiCEM 2 |
| Final restoration material — anterior | Cut-back zirconia + porcelain, Variolink |
| Post-treatment radiographs | No pathologic findings (panoramic + TMJ) |
