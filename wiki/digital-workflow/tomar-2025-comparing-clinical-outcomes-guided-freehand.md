## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 13 systematic reviews/meta-analyses (PRISMA + PROSPERO; PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Google Scholar, Cochrane; Jan 2000–Jul 2025) comparing guided (static/dynamic) vs freehand dental implant placement on accuracy, marginal bone loss (MBL), survival, and complications; AMSTAR-2 appraised, descriptively synthesized.

Guided surgery was more accurate than freehand (SMD for coronal deviation −0.97; 95% CI −2.27 to −0.32; P<.01), but MBL and implant survival were statistically comparable between techniques (RR 1.02; 95% CI 0.98–1.06; P=.28, both >high survival), and complications trended lower with guided surgery without reaching significance (RR 0.88; 95% CI 0.74–1.04; P=.11).

Accuracy superiority of guided surgery does not translate into a survival or MBL advantage — freehand remains reliable in experienced hands; **abstract-only page (paywalled, full text not retrieved)**, and one key statistic (coronal-deviation SMD) is numerically identical to an unrelated umbrella review published in the same journal four months earlier — see Summary for the data-integrity caveat.

가이드 수술이 프리핸드보다 정확했으나(관상부 편차 표준화평균차, SMD −0.97; 95% CI −2.27~−0.32; P<.01), MBL·임플란트 생존율은 기법 간 통계적 유의차 없음(위험비, RR 1.02; 95% CI 0.98–1.06; P=.28, 둘 다 높은 생존율), 합병증은 가이드 기법에서 낮아지는 경향이 있으나 유의하지 않음(RR 0.88; 95% CI 0.74–1.04; P=.11).

## Summary

**Abstract-only — full text not retrieved (paywalled).** This page is built from the structured abstract of Tomar, Chaudhary, and Ganguly (J Prosthet Dent 2026;135(5):e53–e59, e-pub 2025 Nov 7); the full text is behind a paywall, so the 13 included reviews' individual data, AMSTAR-2 scores, and full effect-size tables are not available. `full_text: false`.

This umbrella review synthesizes 13 systematic reviews/meta-analyses comparing guided (static or dynamic computer-assisted) versus freehand dental implant placement, following the same "accuracy-vs-outcome" logic already established in this wiki's guided-surgery cluster. Guided/computer-assisted placement showed better positional accuracy than freehand (coronal deviation SMD −0.97; 95% CI −2.27 to −0.32; P<.01), but this accuracy advantage did **not** translate into a survival or MBL advantage — both techniques showed comparable, high MBL/survival outcomes (RR 1.02; 95% CI 0.98–1.06; P=.28) and low but statistically indistinguishable complication rates (RR 0.88; 95% CI 0.74–1.04; P=.11). This result directly **reinforces** [[digital-workflow/shirani-2026-computer-assisted-vs-freehand-implant-placement-sr-ma]], a separate SR+MA of RCTs that similarly found no significant CAIS-vs-freehand difference in overall MBL/survival — an umbrella-level and an RCT-level SR+MA now independently converge on "accuracy ≠ outcome superiority."

**Data-integrity caveat**: the reported coronal-deviation statistic (SMD −0.97, 95% CI −2.27 to −0.32, P<.01) is numerically identical, down to the decimal, to the pooled coronal-deviation statistic reported by [[digital-workflow/mehta-2025-accuracy-assessment-robot-assisted-dental]] — a different umbrella review (different DOI: 10.1016/j.prosdent.2025.06.032 vs 10.1016/j.prosdent.2025.10.039; different comparison: robot-assisted vs comparators, not guided vs freehand) published four months earlier in the same journal (J Prosthet Dent). This could be coincidence, an abstract-writing artifact, or a data-reuse error; it is not proof of misconduct, but it means this specific effect size should not be treated as independently corroborating evidence until the full texts can be cross-checked. All other reported figures in this page (RR for survival/complications) are distinct from mehta-2025 and unaffected by this concern.

Clinically, the paper's bottom line is conventional and consistent with the wiki's existing guided-vs-freehand literature: guided surgery improves placement accuracy and may reduce surgical risk in complex cases, but freehand placement remains a reliable option with comparable long-term outcomes when performed by experienced clinicians — the choice should weigh case complexity, clinician expertise, and available resources rather than defaulting to one technique.

## Key Contributions

- Umbrella-level (13 SRs/MAs) confirmation that guided/computer-assisted implant placement is more accurate than freehand (coronal deviation SMD −0.97), consistent with the wiki's existing accuracy-domain NMAs.
- Independently reinforces, at a higher evidence tier, the finding from [[digital-workflow/shirani-2026-computer-assisted-vs-freehand-implant-placement-sr-ma]] that guided surgery's accuracy advantage does not extend to a statistically significant MBL or survival benefit over freehand (RR 1.02, P=.28).
- Frames technique selection as case-complexity- and clinician-expertise-dependent rather than a blanket recommendation for guided surgery.

## Methodology

Umbrella review (PRISMA 2020 + PROSPERO registration) of 13 systematic reviews/meta-analyses comparing guided (static or dynamic) vs freehand dental implant placement, searched across PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Google Scholar, and Cochrane Library (Jan 2000–Jul 2025). AMSTAR-2 used for methodological quality appraisal; effect sizes extracted directly from the included meta-analyses and synthesized descriptively (no new pooled meta-analysis conducted). Outcomes: implant placement accuracy, marginal bone loss, survival, complications.

## Results

|---|---|
