## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (AMSTAR-2-graded) of 8 systematic reviews covering 5130 implants in 1448 models/patients, comparing robot-assisted dental implant surgery accuracy to dynamic/static computer-assisted or freehand placement.

Robot-assisted surgery showed lower apical and angular deviations than comparator methods across included reviews; pooled random-effects meta-analysis of coronal deviation favored robots (SMD −0.97, 95% CI −2.27 to −0.32; P<.01), though individual-review coronal findings were mixed and robotic automation levels varied (fully automatic, semi-automatic, passive).

Clinically robot-assisted placement appears to improve positional accuracy (apical, coronal, angular) over dynamic/static guided or freehand placement, but the evidence base is thin (8 eligible reviews from 269 records) and heterogeneous across robot automation levels, limiting confidence in the pooled coronal effect.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰 (AMSTAR-2 등급평가) — SR 8편 종합, 임플란트 5130개·모델/환자 1448명, 로봇보조 치과 임플란트 수술(Robot-assisted Dental Implant Surgery)의 정확도를 동적/정적 컴퓨터가이드 또는 프리핸드 식립과 비교.

로봇보조 수술은 포함된 리뷰 전반에서 첨단부(apical) 및 각도(angular) 편차가 더 낮았고, 관상부(coronal) 편차의 무작위효과모형(Random-effects Model) 메타분석에서 로봇에 유리한 결과(표준화평균차, Standardized Mean Difference, SMD −0.97, 95% CI −2.27~−0.32; P<.01)를 보였으나, 개별 리뷰 간 관상부 결과는 혼재됐고 로봇 자동화 수준(완전자동·반자동·수동보조)이 다양했다.

임상적으로 로봇보조 식립은 동적/정적 가이드 또는 프리핸드 대비 위치 정확도(첨단부·관상부·각도)를 개선하는 것으로 보이나, 근거기반이 얇고(269건 중 8개 리뷰만 최종 포함) 로봇 자동화 수준 간 이질성이 커 관상부 풀링 효과의 확신도는 제한적이다.

## Summary

This umbrella review of systematic reviews screened 269 records and included 8 systematic reviews (5130 implants, 1448 models/patients) examining positional accuracy of robot-assisted dental implant surgery, with AMSTAR-2 methodological quality appraisal and a random-effects meta-analysis of non-duplicate primary studies. Robot-assisted surgeries had lower apical and angular deviations than dynamic/static computer-assisted or freehand implant surgeries across the included reviews. Coronal deviation results were mixed at the individual-review level, but the pooled meta-analysis favored robots (SMD −0.97, 95% CI −2.27 to −0.32; P<.01). Robotic automation level (fully automatic, semi-automatic, passive) varied across the included evidence and was not uniformly associated with the same accuracy gains. Clinically, this supports robot-assisted surgery as generally more accurate than guided/freehand alternatives, but the thin evidence base (only 8 reviews) and automation-level heterogeneity mean the pooled coronal advantage should be interpreted cautiously, and it should be reconciled against same-tracker RCT evidence in the wiki that found the robot's reliable advantage is angular rather than linear/coronal.

## Key Contributions
- First SR-of-SRs (umbrella review) on robot-assisted dental implant positional accuracy, with formal AMSTAR-2 quality grading of the 8 included systematic reviews.
- Meta-analyzed non-duplicate primary studies pooled across the constituent reviews (not just narrative synthesis of review-level conclusions), addressing double-counting of overlapping primary studies.
- Stratifies conclusions by robotic automation level (fully automatic / semi-automatic / passive), highlighting that "robot-assisted" is not a single homogeneous intervention.

## Methodology
Umbrella review (JBI methodology, PRISMA 2020 reporting) searching PubMed-MEDLINE, Science Direct, Scopus, and Embase to April 2025 (2 independent reviewers, Rayyan screening). AMSTAR 2 used for quality appraisal of included SRs. Random-effects meta-analysis performed on non-duplicate primary studies pooled from the 8 included reviews (5130 implants, 1448 models/patients), evaluating apical, coronal, and angular deviation of robot-assisted vs dynamic-CAIS, static-CAIS, or freehand implant placement.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |

| Records screened → reviews included | 269 → 8 systematic reviews |
| Pooled sample | 5130 implants in 1448 models/patients |

| Angular deviation | Lower with robot-assisted surgery vs comparators (consistent across reviews) |
| Coronal deviation | Mixed at review level; pooled meta-analysis SMD −0.97, 95% CI (−2.27, −0.32), P<.01, favoring robots |
