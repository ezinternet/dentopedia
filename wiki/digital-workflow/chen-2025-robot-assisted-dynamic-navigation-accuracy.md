## Three-line Summary
Retrospective single-center study (Clin Oral Implants Res 2025, 57 single-tooth implants, Jan–Dec 2023) comparing semi-autonomous robotic surgery (sa-RASS, n=29) vs dynamic navigation system (DNS, n=28) for implant placement accuracy measured by post-op CBCT.

The sa-RASS produced significantly smaller platform deviation (0.91 vs 1.26 mm, p<0.001) and apex deviation (1.06 vs 1.51 mm, p<0.001), but angular deviation did not differ significantly (3.07° vs 3.71°, p>0.05).

The semi-autonomous design keeps the operator partly in the loop, which likely explains the smaller linear advantage over navigation but absent angular advantage seen in fully autonomous robot studies.

## 세줄요약
후향적 단일기관 연구 (Clin Oral Implants Res 2025, 단일치 임플란트 57개, 2023년): 반자율 로봇 수술(sa-RASS, n=29) 대 동적 내비게이션 시스템(DNS, n=28)의 식립 정확도를 술후 CBCT로 비교.

sa-RASS가 플랫폼 편차(0.91 vs 1.26 mm, p<0.001) 및 첨부 편차(1.06 vs 1.51 mm, p<0.001)에서 유의하게 낮은 오차를 보였으나, 각도 편차는 유의한 차이가 없었다(3.07° vs 3.71°, p>0.05).

반자율 설계가 술자를 일부 개입시키는 구조여서 선형 정확도는 유리하나, 완전 자율 로봇 연구에서 보이는 각도 이점은 나타나지 않은 것으로 해석된다.

## Summary
This retrospective study compared a novel semi-autonomous robotic-assisted surgery system (sa-RASS) with a dynamic navigation system in 57 single-tooth implant placements. sa-RASS produced significantly smaller platform and apex deviations, but angular deviation did not differ significantly between systems — contrasting with autonomous-robot studies that show a clear angular advantage. The semi-autonomous design keeps the operator partly in the loop, which may explain the smaller angular gain.

## Key Contributions
- Introduces a semi-autonomous (haptic-constrained) robot to the comparison, distinct from fully autonomous robots.
- Linear (platform/apex) accuracy favored the robot; angular accuracy did not.

## Methodology
Retrospective, single center, Jan–Dec 2023. 57 patients/57 implants (sa-RASS 29, DNS 28), partial edentulism. CBCT-measured platform, apex, angular deviation. KS test then t-test/Mann–Whitney.

## Results
- Platform: 0.91 ± 0.46 vs 1.26 ± 0.51 mm, p < 0.001.
- Apex: 1.06 ± 0.60 vs 1.51 ± 0.56 mm, p < 0.001.
- Angular: 3.07° ± 1.66° vs 3.71° ± 1.64°, p > 0.05.
