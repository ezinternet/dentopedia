## Three-line Summary
Clinical study (10 participants, 6 IOS): Primescan achieved the highest complete-arch trueness (41 ±10 µm) among all tested scanners in a real-patient setting. Primescan 2 (the successor model) and MEDIT i900 performed comparably; Emerald S showed significantly worse trueness (96 µm). Conventional polyether impression (21 µm precision) still outperformed all IOS for repeatability.

## 세줄요약
임상연구(10명, 6 구강내스캐너(IOS)): 실제 환자에서 프라임스캔(Primescan)이 전악 진도성(trueness) 41 µm로 최우수. 프라임스캔 2(Primescan 2) · 메딧 i900(MEDIT i900)과 비유의적 차이. 폴리에테르 인상(정밀도 21 µm)은 여전히 모든 IOS보다 반복재현성 우위.

## Summary
Ender et al. recruited 10 adults and scanned maxillary and mandibular complete arches with six current-generation IOS (Emerald S, MEDIT i900, Primescan, Primescan 2, Aoralscan 3, TRIOS 5), three full-arch scans each, plus a conventional polyether impression as reference. Trueness was computed as the 90th–10th percentile deviation of each IOS scan against the corresponding conventional model; precision was intragroup pairwise deviation. Primescan recorded the best trueness (41 µm) with no significant difference from Primescan 2 or MEDIT i900, confirming that multiple IOS can achieve clinically acceptable complete-arch accuracy. Emerald S was significantly worse than all others. Conventional impression retained statistically superior precision (21 µm vs. ≥40 µm for IOS), though all IOS trueness values fell below the 100 µm clinical threshold.

## Key Contributions
- First head-to-head in vivo comparison of Primescan and Primescan 2 — no significant difference in trueness or precision
- Establishes Primescan as top-performing IOS for complete-arch trueness in clinical use (not just bench testing)
- Conventional impression still significantly outperforms all IOS in precision; difference likely not clinically meaningful given all trueness values < 100 µm
- Emerald S significantly inferior to all other IOS (trueness 96 µm, precision 82 µm) — the outlier
- Provides in vivo confirmation of Buhl et al. 2025 in vitro findings from the same research group

## Methodology
Clinical study (in vivo, prospective cross-sectional), single operator. N=10 adults; complete maxillary and mandibular arch scans. IOS: Emerald S (EME), MEDIT i900 (MED), Primescan (PRI), Primescan 2 (PS2), Aoralscan 3 (SHI), TRIOS 5 (TRI); 3 scans per IOS per participant. Reference: conventional polyether impression → type IV gypsum model → laboratory scanner. Trueness: (90th–10th percentile deviation)/2 per scan vs. reference model. Precision: mean pairwise deviation within IOS group. Statistics: Welch ANOVA + Games-Howell post-hoc, linear mixed-effects model, α=0.05.

## Results
**Trueness (µm, mean ±SD):** PRI **41 ±10** = PS2 = MED < SHI ≈ TRI < EME 96 ±29 (all p < 0.05 for EME vs. others; PRI vs. PS2, PRI vs. MED, PS2 vs. MED all p > 0.05).
**Precision (µm):** CONV 21 ±4 < PRI/PS2/MED cluster ≈ SHI/TRI cluster < EME 82 ±38 (CONV significantly better than all IOS, p < 0.001).
All IOS trueness < 100 µm (clinical acceptability threshold). EME significantly inferior to all others on both metrics.
