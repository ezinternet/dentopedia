## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 9 RCTs (12 publications, 395 participants, 1,242 implants; search to Nov 2024) comparing computer-assisted implant surgery (CAIS) vs freehand implant placement (FHIP) on clinical/radiographic outcomes — marginal bone loss (MBL), implant survival, PROs, complications — with Cochrane RoB + GRADE.

Overall MBL and implant survival showed NO statistically significant CAIS-vs-FHIP difference (both >98% survival); subgroups favored CAIS in fresh sockets (MBL P=.04), patient satisfaction (P=.03) and pink esthetic score (P=.009), while FHIP had fewer biologic complications (P=.04) and lower MBL than tissue-supported CAIS (P=.03).

Accuracy superiority of guided surgery does NOT translate into survival superiority; use CAIS for fresh-socket/esthetic cases, but a tissue-supported guide can underperform open-flap freehand — evidence certainty very low to moderate, 6/9 studies high risk of bias.

## 세줄요약

9개 RCT(12편, 참가자 395명, 임플란트 1,242개, 2024년 11월까지 검색)를 종합한 SR+MA로, 컴퓨터보조 임플란트수술(CAIS)과 자유수식립(FHIP)을 임상·방사선 결과(MBL, 생존율, PRO, 합병증)로 비교하고 Cochrane RoB·GRADE로 평가.

전체 MBL·생존율은 CAIS-FHIP 유의차 없음(양쪽 생존율 >98%); 하위그룹에서 CAIS는 신선발치와 MBL(P=.04)·환자만족도(P=.03)·핑크심미점수(P=.009)에서 우수, FHIP는 생물학적 합병증이 적고(P=.04) 조직지지형 CAIS보다 MBL이 낮음(P=.03).

가이드 수술의 정확도 우위가 생존율 우위로 이어지지 않음 — 신선발치와·심미 증례엔 CAIS, 단 점막지지 가이드는 개방피판 freehand보다 나쁠 수 있음; 근거등급 very low~moderate, 9편 중 6편 high RoB.

## Summary

**Abstract-only — full text not retrieved (paywalled).** This page is built from the structured abstract of Shirani & Emami (IJOMI 2026;41(3):359–370B); the full text is behind a paywall, so pooled effect sizes, confidence intervals, heterogeneity (I²), and the study-by-study table are not available. `full_text: false`. Reported n, P-values and subgroup directions are taken verbatim from the abstract; nothing is inferred beyond it.

This SR+MA answers a different question than the guided-surgery **accuracy** literature the wiki already holds. Accuracy NMAs (Schiavon 2025, Nava 2026, Xing 2024) show that static/dynamic guided surgery places implants closer to plan (smaller coronal/apical/angular deviation) than freehand. Shirani asks whether that positional advantage carries through to **clinical and radiographic outcomes** — MBL, implant survival, patient-reported outcomes (PROs), and complications — and finds it largely does not: pooled across 9 RCTs, CAIS and FHIP show **no statistically significant difference** in overall MBL or survival, with both modalities exceeding 98% survival.

The value is in the subgroups. CAIS was favored where planning precision plausibly matters most — **fresh-socket placement** (lower MBL, P=.04) — and on **patient-centered/esthetic** endpoints (satisfaction P=.03; pink esthetic score P=.009). Conversely FHIP showed **fewer biologic complications** (P=.04), and notably **lower MBL than tissue-supported CAIS** (P=.03): a mucosa-supported guide, with its weaker registration stability, can perform worse than an open-flap freehand approach. The take-home for the digital-workflow decision ladder is that "guided vs freehand" is the wrong axis — *guide support quality and case context* drive outcomes, and accuracy gains are necessary but not sufficient for better clinical results.

Evidence certainty (GRADE) ranged very low to moderate and 6 of 9 studies were at high risk of bias, so all subgroup signals are hypothesis-level.

## Key Contributions

- Moves the CAIS-vs-FHIP comparison from the **accuracy** domain (where guided surgery wins) to the **outcome** domain (MBL/survival/PRO/complications), showing accuracy superiority does NOT translate into survival superiority — both >98%.
- Localizes CAIS benefit to **fresh-socket** cases and **esthetic/patient-reported** endpoints rather than a blanket MBL/survival advantage.
- Flags a concrete caveat: **tissue-supported CAIS can underperform freehand on MBL** (P=.03); open-flap freehand had fewer biologic complications — support/registration type matters.

## Methodology

- SR+MA of RCTs only; five databases (PubMed, Embase, CINAHL, Scopus, Web of Science) + handsearch + cross-reference; literature to November 2024.
- 12 publications from 9 RCTs; 395 participants; 1,242 implants.
- Outcomes: MBL, implant survival, patient satisfaction, pink esthetic score, probing depth, biologic complications.
- Meta-analysis with sensitivity + subgroup (fresh vs healed socket; tissue-supported vs open-flap) analyses; Cochrane RoB; GRADE certainty.
- Pooled estimates / CI / I² not extractable from abstract.

## Results

| Outcome | Direction | P |
|---|---|---|
| Overall MBL | No significant difference | NS |
| Overall survival | No difference; both >98% | NS |
| MBL, fresh socket | CAIS lower (favored) | .04 |
| Patient satisfaction | CAIS higher | .03 |
| Pink esthetic score | CAIS better | .009 |
| Biologic complications | FHIP fewer | .04 |
| MBL vs tissue-supported CAIS | FHIP lower (favored) | .03 |

Certainty very low → moderate; 6/9 studies high RoB; sensitivity analyses robust for most outcomes.
