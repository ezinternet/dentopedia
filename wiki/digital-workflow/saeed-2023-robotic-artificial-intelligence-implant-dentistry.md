## Three-line Summary
Narrative review (Cureus 2023, Ministry of Health Saudi Arabia) covering the integration of robotic technology and artificial intelligence in implant dentistry, including placement precision, AI-driven diagnostics and planning, and case-based outcomes.

Robotic systems improve placement accuracy and reduce human error; AI algorithms analyze patient data to optimize diagnosis, treatment planning, and implant design, with case studies demonstrating improved survival rates and patient satisfaction.

Significant challenges remain including ethical considerations, high costs, and defining accountability between human expertise and technology, requiring further clinical evidence and interdisciplinary frameworks.

## Summary
Narrative review (Ministry of Health, Saudi Arabia) on integration of robotic technology and AI in implant dentistry. Covers robotic precision in implant placement, AI-driven diagnostics and planning, successful case outcomes, ethical considerations, and future directions.

## Key Contributions
- **Robotic systems**: unparalleled placement accuracy; reduces human error; improves treatment outcomes
- **AI algorithms**: analyze patient data for diagnosis, treatment planning, implant design optimization
- Case studies: improved implant survival rates and patient satisfaction demonstrated
- **Challenges**: ethical considerations, balance between human expertise and technology reliance
- **Future**: digital workflow integration; interdisciplinary collaboration essential
