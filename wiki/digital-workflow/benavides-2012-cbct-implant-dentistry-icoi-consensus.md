## Three-line Summary

Consensus statement (ICOI panel; systematic PubMed review, Jan 2000–Jul 2011) on CBCT in implant dentistry, organized as diagnostics, planning, surgical guidance and postimplant evaluation.

CBCT is supported for linear measurement, 3-D ridge topography, proximity to vital structures and surgical-guide fabrication; CBCT bone-density measurement, CBCT-aided navigation and postimplant artifacts remain under-researched.

Justify each scan on individual need, use the smallest field of view, and interpret the whole volume; the abstract gives no numeric effect sizes.

## 세줄요약

합의문 (ICOI 패널; 2000년 1월–2011년 7월 PubMed 체계적 검토) — 치과 임플란트의 CBCT를 진단·계획·수술 가이드·식립 후 평가로 정리.

CBCT는 선형 계측, 3차원 치조제 지형, 중요 구조물 근접성, 수술 가이드 제작에 지지되며, CBCT 골밀도 측정·CBCT 보조 항법·식립 후 아티팩트는 근거가 부족.

개별 필요에 따라 정당화하고 최소 FOV를 쓰며 전체 볼륨을 판독할 것; 초록에 효과 수치는 없음.

## Summary

The ICOI consensus frames CBCT as an adjunct to conventional imaging, to be used when two-dimensional radiographs cannot show the three-dimensional anatomy of an implant or augmentation site. Its use-indications (linear measurement, ridge topography, vital-structure proximity, guide fabrication) are the ones this panel considered best supported. The benefit-versus-risk and history-first rules are the panel's core safety message.

## Key Contributions

- Four-domain map of the CBCT-implant literature (2000–2011).
- Explicit list of supported and unresolved uses.
- A justification-and-field-of-view rule set for clinicians.

## Methodology

Systematic PubMed search (2000–2011), with oral presentations by invited panelists at an ICOI meeting in Seoul (Oct 2011). Synthesis is consensus-based; formal evidence grading is not visible in the abstract.

## Results

Qualitative: CBCT is recommended for listed planning and guidance uses; bone-density, navigation and artifact questions are open. No numeric results in the abstract.
