## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (4 maxillary arch models, 7 IOS, virtual-fit method, n=12 per IOS): Mediti700 (43 µm), Primescan (42 µm), and EmeraldS were in the best homogenous subset for marginal gap; Primescan fell to the second-best subset for precision (MAE), behind Mediti700, Trios5, and EmeraldS. Helios600 (118 µm) and Aoralscan3 showed the worst marginal fit; increasing cement space to 70+20 µm rescued Lumina and Aoralscan3 fit. EmeraldS, Mediti700, Primescan, and Trios5 all meet accepted fit criteria for complete-arch fixed restorations.

## Summary
Borbola et al. scanned four maxillary arch models with seven current-generation IOS (Aoralscan3, EmeraldS, Helios600, Lumina, Mediti700, Primescan, Trios5) and an industrial reference scanner, then applied the virtual-fit method to evaluate marginal and internal gaps for two cement-space groups (70 µm vs 70+20 µm). Primescan ranked 2nd for marginal gap trueness (42 µm, same subset as Mediti700), consistent with its established top-tier performance, but placed in the second precision subset (behind Mediti700, Trios5, EmeraldS). Helios600 showed the worst marginal fit (118 µm) and was the only IOS that failed standard clinical criteria. Increasing cement space by 20 µm at the margin selectively improved fit for Lumina and Aoralscan3 — devices that struggle with trueness — while top-tier IOS showed no meaningful change, suggesting 70 µm is already optimized for Primescan and Trios5-class scanners. EmeraldS — a less-established scanner — unexpectedly ranked in the top precision subset, warranting independent confirmation.

## Key Contributions
- 7-way IOS comparison with virtual-fit method adds nuance to Primescan ranking: 2nd (not 1st) for marginal gap trueness; 2nd group for precision
- EmeraldS achieves top-tier performance in both metrics — a newer scanner not represented in previous network meta-analyses
- Trios5 precision (MAE) rank is superior to its trueness rank — metric choice affects IOS selection decisions
- Cement space increase (70→90 µm) rescues fit for mid-tier scanners without benefit for top-tier scanners
- Establishes that at least 4 contemporary IOS (EmeraldS, Mediti700, Primescan, Trios5) are clinically interchangeable for complete-arch fixed prosthetics

## Methodology
In vitro comparative study. N=4 maxillary arch models with tooth abutments; each scanned by industrial reference (n=1) and 7 IOS (each n=12). Two complete-arch fixed framework designs: Group 70 (70 µm internal cement space) and Group 70+20 (70 µm internal + 20 µm additional at margin). Virtual-fit method: framework STL superimposed onto reference scan via non-penetrating algorithm. Outcomes: marginal gap, internal gap (µm), MAE (precision). Statistics: ANOVA-based homogenous subsets.

## Results
**Marginal gap trueness — Group 70:**
- Subset 1: Mediti700 43 µm, **Primescan 42 µm**, EmeraldS (value not reported in abstract)
- Subset 2: Lumina 67 µm, Aoralscan3 70 µm, Trios5 70 µm
- Subset 3: Helios600 118 µm

**MAE precision — Group 70:**
- Subset 1: Mediti700, Trios5, EmeraldS
- Subset 2: **Primescan**
- Subset 3: Lumina, Helios600
- Subset 4: Aoralscan3

**Group 70+20 effect:**
- Lumina, Aoralscan3: marginal gap significantly decreased
- EmeraldS, Aoralscan3: MAE significantly decreased
- Primescan, Trios5, Mediti700: no significant improvement (already optimal)
