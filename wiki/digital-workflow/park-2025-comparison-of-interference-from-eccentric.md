## Three-line Summary

Dynamic jaw motion tracking (DJMT) integrated into CAD software significantly reduces intraoral occlusal adjustment for zirconia crowns compared to conventional fabrication. In 15 patients (30 crowns), mean RMS adjustment was 67.5 µm (DJMT) vs 91.8 µm (conventional), with P = .028. The finding supports wider clinical adoption of DJMT for single-crown restorations to improve prosthetic efficiency.

## 세줄요약

동적 하악운동 추적 (Dynamic Jaw Motion Tracking, DJMT)을 CAD 소프트웨어에 통합하면 기존 방법 대비 지르코니아 크라운의 구내 교합 조정량이 유의하게 감소한다. 15명 환자 (30개 크라운)에서 평균 RMS 조정값은 DJMT 67.5 µm 대 기존 방법 91.8 µm (P = .028)였다. 이 결과는 단일 크라운 수복에서 DJMT의 임상 활용 확대를 지지한다.

## Summary

This double-blind randomized clinical trial (RCT) compared intraoral occlusal adjustment requirements for zirconia crowns fabricated using dynamic jaw motion tracking (DJMT) versus conventional methods. Fifteen patients each received two crowns — one per method — with crowns blind-coded to prevent clinician bias. Post-fitting adjustments were quantified by scanning crowns before and after occlusal adjustment and computing RMS deviations using 3D inspection software (Geomagic Control X). DJMT-designed crowns required significantly less adjustment (67.5 µm vs 91.8 µm, P = .028), suggesting that replicating patient-specific mandibular movement pathways in the CAD process reduces eccentric occlusal discrepancies. No location effect (anterior vs posterior) was found (P = .450).

## Key Contributions

1. **Quantitative clinical evidence**: First clinical study to quantify DJMT benefit for single-crown zirconia restorations using RMS 3D inspection (Geomagic Control X).
2. **26.5% adjustment reduction**: DJMT yielded mean 67.5 µm vs 91.8 µm conventional — statistically significant (P = .028).
3. **Double-blind design**: Blind-coded crowns eliminate clinician selection bias, strengthening internal validity.
4. **Location-independent benefit**: No anterior vs posterior difference (P = .450) — DJMT benefit applies broadly.
5. **Replicable methodology**: RMS + color deviation map framework provides a standard outcome measure for future digital occlusion studies.

## Methodology

**Device**: Zebris for Ceramill (Amann Girrbach) optic facebow system with LED lower jaw sensor.

**DJMT procedure**:
- Facebow aligned at external auditory meatus bilaterally
- Para-occlusal resin attachment on mandibular anteriors holds lower jaw sensor
- Mandibular movements recorded 3× each: left lateral, right lateral, protrusive, opening
- Data integrated with exocad CAD for digital facebow transfer + dynamic occlusal simulation

**Conventional procedure**: Rubber impression → plaster cast → desktop scan → manual technician CAD adjustment.

**Zirconia fabrication**: 5-axis milling (DOF CRAFT 5X DC), CeramillZolid HT presintered blocks, sintered per manufacturer protocol.

**Outcome**: MEDIT i700 intraoral scanner pre/post-adjustment; Geomagic Control X RMS computation; paired t-test (α = 0.05, SPSS 27.0).
