## Three-line Summary

In-vitro controlled study (BMC Oral Health 2025;25:753; Sun Yat-sen University; resin edentulous mandibular master model, 6 parallel implants, n=10/group): four groups — conventional splinted open-tray (CON), IOS (TRIOS3), photogrammetry PG-1 (Icam4D), PG-2 (PIC); evaluated by reverse engineering software (3D linearity, angularity, RMS deviations).

Trueness RMS (µm): PG-1 10.87, PG-2 13.35, CON 40.50, IOS 91.75; precision RMS: PG-1 3.7, PG-2 4.6, CON 36.55, IOS 82.8; angularity trueness: PG 0.12-0.14°, CON 0.35°, IOS 0.52°; all inter-group differences significant (p<0.01 or p<0.05).

Both photogrammetry (PG) systems showed 4–8× better RMS trueness than IOS (TRIOS3), and conventional outperformed IOS; for 6-implant full-arch rehabilitation, PG > conventional > IOS is the accuracy ranking.

## 세줄요약

In-vitro 대조 연구 (BMC Oral Health 2025;25:753; 쑨야트센 대학교; 수지 무치악 하악 마스터 모델, 6개 평행 임플란트, 군당 n=10): 4군 — 전통 스플린트 개방형 트레이 (CON), 구강내 스캐닝 (Intraoral Scanning, IOS) (TRIOS3), 광측정술 1 (PG-1; Icam4D), 광측정술 2 (PG-2; PIC); 역공학 소프트웨어로 3D 선형·각도·균방평균제곱근 (Root Mean Square, RMS) 편차 평가.

진실도 RMS (µm): PG-1 10.87, PG-2 13.35, CON 40.50, IOS 91.75; 정밀도 RMS: PG-1 3.7, PG-2 4.6, CON 36.55, IOS 82.8; 각도 진실도: PG 0.12-0.14°, CON 0.35°, IOS 0.52°; 군간 차이 모두 유의 (p<0.01 또는 p<0.05).

두 광측정술 (Photogrammetry, PG) 시스템 모두 IOS (TRIOS3) 대비 RMS 진실도 4–8배 우수; 전통 인상이 IOS 상회; 6임플란트 전악 재활에서 정확도 순위: 광측정술 > 전통 인상 > 구강내 스캐닝.

## Summary

In-vitro head-to-head comparison (n=40 total, 10/group) of four impression techniques for 6-implant edentulous mandibular rehabilitation. The two photogrammetry systems (Icam4D and PIC) achieved RMS trueness of 10.87 and 13.35 µm respectively — well below the conventional impression (40.50 µm) and dramatically superior to IOS/TRIOS3 (91.75 µm). Precision results showed an even wider gap (PG 3.7–4.6 µm vs IOS 82.8 µm). Both PG systems were statistically equivalent to each other, suggesting that the photogrammetric principle (not a specific brand) drives accuracy. This study provides the clearest in-vitro benchmarks to support the SR+MA findings of Prasad 2026 and anchors rung 5 of the IOS decision ladder in the digital-workflow overview.

## Key Contributions
- In-vitro head-to-head: Icam4D and PIC photogrammetry vs TRIOS3 IOS vs conventional (n=10/group, 6-implant mandibular arch)
- Icam4D PG: RMS trueness 10.87 µm — single best result; PIC 13.35 µm (equivalent, p>0.05)
- IOS (TRIOS3): RMS trueness 91.75 µm — worst in this 4-group study for multi-implant full-arch
- Conventional intermediate: RMS 40.50 µm (better than IOS for full-arch, as expected)
- Precision gap even larger than trueness: PG-1 3.7 µm vs IOS 82.8 µm (22× difference)
- Null hypothesis rejected for all inter-group comparisons except PG-1 vs PG-2

## Methodology
- **Model**: Resin edentulous mandibular master model; 6 parallel implants; screw-retained abutments
- **Groups (n=10 each)**:
  - CON: conventional splinted open-tray impression (zinc oxide–eugenol paste, splinted impression copings)
  - IOS: TRIOS3 (3Shape) full-arch intraoral scan
  - PG-1: Icam4D photogrammetric system
  - PG-2: PIC photogrammetric system
- **Reference**: Laboratory scanner (Medit T510) used to digitize master model and CON group casts
- **Analysis**: STL export → reverse engineering software (3D linearity X/Y/Z axes, angularity, RMS)
- **Statistics**: Kruskal-Wallis + post-hoc pairwise; p<0.05

## Results

### Trueness (vs master model)
| Group | 3D Linear (µm) | Angularity (°) | RMS (µm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PG-1 (Icam4D) | 25.23 | 0.12 | **10.87** |
| PG-2 (PIC) | 28.15 | 0.14 | **13.35** |
| CON | 66.05 | 0.35 | 40.50 |
| IOS (TRIOS3) | 78.58 | 0.52 | 91.75 |

### Precision (repeatability within group)
| Group | Linear (µm) | Angularity (°) | RMS (µm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PG-1 (Icam4D) | 15.80 | 0.10 | **3.7** |
| PG-2 (PIC) | 17.78 | 0.13 | **4.6** |
| CON | 39.32 | 0.24 | 36.55 |
| IOS (TRIOS3) | 45.33 | 0.38 | 82.8 |

- Significant differences: X, Y-axis, 3D linearity, angularity, RMS (trueness, p<0.01); X, Y-axis, 3D linearity, XZ-plane, RMS (precision, p<0.05)
- PG-1 vs PG-2: no significant difference in any metric

## Implications
- For 6-implant full-arch mandibular rehabilitation: photogrammetry is the evidence-based first choice for impression accuracy
- Conventional splinted open-tray remains viable (40.50 µm RMS) where photogrammetry is unavailable
- IOS (TRIOS3) should not be used as the sole impression method for multi-implant full-arch rehabilitation (91.75 µm RMS)
- Both Icam4D and PIC systems perform equivalently — system availability/cost drives selection
- Results align with Prasad 2026 SR+MA pooled estimate (RMS trueness 48 µm for extraoral PG)

## Limitations
- In-vitro only — clinical saliva, soft tissue movement, patient cooperation not modeled
- Single mandibular model, 6 parallel implants — tilted implants (All-on-X) not included
- Conventional reference group digitized via lab scanner (potential reference-chain bias)
- No passive fit test of fabricated prostheses — accuracy of impression ≠ final framework fit
