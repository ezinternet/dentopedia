## Three-line Summary
In vitro µ-CT study (n=20 endodontically treated premolars): Primescan achieved significantly higher trueness and precision than TRIOS 4 for post-space scanning at both depths (10 mm, 12 mm). Increasing preparation depth significantly worsened accuracy for both scanners. The study establishes feasibility of IOS for post-space digitization but sets no clinical acceptance threshold.

## Summary
Wildhagen et al. prepared 20 extracted mandibular premolars with post spaces at two depths (10 mm, 12 mm) and two diameters (ISO 090 and ISO 110), scanned them with Primescan and TRIOS 4, and compared the digital impressions against µ-CT reference scans using best-fit alignment at three cross-sections. Primescan demonstrated superior trueness (p < 0.001) and precision (p < 0.001) versus TRIOS 4 at both depths, with the advantage most pronounced at 12 mm. Increasing depth significantly degraded accuracy for both scanners (p < 0.001), while preparation diameter had no significant effect on trueness. A strong trueness–precision correlation (r = 0.838) was observed. The study is a feasibility proof-of-concept; no clinical acceptance threshold for post-space IOS accuracy is currently defined.

## Key Contributions
- First µ-CT-validated feasibility study of IOS for post preparation digitization
- Primescan > TRIOS 4 for narrow deep preparations — advantage attributable to active-triangulation vs. confocal technology in deep channels
- Depth is the dominant accuracy driver (10 → 12 mm worsens both scanners significantly); diameter is not significant for trueness
- Strong trueness–precision correlation suggests performance generalizes across specimens within scanner
- Opens pathway to eliminate conventional post impressions for root-canal-treated teeth — clinical threshold work needed next

## Methodology
In vitro comparative study. N=20 extracted mandibular premolars; coronal substance loss simulated; standard endodontic access and obturation performed; post spaces at depths 10 mm and 12 mm, diameters ISO 090 and ISO 110 (n=5 per group, 2 scanners × 2 depths × 2 diameters = 20 combinations). IOS: Primescan (Dentsply Sirona), TRIOS 4 (3Shape). Reference: µ-CT (micro-computed tomography, <10 µm resolution). Analysis: STL superimposition at 3 cross-sectional levels, mean deviation reported. Statistics: three-way ANOVA (scanner × depth × diameter), post-hoc tests, Pearson r, α=0.05.

## Results
**Trueness:** Primescan > TRIOS 4 (p < 0.001); scanner × depth interaction p < 0.001 (Primescan advantage larger at 12 mm).
**Precision:** Primescan > TRIOS 4 (p < 0.001); particularly pronounced at 12 mm depth.
**Depth effect:** 12 mm worse than 10 mm for both scanners (p < 0.001).
**Diameter:** no significant effect on trueness; minor effect on precision (p = 0.041, ISO 090 slightly worse).
**Trueness–precision correlation:** r = 0.838 (p < 0.001).
