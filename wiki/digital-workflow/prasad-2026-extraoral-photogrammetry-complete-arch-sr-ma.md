## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (J Prosthet Dent 2026, 136:e79-e101; AAFP Committee on Research in Fixed Prosthodontics; 5 databases to August 2025): 29 studies included — 3 reverse impression, 26 extraoral photogrammetry (PG); JBI quality appraisal; meta-analysis of studies reporting mean ± SD linear and angular discrepancies from extraoral PG systems.

Pooled extraoral PG accuracy: mean linear discrepancy 27 µm, angular 0.21°, linear trueness 69 µm, precision 43 µm, angular trueness 0.23°, angular precision 0.15°, RMS trueness 48 µm; reverse impression variable but generally within clinically acceptable limits.

Conclusions: extraoral PG techniques are clinically acceptable for complete-arch implant position capture; reverse impression promising but evidence limited; both methods address the long-span accuracy degradation of intraoral scanners (Intraoral Scanner, IOS).

SR+MA (J Prosthet Dent 2026, 136:e79-e101; AAFP 고정성 보철 연구위원회; 5개 DB, 2025년 8월까지): 29편 포함 — 역인상(reverse impression) 3편, 구외 광측정술 (Extraoral Photogrammetry, PG) 26편; JBI 비판적 평가; 평균 ± 표준편차 선형·각도 불일치를 보고한 연구의 메타분석.

구외 PG 풀링 정확도: 평균 선형 불일치 27µm, 각도 0.21°, 선형 진실도 69µm, 정밀도 43µm, 각도 진실도 0.23°, 각도 정밀도 0.15°, 균방평균제곱근 (Root Mean Square, RMS) 진실도 48µm; 역인상은 가변적이나 대체로 임상 허용 범위.

결론: 구외 PG 기법은 전악 임플란트 위치 채득에 임상적으로 허용 가능; 역인상은 유망하나 근거 제한적; 두 방법 모두 긴 스팬에서 IOS 정확도 저하 문제를 해결.

## Summary

First SR+MA (AAFP committee, Prasad–Revilla-León 2026) to quantitatively pool extraoral photogrammetry accuracy data for complete-arch implant scanning. Twenty-nine studies met inclusion: 3 reverse impression, 26 extraoral PG. Meta-analysis of the 26 PG studies yielded a mean linear discrepancy of 27 µm and RMS trueness of 48 µm — establishing the first evidence-based benchmark for extraoral PG in multi-implant full-arch rehabilitation. Reverse impression showed promising but highly variable accuracy; both techniques were introduced to overcome the long-span accuracy degradation observed for IOS (confirmed by Vitai 2023 NMA and Buhl 2025 in-vitro in this wiki).

## Key Contributions
- First SR+MA pooling extraoral photogrammetry trueness and precision data for complete-arch implant scanning (29 studies; AAFP committee; J Prosthet Dent 2026)
- Pooled extraoral PG: mean linear discrepancy 27 µm (acceptable for multi-unit passive fit); RMS trueness 48 µm
- Extraoral PG: "clinically acceptable for capturing implant positions" — first SR-level endorsement
- Reverse impression: promising but only 3 studies; insufficient evidence for clinical endorsement
- Directly addresses IOS long-span accuracy limitation — motivates the extraoral approach

## Methodology
- Design: SR+MA (PRISMA)
- Databases: PubMed/Medline, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane + manual search; to August 2025
- Inclusion: studies evaluating trueness and/or precision of complete-arch implant scans via reverse impression or extraoral PG
- Quality assessment: JBI critical appraisal tool, dual review, third reviewer for disagreements
- Meta-analysis: studies reporting mean ± SD linear and angular discrepancies from extraoral PG systems
- Publication bias: funnel plot + Egger test

## Implications
- Establishes extraoral PG (Icam4D, PIC, etc.) as evidence-based option for multi-implant full-arch rehabilitation where IOS accuracy is insufficient
- Clinical decision ladder (IOS ladder rung 5): "임플란트 여러 개 splinted 보철 → photogrammetry 1st choice"
- 27 µm pooled linear discrepancy is clinically meaningful for passive fit of multi-unit frameworks (tolerance threshold ~50-100 µm depending on framework span)
- Reverse impression remains unvalidated at SR level — further study needed
