## Three-line Summary
Narrative review (Cureus 2023, Saudi Arabia) covering ML, DL, and ANN applications in implant dentistry across diagnosis, treatment planning, image analysis, and implant placement guidance.

Deep learning image analysis enhances implant placement precision and reduces risk by optimizing esthetics, while AI-driven data analytics enable patient-specific treatment strategies with improved success rates.

Limited standardization across AI models and absence of a single clinical guideline warrant caution before widespread clinical adoption of AI-driven implant workflows.

## 세줄요약
서사 리뷰 (Cureus 2023, 사우디아라비아): ML·DL·ANN을 활용한 임플란트 치의학 진단, 치료계획, 영상 분석, 식립 가이드 전반을 다룬 리뷰.

딥러닝 영상 분석이 임플란트 식립 정밀도를 높이고 위험을 줄이며 심미성을 최적화하고, AI 기반 데이터 분석이 환자별 치료 전략과 성공률 향상을 가능하게 한다.

AI 모델 간 표준화 부족과 단일 임상 지침의 부재로 인해 AI 기반 임플란트 워크플로우의 광범위한 임상 도입에 주의가 필요하다.

## Summary
Narrative review (Saudi Arabia) on AI transformation in implant dentistry. Covers ML, DL, and ANN applications for diagnostics, treatment planning, and patient-specific strategies. AI promises personalized, efficient oral healthcare but lacks standardized clinical validation.

## Key Contributions
- AI applications: diagnosis, treatment planning, image analysis, implant placement guidance
- **DL image analysis**: enhances precision of implant placement; reduces risks; optimizes esthetics
- **AI-driven data analytics**: patient-specific treatment strategies; improved success rates
- Promise: personalized, efficient oral healthcare transformation
- Limitation: limited standardization across AI models; no single clinical guideline
