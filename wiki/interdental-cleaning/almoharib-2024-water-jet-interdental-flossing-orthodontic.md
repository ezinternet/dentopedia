## Three-line Summary

Single-blind, randomized, parallel clinical trial (n=30 active fixed-orthodontic patients, King Saud University, Riyadh) comparing water jet flossing (n=18) vs conventional interdental flossing (n=12), each combined with manual toothbrushing, over 2 weeks.

Both groups significantly reduced Plaque Index and Gingival Bleeding Index from baseline to day 14 (interdental: PI −16.13%, BI −23.57%, both p≤0.01; water jet: PI −21.87%, BI −32.29%, both p<0.0001); the between-group difference favored water jet flossing numerically but was not statistically significant (PI p=0.279, BI p=0.172).

Water jet flossing and interdental flossing are both effective adjuncts to toothbrushing for orthodontic patients with no significant superiority of one over the other; small sample size (n=30, unequal groups 12 vs 18) and short 2-week follow-up limit generalizability and preclude long-term/gingival-index-grade conclusions.

## 세줄요약

단일맹검 무작위 병렬(parallel) 임상시험(교정 고정장치 활성 치료 중인 환자 n=30, 사우디 킹사우드대): 워터젯 플로싱(water jet flosser, n=18) vs 전통적 치간치실(interdental floss, n=12)을 칫솔질과 병용해 2주간 비교.

두 군 모두 기저치 대비 치태지수(Plaque Index, PI)와 치은출혈지수(Gingival Bleeding Index, BI)가 유의하게 감소(치실군: PI −16.13%, BI −23.57%, 모두 p≤0.01; 워터젯군: PI −21.87%, BI −32.29%, 모두 p<0.0001); 군간 비교에서는 워터젯이 수치상 우세했으나 통계적으로 유의하지 않음(PI p=0.279, BI p=0.172).

워터젯 플로싱과 치간치실 모두 교정 환자의 칫솔질 보조 도구로 효과적이며 어느 한쪽의 유의한 우월성은 없음; 소규모 표본(n=30, 12 vs 18 불균형)과 짧은 2주 추적기간이 일반화 가능성을 제한하고 치은지수(Gingival Index, GI) 등급 변화 등 장기적 결론은 도출할 수 없다.

## Summary

Fixed orthodontic appliances trap plaque around brackets, archwires, and elastics, increasing risk of gingival inflammation, decalcification, and soft-tissue recession. This single-blind, randomized, parallel-group RCT compared two patient-administered interdental cleaning aids — a **water jet flosser** and **conventional interdental (string) flossing**, each combined with manual toothbrushing — in 30 patients undergoing active fixed-orthodontic treatment at King Saud University, Riyadh. Plaque Index (PI), Gingival Bleeding Index (BI), and Gingival Index (GI) grade were recorded at six representative teeth at baseline and after 2 weeks.

Both interventions produced statistically significant reductions in PI and BI from baseline to 2 weeks. The water jet flossing group showed numerically larger reductions (PI −21.87% vs −16.13%; BI −32.29% vs −23.57%), but the **between-group difference was not statistically significant** for either PI (p=0.279) or BI (p=0.172). Neither group showed a significant shift in GI grade distribution over the 2-week period. The authors conclude both methods are reliable, effective adjuncts for orthodontic oral hygiene, with water jet flossing showing only a non-significant numerical edge.

## Key Contributions

- A parallel-group (not split-mouth) RCT directly comparing water jet flossing vs interdental flossing in active fixed-orthodontic patients — a design that complements the split-mouth super-floss-vs-water-flosser comparison already in the wiki.
- Uses three concurrent periodontal indices (BI, PI, GI grade) at six FDI-numbered index teeth, at baseline and 2-week follow-up.
- Independently reinforces the broader orthodontic interdental-cleaning literature's conclusion: water flossers/oral irrigators are **not statistically superior** to string floss, though both are effective adjuncts to brushing.
- Reports the actual (unequal, 12 vs 18) randomization outcome despite an equal-group sample-size calculation, a useful methodological caveat for readers of small orthodontic RCTs.

## Methodology

- **Design**: single-blind (clinician-blinded), randomized, parallel clinical trial; TCTR20230926005; CONSORT-followed.
- **Population**: n=30 adults (≥18 y) undergoing active fixed-orthodontic treatment with full-mouth brackets/archwires, ≥24 natural teeth, <5 mm anterior crowding/spacing, adequate manual dexterity, good periodontal health; excluded systemic disease, gingiva-altering medications, active periodontal disease, removable appliances, no-mouthwash-use.
- **Groups**: (1) water jet flosser + manual toothbrush + fluoridated toothpaste (n=18); (2) interdental floss + manual toothbrush + fluoridated toothpaste (n=12). Toothbrushing 2×/day for 2 min; assigned interdental aid once daily at night; no other hygiene aids permitted.
- **Outcomes**: Bleeding Index (BI), Plaque Index (PI, both presence/absence across 4 surfaces), Gingival Index (GI, 0–3 grade) at 6 FDI index teeth (16, 12, 24, 36, 32, 44), baseline and day 14.
- **Analysis**: SPSS v26; Mann-Whitney U (between-group) and Wilcoxon signed-rank (within-group) for non-normal PI/BI; Fisher's exact test for GI grade distribution; significance at p≤0.05.
- **Key limitation**: small, unequal-sized groups (12 vs 18) and only a 2-week follow-up window — no long-term or compliance data.

## Results

| Measure | Interdental floss (n=12) | Water jet (n=18) | Between-group p |
|---|---|---|---|
| PI baseline (median %) | 37.50 | 36.45 | 0.932 |
| PI 2-week (median %) | 21.37 | 14.58 | 0.279 |
| BI baseline (median %) | 47.91 | (mean 5.61 pts higher) | 0.518 |
| BI 2-week (median %) | 20.83 | 15.62 | 0.172 |
| PI change, baseline→2wk (median % diff) | 16.13 (p=0.010) | 21.87 (p<0.0001) | — |
| BI change, baseline→2wk (median % diff) | 23.57 (p=0.002) | 32.29 (p<0.0001) | — |

- GI grade distribution: interdental group 83.3%→91.7% grade 2 (16.7%→8.3% grade 3); water jet group 94.4%→100% grade 2 (5.6%→0% grade 3) — no significant within- or between-group GI change in either group.
- Both aids produced statistically significant within-group PI/BI reduction; neither aid was statistically superior to the other between groups.
