## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Aust Endod J 2026) of risk factors for vertical root fracture (VRF) in endodontically treated teeth, split into treatment-related factors (instrument-induced dentinal microcracks, dentine volume and fracture resistance after access cavity/canal preparation) and occlusal factors (high-risk occlusal forces, root stress changes from crown morphology and crown restoration).

The abstract reports no numeric results; its main finding is an evidence-imbalance statement: occlusal analysis techniques and force measurement methods remain limited, with far fewer studies than on treatment-related risk factors.

The review proposes flexible sensor technology as a future route to measure occlusal risk factors; as an abstract-only narrative review with no stated search method, it supports framing and research direction, not clinical thresholds.

## 세줄요약

내러티브 리뷰 (Narrative review; Aust Endod J 2026) — 근관치료치 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)의 위험인자를 치료 관련 요인(기구에 의한 상아질 미세균열 (Dentinal Microcrack), 접근와동·근관 성형에 따른 상아질 용적·파절저항성 변화)과 교합 요인(고위험 교합력, 치관 형태·치관 수복에 따른 치근 응력 분포 변화)으로 나누어 정리.

초록에는 수치 결과가 없으며, 핵심 메시지는 근거의 불균형: 교합 분석 기법과 교합력 측정 방법은 제한적이어서 치료 관련 위험인자 연구보다 연구 수가 훨씬 적음.

향후 교합 위험인자 탐색에 유연 센서 (Flexible Sensor) 기술을 제안; 초록만 확인된 내러티브 리뷰이고 검색 방법이 명시되지 않아 임상 기준치가 아니라 문제 틀 짜기·연구 방향 근거로만 사용 가능.

## Summary

> The PMC record returned an empty body, so the full text was not retrieved; content below is grounded in the PubMed abstract only ([DOI 10.1111/aej.70090](https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.70090)). abstract-only — full text not retrieved.

According to PubMed, this narrative review from Nanjing University addresses risk factors for vertical root fracture (VRF) in endodontically treated teeth, organized as treatment-related and occlusal factors. The treatment-related literature it covers is experimental: dentinal microcracks produced by different canal preparation instruments, and changes in dentine volume and fracture resistance after different access cavity and canal preparation approaches. The occlusal literature covers high-risk occlusal forces and how crown morphology and crown restoration alter root stress distribution. The authors state that occlusal analysis and force measurement methods remain limited, so occlusal studies are far fewer than treatment-related ones, and they propose flexible sensor technology for future occlusal risk-factor work. The abstract gives no effect sizes or the direction of individual findings, so it cannot be used to settle the access-cavity or shaping-size question on its own.

## Key Contributions

- Frames VRF risk in endodontically treated teeth as two axes: treatment-related and occlusal.
- Names the evidence gap on the occlusal axis (limited analysis and force measurement methods; far fewer studies).
- Proposes flexible sensor technology as the future measurement route for occlusal risk factors.
