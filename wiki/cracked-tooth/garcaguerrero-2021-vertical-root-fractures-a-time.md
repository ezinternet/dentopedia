## Three-line Summary

Nested case-control study (STROBE; 90 surgically confirmed VRF cases vs 270 time-matched controls among endodontically treated teeth, two Colombian university postgraduate endodontic clinics) analysing clinical risk factors in three post-treatment time windows (1–5, 6–10, 11–17 years).

Retreatment (versus primary treatment) raised VRF odds in the 1–5 year window (OR 12.19, 95% CI 3.64–40.81) and the 6–10 year window (OR 4.28, 95% CI 1.08–17.07); intracanal medication (calcium hydroxide) was associated with VRF only after >10 years (OR 8.55, 95% CI 2.06–35.47); intraradicular posts were protective in the first 10 years (OR 0.22 and 0.26); 53.3% of cases appeared within 5 years.

Risk factors act at different times, so surveillance of retreated teeth is most critical in the first 5 years; limitations are retrospective records from two clinics, surgically confirmed cases only (selection toward teeth that were explored), wide confidence intervals and internal inconsistencies in the reported cohort size and prevalence.

## 세줄요약

중첩 환자-대조군 연구 (Nested case-control; STROBE; 콜롬비아 2개 대학 근관치료 대학원 클리닉; 수술로 확인된 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 90례 vs 시간 매칭 대조 270개, 근관치료치) — 치료 후 3개 시간대 (1–5년, 6–10년, 11–17년)별 임상 위험요인 분석.

재근관치료 (Retreatment)는 초회 치료 대비 1–5년 구간 (OR 12.19, 95% CI 3.64–40.81)과 6–10년 구간 (OR 4.28, 95% CI 1.08–17.07)에서 VRF 위험을 높였고, 근관 내 약제 (수산화칼슘, Calcium Hydroxide)는 10년 초과 구간에서만 연관 (OR 8.55, 95% CI 2.06–35.47); 근관 내 포스트 (Post)는 처음 10년간 보호 요인 (OR 0.22, 0.26); 전체 사례의 53.3%가 5년 이내 발생.

위험요인마다 작용 시점이 달라, 재근관치료 치아는 처음 5년 추적이 가장 중요함; 한계는 후향적 기록, 수술 탐색이 이뤄진 치아만 사례로 확정한 선택 편향, 넓은 신뢰구간, 코호트 크기·유병률 보고의 내적 불일치.

## Summary

This nested case-control study from two Colombian university endodontic postgraduate clinics compared 90 endodontically treated teeth with surgically confirmed vertical root fracture (VRF) against 270 time-matched controls, stratifying analysis by time since treatment (1–5, 6–10, 11–17 years). Retreatment carried the dominant early risk (OR 12.19 at 1–5 years; OR 4.28 at 6–10 years), intraradicular posts were protective in the first decade, and intracanal calcium hydroxide became significant only beyond 10 years (OR 8.55). Over half of the cases (53.3%) appeared within five years of treatment. The authors conclude that retreatment is a primary and intracanal medication a secondary VRF risk factor, but the design (surgically confirmed cases only, wide CIs, reporting inconsistencies) limits how firmly these ORs can be transferred to practice.

## Key Contributions

- Time-stratified view of VRF risk after root canal treatment rather than a single pooled estimate.
- Retreatment as the early (first 5–10 years) risk factor; long-term calcium hydroxide dressing as a late (>10 years) risk factor.
- Posts associated with *lower* VRF odds (OR 0.22 and 0.26) in the first 10 years.
- Time-to-diagnosis distribution: 53.3% (1–5 y), 28.8% (6–10 y), 17.7% (≥11 y).

## Methodology

Nested case-control within a reconstructed cohort of endodontically treated teeth (records to 2020; cohort stated as 481 in Methods/Results and 450 in the Abstract). Cases: VRF confirmed by flap elevation and operating-microscope inspection. Controls: clinically and radiographically normal treated teeth, three per case, matched on time since treatment. Exclusions: orthodontic treatment, trauma, caries, coronal fracture. Exposures: tooth type, treatment type (primary/retreatment/apical surgery), appointments, intracanal medication, filling extension, restoration, post, adjacent implant. Chi-square plus adjusted logistic regression per time group.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Retreatment vs primary, 1–5 y | OR 12.19 (95% CI 3.64–40.81), P<0.0001 |
| Retreatment vs primary, 6–10 y | OR 4.28 (95% CI 1.08–17.07), P=0.039 |
| Apical surgery vs primary | Trend only (OR 3.65 and 4.76; P=0.06 and 0.085) |
| Intraradicular post, 1–5 y / 6–10 y | OR 0.22 (0.08–0.57) / OR 0.26 (0.09–0.78) — protective |
| Number of appointments, 1–5 y | OR 0.36 (0.17–0.79) |
| Intracanal medication, >10 y | OR 8.55 (2.06–35.47), P=0.003 (bivariate OR 6.16) |
| Case timing | 53.3% / 28.8% / 17.7% in 1–5 / 6–10 / ≥11 y |
| Molar vs premolar share of VRF | 42% vs 30%, not significant |

Caveats: the "16.42% prevalence" is not estimable from a 1:3 matched case-control sample; the Abstract's "retreatment ... five to ten years" disagrees with the Results (largest effect at 1–5 years); the Group 2 narrative states a 12% reduction in fracture probability for retreated teeth, contradicting the positive OR. Calcium hydroxide causation rests on cited in vitro work, not on this study.
