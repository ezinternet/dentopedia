## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo diagnostic-accuracy study: 18 endodontically treated single-rooted human teeth (8 with induced vertical root fracture, 10 intact) placed in an anthropomorphic phantom and scanned without a post, with a Ni-Cr post and with a Co-Cr post on one photon-counting CT (NAEOTOM Alpha) and four CBCT devices (270 scans), read blind by 5 oral and maxillofacial radiologists.

NewTom VGi evo and PCCT had significantly higher AUC than Veraview X800 regardless of post material (e.g. Ni-Cr AUC 0.71 NewTom, 0.64 PCCT, 0.48 Veraview); with a Ni-Cr post PCCT sensitivity was 0.65 vs 0.22 (3D Accuitomo) and 0.10 (Veraview), and sensitivity did not change significantly across post conditions for NewTom, CS9600 or PCCT; specificity did not differ among devices or conditions.

The authors conclude PCCT performs comparably to high-resolution CBCT under controlled ex vivo conditions and propose it only as an incidental, secondary read when a PCCT scan already exists, not as a replacement for CBCT (higher dose, cost, 0.2 mm voxel); AUC values were modest (0.48-0.76 with wide SDs), 8 fractured teeth, one PCCT system, no clinical predictors, so patient-level accuracy is unknown.

## Summary

This ex vivo study (Federal University of Paraná, UNICAMP, KU Leuven) compared a photon-counting CT scanner (NAEOTOM Alpha, 0.2 mm voxel) with four CBCT devices pre-selected for acceptable metal-artefact behaviour for detecting vertical root fractures (VRF) in 18 extracted teeth, 8 of them fractured with a universal testing machine. Each root was scanned in a soft-tissue-equivalent phantom without a post, with a nickel-chromium post and with a cobalt-chromium post (270 scans), and five radiologists scored them blind. PCCT and the NewTom VGi evo had significantly higher AUC than the Veraview X800 regardless of post material, and with metal posts PCCT, CS9600 and NewTom kept sensitivity at about 0.57-0.72 while Accuitomo (0.22-0.40) and Veraview (0.10) dropped; specificity did not differ. The authors regard PCCT as a possible incidental read when a hospital PCCT of the dental arches already exists, not as a replacement for CBCT, because of dose, cost and the 0.2 mm detectability floor. Absolute performance was modest (AUC 0.48-0.76), fractures were few and artificially induced, and the finding is from a single PCCT system.

## Key Contributions

- First PCCT vs multi-device CBCT comparison for VRF in teeth with metal posts (authors' claim); in the wiki it is the only PCCT paper.
- Device dependence of the metal-post penalty: sensitivity fell significantly with posts on Accuitomo and Veraview, not on NewTom, CS9600 or PCCT.
- Radiation-justification framing: use PCCT for VRF only when a scan already exists for another indication.

## Methodology

- **Design**: ex vivo diagnostic-accuracy study (PRILE 2021); 18 teeth (8 VRF induced by Instron 8802, confirmed by transillumination; 10 intact), prepared with Reciproc Blue 40.06; roots in an anthropomorphic phantom (skull plus mandible with Mix D).
- **Conditions x devices**: no post, Ni-Cr post, Co-Cr post x PCCT (0.2 mm voxel), 3D Accuitomo 170 (0.08), Veraview X800 (0.08), NewTom VGi evo (0.125), Carestream 9600 (0.075); 270 scans.
- **Readers / analysis**: 5 radiologists, 5-point scale, generic DICOM viewer; AUC, sensitivity, specificity; two-way ANOVA with Tukey (alpha 0.05); kappa agreement; post hoc power 90%.

## Results

Device order: Accuitomo / Veraview / NewTom / CS9600 / PCCT (column headers were lost in the PMC text export; order from Table 1 and checked against the Results text).

| Metric (mean) | Condition | Accuitomo | Veraview | NewTom | CS9600 | PCCT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AUC | No post | 0.73 | 0.53 | 0.76 | 0.70 | 0.76 |
| AUC | Ni-Cr | 0.52 | 0.48 | 0.71 | 0.67 | 0.64 |
| AUC | Co-Cr | 0.60 | 0.51 | 0.70 | 0.65 | 0.70 |
| Sensitivity | No post | 0.67 | 0.47 | 0.75 | 0.47 | 0.75 |
| Sensitivity | Ni-Cr | 0.22 | 0.10 | 0.62 | 0.72 | 0.65 |
| Sensitivity | Co-Cr | 0.40 | 0.10 | 0.62 | 0.67 | 0.57 |
| Specificity | Ni-Cr | 0.80 | 0.86 | 0.78 | 0.62 | 0.66 |
| Specificity | Co-Cr | 0.80 | 0.92 | 0.70 | 0.60 | 0.82 |

- AUC: NewTom and PCCT significantly higher than Veraview for both post materials (p < 0.05); Accuitomo and CS9600 intermediate.
- Sensitivity: no device differences without a post; with Ni-Cr, CS9600 and PCCT significantly higher than Accuitomo and Veraview; with Co-Cr, NewTom, CS9600 and PCCT higher than Veraview. Within device, NewTom, CS9600 and PCCT showed no significant change across conditions.
- Specificity: no significant differences (p > 0.05). Inter-examiner kappa 0.34-0.70; intra-examiner 0.45-0.78.
- Caveats: AUC SDs up to 0.31; Veraview read in a non-native viewer; ex vivo with no clinical predictors (PDL widening, bone defects, symptoms); PCCT must see a fracture of at least about 0.2 mm width.
