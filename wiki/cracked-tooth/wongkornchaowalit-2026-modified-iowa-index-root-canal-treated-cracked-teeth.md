## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 263 root canal-treated cracked posterior teeth (1–5 years, mean 40.3 months) staged with a modified Iowa Index that subdivides each stage by crack extension. Success 82.9%, survival 89.7%; 5-year estimated survival 78.6%; Stage IV-R (radicular crack plus pocket ≥5 mm) had survival 63.0% and success 33.3%, versus 86.5–100% survival in stages I–III. Abstract-only — effect sizes for the seven independent predictors were not available, and 1–5-year follow-up limits long-term inference.

## 세줄요약

근관치료를 받은 구치부 균열치아 263개를 1~5년(평균 40.3개월) 추적한 후향적 코호트로, 각 Iowa 단계를 균열 범위로 세분화한 수정 Iowa 지수를 적용하였다. 성공률 82.9%, 생존율 89.7%, 5년 추정 생존율 78.6%였고, 근단 방향 균열과 치주낭 ≥5 mm가 겹친 IV-R 단계는 생존율 63.0%, 성공률 33.3%로 I~III단계(생존율 86.5~100%)와 크게 달랐다. 초록만 확보하여 7개 독립 예측인자의 효과크기는 알 수 없고, 추적기간이 1~5년으로 짧다.

## Summary

abstract-only — full text not retrieved. This single-cohort study evaluated outcomes of root canal treatment in 263 cracked posterior teeth and asked which factors predict failure. The Iowa Index was modified by subcategorizing each stage by crack extension, and outcomes were judged with strict clinical and radiographic criteria. Cox regression identified seven independent failure predictors. Teeth with probing depths <5 mm (Stages I–III) did well, whereas teeth with both radicular extension and a ≥5 mm pocket (Stage IV-R) had a poor prognosis. The authors propose treating that combination as a high-risk profile when judging treatment potential.

## Key Contributions

1. Adds a crack-extension axis to the Iowa Index and shows stage-wise prognostic separation.
2. Names seven independent failure predictors, including modifiable ones (occlusal splint in parafunction; restoration type).
3. Defines Stage IV-R as the high-risk profile (survival 63.0%, success 33.3%).

## Methodology

- Retrospective cohort, 263 cracked posterior teeth, 1–5 year follow-up (mean 40.3 months)
- Strict clinical and radiographic success criteria; survival defined separately
- Cox regression for failure predictors
- Other methodological details not retrieved (abstract-only)

## Results

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Overall success | 82.9% |
| Overall survival | 89.7% |
| Estimated survival, 2 y / 5 y | 99.2% / 78.6% |
| Stages I–III (PD <5 mm) success / survival | 78.9–98.3% / 86.5–100% |
| Stage IV-R success / survival | 33.3% / 63.0% |

Independent predictors of failure: increasing age; pocket depth ≥5 mm along the crack line; radicular extension ≥3 mm; preoperative periapical lesion; multiple crack lines; no occlusal splint in patients with parafunctional habits; restoration with onlay or composite.
