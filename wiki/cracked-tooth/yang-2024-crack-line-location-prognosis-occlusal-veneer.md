## Three-line Summary

Prospective study (n=80) classifying crack lines as Type I (within occlusal veneer preparation boundary) vs Type II (beyond boundary) to predict pulp vitality outcomes after occlusal veneer treatment.

Type II cracks — extending beyond the veneer preparation margin — showed significantly inferior pulp prognosis, providing the first crack-location-based classification for stratifying occlusal veneer treatment risk.

The study supports using crack line location identified during preparation as an intraoperative decision point for patient counseling and pulp risk stratification when selecting occlusal veneer as the restorative approach.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상 연구(n=80): 균열선이 교합 비니어 삭제 경계 내(Type I) vs 초과(Type II)로 분류되며, Type II가 치수 활력 예후 열등.

Type II 균열은 치수 합병증 위험이 유의하게 높아, 교합 비니어 선택 전 균열선 위치 확인이 핵심 임상 결정 요인임을 처음 전향적으로 입증.

교합 비니어 삭제 시 균열선 위치를 Type I/II로 분류해 환자 상담 및 치수 위험도 층화에 활용할 것을 권고.

## One-line Summary

Prospective study (n=80): crack line location relative to occlusal veneer preparation boundary (Type I: within; Type II: beyond) is a significant predictor of pulp vitality prognosis.

## 한줄요약

전향적 연구(n=80): 균열선 위치 Type I(삭제 내부)·Type II(경계 초과)가 교합 비니어 후 치수 예후의 유의한 분류 기준.

## Summary

Yang et al. conducted a prospective study on 80 cracked teeth receiving occlusal veneer preparation, introducing a pragmatic crack classification: Type I (crack line contained within preparation boundaries) and Type II (crack line extending beyond preparation boundaries). Type II cracks were associated with significantly inferior pulp vitality outcomes, demonstrating that crack extent relative to preparation margin is a clinically meaningful prognostic factor. This intraoperative classification enables real-time risk stratification during tooth preparation, providing the first prospective evidence for using crack line position to guide patient counseling about pulp risk when occlusal veneer is the chosen restorative approach.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces Type I/II crack classification based on relationship to occlusal veneer preparation boundary — a clinically determinable intraoperative variable
- First prospective evidence that crack line location predicts pulp vitality prognosis after occlusal veneer treatment
- Supports occlusal veneer as a viable conservative option for Type I cracks
- Provides patient counseling framework: Type II → higher pulp risk disclosure required
- Complements outcome data from cuspal coverage studies (Malentacca 2026, Zhang 2024 SR+MA) with a within-category prognostic stratifier

## Methodology

Prospective clinical study, n=80 cracked teeth. Occlusal veneer preparation performed; crack line location assessed intraoperatively and classified as Type I (within preparation boundary) vs Type II (extends beyond). Primary outcome: pulp vitality status at follow-up. Study conducted at single center; follow-up duration not specified in abstract. J Dent Sci (Elsevier/Taiwan Association of Dental Sciences), 2024.

## Results

- Type II (crack beyond preparation boundary): significantly inferior pulp vitality prognosis vs Type I
- Crack line location = independent predictor of pulp outcome (P<.05 inferred)
- Specific effect sizes (OR, HR, AUC) and survival rates not available from abstract
