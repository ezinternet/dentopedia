## Three-line Summary

A retrospective cohort of 561 cracked teeth at Taihe Hospital identified the distal marginal ridge as the highest-risk crack location (failure rate 16.81%) and established two critical pulpitis progression windows at 0–1 month (48.97%) and 3–6 months (27.84%). For early-stage cracked teeth, full-coverage crowns achieved a 1-year success rate of 78.05% versus 50.00% for composite resin (χ²=5.676, P<0.05). Multivariable logistic regression confirmed full-coverage crown (OR=5.57) and normal pulp status (OR=4.52) as independent protective factors for treatment success.

## 세줄요약

Taihe Hospital의 561증례 후향적 코호트에서 원심변연융선 균열(39.45%)이 가장 높은 실패율(16.81%)을 보였으며, 치수염 진행의 두 위험 창(0~1개월: 48.97%, 3~6개월: 27.84%)을 확인하였다. 조기 균열치아에서 전장관(Full-Coverage Crown, FCC)의 1년 성공률(78.05%)은 복합레진(50.00%)보다 유의하게 높았다. 다변량 분석에서 전장관(OR=5.57, 95%CI 1.44–21.56)과 정상 치수 상태(OR=4.52, 95%CI 1.23–16.69)가 독립적 보호인자로 확인되었다.

## Summary

This retrospective cohort enrolled 561 patients diagnosed with cracked teeth between 2021 and 2024 at a single center. The 40–49-year age group was most affected (29.59%), and maxillary first molars were the most commonly involved tooth (37.01%), with occlusal discomfort as the predominant symptom (72.73%). Cracks involving the distal marginal ridge were most frequent and carried the highest failure rate. A subgroup of 67 early-stage cases treated with composite resin or full-coverage crowns revealed a clinically and statistically significant advantage for full-coverage crowns at 1 year, confirmed by multivariable logistic regression adjusting for pulp status and other covariates.

## Key Contributions

- Two critical pulpitis progression windows: **0–1 month** (48.97%) and **3–6 months** (27.84%) — these are the intervals of highest risk if untreated
- Distal marginal ridge crack direction has the highest failure rate (16.81%, χ²=16.285, P<0.05) among all crack locations
- Full-coverage crown success 78.05% vs composite resin 50.00% for early-stage teeth (χ²=5.676, P<0.05)
- Independent protective factors: full-coverage crown (OR=5.57, 95%CI 1.44–21.56) and normal pulp status (OR=4.52, 95%CI 1.23–16.69)
- Epidemiological profile: middle-aged adults (40–49 y), maxillary first molars, occlusal discomfort as primary presentation

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, single-center (Taihe Hospital)
- **N**: 561 patients (309 males 55.08%, 252 females 44.92%)
- **Period**: June 1, 2021 – December 31, 2024
- **Subgroup**: 67 early-stage cracked teeth treated with composite resin (n not specified) or full-coverage crowns (n not specified)
- **Primary outcome**: 1-year treatment success = normal occlusal function + symptom-free + no tooth mobility
- **Statistics**: Chi-square / Fisher's exact (bivariate); multivariable logistic regression (prognostic factors)
