## Three-line Summary

Case-control study (n=83 cracked molars + 83 matched controls) using 3D intraoral scans and digital design software to quantify occlusal morphology differences between cracked and intact molars.

Maxillary cracked teeth showed crack-initiation-side (CIS) functional cuspal inclination of 31.87° vs 26.01° in controls; mandibular CIS functional cuspal height independently carried OR=8.177 in multivariate regression — the highest single predictor identified.

Steeper and taller functional cusps on the crack initiation side are independently associated with molar cracking, providing the first reference intervals for morphology-guided risk detection and occlusal adjustment planning.

## 세줄요약

3D 인트라오럴 스캔 형태계측 증례-대조 연구(n=83+83): 균열치 대구치는 정상 대조군 대비 기능교두 경사도가 CIS에서 약 4~6° 가파르고 높이가 0.2~0.4 mm 더 높다.

다변량 분석에서 하악 CIS 기능교두 높이 OR=8.177로 단일 지표 중 최고 위험이며, 상악 CIS 경사도 OR=1.098, 상악 CPS 높이 OR=3.407.

이 수치들이 임상에서 교두 형태 기반 균열 위험 조기 탐지 및 교합 조정 계획에 활용할 수 있는 최초의 정량적 참조 기준을 제시한다.

## One-line Summary

Case-control 3D morphometric study (n=83+83) demonstrating that steeper and taller functional cusps — especially CIS cuspal height on mandibular molars (OR=8.177) — independently predict molar cracking.

## 한줄요약

3D 형태계측 증례-대조 연구(n=83+83): 하악 CIS 기능교두 높이(OR=8.177)를 포함, 기능교두 경사도·높이가 균열 대구치의 독립적 위험인자.

## Summary

Deng et al. used 3D intraoral scans (Trios, 3Shape) and digital design software to compare occlusal morphology between 83 cracked molars (68 patients) and 83 matched intact molar controls (69 volunteers). Crack initiation side (CIS) and propagation side (CPS) were identified separately. Paired t-tests detected significantly steeper functional cuspal inclination and greater functional cuspal height on the CIS in both arches. Functional cusp slopes were paradoxically flatter in cracked teeth. Multivariate logistic regression identified CIS inclination (OR 1.098) and CPS height (OR 3.407) as independent predictors in the maxillary arch, and CIS functional cuspal height (OR 8.177) in the mandibular arch. The study provides the first quantitative reference intervals linking specific cusp geometry to cracking risk, with clinical implications for early risk stratification and occlusal morphology-guided adjustment.

## Key Contributions

- First 3D morphometric quantification of CIS vs CPS cusp geometry as independent cracking risk factors
- Mandibular CIS functional cuspal height OR=8.177 — highest single predictor in either arch
- Identified direction asymmetry: CIS effects dominate; CPS height significant only in maxillary arch
- Functional cusp slopes are flatter in cracked teeth — counter-intuitive finding adding nuance to "steep cusp = crack risk" dogma
- Provides clinical thresholds: maxillary functional cusp inclination ~26° and mandibular ~24.5° as reference means for intact controls

## Methodology

Paired case-control design. 83 cracked permanent molars from 68 patients vs 83 matched controls from 69 volunteers. Matching criteria not detailed in abstract. Intraoral scans acquired with Trios (3Shape); measurements performed in 3D digital design software. Variables: functional/nonfunctional cuspal inclination (angle to occlusal plane), height (tip to cervical landmark), and slope; CIS and CPS classified per clinical examination. Statistical analysis: paired t-test for between-group morphology comparison; multivariate logistic regression for independent OR estimation.

## Results

**Maxillary arch:**
- CIS functional cuspal inclination: cracked 31.87±7.72° vs control 26.01±7.01° (P<.05)
- CPS functional cuspal inclination: cracked 29.03±7.50° vs control 25.61±7.47° (P<.05)
- Functional cuspal heights 0.2–0.4 mm higher in cracked teeth (P<.05)
- Multivariate OR: CIS inclination 1.098 (per degree), CPS height 3.407

**Mandibular arch:**
- CIS functional cuspal inclination: cracked 30.79±7.89° vs control 24.53±6.97° (P<.05)
- Functional cuspal heights 0.2–0.4 mm higher in cracked teeth (P<.05)
- Multivariate OR: CIS functional cuspal height 8.177

**Both arches:** functional cusp slopes flatter in cracked teeth.
