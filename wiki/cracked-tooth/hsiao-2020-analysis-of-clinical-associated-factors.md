## Three-line Summary

Retrospective chart-based cohort (Taipei Medical University Hospital, 2012-2018; 359 endodontically treated, symptomatic teeth from 275 patients that underwent microscope-assisted endodontic microsurgery with methylene-blue staining; VRF found in 67 teeth, 18.7% on a tooth basis, 22.5% on a patient basis).

In multivariable logistic regression, age >50 years (adjusted OR 3.20, 95% CI 1.81-5.64) and molars (adjusted OR 4.31, 2.24-8.27) or premolars (adjusted OR 2.61, 1.16-5.86) versus incisors were significant; gender, root canal treatment history, restoration type and post presence/type/extension were not; fractured molar roots were mainly maxillary mesiobuccal (15/16) and mandibular mesial (9/13).

Age and tooth type flag teeth in which VRF should be suspected, but the sample contains only surgery-referred failing teeth with no non-endodontically treated or extracted comparators, so prevalence and null findings (post, restoration) are not generalisable.

## 세줄요약

후향적 의무기록 코호트(대만 타이베이의대병원 2012-2018; 증상이 지속되는 근관치료치 중 현미경 하 치근단 미세수술을 받은 359개 치아, 275명, 메틸렌블루 염색으로 확인) — 수직치근파절(Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)은 67개 치아로 치아 기준 18.7%, 환자 기준 22.5%.

다변량 로지스틱 회귀에서 50세 초과(보정 OR 3.20, 95% CI 1.81-5.64), 대구치(보정 OR 4.31, 2.24-8.27)와 소구치(보정 OR 2.61, 1.16-5.86; 절치 대비)가 유의했고, 성별·근관치료 이력·수복물 종류·포스트(Post) 유무/종류/연장은 유의하지 않았으며, 대구치 파절 치근은 상악 근심협측근(15/16)과 하악 근심근(9/13)에 집중됨.

연령과 치아 종류는 VRF를 의심할 단서이나, 대상이 수술 의뢰된 실패 치아뿐이고 비근관치료치·발치된 치아 대조가 없어 유병률과 포스트·수복물의 무효 결과는 일반화하기 어려움.

## Summary

This retrospective chart review from Taipei Medical University Hospital examined 359 endodontically treated, persistently symptomatic teeth (275 patients) that underwent microscope-assisted endodontic microsurgery with methylene-blue staining, and found vertical root fracture (VRF) in 67 teeth (18.7% per tooth, 22.5% per patient). Age over 50 years (adjusted OR 3.20) and molar (adjusted OR 4.31) or premolar (adjusted OR 2.61) tooth type, relative to incisors, were the only independent associated factors; gender, root canal treatment history, restoration type and post characteristics were not significant. Within fractured molars, the maxillary mesiobuccal root (15/16) and mandibular mesial root (9/13) were almost always the site. The sample contains only surgery-referred failing teeth, so it supports vigilance for VRF in older patients and premolars/molars rather than providing population prevalence or evidence that posts are harmless.

## Key Contributions

- Primary microsurgery-confirmed cohort data with adjusted ORs for age and tooth type, across all tooth classes.
- Null associations for gender, tooth location, root canal treatment history, restoration type and post presence/type/extension (all p>0.05; fibre posts 0/13, all in incisors).
- Root-site distribution in fractured molars (maxillary mesiobuccal 93.7%, mandibular mesial 69.2%), matching the sibling reviews.

## Methodology

Single-centre retrospective chart and radiograph review (Jan 2012-Jul 2018); inclusion: endodontically treated teeth with persistent signs/symptoms undergoing endodontic microsurgery; exclusion: incomplete data. VRF confirmed by methylene-blue staining and microscope inspection. Pearson chi-square, then univariate and multivariable binary logistic regression (SPSS 22). Variables: age (<=50 vs >=51), gender, tooth type/location, treatment history (non-surgical vs non-surgical plus surgical), restoration, post.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| VRF prevalence | 67/359 teeth (18.7%); 62/275 patients (22.5%) |
| Age >=51 vs <=50 | 31.9% vs 12.6%; adjusted OR 3.20 (1.81-5.64) |
| Molars / premolars / canines vs incisors | 33.7% / 25.0% / 23.8% vs 10.6%; adjusted OR 4.31 / 2.61 / 2.28 (canine NS, p=0.154) |
| Gender, location, RCT history | NS (p=0.388, 0.189, 0.134) |
| Cuspal coverage vs direct restoration | 20.8% vs 13.0%, crude OR 1.76 (0.92-3.40), p=0.090 |
| Post presence | 17.7% (present) vs 19.7% (absent), p=0.630; screw posts 7/21 (33.3%), p=0.079 across post types |
| Molar root sites | maxillary mesiobuccal 15/16; mandibular mesial 9/13 |
