## Three-line Summary

Practice-guideline development study (Korean Clinical Imaging Guidelines adaptation methodology): 20 guidelines retrieved (Ovid-Medline, Embase, GIN, NGC, KoreaMed, KMbase, KoMGI; 2000 to Sept 2023), 3 included, supplemented by 22 recent primary/secondary studies, for one key question (periapical radiography vs CBCT in chewing-pain patients with suspected VRF).

Two recommendations were finalized by a 6-member two-round Delphi panel (Rec 1: grade A, evidence level II, mean agreement 8.0 [SD 1.2]; Rec 2: grade B, evidence level II, mean 7.6 [SD 1.6]): periapical radiography is the preferred first imaging method, and small-FOV CBCT may be considered only when intraoral radiographs are insufficient.

CBCT use in root-filled teeth is constrained by beam-hardening/streak artifacts (pooled CBCT sensitivity in filled teeth 72.76% [SD 18.73], range 30-92%), only 1 of 3 source guidelines scored above 50 on AGREE II "Rigor of development", and tooth-site/root-number-specific advice could not be given for lack of literature.

## 세줄요약

진료지침 개발 연구(한국 임상영상진료지침 (Korean Clinical Imaging Guidelines, K-CIG) 적응 방법론): 20편의 지침을 검색(2000-2023.9)해 3편을 채택하고 최신 연구 22편을 보충하여, "저작통 + 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 의심 환자에서 치근단방사선사진 (Periapical radiography, PA) 대비 콘빔 CT (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT)" 단일 핵심질문을 다룸.

전문가 6인 2회 델파이 합의로 권고 2개 확정(권고 1: 등급 A, 근거수준 II, 평균 합의도 8.0 [SD 1.2]; 권고 2: 등급 B, 근거수준 II, 평균 7.6 [SD 1.6]): PA를 1차 영상으로 선호하고, 구내방사선사진으로 정보가 부족할 때만 소조사야 (small field of view, FOV) CBCT를 고려.

근관충전치에서는 빔경화 (beam hardening)·줄무늬 인공물로 CBCT 사용이 제한됨(충전치 CBCT 민감도 평균 72.76% [SD 18.73], 범위 30-92%); 채택된 3개 지침 중 AGREE II 개발의 엄격성 영역 50점 초과는 1편뿐이며, 치아 위치·치근 수별 권고는 근거 부족으로 제시하지 못함.

## Summary

Kim et al. (Oral Radiol 2024) developed the first Korean evidence-based imaging guideline for teeth suspected of vertical root fracture (VRF), using the K-CIG adaptation method: a guideline search across seven databases yielded 20 records, of which 3 guidelines were adopted (only one scoring above 50 on the AGREE II rigor domain), and 22 later primary and secondary studies were added to an evidence table. A six-member Delphi panel finalized two recommendations in two rounds. Periapical radiography, acquired at several horizontal angulations, is the preferred first examination for chewing-pain teeth with suspected VRF, and a small field-of-view CBCT may be added only when intraoral films are inconclusive. CBCT confirmation is considered unnecessary when VRF is already visible on a periapical film, and its value in root-filled teeth is limited by gutta-percha and metal-post artifacts, although recent studies disagree depending on voxel size, detector type and scanner.

## Key Contributions

- Formal, graded imaging-modality sequence for suspected VRF (periapical first; small-FOV CBCT when inconclusive), with Delphi agreement means of 8.0 and 7.6 on a 9-point scale.
- Transparent appraisal showing how thin the guideline base is (3 guidelines, AGREE II scores 90, 33 and 31).
- Synthesis of conflicting recent evidence on CBCT in obturated teeth, naming voxel size (0.2-0.3 mm), flat-panel detectors and scanner model as modifiers.
- Radiologic feature list for VRF (fracture line, fragment separation, canal-wall gap, vertical bone loss, halo/J-type radiolucency).

## Methodology

Adaptation of existing guidelines under the Korean Clinical Imaging Guidelines (K-CIG) method; committee, working group and six-member consensus group; single PICO (chewing-pain patients with suspected VRF; CBCT vs periapical radiographs; diagnostic possibility of VRF). Search 2000 to September 2023 in Ovid-Medline, Embase, GIN, NGC (to June 2018), KoreaMed, KMbase, KoMGI; two reviewers; K-AGREE II appraisal; recommendation grades A/B/C/I and evidence levels I-IV; two anonymous Delphi rounds rated 1-9.
