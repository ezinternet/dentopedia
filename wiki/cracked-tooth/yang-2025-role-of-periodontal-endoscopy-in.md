## Three-line Summary

Single-centre prospective cohort (Xi'an Jiaotong University, July 2022 to June 2023): 112 analysed teeth with suspected VRF (S-VRF; isolated pocket >6 mm plus swelling, sinus tract or occlusal pain, no definite fracture line on CBCT; 90.2% root-filled), all examined with periodontal endoscopy after scaling and methylene-blue staining, and treatment plans (flap surgery 56, root canal treatment 48, extraction 11) then re-assigned.

VRF was confirmed in 70/112 teeth (62.5%); endoscopy detected 59 (sensitivity 84.3%, 95% CI 75.0-91.1%; specificity and PPV 100%; NPV 79.25%; AUC 0.92), and the endoscopy-based re-plan avoided planned flap surgery in 36/56 (64.3%) and planned root canal (re)treatment in 19/45 (42.2%); interobserver kappa rose from 0.444 (CBCT alone) to 0.842 (CBCT + endoscopy).

Periodontal endoscopy looks like a useful chairside adjunct after a CBCT-negative work-up, but a positive finding is far more reliable than a negative one (11 of 70 VRFs missed, mainly premolars with narrow or interproximal access), and the design (convenience sample, 62.5% VRF prevalence, endoscopy findings steering the treatment that verified them, 1-year follow-up) limits generalisation.

VRF는 70/112 (62.5%)에서 확인되었고 치주내시경은 59개를 검출 (민감도 84.3%, 95% CI 75.0-91.1%; 특이도·양성예측도 100%; 음성예측도 79.25%; 곡선하면적 (Area Under the Curve, AUC) 0.92); 내시경 기반 재계획으로 계획된 판막수술 36/56 (64.3%)과 근관 (재)치료 19/45 (42.2%)를 피함; 관찰자 간 일치도 (Cohen's kappa)는 CBCT 단독 0.444 → CBCT+내시경 0.842.

## Summary

Yang et al. (BMC Oral Health 2025) ran a single-centre prospective cohort of 112 analysed teeth (115 enrolled) with suspected vertical root fracture (S-VRF): an isolated pocket deeper than 6 mm with swelling, sinus tract or occlusal pain, but no definite fracture line on CBCT. Every tooth received periodontal endoscopy after scaling and methylene-blue staining, and the pre-endoscopic plan set by two endodontists (flap surgery 56, root canal treatment 48, extraction 11) was then reassigned. VRF was confirmed in 70 teeth (62.5%) by microscopic crack identification at extraction, flap exploration or canal exploration, or by failure within one year; endoscopy detected 59 of them (sensitivity 84.3%, PPV 100%, NPV 79.25%, AUC 0.92). The authors propose endoscopy as an adjunct after a CBCT-negative work-up, before committing to extraction, exploratory flap or root canal (re)treatment. Because the sample was convenience-based with very high VRF prevalence and endoscopy findings steered the verification pathway, the accuracy figures should be read as optimistic, single-centre estimates.

## Key Contributions

- A standardised chairside protocol (anaesthesia, sheath into pocket, observe, scale, methylene-blue stain, re-observe; continuous blue line >2 mm = VRF) for CBCT-negative S-VRF.
- Effect on decisions: planned flap surgery avoided in 36/56 (64.3%) and planned root canal (re)treatment in 19/45 (42.2%); interobserver kappa 0.444 (CBCT alone) to 0.842 (CBCT + endoscopy).
- Diagnostic profile: sensitivity 84.3% (95% CI 75.0-91.1%), specificity and PPV 100%, NPV 79.25%, AUC 0.92; a positive endoscopic crack is close to confirmatory, a negative one does not exclude VRF.
- Names three concrete miss mechanisms (narrow coronal bone defect blocking sheath entry, interproximal fracture inaccessible beside adjacent teeth, shallow early crack below probe resolution), all seen in premolars.

## Methodology

Prospective cohort, July 2022 to June 2023, Xi'an Jiaotong University Stomatological Hospital (IRB KY-QX-20240004). Adults 18-70 with S-VRF; excluded for poorly controlled diabetes or hypertension, generalised periodontitis, known root fracture/iatrogenic crack, pregnancy. 86 molars, 25 premolars, 1 anterior; 90.2% root-filled. Endoscopy (P100) by periodontists with >10 years' experience, read jointly with an endodontist. Reference standard: microscopic crack identification after extraction or root amputation, microscopic flap exploration, crown removal or canal exploration, or clinical failure within 1 year. Outcomes: avoided procedures, sensitivity/specificity/predictive values, AUC, kappa, logistic regression.

Interpretation cautions: (1) all teeth were CBCT-negative for a definite fracture line by inclusion, so the "accuracy gain over CBCT" is a re-planning comparison, not a head-to-head CBCT accuracy study; (2) verification was uneven (endoscopy-positive teeth extracted and examined microscopically; endoscopy-negative root canal teeth confirmed by canal exploration and 1-year follow-up); (3) the abstract-vs-body percentage for avoided root canal treatment, the 59/59 specificity notation, and the Table 1 probing-depth row (100% >=8 mm vs inclusion >6 mm) are internal inconsistencies of the source; (4) the Discussion's claims of female predominance and root-canal-accelerated VRF are not supported by the paper's own Table 1 (sex p=0.367, endodontic treatment p=0.07) and are not carried forward here.
