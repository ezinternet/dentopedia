## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (no stated search strategy, no formal appraisal; Med Sci Monit 2024) of management of vertical root fracture (VRF) with a tooth-preservation focus: root amputation/resection/hemisection, laser-mediated fusion, calcium hydroxide dressing, fracture-line bonding with or without intentional replantation, and extraction.

Cited figures are VRF incidence of 4-32% in root-canal-treated teeth, 80% of VRFs detected within 2 years of first symptoms, and dentin weakening of 23-43.9% after calcium hydroxide filling (Rosenberg 2006); preservation evidence consists of case reports (e.g., 6 teeth bonded with 4-META/MMA-TBB resin and replanted; a 12-month incisor case) and the review gives no pooled success rates.

Treatment is reasonable for incomplete fractures with intact periodontium in a cooperative patient, whereas extraction suits split-tooth-like fractures, severe bone loss, chronic infection or medically compromised patients; the authors themselves call for standardized protocols and long-term efficacy studies.

## 세줄요약

내러티브 리뷰 (검색 전략·비판적 평가 미기재; Med Sci Monit 2024) — 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)의 치아 보존 중심 관리: 치근 절단·절제·반절 (Root Amputation/Resection/Hemisection), 레이저 융합, 수산화칼슘 (Calcium Hydroxide) 드레싱, 파절선 접착 ± 의도적 재식립 (Intentional Replantation), 발치.

인용 수치는 근관치료치 VRF 발생률 4-32%, VRF의 80%가 초기 증상 후 2년 내 발견, 수산화칼슘 근관충전 후 상아질 약화 23-43.9% (Rosenberg 2006); 보존 근거는 증례 수준 (예: 4-META/MMA-TBB 레진으로 접착 후 재식립한 6개 치아, 12개월 추적 전치부 1례)이며 통합 성공률은 제시하지 않음.

불완전 파절·치주 건강·협조적 환자에서 치료를 고려하고, 분할치 (Split Tooth) 양상·심한 골소실·만성 감염·전신질환 환자에서는 발치가 적합; 저자 스스로 표준 프로토콜과 장기 효능 연구가 필요하다고 명시.

## Summary

This narrative review from the University of Prishtina (Salihu and Stavileci, Med Sci Monit 2024) surveys how vertical root fractures can be managed, with an explicit emphasis on preserving the tooth rather than extracting it. It covers root amputation/resection/hemisection, CO2 and Nd:YAG laser fusion, calcium hydroxide dressing, fracture-line bonding (intracanal, flap-based, or extraction with extraoral bonding and intentional replantation), and extraction. The preservation reports it cites are single cases or small series (for example 6 teeth bonded with 4-META/MMA-TBB resin and replanted, and an incisor case healed at 12 months), so no pooled success rate is given. The decision rule in the conclusions is that incomplete fractures with intact periodontal bone in a cooperative patient may be treated, while split-tooth-like fractures, bone destruction, chronic infection, or medically compromised patients should be extracted; prevention (occlusal control, conservative access, appropriate posts, avoiding excessive condensation force) is called the best treatment.

## Key Contributions

- One-paper survey of five VRF management options with their advantages and disadvantages.
- Two stated treatment goals: remove microbial invasion at the fracture line and prevent periodontal tissue loss.
- Treat-versus-extract criteria based on fracture completeness, periodontal/bone status, infection, patient cooperation and systemic health.
- Position that intentional replantation be tried before implant replacement (cheaper; implant remains available if it fails).

## Methodology

Narrative review of case reports, small series and in vitro work; no database list, search dates, eligibility criteria or risk-of-bias tool is reported, and no quantitative synthesis was performed.

## Results

| Topic | Statement in the paper |
|---|---|
| VRF incidence in root-canal-treated teeth | 4% to 32% |
| Time to detection | 80% detected within 2 years of first symptoms |
| Imaging | CBCT more accurate than periapical radiography (3D, less distortion) |
| Resection/hemisection | MTA-sealed apical resection (Taschieri protocol) for incomplete VRF; hemisection case successful at 3 years; resected root without root-end filling may fracture |
| CO2 laser | works best with thin, well-approximated fractures, full-coverage crown, perpendicular access; Nd:YAG ineffective for fusion in 81 single-rooted teeth (1996 study) |
| Calcium hydroxide | dentin weakened 23-43.9% (Rosenberg 2006) vs newer studies finding no increased fracture risk |
| Bonding + replantation | 6 teeth (incisor, canine, premolar, 3 molars) with 4-META/MMA-TBB; incisor with dual-cure resin + CEM cement, 18 min, 10-day splint, asymptomatic at 12 months; root resorption remains a problem |
| Extraction | choice for complete fractures, intraosseous fractures with periodontal pain, deep dehiscence (then implant) |
