## Three-line Summary

This editorial announces the strategic vision of the Korean Academy of Preventive Dentistry and Oral Health for its journal, outlining three academic directions: expanding basic and clinical preventive research, nurturing creative talent through a fair review system, and strengthening interdisciplinary convergence and global networking.

The journal aims to embrace emerging research trends including evidence-based dentistry, oral health big data, quality-of-life research involving oral frailty, and artificial intelligence applications, while bridging laboratory findings to clinical practice and policy.

The editorial serves as a roadmap for the journal's evolution into a leading academic platform encompassing oral health, basic and clinical sciences, and dental hygiene, with a commitment to protecting the oral health of Koreans and contributing to global dental health.

## Summary

This presidential editorial from the Korean Academy of Preventive Dentistry and Oral Health, published in 2026, announces the strategic vision for the Journal of the Korean Academy of Oral Health as it enters a new phase of development. The editorial reflects on the journal's 60-year history since the academy's founding in 1961 and acknowledges the contributions of members and researchers to establishing the academic foundation for national oral health policies and surveys in Korea. Recognizing rapidly changing societal and academic demands, the president outlines three core academic directions for the journal's future. First, the journal will actively promote research that expands basic and clinical preventive sciences while embracing emerging trends including evidence-based dentistry, oral health big data, quality-of-life research incorporating oral frailty, and artificial intelligence applications. Second, the journal aspires to become a hub for nurturing creative talent through a fair, constructive, and efficient review system that supports early-career researchers. Third, the journal will strengthen interdisciplinary convergence domestically and global academic networking internationally with organizations such as FDI, WHO, and the Asian Conference of Oral Health and Dentistry. The editorial concludes with a commitment to serving as an academic safeguard for the oral health of Koreans and preserving smiles worldwide, while inviting submissions from researchers.
