## Three-line Summary
SR + MA (7 clinical trials, 5 databases searched to December 2021, Cochrane RoB) evaluating professionally applied fluoride therapies for prevention and arrest of root caries in older adults.

38% SDF showed both prevention (prevented fraction 25–71%) and arrest (24-month arrest rate 42%, 95% CI 33–49%); 5% NaF varnish achieved a 64% prevention fraction and 1.23% APF gel 32% — SDF was the only agent with arrest evidence.

Clinically, SDF is the preferred professionally applied agent for older adults with active root caries given its dual prevention-plus-arrest profile; NaF varnish is effective for prevention in patients where SDF staining is unacceptable.

## 세줄요약
SR+MA(임상시험 7편, 5개 데이터베이스 2021년 12월까지, Cochrane RoB) — 노인의 근면 우식에 대한 전문가 적용 불소요법(예방·정지) 평가.

38% SDF는 예방(예방분율 25–71%)과 정지(24개월 정지율 42%, 95% CI 33–49%) 모두 효과; 5% NaF 바니시 64% 예방, 1.23% APF 젤 32% 예방 — 정지 근거는 SDF에만 존재.

임상적 의의: 활동성 근면 우식 노인에게는 예방·정지 이중 효과의 SDF가 최우선 전문가 적용제; NaF 바니시는 SDF 착색이 수용 불가한 경우 예방에 효과적.
