## Three-line Summary
SR + MA (28 studies, 27,927 older adults, 6 databases searched to March 2024, random-effects model with subgroup and meta-regression) estimating oral frailty prevalence and quantifying its associations with unfavourable health outcomes.

Pooled oral frailty prevalence was 32% (95% CI 24–41%); oral frailty was significantly associated with physical frailty (OR 1.67), malnutrition (OR 2.27), low dietary variety (OR 1.98), and social withdrawal (OR 1.42), with high inter-study heterogeneity by setting and assessment tool.

Clinically, the strong malnutrition link (OR 2.27) makes oral frailty screening a high-yield intervention target in geriatric care, and the social-withdrawal association points to a broader functional decline cascade beyond chewing alone.

## 세줄요약
SR+MA(28편, 노인 27,927명, 6개 DB 2024년 3월까지, 랜덤효과 모형 하위군·메타회귀) — 구강노쇠(Oral Frailty, OF) 유병률 추정 및 불량결과와의 연관 정량화.

OF 통합 유병률 32%(95% CI 24–41%); OF는 신체쇠약(Hazard Ratio/OR 1.67)·영양실조(OR 2.27)·식이다양성 저하(OR 1.98)·사회위축(OR 1.42)과 유의하게 연관, 세팅·도구별 이질성 높음.

임상적 의의: 영양실조 연관(OR 2.27)이 강해 노인진료에서 구강노쇠 선별이 고효율 개입 표적이며, 사회위축 연관은 단순 저작 기능 이상을 넘는 기능 저하 연쇄를 시사.
