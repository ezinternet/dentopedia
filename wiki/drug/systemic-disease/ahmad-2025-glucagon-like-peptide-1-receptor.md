## One-line Summary
Narrative review (10 in vitro, 9 animal, 1 retrospective clinical study) finding that GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 수용체 작용제, GLP-1 Receptor Agonist, GLP-1RA) reduce peri-implant marginal bone loss and promote periodontal regeneration in T2DM via Wnt/β-catenin and MAPK pathways, with effects largely independent of glycemic control.

## Summary
GLP-1 receptor agonists — the drug class including semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy), liraglutide (Victoza), and exenatide — are now among the most commonly prescribed agents for T2DM and obesity. This narrative review synthesizes preclinical and limited clinical evidence on their effects on periodontal tissues and dental implants. In vitro work shows GLP-1RAs stimulate osteogenic differentiation of periodontal ligament stem cells via Wnt/β-catenin and MAPK pathways even under high-glucose inflammatory conditions. Animal models demonstrate reduced alveolar bone resorption and enhanced implant osseointegration, with effects independent of glycemic improvement, suggesting direct pleiotropic actions. The single clinical study found significantly less peri-implant marginal bone loss in GLP-1RA-treated patients vs insulin and metformin groups (p < 0.01). The evidence base is dominated by preclinical work; no RCT exists.

## Key Contributions
- First synthesis of GLP-1RA effects specifically on periodontal and peri-implant tissues
- Establishes glycemic-independent mechanism: Wnt/β-catenin + MAPK → periodontal ligament stem cell (치주인대줄기세포, Periodontal Ligament Stem Cell, PDLSC) osteogenesis
- Advanced delivery (exenatide-PLGA microspheres) shows further enhanced peri-implant osseointegration in diabetic models
- Clinical signal: GLP-1RA < insulin, metformin for peri-implant bone loss (p < 0.01) — 1 retrospective cohort
- Implications for increasingly common GLP-1RA-using patients presenting for implant therapy

## Methodology
- Narrative review; no formal PRISMA protocol
- Included: 10 in vitro studies, 9 preclinical animal studies, 1 clinical retrospective cohort study
- Search: multiple databases; no explicit search string reported
- Clinical study: compared peri-implant marginal bone loss among T2DM peri-implantitis patients on GLP-1RA vs insulin vs metformin

## Results
**In vitro:** Exenatide and liraglutide → ↑ PDLSC osteogenic differentiation via Wnt/β-catenin + MAPK; SDF-1 synergy → ↑ proliferation and bone regeneration even in high-glucose/inflammatory conditions.

**Animal (9 studies):** GLP-1RAs ↓ periodontal bone resorption, ↓ oxidative stress, ↓ inflammatory mediators; exenatide-PLGA microspheres → significantly enhanced peri-implant osseointegration vs control in diabetic rodent models; effects observed independent of blood glucose improvement.

**Clinical (1 retrospective cohort):** GLP-1RA group showed significantly less clinical attachment loss and radiographic marginal bone loss than insulin and metformin groups (p < 0.01). Sample size and confounders not fully reported.
