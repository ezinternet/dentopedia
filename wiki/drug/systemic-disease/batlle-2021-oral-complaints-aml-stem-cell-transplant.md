## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center cohort study of oral complications in AML patients receiving allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (allo-HSCT) — mucositis, oral GVHD, and xerostomia assessed.

Oral GVHD incidence approximately 30–50% (lichenoid, hyperkeratotic, and ulcerative patterns); mucositis severity correlated with transplant-related morbidity and post-transplant survival; pre-transplant dental clearance reduced infectious complications.

High oral complication burden in allo-HSCT patients requires coordinated dental-oncology management; pre-HSCT oral health optimization is a key preventive lever.

## 세줄요약

동종조혈모세포이식(allo-HSCT)을 받은 급성골수성백혈병(AML) 환자의 구강 합병증(점막염·구강 이식편대숙주병·구강건조증)을 평가한 후향적 단일기관 코호트 연구.

구강 이식편대숙주병(GVHD) 발생률 약 30–50%(태선양·과각화·궤양형); 점막염 중증도가 이식 관련 이환율·생존과 상관; 이식 전 치과 위생 처치는 감염 합병증을 감소.

Allo-HSCT 환자는 구강 합병증 부담이 높아 치과-종양내과 협진 관리가 필요하며, 이식 전 구강 건강 최적화가 핵심 예방 전략이다.

## Summary
Retrospective analysis of oral complications in AML patients receiving allogeneic HSCT: mucositis, oral GVHD, xerostomia.

## Key Contributions
- Oral GVHD incidence ~30–50%; lichenoid, hyperkeratotic, and ulcerative patterns
- Mucositis severity correlates with transplant-related morbidity and survival
- Pre-transplant dental clearance reduces infectious complications

## Methodology
Retrospective cohort; single center.

## Results
High oral complication burden in allo-HSCT. Oral GVHD requires coordinated dental-oncology management. Pre-HSCT oral health optimization is key.
