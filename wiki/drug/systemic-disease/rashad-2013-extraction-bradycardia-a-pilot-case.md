## Three-line Summary

Prospective case-crossover pilot study in 61 systemically healthy, normotensive adults (27 male/34 female, 18–59 y) undergoing one simple posterior mandibular molar extraction, with heart rate (HR) recorded by pulse oximeter before anesthesia, during extraction (lowest value), and immediately after.

Mean HR fell monotonically from 88.4 ± 14.1 to 86.1 ± 13.4 to 81.4 ± 11.4 bpm, with p = 0.000 for each of the three pairwise comparisons; age group had no effect (p = 0.70/0.88/0.84) and the before→during fall was absent in females (p = 0.97).

Bradycardia appears even under adequate anesthesia and minimal surgical trauma, and without any preceding HR rise — evidence for a peripheral trigeminocardiac reflex rather than a biphasic vasovagal reflex; the pilot has no control arm and reports no BP, so the cardioinhibitory/vasodepressor split cannot be resolved.

## 세줄요약

전향적 case-crossover 예비연구 (Prospective Case-crossover Pilot Study) — 전신질환·심폐질환 병력이 없는 정상혈압 성인 61명(남 27·여 34, 18–59세)의 하악 후치아 단순 발치 1개씩에서 국소마취 전·발치 중(최저 심박수)·발치 직후 심박수 (Heart Rate, HR)를 펄스옥시미터로 측정했다.

평균 심박수는 88.4 ± 14.1 → 86.1 ± 13.4 → 81.4 ± 11.4 bpm으로 단조 감소했고 세 쌍비교 모두 p = 0.000이었으며, 연령군 간 차이는 없었고(p = 0.70/0.88/0.84) 여성에서는 마취 전→발치 중 감소가 유의하지 않았다(p = 0.97).

충분한 국소마취와 최소 외상 조건에서도, 선행 심박수 상승 없이 서맥이 나타나 이상성(二相性) 미주신경성 반사보다 말초 삼차신경-심장 반사 (Trigeminocardiac Reflex, TCR)를 지지하나, 대조군 없음·혈압 미보고·단일기관 예비연구라 심억제형/혈관억제형 구분은 불가능하다.

## Summary

This single-center prospective case-crossover study measured heart rate in 61 normotensive, otherwise healthy adults (18–59 y; 31.1 ± 9.9) who each underwent one simple posterior mandibular molar extraction under 4% articaine with 1:100,000 epinephrine via inferior alveolar nerve block. HR was captured with a pulse oximeter at three points — before injection, during extraction (the lowest value detected), and immediately after — and compared with paired t-tests. Mean HR declined steadily from 88.4 ± 14.1 bpm to 86.1 ± 13.4 bpm and then 81.4 ± 11.4 bpm, each pairwise difference reaching p = 0.000; the pattern held across age groups (no between-group differences, p = 0.70/0.88/0.84) and in both sexes, except that female HR did not differ between pre-anesthesia and intra-extraction (p = 0.97). No vasovagal reaction or syncope occurred. Because the fall was not preceded by a rise, the authors argue that the classical biphasic vasovagal explanation fits poorly and that a peripheral trigeminocardiac reflex — trigeminal afferents relaying to the vagal motor nucleus — better explains extraction bradycardia, and they recommend HR monitoring even for simple extractions.

## Key Contributions

- Quantifies extraction-related bradycardia in a homogeneous, low-risk population: a ~7 bpm drop from baseline to post-extraction, significant at every pairwise step.
- Demonstrates a **monophasic** HR decline (no pre-bradycardic rise), directly challenging the biphasic vasovagal reflex narrative that dominates dental emergency teaching.
- Separates the haemodynamic response from pain and anxiety to a degree: anesthesia was verified with a dental explorer, a single surgeon used a deliberately minimal-trauma technique, and no flap/decoronation/root sectioning was allowed.
- Reports a sex-specific pattern (female before→during p = 0.97) and confirms age-independence of the response across nine age bands.
- Positions the finding as one of the first descriptions of a **peripheral** TCR during tooth extraction, distinct from the ~20% HR drop described in central (neurosurgical/skull-base) TCR.

## Methodology

- **Design**: prospective case-crossover (within-patient, three time points); self-described pilot; simple consequential sampling, no randomization, no control arm.
- **Setting**: Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery Department, Azad University of Medical Sciences, Tehran, Iran; late 2011; protocol peer-reviewed and approved; Helsinki Declaration; chair-side written informed consent.
- **Population**: n = 61 patients / 61 teeth — 34 third (55.7%), 14 second (23%), 13 first (21.3%) mandibular molars; fully erupted third molars limited to level A depth and Class I available space. Excluded: cardiovascular or pulmonary disease history; any case needing a flap, decoronation, or root fragmentation.
- **Anesthesia**: up to two cartridges of 4% articaine with 1:100,000 epinephrine (Ultracaine DSF, Sanofi-Aventis), conventional inferior alveolar nerve block; anesthesia checked with a dental explorer.
- **Measurement**: Nellcor OxiMax IM-N-550 pulse oximeter; HR at baseline (before injection), during extraction (lowest detected), immediately after. No continuous recording, no BP values reported per patient.
- **Operator**: one oral and maxillofacial surgeon for all cases, minimizing trauma and avoiding inadequate anesthesia.
- **Statistics**: PASW Statistics 18; mean ± SD; paired t-test for the three pairwise comparisons; α = 0.05; stratified by age group and sex.
