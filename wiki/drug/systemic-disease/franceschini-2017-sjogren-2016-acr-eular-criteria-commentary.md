## Three-line Summary

Editorial commentary (BMC Med 2017) explaining the key changes in the 2016 ACR/EULAR primary Sjögren syndrome (pSS) classification criteria compared with the 2002 AECG criteria, with focus on the removal of symptom-only items, adoption of the Ocular Staining Score (OSS), and enhanced biomarker emphasis.

The 2016 criteria retain minor salivary gland biopsy (≥1 focus/4 mm²) and anti-SSA/Ro seropositivity as dominant items; symptom-only dry-eye/dry-mouth items are removed; OSS replaces the van Bijsterveld score; the new weighted criteria are more objective and reproducible.

The revised criteria are better suited for clinical trial patient selection but are less sensitive for early or seronegative pSS; dental clinicians should expect that patients presenting with sicca symptoms but lacking anti-SSA/Ro or biopsy findings may no longer meet the new classification threshold.

## 세줄요약

2002 AECG 기준 대비 2016 ACR/EULAR 일차 쇼그렌증후군(pSS) 분류 기준의 주요 변경 사항(증상 단독 항목 삭제·Ocular Staining Score(OSS) 도입·바이오마커 강화)을 설명한 편집 해설(BMC Med 2017).

2016 기준은 소타액선 생검(≥1 focus/4 mm²)과 anti-SSA/Ro 혈청양성을 핵심 항목으로 유지; 증상 단독 안구건조·구강건조 항목은 삭제; OSS가 van Bijsterveld 점수를 대체; 가중 점수 체계로 객관성·재현성 향상.

개정 기준은 임상시험 환자 선택에 적합하나 초기 또는 혈청음성 pSS에는 감도가 낮아, 시카 증상이 있어도 anti-SSA/Ro 음성·생검 음성인 환자는 새 기준을 충족하지 못할 수 있음을 치과 임상가가 인지해야 한다.

## Summary
Commentary explaining key changes in 2016 criteria versus AECG 2002: removal of symptom-only items, adoption of OSS, biomarker emphasis.

## Key Contributions
- Minor salivary gland biopsy retained; ≥1 focus/4mm² threshold preserved
- OSS (ocular staining score) replaces van Bijsterveld score
- Symptoms-only items removed; objective biomarkers dominate new criteria

## Methodology
Editorial commentary.

## Results
New criteria more objective and reproducible; better suited for clinical trial patient selection. Less sensitive for early/seronegative pSS.
