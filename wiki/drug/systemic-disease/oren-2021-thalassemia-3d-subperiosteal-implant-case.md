## Three-line Summary

Case report (J Med Case Rep 2021) of a 39-year-old beta-thalassemia major (BTM) patient with near-complete cortical bone loss from erythroid marrow hyperplasia, treated with full-mouth extraction, maxillary bone resection, and CAD/CAM-designed DMLS-printed Ti-6Al-4V custom subperiosteal implants. Three-year follow-up demonstrated no implant complications, stable occlusion, and restored masticatory and phonetic function, establishing a surgical pathway where conventional endosseous implants are impossible. Perioperative hematological preparation (2 units RBC + 2 units FFP + tranexamic acid + prophylactic antibiotics) was essential given BTM-associated coagulopathy.

## One-line Summary
Case report (J Med Case Rep 2021) demonstrating successful rehabilitation of a 39-year-old beta-thalassemia major patient using DMLS-printed custom subperiosteal titanium implants after full-mouth extraction and maxillary resection, with 3-year complication-free follow-up.

## Summary
A 39-year-old Bedouin male with beta-thalassemia major (BTM, IVS2-1 G>A homozygous) was referred for severe maxillary hyperplasia and periodontal disease. Physical examination revealed pancytopenia, ferritin 17,133 ng/mL, near-absent albumin, and coagulopathy consistent with liver cirrhosis. The jaws showed radiographically near-complete absence of trabecular bone, expanded medullary space, and thinned cortices — making conventional endosseous implant primary stability impossible. The treatment plan involved full-mouth extraction, 12mm vertical maxillary bone resection (osteotomy wafer-guided), and insertion of custom CAD/CAM-designed DMLS-printed Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 subperiosteal implants extending bilaterally to the zygomatic buttress and mandibular external oblique ridge. Presurgical preparation: 2 units packed RBC, 2 units FFP, tranexamic acid 1g, cephalosporin 2g. Intraoperative blood loss was ~650 mL from hyperplastic marrow ooze. Fixed prostheses were delivered at 6 weeks. At 3-year follow-up: no dehiscence, no implant complications, substantially improved facial profile, masticatory function, and speech.

## Key Contributions
- First reported case of combined maxillary deformity resection + DMLS-printed custom subperiosteal implant rehabilitation in BTM
- Explains the mechanical basis of endosseous implant failure in BTM: marrow erythroid hyperplasia expands medullary cavities → cortical bone dissolution → zero primary stability
- Documents the required perioperative hematological support protocol for BTM surgical patients
- Demonstrates 3D planning (ABGuidedService) + DMLS printing as the enabling technology for this approach

## Methodology
Single case report with 3-year follow-up. Preoperative virtual planning via 3D CT, CAD/CAM simulation. Surgical: GA + nasal intubation + local lidocaine/epinephrine infiltration. Implant: Ti-6Al-4V custom subperiosteal frames fixed with Ti-6Al-7Nb 2.4mm screws at zygomatic buttress, retromolar, and chin. Post-op panoramic and lateral cephalometric radiographs; 1-month impression; 6-week prosthesis delivery.

## Results
- No conventional implant primary stability achievable due to absent trabecular/cortical bone
- Intraoperative blood loss ~650 mL; post-op Hb 9.8 g/dL after 1 unit RBC
- 6-day hospitalization; no wound dehiscence
- 3-year follow-up: stable occlusion, no implant failure, improved mastication/speech/swallowing, competent lip closure, no gummy smile
