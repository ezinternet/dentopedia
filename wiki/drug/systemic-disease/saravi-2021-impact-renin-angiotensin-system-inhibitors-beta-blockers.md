## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=377 implants, 196 patients): RAS inhibitors significantly associated with higher secondary implant stability at exposure (ISQ +3.59, p=0.025); beta-blockers showed no significant effect.

## 한줄요약
후향코호트(377임플란트/196환자): 레닌-안지오텐신계 (Renin-Angiotensin System, RAS) 억제제가 이차 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 유의하게 향상(+3.59, p=0.025); 베타차단제 단독 효과 없음.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=377 implants/196 patients, 2015–2020, Germany); ISQ measured at insertion (primary stability) and at exposure ~120 days (secondary stability) via resonance frequency analysis; groups: NAH (331), BB (13), BB+RAS (11), RAS (22); multivariable linear mixed regression adjusted for age, sex, jaw, bone status.

RAS inhibitors significantly associated with higher secondary ISQ (reg. coeff. +3.59, 95% CI 0.46–6.71, p=0.025); beta-blockers showed no significant effect (reg. coeff. −0.93, p=0.648); primary stability at insertion unchanged across all groups.

First clinical study to separately assess RAAS inhibitors vs beta-blockers in implant stability — RAAS pathway (Ang II suppression per Shimizu 2008) appears clinically relevant to osseointegration; β-blocker null result highlights drug-class specificity and need for adequately powered subgroup analysis.

## 세줄요약

후향코호트(377임플란트/196환자, 2015–2020, 독일); 공명주파수분석(RFA)으로 삽입 시 일차·노출 시(~120일) 이차 ISQ 측정; 4군(정상혈압·BB·BB+RAS·RAS 단독); 다변량 선형혼합 회귀분석.

RAS 억제제: 이차 ISQ 유의하게 높음(회귀계수 +3.59, 95% CI 0.46–6.71, p=0.025); 베타차단제 단독: 유의하지 않음(−0.93, p=0.648); 일차안정성은 군간 차이 없음.

RAAS 억제제 대 베타차단제 임플란트 안정성 분리 비교 최초 임상 연구 — Shimizu 2008의 Ang II→RANKL 경로 차단이 임상적으로 유효함을 시사; 베타차단제 효과 없음은 검정력 부족 또는 기전 차이 가능성.

## Summary
This German retrospective cohort (2015–2020, 377 implants/196 patients) is the first clinical study to stratify antihypertensive drug classes — beta-blockers (BB), RAS inhibitors, and combination — against implant stability measured by radiofrequency analysis at two time points. RAS inhibitors were significantly associated with higher ISQ at implant exposure (secondary stability, reflecting osseointegration), with a regression coefficient of +3.59 (95% CI 0.46–6.71, p<0.05) after adjustment for age, sex, jaw, implant system, and healing time. Beta-blockers alone showed no significant benefit, potentially because non-selective and selective subtypes were not differentiated. All ISQ values exceeded 70 (high stability threshold), making clinical relevance of the differences theoretical.

## Key Contributions
- First clinical study comparing RAS inhibitor vs beta-blocker subgroups on implant stability (ISQ)
- RAS inhibitors: significantly higher secondary stability (post-osseointegration)
- Beta-blockers alone: no significant benefit on either primary or secondary stability
- Primary stability (insertion ISQ): no significant differences across any AHT subgroup
- Largest dataset on AHT subgroup analysis for implant stability at time of publication

## Methodology
Retrospective cohort, 196 patients, 377 implants (46 in AHT users: BB=13, Comb=11, RAS=22). Implant systems: Straumann SLA (76.7%) and Thommen Inicell (23.3%). ISQ measured at insertion (primary) and ~120 days at exposure (secondary) via Osstell Mentor. Multivariable linear mixed regression adjusted for age, sex, jaw, region, bone status, implant system, healing time, primary stability. STROBE-compliant. Exclusion: periodontitis history, antiresorptives, heavy smoking (>20/day), ASA ≥III.

## Results
Secondary stability (ISQ at exposure): RAS reg. coeff. +3.59 (95% CI 0.46–6.71, p<0.05); Comb reg. coeff. +2.71 (p=0.224); BB reg. coeff. −0.93 (p=0.648). Mean ISQ at exposure: RAS=76.64, Comb=78.36, BB=72.00, NAH=73.7. Primary stability: no subgroup differences. Age ≥70 was significantly more likely in AHT group (OR 2.30, p=0.009). Mandibular > maxillary: +8.18 ISQ (p<0.001).
