## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (56 publications, IJID 2022, Kiel group) examining the impact of diabetes mellitus on dental implant complication rates, stratified by glycemic control (HbA1c threshold).

Poorly controlled diabetes (HbA1c ≥9%) significantly increases peri-implantitis and long-term implant loss; controlled diabetes (HbA1c <8%) shows implant success similar to healthy controls; prediabetes may increase peri-implant disease but has no clear effect on implant survival.

DM is not a contraindication when HbA1c <8%; perioperative amoxicillin 2 g + CHX 0.12% mouthwash for 2 weeks is the standard protective protocol; delayed loading is preferred in diabetic patients.

## 세줄요약

SR(56편, IJID 2022, 킬 대학 그룹) — 당뇨 환자에서 임플란트 합병증 발생률을 혈당 조절 정도(HbA1c 역치)로 층화하여 검토.

조절 불량 당뇨(HbA1c ≥9%)는 치주염과 장기 임플란트 실패를 유의하게 증가; 조절 양호 당뇨(HbA1c <8%)는 건강 대조군과 유사한 성공률; 당뇨전단계(prediabetes)는 치주질환 증가 가능성이 있으나 생존율에 대한 명확한 영향 없음.

HbA1c <8%이면 당뇨는 임플란트 금기 아님; 술전 아목시실린 2g + 2주 CHX 0.12% 가글이 표준 예방 프로토콜; 당뇨 환자에서 지연부하 선호.

## Summary
Systematic review (56 publications) examining whether diabetes increases implant complication rates. **Controlled DM = similar implant success to healthy controls.** Poorly controlled DM (HbA1c ≥9%) significantly increases peri-implantitis and long-term implant loss. Perioperative antibiotics + CHX are standard and improve outcomes.

## Key Results

| DM Status | Peri-implantitis | Long-term Implant Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Poorly controlled (HbA1c ≥9%) | ↑ especially early | ↑ |
| Controlled (HbA1c <8%) | Similar to control | Similar to control |
| Prediabetes | Possible ↑ peri-implant disease | No clear effect on survival |

## Clinical Protocol for DM Patients

```
Pre-treatment:
  └─ Check HbA1c
     ├─ <8%:  proceed with standard implant protocol
     ├─ 8–9%: proceed with enhanced precautions
     └─ ≥9%:  defer until glycemic control improved

Perioperative:
  └─ Antibiotics (amoxicillin 2g PO preoperative + 5 days)
  └─ CHX 0.12% mouthwash × 2 weeks post-op

Loading:
  └─ Delayed loading protocol preferred
  └─ Extended osseointegration time expected
```

## Clinical Takeaway
- DM is **NOT a contraindication** for implants if HbA1c <8%
- HbA1c is the mandatory pre-implant screening test
- Perioperative anti-infective protocol is essential in all DM patients
