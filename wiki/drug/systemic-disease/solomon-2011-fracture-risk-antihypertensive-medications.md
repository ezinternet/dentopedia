## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=376,061 Medicare hypertension patients): ARB (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.68–0.86) and thiazide diuretics (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.76–0.97) significantly reduced fracture risk vs CCB; BB, ACEi, and loop diuretics were not significantly different.

## Summary
This large retrospective cohort study from Brigham and Women's Hospital examined fracture risk across all antihypertensive drug classes using Medicare claims data. The analysis of 376,061 patients newly starting single-drug antihypertensive therapy found significant differential fracture risk by drug class. Using CCB as the reference, ARB users had 24% lower fracture risk (HR 0.76) and thiazide diuretic users had 15% lower risk (HR 0.85). Loop diuretics carried the highest fracture rate (49.0/1000 person-years), while thiazides had the lowest (28.5/1000). BB and ACEi showed no significant difference from CCB. These findings provide large-scale epidemiological support for class-specific bone effects of antihypertensive drugs.

## Key Contributions
- Largest human cohort (n=376,061) quantifying AHT drug-class effects on fracture risk
- ARB and thiazide each independently reduce fracture risk — different mechanisms converging on bone protection
- BB null result for fracture risk aligns with Saravi 2021 null result for ISQ in BB subgroup
- Loop diuretics carry excess fracture risk — clinically important contraindication for bone-fragile patients
- Claims-database design allows for large-scale adjustment for comorbidities

## Methodology
Retrospective cohort. n=376,061 Medicare beneficiaries with diagnosis of hypertension, no antihypertensive prescription in prior 365 days, beginning single-drug therapy. Followed for osteoporotic fracture events via health care utilization data. Adjusted Cox proportional hazards regression. Reference group: CCB users. Adjusted for relevant comorbidities and comedications accessible in claims data.

## Results
- Overall fracture rate: 35.2/1000 person-years (95% CI 34.4–36.1)
- Thiazide: lowest fracture rate 28.5/1000 (95% CI 25.4–31.9)
- Loop diuretics: highest fracture rate 49.0/1000 (95% CI 46.1–52.1)
- ARB vs CCB: HR 0.76 (95% CI 0.68–0.86) — significant fracture risk reduction
- Thiazide vs CCB: HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.76–0.97) — significant fracture risk reduction
- Loop diuretic vs CCB: NS
- BB vs CCB: NS
- ACEi vs CCB: NS
