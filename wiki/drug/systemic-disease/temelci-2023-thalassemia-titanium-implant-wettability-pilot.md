## Three-line Summary

In vitro pilot study (Biomimetics 2023, n=126: 42 BTM patients, 42 carriers, 42 healthy controls) measuring blood contact angles on Grade 5 titanium (RBM/SBM surface) collected immediately before scheduled transfusion. Despite confirmed significant differences in RBC, hemoglobin (BTM ~10 vs healthy ~14 g/dL), and platelets (BTM ~500 vs healthy ~320 ×10³/µL), no significant between-group difference was found for any contact angle metric (theta-mean BTM 89.98° vs healthy 87.59°, p>0.05). Implant surface characteristics dominate blood rheology in determining initial wettability, removing one theoretical barrier to implant use in BTM while leaving wound healing and bone quality as remaining concerns.

## 세줄요약

인 비트로 파일럿(Biomimetics 2023, n=126: BTM 42명·보인자 42명·정상 42명) — 수혈 직전 Grade 5 티타늄(RBM/SBM 표면)에 대한 혈액 접촉각 측정. BTM 환자는 적혈구·혈색소·혈소판 수치 모두 정상인과 유의하게 다름에도 접촉각(theta-mean) 세 그룹 간 유의차 없음(BTM 89.98° vs 정상 87.59°, p>0.05). 티타늄 표면 특성이 혈액 유변학보다 임플란트 초기 습윤성을 지배함 확인; 혈액학적 장벽은 제거되나 창상 치유 지연·골질 저하는 여전히 고려 필요.

## One-line Summary
In vitro pilot study (Biomimetics 2023, n=126) showing no significant difference in blood wettability on Grade 5 titanium implant surfaces between beta-thalassemia major patients, carriers, and healthy controls — suggesting implant surface characteristics dominate over blood rheology differences.

## 한줄요약
Biomimetics 2023 파일럿(n=126) — β-지중해빈혈 환자 혈액이 Grade 5 티타늄 임플란트 표면에서 정상인 대비 습윤성 차이 없음(p>0.05) 확인; 임플란트 표면 특성이 혈액 유변학보다 골유착 초기 과정을 더 강하게 지배함을 시사한다.

## Summary
First in vitro study measuring contact angles of beta-thalassemia major (BTM) patients' blood on Grade 5 titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) implant discs. Blood samples were collected immediately before scheduled transfusion (worst-case rheology for BTM) from 42 BTM patients, 42 BT carriers, and 42 healthy controls at the Dr. Burhan Nalbantoğlu Thalassemia Center, Cyprus. Despite confirmed significant differences in RBC, hemoglobin (BTM 9.91–10.09 g/dL vs healthy 13.5–15.3 g/dL), and platelets (BTM 470–525K vs healthy 317–324K per μL), none of the three contact angle metrics (theta-mean, theta-circular, theta-ellipse) showed significant between-group differences. The RBM/SBM-surfaced implant discs (~Sa 1.69–1.73 μm) demonstrated similar wettability across all groups, consistent with prior research showing surface properties outweigh liquid rheology in determining static contact angles. However, the authors caution that delayed wound healing, reduced cortical bone quality, and increased platelet aggregability in BTM still present clinical challenges not captured by this static measurement.

## Key Contributions
- Establishes that BTM blood rheological differences do not translate to inferior implant surface wetting — removing one theoretical barrier to implant use in BTM
- Provides the first systematic contact angle data for BTM blood on dental-grade titanium (RBM/SBM surface)
- Reviews all three prior BTM dental implant cases in the literature: Misch 1998 (HA-coated, type IV bone, success), Pektaş 2015 (primary stability issues + overbleeding after osteotomy + implant), Oren 2021 (custom subperiosteal implants, 3-year success)
- Identifies Cyprus as high-prevalence BTM region (1/1000 homozygotes; 1/6 carriers) — a clinically underserved population

## Methodology
Cross-sectional in vitro study. n=126 (42 BTM, 42 carriers, 42 healthy). Blood collected 1 day pre-transfusion via fingertip lancet; 0.05 mL transferred to Grade 5 Ti disc (10mm, RBM/SBM surface, Sa=1.69–1.73 μm) immediately. Photos at 5s, Nikon D750 macro. Contact angles measured with ImageJ Contact Angle plugin (theta-mean, theta-circular, theta-ellipse), each repeated 5× per side and averaged. Statistics: Welch ANOVA/Kruskal-Wallis + Bonferroni correction. p<0.05 threshold.

## Results
- Blood features: BTM significantly lower RBC and HGB, significantly higher PLT vs non-BTM (p<0.05 for all)
- Contact angles: theta-mean BTM 89.98° vs Healthy 87.59° vs Carrier 85.79° — not significant (p>0.05)
- All three contact angle metrics showed identical non-significant pattern
- Reference: distilled water 103.03°, isotonic water 102.14° on same surface
