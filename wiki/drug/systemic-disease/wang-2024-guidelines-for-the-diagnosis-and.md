## Three-line Summary

Consensus guideline from the Chinese Pediatric Cardiology Society et al. (revised 2024, literature to April 2024) defining the etiology classification, diagnostic cutoffs, and treatment ladder for neurally mediated syncope (NMS) in children and adolescents. NMS causes 70%–80% of pediatric syncope (vasovagal syncope and POTS ≈95% of NMS) while cardiac syncope is only 2%–3% but carries sudden-death risk; diagnosis is history plus active standing test / head-up tilt test / active sitting test with age-banded numbers (POTS: HR rise ≥40 bpm or upright HR ≥130 bpm at 6–12 y, ≥125 bpm at 12–18 y). The dental value is the referral filter — exercise-induced syncope, abnormal ECG, family history of sudden death, infancy onset and no-prodrome events mean refer, not treat chairside — plus a non-pharmacological first line in which the only chairside-relevant items are water/salt and trigger avoidance.

## 세줄요약

중국소아심장학회 등의 2024 개정 합의 지침으로, 소아·청소년 미주신경성 실신 (Neurally Mediated Syncope, NMS)의 원인 분류·진단 수치 기준·치료 사다리를 정리했다. 소아 실신의 70%–80%는 NMS(혈관미주신경성 실신 (Vasovagal Syncope, VVS)과 기립성 빈맥 증후군 (Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome, POTS)이 NMS의 약 95%)이고 심장성 실신 (Cardiac Syncope, CS)은 2%–3%에 불과하지만 돌연사 위험이 있어, 운동 중 실신·비정상 심전도·돌연사 가족력·영아기 발병·전구증상 없는 실신은 의자 옆 치료 대상이 아니라 의뢰 대상이다. 치과에서 실제로 쓸 수 있는 부분은 이 의뢰 필터와 비약물 1차 치료(수분 30–50 mL/kg/day, 염분/경구수분보충염 (Oral Rehydration Salts, ORS), 유발인자 회피)이며, 미도드린·메토프롤롤은 소아심장과의 만성 치료이지 chairside 응급약이 아니다.

## Summary

This is a pediatric cardiology consensus guideline, not a dental document, and it should be read that way: its diagnostic tests (head-up tilt test, active sitting test) are referral endpoints a dentist will never perform. What transfers to the dental setting is the **classification and the referral filter**. NMS accounts for 70%–80% of syncope in children and adolescents, cardiac syncope for 2%–3%, and unexplained syncope about 20%; within NMS, VVS and POTS make up roughly 95%. The 2024 revision adds two sitting-position syndromes (sitting tachycardia syndrome, STS; sitting hypertension, SHT) and emphasizes comorbidities — 30%–40% of children with VVS and/or POTS also have allergic disease, and migraine, sleep disorders, hyperventilation syndrome and joint hypermobility are common.

Diagnosis is built in three steps: history, examination, supine and upright blood pressure, and supine and upright ECG triage the patient into definite, suggestive, or unexplained; only the unexplained group proceeds to postural testing. The cutoffs are numeric and age-banded, which is exactly why a dentist cannot apply them in the chair — a positive POTS response is a heart-rate rise ≥40 bpm or a maximum upright heart rate ≥130 bpm (6–12 y) / ≥125 bpm (12–18 y), with any significant blood-pressure fall excluded.

Treatment is a ladder: education and trigger avoidance → autonomic function exercise (tilt training, dry-towel wiping) → water 30–50 mL/kg/day plus salt or oral rehydration salts for at least 2 months → drug therapy (midodrine, metoprolol, fludrocortisone) only for recurrent syncope with injury risk after non-pharmacological failure → pacemaker in selected malignant cases. Prognosis is good: 86% of POTS patients are symptom-remitted 5 years after diagnosis, and follow-up is set at 1–3 months after diagnosis.

## Key Contributions

- Etiologic classification separating NMS subtypes (VVS vasoinhibitory / cardioinhibitory / mixed, POTS, OH, OHT, STS, SHT, situational syncope, carotid sinus syndrome) from cardiac and unexplained syncope.
- Numeric, age-banded diagnostic cutoffs for each orthostatic syndrome, replacing "it looked like a vasovagal" with measurable criteria.
- Explicit red-flag logic for cardiac syncope: exercise-induced events and abnormal ECG are named the strongest clues, with family history of hereditary heart disease or sudden death, infancy/early-childhood onset, and abnormal ECG all routing the patient to cardiology work-up (Holter, implantable loop recorder, exercise test, electrophysiology, imaging, genetic testing).
- Non-pharmacological-first treatment ladder with doses and contraindications for the drug step.
- Situational syncope triggers include **teeth brushing** and swallowing — the two triggers a dentist can actually witness or be told about.
- Biomarker tables intended to individualize which treatment is likely to work (not validated prospectively, and not usable in a dental office).

## Methodology

Guideline revision by expert groups: evidence collected from PubMed, Cochrane, EMBASE, China Biomedical Database and Chinese Journal Full-text Database through April 2024 (search terms "syncope", "children", "adolescents", "diagnosis", "treatment"), preliminary 2018-edition recommendations re-evaluated, then open-ended expert discussion to form recommendations. Each statement carries a recommendation class (I / IIa / IIb) and evidence level (A / B / C). Population: children and adolescents; no adult data, no dental-setting data.

## Results

**Etiology and diagnostic cutoffs (verbatim from the guideline)**

| Item | Value / criterion |
|---|---|
| NMS share of pediatric syncope | 70%–80% |
| Cardiac syncope share | 2%–3% (high sudden-death risk) |
| Unexplained syncope share | ~20% |
| VVS + POTS within NMS | ~95% |
| VVS (HUTT positive) | SBP ≤80 mmHg or DBP ≤50 mmHg or mean pressure ↓≥25%; HR <75 bpm (4–6 y) / <65 (>6–8 y) / <60 (>8 y); sinus arrest or junctional escape; II–III° AV block or cardiac arrest ≥3 s |
| Malignant VVS | Cardiac arrest >3 s during a syncope episode or during the test |
| POTS | HR rise ≥40 bpm or max upright HR ≥130 bpm (6–12 y) / ≥125 bpm (12–18 y) within 10 min of upright posture, excluding SBP ↓>20 / DBP ↓>10 mmHg |
| OH | SBP ↓≥20 mmHg and/or DBP ↓≥10 mmHg within 3 min of standing |
| OHT | SBP ↑≥20 mmHg and/or DBP ↑≥25 mmHg (6–12 y) or ≥20 mmHg (12–18 y); or upright BP ≥130/90 (6–12 y) / ≥140/90 mmHg (12–18 y) |

**POTS modifiable risk factors** — water intake <800 mL/day 3.88×; sleep <8 h/day 5.91×; supine HR +10 bpm 1.58×. **OHT** — overweight (BMI >85th percentile) 6.07×, obesity (BMI >95th percentile) 7.48×; each extra hour of sleep −74.3%.

**Treatment ladder (VVS; POTS follows the same non-drug steps)**

| Step | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. Education | Trigger avoidance (prolonged standing, rapid position change, sudden stop after exercise, crowded/stuffy rooms, emotional stress including painful stimuli or a medical operation), presyncope recognition, counter-pressure maneuvers, psychological support |
| 2. Autonomic exercise | Tilt training: standing ~15 cm from a wall, 5 min twice daily → up to 30 min/session; dry-towel wiping of forearms and both legs, 5 min per part twice daily |
| 3. Water / salt | Water up to 30–50 mL/kg/day (max 1500–1700 mL), aim for clear urine; salt or ORS ≥2 months then re-evaluate. ORS preferred in VVS vasoinhibitory type; **contraindicated** in hypertension, kidney disease, heart failure |
| 4. Drugs (only if >2 syncopes/6 months or >3/year, with injury risk, and non-drug therapy failed) | Midodrine 1.25–2.5 mg/dose once or twice daily → 2.5 mg three times daily after 2–4 wk (not if baseline BP >95th percentile); metoprolol 0.5 mg/kg/day bid, max 2 mg/kg/day (contraindicated in sinus bradycardia, II–III° AV block, bronchial asthma); fludrocortisone; sertraline only as later option |
| 5. Specialist | Pacemaker if recurrent syncope with asystole >4 s or after resuscitation; specialist ablation in selected cases |

**Follow-up**: 1–3 months after diagnosis/treatment, then symptom-driven; efficacy judged by symptom control, not by a negative HUTT. 86% of children/adolescents with POTS symptom-remitted 5 years after diagnosis.

## 치과 적용 — 의자 옆에서 쓸 수 있는 것과 없는 것

> 이 절은 지침 내용을 치과 맥락으로 옮긴 해석이다. 지침 본문에는 치과·국소마취·의자 체위에 대한 내용이 전혀 없다.

**1) 실신을 "미주신경성"으로 단정하기 전에 확인할 소아·청소년 적신호 → 치료하지 말고 의뢰**

| 적신호 | 지침 근거 |
|---|---|
| 운동 중(운동 직후 포함) 발생한 실신 | 운동 유발 실신과 비정상 심전도가 심장성 실신의 가장 강력한 단서 |
| 비정상 심전도 (기존 검사 결과 포함) | 위와 동일 |
| 유전성 심질환·돌연사 가족력 | 심장성 실신 의심 → 유전/metabolic screening 고려 |
| 영아기·어린 소아기 발병 | 심장성 실신 의심 |
| 전구증상 없이 갑작스러운 실신, 반복되는 실신 | NMS는 대개 전구증상·유발인자가 있다 — 이 특징이 없으면 감별 필요 |
| 실신으로 인한 손상(낙상·타박) | 약물 치료 적응증 판단에도 쓰이는 기준 |

**2) 유발인자 문진에서 물어볼 것** — 오래 서 있기, 자세 급변(눕거나 앉아 있다가 일어나기), 운동 후 갑작스러운 정지, 밀폐·혼잡한 환경, 통증이나 시술 같은 정서적 스트레스, 그리고 **양치(teeth brushing)·삼킴(swallowing)**. 양치·삼킴 실신은 지침이 명시한 상황성 실신 (Situational Syncope, SS) 유발인자다. 탈수·설사·구토·빈혈·월경·이뇨제도 혈량을 줄여 실신 가능성을 높인다.

**3) 의자 옆에서 할 수 있는 것** — 지침의 1단계(교육)와 일부 3단계가 여기에 해당한다.
- 전구증상(어지럼, 식은땀, 시야 흐림, 메스꺼움) 확인 시 즉시 체위 변경: 앉히거나 눕히고 다리 거상. 지침도 "대부분 짧은 시간에 완화된다"고 기술.
- 물 섭취 권고: 30–50 mL/kg/day (최대 1500–1700 mL). 단기 chairside 처치가 아니라 가정 지도 항목.
- 염분/ORS는 **고혈압·신장질환·심부전이 있으면 금기** — 소아에서도 전신질환 병력 확인 후 권고.
- 보호자에게 진단명·유발인자·전구증상 대응을 설명하고, 반복 실신 시 소아심장과 의뢰.

**4) 의자 옆에서 하면 안 되는 것**
- 미도드린·메토프롤롤·플루드로코르티손을 응급약으로 쓰지 않는다. 이들은 소아심장과가 반복 실신 환자에서 수 주~수 개월 단위로 조정하는 만성 치료이며, 지침의 적응증은 "6개월에 2회 초과 또는 연 3회 초과 + 손상 위험 + 비약물 치료 실패"다.
- 경두개 경사 검사 (Head-Up Tilt Test, HUTT)·능동 기립 검사·앉은자세 검사는 장비와 프로토콜이 필요한 진단 검사다 — 치과에서 시행하거나 "양성/음성"을 판정하지 않는다.
- 기립성 저혈압·고혈압·POTS를 의자 옆 수치로 자가 진단하지 않는다. 지침의 기준은 연령대별(4–6, 6–12, 12–18세)로 다르고, 아침/저녁 변동까지 고려한다.

**5) 성인 환자에 그대로 적용 금지** — 이 지침은 소아·청소년 전용이다. 성인 시술 중 실신의 병태생리·응급처치는 [[drug/systemic-disease/malave-2022-vasovagal-reactions-during-interventional-pain]]를, 치과 응급 감별(아나필락시스)은 [[drug/systemic-disease/jevon-2020-management-anaphylaxis-dental-practice-update]]를 본다.
