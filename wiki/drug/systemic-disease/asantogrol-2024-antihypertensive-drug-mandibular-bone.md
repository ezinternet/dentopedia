## One-line Summary
Retrospective case-control (n=230+230): all antihypertensive drug classes produced significantly higher mandibular bone fractal dimension (FD) vs controls (p<0.001), indicating denser trabecular architecture; thiazide effect limited to cortical bone only.

## 한줄요약
후향적 증례-대조 (n=230+230): 전 항고혈압약 클래스에서 하악골 프랙탈 차원 (Fractal Dimension, FD)이 대조군보다 유의하게 높음 (p<0.001) — 임플란트 골유착에 유리한 치밀 골 구조의 첫 인간 방사선학적 증거.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case-control (n=230 antihypertensive users vs n=230 normotensive controls) using fractal dimension (FD) analysis via ImageJ on panoramic radiographs; 5 mandibular ROIs assessed across five drug classes (BB, ACEi, ARB, CCB, thiazide, combination).

All drug classes produced significantly higher FD in mandibular bone vs controls (p<0.001 for all trabecular ROIs); thiazide diuretics achieved significance only in cortical ROI; higher FD indicates denser, more interconnected trabecular architecture.

First human radiographic study directly linking antihypertensive medication use to improved mandibular bone quality — provides translational support for the osseointegration substrate advantage seen clinically in Wu 2016 and Masri 2024, and mechanistically in Huang 2024 and Shimizu 2008.

## 세줄요약

후향 증례-대조(항고혈압약 복용 230명 vs 정상혈압 230명); ImageJ 프랙탈 차원(Fractal Dimension, FD) 분석, 하악 5개 관심영역(Region of Interest, ROI); BB·ACEi·ARB·CCB·치아자이드·복합 5개 약물 클래스 비교.

전 약물 클래스에서 하악 해면골 FD가 대조군보다 유의하게 높음(p<0.001); 치아자이드는 피질골(ROI 5)에서만 유의 — 약물 클래스별 해면골·피질골 효과 차이.

항고혈압약 복용이 하악골 골밀도를 직접 개선함을 방사선 지표로 최초 입증한 인체 연구 — Wu 2016·Masri 2024의 임상 보호 효과와 Shimizu 2008·Huang 2024의 기전 연구를 잇는 번역 증거.

## Summary
This Turkish retrospective case-control study compared mandibular bone quality between 230 antihypertensive (AHT) drug users and 230 sex-matched normotensive controls using fractal dimension (FD) analysis on panoramic radiographs. Five standardised ROIs (4 trabecular, 1 cortical) were evaluated via ImageJ. All AHT drug subgroups — including BB, ACEi, ARB, CCB, and combination therapy — showed significantly higher FD than controls across trabecular ROIs (p<0.001), indicating denser, more architecturally complex bone. Thiazide diuretics reached significance only in the cortical ROI (ROI 5), suggesting trabecular effects may be thiazide-sparing. Higher FD corresponds to more complex bone architecture, which is associated with better implant primary stability and osseointegration potential.

## Key Contributions
- First human radiographic study directly measuring mandibular bone quality in AHT users
- All drug classes (BB, ACEi, ARB, CCB, combination) improve trabecular bone FD — not class-specific for trabecular bone
- Thiazide diuretics show only cortical bone effect — different mechanism from other AHT classes
- Bridges animal mechanistic data (Huang 2024, Uchibori 2020) to clinical relevance in dental patients
- Panoramic FD methodology is clinically accessible — no additional imaging required beyond standard pre-implant workup

## Methodology
Retrospective case-control. n=460 (230 AHT users, 230 sex-matched normotensive controls). Panoramic radiographs retrospectively analysed. FD calculated with ImageJ using box-counting method at 5 mandibular ROIs. Subgroup analysis by drug class: BB, ACEi, ARB, CCB, thiazide diuretic, combination therapy. Statistical comparison of mean FD between AHT subgroup and control group.

## Results
- All AHT group vs controls: significantly higher FD in all 5 ROIs (p<0.001)
- BB, ACEi, ARB, CCB, combination subgroups: each higher FD vs controls in ROIs 1–5 (p<0.001)
- Thiazide subgroup: significant only in ROI 5 (cortical; p<0.001); ROIs 1–4 (trabecular) not significant
- Higher FD = denser, more complex trabecular architecture = improved osseointegration substrate
