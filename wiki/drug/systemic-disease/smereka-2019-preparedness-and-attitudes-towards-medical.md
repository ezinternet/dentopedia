## Three-line Summary

A cross-sectional survey of 419 Polish dentists found vasovagal syncope (46.30%) as the most common dental office emergency, followed by orthostatic hypotension (18.85%) and hyperventilation crisis (18.61%); anaphylactic shock co-occurred with mild allergic reactions at 16.23%. Despite relatively low serious-emergency rates (80.66% had zero EMS calls in 12 months), major competence gaps exist: 74.47% lacked confidence in hypertensive crisis, 55.13% in anaphylaxis, and 41.29% in cardiac arrest management. Results are comparable across countries, and the study calls for improved undergraduate/postgraduate emergency training and wider availability of emergency equipment.

## Background

Medical emergencies in dental offices are recognised across most countries as a preparedness challenge. Prior studies had analysed emergency prevalence and dentist readiness in many countries, but Poland had no such data before this study. This survey provides a European mid-sized-country perspective on the epidemiology of dental emergencies and the self-assessed competence of its dental workforce.

## Methods

- Design: Cross-sectional questionnaire survey
- Sample: n = 419 Polish dentists
- Instrument: 18-item questionnaire covering CPR training, emergency equipment availability, emergency prevalence, and self-assessed competence
- Analysis: Statistica 13.3; standard deviation for variability; Pearson's linear correlation coefficient for correlation strength

## Key Findings

### Emergency Prevalence (preceding 12 months)

| Emergency Type | % Dentists Experiencing ≥1 Case |
|---|---|
| Vasovagal syncope (VVS) | 46.30% |
| Orthostatic hypotension | 18.85% |
| Hyperventilation crisis | 18.61% |
| Mild allergic reactions | 16.23% |
| Hypoglycaemia | 15.99% |
| Seizures | 11.81% |

미주신경성 실신 (Vasovagal Syncope, VVS)이 단연 1위이며, 기립성 저혈압 (Orthostatic Hypotension)과 과환기 위기가 2·3위. 아나필락시스 (Anaphylaxis) / 경증 알레르기 반응은 4위.

### EMS Call Frequency (preceding 12 months)

| Calls | % Dentists |
|---|---|
| 0 | 80.66% |
| 1 | 12.65% |
| 2 | 4.53% |
| 3 | 1.20% |
| 4 | 0.48% |
| 5–10 | 0.48% |
| >10 | 0% |

중증 응급은 드물다: 80.66%가 12개월간 EMS 호출 없음.

### Self-Assessed Competence Gaps

| Emergency | % Not Confident |
|---|---|
| Hypertensive crisis | **74.47%** |
| Asthma | 55.61% |
| Anaphylactic shock | 55.13% |
| Seizures | 52.99% |
| Cardiac arrest | 41.29% |

역량 격차가 가장 큰 항목은 고혈압 위기 (Hypertensive Crisis) 74.47%이며, 아나필락시스 (Anaphylaxis) 55.13%와 천식 (Asthma) 55.61%도 심각. 심정지 (Cardiac Arrest) 41.29%는 상대적으로 낮지만 여전히 높은 수준.

## Clinical Relevance

1. **VVS dominates** — vasovagal syncope (VVS) is the primary dental emergency by frequency; positioning and basic supportive care protocols should be most drilled
2. **Anaphylaxis gap** — despite 16.23% prevalence of allergic events, 55.13% lack anaphylaxis management confidence; this is the critical gap for patient safety given the potential lethality (connects to [[drug/systemic-disease/jevon-2020-management-anaphylaxis-dental-practice-update]])
3. **Low serious-emergency rates ≠ low need for training** — 80.66% had zero EMS calls, but when serious events occur (cardiac arrest, anaphylaxis), rapid correct response is life-saving
4. **Comparability** — Polish data aligns with other countries, suggesting systemic global undertraining rather than a Poland-specific issue

## Limitations

- Abstract-only source; detailed subgroup analyses, equipment availability data, and CPR training frequencies not available from abstract
- Self-assessed competence may not reflect actual performance
- Cross-sectional design; no longitudinal follow-up
- Questionnaire-based; response rate and sampling method not extractable from abstract

## Conclusion

Dental office emergency prevalence in Poland mirrors international data. VVS is the dominant event; anaphylaxis, cardiac arrest, and seizures are less frequent but competence gaps are largest for these conditions. The study recommends enhanced undergraduate and postgraduate emergency training and wider dental office emergency equipment availability.
