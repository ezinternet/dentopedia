## Three-line Summary

Cochrane SR+MA pub5 (search through Nov 2025): 15 RCTs, 2874 participants — updates pub4 (2013) with nearly 3× more trials and participants.

Prophylactic antibiotics (primarily single preoperative 2g amoxicillin) probably reduce implant failures (RR 0.34; 95% CI 0.22–0.53; NNT=19), prosthesis failures, and postoperative infections vs placebo/no antibiotics (all moderate certainty).

Single dose is equivalent to multiple doses; no meaningful difference between preop vs postop timing or amoxicillin vs clindamycin — insufficient evidence to determine optimal regimen.

## Summary

This 2026 Cochrane update (pub5) by Esposito et al. is the most comprehensive RCT synthesis of antibiotic prophylaxis at dental implant placement to date, extending the search from 2013 (pub4) through November 2025. Including 15 RCTs (vs 6 in pub4) with 2874 participants across Europe, North America, and the Middle East, the review confirms that a single preoperative 2g oral dose of amoxicillin probably reduces early implant failures from approximately 80 to 27 per 1000 participants (NNT≈19; RR 0.34; moderate certainty), and similarly reduces prosthesis failures and postoperative infections. Single-dose regimens are equivalent to multi-dose regimens; timing (pre- vs post-operative) and antibiotic choice (amoxicillin vs clindamycin) yield no detectable differences, though evidence for these comparisons is low to very low certainty. One hospitalisation-requiring allergic reaction was reported in a multiple-dose group. The review concludes that antibiotic prophylaxis is justified but that the optimal regimen beyond the standard single preoperative amoxicillin dose remains unknown.

## Key Contributions

- Updates pub4 (6 RCTs, NNT=25) with 15 RCTs (NNT=19), strengthening the benefit estimate
- First Cochrane update to directly compare single vs multiple doses (low certainty: equivalent)
- Documents one serious adverse event (hospitalisation) — argues for parsimony
- GRADE: moderate certainty for implant failure, prosthesis failure, and infection outcomes
- Confirms that the standard "2g amoxicillin 1 hour preop" protocol is evidence-based and parsimonious

## Methodology

Cochrane SR+MA (pub5); databases searched Jan 2013–Nov 2025 (CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, WHO ICTRP, ClinicalTrials.gov). Eligible RCTs: minimum 3-month follow-up, ≥10 per arm, no concomitant bone augmentation. 15 RCTs included; 8 low risk of bias, 7 high risk. Random-effects MA; RR with 95% CI; GRADE.
