## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (University of Genoa, Dent J 2025) of RCTs and cohort studies evaluating antibiotic prophylaxis for postoperative complication prevention after third-molar extractions, published following the Camps-Font 2024 NMA.

Antibiotic prophylaxis offers only marginal benefit for routine simple third-molar extractions in healthy patients; benefit is somewhat clearer but still modest for surgically impacted extractions involving greater soft- and hard-tissue disruption.

Routine antibiotic prophylaxis in healthy adults undergoing simple third-molar extraction is not justified given the high NNT (approximately 25 for dry socket per Camps-Font 2024); prescription should be reserved for complex impacted cases with elevated infection risk.

## 세줄요약

Univ Genoa(Dent J 2025)에서 발표한 체계적 문헌고찰로, 제3대구치 발치 후 합병증 예방을 위한 항생제 예방투여의 RCT 및 코호트 연구를 평가; Camps-Font 2024 NMA 이후 발표.

건강한 환자의 단순 제3대구치 발치에서 항생제 예방은 효과가 미미하고, 매복·외과적 발치에서도 효과가 약간 더 명확하지만 여전히 제한적임.

건강한 성인의 단순 발치에서 루틴 예방투여는 높은 치료 필요 수(NNT ≈ 25, Camps-Font 2024)를 고려할 때 정당화되지 않으며, 고위험 매복 발치(Pell-Gregory III/C 등)에 한해 고려해야 한다.

## Summary
SR on antibiotic prophylaxis in third-molar extraction. Confirms that prophylaxis offers only marginal benefit for routine simple extractions, with somewhat clearer benefit (still modest) for surgically impacted extractions where soft/hard tissue disruption is greater.

## Key Contributions
- Updated 2025 SR after the NMA-level work (Camps-Font 2024).
- Discriminates simple vs surgical extraction context — important nuance for routine practice decisions.

## Methodology
SR of antibiotic prophylaxis RCTs/cohorts in third molar extraction.

## Results
[확인] Prophylaxis marginal in simple extractions; modest in surgical/impacted extractions; NNT remains high (25 for dry socket per Camps-Font NMA), arguing against routine prescription in healthy patients.

## Limitations
- SR without NMA (Camps-Font 2024 provides the NMA)
- Subgroup data by impaction class limited

## Clinical Implication
For Korean dental practice: routine antibiotic prophylaxis for simple third molar extraction in healthy adults is not justified. For complex impacted cases (Pell-Gregory III/C, anatomically high-risk), consider preoperative single-dose amoxicillin 2g — but the NNT remains high and the AMR argument increasingly weighs against it.
