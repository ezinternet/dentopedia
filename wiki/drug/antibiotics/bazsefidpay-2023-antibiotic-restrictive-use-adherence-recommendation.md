## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional adherence audit at Karolinska Institutet (Sweden, BMC Oral Health 2023) evaluating compliance with restrictive dental antibiotic prescribing guidelines and monitoring for safety signals within that framework.

Restrictive antibiotic prescribing was confirmed safe — no excess post-treatment complications were observed — but adherence to guidelines varied substantially across dentists and practices.

Sweden's long-established restrictive-prescribing culture validates the safety of this approach; the key implementation challenge identified is not efficacy but inter-clinician adherence variability, which requires targeted behavioral interventions.

## 세줄요약

스웨덴 Karolinska Institutet 단면 감사 연구(BMC Oral Health 2023)로, 치과 항생제 제한적 처방(Restrictive Prescribing) 가이드라인의 준수율과 안전성을 평가.

제한적 처방은 안전함이 확인되었고(과잉 합병증 없음), 치과의사 및 기관별 가이드라인 준수율은 상당한 편차를 보였다.

스웨덴의 장기적 제한적 처방 문화가 안전성을 입증하며, 핵심 과제는 효능이 아닌 임상의 간 adherence 변동성으로 표적화된 행동 중재가 필요하다.

## Summary
Swedish Karolinska Institutet observational study assessing adherence to restrictive antibiotic prescribing guidelines in dentistry and the safety profile of the restrictive approach. Confirms that restrictive prescribing — long the Swedish norm — is clinically safe and identifies adherence gaps as the next intervention target.

## Key Contributions
- Swedish national-level data supporting the restrictive-prescribing approach.
- Demonstrates safety of restrictive use (no excess complications).
- Identifies adherence variability as the implementation challenge.

## Methodology
Adherence audit + complication monitoring within Swedish restrictive prescribing context.

## Results
[확인] Restrictive prescribing safe; adherence variable across dentists/practices.

## Limitations
- Single country; Sweden has long-established restrictive prescribing culture
- Observational
