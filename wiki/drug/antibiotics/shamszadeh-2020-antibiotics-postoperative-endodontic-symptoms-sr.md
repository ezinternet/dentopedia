## Three-line Summary

Prophylactic oral antibiotics do not significantly reduce post-operative pain or swelling following endodontic treatment in adults with pulpal necrosis. Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs (n=690) for pain and 4 RCTs (n=149) for swelling found no significant SMDs at any time point (6–72 h). Current evidence does not support antibiotic prophylaxis for managing post-endodontic symptoms.

## 세줄요약

예방적 항생제 (prophylactic antibiotics)는 괴사 치수 (pulpal necrosis) 환자의 근관치료 후 통증 및 부종 감소에 유의미한 효과가 없다. 통증 8건의 무작위 대조시험 (Randomised Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=690), 부종 4건의 RCT (n=149)에서 6·12·24·48·72시간 모든 시점의 표준화 평균차 (Standardised Mean Difference, SMD)가 통계적으로 유의하지 않았다. 현 근거는 근관치료 후 증상 예방 목적의 항생제 처방을 지지하지 않는다.

## Summary

This 2020 systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated whether prophylactic oral antibiotics reduce post-operative pain and swelling in adults undergoing endodontic treatment for pulpal necrosis. Searching all RCTs up to April 2020, the authors pooled data using standardised mean differences (SMDs) under a random-effects inverse-variance model.

Across eight RCTs for pain (n=690), antibiotic administration produced no statistically significant reduction in pain at 6, 12, 24, 48, or 72 hours post-operatively. Across four RCTs for swelling (n=149), there was likewise no significant effect at 24, 48, or 72 hours. The authors conclude that prophylactic antibiotics are not supported for managing post-endodontic symptoms.

## Key Contributions

- Provides pooled quantitative evidence (SR/MA level) that prophylactic antibiotics lack efficacy for post-endodontic pain and swelling
- Quantifies null effect with SMDs and 95% CIs across five time points for pain and three for swelling
- Supports antibiotic stewardship: routine prophylaxis in non-complicated necrotic pulp cases is not evidence-based

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis
- **Population**: Adults with pulpal necrosis undergoing endodontic treatment
- **Intervention**: Prophylactic oral antibiotics vs. placebo
- **Outcomes**: Post-operative pain, swelling, combined pain+swelling
- **Statistical approach**: SMD, 95% CI; random-effects inverse-variance model
- **Pain pool**: 8 RCTs, n = 690
- **Swelling pool**: 4 RCTs, n = 149
- **Time points assessed**: 6, 12, 24, 48, 72 hours

## Results

### Post-operative Pain

| Time | SMD | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 h | −0.008 | −0.279 – 0.264 | .95 |
| 12 h | −0.080 | −1.39 – 1.23 | .90 |
| 24 h | −0.044 | −0.29 – 0.20 | .72 |
| 48 h | 0.18 | −0.26 – 0.62 | .42 |
| 72 h | −0.050 | −0.33 – 0.23 | .723 |

### Post-operative Swelling

| Time | SMD | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 h | 0.29 | −0.72 – 1.32 | .56 |
| 48 h | −0.23 | −0.98 – 0.51 | .54 |
| 72 h | −0.03 | −1.25 – 1.33 | .96 |

No statistically significant antibiotic effect was found at any time point for either outcome.
