## One-line Summary
Cochrane Protocol (2026): systematic review in progress to evaluate systemic antibiotic prophylaxis vs no antibiotics for dental implant placement with concomitant bone augmentation — no results yet.

## Three-line Summary

Visconti et al. (2026) registered a Cochrane protocol (CD016385) for a systematic review evaluating systemic prophylactic antibiotics versus no antibiotics for dental implant placement when performed with concomitant bone augmentation procedures, specifically targeting guided bone regeneration (GBR) with resorbable or non-resorbable membranes and sinus floor elevation. This protocol addresses a clinically important gap: existing antibiotic prophylaxis evidence primarily comes from studies of implant placement without augmentation, while augmented procedures carry higher infection risk.

The protocol establishes rigorous methodology including only parallel-group RCTs with ≥10 participants per arm and ≥3-month follow-up, with the statistical unit as the participant (not implant) to avoid unit-of-analysis errors. Planned subgroup analyses will examine augmentation type (horizontal vs vertical GBR, resorbable vs non-resorbable membrane). Outcomes of critical importance are implant failure and prosthetic failure at ≤1 year; important outcomes include postoperative infection and adverse events (gastrointestinal, allergic).

As of the protocol stage, no results are available. One pre-existing RCT found no benefit from systemic antibiotics in healthy patients with simultaneous GBR, while conflicting findings exist in the broader literature. The companion Cochrane review for implants without augmentation (Esposito 2013) provides context for interpreting future results from this protocol.

## 세줄요약

비스콘티 등(2026)은 골증대술(Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR 또는 상악동 거상) 동반 임플란트 식립 시 전신 예방적 항생제(Prophylactic Antibiotic) 투여 대 비투여를 평가하는 코크란 프로토콜(CD016385)을 등록했다. 기존 항생제 예방 근거가 주로 골증대술 비시행 사례에서 비롯됐다는 갭을 겨냥한다.

분석 단위를 임플란트가 아닌 참여자로 설정해 단위 분석 오류를 방지한다. 흡수성·비흡수성 차폐막 별, 수평·수직 GBR 별 하위군 분석이 계획됐다.

현재 프로토콜 단계로 결과는 없다. 기존 1건의 RCT가 건강한 환자의 동시 GBR에서 전신 항생제 편익이 없음을 보고했으나 문헌 전체에 상충되는 결과가 존재한다. 비골증대술 임플란트 항생제 예방 코크란 리뷰(에스포시토 2013)가 향후 결과 해석의 맥락을 제공한다.

## Summary
> [!note] Protocol — No Results Yet
> This is a Cochrane Protocol (CD016385). The full review is currently in progress. This page will be updated when results are published.

This Cochrane protocol addresses a clinically important gap: existing evidence on antibiotic prophylaxis focuses on standard implant placement (without bone augmentation), yet augmented cases carry higher infection risk due to increased surgical complexity and use of biomaterials. The protocol will systematically evaluate whether and how systemic antibiotics benefit patients undergoing implant placement with simultaneous bone augmentation (grafts, GBR, sinus lift). It complements the existing Cochrane review on antibiotic prophylaxis for non-augmented implant placement.

## Key Contributions
- Targets a distinct higher-risk clinical subgroup: implants + bone augmentation vs standard implant placement
- Covers all augmentation types: bone grafts (autogenous, xenograft, allograft, synthetic), GBR with barrier membranes (resorbable and non-resorbable), simultaneous sinus lift
- Critical outcomes: implant failure, prosthetic failure (at ≤1 year)
- Important outcomes: postoperative infection, adverse events (GI, allergic reactions) (at 3 months)
- Companion review to: existing Cochrane review on antibiotics for non-augmented implant placement

## Methodology
- Design: Cochrane SR of parallel-group RCTs, minimum 3-month follow-up, ≥10 per arm
- Population: adults >18 undergoing implant placement with concomitant bone augmentation
- Comparisons: antibiotics vs no antibiotics/placebo; different regimens
- Synthesis: random-effects, Mantel-Haenszel, RR + 95% CI
- Certainty: GRADE (summary of findings for 4 comparison types)
- Subgroup analyses: augmentation type, horizontal vs vertical GBR, resorbable vs non-resorbable membrane
