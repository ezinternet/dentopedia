## Summary
> [!note] Protocol — No Results Yet
> This is a Cochrane Protocol (CD016385). The full review is currently in progress. This page will be updated when results are published.

This Cochrane protocol addresses a clinically important gap: existing evidence on antibiotic prophylaxis focuses on standard implant placement (without bone augmentation), yet augmented cases carry higher infection risk due to increased surgical complexity and use of biomaterials. The protocol will systematically evaluate whether and how systemic antibiotics benefit patients undergoing implant placement with simultaneous bone augmentation (grafts, GBR, sinus lift). It complements the existing Cochrane review on antibiotic prophylaxis for non-augmented implant placement.

## Key Contributions
- Targets a distinct higher-risk clinical subgroup: implants + bone augmentation vs standard implant placement
- Covers all augmentation types: bone grafts (autogenous, xenograft, allograft, synthetic), GBR with barrier membranes (resorbable and non-resorbable), simultaneous sinus lift
- Critical outcomes: implant failure, prosthetic failure (at ≤1 year)
- Important outcomes: postoperative infection, adverse events (GI, allergic reactions) (at 3 months)
- Companion review to: existing Cochrane review on antibiotics for non-augmented implant placement

## Methodology
- Design: Cochrane SR of parallel-group RCTs, minimum 3-month follow-up, ≥10 per arm
- Population: adults >18 undergoing implant placement with concomitant bone augmentation
- Comparisons: antibiotics vs no antibiotics/placebo; different regimens
- Synthesis: random-effects, Mantel-Haenszel, RR + 95% CI
- Certainty: GRADE (summary of findings for 4 comparison types)
- Subgroup analyses: augmentation type, horizontal vs vertical GBR, resorbable vs non-resorbable membrane
