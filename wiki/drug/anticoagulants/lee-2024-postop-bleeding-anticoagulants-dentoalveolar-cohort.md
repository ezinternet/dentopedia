## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=537, Yonsei): 6.5% post-op bleeding overall; atrial fibrillation (OR=6.05) and vitamin K inhibitors (OR=3.68) highest risk in maintenance group; extraction+bone graft and multiple implants increase procedure risk.

## 한줄요약

## Summary
This retrospective cohort study (n=537) used a standardized common data model to analyze post-operative bleeding risk in anticoagulant patients undergoing dentoalveolar surgery (extraction, periodontal flap, or implant) at Yonsei University Dental Hospital between 2016–2021. Patients were divided into those who maintained anticoagulants (n=245) versus those who discontinued (n=292) by 1–7 days. Overall bleeding occurred in 6.5% (35/537), with 8.6% in the maintenance group versus 4.8% in the discontinuation group. In the maintenance group, atrial fibrillation (OR=6.05) and vitamin K inhibitors vs. DOACs/PAIs (OR=3.68) were the dominant independent risk factors. Procedure-related risk factors included extraction combined with bone grafting, multiple implants, and maxillary arch involvement. All bleeding episodes were managed with local hemostatic measures.

## Key Contributions
- First large cohort (n=537) covering all dentoalveolar procedures (not just extraction) in anticoagulant patients using standardized CDM
- Vitamin K antagonists carry ~3.7× higher OR than DOACs or antiplatelet agents in the maintenance group
- Atrial fibrillation is the most powerful systemic risk factor (OR=6.05), exceeding anticoagulant drug type
- Extraction+bone graft is independently higher risk than extraction alone — has clinical planning implications
- Multiple implants and maxillary arch involvement are independent procedure-related risk factors

## Methodology
Retrospective cohort, single center, Yonsei University Dental Hospital. Patients on anticoagulants (PAI, VKI, or DOAC) undergoing dentoalveolar surgery 2016–2021. Maintenance group (n=245) vs. discontinuation group (n=292, 1–7 days pre-procedure). Outcomes: post-operative bleeding events. Multivariate logistic regression for risk factor identification. CDM/SCRAP 2.0 platform for standardized data collection.

## Results
- Overall bleeding: 35/537 (6.5%)
- Maintenance group: 21/245 (8.6%); discontinuation: 14/292 (4.8%)
- Anticoagulant type (p=0.037), extraction+bone graft (p=0.016), implant type (p=0.032) significantly related to bleeding rate
- Multivariate (maintenance group): AF OR=6.051; VKI OR=3.679
- DOACs: bleeding rate comparable to PAI; lower than VKI
- All bleeding episodes controlled by local hemostatic measures
