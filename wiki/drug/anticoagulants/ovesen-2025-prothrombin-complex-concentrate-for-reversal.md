## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of RCTs only (5 trials, 825 participants; GRADE + Trial Sequential Analysis) of prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC) in VKA- or factor Xa-related critical bleeding.
VKA: PCC vs FFP showed no difference in mortality (RR 1.05, 0.27–4.05) or serious adverse events (RR 1.33, 0.94–1.88), but PCC conclusively reduced tardy INR correction (RR 0.41, 0.32–0.52); factor Xa: PCC had poorer haemostatic efficacy than andexanet alfa (RR 1.46, 1.15–1.85) with a borderline lower thromboembolism signal (RR 0.55, 0.30–1.00).
PCC corrects INR better than FFP but RCTs cannot yet show it improves patient outcomes; guidelines still uniformly recommend PCC first-line for life-threatening VKA bleeding.

## 세줄요약

VKA 또는 Xa 인자 억제제 관련 치명적 출혈에서 프로트롬빈 복합체 농축물 (Prothrombin Complex Concentrate, PCC) 을 본 RCT 한정 체계적 고찰 (5개 시험, 825명; GRADE + 시험순차분석 (Trial Sequential Analysis, TSA)).
VKA: PCC vs 신선동결혈장 (Fresh Frozen Plasma, FFP) 사망 (RR 1.05, 0.27–4.05)·중대 이상반응 (RR 1.33) 차이 없음, 그러나 지연된 국제정상화비율 (International Normalized Ratio, INR) 교정은 PCC가 확정적으로 감소 (RR 0.41, 0.32–0.52); Xa 억제제: PCC가 안덱사넷 알파 (Andexanet Alfa) 보다 지혈 효과 열세 (RR 1.46), 혈전색전은 경계선상 적음 (RR 0.55, 0.30–1.00).
PCC는 FFP보다 INR을 잘 교정하지만 환자 예후 개선은 RCT로 아직 입증되지 않았다 — 그럼에도 가이드라인은 생명을 위협하는 VKA 출혈의 1차 치료로 PCC를 일관되게 권고한다.

## Summary

Restricting evidence to randomised trials, this review finds that the laboratory advantage of PCC is solid while its patient-level advantage is unproven. In VKA-related critical bleeding, PCC reduced tardy INR correction with no heterogeneity and crossed the Trial Sequential Analysis superiority boundary, yet mortality, serious adverse events, thromboembolism and functional outcome did not differ from FFP, and every patient-relevant outcome was rated low or very low certainty. For factor Xa-related bleeding, PCC achieved haemostasis less often than andexanet alfa, while usual care (86% PCC) showed a borderline lower thromboembolism rate. The authors note that guidelines unanimously keep PCC first-line for VKA bleeding and recommend 4F-PCC or activated PCC for DOAC bleeding when specific antidotes are unavailable, and that no RCT compares idarucizumab with PCC.

## Key Contributions

- INR superiority of PCC over FFP is **conclusive** in RCTs (RR 0.41 for tardy correction).
- Mortality benefit seen in mixed-design meta-analyses is **not reproduced** in RCTs (RR 1.05).
- Factor Xa: andexanet gives better haemostasis than PCC (RR 1.46 for poor efficacy with PCC) but with a possible thromboembolic cost.
- Guideline consensus summarized: PCC first-line for VKA critical bleeding; FFP second-line.

## Methodology

- CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, LILACS, BIOSIS, Web of Science, registries; clinical study reports requested from regulators.
- 12,670 records → 5 RCTs, 825 participants (VKA 3 trials, 291; factor Xa 2 trials, 534); all open-label, all high risk of bias.
- Random-effects meta-analysis, Trial Sequential Analysis, Bayes factors, GRADE.
