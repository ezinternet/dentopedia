## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of ED reversal strategies for warfarin, heparin/LMWH, fondaparinux, and DOACs (dabigatran, apixaban, rivaroxaban, edoxaban, betrixaban) plus antiplatelets. Warfarin: IV vitamin K (onset 1–2 h, peak 4–6 h) ± 4-factor PCC (10–30 min, duration 12–24 h) or FFP (10–15 mL/kg). LMWH: protamine 1 mg per 1 mg enoxaparin if given within 8 h (~60% reversal). DOAC: no reversal if last dose >18 h; idarucizumab 5 g IV for dabigatran, andexanet alfa for factor Xa inhibitors.

## Summary

Emergency reversal reference covering hemostasis basics and agent-specific reversal. Key pharmacokinetic anchors: heparin half-life ~60 min; LMWH SC half-life 3–6 h; fondaparinux 17–21 h. DOAC peak ~2 h, steady state day 4; clinical effect diminishes so that at 18 h after last dose no reversal is indicated. Reversal decisions should weigh harm/benefit; morbidity/mortality benefit of reversal not definitively demonstrated.

## Key Contributions

- Warfarin reversal: IV vitamin K 10 mg (2012 ACCP) for life-threatening bleeding; onset 1–2 h, peak 4–6 h; never SC/IM. FFP 10–15 mL/kg (4–5 units), intrinsic INR 1.5, no benefit below INR 1.7, onset 13–48 h. PCC onset 10–30 min, immediate INR <1.5, duration 12–24 h; co-administer vitamin K to prevent rebound.
- LMWH: protamine 1 mg per 1 mg enoxaparin if administered within prior 8 h; only ~60% reversal of Xa effect; >50 mg protamine causes anticoagulation (factor V inhibition).
- Dabigatran: idarucizumab (Praxbind) 5 g IV (2×2.5 g vials); RE-VERSE AD: 88–98% rapid reversal; 30-day thrombotic events 4.8%.
- Factor Xa inhibitors: andexanet alfa (Andexxa) ANNEXA-4 — 92% median anti-Xa reduction, 82% good/excellent hemostasis at 12 h; 30-day mortality 14%, thrombotic events 10%.
- Antiplatelets: DDAVP 0.4 mcg/kg IV one-time for ICH on antiplatelets; platelets 2–3 units/apheresis units.

## Methodology

Narrative review; no original data. Synthesizes guidelines, RCTs (RE-VERSE AD, ANNEXA-4), volunteer studies, meta-analyses. Dosing tables referenced but not fully reproduced in extracted text.
