## One-line Summary

Triple-blind split-mouth RCT (n=30 warfarin patients, INR 2.0–3.5) showing TXA-soaked Gelfoam significantly reduces post-extraction pain from day 3 and accelerates socket healing vs saline Gelfoam, with no difference in hemostasis time.

## 한줄요약

와파린 환자(INR 2.0–3.5) triple-blind split-mouth RCT (n=30): TXA 적신 Gelfoam이 3일째부터 통증 유의하게 낮추고(p<0.001) 치유 가속; 지혈 시간은 차이 없음.

## Summary

This triple-blind split-mouth RCT from Alexandria University enrolled 30 warfarin patients (INR 2.0–3.5, diagnoses: rheumatic heart disease, DVT, AF) requiring bilateral single-rooted extractions. All patients continued warfarin without dose modification. Test sockets received Gelfoam pre-soaked in 4.8% TXA; control sockets received saline-soaked Gelfoam, with suture in both arms. The key finding was a significant reduction in VAS pain beginning at day 3 (p=0.005) and continuing through day 14 (p<0.001), along with significantly better Landry Healing Index scores at days 7 and 14. Hemostasis time was not significantly different between groups — suggesting that the Gelfoam scaffold itself may provide adequate hemostasis in this INR range, and TXA's added value is primarily antifibrinolytic (maintaining the formed clot) rather than hemostatic (accelerating clot formation). No rebleeding, dry socket, or infection occurred in either group.

## Key Contributions

- Establishes TXA-soaked Gelfoam as superior to saline Gelfoam for pain control and healing in warfarin patients — extends TXA benefit beyond hemostasis
- Triple-blind design and split-mouth pairing represent highest methodological quality among local hemostatic RCTs in anticoagulated patients in this wiki
- Finding that hemostasis time is equivalent between groups challenges the assumption that TXA's primary socket benefit is speed of initial hemostasis
- Demonstrates that warfarin continuation at INR 2.0–3.5 is compatible with safe extraction when a gelatin sponge is used with suture

## Methodology

**Design**: triple-blind (patient, operator, assessor) split-mouth RCT; Alexandria University; registered PACTR202312726741851. **Population**: 30 warfarin patients, INR 2.0–3.5 (rheumatic heart disease, DVT, AF); bilateral single-rooted extractions; ≥2-week interval between sides. **Intervention**: Gelfoam soaked in TXA 4.8% + 3-0 silk suture. **Control**: Gelfoam soaked in saline + suture. **Outcomes**: VAS pain (10-cm) at days 1/3/7/14; Landry Healing Index at days 7/14; hemostasis time (min). **Statistics**: SPSS v26; Mann-Whitney U, Wilcoxon, Friedman; p<0.05.

## Results

| Day | TXA-Gelfoam VAS | Saline-Gelfoam VAS | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5.53 ± 0.93 | 5.47 ± 0.87 | NS |
| 3 | 2.67 ± 0.85 | 3.13 ± 0.43 | 0.005 |
| 7 | 0.73 ± 0.78 | 1.80 ± 1.00 | <0.001 |
| 14 | 0.13 ± 0.35 | 0.80 ± 0.61 | <0.001 |

- Landry Healing Index: TXA significantly higher at days 7 and 14 (p<0.001)
- Hemostasis time: no significant difference between groups
- Complications: 0 rebleeding, 0 dry socket, 0 infection in either group
