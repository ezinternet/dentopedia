## Three-line Summary
Single-center cross-sectional study (University of Genova, Italy; BMC Oral Health 2024) of 93 patients, adapting the Ivy bleeding-time test to the oral cavity to compare intraoral bleeding time during a professional oral hygiene (scaling/polishing) session across six drug-exposure groups: VKA (warfarin/acenocoumarol), NOAC-DTI (dabigatran), NOAC-AntiXa (apixaban/rivaroxaban), SAPT, DAPT, and a drug-free control.

Mean bleeding time was longest for VKA (203 s) and DAPT (190 s) — both significantly higher than control (50 s, p<0.001) — and DTI (120 s) was also significant (p=0.004), while AntiXa (98 s) and SAPT (105 s) did not differ significantly from control; time since last dose (<5 h vs ≥5 h) showed no significant within-group difference, though NOAC groups showed a non-significant trend toward shorter bleeding time closer to the next dose.

None of the prolonged bleeding interfered with completion of the hygiene session (gauze pressure alone sufficed in every case), supporting uninterrupted anticoagulant/antiplatelet therapy for routine scaling; the authors speculate 2–3 h before the next NOAC dose as a candidate (non-mandatory) scheduling window, but the study is single-center, unpowered, and did not record periodontal parameters (BOP%, PD, plaque index).

## 세줄요약
이탈리아 제노바 대학(University of Genova) 단일기관 단면연구(cross-sectional study, BMC Oral Health 2024, n=93) — 구강 내로 변형 적용한 Ivy 출혈시간 검사(bleeding time test)로 전문가 구강위생관리(scaling/polishing) 시술 **중** 발생하는 구강내 출혈시간을 비타민K길항제(Vitamin K Antagonist, VKA), 직접트롬빈억제제(Direct Thrombin Inhibitor, DTI), factor Xa억제제(AntiXa), 단일항혈소판요법(Single Antiplatelet Therapy, SAPT), 이중항혈소판요법(Dual Antiplatelet Therapy, DAPT), 무복용 대조군 6개 군 간 비교.

평균 출혈시간은 VKA 203초·DAPT 190초로 가장 길어 대조군(50초) 대비 유의하게 증가(p<0.001), DTI(120초)도 유의(p=0.004)했으나 AntiXa(98초)·SAPT(105초)는 대조군과 유의차 없음; 마지막 복용 후 경과시간(5시간 기준 분할)은 군내 유의차 없었으나 NOAC 군에서는 복용 간격이 지날수록 출혈시간이 짧아지는 비유의적 경향 관찰.

출혈 연장이 있었던 군에서도 거즈 압박만으로 시술 완료에 지장 없었음 — 정규 스케일링 전 항응고·항혈소판 요법 중단이 불필요함을 지지하며, 저자들은 NOAC의 경우 다음 복용 2–3시간 전을 (중단 없이) 예약 배치의 후보 시점으로 제안하나, 단일기관·검정력분석 미실시·치주 지표(BOP%, PD, 치태지수) 미기록이라는 한계가 있음.

## Summary
This cross-sectional study at the University of Genova recruited 93 patients on regular biannual dental-hygiene recall — 79 taking an anticoagulant or antiplatelet drug, plus 14 drug-free controls — and adapted the classic Ivy bleeding-time test to the oral cavity: the first bleeding-on-probing site found during full-mouth probing became the assay site, with gauze pressure applied every 15 seconds until spontaneous hemostasis. Patients were then treated with a routine professional oral hygiene session (ultrasonic scaling + polishing). Six groups were compared: VKA (warfarin/acenocoumarol), NOAC-DTI (dabigatran), NOAC-AntiXa (apixaban/rivaroxaban), SAPT, DAPT, and control. VKA (203 s) and DAPT (190 s) produced the longest bleeding times, roughly 4× and 3.8× the control (50 s) respectively (both p<0.001); DTI (120 s) was also significantly elevated (p=0.004), while AntiXa (98 s) and SAPT (105 s) were not significantly different from control. Critically, none of the prolonged bleeding required anything beyond gauze tamponade — no group needed sutures, hemostatic drugs, or cautery — meaning the drug classes with the longest measured bleeding time still did not translate into a procedure-disrupting clinical event. This is the only paper in this wiki's anticoagulant holdings that measures bleeding during a non-surgical, routine scaling session rather than extraction or implant surgery, making it directly relevant to dental-hygienist chairside practice.

## Key Contributions
- First adaptation of the Ivy bleeding-time test to an intraoral site (versus the classic volar-forearm incision), enabling direct measurement of drug-class effect during a routine hygiene procedure rather than inference from postoperative bleeding reports.
- Six-arm comparison (VKA, NOAC-DTI, NOAC-AntiXa, SAPT, DAPT, drug-free control) rather than the field's more common two-arm continue-vs-discontinue design, ranking drug classes directly against each other and against a true untreated baseline.
- Empirically confirms that DAPT (aspirin + clopidogrel) produces roughly double the bleeding time of either drug alone (SAPT) — consistent with mechanism (COX inhibition + ADP-receptor blockade acting on separate platelet-activation pathways).
- Shows VKA and DAPT bleeding times 3–4× control are still manageable with gauze pressure alone during a non-surgical procedure, i.e., a statistically significant difference did not translate into a clinically unmanageable one.
- From a secondary, non-significant timing analysis, proposes 2–3 hours before the next NOAC dose as a candidate scheduling window that avoids both peak anticoagulation and medication interruption — framed explicitly by the authors as speculation, not a validated protocol.

## Methodology
- **Design**: single-center cross-sectional study, Italy; ethics approval CERA 2024/08; written informed consent.
- **Population**: 93 patients on regular biannual dental-hygiene recall, no heavy calculus, natural or tooth-supported teeth; excluded if <18y, pregnant/lactating, MI within 6 months, immunosuppressed, uncontrolled diabetes, head/neck radiation history, periodontitis, edentulous, or implant-supported restorations.
- **Groups (n, mean age)**: VKA 27 (62y) · NOAC-DTI 15 (58y) · NOAC-AntiXa 13 (60y) · SAPT 12 (56y) · DAPT 12 (56y) · Control 14 (49y).
- **Bleeding-time protocol**: 1-min chlorhexidine rinse → full-mouth bleeding-on-probing charting (tooth 1.6→2.6, vestibular, PCP UNC 15 probe) → first bleeding site timed by a second, blinded-to-group operator, gauze pressure every 15 s until spontaneous hemostasis ("Ivy time," seconds) → ultrasonic scaling (EMS) + polishing (Cleanic paste, rubber cup), hand scaler as needed.
- **Statistics**: non-parametric (small, non-Gaussian subgroups) — Kruskal-Wallis + Dunn's post-hoc, or two-way ANOVA + Dunnett post-hoc; median [IQR]; α=0.05. Secondary median-split analysis (5 h) for time-since-last-dose.

## Results
| Group | Drug(s) | n | Mean bleeding time (s) | Range (s) | vs. Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Control | none | 14 | 50 | 39–61 | — |
| AntiXa | apixaban, rivaroxaban | 13 | 98 | 74–160 | ns |
| SAPT | aspirin or clopidogrel | 12 | 105 | 50–120 | ns |
| DTI | dabigatran | 15 | 120 | 60–155 | p = 0.004 |
| DAPT | aspirin + clopidogrel | 12 | 190 | 143–240 | p < 0.001 |
| VKA | warfarin, acenocoumarol | 27 | 203 | 140–281 | p < 0.001 |

- No clinically significant bleeding episode escalated beyond gauze tamponade in any group.
- Time-since-last-dose (<5 h vs ≥5 h) showed no significant within-group difference; VKA stayed >200 s regardless of timing (consistent with warfarin's 48–72 h half-life). NOAC groups trended toward shorter bleeding time at ≥5 h, non-significantly.
- Within NOAC, rivaroxaban produced the longest bleeding time, followed by dabigatran, then apixaban (Discussion-level ordering).
