## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 13 studies (5 randomised, 8 observational; searched to December 2015) comparing prothrombin complex concentrate (PCC) with fresh frozen plasma (FFP), each given with vitamin K, for urgent warfarin reversal in major bleeding or urgent surgery.
PCC reduced all-cause mortality (OR 0.56, 95% CI 0.37–0.84), achieved INR normalization more often (OR 10.80) and 6.5 h faster, and reduced volume overload (OR 0.27), without more thromboembolism (OR 0.91, NS); haemostasis OR 2.00 was NS.
Supports PCC over FFP as the factor-replacement partner to vitamin K; the mortality benefit rests largely on observational data and was not reproduced in RCT-only analyses.

## 세줄요약

주요 출혈 또는 응급 수술 시 와파린 (Warfarin) 긴급 역전에서 비타민 K와 함께 투여한 프로트롬빈 복합체 농축물 (Prothrombin Complex Concentrate, PCC) 과 신선동결혈장 (Fresh Frozen Plasma, FFP) 을 비교한 SR+MA (13편 — 무작위 5, 관찰 8; 2015년 12월까지).
PCC가 전체 사망 감소 (OR 0.56, 95% CI 0.37–0.84), 국제정상화비율 (International Normalized Ratio, INR) 정상화 증가 (OR 10.80)·6.5시간 단축, 용적 과부하 감소 (OR 0.27); 혈전색전증 차이 없음 (OR 0.91), 지혈 달성 OR 2.00은 유의하지 않음.
비타민 K와 짝지을 인자 보충제로 FFP보다 PCC를 지지하나, 사망 이득은 주로 관찰연구에 기대며 RCT만 본 분석에서는 재현되지 않았다.

## Summary

The authors define urgent warfarin reversal as vitamin K combined with coagulation-factor replacement, then ask which replacement — PCC or FFP — performs better on clinically relevant outcomes. Pooling 13 studies, PCC was associated with lower all-cause mortality, far higher odds of INR normalization, INR correction about 6.5 hours sooner, and less transfusion-associated volume overload, with no excess thromboembolism. Achieved haemostasis and red-cell transfusion did not differ significantly. Because 8 of 13 studies were observational, the mortality signal is open to confounding by indication; a later RCT-only review did not reproduce it.

## Key Contributions

- Reversal = **vitamin K + PCC (or FFP)** — vitamin K is not replaced by PCC, it is paired with it.
- PCC beats FFP on speed and completeness of INR correction and on volume overload.
- Significant mortality reduction (OR 0.56) in a mixed-design pool.

## Methodology

- MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Library to December 2015.
- 13 studies: 5 randomised, 8 observational; warfarin patients with major bleeding or needing urgent surgery.
- Abstract only: risk-of-bias assessment, heterogeneity and patient counts not available in this ingest.

## Results

| Outcome | PCC vs FFP |
|---|---|
| All-cause mortality | OR 0.56 (0.37–0.84), p=0.006 |
| Haemostasis | OR 2.00 (0.85–4.68), NS |
| INR normalization | OR 10.80 (6.12–19.07) |
| Time to INR correction | −6.50 h (−9.75 to −3.24) |
| RBC transfusion | OR 0.88 (0.53–1.43), NS |
| Volume overload | OR 0.27 (0.13–0.58) |
| Thromboembolism | OR 0.91 (0.44–1.89), NS |

**Dental reading.** The setting is major bleeding and urgent surgery in hospital. For a dental office, the transferable point is the order of tools: vitamin K for the INR, PCC when correction must be immediate — both decided by the prescriber or emergency team, after local haemostasis has failed.
