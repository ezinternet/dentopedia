## Three-line Summary

Prospective split-mouth RCT (n=100 antiplatelet patients, 71% SAPT/29% DAPT) comparing chitosan hemostatic sponge vs cotton gauze after dental extraction without drug discontinuation.
Chitosan achieved hemostasis in median 0.67 min vs 4.5 min for gauze (p<0.001); 100% sockets hemostatic by 3 min vs only 11% for gauze; lower VAS pain and higher Landry healing index at day 7.
Chitosan activates platelets via TLR2 pathway even under antiplatelet pharmacology; dry socket rate not significantly different (1% vs 3%); supports use without antiplatelet discontinuation.

## One-line Summary

Prospective RCT (n=100 antiplatelet patients) demonstrating chitosan sponge achieves hemostasis in 0.67 min vs 4.5 min for gauze (p<0.001), with superior pain and healing outcomes at 7 days.

## 한줄요약

항혈소판제(SAPT·DAPT) 환자 RCT (n=100): 키토산 스폰지 중앙값 0.67분 vs 거즈 4.5분(p<0.001), 3분 내 지혈 100% vs 11%; 통증·치유도 7일째 키토산군 유의하게 우수.

## Summary

This prospective randomized study enrolled 100 adults on uninterrupted antiplatelet therapy (71% single-agent aspirin, 29% DAPT) requiring simple molar extractions in Indore, India (May 2023–Oct 2024). Group A received a chitosan hemostatic sponge (~1×1 cm, compressed 30–40 s, then removed after saline irrigation); Group B received standard cotton gauze under biting pressure. The primary outcome — time to hemostasis measured by stopwatch — showed a dramatic difference: median 0.67 min (chitosan) vs 4.5 min (gauze), with all chitosan sites hemostatic within 3 min compared to only 11% of gauze sites. Secondary outcomes confirmed additional benefits: lower VAS pain and higher Landry Healing Index at 7 days (both p<0.001). Dry socket was rare and comparable (1% vs 3%; NS). No drug discontinuation was required.

## Key Contributions

- Largest prospective RCT of chitosan vs gauze specifically in antiplatelet patients (n=100)
- Demonstrates chitosan efficacy across both SAPT and DAPT without drug withdrawal
- Confirms secondary benefits: pain and healing advantages beyond hemostasis speed
- Mechanistic insight: chitosan activates platelets via TLR2, bypassing antiplatelet pharmacology
- Very low dry socket rate (1%) with chitosan despite continued antiplatelet therapy

## Methodology

**Design**: prospective randomized; split-mouth where possible; May 2023–Oct 2024; Indore, India. **Population**: 100 adults (mean age 63.76±9.14 y; 75% male; 74% hypertensive, 34% diabetic) on uninterrupted antiplatelet therapy; Hb >9 g/dL, platelets ≥100,000/μL; NSAIDs avoided post-procedure. **Allocation**: patient/statistician blinded; operator unblinded. **Statistics**: SPSS v25; Wilcoxon signed-rank; McNemar.

## Results

- Hemostasis time: chitosan median 0.67 min (IQR 0.59–0.73) vs gauze 4.5 min (IQR 4.0–5.0); p<0.001
- 3-min hemostasis: 100% vs 11%; p<0.001
- DAPT subgroup: longer times (5.34 vs 4.11 min) but chitosan consistently effective
- VAS pain day 7: median 1.0 (IQR 0–1) vs 1.0 (IQR 1–2); p<0.001
- Landry day 7: median 5.0 (IQR 4–5) vs 4.0 (IQR 3–5); p<0.001
- Dry socket: 1% vs 3%; p=0.625 (NS)
