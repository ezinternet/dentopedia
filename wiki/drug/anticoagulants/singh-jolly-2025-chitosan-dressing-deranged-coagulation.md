## One-line Summary

Prospective split-mouth RCT (n=102 with deranged coagulation; 204 sockets) showing chitosan achieves 83.1% hemostasis at 10 min vs 18.8% for gauze, mean time 15.10 vs 45.20 min (p<0.001).

## 한줄요약

## Summary

This prospective split-mouth RCT from PGIMER Chandigarh enrolled 102 patients with genuinely deranged coagulation profiles (86 on anticoagulant therapy with INR 1.5–4, 15 with liver cirrhosis, 1 with thrombocytopenic purpura) requiring bilateral extractions. A chitosan dental dressing (test side) was compared directly against standard gauze pressure (control side) within the same patient. Medications were continued without alteration. The chitosan site achieved hemostasis in a mean 15.10 min vs 45.20 min for gauze (p<0.001); 83.1% of chitosan sockets were hemostatic within 10 min compared to only 18.8% of gauze sockets. An additional significant finding was reduced swelling on chitosan sides (3.9% vs 15.7%, p<0.05). Dry socket was numerically higher with chitosan (5.9% vs 1%) but not statistically significant. This study is the highest-risk hemostatic RCT currently in this wiki, operating at INR levels (up to 4.0) where gauze alone clearly fails.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates chitosan efficacy at INR up to 4.0 and in liver-cirrhosis coagulopathy — conditions where antiplatelet-focused studies (Agrawal 2025) cannot be extrapolated
- Split-mouth design eliminates between-patient INR variability: 102 patients, 204 sockets
- Shows that medication continuation (no bridging, no dose change) is safe when chitosan dressing is used
- Secondary benefit: significant reduction in post-extraction swelling with chitosan (anti-inflammatory mechanism via TLR2 + IL-1β modulation proposed)
- Dry socket signal (5.9% vs 1%) warrants monitoring in future larger trials

## Methodology

**Design**: prospective split-mouth RCT; PGIMER Chandigarh, India. **Population**: 102 patients with deranged coagulation requiring ≥2 extractions: 86 anticoagulated (INR 1.5–4), 15 liver cirrhosis, 1 thrombocytopenic purpura. Anticoagulants/medications continued unchanged. **Matching**: single-rooted vs single-rooted, multi-rooted vs multi-rooted. **Chitosan dressing**: placed in socket + finger pressure + standard gauze; removed following day. **Control**: standard gauze pack only. **Follow-up**: days 1, 3, 7. **Outcomes**: hemostasis at 10/30/60 min, mean hemostasis time, dry socket, infection, swelling.

## Results

| Hemostasis checkpoint | Chitosan (test) | Gauze (control) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 min | 58.8% | — |
| 10 min | 83.1% | 18.8% |
| 30 min | 93.9% | 35.5% |
| 60 min | 99.8% | 99.5% |

- Mean hemostasis time: **15.10 ± 12.88 min** vs **45.20 ± 20.62 min**; p<0.001
- Swelling: 3.9% vs 15.7%; p<0.05
- Dry socket: 5.9% vs 1.0%; p>0.05 (NS)
- Infection: 0 in both groups
