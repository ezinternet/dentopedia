## Three-line Summary

A case report of a hemodynamically unstable patient with enoxaparin-induced abdominal wall hematoma, last enoxaparin dose 21.5 h prior, presenting with therapeutic anti-Xa of 0.8 IU/mL; protamine sulfate 50 mg IV (0.5 mg per 1 mg enoxaparin) was administered. Renal dysfunction can accumulate enoxaparin and prolong therapeutic anti-Xa assays beyond 12 h. Future reversal dosing should be guided by the initial anti-Xa assay.

## 세줄요약

Enoxaparin 마지막 투여 21.5시간 후 발생한 복벽 혈종 환자에 대해 항-Xa 0.8 IU/mL로 protamine sulfate 50 mg IV(1 mg enoxaparin당 0.5 mg)를 투여한 사례 보고. 신기능 저하 시 enoxaparin 축적되어 12시간 이상 항-Xa가 치료 수준으로 유지될 수 있음. 향후 protamine 용량 결정 시 초기 항-Xa 검사를 기준으로 삼아야 함.

## Summary

Case report: hemodynamically unstable patient with enoxaparin-induced abdominal wall hematoma/hemorrhage, last enoxaparin 21.5 h prior, therapeutic anti-Xa 0.8 IU/mL on ED assessment; protamine sulfate 50 mg IV (0.5 mg per 1 mg enoxaparin) administered to reverse anticoagulation alongside resuscitation. Renal dysfunction can accumulate enoxaparin and prolong therapeutic anti-Xa beyond 12 h; future reversal dosing should be guided by initial anti-Xa assay.

## Key Contributions

- Protamine reversal effective at 21.5 h post-enoxaparin with therapeutic anti-Xa 0.8 IU/mL.
- Renal accumulation prolongs enoxaparin anti-Xa beyond 12 h.
- Recommends anti-Xa-guided protamine dosing.
