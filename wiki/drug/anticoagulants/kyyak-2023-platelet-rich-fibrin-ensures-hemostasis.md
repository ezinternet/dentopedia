## Three-line Summary

Prospective, double-blind, randomized split-mouth trial (Mainz, n=21 patients on FXa inhibitors, 42 extraction sockets) comparing a PRF plug vs a gelatine sponge without stopping the anticoagulant.

Mild oozing occurred at 28 of 42 sites (67%), 24 stopped within 30 min of gauze pressure and the longest within 1.5 h; no hemorrhage or delayed bleeding, and no PRF vs GS difference (all p>0.05).

PRF and GS are both acceptable socket inserts with sutures; FXa inhibitors need not be interrupted for simple extractions, but n=21, pooled drugs and no no-agent control limit inference.

## 세줄요약

전향적 이중맹검 무작위 분할구강 시험(마인츠, Xa 인자 억제제 복용 환자 21명, 발치와 42개)으로, 항응고제를 중단하지 않고 혈소판풍부피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)과 젤라틴 스펀지 (Gelatine Sponge, GS)를 비교했다.

## Summary

This split-mouth randomized trial from University Medical Center Mainz (source: PubMed, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00784-023-05317-3)) tested whether an autologous platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) plug controls post-extraction bleeding as well as a gelatine sponge (GS) in 21 patients taking rivaroxaban, apixaban or edoxaban without drug interruption. Each patient had one tooth extracted per side of the same jaw (42 sockets), randomized by sealed envelope, with a blinded assessor. Mild oozing occurred at 28 sockets and always stopped with gauze pressure within 1.5 h; there was no hemorrhage, no delayed bleeding and no PRF vs GS difference. The authors conclude that FXa inhibitors need not be interrupted for simple extractions and that either insert plus sutures is acceptable. The study is underpowered for drug-level or risk-factor comparisons and has no no-agent control socket.

## Key Contributions

- Randomized, blinded head-to-head of PRF vs a gelatine sponge specifically in patients on direct FXa inhibitors, with the drug continued.
- Time-resolved bleeding assessment up to day 7 with a blinded assessor.
- Hypothesis-generating signals: posterior mandibular sockets, age >85 y and higher-dose regimens had the longest oozing.

## Methodology

- Split-mouth, double-blind, sealed-envelope randomization; Mainz OMFS department, May-December 2022; ethics approval Prot. No. 2021-16116.
- Inclusion: FXa-inhibitor monotherapy, normal coagulation, two teeth to extract on contralateral sides of one jaw. Exclusions included hematologic disease, antiresorptive therapy, teeth needing osteotomy or subperiosteal preparation, and sinus communication.
- Test: PRF plug (A-PRF tubes, 1200 rpm, 177 g, 8 min). Control: Hygitec gelatine sponge. Both sockets curetted and closed with cross-mattress Vicryl 3/0 sutures; articaine 4% with epinephrine 1:200,000; drug taken as usual.
- Bleeding graded by gauze-tamponade duration (30 min, 1 h, 1.5 h) and severity (mild, moderate, severe) at 0, 30 min, 1 h, 1.5 h, 24 h and day 7.
- Paired t-test and Kruskal-Wallis; drug subgroups descriptive only.

## Results

| Outcome | Finding |
|---|---|
| Patients | 21; mean age 71 ± 2.8 y (45-89); 11 F / 10 M; 3 smokers |
| Drugs | rivaroxaban 9, apixaban 7, edoxaban 5 |
| Oozing events | 28 of 42 sites (67%): 24 stopped at 30 min, 2 at 1 h, 2 at 1.5 h |
| Severe or delayed bleeding | none; nothing after 1.5 h or at day 7 |
| PRF vs GS | no difference (all p>0.05) |
| By drug (events) | rivaroxaban 18, apixaban 14, edoxaban 10 (p=0.15 t-test; p=0.45 KWT); both 1.5-h cases on apixaban |
| Mandible vs maxilla | 18 vs 10 events (p=0.0008 t-test; p=0.02 KWT) |
| Posterior vs anterior | 16 vs 12 events (p=0.03 t-test; p=0.23 KWT) |
| Time since last dose | no association (p=0.1875) |

- Max creatinine 1.28 mg/dl; no correlation observed (not statistically tested). Both prolonged cases were >85 y.
- Caveats: no no-agent control; sutures in both arms; 21 patients; pooled FXa inhibitors; simple extractions only. The title says single-tooth removal, but each patient contributed two extractions.
