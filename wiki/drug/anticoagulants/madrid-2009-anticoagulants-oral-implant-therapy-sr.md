## One-line Summary
SR (19 studies, 1966–2008): OAT patients (INR 2–4) do not have significantly higher post-op bleeding than non-OAT patients; implant placement is NOT contraindicated under OAT; local hemostats sufficient.

## Summary
This systematic review searched Medline, Cochrane, and EMBASE (1966–2008) and included 19 studies (5 RCT, 11 CCT, 3 prospective case series) evaluating oral surgery procedures (mostly extractions) in patients on oral anticoagulation therapy (OAT). Results were highly homogeneous: minor bleeding occurred in few patients, with no significant difference between OAT-continued versus OAT-discontinued groups. Post-operative bleeding did not correlate with INR value within therapeutic range (2–4). Topical hemostatic agents were effective. No thromboembolic events were reported in any study. The authors concluded that OAT discontinuation is not recommended for minor oral surgery procedures including single tooth extraction and implant placement.

## Key Contributions
- First systematic review specifically addressing IMPLANT therapy (not just extraction) in OAT patients
- Establishes that OAT (INR 2–4) is NOT a contraindication for dental implant placement
- Confirms INR within range does not predict bleeding risk — questioning INR-based decision making
- Local hemostats (tranexamic acid, gelatin sponge, cellulose gauze) are effective and sufficient
- Defines scope of "safe" continuation: single extraction, implant without autogenous graft, no extensive flap
- Pre-DOAC era landmark — provides historical baseline for subsequent NOAC literature

## Methodology
Systematic review. Databases: Medline, Cochrane CDSR, CENTRAL, EMBASE (1966–December 2008). Language: English only. Included: 19 studies (5 RCT, 11 CCT, 3 prospective case series). Predominantly extraction studies; limited implant-specific data. Compared OAT continuation, discontinuation, and heparin bridging strategies. Heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis.

## Results
- OAT patients (INR 2–4) continued: no significant increase in post-op bleeding versus controls
- INR value within 2–4: NOT correlated with bleeding risk
- Local hemostats effective for bleeding control when OAT continued
- No thromboembolic events in any of 19 studies
- Recommends against OAT discontinuation for: single extraction, implant placement (without autogenous graft, extensive flap, or osteotomy outside bony envelope)
- Implant placement: NOT contraindicated under OAT
