## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with network meta-analysis of 8 randomized trials (search to 29 Oct 2022) on vitamin K antagonist (coumarin) management before dental surgery; DOAC trials were not included.

A 2-day coumarin suspension had lower bleeding risk than continuous full-dose therapy (OR 0.30, 95% CI 0.09-0.97); low-dose coumarin (OR 0.58) and LMWH (OR 0.79) did not differ; no thromboembolic events; gauze plus tranexamic acid was the most effective local agent.

Bleeding was mostly mild and locally controllable, but heterogeneity, unclear bias and few low-risk trials limit confidence, and the authors' conclusion sits awkwardly with their own 2-day suspension result.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 8편을 대상으로 한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA); 치과 수술 전 비타민K길항제 (Vitamin K Antagonist, VKA, 쿠마린) 관리 전략 비교이며 직접경구항응고제 (Direct Oral Anticoagulant, DOAC) 시험은 제외.

줄2: 2일 쿠마린 중단군이 지속 full-dose군보다 출혈 위험이 낮았고 (OR 0.30, 95% CI 0.09-0.97), 저용량 쿠마린 (OR 0.58)·저분자량 헤파린 (Low-Molecular-Weight Heparin, LMWH; OR 0.79)은 차이 없음; 혈전색전증 (Thromboembolism) 없음; 거즈+트라넥삼산 (Tranexamic Acid)이 국소 지혈에 가장 효과적.

줄3: 출혈은 대부분 경미하고 국소 지혈로 조절되었으나 이질성·불명확한 편향위험·소수의 저위험 연구가 한계이며, "중단해도 출혈 증가 없음"이라는 저자 결론은 자체 결과와 긴장 관계.

## Summary

Source: PubMed, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20075293) (Int J Environ Res Public Health 2023). This PROSPERO-registered (399590) systematic review screened 2051 records and included 8 RCTs, all testing coumarin-type vitamin K antagonists around dental surgery (one also reduced-dose, two 2-day suspension, one LMWH). A network meta-analysis restricted to low-risk-of-bias trials found a 2-day suspension associated with less bleeding than continuous full-dose coumarin (OR 0.30, 95% CI 0.09-0.97), whereas reduced-dose coumarin and LMWH did not differ significantly. No thromboembolic complications were reported and bleeding was generally mild, occurring on day 1 and controlled locally.

## Key Contributions

- Only network meta-analysis in this folder, comparing coumarin regimens and local haemostatic agents in one model.
- Quantifies regimen comparisons against continuous full-dose coumarin with confidence intervals.
- Narrative update (cited literature, not pooled) on antiplatelet, warfarin, heparin and DOAC handling.

## Methodology

PubMed/Medline, EMBASE, Cochrane to 29 Oct 2022; eligibility listed RCTs and prospective/retrospective studies at screening, non-English, reviews, case reports and in vitro excluded. OHAT risk-of-bias (RevMan 5.5), low risk = at least 5/7; only low-risk studies entered the network models (Owen et al. method).

## Results

- 2051 records, 82 full texts, 8 included (74 excluded: 36 off topic, 23 non-English, 3 case reports/series, 12 reviews).
- Preoperative INR heterogeneous but always below 3.0; bleeding mostly within 24 h; delayed bleeding in 4 studies.
- Risk of bias: over 75% unclear for performance and detection; only one RCT scored 7/7.

| vs continuous full coumarin | OR (95% CI) | p |
|---|---|---|
| 2-day suspension | 0.30 (0.09-0.97) | <0.05 |
| Low-dose coumarin | 0.58 (0.21-1.62) | >0.05 |
| LMWH | 0.79 (0.28-2.21) | >0.05 |

Gauze plus tranexamic acid was reported as most effective for early bleeding (p<0.05); effect sizes not given in the retrieved text.

## Limitations and Appraisal

- The conclusion that holiday does not raise bleeding, and that withdrawal or bridging carries more thrombotic than bleeding risk, is not supported by the pooled data: no thromboembolic events occurred, and the significant finding favoured suspension with a CI reaching 0.97.
- Antiplatelet and DOAC statements derive from cited literature; pooled trials were coumarin-only.
- Few low-risk trials; heterogeneous protocols.
