## One-line Summary

4-arm RCT (n=64 healthy molar extractions) showing green tea tannin extract (5.62 min), methanolic (7.50 min), and aqueous (8.44 min) extracts all dramatically reduce hemostasis time vs saline gauze (61.56 min; p<0.001).

## 한줄요약

건강 환자 대구치 발치 4군 RCT (n=64): 녹차 탄닌 추출물(TE) 5.62분·메탄올 추출물(ME) 7.50분·수성 추출물(AE) 8.44분 vs 식염수 거즈 61.56분 (p<0.001); 모든 녹차 추출물군 유의하게 우수.

## Three-line Summary

Four-arm parallel RCT (n=64, healthy patients, molar extractions) testing green tea extracts as topical haemostatic agents against saline gauze.

Mean haemostasis time was 5.62 min with tannin extract, 7.50 min with methanolic extract and 8.44 min with aqueous extract versus 61.56 min for saline gauze (all p<0.001).

All extracts stopped bleeding far faster than the control, but the control is not standard dry-gauze care, only healthy patients were studied (no anticoagulated data) and the data come from the abstract only.

## 세줄요약

건강한 환자의 대구치 발치 후 녹차 추출물을 국소 지혈제로 식염수 거즈와 비교한 4군 평행 RCT (64명)이다.

평균 지혈 시간은 탄닌 추출물 (Tannin Extract) 5.62분, 메탄올 추출물 7.50분, 수성 추출물 8.44분으로 식염수 거즈 61.56분보다 모두 유의하게 짧았다 (모두 p<0.001).

모든 추출물이 대조군보다 훨씬 빨리 지혈했으나 대조군이 표준 건조 거즈 처치가 아니고, 건강한 환자만 대상이라 항응고제 복용자 자료가 없으며, 초록만으로 정리되었다.

## Summary

This 4-arm parallel RCT from Pakistan evaluated three green tea extract formulations (tannin extract, methanolic extract, aqueous extract) against saline-soaked gauze in 64 healthy adults undergoing molar extraction (n=16 per arm). All three extract formulations achieved hemostasis dramatically faster than the control: tannin extract was fastest at 5.62 min, methanolic 7.50 min, aqueous 8.44 min — all p<0.001 vs the 61.56 min observed in the saline gauze group. The proposed mechanism is twofold: astringent vasoconstriction from polyphenols/tannins, and protein precipitation forming a fibrin-like scaffold. No adverse events were reported in the abstract. This study is abstract-only and provides the only natural-product hemostatic datapoint in this wiki; the unusually long control arm time (61.56 min) likely reflects a deliberately passive gauze protocol rather than standard clinical practice.

## Key Contributions

- First natural-product hemostatic RCT in this wiki: establishes green tea extracts as effective hemostatics in healthy patients
- Demonstrates that tannin extract outperforms methanolic and aqueous fractions, suggesting polyphenol concentration drives hemostatic speed
- Provides mechanism rationale: astringent tannin effect + protein precipitation → functional coagulation scaffold
- Caution: very long control hemostasis time (61.56 min) suggests protocol deviation or unusually passive gauze pressure; reduces external validity of absolute time comparisons

## Methodology

**Design**: parallel-group RCT; 4 arms; n=64 (16/arm); Pakistan. **Population**: healthy adults, molar extraction, no anticoagulants/antiplatelets. **Interventions**: G1 tannin extract, G2 methanolic green tea extract, G3 aqueous green tea extract, G4 saline-soaked gauze. **Primary outcome**: hemostasis time (minutes). **Note**: abstract-only; formulation concentrations, blinding, allocation concealment not available.

## Results

| Group | Mean hemostasis time (min) |
|---|---|
| Tannin Extract (TE) | 5.62 |
| Methanolic Extract (ME) | 7.50 |
| Aqueous Extract (AE) | 8.44 |
| Saline Gauze (control) | 61.56 |

- All three extract groups significantly faster than control (p<0.001)
- No adverse events reported in abstract
- **Caveat**: 61.56-min control arm likely reflects minimal/passive gauze protocol; healthy patient control hemostasis typically ≤5 min with proper pressure
