> - 항혈전제 역전 가이드: 와파린→비타민 K + PCC, apixaban/rivaroxaban→andexanet alfa, dabigatran→idarucizumab, UFH/LMWH→프로타민, 항혈소판제→DDAVP/혈소판/TXA.
> - 비타민 K IV/PO는 24 h INR 효과 유사하나 IV가 더 빠름; 둘 다 4 h 후 INR >4 → 즉시 교정 시 PCC 병용.
> - 프로타민: UFH 1 mg/100 units IV (max 50 mg, 2–4 h 이내); LMWH <8 h 시 1 mg/1 mg, >8 h 시 0.5 mg/1 mg; LMWH 효과의 ~50%만 역전.
> - Andexanet alfa: anti-Xa 활성 2–5분 내 94%(apixaban)/92%(rivaroxaban) 감소, 4 h 후 반등.
> - 항혈소판제 platelet aggregation 회복: aspirin/clopidogrel/ticagrelor ~4–5일, prasugrel ~7일.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of anticoagulant/antiplatelet reversal: vitamin K + PCC for warfarin, andexanet alfa for apixaban/rivaroxaban, idarucizumab for dabigatran, protamine sulfate for UFH/LMWH, with antiplatelet reversal via DDAVP/platelets/TXA.

## 세줄요약

항혈전제 역전에 대한 내러티브 리뷰: 와파린 역전에 비타민 K + PCC, DOAC 역전에 andexanet alfa/idarucizumab, UFH/LMWH 역전에 프로타민, 항혈소판제 역전에 DDAVP/혈소판/TXA를 정리한다.

## Summary

Practical reversal guide for clinicians. Warfarin reversal with vitamin K (IV more rapid than PO, similar at 24 h) plus PCC for immediate correction. DOAC reversal with andexanet alfa (anti-Xa) and idarucizumab (dabigatran). UFH/LMWH with protamine sulfate at weight/time-based dosing. Antiplatelet reversal with DDAVP, platelet transfusion, TXA/EACA.

## Key Contributions

- Reversal-agent mapping with doses/routes/timing.
- Protamine dosing by elapsed time for LMWH.
- Platelet recovery windows by agent.

## Methodology

Narrative review synthesizing RCTs and studies; no new data.

## Results

| Agent | Reversal | Dose/Route/Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Warfarin | Vitamin K (phytonadione) | IV more rapid than PO; similar INR at 24 h; ~50% INR<2 at 24 h IV monotherapy |
| Warfarin | PCC (4F) | INR-driven or fixed; 25–50 U/kg for DOAC; hemostasis 93% with vitamin K |
| UFH | Protamine sulfate | 1 mg/100 units IV, max 50 mg, within 2–4 h |
| LMWH | Protamine sulfate | 1 mg/1 mg if <8 h; 0.5 mg/1 mg if >8 h; reverses ~50% of effect |
| Apixaban/Rivaroxaban | Andexanet alfa | IV bolus; anti-Xa ↓94%/92% in 2–5 min; rebound at 4 h |
| Dabigatran | Idarucizumab | 5 g IV |
| Antiplatelets | DDAVP / platelets / TXA | Recovery 4–5 d (aspirin/clopidogrel/ticagrelor), 7 d (prasugrel) |
