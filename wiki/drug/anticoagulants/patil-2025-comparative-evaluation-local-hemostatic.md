## One-line Summary

5-arm RCT (n=60 healthy molar extractions) ranking Botroclot (0.87 min) > TXA (0.93 min) > chitosan (1.13 min) >> pressure gauze (1.80 min) for hemostasis time, all with p=0.001.

## 한줄요약

## Summary

This parallel-group RCT enrolled 60 healthy adults undergoing first or second molar extraction in Dhule, India (Feb–Apr 2025). Patients were randomized across 5 arms (n=12 each) to receive one of: Botroclot (hemocoagulase 0.2 CU + CHX 0.1%), Celox™ chitosan gauze, adrenaline 1:1,000-soaked gauze, tranexamic acid (TXA) gauze, or saline gauze applied without external pressure. The primary outcome was observation time (OT) — minutes to complete hemostasis, assessed by a blinded observer. One-way ANOVA (F=15.45, p=0.001) with Tukey post-hoc identified Botroclot, TXA, and chitosan as significantly faster than gauze; adrenaline did not achieve significance (p=0.027 vs gauze but not adjusted). No rebleeding events occurred in any group. This study establishes a head-to-head agent hierarchy under identical conditions in healthy patients — a reference for the anticoagulant-patient literature where most studies test single agents.

## Key Contributions

- First 5-arm RCT directly benchmarking Botroclot, TXA, chitosan, adrenaline, and pressure gauze in the same cohort and technique
- Establishes that Botroclot and TXA are statistically equivalent (p=0.992) and both equivalent to chitosan (p=0.331, 0.593) — clinically interchangeable in healthy patients
- Adrenaline's hemostatic speed correlated with baseline bleeding time (r=−0.57, p=0.018); chitosan and Botroclot did not — suggesting mechanism-independent action for the latter two
- Safe profile: zero rebleeding events across all 60 patients

## Methodology

**Design**: parallel-group RCT; n=60 (12/arm); Feb–Apr 2025; Dhule, India. **Population**: systemically healthy adults 20–50 years, molar extraction for periodontal/carious/traumatic reasons, normal BT/CT/INR/CBC. Agents applied passively without external pressure. **Randomization**: computer-generated (R v4.3.2); allocation via SNOSE; assessor- and patient-blinded. **Statistics**: one-way ANOVA, Tukey post-hoc, Pearson r; p<0.05.

| TXA | 0.93 ± 0.39 |
| Chitosan | 1.13 ± 0.27 |
| Adrenaline | 1.38 ± 0.25 |
| Gauze | 1.80 ± 0.41 |

## Results

- ANOVA F=15.45, p=0.001
- Post-hoc significant: Botroclot vs Gauze (p=0.001), TXA vs Gauze (p=0.001), Chitosan vs Gauze (p=0.001), TXA vs Adrenaline (p=0.014), Botroclot vs Adrenaline (p=0.004)
- Not significant: Botroclot vs TXA (p=0.992), Botroclot vs Chitosan (p=0.331), Chitosan vs TXA (p=0.593)
- Rebleeding at 30 min: 0/60 patients
