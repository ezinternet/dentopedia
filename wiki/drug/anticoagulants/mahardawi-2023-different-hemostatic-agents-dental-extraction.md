## Three-line Summary

Network meta-analysis of 23 RCTs (22 in quantitative synthesis) of hemostatic agents after dental extraction in patients on oral antithrombotic therapy, reference = conventional gauze/cotton pressure ± sutures.

Tranexamic acid (TXA) was the only agent with significantly fewer bleeding events (OR 0.27, 95% CI 0.10–0.69); cyanoacrylate ranked best but was borderline (OR 0.03, P=0.051); chitosan and collagen plug were fastest to hemostasis (SMD −9.78 and −10.13) yet ranked last for bleeding events.

Certainty was low to very low for time to hemostasis and moderate for TXA; most trials had risk-of-bias concerns and few antiplatelet-only trials were available.

## 세줄요약

구강 항혈전제 복용 환자의 발치 후 지혈제를 비교한 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA) — RCT 23편(정량 합성 22편), 기준군은 거즈·솜 압박 ± 봉합.

출혈 사건은 트라넥삼산 (Tranexamic Acid, TXA)만 유의하게 감소(OR 0.27, 95% CI 0.10–0.69); 시아노아크릴레이트 조직접착제는 순위 1위이나 경계 유의성(OR 0.03, P=0.051); 키토산·콜라겐 플러그는 지혈 시간이 가장 짧았으나(SMD −9.78, −10.13) 출혈 사건 순위는 최하위.

근거 확실성은 지혈 시간 낮음~매우 낮음, TXA는 중등도(GRADE); 다수 시험이 편향 위험 우려가 있고 항혈소판제 단독 시험이 적음.

## Summary

According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-39023-7)), this PRISMA-NMA (PROSPERO CRD42023408207) pooled 23 RCTs comparing 11 local hemostatic agents (chitosan dressing, oxidized regenerated cellulose, TXA, fibrin, collagen plug, bismuth subgallate, gelatin sponge, feracrylum, epsilon aminocaproic acid, Ankaferd blood stopper, cyanoacrylate) against conventional measures after extraction in patients on antithrombotic therapy (14 anticoagulant, 6 antiplatelet, 3 unspecified studies). TXA was the only agent significantly reducing bleeding events (OR 0.27; 0.24 in anticoagulated patients; 0.12 in simple extractions). Chitosan and collagen plug gave the fastest hemostasis but the highest later bleeding odds, even worse than conventional measures. The authors conclude cyanoacrylate and TXA are favorable and that chitosan or collagen should be second to TXA.

## Key Contributions

- Adds a bleeding-event outcome to a literature dominated by time-to-hemostasis endpoints.
- Dissociates speed of hemostasis from later rebleeding for chitosan and collagen plug (proposed mechanism: low mechanical strength, dislodgement, unpredictable degradation).
- Flags gelatin sponge as worse than conventional measures in the anticoagulated analysis (possible socket over-expansion impairing clot formation).

## Methodology

- Searched PubMed/Medline, CENTRAL, Scopus and grey literature to March 20, 2023; two independent screeners; RoB 2 for bias.
- Excluded trials where controls stopped or modified antithrombotic therapy.
- Frequentist random-effects NMA (R netmeta); SMD (time to hemostasis), OR (bleeding events); P-score ranking; GRADE via CINeMA.
- Subgroups: anticoagulated patients; simple extraction only.

## Results

| Outcome | Key finding |
|---|---|
| Time to hemostasis (9 studies, I² 98.3%) | Chitosan SMD −9.78 (−12.78 to −6.78); collagen plug SMD −10.13 (−15.53 to −4.73); Ankaferd longer than chitosan (SMD 7.39) |
| Bleeding events, all (16 studies, I² 12.5%) | TXA OR 0.27 (0.10–0.69); cyanoacrylate OR 0.03 (0.0008–1.02), P=0.051 |
| Bleeding events, anticoagulated (11 studies) | TXA OR 0.24 (0.08–0.77); chitosan and collagen higher odds than TXA; gelatin sponge worse than conventional |
| Bleeding events, simple extraction (12 studies) | TXA OR 0.12 (0.02–0.71) |

- Risk of bias: 8 low, 14 some concerns, 1 high.
- Certainty: TXA moderate (downgraded for heterogeneity); time-to-hemostasis low to very low; TXA vs collagen plug high overall, moderate in anticoagulated, low in simple-extraction-only.
- Limitations: no antiplatelet-only analysis, teeth not specified, cyanoacrylate estimate imprecise and indirect, most trials potentially biased.
