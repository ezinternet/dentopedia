## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis of 3 RCTs and 16 cohort studies (2235 patients) found fixed-dose 4-PCC for emergent VKA reversal reduced dose, administration time, mortality, and thromboembolic events while improving clinical hemostasis, but was less likely to achieve INR <1.5. A fixed dose of at least 2000 IU may be optimal, with additional variable dosing for ICH, high INR, or heavy patients.

## 세줄요약

비타민 K 길항제 (Vitamin K Antagonist, VKA) 응급 역전을 위한 4-factor 프로트롬빈 복합체 농축물 (4-Factor Prothrombin Complex Concentrate, 4-PCC) 고정 용량 vs 가변 용량을 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석(3 RCT + 16 코호트, 2235명)이다. 고정 용량은 용량·투여 시간·사망률·혈전색전증 감소와 임상 지혈 개선을 보였으나 INR <1.5 달성률은 낮았다. 최소 2000 IU 고정 용량이 최적일 수 있으며, ICH·고INR·고체중 환자에는 추가 가변 용량이 필요할 수 있다.

## Summary

Comprehensive meta-analysis comparing fixed- vs variable-dose 4-PCC for emergent VKA reversal. Fixed-dose regimens offer advantages in dose reduction, speed of administration, clinical hemostasis, mortality, and TEEs, at the cost of lower INR<1.5 achievement. Evidence is mixed in quality (mostly retrospective cohorts) but mortality/TEE reductions are high-certainty.

## Key Contributions

- 3 RCTs + 16 cohort studies, 2235 patients total.
- Fixed-dose: lower 4-PCC dose (1537.3 vs 2222.2 IU), shorter order-to-needle (39 vs 72 min), PCC-to-INR (147.4 vs 192.5 min).
- Improved clinical hemostasis (74.1% vs 61.6%; OR 1.7).
- High-certainty reduction in mortality (cohorts 15.6% vs 18.8%; RCTs 4.7% vs 7.2%) and TEEs (cohorts 2.4% vs 4.4%).
- Less likely to achieve INR <1.5 (cohorts 64.6% vs 74.3%; RCTs 66.7% vs 88.6%); no clear difference for INR <2.
- Exploratory: ≥2000 IU fixed dose may be optimal.

## Methodology

Systematic review (Cochrane/PRISMA), searched PubMed/Embase/Ovid MEDLINE Jan 2000–Aug 2023. Quality via NOS (cohorts) and ROBINS-I (RCTs); GRADE certainty. Random-effects meta-analysis in R `meta`. Subgroups by study type, indication (ICH), baseline INR, weight, 4-PCC dose.
