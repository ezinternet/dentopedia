## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 13 randomized trials in adults on an anticoagulant or antiplatelet who had minor oral surgery, comparing stopping the drug (control) with continuing it (test); searched PubMed-Medline, Scopus, Embase.

Continuing therapy was associated with more intraoperative bleeding (reported as 157% higher; 95% CI 1.40-4.71; warfarin subgroup RR 1.79, 95% CI 1.00-3.21), but no significant difference in postoperative bleeding (RR 0.81, 95% CI 0.54-1.22, p=0.42).

Minor oral surgery can be done without altering a single-drug regimen, with local hemostatic measures strongly advised; abstract-only, so risk of bias and heterogeneity are unverified.

항응고제 또는 항혈소판제를 복용하는 성인의 소수술에서 약 중단군(대조)과 지속군(시험)을 비교한 무작위 임상시험 13편의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석(Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA).

## Summary

This systematic review with meta-analysis (Clin Oral Investig 2021) asked whether the pharmacological regimen must be altered in adults on a single anticoagulant or antiplatelet drug who need minor oral surgery. Only randomized trials (parallel or crossover) were included, comparing patients who stopped medication before the procedure (control) with those who did not (test). Thirteen studies were pooled.

Patients who continued therapy bled more during surgery, yet postoperative bleeding did not differ. The authors conclude minor oral surgery is safe without regimen modification and that local hemostatic measures are strongly advised.

## Key Contributions

- RCT-only pooled comparison of stopping vs continuing single-drug anticoagulant or antiplatelet therapy for minor oral surgery.
- Distinguishes intraoperative (higher with continuation) from postoperative bleeding (no difference).
- Warfarin subgroup shows the intraoperative excess (RR 1.79, 95% CI 1.00-3.21).

## Methodology

Databases: PubMed-Medline, Scopus, Embase. Design: RCTs, parallel or crossover. Groups: stopped medication (control) vs continued (test). Pooled risk ratios; subgroups for anticoagulants and antiplatelets. Risk-of-bias assessment and heterogeneity are not reported in the abstract.

## Results

- 13 studies included.
- Intraoperative bleeding: 157% higher with continued therapy (95% CI 1.40-4.71).
- Warfarin subgroup, intraoperative bleeding: RR 1.79 (95% CI 1.00-3.21).
- Postoperative bleeding: RR 0.81 (95% CI 0.54-1.22), p=0.42.
- Antiplatelet subgroup numbers were not given in the abstract.
