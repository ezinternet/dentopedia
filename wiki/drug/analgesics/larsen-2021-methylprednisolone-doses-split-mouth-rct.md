## One-line Summary
Double-blind split-mouth RCT (n=52) comparing methylprednisolone 20/30/40 mg IM vs placebo found no significant benefit for pain, trismus, or OHIP-14 QoL after mandibular third molar surgery; age and smoking were stronger predictors than drug dose.

## 한줄요약
이중맹검 split-mouth RCT (n=52)에서 메틸프레드니솔론(Methylprednisolone) 20/30/40 mg 근주는 사랑니 발치 후 통증·개구제한(Trismus)·삶의 질(OHIP-14) 모두에서 위약 대비 유의한 차이 없음; 나이·흡연이 더 강한 예후 인자.

## Summary
Larsen et al. conducted a double-blind, split-mouth RCT at Aalborg University Hospital (March 2018–January 2019) in 52 patients with bilateral impacted mandibular third molars. Patients were randomized to intraoperative masseter IM injection of 20, 30, or 40 mg methylprednisolone or placebo saline. Outcomes (VAS pain, interincisal distance for trismus, OHIP-14 QoL) were assessed at days 1, 3, 7, and 30. No significant differences were found between any methylprednisolone dose and placebo. Increasing age predicted worse pain and QoL (p=0.00), and smoking combined with longer surgery time predicted reduced mouth opening (p=0.00). The study concludes there is no evidence to support methylprednisolone for routine post-M3 morbidity reduction, and dose escalation does not provide proportional benefit.

## Key Contributions
- Split-mouth design eliminates between-patient confounders — highest achievable internal validity for dose comparison
- Null result across all three doses (20/30/40 mg) argues against dose-response assumption
- Identifies non-pharmacological predictors (age, smoking, surgery duration) as clinically more impactful than steroid choice
- OHIP-14 as QoL endpoint adds patient-reported dimension beyond pain/trismus measures

## Methodology
Double-blind split-mouth RCT. n=52 (16M/36F), mean age 25.9y (18–39), bilateral impacted M3. Four groups: placebo / 20mg / 30mg / 40mg methylprednisolone, IM masseter injection intraoperatively. Follow-up: days 1, 3, 7, 30. Outcomes: VAS pain, interincisal distance, OHIP-14. Statistics: Generalized Estimating Equations (GEE), 95% CI. GCP/Helsinki II/CONSORT compliant. Power calculated for 20mm VAS difference.

## Results
- Pain (VAS): NS for all methylprednisolone doses vs placebo
- Trismus (interincisal distance): NS for all doses vs placebo
- QoL (OHIP-14): NS for all doses vs placebo
- Age ↑ → pain ↑ and QoL worsening (p=0.00)
- Smoking + longer surgery → trismus ↑ in early healing (p=0.00)
- No dose-response gradient across 20/30/40 mg
- No adverse effects
