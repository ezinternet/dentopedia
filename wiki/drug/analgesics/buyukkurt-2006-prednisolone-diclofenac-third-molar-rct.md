## Three-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (n=45) from Turkey compared immediate postoperative IM injection of prednisolone 25 mg alone, prednisolone 25 mg combined with diclofenac, or saline control in patients undergoing lower third molar removal.
The prednisolone+diclofenac combination was significantly superior to both prednisolone alone and control for pain at 6h, trismus at days 2 and 7, and swelling at day 7 (all p<0.05); prednisolone alone reduced swelling vs control at day 2 only.
Results support combining a corticosteroid with an NSAID for comprehensive postoperative symptom control, though the small sample (n=45, 15 per group) and abstract-only access limit the study's generalizability.

## One-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (n=45) found prednisolone 25 mg IM + diclofenac IM combination superior to prednisolone alone and control for pain at 6h, trismus at days 2 and 7, and swelling at day 7; prednisolone alone outperformed control for swelling at day 2 only.

## Summary
Buyukkurt et al. randomized 45 patients undergoing lower third molar removal to immediate postoperative IM injection of: prednisolone 25 mg alone, prednisolone 25 mg + diclofenac, or saline control. Pain was assessed by VAS at hours 6 and 7 on the day of surgery; trismus and swelling at days 2 and 7. The prednisolone+diclofenac combination was significantly superior to both prednisolone alone and control for pain at 6h, trismus at days 2 and 7, and swelling at day 7. Prednisolone alone significantly reduced swelling vs control at day 2 but not other endpoints. The combination's superiority is interpreted as synergism: corticosteroid reduces vascular permeability/edema while NSAID blocks prostaglandin-mediated pain and inflammatory cascade.

## Key Contributions
- One of the few RCTs examining steroid+NSAID combination vs steroid alone vs control in M3 surgery
- Demonstrates additive benefit of combining two anti-inflammatory mechanisms
- Early pain timepoints (hours 6 and 7) capture immediate postoperative window — clinically actionable
- Authors conclude combination appropriate when "extensive postoperative soft tissue swelling is anticipated"

## Methodology
Three-arm RCT. n=45 (15 per group). Intervention: immediate postoperative IM injection. Groups: I=prednisolone 25mg IM; II=prednisolone 25mg + diclofenac IM; III=saline control. Outcomes: VAS pain (hours 6, 7), trismus (days 2, 7), swelling (days 2, 7). Statistics: ANOVA. Setting: Turkey.

## Results
- Pain at 6h: Prednisolone+diclofenac > control (p<0.05); prednisolone alone NS vs control
- Pain at 7h: Both prednisolone groups > control (p<0.05)
- Trismus at days 2 and 7: Prednisolone+diclofenac > prednisolone alone and control (p<0.05)
- Swelling at day 2: Both prednisolone groups < control (p<0.05)
- Swelling at day 7: Prednisolone+diclofenac < prednisolone alone and control (p<0.05)
- No adverse effects
