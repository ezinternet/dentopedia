## Three-line Summary

Systematic review using the Navigation Guide environmental-health methodology (46 studies through Feb 2025) examining prenatal acetaminophen exposure and offspring neurodevelopmental disorders (ASD, ADHD); qualitative synthesis only (no meta-analysis) due to heterogeneity.

Of 46 studies, 27 reported positive associations with neurodevelopmental disorders, 9 null, and 4 negative (protective); higher-quality studies were more often positive. The abstract concludes the evidence is consistent with an association and advises pregnant women to limit acetaminophen, whereas the discussion's final recommendation is judicious use — lowest effective dose, shortest duration, under medical guidance — rather than a broad limitation, because untreated maternal fever and pain carry their own risks (neural tube defects, preterm birth).

This is observational, association-level evidence (qualitative synthesis, no pooled estimate): confounding, including by indication (fever, infection), was graded in the Navigation Guide risk-of-bias assessment but cannot be excluded, and in the largest cohort (Ahlqvist 2024, Sweden, N=2,480,797) modest associations attenuated to the null in the sibling-controlled analysis (ADHD HR 1.07 → 0.98), which the authors attribute partly to non-specific exposure ascertainment; for dental prescribing, use acetaminophen at the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration.

## 세줄요약

Navigation Guide 방법론 기반 체계적 리뷰(46편, 2025년 2월까지): 산전 아세트아미노펜(Acetaminophen) 노출과 자손의 신경발달장애(Neurodevelopmental Disorder, NDD; 자폐스펙트럼장애(ASD)·주의력결핍과잉행동장애(ADHD)) 연관성, 연구 이질성으로 메타분석 불가, 정성 종합.

46편 중 27편 양성 연관/9편 결과 없음/4편 음성(보호), 고품질 연구일수록 양성 경향; 초록 결론은 "임신부에게 사용 제한 권고"이나 본문 최종 권고는 **광범위한 제한이 아니라 최소 유효용량·최단 기간의 신중한 사용** — 치료하지 않은 산모 발열·통증도 신경관 결손·조산 위험이 있기 때문.

관찰 연구라 연관≠인과; 교란(적응증에 의한 교란 포함)은 비뚤림 평가에 반영됐으나 배제 불가, 최대 코호트(Ahlqvist 2024 스웨덴 N=2,480,797)는 형제 대조 분석에서 연관이 null로 약화(ADHD HR 1.07 → 0.98, 저자는 노출 측정의 비특이성을 지적) — 치과 처방에서는 최소 용량·최단 기간 원칙 적용.

## Summary

Acetaminophen is named the first-line analgesic for odontogenic pain in pregnancy in [[drug/antibiotics/aliabadi-2022-antibiotic-use-endodontic-treatment-pregnancy]]. This systematic review applied the **Navigation Guide** environmental-health methodology to 46 studies of prenatal acetaminophen exposure and offspring neurodevelopmental disorders (NDDs). **27 reported positive associations, 9 null, 4 negative (protective), and higher-quality studies were more likely positive** (by outcome: ADHD 20 studies — 14 positive, 3 null, 1 inverse, 2 mixed; ASD 8 — 5 positive, 2 null, 1 mixed; other NDDs 18 — 6 positive, 2 null, 4 inverse, 6 mixed). The paper carries two conclusions that should be read together: the abstract advises pregnant women to limit acetaminophen, while the discussion recommends **judicious use — lowest effective dose, shortest duration, under medical guidance — rather than a broad limitation**, noting that untreated maternal fever and pain pose risks such as neural tube defects and preterm birth. **Clinical caveat: this is observational, association-level evidence from a qualitative synthesis.** Confounding (including confounding by indication) was rated within the risk-of-bias assessment but cannot be excluded, and in the largest cohort (Ahlqvist 2024, Swedish national cohort) modest full-cohort associations attenuated to the null in sibling-controlled analyses — a result the authors question on exposure-ascertainment grounds. For dental prescribing: acetaminophen remains the analgesic used for the pregnant patient when one is needed, at the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration.

## Key Contributions

1. Comprehensive Navigation-Guide grading of the prenatal-acetaminophen–NDD literature (46 studies).
2. Quality-stratified direction-of-association tally showing a predominance of positive studies.
3. A precautionary "limit use" recommendation that reframes acetaminophen's blanket safe-in-pregnancy status.

## Methodology

- Systematic PubMed search through Feb 25, 2025; predefined inclusion criteria.
- Risk-of-bias and strength-of-evidence rating per Navigation Guide.
- Qualitative synthesis (no meta-analysis) due to heterogeneity → `sr` (no MA).

## Results

| Direction of association | Studies (of 46) |
|---|---|
| Positive (link to NDD) | 27 |
| Null (no link) | 9 |
| Negative (protective) | 4 |

Higher-quality studies → more often positive. Abstract conclusion: evidence consistent with an association; advise limiting acetaminophen in pregnancy. Discussion recommendation: judicious use (lowest effective dose, shortest duration, under medical guidance) rather than a broad limitation, given the risks of untreated maternal fever and pain. Sibling-controlled analyses: Ahlqvist 2024 (Sweden) ADHD HR 1.07 in the full cohort vs 0.98 (95% CI 0.94–1.02) within siblings; Gustavson 2021 (Norway) long-term exposure aHR 1.06 (95% CI 0.51–2.05) within families.
