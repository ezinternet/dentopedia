## Three-line Summary
PROSPERO-registered, PRISMA-compliant SR+MA (CRD42020161341) of 7 RCTs compared preemptive dexamethasone vs methylprednisolone specifically in mandibular third molar surgery, searching six databases including PubMed, CINAHL, Scopus, and Cochrane Central.
Dexamethasone and methylprednisolone were equivalent for pain, rescue analgesia, and swelling, but dexamethasone was significantly superior for trismus reduction at postoperative day 2 (SMD −0.69 mm, 95% CI −1.01 to −0.38, p<0.0001).
The trismus advantage of dexamethasone was the only differentiating outcome, with overall evidence rated low to moderate due to heterogeneity and unclear risk of bias in 4 of 7 studies, warranting larger standardized RCTs.

## Summary
Singh et al. performed a PROSPERO-registered, PRISMA-compliant SR+MA comparing preemptive dexamethasone vs methylprednisolone specifically in mandibular third molar surgery. Six databases were searched; 7 RCTs met inclusion criteria. Pooled analyses showed no significant difference in pain (VAS), rescue analgesics consumed, or swelling. However, forest plot analysis revealed dexamethasone achieved significantly greater trismus reduction at postoperative day 2, with an estimated SMD of −0.69 mm (95% CI: −1.01 to −0.38, p<0.0001). No adverse effects were reported in any included study. Evidence quality was rated low to moderate due to heterogeneity, and the authors call for larger RCTs with standardized protocols.

## Key Contributions
- Only SR+MA restricting comparison to dexamethasone vs methylprednisolone (excludes all other corticosteroids)
- Quantifies trismus advantage of dexamethasone with pooled SMD for the first time
- No adverse effects in single-dose perioperative use across 7 RCTs — safety signal
- Provides prior foundation that Libório 2025 SR+MA subsequently confirmed

## Methodology
SR+MA (PRISMA). PROSPERO CRD42020161341. Databases: PubMed, CINAHL, Scopus, DOSS, Cochrane Central, Web of Science. Inclusion: RCTs, preemptive dexamethasone vs methylprednisolone, mandibular M3, local anesthesia, healthy/asymptomatic patients. Exclusion: other corticosteroids, other procedures. n=7 RCTs. Risk of bias: low (3), unclear (4). Outcomes: VAS pain, rescue analgesics, swelling, trismus, adverse events.

## Results
- Pain (VAS): NS — dexamethasone = methylprednisolone
- Rescue analgesics: NS
- Swelling: NS
- Trismus at day 2: Dexamethasone significantly > methylprednisolone
  - SMD = −0.69 mm (95% CI: −1.01 to −0.38), p<0.0001
- Adverse effects: None in any included study
- Overall evidence: Low to moderate quality
