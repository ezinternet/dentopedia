## Three-line Summary
Single-blind placebo-controlled RCT (n=60) from Iran compared 40 mg methylprednisolone IM injected into the masseter muscle vs gluteal muscle vs no intervention, administered 30 minutes before impacted lower third molar surgery.
No significant differences were found across the three groups for pain (p=0.285), ultrasound-measured edema (p=0.250), or trismus (p=0.337) at any assessment point.
The null result for both injection routes challenges the local-depot hypothesis and, taken together with Larsen 2021, questions the routine use of methylprednisolone for third molar postoperative morbidity regardless of injection site.

## One-line Summary
Single-blind placebo-controlled RCT (n=60) found no significant difference in pain (p=0.285), edema by ultrasound (p=0.250), or trismus (p=0.337) between 40 mg methylprednisolone injected into masseter muscle, gluteal muscle, or no intervention before third molar surgery.

## Summary
Gholami et al. conducted a single-blind RCT in 60 patients (31F/29M, ages 19–35) with impacted lower third molars, randomized to 40 mg methylprednisolone IM in masseter, 40 mg methylprednisolone IM in gluteal muscle, or no medication (30 min preoperatively). Pain was assessed by VAS at days 1, 5, 7; edema by ultrasound imaging at days 5 and 7; trismus by interincisal distance at days 5 and 7. Friedman (within-group) and Kruskal-Wallis (between-group) tests found no significant differences on any outcome across the three groups. The study specifically challenges the local-depot hypothesis — that masseter injection provides superior tissue-level drug concentration near the surgical site.

## Key Contributions
- Only RCT directly comparing masseter vs gluteal IM route for methylprednisolone in M3 surgery
- Ultrasound-based edema measurement — objective, superior to facial tape measurement
- Null result for both routes vs control — does not support routine methylprednisolone for M3 regardless of injection site
- Consistent with Larsen 2021 null result; collectively challenges positive SR+MA conclusions

## Methodology
Single-blind, placebo-controlled RCT. n=60 (31F/29M), age 19–35. Three groups (n=20 each): masseter IM / gluteal IM / no intervention, 30 min preoperatively. Dose: 40 mg methylprednisolone. Outcomes: VAS pain (days 1, 5, 7); ultrasound edema (days 5, 7); interincisal distance trismus (days 5, 7). Statistics: Friedman test (within-group); Kruskal-Wallis (between-group); α=0.05.

## Results
- Pain: p=0.285 (CI=−2.91±0.40) — NS across all 3 groups
- Edema: p=0.250 (CI=0.22±0.42) — NS
- Trismus: p=0.337 (CI=−5.93±2.22) — NS
- Neither masseter nor gluteal route superior to no intervention
- No adverse effects reported
