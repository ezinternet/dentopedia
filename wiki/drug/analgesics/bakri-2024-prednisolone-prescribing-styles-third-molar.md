## Three-line Summary

Randomized split-mouth single-blind clinical trial (n=15, 18–30y, bilateral impacted mandibular third molars, Jazan University) comparing two oral prednisolone prescribing styles after surgical extraction: single dose (25 mg once) vs tapered dose (5 mg three times/day day 1, twice/day day 2, once/day day 3).

Tapered dosing produced significantly greater postoperative mouth opening and significantly less facial swelling (corner-of-mouth-to-tragus and canthus-to-mandibular-angle distances) at days 1/3/7 than the single dose; pain VAS showed no significant difference between regimens at any timepoint.

Supports a 3-day tapered oral prednisolone regimen over a single postoperative dose for reducing swelling/trismus after third-molar extraction — limited by very small sample (n=15), single-center design, and no placebo arm (both groups received a corticosteroid).

## 세줄요약

무작위 분악(split-mouth) 단일맹검 임상시험(n=15, 18–30세, 양측 매복 하악 사랑니, Jazan University): 술후 경구 프레드니솔론(Prednisolone) 처방 스타일 2종 비교 — 단일용량(25mg 1회) vs 체감용량(tapered dose, 5mg을 1일차 3회·2일차 2회·3일차 1회).

체감용량군이 단일용량군 대비 술후 개구량(day 1/3/7)이 유의하게 크고, 안면부종(구각-이주 거리 및 외안각-하악각 거리)이 유의하게 적음; 통증 VAS는 두 군 간 전 시점에서 유의차 없음.

사랑니 발치 후 부종·개구제한 감소에는 단일고용량보다 3일 체감용량 경구 프레드니솔론(Prednisolone) 요법을 지지 — 매우 작은 표본(n=15)·단일기관·위약군 부재(양쪽 다 스테로이드 투여)가 한계.

## Summary

This randomized, split-mouth, single-blind trial (Bakri et al., JKAOMS 2024) compared two oral prescribing styles of prednisolone in 15 patients undergoing bilateral surgical extraction of impacted mandibular third molars: a single 25 mg postoperative dose versus a 3-day tapered regimen (5 mg TID → BID → QD). The tapered regimen produced significantly better outcomes for mouth opening and facial swelling at postoperative days 1, 3, and 7, while pain VAS scores did not differ significantly between the two regimens at any measured timepoint. The authors position oral corticosteroid dosing as a practical, needle-free alternative for clinicians managing third-molar postoperative sequelae, and argue tapering matters because steroid-related swelling in treated patients typically peaks around day 3, requiring sustained rather than single-bolus coverage.

## Key Contributions

- First trial (per the authors) to isolate the *prescribing-style* variable (single dose vs tapered) for oral prednisolone in third-molar surgery, rather than steroid-vs-placebo or steroid-vs-different-steroid.
- Shows a dosing-schedule effect on swelling/trismus independent of drug choice — a 3-day taper outperforms a single bolus even within the same drug.
- Frames oral dosing as a practical alternative to IM/IV corticosteroid administration for clinicians (implantologists, periodontists, GPs) without injection access.

## Methodology

Randomized, split-mouth (within-patient), single-blind trial. n=15 (8F/7M), age 18–30, bilateral Pell & Gregory Class II Position B impacted mandibular third molars, surgeries ≥21 days apart per side, single surgeon. Group A: prednisolone 25 mg PO single postoperative dose. Group B: prednisolone 5 mg PO tapered over 3 days. Both groups also received standard ibuprofen 400 mg TID ×3 days. Outcomes (mouth opening, facial swelling via COM-TRG and canthus-mandibular angle distances, pain VAS) measured preop, immediately postop, and postoperative days 1/3/7. Unpaired t-test / chi-square, α=0.05.

## Results

| Outcome | Group A (single 25mg) | Group B (tapered 5mg×3d) | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mouth opening, day 1/3/7 | Lower | Significantly higher | p<0.05, all 3 timepoints |
| Facial swelling (COM-TRG) | Less decrease over time | Significantly greater decrease | p<0.05 |
| Canthus–mandibular angle | Less decrease over time | Significantly greater decrease | p<0.05 |
| Pain VAS, all timepoints | — | No significant difference | NS |

Background pharmacology cited in the paper's Discussion (literature context, not this trial's own data): prednisolone ≈5–6× more potent than hydrocortisone; methylprednisolone ≈5× more potent with an intermediate half-life of 18–30h; effective corticosteroid dose range ≈80–625 mg hydrocortisone-equivalent; post-extraction steroid-treated swelling typically peaks on day 3, motivating a ≥3-day course over single-dose.
