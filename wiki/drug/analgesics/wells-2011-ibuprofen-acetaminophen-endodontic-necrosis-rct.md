## Three-line Summary

Prospective double-blind RCT (J Endod 2011, n=71, OSU) in emergency endodontic patients with pulp necrosis, apical radiolucency, and moderate-to-severe preoperative pain — a high-baseline-pain subgroup.

Ibuprofen 600 mg + acetaminophen 1,000 mg q6h produced significantly less postoperative pain and significantly less rescue-medication use than ibuprofen 600 mg alone after emergency debridement.

In the most severe endodontic pain presentations, ibuprofen + APAP combination is the protocol of choice; single-NSAID monotherapy is inferior in this high-baseline-pain context.

## 세줄요약

전향적 이중맹검 RCT (J Endod 2011, n=71, OSU): 치수괴사+근첨방사선투과+중-중증 술전 통증을 동반한 응급 근관치료 환자(고기저통증 하위군).

응급 근관 처치 후 이부프로펜 600 mg + APAP 1,000 mg q6h가 이부프로펜 600 mg 단독 대비 통증 및 구제 진통제 사용 유의하게 감소.

가장 심한 근관 통증 환자에서 이부프로펜+APAP 병용이 1차 선택; 단독 NSAID는 이 고기저통증 상황에서 열등함 확인.

## Summary
Prospective randomized double-blind RCT in 71 emergency endodontic patients with high pre-op pain (necrosis + apical radiolucency + moderate-to-severe pain). After emergency debridement, randomized to ibuprofen 600mg q6h alone vs ibuprofen 600mg + APAP 1000mg q6h. Combination superior in pain reduction and reduced rescue analgesic use.

## Key Contributions
- Real-world emergency endodontic patient cohort (high baseline pain).
- Confirms ibuprofen + APAP > ibuprofen alone in this indication.
- Adds clinical-context endorsement to the NNT-based Moore 2015 framework.

## Methodology
Prospective double-blind RCT; n=71; ibuprofen 600mg q6h vs ibuprofen 600mg + APAP 1000mg q6h post emergency debridement.

## Results
[확인] Combination → significantly less pain + less rescue medication use.

## Limitations
- Small (n=71), single-center
- No combo with codeine/opioid arm
- Single year of testing

## Clinical Implication
For Korean endodontic emergency practice: combination ibuprofen + APAP is the protocol of first choice for symptomatic necrosis cases. Single-NSAID monotherapy is inferior.
