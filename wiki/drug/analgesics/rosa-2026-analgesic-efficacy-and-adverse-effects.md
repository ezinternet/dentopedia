## Three-line Summary

Meta-analytic systematic review of 6 RCTs (582 patients) of paracetamol + codeine versus placebo or other analgesics for postoperative pain after third molar surgery.

The combination significantly reduced pain and delayed rescue medication (OR=0.45, 95% CI 0.30-0.68), but adverse events were common: drowsiness 25% (NNH=7), nausea 18% (NNH=11), dizziness 12% (NNH=15).

It is effective especially when NSAIDs are contraindicated, but clinicians should anticipate sedation and gastrointestinal symptoms and counsel patients accordingly; only the abstract was available.

## 세줄요약

제3대구치 발치 후 통증에서 파라세타몰+코데인 병용을 위약 또는 다른 진통제와 비교한 무작위대조시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 6편(582명)의 메타분석 체계적 문헌고찰.

## Summary
This meta-analytic systematic review pooled 6 RCTs (582 patients) of paracetamol (acetaminophen) combined with codeine for postoperative pain after third molar surgery, searching PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, and the Cochrane Library with a random-effects model. The combination significantly reduced pain and delayed rescue medication use (OR=0.45, 95% CI 0.30-0.68). Adverse events were common: drowsiness 25% (NNH=7), nausea 18% (NNH=11), dizziness 12% (NNH=15). The authors conclude it is effective, especially for patients contraindicated for NSAIDs, with attention to sedation and gastrointestinal symptoms. Only the abstract was retrieved.

## Key Contributions
- Quantifies nausea, drowsiness, and dizziness for paracetamol + codeine in the third molar model with NNH values
- Shows delayed rescue medication need versus placebo or other analgesics
- Frames the combination as an option when NSAIDs are contraindicated

## Methodology
Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs comparing paracetamol + codeine with placebo or other analgesics; databases PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Library; random-effects pooling; outcomes pain reduction, rescue medication use, adverse effects (OR, NNT/NNH). n = 6 RCTs, 582 patients.
