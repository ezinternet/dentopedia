## Three-line Summary

Single-sheet patient-education handout (1 page) from the FDA-supported RESPITE project (ADA / University of Pittsburgh / University of Pennsylvania), presenting the ADA 2024 acute dental pain medication ladder for post-extraction pain to patients across three age bands — adolescents 12–<17 y, adults 17–<65 y, older adults 65+.

Content mirrors the clinician guideline exactly: first line is over-the-counter non-opioid — ibuprofen 400 mg (max 2,400 mg/d) or naproxen sodium 440 mg (max 1,100 mg/d), each optionally combined with acetaminophen 500 mg (max 4,000 mg/d); acetaminophen 1,000 mg (max 4,000 mg/d) alone when NSAIDs are contraindicated; opioid-containing steps are prescription-only, split-tablet (acetaminophen 325 mg + oxycodone 5 mg or hydrocodone 5–7.5 mg, plus a second acetaminophen 325 mg tablet), and carry an explicit "lowest effective dose, fewest tablets, shortest duration" constraint. For surgical extractions the sheet additionally offers a one-time long-acting local anesthetic for extended temporary pain management.

What the sheet adds over the clinician version is entirely presentational and is the reason to hold it: a 0–10 pain scale for patient self-report, a **days-of-recovery timeline** (day of surgery → day after → next day or day 3) that sets expectations for when pain should be receding, a per-option "hours of pain relief" bar so patients can compare duration rather than only strength, and pictographic side-effect warnings (dizziness, drowsiness, nausea/vomiting, constipation, numbness, needle) with a severe-liver-damage flag on acetaminophen and an adolescent misuse-risk flag on opioids.

## 세줄요약

미국 식품의약국 (Food and Drug Administration, FDA) 지원 RESPITE 프로젝트(미국치과의사협회 (American Dental Association, ADA)·피츠버그대·펜실베이니아대)의 **환자용 한 장짜리 유인물**(1 page): 발치 후 통증 상황의 ADA 2024 급성 치통 약물 사다리를 청소년(12–17세 미만)·성인(17–65세 미만)·고령자(65세 이상) 세 연령대에 걸쳐 환자에게 제시한다.

내용은 술자용 지침과 동일하다 — 1차는 일반의약품 비오피오이드: 이부프로펜 (Ibuprofen) 400mg(1일 최대 2,400mg) 또는 나프록센 나트륨 (Naproxen Sodium) 440mg(1일 최대 1,100mg), 각각 아세트아미노펜 (Acetaminophen) 500mg(1일 최대 4,000mg) 병용 선택 가능. 비스테로이드성 소염진통제 (Nonsteroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug, NSAID) 금기 시 아세트아미노펜 1,000mg 단독. 오피오이드 (Opioid) 포함 단계는 처방의약품이며 분할정 방식(아세트아미노펜 325mg + 옥시코돈 5mg 또는 하이드로코돈 5–7.5mg, 별도 아세트아미노펜 325mg 1정)에 "최소 유효용량·최소 정수·최단 기간" 제약이 명시된다. 외과적 발치에서는 진통 연장 목적의 장시간작용 국소마취제 1회 투여가 추가 선택지로 제시된다.

**술자용 대비 이 유인물의 고유 기여는 전적으로 표현 방식이며 그것이 보유 이유다** — 환자 자가보고용 0–10 통증척도, **회복일 타임라인**(수술 당일 → 다음날 → 그 다음날 또는 3일차)으로 통증이 언제쯤 줄어드는지 기대치를 설정하고, 선택지별 "진통 지속시간" 막대(세기가 아니라 지속시간으로 비교하게 함), 그림 부작용 경고(어지러움·졸음·오심구토·변비·감각마비·주사), 아세트아미노펜의 중증 간손상 경고와 청소년 오남용 위험 경고.

## Summary

This is the patient-facing half of the ADA 2024 acute dental pain package. The clinical substance — which drug, what dose, what ceiling — is identical to the clinician chairside guide; nothing here is new evidence, and no number on this sheet should be cited to it rather than to the parent guideline.

Its value is the translation layer, and that layer is worth holding separately because it is the hard part of the consultation. The sheet gives the patient a 0–10 scale to report pain on, lays out a **days-of-recovery timeline** (day of surgery → day after → next day or day 3) so the patient knows when pain should be receding, and compares options by **how many hours of relief** each buys rather than by pharmacological class. Side effects appear as pictograms, so the risk column stays readable without vocabulary. Two hazards are called out visually rather than in prose: severe liver damage on acetaminophen, and misuse or substance use disorder in adolescents on the opioid steps, where caregiver consent is advised.

Reading it in a Korean practice requires localization, not translation. The over-the-counter availability, unit strengths, and above all the opioid options reflect the US market. The wiki's own clinician reference records that oxycodone, codeine, and tramadol performed **equivalently to placebo** in network meta-analysis — so the transferable content is the non-opioid ladder and the communication design, not the opioid tail.

## Key Contributions

- Renders the ADA 2024 medication ladder for post-extraction pain at patient reading level with no alteration of doses or ceilings
- Adds a **0–10 pain scale** as the patient-side entry point — absent from the clinician guides
- Reframes option comparison around **duration of relief** rather than potency
- Encodes side effects pictographically, keeping the risk column accessible
- Preserves the guideline's age banding (adolescent / adult / older adult) so patient and clinician sheets align in a shared-decision conversation

## Methodology

Not a study. A patient-education artifact produced by the ADA Science and Research Institute with the University of Pittsburgh and the University of Pennsylvania under FDA grant U01FD007151, derived from the guideline development process described in [[drug/analgesics/carrasco-labra-2024-pharmacologic-management-acute-dental-pain-guideline]]. 1 page, single-sheet infographic format.

## Results

| Option | Unit dose | Maximum daily |
|---|---|---|
| Ibuprofen | 400 mg | 2,400 mg |
| Naproxen sodium | 440 mg | 1,100 mg |
| Ibuprofen + acetaminophen | 400 mg + 500 mg | 2,400 mg / 4,000 mg |
| Naproxen sodium + acetaminophen | 440 mg + 500 mg | 1,100 mg / 4,000 mg |
| Acetaminophen alone (NSAID contraindicated) | 1,000 mg | 4,000 mg |
| Acetaminophen + oxycodone / hydrocodone (Rx) | 325 mg + 5 mg / 5–7.5 mg | acetaminophen 4,000 mg |
