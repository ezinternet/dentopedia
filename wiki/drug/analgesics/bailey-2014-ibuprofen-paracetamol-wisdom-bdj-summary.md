## Three-line Summary

1. This BDJ Verifiable CPD Paper summarizes the Cochrane systematic review (CD004624) comparing single-dose oral analgesics for pain after surgical removal of impacted mandibular third molars.
2. Ibuprofen 400 mg is superior to paracetamol 1000 mg (RR 1.47; NNT = 4), and the combination tablet (ibuprofen 200 mg + paracetamol 400 mg, Nuromol) provides the greatest efficacy (RR 1.77; NNT = 3), based on 7 RCTs (2241 participants) with high-quality evidence.
3. This summary article is superseded by the primary Cochrane review and is retained for its practitioner-facing distillation of the evidence.

## Summary

This article, published in the British Dental Journal as a Verifiable CPD Paper, is a narrative summary of the Cochrane systematic review CD004624 (Bailey et al. 2013). It presents the same evidence base — 7 double-blind randomized controlled trials (RCTs) involving 2241 participants — comparing single-dose oral analgesics for pain relief after surgical removal of impacted mandibular third molars. The BDJ summary distills the Cochrane findings for a general dental audience, highlighting ibuprofen 400 mg as superior to paracetamol 1000 mg, and the combination product (ibuprofen 200 mg + paracetamol 400 mg, Nuromol) as providing the greatest pain relief. Evidence quality was rated as high across the primary comparisons. No original data were added by this summary; all numerical results derive from the Cochrane meta-analysis.

## Key Contributions

- **Ibuprofen 400 mg vs paracetamol 1000 mg**: 47% more patients experienced at least 50% pain relief (RR 1.47, 95% CI 1.28–1.69; NNT = 4)
- **Combination (ibuprofen 200 mg + paracetamol 400 mg) vs paracetamol 1000 mg**: 77% more effective (RR 1.77, 95% CI 1.32–2.39; NNT = 3)
- **Aspirin 600–900 mg vs paracetamol 1000 mg**: No significant difference (RR 1.15, 95% CI 0.93–1.42)
- **Codeine 60 mg + paracetamol 600 mg vs paracetamol 600 mg**: Non-significant trend (RR 1.24, 95% CI 0.97–1.60)
- Approximately 1 in 4 patients experienced at least 50% pain relief over 6 hours with most active analgesics; placebo response was approximately 1 in 10
- IBU 400 mg recommended as first-line due to superior efficacy, tolerability, and cost-effectiveness

## Methodology

This is a narrative summary article, not a primary systematic review. The Cochrane review it summarizes (CD004624) included 7 double-blind, single-dose, randomized, placebo-controlled trials with 2241 participants, using validated patient-reported outcomes (≥50% pain relief over 6 hours; patients using remedication). The BDJ article synthesized these findings for a practitioner audience without adding new data or analysis.
