## One-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (n=186) found prednisolone 40 mg (oral or submucosal) significantly improved OHRQoL vs no prednisolone after third molar surgery, with submucosal injection superior to oral administration (OHIP-14, p=0.001).

## 한줄요약
3군 RCT (n=186)에서 프레드니솔론(Prednisolone) 40 mg(경구 또는 점막하 주사)은 미투여 대비 구강건강관련 삶의 질(OHRQoL, OHIP-14)을 유의하게 개선했고, 점막하 주사가 경구 투여보다 우월했다(p=0.001).

## Summary
Ibikunle et al. randomized 186 patients undergoing third molar surgery into three equal groups: oral prednisolone 40 mg (Group A), submucosal injection 40 mg prednisolone (Group B), or no prednisolone (Group C). QoL was assessed using OHIP-14 preoperatively and at postoperative days 1, 3, and 7. All groups showed significant postoperative QoL deterioration peaking at day 1 with gradual recovery. Both prednisolone groups showed significantly better QoL than controls. Submucosal injection (Group B) produced significantly less QoL deterioration than oral administration (Group A, p=0.001). This is one of the few RCTs using QoL (rather than pain/trismus/swelling) as primary endpoint, providing patient-centered evidence for submucosal prednisolone. Contrasts with Larsen 2021 (methylprednisolone, OHIP-14 null result) — different drug, positive signal.

## Key Contributions
- Largest single RCT (n=186, equal groups) with OHIP-14 as primary endpoint for M3 surgery corticosteroid research
- Demonstrates submucosal route superiority over oral route for QoL outcomes
- Positive QoL signal for prednisolone contrasts with Larsen 2021 null result for methylprednisolone — drug-level difference or route effect?
- Patient-reported outcome (PRO) focus aligns with modern evidence standards

## Methodology
Three-arm RCT. n=186 (62 per group). Groups: A=oral prednisolone 40mg; B=submucosal injection prednisolone 40mg; C=no prednisolone. Assessment: preoperative + days 1, 3, 7. Primary outcome: OHIP-14 total and subscale scores. Classification: affected (OHIP ≤28) vs not affected (>28). Institution: Nigeria (tertiary hospital).

## Results
- All groups: Significant OHIP-14 score increase postoperatively (worse QoL), most marked at day 1
- Groups A+B vs C: Prednisolone significantly associated with better QoL and earlier recovery (p<0.05)
- Group B vs A: Submucosal injection significantly less QoL deterioration (p=0.001)
- Pattern: Day 1 worst → gradual improvement through day 7
