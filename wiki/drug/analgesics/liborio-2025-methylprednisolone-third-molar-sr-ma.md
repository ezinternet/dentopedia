## Three-line Summary
PRISMA-compliant, PROSPERO-registered SR+MA (CRD42024512561) of 9 RCTs (2013–2023), with 5 meta-analyzed, specifically evaluated methylprednisolone in impacted third molar surgery — the first SR+MA isolating this drug from other corticosteroids.
Methylprednisolone significantly reduced trismus at 48h (p<0.01, I²=0%) and 7d (p<0.05, I²=0%) but had no significant effect on pain (p=0.85 at 24h), and dexamethasone outperformed methylprednisolone for trismus reduction in head-to-head comparison.
With zero heterogeneity for trismus outcomes and no adverse effects reported across included studies, the review supports methylprednisolone for trismus and edema management while recommending dexamethasone as preferred when trismus reduction is the primary clinical goal.

## Summary
Libório et al. conducted a PRISMA-compliant, PROSPERO-registered SR+MA specifically evaluating methylprednisolone in impacted third molar surgery. Nine RCTs from 2013–2023 met inclusion criteria; five were meta-analyzed. Pain at 24h and 7d showed no significant effect vs controls (I²=47%, p=0.85). However, interincisal distance (trismus proxy) significantly favored methylprednisolone at 48h (I²=0%, p<0.01) and 7d (I²=0%, p<0.05). Head-to-head comparison revealed dexamethasone superior for trismus reduction, while methylprednisolone achieved better interincisal level restoration. No adverse effects were reported. The authors conclude methylprednisolone is safe and effective for trismus and edema but not for pain control, and dexamethasone should be preferred when trismus is the primary concern.

## Key Contributions
- First SR+MA isolating methylprednisolone only (not pooled with all corticosteroids)
- Zero heterogeneity (I²=0%) for trismus outcomes — robust and reliable pooled estimate
- Establishes drug-level hierarchy: dexamethasone > methylprednisolone for trismus; methylprednisolone ≥ dexamethasone for interincisal restoration
- PROSPERO registration and contemporary (2013–2023) evidence window

## Methodology
PRISMA SR+MA. PROSPERO CRD42024512561. Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE + Scopus + manual search. Inclusion: RCTs, 2013–2023, English, methylprednisolone for impacted M3, ≥10 patients, non-smokers, no uncontrolled systemic disease. Risk of bias: revised Cochrane RoB tool. 9 studies included total; 5 meta-analyzed for pain and interincisal outcomes.

## Results
- Pain at 24h: I²=47%, p=0.85 — NS (methylprednisolone = control)
- Pain at 7d: NS
- Interincisal distance at 48h: I²=0%, methylprednisolone > control (p<0.01)
- Interincisal distance at 7d: I²=0%, methylprednisolone > control (p<0.05)
- vs Dexamethasone (head-to-head): Dexamethasone better for trismus; methylprednisolone better for interincisal opening
- Adverse effects: None across all included RCTs
