## Three-line Summary

Narrative review quantifying adverse event rates for tooth extraction across five major medical comorbidities: DM (10-15% intraoperative hypoglycemia risk), anticoagulation (8-12% postoperative bleeding vs 2-3% controls), hypertension (20-25% perioperative BP elevation), asthma/COPD (3-6% bronchospasm), and immunosuppression (up to 20% postoperative infection). The review provides five structured clinical tables and proposes evidence-based safe-to-proceed thresholds: HbA1c <9%, INR ≤3.5, and BP <180/110 mmHg. A stepwise safety ladder for pre-, intra-, and postoperative care aims to standardize risk decision-making globally.

## Summary

Tooth extraction in medically compromised adults carries substantially elevated risks of bleeding, infection, delayed healing, and systemic complications compared to healthy patients. This 2026 narrative review synthesizes complication prevalence data and emergency management strategies across five high-risk medical categories, providing structured clinical decision tables and validated thresholds to guide safe practice.

The review covers 5 comorbidity domains: (1) diabetes mellitus — the most prevalent condition at 8-12% of dental patients, with intraoperative hypoglycemia as the primary acute risk; (2) anticoagulation therapy — postoperative bleeding more than trebled versus controls; (3) hypertension — the most prevalent systemic disease in adults at up to 30%, with high perioperative BP fluctuation risk; (4) asthma/COPD — smaller absolute prevalence but meaningful bronchospasm risk under stress; (5) immunosuppression/transplant — highest infection rate ceiling at 20%.

The clinical decision framework centers on three validated cut-offs that determine safe-to-proceed vs. defer/refer decisions: HbA1c <9% for diabetic patients, INR ≤3.5 for anticoagulated patients, and BP <180/110 mmHg for hypertensive patients. A stepwise safety ladder operationalizes pre-, intra-, and postoperative care across all five domains.

## Key Contributions

- First paper in this wiki to co-locate quantitative adverse-event rates for five comorbidity categories in the context of tooth extraction specifically
- Validates HbA1c <9% as a safe surgery threshold (aligns with implant-placement evidence: [[implants/survival/wagner-2022-diabetes-mellitus-dental-implants-sr]])
- Confirms INR ≤3.5 as a safe-to-proceed threshold, adding a tooth-extraction-specific data point alongside broader anticoagulant reviews
- Introduces a "safety ladder" framework concept for structured pre/intra/postoperative management — not seen in prior wiki pages
- Five structured tables covering medical classifications, thresholds, emergency management, referral criteria, and stepwise protocols — a potentially teachable clinical reference

## Methodology

- **Design**: Narrative review (no systematic search strategy reported in abstract)
- **Population**: Medically compromised adults undergoing tooth extraction
- **Domains covered**: DM, anticoagulation, hypertension, asthma/COPD, immunosuppression/transplant
- **Output artifacts**: 5 structured clinical tables
- **Limitations**: Abstract-only ingest; narrative design without reported bias assessment; primary sources for cited prevalence figures not verifiable from abstract alone

## Results

| Condition | Dental patient prevalence | Adverse event | Risk rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diabetes mellitus (DM) | 8–12% | Intraoperative hypoglycemia | 10–15% |
| Anticoagulated patients | — | Postoperative bleeding | 8–12% (vs 2–3% control) |
| Hypertension | Up to 30% of adults | Perioperative BP elevation | 20–25% |
| Asthma / COPD | 7–10% | Bronchospasm | 3–6% |
| Immunosuppressed / transplant | — | Postoperative infection | Up to 20% |

**Safe-to-proceed thresholds:**

| Parameter | Threshold | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| HbA1c | < 9% | Diabetes mellitus |
| INR | ≤ 3.5 | Anticoagulation therapy |
| Blood pressure | < 180/110 mmHg | Hypertension |
