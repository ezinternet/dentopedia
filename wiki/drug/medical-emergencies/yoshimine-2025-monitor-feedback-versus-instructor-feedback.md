## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=34 dental students, 2 groups) at Kagoshima University compared monitor feedback (real-time CPR simulator metrics without instructor) vs. instructor feedback during a post-pandemic BLS training session.

Monitor feedback significantly improved chest compression (CC) depth; instructor feedback significantly improved CC rate — neither modality alone optimized both components.

For effective self-directed BLS training, monitor feedback should be used for CC depth and instructor feedback for CC rate; the findings support a complementary hybrid approach as pandemic-driven self-study environments expand.

## 세줄요약

카고시마대 치과대학생 무작위대조시험 (n=34, 2군) — 모니터 피드백 (교관 없이 CPR 시뮬레이터 실시간 지표) vs 교관 피드백 비교.

모니터 피드백은 흉부압박 (Chest Compression, CC) **깊이**를 유의하게 개선; 교관 피드백은 CC **속도**를 유의하게 개선 — 어느 단독 방식도 두 요소를 동시에 최적화하지 못함.

자기주도 BLS 훈련에서 깊이는 모니터, 속도는 교관 피드백을 결합하는 하이브리드 방식이 권장됨.

## Study Design

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | RCT, pre-post test |
| n (analyzed) | 34 (17 per group; 40 enrolled, 6 excluded by attrition) |
| Population | Dental students, Kagoshima University, Japan |
| Monitor feedback group | CPR training with real-time simulator metric display; no instructor present |
| Instructor feedback group | Traditional face-to-face instructor-guided CPR training |
| Primary outcomes | CC depth, CC rate, CC fraction, tidal volume |
| Assessment | Pre-test → training → post-test (same day) |
| Setting | Pandemic period (COVID-19, Japan 2020–2023) |

## Key Results

| Outcome | Monitor Feedback | Instructor Feedback | Between-group |
|---|---|---|---|
| CC depth | ↑ significant (p < 0.05) | NS improvement | Monitor > Instructor |
| CC rate | NS improvement | ↑ significant (p < 0.05) | Instructor > Monitor |
| CC fraction | Not significantly different between groups | — | — |
| Tidal volume | Not significantly different between groups | — | — |

**Key interpretation**: CC depth and CC rate require different feedback channels. Monitor feedback provides real-time visual cues optimal for physical precision (depth); instructor verbal cues are superior for rhythmic guidance (rate).

## Clinical / Educational Implications

1. **Hybrid training**: Combine monitor (depth) + instructor (rate) feedback for comprehensive BLS quality
2. **Self-directed learning feasibility**: Monitor feedback alone can partially substitute for instructors when access is limited — but CC rate training still requires instructor involvement
3. **Pandemic-era self-study**: Post-pandemic expansion of simulation-based self-study in dental schools is feasible for depth training
4. **Curriculum design**: Dental schools should structure BLS training phases — simulator self-study first (depth), then instructor session (rate)

## Limitations

- Small sample (n=34); single institution; no long-term retention follow-up
- Short-term training effect only; no data on skill retention at weeks/months
- Pandemic context may limit generalizability to normal educational conditions
- Only 4 CPR quality metrics assessed; clinical outcome data (actual resuscitation) not available
