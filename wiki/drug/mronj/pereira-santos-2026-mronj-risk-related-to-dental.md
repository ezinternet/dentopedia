## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42017067170) of 10 studies (1 RCT, 2 cohorts, 1 case-control, 6 case series; 8220 patients, mean age 71.45 ± 6.30 yr, mean follow-up 91.3 mo) on MRONJ risk around dental implants in osteoporosis patients treated with denosumab.

No meta-analysis (heterogeneity); in the comparative pool of 5428 examined patients (5043 osteoporotic, 366 on denosumab with implants, 1817 implants), 18 osteoporotic denosumab patients developed implant-related MRONJ involving 24 implants; implant failure rates under denosumab ranged 6%–100% across series (Seki case-control: 11.2% vs 2.9% control).

Cases fit an IPTO (implant-presence-triggered) pattern more than ISTO (surgery-triggered) — all cases in Seki and Massaad & Magremanne were IPTO while the ISTO-oriented Otto series had zero MRONJ; BP→denosumab transition patients showed higher incidence and recurrence, so placement is not contraindicated but failed/peri-implantitis-inflamed implants can trigger necrosis.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (PROSPERO CRD42017067170): 데노수맙 (Denosumab) 치료 골다공증 (Osteoporosis) 환자의 치과 임플란트 (Dental implant) 관련 약물 관련 악골 괴사 (Medication-Related Osteonecrosis of the Jaw, MRONJ) 위험을 10편(RCT 1·코호트 2·환자대조군 1·증례군 6, 총 8220명, 평균 연령 71.45±6.30세, 평균 추적 91.3개월)에서 평가.

이질성으로 메타분석 불가; 비교 풀 5428명(골다공증 5043·데노수맙+임플란트 366명·임플란트 1817개) 중 **18명이 임플란트 관련 MRONJ 발생(24개 임플란트)**; 데노수맙 하 임플란트 실패율은 증례군 간 6%~100% 편차(Seki: 항흡수제군 11.2% vs 대조군 2.9%).

사례는 수술 유발(ISTO)보다 **기존 임플란트 존재 유발(IPTO) 패턴 우세**(Seki·Massaad 전 사례 IPTO, Otto 증례군 0건); BP→데노수맙 전환군에서 발생률·재발 모두 높아 식립 자체는 금기가 아니나 실패·이식 주변염 (Peri-implantitis) 임플란트가 괴사 유발인자가 될 수 있음.

## Summary

PROSPERO-registered systematic review (CRD42017067170) of 10 studies assessing MRONJ risk related to dental implants in osteoporosis patients treated with denosumab, including 1 RCT, 2 retrospective cohorts, 1 case-control study, and 6 case series totaling 8220 patients with a mean follow-up of 91.3 months. Methodological heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis, so a narrative synthesis was performed across a comparative pool of 5428 examined patients, of whom 366 were on denosumab with 1817 implants analyzed. Among these denosumab-treated implant patients, 18 developed MRONJ involving 24 implants — a low absolute incidence judged significant enough to warrant caution. Observed cases consistently fit an implant-presence-triggered (IPTO) rather than surgery-triggered (ISTO) pattern, and patients transitioning from bisphosphonates to denosumab showed higher incidence and post-surgical recurrence. The authors conclude that implant placement under osteoporosis-dose denosumab is not inherently contraindicated, but failed or peri-implantitis-inflamed implants may act as local triggers for osteonecrosis, mandating close monitoring.

## Key Contributions

- First denosumab-specific systematic review of the implant–MRONJ interface in osteoporosis (10 studies, 8220 patients)
- Quantitative anchor: 18/366 denosumab-treated implant patients developed MRONJ (24 implants involved)
- Systematic application of the IPTO vs ISTO trigger taxonomy — observed cases are predominantly implant-presence-triggered, shifting the clinical question from placement safety to implant survival/maintenance safety
- Compiled DEN-context implant failure rates (6%–100% across series; Seki 11.2% ART vs 2.9% control) and peri-implantitis prevalence (Seki: 18.2% implants, 13.1% patients)
- Confirms BP→DEN sequential therapy as a risk amplifier in the implant context (higher incidence + higher post-surgical recurrence, Voss 2018)

## Methodology

- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD42017067170; PRISMA 2020
- **Databases/search**: PRISMA-guided systematic search (registered protocol); narrative/descriptive synthesis only — meta-analysis precluded by heterogeneity
- **Quality tools**: RoB 1 (RCT), Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (cohorts: Cheng 8/9, Voss 7/9 stars), JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist (case series/case-control); two independent assessors + third-party adjudication
- **PRISMA flow outcome**: 10 studies included — Watts 2019 (RCT, n=4550), Cheng 2022 (cohort, n=3486), Voss 2018 (retrospective, n=52), Seki 2025 (case-control, n=61), Bagan 2016 / Escobedo 2020 / Massaad & Magremanne 2022 / Otto 2023 / Smith 2022 / Tempesta 2022 (case series, n=10/7/6/16/13/19)
- **Only low-quality study**: Bagan 2016 (high risk of bias, JBI) — retained for clinical relevance

| Smith 2022 | Case series | 13 | 6 | 2 pts (peri-implantitis reclassified as MRONJ) |
| Tempesta 2022 | Case series | 19 | 37 | 19 pts (PI-MRONJ-like, maxilla ×5) |
| Escobedo 2020 | Case series | 7 | 33 | 2 pts (IPTO confirmed) |
| Bagan 2016 | Case series | 10 | 1 | 1 pt (stage I, conservative, not cured) |

**Comparative pool totals**: 5428 patients examined · 5043 osteoporotic · 366 on denosumab with implants · 1817 implants · **18 MRONJ cases in osteoporotic DEN patients with implants · 24 implants affected**

**Benchmarks cited**: Watts 2019 overall DEN MRONJ incidence 5.2 per 10,000 patient-years; duration-dependent risk 0.04% (3 yr) → 0.06% (5 yr) → 0.44% (10 yr); Jung 2022 extraction cohort 4.1%; Stavropoulos 2018 — implant presence ~30%, installation/explantation ~16% of MRONJ triggers.
