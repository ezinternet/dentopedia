## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center cohort (n=128 denosumab patients, Munich 2011–2020): 40 controls without MRONJ, 46 with MRONJ on denosumab alone, 42 with MRONJ after a bisphosphonate→denosumab switch.

Logistic regression identified hormonal therapy (OR 6.33 [1.41–28.43]), chemotherapy (OR 2.97), hypertension (OR 2.96), breast cancer (OR 2.83), denture use (OR 2.74), periodontal disease (OR 2.46), apical ostitis (OR 2.04), and longer ART duration (OR 1.07/month) as significant MRONJ risk factors; high-dose 120 mg denosumab predominated in MRONJ groups (p<0.01).

The BP→denosumab switch did not affect MRONJ onset (p=0.86), while drug holiday (OR 0.24 [0.07–0.79]) and dental implants present before ART (OR 0.85) were associated with reduced risk.

## Summary

Wick et al. (Clin Oral Investig 2022) conducted a retrospective single-center cohort study of 128 denosumab-treated patients in Munich (2011–2020), divided into controls without MRONJ (n=40), MRONJ on denosumab alone (n=46), and MRONJ after a bisphosphonate→denosumab switch (n=42). Logistic regression identified oncologic co-medication (hormonal therapy OR 6.33; chemotherapy OR 2.97), vascular comorbidity (hypertension OR 2.96; diabetes mellitus significant only univariately), breast cancer (OR 2.83), and oral-inflammatory conditions (periodontal disease OR 2.46; apical ostitis OR 2.04; denture use OR 2.74) as independent risk factors for MRONJ onset, with high-dose 120 mg denosumab strongly concentrated in the MRONJ groups (p<0.01). The BP→denosumab switch itself did not influence onset (p=0.86). Two counterintuitive protective associations emerged: periprocedural drug holiday (OR 0.24, >75% risk reduction — hypothesis-generating, only 13 treated patients) and dental implants present before ART (OR 0.85), which the authors attribute to superior oral hygiene among implant patients. The risk-factor profile closely mirrors known bisphosphonate-MRONJ risk factors, underlining local inflammation as the common triggering event despite the two drug classes' divergent pharmacokinetics.

## Key Contributions

- First adequately powered three-group denosumab-MRONJ risk-factor analysis (prior largest comparison n=14), adding a dedicated BP→denosumab switch arm
- Adjusted risk-factor hierarchy for denosumab: hormonal therapy > chemotherapy > hypertension > breast cancer > denture use > periodontal disease > apical ostitis > ART duration
- Dose dependence confirmed at onset level: 120 mg in 76.1%/71.4% of MRONJ groups vs 32.5% of controls (p<0.01)
- BP-switch neutrality (p=0.86): prior bisphosphonate exposure does not alter MRONJ onset risk under denosumab
- Dental implants before ART are not a risk factor for denosumab-MRONJ (OR 0.846) — peri-implant hygiene matters more than implant presence
- Drug holiday associated with >75% MRONJ risk reduction (OR 0.241) — explicitly flagged as low-n and requiring prospective confirmation

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective single-center cohort; "Medicine and Aesthetics" Clinic for Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Munich; 2011–2020; ethics BLÄK 2020–1228
- **Screening**: 273 denosumab-treated patients → 145 excluded (head-and-neck irradiation, maxillofacial metastatic disease, missing follow-up/X-ray/history) → n=128
- **Groups**: I control (denosumab, no MRONJ, n=40); II Test 1 (denosumab + MRONJ, n=46, 49 lesions); III Test 2 (BP→denosumab switch + MRONJ, n=42, 48 lesions)
- **Inclusion**: ≥1 denosumab dose; AAOMS MRONJ criteria (groups II/III); panoramic X-ray; ≥12-month follow-up; complete medical history
- **Cohort**: female 61.7%; median age 72.2 y; cancer 67.2% (breast 31.3%, prostate 24.2%); osteoporosis 44.5%; bone metastasis 67.2%; 120 mg 60.9%; prior BP 32.8% (zoledronate 16.4%); median ART duration 38.7 months; follow-up 14.0 months
- **Care pathway**: yearly prophylactic exams per 2018 German MRONJ guidelines; single-surgeon surgical protocol with amoxicillin/clavulanate 875/125 mg from 1 week preop to 3 weeks postop (clindamycin 600 mg if allergic); no investigator-recommended drug holiday (prescriber-initiated in 13 cases, 10.2%)
- **Statistics**: SPSS v21 + R 4.0.3; Kolmogorov–Smirnov; t-test/chi-square/ANOVA or Kruskal–Wallis with Bonferroni post hoc; binary logistic regression

## Results

**Univariate significance vs control** (chi-square/t-test): underlying disease p=0.01; denosumab dosage p<0.01; co-medications p=0.05; vascular diseases p=0.04; dental inflammations significant; corticosteroids p=0.04; diabetes mellitus p=0.04; medication switch p=0.86 (NS); sex, smoking, alcohol NS.

**Logistic regression — risk-elevating predictors**:

| Variable | Odds ratio [95% CI] | p |
|---|---|---|
| Hormonal therapy | 6.33 [1.41–28.43] | 0.016 |
| Chemotherapy / molecular targeted | 2.97 [1.18–7.46] | 0.021 |
| Hypertension | 2.96 [1.22–7.16] | 0.016 |
| Breast cancer | 2.83 [1.12–7.12] | 0.027 |
| Denture use | 2.74 [1.20–6.28] | 0.017 |
| Periodontal disease | 2.46 [1.12–5.40] | 0.026 |
| Apical ostitis | 2.04 [0.92–4.51] | 0.018 |
| Duration of ART (per month) | 1.07 [1.00–1.13] | 0.037 |

**Risk-decreasing variables**:

| Variable | Odds ratio [95% CI] | p |
|---|---|---|
| Drug holiday | 0.241 [0.073–0.792] | 0.019 |
| Dental implants prior to ART | 0.846 [0.189–1.018] | 0.043 |

**Lesion characteristics (88 MRONJ patients, 97 lesions)**: mandible 68.0%, maxilla 32.0%, both jaws 9 patients (7.0%); surgical therapy 92.0%; median time since MRONJ diagnosis 3.0 years.

**Interpretation anchors**: high-dose 120 mg concentrated in MRONJ groups (76.1%/71.4% vs 32.5% control); cancer predominated in MRONJ groups (72.5%/65.2%) vs osteoporosis in controls (predominant); implant patients had fewer periodontitis cases, plausibly explaining the neutral-to-protective implant signal.
