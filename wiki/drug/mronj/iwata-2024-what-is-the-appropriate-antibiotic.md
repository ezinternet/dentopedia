## Three-line Summary

Multicenter retrospective study (10 Japanese hospitals, 2012–2022): 68 cancer patients on high-dose denosumab 120 mg q4wk underwent 173 tooth extractions; MRONJ developed after 32/173 teeth (18.4%).

Antibiotic practice was highly non-uniform across hospitals/surgeons; postoperative antibiotics were given after 91.9% of extractions (amoxicillin 78.0% of courses), yet no significant association existed between MRONJ and antibiotic dosage or duration (p=0.164–1.000 across all comparisons).

In the amoxicillin-only subset (123 teeth, 51 patients; MRONJ 21/123 teeth, 17.0%), neither dose (500 vs 750 mg/day, p=1.000) nor duration (2 vs 3 days, p=1.000; >3 days: 4/47 teeth, 8.5%) affected MRONJ — supporting a single preoperative amoxicillin dose as sufficient.

## Summary

Iwata et al. (Cureus 2024) performed the first multicenter survey of perioperative antibiotic administration during tooth extraction in patients receiving high-dose denosumab (120 mg q4wk), covering 68 cancer patients and 173 extracted teeth across 10 Japanese hospitals (2012–2022). MRONJ developed after 18.4% of extractions (32/173 teeth), and practice varied widely: preoperative antibiotics in only 41.0% of cases but postoperative antibiotics in 91.9%, with amoxicillin dominant (78.0% of postoperative courses) and roughly 40% of courses exceeding 3 days. Across all comparisons — route (oral vs intravenous), dosage, and duration up to 7 days — no significant association with MRONJ development emerged, a null result replicated in the homogeneous amoxicillin-only subset (123 teeth; dose p=1.000, duration p=1.000). The authors conclude that perioperative antibiotics alone cannot prevent MRONJ and that, given antimicrobial-resistance stewardship, a single preoperative amoxicillin dose is likely adequate for this highest-risk population.

## Key Contributions

- First study to investigate actual antibiotic administration status during extraction in high-dose denosumab patients and correlate it with MRONJ development
- Quantifies the guideline vacuum: AAOMS 2022 gives no antibiotic criteria for extraction under antiresorptives; JACOJ 2023 merely references JCOPG-APS 2016, which lacks an antiresorptive category — hence the observed inter-hospital variability
- Null result with clinical weight: escalating route (IV), dose, or duration (up to 7 days) showed no MRONJ benefit in 173 teeth, replicated in the 123-tooth AMPC-only subset
- AMR stewardship implication: ~40% of extractions received antibiotics >3 days without demonstrated benefit → single preoperative AMPC dose is the defensible default
- Tooth-level MRONJ incidence benchmark for oncologic denosumab extractions: 18.4% overall, 17.0% under AMPC

## Methodology

- **Design**: Multicenter retrospective cohort; 10 hospitals in Hyogo, Japan; January 2012 – December 2022; IRB Kakogawa Central City Hospital 2022-20 (opt-out)
- **Population**: 68 patients aged ≥18 y on denosumab 120 mg every 4 weeks for solid-cancer bone metastases or multiple myeloma; exclusions: pre-existing MRONJ at extraction, jaw radiation, jaw metastatic disease, opt-out
- **Outcome/staging**: MRONJ per AAOMS 2022 (exposed bone/probeable fistula >8 weeks); stage 0 excluded per JACOJ 2023 convention
- **Exposures**: preoperative antibiotic content (71 procedures: AMPC 52.1%, CLDM 22.5%, IV agents 21.2%) and postoperative content (159 procedures: AMPC 78.0%), each categorized by dose and duration (1–7 days)
- **AMPC-only subset**: 123 teeth / 51 patients (median age 69.0, range 23–88; breast 35.3%, lung 23.5%, prostate 17.6%; chemotherapy 45.1%; median Dmab duration 6.0 months, range 0–108; drug holiday 7.8%)
- **Statistics**: Ekuseru-Toukei 2016; Fisher's exact / chi-squared tests; p<0.05

## Results

**All teeth (n=173)**: MRONJ 32 (18.4%). Preoperative antibiotics 41.0% vs none 59.0%; postoperative antibiotics 91.9% vs none 8.1%.

| Comparison | Result | p |
|---|---|---|
| Route sequence, pre+post (oral→oral vs IV-containing) | oral→oral 12 vs 22 among non-events | 0.336 |
| Duration, pre+post (2 / 3 / ≥4 days) | NS | 0.633 |
| Duration, only postop (1–2 / 3 / 4–5 days) | NS | 0.164 |
| Antibiotics >3 days | MRONJ 11/71 teeth (15.5%); 7-day courses (2 teeth): 0 events | — |

**AMPC-only subset (n=123 teeth, 51 patients)**: MRONJ 21/123 teeth (17.0%); patient-level 15/51 (29.4%) — stage 1: 9, stage 2: 6, stage 3: 0.

**Interpretation anchors**: high-dose denosumab carries higher MRONJ risk than zoledronate (9.6% vs 4.8%, p=0.009; meta-analytic RR 1.41 [1.01–1.95]); prior German IV-penicillin protocol achieved 7.5% (3/40 teeth) but was judged insufficient evidence; drug holiday was rare in this cohort (7.8%), so results reflect extraction under ongoing denosumab.
