## Three-line Summary

Korean Labor Standards Act (in force 2026-10-08) §65 and §74, plus Enforcement Decree Annex 4 (amended 2021-11-19), setting employer duties toward pregnant workers in every workplace, including dental clinics.

Annex 4 prohibits employing pregnant women in radiation work whose dose exceeds the Nuclear Safety Act limits; §74⑤ requires converting a pregnant worker to easier work on her request and bans overtime; §74⑦ grants a 2-hour daily reduction before 12 weeks or after 32 weeks without pay cut.

For a dental clinic, the request-based job conversion (§74⑤) is the directly enforceable lever — a pregnant hygienist can ask to be taken off radiography — while the Annex 4 radiation ban is tied to Nuclear Safety Act limits that typical shielded dental work does not approach.

## 세줄요약

근로기준법(2026-10-08 시행) 제65조·제74조와 시행령 별표 4(2021-11-19 개정) — 치과를 포함한 모든 사업장의 임신 근로자 보호 의무.

별표 4: 원자력안전법상 선량한도를 초과하는 방사선 업무에 임신 중 여성 사용 금지. 제74조⑤: 임신 중 시간외근로 금지, 본인 요구 시 쉬운 종류의 근로로 전환 의무. 제74조⑦: 임신 12주 이내·32주 이후 1일 2시간 단축, 임금 삭감 금지.

치과에서 직접 작동하는 장치는 제74조⑤의 요구 시 업무 전환 — 임신 치위생사는 촬영 업무 제외를 요구할 수 있다. 별표 4의 방사선 금지는 원자력안전법 한도에 연동돼 있어 차폐된 치과 촬영은 그 수준에 이르지 않는다.

## Summary

§65 prohibits employing pregnant or post-partum (<1 year) women in morally or hygienically harmful or dangerous work, with the occupations specified by presidential decree. Enforcement Decree Annex 4 lists, for pregnant women: (1) radiation work in which the dose to radiation workers under the Nuclear Safety Act §91(2) exceeds the dose limit; (3) work with a high risk of contamination by pathogens such as CMV or hepatitis B, with an exception for licensed doctors, nurses, radiographers and trainees; (4) work requiring severe bending or sustained crouching or forward bending; and (5) lifting ≥5 kg continuously or ≥10 kg intermittently. §74 grants 90 days of maternity leave (120 for multiples), bans overtime for pregnant workers, mandates conversion to easier work on the worker's request, guarantees return to the same or equivalent post, allows a 2-hour daily reduction before 12 weeks or after 32 weeks (whole pregnancy if at risk) without pay cut, and allows changing start and end times.

## Key Contributions

- Request-based conversion to easier work (§74⑤) is mandatory for the employer — the legal basis for letting a pregnant hygienist opt out of radiography.
- The radiation prohibition (Annex 4-1) is conditional on exceeding Nuclear Safety Act limits — it is not a blanket ban on radiation work during pregnancy.
- Other Annex 4 items are relevant to dental assisting during pregnancy: pathogen-exposure work (the licence exception covers doctors, nurses and radiographers; dental hygienists are not named) and sustained forward-bent posture.
- Shortened hours (§74⑦) and no overtime (§74⑤) apply regardless of radiation duties.

## Results

| Provision | Employer duty |
|---|---|
| §65 + Annex 4-1 | No radiation work exceeding Nuclear Safety Act dose limits |
| Annex 4-3 | No high pathogen-contamination work (exception: licensed doctor/nurse/radiographer) |
| Annex 4-4 | No work requiring severe bending or sustained crouching/forward bending |
| Annex 4-5 | No lifting ≥5 kg continuous / ≥10 kg intermittent |
| §74① | Maternity leave 90 days (premature 100; multiples 120) |
| §74⑤ | No overtime; convert to easier work **on the worker's request** |
| §74⑥ | Return to same or equivalent-pay post |
| §74⑦⑧ | 2 h/day reduction <12 wk or >32 wk (whole pregnancy if at risk), no pay cut |
| §74⑨ | Allow change of start/end time keeping daily hours |

## Limitations

Whether dental hygienists fall under the Annex 4-3 licence exception is not stated (only doctor, nurse, radiographer named) — not interpreted here. Extract covers §65, §74 and Annex 4 pregnant-woman row only.
