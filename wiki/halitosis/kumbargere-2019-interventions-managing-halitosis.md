## Three-line Summary
Cochrane systematic review (2019, first publication) of 44 RCTs (1,809 participants, age 17–77) comparing interventions for managing halitosis due to oral diseases — mechanical tongue cleaning, chewing gums, systemic deodorising agents, topical agents, toothpastes, mouth rinses, tablets, combination methods.
Low to very low certainty evidence (GRADE ⊕⊝⊝⊝ to ⊕⊕⊝⊝) for all comparisons: mechanical tongue cleaning vs no cleaning (MD −0.20 OLT, 2 RCTs, 46 pts), 0.6% eucalyptus gum vs placebo (MD −0.10 OLT, 1 RCT, 65 pts), 1000 mg champignon extract vs placebo (MD −1.07 VAS, 1 RCT, 40 pts), hinokitiol gel vs placebo (MD −0.27 OLT, 1 RCT, 18 pts), 0.3% triclosan toothpaste vs control (MD −3.48 OLT, 1 RCT, 81 pts), CHX+zinc acetate mouthwash vs placebo (MD −0.20 OLT, 1 RCT, 44 pts), brushing + cetylpyridinium mouthwash vs brushing (MD −0.48 OLT, 1 RCT, 70 pts).
No single intervention or concentration proven superior; most trials short-term (1–4 weeks), high/unclear risk of bias; well-planned RCTs with standardised interventions urgently needed.

## Summary
This Cochrane Intervention Review (first published 2019, incorporating previous reviews on tongue cleaning and mouth rinses for halitosis) assessed the effects of various interventions for managing halitosis due to oral diseases. The review included 44 randomised controlled trials involving 1,809 participants aged 17–77 years. Interventions were categorised into eight groups: mechanical debridement (tongue cleaning), chewing gums, systemic deodorising agents, topical agents, toothpastes, mouth rinses/mouthwashes, tablets, and combination methods. Most trials reported short-term follow-up (1–4 weeks); only one trial reported 3-month follow-up. Risk of bias was low in 3 studies, high in 16, and unclear in 25. **The certainty of evidence (GRADE) was rated low to very low for all comparisons**, primarily due to risk of bias and small sample sizes. Key findings: mechanical tongue cleaning vs no cleaning showed very low-certainty evidence of reduction in dentist-reported OLT score (MD −0.20, 95% CI −0.34 to −0.07, 2 RCTs, 46 participants); 0.6% eucalyptus chewing gum vs placebo gum showed very low-certainty evidence (MD −0.10, 95% CI −0.31 to 0.11, 1 RCT, 65 participants); 1000 mg champignon extract vs placebo showed very low-certainty evidence for patient-reported VAS (MD −1.07, 95% CI −14.51 to 12.37, 1 RCT, 40 participants); hinokitiol gel vs placebo gel showed very low-certainty evidence (MD −0.27, 95% CI −1.26 to 0.72, 1 RCT, 18 participants); 0.3% triclosan toothpaste vs control showed very low-certainty evidence (MD −3.48, 95% CI −3.77 to −3.19, 1 RCT, 81 participants); CHX + zinc acetate mouthwash vs placebo showed very low-certainty evidence (MD −0.20, 95% CI −0.58 to 0.18, 1 RCT, 44 participants); brushing + cetylpyridinium mouthwash vs brushing showed low-certainty evidence (MD −0.48, 95% CI −0.72 to −0.24, 1 RCT, 70 participants). No data were reported for patient-reported outcomes in most comparisons, and adverse events were poorly reported. The authors conclude that **there is insufficient evidence to determine which intervention works better**, and well-planned RCTs standardising interventions and concentrations are urgently needed.

- **모든 비교 '낮음~매우 낮음 확실성'(GRADE ⊕⊝⊝⊝~⊕⊕⊝⊝)** — 비뚤림 위험 높음·표본 작음·단기 추적·단일 연구 결과 다수
- 환자 보고 결과(Patient-reported OLT/VAS) 데이터 거의 없음, 부작용 보고 미흡
- **어떤 중재·농도도 우월성 입증 안 됨** — 표준화된 프로토콜의 고품질 RCT 필요

## Methodology
- **Design**: Cochrane systematic review (Intervention Review) — standard Cochrane methodology
- **Databases**: Cochrane Oral Health Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE Ovid (1946–2019), Embase Ovid (1980–2019), LILACS BIREME, Indian Medical Journals, OpenGrey, CINAHL EBSCO, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP, ISRCTN, CTRI-India (to 8–19 Apr 2019)
- **Selection**: RCTs, adults ≥16 years, any intervention for oral-disease halitosis vs other/placebo/no intervention, minimum 1-week follow-up; excluded quasi-RCTs, <1 week follow-up, advanced periodontitis
- **n**: 44 RCTs, 1,809 participants (age 17–77)
- **Outcomes**: Primary — dentist-reported OLT (organoleptic test) score, patient-reported OLT/VAS; Secondary — VSC (H₂S, CH₃SH, (CH₃)₂S), adverse events
- **Analysis**: Mean differences with 95% CI; GRADE assessment; risk of bias (3 low, 16 high, 25 unclear)
