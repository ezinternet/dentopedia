## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 구취 (Halitosis)는 85–90%가 구강 내 원인(설태, 치주질환, 치성 감염)이며, 나머지 10–15%는 구강 외 원인(ENT 10%, 위장/내분비 5%)이다.
> - 국제구취연구학회 (International Society for Breath Odor Research, ISBOR) 분류: 진성 구취(생리적/병적 구강 내·구강 외) vs 망상성 구취(가성 구취·구취 공포증).
> - 구강 내 휘발성 황화합물 (Volatile Sulfur Compounds, VSC)의 90%는 H₂S와 메틸메르캅탄; 주요 생성균은 그람 음성 혐기성 세균(T. denticola, P. gingivalis, F. nucleatum 등).
> - 치료 4단계: ① 기계적 제거(설태 긁개가 칫솔보다 VSC 75% vs 45% 감소로 우월) → ② 화학적 항균(클로르헥시딘(Chlorhexidine, CHX) 0.2% = 금표준, VSC 43% 감소) → ③ VSC 중화(아연·이산화염소) → ④ 마스킹(단기 효과만).
> - CHX + 세틸피리디늄 클로라이드 (Cetylpyridinium Chloride, CPC) + 아연 젖산염 조합이 단독보다 우월한 시너지 효과 (코크란 리뷰 확인).
> - 프로바이오틱스(Streptococcus salivarius K12, Lactobacillus salivarius, Weissella cibaria)는 보조 치료로 VSC 억제 가능성 있음; 대규모 RCT 미흡.
> - 구강 외 원인: 편도 결석은 VSC 위험 10배 증가; 대사 질환(당뇨병성 케톤산증·신부전·간부전·트리메틸아민뇨증)은 특징적 구취 패턴 각각.
> - 가성 구취와 구취 공포증은 구강 치료 불가; 임상 심리 / 정신과 의뢰 필수.
> - 임상 takeaway: 모든 구취 환자는 구강 검진 우선, 원인별 다학제 의뢰(이비인후과·소화기내과·정신건강의학과) 체계가 핵심.

## Three-line Summary

This 2022 narrative review by Izidoro et al. synthesizes the ISBOR classification of halitosis—genuine (physiological/pathological) versus delusional (pseudohalitosis/halitophobia)—alongside the multifactorial etiology dominated by intra-oral causes in 85–90% of cases. Standard treatment follows a four-phase intra-oral ladder (mechanical removal, chemical antibacterials, VSC neutralization, masking), with CHX 0.2% as the gold-standard rinse (43% VSC reduction) and tongue scrapers outperforming toothbrushes (75% vs. 45% VSC reduction). Novel approaches—probiotics (S. salivarius K12, L. salivarius, W. cibaria) and zinc-CHX combinations—show promise but require robust RCTs to confirm clinical efficacy.

## 세줄요약

Izidoro 등(2022)은 ISBOR 분류(진성·망상성 구취)와 구강 내 원인 중심의 다인성 병인론을 종합한 서술적 리뷰를 발표했다. 치료는 4단계 구강 내 사다리(기계적 제거 → 화학적 항균 → VSC 중화 → 마스킹) 구조이며 CHX 0.2%가 금표준(VSC 43% 감소), 설태 긁개가 칫솔보다 우월하다(VSC 75% vs 45%). 프로바이오틱스와 아연-CHX 복합제는 가능성이 있으나 대규모 RCT가 부족하여 임상 적용 근거가 미흡하다.

## Summary

Halitosis affects an estimated 2.4–78% of the population (high variability due to measurement heterogeneity). The ISBOR framework classifies it as genuine (further divided into physiological and intra-oral/extra-oral pathological) versus delusional (pseudohalitosis and halitophobia). VSCs—primarily hydrogen sulfide and methyl mercaptan—produced by anaerobic Gram-negative bacteria are the central olfactory mechanism. The oral cavity is the source in 85–90% of cases, with tongue coating being the dominant contributor followed by periodontal disease and odontogenic factors. Treatment is individualized: intra-oral halitosis is managed through a four-phase ladder, while extra-oral causes demand multidisciplinary referral depending on the organ system involved (ENT, GI, endocrinology, psychiatry).

## Key Contributions

- **ISBOR classification operationalized**: Provides clinician-ready decision tree distinguishing genuine halitosis subtypes from delusional variants (pseudohalitosis, halitophobia) with distinct management pathways.
- **Tongue scraper superiority confirmed**: 75% VSC reduction vs. 45% with toothbrush (van der Sleen SR); posterior tongue sulcus terminalis identified as highest-load zone.
- **CHX gold standard with quantified benchmarks**: 0.2% CHX achieves 43% VSC reduction and 50% organoleptic score reduction throughout the day; CHX + CPC + zinc lactate (0.05%/0.05%/0.14%) synergistic combination confirmed by Cochrane review.
- **Probiotic horizon**: S. salivarius K12, L. salivarius, and W. cibaria demonstrate VSC inhibition in vitro/in vivo; oral and gut probiotic routes both explored.
- **Extra-oral spectrum mapped**: Tonsilloliths = 10-fold VSC risk; GI tract responsible for only ~0.5% of halitosis despite patient attribution; metabolic causes (DM ketoacidosis, uremia, fetor hepaticus, trimethylaminuria) produce distinct odor signatures.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Comprehensive narrative review; no systematic search protocol or PRISMA reporting.
- **Classification framework**: ISBOR taxonomy applied throughout.
- **VSC measurement context**: Gas chromatography-mass spectrometry referenced for extra-oral diagnosis; organoleptic scoring used as clinical outcome in cited trials.
- **Treatment evidence levels**: Mix of RCTs, Cochrane reviews, in vitro/in vivo studies, and expert consensus; no pooled meta-analysis performed by authors.

## Results

**Etiology**
- 85–90% of halitosis: intra-oral (tongue coating > periodontal disease > odontogenic)
- ~10%: ENT (sinusitis, tonsilloliths, bronchiectasis, lung abscess)
- ~5%: gastrointestinal/endocrine/metabolic
- ~0.5% only: gastric origin (despite common patient belief)
- Xerostomia: medication is the most common cause; ~25% of elderly affected

**Intra-oral treatment efficacy**
- Tongue scraper: 75% VSC reduction (vs. 45% toothbrush)
- CHX 0.2%: 43% VSC reduction, 50% organoleptic score reduction
- CHX + CPC + zinc lactate: significant organoleptic score reduction (Cochrane, n=293 across 5 RCTs); increased tooth/tongue staining as side effect
- Zinc 0.3% + CHX 0.025%: H₂S drops 0.16% at 1 h → 0.75% at 3 h (synergistic)
- Chlorine dioxide: 29% odor reduction at 4 h
- Essential oils (Listerine): ~25% reduction, ~3 h duration only
- Probiotics (S. salivarius K12, L. salivarius, W. cibaria): VSC inhibition in vitro/in vivo; clinical benefit for physiological halitosis and periodontal bleeding

**Extra-oral treatment principles**
- Halitophobia: psychologic/psychiatric referral; dental treatment futile
- Xerostomia: sialogogues, artificial saliva, pilocarpine 5–10 mg/day; cevimeline 30 mg TID for severe cases (Sjögren, post-radiotherapy)
- Systemic causes: treat underlying disease; multidisciplinary team (dentist, otolaryngologist, nutritionist, gastroenterologist, clinical psychologist)
