## Three-line Summary

This RCT with 52 participants assessed audiovisual instructions with weekly reminders vs verbal instructions alone on Hawley retainer wear compliance over 6 months. Mean daily wear time was 14.9h (±4.9) at T1 and 14.3h (±5.4) at T2; the audiovisual group showed significantly better compliance at 6 months (p=0.033) but not at 3 months (p=0.065). No significant differences in gingival or plaque index scores between groups.

## Summary

This randomized clinical trial evaluated whether multimodal (audiovisual + electronic reminder) interventions improve compliance with removable retainer wear compared to standard verbal instructions. Using TheraMon microsensors for objective wear-time measurement, the study found that while both groups showed suboptimal compliance (well below the prescribed 22 h/day), the audiovisual intervention group demonstrated significantly better adherence at the 6-month mark. The findings suggest that multimodal behavioral interventions can have a sustained positive effect on retainer compliance, though the overall wear time remained below prescription levels.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT combining audiovisual integration with weekly electronic reminders for retainer compliance
- Objective wear-time measurement via TheraMon microsensor
- Demonstrates long-term (6-month) effectiveness of multimodal behavioral intervention
- Highlights persistent compliance gap (actual ~14h vs prescribed 22h)

## Methodology

RCT at Zhengzhou University. 52 participants (mean age 26.1y) randomly assigned to audiovisual + weekly reminder (n=?) or verbal instructions alone (n=?). Hawley retainers equipped with TheraMon microsensors. Wear time measured at 3 months (T1) and 6 months (T2). Gingival index, plaque index, and patient experience assessed at T2. Trial registration: TCTR20230220002.

## Results

- Mean wear at T1 (3mo): 14.9 ± 4.9 h/day
- Mean wear at T2 (6mo): 14.3 ± 5.4 h/day
- 3mo compliance: NS between groups (p=0.065)
- 6mo compliance: audiovisual significantly better (p=0.033)
- Gingival index: NS (p=0.165)
- Plaque index: NS (p=0.173)
- Satisfaction with instruction delivery: audiovisual higher
