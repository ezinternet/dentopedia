## Three-line Summary

This cross-sectional study (n=41 untreated adolescents, UMGS archive) measured maxillary second molar crown angulations digitally with 3D Slicer and compared them to Andrews' historical manual measurements. Fully erupted upper second molars showed significantly more distal crown tip (−18.9° vs +0.4°, P<0.0001) and more lingual inclination (−10.6° vs −8.0°, P=0.0118) than Andrews' values. These findings challenge Andrews' mesial-tip observation and suggest the tip prescription for preadjusted brackets at the maxillary second molar may require revision.

## 세줄요약

디지털 치과 모형(3D Slicer)으로 정상 교합 청소년 41명(UMGS)의 상악 제2대구치 치관 경사를 측정하고 Andrews 표본의 수작업 계측값과 비교하였다. 근원심 경사(팁): UMGS −18.9°(원심 경사) vs Andrews +0.4°(근심 경사)(P<0.0001), 협설 경사(토크): UMGS −10.6° vs Andrews −8.0°(P=0.0118)로 유의한 차이가 확인되었다. Andrews의 관찰과 반대되는 원심 경사가 존재함을 근거로, 기성 브래킷 처방 (Preadjusted Appliance Prescription)의 제2대구치 팁 값 재검토를 제안하였다.

## Study Design

- **Design:** Cross-sectional; two-sample comparison
- **Sample 1 (UMGS):** 41 subjects (16 F, 25 M), mean age 14.9 ± 1.3 years; digital dental casts from the University of Michigan Growth Study with fully erupted second molars in occlusion
- **Sample 2 (Andrews):** Historical untreated Class I subjects with normal overbite/overjet (Andrews' landmark Six Keys dataset)
- **Measurement tool:** 3D Slicer (open-source software) for UMGS; manual measurements for Andrews' sample
- **Statistics:** Two mixed effect models (sagittal and transversal inclinations); random effect = subjects; fixed effects = group, arch side (R/L), group×side interaction

## Key Findings

### Crown Angulation (Mesiodistal Tip)

| Sample | Mean tip | Direction | P vs Andrews |
|---|---|---|---|
| UMGS (n=41) | −18.9° | **Distal** crown tip | <0.0001 |
| Andrews | +0.4° | Mesial crown tip | — |

The UMGS second molars are tipped significantly more distally than Andrews' sample. Critically, the direction is **reversed**: UMGS shows distal tip where Andrews found slight mesial tip.

### Crown Inclination (Transversal / Labiolingual Torque)

| Sample | Mean inclination | P vs Andrews |
|---|---|---|
| UMGS (n=41) | −10.6° (lingual) | 0.0118 |
| Andrews | −8.0° (lingual) | — |

Both samples show lingual crown inclination, but UMGS second molars are significantly more lingually inclined.

### No Significant Side (Right vs Left) Interaction

The group×side interaction was not a significant modifier, indicating the angulation differences are symmetric.

## Clinical Significance

Preadjusted appliance (straight-wire) brackets encode tip and torque prescriptions based on Andrews' Six Keys measurements from the 1970s. This study's 3D digital data from a contemporary growth study sample shows that:

1. **Tip prescription** for the maxillary second molar in current bracket systems (+0.4° based on Andrews) may substantially underestimate the distal crown tipping present in normally occluded adolescents.
2. The **magnitude** of the discrepancy (∼19° difference in tip) is clinically large.
3. **Torque prescription** also differs, though by a smaller margin (∼2.6°).

These findings imply that if brackets are programmed to Andrews' values, the upper second molar may need additional first-order (tip) adjustment during treatment to achieve a naturally occurring final position.

## Limitations

- Abstract-only; methodological details (e.g., landmark definitions, inter-rater reliability, blinding) not assessable.
- UMGS sample is adolescent (14.9 y); Andrews' sample age and sex distribution not fully comparable from abstract alone.
- Andrews' measurements were performed manually; digital vs manual methodology difference may contribute to discrepancy.
- Generalizability across ethnicities unclear (UMGS is predominantly North American/Caucasian).
