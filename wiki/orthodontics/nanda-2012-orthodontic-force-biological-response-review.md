## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of the biological response of the PDL and alveolar bone to orthodontic force, integrating neurotransmitter, arachidonic-acid-metabolite, cytokine, and enzymatic mediators into a unified framework.

Substance P, CGRP, and VIP released from sensory nerve endings, PGE₂ and leukotrienes from arachidonic acid metabolism, and cathepsin K and MMPs for collagen matrix degradation all act in concert to drive PDL remodeling; force magnitude determines the response trajectory.

The optimal orthodontic force concept is framed as a range: insufficient force yields no movement; excessive force produces necrosis and hyalinization; the clinical goal is a sustained, productive remodeling zone between these extremes.

## 세줄요약

신경전달물질·아라키돈산 대사물·사이토카인·효소를 통합한 교정력에 대한 PDL·치조골 생물학적 반응 narrative review.

Substance P·CGRP·VIP(신경), PGE₂·류코트리엔(아라키돈산), Cathepsin K·MMP(콜라겐 분해)가 협동하여 PDL 개조를 구동; 힘의 크기가 반응 경로를 결정.

최적 교정력의 범위: 부족하면 이동 없음, 과다하면 괴사·초자화; 임상 목표는 두 극단 사이의 지속적·생산적 개조 구역을 유지하는 것.

## Summary
Concise review of neurotransmitters, arachidonic acid metabolites, cytokines, and enzymes mediating PDL and alveolar bone remodeling under orthodontic force.

## Key Contributions
- Neurotransmitters: Substance P, CGRP, VIP released from sensory nerve endings
- Arachidonic acid metabolites: PGE₂, leukotrienes stimulate osteoclastogenesis
- Cathepsin K and MMPs essential for collagen matrix degradation

## Methodology
Narrative review.

## Results
Optimal orthodontic force range concept: insufficient force → no movement; excessive → necrosis/hyalinization. Multiple mediators act in concert for PDL remodeling.
