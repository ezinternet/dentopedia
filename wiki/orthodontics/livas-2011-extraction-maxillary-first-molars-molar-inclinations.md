## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cephalometric study of 37 Class II Division 1 patients treated with maxillary first molar extraction + Begg technique versus 54 archival untreated controls (Nittedal Growth Material), followed ≥3.6–3.7 years, measuring M2 and M3 inclinations relative to palatal plane (PP) and functional occlusal plane (FOP).

Extraction produced dramatic M2 uprighting (M2-PP: 26.7° → 6.9°) and ~fourfold greater M3 uprighting vs controls (19.9° vs 5.6° relative to FOP), with M2 shifting from distal to mesial inclination (M2-FOP: 14.2° → −6.2°, P < 0.0001).

Extraction of maxillary first molars significantly uprights M2 and M3 and facilitates normal third molar eruption in Class II Division 1 patients, providing cephalometric support for this extraction protocol.

## 세줄요약

Class II Division 1 부정교합 환자 37명(평균 연령 13.2세)을 상악 제1대구치 발치 + Begg 기법으로 치료 후 비치료 대조군 54명(Nittedal 성장자료)과 측방두부계측방사선(Lateral Cephalometric Radiograph)으로 비교한 후향적 연구.

발치군은 제2대구치 (Second Molar, M2)의 구개평면 (Palatal Plane, PP) 대비 기울기가 26.7°→6.9°로 크게 개선됐고, 기능교합평면 (Functional Occlusal Plane, FOP) 기준 제3대구치 (Third Molar, M3) 직립화가 대조군의 약 4배(19.9° vs 5.6°)였으며 M2는 원심경사에서 근심경사로 역전됐다(P < 0.0001).

상악 제1대구치 발치 교정은 Class II Division 1 증례에서 M2·M3 직립화와 제3대구치 정상 맹출을 촉진하며, 이 발치 프로토콜의 교정적 유익성을 지지하는 측방두부계측 근거를 제공한다.

## Summary

This retrospective study quantifies molar inclination changes after orthodontic extraction of maxillary first molars in Class II Division 1 patients. Using standardized lateral cephalometric radiographs, the authors compared 37 extraction-treated patients (Begg technique) against 54 untreated archival controls at baseline (T1) and at least 3.7 years post-treatment (T2). Both groups showed spontaneous improvement in M2 inclination relative to PP, but the extraction group showed substantially greater uprighting and a reversal of M2 from distally inclined to mesially inclined relative to FOP — a change not seen in controls. The most striking finding was M3 uprighting: controls improved ~5.6° relative to FOP through growth, while the extraction group improved ~19.9° — nearly four times as much, with the extraction group achieving final M3 inclinations that 57% of the control group never reached. This provides cephalometric evidence that the maxillary first molar extraction protocol, beyond correcting Class II sagittal relationships, creates spatial conditions that favor normal M3 eruption.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrated that maxillary first molar extraction produces a direction reversal of M2 inclination relative to FOP (distal → mesial), not merely a reduction in distal tilt
- Quantified M3 uprighting benefit as ~4× greater than normal growth alone (19.9° vs 5.6° relative to FOP)
- Showed the maximum M3 distal inclination (9.4°) achieved by all extraction patients at T2 was only attained by 43% of controls
- Established cephalometric rationale for using first molar extraction as a strategy to preempt M3 impaction in Class II Div 1 cases

## Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Retrospective case-control |
| Experimental group | n = 37 (18M/19F); mean age 13.2 ± 1.62 yr; Class II Div 1; overjet ≥ 4 mm; maxillary first molar extraction + Begg technique |
| Control group | n = 54 (18M/36F); mean age 13.4 ± 1.99 yr; untreated Class I/II; Nittedal Growth Material (Oslo University, Norway) |
| Time points | T1 = start of active treatment; T2 = ≥3.7 yr post-treatment (experimental), ≥3.6 yr follow-up (control) |
| Imaging | Standardized lateral cephalometric radiographs |
| Measurements | M2 and M3 inclination relative to PP and FOP |

## Results

**Palatal Plane (PP) reference:**

| Group | M2-PP T1 (°) | M2-PP T2 (°) | M3-PP T1 (°) | M3-PP T2 (°) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Control | 17.7 ± 5.81 | 11.9 ± 4.61 | 30.1 ± 8.54 | 19.6 ± 9.01 |
| Extraction | 26.7 ± 5.75 | 6.9 ± 6.76 | 32.2 ± 7.90 | 12.8 ± 7.36 |

Both groups showed significant within-group improvement in M2-PP and M3-PP.

**Functional Occlusal Plane (FOP) reference:**

| Measurement | Control | Extraction |
|---|---|---|
| M2-FOP T1 (°) | (not sig. changed at T2) | 14.2 ± 4.62 |
| M2-FOP T2 (°) | — | −6.2 ± 6.10 (P < 0.0001) |
| M3-FOP ΔT2–T1 (°) | 5.6 uprighting | 19.9 uprighting (~4×) |

- Extraction group M2-FOP reversed from distal to mesial inclination (P < 0.0001); no change in controls
- Maximum M3 distal inclination at T2 in extraction group: 9.4° — attained by only 43% of controls
