## One-line Summary

SR (8 studies): lower incisor intrusion with clear aligners achieves 0.4–1.5 mm with 35–65% predictability; 7/8 studies carry serious risk of bias; GRADE certainty is moderate-to-low.

## 한줄요약

## Summary

This systematic review specifically evaluated the predictability of lower incisor intrusion with clear aligners in permanent dentition. Eight clinical studies met eligibility criteria. Mean intrusion values ranged from 0.4 to 1.5 mm with predictability between 35% and 65%. Four key influencing factors were identified: magnitude of planned movement, use of auxiliaries (attachments and elastics), refinement strategies, and patient-specific factors (age and compliance). Substantial heterogeneity in measurement methods (CBCT, cephalometry, digital models) and clinical protocols prevented meta-analysis. ROBINS-I found serious risk of bias in 7/8 studies; GRADE certainty was moderate to low. Long-term outcomes and patient-centered measures were inadequately assessed.

## Key Contributions

- Isolated lower incisor intrusion from overall deep bite correction literature
- Predictability range: 35–65% (substantially below planned correction)
- Identified 4 influencing factors: movement magnitude, auxiliaries, refinement frequency, patient factors
- ROBINS-I: 7/8 studies = serious risk of bias
- GRADE: moderate to low certainty — clinical use requires cautious interpretation

## Methodology

Design: SR (PRISMA). Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Cochrane (5 databases). Population: permanent dentition, clear aligner lower incisor intrusion. 8 clinical studies. Risk of bias: ROBINS-I. Certainty: GRADE. Outcomes: achieved vs planned intrusion (mm), predictability (%), influencing factors, auxiliaries used.

## Results

- Mean intrusion range: 0.4–1.5 mm
- Predictability range: 35–65%
- Risk of bias: 7/8 studies serious (ROBINS-I)
- GRADE: moderate to low certainty
- Key moderators:
  - Larger planned movement = lower predictability
  - Attachments + elastics = better outcomes
  - Refinement frequency affects final result
  - Age and compliance = patient-specific predictors
