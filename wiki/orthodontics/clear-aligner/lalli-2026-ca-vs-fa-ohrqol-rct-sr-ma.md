## One-line Summary

RCT-only SR+MA (2 trials, n=74) comparing CA vs FA on OHIP-14: CA significantly better in psychological discomfort (WMD −1.28, p=0.007) and psychological disability (WMD −1.63, p<0.001); no significant difference in physical, functional, or overall OHRQoL; TSA confirms preliminary signal for psychological domains and futility for handicap.

## 한줄요약

## Summary

This PRISMA-compliant, PROSPERO-registered (CRD420251051003) systematic review and meta-analysis is the first to restrict inclusion to randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and apply Trial Sequential Analysis when comparing CA and FA effects on oral health-related quality of life (OHRQoL). Two RCTs (Borsato et al., Alhafi et al.; total n=74; Angle Class I malocclusion, non-extraction, permanent dentition) were identified from a search of five major databases up to March 2026. Both appliance systems caused a transient early deterioration in OHIP-14 scores at ~1 month post-placement, with subsequent recovery. Pooled random-effects meta-analysis found statistically significant advantages for CA only in the psychological discomfort (WMD −1.28; 95% CI: −2.21 to −0.35; p=0.007; I²=0%) and psychological disability (WMD −1.63; 95% CI: −2.51 to −0.74; p<0.001; I²=0%) domains, while no significant differences emerged for functional limitation, physical pain, physical disability, social disability, handicap, or overall OHIP-14 score. TSA supported the preliminary signals for both psychological domains (Z-curve crossed O'Brien–Fleming monitoring boundary) but confirmed that most other domains remain underpowered; for handicap, TSA crossed the futility boundary, suggesting that a clinically relevant difference is unlikely to emerge. GRADE certainty ranged from low to moderate across all outcomes.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA with pooled OHIP-14 domain WMDs restricted to RCTs comparing CA vs FA in Angle Class I patients
- Applied TSA to CA vs FA OHRQoL literature for the first time — adjudicating reliability and statistical futility per domain
- Demonstrated that CA advantage over FA is domain-specific (psychological only), not global — important for patient counseling
- TSA futility boundary for handicap domain: no clinically meaningful CA advantage is expected in that domain
- GRADE low-to-moderate for all outcomes underscores the pressing need for larger, standardized RCTs with long-term follow-up

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR+MA; PRISMA-compliant; PROSPERO CRD420251051003
- **Search**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL; up to 2026-03-15; English only; no date restriction
- **Included studies**: 2 RCTs; pooled n=74 (37 CA, 37 FA); mean age 16.56–29.25 years; mild-moderate Class I crowding >4mm
- **Inclusions**: RCTs; Angle Class I; permanent dentition; non-extraction; no surgery or TADs; OHIP-14 quantitative data available for longitudinal comparison
- **Exclusion**: Non-RCTs; Class II/III; extraction cases; lingual/ceramic appliances; systemic disease; mixed/deciduous dentition
- **Primary outcome**: OHIP-14 change from T0 to T1 (~1 month); 7 domain scores + overall score
- **Statistics**: Random-effects WMD; I²; Cochran Q; Trial Sequential Analysis (type I error 5%, power 80%, O'Brien–Fleming boundaries); R metafor; SD imputed via Wan et al. + correlation r=0.488
- **RoB**: Cochrane RoB 2; Borsato = low risk; Alhafi = some concerns; GRADE = low to moderate
- **Inter-rater**: Cohen's kappa = 0.66 (substantial agreement; n=4 reviewers; 5th for ties)

### Qualitative trajectories

Both groups showed peak OHIP-14 deterioration at T1 (~1 month) followed by progressive recovery. CA group consistently showed lower or similar scores across time points in most domains. Overall OHIP-14 trajectories by T4 were comparable between groups.

## Three-line Summary

- RCT-only SR+MA (2 trials, n=74; PROSPERO CRD420251051003; 5 databases to March 2026) comparing clear aligners versus fixed appliances on OHIP-14 domains in Angle Class I non-extraction patients, applying Trial Sequential Analysis.
- Clear aligners were significantly better only in psychological discomfort (WMD −1.28; p=0.007; I²=0%) and psychological disability (WMD −1.63; p<0.001; I²=0%); no significant difference in physical pain, functional limitation, or overall OHIP-14; TSA crossed the futility boundary for the handicap domain; GRADE low to moderate.
- Clear aligner OHRQoL advantage is domain-specific (psychological only) and early rather than global; patient counseling should emphasize esthetic-related psychological comfort; large standardized RCTs with long-term follow-up are needed.

- CA가 심리적 불편 (Psychological Discomfort) WMD −1.28 (p=0.007; I²=0%), 심리적 장애 (Psychological Disability) WMD −1.63 (p<0.001; I²=0%)에서만 유의 우위; 신체 통증·기능·전반 OHRQoL 차이 없음; TSA 핸디캡 도메인 무용 경계 (Futility Boundary) 통과; GRADE 저-중등도.
- CA OHRQoL 이점은 심리 영역 한정·단기 효과이며 전체적 우위 아님; 환자 상담 시 심미 관련 심리 안정감 중심 설명; 장기 추적 대규모 RCT 필요.
