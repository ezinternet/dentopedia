## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of 140 studies (inception to December 2025) synthesizing OHRQoL across all orthodontic treatment types via a 3-phase trajectory model. Clear aligners outperform fixed appliances on OHRQoL in 65–75% of comparative studies; pain peaks at 24–48h after each aligner change and resolves in 4–7 days. Overall OHRQoL decline during aligner treatment is only 8–12%.

## 세줄요약

140편 내러티브 리뷰: 초기적응·안정화·종료/유지 3단계 OHRQoL 궤적 모델 제시. 비교연구 65–75%에서 CA 우수; 통증 24–48h 피크 후 4–7일 소실. CA 치료 중 OHRQoL 저하 8–12%로 미미.

## Background

Orthodontic treatment invariably affects patients' daily lives — in speech, diet, social interactions, and comfort — but the trajectory and magnitude of these effects across treatment phases and appliance types lacked comprehensive synthesis. This review addressed that gap with the broadest temporal scope available (inception to December 2025).

## Methods

Narrative review. 140 studies included. No formal systematic search or PROSPERO registration. Synthesis organized by treatment phase (initial/adaptation, stabilization, completion/retention) and outcome domain (pain, aesthetics, speech, social, functional). No formal risk-of-bias assessment. Publication bias not assessed.

## Results

### 3-Phase OHRQoL Trajectory

| Phase | Characteristics | CA vs FA |
|---|---|---|
| Initial adaptation | Highest OHRQoL impact; driven by pain | CA significantly less impact |
| Stabilization | Gradual improvement; adaptation sets in | CA advantage maintained |
| Completion/retention | Near-baseline or improved | Converge — both recover |

### Key Effect Estimates

| Outcome | CA | FA | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain peak timing | 24–48h post-change | Immediately on activation | 87% of studies |
| Pain duration | Resolves 4–7 days | Persists longer | 87% of studies |
| OHRQoL comparative advantage | 65–75% of studies favor CA | — | Meta-narrative |
| OHRQoL decline during Tx | 8–12% | Higher | — |
| Speech impact | Transient (days–weeks) | Less initial | — |

## Clinical Takeaway

Pre-emptive analgesia timed to aligner changes (24–48h window) is the highest-leverage pain intervention. Counseling patients that OHRQoL will follow a predictable 3-phase trajectory — initial discomfort → improvement → recovery — can improve acceptance and compliance. The 8–12% OHRQoL decline figure is a useful, patient-friendly benchmark.
