## Three-line Summary

Retrospective clinical study, n=50 (25 clear aligners vs 25 fixed braces), mild-to-moderate Angle's Class III malocclusion (ANB -4° to 0°), no prior orthodontic treatment or craniofacial syndrome.

Aligners showed shorter treatment duration (18±3 vs 22±4 months, p<0.05) and higher compliance (92% vs 75%, p<0.05); overjet correction was comparable (3.2±0.5 vs 3.8±0.4 mm, p>0.05); but braces achieved greater skeletal (ANB) improvement (1.5±0.5° vs 0.8±0.3°, p<0.05) and lower refinement need (5% vs 10%).

Clear aligners are a viable dental-camouflage option for mild-to-moderate Class III cases favoring esthetics/compliance/speed, but fixed braces remain preferred when skeletal correction is the priority; retrospective, single-center, small (n=25/arm), excludes surgical-severity Class III.

## 세줄요약

후향적 임상연구, n=50 (투명교정 25명 vs 고정식 장치 25명), 경중등도 Angle's Class III 부정교합(ANB -4°~0°), 이전 교정치료·두개안면 증후군 없음.

투명교정(Clear Aligner, CA)이 치료기간 단축(18±3 vs 22±4개월, p<0.05)과 순응도 우위(92% vs 75%, p<0.05)를 보였고, 전치돌출(overjet) 개선은 유사(3.2±0.5 vs 3.8±0.4mm, p>0.05)했으나, 고정식 장치(Fixed Appliance, FA)가 골격적(ANB) 개선(1.5±0.5° vs 0.8±0.3°, p<0.05)이 더 크고 재조정 필요율도 낮았다(5% vs 10%).

투명교정은 심미·순응도·속도를 중시하는 경중등도 Class III 치성 보정에 실행 가능한 대안이나, 골격 교정이 우선인 경우 고정식 장치가 여전히 선호되며, 후향적·단일기관·소규모(군당 n=25)·수술 대상 중증 Class III 배제라는 한계가 있다.

## Summary

This retrospective study compared clear aligners (CA, n=25) against fixed multibracket braces (FA, n=25) in 50 patients with mild-to-moderate Angle's Class III malocclusion (ANB -4° to 0°), excluding cases requiring orthognathic surgery. Aligners achieved faster treatment (18±3 vs 22±4 months) and higher patient compliance (92% vs 75%), with comparable overjet correction (3.2 vs 3.8 mm, p>0.05). However, braces produced significantly greater skeletal (ANB) correction (1.5° vs 0.8°, p<0.05) and a lower need for post-treatment refinement (5% vs 10%). The authors conclude aligners are effective for dental-level correction and patient-centered outcomes in mild-moderate Class III, while braces remain the preferred modality when skeletal modification is the treatment goal or severity increases.

## Key Contributions

- First study in this wiki to directly compare CA vs FA clinical outcomes within a Class III-specific cohort (rather than a mixed-malocclusion sample that excludes or under-represents Class III, as in [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/baneshi-2024-clear-aligners-malocclusion-sr-ma]]).
- Quantifies a differentiated trade-off for Class III correction: CA wins on speed/compliance, ties on dental correction, loses on skeletal correction and refinement rate — a more granular picture than the case-report-only evidence previously available.
- Supports mechanistic reasoning (continuous vs intermittent force delivery) as the likely driver of the FA skeletal advantage, consistent with orthodontic biomechanics literature cited elsewhere in the wiki.

## Methodology

Retrospective chart review of 50 patients with mild-to-moderate skeletal Class III (ANB -4° to 0°), no prior orthodontic treatment, good periodontal health, no craniofacial syndrome; severe skeletal Class III requiring orthognathic surgery was excluded. Group 1 (n=25): clear aligners. Group 2 (n=25): fixed braces. Pre-/post-treatment lateral cephalograms and dental models assessed overjet/overbite change and ANB angle change; compliance was self-reported wear-time (aligners only); post-treatment stability tracked over 12 months. Outcomes compared with t-tests.
