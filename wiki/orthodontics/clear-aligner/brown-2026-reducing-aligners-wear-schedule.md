## Three-line Summary

A 2-arm single-blinded RCT (n=38 completers from 45 randomized; Oct 2023–Mar 2025) compared 3-day aligner change protocol (0.11 mm/1° per tray) vs conventional 7-day protocol (0.25 mm/2° per tray) for mandibular incisor movement in Class I crowding. Linear deviations were clinically equivalent (both within ABO 0.5 mm threshold); angular deviations on teeth 31/41 reached statistical significance but remained under the 2° clinical threshold. The 3-day group required significantly more refinements and clinical visits with no gain in predictability, treatment duration, or occlusal outcomes.

## Background

Clear aligner therapy (투명교정 치료, CAT) typically employs a 7- to 14-day change interval with activation of approximately 0.25 mm and 2° per tray. Reducing the change interval to 3 days (with proportionally reduced activation) has been proposed as a strategy to reduce the total number of trays, potentially decreasing overall treatment cost and laboratory fees. However, the impact of this interval reduction on tooth movement predictability (치아이동 예측도) and treatment efficiency had not been tested in a prospective RCT prior to this study.

## Methods

- **Design**: Two-arm, parallel-group, single-blinded, single-center prospective RCT
- **Period**: October 2023 – March 2025; **Setting**: University orthodontic clinic
- **Randomization**: Fixed block randomization; concealed sequentially numbered envelopes
- **Blinding**: Outcome assessors blinded to group allocation
- **Population**: Adults 18–30 years, Class I malocclusion, moderate-to-severe mandibular anterior crowding (n=45 randomized; n=38 completers; 7 excluded for non-compliance)

### Protocols

| Parameter | 3-day protocol | 7-day protocol |
|---|---|---|
| Change interval | 3 days | 7 days |
| Linear activation per tray | 0.11 mm | 0.25 mm |
| Angular activation per tray | 1° | 2° |

## Primary Outcome

Deviation between predicted (계획된 치아이동) and achieved mandibular incisor tooth movement after the first aligner series — assessed across 3 linear and 3 angular dimensions.

## Results

### Linear deviations (선형 이동 편차)

- Comparable between 3-day and 7-day groups
- Both within ABO clinical threshold of **0.5 mm** — clinically equivalent

### Angular deviations (각도 이동 편차)

| Tooth | Outcome | p-value | Exceeds 2° threshold? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 31 | Statistically significant deviation | 0.040 | No |
| 41 | Statistically significant deviation | 0.003 | No |
| 41 (buccolingual inclination) | Statistically significant | 0.005 | No |

### Clinical efficiency

- **Refinements**: 3-day group required significantly more refinement stages
- **Clinical visits**: 3-day group required significantly more visits
- **Overall treatment duration**: No significant difference between groups
- **Final occlusal outcomes**: No significant difference
- **Adverse effects**: None reported

## Discussion

The 3-day protocol produced per-tray activation of 0.11 mm/1° — approximately 44% of the 7-day protocol. Despite this reduction, the cumulative biological response over matched treatment periods yielded comparable final outcomes. The requirement for more refinements in the 3-day group implies that per-tray under-activation led to incomplete tooth movement expression, necessitating correction stages that offset any efficiency gain. The finding corroborates the conclusion of [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/albaqshi-2025-invisalign-predictability-factors-compliance-sr]] that inadequate wear time is the leading cause of suboptimal outcomes — here, reduced activation per interval appears to function analogously.

## Limitations

- Abstract-only; sample size n=38 completers is modest
- Single-center design limits generalizability
- ClinicalTrials.gov registration number not disclosed in abstract
- Restricted to mandibular anterior teeth and Class I crowding; findings may not generalize to posterior segments, Class II/III malocclusions, or different tooth types
- Drop-out rate: 7/45 (15.6%) all due to non-compliance — introduces selection bias favoring compliant patients

## Clinical Takeaway

A 3-day clear aligner change interval does not improve tooth movement predictability (치아이동 예측도) and significantly increases refinement burden and clinical visits. The conventional 7-day protocol (or longer) is preferred for efficiency. Wear duration (마모 시간)—not merely tray count—is a critical determinant of aligner effectiveness.
