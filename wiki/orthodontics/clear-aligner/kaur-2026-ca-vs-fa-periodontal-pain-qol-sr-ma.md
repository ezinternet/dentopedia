## One-line Summary

SR+MA of 32 studies (28 pooled) finding clear aligners superior to fixed appliances across all five domains: PI MD −0.437, GI MD −0.233, PD MD −0.332, pain SMD −0.419, and QoL SMD −0.970 at end of treatment, with GRADE moderate-to-high certainty.

## Summary

This PRISMA-registered SR+MA searched five major databases through March 2025, including 32 studies (17 RCTs, 15 observational; total sample sizes 28–145 per study) comparing clear aligner therapy (CA) to fixed appliances (FA). Periodontal indices — plaque index (PI), gingival index (GI), and probing depth (PD) — were pooled by mean difference (MD); pain intensity and quality of life (QoL) were pooled by standardized mean difference (SMD) to accommodate heterogeneous instruments. CA showed statistically significant advantages on all five outcome domains; GRADE certainty was high for GI and PD, and moderate for PI, pain, and QoL. Temporal subgroup analysis reveals that QoL advantage diminishes over time and is not statistically significant at 12 months (SMD −0.531, p=0.108), while periodontal benefits show zero heterogeneity (I²=0% for GI and PD). Publication bias was detected for pain and PI (Egger significant), suggesting possible overestimation; QoL and periodontal indices were unaffected. The authors emphasize that statistically significant PD and GI reductions are modest in absolute terms and may lack individual-level clinical significance.

## Key Contributions

- Largest multidomain meta-analysis simultaneously quantifying PI, GI, PD, pain, and QoL under a single PRISMA/GRADE framework
- Demonstrates that CA periodontal advantage (GI, PD) is highly consistent across studies (I²=0%) — the most robust finding in the paper
- Temporal stratification (1 week, 10 days, 1 month, 6 months, 12 months) reveals peak QoL benefit in the first month that fades but persists through end of treatment
- Identifies publication bias as a caveat for pain and PI estimates, while confirming QoL estimate robustness (fail-safe N=898, no funnel asymmetry)
- Integrates GRADE profiles enabling clinicians to weigh certainty level alongside effect size

## Methodology

- Design: SR + meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020); INPLASY 202530100
- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane Library, Embase, ScienceDirect + ProQuest gray literature + hand search of AJO-DO, EJO, Angle Orthodontist
- Date range: inception to March 2025
- Population: orthodontic patients, any age/sex; exclusions: severe periodontal disease, craniofacial anomalies, orthognathic surgery
- Study designs: RCTs (n=17) and observational comparative studies (n=15 — cohort, case-control)
- Risk of bias: RoB 2 (RCTs), ROBINS-I (observational); overall mostly low-to-moderate
- Statistics: Comprehensive Meta-Analysis v3.7; random-effects models; heterogeneity by I², Q-statistic, τ²; subgroups by follow-up duration and study design
- Publication bias: funnel plots + Egger regression + Begg rank correlation + fail-safe N + Duval-Tweedie trim-and-fill

## Results

**Periodontal outcomes (CA vs FA; negative values favor CA):**

| Outcome | MD (95% CI) | p | I² |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plaque Index (PI) | −0.437 (−0.507 to −0.367) | <0.001 | 39.88% non-RCT; 0% RCT |
| Gingival Index (GI) | −0.233 (−0.296 to −0.170) | <0.001 | 0% |
| Probing Depth (PD) | −0.332 (−0.431 to −0.234) | <0.001 | 0% |

**Pain (SMD; negative values favor CA):**
- Overall: SMD = −0.419 (p<0.001); I² = 23.5% (low-to-moderate)
- Greatest effect early: 1-week I²=42.1%, 2-week I²=69.9%

**Quality of Life (SMD; negative values favor CA):**
- 1 week: SMD = −0.985 (p<0.001); I² = 74%
- 10 days: SMD = −0.895 (p<0.001)
- 1 month: SMD = −0.829 (p<0.001)
- End of treatment: SMD = −0.970 (95% CI −1.410 to −0.520, p<0.001)
- 12 months: SMD = −0.531 (95% CI −1.178 to 0.116, p=0.108) — NOT significant

**Clinical magnitude caution:**
- PD reduction 0.332 mm is well below periodontal thresholds; population-level trend not individual benefit
- GI reduction ≈8% on 0–3 scale; no established minimal clinically important difference in orthodontic context
- PI reduction ≈15%; possibly confounded by study-specific oral hygiene instructions
- QoL at 1 week (SMD −0.985) = large effect, clinically meaningful for treatment adherence

**GRADE certainty:** High for GI, PD; Moderate for PI, pain, QoL
