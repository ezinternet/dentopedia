## One-line Summary

SR+MA (6 CBCT-exclusive comparative studies; 392 patients) — clear aligner therapy is associated with significantly less external apical root resorption than fixed appliances (MD = −0.50 mm; 95% CI −0.79 to −0.21; p<0.001; I²=60.8%; GRADE low certainty).

## Summary

This PROSPERO-registered SR+MA (CRD420261320269) addressed a methodological gap in the EARR literature by restricting inclusion exclusively to studies using quantitative CBCT-based linear root length measurements (mm) when comparing clear aligner therapy (CA) versus fixed orthodontic appliances (FA). Six eligible studies comprising 392 patients met the stringent imaging inclusion criterion. Random-effects meta-analysis using the DerSimonian–Laird estimator yielded a pooled MD of −0.50 mm (95% CI −0.79 to −0.21; p<0.001) favoring CA, with moderate heterogeneity (I²=60.8%). Pre-specified subgroup analysis by extraction protocol was limited by a single extraction study (MD −0.95 mm, descriptive only); the non-extraction/mixed subgroup (k=5) showed MD −0.41 mm remaining significant. Comprehensive sensitivity analyses confirmed effect stability across leave-one-out permutations (range −0.41 to −0.60 mm). GRADE certainty was rated low due to non-randomized study designs, residual confounding from case complexity differences, and the small number of eligible studies. The biomechanical rationale — staged intermittent aligner forces vs continuous fixed-appliance force systems reducing cumulative PDL stress and hyalinization — is biologically consistent with the observed direction of effect.

## Key Contributions

- Resolves the imaging-heterogeneity problem of prior EARR reviews by admitting only CBCT-based linear measurements, eliminating 2D projection error artifacts from the pooled estimate.
- Provides the first SR+MA-level evidence of a statistically significant CA-vs-FA EARR advantage using a homogeneous measurement standard (MD −0.50 mm, p<0.001).
- Extraction-protocol subgroup signal (MD −0.95 mm, single study) is hypothesis-generating for higher biomechanical-risk protocols, pending confirmatory multi-study evidence.
- Leave-one-out stability (−0.41 to −0.60 mm) demonstrates that the pooled direction is not driven by any single dominant study.
- Contextualizes the apparent contradiction with Bespalez-Neto 2026 RCT (no CA-vs-FA difference): heterogeneous case selection, smaller RCT power, and different measurement methods (AI-3D volumetric surface vs. linear CBCT landmark) account for the discordance rather than true biological contradiction.

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR+MA; PROSPERO CRD420261320269; PRISMA 2020
- **Search**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, CENTRAL, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP, Google Scholar; inception to January 2026; English-only
- **PICO**: P = orthodontic patients; I = clear aligner (thermoplastic); C = fixed appliances; O = EARR by CBCT linear root length change (mm)
- **Exclusion triggers**: non-CBCT imaging, 2D radiographs, no FA comparator, non-extractable data, pooled statistics unavailable, review articles
- **Studies included**: k=6; n=392; mix of extraction, non-extraction, and mixed protocols; ROBINS-I risk of bias (no RCTs)
- **Statistical model**: DerSimonian–Laird random-effects; MD = CA − FA; Cochran Q + I² heterogeneity; subgroup by extraction protocol; leave-one-out; Egger test (exploratory, underpowered)
- **GRADE**: Low (non-randomized, residual confounding, moderate heterogeneity, small k)

## Results

**Primary outcome — CBCT linear EARR, CA vs FA:**
- **Pooled MD = −0.50 mm** (95% CI −0.79 to −0.21; p<0.001)
- I² = 60.8% (moderate); direction favored CA in 5/6 studies; one study near-null

**Subgroup — Extraction protocol:**
- Non-extraction/mixed (k=5): MD = −0.41 mm (95% CI −0.69 to −0.13; I²=52.5%)
- Extraction (k=1, descriptive): MD = −0.95 mm (95% CI −1.42 to −0.48) — no pooled inference

**Sensitivity:**
- Excluding serious-risk study: MD = −0.53 mm (95% CI −0.81 to −0.25; I²=55%)
- Excluding extraction study: MD = −0.41 mm (95% CI −0.69 to −0.13; I²=52.5%)
- Leave-one-out range: −0.41 to −0.60 mm — all statistically significant

**Reporting bias**: Egger p=0.28, non-significant; funnel plot exploratory (k<10 underpowered)

**GRADE**: Low certainty — results are exploratory and hypothesis-supporting, not clinically definitive
