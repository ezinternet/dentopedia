## Three-line Summary

Scoping review of 37 studies (5 databases, adults ≥18y) mapping the patient experience evidence base for clear aligner treatment. Pain/OHRQOL were most studied; only 36% of patients achieved full compliance (≥22h/day). High aesthetic satisfaction coexisted with compliance struggles.

## 세줄요약

37편 범위검토(성인 ≥18세): 통증·OHRQoL이 주요 연구 영역; 완전 순응(≥22h/일) 36%에 불과. 심미 만족도는 높으나 순응도와 독립. 장기(>18개월) PRO 데이터·복잡 보조기구 사용 순응도 연구 공백.

## Background

Despite the rapid adoption of clear aligner therapy in adult orthodontics, the breadth of patient experience research — spanning pain, quality of life, aesthetics, speech, and compliance — had not been systematically mapped. This scoping review provides the first comprehensive landscape of what is known and where evidence gaps remain.

## Methods

Scoping review following Arksey and O'Malley framework. Five databases searched (dates not specified). Inclusion: adults ≥18y, clear aligner treatment, PRO outcomes (pain, OHRQOL, aesthetics, speech, compliance/satisfaction). 37 studies included. Narrative synthesis. No formal risk-of-bias tool (scoping design). No PROSPERO registration.

## Results

### Evidence Coverage by Domain

| Domain | Coverage | Evidence quality |
|---|---|---|
| Pain/discomfort | High (most studied) | Moderate — short-term studies dominate |
| OHRQoL | High | Moderate |
| Aesthetics/psychosocial | Moderate | Moderate |
| Speech | Low-moderate | Low (mostly descriptive) |
| Compliance/satisfaction | Moderate | Low (heterogeneous definitions) |

### Key Quantitative Findings

- Full compliance (≥22h/day): **36%** of patients across studies
- Aesthetic satisfaction: consistently high — motivation driver
- Speech: transient impact; resolves within weeks as tongue adapts
- Compliance predictors: younger age, higher education, shorter treatment, higher initial motivation

### Evidence Gaps Identified

- Long-term PRO data beyond 18 months: absent
- Compliance with attachments and elastics (complex mechanics): understudied
- Economic impact and treatment burden: minimal evidence
- Subgroup analyses by malocclusion complexity: limited

## Clinical Takeaway

The 36% full-compliance rate is a clinically important benchmark — well below the assumed ≥22h/day threshold and the 21.8h/day self-report in Patil 2026 (likely over-reporting). Compliance monitoring strategies (temperature-sensitive indicators, SmartTrack material tracking) and targeted counseling of compliance predictors at treatment onset are evidence-supported priorities.
