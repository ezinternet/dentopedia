## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated the predictability of tooth movement and clinical effectiveness of clear aligners in premolar extraction cases. Twenty studies (536 aligner, 173 fixed appliance patients) were included from 7 databases. Meta-analyses demonstrated significant biomechanical limitations: excessive mesial tipping of maxillary first molars (MD −6.08°), under-retraction of maxillary incisors (−1.93 mm), increased vertical displacement, root divergence, and prevalent anchorage loss. Compared to fixed appliances, clear aligners showed inferior root control and ABO-OGS scores. While 4 RCTs provided high-quality evidence, the literature remains predominantly retrospective with moderate overall risk of bias. Strategic overcorrections and adjunctive mechanics are recommended for extraction-based clear aligner treatment.

## Key Contributions

- Meta-analysis quantified maxillary 1st molar mesial tipping in extraction cases: MD −6.08° (CI: −7.89 to −4.26)
- Meta-analysis quantified maxillary incisor under-retraction: −1.93 mm (CI: −2.15 to −1.71)
- Root divergence and anchorage loss established as prevalent structural complications
- Clear aligners inferior to fixed for ABO-OGS and occlusal contact scores
- Overcorrection and adjunctive mechanics identified as necessary clinical adaptations

## Methodology

Design: SR+MA (PRISMA). 7 databases to April 2025. Inclusion: ≥12 years, premolar extractions, clear aligner treatment. 20 studies (n=536 aligner, n=173 fixed). Primary outcomes: predicted vs achieved tooth movement, root angulation/parallelism. Secondary: treatment duration, ABO-OGS, PAR. Risk of bias: SBU + CRD criteria. Analysis: random-effects model for homogeneous outcomes. PROSPERO CRD42024613540.

## Results

- Max 1st molar mesial tipping: MD −6.08° (95% CI: −7.89 to −4.26)
- Max incisor under-retraction: MD −1.93 mm (95% CI: −2.15 to −1.71)
- Increased vertical displacement of maxillary incisors
- Root divergence: prevalent across studies
- Anchorage loss: prevalent across studies
- ABO-OGS and occlusal contacts: inferior for aligners vs fixed (4 RCTs, high-quality)
- Overall RoB: moderate (predominantly retrospective)
