## Three-line Summary

This RCT compared external apical root resorption (EARR) on incisors after non-extraction Class I malocclusion treatment using clear aligners (n=15) vs fixed appliances (n=17) with novel 3D AI-based CBCT surface analysis. Overall median EARR was −0.72 mm with no significant difference between groups; upper lateral incisors showed significantly greater EARR than lower incisors (p=0.002) and upper central incisors (p<0.001). No significant predictor was found for age, sex, crowding severity, or treatment duration.

## Summary

This innovative RCT used AI-based 3D CBCT surface models to quantify external apical root resorption following orthodontic treatment with clear aligners versus fixed appliances. In 32 adult patients with Class I malocclusion and moderate crowding, both appliance types produced comparable amounts of root resorption (median −0.72 mm), with upper lateral incisors being the most susceptible teeth. The novel 3D methodology provides more comprehensive volumetric analysis than traditional 2D radiographic methods, setting a new standard for EARR assessment in orthodontic research.

## Key Contributions

- Novel 3D AI-based CBCT surface analysis methodology for EARR quantification
- First RCT using this technology to compare CA vs fixed appliances
- Upper lateral incisors most susceptible to EARR regardless of appliance type
- No appliance-type, age, sex, or treatment-duration effect on EARR magnitude

## Methodology

Randomized clinical trial at Uniderp and Universidade Federal Fluminense (Brazil) with University of Michigan collaboration. 32 adults (CA: 15, fixed: 17), mean age 22.3y, Class I malocclusion with moderate crowding (Little's Index 4.76 mm). Pre/post-treatment CBCT scans. EARR measured via AI-based 3D surface model analysis. Treatment duration mean 24.2 months.

## Results

- Overall median EARR: −0.72 mm
- CA: −0.71 mm; Fixed: −0.72 mm (NS between groups)
- Upper lateral incisors: greatest EARR (p=0.002 vs lower, p<0.001 vs upper central)
- No significant predictors: age, sex, crowding severity, treatment duration
