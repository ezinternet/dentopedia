## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 투명교정장치 부착체(clear aligner attachment)를 심미 보철물에 접합하는 방법을 다룬 최초의 전용 체계 문헌고찰(SR) — 9편 실험실연구, 4가지 재료 유형 포괄
> - 리튬 디실리케이트(lithium disilicate, LD)와 모놀리식 지르코니아(monolithic zirconia, MZ) 모두에서 불산(hydrofluoric acid, HFA) 에칭 + Filtek Z350 복합레진이 최고 전단결합강도(shear bond strength, SBS)를 산출
> - HFA 최적 농도: 리튬 디실리케이트에서 9.5–9.6%; 3D 프린팅 레진에서 4% (air abrasion이 HFA보다 우세)
> - 모놀리식 지르코니아에서는 HFA ≥ air abrasion > 레이저 순; 실란(silane) + Superbond C&B 조합이 33.25 ± 4.57 MPa로 가장 높은 수치 보고
> - 표면거칠기(surface roughness, Ra) ↑가 결합강도 ↑을 의미하지 않음 — 접착은 다인자(roughness + 표면에너지 + 젖음성 + 화학결합 + 접착제)의 복합결과
> - 유동형 복합레진(flowable composite)은 충전형(packable, Filtek Z350)에 비해 일관되게 낮은 결합강도 — 부착체 제작 시 packable 우선
> - 3D 프린팅 보철물: air abrasion + Single Bond Universal (SBU) = 14.84 ± 2.24 MPa; 비스아크릴 복합재: 비열 플라즈마(non-thermal plasma) 처리가 가장 우수
> - 임상 주의: HFA는 고부식성 — 고무댐(rubber dam) 장벽 필수; 샌드블라스팅이 임상 대안
> - 메타분석 불가: HFA 농도·샌드블라스팅 프로토콜·서모사이클링 조건·접착제 종류 등 이질성 과다 → 서술적 합성만
> - 한계: 장석 도자기(feldspathic porcelain), VITA Enamic, PMMA 등 다른 재료 미포함; 전임상(in vitro)만, 임상 데이터 없음

## Three-line Summary

A PRISMA-compliant SR of 9 in vitro studies (2015–2025) evaluated surface treatments and bonding agents for clear aligner attachments on aesthetic crown materials (lithium disilicate, monolithic zirconia, 3D-printed resin, bis-acryl composite). Hydrofluoric acid (HFA) etching combined with Filtek Z350 composite consistently produced the highest shear bond strength (SBS) on both lithium disilicate and monolithic zirconia across included studies. However, surface roughness did not linearly predict bond strength, and narrative synthesis without meta-analysis was required due to substantial study heterogeneity.

## 세줄요약

투명교정장치 부착체(clear aligner attachment)를 심미 보철물(리튬 디실리케이트·모놀리식 지르코니아·3D 프린팅 레진·비스아크릴)에 접합하는 방법을 다룬 PRISMA 체계 문헌고찰 — 9편 실험실연구(2015–2025). 불산(hydrofluoric acid, HFA) 에칭 + Filtek Z350 복합레진이 리튬 디실리케이트와 모놀리식 지르코니아에서 가장 높은 전단결합강도(shear bond strength, SBS)를 보였으며, 표면거칠기(surface roughness, Ra) 증가가 결합강도 향상을 선형적으로 보장하지 않는다는 점이 강조되었다. 이질성으로 인해 메타분석 불가, 서술적 합성에 그쳤고 모든 근거가 실험실 연구라는 한계가 있다.

## Summary

This SR fills a specific clinical gap: adult clear aligner patients who have aesthetic crown restorations (lithium disilicate, monolithic zirconia) need reliable attachment bonding on crown surfaces — not natural enamel. Nine in vitro studies (2015–2025) were identified across Scopus, PubMed, and Web of Science from 260 initial records. Risk of bias assessed with the QUIN tool: 7 low risk, 2 medium risk.

The primary finding is that hydrofluoric acid etching (1 min, ~9.5–9.6%) combined with Filtek Z350 packable composite resin achieves the highest SBS for both lithium disilicate and monolithic zirconia crown materials. Sandblasting (air abrasion with 50 µm Al2O3) is the preferred clinical alternative because HFA requires strict isolation (rubber dam) due to its highly corrosive nature.

A novel finding underscored across studies: bond strength depends not only on surface roughness but on the interaction between surface topography, surface energy, wettability, and chemical bonding mechanisms. Excessive roughness may create voids and stress concentration sites that paradoxically reduce bond strength.

The 3D-printed crown with prefabricated attachment is identified as a promising multidisciplinary option when both prosthodontic rehabilitation and orthodontic alignment are required — current evidence suggests higher strength than conventional attachment bonding to crowns.

## Key Contributions

1. **First SR** dedicated to clear aligner attachment bonding on aesthetic crown materials (not natural tooth enamel)
2. **HFA protocol hierarchy established**: 9.5–9.6% HFA + Filtek Z350 = optimal for LD and MZ; 4% HFA less effective than AA on 3D-printed resin
3. **Ra ≠ SBS** (non-linear relationship confirmed): adhesion is multifactorial — roughness alone insufficient predictor
4. **Packable > flowable**: Filtek Z350 (packable) consistently outperforms flowable variants for attachment fabrication
5. **Primer selection matters on zirconia**: MDP and Universal primers outperform silane alone; silane + Superbond C&B combination = 33.25 ± 4.57 MPa (highest reported)
6. **3D-printed crown with integrated attachment**: innovative approach with good initial SBS, pending further validation
7. **Meta-analysis precluded**: substantial heterogeneity in HFA concentrations, sandblasting protocols, thermocycling, bonding agents

## Methodology

- **Design**: PRISMA 2020 systematic review
- **PROSPERO**: CRD420251174624
- **Search period**: 2015–2025
- **Databases**: Scopus (n=104), Web of Science (n=79), PubMed (n=77) → 260 total; 120 duplicates removed → 140 screened → 9 included
- **Inclusion**: in vitro studies, clear aligner attachments on aesthetic crown materials, English, full-text available
- **Exclusion**: studies on natural teeth; SRs/MAs; in vivo (human/animal); non-English
- **Screening**: Rayyan software; dual independent (AK + CS); MR = tie-breaker
- **Risk of bias**: QUIN tool; formula = (score/max) × 100; threshold: low (<33%), medium (33–66%), high (>66%) — note: 7/9 low risk, 2/9 medium
- **Synthesis**: Narrative only (heterogeneity precludes meta-analysis)

## Results

### Shear Bond Strength by Crown Material

**Lithium Disilicate:**

| Study | Best Protocol | Best SBS (MPa) | Worst |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alsaud 2022 (n=120) | AA + AUB + Filtek Z350 | 21.80 ± 3.86 | PhA + SBU + Z350 flowable = 0.96 ± 1.86 |
| Fayyadh 2024 (n=80) | HFA + Filtek Z350 | 16.43 ± 2.09 | PhA (4.421 MPa) |

- HFA > AA > Er,Cr:YSGG > phosphoric acid on LD
- Assure Universal Bond (AUB) > Single Bond Universal (SBU) on LD

**Monolithic Zirconia:**

| Study | Best Protocol | Best SBS (MPa) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alhunayni 2025 (n=66) | HFA + SBU + Filtek Z350 | Highest in group | HFA > AA (10.59 ± 2.76) > laser (8.33 ± 1.37) |
| Nitasnoraset 2024 (n=50) | HFA + silane + Superbond C&B | 33.25 ± 4.57 | All HFA-pretreated; bonding alone = 4.55 ± 0.99 |
| Arslan 2023 (n=102) | Filtek Z250 + silane + diamond bur | ~3.19 MPa | All values low; flowable worst (1.58 MPa) |
| Nguyen 2025 (n=108) | AA 30s + Universal/MDP primer | Highest | MDP & Universal > silane at all durations |
| Çokakoğlu 2024 (n=40) | MZ + AA + Monobond Plus | 14.20 ± 6.26 | LD + HFA + Monobond Plus = 6.02 ± 2.19 (lowest) |

**3D-Printed Definitive Resin:**
- Criollo-Barrios 2025 (n=72): AA + Single Bond Universal = 14.84 ± 2.24 MPa (best); AA + TXT = 10.36 ± 1.76 MPa; no treatment = lowest
- HFA produced higher SBS than TXT primer but less than AA

**Bis-Acryl Composite:**
- Shahin 2024 (n=120): Non-thermal plasma treatment (NTPT) > Er:YAG (9.68 ± 2.03 MPa) > sandblasting (9.15 ± 3.29 MPa) > diamond/carbide burs (lowest)

### Surface Roughness (Ra) — 5 of 9 Studies

| Study | Material | Highest Ra treatment | Value (µm) | Lowest Ra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alsaud 2022 | LD | Air abrasion | 1.20 ± 0.30 | No treatment 0.24 ± 0.08 |
| Fayyadh 2024 | LD | Sandblasting | 1.253 ± 0.12 | PhA 0.399 ± 0.08 |
| Alhunayni 2025 | MZ | HFA | 0.82 | Laser 0.63 |
| Nguyen 2025 | Zirconia | Sandblasting 30s | Highest | Sandblasting 10s = lowest |
| Criollo-Barrios 2025 | 3D resin | Air abrasion | 2.21 ± 0.30 | Control 0.07 ± 0.03 |

Key: On LD, air abrasion produces the highest Ra but HFA produces the highest SBS → Ra ≠ SBS predictor.

### Clinical Recommendations (from SR)

1. **LD crowns**: Etch with HFA (9.5–9.6%, 1 min) + Filtek Z350 composite → highest SBS; avoid phosphoric acid
2. **Monolithic zirconia**: HFA + Filtek Z350 or silane + Superbond C&B after HFA; sandblasting = clinical alternative; MDP/Universal primers preferred over silane
3. **3D-printed resin**: Sandblast (50 µm Al2O3) + Single Bond Universal
4. **All protocols**: Use packable composite (Z350) over flowable; isolate well when using HFA (rubber dam)
5. **HFA safety**: Highly corrosive — caution; gingival barrier mandatory
