## Three-line Summary

In vitro color stability comparison of 4 clear aligner brands (Invisalign, ClearCorrect, Beforedent, Käse Aligner; n=704 total, 176/brand, 0.5–0.55 mm thickness) immersed in coffee, tea, cola, and artificial saliva at 6 h and 12 h; FTIR polymer identification and 3D laser surface microscopy performed.

Invisalign exceeded the clinically unacceptable threshold (ΔE*>3.3) in coffee at both 6 h and 12 h; ClearCorrect only at coffee 12 h; Beforedent and Käse Aligner remained within acceptable ΔE* in all beverages; coffee caused greatest staining in all brands; Invisalign's inner surface roughness (Sa=1.97 μm) was significantly higher than all others (p<0.001).

FTIR confirmed Invisalign = aromatic thermoplastic polyurethane (PU), ClearCorrect = polyester outer layer (PETG/PU core/PETG tri-layer ClearQuartz), Beforedent and Käse = PETG; the higher polarity carbamate group (-NHCOO-) of PU systematically drives greater water and pigment absorption vs the ester group (-COO-) of PETG.

## Background

According to PubMed ([DOI 10.1186/s12903-025-06343-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-06343-1)), aligner discoloration from beverages is a clinically significant issue because it affects patient self-esteem, can cause noncompliance, and is directly linked to the polymer chemistry used by each manufacturer. Different brands use different thermoplastics — polyurethane (PU), polyethylene terephthalate glycol (PETG), polyethylene terephthalate (PET) — with distinct color stability profiles. This study is the first to compare color stability across four brands simultaneously with molecular-level FTIR confirmation of polymer identity, and the first to detect the ClearCorrect material change from PU to PETG outer layer (ClearQuartz tri-layer).

## Methods

- **Design**: In vitro two-way factorial design
- **Samples**: 704 flat samples (7 mm diameter, 0.5–0.55 mm thick) from labial surface of each aligner at central incisor position; 176 per brand; 22 per group
- **Brands**: Invisalign (Align Technology, USA), ClearCorrect (Straumann Group, Switzerland), Beforedent (Beforedent Co., Seoul, Korea), Käse Aligner (Käse Aligner Co., Bangkok, Thailand)
- **Beverages**: Coffee (3g Nescafe/180ml), original Coca-Cola, red tea (4g ChaTraMue/300ml/5min steep), artificial saliva pH 7.36 (control)
- **Immersion**: 37±1°C, 6h (=1 week in-vivo contact) and 12h (=2 weeks)
- **Color**: CIE L*a*b* via Ultrascan PRO spectrophotometer (Specular Component Included Reflectance mode, 7 mm area of view); ΔE* = √[(ΔL*)² + (Δa*)² + (Δb*)²]; clinically unacceptable ΔE* >3.3
- **FTIR-ATR**: Spectrum One PerkinElmer, 400–4000 cm⁻¹, 4 cm⁻¹ resolution; only on groups with ΔE*>3.3
- **Surface**: 3D laser scanning microscope LEXT OLS5100 (Olympus) at 10x; inner and outer surfaces; before and after 12h immersion
- **Statistics**: 2-way ANOVA + Bonferroni; 1-way ANOVA + Tukey for surface roughness

## Results

**Clinically unacceptable staining (ΔE* >3.3):**

| Brand | Coffee 6h | Coffee 12h | Tea | Cola | Saliva |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invisalign | **✗ (>3.3)** | **✗ (>3.3)** | Noticeable | Trace-slight | Trace |
| ClearCorrect | Noticeable | **✗ (>3.3)** | Noticeable | Trace-slight | Trace |
| Beforedent | Noticeable | Noticeable | Slight | Trace | Trace |
| Käse Aligner | Noticeable | Noticeable | Slight | Trace | Trace |

- In coffee, all brands significantly differed from each other (p<0.001) except Beforedent vs Käse Aligner
- In tea, Invisalign was significantly different from all other brands (p<0.001)
- Cola and artificial saliva: no significant color difference across brands

**Inner surface roughness (Sa, μm):**

| Brand | Sa inner |
|---|---|
| Invisalign | **1.97 μm** ↑ (significantly different, p<0.001) |
| Käse Aligner | 0.54 μm |
| ClearCorrect | 0.40 μm |
| Beforedent | 0.39 μm |

Invisalign's inner surface showed dense parallel wavy grooves creating highest porosity; ClearCorrect had uneven shallow pits; Beforedent smooth; Käse had regularly-arranged narrow-spaced pits.

**FTIR polymer identification:**

| Brand | Polymer |
|---|---|
| Invisalign | Aromatic thermoplastic polyurethane — SmartTrack LD30; PETG inner layer / PU outer layer |
| ClearCorrect | Polyester (PETG outer / PU core / PETG outer) — ClearQuartz tri-layer |
| Beforedent | PETG (polyethylene terephthalate) |
| Käse Aligner | PETG (polyethylene terephthalate) |

**Key polymer chemistry**: PU carbamate group (-NHCOO-) is more polar than PETG ester group (-COO-) → greater hydrogen bonding with water molecules → more water absorption → more pigment penetration and surface swelling with microcracks.

## Clinical Takeaway

1. **Remove aligners before drinking coffee or tea** — this recommendation applies to all brands but is especially critical for Invisalign and ClearCorrect.
2. **For patients with high aesthetic demands or coffee/tea habits**, PETG-based aligner brands (Beforedent, Käse, or other PETG options) offer superior color stability.
3. **ClearCorrect has upgraded its polymer** from PU to PETG outer layer (ClearQuartz tri-layer) — this represents a meaningful material improvement confirmed by FTIR; older color stability data for ClearCorrect may overestimate its staining.
4. **Cola and water**: all brands showed only trace staining — patients need not remove aligners for water or low-pigment beverages.
