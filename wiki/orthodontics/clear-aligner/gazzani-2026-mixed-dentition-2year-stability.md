## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (n=20, mean age 8.3y, Invisalign First, "molars move first" protocol) with 2-year post-treatment follow-up without any retention appliance. Significant transversal expansion (U6-6 +2.1mm, L6-6 +2.4mm) and molar derotation were maintained at 2 years; only lower left molar torque showed significant relapse (-1.1°). Little's Irregularity Index remained stable at 0.4mm.

## 세줄요약

n=20 혼합치열기 Invisalign First, 유지장치 없이 2년 추적: 측방 확장·탈회전·배열 대부분 안정. 하악 좌측 제1대구치 토크만 -1.1° 유의 회귀. Little 지수 0.4mm(치료 직후)→0.4mm(2년 후) 유지.

## Background

Long-term stability of clear aligner outcomes in growing patients in the mixed dentition has remained poorly documented. Invisalign First was designed for early mixed dentition expansion, but whether the achieved arch changes could be maintained without retention over a meaningful follow-up period was unknown before this study.

## Methods

Retrospective cohort; initial pool 55 patients treated January 2021–December 2022 → n=20 after exclusions. Inclusion: early mixed dentition, fully erupted first permanent molars, anterior crowding, transverse discrepancy ≤6mm, adequate compliance. Treatment: Invisalign First, molars-move-first expansion protocol, 4–6mm planned expansion, 0.25mm/stage, 7-day aligner change, ~60 aligners/arch, no retention planned. Digital scans with iTero; Viewbox 4.0 measurements. Method error Kendall's W >0.95. Friedman ANOVA + Wilcoxon signed-rank. T0–T1 interval: 17±1.8 months; T1–T2: 24±1.4 months.

## Results

### Transversal Changes (mm)

| Measurement | T1-T0 (end Tx) | T2-T1 (2-yr follow-up) | T2-T0 (net) |
|---|---|---|---|
| U6-6 mesial | +2.1* | -0.1 (NS) | +2.0* |
| U6-6 distal | +1.8* | -0.1 (NS) | +1.7* |
| L6-6 mesial | +2.4* | -0.1 (NS) | +1.6* |
| L6-6 distal | +2.0* | -0.1 (NS) | +1.9* |

*p<0.05

### Other Outcomes

| Outcome | T1-T0 | T2-T1 | T2-T0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Little's Index | 7.5→0.4mm* | 0.4→0.4mm (NS) | stable* |
| Henry's angle (derotation) | Significant both sides | NS | Significant |
| Overjet | -1.5mm* | NS | -1.9mm* |
| LL-6 torque | +n/a | **-1.1°*** | — |

*p<0.05; LL-6 = lower left first molar torque

## Clinical Takeaway

Invisalign First with the molars-move-first protocol can achieve durable transversal expansion and alignment in early mixed dentition that appears largely stable at 2 years without retention. The single significant relapse point (lower left molar torque -1.1°) is minor. However, the small sample, retrospective design, and absence of controls mandate cautious clinical interpretation — clinicians may still consider retention for individual risk management, particularly in patients with residual skeletal transverse deficit.
