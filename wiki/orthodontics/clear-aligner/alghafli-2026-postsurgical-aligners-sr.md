## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (10 studies, 2 RCTs, n=258) of clear aligners in post-surgical orthodontic management. Skeletal/dental stability was comparable to fixed appliances; RCT evidence showed significantly better OHIP-14 (p=0.004), OQLQ-22 (p=0.002), and periodontal health with clear aligners. Surgery-first protocols with CA reduced treatment duration by ~4.8 months vs fixed appliances.

## 세줄요약

10편(RCT 2편, n=258) SR: 수술 후 CA 골격·치아 안정성은 FA와 동등(단기). RCT 근거: OHIP-14(p=0.004)·OQLQ-22(p=0.002)·치주건강 모두 CA 유의하게 우수. 수술-우선+CA 치료기간 18.05 vs 22.83개월로 단축(p<0.05).

## Background

Post-surgical orthodontic management has traditionally relied on fixed appliances. With improved aligner biomechanics and the growing use of surgery-first approaches (SFA), clear aligners are being explored as an alternative for post-surgical finishing. Evidence quality across stability, efficiency, and patient-reported outcomes required synthesis.

## Methods

Systematic review, PRISMA guidelines. Databases: PubMed, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library (inception to October 2025). Population: patients undergoing orthognathic surgery with aligner-based post-surgical management. RoB 2.0 (RCTs); Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (non-RCTs); JBI checklist (case series). No meta-analysis due to heterogeneity. 10 studies included: 2 RCTs, 6 retrospective/case-control, 2 case series.

## Results

### Skeletal/Dental Stability

| Study | Design | n | Key stability finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Li 2024 (RCT) | SFA, Class III | 74 | No significant skeletal relapse CA vs FA at 6mo; SFA+CA 18.05 vs 22.83mo* |
| Moon 2021 | Retrospective | 15 | No significant difference 6-mo stability |
| Amodeo 2020 | Case series | 12 | Stable Angle Class I at 1-year |
| Kwon 2022 | Retrospective | 15 | 87% PAR score improvement |

*p<0.05

### Efficiency

| Study | Key efficiency finding |
|---|---|
| Meazzini 2024 (n=26) | Pre-surgical appointments: CA 5.1 vs FA 10.2 (p<0.05) |
| Li 2024 (RCT) | Shorter total duration: SFA+CA vs SFA+FA |

### Perioperative Outcomes (Kankam 2019, n=33)

OR time, hospital stay, diet, narcotic use, postoperative edema: no significant differences.

## Clinical Takeaway

Post-surgical clear aligner management is a validated, evidence-based option. RCT-level evidence supports significantly better patient-reported quality of life (OHIP-14, OQLQ-22) and periodontal health vs fixed appliances. Surgery-first + CA reduces treatment duration. Case selection and patient compliance remain critical — patients must be highly motivated for ≥22h/day wear in the challenging post-surgical recovery period.
