## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial, n=34 (CA 20, FA 14), skeletal Class III patients treated with Surgery-First Approach (bimaxillary: LeFort I + BSSO) comparing clear aligners (Invisalign) vs conventional fixed appliances, with OHRQoL measured via OQLQ, OHIP-14 and SF-36 at pre-treatment (T0), 1-week post-appliance (T1), 4-week post-surgery (T2) and 6-month post-surgery (T3).

Both groups showed significant progressive intra-group OHRQoL improvement over time (most domains significant by T2–T3), but no statistically significant between-group difference in OQLQ, OHIP-14, or SF-36 scores at any timepoint (all p>0.05); OHIP-14 intra-group time-effect reached significance only in the CA group (p=0.02 vs FA p=0.1).

Appliance choice (aligner vs fixed) does not appear to differentially affect OHRQoL trajectory in Surgery-First-treated skeletal Class III patients, but small unequal groups (20 vs 14), non-randomized consecutive allocation, and a 6-month endpoint limit the strength and durability of this conclusion.

## Summary

This prospective, non-randomized controlled trial (REBEC + ClinicalTrials.gov registered, [DOI 10.1111/ocr.70054](https://doi.org/10.1111/ocr.70054)) compared oral health-related quality of life (OHRQoL, 삶의 질(Quality of Life, QoL)) in skeletal Class III patients undergoing bimaxillary orthognathic surgery via the **Surgery-First Approach (SFA)**, using either clear aligners (Invisalign, n=20) or conventional fixed appliances (Roth-prescription brackets, n=14). Groups were confirmed comparable at baseline in Wits, IGC, and IOTN-AC scores despite non-randomized consecutive allocation. Three validated instruments — OQLQ (orthognathic-specific), OHIP-14 (general oral-health), SF-36 (general health) — were administered at pre-treatment, 1-week post-appliance, 4-week post-surgery, and 6-month post-surgery. Both groups improved significantly over time across nearly all domains, with the bulk of improvement consolidating between the post-surgical and 6-month timepoints, but **no between-group differences were found at any timepoint on any instrument**. The one notable within-group asymmetry was that OHIP-14's overall time-effect reached statistical significance only in the CA group.

## Key Contributions

- First controlled (non-case-report) comparison of CA vs FA OHRQoL specifically within a **Class III Surgery-First Approach** context — the wiki's prior Class III aligner evidence (see Related Papers) was limited to two n=1 case reports.
- Triple-instrument design (OQLQ + OHIP-14 + SF-36) — the authors state this is the first study to add SF-36 to orthognathic-specific instruments in aligner-treated patients.
- Documents a specific CA-SFA clinical protocol: cutouts for buttons planned in the first 10 aligners (molar-to-canine, both arches) for trans-/post-surgical stability, accelerated 5-day aligner-change cycle for the first 3 months post-surgery.

## Methodology

Prospective controlled trial, consecutive (non-randomized) allocation: CA-eligible patients enrolled until minimum sample reached (n=14/group per power calculation, MCID 12pt, SD 10.5, α=0.05, power 80%), then subsequent eligible patients allocated to FA. Inclusion: no growth potential (CS6), skeletal Class III (negative Wits), indication for bimaxillary surgery. Exclusion: craniofacial anomaly, TMJ disease, periodontal compensation issues, need for extraction (except 3rd molars), curve of Spee >6 mm, mandibular asymmetry >6 mm. Surgery: LeFort I + BSSO by one surgeon; genioplasty in 6 CA / 5 FA patients. Statistics: Friedman + Durbin-Conover post hoc (within-group), Mann-Whitney (between-group), α=0.05.

## Results

| Instrument | Within-group time effect | Between-group difference (any T) |
|---|---|---|
| OQLQ (orthognathic-specific) | Significant in both groups (p<0.001) | None (all p>0.05) |
| OHIP-14 (general oral health) | Significant in CA only (p=0.02); FA p=0.1 | None (all p>0.05) |
| SF-36 (general health) | Significant improvement by T3 in both (e.g. physical functioning, role-physical) | None (all p>0.05) |
| IOTN-AC (professional + self aesthetic) | Significant in both (p<0.001) | None (all p>0.05) |

Baseline comparability confirmed: Wits −9.5 (CA) vs −8.2 (FA), p=0.45; IGC 37.1 vs 35.6, p=0.83; IOTN AC-E 6.1 vs 5.2, p=0.25. Most OHRQoL gains consolidated between the 4-week-post-surgery and 6-month timepoints (T2→T3) rather than immediately after appliance installation (T0→T1), in both groups.
