## Three-line Summary

Single-arm pre/post clinical study (prospective vs retrospective enrolment is not stated in the retrieved abstract), 38 adult patients (mean age 25.4 y) undergoing Invisalign treatment requiring maxillary molar distalization, comparing predicted vs achieved tooth movement via iTero digital models superimposed on the palatal rugae area.

142 maxillary molars (71 first, 71 second) measured for distal movement; predicted movement differed significantly from achieved for both first and second molars (P<0.0001); distal molar movement correlated significantly with anterior anchorage loss at the central incisor (r=0.3900, P<0.008) and lateral incisor (r=0.3595, P<0.013).

Invisalign can achieve a mean prescribed distalization of 2.6 mm in adults, but clinicians should anticipate anterior anchorage loss as a countereffect (especially with large initial overjet) and plan overcorrection/auxiliaries early; the exact achieved-vs-predicted efficiency percentage is not recoverable from the abstract alone.

## 세줄요약

단일군 전후비교 임상연구(전향/후향 여부는 초록에 미기재), 상악 대구치 원심이동(distalization)이 필요한 인비절라인(Invisalign) 치료 성인 38명(평균 25.4세) — 구개주름(palatal rugae) 부위 정합(registration)으로 중첩한 iTero 디지털 모델을 이용해 예측 대 실제 치아이동 비교.

상악 대구치 142개(제1대구치 71, 제2대구치 71) 원심이동 측정; 제1·제2대구치 모두 예측값과 실제값이 유의하게 차이(P<0.0001); 대구치 원심이동량과 전치부 고정원 손실(anchorage loss)이 중절치(r=0.3900, P<0.008)·측절치(r=0.3595, P<0.013)에서 유의한 상관관계.

성인에서 평균 2.6mm 처방 시 인비절라인 상악 대구치 원심이동이 성공적으로 가능하나, 특히 초기 overjet이 큰 경우 전치부 고정원 손실이라는 역효과를 예상하고 조기에 과교정(overcorrection)·보조장치 계획이 필요; 정확한 달성/예측 효율 % 수치는 초록만으로는 확인 불가.

## Summary

This single-arm clinical study (Saif et al., Am J Orthod Dentofacial Orthop 2022;161(4):e372-e379, PMID 34974928) examined 38 adult Invisalign patients (mean age 25.4 years) requiring maxillary molar distalization, using a distinctive measurement approach: palatal-rugae-area registration to superimpose pre- and post-distalization iTero digital models. Across 142 measured maxillary molars (71 first, 71 second), predicted distal movement differed significantly from the achieved outcome for both first and second molars (P<0.0001) — i.e., aligners under-delivered the prescribed distalization, consistent with the broader clear-aligner literature on achieved-vs-predicted expression gaps. The study also found a statistically significant correlation between the magnitude of molar distal movement and anterior anchorage loss (central incisor r=0.3900, P<0.008; lateral incisor r=0.3595, P<0.013), reinforcing the biomechanical expectation that distalizing posterior teeth reciprocally proclines/loses anchorage in the anterior segment. Clinically, the authors conclude Invisalign can successfully deliver a mean prescribed distalization of 2.6 mm in adults, but clinicians should anticipate the anterior anchorage-loss countereffect — especially in patients with a large initial overjet — and plan overcorrection or auxiliaries early rather than reactively. A notable author highlight states that attachments did not enhance the molars' own achieved distal movement, a specific finding that narrows the broader claim (from [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/nucera-2022-composite-attachments-clear-aligners-sr]]) that composite attachments generally improve mesio-distal movement and posterior anchorage. This source is abstract-only (paywalled AJODO landing page); the exact achieved-as-percentage-of-predicted efficiency figure is not stated in the retrieved text and should not be inferred.

## Key Contributions
- Introduces palatal-rugae-area registration as the digital-model superimposition method for measuring Invisalign molar-distalization outcomes.
- Quantifies a statistically significant predicted-vs-achieved gap for both maxillary first and second molar distalization (both P<0.0001).
- Establishes a significant correlation between molar distal movement magnitude and anterior anchorage loss at both central and lateral incisors.
- Reports that attachments did not enhance the molars' own distalization movement — a caveat on attachment efficacy specific to this movement type.

## Methodology
Single-arm pre/post digital-model comparison, n=38 adult patients (mean age 25.4 y) requiring maxillary molar distalization with Invisalign. Two iTero digital models per patient (pre-treatment; post first + second molar distalization) superimposed via palatal rugae registration. 142 molars (71 M1, 71 M2) measured for distal movement; 228 anterior teeth evaluated for anchorage loss. Evidence level `retrospective` — the retrieved abstract never states whether patients were enrolled prospectively or records reviewed retrospectively, so the lower rung is assigned deliberately rather than inferring the higher one.
