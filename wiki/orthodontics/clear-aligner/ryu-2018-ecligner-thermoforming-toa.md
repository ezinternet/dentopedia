## Three-line Summary

In-vitro multiscale study comparing four thermoplastic aligner materials (Duran, eCligner, Essix A+, Essix ACE) on transparency, water absorption/solubility, surface hardness, flexure modulus, and elastic modulus before and after thermoforming. eCligner (Orapix, Seoul, Korea) showed uniquely decreased water solubility post-thermoforming, stable transparency and surface hardness, and increased flexure modulus at 0.5 and 0.75 mm, though its pre-thermoforming water absorption was highest among tested brands. Thermoforming significantly altered properties in all four materials, rejecting the null hypothesis of property stability.

## 세줄요약

열가소성 교정 소재 4종(Duran, eCligner, Essix A+, Essix ACE)을 열성형 전후 다중 물성 비교한 in-vitro 연구. eCligner는 열성형 후 수분 용해도 감소, 투명도·경도 유지, 굴곡 탄성계수 증가라는 독특한 거동을 보였으나 열성형 전 수분 흡수율이 4종 중 최고. 열성형은 모든 소재에서 유의한 물성 변화를 유발해 귀무가설 기각.

## Background

Transparent orthodontic aligners (투명 교정 장치, Transparent Orthodontic Aligner, TOA) rely on thermoplastic materials whose mechanical and physical properties determine clinical force delivery and patient outcomes. The majority of aligner manufacturers use modified 폴리에틸렌 테레프탈레이트 글리콜 (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol, PETG), though polycarbonate, thermoplastic polyurethane, and copolyesters are also used. eCligner is a Korean brand manufactured by Orapix (Seoul, Korea), competing in the same PETG-based aligner market segment as Duran (Germany), Essix A+, and Essix ACE (USA).

Despite widespread clinical use, no standard method existed for evaluating thermoplastic TOA materials when this study was published. This study applies ISO 20795-2 (Orthodontic base polymer) as a framework, filling that gap.

## Methods

Four brands tested across multiple thicknesses:
- Duran (Scheu Dental, Germany): 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 mm
- eCligner (Orapix, Seoul, Korea): 0.5, 0.75 mm
- Essix A+ (Dentsply Raintree Essix, USA): 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 mm
- Essix ACE (Dentsply Raintree Essix, USA): 0.75, 1.0 mm

Test battery (all pre- and post-thermoforming):
1. Transparency: spectrophotometry (400–700 nm, Konica Minolta CM-3500D)
2. Water absorption / solubility: ISO 20795-2, 37°C ± 1°C, 14 days
3. Surface hardness: Knoop microindentation (9.8 N, 10 s)
4. Flexure modulus: 3-point bending (Instron 3366; 0.5–1.0 mm strain; 5 mm/min)
5. Elastic modulus: tensile test (Instron 5942; 12 mm/min)

Model: maxillary central incisor dimensions for Korean adults (crown 8.5 mm height × 7 mm width). Biostar thermoforming caster. Statistics: one-way ANOVA + Tukey; Student's t-test (p < 0.01).

## Key Results — eCligner

| Property | eCligner finding | vs. comparators |
|---|---|---|
| Water absorption (pre-thermoforming) | Highest among all 4 brands | ↑↑ |
| Water solubility (post-thermoforming) | Decreased (unique) | Others all increased |
| Transparency | No significant change pre/post | Others (Duran, Essix A+) decreased |
| Surface hardness | No change pre/post; greater than Essix A+/ACE after | Stable |
| Flexure modulus 0.5 mm | Increased after thermoforming | Same direction as Duran, Essix A+ |
| Flexure modulus 0.75 mm | Increased significantly | Duran also increased; Essix A+/ACE did not |
| Elastic modulus 0.75 mm | No significant change | Duran/Essix ACE decreased |

### General law (all materials)

- Thermoforming reduces flexural forces and tensile forces across all brands and thicknesses
- Flexure modulus is inversely proportional to thickness: **thinner = stiffer aligner**
- Water absorption increased in all four materials after thermoforming

## Clinical Implications

eCligner's unique post-thermoforming profile has several clinical interpretations:

1. **Water solubility decrease** after thermoforming suggests the material becomes less prone to dissolution in saliva — potentially favorable for intraoral stability and dimensional integrity during wear
2. **Water absorption pre-thermoforming highest** — unprocessed eCligner sheets absorb more water; however, the clinical aligner is used post-thermoforming where absorption is reduced
3. **Stable hardness and transparency** — lower risk of aesthetic degradation; hardness stability may indicate predictable force profile over the wear cycle
4. **Increased flexure modulus post-thermoforming** — the material becomes mechanically stiffer after aligner fabrication, which may provide higher orthodontic forces per unit thickness compared to pre-thermoforming predictions

**Clinical selection note**: The study recommends careful material selection by TOA type, since decrease in flexural forces after thermoforming may cause permanent deformation during treatment. eCligner shows favorable hardness and transparency stability but its elevated water absorption warrants monitoring for dimensional change and fit loss over extended wear cycles.

## Limitations

- No intraoral simulation (temperature cycling, saliva, humidity, cyclic insertion/removal fatigue)
- eCligner available only at 0.5 and 0.75 mm — full thickness-range comparison with Duran and Essix A+ not possible
- No direct tooth movement force measurement or clinical outcome assessment
- Study was conducted in 2018; eCligner formulation may have evolved since publication
