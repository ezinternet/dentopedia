## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT cohort (n=94 patients), extraction orthodontic cases treated with clear aligner therapy — pre-treatment (T0) and post-treatment (T1) CBCT quantified labial/palatal bone thickness (LBT/PBT) and height (LBH/PBH) changes around maxillary central incisors, related to 3D tooth displacement pattern via multiple linear regression.

Palatal bone defect was significant while labial bone was largely maintained; movement-pattern-specific risk factors emerged — retraction (↓PBT/↑LBT), crown retraction/uncontrolled tipping (↓PBH, dominant factor), incisor intrusion (↑PBT, ↓LBT, defect of both LBH and PBH), root distal movement (LBT/LBH resorption), lingual inclination (LBT loss).

Movement pattern — not just retraction magnitude — determines the site and type of alveolar bone risk around maxillary central incisors, so CAT treatment planning should anticipate the predicted 3D tooth displacement vector to avoid alveolar bone defect; abstract-level source only (paywalled Wiley Early View, no accessible full-text tables/effect sizes).

## 세줄요약

후향적 CBCT 코호트 (n=94명), 투명교정(CAT) 발치 증례 — 치료 전(T0)·후(T1) CBCT로 상악 중절치 주위 순측/구개측 골두께(LBT/PBT) 및 골높이(LBH/PBH) 변화를 3차원 치아 이동 양상과 다중선형회귀로 연관지어 분석.

구개측 골결손이 유의했고 순측골은 대체로 유지됨; 이동 양상별 위험 요인 — 후방이동(retraction, PBT↓/LBT↑), 치관후방이동/uncontrolled tipping(PBH↓, 주요인), 절치 압하(intrusion, PBT↑/LBT↓ + LBH·PBH 모두 결손), 치근원심이동(LBT/LBH 흡수), 설측경사(LBT 소실).

단순 후방이동량이 아니라 이동 "양상"이 상악 중절치 치조골 위험의 부위·유형을 결정하므로, CAT 치료계획 시 예측 3차원 치아이동 벡터를 고려해 치조골 결손을 예방해야 함; 초록 수준 소스(페이월 Wiley Early View, 본문 표·효과크기 접근 불가).

## Summary

This retrospective CBCT study (n=94 patients) examines what drives alveolar bone dimensional change around the maxillary central incisor in extraction cases treated with clear aligner therapy (CAT). Using paired pre-treatment (T0) and post-treatment (T1) CBCT, the authors measured labial bone thickness (LBT), palatal bone thickness (PBT), labial bone height (LBH), and palatal bone height (PBH) at different root levels, then related these changes to the 3D tooth displacement pattern (retraction, intrusion, root distal movement, lingual inclination, and controlled vs uncontrolled tipping) via multiple linear regression with backward elimination. The central finding is a labial–palatal asymmetry: palatal bone defect was significant while labial bone was largely preserved, and different movement components produced distinct, sometimes opposite-direction, effects on each bone compartment. The paper's clinical message is that treatment planning should anticipate the predicted tooth movement pattern from the aligner setup — not just the net retraction distance — to flag and mitigate alveolar bone risk before finalizing a case.

This source could only be built from the abstract and reference list; the full-text body (tables, exact subgroup sizes, regression coefficients/CIs) sits behind a Wiley Early View paywall with no PMC full-text mirror, so quantitative precision beyond directional findings is not available.

## Key Contributions

- Identifies **tooth movement pattern** (retraction, intrusion, root distal movement, lingual inclination, tipping type), rather than extraction status or net retraction distance alone, as the key determinant of alveolar bone dimensional change around the maxillary central incisor in CAT extraction cases.
- Demonstrates a **labial vs palatal asymmetry**: palatal bone bears the significant defect while labial bone is comparatively maintained — a compartment-specific risk signature.
- Distinguishes **uncontrolled tipping vs controlled tipping** as producing different bone-dimensional change patterns, reinforcing that movement quality (not just endpoint) matters for periodontal/osseous safety.
- Uses regression to attribute specific outcomes (PBT/LBT/PBH/LBH change) to specific movement components rather than reporting only aggregate pre/post change — a more actionable, mechanism-level framework for treatment planners.

## Methodology

Retrospective cohort, single institution (Wuhan University School & Hospital of Stomatology, China). 94 patients with extraction orthodontic treatment via clear aligner therapy; focus tooth: maxillary central incisor. Paired CBCT at T0 (pre-treatment) and T1 (post-treatment) used to measure LBT, PBT, LBH, PBH at different root levels, and to derive 3D tooth displacement (retraction, intrusion, root distal movement, crown/lingual inclination, controlled vs uncontrolled tipping). Statistics: paired t-test/Wilcoxon signed-rank test for T0-vs-T1 within-group comparison; two-sided Welch's t-test/Mann–Whitney U test for between-movement-pattern comparisons; multiple linear regression with backward elimination to identify independent factors associated with each bone-change outcome. Trial registration: HGGC-227.

## Results

- **Palatal bone**: significant defect (loss) after treatment.
- **Labial bone**: basically maintained (no significant loss).
- **Tipping type**: uncontrolled vs controlled tipping produced different bone-dimensional change patterns.
- **Tooth retraction** → ↓PBT, ↑LBT.
- **Crown retraction** (uncontrolled tipping) → dominant factor for ↓PBH.
- **Incisor intrusion** → ↑PBT, ↓LBT, and bone defect of both LBH and PBH (most adverse pattern across both compartments).
- **Root distal movement** → resorption of LBT and LBH.
- **Lingual inclination** → evidently related to LBT loss.
- No numeric effect sizes, p-values, or regression coefficients were retrievable (abstract-only source; full-text/supplementary Data S1 inaccessible).
