## Three-line Summary

Prospective comparative study (n=60; 30 CA, 30 FA) in complex malocclusions showing equivalent PAR reduction between modalities; fixed appliances had shorter treatment duration for extraction-heavy cases, but clear aligners showed significantly better OHIP-14 at 1 month (p<0.05) that converged by treatment completion. Complex cases with aligners require meticulous staging, auxiliaries, and high compliance.

## 세줄요약

n=60 복잡 부정교합 전향 비교: PAR 감소 두 군 동등; FA 치료기간 유의 단축(발치 역학). OHIP-14 1개월 CA 우수→치료 완료 시 수렴. 복잡케이스 CA 성공 전제: 정밀 스테이징·높은 순응도.

## Background

Clear aligners have been historically considered unsuitable for complex malocclusions requiring extractions, significant rotation or torque corrections, or vertical control. With advances in attachment design, auxiliary mechanics, and staging software, aligners are increasingly being applied to complex cases — but comparative outcome data (occlusal and PRO) in this population were limited.

## Methods

Prospective comparative study; university orthodontic clinic. Adults 16–35y. Complex malocclusion defined as ≥2 of: severe crowding (≥6mm ≥1 arch), premolar extraction plan, significant rotation (≥15°) or incisor torque correction (≥8°), deep/open bite requiring vertical control, clinically relevant asymmetry. n=60 (30 CA, 30 FA). CA: attachments, elastics, and refinements permitted. FA: preadjusted edgewise. Primary outcome: PAR change T0 to completion. Secondary: treatment duration, clinical visits, refinements, OHIP-14 at 1 month and completion. Independent t-test or Mann-Whitney; chi-square; p<0.05.

## Results

| Outcome | CA | FA | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAR reduction | Substantial | Substantial | p>0.05 (equivalent) |
| Treatment duration | Longer | **Shorter** | p<0.05 |
| Clinical visits | Fewer | More | — |
| Refinement stages | More | Fewer | — |
| OHIP-14 (1 month) | **Better** | Higher | p<0.05 |
| OHIP-14 (completion) | Equivalent | Equivalent | NS |
| Extraction space closure | Adequate | **Better** | — |
| Root control | Adequate | **Better** | — |

Approximately 2/3 of patients had severe crowding; approximately 1/2 required premolar extractions.

## Clinical Takeaway

Clear aligners can achieve equivalent occlusal improvement to fixed appliances even in complex cases, but at the cost of longer treatment time and more refinement stages — especially for extraction mechanics. The short-term OHIP-14 advantage (month 1) may be a decisive factor for complex patients who value aesthetics and comfort during treatment. Long-term QoL equivalence at completion allows evidence-based patient choice between modalities.
