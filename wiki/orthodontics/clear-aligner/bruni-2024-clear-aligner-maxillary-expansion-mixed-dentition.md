## Three-line Summary

Prospective interventional cohort (n=40 mixed-dentition children, mean age 8.7 y, transverse discrepancy ≤5 mm) treated with Invisalign First, comparing achieved maxillary dentoalveolar expansion against the ClinCheck plan, stratified by tooth region (deciduous canine / first deciduous molar / permanent first molar) at cusp and gingival levels.

Accuracy fell anterior→posterior — inter-canine 87.7% (cusp)/82.7% (gingival), inter-deciduous-molar 84.9%/80.5%, inter-permanent-molar 77.8%/67.9%; regression showed only ~0.58–0.72 mm clinical expression per 1 mm planned (overall 80.2% of target).

Clear aligners deliver predictable dentoalveolar (not orthopedic) maxillary expansion in growing children, but molar widening is mostly buccal crown tipping — plan overcorrection (~7 mm ClinCheck for 5 mm clinical at the permanent molar) and choose RME when a true sutural effect is needed.

## 세줄요약

혼합치열 소아 40명(평균 8.7세, 횡적 부조화 ≤5 mm)을 인비절라인 퍼스트로 치료한 전향적 코호트로, ClinCheck 가상계획 대비 상악 치조성 확장 정확도를 유견치·제1유구치·제1대구치 구간별(교두/치은 레벨)로 비교했다.

정확도는 전방→후방으로 감소(견치간 87.7%/82.7%, 유구치간 84.9%/80.5%, 대구치간 77.8%/67.9%); 회귀상 계획 1 mm당 임상 0.58–0.72 mm만 발현(전체 목표의 80.2%).

투명교정은 성장기 소아에서 (봉합 열개가 아닌) 치조성 상악 확장을 예측가능하게 얻지만 대구치는 대부분 협측 치관경사라 overcorrection이 필요(영구대구치 5 mm 임상확장 위해 ClinCheck 약 7 mm 계획)하며, 진정한 정형효과가 필요하면 CAT 대신 RME를 택해야 한다.

## 한줄요약

혼합치열(mixed dentition) 소아 40명 전향연구 — 투명교정(Clear Aligner Treatment, CAT)의 상악 치조성 확장(dentoalveolar expansion)은 전방 유견치 87.7%에서 후방 영구대구치 67.9%로 정확도가 떨어지며, 대구치 확장은 협측 치관경사(buccal crown tipping) 위주여서 과교정(overcorrection)이 필요하다.

## Summary

This prospective interventional cohort from the University of Turin (published in the Korean Journal of Orthodontics) tested whether clear aligners predictably deliver planned maxillary transverse expansion in mixed-dentition children. Forty patients (mean 8.7 years) with a transverse discrepancy ≤5 mm were treated with Invisalign First and no auxiliaries beyond attachments; achieved expansion (final intraoral scan) was compared against the ClinCheck virtual plan at three arch regions and two vertical reference levels (cusp and gingival). Measurement reproducibility was excellent (ICC 0.90–0.95). Accuracy declined from the anterior to the posterior arch and from the cusp to the gingival level, and multiple regression showed that only roughly 0.6 mm of clinical expansion is expressed per 1 mm planned. The pattern — cusp accuracy exceeding gingival accuracy, most markedly at the permanent molar — indicates that aligner "expansion," especially posteriorly, is largely buccal crown tipping rather than bodily movement. Clinically, CAT is a viable dentoalveolar expansion option in growing children with moderate transverse deficiency, provided the clinician overcorrects (≈7 mm ClinCheck to obtain 5 mm at the permanent molar) and reserves RME for cases needing a genuine orthopedic/sutural effect.

## Key Contributions

- First region-stratified quantification of clear-aligner maxillary **dentoalveolar** expansion accuracy specifically in **mixed dentition** (deciduous canine → first deciduous molar → first permanent molar).
- Documents a reproducible **anterior→posterior accuracy gradient** and a **cusp > gingival** accuracy pattern that fingerprints crown tipping vs bodily movement.
- Derives a practical **overcorrection estimate**: ~0.6 mm clinical per 1 mm planned → plan ~7 mm ClinCheck to achieve ~5 mm clinical at the permanent molar.
- Shows aligner expansion in children (80.2% overall) is more predictable than the ~70% reported for adults, attributed to material advances and age-related biological responsiveness.

## Methodology

- **Design**: prospective interventional cohort; consecutive referrals Sept 2020–Mar 2022; 61 recruited → 40 analyzed (18 M, 22 F).
- **Inclusion**: mixed dentition, transverse maxillary deficiency with inter-arch posterior discrepancy ≤5 mm.
- **Appliance/protocol**: Invisalign First Phase I, attachments only (no IPR, no extraction); ClinCheck sequential expansion (permanent molars buccalized first using the rest of the arch as anchorage, then deciduous molars/canines); 0.15 mm extrusion + 2° extra buccal root torque per stage as overcorrection; weekly aligner change; reviewed q4 weeks; mean 9 ± 1 months, 6 ± 2 visits.
- **Outcomes**: STL comparison (T0 initial IOS, T1/2 planned final, T1 achieved IOS) in Geomagic Control X; inter-dental distances at cusp and gingival (zenith) levels for canine/deciduous-molar/permanent-molar.
- **Statistics**: ICC (>0.9 threshold); Student's t test (planned vs obtained per tooth); multiple regression (mm clinical per mm planned); Kravitz percent-accuracy formula; a priori min n=22 for 80% power.

## Results

| Region | Cusp accuracy | Gingival accuracy | Regression estimate (mm/mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inter-deciduous canine | 87.7% | 82.7% | 0.65 / 0.65 |
| Inter-first deciduous molar | 84.9% | 80.5% | 0.72 / 0.58 |
| Inter-first permanent molar | 77.8% | 67.9% | 0.61 / 0.66 |

- **Reproducibility**: ICC 0.90–0.95.
- **Significance (planned vs obtained)**: significant differences at inter-canine gingival, inter-deciduous-molar (cusp & gingival), and permanent-molar gingival; permanent-molar **cusp** expansion was **not** significantly different from plan.
- **Magnitude**: overall dentoalveolar expansion = 80.2% of planned; ~3.5 mm achieved at the permanent molar and >4 mm at the first deciduous molar with a single aligner set.
- **Interpretation**: cusp > gingival accuracy (especially at molars) ⇒ buccal crown tipping dominates over bodily translation posteriorly; younger patients out-perform the ~70% adult benchmark.

## Clinical Bottom Line

- Use CAT for **dentoalveolar** correction of moderate (≤5 mm) transverse maxillary deficiency in growing children — not for cases requiring an **orthopedic/sutural** effect (choose RME there).
- **Overcorrect**, particularly at permanent molars: plan ~7 mm in ClinCheck to net ~5 mm clinically; consider 14-day aligner wear for molar control and buccal attachments on deciduous canines/first molars to counter tipping.
- Set expectations that posterior expansion is largely crown tipping; gingival-level (true skeletal-base) widening lags the cusp-level number.
