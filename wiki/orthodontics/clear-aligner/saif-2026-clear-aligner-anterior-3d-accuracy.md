## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (40 adults avg 27.8 years, Invisalign, non-extraction, first aligner set without refinement; 480 anterior teeth; 10-day change, avg 27 aligners/9 months) comparing ClinCheck predicted and actual achieved positions across 4 movement types (vertical movement, mesiodistal rotation, mesiodistal tipping, buccolingual tipping) in all anterior teeth.

Overall mean accuracy 50.3% (upper 47.6%, lower 53%); worst: upper incisor intrusion 23.1%; best upper: distal crown tipping 64.85%, mesial rotation 63.32%; best lower: lingual tipping 75.52%, labial tipping 66.51%; all movement types showed significant predicted-vs-achieved differences (p<0.05).

ClinCheck systematically overestimates anterior tooth movement in all directions; intrusion is the most clinically challenging movement; refinements are expected for virtually all anterior cases after the initial aligner set.

## Background

According to PubMed ([DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-40219-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-40219-w)), anterior tooth alignment is aesthetically and functionally critical, yet the accuracy of clear aligner treatment in achieving anterior positions has been inconsistently studied — many prior studies include refinements, mix anterior and posterior teeth, or analyze post-treatment results rather than first-set performance. This study uniquely isolates accuracy of the first aligner set without any midcourse correction or refinement, establishing a true first-phase baseline for Invisalign anterior tooth movement. With 40 adult patients and 480 anterior teeth analyzed across 4 movement types simultaneously, it is one of the most comprehensive single-institution anterior accuracy studies to date.

## Methods

- **Design**: Retrospective; Declaration of Helsinki; IRB XJTU1AF2022LSK-186
- **Sample**: 40 adults (18M/22F), avg age 27.8 ± 2.67 years; Invisalign-certified orthodontists; Xi'an Jiaotong University
- **Treatment period**: January 2019 – February 2024
- **Protocol**: 10-day aligner wear; 0.25 mm max linear/2° rotation/1° tipping per aligner; avg 27 aligners; avg 9 months treatment
- **Inclusion**: Adults, non-extraction (except 3rd molars), completed first aligner set without midcourse correction or refinement, no active periodontal disease or systemic conditions
- **Evaluation**: Initial and final intraoral scans + ClinCheck predicted final digital models; 480 anterior teeth (upper + lower, central incisors through canines)
- **4 movement types**: Vertical (intrusion/extrusion mm), mesiodistal rotation (°), mesiodistal crown tipping (°), buccolingual crown tipping (°)
- **Sample size**: G*Power calculated; 38 patients required (α=0.05, 85% power, d=0.5, thresholds: 0.25 mm linear, 2° angular)
- **Statistics**: Paired t-tests or Wilcoxon signed-rank; Cohen's d effect sizes; ICC intraexaminer reliability
- **Attachments**: anterior teeth used vertical rectangular attachments (VRA), horizontal rectangular attachments (HRA), optimized rotation/root control, G6 optimized; power ridges on lateral incisors

## Results

**Overall anterior accuracy:**

| Arch | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| All anterior teeth | **50.3%** |
| Upper anterior | 47.6% |
| Lower anterior | 53.0% |

**Movement-specific accuracy (upper dentition):**

| Movement | Accuracy | Rank |
|---|---|---|
| Distal crown tipping | **64.85%** | Best upper |
| Mesial rotation | 63.32% | 2nd |
| [Other tipping] | Intermediate | — |
| Vertical (intrusion of max central incisors) | **23.1%** | Worst overall |

**Movement-specific accuracy (lower dentition):**

| Movement | Accuracy | Rank |
|---|---|---|
| Lingual crown tipping | **75.52%** | Best lower |
| Labial crown tipping | 66.51% | 2nd |

- **All 4 movement types**: significant predicted vs achieved differences (p<0.05)
- **Upper arch**: maxillary central incisors showed greatest discrepancy (intrusion worst)
- **Lower arch**: tipping movements more predictable than upper arch equivalents
- **Baseline characteristics**: avg overjet 3.81 mm, avg overbite 3.85 mm, upper crowding 3.5 mm, lower crowding 3.6 mm

## Clinical Takeaway

1. **Baseline expectation: ~50% accuracy** for anterior tooth positions after the first Invisalign aligner set. Plan refinements as standard, not exception.
2. **Upper incisor intrusion (23.1%)** is the least predictable movement — requires aggressive overcorrection in ClinCheck. In deep overbite cases this is a critical planning limitation.
3. **Tipping movements** (especially lower lingual tipping 75.52%) are the most achievable anterior CA movements — appropriate for leveling and aligning cases.
4. **10-day aligner change** protocol used; even with this more frequent change schedule, accuracy remained at ~50%. This suggests aligner wear compliance or inherent biomechanical limits — not timing alone — drive the accuracy gap.
5. **Refinements are clinically expected** and should be budgeted into treatment planning for virtually all anterior CA cases.
