## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional study (n=112 adults, 4 groups: initial/final × CA/FA) using the DIDL questionnaire. Initial fixed appliance phase had 35.7% dissatisfied patients; final clear aligner phase had zero dissatisfied patients. Adaptation over treatment time was the key driver of improving experience in both modalities.

## 세줄요약

112명 성인 DIDL 4그룹 단면연구: 고정장치 초기 불만족 35.7% vs CA 후기 불만족 0% — 극명한 대비. DIDL 총점은 그룹 간 차이 없음(적응이 균등화). 장치 장착 직후 환자 관리가 임상 핵심.

## Background

Patient experience during orthodontic treatment varies considerably depending on appliance type and treatment phase, yet the Dental Impacts on Daily Living (DIDL) instrument — which captures appearance, pain, comfort, general performance, and eating domains with weighted scoring — had not been used in a phase-stratified CA vs FA comparison prior to this study.

## Methods

Cross-sectional; 4 independent groups (n=28 each): Initial FA / Final FA / Initial CA / Final CA. Adults ≥18y, Fluminense Federal University orthodontic clinic, Brazil. DIDL questionnaire (36 items, 5 domains; Brazilian Portuguese validation by Leão and Sheiham). Score range: -1 (fully dissatisfied) to +1 (fully satisfied); <0 = dissatisfied. Mann-Whitney test; Fisher's exact test for satisfaction categories.

## Results

### Satisfaction Distribution

| Group | Dissatisfied (<0) | Partial (0–0.7) | Satisfied (>0.7) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial FA | 35.7% | 64.3% | 0% |
| Initial CA | 14.3% | 82.1% | 3.6% |
| Final FA | 10.7% | 82.1% | 7.1% |
| Final CA | **0%** | 100% | 0% |

Fisher p=0.001 (overall). Total DIDL scores: no significant between-group differences (p>0.05).

### Domain Highlights

| Domain | Notable finding |
|---|---|
| General performance | Initial → final FA significant improvement (p=0.025) |
| Comfort | Final CA: 0 breathing interference vs 16/28 FA; 2 vs 8 gingival bleeding |
| Pain | No significant between-group differences |
| Appearance | Not significantly different — both modalities preserve self-image |

## Clinical Takeaway

The initial placement window for fixed appliances is the critical high-dissatisfaction moment. Proactive counseling, pain management protocols, and accessible follow-up immediately post-bonding can address the 35.7% initial dissatisfaction rate. Clear aligners do not produce this sharp initial dissatisfaction spike, making them advantageous for patients sensitive to early treatment disruption.
