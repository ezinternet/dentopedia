## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=55 growing Class II patients, CVM 2–3) comparing Invisalign MAA, Activator, and untreated controls over 8 months using CBCT volumetric analysis. Neither treatment group showed statistically significant differences from controls in mandibular or maxillary volumetric change (ANCOVA adjusted: mandible p=0.877, maxilla p=0.952). Intragroup MAA maxillary increase was significant (p=0.009) but did not translate to an intergroup difference, reinforcing that short-term Class II correction is predominantly positional, not volumetric.

## Summary

This single-center RCT enrolled 55 skeletal Class II individuals in pubertal growth acceleration (CVM stages 2–3) at Batman University, Turkey. Participants were randomized to Invisalign MAA (n=20, mean 8.15 months treatment), conventional Activator (n=20, mean 7.15 months), or untreated control (n=15). Volumetric analysis of mandible and maxilla via CBCT using Mimics 15.0 was performed at baseline and post-treatment. Despite volumetric increases in both treatment groups, no statistically significant intergroup differences were found. After ANCOVA adjustment for baseline volume imbalance and inter-scan interval heterogeneity, results remained null. The study concludes that short-term functional Class II correction is predominantly positional/dentoalveolar rather than resulting in true skeletal volumetric growth, with longer observation periods required to detect cumulative remodeling.

## Key Contributions

- First 3D CBCT volumetric RCT directly comparing Invisalign MAA vs. Activator vs. untreated controls
- ANCOVA-adjusted null finding: despite visible volumetric trends, treatment group differences are not significant when baseline imbalances are controlled
- Intragroup MAA maxillary volume increase (p=0.009) suggests positional expansion artifact, not structural growth benefit over controls
- Confirms that short-term functional appliance effects are mainly dentoalveolar/positional — consistent with 2D cephalometric literature
- ICC >0.90 validates CBCT volumetric segmentation reproducibility for this type of outcome

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-center parallel-arm RCT, CONSORT-reported; retrospective CBCT analysis
- **n=55** growing patients (CVM 2–3, mean age ~12.5y), skeletal Class II (ANB>4°, SNB<78°), overjet +4–+8mm
- **MAA (n=20)**: Invisalign MAA, 2mm increments q8 weeks, follow-up q5–6 weeks, mean 8.15 months
- **Activator (n=20)**: Projet Bite Wafer, full-time (except meals/sport), mean 7.15 months
- **Control (n=15)**: untreated observation, 6 months
- **CBCT**: i-CAT 3D; Mimics 15.0 segmentation; HU 226–3071; intra-operator ICC >0.90
- **Stats**: Kruskal-Wallis H (intergroup), Wilcoxon signed-rank (intragroup), ANCOVA (covariates: baseline volume, inter-scan interval)
