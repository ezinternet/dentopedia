## Three-line Summary

Ten retrospective studies (n=421) of clear aligner therapy for adult anterior open bite yielded a pooled overbite increase of 3.07 mm (primary, 5 studies, I²=93%) and 2.89 mm (extended sensitivity, 7 studies, I²=96%), both with very low GRADE certainty. The descriptive evidence map identified anterior dentoalveolar compensation (incisor extrusion/retroclination) as the most consistently reported mechanism, with variable, protocol-dependent posterior vertical change and limited mandibular autorotation; predictable skeletal correction was not established. Nine of ten studies had serious risk of bias (ROBINS-I), and comparative evidence confirmed that clear aligner therapy and skeletal anchorage-based posterior intrusion are mechanistically distinct.

## 세줄요약

성인 전방 개방 교합 (Anterior Open Bite, AOB) 10개 연구 (421명)에서 투명교정 (Clear Aligner Therapy, CAT) 후 과개교합 개선량은 주요 분석 3.07 mm, 민감도 분석 2.89 mm로 이전 효능 SR (2.76 mm)과 유사하나 이질성 (I²=93–96%) 및 GRADE 근거 확실성이 매우 낮았다. 치아치조 변화 (Dentoalveolar Changes) — 절치 돌출 (Incisor Extrusion) 및 경사 변화 — 가 가장 일관된 교정 경로였으며, 예측 가능한 골격 교정 (Skeletal Correction)은 현재 근거로 뒷받침되지 않는다. 골격 정박 (Skeletal Anchorage) 기반 구치부 압하 (Posterior Intrusion) 프로토콜과 투명교정은 기전이 다르므로 임상 결과를 동일하게 해석해서는 안 된다.

## Summary

This systematic review, evidence map, and GRADE certainty appraisal addresses a gap left by previous efficacy-focused syntheses of clear aligner therapy (CAT) for adult anterior open bite (AOB): *what* dentoskeletal changes are reported, and how confidently can they be interpreted?

Ten clinical studies (421 aligner-treated adults, 2017–2025) were included. The primary exploratory random-effects meta-analysis of 5 completed-treatment cohorts (n=206) yielded a pooled overbite increase of **3.07 mm** (95% CI 2.46–3.69 mm; I²=93%). An extended sensitivity analysis adding two further studies (n=322) yielded **2.89 mm** (95% CI 2.21–3.57 mm; I²=96%). These estimates are close to the 2.76 mm reported in the prior efficacy SR by Charoenrat 2025, but with critical caveats.

The descriptive evidence map classified reported dentoskeletal findings as:
- **Anterior dentoalveolar** (most consistent): incisor extrusion, incisor retroclination, changes in incisor inclination
- **Posterior vertical** (variable): some molar intrusion reported, but not uniform — depends on sagittal/vertical phenotype and protocol
- **Mandibular autorotation/plane angle** (limited, secondary): reported in subset; small magnitude in adults without skeletal anchorage
- **Skeletal correction** (not established): comparative studies show CAT and TAD/miniplate-supported intrusion achieve overbite improvement through different pathways

GRADE certainty was **very low** for all five assessed outcomes (overbite increase, incisor change, molar intrusion, mandibular plane change, predictable skeletal correction). Nine of ten nonrandomized studies had serious overall risk of bias (ROBINS-I). The only RCT (Withayanukonkij 2023) had some concerns and reported only a 6-month interim endpoint.

## Key Contributions

1. **Evidence map framework**: First SR to explicitly map reported dentoskeletal findings as direct, indirect, or insufficient for mechanistic interpretation — moves the field beyond efficacy numbers toward mechanism understanding
2. **GRADE appraisal**: Provides formal certainty assessment across five outcomes; all very low, signaling the limits of current evidence
3. **Mechanistic distinction**: Confirms that overbite improvement from CAT reflects primarily dentoalveolar compensation, not predictable skeletal correction equivalent to skeletal anchorage protocols
4. **Heterogeneity quantified**: I²=93–96% highlights that the pooled estimate should not be treated as a reliable individual treatment predictor
5. **Research agenda**: Provides clear requirements for future studies (prospective, comparative, component-level outcomes, blinded evaluation, long-term retention)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review, descriptive evidence map, exploratory meta-analysis, GRADE appraisal
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD420261376480
- **Search**: PubMed, EMBASE, Scopus, Cochrane Library; Jan 2010–April 2026 (final search April 30, 2026)
- **Eligibility**: Adult/predominantly adult AOB cohorts treated with CAT; overbite change or dentoskeletal outcomes; RCTs and nonrandomized clinical studies
- **Risk of bias**: ROBINS-I (nonrandomized); RoB 2 (RCT)
- **Evidence mapping**: Findings classified as direct/indirect/insufficient for mechanistic interpretation
- **Statistical**: Random-effects inverse-variance meta-analysis; heterogeneity via I², tau², chi²; no publication-bias test (n<10 studies)
- **Certainty**: GRADE framework for 5 key outcomes

### Evidence map — dentoskeletal changes

**Anterior dentoalveolar** (most consistently reported)
- Incisor extrusion: Harris 2020, Moshiri 2017, Arwa 2025
- Incisor retroclination: Garnett 2019, Arwa 2025
- Phenotype-dependent (Suh 2022): maxillary incisor extrusion dominant in dental AOB; mandibular incisor extrusion in skeletal AOB
- Caution: extrusion is one of the less predictable aligner movements

**Posterior vertical change** (variable, protocol-dependent)
- Some molar intrusion: Moshiri 2017, Harris 2020, Arwa 2025 (maxillary molars on CBCT)
- Vertical position maintained (no significant molar intrusion): Garnett 2019
- Suh 2022: posterior vertical effects not uniform across phenotypes
- Rask et al. (contextual): available evidence does not support claim that CAT provides better vertical dimension control than fixed appliances

**Mandibular autorotation / plane angle**
- Reported as secondary response in: Moshiri 2017 (counterclockwise rotation), Harris 2020 (slight autorotation), Suh 2022 (plane reduction in skeletal AOB)
- Magnitude limited in adults without skeletal anchorage
- Interpretation limited by cephalometric superimposition variability (Ovard et al.: same cephalograms may be interpreted differently)

**Comparative evidence**
- Steele 2022 vs miniplate: CAT = dentoalveolar correction; miniplate = more direct posterior intrusion; mechanistically different
- Chamberland & Nataf 2024 vs TAD double-arch: TAD showed greater mandibular counterclockwise rotation; CAT relied more on dentoalveolar changes
- Withayanukonkij 2023 RCT vs miniscrew intrusion (6-mo): different mechanisms confirmed
