## Three-line Summary

Pilot cross-over study submitting identical records of 10 patients (ages 12–40) to Invisalign, ClearCorrect, 3M Clarity, and Spark with standardized 14-day prescriptions; compared number of aligners, attachments, IPR, incisor vertical movement, canine/molar relationship, and arch widths.

Significant differences in # aligners (p=0.003; ClearCorrect lowest avg 19.7, 3M Clarity highest avg 32.7), # attachments (p<0.001; ClearCorrect avg 9, 3M Clarity avg 17.8), and final canine Class I relationship (p=0.013; Invisalign 65%, 3M Clarity 70%, Spark 20%, ClearCorrect 15%); IPR, incisor vertical movement, molar Class I, and arch widths were not significantly different.

ClearCorrect plans the fewest aligners and attachments (aided by its 2 mm supragingival flat trim) but at the cost of poor canine Class I planning; Spark is comparable to Invisalign except for a canine Class I deficiency; 3M Clarity prescribes the most via a staged dual-thickness approach; virtual treatment setups for the same patient differ meaningfully across brands.

## 세줄요약

동일 환자 10명을 Invisalign·ClearCorrect·3M Clarity·Spark에 동시 제출, 표준 처방으로 가상치료계획(Virtual Treatment Setup, VTS)을 비교한 파일럿 교차 연구.

교정장치 수(p=0.003; ClearCorrect 최소 19.7개, 3M Clarity 최다 32.7개), 어태치먼트 수(p<0.001; ClearCorrect 9개 vs 3M Clarity 17.8개), 최종 견치 Class I 관계(p=0.013; Invisalign 65%, 3M Clarity 70%, Spark 20%, ClearCorrect 15%)에서 유의한 차이; IPR·수직이동·구치 관계·치궁 폭경은 차이 없음.

ClearCorrect는 가장 적은 교정장치/어태치먼트를 처방하지만 견치 교합 달성 계획이 불충분; Spark는 견치 Class I 결함 외 Invisalign과 유사; 3M Clarity는 단계별 이중두께로 치료를 길게 계획; 같은 환자에 대해 브랜드마다 VTS가 의미 있게 다르다.

## Background

According to PubMed ([DOI 10.1590/2177-6709.30.3.e2524243.oar](https://doi.org/10.1590/2177-6709.30.3.e2524243.oar)), approximately 90% of clear aligner (CA) research focuses exclusively on Invisalign, leaving a significant knowledge gap about other brands. After Invisalign's patents expired in 2017, companies including ClearCorrect (Straumann), 3M Clarity, and Spark (Ormco) entered the market with distinct materials, trim designs, and planning algorithms. The **virtual treatment setup (VTS)** is the foundation of clear aligner therapy — it determines number of aligners, attachment design, IPR, and projected occlusal outcomes. This study is, to the authors' knowledge, the first to directly compare VTS outputs across four brands for the same patient records under standardized conditions.

## Methods

- **Design**: Pilot cross-over VTS comparative study
- **Sample**: 10 patients (ages 12–40), all permanent teeth, no surgery/implants/deciduous dentition
- **Brands**: Invisalign, ClearCorrect, 3M Clarity, Spark — each received identical records
- **Standardization**: 14-day aligner change; clinical preferences (expansion limits, IPR staging, overjet/overbite targets) standardized; no written special instructions to minimize technician input; prescriptions selected via drop-down menus only
- **Variables**: # aligners, # attachments, IPR per arch, vertical movement maxillary central incisors, final canine & molar Class I relationship, intercanine & intermolar widths
- **Statistics**: Kruskal-Wallis + pairwise Wilcoxon (Bonferroni); chi-square; G*Power post-hoc analysis
- IRB: University of Maryland #HP-00102571

**Brand material/design overview:**

| Brand | Company | Release | Material | Thickness | Trim | Gingival height |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invisalign | Align Technology | 1998 | SmartTrack (multi-layer aromatic TPU) | 0.75 mm | Scalloped | At gingival margin |
| ClearCorrect | Straumann | 2009 | ClearQuartz (tri-layer: PETG/PU/PETG) | 0.75 mm | Flat | 2 mm above gingival margin |
| 3M Clarity | 3M | 2018 | Flex 0.625 mm / Force 0.75 mm (5-layer blend) | Dual | Flat | At gingival margin |
| Spark | Ormco | 2019 | TruGen / TruGen XR | 0.75 mm | Scalloped | At gingival margin |

## Results

| Parameter | Invisalign | ClearCorrect | 3M Clarity | Spark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Avg # aligners | ~25 | **19.7** | **32.7** | ~22 |
| Avg # attachments | ~12 | **9** | **17.8** | ~11 |
| Bilateral canine Class I | 65% | 15% | 70% | 20% |

**Pairwise significance (# aligners)**: ClearCorrect vs Invisalign p=0.050; ClearCorrect vs 3M Clarity p=0.023

**Pairwise significance (# attachments)**: ClearCorrect vs Invisalign p<0.001; ClearCorrect vs 3M Clarity p=0.002; ClearCorrect vs Spark p=0.005; 3M Clarity vs Spark p=0.025

**Final molar relationship**: All brands projected bilateral molar Class I in >90% of cases — no significant difference.

**IPR per arch**: No significant difference across brands. Exploratory VTS review found no consistent IPR placement pattern by tooth location — authors caution that software-driven IPR may not align with clinical need.

**Arch widths**: Intercanine width change 2.2–2.7 mm (similar across brands); intermolar width change 2.0 mm (similar). Note: 3M Clarity showed greater intermolar expansion vs Invisalign but not statistically significant. Authors flag that intercanine expansion >0 mm risks relapse.

## Clinical Takeaway

1. **ClearCorrect** plans the fewest aligners and attachments, aided by its 2 mm supragingival flat trim (increases stiffness and force near center of resistance, allowing attachment reduction). However, only 15% of ClearCorrect setups achieved bilateral canine Class I — clinicians must manually correct this.
2. **3M Clarity's** dual-thickness approach (Flex 0.625 mm for rotation/proclination, Force 0.75 mm for expansion/torque/intrusion) generates the most aligners. The staged material strategy is biomechanically unvalidated — published literature shows 0.625 vs 0.75 mm produce similar forces/moments.
3. **Spark** performs similarly to Invisalign for most parameters except canine Class I planning (20% vs 65%) — a potential selection factor when ideal Class I canine outcome is critical.
4. **All brands** plan intercanine expansion of 2.2–2.7 mm regardless of clinical need — clinicians should review and limit expansion in VTS to prevent relapse.
5. VTS ≠ clinical outcome. The paper is explicit: differences in planned setups do not necessarily translate to differences in clinical results, and refinements can compensate for planning deficiencies.
