## Three-line Summary

This RCT compared clear aligner therapy (CAT) vs conventional fixed appliances for deep bite malocclusion at a Vietnamese national hospital. CAT demonstrated significantly shorter treatment duration (20.77 ± 6.43 months vs 27.47 ± 4.81 months, P<.001) with comparable overbite improvement (P=.461) and overjet reduction (P=.603). Patient satisfaction was significantly higher in the CAT group across all categories (P<.001), while fixed appliances showed greater impact on vertical facial dimensions.

심부교합 환자에서 투명교정(CAT) 대 고정교정의 임상 효능과 환자 만족도를 비교한 베트남 국립병원 RCT. CAT는 유의하게 짧은 치료 기간(20.77±6.43개월 대 27.47±4.81개월, P<.001)을 보였으며, 심부교합 개선(P=.461)과 양악 겹침 감소(P=.603)는 두 그룹 간 유의한 차이 없음. 환자 만족도는 모든 범주에서 CAT 그룹이 유의하게 높았음(P<.001). 고정교정은 수직 안면 치수에 더 큰 영향을 미쳤음.

## Summary

This prospective RCT from Vietnam's National Hospital of Odonto-Stomatology compared clear aligner therapy with conventional fixed appliances for treating deep bite malocclusion. Both treatment modalities effectively corrected the deep bite, but clear aligners achieved this in a significantly shorter treatment duration with markedly higher patient satisfaction. The cephalometric analysis revealed interesting mechanistic differences: fixed appliances had greater impact on vertical facial dimensions (likely through molar intrusion), while clear aligners were associated with upper incisor proclination. This suggests the two approaches achieve deep bite correction through different biomechanical pathways.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT specifically comparing CAT vs fixed appliances for deep bite
- CAT offers shorter treatment duration (20.8 vs 27.5 months) with comparable outcomes
- Significantly higher patient satisfaction with CAT across all categories
- Cephalometric evidence of different biomechanical pathways (vertical control vs proclination)

## Methodology

Prospective RCT at National Hospital of Odonto-Stomatology, Hanoi. IRB: Institute of Clinical Medicine and Pharmacy 108 (No. 425/QD-VNC). Patients treated with either clear aligners or conventional braces. Outcomes: treatment duration, overbite, overjet, cephalometric changes, patient satisfaction questionnaire.

## Results

- Treatment duration: CAT 20.77 ± 6.43 mo vs Fixed 27.47 ± 4.81 mo (P<.001)
- Overbite improvement: NS (P=.461)
- Overjet reduction: NS (P=.603)
- Patient satisfaction: CAT higher all categories (P<.001)
- Vertical dimensions: fixed appliances greater impact
- Upper incisor proclination: CAT associated
