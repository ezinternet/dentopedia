## Three-line Summary

This SR with single-arm meta-analysis synthesized 34 studies (10 RCTs, 6 non-randomized, 18 cohort) on adverse effects of removable orthodontic aligners. Pain peaked within 24h, decreased by day 3, and was minimal by 1 week. Apical root resorption showed mean linear loss of −0.33 mm (95% CI −0.55 to −0.11) and volumetric loss of −4.37 mm³ (95% CI −5.51 to −3.24), with other reported outcomes including periodontal changes, WSLs, speech alterations, halitosis, and TMD symptoms.

## Summary

This is the largest systematic review to date examining adverse effects associated with removable orthodontic aligners. The single-arm meta-analysis quantified pain and root resorption outcomes across multiple studies. Pain follows a predictable pattern: peaking within the first 24 hours after aligner insertion/update, decreasing substantially by day 3, and becoming minimal by 1 week. Root resorption, while statistically quantifiable, appears clinically small (−0.33 mm linear). The comprehensive mapping of adverse effects — from common (pain, root resorption) to less frequently reported (speech changes, halitosis, TMD symptoms) — provides a valuable reference for clinical informed consent and patient counseling.

## Key Contributions

- Largest SR on aligner adverse effects (34 studies)
- First meta-analysis of aligner-related root resorption (linear and volumetric)
- Comprehensive mapping of all adverse effect categories
- Risk of bias assessment using RoB 2 and ROBINS-I tools

## Methodology

SR with single-arm meta-analysis. Seven databases (Cochrane, Embase, LILACS, Livivo, MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science) + gray literature. No language/date restrictions. Two calibrated reviewers. RoB 2 for RCTs, ROBINS-I for non-randomized. Meta-analysis for pain, root resorption, and plaque only (heterogeneity limited other outcomes). Data: OSF (doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/Z9HCG).

## Results

- 34 studies included (10 RCTs, 6 non-randomized, 18 cohort)
- Pain: peaked 24h, minimal by 1 week
- Root resorption: linear −0.33 mm (95% CI −0.55 to −0.11)
- Root resorption: volumetric −4.37 mm³ (95% CI −5.51 to −3.24)
- Plaque at 3 months: inconclusive
- RCTs: predominantly low ROB; others: moderate-high ROB
