## Three-line Summary

First split-mouth RCT (n=50 adults, 800 attachments, 140 days) comparing conventional flowable (3M Filtek Supreme Flowable) vs. bulk-fill flowable (3M Filtek Bulk Fill Flowable) composites for aligner attachment fabrication. Overall failure rate was 18.75%; Cox regression showed no significant difference between materials (HR=0.84, 95%CI 0.60–1.16, p=0.293). Molars had higher failure rates than premolars in both groups; regular flowable composites are a cost-effective equivalent to bulk-fill for attachment fabrication.

## Summary

This split-mouth RCT conducted at a private orthodontic practice in Brazil enrolled 50 adult patients with Class I mild crowding treated with Invisalign SmartTrack aligners. Each patient's right and left sides received different materials, allowing intra-individual comparison. 800 posterior attachments (4 per quadrant) were placed using either 3M Filtek Supreme Flowable or 3M Filtek Bulk Fill Flowable and evaluated over 10 aligner sets (14-day intervals, 140 total days). The overall failure rate was 18.75% (150 attachments). No significant difference was found between materials in survival rate, individual tooth analysis, or time-to-failure analysis. This study confirms that material choice (flowable vs. bulk-fill flowable) does not affect clinical attachment durability, providing clinicians with greater flexibility and potential cost savings.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT specifically comparing flowable vs. bulk-fill flowable composites for aligner attachment fabrication
- Split-mouth design: minimizes confounding from patient-level variation (strongest design for bilateral comparison)
- Clinically actionable: regular flowable composite (cheaper, more available) is interchangeable with bulk-fill flowable without compromising survival
- Identifies molar location as higher-risk for attachment failure, independent of material
- 140-day follow-up covering 10 aligner sets represents realistic short-course treatment evaluation

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-center split-mouth RCT; 1:1 allocation; CONSORT-reported
- **n=50** adults (21M/29F), mean age 37.86±6.90y, Class I, LII ≤2mm, complete permanent dentition
- **SF**: 3M Filtek Supreme Flowable (conventional flowable composite)
- **BF**: 3M Filtek Bulk Fill Flowable
- **Bonding**: 37% phosphoric acid etching + 3M Adper Single Bond 2 adhesive + Emitter D curing light
- **Aligners**: Invisalign SmartTrack; 10 aligners × 14 days = 140 days
- **Attachments**: 4 per quadrant, posterior only (14-17, 24-27, 34-37, 44-47)
- **Primary outcome**: attachment survival rate at end of aligner #10
- **Secondary**: time to failure (Cox regression, Kaplan-Meier at 30/60/90/120/140 days)
- **Blinding**: measurement evaluator blinded; operators and patients unblinded
- **Error study**: Dahlberg formula; no significant systematic errors
