## Three-line Summary

Comprehensive narrative review (AJODO 2006) updating the classical pressure-tension theory of orthodontic tooth movement with the cytokine and molecular signaling networks identified by late-20th-century research.

Pressure-side osteoclast activation (driven by IL-1β, TNF-α, PGE₂, substance P, CGRP, and the RANKL/OPG axis) drives bone resorption, while tension-side osteoblast activation drives new bone apposition; this duality operates at the cellular, molecular, and tissue levels simultaneously.

Optimal orthodontic force sustains productive bone remodeling without complete PDL vascular occlusion; heavy force causes hyalinization (necrotic PDL zone), delaying tooth movement until undermining resorption restores it.

## 세줄요약

고전적 압박-장력 이론을 사이토카인·분자 신호 네트워크로 갱신한 포괄적 narrative review (AJODO 2006).

압박측: IL-1β·TNF-α·PGE₂·Substance P·CGRP·RANKL/OPG 축 → 파골세포 활성 → 골흡수; 장력측: 조골세포 활성 → 신생골 형성; 세포·분자·조직 층위에서 동시 진행.

최적 교정력은 PDL 혈관 완전 폐색 없이 골개조를 유지; 과중한 힘은 초자화(hyalinization)를 초래해 치아이동이 지연됨.

## Summary
Comprehensive review integrating cellular, molecular, and tissue-level responses to orthodontic force: pressure-tension theory updated with cytokine and molecular signaling.

## Key Contributions
- Pressure side: osteoclast activation, resorption lacunae formation
- Tension side: osteoblast activation, new bone apposition
- Key signaling: IL-1β, TNF-α, PGE₂, substance P, CGRP, RANKL/OPG axis

## Methodology
Comprehensive narrative review of orthodontic biology literature.

## Results
Optimal orthodontic force induces bone remodeling without vascular occlusion. Hyalinization (cell-free necrotic PDL zone) occurs with heavy forces, delaying tooth movement.
