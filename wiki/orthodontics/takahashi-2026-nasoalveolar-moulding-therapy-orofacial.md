## Three-line Summary

Takahashi et al. (2026) registered a Cochrane protocol (CD016359) for a systematic review to assess the benefits and harms of nasoalveolar moulding (NAM) therapy compared to no presurgical orthopaedic treatment in children with orofacial clefts. NAM uses a customised intraoral appliance combined with a nasal stent to reshape nasoalveolar tissues before primary lip and palate repair surgery; it is widely used at specialist cleft centres but remains controversial due to limited high-quality comparative trials.

The protocol establishes the comparison framework: NAM therapy versus no presurgical orthopaedic treatment (PSO), with planned assessment of surgical outcomes, nasoalveolar symmetry, and patient/parent burden. This is the first Cochrane protocol specifically for NAM, authored by researchers at Kanagawa Dental University, Showa Medical University, and the University of Tokyo. The historical challenge in this area is conducting RCTs in neonatal populations, which has historically limited evidence to observational studies and expert opinion.

As of the protocol stage, no results are available. When completed, the full review will provide the highest-level evidence synthesis for a resource-intensive intervention that requires specialised equipment, frequent clinical appointments, and significant parental commitment in the first weeks of life, directly informing NAM recommendation in multidisciplinary cleft team pathways.

## 세줄요약

다카하시 등(2026)은 구순구개열(Orofacial Cleft) 소아에서 비치조 성형술(Nasoalveolar Moulding, NAM) 치료 대 무수술 전 정형치료(Presurgical Orthopaedic, PSO)를 비교하는 최초의 코크란 프로토콜(CD016359)을 등록했다. NAM은 맞춤형 구강내 장치와 비강 스텐트로 1차 구순·구개 수술 전 비치조 연조직을 재형성하는 기법으로, 전문 구순구개열 센터에서 광범위하게 사용되나 고품질 비교 근거가 부족하다.

수술 결과, 비순 대칭성, 환자/보호자 부담이 평가 예정 결과 지표다. 신생아 집단 RCT 수행의 윤리적·물류적 어려움이 역사적으로 근거를 관찰 연구와 전문가 의견에 의존하게 했다.

현재 프로토콜 단계로 결과가 없다. 완성 시 첫 수 주 이내에 집중적인 임상 방문과 보호자 헌신을 요구하는 자원 집약적 중재에 대한 최고 수준 근거 종합의 토대를 마련하며, 다학제 구순구개열 팀의 NAM 권고 여부 결정에 직접 활용될 것이다.

## Summary
> [!note] Protocol — No Results Yet
> This is a Cochrane Protocol (CD016359). The full review is currently in progress. This page will be updated when results are published.

This Cochrane protocol addresses NAM therapy — a presurgical orthopaedic (PSO) technique using a custom intraoral appliance plus nasal stent to reshape the nasal and alveolar tissues before primary surgical repair in neonates with orofacial clefts (cleft lip, cleft palate, or both). NAM is widely used at specialized cleft centres but remains controversial: proponents argue it reduces surgical complexity and improves nasal-lip symmetry, while critics note high resource demands, intensive parental commitment, and the lack of high-quality comparative trials. This protocol establishes the first Cochrane framework to evaluate NAM in a systematic and rigorous manner.

## Key Contributions
- First Cochrane protocol for NAM therapy in orofacial clefts
- Establishes comparison framework: NAM therapy vs no presurgical orthopaedic treatment
- Population covers both cleft lip and cleft palate variants
- Outcomes will include: surgical outcomes, nasal and lip symmetry, patient/parent burden
- Authors: Kanagawa Dental University; Showa Medical University; University of Tokyo

- Population: children with orofacial clefts (cleft lip, cleft palate, or both)
- Intervention: NAM therapy (intraoral appliance + nasal stent)
- Comparator: no presurgical orthopaedic treatment
