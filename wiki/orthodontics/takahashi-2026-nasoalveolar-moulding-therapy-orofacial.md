## Summary
> [!note] Protocol — No Results Yet
> This is a Cochrane Protocol (CD016359). The full review is currently in progress. This page will be updated when results are published.

This Cochrane protocol addresses NAM therapy — a presurgical orthopaedic (PSO) technique using a custom intraoral appliance plus nasal stent to reshape the nasal and alveolar tissues before primary surgical repair in neonates with orofacial clefts (cleft lip, cleft palate, or both). NAM is widely used at specialized cleft centres but remains controversial: proponents argue it reduces surgical complexity and improves nasal-lip symmetry, while critics note high resource demands, intensive parental commitment, and the lack of high-quality comparative trials. This protocol establishes the first Cochrane framework to evaluate NAM in a systematic and rigorous manner.

## Key Contributions
- First Cochrane protocol for NAM therapy in orofacial clefts
- Establishes comparison framework: NAM therapy vs no presurgical orthopaedic treatment
- Population covers both cleft lip and cleft palate variants
- Outcomes will include: surgical outcomes, nasal and lip symmetry, patient/parent burden
- Authors: Kanagawa Dental University; Showa Medical University; University of Tokyo
