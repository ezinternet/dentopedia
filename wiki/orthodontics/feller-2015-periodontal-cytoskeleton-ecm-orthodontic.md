## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of cytoskeletal and extracellular matrix (ECM) biomechanics in periodontal ligament (PDL) cell response to orthodontic force: actin cytoskeletal remodeling and ECM proteins (fibronectin, vitronectin, collagen I/III) transmit force via integrin-FAK-MAPK outside-in mechanotransduction, driving adaptive gene expression for bone remodeling.

Integrin-mediated outside-in signaling activates FAK and downstream MAPK pathways, converting mechanical strain in the PDL into transcriptional programs that recruit osteoclasts on the pressure side and osteoblasts on the tension side.

This mechanotransduction framework provides the molecular substrate underlying the cellular events observed in OTM animal models and the biomarker shifts measurable in gingival crevicular fluid during orthodontic treatment.

## 세줄요약
교정력에 반응하는 치주인대(PDL) 세포골격(actin) 및 세포외기질(ECM: fibronectin·vitronectin·collagen I/III) 생체역학 내러티브 리뷰 — integrin-FAK-MAPK outside-in 기계신호 전달이 골개조 유전자 발현을 구동.

압박측에서 integrin 활성화 → FAK 인산화 → MAPK 경로가 파골세포 모집을 유도하고, 장력측에서는 조골세포 활성화 프로그램이 작동하여 PDL 리모델링 발생.

이 기계신호 전달 프레임워크는 교정 동물 모델에서 관찰된 세포 사건과 교정 치료 중 GCF에서 측정되는 바이오마커(PGE2, MMP, RANKL/OPG) 변화의 분자적 기반을 제공.

## Summary
Review focusing on cytoskeletal and ECM biomechanics in PDL cell response to orthodontic force: integrin-mediated mechanotransduction.

## Key Contributions
- Actin cytoskeletal remodeling transmits mechanical force to nucleus
- ECM proteins (fibronectin, vitronectin, collagen I/III) expression changes under force
- Integrin-FAK outside-in signaling activates gene expression for bone remodeling

## Methodology
Narrative review.

## Results
PDL cell mechanotransduction via integrin-FAK-MAPK pathway drives adaptive gene expression for osteoclast/osteoblast recruitment.
