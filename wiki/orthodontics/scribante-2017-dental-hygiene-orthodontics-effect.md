## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (BioMed Research International, 2017) using 200 bovine mandibular incisors bonded with 4 different lingual orthodontic brackets (STB, TTR, Idea, 2D) plus a vestibular control bracket (Victory), testing the effect of ultrasonic instrumentation (UI, Mini Piezon G1 tip, 8 W, 15 s/side) on shear bond strength (SBS) and adhesive remnant index (ARI).
UI significantly reduced SBS in the three lingual brackets with smaller mesh/base area (TTR, Idea, 2D; p<0.05) — some post-UI values fell below the clinically accepted minimum bond strength of 6–8 MPa — while the vestibular control (Victory) and the large-base lingual bracket (STB) showed no significant SBS change; bracket base area correlated linearly with the magnitude of SBS reduction, and UI also raised ARI scores (more adhesive left on the bracket, less on tooth) for the same vulnerable brackets.
Clinical implication: dental hygienists should minimize ultrasonic instrumentation time/pressure directly on bracket bases of small-mesh-area lingual brackets to avoid unintended bond failure; key limitation is bovine (not human) enamel and a single scaler-tip/power protocol.

## Summary
This in vitro study addresses a practical intersection of dental hygiene and orthodontics: whether routine ultrasonic scaling around lingual (esthetic, tongue-side) orthodontic brackets can weaken their bond. Two hundred bovine mandibular incisors were bonded with four different lingual bracket systems (STB, TTR, Idea, 2D) plus a vestibular control (Victory), half of each group exposed to 1 minute of ultrasonic instrumentation (UI) with a piezoelectric scaler and G1 tip. Shear bond strength (SBS) and adhesive remnant index (ARI) were measured after debonding on a universal testing machine. UI significantly lowered SBS — in some cases below the 6–8 MPa clinically adequate threshold — for the three brackets with smaller base/mesh area (TTR, Idea, 2D), while the vestibular control and one large-base lingual bracket (STB) were unaffected; linear regression confirmed base area as the driving variable. The clinical takeaway for dental hygienists is procedural caution (limiting instrumentation time/pressure) specifically around small-base lingual brackets, rather than avoiding ultrasonic scaling in orthodontic patients altogether.

## Key Contributions
- Tests UI effect across **four different lingual bracket systems** plus a vestibular control, rather than a single bracket type as in prior single-device studies.
- Establishes a **linear relationship** between bracket base/mesh area and SBS reduction magnitude after UI: small-base brackets (TTR, Idea, 2D) lost significant bond strength; large-base brackets (Victory, STB) did not.
- Shows some post-UI SBS values fall **below the clinically accepted minimum bond strength (6–8 MPa)** — a real risk of unwanted bracket failure from routine hygiene procedures, not merely a statistical effect.
- UI also **increased ARI scores (2–3)** for the same vulnerable brackets — more adhesive remained on the bracket base rather than the tooth, a failure mode associated with lower enamel-fracture risk at eventual debonding.

## Methodology
- 200 bovine mandibular incisors, randomized into 10 groups (n=20): 5 bracket types × {UI, no-UI control}.
- Brackets (all for maxillary central incisors, bonded to bovine buccal surface for test repeatability): STB (Ormco), TTR (Rocky Mountain Orthodontics), Idea (Leone), 2D (Forestadent) — lingual; Victory (3M) — vestibular control.
- Standard acid-etch bonding protocol (37% orthophosphoric acid, Transbond XT primer/resin, light cure).
- UI: Mini Piezon (EMS) piezoelectric scaler, G1 tip, 8 W, 0° tip angulation, 15 s/side (1 min/bracket total), water coolant, single operator.
- Outcome measures: SBS (Instron universal testing machine, shear mode, converted N→MPa) and ARI (stereomicroscope ×20, 0–3 scale).
- Stats: two-way ANOVA + Tukey, linear regression (base area vs. SBS reduction), chi-square (ARI distribution), α=0.05.

## Results
| Bracket (type) | Base/mesh area | Effect of UI on SBS | Effect of UI on ARI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Victory (vestibular control) | Large, sandblasted | No significant change | No significant change |
| STB (lingual) | Large, irregular mesh | No significant change | No significant change |
| TTR (lingual) | Small, narrow/irregular grooves | Significant decrease (p<0.05) | Significant increase to 2–3 |
| Idea (lingual) | Small, conventional 80-gauge mesh | Significant decrease (p<0.05) | Significant increase to 2–3 |
| 2D (lingual) | Small, 45° retention-wire angle | Significant decrease (p<0.05) | Significant increase to 2–3 |

Overall SBS range across all groups: ~3–30 MPa (clinically adequate minimum cited: 6–8 MPa; theoretical ceiling to avoid enamel damage: ~40–50 MPa).
