## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (189 patients, 672 lithium disilicate LVs, single operator, 1–15 years follow-up, Strasbourg) quantified the impact of dentin exposure in three tiers (GA1 enamel-only / GA2 <30% dentin / GA3 >30% dentin) on veneer survival; 15-year cumulative estimated survival 96%, observed 98.4%.

Dentin exposure significantly increased failure risk: GA1 vs. GA3 (p=0.033), OR=4.67 for failure with >30% dentin; endodontically treated teeth (ETT, n=38) showed survival 95.0% vs. 96.0% vital (p=0.289, NOT significant), OR=1.68.

Enamel preservation is the primary survival determinant of ceramic LVs; ETT does not significantly impair survival, but OR=1.68 warrants careful case selection, and esthetic complications from discoloration of ETT require separate management.

## Summary

Etienne et al. (2025) performed a 15-year retrospective single-center study of 672 lithium disilicate ceramic laminate veneers placed by a single clinician (OE) and fabricated by a single ceramist (DW), followed in 189 patients (mean age 47). Veneers were categorized based on post-etching photographs into GA1 (enamel only, n=290), GA2 (<30% dentin, n=306), and GA3 (>30% dentin, n=76). ETT (n=38) were not excluded. The overall 15-year estimated survival was 96% (observed 98.4%, 11 failures). Dentin exposure >30% significantly increased failure (GA1 vs. GA3, p=0.033, OR=4.67). ETT survival (95.0%) was not significantly different from vital teeth (96.0%, p=0.289), though OR=1.68 indicates a modestly elevated risk. The primary esthetic complications in ETT arose from underlying discoloration rather than adhesive failure.

## Key Contributions

- Largest single-operator retrospective with up to 15-year follow-up for ceramic LVs (n=672)
- First study to tier dentin exposure risk with explicit OR values: GA1→GA3 OR=4.67
- Confirms ETT is not a significant predictor of LV failure in modern adhesive era (p=0.289)
- Demonstrated newer amine-free dual-cure cement (GC LinkForce) performed at 99.6% vs. VL 95.5% — although difference NS, trend favors modern systems
- Esthetic complications in ETT (discoloration) attributed to masking challenge, not bonding failure

## Methodology

Single-center retrospective study (University of Strasbourg, France); 189 patients, 672 LVs (incisors + canines, maxilla + mandible); all veneers lithium disilicate IPS e.max Press; single operator + ceramist. Dentin exposure quantified via ImageJ analysis of post-etching photographs. ETT identified by radiographs. Preparation: APT mock-up technique with depth-gauge burs. Two cement systems used across the study period: VL (Variolink II, ExciTE F DSC, 2001–2015) and GC (G-CEM LinkForce + G-Premio BOND, 2015–2023). Follow-up 1–180 months (mean 5.98 years). Kaplan-Meier with Log-Rank (Mantel-Cox) pairwise comparison; OR calculated for failure by dentin exposure and ETT status.

## Results

- **Observed survival**: 98.4% (11 failures / 672 veneers)
- **15-year estimated survival**: 96%
- **By dentin exposure**:
  - GA1 (enamel only): 96.7% estimated, 2 failures (99.3% observed)
  - GA2 (<30% dentin): 95.3% estimated, 5 failures
  - GA3 (>30% dentin): 93.9% estimated, 4 failures (84.7% observed)
  - GA1 vs. GA3: p=0.033 (significant)
- **OR for failure**: Any dentin 3.47; >30% dentin 4.67; ETT 1.68
- **ETT**: NVT 95.0% vs. VT 96.0%, p=0.289 (NOT significant)
- **Preparation design**: Butt-margin vs. incisal overlap — no significant difference
- **Cement**: VL 95.4% vs. GC 99.6% (no significant difference)
- **Failure types**: Fracture/chipping 5, debonding 1, tooth loss 1, esthetic 3, sensitivity 1
