## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (case reports 1980-2024, single database) plus one clinical case: 27-year-old woman, 10 hand-crafted lithium disilicate (LDS) veneers (second premolar to second premolar) bonded under rubber dam, 2-year follow-up.

The 8 case reports reviewed (Table 2) all use hydrofluoric acid (5-10%, 20 s to 1 min) then silane on the ceramic and phosphoric-acid etch plus adhesive on the tooth; case protocol: HF 20 s, silane 60 s, adhesive, 29-micron Al2O3 sandblast, 37% phosphoric acid 15 s, light-cure cement (Variolink Esthetic LC), 20 s per surface.

Evidence is qualitative only (no meta-analysis, PRISMA not followed, satisfaction self-reported, n=1, 2-year follow-up); the practical message is to follow manufacturer times, minimize dentin exposure, and bond under total (preferably non-latex) rubber-dam isolation.

## Summary

Rojas-Rueda et al. (2025, Biomimetics) present a brief narrative review of published case reports describing every bonding step for labial lithium disilicate (LDS) veneers, plus one clinical case bonded under rubber dam. The authors argue that bonding to enamel outperforms bonding to dentin, so minimally invasive preparation and full isolation are central, and that manufacturer instructions should be followed because application times vary between products.

## Key Contributions

- Side-by-side table of the bonding protocols in 8 case reports (2012-2024): HF concentration 5-10% and time 20 s to 1 min, silane 60 s (or self-etching Monobond Etch and Prime), phosphoric-acid etch of enamel 15-60 s, then adhesive and resin cement.
- Table of LDS veneer survival evidence (5 sources, 89.1% to 99.83%).
- A worked clinical workflow: diagnostic wax-up, intra-oral mock-up, preparation through the mock-up, hand-crafted veneers, dry and paste try-in, rubber-dam bonding, night guard, 2-year recall.

## Methodology

- Narrative review: MEDLINE (PubMed) and Google, January 1980 to December 2024, English; inclusion of case reports of labial LDS veneers describing all bonding steps; excluded in vitro, retrospective, reviews, implant-supported cases. Two reviewers, third for disagreements. No quantitative analysis, no PRISMA.
- Case: 27-year-old woman, 10 hand-crafted IPS e.max Ceram veneers (second premolar to second premolar), butt-joint preparation, PVS impression.
- Bonding sequence: ceramic - HF (Porcelain Etch) 20 s, silane (Monobond Plus) 60 s, OptiBond FL adhesive; tooth - rubber dam, water/29-micron Al2O3 sandblast, 37% phosphoric acid 15 s, OptiBond FL; cement - Variolink Esthetic LC, 20 s cure per facial, interproximal, mesial and distal surface.

## Results

- Case reports (Table 2) show minor differences in ceramic cleaning and in whether primer and adhesive are combined; all describe good short-term outcomes (follow-up 1 year in Schmitter 2012 and 2 years in Souza 2020; others report clinical acceptability or satisfaction).
- Survival data (Table 3, as tabulated by the authors): Klein 2021 SR+MA 96.8% at 10.4 years; Malchiodi 2019 98.7% (79 veneers, ~3 years); Imburgia 2021 99.83% (1075 veneers, 105 patients, 30.8 months); Nejatidanesh 2018 97.8% at 5 years; Sen 2024 89.1% (Section 4.2 text says 92.7% - internal inconsistency).
- HF times by ceramic (Table 4): feldspathic 9.5% for 2-2.5 min; leucite 60 s; lithium disilicate 20 s.
- Case: patient satisfied with contour, shape and shade; satisfied at 2 years; restorations and surrounding tissue judged to meet esthetic and functional needs (no quantitative outcome measure).
- Discussion points: bonding to enamel exceeds bonding to dentin; reduction guides give more precise, less invasive preparation; 1.2 mm LDS veneers masked discolored substrate in an in vitro study cited by the authors; light-cured cement preferred for veneers (working time, color stability); non-latex dam advised; a 2016 Cochrane review (direct restorations) associates rubber dam with lower failure.

## Limitations (appraisal)

Narrative, one database plus Google, no risk-of-bias appraisal, no comparison of protocols; single case with subjective outcome and 2-year follow-up. Not a source of comparative efficacy claims - use for procedural description, and use [[veneers/hajeer-2026-lithium-disilicate-zirconia-veneer-retention-sr-ma]] and [[veneers/klein-2025-ceramic-laminate-veneer-survival-complications-sr-ma]] for quantitative evidence.
