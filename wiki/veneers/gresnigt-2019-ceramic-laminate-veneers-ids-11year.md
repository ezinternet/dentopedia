## Three-line Summary

Prospective 11-year clinical trial (104 patients, 384 feldspathic ceramic LVs, Groningen) with four pre-specified subgroups: no prior restoration, pre-existing composite, >50% dentin exposure (IDS applied), and endodontically treated teeth (ETT, n=43) — the first prospective long-term study to include ETT without exclusion.

IDS significantly improved survival in teeth with >50% dentin exposure (96.4% vs. 81.8%, significant); vital vs. ETT survival was 95.6% vs. 88.1% (NOT significant); 19 failures total (debonding n=3, fracture n=15, extraction n=1).

ETT is NOT a contraindication for ceramic laminate veneers by survival criteria, but presents a clinically significant challenge of color mismatch versus adjacent vital teeth; IDS is mandatory when dentin exposure exceeds 50%.

## Summary

Gresnigt et al. (2019) conducted an 11-year prospective clinical trial at the University of Groningen evaluating 384 feldspathic ceramic laminate veneers placed on maxillary anterior teeth in 104 patients. Unlike most prior studies, endodontically treated teeth (ETT, n=43) were deliberately included rather than excluded. Four subgroups were pre-specified: teeth without prior restorations (n=225), teeth with pre-existing composite resin (n=159), teeth with >50% dentin exposure during preparation where IDS was applied (n=87), and ETT (n=43). The primary finding was that IDS significantly improved survival when dentin exposure exceeded 50% (96.4% vs. 81.8%). No significant survival difference was found between vital (95.6%) and endodontically treated teeth (88.1%). ETT did, however, show significantly greater color mismatch compared to vital teeth.

## Key Contributions

- First prospective trial of >10 years that includes ETT without exclusion — establishing clinical acceptability
- IDS proven to substantially benefit teeth with major dentin exposure (>50%), a landmark finding for clinical protocol
- ETT survival (88.1%) numerically lower but statistically non-significant vs. vital (95.6%)
- Color mismatch is the primary clinical concern for ETT, not survival
- Pre-existing composite restorations do not significantly affect LV survival if properly conditioned (silica coating + silanization)

## Methodology

Prospective clinical trial (NCT03645551); 104 patients, mean age 42.1 years; 384 feldspathic ceramic LVs on maxillary anterior teeth; incisal overlap preparation using mock-up technique. IDS applied when >50% dentin exposed during preparation. Existing composites were silica-coated and silanized rather than removed. ETT not excluded. Outcome assessment by calibrated evaluators using modified USPHS criteria at baseline and 11-year follow-up.

## Results

- 19 total failures: debonding n=3, fracture n=15, extraction (endodontic) n=1
- IDS benefit (>50% dentin): 96.4% vs. 81.8% survival (statistically significant)
- Vital vs. ETT: 95.6% vs. 88.1% (NOT significant)
- Pre-existing composite vs. none: 84.6% vs. 95.5% (NOT significant)
- ETT: significantly more color mismatch vs. vital teeth
- Smokers: significantly more marginal discoloration (intervention not required)
- OHIP patient satisfaction: favorable across all subgroups
