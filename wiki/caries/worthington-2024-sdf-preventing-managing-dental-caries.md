## One-line Summary
Cochrane SR (29 RCTs, 13,036 participants, pub2): SDF probably prevents new root caries vs placebo (moderate certainty, MD -0.79 surfaces); evidence uncertain for most other outcomes; staining impact poorly reported.

## 한줄요약

## Summary
This Cochrane SR is the most comprehensive evaluation of silver diamine fluoride (SDF) for caries prevention and management, including 29 RCTs with 13,036 participants (predominantly children, with 1016 older adults for root caries studies). SDF is a low-cost topical liquid that can be applied by dentists or trained personnel, but permanently stains treated surfaces black or dark brown. The only moderate-certainty finding is that SDF probably prevents new root caries compared to placebo (MD -0.79 surfaces, 95% CI -1.40 to -0.17). For most other outcomes — including prevention of new caries in primary/coronal permanent teeth, caries arrest, and comparisons with fluoride varnish — the evidence is of very low certainty. All 29 studies had high risks of bias, and the staining impact on patient acceptability was poorly reported.

## Key Contributions
- SDF probably prevents new root caries vs placebo: MD -0.79 surfaces (95% CI -1.40 to -0.17), moderate certainty
- SDF may help arrest caries in primary dentition vs placebo: MD 0.86 surfaces (0.39–1.33), low certainty
- SDF vs fluoride varnish: may be little/no difference for primary dentition caries prevention (MD 0.00, low certainty)
- Cannot determine optimal SDF application approach (frequency, concentration, duration)
- Critical gap: staining (black discoloration) not adequately evaluated for patient acceptability

## Methodology
- 29 RCTs: 12,020 children, 1016 older adults; evidence to June 2023
- All studies had high risks of bias
- 5 comparison groups: vs placebo, different SDF approaches, vs fluoride varnish, vs sealants/resin infiltration, vs ART + GIC
- Outcomes: caries prevention, arrest, secondary prevention, adverse effects, dental pain, aesthetics
- Certainty: GRADE

## Results
**SDF vs placebo/no treatment (key outcomes):**
| Outcome | Population | Effect | Certainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Root caries prevention | Older adults | MD -0.79 surfaces (95% CI -1.40 to -0.17) | Moderate |
| Primary dentition caries prevention | Children | Possibly helpful | Very low |
| Primary dentition caries arrest | Children | MD 0.86 (0.39–1.33) | Low |
| Coronal permanent prevention | Children | Possibly helpful | Very low |
| All other outcomes | — | Very uncertain | Very low |

**SDF vs fluoride varnish:**
- Primary dentition caries prevention: MD 0.00 (95% CI -0.26 to 0.26); Low certainty
