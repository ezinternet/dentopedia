## Three-line Summary

QLF (QrayPen C) and NIRI (iTero Element 5D) were compared against ICCMS bitewing radiography across 297 posterior proximal sites in 66 adults using three pre-specified radiographic depth thresholds. Both modalities showed only moderate AUC (~0.73) for the any-caries threshold (enamel lesions) but high accuracy for dentin-level thresholds (AUC 0.90–0.98). NIRI was statistically superior to QLF only at the deeper-dentin threshold (AUC 0.978 vs 0.961, DeLong p=0.036); substantial inter-method agreement was observed (κ=0.707).

## 세줄요약

양광유발형광 (Quantitative Light-Induced Fluorescence, QLF, QrayPen C)과 근적외선영상 (Near-Infrared Imaging, NIRI, iTero Element 5D)을 국제우식분류관리시스템 (International Caries Classification and Management System, ICCMS) 방사선 기준 3단계 임계값에 따라 인접면 우식 (proximal caries) 297개 부위에서 비교한 진단정확도 연구. 초기 법랑질 우식 임계값에서는 두 방법 모두 곡선하면적 (Area Under Curve, AUC) 약 0.73의 보통 수준 정확도를 보였으나, 상아질 침범 및 심부 상아질 임계값에서는 AUC 0.90–0.98의 높은 정확도를 기록하였다. 심부 상아질 임계값에서만 근적외선영상이 양광유발형광보다 통계적으로 유의하게 우수하였으며(DeLong p=0.036), 두 방법의 전반적 일치도는 κ=0.707로 상당한 수준이었다.

## Summary

This cross-sectional diagnostic accuracy study (STARD 2015) compared two non-ionizing optical adjuncts — QLF (QrayPen C, 0–3 QS score) and NIRI (iTero Element 5D, 0–3 NIRI score) — against ICCMS bitewing radiography as the reference standard for proximal caries detection.

The study enrolled 66 adults (mean age 28.1y, ASA I–II) at Marmara University, Istanbul, Turkey (July 2025 – January 2026). One quadrant per participant was assessed, yielding 297 posterior proximal sites. The ICCMS reference classified sites as sound (R0, 68.0%), enamel caries (R1–R2, 20.9%), outer dentin caries (R3, 5.1%), and deeper dentin caries (R4–R6, 6.1%).

Three pre-specified thresholds were analyzed:
1. **Any caries** (R0 vs R1–R6): the most clinically challenging — bitewing radiography explicitly under-detects enamel lesions at this threshold
2. **Dentin involvement** (R0–R2 vs R3–R6): the clinically pivotal treatment-planning threshold
3. **Radiographically deeper dentin** (R0–R3 vs R4–R6): advanced lesion detection

Both methods performed similarly at the any-caries and dentin-involvement thresholds (no significant AUC difference). At the deeper-dentin threshold, NIRI was statistically superior (DeLong p=0.036). QLF maintained higher specificity for early lesions (88.1%), while NIRI demonstrated higher sensitivity for deeper lesions (83.3%).

A key methodological acknowledgment: the reference standard (bitewing radiography) is itself imperfect — it under-detects early enamel demineralization, which means AUC values at the any-caries threshold likely understate the true diagnostic performance of both optical methods.

## Key Contributions

1. **First in vivo head-to-head QLF vs NIRI comparison at ICCMS-defined thresholds**: establishes a robust paired comparison framework tied to clinically meaningful radiographic depth categories.
2. **Threshold-dependent performance**: high AUC at dentin thresholds (0.90–0.98) contrasts with moderate AUC at the enamel threshold (~0.73), clarifying where each modality adds clinical value.
3. **NIRI statistically superior at deeper dentin only** (DeLong p=0.036): for the most advanced lesion category, NIRI (AUC 0.978, Sens 83.3%) outperforms QLF (AUC 0.961, Sens 72.2%).
4. **QLF more specific for early lesions**: QS ≥1 specificity 88.1% vs NIRI 78.2% — QLF reduces false positives at the any-caries threshold.
5. **Substantial inter-method agreement** (Spearman ρ=0.735, κ=0.707): supports clinical interchangeability except at the deepest threshold.
6. **Imperfect reference standard acknowledged**: bitewing under-detection of enamel lesions biases the any-caries results downward — a limitation that also applies to all prior studies using radiography as the sole reference.

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Cross-sectional diagnostic accuracy (STARD 2015) |
| Setting | Marmara University, Istanbul, Turkey |
| Recruitment | July 2025 – January 2026 |
| Sample | 66 adults, 297 proximal sites (one quadrant/participant) |
| Mean age | 28.1 years; ASA I–II |
| Index test 1 | QrayPen C (QLF), QS score 0–3, AIOBIO, Seoul |
| Index test 2 | iTero Element 5D (NIRI), NIRI score 0–3, Align Technology |
| Reference standard | ICCMS bitewing radiography (R0–R6 → 4 categories) |
| AUC comparison | DeLong method (paired samples) |
| Agreement | Spearman correlation, linear-weighted kappa |

## Results

**Diagnostic accuracy by threshold and method**:

| Threshold | Method | AUC | Sensitivity | Specificity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Any caries (R0 vs R1–R6) | QLF (QS ≥1) | 0.729 | 54.7% | 88.1% |
| Any caries (R0 vs R1–R6) | NIRI (≥1) | 0.727 | 61.1% | 78.2% |
| Dentin involvement (R0–R2 vs R3–R6) | QLF (QS ≥2) | 0.909 | 81.8% | 95.5% |
| Dentin involvement (R0–R2 vs R3–R6) | NIRI (≥2) | 0.896 | 81.8% | 93.9% |
| Deeper dentin (R0–R3 vs R4–R6) | QLF (QS =3) | 0.961 | 72.2% | 96.4% |
| Deeper dentin (R0–R3 vs R4–R6) | NIRI (=3) | **0.978** | **83.3%** | 97.1% |

**AUC comparison (DeLong)**:

| Threshold | p-value | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Any caries | 0.902 | No significant difference |
| Dentin involvement | 0.605 | No significant difference |
| Deeper dentin | **0.036** | NIRI statistically superior |

**Inter-method agreement**: Spearman ρ=0.735 (p<0.001); linear-weighted κ=0.707 (substantial)

**Clinical interpretation**:
- At the dentin-involvement threshold (the treatment-planning threshold), both methods perform near-equivalently and well (AUC ~0.90) — either can be used as an adjunct
- At the deeper-dentin threshold, NIRI offers a meaningful sensitivity advantage (83.3% vs 72.2%) with equivalent specificity
- At the any-caries (enamel) threshold, neither method is accurate enough to replace clinical judgment; QLF's higher specificity (88.1%) makes it the better choice when avoiding false positives is prioritized
- Neither method replaces bitewing radiography when ICCMS staging is clinically required
