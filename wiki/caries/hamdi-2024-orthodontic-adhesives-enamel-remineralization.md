## Three-line Summary
PRISMA/PROSPERO SR of 24 in vitro/in situ studies evaluating remineralization and anti-demineralization effects of bioactive-filler or fluoride-releasing orthodontic adhesives on bracket-adjacent enamel.

All included studies (hardness, micro-CT, PLM) found significant remineralization for experimental adhesives vs controls; bioactive glass (BAG), n-HAP, n-ACP, nCaF2 were the main active fillers.

Evidence is promising but weak: 15/24 studies high risk of bias, no meta-analysis feasible due to heterogeneity; clinical translation requires studies with >28-day pH cycling and standardized protocols.

## 세줄요약
PRISMA/PROSPERO SR (in vitro 24편): 생체활성 필러(BAG·n-HAP·n-ACP·n-AFCP·nCaF2) 또는 불소방출 교정 접착제의 브라켓 주위 법랑질 재광화·탈회억제 효과 평가.

경도·micro-CT·PLM 전 연구에서 실험 접착제가 대조군보다 유의하게 재광화·탈회억제; BAG(솔-겔형), Fuji Ortho LC, Transbond Plus 등이 주요 수행자.

근거는 유망하나 약함(15/24 고비뚤림, 이질성으로 메타분석 불가); 임상 적용에는 28일 이상 pH 순환 및 표준화 연구가 필요.

## Summary
BMC Oral Health 2024 SR (Egypt) answering "Do orthodontic adhesives remineralize or prevent demineralization of enamel around brackets?". From 1712 hits (2005–2024), 24 in vitro/in situ studies were included. The strategy is to make the adhesive itself therapeutic — loading it with bioactive/remineralizing fillers so it counteracts the white-spot lesions (WSLs) that arise at the plaque-retentive bracket margin (reported WSL prevalence 33.8–97%).

## Key Contributions
- **Additive landscape**: 13 studies bioactive glass (BAG; some Ag/Zn/F-doped), 2 n-ACP, 1 n-HAP, 1 nCaF2, 1 n-AFCP, 1 ammonium-methacrylate, plus 4 combination systems; 7 evaluated commercial adhesives.
- **Consistent remineralization signal**: every cross-sectional-hardness (7), micro-CT (9) and PLM (4) study found significant remineralization extent for the experimental/additive adhesives vs control. BAG mechanism = Ca/PO4/Si release → silica-rich layer → HA nucleation (sol-gel BAG preferred). Commercial performers: Fuji Ortho LC (RMGIC, F release to 6 mo), Transbond Plus, Quick Cure.
- **Methodology framework**: classifies assessment into remineralization extent (micro-CT/PLM/TMR/cross-sectional hardness), superficial mineralization (surface hardness/DIAGNOdent/ΔE photography), chemical (XRD/FTIR/Raman/EDS/XPS), and micromorphology (SEM/AFM); recommends micro-CT + XRD/Raman + AFM.

## Methodology
PRISMA-guided, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42024574945) SR; PubMed + Scopus + Web of Science, 2005–2024. PECOS eligibility, restricted to in vitro/in situ studies with an ideal chemical (mostly pH-cycling, acetate buffer pH ~4.3–5) or biofilm cariogenic challenge. No meta-analysis (heterogeneity).

## Results
- Bioactive-filler and fluoride-releasing adhesives significantly out-remineralized/anti-demineralized controls across techniques.
- Ion release (8 studies): sustained fluoride (Fuji Ortho LC 6 mo; nCaF2 56 d) and Ca/P (up to 42 d), though tests were short and used non-clinical disc geometry.
- **Evidence caveat**: 15/24 high risk of bias, 19/24 no sample-size calculation, 14/24 pH-cycling shorter than the ~23–28 d needed to mimic clinical remineralization; conclusion is "promising but weak."
