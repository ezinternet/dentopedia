## One-line Summary
Cochrane SR (157 non-randomised studies, pub3): contemporary (post-1975) evidence on community water fluoridation is very limited and very low certainty; older pre-1975 evidence suggests benefit but may not apply to current dental care contexts.

## 한줄요약

## Summary
This Cochrane SR is the most comprehensive evaluation of community water fluoridation (CWF) for caries prevention, covering 157 non-randomised studies. The review makes a critical distinction between pre-1975 evidence (conducted before widespread fluoride toothpaste adoption) and post-1975 evidence (contemporary context). Pre-1975 evidence consistently suggests fluoridated water reduces caries prevalence (DMFT and caries-free proportions), but this evidence is very low certainty by modern standards and may not apply to contemporary dental care environments with widespread topical fluoride access. Contemporary (post-1975) evidence is sparse and has very low certainty due to severe methodological limitations. Dental fluorosis (white spots or mottling on teeth) is associated with fluoridation across all time periods. The review highlights a fundamental evidence gap: no high-quality contemporary trials of CWF exist.

## Key Contributions
- Pre-1975 evidence suggests CWF reduces DMFT (mean difference ~1.8) and increases caries-free proportions — but very low certainty
- Post-1975 (contemporary) evidence is very sparse and very low certainty — cannot draw meaningful conclusions
- Dental fluorosis: consistently associated with CWF across time periods (very low certainty on magnitude)
- Critical context: pre-1975 trials conducted before fluoride toothpaste was widely available; results may not transfer to current dental care environment
- Policy implication: existing CWF programs rest primarily on historical pre-1975 evidence

## Methodology
- 157 non-randomised studies; evidence to August 2023
- Pre-1975 vs post-1975 stratification as primary analytical distinction
- Study designs: primarily ecological, cohort, before-after
- Outcomes: DMFT, proportion caries-free, dental fluorosis prevalence/severity
- Certainty: GRADE (all outcomes very low)
- Limitations: no RCTs; ecological confounders; secular trends in background fluoride exposure; impossible to blind

## Results
**Pre-1975 studies:**
| Outcome | Direction | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| DMFT reduction | Favours CWF (MD ~1.8) | Very low |
| Proportion caries-free | Favours CWF | Very low |
| Dental fluorosis | Associated with CWF | Very low |

**Post-1975 (contemporary) studies:**
| Outcome | Direction | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| DMFT | Insufficient data, inconsistent | Very low |
| Dental fluorosis | Associated with CWF | Very low |
