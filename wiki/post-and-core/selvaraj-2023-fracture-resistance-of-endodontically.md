## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42021295212) of 18 in-vitro studies on fracture resistance of endodontically treated posterior teeth restored with fiber-reinforced composites (FRC) vs. multiple comparators; meta-analysis precluded by high heterogeneity.

FRC materials (EverX Posterior, Ribbond, Vectris) showed higher fracture resistance than conventional hybrid/nanohybrid composites in 15 of 16 studies (strong evidence); evidence vs. bulk fill was limited/conflicting, vs. fiber posts and inlays moderate, and no data existed for endocrowns.

Most included studies carry moderate-to-high risk of bias and use static in-vitro loading, limiting direct clinical translation; prospective RCTs comparing FRC to endocrowns and bulk fill are still absent.

PRISMA·PROSPERO(CRD42021295212) 기반 체계적 문헌고찰(SR): 근관치료된 구치부 치아의 파절저항에 대해 섬유강화복합레진 (Fiber-Reinforced Composite, FRC) vs 다양한 비교대상 18편 인비트로 연구 종합; 높은 이질성으로 메타분석 불가.

## Summary

This 2023 BMC Oral Health systematic review is the first SR to synthesize in-vitro fracture-resistance evidence specifically for fiber-reinforced composite (FRC) restorations in endodontically treated posterior teeth, searching five databases from January 2000 to May 2023 and including 18 studies (796 initially retrieved). The primary finding is strong evidence — 15 of 16 studies with p<0.05 — that FRC materials (principally EverX Posterior and Ribbond) outperform conventional hybrid and nanohybrid composites in fracture resistance. Evidence against bulk-fill composites was limited and conflicting, while comparisons against fiber posts, ceramic inlays, and amalgam provided only moderate evidence favoring FRC. No study compared FRC-restored teeth to endocrowns. High heterogeneity across tooth type, cavity geometry, fiber placement, and testing parameters precluded meta-analysis; most studies carried moderate-to-high risk of bias, constraining clinical generalizability.

## Key Contributions

- First systematic review dedicated to fracture resistance of FRC materials in endodontically treated posterior teeth.
- Stratified evidence synthesis across seven comparator categories: conventional hybrid composites, bulk fill, fiber posts, inlays, endocrowns, amalgam, and silorane composites.
- Material-specific ranking: EverX Posterior consistently outperformed Ribbond in head-to-head comparisons (3 studies); Ribbond outperformed Vectris in limited data.
- Risk of bias assessed via 13-parameter modified JBI & CRIS tool, revealing widespread reporting deficiencies.
- Identified complete evidence gap for FRC vs. endocrown — an increasingly common clinical decision point.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA); PROSPERO CRD42021295212.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar, LILACS + manual reference search; January 2000 – May 2023.
- **Inclusion**: In-vitro studies only; fully formed extracted human teeth, endodontically treated; FRC as intervention; fracture resistance measured by universal testing machine.
- **Exclusion**: Non-English, anterior incisal fracture simulations, fiber on occlusal surface only, FRC as substrate for inlay/onlay, crowns without cavity specification.
- **Studies included**: 18 (qualitative synthesis); meta-analysis not feasible due to high heterogeneity.
- **Risk of bias**: Two independent reviewers; 13-parameter checklist (JBI & CRIS).

## Results

| Comparison | Studies (n) | Outcome | Evidence Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| FRC vs. conventional hybrid/nanohybrid composite | 16 | FRC > conventional in 15 studies (p<0.05); 3 no significant difference | **Strong** |
| FRC vs. bulk fill composite | 5 | Conflicting: 2 FRC>bulk fill, 1 SDR>FRC, 1 no difference | **Limited/Conflicting** |
| FRC vs. fiber posts | Limited | EverX Posterior > Ribbond (fiber-ribbon type) in packable comparisons | **Moderate** |
| FRC vs. ceramic inlays | Limited | Moderate evidence for comparable or lower FRC performance | **Moderate** |
| FRC vs. amalgam | 1 | FRC > amalgam; amalgam failures = root fractures (unfavorable mode) | **Moderate** |
| FRC vs. endocrowns | 0 | No comparative data | **None** |
| FRC vs. silorane composites | 1 | FRC > silorane | **Insufficient** |

### Material-Specific Summary

| FRC Material | Studies | Key Finding |
|---|---|---|
| EverX Posterior (packable short e-glass fiber) | 13 | Higher fracture resistance vs. conventional composites in 8/13; consistently outperformed Ribbond in 3 head-to-head studies |
| Ribbond (UHMWPE polyethylene fiber ribbon) | 9 | Increased fracture resistance vs. conventional hybrid and Vectris; inconsistent vs. other FRC types |
| Vectris / Interlig | 2–3 each | Limited data; EverX Posterior > Interlig in one comparison |
