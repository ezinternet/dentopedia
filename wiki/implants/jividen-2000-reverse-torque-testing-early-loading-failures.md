## Three-line Summary

Review (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2000) questioning reverse torque testing as a screen for early loading failure.

The authors argue that its shear loading may cause early crestal bone loss and implant failure, especially in low-density bone, and they list unevaluated variables (density, rotation, size/design).

They introduce a failure nomenclature; the abstract has no quantitative failure data.

## Summary

Jividen and Misch treat reverse torque testing as a possible source of the very early failures it is meant to catch. The useful contribution is the critique and the failure nomenclature.

## Key Contributions

- Critique of reverse torque testing.
- Proposed nomenclature for implant failures.
