## Three-line Summary

Preliminary multicentre clinical report (Implant Dentistry, 1998) on a bone-quality-based implant system with four density-specific designs.

364 consecutive implants placed in five centres reached 98.9% overall surgical survival, and the authors state that all bone densities may have similar initial survival.

The report is preliminary, with no comparator arm or long-term follow-up in the abstract; do not use it as a long-term benchmark.

## 세줄요약

예비 다기관 임상 보고 (Implant Dentistry, 1998) — 4단계 골밀도별 설계를 가진 골질 기반 임플란트 시스템.

5개 센터에서 연속 식립한 364개 임플란트의 전체 수술 생존율은 98.9%, 모든 골밀도에서 초기 생존율이 비슷할 수 있다고 보고.

예비 보고이며 비교군과 장기 추적이 초록에 없으므로 장기 기준으로 쓰지 말 것.

## Summary

Misch et al. propose density-specific implant geometry, and the first clinical series suggests similar early survival across density categories. The series is too early and too uncontrolled to confirm the design claim.

## Key Contributions

- Density-categorized implant system with compression-oriented thread.
- Early multicentre survival series.

## Methodology

FEA and animal studies preceded a five-centre prospective placement series.

## Results

364 implants; 98.9% surgical survival; similar early survival across densities (authors' statement).
