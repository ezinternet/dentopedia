## Three-line Summary

2-year prospective clinical study (28 patients, 37 jaws, 179 Straumann Roxolid BLT implants: 67×3.3 mm NDI + 97×4.1 mm + 15×4.8 mm; 78% immediately loaded; axial and tilted mixed, 17–30°).

Overall implant CSR 99.4%, NDI CSR 98.5%; mean MBL 0.51 mm at 1yr and 0.73 mm at 2yr; 6-implant prostheses showed significantly lower MBL than 4-implant (0.35 vs 0.63 mm, P=0.006 at 1yr); NDI MBL at 2yr was significantly higher than 4.1 mm implants (1.02 vs 0.44 mm, P=0.035).

NDIs are viable for full-arch rehabilitation in atrophic ridges as an alternative to bone grafting, but carry higher 2yr MBL; 6 implants distribute load better than 4; immediate loading equivalence confirmed.

## 세줄요약

2년 전향 임상 연구(28명, 37악궁, Straumann Roxolid BLT 179개: NDI 3.3 mm 67개; 즉시 부하 78%).

전체 CSR 99.4%, NDI 98.5%; 1년 MBL 0.51 mm, 2년 0.73 mm; 6개 임플란트 지지 시 4개보다 MBL 유의하게 낮음(0.35 vs 0.63 mm, P=0.006); 2년째 NDI MBL(1.02 mm)이 표준직경(0.44 mm)보다 유의하게 높음(P=0.035).

심한 위축 증례에서 NDI는 골이식 회피 옵션 — 단 2년 MBL이 표준직경보다 높고 임플란트 수 증가(6개)가 MBL 감소에 유의미; 야간 마우스가드 및 촘촘한 리콜 필수.

## Summary

According to PubMed, Coskunses and Tunc (Kocaeli University) retrospectively analyzed immediately loaded full-arch prostheses using a combination of narrow-diameter Ti-Zr (Roxolid®, 3.3 mm) and standard-diameter (4.1 mm, 4.8 mm) Straumann BLT implants over 2 years. The key question was whether NDIs (which avoid the need for bone augmentation in narrow ridges) perform acceptably alongside standard implants.

**Critical finding on implant number**: 6-implant-supported prostheses had significantly lower 1-year MBL than 4-implant prostheses (0.35 vs 0.63 mm, P=0.006). This aligns with Brånemark (1995) who reported more complications with 4 implants. Clinically this supports adding a 5th or 6th implant in the maxilla when bone allows.

**NDI MBL caveat**: while NDI survival was equivalent to standard diameter, 2-year MBL was significantly higher (1.02 mm NDI vs 0.44 mm 4.1 mm). This is within published acceptable thresholds but warrants close monitoring, especially in the posterior where biting forces are higher.

Immediate vs late loading: no significant MBL difference (consistent with broader literature).

## Key Contributions

- First report of 3.3 mm Ti-Zr NDIs specifically in fixed full-arch rehabilitation
- Demonstrates 6-implant biomechanical advantage over 4-implant for MBL
- Confirms immediate loading equivalence to delayed loading at 2 years
- Night guard for all patients: provisional fractures (16%) occurred only in undetected bruxers not using guards

## Methodology

- 37 jaws (19 mandible, 18 maxilla); minimum 4 implants/jaw
- Distal tilting 17–30° with 30° MUA; immediate loading threshold: IT ≥35 Ncm AND ISQ ≥65
- Definitive prosthesis 4–6 months: Ti or CoCr framework + ceramic or composite
- MBL by ImageJ on standardized paralleling technique periapical radiographs
