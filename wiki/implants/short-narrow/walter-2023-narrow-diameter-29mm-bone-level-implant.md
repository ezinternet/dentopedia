## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-arm multicenter PMCF study (n=41, 5 German centers) evaluating the Straumann® 2.9 mm bone-level tapered (BLT) Roxolid®/SLActive implant for single-tooth replacement at maxillary lateral and mandibular incisor sites over 12 months.
Implant survival 92.7% (95%-CI 79.0–97.6%) at 1 year; success rate 89.7%; mean relative MBL −0.3 mm (±0.42); PES 11.6/14 at 12 months; 3 implant losses all before provisional crown insertion; 0 fractures.
The 2.9 mm two-piece BLT implant is safe and viable for narrow anterior gaps, but the survival is modest vs SR+MA benchmarks and the wide CI and short follow-up limit definitive conclusions.

## Summary

Walter et al. (2023) report the first multicenter prospective clinical data for the Straumann® 2.9 mm BLT Roxolid® SLActive implant — a two-piece bone-level device classified as Category 2 under the updated Schiegnitz NDI classification (diameter 2.5–<3.3 mm). The study was a post-market clinical follow-up (PMCF) conducted at five German centers from 2015 to 2019, placing 41 implants at narrow anterior single-tooth gaps (predominantly maxillary lateral incisors: 85.4% of sites). The 1-year Kaplan–Meier survival of 92.7% is clinically acceptable but notably lower than the ~97% pooled survival reported for Category 2/3 NDIs in SR+MAs, primarily because all three losses occurred early (before provisional crown), suggesting patient or site selection issues rather than long-term mechanical failure. Zero implant fractures support the fracture-resistance advantage of Roxolid® Ti-Zr alloy at this ultra-narrow diameter. The PES of 11.6/14 indicates satisfactory soft-tissue esthetics. Bone augmentation was required in 34.1% of cases, underscoring that even 2.9 mm NDIs do not always eliminate the need for augmentation.

## Key Contributions

- First product-specific multicenter prospective data for Straumann® 2.9 mm BLT Roxolid®/SLActive in single-tooth anterior restorations
- Applied updated Schiegnitz (2021) two-piece Category 2 classification — correctly separates this implant from mini-implants (Category 1)
- Zero implant fractures in 41 implants at 12 months — supports Roxolid® fracture resistance at 2.9 mm diameter
- PES 11.6/14 at 12 months — confirms acceptable peri-implant soft tissue esthetics
- 34.1% simultaneous bone augmentation rate — documents real-world augmentation need even with NDI

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective, single-arm, multicenter PMCF; STROBE-compliant
- **Implant**: Straumann® BLT ∅ 2.9 mm SC, Roxolid®, SLActive; lengths 10/12/14 mm
- **Sites**: Maxillary lateral incisors (FDI 12/22) + any mandibular incisor (31–42), single-tooth gap
- **n**: 41 patients / 41 implants; 5 centers (Germany); Oct 2015–Dec 2019
- **Primary**: Implant survival at 12 months (Kaplan–Meier)
- **Secondary**: Success, PES (0–14), marginal bone level change (periapical radiograph), PI, mSBI, PPD, adverse events
- **Success definition**: Survival + no pain/dysesthesia + no peri-implant suppuration + no mobility + no radiolucency
- **Bone augmentation**: Simultaneous in 34.1%; collagen membrane in 78.6% of augmented cases

## Results

### Primary and Secondary Outcomes

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Implant survival at 12 months | **92.7%** (95%-CI 79.0–97.6%) |
| Implant success at 12 months | **89.7%** (95%-CI 75.8–97.1%) |
| Implant losses | 3/41 (all before provisional crown) |
| Implant fractures | **0** |
| PES at 6 months | 11.2 (95%-CI 10.5–11.9) |
| PES at 12 months | 11.6 (95%-CI 11.1–12.1) |
| Relative MBL at 12 months | −0.3 mm (SD ±0.42; range 0.45 to −1.54 mm) |
| Absolute MBL at final crown | −1.1 mm (SD ±0.62) |
| PPD median | 2.0 mm (range 1–4 mm) |

### Bone Augmentation Subgroup

- 14/41 (34.1%) required simultaneous augmentation (lateral: 71.4%, contour: 28.6%)
- Resorbable collagen membrane used in 78.6% of augmented cases

### Patient & Site Characteristics

- Mean age: 44.5 ± 18.3 years; 58.5% male
- Bone quality: Type II 24.4%, **Type III 65.9%**, Type IV 9.8% (predominantly soft bone)
- Non-smokers: 80.5%; current smokers (<10/day): 12.2%
- Most frequent reason for tooth loss: unsuccessful endodontic treatment (29.3%), fractured teeth (24.4%)
- Primary stability achieved in 40/41 (97.6%)
