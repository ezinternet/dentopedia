## Three-line Summary
1. RCT만 포함한 시나리오별 체계적 고찰·메타분석·TSA·GRADE에서 단경(≤6 mm) 대 장경(≥10 mm) 임플란트를 비교했다.
2. 상악동거상(6 mm), 자연골(≤6 mm), 전악 수복(≤6 mm)은 5년 생존/합병증에서 장경과 유사(고/중등도 근거)하다.
3. 하악골 수직골증대 및 4~5 mm 개별 길이는 근거가 불충분하거나 낮아 일반화에 제한이 있다.

## 세줄요약
1. 임상 시나리오(상악동거상, 하악골골증대, 자연골, 전악재건)별로 단경임플란트(≤6 mm)와 장경임플란트(≥10 mm)의 예후를 RCT 근거만으로 평가했다.
2. 상악동거상 후 6 mm, 자연골에 식립된 ≤6 mm, 전악 재건 시 ≤6 mm은 장경과 유사한 5년 생존율·합병증을 보이며 근거 수준은 중등도~높음이다.
3. 하악골 골증대 시나리오와 4 mm/5 mm 단독 비교에서는 현재 RCT 근거가 불충분하여 신중한 적용이 권고된다.

## Summary
Ravidà et al. (2024, J Clin Periodontol) performed a systematic review of RCTs comparing short (≤6 mm) with long (≥10 mm) dental implants stratified by clinical scenario and follow-up, integrating meta-analysis, trial sequential analysis, and GRADE. The authors found similar survival and complication rates for 6-mm implants after maxillary sinus floor augmentation and for ≤6-mm implants placed in native bone or for full-arch rehabilitation at 5 years (moderate-to-high certainty). Evidence was insufficient or low certainty for short implants in vertically augmented mandibular bone and for some individual lengths (4–5 mm). The review emphasizes scenario-specific interpretation and the need for longer-term RCTs and adherence to core outcome sets.

## Key Contributions
- First comprehensive scenario-stratified synthesis (by site/augmentation and by implant length) of RCT evidence comparing ≤6 mm vs ≥10 mm.
- Combined TSA with GRADE to assess information size and certainty per scenario/timepoint.
- Detailed tabulation of survival/MBL/complications by scenario and length (4,5,6 mm).
- Clinically actionable conclusion: short implants can be reasonable alternatives in specific scenarios where augmentation is avoided, but not universally supported.

## Methodology
- Inclusion: only RCTs comparing ≤6 mm vs ≥10 mm in humans.
- Outcomes: implant survival, marginal bone level (MBL), biologic/prosthetic complications.
- Analyses: random-effects/meta-analysis as appropriate, trial sequential analysis, risk of bias (RoB2), GRADE certainty.
- Subgrouping by clinical scenario and specific short lengths where data allowed.

## Results
| Clinical scenario | 5-year survival/complications (≤6 mm vs ≥10 mm) | GRADE/certainty (from review) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maxillary sinus lift | Similar | High (for 6 mm) | Strongest support for 6 mm in this scenario |
| Vertical ridge augmentation (mandible) | Unclear/inconclusive | Low/very low or insufficient | Few RCTs; no solid high/mod certainty support at 5y |
| Native bone (either jaw) | Similar | Moderate | Supported for ≤6 mm overall |
| Full-arch rehabilitation (either jaw) | Similar | Moderate | Supported for ≤6 mm overall |
| Prosthetic complications (overall) | No significant difference (RR~1.03) | Low-Moderate overall | Generally comparable across scenarios |
