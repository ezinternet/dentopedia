## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251042692; MEDLINE/Embase/Scopus to May 2025; 3 RCTs + 5 prospective cohorts; 181 patients, 189 short implants <8 mm) on survival, MBL, prosthetic complications and PROMs of short implants supporting overdentures.

At 1 year, survival was 97.2% (95% CI 93.1-98.9%; I2 = 0%) and pooled MBL 0.53 mm (95% CI -0.20 to 1.27; I2 = 95.8%); 45.7% of patients had at least one maintenance event (matrix/insert replacement 24.7%, relining 14.0%), with overdenture fracture in 3.1%.

Short implants can be considered for overdentures where vertical bone height is limited, but only survival is moderate-certainty; evidence is short term and complication/PROM data are low to very low certainty.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251042692; MEDLINE/Embase/Scopus 2025년 5월까지; RCT 3편 + 전향적 코호트 5편; 환자 181명, 짧은 임플란트(<8 mm) 189개) — 임플란트 오버덴처 지지 짧은 임플란트의 생존율·변연골 변화 (marginal bone level change, MBL)·보철 합병증·환자보고결과 (PROMs).

1년 임플란트 생존율 (implant survival rate) 97.2% (95% CI 93.1-98.9%; I2 = 0%), MBL 평균 0.53 mm (95% CI -0.20~1.27; I2 = 95.8%); 환자 45.7%가 1회 이상 유지관리 사건(매트릭스/인서트 교체 24.7%, 리라이닝 14.0%), 오버덴처 파절 3.1%.

수직 골높이가 제한된 경우 오버덴처에 짧은 임플란트를 고려할 수 있으나, 확실성은 생존율만 moderate이고 근거는 단기이며 합병증·PROMs는 low~very low.

## Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of short dental implants (<8 mm) supporting implant overdentures: 8 studies (3 RCTs, 5 prospective cohorts), 181 patients, 189 implants. One-year survival was 97.2% (95% CI 93.1-98.9%; I2 = 0%). Pooled MBL was 0.53 mm (95% CI -0.20 to 1.27) with very high heterogeneity (I2 = 95.8%). Prosthetic complications were predominantly minor: 45.7% of patients had at least one maintenance event, most often matrix/insert replacement (24.7%) and relining (14.0%); overdenture fracture was uncommon (3.1%). PROMs showed consistently high satisfaction but were reported heterogeneously. GRADE certainty was moderate for survival and low to very low for MBL, complications and PROMs. The authors conclude short implants are a practical option when vertical bone height is limited, with reduced surgical burden, while acknowledging that the evidence is short term.

## Key Contributions

- Overdenture-specific pooled estimate for short (<8 mm) implants, combining survival, MBL, complications and PROMs.
- Quantifies expected maintenance burden (matrix/insert replacement, relining) for counselling.
- Transparent GRADE ratings showing where evidence is weak.

## Methodology

- PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420251042692; MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus to May 2025.
- RoB 2 for RCTs; ROBINS-I V2 for cohorts.
- Survival: logit transformation, Hartung-Knapp random-effects; MBL: inverse-variance random-effects; complications: RE-GLMM; PROMs narrative.

## Results

- Survival at 1 year: 97.2% (95% CI 93.1-98.9%; I2 = 0%); GRADE moderate.
- MBL: 0.53 mm (95% CI -0.20 to 1.27; I2 = 95.8%); GRADE low to very low.
- At least one maintenance event: 45.7% of patients; matrix/insert replacement 24.7%; relining 14.0%; overdenture fracture 3.1%.
- PROMs: consistently high satisfaction; heterogeneous reporting.

## Limitations

- Abstract-only: study-level details (follow-up beyond 1 year, attachment type, arch, implant length distribution) not available.
- Short-term evidence; long-term predictability not established.
- MBL pooled with I2 = 95.8% and a CI spanning zero; small total sample (181 patients).
- Low to very low certainty for MBL, complications and PROMs; heterogeneous PROM reporting.
