## Three-line Summary

Retrospective 5-year study of 79 patients (155 Bicon locking-taper implants: 5, 6, 8 mm) placed with modified osteotome sinus floor elevation and β-TCP graft in atrophic posterior maxilla (mean RCBH 4.45 mm), restored with single crowns.

Overall implant survival was 94.84% (147/155); by length: 5 mm 93.75%, 6 mm 94.00%, 8 mm 100%; mean RCBH increased from 4.45 mm to 9.25 mm post-placement and stabilized at 5.25 mm at 5 years; mean crestal bone loss was −0.36 mm.

Simultaneous internal sinus lift with short/ultra-short implants for single-crown restoration is a predictable option in atrophic posterior maxilla with RCBH <5 mm, showing stable 5-year bone gain and minimal crestal resorption despite partial β-TCP resorption.

## Summary

This single-center retrospective case series from the University of Verona evaluates short (6–8 mm) and ultra-short (5 mm) Bicon locking-taper implants placed simultaneously with a modified osteotome sinus floor elevation (β-TCP graft) in atrophic posterior maxilla. With 79 patients and 155 implants followed for 5 years, the study reports 94.84% overall survival, stable crestal bone levels (−0.36 mm loss), and initial sinus height gain of 4.8 mm that partially resorbs to 1.46 mm at 5 years due to β-TCP degradation. Membrane perforation occurred in 7.74% of cases without implant loss. The data support simultaneous internal sinus lift with ultra-short implants as a viable, less-invasive alternative to lateral window sinus augmentation for single-crown restorations in sites with <5 mm residual bone height.

## Key Contributions

- 5-year dimensional data (RCBH, sinus lift height, CBL) for ultra-short (5 mm) implants with simultaneous transcrestal sinus elevation — a combination under-represented in SR/MA-level evidence.
- Demonstrates clinical predictability of single-crown restoration supported by 5 mm implants in RCBH <5 mm sites.
- Documents β-TCP graft resorption trajectory over 5 years (4.8 mm → 1.46 mm sinus lift height).
- Quantifies membrane perforation rate (7.74%) as a manageable complication with no attributable implant failure.

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Single-center retrospective case series |
| Sample | 79 patients; 155 implants |
| Implant system | Bicon locking-taper (lengths: 5 mm n=32, 6 mm n=100, 8 mm n=23) |
| Sinus lift technique | Modified osteotome SFE (transcrestal/internal), simultaneous with implant placement |
| Graft material | β-TCP |
| Restoration | Single crowns via Integrated Abutment Crown (IAC) technique |
| Imaging | CBCT + periapical radiographs at all timepoints |
| Follow-up | 5 years (mean) |

## Results

**Survival**
- Overall: 94.84% (147/155)
- 5 mm: 93.75% (30/32)
- 6 mm: 94.00% (94/100)
- 8 mm: 100% (23/23)

**Bone Height & Sinus Lift**
- Mean RCBH: 4.45 mm pre-op → 9.25 mm post-placement → 5.25 mm at 5 yr
- Sinus lift height: 4.8 mm at placement → 1.46 mm at 5 yr (β-TCP resorption)
- Crestal bone loss: mean −0.36 mm over 5 years
- F-BIC shift: −0.62 mm

**Complications**
- Membrane perforation: 12/155 (7.74%); no implant loss attributed to perforation

**Patient-reported outcomes**
- VAS satisfaction: 8.2 (baseline) → 9.0 (5 years)

## Limitations

- Retrospective design; no control group (no lateral SFE or standard-length arm)
- Non-homogeneous group distribution (100 six-mm vs 32 five-mm vs 23 eight-mm)
- Single center; possible selection bias
- β-TCP resorption reduces sinus gain; long-term bone stability depends on neo-ossification rather than graft persistence
- Crown-to-implant ratio not analyzed by length group
