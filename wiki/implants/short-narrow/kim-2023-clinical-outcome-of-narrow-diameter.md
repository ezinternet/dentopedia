## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 274 narrow-diameter implants (≤3.5 mm) placed 2013–2018 at Kyung Hee University Dental Hospital (Korea), with ≥1 year follow-up after final prosthesis; Kaplan-Meier survival, Cox risk-factor model, and radiographic marginal bone loss (MBL).

The 3-year cumulative survival rate was 92.4% (19 failures: 12 early osseointegration failures, 7 peri-implantitis); smoking (OR=4.758) and osteoporosis (OR=3.420) were significant risk factors, Ti-Zr alloy survival was 94.6%, and mean MBL was 0.33±0.32 mm vertical and 0.18±0.17 mm horizontal.

NDIs functioned stably with no fixture or abutment fracture and 91.7% 3-year survival in the posterior region (n=108), but smokers and osteoporotic patients warrant cautious selection and the retrospective single-centre design calls for prospective confirmation.

## Summary
Single-centre retrospective chart and radiograph review from the Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Kyung Hee University Dental Hospital. 274 NDIs (diameter ≤3.5 mm, categories 2 and 3) with at least 1 year of follow-up after final prosthesis were analysed (patient count not stated in the text). Mean age 54 years, 65.3% female, 60.6% anterior placement, 10.0 mm length in 78.5%.

## Key Contributions
- 3-year cumulative survival of 92.4%; 19 failures, of which 12 were early losses from failed osseointegration (≤1 year) and 7 were peri-implantitis.
- Smoking (OR=4.758) and osteoporosis (OR=3.420) were significantly associated with failure (P<0.05); other systemic diseases, implant characteristics, prosthesis type and bone grafting were not.
- Ti-Zr alloy survival 94.6%, with about 0.1 mm less bone loss than other materials.

## Methodology
Implants placed January 2013–December 2018, follow-up data to 2020. Failure = fixture loss or conditions contrary to the success criteria (immobility, radiolucency or bone loss >1.5 mm, persistent pain/paresthesia/discomfort/infection). MBL measured on standardized digital periapical radiographs at placement and 1 year after loading at four sites (ICC >0.9). Kaplan-Meier with log-rank, Cox proportional hazards, t/Welch/Kruskal-Wallis, chi-square; SPSS 25. IRB KH-DT20014.

## Results
- [확인] 3-year cumulative survival 92.4% (274 NDIs); 19 failures.
- [확인] MBL 0.33±0.32 mm vertical, 0.18±0.17 mm horizontal; smokers 0.72 mm and 0.38 mm.
- [확인] Screw loosening 5.5%, porcelain fracture 2.2%; no fixture or abutment fracture; complications more frequent with CpTi, stock abutments and posterior sites.
- [확인] Posterior NDIs (n=108; 38 splinted FDPs, 56 single FDPs, 14 with pontics): 3-year survival 91.7%, vertical MBL 0.32±0.29 mm, greater in molars than premolars.

## Limitations
- Retrospective, non-randomized, single-centre; the authors acknowledge uncontrolled bias.
- Many dropouts limited interpretation of long-term follow-up.
- Ti-Zr NDIs were mostly placed in premolar sites; confounding by material, site and surface is possible. Estimates from the Cox model are reported as OR in the paper.
- Authors consider routine NDI use for single molar replacement premature.

## Clinical Implication (Korean Practice)
In this Korean hospital cohort, NDIs gave about 92% 3-year survival without fixture fracture, supporting their use where ridge width is limited. Smoking and osteoporosis were associated with several-fold higher failure, so these patients need counselling and careful case selection; for molar sites, Ti-Zr NDIs should be treated cautiously until prospective data exist.
