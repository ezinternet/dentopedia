## Three-line Summary

Five-year prospective cohort (n=13 head-and-neck cancer patients, 44 short implants in 4 microvascular bone free flap types — fibula, iliac crest, scapula; 63-month follow-up): implant survival and quality-of-life in oncologic-reconstruction jaws.

Survival 97.1% (95% CI 80.9–99.6%); success 88.2% (95% CI 71.6–95.4%) at 63 months; quality of life significantly improved, especially in food intake and articulation.

First substantial prospective QoL data for short implants in free-flap-reconstructed jaws; key limitation is small heterogeneous sample (n=13) with no comparator arm.

## 세줄요약

5년 전향 코호트 (n=13 두경부암 환자, 44개 단기 임플란트, 4가지 유형 혈관부착 유리골피판 — 비골·장골·견갑골; 63개월 추적): 종양 재건부 단기 임플란트 생존율과 삶의 질 평가.

생존율 97.1% (95% CI 80.9–99.6%); 성공율 88.2% (95% CI 71.6–95.4%) at 63개월; 삶의 질(저작·언어) 유의미하게 개선.

유리피판 재건부 단기 임플란트의 최초 전향적 QoL 데이터; 소표본(n=13)·이질적 피판 유형·대조군 없음이 주요 한계.

## Summary
Five-year prospective study of 13 head-and-neck cancer patients (44 implants) who received short dental implants in microvascular bone free flaps (fibula/iliac crest/scapula). Assesses survival, success, and quality-of-life.

## Key Contributions
First substantial prospective QoL data for short implants placed in free-flap-reconstructed jaws — directly informs oncologic-reconstruction prosthodontic protocols.

## Methodology
Prospective cohort; 13 patients, 44 implants in 4 free-flap types. 63-month follow-up. QoL questionnaires at multiple time points.

## Results
Survival 97.1% (95% CI: 80.9–99.6%) at 63 months. Success 88.2% (95% CI: 71.6–95.4%). QoL improved especially in food intake and articulation.

## Limitations
Small sample (n=13); heterogeneous flap types; no comparator arm.
