## One-line Summary
Retrospective (n=16, 48 implants, 6.5mm): 100% implant and prosthesis survival at 14 months; short-short splinting produced less distal marginal bone loss (0.37 mm) than short-long splinting (0.94 mm).

## 한줄요약
후향적(n=16, 임플란트 48개, 6.5mm): 14개월 생존율 100%; 단축-단축 스플린팅 원심 변연골 소실 0.37mm로 단축-장 스플린팅 0.94mm보다 유의하게 낮음.

## Summary
This retrospective study reviewed 16 patients who received 48 BTI implants (6.5mm length, acid-etched surface) to support two-implant fixed partial prostheses in premolar-molar regions. All implants were immediately loaded. Two groups: short-short (SS, both implants 6.5mm, n=8) and short-long (SL, one 6.5mm plus one longer, n=8). At mean 14 months follow-up, both groups achieved 100% implant and prosthesis survival. However, distal marginal bone loss was significantly higher in the SL group (0.94 ± 0.66 mm) versus the SS group (0.37 ± 0.55 mm), suggesting that biomechanical stress differences at the junction of implants with different lengths may influence bone response.

## Key Contributions
- Demonstrates that immediate loading of 6.5mm implants in premolar-molar regions is safe (100% survival)
- Splinting two short implants (SS) is biomechanically superior to splinting short+long (SL) at the distal junction
- Clinical confirmation of stress-distribution principle: mismatched implant lengths create differential loading
- Supports 6.5mm as a viable option avoiding sinus lift in atrophic posterior maxilla

## Methodology
Retrospective cohort, single private center (BTI Institute, Spain). n=16 patients, 48 implants (all 6.5mm BTI, acid-etched surface). Immediately loaded. SS group: both 6.5mm (n=8, 16 implants); SL group: one 6.5mm + one longer (n=8, 32 implants). Mean follow-up: 14 ± 5 months. Primary: implant survival. Secondary: marginal bone stability (standardized periapical radiographs), prosthesis survival.

## Results
- Implant survival: 100% (both groups)
- Prosthesis survival: 100% (both groups)
- Distal MBL: SS 0.37 ± 0.55 mm vs. SL 0.94 ± 0.66 mm (p < 0.05)
- Mesial MBL: no significant difference
