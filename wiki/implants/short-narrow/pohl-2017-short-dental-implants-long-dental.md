## Three-line Summary

Study type/context: Multicentre RCT in posterior maxilla with residual bone height 5–7 mm comparing 6 mm short implants (GS) vs 11–15 mm long implants + sinus grafting (GG).  
Primary result with numbers: At 3 years, implant survival was 100% in both groups; marginal bone level change from implant placement to FU-3 was −0.43 ± 0.58 mm (GG) and −0.44 ± 0.56 mm (GS) with no between-group difference.  
Clinical implication/key limitation: Both treatment modalities showed comparable survival and marginal bone levels at 3 years, suggesting short implants may be a viable alternative to sinus augmentation in this indication.

## Summary

Pohl et al. report 3-year results of a multicentre RCT comparing 6 mm short implants versus 11–15 mm long implants with simultaneous sinus floor elevation in patients with posterior maxillary partial edentulism and residual bone height 5–7 mm. At 3 years, implant survival was 100% in both groups with no significant difference in marginal bone level changes between groups. Small between-group differences were observed for probing pocket depth at FU-3, but overall clinical and radiographic outcomes were comparable. The findings support short implants as an alternative to long implants with sinus augmentation in this specific indication.

## Key Contributions

- Provides 3-year randomized controlled data extending the 1-year results (Thoma et al. 2015).
- Confirms high survival (100%) for both strategies at medium-term follow-up.
- Demonstrates comparable marginal bone remodeling between short implants and long implants with sinus grafting.

## Methodology

- Design: Multicentre randomized controlled clinical trial (101 patients)
- Intervention: 6 mm short implants (GS) vs 11–15 mm long implants + lateral sinus floor elevation with grafting (GG)
- Restorations: Single crowns loaded at 6 months (PR)
- Follow-up: Yearly examinations up to 3 years (FU-3)
- Outcomes: Implant survival, marginal bone level (MBL), probing pocket depth (PPD), bleeding on probing (BoP), plaque control record (PCR)

## Results

| Outcome | Group GG (long + SFE) | Group GS (short 6 mm) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant survival (FU-3) | 100% | 100% | — |
| MBL at FU-3 | 0.45 mm | 0.44 mm | >0.05 |
| MBL change IP→FU-3 | -0.43 ± 0.58 mm | -0.44 ± 0.56 mm | >0.05 |
| MBL change PR→FU-3 | -0.25 ± 0.58 mm | -0.1 ± 0.54 mm | — (significant within GG only) |
| PPD at FU-3 | — | — | 0.035 (between-group) |
| BoP/PCR at FU-3 | Similar | Similar | NS |
