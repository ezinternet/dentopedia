## Three-line Summary

Multicentre RCT (91 patients, 101 implants) compared 6 mm short implants (residual bone height 6–8 mm) vs 11–15 mm long implants with sinus floor elevation (residual bone height 3–6 mm) at 1 year of loading.

Patient-reported outcomes (OHIP-49, VAS pain/function/aesthetics/general satisfaction) showed no significant differences between groups; the SFE approach was more invasive procedurally.

Establishes the trial cohort for Part 2 radiographic outcomes and longer-term follow-up of the same RCT.

## 세줄요약

잔존골높이 6–8 mm인 부위에 6 mm 단폭경 임플란트, 3–6 mm인 부위에 부비동저상술 후 11–15 mm 장폭경 임플란트를 비교한 다기관 무작위대조시험(RCT, 91명 101개 임플란트), 1년 하중 결과.

환자보고지표(OHIP-49, 통증/기능/심미/전반적 만족도 VAS)는 양군 간 유의차 없었으며, 부비동저상술군이 절차상 더 침습적이었음.

본 논문은 동일 코호트의 방사선학적 결과(Part 2) 및 장기 추적 연구의 기초를 제공함.

## Summary

This is Part 1 of a multicentre RCT comparing short (6 mm) implants without sinus floor elevation versus longer (11–15 mm) implants with sinus floor elevation in the posterior maxilla. At 1 year of loading, patient-reported outcomes were equivalent between groups despite the greater surgical morbidity of augmentation. The findings support consideration of short implants as an alternative when residual bone height permits, while longer follow-up is needed to confirm clinical/radiographic performance.

## Key Contributions

- **Clinical question**: Direct RCT comparison of short implants vs long implants + SFE in anatomically restricted posterior maxilla.
- **PROM equivalence**: No significant between-group differences in OHIP-49 or VAS at 1 year.
- **Trial design**: Multicentre, protocol-driven allocation by residual bone height with a defined framework for Part 2.
- **Context for decision-making**: Highlights trade-off between avoiding augmentation (short implants) and established long-implant approach, with patient experience comparable at 1 year.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Multicentre randomized controlled trial.
- **Population**: 91 patients, 101 implants in posterior maxilla.
- **Groups**: Test — 6 mm implants, residual bone height 6–8 mm (no SFE). Control — 11–15 mm implants, residual bone height 3–6 mm (with SFE).
- **Outcomes (Part 1)**: Demographics and patient-reported outcomes (OHIP-49, VAS for pain/function/aesthetics/general satisfaction) at 1 year of loading.
- **Funding**: Dentsply Implants.

## Results

| Outcome | Result (1 year) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| OHIP-49 (total/domains) | No significant between-group differences | Patient-reported oral health impact comparable. |
| VAS (pain, function, aesthetics, general satisfaction) | No significant between-group differences | Similar satisfaction despite different procedures. |
| Surgical morbidity | Higher in long-implant + SFE group (expected) | More invasive augmentation procedure. |
| Demographics/baseline | Comparable between groups | Adequate randomization balance. |
