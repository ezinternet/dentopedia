## Three-line Summary

Literature review (Dentistry Today, 2005) arguing that short (<10 mm) posterior implants need biomechanical load management because of crown height, bite force and lower bone density.

Load-reduction measures listed are removal of lateral contacts and cantilevers, more implants, larger diameter or surface area, and splinting.

The 99% survival figure in the abstract is quoted from an earlier study and is not this review's own result.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Dentistry Today, 2005) — 단축 (<10 mm) 구치부 임플란트는 크라운 높이, 교합력, 낮은 골밀도 때문에 생체역학적 하중 관리가 필요하다고 주장.

제시된 하중 감소 방법은 측방 접촉·캔틸레버 제거, 임플란트 수 증가, 직경·표면적 증가, 고정.

초록의 99% 생존율은 인용된 선행 연구 수치이며 본 리뷰의 결과가 아님.

## Summary

Misch reasons from mechanism (higher posterior forces, less bone, longer crown height) to a design rule: when short implants are used, reduce force per implant and increase effective load-bearing area. This is a rationale paper; the survival figure it quotes must be checked at its source.

## Key Contributions

- Mechanism-based rationale for short posterior implants.
- Checklist of biomechanical load-reduction measures.

## Methodology

Narrative literature review; no search strategy in the abstract.

## Results

Qualitative rationale. Quoted survival figure (99%) is from a cited study.
