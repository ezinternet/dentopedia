## Three-line Summary

3.5-year single-center retrospective (36 patients, 288 All-on-Four implants) with individual-implant-level OPT MBL measurement at 18, 30, and 42 months post-restoration.

100% overall implant survival; tilted posterior implants showed significantly higher MBL than axial anterior implants at all time points; by 3.5yr maxilla averaged 0.770 mm and mandible 0.713 mm; highest individual losses at 14DA and 24DA (disto-approximal of tilted maxillary implants); smoking/comorbidities amplified bone loss.

Tilted implants carry a measurable MBL premium vs axial in All-on-Four, contradicting some SRs that pool underpowered studies; absolute values remain clinically acceptable with good hygiene, but disto-approximal surface of posterior maxillary implants is the highest-risk site.

## 세줄요약

생존율 100%; 경사 원심 임플란트는 전방 축방향 임플란트보다 전 추적 시점에서 MBL 유의하게 큼; 3.5년 상악 0.770 mm, 하악 0.713 mm; 최고 손실 부위 14DA·24DA; 흡연·전신질환이 특정 위치 골소실 악화(P<.05).

## Summary

According to PubMed, this Szeged University retrospective study followed 36 patients (288 Nobel Biocare All-on-Four implants) for up to 42 months post-restoration. All-on-Four protocol was strictly followed: 2 axial anterior + 2 distally tilted posterior per jaw, immediate loading, provisional acrylic then definitive milled titanium framework. Individual implant-level OPT measurements using CLINIVIEW software, calibrated to implant dimensions.

Key finding: **tilted implants had significantly greater MBL than axial implants at every time point** — contradicting the pooled SR literature (e.g., Durkan et al. meta-analysis: 0.34–1.14 mm axial vs 0.43–1.13 mm tilted, no significant difference). This discrepancy is likely because SRs include underpowered studies. In subgroup analyses, smoking and comorbidities produced significantly higher bone loss at specific positions (14MA, 24DA, 44DA), though patient-level pooled analysis was non-significant (insufficient n).

The bone loss kinetics followed a saturation pattern — rapid resorption in year 1, then flattening — consistent with other long-term implant studies.

## Key Contributions

- Individual implant-level (not patient-level) analysis reveals position-specific MBL differences that aggregate analyses miss
- Demonstrates tilted > axial MBL association in clinical All-on-Four data (vs SR consensus of equivalence)
- Identifies maxillary posterior disto-approximal surface (14DA, 24DA) as highest-risk MBL location
- Smoking and comorbidities amplify position-specific bone loss

## Methodology

- Single-center retrospective; n=36 patients, 288 implants
- Baseline at 3 months post-restoration; ΔBL calculated at T1(18m), T2(30m), T3(42m)
- OPT 640×480 px; CLINIVIEW analysis; calibrated to implant dimensions per implant
- Independent blinded researcher for all measurements
- Statistics: Shapiro-Wilk, t-test, one-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc; P<0.05

## Results

| Time point | Maxilla MBL | Mandible MBL |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| Baseline (3m) | 0.178±0.017 mm | 0.184±0.015 mm |
| T1 (18m) | 0.558±0.029 mm | 0.484±0.024 mm |
| T3 (42m) | **0.770±0.029 mm** | **0.713±0.026 mm** |

- Tilted vs axial: significantly different at all time points (P<0.05)
- MA vs DA: no significant difference overall
- Age: no significant linear correlation with MBL
- Highest position at T3: 24DA (−1.066±0.081 mm), 14DA (−1.001±0.101 mm)
