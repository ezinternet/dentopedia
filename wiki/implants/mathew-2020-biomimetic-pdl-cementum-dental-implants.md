## Three-line Summary

Scoping review (MEDLINE 1989–2018, n=1,395 screened → 12 included animal studies + 1 human trial) of in vivo studies reporting structural features and functional efficiency of artificial PDL or cementum developed around titanium dental implants.

Evidence progression: retained/adjacent roots → exogenous scaffolds (PLG, ePTFE, Dacron) → cultured PDL cells from extracted teeth → bio-hybrid implants with dental follicle stem cells and immortalized human cells (most advanced generation).

One human trial (Gault 2010 ligaplant) confirmed mobility equivalent to natural teeth; two bio-hybrid implant studies demonstrated orthodontic-force-mediated bone remodeling and nociceptive response via nerve fiber innervation in newly formed PDL.

## 세줄요약

골유착 임플란트 주위 인공 PDL (Periodontal Ligament, 치주인대)·백악질 (Cementum) 형성의 구조·기능적 특성을 보고한 생체 내(in vivo) 연구를 스코핑 고찰(MEDLINE 1989–2018, 1,395편 선별 → 동물 12편 + 인체 1편).

증거 진화: 잔존/인접 치근 → 외래 스캐폴드(PLG·ePTFE·Dacron) → 발거치 PDL 배양 세포 → 배아성 치낭 줄기세포·불멸화 인체세포 기반 바이오하이브리드 임플란트 (Bio-hybrid Implant, 최신 세대).

인체 1편(Gault 2010 리가플란트)에서 자연치와 동등한 이동성 확인; 바이오하이브리드 임플란트 2편에서 교정력 매개 골 리모델링과 신경섬유 분포(통증 감지)를 통한 유해 자극 반응 입증.

## Summary

The biomechanical deficits of osseointegrated implants — no shock absorption, no proprioception, no orthodontic movement — stem from the absence of a functional PDL. This 2020 scoping review traces 30 years of attempts to engineer PDL and cementum around titanium implants in vivo. Starting from early models using retained tooth roots, the field progressed through exogenous scaffolds and cultured PDL cell suspensions toward the most advanced generation: bio-hybrid implants wrapping titanium in embryonic dental follicle tissue (ED18.5-DF), which reproducibly induced PDL fiber formation with Sharpey's fiber insertion, orthodontic-force-responsive bone remodeling, and nerve innervation with measurable nociception. The sole human trial — the Gault 2010 ligaplant — demonstrated tight integration without bone contact and mobility equivalent to natural teeth, confirming that the concept translates to humans in principle. However, across all 13 included studies, no complete functional sensory recovery was documented, and autologous cell sourcing remains an unresolved prerequisite for clinical viability.

## Key Contributions

- Structured taxonomy of 4 biomimetic implant strategies by PDL source: (1) retained natural roots, (2) exogenous scaffold biomaterials (PLG, ePTFE, Dacron), (3) cultured PDL cells from extracted teeth, (4) bio-hybrid implants with stem cell-derived tissues
- Confirmed that HA-coated titanium produces better-organized fibrous tissue with perpendicularly oriented collagen vs uncoated titanium alloy
- Documented two bio-hybrid implant studies (Oshima 2014, Nakajima 2016) with:
  - Orthodontic tooth movement via bone remodeling (osteoclast + osteoblast activity) in response to applied forces
  - Antineurofilament-immunoreactive nerve fiber ingrowth into new PDL
  - C-Fos-immunoreactive neuron response 2 h post-orthodontic force (nociceptive confirmation)
- Critical gap identified: complete functional recovery with absolute sensory perception not yet demonstrated in any model; autologous cell sources needed

## Methodology

- **Design**: Scoping review (PRISMA flow)
- **Database**: MEDLINE via Ovid
- **Date range**: January 1989 – January 2, 2019
- **Initial hits**: 1,395; after exclusion (periodontal tissue regeneration/bone defect focus): 44 full-text reviewed → **12 included**
- **Inclusion criteria**: in vivo studies; artificial PDL or cementum around titanium dental implants; commercially pure titanium; microscopically characterized
- **Excluded**: review articles, hypothetical studies, extra-oral implants, bone implants, periodontal defect regeneration

## Results

**12 included studies by source type:**

| Author (Year) | Source type | Duration | Key finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buser 1990 | Retained roots | 12 months | Root cementum on implant surface + PDL-like space with perpendicular fibers |
| Warrer 1996 | Retained roots (canine) | 3 months | Sharpey's fibers perpendicular where PDL invasion allowed |
| Urabe 1999 | Adjacent PDL cells | 3 months | HA > titanium alloy: uniform fibrous tissue + calcified cementum-like tissue |
| Choi 2000 | Cultured PDL cells | 3 months | Cementum-like tissue + PDL-like layer on 37% of implant surfaces |
| Marei 2009 | PLG scaffold | 1 month | Sharpey's-like fibers, cementum tightly adherent, bone remodeling |
| Gault 2010 | Ligaplant (human) | 18 days–3 months | Firm integration, new bone, mobility = natural teeth |
| Lin 2011 | Rat PDL progenitors + Matrigel | 18 weeks | Osseointegration + thin cementum layer + poorly vascularized PDL |
| Oshima 2014 | Bio-hybrid (ED18.5-DF) | 1 month | Orthodontic movement, nerve innervation, osteoclast/osteoblast confirmed |
| Nakajima 2016 | Bone-attached + rat PDL (HA+Pt) | 40 days | Orthodontic movement + nociception via C-Fos-immunoreactive neurons |
| Lee 2017 | Immortalized human cells (4 types) | 56 days | PDL markers positive; aligned fibrous tissue; PLAP-1 (PDL-specific marker) expressed |

**Functional outcomes confirmed in included studies:**
- Shock absorption: confirmed (Gault mobility scores, Caiazza shear stress stability)
- Orthodontic tooth movement: confirmed in bio-hybrid studies (Oshima, Nakajima)
- Nociception (pain sensation via PDL nerve fibers): confirmed in bio-hybrid studies
