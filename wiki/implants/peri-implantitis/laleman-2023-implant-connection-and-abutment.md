## Three-line Summary

Narrative review evaluating whether implant connection and abutment characteristics (material, design, transmucosal configuration, handling) act as predisposing or precipitating factors for peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis.

Direct evidence that these features cause peri-implant disease is limited; the authors favour internal conical connections with inherent platform switching over flat-to-flat or external connections, early coronal placement of the prosthetic interface (tissue-level implant or one-abutment-one-time) to reduce disconnections and cement remnants, titanium or zirconia in the transmucosal portion, and higher implants (≥2 mm) with an emergence angle below 30°.

Recommendations are largely extrapolated from early bone-loss endpoints rather than disease incidence — useful as a hypothesis-generating component-selection framework, not as firm evidence-based clinical mandates.

## 세줄요약

서술적 리뷰: 임플란트 연결부와 지대주 특성(재료·형태·점막통과부 구성·조작 횟수)이 임플란트주위점막염(Peri-implant Mucositis)·임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis)의 선행·유발 인자인지를 검토.

직접 근거는 제한적이나, 골수준 임플란트에서는 내원뿔 연결(Internal Conical Connection, 플랫폼 스위칭 내재)이 평면-평면·외부 연결보다 유리하고, 보철 계면을 가능한 한 치은정 수준으로 빨리 옮겨 탈착 횟수와 시멘트 잔여물을 줄이며(조직수준 임플란트 또는 원-어버트먼트-원-타임), 점막통과부는 티타늄·지르코니아가 바람직하고, 높이 ≥2 mm + 출현각도(Emergence Angle, EA) <30°가 유리하다고 제안.

권고 대부분이 초기 골소실이라는 간접 지표에서 외삽된 것이라, 가설 생성용 부품 선택 틀로 읽어야지 확정적 근거 기반 지침으로 읽어서는 안 됨.

## Summary

This narrative review asks whether the prosthetic components themselves — connection geometry, abutment material and contour, and the number of abutment manipulations — predispose to or precipitate peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis. The authors find little direct disease-outcome evidence and instead assemble a directional framework from early crestal bone loss, soft-tissue seal and biocompatibility data. For bone-level implants, an internal conical connection with inherent platform switching is preferred over flat-to-flat or external connections. They recommend moving the prosthetic interface coronally toward the juxta-mucosal level as early as possible — by choosing a tissue-level implant or a transmucosal one-abutment-one-time abutment — to reduce disconnections and cement remnants. Titanium or zirconia are preferred for the transmucosal portion where material biocompatibility evidence is absent, and higher abutments (≥2 mm) with an emergence angle below 30° appear favourable. The paper explicitly labels most of this as indirect and calls for more research before firm recommendations.

## Key Contributions
- Frames connection/abutment choice as *predisposing or precipitating*, not solely causative, of peri-implant disease.
- Conical internal connection (inherent PS) preferred for bone-level implants on early bone-loss grounds.
- Early coronal prosthetic-interface positioning (tissue-level or OAOT) proposed to reduce manipulation and cement remnants.
- Material biocompatibility caveat: titanium/zirconia preferred in the transmucosal portion.
- Geometric signal: ≥2 mm abutment height with emergence angle <30°.

## Methodology

Narrative (critical) review of clinical and preclinical literature on implant connections, abutment materials/design and abutment handling. No PRISMA systematic search or formal evidence-grading scheme is reported. The evidence base leans on early crestal bone loss, histologic soft-tissue seal and biomechanical microgap studies, with direct peri-implant disease incidence explicitly acknowledged as sparse.

## Results

| Domain | Directional conclusion | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Connection type (bone-level) | Internal conical with inherent PS > flat-flat / external | Early bone loss (indirect) |
| Prosthetic interface position | Move coronally to juxta-mucosal ASAP | Disconnection count, cement remnants, seal |
| Abutment protocol | Tissue-level or transmucosal OAOT | Reduced manipulation |
| Transmucosal material | Titanium or zirconia preferred | Biocompatibility evidence gap |
| Height / emergence angle | ≥2 mm higher, EA <30° | Radiographic/histologic association |
