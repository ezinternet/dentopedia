## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=406 implants / 223 patients; Goethe University Frankfurt; April 2018–June 2023) comparing four surgical peri-implantitis protocols — OFD, implantoplasty, reconstructive, combined — all with titanium brush decontamination; mean follow-up 30.80±19.62 months.

Treatment success at 1 year (max PD ≤5 mm + BOP ≤1 site + no suppuration) was 54.7% overall with no significant group differences: OFD 62.5%, implantoplasty 58.3%, reconstructive 55.4%, combined 48.3%; 40 implants (9.9%) lost, 38 (9.4%) required surgical retreatment.

Systemic antibiotics (pre-op OR=3.54; pre+post-op OR=4.49) and surgeon experience (OR=0.12) independently predicted treatment success; molar site, antibiotics, and baseline suppuration predicted implant loss; overdenture and baseline max PD predicted surgical retreatment; post-hoc power 27% — group equivalence cannot be inferred.

## 세줄요약

후향적 코호트연구 (Retrospective Cohort Study) (n=406개 임플란트/223명; 프랑크푸르트 괴테대; 2018.4–2023.6): 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 4가지 외과 술식 — 개방형 피판술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD), 임플란토플라스티 (Implantoplasty), 재생 수술 (Reconstructive), 병합술식 (Combined) — 전 군 티타늄 브러시 (Titanium Brush) 표면 처리, 평균 추적 30.80±19.62개월.

1년 치료 성공률 전체 54.7%; 군간 유의 차이 없음 (OFD 62.5%, 임플란토플라스티 58.3%, 재생 55.4%, 병합 48.3%); 임플란트 소실 40개 (9.9%), 외과적 재치료 38개 (9.4%).

전신 항생제 (술전 OR=3.54, p=0.04; 술전+술후 OR=4.49, p=0.02) 및 술자 경험 (OR=0.12, p=0.003)이 치료 성공 독립 예측 인자; 구치부·항생제·기저 화농이 임플란트 소실, 피개의치·기저 최대 탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD)가 외과적 재치료 예측; 사후 검정력 27% — 군간 동등성 추론 불가.

## Summary

This retrospective cohort from the Schwarz group (Frankfurt) addresses a gap that controlled trials cannot yet fill: multi-protocol, long-term real-world outcomes from a single standardised academic centre. Four hundred and six implants were surgically treated using titanium brush decontamination across all four major protocols. The primary finding — no significant difference in 1-year treatment success between protocol groups (54.7% overall) — must be interpreted with caution given the post-hoc power of only 27%. The study's most clinically relevant finding is that modifiable non-protocol variables outperformed protocol choice as predictors: systemic antibiotic use more than tripled treatment success probability, and surgeon experience was equally powerful. The paradox that antibiotics also predicted implant loss (OR=3.13 for pre+post-op) likely reflects prescription bias: antibiotics were more often used in more complex or suppurative cases, which independently carried higher loss risk. Combined protocol carried numerically the highest implant loss (16.9%) and lowest success (48.3%), consistent with being applied to the most complex defects. Success rates declined substantially over time, reaching approximately 27–42% at 5 years across multiple success criteria.

## Key Contributions

- Largest single-centre cohort comparing OFD, implantoplasty, reconstructive, and combined peri-implantitis surgery under standardised surface decontamination
- No statistically significant protocol group difference in 1-year success — but 27% power means this is inconclusive, not equivalence
- Systemic antibiotics and surgeon experience are stronger predictors of success than surgical protocol
- Molar site (OR=3.21), baseline suppuration (OR=4.51), and overdenture (OR=3.59) identified as high-risk patient/site factors
- Longitudinal decline: ~55% success at 1 year → ~27–42% at 5 years — illustrates chronicity of disease and maintenance burden
- Antibiotic association with both success AND implant loss underscores confounding by indication in observational data

## Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Retrospective cohort (single centre, chart review); STROBE-compliant |
| Setting | Goethe University Frankfurt, Dept. Oral Surgery & Implantology |
| Period | April 2018 – June 2023 |
| n | 406 implants / 223 patients; 4 trained oral surgeons |
| Surface decontamination | Titanium brush (Hans Korea Co. Ltd.) — identical across all protocol groups |
| OFD | Open-flap debridement ± granulation tissue removal only (n=37) |
| Implantoplasty | OFD + implantoplasty for supracrestal component (n=39) |
| Reconstructive | OFD + bone graft ± collagen membrane for intrabony defect (n=241) |
| Combined | Implantoplasty + reconstructive (n=89) |
| Primary outcome | Treatment success at 1 year: max PD ≤5 mm + BOP ≤1 site + no suppuration (Criterion 1) |
| Mean follow-up | 30.80 ± 19.62 months |
| Post-hoc power | 27% (Cramér's V=0.1035, n=274 at 1 year) |
