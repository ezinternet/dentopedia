## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42024567630; MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Cochrane to December 2025; 3870 records) qualitatively synthesizing 4 cohort studies (3 retrospective, 1 prospective; 70-100 patients, 165-260 implants each; follow-up 12-276 months) on residual probing pocket depth (PPD) after active periodontal therapy and later peri-implantitis in treated periodontitis patients.

All 4 studies directionally linked persisting residual pockets to more peri-implantitis: Vagia 2021 HR 1.06 (1.02-1.10) per residual site with PPD >3 mm; Kumar 2018 OR 2.73 (1.45-5.16); Zhang 2018 >10% of sites with PPD >=6 mm significantly associated (HR 3.62 [1.05-12.51] in Table 2, HR 6.71 [1.84-24.47] in the text); Pjetursson 2012 4.1 vs 1.9 residual pockets in patients with vs without peri-implantitis. No meta-analysis was possible, and no study assessed furcation involvement.

Residual pockets look like a plausible, modifiable risk indicator that argues for periodontal re-evaluation before placement and tighter supportive care, but only 4 heterogeneous observational studies (moderate risk of bias in 3) with non-uniform exposure/outcome definitions and maintenance regimens support it, so no pocket-depth threshold can be set.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR; PROSPERO CRD42024567630; 4개 DB, 2025년 12월까지, 3870편 검색)으로 코호트 4편(후향 3·전향 1; 연구당 환자 70-100명·임플란트 165-260개; 추적 12-276개월)을 정성적으로 종합 - 활성 치주치료 후 잔존 치주낭 (Residual Pocket, PPD)과 이후 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 발생의 관계.

4편 모두 잔존 치주낭이 임플란트주위염 증가와 연관된 방향: Vagia 2021 PPD >3 mm 잔존 부위당 HR 1.06 (1.02-1.10); Kumar 2018 OR 2.73 (1.45-5.16); Zhang 2018 PPD >=6 mm 부위 >10% 시 유의(표 HR 3.62 [1.05-12.51], 본문 HR 6.71 [1.84-24.47]); Pjetursson 2012 임플란트주위염군 4.1개 vs 비발생군 1.9개 잔존 치주낭. 메타분석 불가, 분지부병변 (Furcation Involvement)은 평가한 연구 없음.

식립 전 치주 재평가와 지지 치주/임플란트 유지관리 강화의 근거가 되는 위험 지표이나, 이질적인 관찰연구 4편(3편 중등도 편향위험)뿐이고 노출·결과 정의와 유지치료 방식이 제각각이라 확정적 치주낭 깊이 기준은 제시할 수 없다.

## Summary

This systematic review asked whether residual periodontal pockets (PPD) persisting after active periodontal therapy, or furcation-involved teeth, at the time of implant placement raise the risk of peri-implantitis in treated periodontitis patients. Four cohort studies (published 2012-2021; one prospective, three retrospective) met the criteria out of 3870 records. All four pointed the same way: more or deeper residual pockets (>=5 or >=6 mm; a larger share of diseased sites) accompanied more peri-implantitis, and one cohort also linked gingivitis/periodontitis on adjacent teeth to peri-implantitis (OR 8.0, 95% CI 1.2-70.1). Quantitative pooling was inappropriate because exposure definitions, peri-implantitis case definitions, follow-up, level of analysis and effect measures differed. The authors read the findings as consistent with guidance to reach periodontal stability before implant placement and to reinforce supportive care when complete pocket elimination is not achievable, while stressing that no threshold can be derived and that furcation involvement was never studied.

## Key Contributions

- Reframes "periodontitis history" as a measurable pre-placement state: the residual pocket burden left after active therapy, not the history alone.
- Consistent direction across all four eligible cohorts despite heterogeneity; both extent (share of diseased sites) and severity (deeper PPD) mattered.
- Adjacent-tooth periodontal inflammation at implant restoration was linked to peri-implantitis in one cohort (OR 8.0), a hypothesis-generating site-level signal.
- Maps the evidence gap: no furcation studies, no standardized peri-implantitis definition, and maintenance quality not separable from residual PPD effect.

## Methodology

Registered (PROSPERO CRD42024567630) PRISMA-guided review; searched MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, Embase and Cochrane CENTRAL up to December 2025, plus manual journal and reference searches. Eligible: patients with >=1 implant and a full-mouth periodontal exam after active therapy and before placement/loading, exposure residual PPD >4 mm and/or furcation involvement >=II, with >=1 year follow-up after loading; exclusively diabetic or exclusively smoking cohorts excluded. Duplicate independent screening (kappa 0.93 and 0.97) and Newcastle-Ottawa Scale appraisal (kappa 0.98); qualitative synthesis because of heterogeneity. Primary outcome peri-implantitis incidence; secondary outcomes implant loss, mucositis, peri-implant clinical parameters, marginal bone level.

| Vagia 2021 | Per residual site with PPD >3 mm: PIKA HR 1.06 (1.02-1.10), PIBE HR 1.07 (1.03-1.12); peri-implantitis in 14 implants (12 in the residual-pocket group of 157 implants, 2 in the non-residual group of 103); survival 99.2% |
| Zhang 2018 | >10% of sites with PPD >=6 mm: 21 of 24 peri-implantitis implants vs 3 of 24; HR 3.62 (1.05-12.51) in Table 2 but HR 6.71 (1.84-24.47) in the text; peri-implantitis 11.2% implant level / 16% patient level |
