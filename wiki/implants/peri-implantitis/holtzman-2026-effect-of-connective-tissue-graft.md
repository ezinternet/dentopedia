## Three-line Summary

Dual-center RCT (Rome, Italy and Belgrade, Serbia; n=32 patients, 16 per arm; 12-month follow-up, no dropouts) comparing GBR plus subepithelial connective tissue graft (test) vs GBR alone (control) for surgical treatment of peri-implantitis, stratified by baseline keratinized mucosa (KM <2 mm vs ≥2 mm).

At 12 months CAL gain was greater with CTG (3.21 ± 1.57 mm vs 1.65 ± 1.28 mm; p = 0.022; primary outcome) and keratinized mucosa width gain was greater (2.25 ± 2.89 mm vs 0.26 ± 1.49 mm; p = 0.010); PPD reduction favored CTG but did not reach significance (3.25 ± 1.59 vs 1.97 ± 1.23 mm; p = 0.052); disease resolution was not different (EFP criteria 62.5% vs 56.3%, p = 0.719).

CTG adds clinical attachment and keratinized mucosa gain but did not change the binary disease-resolution endpoint at 1 year; with n=32, a single 12-month follow-up, and no histology, whether the soft-tissue benefit translates into long-term implant health remains unproven.

## 세줄요약

이중 기관 무작위대조시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)(이탈리아 로마·세르비아 베오그라드; 환자 32명, 군당 16명; 12개월 추적, 탈락 없음): 임플란트주위염 수술치료에서 골유도재생술(Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) + 상피하 결합조직이식(subepithelial Connective Tissue Graft, CTG)(시험군) vs GBR 단독(대조군) 비교, 기저 각화점막(Keratinized Mucosa, KM) <2 mm / ≥2 mm로 층화.

12개월 임상부착수준(Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득량은 CTG군이 더 컸고(3.21 ± 1.57 mm vs 1.65 ± 1.28 mm; p = 0.022; 일차 결과), 각화점막 폭(KMW) 증가도 더 컸다(2.25 ± 2.89 mm vs 0.26 ± 1.49 mm; p = 0.010). 탐침깊이(Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 감소는 CTG군에서 더 컸으나 유의하지 않았고(3.25 ± 1.59 vs 1.97 ± 1.23 mm; p = 0.052), 질환 해소율은 차이가 없었다(EFP 기준 62.5% vs 56.3%, p = 0.719).

CTG는 부착 획득과 각화점막 증가를 더하지만 1년 시점 이진 질환 해소 지표는 바꾸지 못했다. n=32, 12개월 추적 1회, 조직학 부재로 연조직 이점이 장기 임플란트 건강으로 이어지는지는 입증되지 않았다.

## Summary

This dual-center randomized trial (Eastman Dental Hospital, Rome, and University of Belgrade) tested whether adding an autogenous palatal subepithelial connective tissue graft to guided bone regeneration improves surgical peri-implantitis treatment. Thirty-two patients (16 per arm) received GBR with collagenated deproteinized bovine bone mineral and a resorbable membrane, with or without the graft, and all completed 12 months. The graft increased clinical attachment level gain (3.21 ± 1.57 vs 1.65 ± 1.28 mm; p = 0.022) and keratinized mucosa width (2.25 ± 2.89 vs 0.26 ± 1.49 mm; p = 0.010), but disease resolution was statistically indistinguishable (62.5% vs 56.3% by EFP criteria). The benefit is therefore a soft-tissue and attachment gain, not a demonstrated improvement in the binary success endpoint at one year.

## Key Contributions
- First RCT (per the authors) of reconstructive peri-implantitis treatment with vs without a subepithelial connective tissue graft.
- Adjunctive CTG increased CAL gain and KMW gain at 12 months; PPD reduction favored CTG (p = 0.052, not significant).
- Disease resolution did not differ by either definition (EFP 62.5% vs 56.3%; Derks 62.5% vs 50.0%), and the effect did not differ by KM stratum (interaction p = 0.942 and p = 0.674).
- The GBR-only control arm reached 56.3% (EFP) and 50.0% (Derks) disease resolution, with milder baseline disease (mean baseline PPD 6.3 and 5.6 mm; bone loss 3.72 and 2.92 mm) than Derks 2022 (8.6 mm; 6.1 mm), which the authors cite for their higher success rates.

## Methodology
Multicenter parallel-group RCT, 1:1, stratified by baseline KM (<2 mm vs ≥2 mm), allocation revealed after flap reflection, blinded clinical and radiographic assessors. Peri-implantitis defined as PPD ≥6 mm with profuse BoP/suppuration and radiographic bone loss beyond early remodeling (implants loaded ≥1 year). Both arms: titanium curette degranulation, titanium brush, erythritol air-polishing, saline irrigation, collagenated DBBM block (Bio-Oss Collagen) and resorbable membrane (Biogide), coronally advanced flap, perioperative amoxicillin-clavulanic acid (10 days). Test arm added a de-epithelialized palatal graft (~1.5 mm) fixed 1 mm apical to the buccal flap margin. Follow-up at 6 and 12 months (radiographs at baseline and 12 months). Primary outcome CAL; nonparametric statistics. Funded by an Osteology Foundation Young Research Grant; no declared conflicts. Registered NCT04323540.

## Results
| Outcome (12 months) | CTG + GBR (n=16) | GBR alone (n=16) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAL gain (mm) | 3.21 ± 1.57 | 1.65 ± 1.28 | 0.022 |
| PPD reduction (mm) | 3.25 ± 1.59 | 1.97 ± 1.23 | 0.052 |
| KMW change (mm) | 2.25 ± 2.89 | 0.26 ± 1.49 | 0.010 |
| Mean bone level baseline to 12 months (mm) | 3.72 ± 1.49 to 1.05 ± 0.90 | 2.92 ± 1.30 to 1.63 ± 1.19 | between-group NS at both time points (0.188; 0.320); time-by-group interaction p < 0.001 |
| Disease resolution, EFP | 62.5% | 56.3% | 0.719 (OR 1.30, 95% CI 0.32-5.33) |
| Disease resolution, Derks | 62.5% | 50.0% | 0.477 (OR 1.67, 95% CI 0.41-6.82) |
| Surgical time (min) | 68.5 ± 26.0 | 60.3 ± 15.8 | NS overall; longer with CTG in KM <2 mm stratum (p = 0.040) |

Buccal recession remained below 1 mm in both groups with no between-group difference. Mucosal thickness rose then fell in the test group (T0-T1 p = 0.027; T1-T2 p = 0.012) with no between-group difference. No membrane exposure, necrosis, or suppuration was recorded in early healing. Caveats: the absolute KMW comparison at 12 months was not significant (p = 0.915), the study was powered for CAL, and no histology was done.
