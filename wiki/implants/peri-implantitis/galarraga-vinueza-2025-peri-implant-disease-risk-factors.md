## Three-line Summary

AO/AAP Best-Evidence Consensus SR+MA (102 studies, 13,030 patients; PROSPERO registered; PECOS framework) providing comprehensive epidemiology and risk indicators for peri-implant diseases using 2018 EFP/AAP case definitions.

Prevalence: peri-implant mucositis 46% (95% CI 41–51%) and peri-implantitis 21% (95% CI 17–24%); 20-year cumulative incidence: mucositis 53%, peri-implantitis 22%; periodontitis and smoking were consistent risk indicators for both conditions.

Obesity is a risk indicator specific to peri-implant mucositis; diabetes mellitus and alcohol consumption are additional risk indicators for peri-implantitis; all associations are observational (risk indicators, not confirmed causal factors).

## Summary
AO/AAP Best-Evidence Consensus systematic review and meta-analysis of 102 studies involving 13,030 patients, providing the most comprehensive and up-to-date epidemiological data on peri-implant diseases. Prevalence: mucositis 46% (CI 41–51%), peri-implantitis 21% (CI 17–24%). Over 20 years, cumulative incidence: mucositis 53%, peri-implantitis 22%. Identifies periodontitis and smoking as consistent risk indicators for both conditions.

## Key Contributions
- Largest and most comprehensive SR+MA on peri-implant disease epidemiology to date
- First to concurrently assess systemic (diabetes, obesity, CVD), behavioral (smoking, alcohol), and patient characteristics as risk indicators
- Confirms periodontitis as the strongest risk indicator for both mucositis and peri-implantitis
- Identifies obesity as risk indicator for mucositis; diabetes and alcohol for peri-implantitis

## Methodology
- PRISMA-compliant; PROSPERO CRD4202346657800
- PECOS framework
- Databases: MEDLINE-PubMed, EMBASE, Cochrane, ClinicalTrials.gov
- Random-effects pooled analyses
- Limitation: observational designs → risk indicators only, not confirmed causal risk factors

## Results
| Outcome | Prevalence (95% CI) | 20-yr Incidence |
|---------|---------------------|----------------|
| Peri-implant mucositis | 46% (41–51%) | 53% |
| Peri-implantitis | 21% (17–24%) | 22% |

**Risk indicators for peri-implant mucositis**: periodontitis, obesity, smoking habits
**Risk indicators for peri-implantitis**: periodontitis, diabetes mellitus, smoking habits, alcohol consumption

### Meta-analysed effects behind the risk indicators

| Indicator | Effect summary (patient level) | I² | Egger p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smoking → peri-implantitis | OR 2.27 (95% CI 1.68–3.06) | 65.2% | 0.4827 |
| **Alcohol → peri-implantitis** (3 studies) | **OR 2.07 (95% CI 1.49–2.85)** | **0.0%** | 0.271 |

- Alcohol's three contributing studies reported patient-level ORs from 1.71 to 2.89 (the highest, Costa et al., OR 2.9, 95% CI 1.09–4.14, p = 0.01).
- Homogeneity (I² = 0.0%) despite only three studies means the *direction* is stable, not that the estimate is precise.
- The authors' own caveat: alcohol's association is supported by **limited evidence**, dose/frequency are unexamined, and more studies are needed before treating it as a confirmed risk factor.
- Mechanistic rationale offered is host immunity — reduced neutrophil, macrophage, and T-cell function — not a direct effect on bone.
