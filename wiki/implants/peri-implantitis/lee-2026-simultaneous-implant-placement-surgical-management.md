## Three-line Summary

Two-case report (Seoul National University Dental Hospital; 3-year follow-up) of implant placed simultaneously with flap debridement and collagenated deproteinized bovine bone mineral grafting of an adjacent compromised site — a residual periodontal intrabony defect (Case 1) or an adjacent peri-implantitis defect (Case 2).

At 3 years both sites were stable with radiographic bone fill of 3.9 mm (tooth #35) and 4.1 mm (implant #16), clinical attachment level gains of 1.7 and 1.8 mm, and the new implants (#36, #15) at −0.7 mm radiographic bone level with PPD ≤3.0 mm and ≤4.0 mm, no recession and no progressive bone loss.

Simultaneous management may avoid staged surgery and reduce morbidity in selected patients with deep 3-wall defects and good plaque control, but two cases without histologic confirmation or a standardized protocol cannot establish generalizability.

## 세줄요약

2증례 보고 (서울대학교 치과병원; 3년 추적관찰): 임플란트 (Implant) 식립과 동시에 인접 손상 부위 — 잔여 치주 골내 결손 (Residual Periodontal Intrabony Defect; 증례 1) 또는 인접 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 골내 결손 (증례 2) — 를 플랩 거상 후 세정하고 교원질 탈단백 소골 무기질 (Collagenated Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral)로 골이식함.

3년 시점에 두 부위 모두 안정적이며 방사선학적 골충전은 3.9 mm (치아 #35), 4.1 mm (임플란트 #16), 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득은 각 1.7·1.8 mm; 신규 식립 임플란트 (#36, #15)는 방사선학적 골수준 (Radiographic Bone Level, RBL) −0.7 mm, 탐침깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) ≤3.0·≤4.0 mm, 후퇴 (Recession, REC) 없이 진행성 골소실 없음.

선택된 환자에서 단계적 술식을 줄이고 술 후 불편을 낮출 수 있으나, 2증례·조직학적 확인 부재·술식 비표준화로 일반화된 프로토콜은 확립 불가 — 신중한 증례 선택·세심한 세정·지지요법 (Supportive Periodontal Therapy)이 전제.

## Summary

This case report from Seoul National University challenges the conventional staged approach to a compromised site adjacent to a planned implant: instead of first treating the residual periodontal or peri-implant lesion and placing the implant in a second operation, the authors elevated a full-thickness flap, debrided the adjacent lesion, placed the new implant and grafted both defects in one procedure. Case 1 treated a 10 mm 3-wall intrabony defect on the distal of tooth #35 alongside a new implant at #36; Case 2 treated a continuous 9 mm Class 1b 3-wall defect spanning the #15 extraction socket and the mesial of an existing peri-implantitis-affected implant #16, using titanium-brush decontamination and a double-layer collagen membrane while retaining the existing prosthesis. At 3 years, both adjacent sites showed radiographic bone fill (3.9 mm and 4.1 mm) and both new implants were functionally stable with no inflammation or progressive bone loss. The comparable gain despite the wider defect in Case 2 is attributed to the 3-wall configuration containing the graft and clot. With only two cases, no histology and slightly different surgical protocols, the authors present this as a feasible strategy for carefully selected patients rather than a generalizable protocol.

## Key Contributions

- First description, to the authors' knowledge, of **simultaneous** new implant placement and surgical management of an adjacent compromised tooth or implant lesion, avoiding a staged procedure.
- Demonstrates the approach in two biologically distinct settings: a residual periodontal intrabony defect (Case 1) and an adjacent peri-implantitis defect (Case 2).
- Combines titanium brush, hydrogen peroxide and doxycycline irrigation for implant surface decontamination with collagenated deproteinized bovine bone mineral grafting and (Case 2) a double-layer resorbable membrane.
- Retains the existing prosthesis in Case 2 (transmucosal healing), simplifying treatment without evidence of compromised 3-year outcomes.
- Documents stable 3-year clinical and radiographic outcomes at both the treated adjacent site and the newly placed implant in each patient.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Two-case report (CARE guidelines; IRB ERI25017), with clinical and radiographic parameters recorded pre-surgery and at 3 years.
- **Population**: n=2 patients (70-year-old non-smoking woman with hypertension, ASA-II; 69-year-old man smoking ~2/3 pack/day, no systemic disease, ASA-II); 2 new implants and 2 adjacent compromised sites.
- **Intervention**: Lingually/palatally shifted crestal incision without vertical releasing incisions, full-thickness flap elevation, granulation-tissue debridement and root planing; Case 1 added a Straumann BL Ø4.8 × 8 mm implant at #36 plus BioOss-Collagen to both defects; Case 2 added a Straumann BLT Ø4.1 × 12 mm implant at #15, titanium-brush/H₂O₂/doxycycline decontamination of #16, BioOss-Collagen and a double-layer Bio-Gide membrane. Final prostheses were connected at 5 months in both cases.
- **Outcomes**: PPD, REC, KGW/KMW, vestibular depth, PI/mPI, SBI/mSBI, FMPI and RBL (six sites per tooth/implant); bone fill derived from baseline-to-3-year RBL change.
- **Follow-up**: 3 years.

## Results

| Outcome | Case 1 — tooth #35 (grafted) | Case 1 — new implant #36 | Case 2 — implant #16 (regenerated) | Case 2 — new implant #15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mean PPD at 3 years | 3.0 mm (Δ −2.5 mm) | 2.5 mm (≤3.0 mm) | 3.5 mm (Δ −1.8 mm) | 3.3 mm (≤4.0 mm) |
| Mean REC change | +0.8 mm (more recession) | None | None | None |
| KGW/KMW | Δ −2.0 mm | 4.0 mm | Δ +1.0 mm | 3.0 mm |
| Plaque / bleeding index | PI unchanged; SBI −1 | mPI 1, mSBI 0 | mPI/mSBI unchanged | mPI 1, mSBI 1 |
| FMPI | 25.8% → 16.7% (−9.1 pp) | — | decreased 11.62% (authors' report) | — |
| Radiographic bone level / fill | **3.9 mm bone fill** | **−0.7 mm** vs shoulder | **4.1 mm bone fill** | **−0.7 mm** vs shoulder |
| CAL gain | 1.7 mm | — | 1.8 mm | — |
| 3-year status | Bone fill, no inflammation | Stable, no bone loss | Stable, no inflammation | Stable, no inflammation |

- Both newly placed implants remained functionally stable with healthy peri-implant soft tissue and no signs of inflammation or progressive bone loss at 3 years.
- A slight increase in recession on the distal of tooth #14 was observed in Case 2, without inflammation.
- The authors note the Case 2 defect was wider (extending into the new implant site) yet yielded comparable bone gain, crediting the shared 3-wall configuration.
