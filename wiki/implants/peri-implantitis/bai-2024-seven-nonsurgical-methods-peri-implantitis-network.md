## Three-line Summary

Network meta-analysis (33 RCTs, 2004–2023) comparing 7 non-surgical adjuncts — laser, PBMT, PDT, systemic antibiotics (SA), probiotics, local antimicrobials (LA), air-powder polishing (APP) — each combined with mechanical debridement (MD) vs MD alone, in peri-implantitis (PI) and peri-implant mucositis (PM).

For PI, PBMT+MD ranked best for pocket probing depth (PPD, SUCRA 75.3%) and SA+MD ranked best for clinical attachment level (CAL, SUCRA 87.4%) and marginal bone loss (MBL, SUCRA 99.9%); for PM, probiotics+MD ranked best for PPD (SUCRA 100%) and plaque index (PLI, SUCRA 83.2%), and SA+MD ranked best for bleeding on probing (BoP, SUCRA 88.1%).

Optimal adjunct differs by outcome and disease stage, so treatment choice should be individualized; evidence is limited by few large direct-comparison trials, heterogeneous 3–12-month follow-up, and small CAL/MBL study numbers, and rankings should still be weighed against guidelines cautioning against routine systemic antibiotic use.

## 세줄요약

네트워크 메타분석(33편 RCT, 2004~2023)이 laser·PBMT·PDT·전신항생제(SA)·프로바이오틱스·국소항균제(LA)·에어파우더폴리싱(APP) 7개 비외과적 보조요법을 기계적 세정(MD)과 병용해 peri-implantitis(PI)·peri-implant mucositis(PM)에서 비교했다.

PI에서는 PBMT+MD가 치주낭깊이(PPD) 개선 1위(SUCRA 75.3%), SA+MD가 임상부착수준(CAL, SUCRA 87.4%)과 변연골소실(MBL, SUCRA 99.9%) 개선 1위였고, PM에서는 프로바이오틱스+MD가 PPD(SUCRA 100%)·치태지수(PLI, SUCRA 83.2%) 개선 1위, SA+MD가 탐침시출혈(BoP) 개선 1위(SUCRA 88.1%)였다.

지표·질환단계별로 최적 보조요법이 달라 개별화 선택이 필요하며, 대규모 직접비교 RCT 부족과 추적기간 이질성(3~12개월)이 한계이고 전신항생제 신중사용을 권고하는 최신 가이드라인과 함께 해석해야 한다.

## Summary

This network meta-analysis (NMA) pooled 33 RCTs (2004–2023, PROSPERO CRD42023447616) to rank 7 non-surgical adjunctive therapies — laser, photobiomodulation therapy (PBMT), photodynamic therapy (PDT), systemic antibiotics (SA), probiotics, local antimicrobials (LA), and air-powder polishing (APP) — each combined with mechanical debridement (MD), against MD alone, across peri-implantitis (PI) and peri-implant mucositis (PM). It is the first NMA to simultaneously compare all 7 modalities and to extend beyond PPD/BoP into plaque index (PLI), clinical attachment level (CAL), and marginal bone loss (MBL). Results show that no single adjunct dominates across outcomes: the best-ranked treatment depends on which clinical indicator matters most (soft-tissue inflammation vs bone/attachment level) and on disease stage (PI vs PM).

## Key Contributions

- First 7-arm NMA of non-surgical PI/PM adjuncts, combining direct + indirect RCT evidence via SUCRA ranking.
- Extends prior pairwise meta-analyses (which focused on PPD/BoP) to also quantify PLI, CAL, and MBL — outcomes tied to antimicrobial efficacy and hard/soft tissue healing.
- Provides an evidence map for individualized adjunct selection when equipment (laser/PDT devices) or patient factors (antibiotic allergy/resistance) limit options.

## Methodology

- PRISMA/Cochrane-compliant systematic review + frequentist random-effects NMA (STATA 17.0, Network package); searches of PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Library through July 25, 2023.
- Adults ≥18y with implant-diagnosed PI or PM (2018 Classification); intervention = non-surgical adjunct + MD vs MD alone; RCTs only, ≥5 patients/group, ≥3-month follow-up.
- Screening funnel: 2,244 records → 887 duplicates removed → 1,299 excluded at title/abstract → 58 full-text reviewed → **33 RCTs included**.
- Modality distribution: MD+laser (8 studies), MD+PBMT (2), MD+PDT (3), MD+SA (6), MD+probiotics (4), MD+LA (10), MD+APP (3).
- Risk of bias (Cochrane RoB tool): 10 studies low overall risk, 4 high risk, remainder some risk.
- SMD with 95% CI; SUCRA (0–100%) ranking; global/local (node-split) inconsistency testing; sensitivity analysis excluding poor/fair-quality trials; funnel plots for publication bias (low bias detected).

## Results

**By outcome and disease stage, top-ranked adjunct (SUCRA) and significance vs MD alone:**

| Disease | Outcome (studies) | Top adjunct | SUCRA | SMD vs MD alone (95% CI) | Significant? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PI | PPD reduction (32 RCTs) | PBMT+MD | 75.3% | not directly reported vs MD (PBMT arm underpowered); SA+MD 1.45 (0.12–2.78), LA+MD 1.30 (0.03–2.56) | SA+MD, LA+MD sig. |
| PI | CAL change (7 studies) | SA+MD | 87.4% | 2.20 (0.38–4.02) | Yes |
| PI | MBL change (6 RCTs) | SA+MD | 99.9% | 3.92 (2.90–4.93) | Yes |
| PM | PPD reduction | Probiotics+MD | 100% | 0.37 (−0.23–0.97) | **No** (top-ranked but NS) |
| PM | PPD reduction (other sig. adjuncts) | PBMT+MD / SA+MD / LA+MD / APP+MD | — | 1.11 (0.38–1.84) / 1.03 (0.02–2.03) / 3.59 (2.43–4.76) / 3.70 (2.58–4.82) | Yes, all 4 |
| PM | BoP reduction (7 RCTs) | SA+MD | 88.1% | 0.77 (0.27–1.28) | Yes |
| PM | BoP reduction (2nd) | Probiotics+MD | 75.7% | 0.60 (0.14–1.06) | Yes |
| PM | PLI reduction (6 RCTs) | Probiotics+MD | 83.2% | — | No pairwise comparison significant |

**Key pattern**: SA+MD is the strongest performer for bone/attachment-level outcomes (CAL, MBL) in PI and for BoP in PM, but the 2023 EFP S3 guideline (Herrera et al., [[implants/herrera-2023-prevention-treatment-peri-implant-efp]]) recommends against routine systemic antibiotic use for peri-implant disease due to patient/public-health risk — the authors explicitly flag this tension and recommend prioritizing other adjuncts, especially for PM and mild PI. Probiotics+MD ranks first by SUCRA for PM soft-tissue outcomes (PPD, PLI) but did not reach statistical significance vs MD alone for PPD — consistent with a narrative-review finding (below) that chemical/pharmaceutical PM adjuncts show within-group but not consistently significant added benefit over mechanical debridement alone.

Publication bias assessed as low via funnel-plot symmetry.
