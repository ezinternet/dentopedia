## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (qualitative synthesis, no meta-analysis) of 17 long-term studies (≥5 years; 23 treatment arms, 839 patients, 1326 implants, mean follow-up 75.4 months) on surgical treatment of peri-implantitis under supportive peri-implant care (~3×/year).

Mean disease resolution was 58.6% (range 30.4–97.7%), progressive bone loss arrestment 69.6%, BOP resolution 59.9%, retreatment need 27.2%, and implant survival 88.6% (75–100%); residual pockets ≥6 mm carried ~8× higher odds of recurrence (OR 7.3–9.2).

Surgical therapy is effective but not curative in the long term — advanced baseline bone loss, residual pockets ≥6 mm, keratinized mucosa <2 mm, and modified implant surfaces predict recurrence; high heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis.

## 세줄요약

임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis) 외과적 치료의 장기(≥5년) 결과를 검토한 체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review, 정성적 종합·메타분석 불가) — 17개 연구(23개 치료군, 환자 839명, 임플란트 1326개, 평균 추적 75.4개월), 임플란트 주위 지지치료(Supportive Peri-implant Care, 약 3회/년) 하에서 분석.

평균 질환 해소율(disease resolution)은 58.6%(범위 30.4–97.7%), 진행성 골소실 억제율 69.6%, 출혈탐침(Bleeding on Probing, BOP) 소실률 59.9%, 재치료 필요률 27.2%, 임플란트 생존율 88.6%(75–100%); 술 후 잔여 주머니(residual pocket) ≥6 mm면 재발 오즈비(Odds Ratio, OR)가 약 8배(OR 7.3–9.2).

외과 치료는 장기적으로 유효하지만 근치(cure)는 아님 — 기저 골소실의 진행 정도, 잔여 주머니 ≥6 mm, 각화점막(Keratinized Mucosa, KM) 폭 <2 mm, 변형된 임플란트 표면이 재발 예측인자; 연구 간 이질성이 커 메타분석은 불가했다.

## Summary

This Periodontology 2000 systematic review asked what surgical treatment of peri-implantitis actually achieves at ≥5 years. Across 17 studies (10 retrospective cohorts, 5 prospective cohorts, 1 RCT, 1 case series; 23 arms, 839 patients, 1326 implants; mean follow-up 75.4 months) conducted under supportive peri-implant care (~3×/year), mean composite disease resolution was 58.6%, progressive bone-loss arrestment 69.6%, BOP elimination 59.9%, and implant survival 88.6% — with retreatment required in 27.2% of cases. Residual pockets ≥6 mm after surgery were the strongest recurrence predictor (~8× odds; OR 7.3–9.2), joined by extent of bone loss (OR 1.3), KM width <2 mm, and modified implant surfaces. The clinical message: surgical peri-implantitis therapy is long-term effective but not curative, and its predictability collapses in advanced lesions or when deep pockets persist after surgery. Heterogeneity in disease-resolution definitions and protocols precluded meta-analysis, and no included study reported mucosal recession or patient-reported outcomes.

## Key Contributions

- Dedicated long-term (≥5 year) synthesis of **surgical** peri-implantitis outcomes — 17 studies, 839 patients, 1326 implants, mean 75.4 months follow-up.
- Quantifies the realistic ceiling of surgery: ~60% disease resolution, ~70% bone-loss arrestment, ~90% implant survival, ~27% retreatment.
- Identifies residual pockets ≥6 mm post-surgery as the dominant modifiable recurrence risk (~8× odds; OR 7.3–9.2).
- Secondary recurrence predictors: extent of bone loss (OR 1.3), KM width <2 mm, modified implant surfaces.
- Exposes reporting gaps: zero studies reported mucosal recession or PROs; retreatment outcomes rarely evaluated.

## Methodology

Systematic review (MEDLINE/PubMed + EMBASE, through June 2025; no language restriction; reference-list hand search). PICOS: adults with ≥1 functioning implant with peri-implantitis (2017 AAP/EFP definition or clear composite criteria); any inflammation-resolving intervention; human clinical trials (RCT/cohort/case-control) with ≥5-year post-treatment follow-up and ≥10 patients. n = 17 studies / 23 arms / 839 patients / 1326 implants. Outcomes: composite disease resolution, PPD/BOP/suppuration, marginal bone level, implant survival, PROs, prognostic factors. Qualitative (narrative) synthesis with mean/range calculations and scatter plots; meta-analysis not attempted due to heterogeneity. Screening: 5840 + 455 records → 41 full texts → 17 included.

## Results

| Outcome | Mean (range) | Arms |
|---|---|---|
| Disease resolution (composite) | 58.6% (30.4–97.7%) | 14 |
| — reconstructive | 62.02% (36.3–97.7%) | — |
| — non-reconstructive | 53.98% (30.4–89%) | — |
| Progressive bone loss arrestment (≥1 mm) | 69.6% (36.3–97.7%) | 14 |
| — reconstructive vs non-reconstructive | 71.86% vs 63.7% | — |
| BOP elimination | 59.9% (18.7–93.4%) | 16 |
| Need for retreatment | 27.2% (7.8–54%) | 7 |
| Implant survival | 88.6% (75–100%) | 19 |

**Recurrence predictors** (4 studies): residual pocket ≥6 mm OR 7.3–9.2; extent of bone loss OR 1.3; KM width <2 mm post-surgery; modified implant surface. Reconstructive vs non-reconstructive survival showed no difference. Mucosal recession and PROs: not reported in any study.
