## Three-line Summary

Single-blind, two-arm parallel RCT (1:1) at University Medical Center Groningen: 59 patients with peri-implantitis (no, one-wall or two-wall bone defects) who failed non-surgical therapy received access-flap surgery with (n=29) or without (n=30) amoxicillin/metronidazole 500/500 mg three times daily for 1 week; follow-up 12 months.

Mean peri-implant BOP was lower in the antibiotic group at T9 (-10.57%, 95% CI -20.17 to -0.97, p=0.03) and T12 (-14.47%, 95% CI -25.9 to -3.04, p=0.01); mean PPD (T12 -0.14 mm, p=0.54), SOP, MBL and composite disease resolution (39.6% vs 23.8%) showed no significant between-group differences at any timepoint.

An isolated BOP effect with no benefit on PPD, MBL or disease resolution does not support routine systemic antibiotics after surgical peri-implantitis therapy; limits are 1-year follow-up, no microbiological data, no examiner calibration and low power for the binary outcome.

## 세줄요약

단일맹검 평행군 무작위대조시험 (Randomised Controlled Trial, RCT, 1:1; 흐로닝언 대학병원): 비외과적 치료에 반응하지 않은 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 환자 59명 (골결손 없음·1벽·2벽)이 접근판막 수술 단독 (n=30) 또는 수술 + 아목시실린/메트로니다졸 (Amoxicillin/Metronidazole) 500/500 mg 1일 3회 1주 (n=29)로 배정되어 12개월 추적됐다.

항생제군의 평균 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)이 T9 (-10.57%, 95% CI -20.17 ~ -0.97, p=0.03)와 T12 (-14.47%, 95% CI -25.9 ~ -3.04, p=0.01)에서 유의하게 낮았으나, 탐침 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD; T12 -0.14 mm, p=0.54), 화농 (Suppuration on Probing, SOP), 변연골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL), 복합 질환 해소율 (39.6% vs 23.8%)은 어느 시점에도 유의차가 없었다.

BOP 단일 지표의 효과만 있고 PPD·MBL·질환 해소의 이득이 없어 수술 후 전신 항생제의 상시 사용은 지지되지 않으며, 한계는 1년 추적, 미생물 검사 부재, 검사자 보정 미시행, 이분형 결과의 낮은 검정력이다.

## Summary

This investigator-blinded RCT randomised 59 patients with peri-implantitis (no, one-wall or two-wall defects) who did not reach resolution after non-surgical submarginal instrumentation to access-flap surgery with (n=29) or without (n=30) one week of amoxicillin/metronidazole 500/500 mg three times daily. Surgery was standardised (titanium curettes, air polishing, 35% phosphoric acid for 1 min, final air polishing). In linear and logistic mixed-effects models, only mean BOP differed: it was lower in the antibiotic group at T9 (-10.57%, p=0.03) and T12 (-14.47%, p=0.01). PPD, SOP, MBL, recession, keratinised mucosa and composite disease resolution did not differ at any timepoint. The authors judge the clinical relevance of the isolated BOP effect uncertain and stress antibiotic stewardship.

## Key Contributions

- Randomised comparison of adjunctive amoxicillin/metronidazole vs none on top of identical access-flap surgery in no-, one- and two-wall defects.
- Only BOP (T9, T12) differed significantly; PPD (T12 -0.14 mm, p=0.54) and MBL (T12 -0.02 mm, p=0.97) did not.
- Exploratory post hoc subgroup analyses using EFP-guideline thresholds (baseline PPD >= 7 mm; baseline suppuration): SOP difference at T6 only (p=0.02), not sustained at T9 and T12.
- Adverse events were predominantly none to mild; the control group reported more pain (p=0.01), gland swelling (p<0.01) and discolouration (p=0.04).

## Methodology

Two-armed, parallel RCT (1:1), fixed block size of four, sealed computer-generated envelopes; investigator and outcome assessor blinded. 123 screened (January 2020 - August 2023), 59 randomised (49 test vs 46 control implants with peri-implantitis). All patients first received non-surgical debridement with air polishing; no patient reached disease resolution, so all were operated. Primary outcomes mean PPD and mean BOP (PPD was added as primary after study start to align with the EFP guideline); secondary outcomes composite disease resolution (PPD <= 5 mm, no BOP at one or more sites, no SOP), SOP, MBL, recession, keratinised mucosa. Assessments at T3, T6, T9, T12; mixed-effects models with last observation carried forward; sample size target 61 patients (at least 51 for 80% power at effect size 0.5).

## Clinical Interpretation

The evidence here supports at most a modest, BOP-only advantage at 9-12 months for one week of amoxicillin/metronidazole after access-flap surgery, without gains in pocket depth, bone level or composite resolution. The authors conclude that the benefit must be weighed against antibiotic-related risks and stewardship principles (narrowest spectrum, shortest duration), and that the EFP guideline's PPD >= 7 mm or suppuration criterion was written for non-surgical therapy and does not directly extend to surgery. Limitations: 1-year follow-up (3 years recommended in the literature they cite), mixed dentate and edentulous patients, no microbiology, packaging-based adherence, no examiner calibration, and limited power for binary outcomes.
