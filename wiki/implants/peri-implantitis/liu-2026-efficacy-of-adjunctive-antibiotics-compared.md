## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD420261328879; five databases to 15 January 2026) of 22 RCTs, 1028 patients (487 antibiotic, 541 non-antibiotic), testing adjunctive local or systemic antibiotics added to mechanical debridement for peri-implantitis against mechanical debridement alone or plus another non-antibiotic adjunct (probiotic, photodynamic therapy, chlorhexidine, enamel matrix derivative, iodine glycerin).

Pooled benefit was modest: PPD WMD −0.69 mm (95% CI −1.04 to −0.33; I² = 91%), CAL −0.55 mm (−1.07 to −0.04; I² = 95%), plaque score −4.07 (−6.80 to −1.33; I² = 0%), BOP −12.45 percentage points (−24.13 to −0.77; I² = 94%); bone level (WMD 0.03 mm, P = 0.39) and mPLI (P = 0.09) did not differ, and local and systemic routes gave similar PPD gains (−0.68 vs −0.70 mm).

Authors conclude the evidence does not support routine antibiotic use; key limitations are very high heterogeneity, pooled heterogeneous controls, no stratification by surgical vs non-surgical setting, unreported smoking/dose/duration data, and sparse adverse-event and resistance reporting.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA; PROSPERO CRD420261328879; 5개 데이터베이스, 2026년 1월 15일까지) — 22편 RCT, 환자 1,028명 (항생제군 487명, 비항생제군 541명). 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis)에서 기계적 변연청결 (Mechanical Debridement, MD)에 국소·전신 항생제를 추가한 효과를, MD 단독 또는 MD + 다른 비항생제 보조요법 (프로바이오틱스, 광역학요법, 클로르헥시딘 등)과 비교했다.

통합 이득은 작았다: 탐침 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) WMD −0.69 mm (95% CI −1.04 ~ −0.33; I² = 91%), 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) −0.55 mm (−1.07 ~ −0.04; I² = 95%), 치태 점수 (Plaque Score, PS) −4.07 (−6.80 ~ −1.33; I² = 0%), 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP) −12.45%p (−24.13 ~ −0.77; I² = 94%). 골 수준 (Bone Level, BL)은 WMD 0.03 mm (P = 0.39)로 차이가 없었고, 국소·전신 경로의 PPD 이득은 비슷했다 (−0.68 vs −0.70 mm).

저자들은 근거가 상시 사용을 뒷받침하지 않는다고 결론짓는다. 핵심 한계는 매우 높은 이질성, 이질적 대조군 통합, 외과/비외과 미구분, 흡연·용량·기간 자료 부재, 이상반응·내성 보고 빈약이다.

## Summary

This meta-analysis pooled 22 RCTs (1028 patients) on antibiotics added to mechanical debridement for peri-implantitis, defined as bone loss ≥2 mm, PPD ≥5 mm and BOP. Controls were debridement alone or debridement plus a non-antibiotic adjunct, and the antibiotics were minocycline, amoxicillin, metronidazole, azithromycin and doxycycline, given locally or systemically. Antibiotics gave modest gains in PPD (−0.69 mm), CAL (−0.55 mm), plaque score and BOP, but not in bone level, and heterogeneity was I² 91–95% for PPD, BOP and CAL. Local and systemic routes were comparable for PPD, and the authors conclude that antibiotics are selected adjuncts rather than routine therapy, with mechanical therapy remaining the cornerstone.

## Key Contributions

- A larger RCT pool (22 trials) than the earlier non-surgical antibiotic SR (6 RCTs), with a predefined local vs systemic contrast.
- Confirms a small, mostly soft-tissue benefit (PPD, CAL, plaque, BOP) and no radiographic bone-level benefit.
- Shows no PPD difference between local and systemic routes (between-subgroup P = 0.95), with plaque score improving only with local antibiotics (−6.57 vs −2.63, systemic not significant).
- Documents the evidence gaps: smoking reported by 10 of 22 trials, adverse events detailed in 6 of 22, and no resistance monitoring in any trial.

## Methodology

PRISMA 2020 SR+MA of RCTs searched in PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Scopus and the Cochrane Library plus ClinicalTrials.gov to 15 January 2026. Random-effects DerSimonian-Laird models were used when I² > 50%, with a Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman check, univariate meta-regression, leave-one-out sensitivity for PPD and an Egger test. Risk of bias used the Cochrane tool and certainty used GRADEpro. Outcomes were BL, BOP, PPD, CAL, mPLI and plaque score.

## Critical Appraisal

- The authors' framing is stronger than their data in places: the Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman CAL result (P = 0.0675) is not significant, BOP does not reach significance in the antibiotic-vs-debridement-alone subgroup (P = 0.07), and GRADE certainty for PPD is rated high despite I² = 91%.
- Pooling debridement alone with probiotic, photodynamic and antiseptic controls means the comparator is not one thing; the announced sensitivity analysis excluding active controls has no reported result in the retrieved text.
- No surgical vs non-surgical stratification is reported, so the estimates cannot be applied to a specific treatment step without caution; the SR+MA conclusion section also refers to "systemic" antibiotics although both routes were pooled.
- Mean PPD and CAL gains of roughly 0.5–0.7 mm are small beside the heterogeneity, and the authors' own long-term message is that antibiotics should be reserved for acute episodes or well-defined cases (for example deep pockets with suppuration, immunocompromised patients).
