## Three-line Summary

Non-inferiority RCT (n=30 patients; Yonsei University Dental Hospital, Seoul) comparing rotating titanium brush vs implantoplasty for surgical peri-implantitis surface decontamination at 12 months.

Titanium brush was non-inferior to implantoplasty for PPD reduction (−3.6 vs −3.3 mm); MBL remained stable with titanium brush (0.0 mm) vs additional bone loss with implantoplasty (0.7 mm; p=0.03); 0 vs 2 implants lost.

Titanium brush cut decontamination time by ~45% (3:01 vs 5:27 min; p=0.006); adjacent peri-implantitis implant was a novel pocket closure failure predictor; OHIP-14 similar between groups.

## 세줄요약

비열등성 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=30명; 연세대 치과대학병원, 서울): 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 수술 시 회전 티타늄 브러시 (Rotating Titanium Brush) vs 임플란토플라스티 (Implantoplasty) 표면 오염 제거 비교, 12개월 추적.

티타늄 브러시는 탐침깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 감소에서 비열등 (−3.6 vs −3.3 mm); 치조골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 변화: 티타늄 브러시 0.0 mm (안정) vs 임플란토플라스티 +0.7 mm 추가 소실 (p=0.03); 임플란트 소실 각 0개 vs 2개.

표면 오염 제거 시간 약 45% 단축 (3분 1초 vs 5분 27초; p=0.006); 포켓 폐쇄 실패 예측 인자: 기저 탐침깊이 (p=0.044), 인접 임플란트주위염 임플란트 (p=0.033); 구강건강영향지수 (OHIP-14) 양군 차이 없음.

## Summary

This non-inferiority RCT from Yonsei University is the first direct head-to-head comparison of rotating titanium brushes vs implantoplasty for implant surface decontamination in surgical peri-implantitis therapy. Thirty patients (15 per arm) underwent resective surgery by a single experienced periodontist; all procedures included osseous recontouring for crater-like defects and flap repositioning at bone level. At 12 months, titanium brushes demonstrated non-inferiority to implantoplasty for the primary endpoint (PPD reduction: −3.6 vs −3.3 mm), while offering two practical advantages: better marginal bone level preservation (0.0 vs 0.7 mm additional loss; p=0.03) and faster surface decontamination (~45% less time). The composite disease-resolution rate (40.0% test vs 26.7% control) was comparable to other surgical RCTs applying the strict EFP 2023 criteria. Both groups showed similar patient-reported outcomes, but the EFP guideline had previously flagged insufficient evidence for implantoplasty recommendations — this RCT provides the first controlled comparison and hints at a bone-preservation advantage for titanium brushes.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT directly comparing titanium brush vs implantoplasty for peri-implantitis surgical decontamination
- Titanium brush non-inferior for PPD reduction but superior for MBL preservation (p=0.03); 0 vs 2 implant losses
- Decontamination time significantly shorter with titanium brush (3:01 vs 5:27 min; p=0.006)
- Adjacent peri-implantitis implant identified as novel independent predictor of pocket closure failure (OR substantially reduced; p=0.033) — possible reflection of cross-contamination or prolonged surgical time
- OHIP-14 and patient experience scores similar; ease of oral hygiene unchanged despite exposed threads

## Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Non-inferiority RCT, 2-arm parallel, 1:1, concealed allocation |
| Setting | Yonsei University Dental Hospital, Seoul (Aug 2021 – Jan 2023) |
| n | 30 patients / 30 implants (15 test / 15 control) |
| Inclusion | Bone loss ≥3 mm + PPD ≥6 mm + BOP/suppuration post-non-surgical |
| Test | Rotating titanium brush (TN brush, Dentium; R-brush, Neobiotech) |
| Control | Implantoplasty: tungsten carbide burs + Arkansas stone → silica polishing burs |
| Surgeon | Single experienced periodontist (J.-K.C.) |
| Outcomes | Composite (implant survival + BOP ≤1 site + PPD ≤5 mm + no ΔMBLoss ≥0.5mm); PPD, BOP, MBL, OHIP-14 at 12 months |
| Non-inferiority margin | 1.2 mm (PPD reduction) |

## Results

| Outcome | Titanium Brush | Implantoplasty | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composite outcome 12 mo | 6/15 (40.0%) | 4/15 (26.7%) | 0.70 |
| Pocket closure (PPD ≤5 mm) 12 mo | 12/15 (80.0%) | 12/15 (80.0%) | 1.00 |
| PPD reduction, mean (mm) | −3.6 ± 1.5 | −3.3 ± 1.2 | 0.29 (non-inferior) |
| MBL change 12 mo (mm) | 0.0 ± 0.6 | +0.7 ± 1.2 | **0.03** |
| Implant loss | 0 | 2 | — |
| Decontamination time | 3 min 1 s | 5 min 27 s | **0.006** |
| Patient satisfaction 12 mo | 7.33 ± 3.27 | 7.85 ± 3.26 | 0.67 |

**Pocket closure failure predictors** (multivariable logistic regression):
- Higher baseline PPD: coeff −1.520, p=0.044
- Adjacent implant affected by peri-implantitis: coeff −13.805, p=0.033
