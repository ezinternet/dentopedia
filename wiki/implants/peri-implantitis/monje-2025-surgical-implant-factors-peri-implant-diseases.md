## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (34 studies; 21 surgical-factor studies with 2752 patients/7591 implants, 13 implant-factor studies with 1192 patients/4072 implants; searches through October 2023) qualitatively synthesizing surgical- and implant-related predictors of peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis for the AO/AAP 2024 Consensus Conference.

Inadequate 3D implant position (malposition) was the strongest, most consistent surgical-related risk factor for peri-implantitis (OR up to 48.2), while no implant macro-/micro-design or system showed consistent superiority; a short (<1.5mm) restorative-margin-to-crestal-bone distance emerged as a possible implant-related predisposing factor (OR=2.29).

High heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis (qualitative synthesis only); the clinical takeaway is that precise 3D implant positioning and tissue-level/transmucosal-abutment design matter more for peri-implantitis prevention than the specific implant brand/surface chosen.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(34편; 수술요인 21편[환자 2752명/임플란트 7591개], 임플란트요인 13편[환자 1192명/임플란트 4072개]; 2023년 10월까지 검색)로 임플란트주위점막염·임플란트주위염의 수술적·임플란트 관련 예측인자를 정성적으로 종합, AO/AAP 2024 합의회의 근거자료.

부적절한 3차원 임플란트 위치(오식립)가 임플란트주위염의 가장 강력하고 일관된 수술 관련 위험요인(OR 최대 48.2)이었던 반면, 임플란트 거대/미세디자인이나 시스템은 일관된 우위를 보이지 못했고, 보철변연-치조정골 거리<1.5mm가 임플란트 관련 소인 가능성(OR=2.29)으로 부상.

이질성이 커서 메타분석은 불가했으며(정성적 종합만), 임상적 시사점은 임플란트주위염 예방에는 특정 브랜드·표면보다 정밀한 3차원 식립 위치와 조직수준/경점막 지대주 디자인이 더 중요하다는 것.

## Summary

This AO/AAP-commissioned systematic review (companion background evidence for [[periodontics/wang-2025-ao-aap-consensus-peri-implant-diseases-conditions]]) asked two focused questions: (1) what surgical-related factors (implant 3D position, surgical technique, staging, grafting, insertion torque) affect onset/progression of peri-implant disease, and (2) what implant-related factors (macro-design, micro-design, system) do the same. Searching MEDLINE/PubMed and Cochrane Library through October 2023, screening 4296 records down to 34 included studies (21 surgical-factor, 13 implant-factor), the authors performed a narrative/qualitative synthesis only — heterogeneity in case definitions and exposures across studies precluded meta-analysis.

The headline finding: **implant malposition** in three dimensions (bucco-lingual, apico-coronal, mesio-distal relative to bone envelope and adjacent implants/teeth) is consistently and sometimes very strongly associated with peri-implantitis, but not with peri-implant mucositis. Effect sizes ranged widely (OR 2.6–48.2) but pointed the same direction across independent retrospective cohorts and case-control studies. Bone regeneration at implant placement carried a smaller incremental risk, particularly when the regeneration itself failed. For implant-related (design/system) factors, the review found no consistent evidence that any specific macro-design, micro-design, or implant system outperforms another — but flagged a short (<1.5mm) restorative-margin-to-crestal-bone distance as a plausible predisposing factor, favoring tissue-level implant designs or transmucosal abutments over deeply subcrestal bone-level restorations.

## Key Contributions

- Confirms **implant malposition** (3D position error relative to alveolar bone envelope and adjacent implants/teeth) as the single most robust surgical-related risk factor identified across the peri-implantitis literature, with the strongest individual estimate at OR=48.2 (Canullo).
- Quantifies **implant-to-implant proximity (<3mm)** as a distinct positional risk (OR=8.6–2.98 depending on study/level) — narrower interproximal space impedes self-performed oral hygiene and/or compromises blood supply.
- Quantifies **excessive apico-coronal (subcrestal) depth** as a risk factor: ≥6mm from adjacent CEJ carried OR=8.5 (Kumar); 1–3mm subcrestal position showed the highest mucositis rates in Obreja's cross-sectional cohort, amplified in periodontitis-history patients (OR=5.33).
- Identifies **short restorative-margin-to-crestal-bone distance (<1.5mm)** as an implant-related predisposing factor for peri-implantitis (OR=2.29), independently reinforced by the RM-AC threshold work in [[implants/peri-implantitis/basak-2024-restoration-margin-alveolar-bone-distance-implant]] (OR=3.42 for MBL at the same 1.5mm cut-point).
- Establishes that **implant macro-/micro-design/system choice** (bone-level vs tissue-level, rough vs hybrid vs laser-microtextured surfaces, brand) is NOT a reliably differentiating factor for peri-implant disease risk in the current comparative literature — contradicting any single-brand or single-surface marketing claim of superior peri-implantitis resistance.
- Notes surgical timing (immediate vs delayed placement) and simultaneous bone/soft-tissue grafting have inconsistent, context-dependent associations rather than uniform risk or protective effects.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA-adherent, PROSPERO CRD501977), PECOS framework, qualitative/narrative synthesis (no meta-analysis due to heterogeneity).
- **Search**: MEDLINE/PubMed + Cochrane Library through October 2023; no language/year restriction; supplemented by Grey Literature Database and citation-chasing.
- **Eligibility**: Human clinical studies (RCT, prospective/retrospective cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, case series) with ≥10 patients, ≥12-month follow-up post-final-prosthesis, using AAP/EFP 2017 World Workshop or a clear composite clinical+radiographic case definition for mucositis/peri-implantitis.
- **Screening**: 4296 records → 71 full-text assessed → 34 included (21 surgical-factor, 13 implant-factor). Two-reviewer full-text assessment with third-reviewer arbitration.
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB2 for RCTs (low/some concerns/high); NIH quality tool for cohort/case-series/cross-sectional (scored out of 14 or 10).

## Results

### Surgical-related factors → peri-implantitis (selected)

| Factor | Study (n patients/implants) | Effect size |
|---|---|---|
| Implant malposition | Canullo 2016 (63/232) | OR=48.2 (95% CI 11.4–204.1) |
| Implant malposition | Canullo 2017 (53/231) | 17.1% of all peri-implantitis attributable |
| Implant-to-implant distance <3mm | Schuldt-Filho 2014 (27/161) | OR=8.6 (implant-level OR=2.98, 95% CI 1.34–7.70) |
| Apico-coronal depth ≥6mm from CEJ | Kumar 2018 (86/222) | OR=8.5 (95% CI 6.7–32.6) |
| Subcrestal position + periodontitis history | Obreja 2021 (200/657) | OR=5.33 (95% CI 0.22–0.94) |
| Immediate vs delayed placement | Ortiz-Echeverri 2023 (155/279) | OR=2.61 (95% CI 1.4–5.1) |
| Deficient alveolar width | Ortiz-Echeverri 2023 | OR=2.9 (95% CI 1.8–4.6) |
| Failed bone regeneration | Canullo 2016 | OR=2.3 |
| Bone augmentation (standard implant into atrophic ridge) | Discussion (ref. 21) | OR=2.35 (95% CI 1.43–3.88) |
| Immediate placement (posterior mandible) | Jemt 2017 (1017/3082) | OR=1.88 |

**Not confirmed as risk factors**: simultaneous connective-tissue grafting was protective in one cross-sectional study (Obreja, lower BOP p=0.02 and PPD p=0.01) but only marginally beneficial for mucositis (not peri-implantitis) in another (Bienz); thick crestal soft tissue was found adverse rather than protective in periodontitis-history patients (Zhang, OR=2.5); implant proximity to natural teeth and subcrestal position were not confirmed in two lower-quality studies (Corbella, Kütan RCT — low risk of bias, no significant biological-complication difference).

### Implant-related factors → peri-implantitis (selected)

| Factor | Study | Effect size |
|---|---|---|
| Implant system (AstraTech / Nobel Biocare vs Straumann reference) | Derks 2016 (588/2277) | OR=3.55 / OR=3.7 vs OR=1 |
| Anodized/fluoride-treated surface vs SLA | Discussion (ref. 48) | OR=3.77 (95% CI 1.60–8.87) and OR=3.55 (95% CI 1.29–9.77) |
| Restorative margin <1.5mm from alveolar bone | Discussion (ref. 48) | OR=2.29 (95% CI 1.21–4.34) |
| Early bone loss ≥0.5mm in year 1 | Windael (10-year study, cited) | OR=5 for future peri-implantitis |
| Tissue-level vs bone-level implants | Marrone 2013 | OR=0.7, ~1/3 lower risk (not significant) |
| Laser-microtextured collar | Guarnieri 2018 vs Kadkhodazadeh 2022 | Conflicting: protective in one retrospective study, no benefit in another |

No implant macro-design, micro-design, or system demonstrated consistent superiority across comparative RCTs/cohorts (Gamper, Glibert, Karoussis, Kahramanoğlu, Parvini, Ravald, Renvert, Serrano) — the review's conclusion is that implant selection matters far less than 3D positioning for peri-implantitis prevention.
