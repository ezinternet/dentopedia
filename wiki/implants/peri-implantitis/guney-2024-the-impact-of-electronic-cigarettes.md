## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA/PICO, 2017–2023 search; 7 studies included from 49 records) assessing e-cigarette effects on peri-implant clinical (PI, PD, BOP), radiographic (bone loss), and immunologic (IL-1β) parameters.

Abstract reports associations with increased PD, bone loss, and IL-1β and decreased BOP in e-cigarette users; pooled effect-size details are not available from abstract-only ingest.

E-cigarettes may pose a peri-implant healing/treatment risk similar in concern to conventional smoking, but evidence is limited by the small number of included studies.

## 세줄요약

전자담배(e-cigarette, 전자담배/베이핑) 사용이 임플란트 주위 건강(peri-implant health)에 미치는 영향을 평가한 PRISMA 기반 체계적 고찰·메타분석(SR+MA, Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis)으로 7개 연구를 포함했다.

초록 기준 전자담배 사용은 탐침 깊이(PD, Probing Depth, 탐침 깊이), 골소실(bone loss), IL-1β(Interleukin-1 beta, 인터루킨-1베타) 증가 및 탐침 시 출혈(BOP, Bleeding on Probing, 탐침 시 출혈) 감소와 연관되었다.

기존 담배와 유사한 잠재적 위험인자로 볼 수 있으나, 포함 연구 수가 적어 정량적 효과크기와 이질성은 전문 확인이 필요하다.

## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis synthesized evidence on electronic cigarette use and peri-implant health across clinical, radiographic, and immunologic outcomes. Seven studies were included from searches across major databases (2017–2023). The abstract indicates adverse associations (higher PD, bone loss, IL-1β; lower BOP), suggesting e-cigarettes may impair peri-implant conditions, while acknowledging the need for better-matched clinical studies.

## Key Contributions

- Multi-domain outcome synthesis (clinical/radiographic/immunologic) for e-cigarette exposure around implants.
- PRISMA/PICO-based evidence selection focused on peri-implant parameters.
- Positions vaping as a potential implant-treatment risk factor alongside conventional smoking.

## Methodology

- Databases: MEDLINE, EMBASE, COCHRANE, SCOPUS (2017–2023).
- Design: PRISMA-guided SR+MA with PICO framework.
- Study selection: 49 records screened; 7 studies included and analyzed (MedCalc).
- Outcomes: PI, PD, BOP, radiographic bone loss, IL-1β.

## Results

- Increased: probing depth (PD), bone loss, IL-1β (reported).
- Decreased: bleeding on probing (BOP) (reported).
- Pooled quantitative estimates/heterogeneity: not available from abstract-only ingest.
