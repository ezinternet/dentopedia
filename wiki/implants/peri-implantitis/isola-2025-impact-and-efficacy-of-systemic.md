## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 18 studies (9 RCTs pooled) evaluating systemic antibiotics (SA) as adjuncts to non-surgical or surgical treatment of peri-implant mucositis (PM) and peri-implantitis (PI); SA provided no benefit for PM; for PI, significant reductions in bleeding on probing (BOP, p=0.007) and probing pocket depth (PPD, p=0.004) at 12 months were observed with adjunctive SA.

However, no significant improvement in marginal bone level (MBL) was found; only 2 studies reported significant microbiological effects (metronidazole reducing P. gingivalis and T. forsythia up to 12 months); evidence remains heterogeneous and inconclusive for long-term outcomes.

Adjunctive systemic antibiotics offer limited clinical benefits for PI — confined to soft tissue parameters with no bone-level effect — and are not supported for PM; further high-quality, long-term studies are needed.

## Summary

Isola et al. (2025) conducted a SR+MA of 18 studies (9 RCTs pooled) evaluating SA as adjuncts to non-surgical PM treatment or non-surgical/surgical PI treatment. Using the ID-COSM consensus outcome set, they assessed BOP, PPD, MBL, and microbiological outcomes at 12 months. SA provided no additional clinical benefit for PM (all outcomes p>0.05). For PI, pooled analysis showed significant soft-tissue improvements: BOP reduction (p=0.007) and PPD reduction (p=0.004) but NO significant MBL improvement (p>0.05). Microbiological benefits were confined to 2 studies showing metronidazole reduced P. gingivalis and T. forsythia up to 12 months. The authors conclude that SA adjunct offers only limited, soft-tissue-only benefits for PI and cannot be routinely recommended. Concerns about antimicrobial resistance and systemic side effects are highlighted as barriers to routine use.

## Key Contributions
- Differentiates SA effects in PM vs PI: no benefit for PM; limited soft-tissue benefit for PI only
- MBL: no significant SA benefit — critical finding for implant survival assessment
- Metronidazole specifically reduces P. gingivalis + T. forsythia (up to 12 months) — only 2 studies
- ID-COSM outcome set used for consistency — methodological strength
- Stewardship warning: SA resistance risks vs marginal soft-tissue benefit must be weighed

## Methodology
- PRISMA; PROSPERO CRD420251059056
- Included: RCTs and non-RCTs; SA as adjuncts to PM non-surgical or PI non-surgical/surgical treatment
- Risk of bias: RoB2 (RCTs), MINORS (non-RCTs)
- 18 qualitative studies; 9 RCTs in meta-analysis
- Outcomes: BOP, PPD, MBL; microbiological: P. gingivalis, T. forsythia, A. actinomycetemcomitans

## Glossary
- **PM (Peri-implant Mucositis)**: Inflammation limited to peri-implant soft tissues; reversible; no bone loss
- **PI (Peri-implantitis)**: Inflammation with progressive marginal bone loss around implant
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level)**: Radiographic measure of bone support; primary implant survival indicator
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing)**: Soft-tissue inflammation marker; improved with SA in PI
- **P. gingivalis / T. forsythia**: Key peri-implant pathogens targeted by metronidazole
