## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA, search to 2022-11-30; 7 prospective cohort studies, 702 patients, 1,959 implants) evaluating smoking and incidence of peri-implantitis.

Smokers had higher peri-implantitis risk at implant level (RR 2.04, 95% CI 1.46–1.85 as reported in abstract, p < .0001) and patient level (RR 2.79, 95% CI 1.42–5.50, p = .003) versus non-smokers.

Moderate-certainty (GRADE) evidence supports smoking as a risk factor for peri-implantitis, relevant for risk-based implant maintenance and smoking-cessation counselling.

## 세줄요약

전향적 코호트 7편(환자 702명, 임플란트 1,959개)을 대상으로 흡연과 임플란트 주위염(peri-implantitis, 임플란트 주위염) 발생 위험을 평가한 PRISMA 기반 체계적 고찰·메타분석(SR+MA, Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis)이다.

초록 보고 기준 흡연자는 비흡연자보다 임플란트 단위(RR, Relative Risk, 상대위험도 2.04, 95% CI 1.46–1.85, p < .0001)와 환자 단위(RR 2.79, 95% CI 1.42–5.50, p = .003)에서 임플란트 주위염 위험이 높았다.

GRADE(근거 확실성 평가) 중등도 근거로 흡연은 조정 가능한 위험인자이며, 임플란트 유지관리와 금연 상담에 반영할 수 있다.

## Summary

This PRISMA-guided systematic review and meta-analysis pooled prospective cohort studies to estimate the association between smoking and incident peri-implantitis. Based on 7 studies (702 patients, 1,959 implants), smokers showed significantly increased risk at both implant and patient levels, with the authors rating certainty as moderate using GRADE. The abstract reports a possible inconsistency in the implant-level confidence interval bounds, so precise interval values should be confirmed from the full text.

## Key Contributions

- Focused synthesis on peri-implantitis incidence (prospective cohorts) rather than mixed peri-implant outcomes.
- Dual-level risk estimates (implant-level and patient-level).
- Certainty-of-evidence framing via GRADE (moderate certainty).

## Methodology

- Search (to 2022-11-30): MEDLINE-PubMed, SCOPUS, EMBASE, ISI Web of Science.
- Inclusion: prospective cohort studies comparing peri-implantitis incidence in smokers vs non-smokers.
- Screening/extraction/risk of bias: performed independently by two authors.
- Meta-analysis: random-effects models.
- Evidence certainty: GRADE.

## Results

- Included studies: 7 (702 patients, 1,959 implants).
- Implant-level risk: RR 2.04 (95% CI 1.46–1.85 as reported), p < .0001.
- Patient-level risk: RR 2.79 (95% CI 1.42–5.50), p = .003.
- Interpretation: moderate certainty evidence for association between smoking and peri-implantitis.
