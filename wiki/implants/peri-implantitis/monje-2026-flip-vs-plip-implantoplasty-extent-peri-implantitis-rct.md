## Three-line Summary

Quasi-randomized trial (n=33 patients / 40 implants; Spain; 4-year enrollment) comparing full-length implantoplasty (FLIP: supracrestal + intrabony) vs partial-length implantoplasty (PLIP: supracrestal only) in combined peri-implantitis surgery with 1-year follow-up.

FLIP achieved superior mSBI reduction (p=0.003) and MBL gain (2.42 vs 1.62 mm; adjusted p=0.009) but significantly greater mucosal recession (1.84 vs 1.33 mm; p=0.006); disease resolution 90.4% (FLIP) vs 64.6% (PLIP) in unadjusted analysis (OR=11.1, p=0.04) — not significant after adjustment (OR=14, p=0.13).

Overall disease resolution 77.5%; smoking independently predicted failure (OR=0.01; p=0.013); quasi-randomization and author COI (royalties on burs used) limit confidence — treat as estimation data pending confirmatory trial.

## 세줄요약

준무작위대조시험 (Quasi-Randomized Controlled Trial) (n=33명/40개 임플란트; 스페인; 4년 모집): 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 병합 수술 시 전장 임플란토플라스티 (Full-Length Implantoplasty, FLIP) vs 부분 임플란토플라스티 (Partial-Length Implantoplasty, PLIP), 12개월 추적.

FLIP은 변형 열구 출혈 지수 (Modified Sulcular Bleeding Index, mSBI) 감소 (p=0.003) 및 치조골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 획득 (2.42 vs 1.62 mm; 보정 p=0.009) 우세; 단 치은퇴축 (Mucosal Recession, MR) 은 FLIP에서 유의하게 더 큼 (1.84 vs 1.33 mm; p=0.006); 질환 해소율 비보정 90.4% vs 64.6% (OR=11.1, p=0.04) — 보정 후 유의하지 않음 (OR=14, p=0.13).

전체 질환 해소율 77.5%; 흡연만이 독립 실패 인자 (OR=0.01; p=0.013); 준무작위 설계 + 주저자 이해충돌 (버 특허 + 골이식재 스폰서) — 확증 연구 전까지 추정 자료로 해석 요망.

## Summary

This quasi-randomized trial by Monje et al. addresses an intraoperative decision that clinicians face when managing combined peri-implantitis defects: should implantoplasty extend into the contained intrabony compartment (FLIP) or be restricted to the supracrestal area (PLIP)? Both groups received the same reconstructive approach (autogenous bone + xenograft + PRF) and the same chemical decontamination for the intrabony surface (NiTi brushes, H₂O₂, tetracycline). At 12 months, FLIP showed significantly better MBL gain and mSBI reduction, and a substantially higher unadjusted disease resolution rate (90.4% vs 64.6%), but this advantage lost significance after adjustment for confounders. The notable trade-off is mucosal recession: FLIP produced mean recession of 1.84 mm vs 1.33 mm for PLIP. The study is limited by quasi-randomization (record-number based), underpowering for its primary endpoint, and a primary author who owns royalties from the burs used and receives industry funding from the bone graft supplier — findings should be interpreted as estimation data for a future powered trial.

## Key Contributions

- First controlled comparison of implantoplasty depth in combined peri-implantitis surgery (FLIP vs PLIP)
- FLIP associated with better MBL gain (adjusted p=0.009) and mSBI improvement (p=0.003) — potential biological rationale: smoother intrabony surface resists biofilm recolonization
- Disease resolution rate trend strongly favors FLIP (90.4% vs 64.6%; OR=14 unadjusted, ~OR=14 adjusted) despite losing significance after adjustment — likely underpowered, not truly null
- FLIP trade-off: ~50% more mucosal recession than PLIP — relevant for esthetic zones
- Smoking independently predicts failure regardless of implantoplasty extent (OR=0.01)
- No implant fractures in study (all standard/wide-diameter; no Ti-6Al-4V; vanadium concern mitigated in modern implants)

## Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Quasi-randomized (last digit of patient record); 2-arm, single-center |
| Setting | Badajoz, Spain (Oct 2021 – Sep 2025) |
| n | 33 patients / 40 implants; FLIP 18/19; PLIP 15/21 |
| Inclusion | Combined defect (IB/IIIB/IIIC), intrabony ≥3 mm, implant ≥3.5 mm, KM ≥2 mm |
| FLIP | Implantoplasty through full implant length (supracrestal + intrabony compartment) |
| PLIP | Implantoplasty supracrestal only; intrabony: NiTi brushes + H₂O₂ + tetracycline |
| Reconstruction | Autogenous bone 1:1 InterOss xenograft + PRF ± collagen membrane |
| Antibiotics | Amoxicillin 750 mg ×2/day ×7 days + CHX 0.2% + chitosan gel ×3/day |
| Recall | 4-month structured peri-implant maintenance |
