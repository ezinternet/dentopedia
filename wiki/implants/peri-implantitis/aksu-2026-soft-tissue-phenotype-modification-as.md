## Three-line Summary

Quasi-randomized clinical trial (43 patients, 55 implants; peri-implant mucositis with keratinized tissue width < 2 mm) comparing free palatal graft (FPG, n = 22) versus no additional intervention (n = 21) one month after oral hygiene instructions and non-surgical treatment.

At 6 months FPG increased keratinized tissue width (MD = 2.36 mm) and tissue thickness (MD = 0.97 mm), and lowered bleeding on probing (BoP) extent (0.75 ± 1.07 vs 1.83 ± 1.20; MD = -1.06; 95% CI -1.67 to -0.44; p = 0.001) with higher treatment success (OR = 8.44); PPD and PISTD did not differ.

Mediation analysis suggested the BoP benefit was largely independent of improved plaque control; the quasi-randomized allocation and abstract-only reading limit certainty.

6개월 시점에 FPG군은 KTW(평균차 2.36 mm)와 조직 두께(0.97 mm)가 증가했고, 탐침출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BoP) 범위가 0.75 ± 1.07 대 1.83 ± 1.20(평균차 -1.06; 95% CI -1.67 ~ -0.44; p = 0.001)으로 낮았으며 치료 성공률도 높았다(OR = 8.44); 탐침깊이(PPD)와 임플란트주위 연조직 열개(Peri-implant Soft-tissue Dehiscence, PISTD)는 차이가 없었다.

## Summary

This quasi-randomized trial evaluated the added value of soft-tissue phenotype modification via free palatal graft (FPG) on top of non-surgical therapy for peri-implant mucositis. Forty-three patients (55 implants) with keratinized tissue width (KTW) < 2 mm were allocated one month after oral hygiene instructions and non-surgical treatment to FPG (n = 22) or no additional intervention (n = 21). At 6 months FPG improved KTW, tissue thickness, plaque extent, BoP extent and severity, and treatment success, without differences in PPD or PISTD. A mediation analysis suggested the effects on peri-implant health were largely independent of improved plaque control.

## Key Contributions

- Interventional evidence that adding FPG to non-surgical mucositis therapy in KTW < 2 mm sites lowers BoP extent at 6 months.
- Treatment success was higher with FPG (OR = 8.44; p = 0.001).
- Mediation analysis: the benefit was not attributed to improved plaque control.

## Methodology

Quasi-randomized, two-arm trial; allocation one month after oral hygiene instructions and non-surgical treatment. Primary outcome: BoP extent (bleeding sites per implant) at 6 months. Secondary: KTW, tissue thickness, vestibular depth, plaque extent, BoP severity, PPD, PISTD, treatment success. Regression analyses for intergroup comparison; mediation analysis for plaque control.
