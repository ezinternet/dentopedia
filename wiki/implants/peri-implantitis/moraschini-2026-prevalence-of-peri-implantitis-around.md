## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD420251273821; RCTs and cohort studies; searches to February 2026; RoB 2 / ROBINS-I) of 19 studies (687 patients, 995 implants: 891 zirconia implants, 104 titanium implants; mean follow-up about 49 months) estimating implant-level peri-implantitis prevalence around zirconia implants (ZIs) and comparing ZIs with titanium implants (TIs).

Pooled implant-level prevalence around ZIs was 2% (95% CI 0%-6%; I2 = 79.3%) at follow-up <=3 years, 2% (0%-8%; I2 = 38.9%) at >3 to 5 years, and 8% (0%-23%; I2 = 82.1%) at >5 years (study-level range 0%-33.3%); five RCTs (109 ZIs, 106 TIs) showed no significant ZI-vs-TI difference (RR 1.66; 95% CI 0.48-5.71; p = 0.42; I2 = 0%).

Low short- to mid-term prevalence with a rising trend after 5 years, but GRADE certainty is low to very low (heterogeneous peri-implantitis definitions, wide prediction intervals up to 0%-67% beyond 5 years, few RCT events), so the non-significant RCT result is not evidence of equivalence.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD420251273821; RCT·코호트; 2026년 2월까지 검색; RoB 2 / ROBINS-I) — 19편 (환자 687명, 임플란트 995개: 지르코니아 임플란트 891개, 티타늄 임플란트 104개; 평균 추적 약 49개월)으로 지르코니아 임플란트 (Zirconia Implant, ZI) 주위 임플란트 수준 임플란트 주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 유병률을 추정하고 티타늄 임플란트 (Titanium Implant, TI)와 비교.

ZI의 통합 임플란트 수준 유병률: 추적 3년 이하 2% (95% CI 0%-6%; I2 = 79.3%), 3-5년 2% (0%-8%; I2 = 38.9%), 5년 초과 8% (0%-23%; I2 = 82.1%) (개별 연구 범위 0%-33.3%); RCT 5편 (ZI 109개, TI 106개)에서 ZI와 TI 간 유의차 없음 (RR 1.66; 95% CI 0.48-5.71; p = 0.42; I2 = 0%).

단기~중기 유병률은 낮고 5년 이후 증가 경향이나, GRADE 근거 확실성은 낮음~매우 낮음 (진단 정의 이질성, 5년 초과 예측구간 0%-67%, RCT 사건 수 적음) — RCT의 비유의 결과를 동등성의 근거로 읽어서는 안 된다.

## Summary

This 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis (Moraschini et al., Clin Implant Dent Relat Res) is the first to take peri-implantitis prevalence as the primary outcome for zirconia implants. Nineteen RCTs and cohort studies (687 patients, 995 implants, mean follow-up about 49 months) gave pooled implant-level prevalence of 2% at <=3 years, 2% at >3 to 5 years, and 8% beyond 5 years, with large heterogeneity and prediction intervals as wide as 0%-67% after 5 years. In five RCTs (109 ZIs vs 106 TIs) the difference between materials was not significant (RR 1.66; 95% CI 0.48-5.71), but certainty was low to very low and the authors explicitly warn against reading this as equivalence. The pooled figures are lower than titanium-dominated overall estimates, but they come from short-follow-up, mostly one-piece, mostly single-crown cohorts with non-uniform case definitions, so a direct "zirconia is safer" comparison is not supported.

## Key Contributions

- First dedicated meta-analysis of implant-level peri-implantitis prevalence around zirconia implants, stratified by follow-up (<=3 y 2%, >3-5 y 2%, >5 y 8%).
- Pooled 5-RCT comparison with titanium: RR 1.66 (95% CI 0.48-5.71; p = 0.42; I2 = 0%).
- Shows prevalence is time-dependent and that implant material alone does not appear decisive, consistent with the multifactorial view of peri-implant disease.
- Honest GRADE appraisal: low to very low certainty; estimates presented as an evidence synthesis, not definitive epidemiology.

## Methodology

PRISMA / PROSPERO CRD420251273821 (registered before selection). Four databases plus gray literature to February 2026; RCTs, controlled trials, and cohorts with ZIs in function at least 12 months; no uniform PD/MBL cutoff imposed. Random-effects generic inverse variance model with cluster adjustment (ICC 0.07), prediction intervals, leave-one-out and critical-risk-of-bias exclusion sensitivity analyses, Egger test (intercept 2.49; p = 0.13), RoB 2 and ROBINS-I, GRADE. Included design mix: 5 RCTs, 12 prospective cohorts, 2 retrospective; 14 of 19 studies one-piece ZIs; mostly single crowns.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Studies / patients / implants | 19 / 687 / 995 (891 ZIs, 104 TIs) |
| Mean follow-up | about 49 months (12-144) |
| Implant-level prevalence, <=3 y (10 studies) | 2% (95% CI 0%-6%); PI 0%-22%; I2 = 79.3% |
| Implant-level prevalence, >3-5 y (5 studies) | 2% (95% CI 0%-8%); PI 0%-19%; I2 = 38.9% |
| Implant-level prevalence, >5 y (4 studies) | 8% (95% CI 0%-23%); PI 0%-67%; I2 = 82.1% |
| Study-level range | 0% to 33.3% |
| ZI vs TI (5 RCTs; 109 vs 106 implants) | RR 1.66 (95% CI 0.48-5.71); p = 0.42; I2 = 0% |
| Mean PD / BOP / MBL / PI around ZIs | 2.98 mm / 28.4% / 1.28 mm / 23.9% (descriptive only) |
| Certainty (GRADE) | low to very low |

Comparison with titanium (interpret cautiously): [[implants/peri-implantitis/diaz-2022-what-is-the-prevalence]] reports 12.53% implant-level (19.53% patient-level) for mostly titanium implants after at least 5 years of function, and [[implants/peri-implantitis/reis-2025-peri-implant-disease-prevalence-world-workshop-criteria]] reports 18.0% implant-level using 2017 World Workshop criteria. The ZI pooled value beyond 5 years (8%, 95% CI 0%-23%) overlaps with these figures; the lower short-term values reflect shorter exposure, small samples, and looser case definitions, not demonstrated material superiority. Only 104 titanium implants appear in this dataset, so the review cannot supply its own titanium prevalence.
