## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional analysis of 169 patients / 349 implants (retrospectively included; Seoul National University Dental Hospital) relating prosthetic features — emergence angle (EA), emergence profile (EP), crown/implant ratio (CIR), splinting — to peri-implantitis diagnosed from bone loss and probing depth on 1- and 5-year radiographs.

EA correlated significantly with marginal bone loss (MBL); greater peri-implantitis prevalence appeared with EA ≥30°, convex EP, and a middle implant splinted to both mesial and distal adjacent implants in bone-level implants, while tissue-level implants showed no such correlation and CIR had no significant effect.

Over-contoured prostheses and bilateral splinting are presented as modifiable local risk factors — but this abstract-only record reports no effect sizes or prevalence percentages, so magnitudes remain unverified.

## 세줄요약

후향적 포함·횡단면 분석(서울대학교치과병원, 환자 169명·임플란트 349개): 출현각(Emergence Angle, EA)·출현윤곽(Emergence Profile, EP)·보철물/임플란트 비율(Crown-to-Implant Ratio, CIR)·스플린트 위치를 1년·5년 방사선 사진의 골소실·탐침 깊이로 정의한 임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis)과 분석.

출현각은 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)과 유의한 상관을 보였고, 골수준(Bone-level) 임플란트에서 EA ≥30°·볼록 EP·양측 인접 임플란트와 스플린트된 중간 임플란트에서 유병률이 유의하게 높았으나 조직수준(Tissue-level) 임플란트에서는 유사 상관이 없었으며 CIR은 유의한 영향이 없었다.

과도하게 윤곽을 만든 보철물과 양측 스플린트가 수정 가능한 국소 위험인자로 제시되나, 초록 전용 기록이라 효과크기·유병률 수치가 없어 크기는 확인할 수 없다.

## Summary

This 2020 cross-sectional study from Seoul National University Dental Hospital enrolled 169 patients with 349 implants retrospectively and asked which prosthetic features influence peri-implantitis after accounting for other known risk factors. Peri-implantitis was defined by peri-implant bone loss plus probing depth, and radiographs taken 1 and 5 years after prosthesis insertion supplied marginal bone loss (MBL), emergence angle (EA), emergence profile (EP), and crown/implant ratio (CIR); splinted position was also recorded. In multivariable generalized estimating equation models, EA correlated significantly with MBL, and a statistically greater peri-implantitis prevalence was seen at EA ≥30°, with a convex EP, and when a middle bone-level implant was splinted to both mesial and distal neighbours. No analogous correlation appeared in tissue-level implants, and CIR had no significant effect. The authors conclude that an over-contoured prosthesis is a critical local confounder and that bilateral splinting raises risk — a modifiable-factor message whose magnitude cannot be checked from the available abstract.

## Key Contributions

- Multivariable analysis of EA, EP, CIR, and splinting against peri-implantitis defined by bone loss + probing depth (169 patients / 349 implants).
- Significant EA ↔ MBL correlation; higher prevalence at EA ≥30° in bone-level implants.
- Convex EP and middle-implant splinting to both adjacent implants identified as higher-risk configurations.
- Explicit bone-level vs tissue-level contrast — the EA/EP association was not seen in tissue-level implants.
- Null result for crown/implant ratio.

## Methodology

- Design: cross-sectional analysis of retrospectively included patients; n = 169 patients, 349 implants.
- Outcome: peri-implantitis by peri-implant bone loss and probing depth.
- Predictors: MBL, EA, EP, CIR from 1- and 5-year post-insertion radiographs; splinted position recorded.
- Statistics: multivariable generalized estimating equation; final prediction model by Cox proportional hazard regression.
- Available artifact: PubMed structured abstract only (`full_text: false`); no full-text body to audit thresholds or model covariates.
