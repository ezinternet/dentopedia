## Three-line Summary

RCT (n = 31 patients with peri-implantitis): surgical reconstruction with autogenous tuberosity bone graft and mechanical debridement, with (test) or without (control) adjunctive electrolytic cleaning (EC); 12-month follow-up.

Both groups improved significantly in PPD, GI and BOP with no between-group differences; radiographic mesial defect fill was greater with EC (4.6 +/- 1.10 mm vs 2.5 +/- 1.77 mm; p = 0.002), while intraoperative distal fill at 6 months did not differ (4.7 +/- 1.41 vs 3.8 +/- 1.72 mm; p = 0.069).

Autogenous reconstruction gave clinical and radiographic improvement in both arms and EC added no clinical benefit; no histology, so re-osseointegration is unconfirmed (abstract-level reading).

## Summary

Puisys et al. (Clin Implant Dent Relat Res 2026) report a randomized controlled trial of 31 patients with peri-implantitis treated by reconstructive surgery using autogenous tuberosity bone grafts. The control group received mechanical debridement and bone grafting; the test group additionally received electrolytic cleaning (EC). At 12 months both groups showed significant PPD reduction at all sites and improved GI and BOP, with no between-group differences. Radiographic mesial defect fill was greater in the EC group (4.6 vs 2.5 mm; p = 0.002), but intraoperative distal fill at 6 months was not significantly different (p = 0.069). The authors conclude that EC gave no significant additional clinical benefit; the absence of histology limits any claim of re-osseointegration. This summary is abstract-level only.

## Key Contributions

- Adds an autogenous (tuberosity) bone reconstruction RCT to a literature dominated by xenograft protocols.
- No clinical advantage of adjunctive EC at 12 months.
- Isolated radiographic mesial fill advantage with EC (p = 0.002) that was not mirrored at the distal site or by clinical parameters.

## Methodology

Randomized controlled clinical trial; 31 patients with peri-implantitis; control = mechanical debridement + bone grafting, test = additional EC. PPD at four sites, GI and BOP at baseline and 12 months; radiographic defect fill (mesial, distal) at baseline and 1 year; intraoperative bone fill at four sites on surgery day and 6 months. Further design details (randomization, blinding, power, defect morphology) are not available from the abstract.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| PPD, GI, BOP vs baseline (both groups) | Significant improvement |
| Between-group differences in PPD, GI, BOP | None significant |
| Radiographic mesial defect fill (test vs control) | 4.6 mm (+/- 1.10) vs 2.5 mm (+/- 1.77); p = 0.002 |
| Intraoperative distal fill at 6 months (test vs control) | 4.7 mm (+/- 1.41) vs 3.8 mm (+/- 1.72); p = 0.069 |
| Suppuration at 1 year | Resolved completely in both groups (p < 0.001); no group difference |
| Histology | Not available |
