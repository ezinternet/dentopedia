## Three-line Summary

Randomized controlled trial (56 randomized, 28 per arm; 48 analyzed: 23 OHI-only patients/66 implants vs 25 OHI + mechanical instrumentation patients/52 implants) at a university periodontology department in Spain, testing whether oral hygiene instructions (OHI) alone can resolve peri-implant mucositis (PM) over 3 months; the trial was not powered for superiority or non-inferiority.
Disease resolution (modified bleeding index, mBI, <= 0.16) at 3 months was 39.1% (OHI) vs 56% (OHI + MI) at the patient level and 36.4% vs 67.3% at the implant level; both arms reduced mBI significantly (p < 0.001) with no significant between-group difference in 3-month mBI (p = 0.408), and compliance was the strongest predictor of resolution (OR = 11.4, p = 0.004).
OHI alone resolves PM in a substantial minority, mainly in compliant patients, but adding professional mechanical instrumentation yields higher resolution; history of periodontitis, posterior implant position and poor accessibility worsen outcomes; limitations are the 3-month follow-up, self-reported compliance and a sample size based on a subjective 50% assumption.

## Summary

This randomized controlled trial (56 randomized, 48 analyzed, 118 implants) compared individualized oral hygiene instructions alone (OHI) with OHI plus professional mechanical instrumentation (OHI + MI) for peri-implant mucositis over 3 months. Resolution (mBI <= 0.16) was 39.1% vs 56% at the patient level and 36.4% vs 67.3% at the implant level, with significant mBI reductions in both arms (p < 0.001) and no significant between-group difference in 3-month mBI (p = 0.408). Patient compliance (OR = 11.4) was the dominant predictor of success; history of periodontitis and posterior implant location predicted worse outcomes. Clinically, hygiene instruction resolves mucositis in a considerable minority, but professional instrumentation adds resolution, and accessibility of the prosthesis for cleaning matters.

## Key Contributions

- Reports the absolute resolution proportion achievable with OHI alone (39.1% patient level; 36.4% implant level), a patient-centered figure.
- Quantifies the added resolution from professional mechanical instrumentation (implant level 67.3% vs 36.4%).
- Identifies compliance, history of periodontitis and posterior implant position as prognostic factors; all implants with prosthesis modification for hygiene access resolved.

## Methodology

Prospective parallel-group RCT, 3-month follow-up, permuted blocks of four with sealed opaque envelopes; blinded calibrated examiners (ICC 0.87-0.98); per-protocol analysis (patient level: Brunner-Langer models; implant level: GEE). Eligible: PM with bleeding on gentle probing (0.20 N) at > 1 site, no bone loss beyond remodelling, > 1 mm keratinized mucosa, non-smokers or light smokers. OHI: twice-daily cleaning plus interproximal brushes; MI arm added ultrasonic plastic-tip and plastic-curette debridement with rubber-cup polishing. Primary outcome: mBI <= 0.16 at 3 months. Sample size 28 per arm assuming 66.6% (OHI + MI) and >= 50% (OHI) resolution.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Analyzed | 48 patients (OHI 23, OHI + MI 25), 118 implants (OHI 66, OHI + MI 52); mean age 63.6 years; 93.8% with periodontitis history |
| Resolution, patient level | 39.1% (95% CI 19.7%-61.5%) OHI vs 56% (95% CI 34.9%-75.6%) OHI + MI |
| Resolution, implant level | 36.4% (95% CI 24.8%-48.0%) OHI vs 67.3% (95% CI 54.6%-80.1%) OHI + MI |
| 3-month median mBI | 0.40 (IQR 0.17-0.94) OHI vs 0.17 (IQR 0.17-0.29) OHI + MI, p = 0.408 |
| mBI reduction | Significant in both groups (p < 0.001); median -1.02 (OHI) vs -1.58 (OHI + MI) |
| Suppuration-free implants at 3 months | 95.4% OHI vs 98.1% OHI + MI |
| Compliance (patient level) | OR = 11.4 (95% CI 2.19-59.2, p = 0.004); success 63.6% compliant vs 13.3% non-compliant |
| OHI-only vs OHI + MI (implant level) | OR = 0.28 (95% CI 0.11-0.71, p = 0.007) |
| History of periodontitis | Higher final mBI (p = 0.010) |
| Posterior implant location | Trend toward lower resolution (p = 0.056) |
| Microbiology | P. gingivalis 21/25 to 12/25 (OHI + MI), 16/23 to 7/23 (OHI); no significant group differences |

Caveats: 3-month follow-up only; self-reported compliance; sample size based on a subjective 50% assumption; implants not randomized. The abstract's implant allocation (52 OHI / 66 OHI + MI) conflicts with the body text and rates (66 OHI / 52 OHI + MI); the body text is followed here.
