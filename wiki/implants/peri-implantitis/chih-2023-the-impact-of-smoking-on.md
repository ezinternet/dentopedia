## Three-line Summary

SR (search to 2023-01, 14 studies) comparing peri-implant microbiota in smokers vs nonsmokers.

Smokers showed higher anaerobic pathogen frequencies and lower microbiota diversity at healthy peri-implant sites, and during transition to disease a shift toward depleted commensals and enriched pathogenic species was reported.

Evidence suggests smoking may influence peri-implant microbiota composition, but authors note limitations and need for further studies.

## 세줄요약

흡연자와 비흡연자의 임플란트 주위 미생물총(peri-implant microbiota, 임플란트 주위 미생물총)을 비교한 연구 14편을 포함한 체계적 고찰(SR, Systematic Review)이다.

건강한 부위에서도 흡연자는 혐기성 병원균(anaerobic pathogen, 혐기성 병원균) 빈도가 높고 다양성이 낮았으며, 질환 이행 시 공생균(commensal, 공생균) 감소와 병원성 종 증가가 보고되었다.

흡연이 임플란트 주위염(peri-implantitis, 임플란트 주위염)으로의 전환에 관여하는 미생물학적 경로를 시사하나, 근거의 한계로 추가 연구가 필요하다.

## Summary

This systematic review synthesized 14 studies evaluating peri-implant microbiota in smokers and nonsmokers. Findings indicate dysbiotic shifts associated with smoking both in clinically healthy peri-implant sites (higher anaerobic pathogens, lower diversity) and during progression to disease (loss of commensals, enrichment of pathogens). Most included studies were rated medium-to-high methodological quality, yet the overall evidence base remains limited.

## Key Contributions

- Consolidates microbiologic evidence linking smoking to peri-implant dysbiosis.
- Distinguishes patterns in healthy sites versus healthy-to-disease transition.
- Provides mechanistic context for clinical associations between smoking and peri-implant disease.

## Methodology

- Databases: MEDLINE/PubMed, Embase, Scopus (up to January 2023), duplicate search, no language restriction.
- Inclusion: studies evaluating peri-implant microbiota of smokers and nonsmokers.
- Quality appraisal: adapted Newcastle-Ottawa scale.
- Synthesis: qualitative/systematic synthesis of 14 included studies.

## Results

- 14 studies included; 85.7% medium-to-high methodological quality (reported).
- Healthy sites in smokers: higher anaerobic pathogen frequencies, lower microbial diversity.
- Healthy-to-disease transition in smokers: depletion of commensals and enrichment of pathogenic species.
- Authors' conclusion: smoking may influence peri-implant microbiota composition; further research needed.
