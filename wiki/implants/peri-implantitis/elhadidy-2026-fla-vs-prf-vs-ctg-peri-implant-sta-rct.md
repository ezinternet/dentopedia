## Three-line Summary

3-arm RCT (BMC Oral Health 2026): 45 patients, thin-phenotype (≤1 mm) maxillary anterior implants; fascial lata allograft (FLA) vs platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) vs subepithelial connective tissue graft (SCTG) for peri-implant soft-tissue augmentation at 6 months.

CTG (SCTG) produced the greatest soft-tissue thickness (STT) gain — confirming the gold standard; FLA was closely comparable with no secondary surgical site; PRF offered modest augmentation suitable for minor enhancements or early healing support.

FLA is a highly promising minimally invasive alternative to CTG for thin-phenotype anterior implants — no donor-site morbidity, biologically integrating, volumetrically comparable to SCTG at 6 months.

## Summary

Elhadidy et al. (BMC Oral Health 2026) conducted a 3-arm parallel RCT at Kafrelsheikh University comparing three approaches for peri-implant soft-tissue augmentation (STA) in thin-biotype maxillary anterior implant sites: subepithelial connective tissue graft (SCTG), platelet-rich fibrin (PRF), and fascia lata allograft (FLA). At 6 months, CTG produced the greatest STT gain, confirming it as the gold standard for maximum thickness. FLA was closely comparable to CTG in STT gain with the advantage of no secondary surgical site — representing a highly promising, minimally invasive alternative. PRF provided modest volume improvement, appropriate for minor enhancements or as an early-healing adjunct. All three approaches were effective in augmenting STT around anterior implants with thin biotypes.

## Key Contributions

- First 3-arm RCT directly comparing FLA vs PRF vs CTG for peri-implant STA simultaneously
- CTG = maximum STT gain (gold standard confirmed); FLA nearly matches without donor site
- PRF: modest augmentation — suitable for minor cases or combination with other biologics
- FLA integrates biologically as acellular collagenous matrix; volumetrically stable at 6 months
- Establishes FLA as strongest minimally invasive CTG alternative in thin-phenotype anterior implants

## Methodology

3-arm parallel RCT; Kafrelsheikh University, Egypt; thin biotype (≤1 mm STT), maxillary anterior single implants, healthy periodontium; n=45 (15/group: SCTG, PRF, FLA); primary outcome STT by ultrasound measurement; 6-month follow-up.

## Results

| Group | STT Gain | Donor Site | Morbidity | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CTG (SCTG) | Greatest | Yes (palate) | Higher | Gold standard |
| FLA | Comparable to CTG | None | Lower | Minimally invasive |
| PRF | Modest | None (autologous) | Lowest | Early healing/minor augmentation |

All 3: effective for peri-implant STT augmentation in thin-phenotype maxillary anterior sites.
