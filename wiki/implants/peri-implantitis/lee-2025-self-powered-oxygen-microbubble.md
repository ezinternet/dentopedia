## Three-line Summary

In vitro and ex vivo study engineering self-powered microbubblers (SM) — cylindrical diatom particles doped with MnO2 nanosheets — to decontaminate anaerobic biofilm trapped within the fixture-abutment interface (FAI) microgap of dental implants.
SM activated by H2O2 generated O2 microbubbles via catalase-mimetic activity, penetrated P. gingivalis biofilm in gelatin microgap models and patient-derived peri-implantitis implants, outperforming 0.2% chlorhexidine and 3% H2O2 alone; abutment-fixture mechanical stability was restored and bacterial leakage reduced.
No in vivo or clinical data; H2O2 cytotoxicity at effective concentrations and industrial-scale synthesis are unresolved barriers to clinical translation.

## 세줄요약

MnO2 나노시트를 규조토(diatom)에 도핑하여 제작한 자가추진 마이크로버블러 (Self-powered Microbubbler, SM)로 임플란트 임시기둥-고정체 접합부 (Fixture-Abutment Interface, FAI) 마이크로갭 내 혐기성 바이오필름을 제거하는 in vitro/ex vivo 연구 (ACS Biomaterials, 2025).
SM + H2O2는 젤라틴 하이드로겔 마이크로갭 모델 및 환자 유래 치주염 임플란트 모두에서 0.2% 클로르헥시딘 (Chlorhexidine, CHX)·3% H2O2 단독 대비 우수한 바이오필름 제거 효과; 기계적 안정성 회복 및 세균 누출 감소 확인.
임상 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)·생체 내 안전성 데이터 전무; 유효 농도에서의 세포독성 및 대량 생산 가능성이 임상 적용의 주요 장벽.

## Summary

This 2025 study from Seoul National University and collaborating institutions presents a nanotechnology-based approach to decontaminating the fixture-abutment interface (FAI) microgap — an anaerobic niche in two-piece bone-level internal implants that harbors peri-implantitis-driving biofilm, yet is inaccessible to conventional antiseptics and mechanical debridement. The authors fabricated self-powered microbubblers (SM) by chemically doping cylindrical porous diatom silica shells with MnO2 nanosheets via APTES surface amine modification and KMnO4 reduction. When suspended in H2O2, the MnO2 nanozyme decomposes H2O2 into O2 via catalase-mimetic activity, generating microbubbles that propel the rigid diatom shells through biofilm — simultaneously disrupting the extracellular polymeric substance (EPS) matrix mechanically and oxygenating the anaerobic niche. In vitro validation against P. gingivalis biofilm in gelatin hydrogel microgap models demonstrated superior disruption versus free MnO2 and conventional antiseptics. Ex vivo testing on patient-derived peri-implantitis explants confirmed that SM + H2O2 outperformed 0.2% chlorhexidine and 3% H2O2 alone in multispecies biofilm decontamination, restored abutment-fixture torque (mechanical stability), and reduced bacterial leakage. Despite an elegant proof-of-concept, the study lacks in vivo safety data, clinical outcome trials, and optimization of the H2O2 delivery dose; cytotoxicity at bactericidal concentrations and industrial synthesis feasibility remain open questions.

## Key Contributions

- Novel self-powered microbubbler (SM) fabrication: MnO2-nanosheet-doped diatom particles (catalase-mimetic nanozyme) with dual biofilm-disruption mechanism — mechanical abrasion (bubble-driven diatom propulsion) + oxygenation of anaerobic niche; neither free MnO2 nor diatom alone achieves equivalent effect
- First ex vivo microgap decontamination study using patient-derived peri-implantitis implants: SM + H2O2 outperforms 0.2% CHX and 3% H2O2 on multispecies clinical biofilm (16S rRNA-confirmed)
- Restoration of abutment-fixture mechanical stability and reduction of bacterial leakage post-decontamination — linking microbiological efficacy to a clinically relevant structural endpoint
- Generalizable platform: SM technology applicable beyond dental implants to any biomedical device microgap (urinary catheters, orthopedic prostheses, cardiac leads)

## Methodology

In vitro and ex vivo experimental study. SM fabricated by APTES amine-functionalization of diatom particles → 50 mM KMnO4 reduction → MnO2 nanosheet deposition (characterized by SEM, TEM, XPS, BET). Biofilm disruption assessed in gelatin hydrogel microgap models (P. gingivalis) and patient-derived peri-implantitis implant fixtures (IRB136-10-19, Seoul National University Dental Hospital) explanted for >6 mm pocket depth, periapical radiolucency, or severe mobility. 16S rRNA V3-V4 amplicon sequencing (Illumina MiSeq 2×300 bp, DADA2/QIIME2/SILVA v3-v4) for inner/outer surface microbiome profiling. Comparators: 0.2% chlorhexidine gluconate, 3% H2O2, free MnO2.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Microgap biofilm disruption (gelatin model) | SM + H2O2 > free MnO2 + H2O2 > 3% H2O2 alone; SM achieves EPS matrix penetration |
| P. gingivalis elimination (in vitro) | SM + H2O2 superior to 0.2% CHX and 3% H2O2 |
| Multispecies biofilm (ex vivo patient implants) | SM + H2O2 outperforms CHX and H2O2 alone; 16S rRNA shift confirmed |
| Abutment-fixture mechanical stability | Restored after SM decontamination (torque measurement) |
| Bacterial leakage from microgap | Reduced post-SM treatment |
| Peri-implantitis prevalence context | 4.7–45% patient-level; 5.9–56% biofilm complication rate (dental implants highest among biomedical devices) |
