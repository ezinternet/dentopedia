## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (146 T2DM vs 60 controls, 6-month follow-up) documenting 43.2% peri-implantitis incidence in T2DM, with significantly higher GCF TNF-α (2.8×), IL-6 (3.1×), IL-1β (4.8×) and lower ISQ at 6 months; high HbA1c, smoking, and brushing frequency <1×/day were independent risk predictors by multivariate logistic regression.

## 한줄요약

## Summary
This single-center retrospective cohort study at Tianjin Fifth Central Hospital enrolled 146 T2DM patients who received dental implants (2021–2023) and 60 age/sex-matched normoglycemic controls. Patients were followed at 1, 3, and 6 months post-restoration with periodontal indices, gingival crevicular fluid (GCF) cytokines, and Osstell ISQ measurements. The T2DM group showed significantly higher probing depth, marginal bone loss, and inflammatory cytokine levels from 3–6 months post-restoration. Multivariate logistic regression identified four independent risk factors for peri-implantitis development. The 43.2% PI incidence rate in T2DM patients over just 6 months highlights the urgency of pre-implant glycemic optimization and intensive supportive therapy.

## Key Contributions
- Quantifies PI incidence in T2DM over 6-month follow-up: 43.2% (63/146)
- Longitudinal GCF cytokine trajectories: T2DM shows rising TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6 vs stabilizing controls
- ISQ comparison: T2DM 65.88 vs control 70.88 at 6 months — clinically relevant 5-point gap
- Identifies 4 independent PI risk predictors and 1 protective factor via multivariate analysis
- Establishes brushing duration ≥3 min as an independent protective factor — actionable hygiene instruction target

## Methodology
- Retrospective cohort; Tianjin Fifth Central Hospital, 2021–2023
- 146 T2DM patients (observation) + 60 normoglycemic controls; age ≥40; single implant per patient
- Exclusion: recent periodontal surgery, antibiotic/immunosuppressant use, <4-month healing
- Follow-up: 1, 3, 6 months post-restoration
- Outcomes: PD, mSBI, mPLI, MBL (radiographic); GCF cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6); serum leukocytes; ISQ
- Statistics: t-test/χ²; multivariate logistic regression for PI risk factors

## Results
**Periodontal indices at 6 months:**
| Index | T2DM (n=146) | Control (n=60) |
|---|---|---|
| Probing depth (mm) | 2.33 ± 0.27 | 1.17 ± 0.11 |
| MBL (mm) | 1.04 ± 0.17 | 0.72 ± 0.09 |
| mSBI | 1.09 ± 0.72 | 0.17 ± 0.38 |

**GCF cytokines at 6 months:**
| Cytokine | T2DM | Control | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| TNF-α (ng/mL) | 1.98 ± 0.18 | 0.70 ± 0.16 | 2.8× |
| IL-6 (ng/mL) | 4.88 ± 0.47 | 1.59 ± 0.08 | 3.1× |
| IL-1β (ng/mL) | 2.08 ± 0.28 | 0.43 ± 0.08 | 4.8× |

**ISQ trajectory:**
| Time | T2DM | Control |
|---|---|---|
| 1 month | 55.49 | 55.48 |
| 3 months | 61.07 | 65.62 |
| 6 months | 65.88 | 70.88 |

**PI incidence**: 63/146 (43.2%) in T2DM over 6 months post-restoration.

**Independent PI risk factors (multivariate logistic regression):**
- High HbA1c level
- Smoking history
- Brushing frequency <1×/day
- Anterior tooth implant site
- *Protective*: Brushing duration ≥3 minutes
