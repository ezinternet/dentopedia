## Three-line Summary
Single-masked, three-arm, 6-month pilot RCT (47 patients, 84 implants) of non-surgical submarginal instrumentation with titanium curettes alone versus plus erythritol air-polishing or plus Er:YAG laser in peri-implantitis.
PPD fell by a mean of 1-1.4 mm at 1 month in all groups (stable at 3 months, slight relapse at 6 months); BoP fell 24%-36% at 1 month with no intergroup differences; therapeutic endpoint rates were only 12%-18%.
Neither adjunct clearly improved outcomes and endpoint achievement was limited; >= 2 mm vestibular keratinized mucosa and Er:YAG use were associated with higher odds of success, pending confirmation in larger studies.

## 세줄요약
단일맹검 3군 6개월 파일럿 RCT(환자 47명, 임플란트 84개): 임플란트주위염에서 티타늄 큐렛 비외과적 처치 단독 대비 에리스리톨 에어폴리싱 또는 Er:YAG 레이저 추가를 비교했다.
탐침 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD)는 모든 군에서 1개월에 평균 1-1.4 mm 감소(3개월 유지, 6개월 약간 재발), 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BoP)은 1개월에 24%-36% 감소했고 군간 차이는 없었으며 치료 종결점 도달률은 12%-18%였다.
어느 보조요법도 뚜렷한 이득이 없고 종결점 도달이 제한적이었으며, 전정측 각화점막 2 mm 이상 및 Er:YAG 사용이 성공 확률과 연관되는 것으로 시사됐으나 더 큰 연구의 확인이 필요하다.

## Summary
This is a pilot randomized clinical trial (abstract-only; full text not retrieved) that evaluated adjunctive mechanical/physical decontamination added to non-surgical submarginal instrumentation in patients with peri-implantitis. Forty-seven patients (84 implants) were randomized to titanium-curette instrumentation alone (control), control plus erythritol air-polishing (test 1), or control plus Er:YAG laser (test 2) and followed for 6 months. PPD decreased in all groups, BoP decreased significantly at 1 month with no intergroup differences, and only 12%-18% of implants reached the therapeutic endpoint. Multilevel analysis suggested associations between >= 2 mm vestibular keratinized mucosa, Er:YAG use, and higher odds of treatment success, but the authors state these require confirmation in larger studies.

## Key Contributions
- Direct comparison of erythritol air-polishing and Er:YAG laser as adjuncts to titanium-curette instrumentation.
- Low therapeutic endpoint rates (12%-18%) irrespective of adjunct.
- Exploratory predictors: >= 2 mm vestibular keratinized mucosa and Er:YAG use.

## Methodology
6-month single-masked RCT, three parallel groups, assessments at baseline, 1, 3 and 6 months. Primary outcome PPD reduction; secondary outcomes BoP reduction and endpoint of therapy (PPD <= 5 mm, BoP <= 1 site, no suppuration). Multilevel logistic model of implant- and patient-level variables. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03451981.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| PPD reduction | Mean 1-1.4 mm at 1 month in all groups; stable at 3 months; slight relapse at 6 months |
| BoP reduction | 24%-36% at 1 month; no further significant change later; no intergroup differences |
| Therapeutic endpoint | 12%-18% of implants |
| Multilevel model | >= 2 mm vestibular keratinized mucosa and Er:YAG use associated with higher odds of success |
| Safety | Uneventful healing except one case of surgical emphysema with erythritol air-polishing |
