## Three-line Summary

Versah manufacturer clinical protocol (2-page, narrative-review) for upper molar immediate implant placement with simultaneous crestal sinus lift in sockets with an intact interradicular septum ≥4 mm.

Six-step workflow: CBCT planning → flapless septum-preserving extraction → Densah OD burs (CCW, 800–1500 rpm, irrigation) for combined septum expansion and Schneiderian membrane elevation → implant placement → graft fill → wide healing abutment for socket sealing.

Eliminates staged sinus augmentation for qualifying molar sites (septum ≥4 mm, adequate residual bone height); not peer-reviewed — clinical outcome data come from the Bleyan 2021 case series.

## 세줄요약

Versah 상악 구치부 즉시식립 + 동시 경치조골 상악동 거상(Crestal Sinus Lift) 프로토콜 (2페이지 제조사 문서): 적응 조건은 상악 구치 치간 격벽(interradicular septum) 폭 ≥4 mm.

6단계 워크플로: CBCT 계획 → flapless 무외상 발치(격벽 보존) → Densah OD 버(CCW, 800–1500 rpm, 관주)로 격벽 확장 + 슈나이더막 동시 거상 → 임플란트 식립 → 골이식 충전 → 광폭 힐링 어버트먼트로 소켓 밀봉.

격벽 ≥4 mm·충분한 잔존 골높이(Residual Bone Height, RBH)를 갖춘 상악 구치부에서 단계적 상악동 증대를 단일 술식으로 대체; 동료심사 미완료 — 임상 결과 데이터는 Bleyan 2021 증례 시리즈 참조.

## Summary
Versah manufacturer protocol (2-page) for the combined molar septum expansion + crestal sinus lift workflow in upper molar sites with intact ≥4 mm interradicular septum.

## Key Contributions
Vendor reference for the combined-indication scenario where molar extraction socket with thick septum allows simultaneous bone expansion and sinus lift — eliminating staged sinus augmentation. Backed by the Bleyan 2021 septum-OD case series.

## Methodology
Six-step clinical protocol: CBCT diagnosis → flapless septum-preserving extraction → Densah CCW expansion + simultaneous sinus lift → implant placement → graft fill → wide healing abutment.

## Results
Protocol outcome: single-procedure rehabilitation of upper molar with simultaneous septum expansion (lateral) and sinus floor elevation (apical); requires ≥4 mm septum width.

## Limitations
Manufacturer marketing document — not peer-reviewed. Requires precise CBCT planning and septum integrity. See [[bleyan-2021-molar-septum-expansion-osseodensification-immediate-implant]] for clinical outcome data including the molar socket classification this protocol implicitly uses.
