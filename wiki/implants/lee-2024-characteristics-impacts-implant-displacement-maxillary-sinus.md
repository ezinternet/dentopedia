## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (J Dent Sci 2024, Seoul National University), 13 patients / 14 implant displacements into the maxillary sinus retrieved by endoscopic-assisted bony-window surgery.

Displacement AFTER functional loading caused significantly worse sinus opacification (3.6 vs 1.7, p=0.019) and osteitis (wall ≥3 mm in 2/5) than perioperative displacement; timing was the only variable independently predicting both opacification and wall thickness (p=0.013), and all 14 cases had inadequate site preparation despite residual bone <5 mm.

Clinical implication: prevent displacement with site-specific bone grafting when residual bone is thin, and remove displaced implants promptly — especially post-loading ones — before osteitis sets in; opacification resolved (0.4) by 6 months after removal.

## Summary

A Seoul National University retrospective cohort that shifts the lens on implants displaced into the maxillary sinus from *how to remove them* to *what damage they do*. Across 13 patients (14 displacements), it grades sinus opacification and wall thickness and asks which patient/timing factors drive sinus pathology. The headline: **displacement after functional loading is markedly worse than displacement during surgery** — higher opacification and true osteitis — and **timing** is the only independent predictor of both. It also exposes a preventable pattern: every case had residual bone under 5 mm yet none had received site preparation (grafting or short implants) before the original placement. Removal reversed the opacification (mean 0.4 at 6 months). It is the graded-evidence companion to the technique-focused [[implants/an-2017-treatment-dental-implant-displacement-maxillary-sinus]].

## Key Contributions

1. Quantifies the **impact** of a displaced implant (opacification 0–4, wall thickness/osteitis) rather than only the retrieval technique.
2. Identifies **timing of displacement** (post-loading ≫ perioperative) as the dominant independent predictor of sinus pathology.
3. Flags a modifiable **site-preparation gap** — no grafting/short implants in any case despite residual bone <5 mm.

## Methodology

Retrospective cohort (IRB ERI24010; Oct 2020–Jan 2024) of patients whose displaced implant was retrieved, with pre/postoperative panoramic, Waters, and CT imaging. Retrieval by modified **endoscopic-assisted sinus surgery**: anterolateral bony window sized for a four-hole microplate, separated and stored in warm saline, implant located under endoscope and removed with suction/forceps/curettes, then the microplate-attached window repositioned and fixed with microscrews. Radiographic metrics: sinus opacification score (0 none → 4 total), sinus wall thickness measured 8 mm above the floor (≥3 mm = osteitis). Statistics: Mann–Whitney U, Fisher exact, Wilcoxon signed-rank, univariate linear regression (p<0.05). n = 13 patients / 14 displacements (one patient twice); 2 excluded for missing CT.

## Results

**Cohort:** 8M/5F, mean age 64.6 ± 9.9 (44–78); 10 smokers; 4 hypertension, 3 diabetes. Original sites: maxillary 2nd molar 9 (64.3%), 1st molar 5 (35.7%). Timing: 9 perioperative, 5 post-loading. Retention: 7 <3 months, 7 >3 months. 12 implants within the sinus, 2 across the floor.

**Residual bone height:** 4.7 ± 1.9 mm overall (perioperative 3.9 ± 1.6 vs post-loading 5.9 ± 1.8, p=0.067).

**Sinus opacification:** preop mean 2.4 ± 1.5 (complete 35.7%, subtotal 14.3%, partial/none 50%). Post-loading **3.6 ± 0.9** vs surgery **1.7 ± 1.3** (**p=0.019**). After removal, improved in all cases; 11 with postop CT fell to **0.4 ± 0.8 at 6 months** (**p=0.002**).

**Sinus wall thickness / osteitis:** 1.6 ± 0.8 mm (displaced) vs 1.1 ± 0.2 mm (normal side, p=0.002). By timing: post-loading 2.3 ± 1.1 vs perioperative 1.2 ± 0.2 (p=0.198, ns). **Osteitis (≥3 mm) in 2/5 (40%) post-loading cases, 0% perioperative.** Longer retention (>3 mo) trended toward worse opacification (3.0 vs 1.7) and wall thickness (2.0 vs 1.2) but not significantly.

**Predictors (univariate regression):** opacification ← age (p=0.034), diabetes (p=0.025), **timing** (p=0.013); wall thickness ← **timing only** (p=0.013). Sex, other systemic disease, retention duration, residual bone, and smoking were not significant.

**Prevention signal:** mean residual bone <5 mm yet **no site preparation or short-implant use in any of 14 cases** — consistent with Galindo-Moreno (~73% no grafting) and a systematic review (>half of 182 patients no pre-implant surgery).
