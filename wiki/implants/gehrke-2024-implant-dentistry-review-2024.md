## Three-line Summary
Narrative review of contemporary implant dentistry covering surface modifications (SLA, SLActive, UV photofunctionalization), zirconia implants, and digital guided surgery over the 2015–2024 decade.

Zirconia implants achieved 95–97% 3-year survival with esthetic advantage in thin biotypes; digital guided surgery (3D-printed templates) reached <1 mm positioning accuracy; titanium remains the gold standard.

Clinically, surface choice and digital planning are complementary tools — neither alone determines outcome, and long-term zirconia data beyond 3 years remain limited.

## Summary
Narrative review of contemporary implant dentistry covering surface modifications, zirconia implants vs titanium, and digital guided surgery accuracy.

## Key Contributions
- Surface modifications: SLA, SLActive, UV photofunctionalization — clinical outcomes compared
- Zirconia implants: growing evidence; 3-year survival 95–97%; esthetic advantage in thin biotype
- Digital guided surgery: 3D-printed guides achieve <1mm accuracy in positioning

## Methodology
Narrative review.

## Results
Titanium remains gold standard. Zirconia implants gaining evidence, especially for esthetics. Digital guided surgery improves predictability for complex implant positioning.
