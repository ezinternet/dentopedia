## One-line Summary
RCT (n=28, CBCT): submerged vs transmucosal healing after simultaneous GBR with implant placement showed no significant difference in vertical bone gain, defect resolution, or buccal bone thickness at 4 months.

## Summary
This randomized controlled trial enrolled 28 patients receiving a single implant with simultaneous GBR due to ≥2 mm coronal bony exposure. Patients were allocated to either a submerged healing approach (SBM: cover screw + flap closure) or a transmucosal healing approach (TSM: healing abutment exposed through mucosa). CBCT measurements were obtained immediately post-surgery (T0) and at 4 months (T1). Primary outcome (vertical bone gain, VGAIN) and all secondary outcomes (defect resolution, radiographic vertical defect height, radiographic bone thickness) showed no statistically significant differences between groups. Within each group, buccal bone thickness significantly decreased from T0 to T1. The study supports clinical equivalence of the two healing approaches when GBR is performed simultaneously with implant placement.

## Key Contributions
- First CBCT RCT to directly compare submerged vs transmucosal healing after simultaneous GBR at implant placement
- No significant difference in vertical bone gain (VGAIN) or defect resolution (p>0.05)
- Buccal bone thickness (RBT) decreased significantly within both groups regardless of healing modality (p<0.05)
- Eliminates the requirement for submerged healing after simultaneous GBR — clinician can choose healing modality without compromising bone outcomes
- Adds Level 1 evidence to the submerged/transmucosal healing protocol decision

## Methodology
Randomized controlled clinical trial. n=28 patients; single implant placed at a restorative-driven position with ≥2 mm coronal exposure requiring simultaneous GBR. Randomized to SBM (submerged healing) or TSM (transmucosal healing). CBCT at T0 (immediate) and T1 (4 months). Primary outcome: VGAIN. Secondary: defect resolution, RVDH, RBT. Statistics: intergroup comparisons for all outcomes; within-group T0→T1 RBT analysis.

## Results
- VGAIN: no significant difference SBM vs TSM (p>0.05)
- Defect resolution: no significant difference (p>0.05)
- RVDH at T1: no significant difference (p>0.05)
- RBT: no significant intergroup difference; significant T0→T1 reduction within both groups (p<0.05)
