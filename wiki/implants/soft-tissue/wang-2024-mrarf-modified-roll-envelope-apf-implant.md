## Three-line Summary

MRARF combines a modified roll envelope flap with an apically repositioned flap at second-stage implant surgery to simultaneously correct buccal contour concavity, insufficient keratinized tissue width, and soft tissue thinness.
Four consecutive cases (July 2021–February 2022) all healed uneventfully, achieving harmonious color, texture, and mucogingival junction position matching adjacent teeth.
The technique is graft-free, minimally invasive, and provides satisfactory vertical and horizontal esthetic gingival contours with adequate keratinized mucosa width and thickness.

## 세줄요약

MRARF는 변형 롤 봉투 판막 (Modified Roll Envelope Flap)과 치조정접근 판막 (Apically Repositioned Flap, ARF)을 결합하여 임플란트 2차 수술 시 협측 함몰·각화점막 폭 (Keratinized Mucosa Width, KMW)·연조직 두께 (Soft Tissue Thickness, STT)를 동시에 교정하는 무이식 (graft-free) 술식이다.
2021–2022년 4증례 모두 합병증 없이 치유되었으며, 인접 치아와 조화로운 색조·질감·치은순행연접부 (Mucogingival Junction, MGJ) 위치를 달성하였다.
최소 침습으로 수직·수평 심미 치은 윤곽 및 충분한 각화점막 폭·두께를 얻을 수 있어 이식재 없는 임플란트 연조직 표현형 교정의 대안으로 제시된다.

## Summary

Wang et al. (2024) present a case series of four patients who underwent implant second-stage surgery using the MRARF technique — a combination of the modified roll envelope flap and an apically repositioned flap. All patients had received dental implants plus guided bone regeneration (GBR) six months prior, and presented at second-stage surgery with labial/buccal soft tissue deficiency and reduced keratinized mucosa width relative to adjacent teeth.

The MRARF design addresses three simultaneous soft-tissue problems: (1) buccal contour concavity, (2) inadequate keratinized tissue width, and (3) insufficient soft tissue thickness — without requiring a connective tissue graft (CTG) or free gingival graft (FGG). Sutures were removed at two weeks, provisional restoration delivered at the same appointment, and a final impression taken at six weeks.

All four sites healed uneventfully with no reported postoperative pain or excessive swelling. A harmonious mucogingival esthetic outcome — color, texture, and MGJ position matching surrounding tissues and adjacent teeth — was achieved in every case. Patient satisfaction was high.

## Key Contributions

1. **Simultaneous triple correction**: MRARF addresses buccal concavity, KM width deficit, and KM thickness deficit in a single surgical intervention at second-stage surgery.
2. **Graft-free design**: Augmentation achieved without CTG or FGG, reducing donor site morbidity and surgical complexity.
3. **Minimally invasive at second-stage**: The technique leverages the existing peri-implant tissue architecture at uncovering, avoiding a separate augmentation surgery.
4. **Esthetic integration**: Harmonious MGJ position, color, and texture match with adjacent natural teeth reported in all cases.

## Methodology

**Patient eligibility criteria**:
- Dental implants + GBR completed 6 months prior
- Presenting for second-stage uncovering
- Each implant site: labial/buccal soft tissue deficiency AND keratinized mucosa width reduced vs. adjacent teeth

**Surgical sequence**:
1. Modified roll envelope flap design incorporating peri-implant tissue
2. Buccal/labial rolling and repositioning to augment volume and keratinized band
3. Apical flap repositioning to widen the keratinized mucosa zone
4. Suture removal + provisional restoration at 2 weeks post-op
5. Final impression at 6 weeks for definitive implant-supported restoration

**Sample**: n = 4 patients, treated July 2021 – February 2022
**Setting**: Department of Periodontology, Second Affiliated Hospital, Zhejiang University, China
**Evidence level**: Case series (no control group, no randomization)
