## Three-line Summary

RCT-only SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD420251034218; 10 RCTs 2011–2024, 3–60 months) recategorizing abutment profiles as narrowed (concave/curved), straight, or widened; only narrowed vs straight was pooled.

Full text: MBL 5 RCTs/282 implants SMD −0.25 (95% CI −0.55 to 0.06; p=0.11; I²=38%) — the abstract instead reports SMD 0.08, p=0.66; PD 2 RCTs SMD −0.05 (p=0.80); PES 2 RCTs SMD −0.04 (p=0.80; abstract −0.12, p=0.51); zenith (MMZ), KMW and thickness narrative only; concave arms held the few single-event failures/fractures.

"No difference" here means imprecise, small, internally inconsistent evidence — not demonstrated equivalence — and there is no pooled convex-vs-concave comparison.

## Summary

**What it did.** Ten RCTs comparing transmucosal abutment shapes were found; the authors recategorized them into narrowed, straight, and widened, and pooled narrowed vs straight for MBL (5 trials), PD (2) and PES (2). Everything else — wide vs cylindrical, convex vs concave, histology, zenith, keratinized mucosa — is reported narratively.

**What the full text changes versus the abstract-based reading (2026-11-21 first ingest).**

1. *The headline MBL number in the abstract is not the one in the Results.* The Results text gives SMD −0.25 (−0.55 to 0.06), p=0.11 with I² 38%, described as "a slight tendency favoring straight abutments"; the abstract gives SMD 0.08, p=0.66. PES also differs (−0.04/p=0.80 vs −0.12/p=0.51).
2. *Convex was not actually pooled against concave.* The abstract lists convex under "straight", but the methods text defines straight as linear and cylindrical, and no forest plot is labeled convex vs concave. The two convex-vs-concave trials (Koutouzis 2019, 2023; small, posterior maxilla) are narrative: no PI/BoP/PD/MBL difference, a soft-tissue-profile difference at several time points (p≤0.02), and one concave implant failure each. The earlier wiki critique — "convex lumped into straight dilutes a convex effect" — overstated what the pooled analyses contain; the correct statement is that **this review gives no pooled evidence on convex vs concave at all**.
3. *The pooled MBL set is partly unidentifiable and possibly overlapping.* The MBL citation includes a non-trial reference (ref 5); the fifth trial is probably Barwacz 2025 (inference from the GRADE text). Two of the three Patil reports — which look like one research program with very similar samples — appear together despite the authors' one-report-per-population rule, and Patil 2011 reported recession, not MBL.
4. *Table and GRADE errors.* The Pérez-Sayans 2022 row in Table 4 duplicates the Patil 2014 text, while the Discussion says Pérez-Sayans found significantly better bone preservation with narrow abutments. PES certainty is labeled moderate after a "two-level downgrade". PD/PES study citations differ between Results (refs 2,3) and Discussion (refs 6,35).
5. *Narrative signals point both ways.* Wide abutments lost more bone than cylindrical (Bernabeu-Mira 2023, p<0.001); concave abutments showed better soft-tissue histology than cylindrical (Camacho-Alonso 2025, p<0.001); concave arms carried isolated failures and fractures.

**How to use it.** In this wiki's judgment the review supports only a narrow claim: across small RCTs, curved/concave vs linear-divergent/cylindrical abutments have not shown a detectable difference in MBL, PD or PES over 3–60 months, with imprecise estimates. It does not show equivalence, does not test convex vs concave, and does not address crown emergence angle. Its weight as counter-evidence to the shape thesis is lower than the abstract implied. Two SR+MAs we hold remain the reference for angle/shape on bone: Liu 2025 (EA >30° subgroup MBL +0.19 mm; convex/wide → more crestal bone remodeling) and Lin 2025 (AO/AAP), which this paper cites with the related letters debate.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT-only pooling of abutment profile on MBL, PD, PES (narrowed vs straight).
- Tabulated narrative outcomes for 10 RCTs, including the only included histology RCT (Camacho-Alonso 2025).
- Flags concave-abutment technical events (fracture, early failure) as a design/material caution.

## Methodology

- PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420251034218; 8 databases + grey literature to 29 Mar 2026.
- Inclusion: RCTs, adults, single implants, ≥1 of MBL/PD/PES.
- κ 0.83 (title/abstract), 0.88 (full text); RoB 2; GRADEpro; RevMan 5.3 random-effects SMD.
- 10,556 records → 5,270 → 18 full texts → 10 RCTs (4 excluded for unavailable full text, 4 for comparing connections).
