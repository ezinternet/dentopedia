## Three-line Summary

Review (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2004) of periodontal plastic-surgical procedures used to create and maintain peri-implant soft-tissue height for esthetics.

The procedures and their clinical applications are reviewed, with emphasis on case selection and treatment planning.

No success rates or outcome comparisons appear in the abstract; check the full text before clinical use.

## Summary

Kazor et al. list the plastic-surgery options available to build and keep peri-implant soft-tissue height, and frame the choice as a case-selection problem. Outcome comparison is outside this abstract.

## Key Contributions

- Case-selection emphasis.
