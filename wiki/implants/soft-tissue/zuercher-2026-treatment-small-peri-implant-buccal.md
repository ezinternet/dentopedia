## Three-line Summary

Pilot RCT (n=16 at 3 years, SCTG=5, GBR=6, control=5) comparing SCTG vs GBR for small buccal bone dehiscences (≤3 mm) at posterior single-implant placement, with profilometric, clinical, and PROM outcomes.

Both SCTG and GBR maintained stable buccal soft-tissue contours from year 1 to year 3 (contour change at 1 mm: median +0.19 mm vs +0.11 mm; at 3 mm: +0.05 mm vs −0.05 mm); peri-implant health, MBL, and OHIP-14 were favorable and comparable between groups.

Small sample limits inferential conclusions; EFP S3 guideline notes no current evidence that soft tissue augmentation alone reduces peri-implant disease in thin-tissue sites; adequately powered RCTs needed.

## 세줄요약

파일럿 RCT(3년 추적, n=16): 단일 임플란트 식립 시 발견된 협측 골결손부(≤3 mm)에 연결조직이식(SCTG)과 유도골재생(GBR)을 무작위 배정, 연조직 윤곽·임플란트 주위 지표·PROMs 비교.

양군 모두 1→3년 buccal contour 변화 미미(1 mm 하방 중앙값: SCTG +0.19 mm, GBR +0.11 mm; 3 mm 하방: +0.05 mm vs −0.05 mm); 임상 지표·변연골소실·OHIP-14 양호하고 군 간 유사.

소규모 파일럿·기술통계 한정 → 충분한 검정력의 RCT 필요; EFP S3 가이드라인은 연조직 단독 증대가 얇은 조직 부위(<2 mm)의 임플란트 주위염을 줄인다는 근거가 없다고 명시.

## Summary

This pilot randomized controlled trial provides the longest published follow-up (3 years) of a randomized comparison between subepithelial connective tissue grafting (SCTG, 연결조직이식) and guided bone regeneration (GBR, 유도골재생) for the management of small buccal bone dehiscences (협측 골결손부, ≤3 mm) at the time of single-implant placement in the posterior maxilla/mandible.

**Key finding**: Both augmentation strategies maintained stable buccal soft-tissue contours and favorable peri-implant health through 3 years, with most tissue remodeling occurring in the first year. Neither approach demonstrated clear superiority in this small sample. SCTG showed a marginally wider keratinized mucosa (각화점막, KM: 3.0 mm vs 2.1 mm), while GBR tended to preserve slightly more buccal bone at the implant shoulder (협측 골두께, BBT-0 median 0.55 mm vs 0.05 mm for SCTG — SCTG does not regenerate bone).

**Important caution from EFP S3 Clinical Practice Guideline**: No current evidence supports that soft tissue augmentation alone reduces the incidence of peri-implant diseases in sites with thin tissues (<2 mm). The biological equivalence observed here should not be interpreted as proof of hard-tissue augmentation being unnecessary.

## Key Contributions

- 3-year pilot RCT evidence for SCTG vs GBR at small buccal dehiscence sites (≤3 mm): both maintain stable buccal contour beyond year 1
- Profilometric STL-based quantification confirms minimal contour change between years 1 and 3 (all medians within ±0.3 mm)
- SCTG achieves greater KM width; GBR preserves more BBT at implant shoulder — different biological mechanisms, similar clinical outcomes
- MBL stable in both groups: SCTG median 0.0 mm, GBR 0.3 mm at 3 years; ICC 0.99
- One peri-implantitis case in SCTG group treated and excluded (n=1/6 original SCTG)
- Pilot data support hypothesis that soft-tissue augmentation alone can maintain buccal contour stability without mandating bone regeneration for small defects — pending confirmatory RCTs

## Methodology

**Design**: Extension of published 1-year pilot RCT (DRKS00012733). Randomization by computer-generated sequence, allocation concealed in sealed opaque envelopes, opened intraoperatively after confirming ≤3 mm dehiscence. University of Zurich, single center.

**Implant**: OsseoSpeed EV (Dentsply Sirona), 3.6 mm (premolar) / 4.2 mm (molar). Transmucosal healing 3–6 months → screw-retained monolithic zirconia crown. Follow-up: baseline (crown delivery ±10 days), year 1, year 3.

**Primary outcome**: Profilometric buccal contour change via STL superimposition (SMOP software) at ROI-1 (1 mm below mid-facial mucosal margin) and ROI-3 (3 mm). Linear tissue width (TW-1, TW-3) also measured.

**Secondary**: PD, BOP, PCR, KM width (6 sites/implant); BBT via CBCT+STL at implant shoulder/−1 mm/−3 mm; MBL via standardized periapical radiograph (ImageJ, thread-pitch calibration); OHIP-14. All descriptive statistics only.

## Results

### Buccal Contour (Year 1 → Year 3 delta, medians)

| | SCTG (n=5) | GBR (n=6) | Control (n=5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ROI-1 | +0.19 mm (IQR: −0.28 to +0.29) | +0.11 mm (−0.20 to +0.30) | +0.27 mm |
| ROI-3 | +0.05 mm (−0.36 to +0.31) | −0.05 mm (−0.22 to +0.07) | +0.11 mm |
| TW-1 | −0.32 mm | −0.17 mm | +0.10 mm |
| TW-3 | −0.14 mm | −0.03 mm | −0.18 mm |

Minimal change in all groups after year 1 — contour stability confirmed at 3 years.

### Peri-implant Parameters at 3 Years

| Parameter | SCTG | GBR | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| PD mean (mm) | 3.4 ± 1.1 | 3.1 ± 0.7 | 3.1 ± 0.7 |
| BOP (mean ratio) | 0.3 ± 0.2 | 0.3 ± 0.2 | 0.1 ± 0.2 |
| PCR (mean ratio) | 0.3 ± 0.2 | 0.2 ± 0.1 | 0.1 ± 0.2 |
| KM width mean (mm) | 3.0 ± 2.0 | 2.1 ± 1.2 | 3.0 ± 1.4 |

### Bone Data at 3 Years (medians)

| | SCTG | GBR | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| BBT-0 (implant shoulder) | 0.05 mm | 0.55 mm | 1.34 mm |
| BBT-1 (−1 mm) | 1.53 mm | 0.82 mm | 1.74 mm |
| BBT-3 (−3 mm) | 2.26 mm | 0.98 mm | 2.07 mm |
| MBL | 0.0 mm (Q1: −0.1; Q3: 0.2) | 0.3 mm (Q1: 0.0; Q3: 2.1) | 0.2 mm |

### PROMs

OHIP-14 median: SCTG 0.0, GBR 0.0, control 3.0 — all groups high oral health-related quality of life.
