## Three-line Summary

Single-center, two-arm parallel RCT (n=60 randomized, n=54 completed at 1 year) comparing screw-retained CAD/CAM zirconia abutments with a linear divergent (control) vs concave (experimental) transmucosal emergence profile in single-tooth anterior maxillary implants, using a delayed (standard healing abutment → definitive CAD/CAM abutment) workflow.

At 1 year, neither mid-facial mucosal zenith change (p=0.853), buccal soft tissue thickness (p=0.674), keratinized mucosal width (p=0.106), nor peri-implant health indicators (MBL, PI, MI, PD, BOP; all p>0.3) differed significantly between groups; implant survival was 100% and restoration success 98.2%.

Clinically, transmucosal abutment macro-morphology (linear divergent vs concave) did not measurably influence 1-year peri-implant mucosal or health outcomes in this delayed-loading, screw-retained, directly-veneered zirconia abutment protocol — contrasting with convex-vs-concave comparisons (e.g., Siegenthaler et al.) that did show significant recession differences, suggesting convexity, not concavity vs linearity, may be the key morphologic risk factor.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 2군 병행배정 RCT (무작위배정 n=60, 1년 완료 n=54) — 전방상악 단일치아 임플란트에서 나사유지형 CAD/CAM 지르코니아 지대주의 경조직-점막 이행부 형태를 선형발산형(대조군) vs 오목형(실험군)으로 비교, 표준 치유지대주 → 최종 CAD/CAM 지대주로 이행하는 지연(delayed) 프로토콜 사용.

1년 시점 협측 점막 정점(mid-facial mucosal zenith) 변화(p=0.853), 협측 연조직 두께(p=0.674), 각화점막폭(Keratinized Mucosal Width, KMW, p=0.106), 그리고 주위염 지표(변연골소실 Marginal Bone Level, MBL·치태지수 PI·점막지수 MI·탐침깊이 PD·탐침시출혈 BOP, 모두 p>0.3) 모두 두 군 간 유의차 없음; 임플란트 생존율 100%, 보철 성공률 98.2%.

임상적으로 이 지연식립·나사유지·직접 도재 베니어 프로토콜에서는 지대주 대형태(선형발산 vs 오목)가 1년 시점 주위 점막·건강 지표에 측정 가능한 영향을 주지 않았다 — 반면 볼록-오목(convex vs concave) 비교(예: Siegenthaler 등)는 유의한 퇴축 차이를 보였으므로, 오목 대 선형이 아니라 볼록형(convexity) 자체가 핵심 위험인자일 가능성을 시사한다.

## Summary

This single-center, two-arm parallel RCT (University of Iowa, IRB #201302798) tested whether the transmucosal transition morphology of custom, screw-retained CAD/CAM zirconia abutments (Atlantis Crown Abutment) — linear divergent (control/predicate design) vs concave (experimental, subtractively modified) — affects peri-implant soft tissue dynamics and health indicators over 1 year in single-tooth anterior maxillary implants. Sixty subjects were randomized after 8 weeks of healing on a standard healing abutment (delayed workflow, no implant provisionalization); 54 completed the 1-year follow-up. Implant survival was 100% and restoration success 98.2%. No significant between-group differences were found for the primary outcome (mid-facial mucosal zenith change) or any secondary outcome (buccal soft tissue thickness, keratinized mucosal width, marginal bone level, plaque index, mucosal index, probing depth, bleeding on probing); time — not abutment morphology — was the significant driver of the modest coronal mucosal migration and KMW gain observed in both groups. STL-based quantification of the actual linear (mm) and volumetric (mm³) morphologic differences between the two abutment designs likewise showed no correlation with mucosal thickness or zenith outcomes.

## Key Contributions

- Direct RCT comparison of **linear divergent vs concave** transmucosal abutment morphology (as opposed to the more commonly studied convex-vs-concave comparison), in a **delayed** (no-provisionalization) clinical workflow that mirrors typical everyday practice.
- Quantitative **STL superimposition** method (linear mm delta, volumetric mm³ delta) objectively characterizing the magnitude of abutment morphologic modification and testing its correlation with clinical mucosal outcomes — found no correlation.
- Tri-axial stereotactic photography protocol (Canfield camera + patient-specific bite fork, ImageJ calibration) for reproducible longitudinal mid-facial mucosal zenith tracking.
- Abutments directly veneered with feldspathic porcelain to 0.5 mm submarginal, eliminating the cement microgap — addressing a recognized biological-complication risk factor independent of the morphology variable under test.
- High overall success benchmark (100% implant survival, 98.2% restoration success at 1 year) with a well-powered sample (54/60 completers, ICC 0.96 for MBL measurement reliability).

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-center, 2-arm parallel RCT; simple randomization by an independent coordinator at the master-impression visit (8 weeks post implant placement), per CONSORT/Declaration of Helsinki/ISO 14155.
- **Implants**: OsseoSpeed EV (Astra Tech, Dentsply Sirona), internal conical connection; guided placement (Simplant Pro, tooth-supported stereolithographic guides, sCAIP) by a single surgeon, platform ≥3.0 mm apical to the planned mucosal zenith.
- **Abutments**: Custom screw-retained CAD/CAM zirconia (Atlantis Crown Abutment, VAD software) — linear divergent (control) vs concave (experimental) transmucosal transition — inserted 4 weeks after master impression, torqued to 25 Ncm.
- **Sample size**: Powered for a minimum clinically relevant 1.0 mm difference in 12-month mid-facial mucosal zenith change (SD 0.4-1.0 mm from prior literature), α=0.05 (12-month) / 0.0125 (Bonferroni, 4 timepoints), 80-90% target power; 30 subjects/arm with 15% attrition allowance.
- **Eligibility**: Adults, single-tooth anterior maxillary replacement (region 5-12), stable adjacent dentition, ≥5.5 mm buccal-lingual bone width, ≥2 mm baseline keratinized mucosa; excluded tobacco use, uncontrolled systemic disease, prior head/neck radiation, pregnancy, etc.
- **Outcomes**: Primary = 1-year mid-facial mucosal zenith change. Secondary = buccal soft tissue thickness, KMW, MBL, PI, MI, PD, BOP, and correlation of abutment STL-derived linear/volumetric change with mucosal thickness/zenith outcomes.
- **Follow-up**: Baseline (final restoration insertion), 1, 3, 6, 12 months.
- **Statistics**: Mixed models (subject = random effect), Pearson correlation, ICC + two-sample t-test for MBL; R v3.6.0.

## Results

| Outcome | Control (linear divergent) baseline → 12mo | Experimental (concave) baseline → 12mo | Between-group p (1y) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-facial mucosal zenith | 9.86 ± 1.15 → 9.62 ± 1.26 mm | 9.77 ± 1.15 → 9.60 ± 1.17 mm | 0.853 |
| Buccal soft tissue thickness | 2.72 ± 1.13 → 2.79 ± 0.52 mm | 2.81 ± 1.13 → 2.60 ± 0.47 mm | 0.674 |
| KMW | 2.70 ± 1.14 → 3.26 ± 1.06 mm | 3.31 ± 1.84 → 3.88 ± 1.64 mm | 0.106 |
| MBL, distal | -0.50 → -0.38 mm | -0.55 → -0.51 mm | 0.771 |
| MBL, mesial | -0.53 → -0.48 mm | -0.54 → -0.51 mm | 0.773 |
| Plaque index | 0.36 → 0.45 | 0.29 → 0.57 | 0.988 |
| Mucosal index | 0.74 → 0.91 | 0.59 → 0.90 | 0.409 |
| Probing depth | 2.53 → 2.67 mm | 2.42 → 2.55 mm | 0.307 |
| BOP (0-6 sites) | 1.48 → 1.48 | 1.10 → 1.61 | 0.917 |

- **Population**: 60 randomized (29 women/31 men, mean age 58.7y); 60 implants — incisor 51.6%, canine 11.7%, premolar 36.7%. 54 completed 1 year (29 concave / 25 linear divergent) after 6 total dropouts (1 abutment-neck fracture, 1 implant failure, 3 missed follow-up, 1 death).
- **Implant survival 100%**, **restoration success 98.2%** (master impression → 12 months).
- Time — not abutment group — was the significant driver of change: mucosal zenith coronal migration significant over time (p<0.001, greatest between abutment delivery and 1 month); KMW gain significant over time (p<0.001); PI (p=0.044) and MI (p=0.001) changed significantly over time; PD (p=0.096) and BOP (p=0.079) trended upward but not significantly.
- **STL superimposition**: predicate (linear) vs concave design differed by a mean linear delta of 0.15 ± 0.14 mm and a mean volumetric delta of -7.95 ± 6.10 mm³ (material subtracted for concavity). Neither the linear nor volumetric change correlated with buccal thickness change (r=-0.16/0.06, p=0.42/0.75) or mucosal zenith change (r=0.02/0.05, p=0.92/0.81).
- Intra-rater MBL measurement reliability: ICC=0.96 (95% CI 0.95-0.97).

**Limitations**: no PES/PROM esthetic assessment; baseline pre-implant mucosal phenotype not recorded; 1-year follow-up only; single center/surgeon/implant-abutment system; no convex-morphology arm, so the trial cannot directly address convex-specific recession risk reported elsewhere.
