## Three-line Summary

Case series (3 patients, 14-month follow-up) evaluated Collagen Graft x1D — a cross-linked porcine-derived collagen matrix — for vestibuloplasty, peri-implant soft tissue augmentation, and alveolar ridge preservation (ARP).

Vestibuloplasty achieved functional attached mucosa with probing depth reduction from 8 to 5 mm; soft tissue augmentation maintained PES 11/14; ARP preserved 73.3% vertical ridge height at 2 months; no complications observed; rapid epithelialization noted at 2 weeks.

Feasibility is demonstrated across three applications with no donor site morbidity, but evidence grade is low (case series, n=3, no control) — cannot claim equivalence to autografts without controlled trials.

## Summary

Song et al. (2026) present a case series of three patients in whom a cross-linked porcine-derived collagen matrix (Collagen Graft x1D, Genoss, Korea) was applied for three distinct soft tissue management scenarios around dental implants and extraction sites. The study addresses a practical clinical question: can a single commercial bilayer collagen matrix substitute for autogenous grafts in varied peri-implant soft tissue contexts?

**Product**: Collagen Graft x1D features a bilayer architecture — porous side placed toward the wound bed to promote revascularization, dense side facing the oral environment. Chemical cross-linking extends resorption time compared to non-cross-linked matrices (e.g., Geistlich Mucograft).

## Key Contributions

- Feasibility data for a Korean-manufactured porcine collagen matrix (Genoss) across three application paradigms not previously documented in the wiki
- Demonstrates applicability of open healing (vestibuloplasty, ARP) and submerged approaches with the same matrix
- PES-based esthetic outcome at 14 months provides a comparable data point to existing XCM literature
- No donor site morbidity reported, supporting patient acceptance rationale for XCM use

## Methodology

**Design**: Descriptive case series; 3 cases; no randomization; no control group; single institution (NHIS Ilsan Hospital, Korea).

**Surgical approaches**:
- **Case 1 — Vestibuloplasty**: Partial-thickness flap apically repositioned; matrix sutured at recipient site; open healing protocol.
- **Case 2 — Soft tissue augmentation (peri-implant)**: Submerged technique; matrix placed beneath coronally advanced flap at second-stage implant surgery; primary closure.
- **Case 3 — Alveolar ridge preservation (ARP)**: Matrix placed over extraction socket; open healing; no primary closure.

**Follow-up**: Cases 1–2: 14 months; Case 3: 2 months.

**Assessments**: Clinical photography, periapical radiographs, PD measurements, PES scoring. No CBCT volumetrics; no histology.

## Results

| Case | Indication | Primary Metric | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vestibuloplasty | PD | 8 mm → 5 mm at 14 mo |
