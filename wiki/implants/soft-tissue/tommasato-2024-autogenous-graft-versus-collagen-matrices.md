## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + network meta-analysis (23 studies in the qualitative synthesis, 16 studies — 11 RCTs and 5 CCTs — in the quantitative NMA; N = 573 sites) comparing autogenous soft tissue grafts (CTG, FGG) with collagen matrices (ADM, xCM, VCMX) for peri-implant soft tissue augmentation, with dimensional thickness change and keratinized mucosa width (KMW) as primary outcomes.
CTG was the best material for increasing peri-implant soft tissue thickness at both 180 and 360 days; ADM gave good buccal thickness increase mainly in the first three months, and vestibuloplasty + FGG was the most effective technique for KMW augmentation after 180 days (FGG best for KMW up to 90 days).
Clinical bottom line: CTG remains the reference for thickness gain and FGG for keratinized width, while ADM and VCMX are acceptable thickness alternatives with lower patient morbidity; recommendations are constrained by the limited number of included studies and high heterogeneity.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 주위 연조직 증대에서 자가이식(CTG·FGG)과 콜라겐 매트릭스(ADM·xCM·VCMX)를 비교한 체계적 고찰 + 네트워크 메타분석(NMA, 질적 23편·양적 16편 — RCT 11·CCT 5, N = 573 부위).
결합조직이식 (CTG, Connective Tissue Graft)이 180·360일 모두 연조직 두께 증가에서 최상위 재료였고, 무세포 진피 기질 (ADM, Acellular Dermal Matrix)은 술 후 3개월 이내 협측 두께 증가에 양호했으며, 전정성형술 + 유리치은이식 (FGG, Free Gingival Graft)이 180일 후 각화점막폭 (KMW, Keratinized Mucosa Width) 증대에 가장 효과적이었다 (90일까지는 FGG 단독이 최상).
임상적 하한선: 두께 확보는 CTG, 각화폭 확보는 FGG가 기준이며 ADM·용적안정형 콜라겐 매트릭스 (VCMX, Volume-Stable Collagen Matrix)는 이환율이 낮은 두께 대안이나, 포함 연구 수 제한과 높은 이질성 때문에 결론의 확실성은 제한적이다.

## Summary

This Italian Academy of Osseointegration (IAO) consensus-conference systematic review with network meta-analysis compared autogenous soft tissue grafts (CTG, FGG) against three matrix types (ADM, xenogeneic collagen matrix, volume-stable collagen matrix) for augmenting peri-implant soft tissues. Twenty-three studies were included qualitatively and 16 (11 RCTs, 5 CCTs) quantitatively, covering N = 573 sites, with soft tissue thickness change and keratinized mucosa width (KMW) as co-primary outcomes and PROMs, volumetric change, operating time, and periodontal measurements as secondary outcomes. CTG ranked best for increasing peri-implant soft tissue thickness at 180 and 360 days, ADM performed well for buccal thickness in the first three months, and vestibuloplasty + FGG was the most effective approach for KMW gain after 180 days (FGG was the best graft for KMW up to 90 days). Clinically, the review supports a two-goal decision rule — autogenous grafts for the dimensional target they win, matrices for lower-morbidity horizontal thickness — with the caveat that the small study pool and high clinical heterogeneity limit certainty. The manuscript is the published proceedings/update of an IAO consensus conference and was self-funded with no declared competing interests.

## Key Contributions

- Provides the first simultaneous ranking of **five** peri-implant soft tissue materials/techniques (CTG, FGG vs ADM, xCM, VCMX) rather than isolated pairwise contrasts.
- Disentangles the thickness goal (CTG optimal at 180 and 360 days) from the keratinized-width goal (FGG / vestibuloplasty + FGG optimal), showing no material wins both.
- Establishes a time dimension to the ranking: ADM is competitive only in the early (≤3 month) buccal thickness window; vestibuloplasty + FGG takes the KMW lead only after 180 days.
- Positions ADM and VCMX as lower-morbidity substitutes for horizontal thickness gain in patients who refuse or cannot undergo palatal harvesting.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + network meta-analysis (NMA).
- **Databases**: Electronic database search (search strategy detailed only in the paywalled full text).
- **Eligibility**: RCTs and CCTs, parallel or split-mouth, ≥10 patients treated; comparisons among CTG, FGG, ADM, xCM, VCMX (and vestibuloplasty + FGG).
- **n**: 23 studies in the qualitative analysis; 16 studies (11 RCTs, 5 CCTs) in the quantitative NMA; N = 573 sites.
- **Primary outcomes**: soft tissue thickness dimensional change; keratinized mucosa width (KMW) change.
- **Secondary outcomes**: (a) patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs); (b) volumetric changes; (c) surgical operating time; (d) other periodontal measurements.
- **Analysis**: Network meta-analysis (multivariate random-effects meta-regression methods; STATA-based network plotting referenced).

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Soft tissue thickness at 180 days | CTG ranked best |
| Soft tissue thickness at 360 days | CTG ranked best |
| Buccal thickness, first ~3 months | ADM showed good results |
| KMW up to 90 days | FGG best graft for increasing keratinized mucosa |
| KMW after 180 days | Vestibuloplasty + FGG most effective technique |
| Lower-morbidity thickness option | ADM, VCMX |
| Evidence base | 23 studies qualitative; 16 studies (11 RCT, 5 CCT), N = 573 sites |

Stated conclusion: "While CTG demonstrated better performance in all the comparison and FGG showed to be the best graft to increase keratinized mucosa up to 90 days, ADM and VCMX may be used to increase soft tissue horizontal thickness with lower patients' morbidity."

Stated limitations: limited number of included studies and high heterogeneity (patients, sites, surgical techniques, outcome measures, follow-up).
