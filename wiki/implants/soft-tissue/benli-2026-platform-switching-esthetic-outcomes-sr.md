## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of in vivo platform-switching (PS) studies, January 2014–June 2024 (MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, hand search); 91 records → 24 included.

PS reported to improve pink esthetic score, mesial/distal papilla height, buccal mucosal level, mucosal zenith change, and VAS esthetics, with superior anterior esthetic integration.

Concludes PS preserves soft tissue and improves esthetics — no meta-analysis or certainty grading in the abstract, so narrative-strength evidence.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰: 2014–2024 플랫폼 스위칭 (PS) in vivo 연구 24편.

PS에서 PES·유두 높이·협측 점막 높이·점막 정점 (Mucosal Zenith) 변화·VAS 심미 개선, 전치부 심미 우수 보고.

PS의 연조직 보존·심미 개선 결론 — 메타분석·확실성 평가 없음, 서술 수준 강도.

## Summary

The PS literature in the wiki is mostly about crestal bone. This review gathers the soft-tissue and esthetic side and reports that PS implants did better on pink esthetic score, papillae, buccal mucosal level and zenith change. The abstract gives directions, not magnitudes, and does not mention risk-of-bias or certainty assessment, and inclusion was "in vivo studies" rather than RCTs. The Cooper 2021 five-year RCT ingested alongside it is a useful check: its PS arm lost more bone than the conical arm and showed no zenith advantage. Treat this SR as a pointer to studies, not as a pooled estimate.

## Key Contributions

- Soft-tissue/esthetic synthesis for PS implants (24 studies).

## Methodology

- 3 databases + hand search; 2014–2024; English; two reviewers; no meta-analysis reported.

## Results

- Direction-only: improved PES, papilla height, buccal mucosal level, zenith change, VAS with PS.
