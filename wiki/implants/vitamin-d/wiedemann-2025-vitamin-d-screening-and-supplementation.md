## Three-line Summary

Narrative literature review of vitamin D3 status and early dental implant failure (EDIF), plus the effect of systemic and topical vitamin D3 supplementation on bone-to-implant contact (BIC) and peri-implant bone level.

Vitamin D deficiency was associated with nearly a fourfold increase in overall EDIF incidence; presurgical supplementation increased osseointegration, BIC and bone-level maintenance and decreased EDIF, even in diabetic subjects.

The authors recommend preoperative serum screening and supplementation for sub-physiologic levels (30 ng/mL or below) and for smokers, diabetics, obese and immunocompromised patients; the evidence is a non-systematic review read from the abstract only.

## Summary

This narrative review (J Biomed Mater Res B, Wiedemann et al. 2025) asks whether preoperative vitamin D deficiency is a risk factor for early dental implant failure (EDIF) and whether screening and supplementation improve outcomes. PubMed, Google Scholar, Cochrane and SCOPUS were searched (peer-reviewed indexed journals only) for the effect of vitamin D3 level on EDIF and peri-implant bone level; the authors then hypothesised that supplementation would offset the deficiency effect and assessed systemic and topical supplementation by BIC and peri-implant bone level. Reported conclusions: deficiency produced nearly a fourfold increase in overall EDIF incidence, and presurgical vitamin D3 increased osseointegration and BIC, enhanced bone-level maintenance and decreased EDIF, including in diabetic subjects. Supplementation is recommended for serum levels of 30 ng/mL or below and for smokers, diabetics, obese and immunocompromised patients. (Abstract-only: study numbers, effect-size CIs, EDIF time window and early-failure rate were not available.)

## Key Contributions

- Frames vitamin D status as a modifiable systemic risk factor for EDIF.
- Nearly fourfold higher overall EDIF incidence with deficiency (abstract-level figure).
- Covers both systemic and topical vitamin D3, assessed by BIC and peri-implant bone level.
- Practical recommendation: add preoperative serum vitamin D testing to the standard implant protocol; supplement at 30 ng/mL or below and in risk groups.

## Methodology

- Literature review across PubMed, Google Scholar, Cochrane, SCOPUS; peer-reviewed, indexed journals only.
- No study count, search dates, or quality appraisal reported in the abstract; setting, n and EDIF definition unavailable.

## Results

- Deficiency: impaired peri-implant bone formation; nearly fourfold overall EDIF incidence.
- Presurgical supplementation: increased osseointegration, BIC, bone-level maintenance; decreased EDIF, also in at-risk groups such as diabetic subjects.

## Limitations

- Abstract only; narrative design with no pooled estimate, CI or stated risk-of-bias method.
- Recommendation builds on an authors' hypothesis and heterogeneous source studies.
