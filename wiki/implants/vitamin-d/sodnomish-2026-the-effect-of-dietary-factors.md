## Three-line Summary

Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR; PROSPERO CRD420251172055) of human clinical studies up to November 2025 on dietary factors and nutrients in dental implant therapy: 27 studies included (13 cohort, 8 RCT, 3 cross-sectional, 2 case-control, 1 uncontrolled interventional), of which 24 examined vitamin D, 1 vitamin B and 1 vitamin C; 9 entered quantitative synthesis.

For early dental implant failure (EDIF, studies with outcomes assessed within 12 months, EDIF follow-up roughly 3-6 months), pooling of 5 studies gave a risk ratio of 1.87 (95% CI 1.01-3.45, p=0.05) for low versus sufficient vitamin D; ISQ at 3 months was higher in vitamin D-sufficient patients (2 studies, mean difference 4.37, 95% CI 0.41-8.33, p=0.03, I2=75%).

Evidence is dominated by observational designs with serious risk of bias in many studies and heterogeneous vitamin D thresholds, so the pooled associations are fragile (the EDIF interval touches 1.0); the authors advise vitamin D screening in at-risk patients, not a standard dosing regimen, and caution that excess levels may also be unfavorable (one 19-year study).

## 세줄요약

PRISMA-ScR 기반 범위 문헌고찰 (scoping review; PROSPERO CRD420251172055)로 2025년 11월까지의 영양·식이 인자와 임플란트 치료 결과에 대한 인체 임상연구 27편(코호트 13, 무작위대조시험 8, 단면 3, 증례-대조 2, 비대조 중재 1)을 포함했고, 24편이 비타민 D, 1편이 비타민 B, 1편이 비타민 C를 다뤘으며 9편이 정량 종합에 들어갔다.

조기 임플란트 실패 (early dental implant failure, EDIF) 5편 통합에서 비타민 D 저하군의 위험비 (risk ratio, RR)는 1.87 (95% CI 1.01-3.45, p=0.05)이었고, 3개월 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)는 비타민 D 충분군에서 평균 4.37 높았다 (2편, 95% CI 0.41-8.33, p=0.03, I2=75%).

근거는 관찰연구 위주이고 상당수가 비뚤림 위험이 심각하며 비타민 D 기준도 이질적이라 통합 결과는 취약하다 (EDIF 신뢰구간이 1.0에 걸침); 저자들은 고위험 환자에서 비타민 D 선별을 고려하되 표준 용량은 제시하지 못하며, 과잉 수치도 불리할 수 있다고 주의한다 (19년 추적 1편).

## Summary

This scoping review (PRISMA-ScR, PROSPERO CRD420251172055) from the University of Basel mapped human clinical evidence on dietary factors and nutrients in dental implant therapy. Three databases (PubMed, Web of Science, Cochrane Library) yielded 592 records (522 after de-duplication); 27 studies were included (13 cohort, 8 RCT, 3 cross-sectional, 2 case-control, 1 uncontrolled interventional), 24 on vitamin D, one on vitamin B (pain/paresthesia) and one on vitamin C (early soft-tissue healing). Nine studies contributed to quantitative synthesis.

For early dental implant failure (EDIF), five studies were pooled (Mantel-Haenszel fixed-effect): low vitamin D versus sufficient vitamin D gave a risk ratio of 1.87 (95% CI 1.01-3.45, p=0.05). Only one individual study showed a significant association; four showed higher EDIF with low vitamin D without reaching significance. Component studies had follow-up of about 3 to 12 months (for example 12 weeks, 4 months, 3-6 months). Implant stability (ISQ) was reported in seven studies, five positive; two comparable studies pooled to a 3-month mean difference of 4.37 ISQ units (95% CI 0.41-8.33, p=0.03, I2=75%). Most of seven short-term marginal bone loss studies reported greater loss with low vitamin D.

Long-term data are sparse: in a 10-year RCT all peri-implantitis cases occurred in vitamin D deficient patients (p=0.04), whereas a 19-year case-control study linked vitamin D excess to more bone loss and failure, suggesting both extremes may be unfavorable. The authors suggest vitamin D screening for higher-risk patients (limited sun exposure, older age, malabsorption, osteoporosis, chronic illness, prior early failure) and supplementation per medical deficiency guidelines; they state evidence does not support a standardized implant-specific dosing regimen.

## Key Contributions

- First scoping synthesis of human clinical nutrition-and-implant studies; evidence is almost entirely vitamin D.
- Pooled EDIF risk ratio 1.87 (95% CI 1.01-3.45) for low vitamin D, borderline at the 5% level.
- Documents vitamin D thresholds (<30 ng/mL low; <20 deficient; <10 severe) and seven published supplementation regimens.
- Identifies absent clinical data for other micronutrients (boron, selenium, copper, iron) and for co-factors of vitamin D metabolism.

## Methodology

- Search to November 2025, English only; RevMan Web; RoB 2 (8 RCTs: 2 low, 3 some concerns, 3 high) and ROBINS-I (non-randomized mostly moderate to serious, 1 critical).
- Early outcomes defined as assessed at 12 months or less; late outcomes beyond 12 months.

## Limitations

- Scoping design; pooled subsets small and heterogeneous (ISQ I2=75%); EDIF lower bound 1.01.
- Observational dominance, serious risk of bias in three of five EDIF studies, variable EDIF windows and vitamin D cut-offs, inconsistent confounder adjustment (smoking, comorbidity, bone quality).
- Internal count inconsistencies (24+1+1=26 versus 27 included; abstract categories sum to 22) and MBL forest plot numbers not given in text.
