## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cross-sectional laboratory analysis of 3300 screw-retained 3Y-TZP monolithic zirconia full-arch prostheses (1900 maxillary, 1400 mandibular) from a Dubai commercial laboratory (2019–2024); life table survival excluding clinician/patient interventions; failure mode categorization.

5-year cumulative survival 91.67%; failure events increasing from 2 (year 1) to 14 (year 5); framework fractures n=38 (leading cause, linked to insufficient vertical space); ceramic chipping n=4 (mandibular, excessive ceramic layering); zero titanium cylinder debonding or fracture events.

Framework fracture is the dominant failure mode in monolithic zirconia full-arch prostheses with a concerning increase in events over time; titanium subcomponents are mechanically reliable at 5 years; vertical prosthetic space is the key design target.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective cross-sectional, laboratory-based
- **Source**: Commercial dental laboratory, Dubai, UAE
- **N**: 3300 (maxillary 1900, mandibular 1400)
- **Material**: 3Y-TZP monolithic zirconia, screw-retained; gingival porcelain only
- **Period**: August 2019–August 2024 (5yr)
- **Analysis**: Life table survival
- **Inclusion for failure**: Technical/material failures only (clinician/patient interventions excluded)

## Key Results

### Survival

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| 5-year cumulative survival | **91.67%** |
| Year 1 failures | 2 |
| Year 5 failures | **14** (⚠ increasing trend) |

### Failure Modes

| Mode | N | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Framework fractures | **38** | **Leading cause**; associated with insufficient vertical space |
| Ceramic chipping | 4 | Mainly mandibular; excessive ceramic layering |
| Titanium cylinder debonding | **0** | Confirmed mechanical reliability |
| Titanium cylinder fracture | **0** | Confirmed mechanical reliability |

## Key Clinical Implication

"Insufficient vertical space" is named as the primary risk factor for the 38 framework fractures. This directly links to the CSSA and height thresholds defined in [[implants/full-arch/papaspyridakos-2026-zirconia-full-arch-140-jaws-8yr]]:

- CSSA ≥80mm² required
- Posterior prosthetic space >10mm required

The increasing failure trend (2→14 events year 1→5) suggests 5-year survival figures underestimate long-term risk; extended follow-up studies are needed.
