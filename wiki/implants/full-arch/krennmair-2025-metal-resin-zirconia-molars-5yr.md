## Three-line Summary

Retrospective 5-year comparison of 14 bimaxillary Zrm-ISFP (metal-resin + zirconia molar quadrants) vs 15 standard r-ISFP patients; PRTC prevalence, implant/prosthesis survival, MBL, and temporal complication distribution assessed.

100% implant and prosthesis survival in both groups; acrylic-tooth fracture (p<0.001) and repair (p=0.015) significantly lower in Zrm-ISFP; total PRTC at 3–5yr post-loading: r-ISFP 34 vs Zrm-ISFP 7; rebasing, cleaning, screw hole repair, MBL, and implant-related complications were equivalent.

Adding zirconia molars to metal-resin full-arch prostheses reduces PRTC by >4× at 5 years while preserving 100% survival, equivalent MBL, and the cost/repairability advantages of the metal-resin framework — a practical hybrid middle ground.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective comparative cohort
- **N patients**: 29 bimaxillary ISFP
- **TG (Zrm-ISFP)**: 14 patients — metal-resin framework + zirconia molar quadrants
- **CG (r-ISFP)**: 15 patients — standard full metal-resin
- **Follow-up**: 5 years post-loading
- **PRTC time periods**: −1yr; >1–3yr; >3–5yr
- **Outcomes**: PRTC, implant/prosthesis survival, MBL, implant-related technical complications

### Survival

| Outcome | Zrm-ISFP (TG) | r-ISFP (CG) |
|---|---|---|
| Implant survival | **100%** | **100%** |
