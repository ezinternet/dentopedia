# Digital Smile Designed Computer-Aided Surgery versus Traditional Workflow in All-on-4: 4-Year RCT

## Three-line Summary
1. **Study design & context**: Single-center RCT (n=50) comparing digital smile design + guided flapless surgery vs. traditional workflow for All-on-4 immediate loading, 4-year follow-up.
2. **Key findings**: Digital group showed significantly less marginal bone loss (0.83 vs 1.12 mm at 4 years, p<0.0001) and 100% implant survival vs. 96–98% in traditional; zero fixture fractures.
3. **Clinical implication**: Digital workflow with flapless guided surgery preserves peri-implant bone and improves patient satisfaction; supports adoption of fully digital All-on-4 protocols.

## 세줄요약
1. **연구 설계·맥락**: 단일센터 RCT(n=50)에서 디지털 스마일 디자인+가이드 무절개 수술을 전통적 All-on-4 즉시부하 워크플로와 4년간 비교.
2. **핵심 결과**: 디지털 그룹이 유의하게 적은 변연골 소실(4년 0.83 vs 1.12 mm, p<0.0001)과 100% 임플란트 생존율(전통 96–98%), 고정체 파절 0건 기록.
3. **임상적 함의**: 무절개 가이드 서저리 기반 디지털 워크플로는 주위골 보존과 환자 만족도 우위 입증; 완전 디지털 All-on-4 프로토콜 도입 근거 마련.

## Summary

This randomized clinical trial compared two workflows for All-on-4 immediate loading rehabilitation: a fully digital protocol (intraoral scanning, digital smile design, virtual implant planning, 3D-printed surgical guide, flapless guided surgery, CAD/CAM provisional prosthesis) versus a traditional protocol (conventional impressions, stone models, wax-up, surgical guide, flap surgery, conventional provisional). Fifty edentulous patients were randomized (25 per group) and followed for 4 years with assessments at 12, 24, 36, and 48 months. Primary outcomes were implant survival rate, marginal bone level (MBL), complications, and patient satisfaction.

## Key Contributions

- **First 4-year RCT** comparing fully digital vs. traditional All-on-4 workflow
- **Digital workflow superiority** demonstrated for marginal bone preservation (0.83 vs 1.12 mm at 4 years, p<0.0001)
- **100% implant survival** in digital group vs. 95–100% in traditional (axial 100%, tilted 95–96.7%)
- **Flapless guided surgery** feasible and beneficial for peri-implant tissue preservation
- **Digital smile design** significantly improved patient satisfaction (93–98% "very effective" ratings)
- **Zero fixture fractures** and fewer prosthetic complications in digital group

## Methodology

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Design** | Randomized controlled trial, parallel groups |
| **Setting** | IRCCS San Raffaele Hospital, Milan, Italy |
| **Participants** | 50 edentulous patients (25 maxilla, 25 mandible per group) |
| **Randomization** | Computer-generated, allocation concealment via sealed envelopes |
| **Intervention** | Digital: DSD + virtual planning + 3D-printed guide + flapless surgery + CAD/CAM provisional |
| **Control** | Traditional: conventional impressions + wax-up + guide + flap surgery + conventional provisional |
| **Implants** | NobelActive (Nobel Biocare), 3.3/3.8 mm diameter, 11–15 mm length |
| **Loading** | Immediate (within 24h) screw-retained provisional prosthesis |
| **Follow-up** | 12, 24, 36, 48 months |
| **Outcomes** | Implant survival, MBL (periapical radiographs, parallel technique), complications, patient satisfaction (VAS) |
| **Analysis** | Chi-square for survival, ANOVA for MBL, intention-to-treat |

## Results

### Implant Survival (48 months)

| Group | Maxilla Axial | Maxilla Tilted | Mandible Axial | Mandible Tilted |
|-------|---------------|----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Traditional (n=100) | 100% (30/30) | 96.7% (29/30) | 100% (20/20) | 95.0% (19/20) |
| Digital (n=100) | 100% (34/34) | 100% (34/34) | 100% (16/16) | 100% (16/16) |

No statistically significant difference between groups (p>0.05). Two implant failures in traditional group (both tilted).

### Marginal Bone Level (mean ± SD, mm)

| Timepoint | Traditional Total | Digital Total | p-value |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|---------|
| 12 months | 1.04 ± 0.29 | 0.68 ± 0.13 | <0.0001 |
| 24 months | 1.08 ± 0.28 | 0.75 ± 0.13 | <0.0001 |
| 36 months | 1.10 ± 0.27 | 0.79 ± 0.11 | <0.0001 |
| 48 months | 1.12 ± 0.26 | 0.83 ± 0.11 | <0.0001 |

Digital group showed significantly less bone loss at all timepoints.

### Complications (48 months)

| Complication | Traditional | Digital |
|--------------|-------------|---------|
| Implant failure | 2 (2%) | 0 |
| Peri-implantitis | 1 (1%) | 0 |
| Fixture fracture | 0 | 0 |
| Abutment unscrewing | 3 (3%) | 1 (1%) |
| Provisional prosthesis fracture | 2 | 2 |
| Definitive prosthesis chipping | 1 | 1 |

### Patient Satisfaction (Very Effective %)

| Aspect | Traditional | Digital |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Mock-up / Smile previsualization | 45% | 93% (DSD) / 98% (mock-up) |
| Surgery | 71% | 94% (guided) |
| Immediate loading | 95% | 92% |
