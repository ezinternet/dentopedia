## Three-line Summary

Multicenter retrospective (42 patients, 43 full-arch prostheses, 171 Straumann BLT SLA Roxolid implants including 37 NDI 3.3 mm; 95% 4-implant jaws with 2 tilted posteriors ≤30°; immediate loading; follow-up 32–55 months).

Overall survival 97.7%; 2-year mean interproximal MBL 0.15 mm (maxilla 0.16 mm / mandible 0.14 mm); no significant difference between tilted and axial MBL; MBL between 12–24 months averaged only 0.09 mm; 0 NDI fractures at 55 months.

Apically tapered implants with aggressive threads achieve adequate primary stability (IT ≥30 Ncm) even in soft bone and fresh extraction sockets; among the lowest published MBL values for full-arch; night guard compliance is critical — all provisional fractures occurred in bruxers not using guards.

## Summary

According to PubMed, this multicenter retrospective from Istanbul and Sakarya evaluated the Straumann Bone Level Tapered (BLT) implant design for full-arch immediate function over up to 55 months. The BLT's apically tapered body with aggressive threads allows osteotomy under-preparation — drilling slightly smaller than the implant to achieve compressive engagement at the apex, producing higher insertion torque in soft (Type III–IV) bone and in fresh extraction sockets.

**Outstanding MBL**: the reported 0.15 mm at 24 months is among the lowest in the full-arch immediate loading literature, consistent with the "stabilization" pattern after initial remodeling. The 12–24 month interval showed only 0.09 mm of additional loss, indicating excellent long-term stability.

**Tilted vs axial equivalence**: consistent with SR literature (Patzelt et al., Menini et al.) — no significant MBL difference at any follow-up point. This supports the biomechanical principle that splinting 4 implants in a full-arch framework neutralizes the bending moment on individual tilted implants.

**Occlusal design described in detail**: tilted implants ≤30°; flat fossa, shallow anatomy; canine-protected occlusion; narrow occlusal table; acrylic resin as shock absorber; night guard from day of surgery for all patients.

## Key Contributions

- 4.5-year follow-up for BLT implants in full-arch immediate loading with excellent MBL
- Documents 0 NDI fractures (Ti-Zr alloy) at 55 months in posterior full-arch positions
- Provides explicit occlusal design protocol to reduce overloading
- All provisional fractures linked to bruxism + night guard non-compliance — emphasizes behavioral compliance

## Methodology

- Pro Arch Guide for tilting control (≤30°); loading threshold IT ≥30 Ncm
- Provisional (day of surgery): acrylic with 10 teeth, no metal framework
- Definitive (4 months): CAD-CAM or laser-sintered metal + ceramic/acrylic, 12 teeth, max cantilever ≤10–12 mm
- MBL: OPG by calibrated assistants using Romexis at 0, 4, 12, 24 months
- Survival: Kaplan-Meier; Log-rank mandible vs maxilla comparison
