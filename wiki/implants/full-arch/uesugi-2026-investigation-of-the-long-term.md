## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 203 Japanese patients (923 implants: 323 zygomatic + 600 conventional) treated with all-on-four immediate loading (Malo protocol + anatomy-guided approach), followed 3–13 years (mean 8.3 yr); Kaplan-Meier survival with implant/patient-level risk factor analysis (log-rank, Cox regression).

ZI implant-level survival 95.9% vs CI 98.7% (p=0.0178); patient-level ZI 94.5% vs CI 97.9% (p=0.16 ns). **Palatal ZI platform: 92.5% survival (HR 18.2, p=0.026)**; vestibular 98.6%, ridge 96.9%. **Systemic disease: patient-level HR 14.9 (p=0.012), implant-level HR not significant**. ZI failures: 11 implants, 6/9 cases sinusitis (67%) — palatal/ridge platforms.

Long-term all-on-four with zygomatic implants shows high but inferior survival vs conventional implants; **palatal platform position is the dominant modifiable implant-level risk factor** (biomechanical off-axis loading + sinus proximity); systemic disease (especially circulatory) drives patient-level risk; sinusitis is the principal failure mode — mandates platform selection strategy and lifelong surveillance.

## Study Design

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Design** | Retrospective cohort, single-center (Showa Univ + Malo Dental Tokyo) |
| **Population** | 203 patients, fully edentulous maxilla, all-on-four concept with ZIs + CIs |
| **Implants** | 323 ZIs (Nobel: Brånemark TiUnite 65, Zygoma 45° 114, Zygoma 0° 144) + 600 CIs (Nobel, ≥3.3mm ant / ≥4.0mm post) |
| **Surgery** | Flap, anatomy-guided approach (AGA) for ZI; surgical guide; no computer-assisted surgery |
| **Loading** | Immediate provisional (Co-Cr reinforced acrylic on Ti cylinders); final at 6 mo (Ti framework + ceramic/acrylic) |
| **Follow-up** | 3–6 months; BOP, suppuration, mucosal status; mean 99.4 ± 34.1 months |
| **Analysis** | Kaplan-Meier survival (patient/implant level); log-rank univariate; Cox multivariable (exploratory) |
| **Failure Definition** | Implant removal; censored at last follow-up if surviving |

## Key Contributions

1. **Largest long-term ZI+CI cohort** (203 pts, 923 implants, up to 13 yr) in Asian population
2. **Palatal platform = 18× failure hazard** — first multivariable quantification of platform position risk
3. **Sinusitis = dominant failure mode (67%)** — directly linked to palatal/ridge platform anatomy
4. **Systemic disease (circulatory) = 15× patient-level hazard** — highlights medical optimization importance
5. **CI survival excellent (98.7%)** — supports maximizing conventional implants when bone allows
6. **Late failures up to 13 yr** — contradicts "early failure only" assumption; mandates lifelong maintenance

## Methodology Details

**ZI Placement (AGA Protocol)**: CBCT preoperative planning → platform position classified as palatal/ridge/vestibular → bony window in maxillary wall → sinus mucosa elevated for drilling visibility → ZI inserted to zygomatic bone → platform height adjusted to alveolar crest

**Prosthetic Protocol**: Multi-unit abutments (35 Ncm straight/45°/60°, 15 Ncm 17°/30°) → open-tray impression → Co-Cr reinforced acrylic provisional same-day → soft diet 2-3 mo → final Ti framework + ceramic (IPS e.max) or acrylic (Bioblend/LIVDENT) at 6 mo

**Statistical Approach**: Kaplan-Meier with numbers at risk; log-rank univariate (pairwise Bonferroni); Cox multivariable with variables significant in univariate + clinically relevant; limited events → exploratory interpretation

## Clinical Decision Framework

```
Zygomatic Implant Platform Selection Priority:
1. VESTIBULAR (98.6% survival) — Preferred; avoids sinus, favorable emergence
2. RIDGE (96.9%) — Acceptable; intermediate risk
3. PALATAL (92.5%, HR 18.2) — AVOID unless anatomically unavoidable
   → If palatal mandatory: intensified sinus surveillance, hygiene reinforcement
```

**Patient Risk Stratification**:
- **Low risk**: No systemic disease, non-smoker, vestibular ZI platform → standard 6-mo maintenance
- **High risk**: Circulatory disease ± diabetes, smoker, palatal/ridge ZI platform → 3-mo maintenance + sinus screening (CBCT/clinical annually)

## Limitations

- Retrospective, single-center (Japanese population) — generalizability to other ethnicities/healthcare systems unclear
- Limited failure events (11 ZI failures) → multivariable Cox exploratory, wide CIs (HR 18.2 CI: 1.4–233)
- Smoking pack-years not calculated (≥10 cig/day binary); severity of systemic disease not graded
- No computer-guided surgery — modern navigation/robotics may alter platform position distribution
- Conventional implant survival not analyzed for risk factors (only 7 failures)
- Immediate loading protocol only — delayed loading outcomes not represented
