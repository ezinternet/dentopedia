## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and prevalence meta-analysis of 10 studies evaluating trans-sinus implants for full-arch rehabilitation of the atrophic maxilla (2,359 screened; PRISMA; PROSPERO CRD42024537320), comparing failure rates against axial/tilted implants.

232 trans-sinus implants (5 failed) vs 675 axial/tilted (5 failed): no statistically significant difference in failure rate (RR=2.80, 95% CI 0.89–8.77, p=0.076); hybrid-corrected estimate RR=2.74, p=0.070; pooled success rates comparable.

Trans-sinus implants offer a viable bone-graft-sparing alternative to axial/tilted implants for severe atrophic maxilla rehabilitation, but further RCTs with longer follow-up are needed before definitive clinical guidelines can be drawn.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석, 10편 포함(2,359편 선별), 경유 상악동(Trans-Sinus) 임플란트 전악수복 실패율 vs 수직·경사 임플란트 비교; PROSPERO CRD42024537320.

경유 상악동 232개(5개 실패) vs 수직·경사 675개(5개 실패): 실패율 유의차 없음 (RR=2.80, 95% CI 0.89–8.77, p=0.076; hybrid RR=2.74, p=0.070); 풀링 성공률 비슷.

경유 상악동은 골이식 최소화 대안으로 생존율 동등 확인; 장기 추적 RCT 필요.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Systematic review + prevalence meta-analysis (PRISMA)
- **N studies**: 10 (screened 2,359)
- **PROSPERO**: CRD42024537320
- **Inclusion**: Full-arch rehabilitation with trans-sinus implants alone or combined with axial/zygomatic implants
- **Comparator**: Axial and tilted implants
- **Meta-analysis method**: Haldane correction + hybrid correction (zero-cell handling)
- **Outcome**: Implant failure rate comparison

## Key Results

### Failure Rate Comparison

| Group | N Implants | Failed | Failure Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trans-sinus | 232 | 5 | ~2.2% |
| Axial/tilted | 675 | 5 | ~0.7% |

| Statistic | RR | 95% CI | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Haldane correction | **2.80** | 0.89–8.77 | 0.076 (NS) |
| Hybrid correction | **2.74** | 0.91–8.17 | 0.070 (NS) |

- Pooled analysis: **comparable success rate**
- Interpretation: numerically higher failure rate for trans-sinus (point estimate ~3×), but CI crosses 1.0 — no statistically significant difference; likely underpowered

### Clinical Implications

- Trans-sinus implants can rehabilitate severely atrophic maxillae without extensive lateral window sinus surgery or block bone grafts
- Indicated when axial/tilted placement is not feasible due to extreme maxillary atrophy
- Technique is less invasive than zygomatic implants for moderately atrophic cases

## Limitations

- Abstract only — detailed heterogeneity (I²), individual study characteristics, follow-up durations, and complication data not available
- Low absolute event count (5+5 failures) — meta-analysis statistically underpowered
- Short-to-medium follow-up in included studies
- No RCTs available; observational studies dominate
