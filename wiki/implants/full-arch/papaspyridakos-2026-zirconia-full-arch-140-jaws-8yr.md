## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study of 140 monolithic zirconia full-arch IFCDPs fabricated with digital workflow at a private practice setting, up to 8yr follow-up; survival and complication assessment plus CSSA and posterior linear height measurements correlated with fracture occurrence.

95.7% survival (6/140 fractured: 3 maxillary, 3 mandibular); non-fractured vs fractured CSSA: maxillary 80.5 vs 62.5mm² (Δ17.9mm², p<0.001); mandibular 79.4 vs 54mm² (Δ25.4mm², p<0.001); posterior prosthetic space: maxillary 10.58 vs 9mm (p=0.018); mandibular 11.89 vs 9.55mm (p<0.001).

CAD design thresholds: connector CSSA ≥80mm² and posterior prosthetic space >10mm to protect monolithic zirconia IFCDPs against fracture.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective observational
- **Setting**: Private practice (Boston, Rochester, San Francisco, New York)
- **N jaws**: 140 edentulous
- **Material**: Monolithic zirconia IFCDPs
- **Fabrication**: Digital workflow (CAD/CAM)
- **Follow-up**: Mean 3yr, range 1–8yr
- **Primary**: Survival and complication rates
- **Secondary**: CSSA and posterior linear height vs fracture correlation

## Key Results

### Survival

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| IFCDPs fractured | 6/140 (3 maxillary, 3 mandibular) |
| Survival rate | **95.7%** |
| Mean follow-up | 3yr (range 1–8yr) |

### CSSA — Fracture Correlation

| Arch | Non-fractured CSSA | Fractured CSSA | Δ | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maxilla | **80.5 mm²** | 62.5 mm² | 17.9 mm² | <0.001 |
| Mandible | **79.4 mm²** | 54.0 mm² | 25.4 mm² | <0.001 |

### Posterior Prosthetic Space — Fracture Correlation

| Arch | Non-fractured height | Fractured height | Δ | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maxilla | **10.58 mm** | 9.00 mm | 1.58 mm | 0.018 |
| Mandible | **11.89 mm** | 9.55 mm | 2.34 mm | <0.001 |

### Recommended CAD Design Thresholds

| Parameter | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Connector CSSA (both arches) | **≥80 mm²** |
| Posterior prosthetic space (linear height) | **>10 mm** |

## CAD Design Checklist (from this study)

Before milling a monolithic zirconia full-arch prosthesis, verify:

1. **Connector CSSA ≥80mm²** at every inter-implant connector
2. **Posterior prosthetic space >10mm** (linear height at distal region)
3. Complement with cantilever parameters from [[implants/full-arch/tirone-2025-cad-parameters-cantilever-fracture]]: CL/CSCA<0.51; CL/SAOL<1.48
