## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cross-sectional laboratory analysis of 3300 screw-retained 3Y-TZP monolithic zirconia full-arch prostheses (1900 maxillary, 1400 mandibular) from a Dubai commercial laboratory (2019–2024); gingival-only veneering; life table survival excluding clinician/patient-driven remakes; failure mode categorization with vertical space documentation.

5-year cumulative survival **91.67%**; **escalating failure trend**: Year 1: 2 events → Year 5: 14 events (cumulative 40). **Framework fractures n=38 (90.5% of failures)** — 34/38 (89.5%) with insufficient vertical prosthetic space (CSSA <80mm², posterior space <10mm); ceramic chipping n=4 (mandibular, excessive occlusal veneering); **zero Ti cylinder debonding/fracture** — mechanical reliability confirmed.

Monolithic zirconia full-arch prostheses show excellent 5-year durability but **framework fracture is the dominant failure mode**, driven predominantly by **insufficient vertical prosthetic space** — directly validating the Papaspyridakos 2024 biomechanical thresholds; **Ti base interface is mechanically reliable**; escalating annual failure rate signals need for >5-year data and patient counseling on long-term risk.

## 세줄요약

후향적, 3300 단일체 지르코니아 전악, 두바이 기공소, 5년 생명표 생존 분석(치은부만 베니어).

5년 생존율 91.67%; 프레임워크 골절 38건(90.5%, 89.5%가 공간 부족), 세라믹 칩핑 4건(하악 과도 베니어); Ti 실린더 문제 0건.

프레임워크 골절 주요 실패; 수직 공간 확보가 최우선 예방; 연도별 급증 추세로 장기 리스크 상담 필요.

## Key Contributions

1. **Largest single-source dataset** (n=3300) for full-arch monolithic zirconia CAFIP survival analysis
2. **5-year survival 91.67%** — defines real-world durability baseline for 3Y-TZP monolithic full-arch
3. **Framework fracture = primary failure (90.5%)**; **insufficient vertical space = principal modifiable risk factor (89.5%)**
4. **Quantifies escalating failure trend** (2→14 events/year) — flags 5-year survival as underestimating long-term risk
5. **Gingival-only veneering strategy validated** — zero chipping in standard protocol; chipping only with occlusal veneering
6. **Ti cylinder/screw interface = zero failures** — screw-retained monolithic connection mechanically dependable
7. **Directly corroborates Papaspyridakos 2024 CSSA/space thresholds** in a massive independent dataset

## Methodology Details

**Fabrication Protocol**: Standardized CAD/CAM workflow; 3Y-TZP (Katana Zirconia HTML Plus, Kuraray Noritake); minimum CSSA targets per protocol; sintering per manufacturer; gingival porcelain (IPS e.max Ceram) only

**Vertical Space Measurement**: From digital master casts (exocad DentalCAD); posterior space measured at first molar region; CSSA at thinnest connector

**Life Table Analysis**: 1-year intervals; events = laboratory remakes for technical/material failure only; censored = lost to follow-up or non-technical remakes; this exclusion minimizes bias in material/technical survival estimation

**Failure Classification**: 
- Framework fracture: complete or partial fracture of zirconia framework
- Ceramic chipping: porcelain fracture limited to veneering layer
- Ti cylinder debonding: separation of Ti base from zirconia
- Ti cylinder fracture: fracture of Ti base/abutment
- Screw loosening/fracture: classified as maintenance (not prosthesis failure)

## Clinical Implications

### Design & Planning Checklist

| Step | Action | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **1. CBCT space analysis** | Measure posterior prosthetic space & plan CSSA | 89.5% fractures linked to space deficiency |
| **2. Enforce thresholds** | CSSA ≥80mm², posterior space >10mm | Papaspyridakos 2024 validated here |
| **3. Maxilla: extra caution** | 4× fracture rate vs mandible | Consider metal-reinforced framework if space marginal |
| **4. Gingival-only veneering** | No occlusal porcelain | Zero chipping in standard protocol |
| **5. Screw-retained monolithic** | Avoid cement-retained | Ti interface 0% failure; retrievability |

### Patient Counseling Script

> "5년 생존율은 91.7%로 우수합니다. 하지만 **연도별 실패가 2건에서 14건으로 늘고 있어** 5년 이후 위험이 더 클 수 있습니다. **가장 중요한 건 '보철물 들어갈 공간' 확보**인데, 공간이 부족하면 프레임이 깨질 위험이 90%에 달합니다. 수술 전 CT로 공간을 꼭 확인하고, 부족하면 다른 설계를 고려하겠습니다. 티타늄 연결부는 지금까지 단 한 건도 문제 없었으니 그 부분은 안심하셔도 됩니다."

### Maintenance Protocol

- **Years 1–2**: 6-month recall (baseline)
- **Years 3–5**: 4-month recall (escalating failure trend)
- **Year 5+**: 3-month recall + CBCT framework screening (if feasible)
- **Screw check**: Every visit (12 loosening, 2 fracture in 3300 units)

## Limitations

- Retrospective laboratory-based — no clinical/patient-reported outcomes (PROMs, masticatory function, satisfaction)
- Single laboratory (Dubai) — manufacturing consistency, material batch, technician skill not generalizable
- Implant system heterogeneity (Nobel, Straumann, MegaGen, others) — not controlled/stratified
- Vertical space from digital casts, not intraoral measurement — potential discrepancy
- Life table assumes constant hazard within 1-year intervals
- Censoring of clinician/patient remakes may underestimate total complication burden
- No data on: occlusal scheme (canine guidance vs group function), parafunction/bruxism, maintenance compliance, opposing dentition
- Follow-up right-censored at 5 years — true long-term survival unknown
