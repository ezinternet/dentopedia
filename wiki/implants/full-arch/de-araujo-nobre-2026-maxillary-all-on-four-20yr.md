## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 740 patients (2960 implants, MALO CLINIC, Lisbon) with immediate full-arch maxillary All-on-4 rehabilitation followed up to 20 years — the longest follow-up and largest sample for this indication.

Implant CSurR was 94.1% at 15 years and 90.7% at 20 years; prosthesis success rate was 98.1%; MBL progressed slowly (1.07 mm @15yr → 1.46 mm @20yr); mechanical complications affected 78.5% of patients and biological complications 35.1%; Cox regression identified smoking (HR=1.93) and biological complications (HR=2.18) as independent risk indicators for implant failure; paradoxically, mechanical complications were protective for MBL >3mm (OR=0.26) through increased maintenance visit frequency.

The maxillary All-on-4 concept is durable at 20 years but carries higher complication burden than the mandibular arch; smoking is a dual-axis risk indicator and should be specifically addressed in pre-surgical counseling; the pseudo-maintenance mechanism offers a model-based rationale for proactive recall scheduling.

## 세줄요약

후향적 코호트(740명, 2960임플란트, MALO CLINIC 리스본) — 상악 즉시 전악 All-on-4 재건 최대 표본·최장 추적.

임플란트 CSurR 90.7%@20yr; 보철 성공율 98.1%; MBL 1.07→1.46mm(15→20yr); 기계적 합병증 78.5%/생물학적 합병증 35.1%; Cox 다변량 — 흡연 HR=1.93, 생물학적 합병증 HR=2.18 (실패 위험인자); 기계적 합병증 OR=0.26 (MBL 보호).

상악 All-on-4 20년 내구성 확인; 상악은 하악보다 합병증 부담 높음; 흡연은 실패+골소실 이중 위험 — 수술 전 금연 상담이 결과에 가장 큰 영향.

## Study Overview

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| Design | Retrospective cohort (STROBE guidelines) |
| Institution | MALO CLINIC, Lisbon, Portugal |
| Patients | 740 (440F, 300M; mean age 55.3yr, range 20–88yr) |
| Arch | Maxilla (full-arch) |

## Risk Indicators

### Implant Failure (Cox Proportional Hazards, Multivariate)

| Risk Factor | HR | p |
|---|---|---|
| **Smoking** | **1.93** | <0.001 |
| **Biological complications** | **2.18** | <0.001 |

### MBL > 3mm (Binary Logistic Regression, Multivariate)

| Factor | OR | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Smoking** | **3.60** | Risk |
| **Biological complications** | **3.70** | Risk |
| **Mechanical complications** | **0.26** | **Protective** |

Mechanism of protection for mechanical complications: increased appointment frequency ("pseudo-maintenance") leads to earlier detection and management of periimplant disease — reducing bone loss even while the mechanical problem itself represents a complication.

### Biological Complications (Binary Logistic Regression, Multivariate)

| Factor | OR | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Age** (younger at baseline) | **0.48** | Risk (younger = more biological complications) |
| **Smoking** | **1.78** | Risk |

### Sensitivity Analysis (Implant Survival @20yr)

| Reported (actuarial) | 90.7% |
| Worst case (all LTF counted as failures) | 59.4% |

## Arch Comparison (Maxilla vs Mandible — same center, same era)

| Metric | Maxilla (this study) | Mandible (companion, 2025) |
|---|---|---|
| Follow-up | 15–20 yr | 20–25 yr |
| N | 740 | 181 |
| Implant CSurR (longest) | 90.7% @20yr | 90% @25yr |
| MBL (longest) | 1.46 mm @20yr | 2.28 mm @25yr |
| Mech. complications | 78.5% | 70.7% |
| Biol. complications (pt) | 35.1% | 24.9% |

Maxillary arch consistently shows higher complication rates — consistent with lower cortical bone density in the posterior maxilla and longer cantilever demands.
