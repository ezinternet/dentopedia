> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **핵심 명제**: 전악수복 고정성 보철물(Fixed Dental Prosthesis, FDP) 4–10년 추적에서 **임플란트 생존율은 높으나(~99.4%), 보철 생존율(Prosthesis Survival Rate)은 5년 80%·10년 60%**로 낮아지고, 합병증 없는 성공(Success)은 5년 14.7%에 불과 — **100% 환자가 최소 1건 소합병증 경험**, 중간 합병증 없는 기간 3년
> - **연구 규모**: 24명 환자, 34악궁, 180개 임플란트; 45.8% 흡연자, 45.8% 이갈이(Bruxism, "probable"), 16.7% 당뇨(DM), 54.2% 치주 병력; 평균 추적 5.21년(4–9년); 단일기관
> - **보철 재료**: 금속-도재(Metal-Ceramic) 66.7%, 지르코니아(Zirconia) 29.2%, 금속-아크릴(Metal-Acrylic) 4.2%; 시멘트 유지 62.5%, 나사 유지(Screw-Retained) 37.5%; 캔틸레버(Cantilever) 5–7.5 mm
> - **이갈이(Bruxism) 효과**: 브룩시즘 → **파절(Chipping) p=0.049**, 하악 소합병증 증가 **p=0.015** (경계선 유의) — 주요 기술 합병증(스크류 파절·보철 파절·어버트먼트 파절) 및 임플란트 1개 실패 모두 이갈이 환자에서 발생
> - **캔틸레버·재료 효과**: 캔틸레버 길이(5–7.5 mm) ↔ 변연골소실(MBL) 상관 없음(p>0.05); 재료 종류·유지 방법 ↔ 합병증률 유의차 없음(p>0.05)
> - **임상 시사점**: 전악수복 보철은 **모든 환자에서 장기 유지관리가 필수**; 이갈이 환자는 강화된 유지관리 계획 및 모놀리식 재료 선택 고려 필요; 흡연+DM 복합인자는 치근단주위염(Peri-implantitis) 위험 증가와 연관
> - **한계**: 소규모(n=24), "probable" 이갈이 진단(폴리솜노그라피 없음), 후향적 설계, 이질적 추적 기간 — 탐색적 연구

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center study of 24 patients (34 edentulous arches, 180 implants, mean follow-up 5.2yr, range 4–9yr) with full-arch implant-supported FDPs; patient-level risk factors including bruxism, smoking, and DM assessed alongside prosthetic design variables.

Implant survival ~99.4% (1 lost); cumulative prosthesis survival 80% at 5yr and 60% at 10yr; success (complication-free) 14.7% at 5yr with median complication-free time 3 years; 100% of prostheses experienced at least one minor complication; "probable" bruxism was significantly associated with chipping (p=0.049) and more minor complications in the mandible (p=0.015).

Cantilever length (5–7.5 mm) and prosthetic material type did not significantly correlate with MBL or complications; the findings underscore that long-term full-arch rehabilitation necessitates lifelong maintenance with special attention to parafunctional risk factors.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일기관 연구, 24명/34악궁/180개 임플란트, 평균 추적 5.2년(4–9년); 전악수복 FDP; 이갈이·흡연·DM·설계 변수별 합병증 평가.

임플란트 생존율 ~99.4%; 보철 누적생존율 5년 80%·10년 60%; 합병증 없는 성공률 5년 14.7%·중간 3년; 100% 최소 1건 소합병증; 이갈이 → 파절 p=0.049·하악 소합병증 p=0.015.

캔틸레버(5–7.5 mm)·재료 종류와 MBL·합병증 간 유의 상관 없음; 전악수복은 모든 환자에서 평생 유지관리 필요.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective observational study, single center (Kocaeli, Turkey)
- **Population**: 24 patients (13M/11F), mean age 58.17 ± 9.73yr (range 39–77yr)
- **N arches**: 34 (20 maxilla / 14 mandible)
- **N implants**: 180 (Straumann, Nobel Biocare, Nucleoss); 24 angled posteriorly (All-on-4 concept)
- **Treatment period**: 2015–2020
- **Follow-up**: Mean 5.21 ± 1.56yr (range 4–9yr)
- **Risk factors**: 45.8% smokers, 45.8% "probable" bruxers (self-report + clinical exam), 16.7% DM, 54.2% periodontal history
- **Prosthetic materials**: Metal-ceramic 66.7%, zirconia-ceramic 29.2%, metal-acrylic 4.2%
- **Retention**: Cemented 62.5%, screw-retained 37.5%
- **Cantilever units**: 37 total; 5–7.5 mm length

## Key Results

### Survival and Success

| Outcome | Rate |
|---|---|
| Implant survival | ~99.4% (1/180 failed) |
| Prosthesis survival (3yr) | 95.8% |
| Prosthesis survival (5yr) | **80%** |
| Prosthesis survival (10yr) | **60%** |
| Success (complication-free, 1yr) | 61.8% |
| Success (complication-free, 3yr) | 26.5% |
| Success (complication-free, 5yr) | **14.7%** |
| Major complication-free survival (5yr) | 91.2% |
| Major complication-free survival (10yr) | 58.1% |
| Median complication-free time | **3 years** |

### Complications Summary

| Complication | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Any minor complication | 100% of prostheses (over full FU period) |
| Chipping/fracture (minor technical) | 25 arches |
| Gingival recession (minor biological) | 10 arches |
| Inflammation (minor biological) | 15 arches |
| Decementation (minor technical) | 10 arches |
| Peri-implantitis (major biological) | 4 arches |
| Prosthesis fracture (major technical) | 3 arches |
| Screw fracture (major technical) | 2 arches |
| Late implant failure | 1 |

### Bruxism Analysis

| Variable | p-value |
|---|---|
| Bruxism → chipping | **0.049** |
| Bruxism → minor complications (mandible) | **0.015** |
| Bruxism → marginal bone loss | NS |
| Material type → complications | NS |
| Retention type → complications | NS |
| Cantilever length → MBL | NS |

- 16 of 25 chipping events occurred in "probable" bruxers
- All prosthesis fractures (3) involved bruxers; 1 of 2 screw fractures in bruxers
- 1 late implant failure in a bruxer
- Note: borderline significance — small sample limits definitive conclusions
