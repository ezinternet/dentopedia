## One-line Summary
Cochrane SR (2 RCTs, 3yr): zygomatic implants probably reduce implant failures and time to rehabilitation vs bone-grafted conventional implants (1.3 vs 444 days), but probably increase complication rate (~80% vs ~50%).

## 한줄요약

## Summary
This Cochrane SR (pub4, evidence to November 2025) assessed zygomatic implants for severely atrophic edentulous maxillae. Two RCTs were included with 3-year follow-up. Zygomatic implants probably reduce implant failures (OR 0.23) and dramatically shorten rehabilitation time (~1 day vs ~444 days) compared to conventional implants in grafted bone, but probably increase complications (OR 4.53; ~80% vs ~50%). The effect on prosthetic failures and patient satisfaction is uncertain due to imprecision. For drilling technique, piezoelectric surgery shows no meaningful difference from rotary drills in outcomes, but rotary drills are approximately 9 minutes faster. Overall evidence is limited and of low to moderate certainty.

## Key Contributions
- Moderate-certainty evidence: zygomatic implants probably reduce implant failures vs grafted conventional implants (OR 0.23, 95% CI 0.06–0.96)
- Moderate-certainty evidence: zygomatic implants probably increase complications (OR 4.53, 95% CI 1.47–13.98; ~80% vs ~50%)
- Moderate-certainty evidence: rehabilitation time ~443 days shorter with zygomatic approach (1.3 vs 444 days)
- No clinically important difference between piezoelectric vs rotary drilling for zygomatic site preparation
- Comparison with subperiosteal implants: no eligible RCTs found

## Methodology
- Design: Cochrane SR; 2 RCTs identified (searched November 2025)
- Comparison 1: parallel-group RCT, n=71; zygomatic (immediate load) vs delayed conventional implants in grafted bone; 3yr follow-up
- Comparison 3: split-mouth RCT, n=20; piezoelectric vs rotary drilling; 3yr follow-up
- Risk of bias: RoB 2; generally low; patient satisfaction in one study at high risk (missing data)
- Synthesis: GRADE; narrative only (insufficient studies for meta-analysis)

## Results
**Zygomatic vs conventional in grafted bone (Comparison 1, 3yr):**
| Outcome | OR / MD | 95% CI | Certainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failures | OR 0.23 | 0.06–0.96 | Moderate |
| Prosthetic failures | OR 0.29 | 0.05–1.56 | Low |
| Complications | OR 4.53 | 1.47–13.98 | Moderate |
| Patient satisfaction | MD +0.40 | −3.05 to +3.85 | Low |
| Time to rehab | MD −443 days | −519 to −367 | Moderate |

**Piezoelectric vs rotary drills (Comparison 3, 3yr):**
| Outcome | OR / MD | 95% CI | Certainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant/prosthetic failures | OR 1.00 | 0.02–50.40 | Low |
| Complications | OR 1.50 | 0.25–8.99 | Low |
| Operative time | MD −9.15 min | −10.41 to −7.89 | Moderate (rotary faster) |
