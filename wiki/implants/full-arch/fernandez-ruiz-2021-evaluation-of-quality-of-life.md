## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT (n=80, 40 per group, two Spanish centers, 2017–2020) comparing patient-reported outcomes of fixed full-arch prostheses on 2–4 zygomatic + 2–4 anterior conventional implants versus All-on-4 (2 axial + 2 tilted 35–45°) in severe atrophic edentulous maxillae; OHIP-14 and a 5-grade satisfaction questionnaire at ≥1 year after definitive prosthesis placement.

Zygomatic-group patients scored significantly better on all seven OHIP-14 domains and total score (18.48±3.42 vs 30.43±4.37, p<0.001) and were more satisfied (77.5% "extremely satisfied" vs 0%, p<0.001), while implant survival was 100% in both groups and peri-implantitis was equivalent (12.2% vs 11.9%, p=0.113); prosthetic complications occurred only in the All-on-4 group (3 fractures, 5 food traps).

In severely atrophic maxillae, zygomatic-based fixed rehabilitation offers equivalent survival but better quality of life and satisfaction than All-on-4; key limitations are non-registration (Spanish data-protection rules), group differences beyond the arch design (implant type, number, anesthesia), and ~20-month follow-up.

## 세줄요약

전향적 무작위대조시험 (RCT, n=80, 군당 40명, 스페인 2개 센터, 2017–2020)으로 심한 위축 무치악 상악에서 관골 임플란트 (Zygomatic Implant, ZI) 2–4개 + 전방 재래식 임플란트 2–4개 기반 고정보철과 All-on-4 (수직 2개 + 경사 35–45° 2개)의 환자 중심 결과를 확정 보철 식립 1년 후 구강건강 영향 지수 (Oral Health Impact Profile-14, OHIP-14)·만족도 설문으로 비교.

ZI군이 OHIP-14 7개 영역과 총점 모두 유의하게 우수했고 (18.48±3.42 vs 30.43±4.37, p<0.001) 만족도도 유의 우위 (‘매우 만족’ 77.5% vs 0%, p<0.001); 임플란트 생존율 (Implant Survival Rate)은 양군 100%, 주위염 (peri-implantitis)은 동등 (12.2% vs 11.9%, p=0.113)이었으나 보철 합병증은 All-on-4군에만 발생 (가철 파절 3건, 음식 저류 5건).

심한 위축 상악에서 ZI 기반 고정수복은 All-on-4와 생존율은 동등하나 환자 삶의 질 (Quality of Life, QoL)·만족도에서 우위; 다만 공개 등록 안 됨, 군간 임플란트 종류·개수·마취법 차이, 약 20개월 단기 추적이 한계.

## Summary

This CONSORT-compliant RCT randomized 80 patients with severely atrophic edentulous maxillae (unsuitable for conventional implants on CBCT) to fixed prostheses on 2–4 zygomatic + 2–4 anterior conventional implants (Group 1, general anesthesia, insertion torque 45–60 Ncm) or to an All-on-4 concept with four premaxillary implants, two axial and two tilted 35–45° (Group 2, IV sedation, torque 45–55 Ncm), all Galimplat internal-connection implants with immediate provisionalization within 48 h and definitive screw-retained metal–resin prostheses at 6 months. At ≥1 year after definitive prosthesis placement (mean 19.40±4.37 vs 20.25±3.01 months, p=0.314), zygomatic-group patients reported significantly better quality of life on every OHIP-14 domain and higher satisfaction, while implant survival was 100% in both groups (396 implants total) and peri-implantitis rates were statistically equivalent (12.2% of zygomatic implants vs 11.9% of All-on-4 implants, p=0.113). Surgical complications were rare and confined to the zygomatic group (one maxillary sinusitis 2.5%, one orbital cellulitis 2.5%), whereas prosthetic complications (three provisional fractures, five food-trap cases) occurred only in the All-on-4 group. The authors conclude that zygomatic-implant rehabilitation of atrophic maxillae provides better patient quality of life and satisfaction than the All-on-4 concept at equivalent survival.

## Key Contributions

- First prospective randomized clinical study to directly compare quality of life and satisfaction between zygomatic-implant-supported and All-on-4 fixed prostheses — no prior head-to-head patient-reported comparison existed.
- Demonstrates that in severe maxillary atrophy the two graftless approaches are biologically equivalent (100% implant survival both groups; equivalent peri-implantitis 12.2% vs 11.9%, p=0.113) but diverge on patient-reported outcomes (all 7 OHIP-14 domains favoring zygomatic group, p≤0.05).
- Documents the prosthetic-compensation trade-off of the All-on-4 concept in the atrophic maxilla: 3 provisional-prosthesis fractures and 5 food-trap cases vs zero prosthetic complications in the zygomatic group.
- Reports ZAGA anatomy distribution for the zygomatic cohort (no ZAGA 0/3/4; 17.27% ZAGA 1, 82.73% ZAGA 2), which the authors use to explain the low peri-implantitis rate via residual maxillary bone anchorage.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective randomized clinical trial, CONSORT-compliant, parallel groups 1:1 (randomization via www.randomization.com); University of Murcia Ethics Committee approval 2674/2019; July 2017–January 2020; two centers (university dental clinic + private clinic). Trial not publicly registered (Spanish data-protection rules).
- **Sample**: 80 patients with severe atrophic edentulous maxillae not suitable for conventional implants (CBCT-confirmed); ASA I/II; exclusions included acute maxillary sinusitis, zygomatic fracture history, diabetes/bisphosphonates/severe osteoporosis, head–neck radiotherapy <18 months. Power 80%, α=0.05.
- **Group 1 (zygomatic)**: 2–4 zygomatic + 2–4 anterior conventional implants (Galimplat, internal connection); general anesthesia with nasal intubation; insertion torque 45–60 Ncm; 236 implants placed (139 ZI, 97 conventional).
- **Group 2 (All-on-4)**: four premaxillary implants, 2 vertical + 2 tilted 35–45° to bypass the maxillary sinus anterior wall, platforms positioned posteriorly to shorten cantilevers; IV sedation; insertion torque 45–55 Ncm; 160 implants.
- **Prosthetics**: immediate resin provisional fixed prostheses within 48 h; definitive screw-retained metal–resin prostheses at 6 months; insertion torque thresholds as above.
- **Outcomes**: OHIP-14 (0–56, higher = worse) and 5-grade satisfaction questionnaire at ≥1 year after definitive prosthesis; peri-implantitis assessed clinically + radiographically (RVG 5100); complications surveillance (sinusitis, paresthesia, oroantral fistula, orbital cellulitis).
- **Statistics**: SPSS 20; Student's t-test (quantitative), Pearson's χ² (qualitative); significance p≤0.05.

## Results

**Follow-up after definitive prostheses**: Group 1 19.40±4.37 (12–22) months vs Group 2 20.25±3.01 (12–24) months, p=0.314. Groups homogeneous for age (p=0.684), sex (p=0.117), smoking (p=0.749), alcohol (p=0.090), antagonist dentition (p=0.679).

**Survival & complications** (396 implants: 236 zygomatic-group [139 ZI + 97 conventional], 160 All-on-4):

| Outcome | Zygomatic group | All-on-4 group |
|---|---|---|
| Implant survival | 100% | 100% |
| Prosthesis survival (both groups pooled) | 96.25% | — |
| Peri-implantitis (implant-level) | 17/139 ZI (12.2%) | 19/160 (11.9%), p=0.113 |
| Peri-implantitis, conventional implants in G1 | 0/97 | — |
| Post-surgical complications | 1 sinusitis (2.5%), 1 orbital cellulitis (2.5%) | 0 |
| Prosthetic complications | 0 | 3 fractures (7.5%), 5 food traps (paper states 15.50%; 5/40 = 12.5%) |

**ZAGA classification** (139 zygomatic implants): ZAGA 1 — 24 (17.27%); ZAGA 2 — 115 (82.73%); no ZAGA 0, 3 or 4.

**OHIP-14** (lower = better quality of life; Student's t-test):

| OHIP-14 domain | Zygomatic (n=40) | All-on-4 (n=40) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Functional limitation | 3.03 ± 1.29 | 6.35 ± 1.84 | <0.001 |
| Physical pain | 2.15 ± 0.89 | 3.80 ± 0.72 | <0.001 |
| Psychological discomfort | 2.20 ± 1.15 | 4.23 ± 1.18 | <0.001 |
| Physical disability | 3.23 ± 1.23 | 5.08 ± 1.65 | <0.001 |
| Psychological disability | 3.38 ± 0.71 | 4.10 ± 1.66 | 0.013 |
| Social disability | 2.60 ± 0.81 | 4.18 ± 1.31 | <0.001 |
| Handicap | 1.93 ± 0.69 | 2.75 ± 0.84 | <0.001 |
| **Total** | **18.48 ± 3.42** | **30.43 ± 4.37** | **<0.001** |

**Satisfaction** (Pearson's χ², p<0.001): extremely satisfied 31 (77.5%) vs 0 (0%); satisfied 9 (22.5%) vs 6 (15.0%); a little satisfied 0 vs 30 (75.0%); no change 0 vs 3 (7.5%); dissatisfied 0 vs 1 (2.5%). The three patients with prosthetic fractures were among the four least-satisfied All-on-4 patients.

## Limitations

- First study of this comparison — no external benchmark to compare results against (authors' own statement).
- The two groups differ in more than the arch design: implant type (zygomatic vs conventional), implant number, and anesthesia protocol all co-vary, so the quality-of-life difference cannot be attributed to the arch design alone.
- Not publicly registered (Spanish data-protection rules) and single region/manufacturer (all implants Galimplat, internal connection).
- Short follow-up (~12–24 months after definitive prosthesis); satisfaction measured with a single 5-grade item rather than a validated multi-item instrument; peri-implantitis cases in zygomatic implants reported without suppuration/hyperplasia detail.
- Source-text note: the food-trap percentage is internally inconsistent in the paper ("15.50%" for 5/40 = 12.5%); both the reported and computed values are shown in Results above.
