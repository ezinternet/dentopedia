## Three-line Summary

This consensus report from Working Group 3 of the 1st GCCG developed four clinical recommendations on advanced diagnostic imaging, augmentation techniques, and complication management for edentulous maxilla rehabilitation. All four reached consensus (≥75% agreement) through anonymous plenary voting by 68-88 participants following the S2k-level guideline framework. Recommendations are grounded in two systematic reviews and international expert surveys.

## 세줄요약

최초 글로벌 컨센서스 임상지침(GCCG) 그룹 3의 합의 보고서로, 상악 무치악 재활을 위한 고급 진단 영상·증대술·합병증 관리에 대한 4개 임상 권고안을 개발. S2k 수준 지침 프레임워크와 구조화된 명목그룹기법을 따라 68-88명 참가자의 익명 투표로 모든 권고안 합의 도달(≥75%). 시스테마틱 리뷰 2편과 국제 전문가 설문을 근거로 하며, PROM·장기 결과·유지 프로토콜에서의 근거 격차를 확인.

## Summary

Working Group 3 of the first Global Consensus for Clinical Guidelines in Implant Dentistry focused on advanced diagnostic imaging, hard and soft tissue augmentation, and complication management for the edentulous maxilla. Through a structured nominal group technique and anonymous voting, four consensus recommendations were developed addressing CBCT-based surgical planning, bone augmentation approaches, soft tissue management, and complication prevention/treatment. The report highlights important evidence gaps, particularly in standardized PROMs, long-term outcomes, and maintenance protocols.

## Key Contributions

- First global consensus on advanced diagnostic imaging for edentulous maxilla
- Evidence-based recommendations on augmentation and complication management
- S2k-level guideline methodology (AWMF framework)
- Identifies critical evidence gaps for future research

## Methodology

S2k-level consensus guideline following AWMF framework. Evidence synthesis included two systematic reviews (Sabri et al. 2026; Saleh et al. 2026) and structured nominal group technique. Draft recommendations discussed at consensus meeting (Boston, June 16-18, 2025) and finalized through anonymous plenary voting. Consensus: ≥75% but ≤95% agreement; strong consensus: >95%.

## Results

- 4 clinical recommendations formulated (all reached consensus)
- Voting participation: 68-88 per recommendation (mean 79/105)
- Covers: diagnostic imaging, augmentation, complication management
- Evidence gaps identified: PROMs, long-term outcomes, maintenance
