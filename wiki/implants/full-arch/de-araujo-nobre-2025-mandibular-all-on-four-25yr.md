## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 181 patients (724 implants, MALO CLINIC, Lisbon) with mandibular immediate full-arch All-on-4 rehabilitation followed for 20–25 years — the longest published follow-up for this protocol.

Implant and prosthesis cumulative survival rates were 90% and 92% at 25 years; MBL continued to progress slowly (1.71 mm @20yr → 2.28 mm @25yr); mechanical complications affected 70.7% of patients; biological complications affected 24.9% at patient level and 11.6% at implant level; smoking and prior contiguous implant failure were risk indicators for biological complications.

The mandibular All-on-4 concept remains structurally viable at 25 years; mandibular outcomes appear more favorable than maxillary (lower complication rates), supporting the differentiated arch-specific risk counseling; the 25-year cohort is critically small (n=3) so estimates carry wide uncertainty.

## 세줄요약

후향적 코호트(181명, 724임플란트, MALO CLINIC 리스본) — 하악 즉시 전악 All-on-4 재건 20–25년 추적, 동 술식 현존 최장 추적.

임플란트·보철 누적생존율 90%/92% @25yr; 변연골소실(MBL) 20년 1.71mm → 25년 2.28mm (완만 진행); 기계적 합병증 70.7% / 생물학적 합병증 24.9% (환자 단위), 11.6% (임플란트 단위); 흡연·인접임플란트 기왕실패가 생물학적 합병증 위험인자.

하악 All-on-4는 25년 장기에서도 실행 가능; 하악은 상악보다 합병증 발생률 낮음; 25년 코호트 n=3으로 해당 수치 해석 주의 필요.

## Study Overview

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| Design | Retrospective cohort (STROBE) |
| Institution | MALO CLINIC, Lisbon, Portugal |
| Patients | 181 (108F, 73M; mean age 59.2 ± 11.9yr) |
| Arch | Mandible (full-arch) |
| Implants | 724 |
| Protocol | All-on-4: 2 axial anterior + 2 tilted posterior; immediate loading |
| Mean follow-up | 264 months (range 240–321) |
| Patients @20yr | 77 |
| Patients @25yr | 3 (⚠ very small) |
| Lost to follow-up | 100/181 (55%) |

| MBL @20yr | 1.71 ± 1.98 mm |
| **MBL @25yr** | **2.28 ± 2.07 mm** |

## Arch Comparison (Mandible vs Maxilla — same center)

| Metric | Mandible (this study) | Maxilla (companion, 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Follow-up | 20–25 yr | 15–20 yr |
| N | 181 | 740 |
| Implant CSurR (longest) | 90% @25yr | 90.7% @20yr |
| MBL (longest) | 2.28 mm @25yr | 1.46 mm @20yr |
| Mech. complications | 70.7% | 78.5% |
| Biol. complications (pt) | 24.9% | 35.1% |

Mandibular arch consistently shows lower complication rates than maxillary — consistent with known arch-specific bone quality differences.
