## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cross-sectional analysis of 3300 screw-retained 3Y-TZP monolithic zirconia complete arch fixed implant-supported prostheses (CAFIPs) from a Dubai commercial dental laboratory (2019–2024); maxillary n=1900, mandibular n=1400; life table survival analysis.
5-year cumulative survival rate 91.67%; primary failure mode: framework fractures n=38 (insufficient vertical space); ceramic chipping n=4 (mandibular over-layering); 0 titanium cylinder failures; escalating failure trend 2 events (year 1) → 14 (year 5).
Full-arch monolithic ZrO₂ CAFIPs show high durability at 5 years; inadequate vertical space is the top modifiable risk factor; Ti screw components demonstrated complete mechanical reliability.

## Summary

This retrospective cross-sectional study analyzed laboratory records of 3300 screw-retained monolithic 3Y-TZP zirconia complete arch fixed implant-supported prostheses (CAFIPs) manufactured over 5 years in Dubai. Only technical/material failures (laboratory returns) were included; clinician- or patient-driven interventions were excluded to minimize bias. The 5-year cumulative survival rate was 91.67% by life table analysis. The dominant failure mode was framework fracture (n=38), primarily associated with insufficient vertical prosthetic space. Ceramic chipping was rare (n=4) and confined to mandibular prostheses with excessive porcelain layering; none occurred in prostheses with porcelain limited to the gingival zone. Critically, zero titanium cylinder debondings or fractures were observed across the entire dataset. A concerning increasing trend in annual failures (2 → 14 events/year) suggests aging-related material degradation or cumulative loading effects require further investigation.

## Key Contributions

- Largest single-source dataset (n=3300) for full-arch ZrO₂ CAFIP 5-year laboratory failure analysis
- 5-year cumulative survival 91.67% — defines real-world durability baseline for monolithic ZrO₂ full-arch
- Framework fracture is primary failure; insufficient vertical space is the principal modifiable risk factor
- Minimal porcelain veneering (gingival zone only) strategy effectively eliminates chipping risk
- Ti cylinder/screw interface shows zero failures — screw-retained connection is mechanically dependable
- Year-on-year failure increase (2→14) flags need for 10-year follow-up data
