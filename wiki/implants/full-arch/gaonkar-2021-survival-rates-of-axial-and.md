# Survival Rates of Axial and Tilted Implants in All-on-4: Systematic Review

## Three-line Summary
1. **Study design & context**: Systematic review of 25 clinical studies (14 prospective, 11 retrospective) evaluating axial vs. tilted implant survival in All-on-4 rehabilitations (72–132 months follow-up).
2. **Key findings**: Cumulative implant survival 94–98%; no significant difference between axial vs. tilted implants or maxilla vs. mandible; prosthesis survival 99–100% at 12 months; mean marginal bone loss 1.3±0.4 mm.
3. **Clinical implication**: All-on-4 is a viable, cost-effective treatment for edentulous jaws; tilted implants do not compromise survival or bone levels; RCT evidence needed for definitive conclusions.

## 세줄요약
1. **연구 설계·맥락**: All-on-4 재활에서 경사 vs 축방향 임플란트 생존율을 평가한 25편 임상연구(14 전향적, 11 후향적) 체계적 고찰, 72–132개월 추적.
2. **핵심 결과**: 누적 임플란트 생존율 94–98%; 경사/축방향 간 유의한 차이 없음, 상악/하악 간 차이 없음; 보철물 생존율 12개월 99–100%; 평균 변연골 소실 1.3±0.4 mm.
3. **임상적 함의**: All-on-4는 무치악 악골에 비용효과적·예측 가능한 치료; 경사 임플란트는 생존율·골레벨 불이익 없음; 확실한 결론 위해 대규모 RCT 필요.

## Summary

This systematic review evaluated the survival rates of axial and tilted implants in the rehabilitation of edentulous jaws using the All-on-4 concept. A comprehensive search across MEDLINE, PMC, Google Scholar, Embase, and Cochrane CENTRAL yielded 380 articles, with 25 studies meeting inclusion criteria (minimum 1-year follow-up, human studies, clinical trials reporting survival rates and bone levels). The review included 14 prospective and 11 retrospective studies, encompassing over 7,000 implants. No RCTs were identified.

## Key Contributions

- **Comprehensive synthesis** of 25 clinical studies on All-on-4 implant survival (largest review at time of publication)
- **No survival difference** between axial and tilted implants confirmed across maxilla and mandible
- **Long-term data** up to 132 months showing cumulative survival 94–98%
- **Prosthesis survival** 99–100% at 12 months; acrylic fracture identified as main complication
- **Marginal bone loss** average 1.3±0.4 mm (12-60 months), no difference by implant angulation or jaw
- **Clinical recommendation** for metal framework reinforcement to prevent acrylic fractures

### Summary of Key Outcomes

- **Implant Cumulative Survival Rate (72-132 months)**: 94% to 98%
- **Axial vs. Tilted**: No statistically significant difference
- **Maxilla vs. Mandible**: No statistically significant difference
- **Prosthesis Survival (12 months)**: 99% to 100%
- **Mean Marginal Bone Loss (12-60 months)**: 1.3 ± 0.4 mm
- **Main Prosthetic Complication**: Acrylic prosthesis fracture (especially in bruxers)
- **Other Complications**: Opposing dentition wear, prosthetic screw loosening
