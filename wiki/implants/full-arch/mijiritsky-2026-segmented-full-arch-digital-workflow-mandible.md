## Three-line Summary

Prospective consecutive series, 14 patients (mean age 62.3y), 119 implants, 14 segmented mandibular ISFCDs fabricated with fully digital workflow (intraoral scan, digital design, milling/printing); mean follow-up 36±6 months (range 24–48 months).

ISR and PSR 100%; mean MBL 0.71±0.24mm; peri-implant mucositis 4.20% (5 implants), peri-implantitis 0.84% (1 implant); zero biomechanical complications (no framework fractures, no screw loosening, no chipping); patient satisfaction 9.2/10 VAS.

Segmented mandibular ISFCDs with digital workflow showed excellent 3-year outcomes — 100% survival, minimal bone loss, no mechanical failures, and high patient satisfaction.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Prospective consecutive case series
- **N patients**: 14 (mean age 62.3±8.4 years)
- **N implants**: 119
- **N prostheses**: 14 segmented mandibular ISFCDs
- **Workflow**: Fully digital (intraoral scanning, digital design, milling/printing)
- **Follow-up**: Mean 36±6 months (range 24–48 months)
- **Primary outcomes**: ISR, PSR, MBL
- **Secondary outcomes**: Biological complications, technical complications, PROMs

## Clinical Implications

The complete absence of biomechanical complications over 3 years is notable. Possible mechanisms:

1. **Segmented design** distributes occlusal stress across multiple smaller units — avoiding the single-point stress concentration of monolithic frameworks
2. **Digital manufacturing** reduces human error in fit and reduces marginal gaps that promote screw loosening
3. **Mandible-specific** — the U/V-shaped mandibular arch may benefit particularly from segmentation by reducing the torque magnification seen with rigid monolithic bars

MBL 0.71mm at 3 years is within the generally accepted threshold of ≤1.0mm/year for the first year and ≤0.2mm/year thereafter.

## Limitations

- Small cohort (n=14) — insufficient power for rare events
- No control group — cannot directly compare segmented vs non-segmented
- Abstract only — segmentation design (number of segments, connector type) and material not described
- 3-year follow-up — inadequate for long-term complications (compare [[implants/full-arch/pelser-2026-ifcd-long-term-complications-17yr]]: 49.5% complication-free at 17yr)
