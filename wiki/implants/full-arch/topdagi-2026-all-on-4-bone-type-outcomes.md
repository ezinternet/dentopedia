## Three-line Summary

Retrospective multicenter cohort study of 250 edentulous patients (125 maxillary, 125 mandibular; 1,000 implants total) receiving All-on-4 implant-supported full-arch prostheses, stratified by Lekholm & Zarb bone types 1–4; mean follow-up 3.6 ± 0.4 years (September 2018–September 2021).

Overall implant survival 94.8%; Type 4 bone 89.1% vs Type 1 bone 98.3% (p < 0.05); peri-implant complications significantly more frequent in lower-density bone; prosthetic complications metal-acrylic 26.9% vs monolithic zirconia 8.9% (p < 0.001); patient satisfaction high across all bone types (VAS 8.7 ± 1.2).

Bone quality and prosthetic material selection are independent determinants of All-on-4 medium-term outcomes; Type 4 bone carries a measurably higher complication burden and warrants individualized treatment planning and closer follow-up.

## Summary

This retrospective multicenter cohort study evaluated 250 edentulous patients who received All-on-4 implant-supported full-arch prostheses at multiple centers between September 2018 and September 2021. Patients were stratified by bone type using the Lekholm & Zarb classification (Types 1–4), which categorizes jaw bone from dense cortical (Type 1) to sparse low-density trabecular (Type 4). One thousand implants were assessed over a mean follow-up of 3.6 ± 0.4 years.

The study's central finding is that bone quality independently predicts All-on-4 implant survival and complication rates, and that monolithic zirconia prostheses show substantially fewer mechanical complications than metal-acrylic alternatives.

## Key Contributions

- Bone-type-stratified All-on-4 survival data in a large cohort (n=1,000 implants): Type 4 bone yields 89.1% vs Type 1 bone 98.3% at medium term
- Direct comparison of metal-acrylic vs monolithic zirconia prosthetic complication rates in the same population: 26.9% vs 8.9% — a ~3× difference (p < 0.001)
- Patient satisfaction (VAS 8.7 ± 1.2) remains high even in challenging bone types despite higher complication burden
- ISQ and insertion torque confirm primary stability variation across bone types as the mechanistic link to outcomes

### Implant Survival by Bone Type

| Bone Type (Lekholm & Zarb) | Description | Implant Survival Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Type 1 | Dense homogeneous cortical bone | **98.3%** |
| Type 2 | Thick cortical layer, dense trabecular core | Not separately reported |
| Type 3 | Thin cortical layer, dense trabecular core | Not separately reported |
| Type 4 | Very thin cortical layer, low-density trabecular | **89.1%** |
| **Overall** | | **94.8%** |

- Type 4 significantly lower than Type 1 (p < 0.05)
- Peri-implantitis and screw loosening significantly more frequent in lower-density bone types (Type 3–4)
