## Three-line Summary

Single-centre retrospective cohort (21 fully edentulous patients, 137 implants placed 2003–2010, mean 10 years 6 months after loading) of fixed hybrid prostheses analysed by implant vertical angulation, bone-graft status and Osstell ISQ.
Marginal bone resorption fell as angulation rose (initial 1.21 → 0.87 → 0.33 mm; final 2.17 → 1.27 → 0.76 mm for <10°, 10–20°, >20°; P=0.005 and P=0.003), overall survival was 94.9% but success only 60.6%, and grafted sites lost more bone (2.36 vs 1.39 mm, P=0.003) with lower success (47.5% vs 71.0%, P=0.005).
Tilting for a fixed hybrid prosthesis did not harm the supporting implants — but with only 19 implants in the >20° group, uncorrected multiple comparisons and angle-sensitive 2D radiography, this is hypothesis-generating against the pooled SR+MA consensus, not a refutation of it.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 후향적 코호트 (완전 무치악 21명, 임플란트 137개, 2003–2010년 식립, 부하 후 평균 10년 6개월) — 고정 하이브리드 보철 (Fixed Hybrid Prosthesis)을 임플란트 수직 경사도·골이식 여부·오스텔 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)로 분석.

경사도가 커질수록 변연골흡수 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)가 감소 (초기 1.21 → 0.87 → 0.33 mm, 최종 2.17 → 1.27 → 0.76 mm; 각 10° 미만·10–20°·20° 초과; P=0.005·P=0.003), 전체 생존율 94.9%이나 성공률은 60.6%였고, 골이식 부위는 더 많은 골소실 (2.36 vs 1.39 mm, P=0.003)과 낮은 성공률 (47.5% vs 71.0%, P=0.005)을 보임.

의도적 경사식립은 지지 임플란트에 해롭지 않았으나, 20° 초과군 19개·다중비교 보정 부재·경사 의존적 2D 계측 때문에 통합 SR+MA와 상충하는 가설생성적 근거로 읽어야 함.

## Summary

This retrospective review asks whether the two features that made fixed hybrid prostheses suspect — deliberately tilted posterior fixtures and cantilevered one-piece frameworks — actually damage the supporting implants. Over a mean 10 years 6 months of loading in 21 patients and 137 implants, mean marginal bone resorption was 1.01 mm at one year and 1.80 mm at the last examination, survival was 94.9%, and the study's most quoted result is that implants with ≥20° vertical angulation lost roughly one-third the bone of implants under 10° (0.76 vs 2.17 mm, P=0.003) with 100% survival and 89.4% success. What the paper genuinely establishes is a *within-cohort association* under a specific treatment philosophy; what it only claims is causation, because angulation is confounded with site (tilted fixtures are the posterior ones placed into native basal bone), with graft status (grafting was used precisely where axial placement was possible), and with a 2D measurement method whose error plausibly scales with tilt. The bone-graft comparison is the sturdier signal — non-grafted sites lost ~1 mm less bone and succeeded 23.5 percentage points more often — and it is also the more actionable one: tilt to find bone rather than graft to allow an axial implant. Against the wiki's SR+MA evidence (axial MBL significantly lower than tilted, P<.0001), this study should be read as the strongest single counter-example available, not as an update of the pooled estimate.

## Key Contributions

- Individual-implant-level, decade-long data for fixed hybrid prostheses specifically — a prosthesis type long-term tilted-vs-axial literature usually replaces with All-on-4.
- Reports a monotonic inverse angulation–MBL relation (0.33/0.87/1.21 mm initial; 0.76/1.27/2.17 mm final across ≤10°, 10–20°, >20°) — the opposite direction to Del Fabbro 2022 SR+MA and to Szabó 2022's All-on-Four cohort.
- Shows the effect is local: implants *sharing the prosthesis* with tilted neighbours had unchanged MBL (2.16 mm and 1.65 mm), so no prosthesis-wide splinting effect is invoked.
- Quantifies the cost of grafting in this indication: final MBL 2.36 vs 1.39 mm (P=0.003) and success 47.5% vs 71.0% (P=0.005) for grafted vs non-grafted sites, with survival and complication rates statistically indistinguishable.
- Locates the ISQ prognostic signal in **secondary** rather than primary stability: secondary ISQ <60 → 100% complications (P=0.01) and 3.48 mm final MBL (P=0.04); primary ISQ was unrelated to every outcome.
- Illustrates how far "success" (60.6%) can sit below "survival" (94.9%) under strict ICOI/Pisa criteria, which makes cross-study success-rate comparison hazardous.

## Methodology

**Design**: single-centre retrospective cohort, surgery 2003–2010, minimum 7 years of follow-up after first prosthesis delivery; IRB B-2211-791-101 (consent waived).

**Sample**: 21 patients, 137 implants supporting full-mouth fixed hybrid prostheses. Systems: Ti-Unite (Nobel Biocare), Osstem US & TS II, AVANA II (Osstem), CMI (Neobiotech) — pooled, not compared. Diameters 3.3/3.75/4/5 mm = n 8/5/99/25; lengths 10/11.5/13/15 mm = n 9/62/59/7.

**Exclusions**: follow-up lost before delivery; implants failing before first delivery (early failure); implants not incorporated into the final framework.

**MBL measurement**: peri-apical radiographs (Heliodent Sirona) read in a PACS program (INFINIT). Shoulder-to-most-coronal-bone–implant-contact distance, mesial and distal, averaged per implant. Baseline = post-surgical radiograph; initial MBL = ~1-year radiograph vs baseline; final MBL = last radiograph vs baseline.

**Exposures**: (1) vertical angulation by ridge-line/fixture-axis angle — Group 1 ≤10° (n=88), Group 2 >10–<20° (n=30), Group 3 ≥20° (n=19); (2) bone graft vs none (n=57 / n=80), and simultaneous (n=56) vs delayed (n=5) within grafted sites; (3) Osstell ISQ ≥60 vs <60, primary (n=89/12) and secondary (n=93/5; 101 implants with records).

**Outcomes**: MBL; biological + prosthetic complications (overall 52.6%); survival and success per ICOI "Health Scale for Dental Implants" 2007.

**Statistics**: SPSS v25; one-way ANOVA (MBL), chi-square (complications/survival/success), Mann–Whitney where group sizes were very unequal. No multiplicity correction, no multivariable model, no a-priori power calculation, no time-to-event analysis.

## Results

**Overall**: initial MBL 1.01 ± 1.13 mm, final 1.80 ± 1.99 mm; complications 52.6%; survival 94.9%; success 60.6%.

| 10–20° | 30 | 0.87 ± 1.06 | 1.27 ± 1.25 | 60.0% | 100% | 73.3% |
| >20° | 19 | 0.33 ± 0.54 | 0.76 ± 0.80 | 52.6% | 100% | 89.4% |

| | Grafted (n=57) | Non-grafted (n=80) |
|---|---|---|

| Complications | 55.7% | 50.0% |
| Survival | 93.4% | 96.0% |

**Simultaneous vs delayed in grafted sites** — delayed numerically better on every endpoint (final MBL 1.58 vs 2.43 mm, complications 20.0% vs 58.9%, success 60.0% vs 46.4%) but n=5 and all P>0.05.

**ISQ**: secondary ISQ <60 (n=5) vs ≥60 → complications 100% vs 59.1% (P=0.01), final MBL 3.48 vs 1.77 mm (P=0.04), success 20.0% vs 62.3% (P=0.06). Primary ISQ <60 (n=12) showed no significant relation to any outcome (P=0.42/0.15/0.77/0.35/0.43).

**Implants sharing a prosthesis with tilted implants**: MBL unchanged (Group-2 prosthesis 1.11 → 2.16 mm; Group-3 prosthesis 0.99 → 1.65 mm), supporting a local rather than framework-level mechanism.

**Dimension subgroups** (descriptive only): 3.3 mm and 3.75 mm → 100% complication occurrence, 25.0% / 20.0% success (n=8 / 5); 10 mm length → 33.3% complications, 88.8% success (8/9); 15 mm → 100% complications, 85.7% survival (n=7).

## Limitations

**Authors' own**: retrospective; heterogeneous implant systems pooled; 2D peri-apical radiography without repositionable film holder, fixed technique or automated measurement; one-piece prosthesis prevents linking upper-structure failure to a specific implant.

**Additional, from this appraisal**:

- **The headline group is 19 implants**, the delayed-placement arm 5, secondary-ISQ <60 5. Three of the paper's most quotable claims rest on n≤19.
- **~A dozen subgroup comparisons at α=0.05 with no correction and no multivariable model**; angulation, graft status, ISQ, arch and implant position are mutually confounded and never adjusted.
- **Site confounding is structural**: ≥20° fixtures are the posterior ones deliberately tilted to avoid the sinus/nerve, i.e. in native basal bone, whereas the <10° group includes anterior and grafted sites. "Tilt loses less bone" and "native bone beats grafted bone" may be one association reported twice.
- **Angle-dependent measurement bias**: reading shoulder-to-bone distance on a tilted fixture in 2D is angle-sensitive, and no per-implant magnification calibration or beam-angulation correction is described — a mechanism that would exaggerate exactly the observed gradient.
- **Success and MBL are not independent**: the strict ICOI/Pisa radiographic criterion mechanically re-penalises the implants already flagged for higher MBL, so the 50.0 → 89.4% success gradient partly restates the MBL gradient.
- **No demographics or risk adjustment** (age, sex, smoking, diabetes, periodontal history, cantilever length, implants per arch, arch distribution), and implants are analysed as independent units although clustered within 21 patients.
- **Crude survival, no Kaplan–Meier/cox model**, no control arm, failure timing not reported.
- **Internal inconsistency**: Abstract/Results text give Group 1 initial MBL as 1.19 mm; Tables 2 and 4 give 1.21 ± 1.18 mm (Tables used here).
- **ISQ missing for 36/137 implants**, selection mechanism unstated.
- **Generalisability**: single Korean tertiary centre, 2003–2010 implant generations, metal-resin hybrid design — not contemporary monolithic zirconia full-arch protocols.
