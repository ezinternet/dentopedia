## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 91 IFCDs (498 implants, 72 patients, University Mainz 2003–2023), mean follow-up 6.8yr (range 0.5–17yr); Kaplan-Meier time-to-first-complication; Andersen-Gill Cox regression for recurrent complications; material comparison (resin-veneered vs ceramic-veneered, base-metal-ceramic vs titanium-resin).

IFCD survival 92.3%; 4.2% implant explantation; 49.5% complication-free; 169 total complications (recurrent included); RV vs CV prosthesis survival NS (p=0.85) but CV significantly better veneer fracture-free survival (p=0.0094); Ti-resin HR=4.25 total complications (p=0.0015) and HR=7.11 veneer fractures (p=0.0029) vs base-metal-alloy-ceramic.

Technical complications dominate the long-term; veneer fractures are the most frequent event; material choice (Ti-resin vs ceramic-veneered metal) is the strongest modifiable predictor of complication burden.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective cohort
- **Center**: University Medical Center Mainz, Germany
- **Period**: 2003–2023
- **N IFCDs**: 91
- **N implants**: 498
- **N patients**: 72
- **Follow-up**: Mean 6.8yr; range 0.5–17yr
- **Statistical analysis**: Kaplan-Meier (time-to-first complication); Andersen-Gill Cox regression (recurrent events); multivariable

## Key Results

### Survival

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| IFCD survival (cumulative) | **92.3%** |
| IFCDs failed | 7 |
| Implant explantation | **4.2%** (21/498) |

### Complication Profile

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Complication-free prostheses | **49.5%** |
| Total complications recorded | 169 (incl. recurrent) |
| Dominant complication type | Technical (veneer fractures) |
| Veneer fractures | 104 (61.5%) |
| Peri-implantitis | 30 (17.8%) |
| Explantations | 21 (12.4%) |

| Comparison | Outcome | Result |
|---|---|---|
| RV vs CV — prosthesis survival | — | **NS** (p=0.85) |
| RV vs CV — veneer fracture-free survival | — | **CV better** (p=0.0094) |

## Clinical Interpretation

The HR=4.25 for Ti-resin vs ceramic-veneered metal is the most actionable finding: Ti-resin prostheses carry a 4× greater long-term complication burden, driven primarily by a 7× higher veneer fracture rate. Despite equivalent overall prosthesis survival (both survive), the complication management burden is dramatically higher.

The 49.5% complication-free rate over a mean 6.8yr follow-up means that over a typical clinical lifetime, most IFCD patients will return with a complication — setting patient expectations and maintenance scheduling is essential.

Biological complications (peri-implantitis, implant loss) occurred less frequently than technical complications, consistent with the broader literature.

Anterior region veneer fractures were significantly more frequent than posterior (1.27±2.38 vs 0.33±0.86 per IFCD, p<0.001); maxillary RV showed higher fracture rate than mandibular (2.03±2.91 vs 0.91±1.83, p=0.004). No significant effect of opposing dentition, FP-Type, implant number, cantilever, implant system, connection, or prosthetic level.
