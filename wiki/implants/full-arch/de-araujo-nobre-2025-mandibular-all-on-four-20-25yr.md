## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 181 patients (724 implants, mean follow-up 264 months, range 240–321) undergoing immediate full-arch mandibular rehabilitation with the All-on-4 concept (2 axial anterior + 2 tilted posterior implants).

Implant and prosthesis CSR were 90% and 92% at up to 25 years; MBL was 1.71 ± 1.98 mm at 20 years and 2.28 ± 2.07 mm at 25 years; mechanical complications were recorded in 70.7% of patients.

The protocol is confirmed safe and reliable at 20–25 years; biological complications and restorative material type (implant failure), male sex (mechanical complications), and smoking (biological complications) are key risk indicators.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective cohort study, single-center (Portugal)
- **Population**: 181 patients (108 female, 73 male), mean age 59.2 ± 11.9 years; fully edentulous mandible
- **Intervention**: All-on-4 concept — 2 axial anterior implants + 2 distally tilted posterior implants per patient; all in immediate function
- **Sample**: 724 implants, 181 fixed full-arch prostheses
- **Follow-up**: Mean 264 months (range 240–321 months); 100 patients lost to follow-up; 77 reached 20-year FU, 3 reached 25-year FU
- **Primary outcomes**: Implant survival and success; prosthesis survival
- **Secondary outcomes**: MBL at 20-year and 25-year FU; mechanical and biological complications
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier (survival); Cox proportional hazards regression (implant failure risk indicators); binary logistic regression (MBL >3 mm, biological and mechanical complications)

## Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Implant cumulative survival rate (CSR) | **90%** at up to 25 years |
| Prosthesis survival | **92%** at up to 25 years |
| Mean MBL at 20 years | 1.71 ± 1.98 mm |
| Mean MBL at 25 years | 2.28 ± 2.07 mm |
| Mechanical complications (patient level) | 70.7% |

Identified by multivariable analysis (Cox regression for implant failure; binary logistic regression for others):

| Outcome | Risk Indicator |
|---|---|
| Implant failure | Biological complications (occurrence); type of restorative material |
| Mechanical complications | Male sex |
| Biological complications | Smoking; previous failure of a contiguous implant |

## Clinical Implications

The All-on-4 mandibular protocol demonstrates durable long-term outcomes over 20–25 years, with implant and prosthesis survival rates (90% and 92%) consistent with shorter follow-up benchmarks reported in systematic reviews. The mean MBL remains within clinically acceptable thresholds at both 20 years (1.71 mm) and 25 years (2.28 mm), indicating stable bone support over the long term.

However, the high incidence of mechanical complications (70.7% patient-level) underscores the need for structured long-term maintenance and prosthetic monitoring protocols. Clinicians should identify high-risk patients before treatment: smokers, patients with previous implant failure in adjacent sites, and patients at biological complication risk should receive enhanced follow-up. The association of restorative material type with implant failure warrants attention at material selection — though material types are not specified in the available abstract.

The extreme attrition (55% lost to follow-up) is a major limitation; the 25-year data rest on only 3 patients and should be interpreted cautiously. This is nonetheless the longest follow-up dataset for the All-on-4 mandibular protocol available in the literature.
