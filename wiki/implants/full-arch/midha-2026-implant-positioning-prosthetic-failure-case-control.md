## Three-line Summary

Retrospective matched case-control (30 prosthetic failure vs 60 matched controls), CBCT measurement of seven implant positioning parameters in full-arch fixed prostheses; prosthetic failure defined by screw loosening, framework fracture, or veneering fracture.

Failure cases had significantly greater mesiodistal angulation (18.4° vs 12.1°), buccolingual angulation (15.7° vs 10.3°), cantilever length (14.2 vs 10.4mm), C/AP ratio (1.42 vs 0.89), and vertical implant position (3.8 vs 2.9mm; all p<0.001); greater A-P spread and inter-implant distance were protective; complications: screw loosening 43.3%, framework fracture 36.7%, veneering fracture 20%.

Implant angulation and cantilever extension are the strongest failure-associated positioning parameters; greater A-P spread and inter-implant distance are protective — prosthetically driven CBCT-guided placement is the primary prevention strategy.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective matched case-control
- **Setting**: Prosthodontics department (Guru Nanak Dev Dental College, India)
- **Period**: January 2020–December 2024
- **N**: 90 (30 prosthetic failure cases, 60 matched controls)
- **Assessment**: CBCT records
- **Parameters**: 7 (mesiodistal angulation, buccolingual angulation, vertical position, A-P spread, cantilever length, C/AP ratio, inter-implant distance)

## Key Results

### Implant Positioning Parameters

| Parameter | Failure group | Control group | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mesiodistal angulation | **18.4±4.2°** | 12.1±3.8° | <0.001 |
| Buccolingual angulation | **15.7±5.1°** | 10.3±3.6° | <0.001 |
| Vertical implant position | **3.8±1.4 mm** | 2.9±0.9 mm | <0.001 |
| Distal cantilever length | **14.2±3.1 mm** | 10.4±2.6 mm | <0.001 |
| C/AP ratio | **1.42±0.31** | 0.89±0.22 | <0.001 |
| A-P spread | Larger = protective | — | — |
| Inter-implant distance | Larger = protective | — | — |

### Complication Types (30 failure cases)

| Complication | N | % |
|---|---|---|
| Screw loosening | 13 | **43.3%** |
| Framework fracture | 11 | **36.7%** |
| Veneering material fracture | 6 | 20.0% |
