## Three-line Summary

Retrospective middle-term study (>3yr) of 31 severely atrophic maxilla patients with hybrid full-arch rehabilitation using 62 extra-maxillary zygomatic + 90 standard implants; implant/prosthesis survival, success, ZICL, MSEE, and mucosal indices assessed.

100% implant survival and 100% prosthesis survival (no losses); implant success 98.4%; prosthesis success 87% (patient unit); 1 mucositis; 7 patients with resolved complications; ZICL1 in >80%; MSEE <4mm in 81%.

Bilateral zygomatic prosthetic configurations are an effective middle-term treatment for severely resorbed maxillae; companion data with Grandi 2025 (same group, 1yr) collectively spans 1yr → 3yr+ outcome confirmation.

## 세줄요약

후향적 연구, 31명 심한 위축 상악, 접합골 62개+표준 90개, 혼합형 전악수복 >3년 추적.

임플란트·보철 생존율 100%; 임플란트 성공 98.4%·보철 성공 87%(환자 단위); 점막염 1명; 합병증 7명 전원 해결; ZICL1 80% 이상; MSEE <4mm 81%.

중기 생존율 우수; 동일 연구진 1년 데이터(Grandi 2025)와 함께 1→3년+ 경과 확인.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective observational, single center
- **N patients**: 31 severely atrophic maxilla
- **N implants**: 152 (62 zygomatic + 90 standard)
- **Technique**: Extra-maxillary (extrasinus) zygomatic insertion
- **Follow-up**: >3 years
- **Outcomes**: Survival, success, mPLI, mBI, MSEE, ZICL, complications

## Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Implant survival | **100%** |
| Prosthesis survival | **100%** |
| Implant success (>3yr) | **98.4%** |
| Prosthesis success (patient unit, >3yr) | **87%** |
| Mucositis | 1 patient (zygomatic site) |
| Mechanical/biological complications | 7 patients — **all resolved** |
| Adequate oral hygiene | 80% |
| Absent/minor mucosal bleeding (mBI) | 77% |
| MSEE <4mm | 81% of sites |
| ZICL1 | >80% of cases |
