## Three-line Summary

Retrospective 1-year analysis of hybrid full-arch maxillary rehabilitation in 35 atrophic maxilla patients using 98 straight + 81 zygomatic implants (extrasinus approach) with angled abutments at 45°, 52.5°, and 60°; biological and prosthetic outcomes, ZICL, and mucosal indices assessed.

Implant survival 100%; prosthesis survival 94.3%; ZICL0-1 in 95% of zygomatic sites; sinus opacity in 7/35 patients (20%), sinus membrane perforation in 2/35 (5.7%); no significant differences among abutment angulation groups for any clinical index.

Hybrid zygomatic-conventional full-arch rehabilitation achieves excellent short-term implant survival regardless of abutment angulation; sinus-related biological complications warrant monitoring even with the extrasinus technique.

## 세줄요약

후향적 연구, 35명 상악 무치악, 수직 98개+접합골 81개, 경사 어버트먼트 45°/52.5°/60°; 혼합형 전악수복 1년 추적.

임플란트 생존율 100%; 보철 생존율 94.3%; ZICL0-1 95%; 부비동 불투명화 7명(20%), 막 천공 2명; 각도별 임상 지수 차이 없음.

단기 결과 양호; 각도 선택 자유; 부비동 합병증 모니터링 필요.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective observational, single center
- **N patients**: 35 edentulous maxilla
- **N implants**: 179 total (98 straight + 81 zygomatic)
- **Abutment angles**: 45° (n=21), 52.5° (n=23), 60° (n=37)
- **Surgical technique**: Extrasinus approach for zygomatic implants
- **Follow-up**: 1 year
- **Outcomes**: Implant/prosthesis survival, MSEE, mPLI, mBI, ZICL, complications

## Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Implant survival | **100%** |
| Prosthesis survival | **94.3%** |
| ZICL0-1 (zygomatic sites) | **95%** |
| Sinus opacity (postop) | 7/35 patients (20%) |
| Sinus membrane perforation | 2/35 patients (5.7%), unilateral |
| Abutment angle effect on clinical indices | **No significant difference** |
