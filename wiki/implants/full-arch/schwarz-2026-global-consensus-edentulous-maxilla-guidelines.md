## Three-line Summary

This S2k-level guideline developed 36 consensus-based recommendations mapped to a Core Outcome Set (COS) of 34 outcomes (10 PROs, 22 ClinROs, 2 subjective) through a three-round Delphi process with 105 experts from 26 countries. The guideline covers all care phases: patient selection, diagnostics, surgical/prosthetic treatment, complication management, and maintenance. It emphasizes shared decision-making, prosthetically driven planning, immediate protocols when feasible, and risk-based maintenance.

## Summary

The first Global Consensus for Clinical Guidelines in Implant Dentistry establishes a comprehensive framework for edentulous maxilla rehabilitation. Through an extensive multi-phase process involving 8 systematic reviews, 6 structured surveys, and a three-round Delphi consensus with 105 international experts, the guideline defines 36 recommendations covering the entire treatment continuum. A novel Core Outcome Set of 34 critically important outcomes integrates patient-reported outcomes with clinician-reported measures, ensuring that treatment decisions reflect both clinical effectiveness and patient lived experience.

## Key Contributions

- First global guideline integrating Core Outcome Sets for implant dentistry
- 36 consensus recommendations across all treatment phases
- COS of 34 outcomes (10 PROs, 22 ClinROs, 2 subjective)
- Clinical workflow checklist and decision trees
- 26-country international expert panel (105 delegates)

## Methodology

Multi-phase S2k-level guideline development: (1) 8 systematic reviews on ClinROs/PROs, (2) 6 structured surveys of clinicians/patients/cross-disciplinary experts, (3) Core Outcome Set via 3-round Delphi (105 experts), (4) nominal group technique for recommendation development, (5) anonymous plenary voting at consensus conference. Consensus threshold: ≥75% agreement.

## Results

- 36 consensus recommendations (all reached consensus)
- COS: 34 outcomes (10 PROs, 22 ClinROs, 2 subjective)
- Clinical workflow checklist + decision trees
- Covers: patient selection → diagnostics → treatment → complications → maintenance
- Emphasis: shared decision-making, prosthetically driven, risk-based maintenance
