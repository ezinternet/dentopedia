## Three-line Summary

Retrospective observational cohort of 151 screw-retained monolithic zirconia IFCDPs (117 patients, private practice, ≥12mo follow-up); CAD parameters—cantilever length (CL), distal cross-sectional connector area (CSCA), screw access opening length (SAOL)—measured pre-milling; designs with CL/CSCA≥0.51 or CL/SAOL≥1.48 were modified before milling.

Zero type II fractures (cantilever fractures); 3.97% overall prosthetic failure rate (6/151: 5 type I mid-span fractures, 1 implant failure); 4 implant failures total.

When CL/CSCA<0.51 and CL/SAOL<1.48 are respected during digital design, cantilever fractures do not occur; mid-span type I fractures require additional design parameters beyond cantilever geometry.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective observational cohort
- **Setting**: Private practice (Cuneo, Italy)
- **Period**: October 2021–May 2023
- **N**: 151 prostheses, 117 patients
- **Follow-up**: ≥12 months post occlusal loading
- **CAD parameters**: CL, distal CSCA, SAOL — measured pre-milling on digital wax-up
- **Cut-off application**: Designs exceeding cut-offs modified before milling
- **Primary outcome**: Type II fractures (distal cantilever fractures)

## Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Type II fractures (cantilever) | **0** |
| Type I fractures (mid-span) | 5 (3.31%) |
| Implant failures | 4 total (2 during temp, 2 post-delivery) |
| Total prosthetic failures | 6 (3.97%) |

### CAD Design Cut-Off Values

| Parameter | Definition | Cut-off |
|---|---|---|
| **CL/CSCA** | Cantilever length / distal cross-sectional connector area | **< 0.51** |
| **CL/SAOL** | Cantilever length / screw access opening length | **< 1.48** |
