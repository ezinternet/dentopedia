## Three-line Summary

PRISMA-2020 systematic review (PROSPERO CRD420261352454) including 20 primary studies (11 All-on-Four, 9 FDP4+) with up to 20-year follow-up, comparing implant survival, prosthesis survival, MBL, and complications; Newcastle–Ottawa Scale quality assessment; narrative synthesis due to heterogeneity.

Prosthesis survival comparable (All-on-Four 85.0–100% vs FDP4+ 88.2–100%); implant survival All-on-Four 89.7–97.9% (10yr) vs FDP4+ 98.9–99.0%; MBL at 5yr All-on-Four up to 1.9 mm vs FDP4+ up to 2.9 mm; metal-ceramic prostheses show lower fracture (7.27–18.2%) than metal-acrylic (7.3–36.7%).

All-on-Four represents a predictable option for edentulous patients avoiding grafting; outcomes comparable to FDP4+ without significant statistical differences; evidence quality limited by predominantly retrospective designs and heterogeneous protocols.

## Summary

This PRISMA-2020-compliant systematic review (PROSPERO CRD420261352454) addresses a focused PICO question: in adult edentulous patients, how does the All-on-Four concept compare with fixed implant-supported prostheses on more than four implants (FDP4+) in terms of implant survival, prosthesis survival, marginal bone loss (MBL), and biological and technical complications? The search covered MEDLINE/PubMed and EMBASE from January 2000 to January 2026. After dual-reviewer screening, 20 primary clinical studies (minimum 4-year follow-up) were included: 11 All-on-Four studies (PIO) and 9 alternative-concept studies (PCO). Methodological quality was assessed with the Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (NOS); overall quality was classified as low-moderate, predominantly due to retrospective designs. A planned meta-analysis was abandoned in favor of narrative synthesis after substantial heterogeneity in prosthetic materials, loading protocols, and outcome definitions was identified.

## Key Results

### Implant Survival

| Concept | 5-year | 10-year | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| All-on-Four (immediate placement) | 94.5–99.6% | 89.7–97.9% | Most studies immediate loading |
| FDP4+ (delayed) | 89.7–95.1% | — | |
| FDP4+ (immediate) | 94.4–96.1% | — | |
| FDP4+ (long-term studies) | — | 98.9–99.0% | 20-year data |

Long-term comparison: All-on-Four 90% at 25yr vs FDP4+ 98.9% at 20yr — numerically higher FDP4+, no formal statistical comparison across studies.

### Prosthesis Survival

| Concept | Range |
|---|---|
| All-on-Four | 85.0–100% |
| FDP4+ | 88.2–100% |

PMMA provisional prostheses: 9.1–81.9% mechanical complications range; 100% survival in FDP4+ provisionals.

### Marginal Bone Loss (MBL)

| Concept | 5-year | 7–10 year | Long-term |
|---|---|---|---|
| All-on-Four | 1.18–1.9 mm | 1.3–2.5 mm | 2.23 mm (15yr, Maló) |
| FDP4+ | 1.18–1.60 mm (reported max 2.9 mm) | — | 1.4 mm (14yr) |

Axial vs tilted: axial 1.0–1.34 mm, tilted 1.10–1.27 mm at 5yr — absolute difference ≤0.07 mm (not clinically meaningful). All-on-Six vs All-on-Four: 0.15 vs 0.24 mm (Toia) and 1.20 vs 1.56 mm (La Monaca) — no statistically significant differences.

### Prosthesis Material and Fracture Rates

| Material | Fracture Rate | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Metal–PMMA (acrylic) | 7.3–36.7% | 7–11 years |
| Metal–ceramic | 7.27–18.20% | variable |
| Metal–zirconia | 5.36–29.7% | variable |
| Zirconia (full) | 0% (single study) | — |

Metal-ceramic produces lower fracture rates than metal-acrylic across comparable follow-up periods. Opposing-arch risk: metal-ceramic implant prosthesis in opposing arch → mechanical complication OR 2.04, crown chipping OR 1.97 (Maló); prefer zirconia crowns in this scenario.

### Biological Complications

- Peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis: prevalent but inconsistently defined and reported
- Implant infections: 0 – 203 cases across included studies (highest: Maló 2019, 203/1072 patients)
- Gingival inflammation: 0 – 189 cases (highest: Hopp 2017, 189 cases)
- Hopp et al. suggested higher peri-implant mucositis prevalence around tilted implants; interpretation limited by diagnostic variability
- No pooled complication rate calculable due to heterogeneity

## Patient Selection

**Bone requirements**: Horizontal width >4 mm, vertical height >8 mm in anterior region to first/second premolar.

**Indications**: Completely edentulous or terminal dentition; insufficient posterior bone without grafting; patient preference for fixed restoration; good general health and oral hygiene compliance.

**Contraindications**: Heavy smoking (>15 cigarettes/day), pregnancy/lactation, poor compliance, insufficient bone, metabolic bone disease, bisphosphonate therapy, head/neck radiotherapy/chemotherapy.

**Risk factors for complications**:
- Uncontrolled diabetes: OR 1.65 (implant failure)
- Smoking: OR 1.65–3.03
- Bruxism: OR 2.71–8.70 (technical complications)
- Periodontitis: established risk factor

## Clinical Workflow Recommendations

1. **Surgical torque**: Minimum 30 Ncm (most studies); AWMF S3 guideline recommends 30–50 Ncm before immediate loading.
2. **Implant angulation**: Distal implants 30–45° tilt; no clinically meaningful difference in outcomes vs axial.
3. **Provisional phase**: PMMA-based provisional, immediate or same-day delivery.
4. **Definitive prosthesis**: Metal-ceramic or metal-zirconia preferred over metal-acrylic (lower fracture). Staged approach (PMMA → zirconia-ceramic) is a clinically reliable strategy.
5. **Opposing arch**: If metal-ceramic implant prosthesis is present, prefer zirconia crowns (OR reduction for chipping 1.97).
6. **Bruxism management**: Protective occlusal splint recommended.
7. **Recall**: Individualized intervals 3–12 months (AWMF S3); fixed prosthesis requires specific hygiene instruction.

## Limitations

- Predominantly retrospective observational studies; no RCTs meeting inclusion criteria
- NOS overall quality: low-moderate
- Substantial heterogeneity in outcome definitions, materials, loading protocols, follow-up durations
- Meta-analysis not feasible; all results are descriptive ranges, not pooled estimates
- Publication bias cannot be excluded
- High-risk patient groups (radiotherapy, antiresorptives) excluded from most studies — limits generalizability
- AWMF S3 cited throughout but not directly synthesized (used as contextual reference)
