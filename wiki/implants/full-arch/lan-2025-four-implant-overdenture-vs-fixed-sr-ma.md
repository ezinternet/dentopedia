## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 16 studies (5,568 implants) comparing 4-implant-supported overdentures (IODs) vs full-arch fixed restorations (IFRs) in maxillary edentulous patients with ≥5yr follow-up; PubMed, Embase, Cochrane; RoB2 and NOS; primary outcome implant survival rate (ISR).

Weighted ISR: IODs 94.5% (CI 92.1–96.9%, I²=84%) vs IFRs 98.5% (CI 97.4–99.5%, I²=78%); prosthesis survival 85.0% (IOD) vs 99.9% (IFR); MBL at 5yr −0.27mm (IOD) vs −1.20mm (IFR); most common complications: retention loss 0.34/patient (IOD), acrylic tooth fracture/dislodgment 0.09/patient (IFR).

IFRs achieve higher implant and prosthesis survival; IODs preserve more marginal bone at 5yr with higher complication frequency; both provide predictable medium-term outcomes with distinct risk profiles.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA, 16편, 5,568 임플란트, 상악 4임플란트 IOD vs IFR, ≥5년 추적.

ISR: IOD 94.5% vs IFR 98.5%; 보철 생존 85.0% vs 99.9%; 5년 MBL −0.27 vs −1.20mm; 주요 합병증: 고정장치 탈락(IOD)·아크릴 골절(IFR).

IFR 생존율 우수; IOD MBL 보존 유리; 둘 다 중기 예측 가능.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Systematic review and meta-analysis
- **Databases**: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane + manual search
- **Inclusion**: ≥10 maxillary edentulous patients, IOD or IFR, ≥5yr follow-up
- **N studies**: 16 (9 IOD, 7 IFR)
- **N implants**: 5,568
- **Bias tools**: RoB2 (RCTs), Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (observational)
- **Primary outcome**: Implant survival rate

## Key Results

### Implant and Prosthesis Survival

| Outcome | IOD (overdenture) | IFR (fixed) |
|---|---|---|
| Weighted ISR | **94.5%** | **98.5%** |
| 95% CI | 92.1–96.9% | 97.4–99.5% |
| I² | 84.22% | 77.88% |
| Prosthesis survival | **85.0%** | **99.9%** |

### Marginal Bone Loss at 5yr

| Measure | IOD | IFR |
|---|---|---|
| MBL | −0.27 ± 1.31 mm | −1.20 ± 0.76 mm |

### Most Common Complications

| Type | Complication | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| IOD | Retention loss | 0.34/patient |
| IFR | Acrylic tooth dislodgment/fracture | 0.09/patient |
