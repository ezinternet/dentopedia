## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 12 studies (11 in MA) comparing metal-zirconia vs metal-acrylic full-arch implant-supported screw-retained prostheses; search 2000–2025; ROBINS-I bias and GRADE certainty evaluation.

Metal-zirconia showed significantly lower prosthetic wear (RR=0.29; 95% CI 0.14–0.59), reduced peri-implantitis (RR=0.67; 95% CI 0.47–0.96), and favorable MBL (SMD=0.36); no significant differences in implant survival, framework fractures, esthetics, or speech; most studies had moderate-to-critical risk of bias.

Metal-zirconia has a superior biological and wear profile over metal-acrylic; well-designed RCTs are needed to confirm survival advantages given the predominantly non-randomized evidence base.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (ROBINS-I, GRADE)
- **Databases**: CENTRAL, PubMed, Embase, Epistemonikos
- **Publication range**: 2000–2025
- **Inclusion**: Non-randomized clinical studies, ≥1yr follow-up, full-arch screw-retained, metal-zirconia vs metal-acrylic comparison
- **Exclusion**: Fully monolithic zirconia, partial FDP, non-full-arch
- **N studies included**: 12 (11 in meta-analysis)

### Evidence Certainty

- Most studies: moderate to critical risk of bias (ROBINS-I) — predominantly confounding
- GRADE certainty: not specified in abstract; expected low-to-moderate given non-randomized evidence

## Clinical Interpretation

Metal-zirconia superstructure (metallic titanium/zirconia substructure + monolithic zirconia) outperforms metal-acrylic on the two most clinically troublesome outcomes: prosthetic wear and biological complications (peri-implantitis, MBL). The 71% reduction in wear risk and 33% reduction in peri-implantitis risk are clinically meaningful, even with high RoB.

Framework fracture equivalence suggests the metallic substructure absorbs structural risk regardless of the ceramic superstructure material.

Practical trade-off: metal-acrylic retains advantages in cost, chair-side repairability, and individual tooth replacement — see [[wiki/implants/full-arch/krennmair-2025-metal-resin-zirconia-molars-5yr]] for a hybrid modification that captures both.
