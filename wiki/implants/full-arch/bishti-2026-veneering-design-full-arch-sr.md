## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 12 prospective studies on all-ceramic implant-supported FDPs (3-4-unit and full-arch) comparing monolithic, micro-veneered, and fully veneered designs; 3Y-TZP zirconia >1000 MPa only; no language or date restrictions.

3-year success rate 89.36% (3-4-unit) and 75.56% (full-arch); 3-year survival 98.64% and 96.45%; annual chipping rate 2.1% (3-4-unit) and 6.9% (full-arch); monolithic/micro-veneered showed significantly lower chipping than fully veneered in 3-4-unit spans (p<0.0001); full-arch design comparison not feasible due to insufficient data.

Monolithic/micro-veneered preferred for short-span i-FDPs; full-arch all-ceramic requires caution with high complication rates and limited comparative evidence; conclusions are restricted to high-strength 3Y-TZP zirconia.

## 세줄요약

SR, 12편, 전향적, all-ceramic i-FDP(3-4유닛·전악), 단일체·마이크로·전체 베니어 비교; 3Y-TZP >1000 MPa 한정.

3년 성공률 89.36%/75.56%, 생존율 98.64%/96.45%; 연간 칩핑율 2.1%(3-4유닛)/6.9%(전악); 단일체·마이크로 < 전체 베니어 칩핑(p<0.0001); 전악 비교 불가.

전악 all-ceramic은 높은 합병증 주의; 단일체/마이크로 권장; 3Y-TZP 한정.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Systematic review (with meta-analysis for selected outcomes)
- **Databases**: Electronic search (databases not specified in abstract)
- **Inclusion**: Prospective clinical studies, ≥1yr follow-up, all-ceramic i-FDP, ≥1 inter-implant pontic
- **Population**: Partially/completely edentulous
- **N studies included**: 12
- **Material scope**: 3Y-TZP zirconia >1000 MPa only

## Key Results

### Survival and Success Rates

| Outcome | 3-4-unit i-FDP | Full-arch i-FDP |
|---|---|---|
| **3-year success rate** | **89.36%** | **75.56%** |
| **3-year survival rate** | **98.64%** | **96.45%** |
| **Annual chipping rate** | **2.1%** | **6.9%** |

### Veneering Design — Chipping Comparison

| Comparison | Result |
|---|---|
| Monolithic vs fully veneered (3-4-unit) | Monolithic significantly lower chipping (p<0.0001) |
| Micro-veneered vs fully veneered (3-4-unit) | Micro-veneered significantly lower chipping (p<0.0001) |
| Full-arch design comparison | **Not feasible — insufficient data** |

### Scope Limitations

- Findings apply **only to high-strength 3Y-TZP zirconia >1000 MPa**
- No conclusions for multilayer zirconia, lithium disilicate, or other ceramics

## Clinical Interpretation

The 6.9%/year chipping rate for full-arch all-ceramic prostheses is a clinically significant complication burden. Over 5 years, cumulative chipping risk approaches 30%. This underscores the importance of:

1. Adequate connector cross-sectional area (CSSA) — see [[implants/full-arch/papaspyridakos-2026-zirconia-full-arch-140-jaws-8yr]] for CSSA thresholds
2. Prosthetic space height management
3. Bruxism screening before material selection — see [[implants/full-arch/acar-2026-full-arch-bruxism-complications-4-10yr]]

The success rate gap (75.56% vs 89.36%) between full-arch and shorter-span restorations reflects the cumulative complication burden over the longer span and cantilever stress.
