## Three-line Summary

Case report with design history (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2000) on a hydroxylapatite-coated mandibular subperiosteal implant.

Apparent bone growth was observed after placement, with revision for acute infection at a permucosal site.

One case; the growth is an observation, and permucosal infection is a documented complication.

## Summary

Fish and Misch describe the subperiosteal implant's design history and one case in which bone appeared to grow around a hydroxylapatite-coated framework. The case is a historical record, not a basis for clinical expectations.

## Key Contributions

- Design history of the tripodal mandibular subperiosteal implant.
- Single case of apparent bone growth with HA coating.

## Methodology

Case report with narrative review.

## Results

One case; apparent bone growth; revision for infection.
