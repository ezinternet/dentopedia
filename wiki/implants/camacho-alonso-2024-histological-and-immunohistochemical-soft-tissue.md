## Three-line Summary

Multicenter split-mouth double-blind RCT, 74 implants in 37 patients (cylindrical vs concave 3-mm abutments), 12-week healing, four Spanish universities; histology, immunohistochemistry and FESEM of retrieved abutments.

Concave abutments showed greater total soft-tissue height (3.57 ± 0.28 vs 2.95 ± 0.27 mm, p<0.001) and barrier-epithelium extension (2.46 ± 0.17 vs 1.89 ± 0.21 mm, p<0.001), more transverse/perpendicular collagen fibers (23.76 ± 5.86 vs 15.68 ± 4.57%, p<0.001), and lower inflammatory/vascular intensity (1.05 ± 0.78 vs 1.97 ± 0.68, p<0.001).

Concave geometry extends the barrier epithelium without increasing abutment height and produces a better-oriented, less inflamed soft-tissue seal in thick phenotypes — but 3-month, 2D, single-design evidence cannot be extrapolated to thin tissue or long-term peri-implant health.

## 세줄요약

다기관 분할구강 이중맹검 무작위대조시험(RCT), 환자 37명·임플란트 74개(원통형 vs 오목형 3 mm 지대주), 12주 치유, 스페인 4개 대학; 조직학·면역조직화학(IHC) 및 회수 지대주 주사전자현미경(FESEM) 분석.

오목형 군에서 연조직 총 높이(3.57±0.28 vs 2.95±0.27 mm, p<0.001)와 장벽상피 연장(2.46±0.17 vs 1.89±0.21 mm, p<0.001)이 유의하게 컸고, 교원섬유의 횡단/수직 배열 비율(23.76±5.86 vs 15.68±4.57%, p<0.001)이 높았으며 염증·혈관 강도(1.05±0.78 vs 1.97±0.68, p<0.001)는 유의하게 낮음.

오목형 형태는 지대주 높이를 늘리지 않고도 장벽상피를 연장해 두꺼운 점막 표현형에서 더 잘 정렬되고 염증이 적은 연조직 봉쇄를 만든다 — 다만 3개월·2차원·단일 디자인 근거라 얇은 점막이나 장기 예후로 확대 해석할 수 없다.

## Summary

This multicenter split-mouth double-blind RCT (37 patients, 74 implants, four Spanish universities) compared 3-mm cylindrical (straight) vs concave (slim) abutments on identical platform-switched, conical-connection bone-level implants, with peri-implant soft tissue harvested at 12 weeks for histometry, polarized-light collagen analysis, immunohistochemistry (CD3/CD79a/CD68/MPO/CD31), and FESEM of the retrieved abutments. Concave abutments produced a significantly taller soft-tissue seal (3.57 vs 2.95 mm) and a longer barrier epithelium (2.46 vs 1.89 mm, both p<0.001), while the supracrestal connective-tissue extension was only marginally larger (1.11 vs 1.03 mm, p=0.069). Collagen fibers were more often oriented transverse/perpendicular to the abutment surface in the concave group (23.76% vs 15.68%), and the inflammatory/vascular score was nearly halved (1.05 vs 1.97), with more tissue remnants adhering to the concave surface (45.12% vs 42.47%). The authors interpret concave macrogeometry as increasing the apico-coronal length of the transmucosal surface without increasing abutment height, lengthening the epithelial seal and producing a mechanically favorable, less inflamed connective-tissue organization — a plausible histologic substrate for the reduced marginal bone loss previously reported with narrow/concave abutments.

## Key Contributions

- Human **histological + immunohistochemical** evidence, rather than radiographic bone alone, that abutment macrogeometry changes the peri-implant soft-tissue seal.
- The greater tissue height around concave abutments is attributable to **barrier-epithelium extension** (p<0.001), not a significant connective-tissue gain (p=0.069).
- More transverse/perpendicular collagen (23.76% vs 15.68%, p<0.001) plus lower CD3/CD79a-driven inflammatory intensity (1.05 vs 1.97, p<0.001) supports a functional, less inflamed seal.
- FESEM quantification of retrieved abutments: more tissue remnants on concave surfaces (p=0.040) but no difference in biofilm coverage or clean titanium area.

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Multicenter split-mouth, double-blind RCT, 2 parallel arms (NCT03888339, CONSORT) |
| Setting | Universities of Murcia, Valencia, Salamanca, Santiago de Compostela (Spain), 2019–2022 |
| n | 37 patients / 74 implants / 74 biopsies (26 men, 11 women; mean 64.38 ± 13.72 y) |
| Implant | Galimplant IPX bone-level, internal conical connection, platform switching, equicrestal, 4–5 × 8–14 mm |
| Arms | 3-mm anodized machined titanium abutments: rotational aesthetic straight (cylindrical) vs rotational slim (concave); identical height/connection/surface; inserted at surgery, 30 Ncm |
| Phenotype | Thick tissue only — baseline mucosa 3.79 ± 1.25 mm; ≥3 mm supracrestal soft tissue, ≥2 mm keratinized mucosa |
| Biopsy | 12 weeks: 6-mm punch, 1.1–1.7 mm tissue collar removed with abutment |
| Assays | HE + Masson-Goldner histometry; IHC (CD3, CD79a, CD68, MPO, CD31, 0–3 scale); polarized-light collagen orientation; FESEM surface analysis |
| Statistics | SPSS 20.0, paired t-test, chi-square; p≤0.05; patient + statistician blinded, examiner not blinded |

| Barrier epithelium (PM–aJE), mm | 1.89 ± 0.21 | 2.46 ± 0.17 | <0.001 |

| Transverse/perpendicular collagen, % | 15.68 ± 4.57 | 23.76 ± 5.86 | <0.001 |

| Biofilm / clean titanium, % | 2.40 / 55.17 | 1.91 / 52.35 | NS |

- Inflammation and vascular proliferation were concentrated in the lower barrier epithelium/supracrestal connective zone for **both** shapes.
- All implants satisfied Misch success criteria; no failures.

### Interpretation boundaries

The 0.62 mm taller seal is an epithelial phenomenon in a **thick-phenotype, periodontally healthy, platform-switched conical implant** sample. The study did not measure marginal bone, stability, or patient outcomes at the 12-week biopsy, and its 2D sections cannot resolve 3D seal topology — so it explains *why* concave abutments might protect bone but does not itself demonstrate long-term benefit.
