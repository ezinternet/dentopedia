## Three-line Summary

Multicenter 5-year RCT: 141 subjects with anterior maxillary single implants in healed ridges and immediate provisionalization, randomized to conical (48), flat-to-flat (49), or platform-switched (44) interfaces; 33/28/27 evaluable at 5 years.

Marginal bone change −0.16 ± 0.45 (conical) vs −0.92 ± 0.70 (flat-to-flat) vs −0.81 ± 1.06 mm (PS), P<.0005; mucosal zenith change minimal in all (−0.10 to +0.08 mm, P>.60); BoP 16–19%, no difference; 6 PS and 8 flat-to-flat early failures.

Conical interface preserved bone better, but the facial mucosal zenith stayed stable with every interface over 5 years.

## 세줄요약

다기관 5년 RCT: 상악 전치부 단일 임플란트·즉시 임시수복 141명, 원뿔 (48)·평면 (49)·플랫폼 스위칭 (44) 연결부 무작위; 5년 평가 33/28/27.

변연골 변화 원뿔 −0.16 vs 평면 −0.92 vs PS −0.81 mm (P<.0005); 점막 정점 변화 전 군 미미 (−0.10~+0.08 mm, P>.60); BoP 16–19% 무차이.

원뿔 연결이 골 보존 우위, 그러나 협측 점막 정점은 연결부와 무관하게 5년간 안정.

## Summary

Three interface designs, one esthetic-zone protocol (healed ridge, immediate provisional), five years. Bone told a clear story: the conical interface lost about 0.16 mm versus roughly 0.8–0.9 mm for flat-to-flat and platform-switched designs. Soft tissue did not follow: the facial mucosal zenith moved by about a tenth of a millimetre in any group, papillae grew similarly, and bleeding was comparable.

For zenith questions this is a useful counterweight to Farronato 2020, which read a 0.6 mm difference in mucosal height at 1 year as connection-driven. Here, with longer follow-up and three arms, interface design changed bone but not zenith position. Caveat: attrition was ~38%, and the interfaces come with different implant systems.

## Key Contributions

- 5-year, three-arm RCT on interface design with zenith as an outcome.
- Dissociation: bone differs by interface, zenith does not.

## Methodology

- 141 subjects; healed ridges; immediate provisionalization; anterior maxilla.
- Clinical, photographic, radiographic outcomes at 6 months then yearly to 5 years.

## Results

| Interface | n placed → 5 y | MBL change (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| Conical | 48 → 33 | −0.16 ± 0.45 |
| Flat-to-flat | 49 → 28 | −0.92 ± 0.70 |
| Platform-switched | 44 → 27 | −0.81 ± 1.06 |

- P<.0005 for MBL; zenith change P>.60; BoP P>.81.
