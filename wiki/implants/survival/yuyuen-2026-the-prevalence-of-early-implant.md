## Three-line Summary

Retrospective nested case-control study at a single Thai university dental hospital: 7,368 implants placed 2016-2023, 114 implants (96 patients) failed before final restoration, compared with 192 randomly selected control patients (1:2).

Early failure prevalence was 1.55%; in multivariable logistic regression, implant diameter under 3.5 mm (OR 6.21, 95% CI 1.88-20.55, p=0.003) and length under 10 mm (OR 2.06, 95% CI 1.12-3.82, p=0.022) were the only significant factors, while untreated periodontitis (OR 2.12, 95% CI 1.07-4.24, p=0.033) was significant only in univariate analysis.

Patient factors (sex, age, diabetes, smoking) were not significant, but missing HbA1c and incomplete smoking data limit these null findings; the single-center retrospective design and small number of cases limit power.

## 세줄요약

태국 단일 대학병원의 후향적 중첩 환자-대조군 연구 (nested case-control): 2016-2023년 식립한 임플란트 7,368개 중 최종 보철 전 114개 (환자 96명)가 실패했고, 무작위 선정한 대조군 환자 192명 (1:2)과 비교했다.

조기 실패 유병률은 1.55%였고, 다변량 로지스틱 회귀에서 직경 3.5 mm 미만 (OR 6.21, 95% CI 1.88-20.55, p=0.003)과 길이 10 mm 미만 (OR 2.06, 95% CI 1.12-3.82, p=0.022)만 유의했으며, 치료받지 않은 치주염 (OR 2.12, 95% CI 1.07-4.24, p=0.033)은 단변량에서만 유의했다.

환자 요인 (성별, 연령, 당뇨, 흡연)은 유의하지 않았으나 당화혈색소 (HbA1c) 기록 누락과 불완전한 흡연력으로 이 음성 결과의 해석에는 한계가 있고, 단일기관 후향 설계와 적은 사례 수로 검정력도 제한적이다.

## Summary

This retrospective nested case-control study analysed hospital records from Maha Chakri Sirindhorn Dental Hospital (Mahidol University, Thailand). Of 7,368 implants placed between 2016 and 2023, 114 were removed before the final restoration (early failure prevalence 1.55%), affecting 96 patients. All failure patients were included as cases; 192 control patients were randomly selected at a 1:2 ratio. When a patient had several failures, one implant was randomly chosen. Implants lost at insertion for no primary stability and implants removed after osseointegration for improper positioning were excluded.

None of the patient factors (sex, age, DM, osteoporosis, antiresorptive drugs, smoking) differed significantly between groups. Among failure patients, 43 had a periodontitis history (20 untreated, 23 treated) and 53 did not. Untreated periodontitis carried a 2.12-fold higher risk than no history (p=0.033; 95% CI 1.07-4.24), whereas treated periodontitis did not (p=0.909). Univariate analysis also linked implant diameter, location, platform level (radiographic) and covering material (cover screw) to early failure, and length showed a trend (p=0.052).

In the multivariable model (DM, periodontitis history, diameter, length, location, platform level, covering material, placement timing), only diameter under 3.5 mm (OR 6.21, 95% CI 1.88-20.55, p=0.003) and length under 10 mm (OR 2.06, 95% CI 1.12-3.82, p=0.022) remained significant. DM showed a non-significant trend (p=0.081); smoking could not be assessed reliably because records lacked current vs former status.

## Key Contributions

- Thai single-center estimate of early implant failure prevalence (1.55%), close to the 1.5% the authors cite from earlier work.
- Narrow (under 3.5 mm) and short (under 10 mm) implants independently associated with early failure in a multivariable model.
- Untreated, but not treated, periodontitis associated with early failure in univariate analysis, which was not retained after adjustment.

## Methodology

- Retrospective nested case-control design; cases = patients with at least one implant removed before final restoration; controls = patients without failure, 1:2 random selection; one implant per patient analysed.
- Early failure defined by timing relative to final restoration (no fixed day window given). Exclusions: no primary stability at insertion; removal after osseointegration for malposition.
- Univariable tests (t-test, Mann-Whitney U, chi-square, Fisher's exact); variables with p<0.10 entered into multiple binary logistic regression; adjusted OR with 95% CI; SPSS 28.
- Candidate factors: patient (sex, age, DM, osteoporosis, antiresorptives, smoking), tooth (periodontal history treated vs untreated), implant (diameter, length, location, platform level, covering material, timing, GBR or sinus lift history, antibiotics, operator experience and others).

## Results

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Implants placed / early failures | 7,368 / 114 (1.55%) |
| Patients with failures / controls | 96 / 192 |
| Untreated periodontitis vs none | 2.12-fold, p=0.033, 95% CI 1.07-4.24 (univariate) |
| Treated periodontitis vs none | p=0.909 |
| Diameter under 3.5 mm (multivariable) | OR 6.21, 95% CI 1.88-20.55, p=0.003 |
| Length under 10 mm (multivariable) | OR 2.06, 95% CI 1.12-3.82, p=0.022 |
| Diabetes mellitus | trend, p=0.081 |
| Implant length (univariate) | p=0.052 |

- Reasons for tooth loss at failed sites: fracture 34.7%, caries 28.6%, periodontitis 12.2%.
- Anterior mandible (vs posterior), crestal platform level and cover-screw use were associated with failure in univariate analysis only; early (vs immediate) placement trended higher, not significant after adjustment.

## Limitations

- Retrospective single-center chart review; missing HbA1c, incomplete smoking history (no current vs former status, quantity or duration), illegible or incomplete records, and missing radiographs for platform-level assessment.
- Few cases limited power (few edentulous and osteoporosis patients); the diameter OR has a very wide CI (1.88-20.55).
- No fixed time window for early failure; narrow and short implants are chosen in compromised sites, so confounding by indication is likely and not excluded by the design.
- Text inconsistencies: sample size paragraph wording (1.5% prior, 6,307 required, "2% +/- 0.3%"); chart codes vs implant numbers as identifiers; year-matched vs simple random controls; tooth-loss cause percentages imply a denominator of 49. Tables 1-4 were not in the retrieved text.

## Clinical Implication (Korean Practice)

임플란트 직경 3.5 mm 미만이나 길이 10 mm 미만을 선택할 때는 조기 실패 위험이 높아질 수 있음을 환자에게 설명하고, 가능하면 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) 등으로 적정 직경·길이를 확보하는 계획을 고려한다. 다만 이 연구는 후향적 단일기관 자료로 좁고 짧은 임플란트가 선택되는 부위의 교란을 배제하지 못하며, 흡연·당뇨의 음성 결과는 기록 누락 때문에 영향이 없다는 증거로 읽으면 안 된다.
