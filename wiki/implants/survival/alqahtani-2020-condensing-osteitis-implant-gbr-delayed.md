## Three-line Summary

A mandibular right second premolar (No. 45) with a large periapical radiopaque mass was diagnosed as condensing osteitis (골화성 골염, Condensing Osteitis, CO) based on bone density and trabeculation pattern. Atraumatic extraction was followed immediately by guided bone regeneration (골유도재생술, Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR), and a dental implant was placed 6 months later. This case report establishes GBR + delayed implant placement as a viable staged protocol for large CO sites.

## Summary

Alqahtani (2020) reports a case of large condensing osteitis at the mandibular right second premolar, managed with atraumatic extraction followed immediately by GBR, then implant placement after 6 months of healing. The paper demonstrates that staged management — respecting the healing biology rather than immediately placing an implant into sclerotic bone — yields a successful implant outcome.

## Key Contributions

- First PubMed-indexed case report of the GBR + delayed protocol specifically for large condensing osteitis
- Provides a differential diagnosis framework: bone-borne (CO, cemento-ossifying fibroma, sclerosing osteomyelitis) vs. tissue-borne entities
- Establishes that atraumatic extraction + immediate GBR + 6-month healing → implant is clinically executable in large CO cases
- Contrasts with the immediate basal implant approach (Hassan 2023), defining two ends of the staging spectrum

## Case Description

**Presentation:**
- Patient with radiopaque osseous mass at root apex of tooth No. 45 (mandibular right second premolar)
- Differential: bone-borne vs. tissue-borne radiopaque jaw lesions

**Diagnosis:** Condensing osteitis — confirmed by clinical and radiologic evaluation
- Bone density characteristics consistent with reactive sclerosis
- Trabeculation pattern distinguishing CO from other entities

**Treatment sequence:**
1. Atraumatic extraction under local anesthesia
2. Immediate GBR at extraction socket
3. 6-month healing period
4. Dental implant placement

## Outcome

- Successful implant placement reported
- No complications noted
- Osseointegration implied (case report format)
