## Three-line Summary

Nested case-control study with risk-set sampling comparing 550 failed implants in 328 subjects against 1156 control implants in 502 subjects (mean follow-up 59.07 months) to identify patient-related risk factors for implant failure.

Odds of failure were elevated with periodontitis (OR 12.82, 95% CI 7.12-23.08), smoking (OR 2.70, 1.60-4.57), immunosuppressive medication (OR 2.75, 1.31-5.76) and type 2 diabetes (OR 1.70, 1.11-2.61); stage III-IV periodontitis was associated with failure independent of diabetes and smoking (OR 1.64, 1.24-2.18), and adherence to periodontal maintenance reduced failure risk.

Early failure was associated with maxillary placement, short length (10 mm or less) and smoking, but the abstract gives no early-failure definition, rate or effect sizes; evidence is retrospective and abstract-only.

## 세줄요약

위험집합 표집 방식의 내포 사례-대조군 연구로, 실패 임플란트 550개(대상자 328명)와 대조 임플란트 1156개(대상자 502명)를 평균 59.07개월 추적하여 환자 관련 위험인자를 분석.

실패 오즈는 치주염 (OR 12.82, 95% CI 7.12-23.08), 흡연 (OR 2.70, 1.60-4.57), 면역억제제 복용 (OR 2.75, 1.31-5.76), 제2형 당뇨병 (OR 1.70, 1.11-2.61)에서 증가; III-IV기 치주염은 당뇨·흡연과 독립적으로 실패와 연관 (OR 1.64, 1.24-2.18)되었고 치주 유지관리 순응은 실패 위험을 낮춤.

조기 실패는 상악 식립, 짧은 길이(10 mm 이하), 흡연과 연관되었으나 초록에는 조기 실패의 정의·비율·효과크기가 없음; 후향적 설계이며 초록 기반.

## Summary
Nested case-control study of patient-related risk factors for dental implant failure, with particular focus on type 2 diabetes (T2DM, 제2형 당뇨병) and periodontitis (치주염). Cases were 550 failed implants in 328 subjects; controls were 1156 implants in 502 subjects; mean follow-up 59.07 months. Univariate odds ratios and multiple logistic regression were used. Periodontitis, smoking, T2DM and immunosuppressive medication were associated with failure; the multivariate model identified periodontitis history, smoking, osteoporosis and statin use as independent risk factors, with adherence to periodontal maintenance protective. A subset analysis linked maxillary placement, short implant length (10 mm or less) and smoking to early failure.

## Key Contributions
- Large implant-level case-control sample (550 failed vs 1156 control implants) with patient-record covariates.
- Quantifies periodontitis and T2DM as independent contributors and reports stage III-IV periodontitis as a risk factor independent of T2DM and smoking.
- Adds immunosuppressive medication, osteoporosis and statin use to the risk-factor list and identifies periodontal-maintenance adherence as protective.
- Provides an (abstract-level) early-failure signal for maxillary site, short length and smoking.

## Methodology
- Design: nested case-control study, risk-set sampling scheme; data collected from patient records. Setting/country is not stated in the abstract.
- Cases: 550 failed implants in 328 subjects. Controls: 1156 implants in 502 subjects. Mean follow-up 59.07 months.
- Analysis: univariate ORs with 95% CI; multiple logistic regression for covariates and independent risk factors.
- Early-failure definition (time window): not given in the abstract.

## Results
- Univariate: smoking OR 2.70 (95% CI 1.60-4.57); T2DM OR 1.70 (1.11-2.61); periodontitis OR 12.82 (7.12-23.08); immunosuppressive medication OR 2.75 (1.31-5.76).
- Stage III and IV periodontitis: OR 1.64 (1.24-2.18), independent of T2DM and smoking.
- Multivariate independent risk factors: periodontitis history, smoking, osteoporosis, statin use; adherence to periodontal maintenance significantly reduced failure risk (effect size not in abstract).
- Early implant failure: associated with maxillary placement, short implant length (10 mm or less) and smoking (no effect sizes or early-failure rate in abstract).

## Limitations
- Abstract-only: setting, early-failure definition and rate, adjusted ORs, covariate coding and the periodontitis reference group are not verifiable.
- Retrospective record-based data; case-control design gives odds ratios, not incidence or risk.
- The periodontitis OR of 12.82 (univariate) versus 1.64 (stage III-IV analysis) come from different comparisons and should not be read as interchangeable.
- Cases and controls are counted by implants within subjects; clustering handling is not described in the abstract.

## Clinical Implication (Korean Practice)
Screen implant candidates for periodontitis history, smoking, T2DM, immunosuppressive medication, osteoporosis and statin use, and enroll those with periodontal disease history in strict periodontal maintenance; treat maxillary and short (10 mm or less) sites with extra caution for early failure, noting that these early-failure associations are abstract-level only.
