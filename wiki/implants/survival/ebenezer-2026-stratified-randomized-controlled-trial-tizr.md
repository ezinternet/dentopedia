## Three-line Summary

Stratified RCT (n=83 patients, 166 implants; CTRI/2017/08/009315) comparing ∅3.3mm vs ∅4.1mm hydrophilic titanium-zirconium (TiZr) implants supporting mandibular distal-extension fixed dental prostheses in HbA1c-stratified hyperglycaemic subjects (<7% vs ≥7%), 12-month follow-up, Bangalore, India.

Implant survival 100%; MBL equivalent between diameters (both 0.27mm, p=0.078); HbA1c ≥7% conferred ~7× higher odds of MBL ≥0.5mm; ISQ higher with ∅4.1mm (79.6 vs 76.1, p<0.001); early healing (Healing Index) better with ∅3.3mm (p=0.005); patient-reported chewing ability improved in all groups.

Implant diameter does not independently drive bone loss in hyperglycaemic patients at 12 months; HbA1c ≥7% is the dominant modifiable risk factor for clinically relevant marginal bone loss.

임플란트 생존율 100%; 변연골소실 (MBL) 직경 간 차이 없음 (0.27mm vs 0.27mm, p=0.078); HbA1c ≥7%는 MBL ≥0.5mm 교차비 (Odds Ratio) ~7배 증가; 임플란트 안정성 지수 (ISQ) ∅4.1mm에서 유의하게 높고 (79.6 vs 76.1, p<0.001); 초기 치유지수 (Healing Index) ∅3.3mm에서 우수 (p=0.005).

## Summary

This stratified RCT enrolled 88 hyperglycaemic participants, stratifying by HbA1c (<7% vs ≥7%) and then randomising within each stratum to receive either ∅3.3mm or ∅4.1mm hydrophilic TiZr implants supporting two-implant fixed dental prostheses in mandibular distal-extension situations. At 12 months (83 evaluable patients, 166 implants), implant survival was 100% with no peri-implantitis in either arm. The primary outcome — MBL change — did not differ significantly between diameters (both medians 0.27mm, p=0.078), but HbA1c ≥7% was associated with approximately sevenfold higher odds of MBL ≥0.5mm compared to the <7% stratum. Secondarily, the ∅4.1mm implants showed significantly higher ISQ (79.6 vs 76.1, p<0.001), while the ∅3.3mm arm had better early healing (Healing Index, p=0.005). Patient-reported chewing ability improved substantially for all participants regardless of diameter or glycaemic category.

## Key Contributions

- First stratified RCT isolating diameter effect (3.3mm vs 4.1mm TiZr) within HbA1c-defined strata
- Quantifies HbA1c ≥7% as ~7× multiplier for clinically relevant MBL (≥0.5mm) — strongest prospective evidence for this threshold
- Demonstrates ISQ advantage of wider diameter but superior early healing for narrow diameter
- 100% survival and zero peri-implantitis in hyperglycaemic cohort at 12 months
- Patient-reported outcome (chewing) — improvement independent of diameter and glycaemic status

## Methodology

- **Design**: Stratified RCT; HbA1c stratification (<7% vs ≥7%), then 1:1 randomisation within stratum
- **N**: 88 enrolled; 83 patients (166 implants) evaluated at 12 months
- **Implant**: Hydrophilic TiZr (Roxolid-class); ∅3.3mm vs ∅4.1mm; mandibular distal-extension; two-implant-supported multi-unit FDP
- **Primary outcome**: MBL change at 12 months post-prosthesis delivery
- **Secondary outcomes**: ISQ (RFA), peri-implant indices, Healing Index, patient-reported outcomes
- **Setting**: Oral Rehabilitation Center, Bangalore, India
- **Registration**: CTRI/2017/08/009315 (prospective)

## Results

| Outcome | ∅3.3mm | ∅4.1mm | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 100% | 100% | — |
| MBL at 12 months (median, IQR) | 0.27mm (0.07–0.68) | 0.27mm (−0.04 to 0.54) | 0.078 (NS) |
| ISQ (mean) | 76.1 | 79.6 | <0.001 |
| Healing Index | Better | — | 0.005 |
| Peri-implantitis | None | None | — |
| Chewing ability change | ↑ p<0.0001 | ↑ p<0.0001 | — |

**HbA1c effect on MBL**: HbA1c ≥7% → ~7× higher odds MBL ≥0.5mm vs HbA1c <7% (primary stratification finding)
