## Three-line Summary

Retrospective chart review (2005-2021) at Seoul National University Dental Hospital, Korea: 464 replaced implants in 370 patients, of which 429 survived and 35 were removed a second time.

Five-year survival of replaced implants was 90.2% with vs 97.0% without a periodontitis history (p = 0.008); independent risk factors for second removal were periodontitis history (aHR 3.417; 95% CI 1.161-10.055) and GBR at first placement (aHR 2.152; 95% CI 1.052-4.397), while non-GBR at replacement was protective (aHR 0.262; 95% CI 0.088-0.778).

Abstract only: periodontitis history and GBR at first placement mark replaced implants at higher risk of second removal; patient nationality is not stated (Korean setting only implied by institution).

## 세줄요약

2005-2021년 서울대학교치과병원 차트를 후향적으로 검토: 1차 제거 후 재식립된 임플란트 464개(환자 370명) 중 429개 생존, 35개 2차 제거.

재식립 임플란트 5년 생존율은 치주염 병력 있음 90.2%, 없음 97.0% (p = 0.008); 2차 제거의 독립 위험인자는 치주염 병력 (aHR 3.417; 95% CI 1.161-10.055)과 1차 식립 시 골유도재생술 (guided bone regeneration, GBR) (aHR 2.152; 95% CI 1.052-4.397)이며 재식립 시 GBR 미시행은 보호 요인 (aHR 0.262; 95% CI 0.088-0.778).

초록 기반: 치주염 병력과 1차 식립 시 GBR이 재식립 임플란트의 2차 제거 위험을 높임; 환자 국적은 명시되지 않고 한국 병원이라는 점에서만 시사됨.

## Summary
Single-center retrospective chart review (Seoul National University Dental Hospital, Korea; 2005-2021) of implants replaced after a first removal. Of 464 replaced implants in 370 patients, 429 survived and 35 had a second removal. Clustering of multiple implants within a patient was considered in the analysis. Nationality/ethnicity of patients is not stated in the abstract; the Korean setting is implied only by the institution.

## Key Contributions
- Focuses on the second-failure risk of replaced (re-implanted) implants, not first-time survival.
- Identifies periodontitis history and GBR history at the first placement as independent risk factors.
- Reports 5-year survival by subgroup with clustering-adjusted multivariable hazard ratios.

## Methodology
Chart review 2005-2021; replaced implants after the first removal split into surviving and second-removal groups; multivariable model adjusted for clinical variables with patient-level clustering. Abstract only: covariates, removal definition and follow-up length are not available.

## Results
[확인] Denominator: 464 implants / 370 patients; 429 surviving, 35 second removal. Early-vs-late timing of second removal is not reported in the abstract.
[확인] 5-year survival: 90.2% (periodontitis history) vs 97.0% (none), p = 0.008; 89.1% (GBR at first placement) vs 93.9% (non-GBR), p = 0.032; 97.6% vs 90.3% for GBR vs non-GBR at replacement (p = 0.026; labels ambiguous).
[확인] aHR for second removal: periodontitis history 3.417 (95% CI 1.161-10.055); GBR at first placement 2.152 (1.052-4.397); non-GBR at replacement 0.262 (0.088-0.778).

## Limitations
- Abstract only; definitions, covariates and follow-up not verifiable.
- Retrospective, single center; 35 events and a wide CI for periodontitis history.
- Abstract inconsistency: 97.6% is attributed to "with GBR" at replacement while non-GBR at replacement is protective (aHR 0.262); labels appear swapped or unclear. "+/-" values are unlabeled (SD vs SE).

## Clinical Implication (Korean Practice)
When replacing a failed implant in a patient with a periodontitis history, or at a site that was grafted with GBR at first placement, counsel on a higher second-removal risk (5-year survival about 90% vs 97% for periodontitis history). Evidence is retrospective and from one Korean university hospital; the abstract does not support a statement on early vs late timing.
