## Three-line Summary

Single-center case-control study (University of Iowa academic dental setting; 61 patients with at least one early implant failure vs 140 age- and gender-matched, randomly selected non-EIF controls) testing surgery start time (SST) and other patient- and therapy-related variables as risk factors for early implant failure (EIF).

SST was **not** significantly associated with EIF (HR 1.9 for afternoon placement, 95% CI 0.9–3.9; p = .105), while pre-placement ridge augmentation (HR 7.5, 95% CI 2.2–25.1; p = .001), intra-operative complications (HR 5.9, 95% CI 2.2–16.3; p < .001), simultaneous soft tissue grafting (HR 5.03, 95% CI 1.3–19.5; p = .020), simultaneous bone grafting (HR 3.7, 95% CI 1.6–8.8; p = .002), and placement with sedation (HR 3.4, 95% CI 1.5–7.5; p = .002) significantly increased multivariable EIF risk.

Clinical implication: the procedural-complexity cluster (grafting, intra-operative complications, sedation) dominates the model; the time-of-day signal — a **novel/chance finding** — stayed non-significant and rests on a single-center, abstract-only case-control, so it cannot yet inform scheduling and requires replication.

## 세줄요약

단일 기관 환자대조연구 (미국 아이오와대 학술 치과 환경; 조기 임플란트 실패 환자 61명 vs 나이·성별 매칭 무작위 비실패 대조군 140명) — 수술 시작 시각 (Surgery Start Time, SST)과 기타 환자·치료 관련 변인이 조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF) 위험에 미치는 영향 검증.

SST는 EIF와 유의하지 않았다 (오후 식립 HR 1.9, 95% CI 0.9–3.9; p = .105). 반면 식립 전 치조제 증대 (HR 7.5, 95% CI 2.2–25.1; p = .001)·수술 중 합병증 (HR 5.9, 95% CI 2.2–16.3; p < .001)·동시 연조직 이식 (HR 5.03, 95% CI 1.3–19.5; p = .020)·동시 골이식 (HR 3.7, 95% CI 1.6–8.8; p = .002)·진정 하 식립 (HR 3.4, 95% CI 1.5–7.5; p = .002)은 다변량에서 EIF 위험을 유의하게 높였다.

임상적 의미: 술식 복잡도 군집 (이식·수술 중 합병증·진정)이 모델을 지배한다; 시간대 신호는 **신규·우연 발견 (novel/chance finding)** 으로 비유의하게 남았고 단일 기관·초록만 수집 환자대조연구에 근거하므로 스케줄링 결정에 쓰기엔 이르고 재현이 필요하다.

## Summary

This single-center case-control study from the University of Iowa's academic dental setting asked whether **surgery start time (SST)** — and other patient- and therapy-related variables — influence early implant failure (EIF). Cases were 61 patients with at least one EIF; controls were 140 age- and gender-matched, randomly selected non-EIF patients, with data pulled from electronic health records and analysed bivariately then multivariately. SST itself was **not** significantly associated with EIF (afternoon placement HR 1.9, 95% CI 0.9–3.9; p = .105), so the time-of-day hypothesis is a suggestive, hypothesis-generating, single-center signal. What the multivariable model did show was a **procedural-complexity cluster**: pre-placement ridge augmentation (HR 7.5), intra-operative complications (HR 5.9), simultaneous soft tissue grafting (HR 5.03), simultaneous bone grafting (HR 3.7), and placement with sedation (HR 3.4) each significantly raised EIF risk, with the number of implants in the oral cavity also named in the conclusions. The authors' bottom line is that operative context and concurrent grafting — not the clock — carried the EIF risk signal in this cohort.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces **surgery start time (SST) / time-of-day** as a candidate early-failure risk factor — the only page in the wiki framing this axis — and reports it honestly as non-significant (HR 1.9, 0.9–3.9, p = .105).
- Quantifies a large **procedural-complexity cluster** on the hazard-ratio scale with full CIs: ridge augmentation HR 7.5, intra-operative complications HR 5.9, simultaneous soft tissue grafting HR 5.03, simultaneous bone grafting HR 3.7, sedation HR 3.4.
- Distinguishes **pre-placement** ridge augmentation (HR 7.5) from **simultaneous** bone grafting (HR 3.7) as separate risk exposures, adding granularity to the "grafted site" signal already pooled by [[implants/survival/clauser-2022-early-implant-failure-grafted]] (OR 1.50).
- Reports effects as HRs with 95% CIs and p-values — richer effect-size reporting than the OR-without-CI abstracts common in this literature.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Case-control study.
- **Setting**: Academic dental setting, University of Iowa (USA).
- **Cases**: 61 patients with at least one early implant failure (EIF).
- **Controls**: 140 age- and gender-matched, randomly selected, non-EIF controls.
- **Data source**: electronic health records.
- **Variables**: surgery start time (SST) and other patient- and therapy-related variables.
- **Analysis**: bivariate and multivariable analyses; effects reported as hazard ratios (HR) with 95% CI.
