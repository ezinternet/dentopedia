## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-centre cohort at Seoul National University Dental Hospital (placement 2006-2011, follow-up over 10 years): 247 RESTORE resorbable-blast-media (RBM) implants in 86 patients, analysed with Kaplan-Meier and Cox regression.

Cumulative survival was 92.5% with 17 late failures at a mean 6.88 years (10 fixture fractures, 6 peri-implantitis, 1 screw fracture); male sex (multivariate HR 14.004, 95% CI 1.840-106.600, p=0.011) and overdenture support (HR 109.345, 95% CI 9.245-1,293.229, p<0.001) were significant risk factors.

Survival at 10-15 years was favourable, but the overdenture estimate rests on 2 implants in 1 patient and the sex effect has very wide CIs, so both are hypothesis-generating.

## 세줄요약

서울대학교치과병원 단일기관 후향 코호트(2006~2011년 식립, 10년 이상 추적): 환자 86명의 RESTORE 흡수성 블라스트 표면 (resorbable blast media, RBM) 임플란트 247개를 카플란-마이어 (Kaplan-Meier) 및 콕스 회귀 (Cox regression)로 분석했다.

누적 임플란트 생존율 (cumulative survival rate) 92.5%, 실패 17개는 평균 6.88년에 발생(고정체 파절 10, 임플란트주위염 6, 나사 파절 1)했으며, 남성(다변량 HR 14.004, 95% CI 1.840-106.600, p=0.011)과 피개의치 지지(HR 109.345, 95% CI 9.245-1,293.229, p<0.001)가 유의한 위험인자였다.

10~15년 생존율은 양호했으나 피개의치는 임플란트 2개(환자 1명)에 근거하고 성별 효과의 신뢰구간이 넓어 가설 생성 수준이다.

## Summary

This retrospective study from Seoul National University Dental Hospital (Republic of Korea; IRB S-D20210020; STROBE-guided) reviewed records and radiographs of patients who received RESTORE RBM-surface implants (Keystone Dental) between March 2006 and April 2011 and were followed more than 10 years (until 2022). Of 360 implants in 134 patients initially evaluated, 247 implants in 86 patients met criteria (at least 10 years of follow-up after prosthetic rehabilitation; adequate records).

The cumulative survival rate (Kaplan-Meier) was **92.5%**. Seventeen implants were removed at a mean of 6.88 years: 10 fixture fractures, 6 peri-implantitis and 1 screw fracture (all late failures). Survival was 88.3% in males (14 failures of 127 implants) vs 96.7% in females (3 of 120). In univariate Cox analysis male sex had HR 4.542 (95% CI 1.305-15.807, p=0.017); overdenture support had HR 15.498 (95% CI 3.105-77.357, p=0.001). In multivariate analysis male sex had HR 14.004 (95% CI 1.840-106.600, p=0.011) and overdenture HR 109.345 (95% CI 9.245-1,293.229, p<0.001).

## Key Findings

- Population: age 19-74 (mean 57.9); 55.1% maxillary, 88.3% posterior; 84.6% were 4.0 mm and 65.6% were 11.5 mm; 72.1% bridges, 27.1% single crowns, 2 implants overdenture; GBR in 33.2%; 80.2% of implants in patients with periodontitis history.
- Not significant in univariate Cox: age (p=0.074), ITI placement type, jaw, anterior/posterior, diameter, length, GBR/SFE, periodontitis history (CSR 92.3% vs 93.6%), hypertension (97.3% vs 90.5%), diabetes (85.7% vs 93.5%), CKD.
- Fixture fractures (10 of 17 failures) exceeded the less-than-1% 5-year fracture rate the authors cite from the literature, which they attribute to longer follow-up and 4.0 mm implants in molar regions.
- The authors suggest greater bite force and higher periodontitis prevalence in men as possible explanations for the sex effect, while noting occlusal load is hard to quantify.

## Limitations

- Retrospective, single-centre, record-based; 113 of 360 implants excluded (follow-up or records), so attrition bias is possible.
- Only 17 events; sparse strata (overdenture 2 implants/1 patient) give extremely wide CIs.
- Multiple implants per patient with no stated clustering adjustment; occlusal load and prosthetic design not controlled.
- Only late failures described; single implant system.

## Clinical Implication (Korean Practice)

In a Korean university-hospital setting, late failure of this RBM implant at 10-15 years was driven more by mechanical complications (fixture fracture, especially 4.0 mm implants in molar regions) than by biological ones, so long-term recall should include occlusal and mechanical assessment, particularly in male patients. The overdenture signal is too thinly supported (2 implants) to guide treatment on its own.
