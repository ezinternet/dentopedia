## One-line Summary
SR+MA (10 cohort studies) found bone grafting significantly associated with early implant failure vs non-grafted sites (OR 1.50, 95% CI 1.06–2.13), establishing a pooled evidence base for the grafted-site risk on osseointegration failure.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (코호트 10편) — 이식부(Bone-Grafted Site)에서 조기 임플란트 실패(Early Implant Failure, EIF; 보철 로딩 전 골유착 실패) 위험이 비이식부 대비 유의하게 높음 (교차비(Odds Ratio, OR) 1.50, 95% CI 1.06–2.13); 골이식 술식이 초기 골유착에 부정적 영향을 줄 수 있어 치료 계획 시 고려 필수.

## Summary
This MEDLINE-based SR+MA (Clauser et al., *Int J Oral Implantol* 2022, 10 cohort studies from 231 screened full-texts) is the first dedicated pooled analysis asking specifically whether bone grafting increases **early** implant failure (EIF — defined as failure of osseointegration before functional loading). Using the Newcastle-Ottawa Scale for quality assessment and two independent reviewers, the authors pooled OR = 1.50 (95% CI 1.06–2.13), indicating grafted sites carry 50% higher odds of EIF vs non-grafted. Data for other risk factors (bone type, smoking, etc.) in the grafted subgroup were insufficient for meta-analysis. The finding aligns directionally with single-cohort data (Yari 2023: augmented site OR 2.24; Bonsmann 2025: sinus augmentation as independent EIF predictor), but provides a dedicated pooled estimate.

## Key Contributions
- Only SR+MA specifically pooling the early failure (pre-loading) risk for grafted vs non-grafted implant sites
- OR 1.50 (95% CI 1.06–2.13) — statistically significant; smallest effect estimate in the grafted-site literature (cohort data suggest higher)
- Strict EIF definition (pre-loading osseointegration failure) enables meaningful cross-study comparison
- Signals grafting may impair initial osseointegration kinetics, not only long-term marginal bone dynamics
- Complements Gurbanov 2024 (long-term ≥10 yr ISR similar), suggesting graft impact is concentrated in the early healing window

## Methodology
- **Design**: SR+MA of observational cohort studies
- **Database**: MEDLINE (PubMed) only
- **Screening**: 231 full-texts → 10 included in quantitative MA
- **Outcome**: EIF = lack of osseointegration before functional loading
- **Pooling**: Odds ratio (OR), random-effects (implied)
- **Quality**: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (non-randomized cohorts)
- **Reviewers**: 2 independent

## Results
- **Primary outcome**: Bone grafting vs no grafting → EIF
  - OR **1.50** (95% CI 1.06–2.13); statistically significant
- Additional risk factor analysis in grafted context: insufficient data for pooling
- Clinical takeaway: grafted sites warrant heightened protocol caution — consider delayed loading, higher insertion torque targets, ISQ monitoring
