## Three-line Summary

Retrospective multicenter cohort of 3555 bone-level SA implants placed in 1757 patients at two Korean university hospitals (2012–2018, observed to May 2025), assessing 5-year survival and risk factors for implant removal.

The 5-year cumulative survival rate was 98.8% (implant-level) and 99.0% (patient-level); periodontitis-related tooth loss (aHR 3.57), poor SPT compliance (aHR 2.35), and GBR presence (no-GBR aHR 0.42) were the only significant multivariable predictors of removal.

Regular SPT largely offsets the periodontitis survival penalty in periodontitis-related sites (98.8% vs 96.3%, p=0.004), but only 48 removals and the absence of serial radiographic bone-level data limit subgroup and peri-implant bone-stability inference.

## 세줄요약

회향적 다기관 코호트 (Retrospective Multicenter Cohort) — 서울대·부산대 두 대학병원에서 2012–2018년 식립된 골수준 (Bone-Level) SA 임플란트 3,555개·환자 1,757명, 2025년 5월까지 추적, 5년 생존율과 실패 위험인자 분석.

5년 누적생존율 (Cumulative Survival Rate, CSR) 임플란트 수준 98.8%·환자 수준 99.0%; 다변량 공유 취약성 (Shared Frailty) Cox에서 치주염으로 인한 치아상실 (교정후 위험비 aHR 3.57), 지지 치주치료 (SPT) 비순응 (aHR 2.35), 골유도재생 (GBR) 시행 부위 (미시행 기준 aHR 0.42)만 유의 — 실패 48개 중 67%는 조기골유착실패.

치주염 상실 치아 부위에서는 규칙적 SPT군 생존율 98.8% vs 비순응군 96.3% (p=0.004)로 유지치료가 생존 격차를 상쇄하나 비치주염 부위에서는 차이 없음 (p=0.64); 제거 사례 48개뿐이고 표준화된 방사선 추적 데이터가 없어 아군 분석과 골수준 안정성 평가는 제한.

## Summary

This retrospective multicenter cohort (STROBE-compliant) analyzed 3555 Osstem TSIII SA bone-level implants placed in 1757 patients at Seoul National University and Pusan National University dental hospitals between 2012 and 2018, with observation to May 2025. Five-year cumulative survival was 98.8% at the implant level and 99.0% at the patient level; only three variables survived multivariable shared-frailty Cox adjustment: tooth loss due to periodontitis (aHR 3.57), poor supportive periodontal therapy (SPT) compliance (aHR 2.35), and receipt of guided bone regeneration (GBR) — whose associated risk the authors attribute to underlying site compromise rather than the grafting procedure itself. Crucially, a stratified analysis showed the survival benefit of regular SPT is concentrated in implants replacing teeth lost to periodontitis (98.8% vs 96.3%, p=0.004), with essentially no SPT-frequency effect in non-periodontitis sites (p=0.64). The clinical message is that periodontitis-driven risk is real but largely mitigable through intensive maintenance, while GBR-related risk flags site severity rather than contraindicating augmentation.

## Key Contributions

- Largest single-system multicenter real-world 5-year survival cohort for bone-level internal-connection SA implants (3555 implants / 1757 patients).
- Dose–response quantification of SPT compliance: once/year HR 12.56, irregular HR 4.26 vs 2–3×/year (univariable); poor compliance aHR 2.35 multivariably.
- First stratified interaction evidence that SPT's implant-survival benefit is conditional on periodontitis etiology (98.8% vs 96.3%, p=0.004 in periodontitis-related sites; p=0.64 elsewhere).
- GBR association reframed as confounding by indication (no-GBR aHR 0.42) — augmentation marks site complexity, not graft harm; explicit "do not avoid, optimize GBR" reading.
- Demonstrates peri-implantitis clusters within patients: removal risk significant at patient level (RR 2.47, p<0.01) but not implant level (RR 1.54, p=0.30) → patient-level risk assessment.

## Methodology

Retrospective multicenter cohort at two Korean university hospitals (IRB ERI23012 / 2023-05-008); 3590 implants in 1783 patients screened, 3555 implants in 1757 patients analyzed (placements 2012-01-01 to 2018-07-31; final observation 2025-05-15; ≥5-year follow-up in 59.3%, ≥8-year in 20.0%). Outcome: implant removal as survival event, causes classified (early failure, position error, anatomical invasion, late failure). Analysis: Kaplan–Meier at implant and patient level; Cox proportional hazards with shared frailty terms for patient and hospital clustering; univariate p<0.20 screening with forward stepwise AIC selection; Schoenfeld residuals for the proportional-hazards assumption. SPT compliance defined by visit frequency only (2–3×/year reference, 1×/year, irregular <1×/year).

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| 5-year CSR | 98.79% implant-level (95% CI 98.36–99.11); 98.96% patient-level (95% CI 98.56–99.26) |
| Long-term plateau | 98.33% from year 8; no removals beyond 8 years |
| Implant removals | 48 implants / 40 patients — early failure 67% (n=32), peri-implantitis 13% (n=6) |
| Peri-implantitis prevalence | 460/3555 implants (12.9%); 288/1757 patients (16.4%) |
| Peri-implantitis → removal | Patient level RR 2.47 (1.26–4.83, p<0.01); implant level RR 1.54 (0.72–3.26, p=0.30) |
| Periodontitis-related tooth loss (multivariable) | aHR 3.57 (1.49–8.56, p=0.005); KM 5-year 97.2% vs 99.1% (p<0.05) |
| Poor SPT compliance (multivariable) | aHR 2.35 (1.32–4.18, p=0.002); univariable once/year HR 12.56, irregular HR 4.26 |
| SPT stratified KM (periodontitis sites) | 98.8%±0.4 vs 96.3%±0.8, p=0.004 |
| SPT stratified KM (non-periodontitis sites) | 99.3%±0.3 vs 99.6%±0.2, p=0.64 |
| No-GBR (multivariable) | aHR 0.42 (0.18–0.96, p=0.043) |
| Non-significant factors | Diabetes, smoking, sinus elevation, bone grafting, age, sex, implant location/diameter (p>0.05) |
