## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 872 bone-level, 2-piece, internal connection implants (microthreaded, Implantium) in 284 patients at Yonsei University Dental Hospital, Korea, followed for a mean of 12.3±2.0 years (from a pool of 1,845 implants/691 patients).

Cumulative survival was 95.2% (implant-level) / 88.4% (patient-level); treatment success (MBL <2mm) was 87.0% / 76.1%; among 830 surviving implants, 113 were "ailing" with mean MBL 4.09±1.44mm; implants ≤8mm had significantly higher risk of failure (HR 3.71) and pathologic MBL (HR 2.00) than longer implants (both P<0.05).

Most failures (78.4%) were late and peri-implantitis-driven, reinforcing that recall/maintenance adherence — not microthread geometry — drove the ailing-implant subgroup; short (≤8mm) implants remain a viable but higher-risk option requiring closer monitoring.

## Summary

This retrospective cohort study, from the Department of Periodontology at Yonsei University Dental Hospital (Seoul, Korea), examined the long-term clinical and radiographic outcomes of a bone-level, 2-piece, internal (conical hex) connection implant system with coronal microthreads (Implantium; Dentium). Of 1,845 implants placed in 691 patients between January 2004 and July 2013, 872 implants in 284 patients met inclusion criteria (≥10-year follow-up, with clinical/radiographic data available both immediately post-placement and beyond 10 years). Mean follow-up was 12.3±2.0 years — one of the longest observation windows reported for this fixture type and among the larger sample sizes in the implant longevity literature.

Cumulative survival was 95.2% at the implant level and 88.4% at the patient level. Treatment success — defined as marginal bone loss (MBL) under 2mm — was 87.0% (implant-level) and 76.1% (patient-level). Of the 42 failed implants, only 5 were early failures (osseointegration failure, before loading); the remaining 37 were late failures, surviving a mean of 9.43±2.77 years before explantation, predominantly (29/37) due to peri-implantitis, with 7 fixture fractures and 1 unretrievable fractured abutment rounding out the mechanical-complication group.

Among the 830 surviving implants, 113 were classified as "ailing" (MBL ≥2mm), with a mean MBL of 4.09±1.44mm — markedly higher than the 0.21±0.51mm seen in healthy implants, and higher than the overall cohort mean of 0.76±1.64mm. The authors attribute this ailing subgroup primarily to inadequate recall/maintenance adherence rather than to the microthread geometry itself — citing prior SEM, animal, and clinical evidence that microthreaded surfaces are actually easier to mechanically decontaminate than larger-thread-pitch surfaces once exposed.

The standout finding on multivariable Cox regression (with shared frailty) was implant length: implants ≤8mm carried a hazard ratio of 3.71 (95% CI 1.494–9.186, p=0.005) for failure and 2.00 (95% CI 1.045–3.833, p=0.036) for pathologic bone loss, compared with implants >8mm. No other patient- or implant-level factor (sex, age, jaw, anterior/posterior position, diameter, GBR, sinus grafting, splinting) reached significance after adjustment. The authors reason that once MBL in an ailing implant exceeds ~4mm, a ≤8mm implant loses over half its osseointegrated support length, whereas a ≥10mm implant retains majority bone support even in an ailing state — plausibly driving clinicians toward earlier explantation decisions for short ailing implants.

## Key Contributions

- One of the largest (872 implants) and longest (mean 12.3 years, up to >18 years per the representative case shown) single-fixture-type retrospective cohorts in the microthreaded internal-connection implant literature
- Direct quantification of the "ailing implant" subgroup: 113/830 surviving implants, mean MBL 4.09±1.44mm — a concrete number for what "peri-implantitis-affected but not yet failed" looks like at >10 years
- Multivariable Cox regression isolating implant length (≤8mm) as the sole independent risk factor for both failure (HR 3.71) and pathologic MBL (HR 2.00), after screening 9 candidate variables via log-rank
- Mechanistic reasoning linking short-implant risk to disproportionate loss of osseointegrated support once MBL progresses (a ≤8mm implant crosses the 50%-support threshold at lower absolute MBL than a longer implant)
- Reinforces that microthread surface geometry does not impair maintainability — the driver of poor outcomes was recall non-adherence, not fixture design
- Low early osseointegration-failure rate (0.6%) and favorable outcomes even among 5 implants with sub-threshold insertion torque (<20 Ncm, "spinning" implants) — 4/5 remained successful long-term

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-center (Yonsei University Dental Hospital) retrospective cohort, STROBE-compliant, IRB-approved (No. 2-2023-0032)
- **Population**: 872 implants / 284 patients (from a pool of 1,845 implants / 691 patients placed 2004–2013), all with ≥10-year follow-up and clinical/radiographic data at both baseline and >10 years; 963 implants excluded for lack of periodic follow-up
- **Exclusions**: physiological disorders, uncontrolled diabetes, immune suppression, prior head/neck radiotherapy, bisphosphonate history, heavy smoking (>10 cigarettes/day), parafunctional habits
- **Implant system**: Implantium (Dentium, Suwon, Korea) — tapered, SLA-surfaced, conical hex internal connection, microthreads below the fixture platform, on market since 2004
- **MBL measurement**: standardized periapical radiographs (extension cone paralleling technique, Rinn positioning device), digital image-processing software (ZeTTA PACS), single calibrated examiner, mesial/distal average from fixture-platform reference line to first bone-to-implant contact
- **Definitions**: healthy = MBL <2mm; ailing = MBL ≥2mm; treatment success = healthy state at final follow-up; early failure = pre-loading removal; late failure = post-loading removal
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier cumulative survival/success curves; log-rank test to screen 9 candidate variables; significant variables entered into multivariable Cox regression with shared frailty (R); significance threshold p<0.05

## Results

|---|---|---|
| 10-year+ cumulative survival | 95.2% | 88.4% |
| Treatment success (MBL <2mm) | 87.0% | 76.1% |

| Failure type | n | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Early (<1yr) | 5 | All osseointegration failure; 4/5 with concurrent GBR or sinus graft |
| Late (≥1yr) | 37 | Mean 9.43±2.77yr to explantation; 29 peri-implantitis, 7 fixture fracture, 1 unretrievable fractured abutment |

| Cox regression (multivariable, shared frailty) | HR | p | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Failure, ≤8mm vs >8mm | 3.71 | 0.005 | 1.494–9.186 |
| Pathologic MBL (≥2mm), ≤8mm vs >8mm | 2.00 | 0.036 | 1.045–3.833 |
| Failure, sinus graft yes vs no | 1.17 | 0.757 (NS) | 0.439–3.103 |

- Overall mean MBL 0.76±1.64mm; ailing (n=113) mean MBL 4.09±1.44mm; healthy mean MBL 0.21±0.51mm
- Pathologic MBL rate: 16.0% (29/182) for implants ≤8mm vs 12.2% (84/690) for implants >8mm
- Fixture fracture incidence 0.8% (7/872) — comparable to other manufacturers' microthreaded internal-connection systems
