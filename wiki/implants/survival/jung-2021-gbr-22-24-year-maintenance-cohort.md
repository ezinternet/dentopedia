## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (original RCT 1994–1996, n=72 patients, 265 implants) with 22–24 year follow-up (median 23.5 years, 39 patients, 147 implants). Implant survival: resorbable collagen+DBBM (BG) 89.3%, nonresorbable e-PTFE+DBBM (GT) 90.2%, pristine bone controls (CT) 93.8% — no significant difference (frailty model p=0.79). Marginal bone levels stable and comparable across groups (BoneL: BG −2.3±1.4mm, GT −3.0±1.5mm, CT −2.3±1.5mm; BoneLbucc: BG −3.0±1.9mm, GT −3.5±2.2mm, CT −2.6±1.8mm). Smoking significantly reduced survival (p=0.012). Yearly recalls first 5 years, then private maintenance — documents real-world long-term GBR surveillance pattern.

## 세줄요약

1994–1996년 시작된 전향적 RCT(72명, 265개 임플란트)의 22–24년 추적 관찰(중앙값 23.5년, 39명 147개 임플란트). 흡수성 콜라겐막+DBBM(BG) 89.3%, 비흡수성 e-PTFE+DBBM(GT) 90.2%, 대조군(CT) 93.8%로 군간 생존율 유의차 없음(frailty model p=0.79). 변연골레벨(BoneL, BoneLbucc) 모든 군에서 안정적·비교 가능. 흡연만 생존율 유의 저하(p=0.012). 초기 5년 연 1회 recall 후 개별 유지관리로 전환된 실제 GBR 장기 서베일런스 패턴 기록.

## Summary

This prospective controlled clinical trial represents the longest documented follow-up of implants placed with simultaneous GBR. Originally randomized in 1994–1996 (72 patients, 265 implants), 39 patients (147 implants) were re-examined after a median of 23.5 years. Three groups were compared: BG (resorbable collagen membrane Bio-Gide + DBBM Bio-Oss), GT (nonresorbable e-PTFE Gore-Tex + DBBM Bio-Oss), and CT (pristine bone, no GBR). Implant survival was 89.3% (BG), 90.2% (GT), and 93.8% (CT) — no significant difference by frailty proportional hazard model (p=0.79). Smoking was the only significant negative predictor of survival (p=0.012). Marginal bone levels (2D BoneL, 3D BoneLbucc/BoneLoral) were stable and comparable across all groups. The surveillance protocol comprised yearly recalls at the university clinic for the first 5 years post-loading, then referral to private practices for regular maintenance — documenting a real-world long-term GBR maintenance pathway. Technical complications at 23.5 years included 84% crown survival, 20.8% ceramic chipping, and 1.9% abutment screw loosening. The GT group showed worse peri-implant mucosal condition (PC score 2.2 vs 1.0 BG, 0.9 CT) and higher suppuration history (34.8% vs 11.8% BG, 14.3% CT).

## Key Contributions

- Longest prospective GBR maintenance cohort (22–24 years) with controlled three-arm comparison
- Real-world surveillance protocol documentation: 5-year structured recalls → private maintenance
- GBR vs non-GBR implant survival equivalence at 23.5 years (89.3–93.8%)
- Rare long-term 3D CBCT data on buccal/oral bone dimensions post-GBR
- Smoking confirmed as significant survival risk factor in GBR sites (p=0.012)
- Technical complication rates at >20 years: crown survival 84%, chipping 20.8%, screw loosening 1.9%
- e-PTFE membrane associated with worse mucosal health and more suppuration vs collagen membrane

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective RCT (1994–1996) with long-term follow-up (2018–2019)
- **Original cohort**: 72 patients (54 women), mean age 75.4±15.7 years, 265 implants (256 Brånemark, 8 Biomed 3i, 1 IMZ)
- **Groups**: BG = Bio-Gide + Bio-Oss (112 implants); GT = Gore-Tex e-PTFE + Bio-Oss (41 implants); CT = pristine bone (112 implants)
- **Surveillance protocol**: Yearly recalls at university clinic ×5 years post-loading → private practice maintenance
- **Follow-up exam**: Health questionnaire, clinical (PI, PPD, BOP, KM, MSTL, PC), technical, 2D periapical (long-cone), 3D CBCT (bucco-oral sections)
- **Radiographic measures**: BoneL (mesial/distal, implant shoulder ref), BoneLbucc/BoneLoral (CBCT, implant shoulder ref), buccal thickness at IS-1/3/5/7mm
- **Statistics**: Mixed models (patient random factor), Kaplan-Meier + frailty Cox model, Spearman correlations
- **Retention**: 39/72 patients (54.2%), 147/265 implants (55.5%); 23 deaths (31.9%), 10 lost (13.9%)

## Results

### Implant Survival (23.5 years median)
| Group | Implants (n) | Survival | Frailty p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| BG (collagen+DBBM) | 68 | 89.3% | 0.79 (NS) |
| GT (e-PTFE+DBBM) | 23 | 90.2% | — |
| CT (pristine bone) | 56 | 93.8% | — |

- Smoking: significant negative effect (p=0.012), no treatment interaction
- Age, follow-up length, location, jaw: NS

### Technical Outcomes (23.5 years)
- Abutment survival: 76.1%
- Crown survival: 84.0%
- Framework fracture: 2.7%
- Ceramic chipping: 20.8%
- Abutment screw loosening: 1.9%
- Color mismatch: 30.3%
- USPHS: Anatomical form Alpha 76.4%, Surface texture Bravo 57.3%, Marginal integrity Alpha 93.8%

### Patient Satisfaction
- Mean VAS: 97.5±5.4% (24/33 patients = 100% satisfaction)

## Limitations

- Heterogeneous group sizes (BG 68, GT 23, CT 56 at follow-up)
- No a priori power analysis
- Implant-based analysis (clustering accounted by mixed models/frailty)
- Non-standardized intraoral radiographs
- CBCT buccal bone <0.5mm potentially undetectable (artifacts)
- Missing baseline defect size/shape data
- 45.8% patient attrition over 23.5 years
