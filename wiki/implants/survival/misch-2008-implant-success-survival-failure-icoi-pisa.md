## Three-line Summary

ICOI Pisa consensus conference statement (Oct 2007; expert panel; no primary data in the abstract) that defines implant success, survival and failure for endosteal root-form implants.

The paper proposes an implant-specific "Health Scale" adapted from natural-tooth indices and places prosthetic support as the first success criterion, with esthetic patient satisfaction as a necessary clinical element.

Use as a definitional anchor when comparing outcome studies; no survival percentage is reported in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

ICOI 피사 합의회의 문서 (2007년 10월; 전문가 패널; 초록에 1차 데이터 없음) — 골내 root-form 임플란트의 성공·생존·실패 정의.

천연치아 지수를 응용한 임플란트 전용 "Health Scale"을 제시하고, 보철 지지를 성공의 첫 기준으로 두며 심미적 환자 만족을 필요 요소로 제시.

결과 연구 비교 시 정의 기준으로 쓰되, 초록에 생존율 수치가 없으므로 생존율 근거로 인용하지 말 것.

## Summary

The Pisa consensus is the definitional layer for implant outcome research. It asks that success be judged first by functional prosthetic support, then by clinical health, and it updates what "survival" and "failure" should mean so that outcome studies can be compared. This is a framework document, not an outcome study.

## Key Contributions

- Implant-specific Health Scale built on natural-tooth indices.
- Updated, explicit definitions of success, survival and failure.

## Methodology

Consensus conference (Pisa, October 2007). The abstract does not describe the voting process or the literature base.

## Results

Qualitative: the Health Scale and revised definitions. No numeric benchmarks in the abstract.
