## Three-line Summary

Systematic review, meta-analysis, and trial sequential analysis of 12 prospective cohort studies (14 reports; 1464 patients, 4425 implants, mean follow-up 89.8 months) comparing implant outcomes between periodontitis-history (PCP) and periodontally healthy (PHP) patients.

History of periodontitis raised implant failure risk by 74% overall (RR 1.74, 95% CI 1.25–2.44, p = 0.001; RR 2.26 at >5 years, 2.61 at 10 years), peri-implantitis incidence four-fold (RR 4.09, 95% CI 1.93–8.58, p < 0.001; 8.58-fold at 10 years), and marginal bone loss by 0.75 mm, while peri-implant probing depth did not differ.

Periodontitis history belongs in pre-implant risk assessment and long-term maintenance planning, but GRADE certainty was low/very low (no RCTs) and trial sequential analysis left peri-implantitis, bone loss, and probing depth inconclusive (required information size not reached).

## 세줄요약

치주염 기왕력군의 임플란트 실패 위험은 전체 74% 증가 (위험비 RR 1.74, 95% CI 1.25–2.44, p = 0.001; >5년 RR 2.26, 10년 RR 2.61), 치주위임플란트염 (Peri-implantitis) 발생은 4배 (RR 4.09, 95% CI 1.93–8.58, p < 0.001; 10년 8.58배), 평균 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 +0.75 mm였으나 임플란트 주위 탐침깊이 (Peri-Implant Probing Depth, PIPD)는 차이 없음.

치주염 기왕력은 술전 위험평가와 장기 유지관리 (Supportive Periodontal Therapy, SPT) 계획에 반영해야 하나 무작위 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 부재로 근거확실도 (GRADE)는 실패 LOW·나머지 VERY LOW이고, TSA상 치주위임플란트염·MBL·PIPD는 필요정보량 (Required Information Size, RIS) 미달로 결론 미확정.

## Summary

This systematic review with meta-analysis and trial sequential analysis (PROSPERO CRD42022369454) pooled only prospective cohort studies — 14 reports on 12 cohorts, 1464 patients and 4425 implants (1617 periodontally healthy [PHP], 2808 periodontitis-history [PCP]) followed 2–20 years (mean 89.8 months) — to test whether a history of periodontitis at implant placement predicts implant failure and peri-implantitis. Raw failure was 4.8% (135/2808) in PCP versus 2.8% (45/1617) in PHP; pooled implant failure risk was 74% greater (RR 1.74, 95% CI 1.25–2.44, p = 0.001) and rose with follow-up duration (RR 2.26 at >5 years, RR 2.61 at 10 years), while peri-implantitis incidence was 4.09-fold greater (95% CI 1.93–8.58) and 8.58-fold greater at 10 years. Marginal bone loss was 0.75 mm greater in PCP, but peri-implant probing depth showed no between-group difference. Trial sequential analysis confirmed only the implant-failure meta-analysis as conclusive (required information size exceeded); peri-implantitis, MBL, and PIPD remained inconclusive, and GRADE rated certainty low (failure) to very low (all other outcomes) because no RCTs exist — the clinically actionable reading is that periodontitis history is a real, duration-amplifying risk factor that warrants risk assessment and intensive maintenance, while the exact magnitude for peri-implantitis and bone loss awaits more data.

## Key Contributions

- 전향 코호트만 선별한 임플란트 실패–치주염 기왕력 메타분석 + TSA — 실패는 RIS(688개) 초과로 "확정", 치주위임플란트염은 RIS(1726개) 미달로 "유의하나 미확정"을 명시적으로 분리.
- 추적기간별 위험 기울기 정량: 실패 RR 1.62(≤5년) → 2.26(>5년) → 2.61(10년), 치주위임플란트염 RR 8.58(10년) — 기왕력 피해가 시간 누적으로 증가.
- 치주위임플란트염 4배(RR 4.09)를 전향연구 5편·884개 임플란트로 확인 — 기존 우산리뷰의 "highly suggestive" 등급(OR 3.84)과 독립적으로 일치.
- MBL(유의, +0.75 mm)과 PIPD(무차이) 분리 — 기왕력의 피해는 골소실로만 나타남.
- NOS(8/12 'Good') + GRADE(실패 LOW, 그 외 VERY LOW) 병용으로 효과 크기와 근거확실도를 동시 제시; 게재본 수치 표기 불일치(≤5년 CI 오자 등) 한계 기록.

## Methodology

SR+MA with trial sequential analysis, PRISMA-reported, PECOS-framed, PROSPERO CRD42022369454. Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE (1196 records), Cochrane Library (607), EMBASE (1498), searched October 2022 with manual updates (+5 articles); 3132 records screened, 36 full texts, 14 reports included = 12 prospective cohorts (1464 patients, 4425 implants; PHP 1617 / PCP 2808; mean follow-up 89.8 months, range 2–20 years). Outcomes: ① implant failure, ② peri-implantitis incidence, ③ mean/annualized MBL, ④ mean/annualized PIPD. Random-effects pooling of log risk ratios and weighted mean differences; appraisals by Newcastle-Ottawa Scale, GRADE, and TSA (required information size), with funnel/Galbraith plots and Egger tests for publication bias.
