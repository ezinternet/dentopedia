## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-centre study (Seoul National University Dental Hospital, implants placed 2014-2019, ≥5-year follow-up): 316 tapered-straight-tapered bone-level SLA implants (Evertis, formerly Luna) in 186 patients; MBL analysed on 171 implants with panoramic radiographs.

Cumulative survival was 98.1% (6 late failures, 3.4-6.7 years after placement, mean follow-up 7.0±1.36 years); no variable was a significant survival risk factor in the mixed-effects Cox model (DM HR 4.670, p=0.059; survival 94.3% with DM vs 98.9% without). Mean MBL was 0.59±1.36 mm mesial and 0.68±1.36 mm distal; DM (coeff 0.614, p=0.026), 2-staged surgery and shallower implant placement depth were associated with more mesial MBL.

Survival was high but the study was underpowered for risk factors (wide CIs, 6 events), early failures were excluded, and MBL came from panoramic radiographs read by one examiner; DM patients warrant closer MBL monitoring.

## Summary
Single-centre retrospective cohort from the Dental Implant Center of Seoul National University Dental Hospital (implants placed January 2014 to December 2019). Included 316 bone-level, internal-connection SLA implants with a tapered-straight-tapered macro-design (Evertis, formerly Luna; Shinhung, Seoul) in 186 patients, all with ≥5 years of follow-up. Survival was defined as the implant and fixed prosthesis remaining in the mouth regardless of complications; failure was implant removal; early failures (before final prosthesis) were excluded. MBL was measured on baseline vs latest (≥5 years) digital panoramic radiographs in 171 implants.

## Key Contributions
- Long-term (≥5 years) survival and MBL data for this implant; the earlier report on the same implant (97.0% cumulative survival) included many <5-year follow-ups and had no MBL data (per the paper).
- Examines 12 candidate risk factors for late failure and, additionally, implant placement depth (IPD) for MBL.

## Methodology
Retrospective, self-described cross-sectional design. Kaplan-Meier survival; mixed-effects Cox model for failure (accounting for several implants per patient); Mann-Whitney / Kruskal-Wallis plus linear mixed-effects models with backward stepwise selection for mesial and distal MBL. Implant diameter groups narrow 3.5, standard 4.0/4.5, wide 5.0/6.0 mm; length short 7.0/8.5, standard 10.0/11.5 mm.

## Results
- 316 implants (186 patients); 6 failures at 3.4-6.7 years; cumulative survival 98.1%; mean follow-up 7.0±1.36 years. Causes: peri-implantitis (4) and fixture fracture (2).
- DM: 94.3% (3/53 failed) vs 98.9% without DM; HR 4.670 (95% CI 0.942-23.149, p=0.059). Mandible 96.8% vs maxilla 99.4% (HR 5.107, p=0.137); mandibular molar 96.2%. 2-staged 97.1% (6/205) vs 1-staged 100.0% (0/111), HR 1.661 (p=0.126). Smokers 100.0% (0/22); smoking and ≥3-unit splint HRs not estimable. No factor significant.
- MBL (171 implants): mesial 0.59±1.36 mm, distal 0.68±1.36 mm. DM mesial 1.43±2.63 vs 0.37±0.60 mm (p=0.067), distal 1.31±2.33 vs 0.52±0.92 mm (p=0.064).
- Mesial MBL model: DM coeff 0.614 (p=0.026); 2-staged surgery coeff 0.410; mesial IPD coeff -0.143 (p=0.046). Distal MBL: distal IPD coeff -0.327 (95% CI -0.467 to -0.187, p<0.001). Deeper placement was associated with less MBL.
- Univariate: 2-staged surgery higher mesial MBL (0.72±1.54 vs 0.19±0.32 mm, p=0.019); standard-length (10.0/11.5 mm) implants higher distal MBL (1.02±1.85 vs 0.43±0.75 mm, p=0.016).

## Limitations
- Retrospective single-centre cohort; selection bias and uncontrolled confounding (acknowledged by the authors).
- Six events only; very wide CIs; non-significant results do not exclude clinically relevant effects (e.g., DM HR 4.670, p=0.059).
- Inclusion required ≥5-year follow-up and early failures were excluded, so estimates apply to late failure among implants that reached that point.
- MBL from 2D panoramic radiographs, single examiner, single session; no reproducibility data in the text. Some printed CIs in the mesial MBL table do not match the stated coefficients.
- Single implant system, no comparator design; DM (53 implants) and smoker (22 implants) subgroups small.

## Clinical Implication (Korean Practice)
In this cohort the late survival of this tapered-straight-tapered implant was high (98.1%) and no host or surgical factor was a statistically significant predictor, but the numerically lower survival and greater MBL in DM patients (survival 94.3%; mesial MBL +0.614 mm in the model) support closer long-term radiographic monitoring. Deeper implant placement was associated with less MBL, and 1-staged surgery with less mesial MBL; both are observational associations from a single centre and should not be read as causal.
