## Three-line Summary

Nationwide Korean NHIS cohort (implants placed July 2014 to December 2019 in patients aged 65 or older, follow-up to December 2020; mean 2.84±1.25 years): 2,354,706 implants in 1,386,570 patients, failure defined by claim-based implant removal procedures and peri-implantitis treatments after prosthesis delivery.

Late implant loss (implant removal) was 12,412 of 2,354,706 implants (0.53%); relative to sandblasted, large-grit, acid-etched (SA) surfaces, adjusted HR for removal was 1.841 for resorbable blast media (RBM) and 1.424 for hydroxyapatite (HA) (p<0.0001), while abutment type was not associated with removal (p=0.653) and peri-implantitis treatment did not differ significantly by surface (p=0.0587).

All surface and abutment groups had a low incidence (highest 3.884 per 1000 implant-years, RBM with one-piece abutment); the study is observational and claim-based, lacks bone, smoking, periodontitis and brand data, and follow-up was at most about 6 years.

## 세줄요약

한국 NHIS 전국 코호트 (2014년 7월~2019년 12월 임플란트 식립, 65세 이상, 2020년 12월까지 추적, 평균 2.84±1.25년): 환자 1,386,570명의 임플란트 2,354,706개; 보철 장착 후 임플란트 제거 청구와 임플란트주위염 (peri-implantitis) 치료 청구를 실패로 정의했다.

후기 임플란트 소실 (제거)은 12,412개 (0.53%)였고, SA 표면 대비 보정 위험비 (hazard ratio, HR)는 흡수성 블라스팅 (resorbable blast media, RBM) 1.841, 수산화인회석 코팅 (hydroxyapatite, HA) 1.424 (p<0.0001)였으며, 지대주 유형은 제거 위험과 무관 (p=0.653), 임플란트주위염 치료는 표면 간 유의차가 없었다 (p=0.0587).

모든 표면·지대주 군의 발생률은 낮았고 (최고 RBM·일체형 지대주 1000 임플란트-년당 3.884), 관찰 연구이며 청구 기반이고 골 상태·흡연·치주염·브랜드 정보가 없고 추적이 최대 약 6년이라는 한계가 있다.

## Summary

This observational cohort used the Korean National Health Insurance Service (NHIS) and National Health Screening databases, which cover the Korean population (about 50 million enrollees). Patient nationality or ethnicity is not stated as a variable in the text; the Korean setting is implied by the data source (NHIS enrollment is described as mandatory for all citizens). Included were implants placed under NHIS implant coverage from July 2014 to December 2019 in patients aged 65 or older, followed from restoration delivery to December 2020. After exclusions the cohort was 2,354,706 implants in 1,386,570 patients (mean age 72.11±5.23 years; 48.99% men). Surfaces were SA (86.7%), RBM (9.52%) and HA (3.78%); abutments were two-piece straight (79.9%), one-piece straight (13.46%) and two-piece angled (6.63%).

Failure is claim-based. Late implant loss was any implant removal procedure after prosthesis delivery: simple removal (mobile implant, taken as osseointegration loss) or complex removal (fracture, nerve damage, peri-implantitis without mobility). Peri-implantitis treatment was peri-implant osteoplasty or surface decontamination. Implants with early failure before restoration were excluded, so early failure was not measured.

Over a mean 2.84 years, implant complication treatments occurred in 14,440 implants (0.61%): removal 12,412 (0.53%; simple 8,770, complex 3,642) and peri-implantitis treatment 2,137 (0.09%). Fully adjusted (Model 4) HRs for removal versus SA were 1.841 for RBM (simple removal 2.365) and 1.424 for HA (simple removal 1.484), all p<0.0001, whereas RBM had a lower HR for complex removal. Abutment type was not associated with overall removal (p=0.653), though one-piece abutments showed more simple and fewer complex removals. The combination RBM plus one-piece abutment had the highest incidence (3.884 per 1000 implant-years) and HR 1.916 (1.734-2.117) versus SA plus two-piece.

## Key Contributions

- Nationwide Korean real-world data (2.35 million implants) comparing implant surfaces and abutment structures in older adults.
- SA surfaces had the lowest hazard of late removal; RBM and HA had 1.8- and 1.4-fold higher HR, yet absolute incidence stayed below 1% crude.
- Abutment type did not change overall removal risk; simple versus complex removal separated osseointegration-type loss from mechanical or other causes.

## Methodology

- Retrospective claim-based cohort (STROBE; IRB-exempt de-identified data), NHIS 2014-2019 placements, follow-up to December 2020, age 65 or older, insurance-covered implants (max two per lifetime, cement-retained non-precious metal-ceramic crowns).
- Exclusions: failure before restoration, custom CAD/CAM abutments, incomplete restoration, oral cancer.
- Kaplan-Meier with log-rank; Cox models 1-4 adjusted stepwise for sex, age, income, residence, disability, comorbidities and tooth type; incidence per 1000 implant-years. Denominator is implants.

## Limitations

- Claim codes define failure (removal, treatment), not clinical or radiographic status; peri-implantitis only counted when treated.
- No bone, grafting, implant dimension or design, splinting, smoking or periodontitis data; no brand data, so surface preparation differences between manufacturers are not captured.
- Elderly (65 or older) insured patients only; mean follow-up 2.84 years (up to about 6 years); early failure excluded and not reported.
- Observational; residual confounding likely. Text inconsistencies: abutment-type p for overall complications 0.9811 in text versus 0.9542 in the PubMed abstract; HA peri-implantitis HR CI excludes 1 yet surface p is 0.0587.
