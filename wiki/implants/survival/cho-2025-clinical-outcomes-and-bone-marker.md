## Three-line Summary

Single-center prospective study at Yonsei University Dental Hospital (Seoul, Korea; April 2022 - May 2024) of 45 bone-level HEPES-surface implants in 36 postmenopausal women (mean age 68 years), split by lowest T-score into Group A (T-score of -2.0 or higher; 21 implants, 17 patients) and Group B (below -2.0; 24 implants, 19 patients), with one year of follow-up.

Implant survival was 100% with no failures (PubMed abstract; the full text reports no failures and ISQ never below 60), so early-versus-late failure timing cannot be analysed; mean marginal bone loss was 0.25-0.37 mm by group and jaw (no significant differences), implant stability stayed above 70 (IST), and change in T-score was -0.16 +/- 0.35 (A) versus -0.01 +/- 0.31 (B), p=0.244.

Authors call it exploratory: small sample, no multivariable adjustment, severe osteoporosis (T-score below -3) and grafted sites excluded, no antiresorptive therapy started during the year, Group B alone received vitamin D; patient nationality is not stated in the text and Korean origin is implied only by the setting.

## 세줄요약

한국 연세대학교치과병원에서 2022년 4월-2024년 5월 시행한 단일기관 전향적 연구: 폐경 후 여성 36명(평균 68세)에게 골수준형 HEPES 표면 임플란트 45개를 식립하고, 최저 T-score 기준으로 A군(-2.0 이상; 17명 21개)과 B군(-2.0 미만; 19명 24개)으로 나누어 1년간 추적했다.

임플란트 생존율은 100%로 실패가 없었으므로(PubMed 초록 기준; 본문도 실패 없음, ISQ 60 미만 없음) 조기 대 후기 실패 시점 분석은 불가하며, 평균 변연골 소실 (marginal bone loss, MBL)은 군·악골별 0.25-0.37 mm로 유의차가 없었고 임플란트 안정성 (IST)은 70 이상을 유지했으며 T-score 변화는 A군 -0.16 +/- 0.35, B군 -0.01 +/- 0.31 (p=0.244)였다.

저자들은 탐색적 연구라고 밝혔다: 소규모 표본, 다변량 보정 없음, 중증 골다공증(T-score -3 미만)과 골이식 필요 부위 제외, 1년간 골흡수억제제 (antiresorptive drug, ARD) 미시작, B군만 비타민 D 보충; 환자 국적은 본문에 명시되지 않았고 한국인이라는 점은 연구 장소로부터 추정될 뿐이다.

## Summary

A single-center prospective study from the Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Yonsei University Dental Hospital (Seoul, Korea), enrolled postmenopausal women needing implants between April 2022 and May 2024 and followed them for one year with endocrinology co-management. Patients were split by their lowest T-score (hip, spine, femoral neck) into Group A (-2.0 or higher) and Group B (below -2.0). The -2.0 threshold approximated the cohort median and is not a WHO class. Patient nationality or ethnicity is not stated in the text; the Korean setting and Korean epidemiological citations are the only indication.

Forty-five Osstem bone-level HEPES-surface implants were placed in 36 patients (mean age 68): Group A 21 implants in 17 patients, Group B 24 implants in 19 patients. All were non-smokers with single crowns. Survival was 100% with no failures reported (stated in the PubMed abstract; the text reports ISQ never below 60), so there is no early-versus-late failure information and no risk-factor effect sizes.

Mean MBL at one year was 0.25 mm (maxilla) and 0.30 mm (mandible) in Group A and 0.37 mm and 0.27 mm in Group B, with no significant differences. Stability stayed above 70 IST in all subgroups, higher in the mandible. T-score change was -0.16 +/- 0.35 (A) versus -0.01 +/- 0.31 (B), p=0.244; vitamin D rose more in Group B (p=0.015, preoperative supplementation) and P1NP fell more (p=0.033). No vertebral fractures appeared.

## Key Contributions

- Prospective Korean data pairing implant outcomes with BMD and bone turnover markers in postmenopausal women, with no antiresorptive therapy started during the year.
- Zero failures and low MBL over one year in a cohort with mean age 68 and lower T-scores.
- Shows no short-term systemic deterioration of BMD associated with implant treatment.

## Methodology

- Single-center prospective, STROBE, registered KCT0007100; 43 screened, 6 excluded (T-score below -3 or graft needed), 37 enrolled, 36 analysed.
- One surgeon, one prosthodontist; Osstem SOI HEPES implants; second stage at 3 months; nine visits.
- MBL on periapical radiographs (mesial/distal) and CBCT (buccal/lingual), single examiner, ICC 0.99, Dahlberg error below 0.03 mm.
- ISQ/IST, PPD, BOP, plaque; DXA BMD; CTx, P1NP, PTH, 25(OH) vitamin D. Paired tests, p<0.05; no multivariable models.

## Limitations

- Small exploratory sample; one-year follow-up; no adjustment; severe osteoporosis and grafted sites excluded; no ARD therapy, so not applicable to bisphosphonate-treated patients.
- Group B alone received vitamin D; surgical and prosthetic variables not standardized.
- Text inconsistencies: 37 enrolled versus 36 analysed unexplained; MBL 0.25-0.37 mm by cell versus 0.54 mm average quoted; survival 100% appears only in the abstract.
