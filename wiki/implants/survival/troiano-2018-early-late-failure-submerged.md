## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis with trial sequential analysis (TSA) of 11 prospective randomized and non-randomized controlled studies directly comparing submerged vs non-submerged implant healing without immediate loading, searched in PubMed, Scopus, Embase and Web of Science.

Non-submerged healing carried a small but higher rate of early implant failure (+2%); late failure (after 6 months from placement) did not differ between protocols, but TSA judged the power of that evidence not high; at 1 year from loading, marginal bone level appeared slightly better preserved with non-submerged healing, though with a very small effect size (0.13 mm).

The wiki's only pooled and trial-sequenced estimate for the healing-protocol contrast, but the early-failure penalty is an absolute 2% difference, immediate-loading studies were excluded by design, and the MBL signal is explicitly underpowered.

## Summary

This systematic review, meta-analysis and trial sequential analysis (Troiano, Lo Russo, Canullo, Ciavarella, Lo Muzio, Laino — University of Foggia / University of Campania "Luigi Vanvitelli", *J Clin Periodontol* 2018;45(5):613-623) pooled 11 prospective randomized and non-randomized controlled studies that directly compared submerged with non-submerged implant healing while excluding immediate loading, searching PubMed, Scopus, Embase and Web of Science, appraising risk of bias with the Cochrane Collaboration Tool for Randomized Clinical Trials. Non-submerged healing carried a small but higher rate of early implant failure (+2%), whereas late failure — defined here as failure more than 6 months after placement — did not differ between protocols, though trial sequential analysis judged the power of that evidence not to be high. Against that, at 1 year from loading non-submerged healing appeared to preserve marginal bone *better* (effect size 0.13 mm), an explicitly underpowered secondary result. The clinically honest reading is that this paper supplies a pooled early-failure penalty on the exposed-protocol arm of very small absolute size, and that its own bone-level and late-failure findings do not follow that direction — it should not be cited as "submerged is better" in full.

## Key Contributions

- The **only pooled and trial-sequenced estimate in this wiki of the submerged vs non-submerged contrast** on implant failure — the SR stratum that [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]] explicitly named as missing ("the baseline SR (Troiano 2018) is not held because it has no open access").
- **Assigns the penalty to a specific arm**: non-submerged healing, +2% early failure. [[implants/survival/giok-2026-factors-implant-failure-umbrella-review]] grades the contrast as high-certainty from RCTs but, being abstract-only, never states which arm carries the risk.
- **Separates early from late failure by an explicit time boundary** (<6 months vs >6 months from placement) — definitional clarity the retrospective cohorts held here lack (Guarnieri: "before or at abutment connection"; Wåhlberg: any event within year 1).
- **Reports a countervailing MBL signal and downgrades it honestly** — non-submerged preserved marginal bone better (0.13 mm) with power the authors describe as low.
- **Uses trial sequential analysis to qualify its own null result** on late failure rather than reporting "no difference" as settled.

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR + MA + trial sequential analysis (TSA).
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, EMBASE, Web of Science.
- **Eligibility**: prospective randomized **and** non-randomized controlled studies with a direct submerged vs non-submerged comparison, **immediate loading excluded**.
- **n**: 11 studies.
- **Outcomes**: early failure (before 6 months from placement), late failure (after 6 months), MBL at 1 year from implant load.
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane Collaboration Tool for Randomized Clinical Trials. Pooling randomized and non-randomized trials without stratification is implied by the eligibility wording (**inference** — the full text is not available to confirm).
- **Not recoverable from the abstract**: search strings and last search date, per-study n, pooled effect measure and CI for the early-failure comparison, heterogeneity (I²), publication-bias testing, risk-of-bias results.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Studies included | 11 |
| Early failure (<6 months from placement) | Small higher rate (**2%**) with non-submerged healing; reported as a rate difference, no OR/RR or CI in the abstract |
| Late failure (>6 months from placement) | No difference between protocols |
| Late-failure evidence power (TSA) | **Not high** — the null is explicitly down-weighted |
| MBL at 1 year from loading | Non-submerged **may better preserve** marginal bone; effect size **0.13 mm** (very small) |
| MBL evidence power | **Low** (authors' own characterization) |
| Immediate loading | Excluded by eligibility — no data |
| Net clinical message | Non-submerged implants: +2% early-failure risk; MBL trend favours non-submerged but underpowered |

No p-values, confidence intervals or heterogeneity statistics appear in the abstract.
