## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 9 studies (18,171 implants, search July 2014) pooling odds ratios for patient-level and implant-level predictors of early implant failure across diverse clinical settings.

Smoking, implant length under 10 mm and maxillary location were statistically significant risk factors; implant diameter was analysed but not reported as significant.

This meta-analysis provides the empirical foundation for the widely cited 2–6% early failure rate range and is frequently referenced for risk stratification prior to implant planning (e.g., Krisam 2019).

## One-line Summary

Meta-analysis of 9 studies (18,171 implants): smoking (OR 1.7, 1.3–2.3), implants shorter than 10 mm (OR 1.6, 1.2–2.2) and maxillary placement (OR 1.3, 1.0–1.6) were the main risk factors for early implant failure (abstract only).

## 한줄요약

9편(임플란트 18,171개) 메타분석에서 흡연(OR 1.7, 1.3–2.3), 10 mm 미만 짧은 임플란트(OR 1.6, 1.2–2.2), 상악 식립(OR 1.3, 1.0–1.6)이 조기 실패의 주요 위험인자였다(초록만).

## Summary

This meta-analysis pooled odds ratios for patient- and implant-related risk factors of early implant failure. Nine studies were included after independent selection and quality scoring (search July 2014), totalling 18,171 implants. Smoking, implants under 10 mm and maxillary location were significant risk factors; implant diameter was analysed but is not reported as significant in the abstract. It is one of the sources behind the 2–6% early-failure range cited by Krisam 2019.

## Key Contributions

- Pooled early-failure ORs: smoking 1.7, short (<10 mm) 1.6, maxilla 1.3.
- Effect sizes are modest compared with single-cohort estimates (e.g., Krisam short-implant OR 5.8), illustrating small-study inflation.
- Large pooled denominators by factor (10,921 for smoking to 16,711 for location).

## Methodology

Meta-analysis; databases searched July 2014; study selection by 2 independent authors with predefined criteria; quality assessment by 3 authors; meta-analysis of estimated ORs for smoking, implant diameter, length and location.

## Results

Nine studies, 18,171 implants (smoking 10,921; diameter 15,260; length 16,075; location 16,711). Smoking OR 1.7 (1.3–2.3); length <10 mm OR 1.6 (1.2–2.2); maxilla OR 1.3 (1.0–1.6). Diameter result not significant per abstract wording.
