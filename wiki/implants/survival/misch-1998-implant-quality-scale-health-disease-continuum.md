## Three-line Summary

Clinical assessment review (Oral Health, 1998) introducing an implant quality scale on a health-to-disease continuum.

Pain and mobility are the primary criteria; any pain, vertical mobility, uncontrolled progressive bone loss or generalized periradiolucency warrants removal.

No validation or reliability data appear in the abstract, so cite it as a clinical framework.

## 세줄요약

임상 평가 리뷰 (Oral Health, 1998) — 임플란트의 건강-질환 연속체를 나타내는 임플란트 품질 척도.

통증과 동요도가 주요 기준이며, 통증·수직 동요·조절되지 않는 골소실·광범위 방사선 투과상은 제거를 고려.

검증 자료가 초록에 없으므로 임상 틀로만 인용할 것.

## Summary

The quality scale turns implant status into a graded clinical judgment with a treatment prognosis attached, rather than a yes/no outcome. It is the clinical companion to the 2008 Pisa definitions.

## Key Contributions

- Health-to-disease grading for implants.
- Removal criteria.

## Methodology

Clinical review; scale adapted from James and modified by Misch.

## Results

Qualitative; no numeric cut-offs in the abstract.
