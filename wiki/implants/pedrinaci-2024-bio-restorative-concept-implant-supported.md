## Three-line Summary

Narrative/concept review (Pedrinaci et al. 2024, J Esthet Restor Dent) proposing the "bio-restorative concept" for implant-supported restorations (ISR): digitally integrate periodontal, surgical, and prosthetic variables to align biologic and restorative goals.

The framework assesses anatomical site characteristics and restorative variables together—digital tooth arrangement, soft/hard tissue and peri-implant phenotype, implant design/3D position, supra-platform components, and phenotype-respecting or modifying surgical planning—within a virtual workflow.

Clinical implication: integrated digital planning may improve predictability and long-term esthetic/functional/biologic outcomes of ISRs, but the paper provides a conceptual synthesis rather than comparative clinical-effectiveness data.

## 세줄요약

개념적 리뷰(Pedrinaci 2024, J Esthet Restor Dent) — 임플란트 지지 수복(ISR)에서 치주·외과·보철 요소를 디지털 워크플로로 통합하는 "bio-restorative concept" 제안.

디지털 치아 배열, 연·경조직/임플란트 주위 표현형(peri-implant phenotype), 임플란트 설계·3차원 위치, 상부구조·출현윤곽, 표현형 보존/개선 수술 계획을 가상 환경에서 동시에 평가하는 계획 프레임을 제시한다.

임상적 의미: 생물학적·보철적 목표를 정렬해 ISR 예측성과 장기 결과 향상에 기여할 수 있으나, 근거는 개념 종합이며 임상 비교 효과크기는 제시되지 않는다.

## Summary

Pedrinaci et al. present the bio-restorative concept as an evolution beyond purely surgically driven or prosthetically driven implant planning. By combining digital diagnostic tools (scans/DICOM superimposition, virtual tooth arrangement) with assessment of peri-implant phenotype and restorative determinants, the approach aims to select implant and supra-platform designs, 3D positions, and surgical strategies that support long-term biologic, functional, and esthetic success of implant-supported restorations. The article details key elements—digital tooth arrangement, soft/hard tissue assessment, implant selection, emergence-profile considerations, and phenotype management—as an integrated planning checklist.

## Key Contributions
- Formalized the **bio-restorative concept** linking biologic phenotype/surgical planning with prosthetic/restorative design in a digital environment.
- Emphasized simultaneous evaluation of anatomical site characteristics and restorative variables before execution.
- Highlighted peri-implant phenotype components and their relevance to implant/restoration planning and long-term outcomes.

## Methodology
- **Type**: Narrative review / conceptual framework article.
- **Evidence form**: Synthesis of concepts, clinical considerations, and cited literature; no primary data or meta-analytic pooling.

## Results
- No quantitative clinical endpoints reported; provides planning principles and a structured checklist of key elements for ISR planning.
- Core message: digital integration of biologic and restorative planning can enhance predictability, efficiency, and patient-centered outcomes.
