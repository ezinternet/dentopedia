## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (UCLA Ogawa group, International Journal of Implant Dentistry 2024): frames the "biological dilemma of osteoblast kinetics" — the Runx2-mediated inverse proliferation–differentiation correlation on microrough titanium surfaces — as the fundamental unsolved problem limiting osseointegration, and reviews three non-coating surface strategies to overcome it.

Three strategies reviewed: (1) nanonodular texturing (TiO₂ sputtered soft-edge surfaces) — 3–5× osteoblast attachment, 2× proliferation, 3-fold greater biomechanical osseointegration in rat femur; (2) meso-scale texturing at scale between micro and macro; (3) UV photofunctionalization (UV-PF) — removes the hydrocarbon pellicle (40–75% surface carbon on aged Ti), restores near-zero contact angle, enhances recruitment/attachment/spreading without sacrificing differentiation advantage of microrough surfaces.

UV-PF's distinctive contribution among the three: it is a physicochemical surface reset that is surface-agnostic and compatible with any topography — a time-based problem (biological aging) solved by a time-triggered intervention.

## 세줄요약

서사 리뷰 (UCLA Ogawa 그룹, Int J Implant Dent 2024): 거친 티타늄 표면의 골아세포 "생물학적 딜레마" — Runx2 매개 증식·분화 역상관 — 를 임플란트 골유합 한계의 근본 미해결 문제로 프레임하고, 이를 극복하는 3가지 비코팅 표면 전략을 종합.

3전략: ①나노결절 텍스처링 (TiO₂ 스퍼터, soft-edge) — 골아세포 부착 3–5×·증식 2×·생체역학 골유합 3배 향상(쥐 대퇴골); ②메소 텍스처링 (마이크로·매크로 중간 스케일); ③자외선 광기능화 (Ultraviolet Photofunctionalization, UV-PF) — 탄화수소 펠리클(노화 티타늄 표면 탄소 40–75%) 제거, 접촉각 ~0° 회복, 초기 모집·부착·증식 향상.

UV-PF의 차별 기여: 표면 형태에 구애받지 않는 "물리화학적 표면 초기화" — 생물학적 노화(시간적 문제)를 처리 개입(시간 기반)으로 해결하며, 나노결절 및 메소 텍스처링과 조합 가능.

## Summary

The clinical observation that osseointegration plateaus at 45–65% BIC — far below the theoretical 100% — reflects an unresolved surface science problem: microrough surfaces that promote osteoblast differentiation simultaneously suppress osteoblast proliferation via a Runx2-mediated inverse kinetic relationship (the "biological dilemma"). This review argues that three non-coating surface strategies overcome this dilemma without compromising implant integrity. Nanonodular texturing converts knife-edge microrough features to soft-edge TiO₂ nano-nodules, achieving 3–5× attachment at 6 h, 2× proliferation, and 3-fold greater biomechanical osseointegration in rat femur studies, while maintaining or amplifying differentiation. Meso-scale texturing operates at a length scale between micro (0.5–5 µm) and macro (>10 µm), modulating cell spreading and migration in ways pure microroughness cannot. UV photofunctionalization addresses a different axis entirely: it removes the hydrocarbon pellicle that accumulates on commercially stored titanium (40–75% surface carbon), which suppresses initial cell recruitment before topographic cues can operate. UV-PF's key advantage is surface-agnosticism — it works as a time-triggered physicochemical reset on top of any topography, amplifying rather than competing with nanonodular or meso strategies.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces the "biological dilemma of osteoblast kinetics" (Runx2-mediated proliferation–differentiation inverse correlation) as the conceptual framework for why microrough surfaces underperform.
- Establishes nanonodular texturing as the most evidence-rich alternative (in vitro + in vivo with quantified biomechanical endpoints).
- Positions UV-PF as a surface-agnostic physicochemical reset operating on the time axis (biological aging) rather than the topographic axis — complementary to, not competing with, nanonodular/meso strategies.
- Proposes the combination of UV-PF + nanonodular texturing as an untested but mechanistically justified additive approach.
- Explicitly frames the BIC plateau (45–65%) as the clinical manifestation of the biological dilemma — ties surface science to measurable clinical outcome.

## Methodology

Narrative review; no systematic search, no PRISMA. Sources: in vitro cell studies (rat osteoblast, human MG63, BMSCs), in vivo animal (rat femur, rabbit), select clinical reports. Primary focus: surface science ↔ osteoblast kinetics. Institution: Weintraub Center for Reconstructive Biotechnology, UCLA School of Dentistry (Ogawa group). Published: International Journal of Implant Dentistry, 10(1), art. 35, July 2024.

## Results

**Nanonodular texturing (vs. knife-edge microrough):**
- Osteoblast attachment at 6 h: 3–5× higher
- Proliferation (days 2–6): ~2× higher
- Differentiation (osteogenic gene expression, calcium deposition): maintained or amplified
- Biomechanical osseointegration (rat femur pushout): 3-fold greater

**UV photofunctionalization:**
- Removes hydrocarbon pellicle (40–75% surface carbon on aged Ti)
- Restores superhydrophilicity (contact angle near 0°)
- Enhances osteoblast recruitment, attachment, spreading, and proliferation on microrough surfaces
- Maintains or amplifies differentiation advantage of microrough surfaces
- Operates on top of any topography (surface-agnostic)

**Meso-scale texturing:** modulates cell spreading and migration at length scale between micro (0.5–5 µm) and macro (>10 µm); details in referenced Ogawa group papers.
