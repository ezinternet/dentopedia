## Three-line Summary

Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR; 77 studies from 452 records; PubMed/Scopus/WoS/Google Scholar through July 2025): 31 studies on decontamination + 46 on osseointegration-related outcomes in in vitro/ex vivo/animal preclinical settings.

CAP demonstrated consistent antibacterial effects (RONS-mediated biofilm disruption) and osseointegration-associated improvements (surface energy increase up to 6×, BIC gains up to >300% in animal models); greatest benefit in early healing (4 weeks, diminishing by 8 weeks); combined CAP + mechanical debridement superior to either alone.

Evidence is highly heterogeneous (protocols, devices, gases) and entirely preclinical — biological plausibility for peri-implantitis established, but clinical effectiveness unproven; no standardized protocol or RCT evidence exists.

## 세줄요약

범위형 체계적 고찰 (PRISMA-ScR, 77편, 2025년 7월까지): 탈오염 31편 + 골유합 46편, 모두 in vitro/ex vivo/동물 전임상 근거.

냉각대기압 플라즈마 (Cold Atmospheric Plasma, CAP)는 RONS 매개 바이오필름 파괴 + 표면에너지 최대 6배 증가 + 동물모델 BIC 최대 300% 이상 향상 등 일관된 효과를 보였으며, 치유 초기(4주)에 가장 뚜렷하고 8주에 감소하는 시간 의존적 패턴; CAP + 기계적 디브리드먼트 병용 > 단독.

근거는 매우 이질적이고 전임상에 국한 — 플라이임플란타이티스(Peri-implantitis) 관리 보조전략으로의 생물학적 타당성은 입증됐으나 임상 효과는 미검증; 표준 프로토콜과 RCT 없음.

## Summary

This PRISMA-ScR scoping review by Shahraki, Bidabadi, and Zhao (Xi'an Jiaotong University + University of Bern) is the first to integrate both decontamination (31 studies) and osseointegration (46 studies) of cold atmospheric plasma for peri-implantitis in a single synthesis, covering evidence through July 2025. From 452 initial records, 77 studies met inclusion criteria. The review found that CAP consistently reduced bacterial biofilm load through RONS generation (reactive oxygen/nitrogen species disrupting microbial cell membranes), with argon-based systems at 60–180 s and 2–5 mm distance representing the most studied protocol. Combined CAP + mechanical debridement outperformed either alone. For osseointegration, CAP enhanced surface energy (Han et al.: 6× increase), ALP expression, and BIC/BAFO in multiple animal models (Coelho: BIC >300%; Giro: BIC >100%). However, Jang et al. found the effect diminished from 4 weeks to 8 weeks, suggesting primary benefits in the early healing phase. Coaxial DBD systems provided more homogeneous surface coverage than jet systems. The review's key limitation: all evidence is preclinical, with no standardized peri-implantitis model and no clinical trials included.

## Key Contributions

- First scoping review integrating dual domains (decontamination + osseointegration) for CAP in peri-implantitis — fills gap between Alqutaibi 2023 (antibacterial only) and Barausse (osseointegration only)
- Quantitative benchmarks: surface energy 6× (Han), BIC >300% (Coelho), BIC >100% (Giro), BAFO >82% (Giro) — all animal/in vitro
- Temporal pattern identified: CAP osseointegration benefit greatest at ~4 weeks, diminishes at 8 weeks (Jang et al.)
- Establishes dual mechanism: antimicrobial (RONS) + surface wettability enhancement — both relevant to peri-implantitis cycle
- Coaxial DBD = more homogeneous than jet for clinical translation; shorter exposure needed

## Methodology

PRISMA-ScR scoping review. PCC framework: Population = peri-implant surfaces/osteoblasts/MSCs; Concept = CAP treatment; Context = preclinical only. Search: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar through July 2025. Initial 452 → 77 included (31 decontamination, 46 osseointegration). Two independent screeners. All study types: in vitro (23), ex vivo (3), animal (15), combined (8). Materials: titanium predominant (40/46 for osseointegration); surface: SLA most common.

## Results

**Decontamination (31 studies):**
- Argon gas (most common), 60–180 s exposure, 2–5 mm distance = standard experimental protocol
- CAP vs. multispecies biofilm: significant CFU reduction with 1–5 min treatment (Panariello et al.)
- Combined CAP + mechanical > monotherapy; mature biofilms (≥72h) more resistant
- Surface microstructure preserved; antifungal activity vs. Candida superior to chlorhexidine/NaOCl

**Osseointegration (46 studies):**
- Han et al.: 6× surface energy increase; fibronectin adsorption enhanced → osteoblast attachment
- Giro et al. (canine, CaP-coated, argon): BIC >100%, BAFO >82% at 3 weeks
- Coelho et al. (canine, rough Ti, argon): BIC >300%, BAFO >30%
- Zheng et al.: increased surface oxygen, cell attachment, osteogenic differentiation
- Jang et al.: BIC/bone volume ↑ at 4 weeks; diminished at 8 weeks
- Teixeira et al.: 20–60 s → increased surface energy, better biomechanical fixation
- ALP, osteocalcin elevated; IL-1β, IL-17 reduced (Zhou et al.)

**Device/gas comparisons:**
- Argon > air for hydrophilicity; oxygen plasma (Smeets) = superior for zirconia cell proliferation
- Coaxial DBD = homogeneous distribution, effective at 30 s; jet = needs longer exposure, risks topography change
