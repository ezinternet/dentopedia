## Three-line Summary

In vivo rabbit femur transaxial-implant study (36 rabbits, Ti6Al4V grit-blasted+acid-etched screw implants, 6-week endpoint) comparing untreated vs 30-second DBD-plasma-treated implants stored in vacuum-sealed vials, contralateral placement, stratified by sex.

In males, DBD plasma treatment significantly increased bone-to-implant contact (micro-CT), adjacent bone volume, mechanical peak torque, and torsional energy versus untreated contralateral implants; in females, plasma did not significantly increase BIC (baseline osseointegration already high), though it did increase torsional stiffness.

DBD plasma restores surface wettability (contact angle 0° vs ~80° untreated) without altering roughness or elemental composition, and its osseointegration benefit appears to depend on baseline bone-forming capacity — most consequential in male/compromised-bone scenarios where endogenous osteogenic signaling has not already reached ceiling.

## 세줄요약

토끼 대퇴골 경축 임플란트 연구(36마리, Ti6Al4V 그릿블라스팅+산부식 나사형 임플란트, 6주 종료시점) — 무처리 vs 진공 밀봉 보관 후 식립 직전 30초 유전체 장벽 방전(Dielectric Barrier Discharge, DBD) 플라즈마 처리군을 반대측 대조로 비교, 성별 층화.

수컷에서는 DBD 플라즈마 처리가 골-임플란트 접촉률(BIC, micro-CT)·인접 골량·최대 제거토크·비틀림 에너지를 유의하게 증가시켰으나, 암컷은 기저 골유착이 이미 높아 BIC 유의 증가 없음(단 비틀림 강성은 증가).

DBD 플라즈마는 거칠기·조성 변화 없이 습윤성만 회복하며(접촉각 0° vs ~80°), 그 이점은 기저 골형성능에 의존 — 내인성 골생성 신호가 이미 천장에 도달하지 않은 시나리오(남성/골질 저하)에서 가장 유의미할 가능성.

## Summary

This rabbit femur study tests a low-cost, benchtop dielectric barrier discharge (DBD) plasma device applied to vacuum-sealed titanium implants immediately before placement, and is the first in this wiki's plasma cluster to explicitly stratify osseointegration outcomes by sex. In males, 30-second DBD plasma treatment significantly increased bone-to-implant contact, adjacent bone volume, and mechanical anchorage (peak torque, torsional energy) relative to untreated contralateral implants. In females, baseline osseointegration was already high regardless of treatment, and plasma produced no significant BIC gain (though torsional stiffness increased). Companion osteoblast culture experiments showed male-derived cells produce higher osteocalcin, BMP2, and osteoprotegerin on titanium surfaces than female-derived cells, offering a cellular correlate for the sex divergence — plausibly mediated by differential estrogen signaling. Surface characterization confirmed DBD plasma restores wettability (contact angle 0° vs ~80° untreated) without altering roughness or elemental composition, consistent with the "reactivation, not new topography" mechanism seen across this wiki's plasma literature.

## Key Contributions

- First sex-stratified in-vivo evaluation of plasma-treated implant osseointegration in this wiki's plasma cluster.
- Low-cost, reusable benchtop DBD device tested on vacuum-sealed (not freshly-manufactured) implants — relevant to shelf-life/aging-reversal use cases.
- Proposes a biological-ceiling explanation: plasma's local wettability effect is masked when systemic (sex-hormone-driven) osteogenic capacity is already high.

## Methodology

Animal in-vivo (36 rabbits, 20 male/16 female, bilateral femur transaxial implants) plus companion in-vitro calvarial-osteoblast assays. 30-second DBD plasma (13 kV, 700 kHz) applied to vacuum-sealed Ti6Al4V GB+AE screw implants immediately before placement; 6-week endpoint; micro-CT, calcified histology, and destructive mechanical torque-to-failure testing.

## Results

| Outcome | Males (plasma vs untreated) | Females (plasma vs untreated) |
|---|---|---|
| BIC (micro-CT) | Significantly higher | No significant difference (high baseline) |
| Adjacent bone volume | Significantly higher | Increased regardless of treatment |
| Peak torque / torsional energy | Significantly higher | Not significantly higher |
| Torsional stiffness | No significant change | Significantly higher |
| Surface wettability | 0° vs ~80° contact angle (both sexes) |
| Roughness / composition | Unchanged by DBD treatment |
