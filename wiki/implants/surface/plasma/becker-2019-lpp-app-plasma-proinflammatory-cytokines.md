## Three-line Summary

In vitro study: 30 Camlog SCREW-LINE Promote plus implants (grade 4 Ti, abrasive-blasted acid-etched) — 10 untreated control, 10 atmospheric-pressure plasma (APP, 1-min plasma jet), 10 low-pressure plasma (LPP, 15-min vacuum chamber) — immersed in whole blood from a single healthy donor, IL1-β/TNF-α mRNA by RT-qPCR at 1/8/24 h.

Control implants triggered a significant IL1-β/TNF-α rise through 8 h that fell by 24 h; both plasma arms significantly suppressed both cytokines at 8 h (APP and LPP, P<0.01–0.05; LPP also significant at 1 h), but **reversed at 24 h** — both APP and LPP showed a significant *increase* in IL1-β (P<0.05) and TNF-α (P<0.001) at that timepoint.

Both plasma modalities are immunologically favorable in the early window (1–8 h) versus untreated titanium, but the paper reports no direct LPP-vs-APP statistical contrast (each compared only to control), and the whole study rests on a **single blood donor** — a major generalizability limit the abstract alone doesn't convey.

## Summary

This is the wiki plasma cluster's first paper to directly juxtapose low-pressure (vacuum-type) and atmospheric-pressure plasma on the same implant material with the same readout, and — now that the full text has been retrieved — the most mechanistically detailed data point on the "does vacuum/low-pressure processing make sense" question the cluster previously lacked. The device parameters answer the original "why does a vacuum protocol need longer than 60 s" question directly: the atmospheric-pressure plasma jet (Plasmatreat FG 5001) was fixed at **1 minute**, while the low-pressure vacuum-chamber system (Diener Femto PCCE) required **15 minutes** — a 15× difference in exposure time between the two device classes, in this specific study, to reach a comparable immunomodulatory direction. This is consistent with the broader mechanistic picture (vacuum/low-pressure discharge is gentler/more diffuse per unit time than a focused atmospheric jet) but the study cannot isolate "vacuum vs atmospheric" as a pure physics variable from "1 min vs 15 min" as a dose variable — the two are fully confounded by device choice in this design.

The results are also more nuanced than the abstract's headline. Both plasma arms suppress IL1-β/TNF-α significantly in the early window (LPP already at 1 h; both at 8 h), matching the "plasma = anti-inflammatory, good for early osseointegration" framing the authors emphasize. But by 24 h, **both plasma arms reverse** to a significant cytokine *increase*, while the control (elevated through 8 h) has by then declined — a time-dependent, non-monotonic pattern the abstract and conclusion do not foreground, echoing the cluster's broader "effect is early and transient, dose/time matters" theme (see [[implants/surface/plasma/lee-2022-plasma-contaminated-zirconia-gingivalis-osteoblast]]'s 60 s-good/300s-bad dose finding, a different endpoint but the same non-monotonicity lesson).

## Key Contributions

- First wiki-held head-to-head test of LPP vs APP on titanium dental implants (same material, same assay), with full device parameters now available.
- Adds a cytokine/immunomodulation endpoint (IL1-β, TNF-α) to a cluster otherwise built on bone-contact and surface-physics endpoints.
- **Directly quantifies the vacuum-vs-atmospheric time asymmetry**: APP 1 min vs LPP 15 min to reach comparable early cytokine suppression — the wiki's first concrete answer to "why would a chairside vacuum protocol take longer than a 60-second jet treatment."
- Surfaces a 24-h reversal (both plasma arms swing to significantly *increased* cytokine expression) not visible from the abstract alone — a caution against reading this paper as unconditionally "plasma = anti-inflammatory."

## Methodology

- **Implants:** Camlog SCREW-LINE Promote plus, grade 4 Ti, abrasive-blasted acid-etched, 9 mm × 4.3 mm. 30 total: 10 control, 10 APP, 10 LPP.
- **Blood:** 65 mL from one healthy 39-year-old male donor, split into 30 × 2 mL portions.
- **APP:** Plasmatreat FG 5001 plasma jet (N₂), 300 V/19 kHz/800 W, nozzle 4 mm from implant, 4 m/s substrate velocity → **1-minute treatment**.
- **LPP:** Diener Femto PCCE vacuum chamber (Ar 100%), 200 W, 50 Pa → **15-minute treatment**.
- Sampling at 1/8/24 h; RT-qPCR (IL1-β, TNF-α) normalized to ACTB/18S rRNA/β2M via ΔΔCt, ≥2-fold change = significant.
- One-way ANOVA + Bonferroni post hoc (SPSS 22.0), P≤0.05.

## Results

**IL1-β:** control ↑ significantly by 8 h (P<0.01) then ↓ by 24 h (P<0.001); APP/LPP ↓ significantly at 8 h (P<0.01, LPP also at 1 h P<0.05), then both **↑ significantly at 24 h (P<0.05)**.

**TNF-α:** control ↑ significantly from 1 h through 8 h (P<0.001), slight decline by 24 h but still elevated; APP/LPP ↓ significantly at 8 h (P<0.05, LPP also at 1 h), then both **↑ significantly at 24 h (P<0.001)**.

No direct LPP-vs-APP statistical test reported (each vs control only).

**Boundary (living-document):** full text now retrieved (upgraded 2026-08-10 from a paywalled abstract-only ingest). **Single blood donor (n=1 human)** — authors themselves flag this and call for ≥3 donors in follow-up; not a powered comparative trial. 24-h study window (ex vivo blood immune reactivity degrades beyond that without nutrient supply). APP-1-min vs LPP-15-min device parameters are fully confounded with "atmospheric vs vacuum" as a mechanism variable — this study cannot separate the two. The 24-h cytokine-increase reversal is present in the data but not mechanistically explained by the authors. In vitro ex vivo whole-blood model, not a clinical or bone-contact osseointegration outcome.
