## Three-line Summary

In vitro + in vivo beagle study comparing chairside non-thermal atmospheric plasma (NTAP)-functionalized titanium against commercial Straumann SLActive hydrophilic implant surface.

NTAP achieved hydrophilicity and early osseointegration (BIC) statistically comparable to SLActive across all timepoints tested, while eliminating SLActive's time-dependent biological aging limitation.

Chairside NTAP is a cost-effective, aging-free alternative to SLActive for early osseointegration; however, evidence is limited to animal/in vitro data and long-term loaded human comparisons are lacking.

## Summary

Frames plasma against the commercial gold-standard hydrophilic surface. NTAP matches SLActive's early osseointegration in dogs while avoiding SLActive's two practical drawbacks (cost, shelf-life biological aging). It's a non-inferiority argument, not superiority.

## Key Contributions

- Direct comparison NTAP vs SLActive (the commercial benchmark).
- Chairside, aging-free, cost-effective positioning.

## Methodology

- NTAP assessed in vitro (cells); in vivo early osseointegration in beagles vs SLActive.

## Results

- NTAP: hydrophilic surface, early osseointegration comparable to SLActive.

**Boundary (living-document):** animal/in vitro; comparable (not superior) to SLActive; no long-term/loaded human data.
