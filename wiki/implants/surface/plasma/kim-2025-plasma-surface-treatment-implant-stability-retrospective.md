## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-arm cohort study (47 patients, 73 implants; ACTILINK™ Reborn vacuum plasma, June 2023–October 2024): ISQ monitored at baseline and weekly through week 4 plus week 8 after chairside plasma applied immediately before placement (60 s cycle ×2, per device Methods — despite the paper's abstract labeling the device "atmospheric pressure").

ISQ rose progressively from 78.97±5.52 at baseline to 83.74±4.36 at week 8 (p<0.001) with no stability dip observed; subgroup analysis showed the greatest gains in implants with low initial stability, mandibular placement, shorter fixtures, and higher insertion torque.

Without a control arm the absence of a stability dip cannot be causally attributed to plasma — this study is hypothesis-generating for the subgroup gradient and complements but does not replace the controlled RCT evidence.

## Summary

Human, real-world, but uncontrolled. It shows a clean ISQ climb with no dip and a clinically intuitive subgroup gradient (the weakest sites gain most). The crucial caveat is the single-arm design: without a control, "no dip" cannot be causally attributed to plasma. Complements the controlled RCTs.

## Key Contributions

- Real-world human ISQ trajectory + subgroup gradient.

## Methodology

- 73 plasma-treated implants/47 patients (Jun 2023–Oct 2024), ACTILINK™ Reborn. ISQ baseline, weekly ×4, week 8. Subgroups by initial ISQ, jaw, size, torque.
- **Device (added 2026-08-10):** ACTILINK™ Reborn (Plasmapp Co., Ltd.) generates plasma under **vacuum (5–10 Torr)**, not atmospheric pressure — the paper's own abstract calls it "non-thermal atmospheric pressure plasma" but the Methods section describes a sealed chamber pumped to partial vacuum, a **terminology inconsistency in the source paper itself**. Each activation cycle = **60 s** in 4 phases: vacuum formation (30 s, to 5 Torr) → plasma exposure (8 s, powered electrode) → decontamination (17 s, vacuum port) → venting (5 s). **Two consecutive cycles applied per fixture** (~120 s chairside total; actual plasma discharge itself only ~16 s of that).

## Results

- No stability dip; ISQ 78.97±5.52 → 83.74±4.36 (week 8, p<0.001).
- Greatest gains: low initial stability, mandible, shorter fixtures, higher insertion torque.

**Boundary (living-document):** no control group; retrospective; short-term ISQ only — needs RCT confirmation. Device is vacuum plasma despite the paper's abstract labeling it "atmospheric pressure" (see device note above).
