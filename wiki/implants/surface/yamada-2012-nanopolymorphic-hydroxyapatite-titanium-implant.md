## Three-line Summary

Rat femur animal study comparing nanopolymorphic crystalline hydroxyapatite (HA) coating (Ca/P=1.66, 55% crystallinity, needle-like nano-architecture) on microroughened titanium vs uncoated microroughened titanium at 2 and 4 weeks.

HA coating increased BIC and push-in force at both healing timepoints and reduced soft-tissue infiltration at the interface; however, the bone-volume benefit was spatially confined to within ≤50 µm of the implant surface, with lower bone volume beyond that zone.

The coating was produced by a mass-production-feasible flame spray + low-temperature calcination method; the ≤50 µm spatial confinement is an important caveat for interpreting bulk osseointegration claims.

## 세줄요약

쥐 대퇴골 동물실험 — 나노다형성 결정질 수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HA) 코팅 (Ca/P=1.66, 55% 결정화도, 침상 나노구조)과 비코팅 미세거칠기 티타늄을 2주·4주에 비교.

HA 코팅이 양 치유 시점에서 골-임플란트 접촉률 (BIC)과 압입력 (push-in force)을 높이고 계면 연조직 침투를 줄였으나, 골용적 효과는 임플란트 표면 50 µm 이내로만 한정되고 그 너머는 오히려 감소.

대량생산 가능한 화염분사+저온 소성 방법으로 제작; ≤50 µm 공간적 한계는 전체 골유착 효과를 해석할 때 중요한 제한점.

## Summary
Animal study (rat femur) demonstrating that nanopolymorphic crystalline hydroxyapatite (HA) coating on microroughened titanium enhances bone-implant integration at both early (2-week) and late (4-week) healing stages. The HA (55% crystallinity, needle-like nano-architecture, Ca/P=1.66, 70% larger surface area) increases BIC, reduces soft tissue infiltration at the interface, and improves push-in force. Effect is spatially confined to ≤50 µm peri-implant zone.

## Key Contributions
- Mass-production feasible: flame spray + low-temperature calcination method
- Soft tissue infiltration reduction = primary mechanism of BIC improvement
- Spatial confinement (≤50 µm): outside this zone, bone volume is actually lower — important caveat
