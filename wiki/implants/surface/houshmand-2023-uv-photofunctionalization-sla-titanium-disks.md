## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (J Adv Periodontol Implant Dent 2023): 24 commercial SLA titanium disks (>4 weeks old, biologically aged) in 3 groups (n=8) — control, UVC (210 nm, 48 h), UVB (310 nm, 48 h) — assessed by EDS/SEM for surface carbon/nitrogen/oxygen atomic concentration and Sessile-Drop contact angle.

UVC significantly reduced surface carbon (0.75 vs 0.95% control, p<0.001) and nitrogen (3.92 vs 4.17%, p<0.001) and produced the lowest contact angle (16.40° vs 129.88° control, p<0.001); UVB reduced contact angle (58.92°) but did NOT significantly reduce carbon (p=0.171) or nitrogen (p=0.785) vs control.

Cites the Roy et al mechanistic model — atmospheric exposure lets the oxidized titanium surface bind hydrocarbon contaminants via carboxyl/amine group reactions, and UV photons dissociate this bond — UVC achieved this, UVB produced hydrophilicity without actually removing the underlying carbon/nitrogen contamination, showing wettability and hydrocarbon removal can dissociate.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 연구(J Adv Periodontol Implant Dent 2023): 상업용 사포처리·대입자·산부식 (Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched, SLA) 티타늄 디스크 24개(4주 이상 경과, 생물학적 노화) 3군(n=8) — 대조군·자외선C (Ultraviolet C, UVC, 210nm, 48h)·자외선B (Ultraviolet B, UVB, 310nm, 48h) — 에너지분산형분광법 (Energy Dispersive Spectroscopy, EDS)/주사전자현미경 (Scanning Electron Microscope, SEM)으로 표면 탄소·질소·산소 원자농도, Sessile-Drop법으로 접촉각 측정.

UVC는 표면 탄소(0.75% vs 대조군 0.95%, p<0.001)·질소(3.92% vs 4.17%, p<0.001)를 유의 감소시키고 접촉각도 최저(16.40° vs 대조군 129.88°, p<0.001); UVB는 접촉각은 낮췄으나(58.92°) 탄소(p=0.171)·질소(p=0.785)는 대조군과 유의차 없음.

Roy et al 기전 모델 인용 — 대기 노출 시 산화된 티타늄 표면이 카르복실기·아민기 반응으로 탄화수소 (Hydrocarbon) 오염물과 결합하며, 자외선 광자가 이 탄화수소-티타늄 결합을 해리시킴 — UVC는 이를 실제로 수행했으나 UVB는 친수성만 만들고 탄소/질소 오염 자체는 못 없애, 습윤성과 탄화수소 제거가 분리될 수 있음을 보여줌.

## Summary

This in vitro study directly compares UVB and UVC photofunctionalization on the same batch of biologically aged (>4 weeks) commercial SLA titanium disks, using EDS (rather than the more common XPS) to quantify surface carbon, nitrogen, and oxygen alongside Sessile-Drop contact angle. UVC (210 nm) significantly reduced both surface carbon and nitrogen and produced a near-superhydrophilic contact angle (16.40°), consistent with the established hydrocarbon-removal literature. UVB (310 nm) lowered the contact angle substantially (129.88° → 58.92°) but did not significantly change surface carbon or nitrogen versus control — a dissociation between wettability improvement and actual decontamination that the paper's discussion attributes to UVB's weaker capacity to break the hydrocarbon-titanium bond. The paper's most useful contribution to this wiki cluster is citing the Roy et al mechanistic model: hydrocarbon contamination is not simply "dirt" but forms when the atmosphere-exposed, oxidized titanium surface chemically binds hydrocarbon contaminants through carboxyl and amine group reactions — UV photons then dissociate that bond. Study limitations (explicitly stated by the authors): no XPS access, single 48 h exposure duration, single commercial disk source, in vitro only with no biological/osteoblast readout.

## Key Contributions

- First paper in this wiki cluster to state *how* hydrocarbon contamination chemically attaches to titanium (atmospheric carboxyl/amine binding to the oxidized surface — Roy et al model), rather than only documenting *that* it accumulates over storage time.
- Same-disk-batch UVB vs UVC head-to-head comparison via EDS.
- Shows hydrophilicity (contact angle) and hydrocarbon removal (surface C/N) are not interchangeable proxies — UVB improved one without the other.

## Methodology

24 SLA commercial titanium disks (Biotem, Korea; 8 mm × 2 mm), >4 weeks old. 3 groups (n=8): control, UVC (~210 nm, 6 W measured at 1 cm), UVB (~310 nm, 3.7 W measured at 1 cm), 48 h exposure at 1 cm. EDS/SEM (TeScan-Mira III, 20 kV, ~200 µm scan, 3 points/disk) for C/N/O weight %. Sessile-Drop contact angle (SCAM-S1, 4 µL deionized water). One-way ANOVA + Tukey/Games-Howell, SPSS 26.

## Results

| Element/measure | UVC | UVB | Control | p (overall) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon (%) | 0.75±0.02 | 0.90±0.07 | 0.95±0.07 | <0.001 |
| Nitrogen (%) | 3.92±0.25 | 4.12±0.76 | 4.17±0.25 | <0.001 |
| Oxygen (%) | 4.62±0.32 | 4.82±0.29 | 4.97±0.52 | 0.064 (NS) |
| Contact angle (°) | 16.40±0.54 | 58.92±0.43 | 129.88±0.70 | <0.001 |

UVC carbon/nitrogen significantly lower than both UVB and control; UVB carbon (p=0.171) and nitrogen (p=0.785) NOT significantly different from control. Contact angle differed significantly across all three pairwise comparisons.
