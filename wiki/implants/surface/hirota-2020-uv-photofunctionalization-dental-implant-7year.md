## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-center 7-year study (JCM 2020, 16 patients, 70 implants) of UV-photofunctionalized implants (15-min pre-placement UV irradiation) across regular, anatomically complex, and cancer-resection cases.

Regular and anatomically complex (atrophied/GBR/sinus-augmented) cases both achieved 100% success at 7 years; cancer-resection cases achieved only 22.2%; ISQ rose +21.9 in complex cases (vs +3.2 regular) by second-stage surgery.

UV-PF overcomes anatomical compromise (severe atrophy) but not pathophysiological compromise (cancer resection/radiation); bone quality, implant location, and cancer resection were independent failure predictors.

## 세줄요약

전향적 단일기관 7년 연구(JCM 2020, 16명·70개 임플란트): 15분 UV 광기능화 (Photofunctionalization, UV-PF) 전처리 임플란트 — 일반·복잡증례·암절제 증례 비교.

일반증례(30개) 및 해부학적 복잡증례(21개) 7년 성공률 100%; 암절제 증례(15개) 22.2%; 복잡증례 임플란트 안정성 지수 (ISQ) 2차 수술 시 +21.9 상승(일반 +3.2).

UV-PF는 해부학적 타협(위축·골증대) 극복 가능하지만 병태생리적 타협(암절제·방사선) 극복 불가 — 골질·부위·암절제가 실패 독립 예측인자.

## Summary
Prospective single-center 7-year study of UV-photofunctionalized implants (15-min pre-placement UV irradiation) in 16 patients (70 implants). Regular and anatomically complex (severely atrophied, requiring GBR/sinus augmentation) cases both achieved 100% success at 7 years. Cancer-resection cases showed 22.2% success. ISQ increased markedly in complex cases (+21.9 at second-stage surgery), confirming accelerated secondary osseointegration from UV-PF.

## Key Contributions
- Longest (7-year) prospective follow-up of UV-PF implants
- UV-PF overcomes anatomical compromise (severe atrophy) but NOT pathophysiological compromise (cancer resection/radiation)
- ISQ +21.9 in complex cases = quantified evidence of UV-PF facilitating secondary osseointegration

## Results
| Group | n | 7-yr success | ISQ change (placement → 2nd stage) |
|-------|---|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| Regular | 30 | **100%** | +3.2 |
| Complex (atrophied) | 21 | **100%** | **+21.9** |
| Cancer resection | 15 | **22.2%** | −3.5 |

Multivariate predictors of failure: bone quality (p<0.05), location (p<0.05), cancer resection (p<0.01).
