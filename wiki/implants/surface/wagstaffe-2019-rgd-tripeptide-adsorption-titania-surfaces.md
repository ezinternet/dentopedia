## Three-line Summary

In vitro surface-science study (Mater Sci Eng C 2019, Univ Manchester + Diamond Light Source): XPS + STM investigation of RGD (Arg-Gly-Asp) tripeptide adsorption on atomically clean single-crystal rutile TiO₂(110) (bare and pyrocatechol-capped) and on model SLA/SLActive dental implant surfaces at pH 4/7/9.2.

RGD binds via deprotonated carboxylate groups to surface Ti atoms; SLActive showed higher RGD uptake than SLA at every pH tested; capping the clean crystal with pyrocatechol counterintuitively *increased* RGD uptake (95% higher N 1s signal) despite being more hydrophobic, because pyrocatechol is more weakly bound and more easily displaced than the naturally occurring adventitious hydrocarbon layer.

Cites Balajka et al. (Science 2018): atmospheric adventitious hydrocarbon contamination on titania is mainly small organic acids (e.g., formic, acetic acid) chemisorbed via carboxylate-cation bridging — NOT oil/grease — and concludes RGD/peptide uptake is governed by how easily this layer can be displaced, not by surface hydrophilicity per se.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 표면과학 연구(Mater Sci Eng C 2019, 맨체스터대 + Diamond Light Source): X선광전자분광법 (X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy, XPS) + 주사터널링현미경 (Scanning Tunneling Microscopy, STM)으로 원자수준 청정 단결정 루타일 TiO₂(110)(무처리·피로카테콜 캡핑)과 SLA/SLActive 모형 임플란트 표면(pH 4/7/9.2)에서 RGD (Arg-Gly-Asp) 삼중펩타이드 흡착 분석.

RGD는 탈양성자화된 카르복실기로 표면 Ti 원자에 결합; SLActive가 모든 pH에서 SLA보다 RGD 흡수량 높음; 청정 결정에 피로카테콜을 캡핑하면 더 소수성임에도 오히려 RGD 흡수 증가(N 1s 신호 95%↑) — 피로카테콜이 자연 형성 탄화수소층보다 약하게 결합해 더 쉽게 치환되기 때문.

Balajka et al(Science 2018) 인용: 대기 중 티타니아 표면의 부착탄소오염 (Adventitious Carbon) 주성분은 기름·유분이 아니라 폼산·아세트산 같은 소형 유기산이며 카르복실기-양이온 브리징 결합으로 화학흡착됨 — RGD/펩타이드 흡수량은 표면 친수성 자체가 아니라 이 부착탄소층의 치환 용이성으로 결정된다는 결론.

## Summary

This surface-science paper is the clearest mechanistic answer in this wiki cluster to "what exactly is titanium hydrocarbon contamination?" Using XPS and STM on atomically clean single-crystal rutile TiO₂(110) and on lab-fabricated SLA/SLActive analog surfaces, the authors show that RGD tripeptide (a cell-adhesion motif proxy for protein adsorption) binds via deprotonated carboxylate groups directly to surface titanium atoms. Counterintuitively, pre-capping the clean crystal with a hydrophobic pyrocatechol monolayer *increased* RGD uptake relative to the bare (more hydrophilic) crystal — because pyrocatechol is weakly bound and easily displaced by RGD, whereas the naturally forming adventitious hydrocarbon layer on real/uncapped surfaces is not. Citing Balajka et al. (Science 2018), the authors identify that layer's actual chemical composition: small organic acids such as formic and acetic acid, which chemisorb in an ordered fashion via carboxylate groups bridging to under-coordinated surface cations — not oil, grease, or an undefined generic film. SLActive (etched and stored under inert N₂/saline) carries less of this layer than SLA (etched and stored in open air) and correspondingly adsorbs more RGD at every pH tested, giving a direct surface-chemistry account of SLActive's early-osseointegration advantage. The paper's central conclusion — that peptide/protein uptake tracks the *displaceability* of the adventitious carbon layer rather than surface hydrophilicity per se — reframes hydrophilicity as a downstream correlate of decontamination, not the causal mechanism itself.

## Key Contributions

- First paper in this wiki cluster to specify the actual chemical identity of "hydrocarbon contamination": small organic acids (formic/acetic acid), not a generic oil/grease film.
- Demonstrates the contamination forms both from air exposure and from air-exposed water (dissolved trace organic acids), with air exposure depositing substantially more.
- Shows hydrophilicity is not a reliable proxy for surface "cleanliness" — a more hydrophobic capped surface out-adsorbed a more hydrophilic uncapped one because the capping layer was more easily displaced.
- Provides a direct surface-chemistry explanation (adventitious-layer thickness/displaceability) for why SLActive shows an early-osseointegration edge over SLA despite similar topography.

## Methodology

XPS (Al Kα, Kratos Axis Ultra) + STM (Omicron AFM-STM) on UHV-cleaned single-crystal rutile TiO₂(110), bare or capped with a pyrocatechol monolayer, exposed to 0.1–1 mg/mL RGD solution. Parallel study on model SLA (air-etched/stored) vs SLActive (N₂/saline-etched/stored) grade-2 Ti disks soaked 24 h in 0.1 mg/mL RGD at pH 4, 7, 9.2, then analyzed by XPS (Ti 2p, N 1s as RGD-uptake proxies; C 1s/O 1s too complex to deconvolute on real surfaces).

## Results

- N 1s-based RGD uptake: TiO₂ 8.8% vs pyrocatechol-capped TiO₂ 17.2% (95% relative increase despite capped surface being more hydrophobic).
- RGD chemisorbs via deprotonated carboxylate groups (O 1s stoichiometry ≈ 2.0:1.0:0.2 for COO⁻:O=C–N:CO(OH), close to fully-deprotonated theoretical ratio).
- RGD overlayer thickness ~18 Å as-deposited, ~6 Å after rinsing (chemisorbed monolayer retained).
- SLActive > SLA in RGD uptake at pH 4, 7, and 9.2 (both lowest at pH 7).
- Mechanistic citation (Balajka 2018, *Science*): atmospheric adventitious hydrocarbon on titania = mainly small organic acids, ordered chemisorption via carboxylate-cation bridging to under-coordinated surface sites.
