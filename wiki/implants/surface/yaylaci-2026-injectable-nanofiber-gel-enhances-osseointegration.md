## Three-line Summary

Rabbit implant study: custom titanium implants placed with an injectable self-assembling peptide-amphiphile nanofiber gel (designed to be osteoinductive, adhesive and antimicrobial) applied in the osteotomy vs a standard SLA surface; evaluated at 3 and 5 wk by micro-CT (BIC, BV/TV) and reverse torque (group sizes not stated in the abstract).

At 5 wk the nanofiber group had higher BIC (58.7% vs 50.5%, P < 0.001), BV/TV (51.8% vs 42.6%, P < 0.001) and reverse torque (53.2 vs 41.8 N·cm, P < 0.01) than SLA.

Single preclinical rabbit study, abstract-level only; the claims that the gel prevents or repairs drilling-induced microdamage and fills microcracks are the authors' design intent, not measured outcomes. Not evidence of clinical benefit in humans.

## 세줄요약

토끼 임플란트 연구: 자가조립 펩타이드 양친매성 (Peptide Amphiphile) 나노섬유 주입형 젤 (골유도·접착·항균 목적 설계)을 골절개부에 적용한 맞춤 티타늄 임플란트 vs 표준 SLA (Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched) 표면. 3주·5주에 미세CT (micro-CT)로 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)과 골체적/전체체적 (BV/TV), 역회전력 (Reverse Torque) 평가 (군별 n은 초록에 없음).

5주에 나노섬유군이 SLA보다 BIC (58.7% vs 50.5%, P < 0.001), BV/TV (51.8% vs 42.6%, P < 0.001), 역회전력 (53.2 vs 41.8 N·cm, P < 0.01) 모두 높았다.

단일 전임상 토끼 연구이며 초록 수준이다. 드릴링 유발 미세손상 예방·복구와 미세균열 충전은 저자의 설계 의도이지 측정된 결과가 아니다. 사람에서의 임상적 이득 근거는 아니다.

## Summary

The authors state that high insertion torque can make conventional bioactive implant coatings delaminate, generating debris. They developed an injectable gel of self-assembling peptide amphiphiles designed to be osteoinductive, adhesive and antimicrobial, applied in the osteotomy. In rabbits, custom titanium implants with the nanofiber interface were compared with SLA implants at 3 and 5 wk by micro-CT and reverse torque. At 5 wk the nanofiber group was higher on BIC, BV/TV and reverse torque. The abstract also says the interface is designed to prevent or repair drilling-induced microdamage and fill peri-implant microcracks; these are design claims, not reported measurements.

## Key Contributions
- Osteotomy-applied flowable liner as an alternative to pre-applied coatings.
- Significant 5-wk gains over SLA in BIC, BV/TV and reverse torque in a rabbit model.

## Methodology
Rabbit model; custom titanium implants with nanofiber interface vs SLA; 3 and 5 wk; micro-CT (BIC, BV/TV) and reverse torque (n not stated in abstract).
