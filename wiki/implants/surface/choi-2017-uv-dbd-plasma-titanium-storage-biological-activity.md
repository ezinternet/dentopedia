## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (Yonsei University + Kwangwoon University Plasma Bioscience Research Center, Korea): Grade IV machined titanium discs treated with UV (15 min) or DBD plasma (15 min) and stored under three conditions (distilled water, vacuum desiccator, ambient air) for 28 days; surface characterized by AFM/XRD/contact angle/XPS; biological activity assessed by albumin adsorption, MC3T3-E1 adhesion, and cytoskeleton development.

Immediate UV and immediate DBD plasma achieved equivalent surface hydrophilicity; among storage methods, only distilled water (dH2O) storage maintained contact angle comparable to fresh treatment at 28 days; vacuum and ambient storage allowed hydrocarbon recontamination (XPS: C recovery), reducing hydrophilicity and biological activity.

The clinical implication is that UV-treated implants stored in dH2O retain bioactivity equivalent to fresh treatment; the study also provides the first published head-to-head UV = DBD plasma equivalence at the surface property level, with equal albumin adsorption, MC3T3-E1 adhesion, and cytoskeletal development.

## 세줄요약

In vitro (연세대학교 + 광운대학교 플라즈마 생명공학연구센터, 한국): Grade IV 기계 가공 티타늄 디스크에 UV(15분) 또는 DBD 플라즈마(15분) 처리 후 3가지 보관 조건(증류수, 진공 건조기, 대기 밀봉)으로 28일 보관; AFM/XRD/접촉각/XPS로 표면 분석, 알부민 흡착·MC3T3-E1 부착·세포골격 형성으로 생물학적 활성 평가.

즉시 UV 처리와 즉시 DBD 플라즈마 처리는 동등한 표면 친수성을 달성; 보관 방법 중 증류수(dH2O) 보관만 28일 후에도 직후 처리 수준의 접촉각을 유지; 진공·대기 보관은 탄화수소 재오염(XPS: 탄소 회복)으로 친수성과 생물학적 활성이 저하됨.

임상 시사점: UV 처리 임플란트를 증류수에 보관하면 직후 처리 수준의 생체활성을 유지 가능; 이 연구는 UV ≈ DBD 플라즈마(표면 특성 수준)의 첫 번째 직접 비교 근거로, 알부민 흡착·MC3T3-E1 부착·세포골격 모두 동등.

## Summary

This Korean in vitro study from Yonsei University (Hwang group) + Kwangwoon University Plasma Bioscience Research Center (Choi EH group) is the first published head-to-head comparison of UV irradiation versus DBD plasma treatment on titanium surfaces at equivalent treatment times (15 min each), with a follow-up storage assessment. Grade IV machined Ti discs were UV-irradiated and then stored in three conditions for 28 days: distilled water, vacuum desiccator (31.3 kPa), or sealed ambient air. DBD plasma was tested immediately after treatment. The key finding is that UV+dH2O storage maintained hydrophilicity, XPS surface chemistry (high Ti, O; low C), albumin adsorption, MC3T3-E1 osteoblast adhesion, and cytoskeletal development comparable to both immediately UV-treated and immediately DBD plasma-treated surfaces (p<0.001 vs. dry-stored UV). Vacuum and ambient storage both allowed hydrocarbon recontamination, losing the biological activity gain. The study establishes: (1) UV and DBD plasma achieve equivalent surface hydrophilicity immediately at equal treatment times; (2) the preservation method, not the treatment method, determines long-term biological activity; (3) wet storage in dH2O is a viable strategy for maintaining UV-treated implant bioactivity.

## Key Contributions

- First published UV vs. DBD plasma equivalence: at 15-min treatment, identical contact angle, albumin adsorption, MC3T3-E1 adhesion, and cytoskeletal development
- Storage determines bioactivity retention: dH2O > vacuum > ambient for UV-treated Ti at 28 days
- XPS confirms mechanism: dH2O prevents hydrocarbon recontamination (maintains Ti⁴⁺, Ti-OH; suppresses C-C/C-H bonds); vacuum and ambient allow C recovery
- Plasma Bioscience Research Center (Kwangwoon) collaboration — Korean plasma expertise + dental biomaterials group

## Methodology

**Material**: Grade IV cpTi machined discs. **Treatment**: UV (15 min) and DBD plasma (15 min). **UV storage groups**: dH2O 28 days, vacuum desiccator 28 days (31.325 kPa), sealed ambient 28 days. **Control**: untreated Ti. **Comparators**: immediate post-UV, immediate post-DBD. **Surface characterization**: AFM (topography), XRD (crystal structure), contact angle (sessile drop), XPS (elemental composition). **Biological**: albumin adsorption (protein binding), MC3T3-E1 osteoblast adhesion (cell count), cytoskeleton development (immunofluorescence).

## Results

- **Contact angle**: UV+dH2O ≈ immediate UV ≈ immediate DBD (near 0°); UV+vacuum and UV+ambient significantly higher (more hydrophobic); p<0.001
- **XPS (surface C)**: UV+dH2O maintained lowest C content; vacuum/ambient showed progressive C recovery (hydrocarbon recontamination)
- **Albumin adsorption**: UV+dH2O > UV+vacuum, UV+ambient; comparable to immediate UV, DBD (p<0.05)
- **MC3T3-E1 adhesion**: UV+dH2O significantly enhanced vs. dry stored; comparable to fresh treatments
- **Cytoskeletal development**: UV+dH2O ≈ immediate UV ≈ immediate DBD (well-spread, organized actin); vacuum/ambient = reduced spreading
- **Crystal structure (XRD)**: no change across all groups (anatase/rutile unchanged)
- **Topography (AFM)**: no significant roughness change across groups
