## Three-line Summary
Systematic review (27 in vitro/in vivo/preclinical studies, no meta-analysis due to heterogeneity) of calcium phosphate (CaP) coatings — HA, β-TCP, OCP, biphasic/composite, ion-doped and multifunctional variants — applied to zirconia (Y-TZP) implants/discs via sol-gel, biomimetic, RF magnetron sputtering, IBAD, laser-assisted, and other deposition methods.

CaP coatings consistently increased osteoblast proliferation and ALP activity in vitro (19/27 studies) and improved bone-to-implant contact (BIC) in vivo (15/27 studies) vs uncoated zirconia — e.g., AlFarraj: 60.3% vs 45.1% (rabbit, 8 wk); Mutsuzaki: 48.3% vs 29.7% (rabbit tibia, 6 wk); Langhoff: 35.4% vs 22.5% at 2 wk narrowing to 46.6% vs 40.5% at 4 wk — with the advantage most pronounced early and attenuating over time.

Marked heterogeneity in coating composition/thickness/roughness/adhesion reporting prevented pooling, no long-term RCT confirms superior clinical outcomes for coated vs uncoated zirconia (unlike titanium, where CaP coating is already clinically established), and the authors conclude CaP-coated zirconia remains investigational pending standardized protocols and long-term follow-up.

> - 결론: 지르코니아(Y-TZP)는 표면이 생체불활성(bioinert)이라 골유착이 티타늄보다 예측성이 떨어지는데, 인산칼슘 (Calcium Phosphate, CaP) 코팅이 이를 보완하는 유망한 전략임을 27편 체계적 문헌고찰로 확인.
> - 코팅 종류: 수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HA)이 가장 흔함, 그 외 β-삼인산칼슘 (β-Tricalcium Phosphate, β-TCP), 옥타칼슘인산 (Octacalcium Phosphate, OCP), 이인산칼슘복합체 (Biphasic Calcium Phosphate, BCP), Mg/Sr/Zn 이온도핑형, 다기능형(BMP-2, FGF-2+LMWH, 은나노입자, 항생제담지 나노입자).
> - 증착법: 생체모방(biomimetic) 침적이 가장 흔하며, RF 마그네트론 스퍼터링, 이온빔보조증착 (Ion Beam-Assisted Deposition, IBAD), 졸-겔(sol-gel), 레이저보조(Nd:YAG/펨토초/Er:YAG), 습식분말분사(wet powder spraying) 등 다양.
> - 핵심 수치: in vitro 19/27편에서 조골세포 증식·알칼리인산분해효소 (Alkaline Phosphatase, ALP) 활성 증가; in vivo 15/27편에서 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 개선 — AlFarraj 60.3% vs 45.1%(토끼 8주), Mutsuzaki 48.3% vs 29.7%(토끼 경골 6주), Langhoff 35.4% vs 22.5%(2주)→46.6% vs 40.5%(4주).
> - 시간축 패턴: CaP 코팅의 BIC 우위는 **초기(1~4주)에 가장 크고 시간이 지날수록 격차가 줄어듦** — 골유착 "가속" 효과이지 최종 BIC 상한을 높이는 효과는 아닌 것으로 해석됨.
> - 방법론 이질성: 코팅 두께(50nm~100µm)·거칠기(Ra 0.08~3.5µm)·접착강도(2.6~52.3 MPa) 편차가 커 메타분석 불가, 다수 연구가 물리적 파라미터 자체를 누락 보고.
> - 품질평가: JBI 9개 항목 중 12편 만점, 저품질 연구 없음(최저 6/9) — 근거의 신뢰도 자체는 준수한 편.
> - 한계: 장기 추적 연구 부재, 코팅 지르코니아의 임상적 우월성을 뒷받침하는 장기 무작위대조군연구 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 전무 — 저자들은 "연구 단계(investigational)"로 명시.
> - 티타늄 CaP 코팅([[implants/surface/bonfante-2012-calcium-phosphate-coatings-plasma-spray]])과의 관계: 티타늄은 이미 자연 산화막으로 골결합이 되는 반응성 표면이라 CaP 코팅 유형 간 차이가 BIC에 반영 안 됐지만(Bonfante 2012), 지르코니아는 생체불활성이라 코팅 유무 자체가 BIC를 크게 좌우 — 같은 코팅전략이 기질(titanium vs zirconia)에 따라 다른 의미를 가짐.
> - 임상 takeaway: 지르코니아 임플란트에 CaP 코팅을 고려할 근거는 전임상 단계에서는 견고하나, 사람 대상 장기 RCT 없이는 "티타늄만큼 예측 가능하다"고 단정할 수 없음.

## Summary
This systematic review synthesizes 27 in vitro, in vivo, and preclinical studies (from 394 initially screened records across PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, and Cochrane) evaluating calcium phosphate (CaP) coatings — hydroxyapatite (HA), β-tricalcium phosphate (β-TCP), octacalcium phosphate (OCP), biphasic and multifunctional composites — applied to zirconia (Y-TZP) dental implants. Because Y-TZP zirconia lacks the stable oxide layer that drives spontaneous osseointegration on titanium, its surface is intrinsically bioinert, motivating bioactive CaP surface modification. Across a wide range of deposition methods (biomimetic precipitation, RF magnetron sputtering, ion beam-assisted deposition, sol-gel, laser-assisted, wet powder spraying), CaP-coated zirconia consistently showed higher osteoblast proliferation and alkaline phosphatase (ALP) activity in vitro and higher bone-to-implant contact (BIC) in vivo than uncoated zirconia, with the largest gains in the earliest healing phase (1–4 weeks) and attenuating differences at later timepoints. Substantial heterogeneity in coating composition, thickness, roughness, and adhesion-strength reporting prevented meta-analysis, and no long-term randomized clinical trial yet confirms that CaP-coated zirconia achieves clinical outcomes comparable to CaP-coated titanium. The authors conclude CaP coatings are a biologically promising but still investigational strategy for zirconia implants, pending protocol standardization and long-term human data.

## Key Contributions
- First systematic review dedicated specifically to CaP coatings on zirconia (rather than titanium, or zirconia grouped generically with other ceramics).
- Maps CaP chemistry (HA, β-TCP, OCP, BCP, ion-substituted, multifunctional) against deposition method (RF sputtering, IBAD, biomimetic, sol-gel, laser, WPS, EPD) and biological readout (proliferation, ALP, BIC).
- Identifies a consistent early-osseointegration-acceleration pattern across independent in vivo models (rabbit, rat, goat, canine, mini-pig) — the CaP advantage narrows, but does not necessarily disappear, over 2–12 weeks.
- Flags reporting heterogeneity in physical coating parameters (roughness, thickness, adhesion) as itself a barrier to translational conclusions, not just individual-study noise.

## Methodology
PRISMA-compliant, OSF-preregistered systematic review; 5-database search (July 2025, no year restriction); 394 records → 27 included after title/abstract and full-text screening; quality appraised with the JBI checklist for quasi-experimental designs (9 items, two blinded reviewers, Cohen's κ=0.82). Heterogeneity across coating type, deposition method, animal model, and outcome reporting precluded meta-analysis (narrative synthesis only).

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Studies included | 27 (from 394 screened; no meta-analysis — heterogeneity) |
| JBI quality (9 items) | 12 studies scored 9/9; lowest score was 6/9 (5 studies) |
| In vitro proliferation/ALP | Increased on CaP-coated vs uncoated zirconia in 19/27 studies (few exceptions: Huang, Pae, Hirano — no significant difference) |
| In vivo BIC — AlFarraj | Zr+HA 60.3% vs Zr 45.1% (rabbit, 8 wk) |
| In vivo BIC — Cheng | 55.3% vs 43.9% (rabbit, 4 wk) |
| In vivo BIC — Mutsuzaki | 48.3% vs 29.7% (rabbit tibia, 6 wk) |
| In vivo BIC — Langhoff | 35.4% vs 22.5% (2 wk) → 46.6% vs 40.5% (4 wk) — gap narrows |
| Coating thickness range | 50–200 nm to 1–5 µm typical; up to 100 µm in press-sintered composites |
| Roughness (Ra) range | 0.08 µm (sputtered) to >3.5 µm (wet-sprayed TZCP) |
| Adhesion strength range | 2.6 MPa (biomimetic CaP, untreated) to 52.3 MPa (heat-treated β-TCP); up to ~38 MPa for sputtered CaP |
| Long-term clinical evidence | None — no RCT confirms superior clinical outcomes for CaP-coated vs uncoated zirconia |
