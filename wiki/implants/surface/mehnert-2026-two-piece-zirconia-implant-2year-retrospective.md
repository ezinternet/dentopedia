## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center study (31 patients, 44 two-piece ATZ ZrO₂ implants Zeramex XT, SC-supported; 63 total implants for SC/FDP/overdentures); Germany 2017–2021; ≥2-year follow-up; STROBE-compliant; industry-funded (CeramTec GmbH).
100% implant survival, 0 fractures; MBL at 24 months 0.20±0.66 mm (NS across periods); BOP significantly decreased 9.1%→1.1% (p=0.002); success rate 93.2% (41/44 implants); 2 minor technical complications; 0 biological complications.
Two-piece ATZ ZrO₂ implants at 2 years show high survival and minimal MBL comparable to titanium; BOP improved significantly; VICARBOscrew (non-metallic PEEK-based screw) had 0 failures.

## Summary

Single-center retrospective study of 35 patients and 63 Zeramex XT ATZ two-piece zirconia implants placed 2017–2021, with ≥2-year follow-up. The primary analysis cohort comprised 31 patients and 44 single-crown (SC)-supporting implants. MBL from implant exposure baseline was minimal and statistically stable across measurement periods (6, 12, 24 months), with mean values below 0.25 mm. BOP improved significantly from 9.1% at baseline to 1.1% at 24 months. Probing depth showed a slight but statistically significant increase (clinically small, Cohen's d=1.51). Implant survival was 100% (63/63); success rate (SC cohort) was 93.2% with 3 implants not meeting strict criteria (2 MBL>1.7 mm, 1 residual BOP) — all retained. The VICARBOscrew (carbon-fiber reinforced polymer non-metallic abutment screw) produced zero failures. COI: T.M. served as paid lecturer for a CeramTec distributor 2014–2024; no independent comparator group.

## Key Contributions

- 2-year follow-up for Zeramex XT — a commercially available two-piece ATZ ZrO₂ implant system
- 100% implant survival; 0 framework fractures — ATZ mechanical reliability supported in clinical setting
- MBL <0.25 mm at all time points; NS between periods — negligible remodeling over 2 years
- BOP significantly reduced at 24 months (p=0.002) — favorable peri-implant soft tissue trend
- VICARBOscrew (non-metallic PEEK-based screw): 0 failures in 44 implant-restorations
- 50% received bone augmentation — results include a high GBR/sinus lift burden
- Success rate 93.2%; 3 implants failed success criteria without requiring removal

## Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Retrospective cohort; STROBE-compliant |
| Ethics | Nordrhein Medical Association, Germany (t2L/2023) |
| Setting | Single private dental office, Germany |
| n (SC analysis) | 31 patients, 44 implants |
| n (total cohort) | 35 patients, 63 implants (SC 44, FDP 13, overdenture 6) |
| Implant system | Zeramex XT (CeramTec Schweiz GmbH); ATZ; Zerafil surface (Al₂O₃ sandblast + hypophosphorous acid etch) |
| Diameters | 4.2 mm (65.9%), 5.5 mm (34.1%) |
| Lengths | 10 mm (61.4%), 12 mm (38.6%) |
| Screw | VICARBOscrew (carbon-fiber reinforced high-performance polymer — non-metallic) |
| Restoration | Monolithic ZrO₂ SC/FDP cemented on ATZ abutments; screw-torqued at 25 Ncm |
| Augmentation | 50% (lateral GBR 34.1%, sinus lift 11.4%, peri-implant gap 4.5%) |
| Healing | Submerged; uncovering at 4 months (6 months for sinus lift) |
| MBL baseline | Time of implant exposure (not placement) |
| Follow-up | 6, 12, 24 months; all patients completed all recalls |
| Funding / COI | CeramTec GmbH; T.M. paid lecturer for distributor 2014–2024 |

## Results

| Parameter | Baseline | 6 months | 12 months | 24 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MBL (mm, mean ± SD) | 0 | 0.24 ± 0.58 | 0.18 ± 0.62 | 0.20 ± 0.66 |
| PD (mm, mean ± SD) | 2.38 ± 0.27 | 2.61 ± 0.34 | 2.72 ± 0.29 | 2.78 ± 0.26 |
| BOP (%, mean ± SD) | 9.1 ± 13.7 | 5.7 ± 8.8 | 2.7 ± 7.1 | 1.1 ± 4.3 |

- MBL NS between periods (p≥0.062); slight (NS) higher MBL in maxilla vs mandible
- MBL lower in augmented sites (NS trend, exploratory)
- PD: significant increase over time (Cohen's d=1.51 at 24mo vs baseline — clinically minimal)
- BOP: significant decrease (24mo vs baseline p=0.002; 24mo vs 6mo p=0.003)
- Implant survival: **100%** (63/63; 0 fractures)
- Implant success (SC group): **93.2%** (41/44); 3 failures: 2 MBL>1.7mm, 1 persistent BOP
- Technical complications: 2 (1 abutment screw fracture during prosthetics; 1 crown misfit → gap)
- Biological complications: **0**
- VICARBOscrew failures: **0**
- ICC: intra-rater 0.97, inter-rater 0.95 (excellent radiographic measurement agreement)
