## Three-line Summary

In vitro + in vivo animal study (3D-printed porous Ti6Al4V, rat BMSCs, rabbit condyle defect model): four groups — no UV control (GC), UVC 270 nm (G270), UVA 365 nm (G365), dual-wavelength UV-AC 270+365 nm (GU) — with 15-minute exposure.

Dual-wavelength UV-AC produced significantly higher ALP activity (day 7 and 14), hydrophilicity, cell viability, and BIC and osteoid bone ratios in vivo than either single-wavelength arm; mechanical properties (compressive strength, elastic modulus) were unchanged by any UV treatment.

Mechanism: UV-AC upregulated focal adhesion proteins (integrin β1, vinculin, talin) and organized actin/myosin cytoskeleton, driving nuclear YAP translocation — adhesion–cytoskeleton–nuclear mechanotransduction cascade as the proximal osteogenic driver.

## 세줄요약

In vitro + in vivo 동물 연구 (3D 프린팅 다공성 Ti6Al4V, 쥐 골수 중간엽줄기세포, 토끼 과두부 결손 모델): 4군 — 비처리 대조군, UVC(270 nm), 자외선A (Ultraviolet A, UVA, 365 nm), 복합 파장 UV-AC(270+365 nm) — 15분 조사.

복합 파장 UV-AC가 단일 UVC 또는 UVA보다 골아세포 인산분해효소 (Alkaline Phosphatase, ALP) 활성·친수성·세포 생존성·골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)·골유사체(osteoid) 비율 모두 유의 우수; 압축강도·탄성계수는 군 간 차이 없음.

기전: UV-AC가 integrin β1·vinculin·talin 발현 증가와 actin/myosin 세포골격 구조화를 통해 핵내 YAP 전좌(translocation) 를 유도 — 접착-세포골격-핵 기계적신호전달 (Adhesion-Cytoskeleton-Nuclear Mechanotransduction) 경로가 골형성 촉진의 근위 기전.

## Summary

This study directly compared four UV photofunctionalization conditions on 3D-printed porous Ti6Al4V implants: no UV (control), UVC (270 nm), UVA (365 nm), and dual-wavelength UV-AC (270+365 nm combined), using a 15-minute exposure in an omnidirectional LED chamber. In vitro, UV-AC produced the lowest contact angle, highest ALP activity at days 7 and 14, and highest cell viability and cytoskeletal organization in rat BMSCs. In vivo, UV-AC showed significantly greater bone-implant contact and osteoid bone ratios in a rabbit bilateral femoral condyle defect model at follow-up (micro-CT and Goldner trichrome histology). The key mechanistic finding is that UV-AC — but not single-wavelength arms — drove a complete adhesion–cytoskeleton–nuclear coupling cascade: upregulation of focal adhesion proteins (integrin β1, vinculin, talin) → organized actin/myosin cytoskeleton → YAP/TAZ nuclear translocation, promoting osteogenic differentiation. This finding challenges the assumption that UVC alone is optimal and suggests synergistic photon interactions across wavelengths produce a qualitatively different surface state.

## Key Contributions

- First direct 3-arm UV wavelength comparison (UVC vs. UVA vs. UV-AC dual) on the **same 3D-printed porous Ti6Al4V implant** with both in vitro and in vivo endpoints.
- UV-AC (dual) > UVC (single) for osseointegration — extends and partially contradicts the implicit "UVC sufficient" reading of Houshmand 2023.
- Mechanotransduction pathway characterization: UV-PF effect operates via integrin/cytoskeleton/YAP axis, not merely wettability.
- UV treatment leaves mechanical integrity of porous scaffold unchanged — safety finding relevant to load-bearing applications.
- In vivo fluorescence labeling confirms faster early bone apposition in UV-AC group.

## Methodology

3D-printed porous Ti6Al4V (5 mm × 6 mm, 400 µm pore, 73% porosity; electron-beam melting). Four groups: GC (no UV), G270 (UVC 270 nm, ≥1.8 J/cm²), G365 (UVA 365 nm, ≥27 J/cm²), GU (UV-AC 270+365 nm combined); 15-min exposure. In vitro: rat BMSCs, CCK-8 (days 3/7/14), ALP (days 7/14), calcein-AM/PI, F-actin, SEM. Surface: contact angle, FE-SEM, EDS, XPS, XRD. Mechanical: MTS universal testing (n=5; compressive strength, elastic modulus). In vivo: rabbit bilateral femoral condyle; micro-CT (1000–2250 HU bone phase; intraporous + 500 µm peri-implant ROIs), Goldner trichrome, fluorescence (calcein green + tetracycline). Statistics: one-way ANOVA + Tukey post hoc.

## Results

- **Wettability**: GU lowest contact angle > G270 > G365 > GC (all significant)
- **ALP day 14**: GU > G270 > G365 > GC (p<0.05 for GU vs. all others)
- **BIC (in vivo)**: GU significantly higher than G270, G365, GC (p<0.05)
- **Osteoid bone ratio**: GU significantly higher than other groups (micro-CT + histology)
- **Mechanical**: no significant difference in compressive strength or elastic modulus across groups
- **Mechanotransduction**: GU showed highest integrin β1, vinculin, talin expression; most organized F-actin; highest nuclear YAP translocation vs. all other groups
