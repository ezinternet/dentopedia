## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (J Dent Res 2014, MC3T3-E1 osteoblasts) providing the first direct dose-response evidence for hydrocarbon deposition on titanium, using controlled C/Ti ratios of 0.3, 0.7, and 1.0 on mirror-polished cpTi.

Hydrocarbon deposition concentration-dependently suppressed osteoblast attachment, spreading, ALP activity, and calcium mineralization; at C/Ti=1.0, ALP and calcium mineralization were reduced by >40%.

Commercial implants carry 17.9–76.5% surface carbon — explaining the well-known BIC plateau of 45–65% — and providing the mechanistic rationale for UV photofunctionalization as a hydrocarbon removal strategy.

## 세줄요약

인비트로(J Dent Res 2014, MC3T3-E1 골아세포): C/Ti=0.3·0.7·1.0 농도 조절 실험으로 탄화수소 침착의 골아세포 활성 억제 용량-반응 관계 최초 직접 실증.

탄화수소 농도 의존적으로 골아세포 부착·퍼짐·ALP 활성·칼슘 무기화 감소; C/Ti=1.0에서 ALP·칼슘 무기화 >40% 감소.

시판 임플란트 표면 탄소 함량 17.9–76.5% → 임상적 골-임플란트 접촉률 (BIC) 정체(45–65%) 원인 규명; UV 광기능화 (Photofunctionalization) 탄화수소 제거 전략의 기전적 근거.

## Summary
In vitro study (MC3T3-E1 cells) providing the first direct dose-response evidence that hydrocarbon deposition on titanium attenuates osteoblast activity. C/Ti ratios (0.3, 0.7, 1.0) were experimentally controlled on mirror-polished cpTi. Cell attachment and spreading were concentration-dependently suppressed. At C/Ti=1.0, ALP activity and calcium mineralization were reduced by >40%. Contextualises the BIC plateau (45-65%) observed clinically as a direct consequence of unavoidable hydrocarbon accumulation on commercial implant surfaces (17.9-76.5% C detected across 34 commercial implants).

## Key Contributions
- First study directly testing hydrocarbon dose-response on osteoblast activity
- Mechanistic explanation for BIC plateau at 45-65% — long-standing mystery resolved
- Commercial implants carry 17.9-76.5% surface carbon → all have compromised osteoconductivity
- Rational basis for UV-PF (removes hydrocarbons) as countermeasure
