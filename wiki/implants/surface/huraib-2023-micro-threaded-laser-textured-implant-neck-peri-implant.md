## Three-line Summary
Systematic review (PRISMA) of 15 controlled human clinical studies (6 RCT, 5 prospective, 4 retrospective; 2013–2022) comparing rough micro-threaded or laser micro-textured (LMS) implant-neck designs against smooth machined-neck implants for marginal bone loss (MBL) and peri-implant soft tissue outcomes; meta-analysis was not feasible due to heterogeneity.
Rough micro-threaded necks showed markedly lower post-loading MBL (0.12±0.17mm to 3.25±0.4mm) than smooth machined necks (0.38±0.51mm to 3.75±0.4mm); LMS-neck implants showed lower 3-year probing depth (PD) than machined-neck implants (2.3±0.7mm vs 3.8±0.8mm), with similar gingival recession between groups.
Roughened micro-threaded/laser-microtextured neck designs significantly reduce functional-loading MBL and PD versus machined necks, but all included RCTs had short follow-up (mostly 12–24 months) and four studies found no significant between-group MBL difference — long-term (>5yr) RCTs are still needed.

## Summary
This PRISMA systematic review synthesized 15 controlled human clinical studies (6 RCTs, 5 prospective and 4 retrospective cohorts, published 2013–2022) investigating whether implant-neck surface micro-topography — rough micro-threading versus laser micro-texturing (LMS, 8–12µm microgrooves, e.g. Laser-Lok) — improves marginal bone loss (MBL) and peri-implant soft tissue outcomes relative to smooth machined-neck implants. Because of substantial heterogeneity in loading protocol, implant site, and follow-up duration across included studies, meta-analysis was not feasible. Rough micro-threaded necks consistently showed lower post-loading MBL than machined necks across most included studies, and LMS-neck implants showed both lower probing depth (PD) and comparable gingival recession versus machined necks over 3-year follow-up. However, four of the fifteen included studies found no statistically significant MBL difference between neck designs, and follow-up in nearly all RCTs was limited to 12–24 months (one reached 60 months), so the review calls for longer-term, well-designed RCTs before the neck-design advantage can be considered conclusively established.

## Key Contributions
- Isolates implant-**neck** micro-topography (micro-threads vs LMS vs machined) as the study variable, distinct from body/thread-surface roughness technology (SLA/CA/UV) already covered elsewhere in this category.
- Quantifies MBL and PD advantages for rough micro-threaded and LMS-neck designs across a 15-study evidence base spanning 12–120 months of follow-up.
- Surfaces an internal contradiction worth flagging clinically: while MBL trends favor rough/micro-threaded necks, one included study found smooth-neck implants had a significantly *lower* peri-implantitis frequency (2.92% vs 14.41%, p<0.001) — MBL and infection risk did not move in the same direction in every study.
- Explains a proposed mechanism for the LMS effect: collagen fibers attach perpendicular (rather than parallel) to the LMS collar, potentially limiting epithelial downgrowth and crestal bone resorption.

## Methodology
PRISMA-guided SR with PICOS framework; searched PubMed, Scopus, and EMBASE (via EBSCO) for English-language studies January 2013–August 2022. 205 electronic + 42 manual hits → 189 after deduplication → 97 full-text reviewed → 15 studies included (6 RCT, 5 prospective, 4 retrospective; participants 15–400 per study, ages 22–80). Primary outcome: MBL (radiographic — periapical films in 10 studies, CBCT in 2, panoramic in 3). Secondary outcomes: bleeding on probing, plaque index, width of attached gingiva, gingival recession, clinical attachment loss (CAL), probing depth (PD). Quality assessed via Cochrane RoB tool (RCTs) and Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (cohort studies); meta-analysis not performed due to heterogeneity.

## Results
| Outcome | Rough micro-threaded / LMS neck | Smooth machined neck |
|---|---|---|
| Post-loading MBL range | 0.12±0.17mm – 3.25±0.4mm | 0.38±0.51mm – 3.75±0.4mm |
| 10-year MBL (LMS) | 1.23±0.21mm | 2.8±0.9mm |
| 2-year MBL (Laser-Lok) | 0.49±0.34mm | 1.07±0.30mm (non-Laser-Lok) |
| 12-month MBL (rough + PS + internal connection) | 0.12±0.17mm | 0.38±0.51mm (machined + external connection) |
| 3-year early-loading PD (LMS) | 2.3±0.7mm | 3.8±0.8mm |
| Gingival recession (3-year) | 1.08±0.4mm | 2.46±0.3mm |
| 5–9 year PD | 4.24±0.49mm | 4.29±1.13mm |
| 5–9 year CAL | 3.95±1.04mm | 3.42±1.67mm |
| Peri-implantitis frequency (one study) | 14.41% (non-smooth) | 2.92% (smooth) |

Meta-analysis: not feasible (heterogeneity). Quality: 4/6 RCTs (67%) high/unclear risk of bias for allocation concealment; 3/6 (43%) performance bias; all cohort studies rated good quality (NOS).
