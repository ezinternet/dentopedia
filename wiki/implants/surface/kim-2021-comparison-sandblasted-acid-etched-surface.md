## Three-line Summary

Prospective randomized controlled trial (J Korean Assoc Oral Maxillofac Surg 2021;47:175-182, Seoul National University Bundang Hospital; 27 patients, 31 implants) comparing SLA implants (Osstem SA) with nano-HA-coated hydrophilic SLA implants (Hiossen NH) in the posterior maxilla with ≥4 mm residual bone height.

Primary ISQ was 73.86 ± 6.40 vs 71.24 ± 5.32 (P = 0.222) and secondary ISQ 79.07 ± 5.21 vs 78.29 ± 4.74 (P = 0.667); the hydrophilic group gained more ISQ during healing (+7.06 vs +5.21, P = 0.265) and mean MBL was 0.045 vs 0.032 mm (P = 0.457).

With primary ISQ ≥65, a 3-month early-loading protocol produced 100% success and survival in both groups — the hydrophilic surface expressed itself as faster stability recovery, not as a better 1-year outcome.

## 세줄요약

전향적 무작위 대조 임상시험 (대한구강악안면외과학회지 2021;47:175-182, 분당서울대; 환자 27명/임플란트 31개): 상악 구치부 잔존골높이 ≥4 mm에서 샌드블라스트·산부식 (Sandblasted and Acid-etched, SLA) 임플란트 (오스템 SA) vs 나노 수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HA) 코팅 친수성 SLA 임플란트 (Hiossen NH)를 3개월 조기부하로 비교.

초기 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 73.86 vs 71.24 (P=0.222), 2차 ISQ 79.07 vs 78.29 (P=0.667); NH군의 치유기 ISQ 상승폭이 더 컸고 (+7.06 vs +5.21, P=0.265), 평균 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 0.045 vs 0.032 mm (P=0.457).

초기 ISQ ≥65 확보 시 상악 구치부 3개월 조기부하는 표면 종류와 무관하게 성공률·생존율 100% — 친수성 표면의 이점은 1년 결과가 아니라 안정성 회복 속도로 나타남.

## Summary

This single-centre RCT asks a narrow, practical question: in the posterior maxilla where primary stability is hardest to obtain, does a hydrophilic nano-HA-coated SLA surface buy anything over plain SLA when the loading protocol is already shortened to 3 months? Thirty-one implants in 27 patients were randomized to Osstem SA or Hiossen NH, with residual bone height ≥4 mm and a hard entry gate of ISQ ≥65 at placement. The answer is a qualified no: primary and secondary ISQ, MBL (~0.04 mm in both groups) and success/survival (100%/100%) were all statistically indistinguishable. What differed was trajectory — the NH group entered with a numerically lower primary ISQ and gained more during healing, so that both groups converged at second-stage measurement. The authors read that steeper recovery as evidence of accelerated secondary stability from surface hydrophilicity, and conclude that a 3-month early-loading protocol is defensible for either surface as long as the ≥65 ISQ gate is respected. Because the two arms used different implant systems rather than the same implant with two surfaces, that mechanistic reading stays an interpretation rather than a demonstrated surface effect.

## Key Contributions

- Provides randomized clinical data for a nano-HA-coated hydrophilic SLA surface in the single most difficult indication for early loading (posterior maxilla, short implants, frequent sinus/GBR co-surgery).
- Shows identical 1-year endpoints (success/survival 100%, MBL 0.045 vs 0.032 mm, all NS) — the surface does not change the outcome when primary stability is adequate.
- Localizes the surface effect to the healing trajectory: +7.06 vs +5.21 ISQ, not significant but directionally consistent with faster secondary stability.
- Operationalizes the loading decision as a threshold — primary ISQ ≥65 — rather than a fixed healing time.

## Methodology

**Design.** Prospective randomized controlled trial, single centre, enrolment June 2018-June 2019. 39 patients screened; 27 patients / 31 implants analyzed.

**Inclusion.** Complete jaw growth; one or two consecutive missing teeth in the unilateral posterior maxilla; >4 mm residual alveolar bone height; sufficient buccopalatal and mesiodistal bone; opposing teeth present; **ISQ ≥65 immediately after placement**.

**Groups.** A = SA implants (Osstem, Korea), 13 patients / 14 implants; B = NH implants (Hiossen, USA) — SLA surface with ≤10 nm bioabsorbable nano-HA partial coating, 14 patients / 17 implants.

**Surgery.** One-stage in 13/14 (A) and 12/17 (B). Adjunctive surgery at placement: A — 9 sinus lift with graft, 4 GBR; B — 5 SLwBG, 3 SLwBG+GBR, 2 SLwoBG, 1 GBR.

**Outcomes.** ISQ at placement (primary) and at second-stage/loading (secondary) by RFA; MBL on radiographs; success and survival.

- ISQ rose significantly within both groups (P < 0.001).
- MBL was independent of one- vs two-stage surgery (A P = 0.143; B P = 0.115) and of adjunctive surgery type in A (P = 0.565). In B the only MBL difference was combined SLwBG+GBR versus no adjunctive surgery (P = 0.024).
- No peri-implantitis, mobility or patient discomfort through the latest visit.

## Limitations

Only 31 implants with ~12.5-month follow-up; underpowered for anything but large differences. The arms differ in implant **system** (manufacturer, macrogeometry, connection), so surface chemistry is confounded with brand design. ISQ is a surrogate for osseointegration and noisy. Randomization method, allocation concealment and blinding are not described. Nearly all implants were short (~8.8 mm) and many had simultaneous sinus lift or GBR, limiting transfer to simpler single-tooth sites.
