## Three-line Summary

Prospective multicenter RCT (n=117 implants; 112 at 1 year) across University of Zurich, Basel, and Graz; screw-retained all-ceramic single crowns on two-piece ZrO₂ implants (Ti-base connection) vs Ti implants (conventional Ti-base), diameter 4.1 mm.
Restoration survival 100% in both groups; no significant differences in overall technical complications or peri-implant soft tissue (PI, PBI, PaI); veneered ZrO₂ crowns had significantly higher complications than monolithic crowns, irrespective of implant material.
ZrO₂ implants demonstrate comparable 1-year prosthetic outcomes to Ti implants; crown design (monolithic vs veneered) is a more important predictor of technical complications than implant substrate.

## Summary

This prospective multicenter RCT from three European university centers randomized patients to receive screw-retained, all-ceramic single crowns on two-piece ZrO₂ implants (with Ti-base abutment connection) or on conventional Ti implants (standard Ti-base). At 1 year (112/117 restorations available), restoration survival was 100% in both groups, with no significant differences in overall technical complications. Notably, crown design was a stronger predictor of complications than implant material: veneered ZrO₂ crowns showed significantly higher complication rates than monolithic restorations regardless of implant type. Peri-implant soft tissue parameters were comparable between groups. These results provide early evidence that ZrO₂ implants with Ti-base connections support equivalent prosthetic outcomes to Ti implants, with MBL data (primary outcome) reported separately.

## Key Contributions

- First multicenter RCT reporting 1-year prosthetic/technical outcomes for two-piece ZrO₂ implants
- 100% restoration survival on ZrO₂ implants — equivalent to Ti implants
- Crown design (monolithic vs veneered) outweighs implant material as complication predictor
- Peri-implant soft tissue parameters equivalent between ZrO₂ and Ti implants at 1 year
- Supports use of two-piece ZrO₂ implants with Ti-base connection for all-ceramic single crowns
