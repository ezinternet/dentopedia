## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth prospective clinical study + parallel in vitro arm (n=10 patients, 20 SA-surface implants; UV irradiation 5 min immediately before placement vs. non-irradiated contralateral): ISQ measured biweekly by resonance frequency analysis to week 14; in vitro cell behavior on SA titanium disks with and without UV.

UV-irradiated implants showed significantly greater implant stability quotient (ISQ) at measurement intervals 3, 4, and 6 (p<0.05); in vitro, UV increased contact angle reduction and roughness, significantly increased cell proliferation at 48 h (p<0.05); type I collagen production was not significantly different.

The selective ISQ effect (intervals 3–4–6 significant, 1–2–5 not) and the null collagen result suggest UV-PF accelerates early proliferative attachment kinetics without affecting late-stage matrix synthesis — a mechanistically consistent finding with the hydrocarbon-removal model.

Split-mouth 전향적 임상 연구 + 병렬 in vitro (n=10, SA 티타늄 임플란트 20개; 식립 직전 5분 자외선 조사 vs. 대측 비조사): 이중 주파수 공명 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) 기반 임플란트 안정성지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)를 14주까지 2주 간격 측정.

## Summary

This study employed a split-mouth prospective design paired with a parallel in vitro arm to evaluate UV photofunctionalization on sandblasted acid-etched (SA) titanium surfaces. Ten patients received SA implants in bilateral sites, with one side receiving 5-minute UV irradiation immediately before placement; ISQ was measured every two weeks to week 14 by resonance frequency analysis. The clinical result showed significantly higher ISQ in the UV arm at measurement intervals 3, 4, and 6, with no significant difference at intervals 1, 2, or 5. The in vitro arm confirmed that UV irradiation reduces contact angle and increases roughness of SA disks, and significantly improves cell proliferation at 48 h without affecting type I collagen production or cell viability. The null collagen finding is mechanistically interesting: it suggests UV-PF selectively enhances early-attachment kinetics (proliferation phase) without altering the late-stage differentiation/matrix synthesis phase, consistent with the hydrocarbon-removal model where the pellicle specifically blocks initial cell-surface interaction. The study's main limitation is sample size (n=10, 20 implants), and the UV device and wavelength are not specified in the abstract, limiting comparison with the Houshmand 2023 wavelength-dependency data.

## Key Contributions

- Provides a split-mouth prospective clinical confirmation of the UV-PF ISQ improvement signal from a novel Brazilian center (independent replication).
- 5-minute pre-treatment protocol — shorter than most published protocols (12–48 h standard, 15–20 min in other clinical studies) — still shows clinical-level ISQ difference.
- Null type I collagen finding at both time points: suggests UV-PF's mechanism is confined to early attachment/proliferation kinetics, not late-stage differentiation.
- Parallel in vitro + clinical design on the same SA+UV protocol confirms that cell-level and clinical-level responses are consistent.

## Methodology

**In vitro:** SA Ti disks, UV irradiation (device and wavelength not specified in abstract; duration 30 min); contact angle, roughness (Ra), cytotoxicity, cell proliferation, adhesion, type I collagen. **Clinical:** 10 patients, split-mouth (randomly assigned SA+UV on one side, non-irradiated contralateral); UV applied for 5 min immediately pre-placement; ISQ measured biweekly by Osstell resonance frequency analysis to week 14 (measurement intervals 1–6). Statistics: not specified in abstract.

## Results

- **Contact angle**: SA + UV significantly lower than SA alone (p<0.05)
- **Roughness**: SA + UV significantly higher (p<0.05)
- **Cell viability**: no significant difference at any time point
- **Cell proliferation at 48 h**: SA + UV significantly greater (p<0.05)
- **Type I collagen**: no significant difference
- **ISQ (clinical)**: UV-irradiated significantly greater at intervals 3, 4, 6 (p<0.05); intervals 1, 2, 5 not significant
