## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (25 patients, 50 PS implants: 25 crestal + 25 at 1 mm subcrestal; posterior mandible) comparing MBL and soft-tissue outcomes over 3 years; within-patient design maximizes internal validity.

1 mm subcrestal: significantly less MBL at 1 yr (0.24 ± 0.11 vs 0.41 ± 0.14 mm; p < 0.001) and 3 yr (0.29 ± 0.15 vs 0.48 ± 0.17 mm; p < 0.001); soft tissue health comparable; survival 100%.

1 mm subcrestal placement in PS implants provides a consistent, statistically significant ~0.17–0.19 mm MBL benefit over crestal at up to 3 years; complements Stacchi 2023 (1 mm vs 2 mm) to form a complete crestal → 1 mm → 2 mm placement depth picture.

## Summary

This split-mouth RCT placed one crestal and one 1 mm subcrestal platform-switched implant in each of 25 patients in the posterior mandible, with 3-year radiographic and clinical follow-up. The within-patient design controls for systemic and site-level confounders, providing strong internal validity. At both 1 and 3 years, 1 mm subcrestal placement showed significantly less MBL with no soft-tissue disadvantage. The magnitude of benefit (~0.17–0.19 mm) is modest but consistent and statistically robust across two timepoints. Together with Stacchi 2023 (which showed no difference between 1 mm and 2 mm), these two trials establish a clinical framework: 1 mm subcrestal is meaningfully better than crestal, but going from 1 mm to 2 mm adds no further average MBL reduction (though 2 mm prevents implant surface exposure).

## Key Contributions
- Split-mouth design provides within-patient comparison (high internal validity)
- Significant MBL benefit of 1 mm subcrestal over crestal at 1yr AND 3yr (p < 0.001 both)
- Δ MBL: ~0.17 mm at 1yr, ~0.19 mm at 3yr — consistent, stable benefit over time
- No soft-tissue disadvantage to 1 mm subcrestal
- Forms a clinical triad with Stacchi 2023 and Terzioglu 2024 on placement depth

## Methodology
- Design: split-mouth RCT; each patient = internal control
- n = 25 patients; 50 implants; posterior mandible
- Implants: bone-level PS design; identical implants in both groups
- Loading: 3-month conventional
- Outcomes: MBL (periapical radiographs), PPD, BOP, plaque score at 1yr, 3yr
- Statistics: Wilcoxon signed-rank test for within-patient comparison
