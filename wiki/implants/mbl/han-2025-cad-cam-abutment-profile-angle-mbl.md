## Three-line Summary

Retrospective clinical study (Yonsei University; 206 patients / 260 posterior single-tooth implant restorations, internal conical connection with platform switching) measuring CAD-CAM abutment profile angle at 0–1, 1–2, and 2–3 mm below the implant-abutment junction against marginal bone loss (MBL) from delivery to ≥7 years.

Convex emergence profile had greater MBL than concave/straight at every follow-up (P<.001); profile angle correlated with MBL only in the 0–1 and 1–2 mm ranges (not 2–3 mm, P>.05), with cut-offs 34° mesial / 28° distal (0–1 mm) and 33° / 20° (1–2 mm).

The angle threshold is depth- and side-dependent near the implant-abutment junction, refining the generic >30° rule — but this is an abstract-only record, so effect sizes and adjusted estimates cannot be checked.

후향적 임상연구(연세대; 환자 206명·후방부 단일치 임플란트 보철 260개, 내부 원추형 연결 + 플랫폼 스위칭): 임플란트-지대주 접합부에서 0–1·1–2·2–3 mm 하방의 CAD-CAM 지대주 프로파일 각도를 장착 시부터 7년 이상의 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)과 분석.

볼록 출현윤곽(Convex Emergence Profile)이 오목·직선형보다 모든 추적 시점에서 MBL이 컸고(P<.001), 각도와 MBL의 상관은 0–1·1–2 mm 구간에서만 유의(2–3 mm는 P>.05); 역치는 0–1 mm에서 근심 34°·원심 28°, 1–2 mm에서 33°·20°.

## Summary

This retrospective clinical study (206 patients, 260 posterior single-tooth restorations; Yonsei University) asked whether the transmucosal contour of CAD-CAM abutments predicts marginal bone loss (MBL) when measured at specific subgingival depths rather than as a single emergence angle. All implants used internal conical seal connections with platform switching. Digital periapical radiographs supplied emergence profile and profile angles at 0–1, 1–2, and 2–3 mm from the implant-abutment junction, with MBL tracked from restoration delivery to 7 years or more. Convex profiles showed greater MBL than concave or straight profiles at every follow-up (P<.001). Angle–MBL correlation was significant only within 0–2 mm of the junction — not at 2–3 mm — and the c-index cut-offs were 34° mesial / 28° distal (0–1 mm) and 33° / 20° (1–2 mm). The practical message is that the harmful angle zone is near the junction and its threshold is side- and depth-specific, not a single ~30° number; magnitudes cannot be checked from the available abstract.

## Key Contributions

- Depth-resolved profile angle (0–1, 1–2, 2–3 mm) instead of one global emergence angle.
- Convex emergence profile → greater MBL at all follow-ups (P<.001).
- Significant angle–MBL correlation confined to the 0–1 and 1–2 mm zones.
- Mesial/distal- and depth-specific c-index cut-offs: 34°/28° and 33°/20°.
- Design-specific population: platform-switched internal conical-seal implants.

## Methodology

- Design: retrospective clinical study; 206 patients / 260 posterior single-tooth implant-supported restorations.
- Implants: internal conical seal connection, platform-switched; CAD-CAM abutments.
- Imaging: digital periapical radiographs; emergence profile and angle at three subgingival distance ranges; MBL from delivery to ≥7 years.
- Statistics: linear mixed model with Bonferroni post hoc for profile × time; Pearson correlation and c-index-derived cut-offs with independent t tests (α=.05).

## Results
