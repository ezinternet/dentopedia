## Three-line Summary

2-year randomized clinical trial (32 patients, 40 submerged bone-level platform-switched implants) comparing 1.5 mm subcrestal placement with epicrestal placement plus soft-tissue tenting over 2 mm healing abutments in patients with vertical soft tissue ≤2 mm.

At 2 years, subcrestal Group 1 lost 0.18 ± 0.32 mm of crestal bone versus 0.51 ± 0.4 mm in epicrestal/tented Group 2 (P = .001); subcrestal bone remodeling was 1.17 ± 0.51 mm.

Epicrestal tenting raised vertical tissue thickness from 1.85 ± 0.26 to 3.65 ± 0.41 mm (P = .005) yet preserved bone less well; subcrestal placement is the more effective bone-preserving strategy in thin-tissue patients (abstract-only).

## 세줄요약

2년 무작위 임상시험(Randomized Clinical Trial, RCT) — 수직적 연조직 두께 ≤2 mm인 32명에게 골수준 플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching, PS) 임플란트 40개를 식립해 1.5 mm 골하방 식립(Subcrestal Placement) 군과 2 mm 치유 지대주(Healing Abutment)로 연조직 텐팅(Soft Tissue Tenting)한 골정상 식립 군을 비교.

2년 후 골하방 군의 골능 소실은 0.18 ± 0.32 mm, 텐팅 골정상 군은 0.51 ± 0.4 mm로 유의한 차이(P = .001)가 있었고, 골하방 군의 골 리모델링은 1.17 ± 0.51 mm였다.

텐팅은 연조직 두께를 1.85 ± 0.26 mm에서 3.65 ± 0.41 mm로 유의하게 증가시켰으나(P = .005) 골 보존은 골하방 식립이 우위 — 얇은 연조직 환자에서 골하방 식립이 더 효과적(초록만 확보).

## Summary

This 2-year randomized clinical trial enrolled 32 patients with vertically thin peri-implant soft tissue (≤2 mm) who received 40 submerged bone-level platform-switched implants, allocated either to 1.5 mm subcrestal placement or to epicrestal placement with soft-tissue tenting over 2 mm healing abutments. At second-stage surgery all implants received 4 mm healing abutments, soft-tissue thickness was measured in the epicrestal group, and the implants were restored with zirconia-based screw-retained single restorations; radiographs were taken at second-stage surgery, restoration delivery, and 2 years. The subcrestal group lost significantly less crestal bone than the epicrestal/tented group (0.18 ± 0.32 mm vs 0.51 ± 0.4 mm, P = .001), while the tenting maneuver significantly increased vertical soft-tissue thickness (1.85 ± 0.26 mm to 3.65 ± 0.41 mm, P = .005). The authors conclude that subcrestal placement is the more effective bone-preserving strategy in thin-tissue patients even though tenting reliably thickens the soft tissue. Only the publisher abstract was retrievable, so all figures here are abstract-level and no full-text tables or confidence intervals were available.

## Key Contributions

- Randomized head-to-head comparison of the two leading thin-tissue strategies — 1.5 mm subcrestal placement versus epicrestal placement with 2 mm healing-abutment soft-tissue tenting — around submerged bone-level platform-switched implants.
- Demonstrates a significant 2-year crestal-bone advantage for subcrestal placement (0.18 ± 0.32 mm vs 0.51 ± 0.4 mm, P = .001) in patients with ≤2 mm vertical tissue.
- Shows that the two aims are separable: tenting significantly increased vertical soft-tissue thickness (P = .005) but did not reproduce the bone-preserving benefit of subcrestal placement.
- Reports subcrestal bone remodeling (bone crest to implant neck) of 1.17 ± 0.51 mm at 2 years, using the authors' explicit distance definitions.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial with 2-year radiographic follow-up (original article).
- **Population**: 32 patients with vertical soft-tissue thickness ≤2 mm; 40 submerged bone-level platform-switched implants.
- **Groups**: Group 1 — 1.5 mm subcrestal placement; Group 2 — epicrestal placement with soft-tissue tenting over 2 mm healing abutments.
- **Prosthetic flow**: 4 mm healing abutments at second-stage surgery, soft-tissue thickness measured in the epicrestal group, zirconia-based screw-retained single restorations.
- **Radiographic timepoints**: Second-stage surgery, restoration delivery, 2-year follow-up.
- **Outcomes**: Bone loss = implant neck to first radiographically visible bone-to-implant contact; bone remodeling = bone crest to implant neck; vertical soft-tissue thickness in the epicrestal group.
- **Statistics**: Mann–Whitney U test, significance threshold 0.05.
- **Evidence note**: Abstract-only (publisher landing page) — no full-text methods detail, allocation/concealment information, or per-patient data available.

## Results

| Outcome | Group 1 — 1.5 mm subcrestal | Group 2 — epicrestal + 2 mm tenting | P value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crestal bone loss at 2 years | 0.18 ± 0.32 mm | 0.51 ± 0.4 mm | .001 |
| Bone remodeling (bone crest → implant neck) | 1.17 ± 0.51 mm | not reported in abstract | — |
| Vertical tissue thickness (epicrestal group) | — | 1.85 ± 0.26 mm → 3.65 ± 0.41 mm after 2 mm healing abutment | .005 |

Authors' stated conclusion: subcrestal implant placement significantly reduces crestal bone loss compared with vertical soft-tissue thickening by tenting of epicrestally placed implants, although soft-tissue tenting significantly increases soft-tissue thickness.
