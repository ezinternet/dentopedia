## Three-line Summary

PRISMA SR+MA of 7 RCTs (395 patients, 645 implants, 2020–2024) comparing one-abutment-one-time (OAOT) vs repeated abutment disconnections in bone-level, platform-switched, conical-connection implants; marginal bone loss (MBL) was the primary outcome.

Pooled random-effects meta-analysis (6 parallel-group RCTs, 603 implants) showed statistically significant MBL reduction favouring OAOT (MD −0.23 mm; 95% CI −0.44 to −0.02); no significant difference in probing depth; implant survival >94% in both groups; I² = 77%.

OAOT provides a modest but consistent crestal bone preservation benefit in PS conical-connection implants, without clear advantages for other peri-implant outcomes; clinical relevance of the 0.23 mm difference should be interpreted cautiously.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA SR+MA, 7 RCT (환자 395명, 임플란트 645개, 2020–2024): 골수준 플랫폼 스위칭(PS, Platform Switching) + 원뿔 연결(Conical Connection) 임플란트에서 1어버트먼트-1회(OAOT, One-Abutment-One-Time) vs 반복 탈착; 주요 결과: 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss).

6 parallel-group RCT 풀링(임플란트 603개): OAOT에서 MBL 유의하게 적음 (MD −0.23 mm; 95%CI −0.44~−0.02); 탐침 깊이(PD, Probing Depth) 차이 없음; 생존율 양군 >94%.

PS + 원뿔 연결 임플란트에서 OAOT는 MBL 보존에 소폭이나 일관된 이점; 임상 유의성은 제한적 — 비심미 부위에서의 0.23 mm 차이는 신중하게 해석.

## Summary

This 2026 SR+MA (PROSPERO-registered) is the first meta-analysis restricted to bone-level, platform-switched implants with **internal conical connections**, comparing the OAOT protocol (definitive abutment placed once at surgery and never removed) against protocols involving repeated disconnections. Seven RCTs (2020–2024, 395 patients, 645 implants) were included; six contributed to the pooled quantitative analysis. All seven studies showed numerically lower MBL with OAOT; only three reached individual statistical significance. The pooled result confirmed a significant reduction: MD −0.23 mm (95% CI −0.44 to −0.02). Secondary outcomes — probing depth, implant survival, and complication rates — were comparable between protocols.

## Key Contributions
- First SR+MA restricted to PS + conical-connection implants for OAOT vs repeated disconnections
- 7 RCTs, 645 implants, follow-up range 1–5 years
- Pooled MBL benefit of OAOT: MD −0.23 mm (statistically significant)
- Probing depth: no significant difference (MD +0.07 mm, NS)
- Survival rates >94% in both groups; prosthetic complications higher in repeated-disconnection group (1 vs 9, single study)
- Substantial heterogeneity (I² = 77%) limits precision of pooled estimate

## Methodology
- PRISMA SR+MA; search: MEDLINE/PubMed; November 2018 – November 2025
- Inclusion: RCTs; bone-level PS implants; internal conical connection; adults ≥18; OAOT vs ≥1 disconnection/reconnection; minimum 10 patients per arm
- Exclusion: flat-to-flat/external hex; tissue-level; mixed connection types; secondary publications of same population
- Primary outcome: MBL (mm); Secondary: PD, survival, complications
- Statistics: random-effects REML; MD with 95% CI; Egger regression for funnel asymmetry
- Risk of bias: JBI checklist — 6/7 studies low risk; 1/7 moderate

## Results

| Study | n implants | Follow-up | OAOT MBL | Control MBL | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Praça 2020 | 24 | 2yr | −0.18 mm | −0.13 mm | 0.792 |
| D'Avenia 2020 | 128 | 5yr | 0.11 mm | 0.48 mm | **0.012** |
| Ríos-Santos 2020 | 258 | 1yr | 0.36 mm | 0.48 mm | >0.05 |
| Hamudi 2021 (split-mouth) | 42 | 3yr | 0.35 mm | 0.57 mm | **0.005** |
| Moreira 2021 | 53 | 1yr | 0.14 mm | 0.21 mm | 0.33 |
| Calatrava 2024 | 80 | 1yr | −0.53 mm | −0.01 mm | **0.008** |
| Sanz-Sánchez 2024 | 60 | 5yr | 0.53 mm | 0.97 mm | 0.063 |

**Pooled (6 parallel RCTs, 603 implants): MD −0.23 mm (95%CI −0.44 to −0.02; I² = 77%)**

- Probing depth (3 studies, 164 implants): MD +0.07 mm (95%CI −0.12 to +0.26; NS)
- Implant systems: Morse Taper M+N, Ankylos C/X, KlocknerVEGA, MISC1 (MIS), ConelogScrew-Line (Camlog)
