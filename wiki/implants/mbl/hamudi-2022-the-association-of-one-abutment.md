## Three-line Summary

Prospective randomised clinical study of 21 patients receiving two adjacent non-submerged platform-switched conical implants; definitive multiunit abutments connected at surgery and never removed (DEF) versus healing abutments disconnected/reconnected 3–5 times (HEA), followed for 3 years.

Survival and success were 100% with no bone loss >1.9 mm; 3-year marginal bone loss was −0.35 ± 0.69 mm (DEF) versus −0.57 ± 0.80 mm (HEA), a statistically significant difference, while peri-implant mucositis occurred in 38.1% (DEF) and 41.9% (HEA).

Immediate definitive-abutment connection reduced bone loss after repeated disconnections, but the authors judged the between-group difference minimal and its clinical relevance doubtful.

전향적 무작위 임상연구(환자 21명, 인접 비매몰 플랫폼 스위칭 원뿔 연결 임플란트 2개씩): 수술 시 장착 후 탈착하지 않은 최종 다유닛 지대주(DEF) vs 치유 지대주를 보철 단계에서 3~5회 탈착·재장착(HEA), 3년 추적.

## Summary

This prospective randomised clinical study compared never-removed definitive multiunit abutments (DEF) with healing abutments disconnected and reconnected three to five times (HEA) in 21 patients each receiving two adjacent non-submerged platform-switched conical implants. At 3 years, no implant was lost and no site exceeded 1.9 mm of bone loss. Marginal bone loss was significantly lower in the DEF group (−0.35 ± 0.69 mm versus −0.57 ± 0.80 mm), while mucositis prevalence was similar (38.1% versus 41.9%). The trial is one of the RCTs pooled in the 2026 one-abutment-one-time meta-analysis (listed there as "Hamudi 2021"). Its importance lies as much in the authors' own interpretation: a statistically significant but small difference was judged of doubtful clinical relevance.

## Key Contributions
- 3-year follow-up in a literature dominated by 6–12 month OAOT comparisons.
- Restricted to platform-switched conical-connection implants — the exact niche isolated by the 2026 SR+MA.
- Reports mucositis alongside MBL: 38.1% vs 41.9%, no separation.
- Authors explicitly temper the clinical relevance of a significant 0.22 mm MBL difference.

## Methodology

Prospective clinical study, consecutive patients treated 2015–2017 by two experienced operators, ethics approval 0002277-1 at Tel Aviv University. Twenty-one patients (6 men, 15 women; mean age 66.23 ± 9.35) with partial edentulism requiring two adjacent implant-supported fixed restorations, full-mouth plaque score <25%, stable periodontitis, ≥8 mm/≥3.75 mm native bone, ≥2 mm buccal wall and ≥2 mm keratinized gingiva. The two adjacent implants per patient were randomly assigned to DEF or HEA (within-patient comparison). Periapical radiographs at baseline, 4–7 months (prosthetic delivery), 1 year and 3 years assessed MBL; survival, success and mucositis were recorded.
