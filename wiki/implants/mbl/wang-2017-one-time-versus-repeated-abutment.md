## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of eight studies (seven RCTs, one controlled clinical study) comparing one-time abutment connection with repeated abutment connection in platform-switched implants; 197 versus 214 implants, with bone, soft-tissue, probing-depth and complication outcomes.

One-time abutment favoured vertical bone change at 6 months (SMD 0.41 fixed / 3.23 random), 12 months (1.51 / 14.81) and 3 years (2.47 / 2.47), and soft-tissue change (0.21 / 0.23); probing depth and post-surgical complications showed no significant difference.

The authors conclude one-time abutment is superior, but explicitly flag small samples and limited original-study quality — the implausibly large random-effects SMDs are a statistical fragility signal, so the direction is more trustworthy than the magnitude.

체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석(무작위대조시험 7편 + 대조임상시험 1편, 총 8편): 플랫폼 스위칭 임플란트에서 1회 지대주 연결군(197개) vs 반복 연결군(214개) 비교; 골·연조직 변화, 탐침 깊이, 합병증 결과.

## Summary

This 2017 systematic review was the first meta-analysis dedicated to the one-time versus repeated abutment question in platform-switched implants. Eight studies (seven randomised trials and one controlled clinical study; 197 vs 214 implants) were pooled for vertical bone change, soft-tissue change, probing depth and complications. One-time abutment connection favoured bone outcomes at 6 months, 12 months and 3 years, and favoured soft-tissue change, with no significant separation in probing depth or complications. The authors concluded superiority while calling for larger, better RCTs. The reported random-effects SMDs (3.23 at 6 months, 14.81 at 12 months) are implausibly large for millimetre-scale bone outcomes and signal model fragility; the useful legacy of this paper is the direction, later quantified far more conservatively as MD −0.23 mm in the 2026 SR+MA.

## Key Contributions
- First meta-analysis focused specifically on OAOT vs repeated connection in platform-switched implants.
- Pools bone change at three time horizons plus soft tissue, probing depth and complications.
- Standardises a confusing terminology set ("repeated", "removable", "dis/reconnected", "healing") under one intervention label.
- Historical anchor: its "superior" conclusion contrasts with the modest modern estimate.

## Methodology

Systematic review + meta-analysis with a structured search of PubMed, Embase and Web of Science; no PROSPERO registration reported. RCTs and controlled clinical studies comparing one-time vs repeated abutment connection in platform-switched implants were included. Studies pooled: bone 6, soft tissue 3, probing depth 2, complications 4. Implant systems included Global, Ankylos, JDEvolution (JdentalCare), Straumann Bone Level and Conelog Screw-Line. Fixed- and random-effects standardised mean differences with 95% CI were reported.

## Results

| Outcome | Fixed-effect SMD | Random-effects SMD | Studies pooled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical bone change, 6 months | 0.41 | 3.23 | 6 (bone total) |
| Vertical bone change, 12 months | 1.51 | 14.81 | 6 (bone total) |
| Vertical bone change, 3 years | 2.47 | 2.47 | 6 (bone total) |
| Soft-tissue change | 0.21 | 0.23 | 3 |
| Probing depth | NS | NS | 2 |
| Post-surgical complications | NS | NS | 4 |

Implant counts: 197 (one-time) vs 214 (repeated). The fixed/random divergence at 6 and 12 months (0.41→3.23; 1.51→14.81) is internally implausible for a bone-change outcome — treat the magnitude as unreliable.
