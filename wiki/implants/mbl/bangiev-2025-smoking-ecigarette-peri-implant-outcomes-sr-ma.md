## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (literature to October 2025; random-effects) comparing cigarette, waterpipe, and e-cigarette vaping vs non-smokers on peri-implant MBL, plaque, BOP, and inflammatory cytokines (IL-1β, TNF-α).

All three modalities significantly increased MBL vs non-smokers — cigarette +2.16 mm, waterpipe +1.90 mm, e-cigarette +0.83 mm — and all paradoxically reduced BOP (nicotine-induced vasoconstriction); risk gradient: CS > WP > EC > NS.

E-cigarettes cause less peri-implant bone loss than cigarettes or waterpipe but still impose a +0.83 mm MBL burden vs non-smokers; the BOP reduction across all modalities masks true inflammatory activity.

## Summary

This SR+MA is the most recent (October 2025) analysis comparing three smoking modalities on peri-implant outcomes. The main finding is a clear risk gradient with cigarettes causing the most MBL (+2.16 mm), followed by waterpipe (+1.90 mm) and e-cigarettes (+0.83 mm), all vs non-smokers. The paradoxical BOP reduction across all three modalities — caused by nicotine-induced vasoconstriction — is an important clinical warning: reduced bleeding does not indicate implant health in smokers. Inflammatory cytokine elevations confirm ongoing inflammation. The data converge with Vámos 2024 (network MA through April 2023) but with updated e-cigarette evidence and explicit cytokine quantification.

## Key Contributions

- Most recent (Oct 2025) MBL quantification for all three smoking types simultaneously
- E-cigarette MBL +0.83 mm vs NS — still significant; not a safe alternative
- BOP paradox confirmed across all three modalities
- Cytokine data: CS highest IL-1β (+239.63 pg/mL); EC elevated IL-1β (+170 pg/mL) and TNF-α (+17.6 pg/mL)
- Risk gradient CS > WP > EC > NS explicitly established

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA; PRISMA; random-effects models
- Search: PubMed; October 2015–October 2025
- Inclusion: Adults with dental implants; any smoking modality vs NS
- Outcomes: MBL, plaque index, BOP, IL-1β, TNF-α
- Statistics: MD (MBL, cytokines), SMD (plaque, BOP)
