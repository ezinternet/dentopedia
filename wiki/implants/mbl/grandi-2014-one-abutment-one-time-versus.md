## Three-line Summary

Multicentre RCT (two private clinics; 28 patients randomized before extraction, 3 excluded for buccal wall fracture; 12 definitive-abutment vs 13 provisional-abutment patients) of immediately loaded post-extractive single implants, comparing abutment non-removal with a provisional abutment later replaced by a custom abutment, with 1-year follow-up after loading.

At 12 months no implant failed; marginal bone loss was 0.11 mm (SD 0.06) with a definitive abutment vs 0.58 mm (SD 0.11) with a provisional abutment — mean difference 0.48 mm (95% CI 0.40–0.55, P<0.0001) — with one biological and one mechanical complication, both successfully treated.

Non-removal of the surgical abutment maintained about 0.5 mm more bone, although the authors caution this may not have a clinical impact; the report is abstract-only with a small analyzed sample.

## Summary

This multicentre randomized controlled trial (Grandi et al., 2014; Eur J Oral Implantol 7(2):141-149) enrolled 28 patients needing a single post-extractive implant in the maxilla or mandible between the left second premolar and right second premolar, randomizing them shortly before extraction to a definitive platform-switched titanium abutment left in place (DA, n=12) or a platform-switched provisional abutment later removed for a custom abutment (PA, n=13); three patients were excluded for buccal wall fracture after extraction. Both arms received a non-occluding provisional single crown at surgery and definitive restoration at 4 months; in the PA arm the impression was made directly on the implant platform after abutment removal, whereas in the DA arm an impression of the abutment was made with a retraction cord. At the 12-month follow-up no implant had failed, one biological complication occurred in DA and one mechanical complication in PA, and all complications were successfully treated. Peri-implant bone loss was 0.11 mm (SD 0.06) in DA versus 0.58 mm (SD 0.11) in PA, a statistically significant mean difference of 0.48 mm (95% CI 0.40–0.55, P<0.0001). The authors concluded that non-removal of abutments placed at surgery maintained approximately 0.5 mm more bone around immediately restored post-extractive single implants than repeated removal, while explicitly noting this bone maintenance may not have a clinical impact. Only the PubMed structured abstract was retrieved, so methodological detail beyond the reported protocol is unavailable.

## Key Contributions

- Tests the one-abutment-one-time (non-removal) protocol specifically in **immediately loaded post-extractive single implants**, extending prior held RCT evidence from healed-ridge/subcrestal sites.
- Reports a large and statistically significant 12-month marginal bone advantage for non-removal: 0.11 mm vs 0.58 mm (mean difference 0.48 mm, 95% CI 0.40–0.55, P<0.0001).
- Demonstrates comparable short-term safety: no implant failures and only two minor, successfully managed complications across both arms.
- Independently corroborates the direction of effect later pooled for OAOT in platform-switched conical-connection implants, from patients outside that review's 2020–2024 inclusion window.

## Methodology

- **Design**: multicentre (two private clinics) randomized controlled trial; randomization shortly before tooth extraction (method/concealment not reported in the abstract).
- **Population**: 28 patients randomized; 3 excluded for buccal wall fracture after extraction; 25 analyzed (DA 12, PA 13). Single post-extractive implant in the maxilla or mandible, left second premolar to right second premolar area.
- **DA (definitive abutment)**: definitive platform-switched titanium abutment at surgery, never removed; impression of the abutment at 4 months using a retraction cord.
- **PA (provisional abutment)**: platform-switched provisional titanium abutment at surgery; removed at 4 months, impression directly on the implant platform, then custom-made definitive abutment.
- **Common protocol**: non-occluding provisional single crown; definitive restoration at 4 months; follow-up to 1 year after loading.
- **Outcomes**: implant failures, complications, marginal peri-implant bone level changes.
- **Limitations of the record**: abstract-only; no DOI in the PubMed record (PII 855781); radiographic measurement, blinded assessment and power calculation not available in the abstract.
