## Three-line Summary

5-year follow-up RCT (60 implants, 30 patients; Ankylos C/X bone-level PS implants) comparing OAOT vs conventional repeated-disconnection protocol in the posterior region; primary outcome was DIB change from surgery to 5 years.

DIB at 5 years: 0.53 mm OAOT vs 0.97 mm conventional (p = 0.063; numerical Δ = 0.44 mm); implant survival 100% in both groups; soft tissue parameters comparable at 5 years.

OAOT shows a 0.44 mm numerical bone advantage at 5 years that does not reach statistical significance; this is the longest follow-up OAOT RCT and the sole long-term data point anchoring the Abayov 2026 SR+MA pooled estimate.

## 세줄요약

5년 추적 무작위 대조 임상시험 (임플란트 60개, 환자 30명; Ankylos C/X 골수준 플랫폼 스위칭(PS) 임플란트): 1어버트먼트-1회(OAOT, One-Abutment-One-Time) vs 반복 탈착, 후방부; 주요 결과: 임플란트-골 거리(DIB, Distance Implant shoulder to Bone) 변화.

## Summary

This 5-year follow-up extends Ríos-Santos 2020 (the same cohort, 1-year primary publication) to provide the longest available RCT data on the OAOT protocol in bone-level platform-switched implants. Thirty patients received Ankylos C/X implants with either an immediate definitive abutment (OAOT, never removed) or a standard protocol with three abutment disconnections. At 5 years, OAOT showed a numerical bone level advantage of 0.44 mm (DIB: 0.53 vs 0.97 mm), but this did not reach statistical significance (p = 0.063). Implant survival remained 100% in both groups. Soft tissue outcomes including probing depth, BOP, and KT width were comparable between groups. This trial is one of seven included in the Abayov 2026 SR+MA, where its 5-year endpoint contributes the longest follow-up data for the pooled estimate (MD −0.23 mm).

## Key Contributions
- Longest follow-up OAOT RCT (5 years)
- Extension of Ríos-Santos 2020 same cohort; confirms no reversal of early trend
- DIB Δ = 0.44 mm in favor of OAOT; p = 0.063 (marginal non-significance)
- 100% survival at 5 years both groups
- No peri-implantitis cases in either group at 5 years
- Demonstrates durability of OAOT peri-implant health even if bone benefit NS at 5yr

## Methodology
- Design: parallel RCT; 5-year extension of Ríos-Santos 2020; Complutense University Madrid
- n = 30 patients; 60 implants (30 OAOT, 30 conventional)
- Implants: Ankylos C/X (Dentsply Sirona; bone-level; PS; conical connection)
- OAOT: definitive abutment at implant placement; retained at all follow-ups
- Conventional: 3 abutment changes (uncovery + impression + final delivery)
- Primary: DIB at 5 years; Secondary: PD, BOP, KT, recession, peri-implantitis, survival
