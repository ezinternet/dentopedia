## Three-line Summary

RCT of 66 bone-level PrimeTaper implants (45 patients) randomized to healing abutment (HA, n=34) or cover screw submerged (CS, n=32) protocol, with definitive zirconia crowns via fully digital workflow and 6-month follow-up. Both groups achieved 100% survival and comparable MBL at 6 months; the HA group had ~75% of implants without BuPA at follow-up (p<0.001), a significant soft-tissue advantage. Healing abutment protocol avoids flap re-entry, reduces bleeding during prosthetic procedures, and is equally effective to the submerged cover-screw approach at 6 months.

## Summary

This randomized controlled trial compared two healing protocols for bone-level single implants: the transmucosal healing abutment approach (non-submerged, 1-stage) and the submerged cover screw approach (2-stage with flap closure). Sixty-six PrimeTaper implants (Dentsply) were placed in 45 healthy adult patients and randomized to healing abutment (n=34) or cover screw (n=32) groups. A fully digital workflow was used for provisional and definitive zirconia crowns at 3–4 months post-surgery. Clinical and radiographic follow-up was performed at baseline, 3 months, and 6 months.

Both protocols achieved 100% implant survival and comparable MBL changes at 6 months. The healing abutment group demonstrated a significant soft-tissue benefit: approximately 75% of HA implants showed no BuPA at follow-up (p<0.001 vs CS group), indicating less mucosal inflammation at the time of prosthetic procedures. The Mombelli modified plaque score improved over time in both groups with no between-group difference. One mucositis case occurred in the HA group. The cover screw protocol required a second surgical procedure (flap re-entry) that the HA protocol entirely avoided, offering a simpler and less invasive treatment sequence.

## Key Contributions

- **Comparable MBL at 6 months**: neither submerged nor non-submerged protocol confers a bone-preservation advantage at 6 months in bone-level implants with digital prosthetics.
- **Soft-tissue advantage of healing abutment**: ~75% BuPA-free rate in HA group (p<0.001) suggests healthier peri-implant mucosa at prosthetic delivery.
- **No flap re-entry with healing abutment**: eliminates a second surgical appointment, reducing patient burden.
- **Digital workflow compatible**: fully digital prosthetic delivery (zirconia, 3–4 months) achieved without compromising clinical outcomes in either arm.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-center RCT
- **Sample**: 66 PrimeTaper implants (Dentsply, Mannheim, Germany) in 45 systemically healthy adult patients
- **Randomization**: Healing abutment (n=34 implants) vs Cover screw submerged with flap (n=32 implants)
- **Placement**: Bone-level, crestal; baseline groups balanced
- **Prosthetics**: Fully digital workflow; provisional crowns then definitive zirconia crowns at 3–4 months
- **Follow-up timepoints**: Baseline, 3 months, 6 months
- **Primary outcomes**: BuSP, BuPA, Mombelli modified plaque score, MBL (radiographic), prosthetic complications

## Results

| Outcome | Healing Abutment (n=34) | Cover Screw (n=32) | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant survival | 100% | 100% | — |
| BuPA absent at FU | ~75% | Significantly lower | p<0.001 |
| MBL at 6 months | Stable | Stable | NS (comparable) |
| Mombelli plaque score | Improved over time | Improved over time | NS |
| Mucositis | 1 case | 0 cases | — |
| Second surgery required | No | Yes | — |

Both techniques yielded stable peri-implant tissues. Healing abutment protocol reduced prosthetic-appointment bleeding and avoided second-stage surgery, with no compromise to bone stability.
