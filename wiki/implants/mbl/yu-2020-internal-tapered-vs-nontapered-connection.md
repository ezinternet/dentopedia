## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (SR+MA) of 7 in-vivo studies — 5 RCTs and 2 prospective cohorts, from 592 screened records — pooling PICO-structured comparisons of internal tapered vs internal nontapered implant-abutment connections.

No difference in implant survival (P=.47; RR 1.01, 95% CI 0.98–1.05) or complications (P=.47; RR 1.21, 95% CI 0.72–2.04), but marginal bone loss (P=.003; MD −0.43 mm, 95% CI −0.73 to −0.14) and probing depths (P=.002; MD −0.24 mm, 95% CI −0.39 to −0.09) were significantly lower with the tapered connection.

Clinical implication: choose the internal tapered connection for crestal-bone preservation rather than for survival gain; the evidence base is small (7 studies, abstract-only record here) and the complication CI is wide, so a taper-linked survival advantage is not supported.

## 세줄요약

계통리뷰와 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA) — 592편 검색 후 2인 독립 선별로 생체 내 무작위대조군시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 5편과 전향적 코호트 (Prospective Cohort) 2편, 총 7편을 대상으로 내부 테이퍼 연결 (Internal Tapered Connection) 과 내부 비테이퍼 연결 (Internal Nontapered Connection) 을 PICO (Population, Intervention, Control, Outcome) 로 비교.

임플란트 생존율(P=.47; 상대위험도 (Relative Risk, RR) 1.01, 95% 신뢰구간 (Confidence Interval, CI) 0.98–1.05)과 합병증 발생률(P=.47; RR 1.21, 95% CI 0.72–2.04)은 유의한 차이가 없었으나, 변연골흡수 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) (P=.003; 평균차 (Mean Difference, MD) −0.43 mm, 95% CI −0.73 to −0.14)와 탐침 깊이 (Probing Depth) (P=.002; MD −0.24 mm, 95% CI −0.39 to −0.09)는 테이퍼 연결에서 유의하게 낮았음.

임상적 함의: 테이퍼 연결의 선택 근거는 생존율 향상이 아니라 치조골 보존이며, 근거 본문이 7편의 소규모 연구에 제한되고 합병증 신뢰구간이 넓어 테이퍼 관련 생존 우위는 지지되지 않음.

## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis (Wuhan University, 2020) asked whether internal **tapered** implant-abutment connections perform better than internal **nontapered** ones — a contrast that had, by the authors' own statement, never been meta-analyzed before. After electronic searches of Cochrane Library, MEDLINE, and Embase plus a manual search (592 records), two reviewers independently screened and included 7 in-vivo studies (5 RCTs, 2 prospective cohorts). Pooled across four outcomes, the two connection types were **comparable** for implant survival (RR 1.01, 95% CI 0.98–1.05, P=.47) and for complication rates (RR 1.21, 95% CI 0.72–2.04, P=.47), whereas the internal tapered connection yielded **significantly less marginal bone loss** (MD −0.43 mm, 95% CI −0.73 to −0.14, P=.003) and **shallower probing depths** (MD −0.24 mm, 95% CI −0.39 to −0.09, P=.002). The practical reading is that taper choice is a **crestal-bone-preservation decision, not a survival decision**: the soft-tissue/bleeding and bone-level signals are where the taper pays off, while hard outcomes — implant loss and complications — show no measurable advantage. Rodrigues 2023 (J Prosthodont, DOI 10.1111/jopr.13655), pooling 12 RCTs and 1006 implants, independently reached the same direction of effect for bone-level outcome (SMD −0.80 mm), which materially strengthens confidence in the MBL finding; note that this page was built from the PubMed abstract only (paywalled full text), so risk-of-bias appraisal, heterogeneity statistics, and sensitivity analyses could not be read.

## Key Contributions

- 내부 테이퍼 vs 내부 비테이퍼 비교가 **기존 메타분석 공백**이었음을 명시하고, 이 문제를 4개 아웃컴으로 한 번에 풀링해 최초의 정량 답을 제시함.
- **"more efficient"라는 제목을 정량으로 분해**: 이득은 MBL(−0.43 mm)과 탐침 깊이(−0.24 mm)에서만 나오고, 생존(생존율 RR 1.01)과 합병증(RR 1.21, CI가 1 포함)은 무차이라는, **아웃컴별로 골라 읽어야 하는** 결론을 제시.
- 테이퍼 우위의 MBL 효과 크기를 메타분석 스케일로 정량화하여, 개별 코호트의 시점별 MBL 산포와 비교 가능한 척도를 제공(5년 시점 소실 보고와 대조 가능).
- 내부 연결 설계 논의가 외부 vs 내부 축에 머물던 상태에서, **내부 연결 내부의 변형**을 임상 근거로 비교한 점에서 기존 서술 리뷰의 공백을 채움.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (SR+MA) of in-vivo comparative studies
- **Question framework**: PICO — patients receiving internal tapered connection implants vs internal nontapered connection, on the prognosis outcomes of implant survival, marginal bone loss, probing depths, and complications
- **Databases**: Cochrane Library databases, MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase — plus a manual search
- **Restrictions**: English-language publications, no time (date) restrictions
- **Eligibility**: In-vivo randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and prospective cohort studies
- **Screening**: 592 records collected → independent screening by 2 reviewers → 5 RCTs + 2 prospective cohorts included
- **Outcomes / effect measures**: Dichotomous outcomes (implant survival, complications) as relative risk (RR); continuous outcomes (marginal bone loss, probing depth) as mean difference (MD); 95% confidence intervals throughout
- **Note**: abstract-only record here — risk-of-bias instrument, heterogeneity (I²/τ²) reporting, publication-bias testing, and sensitivity analysis are **not verifiable** from this source

## Results

**Pooled effect estimates — internal tapered vs internal nontapered**

| Outcome | Effect size | 95% CI | P | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implant survival rate | RR 1.01 | 0.98–1.05 | .47 | no significant difference |
| Complications | RR 1.21 | 0.72–2.04 | .47 | no significant difference |
| Marginal bone loss | MD −0.43 mm | −0.73 to −0.14 | .003 | **tapered significantly lower** |
| Probing depth | MD −0.24 mm | −0.39 to −0.09 | .002 | **tapered significantly lower** |

**Reading the intervals**

- Both continuous-outcome CIs sit entirely **below zero** (−0.73 to −0.14; −0.39 to −0.09) — the bone-level and soft-tissue advantages are unambiguous in direction.
- The complication point estimate (RR 1.21) leans unfavourably for the taper, but its CI (0.72–2.04) straddles 1 — this is **statistical equivalence of evidence, not evidence of harm**. Do not cite RR 1.21 as a taper penalty.
- Survival RR 1.01 with CI 0.98–1.05 is tight, but the paper concludes "comparable," not formally equivalent — the CI width reflects the pooled study count, not a pre-specified equivalence margin.

**Where this sits in the MBL literature**

| Page | Design | n | Contrast | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **This paper (Yu 2020)** | SR+MA | 7 studies (5 RCT + 2 cohort) | internal tapered vs internal nontapered | MBL −0.43 mm, favours tapered |
| Rodrigues 2023 (DOI 10.1111/jopr.13655) | SR+MA | 12 RCTs / 1006 implants | internal tapered vs internal nontapered | SMD −0.80 mm, favours tapered (page not yet ingested) |
| [[implants/mbl/kim-2022-abutment-connection-mbl-survival]] | retrospective cohort | 374 implants / 175 patients | external hex vs internal 11° conical hex | 1 yr MBL favours internal (p<0.001); gap gone at 5 yr |

The open question this leaves: if the taper advantage is real, does it persist beyond the remodelling window? Kim 2022 reports the between-group MBL gap disappearing by year 5, whereas this pooled estimate (and Rodrigues 2023's larger pool) is not stratified by follow-up in the abstract. Long-horizon RCT data remain the gap.
