## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (103 patients, 103 internal-hex 4.6 mm implants with horizontally matching connections) comparing 1-year crestal bone loss in untreated thin mucosa, thin mucosa thickened with an allogenic membrane, and naturally thick mucosa.

One-year crestal bone loss: untreated thin tissue (A, n=34) 1.65 ± 0.08 mm mesial / 1.81 ± 0.06 mm distal; membrane-thickened thin tissue (B, n=35) 0.31 ± 0.05 / 0.34 ± 0.05 mm; naturally thick tissue (C, n=34) 0.44 ± 0.06 / 0.47 ± 0.07 mm — A vs B and A vs C significant (p = .000), B vs C not significant (p = .166 mesial, p = .255 distal).

Thin mucosa drives early crestal bone loss even around matching-connection implants, and allogenic-membrane thickening reduces resorption to the level of naturally thick tissue — a soft-tissue route to crestal preservation independent of platform switching.

## 세줄요약

전향적 대조 임상시험(환자 103명, 직경 4.6 mm 내측 육각·수평 일치 연결 임플란트 103개) — 치료하지 않은 얇은 점막, 동종이식막(Allogenic Membrane)으로 두껍게 한 얇은 점막, 선천적으로 두꺼운 점막에서 1년 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss)을 비교.

1년 골소실: A군(얇은 점막, n=34) 근심 1.65 ± 0.08 mm·원심 1.81 ± 0.06 mm, B군(막으로 두껍게, n=35) 0.31 ± 0.05·0.34 ± 0.05 mm, C군(두꺼운 점막, n=34) 0.44 ± 0.06·0.47 ± 0.07 mm; A-B·A-C 간 유의(p = .000), B-C 간 비유의(근심 p = .166, 원심 p = .255).

임상 함의: 수평 일치 연결 임플란트에서도 얇은 점막이 초기 변연골 소실을 유발하며, 동종이식막으로 연조직을 두껍게 하면 자연적 두꺼운 점막 수준까지 골소실을 줄일 수 있다 — 플랫폼 스위칭(PS, Platform Switching)과 별개의 연조직 경로.

## Summary

This prospective controlled clinical trial followed 103 patients receiving 103 internal-hex implants of 4.6 mm diameter with a regular, horizontally matching implant-abutment connection. Patients were grouped by gingival thickness: untreated thin tissue (A, n=34), thin tissue thickened with an allogenic membrane (B, n=35), and naturally thick tissue (C, n=34); groups A and C were treated in one stage and group B in two stages. At 1 year, untreated thin-tissue implants had lost 1.65 ± 0.08 mm mesially and 1.81 ± 0.06 mm distally, while membrane-thickened sites lost only 0.31 ± 0.05 and 0.34 ± 0.05 mm, and naturally thick sites 0.44 ± 0.06 and 0.47 ± 0.07 mm. The differences A vs B and A vs C were significant both mesially and distally (p = .000), whereas B vs C was not (p = .166 mesial, p = .255 distal). The findings identify thin mucosa as a risk factor for early crestal bone loss even without platform switching, and support soft-tissue thickening as a modifier of crestal remodeling.

## Key Contributions

- Isolates soft-tissue thickness as the variable: every implant shared the same 4.6 mm internal-hex geometry and horizontally matching connection.
- Quantifies a large crestal-bone difference attributable to mucosal thickness — roughly 1.65–1.81 mm in untreated thin tissue versus 0.31–0.47 mm once thin tissue is thickened or naturally thick.
- Demonstrates that membrane-thickened thin tissue becomes statistically equivalent to naturally thick tissue (B vs C: p = .166 mesial, p = .255 distal).
- Adds a soft-tissue-based route to crestal preservation alongside the wiki's connection-geometry (platform switching) and microgap-position strategies.

## Methodology

- Design: prospective controlled clinical trial; group assignment by gingiva thickness rather than randomization.
- n = 103 patients / 103 internal hex implants, 4.6 mm diameter, regular connection; A n=34 (thin), B n=35 (thin + allogenic membrane, two-stage), C n=34 (thick).
- Groups A and C: one-stage approach; group B: two-stage approach.
- Radiographs at implant placement, 2 months after healing, after restoration, and at 1-year follow-up.
- Primary outcome: crestal bone loss measured mesially and distally; significance set at 0.05.
