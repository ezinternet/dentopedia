## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (131 patients; 308 of 315 implants analysed) of bone-level internal conical connection implants in the posterior maxilla, testing abutment height (<2 vs ≥2 mm) against radiographic marginal bone loss (MBL) at loading, 6 and 18 months.

Short abutments lost more bone: mesial MBL 0.180/0.565 mm vs long 0.063/0.295 mm at 6/18 months; MBL rates 0.069/0.049 vs 0.039/0.024 mm/month (abutment F(1,1216) = 58.25, p < .01; continuous height p < .001).

Bone loss was faster in the first 6 months than the next 12; grafted bone, periodontitis and smoking also increased MBL rates — supporting ≥2 mm abutments within a non-randomized, panoramic-radiograph cohort.

## Summary

One of the earliest clinical studies to isolate prosthetic abutment height as a determinant of peri-implant marginal bone loss (MBL), this 2014 Journal of Dental Research retrospective cohort followed 131 patients with 315 bone-level Astra Tech internal conical connection implants in the posterior maxilla (7 implants excluded for missing data → 308 analysed). Standardized panoramic radiographs at loading, 6 and 18 months were read by one calibrated examiner, and MBL rates (mm/month) were modelled with a linear mixed model controlling for age, sex, smoking, periodontal status, restoration type, bone substratum, and implant width/length. Short abutments (<2 mm) lost substantially more bone than long ones (≥2 mm): mesial MBL 0.180 vs 0.063 mm at 6 months and 0.565 vs 0.295 mm at 18 months, with abutment height the strongest term in the model (F(1,1216) = 58.25, p < .01) and a significant continuous predictor (p < .001). Bone loss followed a non-linear trajectory — faster in the first 6 months than over the next 12 — and grafted bone, periodontitis and smoking independently raised MBL rates. The authors' conclusion is to account for abutment height when aiming to maintain crestal bone; this page was upgraded from an abstract-only ingest to the full-text PDF supplied by the user on 2026-11-25, and flagged source-table inconsistencies are listed under Results/Limitations.

## Key Contributions

- Identifies abutment height as the strongest measured factor for early MBL (abutment F(1,1216) = 58.25, p < .01; continuous predictor p < .001).
- Provides absolute mesial/distal MBL values at 6 and 18 months for <2 mm vs ≥2 mm abutments, not just a direction of effect.
- Quantifies the non-linear post-loading course: 0.069 → 0.049 mm/month (short) and 0.039 → 0.024 mm/month (long) between the first 6 and the next 12 months.
- Adjusts for grafted vs pristine bone, periodontitis, smoking, sex, restoration type and implant dimensions in a mixed model, establishing them as co-drivers rather than incidental differences.
- Supplies early observational support for the ≥2 mm rule later tested in the SR+MA and RCT literature held in this wiki.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective correlational study; consecutive patients at two private practices, restored September 2008 – July 2010; ethics approval, University of Granada.
- **Eligibility**: 18–85 yr; ≥2 splinted posterior-maxilla implants in a screw-retained structure; ≥5 mm residual crestal bone; surgery by the lead author.
- **Implants**: Astra Tech AB (Mölndal, Sweden), bone-level, all cases.
- **Sample**: 315 implants in 131 patients; 7 implants from 3 patients excluded for missing data → 308 (137 long / 44.5%, 171 short / 55.5%).
- **Abutment heights**: 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4 mm; SA <2.00 mm, LA ≥2.00 mm; also modelled continuously.
- **Outcome**: Panoramic radiographs (Kodak ACR-2000) at placement, loading, 6 and 18 months; one calibrated examiner using Dent-A-View v1.0; mesial/distal platform-to-crest measurements corrected by known implant dimensions; rates in mm/month.
- **Adjustment**: Linear mixed model (scaled-identity repeated covariance, Schwarz BIC) with age, sex, smoking, periodontal status (≥4 sites CAL ≥3 mm), restoration type, bone substratum, implant width/length; Bonferroni correction where required.
- **Not in the supplied PDF**: the electronic-only supplemental appendix (Appendix Tables 1–3).

| SA (<2 mm) | Mesial | 0.180 (0.025) | 0.565 (0.043) |

| LA (≥2 mm) | Mesial | 0.063 (0.014) | 0.295 (0.043) |

**MBL rates (mm/month), first vs second interval, and mixed-model effects:**

| Factor | 0–6 mo | 6–18 mo | Reported effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| SA | 0.069 (0.004) | 0.049 (0.004) | SA vs LA p < .01 |

Mixed-model F tests in the Results text: abutment height F(1,1216) = 58.25, p < .01; time F(1,126) = 23.53, p < .01; bone type F(1,1216) = 6.69, p = .01; smoking F(1,1216) = 19.30, p < .01; periodontitis F(1,1216) = 6.42, p = .01. Text values: periodontal 0.051 vs non-periodontal 0.040 mm/month; grafted 0.050 vs pristine 0.040 mm/month; heavier smoking 0.0009 mm/month. Men had higher rates than women (p = .044); wider implants lower rates (p = .05); Bonferroni-corrected restoration order full-arch < fixed partial denture < overdenture. Overdentures were more frequent with short abutments (p = .01), and long-abutment implants were slightly wider (p = .01).
