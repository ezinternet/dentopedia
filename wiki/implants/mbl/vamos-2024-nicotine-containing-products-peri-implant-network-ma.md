## Three-line Summary

Bayesian network SR+MA (39 studies; 32 in quantitative network MA) comparing peri-implant MBL across cigarette (CS), waterpipe (WP), e-cigarette (EC), smokeless tobacco (ST), and non-smoker (NS) groups; first network MA to rank all four nicotine product categories simultaneously.

NS had the smallest MBL; CS: +1.34 mm (CrI 0.85–1.79), WP: +1.58 mm (CrI 0.84–2.35), ST: +2.53 mm (CrI 1.20–3.87) — all significant vs NS; EC: +0.52 mm (CrI −0.33 to 1.36) — not significant.

Risk gradient for MBL: ST > WP > CS > EC > NS; e-cigarettes do not significantly elevate MBL over non-smokers; all combusted and smokeless tobacco products significantly worsen peri-implant bone.

## Summary

This Bayesian network MA is the first to rank four different nicotine product types simultaneously for peri-implant outcomes. Using data from 32 observational studies, it established a clear MBL risk gradient: smokeless tobacco users lose the most bone (+2.53 mm vs NS), followed by waterpipe (+1.58 mm) and cigarette smokers (+1.34 mm). E-cigarettes showed a numerically higher MBL (+0.52 mm) but this did not reach statistical significance. The NS group ranked first for all primary and secondary outcomes. The pattern persisted in subgroup analyses by maintenance control and smoking habit, strengthening the causal interpretation.

## Key Contributions

- First network MA ranking 4 nicotine product types + NS for peri-implant MBL
- Risk gradient established: ST > WP > CS > EC > NS
- E-cigarettes: statistically non-inferior to NS for MBL — clinically relevant null finding
- Smokeless tobacco: highest MBL burden (+2.53 mm) — highest-risk product
- Probabilistic Bayesian ranking of 5 groups in one analysis

## Methodology

- Design: Bayesian network SR+MA; PRISMA
- Search: PubMed, EMBASE, Web of Science, CENTRAL; to April 2023
- Inclusion: Observational; adults with dental implants; 5 nicotine product groups
- n: 39 studies (qualitative), 32 in network MA
- Primary: MBL
- Statistics: Bayesian random-effects; credible intervals; QUIPS, CINeMA
