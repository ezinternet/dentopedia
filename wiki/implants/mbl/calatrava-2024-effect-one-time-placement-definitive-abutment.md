## Three-line Summary

Single-center RCT (80 implants, 40 patients) comparing one-abutment-one-time (OAOT) vs 3 abutment disconnections at 6 and 12 months; bone-level platform-switched conical-connection implants (ConelogScrew-Line, Camlog) in the posterior jaw.

OAOT group: MBL −0.13 mm vs −0.61 mm in the disconnection group at 6 months (p = 0.008, Δ = 0.48 mm); keratinized tissue width, probing depth, and bleeding on probing all significantly better in OAOT; survival 100% in both groups.

OAOT provides clinically meaningful bone and soft-tissue preservation in PS conical-connection implants; this is the strongest individual signal in the Abayov 2026 SR+MA (7 included RCTs, pooled MD −0.23 mm).

## Summary

This single-center RCT randomized 40 patients (80 implants; 40 OAOT, 40 repeated-disconnection) to compare tissue outcomes at 6 and 12 months. All implants were bone-level platform-switched Camlog ConelogScrew-Line implants with conical connection. The OAOT group received definitive abutments at surgery and maintained them throughout the study; the control group underwent three abutment disconnections and reconnections during the healing and prosthetic phases. At 6 months, OAOT showed significantly less MBL (p = 0.008) and better soft-tissue parameters. The trial provides the strongest individual evidence for OAOT in the Abayov 2026 SR+MA. It is also a key supporting study for the clinical superiority narrative when combining OAOT with PS conical-connection implants.

## Key Contributions
- Strongest single-study OAOT signal: p = 0.008 at 6 months (Δ = 0.48 mm MBL)
- Comprehensive soft-tissue outcomes: KT width, PD, BOP all favored OAOT
- Bone-level PS conical-connection (Camlog) — confirms implant-system-specific benefit
- 12-month RCT with patient-level randomization
- Direct ancestor citation in Abayov 2026 SR+MA (included in pooled analysis)

## Methodology
- Design: single-center parallel-group RCT; 1:1 allocation
- Population: 40 patients needing 2 implants; posterior maxilla/mandible
- Implants: Camlog ConelogScrew-Line (bone-level; PS; conical connection)
- OAOT: definitive titanium abutment placed at surgery, no removal
- Control: 3 disconnections (implant uncovery, impression, final delivery)
- Outcomes: MBL (primary), KT width, probing depth, BOP at 6 and 12 months
- Statistics: Mann-Whitney U; significance p < 0.05
