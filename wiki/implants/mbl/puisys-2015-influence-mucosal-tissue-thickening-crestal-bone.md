## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (97 patients, 97 bone-level 4.1 mm Straumann implants) comparing crestal bone loss in untreated thin mucosa, thin mucosa thickened with an allogenic membrane, and naturally thick mucosa at 2 months and 1 year.

At 1 year, untreated thin tissue (T1, n=33) lost 1.22 ± 0.08 mm mesially and 1.14 ± 0.07 mm distally, versus 0.24 ± 0.06 / 0.19 ± 0.06 mm after membrane thickening (T2, n=32) and 0.22 ± 0.06 / 0.20 ± 0.06 mm in thick mucosa (C, n=32); T1 vs T2 and T1 vs C were significant (P = 0.000), T2 vs C were not (P = 0.909 mesial, 0.312 distal).

Thin mucosa drives crestal bone loss around bone-level implants, and allogenic-membrane thickening reduces it to the level of naturally thick tissue — a soft-tissue route to crestal preservation.

## 세줄요약

전향적 대조 임상시험(환자 97명, 골수준(Bone-Level) 직경 4.1 mm Straumann 임플란트 97개) — 치료하지 않은 얇은 점막, 동종이식막(Allogenic Membrane)으로 두껍게 한 얇은 점막, 선천적으로 두꺼운 점막에서 2개월·1년 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss)을 비교.

1년 골소실: T1군(얇은 점막, n=33) 근심 1.22 ± 0.08 mm·원심 1.14 ± 0.07 mm, T2군(막으로 두껍게, n=32) 0.24 ± 0.06·0.19 ± 0.06 mm, C군(두꺼운 점막, n=32) 0.22 ± 0.06·0.20 ± 0.06 mm; T1-T2·T1-C 간 유의(P = 0.000), T2-C 간 비유의(근심 P = 0.909, 원심 P = 0.312).

임상 함의: 얇은 점막은 골수준 임플란트 주변 변연골 소실을 유발하며, 동종이식막으로 연조직을 두껍게 하면 자연적 두꺼운 점막 수준까지 골소실을 줄일 수 있다 — 플랫폼 스위칭(PS, Platform Switching)과 별개의 연조직 경로.

## Summary

This prospective controlled clinical trial followed 97 patients, each receiving one 4.1 mm bone-level implant (Institut Straumann AG, Switzerland) placed in the posterior mandible in a one-stage approach and restored with a single screw-retained metal-ceramic restoration. Patients were grouped by vertical gingival thickness: untreated thin mucosa (T1, ≤2 mm, n=33), thin mucosa thickened with an allogenic membrane (T2, n=32), and naturally thick mucosa (C, >2 mm, n=32). At 2 months, crestal bone loss was 0.75 ± 0.11 mm mesial / 0.73 ± 0.10 mm distal in T1, 0.16 ± 0.06 / 0.20 ± 0.06 mm in T2, and 0.17 ± 0.05 / 0.18 ± 0.03 mm in C. At 1 year, T1 had lost 1.22 ± 0.08 / 1.14 ± 0.07 mm versus 0.24 ± 0.06 / 0.19 ± 0.06 mm in T2 and 0.22 ± 0.06 / 0.20 ± 0.06 mm in C; T1 vs T2 and T1 vs C were significant at both time points (P = 0.000), while T2 vs C was not (2 months: P = 0.861 mesial, 0.827 distal; 1 year: P = 0.909 mesial, 0.312 distal). The findings identify thin mucosa as a risk factor for crestal bone loss around bone-level implants and support allogenic-membrane thickening as a way to bring resorption down to the level seen in naturally thick tissue. ## Key Contributions

- Isolates soft-tissue thickness as the compared variable while holding implant type and diameter constant (all 97 implants were 4.1 mm bone-level Straumann implants).
- Quantifies a large crestal-bone difference attributable to mucosal thickness — 1.22–1.14 mm at 1 year in untreated thin tissue versus 0.24–0.19 mm after membrane thickening and 0.22–0.20 mm in naturally thick tissue.
- Shows the divergence is already established at 2 months (0.75/0.73 mm in T1 vs 0.16/0.20 mm in T2 vs 0.17/0.18 mm in C), i.e. an early-healing/remodeling phenomenon.
- Demonstrates that membrane-thickened thin tissue becomes statistically indistinguishable from naturally thick tissue (T2 vs C: P = 0.909 mesial, 0.312 distal at 1 year).
- Supplies the bone-level-implant counterpart to the same group's horizontally matching-connection thickening trial and to the platform-switched thin-mucosa observation.

## Methodology

- Design: prospective controlled clinical trial; group assignment by vertical gingival thickness (thin ≤2 mm vs thick >2 mm), not stated as randomized.
- n = 97 patients (28 men, 69 women; mean age 47.3 ± 1.2 years) / 97 implants; T1 thin untreated n=33, T2 thin + allogenic membrane n=32, C naturally thick n=32.
- Intervention: 4.1 mm bone-level implants (Institut Straumann AG, Switzerland) placed in the posterior mandible in one stage; after integration restored with single screw-retained metal-ceramic restorations.
- Site: posterior mandible; T2 mucosa thickened with an allogenic membrane during implant placement.
- Radiographs: after implant placement, 2 months after healing, after prosthetic restoration, and at 1-year follow-up.
- Primary outcome: crestal bone loss measured mesially and distally; Mann–Whitney U-test, significance set at 0.05.
- Source limitation: PubMed abstract only; full Methods/Results body not retrieved.

- The 0.98 mm mesial / 0.95 mm distal absolute 1-year gap between T1 and T2 favors membrane thickening; T2 tracks naturally thick tissue (C) closely at both time points.
