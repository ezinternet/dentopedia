## Three-line Summary

Double-blind randomized clinical trial (25 patients, 77 implants) comparing concave (Slim) versus cylindrical (Straight) abutments inserted with a one-abutment-one-time protocol and followed for 6 months.

Early global marginal bone loss (MBL) at 6 months was −0.6 ± 0.7 mm with cylindrical abutments versus −0.4 ± 0.6 mm with concave abutments (p = 0.030); the mixed-model concave coefficient was 2.8231 (95% CI 1.4379 to 4.2083, p = 0.0002).

Concave geometry reduced early MBL, but follow-up stopped at 6 months and the height-by-shape interaction was negative (−1.3340, p = 0.0001), so the height rule cannot be transferred across shapes.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 무작위 대조 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) — 환자 25명·임플란트 77개; 일회 지대주 일회 장착 (One-Abutment-One-Time, OAOT) 프로토콜로 오목형 (Concave, Slim) 대 원통형 (Cylindrical, Straight) 지대주를 비교하고 6개월 추적.

6개월 조기 전체 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 원통형 −0.6 ± 0.7 mm, 오목형 −0.4 ± 0.6 mm(p = 0.030); 혼합선형회귀에서 오목형 계수는 2.8231(95% 신뢰구간 1.4379~4.2083, p = 0.0002).

오목형 형태가 조기 골소실을 줄였으나 추적 기간이 6개월로 제한되고 높이×형태 상호작용이 음수(−1.3340, p = 0.0001)여서 높이 규칙을 형태 간에 그대로 적용할 수 없음.

## Summary

This double-blind randomized trial (Clinical Oral Implants Research 2022;33(10):1038–1048, PMID 35869615, PMC9796139) enrolled 25 patients receiving 4 × 10 mm internal-connection implants in healed posterior sites, with the definitive abutment inserted at surgery and never removed. Eighty implants were randomized to cylindrical (Straight™) or concave (Slim™) abutments; three were lost (two osseointegration failures, one concave abutment fracture), leaving 38 versus 39 for analysis. The concave group had less early global MBL at 6 months both in the reported text (−0.4 ± 0.6 vs −0.6 ± 0.7 mm, p = 0.030) and in Table 2 (−0.17 vs −0.62 mm, p = 0.001) — the two reported concave values disagree and both are kept verbatim here. A mixed linear regression gave a positive concave coefficient (2.8231, p = 0.0002), a positive abutment-height coefficient (1.0633, p = 0.0001), and a negative concave × height interaction (−1.3340, p = 0.0001): taller abutments raised the mean bone level in cylindrical arms but lowered it in concave arms. The difference was already visible at 8 weeks before loading and narrowed by 6 months. Because follow-up ended at 6 months, this is evidence about *early* MBL only; the same group's 24-month trial ([[implants/seijas-naya-2025-concave-straight-abutment-bone-rct]]) is the longer-term companion.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT from this group to isolate abutment horizontal geometry (concave vs cylindrical) while holding implant diameter/length, insertion depth and the one-abutment-one-time protocol constant.
- Early separation by 8 weeks (preloading), with a mixed-model time effect that was larger at 8 weeks than at 6 months — supports a peri-implant soft-tissue-space mechanism rather than a late prosthetic one.
- Quantitative shape × height interaction: the usual "≥2 mm is protective" rule from [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]] does not transfer unchanged to concave abutments.
- Six-month precursor to a 24-month shape × height RCT and an included trial in the 2026 abutment-design SR+MA ([[implants/soft-tissue/calazans-neto-2026-abutment-design-peri-implant-esthetic-sr-ma]]), whose pooled estimate was imprecise.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Balanced, randomized, double-blind, two-parallel-arm trial without a control group; CONSORT-based; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03796494; independent monitoring board; one interim analysis at 50% enrolment/follow-up with no protocol change.
- **Setting**: University of Santiago de Compostela, Spain; Feb 2020–Jul 2021; final sample 25 patients (10 men, 15 women; mean 56.7 ± 10.9 years), 77 implants (38 cylindrical, 39 concave).
- **Implants**: IPX (Nueva Galimplant) 4 × 10 mm, Ti-IV, 11° internal conical connection, Nanoblastplus surface (Ra 1.7 µm); placed ≥6 months post-extraction in mature bone, subcrestally by 0.9 ± 0.8 mm on average.
- **Abutments**: 2 or 3 mm Straight™ (cylindrical) or Slim™ (concave) antirotational screw-retained abutments, all placed at surgery at ≥25 Ncm; definitive metal-ceramic screw-retained prostheses at 8 weeks (20 Ncm).
- **Randomization/blinding**: SPSS 24.0 macro, randomized by implant location and abutment type; patients and data analyst blinded.
- **Outcomes**: MBL by standardized digital intraoral radiography at 8 weeks and 6 months (implant neck as reference; mesial and distal), ISQ/RFA, periodontal indices, demographic/topographic variables. Two independent observers; kappa 0.82 (probing depth) and 0.93 (MBL).
- **Analysis**: Implant as unit; mixed linear regression with fixed effects for time, group, height and their interaction, random effects weighted for implants per patient (R 4.1.1); p < 0.05.

## Results

| Outcome (mm) | Cylindrical (n = 38) | Concave (n = 39) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preloading (8 weeks) MBL, average | −0.50 (0.51) | −0.18 (0.35) | 0.020 |
| Post-loading (6 months) MBL, average | −0.11 (0.46) | 0.002 (0.32) | 0.100 |
| Global MBL, average (Table 2) | −0.62 (0.72) | −0.17 (0.32) | 0.001 |
| Global MBL, average (Abstract/Results) | −0.6 ± 0.7 | −0.4 ± 0.6 | 0.030 |

- Mesial and distal global MBL also favoured concave (p = 0.029 and p = 0.004).
- Mixed model (average MBL): cylindrical intercept −1.3730 (t = −2.4893, p = 0.0139); concave 2.8231 (95% CI 1.4379 to 4.2083, t = 4.0957, p = 0.0002); height 1.0633 (p = 0.0001); concave × height −1.3340 (95% CI −1.9685 to −0.6994, t = −4.2246, p = 0.0001).
- Bleeding index 16.9 ± 11.5% → 8.4 ± 8.8%, plaque 12.3 ± 5.2% → 29.5 ± 25.4%, probing depth 3.9 ± 2.2 → 3.4 ± 1.2 mm; no significant between-group differences.
- Mean ISQ 69.1 ± 10.9 (23–82.3) at placement and 71.3 ± 7 after abutment insertion, without group differences.
- Two implant failures (2.6%) and one concave abutment fracture (0.8%); no mucositis, crown mobility or ceramic chipping.
- **Source inconsistency [확인: papers/perezsayans-2022-impact-abutment-geometry-early-marginal.txt lines 17, 182, 225]**: the paper's own Table 2 reports the concave global average as −0.17 (0.32) mm while the abstract and Results text state −0.4 ± 0.6 mm; the abstract's cylindrical-intercept CI also matches the distal rather than the average model. Values are preserved verbatim and should not be treated as a single settled estimate.
