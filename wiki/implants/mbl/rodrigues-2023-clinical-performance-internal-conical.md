## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis of 12 RCTs (678 patients, 1006 implants: internal conical n=476 vs internal non-conical n=530), registered PROSPERO CRD42021237170, searching PubMed/Web of Science/Embase/Cochrane/ProQuest through March 2022.

Internal conical connections showed lower marginal bone loss (SMD −0.80 mm, p=0.004) and fewer prosthetic complications (RR 0.16, p=0.01) than internal non-conical connections, but **no** difference in implant survival (RR 0.54, p=0.10) or biological complications (RR 0.90, p=0.82).

Conical selection can be justified on bone-preservation and mechanical grounds only — certainty of evidence is low to moderate by GRADE, and the authors themselves call for well-conducted long-follow-up trials.

## 세줄요약

12개 무작위 대조군 시험 (RCT, Randomized Controlled Trial)을 합친 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis, SR+MA) — 총 678명, 1006개 임플란트(내부 원뿔 연결 476개 vs 내부 비원뿔 연결 530개), PROSPERO CRD42021237170 등록, 2022년 3월까지 5개 데이터베이스 검색.

내부 원뿔 연결 (internal conical connection, ICC)이 내부 비원뿔 연결 (internal non-conical connection, INCC)보다 변연골흡수 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)가 적었고(SMD −0.80 mm, p=0.004) 보철적 합병증도 적었다(RR 0.16, p=0.01). 그러나 **임플란트 생존율(RR 0.54, p=0.10)과 생물학적 합병증(RR 0.90, p=0.82)은 유의한 차이가 없었다**.

원뿔 연결 선택은 골보존·기계적 측면에서만 근거가 있고, 근거 확실성은 GRADE 기준 낮음~중간(low to moderate)이며 저자들은 장기 추적 RCT가 필요하다고 결론지었다.

## Summary

This systematic review with meta-analysis pooled 12 randomized controlled trials (678 patients, 1006 implants) to ask whether internal conical connections (ICC) perform better than internal non-conical connections (INCC) for implant-supported restorations, pooling two connection types that prior literature had often conflated with the external-vs-internal comparison. ICC showed significantly lower marginal bone loss (SMD −0.80 mm, p=0.004) and a markedly lower risk of prosthetic complications (RR 0.16, p=0.01), but did **not** differ from INCC in implant survival (RR 0.54, p=0.10) or biological complications (RR 0.90, p=0.82). Risk of bias was low in most included trials with some concerns in others, and GRADE certainty of evidence ranged low to moderate. The authors conclude ICC is the more favorable treatment option because of better peri-implant bone preservation and lower prosthetic complication probability, while explicitly calling for well-conducted long-term follow-up studies — the clinical claim rests on bone and mechanical outcomes, not on survival.

## Key Contributions

- 내부 원뿔 vs 내부 비원뿔이라는 **직접 대조**만으로 골흡수와 합병증을 함께 평가한 SR+MA — 이 위키에서 연결 타입 관련 최고 증거 가중치
- 원뿔 연결의 이점을 **골보존 + 보철적 합병증** 두 축으로 한정하고, 생존율·생물학적 합병증의 **무차이를 명시적으로 보고** — 효과크기(p=0.10의 RR 0.54)만으로 우위를 주장하지 않은 균형 잡힌 결론
- RCT만 포함하고 RoB 2.0 + GRADE를 병행 적용해 근거 확실성(low~moderate)을 노출
- Yu 2020 메타분석(내부 테이퍼 vs 비테이퍼, MD −0.43 mm, P=.003)과 **동일 방향의 MBL 이점** — 별도 메타분석 간 방향성 교차 확인

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review with meta-analysis, RCTs only; PROSPERO CRD42021237170
- **Databases**: PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane, ProQuest — 2인 독립 검색, 2022년 3월까지, 언어·연도 제한 없음, 참고문헌 수동 검색 병행
- **n**: 12 RCTs / 678 patients / 1006 implants (ICC n=476, INCC n=530)
- **Statistics**: 표준화 평균차 (Standardized Mean Difference, SMD) for bone loss; 위험비 (Risk Ratio, RR) for implant survival and complication rates
- **Appraisal**: Risk of Bias 2 (RoB 2.0); GRADE for certainty of evidence
- **Access note**: 구독 논문 — 초록만 확보. 이질성 지표(I²), 하위그룹 분석, 임플란트 브랜드 분포 등 본문 수치는 미확인이며 추정하지 않음
