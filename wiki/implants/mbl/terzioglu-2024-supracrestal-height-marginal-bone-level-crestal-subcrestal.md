## Three-line Summary

Randomized clinical trial (34 patients, 80 platform-switching posterior implants) testing supracrestal tissue height (STH ≤2 vs >2 mm) × crestal/subcrestal placement on marginal bone level change (ΔMBC).

Mean STH was 1.65 mm in group A vs 3.11 mm in group B; significantly more mesial ΔMBC occurred in A-I (thin STH + crestal) than B-I at T0–T1 (6 months), whereas ΔMBC did not differ by placement depth when STH >2 mm.

Abstract-only ingest: STH >2 mm protects marginal bone early regardless of depth and subcrestal placement is advised when STH is insufficient; exact ΔMBC values were not retrievable.

## Summary

This randomized clinical trial enrolled 34 patients receiving 80 platform-switching posterior implants and crossed site-level supracrestal tissue height (STH ≤2 mm group A vs >2 mm group B) with apico-coronal placement (crestal I vs subcrestal II), giving four subgroups. STH and placement depth were measured intraoperatively from mesial and distal aspects, and mesial/distal marginal bone levels were read on periapical radiographs at T0 (immediately), T1 (6 months), T2 (9 months), and T3 (12 months) after functional loading; the difference was reported as ΔMBC. Mean STH was 1.65 mm in group A and 3.11 mm in group B. At 6 months, mesial ΔMBC was significantly greater in A-I than B-I, while within the STH >2 mm group crestal and subcrestal placement did not differ significantly. The authors conclude that STH >2 mm is needed to preserve marginal bone early regardless of depth, and that subcrestal placement helps when STH is insufficient. This is an abstract-only ingest: per-group ΔMBC magnitudes and the later timepoint data were not available in the publisher preview.

## Key Contributions

- Randomized trial of STH × placement-depth interaction in platform-switching posterior implants (null hypothesis: STH has no effect on ΔMBC).
- Four-group design: A-I (≤2 mm, crestal), A-II (≤2 mm, subcrestal), B-I (>2 mm, crestal), B-II (>2 mm, subcrestal).
- Mesial and distal ΔMBC analyzed separately — the authors state no prior study had done so for subcrestal depth vs marginal bone change.
- Four radiographic timepoints (T0–T3) out to 12 months post-loading.
- Clinically actionable threshold proposed: STH >2 mm for early marginal bone preservation; subcrestal placement when STH is insufficient.

## Methodology

- **Design**: randomized clinical trial, single center (Kutahya Health Sciences University; approval 30.09.2020, no. 2020-05/07; NCT05595746; CONSORT; Declaration of Helsinki).
- **Population**: 34 patients, 80 platform-switching posterior implants.
- **Groups**: main A (STH ≤2 mm, mean 1.65 mm) vs B (STH >2 mm, mean 3.11 mm); subgroup I crestal, II subcrestal.
- **Measurements**: intraoperative STH and placement depth from mesial and distal aspects; KMW also reported.
- **Outcome**: ΔMBC = difference in mesial and distal peri-implant marginal bone levels on periapical radiographs at T0, T1 (6 mo), T2 (9 mo), T3 (12 mo) after functional loading.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Mean STH, group A / group B | 1.65 mm / 3.11 mm |
| Subcrestal depth, A-II vs B-II (mesial & distal) | No significant difference (p > 0.05) |
| Mesial ΔMBC, A-I vs B-I at T0–T1 (6 mo) | Significantly greater in A-I |
| ΔMBC by crestal vs subcrestal within STH >2 mm | Not statistically significant |
| Protective STH threshold stated in Discussion | lower ΔMBC associated with STH above 3 mm |
| Conclusion threshold | STH >2 mm necessary to preserve marginal bone, especially early, regardless of depth |
| Exact ΔMBC (mm), T2/T3 values | Not present in retrieved abstract-only preview |
