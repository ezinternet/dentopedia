## Three-line Summary

Prospective, controlled, randomised, double-blind two-centre trial in 53 platform-switched posterior implants (patients could receive 1–4 implants); definitive abutment placed at surgery and never removed (DA) versus healing abutment disconnected/reconnected three times (PA), with 12-month follow-up.

Mean bone loss was 0.14 ± 0.18 mm (DA) versus 0.23 ± 0.29 mm (PA) from surgery to 6 months and 0.14 ± 0.21 versus 0.21 ± 0.27 mm from 6 to 12 months — both non-significant, with total 12-month loss also non-significant (p=0.330); survival 100%, mean insertion ISQ 80.9.

The one-abutment-one-time protocol was at least **equivalent**, not demonstrably superior, to repeated healing-abutment manipulation in healed posterior sites.

## 세줄요약

전향적·대조·무작위·이중맹검 2기관 시험(플랫폼 스위칭 후방부 임플란트 53개, 환자당 1~4개): 수술 시 최종 지대주를 장착 후 제거하지 않은 군(DA) vs 치유 지대주를 3회 탈착·재장착한 군(PA), 12개월 추적.

## Summary

This double-blind randomised trial evaluated the OAOT protocol in healed posterior sites — a setting the authors note had unclear evidence compared with immediate single-tooth placements. Fifty-three platform-switched implants in partially edentulous patients were allocated to a definitive abutment placed at surgery and never removed (DA) or a healing abutment disconnected and reconnected three times (PA). Bone loss was numerically smaller in the DA arm at both 6 and 12 months, but no difference reached statistical significance (total 12-month p=0.330), and all implants survived. The authors conclude that OAOT has at least an equivalent effect on peri-implant bone-level changes. This is the null trial within the 2026 OAOT meta-analysis (as "Moreira 2021", 0.14 vs 0.21 mm, p=0.33) and an important counterweight to larger positive single-study signals.

## Key Contributions
- Double-blind design — uncommon in the OAOT literature — reducing radiographic measurement bias.
- Tests healed posterior sites rather than the more commonly studied immediate/single-tooth setting.
- Non-significant result: OAOT at least equivalent, no demonstrated superiority.
- ISQ ≥50 required for randomisation (mean 80.9 at surgery) — high primary-stability context.

## Methodology

Prospective, controlled, randomised, double-blind clinical trial at two centres, approved by the University of Coimbra ethics committee (CE-048/2018, June 2018). Adults ≥18 with posterior missing teeth (FDI 4–7), >4 months post-extraction healing, 1–4 platform-switched implants per patient and insertion ISQ ≥50. DA = definitive abutment at surgery, never removed; PA = healing abutment disconnected/reconnected three times. Abutment heights of 1, 2 or 3 mm were selected by mucosal height. Primary outcome = vertical bone level change at 6 and 12 months; secondary = mobility, BOP, probing depth and plaque index.
