## Three-line Summary

Single-centre retrospective cohort (Mir Dental Hospital, Daegu, South Korea; 58 patients, 117 machined long-neck ARi implants, anterior/premolar sites, placed Feb 2022 to Dec 2023) with 1-year post-loading follow-up.

Implant-level cumulative survival was 97.43% (3 failures; patient-level 94.83%), success (no mobility/symptoms/radiolucency, MBL under 1.5 mm) was 91.45% implant-level and 82.76% patient-level, mean MBL was 0.46 +/- 1.05 mm, and CBCT bone width/height changes were about 0.2 mm or less.

Short-term outcomes were acceptable, but follow-up was only 1 year, GBR was non-randomized, CBCT data covered only 32 implants, and authors include implant-manufacturer employees/founder, so the evidence is preliminary.

## Summary

This retrospective cohort at a Korean private dental hospital (Mir Dental Hospital, Daegu) evaluated a novel implant (Ari, Megagen) with a threaded body anchored in dense basal bone and a machined, micro-grooved long neck (cuff 4-10 mm) intended for atrophic anterior ridges. Fifty-eight non-smoking patients (mean age 54.36 +/- 9.32 years) received 117 implants (maxillary anterior 53, maxillary premolar 25, mandibular anterior 26, mandibular premolar 13), 76.92% with simultaneous GBR, between February 2022 and December 2023.

Three implants failed (cumulative implant-level survival 97.43%, patient-level 94.83%): two early (osseointegration failure; retrograde peri-implantitis) and one late (peri-implantitis at 553 days). All three had simultaneous GBR and two occurred in osteoporotic patients, although the authors state no causal association was established. Of 114 implants assessed at 1 year, 7 had MBL above 1.5 mm, giving success of 91.45% (implant-level) and 82.76% (patient-level). Mean MBL was 0.46 +/- 1.05 mm.

## Key Contributions

- Cohort data (117 implants) for a machined long-neck basal-anchored design, previously supported only by case reports.
- 1-year survival 97.43%, success 91.45% (implant-level); 94.83% and 82.76% (patient-level).
- CBCT buccal width change -0.03 to -0.07 mm and palatal -0.07 to -0.10 mm across 0-5 mm levels; vertical -0.05 mm (buccal) and -0.15 mm (palatal).
- GBR subgroup (24 vs 8 implants): localized advantage only at HW3_b (0.167 mm, p = 0.0338) and VH_p (0.348 mm, p = 0.0144).

## Methodology

- Retrospective cohort, STROBE-reported, IRB approved; single centre in South Korea.
- Implants placed at least 2 months after extraction, CBCT-planned, envelope full-thickness flap, platform at crestal level; GBR (resorbable membrane + synthetic or bovine graft) if dehiscence at cuff; cement-retained prostheses after at least 3 months of provisionalization.
- Survival = functional, in situ; failure = removal for mobility, infection, persistent pain or severe bone loss; early within 1 year, late after. Kaplan-Meier at implant and patient level, with non-failures censored at last follow-up.
- Success = no mobility, no recurrent symptoms, no continuous radiolucency, MBL under 1.5 mm at 1 year after loading.
- MBL from panoramic radiographs (ImageJ, single examiner); bone width/height from superimposed CBCT (0, 1, 3, 5 mm apical to shoulder), 32 implants in 16 patients; GBR vs non-GBR by linear mixed-effect model.

Failed implants: osseointegration failure at 17 days (hypertension, hyperlipidaemia; insertion torque 20); retrograde peri-implantitis at 203 days (osteoporosis; torque 70); peri-implantitis at 553 days (osteoporosis; torque 30). Cohort had no smokers and 5 diabetic patients (7 implants).

## Limitations

- Retrospective, single-centre, no control group; patient selection and surgical protocol not controlled.
- GBR not randomized (applied where indicated), CBCT subgroup small (24 vs 8 implants), so GBR conclusions are tentative.
- Only 1 year of follow-up and 3 events; no probing depth, bleeding on probing or plaque data; single examiner per measurement; MBL from panoramic images.
- No adjusted risk-factor analysis; no smokers in the cohort.
- Reported success 91.45% corresponds to 107/117 although 114 implants were described as assessed.
- Manufacturer-affiliated authors (two employees, one founder).
