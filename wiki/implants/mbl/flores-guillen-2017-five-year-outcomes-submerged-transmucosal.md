## Three-line Summary

5-year single-blind RCT (University Complutense of Madrid, n=40 enrolled/30 completed; subset of a multicenter trial) comparing single bone-level implants with platform switching and conical IAC placed in non-molar sites under either submerged (cover screw) or transmucosal (healing abutment) healing: 100% survival rate in both groups, no significant differences in any clinical or radiographic outcome.

Mean crestal bone change from placement to 5 years was 0.59 ± 0.92 mm (submerged) vs 0.78 ± 1.03 mm (transmucosal) — a 0.19 mm numerical gap in the same direction as Troiano 2018 (0.13 mm) and Moustafa Ali 2018 (0.12 mm), none reaching clinical significance; one peri-implantitis case total; patient satisfaction high in both groups.

Provides the longest direct RCT comparison of these two protocols in single-tooth non-molar bone-level implants; its null survival finding is consistent with Troiano 2018's "no late-failure difference" conclusion and contrasts with Troiano's early-failure penalty on the non-submerged arm.

## 세줄요약

5년 단일맹검 무작위 대조시험 (마드리드 컴플루텐세大, n=40 등록/30 완료; 다기관 RCT의 하위분석) — 비구치부 단일 bone-level 임플란트(플랫폼 스위칭, 원추형 임플란트-지대주 연결 [Implant-Abutment Connection, IAC])에서 매몰형(커버스크류) 대 비매몰형(치유지대주) 치유 비교: 양군 임플란트 생존율 (Implant Survival Rate, ISR) 100%, 임상·방사선 지표 모두 유의차 없음.

식립기준선에서 5년까지 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL): 매몰형 0.59 ± 0.92 mm vs 비매몰형 0.78 ± 1.03 mm — 0.19 mm 수치 차이(비매몰형 불리); 이 방향은 Troiano 2018(0.13 mm)·Moustafa Ali 2018(0.12 mm)과 일치하며, 어느 쪽도 임상적 유의차 없음; 임플란트 주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 진단 1명만.

단일치아 비구치부 bone-level 임플란트에서 두 프로토콜을 직접 비교한 최장기 RCT; 생존율 null 결과는 Troiano 2018의 "만기 실패 차이 없음" 결론과 부합하고, Troiano의 비매몰형 조기 실패 페널티와는 대조됨(이 RCT는 실패가 0건으로 페널티 측정 불가).

## Summary

This 5-year randomized clinical trial from University Complutense of Madrid (Flores-Guillen, Álvarez-Novoa, Barbieri, Martín, Sanz; *J Clin Periodontol* 2018;45:125–135) enrolled 40 patients in a subset of a multicenter study and randomized them to receive a single bone-level implant with platform switching and a conical IAC under either submerged or transmucosal healing in non-molar positions. At 5 years, 30 patients remained; implant survival was 100% in both groups. Mean radiographic crestal bone change from baseline was 0.59 (0.92) mm for submerged and 0.78 (1.03) mm for transmucosal — a numerically transmucosal-disadvantaged gap of 0.19 mm that did not reach statistical significance. No clinical or patient-reported measure differed between groups. Peri-implantitis (defined as ≥2 mm crestal bone change + BOP) occurred in one patient only. Patient satisfaction was high in both arms.

## Key Contributions

- Longest direct RCT comparing submerged vs transmucosal healing in single-tooth bone-level implants: 5-year follow-up in a non-molar, platform-switched implant cohort
- 100% survival confirms that neither protocol confers a late-failure advantage — aligns with Troiano 2018 SR+MA late-failure null result
- MBL gap of 0.19 mm (S better) is numerically the largest of the three pooled comparisons (Troiano 0.13, Moustafa Ali 0.12) but the only RCT contributing 5-year data for this implant type
- Single peri-implantitis case across 30 patients / both protocols — no protocol-specific peri-implantitis signal
- Patient satisfaction equally high — transmucosal protocol's clinical simplicity (no second surgery) does not cost satisfaction loss

## Methodology

- Design: Single-blind RCT; subset analysis of a multicenter study
- n: 40 enrolled; 30 at 5 years
- Implant: Bone-level, platform switching, conical implant-abutment interface; non-molar sites
- Healing: S = submerged (cover screw); N = transmucosal (healing abutment)
- Outcomes: Crestal bone level change (radiographic), clinical parameters, patient-reported outcomes
- Peri-implantitis definition: ≥2 mm crestal bone change + BOP
