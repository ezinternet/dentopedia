## Three-line Summary

PRISMA SR+MA (11 RCTs, 699 implants) comparing OAOT vs repeated abutment disconnections and their effect on peri-implant bone level change; broader inclusion criteria than Abayov 2026 (all implant connection types included).

OAOT significantly reduced peri-implant bone loss compared to repeated disconnections (pooled MD favoring OAOT; exact values unavailable from abstract); 11 RCTs, 699 implants analyzed.

A second SR+MA confirming the OAOT bone preservation direction across a wider implant range; convergence with Abayov 2026 (MD −0.23 mm, PS+conical-only) from an independent analysis strengthens the OAOT evidence base.

## Summary

This PRISMA SR+MA evaluates the effect of definitive abutment timing on peri-implant bone changes by pooling 11 RCTs (699 implants). Unlike the Abayov 2026 SR+MA — which restricted analysis to platform-switched conical-connection implants — Nunes 2025 applies broader inclusion criteria, capturing a wider range of implant designs. The pooled result confirms a statistically significant OAOT benefit on bone preservation. The independent confirmation across two SR+MA with different inclusion criteria strengthens confidence in the overall OAOT bone-preservation finding, while the comparison between their pooled effect sizes (once full text is available) will clarify whether the benefit is amplified by PS+conical design or generalizes across implant types.

## Methodology
- Design: PRISMA SR+MA
- Search: standard databases; through 2024
- Inclusion: adults with dental implants; OAOT vs repeated disconnections; RCTs
- Implant types: all connection types (not restricted to PS/conical)
- 11 RCTs included, 699 implants
- Outcome: peri-implant bone level change (primary)
- Statistics: random-effects meta-analysis; subgroup analyses

## Results
- OAOT significantly reduces peri-implant bone loss vs repeated disconnections
- 11 RCTs; 699 implants
- Exact pooled MD, 95% CI, and I²: pending full-text retrieval (abstract-only ingest)
- Subgroup analyses and heterogeneity statistics reported in full text
