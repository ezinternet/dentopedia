## Three-line Summary

Randomised clinical trial (80 patients, 80 internal hex platform-switched implants placed equicrestally in the posterior mandible with a two-stage protocol) crossing vertical mucosal thickness (thin <= 2.0 mm vs thick > 2.0 mm) with randomly assigned 1-mm or 3-mm abutments for single screw-retained crowns, followed to 18 months of loading.

At 6 months of loading (T2) marginal bone loss (mean +/- SE) was 0.61 +/- 0.09 mm (thin) and 0.64 +/- 0.07 mm (thick) with 1-mm abutments versus 0.32 +/- 0.07 mm (thin) and 0.26 +/- 0.04 mm (thick) with 3-mm abutments; the greatest loss occurred in the first 6 months, and 74 of 77 implants were functioning at 18 months (96% survival).

Short (1-mm) abutments lost more bone than 3-mm abutments in both mucosal-thickness strata, and vertical mucosal thickness had no significant influence — abstract-only, so exact p-values, CIs and later-time-point values are not available.

## 세줄요약

무작위 대조 임상시험 (Randomized Clinical Trial, RCT) — 하악 구치부에 내부 육각 연결 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS) 임플란트 80개(환자 80명)를 골정상 (Equicrestal) 이단계 (Two-Stage) 방식으로 식립, 수직 점막 두께 (Vertical Mucosal Thickness; 얇음 ≤ 2.0 mm / 두꺼움 > 2.0 mm)와 무작위 배정된 1 mm·3 mm 지대주 (Abutment)를 교차해 기능 부하 18개월까지 추적.

기능 부하 6개월(T2) 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL; 평균 ± SE)은 1 mm 지대주군 얇은 점막 0.61 ± 0.09 mm·두꺼운 점막 0.64 ± 0.07 mm, 3 mm 지대주군 얇은 점막 0.32 ± 0.07 mm·두꺼운 점막 0.26 ± 0.04 mm; 골소실은 첫 6개월에 가장 컸고 18개월에 77개 중 74개 기능 (생존율 96%).

1 mm 지대주가 두 점막 두께 층 모두에서 3 mm 지대주보다 골소실이 컸고 수직 점막 두께는 유의한 영향이 없었다 — 초록만 확보되어 정확한 p값·신뢰구간·이후 시점 수치는 확인 불가.

## Summary

This randomised clinical trial (Int J Oral Implantol 2020;13(3):279-290) asked whether vertical mucosal thickness modifies the marginal bone loss seen with short versus long prosthetic abutments, and how that loss progresses to 18 months of loading. Eighty internal hex platform-switched implants were placed equicrestally with a two-stage protocol in the posterior mandible of 80 patients grouped by thin (<= 2.0 mm) or thick (> 2.0 mm) vertical mucosa. Elevated abutments of 1 mm or 3 mm were randomly assigned within each thickness group for single screw-retained crowns. Three patients dropped out at T2 and 74 of 77 implants were functioning at T4 (96% survival). At T2 the 1-mm groups lost significantly more bone than the 3-mm groups (0.61 +/- 0.09 and 0.64 +/- 0.07 mm vs 0.32 +/- 0.07 and 0.26 +/- 0.04 mm, thin and thick mucosa respectively), the thin-versus-thick contrast within each abutment height was not significant per the authors' conclusion, and most loss occurred in the first 6 months of function. Because insertion depth was held equicrestal, the result isolates abutment height more cleanly than designs that vary height and depth together. Only the PubMed abstract was available, so effect sizes, confidence intervals and exact p-values are not reported here.

## Key Contributions

- Varies abutment height (1 mm vs 3 mm) while keeping equicrestal placement, unlike [[implants/mbl/pico-2019-abutment-height-implant-depth-thin-mucosa]], where height and subcrestal depth were changed together.
- Reports a null effect of vertical mucosal thickness (<= 2.0 mm vs > 2.0 mm) on marginal bone loss: 0.61 +/- 0.09 vs 0.64 +/- 0.07 mm with 1-mm abutments, 0.32 +/- 0.07 vs 0.26 +/- 0.04 mm with 3-mm abutments at T2.
- Randomised confirmation of the direction suggested retrospectively by [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]] and pooled in [[implants/mbl/tajti-2024-less-marginal-bone-loss]].
- Documents that the greatest marginal bone loss occurs during the first 6 months of function.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomised clinical trial with 18-month post-loading clinical and radiographic evaluation (PMID 32879932).
- **Population**: 80 implants in 80 patients, posterior mandible; strata by vertical mucosal thickness, thin (<= 2.0 mm) and thick (> 2.0 mm).
- **Implants**: internal hex platform-switched; equicrestal; two-stage protocol.
- **Intervention**: 1-mm or 3-mm elevated abutment randomly assigned for single screw-retained crown.
- **Time points**: T0 placement; T1 crown delivery at 4 months; T2, T3, T4 at 6, 12, 18 months of loading.
- **Outcome**: mesial and distal marginal bone loss.
- **Evidence note**: abstract-only — blinding, concealment, radiographic method, per-cell n, exact p-values and CIs are not available.

## Results

| T2 (6 months of loading), mean +/- SE | Thin mucosa (<= 2.0 mm) | Thick mucosa (> 2.0 mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 1-mm abutment | 0.61 +/- 0.09 mm | 0.64 +/- 0.07 mm |
| 3-mm abutment | 0.32 +/- 0.07 mm | 0.26 +/- 0.04 mm |

- 74 of 77 implants functioning at T4 (96% survival); three dropouts at T2.
- 1-mm abutment groups showed significantly greater T2 bone loss than 3-mm groups (exact p-values not in the abstract); mucosal thickness had no significant influence.
- Greatest marginal bone loss occurred during the first 6 months of function; T3/T4 values are not given in the abstract.
- **Interpretation limits**: single configuration (platform-switched internal hex, equicrestal, two-stage, posterior mandible); thickness comparison is a stratum within an abutment-height trial with unstated per-cell n.

## Relation Judgment

- **Mucosal thickness vs [[implants/mbl/puisys-2015-influence-mucosal-tissue-thickening-crestal-bone]] — typed `refines`, not `contradicts`.** Puisys (bone-level 4.1 mm implants, one-stage, 1 year, same <= 2 vs > 2 mm cut-off) found thin mucosa lost 1.22 / 1.14 mm vs 0.22 / 0.20 mm in thick mucosa. This trial found no significant thickness effect. The headline findings oppose, but the settings differ (platform-switched internal hex, two-stage, abutment height randomised, bone loss reported at 6 months of loading), so the paper is read as qualifying the scope of the thin-mucosa rule rather than overturning it.
- **vs [[implants/mbl/linkevicius-2009-reaction-crestal-bone-around-implants]] — no typed edge.** The bone-level control arm there also showed no thickness effect (F[2,29]=0.73; P=.503), consistent with this trial's equicrestal result; the thickness effect appeared only for supracrestally placed implants, which this trial did not test.
- **Abutment height** — typed `reinforces` to the retrospective Galindo-Moreno cohort and the Pico RCT (independent confirmation of the direction). No typed edge to [[implants/mbl/tajti-2024-less-marginal-bone-loss]]: the abstract does not show whether that SR+MA pooled this trial, so independence cannot be established.
