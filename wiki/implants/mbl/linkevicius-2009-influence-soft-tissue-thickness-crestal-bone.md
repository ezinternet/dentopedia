## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (abstract-only) with 46 implants in 19 patients — 23 placed about 2 mm supracrestally and 23 at bone level — with supracrestal test sites split by probe-measured gingival thickness into thin (A) and thick (B) groups and radiographs at placement and 1 year.

Thin-mucosa test implants lost 1.61 +/- 0.24 mm (SE; range, 0.9 to 3.3 mm) mesially and 1.28 +/- 0.167 mm (range, 0.8 to 2.1 mm) distally versus 0.26 +/- 0.08 mm and 0.09 +/- 0.05 mm in thick mucosa, while bone-level controls lost 1.8 +/- 0.164 mm and 1.87 +/- 0.166 mm; ANOVA showed a significant thin-vs-thick difference on both aspects.

Initial gingival tissue thickness at the crest significantly influences marginal bone stability — with 2.0 mm or less tissue, crestal bone loss up to 1.45 mm may occur despite a supracrestal implant–abutment interface.

## 세줄요약

전향적 대조 임상시험(초록만 확보) — 환자 19명·임플란트 46개(시험군 23개는 약 2 mm 치조정 상방, 대조군 23개는 골수준 식립); 시험군은 탐침으로 측정한 점막 두께에 따라 얇은 군(A)·두꺼운 군(B)으로 나누고 식립 시와 1년 후 구내 방사선촬영으로 치조정골 변화를 측정.

얇은 점막 시험군 골소실은 근심 1.61 +/- 0.24 mm(SE; 범위 0.9~3.3 mm)·원심 1.28 +/- 0.167 mm(범위 0.8~2.1 mm), 두꺼운 점막군은 근심 0.26 +/- 0.08 mm(범위 0.2~0.9 mm)·원심 0.09 +/- 0.05 mm(범위 0.2~0.6 mm), 골수준 대조군은 근심 1.8 +/- 0.164 mm·원심 1.87 +/- 0.166 mm였고 분산분석(ANOVA, Analysis of Variance)에서 시험군의 얇은·두꺼운 군 간 차이는 근심·원심 모두 유의.

식립 시 치조정 연조직 두께는 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 안정성에 유의한 영향을 준다 — 점막이 2.0 mm 이하면 임플란트-지대주 연결부(Implant–Abutment Interface)를 상방에 두어도 최대 1.45 mm의 골소실이 생길 수 있다.

## Summary

This 1-year prospective controlled clinical trial (Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants 2009;24(4):712-9) evaluated whether initial gingival tissue thickness influences crestal bone loss around implants placed about 2 mm supracrestally versus at bone level. Forty-six implants were placed in 19 patients: 23 test implants at about 2 mm supracrestally and 23 controls at bone level; tissue thickness was measured with a periodontal probe before placement, and the test implants were divided into thin (A) and thick (B) groups. After healing, metal-ceramic cement-retained prostheses were constructed, and intraoral radiographs at placement and 1 year supplied mesial and distal crestal bone measurements. Thin-mucosa test implants lost 1.61 +/- 0.24 mm (SE) mesially and 1.28 +/- 0.167 mm distally, against 0.26 +/- 0.08 mm and 0.09 +/- 0.05 mm in thick mucosa, while bone-level controls lost 1.8 +/- 0.164 mm and 1.87 +/- 0.166 mm; ANOVA found a significant thin-vs-thick difference on both aspects. The authors conclude that initial gingival thickness is a significant influence on marginal bone stability and that 2.0 mm or less tissue may permit up to 1.45 mm of crestal bone loss despite supracrestal positioning of the implant–abutment interface. Only the PubMed abstract was retrieved, so all values and limitations below reflect that text alone.

## Key Contributions

- Crosses placement depth (about 2 mm supracrestal vs bone level) with initial soft-tissue thickness in one small controlled cohort of 19 patients and 46 implants.
- Reports mesial and distal crestal bone loss separately, with ranges, rather than a single pooled thickness-band mean.
- Finds a significant thin-vs-thick difference around supracrestal implants (ANOVA, both aspects) while bone-level controls lose around 1.8–1.87 mm regardless.
- States an actionable 2.0 mm tissue-thickness boundary and a 1.45 mm upper estimate of crestal bone loss even with a supracrestal implant–abutment interface.

## Methodology

- **Design**: 1-year prospective controlled clinical trial; no randomization or allocation concealment described in the retrieved abstract.
- **Population**: 19 patients, 46 implants — 23 test and 23 control.
- **Placement**: Test about 2 mm supracrestal; control at the bone level.
- **Thickness grouping**: Measured with a periodontal probe before implant placement; test implants divided into group A (thin) and group B (thick).
- **Prostheses**: Metal-ceramic cement-retained restorations after healing.
- **Measurement**: Intraoral radiographs at implant placement and after 1 year; crestal bone changes read mesially and distally.
- **Statistics**: Analysis of variance (ANOVA) for the thin-vs-thick comparison on each aspect; no F or p values in the abstract.

## Results

| Group (n) | Mesial bone loss at 1 year | Distal bone loss at 1 year |
|---|---|---|
| Test A — thin mucosa (23 test implants total) | 1.61 +/- 0.24 mm (SE; range, 0.9 to 3.3 mm) | 1.28 +/- 0.167 mm (range, 0.8 to 2.1 mm) |
| Test B — thick mucosa (23 test implants total) | 0.26 +/- 0.08 mm (range, 0.2 to 0.9 mm) | 0.09 +/- 0.05 mm (range, 0.2 to 0.6 mm) |
| Control — at bone level (23 implants) | 1.8 +/- 0.164 mm (range, 0.6 to 4.0 mm) | 1.87 +/- 0.166 mm (range, 0.0 to 4.1 mm) |

- ANOVA: significant difference between test A (thin) and test B (thick) on both mesial and distal aspects; test statistics are not reported in the abstract.
- Author-stated conclusion: tissue thickness ≤2.0 mm permits crestal bone loss up to 1.45 mm despite a supracrestal implant–abutment interface.
- Source-text inconsistencies retained verbatim: the thick-mucosa distal range (0.2 to 0.6 mm) lies above its mean (0.09 +/- 0.05 mm), and the 1.45 mm conclusion figure is below the thin-group mesial mean (1.61 +/- 0.24 mm). Neither is reconciled because the full text was not retrieved.
