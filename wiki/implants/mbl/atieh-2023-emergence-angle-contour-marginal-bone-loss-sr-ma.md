## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (4 studies; 912 implants; 397 participants; 3.8–10.9yr follow-up) comparing peri-implant MBL and peri-implantitis risk between implant restorations with emergence angle >30° vs ≤30°; low-to-moderate evidence quality.

Overall MBL difference between >30° and ≤30° groups was not significant (MD 0.80 mm; p = 0.09); in platform-matched implants the ≤30° subgroup had significantly less MBL; convex profile had a 2.3× higher peri-implantitis rate vs concave/straight (57.8% vs 21.3%; RR 2.32; p = 0.02).

Emergence angle alone is not a reliable MBL predictor, but a convex emergence profile significantly elevates peri-implantitis risk; concave or straight profiles should be preferred over convex regardless of exact angle.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (4편; 임플란트 912개; 397명; 최대 10.9년 추적): 출현각도(EA, Emergence Angle) >30° vs ≤30°, 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss) 및 임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis) 비교.

전체 MBL 차이 통계 미달 (MD 0.80mm; p=0.09); 플랫폼 매칭(PM, Platform-Matched) 임플란트 서브그룹에서 ≤30°군 유의하게 MBL 적음; 볼록 출현 윤곽(Convex Profile) → 임플란트주위염 57.8% vs 오목·직선형 21.3% (위험비(RR, Risk Ratio) 2.32, p=0.02).

출현각도 단독으로 MBL 예측력은 제한적; 볼록 윤곽은 임플란트주위염 2.3배 위험 → 오목·직선형 출현 윤곽 선택이 임상 권고.

## Summary

This SR+MA pooled 4 observational studies (912 implants, up to 10.9 years) comparing implant restorations with emergence angle >30° vs ≤30°. The primary finding is that overall MBL difference did not reach statistical significance (MD 0.80 mm; 95% CI −0.13 to 1.72; p = 0.09), but this masks an important subgroup pattern: in platform-matched implants (no platform switching), ≤30° was significantly better. More clinically decisive is the emergence profile finding: convex profile was associated with a 2.3-fold higher peri-implantitis rate (57.8% vs 21.3%, RR 2.32). The profile shape effect is independent of angle and may be the more actionable clinical parameter.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA pooling EA >30° vs ≤30° on MBL + peri-implantitis outcomes
- Overall MBL NS, but PM implant subgroup shows clear ≤30° benefit
- Convex profile: significantly higher peri-implantitis (RR 2.32, p = 0.02)
- Long follow-up (up to 10.9 years) — stronger real-world evidence than early RCTs
- Separates angle from profile as independent predictors

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA; PRISMA guidelines
- Inclusion: Observational studies; EA >30° vs ≤30° comparison
- n: 4 studies; 912 implants (455 EA>30°, 457 EA≤30°); 397 participants
- Follow-up: 3.8–10.9 years
- Outcomes: MBL (primary), peri-implantitis prevalence, periodontal parameters
- Statistics: Random-effects model; risk ratio for peri-implantitis

## Results

| Outcome | EA >30° / Convex | EA ≤30° / Concave/Straight | Effect size | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MBL overall | Higher | Lower | MD 0.80 mm (CI −0.13 to 1.72) | 0.09 (NS) |
| MBL (PM subgroup) | Higher | Lower | Significant | <0.05 |
| Peri-implantitis rate | 57.8% | 21.3% | RR 2.32 (CI 1.12–4.82) | 0.02 |

- Evidence certainty: Low to moderate (GRADE)
