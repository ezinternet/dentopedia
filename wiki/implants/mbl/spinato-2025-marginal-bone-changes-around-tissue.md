## Three-line Summary

Multicenter prospective study (six clinical centers; 47 patients/47 single posterior-mandibular tissue-level implants analysed, 46 functioning at 18 months) placed at three depths by vertical mucosal thickness (thin 2 mm subcrestal, medium 1 mm subcrestal, thick equicrestal), tracking marginal bone from crown delivery (T1) to 18 months of loading (T2) with multiple linear regression.

From T1 to T2 mean marginal bone resorption was 0.14 ± 0.56 mm overall (Group 1 0.41 ± 0.59, Group 2 0.08 ± 0.56, Group 3 bone gain 0.07 ± 0.43 mm), with no significant association with age, gender, smoking, periodontitis history, mucosal thickness, torque, collar height, emergence angle or crown width; from placement (T0) to T2, resorption was 1.05 ± 0.58 / 0.37 ± 0.64 / 0.25 ± 0.70 mm in thin/medium/thick mucosa, and thin mucosa (p = 0.007) and smoking (p = 0.028) were the significant predictors.

Around tissue-level implants the prosthetic variables tested did not drive bone change after crown delivery — the bone is largely committed during unsubmerged healing, so depth should be matched to mucosal thickness at surgery; limited by n = 47, one mandibular site, 2D periapical radiographs and 18 months.

## 세줄요약

다기관 전향 연구 (Multicenter Prospective Study, 6개 임상센터) — 후방 하악 단일 조직수준 (Tissue-Level) 임플란트 47명(18개월 시점 기능 중 46개)을 수직 점막 두께에 따라 3단계 깊이(얇음 2 mm 골하방·보통 1 mm 골하방·두꺼움 골정상)로 식립하고, 보철물 장착 (T1)부터 하중 18개월 (T2)까지 변연골 변화 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)를 다중 선형회귀로 분석.

T1–T2 평균 변연골 흡수는 전체 0.14 ± 0.56 mm (1군 0.41 ± 0.59, 2군 0.08 ± 0.56, 3군은 0.07 ± 0.43 mm 골 증가)였고 연령·성별·흡연·치주염 병력·점막 두께·식립 토크·경점막 칼라 높이 (Transmucosal Collar Height, TCH)·출현각 (Emergence Angle, EA)·크라운 근원심 폭과 유의한 연관이 없었으며, 식립 시점 (T0)–T2 흡수는 얇은/보통/두꺼운 점막에서 1.05 ± 0.58 / 0.37 ± 0.64 / 0.25 ± 0.70 mm로 얇은 점막 (p = 0.007)과 흡연 (p = 0.028)이 유의한 예측인자.

조직수준 임플란트에서는 검사한 보철 변수가 크라운 장착 후 골 변화를 좌우하지 않았다 — 골 변화는 비매몰 치유기에 대부분 결정되므로 수술 시 점막 두께에 맞춘 식립 깊이가 중요하며, 다만 n = 47·하악 단일 부위·2차원 치근단 방사선·18개월이라는 한계가 있다.

## Summary

This multicenter prospective study (STROBE; NCT05363306) followed 47 patients, each with one tissue-level implant (3-mm convergent machined collar, external hexagon) in the native posterior mandible, from crown delivery (T1) to 18 months of functional loading (T2); the same patients were part of a previously published 5-month trial covering placement (T0) to crown delivery. Implants were placed according to vertical mucosal thickness — 2 mm subcrestal in thin (<2.5 mm), 1 mm subcrestal in medium (2.5–3.5 mm) and equicrestal in thick (>3.5 mm) mucosa — left unsubmerged for 5 months, and restored with screw-retained metal-ceramic crowns. From T1 to T2 mean marginal bone resorption was 0.14 ± 0.56 mm with no significant association with host factors or with the prosthetic variables crown mesio-distal dimension, emergence angle and transmucosal collar height; across T0 to T2, resorption was 1.05 ± 0.58 mm in thin, 0.37 ± 0.64 mm in medium and 0.25 ± 0.70 mm in thick mucosa, with thin mucosa and smoking as the significant predictors. The authors conclude that bone stays stable after crown delivery around tissue-level implants and that the thin-mucosa effect arises mainly during healing before prosthesis delivery.

## Key Contributions

- Extends a 5-month trial to 18 months of loading, separating the pre-crown (T0–T1) from the post-crown (T1–T2) bone-change periods.
- Tests three prosthetic variables directly in tissue-level implants (crown mesio-distal dimension, emergence angle, transmucosal collar height >2 vs ≤2 mm) — none was associated with T1–T2 bone change.
- Thin mucosa (regression coefficient −0.365 per unit of mucosal thickness, p = 0.007; the unit is not stated in the retrieved text) and smoking (p = 0.028) predicted total T0–T2 resorption.
- Bone loss exposing the rough surface (BL) occurred in 0/16, 1/15 and 7/15 implants in thin/medium/thick groups: the thick-mucosa equicrestal group had the most BL. The authors suggest placing the machined collar about 0.5–1 mm below the crest even in thick mucosa.

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Multicenter prospective (STROBE), NCT05363306; continuation of a 5-month trial; one implant per patient |
| Centers | Six clinical centers; calibration meeting before the study |
| Patients | 49 enrolled; 2 Group 1 lost to follow-up; 47 analysed (25 M / 22 F; 58.3 ± 12.2 y); 1 Group 2 implant failed at 9 months of loading → 46 in function (16 / 15 / 15) |
| Implant | i-Smart tissue-level, 3-mm convergent machined collar, moderately rough surface, external hexagon; 8 or 10 mm; unsubmerged 5-month healing |
| Groups | Group 1 thin mucosa (<2.5 mm), 2 mm subcrestal; Group 2 medium (2.5–3.5 mm), 1 mm subcrestal; Group 3 thick (>3.5 mm), equicrestal |
| Crown | Screw-retained metal-ceramic, cobalt-chromium, connected directly to the collar; margin at platform or collar level; 30 Ncm |
| Radiographs | Periapical, long-cone, patient-specific bite jig; T0 (placement), T1 (crown), T2 (18 months loading); two calibrated blinded examiners |
| Prosthetic variables | CMDD (≥10 / <10 mm), emergence angle, TCH (>2 / ≤2 mm) at T1 |
| Statistics | One-way ANOVA + Tukey; multiple linear regression (enter); p < 0.05; power calculated for 15 patients per group |

## Results

| Outcome | All | Group 1 (thin) | Group 2 (medium) | Group 3 (thick) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 to T2 resorption (mm) | 0.14 ± 0.56 | 0.41 ± 0.59 | 0.08 ± 0.56 | gain 0.07 ± 0.43 |
| T0 to T2 resorption (mm) | 0.57 ± 0.72 | 1.05 ± 0.58 | 0.37 ± 0.64 | 0.25 ± 0.70 |
| BL (rough surface exposed) at T2 | 8/46 | 0/16 | 1/15 | 7/15 |
| Smokers (Table 1) | 12/46 | 6 (37.5%) | 2 (12.5%) | 4 (26.7%) |

- T1–T2: Tukey post hoc showed Group 1 vs Group 3 p = 0.041 (reported in the Results text, although the Abstract and Conclusion state no significant differences between groups for this interval).
- T0–T2: Group 1 vs Group 2 p = 0.015; Group 1 vs Group 3 p = 0.003.
- Regression T1–T2 (mesial/distal): no variable significant — e.g. TCH short 0.012 (p = 0.958) / 0.009 (p = 0.973); EA −0.013 (p = 0.369) / 0.010 (p = 0.607); mucosal thickness −0.150 (p = 0.338) / −0.176 (p = 0.251); CMDD <10 mm 0.125 (p = 0.637) / −0.094 (p = 0.736).
- Regression T0–T2: mucosal thickness −0.365 [−0.623 to −0.106], p = 0.007; smoking "No" 0.580 [0.067 to 1.092] with smokers as reference, p = 0.028 (text describes a positive association between smoking and resorption; the sign convention of the dependent variable is not explained in the retrieved text); age, gender, periodontitis history and insertion torque not significant.
- Failure: 1 implant (Group 2) after 9 months of loading; no complications recorded among the remaining 46.

Limitations (authors): n = 47 in one anatomic region (posterior mandible); 18-month follow-up; 2D periapical radiographs only; other prosthetic factors (crown-to-implant ratio, occlusal table width, cusp inclination) not analysed; multicenter operator variability. Smoking was unevenly distributed across groups (Table 1), and the paper does not discuss this.
