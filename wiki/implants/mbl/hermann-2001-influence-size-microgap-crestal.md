## Three-line Summary

Randomized canine study (60 two-piece SLA titanium implants, 5 hounds, 6 subgroups, interface 1 mm above the crest, unloaded, 3 months, non-decalcified histology) comparing three microgap sizes (<10, ~50, ~100 µm) in laser-welded (no movement) vs screw-retained (movement possible) implant–abutment pairs.

Laser-welded implants: interface-to-first-bone-contact 1.06–1.28 mm; screw-retained implants: 1.65–1.72 mm, significantly greater loss in every size pairing (P<0.01, P<0.02, P<0.01); microgap size had no effect.

Animal, unloaded, short-term data: component movement, not microgap size, drives crestal bone loss, and loss occurs even with microgaps <10 µm when movement is possible.

## 세줄요약

무작위 개 실험 (2-piece SLA 티타늄 임플란트 60개, 5마리, 6개 하위군, 접합부는 치조정 1 mm 상방, 비하중, 3개월, 비탈회 조직계측): 미세간극 (microgap) 크기 3종 (<10, ~50, ~100 µm)을 레이저 용접 (움직임 없음) vs 나사 고정 (움직임 가능) 임플란트-지대주 쌍에서 비교.

레이저 용접군: 접합부-첫 골접촉 거리 1.06–1.28 mm; 나사 고정군: 1.65–1.72 mm로 모든 크기 쌍에서 유의하게 큰 골소실 (P<0.01, P<0.02, P<0.01); microgap 크기 자체는 영향 없음.

동물·비하중·단기 자료: 치조정 골소실은 microgap 크기가 아니라 구성요소 간 움직임이 좌우하며, 움직임이 가능하면 <10 µm 간극에서도 골소실이 발생.

## Summary

Canine histometric study of 60 unloaded, non-submerged 2-piece implants with abutments connected at first-stage surgery. Laser-welded pairs lost less crestal bone than screw-retained pairs at every microgap size, and microgap size itself did not change the outcome.

## Key Contributions

- First data on implant–abutment movement and microgap size effect on crestal bone
- Movement significantly increased bone loss (P<0.01–0.02 per pairing); size did not
- Significant loss persisted at <10 µm when movement was possible

## Methodology

Randomized animal study, 5 hounds, 60 implants in groups A–F, interface 1 mm above crest. A–C laser-welded with microgaps <10, ~50, ~100 µm; D–F screw-retained with the same microgaps. Sacrifice at 3 months; non-decalcified histometry of interface-to-fBIC distance.

## Results

| Microgap | Welded (mm) | Screw-retained (mm) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| <10 µm | 1.06 ± 0.46 | 1.72 ± 0.49 | <0.01 |
| ~50 µm | 1.28 ± 0.47 | 1.71 ± 0.43 | <0.02 |
| ~100 µm | 1.17 ± 0.51 | 1.65 ± 0.37 | <0.01 |

Limitations: abstract-only, canine, unloaded, 3 months; welded abutments are not a clinical configuration.
