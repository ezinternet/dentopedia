## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical study (24 patients, 48 immediately restored subcrestal tapered implants, 3.5 mm diameter) comparing abutment removal at 24 weeks (12 patients) with one-abutment-one-time (12 patients) in partial posterior mandibular edentulism.
All 48 implants osseointegrated and were clinically stable at 6 months; vertical bone healing showed no significant between-group difference, while a small but significant horizontal bone loss above the platform appeared between 6 months and 1 year only in the removal-control group.
Non-removal of the surgical abutment significantly reduced horizontal crestal remodeling, but the abstract reports no numeric bone values or p-values — endpoint-specific, small-sample evidence.

## Summary

This 2011 prospective controlled clinical study treated 24 patients with partial posterior mandibular edentulism, each receiving two immediately restored, subcrestally placed tapered implants (3.5 mm diameter; 48 implants total) in healed sites. At 24 weeks, 12 patients followed the standard protocol with abutment removal and direct impressions on the implant platform, while 12 followed the one-abutment-one-time (OAOT) protocol with impressions made using snap-on abutment copies; final restorations were delivered approximately 6 months after insertion. All implants osseointegrated and were clinically stable at 6 months. Vertical bone healing did not differ significantly between groups, but a small yet significant horizontal bone loss in the hard tissue over the implant platform occurred between the 6-month and 1-year follow-up in the removal-control group. The authors conclude that non-removal of the abutment placed at surgery significantly reduces horizontal bone remodeling in this setting. Because the artifact is a structured abstract only, no numerical bone values or p-values are available.

## Key Contributions

- Adds the **horizontal (crestal remodeling)** endpoint to the OAOT question, which is usually assessed only by vertical marginal bone loss (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL).
- Tests OAOT in an early, defined context: immediately restored/splinted, subcrestal tapered implants in healed posterior mandibular sites.
- Shows a dissociation of endpoints — vertical null, horizontal favoring non-removal — that qualifies a conclusion based on vertical MBL alone.
- Documents a radiographic follow-up plan out to 36 months, with the reported horizontal difference localized to the 6-month-to-1-year interval.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective controlled clinical study; 24 consecutively treated patients, 48 implants; allocation method not described in the abstract.
- **Population/device**: Partial posterior mandibular edentulism, healed sites; two 3.5 mm diameter tapered implants per patient, placed subcrestally.
- **Immediate restoration**: Implants splinted with a temporary restoration positioned to avoid occlusal contact; final restoration ~6 months after insertion.
- **Control (n = 12 patients)**: At 24 weeks, abutments removed and impressions made directly on the implant platform.
- **OAOT (n = 12 patients)**: At 24 weeks, impressions made on the in-place abutments via snap-on abutment copies; no abutment removal.
- **Outcomes**: Vertical and horizontal bone changes on periapical radiographs immediately after surgery and at 6, 12, 24, and 36 months; clinical stability at 6 months.

## Results

| Outcome | Result (as reported in the abstract) |
|---|---|
| Osseointegration / stability | All 48 implants osseointegrated and clinically stable at 6 months |
| Vertical bone healing | No statistically significant between-group difference; values and p-value not reported |
| Horizontal bone above the platform | Small but significant loss between 6-month and 1-year follow-up in the control (removal) group only; values and p-value not reported |
| Radiographic time points | Surgery (immediate), 6, 12, 24, 36 months; later time-point results not reported in the abstract |
| Authors' conclusion | Non-removal of the surgical abutment significantly reduces horizontal bone remodeling around immediately restored subcrestal tapered implants in partial posterior mandibular edentulism |
