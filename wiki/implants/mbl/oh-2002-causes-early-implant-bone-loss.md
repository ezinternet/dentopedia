## Three-line Summary

Review (Journal of Periodontology, 2002) of six hypothesized causes of early crestal bone loss around endosteal implants.

Biologic-width reformation, a microgap at or below the crest, occlusal overload and crest module are judged most likely; surgical trauma and peri-implantitis may contribute.

Causation is unresolved; the authors call for randomized trials, and the abstract gives no effect sizes.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Journal of Periodontology, 2002) — 골내 임플란트 주위 초기 치조정 골소실의 여섯 가지 가설 원인.

생물학적 폭 재형성, 골능선 이하 미세간극, 교합 과부하, 크레스트 모듈이 가장 유력하며, 수술 외상과 주위염도 기여할 수 있음.

원인은 미해결이며 저자들은 무작위 대조 시험을 촉구; 초록에 효과 크기 없음.

## Summary

This is the early-bone-loss etiology map. It shows that the competing explanations were already on the table in 2002, and that the authors saw the question as open.

## Key Contributions

- Six-cause framework for early crestal bone loss.
- Ranking of the most plausible causes at the time.

## Methodology

Narrative review of six candidate factors.

## Results

Qualitative ranking; typical loss ≤0.2 mm per year after year one, as stated in the abstract.
