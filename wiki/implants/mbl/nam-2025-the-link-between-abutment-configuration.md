## Three-line Summary

Retrospective radiographic study (Yonsei University; 167 posterior single-tooth restorations with CAD-CAM abutments — 84 placed >1 mm subcrestally in 76 patients, 83 equicrestal in 73) comparing abutment profile angle measured from three different references against marginal bone loss (MBL).

Angle–MBL correlations were significant at 0–1 and 1–2 mm for the equicrestal platform-referenced (EI) and subcrestal bone-crest-referenced (SB) groups (P<.05) but not at 2–3 mm; in the subcrestal platform-referenced (SI) group they were significant at all three ranges, with generally smaller cut-off angles.

Subcrestal implants show a deeper angle–bone-loss coupling when the angle is referenced from the implant platform; abstract-only, so the actual cut-off values and effect sizes are not verifiable here.

## 세줄요약

후향적 방사선학 연구(연세대; CAD-CAM 지대주 후방부 단일치 보철 167개 — 골하 1 mm 초과 식립 84개/76명, 골정 식립 83개/73명): 기준점 세 가지(골정군 플랫폼, 골하군 플랫폼, 골하군 인접 골정)에서 잰 프로파일 각도(Profile Angle, PA)와 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)의 관계를 비교.

골정군(플랫폼 기준)과 골하군(골정 기준)은 0–1·1–2 mm에서 각도–MBL 상관이 유의(P<.05)했으나 2–3 mm는 미유의; 골하군을 플랫폼 기준으로 잰 경우에는 0–1·1–2·2–3 mm 모두 유의했고 역치도 전반적으로 더 작았다.

골하 식립에서는 플랫폼 기준 각도가 더 깊은 구간까지 골소실과 연관 — 초록 전용 기록이라 실제 역치값·효과크기는 확인 불가.

## Summary

This retrospective study from Yonsei University examined 167 posterior single-tooth implant restorations with CAD-CAM abutments, split between implants placed more than 1 mm subcrestally (84 implants, 76 patients) and equicrestally (83 implants, 73 patients). It asked not only whether the abutment profile angle relates to marginal bone loss but also whether the answer changes with the reference used to measure the angle: the implant platform or the adjacent bone crest. Correlations were confined to the 0–1 and 1–2 mm ranges for equicrestal platform-referenced and subcrestal crest-referenced angles, while subcrestal platform-referenced angles correlated with bone loss across all three ranges (0–1, 1–2 and 2–3 mm) and yielded smaller cut-off values. The practical message is that the "angle threshold" is not a single number: it depends on vertical implant position and on how the angle is referenced. Only the abstract was retrievable, so the cut-off values themselves cannot be checked or cited from this page.

## Key Contributions

- First comparison of **three profile-angle reference schemes** (equicrestal/platform, subcrestal/platform, subcrestal/bone crest) against MBL in the same study.
- Demonstrates that the depth range of significant angle–MBL correlation **extends to 3 mm only in subcrestal platform-referenced measurements**.
- Derives depth-specific cut-off angles via C-index analysis (values not reported in the abstract).
- Positions subcrestal placement as an effect modifier of restoration-contour risk, complementing the earlier equicrestal analysis by the same group.

## Methodology

- **Design**: retrospective review of electronic medical records of patients in regular maintenance.
- **Sample**: 167 posterior single-tooth implant restorations with CAD-CAM abutments — 84 subcrestal (>1 mm) in 76 patients, 83 equicrestal in 73 patients.
- **Groups**: EI (angle from platform, equicrestal), SI (angle from platform, subcrestal), SB (angle from adjacent bone crest, subcrestal).
- **Measurements**: profile angle over 0–1, 1–2 and 2–3 mm distance ranges; MBL as implant shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact distance.
- **Statistics**: Pearson correlation, linear regression, C-index cut-offs, independent t tests (α=.05).
- **Unavailable in abstract**: follow-up duration, numerical cut-offs, adjustment variables, imaging protocol.

## Results

| Group | Reference | 0–1 mm | 1–2 mm | 2–3 mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EI (equicrestal) | implant platform | significant, P<.05 | significant, P<.05 | NS, P>.05 |
| SB (subcrestal) | adjacent bone crest | significant, P<.05 | significant, P<.05 | NS, P>.05 |
| SI (subcrestal) | implant platform | significant, P<.05 | significant, P<.05 | significant, P<.05 |

- SI cut-off values were generally smaller than EI/SB at corresponding ranges (exact numbers absent from the abstract).
- Conclusion: angles within 2 mm of the bone crest correlate with MBL in both placement groups; platform-referenced angles in subcrestal implants correlate across 0–3 mm.
