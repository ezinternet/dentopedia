## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT (60 implants, 3 groups by STH × placement depth) testing whether supracrestal tissue height (STH) modifies MBL outcomes around platform-switched bone-level implants at 1 year post-loading.

STH ≤ 3 mm + equicrestal placement produced the greatest MBL; thick STH (> 3 mm) or subcrestal placement compensated for thin-tissue deficit; STH identified as an independent predictor of peri-implant bone remodeling.

STH is a clinically actionable predictor of MBL in PS implants; thin-tissue sites should favor subcrestal placement to re-establish biological space and prevent bone loss.

## 세줄요약

전향적 무작위 대조 임상시험 (임플란트 60개, STH×식립깊이 3군): 초치조정 조직 높이(STH, Supracrestal Tissue Height)가 플랫폼 스위칭(PS, Platform Switching) 골수준 임플란트 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss)을 조절하는지, 1년 추적.

STH ≤ 3mm + 치조정 식립(Equicrestal Placement) 조합 → 최대 MBL; 두꺼운 STH(>3mm) 또는 하방 식립(Subcrestal Placement)이 보상 효과; STH가 MBL의 독립 예측인자.

임상 적용: STH 얇은 부위는 하방 식립으로 생물학적 공간(Biological Width) 확보 → MBL 예방.

## Summary

This prospective RCT allocated 60 implants into groups based on site-level supracrestal tissue height (STH) and planned placement depth in bone-level PS implants. The key finding is that STH below 3 mm, when combined with equicrestal placement, produces significantly more MBL than either thick-tissue sites or subcrestal placement. This mechanistically links the bone-preserving effect of subcrestal placement — observed in Stacchi 2023 — to the biological need for STH to accommodate peri-implant biological space. The study closes a mechanistic gap: Stacchi 2023 showed that 2 mm subcrestal prevents bone surface exposure without improving average MBL; Terzioglu 2024 suggests the explanation is that thin-tissue sites need the extra depth to re-establish biological space, while thick-tissue sites are unaffected.

## Key Contributions
- STH established as independent predictor of MBL in PS bone-level implants
- Equicrestal placement in thin-tissue (STH ≤ 3 mm) sites: highest MBL risk
- Subcrestal placement or thick STH compensates for tissue deficit
- Provides mechanistic bridge between placement depth studies and biological width theory
- Practical clinical decision rule: measure STH before choosing placement depth

## Methodology
- Design: prospective parallel RCT; 3 groups (crestal/subcrestal × thin/thick STH)
- n = 60 implants; posterior region; partially edentulous patients
- Implants: bone-level PS design; definitive abutments connected at loading
- STH measurement: probe-based at implant placement
- Follow-up: 1 year post-loading
- Outcomes: MBL (primary, radiographic); STH measured clinically

## Results

| Group | STH | Placement | MBL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Worst | ≤ 3 mm | Equicrestal | Highest (significant) |
| Better | > 3 mm | Equicrestal | Significantly less |
| Better | ≤ 3 mm | Subcrestal | Compensated |
| Best | > 3 mm | Subcrestal | Lowest |

- STH independently predicts MBL in multivariate regression
- Exact MBL values: pending full text (abstract-only page)
- 1-year implant survival: 100%
