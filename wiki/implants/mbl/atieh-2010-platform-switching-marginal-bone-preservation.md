## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA; searched to 15 March 2010; 10 controlled trials, 1,239 implants) comparing marginal bone-level change and implant failure of platform-switched vs platform-matched implants.

Platform switching reduced marginal bone loss (MD −0.37 mm; 95% CI −0.55 to −0.20; P<0.0001) with no difference in implant failure (RR 0.93; 95% CI 0.34–2.95; P=0.89); an implant–abutment diameter difference ≥0.4 mm was associated with a more favorable bone response and bone resorption was inversely related to the mismatch.

Supports PS for bone preservation, but the included set is small and early (to 2010); the authors call for long-term, well-conducted RCTs.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (SR+MA; PRISMA 지침, 2010년 3월 15일까지 검색; 대조 임상시험 10편, 임플란트 1,239개): 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS) 임플란트와 플랫폼 매칭 (Platform Matching, PM) 임플란트의 변연골 수준 변화와 임플란트 실패율을 비교.

PS는 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)을 유의하게 감소 (MD −0.37 mm; 95% CI −0.55~−0.20; P<0.0001), 임플란트 실패율은 차이 없음 (RR 0.93; 95% CI 0.34–2.95; P=0.89); 임플란트-지대주 직경 차 ≥0.4 mm에서 골반응이 더 양호했고 골흡수는 mismatch 정도에 반비례.

PS의 골보존 효과를 지지하나 포함 연구가 적고 이른 시기 (2010년까지)이며, 저자들도 장기 고품질 RCT가 필요하다고 명시.

## Summary

PRISMA-guided SR+MA of ten controlled trials (1,239 implants) comparing platform-switched with platform-matched implants. PS gave 0.37 mm less marginal bone loss with no difference in failure, and larger implant–abutment mismatch (≥0.4 mm) was associated with better bone response.

## Key Contributions

- Pooled MBL benefit of PS over PM: MD −0.37 mm
- No difference in implant failure (RR 0.93)
- Dose–response with implant–abutment mismatch; ≥0.4 mm subgroup more favorable

## Methodology

SR+MA following PRISMA. Searched MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Oral Health Group Trials Register, CENTRAL, UK National Research Register, ANZCTR, DARE and CPCI up to 15 March 2010, plus hand searches and author contact. Controlled trials comparing PS and PM were included; MD for continuous and RR for dichotomous outcomes, 95% CI.

## Results

- MBL: MD −0.37 (95% CI −0.55 to −0.20), P<0.0001, favoring PS
- Failure: RR 0.93 (95% CI 0.34–2.95), P=0.89
- Subgroup: mismatch ≥0.4 → more favorable bone response

Limitations: abstract-only; risk-of-bias detail not reviewed; search ended 2010.
