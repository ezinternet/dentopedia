## Three-line Summary

7-year RCT (Universitat Internacional de Catalunya, Barcelona; n=37 subjects, 63 subcrestally-placed BL implants; originally 65 subjects/99 implants at 1-year) comparing 1-mm (control) vs 3-mm (test) definitive abutment heights: 3-mm abutments showed significantly less total MBL at 7 years — mesial (-0.27 ± 1.32 vs -0.42 ± 0.50mm, p<0.001) and distal (-0.33 ± 1.39 vs -0.35 ± 0.38mm, p<0.001).

Peri-implantitis prevalence was descriptively lower with 3-mm abutments (3.2% vs 18.2%) but did not reach statistical significance (p=0.158); KM width <2mm was independently associated with higher peri-implant disease risk regardless of abutment height; the abutment height effect on MBL appeared strongest in the first year and attenuated thereafter (1yr-to-7yr: only mesial difference still significant, p=0.049; distal NS, p=0.749).

**Mechanistic implication**: taller abutment moves the microgap/biological-width re-establishment zone coronally (away from alveolar bone), providing direct RCT evidence for the biological width mechanism proposed to explain TL vs BL bone loss differences.

## 세줄요약

7년 무작위 대조시험 (바르셀로나 카탈루냐국제대, n=37명/63 임플란트; 최초 65명/99개): 3mm 지대주 (Abutment) vs 1mm 지대주 — 3mm 군에서 7년 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 유의하게 적음 (근심 -0.27 vs -0.42mm, p<0.001; 원심 -0.33 vs -0.35mm, p<0.001).

임플란트 주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 유병률 기술적으로 낮음 (3.2% vs 18.2%) 이나 통계 유의 미달 (p=0.158); 각화 점막 (Keratinized Mucosa, KM) 폭 <2mm는 두 군 모두에서 독립적으로 임플란트주위질환 위험 증가; 지대주 높이의 MBL 보호 효과는 1년 이내 가장 강하고 이후 약화.

**기전적 함의**: 지대주 높이가 클수록 미세 간극 (Microgap) 과 생물학적 폭경 (Biological Width) 재형성 영역이 치조골 상방으로 이동 → TL 임플란트의 낮은 MBL을 설명하는 IAJ 위치 가설에 대한 직접 RCT 근거.

## Summary

This 7-year follow-up RCT (Muñoz et al., *J Clin Periodontol* 2025) enrolled 65 patients with 99 subcrestally-placed (1.5mm) two-piece implants (Klockner Implant System) randomized to receive either 1-mm or 3-mm definitive titanium abutments at the same surgical appointment. At 7 years, 37 subjects with 63 implants remained. The 3-mm abutment group maintained significantly more coronal bone position at both mesial and distal sites from surgery to 7 years (p<0.001). The peri-implantitis rate was numerically lower in the 3-mm group (3.2% vs 18.2%, 1 vs 6 implants) without reaching statistical significance (p=0.158), likely due to insufficient power from the high dropout rate (43%). KM width was consistently associated with peri-implant health status in both groups.

## Key Contributions

- Strongest long-term RCT evidence that taller abutments preserve marginal bone in subcrestally-placed BL implants (7yr, p<0.001)
- The abutment-height effect is front-loaded: the critical window is 0-1 year when physiological bone remodeling occurs around the microgap; beyond 1 year, additional MBL is modest in both groups
- Peri-implantitis trend (18.2% vs 3.2%) mechanistically supports TL-like IAJ position, even if underpowered for significance
- KM <2mm: independently linked to higher disease risk regardless of abutment height — supports KM management as a universal risk-mitigation strategy
- Posterior mandible location: 1-mm abutment group had 7× higher disease risk vs maxilla — location-abutment height interaction

## Methodology

- Design: 7-year RCT (follow-up of prior 1-year study)
- n: 37 subjects / 63 implants at 7yr (from 65/99 at 1yr; 43% dropout)
- 1-mm group: 17 subjects, 32 implants; 3-mm group: 20 subjects, 31 implants
- Placement: Subcrestal 1.5mm; implant: Klockner (Klockner, Barcelona)
- Abutments: Definitive at surgery; 1mm vs 3mm height; screw-retained restoration after 16 weeks
- Radiographic: paralleling cone technique; individualized silicone keys; blinded examiner (ICC=0.99)
- Peri-implant disease: 2017 World Workshop criteria
- PIMT: 3-6 month schedule (83.8% non-compliant or erratic at 7yr)

## Results

| Outcome | 1-mm abutment | 3-mm abutment | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mesial MBL (surgery→7yr) | -0.42 ± 0.50 mm | -0.27 ± 1.32 mm | <0.001 |
| Distal MBL (surgery→7yr) | -0.35 ± 0.38 mm | -0.33 ± 1.39 mm | <0.001 |
| Mesial MBL (1yr→7yr) | -0.22 ± 0.48 mm | -0.24 ± 1.34 mm | 0.049 |
| Distal MBL (1yr→7yr) | -0.10 ± 0.45 mm | -0.33 ± 1.38 mm | 0.749 (NS) |
| Peri-implantitis (7yr) | 6/33 (18.2%) | 1/31 (3.2%) | 0.158 (NS) |
| Peri-implant mucositis | 18/33 (54.6%) | 23/31 (74.2%) | NS |

KM width (both groups): implants with peri-implant mucositis had ~1mm less KM than healthy implants (1mm group p=0.010; 3mm group p<0.001).
