## Three-line Summary

Cluster randomized controlled trial (70 platform-switched internal-hex implants placed equicrestally; thin mucosa <=2.0 mm vs thick mucosa >2.0 mm) assigning 1-mm (short) or 3-mm (long) prosthetic abutments after 3 months of submerged healing, with radiographic marginal bone loss (MBL) followed to 12 months of loading.

At 12 months of loading 66 implants were functioning (two dropouts, two failures; 97% survival); mean MBL versus placement (T0) was 0.59-0.80 mm in the short-abutment groups and 0.28-0.37 mm in the long-abutment groups, a significant difference irrespective of vertical mucosal thickness, with most resorption in the first 6 months after loading, particularly around short abutments.

In these equicrestal platform-switched implants, abutment height rather than mucosal thickness drove MBL (abstract-only; no exact p-values, CIs or per-group sample sizes available).

## 세줄요약

클러스터 무작위 대조시험 (Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial) — 골정상 (Equicrestal) 식립한 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS) 내부 육각 임플란트 70개를 점막 두께 얇음(<=2.0 mm)·두꺼움(>2.0 mm) 두 군으로 나누고, 3개월 매몰 치유 후 1 mm(짧은) 또는 3 mm(긴) 지대주 (Abutment)를 배정해 부하 12개월까지 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)을 방사선으로 추적.

부하 12개월에 66개 기능 중 (탈락 2, 실패 2; 생존율 97%); 식립 시점 (T0) 대비 평균 MBL은 짧은 지대주군 0.59-0.80 mm, 긴 지대주군 0.28-0.37 mm로 수직 점막 두께와 무관하게 유의한 차이였고, 골흡수는 부하 후 첫 6개월에 가장 컸으며 특히 짧은 지대주에서 두드러졌다.

골정상 식립 PS 임플란트에서는 점막 두께가 아니라 지대주 높이가 MBL을 좌우했다 (초록만 확보 — 정확한 p값·신뢰구간·군별 표본수는 미확인).

## Summary

This cluster randomized controlled trial (Clin Oral Implants Res 2019;30(7):649-659) asked how abutment height and vertical peri-implant mucosal thickness act together on marginal bone loss (MBL) during biological width establishment — the authors state that no earlier clinical study had evaluated their simultaneous effect. Seventy platform-switched internal-hex implants were placed equicrestally in patients with thin (<=2.0 mm) or thick (>2.0 mm) mucosa. After three months of submerged healing, 1-mm (short) or 3-mm (long) abutments were randomly assigned for single screwed crowns, and MBL was measured on radiographs at placement (T0), delivery (T1), and 6 (T2) and 12 months (T3) of loading. At 12 months 66 implants were functioning (two dropouts, two failures; 97% survival). MBL versus T0 was 0.59-0.80 mm with short abutments and 0.28-0.37 mm with long abutments, significant irrespective of mucosal thickness, and the loss was concentrated in the first 6 months after loading, especially around short abutments. Only the PubMed abstract was available, so group sizes, SDs, CIs and exact p-values are not captured.

## Key Contributions

- Crosses abutment height with mucosal thickness in a randomized design while holding placement depth constant (all equicrestal), unlike [[implants/mbl/pico-2019-abutment-height-implant-depth-thin-mucosa]] where height and depth were co-varied.
- Randomized confirmation of the short-abutment disadvantage first reported retrospectively in [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]].
- Reports no significant mucosal-thickness effect on MBL in equicrestal platform-switched implants once abutment height is accounted for.
- Shows the timing: most resorption occurred in the first 6 months after loading.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Cluster randomized controlled trial; radiographs at T0 (placement), T1 (restoration delivery), T2 (6 months of loading), T3 (12 months of loading).
- **Implants**: 70 platform-switched, internal-hex, placed equicrestally.
- **Mucosa groups**: thin (<=2.0 mm) vs thick (>2.0 mm) vertical mucosal thickness.
- **Intervention**: after 3 months of submerged healing, 1-mm (short) vs 3-mm (long) abutments randomly assigned; single-crown screwed restorations.
- **Outcome**: radiographic MBL.
- **Evidence note**: abstract-only; group n, SDs, CIs, exact p-values, randomization/blinding detail and measurement method are not available.

- 66 implants functioning at 12 months (two dropouts, two failures): 97% survival.
- Greatest resorption in the first 6 months after loading, particularly around short abutments.
- **Interpretation limits**: the abstract gives only ranges for the four height-by-thickness cells, not a separate value per cell; follow-up is 12 months of loading; all implants were equicrestal, so thin-mucosa risk under supracrestal placement (see [[implants/mbl/linkevicius-2009-reaction-crestal-bone-around-implants]]) is not tested here.
