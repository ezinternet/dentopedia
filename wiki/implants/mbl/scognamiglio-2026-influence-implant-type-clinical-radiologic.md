## Three-line Summary

Retrospective non-interventional analysis (87 patients, 146 implants; 73 Straumann TL vs 73 BLX, 3-year private practice) found TL achieved 100% survival vs BLX 98% (3 failures), with TL showing mesial bone gain between 2-3 years (+0.08mm, p=0.018) and statistically superior mesial bone levels at 3 years (Δ=0.21mm, p=0.041), while BLX showed progressive distal bone loss (0.15mm, p=0.038).

All complications occurred in the BLX group (3 chipping, 1 mucositis, 3 failures vs 0 in TL); both groups remained within Albrektsson success criteria (<0.15mm/year); keratinized mucosa (KM) >2mm was especially protective for BLX implants.

A significant limitation is that groups differed substantially at baseline: BLX had more GBR procedures (56 vs 17), more anterior maxilla placement, and all 24 immediate placements — limiting causal attribution to implant type alone.

## 세줄요약

합병증은 전부 BLX 군 발생 (파절 3건·점막염 1건·실패 3건); 양군 모두 Albrektsson 기준 이내; 각화 점막 (Keratinized Mucosa, KM) >2mm는 특히 BLX에서 보호 효과.

## Summary

This retrospective cross-sectional study from a private practice in Mendrisio, Switzerland (in collaboration with University of Bern) compared 73 Straumann Tissue-Level (Standard Plus SLA) implants against 73 BLX (Straumann SLActive) implants in 87 patients across 4 time points (6mo, 1yr, 2yr, 3yr). TL implants demonstrated 100% survival at 3 years; BLX had 3 failures (98% survival). TL showed mesial bone gain between 2 and 3 years, while BLX exhibited progressive distal bone loss. All complications occurred in the BLX group. The IAC (Implant-Abutment Connection) supragingival position of TL implants is proposed as the mechanism distancing the microgap from the alveolar crest, reducing inflammatory bone resorption.

## Key Contributions

- First direct TL vs BLX (modern fully-tapered Straumann BL) comparison in private practice setting — adds real-world evidence beyond RCT conditions
- TL showed active bone gain (not merely less loss) at mesial surface between 2-3 years — unusual finding suggesting biologic niche stability
- BLX distal bone loss progression over 3yr contrasts with TL stability
- KM >2mm finding: especially important for BLX outcomes — clinically actionable
- All 3 implant failures and all soft tissue complications concentrated in BLX group

## Methodology

- Design: Retrospective, non-interventional, cross-sectional
- n: 87 patients, 146 implants (73 TL SLA vs 73 BLX SLActive)
- Follow-up: 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 3 years
- Radiographic: standardized periapical radiographs, mesial and distal bone level measurement
- **Baseline differences**: BLX had 56 GBR procedures (vs 17 TL); BLX placed predominantly anterior maxilla; all 24 immediate placements in BLX group; TL predominantly posterior mandible
