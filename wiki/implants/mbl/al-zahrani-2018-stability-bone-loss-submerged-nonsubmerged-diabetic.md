## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical trial (Riyadh, Saudi Arabia; n=35 patients, 118 implants; 7-year follow-up) comparing submerged vs non-submerged single-tooth implants with a 2×2 diabetic/non-diabetic stratification: non-submerged implants showed significantly higher peri-implant bone loss (PIBL) specifically in type 2 diabetic (T2DM) patients at 2, 3, and 7 years (p<0.05), while ISQ (RFA stability) did not differ between healing protocols at any time point.

In non-diabetic patients the protocol effect on PIBL was not significant; T2DM itself independently drove higher bone loss across both protocols; the 3-month ISQ change within the T2DM group was significant (p=0.008) while inter-group ISQ at baseline and 3 months was not (p>0.05), suggesting diabetes modifies secondary stability dynamics rather than primary stability.

The paper is the only wiki entry combining the submerged/non-submerged contrast with a diabetic vs non-diabetic stratification over long-term follow-up, and it establishes the critical exception to the general "no significant protocol difference" consensus: **non-submerged healing is disproportionately harmful in T2DM**.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상시험 (리야드, 사우디아라비아; n=35명/118 임플란트; 7년 추적): 매몰형 vs 비매몰형 × 제2형 당뇨환자 (T2DM) vs 비당뇨 2×2 층화 — 비매몰형에서 T2DM 환자의 임플란트주위골소실 (Peri-Implant Bone Loss, PIBL)이 2·3·7년 전 시점에서 유의하게 높았고 (p<0.05), 공명주파수분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)는 두 치유 프로토콜 간 차이 없음.

비당뇨 환자에서는 프로토콜에 따른 PIBL 유의차 없음; T2DM 자체가 두 프로토콜 모두에서 독립적으로 골소실을 증가시킴; 3개월 시점 ISQ 변화는 T2DM 군 내에서 유의(p=0.008)하나 두 프로토콜 간은 비유의(p>0.05) — 당뇨가 2차 안정성 획득 역학에 영향을 미침.

위키 내 유일하게 매몰형/비매몰형 비교를 당뇨 층화로 7년 추적한 논문 — "프로토콜 차이 없음" 컨센서스에 대한 **당뇨 환자 예외 신호**: 비매몰형은 T2DM 환자에서 불균형적으로 골소실이 높다.

## Summary

This prospective clinical trial from Ministry of National Guard Health Affairs (Al Zahrani and Al Mutairi, Riyadh; *Brazilian Oral Research* 2018;32:e57) enrolled 35 T2DM and non-diabetic patients receiving 118 dental implants (10–12 mm height, 3.3–4.1 mm diameter) under submerged or non-submerged healing, with Osstell ISQ measurements at fixture placement and 3 months and digital radiographic bone loss at 2, 3, and 7 years. Non-submerged implants showed significantly higher PIBL in T2DM patients vs non-diabetics at every time point. In non-diabetics, no significant protocol difference in PIBL was detected. ISQ did not differ between protocols inter-group, though T2DM patients showed a significant intra-group ISQ change over 3-month healing (p=0.008). The paper attributes excess bone loss in non-submerged T2DM implants to the immune-inflammatory status of diabetes under early transmucosal exposure.

## Key Contributions

- **Critical diabetes exception**: Non-submerged implants carry a significantly higher bone loss burden specifically in T2DM patients — the general "NS protocol difference" does not extend to diabetics
- **7-year stratified longitudinal data**: longest follow-up with T2DM/NT2DM stratum in a submerged vs non-submerged design
- **ISQ protocol equivalence**: Healing protocol does not affect primary or 3-month secondary stability (ISQ NS inter-group) — failure and bone loss effects are not mediated through stability
- **Mechanism signal**: T2DM-specific PIBL excess attributed to immune-inflammatory status amplifying exposure-site susceptibility in non-submerged healing
- Subgrouping allows distinction: diabetes and non-submerged interact specifically; non-diabetic patients can accept non-submerged without the bone loss penalty

## Methodology

- Design: Prospective clinical trial
- n: 35 patients (T2DM + NT2DM), 118 implants; mean height 10–12 mm, diameter 3.3–4.1 mm
- Healing protocol: Submerged vs non-submerged
- Stability: Osstell ISQ at fixture placement and 3 months
- Bone loss: Digital radiographs at 2, 3, and 7 years
- Statistical threshold: p < 0.05
