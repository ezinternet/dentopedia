## Three-line Summary

Clinical cast-based study of 58 wax patterns on single implants placed in healed, ungrafted sites; 10 raters graded cervical crown form good/fair/poor against implant position measured from a wire joining the adjacent teeth's gingival zeniths.

Horizontal distance (zenith line → implant facial surface) of 2.0–3.0 mm gave good contours (>3.0 or <2.0 mm fair/poor); vertical distance (zenith line → platform) of 3.0–4.0 mm was good, ≤1.0 mm poor.

Proposes 2–3 mm horizontal / 3–4 mm vertical guidelines from adjacent zeniths, with out-of-window sites flagged for bone grafting — subjective wax-up grading, not tissue outcomes.

## 세줄요약

치유 부위 단일 임플란트 모형 왁스업 58개, 평가자 10명이 인접치 치은 정점 (Gingival Zenith) 연결선 기준 위치별 치경부 치관 형태를 양호/보통/불량 판정.

수평 (정점선→임플란트 순면) 2.0–3.0 mm 양호, 그 외 보통/불량; 수직 (정점선→플랫폼) 3.0–4.0 mm 양호, ≤1.0 mm 불량.

인접 정점 기준 수평 2–3 mm·수직 3–4 mm 식립 가이드 제안, 범위 이탈 시 골이식 고려 — 조직 결과가 아닌 주관 평가.

## Summary

The adjacent teeth's gingival zeniths give a chairside and digital reference that already encodes where the new crown's margin should sit. Measuring from a wire across those zeniths, implants whose facial surface lay 2–3 mm palatal and whose platform lay 3–4 mm apical produced wax-ups judged to have good cervical form. Too facial (<2 mm) or too palatal (>3 mm), or too shallow (≤1 mm), compromised the form — and the authors read those positions as the ones that would have needed grafting to fix.

How this connects to emergence angle: a shallow or facially placed platform forces the restoration to flare abruptly from platform to margin, i.e. a wide restorative emergence angle and a convex buccal contour — the configuration the wiki's emergence-profile evidence associates with recession and bone loss. This paper supplies the placement-side numbers for avoiding it, though it never measured tissue outcomes.

## Key Contributions

- Zenith-line reference for 3D implant position in healed sites.
- Numeric windows: horizontal 2.0–3.0 mm, vertical 3.0–4.0 mm.
- Position outside the window → grafting indication.

## Methodology

- 58 wax patterns, healed ungrafted sites; 5 faculty + 5 graduate-student raters; good/fair/poor scale.
- Horizontal and vertical distances measured from a round wire connecting adjacent gingival zeniths.

## Results

| From adjacent zenith line | Good | Fair/poor |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal → implant facial | 2.0–3.0 mm | <2.0 or >3.0 mm |
| Vertical → platform | 3.0–4.0 mm | ≤1.0 mm poor |
