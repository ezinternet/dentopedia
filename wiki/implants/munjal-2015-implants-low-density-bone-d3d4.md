## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinico-radiographical study (n=14 patients, D3/D4 low-density jaw bone, square-thread Maestro™ implants, 12-month follow-up) evaluating survival and peri-implant bone behavior.

Implant survival rate was 92.3%; bone loss was statistically non-significant; peri-implant soft tissue indices improved over the observation period.

Poor bone quality (D3/D4) is not an absolute contraindication — appropriate implant design (square-thread for lower shear force vs V-thread) allows acceptable clinical outcomes; the small sample (n=14) limits generalizability.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상·방사선학적 연구 (n=14명, D3/D4 저밀도 악골, 사각 나사산 Maestro™ 임플란트, 12개월 추적) — 생존율과 치주골 변화 평가.

생존율 92.3%; 골소실 통계적으로 유의차 없음; 임플란트 주위 연조직 지수는 관찰 기간 동안 개선.

D3/D4 저밀도골은 절대 금기 아님 — 사각 나사산 (Square-thread) 설계(전단력 V-thread 대비 10분의 1)로 수용 가능한 임상 결과; n=14로 일반화 제한.

## Summary
Prospective clinico-radiographical study evaluating Maestro™ implants (square-thread design, D3/D4 optimized) in 14 patients with low-density (D3/D4) jaw bone. At 12 months: 92.3% survival rate, statistically non-significant bone loss, and acceptable peri-implant soft tissue health. Demonstrates that poor bone quality is not an absolute contraindication when appropriate implant design is used.

## Key Contributions
- Supports clinical viability of implants in D3/D4 bone with appropriate design selection
- Square-thread design: 10× less shear force than V-thread — biomechanical rationale for D3/D4
- 92.3% survival rate meets acceptable clinical threshold for challenging bone
