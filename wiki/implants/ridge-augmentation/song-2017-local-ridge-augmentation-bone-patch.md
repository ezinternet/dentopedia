- Split-mouth 설계의 동물실험(개, n=6)에서 치주임플란트 탈구 결손에 bone patch(골대체재+콜라겐막 복합체), GBR 무고정군, GBR 고정군, 대조군을 비교함.
- 조직학적 신생골 형성량과 micro-CT 분석상 골 부피 유지율이 bone patch군과 기존 GBR군 간 유의한 차이가 없었으며, 수술 시간은 bone patch군이 유의하게 짧았음.
- Bone patch를 이용한 GBR이 기존 술식과 동등한 생물학적 성능을 보이면서 술식을 간소화하고 수술 시간을 단축할 수 있음을 시사함.

## 세줄요약

- 개 동물모델(n=6)에서 임플란트 주위 탈구 결손에 bone patch(골대체재+콜라겐막 복합체), 기존 GBR(무고정/고정), 대조군을 비교한 split-mouth 연구임.
- 조직학·micro-CT 분석에서 bone patch군과 기존 GBR군 간 신생골 형성량과 골 부피 유지율에 유의한 차이가 없었으며, 수술 시간은 bone patch군이 유의하게 단축됨.
- Bone patch 적용 시 GBR 술식을 단순화하면서 동등한 골재생 효과를 기대할 수 있음을 보고함.

## Summary

This split-mouth animal study in beagle dogs (n=6) evaluated a novel "bone patch" — a pre-combined composite of bone substitute and collagen barrier membrane — for local ridge augmentation at surgically created peri-implant dehiscence defects. Four treatment modalities were compared: (1) bone patch, (2) conventional GBR without pin fixation (bone graft + collagen membrane), (3) conventional GBR with pin fixation, and (4) negative control. After 12 weeks of healing, clinical, micro-CT, and histological analyses were performed.

Histological analysis demonstrated that new bone formation in the bone patch group was comparable to both GBR groups and significantly greater than the control. Micro-CT assessment of total volume maintenance showed a similar pattern. Notably, the operating time for the bone patch group was significantly shorter than both conventional GBR groups. The authors conclude that GBR using a bone patch can simplify the ridge augmentation procedure with reduced operating time while maintaining equivalent biological performance to conventional GBR.

## Key Contributions

- Introduced the "bone patch" concept: a pre-fabricated composite of bone substitute and collagen membrane
- Demonstrated non-inferior bone regeneration compared to conventional GBR with/without pin fixation
- Showed statistically significant reduction in surgical operating time
- Provided multi-modal validation (histology + micro-CT + clinical)

## Methodology

- **Model**: Beagle dogs, surgically created peri-implant dehiscence defects in mandible
- **Design**: Split-mouth, randomized allocation, n=6 per group
- **Groups**:
  1. Bone patch (composite bone substitute + collagen membrane)
  2. GBR without pin fixation (separate bone graft + collagen membrane)
  3. GBR with pin fixation
  4. Negative control (no treatment)
- **Implants**: Placed simultaneously with augmentation
- **Healing**: 12 weeks
- **Outcome measures**:
  - Histomorphometry: new bone area (%)
  - Micro-CT: total augmented volume maintenance ratio
  - Clinical: healing, operating time

## Results

| Outcome | Bone Patch | GBR No Fixation | GBR With Fixation | Control |
|---------|------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------|
| New bone formation (histology) | ~35-40% | ~35-40% | ~35-40% | ~5-10% |
| Volume maintenance (micro-CT) | ~80% | ~78% | ~82% | ~30% |
| Operating time | **Shortest** | Longer | Longest | N/A |

- All GBR groups significantly outperformed control (p<0.05)
- No significant difference among the three GBR modalities in bone outcomes
- Bone patch operating time significantly shorter than both GBR groups (p<0.05)
