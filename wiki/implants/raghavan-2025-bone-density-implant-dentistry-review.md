## Three-line Summary

Narrative review synthesizing bone density classification systems (Misch D1–D4, Lekholm-Zarb types I–IV), CBCT Hounsfield unit (HU) ranges, and the biomechanical mechanisms by which bone quality affects osseointegration and implant primary stability.

D1 bone (>1250 HU) requires careful heat management; D4 bone (150–350 HU, posterior maxilla) demands significant protocol modification — undersized osteotomy, extended healing, and osseodensification (OD) as an adjunct technique.

Preoperative bone density assessment via CBCT is presented as the critical treatment-planning step linking bone quality classification to surgical protocol and implant design selection.

## 세줄요약

서술적 고찰(Narrative Review) — 골밀도(Bone Density) 분류 체계(Misch D1–D4, Lekholm-Zarb I–IV), CBCT 하운스필드 단위(Hounsfield Unit, HU) 기준값, 골질이 골유착(Osseointegration) 및 1차 안정성(Primary Stability)에 미치는 생역학적 기전 종합.

D1 골(>1250 HU): 표준 술식, 발열 관리 주의; D4 골(150–350 HU, 상악 구치부): 언더사이즈 드릴링(Undersized Drilling)·부하 지연(Delayed Loading)·골밀도화(Osseodensification, OD) 필요.

임플란트 계획 시 CBCT를 통한 사전 골밀도 평가가 수술 프로토콜과 임플란트 설계 선택을 결정하는 핵심 단계임을 강조.

## Summary
Concise narrative review covering bone density classification systems (Misch D1–D4, Lekholm-Zarb), measurement by CBCT Hounsfield units, and the clinical implications for implant dentistry. Provides practical guidance for adapting surgical technique and loading protocols based on bone density: undersized drilling in D3/D4 bone, modified healing times, and appropriate implant design selection.

## Key Concepts
| Misch type | Description | HU range | Clinical approach |
|------------|-------------|----------|------------------|
| D1 | Dense cortical | >1250 | Standard; careful heat management |
| D2 | Thick cortical + coarse trabecular | 850–1250 | Standard |
| D3 | Thin cortical + fine trabecular | 350–850 | Undersized osteotomy; extended healing |
| D4 | Fine trabecular only | 150–350 | Significant modification required; OD beneficial |
