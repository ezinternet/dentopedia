## Three-line Summary
Prospective multicenter case series across three Chinese centers enrolled 45 patients with Kennedy class I or II mandibular edentulism (107 OsseoSpeed TX implants, one-stage placement) and delivered early-loaded splinted fixed prostheses at 6–8 weeks, with 12-month follow-up.
At 12 months, implant survival was 100% and mean marginal bone change was +0.08 ± 0.41 mm (slight gain, patient level), with maintained soft tissue health and no dropouts.
This study provides the 12-month baseline data for the same cohort extended to 3 years by Liu 2019, confirming early splinted loading feasibility in posterior mandible, though the single-arm design limits evidence hierarchy.

## One-line Summary
Prospective multicenter case series (n=45, 107 implants): 100% survival at 12 months; MBL +0.08 mm (bone gain); first multicenter confirmation of early loading safety for splinted OsseoSpeed TX in posterior mandible.

## 한줄요약
전향적 다기관(n=45, 임플란트 107개): 12개월 생존율 100%; 변연골 +0.08mm 경미한 골증가; 하악 구치부 스플린팅 조기 부하 프로토콜의 최초 다기관 확인.

## Summary
This prospective multicenter case series enrolled 45 patients with Kennedy class I or II mandibular edentulism across three Chinese centers. A total of 107 OsseoSpeed TX implants were placed (2–3 per edentulous region, one-stage). Screw-retained splinted fixed prostheses were delivered at 6–8 weeks. At 12 months, implant survival was 100%, mean marginal bone change was +0.08 ± 0.41 mm (slight gain), and soft tissue health was maintained. This study serves as the baseline report for the same cohort followed to 3 years by Liu 2019.

## Key Contributions
- First multicenter case series specifically evaluating early loading of splinted implants in posterior mandible
- 100% survival at 12 months with minimal marginal bone change (slight gain)
- Establishes baseline data for Liu 2019 (3-year follow-up of same cohort)
- Kennedy class I and II edentulism — common clinical presentation with limited prosthetic options

## Methodology
Prospective multicenter (3 Chinese centers), single arm. n=45, 107 implants. OsseoSpeed TX (fluoride-modified, tapered apex). One-stage placement. Screw-retained splinted fixed prosthesis at 6–8 weeks. Follow-up at 6 and 12 months. Statistical unit: patient level (mean implants per patient). Data pooled from three centers.

## Results
- Implant survival: 100%
- MBL: +0.08 ± 0.41 mm (12 months, patient level)
- Soft tissue: healthy (no significant probing depth or bleeding changes)
- No dropouts at 12-month endpoint
