## Three-line Summary

Updated PRISMA systematic review (INPLASY-registered, SYRCLE risk-of-bias) of 12 preclinical studies (2015–Aug 2025) on parathyroid hormone (PTH; teriparatide, PTH 1–34) supplementation and dental implant osseointegration in osteoporotic OVX/ORX rat and rabbit models — **no human studies existed**.

Intermittent PTH consistently increased bone-to-implant contact (BIC), bone volume fraction (BV/TV), and removal torque vs osteoporotic controls; combination regimens (PTH + vitamin D, PTH + raloxifene, or local PTH-loaded scaffolds) outperformed monotherapy, with local PTH-functionalised bioglass restoring stability to healthy-animal levels.

This is **animal-only** evidence with high heterogeneity, small samples (n=10–80), short follow-up (2–12 wk), 7/12 studies at high risk of investigator-blinding bias, and heavy same-group author overlap; PTH remains an experimental adjunct with strong biological plausibility but **no human trial support** — clinical translation is unproven.

## 세줄요약

골다공증 유발 난소/고환 절제 쥐·토끼 모델에서 부갑상선호르몬(Parathyroid Hormone, PTH; 테리파라타이드, PTH 1–34)의 임플란트 골유착 효과를 다룬 12편 전임상 연구의 업데이트 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, PRISMA·INPLASY·SYRCLE, 2015–2025.8) — 사람 연구는 0편.

간헐적 PTH 투여는 골-임플란트 접촉률(BIC)·골부피율(BV/TV)·제거토크를 대조군 대비 일관되게 증가; 병용요법(PTH+비타민 D, PTH+랄록시펜, 국소 PTH-담지 스캐폴드)이 단독요법을 능가하고, 국소 PTH-기능화 바이오글라스는 건강 동물 수준까지 회복.

동물 전용 근거로 이질성 큼 — 소표본(n=10–80)·짧은 추적(2–12주)·7/12편 연구자 맹검 고위험·동일 저자군 중복이 한계이며, 인체 임상시험 근거는 전무 → PTH는 생물학적 개연성은 강하나 임상 적용은 미검증된 실험적 보조제.

## Summary

The wiki frames osteoporosis and its drugs almost entirely as **hazards** to implant therapy: osteoporosis lowers long-term implant survival (short-term 97.9–100% but 5–10 yr 82.6–94.1% with greater marginal bone loss, per [[implants/kim-2026-dental-implant-osteoporosis-osteosclerosis]]), and the antiresorptive agents used to treat it (bisphosphonates, denosumab) raise MRONJ risk ([[overviews/drug-mronj-antiresorptive-overview]]). Kindaro 2026 opens the opposite branch — whether an **osteoanabolic** drug can push osseointegration the *other* way. Parathyroid hormone is the mechanistic mirror image of the antiresorptives: given intermittently at low dose it *builds* bone (downregulating sclerostin, driving osteoblast differentiation and matrix deposition), whereas antiresorptives *suppress* osteoclastic resorption.

This is an updated systematic review (updating Javed 2015) of preclinical evidence only. From 177 records, 12 studies met the PICO — all **animal interventional** (Sprague-Dawley/Wistar rats, New Zealand rabbits; osteoporosis induced by ovariectomy or orchiectomy ± glucocorticoids; implants in tibia, femur, or maxilla). Because every included study was animal, the authors used the SYRCLE animal risk-of-bias tool and performed a narrative synthesis — heterogeneity in species, dosing (0.5–80 μg/kg/day), timing, and outcome measures precluded meta-analysis. Intermittent PTH consistently improved BIC, BV/TV, and biomechanical stability versus osteoporotic controls, and combination regimens beat monotherapy. But the evidence base is small, short-term, methodologically heterogeneous, and — critically — contains **zero human studies**. The authors conclude PTH is an experimental adjunct with strong biological plausibility that is **not yet ready for routine clinical use**; rigorous human trials are required before any patient-facing recommendation.

## Key Contributions

- **First osteoanabolic-rescue page in the wiki.** Complements the existing risk/hazard framing (osteoporosis lowers survival; antiresorptives raise MRONJ) and the modifiable-adjunct framing (vitamin D) with a *pharmacologic-anabolic* branch.
- Stratifies preclinical PTH evidence along three decision-relevant axes: **pre- vs post-implantation timing**, **systemic vs local delivery**, **monotherapy vs combination**.
- Shows a more reproducible anabolic signal than the mixed 2015 review, while being explicit that the signal is entirely preclinical.

## Methodology

- Updated systematic review, PRISMA 2020, prospectively registered (INPLASY202550047). Search Jan 2015 – Aug 2025 (PubMed, Ovid MEDLINE, Cochrane, Scopus, Web of Science).
- 177 retrieved → 86 duplicates removed → 91 screened → 19 full text → **12 included**. Cohen's kappa = 1.0.
- All 12 studies animal interventional → **SYRCLE RoB** tool (10 signalling questions, 6 bias domains; no summary score).
- Samples 10–80 animals; follow-up 2–12 wk; PTH 0.5–80 μg/kg/day, usually 3–5×/wk SC, therapy 4–8 wk (one to 12 wk).
- Outcomes: micro-CT (BV/TV, trabecular architecture, BMD), histomorphometry (BIC), biomechanics (removal/push-out/pull-out/insertion torque), ± IHC/SEM. **Narrative synthesis, no meta-analysis.**

## Results

- **Overall**: intermittent PTH significantly raised BIC, BV/TV, removal torque vs osteoporotic controls; thicker trabeculae, less trabecular separation, lower porosity.
- **Timing** — pre-op teriparatide 4 wk ≈ post-op 8 wk (Cui); pre-treatment accelerates osseointegration and shortens the compromised-stability window. A 1-week post-op course (Al-Omari 2025) improved soft tissue but only marginally improved bone → short courses insufficient.
- **Delivery** — systemic PTH improves peri-implant density but risks transient hypercalcaemia/nausea and cortical porosity (dose/frequency-dependent). Local PTH-functionalised bioglass (Gomes-Ferreira 2022) restored BIC and biomechanical stability to healthy-animal levels; teriparatide-loaded octacalcium-phosphate/collagen scaffolds gave 29.2–38.1% bone formation vs 17.5% scaffold-only.
- **Combination** — PTH + vitamin D (Gomes-Ferreira 2023) synergistic (↑BV/TV, trabecular thickness, ↓porosity); PTH + raloxifene (Demirbaş) high BIC/removal torque (anabolic + antiresorptive complementarity); PTH + low-magnitude high-frequency loading (Shibamoto) > either alone. Combination consistently beat monotherapy.
- **Null/negative** — Park: trabecular thickness ↑ (p=0.023) but BIC/BV/TV changes not significant (introduction also notes Kucher no BIC gain under hyperglycaemia; Ohkawa no bone-volume/shear-strength difference).
- **Risk of bias** — all low on baseline characteristics and selective reporting; **all unclear on allocation concealment**; **7/12 high risk on investigator blinding**. Cui and Al-Omari (2024) strongest (low risk 8/10 domains). Substantial same-group author overlap (Oki, Gomes-Ferreira, Al-Omari) raises confirmation/publication-bias concern.

## Clinical Bottom Line

PTH/teriparatide has strong biological plausibility as an osteoanabolic adjunct to improve implant osseointegration in osteoporotic bone, and preclinical animal data are encouragingly consistent — especially for intermittent dosing, pre-operative timing, local delivery, and combination with vitamin D or raloxifene. **However, this evidence is entirely animal (rat/rabbit) with no human studies, high heterogeneity, and notable bias.** It must NOT be translated into a patient protocol. It stands as a mechanistic counterpoint to the wiki's osteoporosis-as-risk and antiresorptive-as-hazard pages — a hypothesis-generating, not practice-changing, result.
