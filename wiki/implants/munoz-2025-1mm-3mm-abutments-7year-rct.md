## Three-line Summary

7-year RCT (37 patients, 63 implants) comparing 1-mm vs 3-mm definitive abutments on subcrestally placed implants (1.5 mm subcrestal); the longest RCT follow-up on this topic to date.

3-mm abutments had significantly less marginal bone loss surgery to year 7 (mesial: −0.27 vs −0.42 mm; p<0.001), and a clinically notable but non-significant lower peri-implantitis rate (3.2% vs 18.2%; p=0.158).

Use 3-mm abutments at subcrestal implants to minimize early-phase bone remodelling; long-term disease prevention depends more on adequate keratinized mucosa and PIMT compliance than abutment height alone.

## 세줄요약

7년 RCT (37명, 63임플란트): 1.5mm 피개매식 임플란트에서 1mm vs 3mm 지대주(abutment)를 무작위 비교; 현재까지 동 주제 최장 RCT.

3mm 지대주군이 수술~7년간 유의하게 낮은 MBL 변화 (근심: −0.27 vs −0.42 mm, p<0.001); 주위염 발생률(3.2% vs 18.2%)은 통계적 유의 미달(p=0.158).

피개매식 임플란트에는 3mm 지대주를 우선 선택하되, 장기 주위염 예방은 각화점막(KM) 폭과 PIMT 순응도가 지대주 높이보다 더 결정적인 인자.

## Summary

This 7-year randomised controlled trial extends the authors' prior 1-year study to provide the longest available follow-up data comparing 1-mm (control) vs 3-mm (test) definitive abutment heights on subcrestally placed implants. All implants (Klockner system) were placed 1.5 mm subcrestally and received definitive screw-retained restorations after 16 weeks.

Of 65 original patients (99 implants), 37 patients (63 implants) completed 7-year follow-up — a 43% dropout rate largely attributed to COVID-19 disruptions, patient mortality, and loss to contact.

The 3-mm abutment group maintained significantly more coronal marginal bone from surgery to year 7. The inter-group MBL difference was greatest in the first year (established in the prior 1-year publication) and narrowed thereafter; by years 1–7, mesial MBL remained significantly lower in the 3-mm group (p=0.049) but distal MBL difference was no longer significant (p=0.749), suggesting abutment height primarily drives early bone remodelling rather than ongoing progressive loss.

Peri-implantitis prevalence was 18.2% in the 1-mm group vs 3.2% in the 3-mm group — a 5.7-fold difference that failed to reach statistical significance (p=0.158), likely due to insufficient power after dropout. Peri-implant mucositis was paradoxically more common in the 3-mm group (74.2% vs 54.6%), possibly reflecting the diagnostic use of bleeding on probing as the primary criterion.

## Key Contributions

- Longest RCT (7 years) comparing 1-mm vs 3-mm abutment heights
- Confirms statistically significant MBL advantage of 3-mm abutments at 7 years (p<0.001)
- Clinically meaningful but underpowered peri-implantitis difference (18.2% vs 3.2%)
- Demonstrates keratinized mucosa width as a consistent independent risk factor for peri-implant mucositis (~1 mm less KM in diseased vs healthy implants, regardless of abutment height)
- Identifies posterior mandibular location as a 7× risk multiplier for peri-implant disease with 1-mm abutments (p=0.039)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Parallel-group RCT, 7-year follow-up; CONSORT 2010 reported
- **Implant system**: Klockner (Barcelona); all placed 1.5 mm subcrestally
- **Groups**: 1-mm abutment (control, n=32 implants at 7y) vs 3-mm abutment (test, n=31 implants at 7y)
- **Primary outcome**: MBL change (IS–fBIC) from year 1 to year 7
- **Radiography**: Paralleling cone technique with silicone bite keys; independent blinded examiner (ICC=0.99)
- **Statistical unit**: Patient; Bonferroni correction applied; no regression (sample too small)
- **PIMT**: 3–6 month intervals; 83.8% non-compliant or erratically compliant at 7 years

## Results

**Marginal Bone Loss (IS–fBIC) — Surgery to 7 Years:**

| Site | 1-mm | 3-mm | p |
|------|------|------|---|
| Mesial | −0.42 ± 0.50 mm | −0.27 ± 1.32 mm | <0.001 |
| Distal | −0.35 ± 0.38 mm | −0.33 ± 1.39 mm | <0.001 |

**Year 1 to Year 7:**
- Mesial: 1-mm −0.22 mm vs 3-mm −0.24 mm; p=0.049 (significant)
- Distal: 1-mm −0.10 mm vs 3-mm −0.33 mm; p=0.749 (NS)

**Peri-Implant Disease at 7 Years:**
- Healthy: 24.2% (1-mm) vs 22.6% (3-mm)
- Mucositis: 54.6% (1-mm) vs 74.2% (3-mm)
- Peri-implantitis: **18.2% (1-mm) vs 3.2% (3-mm)**; p=0.158 (NS)

**Risk Modifiers:**
- Posterior mandible (1-mm): OR 7× peri-implant disease vs maxilla (p=0.039)
- Mucosal thickness >2 mm + 3-mm abutment: higher mucositis risk (p=0.004)
- KM width: ~1.05 mm (1-mm group) and 1.27 mm (3-mm group) less KM in mucositis vs healthy implants
- Smoking, PIMT compliance, interproximal hygiene device: not significantly associated with disease in either group
