## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (overview of reviews) of 6 systematic reviews (2014–2023) comparing titanium (Ti) vs zirconia (Zr) dental implants across survival, success, marginal bone loss (MBL), probing depth, plaque index, bleeding on probing, pink esthetic score, and osseointegration.

Ti showed higher survival (92.6–100% vs Zr 87.5–93.3%) and success rates in most included reviews (Elnayef 2017: Zr ~89% higher failure risk; Duan 2023 meta-analysis: success RR 0.87, p=0.03 favoring Ti), while MBL/PPD/PI/BoP were largely comparable or mixed; Zr showed better esthetics in some reviews and faster early bone apposition, reversing by 4 weeks.

Narrative synthesis only, no cross-review meta-analysis; the material-level "Ti > Zr" conclusion is not stratified by implant design and should be read against [[implants/mohseni-2024-clinical-outcomes-zirconia-implants]], whose larger SR+MA finds zirconia comparable to titanium at 10 years when one-piece, non-drill-prepared designs are used.

티타늄이 대부분 SR에서 생존율(92.6–100% vs 지르코니아 87.5–93.3%)·성공률 우위(Elnayef 2017: 지르코니아 실패위험 약 89% 높음; Duan 2023 메타분석 성공률 RR 0.87, p=0.03 티타늄 우세), MBL·탐침깊이·치태지수·출혈지수는 대체로 비슷하거나 혼재; 지르코니아는 일부 SR에서 심미 우위 및 초기(2주) 골접촉 우세(4주차 역전).

정량 통합 없는 서술 종합이며, 재료 단위의 "Ti > Zr" 결론은 임플란트 설계(1-piece vs 2-piece)로 층화되지 않음 — 더 크고 최신인 [[implants/mohseni-2024-clinical-outcomes-zirconia-implants]] SR+MA는 1-piece·비드릴가공 설계 시 지르코니아가 10년간 티타늄과 동등함을 보고해 함께 해석 필요.

## Summary
This 2026 umbrella review (overview of 6 systematic reviews, 2014–2023) directly compares titanium and zirconia dental implants across survival, success, MBL, probing depth, plaque index, bleeding on probing, pink esthetic score, and osseointegration. Unlike prior zirconia-only syntheses in this wiki (Mohseni 2024, Pieralli 2017), it frames zirconia against titanium as the explicit comparator. Five of six included reviews favored titanium on survival, and pooled sub-analyses (Duan 2023) found a statistically significant success-rate advantage for titanium (RR 0.87, p=0.03) though the survival difference was not significant (RR 0.91, p=0.10). Soft-tissue and esthetic outcomes were more mixed, with zirconia favored on esthetics in one review. The review's own limitation — no stratification by implant design (one-piece vs two-piece, chairside-drilled vs factory-finished) — is exactly the axis Mohseni 2024 identifies as the true driver of zirconia failure, creating a genuine point of tension in the literature rather than a simple material-superiority finding.

## Key Contributions
- First wiki-held source directly pooling Ti-vs-Zr comparative outcome data across multiple SRs rather than characterizing zirconia implants in isolation.
- Reports per-outcome vote-counting across 6 SRs (how many favored Ti/Zr/equal) plus two formal meta-analytic estimates (Duan 2023: survival RR 0.91 NS, success RR 0.87 p=0.03).
- Surfaces osseointegration timing crossover: zirconia ahead at 2 weeks, titanium ahead at 4 weeks (Sivaraman 2018 in vitro bone-to-implant contact data).

## Methodology
PRISMA-guided overview of reviews, PICOS-defined (adults, ≥2 implants, Ti vs Zr/hybrid comparator), searched PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar for SRs (with/without meta-analysis) published 2014–March 2023 with ≥6-month follow-up. 3,364 records screened → 6 SRs included after duplicate/automated/title-abstract exclusion and full-text assessment by two independent reviewers with third-reviewer arbitration. Quality appraised with AMSTAR-2 (4 high, 1 moderate, 1 low). No quantitative pooling was performed across the six included SRs — synthesis is descriptive/vote-counting, supplemented by the meta-analytic point estimates already reported within two of the source SRs (Duan 2023, Elnayef 2017).

## Results
- Survival: Ti > Zr in 5/6 SRs; Zr range 87.5–93.3%, Ti range 92.6–100%. Duan 2023 MA: RR 0.91 (95% CI 0.82–1.02, p=0.10, I²=0%). Elnayef 2017: Zr ~89% higher failure risk than Ti.
- Success: Ti > Zr in 2/4 SRs, equal in 2/4. Duan 2023 MA: RR 0.87 (95% CI 0.78–0.98, p=0.03, I²=0%).
- MBL: Zr worse in 2/4 SRs, equal in 2/4 (Ti −1.17 to −0.125 mm; Zr −1.38 to −0.25 mm).
- PPD, PI, BoP: mixed direction, mostly non-significant across 2 SRs each.
- PES: 1 SR favored Zr, 1 favored Ti.
- Osseointegration: comparable overall (1 SR); Zr higher bone-to-implant contact at 2 weeks (54–55% vs 42–52%), Ti higher at 4 weeks (68–91% vs 62–80%).
