## Three-line Summary

3-year prospective controlled trial (CID 2026): 87 implants, 43 patients; Hybrid Funnel Technique (HFT — selective cortical preparation + medullary osteocompaction) vs conventional drilling using identical bioactive-surface implants.

HFT significantly reduced MBL at both 1 year (0.33 vs 0.78 mm, p=.006) and 3 years (0.46 vs 1.34 mm, p<0.001); in multivariate analysis, site preparation technique was the only independent predictor of MBL (β=−1.10 mm); HFT also showed lower BoP and PI.

Implant site preparation technique determines long-term marginal bone stability more than surface treatment — osteocompaction during osteotomy provides a sustained biological advantage over subtractive drilling.

## Summary

Canullo et al. (CID 2026) conducted a 3-year prospective non-randomized controlled trial comparing HFT vs conventional subtractive drilling for implant site preparation. Both groups received identical bioactive-surface implants, isolating the osteotomy technique effect. HFT combines selective cortical bone shaping with medullary bone osteocompaction (compacting rather than removing bone). At 1 and 3 years, HFT implants showed significantly less MBL. In multivariate linear mixed-effects models, site preparation technique was the only variable independently associated with 3-year MBL — not insertion torque, not patient factors. HFT also maintained better peri-implant soft tissue health (lower BoP and PI).

## Key Contributions

- Site preparation technique (HFT vs conventional) is the single independent predictor of 3-year MBL
- HFT advantage over conventional: 0.88 mm less MBL at 3 years
- Identical implant surface in both groups — surface chemistry effect isolated out
- Osteocompaction principle (preserving bone architecture vs thermal/mechanical damage from subtractive drilling) reduces crestal bone remodeling
- BoP and PI both lower in HFT — better long-term peri-implant health
- Challenges the "surface treatment is key" narrative — technique matters as much or more

## Methodology

Prospective non-randomized CCT; multicenter (Rome, Genova, Foggia, Bern); n=87 implants 43 patients (HFT n=45, conventional n=42); identical bioactive-surface implants; MBL by radiograph at 1yr and 3yr; BoP, PI at 3yr; linear mixed-effects models.
