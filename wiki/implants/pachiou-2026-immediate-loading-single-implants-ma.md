## Three-line Summary

De novo RCT-level MA (J Prosthet Dent 2026): 7 SRs + 2 additional RCTs → 40 unique trials, >1200 single implants; immediate loading vs conventional or early loading, 1–5 year stratified analysis.

No significant differences in implant survival at 1, 2, 3, or 5 years (RR 1.00–1.01 throughout); MBL differences clinically negligible; patient satisfaction and complication rates comparable (all moderate certainty for survival).

When applied under appropriate clinical conditions, immediate loading of single implants is equivalent to early or conventional loading across all follow-up intervals studied.

## Summary

Pachiou et al. (University of Zurich, J Prosthet Dent 2026) conducted a de novo RCT-level meta-analysis to overcome the well-known problem of double-counting primary trials across systematic reviews (CCA=11.5%). By re-extracting all eligible primary RCTs from 7 identified SRs plus 2 additional RCTs, they assembled 40 unique trials with >1200 single implants and 1–10 years of follow-up. Time-stratified meta-analyses showed no statistically significant differences in implant survival between immediate and conventional loading at 1, 2, 3, or 5 years (RR 1.00–1.01; all moderate certainty). Marginal bone loss differences were clinically negligible at all time points. Patient-reported outcomes and complication rates were comparable. The conclusion—that immediate loading of single implants is equivalent to conventional loading under appropriate clinical conditions—is now supported by the largest and most methodologically rigorous synthesis to date.

## Key Contributions

- Eliminates double-counting by re-extracting primary RCTs from 7 SRs (CCA=11.5%)
- Time-stratified survival analysis (1, 2, 3, 5 years): all non-significant for immediate vs conventional
- Confirms MBL differences clinically negligible across all time points
- GRADE moderate certainty for survival; low-very low for secondary outcomes
- Largest single synthesis on this topic: 40 unique trials, >1200 single implants

## Methodology

De novo RCT-level MA. Databases: PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Epistemonikos, Cochrane (through Oct 2025). 7 SRs restricted to RCTs identified; all eligible primary RCTs re-extracted (40 unique). CCA=11.5%. Random-effects MA; RR for implant survival/complications; MD for MBL; SMD for patient outcomes. GRADE.

## Results

**Immediate vs Conventional Loading — Implant Survival:**

| Follow-up | RR | 95% CI | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 year | 1.00 | 0.98–1.02 | NS |
| 2 years | 1.01 | 0.96–1.05 | NS |
| 3 years | 1.01 | 0.97–1.05 | NS |
| 5 years | 1.00 | 0.97–1.03 | NS |

**Immediate vs Early Loading — 1 year:** RR 0.99 (95% CI 0.96–1.02); NS

**MBL:** Minimal differences; clinically negligible at all time points

**Patient satisfaction:** No significant difference

**Complications:** Infrequent; no significant intergroup difference
