## Three-line Summary

Preclinical in vivo animal study (rat maxillary extraction sockets; control = minimal implant–osteotomy misfit vs test = high misfit that osseo-densified the peri-implant bone), designed to link misfit, insertion torque, and implant stability to their short- and longer-term effects on peri-implant bone — the only held paper besides Trisi 2011 that *assigns* the mechanical exposure rather than binning it post-hoc.

High-misfit implants produced micro-fractures and an extensive zone of dying osteocytes, a pro-resorptive environment (TRAP activity, cathepsin K IHC), and no new bone formation (absent ALP activity, absent collagen I IHC); µCT, BIC quantification, vimentin and IL1-β IHC demonstrated early implant failure presenting as a crater-like lesion filled with fibrous, inflamed granulation tissue — **the abstract reports no n, no torque values, no misfit magnitudes, no BIC percentages, no timepoints, and no p-values**, so this paper contributes a mechanism and a direction, not a number.

Establishes that a ceiling on densification exists — "excessive osseo-densification can lead directly to osseo-destruction" — but does not locate it (two-point contrast, "excessive" undefined), and transfer is bounded by rat maxilla ≠ human jaw, fresh extraction socket ≠ healed ridge, and misfit ≠ bur-mediated OD.

## 세줄요약

전임상 생체 내 동물실험 (쥐 상악 발치와; 대조군 = 임플란트–골삭제 간 최소 미스핏(misfit) vs 시험군 = 주위골을 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD)시키는 고도 미스핏) — 미스핏·삽입 토크 (Insertion Torque, IT)·임플란트 안정성이 주위골에 미치는 단기·장기 효과를 연결하는 설계이며, 보유 논문 중 Trisi 2011과 함께 노출을 **사후 분류가 아니라 실험적으로 배정한** 단 두 편 중 하나다.

고미스핏군에서 미세골절 + 광범위한 골세포 (Osteocyte) 사멸 구역, 흡수 우세 환경 (주석산 저항성 산성 인산분해효소 (Tartrate-Resistant Acid Phosphatase, TRAP) 활성 · 카텝신 K (Cathepsin K) 면역염색 (Immunohistochemistry, IHC)), 신생골 형성 부재 (알칼리성 인산분해효소 (Alkaline Phosphatase, ALP) 활성 및 제1형 교원질 (Collagen I) IHC 음성); 마이크로 CT (µCT) · 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 정량 · 비멘틴 (Vimentin) · 인터루킨-1β (IL1-β) IHC로 조기 실패 확인 — 섬유성 염증성 육아조직으로 채워진 분화구 모양 병소. **초록에 표본수·토크값·미스핏 크기·BIC 수치·시점·p값이 일절 없어** 이 논문의 기여는 수치가 아니라 기전과 방향이다.

골밀도화에 **천장이 존재함**을 확립한다 ("과도한 골밀도화는 곧바로 골파괴 (Osseo-destruction)로 이어진다") — 그러나 그 천장의 **위치는 찾지 못한다** (2점 대조뿐, "excessive" 미정의). 쥐 상악 ≠ 사람 턱 · 발치와 ≠ 치유된 치조제 · 미스핏 ≠ 버 매개 OD라는 전이 한계 안에서만 읽어야 한다.

## Summary

Some misfit between implant and osteotomy is *required* for primary stability, and undersizing the osteotomy is the standard way to create it. Coyac et al. asked what happens when that misfit is large. Placing implants into rat maxillary extraction sockets, they contrasted a minimal-misfit control against a high-misfit test group in which the implant osseo-densified the surrounding bone — and then followed the bone biologically rather than mechanically.

The test group did not merely fail to gain; it self-destructed along a stageable pathway. Misfit-induced stresses fractured the peri-implant bone microscopically and killed osteocytes across an extensive zone. The interface turned pro-resorptive (TRAP activity, cathepsin K) while the formation program never started (no ALP, no collagen I) — resorption without replacement. µCT, BIC quantification, vimentin and IL1-β IHC then documented early implant failure as a crater-like lesion filled with fibrous, inflamed granulation tissue. The authors' framing is deliberately causal: by controlling every other risk indicator, they conclude that **excessive osseo-densification leads directly to osseo-destruction**.

This matters to the wiki for a specific structural reason. The held densification literature is heavily weighted toward benefit, and — crucially — most of it measures **mechanics without healing**: Huwais 2017 is a porcine bench study whose 3× BIC is an immediate post-insertion measurement, and Tabassum 2021 is an ex vivo goat model that explicitly defers to "living bone models." Both establish that compaction buys insertion torque and contact *at time zero*. Neither can observe what that compacted, strained bone does over the following weeks. Coyac supplies exactly that missing arm, and the answer — in this bed, at this magnitude — is that the time-zero gain is not merely lost but inverted.

**The paper's claim is about excess, not about densification.** It offers no threshold, no dose axis, and no intermediate arm; "excessive" is never operationalized. It therefore proves a ceiling exists without locating it, and nothing here licenses a clinical misfit or torque limit, or a retreat from OD as a technique. Read against the substantial held evidence of OD benefit, this is the boundary condition on that evidence — not a refutation of it.

## Key Contributions

### 1. It assigns the mechanical exposure — and finds harm

[[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]] names as its decisive gap that **all three human studies binned torque post-hoc**, leaving "high torque" inseparable from "dense cortical bone," and that the only study which experimentally assigned the exposure ([[implants/isq/trisi-2011-high-low-implant-torque-histology-sheep]], sheep) **found no harm**. Coyac is the second assigned-exposure study in the wiki, and it breaks that pattern: misfit is set by design, and destruction follows.

This does not close the overview's gap — the gap is specifically about *human* studies, and a rat is not a human. What it does is remove the overview's incidental asymmetry, in which every assigned-exposure datapoint pointed toward safety. The causal reading of the harm data now has an experimental animal correlate; the confounded-marker reading no longer holds the design high ground uncontested.

### 2. It reopens the osseocompression mechanism on the exact marker that closed it

The wiki records Trisi 2011 as *directly refuting* the osseocompression-necrosis hypothesis: at 110 Ncm in dense sheep mandibular cortex, **no osteocyte lacunar emptying, no avascular necrotic zones**. Coyac reports an **extensive zone of dying osteocytes** — the same cell, the same class of insult, the opposite result.

These findings do not literally conflict; they are compatible in the strict sense, because the beds differ (see §4). What changes is the *scope* of Trisi's refutation. "Compression does not kill osteocytes" was doing universal work in the wiki; it is now bounded to beds that can absorb the strain, at magnitudes below some unlocated threshold. Trisi's result stands; its reach does not.

### 3. Resorption without formation is the distinguishing signature

The marker set separates two very different failures, and the distinction is the paper's real mechanistic payload:

| Program | Markers | Coyac test group |
|---|---|---|
| Resorption | TRAP activity, cathepsin K IHC | **Active** |
| Formation | ALP activity, collagen I IHC | **Absent** |

Trisi's 7-day stability dip was resorption *followed by* greater reformation — remodeling, not pathology. Coyac's interface resorbs and never rebuilds, because the osteocytes that would have signaled the rebuild are dead. This is why the endpoint is a crater filled with granulation tissue rather than a remodeled interface, and it is a mechanistically distinct event from the transient dip — not a more severe version of it.

### 4. The transfer limits are load-bearing, not boilerplate

Three mismatches bound every clinical inference from this paper, and each cuts in a specific direction:

- **Rat maxilla ≠ human jaw.** Different bone volume, cortical thickness, remodeling rate, healing kinetics. No held paper validates this bridge — the same species-bridge objection the overview raises against Trisi's sheep applies here with equal force, and honesty requires applying it symmetrically rather than only to the paper one disagrees with.
- **Fresh extraction socket ≠ healed ridge.** The wiki's clinical harm signal (Marconcini, Aldahlawi) lives at the **healed dense mandibular crest**. Coyac's bed is a fresh maxillary socket — a compromised, partly resorbing envelope with different vascularity and mechanical support. This is a compartment mismatch, and by the overview's own compartment logic it must not be elided.
- **Misfit ≠ bur-mediated OD.** The exposure is an undersized osteotomy producing geometric interference, not counterclockwise Densah-bur compaction. The bridge is real — Huwais documents the spring-back/elastic-strain interference fit that *is* misfit, and Coyac treats densification as misfit's consequence — but they are not interchangeable interventions, and no bur protocol was tested.

### 5. It proves a ceiling exists without locating it

The conclusion is explicitly about *excess*, yet there is no threshold, no dose axis, and only a two-point contrast (minimal vs high misfit). No monotonicity, breakpoint, or safe band can be inferred. The correct clinical import is a change in the shape of the question — from "how much stability can compaction buy?" to "how much compaction is too much?" — not an answer to it.

## Methodology

**Design:** preclinical in vivo controlled animal experiment, two arms, with a biomechanical/computational stress-strain component implied by the title and the "computational modeling" keyword.

**Model:** rat; implants placed in **maxillary extraction sites** (fresh sockets).

**Groups:**
- Control — implants with **minimal misfit** relative to the osteotomy
- Test — implants with a **high degree of misfit**, which therefore **osseo-densified** the peri-implant bone

**Stated aim:** to understand the relationship between misfit, insertion torque, implant stability, and their cumulative short- and longer-term effects on peri-implant bone.

**Outcomes named:** µCT imaging; BIC quantification; histology (micro-fractures, osteocyte viability); enzyme histochemistry (TRAP, ALP); IHC (cathepsin K, collagen I, vimentin, IL1-β).

**Not recoverable from the abstract** — and therefore stated nowhere on this page: number of animals or implants, misfit magnitude (osteotomy-to-implant diameter differential), insertion torque values, implant stability values, sacrifice timepoints, BIC percentages, any p-value or effect size, and all finite-element parameters and outputs. This page is an abstract-only ingest (no PMC copy exists); retrieving the full text would materially upgrade it.

## Results

> **No quantitative benchmarks exist on this page.** The PubMed abstract is entirely qualitative. The rows below are the authors' directional findings, not measured values held by this wiki. **Do not cite a number from this paper — there is none to cite.**

| Domain | Test (high misfit, osseo-densified) vs Control (minimal misfit) |
|---|---|
| Peri-implant bone integrity | **Micro-fractures** produced by misfit-induced stresses |
| Osteocyte viability | **Extensive zone of dying osteocytes** |
| Resorptive activity | **Pro-resorptive environment** — TRAP activity ↑, cathepsin K IHC positive |
| Bone formation | **Absent** — no ALP activity, no collagen I IHC |
| Inflammation / repair tissue | Vimentin, IL1-β IHC consistent with fibrous, inflamed granulation tissue |
| Implant outcome | **Early failure** — crater-like lesion filled with fibrous, inflamed granulation tissue |
| BIC | Quantified; contributed to the failure demonstration (direction unfavorable). Percentages not in abstract. |

**Authors' conclusion:** "By controlling every other risk indicator, we confirmed how excessive osseo-densification can lead directly to osseo-destruction."

### What this does and does not license

| Licensed by this paper | **Not** licensed by this paper |
|---|---|
| Densification has a destructive regime | Any misfit, torque, or drill-diameter limit |
| Osteocyte death → resorption-without-formation is a viable failure pathway | That this pathway operates in human healed ridges |
| Compression-benefit findings measured without healing may invert after healing | That OD is harmful, or that the wiki's OD-benefit evidence is overturned |
| Trisi's "no necrosis" is bed- and magnitude-bounded | That Trisi is wrong, or that 110 Ncm is unsafe in dense cortical bone |
