> [!warning] Withdrawn Cochrane Review (2018)
> Cochrane Oral Health withdrew this review as of Issue 5, 2018 (23 May 2018). Reason: "substantially out-of-date and does not meet current Cochrane methodological and reporting standards, but is correct at the date of publication." Authors were unable to update it. The review has been withdrawn from the Cochrane Library, but previous versions remain accessible in the 'Other versions' tab. **This wiki page preserves the 2009 version (pub3) for evidence-trail completeness; its conclusions are superseded by later SRs.**

## Three-line Summary

Cochrane systematic review (search to January 2009) comparing one-stage (transmucosal/non-submerged) versus two-stage (submerged) implant placement for replacing missing teeth.

Only one eligible RCT (Barber 1996, n=40) was identified and included in the 2009 update — the original 2007 version had zero included studies. The single RCT showed no significant difference in implant failure between one-stage and two-stage placement at 1-year follow-up.

The review was **withdrawn in 2018** by Cochrane Oral Health because it was substantially out-of-date, did not meet current Cochrane methodological standards, and the authors were unable to update it. Conclusions are correct only as of the 2009 publication date.

## Summary

This Cochrane review was the first (and only) Cochrane-branded systematic review directly comparing one-stage versus two-stage implant placement — the foundational surgical-technique question of submerged vs non-submerged healing. The 2009 update (pub3) included a new search to January 2009 and re-assessed eligibility, resulting in the inclusion of one RCT (Barber 1996, n=40) that had been excluded in the original 2007 version. That single small trial found no significant difference in implant failure at 1 year. No meta-analysis was possible. In 2018, Cochrane Oral Health formally withdrew the review as "substantially out-of-date" and not meeting current methodological standards. The evidence base at withdrawal was critically insufficient (1 RCT, n=40). Later non-Cochrane systematic reviews with broader inclusion criteria and more studies have superseded this review.

## Key Contributions

- Only Cochrane-branded SR on one-stage vs two-stage implant placement, establishing the evidence-trail origin for this clinical question.
- The 2009 update demonstrated how re-assessment of eligibility criteria changed the included evidence set (Barber 1996 moved from excluded to included).
- Rare example of a Cochrane review formally **withdrawn for obsolescence** (not error) with a transparent public reason — valuable for teaching evidence currency and review lifecycle.

## Methodology

Cochrane intervention review; searches of Cochrane Oral Health Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE, EMBASE to January 2009; RCTs comparing one-stage (transmucosal healing abutment, no second surgery) vs two-stage (submerged cover screw, second-stage uncovering) placement in partially/fully edentulous adults. Risk of bias assessed per Cochrane Handbook. Screening, data extraction, and RoB assessment in duplicate. The original 2007 version (pub1) found zero eligible RCTs; the 2009 update (pub3) re-evaluated and included Barber 1996 (n=40).

- **No meta-analysis performed** — only one included study.
- Authors' 2009 conclusion: "There is no evidence of a difference in implant failure between one-stage and two-stage surgery... The evidence is insufficient to permit any conclusion."
