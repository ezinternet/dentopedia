## Three-line Summary

In vitro 2×2 factorial study (bovine rib, n=80 sites) comparing continuous vs intermittent drilling × room-temperature (23.7°C) vs chilled (6.0°C) saline irrigation on bone temperature during implant osteotomy.

Continuous vs intermittent drilling showed no significant temperature difference at the same irrigation temperature (p=0.45); chilled saline (6.0°C) significantly reduced bone temperature vs 23.7°C regardless of technique (p=0.0001); all groups remained below 47°C.

Drilling technique (continuous vs intermittent) is not an independent thermal variable — irrigation temperature is the decisive factor.

## Summary

This in vitro study used a 2×2 factorial design (continuous vs intermittent drilling × 23.7°C vs 6.0°C irrigation) to evaluate bone temperature during implant site preparation in fresh bovine rib specimens. The key finding was that the choice of drilling technique — continuous or intermittent — produced no statistically significant thermal difference when irrigation temperature was held constant (p=0.45). In contrast, cooled saline (6.0°C) significantly reduced bone temperature compared to room-temperature saline (p=0.0001) under both techniques. All four groups stayed below the 47°C osteonecrosis threshold.

This result challenges a blanket recommendation to use intermittent (peck) drilling as a primary thermal management strategy, suggesting that chilling the irrigation is a more reliable lever. The specific peck interval was not disclosed in the abstract.

## Key Contributions

- Head-to-head comparison of continuous vs intermittent drilling in controlled in vitro conditions — isolates technique as the variable while holding irrigation volume and other factors constant.
- No significant thermal benefit from intermittent drilling at the same irrigation temperature.
- Confirms irrigation temperature as the primary modifiable variable, consistent with Kapse 2022.

## Methodology

- **Design**: In vitro experimental, 2×2 factorial
- **Bone model**: Fresh bovine rib (16 blocks, 5 sites each; 13 mm depth)
- **N**: 80 osteotomy sites
- **Groups**: A (continuous, 23.7°C), B (intermittent, 23.7°C), C (continuous, 6.0°C), D (intermittent, 6.0°C)
- **Measurement**: Thermal probe (type not specified in abstract)
- **Statistics**: Wilcoxon signed-rank test
- **Peck interval**: Not specified in abstract

## Results

- A vs B (technique, room-temp): W=86, p=0.45 — **not significant**
- B vs D (intermittent: room vs cold): W=45, p=0.0005 — **significant**
- C vs D (cold: continuous vs intermittent): W=41, p=0.003 — **significant** (cold continuous slightly better than cold intermittent)
- All groups: temperature < 47°C threshold

Exact mean temperatures not available from abstract.
