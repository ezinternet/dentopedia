## Three-line Summary

Review (Implant Dentistry, 2003) of how implant biomechanical design (geometry, material, surface, thread) affects osseointegration and long-term success.

The authors argue there is no single optimal design, and that strength, interfacial stability and load transfer can be engineered through materials, surfaces and thread design.

The abstract notes limited information on thread design's effect on overall success, so treat design claims as engineering rationale.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Implant Dentistry, 2003) — 임플란트 생체역학 설계 (형상·재료·표면·나사산) 와 골유착 및 장기 성공의 관계.

단일한 최적 설계는 없으며, 재료·표면·나사산 설계로 강도, 계면 안정성, 하중 전달을 공학적으로 높일 수 있다고 주장.

나사산 설계가 전체 성공에 미치는 영향은 자료가 제한적이므로 설계 주장은 공학적 근거로 볼 것.

## Summary

Steigenga et al. treat implant design as a set of interrelated engineering trade-offs rather than a ranking of systems. Their point is that osseointegration quality depends on geometry, surface and load transfer together.

## Key Contributions

- Engineering framing of implant design as interrelated factors.
- Explicit thread-design evidence gap.

## Methodology

Literature review of design elements in use; no search method stated.
