## One-line Summary
Prospective cohort (n=30 implants, IT≤20 Ncm, 1-year follow-up): 100% survival for 29 followed implants; ISQ rose from 67 to 80 in 3 months; 3 "spinners" (<5 Ncm) all osseointegrated — low IT alone does not predict failure.

## 한줄요약

## Summary
Norton enrolled a self-selected cohort of 30 implants whose insertion torque fell at or below 20 Ncm, including 3 "spinners" with IT <5 Ncm. All patients were followed for 1 year. Despite very low initial torques — and in the case of spinners, virtually no mechanical fixation at placement — 29/29 followed implants achieved osseointegration (one excluded for lost follow-up data; overall 96.7%). Mean ISQ improved from 67 MD/65 BL at surgery to 80 MD/79 BL at prosthetic loading (~3 months), demonstrating that secondary stability (biological osseointegration) compensated for low primary stability. Marginal bone loss was favorable: 71.4% showed no loss, only 7.2% exceeded 0.5 mm. No implants were lost during functional loading. Immediate temporization in a subset (ISQ ≥55) showed no adverse ISQ or bone outcomes. Norton argues that IT measures rotational friction, not axial stability — making IT a poor predictor of implant success.

## Key Contributions
- Prospective data proving IT≤20 Ncm is compatible with osseointegration and favorable bone levels
- "Spinners" (IT<5 Ncm): all three osseointegrated — strongest clinical evidence that very low IT ≠ failure
- ISQ recovery from surgery to loading is the meaningful stability trajectory (not initial IT)
- Immediate temporization safe when ISQ≥55, even with very low IT
- Mechanistic argument: IT = rotational friction; ISQ = axial stiffness — different constructs

## Methodology
- Closed-cohort prospective; n=30 implants (22 patients), self-selected IT≤20 Ncm
- Torque subgroups: <5 Ncm (n=3), 5–10 (n=9), 10 (n=4), 15 (n=8), 20 Ncm (n=6)
- ISQ MD + BL at surgery and prosthetic loading (~3 months); 1-year bone loss
- Location: 22 maxillary, 8 mandibular; 10 incisors, 10 premolars, 9 molars, 1 canine
- Immediate temporization: esthetic need + ISQ≥55 (n=9, including 6 immediate extractions)
- 1-year marginal bone levels relative to fixed reference point

## Results
- ISQ at surgery: 67.2 MD / 64.5 BL → 3 months: 80.0 MD / 79.1 BL
- Survival: 29/29 (100%); including excluded: 96.7%
- Bone loss: 71.4% none; 21.4% 0.1–0.5mm; 7.2% >0.5mm (max 2.82mm, 1 outlier)
- Immediate temporization: no significant ISQ or bone loss difference
- Spinners (<5 Ncm): all 3 osseointegrated
- No correlation between IT and ISQ (Spearman, NS)
