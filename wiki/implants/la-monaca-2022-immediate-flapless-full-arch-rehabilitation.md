## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center cohort (n=28 edentulous patients, 32 prostheses [17 all-on-4/15 all-on-6], 164 implants) evaluating immediate, flapless, computer-guided (NobelGuide) full-arch rehabilitation over a mean follow-up of 6.46 ± 2.236 years (range 1-10 years).

Cumulative implant survival was 89.7% for all-on-4 vs 99.0% for all-on-6 (7 vs 1 failure); marginal bone loss reached 1.38±1.28 mm at 5 years and 2.09±0.56 mm at 10 years with no significant all-on-4 vs all-on-6 or tilted vs axial difference, but significantly more MBL with natural-tooth opposing dentition (1.70 vs 1.01 mm, p=0.017).

All-on-6 had markedly fewer biological complications than all-on-4 (1.0% vs 10.3%, p=0.014), while overall prosthesis "success" (free of any modification) was low in both groups (51.5%), reflecting long-term prosthetic maintenance burden.

## Summary

This single-center retrospective cohort study (Sapienza University of Rome, patients treated 2009-2019) reports up to 10-year clinical and radiographic outcomes for immediate, flapless, computer-guided (NobelGuide, Nobel Biocare) full-arch fixed prostheses supported by either 4 implants (2 axial + 2 distally tilted) or 6 implants (all axial). It is among the longest follow-up datasets for this specific guided-surgery/immediate-loading protocol and provides a head-to-head 4-vs-6 implant comparison within one center and one protocol, plus regression-identified risk factors for marginal bone loss.

## Key Contributions

- Long-term (mean 6.46 years, up to 10 years) outcomes for computer-guided flapless immediate-loading full-arch rehabilitation — a follow-up duration rarely reported for this protocol (most prior literature: 1-60 months).
- Direct within-protocol comparison of all-on-4 vs all-on-6 configurations for implant/prosthesis survival, MBL, and complications.
- Multiple regression identifying implant length, opposing natural dentition, buccal bone thickness, and crestal soft tissue thickness as independent predictors of 5-year MBL.
- Detailed case-level documentation of implant failures and peri-implantitis management outcomes.

## Methodology

- Retrospective cohort, STROBE-guided, ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05307029.
- 28 edentulous patients (20F/8M, mean age 67.75±8.627y), 32 prostheses (17 all-on-4/15 all-on-6), 164 implants; mean follow-up 6.46±2.236 years (range 1-10y).
- Double-scan CT/CBCT technique with radiopaque markers → virtual planning in Procera NobelGuide software → stereolithographic surgical template → flapless implant placement → prefabricated all-acrylic resin fixed complete prosthesis for immediate loading.
- Group 1 (all-on-4): 2 axial + 2 tilted implants; Group 2 (all-on-6): 6 axial implants; allocation by clinician judgment (not randomized).
- Outcomes: implant survival, prosthesis success/survival (Kaplan-Meier), MBL (backward stepwise multiple linear regression, adjusted for age, gender, jaw, implant location/angulation, MUA height), biological and prosthetic complications.

## Results

- **Implant survival**: 89.7% all-on-4 (7 failures) vs 99.0% all-on-6 (1 failure).
- **Prosthesis success** (no modification): 51.5% overall (58.8% all-on-4/43.8% all-on-6, NS); **prosthesis survival**: 88.2% all-on-4, no failures all-on-6 (NS).
- **MBL**: 1.38±1.28mm at 5y, 2.09±0.56mm at 10y; no significant all-on-4 vs all-on-6 (p=0.104) or tilted vs axial (p=0.385) difference at 5y.
- Natural-tooth opposing dentition significantly increased MBL (1.70 vs 1.01mm for implant-supported opposing, p=0.017).
- Regression predictors of 5y MBL: implant length (β=-0.200, p=0.002, protective), opposing natural teeth (β=0.980, p<0.001, risk factor), crestal soft tissue thickness (β=0.351, p=0.018, risk factor), buccal bone thickness (β=-0.383, p=0.070, trend protective).
- **Biological complications**: significantly lower in all-on-6 (1.0%, 1 mucositis) than all-on-4 (10.3%, peri-implantitis in 2 subjects), p=0.014.
- **Prosthetic complications**: 3 detachments, 10 chippings/fractures, 3 severe occlusal wears; no framework/screw fractures.
