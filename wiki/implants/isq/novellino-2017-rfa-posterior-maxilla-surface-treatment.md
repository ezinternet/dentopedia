## One-line Summary
RCT (n=64 implants, 21 patients, posterior maxilla, 16 weeks) found hydrophilic SAE implants reach ISQ ≥70 at 2.24× the rate of standard SAE, with statistically higher ISQ values from week 8 onward.

## 한줄요약
상악 구치부 RCT (64개 임플란트, 21명, 16주): 친수성 SAE 표면이 일반 SAE보다 ISQ ≥70 도달 2.24배 빠르고(Cox HR 2.24, CI 1.62–3.11), 8주 이후 ISQ 유의하게 높음 (ANOVA p<0.01).

## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (64 implants, 21 patients, posterior maxilla) comparing hydrophilic and standard sandblasted acid-etched (SAE) surfaces by resonance frequency analysis over 16 weeks.

Hydrophilic implants had higher ISQ from 8 to 16 weeks (ANOVA p<0.01) and reached ISQ ≥70 2.24 times faster (Cox HR 2.24, 95% CI 1.62–3.11); insertion torque was similar (34.7 vs 36.1 Ncm) and 1-year survival was 100% in both.

A hydrophilic surface may shorten the wait to a stable ISQ in soft posterior maxillary bone, but the study is single-centre and single-surgeon, uses ISQ ≥70 as a surrogate and covers D3/D4 bone with one implant system.

## 세줄요약

상악 구치부에서 친수성과 일반 SAE (sandblasted acid-etched) 표면을 16주간 공진주파수분석 (RFA)으로 비교한 분할구강 RCT (임플란트 64개, 환자 21명)이다.

친수성 임플란트는 8~16주에 ISQ가 더 높았고 (ANOVA p<0.01) ISQ ≥70 도달이 2.24배 빨랐으며 (Cox HR 2.24, 95% CI 1.62–3.11), 식립 토크는 비슷했고 (34.7 대 36.1 Ncm) 1년 생존율은 두 군 모두 100%였다.

무른 상악 구치부 골에서 친수성 표면이 안정 ISQ까지의 대기 기간을 줄일 수 있으나, 단일기관·단일술자 연구이고 ISQ ≥70을 대리 지표로 썼으며 D3/D4 골과 단일 임플란트 시스템에 한정된다.

## Summary
This split-mouth RCT at the University of São Paulo placed 64 implants (32 SAE "Neoporos", 32 hydrophilic SAE "Acqua"; same 4.3×10mm conical design, Neodent) in posterior maxillae (D3/D4 bone) of 21 healthy non-smoking patients. ISQ was measured by Osstell at 8 time points from placement through 16 weeks. The hydrophilic surface showed no ISQ advantage in the early weeks (T0–T4), but diverged significantly from week 5 onward (ANOVA p<0.01). Cox regression showed hydrophilic implants reached ISQ ≥70 at 2.24× the rate of standard SAE (HR 2.24, 95% CI 1.62–3.11). Both groups achieved 100% survival at 1-year follow-up, indicating the ISQ acceleration does not necessarily translate to early failure prevention at this scale.

## Key Contributions
- Isolates surface chemistry effect on ISQ by keeping macro-geometry, length, and diameter identical — cleaner evidence than studies comparing different implant systems
- Quantifies the acceleration benefit: HR 2.24 for reaching ISQ ≥70, relevant for centers making early/immediate loading decisions
- Confirms the "ISQ dip" in D3/D4 posterior maxilla occurs in both groups at weeks 1–5, but hydrophilic surface shortens recovery
- 100% survival in both groups at 1 year — no clinical loading outcome difference despite ISQ advantage

## Methodology
- **Design**: Split-mouth RCT, single centre, single surgeon (MMN blinded to randomization, data collector blinded)
- **Population**: n=21 patients, ASA I, non-smokers; posterior maxilla only; ≥8mm bone height, ≥6.3mm width; D3/D4 bone; exclusions: grafted sites, DM, periodontitis, bruxism, bisphosphonates
- **Implants**: Neodent Drive CM, 4.3×10mm, conical compressive; SAE (Neoporos) vs hydrophilic SAE (Acqua)
- **Follow-up**: 16 weeks ISQ, 1-year clinical survival
- **ISQ**: Osstell Smartpeg A3, buccal + mesial at each timepoint, average recorded
- **Statistics**: ANOVA repeated measures (Greenhouse-Geisser correction), Kaplan-Meier with ISQ ≥70 cutoff, Cox regression for HR

## Results
- **ISQ trajectory**: Hydrophilic > SAE from T5 (8w) through T7 (16w), ANOVA p<0.01
- **ISQ range**: SAE 42–81; hydrophilic 32.5–82.5
- **Cox HR for ISQ ≥70**: 2.24 (95% CI 1.62–3.11, p<0.01)
- **Insertion torque**: SAE 36.06±5.99 Ncm vs hydrophilic 34.66±6.94 Ncm (NS)
- **Survival at 1 year**: 100% both groups (1 patient dropout, 2 implants lost to follow-up)
