## One-line Summary
Prospective pilot cohort (n=50; 21 T2DM, 29 osteoporosis; 12-month Osstell ISQ + Periotest) showing T2DM has lower ISQ than osteoporosis at 6 months (62.10 vs 67.04, p=0.02) but both conditions improve significantly by 12 months through distinct osseointegration pathways.

## 한줄요약
T2DM 21명 vs 골다공증 29명 12개월 전향 파일럿: 6개월 ISQ 당뇨 62.10 vs 골다공증 67.04 (p=0.02), 12개월에 두 군 모두 유의하게 개선; Periotest는 당뇨 군 12개월 호전 더 유의.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective pilot cohort (n=50: 21 T2DM, 29 osteoporosis) comparing implant stability by ISQ and Periotest over 12 months.

ISQ was lower in T2DM at 6 months (62.10 vs 67.04, p=0.0215) and the gap narrowed by 12 months (65.79 vs 68.93); both groups improved, and Periotest change at 12 months was more pronounced in the diabetic group (p=0.0001).

Both conditions can reach acceptable stability but diabetic implants stabilise later; the small single-centre pilot without blinding or HbA1c data needs confirmation.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 안정성을 12개월간 ISQ와 Periotest로 비교한 전향적 파일럿 코호트 (50명: 제2형 당뇨 21명, 골다공증 29명)다.

6개월 ISQ는 당뇨군이 더 낮았고 (62.10 대 67.04, p=0.0215) 12개월에는 차이가 줄었으며 (65.79 대 68.93), 두 군 모두 호전되었고 12개월 Periotest 변화는 당뇨군에서 더 뚜렷했다 (p=0.0001).

두 질환 모두 허용 가능한 안정성에 도달할 수 있으나 당뇨군은 안정화가 늦다; 소규모 단일기관 파일럿이며 눈가림과 HbA1c 자료가 없어 확인이 필요하다.

## Summary
This prospective pilot study enrolled 50 patients — 21 with T2DM and 29 with osteoporosis — and measured dental implant stability using both Osstell ISQ (임플란트 안정성 지수, Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) and Periotest M at 6 and 12 months post-placement. The study provides direct head-to-head ISQ data comparing two common systemic conditions that compromise bone quality through fundamentally different mechanisms. Osteoporosis primarily reduces bone density and primary mechanical stability, while T2DM impairs bone healing, vascularity, and immune response, affecting secondary biological stability. Despite lower ISQ in T2DM at 6 months, both groups showed significant improvement by 12 months, suggesting successful osseointegration is achievable in both conditions with appropriate management.

## Key Contributions
- Direct ISQ comparison between T2DM and osteoporosis in the same protocol — fills a gap addressed only narratively in prior reviews
- Dual measurement (Osstell + Periotest) provides complementary mechanical and biological stability data
- 12-month longitudinal data: both conditions show significant within-group ISQ improvement
- ISQ gap narrows over time (4.94 at 6mo → 3.14 at 12mo) — T2DM catching up
- Both conditions remain amenable to implant therapy with individualized management

## Methodology
- Prospective cohort (pilot)
- n = 50 patients: 21 T2DM, 29 osteoporosis; single-center
- Instruments: Osstell Mentor (ISQ) + Periotest M at 6 and 12 months post-implant placement
- Statistical analysis: between-group comparisons at each time point; within-group time-series comparisons; α = 0.05
- No HbA1c or DEXA density data reported

## Results
**Osstell ISQ:**
| Time | Osteoporosis (n=29) | T2DM (n=21) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 months | 67.04 ± 5.42 | 62.10 ± 5.46 | 0.0215 |
| 12 months | 68.93 ± 4.83 | 65.79 ± 3.87 | — |

**Periotest M (more negative = more stable):**
| Time | Osteoporosis | T2DM | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 months | -3.34 ± 1.59 | -2.81 ± 1.44 | 0.0001 |

- Both groups: significant ISQ improvement from 6 to 12 months (p < 0.05 within each group)
- ISQ gap narrowed: 4.94 (6 months) → 3.14 (12 months) — diabetes group catching up
- T2DM showed more significant improvement in Periotest values from 6 to 12 months
