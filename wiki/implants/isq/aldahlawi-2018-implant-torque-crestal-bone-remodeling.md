## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study (113 NobelActive implants, 66 patients) grouping achieved insertion torque (삽입 토크, IT) into high (>55 Ncm, mean 67.35 Ncm) vs control (≤55 Ncm, mean 37.9 Ncm) to measure crestal bone loss (변연골 소실, MBL).

High-IT group showed significantly greater MBL (0.95±1.60 mm vs 0.18±0.68 mm, P<0.05); mandibular sites were the highest-risk subgroup with 2.12±1.99 mm vs maxillary 0.25±0.65 mm; all 6 implant failures occurred in the high-IT group (overall survival 94.6%).

This study corroborates Marconcini 2018 in a different implant system (NobelActive vs Blossom™ groove design) and refines the harm threshold to >55 Ncm, with the mandible-specific effect pointing to cortical bone density as the key mediator.

## Summary

Aldahlawi et al. (2018) performed a retrospective analysis of 113 NobelActive implants placed in 66 patients, classified post-hoc into a high-IT group (>55 Ncm, mean 67.35±4.0 Ncm) and a control group (≤55 Ncm, mean 37.9±12.62 Ncm). Radiographic crestal bone loss was significantly greater in the high-IT group overall (0.95±1.60 mm vs 0.18±0.68 mm; P<0.05). The effect was markedly amplified in mandibular sites, where high-IT implants lost 2.12±1.99 mm vs only 0.25±0.65 mm in the maxilla (P<0.05). All 6 implant failures occurred in the high-IT group, yielding an overall survival rate of 94.6%. The authors conclude that IT exceeding 55 Ncm — the approximate mean torque when clinicians aim for high primary stability with NobelActive in dense bone — produces clinically significant crestal bone remodeling, particularly in the mandible.

## Key Contributions

- Corroborates Marconcini 2018's ≥50 Ncm harm threshold using a **different implant system** (NobelActive) and retrospective real-world data
- Identifies **>55 Ncm as the practical clinical overtorque zone**: mean Group A torque 67.35 Ncm is where NobelActive tends to end up when placed in dense mandibular bone without controlled torque limiting
- Quantifies the **mandible-specific vulnerability**: 2.12 mm MBL vs 0.25 mm in the maxilla at the same IT range — 8× greater harm in dense cortical bone
- All implant failures (n=6) concentrated in the high-IT group — suggests MBL magnitude at these torque levels has clinical consequences beyond the surrogate metric

## Methodology

Retrospective cohort; 113 NobelActive implants, 66 patients. Post-hoc grouping by achieved IT: Group A >55 Ncm (mean 67.35±4.0 Ncm) vs Group B ≤55 Ncm (mean 37.9±12.62 Ncm). Outcome: radiographic crestal bone loss (mm). Site-specific sub-analysis: mandible vs maxilla. Implant survival recorded. Statistical threshold P<0.05.
