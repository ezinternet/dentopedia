## Three-line Summary
Pilot parallel-arm RCT (21 patients, 90 implants: 44 bone-level [BL], 46 tissue-level [TL]; BioHorizons, Univ. of Barcelona) comparing insertion torque value (ITV) and implant stability quotient (ISQ, Penguin RFA, horizontal and vertical) at placement and after 90 days.
No significant ITV difference (BL 36.0 ± 11.5 vs TL 38.0 ± 11.5 Ncm, p = 0.381) or baseline ISQ; at 90 days horizontal ISQ was marginally higher for TL (66.5 ± 20.9 vs 57.1 ± 18.9, p = 0.029); ITV correlated with baseline ISQ (r = 0.493, p < 0.001) but not with 90-day ISQ.
Implant design (BL vs TL) does not govern primary stability, and secondary stability depends on biological healing rather than initial mechanical engagement — a feasibility pilot with no a priori power calculation.

## Summary
This pilot randomized clinical trial compared primary-stability metrics — insertion torque value (ITV) and the implant stability quotient (ISQ, via the Penguin RFA device) measured horizontally and vertically — between bone-level (BL) and tissue-level (TL) implants of identical intraosseous macrogeometry and osteotomy protocol, at placement and at 90 days. Across 21 patients and 90 implants (44 BL, 46 TL) no implants were lost. ITV and baseline ISQ did not differ between BL and TL implants, while mandibular sites showed higher torque than maxillary sites (p = 0.001). At 90 days the TL group showed a marginally higher horizontal ISQ (66.5 vs 57.1, p = 0.029). ITV correlated moderately with baseline ISQ (r = 0.493, p < 0.001) but not with 90-day ISQ, indicating that secondary stability is governed by biological healing rather than initial mechanical engagement. Clinically, implant design can be chosen on prosthetic and soft-tissue grounds without compromising primary stability.

## Key Contributions
- Isolates the design axis (collar/platform and microgap location) by matching intraosseous geometry, threads, surface, and osteotomy between BL and TL implants from one manufacturer.
- Documents no significant BL-vs-TL difference in ITV or baseline ISQ, with only a marginal 90-day horizontal ISQ advantage for TL implants.
- Confirms a moderate ITV-baseline ISQ correlation (r = 0.493) that dissociates from 90-day stability, reinforcing that insertion torque does not predict secondary stability.

## Methodology
Parallel-arm pilot RCT (randomized via Randomizer Numbers app v1.4.0) at the University of Barcelona Dental Hospital (ethics CEIC HOUB 2022-033-1; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07288606). 21 patients (13 M/8 F, age 19–84) received 90 BioHorizons Camlog implants (44 BL, 46 TL): tapered internal hexagon, aggressive buttress threads, laser-lock neck, resorbable blast-textured body; BL 1.5 mm conical internal connection placed flush with crest, TL 45° conical hexagon with 2.3 mm transmucosal neck placed supracrustal; diameters 3.0–4.6 mm, lengths 7.5–12 mm. ITV recorded in Ncm at placement; ISQ (Penguin, MulTi-peg ~5 Ncm) recorded horizontally and vertically at baseline and 90 days. Nonparametric analysis (Shapiro–Wilk, Mann–Whitney U, Spearman correlation, Holm correction, linear mixed-effects models adjusted for clustering); α = 0.05; no a priori power calculation (feasibility trial).

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| ITV BL vs TL | 36.0 ± 11.5 vs 38.0 ± 11.5 Ncm, p = 0.381 (NS) |
| Baseline ISQ-H / ISQ-V BL vs TL | p = 0.928 / p = 0.874 (NS) |
| 90-day ISQ-H BL vs TL | 57.1 ± 18.9 vs 66.5 ± 20.9, p = 0.029 (marginal) |
| 90-day ISQ-V / overall ISQ | p = 0.677 / p = 0.073 (NS) |
| Mandible vs maxilla torque | 40.8 ± 9.3 vs 34.5 ± 12.5 Ncm, p = 0.001 |
| ITV–baseline ISQ correlation | r = 0.493, p < 0.001 |
| ITV–90-day ISQ correlation | not significant |
| Implant survival / complications | 0/90 lost; uneventful |
