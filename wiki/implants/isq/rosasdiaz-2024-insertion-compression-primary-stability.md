## One-line Summary
In vitro study (n=120 implants, polyurethane type II-A bone) demonstrating that under-milling degree (0.2/0.5/0.8mm) dramatically changes ITV (25→63 Ncm) but does not significantly alter ISQ (67.6 vs 65.7, p=0.166), evidencing ITV–ISQ dissociation under compression protocols.

## Summary
Rosas-Díaz et al. (2024) investigated how different under-milling compression protocols (0.2, 0.5, 0.8mm undersized osteotomy) influence primary stability in type II-A bone quality (polyurethane model, 120 implants across 4 diameters and 5 lengths). ITV rose dramatically with greater compression (25.1 Ncm at 0.2mm vs 63.2 Ncm at 0.8mm; p<0.001), but ISQ was not significantly different across protocols (67.6 vs 65.7; p=0.166). Multivariate analysis (MANOVA) showed compression protocol explains 63.9% of combined ITV+ISQ variability, with implant diameter accounting for 27.0% and length for 12.1%. No significant interactions were found. This dissociation between ITV and ISQ challenges the clinical assumption that higher insertion torque reflects proportionally higher resonance-frequency-defined stability in medium-density bone.

## Key Contributions
- Direct in-vitro evidence that ITV and ISQ do NOT track together under compression protocols in type II-A bone
- 0.8mm under-milling yields 2.5× higher ITV than 0.2mm, with no significant ISQ advantage
- MANOVA: compression protocol dominant factor (ηp²=0.639) for combined ITV+ISQ variance
- Implant diameter second most influential (ηp²=0.270 > length 0.121)
- No interaction effects: each variable acts independently on stability outcomes

## Methodology
In-vitro study. n=120 dental implants. Polyurethane blocks equivalent to Lekholm & Zarb type II-A bone (modified Rosas et al. classification). Implant diameters: 3.5, 3.8, 4.5, 5.0mm. Implant lengths: 8.5, 10.0, 11.5, 13.0, 15.0mm. Under-milling protocols: 0.2mm, 0.5mm, 0.8mm undersized osteotomy. Primary stability measured immediately post-placement: ITV (Ncm) and ISQ (Osstell RFA). Statistical: MANOVA with partial eta squared (ηp²); p<0.05.

## Results
**ITV (significant, p<0.001):**
- 0.8mm under-milling: 63.2 ± 14.9 Ncm
- 0.2mm under-milling: 25.1 ± 8.3 Ncm

**ISQ (NOT significant, p=0.166):**
- 0.2mm: 67.6 ± 5.4
- 0.5mm: 65.8 ± 3.4
- 0.8mm: 65.7 ± 4.0

**Variance explained (ηp²):**
- Compression protocol: 0.639 (63.9%)
- Implant diameter: 0.270 (27.0%)
- Implant length: 0.121 (12.1%)
- All interactions: not significant (p>0.05)
