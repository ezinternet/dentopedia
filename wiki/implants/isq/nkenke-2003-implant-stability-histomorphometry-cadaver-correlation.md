## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo human cadaver correlation study (48 stepped-cylinder implants placed in 4 unfixed maxillae and mandibles) correlating primary implant stability with CT-derived bone mineral density (BMD) and histomorphometric parameters of each implant site.

Primary stability correlated with both CT-derived BMD and histomorphometric parameters, grounding the claim that primary stability is a direct read-out of local bone quality.

Key limitation: small donor count (n=4 jaws) and no healing phase, so findings reflect only the mechanical (primary) stability component and cannot address osseointegration outcomes.

## 세줄요약

체외 인체 카데바 연구(고정되지 않은 상·하악 4개 악골에 계단형 실린더 임플란트 48개 식립): CT 유래 골밀도 (Bone Mineral Density, BMD)와 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry) 지표를 임플란트 1차 안정성 (Primary Stability)과 상관 분석.

1차 안정성은 CT 유래 골밀도 및 조직형태계측 지표 모두와 상관을 보여, 1차 안정성이 국소 골질의 직접적 반영임을 형태학적으로 뒷받침.

주요 한계: 공여자 수 적음(악골 4개)·치유 단계 없음 — 기계적(1차) 안정성만 반영하며 골유착 결과를 직접 평가할 수 없음.

## Summary
This foundational ex vivo study establishes the biological substrate of primary stability. Using 48 stepped-cylinder implants placed in four unfixed human maxillae and mandibles, it correlated measured primary stability with CT-derived bone mineral density and histomorphometric parameters of each site. The result grounds the now-standard claim that primary stability is a read-out of local bone quality — the mechanistic anchor beneath modern insertion-torque and ISQ correlation studies. Limitations are the small donor count and absence of any healing phase.

## Key Contributions
- Direct correlation of primary stability with both BMD and histomorphometry in human bone.
- CT-based objective site characterization.
- Historical/mechanical anchor for stability–bone-quality reasoning.

## Methodology
Ex vivo human cadaver study; 4 unfixed maxillae/mandibles; 48 stepped cylinder screw implants; BMD by CT; histomorphometric parameters; correlation with primary stability.
