## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-surgeon, single-implant-system study of 329 SICace implants in 177 patients used multivariate linear regression to identify which of 11 candidate factors significantly influence ISQ at T1 (immediately post-placement) and T2 (pre-restoration).

Seven factors were significant at T1 (sex, maxillary/mandibular location, immediate/delayed implantation, bone grafting, implant diameter, I-/II-stage healing, insertion torque; standardized coefficients 0.111–0.286), while only three factors remained significant at T2 (implant diameter β=0.414, insertion torque β=0.150, T1–T2 interval β=0.191); implant diameter and insertion torque were the only two factors significant at both time points.

Provided regression equations to estimate ISQ from clinical parameters, but the authors explicitly flagged the model as likely surgeon-, system-, and clinic-specific — a caveat later confirmed by the authors' own 2017 multi-surgeon follow-up.

## Summary

This retrospective study by Huang et al. (ACTA Amsterdam / Best & Easy Dental Clinic, Hangzhou) analyzed 329 SICace implants placed in 177 patients by a single experienced surgeon between 2012 and 2015, using resonance frequency analysis (RFA) to record ISQ at T1 (immediately after placement) and T2 (immediately before restoration). Multivariate linear regression across 11 candidate factors identified 7 significant predictors of T1 ISQ (sex, maxillary/mandibular location, immediate/delayed implantation, bone grafting, implant diameter, I-/II-stage healing pattern, insertion torque) but only 3 significant predictors of T2 ISQ (implant diameter, insertion torque, T1–T2 time interval). Implant diameter and insertion torque were the only factors significant at both timepoints, while implant length and bone type were not significant at either. The study produced closed-form regression equations for T1 and T2 ISQ estimation but explicitly cautioned that the model may be specific to this surgeon, implant system, and clinic — a limitation borne out by the authors' 2017 multi-surgeon, multi-system follow-up study.

## Key Contributions

- First multivariate linear regression model quantifying weight coefficients (not just significance) of clinical factors on ISQ at both T1 and T2
- Demonstrates that T1 and T2 ISQ are governed by largely non-overlapping factor sets, with only implant diameter and insertion torque significant throughout
- Bone type (Lekholm & Zarb) and implant length were not significant predictors at either timepoint in this cohort
- Immediate implantation produced significantly lower T1 ISQ than delayed (73.68±6.50 vs 75.82±5.49) but no significant T2 difference, supporting that immediate placement does not compromise secondary stability with careful case selection

## Methodology

Single-surgeon, single-implant-system (SICace) retrospective cohort, 329 implants / 177 patients, Best & Easy Dental Clinic, Hangzhou, China (2012–2015). ISQ measured with Osstell Mentor (mean of 4 sites) at T1 and T2. 11 candidate factors (sex, age, jaw location, immediate/delayed placement, bone grafting, implant diameter, implant length, I-/II-stage healing, insertion torque, bone type, T1–T2 interval) entered into multivariate linear regression (SPSS 21.0, p<0.05, 95% CI); Kruskal–Wallis and paired t-tests used for supplementary site/timepoint comparisons.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| T1 significant factors (β) | sex 0.111, maxillary/mandibular 0.121, immediate/delayed 0.148, bone grafting −0.235, implant diameter 0.119, I-/II-stage 0.241, insertion torque 0.286 |
| T2 significant factors (β) | implant diameter 0.414, insertion torque 0.150, T1–T2 interval 0.191 |
| Factors significant at both T1 & T2 | implant diameter, insertion torque |
| Factors not significant at either | implant length, bone type (Lekholm & Zarb), age |
| Mean ISQ | T1 74.34±6.75; T2 77.00±4.89 |
| Immediate vs delayed T1 ISQ | 73.68±6.50 vs 75.82±5.49 (p=0.038) |
| Immediate vs delayed T2 ISQ | 77.00±4.30 vs 77.63±4.07 (p=0.334, NS) |
| Implant failure rate | 0.6% (2/331) |
