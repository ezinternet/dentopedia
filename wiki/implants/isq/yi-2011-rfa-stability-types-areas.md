## One-line Summary
Retrospective study (n=206, 131 patients, Korea) found mandible > maxilla ISQ (p<0.001), 4.8mm > 4.1mm for SLA implants (p<0.001), and SLA (Straumann) > RBM (Osstem) at 3 months, significantly at posterior mandible (p=0.045) and premolar maxilla (p=0.032).

## 한줄요약
한국 후향적 연구 (206개, 131명): 3개월 ISQ = 하악 > 상악, 직경 4.8 > 4.1 (SLA 한정), SLA (Straumann) > RBM (Osstem) — 특히 하악 구치부(p=0.045)·상악 소구치부(p=0.032).

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study from Korea (206 implants, 131 patients) comparing ISQ at 3 months by jaw, diameter and surface (Straumann SLA vs Osstem RBM).

ISQ was higher in the mandible than the maxilla (p<0.001), higher with 4.8 mm than 4.1 mm SLA implants (p<0.001), and higher with SLA than RBM at the posterior mandible (p=0.045) and premolar maxilla (p=0.032).

Site and diameter matter for early stability, but surface cannot be isolated because the two systems also differ in macro-geometry; single time point, retrospective, and detailed tables were only partly extractable from the Korean-language text.

## 세줄요약

악골 부위, 직경, 표면 (Straumann SLA 대 Osstem RBM)에 따른 3개월 ISQ를 비교한 한국 후향적 연구 (임플란트 206개, 131명)다.

ISQ는 하악이 상악보다 (p<0.001), SLA 4.8 mm가 4.1 mm보다 (p<0.001) 높았고, SLA가 RBM보다 하악 구치부 (p=0.045)와 상악 소구치부 (p=0.032)에서 높았다.

부위와 직경은 초기 안정성에 영향을 주지만 두 시스템은 매크로 형태도 달라 표면 효과만 분리할 수 없다; 단일 시점의 후향적 연구이며 한국어 본문이라 상세 표는 일부만 추출되었다.

## Summary
Single-clinic retrospective analysis of 206 implants in 131 Korean patients measured ISQ at 3 months (abutment connection) using Osstell Mentor. Two implant systems were compared: Straumann SLA™ and Osstem SSǎ™ (RBM surface). Mandibular implants consistently outperformed maxillary implants. Within the SLA group, larger-diameter (4.8mm) implants showed significantly higher ISQ than 4.1mm implants. SLA surface showed higher ISQ than RBM (SSǎ) specifically at posterior mandible and premolar maxilla regions; differences at other sites were not statistically significant. This study provides Korean clinical benchmarks for ISQ at the secondary stability phase, though it cannot isolate surface effects from macro-geometry differences between the two systems.

## Key Contributions
- Provides ISQ benchmarks from a Korean clinical setting comparing two widely used implant systems (Straumann SLA vs Osstem RBM) at 3-month healing
- Confirms jaw position as the strongest predictor of ISQ — mandible consistently higher regardless of surface or diameter
- Demonstrates diameter effect in SLA group: 4.8mm implants show meaningfully higher ISQ than 4.1mm, consistent with greater bone-implant contact area
- Identifies regional specificity: SLA–RBM difference is statistically detectable only at posterior mandible and premolar maxilla, not globally

## Methodology
- **Design**: Retrospective cross-sectional; single clinic (Apple Tree Dental Clinic, Bucheon); collaborative analysis with Seoul National University
- **Population**: n=206 implants, 131 patients; placement by single experienced surgeon (10 years experience), per-manufacturer protocol
- **ISQ measurement**: Osstell Mentor, 3 months post-fixture (before abutment connection), buccal + lingual, average
- **Factors analyzed**: Implant type (SLA vs SSǎ/RBM), surgery area (upper/lower, anterior/posterior), diameter (4.1 vs 4.8mm), systemic disease, smoking
- **Statistics**: SPSS (SPSS Inc., Chicago IL); p<0.05 threshold

## Results
| Factor | Finding | p-value |
|---|---|---|
| Mandible vs maxilla | Mandible significantly higher ISQ | <0.001 |
| Diameter 4.8 vs 4.1 (SLA only) | 4.8mm higher ISQ | <0.001 |
| SLA vs SSǎ — posterior mandible | SLA > SSǎ | 0.045 |
| SLA vs SSǎ — premolar maxilla | SLA > SSǎ | 0.032 |
| SLA vs SSǎ — other regions | No significant difference | NS |
