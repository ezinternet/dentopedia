## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study (n=235 implants, 93 patients, University of Granada) using multivariate regression (SUDAAN, clustered sampling) to identify predictors of Osstell ISQ at 12 weeks post-placement.

No patient variable predicted 12-week ISQ; narrow implant diameter (P<.001) and maxillary location (P=.006) predicted worse ISQ; use of plasma rich in growth factors (PRGF) predicted the best 12-week ISQ (P=.011).

Wider-diameter, mandibular implants humidified with PRGF at placement show the highest secondary stability at 12 weeks; abstract-only source (full text paywalled) limits recoverable effect sizes/model detail.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상 연구(n=235 임플란트, 93명, 그라나다 대학): 다변량 회귀(SUDAAN, 군집 표본)로 식립 12주 후 Osstell 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)의 예측 인자를 분석.

환자 변수는 12주 ISQ와 무관; 좁은 임플란트 직경(P<.001)·상악 위치(P=.006)는 낮은 ISQ와 연관; 성장인자 농축 혈장(Plasma Rich in Growth Factors, PRGF) 사용은 가장 높은 12주 ISQ와 연관(P=.011).

넓은 직경·하악 위치·PRGF 습윤 처리 임플란트가 12주 이차 안정성이 가장 높음; 초록만 확보되어(전문 유료화) 세부 효과크기·모델 정보는 제한적.

## Summary

This prospective clinical study from the University of Granada (n=235 implants, 93 patients) modeled predictors of dental implant secondary stability — Osstell resonance-frequency ISQ measured 12 weeks after placement — using multivariate regression with SUDAAN software to correctly account for clustered sampling (multiple implants per patient). Predictors were grouped into patient variables, implant variables (including diameter and maxillary vs mandibular localization), operative/surgical-technique variables (including intraoperative use of plasma rich in growth factors, PRGF), and baseline ISQ. No patient-level variable (age, sex) was associated with 12-week ISQ. Among implant variables, narrow diameter and maxillary localization independently predicted worse 12-week ISQ. Among surgical-technique variables, PRGF use was the standout predictor of better 12-week ISQ. The authors conclude that implant diameter, anatomical location, and PRGF humidification of the implant surface all influence secondary stability at 12 weeks of healing — stability was lower with narrower implants and maxillary placement, and higher when PRGF was used. Only the published abstract was available for this summary; exact effect sizes, the full adjusted regression model, and the PRGF application protocol are behind the publisher paywall.

## Key Contributions

- Narrow implant diameter independently predicts worse 12-week ISQ (P<.001) in a 235-implant multivariate cohort.
- Maxillary localization independently predicts worse 12-week ISQ (P=.006) vs mandible, consistent with lower maxillary cortical density.
- Intraoperative PRGF use is the strongest surgical-technique predictor of better 12-week ISQ (P=.011) — an early clinical signal for a platelet-concentrate adjunct improving secondary (not just primary) stability.
- No patient-level factor (age, sex) was associated with 12-week ISQ in this cohort.

## Methodology

Prospective cohort, 235 implants in 93 patients (47 men, 46 women; mean age 52.13 ± 11.23 years, range 27–76) at the University of Granada Oral Surgery and Implantology Clinic. Predictor variables spanned patient, implant (diameter, localization), operative (including PRGF), and baseline-ISQ domains. Outcome: Osstell ISQ at 12 weeks post-placement. Multivariate regression with SUDAAN software to adjust for clustered sampling (multiple implants per patient). Full model specification and PRGF application protocol are not recoverable from the abstract alone.

## Results

| Variable | Effect on 12-week ISQ | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Patient variables (age, sex) | No association | NS |
| Narrow implant diameter | Worse ISQ | P<.001 |
| Maxillary localization | Worse ISQ (vs mandible) | P=.006 |
| PRGF use (surgical technique) | Best ISQ | P=.011 |
