## Three-line Summary

Animal study (1 beagle, 6 implants, 5 trained examiners) compared inter-rater reliability via ICC for five implant stability methods: ISQ (Osstell Mentor), ISV (EasyCheck), radiographic MBL, BIC, and ITBD.

Histometric parameters yielded highest ICC (ITBD 0.854, BIC 0.806); ISQ was moderate-high (0.774); radiograph moderate (0.638); ISV was low (0.447); all differences statistically significant (p<0.05).

For clinical non-invasive assessment, ISQ is the most reproducible cross-examiner tool; ISV (EasyCheck) should not be used interchangeably between clinicians given its low inter-rater agreement.

## 세줄요약

1마리 비글견(임플란트 6개)에 5명의 훈련된 검사자가 ISQ·ISV·방사선·BIC·ITBD를 측정해 검사자 간 신뢰도(ICC)를 비교한 동물 연구다.

조직계측 변수가 최고 신뢰도(ITBD ICC=0.854, BIC ICC=0.806), ISQ 중등도-높음(0.774), 방사선 중등도(0.638), ISV 낮음(0.447)으로 모두 유의하였다(p<0.05).

비침습적 임상 도구 중 ISQ(Osstell)는 허용 가능한 검사자 간 재현성을 가지나, ISV(EasyCheck)는 검사자 간 신뢰도가 낮아 검사자가 바뀌면 비교 불가능하다.

## Summary

Gu et al. (2025) conducted an animal study to systematically compare the inter-rater reliability of five implant stability measurement methods. One beagle dog received six standard titanium implants, and five trained junior clinicians each independently performed all five measurement methods at 8 weeks post-implantation. Intraclass correlation coefficients (ICC) were calculated using a two-way random-effects model.

The reliability hierarchy was: ITBD (0.854) > BIC (0.806) > ISQ (0.774) > Radiograph (0.638) > ISV (0.447). All values were statistically significant (p<0.05), but only ISV fell below the conventional 0.75 threshold for "good" reliability.

## Key Contributions

- Establishes an **inter-rater reliability hierarchy** across five stability measurement methods in a single controlled model
- Confirms ISQ (Osstell Mentor) as the most reproducible non-invasive clinical tool (ICC=0.774)
- Identifies **ISV (EasyCheck) as poorly reproducible** across examiners (ICC=0.447) — a critical finding for multi-examiner clinical trials using this device
- Histomorphometric parameters (BIC, ITBD) are gold-standard for reproducibility but require sacrifice — not applicable in living patients
- Provides empirical ICC benchmarks directly applicable to study design and clinical protocol standardization

## Methodology

- **Animal model**: 1 beagle dog, 6 implants placed in healed mandibular ridges
- **Examiners**: 5 junior dental clinicians, trained via standardized protocol
- **Methods tested**:
  - ISQ: Osstell Mentor (RFA, electromagnetic resonance frequency analysis)
  - ISV: EasyCheck (alternative RFA-based device)
  - Radiographic MBL: standardized periapical radiographs
  - BIC (골-임플란트 접촉률, Bone-to-Implant Contact): histomorphometry
  - ITBD (나사산간 골밀도, Inter-Thread Bone Density): histomorphometry
- **Statistics**: ICC (two-way random-effects model, 95% CI), Shapiro-Wilk normality test
- **Sacrifice/histology**: 8 weeks post-implantation

## Results

| Method | Device | ICC | Reliability |
|---|---|---|---|
| ITBD | Histomorphometry | 0.854 | Very high |
| BIC | Histomorphometry | 0.806 | High |
| ISQ | Osstell Mentor | 0.774 | Moderate-high |
| Radiograph | Periapical X-ray | 0.638 | Moderate |
| ISV | EasyCheck | 0.447 | Low |

- All ICC values p<0.05
- ISV is the only method below the 0.75 "good" threshold
- For in vivo clinical use, ISQ is the most reliable non-invasive option
