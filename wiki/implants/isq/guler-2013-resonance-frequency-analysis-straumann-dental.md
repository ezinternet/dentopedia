## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study of 208 Straumann implants (164 standard SLA + 44 SLActive) across all four jaw regions, ISQ measured with the Osstell mentor at placement and during the healing period.

ISQ increased significantly during healing; posterior maxilla had the lowest ISQ (except at initial measurement); implant length had no significant influence (P > .05), while at the second measurement 4.8 mm diameter and male sex were significantly higher, and SLActive was transiently higher than SLA (P = 0) but equal at final measurement.

Repeated ISQ measurement of a specific implant has some diagnostic benefit — healing-period stability is best judged over time and by region, not by a single reading.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상연구: 모든 악궁 부위에 식립된 Straumann 임플란트 208개(표준 SLA 164개 + SLActive 44개)를 대상으로 Osstell mentor로 식립 시와 치유 기간 중 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)를 측정.

치유 중 ISQ가 유의하게 증가; 후상악이 최저(초회 측정 제외); 임플란트 길이는 유의 영향 없음(P > .05), 두 번째 측정에서 남성·4.8mm 직경이 유의하게 높았고(SLActive도 일시적으로 유의 높음, P = 0) 최종 측정에선 표면 간·4.8 vs 4.1mm 간 차이 없음.

반복 ISQ 측정이 진단적 이득을 가지며 치유 중 안정성은 단회 측정이 아니라 시간·부위 맥락으로 판단해야 함.

## Summary

This prospective clinical study measured implant stability quotient (ISQ) values with the Osstell mentor in 208 Straumann dental implants — 164 standard SLA and 44 SLActive — in all four jaw regions, at placement and across the healing period. ISQ increased significantly over healing. The posterior maxilla showed the lowest ISQ values at every timepoint except the initial measurement, with no significant difference among the anterior mandible, posterior mandible, and anterior maxilla. Implant length had no significant influence on ISQ, whereas implant diameter, sex, and (transiently) surface type did: 4.8 mm diameter and male sex were significantly higher at the second measurement, and SLActive exceeded SLA at the second measurement only to equalize by the final one. The authors conclude that repeated ISQ measurements of a specific implant carry some diagnostic benefit — net healing-gain and regional profile matter more than a single reading.

## Key Contributions

- Large single-cohort healing-period ISQ trajectory (208 implants; 164 SLA + 44 SLActive) spanning all four jaw regions.
- Factor profile: posterior maxilla lowest (post-initial), implant length null, 4.8 vs 4.1 vs 3.3 mm diameter gradient, male > female at second measurement, transient SLActive advantage.
- Practical conclusion that serial ISQ measurement on the same implant has diagnostic benefit during osseointegration.

## Methodology

Prospective clinical study (abstract-level detail only — no patient count, absolute ISQ magnitudes, exact measurement intervals, or statistical model stated in the abstract). n = 208 Straumann implants (164 standard SLA, 44 SLActive) across all jaw regions. ISQ measured with the Osstell mentor at implant placement and during the healing period. Factors analyzed: jaw region, implant length, implant diameter, sex, surface type (SLA vs SLActive).

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| ISQ over healing | significant increase across the healing period |
| Region | posterior maxilla lowest at all measurements except the initial; no significant difference among anterior mandible, posterior mandible, anterior maxilla |
| Implant length | no significant influence on ISQ (P > .05) |
| Sex | second measurement significantly higher in men than women (P < .05) |
| Diameter | second measurement significantly highest at 4.8 mm; final: 4.8 ≈ 4.1 mm, both significantly higher than 3.3 mm (P < .05) |
| SLA vs SLActive | insertion: no difference; second measurement: SLActive significantly higher (P = 0 as printed in abstract); final: no difference |
| Clinical conclusion | repeated ISQ measurements of a specific implant have some diagnostic benefit |
