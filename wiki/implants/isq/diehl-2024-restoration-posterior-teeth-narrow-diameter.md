## Three-line Summary

4-year follow-up case-control study (n=26; 11 T2DM [HbA1c >6.5%, mean 7.35±0.71%] vs 15 normoglycemic [HbA1c <6.0%]) of 3.3mm tissue-level TiZr (Roxolid/SLActive) narrow-diameter implants (NDIs) in posterior maxilla/mandible; Witten/Herdecke University, Germany; 26 of original 32 patients reached 48-month follow-up.

100% implant survival in both groups; all peri-implant parameters (PD, CAL, BOP, PBI, recession) and radiographic ΔMBL (T2DM 0.09±0.89mm vs control 0.15±0.83mm; p=0.875) were statistically non-significant; no technical complications; MBL stable from 12 months to 48 months.

Tissue-level 3.3mm TiZr NDIs deliver equivalent peri-implant biological outcomes in uncontrolled T2DM (HbA1c >6.5%) and healthy patients over 4 years, supporting their use as a minimal-invasive augmentation-avoiding solution in posterior zones with reduced ridge width.

## 세줄요약

4년 추적 케이스-컨트롤 연구 (n=26; T2DM군 11명 HbA1c >6.5%, 정상혈당군 15명 HbA1c <6.0%) — 3.3mm 조직수준 티타늄-지르코늄 (TiZr, Roxolid/SLActive) 협경임플란트 (Narrow-Diameter Implant, NDI), 후방부 배치; 독일 Witten/Herdecke 대학; 원래 32명 중 26명 48개월 완료.

48개월 생존율 100%; 탐침깊이 (PD), 부착수준 (CAL), 탐침출혈 (BOP), 유두출혈지수 (PBI), 치은퇴축 모두 비유의; 변연골소실 (ΔMBL) T2DM 0.09±0.89mm vs 대조군 0.15±0.83mm (p=0.875, 비유의); 기술적 합병증 없음.

3.3mm 조직수준 TiZr 협경임플란트는 불량 혈당조절 당뇨 (HbA1c >6.5%) 환자에서 4년간 정상혈당군과 동등한 임플란트주위 생물학적 반응을 보여, 후방부 수평골 폭 부족 시 수술적 증대술 회피를 위한 예측 가능한 최소침습 옵션으로 지지된다.

## Summary

This 4-year follow-up of the original Diehl et al. case-control study reports on 26 surviving patients (11 T2DM, 15 normoglycemic) of the original 32-patient cohort. All patients had received one 3.3mm tissue-level TiZr NDI (Roxolid, SLActive, Straumann) in the posterior maxilla or mandible. T2DM patients were defined by HbA1c >6.5% (mean 7.35% at follow-up); controls had HbA1c ≤6.0%. At 48 months, overall survival was 100% for both groups. Neither marginal bone loss (ΔMBL 0.09 vs 0.15mm, p=0.875) nor any clinical parameter (PD, CAL, BOP, PBI, recession) differed significantly between groups. MBL showed no further progression from the 12-month baseline, indicating a stable crestal bone plateau. The tissue-level connection design may contribute to this stability through its supracrestal placement.

## Key Contributions

- Extends Diehl 2022 (2-year) to 4-year follow-up for same NDI cohort
- Confirms 100% survival and non-significant MBL differences maintained at 48 months
- Demonstrates MBL stability plateau from 12 months to 48 months — no progressive bone loss
- Highlights T2DM-related dropout bias: 5/16 T2DM patients dropped vs 1/16 controls, raising mean HbA1c of completers
- Contextualises tissue-level connection design as potentially protective for crestal bone stability

## Methodology

- **Design**: 4-year follow-up analysis nested within prospective case-control study (NCT04630691)
- **N**: 26 patients (11 T2DM, 15 normoglycemic); 29 implants total (13 T2DM, 16 control)
- **Implant**: 3.3mm tissue-level TiZr (Roxolid®, SLActive®, Straumann); lengths 8–12mm; posterior maxilla/mandible; single crown or FPD
- **T2DM group**: HbA1c >6.5% (mean 7.35±0.71% at 4yr), uncontrolled T2DM
- **Control group**: HbA1c ≤6.0%, non-diabetic
- **Primary outcome**: Radiographic ΔMBL baseline → 48 months (ImageJ2, parallel-technique periapicals)
- **Secondary**: PD, CAL, BOP, PBI, recession at T1 (24mo) and T2 (48mo)
- **Statistics**: Student's t-test; α=0.05; Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality
