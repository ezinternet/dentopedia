## One-line Summary
Cross-sectional study (n=60 implants, 18 patients) demonstrating ICC=0.96 for Osstell ISQ with SmartPeg Types I and II, confirming excellent repeatability and reproducibility in a clinical setting.

## 한줄요약
단면 연구(n=60, 18명): SmartPeg I·II 모두 ICC=0.96 — Osstell 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ) 임상 반복성·재현성 우수 확인.

## Summary
Parmar et al. (2024) assessed the repeatability and reproducibility of the Osstell ISQ system using two SmartPeg types (I and II) across 60 implants in 18 patients. Each implant received 3 sequential measurements per SmartPeg (6 total). Mean ISQ values were ~71 for both SmartPeg types with minimal between-measurement drift. Intraclass correlation coefficients reached 0.96 for both SmartPeg types, meeting the threshold for excellent reliability. The authors conclude that the Osstell ISQ system demonstrates near-excellent repeatability and reproducibility for clinical use.

## Key Contributions
- ICC=0.96 for both SmartPeg Type I and Type II in a real clinical (not lab) setting
- Mean ISQ stability: ~71 across all 3 sequential measurements per SmartPeg
- Equivalent or <3 ISQ variability in 46.3% (Type I) and 58.6% (Type II) of cases
- Small residual inter-measurement drift (<3 ISQ) acceptable for clinical loading decisions
- Adds clinical validity to controlled in-vitro ICC data from earlier bench studies

## Methodology
Cross-sectional study; 18 patients, 60 implants; Osstell ISQ device with SmartPeg Types I and II; 3 consecutive measurements per SmartPeg per implant (6 total per implant); ICC calculated for repeatability and reproducibility. Implant type, site, bone quality, and loading stage not described.

## Results
- SmartPeg I: ISQ = 71.36 / 71.31 / 71.65 (measurements 1–3); ICC = 0.96
- SmartPeg II: ISQ = 71.02 / 71.58 / 71.76 (measurements 1–3); ICC = 0.96
- Equivalent values or <3 ISQ difference: 46.3% (Type I), 58.6% (Type II)
- Repeatability coefficient: 0.96 (both); reproducibility: 0.96 (both)
