## Three-line Summary
Analytical observational study validating a new PITV (Progressive Insertion Torque Value)-based 3-type primary stability classification (High ≥50 Ncm / Moderate 30–<50 Ncm / Low <30 Ncm with curve-trajectory subtypes) across 1,250 torque interpretations by 25 calibrated dentists.
Reliability was almost-perfect (intraexaminer κ=0.82–0.95; interexaminer κ=0.81–0.89; overall κ=0.84, p<0.001) but predictive discrimination was only moderate (AUC=0.69, 95% CI 0.56–0.81); clinical outcome linkage (ISQ, bone loss, survival) not yet validated.
Provides a reproducible, standardized framework for reading the torque curve as a clinical decision input for loading protocols — but AUC=0.69 marks it as supportive rather than definitive until outcome validation follows.

## Summary
Rosas-Díaz et al. (same Peruvian group as the 2024 in vitro study) developed and validated a classification system for primary implant stability based on the Progressive Insertion Torque Value (PITV) curve shape and final torque magnitude. The system classifies implants into Type I (High, ≥50 Ncm), Type II (Moderate, 30–<50 Ncm), and Type III (Low, <30 Ncm), with additional subtypes based on torque-curve trajectory (4 subtypes for Types I-II, 6 subtypes for Type III). Twenty-five dentists (general and specialists) were trained and assessed 1,250 torque-curve interpretations. The system showed almost-perfect intra- and inter-examiner agreement (k=0.82–0.95 intraexaminer; k=0.81–0.89 interexaminer; overall k=0.84). However, predictive validity (AUC=0.69) was only moderate, suggesting the classification is reproducible but has limited ability to predict clinical outcomes.

## Key Contributions
- New PITV classification: 3 types by final torque, 10 subtypes total by curve trajectory
- Near-perfect reliability across training levels (general dentists and specialists)
- Identifies that specialists have higher intraexaminer agreement (k=0.95) than general dentists (k=0.82)
- Provides a standardized framework for torque-based loading protocol decisions
- Moderate predictive validity — reproducibility is confirmed, but outcome validity needs clinical study

## Methodology
- n=1,250 implant torque-curve interpretations; 25 dentists trained in standardized protocol
- Classification: Type I ≥50 Ncm (4 subtypes A–D), Type II 30–<50 Ncm (4 subtypes A–D), Type III <30 Ncm (6 subtypes A–F) based on curve trajectory
- Agreement: Cohen's κ (intraexaminer), Fleiss' κ (interexaminer), PABAK
- Predictive validity: ROC/AUC, calibration intercept/slope, Brier score

## Results
- Intraexaminer: general dentists k=0.82, specialists k=0.95
- Interexaminer: general dentists k=0.81, specialists k=0.89
- Overall k=0.84 (p<0.001)
- AUC=0.69 (95%CI: 0.56–0.81) — moderate predictive discrimination
- Same classification thresholds as established clinical practice (30/50 Ncm cutoffs)
