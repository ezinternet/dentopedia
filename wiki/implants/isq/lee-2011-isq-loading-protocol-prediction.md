## Three-line Summary

Prospective 5-month clinical study (Asan Medical Center) tracking ISQ (Osstell) at implant placement and monthly to establish correlations between insertion torque, bone density, and stability trajectory.

ISQ correlated significantly with insertion torque and bone density type (D1/D2 > D3/D4); ISQ ≥65 at placement predicted eligibility for early or immediate loading protocols.

ISQ trajectory followed the expected stability dip at 3–4 weeks with recovery by 8–12 weeks, confirming the primary-to-secondary stability transition as a universal healing pattern.

## 세줄요약

전향적 5개월 임상 연구(아산의료원): 식립 시 및 매월 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ; Osstell) 추적으로 삽입토크·골밀도와 ISQ의 상관관계 및 안정성 변화 패턴 분석.

ISQ는 삽입토크 및 골밀도 유형과 유의한 양의 상관; 골질 D1/D2 > D3/D4 순으로 높은 ISQ 확인; 식립 시 ISQ ≥65가 조기/즉시부하 프로토콜 선택의 예측 지표임을 확인.

ISQ 변화 곡선은 3–4주 안정성 감소(Stability Dip) 후 8–12주 회복의 전형적인 1차→2차 안정성 전환 패턴을 보여 보편적 치유 패턴임을 재확인.

## Summary
Prospective 5-month clinical study investigating correlations between ISQ values (Osstell) and implant insertion variables (torque, bone density) as predictors for loading protocol selection. Demonstrates significant correlations between insertion torque and ISQ, and between bone density (type) and ISQ. ISQ ≥65 at placement emerges as a threshold for early/immediate loading protocols.

## Key Findings
- Significant positive correlation between insertion torque and ISQ at placement
- Bone density type significantly affects ISQ: D1/D2 > D3/D4
- ISQ ≥65 at placement supports early/immediate loading
- ISQ trajectory monitored monthly: typical stability dip at 3–4 weeks, recovery by 8–12 weeks
