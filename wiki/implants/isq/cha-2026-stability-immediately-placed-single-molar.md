## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center study (45 patients, 52 implants) of immediate flapless single-molar placement comparing Osstell ISQ (RFA) versus AnyCheck (IST/DCA) stability at implant placement and prosthetic impression.
Both values rose significantly (ISQ 76.0±6.8→81.8±5.2, IST 77.6±6.0→82.5±3.7; both p<.001; Cohen's d 1.09 and 0.75); categorical agreement at the ≥75 cutoff improved 69.2%→92.3% and Bland–Altman bias was −1.54/−0.65 (n.s.).
Devices were clinically comparable once osseointegration was established but not fully interchangeable (LoA still ≈±10 at impression); the authors advise using the same device for longitudinal monitoring of an individual implant.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일기관 연구(45명, 52개 임플란트): 발치 즉시 플랩리스 단일 구치부 식립에서 공명주파수분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) 기반 Osstell 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)와 감쇠능 분석 (Damping Capacity Analysis, DCA) 기반 AnyCheck 임플란트 안정성 검사 (Implant Stability Test, IST)를 식립 시와 인상 채득 시 비교했다.

## Summary

This retrospective single-center study (Inje University Sanggye Paik Hospital, 2020–2022) compared two non-invasive implant stability devices in 45 patients receiving 52 immediately placed, flapless single molar implants: Osstell ISQ (resonance frequency analysis, RFA) and AnyCheck (damping capacity analysis, DCA, yielding the IST value). Stability was measured on the day of placement and again at prosthetic impression (mean interval 84.3±29.4 days). Both ISQ and IST increased significantly over time with large effect sizes, and their agreement improved as osseointegration progressed — categorical agreement at the ≥75 cutoff rose from 69.2% to 92.3% and Bland–Altman limits of agreement narrowed from ≈28 to ≈16 units. All 52 implants succeeded clinically over a mean 42.0±17.7 months of follow-up. The authors conclude that once osseointegration is established the devices give clinically comparable measurements but are not fully interchangeable, and recommend using the same device consistently when monitoring an individual implant longitudinally.

## Key Contributions

- Clinical head-to-head of RFA (Osstell ISQ) and DCA (AnyCheck IST) specifically in immediately placed single molar implants with a flapless technique, at both placement and prosthetic impression.
- Both devices tracked the same upward stability trajectory with large effect sizes (Cohen's d 1.09 ISQ, 0.75 IST) and converging variability (SD fell 24% for ISQ, 38% for IST).
- Inter-device agreement improved with osseointegration (69.2%→92.3% categorical agreement at ≥75; bias −1.54→−0.65, n.s.; LoA ≈28→≈16 units).
- Explicit conclusion that the devices are comparable but NOT interchangeable once osseointegration is established, with a same-device-for-longitudinal-monitoring recommendation.
- Practical advantage of AnyCheck in flapless immediate placement: percussion measurement with the healing abutment in place, avoiding repeated SmartPeg removal/re-attachment.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective, single-center chart review (2020–2022); IRB No. 2025-12-005, waived consent.
- **Population**: 45 patients (18 male, 27 female; mean age 69.1 years), 52 implants (24 maxillary, 28 mandibular); extraction indications — periodontal disease 18, caries 14, residual roots 9, fracture 11.
- **Implants**: Osstem TS III SA (31), SS III SA (3), TS III SOI (2), Dentium SimpleLine (13), Dentis SQ (3); diameters 4.5–6.0 mm (5.0 mm in 41/52), lengths 7.0–10.0 mm; bone grafting at 33/52 sites.
- **Measurements**: Osstell ISQ — SmartPeg, 4-direction mean; AnyCheck IST — single buccal percussion, 6 automated taps; ISQ first then IST, by one experienced surgeon. Placement and prosthetic impression (84.3±29.4 days apart); impression timing set by surgeon judgment (bone condition + insertion torque), not by ISQ/IST.
- **Statistics**: paired t-test + Cohen's d, Passing–Bablok regression + Pearson r, Bland–Altman bias/95% LoA, categorical agreement (≥75 primary; ≥70 sensitivity) with percent agreement and Cohen's κ (Landis & Koch); SPSS 25.0 / R 4.5.2.

## Results

All 52 implants were clinically successful with no failures or complications (mean post-restoration follow-up 42.0±17.7 months); no missing stability data.
