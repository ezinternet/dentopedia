## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth in vivo study (30 patients, 60 implants) directly comparing tapered vs cylindrical implants for ISQ and peri-implant tissue outcomes at T0 (placement), 3 months, and 6 months.

Tapered implants showed significantly higher ISQ at all three timepoints (p<0.05), plus lower postoperative pain VAS, less bleeding on probing, and shallower probing depths compared to cylindrical implants.

Both designs achieved 100% survival, but tapered geometry was superior for ISQ-based loading eligibility and peri-implant tissue health; results conflict with kim-2009, which found bone quality — not implant system/design — drives the ISQ trajectory.

두 군 모두 생존율 100%이나, ISQ 기반 부하 적합성과 임플란트 주위 조직 건강에서 테이퍼형 우세; 골질이 임플란트 시스템보다 ISQ 변화 궤적을 결정한다는 kim-2009와 결과 상충.

## Summary
Split-mouth in vivo study comparing tapered vs cylindrical implants in 30 patients (60 implants total). At all timepoints (T0, 3mo, 6mo), tapered implants showed significantly higher ISQ, lower pain VAS, and better peri-implant tissue health. 100% survival in both groups.

## Results

| Parameter | Tapered | Cylindrical | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISQ (all timepoints) | Higher | Lower | p<0.05 |
| Pain VAS | Lower | Higher | p<0.05 |
| BOP | Less | More | p<0.05 |
| Probing depth | Shallower | Deeper | p<0.05 |
| Survival | 100% | 100% | NS |

## Clinical Takeaway
- Tapered implant design provides superior initial and secondary stability (ISQ) compared to cylindrical
- Additional benefits: less postoperative pain and better peri-implant tissue response
- Both designs are survivable, but tapered is preferred for ISQ-based immediate/early loading protocols
