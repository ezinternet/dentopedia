## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study (48 patients, 66 Zimmer Tapered Screw-Vent 4.5 mm implants, non-submerged healing) comparing marginal bone levels of implants placed at maximum insertion torque (MIT) >70 Ncm (n=42; mean 110.6, range 70.8–176 Ncm) versus 30–50 Ncm (n=9; mean 37.1 Ncm).

All implants were clinically stable after 2–3 months; mean marginal bone resorption was 0.72 mm (high torque) vs 1.03 mm (control) at loading and 1.24 mm vs 1.09 mm at 1 year, with no significant between-group difference in bone stability or implant success.

High torque up to 176 Ncm did not prevent osseointegration in the short term, but the evidence is weak: a 9-implant control arm, post hoc (not assigned) torque groups, one implant system, and only 1 year of follow-up.

## Summary

Khayat et al. prospectively followed 66 tapered Zimmer Tapered Screw-Vent implants (4.5 mm) in 48 patients, recording maximum insertion torque electronically, to test the hypothesis that very high compression torque disturbs local microcirculation and causes osteocyte necrosis and bone resorption. The 42 implants seated above 70 Ncm (mean 110.6 Ncm, up to 176 Ncm) were compared with 9 implants seated at 30–50 Ncm. After 2–3 months of non-submerged healing every implant was clinically stable, and marginal bone levels at loading and 1 year later did not differ significantly between groups; success rates were also similar. The paper is the human clinical counterpart to Trisi 2011's sheep histology at essentially the same torque (≈110 Ncm), and is very likely one of the constituent studies pooled in the Lemos 2020 SR+MA (the wiki cites it elsewhere by its print year, "Khayat 2013"). Its clinical weight is limited: torque was not assigned (it reflects site density), the control arm is tiny, and 1 year is too short to see the 3-year crestal harm reported by Marconcini 2018.

## Key Contributions

- The **highest human insertion-torque range** in this wiki cluster (70.8–176 Ncm, mean 110.6 Ncm): osseointegration was not prevented at any recorded torque
- Clinical-level test of the osteocyte-necrosis / microcirculation hypothesis — negative at the osseointegration endpoint
- Group-by-timepoint MBL values (loading, 1 year) — primary data underlying the pooled "no MBL difference" conclusion at the SR/MA level
- **Trajectory signal (derived, not author-tested):** from loading to 1 year, group means imply ~0.52 mm further loss in the high-torque group vs ~0.06 mm in controls — the high-torque group went from less bone loss than controls to more. Not significant and underpowered, but the direction is the same as the post-loading crestal harm reported by longer studies; a 1-year "null" here is not evidence that the crest is spared long-term

## Methodology

**Design:** Prospective clinical study; torque groups defined by recorded MIT (post hoc classification — not randomized, not assigned)
**Population:** 48 patients, 66 implants (partially edentulous maxilla and mandible per MeSH); analysed: control 9 implants (MIT 30–50 Ncm), experimental 42 implants (MIT >70 Ncm); 15 implants not assigned to either group in the abstract (presumably 50–70 Ncm; not stated)
**Implant:** Zimmer Tapered Screw-Vent, Ø4.5 mm
**Torque capture:** electronic device (Tohnichi STC200CN)
**Healing:** non-submerged, 2–3 months before loading
**Outcomes:** clinical stability; radiographic marginal bone levels at loading and 1 year after loading; implant success rate

## Results

| Group | n implants | MIT mean (range) | MBL at loading, mm (SD) | MBL at 1 year, mm (SD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Control (low torque) | 9 | 37.1 Ncm (30–50) | 1.03 (0.44) | 1.09 (0.62) |
| Experimental (high torque) | 42 | 110.6 Ncm (70.8–176) | 0.72 (0.56) | 1.24 (0.75) |

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Clinical stability at 2–3 months | All implants stable (both groups) |
| Between-group bone stability | No significant difference (p not given in abstract) |
| Implant success rate | No significant difference (figures not given in abstract) |
| Loading → 1-year change (derived from group means) | ~+0.52 mm high torque vs ~+0.06 mm control — not tested |

**Limitations:** control n=9; torque confounded by site bone density (no assignment); single implant system and diameter; 1-year follow-up; 15/66 implants unaccounted for in the comparison; abstract-only (no p-values, per-arch data, or dropout information).
