## Three-line Summary

Retrospective chart review (Kuwait University Dental Center, 2016–2020) of 135 patients with 305 anodically oxidized (TiUnite) tapered NobelReplace implants placed by a single surgeon, relating secondary ISQ measured at superstructure connection (mean 20.3 ± 8.8 weeks post-placement) to 7 implant- and protocol-related factors via group comparisons and multivariate linear regression.

Univariate, platform diameter (P<0.0001; 3.5 mm ≈72 vs 4.3 mm ≈78 vs 5.0 mm ≈81 patient-level), surgical protocol (one vs two-stage, P=0.006), insertion torque (<35 vs >35 Ncm, P=0.001) and primary stability (achieved vs not, P=0.001) each showed significant secondary ISQ differences; in the regression only platform diameter remained a significant positive predictor (patient-level B=5.25, P<0.0001, adjusted R²=0.25; implant-level B=4.28, P<0.0001, R²=0.11); cohort mean secondary ISQ 77.30 ± 7.22 (patient-level) and 77.15 ± 7.40 (implant-level).

Wider platforms increase secondary stability before final restoration — clinically useful for soft-bone sites and for reaching ISQ thresholds such as ≥65 for immediate loading — while length, loading protocol, and (at patient level) simultaneous grafting showed no significant effect; generalization is limited by the retrospective single-surgeon, single-implant-system design.

## 세줄요약

쿠웨이트단대 구강클리닉 2016–2020 후향 차트리뷰(환자 135명, 산화피막 TiUnite tapered 노벨리플레이스 임플란트 305개, 단일 술자): 상부구조 연결 시점(식립 후 평균 20.3 ± 8.8주)의 이차 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)와 7개 임플란트·프로토콜 관련 인자의 관계를 집단비교와 다변량 선형회귀로 평가.

더 넓은 플랫폼이 상부구조 연결 전 이차안정성을 높이므로(연조직골 부위·즉시하중 ISQ 문턱 ≥65 달성에 유용), 길이·하중 프로토콜·(환자단위) 동시골이식은 유의 영향 없음 — 단, 후향·단일술자·단일임플란트시스템 설계로 일반화는 제한적.

## Summary

This retrospective cohort study (135 patients, 305 TiUnite oxidized implants, single surgeon) examined which of 7 implant- and protocol-related factors explain the secondary implant stability quotient (ISQ) measured by resonance frequency analysis (RFA) at superstructure connection, roughly 5 months after placement. Univariate comparisons found significantly lower secondary ISQ for 3.5 mm platforms, two-stage surgery, low (<35 Ncm) insertion torque, and failed primary stability, while length, loading protocol, and patient-level grafting showed no effect. However, a multivariate linear regression adjusted for patient covariates reduced those associations to a single significant predictor: platform diameter (B=5.25, P<0.0001; adjusted R²=0.25), with 3.5 mm implants scoring ~6–7 ISQ units lower than 4.3–5.0 mm implants. The cohort mean ISQ stayed high (~77), indicating the oxidized surface achieves firm secondary stability across subgroups. For clinical planning, choosing a wider platform is the factor most likely to raise measured stability before restoration — relevant to soft-bone situations and immediate-loading ISQ thresholds — while torque- and protocol-related differences should not be over-interpreted at this timepoint.

## Key Contributions

- Platform diameter is the **only** significant positive predictor of secondary (post-healing) ISQ in a confound-adjusted regression for a uniform oxidized implant system (patient-level B=5.25, P<0.0001; implant-level B=4.28).
- Univariate-significant factors (insertion torque, primary stability, surgical protocol, implant-level grafting) all become non-significant after adjustment — causal claims from comparative designs alone are not supported.
- The diameter effect is driven by the 3.5 mm platform (3.5 vs 4.3/5.0 significant; 4.3 vs 5.0 NS), explaining and qualifying a prior null diameter result in the same TiUnite system.
- Cohort secondary ISQ remains uniformly high (~77) despite subgroup differences, implying limited biological significance of the observed variation at this healing stage.

## Methodology

Retrospective STROBE-compliant chart review (2016–2020, Kuwait University Dental Center). 135 patients / 305 TiUnite (anodically oxidized) tapered NobelReplace conical-connection implants placed by the same investigator; inclusion aged 21–90, ASA 1–2, normoglycemic or controlled diabetic (HbA1C <8%), RFA at superstructure connection. Secondary ISQ measured with Osstell IDx (mean 20.3 ± 8.8 weeks post-placement). Factors: length, platform diameter, loading protocol, surgical protocol, simultaneous grafting, primary stability (no detectable mobility), insertion torque (<35/>35 Ncm). Mann–Whitney U / Kruskal–Wallis plus hierarchical multivariate linear regression adjusting for age, gender, smoking, diabetes, healing time, implantation site; patient-level analysis used one blindly selected implant per patient (G*Power target 270/100, actual 305/135).

## Results

Mean secondary ISQ: 77.30 ± 7.22 (patient-level), 77.15 ± 7.40 (implant-level).

Patient-level (implant-level in parentheses) secondary ISQ by platform diameter (P<0.0001; 3.5 vs 4.3 and vs 5.0 significant, 4.3 vs 5.0 NS):

| Platform diameter | Patient N (%) | Patient ISQ (SD) | Implant n (%) | Implant ISQ (SD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.5 mm | 40 (30%) | 72.09 (6.46) | 71 (23%) | 72.78 (7.72) |
| 4.3 mm | 48 (36%) | 78.00 (6.75) | 141 (46%) | 77.82 (6.88) |
| 5.0 mm | 47 (35%) | 81.01 (5.61) | 93 (31%) | 79.46 (6.52) |

Significant univariate comparisons (patient-level): one- vs two-stage 79.54 vs 76.42 (P=0.006); torque >35 vs <35 Ncm 78.36 vs 74.59 (P=0.001); primary stability achieved vs not 77.97 vs 73.43 (P=0.001). NS: implant length (P=0.07), loading protocol (P=0.28), grafting at patient level (P=0.33); implant-level grafting significant (grafted lower: 76.27 vs 78.46, P=0.01).

Multivariate regression (final adjusted model): platform diameter the only significant predictor — B=5.25, P<0.0001, adjusted R²=0.25 (patient-level); B=4.28, P<0.0001, R²=0.11 (implant-level). Insertion torque (P=0.95), primary stability (P=0.15), length (P=0.80) and protocol variables non-significant after adjustment.
