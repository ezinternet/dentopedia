## Three-line Summary

Narrative literature review with systematic database search of all factors reported to influence ISQ (Journal of Oral Biology and Craniofacial Research 2020), synthesizing evidence from multiple clinical and experimental studies.

17 ISQ-influencing factors were identified; of these, only 6 are clinically predictive and only 2 — local bone quality and implant macro-design — have a well-documented scientific basis; the other 11 have limited or inconsistent evidence.

A single ISQ number without bone quality context is insufficient for clinical decisions; ISQ must be interpreted together with bone type, as local cortical density is the dominant driver and most modifiable factor is macro-design.

## 세줄요약

데이터베이스 검색을 포함한 서술적 문헌고찰(Journal of Oral Biology and Craniofacial Research 2020)로, ISQ에 영향을 미치는 모든 인자에 관한 임상·실험 연구들을 종합하였다.

총 17개 ISQ 영향인자 확인; 이 중 임상적 예측력을 지닌 인자는 6개, 충분한 과학적 근거를 가진 인자는 2개(국소 골질·임플란트 거시디자인)에 불과; 나머지 11개는 근거가 제한적이거나 일관성 없다.

골 타입 맥락 없이 ISQ 수치 단독으로는 임상 결정이 불충분하며, 가장 강력한 결정인자는 국소 피질골 밀도이고 가장 수정 가능한 인자는 거시디자인이다.

## Summary
Narrative review with systematic database search identifying all factors reported to influence ISQ. Found 17 factors total (13 basic + 4 bone quality subfactors). Critical filtering: only **6 clinically predictive**, only **2 with well-documented scientific basis** — local bone quality and implant macro-design.

## 17 ISQ-Influencing Factors

| Tier | Factors |
|---|---|
| **Well-documented (2)** | Local bone quality, implant macro-design |
| **Clinically predictive (6 total)** | + Insertion torque, site preparation, healing time, measurement direction |
| **Other identified (11)** | SmartPeg type, measurement direction variation, device type, resonance frequency, implant length, diameter, surface treatment, patient factors, etc. |

## Clinical Takeaway
- ISQ is a valid stability indicator but **must be contextualized** with bone type and site
- A single ISQ number without bone quality context is insufficient for clinical decisions
- Local bone quality (cortical thickness, density) is the dominant driver — consistent with CBCT-ISQ correlation studies
- Macro-design (tapered vs parallel) is the most modifiable controllable factor
