## Three-line Summary

3-arm RCT (BMC Oral Health 2026): 30 patients in posterior maxilla; conventional osteotomy vs Summers' osteotome vs osseodensification (OD), all using the same Straumann BLT SLActive 4mm×10mm implant; ISQ measured at placement, 3m, and 6m.

No statistically significant differences in ISQ at any time point between the three groups; numerical trends showed OD marginally higher and Summers' osteotome marginally lower; 100% survival at 6 months.

Underpowered exploratory trial (n=10/group) — cannot exclude real ISQ differences; findings directionally consistent with broader OD evidence but fail to reach statistical significance.

## Summary

Venkatesh et al. (BMC Oral Health 2026) conducted a 3-arm RCT at Nitte University comparing conventional osteotomy, Summers' osteotome, and osseodensification for single implant placement in the posterior maxilla. All groups used the same Straumann BLT SLActive implant (4mm×10mm). Implants were placed non-submerged with immediate provisionalization (not functionally loaded). ISQ was recorded by Osstell RFA at placement, 3 months, and 6 months. One-way ANOVA found no statistically significant differences at any time point. Numerically, OD trended marginally higher and Summers' osteotome marginally lower. All 30 implants achieved 100% survival at 6 months. The study is acknowledged as exploratory and underpowered (n=10/group).

## Key Contributions

- Direct 3-arm head-to-head comparison using identical implant system (controls design confounders)
- No statistically significant ISQ differences — but underpowered (n=10/group)
- Numerical trend: OD ≥ conventional > osteotome — directionally consistent with pooled evidence
- All techniques adequate for immediate provisionalisation in posterior maxilla
- Retrospective registration is a methodology limitation

## Methodology

3-arm RCT (exploratory); Nitte University, India; n=30 (10/group); Straumann BLT SLActive 4×10mm; conventional/osteotome/OD; non-submerged + immediate provisional (no functional loading); Osstell at placement/3m/6m; one-way ANOVA + Tukey; CTRI/2021/09/036513 retrospective.
