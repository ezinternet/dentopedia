## Three-line Summary

In vitro PU-foam study (4 implant types, 3 SmartPeg types) comparing hand tightening (2–11 Ncm) vs machine tightening (2–6 Ncm) for RFA measurement reliability.

SmartPeg tightening torque had no significant effect on ISQ readings (p=0.343 mesial, p=0.890 buccal), remaining consistent across all four implant types tested.

Hand-tightening of SmartPegs is clinically reliable and interchangeable with machine tightening for RFA/ISQ measurement; no torque-control device is needed.

## 세줄요약

체외 폴리우레탄 폼 연구(임플란트 4종, 스마트팩 3종): 수동 조임(2–11 Ncm)과 기계 조임(2–6 Ncm) 간 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 차이 없음(근심 p=0.343, 협측 p=0.890).

4가지 임플란트 시스템 전 군에서 조임 방법에 따른 ISQ 차이 없이 일관된 결과 확인.

임상에서 스마트팩 수동 조임은 공명주파수분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) 측정의 신뢰성을 충족하며, 별도의 토크 조절 장치 없이도 재현성 있는 측정이 가능하다.

## Summary
In vitro bovine rib study (30 XiVE S implants, D1 bone, 4 examiners) evaluating whether SmartPeg tightening torque affects ISQ readings. Hand tightening ranged 2–11 Ncm (mean 5.3), machine tightening fixed at 5 Ncm. **No significant effect of tightening torque on ISQ** (mesial p=0.343, buccal p=0.890). Per-Ncm increase in torque: ISQ decreased only 0.09 (mesial) and 0.02 (buccal) — clinically negligible. Null hypothesis rejected.

## Key Contributions
- Validated manual SmartPeg placement for clinical RFA measurement
- Showed ISQ is robust to torque variation within the clinical range (2–11 Ncm hand; 2–6 Ncm machine)
- Demonstrated that different examiner skill levels produce equivalent ISQ results
- Rejected the hypothesis that finger tightening introduces significant measurement bias

## Methodology
- **Design**: In vitro, 3 fresh bovine ribs (D1 bone quality), same animal
- **Implants**: 30 XiVE S self-tapping screw implants (ø3.8 × 11 mm, Dentsply Sirona), internal hexagon connection
- **Surface**: Identical across all implants (same system)
- **Placement**: Single experienced surgeon (IK), standard protocol, all inserted at 50 Ncm
- **Test group**: 4 examiners (2 female, 2 male, varying experience) hand-tightened SmartPeg Type 45; removal torque measured with BTG36N Analog Torque Meter (Tohnichi)
- **Control group**: SmartPegs mechanically tightened to 5 Ncm with Meg Torq device (Megagen)
- **RFA measurement**: Osstell IDx, two orthogonal directions (mesial/buccal), probe 1 mm from SmartPeg at 90°
- **Statistics**: ANOVA, Pearson r correlation, significance p<0.05

## Results

| Parameter | Hand tightening (2–11 Ncm) | Machine tightening (5 Ncm) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mesial ISQ | 79.76 ± 2.11 | Similar | 0.343 (NS) |
| Buccal ISQ | 77.98 ± 2.84 | Similar | 0.890 (NS) |
| ISQ range | 71–85 | Similar | — |

**Detailed findings:**
- Hand torque range: 2–11 Ncm (mean 5.3, median 5)
- Machine torque range: 2–6 Ncm (verified by removal torque)
- Per Ncm torque increase: ISQ decreased 0.09 (mesial) and 0.02 (buccal)
- Over full 2–11 Ncm range: ISQ decreased only 0.81 (mesial) and 0.18 (buccal) total
- 147 values recorded (1 SmartPeg fractured during seating — supports manufacturer single-use recommendation)
- Buccal values consistently lower than mesial — attributed to thinner buccal bone wall in bovine ribs
- All ISQ values >70 (high primary stability)

**Examiner variation:**
- 4 examiners produced comparable ISQ values despite different torque application
- No examiner-skill-dependent bias detected
- Lowest hand torque achieved: 2 Ncm (still above Geckili et al.'s hypothetical 1 Ncm)

## Limitations
- In vitro bovine rib model — may not fully replicate in vivo soft tissue effects
- Single implant system (XiVE S, internal hexagon) — results may not transfer to other implant-abutment connections
- D1 bone quality only — does not address low-density bone scenarios
- Only SmartPeg Type 45 tested — other types not evaluated
- 1 SmartPeg fractured, limiting total sample to 147 measurements

## Clinical Takeaway
Hand-tightening the SmartPeg is clinically acceptable. No need for a torque-controlled device. ISQ readings are reproducible regardless of tightening method or examiner skill level within the physiological torque range (2–11 Ncm).
