## Three-line Summary

Biomechanics primer (Journal of Oral Implantology, 1992) on how dental implants transfer force to surrounding tissues.

It covers force components, moments, transfer mechanisms, impact and stress-strain relationships, linked to implant longevity and restorative planning.

Conceptual; no numeric data or clinical outcomes in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

생체역학 입문 (Journal of Oral Implantology, 1992) — 임플란트에서 주변 조직으로의 힘 전달.

힘의 성분, 모멘트, 전달 기전, 충격, 응력-변형률 관계를 다루며 장기 성공과 보철 계획에 연결.
