## Three-line Summary

ICOI consensus conference panel report, Part 2 (Las Vegas, Jun 2004; multi-author expert panel) on crown-height space (CHS) for implant dentistry.

The panel summarizes general guidelines that emerged, while stating that a single opinion was not reached on most issues.

Cite as expert opinion; the abstract gives no numeric CHS thresholds.

## 세줄요약

ICOI 합의회의 패널 보고서 2부 (라스베이거스, 2004년 6월; 다저자 전문가 패널) — 임플란트 치의학의 크라운 높이 공간(CHS).

패널은 도출된 일반 지침을 요약하되, 대부분 쟁점에서 단일 의견에 도달하지 못했음을 명시.

전문가 의견으로 인용할 것; 초록에 CHS 수치 기준이 없음.

## Summary

Part 2 completes the ICOI crown-height-space guidelines begun in Part 1. Its value for the wiki is as a record of which restorative-space questions the panel considered answerable as general guidance and which it did not.

## Key Contributions

- Second half of the ICOI CHS guideline set.
- Explicit acknowledgement of limited consensus.

## Methodology

Consensus conference (Jun 26–27, 2004) with pre-, intra- and post-meeting panel communication. The abstract does not describe the grading method.

## Results

Qualitative guidelines only; no thresholds in the abstract.
