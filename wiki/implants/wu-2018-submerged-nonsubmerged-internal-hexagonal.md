## Three-line Summary

Retrospective 5-year cohort (114 bone-level XiVE S plus implants with internal hexagonal connections in 72 patients, Sun Yat-sen University / Xiamen Stomatological Hospital, China), all immediately implanted, comparing submerged vs nonsubmerged healing and testing guided bone regeneration (GBR) application, implant site, length and diameter as potential influencing factors.

5-year cumulative survival rate (CSR) was 94% (submerged) vs 96% (nonsubmerged), with no statistically significant difference in marginal bone loss (MBL) between the two protocols, and no significant difference in CSR by healing protocol, GBR application, implant site or implant length.

Both protocols are defensible for immediately placed bone-level internal-hexagonal implants and neither implant length, site nor GBR use altered long-term success — but as a non-randomised, single-system, abstract-only record with no MBL values, no p-values and no per-protocol group sizes, its null cannot be power-checked and must not be read as outranking the pooled 0.12 mm MBL effect reported for submerged placement by [[implants/moustafa-ali-2018-submerged-vs-nonsubmerged-implant]].

5년 누적 생존율 (Cumulative Survival Rate, CSR)은 침습식 94% vs 비침습식 96%였고, 두 프로토콜 간 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)에는 유의한 차이가 없었으며, 치유 프로토콜·GBR 적용·식립 부위·길이 어디에서도 생존율의 유의한 차이가 관찰되지 않음.

두 프로토콜 모두 즉시 식립 골수준 내부 육각형 임플란트에서 타당하고 임플란트 길이·부위·GBR 적용은 장기 성공에 영향을 주지 않음 — 다만 비무작위·단일 시스템(XiVE)·초록만 확보한 기록으로 MBL 수치·p값·그룹별 표본수가 없어 음성(null) 결과의 검정력을 확인할 수 없고, 침습식에서 유의한 MBL 차이(평균차 MD 0.12 mm)를 보고한 [[implants/moustafa-ali-2018-submerged-vs-nonsubmerged-implant]]의 풀링 결과를 대체하지도 않음.

## Summary

This retrospective cohort (Wu S, Wu X, Shrestha R et al. — Sun Yat-sen University Guanghua School of Stomatology / Xiamen Stomatological Hospital / Guangzhou Shangde Dental Clinic, *J Prosthodont* 2018;27(2):101-107) followed **114 bone-level XiVE S plus implants with internal hexagonal connections** placed in **72 patients** by immediate implantation for 5 years, and asked whether the submerged vs nonsubmerged healing protocol — and the potential influencing factors of guided bone regeneration, implant site, implant length and implant diameter — affect long-term outcome. Five-year cumulative survival was **94% (submerged) vs 96% (nonsubmerged)**, and the **t-test comparison of marginal bone loss found no statistically significant difference** between the protocols; log-rank comparisons found no significant CSR difference by healing protocol, GBR application, implant site or implant length. The authors conclude that high CSRs and good marginal bone levels are achieved at 5 years with both techniques and that implant length, site and GBR use do not affect long-term success. The paper's real contribution is structural rather than numerical: it is the **only page in this wiki that varies the healing protocol within a single implant system, connection design, bone level and placement timing**, so unlike the pooled reviews and the one-piece-vs-two-piece RCT it cannot be explained away by brand or surface differences — and on that controlled axis it still finds no MBL effect. Read honestly, this is a **weaker-weight null than the SR+MA evidence already held on this axis**: no MBL values, no p-values, no per-protocol n, non-randomised allocation, and an internal hexagonal design generation that predates platform switching.

**Retrieval note**: page built from the PubMed abstract only (PMID 29143389, no PMCID; Wiley full text paywalled) — see the [[implants/wu-2018-submerged-nonsubmerged-internal-hexagonal|source page]]. The MBL figures circulating elsewhere in this wiki (1.2 mm at year 1 + 0.78 mm over years 1–5) are a **secondary citation** from [[implants/mbl/mortazavi-2021-bone-loss-tissue-bone-level-implants]], not values reported in this abstract.

## Key Contributions

- **The within-system control the protocol literature lacked.** Same bone-level design, same internal hexagonal connection, same immediate-placement timing in both arms — the flap/loading decision is the only varying exposure. This removes the brand-and-surface confounding that limits [[implants/pirc-2026-one-piece-two-piece-implants-15year-rct]] (one-piece Straumann SLA vs two-piece Brånemark TiUnite, 0.08 vs 1.53 mm MBL) and therefore tests whether the submerged/non-submerged architecture itself carries an MBL penalty.
- **5-year survival estimate for a displaced design generation** — bone-level implants with a non-platform-switched internal hexagonal connection, now largely superseded clinically, so the value is mechanistic and historical as much as prescriptive.
- **Modifiers tested and cleared in the same cohort**: guided bone regeneration application, implant site, implant length and implant diameter did not alter long-term success, ranking the *surgical protocol* below those case-level choices for outcome in this setting.
- **Explicit non-inferiority framing by omission**: neither protocol is recommended over the other, so the paper functions as a decision-support anchor ("choose the flap for soft-tissue and esthetic reasons") rather than a bone-loss claim — consistent with, and weaker than, the pooled reviews' clinical reading.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, 5-year follow-up, single implant system. Protocol was a clinical decision, not a random allocation.
- **Sample**: 114 bone-level implants (XiVE S plus, internal hexagonal connections) in 72 patients, all immediately implanted.
- **Exposure**: submerged vs nonsubmerged healing protocol.
- **Potential influencing factors**: GBR application, implant site, implant length, implant diameter.
- **Outcomes and statistics**: MBL between protocols by **t-test**; CSR by **life table method** with **Kaplan–Meier** curves; between-group CSR comparisons by **log-rank test**.
- **Not recoverable from the abstract**: inclusion/exclusion criteria, patient demographics, extraction-to-placement interval, primary-stability thresholds, loading/prosthetic protocol, radiographic technique and MBL timepoints, **MBL values**, p-values, confidence intervals, graft material, attrition, ethics approval, funding.

## Results

| Outcome | Submerged | Nonsubmerged |
|---|---|---|
| 5-year cumulative survival rate (CSR, life table) | **94%** | **96%** |
| Marginal bone loss (t-test) | No statistically significant difference | No statistically significant difference |

| Factor tested for CSR (log-rank) | Result as reported |
|---|---|
| Healing protocol | No statistically significant difference |
| Guided bone regeneration (GBR) | No statistically significant difference |
| Implant site | No statistically significant difference |
| Implant length | No statistically significant difference |
| Implant diameter | Tested per methods, **not listed among the non-significant results** — status unrecoverable |

- **Scale**: 114 implants · 72 patients · 5 years · immediate implantation throughout · XiVE S plus.
- **Authors' conclusion**: high CSRs and good marginal bone levels at 5 years with **both** techniques; implant length, site and GBR application did not affect long-term success.

**Caveats attached to these numbers:**

- The 94% vs 96% split is a **2-percentage-point difference reported as non-significant**, with no p-value, CI, log-rank statistic or per-protocol n in the abstract — so its power cannot be checked, and it must not be restated as *equivalence* (no equivalence or non-inferiority margin was designed).
- **"No statistically significant difference in MBL" carries no magnitude** — no value in mm for either arm at any timepoint. This null therefore cannot be placed on the same effect-size axis as the pooled MD 0.12 mm (95% CI 0.00–0.24) in [[implants/moustafa-ali-2018-submerged-vs-nonsubmerged-implant]] or the 0.13 mm effect in [[implants/survival/troiano-2018-early-late-failure-submerged]].
- "Good marginal bone levels" is **qualitative** — no threshold, mean, SD or range reported.
- **Abstract-level inconsistency on diameter, reproduced as found**: the methods state log-rank comparisons covered "sites, lengths, **and diameters**", while the results enumerate only MBL, healing protocol, GBR, site and length. Whether the diameter comparison was null, underpowered or dropped cannot be determined.
- The **1.2 mm (year 1) + 0.78 mm (years 1–5)** MBL figures associated with "Wu 2018" in [[implants/mbl/mortazavi-2021-bone-loss-tissue-bone-level-implants]] are a secondary citation from that SR's data table and are **not** present in this abstract; they are flagged as pending full-text confirmation rather than adopted.

## Related Papers

- [[implants/moustafa-ali-2018-submerged-vs-nonsubmerged-implant]] — `refines`. **Same question, opposite statistical direction — the tension is stated here, not hidden.** That SR+MA of 6 RCTs reports **significantly more MBL with submerged placement** (MD 0.12 mm; 95% CI 0.00–0.24; I² = 0%, P = .04), judged by its own authors *not clinically relevant*, with 4 of 6 RCTs at high risk of bias and a CI lower bound sitting on 0.00 mm. This cohort reports **no significant MBL difference** and a 2-point survival gap in the same numerical direction (submerged lower). Reconciling honestly: the SR+MA **outranks this cohort** on both design and evidence weight, so the correct reading is **not** "the null refutes the pooled effect" — it is that a 114-implant non-randomised cohort is underpowered to resolve a difference of the pooled magnitude. The typed edge is `refines` (this cohort narrows where the pooled 0.12 mm can be said to apply — one bone-level internal-hex XiVE system, immediately placed sites only), not `contradicts`. Its page already records this paper as a pending reconciliation target; the reciprocal edge is not written here because existing pages are not edited at ingest.
- [[implants/survival/troiano-2018-early-late-failure-submerged]] — `refines`. The same axis from the pooled side, with the **opposite** arm assignment: Troiano puts a +2% early-failure penalty on **non-submerged** healing (early = <6 months from placement) and a 0.13 mm MBL trend favouring non-submerged, both explicitly underpowered. This cohort finds no significant difference on either outcome — but it reports **no early-vs-late failure split**, so it cannot test the +2% directly, and its 5-year cumulative figure is compatible with a small early penalty in either arm. Qualifies the pooled estimate's reach; does not overturn it.
- [[implants/pirc-2026-one-piece-two-piece-implants-15year-rct]] — `refines`. The **clinical mirror image**: one-piece implants are non-submerged by design, two-piece submerged, and Pirc's two-piece arm lost 1.53 mm MBL vs 0.08 mm. But Pirc's arms differ in brand and surface (Straumann SLA vs Brånemark TiUnite) as well as architecture — a magnitude ~13× the 0.12 mm protocol effect, which Pirc's own page warns against extrapolating. This cohort is the missing **within-system control**: holding system and surface constant and varying only the protocol produced **no MBL difference**, which argues that Pirc's 1.45 mm gap is driven by the system/surface pair rather than by the submerged-vs-non-submerged architecture itself.
- [[implants/mbl/al-amri-2016-crestal-bone-loss-submerged]] — prose only, no typed edge. The closest published consensus to this cohort's null: a systematic review of 13 studies in which **5 of 6 human studies found no significant submerged-vs-nonsubmerged crestal bone-loss difference**, reached 2 years earlier by a much weaker method (a vote count with no pooled estimate, half the evidence base canine, and platform-switching studies excluded outright).
- [[implants/mbl/mortazavi-2021-bone-loss-tissue-bone-level-implants]] — prose only, no typed edge (that SR **includes** this paper among its 38 trials, so a `reinforces` edge would be circular). This is the **primary source** behind one of its data-table rows (the 1.2 mm / 0.78 mm MBL split), and its conclusion — that *design* levers (platform switching, Morse taper, SLActive) dominate MBL while the *procedural* lever is smaller and more uncertain — is the axis-level statement this cohort's null is consistent with.
- [[implants/mbl/kim-2022-abutment-connection-mbl-survival]] — prose only, no typed edge. Same connection family and the same null-on-survival pattern (93.3% vs 90.7% at 8 years), but it identifies the abutment **connection** as an MBL driver at 1 year (external hex 1.23 vs internal 0.72 mm, p<0.001, gap gone by year 5) — a more plausible crestal-bone lever than flap design. It excluded immediate placement, so the cohorts do not overlap.
- [[implants/liu-2021-clinical-radiographic-performance-one-piece]] — prose only. The pooled (SR+MA) one-piece vs two-piece contrast: the same mirror axis as Pirc, at synthesis level, and the design-level counterpart to this cohort's within-system protocol test.
- [[implants/survival/giok-2026-factors-implant-failure-umbrella-review]] — prose only, no typed edge. Grades "submerged vs nonsubmerged healing" as a **high-certainty** RCT-derived failure-risk association but, being abstract-only, never names the penalised arm. This cohort cannot adjudicate that claim: its 2-point survival gap is non-significant and it does not split early from late failure.
