## Three-line Summary

Internal irrigation via a 3D-printed guide with dedicated channels reduced mean ΔT to 3.50°C (solid model) and 0.84°C (bilayered model) during guided osteotomy, versus 31.41°C and 13.31°C with no irrigation, and 9.48°C and 2.45°C with external irrigation. Irrigation route — not merely presence of irrigation — is the key determinant of thermal safety in guided implant drilling. Bilayered resin models showed lower ΔT than solid models by infrared thermography (p = 0.009), highlighting that model substrate affects measured thermal risk.

## 세줄요약

안내 골삭제 시 3D 프린팅 가이드 내부 채널을 통한 내부 관류 (Internal Irrigation, II)는 온도 상승을 이중층 모델 기준 0.84°C로 최소화했으며, 관류 없음(13.31°C)·외부 관류(2.45°C) 대비 현저히 낮았다. 관류 유무뿐 아니라 관류 경로가 열 위험도의 핵심 변수임을 처음으로 직접 비교 데이터로 제시했다. 피질골-해면골 구조를 모사한 이중층 레진 모델은 단일 고형 모델보다 낮은 온도 상승을 보여(IR 열화상 p = 0.009), 실험 기질 선택이 열 측정값에 영향을 미침을 시사한다.

## Summary

This in vitro study compared heat generation during guided implant osteotomy across three irrigation conditions (none, external, internal) in two resin mandibular model types (solid, bilayered). Drilling was performed sequentially (2.0–4.2 mm, 18 mm depth, 1200 rpm) through a static surgical guide. Temperature was captured simultaneously by infrared thermography and an internal thermistor. Internal irrigation, delivered via dedicated channels in a 3D-printed guide, produced the lowest ΔT in both model types and by both measurement methods. Bilayered models demonstrated significantly lower ΔT than solid models under infrared thermography (p = 0.009), suggesting that homogeneous solid models may overestimate thermal risk in bone with mixed cortical/trabecular architecture.

## Key Contributions

- Quantifies **irrigation route effect** in a fully guided-surgery context: internal > external > no irrigation for heat reduction
- Internal irrigation reduced ΔT by ~90% vs. no irrigation (solid: 31.41°C → 3.50°C)
- Dual measurement approach (infrared + thermistor) reveals **methodological sensitivity**: bilayered model advantage was significant by IR but inconsistent by thermistor
- Bilayered model as a more realistic substrate than homogeneous solid resin for bone thermal simulation

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | In vitro comparative thermal analysis |
| Model types | Solid resin mandible; bilayered resin (cortical + trabecular analog) |

| Drill sequence | 2.0–4.2 mm sequential, 18 mm depth, 1200 rpm |
| Temperature methods | Infrared thermography (surface) + internal thermistor (subsurface) |
| Primary outcome | ΔT from baseline (°C) |

## Results

| Condition | Solid ΔT (°C) | Bilayered ΔT (°C) |
|---|---|---|
| No irrigation | 31.41 | 13.31 |
| External irrigation | 9.48 | 2.45 |
| Internal irrigation | 3.50 | 0.84 |

- Bilayered < Solid by IR thermography: p = 0.009; thermistor: less consistent
- All internal irrigation values are well below the thermal osteonecrosis (열성 골괴사, Thermal Osteonecrosis, TO) threshold of 47°C (assuming physiological baseline ~37°C, ΔT must exceed ~10°C to risk injury)
