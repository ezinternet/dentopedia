## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT (n=32 implants, healed posterior maxilla, single-tooth replacement, immediate loading within 48–72 h) randomizing undersized drilling (축경 드릴링, Group I, n=16) vs single drilling technique using the HaeNaem Co. One Drill System Kit (단일 드릴링, Group II, n=16), Neobiotech IS II active implants, 12-month follow-up.

No significant between-group difference on any outcome (all P > 0.05): insertion torque 35.0±2.58 vs 35.62±2.50 Ncm (P=0.49), ISQ 77.3 vs 76.3 down to 76.1 vs 75.5 at 12 months (P=0.54–0.74), buccal MBL 0.47 vs 0.70 mm at 12 months (P=0.06); every implant successful with no complications (100%).

Both techniques are clinically equivalent in poor-density posterior maxilla under an immediate-loading protocol; the single-drilling merits claimed (less heat/mechanical damage/friction, shorter operative time) are author claims, not measured outcomes, and the small sample plus short follow-up limit generalization.

## 세줄요약

전향적 RCT (n=32, 치유된 상악 구치부 단일치, 48–72시간 내 즉시부하) — 축경 드릴링 (undersized drilling, Group I, n=16) vs 단일 드릴링 (single drilling, HaeNaem One Drill System Kit, Group II, n=16), Neobiotech IS II active, 12개월 추적.

모든 지표에서 군간 유의차 없음 (P>0.05): 삽입 토크 (insertion torque) 35.0 vs 35.6 Ncm, 임플란트 안정성 지수 (ISQ) 76–78로 전 시점 동등, 12개월 협측 변연골 소실 (MBL) 0.47 vs 0.70 mm (P=0.06), 생존율 100%.

저밀도 상악 구치부 즉시부하에서 두 술식은 동등하며, 단일 드릴링의 장점(발열·기계손상 감소, 술시각 단축)은 저자 주장일 뿐 측정 결과가 아님 — 소표본·단기추적으로 일반화에 한계.

## Summary

This prospective randomized clinical trial compared two simplified osteotomy protocols for immediately loaded single implants in the posterior maxilla — a low-density region where drilling technique is thought to affect primary stability. 32 patients (16 per group) received Neobiotech IS II active implants prepared either by undersized drilling (축경 드릴링, manufacturer sequence with the final drill skipped, 800–1200 rpm) or by single drilling (단일 드릴링) with the HaeNaem Co. One Drill System Kit (guiding drill then a single drill at the implant diameter, 800 rpm). After 12 months both techniques produced statistically indistinguishable results on insertion torque, ISQ at every time point, soft-tissue indices, bone density, and marginal bone loss — all P > 0.05 — with 100% implant success and no complications. The paper positions single drilling as a patient- and clinician-friendly simplification, but its claimed advantages (less heat, less mechanical damage, shorter operative time) are asserted rather than measured, and the small sample shortens confidence.

## Key Contributions

- Head-to-head RCT showing single drilling (one drill at implant diameter) is clinically equivalent to undersized drilling for immediately loaded implants in the posterior maxilla across 12 months.
- First non-retracted clinical outcome dataset for the HaeNaem Co. One Drill System Kit (guiding drill 3.5 mm at 800 rpm → single drill at implant diameter).
- Confirms ≥ 35 Ncm insertion torque and > 70 ISQ primary stability are achievable in poor-density posterior maxilla by either technique, with no stability dip over 12 months.
- Adds a direct clinical comparison to the "alternative site-preparation raises stability" literature, which here shows no advantage for the more instrument-intensive undersized protocol.

## Methodology

Prospective RCT (CONSORT workflow); computer-generated randomization (SPSS v27.0) assigned 16 implants per arm — Group I undersized drilling (final drill skipped), Group II single drilling (One Drill System Kit, HaeNaem Co., Ltd, Korea). Healed posterior-maxilla single-tooth sites, age ≥18 y, bone height ≥10 mm; smokers and uncontrolled systemic disease excluded. Paracrestal full-thickness flap, Neobiotech IS II active implants placed 1 mm subcrestal, immediate loading within 48–72 h (metal-ceramic, temporary cement). Outcomes: insertion torque (manual ratchet), ISQ (immediate, 3/6/12 mo), mBI and peri-implant probing depth (loading, 3/6/12 mo), buccal/palatal bone density (preop, immediate, 6/12 mo), buccal/palatal MBL (6/12 mo). Independent t-test, paired t-test, repeated-measures ANOVA, Fisher exact; α=0.05, two-sided. IRB Mansoura University A03012024OS; NCT06770231.

Both groups: insertion torque ≥ 35 Ncm, ISQ > 70 at every time point, 100% survival, no complications.
