## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (Istanbul University, n=160 osteotomies) compared four drill systems — conventional stainless steel (CS), velodrill (VD), ceramic zirconia (CD), and bone-enhancing (BE) — at standardized 800 rpm, 50 mL/min saline irrigation, infrared thermography.

Ceramic drills generated the highest mean surface temperature (24.82 ± 2.69°C; peak 33.80°C) and required the longest drilling time (17.07 ± 6.82 s vs ~10 s for others), with one drill fracture at 26th use; CS, VD, and BE were statistically comparable in drilling time and significantly faster than CD (p<0.0001).

All systems remained below the 47°C thermal necrosis threshold under adequate irrigation, but the ceramic drill's low thermal conductivity and extended contact time represent a clinically relevant risk in dense bone, multi-osteotomy cases, or with suboptimal irrigation.

인비트로 (이스탄불대, 160 와위) — CS(스테인리스), VD(벨로드릴), CD(세라믹), BE(골증강) 4가지 드릴 시스템 비교; 800rpm·50mL/min 식염수·적외선 열화상.

세라믹 드릴: 최고 평균 온도 24.82°C(최대 33.80°C)·최장 드릴링 시간 17.07s; 26번째에 드릴 파절 1건; CS·VD·BE 드릴링 시간 세라믹 대비 유의하게 짧음(p<0.0001).

모든 시스템 표준 세척 조건에서 47°C 이하 유지 — 그러나 세라믹은 낮은 열전도율·긴 접촉시간으로 조밀 피질골·다중 와위·세척 불충분 시 위험.

## Summary

Istanbul University Faculty of Dentistry, Research Laboratory. 160 osteotomies (40/system) in fresh bovine rib cortical bone. Four systems: CS (Straumann 5-step), VD (Straumann Velodrill 6-step), CD (Z-Systems zirconia 4-step), BE (Legend Group 8-step). All systems prepared a 4mm × 10mm osteotomy. Standardized: 800 rpm, 2 kg axial load, 50 mL/min saline irrigation at 23°C; room temperature 23 ± 1°C. Infrared thermal camera (FLIR E6-XT, 14-bit, 240×180 pixels, ε=0.96 for bone) at fixed 20 cm perpendicular distance. Performance monitored in four consecutive 10-drill subgroups. Sample size by G*Power (power 0.8, α=0.05, f=0.25 one-way ANOVA).

## Key Contributions

- Ceramic (zirconia) drills generate significantly more heat and require 70% longer drilling time vs stainless steel — due to low thermal conductivity and prolonged bone-tool contact
- All systems safe below 47°C threshold under adequate irrigation, but ceramic showed highest absolute peak (33.80°C) with widest variance
- One ceramic drill fractured at 26th use — mechanical brittleness is a clinical safety concern in addition to thermal risk
- Conventional SS and velodrill offer the most stable thermal and temporal profiles; BE performs comparably despite non-cutting displacement mechanism
- Cumulative use analysis: ceramic drill performance variability increases over repetitions, suggesting 5–10 use limit vs 15–20 for other systems (hypothesis for future validation)

## Methodology

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Design | In vitro, comparative, 4 groups |
| Bone model | Fresh bovine rib cortical bone (mid-diaphyseal segments, 2mm thickness) |
| Groups | CS (Straumann SS 5-step), VD (Straumann Velodrill 6-step), CD (Z-Systems ceramic 4-step), BE (Legend Group 8-step) |
| n per group | 40 osteotomies |
| Osteotomy dimensions | 4mm diameter × 10mm depth |
| Speed | 800 rpm constant |
| Axial load | 2 kg |
| Irrigation | 50 mL/min saline, 23°C, external |
| Temperature measurement | FLIR E6-XT infrared camera; surface measurement before and after each drilling |
| Statistical analysis | Shapiro-Wilk normality; Kruskal-Wallis + Dunn's multiple comparisons |

## Results

| System | Mean Temp (°C) | Range (°C) | Mean Time (s) | Range (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CS (stainless steel) | 23.79 ± 1.08 | 22.30–29.20 | 10.11 ± 5.94 | 4.58–35.62 |
| VD (velodrill) | 24.45 ± 1.15 | 22.90–28.20 | 10.24 ± 4.89 | 4.11–24.68 |
| CD (ceramic) | **24.82 ± 2.69** | **23.10–33.80** | **17.07 ± 6.82** | 3.51–31.08 |
| BE (bone-enhancing) | 24.40 ± 1.50 | 22.90–29.80 | 9.87 ± 4.68 | 5.30–23.51 |

**Pairwise temperature (1–40 total, Dunn's)**:
- CS vs VD: p<0.0001 (VD higher)
- CS vs BE: p=0.0125 (BE higher)
- CS vs CD: p=0.0723 (NS — but CD had highest absolute peak)
- CD vs others on drilling time: all p<0.0001

**Subgroup analysis** (reps 21–30): CS vs VD p<0.0001, CS vs BE p=0.0379 — temperature gap widens with drill use.

## Clinical Implications

- 세라믹 드릴은 임상에서 **다중 와위(연속 5와위 이상), 조밀 피질골(D1–D2), 세척 제한 상황**에서 재고 필요 — 낮은 열전도율이 발열 집적 유발
- 세라믹 드릴 파절 위험 → 5–10회 사용 후 점검, 이후 교체 권고(저자 제안, 임상시험 대기 중)
- Velodrill·BE 시스템은 CS와 유사한 열 프로파일 + 짧은 드릴링 시간 → 임상적으로 동등하게 안전
- 47°C 이하라도 반복 다중 와위에서 온도 누적 효과 고려 — 충분한 세척 + 간헐 드릴링 프로토콜 병행
