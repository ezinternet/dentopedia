## Three-line Summary

Standardized in vitro study (Medical University of Vienna, n=240 preparations) compared static computer-assisted implant surgery (정적 컴퓨터 유도 임플란트 수술, S-CAIS) vs conventional implant preparation (일반 임플란트 와위 형성, CIP) using a CNC apparatus (Lloyd LS1) and automated intermittent protocol in BoneSim bovine bone, measuring temperature at multiple depths with K-type thermocouple arrays.

Significant thermal differences between S-CAIS and CIP occurred only with 3.5mm drills: CIP was hotter without irrigation at 10mm depth (17.36 vs 15.05°C, p=0.0115) while S-CAIS was hotter with irrigation at 12mm depth (9.14 vs 6.00°C, p=0.0101); for 2.2mm and 2.8mm drills, both techniques were statistically equivalent.

External irrigation dramatically reduced temperature for both techniques regardless of diameter or depth — all values stayed safely below 47°C with irrigation, and all exceeded this threshold without irrigation.

## 세줄요약

인비트로 (빈의과대, 240 와위, CNC 장치) — S-CAIS vs CIP, 3가지 직경(2.2/2.8/3.5mm) × 2 깊이(10/12mm) × 세척 유무 × 기법 2종; K형 열전대 다층 배열(3/6/9/12/15mm 깊이).

유의한 기법간 차이는 **3.5mm 드릴에서만**: 10mm/무세척에서 CIP가 더 뜨겁고(p=0.0115), 12mm/유세척에서 S-CAIS가 더 뜨거움(p=0.0101); 2.2/2.8mm는 양 기법 동등.

외부 세척은 기법·직경에 관계없이 온도를 극적으로 낮춤 — 세척 있으면 전부 47°C 이하, 없으면 전부 초과.

## Summary

Medical University of Vienna, Department of Prosthodontics and Dental Material Sciences. First fully automated protocol for implant surgery heat comparison, eliminating inter-operator variability. CNC material testing apparatus (Lloyd LS1): standardized 800 rpm rotation, 30 rpm intermittent (5s drilling/5s pause), 2.2N axial load. BoneSim composite bovine bone blocks (56×18×10mm, type-2 density, 3mm cortical + 15mm cancellous layer). Straumann PRO stainless-steel twist drills (2.2, 2.8, 3.5mm). Multiple type-K thermocouple sensor arrays at 1mm and 2mm from osteotomy; measurement depths 3/6/9/12/15mm from surface. 240 total preparations: 10 repetitions per constellation (3 diameters × 2 depths × 2 irrigation × 2 techniques).

## Key Contributions

- S-CAIS is NOT globally hotter than CIP — significance depends on drill diameter, depth, and irrigation
- Problematic interaction: at 3.5mm diameter + 12mm depth + WITH irrigation, S-CAIS exceeds CIP (9.14 vs 6.00°C ΔT, p=0.0101) — the guide sleeve impedes external coolant delivery efficiency at large diameter and depth
- CIP shows reverse advantage at 3.5mm without irrigation at 10mm depth (17.36 vs 15.05°C, p=0.0115) — free coolant access eliminates this when irrigation is restored
- 2.2mm and 2.8mm drills: S-CAIS and CIP are thermally equivalent regardless of depth and irrigation
- External irrigation is the dominant factor — more important than technique choice for preventing thermal necrosis

## Results

### Temperature ΔT (°C from baseline), Without Irrigation

| Diameter | Depth | S-CAIS | CIP | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.2mm | 10mm | 13.06 | 13.62 | NS |
| 2.2mm | 12mm | ~14 | ~14 | NS |
| 2.8mm | 10mm | 16.70 | 16.54 | NS |
| 2.8mm | 12mm | ~17 | ~17 | NS |
| **3.5mm** | **10mm** | **15.05** | **17.36** | **p=0.0115** |
| 3.5mm | 12mm | ~19 | ~20 | NS |

### Temperature ΔT (°C from baseline), With External Irrigation

| Diameter | Depth | S-CAIS | CIP | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.2mm | 10mm | ~4 | ~4 | NS |
| 2.2mm | 12mm | ~5 | ~5 | NS |
| 2.8mm | 10mm | ~6 | ~5 | NS |
| 2.8mm | 12mm | ~7 | ~6 | NS |
| 3.5mm | 10mm | ~8 | ~7 | NS |
| **3.5mm** | **12mm** | **9.14** | **6.00** | **p=0.0101** |

**47°C threshold**: All preparations exceeded threshold WITHOUT irrigation; all stayed safely below WITH irrigation.

**Depth of peak temperature**: shifts deeper with irrigation (superficial layers cooled preferentially).

## Clinical Implications

- **3.5mm 이상 드릴 + 12mm 이상 깊이 가이드 수술**에서는 외부 세척 전달 효율이 저하 — 세척 유량 증량 또는 냉세척(10°C) 병행 권고
- 2.2mm·2.8mm 드릴 범위에서 S-CAIS는 CIP와 열적으로 동등 — 정확도 장점을 위해 가이드 사용 시 추가 열 위험 없음
- 외부 세척은 **기법 선택보다 우선하는 요인** — 세척 유무 효과가 기법 차이보다 훨씬 크다
- 무세척 상태에서 3.5mm/10mm에서 CIP가 S-CAIS보다 더 뜨거운 점은 가이드가 드릴 냉각보다 클리어런스 손실이 적을 수 있음을 시사 (직경·깊이 조합에 따라 역전)
- 임상 적용 요약: **피질골 전층(≥10mm) + 대직경(3.5mm)에서 가이드 수술 시 외부 세척을 특히 의식적으로 관리**; 그 외 조건은 기법 중립

## Methodology

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Design | In vitro, automated, fully controlled |
| Bone model | BoneSim composite bovine (D2-type, 3mm cortical + 15mm cancellous) |
| CNC apparatus | Lloyd LS1; 800 rpm; 30 rpm intermittent (5s/5s) |
| Drills | Straumann PRO SS: 2.2, 2.8, 3.5mm |
| Osteotomy depths | 10mm and 12mm |
| Temperature sensors | K-type thermocouple arrays; 1mm and 2mm from osteotomy; depths 3/6/9/12/15mm |
| n per condition | 10 repetitions |
| Total preparations | 240 |
| Statistical analysis | Mixed model repeated measures; significance p<0.05 |

## Limitations

- In vitro only: no vascularity, no physiological temperature, no patient-specific cortical/cancellous variation
- Room temperature conditions (not 37°C body temperature — adds ~2–3°C buffer vs in vivo)
- Closed guide sleeve only (one design); open guide or different sleeve diameter not tested
- 10mm and 12mm depth only — shallower insertions (6–8mm) not directly covered
