## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo study (40 sheep iliac crest specimens, D3 bone, 192 implant sites) comparing three guided surgery protocols — high-speed 900 rpm/24°C irrigation, low-speed 300–600 rpm/no irrigation, and high-speed/10°C cold irrigation — measuring intraosseous temperature with K-type thermocouples at 100ms sampling.

Cold irrigation (10°C) was the only significantly different group, producing the lowest mean temperature (28°C) vs 24°C (30.5°C) and no-irrigation (31°C); critically, low-speed drilling without irrigation offered NO thermal advantage and caused 8 specimens to exceed the +10°C critical threshold at 600 rpm.

Guided surgery is thermally safe under effective external irrigation; the popular "low-speed without irrigation" belief is unsupported and demonstrably harmful in D3 bone.

## 세줄요약

양 장골능 엑스비보(n=40, D3, 696 HU, 총 192 와위) — 3군 비교: A군(900rpm/24°C 세척), B군(300–600rpm/세척없음), C군(900rpm/10°C 냉세척); K형 열전대 100ms 샘플링.

냉세척(C군) 유일하게 유의하게 낮음(28°C), A군(30.5°C)·B군(31°C)은 유의차 없음; B군 600rpm에서 8개 시편 +10°C 임계치 초과 — "저속무세척=안전" 믿음은 실증적으로 틀렸다.

가이드 수술은 충분한 외부 세척 하에서 열적으로 안전; 냉세척(10°C)이 가이드 수술에서 가장 효과적인 냉각 전략.

## Summary

Sakarya University / Istanbul University Faculty of Dentistry. Ex vivo study using 40 fresh sheep iliac crest bone specimens (D3 density, CBCT-confirmed mean 696 HU). Total 192 implant sites. Stereolithographic surgical guide fabricated with Real Guide 5.0 software. Four guided implant sizes: 2.9×8.5mm, 2.9×13mm, 3.8×8.5mm, 3.8×13mm; control: 3.5×10mm conventional. K-type thermocouple 1mm from each osteotomy site, 100ms data acquisition interval.

Three groups:
- **Group A** (n=80): Guided + conventional, 900 rpm, 24°C irrigation (100 mL/min)
- **Group B** (n=80): Guided + conventional, 300–600 rpm, no irrigation
- **Group C** (n=36): Guided + conventional, 900 rpm, 10°C irrigation

## Key Contributions

- Cold irrigation (10°C) is the most effective single intervention for thermal management in guided implant surgery — significantly lower than both standard irrigation and no-irrigation protocols
- Low-speed drilling without irrigation is NOT thermally safe and exceeded the +10°C critical threshold in 8/40 specimens at 600 rpm; 5/40 exceeded it at 300 rpm
- Guided surgery is thermally safe under adequate external irrigation — the guide per se does not necessitate protocol modification beyond effective coolant delivery
- Under cold irrigation: wider bur diameter (3.8mm) at 8.5mm depth did NOT significantly increase temperature — cold irrigation masks the diameter effect at shallower depths
- Larger diameter and greater depth increased temperature in ALL groups; cold irrigation mitigates but does not eliminate these effects at 13mm depth

## Results

| Group | Protocol | Mean Temp (°C) | Significantly Different |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 900 rpm + 24°C irrigation | 30.5 ± 4 | — |
| B | 300–600 rpm + no irrigation | 31 ± 5 | NS vs A |
| **C** | **900 rpm + 10°C irrigation** | **28 ± 3** | **p=0.001 vs A and B** |

**Critical threshold exceedances in Group B (no irrigation)**:
- 600 rpm: 8/40 specimens exceeded +10°C
- 300 rpm: 5/40 specimens exceeded +10°C

**Diameter effect**: 3.8mm vs 2.9mm significantly higher in all groups (p=0.001)

**Depth effect**: 13mm > 8.5mm in all groups; Group C attenuated but did not eliminate depth-related increase at 13mm

**Key interaction**: Under 10°C irrigation, wider diameter (3.8mm) at 8.5mm depth showed no significant temperature increase — cold irrigation neutralizes diameter effect at shallow depth

## Clinical Implications

- **냉세척(10°C)** 은 가이드 수술에서 가장 적극적인 열 관리 전략으로 권고 가능 — 기존 24°C 생리식염수 대비 유의한 온도 저하
- **"저속 무세척" 프로토콜은 임상적으로 금기** — D3 골 기준으로 600rpm 세척없음이 47°C를 넘는 경우 발생(+10°C ≥ 37°C 기저 = ≥47°C 도달 가능); 가이드 수술에서 더욱 위험
- 가이드 수술 자체는 충분한 외부 세척이 유지되는 한 열적으로 CIP 대비 크게 불리하지 않음 — 가이드 가 세척 경로를 막는 경우는 예외
- 깊은 와위(13mm 이상) + 큰 직경(3.8mm) 동시 조합은 냉세척 하에서도 추가적 주의 필요 (온도 감소 효과가 약화)
- 치밀도(D3, HU 696) 환경에서 도출된 결과 — D1·D2 피질골에서는 더 보수적 접근 필요

## Methodology

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Design | Ex vivo, 3-group comparative |
| Bone model | Fresh sheep iliac crest, D3, 696 HU (CBCT-verified) |
| n | 40 specimens, 192 total osteotomy sites |
| Groups | A: 900rpm+24°C / B: 300-600rpm+no irrigation / C: 900rpm+10°C |
| Implant sizes | 2.9×8.5, 2.9×13, 3.8×8.5, 3.8×13mm guided; 3.5×10mm conventional control |
| Guide system | SLA stereolithographic (Real Guide 5.0) |
| Temperature sensor | K-type thermocouple at 1mm from osteotomy, 100ms interval |
| Statistical analysis | ANOVA (normal distribution) or Kruskal-Wallis + Tukey/Dunn's post-hoc |

## Limitations

- Ex vivo: no vascularity, no physiological body temperature (±1°C room temp not body 37°C)
- No standardized cortical/cancellous thickness ratio across specimens
- Single guide design — different guide openness may alter coolant delivery significantly
- Temperature measured at fixed 1mm distance — peak bone surface temperature not captured
