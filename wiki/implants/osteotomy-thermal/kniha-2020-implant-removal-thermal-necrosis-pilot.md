## Three-line Summary

Porcine cadaver pilot (Clin Oral Investig 2020; 14 pig heads, 140 drill samples, groups of n=10): two tempering pistons applied 49–56°C/10s (warm) and 5–1°C/30s (cold) to Camlog 3.8 mm osteotomy sites; histomorphometry (osteocyte condition 0–3; matrix degeneration 0–3) + SEM/EDX/TEM.

**51°C/10s** = matrix degeneration threshold (significant vs control, p<0.01); **5°C/30s** = cold matrix degeneration threshold; osteocyte condition not significantly affected at 10 s even at 56°C; 53°C→decalcification (Ca/P rise) + mitochondrial swelling; 56°C→ER dilation; jaw location (maxilla vs mandible) weakly correlated, not significant.

Time compression finding: matrix-level damage occurs at 51°C/10s, substantially shorter than canonical 47°C/1min — consistent with the temperature×time reciprocal relationship; cadaver model (no blood flow) means true in vivo thresholds are likely higher.

## 세줄요약

돼지 사체 (Porcine Cadaver) 파일럿 연구 (Clin Oral Investig 2020; 돼지머리 14개, 천공 샘플 140개, 그룹당 n=10): 온열 가열기(49–56°C/10초)와 냉각 가열기(5–1°C/30초)를 Camlog 3.8 mm 골 형성 (Osteotomy) 부위에 적용; 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry) (골세포 (Osteocyte) 상태 0–3; 기질 변성 (Matrix Degeneration) 0–3) + 주사전자현미경 (SEM)/에너지분산형X선분석 (EDX)/투과전자현미경 (TEM).

**51°C/10초** = 기질 변성 임계값 (p<0.01 유의하게 증가); **5°C/30초** = 냉각 기질 변성 임계값; 골세포 조건은 10초 노출에서는 56°C에서도 유의하게 악화되지 않음; 53°C→탈석회화 (Ca/P 상승) + 미토콘드리아 팽윤; 56°C→소포체 (ER) 확장; 상·하악 위치 상관성: 미약(유의하지 않음).

시간 압축 결론: 기질 손상은 51°C/10초에서 발생 — 정준 임계값 47°C/1분보다 훨씬 짧은 노출 시간; 온도×시간 역수 관계 (Reciprocal Relationship) 로 설명 가능; 사체 모델(혈류 없음) 한계로 실제 생체 내 임계값은 더 높을 가능성.

## Summary

This in vitro pilot study (Aachen group, same team as the concurrent SR) tested porcine cadaver jaw bones to identify temperature/time thresholds suitable for a controlled thermo-explantation procedure — targeted intentional osteonecrosis to loosen osseointegrated implants without extensive jaw damage. The rationale: existing case reports of accidental implant loosening after uncontrolled heating suggested the concept is viable, but no controlled threshold data existed for a clinically safe implementation.

Custom tempering pistons fit the 3.8 mm Camlog osteotomy sites and held precise temperatures via thermocouple feedback. Main findings: **51°C for 10 seconds** causes significant bone matrix degeneration (but not necessarily osteocyte death by count), establishing the minimum warm threshold for this application window. Cold thermo-explantation potential also exists: **5°C for 30 seconds** causes significant matrix degeneration via cryo-injury.

A key observation is the dissociation between osteocyte count (less sensitive at 10 s) and matrix degeneration (more sensitive) — matrix degeneration scores were the more reliable endpoint for short-duration exposures. At 53°C, EDX shows decalcification (rising Ca/P) and TEM shows mitochondrial injury; at 56°C, the ER is additionally stressed. These ultrastructural findings validate that the histomorphometric scores reflect genuine cellular damage.

The critical limitation: absence of blood flow in cadaver tissue. Blood flow in living bone dissipates heat faster, meaning the in vivo threshold for the same tissue damage is likely higher than 51°C/10s. The planned rat in vivo study (published as Kniha 2023) tests whether these thresholds produce actual implant loosening in vascularized bone.

## Clinical and Research Implications

1. **For thermo-explantation**: 51°C/10s is the minimum warm threshold in cadaveric (avascular) porcine jaw; actual clinical protocol will need in vivo validation — likely higher temperature or longer duration required due to blood flow cooling.
2. **For drilling safety (inverse inference)**: brief transient peaks >51°C during osteotomy preparation may not cause sustained matrix damage if exposure is <10 s; duration matters as much as temperature.
3. **Cold thermo-explantation is feasible** but under-studied; 5°C/30s threshold suggests cryoprobe-based explantation warrants investigation.
4. **Bone-type consideration**: study found no significant jaw-location effect (maxilla vs mandible), but small sample size limits this conclusion; Kniha 2020 SR's finding of cancellous > cortical susceptibility at 1 min may not apply equally at 10 s.
