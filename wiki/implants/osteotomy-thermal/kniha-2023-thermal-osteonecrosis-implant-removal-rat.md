## Three-line Summary

In vivo rat tibia study (48 Sprague–Dawley rats, 96 mini-screws, 6 temperatures 2–4°C/48–50°C × 1 min) measuring EDX mineralogy, TEM osteocyte morphology, ISQ, and radiographic bone level at baseline and 7 days to identify candidate thermoexplantation thresholds.

50°C/1 min caused irreversible osteocyte necrosis on TEM and significant EDX increases in calcium (25.31 wt%), phosphate (12.40 wt%), sodium, and sulfur (all p < 0.01); 2°C was the most damaging cold temperature; ISQ and radiographic bone loss trends were present but not statistically significant over 7 days.

This preliminary animal study validates 50°C/60 s as the candidate threshold for controlled thermoexplantation; non-osseointegrated screws in a rat model limit direct clinical translation, necessitating a follow-up pig study with osseointegrated implants.

## 세줄요약

In vivo 쥐 경골 연구 (48마리, 96개 미니나사, 2–4°C·48–50°C 각 1분) — EDX 무기질 조성, TEM 골세포 형태, ISQ (임플란트 안정성 지수, Implant Stability Quotient), 방사선 골높이를 수술 직후·7일에 측정해 열폭발적제거 후보 임계값 탐색.

50°C/1분에서 비가역적 골세포 괴사 (TEM: 공 소강), EDX 칼슘·인산염·나트륨·황 유의 증가(p < 0.01); 냉각 중에서는 2°C가 가장 심한 손상; ISQ 감소·방사선 골소실 증가 경향이나 7일 이내 유의차 없음.

50°C/60초를 열폭발적제거 (Thermoexplantation) 임계값 후보로 확인; 비골유착 나사·단기 7일 추적의 한계 — 골유착 임플란트 돼지 모델 후속 연구가 계획됨.

## Summary

This in vivo rat study from RWTH Aachen (Kniha et al., 2023) is the first controlled live-animal experiment evaluating temperature–time combinations for thermoexplantation — the concept of deliberately inducing minimal peri-implant osteonecrosis to loosen osseointegrated implants for atraumatic removal. Six discrete temperatures (cold: 2, 3, 4°C; warm: 48, 49, 50°C) were applied for 1 min to rat tibiae before mini-screw insertion (contralateral untreated side = control). Endpoints were EDX bone mineral composition, TEM osteocyte morphology, Osstell ISQ (임플란트 안정성 지수, ISQ), and radiographic crestal bone level.

## Temperature Thresholds and Damage Grading

| Temperature | TEM Osteocyte Damage | EDX Signature | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4°C | Vacuolization, detachment | Minor changes | Moderate |
| 3°C | Vacuolization, shrinkage | Minor changes | Moderate |
| **2°C** | Necrosis, empty lacunae | Ca/P increase | **Severe (cold)** |
| 48°C | Partial injury, not uniform | Ca, P increase | Moderate-severe |
| 49°C | Vacuolization, shrinkage, detachment | Ca, P increase (p < 0.01) | Severe |
| **50°C** | Nearly complete necrosis, empty lacunae | Ca 25.31%, P 12.40% (p < 0.01) | **Irreversible** |

Hot > cold in severity. Within warm range: damage worsened monotonically with temperature increase.

## EDX Mineral Composition Findings

Energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (에너지분산형 X선 분석법, EDX) at 8 points per sample showed:
- **Calcium** and **phosphate**: significant increase from 48°C to 50°C (p < 0.01), and across cold gradient (3°C–50°C p = 0.01)
- **Ca/C ratio** (calcification index): increased with worsening thermal damage — marker of bone matrix calcification change in damaged tissue
- **Ca/P ratio**: increased, contrary to osteoporosis pattern (where Ca/P drops) — reflects thermal denaturization pattern
- **Carbon**: decreased parallel to calcium increase — organic matrix component reduction
- **Sulfur**: decreased from 4°C→2°C and 48°C→50°C (p < 0.01) — linked to connective tissue matrix disruption

## ISQ and Radiographic Findings

- **ISQ (임플란트 안정성 지수, ISQ)**: Non-significant decreasing trend at 48–50°C over 7 days. Inhomogeneous result at cold temperatures (3–4°C). Values are inherently lower than human implant ISQ due to mini-screw geometry — authors note this is a proof-of-concept that ISQ measurement is feasible in small animal models for the planned pig study.
- **Radiographic crestal bone loss**: Distance from implant shoulder to bone contact increased with temperature stimulus but did not reach significance at 7 days (p > 0.05). Authors note plain-film sensitivity for early osteonecrosis is only ~41%; CT/MRI recommended for future protocols.

## Clinical Relevance: Thermoexplantation Concept

Thermoexplantation (열폭발적제거) aims to produce minimal, controlled peri-implant osteonecrosis to loosen osseointegrated implants without the trauma of trephine burs or reverse-torque devices. Current clinical data (laser, ultra-high-frequency devices) use uncontrolled heat. This study fills a gap by establishing:

- **50°C/60 s** as the candidate threshold where thermal damage is maximal yet (in theory) controllable
- EDX and TEM as validation endpoints for damage confirmation
- ISQ as a potential non-invasive loosening detection metric in future studies

This does NOT mean 50°C is safe or ready for clinical use — the implants in this study were non-osseointegrated screws in a rat tibia, not dental implants in jaw bone. The authors explicitly state that a pig study with osseointegrated dental implants is the next required step.

## Limitations

- Non-osseointegrated mini-screws (not dental implants); actual thermoexplantation requires loosening of osseointegrated bone–implant contact
- Short 7-day follow-up — bone loss typically manifests at 3+ weeks post-necrosis (Eriksson & Albrektsson 1983)
- Rat tibia ≠ human jaw — bone density, geometry, and healing biology differ substantially
- ISQ values not comparable to human implant ISQ baselines (mini-screw adaptation)
- Single examiner; cold temperature cryoexplantation threshold still undefined
