## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (Malmö University, Medicina 2022) comprehensively evaluating the effect of smoking on both implant survival/failure rates and marginal bone loss (MBL) in smokers vs non-smokers.

Smokers showed significantly higher implant failure rates and significantly greater MBL than non-smokers, confirming a negative effect of smoking on both primary endpoints.

Smoking is a confirmed independent risk factor for both implant failure and bone loss — part of the convergent 2020–2024 SR+MA cluster (Naseri 2020, Mustapha 2022, Fan 2024) that forms the modern evidence base for smoking–implant counseling.

## Summary
SR+MA from Malmö University on smoking effect across implant outcomes (survival/failure + marginal bone loss). Confirms negative smoking effect on both endpoints. Part of the cluster of 2020-2024 smoking-implant SR+MAs (Naseri 2020, Mustapha 2022, Fan 2024) that collectively define the modern evidence base.

## Key Contributions
- Covers both failure and MBL outcomes (not just one).
- Adds to the convergent 2020-2024 SR+MA evidence cluster.

## Methodology
PRISMA SR+MA; pooled effect sizes for smoker vs non-smoker.

## Results
[근거강함] Smokers: higher implant failure and greater MBL vs non-smokers.

## Limitations
- Observational data, no RCT possible
- Smoking definition heterogeneity
