## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (9 comparative studies qualitative, 7 meta-analyzed; NDIs ≤3.5 mm, range 2.5–3.5 mm) comparing immediate vs early/delayed loading of narrow-diameter implants supporting fixed restorations (single crowns, FDPs, full-arch); search to January 2026, PROSPERO CRD420250654797, GRADE.

Implant failure did not differ by loading protocol short-term (<5 y: 4 studies/808 implants, RR 1.41, 95% CI 0.66–3.00, I²=0%) or long-term (≥5 y: 4 studies/502, RR 1.10, 95% CI 0.49–2.47, I²=0%); short-term MBL, biological (RR 0.81, 95% CI 0.10–6.66) and prosthetic complications comparable — certainty LOW to VERY LOW.

Immediate loading of NDIs is a viable, time-efficient option for fixed restorations in well-selected cases; a very-low-certainty signal of higher long-term biological complications with immediate loading (2 observational studies) is hypothesis-generating and mandates careful case selection.

SR+MA (정성 9편, 정량 7편; NDI ≤3.5 mm, 2.5–3.5 mm) — 고정성 보철(단일크라운·FDP·full-arch)을 지지하는 좁은 직경 임플란트(Narrow-Diameter Implant, NDI)의 즉시 하중 대 조기/지연 하중 비교; 검색 2026년 1월, PROSPERO CRD420250654797, GRADE.

## Summary

This SR+MA isolates a question the prior NDI literature left tangled: for narrow-diameter implants (≤3.5 mm) carrying *fixed* restorations, does immediate loading compromise outcomes versus early/delayed loading? Requiring direct within-study comparison, it captured 9 comparative studies (7 meta-analyzed; single crowns, FDPs, and full-arch; diameters 2.5–3.5 mm). Pooled implant-failure risk ratios were statistically indistinguishable between loading protocols in both the short (<5 y: RR 1.41) and long term (≥5 y: RR 1.10), with zero heterogeneity. Short-term marginal bone loss, biological, and prosthetic complications were likewise comparable. The one dissonant signal is long-term biological complications, which two observational studies reported more often in immediately loaded NDIs — but this rests on very-low-certainty evidence and is explicitly framed as hypothesis-generating. The overall GRADE certainty ceiling (LOW for survival, VERY LOW elsewhere), driven by non-randomized designs and sparse events, is the honest headline: immediate loading of NDIs looks safe and time-efficient in appropriately selected fixed-restoration cases, but primary stability, case selection, and long-term monitoring remain the operative safeguards.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to isolate the **loading-timing axis** (immediate vs early/delayed) specifically for NDIs supporting fixed restorations.
- No difference in pooled implant-failure risk by loading protocol, short- or long-term, with I²=0%.
- Identifies the **long-term biological-complication signal** as the key gap (higher with immediate loading, hypothesis-generating only).
- Transparent GRADE downgrading exposes how thin the evidence base still is.

## Methodology

- PRISMA 2020 SR+MA; PROSPERO CRD420250654797; Cochrane Handbook.
- PICO: partially edentulous fixed NDI-supported restorations; immediate vs early/delayed loading; survival (primary), biological/prosthetic complications (secondary).
- NDI ≤3.5 mm (included 2.5–3.5 mm). MEDLINE/Embase/Scopus + grey literature to Jan 10, 2026.
- Included RCTs + non-randomized comparative studies + case series ≥10 patients; removable restorations excluded; required within-study loading comparison.
- RoB 2 (RCT) + ROBINS-I V2 (non-randomized); random-effects; RCT and non-randomized analyzed separately; pooling only with ≥3 comparable studies, otherwise narrative. GRADE for certainty.
- Included studies: Henn 2025, Kim 2024, Anitua 2020, Slagter 2020, Altinci 2016, MacLean 2016, Malo 2011, Degidi 2009 (RCT), Degidi 2008.

## Results

- **Implant failure <5 y**: 4 studies / 808 implants — RR 1.41 (95% CI 0.66–3.00), I²=0%; GRADE LOW.
- **Implant failure ≥5 y**: 4 studies / 502 implants — RR 1.10 (95% CI 0.49–2.47; p=0.73), I²=0% (Q=0.88, p=0.83); GRADE LOW.
- **MBL <5 y**: 3 studies / 345 implants (91 IL / 254 E-DL) — narrative, comparable; GRADE VERY LOW. (≥5 y: 1 study → narrative.)
- **Biological complications <5 y**: 3 studies / 795 implants (316 IL / 479 E-DL), only 2 events — RR 0.81 (95% CI 0.10–6.66; p=0.71), I²=0%; GRADE VERY LOW.
- **Biological complications ≥5 y**: 2 studies / 242 implants (95 IL / 147 E-DL), 30 events — narrative; both suggest **higher incidence with immediate loading**; hypothesis-generating.
- **Prosthetic complications <5 y**: 3 studies / 817 implants (328 IL / 489 E-DL), 5 events — narrative, comparable; GRADE VERY LOW. (≥5 y: 1 study → narrative.)
