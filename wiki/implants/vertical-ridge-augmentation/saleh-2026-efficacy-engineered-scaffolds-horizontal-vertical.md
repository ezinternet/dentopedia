## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD42024611887; 13 studies = 6 single-center RCTs + 7 prospective case series, 241 patients, 202 defects; 9 studies pooled) of patient-specific CAD/CAM "engineered scaffolds" for staged horizontal and vertical ridge augmentation; 9 of 13 used customized titanium mesh.

Pooled bone gain at 6-9 months was 4.96 mm horizontal (95% CI 4.48-5.44), 5.64 mm vertical (5.26-6.02) and 1307 mm3 volumetric (1000-1700), but overall complication rate was 21.7% (early 24.3%, late 16.5%); meta-regression linked light smoking (about -2.4 mm horizontal) and expert surgeons (+2.42 mm) to outcome, and GRADE certainty was moderate for linear gain but low for volume and complications.

Customized scaffolds regenerate substantial bone in all dimensions, but the high complication rate makes results unpredictable, so use should be restricted to selected cases (non-smokers, experienced operators); evidence is dominated by titanium mesh, with few RCTs and short follow-up.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA; 단일기관 RCT 6편 + 전향적 증례군 7편, 환자 241명, 결손 202개, 정량합성 9편)으로 환자맞춤형 CAD/CAM 스캐폴드(engineered scaffold)를 이용한 2단계 수평·수직 치조제 증대술을 분석했고, 13편 중 9편이 맞춤형 티타늄 메쉬 (titanium mesh)였다.

6-9개월 시점 합산 골 증가량은 수평 4.96 mm, 수직 5.64 mm, 체적 1307 mm3였지만 전체 합병증률은 21.7% (조기 24.3%, 후기 16.5%)였고, 메타회귀에서 경도 흡연(수평 약 -2.4 mm)과 숙련 술자(+2.42 mm)가 결과와 연관되었으며 GRADE 근거 확실성은 선형 골 증가량 '중등도', 체적·합병증은 '낮음'이었다.

맞춤형 스캐폴드는 모든 방향에서 상당한 골 증가를 얻지만 합병증이 많아 예측성이 낮으므로 비흡연자·숙련 술자 등 선택된 증례에 한정해야 하며, 근거는 티타늄 메쉬 중심이고 RCT가 적고 추적기간이 짧다.

## Summary

This SR+MA pooled 13 clinical studies (6 single-center RCTs, 7 prospective case series; 241 patients, 202 defects, mean age 47.1 years) of CAD/CAM-designed "engineered scaffolds" used in staged horizontal and vertical alveolar ridge augmentation: nine customized titanium meshes, two PEEK shells, one 3D hydroxyapatite block and one customized allogeneic bone block (CABB). Most grafts were autogenous bone mixed with xenograft (11 of 13 studies). Pooled gains at 6-9 months were 4.96 mm (horizontal), 5.64 mm (vertical) and 1307 mm3 (volumetric), with an overall complication rate of 21.7%. Light smoking, less experienced operators and defect type modulated bone gain. The authors conclude that scaffolds are promising for complex defects but that the high complication rate limits them to selected cases and experienced surgeons.

## Key Contributions

- First pooled estimate of patient-specific scaffold outcomes across materials, including volumetric (CBCT) gain, with early/late complication rates and GRADE ratings.
- Identifies modifiers via mixed-effects meta-regression: light smoking reduced gain (horizontal about -2.4 mm, p = 0.031; vertical multivariable -0.78 mm, p = 0.01); expert operators gained 2.42 mm more horizontally than graduate students (p = 0.021); combined defects gained more vertically (+3.11 mm) and volumetrically (+720 mm3) than purely horizontal ones.
- Shows the resorbable-membrane question is unresolved: membrane use was associated with higher horizontal (5.64 vs 2.48 mm) and volumetric (1715 vs 900 mm3) gain but lower vertical gain (4.26 vs 6.36 mm), with no significant difference in early or late complications.
- Bone-graft-material angle: no head-to-head graft comparison existed; autogenous particles plus xenograft were associated with +745 mm3 volumetric gain versus 3D hydroxyapatite plus xenograft, and only one CABB study (allogeneic) was available.

## Methodology

PRISMA 2020 review (PROSPERO CRD42024611887) searching MEDLINE, EMBASE, Scopus and Cochrane CENTRAL plus grey literature (16,539 records; 10,509 screened). Eligible: RCTs, prospective clinical studies and prospective case series with >= 4 months follow-up and >= 8 patients. Random-effects REML pooling; proportional meta-analysis for complications; mixed-effects meta-regression with FDR correction; RoB 2 and ROBINS-I V2; GRADE. Nine studies entered quantitative analysis (3 horizontal, 5 vertical, 5 volumetric).

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Horizontal bone gain | 4.96 mm (4.48-5.44); with resorbable membrane 5.64 mm vs without 2.48 mm |
| Vertical bone gain (145 cases) | 5.64 mm (5.26-6.02); without membrane 6.36 mm vs with 4.26 mm |
| Volumetric gain (83 cases) | 1307 mm3 (1000-1700); with membrane 1715 mm3 vs without 900 mm3 |
| Overall complications | 21.7% (16.4-27.0) |
| Early (< 4 wk) | 24.3% (17.8-30.8); no membrane 25.6% vs membrane 21.4%, not significant |
| Late (>= 4 wk) | 16.5% (7.3-25.7); no membrane 18.0% vs membrane 11.1%, not significant |
| Risk of bias | 3/6 RCTs low risk; all non-randomized studies moderate-to-serious |
| GRADE | Moderate (horizontal, vertical); low (volumetric, complications) |

Limitations noted by the authors: titanium-dominated evidence, only one RCT against a gold-standard comparator (Ti-reinforced d-PTFE mesh), short follow-up, few PROMs, exploratory material subgroups. Some values differ slightly between Abstract, Results and Discussion, so subgroup figures are approximate.
