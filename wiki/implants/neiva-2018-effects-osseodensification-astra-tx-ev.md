## Three-line Summary

Conference e-poster abstract (sheep iliac bone, 6 animals, n=36/system) comparing osseodensification (OD, Densah CCW), Densah CW, and standard drilling (R) on Astra TX and EV implant systems at 6 weeks.

OD substantially raised insertion torque (IT) and RFA regardless of implant system; however, BIC and BAFO improved mainly for the EV (newer, aggressive-thread) system — the TX system showed minimal BIC/BAFO difference between drilling protocols.

OD efficacy on histological osseointegration outcomes is implant-design dependent, not universal — the interaction between osteotomy protocol and implant macrogeometry matters; caveat: abstract only, no quantitative values reported.

EAO 2018 e-poster abstract (양, 6마리, 장골, n=36/시스템) — Astra TX vs EV 임플란트에 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD, Densah CCW) vs Densah CW vs 표준 절삭 (R) 비교, 6주 관찰.

삽입 토크 (Insertion Torque, IT)·공명주파수분석 (RFA)은 OD가 시스템 무관 압도적 우위; 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)/BAFO는 EV(신형·공격적 나사산)에서 OD > R 대폭 우위, TX는 프로토콜 간 차이 미미.

OD의 조직학적 골유착 효과는 임플란트 설계 의존적 — 골삭제 프로토콜과 임플란트 거대형태 간 상호작용이 중요; 단, e-poster abstract로 정량 수치 미보고, 해석 주의.

## Summary
Conference e-poster abstract (Clinical Oral Implants Research Vol 29 Suppl S17) reporting sheep iliac-bone study comparing osseodensification (Densah CCW), Densah clockwise, and standard subtractive drilling on Astra TX and EV implant systems.

## Key Contributions
Macrogeometry × osteotomy-protocol interaction effect: Astra EV (newer, more aggressive thread) benefits substantially from OD on BIC/BAFO; the older TX system does not. Suggests OD efficacy is implant-design dependent, not universal.

## Methodology
6 male sheep, 3 implants of each type (TX, EV) bilaterally in iliac bone, n=36/system. Three osteotomy protocols per implant: regular drilling (R) with 2.0/3.2/3.8 mm twist drills; Densah CW (2.0/2.8/3.8 mm multifluted, clockwise); Densah CCW (osseodensification). IT and RFA at placement, 6-week healing, histology.

## Results
Densah protocols showed substantially higher IT and RFA than R, independent of implant system. TX: minimal difference between R and Densah on BIC/BAFO. EV: large advantage for Densah over R on BIC/BAFO. EV > TX across all protocols on BIC/BAFO. Histology: autogenous bone chip debris in trabecular spaces and in contact with implant in OD groups, rarely with R.

## Limitations
Conference abstract — no peer-reviewed full paper. Small n (6 animals). Sheep ilium model, not jaw bone. No quantitative values (IT, RFA, BIC%) reported in abstract — qualitative only. 6-week timepoint only; no long-term outcome. No statistical detail beyond "substantially higher".
