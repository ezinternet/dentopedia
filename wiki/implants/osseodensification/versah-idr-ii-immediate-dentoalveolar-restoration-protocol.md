## Three-line Summary

Versah IDR II manufacturer protocol (2-page, REV06, narrative-review) for severely compromised sockets with full buccal-wall loss and thin biotype/gingival recession: atraumatic extraction → Densah OD prep → palatal-wall implant anchorage → triple graft from maxillary tuberosity → screw-retained provisional.

Outcome is single-stage rehabilitation of a high-risk esthetic socket relying on palatal-wall primary stability and the combined bone + soft-tissue (triple) graft for buccal-wall reconstruction; cemented restorations are explicitly contraindicated.

Operator-dependent technique (tuberosity harvest, sole palatal anchorage); not peer-reviewed and outcome variance is unquantified in the protocol document.

## 세줄요약

Versah IDR II 프로토콜(2페이지, REV06) — 협측골 완전 소실 + 얇은 biotype/치은퇴축 증례에 대한 즉시 임플란트 식립 적응증의 제조사 프로토콜.

무외상 발치 + Densah OD 버 준비 → **구개벽(palatal wall)에서만 1차 안정성 확보** → 상악 결절에서 골+연조직 합성 triple graft 채취·충전 → 즉시 스크루 유지 임시보철(cement 방식 금지) → 최소 4개월 치유 후 최종 screw-retained 크라운.

제조사 마케팅 문서로 동료 심사 미통과; 결절 채취 및 구개벽 단독 고정이 술자 의존적이며 임상 결과 수치 미제시로 근거 수준이 낮다.

## Summary
Versah Immediate Dentoalveolar Restoration II manufacturer protocol (2-page, REV06) for severely compromised sockets with full buccal wall loss and thin/recessed soft tissue — single-procedure implant + bone/soft-tissue graft + provisional.

## Key Contributions
Vendor reference protocol for the IDR II indication: severe socket compromise (no buccal wall + thin biotype + recession). Differs from IDR I by harvesting a combined bone + soft tissue (triple) graft from maxillary tuberosity, with mandatory screw-retained provisional (cemented contraindicated).

## Methodology
Six-step clinical protocol: atraumatic extraction + degranulation → Densah OD prep → palatal wall anchorage → triple graft harvest → screw-retained provisional → 4-month healing → screw-retained final crown.

## Results
Protocol outcome: single-stage rehabilitation of high-risk esthetic socket; relies on palatal wall implant anchorage and triple graft for buccal wall reconstruction. Cemented restorations explicitly contraindicated.

## Limitations
Manufacturer marketing document — not peer-reviewed. Highly operator-dependent (triple graft harvest from tuberosity, primary stability solely from palatal wall). Outcome variance not quantified in protocol. Cross-reference [[darosa-2019-immediate-dentoalveolar-restoration-osseodensification-periodontal]] for the peer-reviewed clinical IDR rationale.
