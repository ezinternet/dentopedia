## Three-line Summary

Minipig tibial-condyle model (6 Gottinger minipigs, 56 SLA ITI cylinder implants): sites prepared by conventional drilling (control A) vs the osteotome technique (experimental B); evaluation at 7 and 28 days by resonance frequency measurement (RFM), removal torque and ground-section histology.

Biomechanical (removal-torque) stability was significantly higher in the drilled control group at day 7 (P = 0.004) and remained 47% higher at day 28, while RFM showed no significant group difference; histology showed fractured peri-implant trabeculae in the osteotome group at day 7 but not at day 28.

The authors conclude that the decreased stability with the osteotome technique is based on peri-implant microfractures; this is the authors' interpretation, and the abstract reports no direct test of mechanism or any human data.

## Summary

Buchter et al. compared conventional drill preparation with the osteotome technique for SLA ITI cylinder implants (56 implants) in the cranial and caudal tibia condyle of six Gottinger minipigs. RFM was recorded at placement and sacrifice; half the animals were sacrificed at 7 and half at 28 days, followed by removal-torque testing and ground-section histology. Removal-torque stability was significantly higher in the drilled control group at day 7 (P = 0.004) and 47% higher at day 28, whereas RFM showed no significant group difference. Fractured peri-implant trabeculae were seen in the osteotome group at day 7 but not at day 28. The authors conclude that the reduced stability is based on peri-implant microfractures.

## Key Contributions
- Large-animal comparison of osteotome vs drilling with both RFM and removal torque.
- Histologic observation of peri-implant trabecular fractures after osteotome use, resolving by day 28.
- Authors' attribution of lower stability to microfractures (a conclusion, not a tested mechanism).

## Methodology
Six Gottinger minipigs; 56 implants in tibia condyles; control A (drills) vs experimental B (osteotome); RFM at placement and sacrifice; day-7 and day-28 sacrifice; removal torque and 30-50 micrometre ground sections. Abstract-only; per-group n not stated.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Removal-torque stability, day 7 | Control (drill) significantly higher than osteotome (P = 0.004) |
| Removal-torque stability, day 28 | Control remained 47% higher |

| Histology, day 7 | Fractured peri-implant trabeculae in osteotome group |
