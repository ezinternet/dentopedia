## Three-line Summary

Sheep in vivo study (Stauber et al. 2024, n=12, 3 and 12 weeks) isolating trabecular bone volume as an independent variable in osseodensification outcomes: osteotomies with intact trabecular bone (Exp.) vs surgically debridged trabecular bone (Neg. Ctrl), both receiving Versah Densah bur osseodensification and Ti-6Al-4V implants. Exp. group showed significantly higher %BIC (30.87% vs 11.42% at 3 wk; p=0.009) and %BAFO at both timepoints, with histological evidence of more advanced woven-to-lamellar bone remodeling at 12 weeks. Osseodensification without trabecular bone was safe (no necrosis, inflammation, or microfractures) but produced markedly inferior osseointegration outcomes.

## Summary

Trabecular bone volume at the osteotomy site is a critical determinant of osseodensification efficacy. This sheep ilium study directly tested this hypothesis by surgically removing trabecular bone from one osteotomy site per animal while leaving the contralateral site intact, with all sites receiving identical Versah Densah bur osseodensification protocol and identical machined-surface Ti-6Al-4V implants.

The mechanism identified: osseodensification burs operating in CCW rotation compact trabecular bone chips into osteotomy walls. These bone chip autografts act as nucleating surfaces at the bone–implant interface — providing a scaffold for mesenchymal cell adhesion, woven bone formation, and subsequent lamellar remodeling. Without trabecular bone, no chips are generated, eliminating this nucleating effect. As a result, %BIC and %BAFO were approximately 3× higher in the trabecular bone group vs the debrided control, with histological evidence of more advanced woven-to-lamellar conversion at 12 weeks in the Exp. group.

Critically, even the absence of trabecular bone produced no harmful effects — no necrosis, inflammation, microfractures, or dehiscence at either 3 or 12 weeks. This establishes a safety floor: osseodensification in primarily cortical bone does not cause harm, but significantly underperforms vs trabecular-bone-present sites.

## Key Contributions

- First pre-clinical study directly isolating trabecular bone presence as an independent variable in osseodensification outcomes
- Bone chip autograft nucleation mechanism confirmed: trabecular chips generated by Densah burs embed into osteotomy walls and accelerate woven bone → lamellar bone sequence
- %BIC approximately 3× higher with trabecular bone present at both 3 and 12 weeks
- Osseodensification without trabecular bone: safe (no adverse events) but inferior outcomes — addresses the Padhye 2020 evidence gap
- 12-week histology shows lamellar remodeling in Exp. group vs only early lamellar reorganization in Neg. Ctrl

## Methodology

Sheep (n=12) ilium model; bilateral within-animal design (Exp. vs Neg. Ctrl). Osseodensification: Versah Densah multifluted tapered bur 2.0→2.8→3.8 mm, 1100 rpm CCW, saline irrigation. Implant: Ti-6Al-4V 4×10 mm machined surface (Emfils, Brazil), 85 N·cm. Healing: 3 wk (n=6) and 12 wk (n=6). Histomorphometry: Stevenel's Blue + Van Gieson staining; %BIC and %BAFO (1 mm ROI); ImageJ; linear mixed model (SPSS v29).
