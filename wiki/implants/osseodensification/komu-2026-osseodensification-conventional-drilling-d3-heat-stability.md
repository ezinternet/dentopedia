## Three-line Summary

An ex vivo study of 60 implants in D3-density bovine rib bone showed osseodensification (골밀도화, OD) yielded significantly higher primary stability than conventional drilling (일반 드릴링, CD): mean 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 77.30 ±1.91 vs 74.68 ±2.05 (p<0.05). OD generated significantly more heat (Tmax 42.73 ±5.14°C vs 37.43 ±4.22°C; p<0.001), with the initial 2.3mm Densah bur producing peak values (45.45 ±5.05°C), and an inverse relationship observed between bur diameter and temperature. Both techniques remained below the critical 47°C 골괴사 임계값 (osteonecrosis threshold) under non-irrigated conditions, supporting OD safety in D3 bone.

## 세줄요약

D3 밀도 소 늑골에 60개 임플란트(골밀도화 30, 일반 드릴링 30)를 비관주 조건에서 식립한 결과, 골밀도화의 평균 임플란트 안정성 지수 (ISQ)가 77.30으로 일반 드릴링 74.68보다 유의하게 높았다(p<0.05). 최고온도(Tmax)는 골밀도화 42.73°C, 일반 드릴링 37.43°C로 골밀도화가 더 높았지만(p<0.001), 첫 2.3mm 버 단계(45.45°C)를 포함해 두 기법 모두 골괴사 임계값 47°C 미만을 유지했다. 버 직경이 커질수록 발열이 감소하는 역상관 관계가 확인됐으며, 비관주 조건에서도 D3 골질에 골밀도화를 안전하게 적용할 수 있음을 시사한다.

## Summary

Kömü et al. (2026) conducted an ex vivo comparative study to simultaneously evaluate primary stability and heat generation from 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD) versus 일반 드릴링 (Conventional Drilling, CD) under non-irrigated (dry) drilling conditions in D3-density bone. The non-irrigated design represents a deliberate worst-case safety test, as irrigation is routinely used clinically to dissipate heat.

**Study setup:** 60 implants placed in D3-density bovine rib bone specimens; 30 OD (Densah bur protocol, counterclockwise), 30 CD (standard sequential protocol); no irrigation. Primary stability measured by 공명 주파수 분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) expressed as ISQ. Heat recorded per drilling step by 적외선 체열검사 (Infrared Thermography).

**Key finding — stability:** OD ISQ 77.30 ±1.91 vs CD 74.68 ±2.05 (p<0.05) — statistically significant advantage consistent with prior literature.

**Key finding — heat:** OD Tmax 42.73 ±5.14°C vs CD 37.43 ±4.22°C (p<0.001) — OD generates more heat, likely due to bone compaction friction. Critically, both groups remained below 47°C, the threshold at which sustained temperature elevation causes thermal 골괴사 (osteonecrosis).

**Bur diameter gradient:** The initial 2.3mm Densah bur produced the highest heat (45.45 ±5.05°C); subsequent larger-diameter burs generated progressively less. This inverse correlation likely reflects that the initial small bur encounters the densest resistance per unit cutting area.

## Methodology

- **Model:** Ex vivo, D3-density bovine rib bone
- **n:** 60 implants (OD n=30, CD n=30)
- **Irrigation:** None (non-irrigated / dry drilling)
- **Stability:** RFA (ISQ scale 1–100; higher = greater stability)
- **Thermal:** Infrared thermography per sequential drilling step
- **OD protocol:** Densah burs (Versah) starting 2.3mm, counterclockwise rotation
- **Evidence level:** In vitro / ex vivo (animal model)
- **Data availability:** Abstract only (full text not in PMC as of 2026-10-19)

## Results

### Primary Stability

| Group | Mean ISQ | SD | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Osseodensification | 77.30 | ±1.91 | p<0.05 vs CD |
| Conventional Drilling | 74.68 | ±2.05 | — |

### Thermal Data

| Group / Step | Mean Tmax (°C) | SD | vs threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| OD overall | 42.73 | ±5.14 | <47°C ✓ |
| CD overall | 37.43 | ±4.22 | <47°C ✓ |
| 2.3mm Densah bur (initial OD step) | 45.45 | ±5.05 | <47°C ✓ |
| Osteonecrosis threshold | 47.00 | — | — |

- OD Tmax significantly higher than CD (p<0.001)
- Inverse correlation: larger bur diameter → lower heat
- Peak risk at smallest bur; risk decreases with each sequential step

### Interpretation

The ~2.6 ISQ advantage of OD over CD is clinically meaningful — particularly in D3 bone where primary stability is a key determinant of osseointegration success. The higher heat from OD reflects bone compaction mechanics (energy converts to friction heat) rather than cutting inefficiency. The 2.3mm bur at 45.45°C means a safety margin of only ~1.5°C before threshold in worst-case non-irrigated conditions; with standard irrigation this margin substantially widens.
