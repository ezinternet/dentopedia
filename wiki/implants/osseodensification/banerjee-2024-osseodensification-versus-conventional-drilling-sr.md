## One-line Summary
SR (8 studies, 170 patients, 334 implants) confirms osseodensification outperforms conventional drilling in implant stability quotient, bone development, and torque across maxillary implant placements.

## 한줄요약
SR(8편, 170명, 334개 임플란트): OD가 기존 드릴링 대비 상악 임플란트 안정성(ISQ)·골형성·삽입토크 모두 우수 — 그러나 바이어스 위험 높아 근거 수준 제한.

## Summary
This PRISMA-compliant SR searched PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Cochrane, and Web of Science through July 2023. Eight studies were identified (170 patients, 334 implants) from Egypt, India, and Brazil. Meta-analysis was not conducted due to heterogeneity. All eight studies consistently reported OD superiority over conventional drilling in ISQ, insertion torque, and bone development. Risk of bias assessment using Newcastle-Ottawa Scale and Cochrane RoB tool revealed high risk in key domains: blinding, allocation concealment, and randomization — flagging that the evidence quality is moderate at best.

## Key Contributions
- Synthesizes 8 comparative studies through 2023 — contemporaneous with other OD SRs but with distinct geographic sample (Egypt, India, Brazil)
- Confirms cross-study consistency: OD wins on ISQ, torque, and bone growth in every included study
- Dual bias tool use (Newcastle-Ottawa + Cochrane RoB) provides more nuanced quality assessment than single-tool reviews
- Identifies specific methodological weaknesses in OD RCT literature: blinding and allocation concealment consistently high-risk
- Notes 7/8 studies were maxillary-only — mandibular OD data underrepresented

## Methodology
- Design: Systematic review; PRISMA guidelines; no PROSPERO registration reported
- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, OpenGrey, Google Scholar (through July 2023)
- PICO: Implant patients (P); OD drilling (I); conventional drilling (C); implant stability + crestal bone levels (O)
- Inclusion: RCTs and non-RCTs comparing OD vs conventional drilling; stability outcomes (ISQ, insertion torque, RFA)
- Exclusion: Animal studies, case reports, reviews, no direct comparison, non-English, piezoelectric comparison
- 113 records → 8 included; 26 assessed full-text
- Bias assessment: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (observational), Cochrane RoB (RCTs)

## Results
- Studies: 8; Patients: 170; Implants: 334
- Geographic distribution: Egypt (4), India (3), Brazil (1)
- Placement sites: Maxilla only (7), maxilla + mandible (1)
- OD vs conventional: Superior in ISQ values, bone development, and insertion torque in all 8 studies
- Bias risk: Low for statistical methods; High for blinding, allocation concealment, random sequence generation
- Notable finding: Arafat & Elbaz (sinus lift study) — significantly higher ISQ with OD vs conventional drilling in posterior maxilla
