## Three-line Summary

Multicenter controlled clinical trial (56 patients, 150 implants) comparing Osseodensification (OD) vs conventional Subtractive Drilling (SD) across diameter, length, arch, and region subgroups.

OD significantly increased insertion torque (IT) and ISQ at all timepoints; ISQ dipped at 3 weeks and recovered at 6 weeks, but OD maintained ISQ ≥68 throughout — below which early loading is typically contraindicated.

Short implants showed no OD–SD difference, indicating a macrogeometry-dependent ceiling; only 6-week follow-up means loaded implant survival data are absent.

## 세줄요약

다기관 대조 임상시험 (56명, 150 임플란트): 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD) vs 일반 절삭 드릴 (Subtractive Drilling, SD)을 직경·길이·악골·부위별로 비교.

OD가 전 비교 시점에서 식립토크 (Insertion Torque, IT) 및 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)를 유의하게 높였으며, 3주 ISQ 저하 후 6주 회복 구간에서도 OD는 ISQ ≥68 유지.

Short 임플란트는 예외(OD–SD 차이 없음)이며 추적기간 6주로 로딩 후 생존 데이터는 없음.

## Summary
Multicenter controlled clinical trial comparing osseodensification (OD) to conventional subtractive drilling (SD) for insertion torque and longitudinal ISQ across implant designs and jaw regions.

## Key Contributions
Provides the first multicenter clinical-trial-level evidence that OD elevates and maintains ISQ above the 68 loading threshold throughout the early healing dip — supporting earlier loading protocols. Short implants do not benefit, indicating macrogeometry-dependent ceiling effects.

## Methodology
56 patients each receiving ≥2 implants (n = 150). Stratified by implant diameter (narrow/regular/wide), length (short/regular/long), arch (maxilla/mandible), region (anterior/posterior). ITs recorded with torque indicator; ISQs by RFA at placement, 3 weeks, 6 weeks.

## Results
OD outperformed SD on IT across all pairwise comparisons except short implants. ISQ values significantly higher for OD at all timepoints; ISQ dipped at 3 weeks and returned to baseline at 6 weeks, but for OD the values remained ≥ 68 throughout. Short implants showed no OD–SD difference.

## Limitations
Not randomized despite "controlled" labeling. Only 6 weeks follow-up — no loaded-implant survival data. Healing dip in ISQ for short implants suggests OD may not overcome biomechanical limits when bone engagement is length-limited.
