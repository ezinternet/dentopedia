## Three-line Summary

Literature review plus case report (Journal of Periodontology, 2001) on a bioengineered implant designed for a predetermined strain-related bone response.

Two implants loaded for one year showed lamellar bone at the interface, with turnover under 5 µm/day, comparable to remote bone.

Two implants is a very small series; the histology is preliminary and does not prove a design effect.

## Summary

Misch et al. propose that implant design can set strain at the bone interface so that lamellar, not woven, bone forms. The case report is the test of that idea, and it is only two implants. Treat it as the origin of the strain-design hypothesis, not as its confirmation.

## Key Contributions

- Strain-based design rationale for interfacial bone remodeling.
- Two-implant, one-year histological case report.

## Methodology

Literature review plus case report; the abstract cites a prior animal study with the same design.

## Results

Lamellar interfacial bone; turnover <5 µm/day; two implants only.
