## Three-line Summary

Review (Implant Dentistry, 1999) arguing that posterior implants need more surface area through diameter and thread design, since length is a secondary factor for stress.

The author's comparisons: conventional diameter-based designs add about 30% surface area while posterior forces are up to >300% higher.

These are design-argument figures, not outcome data; survival is not reported in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Implant Dentistry, 1999) — 구치부 임플란트는 길이보다 직경·나사산으로 표면적을 늘려야 응력 감소에 유리하다고 주장.

저자의 비교: 통상 직경 중심 설계는 표면적을 약 30%만 늘리는 반면, 구치부 힘은 >300% 높음.

설계 논리의 수치이며 결과 데이터가 아님; 생존율은 초록에 없음.

## Summary

Misch's posterior-design rule is to raise surface area by design rather than length. The FEA-based reasoning is the core, and the abstract does not test it clinically.

## Key Contributions

- Surface-area-first design rule for posterior implants.
- FEA-supported argument that length is secondary.

## Methodology

Review with finite element analysis citation.

## Results

Author-stated comparisons only; no survival data.
