## Three-line Summary
Prospective RCT with 50 patients (25 immediate loading, 25 delayed loading) receiving single-tooth implants in healed maxillary aesthetic zone sites (teeth 15–25), evaluated at 5-year follow-up.
At 5 years, implant survival was 100% (IL) vs 95.8% (DL); success rates 91.7% vs 83.3%; mean marginal bone loss −0.50 mm (IL) vs −0.54 mm (DL) (p=0.782); no significant differences in papilla index, PES, or WES.
Immediate and delayed loading protocols yield comparable 5-year clinical and aesthetic outcomes for single-tooth implants in the maxillary aesthetic zone; smoking significantly increased marginal bone loss (p=0.021).

## Summary
This prospective randomized clinical trial compared immediate loading (IL) versus delayed loading (DL) protocols for single-tooth implants placed in healed sites of the maxillary aesthetic zone (teeth 15–25). Fifty patients were randomized, with 24 per group available at the 5-year follow-up. All implants were Tapered Internal (BioHorizons) placed with at least half the Laser-Lok collar in contact with bone. The IL group received a screw-retained temporary crown with light centric contacts for 2 months before definitive restoration; the DL group underwent a two-stage procedure with 4 months healing before provisionalization. At 5 years, both groups showed excellent and statistically indistinguishable outcomes: survival 100% vs 95.8%, success 91.7% vs 83.3%, MBL −0.50 vs −0.54 mm (p=0.782), and similar papilla index, PES, and WES scores. The only significant predictor of greater bone loss was smoking (p=0.021). This study provides Level 1 evidence that immediate loading of single-tooth implants in healed maxillary aesthetic sites is a viable alternative to conventional delayed loading.

## Key Contributions
- First 5-year RCT data comparing IL vs DL for single-tooth implants specifically in the maxillary aesthetic zone
- Comprehensive outcome assessment combining hard tissue (MBL), soft tissue (papilla index), and aesthetic (PES/WES) metrics
- Demonstrates that papilla index differences observed at 1 year (favoring DL) resolve by 5 years
- Identifies smoking as the only significant risk factor for increased marginal bone loss across both protocols

## Methodology
**Design**: Prospective parallel-group RCT, single-center (Centre of Dental Specialist Care, Malmö, Sweden), registered at ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT02770846). Randomization via sealed envelopes; surgeon blinded to allocation during implant placement.
**Population**: 50 patients ≥18 years with single-tooth edentulous space in maxilla (teeth 15–25), healed ridge (≥4 months post-extraction or agenesis), full-mouth plaque score ≤25%, no bone grafting needed. Excluded: systemic contraindications, poor hygiene, need for augmentation.
**Interventions**: 
- IL (n=25): Immediate provisional screw-retained crown (light centric contact, no eccentric contacts) for 2 months → definitive crown
- DL (n=25): Two-stage surgery, 4 months submerged healing → screw-retained provisional → definitive crown
- All definitive crowns: titanium base + individually fabricated zirconia abutment + GC Initial veneer; screw-retained where possible
**Outcomes**: Primary — implant survival/success (Albrektsson & Zarb criteria), MBL (digital periapical radiographs, parallel technique, ImageJ calibrated to 1.0 mm thread distance). Secondary — papilla index (Jemt), PES (Fürhauser), WES (Belser). Assessed at 3, 6, 12 months, and 5 years.
**Statistics**: SPSS v26; t-test/Mann-Whitney for continuous, χ²/Fisher's exact for categorical; Wilcoxon signed-rank for within-group longitudinal comparisons; α=0.05.

## Results
| Outcome | Immediate Loading (n=24) | Delayed Loading (n=23–24) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5-year implant survival | 100% (24/24) | 95.8% (23/24) | — |
| 5-year implant success | 91.7% (22/24) | 83.3% (20/24) | — |
| 5-year MBL (mean ± SD) | −0.50 ± 0.73 mm | −0.54 ± 0.65 mm | 0.782 |
| 1-year MBL (mean ± SD) | −0.57 ± 0.53 mm | −0.70 ± 0.58 mm | 0.430 |
| 5-year papilla index (mesial) | 2.54 ± 0.51 | 2.57 ± 0.73 | 0.518 |
| 5-year papilla index (distal) | 2.33 ± 0.67 | 2.39 ± 0.84 | 0.527 |
| 5-year PES | 11.00 ± 2.08 | 11.17 ± 2.39 | 0.501 |
| 5-year WES | 7.63 ± 1.53 | 7.91 ± 1.53 | 0.365 |
| Almost perfect aesthetic (PES≥12 & WES≥9) | 20.8% (5/24) | 30.4% (7/23) | — |
| Aesthetic failure (PES≤7 or WES≤5) | 12.5% (3/24) | 13.0% (3/23) | — |
| Complete aesthetic failure (PES≤7 & WES≤5) | 4.2% (1/24) | 8.7% (2/23) | — |

**Subgroup analyses**:
- Smoking: smokers −1.90 ± 0.90 mm vs non-smokers −0.40 ± 0.58 mm MBL (p=0.021)
- Sex: no significant difference (male −0.61 ± 0.72 vs female −0.46 ± 0.66 mm, p=0.374)
- Retention: cemented −0.51 ± 0.74 vs screw-retained −0.52 ± 0.66 mm (p=0.808)
- PES improved significantly from 1-year to 5-year in both groups (IL p=0.017, DL p=0.046); WES stable

**Complications**: 1 DL implant lost at 3 months (smoker); 1 DL technical complication (titanium base loss at 52 months); 1 IL crown replaced for aesthetic reasons after adjacent veneering (47 months).
