## Three-line Summary

Short concept paper (J Dent Symp 1993) describing the rationale and protocol for gradually loading an implant after the initial bone interface is established.

The abstract states that bone density is the most critical factor in setting the healing time between first- and second-stage surgery and between the five appointments of a cement-retained prosthesis; no numbers, intervals or outcomes are given.

It is a rationale/protocol statement, not evidence of effect; the abstract-only record cannot say what the five appointments or intervals actually are.

## Summary

Abstract-level record of Misch's 1993 concept paper on the rationale and protocol for gradually loading an implant after the initial bone interface forms. The abstract states only that bone density is the main determinant of healing time between first- and second-stage surgery and between the five appointments of a cement-retained prosthesis. It contains no data; it is held as the origin of the progressive-loading protocol, not as evidence of effect.
