## Three-line Summary

Concept/technique paper (Implant Dent 1994) on the prosthodontic implementation of progressive bone loading for root-form implants using acrylic transitional prostheses with reinforced cast substructures.

The technique, termed progressive prosthetic transference, is described as applicable in arches with as few as three remaining natural roots and as a clinical execution of biologic, biomechanical and physiologic principles compatible with fostering and maintaining osseointegration; no numbers or outcomes are given.

It is a technique description, not outcome evidence; the abstract-only record cannot say how load is stepped or what results were seen.
