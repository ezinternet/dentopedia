## Three-line Summary
Single-center RCT enrolling 72 partially edentulous patients (187 implants) compared immediate loading (IL) versus 3-month delayed loading (DL) following fully guided implant surgery, with IL eligibility gated at IT >20 Ncm and ISQ >65.
At 1-year follow-up, IL showed significantly lower survival in both ITT (83.4% implant-level) and per-protocol (90.2%) analyses versus 100% DL survival (all p<0.01), while marginal bone loss remained below 0.1 mm in both groups with no intergroup difference.
Even when standard primary stability thresholds are met, fully guided surgery does not mitigate the higher failure risk of immediate loading; delayed loading should remain the preferred protocol for guide-assisted cases until further long-term evidence is available.

## One-line Summary

RCT (n=72 patients, 187 implants, 1 year) showed immediate loading after fully guided implant surgery significantly increased failure risk vs. 3-month delayed loading (implant survival 83.4% ITT vs. 100%), despite requiring IT >20 Ncm and ISQ >65 for IL eligibility.

## 한줄요약

완전 가이드 수술(fully guided surgery) 후 즉시로딩(Immediate Loading, IL)은 3개월 지연로딩(Delayed Loading, DL) 대비 유의하게 낮은 임플란트 생존율을 보인 RCT (83.4% ITT vs. 100%, p<0.01); 변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 두 군 모두 <0.1 mm로 차이 없었다.

## Summary

This single-center RCT at Yonsei University enrolled 72 partially edentulous patients (187 implants) and randomized them to immediate loading (IL, n=36 patients, 93 implants) or 3-month delayed loading (DL, n=26 patients, 94 implants), all implants placed via fully guided surgery. IL eligibility required achieving IT >20 Ncm and ISQ >65 at placement; 4 IL-group patients (7 implants) failed to meet this threshold and did not receive IL. At 1-year follow-up, the DL group achieved 100% survival at both patient and implant levels, whereas IL showed significantly lower survival in both intention-to-treat (ITT: 69.4% patient, 83.4% implant) and per-protocol (PP: 78.1% patient, 90.2% implant) analyses (all p<0.01). MBL was below 0.1 mm in both groups with no intergroup difference. The findings indicate that even when standard IL stability thresholds are met, the fully guided surgical context does not protect against the higher failure risk inherent to immediate loading protocols.

## Key Contributions

- Provides the first RCT directly comparing IL vs. DL in the specific context of fully guided implant surgery
- Demonstrates that IT >20 Ncm + ISQ >65 eligibility gates are insufficient to equalize IL and DL outcomes in guide-assisted cases
- Confirms MBL is loading-timing-independent at 1 year (both groups <0.1 mm)
- Both ITT and PP analyses consistently favor DL, strengthening the argument against IL in fully guided cases
- Shows ~11% of IL-assigned patients could not receive IL even with guided placement, indicating that primary stability is not guaranteed by digital guidance alone

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective RCT, 1-year follow-up, single center (Yonsei University, Seoul)
- **Population**: 72 partially edentulous adults; IL n=36 (93 implants), DL n=26 (94 implants)
- **Intervention**: Fully guided (template-guided, static guide) implant surgery for all patients. IL group received prefabricated provisional prosthesis at surgery if IT >20 Ncm AND ISQ >65. DL group loaded conventionally at 3 months
- **Primary outcome**: 1-year implant survival rate (ITT and PP)
- **Secondary outcome**: MBL measured by CBCT
- **Statistical threshold**: p <0.05; intergroup survival compared for all four metrics (patient-level ITT, patient-level PP, implant-level ITT, implant-level PP)

## Results

| Analysis | IL Patient | IL Implant | DL Patient | DL Implant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Survival (ITT) | 69.4% | 83.4% | 100% | 100% |
| Survival (PP) | 78.1% | 90.2% | 100% | 100% |
| MBL | <0.1 mm | <0.1 mm | <0.1 mm | <0.1 mm |

All IL vs. DL survival differences: p <0.01. MBL difference: p >0.05.
