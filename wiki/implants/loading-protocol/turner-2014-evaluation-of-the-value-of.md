## Three-line Summary

Prospective study, 15 volunteers, 25 Ankylos implants (11 conventional PFM crowns in full occlusion, 14 progressive composite-resin provisional crowns in light contact with soft diet for 45 days), assigned "randomly" per the text; Periotest value (PTV) measured at cementation and after 45 days, stratified by bone quality judged from drilling resistance.

Progressive loading increased rigidity in both good bone (−4.8 → −5.6, P = 0.0143) and poor bone (−3.5 → −5.01, P = 0.000264), whereas conventional loading in poor bone lost rigidity (−2.08 → −1.38, P = 0.0211) and in good bone did not change significantly (−2.5 → −2.74, P = 0.0723).

The authors conclude that poor-quality bone should be loaded progressively; but the subgroups are 5–8 implants, PTV was taken on abutments in one arm and on crowns in the other, only within-group tests were done and 45 days is short, so the conclusion is suggestive only.

## 세줄요약

전향적 연구, 지원자 15명, Ankylos 임플란트 25개(대조 11개: 완전 교합의 금속-도재 크라운 일반 부하, 시험 14개: 복합레진 임시 크라운 경접촉 + 45일 연식 식이 점진적 부하), 본문상 무작위 배정; 골질(드릴링 저항으로 판정)별로 Periotest 값(PTV)을 접착 시점과 45일 후에 측정.

점진적 부하는 양호한 골(−4.8 → −5.6, P = 0.0143)과 불량한 골(−3.5 → −5.01, P = 0.000264) 모두에서 강성을 높였고, 일반 부하는 불량한 골에서 강성이 감소(−2.08 → −1.38, P = 0.0211), 양호한 골에서는 유의한 변화가 없었다(−2.5 → −2.74, P = 0.0723).

저자는 불량한 골은 점진적으로 부하해야 한다고 결론짓지만, 하위군이 5–8개이고 한쪽은 지대주에서, 다른 쪽은 크라운에서 측정했으며 군내 검정만 했고 45일로 짧아 시사 수준이다.

## Summary

A small prospective study (25 Ankylos implants, 15 volunteers) compared progressive loading with composite-resin provisional crowns in light contact and soft diet for 45 days against conventional PFM crowns in full occlusion, using Periotest rigidity stratified by bone quality. Progressive loading increased rigidity in good and poor bone, while conventional loading in poor bone reduced it. Measurement sites differed between arms, subgroups held 5–8 implants and only within-group tests were reported, so the result is suggestive; the endpoint is implant mobility, not bone density.

## Key Contributions

- Compares progressive vs conventional loading within good and poor bone quality using implant rigidity (Periotest).
- Reports that conventional full-occlusion loading in poor bone reduced rigidity over 45 days while progressive loading increased it.
- Proposes that good-quality bone may not need progressive loading.

## Methodology

- 15 volunteer patients (private practice), missing posterior teeth in both jaws; randomly assigned to control or experimental (method of randomization not described).
- 25 Ankylos implants (grit-blasted, thermally acid-etched), 8–14 mm long, 3.5 or 4.5 mm wide; 3-month healing before uncovering.
- Bone quality (good/poor) judged by drilling resistance: progressive 6 good / 8 poor; conventional 5 good / 6 poor.
- Progressive group (14 implants): composite resin provisional crowns in light contact (100 µm articulating paper, slight resistance) for 45 days, soft diet; PTV taken on abutments at provisional cementation and after provisional removal at 45 days.
- Control (11 implants): final PFM crowns in full occlusion, no diet restriction; PTV taken on the crown margin at cementation and 45 days.
- Three Periotest readings averaged per implant; paired Student's t-test, one-tailed, significance at 0.025.

## Results

| Group (implants) | PTV at cementation | PTV at 45 days | One-tailed P | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conventional, good bone (5) | −2.5 | −2.74 | 0.0723 | no significant change |
| Conventional, poor bone (6) | −2.08 | −1.38 | 0.0211 | significant loss of rigidity |
| Progressive, good bone (6) | −4.8 | −5.6 | 0.0143 | significant gain in rigidity |
| Progressive, poor bone (8) | −3.5 | −5.01 | 0.000264 | significant gain in rigidity |

Lower (more negative) PTV means a stiffer implant. Group-by-group counts are as stated in Methods; the Results tables themselves were not reproduced in the extracted text.

## Limitations

- Authors' stated limits: PTV taken on abutments (progressive) vs on PFM crowns (conventional); only unsplinted single crowns; suggest splinted crowns/bridges and resonance frequency analysis in future work.
- Subgroups of 5–8 implants; patients were volunteers; randomization and allocation concealment not described; single operator; no blinding mentioned. [미검증 beyond text read]
- Only paired within-group tests are reported; no between-group comparison of change was given, and baseline PTV differed (progressive −3.5/−4.8 vs conventional −2.08/−2.5).
- 45-day follow-up; endpoint is implant mobility (Periotest), not bone density or crestal bone, despite the title and abstract language about density; no implant survival data.
- Different loading materials and diet in the two arms confound the staging effect.
