## Three-line Summary
- Ex vivo bench study (n = 9 porcine rib bone samples): three titanium (Titan SLA, Straumann) and three zirconia (Pure Ceramic Implant, Straumann) dental implants in three geometries (3.3x8 / 4.1x8 / 4.1x14 mm) scanned at 3T T1W turbo spin echo, with micro-CT (16 um) as reference.
- Artifact extent: titanium 2.57 ± 1.09 mm vs zirconia 0.37 ± 0.20 mm (p<0.05); measurement site (p=0.73) and implant geometry (p=0.43) had no effect.
- Clinical implication: geometry cannot be tuned to reduce MRI artifacts — material choice can (~7-fold lower artifact with zirconia); artifact pattern is scan-direction dependent.

## Summary
This ex vivo study evaluated whether dental implant geometry influences the severity of MRI artifacts independent of material. Six implants (three titanium SLA and three zirconia, Straumann) in three geometries (3.3x8 / 4.1x8 / 4.1x14 mm) were placed in nine porcine rib bone samples, scanned with a 3T T1W turbo spin echo protocol, and measured against micro-CT reference images of the implant bed. Titanium produced a mean artifact zone of 2.57 ± 1.09 mm versus 0.37 ± 0.20 mm for zirconia (p<0.01 material effect), but neither measurement site (p=0.73) nor implant geometry (p=0.43) affected artifact extent. Clinically, artifact reduction in MRI is therefore achieved through material selection — zirconia implants yield roughly a 7-fold smaller artifact — not by choosing a narrower or shorter implant.

## Key Contributions
- First controlled comparison isolating implant geometry (diameter x height) from material as an independent MRI-artifact factor at 3T.
- Quantified a ~7-fold smaller artifact zone for zirconia vs titanium (0.37 vs 2.57 mm), intrarater reliability 0.92.
- Shows geometry does not drive artifact extent, refuting the extrapolation from larger fixation-screw data that longer implants artifact more.

## Methodology
- **Design**: Ex vivo bench experiment (porcine rib bone, n = 9, G*Power-calculated; formaldehyde-fixed)
- **Implants**: Titanium (Titan SLA) and zirconia (Pure Ceramic Implant), geometries 3.3x8 / 4.1x8 / 4.1x14 mm
- **MRI**: 3T Philips, 8-channel SENSE foot/ankle coil, T1W turbo spin echo, TR/TE 25/3.5 ms, FOV 100x100x90 mm, voxel 0.22x0.22x0.50 mm, scan time 11:18, phase-encoding RL
- **Control**: micro-CT (SkyScan 1272, 80 kV, 125 mA, 16 um voxel) of implant bed before placement
- **Measurement**: linear distance (mm) from implant-bed surface (micro-CT) to artifact border (MRI) at vestibular-lingual, mesio-distal, and apex; 3 repeats
- **Statistics**: within-subjects ANOVA (Greenhouse-Geisser where sphericity violated), p <= 0.05
