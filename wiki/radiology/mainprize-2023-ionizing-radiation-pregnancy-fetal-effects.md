## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Abdom Radiol 2023; Sunnybrook/Toronto) combining ICRP and ACR-SPR data on conceptus tissue reactions and cancer risk by gestational age, plus conversion-factor estimates of conceptus dose for common radiological examinations.

No observable tissue reactions below 50 mGy at any gestational age; 50–100 mGy gives only "potential effects" before 17 weeks; above 100 mGy effects become clinically detectable before 27 weeks; stochastic cancer risk ~0.4% lifetime per 10 mGy (1 childhood cancer death per 1,700 at 10 mGy).

Examinations that keep the conceptus out of the primary beam (head, neck, chest, extremities) give conceptus doses far below thresholds (PA chest ≈0.001 mGy) and should not be withheld; fetal/gonadal shielding is no longer recommended.

## 세줄요약

태아가 1차선 밖에 있는 검사(두경부·흉부·사지)는 태아 선량이 역치보다 훨씬 낮아(흉부 PA 약 0.001 mGy) 보류할 이유가 없고, 태아·생식선 차폐는 더 이상 권고되지 않는다.

## Summary

The review frames fetal radiation effects in two classes. Tissue reactions (formerly "deterministic effects") have thresholds, defined since ICRP 103 as the dose affecting >1% of those exposed: pregnancy loss, malformation, growth restriction, microcephaly and intellectual disability. Stochastic effects (childhood cancer) are modelled as linear no-threshold. Following the ACR-SPR practice parameter, there are no observable effects below 50 mGy at any gestational age; between 50 and 100 mGy only "potential effects" before 17 weeks; above 100 mGy and before 27 weeks tissue reactions become clinically detectable and increasingly probable; after 27 weeks no relevant tissue reactions have been observed in the diagnostic range. The cancer rule of thumb is ~0.4% additional lifetime risk per 10 mGy, with large uncertainty. Conceptus dose is estimated as conversion factor × entrance air kerma; for examinations with the conceptus outside the primary beam the dose is in the µGy range. The ACR-SPR algorithm classes examinations expected to exceed 50 mGy as elevated ("substantial") risk requiring planning and consent. Gonadal/fetal shielding is no longer best practice.

## Key Contributions

- Gestational-age threshold table (adapted from ACR-SPR/ICRP): 0–2 weeks all-or-none 50–100 mGy; organogenesis malformations ~200 mGy; severe intellectual disability 60–310 mGy (early fetal) and 250–280 mGy (18–25 weeks); intellectual deficit >100 mGy.
- Practical threshold summary: <50 mGy no observable effect; 50 mGy = ACR-SPR "potential" threshold triggering planning.
- Counselling statement: at diagnostic doses there is >99% likelihood the conceptus is unaffected.
- Out-of-beam examinations (chest PA 0.001 mGy, chest CT 0.09 mGy) are orders of magnitude below thresholds.

## Methodology

Narrative review of ICRP/ACR-SPR reports and literature on entrance kerma and Monte Carlo conversion factors; dose to conceptus estimated as D = g × K for common radiography, CT and nuclear-medicine procedures.

## Results

| Gestational stage (post-conception) | Effect | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 weeks | All-or-none (embryo death) | 50–100 mGy |
| Organogenesis (3–8 weeks) | Congenital anomalies | ~200 mGy |
| Early fetal | Severe intellectual disability (high risk) | 60–310 mGy |
| Early fetal | Intellectual deficit | >100 mGy (0.25–0.29 IQ points/10 mGy) |
| Early fetal | Microcephaly | 200 mGy |
| 18–25 weeks | Severe intellectual disability | 250–280 mGy |
| >26–27 weeks | Little chance of birth defects in diagnostic range | — |
| Any stage | No observable effects | <50 mGy |
| Stochastic | Lifetime cancer risk | ~0.4% per 10 mGy; childhood cancer death 1/1,700 at 10 mGy |

Typical conceptus doses (not in primary beam): PA chest 0.001 mGy; lateral chest 0.002 mGy; chest CT 0.09 mGy; mammography <0.01 mGy. In primary beam: AP abdomen 4.5 mGy; CT abdomen ~2.2 mGy; CT pelvis up to 25 mGy.

## Limitations

Narrative review without systematic search; threshold table adapted from secondary sources with wide ranges; dental examinations not specifically covered; written for patients, not occupational exposure.
