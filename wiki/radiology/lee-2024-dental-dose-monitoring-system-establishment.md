## Three-line Summary

Narrative review and system-development paper (Yonsei OMFR, J Korean Dent Assoc 2024) proposing a national-level dose monitoring system for Korean dental radiography covering three modalities: intraoral, panoramic, and CBCT.

The work surveys existing equipment architecture, designs an integrated national data-collection plan, and reports completion of a pilot operation; nationwide deployment and future supplements are pending expert consensus.

The system addresses the gap that Korea's dental dose data-aggregation infrastructure lags behind DRL adoption (DRLs established 2009/2018), requiring a unified monitoring platform to enable meaningful national benchmarking.

## 세줄요약

연세대 구강악안면방사선과가 『대한치과의사협회지』 2024년에 발표한 한국 치과 방사선 국가 선량 모니터링 시스템 제안 및 시스템 개발 논문(구내, 파노라마, CBCT 3개 모달리티 대상).

기존 장비 구조 조사, 통합 국가 데이터 수집 계획 설계, 파일럿 운영 완료 보고; 전국 배포 및 향후 보완은 전문가 의견 수렴 후 결정 예정.

한국의 치과 DRL 도입(2009/2018) 대비 선량 데이터 집계 인프라가 미성숙한 격차를 메우기 위해 통합 모니터링 플랫폼이 필요하다는 점을 강조.

## Summary
Yonsei University Oral and Maxillofacial Radiology proposal for a national-level dose monitoring system covering three core dental X-ray modalities — intraoral, panoramic, and CBCT. The work surveys the existing equipment landscape, builds an integrated data-collection plan, and reports a pilot operation. Future system supplements are to be confirmed through gathered expert opinion.

## Key Contributions
- Maps the existing intraoral / panoramic / CBCT equipment architecture
- Proposes integrated national-level data-collection plan
- Reports pilot operation outcomes and future-extension recommendations

## Methodology
- Design: system development + survey
- Integrated data-collection plan
- Pilot operation
- Expert opinion gathering for system refinement

## Results
- Dental X-ray equipment structure (3 modalities) analyzed
- National-level dose monitoring system piloted
- Future extension and supplements outlined

## Limitations
- Pilot-stage; nationwide deployment not yet confirmed
- Operator and imaging-mode variability standardization pending
- Unified reporting platform absent
