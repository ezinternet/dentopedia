## Three-line Summary

Case report of bilateral earrings on a Planmeca ProMax panoramic radiograph producing three simultaneous image types: a real image, a typical (blurred, contralateral) ghost image, and a newly described pseudo-ghost (ghost-like real) image.

The pseudo-ghost arises because the scanner's center of rotation extends beyond the jaw, creating a double-image zone where bilateral symmetric objects appear as an extra real-like image — distinct from a classic ghost and explained via beam path geometry confirmed with dry-mandible simulation.

This case refines chairside artifact interpretation by adding a third image category, enabling better differentiation of earring artifacts from pathology on panoramic radiographs.

## 세줄요약

파노라마 방사선 사진(Planmeca ProMax)에서 귀걸이로 인해 실상(Real Image), 전형적 허상(Ghost Image), 새로운 유사허상(Pseudo-Ghost Image) 세 종류의 상이 동시에 형성된 증례 보고.

유사허상은 스캐너 회전중심(Center of Rotation, CoR)이 악골 밖으로 확장되는 구간에서 발생하며, 빔 경로·각도 분석 및 건조 하악골 시뮬레이션으로 기전 규명.

파노라마 판독 시 허상을 세 유형으로 구분할 수 있어, 귀걸이 허상과 병소의 오진을 방지하는 데 기여.

## Summary

Adds the 'pseudo-ghost' category to panoramic interpretation and ties it to scanner rotation geometry — refines chairside differentiation of artefacts from pathology.

## Key Contributions

- Distinguishes typical ghost vs pseudo-ghost (ghost-like real) images from bilateral earrings.
- Shows Planmeca ProMax center of rotation extending beyond the jaw, creating a double-image zone.
- Explains formation zones from X-ray beam path/angle, with dry-mandible simulation.

## Methodology

Case report: real + two ghost images per side analyzed by beam path/angle; radiopaque marker on dry-mandible condyle to simulate pseudo-ghost vs typical ghost.

## Results

Adds the 'pseudo-ghost' category to panoramic interpretation and ties it to scanner rotation geometry — refines chairside differentiation of artefacts from pathology.
