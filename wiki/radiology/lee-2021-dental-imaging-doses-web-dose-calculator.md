## Three-line Summary

Narrative review compiling post-2010 organ and effective-dose data for dental imaging modalities (intraoral, panoramic, CBCT) stratified by machine, age, and technique, plus development of the free dentaldose.org patient-facing dose calculator.

Mean effective doses were: intraoral 1.32 µSv (range 0.60–2.56), panoramic 17.93 µSv (3.47–75.00), and CBCT 121.09 µSv (17.10–392.20); children receive ~29% higher CBCT dose, and large FOV adds ~1.6× over small FOV.

The intraoral ≪ panoramic ≪ CBCT dose hierarchy is confirmed, and the web calculator contextualizes patient dose against natural and medical radiation sources to support informed consent discussions.

## 세줄요약

2010년 이후 구내, 파노라마, CBCT 장기·유효선량 문헌을 기기·연령·기법별로 집계한 내러티브 리뷰와 환자 친화적 무료 웹 계산기(dentaldose.org) 개발.

평균 유효선량: 구내 1.32 µSv(0.60–2.56), 파노라마 17.93 µSv(3.47–75.00), CBCT 121.09 µSv(17.10–392.20); 소아는 CBCT 선량이 성인보다 약 29% 높고, 대형 FOV는 소형 대비 약 1.6배.

구내 ≪ 파노라마 ≪ CBCT 선량 위계 확인, 웹 계산기는 자연방사선·의료방사선과 비교해 동의 설명 지원.

## Summary

Quantifies the intraoral≪panoramic≪CBCT dose hierarchy and the multiplicative effect of age and FOV, packaged as a patient-facing calculator comparing dental doses to natural/medical sources.

## Key Contributions

- Comprehensive organ/effective-dose table from post-2010 literature, stratified by dosimetry method, machine, age, technique.
- Mean effective dose: intraoral 1.32 (0.60–2.56) µSv; panoramic 17.93 (3.47–75.00) µSv; CBCT 121.09 (17.10–392.20) µSv.
- Child CBCT ~29% higher than adult; large FOV (>150 cm²) ~1.6× small FOV (<50 cm²); max child large-FOV CBCT 392.2 µSv ≈ 13% of annual natural background (3110 µSv). Free dentaldose.org calculator.

## Methodology

PubMed review of post-2010 organ/effective-dose reports; summarization by sub-group; representative Monte Carlo simulations of intraoral, panoramic, CBCT to visualize head dose distribution; interactive web app.

## Results

Quantifies the intraoral≪panoramic≪CBCT dose hierarchy and the multiplicative effect of age and FOV, packaged as a patient-facing calculator comparing dental doses to natural/medical sources.
