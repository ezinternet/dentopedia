## Three-line Summary

Phantom dosimetry and image-quality study comparing ultra-low-dose (ULD), low-dose (LD), and standard (STD) protocols on two CBCT units (ProMax 3D Mid and Orthophos SL), with subjective quality scoring by 4 oral and maxillofacial radiologists across 3 human-equivalent phantoms.

ULD and LD protocols achieved 'acceptable-to-good' image quality on a 5-point Likert scale while reducing effective dose approximately 6-fold compared to standard protocols; high tube voltage settings reduced dose without degrading diagnostic image quality.

ULD/LD protocols are clinically adoptable for routine diagnosis and treatment planning, and should be preferred as default over standard-dose modes; high tube voltage is a practical dose-reduction lever.

## 세줄요약

ProMax 3D Mid·Orthophos SL 두 CBCT 장치에서 초저선량(Ultra-Low-Dose, ULD)·저선량(Low-Dose, LD)·표준(Standard, STD) 프로토콜을 팬텀으로 비교한 선량·화질 연구 — 구강악안면방사선의 4인이 인체유사 팬텀 3종의 화질을 5점 리커트 척도로 평가.

ULD·LD 프로토콜에서 화질 '수용~양호', 표준 대비 유효선량 ~6배 절감; 고관전압(High Tube Voltage) 설정이 화질 저하 없이 선량 절감에 효과적.

ULD·LD 프로토콜은 일상 진단·치료계획에 채택 가능하며 표준선량 모드보다 기본 사용이 권장됨; 고관전압은 실용적 선량절감 수단.

## Summary

Standard mode gives highest quality but ULD/LD remain diagnostically acceptable at a fraction of the dose; supports defaulting to lower-dose modes for routine tasks.

## Key Contributions

- ULD and LD protocols achieved 'acceptable-to-good' subjective quality on 5-point Likert across 4 radiologists.
- ~6-fold dose reduction vs standard protocols.
- High-tube-voltage protocols reduce dose without degrading image quality → ULD/LD adoptable as routine diagnosis/planning.

## Methodology

3 human-equivalent phantoms scanned in ULD/LD/STD modes on ProMax 3D Mid and Orthophos SL; quality scored by 4 OMF radiologists; intra/inter-rater agreement; effective doses computed.

## Results

Standard mode gives highest quality but ULD/LD remain diagnostically acceptable at a fraction of the dose; supports defaulting to lower-dose modes for routine tasks.
