## Three-line Summary

Narrative review combined with a manufacturer-survey of 87 commercially available CBCT devices from 34 manufacturers in 11 countries (through February 2025), analyzing acquisition and reconstruction parameters to derive per-specialty clinical decision-support charts for FOV, voxel size, and exposure selection.

Parameter variability across and within CBCT models is substantial (tube voltage 50–120 kV, current 1–17 mA, effective dose ~3–500 µSv, DAP 10–5,600 mGy·cm²); decision-support charts map indication to required spatial resolution and FOV for endodontics (high resolution, small FOV), orthodontics/OMFS (larger FOV), periodontology, prosthodontics/implants, and pediatrics.

Clinicians should select CBCT parameters based on specialty-specific resolution and FOV requirements rather than device defaults; the wide dose range across units demands active optimization rather than default preset use.

CBCT 기종 간·기종 내 파라미터 편차 큼(관전압 50–120 kV, 관전류 1–17 mA, 유효선량 ~3–500 µSv, DAP 10–5,600 mGy·cm²); 의사결정 차트: 근관치료(고해상도·소 FOV), 교정/구강악안면외과(대 FOV), 치주·보철/임플란트·소아 등 전공별 매핑.

## Summary

Parameter variability across — and within — models markedly changes dose. Authors map indication → required spatial resolution / FOV / exposure, e.g. high spatial resolution + small FOV for endodontics, larger FOV for orthodontics/OMFS, with optimization left adjustable by the operator.

## Key Contributions

- 87 commercially available CBCT devices from 34 manufacturers (11 countries) catalogued; FOV and voxel size most consistently reported.
- Decision-support charts per specialty: prosthodontics/implant, periodontology, OMFS, paediatric/orthodontics, endodontics.
- Documents the large parameter spread driving dose: tube voltage 50–120 kV, current 1–17 mA, DAP 10–5600 mGy·cm², effective dose ~3–500 µSv.

## Methodology

Literature review plus manufacturer websites, manuals, and direct inquiries (through Feb 2025); descriptive + quantitative categorization of acquisition/reconstruction parameters to derive per-specialty device requirements.

## Results

Parameter variability across — and within — models markedly changes dose. Authors map indication → required spatial resolution / FOV / exposure, e.g. high spatial resolution + small FOV for endodontics, larger FOV for orthodontics/OMFS, with optimization left adjustable by the operator.
