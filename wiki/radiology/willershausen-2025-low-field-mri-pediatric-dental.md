## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-center study (n=16 pediatric patients, mean age 12.4 y, 49 ectopic/supernumerary teeth) comparing radiation-free 0.55 T MRI (0.6 mm isotropic 3D T1w FLASH, ~9:45 min acquisition) against same-day ultra-low-dose CT (CTDI 0.43 mGy) as reference, with image quality scored by 3 senior readers across 7 tooth structures.

0.55 T MRI achieved similar valid-image quality to ULD-CT for tooth axis, root morphology, root resorption, and cysts; motion artifacts were not significantly different; some structural details not yet optimally resolved at this field strength.

Low-field MRI is an emerging radiation-free alternative for selected pediatric dental indications such as supernumerary/ectopic teeth, though spatial-resolution limits mean high-resolution CT/CBCT remains needed for advanced diagnostic tasks.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 전향적 단일기관 연구(소아 16명, 평균 12.4세, 과잉치·이소치 49개) — 방사선 없는 0.55 T MRI(~10분 촬영)와 동일 날 초저선량 CT(CTDI 0.43 mGy)를 7개 치아 구조에 대해 화질 비교.

줄2: 0.55 T MRI는 치축·치근·치근흡수·낭종에서 초저선량 CT와 유사한 유효 화질 달성; 운동 인공물 차이 없음; 일부 구조에서는 미흡.

줄3: 저자기장 MRI는 특정 소아 치과 적응증(과잉치·이소치)에서 방사선 없는 대안이 될 수 있으나, 고해상도 영상이 필요한 진단 과제엔 CT/CBCT가 여전히 필요.

## Summary

Positions low-field MRI as an emerging radiation-free alternative for selected paediatric dental indications, with current spatial-resolution limits — the conceptual counterpoint to the dose-reduction cluster.

## Key Contributions

- 0.55 T MRI is feasible and radiation-free for delineating ectopic/supernumerary teeth in children.
- IQ similar to ULD-CT (CTDI 0.43 mGy) for tooth axis, root, root resorption, and cysts; motion artifacts not significantly different.
- Not yet optimal for all tooth/jaw structures; high-res CT/CBCT still needed for advanced indications. MRI acquisition ~9:45 min.

## Methodology

Prospective single-center: 16 paediatric patients (mean 12.4 y; 49 ectopic/supernumerary teeth) underwent same-day ULD-CT (reference) and 0.55 T MRI (0.6 mm isotropic 3D T1w FLASH); IQ for 7 structures scored twice by 3 senior readers (5-point Likert), dichotomized valid/non-valid.

## Results

Positions low-field MRI as an emerging radiation-free alternative for selected paediatric dental indications, with current spatial-resolution limits — the conceptual counterpoint to the dose-reduction cluster.
