## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PROSPERO-registered, 5 observational studies, 2001–2021) of interpretive radiographic optical artifacts — triangular-shaped radiolucencies (TSR), Mach band, cervical burnout — on intraoral bitewing/periapical images.

Non-carious TSR prevalence on maxillary molars: ~26% raw (pooled 0.308, I²≈98%), markedly higher in primary (~51%) vs permanent (~27%) molars; optical effects misread as caries/fracture in ~13% of observations (pooled 0.133, I²=89%).

~13% false-positive rate drives unnecessary restorative/endodontic/extraction treatment; defense strategies include the masking test, digital contrast adjustment, beam-angle control, and correlating every radiograph with clinical examination; GRADE certainty low to very low.

PROSPERO 등록 SR+MA (관찰 연구 5편, 2001–2021) — 구내 bitewing/치근단 영상의 해석적 광학 아티팩트 (삼각형 방사선투과상 Triangular-Shaped Radiolucency, TSR; 마하밴드 Mach band; 치경부 소각 cervical burnout) 분석.

## Summary

Where Elkhateeb 2022 covers the *technical* PA artifacts, this SR+MA covers the **interpretive** ones — the radiographic **optical effects that masquerade as caries** and drive overdiagnosis. It analyzes three phenomena: **cervical burnout** (tangential beam across the curved proximal surface near the CEJ gives a false radiolucency), the **Mach band effect** (a visual-system contrast illusion at density boundaries such as the EDJ), and **triangular-shaped radiolucencies (TSR)** on the mesial of maxillary molars (Carabelli-cusp superimposition). Pooling five studies, non-carious TSR prevalence on maxillary molars was ~**26%** (pooled 0.308), with a strong **pediatric skew** — ~51% in primary vs ~27% in permanent molars, and >60% in second primary molars. Critically, these optical effects were **misread as caries or fracture in ~13% of observations** (range 4.6–26%), a direct route to unnecessary restorative, endodontic, or extraction treatment. Certainty is **low to very low** (only 5 observational studies, I²>90%). The practical defenses are the **masking test** (cover the enamel margin — if the radiolucency vanishes it was a Mach band), grey-level/contrast software, beam-angle control, and correlating every radiograph with clinical examination.

## Key Contributions

- First meta-analytic quantification of **interpretive PA/bitewing artifacts** (Mach band, cervical burnout, TSR) and the **false-positive caries** they cause.
- **TSR prevalence ~26%** on maxillary molars, **~51% in primary** dentition — a pediatric overdiagnosis red flag.
- **~13% false-positive** rate → concrete overtreatment risk.
- Consolidates **mitigation strategies** (masking test, contrast software, beam angle, AI) and correctly grades the evidence **low–very low**.

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR+MA, PRISMA + Cochrane Handbook, PROSPERO CRD420251083823.
- **Search**: Cochrane/PubMed/Scopus + grey literature; "triangular-shaped radiolucencies" OR "Mach band". 640 → 5 studies (2 prevalence, 3 false-positive), published 2001–2021.
- **Appraisal**: AXIS (prevalence), QUADAS-2 (diagnostic), GRADE certainty; OpenMeta[Analyst] DerSimonian–Laird random effects; Egger's test + funnel plot.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| TSR prevalence (maxillary molars, raw) | 270/1021 = **26.44%** |
| TSR pooled (random effects) | 0.308 (95% CI 0.129–0.487), I²≈98% |
| Permanent molars | 0.199 (158/802) |
| Primary molars | **0.477** (112/223); pooled 51.1% vs 27.4% permanent |
| False-positive (caries/fracture) | 60/464 = **12.93%** (pooled 0.133, I²=89%) |
| FP range | 4.6% (Tabari, TSR) – 26% (Nielsen, Mach band as fracture) |
| Certainty (GRADE) | primary LOW, secondary VERY LOW |

Publication bias: Egger p=0.57 (no clear asymmetry, but low power). Mitigation: **masking test**, digital grey-level/contrast analysis, beam angle/centering, AI post-processing; always correlate with clinical exam.
