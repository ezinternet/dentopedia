## Three-line Summary

In-vitro phantom dosimetry study (female Alderson-Rando phantom, 4 panoramic units: Orthophos XG Plus, ProMax 2D Classic, Viso G7, Veraview X800) measuring organ-specific equivalent doses at thyroid and eye lens using ionization chambers and radiochromic films.

Thyroid equivalent dose ranged 0.30–1.46 µSv and eye lens dose ranged 0.88–4.24 µSv across devices, with significant inter-device differences (Friedman p<0.001); anterior dose distribution follows the fan-beam rotational isocenter trajectory.

Absolute thyroid and eye lens doses from panoramic radiography are very low and device-dependent, providing quantitative context supporting shielding-discontinuation guidance for modern panoramic equipment.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 여성 Alderson-Rando 팬텀을 이용한 파노라마 4기종(Orthophos XG Plus 등) 갑상선·수정체 등가선량 체외 측정 연구 (전리함 + 방사색소 필름).

줄2: 갑상선 등가선량 0.30~1.46 µSv, 수정체 0.88~4.24 µSv로 기기간 유의한 차이 확인(Friedman p<0.001); 전방부 선량은 회전 등심점 궤적을 따름.

줄3: 파노라마 촬영의 갑상선·수정체 절대 선량은 매우 낮고 기기 의존적이어서, 현대 파노라마 장비에서 차폐대 생략을 지지하는 근거 제공.

## Summary

Absolute organ doses are very low and device-dependent, reinforcing that thyroid/eye doses from panoramic are small — context for shielding-discontinuation guidance.

## Key Contributions

- Thyroid equivalent dose 0.30–1.46 µSv; eye lens 0.88–4.24 µSv across 4 modern panoramic devices.
- Significant inter-device differences (Friedman p<0.001); anterior dose follows the fan-beam rotational isocenter trajectory.
- Radiochromic-film mapping from orbital floor to thyroid characterizes the dose distribution.

## Methodology

Female Alderson-Rando phantom with 3D-printed slabs; ionization chamber in orbit and thyroid + 5 radiochromic films; repeated exposures on Orthophos XG Plus, ProMax 2D Classic, Viso G7, Veraview X800.

## Results

Absolute organ doses are very low and device-dependent, reinforcing that thyroid/eye doses from panoramic are small — context for shielding-discontinuation guidance.
