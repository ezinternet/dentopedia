## Three-line Summary

In-vitro fatigue-bond-strength study (J Funct Biomater 2022) comparing OptiBond FL, Prime&Bond NT, Clearfil SE Bond 2, G2-Bond Universal (two-step HEMA-free), and Scotchbond Universal Plus on enamel and dentin with SEM interface analysis.

In etch-and-rinse mode, G2-Bond Universal achieved significantly higher enamel fatigue bond strength than all comparators; Prime&Bond NT showed the lowest dentin fatigue bond strength.

A HEMA-free two-step universal formulation provides superior enamel bond durability under cyclic loading, a more clinically representative durability metric than static bond strength.

## 세줄요약

OptiBond FL·Prime&Bond NT·Clearfil SE Bond 2·G2-Bond Universal(2단계 HEMA-free)·Scotchbond Universal Plus를 법랑질·상아질에 적용한 in-vitro 피로결합강도 연구(J Funct Biomater 2022) + SEM 계면 분석.

Etch-and-rinse 모드에서 G2-Bond Universal이 법랑질 피로결합강도 유의 최고; Prime&Bond NT는 상아질 피로결합강도 최저.

HEMA-free 2단계 universal 제형이 정적 강도보다 임상 대표성이 높은 반복하중(피로) 조건에서 법랑질 결합 내구성 우위를 보인다.

## Summary
This study compared bond durability via fatigue bond strength (plus SEM of enamel/dentin interfaces) for OptiBond FL, Prime&Bond NT, Clearfil SE Bond 2, G2-Bond Universal (two-step HEMA-free), and Scotchbond Universal Plus. In etch-and-rinse mode, G2-Bond Universal showed significantly higher enamel fatigue bond strength than the others, while Prime&Bond NT showed the lowest dentin fatigue bond strength. The work supports the durability advantage of a HEMA-free two-step universal formulation.

## Key Contributions
- Uses fatigue bond strength (cyclic loading) rather than static bond strength, a more clinically representative durability metric.
- Demonstrates HEMA-free two-step universal advantage in enamel.

## Methodology
In-vitro fatigue bond strength testing + SEM of bonded enamel and dentin interfaces; multiple adhesive categories incl. gold-standard OptiBond FL (E&R) and Clearfil SE Bond 2 (SE).

## Results
E&R mode: G2-Bond Universal enamel fatigue bond strength significantly highest; Prime&Bond NT dentin fatigue bond strength lowest.
