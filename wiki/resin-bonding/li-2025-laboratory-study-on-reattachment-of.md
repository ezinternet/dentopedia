## Three-line Summary

In vitro laboratory study comparing 4-META/MMA-TBB resin (Super-Bond C&B), iRoot BP Plus, Fuji IX glass ionomer cement (GIC) and Clearfil SA Luting for vertical root fracture (VRF) reattachment: L929 fibroblast biocompatibility (CCK-8, live/dead staining, flow cytometry), fracture resistance (n = 20/group, bovine incisor roots) and methylene-blue microleakage (n = 10/group).

4-META/MMA-TBB resin was as biocompatible as iRoot BP Plus on flow cytometry (P > 0.05), restored fracture resistance to 220.9 +/- 45.0 N (79.3% of sound roots, 278.5 N) and had the shortest dye penetration (0.63 mm vs Clearfil SA Luting 1.44 mm and Fuji IX GIC 2.08 mm); Fuji IX GIC was weakest (53.2 N), and Clearfil SA Luting was the most cytotoxic.

Bench-only evidence on bovine roots with a gutta-percha wedge load, no aging or periodontal healing: it supports material choice (resin over GIC) for fragment reattachment but cannot establish clinical success, and the bonding interface remained the weak point (mostly mixed Type-III failures).

## Summary

This in vitro study from Qingdao University tested whether 4-META/MMA-TBB resin (Super-Bond C&B), already used clinically for vertical root fracture (VRF) fragment bonding, is a sound root-repair material. Against iRoot BP Plus, Fuji IX glass ionomer cement (GIC) and Clearfil SA Luting, the resin showed acceptable L929 fibroblast biocompatibility (Grade 0-1; viable-cell fraction similar to iRoot BP Plus), while Clearfil SA Luting was clearly cytotoxic (Grade 2-3). In bovine roots with artificial VRF, reattachment with the resin recovered 220.9 N (79.3% of the 278.5 N of sound roots), not different from Clearfil SA Luting (184.0 N) but far above Fuji IX GIC (53.2 N), and it had the shortest methylene-blue penetration (0.63 mm vs 1.44 and 2.08 mm). Failures were mostly mixed Type-III with interfacial debonding in the resin groups, so the bonding interface remains the weak point. This is bench evidence only: bovine roots, a wedge-type load and no aging mean it supports choosing a resin over GIC for reattachment but cannot predict clinical survival.

## Key Contributions

- Fills the material-level gap behind the clinical VRF reattachment case series: biocompatibility, fracture resistance and sealing of 4-META/MMA-TBB resin tested together against three comparators.
- Resin vs GIC is a large, consistent difference on both strength (about 4x) and sealing (about 3x shorter dye penetration), echoing the poor outcomes of GIC-based repairs in the case-series literature.
- Self-adhesive resin cement (Clearfil SA Luting) gave similar strength to the 4-META/MMA-TBB resin but the worst cytotoxicity, so strength alone should not drive the choice of material that sits against periodontal tissue.

## Methodology

In vitro. Biocompatibility on mouse L929 fibroblasts with ISO 10993-12 extracts (CCK-8 at 1/2/3/5 days, live/dead staining, Annexin V/PI flow cytometry; 5 replicates per group). Bovine incisor roots (15 mm, 7-year-old cattle) with VRF created by notching and splitting; reattached with 4-META/MMA-TBB resin, Fuji IX GIC or Clearfil SA Luting (iRoot BP Plus excluded for weak bonding); fracture test n = 20 per group (1.5 mm steel pin on gutta-percha, 1 mm/min, 0.2 mm silicone PDL layer) with failure-mode classification, microleakage n = 10 per group (2% methylene blue, 7 days). One-way ANOVA + Tukey, alpha = 0.05.

## Results

| Measure | Sound control | 4-META/MMA-TBB | Clearfil SA Luting | Fuji IX GIC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fracture resistance (N) | 278.50 +/- 45.19 | 220.90 +/- 45.01 | 184.00 +/- 45.45 | 53.16 +/- 15.76 |
| % of sound root | 100% | 79.3% | 66.1% | ~19% (derived) |
| Failure mode, Type-II / Type-III (n) | n/a | 2 / 18 | 1 / 19 | 0 / 20 |
| Dye penetration (mm) | n/a | 0.63 +/- 0.15 | 1.44 +/- 0.19 | 2.08 +/- 0.17 |
| CCK-8 cytotoxicity grade | n/a | 0-1 (RGR > 75%) | 2 (day 1), 3 (days 2-5) | 0-1 |

Resin vs resin fracture resistance: not significant (P > 0.05); both groups below the sound control and above GIC (P < 0.05). Flow cytometry: viable-cell proportion for the resin and iRoot BP Plus similar (P > 0.05), lowest for Clearfil SA Luting. Limits: bovine roots, wedge-type loading, no aging, L929 cells rather than periodontal cells, no in vivo healing data.
