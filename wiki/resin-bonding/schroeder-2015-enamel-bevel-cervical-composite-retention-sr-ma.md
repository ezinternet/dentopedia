## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised clinical trials (RCTs) comparing enamel bevelling with no bevel for composite restorations in non-carious cervical lesions (NCCLs) of adults with at least 1-year follow-up: 1356 records screened, 14 abstracts retained, 4 trials after full-text review, of which only 2 had low risk of bias.

The pooled risk difference for retention was 0.0 (95% CI -0.04 to 0.04; p = 0.91) and for marginal discolouration 0.05 (95% CI -0.02 to 0.13; p = 0.17), i.e. no superiority of bevelled restorations at the 1-year short-term follow-up.

There is not enough evidence to support bevelling over a non-bevelled technique in NCCLs, especially for longer periods; the conclusion rests on only two low-risk-of-bias RCTs, so better standardised and longer-term RCTs are needed.

## 세줄요약

비치아우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 복합레진 수복에서 법랑질 사면 형성 (Enamel Bevel) 대 비형성을 비교한 1년 이상 추적 무작위 임상시험 (Randomised Clinical Trial, RCT)의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis): 1356건 검색, 초록 선별 후 14편, 전문 검토 후 4편, 이 중 비뚤림 위험 (Risk of Bias) 낮음은 2편뿐.

유지율 (Retention)의 통합 위험차 (Risk Difference)는 0.0 (95% CI -0.04~0.04; p = 0.91), 변연 변색 (Marginal Discolouration)은 0.05 (95% CI -0.02~0.13; p = 0.17)로, 1년 단기 추적에서 사면 형성군의 우월성은 관찰되지 않았다.

사면 형성이 비형성보다 낫다고 뒷받침할 근거가 부족하며 (특히 장기 추적), 결론이 비뚤림 위험 낮은 RCT 2편에 근거하므로 표준화된 장기 RCT가 더 필요하다.

## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis asked whether enamel bevelling, compared with no bevel, improves retention and marginal discolouration of composite restorations in adult non-carious cervical lesions (NCCLs), using only RCTs with at least 1-year follow-up. Of 1356 records, four RCTs were included and only two had low risk of bias. The pooled risk difference was 0.0 (95% CI -0.04 to 0.04; p = 0.91) for retention and 0.05 (95% CI -0.02 to 0.13; p = 0.17) for marginal discolouration, so no superiority of bevelling was seen at 1 year. The authors conclude that evidence is insufficient to support bevelling over non-bevelled preparation for longer periods and that standardised, longer-term RCTs are needed. This is an abstract-only record; trial-level data were not available.

## Key Contributions
- RCT-only synthesis isolating the enamel-bevel variable in NCCL composite restorations.
- Pooled retention risk difference of 0.0 and no significant difference in marginal discolouration at 1 year.
- Highlights a thin evidence base (2 low-risk-of-bias RCTs, short follow-up).

## Methodology
Systematic review and meta-analysis; searches of MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, LILACS, BBO, Cochrane Library, SIGLE, IADR abstracts, trial registries and thesis databases without restriction; RCTs in adult NCCLs comparing bevelled and non-bevelled composite restorations with at least 1-year follow-up; Cochrane risk of bias tool; 1356 records, 14 screened to full text, 4 included.

## Results
| Outcome | Risk difference | 95% CI | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retention | 0.0 | -0.04 to 0.04 | 0.91 |
| Marginal discolouration | 0.05 | -0.02 to 0.13 | 0.17 |

Interpretation caveat: absence of evidence of benefit from two low-risk-of-bias RCTs at 1 year is not proof of equivalence over longer follow-up; the authors themselves state the body of evidence is insufficient.
