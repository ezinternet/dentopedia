## Three-line Summary

Interim 18-month analysis of a 36-month cluster-randomized trial (NCT02631161): 88 patients (50-70 y) with 175 sclerotic non-carious cervical lesions (sNCCLs) restored without mechanical preparation with glass hybrid (EQUIA Forte Fil/Coat) or resin composite (OptiBond FL + Filtek Supreme XTE).
Of 160 restorations reassessed (78 patients, mean 18 months), 15 (18%) failed in GH and 11 (12%) in RC with no significant survival difference (p = 0.904, Cox); retention loss was the most common failure in both groups, and GH took less chairtime (8.6 ± 4.3 vs 11.7 ± 5.7 min, p < 0.001).
GH may be a suitable alternative to RC in sNCCLs at this interim point; this is abstract-only data from an interim analysis, so longer follow-up is needed.

재평가된 160개 수복물 중 실패는 GH 15개 (18%), RC 11개 (12%)로 생존율 차이 없음 (p = 0.904, Cox); 두 군 모두 유지 상실 (Retention Loss)이 가장 흔한 실패 원인이었고, 시술 시간은 GH가 짧았음 (8.6 ± 4.3분 vs 11.7 ± 5.7분, p < 0.001).
이 중간 시점에서 GH는 sNCCL에서 RC의 대안이 될 수 있으나, 초록 수준 정보의 중간 분석이므로 장기 추적 결과 확인이 필요.

## Summary

This is the 18-month interim analysis of a 36-month cluster-randomized trial (NCT02631161) comparing a glass hybrid (EQUIA Forte Fil/Coat) with an adhesive plus nanofilled resin composite (OptiBond FL / Filtek Supreme XTE) for sclerotic non-carious cervical lesions, restored without mechanical cavity preparation. Eighty-eight patients aged 50-70 years with 175 lesions were randomized; 160 restorations in 78 patients were assessed by FDI criteria after a mean of 18 months. Failure occurred in 18% of GH and 12% of RC restorations without a significant survival difference (p = 0.904), and retention loss was the dominant failure mode in both arms. GH placement was about 3 minutes faster. Only the PubMed abstract was available, so this page contains no detail beyond it.

## Key Contributions
- Randomized comparison of GH vs RC specifically in sclerotic (hypermineralized-dentin) NCCLs placed without cavity preparation.
- Retention loss identified as the leading failure mode for both restorative strategies (relevant to cervical restoration dislodgement).
- Risk factors for failure: younger patient age (OR 0.90 per year of age, 0.81-0.99) and mandibular teeth (2.89, 1.00-8.31).
- Chairtime advantage for GH (8.6 vs 11.7 min).

## Methodology
Cluster-randomized controlled trial (NCT02631161), 88 patients (50-70 y), 175 sNCCLs; GH (EQUIA Forte Fil/Coat, GC) vs RC (OptiBond FL, Kerr + Filtek Supreme XTE, 3M). No mechanical cavity preparation; treatment time recorded. Evaluation by FDI criteria after 18 months (min/max 8/25). Multi-level Cox regression for survival factors; generalized linear mixed model for treatment time.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Reassessed | 78 patients / 160 restorations |
| Failed restorations | GH 15 (18%) vs RC 11 (12%); p = 0.904 (Cox) |
| Most common failure | Retention loss (both groups) |
| Patient age | OR 0.90 (0.81-0.99) per year — older patients lower risk |
| Mandibular teeth | 2.89 (1.00-8.31) — higher risk |
| Treatment time | GH 8.6 ± 4.3 min vs RC 11.7 ± 5.7 min (p < 0.001) |

Limitations: abstract-only; interim analysis; failure counts by mode and dropout details not available.
