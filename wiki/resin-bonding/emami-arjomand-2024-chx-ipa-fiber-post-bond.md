## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (54 single-canal mandibular premolars, 6 subgroups of n=9) comparing distilled-water control, 2% chlorhexidine (CHX), and 70% isopropyl alcohol (IA) root-dentin conditioning before fiber post cementation with TheraCem self-adhesive cement, testing immediate (24h) and late (post-thermocycling) push-out bond strength (PBS).

The IA group had the highest PBS (21.12 MPa immediate) and the control group the lowest after thermocycling (7.48 MPa); IA significantly outperformed both CHX and control at all timepoints and root levels (P<0.001), while CHX outperformed the water control (P<0.05); PBS declined significantly after thermocycling and from cervical to apical root thirds in every group.

Isopropyl alcohol conditioning of root-canal dentin before self-adhesive resin cement cementation of fiber posts meaningfully improves immediate and long-term bond durability compared to chlorhexidine or no conditioning — but the in vitro thermocycling protocol (5000 cycles ≈ 6 months) cannot fully predict clinical longevity.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 연구 (하악 단근관 소구치 54개, 6개 하위군 각 n=9) — 파이버 포스트를 TheraCem 자가접착 레진시멘트(Self-Adhesive Resin Cement)로 합착하기 전 증류수 대조군 vs 2% 클로르헥시딘(Chlorhexidine, CHX) vs 70% 이소프로필알코올(Isopropyl Alcohol, IA)로 근관 상아질을 전처리 후 즉시(24시간)·후기(열순환 후) 푸시아웃 결합강도(Push-out Bond Strength, PBS) 비교.

IA군이 즉시 PBS 최고치(21.12 MPa), 대조군이 열순환 후 최저치(7.48 MPa)를 기록; IA가 모든 시점·치근 부위에서 CHX·대조군을 유의하게 압도(P<0.001)했고, CHX는 증류수 대조군보다 우위(P<0.05); 모든 군에서 열순환 후, 그리고 치경부→치근단부로 갈수록 PBS가 유의하게 감소.

자가접착 레진시멘트(Self-Adhesive Resin Cement)로 파이버 포스트를 합착하기 전 이소프로필알코올(Isopropyl Alcohol, IA)로 근관 상아질을 전처리하면 클로르헥시딘(Chlorhexidine, CHX)이나 무처리 대비 즉시·장기 결합 내구성이 유의하게 향상되나, in vitro 열순환 프로토콜(5000회≈6개월)은 실제 임상 수명을 완전히 예측하지 못하는 한계가 있음.

## Summary

This in vitro study from Islamic Azad University (Tehran, Iran) tested whether pre-cementation dentin conditioning agent choice affects fiber post retention when using a modern self-adhesive resin cement (TheraCem, Bisco). Fifty-four single-canal mandibular premolars were endodontically treated, prepared for post space, and randomly assigned to root-dentin conditioning with distilled water (control), 2% chlorhexidine (CHX), or 70% isopropyl alcohol (IA), before fiber post cementation with TheraCem. Each group was split for immediate (24h) versus late (post-thermocycling, 5000 cycles) push-out bond strength (PBS) testing across cervical, middle, and apical root sections. IA produced the strongest bonds at every timepoint and root level, followed by CHX, with the untreated control performing worst — an effect the authors attribute to IA's low polarity displacing less dentinal-tubule moisture than ethanol while still improving resin-monomer wettability and degree of conversion, which is particularly relevant for self-adhesive cements that specifically require a not-too-dry dentin surface. All groups lost significant bond strength after thermocycling and toward the apical root third, consistent with known hydrolytic degradation and reduced apical access/light-cure limitations.

## Key Contributions

- First direct comparison of CHX and isopropyl alcohol as dentin conditioning agents specifically for fiber post cementation with a self-adhesive resin cement (TheraCem).
- Demonstrates a dose-independent hierarchy (IA > CHX > control) holding across both immediate and aged (post-thermocycling) bond strength and across all three root thirds.
- Shows CHX's PBS benefit over an untreated control persists with a non-etching self-adhesive cement, but is smaller than IA's — a nuance relevant to protocol selection when using self-adhesive rather than etch-and-rinse cements.

## Methodology

54 single-canal mandibular premolars, endodontically treated (ProTaper rotary, NaOCl/EDTA irrigation, gutta-percha + AH26 obturation), post space prepared with Peeso reamers (7 mm from CEJ). Randomized to 3 groups (n=18): distilled water control, 2% CHX, 70% IA (60-second irrigation, 27-gauge needle), then air-dried. Fiber posts (#2) cemented with TheraCem self-adhesive cement per manufacturer instructions, light-cured 60s, core build-up with Estelite Sigma Quick composite. Each group subdivided (n=9) into immediate (24h) and late (5000 thermal cycles, 5°-55°C, ≈6 months clinical aging) PBS testing. Roots sectioned into 1±0.1 mm slices (cervical/middle/apical); push-out on a universal testing machine (500 N, 0.5 mm/min). Failure mode assessed under stereomicroscopy (×40). Repeated-measures ANOVA + Tukey's test, α=0.05.

## Results

| Group | Timepoint | Coronal (MPa) | Middle (MPa) | Apical (MPa) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Control | Immediate | 16.66±1.91 | 12.80±2.44 | 7.73±2.35 |
| Control | Late (post-thermocycling) | 13.22±2.41 | 9.61±1.70 | 7.48±1.20 |
| CHX | Immediate | 17.75±2.97 | 14.34±3.13 | 10.75±2.63 |
| CHX | Late (post-thermocycling) | 17.04±1.19 | 14.13±1.81 | 10.04±2.41 |
| IA | Immediate | 21.12±1.54 | 19.40±2.29 | 15.80±3.23 |
| IA | Late (post-thermocycling) | 20.25±1.48 | 19.45±2.27 | 15.30±2.86 |

- IA significantly higher than CHX and control at all timepoints and root sections (P<0.001).
- CHX significantly higher than control, immediate and late (P<0.05).
- PBS decreased significantly after thermocycling in all groups (P<0.003).
- PBS decreased significantly from cervical to apical in all groups (P<0.001).
- Predominant failure mode across groups/timepoints: adhesive failure at the cement-dentin interface (post fully enveloped by cement).
