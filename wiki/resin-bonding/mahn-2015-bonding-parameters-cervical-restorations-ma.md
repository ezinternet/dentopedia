## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective clinical trials of Class V (cervical) restorations with at least 18 months of observation (MEDLINE and SCOPUS): 81 studies, 185 experiments, 47 adhesives, covering composite resin, glass-ionomer cement (GIC), resin-modified glass ionomer (RMGIC) and polyacid-modified resin composite (PMRC), analyzed with a linear mixed model with a random study effect.

At 5 years, on average 12.3% of cervical restorations were lost, 27.9% showed marginal discoloration and 34.6% showed deteriorated marginal integrity (clinical index: 17.4% failures at 5 years, 32.3% at 8 years); rubber-dam isolation combined with roughened dentin gave significantly higher retention than unprepared dentin or no rubber dam (p < 0.05), enamel beveling had no influence, and one-step self-etch adhesives had a significantly worse clinical index than two-step self-etch (p = 0.026) and three-step etch-and-rinse (p = 0.002).

Two-step self-etch or three-step etch-and-rinse adhesives with composite resin are preferable to one-step self-etch adhesives, GIC-based materials and PMRCs for cervical lesions; this is an abstract-only record (full text not retrieved), and the isolation/dentin-roughening findings come from between-experiment comparisons rather than randomized contrasts.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상시험 기반 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis): 관찰기간 18개월 이상의 5급 (Class V) 치경부 수복물 전향적 임상시험 81편·185개 실험·접착제 47종 (복합레진, 글라스아이오노머 시멘트 (Glass-Ionomer Cement, GIC), 레진강화형 글라스아이오노머 (Resin-Modified Glass Ionomer Cement, RMGIC), 폴리애시드 변형 레진 복합체 (Polyacid-Modified Resin Composite, PMRC)), 연구 임의효과를 포함한 선형 혼합모형으로 분석.

5년 시점 평균 12.3%가 탈락 (유지 소실), 27.9%가 변연 변색, 34.6%가 변연 적합도 저하를 보였다 (임상 지수: 5년 실패율 17.4%, 8년 32.3%); 러버댐 (Rubber Dam) 격리와 상아질 조면화를 함께 시행한 군이 상아질 비처리군 또는 러버댐 미사용군보다 유지율이 유의하게 높았고 (p < 0.05), 법랑질 사면 형성 (Beveling)은 영향이 없었으며, 1단계 자가산부식 접착제 (One-step Self-etch Adhesive)는 2단계 자가산부식 (p = 0.026) 및 3단계 산부식-수세 접착제 (Etch-and-Rinse Adhesive, p = 0.002)보다 임상 지수가 유의하게 나빴다.

치경부 병소에는 1단계 자가산부식 접착제·GIC 계열·PMRC보다 2단계 자가산부식 또는 3단계 산부식-수세 접착제와 복합레진 조합이 권장된다; 초록만 확보한 기록 (전문 미확보)이며, 격리·상아질 조면화 결과는 무작위 대비가 아닌 실험 간 비교에서 나온 것이라는 한계가 있다.

## Summary
This systematic review and meta-analysis pooled 81 prospective clinical trials (185 experiments, 47 adhesives) of Class V restorations with at least 18 months of follow-up. On average 12.3% of restorations were lost, 27.9% showed marginal discoloration and 34.6% showed marginal-integrity deterioration at 5 years, with a clinical index of 17.4% failures at 5 years and 32.3% at 8 years; secondary caries was almost absent. Retention was significantly higher when rubber-dam isolation was combined with roughened dentin, whereas enamel beveling had no effect. One-step self-etch adhesives performed significantly worse than two-step self-etch and three-step etch-and-rinse adhesives, so the authors favor the latter two with composite resin over one-step self-etch, GIC-based materials and PMRCs. Because only the abstract was retrieved, per-adhesive estimates and bias assessment are unavailable.

## Key Contributions
- Benchmarks for long-term cervical restoration deterioration (retention, discoloration, marginal integrity) at 5 and 8 years.
- Operative-technique findings: rubber dam plus dentin roughening favors retention; enamel beveling does not matter.
- Adhesive-class ranking with one-step self-etch inferior to two-step self-etch and three-step etch-and-rinse.

## Methodology
Systematic review and meta-analysis (MEDLINE, SCOPUS; English; prospective trials with at least 18 months of observation reporting retention, marginal discoloration, marginal integrity, marginal caries and operative technique). A linear mixed model, log(-log(Y/100)) = beta + alpha log(T) + error with a random study effect, summarized the non-linear deterioration of each experiment. Abstract-only: full text not retrieved.
