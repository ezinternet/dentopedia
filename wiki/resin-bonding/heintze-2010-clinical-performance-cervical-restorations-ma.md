## Three-line Summary

Meta-analysis of prospective clinical studies (observation of at least 18 months) of cervical restorations made of composite and glass-ionomer derivatives: 50 clinical studies involving 40 adhesive systems, analyzed with linear mixed models containing study and experiment effects.

On average 10% of cervical fillings were lost and 24% showed marginal discoloration at 3 years (range 0-50% retention loss, 0-74% discoloration); secondary caries was hardly detected; 2-step self-etch adhesives performed best and 1-step self-etch worst, restorations on prepared/roughened dentin/enamel showed significantly higher retention than on unprepared dentin (p<0.05), while enamel beveling and isolation type (rubber dam vs cotton rolls) had no significant influence.

The authors advise 2-step self-etch or 3-step etch-and-rinse adhesives over 1-step self-etch and glass-ionomer derivatives, and roughening dentin (and enamel) before placement; this is an abstract-only record, and the roughening and isolation findings come from between-experiment comparisons rather than randomized contrasts.

## 세줄요약

관찰기간 18개월 이상의 치경부 수복물 전향적 임상연구를 대상으로 한 메타분석 (Meta-Analysis): 복합레진 및 글라스아이오노머 계열 수복물, 임상연구 50편·접착 시스템 40종, 연구 효과와 실험 효과를 포함한 선형 혼합모형으로 분석.

3년 시점 평균 10%가 탈락 (유지 소실), 24%가 변연 변색을 보였고 (범위: 탈락 0-50%, 변색 0-74%) 이차우식은 거의 없었다; 2단계 자가산부식 접착제 (Two-step Self-etch Adhesive)가 가장 좋고 1단계 자가산부식 접착제 (One-step Self-etch Adhesive)가 가장 나빴으며, 상아질·법랑질을 삭제·조면화한 수복물이 비처리 상아질보다 유지율이 유의하게 높았으나 (p<0.05), 법랑질 사면 형성 (Beveling)과 격리 방식 (러버댐 (Rubber Dam) 대 면구)은 유의한 영향이 없었다.

1단계 자가산부식 접착제·글라스아이오노머 계열보다 2단계 자가산부식 또는 3단계 산부식-수세 접착제 (Etch-and-Rinse Adhesive)를 권장하고 수복 전 상아질 (및 법랑질) 조면화를 권고했다; 초록만 확보한 기록 (전문 미확보)이며 조면화·격리 결과는 무작위 대비가 아닌 실험 간 비교에서 나온 것이라는 한계가 있다.

## Summary
This meta-analysis pooled 50 prospective clinical studies (40 adhesive systems, observation of at least 18 months) of cervical restorations in composite and glass-ionomer derivatives. About 10% of restorations were lost and 24% showed marginal discoloration at 3 years, with very wide between-study variability and hardly any secondary caries. The adhesive/restorative class had the strongest influence on retention: 2-step self-etch performed best and 1-step self-etch worst. Restorations on prepared or roughened dentin/enamel were retained significantly better than those on unprepared dentin, whereas enamel beveling and isolation type had no significant influence. Only the abstract was retrieved, so effect sizes and bias assessment are unavailable.

## Key Contributions
- Early quantitative benchmark for cervical restoration deterioration at 3 years (10% lost, 24% marginal discoloration).
- Adhesive-class ranking for retention (2-step self-etch best, 1-step self-etch worst).
- Operative-technique findings: roughening dentin/enamel helps retention; enamel beveling and isolation type do not.

## Methodology
Meta-analysis of prospective clinical studies of cervical restorations with at least 18 months of observation; linear mixed models with study and experiment effects. Search databases are not stated in the abstract. Abstract-only: full text not retrieved.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Studies / adhesive systems | 50 / 40 |
| Restorations lost at 3 years | ~10% (range 0-50%) |
| Marginal discoloration at 3 years | ~24% (range 0-74%) |
| Secondary caries | hardly any detected |
| Adhesive/restorative class | most significant influence; 2-step self-etch best, 1-step self-etch worst; 3-step etch-and-rinse, GIC/RMGIC, 2-step etch-and-rinse and PMRC in between |
| Prepared/roughened dentin/enamel vs unprepared dentin | significantly higher retention (p<0.05) |
| Enamel beveling | no significant influence |
| Isolation (rubber dam vs cotton rolls) | no significant influence |
