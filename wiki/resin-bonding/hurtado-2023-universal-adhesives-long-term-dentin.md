## Three-line Summary
In-vitro study of three universal adhesives (Clearfil SE Bond, Scotchbond Universal, Xeno Select) bonded to dentin and aged with 6-month water storage, measuring µTBS and nanoleakage.

After 6-month aging, Clearfil SE Bond and Scotchbond Universal maintained significantly higher µTBS and lower nanoleakage than Xeno Select.

10-MDP-containing universal adhesives show durable dentin bonding over time; selecting an adhesive without MDP chemistry risks accelerated hybrid-layer degradation.

## 세줄요약
인 비트로 연구 — 유니버설 접착제 3종(Clearfil SE Bond, Scotchbond Universal, Xeno Select)을 상아질에 접착 후 6개월 수중 노화하여 µTBS와 nanoleakage를 평가.

6개월 노화 후 Clearfil SE Bond·Scotchbond Universal이 Xeno Select보다 유의하게 높은 µTBS와 낮은 nanoleakage를 나타냈다.

10-MDP 함유 유니버설 접착제는 장기 상아질 접착 내구성이 우수하며, MDP가 없는 접착제 선택 시 혼성층 조기 분해 위험이 있다.

## Summary
This study evaluated the long-term (6-month water storage) microtensile bond strength (µTBS) and nanoleakage of universal adhesives to dentin. Clearfil SE Bond (with Scotchbond Universal) outperformed Xeno Select, showing higher µTBS and lower nanoleakage after aging, reinforcing the durability advantage of 10-MDP-containing systems.

## Key Contributions
- Long-term (6-month) aging data with both µTBS and nanoleakage endpoints.
- Reinforces 10-MDP system durability versus a weaker comparator (Xeno Select).

## Methodology
In-vitro µTBS + nanoleakage of universal adhesives to dentin after 6-month water storage.

## Results
CSE and Scotchbond Universal > Xeno Select: higher µTBS, lower nanoleakage after 6-month aging.
