## Three-line Summary

SR and NMA (82 studies, 22 adhesives): G2BU ranks highest in self-etch mode (SUCRA 5.86%, before and after ageing) while ZU ranks highest in etch-and-rinse mode (SUCRA 4.21–6.32%); GPB and FU consistently rank lowest across all conditions.

After ageing, CUBQ and IBU show the steepest bond degradation in E-R mode, while most other adhesives remain comparable to Scotchbond Universal (SBU) reference.

Clinical decision: choose G2BU for S-E or ZU for E-R applications; avoid GPB, FU, and CUBQ for long-term bonding.

## 세줄요약

SR+네트워크 메타분석 (82편, 22종): S-E 모드 1위 G2BU(SUCRA 5.86%), E-R 모드 1위 ZU(6.32%); GPB·FU 노화 전후 모두 최저.

노화 후 CUBQ·IBU는 E-R 모드에서 급격한 접착력 저하 — 제형 전략이 특정 기능 단량체 존재보다 성능 결정 인자.

임상 선택: S-E → G2BU, E-R → ZU; GPB·FU·CUBQ 장기 사용 회피.

## Background

Universal adhesives (UAs) can be applied in self-etch (S-E), selective-etch, or etch-and-rinse (E-R) modes, providing clinical flexibility. Since Scotchbond Universal was introduced in 2011 as the first such system, over 20 competing products have entered the market. Conventional pairwise meta-analyses (hardan-2021, hong-2021) compared adhesives with limited direct comparisons. This 2026 network meta-analysis enables simultaneous ranking of 22 adhesives using SUCRA values derived from both direct and indirect evidence.

- **Search**: PubMed, Scopus, WoS; search date November 15, 2025; PROSPERO CRD420261279711
- **Control**: Scotchbond Universal (SBU) — reference in all included studies
- **Inclusion**: in vitro, sound human/bovine dentin; S-E or E-R; microtensile/shear/microshear bond strength (MPa)
- **Exclusion**: caries-affected/primary/other substrates; no SBU comparator; experimental formulations
- **82 studies** from 1677 records; 2013–2025

## Methods Summary

- 22 adhesives in S-E networks (before/after ageing); 18 in E-R networks
- Mean bond strength: S-E before ageing 31.02 MPa; E-R before ageing 34.68 MPa; S-E after ageing 28.27 MPa; E-R after ageing 34.69 MPa
- Risk of bias: 52.4% low, 46.4% moderate, 1.2% high
- Inconsistency: Nodesplit model; inconsistencies in 3–6 pairs per analysis (excluded from final)
- SUCRA: lower = better (probability of being best)

## Clinical Significance

1. **S-E preference → choose G2BU**: consistently top-ranked in both time points; two-step primer + adhesive architecture may explain superior performance
2. **E-R preference → choose ZU**: consistently top-ranked; ZU edges SBU in both aging conditions
3. **Avoid GPB and FU**: significantly lower than SBU in multiple conditions — both S-E and E-R, before and after ageing
4. **CUBQ risk in E-R long-term**: best quick-application product in short-term but worst performer in E-R after ageing → use in S-E mode or accept short-term limitation
5. **TUB is E-R mode if selected**: poor in S-E but comparable in E-R
6. **Functional monomers not sufficient**: having 10-MDP does not guarantee high SUCRA rank — formulation totality matters more than monomer presence alone
7. **Most adhesives acceptable short-term**: only 8/22 (S-E) or 3/18 (E-R) are significantly inferior before ageing — long-term data is the differentiator

## Limitations

- In vitro only — clinical validation needed
- Inclusion required SBU as control → excludes studies without this reference (selection bias)
- Surface preparation heterogeneity (#180–#4000 grit) affects absolute MPa values
- 29.2% of studies did not report surface treatment time
- 45.7% of studies did not apply ageing → ageing networks smaller and less powered
- Inconsistencies in 3–6 pairs per analysis; excluded via Leave-One-Out (results unchanged)
