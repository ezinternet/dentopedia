## Three-line Summary

In-vitro bench study (80 bovine lower-incisor root models with a 300 um fracture gap, n=16/group, 5 groups, 50,000 cycles of 70 N loading simulating 1 month of mastication) comparing a 4-META/MMA-TBB resin control (SB-G) with a chemically cured bonding agent plus blue core composite (UB + ESTECORE, EC) under four dentin surface treatments for repair of vertically fractured roots.

EC-G (10% citric acid / 3% ferric chloride "10-3" solution + UB + EC) had the highest microtensile bond strength (46.43 MPa vs 39.36 MPa for SB-G; p=0.0092 vs SB-G, p=0.0027 vs EC, p=0.0051 vs EC-P) and the most cohesive-in-dentin failures; the 40% phosphate pretreatment (EC-P) leaked significantly more than other EC groups, but only at the 6.5 mm level (p=0.0227), with no difference at 1 mm or 13 mm.

Bench-only evidence (bovine teeth, 1 month of loading, no cores/crowns/periodontal tissue, no biocompatibility testing), so it supports a candidate material/pretreatment for the adhesive-repair + intentional-replantation technique but gives no clinical survival information.

## Summary

Shimizu and colleagues tested whether replacing the unfilled 4-META/MMA-TBB resin cement (Super-Bond) with a chemically cured bonding agent (Tokuyama Universal Bond) plus a blue core composite (ESTECORE), combined with different dentin pretreatments, improves bonding durability and sealing in a bovine-tooth model of the "adhesive restoration + intentional replantation" repair of vertical root fractures. After 50,000 loading cycles (about 1 month of mastication, the temporary-splint period), the 10-3 solution + UB + EC group had the highest microtensile bond strength (46.43 MPa vs 39.36 MPa for the Super-Bond control) and the highest share of cohesive failures in dentin. Microleakage did not depend on the adhesive material, but 40% phosphate pretreatment leaked more than the other EC groups at the mid-root level. The authors conclude EC is a suitable adhesive system for this repair and that the 10-3 solution pretreatment is advantageous, while acknowledging the model covers only short-term splinting and no cores or crowns.

## Key Contributions

- Bench comparison of a composite-based adhesive system (UB + EC) against Super-Bond for VRF fragment bonding, with dentin pretreatment as the second variable.
- Reports that the pretreatment, not the material, drove microleakage differences, and that 10-3 solution and EDTA were comparable and better than 40% phosphate on sealing.
- Gives handling rationale for the composite option (syringe delivery, visible blue colour against dentin, short light-cure) over Super-Bond's poor operability, colour discrimination and long curing.

## Methodology

80 bovine lower incisors, 5 groups of 16 (SB-G control, EC, EC-G, EC-P, EC-E); 17 mm root models with a 300 um gap, mounted in silicone simulating PDL/alveolar bone; 70 N, 2 Hz, 50,000 cycles in 37 C water; silver-nitrate microleakage scored 1-4 at 1, 6.5, 13 mm (Kruskal-Wallis, Steel-Dwass); μTBS on 1 mm2 beams (ANOVA, Tukey); failure-mode classification with SEM.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| μTBS, highest group | EC-G 46.43 MPa; SB-G control 39.36 MPa; overall ANOVA p<0.001 |
| EC-G vs comparators | SB-G p=0.0092; EC p=0.0027; EC-P p=0.0051 |
| Microleakage by cut level | no difference among levels within a group (p>0.05) |
| Microleakage by group | EC-P differs from other EC groups at 6.5 mm only (p=0.0227); none at 1 mm or 13 mm |
| SB-G failure modes | adhesive 57.14%; cohesive in material 34.07% |
| EC-G failure modes | fewest adhesive, most cohesive-in-dentin failures |

Per-group tables and figures were absent from the retrieved text; only prose values are recorded. The abstract's statement that EC, EC-G and EC-E leaked significantly less than EC-P is supported in the Results only at 6.5 mm.

Limitations: bovine teeth; 1 month of loading only; no cores/crowns, blood contamination, periodontal cells or actual replantation; biocompatibility of the core composite untested (all stated or implied by the authors); manufacturer products, with funding/conflict statements not in the retrieved text.
