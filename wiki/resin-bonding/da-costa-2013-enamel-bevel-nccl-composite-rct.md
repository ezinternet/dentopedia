## Three-line Summary

Within-patient clinical trial (randomization not stated in the abstract; classified as rct) in 42 adults (more than 20 teeth in occlusion, at least two NCCLs each; 84 restorations) comparing non-beveled cavities with a short enamel bevel (45 degrees, 1-2 mm) restored with a two-step etch-and-rinse adhesive (ExciTE) and resin composite (4 Seasons), evaluated at baseline, 6 and 12 months with modified USPHS criteria.

The 12-month retention rate was 91% (95% CI 77-97%) with bevel versus 88% (95% CI 73-95%) without bevel, a non-significant difference, and no other USPHS criterion differed between groups (Fisher test, p > 0.05); no significant change across recall times within either group (McNemar test, p > 0.05).

Enamel beveling does not appear to be necessary for retention of composite restorations in NCCLs restored with a two-step etch-and-rinse system, but follow-up is only 12 months, the sample is small, and this abstract-only record cannot be checked for allocation details or generalized to self-etch or universal adhesives.

## 세줄요약

환자 내 비교 임상시험 (초록에 무작위 배정 명시 없음, RCT로 분류): 성인 42명 (교합 치아 20개 초과, 비우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 2개 이상), 수복물 84개를 법랑질 사면 없음 (대조)과 짧은 사면 (45도, 길이 1-2 mm)으로 나누어 2단계 산부식-수세 접착제 (Etch-and-Rinse Adhesive)와 복합레진으로 수복하고, 수정 미국공중보건국 (USPHS) 기준으로 기저·6개월·12개월에 평가.

12개월 유지율 (Retention Rate)은 사면군 91% (95% 신뢰구간 77-97%), 비사면군 88% (73-95%)로 유의한 차이가 없었고, 다른 평가 항목에서도 군 간 차이가 없었다 (Fisher 검정, p > 0.05); 각 군 내에서 회복 시점 간 변화도 유의하지 않았다 (McNemar 검정, p > 0.05).

2단계 산부식-수세 접착제를 쓰는 NCCL 복합레진 수복에서는 법랑질 사면 형성이 필수는 아닐 수 있다; 다만 추적 12개월·소규모 표본이며, 초록만 확보한 기록 (전문 미확보)이라 배정·눈가림 세부와 자가산부식·유니버설 접착제로의 일반화는 확인할 수 없다.

## Summary
This clinical trial compared beveled and non-beveled enamel margins in 84 composite restorations placed in non-carious cervical lesions of 42 patients, using a two-step etch-and-rinse adhesive and the resin composite 4 Seasons. At 12 months retention was 91% with a short 45-degree bevel and 88% without, with overlapping confidence intervals and no difference in other modified USPHS criteria. The authors conclude that beveling may not be clinically relevant for retention in NCCLs when a two-step etch-and-rinse system is used. Only the PubMed abstract was available, so randomization details and lesion characteristics could not be verified.

## Key Contributions
- Direct clinical test of enamel beveling in NCCL composite restorations, where laboratory data favor beveling but clinical data were scarce.
- 12-month retention 91% (77-97%) beveled vs 88% (73-95%) non-beveled; no difference in other criteria.
- Supports omitting the bevel (and the extra tooth reduction) with two-step etch-and-rinse adhesives.

## Methodology
Forty-two patients with more than 20 teeth in occlusion and at least two NCCLs; cavities allocated to non-beveled (control) or short bevel (45 degrees, 1-2 mm, diamond bur). Restored with ExciTE (2-step etch-and-rinse) and 4 Seasons composite (Ivoclar Vivadent); 84 restorations evaluated at baseline, 6 and 12 months by modified USPHS criteria. Fisher test between groups; McNemar test across recalls. Abstract-only: full text not retrieved.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| 12-month retention, beveled | 91% (95% CI 77-97%) |
| 12-month retention, non-beveled | 88% (95% CI 73-95%) |
