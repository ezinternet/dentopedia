## Three-line Summary

In vitro study of 80 bovine-incisor blocks with simulated conical Class V cavities (enamel margins, internal dentin walls), 8 groups of n=10 in a 2 x 2 x 2 design: 1-mm enamel bevel vs none, universal adhesive (Ambar Universal APS, 10-MDP, pH ~2.57) in etch-and-rinse vs self-etch mode, conventional nanohybrid vs bulk-fill composite; outcome was push-out bond strength after 24 h (three-way ANOVA, Tukey).

Bevel did not affect bond strength (p=0.177); etch-and-rinse beat self-etch (p=0.042; cell means 9.85-13.7 vs 9.95-12.3 MPa) and bulk-fill beat conventional composite (p<0.001); no interactions (p>0.05). All cell means lay within 9.85-13.7 MPa.

Within this bench model, beveling gave no bond-strength gain, so it should be reserved for esthetic needs; the adhesive-strategy gap was small and, like the rest, is a 24-h bovine-tissue result without aging or failure-mode analysis that cannot be extrapolated directly to clinical practice.

## 세줄요약

소 절치 (bovine incisor) 블록 80개에 원추형 5급 와동 (법랑질 변연, 내면 상아질)을 형성한 시험관 내 연구 (In vitro): 법랑질 사면 (Bevel) 유무 × 유니버설 접착제 (Universal Adhesive) 산부식-수세 (Etch-and-Rinse, ER) 대 자가산부식 (Self-Etch, SE) × 일반 복합레진 대 벌크필 복합레진 (Bulk-fill Composite)의 2×2×2 설계, 군당 n=10, 24시간 후 푸시아웃 결합강도 (Push-out Bond Strength) 측정.

사면은 결합강도에 영향이 없었고 (p=0.177), ER이 SE보다 유의하게 높았으며 (p=0.042), 벌크필이 일반 복합레진보다 유의하게 높았다 (p<0.001); 상호작용은 없었다 (p>0.05). 전체 셀 평균은 9.85-13.7 MPa 범위였다.

이 실험 모델에서는 사면 형성이 결합강도를 높이지 못했으므로 심미 목적이 있을 때만 시행하는 것이 합리적이다; 다만 24시간·소 치아·노화 및 파절양상 분석 없는 결과라 임상에 직접 외삽할 수 없다.

## Summary

This Brazilian bench study used 80 bovine-incisor blocks (n=10 per group) with standardized conical cavities (2.0 mm deep, 1.8 mm wide) to test three factors on push-out bond strength: a 1-mm, ~45-degree enamel bevel, application mode of a 10-MDP universal adhesive (etch-and-rinse vs self-etch without selective enamel etching), and composite type (nanohybrid placed in two increments vs non-flowable bulk-fill in one 2-mm increment). Bevel had no significant effect (p=0.177) and no interaction with the other factors; etch-and-rinse (p=0.042) and bulk-fill (p<0.001) each raised bond strength. The authors conclude bevel is unnecessary for bonding in enamel-margin Class V cavities and should be kept for esthetic zones, while stressing that this is a short-term laboratory result.

## Key Contributions

- Bench evidence that a 1-mm enamel bevel does not improve Class V push-out bond strength with a universal adhesive in either etching mode, agreeing in direction with the clinical meta-analysis [[resin-bonding/mahn-2015-bonding-parameters-cervical-restorations-ma]] (enamel beveling had no clinical influence).
- Supports etch-and-rinse use of a universal adhesive over self-etch without selective enamel etching, in line with the NCCL SR+MA favoring etch-and-rinse [[resin-bonding/assis-2023-acid-etching-universal-adhesive-nccl-sr-ma]].
- Shows a bulk-fill composite out-performing a conventional nanohybrid in a high C-factor cavity (bench only).

## Methodology

- Bovine incisor blocks 7 x 7 mm (1 mm enamel + 1 mm dentin); conical cavity 2.0 mm deep, 1.8 mm diameter at enamel, bur #2130; bevel freehand with cone bur #4130 at ~45 degrees, ~1.0 mm extension, measured per specimen and added to bonded area.
- Ambar Universal APS (FGM; 10-MDP, pH ~2.57). ER: 37% H3PO4 15 s on enamel and dentin, rinse 15 s; SE: no etching. Cure 10 s at 1,400 mW/cm2.
- Opallis (nanohybrid, 2 x 1 mm) vs Opus Bulk Fill APS (non-flowable, 1 x 2 mm); 24 h in water at 37 C; discs finished.
- Push-out at 0.5 mm/min; MPa = N / total bonded area. Three-way ANOVA + Tukey, alpha 5%; normality (Shapiro-Wilk p=0.09) and homogeneity (Levene p=0.432) satisfied.

Marginal means computed here from the cell means (equal n; not reported by the authors): bevel 11.14 vs no bevel 11.89 MPa; ER 12.09 vs SE 10.94 MPa; bulk-fill ~12.68 vs conventional ~10.35 MPa. Failure modes were not analyzed. The authors describe the ER-SE gap as relatively small in absolute terms, particularly with conventional composite.

**Limitations:** 24-h storage, no aging; bovine tissue; enamel-only margins (real NCCLs often have dentin/cementum cervical margins); bevel area added to the bonded-area denominator; SE mode without selective enamel etching; one adhesive and one composite per category; bulk-fill translucency may limit esthetic use in the cervical region.
