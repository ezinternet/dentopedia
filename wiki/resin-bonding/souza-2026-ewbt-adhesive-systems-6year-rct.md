## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (Clin Oral Investig 2026, Brazil): 148 NCCLs in 67 adults randomized to water-wet 3-step E&R control (NE) or ethanol-wet bonding (EWBT, 100% ethanol 60 s) with 3-step E&R (EMP), hydrophobic bond only (EB), or universal adhesive (EU); followed 6 years (72–83 months).

6-year retention: NE 88.9%, EU 81.1%, EMP 77.8%, EB 48.7% (log-rank p=0.008); EB inferior to every other group (p=0.030/0.005/0.015) from 6 months onward, while the three hydrophilic protocols did not differ.

EWBT gives no longevity benefit with hydrophilic adhesives and makes an undiluted hydrophobic bonding resin clinically unacceptable — EWBT is not recommended for routine NCCL bonding; caveats are no cluster adjustment, LOCF imputation, and 72.9% recall.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 RCT(브라질): 성인 67명의 비우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 148개를 수분 습윤 3단계 산부식-수세 (Etch-and-Rinse, E&R) 대조군(NE) 또는 에탄올 습윤 접착술 (Ethanol-Wet Bonding Technique, EWBT) + 3단계 E&R(EMP)·소수성 본드 단독(EB)·유니버설 접착제(EU)로 배정, 6년(72–83개월) 추적.

6년 유지율: NE 88.9%, EU 81.1%, EMP 77.8%, EB 48.7%(로그순위 p=0.008); EB만 6개월부터 나머지 모든 군보다 유의하게 열등, 친수성 3군 간 차이 없음.

EWBT는 친수성 접착제에서 수명 개선 효과가 없고 순수 소수성 본딩 레진과는 임상적으로 부적합 — 일상적 NCCL 접착에 권고되지 않음; 한계는 환자 내 군집효과 미보정·마지막 관측값 이월 (LOCF) 대치·회수율 72.9%.

## Summary

The longest clinical follow-up of the ethanol-wet bonding technique to date. EWBT was proposed to replace residual water in the etched collagen network with ethanol so that hydrophobic monomers can infiltrate and form a less hydrolysis-prone hybrid layer. At 6 years, pairing EWBT with the undiluted hydrophobic Bond bottle of Scotchbond Multi-Purpose (EB) — the purest clinical form of the concept with commercial materials — lost over half of its restorations, with the failure curve separating from 6 months. When EWBT preceded hydrophilic adhesives (a 3-step E&R with primer, or a 10-MDP universal adhesive), retention was statistically indistinguishable from the conventional water-wet control, so the extra 60 s step bought nothing. The authors conclude that adhesive hydrophilicity, not ethanol pretreatment, drives long-term clinical behaviour.

## Key Contributions

- 6-year data where prior clinical EWBT trials stopped at 6–24 months.
- Separates EWBT effect by adhesive hydrophilicity: neutral with hydrophilic systems, harmful with a purely hydrophobic resin.
- Overturns the 12-month "equal performance" conclusion of [[resin-bonding/mortazavi-2012-ethanol-wet-bonding-clinical-trial]], which tested the same EWBT + hydrophobic-bond-only concept.

## Methodology

- Randomized at restoration level with a "no repetition" rule (each lesion in a patient got a different protocol); patients and two calibrated evaluators blinded, operator not.
- Groups: NE 36, EU 37, EMP 36, EB 39 restorations. Canines/premolars, NCCL ≥1 mm deep; no bevel; 37% H3PO4 15 s; EWBT = 100% ethanol actively 60 s, excess air-dried.
- Adhesives: Scotchbond Multi-Purpose (primer + bond, or bond only in EB); universal adhesive (Single/Scotchbond Universal, 10-MDP). Composite Filtek Z350 XT.
- Modified USPHS at 1 week, 6 mo, 18 mo, 6 y. ITT with LOCF; Kaplan-Meier + log-rank; Fisher's exact per time point.

## Results

| Group | 6 mo | 18 mo | 6 y retention | 6 y no post-op sensitivity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NE — water-wet + 3-step E&R | 100% | 100% | **88.9%** | 83.3% |
| EU — EWBT + universal | 97.3% | 94.6% | **81.1%** | 75.7% |
| EMP — EWBT + 3-step E&R | 97.2% | 94.4% | **77.8%** | 77.8% |
| EB — EWBT + hydrophobic bond only | 76.3% | 60.5% | **48.7%** | 46.2% |

- Overall log-rank p=0.008; EB vs NE p=0.030, vs EU p=0.005, vs EMP p=0.015; NE/EU/EMP not different.
- Overall 6-year survival 73.6% (109/148); recall 72.9% (42 patients, 108 restorations).
- EB also worse in marginal discoloration and adaptation at 6 and 18 months; at 6 years all groups had degraded and these differences were no longer significant (discoloration p=0.179; adaptation p=0.111).
- No secondary caries or anatomic-form change in any surviving restoration.
- Reporting inconsistency: Table 4 lists EB as n=38 (47.4%) while text and abstract use n=39 (48.7%).

## Clinical Implications

- **Do not add EWBT routinely.** With hydrophilic adhesives (3-step E&R with primer, universal adhesives), it adds a 60 s technique-sensitive step with no retention benefit at 6 years.
- **Never pair EWBT with a hydrophobic bond resin alone** (skipping the primer) — ~half of restorations failed by 6 years, with failures visible by 6 months.
- 10-MDP universal adhesives already provide chemical stability; the authors call EWBT redundant there.
- In-vitro EWBT durability gains ([[resin-bonding/talungchit-2014-ethanol-wet-bonding-chlorhexidine-resin-dentin-durability]], [[resin-bonding/comba-2024-effects-etching-time-ethanol-wet]]) did not translate clinically — another example of the in-vitro/clinical gap in bond durability research.

## Limitations

- Restoration-level analysis without cluster adjustment; LOCF may underestimate failure; 72.9% recall; operator unblinded; single centre.
- Commercial undiluted hydrophobic bond only — ethanol-solvated experimental hydrophobic primers (the original EWBT concept) were not tested.
