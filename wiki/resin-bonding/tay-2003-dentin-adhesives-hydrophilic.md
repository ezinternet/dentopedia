## Three-line Summary

Foundational narrative review (Tay & Pashley, J Can Dent Assoc 2003) tracing how each simplification step in adhesive evolution — from 3-step total-etch to 1-step self-etch — increased hydrophilicity and water permeability, covering water-blister formation, postoperative sensitivity, hybrid-layer degradation, and dual-cure incompatibility.

Progressive simplification increases hydrophilicity; 1-step self-etch adhesives are the most hydrophilic and least durable; water blisters form when dentin fluid permeates polymerized adhesive films, creating a pumping effect that accelerates hydrolytic hybrid-layer degradation; simplified acidic adhesives also inhibit dual-cure composite polymerization.

3-step total-etch adhesives (separate etch + primer + adhesive) remain the most durable; the clinical implication is to avoid simplified adhesives in mechanically demanding or long-span indirect restorations, or to interpose a hydrophobic layer over 1-step adhesives when dual-cure composites are required.

## 세줄요약

기초 서술 리뷰 (Tay & Pashley, J Can Dent Assoc 2003): 3단계 전부식 → 1단계 자가산부식으로 이어지는 접착제 단순화 각 단계가 친수성과 수분 투과성을 높이는 과정을 추적하며, 수포 형성, 술후 과민증, 혼성층 열화, 이중경화 부적합을 다룸.

단순화할수록 친수성 증가; 1단계 자가산부식이 가장 친수적이고 내구성 최저; 중합된 접착제막을 통해 상아질 수분이 투과해 수포(water blister)를 형성하고 혼성층 가수분해 열화를 촉진; 간편화된 산성 접착제는 이중경화 복합레진 중합 저해.

3단계 전부식 접착제(별도 산부식+프라이머+접착제)가 내구성 최고; 기계적 부하가 큰 간접 수복물에서는 단순화 접착제 회피, 이중경화 복합레진 사용 시 1단계 접착제 위에 소수성 오버레이어 적용이 임상적 함의.

## Summary
Foundational narrative review by Tay & Pashley establishing the "too hydrophilic" concept for simplified dentin adhesives. As manufacturers simplified from 3-step TE → 2-step single bottle → 1-step SE, each simplification increased hydrophilicity and water permeability, leading to bond degradation and dual-cure incompatibility.

## Hydrophilicity Spectrum

| System | Steps | Hydrophilicity | Durability |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-step TE (e.g. OptiBond FL) | Etch + Primer + Adhesive | Lowest | Best |
| 2-step TE (1-bottle) | Etch + Primer/Adhesive | ↑ | ↓ |
| 2-step SE (e.g. Clearfil SE Bond) | SE Primer + Adhesive | ↑↑ | ↓↓ |
| 1-step SE | All-in-one | Highest | Worst |

## Core Problem: Water Blister Formation
- Dentin continuously weeps fluid (hydraulic conductance)
- Hydrophilic adhesive → water permeates through polymerized film
- Water blisters trapped at interface → pumping effect → postoperative sensitivity
- Blisters accelerate hydrolytic degradation of hybrid layer

## Dual-Cure Incompatibility
- Simplified acidic adhesives inhibit tertiary amine activators in dual-cure composites
- Result: inadequate polymerization at interface when dual-cure or chemical-cure composite is used over 1-step SE adhesive
- Solution: interpose a separate hydrophobic adhesive layer before dual-cure composite
