## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (6 databases, 1,538 records → 14 in vitro studies, 11/14 low risk of bias) evaluating the effect of collagen crosslinkers — EDTA, phytic acid (IP6), proanthocyanidin, rosmarinic acid, sodium ascorbate — on resin-dentin bond strength after NaOCl irrigation.

Collagen crosslinkers overall improved bond strength on NaOCl-treated dentin; the magnitude of benefit was modulated by NaOCl concentration and exposure duration, with higher/longer NaOCl exposure making crosslinker reconditioning most relevant.

At the endodontic-restorative interface, applying a collagen crosslinker after NaOCl irrigation can partially rescue NaOCl-oxidized dentin collagen and restore resin-dentin bond strength — but all evidence is in vitro.

## 세줄요약

6개 데이터베이스(1,538건 → 14편 포함, 11/14 비뚤림위험 낮음)를 검색해 NaOCl 처리 상아질에서 콜라겐 가교제(EDTA·phytic acid/IP6·proanthocyanidin·rosmarinic acid·sodium ascorbate)의 레진-상아질 결합강도 효과를 평가한 SR+MA.

콜라겐 가교제는 NaOCl 처리 상아질의 결합강도를 전반적으로 개선하였으며, 효과 크기는 NaOCl 농도 및 노출 시간에 의해 조절되어 고농도·장시간 세정 후 재조절이 가장 유효.

근관치료-수복 계면에서 NaOCl 세정 후 콜라겐 가교제를 적용하면 산화 손상된 상아질 콜라겐을 부분 회복하고 결합강도를 복원할 수 있으나, 현재 근거는 모두 in vitro 수준이다.

## Summary
Sodium hypochlorite, a standard root-canal irrigant, oxidizes dentin collagen and degrades the resin-dentin bond — a problem at the endodontic-restorative interface. This meta-analysis of 14 in vitro studies (most at low risk of bias) found that applying collagen crosslinkers (EDTA, phytic acid/IP6, proanthocyanidin, rosmarinic acid, sodium ascorbate) after NaOCl exposure generally restored bond strength. The benefit was not uniform: it depended on the NaOCl concentration and exposure time, so crosslinker reconditioning is most relevant after higher-concentration or longer irrigation.

## Key Contributions
- Quantifies crosslinker-mediated recovery of NaOCl-compromised resin-dentin bonds.
- Subgroup signals by crosslinker type and NaOCl concentration/duration.

## Methodology
Six databases; in vitro studies of crosslinker effect on NaOCl-treated dentin bond strength; 1,538 → 14 included; 11/14 low RoB; bond-strength meta-analysis with subgroup comparisons.

## Results
Overall advantage of crosslinkers for bond strength on NaOCl-treated dentin, influenced by NaOCl concentration and exposure duration.
