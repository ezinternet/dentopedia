## Three-line Summary

Double-blind split-mouth multicenter RCT (n=50, 200 NCCLs, 4 strategies: SEE/SET/ERDry/ERWet) evaluating Futurabond U universal adhesive over 7.5 years — the longest follow-up to date for a universal adhesive in NCCLs, with 135 restorations analyzed at final recall.

Retention rates at 7.5 years: SEE 72.7%, SET 76.5%, ERDry 76.5%, ERWet 79.4% — no statistically significant difference between strategies (p>0.05); secondary outcomes (marginal staining, marginal adaptation) also showed no group differences.

The simplest bonding technique (self-etch only, SEE) performs equivalently to more complex protocols (etch-and-rinse) at 7.5 years, supporting protocol simplification in NCCL restorations without compromising longevity.

## Summary

This double-blind split-mouth two-center RCT enrolled 50 participants with ≥4 NCCLs, each receiving all four bonding strategies (SEE, SET, ERDry, ERWet) with Futurabond U (Voco) universal adhesive. Restorations were evaluated at baseline, 6 months, 1, 3, 5, and 7.5 years using FDI and USPHS criteria. At 7.5 years, 135 of 200 restorations were analyzed (84% recall). Retention rates were 72.7% (SEE), 76.5% (SET and ERDry), and 79.4% (ERWet), with no statistically significant differences (p>0.05). Secondary outcomes (marginal staining, marginal adaptation defects) also showed no significant group differences. One secondary caries occurred in the ERWet group.

## Key Contributions

- Longest follow-up (7.5 years) for a universal adhesive in NCCLs using a rigorous double-blind split-mouth multicenter RCT design
- No significant difference in retention between SEE, SET, ERDry, and ERWet strategies at 7.5 years (72.7–79.4%)
- Self-etch only (SEE) — the simplest technique — was not inferior to etch-and-rinse strategies
- Futurabond U (Voco) demonstrated satisfactory 7.5-year clinical performance regardless of bonding strategy

## Methodology

- **Design**: Double-blind split-mouth two-center RCT
- **Participants**: 50 adults with ≥4 NCCLs; split-mouth (all 4 strategies per participant)
- **Total restorations**: 200 (n=50 per group); 135 analyzed at 7.5yr
- **Adhesive**: Futurabond U (Voco) — single-dose, dual-cure universal adhesive
- **Strategies**: SEE (self-etch only), SET (selective enamel etch + self-etch), ERDry (etch-and-rinse, dry dentin), ERWet (etch-and-rinse, wet dentin)
- **Evaluation**: FDI criteria and USPHS criteria at baseline, 6m, 1yr, 3yr, 5yr, 7.5yr
- **Recall rate**: 84% at 7.5yr

## Results

| Strategy | Retention rate (7.5yr) | Restorations lost |
|---|---|---|
| SEE (self-etch only) | 72.7% | 9 |
| SET (selective etch + SE) | 76.5% | 8 |
| ERDry (E&R, dry dentin) | 76.5% | 8 |
| ERWet (E&R, wet dentin) | 79.4% | 7 |

- Overall: 32/200 restorations lost retention at 7.5 years
- No significant differences between groups for retention (p>0.05)
- 16 restorations with minor marginal staining (no group difference)
- 28 restorations with minimal marginal adaptation defects (no group difference)
- 1 secondary caries (ERWet group)

## Clinical Implications

- The simplest protocol (SEE — self-etch only, no etch step) achieves comparable longevity to etch-and-rinse techniques in NCCLs at 7.5 years
- Clinicians can choose bonding technique based on clinical preference and case factors (dentin moisture, enamel margin proportion) without concern for differential long-term retention
- Universal adhesives appear well-suited for NCCL restorations, with satisfactory performance across all bonding modes
