## Three-line Summary

1-year RCT (Dent Res J 2012; 36 NCCLs in 12 patients, Isfahan, Iran) comparing three adhesive strategies for cervical restorations: OptiBond FL (3-step etch-and-rinse), Clearfil SE Bond (2-step self-etch), and ethanol wet bonding (EWB) using the OptiBond FL adhesive component.

All groups achieved 100% retention at 6 months; at 12 months OptiBond FL and Clearfil SE Bond maintained 100% while EWB fell to 91.67%; no statistically significant difference between groups (p=0.358).

EWB showed a numerically inferior but non-significant result at 12 months, likely because the sample (36 lesions) was underpowered and the follow-up too short for the incremental durability advantage of EWB to manifest; EWB remains technique-sensitive.

## 세줄요약

1년 RCT(Dent Res J 2012; NCCL 36개, 환자 12명, 이란 이스파한) — 치경부 수복에서 3가지 접착 전략 비교: OptiBond FL(3단계 산부식), Clearfil SE Bond(2단계 자가산부식), 에탄올 습윤접착법(EWB).

6개월 전 군 유지율 100%; 12개월 OptiBond FL·Clearfil SE Bond 100% 유지, EWB 91.67%로 저하; 군간 유의차 없음(p=0.358).

EWB는 12개월에서 수치상 열세였으나 통계적으로 무의미 — 표본(36개)의 검정력 부족 및 EWB의 장기 내구성 이점이 발현되기에 너무 짧은 추적 기간이 원인으로, 기술 민감성이 높은 EWB 임상 적용에는 주의가 필요하다.

## Summary
1-year RCT (36 NCCLs, 12 patients, Isfahan, Iran) comparing three adhesive strategies in cervical restorations: OptiBond FL (3-step E&R), Clearfil SE Bond (2-step SE + enamel etch), and ethanol wet bonding (EWB) with OptiBond FL adhesive component. No significant difference between groups at 12 months.

## Retention Results

| Group | 6-month | 9-month | 12-month |
|---|---|---|---|
| OptiBond FL (E&R) | 100% | 100% | **100%** |
| Clearfil SE Bond | 100% | 100% | **100%** |
| Ethanol wet bonding | 100% | 100% | **91.67%** |

- p=0.358 at 12 months — no significant difference
- No marginal defects, discoloration, or caries in any group
- All teeth remained vital

## Ethanol Wet Bonding Concept
- Dentin progressively dehydrated with ascending ethanol concentrations
- Collagen network maintained in expanded (non-collapsed) state without water
- Adhesive penetrates hydrophobic dentin → theoretically more durable hybrid layer
- Clinical evidence here: **numerically lower** retention at 12mo vs E&R/SE Bond (NS)

## Limitation Context
- 36 NCCLs is small; NS finding may be underpowered
- 12 months too short for EWB advantage to manifest (degradation is gradual)
- EWB is technique-sensitive; operator variability
