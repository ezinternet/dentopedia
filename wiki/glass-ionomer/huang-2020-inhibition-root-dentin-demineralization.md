## Three-line Summary

RMGI cement (Rely X Luting Plus) produced the largest net demineralization inhibition zone at 7700 ± 2500 µm; all three bioactive cements showed net inhibition — Ceramir at 3800 ± 1900 µm, TheraCem at 2100 ± 2600 µm, Activa at 1400 ± 700 µm — whereas self-adhesive resin cement (Rely X Unicem 2) showed net demineralization at −2000 ± 1700 µm. This in vitro pH-cycling model at CEJ restorations (n=10/group, 30 days, polarized light microscopy) demonstrates ion-releasing luting cements provide meaningful secondary caries resistance. Clinically, RMGI or bioactive cements may be preferable over conventional resin cement for high-caries-risk patients receiving crown restorations.

## 세줄요약

레진 강화형 글래스아이오노머 시멘트 (Resin-Modified Glass Ionomer, RMGI) (Rely X Luting Plus)가 7700 ± 2500 µm의 가장 큰 탈회 억제 영역을 보였고, 바이오액티브 시멘트 (Bioactive Cement) 3종(Ceramir 3800 µm, TheraCem 2100 µm, Activa 1400 µm)은 모두 순탈회 억제를 나타냈으며, 자기접착 레진 시멘트 (Self-Adhesive Resin Cement) (Rely X Unicem 2)는 −2000 µm로 오히려 탈회가 진행됐다. 백악질-에나멜 경계부 (Cementum-Enamel Junction, CEJ)에 수복 후 pH 사이클링 30일·편광 광학현미경 (Polarized Light Microscopy) 정량 평가(n=10/그룹)로, 이온 방출 시멘트가 치근 상아질 (Root Dentin) 탈회를 억제하되 RMGI 대비 최대 49%(Ceramir), 최소 18%(Activa) 수준임을 확인했다. 이차우식 (Secondary Caries) 고위험 환자의 크라운 합착 시 RMGI 또는 바이오액티브 시멘트가 우선 고려된다.

## Summary

Fifty extracted molars were prepared at the CEJ and restored with five luting cement types. After 30-day pH cycling (alternating demineralization pH 4 / remineralization solution), 100 µm sections were evaluated with polarized light microscopy to measure net lesion depth. RMGI cement (LUT) established the reference for remineralization-dominant inhibition; bioactive cements (CER, THE, ACT) all achieved positive net inhibition albeit lower; conventional self-adhesive resin cement (UNI) yielded net demineralization. The study provides the first direct comparison of bioactive luting cements to RMGI for root dentin demineralization inhibition at crown margins.

## Key Contributions

- Quantifies demineralization inhibition of three commercial bioactive luting cements against RMGI and resin cement benchmarks in a single controlled study
- Establishes that bioactive cements provide real but reduced demineralization protection vs RMGI (18–49% of RMGI inhibition)
- Demonstrates that self-adhesive resin cement (Rely X Unicem 2) actively promotes root dentin demineralization under pH-cycling conditions
- Supports selecting RMGI or bioactive luting cement over conventional resin cement in high-caries-risk patients

## Methodology

- **Design**: In vitro pH-cycling comparative study
- **Specimens**: n = 50 extracted molars, CEJ preparation, n = 10 per group
- **Groups**:
  | Code | Product | Type |
  |---|---|---|
  | LUT | Rely X Luting Plus | RMGI |
  | CER | Ceramir Crown and Bridge | Bioactive (calcium aluminate silicate) |
  | THE | TheraCem | Bioactive (calcium silicate/MDP) |
  | ACT | Activa Bioactive Cement | Bioactive (calcium silicate/ionic resin) |
  | UNI | Rely X Unicem 2 | Self-adhesive resin cement |
- **Cycling**: 30 days × [demineralization solution (pH 4) + remineralization solution]
- **Sections**: 100 µm transverse sections
- **Measurement**: Polarized light microscopy — net lesion depth (µm); positive = inhibition zone, negative = demineralization zone

## Results

| Cement | Type | Net lesion depth (µm, mean ± SD) | % of RMGI |
|---|---|---|---|
| LUT (Rely X Luting Plus) | RMGI | 7700 ± 2500 | 100% (reference) |
| CER (Ceramir Crown and Bridge) | Bioactive | 3800 ± 1900 | 49% |
| THE (TheraCem) | Bioactive | 2100 ± 2600 | 27% |
| ACT (Activa Bioactive Cement) | Bioactive | 1400 ± 700 | 18% |
| UNI (Rely X Unicem 2) | Self-adhesive resin | −2000 ± 1700 | — (net demineralization) |

**Statistical ranking**: LUT > CER, THE, ACT > UNI

**Key observation**: Large SD for THE (±2600 µm) overlaps both ACT and LUT ranges, indicating high inter-specimen variability for TheraCem.
