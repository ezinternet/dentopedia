## Three-line Summary

This network meta-analysis evaluated the effects of 13 different mouthwashes on preventing oral mucositis in patients undergoing radiotherapy and chemotherapy, including 36 RCTs (3457 patients). According to SUCRA rankings, aloe vera juice was most effective for OM prevention, followed by Chinese herbal medicine, Bing Peng San, honey, Lactobacillus brevis, chamomile, riboflavin, Kangfuxin Ye, benzydamine, turmeric, sucralfate, povidone-iodine, and chlorhexidine.

Aloe vera juice, Chinese herbal medicine, and honey are significantly more effective than povidone-iodine, chlorhexidine, and placebo for preventing oral mucositis.

## 세줄요약

본 네트워크 메타분석은 방사선 및 항암화학요법을 받는 환자의 구강점막염(OM) 예방에 대한 13가지 구강세정제의 효과를 36개의 무작위대조시험(RCT, 3457명 환자)을 통해 평가하였다. SUCRA 순위 결과, 알로에 베라 주스가 OM 예방에 가장 효과적이었으며, 그 다음으로 한약재, 빙붕산, 꿀, 락토바실러스 브레비스, 카모마일, 리보플라빈, 강복신액, 벤지다민, 심황, 수크랄페이트, 포비돈-아이오딘, 클로르헥시딘 순이었다.

알로에 베라 주스, 한약재, 꿀은 포비돈-아이오딘, 클로르헥시딘, 위약보다 구강점막염 예방에 유의하게 더 효과적이었다.

## Summary

This network meta-analysis evaluated the preventive effects of 13 different mouthwashes on oral mucositis (OM) in patients undergoing radiotherapy and chemotherapy. Thirty-six randomized controlled trials (3457 patients) were included. According to SUCRA probability ranking, aloe vera juice was the most effective intervention, followed by Chinese herbal medicine, Bing Peng San, honey, Lactobacillus brevis, chamomile, riboflavin, Kangfuxin Ye, benzydamine, turmeric, sucralfate, povidone-iodine, and chlorhexidine. Aloe vera juice, Chinese herbal medicine, and honey were significantly more effective than povidone-iodine, chlorhexidine, and placebo (p < 0.05 for all). The authors concluded that aloe vera juice may be the best mouthwash for preventing OM, although further high-quality RCTs are needed to confirm these findings.

## Key Contributions

- Aloe vera juice ranked #1 for OM prevention among 13 mouthwashes
- Chinese herbal medicine ranked #2; Bing Peng San ranked #3
- Honey ranked #4; effective and widely available
- Aloe vera, Chinese herbs, and honey significantly superior to PVP-I, CHX, and placebo
- Chlorhexidine ranked lowest for OM prevention (SUCRA #13)
- 36 RCTs from both English and Chinese literature included

## Methodology

This network meta-analysis followed PRISMA guidelines and was registered with INPLASY (202250061). A comprehensive search was conducted in PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, and CNKI up to April 2023. Eligible studies were RCTs evaluating mouthwashes for OM prevention in patients undergoing chemoradiotherapy. Thirty-six RCTs (3457 patients) evaluating 13 mouthwashes were included. Risk of bias was assessed using Cochrane RoB tool. Direct meta-analysis used RevMan 5.3 (random-effects model). Network meta-analysis used Stata 16.2 with consistency model (p = 0.945 for inconsistency). SUCRA probability ranking was used to rank interventions. Heterogeneity was assessed with I² and closed-loop tests.
