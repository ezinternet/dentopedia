## Three-line Summary

PubMed narrative literature review (2014) comparing topical corticosteroids, antimicrobials, NSAIDs, and low-level laser therapy (LLLT) for recurrent aphthous stomatitis (RAS) treatment.

Topical corticosteroids (triamcinolone, clobetasol) have the strongest evidence for pain reduction and healing-time shortening and remain the gold standard; LLLT is a safe and effective adjunct that reduces pain and ulcer size; chlorhexidine mouthwash gives modest reduction in ulcer frequency.

Topical steroids are first-line for symptom management; LLLT is particularly useful for patients with frequent recurrences; no treatment currently achieves complete long-term prevention or addresses the underlying etiology of RAS.

## 세줄요약

국소 스테로이드·항균제·NSAIDs·저출력 레이저 치료(LLLT)를 비교한 재발성 아프타성 구내염(RAS) 치료에 관한 PubMed 내러티브 문헌 고찰(2014).

국소 스테로이드(트리암시놀론, 클로베타솔)가 통증 감소·치유 시간 단축에 가장 강한 근거로 표준 치료; LLLT는 통증·궤양 크기 감소에 안전하고 효과적인 보조 치료; 클로로헥시딘 가글은 궤양 빈도 소폭 감소.

증상 관리에 국소 스테로이드가 1차; LLLT는 재발이 잦은 환자에 유용; 현재 어떤 치료도 장기적 완전 예방이나 RAS의 근본 원인 해결에는 이르지 못함.

## Summary
Literature review comparing topical corticosteroids, antimicrobials, NSAIDs, and low-level laser therapy for RAS.

## Key Contributions
- Topical steroids (triamcinolone, clobetasol) strongest evidence for pain and healing time reduction
- Low-level laser therapy (LLLT): safe and effective adjunct reducing pain and ulcer size
- Chlorhexidine mouthwash: modest reduction in ulcer frequency

## Methodology
PubMed narrative review.

## Results
Topical corticosteroids remain gold standard. LLLT effective and safe alternative especially for frequent recurrences.
