## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of recurrent aphthous stomatitis (RAS) covering epidemiology (~20% adult prevalence), multifactorial pathogenesis (immunological, genetic, nutritional, stress), trigger factors, and a stepwise treatment algorithm.

Key triggers include local trauma, emotional stress, iron/B12/folate deficiency, and gluten sensitivity; nutritional correction resolves a subset of cases; treatment steps from topical corticosteroids and analgesic mouthwashes up to systemic immunosuppressants.

RAS is self-limiting but frequently recurrent with significant quality-of-life impact — distinguishing nutritional/systemic causes is clinically important as it identifies a correctable subset.

## 세줄요약

서술 리뷰 — 재발성 아프타성 구내염(Recurrent Aphthous Stomatitis, RAS)의 역학(성인 ~20% 유병률)·다인성 병인(면역·유전·영양·스트레스)·유발인자·단계적 치료 알고리즘을 개관.

주요 유발인자: 국소 외상, 정서적 스트레스, 철분·비타민B12·엽산 결핍, 글루텐 감수성; 영양 교정으로 일부 완치 가능; 치료는 국소 스테로이드·진통 함수제에서 전신 면역억제제까지 단계적.

RAS는 자연회복되나 재발이 잦고 삶의 질 영향이 크므로, 영양/전신 원인 감별이 교정 가능한 아형을 찾는 임상적으로 중요한 과정.

## Summary
Review covering RAS epidemiology (~20% adults), pathogenesis, trigger factors, and stepwise treatment algorithm.

## Key Contributions
- ~20% adult prevalence; family history common; onset in childhood/adolescence
- Triggers: trauma, stress, iron/B12/folate deficiency, gluten sensitivity
- Step-up treatment: topical → systemic for frequent/major ulcers

## Methodology
Narrative review.

## Results
RAS is self-limiting but recurrent; quality of life impact significant. Nutritional correction (iron, B12, folate) resolves subset.
