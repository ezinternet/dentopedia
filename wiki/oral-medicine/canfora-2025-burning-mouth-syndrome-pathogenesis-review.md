## Three-line Summary

PubMed narrative review (2025) of burning mouth syndrome (BMS) pathogenesis integrating peripheral small-fiber neuropathy, central sensitization (altered trigeminal pain processing), hormonal (estrogen decline), and psychological factors into a multifactorial model.

Small-fiber neuropathy is the predominant peripheral mechanism; central sensitization involves altered pain processing in trigeminal pathways; estrogen decline (postmenopausal) and psychological comorbidities amplify the symptom complex; the review proposes an ICD-11-based diagnostic algorithm.

Primary BMS (no identifiable cause) is a diagnosis of exclusion requiring systematic rule-out of secondary causes (local, systemic, medication-induced); secondary BMS should be treated by addressing the underlying cause first; no treatment achieves complete long-term remission for primary BMS.

## 세줄요약

말초 소섬유 신경병증·중추 민감화(삼차신경 통증처리 변화)·호르몬(에스트로겐 감소)·심리적 요인을 통합한 구강작열감증후군(BMS) 병인론 최신 PubMed 내러티브 리뷰(2025)로 ICD-11 기반 진단 알고리즘 제안.

소섬유 신경병증이 주요 말초 기전; 중추 민감화는 삼차신경로의 변형된 통증처리 포함; 폐경 후 에스트로겐 감소와 심리적 동반질환이 증상을 증폭; ICD-11 기반 진단 알고리즘 제시.

1차성 BMS(원인 없음)는 이차성 원인(국소·전신·약물) 체계적 배제 후 진단; 이차성은 기저 원인 치료 우선; 1차성 BMS의 완전한 장기 관해를 달성하는 치료는 현재 없음.

## Summary
Updated review of BMS pathogenesis integrating peripheral/central neuropathy, hormonal (estrogen decline), and psychological factors; ICD-11 diagnostic algorithm proposed.

## Key Contributions
- Small fiber neuropathy as peripheral mechanism of BMS
- Central sensitization: altered pain processing in trigeminal pathways
- Diagnostic algorithm: primary (no cause) vs secondary (treatable cause) BMS

## Methodology
PubMed narrative review with diagnostic algorithm proposal.

## Results
BMS etiology multifactorial: neuropathic + hormonal + psychological. Primary BMS: diagnosis of exclusion. Secondary: treat underlying cause first.
