## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of BMS management strategies synthesizing RCT and clinical evidence for pharmacological, psychological, and multimodal approaches.

Topical clonazepam rinse-and-spit has the strongest RCT evidence for pain reduction; CBT reduces psychological distress; low-dose TCAs (amitriptyline) and gabapentinoids address the neuropathic component.

No single cure exists for BMS; a combination multimodal approach is most effective, and treatment should be tailored to the individual patient's dominant subtype.

## 세줄요약

BMS 관리 전략 내러티브 리뷰 — 약물·심리·다학제 병합 접근에 대한 RCT 및 임상 근거 종합.

국소 클로나제팜(rinse-and-spit)이 통증 감소에 가장 강한 RCT 근거; CBT는 심리적 고통 감소; 저용량 TCA(아미트립틸린)·가바펜티노이드가 신경병성 요소를 다룸.

BMS의 단일 치료법은 없으며 다학제 병합이 가장 효과적; 환자의 주요 아형에 맞춘 개별화 치료가 필요.

## Summary
Comprehensive management review for BMS covering pharmacological (topical clonazepam, TCAs, gabapentinoids), psychological (CBT), and multimodal approaches.

## Key Contributions
- Topical clonazepam rinse-and-spit: strongest RCT evidence for pain reduction
- CBT reduces psychological distress and catastrophizing in BMS
- Low-dose TCA (amitriptyline) and gabapentinoids for neuropathic component

## Methodology
PubMed systematic review base with narrative synthesis.

## Results
No single cure for BMS. Combination approach most effective. Topical clonazepam highest evidence (multiple RCTs).
