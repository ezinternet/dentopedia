## Three-line Summary
Cochrane systematic review of 23 RCTs (1,121 participants, 83% female) comparing any intervention vs placebo for burning mouth syndrome (BMS) symptom relief and quality of life (QoL).
Very low-quality evidence (GRADE ⊕⊝⊝⊝) of short-term benefit from electromagnetic radiation (1 RCT, 58 pts), topical benzodiazepines/clonazepam (2 RCTs, 111 pts), physical barriers (1 RCT, 50 pts), and anticonvulsants/gabapentin (1 RCT, 100 pts); long-term benefit from psychological therapy (1 RCT, 30 pts), capsaicin oral rinse (1 RCT, 18 pts), and topical clonazepam (1 RCT, 66 pts).
Overall insufficient evidence to support or refute any intervention for BMS; all outcomes graded very low quality due to high risk of bias in 18/23 trials and small sample sizes.

## 세줄요약
코크란 체계적 문헌고찰(SR), 23개 RCT(1,121명, 여성 83%)를 대상으로 구강작열감증후군(BMS) 치료 중재 vs 위약 비교 — 증상 완화 및 삶의 질(QoL) 평가.
전자기파(1 RCT, 58명), 국소 벤조디아제핀/클로나제팜(2 RCT, 111명), 물리적 차단(1 RCT, 50명), 항경련제/가바펜틴(1 RCT, 100명)에서 단기 증상 완화 '매우 낮은 질' 근거 확인; 심리치료(1 RCT, 30명), 캡사이신 구강세척(1 RCT, 18명), 국소 클로나제팜(1 RCT, 66명)에서 장기 증상 완화 매우 낮은 질 근거.
전체적으로 **모든 중재에 대해 매우 낮은 질 근거**(GRADE ⊕⊝⊝⊝)만 존재, 23개 중 18개 연구가 높은 비뚤림 위험 — 현재 어떤 BMS 치료도 지지하거나 반박할 충분한 근거 **없음**.

## Summary
This Cochrane Intervention Review (Cochrane Oral Health Group) updated previous versions (2000, 2005) to evaluate the effectiveness and safety of any intervention versus placebo for managing burning mouth syndrome (BMS). The review included 23 randomized controlled trials (1,121 participants, predominantly female) published between 1995 and 2015. Interventions were categorized into nine groups: antidepressants/antipsychotics, anticonvulsants, benzodiazepines, cholinergics, dietary supplements, electromagnetic radiation, physical barriers, psychological therapies, and topical treatments. Only one RCT was assessed at low risk of bias overall; four had unclear risk and 18 were at high risk of bias. Consequently, the overall quality of evidence (GRADE) was rated **very low** for all interventions and all outcomes. Very low-quality evidence suggested possible short-term symptom relief with electromagnetic radiation, topical clonazepam, physical barriers (thin plastic tongue covers), and gabapentin; and possible long-term relief with psychological therapy, capsaicin oral rinse, and topical clonazepam. No evidence of difference was found for antidepressants (trazodone), dietary supplements (alpha-lipoic acid), or other topical treatments. Adverse events were poorly reported; antidepressants increased dizziness/drowsiness, and alpha-lipoic acid increased headache and gastrointestinal complaints. The authors conclude that there is currently **insufficient evidence to support or refute the use of any intervention** for BMS, and further well-designed clinical trials with standardized outcomes are urgently needed.

- 9개 중재 범주(약물·비약물·물리·심리) 총망라 평가 — 임상 진료 가이드라인 근거로 활용 가능

## Methodology
- **Design**: Cochrane systematic review (Intervention Review) — standard Cochrane methodology
- **Databases**: Cochrane Oral Health's Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE Ovid (1946–2015), Embase Ovid (1980–2015), ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP
- **Selection**: RCTs comparing any intervention vs placebo in people with BMS; no language/date restrictions
- **n**: 23 RCTs, 1,121 analysed participants (83% female)
- **Outcomes**: Primary — symptom relief (pain/burning, VAS or similar), change in QoL; Secondary — change in taste, dryness sensation, adverse events
- **Analysis**: Short-term (≤3 months) vs long-term (>3 to ≤6 months); random-effects meta-analysis where appropriate; GRADE assessment for all outcomes

| Short-term symptom relief — physical barriers (tongue covers) | Benefit (1 RCT, 50 pts) — very low quality |

| Short-term symptom relief — dietary supplements (alpha-lipoic acid) | Insufficient/contradictory — very low quality |
| Long-term symptom relief — psychological therapy | Benefit (1 RCT, 30 pts) — very low quality |

| Long-term symptom relief — dietary supplements | No difference — very low quality |
| QoL short-term — electromagnetic radiation | Improvement (1 RCT, 58 pts) — very low quality |
