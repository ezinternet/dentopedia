## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 14 RCTs evaluating various nasal irrigation solutions versus normal saline in post-FESS patients with chronic rhinosinusitis.
Primary outcomes (SNOT-22 and LKES scores) showed budesonide irrigation significantly outperformed normal saline, though overall pooled SMD for symptoms (−0.69, 95% CI −1.64 to 0.27, p=0.157) and endoscopic scores (−0.48, 95% CI −1.32 to 0.36, p=0.264) did not reach statistical significance due to high heterogeneity.
Clinical implication: budesonide nasal irrigation appears most effective for post-FESS symptom and endoscopic score improvement, but substantial heterogeneity and publication bias warrant cautious interpretation.

## 세줄요약

기능적 내시경 부비동 수술(FESS) 후 만성 비부비동염 환자 14개 RCT를 대상으로 다양한 비강 세척액(생리식염수, 부데소나이드, 고장성 식염수, 포비돈요오드 등)을 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석으로 비교.
주요 결과(SNOT-22, LKES)에서 부데소나이드 세척이 생리식염수 대비 유의하게 우수했으나, 전체 풀링된 증상(SMD −0.69, 95% CI −1.64~0.27, p=0.157) 및 내시경 점수(SMD −0.48, 95% CI −1.32~0.36, p=0.264)는 높은 이질성으로 통계적 유의성 미도달.
임상적 함의: FESS 후 비강 세척 시 부데소나이드가 증상과 내시경 소견 개선에 가장 효과적이나, 상당한 이질성과 출판 편향이 있어 신중한 해석 필요.

## Summary

This PRISMA-compliant systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42023499268) evaluated the comparative efficacy of various nasal irrigation solutions against normal saline in adult patients undergoing functional endoscopic sinus surgery (FESS) for chronic rhinosinusitis (with or without nasal polyps). Fourteen randomized controlled trials (≈1,000+ participants) were included, investigating budesonide, hypertonic saline, povidone-iodine, amphotericin B, hypochlorous acid, sodium hyaluronate, and steroid-impregnated dressings. Outcomes were patient-reported symptom scores (SNOT-22) and clinician-assessed endoscopic scores (Lund-Kennedy Endoscopic Score, LKES) at 4–12 weeks postoperatively. The random-effects meta-analysis found that while budesonide irrigation consistently demonstrated superiority over normal saline for both SNOT-22 (SMD −1.42, 95% CI −2.10 to −0.74) and LKES (SMD −1.15, 95% CI −1.78 to −0.52), the overall pooled estimates across all solutions did not reach statistical significance due to substantial heterogeneity (I² 84–89%) and suspected publication bias. Cochrane RoB 2 assessment rated most trials as "some concerns" primarily due to blinding limitations. GRADE certainty for primary outcomes was rated LOW to VERY LOW.

## Key Contributions

1. **First comprehensive SR+MA on post-FESS irrigation solutions** — synthesizes 14 RCTs comparing normal saline against 7+ agent classes (steroids, hypertonics, antimicrobials, surfactants).
2. **Dual validated outcome metrics** — combines patient-reported (SNOT-22) and clinician-assessed (LKES) endpoints, enabling symptom–endoscopy correlation.
3. **Subgroup analysis by agent class** — isolates budesonide as the only solution with consistent, statistically significant benefit over normal saline across both outcomes.
4. **Risk-of-bias transparency** — applies Cochrane RoB 2; identifies blinding of outcome assessors as the dominant concern.
5. **Heterogeneity & publication bias quantified** — I² >75% for primary outcomes; funnel plot asymmetry and Egger's test (p<0.1) signal small-study effects.

## Methodology

### Search Strategy & Eligibility
- **Databases**: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science, Scopus (inception to March 2023)
- **Design**: Parallel-arm RCTs only; adults (≥18 yr) undergoing FESS for CRSwNP or CRSsNP
- **Intervention**: Any nasal irrigation solution (steroid, hypertonic saline, antimicrobial, surfactant, etc.)
- **Comparator**: Normal saline (0.9% NaCl) irrigation
- **Outcomes**: SNOT-22 (primary), LKES (secondary); minimum follow-up 4 weeks post-op
- **Language**: English only

### Data Extraction & Synthesis
- **Model**: Random-effects (DerSimonian-Laird) with standardized mean difference (SMD)
- **Heterogeneity**: Cochran's Q, I²; I² >75% = substantial
- **Subgroups**: Pre-specified by irrigation agent (budesonide, hypertonic saline, povidone-iodine, others)
- **Sensitivity**: Leave-one-out; exclusion of high-RoB trials
- **Publication bias**: Funnel plots, Egger's regression test
- **Software**: R (meta, metafor packages); PRISMA 2020 flow diagram

## Results

### SNOT-22 (Symptom Score) — 6 Studies
- **Overall pooled SMD**: −0.69 (95% CI −1.64 to 0.27), p=0.157 — **NS**
- **I² = 89%** (substantial heterogeneity)
- **Budesonide subgroup** (3 studies): SMD −1.42 (95% CI −2.10 to −0.74), p<0.001 — **significant**
- **Hypertonic saline subgroup** (2 studies): SMD −0.31 (95% CI −1.12 to 0.50), p=0.45 — NS
- **Povidone-iodine subgroup** (2 studies): SMD −0.58 (95% CI −1.45 to 0.29), p=0.19 — NS

### LKES (Endoscopic Score) — 6 Studies
- **Overall pooled SMD**: −0.48 (95% CI −1.32 to 0.36), p=0.264 — **NS**
- **I² = 84%** (substantial heterogeneity)
- **Budesonide subgroup** (3 studies): SMD −1.15 (95% CI −1.78 to −0.52), p<0.001 — **significant**
- **Hypertonic saline subgroup** (2 studies): SMD −0.22 (95% CI −0.98 to 0.54), p=0.57 — NS
- **Other agents** (amphotericin B, HOCl, PVP-I): inconsistent, wide CIs

### Risk of Bias (Cochrane RoB 2)
- **Randomization**: 10/14 low risk, 4/14 some concerns
- **Allocation concealment**: 8/14 low, 6/14 some concerns
- **Blinding (participants/personnel)**: 12/14 some concerns (irrigation solutions hard to blind)
- **Blinding (outcome assessment)**: 9/14 some concerns (endoscopic scoring often unblinded)
- **Incomplete outcome data**: 12/14 low risk
- **Selective reporting**: 11/14 low risk

### Publication Bias
- Funnel plot asymmetry visible for both SNOT-22 and LKES
- Egger's test: p=0.08 (SNOT-22), p=0.06 (LKES) — suggestive of small-study effects
- Trim-and-fill adjustment did not materially change conclusions
