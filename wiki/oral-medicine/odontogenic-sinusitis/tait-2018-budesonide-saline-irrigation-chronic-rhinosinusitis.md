## Three-line Summary

Double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT (n=80 randomized, 61 completed) evaluating budesonide (1 mg/day) added to large-volume, low-pressure saline irrigation for 30 days in chronic rhinosinusitis patients. Mean SNOT-22 improvement was 20.7 points (budesonide) vs 13.6 points (saline alone), mean difference 7 points (95% CI −2 to 16, Cohen d=0.39). 79% achieved MCID (≥9 points) with budesonide vs 59% with saline (difference 20%, 95% CI −2.5% to 42.5%); endoscopic scores improved 3.4 vs 2.7 points.

## Summary

This RCT provides the first high-quality evidence that adding budesonide (1 mg/day via 2 × 0.5 mg capsules) to daily large-volume, low-pressure saline irrigation for 30 days yields clinically meaningful incremental benefit over saline alone in chronic rhinosinusitis (CRS). The primary outcome (SNOT-22 change) showed a 7-point mean difference (95% CI −2 to 16, Cohen d=0.39), with 79% vs 59% achieving the 9-point MCID (OR 2.6). Objective endoscopic scores (Lund-Kennedy) also favored budesonide (3.4 vs 2.7 point improvement). Importantly, benefit was pronounced in patients *without* prior sinus surgery (+10.1 points) but absent in those with prior surgery (−0.1 points), and was reduced in patients with nasal polyps. No adverse events were reported. The wide confidence intervals including the null for the primary outcome indicate imprecision; larger trials are warranted.

## Key Contributions

1. **First placebo-controlled RCT** for budesonide in saline irrigation for CRS — upgrades evidence from 3 small uncontrolled prospective cohorts (n=8-31).
2. **Dual outcome validation**: Patient-reported (SNOT-22, CGI) + objective endoscopic (Lund-Kennedy) concordance.
3. **Clinically relevant effect size**: Medium effect (d=0.39); NNT ≈ 5 for MCID achievement (79% vs 59%).
4. **Practice-changing subgroup signal**: Budesonide irrigation may benefit *non-surgical* CRS patients (contrary to prior belief that surgical opening is needed for topical delivery).
5. **Safety profile**: No related adverse events over 30 days; supports short-term tolerability.

## Methodology

### Design
- Single-center, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel RCT
- Block randomization with allocation concealment (numbered bottles 1-80)
- ITT primary analysis; mixed general linear model for repeated measures
- ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02696850

### Participants (n=74 baseline, 61 completed)
- **Inclusion**: Age ≥18, CRS ≥12 weeks (EPOS), ≥2 cardinal symptoms, endoscopic/CT inflammation
- **Exclusion**: Mucociliary disorders, recent antibiotics/surgery, steroid allergy, pregnancy, chronic systemic steroids
- **Baseline**: Mean age 51±14.7 yr, 68% female, 90% White; 25% nasal polyps (imbalanced), 28% prior surgery

### Intervention
| Arm | Regimen |
|---|---|
| Budesonide | NeilMed 8-oz bottle + saline + 2 × 0.5 mg budesonide capsules (1 mg/day), once daily × 30 days |
| Placebo | Identical saline + 2 × lactose capsules, once daily × 30 days |

### Outcomes
- **Primary**: Δ SNOT-22 (0-110) baseline → post-treatment
- **Secondary**: CGI 7-point scale; Δ Lund-Kennedy endoscopic score (0-20)
- **MCID**: ≥9-point SNOT-22 reduction
- **Analysis**: Mixed model (time × group interaction), per-protocol sensitivity, subgroup by polyps/surgery/comorbidity

## Results

### Primary Outcome — SNOT-22 Change
| Group | n | Mean Δ (SD) | Mean Diff (95% CI) | Effect Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Budesonide | 29 | 20.7 (17.9) | 7.0 (−2 to 16) | Cohen d = 0.39 |
| Saline | 32 | 13.6 (18.8) | | |

### Responder Analysis (MCID ≥9 points)
| Group | Achieved MCID | % | Difference (95% CI) | OR (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Budesonide | 23/29 | 79% | 20% (−2.5% to 42.5%) | 2.6 (0.84–8.2) |
| Saline | 19/32 | 59% | | |

### Objective Outcome — Lund-Kennedy Endoscopic Score
| Group | n | Mean Δ (SD) | Mean Diff (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budesonide | 29 | 3.4 (2.3) | 0.7 (−0.6 to 2.0) |
| Saline | 32 | 2.7 (1.9) | |

### Patient-Reported Global Improvement (CGI)
- Budesonide: 24/29 (83%) "minimally/much/very much improved"
- Saline: 20/32 (63%)
- Difference 16% (95% CI −6% to 38%)

### Subgroup Analyses (SNOT-22 mean difference)
| Subgroup | Budesonide n | Saline n | Mean Diff (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **No prior surgery** | 20 | 23 | **+10.1 (−1.1 to 21.3)** |
| Prior surgery | 9 | 9 | −0.1 (−19.6 to 19.4) |
| **No polyps** | 18 | 26 | **+10.2 (−1.6 to 22.1)** |
| Polyps present | 10 | 6 | −4.1 (−20.4 to 12.2) |
| Comorbidity (ACE-27) | — | — | Not a confounder |

### Per-protocol (excluding 1 noncompliant budesonide)
- Δ SNOT-22: 22.1 vs 13.6, diff 8.5 (95% CI −0.6 to 18.0)
- MCID: 82% vs 59%, diff 23% (95% CI 1% to 45%), OR 3.15 (0.95–10.42)

### Safety
- **Zero treatment-related adverse events** in either group
- Adrenal function not assessed (4-week duration)
