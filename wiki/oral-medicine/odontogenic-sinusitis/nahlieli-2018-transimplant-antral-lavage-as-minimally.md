## Three-line Summary

Preliminary uncontrolled series of 11 consecutive patients (mean age 68.1 years) with chronic rhinosinusitis (CRS) treated by transimplant antral lavage through an implant with an internal port (dental implant valve approach, DIVA): 3 via previously installed implants, 8 combined with implant placement.

Symptomatic improvement began from day 1; 9 of 11 patients had complete relief of most symptoms (nasal obstruction/discharge, anosmia/hyposmia) at day 30, follow-up nasal endoscopy showed no active sinus disease, and all implants were osseointegrated and in prosthetic use with no recurrence in the follow-up period.

Suggests an implant with an internal port can double as an oroantral irrigation channel, but with no control group, no stated follow-up duration and no odontogenic/non-odontogenic CRS split in the abstract, it is hypothesis-generating only.

## 세줄요약

만성 비부비동염 (Chronic Rhinosinusitis, CRS) 환자 11명(평균 연령 68.1세)을 연속 모집한 예비 비대조 증례군 — 내부 포트가 있는 임플란트 (dental implant valve approach, DIVA)를 통한 임플란트 경유 상악동 세척 (transimplant antral lavage); 기존 식립 임플란트 3명, 식립과 동시 시행 8명.

치료 첫날부터 증상 호전이 시작되었고 30일째 11명 중 9명에서 대부분의 증상(비폐색·콧물, 후각상실/저하)이 완전히 해소되었으며, 추적 비내시경에서 활동성 부비동 질환 소견이 없었고 모든 임플란트가 골유착 상태로 보철에 사용 중이며 추적 기간 재발이 없었다.

내부 포트 임플란트가 구강-상악동 세척 통로를 겸할 수 있음을 시사하나, 대조군·추적 기간·치성/비치성 CRS 구분이 초록에 없어 가설 생성 수준에 머문다.

## Summary

Nahlieli et al. (2018) report a preliminary series of 11 consecutive patients with chronic rhinosinusitis (CRS), mean age 68.1 years, treated with a transimplant lavage technique using an implant that has an internal port (dental implant valve approach, DIVA) as an oroantral port. Three patients were treated through previously installed implants and eight had the CRS treatment combined with implant placement. Once the implant was fully installed, the sinus membrane was punctured through the implant channel; after pus drainage the sinus was irrigated with normal saline and injected with a steroid solution (100 mg hydrocortisone). Symptoms improved from the first day, nine patients had complete relief of most symptoms at day 30, nasal endoscopy showed no active sinus disease, and all implants were osseointegrated and in prosthetic use. This record is abstract-only; the full text was not retrieved.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes an implant with an internal central port and integral sealing screw as a route for irrigation, observation and further treatment of the maxillary sinus in CRS.
- Describes a combined workflow in which implant placement and sinus treatment are done in the same session (8 of 11 patients).
- Reports early symptom relief and absence of recurrence with preserved osseointegration in a small series.

## Methodology

Preliminary report of 11 consecutive cases (uncontrolled; the abstract does not state prospective or retrospective design). Intervention: puncture of the sinus membrane through the implant channel, pus drainage, normal saline irrigation, then injection of 100 mg hydrocortisone. Outcomes: symptoms (nasal obstruction/discharge, anosmia/hyposmia), follow-up nasal endoscopy, implant osseointegration and prosthetic use, clinical and radiologic stability.
