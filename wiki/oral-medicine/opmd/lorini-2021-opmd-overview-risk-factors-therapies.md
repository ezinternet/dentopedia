## Three-line Summary

Narrative overview (Cancers 2021, PubMed-based) mapping the full oral potentially malignant disorder (OPMD) spectrum — leukoplakia (OLK), OLP, erythroplakia (OE), oral submucous fibrosis (OSF), actinic cheilitis — to their respective risk factors, malignant transformation rates, and available therapies.

OLK MTR ~3–17% (non-homogeneous highest; size >200 mm² additional risk); OLP MTR ~0.5–3%; OSF requires surgical intervention when mouth opening <35 mm; OLP first-line: topical corticosteroids; second-line: tacrolimus; OSF primary etiology: areca nut.

The clinical value is a single reference mapping each OPMD to its risk stratification and therapy algorithm, supporting evidence-based surveillance intensity and treatment selection across lesion types.

## 세줄요약

구강잠재악성질환(OPMD) 전 스펙트럼(백반증·OLP·홍반증·구강점막하섬유증(OSF)·광선구순염)의 위험인자·악성전환율·치료를 매핑한 내러티브 개요 (Cancers 2021, PubMed 기반).

백반증(OLK) 악성전환율(MTR) ~3–17% (비균질형·면적>200mm² 고위험); OLP MTR ~0.5–3%; OSF는 개구도<35mm 시 수술; OLP 1차: 국소 코르티코스테로이드, 2차: 타크로리무스; OSF 주요 원인: 아레카넛.

각 OPMD별 위험층화 및 치료 알고리즘을 단일 참고문헌으로 제공해 병소 유형별 추적 강도·치료 선택을 근거 기반으로 지원.

## Summary
Narrative overview mapping OPMD subtypes (OLK, OLP, OE, OSF, actinic cheilitis) to their respective risk factors, transformation rates, and available therapies.

## Key Contributions
- OLK risk stratification: homogeneous vs non-homogeneous; size >200mm²
- OLP treatment: topical corticosteroids (1st line), tacrolimus (2nd line)
- OSF: areca nut primary cause; severity-based surgical algorithm

## Methodology
PubMed narrative review.

## Results
OLK MTR ~3–17%; non-homogeneous highest. OLP MTR ~0.5–3%. OSF requires surgical intervention when mouth opening <35mm.
