## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PubMed/Medline) restricting analysis to 23 high-quality studies with histopathologically confirmed oral lichen planus (OLP) meeting WHO/Li modification criteria and ≥5-year follow-up, to correct inflated MTR estimates caused by diagnostic misclassification in earlier literature.

True malignant transformation rate (MTR) is 0.5–1.4% — substantially lower than earlier ~5% estimates; median time to transformation ~5 years; erosive/atrophic subtypes carry higher transformation risk.

Rigorous histopathological confirmation is essential before classifying OLP as high malignant-risk; the practical implication is that routine surveillance intensity should be calibrated to subtype (erosive/atrophic > reticular).

## 세줄요약

조직병리학적 확진 + WHO/Li 기준 충족 + 최소 5년 추적이라는 엄격 포함기준으로 고품질 연구 23편만 선별한 체계적 문헌고찰 — 기존 과대추정 교정 목적.

진정한 구강편평태선(Oral Lichen Planus, OLP) 악성전환율(MTR) 0.5–1.4% — 기존 ~5% 추정치보다 현저히 낮음; 중앙 전환소요기간 ~5년; 미란성/위축성 아형에서 위험 더 높음.

진단 오분류가 MTR을 과대추정하므로 조직병리 확진 필수; 임상적으로 추적 강도는 미란성/위축성 > 망상형 순으로 차등 적용 필요.

## Summary
Systematic review restricting analysis to histopathologically confirmed OLP studies meeting WHO/Li criteria, recalculating true malignant transformation rate.

## Key Contributions
- Strict inclusion: histopathologically confirmed OLP + minimum 5-year follow-up
- True MTR 0.5–1.4% — substantially lower than earlier estimates (~5%)
- Erosive/atrophic subtypes carry higher transformation risk

## Methodology
PubMed/Medline systematic search; WHO + Li modification criteria applied.

## Results
23 high-quality studies included. Pooled MTR 0.5–1.4%. Median time to transformation ~5 years.
