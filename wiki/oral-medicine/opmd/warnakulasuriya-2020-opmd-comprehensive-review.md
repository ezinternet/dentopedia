## Three-line Summary

Comprehensive narrative review (Oral Oncology 2020, PubMed/Cochrane) of the full oral potentially malignant disorder (OPMD) spectrum — WHO classification, risk factors, malignant transformation rates, and management across leukoplakia, erythroplakia, OLP, OSF, and actinic cheilitis.

OLP MTR ~0.5–1.4% (strict criteria) to 0.4–12.5% (all studies); leukoplakia MTR ~1–17.5% (non-homogeneous highest); erythroplakia carries the highest per-lesion malignant risk; risk-factor elimination (smoking cessation) is first-line management.

The review serves as the authoritative clinical reference for the full OPMD spectrum, linking WHO classification through evidence-based MTR estimates to surveillance and treatment decision trees.

## 세줄요약

구강잠재악성질환(OPMD) 전 스펙트럼(WHO 분류: 백반증·홍반증·OLP·구강점막하섬유증(OSF)·광선구순염)의 위험인자·악성전환율·관리를 아우르는 포괄 내러티브 고찰 (Oral Oncology 2020, PubMed/Cochrane).

OLP 악성전환율(MTR) ~0.5–1.4%(엄격 기준) ~ 0.4–12.5%(전체 연구); 백반증 MTR ~1–17.5%(비균질형 최고); 홍반증이 병소 단위 악성 위험 가장 높음; 위험인자 제거(금연)가 1차 관리.

WHO 분류부터 근거 기반 MTR 추정치, 추적·치료 의사결정까지 OPMD 전 스펙트럼의 권위 있는 임상 참고문헌 역할.

## Summary
Comprehensive narrative review covering the OPMD spectrum: definition, WHO classification, risk factors, malignant transformation rates, and management.

## Key Contributions
- WHO OPMD classification: leukoplakia, erythroplakia, OLP, OSF, actinic cheilitis
- Malignant transformation rates: OLP 0.4–12.5%, leukoplakia 1–17.5%
- Management: smoking cessation, lesion excision, surveillance protocols

## Methodology
Narrative review based on PubMed and Cochrane literature.

## Results
OLP MTR ~0.5–1.4% (strict criteria). Leukoplakia MTR ~3–17%; non-homogeneous higher risk. Risk factor elimination is first-line management.
