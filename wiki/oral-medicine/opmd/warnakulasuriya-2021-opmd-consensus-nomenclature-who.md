## Three-line Summary

WHO Collaborating Centre international consensus report (expert seminar + Delphi + literature review) standardizing nomenclature and classification of oral potentially malignant disorders (OPMDs) to reduce diagnostic heterogeneity across research and clinical practice.

The consensus formally adopts 'potentially malignant disorder' over 'pre-malignant', recognizes 9 OPMDs including OLP, leukoplakia, erythroplakia, OSF, and actinic cheilitis, and applies three-tier malignant-transformation risk stratification; proliferative verrucous leukoplakia (PVL) is flagged as high-risk.

Standardized OPMD terminology enables valid cross-study comparison of malignant transformation rates and risk factors — the foundational document underpinning all subsequent OPMD epidemiology and clinical research in this wiki.

## 세줄요약

WHO 구강암 협력센터 국제 전문가 세미나+Delphi+문헌고찰로 구강잠재악성질환(Oral Potentially Malignant Disorder, OPMD) 명칭·분류를 표준화한 합의문 — 연구·임상 전반의 진단 이질성 해소 목적.

'잠재적 악성 장애(potentially malignant disorder)' 용어를 '전암성(pre-malignant)' 대신 공식 채택; OLP·백반증·홍반증·OSF·광선구순염 포함 9개 OPMD 인정; 악성전환 위험 3단계 계층화; 증식성 우모상 백반증(PVL) 고위험군 지정.

표준화된 OPMD 용어로 악성전환율·위험인자 연구 간 유효한 비교 가능 — 이 위키의 모든 OPMD 역학·임상 연구의 기반 문헌.

## Summary
WHO-convened international consensus establishing standardized nomenclature and classification for OPMDs, replacing older pre-malignant terminology.

## Key Contributions
- Official adoption of 'potentially malignant disorder' over 'pre-malignant'
- Nine conditions recognized as OPMDs including OLP, leukoplakia, erythroplakia, OSF, actinic cheilitis
- Three-tier risk stratification (low/moderate/high malignant transformation risk)

## Methodology
International expert seminar + Delphi consensus + literature review.

## Results
Consensus: 9 OPMDs recognized. PVL identified as high-risk. Standardized terminology reduces diagnostic heterogeneity across studies.
