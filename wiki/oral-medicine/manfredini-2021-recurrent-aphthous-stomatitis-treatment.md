## Three-line Summary

PubMed narrative review of recurrent aphthous stomatitis (RAS) treatment covering classification into three types (minor <1 cm, major >1 cm with scarring, herpetiform multiple 1–2 mm) and a stepwise treatment ladder from topical to systemic agents.

First-line treatment is topical corticosteroids (triamcinolone acetonide) and hyaluronic acid; colchicine 0.5 mg bid has good evidence for severe/frequent RAS; thalidomide and dapsone are reserved for refractory major RAS; trigger correction (stress, iron/B12 deficiency) is integral.

RAS management is symptom-directed and recurrence-reduction focused rather than curative — the review provides a practical clinical framework but lacks systematic evidence grading.

## 세줄요약

PubMed 서술 리뷰 — 재발성 아프타성 구내염(Recurrent Aphthous Stomatitis, RAS)의 분류(소형 <1 cm·대형 >1 cm 반흔형·포진형 다발 1–2 mm)와 국소~전신 단계적 치료 사다리(treatment ladder)를 정리.

1차 치료: 국소 스테로이드(트리암시놀론 아세토나이드, triamcinolone acetonide)·히알루론산; 중증·빈발 RAS: 콜히친(colchicine) 0.5 mg 1일 2회에 양호한 근거; 난치성 대형 RAS: 탈리도마이드(thalidomide)·답손(dapsone); 유발인자(스트레스·영양 결핍) 교정 병행.

RAS 치료는 완치가 아닌 증상 완화·재발 감소 지향 — 실용적 임상 프레임워크를 제공하나 체계적 근거 등급 평가는 부재.

## Summary
Review of RAS classification (minor/major/herpetiform), pathomechanism, and stepwise treatment from topical to systemic.

## Key Contributions
- RAS classification: minor (<1cm, heals in 1–2 weeks), major (>1cm, scarring), herpetiform (multiple 1–2mm)
- 1st-line: topical corticosteroids (triamcinolone acetonide), hyaluronic acid
- Severe recurrent RAS: colchicine, dapsone, or thalidomide

## Methodology
PubMed narrative review.

## Results
Topical steroids reduce pain and healing time. For severe/frequent RAS: colchicine 0.5mg bid shows good evidence. Triggers (stress, nutritional deficiency) must be addressed.
