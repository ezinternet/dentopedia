## Three-line Summary

Prospective study (n=12, RCBH 6–8 mm) evaluating the HaeNaem Zero Bone Loss bur kit for OD-based indirect sinus lift at a single center, with CBCT assessment at 4 months (Cureus, 2024).

Reported statistically significant improvement in alveolar bone height (p < 0.01); however, this paper was **officially retracted** by Cureus (PMID: 39575356; DOI: 10.7759/cureus.73130) — all reported findings are invalid.

Do not cite this paper as clinical evidence for osseodensification or indirect sinus lift outcomes under any circumstances.

## 세줄요약

HaeNaem Zero Bone Loss 버 키트를 이용한 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD) 간접 상악동 거상 전향적 연구 (n=12, 잔존골고도 (Residual Crestal Bone Height, RCBH) 6–8 mm); CBCT 4개월 추적 (Cureus 2024).

골고도 유의한 개선 보고 (p<0.01) — 그러나 **Cureus에서 공식 철회됨** (PMID: 39575356); 모든 수치 무효.

임상 근거로 절대 인용 불가 — 치조정접근 상악동 거상 (Transcrestal Sinus Floor Elevation, TSFE) 또는 OD 임상 결과에 이 논문을 사용하지 말 것.

## ⚠️ RETRACTED — DO NOT CITE

This paper has been **retracted**. Confirmed "Retracted Publication" via PubMed (PMID: 39575356). Do not use as clinical evidence. (set 2026-09-18)
