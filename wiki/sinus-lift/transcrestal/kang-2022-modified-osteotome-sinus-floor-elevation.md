## Three-line Summary

Non-randomized controlled trial (IJERPH 2022;19:8019, DOI 10.3390/ijerph19138019) of a modified osteotome sinus floor elevation (OSFE) for **two adjacent implants** in maxillary posterior multiple edentulous spaces: 18 patients with RBH < 5 mm vs 12 with RBH ≥ 5 mm; 30 patients total, 37–79 years, two implants each.

Technique: CAS drill preparation → dome-shaped sinus curettes (Dentium DASK kit) release Schneiderian membrane tension by blunt dissection around the cavity and **between the two implants** → osteotome elevates membrane to desired height → graft fill → simultaneous implant placement.

At 6 months: 100% implant survival, zero membrane perforation (vs ~28% reported for RBH < 5 mm), no complications; graft height apically between implants stabilized at 8.92 mm, graft resorption statistically comparable to the RBH ≥ 5 mm group (no significant difference).

## Overview

Conventional transcrestal sinus floor elevation is preferred for RBH > 5 mm; below that, membrane perforation risk rises steeply. This trial (Apr 2016–Jun 2019, West China Hospital of Stomatology, Sichuan University) demonstrates a curette-based tension-release modification of the classic osteotome (Summers-type) technique enabling predictable placement of two adjacent implants simultaneously even at RBH < 5 mm — a configuration the authors note few studies have addressed, particularly regarding membrane perforation and inter-implant bone formation.

## Key Findings

- **Design**: prospective non-randomized controlled trial; 30 patients (15 M/15 F, 37–79 y); 2 adjacent implants per patient (e.g., 15/16, 26/27, 25/26). Groups by RBH: 0–4 mm (n=9; mean RBH 2.84±0.94 / 2.71±0.82 mm mesial/distal), 4–5 mm (n=9; 4.66±0.31 / 4.48±0.38 mm), 5–7 mm (n=12; 6.09±0.67 / 6.04±0.74 mm). Sinus width 8.91–17.31 mm.
- **Modified OSFE steps**: (1) CAS (Dentium) drill preparation; (2) dome-shaped sinus curette from the Dental Advanced Sinus Kit (DASK, Dentium) gently releases membrane tension by blunt dissection in all directions — crucially also detaching the membrane **between the two implants** to create space for graft; (3) osteotome elevates the membrane to the desired height; (4) bone graft fills the elevated space; (5) implants placed simultaneously.
- **Survival**: 100% at 6 months (all 30 patients, 60 implants).
- **Membrane perforation**: **0 cases**, including the RBH < 5 mm groups — vs 28% reported in the literature for RBH < 5 mm (perforation typically located at the center of the elevated membrane). Authors attribute avoidance to the tension-release step.
- **Graft height**: 6-month apical graft height (aGH) between the two implants stabilized at **8.92 mm**; graft resorption (GR) showed **no significant difference** across the three RBH groups — the modified technique achieves in RBH < 5 mm the same graft stability as RBH ≥ 5 mm.
- **Safety**: no related complications or adverse effects; cited lateral-window (LSFE) benchmark: 44.74% severe pain during first 2 days, 97.36% swelling within a week.
- **Recommended for**: elderly patients or those unsuitable for longer/lateral procedures — shorter treatment, fewer complications, minimized membrane perforation risk.

## Clinical Implications

1. For adjacent implants with RBH < 5 mm, the curette-based tension release — specifically including the **inter-implant membrane** — appears to be the operative step preventing the ~28% expected perforation rate; do not skip blunt dissection in all directions.
2. Two-stage simultaneous placement is feasible regardless of RBH (0–4 mm included) when membrane elevation is atraumatic; expect ~8.9 mm graft height between implants at 6 months with resorption comparable to ≥5 mm RBH cases.
3. The modified OSFE is a strong option for elderly/systemically constrained patients where lateral sinus floor elevation morbidity (pain/swelling rates above) is undesirable.
