## One-line Summary

Retrospective case series (n=15, 20 implants): CAS-Kit hydraulic TSFE with minimum RBH 2mm achieved 9.6±2.4mm bone gain and 100% implant survival at 9-month follow-up with no membrane perforations.

## Summary

Ceruso et al. conducted a single-center retrospective case series evaluating the CAS-Kit hydraulic transcrestal sinus lift combined with tissue-level implants (PRAMA, Sweden & Martina) in 15 patients with 20 implants, with a minimum RBH of 2mm. All implants achieved osseointegration without failure or complication over a mean follow-up of 19.5±6.4 months. Mean bone gain was 9.6±2.4mm, and overall membrane elevation settled from 13.8±1.8mm at placement to 13.0±1.6mm at 9-month crown delivery (p=0.000), a clinically acceptable 0.8mm remodeling. Bone above the implant tip was maintained at 3.0±1.2mm at 9 months. The study extends the minimum RBH indication for CAS-Kit TSFE from the conventional 3–5mm threshold down to 2mm, complementing FEA evidence (Lin 2025) that hydraulic pressure generates 47% less force than osteotomes.

## Key Contributions

- Extends CAS-Kit TSFE minimum RBH indication to **2.0mm** (15 patients, 20 implants, 0 failures).
- Validates Lin 2025 FEA prediction: hydraulic technique produces no membrane perforations in clinical practice at this series' scale.
- Membrane elevation remodeling is minimal (0.8mm over 9m), supporting graft volume stability.
- Bone above implant tip maintained at 3.0mm — relevant for osseointegration quality in elevated sinus floor.
- PRAMA tissue-level implant + CAS-Kit combination achieves stable outcomes without biological or technical complications.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective case series; single center, Rome 2021–2024.
- **n**: 15 patients, 20 implants (21/36 candidates excluded, mostly for missing follow-up radiograph).
- **Implant**: PRAMA tissue-level, ZirTi surface, BOPT design, seating torque ≥25 Ncm.
- **Graft**: Bio-Oss 0.25–1mm (bovine xenograft), all sites grafted.
- **Imaging**: CBCT for planning; panoramic at placement and 9 months.
- **Statistics**: Wilcoxon signed-rank test, p<0.05; implant as statistical unit.
