## Three-line Summary

Preliminary study (n=31 implants, 27 patients, Bangkok) using CBCT to evaluate bone formation 6 months after osteotome sinus floor elevation (OSFE) without grafting in areas 14–17 and 24–27; correlations between residual bone height (RBH), implant protrusion length (IPL), and endo-sinus bone gain (ESBG) analyzed with Pearson correlation.

Significant new bone formation at 6 months (RBH increased from 7.14 to 8.95 mm, mean ESBG 1.8 mm, p<0.05); IPL (mean 2.02 mm) was positively correlated with ESBG (p<0.05); RBH was negatively correlated with ESBG; the efficiency of bone formation per IPL decreases as IPL increases; 100% survival.

Graft-free OSFE yields predictable short-term bone formation around protruding implants; IPL is the key modifiable driver of ESBG and should be optimized (positive effect with diminishing returns above ~4 mm per later long-term data).

6개월 유의한 신생골 형성 확인 (RBH 7.14 → 8.95 mm, 평균 ESBG 1.8mm, p<0.05); IPL (평균 2.02mm)이 ESBG와 양의 상관관계 (p<0.05); RBH는 ESBG와 음의 상관관계; IPL 증가 시 골형성 효율(ESBG/IPL 비율)은 감소; 생존율 100%.

## Summary

This preliminary study from Srinakharinwirot University, Bangkok evaluated new bone formation using CBCT in 31 implants (27 patients) that underwent graft-free OSFE (Summers technique) in the posterior maxilla (areas 14–17 and 24–27). Pre-operative CBCT was taken, and clinical and radiographic re-examination including CBCT was repeated at 6 months post-implantation. Measurements included RBH before surgery, new bone level at 6 months, ESBG, and IPL.

**Key CBCT findings:**
- Pre-op mean RBH: 7.14 ± 1.07 mm
- 6-month bone level: 8.95 ± 1.17 mm
- Mean ESBG: 1.8 ± 0.79 mm
- Mean IPL: 2.02 ± 0.73 mm

**Correlations:**
- IPL and ESBG: significantly positive (p<0.05) — longer protrusion = more bone gain
- RBH and ESBG: negative — shorter pre-op residual bone → greater relative bone gain
- Percentage bone formation/IPL decreases as IPL increases — diminishing returns at higher IPL values

100% implant survival at 6 months. No details of complications reported.

## Key Contributions

- First CBCT-based systematic measurement of IPL-ESBG correlation in Thai population using graft-free OSFE
- Confirms that graft material is not necessary for bone formation when membrane is properly elevated
- Demonstrates that IPL is the primary driver of ESBG — supported by subsequent 3–9 year data from Yu 2021
- Identifies diminishing bone formation efficiency at higher IPL — early evidence for an IPL ceiling effect

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective case series with prospective CBCT follow-up
- **n**: 31 implants; 27 patients; posterior maxilla (areas 14–17, 24–27)
- **Inclusion**: History of OSFE without grafting; CBCT available pre-op
- **Outcomes**: RBH, ESBG, IPL (CBCT measurement); implant survival
- **Statistics**: Paired t-test (pre vs post bone level); Pearson correlation

## Results

Significant bone gain at 6 months (p<0.05). Mean ESBG 1.8 mm. IPL positively correlated with ESBG (p<0.05). RBH negatively correlated with ESBG. 100% survival.
