## Summary
This 8-year prospective cohort evaluated 89 posterior maxilla cases requiring sinus lift with simultaneous implant placement, divided into graftless (n=74) and grafted control (n=15) groups. With a minimum 60-month follow-up, the graftless group achieved a 97.76% implant success rate, notably exceeding the 86.6% seen in the grafted group. Residual bone height was identified as a statistically significant predictor of success in both groups, reinforcing its role as the primary patient selection criterion for simultaneous transcrestal sinus lift. The study provides among the strongest long-term evidence supporting graftless approaches when adequate residual bone height is present.

## Key Contributions
- Largest long-term prospective cohort (≥8 years, n=74 graftless) for simultaneous graftless sinus lift
- Graftless success rate 97.76% exceeds grafted control 86.6% — counterintuitive result challenging graft necessity
- Confirms residual bone height as the primary predictor of success (P<0.05)
- Provides long-term evidence supporting graftless as first-line when residual bone height criteria are met

## Methodology
- Design: Prospective cohort, 8 years
- Setting: Single center, Athens, Greece
- n=89 total: 74 graftless (test), 15 grafted (control)
- Indication: Posterior maxillary implants requiring sinus floor elevation
- Simultaneous implant placement at time of sinus lift
- Follow-up: Minimum 60 months (5 years)
- Primary outcome: Implant success rate; secondary: residual bone height as predictor

## Results
| Group | n | Implant success rate |
|---|---|---|
| Graftless (test) | 74 | **97.76%** |
| Grafted (control) | 15 | 86.6% |

- Residual bone height: significant predictor of success in both groups (P<0.05)
- Graftless approach showed superior success rate vs grafted control
- No significant complications specific to graftless technique reported

## Three-line Summary

- Prospective 8-year cohort study (n=89 posterior maxilla cases: 74 graftless, 15 grafted control; minimum 60-month follow-up; single center, Athens) comparing graftless vs grafted simultaneous transcrestal sinus lift with dental implant placement.
- Graftless group achieved 97.76% implant success rate vs 86.6% in the grafted control; residual bone height was a statistically significant predictor of success in both groups (p<0.05).
- Long-term evidence supports graftless simultaneous transcrestal sinus lift as first-line when adequate residual bone height criteria are met; the graftless approach yielded superior success compared to grafted controls at 8 years, challenging the necessity of bone grafting.

- Graftless군 임플란트 성공률(Implant Success Rate) 97.76% — 골이식 대조군(86.6%) 초과; 잔존골 높이(Residual Bone Height, RBH)가 두 군 모두에서 유의한 성공 예측 인자(p<0.05).
