## Three-line Summary

Prospective study (n=60; RBH ≥5 mm) comparing graftless transalveolar sinus floor elevation with simultaneous implant placement in freshly extracted sockets (Group A, n=30) vs healed sockets (Group B, n=30) using uniform B&B dental implants (4.5×10 mm) at Jiblah University, Yemen.

Both groups achieved 100% implant survival with no biological or mechanical complications; primary ISQ was satisfactory and did not differ significantly between groups (p=0.38); intrasinus bone formation was slightly higher in fresh sockets (0.22 vs 0.11 mm) but not statistically significant (p=0.26); CBCT confirmed at ≥12 months.

Graftless simultaneous SFE via transalveolar approach is predictable for both fresh and healed sockets with RBH ≥5 mm — fresh socket status does not adversely affect outcomes and may marginally improve intrasinus bone gain via blood clot enrichment.

## Summary

This prospective study from Jiblah University, Yemen introduced a novel graftless maxillary sinus lifting approach for simultaneous implant placement, comparing outcomes in freshly extracted vs healed posterior maxillary sockets (RBH ≥5 mm). All procedures used identical B&B dental implants (4.5 mm × 10 mm). The surgical protocol involved drilling 0.5 mm before the sinus floor, gradual drilling with final bur 1 mm smaller than implant diameter, manual then wrench-assisted insertion to bicortical anchorage, and sinus membrane tenting to full implant length. Fresh sockets received xenograft only in the jumping zone (buccal gap) — not for sinus augmentation.

CBCT was taken pre-operatively and ≥12 months post-surgery, measuring intrasinus implant height with and without bone formation. ISQ was measured at implant placement. No prophylactic antibiotics details specified.

Key finding: socket type (fresh vs healed) does not significantly influence primary stability or intrasinus bone formation in graftless simultaneous TSFE with RBH ≥5 mm.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates 100% survival in both fresh and healed socket groups with zero complications (n=60, ≥12 months)
- ISQ non-inferiority: fresh sockets do not compromise primary stability vs healed sockets
- Fresh sockets trend toward slightly higher intrasinus bone formation (blood clot more abundant in fresh socket environment), though NS at n=30/group
- Supports graftless transalveolar SFE as low-cost, low-morbidity alternative — no donor site, no graft material for sinus compartment

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective clinical study; n=60 (30+30); single center, single experienced implantologist
- **Inclusion**: Posterior maxilla, RBH ≥5 mm, age 27–59, systemically healthy, non-smokers, no sinus pathology
- **Implant**: B&B dental (Italy), 4.5 mm × 10 mm; uniform for both groups
- **Sinus lifting**: Transalveolar; drill to 0.5 mm before sinus floor cortex; final bur 1 mm narrower than implant
- **Graft**: None for sinus compartment; xenograft only for jumping zone in fresh sockets
- **Follow-up**: 12–36 months; CBCT at baseline and ≥12 months
- **Statistics**: Independent t-test; α=0.05; G*Power: n=30/group, 85% power
