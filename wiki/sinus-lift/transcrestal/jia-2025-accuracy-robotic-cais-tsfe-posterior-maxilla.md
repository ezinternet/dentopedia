## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case series (n=40 patients/40 implants, posterior maxilla, 80% delayed placement, RBH 4–9 mm) evaluating robotic computer-assisted implant surgery (r-CAIS, Remebot) combined with manual transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE) using Summers osteotomes, with CBCT-based planned-vs-placed accuracy assessment.

Angular deviation was 1.25° ± 0.61°, global coronal/apical deviations 0.50/0.54 mm; Schneiderian membrane perforation rate was 2.5% (1/40) — substantially below the 7.2% benchmark for osteotome-only TSFE; only implant placement timing significantly affected deviations in LMM.

r-CAIS with a 1-mm sinus floor safety margin achieves high directional accuracy in the posterior maxilla with a low membrane perforation rate, supporting robotic guidance as a safe and precise approach for the anatomically constrained posterior maxillary implant site.

## Summary

Posterior maxillary implant placement faces a unique directional challenge: insufficient residual bone height (RBH) due to sinus pneumatization forces a narrow safe axial window — too short risks poor primary stability, too angled risks Schneiderian membrane perforation. This single-center case series tests whether robotic guidance narrows that risk window.

The Remebot system plans the implant axis prosthetically, computes the sequential drilling path with a 1-mm safety margin below the sinus floor, then executes osteotomy autonomously while displaying real-time depth/force/orientation feedback. After robotic drilling, the surgeon manually elevates the sinus floor with Summers osteotomes before the robot seats the implant. This hybrid protocol separates the two highest-error steps: direction (robot-controlled) and membrane elevation (surgeon-controlled).

The result: angular deviation of 1.25° — considerably lower than conventional freehand or even dynamic CAIS — and membrane perforation rate of 2.5%, less than one-third of the published osteotome benchmark (7.2%, Stacchi). The single perforation occurred at a #17 site with challenging anatomy (Type III bone, 7 mm RBH, CBCT registration). No other intraoperative or postoperative adverse events occurred at 6 months. LMM showed none of the seven subgroup factors (position, arch side, bone quality, implant type/size, registration method) significantly altered accuracy, except placement timing.

Limitation: no control group and n=40 means no definitive efficacy claim over s-CAIS or d-CAIS for TSFE; long-term peri-implant outcomes not yet reported.

## Key Contributions

- Quantifies angular deviation of r-CAIS for posterior maxilla TSFE: **1.25° ± 0.61°** — the tightest reported in a TSFE clinical series
- Documents Schneiderian membrane perforation rate at **2.5%** under r-CAIS, vs 7.2% in osteotome-only meta-analysis
- Operationalizes the safe direction protocol: **1-mm sinus floor safety margin** in robotic planning → near-zero perforation risk
- LMM: IOS registration is as accurate as CBCT registration — reduces radiation exposure
- 80% delayed-placement cases confirm the finding applies to the standard (non-immediate) posterior maxillary context

## Methodology

- **Design**: retrospective case series, STROBE; Xi'an Jiaotong University, Nov 2022–Dec 2024; n=40, no control
- **Robot**: Remebot; trueness 156 µm, precision 33 µm; real-time tracking via reflective-sphere marker on adjacent teeth
- **CBCT**: Orthophos XG, 110 kV/5 mA, 0.3 mm voxel; pre-op planning + immediate post-op accuracy check
- **Inclusion**: RBH 4–9 mm; no sinus infection/pathology; no bisphosphonates, heavy smoking, bruxism, uncontrolled perio
- **Protocol**: prosthetically-driven planning → 1 mm margin below sinus floor → robotic osteotomy (flapless) → manual Summers osteotome TSFE → robotic implant seating
- **Registration**: CBCT (n=19) or IOS-STL overlay (n=21); marker positioned outside robotic arm path
- **Accuracy**: RemebotDent v1.0 superimposition; 7 metrics; ICC 0.96 (intra-examiner)
- **Statistics**: LMM (7 fixed effects); SPSS 27.0; p<0.05

## Results

**Accuracy metrics (planned vs placed):**

| Metric | Mean ± SD | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| Global coronal deviation | 0.50 ± 0.22 mm | 0.43–0.57 mm |
| Global apical deviation | 0.54 ± 0.21 mm | 0.47–0.60 mm |
| **Angular deviation** | **1.25° ± 0.61°** | 1.06°–1.45° |
| Lateral coronal | 0.33 ± 0.14 mm | — |
| Lateral apical | 0.37 ± 0.14 mm | — |

**Complications:** sinus membrane perforation 2.5% (1/40); no other adverse events at 6 months.

**LMM:** only timing (early vs delayed) p<0.05 on coronal and apical deviations; all other 6 factors NS.

**Patient composition:** 32 delayed / 8 early; 30 molars / 10 premolars; 22 Type III / 18 Type IV bone; RBH mean 6.4 ± 1.5 mm.
