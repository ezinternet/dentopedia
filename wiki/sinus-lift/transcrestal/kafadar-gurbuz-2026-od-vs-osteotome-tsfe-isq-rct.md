## Three-line Summary

RCT (Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants 2026): 28 patients, 42 implants; osseodensification (OD, Densahbur) vs Summers' osteotome technique for transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE) with simultaneous implant placement in posterior maxilla with limited bone height.

OD significantly superior for primary stability: ISQ 75.09 vs 67.50 (MD +7.59, p=.013) and insertion torque 32.95 vs 26.25 Ncm (MD +6.70, p=.008); OD also yielded less analgesic consumption and better OHIP scores; MBL and 100% survival comparable.

Osseodensification is the preferred osteotomy technique for TSFE — delivers significantly greater primary stability and better patient-reported outcomes while maintaining equivalent bone preservation and implant survival.

## Summary

Kafadar-Gürbüz et al. (Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants 2026) conducted a prospective RCT at Ege University comparing OD using Densahbur drills vs Summers' osteotome for TSFE with simultaneous implant placement in patients with limited posterior maxillary bone height. OD yielded significantly higher ISQ (MD +7.59, 95%CI 1.68–13.50, p=.013) and insertion torque (MD +6.70 Ncm, 95%CI 1.87–11.53, p=.008). Secondary benefits included less analgesic consumption and better post-loading OHIP scores. MBL was comparable and both groups achieved 100% survival, confirming that OD's advantage is in primary stability and patient experience, not bone outcomes at short follow-up.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT comparing OD vs osteotome specifically for TSFE + simultaneous implant
- OD: ISQ advantage of +7.59 units (p=.013) — clinically meaningful primary stability gain
- OD: IT advantage of +6.70 Ncm (p=.008) — reduced risk of under-torque in grafted sites
- OD: less analgesic use (p=.016) and better OHIP after loading (p=.008)
- MBL and survival comparable — OD does not compromise bone stability
- Extends OD superiority from conventional sites into TSFE context

## Methodology

RCT; Ege University, Turkey; n=28 patients, 42 implants; OD (Densahbur, n=22) vs osteotome (n=20) at TSFE + simultaneous placement; ISQ (Osstell) + IT at placement; MBL, VAS, OHIP-14 at follow-up; linear mixed-effects models, Brunner-Langer.
