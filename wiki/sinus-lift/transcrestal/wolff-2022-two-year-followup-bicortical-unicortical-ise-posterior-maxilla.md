## Three-line Summary

Pilot 2-year follow-up of the Hsu 2016 RCT (32 patients/37 implants, RBH 7–11 mm), comparing bicortical fixation (BF) vs indirect sinus elevation (ISE) vs unicortical (UC) on Periotest stability, intrasinus bone formation, and complications.
PTV at 2 years: BF −1.69 vs UC −1.76 vs ISE −2.22, no significant difference (p=0.76); intrasinus bone gain: ISE > BF for horizontal gain (1.99 vs 1.47 mm, p=0.03) and non-significantly for vertical (3.15 vs 2.35 mm, p=0.13).
Two-year stability is equivalent across all three techniques; ISE generates more intrasinus bone, but BF avoids graft and additional surgical morbidity with equal long-term stability.

## Summary

University of Minnesota 2-year follow-up of the Hsu 2016 randomized pilot trial. Of the original 38 patients/45 implants, 32 patients with 37 implants were available at 2 years. Periotest stability was used instead of ISQ (Osstell) as the primary long-term measure. The three techniques converged to identical stability (PTV range −1.69 to −2.22, all negative values indicating good osseointegration), with no statistically meaningful difference. However, intrasinus bone formation on CBCT showed a significant advantage for ISE in horizontal bone gain — the graft and greenstick fracture drove measurably more intrasinus bone at 2 years than the bicortical approach. The clinical relevance of this extra bone is unclear given equivalent stability. The study reinforces bicortical fixation as the pragmatic first-choice technique: no graft, no osteotome, equivalent stability at 2 years.

## Key Contributions

- First 2-year RCT-level comparison of BF vs ISE vs UC for posterior maxilla implants
- Confirms stability equivalence extends beyond the initial 3–6 months (Hsu 2016) to 2 years
- Quantifies intrasinus bone gain: ISE generates more, but stability outcome is identical
- No peri-implantitis or implant failures reported — safety record maintained at 2 years
- Adds intrasinus bone formation comparison data to the bicortical evidence base

## Results

| Technique | 2-yr PTV | Intrasinus horizontal bone | Intrasinus vertical bone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bicortical fixation | −1.69 ± 0.80 | 1.47 ± 0.16 mm | 2.35 ± 0.38 mm |
| Indirect sinus elevation | −2.22 ± 0.60 | **1.99 ± 0.17 mm** | 3.15 ± 0.43 mm |
| Unicortical | −1.76 ± 0.80 | — | — |

PTV group comparison: p=0.76 (NS). ISE vs BF horizontal bone: p=0.03. ISE vs BF vertical bone: p=0.13.
