## Three-line Summary

Prospective study (17 patients, 26 implants, RBH 4–8 mm) of graftless crestal hydraulic sinus lift (치조정 수압 상악동 거상술, CHSL) using the CAS kit (Hiossen) with simultaneous implant insertion.

100% implant survival at 1 year; mean bone height gain 5.6 mm (6.5 → 11.7 mm); mean insertion torque 32 Ncm; zero membrane perforations.

Graftless CHSL with simultaneous implant placement is a predictable minimally invasive option; bone formation is driven by the blood clot under the implant-tented sinus membrane without any grafting material.

## Summary

This prospective interventional study evaluated graftless crestal hydraulic sinus lift (CHSL) combined with simultaneous implant insertion in 17 patients (26 implants, Osstem TS III) with residual bone heights of 4–8 mm at a single center in India. The procedure used the CAS kit (Hiossen) to deliver controlled hydraulic pressure via saline injection through the osteotomy, elevating the sinus membrane without perforation in all cases. CBCT was performed pre-operatively and at 6 months; implant survival was assessed at 1 year using Buser et al. (2002) criteria.

At 1 year, implant survival was 100%. Mean bone height increased from 6.5 mm to 11.7 mm (gain 5.6 mm). Mean insertion torque was 32 Ncm and no membrane perforations occurred. Radiographic evidence confirmed new bone formation around the implant apex from the blood clot under the tented membrane, consistent with the PILi-driven mechanism identified by Duan et al. (2017).

The study adds single-center prospective data for the hydraulic CHSL sub-technique specifically, complementing the broader graftless evidence base. Limitations include small sample, short follow-up, no control group, single surgeon, and no histologic confirmation.

## Key Contributions

- 100% 1-year implant survival with graftless hydraulic CHSL and simultaneous placement (RBH 4–8 mm)
- Mean bone gain 5.6 mm without any grafting material — blood clot alone sufficient scaffold
- CAS kit (Hiossen) hydraulic technique achieved zero membrane perforations
- Adds prospective single-technique data to the graft-free sinus lift evidence pool

## Methodology

- Design: Prospective interventional study
- n: 17 patients, 26 implants (Osstem TS III)
- Inclusion: RBH 4–8 mm; adequate implant primary stability achievable
- Technique: CAS kit (Hiossen) crestal hydraulic sinus lift — saline under controlled pressure through the osteotomy; simultaneous implant insertion
- Imaging: CBCT pre-op and 6 months post-op
- Follow-up: 1 year clinical examination
- Survival criteria: Buser et al. (2002)
