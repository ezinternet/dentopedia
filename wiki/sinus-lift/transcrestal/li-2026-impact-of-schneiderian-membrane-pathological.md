## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort of 89 patients (123 implants) undergoing transcrestal sinus floor elevation with simultaneous implant placement, grouped as normal, primary pathological thickening (PPT) or odontogenic pathological thickening (OPT) of the Schneiderian membrane.

No early implant failure or complication occurred and ISQ did not differ (p = 0.116); OPT had higher membrane perforation (30.4% vs 9.5% normal and 7.7% PPT, p = 0.023), lower endo-sinus bone gain (2.88 ± 1.59 vs 4.02 ± 1.63 mm, p = 0.015) and higher graft resorption (2.98 ± 1.84 vs 1.37 ± 1.15 mm, p < 0.001).

Neither thickening type affected early implant success, but odontogenic thickening raises perforation risk and lessens new bone; abstract-only evidence with small groups.

## Summary
Prospective cohort of 89 patients and 123 implants placed simultaneously with transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE), stratified by Schneiderian membrane status (normal, PPT, OPT). Outcomes were membrane perforation, early implant failure, postoperative complications, ISQ, and CBCT measures (SMT, RBH, SFEH, endo-sinus bone gain, graft material resorption height) at baseline, immediately post-op and 6 months.

## Key Contributions
- Distinguishes primary from odontogenic membrane thickening in TSFE.
- Early failure: 0 observed (no early failure definition or time window given in the abstract); no failure risk-factor effect sizes are therefore estimable.
- OPT: perforation 30.4% vs 9.5% (normal) and 7.7% (PPT), p = 0.023; ESBG 2.88 ± 1.59 vs 4.02 ± 1.63 mm (p = 0.015); GMRH 2.98 ± 1.84 mm vs 1.37 ± 1.15 (normal) and 1.41 ± 1.26 (PPT), p < 0.001.
- PPT did not differ from normal in ESBG/GMRH (p > 0.05).

## Methodology
Prospective cohort; setting and country not stated in the abstract; group sizes not stated. All patients had TSFE with simultaneous implant placement. Abstract-only: further detail not available.

## Results
[확인] No early failure or postoperative complications; ISQ not different among groups (p = 0.116); OPT worse for perforation, ESBG and GMRH as above.

## Limitations
- Abstract-only: early-failure window, group sizes, graft material, follow-up beyond 6 months not verifiable.
- Zero events means the early-success conclusion cannot identify risk factors.
- Non-randomized, 123 implants; perforation denominators per group unstated.

## Clinical Implication (Korean Practice)
In this cohort, thickened membrane of odontogenic origin did not compromise early implant success with simultaneous TSFE, but expect more perforations and less bone gain; identifying odontogenic membrane thickening on pre-operative CBCT is relevant to counselling and technique planning.
