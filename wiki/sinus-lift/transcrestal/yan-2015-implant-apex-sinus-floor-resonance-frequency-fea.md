> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - 유한요소분석 (FEA) 10모델, 후방 상악; 임플란트 첨단-상악동저 거리 변화 (골 높이 10–14 mm) + 상악동저 피질골 두께 변화 (0.5–2.0 mm); 4×10 mm Nobel Biocare; 129 N 30°
> - **핵심 발견 ①**: 쌍겹질 임플란트에서 상악동저 피질골이 치조정 피질골의 응력을 대신 부담 — 치조정 골소실 위험 감소
> - **핵심 발견 ②**: 첨단 침입 깊이 ↑ → 축방향 공명주파수 (Axial Resonance Frequency) **선형 증가** — 쌍겹질의 안정성 이점 기전 설명
> - **핵심 발견 ③**: 협설방향 공명주파수 (Buccolingual RF = ISQ 방향)는 상악동저 피질골 접촉에 **유의미 변화 없음** — ISQ가 쌍겹질 이점을 과소평가할 수 있는 이유
> - 즉시 하중 (Immediate Loading) 시 쌍겹질 변위가 단겹질 대비 유의하게 작음 — 즉시 하중에 가장 이득
> - 상악동저 피질골 두께 0.5–2.0 mm: 두께 ↑ → 상악동저 응력 증가 but 치조정 피질골에는 미미한 영향
> - **임상 takeaway**: 쌍겹질 고정의 1차 이점은 축방향 안정성 + 즉시 하중 변위 감소; ISQ(협설 RFA)로는 쌍겹질 이점 전체를 포착 못 함 — 임상 연구의 ISQ 결과보다 실제 안정성 이점이 클 수 있음
> - 향후 연구 방향: 오스테오톰 + 쌍겹질(Ahn 2012 의 ISQ 이득 소실 이유) FEA 검증, 초단기 임플란트 확장(Zheng 2025)

## Three-line Summary

3D FEA, 10 posterior maxilla models; Group 1 (n=6): alveolar ridge 10–14 mm with varying apex-to-sinus-floor distance; Group 2 (n=4): fixed ridge 11 mm with varying sinus floor cortex thickness (0.5–2.0 mm); 4 mm × 10 mm Nobel Biocare; 129 N at 30°; full osseointegration and immediate loading (μ=0.3) interfaces compared.
Bicortical implantation shifted stress from crestal to sinus floor cortex; axial resonance frequency increased linearly with apex penetration depth; buccolingual resonance frequency (the direction Osstell ISQ measures) was NOT significantly changed by sinus floor engagement.
Sinus floor cortex engagement primarily benefits axial stability and immediate loading (reduced displacement); ISQ-based assessment may underestimate bicortical fixation benefit because buccolingual RF is insensitive to sinus floor contact.

## 세줄요약

3D FEA 10모델, 후방 상악; 임플란트 첨단-상악동저 거리 변화 (골 높이 10–14 mm) + 피질골 두께 변화 (0.5–2.0 mm); 4×10 mm Nobel Biocare; 129 N 30°.
쌍겹질 임플란트에서 치조정 응력이 상악동저 피질골로 이동; 첨단 침입 깊이 ↑ → 축방향 공명주파수 선형 증가; 협설방향 공명주파수 (ISQ 방향)는 상악동저 접촉에 무반응.
쌍겹질 고정의 1차 이점은 축방향 안정성·즉시 하중 변위; ISQ는 쌍겹질 이점을 과소평가 가능.

## Summary

Seminal FEA study isolating the apex–sinus floor interaction in the posterior maxilla. The study used two groups: one varying bone height (implant-to-floor distance) and one varying sinus floor cortex thickness, holding all other variables constant. The central finding is a mechanistic explanation for why bicortical fixation improves some stability metrics but not others. Axial resonance frequency (measured along the implant long axis) increases linearly as the apex penetrates the sinus floor cortex — providing the FEA basis for the clinical ISQ improvements seen in Bhave 2023. However, buccolingual resonance frequency — the direction that Osstell Smartpeg predominantly measures, yielding the ISQ reading — is not significantly changed by sinus floor engagement. This explains why Lombardi 2024 found bicortical engagement predicts IT but not ISQ in multivariate analysis. The most dramatic bicortical benefit is in immediate loading: reduced implant displacement when the apex engages the sinus floor cortex. Sinus floor cortex absorbs stress that would otherwise concentrate at the crestal cortex, potentially reducing marginal bone loss risk under loading.

## Key Contributions

- First FEA isolating the apex–sinus floor interaction as a single variable
- Explains why bicortical fixation improves IT but not necessarily ISQ: axial vs buccolingual RF distinction
- Axial RF increases linearly with apex penetration depth — dose-response relationship
- Bicortical fixation most beneficial under immediate loading (displacement reduction)
- Stress redistribution FROM crestal cortex TO sinus floor cortex — mechanistic basis for reduced MBL
- Sinus floor cortex thickness (0.5–2.0 mm) affects sinus floor stress but not crestal cortex substantially

## Results

| Finding | Conventional loading | Immediate loading |
|---|---|---|
| Crestal cortex stress: bicortical vs distant apex | **Reduced** | **Substantially reduced** |
| Axial resonance frequency vs penetration depth | Linear increase (r≈1) | Linear increase |
| Buccolingual resonance frequency (ISQ direction) | No significant change | No significant change |
| Implant displacement: bicortical vs unicortical | Smaller | **Significantly smaller** |
| Sinus floor cortex von Mises (bicortical): | 58.69 MPa | 73.44 MPa (>crestal 58.69) |

Sinus membrane von Mises: increased under immediate loading with apex in cortex but below damage thresholds.
