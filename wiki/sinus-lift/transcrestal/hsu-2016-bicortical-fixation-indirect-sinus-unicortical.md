## Three-line Summary

Randomized 3-arm clinical trial, 38 patients / 45 implants, RBH 7–11 mm posterior maxilla; bicortical fixation (stopper-drill engages both crests) vs indirect sinus elevation (greenstick + graft) vs unicortical; ISQ at placement and 3–6 months.
Primary ISQ: indirect sinus (75.9) > bicortical (71.4) > unicortical (69.6) — all p=0.13 (NS); secondary ISQ: all converged to ~80 (p>0.999); CBCT bone density correlated with primary ISQ (r=0.37).
No technique achieved superior secondary stability; bicortical is simpler and less costly than ISE, and allows longer implants than unicortical — recommended as the preferred approach for RBH 7–11 mm.

## Summary

University of Minnesota randomized trial comparing three approaches for implant placement in 7–11 mm RBH posterior maxilla. Bicortical fixation used stopper drills to simultaneously engage the alveolar crest and sinus floor without osteotome — allowing longer implants (mean 11.4 mm) than unicortical (8.1 mm). Indirect sinus elevation used greenstick fracture with bone graft (11.8 mm implants). While ISE showed numerically highest primary ISQ (75.9), the three-way comparison failed to reach significance (p=0.13). At stage-2 surgery (3–6 months), all techniques converged to identical secondary ISQ (~80, p>0.999). CBCT bone density was the significant predictor of primary ISQ (r=0.37), more so than technique choice. The authors conclude bicortical fixation is the preferred technique: simpler than ISE, avoids graft, and provides longer implants than unicortical — all with equivalent long-term stability.

## Key Contributions

- Only RCT directly comparing all three posterior maxilla bicortical strategies simultaneously
- Non-inferiority demonstrated: bicortical primary ISQ not worse than ISE (p=0.13)
- Secondary stability convergence (~80 ISQ) confirms early technique differences clinically irrelevant
- CBCT bone density shown to be the dominant predictor of primary stability (r=0.37)
- Bicortical achieves longer implants than unicortical without graft requirement

## Results

| Technique | Primary ISQ | Secondary ISQ | Implant length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indirect sinus elevation | 75.9 ± 2.3 | 80.0 ± 1.3 | 11.8 mm |
| Bicortical fixation | 71.4 ± 2.1 | 79.9 ± 1.2 | 11.4 mm |
| Unicortical fixation | 69.6 ± 2.1 | 80.0 ± 1.2 | 8.1 mm |

- Primary ISQ: p=0.13 (no significant difference)
- Secondary ISQ: p>0.999
- CBCT bone density vs primary ISQ: r=0.37 (p significant)
