## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo comparative study using 5 mm thick porcine iliac crest blocks to simulate atrophic maxilla in sinus floor elevation; 7 Straumann implant variants (SP, TE, BL, BLT, BLX in 10 mm; SP short and BLX short in 6 mm) evaluated for maximum insertion torque (MIT) and implant stability quotient (ISQ).

BLX 10 mm showed significantly higher MIT than SP, BL (p<0.01) and TE (p<0.05), and significantly higher ISQ than all other groups (p<0.01); BLX short 6 mm outperformed SP short in both metrics, suggesting BLX macrodesign compensates partly for shorter length.

In a 5 mm residual bone SFE simulation, BLX's variable-pitch tapered thread design confers superior primary stability vs conventional Straumann designs — implant selection is a controllable variable when primary stability is critical for simultaneous graftless SFE.

## 세줄요약

돼지 장골두 5mm 블록(위축 상악 + 상악동거상 시뮬레이션)에서 Straumann 7가지 임플란트 변형(SP·TE·BL·BLT·BLX 10mm; SP short·BLX short 6mm)의 최대삽입토크 (Maximum Insertion Torque, MIT)와 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)를 비교한 생체외 연구.

BLX 10mm는 SP·BL 대비 MIT 유의 우위(p<0.01), TE 대비(p<0.05); ISQ는 전군 대비 유의 우위(p<0.01); BLX short 6mm는 SP 10mm 대비 MIT·ISQ 모두 유의 우위 — 매크로디자인이 길이를 부분 보상.

5mm 잔존골 SFE 시뮬레이션에서 BLX의 가변 피치 테이퍼 나사산 디자인이 초기 안정성 우위를 제공 → 무이식 동시 식립 SFE 시 임플란트 선택이 조절 가능한 변수임을 시사.

## Summary

This ex vivo study from Kyushu University (2022) evaluated primary stability of five Straumann 10 mm implant macrodesigns (Standard Plus [SP], Tapered Effect [TE], Bone Level [BL], Bone Level Tapered [BLT], and BLX) plus two 6 mm variants (SP short, BLX short) in a sinus floor elevation (SFE) simulated model. Forty-two porcine iliac crest blocks (5 mm thick) were used to represent residual bone height (잔존골 높이, RBH) in atrophic posterior maxilla. All implants had SLActive surface. MIT was recorded with a digital torque driver; ISQ was measured with Osstell Mentor RFA from four directions.

**Primary stability hierarchy (10 mm group):**
- MIT: BLX > BLT > TE > BL ≥ SP (BLX vs SP/BL p<0.01; BLX vs TE p<0.05)
- ISQ: BLX > all others (p<0.01); BLT 2nd, but inter-group differences among BLT/TE/BL/SP not always significant

**Length effect (BLX 6mm vs SP 6mm; BLX 10mm vs SP 10mm):** BLX significantly outperformed SP regardless of length for both MIT and ISQ (p<0.01), indicating macrodesign advantage exceeds length advantage in this 5 mm bone model.

The BLX's combination of tapered body, variable thread pitch (1.125 mm), and compressive thread geometry explains the higher compression-based primary stability in cancellous-only bone columns.

## Key Contributions

- First direct comparison of BLX vs 4 conventional Straumann implant families in an SFE-simulated model
- Demonstrates BLX ISQ superiority is consistent regardless of implant length
- BLT also outperforms SP and BL — tapered macrodesigns as a category outperform cylindrical in this low-density, low-RBH model
- Provides quantitative MIT and ISQ data to guide implant selection for graftless simultaneous SFE (tenting technique)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Ex vivo, controlled comparative
- **Bone model**: 5 mm porcine iliac crest blocks (cancellous bone, simulating 5 mm RBH); final drill diameter 3.5 mm for all groups
- **Groups**: 7 implant variants, 6 implants per group (n=42 total)
- **MIT**: CEDAR digital torque driver (Sugisaki Keiki)
- **ISQ**: Osstell Mentor (Model 6.0), 4-direction average
- **Statistics**: One-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD; α=0.05

## Results

BLX 10 mm: highest MIT (vs SP/BL p<0.01, vs TE p<0.05) and ISQ (vs all p<0.01). BLT 10 mm: higher MIT than BL (p<0.05) and SP (p<0.01). BLX short 6 mm: higher MIT and ISQ than SP short (p<0.01). BLX 6 mm short vs SP 10 mm: BLX short still significantly higher ISQ.
