## Three-line Summary

Prospective two-arm comparative study (n=60, posterior maxilla, RBH 4-7 mm with oblique sinus floor, Feb 2024-Mar 2025) comparing crestal sinus floor elevation with Densah burs alone (Group I, n=30) vs Densah + adjunctive grafting (Group II, n=30), all with simultaneous implant placement.

Elevation gain was significantly greater without graft (4.22 ± 0.55 vs 3.96 ± 0.29 mm, P=0.042), while RFA implant stability was significantly higher with graft (74.33 ± 4.86 vs 67.76 ± 4.05 ISQ, P<0.001); final seating torque showed no difference (40.33 ± 4.5 vs 38.66 ± 3.9 Ncm, P=0.133).

Densah crestal SFE is a predictable technique for 4-7 mm RBH: grafting buys secondary stability (ISQ >70, favorable for early loading) at a slight elevation cost, but conclusions are limited by short (3-month) follow-up and an unverified randomization claim.

## 세줄요약

잔존골 높이(Residual Bone Height, RBH) 4-7 mm + 경사진 동저(oblique sinus floor) 후방 상악 60명(남 25/여 35, 근평균 39.2세)을 Densah 버 골밀도화(Osseodensification, OD) 단독군(I)과 Densah + 부가 이식재군(II)으로 나눈 전향적 비교 임상연구(2024.2-2025.3).

거상량은 무이식군(I)이 유의하게 큼(4.22 ± 0.55 vs 3.96 ± 0.29 mm, P=0.042), 공진주파수분석(Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)는 이식군(II)이 유의하게 높음(74.33 ± 4.86 vs 67.76 ± 4.05, P<0.001), 최종 식립토크는 양군 차이 없음(P=0.133).

RBH 4-7 mm 경사 동저에서 Densah 경치조골 상악동거상(Crestal Sinus Floor Elevation, CSFE)은 예측 가능한 술식 — 이식재는 ISQ>70의 2차 안정성을 얻되 경미한 거상량 손실이 있으며, 3개월 단기추적과 검증되지 않은 무작위배정 주장이 해석에 주의를 요함.

## Summary

Prospective two-arm clinical study of 60 patients (25M/35F, mean age 39.2 yr) with posterior-maxilla implants, RBH 4-7 mm, and radiographically confirmed oblique sinus floors, comparing Densah-bur crestal sinus floor elevation alone (Group I, n=30) versus with adjunctive grafting (Group II, n=30), all with simultaneous implant placement. The no-graft group achieved significantly greater vertical elevation (4.22 vs 3.96 mm; P=0.042), attributed to a membrane tenting effect, while the grafted group achieved significantly higher RFA stability (74.33 vs 67.76 ISQ; P<0.001), attributed to graft-enhanced secondary stability. Seating torque did not differ (P=0.133), indicating OD compaction alone provides adequate primary stability. The graft-vs-no-graft trade-off — elevation against stability — is a novel comparison for this oblique-floor niche, but the 3-month follow-up, unreported perforation count, and unsubstantiated randomization claim temper the conclusions.

## Key Contributions

- First head-to-head graft-vs-no-graft comparison within Densah crestal SFE in the oblique-floor + 4-7 mm RBH niche: graftless gives more immediate elevation, grafting gives higher RFA stability.
- Largest prospective cohort (n=60) for this specific niche, extending the single-arm n=16 evidence of Shalash et al. 2023 with a concurrent comparator.
- Primary stability (seating torque) independent of grafting (~38.7-40.3 Ncm, P=0.133) — OD bone compaction suffices regardless of graft choice.
- Grafted group reached ISQ >70, cited as the favorable threshold for early loading.

## Methodology

Prospective comparative clinical study (Feb 2024 - Mar 2025, single center, Riyadh). The abstract claims patients were "randomized into two groups"; the Methods state only "Participants were randomly allocated into two groups (n = 30 each)" with **no** randomization method, allocation concealment, or blinding described — classified here as a prospective comparative study, not a verified RCT. Inclusion: RBH 4.0-7.0 mm, oblique sinus floor (radiographically confirmed), adequate ridge dimensions, ASA I-II. Exclusion: active sinus pathology, uncontrolled systemic disease, >10 cigarettes/day, prior grafting at the site. Group I (n=30): Densah crestal SFE without graft; Group II (n=30): Densah crestal SFE with adjunctive grafting. Procedure: pre-op panoramic → crestal incision/flap → sequential reverse-mode Densah osteotomies with controlled membrane elevation → graft in Group II → simultaneous implant placement → final seating torque and RFA (Osstell) recorded at placement → immediate post-op periapical. Follow-up at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months. The quantitative definition of "vertical elevation achieved" is not specified in the text; imaging was panoramic + periapical, **no CBCT**. Statistics: SPSS 29, Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality, independent t-test, P ≤ 0.05; sample-size calculation (≥22/group) inflated to 30/group for attrition.

## Results

| Outcome | Group I (no graft, n=30) | Group II (graft, n=30) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Final seating torque (Ncm) | 40.33 ± 4.5 | 38.66 ± 3.9 | 0.133 (NS) |
| Vertical elevation achieved (mm) | 4.22 ± 0.55 | 3.96 ± 0.29 | 0.042* |
| RFA score (ISQ) | 67.76 ± 4.05 | 74.33 ± 4.86 | <0.001* |

- (* significant; NS = not significant)
- Sex: Group I 12M/18F; Group II 13M/17F. Age bands similar (largest 41-50 yr, 40% in each group).
- No membrane perforation count reported. Abstract's "20-65 years" vs body's "20 to >60 years (mean 39.2)" — minor inconsistency.
