## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study (n=105 implants, 65 patients, Sun Yat-sen University) assessing influence of implant protrusion length (IPL) on long-term outcomes of non-grafting osteotome sinus floor elevation (OSFE); mean follow-up 5.04 years (range 3–9 years); IPL divided into three groups (<2mm, 2–<4mm, ≥4mm).

Cumulative survival rates: 96.4% implant-based, 94.1% patient-based; mean ESBG 1.95 ± 0.88 mm, MBL 0.58 ± 0.68 mm, BICL 5.51 ± 1.47 mm; ESBG positively correlated with IPL, but bone formation efficiency (%ESBG/IPL) significantly decreased when IPL ≥4 mm (p=0.02).

An optimal IPL range within 4 mm is recommended for non-grafting OSFE: longer protrusion increases absolute ESBG but with diminishing efficiency and potential risk of membrane strain — IPL is the single most influential factor for intrasinus bone formation in the graft-free tenting model.

## Summary

This long-term retrospective study from Sun Yat-sen University Hospital of Stomatology (Guangzhou, China) analyzed 105 implants in 65 patients who underwent non-grafting OSFE with simultaneous implant placement, with a mean follow-up of 5.04 years (range 3–9 years). IPL was measured on postoperative panoramic or periapical radiographs and grouped: Group 1 (<2 mm), Group 2 (2–<4 mm), Group 3 (≥4 mm). Outcomes measured at latest follow-up included ESBG, MBL, BICL, and %ESBG (efficiency metric).

Three implants failed (survival criteria: no mobility, no intractable pain, no peri-implantitis requiring removal), yielding 102 evaluable implants (62 patients).

**Survival:** 96.4% (implant-based), 94.1% (patient-based) at 3–9 years.

**ESBG by IPL group:** Positive correlation confirmed across all groups. When IPL exceeded 4 mm (Group 3), bone formation per unit of IPL (%ESBG) significantly decreased (p=0.02), indicating a ceiling/efficiency drop above 4 mm — possibly due to limits of membrane elasticity and osteogenic capacity.

**Multiple linear regression:** IPL emerged as the strongest predictor of ESBG among variables analyzed (RBH, implant length, diameter, etc.).

## Key Contributions

- Establishes 4 mm as the practical IPL ceiling for efficient bone formation in graft-free OSFE
- Provides longest average follow-up (5.04 years) among dedicated non-grafting OSFE IPL studies as of publication
- Survival 96.4% is consistent with meta-analytic estimates for graft-free transcrestal SFE
- MBL of 0.58 mm at 5 years is within acceptable range (<1 mm crestal bone loss)
- BICL 5.51 mm represents substantial osseointegration depth despite sinus protrusion

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective single-center cohort; Sun Yat-sen University
- **n**: 105 implants, 65 patients; 3–9 years (mean 5.04 years)
- **Groups**: G1 IPL <2mm; G2 2mm ≤ IPL <4mm; G3 IPL ≥4mm
- **Outcomes**: ESBG, MBL, BICL, %ESBG at latest follow-up
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier survival; multiple linear regression; one-way ANOVA
- **Failure criteria**: Implant removal due to mobility, pain, or peri-implantitis

## Results

- Cumulative survival: 96.4% (implant), 94.1% (patient)
- Mean ESBG: 1.95 ± 0.88 mm; MBL: 0.58 ± 0.68 mm; BICL: 5.51 ± 1.47 mm
- IPL ≥4mm: significantly decreased %ESBG (p=0.02)
- IPL is primary ESBG determinant in multiple regression
