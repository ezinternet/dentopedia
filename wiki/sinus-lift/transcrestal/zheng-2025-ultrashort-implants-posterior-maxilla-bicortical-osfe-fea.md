> - 유한요소분석 (Finite Element Analysis, FEA) 14모델: 초단기 임플란트 (3–6 mm, 직경 4.1 mm) × 3술식 (단겹질 고정/쌍겹질 고정/치조정접근 상악동 거상) × 잔존골 높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 3–5 mm

> - **쌍겹질 고정 vs 단겹질 고정** (RBH=5 mm, 3 mm 임플란트): 취소골 변형률 3.3–3.4배 감소 — 단겹질 strain 23,000 vs 쌍겹질 훨씬 낮음

> - 모든 모델에서 임플란트 변위 <150 μm (골유착 임계값 충족); 피질골 응력은 생리적 한계 이내
> - OSFE 모델에서 임플란트 주위 0.5 mm 신생골 형성 효과 포함 — OSFE 안정성이 과대평가될 수 있음 (한계)
> - 쌍겹질 달성 가능 조건: RBH 3 mm → 3–4 mm 임플란트; RBH 4 mm → 3–5 mm; RBH 5 mm → 4–6 mm; 임플란트 길이 < RBH는 단겹질

## Three-line Summary

FEA, 14 models: 4 ultrashort implant lengths (3–6 mm, 4.1 mm diameter) × 3 surgical approaches (unicortical / bicortical / OSFE) in posterior maxilla with RBH 3–5 mm; 150 N at 30° oblique load; von Mises stress, cancellous bone strain, and implant displacement as outcomes.
6 mm implants showed the lowest stress and displacement across all conditions; bicortical fixation reduced cancellous bone strain 3.3–3.4× vs unicortical for 3 mm implants at RBH=5 mm; OSFE with 6 mm implant achieved the best overall biomechanical profile.
For RBH 3–5 mm, 6 mm ultrashort implant + OSFE is the biomechanically optimal approach; bicortical is superior to unicortical but is outperformed by OSFE with sufficient implant length in the most atrophic bone.

## Summary

FEA study from Dalian University comparing 12 ultrashort implant models (4 lengths × 3 approaches) plus 2 controls (8, 10 mm) in posterior maxilla with residual bone height 3–5 mm. This is the first FEA to directly compare unicortical, bicortical, and OSFE approaches for ultrashort implants under matched conditions. The key finding is hierarchical: implant length dominates over surgical approach — 6 mm performs better than 3 mm regardless of approach. Within a given length, the hierarchy is OSFE > bicortical > unicortical for stress distribution. For 3 mm implants in 5 mm bone, bicortical reduces cancellous bone strain 3.3–3.4× vs unicortical, a mechanically significant difference that explains the ISQ and clinical stability advantages seen in in vitro and clinical studies. OSFE with a 6 mm implant introduces 0.5 mm of new bone around the apex, further distributing load and yielding the best overall profile. All models remain within physiological limits (displacement <150 μm; cortical stress below fracture limits), supporting the general safety of ultrashort implants in this bone height range.

## Key Contributions

- First FEA comparing unicortical / bicortical / OSFE for ultrashort implants (3–6 mm) in RBH 3–5 mm
- Implant length is the dominant variable — 6 mm consistently best regardless of approach
- Bicortical reduces cancellous bone strain 3.3–3.4× vs unicortical for shortest implants (RBH=5, 3 mm)
- OSFE with 6 mm implant achieves best stress profile, surpassing bicortical
- All models satisfy 150 μm displacement threshold; no model risks bone overload
- Bicortical fixation achievable only when implant length > RBH; length=RBH → apex just contacts cortex; length < RBH → unicortical only
