## Summary

Di Frischia et al. report a 68-year-old patient with critically limited bone (RBH 3.6mm and 2.5mm at sites 1.6 and 1.7) who underwent simultaneous CAS-Kit hydraulic TSFE and PRAMA tissue-level implant placement. Sequential saline injection (0.5cc increments, site 1.6 temporarily sealed during 1.7 injection) elevated the Schneiderian membrane to achieve 14.1mm and 11.4mm bone gain respectively. Final torque of ~30 Ncm was attributed to favorable sinus floor anatomy, under-preparation of implant site, and tapered implant macro-design. Valsalva testing confirmed membrane integrity. At 3-year follow-up, bone height was radiographically stable with no complications. The case establishes a proof-of-concept that CAS-Kit TSFE can succeed below the conventional 3mm RBH minimum when anatomical conditions are favorable.

## Key Contributions

- **Proof-of-concept for RBH 2.5mm**: successful CAS-Kit TSFE below the widely accepted 3mm minimum — adds an extreme data point.
- **Dual-site sequential hydraulic injection protocol**: temporary sealing of first osteotomy essential to maintain pressure for second-site elevation.
- **30 Ncm torque mechanism**: three contributing factors — residual medial/distal bone anatomy, under-preparation, tapered self-tapping design.
- **Saline volume-elevation relationship**: each 0.5cc → ~3mm of membrane lift (consistent with Ro et al. clinical hydraulic pressure data cited by Lin 2025).
- 3-year stability documented (bone height) — longest follow-up in this CAS-Kit+PRAMA series.

## Methodology

- **Patient**: 68F, non-smoker, missing 1.6 and 1.7; no systemic disease.
- **RBH**: 3.6mm (1.6) and 2.5mm (1.7) by CBCT.
- **Implants**: PRAMA 3.8×10mm tissue-level, ≈30 Ncm torque.
- **Graft**: Bio-Oss 0.25–1mm, 0.5cc per site.
- **Hydraulic injection**: sterile saline in 0.5cc steps; total 2.0cc (both sites) + 1.0cc additional at 1.7; first site temporarily sealed.
- **Follow-up**: 3 years radiographic.

## Results

| Parameter | Site 1.6 | Site 1.7 |
|---|---|---|
| Preoperative RBH | 3.6 mm | 2.5 mm |
| Postoperative bone height | 17.8 mm | 13.8 mm |
| Bone gain | **14.1 mm** | **11.4 mm** |
| Insertion torque | ~30 Ncm | ~30 Ncm |
| Membrane perforation | None | None |
| Complications | None | None |
| 3-year stability | Stable | Stable |
