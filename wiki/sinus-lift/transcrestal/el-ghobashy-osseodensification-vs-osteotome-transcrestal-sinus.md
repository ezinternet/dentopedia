## Three-line Summary
RCT (n=11 patients, 12 implants, RBH 5–8 mm, 6-month follow-up) comparing osseodensification using Densah burs (OD) vs osteotome technique for transcrestal maxillary sinus floor elevation with simultaneous implant placement.

OD achieved significantly higher primary stability ISQ (66.17 vs 54.83, p=0.043) and significantly superior secondary stability at follow-up, with shorter operating time (p=0.002); no significant difference in postoperative pain or edema between techniques.

OD via Densah burs is preferable to osteotome for transcrestal sinus lift at RBH 5–8 mm, delivering higher primary stability critical for osseointegration with equivalent patient morbidity and faster surgery.

## 세줄요약
잔존골높이(RBH) 5–8 mm에서 Densah 골밀도화(OD) vs 오스테오톰 경치조골 상악동거상 동시 임플란트 식립을 비교한 RCT(11명, 12개 임플란트, 6개월).

OD가 1차 안정성 ISQ 유의하게 높음(66.17 vs 54.83, p=0.043), 2차 안정성도 우월, 수술 시간 단축(p=0.002); 술후 통증·부종에는 유의한 차이 없음.

RBH 5–8 mm 경치조골 상악동거상 시 Densah OD가 오스테오톰보다 높은 1차 안정성과 빠른 수술을 제공하며 환자 이환율은 동등하므로 OD를 우선 고려할 수 있다.

## Summary
Randomized clinical trial comparing osseodensification using Densah burs versus osteotome technique for transcrestal maxillary sinus floor elevation in cases with RBH 5–8mm. OD technique achieves significantly higher primary and secondary implant stability (ISQ) compared to osteotome, with shorter operating time. No difference in postoperative pain or edema.

## Key Contributions
- Direct RCT comparison: OD vs osteotome TSFE in RBH 5–8mm
- OD primary ISQ significantly higher (66.17 vs 54.83, p=0.043)
- OD secondary stability also significantly superior
- OD is faster technique (p=0.002) with equivalent patient comfort
