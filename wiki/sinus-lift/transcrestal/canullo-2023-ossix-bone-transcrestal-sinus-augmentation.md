## Three-line Summary

Prospective pilot study (Canullo et al., University of Genova/Milan, Clin Implant Dent Relat Res 25(5):974-983, 2023) evaluating **OSSIX™ Bone** (HA/GLYMATRIX cross-linked collagen sponge) as the sole graft material for transcrestal sinus floor augmentation (t-SFA) with simultaneous implant placement in 24 patients (RBH >4 mm).

ISQ improved from **62.19 ± 8.09 at placement to 76.91 ± 4.50 at 6 months**; graft height remained stable (7.24 mm immediately → 5.46 mm at 12 months) with time-series correlograms confirming no ongoing resorption; **zero residual graft detected** = complete ossification.

Within pilot-study limitations, OSSIX Bone confirmed as a "no-remnant" sinus graft with excellent stability profile and manageability; buccolingual sinus dimension correlates with graft volume at 1 year.

## 세줄요약

전향적 파일럿 (Canullo 외, 제노바/밀라노, CIDRR 2023) — OSSIX™ Bone(HA + 당가교 콜라겐 스펀지)을 치조정접근 상악동 거상 (TSFE) 단독 이식재로 사용. 잔존골 높이 >4mm, 24명(22명 완료), 1년 CBCT.

임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 62→77, 이식재 높이 7.24→5.46mm (**안정적**), 잔존 이식재 0% = 완전 골화. 협구개 폭이 이식재 부피와 유의 상관.

임상 함의: OSSIX Bone은 TSFE에서 "잔존재 없는" 완전 골화 달성 + 우수한 이식재 안정성 — GLYMATRIX 플랫폼의 상악동 적용 근거.

## Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| ISQ at placement | 62.19 ± 8.09 |
| ISQ at 6 months | **76.91 ± 4.50** |
| Graft height — immediate | 7.24 ± 1.94 mm |
| Graft height — 12 months | **5.46 ± 2.04 mm** |
| Graft volume | 1085.8 ± 733.4 mm³ (stable, time-series confirmed) |
| Residual graft | **0% (complete ossification)** |
| Chewing interference | 86% none |
| PIL → GH correlation | Significant (P=0.02 at 6mo, P=0.03 at 12mo) |

## Clinical Decision Point

> **OSSIX Bone for TSFE**: Consider when complete graft resorption (no remnants) is desired, RBH ≥4 mm, and simultaneous implant placement is planned. Buccolingual sinus width predicts achievable graft volume — measure pre-operatively. Pilot evidence only; no RCT vs control exists.

## Limitations

- No control group; pilot (n=22)
- RBH >4 mm only; severely atrophic maxillae not tested
- No histologic biopsy; CBCT endpoint only
- 12-month max follow-up; implant survival not reported
