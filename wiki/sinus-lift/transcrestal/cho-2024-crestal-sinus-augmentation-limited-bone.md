## Three-line Summary

Retrospective observational study (n=11, Seoul St. Mary's Hospital) evaluating crestal approach sinus augmentation using a specialized kit (Shinhung/Osstem) with xenograft (Bio-Oss) in patients with residual alveolar bone height (RBH) <3 mm — a population traditionally referred for lateral window surgery.

Vertical bone height increased from 2.1 ± 0.5 mm to 8.8 ± 0.9 mm (Δ6.7 mm, p<0.001); bucco-palatal width from 10.6 ± 2.9 mm to 12.2 ± 2.2 mm (p<0.01); no membrane perforations or significant complications during a staged protocol (mean 202 days healing before implant placement).

Demonstrates that transcrestal sinus augmentation (치조정 접근 상악동 거상, TSFE) can achieve clinically adequate bone height even with RBH <3 mm, challenging the traditional 5–8 mm minimum threshold; however, n=11 with no control group and no implant survival data restricts practice-changing conclusions.

## 세줄요약

후향적 관찰 연구 (n=11, 서울성모병원) — 잔존골 높이 (RBH) <3 mm (기존 적응증 하한 5–8 mm 미달) 환자에게 전용 치조정 접근 키트 (crestal approach kit)와 이종골 (Bio-Oss)을 이용한 치조정 접근 상악동 거상 (TSFE) 평가.

수직 골높이 2.1 ± 0.5 mm → 8.8 ± 0.9 mm (Δ6.7 mm, p<0.001), 협-구개 폭경 (Bucco-Palatal Width) +1.6 mm (p<0.01); 막 천공·합병증 없음; 2단계 프로토콜 (평균 202일 치유 후 임플란트 식립).

잔존골 <3 mm에서도 TSFE로 충분한 골높이 달성 가능 — 기존 적응증 확장 가능성 시사; 단, 소규모(n=11), 대조군 없음, 임플란트 생존율 데이터 부재로 임상적 실천 변경 근거로는 제한적.

## Summary

This retrospective observational study from Seoul St. Mary's Hospital, Korea evaluated the use of a specialized crestal approach kit for maxillary sinus floor elevation in 11 patients (9M, 2F; mean age 63.6 ± 10.9 years) with residual alveolar bone height <3 mm — a clinical scenario traditionally managed by lateral window sinus surgery. The staged protocol used Bio-Oss xenograft with collagen membrane, with implant placement deferred until approximately 202 days post-augmentation.

Key radiographic findings via CBCT (콘빔 CT, Cone-Beam Computed Tomography):

| Dimension | Pre-op | Post-op | Δ | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical bone height | 2.1 ± 0.5 mm | 8.8 ± 0.9 mm | +6.7 mm | <0.001 |
| Bucco-palatal width | 10.6 ± 2.9 mm | 12.2 ± 2.2 mm | +1.6 mm | <0.01 |
| Mesio-distal width | 9.9 ± 1.2 mm | 10.2 ± 1.2 mm | +0.3 mm | NS |

No membrane perforations (슈나이더막, Schneiderian membrane) and no significant postoperative complications were recorded. The Wilcoxon signed-rank test was used for statistical analysis given the small sample size.

## Key Contributions

- Extends the transcrestal sinus augmentation (TSFE) indication to RBH <3 mm, which is below the conventional 4–5 mm minimum commonly cited in the literature
- Achieves a mean post-operative vertical bone height of 8.8 mm from a baseline of 2.1 mm — a gain sufficient for implant placement
- Reports zero membrane perforation rate despite extremely limited residual bone, suggesting the specialized crestal kit design may allow safer elevation under constrained anatomical conditions
- Supports a staged rather than simultaneous approach for very low-RBH cases

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective observational cohort (single center, single surgeon)
- **Inclusion**: Patients requiring posterior maxillary implants with CBCT-confirmed RBH <3 mm
- **Kit**: Shinhung/Osstem crestal approach sinus kit (hydraulic/mechanical membrane elevation)
- **Graft**: Xenograft (Bio-Oss, deproteinized bovine bone mineral) + collagen membrane
- **Protocol**: Staged — sinus augmentation first; implant placement after mean 202.3 ± 37.7 days healing
- **Imaging**: CBCT pre-operatively and post-augmentation (mean 202 days post-op)
- **Statistics**: Wilcoxon signed-rank test (paired, non-parametric)
