## Three-line Summary
PRISMA-ScR scoping review (Michigan group; 633 records screened, 37 included) mapping 25 years of transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE) clinical and radiographic evidence across 4 treatment modalities.

The review classifies TSFE into 4 modalities (osteotome plus 3 alternatives), and synthesizes primary outcomes of radiographic bone levels and implant failure beyond 5 years, with ISQ at placement and complication rates as secondary outcomes — no statistical pooling was performed due to study heterogeneity.

This scoping review provides the most comprehensive landscape map of TSFE evolution; for site-specific decisions, it should be paired with modality-specific meta-analyses and long-term stability data.

## 세줄요약
Michigan 그룹이 수행한 25년간의 경치조골 상악동거상술(TSFE)에 대한 PRISMA-ScR 범위 고찰(633편 검색 → 37편 포함, 4가지 술식 분류).

TSFE를 오스테오톰 + 3가지 대안(골밀도화, 풍선, 유압 등) 4개 술식으로 분류하고, 5년 이상 방사선학적 골수준·임플란트 실패율을 1차 결과로, ISQ와 합병증을 2차 결과로 종합; 이질성으로 인해 통계적 통합 불가.

이 범위 고찰은 TSFE 발전 전반을 가장 포괄적으로 정리한 지형도 역할을 하며, 술식 선택에는 술식별 메타분석 및 장기 안정성 데이터와 함께 활용해야 한다.

## Summary
PRISMA-ScR scoping review of 25-year TSFE literature. 633 records → 37 included. Identifies **4 treatment modalities** (osteotome + 3 alternatives). Primary endpoints: radiographic bone levels + implant failure >5 yr; secondary: ISQ at placement + complications.

## TSFE Modality Landscape
1. Traditional osteotome (control reference)
2. + 3 alternative modalities (osseodensification, balloon, hydraulic, etc. — see paper for full taxonomy)

## Why This Paper Matters
- **Synthesizes 25 years of TSFE evolution** — useful map of what's been studied
- Primary outcome focus on **5-year failure** is unusually long for sinus lift reviews
- Includes ISQ as secondary outcome — bridges to RFA literature
- Same Michigan group as Stuhr 2025 (long-term review) → consistent framework
