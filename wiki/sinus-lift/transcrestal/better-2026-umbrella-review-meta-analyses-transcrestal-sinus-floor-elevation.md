## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 30 meta-analyses (2015–April 2026) on transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE): AMSTAR 2 confidence was MODERATE in 9, LOW in 11, CRITICALLY LOW in 10, and none HIGH; pool-level Corrected Covered Area (CCA) was 0.92% but 10.90% in the graftless osteotome cluster.

Implant survival was uniformly high (95–100%) across short implants vs sinus floor elevation, transcrestal vs lateral access, and transcrestal techniques, with no clear survival advantage for any technique, so survival alone cannot rank techniques.

Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV) was reported mainly with mallet-driven osteotome procedures, but events were few and reporting inconsistent, so this is a suggestive signal, not causal proof; the review was not prospectively registered.

## 세줄요약

경치조골 상악동 거상 메타분석 30편(2015~2026.4)을 대상으로 한 우산 리뷰 (umbrella review): AMSTAR 2 신뢰도는 중간 9편·낮음 11편·매우 낮음 10편, 높음 0편이며, 전체 중복도 (Corrected Covered Area, CCA)는 0.92%이나 무이식 골내세공기 (osteotome) 군집에서는 10.90%.

임플란트 생존율은 단축 임플란트 대 상악동 거상, 경치조골 대 측방 접근, 경치조골 기법 간 비교 모두에서 95–100%로 높고 어떤 기법도 생존율 우위가 없어, 생존율만으로는 기법 순위를 매길 수 없음.

양성 돌발성 체위성 현훈 (Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo, BPPV)은 주로 망치 타격식 골내세공기 술식에서 보고되었으나 사건 수가 적고 보고가 일관되지 않아 인과가 아닌 시사적 신호이며, 본 리뷰는 사전 등록되지 않았음.

## Summary

Umbrella review of 30 meta-analyses on transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE), 2015–April 2026, organised around three separate decisions: (1) short implants vs sinus floor elevation, (2) transcrestal vs lateral access, (3) technique choice within TSFE. No new pooled estimate was computed (narrative synthesis to avoid double-counting primary studies). **No included review reached HIGH AMSTAR 2 confidence.** The authors conclude the meta-analytic evidence supports high implant survival across main options but not a simple ranking of techniques.

## Methodology

AMSTAR 2 with verbatim per-item evidence; Corrected Covered Area at pool and cluster level; impact vs non-impact biomechanical classes used as interpretive framework only. Not prospectively registered (protocol finalised after screening began); English only; extraction verified by a second reviewer but not fully duplicated.

## Results

| Domain | Finding |
|---|---|
| AMSTAR 2 | 0 HIGH / 9 MODERATE / 11 LOW / 10 CRITICALLY LOW |
| Overlap (CCA) | pool 0.92% (slight); graftless osteotome cluster 10.90% (high); short-implant vs SFE cluster 6.8% (moderate) |
| Survival | 95–100% in all 30 MAs; graftless TSFE 97% (1034 implants), grafted vs graftless RR 0.55 (95% CI 0.26–1.19); short implants vs SFE RR 0.97–1.08, all non-significant |
| Membrane perforation (11/30 MAs) | transcrestal vs lateral RR 0.71 at RBH ≤6 mm (CI includes 1); network MA SUCRA 0.94 TSFE-reamer vs 0.07 lateral-window-rotary; non-grafted osteotome 3.08% |
| BPPV (4 MAs) | concentrated in mallet-driven osteotome techniques; Kadkhodazadeh 2024: 4 of 17 RCTs (3 osteotome); Starch-Jensen 2025: 0 vs 1–5 cases (p = 0.002); Cobo-Vázquez 2025: 1/24 (4.17%) osteotome only, overall complications 2.78% vs 14.32% |
| PROMs | one dedicated MA (LOW); sparse; less invasive procedures generally favoured |

Graftless-osteotome and short-implant equivalence conclusions rest on a dense, repeatedly re-analysed primary-study base (notably Nedir 2013/2016, Si 2013/2016), so a single new high-quality trial could shift them.

## Clinical Insights

- Survival does not discriminate between TSFE techniques — choose the least invasive option that fits the anatomy and treatment goal.
- The osteotome-associated BPPV/discomfort signal is worth discussing with patients, but rests on few events, varying definitions and LOW/CRITICALLY LOW reviews; impact vs non-impact mechanics is plausible context, not a finding of this review.
- The osseodensification ISQ and complication advantages reported by Starch-Jensen 2025 come from a review this umbrella re-rated to LOW confidence — treat them as promising, not settled.
