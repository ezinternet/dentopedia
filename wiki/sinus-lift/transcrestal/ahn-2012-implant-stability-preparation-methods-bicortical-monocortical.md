## Three-line Summary

In vitro 2×3 factorial design, 60 implants (4.0×10 mm) in polyurethane blocks; cortical condition (monocortical vs bicortical) × preparation method (standard, underpreparation, osteotome); IT, ISQ (RFA), and removal torque measured.
Preparation method significantly affected all stability metrics; cortical location affected only removal torque (p<0.001); significant interaction (p=0.045): bicortical > monocortical for ISQ only with standard preparation (p=0.039); no location effect on ISQ with underpreparation or osteotome.
To maximise the bicortical ISQ benefit, standard preparation is required; osteotome-achieved bicortical fixation does not produce ISQ gains over monocortical, making drill-based bicortical protocols preferable.

## 세줄요약

체외 2×3 요인설계, 60개 임플란트(4.0×10 mm), 폴리우레탄; 피질골 조건(단겹질/쌍겹질) × 준비방식(표준/과소준비/오스테오톰); IT·ISQ·제거토크 측정.
준비방식이 모든 안정성 지표에 유의; 피질골 위치는 제거토크에만 유의(p<0.001); 표준 준비에서만 쌍겹질 > 단겹질 ISQ(p=0.039).

## Summary

Korean in vitro study from Kyung Hee University examining how the interaction between cortical condition and preparation method affects primary stability in a posterior maxilla analog. The key novel finding is the significant interaction: bicortical fixation only produces a measurable ISQ advantage over monocortical when combined with standard preparation (drill to manufacturer's specifications). When underpreparation or the osteotome technique is used, the bicortical advantage disappears — ISQ becomes equivalent across cortical conditions. Removal torque, however, was significantly higher in bicortical blocks regardless of preparation method, suggesting that bicortical engagement aids osseointegration quality (removal torque) more than initial resonance stability (ISQ). The correlations among IT, ISQ, and RT were moderate (r=0.48–0.53), consistent with Han 2016's finding (r=0.58).

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates preparation × cortical condition interaction — a finding absent from Han 2016 (single protocol only)
- Bicortical ISQ advantage is preparation-specific: standard drill protocol required
- Osteotome technique reduces ISQ in both cortical conditions — clinically relevant given OSFE popularity
- Removal torque bicortical advantage is preparation-independent — validates secondary stability benefit
- Confirms moderate IT–ISQ correlation (r=0.529) consistent across studies

## Results

| Factor | Effect on ISQ | p-value |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation method | Significant | <0.05 |
| Cortical location (mono vs bi) | Only on removal torque | <0.001 |
| Interaction (location × prep) | Significant | 0.045 |
| Standard prep: bicortical vs monocortical | **Bicortical > monocortical** | **0.039** |
| Underprepared: bicortical vs monocortical | No difference | 1.00 |
| Osteotome: bicortical vs monocortical | No difference | 1.00 |

Pearson correlations: IT–ISQ r=0.529, IT–RT r=0.517, RT–ISQ r=0.481 (all p<0.001)
