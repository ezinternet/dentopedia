## Three-line Summary

A CBCT study of 148 patients found the bony canal of the alveolar antral artery (AAA) in 57.0% of cases, with higher canal width in men and a trend toward greater AAA presence in older patients. After tooth loss the distance from the sinus floor to the AAA increases while the distance from the hard palate level stays constant. Practical recommendations include osteotomy shifting, piezo surgery for intrasinus AAA, double-window for intrabony AAA, and ligature for canals >3 mm.

## 세줄요약

148명 CBCT 환자를 분석한 연구로, 상악동 측벽의 치조동맥관(alveolar antral artery, AAA)은 57.0%에서 발견되었고 남성에서 관경이 유의하게 컸으며 고령 환자에서 더 흔한 경향을 보였다. 치아 상실 후 상악동저와 AAA 간 거리는 증가하나 경구개 수준과의 거리는 일정했다. 임상적으로 관경 >3 mm 출혈엔 결찰, 관내형엔 이중창, 동굴내형엔 피에조 수술 등 관리법을 제시한다.

## Summary

This cross-sectional CBCT study (148 patients) characterizes the intrabony alveolar antral artery (AAA), an anastomosis between the posterior superior alveolar artery and the infraorbital artery that complicates lateral sinus lift. It reports presence, canal dimensions, distances to sinus floor and hard palate, and clinical management recommendations by AAA type and diameter.

## Key Contributions

- AAA bony canal present in 57.0% (men 65.9%, women 52.5%); bilateral in 52.8% when imaging allows.
- Mean canal height/width 0.9 mm; canal width significantly larger in men (p=0.015).
- Age trend toward greater presence (50% <20 yrs, 58.8% 21–40 yrs, 63.6% >40 yrs; p=0.064, not significant).
- Hard palate level as stable reference: M1 2.6 mm, P2 3.2 mm, P1 7.7 mm above hard palate.
- Sinus floor distances: P1 6.4, P2 7.3, M1 8.4, M2 8.8 mm.
- Tooth loss increases sinus floor–AAA distance (p=0.067); hard palate distance constant.

## Methodology

- Observational CBCT study; 251 scans evaluated, 103 excluded, 148 valid.
- Measurements per Chan et al. 2014 sinus classification; statistics: chi-square/Fisher, Mann-Whitney U, Shapiro-Wilk; α=0.05.
