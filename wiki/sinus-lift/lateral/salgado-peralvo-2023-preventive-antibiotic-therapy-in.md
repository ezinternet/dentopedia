## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 12 studies (search 2006–2021) evaluating whether preventive antibiotic therapy reduces sinus graft infection or dental implant failure after maxillary sinus elevation surgery; descriptive synthesis only (insufficient data for meta-analysis).

The only retrospective study comparing antibiotic vs no antibiotic showed no significant implant failure difference; the only RCT comparing duration (surgery day vs 7 additional postoperative days) found no significant difference in sinus infection rates.

Insufficient evidence to support or oppose routine antibiotic prophylaxis in sinus elevation, or to recommend any protocol as superior.

## 세줄요약

상악동 거상(Maxillary Sinus Elevation) 예방적 항생제 SR(12편 임상연구, 검색 2006–2021): 동 이식 감염 및 임플란트 실패 감소 여부 평가; 메타분석 불가.

항생제 사용 vs 미사용 비교(1편 후향적 연구): 임플란트 실패 유의 차이 없음; 항생제 기간 비교(수술 당일 vs 7일 추가)(1편 RCT): 동 감염률 군 간 유의 차이 없음.

상악동 거상 시 예방적 항생제 사용·미사용 지지 근거 모두 불충분 — 어떤 프로토콜도 우월성 권고 불가.

## Summary

Salgado-Peralvo et al. (2023) conducted a systematic review of studies examining whether preventive antibiotic therapy reduces sinus graft infection or implant failure following maxillary sinus elevation surgery. Despite searching 5 databases from December 2006 to December 2021, only 12 studies met inclusion criteria, and data were too heterogeneous for meta-analysis. Among the 12 studies, only 1 retrospective study directly compared antibiotic use vs no antibiotics — it showed no statistically significant difference in implant failure rate and provided no data on sinus infection. Only 1 RCT compared antibiotic duration (surgery day only vs 7 additional postoperative days), finding no significant difference in sinus infection rate between protocols. The authors conclude there is insufficient evidence to recommend for or against routine antibiotic prophylaxis in sinus elevation, nor to identify an optimal protocol.

## Key Contributions
- First SR specifically focused on antibiotic prophylaxis for sinus elevation procedures
- Covers both primary outcomes: sinus graft infection AND implant failure
- Conclusion: cannot recommend routine use or any specific protocol
- Identifies critical evidence gap: no adequately powered RCT vs placebo
- Aligns with broader antimicrobial stewardship trend in implant dentistry

## Methodology
- Databases: MEDLINE (PubMed), Web of Science, Scopus, LILACS, OpenGrey
- Search: December 2006 – December 2021
- Inclusion: prospective and retrospective comparative studies, ≥50 patients, English
- Exclusion: animal studies, SRs/MAs, case reports, letters
- 12 studies included; descriptive synthesis only

## Results
| Comparison | Studies | Key Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Antibiotic vs no antibiotic | 1 retrospective study | No significant difference in implant failure; sinus infection not reported |
| Short vs long antibiotic duration | 1 RCT | No significant difference in sinus infection rate |

- Overall: insufficient evidence for any recommendation regarding routine prophylaxis or optimal protocol
