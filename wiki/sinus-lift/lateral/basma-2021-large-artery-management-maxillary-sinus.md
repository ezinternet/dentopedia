## Three-line Summary

Case report of lateral window SFE in a 58-year-old female with CT-identified large PSA branch within the lateral sinus wall bony canal, managed with piezoelectric device.
Artery was elevated en bloc with the Schneiderian membrane without hemorrhage; postoperative healing was uneventful.
Piezoelectric membrane elevation is a viable technique to avoid PSA branch laceration when a large vessel is identified preoperatively on CT.

## 세줄요약

CT에서 측벽 골관 내 대직경 후상치조동맥 (Posterior Superior Alveolar Artery, PSAA) 분지가 확인된 58세 여성의 측방 상악동 골이식 증례보고.
피에조전기 기구 (piezoelectric device) 로 슈나이더막 (Schneiderian membrane) 과 함께 혈관을 출혈 없이 거상; 술후 합병증 없음.

## Summary

This case report describes the management of a large posterior superior alveolar artery (PSA) branch encountered during maxillary sinus floor augmentation. A healthy 58-year-old female with a planned implant-supported prosthesis had a preoperative CT revealing a bony canal with a large PSA branch in the lateral sinus wall. Rather than risking arterial laceration, the surgeon used a piezoelectric device to elevate the artery intact en bloc with the Schneiderian membrane throughout the entire procedure. No hemorrhage occurred and postoperative healing was uneventful. This approach illustrates that when large PSA branches are identified preoperatively, planned piezoelectric elevation with membrane can convert a potential hemorrhagic complication into a non-event.

## Key Contributions

- **Clinical proof of concept**: Intact PSA branch displacement with Schneiderian membrane is feasible with piezoelectric surgery
- **Management algorithm**: Preoperative CT identification → plan "elevation with membrane" strategy → no cauterization/ligation required
- **Piezoelectric selectivity**: Ultrasonic device damages bone but not soft tissue/vessels, enabling atraumatic vessel elevation
- **Strategy shift**: Large-vessel preoperative detection changes management from "control hemorrhage" to "prevent encounter"

## Methodology

- **Design**: Case report
- **Patient**: 58-year-old healthy female, planned for implant-supported fixed prosthesis
- **Preoperative**: CT identified bony canal with large PSA branch on lateral wall
- **Surgical technique**: Lateral window SFE using piezoelectric device system
- **Key maneuver**: Elevation of PSA branch en bloc with Schneiderian membrane throughout procedure
- **Outcome metric**: Presence/absence of hemorrhage, postoperative healing

## Results

- **Intraoperative hemorrhage**: None
- **Membrane perforation**: None
- **Postoperative healing**: Uneventful
- **Mechanism**: Piezoelectric ultrasonic cutting frequency selectively disrupts calcified tissue; soft tissue/vessels remain intact under the instrument
