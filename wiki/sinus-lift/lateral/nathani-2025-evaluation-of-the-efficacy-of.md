## Three-line Summary

Single-center prospective single-arm study (12 enrolled, 10 analysed at 6 months) of lateral-window direct sinus augmentation in ≤5 mm residual-height sinuses using 1:1 local autogenous bone + biphasic calcium phosphate (30% HA / 70% β-TCP) with piezosurgical elevation and a PRF membrane.
At 6 months subantral height rose from 4.09 ± 0.57 mm to 12.62 ± 0.99 mm (gain 8.53 ± 1.07 mm, p < 0.001); biopsy histomorphometry showed 48.08 ± 2.41% new bone, 27.25 ± 3.18% soft tissue and 24.36 ± 2.91% residual graft, with no membrane perforation.
The 1:1 autograft + 30/70 BCP mix gives predictable height gain and favorable new bone, but the single-arm n = 10, 6-month horizon and unrecorded implant torque preclude causal or long-term claims.

## Summary

The question is what actually forms when a 1:1 autogenous-bone : biphasic-ceramic graft is packed into a ≤5 mm residual-height sinus through a lateral window. This prospective single-arm study (12 enrolled, 10 analysed) harvested 6-month trephine biopsies and paired them with CBCT height measurements. Subantral height nearly tripled (4.09 → 12.62 mm; +8.53 mm), and the biopsy tissue budget was 48% new bone / 27% soft tissue / 24% residual graft — a higher new-bone fraction than the allograft-based 30/70 HA/β-TCP series in [[sinus-lift/lateral/nguyen-2026-histological-and-histomorphometric-assessment-of]] (31.28%), though the two are not randomized against each other and differ in graft component and residual-height entry criteria. Piezosurgical elevation plus a prophylactic PRF membrane produced zero perforations, and healing was uneventful with only mild, self-resolving pain/swelling. The result is a clean demonstration that this specific graft recipe works in a tight residual-height scenario, not proof it beats a comparator.

## Key Contributions

- Direct 1:1 autograft + 30/70 BCP clinical, radiographic and histologic evidence at ≤5 mm residual height — a ratio seldom isolated.
- Complete 6-month tissue budget: NB 48.08%, soft tissue 27.25%, residual graft 24.36%, with favorable osteoblast/osteocyte/angiogenesis findings.
- Zero membrane perforation using piezosurgery + PRF membrane sealing in a small atrophic series.
- Supplies a real clinical arm for the ≤6 mm RBH material question that [[sinus-lift/lateral/somngam-2025-new-bone-formation-biphasic-calcium]] ranks only indirectly.

## Methodology

- Single-center, prospective, interventional, single-arm; 6-month follow-up.
- 45 screened → 12 enrolled (18–55 y, <5 mm CBCT residual bone, non-smokers, no systemic meds) → 2 dropouts → 10 analysed.
- Graft: local autogenous bone (bone scraper) mixed 1:1 with HA/β-TCP 30/70 (Osteon II, Dentium; 1.0–2.0 mm particles).
- Surgery: piezosurgical lateral window (Tatum in-fracture), 360° elevation, Valsalva check, PRF membrane under and over the window, 4-0 sutures.
- Assessment: CBCT height (baseline, 6 mo); 4-mm trephine biopsy at 6 mo (decalcified H&E, GIMP morphometry); VAS pain/swelling days 1/3/5/7.

## Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Subantral height baseline → 6 mo | 4.09 ± 0.57 → 12.62 ± 0.99 mm |
| Height gain | 8.53 ± 1.07 mm (95% CI 7.76–9.30; p < 0.001) |
| New bone (6 mo) | 48.08 ± 2.41% |
| Soft (fibrous) tissue | 27.25 ± 3.18% |
| Residual graft | 24.36 ± 2.91% |
| Membrane perforation | 0 / 10 |
| Pain / swelling | Mild (VAS 1–3), resolved by day 7 |

- Osteoblasts present in all sections (+ 30%, ++ 70%); osteocytes ++ 80% / +++ 20%; capillary angiogenesis; minimal inflammatory cells.
- Implants placed after biopsy "gained acceptable torque," but torque/ISQ was not a recorded parameter.

## Limitations

- Single-arm, no comparator — the 1:1 ratio's incremental value over either component is unknown.
- Final n = 10 (2 of 12 lost), single center, 6-month horizon only.
- Residual graft at 6 months is a snapshot, not long-term resorption.
- Implant stability was observed qualitatively but not measured.
- Not randomized against the allograft-based 30/70 BCP + PRF cohort it is compared to in prose.
