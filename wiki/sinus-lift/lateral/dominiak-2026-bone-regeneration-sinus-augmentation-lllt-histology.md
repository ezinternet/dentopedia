## Three-line Summary

Exploratory 2×2 factorial randomized clinical trial (single centre, Wrocław Medical University, Poland, 2023–2024; 20 patients, four groups of n=5) of lateral-window maxillary sinus augmentation with allogeneic (0.5 mm cortico-cancellous) vs xenogeneic (0.25–1.0 mm spongious) graft, each with or without adjunctive LLLT (635 nm, 6 J/cm², 1 min, days 0/3/7), assessed by CBCT bone gain and 6-month trephine-biopsy histology.

Bone gain was achieved in every site (implant placement in all 20): 7.53 ± 3.32 mm with LLLT vs 7.02 ± 2.00 mm without (NS), and 7.68 ± 2.77 mm with allograft vs 6.87 ± 2.67 mm with xenograft (NS); trabecular bone was present in all samples, and the only significant histological difference was more frequent mild inflammatory cell infiltrates in LLLT sites (60% vs 10%, p = 0.029).

Both graft classes were biocompatible and effective at 6 months, but adjunctive 635 nm LLLT produced no measurable clinical or radiological benefit in this underpowered exploratory cohort — and the trial reports semiquantitative histology scores rather than histomorphometry, no interaction analysis, and no sample-size calculation.

## Summary

This single-centre Polish trial reports the predefined secondary endpoints — 6-month histology and CBCT bone gain — of a randomized study whose primary outcome was anti-HLA antibody formation after bone regeneration. Twenty patients with residual bone height below 5 mm were allocated 1:1:1:1 by sealed opaque envelopes to a 2 × 2 factorial design crossing graft class (allogeneic cortico-cancellous granules, 0.5 mm, Biobank vs xenogeneic spongious granules, 0.25–1.0 mm, Geistlich) with adjunctive low-level laser therapy (diode 635 nm, 0.5 cm² spot, 6 J/cm² for 1 min continuous, immediately post-surgery and on days 3 and 7). All windows were closed over a resorbable collagen membrane, and at six months a trephine biopsy was taken at implant placement, decalcified, H&E-stained and scored semiquantitatively by two blinded readers for inflammatory cell infiltrates, trabecular bone-to-connective tissue ratio and bone morphology; bone gain was CBCT postoperative height minus residual height.

Every site gained bone and received an implant. Bone gain was 7.53 ± 3.32 mm with LLLT vs 7.02 ± 2.00 mm without (NS) and 7.68 ± 2.77 mm with allograft vs 6.87 ± 2.67 mm with xenograft (NS), from comparable baseline residual heights. Bone tissue appeared in all 20 samples and TB/CT and bone-morphology scores did not differ; the single significant finding was more frequent mild inflammatory infiltrates in LLLT-treated sites (60% vs 10%, p = 0.029), which the authors read as altered healing kinetics rather than harmful inflammation, since no sample exceeded a score of 2 and neither tissue organization nor bone gain was worse. They attribute the missing LLLT benefit to the sinus's already favourable vascular/osteoconductive environment and to the shallow penetration of 635 nm red light into the grafted compartment, and label the study exploratory: n = 5 per cell, no sample-size calculation, no interaction analysis, and ordinal histology instead of histomorphometry.

## Key Contributions

- **First page in the wiki testing adjunctive LLLT in lateral sinus augmentation** — the held sinus evidence covers graft material, membranes, PRF/PRP and perforation risk, but not photobiomodulation at all.
- **Randomized, allocation-concealed 2 × 2 design** that varies graft class and LLLT simultaneously (four cells of n = 5), so both factors are prospectively controlled rather than compared across studies.
- **Paired histology and CBCT in the same patients**: confirms that radiographically measured bone gain corresponds to genuine trabecular bone in all 20 samples, not fibrous scar — the check that radiographic sinus-lift literature usually lacks.
- **Full LLLT dosimetry reported** (wavelength, spot size, energy density, exposure time, mode, session schedule) — the parameter set that makes an LLLT null result interpretable rather than uninformative.
- **A clearly non-confirmatory harm signal**: p = 0.029 for more frequent mild infiltrates in irradiated sites, reported without being spun into either "LLLT is safe" or "LLLT is harmful", and paired with an explicit penetration-depth hypothesis.
- **Transparent self-limitation**: the authors state plainly that no sample-size calculation was possible (biopsy ethics) and that the study is hypothesis-generating.

## Methodology

- **Design**: single-centre, parallel-group, exploratory 2 × 2 factorial RCT (CONSORT); secondary outcomes of NCT07474857; ethics KB 10/2023N (Wrocław Medical University); 2023–2024.
- **Participants**: 20 consecutively recruited adults needing lateral-window sinus augmentation before implant placement; inclusion RBH < 5 mm, ridge width > 7 mm, ≥ 2 mm keratinized tissue; exclusions included plaque index > 20%, active sinus infection/large cysts, prior sinus surgery, immunosuppression, head/neck radiotherapy.
- **Randomization**: simple randomization by an investigator not operating (A.B.); sealed opaque envelopes opened after enrolment; 1:1:1:1.
- **Groups**: G1 xenograft, G2 xenograft + LLLT, G3 allograft, G4 allograft + LLLT (n = 5 each).
- **Surgery**: 4% articaine 1:200,000; full-thickness flap with distal release; 8 mm diamond-drill antrostomy (DASK® kit, Dentium) under saline; membrane elevation; graft; Bio-Gide® collagen membrane; 5/0 nylon. Graft volume chosen intraoperatively and not measured.
- **LLLT protocol**: diode laser Lasotronix®, **635 nm**, beam 0.5 cm², **6 J/cm²**, **1 min continuous**, intraoral over the osteotomy and overlying soft tissue, at **day 0, day 3 and day 7** (3 sessions).
- **Radiology**: CBCT (Planmeca Viso G7, FOV 300 × 200 mm; Romexis 6.0) at baseline and 6 months; RBH and postoperative height at the planned implant site; BG = difference; blinded assessor.
- **Histology**: 6-month trephine biopsy (Khoury–Meisinger) at implant placement; 4% formalin, 10% EDTA decalcification, paraffin, 4 µm sections, H&E; Olympus BX41 at 20×; two blinded examiners with consensus.
- **Outcome scoring** (ordinal, no histomorphometry): ICI 0–3 (<1/<3/<5/<8 per HPF); TB/CT 1–3 (trabecular predominates → connective tissue predominates); BM 1–3 (large bone areas → numerous small islands).
- **Statistics**: STATISTICA 13.3; Mann–Whitney U and independence tests; α = 0.05; main-factor comparisons only, **no interaction or four-group analysis**; no sample-size calculation.

## Results

**Course and demographics**

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Completion | all 20 patients completed; no major complications; implant placed in every case |
| Sex distribution | G1, G3 male-predominant; G2, G4 female-predominant |
| Mean age | ~45 y (G1, G2); ~52 y (G3); slightly > 59 y (G4); NS |

**Baseline residual bone height (mm, mean ± SD)**

| Comparison | Arm 1 | Arm 2 | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| LLLT vs no LLLT | 2.80 ± 1.25 | 2.31 ± 1.76 | NS |
| Allograft vs xenograft | 2.89 ± 1.76 | 2.00 ± 1.21 | NS |

**CBCT bone gain at 6 months (mm, mean ± SD)**

| Comparison | Arm 1 | Arm 2 | Difference | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LLLT vs no LLLT | 7.53 ± 3.32 | 7.02 ± 2.00 | +0.51 mm (LLLT; wider SD) | NS |
| Allograft vs xenograft | 7.68 ± 2.77 | 6.87 ± 2.67 | +0.81 mm (allograft) | NS |

**Histology at 6 months**

| Parameter | Result | Statistics |
|---|---|---|
| Bone presence | bone tissue in 20/20 samples | — |
| Inflammatory cell infiltrates | scores mostly 0–1; none above 2; mild infiltrates in 60% of LLLT sites vs 10% of controls | **p = 0.029** |
| TB/CT ratio | most samples "comparable amounts" of trabecular bone and loose connective tissue | NS |
| Bone morphology | mixed large mineralized areas + small trabecular islands (intermediate-stage regeneration); qualitative more uniform trabeculation in LLLT (80% vs 70%) | NS |

**Authors' reading** — the infiltrate difference is interpreted as altered healing kinetics / ongoing remodeling at 6 months, not clinically relevant inflammation (no sample exceeded score 2 and neither organization nor bone gain was impaired). The absence of an LLLT benefit is attributed to (a) the sinus's intrinsically high vascularity and osteoconductivity blunting any additive effect, and (b) limited penetration of 635 nm visible red light into the deep grafted compartment, where near-infrared would reach further. Reported external benchmarks: Mazor et al. RBH ~2.9 ± 0.9 mm with 10.1 ± 0.9 mm bone gain; Tajima et al. 4.28 ± 1.00 → 11.8 ± 1.67 mm.

**How to read this page**: the trial is best used as (i) the wiki's only randomized LLLT-in-sinus datapoint, and (ii) a paired histology–CBCT confirmation that both graft classes yield real trabecular bone at 6 months. It should not be cited for an LLLT effect size in either direction — the design could not detect one (n = 5 per cell, no interaction term, ordinal outcomes).
