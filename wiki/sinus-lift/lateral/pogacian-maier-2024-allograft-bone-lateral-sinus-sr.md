## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA, STROBE-based risk of bias, RevMan random-effects) of 7 clinical studies (4 prospective, 3 retrospective; 238 patients) using allograft (FDBA, DFDBA) in lateral-window sinus floor elevation versus xenograft (DBB), alloplast (BCP) or autogenous bone; 321 records retrieved, 20 full texts assessed.

Pooled FDBA vs DBB mean bone volume at 6–9 months: WMD −0.17 [−0.69, 0.36], p = 0.53, I² = 81%; pooled implant survival FDBA vs autogenous bone at 6–12 months: RR 1.00 [0.96, 1.05], p = 1.00, I² = 0% — no detectable difference in either comparison.

Allograft is a usable sinus-graft option, but the evidence base is small, observational-heavy, short in follow-up and thin on histology and complications, so superiority over other graft classes cannot be concluded.

## Summary

This systematic review (PRISMA; 7 included clinical studies, 4 prospective and 3 retrospective; 238 patients) examined allograft bone (FDBA, DFDBA) used with a collagen membrane in lateral-window maxillary sinus floor elevation, compared with deproteinized bovine bone, biphasic calcium phosphate or autogenous bone. Two outcome pairs could be pooled with random-effects models: bone volume at 6–9 months (FDBA vs DBB: WMD −0.17 [−0.69, 0.36], p = 0.53, I² = 81%) and implant survival at 6–12 months (FDBA vs autogenous bone: RR 1.00 [0.96, 1.05], p = 1.00, I² = 0%). Neither comparison showed a difference, and histomorphometry (2 studies) could not be pooled. The authors conclude that allograft can be used in the lateral sinus lift, but the small number of studies, observational designs, short follow-up and sparse complication data prevent any claim of superiority over xenograft, alloplast or autogenous bone.

## Key Contributions

- Allograft-specific SR for the lateral window approach, restricted to head-to-head comparisons with DBB, BCP or autogenous bone.
- No significant difference for FDBA vs DBB bone volume or FDBA vs autogenous bone implant survival.
- Maps the evidence gap: very few RCTs, histology in 2 studies, inconsistent reporting of implant type, loading time, follow-up and complications.

## Methodology

PubMed, Web of Science and Scopus searched to October 2023 (321 records; 266 after de-duplication; 20 full texts; 7 included), plus grey literature and hand search; English only; kappa 0.95. Risk of bias via the STROBE checklist; analysis in RevMan 5.4 (RR with Mantel–Haenszel for implant survival, WMD with inverse variance for bone measures; I² for heterogeneity).
