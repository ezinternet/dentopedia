## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT study (n=30 sinuses: 15 with interfering septa vs 15 septa-free controls, all lateral-window SFE, imaged at T0/1 week/9 months) examining whether septa modulate post-operative Schneiderian membrane swelling and ostium/infundibulum obstruction over time.

Post-op mucosal swelling peaked at 1 week (mean height increase ~5.7 mm septa / ~7.1 mm controls), transiently obstructing 3/15 infundibula in the septa group vs 5/15 in controls; all obstructions and swelling fully resolved by 9 months with zero residual pathology; septa caused 4/15 perforations vs 0/15 in controls.

Transient 1-week ostium obstruction after lateral SFE is common and self-limiting; septa raise perforation risk (26.7%) but do not worsen ostium involvement from post-op edema.

## Summary

After lateral-window maxillary sinus floor elevation (SFE), the Schneiderian membrane undergoes a **transient "virtual" thickening** driven by post-operative clot and edema — not true mucosal disease. This retrospective CBCT study asked whether **interfering septa** modify that swelling and, critically, whether it obstructs the **maxillary sinus ostium (MSO) / infundibulum** drainage path over time.

Across 30 sinuses imaged pre-op (T0), at 1 week (T1w), and at 9 months (T9m), the swelling peaked at 1 week and **regressed to baseline by 9 months with no residual infundibular obstruction in either group**. Counter to the expectation that septa (which fragment the sinus and complicate elevation) would worsen ostium involvement, the **septa-free control group tended to swell more and obstructed more infundibula** (5 vs 3). The authors hypothesize that septa may physically limit the spread of edema toward the palatal wall and ostium. Septa remained a clear **perforation hazard** (4/15, 26.7% vs 0/15). No case produced clinical sinusitis symptoms.

Clinical takeaway: transient 1-week ostium/infundibulum obstruction after lateral SFE is common and self-limiting; the presence of septa raises perforation risk but does not, in this small series, increase the risk of ostium involvement from post-op edema.

## Key Contributions

- Documents for the first time whether **septa modulate ostium/infundibulum involvement** by post-SFE mucosal swelling over time.
- Establishes the **time course**: swelling peaks at 1 week, fully regresses by 9 months, with all infundibular obstructions resolving.
- Shows septa did **not** worsen ostium involvement — controls tended to swell more and obstruct more infundibula.
- Reaffirms septa as a **perforation risk** independent of ostium involvement (26.7% vs 0%).

## Methodology

- **Design**: secondary retrospective CBCT analysis of records from RCTs (University Corporation Rafael Núñez, Cartagena de Indias, Colombia; ethics # CURN-0002-).
- **Sample**: 88 sinuses / 75 patients screened → **15 sinuses with interfering septa** (12 patients, mean age 55.2 ± 9.4 y) + **15 randomly selected septa-free control sinuses** (15 patients, mean age 57.8 ± 8.1 y). All lateral-window SFE, residual crest ≤4 mm.
- **Grafts**: 10 sinuses/group OsteoBiol Gen-Os + OsteoBiol Evolution membrane; 5/group Cerabone + Collprotect membrane.
- **Imaging**: 3D Accuitomo 170 CBCT at T0 / T1w / T9m; measured in i-Dixel 2.0. Coronal-view measures — MSO-to-floor distance, MSO diameter, infundibulum length, infundibula out of patency, mucosa thickness, edema-to-ostium distance; lateral-view edema extension relative to base-of-nose axis.
- **Septa**: height, ostium-to-septum distance, Irinakis orientation classification.
- **Statistics**: Mann–Whitney (groups), Wilcoxon (periods, exploratory), SPSS v19.0.

## Results

| Parameter | Timepoint | Septa (n=15) | Control (n=15) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mucosa thickness | T0 | 1.1 ± 0.7 mm | 1.0 ± 0.7 mm |
| MSO diameter | T0 | 1.7 ± 0.4 mm | 2.1 ± 0.8 mm |
| Infundibulum length | T0 | 8.5 ± 1.3 mm | 9.2 ± 2.1 mm |
| Infundibula obstructed | T0 | 0 | 0 |
| Mucosa width | T1w | 6.7 ± 7.8 mm | 8.2 ± 7.0 mm |
| Mean mucosal height increase | T1w | ~5.7 mm | ~7.1 mm |
| MSO diameter | T1w | 1.0 ± 0.6 mm | 1.0 ± 0.8 mm |
| Edema-to-ostium distance | T1w | 11.8 ± 10.7 mm | 7.5 ± 8.8 mm (p=0.310) |
| **Infundibula obstructed** | **T1w** | **3/15 (20%)** | **5/15 (33.3%)** |
| Infundibula obstructed | T9m | 0 | 0 |

- **Septa baseline**: mean height 6.3 ± 2.4 mm (range 2.9–9.8), mainly molar region, 9.6 ± 4.9 mm distal to ostium. Irinakis: 11 Class I, 1 Class II, 3 Class IV. 10 single / 3 double / 2 triple septa.
- **1 week**: swelling peaks; edema sits ~4.3 mm closer to the ostium in controls; more infundibula obstructed in controls (5 vs 3). None significant.
- **9 months**: MSO diameter and septa-group mucosa normalized; control mucosa remained ~1.2 mm thicker than T0 (one asymptomatic case). Only significant septa-vs-control differences: mucosa thickness (p=0.028) and mucosa area (p=0.029), coronal view. **No infundibular obstruction; all sinuses pathology-free.**
- **Perforations**: 4/15 (26.7%) in septa group (≈1, 2, 3, 3×5 mm), managed with collagen membrane; 0/15 in controls.
- **Limitations**: 2D CBCT analysis, retrospective, small n, edema vs true-thickness ambiguity.
