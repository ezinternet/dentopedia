## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and individual-patient-data (IPD) plus aggregate-data meta-analysis (11 observational studies, 660 implants, 17 failures) of graftless lateral sinus floor elevation (SFE) with simultaneous implant placement; published in Journal of Cranio-Maxillofacial Surgery, September 2018.

Pooled aggregate survival 97.5% (95% CI 95.8–98.9%); bone height gain 5.7 mm (95% CI 4.78–6.56); MBL 1.2 mm; ISQ 70.5; membrane perforation 20.0%; sinus infection 3.0%; IPD 5-year survival 93.1% (mean survival time 6.7 years); longer implants (>13mm) had HR 0.64 (protective).

Graftless lateral SFE is safe and effective with high survival rates, especially for implants >13 mm in length — individual-patient data analysis confirms the technique's long-term viability while identifying implant length as the single most significant predictive factor for survival.

## Summary

This is the strongest quantitative evidence available for graftless lateral sinus floor elevation (blood clot only, no graft material) as of 2018. The study combined systematic review with both individual-patient data (IPD) and aggregate-data meta-analysis approaches to assess survival rates and secondary outcomes across 11 observational studies (660 implants, 17 failures).

**Methods:** Freeman-Tukey double-arcsine transformation stabilized proportions for pooling aggregate survival. Bone gain and MBL were meta-analyzed using change-from-baseline with sampling variance. IPD analysis used Kaplan-Meier for 5-year survival probability and Cox proportional hazards regression to identify covariates.

**Key aggregate results:**

| Outcome | Pooled | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| Survival rate | 97.5% | 95.8–98.9% |
| Bone height gain | 5.7 mm | 4.78–6.56 |
| MBL | 1.2 mm | 0.84–1.51 |
| ISQ at follow-up | 70.5 | 64.2–76.9 |
| Membrane perforation | 20.0% | 10.9–30.6 |
| Sinus infection | 3.0% | 0.5–6.8 |

**Individual-patient data (IPD) results:**
- 5-year survival: 93.1% (SE 2.67%)
- Mean survival time: 6.7 years (SE 0.08)
- Cox regression: longer implants (>13mm) HR = 0.64 (95% CI 0.41–0.99) — significantly protective

**Practical implication:** For graftless lateral SFE, selecting implants >13 mm is the single modifiable factor shown to improve survival. The technique is viable for posterior maxillary rehabilitation without the cost and morbidity of graft materials.

## Key Contributions

- First to apply both IPD and aggregate meta-analysis to graftless lateral SFE
- Establishes 97.5% aggregate and 93.1% 5-year IPD survival benchmarks
- Identifies implant length >13 mm as protective (HR 0.64) — actionable selection criterion
- Membrane perforation rate 20.0% is a critical complication to track (though not shown to significantly reduce survival when properly managed)
- ISQ of 70.5 confirms adequate primary stability is achievable without graft in lateral approach

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR + IPD and aggregate meta-analysis; observational studies only (no RCTs available)
- **n**: 11 studies; 660 implants; 17 failures
- **Inclusion**: Prospective and retrospective studies; graftless lateral SFE with simultaneous implant placement
- **Statistical methods**: Freeman-Tukey for survival pooling; changes-from-baseline for bone gain/MBL; Kaplan-Meier + Cox (IPD)
- **Secondary outcomes**: Bone gain, MBL, ISQ, membrane perforation, sinus infection

## Results

- Aggregate survival: 97.5%
- IPD 5-year survival: 93.1%
- Bone height gain: 5.7 mm
- MBL: 1.2 mm; ISQ: 70.5
- Membrane perforation: 20%; sinus infection: 3%
- >13mm implants: HR 0.64 (protective)
