## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=30, 15 per arm; mean age 46.33±7.25 years; Mashhad, Iran, 2021–2022) of lateral-window maxillary sinus floor augmentation with mixed mineralized/demineralized freeze-dried bone allograft, randomized to 0.2–0.4 mm absorbable collagen membrane coverage of the osteotomy window versus leaving it uncovered, with 3 mm trephine core histomorphometry at 6 months.

Newly formed bone area was significantly greater with membrane coverage (100.47±16.37 vs 85.27±12.37, p<0.001) and connective tissue area significantly smaller (41.20±12.82 vs 54.20±14.50, p=0.015), but native bone area was significantly greater in the uncovered group (100.27±11.45 vs 79.33±13.76, p<0.001) — the membrane shifted core composition rather than uniformly increasing all compartments.

Covering the lateral window with a collagen membrane is worth considering when the graft is allograft, but areas are reported in µm² without normalization, no post-implant follow-up was done, and the authors' "highly advocated" conclusion rests on 30 single-center patients.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 30명, 군당 15명, 평균 46.33±7.25세, 이란 마슈하드, 2021–2022): 동종골 (Allograft)인 광물화/탈회 동결건조골이식재 (Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft, FDBA / Demineralized Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft, DFDBA) 혼합 1000–2000 µm 이식재로 측방창 상악동거상술 (Lateral Window Sinus Floor Augmentation)을 시행하고, 0.2–0.4 mm 흡수성 콜라겐 차폐막 (Collagen Membrane) 피개 유무를 6개월 트레핀 (Trephine) 생검 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry)으로 비교.

차폐막 피개군에서 신생골 (Newly Formed Bone) 면적이 유의하게 컸고 (100.47±16.37 vs 85.27±12.37, p<0.001), 결합조직 (Connective Tissue) 면적은 유의하게 작았으며 (41.20±12.82 vs 54.20±14.50, p=0.015), 반대로 잔류골 (Native Bone) 면적은 비피개군이 유의하게 컸다 (100.27±11.45 vs 79.33±13.76, p<0.001) — 막은 코어 조성을 재분배했을 뿐 모든 구획을 일률적으로 늘리지는 않았다.

동종골을 사용하는 측창 상악동거상술에서는 콜라겐 막 피개를 고려할 만하나, 면적이 총 코어 면적으로 정규화되지 않은 µm² 절대값이고 임플란트 식립 후 추적이 없으며 단일기관 30명이라는 한계로 "적극 권장" 결론은 잠정적이다.

## Summary

This double-blind RCT randomized 30 healthy adults (15/15) needing posterior maxillary implants with 3–5 mm residual bone to lateral-window sinus floor augmentation with a mineralized + demineralized freeze-dried bone allograft (1000–2000 µm), with or without a 0.2–0.4 mm absorbable collagen membrane over the osteotomy window. At 6 months, a 3 mm trephine core taken immediately before implant placement was sectioned and measured in ImageJ. Membrane coverage produced significantly more newly formed bone (100.47±16.37 vs 85.27±12.37, p<0.001) and significantly less connective tissue (41.20±12.82 vs 54.20±14.50, p=0.015), while native bone area was significantly larger in the uncovered arm (100.27±11.45 vs 79.33±13.76, p<0.001); histology showed lamellar and woven new bone with no necrosis, foreign-body reaction or notable inflammation in any specimen. The authors conclude that collagen membrane coverage should be routine in the lateral window approach, but the absolute-area reporting, absence of post-implant outcomes, single-center n=30 and exclusion of perforations and septated sinuses keep the recommendation provisional.

## Key Contributions

- Isolates the membrane question in an allograft-only lateral window model — the authors note that no study in the Starch-Jensen 2019 SR+MA used allograft, so this is the first direct test of membrane coverage with FDBA/DFDBA.
- Shows the membrane effect is compositional: newly formed bone up ~18% relative and connective tissue down ~24% relative, but native bone down ~21% relative, so total core "bone" is not simply increased.
- Adds within-arm correlation structure: in uncovered sites connective tissue correlated inversely with native (r=−0.674, p=0.006) and new bone (r=−0.699, p=0.004) and native with new bone strongly positively (r=0.984, p<0.001); in membrane-covered sites only native vs new bone correlated, inversely (r=−0.515, p=0.050).
- Confirms a clean histologic safety profile for the absorbable membrane: no necrosis, no foreign-body reaction, no prominent inflammation at 6 months.

## Methodology

Double-blind RCT (block randomization by a blinded nurse, sequentially numbered opaque envelopes, concealed allocation and blinded histologic examiner/data analyst); CONSORT and Declaration of Helsinki; IRCT 20200125046247N1. Single center, Maxillofacial Surgery Department, Mashhad Dental School, October 2021–October 2022. Inclusion: healthy adults >18 y, ASA I–II, posterior edentulous maxilla, residual alveolar bone height 3–5 mm. Exclusion: contraindications to implant placement, sinus pathology, sinus septa, intraoperative Schneiderian membrane perforation, loss to follow-up. All surgeries by one team under local anesthesia via lateral window; graft = freeze-dried bone allograft (mixed mineralized/demineralized, 1000–2000 µm) loosely packed. **Control = collagen membrane** (0.2–0.4 mm absorbable, 15 patients); **test = no membrane** (intact-periosteum flap repositioned, 15 patients). Co-amoxiclav 625 mg TID × 7 days, Silk 3-0 closure, sutures out day 7. Outcomes: area of newly formed bone, native bone and connective tissue on H&E sections (10% formalin, 7% nitric acid decalcification, 5 µm sections, ImageJ); inflammation, necrosis and tissue quality by light microscopy. Statistics: independent/paired t-test, chi-square, Pearson correlation, α=0.05, Shapiro-Wilk normality (all normal).

## Results

| Outcome | With membrane (n=15) | Without membrane (n=15) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connective tissue area | 41.20 ± 12.82 (range 20–68) | 54.20 ± 14.50 (range 33–76) | 0.015 (T=2.59) |
| Native bone area | 79.33 ± 13.76 (range 58–108) | 100.27 ± 11.45 (range 84–117) | <0.001 (T=4.53) |
| Newly formed bone area | 100.47 ± 16.37 (range 60–123) | 85.27 ± 12.37 (range 64–104) | <0.001 (T=2.87) |
| Age | 46.07 ± 7.12 | 46.60 ± 7.62 | 0.845 |
| Sex distribution | 7 M / 8 F | 8 M / 7 F | 0.715 |
| Sex effect on any histomorphometric variable | — | — | no significant difference |

Correlations (Pearson): **no-membrane arm** — connective tissue vs native bone r=−0.674 (p=0.006), connective tissue vs new bone r=−0.699 (p=0.004), native vs new bone r=0.984 (p<0.001). **Membrane arm** — native vs new bone r=−0.515 (p=0.050) only significant; connective tissue vs native r=−0.098 (p=0.727), connective tissue vs new bone r=−0.346 (p=0.206).

Histology: newly formed trabeculae of mature lamellar plus unorganized woven bone in all specimens, larger osteocyte lacunae and higher osteocyte density in new bone; no prominent inflammation (scarce inflammatory cells at most), no necrosis, no foreign-body reaction in either arm.

Interpretive caveats: areas are printed as µm² with no total core area or percentage given, so the three compartments sum to 239.7 (uncovered) and 221.0 (membrane) and cannot be read as fractions of the core; group labels are inverted versus convention (control = with membrane); CBCT at 6 months was taken but graft volume was not reported as an outcome; the authors read the Starch-Jensen 2019 SR+MA as concordant, although that SR pooled no significant new-bone difference.
