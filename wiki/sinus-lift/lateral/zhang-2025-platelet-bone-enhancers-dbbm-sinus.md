## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA; 16 studies, 372 patients, 455 procedures) of platelet-derived bone enhancers (PRP, PRF, PRGF) as adjuncts to deproteinized bovine bone matrix (DBBM) in two-stage maxillary sinus floor elevation.

Bone enhancers significantly increased new bone formation (MD=5.92, 95%CI 2.17–9.67, p=0.002) and reduced residual graft volume (MD=−1.93, 95%CI −2.25 to −1.61, p<0.001), but had no significant effect on immediate implant stability (RFA) or implant survival rate (RR=1.03, p=0.252).

Subgroup analysis shows PRF/PRGF (not PRP) drive the residual-graft reduction, and only PRF significantly improves new bone formation on its own — clinical benefit is enhancer-type specific, not a class effect, and long-term implant survival data remain limited.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(PRISMA; 16개 연구, 372명, 455건 시술): 2단계 상악동거상술에서 탈단백우골기질(Deproteinized Bovine Bone Matrix, DBBM)에 혈소판 유래 골증강제(PRP·PRF·PRGF)를 병용한 효과 평가.

혈소판 증강제 병용 시 신생골 형성 유의 증가(MD=5.92, p=0.002), 잔류이식재량 유의 감소(MD=−1.93, p<0.001); 그러나 즉시 임플란트 안정성(공진주파수분석, Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA)·임플란트 생존율(RR=1.03, p=0.252)에는 유의차 없음.

하위군 분석상 잔류이식재 감소는 PRF·PRGF에서만 유의(PRP는 유의하지 않음)하고 신생골 형성 단독 유의 효과도 PRF에서만 확인됨 — 효과는 증강제 종류별로 다르며 계열 전체 효과가 아님; 장기 임플란트 생존 자료는 부족.

## Summary

This PRISMA-guided systematic review and meta-analysis pooled 16 studies (14 RCTs, 2 case-control; 372 patients, 455 procedures) evaluating platelet-rich plasma (PRP), platelet-rich fibrin (PRF), or plasma rich in growth factors (PRGF) as adjuncts to DBBM in two-stage maxillary sinus floor elevation. Across the pooled cohort, adding a platelet-derived enhancer significantly increased new bone formation (MD=5.92%, p=0.002) and significantly reduced residual graft volume (MD=−1.93, p<0.001), with no significant difference in graft resorption rate, fibrous tissue percentage, immediate implant stability (RFA), or implant survival rate. Subgroup analysis by enhancer type showed the new-bone-formation benefit is significant only for PRF, and the residual-graft reduction is significant only for PRF and PRGF — PRP alone showed no significant benefit on residual graft, echoing earlier autogenous-bone findings (Raghoebar 2005) that PRP does not reliably improve graft outcomes. Neither implant stability nor survival was affected by enhancer type, healing interval, or DBBM particle size in any subgroup.

## Key Contributions

- Largest pooled synthesis to date restricted specifically to two-stage sinus floor elevation with DBBM ± platelet-derived enhancer, and the first to subgroup by all three enhancer types (PRP, PRF, PRGF) together in one meta-analysis.
- Confirms a genuine bone-neoformation benefit (MD=5.92%) robust to sensitivity analysis and with non-significant publication bias.
- Establishes that residual-graft-volume reduction is an PRF/PRGF-specific effect, not a PRP effect — refines the "platelet concentrate helps" narrative into enhancer-specific guidance.
- Reconfirms across the largest sample to date that implant stability (RFA) and implant survival are unaffected by platelet-derived adjuncts, regardless of enhancer type, healing interval, or DBBM particle size.
- Subgroup analysis by healing interval (4/5/6/8 months) and DBBM particle size (0.25–1 mm vs 1–2 mm) narrows down which protocol combinations show the clearest new-bone-formation signal (4-month and 6-month intervals; 0.25–1 mm particle size).

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA; PRISMA checklist (Appendix 1)
- Databases: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science, Scopus, Google Scholar (inception–Feb 2025) + reference hand-search
- Included: 16 studies (14 RCTs, 2 case-control); two-stage (delayed) implant placement after DBBM ± PRP/PRF/PRGF sinus augmentation
- N = 372 patients / 455 procedures; mean age 43.5–56.9 years; male 20–80%
- Quality: Cochrane RoB tool (RCTs); Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (case-control, both scored 8/9)
- Statistics: MD (continuous) / RR (binary), random- vs fixed-effects by I², leave-one-out sensitivity, funnel plot + Egger's/Begg's tests, trim-and-fill if biased; subgroups by enhancer type, healing interval, DBBM particle size; R v4.3

## Results

| Outcome | MD/RR | 95% CI | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| New bone formation | MD +5.92 | 2.17 to 9.67 | 0.002 |
| Residual graft volume | MD −1.93 | −2.25 to −1.61 | <0.001 |
| Graft resorption rate | MD +0.24 | −4.75 to 5.23 | 0.925 (NS) |
| Fibrous tissue % | MD −0.97 | −7.33 to 5.40 | 0.765 (NS) |
| Immediate implant stability (RFA) | MD −4.62 (excl. outlier: −1.64) | −11.02 to 1.78 | 0.157 (NS) |
| Implant survival rate | RR 1.03 | 0.98 to 1.07 | 0.252 (NS) |

Subgroup findings:
- New bone formation: significant only in PRF subgroup (MD=7.54), and in 4-month (MD=13.51) / 6-month (MD=5.40) healing-interval subgroups and 0.25–1 mm particle-size subgroup (MD=7.68).
- Residual graft: significant reduction in PRF (MD=−4.32) and PRGF (MD=−1.89) subgroups — PRP subgroup NOT significant; also significant across 4/6/8-month intervals and both particle-size strata.
- Implant stability/survival: no significant subgroup ever reaches significance, regardless of enhancer type, interval, or particle size.
- Publication bias flagged for implant survival (Begg's p=0.011) but trim-and-fill adjustment leaves the null result unchanged (RR=1.02, p=0.390).
