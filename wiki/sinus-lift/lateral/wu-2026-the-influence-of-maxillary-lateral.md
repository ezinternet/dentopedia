## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center CBCT study (35 consecutive sinuses/patients, Xiamen Stomatological Hospital, Sept 2023–Dec 2024) of lateral sinus elevation with collagen sponges only (no bone graft) plus simultaneous implant placement, testing whether preoperative lateral wall thickness (LWT) predicts horizontal crestal bone change at 6 months.

Crestal width fell from 10.97±2.63 to 8.85±2.42 mm (change 2.08±1.06 mm); LWT <1 mm lost significantly more width than LWT >1 mm (2.45±0.97 vs 1.54±0.98 mm, p=0.01), with a medium negative correlation between LWT and width change (r=−0.47, p=0.004) and between residual bone height (RBH) and width change (r=−0.36, p=0.03).

Graftless lateral sinus lift is vertically safe (100% short-term survival, no complications) but costs ~2 mm of horizontal crestal bone — a dimension no vertical endpoint captures — and thin walls (<1 mm) plus low RBH mark the high-remodeling group; retrospective design, n=35, and no power calculation limit inference.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일기관 콘빔 전산화단층촬영 (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT) 연구 (연속 35 증례/환자, Xiamen Stomatological Hospital, 2023-09–2024-12): 콜라겐 스펀지만 사용한 무이식 측방접근 (Graftless Lateral Approach) + 동시 임플란트 식립에서 술전 측방골벽 두께 (Lateral Wall Thickness, LWT) 가 6개월 수평 치조정 골 변화를 예측하는지 평가.

치조정 골폭 (Crestal Bone Width) 은 10.97±2.63 → 8.85±2.42 mm (변화 2.08±1.06 mm); LWT <1 mm 군이 >1 mm 군보다 유의하게 더 많이 감소 (2.45±0.97 vs 1.54±0.98 mm, p=0.01), LWT 와 골폭 변화의 중등도 음의 상관 (r=−0.47, p=0.004), 잔존골 높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 와도 음의 상관 (r=−0.36, p=0.03).

무이식 측방 상악동거상술은 수직적으로는 안전 (단기 생존율 100%, 합병증 0) 하지만 수평 치조정 골을 약 2 mm 잃는다 — 수직 지표로는 안 잡히는 축 — 이며, 얇은 골벽 (<1 mm) + 낮은 RBH 가 고위험군; 후향적 설계·n=35·검정력 계산 없음이 해석의 한계.

## Summary

This single-center retrospective cohort analyzed 35 consecutive lateral sinus elevations (residual bone height <5 mm) in which the sinus was elevated with six saline-soaked collagen sponges only — no particulate graft — and implants were placed simultaneously. Crestal bone width, lateral wall thickness and residual bone height were measured on immediate-postoperative and 6-month CBCT (same scanner, 0.2 mm³ voxel, mean of three consecutive slices, two calibrated examiners). Mean crestal width dropped from 10.97±2.63 to 8.85±2.42 mm (2.08±1.06 mm loss, range 0.24–4.35) with 100% short-term implant survival and no complications. Splitting at a 1 mm lateral wall thickness cut-off, thin-wall cases lost 2.45±0.97 mm versus 1.54±0.98 mm in thick-wall cases (p=0.01) from balanced baseline anatomy, and Pearson analysis showed moderate inverse correlations for both LWT (r=−0.47, p=0.004) and RBH (r=−0.36, p=0.03). The clinical reading: the graftless technique is vertically dependable but horizontally costly, and the thinnest-walled, most resorbed ridges pay the most — so LWT and RBH belong in preoperative CBCT planning, especially where emergence profile and peri-implant soft tissue are esthetically critical.

## Key Contributions

- Shifts the outcome axis of graftless lateral sinus elevation from **vertical to horizontal**: measures crestal width change directly, which graft height, endo-sinus bone and survival endpoints all miss.
- Establishes a **preoperative CBCT predictor with a usable threshold**: LWT <1 mm vs >1 mm separates 2.45 mm from 1.54 mm of horizontal loss (p=0.01) in groups matched on baseline RBH, width and density.
- Adds **RBH as a second inverse predictor** (r=−0.36, p=0.03) — the most resorbed ridge, already the indication for the lateral approach, remodels the most.
- Documents that a 100%-survival, zero-complication graftless protocol can still lose ~2 mm of crestal width in 6 months, i.e. survival is not a sufficient outcome measure.
- Offers a mechanistic account (flap-induced mucoperiosteal ischemia + thin cortex mechanically/ biologically less resistant, poor posterior maxillary density) and names the high-risk phenotype for tailored planning.

## Methodology

Retrospective review of all consecutive lateral sinus lifts by one experienced surgeon (Sept 2023–Dec 2024), n=35 of 50 screened (excluded: bone graft n=8, membrane perforation n=1, window border <3 mm from sinus floor n=3, missing CBCT n=3). Inclusion: RBH <5 mm, collagen-sponge-only elevation, simultaneous implant, both immediate and 6-month CBCT.

Surgery: full-thickness flap via mid-crestal + two vertical incisions; 8×8 mm window with diamond bur 3–5 mm above the estimated sinus floor; membrane reflected with a periosteal elevator braced against the medial wall; six saline-soaked collagen sponges (20×20×5 mm) per site; window covered with compressed non-soaked collagen; no other graft.

Imaging: same Newtom CBCT (100 kVp, 20 mA, 3.2 s, 0.2 mm³ voxel), BlueSky Bio multiplanar reconstruction; all variables averaged over three consecutive slices; two calibrated examiners with kappa reliability. Crestal width measured at the implant-site center on a line perpendicular to the implant axis through the coronal platform; **LWT at ~10 mm above the sinus floor** (3–4 mm RBH + 7 mm); RBH in the same reference frame; density by Misch D-I–D-IV.

Statistics: SPSS 27, Shapiro-Wilk, Student t-test (LWT groups), Chi-square (density), Pearson correlation with bone-density subgroup analysis, α=0.05. No power calculation (authors: first study on the topic).
