## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PubMed/Google Scholar/Cochrane CENTRAL, 1997–October 2014, A B Shetty Institute, India) of direct (lateral window) sinus membrane elevation without graft material during implant placement, including both human clinical and animal studies with ≥6 months follow-up.

Average bone gain ranged 2.37–10 mm; implant survival rate ranged 79.9–100% across included studies; heterogeneity prevented pooled meta-analysis; no evidence found proving grafting is necessary in all direct sinus lift procedures.

The graftless direct sinus lift technique is a viable treatment option when limitations are considered — but the wide survival range (79.9–100%) reflects study quality variability and underscores the need for more standardized, long-term data (addressed subsequently by Dongo 2018 meta-analysis).

## Summary

This systematic review from A B Shetty Memorial Institute of Dental Sciences, Mangalore, India reviewed the literature on direct (lateral window) sinus floor elevation without graft material during implant placement. PubMed, Google Scholar, and Cochrane CENTRAL were searched from 1997 (first known graftless SFE study) to October 2014. Eligibility criteria: (1) direct sinus lift without graft during implant placement; (2) human or animal studies; (3) ≥6 months follow-up. Two independent reviewers evaluated articles; a third author resolved disagreements. Due to high heterogeneity across study designs, data were not pooled.

**Bone gain outcomes:** Wide range (2.37–10 mm) across studies — variation attributable to differences in RBH, implant length/protrusion, technique, and follow-up duration.

**Survival rates:** 79.9–100% across studies. The lower end (79.9%) reflects older studies or lower-quality evidence; well-designed prospective studies clustered near 95–100%.

**Prior meta-analysis context:** Previous meta-analysis cited by authors showed similar survival regardless of graft type (autogenous, allogenic, alloplastic) — setting up graftless as a non-inferior alternative.

**Conclusion:** Graftless direct sinus lift is suggested as a viable treatment option, with the caveat that it has limitations and further standardized research is needed.

## Key Contributions

- Consolidates graftless lateral SFE evidence from Indian academic perspective (1997–2014)
- Includes both human clinical and animal studies — provides mechanistic/histological context alongside clinical outcomes
- Documents wide range of bone gain (2.37–10 mm) revealing heterogeneity problem in the field
- Reinforces that graft material is not evidentially mandatory for direct sinus lift

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review
- **Search**: PubMed, Google Scholar, Cochrane CENTRAL; 1997–October 2014
- **Inclusion**: Direct sinus lift without graft during implant placement; human or animal; ≥6 months follow-up
- **Reviewers**: 2 independent; third for consensus
- **Analysis**: No meta-analysis (heterogeneity); qualitative synthesis
- **Primary outcomes**: Bone height gain; implant survival
- **Secondary outcomes**: Implant stability; complications

## Results

- Bone gain: 2.37–10 mm range across studies
- Survival: 79.9–100% across studies
- Heterogeneity: data not pooled
- Conclusion: graftless direct SFE viable, with limitations
