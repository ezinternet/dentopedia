## One-line Summary
RCT (n=14, RBH ≤4mm): bioabsorbable screws as graftless sinus space-maintainers achieved equivalent histomorphometric NBF to xenograft (37.3% vs 35.2%, p=0.324) but significantly lower radiographic bone height (8.43 vs 12.07mm, p<0.001).

## Summary
This double-blind RCT tested bioabsorbable screws (Inion FreedomScrews™) as space-maintaining devices in graftless lateral window sinus augmentation (n=7) against DBBM xenograft control (n=7) in patients with RBH ≤4mm. After 8 months, the xenograft group showed significantly greater radiographic bone height (12.07 vs 8.43mm, p<0.001). However, histomorphometric analysis of trephine core biopsies showed no significant difference in new bone formation area (37.3% graftless vs 35.2% xenograft, p=0.324). Both groups demonstrated well-organized, vascularized bone with osteoblastic rimming and no inflammation. All 14 implants achieved primary stability at 8 months. The study supports graftless lateral sinus lift as capable of generating equivalent bone quality to xenograft, while highlighting that space maintenance — not osteogenic potential — is the primary limiting factor.

## Key Contributions
- First RCT using bioabsorbable screws as the sole space-maintaining device in graftless lateral window sinus lift
- Histomorphometric equivalence between graftless and xenograft groups despite radiographic inferiority of graftless — confirms Schneiderian membrane's osteogenic potential
- Graftless sites: no residual foreign particles; xenograft sites: DBBM remnants visible — potential advantage for later remodeling
- Bioabsorbable design eliminates second surgery for hardware removal (vs titanium screws/mesh)
- Identifies space maintenance quantity (not histology) as the limiting variable for graftless optimization

## Methodology
- Design: Double-blind, parallel-group RCT; n=14 (7 per group)
- Patients: 12F/2M; mean age 42.4±7.4 years; RBH ≤4mm; posterior maxilla
- Surgical approach: Lateral window (both groups)
- Control: DBBM xenograft (UBGEN, Italy) + collagen membrane
- Intervention: Two bioabsorbable screws (Inion FreedomScrews™, 2.0mm × 9–11mm) lateral-medially above osteotomy + collagen membrane
- Outcomes: CBCT bone height (preop vs 8 months, OnDemand3D); histomorphometry (H&E, ImageJ, ×10); implant primary stability at 8 months
