## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-surgeon cohort (105 patients, 131 sinuses, 266 implants; residual bone height 2.7 ± 0.9 mm) comparing collagen barrier (CB, 33 sinuses), repositioned bone fragment (RW, 24) and no treatment (UT, 64) of the bony access window in lateral sinus floor elevation, with CBCT follow-up of 4.3 ± 1.4 years and mixed-effects modelling.

Window treatment had no significant effect on radiographic window healing (morphology, corticalization), augmented bone height change, or marginal bone level change (all p > 0.05); survival was 100% (CB), 96.9% (RW) and 100% (UT), augmented bone height loss was 1.3–1.4 mm in all groups, and marginal bone change averaged 0.1 ± 0.5 mm.

An untreated lateral window healed as flat and corticalized as a covered one, so routine barrier placement over an intact-membrane window is not supported by this cohort — but the retrospective design, absent histology and non-standardized window size cap that conclusion, and smoking carried OR 6.84 for the 41.2% membrane perforation rate.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일술자 코호트 (환자 105명·상악동 131개·임플란트 266개, 잔존골 높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 2.7 ± 0.9 mm) — 측방접근 상악동거상술 (Lateral Sinus Floor Elevation, LSFE)의 골성 접근창 (Bony Access Window) 처리를 콜라겐 차폐막 (Collagen Barrier, CB) 33개, 골편 재위치 (Repositioned Window, RW) 24개, 무처치 (Untreated, UT) 64개로 나누어 평균 4.3 ± 1.4년 추적.

창 처리 방법은 방사선학적 창부위 치유 (형태·피질화), 이식골 높이 변화, 변연골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 변화 모두에 유의한 영향이 없었고 (모두 p > 0.05), 생존율은 CB 100%·RW 96.9%·UT 100%, 이식골 높이 감소는 전 군 1.3–1.4 mm, MBL 변화는 평균 0.1 ± 0.5 mm.

슈나이더막 (Schneiderian Membrane)이 온전한 경우 창을 덮지 않아도 편평하게 피질화되어 일상적 차폐막 적용은 지지되지 않으나, 후향적 설계·조직학 부재·비표준화된 창 크기가 근거를 제한하며, 점막 천공률 41.2%에서 흡연이 교차비 (Odds Ratio, OR) 6.84로 가장 큰 조절 가능 위험인자였다.

## Summary

This retrospective cohort from a single experienced periodontist (Hanyang University Hospital IRB, patients treated 2016–2020) asked whether the bony access window of a lateral sinus floor elevation needs to be closed at all. Across 131 sinuses in 105 patients with a mean residual bone height of 2.7 ± 0.9 mm and 266 simultaneously placed implants, the window was either covered with a cross-linked collagen membrane (CB, 33 sinuses), closed by repositioning the detached bone fragment (RW, 24), or left untreated (UT, 64). After 4.3 ± 1.4 years, CBCT showed a predominantly flat and fully corticalized healed window in every group (full corticalization 90.9%, 85.3% and 73.4%), no particle scattering outside the sinus, an augmented bone height loss of only 1.3–1.4 mm, and a marginal bone level change of 0.1 ± 0.5 mm. Mixed multinomial and linear models found no significant effect of window treatment on any of these outcomes (p > 0.05); implant survival was 100% in CB and UT and 96.9% in RW, where a single patient lost two implants to failed osseointegration. Schneiderian membrane perforation occurred in 54/131 sinuses (41.2%) without any post-healing sino-nasal symptoms, and was significantly predicted by sex, smoking (OR 6.84) and lower residual bone height. The clinical message is that covering the lateral window is optional rather than mandatory when the membrane is intact — the residual rationale for a barrier is graft containment and protection, not a demonstrated healing advantage — while the retrospective, non-randomized, single-surgeon design and the absence of histology limit how firmly that can be stated.

## Key Contributions

- Provides the first three-arm clinical comparison of lateral-window handling (collagen barrier vs repositioned bone fragment vs nothing at all) in a single lateral-SFE cohort, rather than the conventional two-group membrane-vs-no-membrane design.
- Demonstrates that an untreated lateral window achieves comparable radiographic healing — flat morphology, full corticalization in 73.4% of untreated sinuses versus 90.9% (CB) and 85.3% (RW), one of only two non-corticalized sites in the whole cohort — at 4.3 ± 1.4 years.
- Quantifies long-term augmented bone stability for three BCP ratios in a containing-type sinus defect: 1.3 ± 2.2 mm (CB), 1.4 ± 1.8 mm (RW), 1.4 ± 1.6 mm (UT) of height loss, all < 1.5 mm, with marginal bone change of 0.1 ± 0.5 mm and 96.9–100% survival across 266 implants.
- Separates "window treatment" from the covariates that actually mattered: lateral wall thickness protected against height loss (B 0.58, p = 0.0211), Osteon 3 shrank more than Osteon 2 between T2 and T3 (B −0.48, p = 0.0108), and window morphology was driven by sex, DM, smoking, implant dimensions, sinus angle and width, wall thickness and RBH.
- Reports a 41.2% membrane perforation rate managed without a collagen patch and without sino-nasal sequelae, with smoking (OR 6.84) and low RBH (OR 0.64 per +1 mm) as significant predictors — useful benchmarking for perforation-risk counselling.

## Methodology

Retrospective cohort (STROBE-reported) of all lateral SFE with simultaneous implant placement performed by one periodontist in a private clinic between January 2016 and December 2020. Inclusion required edentulous posterior maxilla with sinus pneumatization, residual bone height < 5 mm, age > 20, good general health and adequate oral hygiene; heavy smokers (> 10/day) and systemic or local sino-nasal contraindications were excluded. A round bur outlined a 15–20 × 6–10 mm window, greenstick-fractured with an osteotome; the membrane was elevated with sinus curettes, a synthetic BCP (Osteon 1/2/3) grafted, and implants (Implantium) placed simultaneously. Groups were defined by window handling: CB = cross-linked collagen membrane extending 2–3 mm past the margin; RW = detached bone fragment repositioned; UT = no treatment. CBCT at T0 (pre-op), T1 (immediately post-op), T2 (prosthesis insertion) and T3 (last follow-up) was superimposed in OnDemand3D and bone height measured along the implant axis; at T3 window healing was scored for morphology (flat/protruded/depressed), corticalization (full/partial) and protrusion extent, and marginal bone level change was measured mesially and distally between T2 and T3. A mixed binomial logistic model (perforation), mixed multinomial logistic model (window healing) and mixed linear models (bone height, marginal bone level) were fitted in SAS 9.4 with patient/implant-level clustering, α = 0.05.
