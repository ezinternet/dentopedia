## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-regression of 43 studies (34 lateral window, 5 osteotome, 2 localized sinus floor, 2 crestal core) on implant survival after maxillary sinus augmentation, published through April 2003.

Lateral window implant survival averaged 91.8% (range 61.7–100%); meta-regression found higher survival with rough-surfaced implants, particulate (vs block) grafts, and membrane coverage of the lateral window, with no difference by autogenous-bone content, timing (simultaneous vs delayed), or publication year.

Landmark early synthesis but abstract-only in this ingest (no PMC full text) and methodologically dated — pre-CBCT, pre-GRADE, mostly retrospective/case-series evidence (only 3 of 43 studies were RCTs); its membrane-benefit finding is directly contradicted by a later, RCT-only SR+MA.

## Summary

Wallace and Froum's 2003 systematic review remains one of the most frequently cited early syntheses of the lateral window maxillary sinus augmentation literature. Drawing on 43 studies through April 2003, the review used meta-regression — an unusual level of statistical rigor for its era — to isolate the effect of specific surgical and material variables on implant survival, rather than reporting a single pooled survival figure. It found lateral-window implant survival averaging 91.8% (61.7–100% range), with rough implant surfaces, particulate (vs block) grafts, and lateral-window membrane coverage each independently associated with higher survival, while autogenous bone content and implant placement timing (simultaneous vs delayed) showed no effect. As an abstract-only ingest (no PMC full text available), this page cannot verify per-study data or the full meta-regression tables; it should be read as a historical technique-comparison benchmark rather than current best evidence — several of its findings, notably the membrane-coverage benefit, have since been directly tested and not replicated by more rigorous, narrower designs.

## Key Contributions
- Quantifies lateral-window implant survival (91.8% average) as a historically-anchored benchmark still widely cited in later reviews.
- Among the first SRs to meta-regress *technique variables themselves* (graft form, membrane use, implant surface, timing) rather than only reporting pooled survival.
- Explicitly stratifies technique subgroups (lateral window vs osteotome vs localized sinus floor vs crestal core) — early recognition that "sinus lift" outcomes are technique-dependent.

## Methodology
Systematic review with meta-regression; MEDLINE + Cochrane + DARE + hand search of 3 major implant journals + unpublished-data outreach, through April 2003. 43 studies included (3 RCT, 5 CT, 12 case series, 23 retrospective); ≥20 interventions and ≥1-year loaded follow-up required per study. Covariates meta-regressed: block vs particulate graft, implant surface, graft material composition, lateral-window membrane use, simultaneous vs delayed placement, follow-up length, publication year, evidence level.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Lateral window implant survival | 91.8% average (range 61.7–100%) |
| Implant surface | Rough > machine-surfaced (higher survival) |
| Graft form | Particulate > block (higher survival) |
| Lateral-window membrane | Present > absent (higher survival) |
| Autogenous bone content (100% vs composite) | No significant difference |
| Simultaneous vs delayed implant placement | No significant difference |
| Follow-up length / publication year / evidence level | No significant difference |
| Study composition | 34 lateral window / 5 osteotome / 2 localized sinus floor / 2 crestal core (of 43 total) |
