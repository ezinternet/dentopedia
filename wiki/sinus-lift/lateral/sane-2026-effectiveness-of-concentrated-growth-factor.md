## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251148756) of 6 studies (185 sinuses) in adults undergoing lateral-window direct sinus augmentation with concentrated growth factor (CGF).
CGF significantly increased histologic new bone formation (pooled SMD 0.94, 95% CI 0.30–1.57, p = 0.004, I² = 41%) and radiographic bone density (SMD 0.89, 95% CI 0.44–1.34, p = 0.0001, I² = 0%), with no significant change in bone height gain (SMD −0.29, p = 0.41) or implant survival (97–100%).
CGF improves bone quality without reducing vertical gain or survival, but the eggshell-derived nano-hydroxyapatite (EHA) + CGF claim is translational inference — no included study tested that combination.

## Summary

This 2026 systematic review with meta-analysis asks whether CGF — an autologous blood-derived biologic rich in growth factors — improves outcomes when added to lateral-window (direct) sinus augmentation with hydroxyapatite graft. It is the first wiki-held quantitative synthesis of CGF restricted to the lateral approach with pooled effect sizes: the earlier [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/al-aroomi-2024-cgf-maxillary-sinus-augmentation-sr]] review was qualitative and mixed transcrestal and lateral techniques. Six studies (3 RCTs, 1 three-arm randomized, 2 non-randomized; 185 sinuses) were pooled under a random-effects model. CGF produced a moderate-to-large increase in histologic new bone formation (SMD 0.94) and a large increase in radiographic bone density (SMD 0.89, no heterogeneity), while bone height gain (SMD −0.29, I² = 75%) and implant survival (97–100%) were statistically indistinguishable from controls. GRADE certainty was high for formation, density and survival, moderate for height. The paper's second aim — translational consideration of eggshell-derived nano-hydroxyapatite (EHA) combined with CGF — is explicitly hypothesis-generating: no included study evaluated EHA, so the pooled effect reflects CGF alone.

## Key Contributions

- First pooled (meta-analytic) estimate of CGF's effect on new bone formation specifically in lateral-window sinus augmentation.
- Separates bone *quality* (histologic new bone %, density) from bone *quantity* (height gain) — CGF helps the former without changing the latter.
- High GRADE certainty for the bone-formation and density signals despite a small study pool.
- Explicitly flags the EHA + CGF evidence vacuum and proposes head-to-head trials against DBBM ± CGF.

## Methodology

- PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420251148756.
- Search: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar (inception–October 2025) + manual reference checks.
- Inclusion: RCTs and comparative clinical studies in adults ≥18 years undergoing direct sinus augmentation.
- Risk of bias: RoB 2 for randomized, ROBINS-I for non-randomized designs.
- Synthesis: random-effects meta-analysis (RevMan Web), SMD/MD with 95% CI; GRADE certainty appraisal.

## Results

| Outcome | Effect (SMD) | 95% CI | p | I² | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New bone formation (histologic) | 0.94 | 0.30–1.57 | 0.004 | 41% | Favors CGF |
| Bone density (radiographic) | 0.89 | 0.44–1.34 | 0.0001 | 0% | Favors CGF |
| Bone height gain | −0.29 | −0.98 to 0.41 | 0.41 | 75% | No difference |
| Implant survival | 97–100% | — | — | — | High in both arms |

- 6 studies / 185 sinuses: 3 RCTs, 1 three-arm randomized trial, 2 non-randomized designs.
- No graft-related complications reported.
- GRADE: high for formation, density and survival; moderate for height gain.

## Limitations

- **Abstract-only artifact** — PMC JATS body was empty; study-level extraction, funnel plot and pre-specified subgroups could not be verified beyond the abstract.
- Small, design-heterogeneous pool (6 studies, mixed randomized/non-randomized).
- Height-gain comparison carries substantial heterogeneity (I² = 75%).
- EHA + CGF was not directly tested; EHA osteoconduction evidence is extrapolated from intraoral (non-sinus) applications.
