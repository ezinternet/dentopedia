## Three-line Summary

RCT (J Dent 2026): 60 patients, 92 implants; tapered vs cylindrical bone-level implant placed simultaneously with lateral sinus floor elevation (LSFE) in the posterior maxilla; ISQ at placement (T1) and 6 months (T2) plus CBCT bone assessment.

Tapered implants achieved significantly higher primary stability (ISQ 70.48 vs 55.04, p<0.001) and secondary stability (ISQ 80.83 vs 78.98, p=0.038); endo-sinus bone gain, MBL, and 3D bone volume were comparable between groups; 100% survival both.

In LSFE-grafted sites, tapered implant design delivers superior primary and secondary stability vs cylindrical — both achieve predictable 6-month outcomes with comparable bone remodeling.

## Summary

Gai et al. (J Dent 2026) conducted a prospective RCT at Zhejiang University comparing a tapered (mild apical taper) vs cylindrical (parallel-walled) bone-level implant system in 60 patients undergoing simultaneous LSFE in the posterior maxilla (92 implants total). ISQ was measured by Osstell RFA at placement (T1) and after 6 months of healing (T2). CBCT assessed endo-sinus bone gain (ESBG), MBL, and 3D bone volume. Tapered implants showed significantly higher ISQ at both T1 and T2, while peri-implant bone changes were comparable. GEE statistics accounted for multiple implants per patient. Both systems achieved 100% survival at 6 months.

## Key Contributions

- First prospective RCT comparing ISQ trajectories of tapered vs cylindrical implants specifically at simultaneous LSFE
- Tapered: significantly higher primary stability (ISQ T1 +15.4 units, p<0.001)
- Tapered: significantly higher secondary stability at 6 months (ISQ T2 +1.85 units, p=0.038) — modest difference
- Peri-implant bone remodeling (ESBG, MBL, BV): equivalent between designs
- Both systems achieve predictable short-term outcomes in posterior maxilla with limited bone

## Methodology

Prospective 2-arm RCT; Zhejiang University; n=60 patients, 92 implants (tapered n=48, cylindrical n=44); simultaneous LSFE; ISQ (Osstell) at T1 (placement) and T2 (6 months); CBCT for ESBG, MBL, 3D-BV at T2; GEE for clustered data; ChiCTR2100042169.
