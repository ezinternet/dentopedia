## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT study (112 patients, 200 sinonasal complexes: 100 dentate, 100 edentulous; University of Connecticut) evaluating maxillary sinus ostium location and patency to define how high the sinus floor can be safely elevated without blocking ostiomeatal-complex (OMC) drainage.

Subtracting mean mucosal-thickening height from mean sinus-floor-to-ostium height yields a calculated "safe augmentation height" of ~27.05 mm (dentate) and ~23.40 mm (edentulous); the ostium lay near the first-molar region in 47% of sinuses, with excellent inter-rater reliability (Krippendorff α 0.89–0.98).

The safe height is well above typical implant lengths but shrinks case-by-case with greater mucosal pathology — supporting preoperative CBCT assessment of ostium height and mucosal thickening before lateral-window sinus augmentation.

## 세줄요약

후향적 콘빔CT(Cone-Beam CT, CBCT) 연구(112명, 200개 부비동복합체: 유치악 100·무치악 100; 코네티컷대) — 상악동공(Ostium) 위치·개통성을 평가해 골이식 시 상악동저를 개구비도복합체(OMC) 배액을 막지 않고 얼마나 안전하게 거상할 수 있는지 규명.

"동저–자연공 높이 − 점막비후 높이"로 계산한 안전 거상 높이는 유치악 ~27.05mm·무치악 ~23.40mm; 자연공은 47%에서 제1대구치 부위에 위치했고 평가자간 신뢰도가 우수(Krippendorff α 0.89–0.98).

안전 높이는 통상 임플란트 길이보다 충분히 높으나 점막 병변이 클수록 증례별로 줄어듦 — 측방접근 상악동거상 전 CBCT로 자연공 높이·점막비후를 평가할 근거.

## Summary

This retrospective CBCT study addresses a gap in preoperative sinus-lift planning: how much can the sinus floor be safely elevated without blocking the maxillary sinus ostium and jeopardizing ostiomeatal-complex (OMC) drainage? Using 200 sinonasal complexes from 112 patients (100 dentate, 100 edentulous), the authors measured sinus-floor-to-ostium height, ridge height, sinus width, and mucosal-thickening height on coronal CBCT sections. The ostium was located near the first molar region in 47% of sinuses. Subtracting mean mucosal-thickening height from mean sinus-floor-to-ostium height yields a calculated "safe augmentation height" of approximately 27.05 mm for dentate and 23.40 mm for edentulous patients — well above typical implant lengths, but the value shrinks case-by-case with greater mucosal pathology. Inter-rater reliability was excellent (Krippendorff α 0.89–0.98), supporting the reproducibility of the measurement protocol for routine chairside use.

## Key Contributions

- First explicit **"safe height" formula** for sinus augmentation: sinus-floor-to-ostium height minus mucosal-thickening height, addressing the prior lack of a standardized protocol.
- Normative dentate vs. edentulous CBCT morphometrics of the maxillary sinus and ostium (200 sinuses, 112 patients).
- Ostium localized to the first-molar region in 47% of sinuses — practical landmark for lateral-window osteotomy design.
- Sex-based differences: sinus-floor-to-ostium height greater in males; alveolar/residual ridge height greater in females (both p<0.05).
- Excellent inter-rater reliability (α 0.89–0.98) supports clinical reproducibility.

## Methodology

- Retrospective CBCT review, single academic center (University of Connecticut, 2017–2018 archive).
- 112 patients (56M/56F), 200 sinonasal complexes: 100 dentate (16–85y) + 100 edentulous (36–91y); each sinus scored individually.
- Coronal-section reference view through the ostiomeatal complex; measurements in Invivo 5.0 (Anatomage).
- Measured: sinus top/middle width, sinus-floor-to-ostium height, alveolar/residual ridge height, mucosal-thickening height.
- 2 calibrated raters + senior radiologist review; Krippendorff α for inter-rater reliability; unpaired t-test for group comparisons.
- Exclusions: age <15y, sinonasal developmental variation, prior sinus surgery/grafting, non-mucosal-thickening pathology.

## Results

| Parameter | Dentate | Edentulous |
|---|---|---|
| Sinus floor → ostium height | 31.40±5.04 mm | 29.50±4.41 mm |
| Alveolar/residual ridge height | 7.17±3.74 mm | 7.59±4.10 mm |
| Mucosal thickening prevalence | 64% | 52% |
| Mucosal thickening magnitude | 4.05±3.19 mm | 6.40±6.18 mm (p<0.05 higher) |
| **Calculated safe augmentation height** | **~27.05 mm** | **~23.40 mm** |

- Ostium in first-molar region: 47% of sinuses.
- Middle width and ridge height significantly higher in dentate patients (p<0.05).
- Sinus-floor-to-ostium height significantly higher in males; ridge height significantly higher in females (both p<0.05).
- Inter-rater reliability (Krippendorff α): sinus measurements 0.98, mucosal thickening height 0.89, ridge height 0.95.
- No standardized safe-height guideline existed in prior literature; authors call for prospective pre-/post-augmentation validation.
