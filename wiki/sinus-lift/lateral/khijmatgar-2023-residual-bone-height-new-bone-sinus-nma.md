## Three-line Summary

Network meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42022331993; 4 databases to 31 Dec 2022) of 67 RCTs / 1955 patients / 2405 lateral maxillary sinus augmentations, running **two separate networks split at residual bone height (RBH) 4 mm** to rank autogenous bone, xenograft, allograft, alloplast, bioactive agents, hyaluronic acid and their combinations by histomorphometric new bone formation.

For RBH <4 mm the SUCRA leaders were XG+AG (8.97, 95% CI −3.60 to 21.56) and XG+AP (1.93), with alloplasts last; for RBH ≥4 mm they were Auto (9.86, CI as printed 12.71 to −15.06), Bio+XG (2.01) and XG+Auto (1.86), with allografts last — the only significant inconsistency loop was XG–AP–Bio+AP.

The ranking flips with RBH, which is the paper's whole point, but GRADE certainty was low for every material (all CIs wide and zero-crossing), so this supports *conditioning* graft choice on RBH rather than naming a winning biomaterial.

## 세줄요약

네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA; PROSPERO CRD42022331993; 4개 데이터베이스, 2022-12-31까지): 무작위 대조 시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 67편·환자 1955명·측방 상악동거상술 2405건을 잔존골높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 4 mm에서 **두 개의 별도 네트워크로 분리**해 자가골·이종골·동종골·합성골·생물활성제·히알루론산 및 조합의 신생골 (New Bone Formation, NBF) 비율 순위를 산출.

RBH <4 mm에서는 누적순위곡선하면적 (Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking, SUCRA) 1–2위가 XG+AG (8.97, 95% CI −3.60–21.56)·XG+AP (1.93)이고 합성골 단독이 최하위; RBH ≥4 mm에서는 Auto (9.86, 인쇄된 CI 12.71 to −15.06)·Bio+XG (2.01)·XG+Auto (1.86)이고 동종골 단독이 최하위; 유의한 비일관성 (Inconsistency Factor, IF) 루프는 XG–AP–Bio+AP 하나.

순위가 RBH에 따라 뒤집히는 것이 이 논문의 핵심이지만 모든 재료의 근거수준 (GRADE)이 낮고 신뢰구간이 0을 가로지르므로, "RBH에 따라 이식재를 조건화하라"는 방향의 근거이지 특정 이식재의 승자 선언은 아니다.

## Summary

This PROSPERO-registered systematic review and frequentist network meta-analysis (STATA v17.0, `metan`) asked whether the choice of grafting biomaterial for lateral maxillary sinus augmentation should depend on residual bone height. Sixty-seven RCTs (1955 patients, 2405 sinus augmentations) survived screening from 707 records; materials were collapsed into six classes — autogenous bone (Auto), xenograft (XG), allograft (AG), alloplast (AP), bioactive agents (Bio), hyaluronic acid (HA) — plus two- and three-way combinations. Instead of one pooled network, the authors split the data at a mean RBH of 4 mm and ran two independent NMAs, ranking treatments with SUCRA and judging replicability with predictive intervals. For RBH <4 mm, xenograft+allograft and xenograft+alloplast ranked first and second and alloplasts alone ranked last; for RBH ≥4 mm, autogenous bone, Bio+XG and XG+Auto ranked first through third and allograft alone ranked last. One closed loop (XG–AP–Bio+AP) showed statistically significant inconsistency; all loops within the <4 mm network were consistent. The authors conclude that no biomaterial performs consistently better across strata, that NBF may depend on RBH, and that material selection must weigh patient-specific and sinus-specific factors — while GRADE certainty was low for every material because all confidence intervals were wide. The clinical reading is therefore conditional: RBH plausibly shifts the ranking, but no ranking here is precise enough to override handling, cost and biological-safety considerations.

## Key Contributions

- **Conditions the graft ranking on RBH** — two separate networks (<4 mm, ≥4 mm) instead of one pooled NMA, which is the structural difference from every sinus-graft NMA ingested before it.
- Supplies the **largest pooled base in this category so far**: 67 RCTs, 1955 patients, 2405 augmentations (vs 34 RCTs / 842 augmentations in Trimmel 2021).
- Triangulates ranking with **SUCRA + predictive intervals + inconsistency factor**, explicitly separating "ranked best in this dataset" from "most likely to replicate" — XG+AG/XG+AP (<4 mm) and Auto/Bio+XG (≥4 mm) appear in both readings.
- Reports the **rank inversions**: first place flips XG+AG → Auto, and last place flips alloplast → allograft between strata, which is the empirical case against a single "best graft" claim.
- States the ceiling honestly: **low GRADE certainty for all materials**, with the one significant inconsistency loop located in the XG–AP–Bio+AP triangle.

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR + NMA; PROSPERO CRD42022331993; PRISMA reporting.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane Central, Web of Science — no language/year limits; hand search of major OMFS and implant journals plus reference lists; last electronic search 9 January 2023.
- **PICO**: any patient needing sinus lift, any RBH, no age/sex limits; intervention = sinus lift with ≥1 biomaterial; comparator = self-healing/no material or a different material; primary outcome = **NBF% by histomorphometry**; secondary = adverse events/complications; biopsy healing time ≥2 months.
- **Inclusion**: RCTs (split-mouth or parallel) with distinct test and control materials. **Excluded**: reviews/letters/case reports/abstracts, duplicates, in vitro and animal studies, and any study using the same biomaterial in both arms.
- **n**: 707 records → 109 duplicates → 598 screened → 266 excluded on title/abstract → 84 full texts → 17 excluded (same graft in both arms 13; RBH not reported 4; not randomised 3; no per-material results 1 — overlapping reasons) → **67 RCTs, 1955 patients, 2405 sinus lifts**.
- **Agreement**: Cohen's kappa 0.92 (title/abstract) and 0.93 (full-text selection).
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane Handbook domains (randomisation, allocation concealment, outcome-assessor blinding, completeness of reporting, withdrawals, other biases); performance bias deliberately unscored because graft material cannot be masked. Low/moderate/high overall.
- **Statistics**: frequentist NMA in STATA v17.0 (`metan`); network geometry and contribution plots; inconsistency via closed-loop IF (significant when lower CI limit excludes zero); SUCRA for ranking; predictive intervals for replication; MDS for rank dissimilarity; Higgins I² with Moses–Shapiro–Littenberg regression and subgroup analyses if I²>50%; Deek's funnel-plot asymmetry; α=0.05 two-sided; GRADE for certainty.
- **RBH handling**: data split by **mean study-level RBH at 4 mm** — a design choice, not a per-sinus measurement.

> CI bounds are reproduced verbatim from the paper's text, whose ordering is internally inconsistent (lower bound printed above the upper bound in most rows). The accompanying prose claim that XG+AG "performed 8.97 times better" is not a valid reading of a percentage-point difference; read the column as a network estimate whose interval crosses zero.

**Consistency**

| Check | Finding |
|---|---|
| Loops within <4 mm network | lower CI limit at zero → **not significant** |
| Entire loop XG–AP–Bio+AP | **statistically significant inconsistency** |
| XG+AG and XG+Auto | no closed loop → no significant IF |

**Predictive intervals (PrI)** — most likely to perform best in future studies: XG+AG and XG+AP (<4 mm); Auto and Bio+XG (≥4 mm). Note the PrI set is **not identical** to the SUCRA top-3 (XG+Auto is third by SUCRA but not a PrI leader), which is the paper's own warning that ranking ≠ replicability.

**Certainty (GRADE)**: **low for all biomaterials**, attributed to wide 95% CIs in direct, indirect and network evidence; the authors state moderate/high certainty would require narrower intervals.

## Related Papers

- [[sinus-lift/lateral/trimmel-2021-relative-performance-various-biomaterials]] — the other sinus-graft NMA in the wiki (34 RCTs, 842 augmentations, 28 subgroups): it puts bovine+BMC and bovine+PRP on top and autologous bone alone 12th (SUCRA 57%), whereas this paper's ≥4 mm network puts Auto first — the two NMAs disagree specifically on autogenous bone, and the RBH split plus the much larger trial base is Khijmatgar's bid to explain why.
- [[sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]] — SR of 49 RCTs on **implant survival** (84.6–100%, no difference between DBBM, autogenous±DBBM and synthetic HA/β-TCP); it supplies exactly the endpoint this NMA lacks, and its null result is a caution against reading NBF% rank differences as survival differences.
- [[sinus-lift/lateral/pogacian-maier-2024-allograft-bone-lateral-sinus-sr]] — allograft-only SR (7 studies, 238 patients; FDBA vs DBB WMD −0.17, p=0.53): Khijmatgar ranks allograft **alone** last at RBH ≥4 mm yet **xenograft+allograft first** at <4 mm, so the two pages together argue allograft's value is as a combination partner in low-RBH sinuses rather than as a stand-alone graft.
- [[sinus-lift/lateral/alkandari-2025-bcp-dbbm-sinus-elevation-sr-ma]] — pairwise BCP vs DBBM (10 RCTs, 328 patients; BCP +3.48% NBF, −8.41% residual graft): this is the "AP vs XG" comparison that the class-collapsed node hides, and it illustrates the within-class heterogeneity the NMA trades away.
- [[sinus-lift/lateral/harlos-2022-histomorphometric-evaluation-of-different-graft]] — RCT in wide antral cavities (RBH remnants <3 mm) where xenogenic+autogenous 70:30 beat xenogenic alone by a wide margin — the kind of single-trial signal behind the XG+Auto node, and a reminder that NBF can also be reported as absolute area rather than percentage.
- [[sinus-lift/lateral/allurkar-2025-implant-stability-residual-bone-macrodesign]] — in vitro evidence that primary stability (ISQ) rises with RBH, a mechanical RBH effect distinct from the biological RBH effect on NBF that this NMA tests.
