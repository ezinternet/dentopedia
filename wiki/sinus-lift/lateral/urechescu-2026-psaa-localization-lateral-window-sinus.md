## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT analysis of 1152 maxillary sinuses correlating PSAA localization to a novel 4-class lateral window height classification framework.
PSAA detected in 68.1%; submucosal course most common (48.6%); mean sinus-floor distance 8.30±4.31 mm; large windows (>11 mm) significantly more associated with intraosseous PSAA (p=0.004).
Individualized preoperative CBCT assessment is recommended to anticipate PSAA course before lateral window planning, particularly when extended superior osteotomies are anticipated.

## 세줄요약

루마니아 코호트 1152 상악동을 대상으로 콘빔CT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT)로 후상치조동맥 (Posterior Superior Alveolar Artery, PSAA) 위치를 측방창 높이 4분류와 대조한 후향적 연구.
검출률 68.1%; 점막하형 (submucosal) 48.6% 최다, 동저 평균거리 8.3±4.3 mm; 대형창 (>11 mm) 에서 골내형 (intraosseous) 비율 유의 증가 (p=0.004; Cramer's V=0.111).
측방 상악동거상술 (Lateral Sinus Augmentation, LSA) 계획 시 개인화된 창 디자인을 위해 술전 CBCT 혈관 해부 평가가 필수적이다.

## Summary

This CBCT-based retrospective study analyzed 1152 maxillary sinuses from a Romanian tertiary center to characterize the prevalence, anatomical location, and sinus-floor distance of the PSAA in the context of lateral window sinus floor elevation planning. The PSAA was visible in 68.1% of evaluated sinuses, most frequently following a submucosal course immediately beneath the Schneiderian membrane (48.6%), with a mean vertical distance from the sinus floor of 8.30 mm (SD 4.31). A novel 4-class radiographic framework was introduced to correlate the vertical position of the simulated lateral window with PSAA course: large windows exceeding 11 mm were significantly more frequently associated with an intraosseous arterial course (p=0.004), suggesting elevated hemorrhage risk when superior osteotomies are required. These findings support routine preoperative CBCT anatomical assessment for individualized lateral window design to minimize vascular complications.

## Key Contributions

- **4-class lateral window height framework**: First description correlating window vertical position (<3mm / 3-8mm / 8-11mm / >11mm above sinus floor) to PSAA encounter probability
- **Large-window intraosseous PSAA association**: Statistical evidence that large windows (>11mm) carry significantly higher intraosseous PSAA exposure risk
- **Population-level PSAA characterization**: n=1152 sinuses, sex-stratified data, comparable to global CBCT literature
- **Distance stratified by window class**: Mean PSAA-floor distances increase systematically: 2.0 mm (<3mm class) → 14.2 mm (>11mm class)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective cross-sectional observational (STROBE-compliant), Ethics E-3790/16.09.2025
- **n**: 1152 maxillary sinuses (547 male, 605 female)
- **Setting**: Municipal Emergency Hospital, Timisoara, Romania; routine diagnostic CBCT scans
- **Equipment**: Planmeca VISOG7 CBCT (110 kV, 8 mA, voxel 0.15 mm); analyzed with OnDemand3D
- **PSAA classification**: Intraosseous / Submucosal / Extraosseous (3 classes)
- **Window classification**: <3mm (no interference), 3-8mm small, 8-11mm medium, >11mm large
- **Statistics**: χ² test of independence; Cramer's V; hierarchical loglinear (sex); inter-rater κ=0.87

## Results

| PSAA course | % of all sinuses |
|---|---|
| Submucosal | 48.6% |
| Intraosseous | 17.6% |
| Extraosseous | 1.8% |
| Not visible | 31.9% |

**Key metrics**:
- Overall detection rate: 68.1% (male 72.4%, female 64.1%)
- Mean PSAA to sinus floor: **8.30 mm** (95% CI 8.00–8.60)
- Large window (>11mm) → intraosseous PSAA 35.0%, standardized residual +2.3 (p<0.05)
- Window class distribution: 3-8mm (44.3%) most common, >11mm (9.1%) least common

| Window class | Mean PSAA-floor distance |
|---|---|
| <3mm | 2.0 ± 0.7 mm |
| 3–8mm (small) | 5.9 ± 2.9 mm |
| 8–11mm (medium) | 9.4 ± 0.9 mm |
| >11mm (large) | 14.2 ± 2.9 mm |
