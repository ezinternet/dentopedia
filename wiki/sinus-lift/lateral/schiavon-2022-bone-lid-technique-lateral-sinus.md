## Three-line Summary
SR+MA (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42020184317) of 5 animal studies (4 rabbit, 1 sheep) and 2 clinical studies (1 RCT, 1 case–control; 15 bone lids, 122 implants) evaluating the repositioned bone lid technique in lateral sinus lift, searched through April 2022.
Random-effects meta-analysis of 3 animal studies found significantly higher new bone formation with the bone lid vs. resorbable membrane at 2 weeks (P=0.04) and 8 weeks (P=0.03, not significant at 4 weeks P=0.08); clinical studies found no significant difference in bone-to-implant contact (92–93.5%), bone regeneration, or implant survival (99–100%) between bone lid, membrane, and autogenous-graft closure.
Moderate evidence supports a bone-formation benefit for the bone lid over resorbable membranes in animals, with a low complication rate (1/15 clinical perforation, no lasting dislocation), but only 2 small, moderate-to-high-risk-of-bias clinical studies (<15-month follow-up) currently exist to confirm this in humans.

## 세줄요약
체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42020184317) — 동물실험 5편(토끼 4, 양 1) + 임상연구 2편(RCT 1, 환자-대조군 1연구; 골개(Bone Lid) 15례, 임플란트 122개), 2022년 4월까지 검색, 측방 상악동거상술(Lateral Sinus Lift) 시 절제한 골창을 원위치로 되돌려 덮는 골개(Bone Lid) 술식을 평가.
무작위효과모형 메타분석(동물실험 3편)에서 골개군이 흡수성 차폐막(Resorbable Membrane)군보다 신생골 형성이 2주(P=0.04)·8주(P=0.03)에 유의하게 높았음(4주는 P=0.08로 비유의); 임상연구에서는 골-임플란트 접촉률(Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC, 92–93.5%)·골재생·임플란트 생존율(99–100%) 모두 골개·차폐막·자가골 이식 간 유의차 없음.
동물실험에서는 골개가 흡수성 차폐막보다 신생골 형성에 유리하다는 중등도 근거가 있고 합병증률도 낮으나(임상 천공 1/15, 지속적 골개 탈락 없음), 인체 적용을 뒷받침할 임상연구는 편향위험 중~고도의 소규모 연구 2편(추적 15개월 미만)뿐이라 추가 확인이 필요.

## Summary
This systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42020184317) evaluated the bone lid technique in lateral sinus lift — repositioning the osteotomized bone window back into place at the end of surgery as an autologous barrier, instead of discarding it or covering the antrostomy with a resorbable or non-resorbable membrane. Five pre-clinical animal studies (4 rabbit, 1 sheep) and two clinical studies (1 RCT, 1 case–control; 15 bone lids, 122 implants) met inclusion criteria after searching MEDLINE, Web of Science, and Cochrane through April 2022. A random-effects meta-analysis of three rabbit studies found significantly higher new bone formation with the bone lid than with a resorbable membrane at 2 weeks (P=0.04) and 8 weeks (P=0.03), though not at 4 weeks (P=0.08). The two clinical studies found no significant difference in bone-to-implant contact, bone regeneration, or implant survival (99–100%) between the bone lid and membrane or autogenous-graft closure, and complications were rare (one membrane perforation among 15 bone lid cases, no lasting bone lid dislocation). The authors conclude the evidence for the bone lid's bone-formation benefit is moderate and animal-derived; only two small, moderate-to-high-risk-of-bias clinical studies with under 15-month follow-up currently support translating this benefit to humans.

## Key Contributions
- First SR+MA to isolate the repositioned bone lid as the specific intervention variable in lateral sinus lift (PROSPERO CRD42020184317, PRISMA-compliant).
- Random-effects meta-analysis (3 rabbit studies) shows significantly higher new bone formation with the bone lid vs. resorbable membrane at 2 and 8 weeks.
- Clinical evidence (1 RCT + 1 case–control, 122 implants) shows no significant difference in BIC, bone regeneration, or implant survival across bone lid, membrane, and autogenous-graft closure, with low complication rates (6.7% perforation, no lasting dislocation).

## Methodology
SR+MA per PRISMA, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42020184317). MEDLINE (PubMed) + Web of Science + Cochrane Central + hand search, English-language studies through 5 April 2022. Included: 5 animal studies (4 rabbit, 1 sheep) + 2 clinical studies (1 RCT, 1 case–control) — 15 bone lids, 122 implants total. Primary outcome: % new bone formation (histomorphometric). Secondary: bone window/lid healing, implant survival. Risk of bias: ARRIVE 2.0 + SYRCLE (animal studies), RoB 2 (RCT), ROBINS-I (case–control).

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| New bone formation, animal MA (2 wk) | Significantly higher, bone lid vs. resorbable membrane (P=0.04) |
| New bone formation, animal MA (4 wk) | Not significant (P=0.08) |
| New bone formation, animal MA (8 wk) | Significantly higher, bone lid (P=0.03) |
| Clinical BIC (micro-CT, retrieved implants) | 93.5% bone lid vs. 92.0% collagen membrane vs. 93.5% autogenous bone — NS |
| Clinical bone regeneration (case–control) | No significant difference, bone lid vs. non-resorbable membrane |
| Implant survival, RCT (n=101) | 100/101 (≈99%) — 1 failure, in the membrane group |
| Implant survival, case–control (n=21) | 100% at 6–12 months |
| Membrane perforation, clinical bone lid cases | 6.7% (1/15), successfully repaired |
| Risk of bias, animal studies (SYRCLE) | Low risk in only 2/5 studies |
| Risk of bias, RCT (RoB 2) | Overall "some concerns" |
