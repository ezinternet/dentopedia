## Three-line Summary
Bayesian network meta-analysis (PRISMA-NMA, PROSPERO CRD42019137740) of 34 RCTs (842 two-stage lateral maxillary sinus augmentations, crestal core biopsy after 5-8 months) ranking 28 biomaterial subgroups by histomorphometric new bone percentage (NB%).
Only 2 of 378 pairwise comparisons were significant (bovine xenograft + bone marrow concentrate [BMC] superior to allograft and to biodegradable copolymer membrane); SUCRA ranked bovine + BMC first (81%), bovine + PRP second (77%), bioactive glass + autologous bone 1:1, nanocrystalline HA in silica gel and bioactive glass 70% each, and autologous bone alone twelfth (57%).
Autologous bone alone was not confirmed as the gold standard at 5-8 months and composites reduce the autograft volume needed, but certainty was low to very low (9 of 34 trials at high risk of bias, 12 subgroups below the optimal information size).

## Summary
This Bayesian network meta-analysis pooled 34 RCTs (842 maxillary sinus augmentations) of two-stage lateral-window grafting with crestal core-biopsy histomorphometry after a normal 5-8 month healing period, comparing 28 biomaterial subgroups defined by material and processing rather than broad class. Only two of 378 comparisons were significant, both favoring bovine xenograft + bone marrow concentrate (over allograft and over a biodegradable copolymer membrane). By SUCRA, bovine + BMC (81%) and bovine + PRP (77%) led, while autologous bone alone ranked twelfth (57%), so the hypothesis that autologous bone yields the highest new bone formation was rejected. The authors conclude that autologous bone is not demonstrably superior at 5-8 months and that biomaterial + autologous bone or cell-concentrate composites can reduce the autograft required, but all comparisons were rated low to very low certainty.

## Key Contributions
- Ranks 28 graft subgroups (by material, processing and composite recipe) in a single Bayesian network for NB% at 5-8 months.
- Shows autologous bone alone is not the top-ranked graft (12th, SUCRA 57%) in this healing window.
- Identifies bovine xenograft + BMC (81%) and bovine + PRP (77%) as the highest-ranked composites, flagged as small-n nodes.
- Documents that early (<5 mo) and late (>8 mo) healing windows cannot be network-pooled with current RCTs.

## Methodology
Search of Cochrane CENTRAL, EBSCO, Embase, MEDLINE and Web of Science to 1 October 2019; 69 RCTs met criteria and 34 (5-8 month healing, connected network) were analyzed. Eligibility: two-stage lateral approach, crestal core biopsy, delayed implant placement; one-stage, non-lateral and combined vertical/horizontal augmentation excluded. Outcome NB% as mean difference (95% credible interval) under a Bayesian random-effects model (R gemtc/JAGS), SUCRA ranking, node-splitting consistency, Egger test, Cochrane RoB, GRADE elements; optimal information size 11 MSAs per group.
