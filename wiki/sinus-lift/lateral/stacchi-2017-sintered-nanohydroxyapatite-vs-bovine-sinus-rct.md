## Three-line Summary

Multicenter split-mouth superiority RCT (4 centers, NCT03077867, CONSORT) in 28 patients with severe bilateral posterior maxillary atrophy (residual crest 2.03 ± 0.75 mm) comparing pure sintered nanohydroxyapatite (NHA) with anorganic bovine bone (ABB) in lateral sinus floor elevation — 52 trephine biopsies at 6 months and 107 implants followed 12 months after loading.

No significant difference in any histomorphometric parameter: vital bone 34.9 ± 15% vs 38.5 ± 17% (p = 0.428), marrow spaces 44.5 ± 18% vs 43.5 ± 23% (p = 0.866), residual graft 20.6 ± 13% vs 22.3 ± 12% (p = 0.638); implant survival in the NHA group was 96.4% after 12 months of loading (cumulative failure 2.8%; 2/55 test vs 1/52 control, p = 0.32).

The prespecified null hypothesis was therefore **accepted** — this synthetic nano-HA is statistically indistinguishable from the bovine xenograft at 6 months, so the material choice can rest on non-efficacy grounds (xenograft refusal, prion/immune concerns, cost) rather than on a histological advantage; the 12-month horizon and the unpaired analysis of paired data are the key caveats.

## 세줄요약

다기관 스플릿마우스 우월성 무작위 대조 시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 이탈리아 4개 센터, NCT03077867): 중증 양측 상악 후방 위축(잔존골높이 2.03 ± 0.75 mm) 환자 28명에서 소결 나노수산화인회석 (Sintered Nanohydroxyapatite, NHA)과 무기질 우골 (Anorganic Bovine Bone, ABB; Bio-Oss)을 좌우 상악동에 각각 이식 — 6개월 코어 생검 52개, 임플란트 107개를 부하 12개월까지 추적.

모든 조직형태계측 항목에서 유의차 없음: 신생골 (New Bone, NB) 34.9 ± 15% vs 38.5 ± 17% (p = 0.428), 골수강 44.5 ± 18% vs 43.5 ± 23% (p = 0.866), 잔류이식재 (Residual Graft) 20.6 ± 13% vs 22.3 ± 12% (p = 0.638); NHA군 임플란트 생존율은 부하 12개월에 96.4%(누적 실패 2.8%; 시험군 2/55 vs 대조군 1/52, p = 0.32).

따라서 사전 설정한 영가설 (null hypothesis)이 **채택**되었다 — 6개월 시점에서 이 합성 나노수산화인회석은 우골 이종골과 통계적으로 구분되지 않으므로, 재료 선택은 조직학적 우위가 아니라 이종골 거부감·프리온/면역 우려·비용 같은 비-효능 근거로 정당화된다 (단, 12개월 추적과 쌍체 설계에 비쌍체 검정을 쓴 점이 핵심 한계).

## Summary

This multicenter (four Italian centers) split-mouth superiority RCT randomized the right and left sinus of 28 patients with severe bilateral posterior maxillary atrophy (crestal height < 3 mm, Cawood & Howell V–VI) to pure sintered nanohydroxyapatite (NHA, Fisiograft Bone Granular; test) or anorganic bovine bone (ABB/Bio-Oss; active control), with allocation concealed in sealed envelopes opened only after Schneiderian membrane elevation. Both sinuses were grafted in the same session through a piezoelectric lateral window. At 6 months, 52 trephine core biopsies (26 per group) were harvested during implant bed preparation from 26 patients — two patients dropped out after three membrane perforations — and 107 implants were placed and submerged. Histomorphometry found no statistically significant difference in vital bone (34.9 ± 15% NHA vs 38.5 ± 17% ABB, p = 0.428), marrow spaces (44.5 ± 18% vs 43.5 ± 23%, p = 0.866) or residual graft material (20.6 ± 13% vs 22.3 ± 12%, p = 0.638); absolute areas were likewise non-significant. Three implants failed before abutment connection (2.8% cumulative; 3.6% test vs 1.9% control, p = 0.32), were replaced with wider fixtures, and implant survival in the NHA group reached 96.4% after 12 months of prosthetic loading with no biological or mechanical complications.

The authors explicitly state that the null hypothesis of no difference was **accepted**: at 6 months the osteoconductive behaviour of this nano-HA alloplast is clinically and statistically comparable to the bovine xenograft, with a numerically (not significantly) lower vital-bone share but also a numerically lower residual-graft share. Their clinical argument is deliberately non-superiority: a synthetic material removes xenograft-specific objections — prion transmission risk, host immune reaction, and patient refusal on religious or dietary grounds (15% would never accept a xenograft, 18% only as a last resort in the cited survey). The main limitations are the short horizon (the planned 5-year follow-up is not reported here) and an analysis that used unpaired tests on paired split-mouth data.

## Key Contributions

- **First split-mouth histological comparison of *pure sintered* NHA against ABB** in lateral sinus augmentation — previous nanocrystalline-HA evidence used HA embedded in a porous silica-gel matrix, a different material construct.
- Within-patient control removes inter-individual healing variability, the dominant confounder in between-patient graft comparisons.
- Pairs a histological endpoint (6-month histomorphometry) with a clinical endpoint (implant survival after 12 months of loading) in the same cohort — a combination most sinus histology studies lack.
- Reports a **powered null result** (24 sinuses/group for effect size 0.6, α = 0.05, power 80%) rather than a superiority claim — the correct evidential shape for the alloplast-vs-xenograft question.
- Adds a mechanistic observation the authors interpret as surface osteoinductivity: osteocytes were more numerous near NHA particle surfaces, linked to EGFR/AKT/ERK 1/2 activation by NHA paste (Kasai et al.).
- Makes the patient-preference argument explicit and quantified, reframing material choice as a preference/safety trade-off rather than an efficacy contest.

## Methodology

- **Design**: multicenter, split-mouth, parallel-group superiority RCT; CONSORT reporting; GCP and Declaration of Helsinki (Fortaleza 2013); registered NCT03077867; ethics approval Comitato Etico Calabria — Sezione Area Nord.
- **Population**: adults ≥ 18 y, bilateral crestal height < 3 mm (Cawood & Howell V–VI), needing staged lateral sinus floor elevation for fixed rehabilitation. Exclusions: recent MI, uncontrolled coagulopathy, HbA1c > 7.5%, head/neck radiotherapy < 24 months, immunocompromise, IV bisphosphonates, bovine collagen allergy, untreated periodontitis, sinus pathology, psychiatric/substance issues, and **intraoperative membrane perforation**.
- **Randomization / allocation concealment**: computer-generated balanced permuted blocks (randomization.com) assigning right/left sinus; numbered sealed opaque envelopes opened after membrane elevation, so surgeons could not anticipate allocation.
- **Surgery**: articaine 4% + epinephrine 1:100,000; full-thickness flap; piezoelectric lateral window (Piezosurgery Touch / Piezotome); membrane elevation with ultrasonic inserts and curettes; Valsalva integrity check; graft; Bio-Gide collagen membrane fixed with two pins; Sentineri suturing with PTFE. Contralateral sinus grafted in the same session with the other material. Amoxicillin 1 g BID × 6 days (clarithromycin if allergic) + ibuprofen 600 mg PRN.
- **Biopsy and implants**: 6 months post-grafting, 3.5 mm trephine core during implant bed preparation under surgical-template guidance; BnxEvo implants placed into the harvest sites, submerged 4 months, then loaded with screwed metal-ceramic prostheses and followed 12 months.
- **Histology**: cold 5% glucose rinse (278 mOsm/L) → 10% phosphate-buffered formalin (pH 7.2) → graded alcohol dehydration at 4 °C (7 days) + absolute alcohol (2 days) → resin pre-infiltration and embedding (Technovit 7200 VLC, vacuum, ~20 days) → 50 µm diamond sectioning → grinding to 30 ± 10 µm → Toluidine Blue/Azure II with acid fuchsin or Pironine G; brightfield and circularly polarized light microscopy.
- **Histomorphometry**: Image-Pro Plus 6.0 with per-image "Calibration Wizard"; measured sample area, vital bone area and %, marrow space area and %, residual graft area and %; two readings per index, repeated if the difference exceeded 5% (intraexaminer control). Single trained examiner (TT); blinding to allocation not reported.
- **Statistics**: SigmaStat 3.5; normality and equal-variance testing; unpaired t-test (parametric) or Mann–Whitney U (nonparametric); α = 0.05.
- **Sample size**: 24 sinuses per group to detect effect size 0.6 in new bone % (α = 0.05, power 80%, DSS Research); 7 patients × 4 centers = 56 sinuses planned to cover dropouts.

**Implant outcomes**

| Outcome | NHA (test) | ABB (control) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implants | 55 | 52 | — |
| Failures at abutment connection | 2 (3.6%) | 1 (1.9%) | 0.32 |
| Cumulative failure rate | 3/107 = 2.8% | — | — |
| Survival after 12 months of loading | 96.4% | 1 failure / 52 | NS |
| Complications at 12 months | none | none | — |

**Context values cited by the authors**: their 6-month new bone (34.9–38.5%) sits inside the synthetic-HA range of 32–38.5% and near autogenous-bone-only sinus grafting (36.8–41%); their survival (96.4%) sits near SR estimates of 97.2–97.4% (autogenous bone only) and 98.2–98.6% (bone substitutes).

**Reading the bottom line**: the direction of the point estimates is mildly pro-ABB for vital bone (+3.6 percentage points) and mildly pro-NHA for residual graft (−1.7 points), but both differences are well inside the noise of a 52-biopsy sample with SDs of 12–17 points. The trial is a *non-difference* result, not a proof of equivalence, and the paired design was analysed unpaired — the true confidence interval on the difference is not reported.
