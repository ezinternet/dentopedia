## One-line Summary
Systematic review of 18 studies: autograft > xenograft ≥ allograft > alloplast for new bone formation; all graft types achieve >90% implant survival; alloplasts inferior in volumetric stability but valid alternative when biologics are contraindicated.

## 한줄요약

## Summary
This PRISMA systematic review analyzed 18 studies (13 RCTs, 5 non-randomized) published from 2010–2025 comparing alloplastic grafts (BCP, β-TCP, HA, bioactive glass) against biologic grafts (autograft, allograft, xenograft) in maxillary sinus floor augmentation. Autogenous bone consistently produced the highest new bone formation (NBF 36.8–41.7%), driven by intrinsic osteogenic and osteoinductive properties. Xenografts (DBBM) showed superior volumetric stability (103%) compared to alloplasts (66%) and slower resorption. All graft types maintained implant survival >90%, suggesting osseointegration outcomes are not the primary discriminating variable. Alloplasts demonstrated adequate bone formation sufficient for implant placement, with the main trade-off being slower remodeling and greater residual graft content. Evidence certainty was rated low (GRADE) due to heterogeneity and study size limitations.

## Key Contributions
- Establishes the NBF hierarchy: autograft (41.7%) > allograft (35.4%) > alloplast (30.3%) > xenograft (24.9% — per Schmitt, but DBBM compensates with scaffold stability)
- Documents xenograft volumetric stability advantage: 103% vs alloplast 66% (Schmitt)
- Confirms that implant survival >90% across all graft types renders survival an insufficient discriminator of material choice
- Identifies alloplastics as appropriate when donor-site morbidity, immunological risk, or disease transmission is a concern
- Highlights that alloplast+autograft composites may not enhance outcomes vs autograft alone

## Methodology
- SR design: PRISMA; registered in PROSPERO (CRD420261351944)
- Search: 8 databases (PubMed, Scopus, WoS, Embase, EBSCO, Google Scholar, ClinicalTrials.gov, Cochrane), Jan 2010–Dec 2025
- Included: 18 studies (13 RCTs + 5 non-randomized); sample sizes 5–56 patients; follow-up 5 months–2 years
- Technique: predominantly lateral window sinus lift
- Outcomes: NBF %, residual graft %, volumetric stability, bone height gain, implant survival, complications
- Risk of bias: RoB 2.0 (RCTs); ROBINS I V2 (cohorts); GRADE evidence grading

## Results
**New bone formation (NBF):**
| Graft type | NBF % (Schmitt) | NBF % (Danesh-Sani) |
|---|---|---|
| Autograft | 41.7% | 36.8% |
| Allograft (FDBA) | 35.4% | — |
| Alloplast (BCP) | 30.3% | 28.2% |
| Xenograft (DBBM) | 24.9% | — |

**Residual graft content:**
- BCP vs FDBA: 25.4% vs 12.5% (Kolerman) — alloplast slower resorption
- Alloplast vs autograft: 32.9% vs 4.8% (Danesh-Sani)

**Volumetric stability** (Schmitt): xenograft 103% > allograft 112% ≈ alloplast 66% (alloplast significantly inferior)

**Implant survival:**
- Lindgren: alloplast = xenograft = 96.8% (survival); success rates: xenograft 95.7% vs alloplast 91.7%
- Mordenfeld: alloplast 91.7% = xenograft 91.3%; marginal bone loss lower with xenograft (1.0 vs 1.4 mm)
- Onișor-Gligor: alloplast failure 7.7% vs autograft 1.9%
- Overall: all >90% survival; biologic grafts may confer marginal advantage in long-term success

**GRADE certainty:** Low across all outcomes for alloplastic grafts

## Three-line Summary

- Systematic review (PRISMA, PROSPERO; 18 studies — 13 RCTs + 5 cohorts; sample sizes 5–56 patients; follow-up 5 months–2 years) comparing alloplastic grafts (BCP, β-TCP, HA, bioactive glass) versus biologic grafts (autograft, allograft, xenograft) in maxillary sinus floor augmentation.
- Autograft yielded the highest new bone formation (NBF 41.7%), followed by allograft (35.4%), alloplast (30.3%), and xenograft (24.9%); xenograft showed superior volumetric stability (103% vs alloplast 66%); all graft types achieved >90% implant survival (GRADE low certainty).
- Alloplastic grafts are a valid clinical alternative when donor-site morbidity or infection risk precludes biologics, but carry inferior regenerative potential and volumetric stability; implant survival alone is not a sufficient discriminator of material choice.

## 세줄요약

- 체계적 문헌 고찰(Systematic Review, SR; PRISMA, PROSPERO; 18편 — 무작위 대조 시험(RCT) 13편 + 코호트 5편; 추적 5개월–2년): 합성골이식재(Alloplast: BCP·β-TCP·HA·생활성 유리) vs 생물학적 이식재(자가골·동종골·이종골) 비교, 상악동저 거상술(Maxillary Sinus Floor Augmentation, MSFA) 맥락.
- 자가골이 신생골 형성(New Bone Formation, NBF) 최고(41.7%), 이어 동종골(35.4%)·합성골(30.3%)·이종골(24.9%); 이종골이 부피 안정성 가장 우수(103% vs 합성골 66%); 전 이식재군 임플란트 생존율 90% 이상(GRADE 낮음).
- 합성골이식재는 공여부 이환율 또는 감염 위험이 있을 때 유효 대안이나, 재생 잠재력과 부피 안정성이 열등; 임플란트 생존율 단독으로는 재료 선택의 결정 인자로 불충분.
