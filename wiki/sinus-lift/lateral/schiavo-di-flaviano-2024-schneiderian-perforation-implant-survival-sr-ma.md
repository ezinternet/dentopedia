## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PubMed/Scopus/Web of Science, May 2024; 10 qualitative, 7 meta-analysis; lateral window approach only; ≥6-month follow-up post-prosthesis) comparing implant survival in perforated vs non-perforated Schneiderian membranes after lateral sinus lift.

Implant survival rate: 97.1% in 480 perforated sinuses (1,044 implants) vs 97.7% in 1,224 non-perforated sinuses (2,725 implants); OR = 0.78 (95% CI 0.49–2.23; p = 0.28); I² = 0% — no significant difference; one-stage vs two-stage placement also showed no difference in perforated sinuses.

Schneiderian membrane perforation, when repaired, does not negatively influence implant survival and is not a contraindication to implant placement; collagen membrane remains the most-used repair material.

천공군 480증례 1,044개 임플란트 생존율 97.1% vs 비천공군 1,224증례 2,725개 임플란트 생존율 97.7%; 교차비(Odds Ratio, OR) = 0.78 (95% CI 0.49–2.23; p = 0.28); 이질성(I²) = 0% — 유의한 차이 없음; 동시 vs 지연 임플란트 식립도 천공군에서 차이 없음 (RR = 1.00).

## Summary

This 2024 SR+MA directly addresses the clinical question of whether Schneiderian membrane perforation during lateral window sinus lift compromises implant outcomes. With 10 qualitative studies and 7 in the meta-analysis (1,666 patients, 5,052 implants), the analysis found an implant survival rate of 97.1% in perforated sinuses vs 97.7% in non-perforated sinuses — a non-significant difference (OR = 0.78, p = 0.28) with zero heterogeneity (I² = 0%). The mean perforation incidence was 29.42%. Collagen membranes were the most-used repair material (7/10 studies), with sutures, PRF, Bichat fat pad, oxidized cellulose, and block grafts also described. A subgroup analysis of one-stage vs two-stage implant placement showed no survival difference in either perforated or non-perforated sinuses. These findings confirm the earlier Diaz-Olivares 2021 SR+MA and support a standardized clinical approach: repair the perforation and proceed.

## Key Contributions

- Updated meta-analysis with OR analysis and zero heterogeneity (I² = 0%) — highest statistical confidence to date
- Confirms diaz-olivares-2021 with broader inclusion (10 qualitative studies, lateral window only)
- First subgroup analysis of one-stage vs two-stage in perforated sinuses: no difference (RR = 1.00)
- Mean perforation incidence 29.42% — consistent with reported 7–56% range
- Summary of all documented repair materials and techniques across included studies

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR+MA (PRISMA)
- **Search**: PubMed/Scopus/Web of Science, May 2024
- **Inclusion**: In vivo human; lateral window + graft; perforation occurred + repair described; ≥6-month follow-up post-prosthesis
- **n**: 10 qualitative, 7 meta-analysis; 1,666 patients; 2,229 sinus lifts; 5,052 implants
- **Outcome**: Implant survival rate (implant present and functional at follow-up)
- **Statistics**: Binary random effects; OR with 95% CI; Review Manager 5.4
- **Risk of bias**: Newcastle–Ottawa Scale; 2 studies scored 7 stars, 4 scored 6 stars (low-moderate risk)

- I² = 0% (no heterogeneity; p = 0.42)
- One-stage vs two-stage in perforated: RR = 1.00 (CI 0.94–1.07; p = 0.95)
