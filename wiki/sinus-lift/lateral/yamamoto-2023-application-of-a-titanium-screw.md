## Three-line Summary

Single case report: during lateral-window sinus floor elevation under local anesthesia, profuse pulsatile hemorrhage from a relatively large PSAA branch at the inner sinus wall was not controlled by vasoconstrictor-soaked gauze, absorbable hemostat, bone wax, or electrocautery.

After a sterile gutta percha point #100 provisionally occluded the bone channel and stopped bleeding, a titanium screw (Le Forte System, 1.4 mm × 6.0 mm) was inserted, achieving immediate complete hemostasis.

The screw was removed at 5 months without complications and the implant was placed; re-bleeding risk is considered low because the channel fills with new bone during healing.

## Summary

A technical note describing a single case of uncontrolled pulsatile hemorrhage from a relatively large posterior superior alveolar artery (PSAA) branch at the inner aspect of the maxillary sinus wall during lateral-window sinus floor elevation. Standard hemostatic measures—vasoconstrictor-soaked gauze compression, absorbable hemostat, bone wax, and electrocautery—were all ineffective. After provisional occlusion with a sterile gutta percha point #100 achieved hemostasis, a titanium screw (Le Forte System, 1.4 mm × 6.0 mm) was inserted into the bone channel, stopping bleeding immediately. Artificial bone grafting followed; the screw was removed at 5 months without complications and the implant was placed.

## Key Contributions

- Rescue technique for uncontrolled PSAA hemorrhage from a bone channel: physical occlusion with a titanium screw after failed routine hemostasis.
- Documents failure of routine measures for a relatively large arterial branch.
- Supports safety of screw removal at ~5 months due to new bone filling the channel.

## Methodology

Single case report (technical note); dental clinic under local anesthesia. Preoperative CT for sinus and intraosseous vessel evaluation; lateral approach sinus floor elevation with piezoelectric diamond insert OT5A. Hemostasis sequence attempted: vasoconstrictor (1:80,000)-soaked gauze, 10 min repeated compression, absorbable hemostat, bone wax, electrocautery — all ineffective. Rescue: distal bone edge removed for visualization; firm pressure; provisional gutta percha point #100; titanium screw (1.4 mm × 6.0 mm). Follow-up: Bio-Oss® graft; screw removed at 5 months; implant placed.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Hemorrhage source | Relatively large PSAA branch at inner sinus wall |
| Routine hemostasis | Ineffective (gauze, hemostat, bone wax, electrocautery) |
| Provisional occlusion | Gutta percha point #100 → complete hemostasis |
| Definitive occlusion | Titanium screw 1.4 mm × 6.0 mm → immediate complete hemostasis |
| Screw removal | 5 months, no complications; implant placed |
