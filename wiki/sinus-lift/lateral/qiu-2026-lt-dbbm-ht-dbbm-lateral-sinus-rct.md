## One-line Summary
Pilot multicenter RCT (n=36, 6-month): LT-DBBM (Bio-Oss®, 300°C) vs HT-DBBM (Gegreen®, high-temp calcination) in LSFE — no significant differences in bone height, volume, resorption rate, or Micro-CT bone microarchitecture; sintering temperature does not affect 6-month clinical outcomes.

## 한줄요약
파일럿 다기관 RCT(n=36, 6개월): 저온소결 DBBM(Bio-Oss®)과 고온소결 DBBM(Gegreen®)은 골높이·골부피·흡수율·Micro-CT 미세구조 모두 유의차 없음 — 소결 온도가 임상 결과에 영향 없음.

## Summary
This pilot RCT (interim analysis of a planned 4-group multicenter trial) compared two DBBM types with different sintering temperatures in lateral window sinus floor elevation: LT-DBBM (Bio-Oss®, sintered at 300°C) and HT-DBBM (Gegreen®, two-step high-temperature calcination at 400–800°C + 800–1200°C). After 6 months and first-stage implant placement with bone core biopsy, comprehensive analysis (3D CBCT volumetry via MIMICS, Micro-CT, histology) showed no significant differences between groups in any measured parameter. Both materials provided sufficient bone height (14.35 vs 14.89mm at T2) and equivalent Micro-CT bone microarchitecture (BV/TV ~13.2–13.9%). Low resorption rates were observed in both (8.32% vs 10.44% volume reduction). Despite theoretical physicochemical differences between LT and HT sintering, clinical outcomes at 6 months were equivalent.

## Key Contributions
- First RCT directly comparing LT-DBBM (Bio-Oss®) vs HT-DBBM (Gegreen®) in LSFE with 3D volumetric + Micro-CT analysis
- Clinical equivalence across all metrics: bone height gain, volume, resorption rate, BV/TV, Tb.Th, Tb.N, Tb.Sp
- Bone height maintained at ~14–15mm at 6 months in both groups — sufficient for implant placement
- Very low resorption rates: 8–10% volume loss at 6 months (minimal)
- Suggests DBBM source country and HT manufacturing do not compromise clinical outcomes

## Methodology
- Design: Pilot multicenter RCT (interim analysis; original: 4-group trial)
- n=36: LT-DBBM n=20 (Bio-Oss®), HT-DBBM n=16 (Gegreen®)
- Particle size: 0.25–1.0mm (both groups matched)
- Technique: Lateral window sinus floor elevation + collagen membrane
- Time points: T0 (preop) → T1 (immediate postop) → T2 (6 months)
- Imaging: CBCT → DICOM → MIMICS 21.0 (3D STL; bone height at implant site; bone volume)
- Micro-CT: BV/TV, Tb.Th, Tb.N, Tb.Sp from bone column (trephine at implant placement)
- Histology: H&E, 4μm sections

## Results
**Bone height (CBCT):** No significant differences
| | LT-DBBM (Bio-Oss®) | HT-DBBM (Gegreen®) |
|---|---|---|
| ΔH1 immediate gain | 12.55 ± 0.42 mm | 12.46 ± 0.59 mm |
| H2 total at 6 months | 14.35 ± 0.44 mm | 14.89 ± 0.65 mm |
| ΔH resorption T1→T2 | -0.96 ± 0.21 mm | -1.05 ± 0.28 mm |

**Bone volume resorption rate:** LT 8.32±3.02% vs HT 10.44±4.63% (NS)

**Micro-CT bone microarchitecture:** All NS
| | LT-DBBM | HT-DBBM |
|---|---|---|
| BV/TV | 13.21 ± 2.75% | 13.92 ± 3.07% |
| Tb.Th (mm) | 0.044 ± 0.008 | 0.061 ± 0.011 |
| Tb.N | 4.76 ± 1.52 | 3.52 ± 1.31 |
| Tb.Sp | 0.41 ± 0.10 | 0.40 ± 0.08 |

**Histology:** Both groups: newly formed mature bone with blood vessels around particles, no adverse inflammation.
