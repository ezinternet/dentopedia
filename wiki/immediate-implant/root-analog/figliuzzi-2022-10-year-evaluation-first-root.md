## Three-line Summary

Single case report (n=1), 50-year-old healthy female with a fractured maxillary second premolar, followed for 10 years after immediate post-extraction placement of a CT/CAD-CAM/DMLS-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V root analogue implant (RAI).

At the 10-year follow-up, crestal bone resorption was 0 mm, probing pocket depth was within normal range (2.5 mm), no bleeding on probing, no gingival recession, no increased mobility, and the patient reported full functional/esthetic satisfaction throughout.

This is the longest-documented follow-up in the RAI literature and supports long-term dimensional stability of additively-manufactured RAIs, but as a single case (n=1) it requires confirmation from larger series before informing general clinical practice.

10년 추적관찰 시점에서 치조정골 흡수는 0mm, 탐침깊이(Probing Pocket Depth, PPD)는 정상범위(2.5mm), 탐침시출혈(Bleeding On Probing, BOP) 없음, 치은퇴축 없음, 동요도 증가 없이 전 기간 동안 기능적·심미적으로 환자 만족도가 높았다.

## Summary

This case report documents the longest published follow-up (10 years) of a custom root analogue implant (RAI) fabricated from CT-derived 3D models using CAD/CAM design and direct metal laser sintering (DLMS/DMLS) in Ti-6Al-4V. The implant was immediately placed into the intact post-extraction socket of a fractured maxillary second premolar in a healthy 50-year-old female, with platform switching and a metal-ceramic restoration cemented with glass ionomer cement. Across recall visits at 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, and 10 years, the implant showed 0 mm radiographic crestal bone resorption, normal probing pocket depth (2.5 mm), no bleeding on probing, no recession, and no mobility increase, alongside sustained patient satisfaction. As a single case, the findings are hypothesis-generating rather than confirmatory, but they serve as a historical proof-of-concept anchor for additive-manufacturing-based RAI technology within the broader RAI evidence base.

## Key Contributions

- Longest documented clinical follow-up (10 years) in the root analogue implant (RAI) literature.
- Demonstrates sustained dimensional stability (0 mm crestal bone resorption) of a DLMS-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V RAI placed immediately into a post-extraction socket without screw fixation.
- Historical anchor case: reportedly the first human application of a CT-scan-derived, CAD/CAM-designed, DLMS-manufactured RAI.
- Reinforces the rationale that a socket-matched, porosity-controlled additive-manufacturing implant surface may support long-term osseointegration comparable to (or better than) conventional threaded implants — though based on n=1.

## Methodology

- **Design/fabrication**: CT scan of the pre-extraction root/alveolus → 3D processing → CAD/CAM-designed RAI → DLMS (direct laser metal sintering) fabrication in Ti-6Al-4V, with platform switching.
- **Patient**: single healthy 50-year-old female, fractured maxillary second premolar, no systemic disease, non-smoker.
- **Surgical protocol**: atraumatic, conservative extraction preserving alveolar walls and periosteal vascularization; RAI press-fit (not screwed) into the intact socket, respecting biological width; metal-ceramic crown cemented with GC Fuji I.
- **Follow-up**: clinical (observation, palpation, percussion, probing) and radiographic (periapical parallel-technique X-ray with customized resin jigs) assessment at T0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, and 10 years.
- **Outcome measures**: probing pocket depth (PPD), bleeding on probing (BOP), gingival recession, mobility, radiographic crestal bone level (bone crest base to implant shoulder distance).
