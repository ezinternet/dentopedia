## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (9 cohort studies, n=2281 sockets): infected vs non-infected extraction sockets in immediate implant placement, up to 5-year follow-up.

No significant difference in implant survival (RR=0.99; 97.64% vs 98.57%); MBL, MGL, PD, mBI all comparable; only WKG showed slight but significant reduction in infected sockets.

Infected sockets are not a contraindication to immediate implantation after thorough debridement and antibiotic prophylaxis; keratinized tissue width should be monitored.

## Summary

Based on articles retrieved from PubMed, this SR+MA ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.5037/jomr.2020.11201)) analyzed 9 cohort studies (2281 sockets: 933 infected, 1348 non-infected) to assess whether immediate implant placement (IIP) in infected extraction sockets is comparable to non-infected sockets. Survival rates were statistically equivalent (RR=0.99, P=0.08). All secondary parameters (MBL, MGL, PD, mBI) showed no significant between-group differences across 1-, 2-, and 3+-year follow-up intervals. The single exception was the width of keratinized gingiva (WKG), which was slightly but significantly lower in infected-socket groups at all time points. All included studies performed granulation tissue removal and most used antibiotic prophylaxis. No RCTs were available — all evidence derived from retrospective or prospective cohort studies, limiting the strength of conclusions.

## Key Contributions

- Confirmed survival equivalence (RR=0.99) across 2281 sockets from 9 studies
- First SR+MA to perform time-stratified meta-analysis (1yr/2yr/3+yr) for MBL, MGL, PD, mBI, WKG
- Identified WKG as the clinically significant differentiating parameter — important for aesthetic zone planning
- Established that all included protocols shared: granulation tissue removal + antibiotic prophylaxis (pre- and/or post-op)

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR+MA, PRISMA-compliant; Joanna Briggs Institute bias tool
- **Search**: PubMed, ScienceDirect, ISI Web of Knowledge, Google Scholar; Jan 2010–Feb 2020
- **Included**: 9 studies (3 retrospective, 6 prospective); 1346 patients, 2281 sockets
- **Model**: Mantel-Haenszel fixed-effects (survival); inverse variance (continuous); random-effects if I²>75%
- **Follow-up**: minimum 6 months; maximum 93 months
