## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis of 18 RCTs (8549 screened, 72 full-text assessed) on timing of implant placement (immediate/early/delayed) and loading (immediate/early/conventional/delayed) in single-tooth implants, evaluated using soft-tissue and esthetic indices (midfacial recession, papilla level, Pink Esthetic Score, White Esthetic Score, Papilla Index).

No statistically significant differences found in esthetic outcomes between placement-timing groups (immediate vs. early vs. delayed) at 1-, 2-, or 10-year follow-up; loading timing (immediate vs. conventional loading) showed no impact on midfacial mucosal margin or esthetic indices in short- and medium-term follow-up.

Both immediate and early implant placement protocols demonstrated stable esthetic treatment outcomes over time; timing of loading protocol does not appear to influence esthetic outcomes in the available evidence.

## 세줄요약

단일치아 임플란트에서 식립 타이밍(즉시/조기/지연)과 부하 타이밍(즉시/조기/종래/지연)이 심미 결과에 미치는 영향을 평가한 체계적고찰 및 메타분석(Systematic Review and Meta-analysis, SR+MA); RCT 18편 포함(8,549편 선별, 72편 전문평가), 중안모 치은퇴축(Midfacial Recession), 유두 높이(Papilla Level), 분홍 심미 점수(Pink Esthetic Score, PES), 백색 심미 점수(White Esthetic Score, WES), 유두 지수(Papilla Index) 등 심미 지표 사용.

식립 타이밍 그룹 간(즉시 vs. 조기 vs. 지연) 1·2·10년 추적에서 심미 결과 유의차 없음; 부하 타이밍(즉시부하 vs. 종래부하)도 중안모 점막 변연 위치 및 심미 지표에 단기·중기적으로 영향 없음.

즉시 및 조기 식립 프로토콜 모두 장기적으로 안정적인 심미 결과를 보였으며, 현재 근거상 부하 프로토콜의 타이밍은 심미 결과에 영향을 주지 않는 것으로 보인다.

## Summary

This ITI Consensus Conference companion systematic review and meta-analysis (Clin Oral Implants Res 2021;32(Suppl 21):28–55) asked whether esthetic outcomes of single-tooth implants differ by the timing of implant placement relative to extraction (immediate/early/delayed) and by the timing of prosthetic loading (immediate/early/conventional/delayed). Four electronic databases were searched to June 2020, screening 8549 records down to 72 full-text assessments and 18 included RCTs (9 addressing placement timing, 9 addressing loading timing), evaluated against objective esthetic measures — midfacial recession, papilla level, Pink Esthetic Score (PES), White Esthetic Score (WES), and Papilla Index. No statistically significant differences emerged between placement-timing groups at 1, 2, or 10 years, nor between loading-timing groups in short- or medium-term follow-up, for any esthetic measure. The authors conclude that, on current evidence, esthetic outcome is not a reason to prefer one placement or loading timing protocol over another in single-tooth implants — though heterogeneous outcome reporting and small per-comparison trial counts limit the strength of that null finding.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to jointly evaluate BOTH placement timing (immediate/early/delayed) AND loading timing (immediate/early/conventional/delayed) against esthetic-specific outcome measures in single-tooth implants — the esthetic-outcome counterpart to survival/bone-level timing reviews.
- Restricts inclusion to trials reporting objective esthetic measures (midfacial recession, papilla level, PES, WES, Papilla Index) rather than survival or radiographic bone level alone.
- Finds no significant esthetic penalty for immediate vs. early vs. delayed placement at 1, 2, or 10 years — challenging the assumption that a longer healing interval before placement improves soft-tissue stability.
- Finds loading protocol (immediate vs. conventional) does not independently drive esthetic outcome differences in single-tooth restorations.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review with meta-analysis (SR+MA); Cochrane Risk of Bias Tool applied.
- **Search**: Four electronic databases searched to June 2020.
- **Screening**: 8549 records identified → 72 full-text assessed → 18 RCTs included (9 evaluating placement timing, 9 evaluating loading timing).
- **Inclusion criteria**: RCTs, minimum 10 patients, ≥1-year follow-up, reporting peri-implant mucosa dimensional change (midfacial recession, papilla level) and/or esthetic indices.
- **Outcomes**: Midfacial recession, papilla level, Pink Esthetic Score (PES), White Esthetic Score (WES), Papilla Index.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Immediate vs. delayed placement (midfacial recession, 1–2y) | Heterogeneous findings; no consistent difference |
| Immediate vs. early placement (recession, papilla level, 1–2y) | No significant difference |
| Immediate vs. conventional loading (midfacial mucosal margin, 1y) | Not statistically significant |
| Pink Esthetic Score (PES) across timing groups | No difference between groups |
| White Esthetic Score (WES) across timing groups | No difference between groups |
| Papilla Index across timing groups | No difference between groups |
| Long-term esthetic stability (1–10y) | Immediate and early placement stable at 1-, 2-, and 10-year follow-up |
