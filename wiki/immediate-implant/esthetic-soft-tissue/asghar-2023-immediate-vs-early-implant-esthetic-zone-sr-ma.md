## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 6 randomised controlled trials comparing immediate implant placement (IIP) versus early implant placement (EIP) protocols in healthy adults requiring single-tooth replacement in the aesthetic zone, with Cochrane ROB-2 risk-of-bias assessment.

No significant difference between IIP and EIP in vertical bone levels (4 studies, 148 patients, MD 0.10, P>0.05) or probing depth (2 studies, 100 patients, MD -0.00, P>0.05); Pink Esthetic Score (PES) was significantly better for EIP versus IIP (P<0.05); implant failure ranged 0–9.3% across studies.

IIP shows clinically comparable bone/periodontal outcomes to EIP but a modest esthetic disadvantage (lower PES) — evidence is limited to 6 RCTs from a condensed evidence-summary format, so long-term confirmatory RCTs are still needed.

## 세줄요약

건강한 성인의 심미부위 단일치 수복에서 즉시식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP)과 조기식립 (Early Implant Placement, EIP)을 비교한 무작위대조시험(RCT) 6편의 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (Systematic Review + Meta-Analysis, SR+MA), Cochrane ROB-2 도구로 비뚤림 위험 평가.

수직 골레벨(4개 연구, 148명, MD 0.10, P>0.05)과 치은열구탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD)(2개 연구, 100명, MD -0.00, P>0.05)는 IIP·EIP 간 유의차 없음; 핑크심미점수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES)는 EIP가 IIP보다 유의하게 우수(P<0.05); 연구 전반 임플란트 실패율 0–9.3%.

골·치주 지표는 IIP와 EIP가 동등하지만 연조직 심미(PES)는 EIP가 근소 우위 — 6편 RCT만 포함된 압축형 근거요약 저널 논문이라 결론은 지시적 수준으로만 해석하고 장기추적 확증 RCT가 필요하다.

## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis, published as a condensed evidence-summary article in Evidence-Based Dentistry, pools 6 randomised controlled trials that directly compare immediate implant placement (IIP) against early implant placement (EIP) — as opposed to the more commonly studied immediate-vs-delayed comparison — for single-tooth replacement in the aesthetic zone. Using Cochrane ROB-2 for quality assessment, the authors found no statistically significant difference between IIP and EIP for vertical bone levels or probing depth, but a statistically significant Pink Esthetic Score (PES) advantage for EIP. Implant failure rates ranged from 0% to 9.3% across the included studies (not pooled). The authors conclude IIP remains clinically viable and broadly comparable to EIP/delayed protocols, while flagging the PES gap and calling for longer-term follow-up research.

## Key Contributions

- Directly isolates the immediate-vs-early (not immediate-vs-delayed) timing comparison specifically within the aesthetic zone, using RCT-only evidence.
- Applies formal Cochrane ROB-2 risk-of-bias assessment to the pooled trials.
- Separates "hard tissue/periodontal equivalence" (bone level, probing depth) from "soft-tissue esthetic outcome" (PES), showing the two do not necessarily track together — IIP and EIP are clinically equivalent on the former but diverge on the latter.

## Methodology

- Systematic review + meta-analysis restricted to RCTs (MEDLINE/OVID, EMBASE/OVID, ISI Web of Science, Cochrane, SCOPUS, Google Scholar).
- Population: healthy adults requiring single-tooth replacement in the aesthetic zone.
- Comparison: IIP protocol vs EIP protocol.
- Risk-of-bias: Cochrane ROB-2.
- Outcomes: vertical bone level (4 studies pooled, n=148), probing depth (2 studies pooled, n=100), Pink Aesthetic Score (PES, narratively significant), implant failure (narratively reported per study, 0–9.3%).

## Results

| Outcome | Studies (n patients) | Effect | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical bone level | 4 studies (148) | MD 0.10 | P > 0.05 (NS) |
| Probing depth | 2 studies (100) | MD -0.00 | P > 0.05 (NS) |
| Pink Esthetic Score (PES) | narrative | EIP > IIP | P < 0.05 (significant) |
| Implant failure | 3 of 6 studies reported failure | 3.84%, 9.3%, 4.45% (remaining studies: 0%) | not pooled |
