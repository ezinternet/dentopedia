## Three-line Summary
PRISMA SR+MA with GRADE assessment of soft-tissue esthetic outcomes (primary: PES; secondary: BoP, PD, papillary recession, midfacial mucosal recession) in anterior maxillary immediate implants with vs without immediate provisionalization (IP).

The IP group trended toward better PES and reduced midfacial mucosal recession; survival was equivalent — findings concordant with Pitman 2022 and Qin 2023.

Immediate provisionalization is recommended when sufficient primary stability exists; the Indian Prosthodontic Society publication gives this guideline regional relevance in South Asian clinical practice.

## 세줄요약
전치부 상악 즉시식립에서 즉시임시치관(IP) 유무에 따른 PES 중심 연조직 심미 결과 PRISMA SR+MA (GRADE 적용); BoP·PD·치유두·midfacial mucosal recession 보조 평가.

IP 군에서 PES 약간 우위·midfacial mucosal recession 감소 경향; 생존율 동등 — Pitman 2022, Qin 2023과 일관된 결론.

1차안정성 충분 시 즉시임시치관 권장; Indian Prosthodontic Society 게재로 남아시아 임상 가이드라인에 영향.

## Summary
PRISMA-compliant SR+MA on PES and secondary soft tissue outcomes (BoP, PD, plaque index, mesial/distal papillary recession, midfacial mucosal recession) of immediately placed implants in anterior maxilla with vs without IP. MEDLINE/Cochrane/Science Direct/GS, English-only, no time limit. Evidence Project RoB tool. GRADE for evidence quality.
