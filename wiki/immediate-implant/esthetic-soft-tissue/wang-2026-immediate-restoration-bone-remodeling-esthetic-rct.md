## Three-line Summary

Parallel-group RCT (n=20, traumatic anterior maxilla requiring single immediate implant placement, IIP) comparing immediate restoration with the patient's native crown stabilized by fiber-reinforced composite (FRC) splints versus conventional delayed restoration.

At 4–5 months, both groups showed 100% implant survival and complete soft-tissue closure; peri-implant bone changes at the implant neck (P=0.878) and 2 mm apically (P=0.667) and mean ISQ (75.6 ± 2.4 vs 74.1 ± 3.4, P=0.271) did not differ significantly.

Clinical implication: a non-loaded FRC-splinted native-crown immediate restoration may provide immediate esthetic rehabilitation after IIP without additional early adverse effects on bone remodeling or osseointegration, but evidence is pilot-scale and short-term.

## Summary

This pilot randomized controlled trial evaluated whether an immediate, non-loaded esthetic restoration using the patient's native crown bonded to adjacent teeth with an FRC splint affects early peri-implant outcomes after immediate implant placement (IIP) in the anterior maxilla. Twenty trauma patients were randomized to the FRC-splinted immediate restoration or delayed restoration. At 4–5 months, CBCT-based labial/peri-implant bone changes and ISQ showed no significant differences between groups, and both groups achieved 100% implant survival with complete soft-tissue closure. The authors concluded that this protocol did not demonstrate additional adverse effects on early bone remodeling or osseointegration and may be an option when conventional immediately loaded provisionalization is constrained by limited primary stability.

## Key Contributions
- Tested a **native-crown + FRC splint** immediate restoration protocol providing esthetics without direct occlusal loading on the implant after IIP.
- Reported early RCT outcomes (bone remodeling, ISQ, survival, soft-tissue closure) versus delayed restoration.
- Proposed a potential pathway to broaden immediate esthetic rehabilitation when primary stability for directly loaded provisionalization is insufficient.

## Methodology
- **Design**: Parallel-group RCT (pilot/exploratory), prospective registration ChiCTR2300068934.
- **Participants**: n=20, maxillary anterior single-tooth trauma requiring IIP.
- **Groups**: Experimental = immediate native-crown restoration stabilized with FRC splint; Control = conventional delayed restoration.
- **Primary outcome**: Labial bone plate/peri-implant bone change on CBCT (baseline to 4–5 months).
- **Secondary outcomes**: Implant survival, ISQ, gingival healing/soft-tissue closure.
- **Follow-up**: 4–5 months postoperatively.
