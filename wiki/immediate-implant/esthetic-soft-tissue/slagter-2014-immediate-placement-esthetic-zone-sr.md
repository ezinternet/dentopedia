## Three-line Summary

Slagter 2014 is a systematic review and pooled regression analysis of 34 studies examining immediate implant placement in the esthetic zone (MEDLINE/EMBASE/CENTRAL through June 2013). One-year survival was 97.1% with mean marginal bone loss of 0.81 mm; pooled regression identified delayed provisionalization (OR 58.03), flap use (OR 19.87), and CTG use (OR 4.56) as independent risk factors for MBL >0.50 mm. Esthetic and patient-satisfaction outcomes were too inconsistently reported for reliable pooled analysis, underscoring the need for standardized reporting protocols.

## 세줄요약

즉시식립 심미구역 34편의 SR+풀링 분석으로 1년 생존율 97.1%, 변연골소실 평균 0.81 mm를 확인했다. 회귀분석에서 지연 가식(OR 58.03)·피판 사용(OR 19.87)·CTG(OR 4.56)이 골소실 >0.50 mm와 유의하게 연관됐다. 심미 결과와 환자 만족도는 보고 불균형으로 신뢰할 수 있는 분석이 불가능해 표준화된 결과 보고가 필요하다.

| Risk Factor | Odds Ratio | 95% CI | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delayed provisionalization | 58.03 | 8.05–418.41 | <0.001 |

| Connective tissue graft (CTG) | 4.56 | 1.72–12.08 | 0.002 |

## Clinical Takeaway

**Immediate provisionalization is strongly protective.** Delayed provisionalization carries a 58-fold increased risk of MBL >0.50 mm — the single strongest modifiable risk factor identified. Where primary stability allows (insertion torque ≥35 Ncm, ISQ ≥70), immediate provisional placement is the preferred protocol in the esthetic zone.

**Flapless surgery reduces bone loss risk.** Flap use carries a nearly 20-fold increased odds of MBL >0.50 mm. Flapless or minimally invasive approaches should be the default when socket anatomy permits; flap elevation should be reserved for cases requiring simultaneous GBR or where visualization is essential.

**CTG association requires nuanced interpretation.** CTG use was associated with higher MBL risk (OR 4.56), likely reflecting selection bias — CTGs are deployed in more demanding soft-tissue deficient cases. This OR should not be interpreted as CTG causing bone loss, but rather as a signal that the underlying case complexity drives both CTG use and worse bone outcomes.

**Esthetic outcome reporting was insufficient.** Standardized esthetic scoring (e.g., pink esthetic score / PES, white esthetic score / WES) and patient-reported outcomes were inconsistently reported across the 34 included studies, preventing any pooled esthetic analysis. Future trials should adopt standardized reporting frameworks.

## Study Characteristics

- **Design**: Systematic review + pooled regression analysis
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, EMBASE, CENTRAL (through June 2013)
- **Included studies**: 34
- **Evidence level**: SR (pooled regression — not formal meta-analysis with effect sizes)
- **Focus**: Single-tooth immediately placed implants in esthetic zone (anterior maxilla primarily)
