## Three-line Summary
SR+MA by the Cosyn group (5 RCTs + 3 NRSs, 409 implants: IIP+CTG 246 vs IIP-only 163, mean follow-up ≥12 months) assessing the effect of connective tissue grafting on vertical midfacial soft tissue change at single immediate implant placement.

IIP+CTG significantly reduced midfacial recession compared with IIP alone; the pooled benefit was ~0.4–0.7 mm in midfacial soft tissue level preservation.

CTG is indicated particularly in thin-biotype cases or when buccal bone loss risk is present, and this SR+MA provides the core evidence base for that recommendation in Type 1A esthetic-zone protocols.

## 세줄요약
Cosyn 그룹 SR+MA (5 RCT + 3 NRS, n=409 임플란트 IIP+CTG 246 vs IIP-only 163, 추적 ≥12개월); 단일 즉시식립 시 결합조직이식이 협측 수직 연조직 변화에 미치는 효과를 평가.

## Summary
To assess effect of CTG (connective tissue graft) on vertical midfacial soft tissue change at buccal aspect following single IIP. PubMed/WoS/EMBASE/Cochrane to Jan 2020. RCTs and NRSs comparing IIP+CTG vs IIP without CTG, mean follow-up ≥12 mo. MAs on RCT data.

## Methodology
PRISMA. Two reviewers. RoB Cochrane (RCT) and ROBINS-I (NRS). Random-effects MA.

## Results
- 5 RCTs + 3 NRSs included; 409 implants (IIP+CTG 246, IIP 163).
- IIP+CTG: vertical midfacial soft tissue change significantly smaller (less recession).
- Pooled effect favors CTG by ~0.4–0.7 mm in midfacial level preservation.

## Clinical Relevance
Thin biotype 또는 협측 골 결손 risk가 있는 IIP에서 CTG 동반이 midfacial recession 예방에 유효. Type 1A esthetic zone에서 standard add-on으로 권장하는 group도 있음. 본 SR+MA가 그 근거 제공.
