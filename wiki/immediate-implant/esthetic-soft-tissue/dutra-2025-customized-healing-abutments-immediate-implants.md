## Three-line Summary
Single-blind RCT (n=31, Brazil) comparing customized healing abutments (CHA) vs PTFE barrier + bovine bone for jumping-gap sealing at immediate implant placement.

The PTFE control group showed significantly greater papilla change (i.e., CHA better preserved papilla); bone ridge volume, keratinized mucosa thickness, and VAS pain were also compared between groups.

Clinical implication: CHA (socket sealing abutment) provides superior papilla preservation over PTFE membrane sealing in immediate implant placement, though the small sample (n=31) limits generalizability.

## 세줄요약
단일 맹검 무작위대조시험 (RCT, n=31, 브라질) — 즉시식립 시 맞춤형 치유 지대주 (Customized Healing Abutment, CHA) vs PTFE 차폐막 + 우골이식 갭 밀봉 비교.

PTFE 대조군이 유의하게 더 큰 유두 변화(즉 CHA가 유두 보존 우수); 골능 부피·각화점막 두께·VAS 통증도 군간 비교.

임상 적용: 소켓 실링 지대주(CHA) 사용 시 PTFE 막 밀봉 대비 유두 보존 유리 — 단, n=31 소규모로 일반화 제한.

## Summary
31 patients needing tooth extraction + immediate implant randomized to control (n=15: extraction + immediate implant + bovine bone in alveolar gap + PTFE barrier + suture) or experimental (n=16: extraction + immediate implant + bovine bone + CHA). CHAs prepared pre-surgery and customized intraoperatively. Outcomes: gingival papilla height, bone ridge volume, keratinized mucosal collar thickness (millimeter probe + dry-point caliper), postoperative pain (VAS). Mann-Whitney test (α=0.05).

## Methodology
단일 senior 술자, single-blind RCT. CHA는 PMMA/composite로 즉시 형상 가공 후 식립과 동시 장착. 대조군은 PTFE 비흡수성 막으로 sealing.

## Results
- Papilla change: control significantly higher (less papilla preservation in control).
- Bone ridge volume, keratinized mucosa thickness: between-group comparisons reported.
- VAS pain: between-group comparisons reported.

## Clinical Relevance
즉시식립 + CHA 사용 시 유두 보존 유리. 단, n=31의 소규모로 일반화 제한. 'socket sealing abutment' 개념의 직접 임상 비교 RCT.
