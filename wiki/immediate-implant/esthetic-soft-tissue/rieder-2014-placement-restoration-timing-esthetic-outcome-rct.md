## Three-line Summary

4-arm RCT randomized 48 patients with failing maxillary incisors into immediate/early placement × immediate/early restoration groups, measuring Pink Esthetic Score (PES) at final restoration delivery. Early placement combined with immediate restoration (EPIR) produced significantly lower PES (6.62 ± 3.24) compared to immediate placement + immediate restoration (IIPIR: 8.47 ± 2.08, p=0.015) and early placement + early restoration (EPER: 8.10 ± 3.25, p=0.047). The EPIR combination should be avoided; experienced surgeons can successfully apply immediate placement with immediate restoration.

## Background

The optimal combination of implant placement timing and restoration timing for anterior maxillary single implants remains debated. While both placement timing (immediate type 1 vs early type 2) and restoration timing (immediate vs early/conventional) influence peri-implant soft-tissue stability, their interaction has not been well characterized in head-to-head RCT comparisons.

## Methods

- **Design**: 4-arm parallel-group randomized clinical trial
- **Sample**: 48 patients, single failing incisor in the maxilla
- **Groups**:
  - Group 1a (IIPIR): Immediate implant placement + immediate restoration (provisionalization at time of surgery)
  - Group 1b (IIPER): Immediate implant placement + early restoration
  - Group 2a (EPIR): Early implant placement + immediate restoration
  - Group 2b (EPER): Early implant placement + early restoration
- **Primary outcome**: Pink Esthetic Score (PES) at final restoration delivery
- **Note**: abstract-only; detailed protocol, blinding, and follow-up duration not available

## Results

### PES by Group

| Group | Protocol | PES (mean ± SD) |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | IIPIR — Immediate placement + immediate restoration | **8.47 ± 2.08** |
| 1b | IIPER — Immediate placement + early restoration | 7.93 ± 3.21 |
| 2a | EPIR — Early placement + immediate restoration | 6.62 ± 3.24 |
| 2b | EPER — Early placement + early restoration | 8.10 ± 3.25 |

### Pairwise Comparisons (significant only)

| Comparison | p-value | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Group 2a (EPIR) vs Group 1a (IIPIR) | p = 0.015 | EPIR significantly worse |
| Group 2a (EPIR) vs Group 2b (EPER) | p = 0.047 | EPIR significantly worse |

Groups 1a vs 1b, 1a vs 2b, and 1b vs 2b were not significantly different (p values not stated in abstract).

## Clinical Takeaway

**The EPIR combination (early placement + immediate restoration) should be avoided** — it produced the worst esthetic outcomes among all four protocols and was significantly inferior to two of the three comparators. When early placement is chosen, pairing it with early restoration (EPER) recovers esthetic performance to levels comparable with immediate placement protocols.

**Immediate placement + immediate restoration (IIPIR) is a viable option** when performed by an experienced surgeon — it achieved the highest mean PES (8.47) and was not significantly different from IIPER or EPER.

**Limitations**: n=48 (approximately 12 per arm); wide SDs suggest high inter-patient variability; abstract-only retrieval precludes assessment of blinding, examiner calibration, follow-up duration, or implant/graft details.
