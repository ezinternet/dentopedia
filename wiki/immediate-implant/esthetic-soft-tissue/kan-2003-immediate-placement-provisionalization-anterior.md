## Three-line Summary

Prospective 1-year study (n=35) of immediate placement plus same-day provisionalization of HA-coated implants in maxillary anterior single-tooth sites; definitive restoration at 6 months.

100% implant survival; MBL −0.26 mm mesial / −0.22 mm distal; midfacial gingival recession −0.55 mm; papilla mesial −0.53 mm / distal −0.39 mm; 100% patient satisfaction.

This early study demonstrates that immediate placement plus same-day provisionalization is feasible in the anterior maxilla with clinically acceptable hard and soft tissue outcomes, validating the concept of provisional-guided emergence profile shaping.

## 세줄요약

전향 1년 연구(n=35) — 상악 전치부 단일치 즉시식립 + 당일 임시치관(HA 코팅 임플란트), 최종 수복 6개월에 시행.

임플란트 생존율 100%; 변연골소실 −0.26 mm(근심)/−0.22 mm(원심); 중안면 치은퇴축 −0.55 mm; 유두 근심 −0.53 mm/원심 −0.39 mm; 환자 만족도 100%.

상악 전치부에서 즉시식립+당일 임시치관이 임상적으로 허용 가능한 경·연조직 결과로 실현 가능함을 보여 주는 초기 연구; 임시치관을 이용한 출현 윤곽 형성 개념을 검증.

## Summary
1-year prospective study (n=35) of immediately placed and provisionalized HA-coated implants in maxillary anterior single-tooth sites. Demonstrates feasibility of immediate placement + immediate provisionalization with favorable hard and soft tissue outcomes within clinical expectations.

## Key Contributions
- 100% survival at 12 months — no implant failures
- MBL change: -0.26±0.40mm mesial, -0.22±0.28mm distal (significant but clinically acceptable)
- Midfacial gingival recession: -0.55±0.53mm
- Papilla levels: mesial -0.53mm, distal -0.39mm
- Patient satisfaction: 100% (none perceived tissue level changes)
- Immediate provisionals allow contour shaping of emerging profile

## Methodology
- 35 patients; maxillary anterior single-tooth replacement
- HA-coated threaded implants; immediate placement + same-day provisional
- Definitive restoration at 6 months
- Assessments at 0, 3, 6, 12 months (clinical + radiographic)
