## Three-line Summary
Systematic review and meta-analysis of 12 studies (106 articles screened) examining soft-tissue changes around immediately placed implants (IIP) with ≥12 months follow-up after functional loading, comparing IIP vs conventional implant placement (CIP) and stratifying by gingival biotype and restoration timing.

MFR was slightly but non-significantly less in CIP vs IIP (MD −0.064 mm, P=0.687); thick biotype IIP showed significantly less MFR (MD −0.478, P<0.001) and better papillary height (MD −0.287, P<0.001) vs thin biotype; immediate provisionalization showed non-significant MFR benefit (MD 0.253, P=0.384) but significant PH advantage (MD −0.519, P=0.028) over conventional restoration.

Thick gingival biotype and immediate provisionalization are key modifiers favoring soft-tissue stability in IIP; findings qualified by high heterogeneity and limited qualified studies, warranting cautious interpretation in clinical application.

## 세줄요약
12편 연구(스크리닝 106편)를 대상으로 한 체계적 고찰·메타분석(Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA)으로, 기능적 부하 후 최소 12개월 추적에서 즉시식립 임플란트(Immediately Placed Implant, IIP)의 연조직 변화를 관습적 식립 임플란트(Conventionally Placed Implant, CIP)와 비교하고, 치은 생물형(Gingival Biotype) 및 수복 시점(즉시 임시수복 여부)에 따라 층화 분석.

정중협측 퇴축(Midfacial Recession, MFR)은 CIP가 IIP보다 약간 적었으나 통계적으로 유의하지 않았고(평균차이, Mean Difference, MD −0.064 mm, P=0.687), 두꺼운 생물형(Thick Biotype) IIP는 얇은 생물형(Thin Biotype) 대비 MFR 유의 감소(MD −0.478, P<0.001)·유두높이(Papillary Height, PH) 유의 개선(MD −0.287, P<0.001)을 보였으며, 즉시 임시수복(Immediate Provisionalization)은 관습적 수복 대비 MFR 이점은 비유의(MD 0.253, P=0.384)했으나 PH 유의 이점(MD −0.519, P=0.028)을 보임.

두꺼운 치은 생물형(Thick Gingival Biotype)과 즉시 임시수복이 즉시식립 임플란트(IIP)의 연조직 안정화 핵심 변수이나, 높은 이질성(Heterogeneity)과 제한된 적격 연구 수로 임상 적용 시 신중한 해석이 필요.

## Summary
This systematic review and meta-analysis (Kinaia et al., *Journal of Periodontology*, 2017) pooled 12 studies out of 106 screened (Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials and MEDLINE, electronic search through January 2016, plus hand search) to quantify soft-tissue behavior around immediately placed implants (IIP) with at least 12 months of follow-up after functional loading. Comparing IIP against conventionally placed implants (CIP), papillary height was significantly better preserved in CIP (cumulative PH MD −0.396 mm, P=0.010; distal PH MD −0.765 mm, P<0.001), while midfacial recession (MFR) did not differ significantly between groups (MD −0.064 mm, P=0.687). Within IIP alone, thick gingival biotype significantly outperformed thin biotype on both MFR (MD −0.478 mm, P<0.001) and cumulative papillary height (MD −0.287 mm, P<0.001), and immediate provisionalization significantly improved papillary height retention over conventional restoration (MD −0.519 mm, P=0.028) without producing a significant MFR difference (MD 0.253 mm, P=0.384). The authors caution that high heterogeneity and the small number of qualifying studies (12 of 106 screened) limit the strength of these conclusions; thick biotype and immediate provisionalization emerge as favorable, but not decisive, modifiers of esthetic soft-tissue outcome around immediately placed implants.

## Key Contributions
- First quantitative meta-analytic synthesis of soft-tissue (not just hard-tissue) outcomes specifically around immediately placed implants (IIP), pooling MFR and papillary height across IIP vs CIP, biotype, and provisionalization-timing comparisons
- Establishes gingival biotype as a significant, meta-analytically measurable predictor of both midfacial recession and papillary height in IIP (thick significantly outperforms thin on both outcomes, P<0.001)
- Provides meta-analytic-level support for immediate provisionalization improving papillary height retention (MD −0.519 mm, P=0.028), while showing it does not significantly change midfacial recession
- Flags the underlying evidence base as thin: only 12 of 106 screened studies met the ≥12-month, post-functional-loading inclusion criteria, with substantial heterogeneity across pooled estimates

## Methodology
Systematic review and meta-analysis. Databases: Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials and MEDLINE (electronic search through January 2016), supplemented by hand search. Eligibility: studies reporting MFR and/or papillary height outcomes for IIP with ≥12 months follow-up after functional loading. From 106 screened articles, 12 met inclusion criteria. Four independent reviewers assessed study quality using established bias-assessment tools. Effect sizes were pooled with random-effects models (Comprehensive Meta-Analysis software), accounting for sample size and treatment-group heterogeneity, across three separate comparisons: (1) IIP vs CIP, (2) thick vs thin gingival biotype within IIP, and (3) immediate provisionalization vs conventional restoration within IIP.
