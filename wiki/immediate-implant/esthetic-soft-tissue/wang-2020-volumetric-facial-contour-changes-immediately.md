## Three-line Summary

Randomized controlled trial (RCT), n=40 subjects with unrestorable maxillary anterior/premolar teeth receiving immediately-placed single implants; compared 12-month 3D facial ridge changes between provisional crown (test) and healing abutment (control) arms.

Linear 3D spatial resorption ranged from 0.1–0.7 mm with no significant intergroup difference; volumetric soft-tissue contour change differed significantly at 12 months, test 11.9% versus control 17.4% (P = 0.04) in favor of immediate provisionalization.

Immediate provisionalization showed superior volumetric soft-tissue preservation at the mid-facial margin and 2–6 mm above the crest (esthetic concern area), though linear marginal resorption measurements were similar between groups; suggests esthetic benefit independent of classical marginal-recession metrics.

## 세줄요약

무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT), 발치가 불가피한 상악 전치부·소구치에 즉시식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP)된 단일임플란트 환자 n=40명 대상; 임시크라운을 이용한 즉시 임시보철 (Immediate Provisionalization)군과 치유지대주 (Healing Abutment)군의 12개월 3차원 안면 치조정 윤곽 변화를 비교.

선형 3차원 공간적 흡수 (Linear 3D Spatial Resorption)는 0.1~0.7mm로 군간 유의차가 없었으나, 12개월 체적 연조직 윤곽 변화 (Volumetric Soft-tissue Contour Change)는 임시보철군 11.9% vs. 치유지대주군 17.4%로 유의한 차이(P=0.04)를 보여 즉시 임시보철에 유리했음.

즉시 임시보철은 협측 치은변연부 (Mid-facial Margin) 및 치조정 상방 2~6mm 부위(심미적 관심영역)에서 우수한 체적 연조직 보존을 보였음 — 선형 변연 흡수 (Linear Marginal Resorption) 측정치는 양 군간 유사했던 것과 대비되며, 고전적 변연 지표와 독립적인 심미적 이득이 존재함을 시사.

## Summary

This randomized controlled trial adds a three-dimensional volumetric analysis of facial ridge contour change to the same immediate-implant esthetic-zone trial population reported by Chan et al. (2019), comparing an immediate non-occluding provisional crown (n=20) against a standard healing abutment (n=20) in 40 patients over 12 months. Using voxel-based registration of digital models, linear 3D spatial resorption (0.1–0.7 mm) did not differ significantly between groups — consistent with the null 2D marginal-level finding reported on the same cohort. Volumetric soft-tissue contour loss, however, was significantly smaller in the provisionalization group (11.9%) than the healing-abutment group (17.4%, P = 0.04), with the advantage concentrated at the mid-facial margin and the 2–6 mm supracrestal zone that is most visible clinically. The findings indicate that immediate provisionalization confers a real, volumetrically measurable esthetic advantage that classical linear or point-level clinical metrics (marginal level, papilla height) are not sensitive enough to detect — a qualification of, rather than a simple addition to, the companion trial's headline conclusion that provisionalization does not improve esthetic outcomes.

## Key Contributions

- First study to quantify 3D volumetric facial-ridge contour change (voxel-based digital model registration) comparing immediate provisionalization vs. healing abutment at immediately placed implants in the esthetic zone
- Found a significant volumetric soft-tissue preservation benefit with immediate provisionalization at 12 months (11.9% vs 17.4% change, P = 0.04) despite no significant difference in linear/point-level resorption between groups
- Localized the volumetric advantage to the mid-facial margin and 2–6 mm supracrestal zone — the region most relevant to visible pink esthetics
- Demonstrates that a volumetric esthetic benefit of immediate provisionalization can exist and go undetected by the traditional 2D clinical metrics (marginal level, papilla height) measured on the same cohort by the companion trial

## Methodology

Randomized controlled trial; n=40 subjects with hopeless/unrestorable maxillary anterior or premolar teeth requiring extraction, randomized immediately after single-implant placement to an immediate non-occluding provisional crown (test, n=20) or a standard healing abutment (control, n=20). Permanent restoration was delivered at 4 months; digital models (intraoral scans/casts or DICOM) were captured at baseline, 4 months, and 12 months and compared via voxel-based registration to compute linear (mm) and volumetric (%) facial-ridge deviations by region.

## Results

| Outcome | Test (Provisional Crown) | Control (Healing Abutment) | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linear 3D spatial resorption (mm) | 0.1–0.7 | 0.1–0.7 | No difference |
| Volumetric soft-tissue contour change at 12 mo | 11.9% | 17.4% | **P = 0.04** (favors test) |
| Mid-facial margin / 2–6 mm above crest preservation | Better | Less preservation | P = 0.04 |
| Buccal bone thickness change | Modest | Modest | No significant difference |
| Implant survival at 12 mo | Similar | Similar | Not significant |
