## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study (J Dent Res 2015;94(9 Suppl); n not reported in the abstract) — sequential 3-dimensional digital surface model superimposition of facial soft tissue and underlying bone in esthetic-zone extraction sites over an 8-week healing period (digital impressions + CBCT), stratified by thin vs thick bone phenotype.

Baseline soft tissue thickness was indistinguishable between phenotypes (0.7 mm thin, 0.8 mm thick), then diverged sharply: thin phenotypes thickened ~7-fold by 8 weeks while thick phenotypes did not change, so measured vertical soft tissue loss was only 1.6 mm — concealing 7.5 mm of underlying vertical bone resorption — and no significant difference in total tissue loss existed between phenotypes at 2, 4, 6 or 8 weeks.

More than 51% of the dimensional change occurred within the first 2 weeks; the spontaneous thickening is proposed as favourable for later bone regeneration and implant therapy in esthetically demanding sites, but the operative corollary is that a clinically acceptable soft-tissue contour is not evidence of adequate bone.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상 연구 (J Dent Res 2015;94(9 Suppl), 초록에 표본수 미기재) — 심미부 발치 부위에서 디지털 임프레션 (Digital Impression) + 선형단층촬영 (Cone Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT)을 이용한 **순차적 3차원 (3-Dimensional, 3D) 디지털 표면 모델 중첩 (Superimposition)** 으로 순측 연조직과 기저골을 8주 치유기 동안 추적, 박벽형(thin bone phenotype) 대 후벽형(thick bone phenotype) 비교.

발치 당시 순측 연조직 두께는 두 표현형에서 구분되지 않았다(박벽형 0.7 mm, 후벽형 0.8 mm). 그러나 8주 후 박벽형에서만 연조직 두께가 **약 7배 증가**하고 후벽형은 변화가 없어 — 측정된 수직 연조직 소실은 1.6 mm에 그쳤고, 그 아래에서 **7.5 mm의 수직 골소실이 가려졌다**. 그 결과 총 조직 소실 (Total Tissue Loss)은 2·4·6·8주 모두에서 두 표현형 간 유의한 차이가 없었다.

치수 변화의 51% 이상이 발치 후 **2주 이내**에 이미 발생했으며, 이러한 자발적 연조직 증후는 심미적 요구도가 높은 부위의 후속 골재생·임플란트 치료에 유리할 수 있다 — 그러나 임상적으로는 **연조직 윤곽이 양호해 보인다고 해서 골이 충분하다는 근거가 되지 못한다**는 것이 실무적 함의다.

## Summary

This prospective study is the soft-tissue companion to the group's 2013 bone analysis, and the first to examine the interplay between facial soft tissue dimensions and the underlying bone anatomy during an 8-week postextraction healing period in the esthetic zone. Using sequential 3D digital surface model superimposition from digital impressions and CBCT, it compared sites with thin versus thick bone phenotypes. The two phenotypes began with essentially identical soft tissue thickness (0.7 vs 0.8 mm) but diverged: thin-bone sites thickened roughly seven-fold by week 8, while thick-bone sites showed no dimensional change. That spontaneous thickening masked 7.5 mm of underlying vertical bone resorption, leaving a measured vertical soft tissue loss of only 1.6 mm and no significant between-phenotype difference in total tissue loss at any timepoint from 2 to 8 weeks. More than 51% of all dimensional alteration occurred within the first 2 weeks. The authors frame the spontaneous thickening as advantageous for subsequent bone regeneration and implant therapy in high-esthetic-demand sites; the clinically important corollary is the reverse face of the same measurement — soft-tissue assessment alone systematically under-reads the bone deficiency in thin-wall sites, so an unremarkable mucosal contour must not be taken as bone adequacy.

## Key Contributions

- First analysis of the **interplay** between facial soft tissue dimensions and underlying bone anatomy during postextraction healing, rather than bone change in isolation
- Shows soft tissue thickness at extraction does **not** identify the bone phenotype (0.7 vs 0.8 mm) — phenotype classification remains a bone-based exercise (CBCT or intraoral probing), which is why the [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2013-ridge-alterations-post-extraction-esthetic]] pre-assessment step cannot be replaced by a mucosal check
- Quantifies spontaneous soft tissue thickening as **phenotype-specific**: ~7-fold in thin-bone sites, absent in thick-bone sites
- Establishes the thickening as a **measurement confounder**, not merely a cosmetic event: it offsets 7.5 mm of vertical bone resorption down to a measured 1.6 mm of soft tissue loss, and erases the thin-vs-thick difference in total tissue loss at 2, 4, 6 and 8 weeks
- Dates the kinetics: >51% of the dimensional alterations occurred within the first 2 weeks, i.e. the decision-relevant window for staging any graft or implant is very early

## Methodology

Prospective clinical study (no randomisation, no control arm; thin vs thick phenotype is a pre-existing anatomical stratification, so phenotype is a prognostic subgroup, not an allocated intervention). Sequential 3-dimensional digital surface model superimpositions of **both** the soft tissue (digital impressions) and the underlying bone (CBCT) were registered across an 8-week healing period, with total tissue loss compared between phenotypes at 2, 4, 6 and 8 weeks. Outcomes: facial soft tissue thickness, vertical soft tissue loss, vertical bone resorption, and combined total tissue loss. **The abstract does not state the sample size**, nor the phenotype cutoff, impression/scanning protocol, registration accuracy, or the statistical test used — those live in the paywalled full text and are not guessed here. Registration: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02403700.

## Clinical Relevance

- A clinically acceptable post-extraction mucosal contour is **not** a valid surrogate for bone volume — spontaneous thickening in thin-wall sites makes the two phenotypes indistinguishable at 2, 4, 6 and 8 weeks on tissue-based measures. Bone phenotype must still be assessed by CBCT or intraoral probing, exactly as [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2013-ridge-alterations-post-extraction-esthetic]] and [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chappuis-2017-dimensional-bone-soft-tissue-post-extraction]] prescribe.
- The masking is a systematic bias in the postextraction-dimension literature at large: any trial or cohort grading postextraction change on soft tissue landmarks alone will under-detect the underlying bone deficit in thin-wall sites. Hard- and soft-tissue endpoints should be read together — this is the same measurement-method sensitivity seen elsewhere in this folder.
- **>51% within 2 weeks** means the site is essentially remodelled before most soft-tissue graft decisions are made; early is the only window in which the finding is actionable.
- The authors read the thickening as an advantage for subsequent bone regeneration and implant therapy in esthetically demanding sites — a thicker soft-tissue envelope over a grafted or implant-restored site. That is a biologically plausible inference from this descriptive study, not a tested outcome (there was no graft or implant arm).
