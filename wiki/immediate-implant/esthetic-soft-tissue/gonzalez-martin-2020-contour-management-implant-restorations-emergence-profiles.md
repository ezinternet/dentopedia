## Three-line Summary

Narrative clinical guideline (IJPRD 2020) defining two zones of the implant abutment/crown transmucosal region — the critical contour and the subcritical contour — for immediate and delayed provisional restorations.

Design and alteration of these zones are stated to affect the peri-implant gingival margin level and zenith, labial alveolar profile, and gingival color; common contouring/macrodesign principles across protocols are proposed (no quantitative data in the abstract).

Gives shared vocabulary for clinician–laboratory communication on provisional shaping; expert opinion rather than outcome evidence.

## Summary

This is the reference paper for the critical/subcritical contour vocabulary. The abstract states that the design and alteration of the **critical** and **subcritical** contours can affect the gingival margin level and **zenith**, the labial alveolar profile, and gingival color. The zone-specific shape rules (which direction of contour change moves the margin which way) are in the full text, not retrieved here. The authors argue that whether the implant was immediate or delayed, the provisional should be managed zone by zone, and that naming the zones lets clinician and technician talk about the same surface.

The zenith tie-in matters for the wiki's emergence-profile synthesis: the critical contour is the restorative lever that places the implant crown's zenith where natural-tooth reference data say it should be.

## Key Contributions

- Two-zone model of the transmucosal restoration.
- Explicit link from contour design to gingival margin level, zenith, labial profile, and color.
- Protocol-agnostic provisional guidelines (immediate and delayed).
