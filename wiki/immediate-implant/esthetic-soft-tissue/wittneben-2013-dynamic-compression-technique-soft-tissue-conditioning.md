## Three-line Summary

Technique article (IJPRD 2013) introducing the dynamic compression technique for conditioning peri-implant soft tissue around bone-level implants with provisional restorations in the esthetic zone.

Stated goals: adequate emergence profile; mucosal course and level in harmony with adjacent gingiva — papilla height/width, mucosal zenith localization, triangular tissue profile — and an accurate proximal contact.

Provides a provisional-shaping protocol with zenith placement as an explicit target; no outcome data in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

술식 논문 (IJPRD 2013): 심미부 골수준 임플란트 주위 연조직을 임시수복물로 형성하는 동적 압박 술식 (Dynamic Compression Technique).

목표: 적절한 출현윤곽, 인접 치은과 조화되는 점막 높이 — 유두 높이·폭, 점막 정점 (Mucosal Zenith) 위치, 삼각 형태 — 와 정확한 인접 접촉.

정점 위치를 명시 목표로 하는 임시수복 형성 프로토콜; 결과 데이터 없음.

## Summary

Where Su 2010 says *which zones* of the restoration shape the mucosa, Wittneben and the Bern group describe *how* to do it chairside with a named provisional-conditioning protocol whose goal is a mucosal margin, papillae and zenith matching the neighbouring teeth. The abstract lists zenith localization alongside papilla dimensions and contact area as explicit end-points. The individual steps are in the full text (not retrieved).

## Key Contributions

- Named, reproducible provisional-conditioning protocol for bone-level implants.
- Mucosal zenith position as an explicit design target.
