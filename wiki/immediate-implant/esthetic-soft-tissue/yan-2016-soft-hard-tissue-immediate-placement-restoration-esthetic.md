## Three-line Summary

This 2016 SR+MA of 13 RCTs compared immediate placement, immediate restoration, and immediate loading of single-tooth implants in the esthetic zone against conventional protocols. All meta-analyzed outcomes — marginal bone level (mesial/distal/mean), papilla height, and midbuccal mucosa level — showed SMDs with 95% CIs crossing zero, indicating no statistically significant soft or hard tissue differences. Immediate protocols in the esthetic zone appear equivalent to conventional protocols for peri-implant tissue outcomes based on RCT evidence through December 2014.

## 세줄요약

심미구역 단일 임플란트 13편 무작위 대조 시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 메타분석: 즉시 식립 (IIP)·즉시 수복 (IR)·즉시 부하 (IL)를 기존 프로토콜과 비교했다. 변연골 소실 (MBL) 근·원심, 치간유두, 구순 정중 점막 모두 표준화 평균차 (Standardized Mean Difference, SMD) 신뢰구간이 0을 포함 — 통계적 유의차 없음. 2014년 12월까지의 RCT 근거 내에서 즉시 프로토콜과 기존 프로토콜의 연조직·경조직 결과는 동등하다.

## Background

Immediate implant placement, immediate restoration (provisionalization at the time of or shortly after placement), and immediate loading have gained popularity to reduce treatment time and support peri-implant tissue esthetics. However, concerns exist that early biomechanical loading or disrupted socket healing may compromise marginal bone levels and soft tissue contour in the esthetic zone. This SR+MA aimed to synthesize RCT evidence on hard and soft tissue changes following these three immediate protocols versus conventional approaches.

## Methods

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Systematic review + meta-analysis |
| Databases | PubMed, EMBASE, Cochrane |
| Search period | Up to December 2014 |
| Study type included | RCTs only |
| N included | 13 RCTs |
| Interventions compared | (1) Immediate placement vs delayed; (2) Immediate restoration vs conventional; (3) Immediate loading vs conventional/delayed loading |
| Primary outcomes | Marginal bone level change (mesial, distal, mean); papilla level; midbuccal mucosa level |
| Effect measure | Standardized mean difference (SMD) with 95% CI |

## Results

### Hard Tissue — Marginal Bone Level

| Comparison | Outcome | SMD (mm) | 95% CI | Sig. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate placement | Mesial bone level | -0.04 | -0.25 to 0.17 | ns |
| Immediate placement | Distal bone level | -0.15 | -0.38 to 0.09 | ns |
| Immediate placement | Mean bone level | 0.05 | -0.18 to 0.27 | ns |
| Immediate loading | Mesial bone level | -0.05 | -0.15 to 0.06 | ns |
| Immediate loading | Distal bone level | -0.02 | -0.09 to 0.05 | ns |

### Soft Tissue

| Outcome | SMD (mm) | 95% CI | Sig. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Papilla mesial | 0.18 | -0.00 to 0.37 | ns (borderline) |
| Midbuccal mucosa level | -0.22 | -1.29 to 0.85 | ns |

All 95% CIs cross zero. The midbuccal mucosa comparison showed wide heterogeneity (CI spans >2 mm), reflecting high variability across included trials. Papilla mesial approached significance (lower bound -0.00), warranting caution.

## Clinical Takeaway

- Immediate placement, immediate restoration, and immediate loading in the esthetic zone can be performed without significantly compromising marginal bone or soft tissue outcomes compared to conventional protocols — based on RCT evidence through 2014.
- The borderline papilla mesial result (SMD 0.18, CI just touching 0.00) suggests papilla response may be sensitive to protocol choice; larger trials are needed.
- The wide midbuccal mucosa CI reflects high study heterogeneity — standardized outcome measurement remains a gap in this literature.
- This 2016 SR is superseded for decision-making by more recent and larger SR+MAs ([[asghar-2023-immediate-vs-early-implant-esthetic-zone-sr-ma]], [[gaddale-2024-immediate-implant-loading-esthetic-tissue-sr-ma]]), but the directional conclusion (equivalence) is consistent with the updated literature.
