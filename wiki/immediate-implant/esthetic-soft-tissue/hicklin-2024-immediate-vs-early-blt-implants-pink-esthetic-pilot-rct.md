## Three-line Summary

Multicenter pilot RCT, n=60 patients (60 Straumann BLT implants, 5 centers, Geneva/Zurich/Belgrade), 12-month follow-up comparing immediately placed vs early placed bone-level-tapered implants restored with all-ceramic crowns cemented to titanium-base abutments in anterior and premolar areas.

At 12 months, 98.3% implant survival; mean implant-shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact 0.15 ± 0.59 mm (no group difference); median ISQ rose from 75.5 to 78.5; mean buccal recession 0.1 ± 0.70 mm (no group difference); papilla gain 0.5 ± 1.47 mm mesially and 0.4 ± 1.36 mm distally.

Both immediate and early BLT placement protocols achieve equivalent bone level stability, ISQ trajectory, and pink esthetic outcomes at 1 year; pilot design (n=60, descriptive analysis only) limits inferential conclusions.

## Summary

This multicenter pilot RCT from Geneva, Zurich, and Belgrade randomized 60 patients (1:1) to immediate or early placement of Straumann BLT implants in anterior and premolar areas, subsequently restoring all implants with all-ceramic crowns cemented to titanium-base abutments. At 12 months, bone levels (mean shoulder-to-first-BIC 0.15 ± 0.59 mm) were stable and indistinguishable between groups. ISQ values increased from a median of 75.5 at baseline to 78.5 at 12 months, indicating continued osseointegration strengthening in both arms. Pink esthetic outcomes (buccal recession 0.1 ± 0.70 mm; papilla gain mesially 0.5 mm, distally 0.4 mm) improved over time with no significant inter-group difference. One implant was lost (98.3% survival). The authors conclude that immediate and early BLT placement protocols are clinically equivalent in radiographic and pink esthetic outcomes at 1 year and that both protocols are viable. As a pilot study, the analysis is descriptive; the work sets the stage for a powered confirmatory RCT.

**Evidence-level note**: Although classified as an RCT, this is explicitly a *pilot* study (n=60, 5 centers). The statistical analysis is descriptive only (no power calculation for group comparisons). Findings are hypothesis-generating and directional, not inferentially confirmed.

## Key Contributions

- Straumann BLT-specific pilot RCT: provides implant-system-level data for immediate vs early timing comparison, complementing system-agnostic SR+MAs (Asghar 2023)
- Demonstrates ISQ increase across both arms, confirming osseointegration progression regardless of placement timing
- Buccal recession (0.1 mm) and papilla gain (~0.5 mm) both favorable and equivalent — soft-tissue profile improves over 12 months in both protocols
- Multicenter (5 centers, 3 countries) design increases generalizability despite small n
- Includes both microveneered ceramic crown types — adds restorative material dimension to the timing comparison

## Methodology

- **Design**: Parallel-group multicenter pilot RCT (1:1 randomization)
- **Population**: Patients needing extraction + single implant in anterior or premolar area, 5 centers
- **Implant system**: Straumann BLT (Institut Straumann)
- **Restoration**: All-ceramic crowns cemented to titanium-base abutments; buccally microveneered in 2 ceramic materials
- **Outcomes**: Radiographic bone levels (shoulder to first BIC), ISQ, pink esthetic outcomes (buccal recession, papilla height)
- **Analysis**: Descriptive; significance level α = 0.05
- **Follow-up**: 12 months

## Limitations

- Pilot design: n=60 underpowered for formal inferential group comparisons; all analyses descriptive
- 12-month follow-up only; long-term bone and soft-tissue stability not demonstrated
- Includes anterior and premolar regions (not exclusively esthetic zone — premolars reduce esthetic-zone applicability)
- Abstract-only ingest (no PMC full text): granular patient selection, exclusion criteria, and dropout details not available
- BLT system-specific: findings may not generalize to other implant designs
