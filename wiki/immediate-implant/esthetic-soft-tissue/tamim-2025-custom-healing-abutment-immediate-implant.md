## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (6 RCTs, n=249 implants) comparing custom healing abutments (CHA) vs stock healing abutments (SHA) for immediate implant placement in the esthetic zone.
CHA significantly improved pink esthetic score (PES) and reduced midfacial mucosal recession (MMR) at 3-6 months and 12 months vs SHA, with no differences in peri-implant health parameters or implant survival.
Supports CHA use in IIP for esthetic soft-tissue outcomes over 12 months (moderate GRADE certainty).

## 세줄요약

즉시식립(IIP) 시 맞춤형 치유지대주(CHA)와 기성 치유지대주(SHA)를 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석(6건 RCT, n=249개 임플란트).
CHA는 3-6개월 및 12개월 모두에서 분홍심미지수(PES)가 유의하게 높고 중안면 점막퇴축(MMR)이 유의하게 적었으며, 주위조직 건강 지표 및 임플란트 생존율에는 차이가 없었다.
즉시식립에서 CHA 사용은 12개월까지 심미적 결과에 유리하며, 근거 확실성은 중등도이다.

## Summary

In immediate implant placement, custom healing abutments (CHAs) provided better esthetic outcomes than stock healing abutments (SHAs): higher PES and less midfacial mucosal recession at 3-6 months and 12 months, with no adverse effects on peri-implant health or implant survival. Evidence is from 6 RCTs (n=249 implants) with moderate GRADE certainty.

## Key Contributions

- Quantifies CHA vs SHA effect specifically in immediate implant placement via SR+MA.
- Demonstrates consistent PES improvement at early and mid-term timepoints.
- Demonstrates reduced midfacial mucosal recession at both timepoints.
- Confirms neutral effect on KMW, PD, BOP, PI, and implant survival.

## Methodology

- Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Library (up to May 2025)
- Inclusion: RCTs comparing CHAs vs SHAs for IIP with ≥3 months follow-up
- Primary: Pink Esthetic Score (PES); Secondary: MMR, KMW, PD, BOP, PI, implant survival
- Risk of bias: RoB2; Certainty: GRADE
- 6 RCTs, 249 implants (124 CHA, 125 SHA)

## Results

- PES 3-6 mo: MD 1.02; 95% CI 0.58-1.45; P<.001; I²=0%
- PES 12 mo: MD 0.89; 95% CI 0.38-1.40; P=.001; I²=0%
- MMR 3-6 mo: MD -0.21 mm; 95% CI -0.37 to -0.05; P=.01; I²=0%
- MMR 12 mo: MD -0.32 mm; 95% CI -0.52 to -0.12; P=.002; I²=0%
- No significant differences for KMW, PD, BOP, PI, or implant survival (moderate GRADE for PES/MMR)
