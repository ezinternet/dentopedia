## Three-line Summary

Single case report (n=1, 30-year-old woman): two non-restorable maxillary central incisors with chronic periapical abscess were extracted atraumatically and flaplessly, abscess cavities curetted and irrigated with saline and chlorhexidine, and two immediate implants (3.5 x 11.5 mm) placed with a 3D-printed guide plus a palatal autogenous soft tissue graft, then feldspathic laminate veneers on the other visible teeth.

Provisionals were recontoured at 10 weeks and replaced at 3 and 4 months; at 3 years the patient was satisfied, no peri-implant disease was noted and radiographs showed osseointegrated implants, but no quantitative outcomes are reported.

The paper documents no pus at extraction, no antibiotic regimen and no bone graft, so it cannot inform immediate placement in acute purulent infection; it is an n=1 technique illustration without a comparator.

## Summary

This 2025 case report from Jurado and colleagues describes a full smile-zone workflow: two immediate implants replacing both maxillary central incisors, combined with feldspathic porcelain laminate veneers on the remaining visible teeth (first molar to first molar). The patient, a 30-year-old woman, presented with "exudated tissue" in the upper anterior area and dissatisfaction with her smile. Both central incisors had prior endodontic treatment, crowns and long metal posts, grade II mobility and apical resorption, and were diagnosed with chronic periapical abscesses (clinical and CBCT), hence non-restorable.

**What the paper says about the infection.** The abstract states the complaint included "periapical infections with purulent exudate at the apex of her central incisors", but the Case Report section records only "exudated tissue" and a diagnosis of chronic periapical abscess. At surgery the sockets were curetted and "the abscess cavity was irrigated with saline solution and chlorohexidine"; no pus at extraction, fistula, swelling, pain or antibiotic use is described anywhere in the paper, and there is no separate timing or delay for infection control. The implants were placed at the same visit. Buccal plate status and any socket-gap grafting are not reported.

**Reading against the rest of the wiki [미검증 — Claude 해석]:** this is a chronic-abscess, single-visit example consistent with the wiki's chronic-lesion pathway (see [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/prati-2017-immediate-early-delayed-implants-endodontic-infections]] and [[overviews/immediate-implant-infected-sites-decision]]), but with no antibiotic data, no comparator and n=1 it adds no evidence for or against acute purulent infection. It should not be cited as support for immediate placement in acute purulent abscess.

## Key Contributions

- Illustrates a combined workflow of two adjacent immediate central-incisor implants with veneers on neighboring teeth, which the authors state has few clinical reports.
- Documents the sequence: digital planning (CBCT + intraoral scan, wax-up, mock-up), guided atraumatic flapless extraction, guided placement, palatal autogenous soft tissue graft, screw-retained non-occluding provisionals used to shape the soft tissue over 4 months, custom impression posts copying the emergence profile, zirconia-titanium-base custom abutments, bonding under dental dam.
- Reports a 3-year follow-up with no peri-implant disease noted.

## Methodology

- Design: single case report (n=1); no IRB approval required per university guidelines; written informed consent obtained.
- Patient history: endodontic treatment and crowns on both central incisors 5 years earlier; first premolar extractions and orthodontics 10 years earlier.
- Surgical steps: periotome and forceps with vertical force only; curettage; saline and chlorhexidine irrigation of the abscess cavity; Helix GM implants (Neodent) 3.5 mm x 11.50 mm via 3D-printed guide; autogenous palatal soft tissue graft placed on the facial aspect of the left-side implant only, with polyethylene sutures; GM temporary abutments and screw-retained provisionals.
- Follow-up visits at 2, 4, 7 and 10 weeks and 3 and 4 months; provisionals recontoured at week 10, replaced at 3 and 4 months; final restorations delivered after contour accepted at 4 months; 3-year follow-up.
- Not reported: antibiotic use, insertion torque/ISQ, bone graft, buccal plate condition, extraction-to-implant timing details beyond same-visit placement.

## Results

- Patient and clinicians satisfied with soft tissue contour at 4 months; patient pleased with shape, contour and shade of the final restorations.
- At 3 years: patient satisfied, "no signs of peri-implant diseases were noted", radiographs show osseointegrated central-incisor implants.
- No numerical outcomes (marginal bone level, recession, PES/WES) reported. Authors' limitations: single case, need for larger series and longer follow-up.
