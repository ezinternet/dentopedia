## Three-line Summary
RCT (n=22 patients, maxillary anterior immediate implants with immediate provisionalization; 6-month follow-up) comparing flapless immediate implant placement with vs. without deproteinized bovine bone–collagen (DBBM+C) socket grafting.
DBBM+C group showed significantly greater soft tissue height at mesiobuccal and distobuccal sites at 3 and 6 months (p < 0.05), and higher buccolingual ridge dimension at 6 months (p < 0.05).
DBBM+C as an adjunct to immediate implant + immediate temporization preserves soft and hard tissue dimensions better than implant placement alone, supporting its use in esthetic-zone protocols.

## Summary
This single-center randomized controlled trial (n=22) evaluated whether grafting the residual socket gap with deproteinized bovine bone mineral and collagen (DBBM+C) during flapless immediate implant placement in the maxillary anterior region improves soft and hard tissue preservation compared with immediate implant placement alone, when both arms received immediate provisionalization. Patients were randomized 1:1 (11 test, 11 control) and followed for 6 months with clinical soft-tissue probing at mesiobuccal, midbuccal, and distobuccal sites plus CBCT-based buccolingual ridge-width measurement. The DBBM+C group maintained significantly greater soft tissue height at the mesiobuccal and distobuccal sites at both 3 and 6 months, and a significantly wider buccolingual ridge dimension at 6 months, with no implant failures or perioperative complications in either arm. These findings support grafting the extraction socket with a collagen-containing xenograft as a low-morbidity adjunct to immediate implant placement plus immediate temporization in esthetically demanding anterior sites, though the small single-center sample and the absence of PES/WES scoring limit how far the esthetic conclusion can be generalized.

## Key Contributions
- First RCT demonstrating that deproteinized bovine bone mineral with collagen (DBBM+C) preserves soft tissue height when used as a socket graft in immediate implant placement combined with immediate temporization
- Evidence that DBBM+C improves buccolingual (buccal–lingual) ridge width at 6 months post-implant versus no graft
- Site-specific, prospective quantification (mesiobuccal, midbuccal, distobuccal) of soft tissue contour change across the immediate-implant esthetic healing timeline

## Methodology
Single-center RCT; 22 patients requiring maxillary incisor extraction with anatomic feasibility for immediate implant placement, randomized 1:1 to test (flapless immediate implant + DBBM+C into the socket gap + immediate temporization) versus control (flapless immediate implant + immediate temporization, no graft). Soft tissue height was probed at baseline, 3 months, and 6 months at mesiobuccal, midbuccal, and distobuccal sites; hard tissue (buccolingual ridge dimension) was assessed by CBCT at baseline and 6 months. Follow-up: 6 months.

## Results

|---|---|---|---|
| Soft tissue height, MB & DB sites (3 mo) | Higher | Lower | < 0.05 |

Note Liu's pooled analysis found no significant soft-tissue-*thickness* benefit from collagen, so this trial's soft tissue *height* gain adds a metric the meta-analysis did not confirm — a partial extension rather than a clean replication.
- [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/zaki-2021-bone-substitute-materials-immediate-implant-sr-ma]] — reinforces: Zaki's 20-RCT SR+MA of bone-substitute-material vs. no graft at the immediate-implant gap found reduced horizontal buccal resorption and improved esthetic score; this trial's simpler two-arm DBBM+C-vs-no-graft design is directionally consistent, though Zaki also flags a complication signal (RR 3.50) that this trial does not report on — a reporting gap, not a contradiction.
- [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/seyssens-2022-immediate-implant-socket-grafting-sr-ma]] — reinforces: the Cosyn-group SR+MA (15 RCTs, n=577) concludes socket grafting reduces horizontal hard-tissue loss at single immediate implant placement; this trial's CBCT buccolingual-width finding is consistent individual-trial support, in a protocol (flapless + immediate provisionalization combined) not broken out separately in Seyssens' pooled estimate.
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/ionescu-2022-open-healing-flapless-ridge-preservation]] — different angle: same biomaterial combination (DBBM + collagen) but used for staged, flapless ridge preservation after extraction with no simultaneous implant, versus this trial's simultaneous immediate-implant + immediate-provisionalization protocol. Complementary evidence that DBBM+collagen performs well across two different clinical timing paradigms rather than a head-to-head comparison.
- [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/pitman-2022-immediate-implant-provisionalization-sr-ma]] — extends: Pitman's SR+MA established that immediate provisionalization itself (vs. delayed/no provisionalization) improves survival, marginal bone level, and soft-tissue outcomes at immediate implant placement, without isolating graft-material choice. This trial holds provisionalization constant across both arms and instead isolates the graft-material variable (DBBM+C vs. none), adding a materials-choice layer underneath Pitman's provisionalization-level finding.
- [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/kan-2003-immediate-placement-provisionalization-anterior]] — different angle / foundational: Kan's 2003 prospective study established the clinical feasibility of immediate placement plus immediate provisionalization in the anterior maxilla using HA-coated implants, without any socket-grafting comparison. This trial builds on the same clinical paradigm 16 years later to isolate a specific adjunct biomaterial's contribution rather than the provisionalization protocol itself.
