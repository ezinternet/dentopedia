## Three-line Summary
3-year prospective multicentre CBCT study (Staas/Meijer/Raghoebar group) of flapless immediate implant placement + provisionalization (FIIPP) in which the implant was systematically positioned ≥2 mm palatal to the buccal crest; buccal crest thickness and height were the primary CBCT outcomes.

Buccal crest thickness and height were maintained over 3 years, with implant survival within the expected high range; no comparator arm with non-palatal positioning was included.

These data provide quantitative mid-term evidence supporting the "≥2 mm palatal" positioning rule as a buccal-bone-preservation strategy in flapless IIPP.

## 세줄요약
Staas/Meijer/Raghoebar 그룹의 3년 다기관 전향적 CBCT 연구 — flapless 즉시식립·임시치관(FIIPP) 시 임플란트를 협측 능선에서 ≥2 mm 구개측에 일관 위치; 협측 능선 두께·높이가 1차 CBCT 결과.

3년 후 협측 능선 두께·높이 모두 보존됨; 생존율 양호; 비구개측 배위와의 대조군 없음.

"≥2 mm 구개측 위치" 원칙이 협측 골보존에 효과적임을 중기 정량 데이터로 뒷받침한다.

## Summary
Prospective multi-center CBCT study of flapless IIPP with implant positioned ≥2 mm palatal to the buccal crest, followed for 3 years. Demonstrates preservation of buccal crest thickness and height.

## Key Contributions
Strong prospective evidence for the "≥2 mm palatal" positioning rule as a buccal-bone-preservation strategy in IIPP, with mid-term follow-up.

## Methodology
Multi-center prospective cohort. Standardized flapless IIPP protocol with palatal positioning. CBCT measurements baseline and 3 years.

## Results
Buccal crest thickness and height maintained over 3 years in the cohort. Implant survival within expected high range.

## Limitations
No comparator arm with non-palatal positioning; flapless requires specific anatomy/operator skill.
