## Three-line Summary

Retrospective review (n=42 single-unit immediate implant restorations, mean 18.9 months in function) examining factors associated with midfacial mucosal recession.

Buccal shoulder position caused ~3× greater midfacial recession than lingual/palatal (1.8 vs 0.6 mm, p<0.001); tissue biotype showed a non-significant trend (thin 1.0 mm vs thick 0.7 mm, p=0.187).

Palatal implant positioning is the single most critical controllable factor for esthetic success in immediate anterior implant placement.

후향적 연구(n=42개 즉시식립 단일치 보철, 평균 18.9개월 기능 후) — 중안면 치은퇴축에 관련된 인자 분석.

협측 숄더 위치 시 설측 대비 약 3배의 중안면 퇴축(1.8 vs 0.6 mm, p<0.001); 조직 생물형은 비유의한 경향만(얇은형 1.0 mm vs 두꺼운형 0.7 mm, p=0.187).

심미부 즉시식립에서 구개측 임플란트 위치 선정이 심미 성공을 위한 가장 중요한 교정 가능 인자이다.

## Summary
Retrospective review of 42 immediate single-unit implant restorations, mean 18.9 months in function. Demonstrates that implant shoulder position (buccal vs lingual) is the dominant determinant of esthetic outcome — buccal shoulder placement results in 3× greater midfacial mucosal recession.

## Key Contributions
- Overall recession: 0.9±0.78mm (p=0.000) — statistically and clinically significant
- **Shoulder position**: buccal 1.8±0.83mm vs lingual 0.6±0.55mm recession (p=0.000) — most important factor
- Tissue biotype: thin 1.0mm vs thick 0.7mm recession — trend only (NS, p=0.187)
- No difference between implant systems (p=0.837)
- Clinical mandate: palatal implant positioning critical in anterior esthetic zone
