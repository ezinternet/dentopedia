## Three-line Summary

RCT-only SR+MA (5 RCTs, 245 subjects; 4 pooled; PubMed/Scopus/Cochrane, 2017–2024, ≥6-month follow-up) evaluating connective tissue grafting (CTG, simultaneous or deferred) at immediate implant placement in the esthetic zone; buccal gingival level was the primary outcome.

The pooled buccal gingival level difference favored CTG with a favorable but non-significant trend (MD 0.09 mm, 95% CI −0.54 to 0.72, p=0.05); the wide confidence interval reflects the small, heterogeneous evidence base.

CTG at immediate implant placement helps maintain — rather than increase — the gingival margin level and is favorable for esthetic outcomes, particularly when combined with an immediate provisional restoration.

## 세줄요약

즉시식립 시 결합조직이식(CTG, 동시 또는 지연)을 평가한 RCT only SR+MA (5 RCT, 245명; 4편 합성; ≥6개월 추적); 협측 치은 수준이 1차 결과.

풀링된 협측 치은 수준 차이는 CTG 유리 방향이나 유의하지 않음(MD 0.09 mm, p=0.05); 넓은 신뢰구간은 소표본·이질성을 반영.

즉시식립에서 CTG는 치은 수준을 '증가'시키기보다 '유지'시키며, 즉시임시치관과 병용 시 심미결과에 유리하다.

## Summary

This RCT-only meta-analysis (5 RCTs, 245 subjects; 4 pooled) evaluated connective tissue grafting (CTG) — simultaneous or deferred — at immediate implant placement in the esthetic zone. The pooled buccal gingival level difference favored CTG but was not statistically significant. The authors conclude that CTG helps maintain the gingival margin level rather than increase soft-tissue volume, and is favorable for esthetic outcomes when immediate placement with a provisional restoration is planned. The wide confidence interval reflects a small, heterogeneous evidence base.

## Key Contributions

- RCT-only synthesis of CTG at immediate implants.
- Frames CTG as level-maintaining rather than volume-increasing.

## Methodology

- SR+MA; PubMed/Scopus/Cochrane; PRISMA; RCTs 2017–2024, esthetic zone, ≥6-mo follow-up.

## Results

- 5 RCTs (n=245); 4 pooled.
- Buccal gingival level: MD 0.09 mm (−0.54 to 0.72, p=0.05) — NS, favorable trend.
