## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 12 RCTs (484 implants, 6–36 months): implant survival is high (96–100%) regardless of adjunctive approach; xenograft, CTG, and SST modestly improve peri-implant hard and soft tissue stability after IIP (GRADE: low-to-moderate certainty), but adjunctive procedures significantly increase short-term postoperative pain (WMD +19.45 mm VAS, 95% CI 0.55–38.36, p=0.04; GRADE: moderate, I²=96.9%).

Xenografts reduce horizontal ridge loss (Cardaropoli 2014: Δ1.23 mm less resorption; Girlanda 2019: greater buccal width); CTG reduces horizontal alveolar collapse and improves soft tissue contour (Guglielmi 2022: ORR1 −1.16 vs −2.09 mm, p=0.0003); SST reduces soft tissue change and improves PES (Venkatraman 2023: −0.15 vs −0.64 mm; PES 12.9 vs 11.2).

Evidence is limited by high heterogeneity, inconsistent outcome reporting (PES in only 4/12 RCTs), no standardized hard/soft tissue methods, and predominantly short follow-up; future research should integrate PROMs and standardize esthetic indices.

## 세줄요약

즉시식립(Immediate Implant Placement, IIP) 보조술식 12 RCT SR+MA(484개 임플란트, 6–36개월): 보조술식 종류와 무관하게 임플란트 생존율 96–100% 유지; 이종골(xenograft)·결합조직이식(Connective Tissue Graft, CTG)·소켓쉴드술식(Socket Shield Technique, SST)은 협측골·연조직 보존에 미미하게 유리하나(근거수준 낮음~보통), 보조술식은 단기 술후 통증을 유의하게 증가시킴(가중평균차(Weighted Mean Difference, WMD) +19.45mm 시각통증척도(Visual Analog Scale, VAS), p=0.04; 근거수준 보통).

이종골: 협측 치조제 폭경 소실 감소(Cardaropoli 2014: Δ1.23mm); CTG: 수평 치조제 보존 유리(Guglielmi 2022: ORR1 −1.16 vs −2.09mm, p<0.001); SST: 연조직 변화 감소 + 핑크심미점수(Pink Esthetic Score, PES) 향상(12.9 vs 11.2).

근거 한계: 높은 이질성(I²=97%), 비표준화 결과 측정(PES 4/12편만 보고), 단기 추적관찰 위주 — 보조술식의 편익이 술후 이환율 증가를 정당화하는지 결론 불가.

## Summary

This SR+MA exclusively enrolled RCTs comparing IIP + adjunctive procedure versus IIP alone in the esthetic zone. Twelve trials (484 participants, one implant each) contributed to qualitative synthesis; four RCTs provided data on postoperative pain for quantitative meta-analysis. The key finding is a consistent tension between potential tissue-level benefits of adjunctive procedures (modest bone/soft tissue preservation, improved PES in select trials) and a confirmed short-term morbidity cost (significantly higher pain scores). Implant survival remained uniformly high irrespective of approach. The absence of a recognized gold standard for IIP comparison makes this review a critical addition to defining evidence-based adjunctive indications.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA restricted to RCTs comparing IIP + adjunct vs IIP alone — no historical controls; rigorous comparison design
- Demonstrates implant survival is not improved by adjuncts (already 96–100%); justification must rest on tissue/esthetic outcomes
- Quantifies the morbidity cost of adjunctive procedures (WMD +19.45mm pain VAS, p=0.04) — patient-centered tradeoff data
- Identifies standardization crisis: PES reported in only 4/12 RCTs; PROMs inconsistently measured
- Establishes per-category evidence base: xenograft (7 RCTs, most evidence); CTG and SST (1 RCT each, early-stage)
- CTG: improves horizontal tissue preservation without vertical bone advantage; SST: best combined hard/soft tissue data

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA (PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD42025649944)
- PICO: Adults, IIP in esthetic zone; adjunctive material (xenograft, alloplast, mixed, CTG, SST); vs IIP alone; implant survival, esthetic scores (PES/WES), peri-implant tissue dimensions, postoperative morbidity
- Search: PubMed/MEDLINE + EMBASE + Scopus + OpenGray + Google Scholar, up to April 2025
- Inclusion: RCTs only; κ=0.91 inter-reviewer agreement
- RoB: Cochrane RoB 2; GRADE for each outcome
- Statistics: random-effects REML; WMD with 95% CI; I² for heterogeneity; 0–10 VAS → 0–100 conversion; NRS treated as VAS-equivalent

## Results

**Adjunctive categories:** 7 xenograft RCTs, 2 alloplastic, 1 mixed graft, 1 CTG, 1 SST

**Implant survival:** 96.0–100% across all groups and all adjunctive types — no adjunctive advantage

**Postoperative pain (meta-analysis, 4 RCTs):**
- WMD +19.45 mm (95% CI 0.55–38.36; p=0.04) favoring control (IIP alone)
- I²=96.9%; exploratory due to high heterogeneity and n=4

**Xenografts (7 RCTs):**
- Cardaropoli 2014 (12mo): ridge width Δ 0.69 vs 1.92mm; ridge height Δ −0.58 vs −1.69mm — grafted significantly better
- Girlanda 2019 (6mo): buccal width 6.57 vs 6.07mm at 1mm apical — grafted significantly better
- Fettouh 2023 (12mo): horizontal collapse 0.5±0.4 vs 0.8±0.4mm — grafted significantly less
- Jacobs 2020, Mastrangelo 2018: no significant hard/soft tissue difference; PES comparable or higher with graft
- Grassi 2019: no-flap/no-graft shows similar bone preservation to flap+graft when buccal plate intact

**CTG (1 RCT — Guglielmi 2022, 6mo):**
- Horizontal ridge: ORR1 −1.16±0.50 vs −2.09±0.53mm (p=0.0003) — CTG significantly better
- Soft tissue contour: Δ −0.32 to −0.04mm (CTG) vs −1.94 to −1.08mm (control) — p<0.05
- KTW gain: 0.60±1.71 vs 0.14±1.28mm
- Postoperative pain VAS (1 wk): 2.73±1.62 vs 1.07±0.70 — CTG significantly higher

**SST (1 RCT — Venkatraman 2023, 12mo):**
- Soft tissue change: −0.15±0.86mm (SST) vs −0.64±0.35mm (control); p<0.05
- PES T12: 12.9±0.8 (SST) vs 11.2±1.4 (control); p<0.05

**GRADE certainty:**
- Implant survival: moderate
- PES/esthetic: low
- Hard/soft tissue dimensions: low
- KTW (CTG): moderate
- Postoperative morbidity (pain): moderate (downgraded for inconsistency)

**RoB:** 2/12 low risk; 4/12 high risk (Domains 2, 4); 10/12 moderate-to-high overall
