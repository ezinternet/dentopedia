## Three-line Summary

First SR+MA exclusively evaluating Vestibular Socket Therapy (VST) for immediate implant placement (7 RCTs, 186 participants): implant survival 99.5%, no shield exposures or peri-implantitis; meta-analysis of three VST-vs-alternative trials shows no significant difference in buccal bone plate thickness at any level (crestal +0.24 mm NS, mid-root +0.32 mm NS, apical +0.19 mm NS; GRADE: very low for all bone outcomes).

VST showed minimal mid-facial recession and favorable Pink Esthetic Scores (10.29–13.17), with one trial demonstrating significant soft tissue advantage over early placement + contour augmentation; no adverse events (shield exposure, dehiscence, peri-implantitis) across all trials.

All 7 RCTs originate from one closely related investigator network in Egypt; independent multicenter replication required before clinical recommendations can be made.

## 세줄요약

전정접근 소켓 치료(Vestibular Socket Therapy, VST)를 이용한 즉시식립(Immediate Implant Placement, IIP)에 대한 첫 체계적 고찰·메타분석(RCT 7편, 186명): 임플란트 생존율 99.5%, 차폐 노출·주위염 없음; VST vs 비교군의 협측골판 두께 메타분석은 모든 수준에서 유의차 없음(근거수준 매우 낮음).

협측 연조직 후퇴 최소, 핑크심미점수(Pink Esthetic Score, PES) 10.29–13.17; 한 편에서 조기식립+윤곽증대 대비 VST의 연조직 유의 우위(Δ1.34 mm) 확인.

7편 모두 이집트 단일 연구자 네트워크 산출물 — 독립적 다기관 복제 전에는 임상 권고 불가.

## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis represents the first synthesis of randomized evidence exclusively evaluating VST — a minimally invasive technique using vestibular tunnel access and a flexible cortical xenogeneic membrane to reconstruct the deficient buccal plate without crestal incision — in conjunction with IIP. Seven RCTs (186 participants, 15 arms) enrolled patients predominantly with Type II extraction sockets (partial/complete loss of facial bone) in the maxillary esthetic zone. Meta-analysis of three trials comparing VST against established alternative techniques showed no statistically significant difference in final buccal bone plate thickness at any anatomical level, with very low certainty (GRADE). The overlapping authorship of this review and the included trials is an acknowledged limitation; independent replication is required.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA restricted to RCTs evaluating VST with IIP (2020–2026)
- 99.5% implant survival; no peri-implant complications across any trial
- No buccal bone plate thickness advantage over comparators (NS at crestal, mid-root, and apical levels)
- Cortical membrane barrier (not graft volume alone) identified as likely key determinant of crestal bone preservation
- VST demonstrates less mid-facial recession vs early placement + contour augmentation (Δ1.34 mm; Elaskary 2022) and equivalent outcomes vs PET and dual-zone
- Geographic and operator independence entirely lacking — all 7 trials from Egyptian VST originator group

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA per PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420261288724
- PICO: Adults ≥18 yr with compromised fresh extraction sockets; VST with IIP; vs early placement+contour augmentation, PET, dual-zone; buccal bone plate thickness (primary), implant survival, MML, papilla, PES
- Search: PubMed/MEDLINE + EMBASE, up to January 2026; 2 databases only
- Eligibility: RCTs only; VST per original Elaskary 2020 protocol
- RoB: Cochrane RoB 2; evidence certainty: GRADE
- Statistics: random-effects (DerSimonian–Laird), MD with 95% CI; meta-analysis only for VST vs distinct alternatives (3 trials); 4 intra-VST comparisons reported narratively

## Results

- 312 records → 7 RCTs included; 20–40 participants each
- RoB: 6/7 "some concerns"; 1/7 high risk
- **Buccal bone plate thickness (3 pooled comparisons):**
  - Crestal: MD +0.24 mm (95% CI −0.13 to 0.61; I²=45%) — NS
  - Mid-root: MD +0.32 mm (95% CI −0.45 to 1.09; I²=82%) — NS
  - Apical: MD +0.19 mm (95% CI −0.48 to 0.87; I²=67%) — NS
- **GRADE**: very low certainty for all three outcomes (risk of bias + imprecision + publication bias + inconsistency)
- **Survival**: 99.5% (185/186)
- **MML**: VST −0.53 mm vs contour augmentation −1.87 mm (Δ1.34 mm; p<0.001; one trial)
- **PES**: 10.29–13.17; 91.6% achieved PES >10
- **VST vs PET**: equivalent at 6 months (one trial)
- **Bone shielding vs dual-zone**: significantly greater crestal/mid-root bone with bone shielding (Elaskary 2024)
