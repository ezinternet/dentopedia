## Three-line Summary
Prospective cohort study using pre-operative CBCT-derived labial bone thickness stratification to quantify its impact on PES/WES esthetic outcomes after immediate implant placement with immediate provisionalization (IIPP) in the esthetic zone.

Esthetic outcomes deteriorated as labial bone thickness decreased; a clinically meaningful inflection in PES/WES scores was observed around the 1–2 mm labial plate threshold.

These findings provide direct outcome evidence underpinning the "1–2 mm labial plate" case-selection rule for IIPP; the non-randomized cohort design and population-specific threshold values limit universal application.

## 세줄요약
심미부 즉시식립·즉시임시치관(IIPP) 후 PES/WES에 대한 술전 CBCT 협측 골두께의 영향을 정량화한 전향적 코호트 연구.

협측 골두께가 줄수록 심미결과 악화; PES/WES 점수에서 임상적으로 의미 있는 변곡이 협측판 1–2 mm 기준 부근에서 관찰됨.

IIPP의 "협측판 1–2 mm" 증례선택 기준을 뒷받침하는 직접적 결과 근거 제공; 비무작위 코호트 설계 및 인구집단 특이적 임계값 적용의 한계가 있다.

## Summary
Prospective cohort study quantifying the impact of labial bone thickness on PES/WES esthetic outcomes after immediate implant placement with immediate provisionalization in the esthetic zone.

## Key Contributions
Provides direct outcome evidence underpinning the "1–2 mm labial plate" rule of thumb in IIPP — useful for case-selection thresholding.

## Methodology
Prospective cohort. Pre-op CBCT-derived labial bone thickness stratification. PES/WES, recession, and bone-level outcomes at follow-up.

## Results
Esthetic outcomes deteriorate as labial bone thickness decreases. The 1–2 mm threshold shows a clinically meaningful inflection.

## Limitations
Cohort (non-randomized); follow-up bound by study horizon; threshold values still population-specific.
