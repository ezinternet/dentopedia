## Three-line Summary
SR+MA of RCT and prospective studies comparing immediate implant placement with vs without immediate provisionalization in the esthetic zone.

5-year survival was ~97% in both groups (equivalent); the immediate-provisionalization group showed better papilla preservation and soft-tissue contour.

Jumping-gap management (graft material choice) is more predictive of hard-tissue outcomes than provisionalization alone.

## 세줄요약
즉시식립 시 임시치관 유무를 비교한 SR+MA — 전향적 연구·RCT 포함.

5년 생존율 약 97%로 양 군 동등; 즉시임시치관 군에서 치유두 보존 및 연조직 외형이 더 유리했다.

점프핑 gap 이식재 관리가 경조직 결과의 핵심 결정 인자이며, 임시치관 단독 효과보다 중요하다.

## Summary
SR+MA comparing immediate implant placement with vs without immediate provisionalization on survival, MBL, and soft tissue outcomes.

## Key Contributions
- Survival rates equivalent regardless of immediate provisionalization
- Immediate provisional group: better papilla preservation and soft tissue contour
- Buccal gap management (graft material) more predictive than provisionalization alone

## Methodology
PubMed/Medline/Cochrane systematic search; RCT and prospective studies.

## Results
5-year survival ~97% in both groups. Immediate provisional benefits soft tissue esthetics. Jumping gap management remains critical for hard tissue outcomes.
