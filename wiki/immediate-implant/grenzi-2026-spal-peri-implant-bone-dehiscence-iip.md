## Three-line Summary
Case study (n=2, Clin Adv Periodontics 2026) describing the SPAL (subperiosteal peri-implant augmented layer) technique applied simultaneously with immediate implant placement (IIP) to correct peri-implant bone dehiscence (PIBD).

Both cases achieved buccal bone-volume restoration and primary wound closure without membrane exposure, offering an operator-demanding but membrane-free dehiscence correction option at IIP.

Clinical implication: SPAL is a technique option for managing buccal bone dehiscence at immediate implant placement without conventional membrane use; evidence remains case-level (n=2) and operator skill demands are high.

## 세줄요약
증례 보고 (n=2, Clin Adv Periodontics 2026) — 즉시식립 (IIP) 동시 골 열개 (peri-implant bone dehiscence, PIBD) 교정을 위한 골막하 주위 증대층 (subperiosteal peri-implant augmented layer, SPAL) 기법 적용.

두 증례 모두 협측 골 부피 회복 및 차폐막 없이 일차 창상 폐쇄 달성.

임상 적용: SPAL은 즉시식립 시 협측 열개 관리에 차폐막 없이 가능한 술식 옵션 — 근거수준은 증례급(n=2)이며 술자 기술 요구도 높음.

## Summary
Case study describing the SPAL (subperiosteal peri-implant augmented layer) technique applied at immediate implant placement to correct peri-implant bone dehiscence.

## Key Contributions
Operationalizes the SPAL technique in IIP context, complementing other dehiscence-correction approaches (Trombelli 2024 BioARP) by the same group.

## Methodology
Case study of two patients undergoing IIP at compromised sockets with SPAL technique. Step-by-step surgical description with imaging follow-up.

## Results
Reported successful bone-volume restoration on the buccal aspect, primary closure, and acceptable peri-implant outcomes.

## Limitations
Case-level evidence (n=2); operator skill demanding.
