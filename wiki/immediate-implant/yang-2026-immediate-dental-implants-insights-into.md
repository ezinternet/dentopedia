## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center cohort (Xiamen, 2013-2022; 1,513 immediate implants in 781 patients; 50 early and 36 late failures; 10-year implant-level cumulative survival 93.15%): factors for early (pre-loading) and late failure analysed with marginal Cox regression.

Early failure: male sex HR 2.48, sinus elevation HR 2.49, maxillary anterior HR 3.15 and posterior HR 2.75 (vs mandibular posterior) increased risk; submerged healing lowered it (HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.30-0.95). Smoking was not collected, so no smoking analysis or smoking-by-healing-protocol interaction was possible.

Submerged healing was chosen when insertion torque was below 20-25 Ncm, so the association is subject to confounding by indication and cannot be read as causal; late-failure findings rest on only 36 events.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일기관 코호트 (중국 샤먼, 2013-2022; 환자 781명, 즉시식립 임플란트 1,513개; 조기 실패 50개·후기 실패 36개; 10년 임플란트 단위 누적생존율 93.15%): 주변 Cox 회귀 (marginal Cox regression)로 조기 (보철 부하 전)·후기 실패 관련 인자 분석.

조기 실패는 남성 (HR 2.48), 상악동 거상 (Sinus Floor Elevation, HR 2.49), 상악 전치부 (HR 3.15)·상악 구치부 (HR 2.75; 기준 하악 구치부)에서 증가하고, 매몰형 치유 (submerged healing)에서 감소 (HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.30-0.95). 흡연은 수집되지 않아 흡연 분석과 흡연×치유방식 상호작용 검정은 불가능했음.

매몰형 치유는 삽입 토크 20-25 Ncm 미만일 때 선택되어 적응증 교란 (confounding by indication)이 있고 인과로 읽을 수 없음; 후기 실패는 36건뿐이라 결과가 탐색적임.

## Summary

This retrospective cohort from one Chinese implant department analysed 1,513 immediate implants (781 patients) placed 2013-2022 and separated early failure (implant loss before prosthetic loading, within about 3-6 months) from late failure (loss after loading). Implant-level cumulative survival was 93.15% at 10 years and patient-level 89.46%. In the multivariable marginal Cox model, male sex, sinus elevation and maxillary sites raised early-failure hazard, while submerged healing lowered it. Smoking was not collected, so the paper cannot say whether the smoking effect differs by healing protocol.

## Key Contributions

- Immediate-placement-only cohort with early/late failure analysed separately (86 losses: 50 early, 36 late).
- Submerged healing associated with lower early failure (HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.30-0.95) after adjustment for surgeon experience, implant position and bone quality.
- Honest confounder disclosure: smoking, oral hygiene, bruxism, occlusal force, systemic disease and medication were unavailable.

## Methodology

- Retrospective EMR cohort, single center (Xiamen), adults with at least one immediate implant; observation to 31 Oct 2023; STROBE.
- Early failure = loss before prosthetic loading (within 3-6 months); late failure = loss after loading.
- Marginal Cox regression (Wei-Lin-Weissfeld) at implant level to handle several implants per patient; FDR-corrected univariate screening; adjusted for surgeon experience, implant position, bone quality.
- Healing mode: "Healing mode (submerged or non-submerged) was selected based on primary stability. Submerged healing was generally selected when insertion torque was < 20-25 Ncm." Above 35 Ncm an immediate temporary restoration was placed, otherwise healing abutments; implants were placed flapless whenever feasible. The paper gives no further definition of "submerged" (no explicit cover-screw or flap-closure wording).
- Smoking was not a covariate and no interaction or stratified analysis by healing protocol was reported.

## Results

| Outcome | Factor | HR (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|
| Early failure | Male sex | 2.48 (1.31-4.69) |
| Early failure | Maxillary sinus elevation | 2.49 (1.05-5.87) |
| Early failure | Maxillary anterior site (vs mandibular posterior) | 3.15 (1.29-7.69) |
| Early failure | Maxillary posterior site (vs mandibular posterior) | 2.75 (1.08-6.98) |
| Early failure | Submerged healing | 0.53 (0.30-0.95) |
| Late failure | Bone graft without membrane | 3.54 (1.07-11.74) |
| Late failure | Diameter 4.0-4.5 mm vs <= 3.75 mm | 0.30 (0.13-0.69) |

Not significant for early failure: age, periodontitis, diameter, length, surgeon experience, bone grafting approach (graft-only trended higher). Not significant for late failure: age, sex, periodontitis, position, sinus elevation, surgeon experience, healing method, length. Survival: 95.57% (1 y), 94.85% (3 y), 93.86% (5 y), 93.15% (10 y) at implant level.

Caveats for use: healing mode was assigned by insertion torque (low torque to submerged), so the direction of confounding by indication should, if anything, favour non-submerged, yet the reverse was seen; the mechanism proposed by the authors (less fibrous encapsulation and microbial contamination) is speculative. The male-sex effect may reflect unmeasured smoking or hygiene per the authors. Only 36 late failures make late-failure HRs unstable. Retrieved text lacked table contents, so per-group counts are not available.
