## One-line Summary
Multicenter RCT (n=39, 1 year) comparing 7mm vs 5mm immediate molar implants found no difference in survival, MBL, or esthetics, but significantly more complications with 7mm (4 vs 0 patients, p=0.047).

## 한줄요약
다기관 RCT (39명, 1년): 대구치 즉시 식립 7mm vs 5mm — 생존율·변연골소실·연조직 미관 차이 없으나 7mm에서 경미 합병증 유의하게 더 많음 (21.1% vs 0%, p=0.047).

## Summary
This multicenter RCT across 5 centres randomised 39 patients (19: 7mm, 20: 5mm BioHorizons Camlog implants) receiving single immediate post-extraction implants in molar sites. Bone gaps were grafted with bone substitute; definitive zirconia crowns were delivered at 4 months; outcomes assessed at 1 year post-loading. No implant failure occurred in the 7mm group (vs 1 failure in the 5mm group), and no significant differences emerged in marginal bone loss (0.83 vs 0.45mm) or pink esthetic scores. However, the 7mm group experienced significantly more minor complications (4 patients vs 0, p=0.047). Given the small sample, authors caution that these are preliminary data and call for larger RCTs.

## Key Contributions
- Provides 1-year survival, MBL, and PES data for wide-diameter (7mm) vs conventional (5mm) immediate molar implants — fills a gap left by earlier Checchi 2017 study
- Demonstrates that esthetic and bone-level outcomes are comparable between diameters at 1 year
- Identifies significantly higher complication risk with 7mm implants — clinically actionable finding even with small n
- Numerically greater MBL in 7mm group (0.83 vs 0.45mm) warrants longer follow-up monitoring
- Korean + European multicenter design provides cross-population generalizability

## Methodology
- **Design**: Multicenter parallel-arm RCT, 5 centres (Korea + Europe); 1:1 randomisation
- **Population**: 39 patients, single immediate post-extraction molar implant need; 0 dropouts
- **Implants**: BioHorizons Camlog 7.0mm (n=19) vs 5.0mm (n=20); gaps grafted with bone substitute
- **Restoration**: Definitive zirconia crown at 4 months post-placement
- **Follow-up**: 1 year after loading
- **Outcomes**: Implant failure, complications, patient satisfaction, MBL, PES

## Results

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failure | 0 (0%) | 1 (5%) | −5.0% (−14.6% to 4.6%) | 1.000 |
| Complications (patients) | 4 (21.1%) | 0 (0%) | +21.1% (2.7%–39.4%) | **0.047** |
| MBL at 1y (mm) | 0.83±1.56 | 0.45±1.19 | +0.39 (−0.53 to 1.30) | 0.396 |
