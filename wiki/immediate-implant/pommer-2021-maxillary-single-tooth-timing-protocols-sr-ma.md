## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of maxillary single-tooth implants in the aesthetic zone with ≥3-year follow-up, cross-tabulating implant placement timing (immediate/early/delayed) against loading timing (immediate/early/delayed); 7 controlled trials meta-analyzed plus pooled data from 29 studies total (965 implants).

No statistically significant differences in long-term survival or marginal bone remodeling were found across any placement-times-loading timing combination tested (e.g. immediate placement + immediate vs. delayed loading, p=.306); data were insufficient to meta-analyze the full combination matrix.

Available long-term evidence does not support preferring one placement/loading timing protocol over another for maxillary single-tooth implants — but the evidence base itself remains too sparse for definitive combination-level conclusions.

## 세줄요약

상악 심미부위 단일치아 임플란트를 ≥3년 추적한 체계적고찰+메타분석; 식립 타이밍(즉시식립 Immediate Placement, IP / 조기식립 Early Placement, EP / 지연식립 Delayed Placement, DP)과 부하 타이밍(즉시부하 Immediate Loading, IL / 조기부하 Early Loading, EL / 지연부하 Delayed Loading, DL)을 교차 비교 — 대조군 연구 7편 메타분석 + 총 29편(임플란트 965개) pooled 분석.

식립×부하 타이밍 조합 어디서도 장기 생존율·변연골개조(Marginal Bone Remodeling)에 유의한 차이 없음(예: 즉시식립+즉시부하 vs 지연부하 p=.306); 전체 조합 매트릭스를 메타분석하기엔 근거가 부족.

현재 근거로는 상악 단일치아 임플란트에서 특정 식립/부하 타이밍 프로토콜을 우선할 근거가 없다 — 다만 근거기반 자체가 조합별 비교로는 아직 희박함.

## Summary

This ITI Consensus Conference companion systematic review and meta-analysis (COIR 2021, Suppl 21) asked whether long-term outcomes of maxillary single-tooth implants in the aesthetic zone differ by the timing of implant placement relative to extraction (immediate IP / early EP / delayed DP) and the timing of prosthetic loading (immediate IL / early EL / delayed DL). Electronic and manual searches identified studies reporting ≥3-year survival rate and/or marginal bone resorption. Seven controlled trials were meta-analyzed across three timing contrasts (IPIL vs. IPDL, DPIL vs. DPDL, EPDL vs. DPDL), and single-arm data from 29 studies (965 implants) were pooled separately. No significant differences emerged for any comparison, nor for marginal bone remodeling, nor for secondary technique factors (one abutment-one time, flap design, simultaneous bone/soft-tissue augmentation). The authors conclude that data remain insufficient to meta-analytically compare all possible placement×loading combinations.

## Key Contributions

- First SR/MA to cross-tabulate implant placement timing AND loading timing together for long-term (≥3y) maxillary single-tooth implant outcomes, rather than analyzing placement timing in isolation.
- Restricts scope to the aesthetic zone, matching the clinical stakes where timing decisions are most debated.
- Combines controlled-trial meta-analysis with pooled single-arm data to extract maximal signal from a sparse comparative-trial literature.
- Tests for confounding by one abutment-one time concept, flap design, and simultaneous augmentation — none showed detectable impact.

## Methodology

- Systematic review with meta-analysis; electronic + manual literature search.
- Population: maxillary single-tooth implants, aesthetic zone.
- Timing variables: Placement (IP/EP/DP) × Loading (IL/EL/DL).
- Outcome window: ≥3 years — survival rate and/or marginal bone resorption.
- 7 controlled trials meta-analyzed across 3 timing contrasts; 29 single-arm studies (965 implants) pooled for descriptive comparison.

## Results

| Comparison | Studies | Result |
|---|---|---|
| IPIL vs. IPDL (immediate placement, immediate vs. delayed loading) | 3 | NS, p=.306 |
| DPIL vs. DPDL (delayed placement, immediate vs. delayed loading) | 2 | NS, p=1.000 |
| EPDL vs. DPDL (early vs. delayed placement, delayed loading) | 2 | NS, p=.600 |
| Pooled analysis (all timing protocols) | 29 studies, 965 implants | No differences in survival or marginal bone remodeling by placement or loading timing |

No impact of one abutment-one time concept, flap design, or simultaneous bone/soft-tissue augmentation could be established. The authors explicitly note insufficient data to meta-analytically compare all placement×loading combinations (only 3 of 9 theoretical combinations had ≥2 controlled trials).
