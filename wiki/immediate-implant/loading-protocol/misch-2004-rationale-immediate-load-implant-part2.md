## Three-line Summary

Narrative biomechanical review (Implant Dentistry, 2004, Part II) on immediate loading of implants in edentulous and partially edentulous patients.

Overload risk is reduced by controlling stress on the transitional restoration, which depends on patient factors, implant position, cantilevers, load direction, contact intensity and diet.

The abstract gives no clinical outcome data; it is a rationale, not evidence that immediate loading is safe.

## Summary

Misch gives a stress-management checklist for transitional restorations on immediately loaded implants. The practical content is which factors raise stress at the developing interface, and which design choices spread load.

## Key Contributions

- Stress-management checklist for immediate-loading transitional restorations.
- Links load-distribution area to implant size, design and surface.

## Methodology

Narrative biomechanical review without a stated search method.

## Results

Qualitative; no outcome numbers in the abstract.
