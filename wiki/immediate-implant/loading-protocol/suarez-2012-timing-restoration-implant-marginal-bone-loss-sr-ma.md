## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 11 studies (8 RCTs, 2 CCTs, 1 retrospective; literature searched to November 2011) comparing marginal bone loss (MBL) between immediate restoration/loading (IR/L), early loading (EL), and conventional loading (CL) in four separate pairwise group analyses covering both delayed-placement and immediate-placement implants. The largest meta-analysis (IR/L + delayed placement vs CL + delayed placement; 6 studies) yielded a 0.09 mm MBL difference (95% CI −0.27 to 0.09 mm; P=0.33) favoring IR/L but not statistically significant; the remaining three pairwise comparisons were also non-significant after adjusting for implant placement level. Loading protocol selection should be guided by factors other than MBL.

## 세줄요약

수복 타이밍이 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)에 미치는 영향을 분석한 SR+MA (11편, 2011년 11월까지); 즉시수복/부하 (Immediate Restoration/Loading, IR/L) vs 기존 부하 (Conventional Loading, CL) 비교(6편)에서 MBL 차이 0.09 mm (P=0.33)로 유의하지 않고, 나머지 3개 비교군도 모두 비유의. 따라서 수복 타이밍은 MBL에 영향을 미치지 않으며, 임상 프로토콜 선택은 MBL이 아닌 다른 요인에 근거해야 한다.

## Methods

**Study design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis
**Databases searched**: 3 electronic databases + hand search; searched until November 2011
**Initial yield**: 1,640 articles screened → 27 full texts evaluated → 11 studies qualified
**Study types**: 8 RCTs, 2 CCTs, 1 retrospective cohort
**Inclusion criteria**: Human clinical studies; MBL comparison between ≥2 loading protocols; ≥12-month follow-up; ≥10 implants per group

**Four comparison groups:**

| Group | Comparison | Placement | n studies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Immediate restoration/loading (IR/L) vs Conventional loading (CL) | Delayed placement (DP) | 6 |
| 2 | Immediate restoration (IR) vs Early loading (EL) | Delayed placement (DP) | 2 |
| 3 | Early loading (EL) vs Conventional loading (CL) | Delayed placement (DP) | 1 |
| 4 | Immediate loading (IL) vs Conventional loading (CL) | Immediate placement (IP) | 2 |

## Results

| Group | MBL difference | 95% CI | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| IR/L + DP vs CL + DP (n=6) | 0.09 mm (favoring IR/L) | −0.27 to 0.09 mm | P=0.33 (ns) |
| IR + DP vs EL + DP (n=2) | No significant difference | — | ns |
| EL + DP vs CL + DP (n=1) | No significant difference | — | ns |
| IL + IP vs CL + IP (n=2) | No significant difference | — | ns |

**Risk of bias**: Eight RCTs identified as having moderate-to-high risk of publication bias.

## Clinical Takeaway

The meta-analysis demonstrates that MBL is equivalent across immediate restoration/loading, early loading, and conventional loading protocols — for both delayed-placement and immediately-placed implants. This null finding has direct clinical implications:

- MBL preservation alone should **not** drive loading protocol selection
- Protocol choice should instead be based on primary stability achieved (insertion torque, ISQ), patient risk factors, esthetic requirements, and operator experience
- For immediately placed implants specifically, the equivalence of IL vs CL + IP MBL supports immediate loading when primary stability criteria are met
