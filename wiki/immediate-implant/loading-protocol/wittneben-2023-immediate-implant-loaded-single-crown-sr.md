## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (Wittneben et al., 2023) of immediately placed and immediately loaded single-tooth implant restorations examining survival, marginal bone loss, and esthetic outcomes across included studies.

Five-year survival was 97.1%, 1-year marginal bone loss −0.32 mm, and anterior pink esthetic score 7.2±1.8; ISQ ≥70 and insertion torque ≥35 N·cm were recommended thresholds for immediate loading.

Buccal bone grafting at placement was associated with better MBL stability, supporting routine gap grafting in the immediate-loading protocol.

## Summary
Systematic review of clinical performance (survival, MBL, esthetic outcomes) of immediately placed and loaded single-tooth implant restorations.

## Key Contributions
- 5-year survival rate 97.1% for immediately placed + loaded implants
- MBL at 1 year: -0.32mm; esthetic PES score 7.2±1.8
- Buccal bone graft at placement associated with better MBL stability

## Methodology
Systematic literature search + qualitative synthesis.

## Results
Immediately placed and loaded implants: excellent survival and acceptable bone/soft tissue outcomes. ISQ ≥70 and insertion torque ≥35 N·cm recommended for immediate loading.
