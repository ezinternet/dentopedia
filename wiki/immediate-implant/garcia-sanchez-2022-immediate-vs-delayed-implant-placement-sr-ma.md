## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis addressing two focused questions on immediate versus delayed dental implant placement (search through November 2019): Q1 pooled 6 head-to-head RCTs comparing immediate vs delayed placement; Q2 pooled 53 studies (22 RCTs, 11 CCTs, 20 case series) of immediate placement alone.

Q1 meta-analyses found no significant difference in implant survival between immediate and delayed placement, but immediate placement showed significantly better marginal bone levels and Pink Esthetic Score (PES) at 1-year post-loading; Q2 found immediate implants had a 97% survival rate and high PES scores (10.36-11.25).

Despite similar survival, immediate implants carried threefold early and twofold delayed complication rates versus delayed placement — success/complication reporting criteria need to be more consistent across future studies.

## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis (search through November 2019; MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane CENTRAL, OpenGray + hand search) tackled the immediate-vs-delayed implant placement timing question with an unusually clean design split. Q1 restricted eligibility to RCTs with a delayed-placement control arm (6 papers), yielding a true head-to-head comparative effect size. Q2 broadened eligibility to any immediate-implant arm — RCT arms, CCTs, and prospective case series (53 papers total: 22 RCTs, 11 CCTs, 20 case series) — to estimate the absolute effect size of immediate placement across eight outcome domains: survival, success, radiographic marginal bone levels, buccal wall height/thickness, peri-implant mucosal margin position, esthetics, and patient-reported outcomes.

The headline finding is a dissociation between survival and other outcomes: Q1's RCT-only pooling found no significant survival difference between immediate and delayed placement, but immediate placement was significantly favored for both marginal bone levels and PES at 1-year post-loading. Q2's broader pooling of immediate-implant arms alone found a 97% survival rate and PES scores of 10.36-11.25 — consistent with immediate placement performing well in absolute terms, though this arm is not directly compared against delayed placement.

Complication reporting is the paper's most clinically actionable — and most under-emphasized — finding: immediate implants showed roughly threefold early complications and twofold delayed complications relative to delayed placement, despite equivalent survival. This decouples "implant survives" from "the course to that survival was uneventful," a distinction easily lost when survival is used as the sole headline metric. Marginal bone loss and gingival/papillary recession data varied substantially across included studies and were not reducible to a single pooled estimate.

Only the structured abstract was retrieved for this ingest (no PMC full text available); risk-of-bias methodology, heterogeneity statistics, and funnel-plot/publication-bias analysis are not captured in this summary.

## Key Contributions

- Separates the immediate-vs-delayed question into a narrow RCT-only comparative analysis (Q1) and a broad pooled single-arm analysis (Q2), rather than blending heterogeneous study designs into one pooled estimate — this design choice is itself a useful evidence-appraisal teaching point (see Related Papers below).
- Meta-analyzes across eight distinct outcome domains (survival, success, MBL, buccal wall height/thickness, mucosal margin position, esthetics, PROMs) rather than survival alone.
- Explicitly quantifies the complication-rate cost of immediate placement (3x early, 2x delayed) even where survival is statistically equivalent — a dimension frequently missing from timing-comparison summaries.

## Methodology

- **Search**: MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane CENTRAL, OpenGray + hand search, to November 2019.
- **Q1 eligibility**: RCTs with delayed implant placement as the control — 6 papers included.
- **Q2 eligibility**: immediate-implant arms of RCTs, CCTs, and prospective case series — 53 papers included (22 RCTs, 11 CCTs, 20 case series).
- **Outcomes assessed**: survival rate, success rate, radiographic marginal bone levels, buccal wall height, buccal wall thickness, peri-implant mucosal margin position, esthetic outcomes, patient-reported outcomes.
- Abstract-only retrieval (`full_text: false`) — statistical model, risk-of-bias tool, and heterogeneity (I²) figures were not extracted from this source.

## Results

| Question | Pool | Survival | Other outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 (comparative) | 6 RCTs | No significant difference, immediate vs delayed | MBL and PES significantly favor immediate at 1-year post-loading |
| Q2 (pooled single-arm) | 53 studies (22 RCT + 11 CCT + 20 case series) | ~97% survival (immediate implants) | PES range 10.36-11.25 |

- Complications: immediate implants ~3x early complications and ~2x delayed complications versus delayed placement.
- Marginal bone loss and gingival/papillary recession data varied widely across included studies — not reducible to a single pooled figure per the abstract.
