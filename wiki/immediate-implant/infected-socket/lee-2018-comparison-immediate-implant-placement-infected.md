## Summary

Based on articles retrieved from PubMed, this systematic review ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/00016357.2018.1453084)) from Seoul National University analyzed 5 nonrandomized CCTs (meta-analysis in 3) comparing immediate implant placement in infected vs non-infected sockets. The primary finding was a non-significant risk difference of -0.02 for survival. For secondary parameters (MBL 0.32mm, PD 0.12mm, mBI 0.07, MGL -0.06mm), no statistical differences were found. The exception was WKG, which showed a significant increase in the infected group — suggesting more challenging aesthetic outcomes. The authors concluded that implants can be safely placed in infected sockets after thorough debridement and that WKG should be considered for aesthetic zone cases. This foundational work preceded larger meta-analyses (Saijeva 2020, Pranckeviciene 2024) that confirmed and extended its conclusions.

## Key Contributions

- Pioneering Korean-authored SR on infected socket IIP (Seoul National University)
- Explicitly framed WKG as an aesthetic management priority in infected socket cases
- Documented the evidence gap (0 RCTs as of 2017) — spurred subsequent larger studies
- Provided WMD data for 6 parameters despite limited sample

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR+MA (fixed-effects model)
- **Search**: Electronic + manual, up to March 2017
- **Study types**: RCTs and CCTs (0 RCTs found; 5 CCTs)
- **Meta-analysis**: 3 studies included
- **Primary outcome**: survival rate (risk difference)
- **Secondary**: MBL, PD, mBI, MGL, WKG (WMDs)
