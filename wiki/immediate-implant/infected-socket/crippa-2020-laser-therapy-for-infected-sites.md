## Three-line Summary

Single case report (n=1, 40-year-old woman, maxillary left lateral incisor 2.2 with periapical periodontitis after failed apicectomy) plus a small non-systematic literature search (PubMed, 1980-2019; 4 clinical laser-plus-immediate-implant articles found).

The socket was decontaminated with an ErCr:YSGG 2780 nm laser (60-90 s), a 3.3 x 10 mm Straumann Roxolid SLA implant was placed at >=35 N with Bio-Oss and a membrane, loaded at 4 months; no implant loss, peri-implantitis or peri-implant bone loss was reported at 5 years (no numerical bone data given).

One uncontrolled case cannot show that laser decontamination changes the outcome of immediate placement in infected sockets; it is hypothesis-level illustration only, and the lesion is not classified as acute or chronic.

## Summary

This case report with a short literature search ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/2328398)) describes one 40-year-old woman whose maxillary left lateral incisor had periapical periodontitis after a failed apicectomy. After atraumatic extraction and flap elevation, the socket was debrided and decontaminated with an Er,Cr:YSGG 2780 nm laser (60-90 s per socket), and a 3.3 x 10 mm Straumann Roxolid SLA implant was placed at >=35 N with Bio-Oss and a barrier membrane, with primary closure. The authors report no implant loss, peri-implantitis or peri-implant bone loss at 5 years. The paper does not classify the lesion as acute or chronic and does not mention purulence or a fistula. Evidence value is very low: one uncontrolled case, and the laser effect cannot be separated from curettage, antibiotics, chlorhexidine and grafting.

## Key Contributions

- Documents explicit laser parameters for socket decontamination before immediate implant placement.
- Reports a single esthetic-zone infected-site immediate implant with a stated 5-year follow-up.
- Identifies (non-systematically) only four clinical laser-plus-immediate-implant articles and calls for an RCT.

## Methodology

- Case report plus PICO-guided PubMed search (1980-2019); no appraisal of included studies.
- Extraction of 2.2 (previous failed apicectomy), full-thickness flap, laser hard-tissue mode 2.0 W / 15 Hz / 40% air / 60% water / 100 mL/min with MZ-6 tip, no tip-tissue contact.
- Implant 3.3 x 10 mm RN, >=35 N, 1 mm below the most apical bone peak; Bio-Oss + GUIDOR matrix barrier (abstract: "resorbable membrane"); PTFE 3/0 primary closure.
- Amoxicillin 1 g x2/day for 6 days; chlorhexidine 0.20% twice daily for 15-20 days; Maryland bridge provisional; loaded at 4 months.

## Results

| Item | Report |
|---|---|
| Implant loss / peri-implantitis / bone loss | None reported |
| Primary stability | >35 N |
| Esthetics | "Satisfactory" at 2-year check (no index) |
| Follow-up | 5 years stated; radiographs at 1, 4, 8, 12 months described; no numeric data |
| Lesion classification | Not given (no acute/chronic label, no purulence/fistula statement) |

Cited (not primary) data: Crippa et al. series 89/94 implants successful (94.6%, 6 months-4 years); Montoya-Salazar 36 implants in chronic periapical lesions with a laser protocol; Kusek 10 cases. Figures and tables were not in the retrieved text.
