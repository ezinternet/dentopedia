## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (n=100: 50 acute periapical infection, 50 control), University of Murcia, Spain; flapless IIP with immediate provisionalization, Biomet 3i implants, 1-year follow-up.

100% survival both groups; all clinical parameters (bleeding, KM, MPR, DPR, MFR, PD) NS between groups (p>0.05); radiographic bone loss 0.35 vs 0.15 mm (p=0.160 NS).

Immediate implant placement with immediate provisionalization is safe in acute periapical infection sites after meticulous debridement + amoxicillin/clavulanate protocol; zero retrograde peri-implantitis at 1 year.

## Summary

This prospective cohort study ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.4317/medoral.23708)) is the primary source for the "Camara et al." data cited in multiple meta-analyses on infected socket immediate implant placement. The authors enrolled 100 patients (50 with active acute periapical pathology including fistula, suppuration, pain, swelling, and mobility; 50 controls without infection) and placed flapless Biomet 3i implants with immediate resin provisional crowns (platform-switched, no occlusal contact). All patients received amoxicillin/clavulanate 875/125 mg TID × 4 days pre-operatively and 7 days post-operatively. After meticulous curettage and 0.12% chlorhexidine irrigation, bovine bone (Endobone) was placed in the buccal gap. At 1-year follow-up, survival was 100% in both groups. No statistically significant differences were found for any clinical or radiographic parameter, including IS-BIC (0.35 ± 0.51 vs 0.15 ± 0.87 mm, p=0.160). Crucially, zero cases of retrograde peri-implantitis were observed in the 50 acutely infected sites.

## Key Contributions

- Primary clinical evidence that **acute periapical infection sites** (fistula, pus, granuloma) can receive immediate implants with immediate provisionalization safely
- First study using flapless technique + immediate provisionalization in acutely infected sites with matched control group
- Defined complete STROBE-compliant protocol: CHX gauze debridement + irrigation + AMOX/CLV × 4+7 days + bovine bone gap fill
- Confirmed zero retrograde peri-implantitis at 1 year despite active purulent infection at time of extraction
- IS-BIC (0.35 mm) lower than Siegenthaler 2007 (1.5 mm) and comparable to Truninger 2011 (0.41 mm)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective cohort, STROBE-compliant; single operator
- **n**: 100 patients (50 test: acute periapical pathology; 50 control: no infection)
- **Test group inclusion criteria**: CBCT evidence + clinical signs (fistula ± suppuration, pain, swelling, pus from sulcus, mobility)
- **Implants**: Biomet 3i (Zimmer Biomet); flapless; torque >35 N/cm² (mean 44.14 ± 5.48 vs 46.46 ± 8.31 N/cm²)
- **Protocol**: Curettage → CHX gauze 1 min → CHX + saline irrigation → Endobone gap fill → screwed provisional (no occlusion)
- **Antibiotics**: amoxicillin/clavulanate 875/125 mg TID ×4 days pre + ×7 days post (clindamycin 300 mg TID if penicillin allergy)
- **Follow-up**: definitive restoration at 6 months; 1-year assessment
- **Radiographic**: digital RVG, ImageJ measurement of IS-BIC (mesial+distal/2)
- **Statistics**: Student's t-test, Pearson chi-squared; p≤0.05

## Results

|---|---|---|---|
| Survival | 100% | 100% | — |
| Bleeding on probing (%) | 24.99 ± 4.99 | 11.52 ± 3.04 | 0.493 NS |

| DPR (mm) | 0.38 ± 0.49 | 0.28 ± 0.48 | 0.123 NS |
| MFR (mm) | 0.35 ± 0.41 | 0.22 ± 0.32 | 0.175 NS |

**Zero retrograde peri-implantitis** in either group at 1 year.

Acute infection etiology in test group: infected fracture 42%, granuloma+pus 30%, failed endodontic treatment 28%.
