## Three-line Summary

Retrospective consecutive cohort (68 patients, 126 implants) of immediate implants in infected sockets using Er,Cr:YSGG laser decontamination, in situ hardening alloplastic graft in the buccal gap, and non-submerged healing.

105 of 110 implants (95.45%) survived after prosthetic loading (16 implants in 8 patients lost to follow-up; loading at 136 +/- 73 days).

Authors conclude survival is similar to published rates for non-infected sites; abstract-only, no infection definition or follow-up length available, single-center uncontrolled series.

## Summary

Based on articles retrieved from PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.34172/jlms.2020.28)), this retrospective record review identified 68 patients (126 implants: 65 maxillary, 61 mandibular) who received immediate implants in infected extraction sockets under a standardized protocol: (1) Er,Cr:YSGG laser decontamination of the socket before implant insertion, (2) an in situ hardening alloplastic bone graft substitute filling the gap between implant and labial plate, and (3) non-submerged healing. Implants were loaded 136 +/- 73 days after placement (range 37-400). Eight patients (16 implants) were lost to follow-up, leaving 110 implants, of which 105 (95.45%) survived after prosthetic loading. The authors state this is similar to published survival for immediate implants in non-infected sites.

## Key Contributions

- Reports a defined, reproducible three-component protocol (laser + hardening alloplast + non-submerged healing) for infected sockets.
- Survival 95.45% (105/110) after loading.

## Methodology

- Design: retrospective record review of consecutive cases; no control group.
- Not available from the abstract: how infection was defined or classified (acute vs chronic, presence of purulence), laser parameters, graft product, antibiotic regimen, follow-up duration. The full text was not retrievable, so these must not be inferred.

## Limitations and Caveats

- Uncontrolled: the laser's independent contribution cannot be separated from the graft and non-submerged healing.
- 16/126 implants (12.7%) lost to follow-up; the 95.45% is per implant among those followed.
- Follow-up length is not stated in the abstract. A later held source ([[sources/elaskary-2024-vst-immediate-implant-infected-sockets]]) cites 48.75 months for this study; this could not be verified from the abstract.
- [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/almugeiren-2024-placement-immediate-dental-implants-extraction]] lists this study among RCTs with n=68; the abstract describes a retrospective record review.
- Because the infection definition is unknown, it should not be used to support a claim about acute purulent sites specifically.
