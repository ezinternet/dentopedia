## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (n=86 patients, 168 immediate implants), Italy; 3 groups — non-infected (85), acute infection (36), chronic infection (47); atraumatic piezo extraction + 30 s ultrasonication at 72 W for infected sites; 1-year follow-up.

1-year survival: non-infected 98.8%, chronic 100%, acute 94.4%; differences NS; no implant lost after loading.

Immediate implant survival is comparable across infected and non-infected sites when ultrasonic debridement protocol is applied.

## Summary

This prospective cohort study ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/cid.12126)) evaluated immediate implant placement using ultrasonic piezo surgery across three infection groups: non-infected (85 implants), acute infection (36 implants), and chronic infection (47 implants). All sites received the same medication and surgical protocol; infected sites additionally received 30-second ultrasonication at 72 W for bacterial abatement. At 1 year, survival rates were 98.8% (non-infected), 100% (chronic), and 94.4% (acute), with no statistically significant differences between groups. No implant was lost after loading. The study is notable for the use of piezo devices for both atraumatic extraction and osteotomy preparation — allowing root removal in one piece and uncomplicated drilling. This paper is cited in Muñoz-Cámara 2020 and included in the Saijeva 2020 SR+MA as one of nine cohort studies.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrated 100% survival in chronically infected sites with piezo protocol
- 94.4% acute survival (NS vs non-infected) — only minor deficit with ultrasonic management
- Introduced ultrasonication (30 s / 72 W) as infection abatement step at infected implant sites
- One of only a few studies distinguishing acute vs chronic infection as separate groups
- Included as one of 9 studies in Saijeva 2020 SR+MA

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective cohort
- **n**: 86 patients, 168 implants (non-infected 85; acute 36; chronic 47)
- **Technique**: Piezo extraction + osteotomy (no flap); ultrasonication 30 s at 72 W (infected sites)
- **Follow-up**: 1 year
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier survival

## Results

| Group | n | 1-year survival |
|---|---|---|
| Non-infected | 85 | 98.8% |
| Chronic infection | 47 | 100% |
| Acute infection | 36 | 94.4% |

All differences NS. No loss after loading.
