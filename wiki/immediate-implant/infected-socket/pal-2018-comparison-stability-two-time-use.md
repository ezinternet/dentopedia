## Three-line Summary

Non-randomized (allocation method not described) two-arm clinical comparison, n=100 patients (50/50), immediate implants in chronically infected sockets (chronic periapical infection of endodontic/endoperiodontal origin) with PRGF applied once (at placement) versus twice (at placement and 1 month later).

Two-time PRGF gave higher ISQ stability at 2 and 3 months and less marginal bone loss at 3 and 6 months (numbers not in retrieved text; Table 1 missing); 14 of the implants failed to osseointegrate (group split not reported); pain and swelling resolved by week 12 in both groups.

Weak evidence: no randomization/blinding described, no failure breakdown, follow-up only 3-6 months, and no acute/purulent sites; the twice-PRGF benefit is hypothesis-generating only.

## 세줄요약

비무작위(배정 방법 미기술) 2군 임상 비교, 환자 100명(50/50), 만성 치근단 감염(치내·치내-치주 기원) 발치와에 즉시 임플란트 식립, 혈소판 유래 성장인자 (Platelet-Rich Growth Factor, PRGF)를 1회(식립 시) vs 2회(식립 시 + 1개월 후) 적용.

PRGF 2회군에서 2·3개월 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)가 더 높고 3·6개월 변연골 소실이 적었으나(수치는 확보된 본문에 없음, 표 1 누락), 14개 임플란트가 골유착 실패(군별 분포 미보고); 통증·부종은 양군 모두 12주에 소실.

근거 약함: 무작위·눈가림 미기술, 실패 내역 없음, 추적 3-6개월, 급성·화농성 부위 미포함이라 PRGF 2회 이점은 가설 수준.

## Summary

Pal et al. (2018, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4103/njms.NJMS_1_18)) compared PRGF applied once versus twice in 100 patients receiving immediate implants in sockets with chronic periapical infection. The labelled arms (placebo vs active comparator) were not described as randomized. Stability (ISQ) was higher at 2 and 3 months and marginal bone loss lower at 3 and 6 months with two-time PRGF, but numeric data are absent from the retrieved text. Fourteen implants failed to osseointegrate without a group breakdown. Pain and swelling resolved by 12 weeks in both groups. Evidence is low-grade; the label evidence_level: prospective reflects a non-randomized comparative clinical design (date is year-only, publication year 2018).

## Key Contributions

- Only page in this cluster testing a PRGF dosing schedule (once vs twice) in infected-socket immediate implants.
- Defines infected sites as chronic periapical infection of endodontic or endoperiodontal origin (clinical + radiographic); no acute/purulent sites described.

## Methodology

- n=100 (50 per group); Group A PRGF at placement; Group B PRGF at placement + 1 month. Allocation method not stated.
- Antibiotics, debridement/irrigation, passive suturing in all; PRGF from 8 ml blood at 270 g, no leukocytes; implant dipped in PRGF, gap filled with PRGF +/- graft; two-stage, 3-month healing.
- Outcomes: VAS pain, swelling, ISQ (Ostell) at baseline/2/3 months, marginal bone loss at 3 and 6 months.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| ISQ | Higher in two-time PRGF at 2 and 3 months (values/p not in retrieved text) |
| Marginal bone loss | Lower in two-time PRGF at 3 and 6 months (no numbers) |
| Failure to osseointegrate | 14 implants (group split not reported) |
| Pain/swelling | Decreased day 1 to week 1, nil by week 12 in both groups |
