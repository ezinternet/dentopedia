## Three-line Summary

Single case report (CARE guidelines; one 34-year-old systemically healthy man, two adjacent mandibular left molar/premolar sites #19 and #20 with a chronic periradicular abscess and a draining buccal sinus) treated with extraction, curettage, two immediate implants and a xenograft.

Both implants were placed with 35 Ncm primary stability after curettage and saline irrigation only, with amoxicillin-clavulanate plus metronidazole for 5 days; CBCT at 4 months showed the 15 x 20 mm lesion healed, and the authors report symptom-free function with cortical bone formation at 2 and 3 years (no implant survival rate, bone-level measurements or complication counts are given).

This is n=1 with no control, no quantified outcomes and a patient-driven time constraint; it illustrates one protocol in a chronic (not acute-purulent) lesion and cannot support efficacy or safety claims — the authors' general conclusion ("safe and predictable") is not supported by the case itself.

## Summary

This CARE-guideline case report describes a systemically healthy 34-year-old man with a chronic periradicular abscess around teeth #19 and #20 (Grade III mobility, buccal draining sinus, 15 x 20 mm CBCT lesion, thinned lingual plate, buccal bone resorption at #20). After extraction, curettage until firm bone and saline irrigation, two wide implants (5.0 and 6.5 mm x 13.5 mm) were placed with 35 Ncm primary stability, the >1 mm jumping gap was filled with bovine xenograft, and customized composite healing abutments were fitted. Amoxicillin-clavulanate plus metronidazole was given for 5 days. CBCT at 4 months showed the lesion healed, screw-retained monolithic zirconia was delivered at 4 months, and the authors report cortical bone formation at 2 and 3 years without giving numeric outcomes. As a single uncontrolled case it is illustrative only.

## Key Contributions

- A worked protocol for immediate implants in a chronic (fistulated, non-acute-described) periradicular lesion in a mandibular posterior site.
- Combines curettage-only debridement, systemic antibiotics, xenograft in the jumping gap and delayed definitive restoration.
- Applies the ITI SAC assessment to label the site "Complex".

## Methodology

Case report; CBCT diagnosis (ITI SAC "Complex"); extraction under articaine block; CL85/CM10 curettes; saline irrigation; two Megagen AnyRidge implants; xenograft 250 micron; customized nanohybrid composite abutment; 5-0 polyglactin; 0.2% chlorhexidine 14 days; augmentin 1000 mg BID + metronidazole 500 mg TID + ibuprofen 400 mg TID for 5 days; IM dexamethasone 8 mg once. Follow-up: 1 week, 2 weeks, monthly, CBCT at 4 months, review at 2 and 3 years.

## Results

| Time | Reported finding |
|---|---|
| 1-2 weeks | Symptom-free, adequate soft tissue healing |
| 4 months | CBCT: lesion fully healed, buccal and lingual bone shield, significant bone formation; zirconia restoration placed |
| 2 years | Healthy bone growth, cortical bone at platform |
| 3 years | Further cortical bone near implant-abutment junction; implants functional |

No survival rate, marginal bone loss values, probing depths or complication counts are given. Lesion classification: chronic periradicular abscess with draining sinus; no acute swelling, purulent exudate at surgery or culture is reported (persistent discharge is the only exudate description).

## Limitations

- n=1, no comparator; outcomes are qualitative and radiographic-narrative. The case cannot support survival, safety or superiority claims.
- Not an acute-abscess case; it does not inform the acute purulent socket question.
- Time constraint of the patient drove the decision; authors state it may limit infection control.
- Discussion statements about survival (96% at 5 years, 93%) are citations to other studies, not results of this report.
