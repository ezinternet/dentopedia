## Three-line Summary

Case series (n=24 immediate implants) placed into debrided infected sockets (subacute periodontal infection, perio-endo infection, chronic periapical infection, periodontal cyst, traumatic infected teeth), follow-up at least 24 months.

All 24 implants were stable at 24 months with no clinical mobility or infection; radiographic crestal bone loss occurred during osseointegration and settled at the first thread.

Survival appears predictable with meticulous debridement plus pre-/post-operative antibiotics, but abstract-only data, no control group and no quantified stability values limit inference.

증례군 (n=24 즉시식립 임플란트): 소파한 감염 발치와 (아급성 치주감염, 치주-근관 병소, 만성 치근단 감염, 치주낭종, 외상성 감염치)에 즉시식립, 최소 24개월 추적.

## Summary

Based on articles retrieved from PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jobcr.2016.10.002)), this Indian case series placed 24 implants immediately into debrided infected sockets. Pathologies were subacute periodontal infection, perio-endo infection, chronic periapical infection, periodontal cyst and traumatic infected teeth. The protocol emphasized meticulous debridement with pre- and post-surgical antibiotics. At 24 months all implants were stable without mobility or infection, and crestal bone loss settled at the first thread. Only the abstract was available.

## Key Contributions

- Includes "subacute periodontal infection" and perio-endo sockets among sites treated by immediate placement, alongside chronic periapical lesions.
- Attributes success to debridement quality and antibiotic coverage (author conclusion).

## Methodology

- 24 immediate implants in infected sockets; follow-up at least 24 months.
- Outcomes: clinical mobility/infection, radiographic crestal bone level.
- Not stated in abstract: prospective/retrospective design, implant system, antibiotic regimen, definition of "subacute", purulence status, primary stability values.

## Results

- 24/24 implants stable at 24 months; no mobility, no infection.
- Radiographic crestal bone loss during osseointegration, stabilizing at the level of the first thread (no numeric values in abstract).
- Title refers to primary stability, but no stability measurement is reported in the abstract.
