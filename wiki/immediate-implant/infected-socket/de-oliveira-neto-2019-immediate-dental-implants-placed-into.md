## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42018092156; 7 databases to May 2018; 8 human clinical trials, 935 implants; Cochrane risk of bias: unclear overall, high for participant/personnel blinding in all studies) comparing immediate implants placed into infected versus non-infected sockets, restricted to studies with antibiotic therapy, ≥1 year follow-up and non-medically-compromised patients.

Implant failure risk ratio 2.99 for infected sites (95% CI 1.04–8.56; p=0.04; I²=0%), survival across studies 90.8–100%; peri-implant bone loss (MD −0.03 mm), plaque, bleeding, probing depth and keratinized mucosa width showed no significant difference, although weighted yearly bone loss was numerically higher in infected groups (0.62 vs 0.49 mm/yr).

The authors conclude infection is a real failure risk factor, but the CI lower bound is 1.04, all studies are non-randomized two-group designs at unclear-to-high risk of bias, and infected sites were pooled without acute/chronic or endodontic/periodontal stratification — which contradicts later, larger syntheses reporting no survival difference.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD42018092156; 2018년 5월까지 7개 DB; 사람 임상연구 8편·임플란트 935개; Cochrane 비뚤림 위험 전반 불명확, 참여자·시술자 눈가림은 전 연구 high risk)로, 항생제 투여·추적 1년 이상·전신질환 없는 환자만 포함해 감염 vs 비감염 발치와 즉시식립을 비교.

감염 부위의 임플란트 실패 위험비 2.99 (95% CI 1.04–8.56, p=0.04, I²=0%), 연구별 생존율 90.8–100%; 변연골 소실(MD −0.03 mm)·치태·출혈·탐침깊이·각화점막 폭은 유의차 없음 (단 연간 가중평균 골소실은 감염군 0.62 vs 0.49 mm로 수치상 높음).

저자는 감염을 실패 위험인자로 결론짓지만 신뢰구간 하한이 1.04이고, 전 연구가 비무작위 2군 설계(불명확~높은 비뚤림 위험)이며, 감염 부위를 급성/만성·근관성/치주성으로 나누지 않고 합산했다 — 이후 더 큰 종합 연구들의 "생존율 차이 없음"과 상충한다.

## Summary

This 2019 SR+MA asked whether immediate implants placed into infected sockets fail more often than those placed into non-infected sockets. Eight human clinical trials (935 implants) met strict criteria: survival as the primary outcome, a non-infected control group, ≥1 year of follow-up, antibiotic therapy for all patients, and no medically compromised patients. Pooled implant failure was almost three times higher in infected sites (RR 2.99, 95% CI 1.04–8.56, p=0.04, I²=0%), while peri-implant bone loss, plaque, bleeding, probing depth and keratinized mucosa width did not differ significantly. The authors also computed follow-up-weighted yearly means, which were numerically less favorable for infected sites in bone loss and plaque; they treat this as exploratory. Overall risk of bias was unclear, with participant/personnel blinding at high risk in every study and randomization/allocation concealment mostly at high risk.

**Reading against the rest of the wiki [미검증 — Claude 해석]:** the significance is borderline (lower CI bound 1.04) and rests on eight non-randomized two-group studies; the paper pools "infected sites" without reporting an acute vs chronic or endodontic vs periodontal split in the retrievable text (the Table 2 legend defines AI = acute infected and CI = chronically infected, but the table content was not in the retrieved PMC text). The wiki's later, larger syntheses find no survival difference under debridement and antibiotics, and the authors themselves recommend three-group designs that separate infection types. The practical takeaway is that this paper flags infection as a risk factor but cannot say which infection types carry it, so it cannot support or refute the acute-vs-chronic distinction used in [[overviews/immediate-implant-infected-sites-decision]].

## Key Contributions

- Only held meta-analysis reporting a statistically significant higher failure risk (RR 2.99) for immediate implants in infected vs non-infected sites.
- Human-clinical-trial-only inclusion, with antibiotic therapy, ≥1 year follow-up and no medically compromised patients as eligibility filters.
- Exploratory follow-up-weighted yearly means for bone loss and plaque index.
- Explicit methodological recommendation: primary studies should use ≥3 groups (non-infected, endodontic-origin, periodontal-origin infection) and report the infection type of every failed implant.

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA following PRISMA-P and the Cochrane Handbook; protocol registered a priori (PROSPERO CRD42018092156).
- Search: PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, CENTRAL, LILACS and Open Grey from inception to May 2018; no language restriction. 3,253 hits → 64 duplicates removed → 3,154 excluded on title/abstract → 34 full texts assessed → 26 excluded → 8 included (kappa 0.90).
- Outcomes: implant survival/failure (primary); peri-implant bone loss, plaque index, bleeding index, probing depth, keratinized mucosa width (secondary).
- Analysis: RR for dichotomous and MD for continuous outcomes, random-effects model, I² > 50% as substantial heterogeneity, Review Manager 5.3, implant as unit of analysis; no funnel plot or sensitivity analysis (too few studies, all same type).

## Results

| Outcome | Studies | Effect | Heterogeneity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failure (infected vs non-infected) | 8 (935 implants) | RR 2.99 (95% CI 1.04–8.56), p=0.04 | I² 0% |
| Peri-implant bone loss | 5 (399 implants) | MD −0.03 mm (−0.09 to 0.04), p=0.46 | I² 0% |
| Bleeding index | 5 | MD 0.05 (−0.01 to 0.10), p=0.08 | I² 0% |
| Plaque index | 5 | MD −0.01 (−0.04 to 0.02), p=0.54 | I² 0% |
| Probing depth | 4 (124 implants) | MD −0.30 mm (−0.64 to 0.05), p=0.09 | I² 79% |
| Keratinized mucosa width | 4 | MD 0.31 mm (−0.11 to 0.73), p=0.15 | I² 25% |

Survival ranged 90.8–100% across the eight studies. Weighted per-year means (mean follow-up 2.6 ± 1.1 years): bone loss 0.62 ± 0.67 mm (infected) vs 0.49 ± 0.39 mm; plaque index 0.62 ± 0.67 vs 0.21 ± 0.13; bleeding index 0.20 vs 0.21; probing depth 1.33 vs 1.53 mm; keratinized mucosa 1.57 vs 1.27 mm. Study-level characteristics and forest plots are in tables/figures not present in the retrieved text.
