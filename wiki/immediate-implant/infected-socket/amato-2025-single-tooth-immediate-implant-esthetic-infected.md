## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=127 patients, 143 single-tooth implants; mean 7-year follow-up, range 2-12 years), Amato/Tarnow group; esthetic zone IIP + immediate provisionalization; 3 groups: non-infected (47), acute infection (56), chronic infection (40).

Survival: non-infected 97.8%, acute 96.4%, chronic 95%; overall 96.5% (5 failures); no significant difference (p=0.8).

Immediate single-tooth implant with immediate provisionalization achieves comparable survival in infected and non-infected esthetic zone sites at up to 12 years; infection status does not predict failure.

## Summary

This retrospective study ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.11607/jomi.11225)) from Amato and Tarnow represents the longest published follow-up (mean 7 years, up to 12 years) for immediate implant placement with immediate provisionalization in infected esthetic zone sites. 143 implants were placed in 127 patients across three infection groups: non-infected (47), acutely infected — perio/endo abscess or fistula (56), and chronically infected — perio pocket or periapical lesion without acute signs (40). The surgical protocol was flapless extraction, thorough debridement, immediate implant, particulate graft, resorbable membrane if buccal plate was compromised, and screw-retained provisional out of occlusion. Survival rates of 97.8%, 96.4%, and 95% respectively (overall 96.5%) showed no significant difference across groups (p=0.8). This study extends the short-term evidence of Muñoz-Cámara 2020 to long-term and confirms protocol robustness in the esthetic zone.

## Key Contributions

- Longest follow-up (7-year mean, 12-year maximum) for infected-site IIP in esthetic zone
- Confirmed NS survival difference across non-infected/acute/chronic infection groups (p=0.8)
- Tarnow co-authorship adds high-impact credibility to the protocol
- Evaluated pink esthetic score + MBL + recession + PD alongside survival — comprehensive outcome set
- Largest acute-infected esthetic-zone cohort (n=56 acute implants)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort
- **n**: 127 patients, 143 single-tooth implants (esthetic zone, maxillary)
- **Groups**: non-infected (47), acute infection (56), chronic infection (40)
- **Protocol**: Flapless, debridement, immediate implant, particulate graft, membrane if buccal plate compromised, screw-retained provisional OOO
- **Follow-up**: Mean 7 years (range 2-12 years)
- **Statistics**: Survival analysis, p=0.8 between groups
