## Three-line Summary

RCT comparing flapless immediate implant placement with versus without xenograft filling of the buccal gap at anterior and premolar sites, assessing buccal bone level changes at 6 and 12 months.

The xenograft-filled group showed significantly less buccal bone resorption at both 6 and 12 months, with no implant failures in either group.

Even with a flapless approach that preserves blood supply and soft tissue contour, xenograft filling of the buccal gap provides additional hard-tissue protection; the combined approach (flapless + graft) appears optimal.

## 세줄요약

전치부 및 소구치 부위에서 무피판 즉시 식립 시 협측 간격의 이종골(Xenograft) 충전 유무를 비교한 RCT(6·12개월 추적).

이종골 충전군에서 6개월·12개월 모두 협측 골 흡수가 유의하게 적었으며, 양 군 모두 임플란트 실패는 없었다.

혈류와 연조직 윤곽을 보존하는 무피판 술식에서도 협측 간격 골이식이 경조직 보호에 추가적으로 기여하므로, 무피판 + 이식의 병합 접근이 최적으로 판단된다.

## Summary
RCT assessing flapless immediate implant placement with vs without xenograft filling on buccal bone level at 6 and 12 months.

## Key Contributions
- Flapless approach preserves blood supply and soft tissue contour
- Xenograft filling group: significantly less buccal bone resorption at 6 and 12 months
- Both groups: no implant failures reported

## Methodology
RCT; anterior and premolar sites; flapless immediate implant.

## Results
Xenograft filling beneficial even with flapless approach. Flapless technique reduces tissue trauma. Combined approach (flapless + graft) optimal.
