## Three-line Summary
Parallel-group RCT (n=24, 12/arm) in the maxillary esthetic zone testing computer-guided immediate implant placement with digital immediate provisionalization, with vs. without grafting the 2–3 mm jumping gap using a 1:1 autogenous:xenograft mixture (dual-zone grafting technique).
At 6 months, median Pink Esthetic Score (PES) was significantly higher in the grafted group than the ungrafted control (13 vs. 11.5, p=0.048), rising intra-group in the grafted arm (11.58→12.42, p=0.039) and falling in the control arm (11.75→11.17, p=0.035), despite no baseline difference immediately postoperatively (p=0.746).
Grafting the jumping gap with an autogenous-xenograft mixture during guided IIP better preserves peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics than leaving it ungrafted, though the trial is small (n=12/arm), single-center, and single-surgeon.

## 세줄요약

## Summary
This parallel-group randomized clinical trial (n=24, 12 per arm) evaluated whether grafting the jumping gap at guided immediate implant placement (IIP) in the maxillary esthetic zone improves peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics. All patients received a CBCT- and intraoral-scan-based 3D-printed surgical guide and a digitally designed, 3D-printed immediate provisional restoration; the study group additionally received a 1:1 autogenous:xenograft bone-graft mixture packed into the 2–3 mm jumping gap up to and into the soft-tissue zone (dual-zone grafting technique), while the control group received no graft. Pink Esthetic Score (PES) did not differ between groups immediately postoperatively (median 11.5 vs. 12, p=0.746) but was significantly higher in the grafted group at 6 months (median 13 vs. 11.5, p=0.048); PES rose over time in the grafted group and fell in the control group. The findings support grafting the jumping gap with an autogenous-xenograft mixture, as part of a fully guided/digital IIP workflow, to better preserve esthetic outcomes — though the trial is small and single-center.

## Key Contributions
- Isolates the jumping-gap graft variable (1:1 autogenous:xenograft vs. none) while holding a fully digital guided-surgery + immediate-provisionalization workflow constant across both arms.
- Shows divergent 6-month PES trajectories: +0.84 mean in the grafted group vs. −0.58 mean in the ungrafted control, despite equivalent baseline PES.
- Validates low-speed (300 rpm, no coolant) intrasocket autogenous-bone harvesting as a viable graft component, avoiding a second donor site/surgery.
- Supports the "dual-zone grafting" concept — graft extending from bone crest into the soft-tissue zone beneath an immediate provisional — as esthetic-preserving.

## Methodology
Parallel 2-arm RCT; 24 patients (12/arm), one non-restorable maxillary incisor/canine per patient with intact labial plate; guided implant placement via 3D-printed surgical guide + digitally designed 3D-printed immediate provisional in both arms; study arm packed the 2–3 mm jumping gap with a 1:1 autogenous:xenograft (Bio-Oss) mixture, control arm left ungrafted; outcome = Pink Esthetic Score (PES) at baseline and 6 months; implants below 35 Ncm insertion torque excluded; registered clinicaltrials.gov NCT04096209.
