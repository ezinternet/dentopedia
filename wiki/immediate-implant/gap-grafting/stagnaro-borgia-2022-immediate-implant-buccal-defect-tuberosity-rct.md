## Three-line Summary

Prospective 1-year RCT (n=24) comparing tuberosity autograft vs xenograft for grafting buccal defects at immediate implant placement sites on buccal bone resorption and implant survival.

Buccal bone resorption was statistically equivalent between groups (autograft 0.61 mm vs xenograft 0.58 mm); both groups achieved 100% implant survival at 1 year.

Tuberosity autograft is a viable alternative to xenograft for buccal defect filling at immediate implant sites without requiring a separate donor-site procedure, as tuberosity bone is routinely accessible in the same field.

## 세줄요약

즉시식립 부위의 협측 결손 충전에 결절부 자가골 vs 이종골을 비교한 전향적 1년 RCT (n=24); 주 결과: 협측 골 흡수량 및 임플란트 생존율.

협측 골 흡수량은 두 군간 동등 (자가골 0.61 mm vs 이종골 0.58 mm); 양군 1년 임플란트 생존율 100%.

결절부 자가골은 별도의 공여 부위 수술 없이 동일 술야에서 채취 가능하며, 즉시식립 협측 결손 충전에 이종골과 동등한 대안이다.

## Summary
1-year RCT comparing tuberosity autograft vs xenograft for filling buccal defects at immediate implant sites.

## Key Contributions
- No significant difference in buccal bone resorption: autograft 0.61mm vs xenograft 0.58mm
- Tuberosity autograft: donor site without additional morbidity
- Both groups: 100% survival at 1 year

## Methodology
Prospective RCT; n=24; 1-year follow-up.

## Results
Tuberosity autograft and xenograft equally effective for buccal defect filling at immediate implant sites. Autograft avoids additional surgical site when tuberosity bone available.
