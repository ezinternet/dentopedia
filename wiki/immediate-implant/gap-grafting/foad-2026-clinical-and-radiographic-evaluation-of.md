## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=16 immediate implants, 8 per group) in medically healthy adults (21–40 yr) with non-restorable single-rooted maxillary anterior/premolar teeth, comparing immediate implant placement (IIP) alone vs IIP + natural bovine bone with hyaluronic acid (cerabone plus) in the fixture–socket gap.

Study group showed higher 4-month implant stability quotient (ISQ) (72.25 ± 2.66 vs 64.88 ± 1.36, p<0.001) and higher 6-month CBCT bone density (1163.38 ± 112.64 vs 1034.13 ± 71.19 HU, p=0.016); buccal bone width reduction was 3.88% vs 15.99% (no significant between-group buccal width difference).

Combined deproteinized bovine bone + hyaluronic acid grafting at IIP favors early implant stability and buccal bone preservation, but the design cannot isolate HA's independent contribution (no DBB-only arm) and follow-up is short — considered preliminary.

## 세줄요약

RCT (n=16 즉시식립 임플란트, 8/군): 건강한 21–40세 성인의 수복불가 단근 상악 전치·소구치에서 즉시식립 단독 vs 즉시식립 + 천연 우골·히알루론산(cerabone plus) 간극 이식 비교.

연구군이 4개월차 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) (72.25 ± 2.66 vs 64.88 ± 1.36, p<0.001) 및 6개월차 CBCT 골밀도 (1163.38 vs 1034.13 HU, p=0.016) 유의 우위를 보였고, 협측골 폭 감소는 3.88% vs 15.99%로 작았다(군간 폭 차는 유의하지 않음).

우골+HA 복합 이식이 초기 안정성·협측골 보존에 유리하나, 탈단백 우골(Deproteinized Bovine Bone, DBB) 단독 대조군 부재로 HA 고유 효과 분리가 불가능하고 추적이 단기여서 예비적(pilot) 근거로 해석해야 한다.

## Summary

This parallel-group RCT (n=16 immediate implants, 8 per group) randomized medically healthy adults with non-restorable single-rooted maxillary anterior or premolar teeth to immediate implant placement alone or with the fixture–socket gap filled with natural bovine bone + hyaluronic acid (cerabone plus). The grafted group achieved significantly higher ISQ at 4 months (72.25 ± 2.66 vs 64.88 ± 1.36, p<0.001) and higher CBCT peri-implant bone density at 6 months (1163.38 ± 112.64 vs 1034.13 ± 71.19 HU, p=0.016). Buccal bone width decreased significantly in both groups but far less in the grafted group (3.88% vs 15.99%). The authors caution that the immediate density advantage reflects graft radiopacity/crystallinity rather than biology, and that the design cannot isolate HA's contribution without a DBB-only arm, so results are preliminary.

## Key Contributions

- First randomized clinical study evaluating natural bovine bone combined with hyaluronic acid specifically as a jumping-gap graft at immediate implant placement (IIP).
- Shows the combined graft lifts 4-month ISQ across the ~70 threshold (72.25) associated with high secondary stability and favorable loading flexibility vs ungrafted IIP (64.88).
- Quantifies buccal bone preservation: 3.88% vs 15.99% width reduction over 6 months, consistent with prior gap-grafting RCTs.
- Provides a methodological caution: baseline bone-density advantage is largely graft radiopacity — longitudinal density change is the interpretable signal.

## Methodology

Parallel-group RCT (CONSORT-compliant) at Suez Canal University, Egypt (Ethics REC/544/2022; trial NCT07199725, retrospectively registered). Sixteen IIPs in 16 patients, randomization via research randomizer software with sealed-opaque-envelope allocation concealment; clinical and radiographic examiners blinded. Control arm: IIP only. Study arm: IIP + natural bovine bone (DBB) with hyaluronic acid (cerabone plus, botiss), hydrated to sticky bone and packed into the marginal gap. Atraumatic extraction (periotomes), osteotomy at 700–900 rpm/40 Ncm, Frontier implants; collagen sponge + 3/0 vicryl closure. Primary outcome: ISQ (Osstell) at placement and 4 months. Secondary: CBCT bone density (2-mm peri-implant zone, HU, OnDemand3D virtual-implant method) and buccal bone width (coronal third) at placement and 6 months. Unpaired Student's t-test per time point, SPSS v26, α=0.05; sample size (G*Power, effect size 0.39, power 80%) = 16 implants.

## Results

| Outcome | Control (IIP alone, n=8) | Study (IIP + DBB/HA, n=8) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISQ immediate | 50.75 ± 4.50 | 55.25 ± 4.33 | 0.061 (NS) |
| ISQ 4 months | 64.88 ± 1.36 | 72.25 ± 2.66 | <0.001 |
| Bone density immediate (HU) | 880.63 ± 76.18 | 1075.88 ± 97.51 | 0.001 |
| Bone density 6 months (HU) | 1034.13 ± 71.19 | 1163.38 ± 112.64 | 0.016 |
| Buccal bone width immediate (mm) | 2.94 ± 1.09 | 3.09 ± 1.58 | 0.831 (NS) |
| Buccal bone width 6 months (mm) | 2.47 ± 1.07 | 2.97 ± 1.61 | 0.468 (NS) |
| Buccal width within-group reduction | 15.99% (p<0.001) | 3.88% (p=0.014) | — |

Within-group ISQ increased 27.84% (control) vs 30.77% (study), both p<0.001; bone density increased 17.43% vs 8.13% (p<0.001 / p=0.001). Age was balanced (29.88 ± 5.08 vs 33.00 ± 6.55, p=0.305).
