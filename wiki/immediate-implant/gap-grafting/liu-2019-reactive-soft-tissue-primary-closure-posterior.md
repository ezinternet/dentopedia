## One-line Summary
Prospective cohort (n=33 posterior immediate implants): reactive soft tissue from compromised socket raised as primary wound closure reduced buccal bone height loss and keratinized mucosa reduction, with 100% survival at 1 year.

## 한줄요약
전향 코호트 (n=33 후방부 즉시식립): 손상 소켓의 반응성 연조직(육아조직+긴결합상피)을 1차 봉합재로 활용하면 협측골 소실 및 각화치은 폭 감소 억제, 1년 생존율 100%.

## Summary
This prospective cohort study investigated whether granulation tissue (GT) and long junctional epithelium — collectively termed "reactive soft tissue" — within compromised posterior extraction sockets could be harnessed as primary wound closure material for immediate implant placement (IIP), rather than being completely debrided. Thirty-three posterior implants were enrolled: in the experimental group (EG), reactive tissue was elevated and sutured as primary closure; in the control group (CG), the reactive tissue was present but insufficient to achieve coverage. CBCT at T1 (immediately post) and T2 (6 months) captured bone dimensional changes; soft tissue measurements spanned T0 (pre-extraction) through T3 (1-year follow-up). The EG showed less reduction in buccal bone height and keratinized mucosa width vs the CG at 6 months. Marginal bone levels and soft tissue changes were not significantly different at 1 year. Implant survival was 100%.

## Key Contributions
- Introduces "reactive soft tissue" (GT + long junctional epithelium) as a primary wound closure resource, challenging the dogma of mandatory debridement
- 100% implant survival at 1-year follow-up
- EG: less buccal bone height loss and less KM width reduction at 6 months vs CG
- Demonstrates feasibility of utilizing biological material already present at the surgical site — no additional grafting or membrane required for wound coverage
- Conceptually bridges tissue engineering (using in-situ regenerative tissue) with practical IIP wound management

## Methodology
Prospective cohort. n=33 posterior immediate implants in compromised sockets (caries/periapical pathology). EG: reactive soft tissue elevated and sutured to achieve primary closure over implant. CG: reactive tissue present but insufficient for coverage (implant not fully covered). CBCT at T1 (immediately post-surgery) and T2 (6 months). Soft tissue at T0, prosthesis delivery, T3 (1 year). Outcomes: implant survival, buccal bone height/width, KM width, MBL.

## Results
- Implant survival: 100%
- Buccal bone height loss: less in EG vs CG (T1→T2 on CBCT)
- KM width change: less decrease in EG vs CG
- MBL at T3: no significant difference EG vs CG
- Soft tissue changes at T3: no significant difference
