## Three-line Summary

Single case report (n=1, 56-year-old woman, tooth 2.1, chronic infection with vertical root fracture and absent buccal plate), 36-month follow-up, plus an exploratory in silico bioinformatic analysis.

Flapless, membrane-free immediate implant with a collagen-sponge/BCP "Roll" and 0.1 mg rhBMP-2 gave a Pink Esthetic Score of 13 (stable M12-M36) and CBCT-visible buccal cortical reconstruction by M18, sustained to M36; bioinformatics flagged BMP2, RUNX2, SPP1, BGLAP as hub genes.

n=1 with no control, no histology, and no PROM; the infection was chronic (no pus or acute abscess documented), so it cannot support claims about acute purulent sites.

## Summary

Based on articles retrieved from PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fdmed.2026.1750133)), this CARE-style case report describes a healthy 56-year-old woman with a fractured, chronically infected maxillary left central incisor (2.1) and an absent buccal plate. After atraumatic extraction and local debridement, a 3.5 x 12 mm implant was placed immediately and subcrestally, and the buccal gap was filled with a "Roll BMP" construct (absorbable collagen sponge wrapped around biphasic calcium phosphate granules, injected with 0.1 mg rhBMP-2). No flap or membrane was used. Over 36 months the Pink Esthetic Score reached 13/14 and CBCT showed a new buccal cortical plate. The authors add an exploratory bioinformatic analysis but acknowledge the single-case limitation.

## Key Contributions

- A flapless, membrane-free buccal-defect reconstruction at immediate implant placement using a rolled collagen/BCP carrier with rhBMP-2.
- 36-month clinical, radiographic (CBCT M18/M24/M36) and PES follow-up of one case.
- Exploratory in silico hub-gene analysis (BMP2, RUNX2, SPP1, BGLAP); hypothesis-generating only.

## Methodology

- Case report per CARE guidelines, outside a registered trial; written informed consent.
- Diagnosis: root fracture, gingival inflammation, secondary caries, periapical radiolucency, absent buccal plate on CBCT, "slight periapical lesion". The paper calls the condition "chronic infection".
- Infection management: atraumatic extraction and "local debridement" only; no antiseptic, curettage protocol details or culture reported. Amoxicillin 1 g q12h x 7 days postoperatively (plus paracetamol and ketoprofen x 3 days). Implant placed the same day (D1).
- Implant: 3.5 x 12 mm INNO, subcrestal. Roll: 0.1 g BCP + Atelocare ACS, rolled to ~7-10 mm wide, packed in buccal gap; 0.1 mg rhBMP-2 hydrated 10-15 min in 0.1 mL saline and injected at three levels. Oraid resorbable pad; bonded provisional; definitive zirconia abutment and lithium disilicate crown at M5.
- Follow-up: M3, M5, M12, M18, M24, M36 (36 months).
- Bioinformatics: GENIE, ShinyGO, STRING (score > 0.4), Cytoscape/cytoHubba (7 algorithms).

## Results

| Time | Finding |
|---|---|
| D1 | Correct 3D implant position; BCP granules visible |
| M3 | Granules lost, initial consolidation; PES 9 |
| M5 | Trabecular maturation; definitive crown; PES 11 |
| M12-M36 | Stable density; PES 13/14 (minor mesial papilla deficiency) |
| M18 | New buccal cortical plate, thicker/denser than contralateral tooth |
| M24-M36 | Corticalization to crestal level sustained |

No pain or procedure-related adverse events reported; no PROM. Bioinformatics: 75 genes, four consensus hub genes (BMP2, RUNX2, SPP1, BGLAP).

Pus/acute abscess: not documented. The only infection descriptors are "chronic infection", gingival inflammation and a periapical radiolucency/"slight periapical lesion"; no sinus tract, suppuration, or acute symptoms besides mobility and discomfort are described.
