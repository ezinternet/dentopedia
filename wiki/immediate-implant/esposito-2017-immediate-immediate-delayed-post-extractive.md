## Three-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (n=210, 70 per arm) randomising single post-extraction implant sites to immediate placement, immediate-delayed placement (6 weeks), or delayed placement (4 months), reporting outcomes at 1 year post-loading (same cohort as the 4-month interim report).

Implant failure: 4/70 (6%) immediate, 4/70 (6.2%) immediate-delayed, 1/70 (1.6%) delayed (P=.369, NS, unchanged from the 4-month data — no new failures in the interim); marginal bone loss -0.25/-0.29/-0.31mm (P=.015); PES 12.52/12.49/11.78 (P<.001), same direction as at 4 months.

At 1 year, the trade-off pattern first seen at 4 months persists essentially unchanged: no statistically significant difference in failures or complications across the three placement timings, but immediate and immediate-delayed placement retain a statistically significant bone-level and esthetic advantage over delayed placement.

## Summary
This is the 1-year post-loading (mature) report of the same 210-patient, 3-arm RCT reported at 4 months in the companion source (Felice et al. 2016). Randomised to immediate, immediate-delayed (6-week), or delayed (4-month) placement, the trial shows no new implant failures between the 4-month and 1-year assessments in any arm — the failure counts are numerically identical (4/70, 4/70, 1/70). The bone-level and pink-esthetic advantage for immediate/immediate-delayed placement over delayed placement, first observed at 4 months, remains statistically significant at 1 year, indicating the early trade-off pattern (slightly higher, non-significant failure risk vs. significantly better bone/esthetics for earlier placement) is stable rather than a short-term artifact.

## Key Contributions
- Confirms at 1-year maturity that the 4-month interim trade-off pattern (higher-but-non-significant failure risk, better bone/esthetics for earlier placement) is stable, not transient
- No new implant failures occurred between the 4-month and 1-year assessments in any of the three arms

## Methodology
Same trial as the 4-month interim report: 210 patients randomised 70/70/70 to immediate, immediate-delayed (6-week), or delayed (4-month) placement, with matched grafting/loading protocol across studies. This report covers 1-year post-loading follow-up.

## Results
| Outcome | Immediate (n=70) | Immediate-delayed 6wk (n=70) | Delayed 4mo (n=70) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failures | 4 (6%) | 4 (6.2%) | 1 (1.6%) | .369 |
| Complications (patients) | 6 | 6 | 4 | .792 |
| Marginal bone loss (1yr) | -0.25 ± 0.17mm | -0.29 ± 0.14mm | -0.31 ± 0.16mm | .015 |
| PES at 1 year | 12.52 | 12.49 | 11.78 | <.001 |
