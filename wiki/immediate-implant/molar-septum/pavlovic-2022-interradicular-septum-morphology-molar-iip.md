## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT cross-sectional study of maxillary first and second molar inter-radicular septa (IRS), classifying morphology and measuring dimensions at multiple levels to operationalize objective criteria for ideal immediate implant placement feasibility.

Substantial site-to-site variability was found in IRS morphology and dimensions, with quantification of the prevalence of "ideal IIP" anatomy in this population, supporting Smith-Tarnow Type A classification criteria.

Pre-surgical CBCT assessment of IRS morphology and dimensions is necessary for molar IIP case selection; a single population cross-sectional design limits direct extrapolation to other ethnic groups.

## 세줄요약

상악 제1·2대구치 격중격 (Inter-Radicular Septum, IRS)의 형태 및 크기를 CBCT로 후향적으로 분류한 횡단면 연구 — ideal 즉시식립 가능 해부학의 유병률을 정량화.

IRS 형태 및 크기는 부위 간 상당한 변이를 보였으며, '이상적 IIP' 해부 구조(Smith-Tarnow Type A 기준 충족)의 빈도를 제공.

구치 즉시식립 적응증 결정에는 CBCT를 통한 IRS 형태·크기 사전 평가가 필수; 단일 집단 횡단면 연구로 다른 인종군에의 직접 외삽에는 한계가 있다.

## Summary
CBCT cross-sectional analysis of maxillary molar inter-radicular septum (IRS) morphology, framed as objective criteria for IIP feasibility.

## Key Contributions
Provides shape/size IRS criteria that operationalize Smith-Tarnow Type A vs B/C in molar IIP planning.

## Methodology
Retrospective CBCT review of maxillary first/second molars. IRS dimensions measured at multiple levels; morphology classified.

## Results
Substantial site-to-site variability; quantifies the prevalence of "ideal IIP" anatomy in this population.

## Limitations
Cross-sectional anatomy-only; single population.
