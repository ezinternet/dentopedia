## Three-line Summary

Atieh et al. (2010) conducted two separate meta-analyses on molar implants: immediate placement into fresh extraction sockets (9 studies, 1,013 implants, 99.0% survival) and immediate restoration/loading in healed molar sites (7 studies, 188 implants, 97.9% survival). Neither protocol showed statistically significant survival differences compared to delayed alternatives. Notably, immediate loading in healed molar sites was associated with significantly less marginal bone loss at 12 months (mean difference −0.31 mm, P=0.005).

## 세줄요약

Atieh 등(2010)은 구치부 임플란트를 대상으로 즉시 식립(9편, 1,013개, 생존율 99.0%)과 치유 후 부위 즉시 수복(7편, 188개, 97.9%)을 각각 메타분석했다. 두 프로토콜 모두 지연 방식 대비 생존율에서 통계적으로 유의한 차이를 보이지 않았다. 치유 후 부위 즉시 수복군에서 12개월 변연골소실이 유의하게 적어(평균 −0.31 mm, P=0.005) 기능적 부하의 골 유지 효과가 시사됐다.

## Methods

### Study Design
Systematic review with two independent meta-analyses; methodology per Academy of Osseointegration Workshop guidelines. Electronic database search (including Cochrane Oral Health Group Trials Register) through November 2008.

### Two Separate Meta-Analyses

**MA-1 — Immediate Placement into Fresh Molar Extraction Sockets**
- Focus: placement timing (immediate Type 1 vs. delayed) for single molar implants
- Studies: 9 | Implants: 1,013

**MA-2 — Immediate Restoration/Loading in Healed Molar Sites**
- Focus: loading/restoration timing (immediate vs. delayed) for single molar implants in healed sites
- Studies: 7 | Implants: 188

### Region
Primarily mandibular molar region.

## Clinical Takeaway

Both immediate placement (99.0%) and immediate restoration/loading (97.9%) demonstrate high survival rates in molar regions, with neither protocol significantly inferior to delayed alternatives. The significant MBL advantage in the immediate loading group of healed-site molar implants supports functional loading as potentially beneficial for peri-implant bone preservation in the posterior mandible. Data are predominantly from mandibular molar sites; extrapolation to maxillary molar regions requires caution.
