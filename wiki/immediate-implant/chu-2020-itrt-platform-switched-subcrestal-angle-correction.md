## Three-line Summary

Comparative prospective study (~12-month follow-up, PRD 2020) evaluating a variable platform-switched subcrestal angle-correction implant for immediate tooth replacement therapy (ITRT) in postextraction sockets, aimed at reconciling prosthetic-driven positioning with bony-housing constraints.

The angle-correction geometry reduced unfavorable socket perforation events and yielded favorable soft-tissue stability and esthetic outcomes even with shorter implants in cases where straight implant alignment would have required socket wall violation.

The angle-correction concept is clinically useful but the study is not a true RCT and carries industry-aligned implant involvement; medium-term follow-up and lack of controls limit evidence strength.

## 세줄요약

전향적 비교 연구(~12개월 추적, PRD 2020): 발치와 즉시치아대체치료(ITRT)에서 각도 수정(angle correction)이 가능한 subcrestal platform-switched 임플란트를 평가.

각도 수정 기하구조가 소켓 천공 사례를 줄이고, 직선 식립 시 소켓 벽을 침범해야 하는 경우에도 단축 임플란트에서 양호한 연조직 안정성과 심미 결과를 제공.

임상적으로 유용한 개념이지만 진정한 RCT가 아니며 산업 계열 임플란트가 사용되어 중기 추적과 대조군 부재로 근거 수준에 한계가 있다.

## Summary
Comparative prospective study evaluating a variable platform-switched subcrestal angle-correction implant in ITRT (immediate tooth replacement therapy), aimed at avoiding socket perforation under uniaxial implant placement.

## Key Contributions
Validates angle-correction implant geometry as a tool to reconcile prosthetic-driven positioning with bony-housing constraints in postextraction sockets.

## Methodology
Comparative prospective design. ITRT cases with the angle-correction implant vs reference. Clinical and radiographic outcomes through ~12 months.

## Results
Favorable soft-tissue stability and esthetic outcomes with the test implant. Reduced incidence of unfavorable socket perforation events.

## Limitations
Industry-aligned implant; not a true RCT; medium-term follow-up.
