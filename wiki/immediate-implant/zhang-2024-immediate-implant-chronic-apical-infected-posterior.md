## Three-line Summary

5-year prospective-style clinical observation (n=36 patients, 41 simple taper retentive implants), Nanchang University 4th Affiliated Hospital, China; immediate implant placement in posterior teeth with chronic apical infection after thorough debridement.

Implant survival 97.6% (1/41 failed, mobility); marginal bone resorption -0.74±1.03 mm (placement→crown) then a further -0.21±0.70 mm (crown→5yr, NS); 1 case of peri-implantitis, no mechanical complications, good patient satisfaction.

Long-term (5-year) data support that immediate implantation after meticulous debridement of chronic apically infected posterior sockets achieves stable outcomes, expanding indications for immediate implantation — abstract-only source (original Chinese-language article; PMC full text body not retrievable).

## 세줄요약

5년 임상관찰 (n=36 환자, 41개 단순 원추형 유지 임플란트), 중국 난창대학교 제4부속병원; 후치부 만성 치근단감염 (chronic apical infection) 치아에 철저한 소파 후 즉시식립.

임플란트 생존율 97.6% (41개 중 1개 동요도로 실패); 변연골 흡수 -0.74±1.03 mm (식립→보철장착), 이후 5년까지 추가 -0.21±0.70 mm (NS); 임플란트주위염 1례, 기계적 합병증 없음, 환자 만족도 양호.

## Summary

This 5-year clinical observation ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.7518/hxkq.2024.2024166)) is, among the wiki's infected-socket immediate implant placement (IIP) papers, one of the longest-followed and the only one specifically isolating the **posterior region**. Thirty-six patients received 41 simple taper retentive implants placed immediately into posterior sockets with chronic apical infection, after thorough debridement of the infected alveolus. Over 5 years post-crown-restoration, survival was 97.6% (one implant failed with mobility). Marginal bone resorption was concentrated in the early phase (placement to crown restoration: -0.74±1.03 mm, statistically significant) and then essentially plateaued (crown restoration to 5-year: -0.21±0.70 mm, not significant) — a pattern consistent with early peri-implant remodeling rather than ongoing infection-driven bone loss. No significant differences emerged by mesial/distal position, jaw, tooth position, or implant length. Peri-implant soft tissue remained healthy in nearly all cases (1 peri-implantitis case), with no mechanical complications and good overall patient satisfaction. **Note**: the original article is Chinese-language and the PMC record's full-text body field was empty on retrieval; this page is built from the structured English/Chinese abstracts only (per the wiki's abstract-only ingestion protocol).

## Key Contributions

- Longest reported (5-year) posterior-region-specific follow-up for chronic-apical-infection IIP among the wiki's infected-socket papers
- Demonstrates a two-phase marginal bone resorption curve: significant early resorption, non-significant later change — suggesting stabilization rather than progressive infection-related bone loss
- No site-specific risk factor identified (mesial/distal, maxilla/mandible, tooth position, implant length all NS) within the chronically-infected posterior cohort

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-center clinical observation, prospective-style enrollment and 5-year follow-up post-crown-restoration
- **n**: 36 patients, 41 simple taper retentive implants; posterior teeth, chronic apical infection
- **Protocol**: thorough debridement of chronic infected alveolar socket → immediate implant placement
- **Follow-up**: soft/hard tissue assessment at implant placement, crown restoration, and 5 years post-restoration
