## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (BMC Oral Health 2024, n=26 patients/41 implants, 1 year): VST + 6-day protocol for immediate implants in infected (Group I, n=19) vs non-infected (Group N, n=21) Elian Type II maxillary sockets.

100% implant survival in both groups; facial bone thickness gain significant over time in both (apical gain I: 2.4 mm, N: 1.63 mm); no significant between-group difference in bone thickness or soft tissue levels, except mesial papilla recession was significantly greater in non-infected group.

Single-operator prospective cohort, 1-year follow-up, small sample (n=26); supports the 6-day decontamination protocol as an effective pre-implant socket preparation strategy for infected type II sockets.

## One-line Summary

Prospective cohort (n=26, 41 implants, 1 year) demonstrating 100% survival and equivalent bone/soft tissue outcomes for VST-based immediate implants in infected vs non-infected type II maxillary sockets using a 6-day decontamination protocol.

## 한줄요약

전향적 코호트 (n=26, 41개 임플란트, 1년): 6-day 소독 프로토콜 + VST 즉시 식립 시 감염·비감염 type II 소켓 간 골두께·점막 수준 동등, 양군 100% 생존.

## Summary

This prospective cohort study compared immediate implant placement using Vestibular Socket Therapy (VST) and a 6-day decontamination protocol in infected (Group I) versus non-infected (Group N) Elian type II maxillary sockets. Twenty-six age-and-sex-matched patients (41 implant sites) were treated at a private clinic in Alexandria, Egypt (2019). The 6-day protocol involved atraumatic extraction, socket debridement, metronidazole irrigation, root cleaning, apical sealing with composite resin, and temporary tooth reimplantation for 6 days to decontaminate the socket before VST. At 1-year follow-up, all 41 implants survived (100%). Facial bone thickness increased significantly over time in both groups, and no statistically significant between-group differences were found except for greater mesial papilla recession in the non-infected group. These findings suggest that active infection, when properly managed by the 6-day protocol, does not compromise the regenerative or esthetic outcomes of VST-based immediate implants.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrated 100% 1-year survival of immediately placed implants in actively infected type II sockets (periapical lesions, fistulae, sinus tracts) using VST + 6-day protocol
- Facial bone thickness gain was clinically significant in infected sockets (apical 2.4 mm) and comparable to non-infected sockets — infection, once decontaminated, does not impair bone regeneration
- Mucosal recession was minimal in infected group (0.29–0.51 mm) — non-inferior to non-infected outcomes
- The 6-day protocol (metronidazole irrigation + temporary root reimplantation) validated as a practical presurgical decontamination strategy
- Mesial papilla preservation was paradoxically better in the infected group — possible selection bias or patient-level anatomical differences

## Methodology

Prospective cohort; n=26 patients, 41 implants; frequency-matched by age and sex; Group I (infected, 13 patients/19 sites) vs Group N (non-infected, 13 patients/21 sites); single operator, single center (Alexandria, Egypt); January–November 2019 recruitment.

**Infection criteria (Group I):** periapical radiolucency (3 sites), fistulae (2 sites), sinus tracts (11 sites), swelling (3 sites).

**6-day protocol:** atraumatic extraction → curettage (Elaskary irrigation curette) → mechanical debridement + metronidazole irrigation (500 mg/100 mL) → root apical third resection → composite resin sealing → tooth reimplanted out of occlusion for 6 days.

**VST:** 1-cm vestibular incision → subperiosteal tunnel (Elaskary elevator) → implant placement (BioHorizons, surgical guide) → cortical membrane shield (OsteoBiol Curved Lamina, 1.0 mm) + xenograft cancellous bone.

**Outcomes:** CBCT facial bone thickness at apical/mid/crestal levels; CBCT mucosal levels (mid/mesial/distal); implant survival. Assessed at baseline and 1 year.

**Statistics:** Mann–Whitney U (between groups); Wilcoxon signed-rank test (within-group time change); p<0.05.

## Results

| Outcome | Group I (infected) | Group N (non-infected) | Between-group p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant survival | 100% (19/19) | 100% (22/22) | — |
| Apical bone gain | ~2.4 mm | ~1.63 mm | NS |
| Mid bone gain | ~1.6 mm | ~0.98 mm | NS |
| Crestal bone gain | ~0.85 mm | ~0.26 mm | NS |
| Mid mucosal recession | 0.51 mm | 1.47 mm | NS |
| Mesial papilla recession | 0.45 mm | 0.39 mm | p<0.05 (N > I) |
| Distal mucosal recession | 0.29 mm | 1.16 mm | NS |

Within-group bone thickness changes (T0 → 1 year): significant in both groups at all levels (p<0.05).
