## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of extraction-socket (ES) classifications with a proposal for a new system combining morphologic and patient-related factors; PubMed, EMBASE, and SCOPUS searched; abstract only (full text not retrieved).

Thirteen ES classifications were identified; the most repeated hard/soft tissue factors were buccal bone dehiscence, interproximal bone, gingival recession, and soft tissue phenotype, while systemic conditions and smoking received minimal attention.

The proposed system's patient-level risk factors address a documented blind spot in anatomy-only case-selection frameworks such as the sagittal root position (SRP) classifications held in this wiki.

기존 ES 분류 13개 확인; 반복 등장 인자는 협측 골 결손, 인접면골, 치은 퇴축, 치은 표현형인 반면 전신 질환·흡연 등 환자 인자는 거의 미다루어짐.

제안 분류의 환자 수준 위험인자는 본 위키의 시상 치근 위치(SRP) 분류 등 해부학 중심 증례선택 체계의 공백을 보완.

## Summary

Abstract-only page. Relevant to IIP case-selection criteria because it appraises 13 existing single-rooted extraction socket classifications and argues that they over-weight morphology while neglecting patient-related factors (systemic conditions, smoking). The proposed system synthesizes prior morphologic cornerstones with those patient-level domains.

> [!warning] Abstract-only
> Full text not retrieved; the proposed classification's actual categories, definitions, and validation data are unavailable. Only abstract-level statements are recorded.

## Key Contributions

- Critical appraisal of 13 single-rooted extraction socket classifications.
- Identifies recurrent core morphologic parameters (buccal dehiscence, interproximal bone, gingival recession, soft tissue phenotype).
- Documents under-representation of patient-related factors (systemic conditions, smoking).
- Proposes a new classification combining morphologic cornerstones with patient factors, aligned to contemporary consensus.

## Methodology

- Systematic search in PubMed, EMBASE, SCOPUS for single-rooted ES classification systems.
- Critical appraisal to extract repeated hard/soft tissue factors and identify gaps.
- New system synthesized from prior cornerstones plus patient-level domains (full taxonomy in full text, not retrieved).

## Results

- 13 ES classifications detected.
- Most repeated factors: buccal bone dehiscence, interproximal bone, gingival recession, soft tissue phenotype.
- Gap: minimal attention to systemic conditions and smoking.

## Limitations

- Abstract-only; proposed categories and validation data unavailable.
- Review-and-proposal without outcome data validating predictive value.
- Single-rooted sockets only; molar/multirooted morphology out of scope.
