## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT cross-sectional study (195 patients, first-molar sites, Dubai) measuring posterior mandibular ridge parameters — crest/base width, heights above/below Point P, lingual concavity depth and angle — to develop the CPDU classification integrating ridge shape, undercut, and IAC position into a single preoperative risk framework.

U-type (undercut) ridge was most prevalent (54.4% L, 52.1% R); a new D-type (divergent: narrow base, wide crest, inherent undercut) fills the gap in prior CPU systems, and an influential/non-influential modifier flags when the undercut lies above IAC in the implant path — the highest-risk configuration for lingual plate perforation.

CBCT is mandatory for posterior mandibular implant planning; D-I and U-I type ridges require modified implant axis to avoid lingual cortical plate perforation, and the classification provides a structured preoperative language for that decision.

## 세줄요약

후향적 CBCT 단면연구(195명, 하악 제1대구치 부위) — 능선정 폭(Wc)·기저부 폭(Wb)·총 치조골 높이(Vcb)·P점 상하 높이(Vc/Vb)·설측 함몰 깊이/각도를 계측해 CPDU 분류 시스템(수렴형/평행형/분기형 + 언더컷 U + influential I/N) 개발.

언더컷형(U-type) 능선이 54.4%로 최다; 기존 CPU 분류의 맹점이던 D형(분기형: 좁은 기저부+넓은 능선정+고유 언더컷) 신설, 하치조 신경관(IAC, Inferior Alveolar Canal) 위상에 따라 influential(I)/non-influential(N)로 구분해 설측 피질골 천공 (Lingual Plate Perforation, LPP) 위험 구간을 명확화.

후방 하악 임플란트는 CBCT 필수 — D-I형·U-I형은 LPP 위험 최고이며, CPDU 분류가 수술 전 식립 방향 계획의 표준 언어를 제공.

## Summary

Posterior mandibular implant placement carries two competing anatomical threats: the inferior alveolar canal (IAC) below and the lingual cortical plate above-and-medially. Existing classification systems (Jung 3-type by concavity depth; Watanabe A/B/C; Braut absent/influential/non-influential; Chan C/P/U) each captured part of the problem but none unified ridge shape + concavity morphology + IAC position in one framework. This cross-sectional CBCT study of 195 patients at a UAE center measures six parameters per site and proposes the CPDU system.

The key clinical insight: lingual undercut can occur in *any* ridge shape (C, P, or the new D-type), and its danger depends on whether it lies above the IAC in the implant path. The new D-type (divergent = narrow base, wide crest) is morphologically similar to the old U-type but is distinguished because its undercut is inherent rather than variable — making step 2 (undercut check) redundant for D. The I/N modifier then adds the IAC proximity dimension: D-I means narrow base + wide crest + undercut above IAC → maximal LPP risk; P-U-N means a parallel ridge with a lingual undercut that does NOT sit in the planned implant path → lower risk.

Prevalence data confirm that U-type/undercut is the dominant mandibular posterior morphology (~54%), so clinicians should assume undercut-risk until CBCT proves otherwise.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces **CPDU classification**: C (convergent) / P (parallel) / D (divergent) + U (undercut) + I or N influential modifier — unifies three previously separate dimensions
- Adds the **D-type** (narrow base, wide crest, inherent undercut) — a morphology that prior Chan CPU system lumped into U-type despite distinct base-width anatomy
- **Influential (I) / non-influential (N) modifier** operationalizes clinical risk: I = undercut above IAC in the implant corridor; N = undercut present but not in the planned path
- Quantifies that lingual concavity is deeper in undercut sites (1.72 vs 0.61 mm, p=0.001) — the undercut is not just a shape but a force multiplier on perforation depth
- Identifies modifiers: males + younger patients + first molar presence = higher/broader ridges → relatively safer anatomy

## Methodology

- **Design**: retrospective cross-sectional CBCT analysis, single center (Dental Hospital, Dubai Health, UAE); MBRU IRB-2021-52; STROBE checklist
- **Sample**: 195 of 350 screened CBCT volumes; 90M/105F; age range 19–70+; all had missing mandibular first molar, ≥8 mm bone height, ≥3.5 mm crest width
- **Imaging**: Sirona Galileos, 15×15×15 cm FOV, 0.15 mm voxel, 85 kVp, 28 mA; OsiriX MD 3D MPR
- **Landmarks**: Line A = horizontal 2 mm coronal to superior IAC border; Point A = Line A × lingual plate; Point P = most prominent lingual cortical point
- **Measurements**: Wc (crest width), Wb (base width), Vcb (total height), Vb (height below P), Vc (height above P), concavity depth (Point P to Line Vb), concavity angle (P–A–Line A)
- **Statistics**: Kolmogorov-Smirnov, Mann-Whitney, Kruskal-Wallis, Pearson/Spearman, IBM SPSS 29.0; intraobserver kappa assessed

## Results

**Ridge type distribution:**

| Type | Left | Right |
|---|---|---|
| U (undercut) | 54.4% | 52.1% |
| P (parallel) | 27.2% | 33.5% |
| C (convergent) | 18.5% | 14.4% |

**Lingual concavity depth by undercut presence:**
| Undercut | Left (mm) | Right (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| Present | 1.72 ± 1.40 | 1.93 ± 1.56 |
| Absent | 0.61 ± 0.64 | 0.71 ± 0.69 |
| *p*-value | 0.001 | <0.001 |

**Concavity depth by ridge type:**
| Type | Left (mm) | Right (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| C | 1.19 ± 0.96 | 1.16 ± 1.09 |
| P | 1.42 ± 1.22 | 1.37 ± 1.03 |
| U | 1.89 ± 1.53 | 2.27 ± 1.74 |

**Height above P point by undercut:**
| Undercut | Left (mm) | Right (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| Present | 17.53 ± 4.39 | 17.78 ± 3.68 |
| Absent | 12.59 ± 6.56 | 15.17 ± 5.50 |
| *p*-value | 0.006 | 0.006 |

**Sex**: males > females for ridge height (p<0.001) and height above P point (p<0.001) bilaterally.
**Age**: <40 years: greater crest width and ridge height.
**First molar present**: greater crest width, total ridge height, concavity depth (right, p<0.001).
**Nearly all U-type ridges exhibit a lingual undercut** (strong categorical association across both sides).

**CPDU Classification System:**

| Step | Parameter | Codes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ridge shape | C, P, D |
| 2 | Undercut (C/P only; D = inherent) | +U or skip |
| 3 | Influential status | I (above IAC, in path) or N |

*Examples: C-U-I, P-U-N, D-I, D-N, C, P*
