## Three-line Summary
Randomized controlled trial, n=50 patients (25 immediate, 25 early), 1-year follow-up comparing immediate placement with immediate provisioning/socket preservation vs. early placement with GBR/delayed loading in maxillary anterior esthetic zone (intact bone walls, interproximal bone intact).

No implant failures in either group; Pink Esthetic Score (PES) comparable at 1 year: immediate group 12.8 ± 1.19 vs. early group 12.5 ± 1.36 (p = .362); mid-buccal mucosal level change: immediate group +0.2 ± 1.02 mm (p = .047) vs. early group 0 mm; crestal bone changes minimal in both (p > .05).

Both protocols achieved excellent esthetic outcomes within 1 year; immediate placement reduced chair time by ~50% but esthetic gain was modest; patient-selection criteria (intact walls, no infection) critical to success; limited to 1-year follow-up.

## 세줄요약
무작위 대조 시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT), n=50명 (즉시 식립 25명, 조기 식립 25명), 1년 추적 — 상악 전치부 심미 부위에서 즉시 식립(즉시 임시치관, 치조제 보존)과 조기 식립(골유도재생, Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR·지연 부하)을 비교, 골벽·치간골 정상 케이스로 한정.

두 군 모두 임플란트 실패 없음(생존율 100%); 1년 후 핑크 심미 지수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES) 유사 — 즉시군 12.8 ± 1.19 vs 조기군 12.5 ± 1.36 (p = .362); 협측 점막 수준 (Mid-Buccal Mucosal Level, MBML) 변화는 즉시군 +0.2 ± 1.02 mm vs 조기군 변화 없음 (p = .047); 치조정 골변화는 양 군 모두 미미(p > .05).

엄격한 환자 선택(정상 골벽·감염 없음) 조건에서는 두 프로토콜 모두 1년 내 우수한 심미 결과(PES>12)를 보이며, 즉시 식립은 의자 시간을 약 50%(127분 vs 259분) 단축; 단기(1년) 소규모(n=50) 연구라는 한계.

## Summary
This 1-year parallel-group RCT (n=50, 1:1 randomization) directly isolates placement **timing** as the study variable in the maxillary anterior esthetic zone, comparing immediate implant placement (Type 1, same-visit, with socket preservation and immediate provisionalization) against early implant placement (4-month healing after socket grafting, then implant placement with guided bone regeneration and delayed restoration). Both arms achieved 100% implant survival at 1 year. Esthetic outcome (Pink Esthetic Score) was statistically equivalent between groups (12.8 vs 12.5, p=.362), and crestal bone change was minimal and equivalent at both mesial and distal sites (p>.05). The only statistically significant difference was a small mid-buccal mucosal level gain in the immediate group (+0.2 mm vs. 0 mm, p=.047). The immediate protocol also cut total chair time roughly in half (127 vs. 259 minutes, p<.001). The authors conclude both protocols deliver excellent esthetics when patients are selected on intact bone walls and absence of infection, with immediate placement offering an efficiency advantage rather than a decisive esthetic one.

## Key Contributions
- Direct RCT comparison of immediate vs. early placement **timing** in the esthetic zone, with immediate provisionalization present in the immediate arm but not confounding the comparison (early arm receives delayed restoration by design, so the trial compares full protocols rather than timing in isolation)
- Quantified a small but statistically significant soft-tissue gain for immediate placement (MBML +0.2 mm, p=.047) — a directional finding, not a recession risk
- Confirmed comparable long-term PES (>12, "excellent") at 1 year for both placement timings when patient-selection criteria (intact walls, no infection) are met
- Demonstrated a ~50% chair-time reduction with the immediate protocol (127 vs. 259 min, p<.001) — an operational-efficiency argument independent of the esthetic outcome
- 100% implant survival in both arms at 1 year

## Methodology
Parallel-group RCT, 50 patients (25 immediate / 25 early), single-tooth gap in maxillary anterior (central incisor to first premolar), intact alveolar bone walls and interproximal bone required. Immediate arm: Type 1 same-visit placement + socket preservation graft/membrane + immediate provisional crown. Early arm: extraction + socket grafting, 4-month healing, then implant placement with GBR and delayed (conventional-timeline) final crown. Outcomes assessed at baseline and 1-year follow-up: Pink Esthetic Score (PES), mid-buccal mucosal level (MBML), crestal bone change (CBC), patient chair time. Blinding not stated (placement timing is not blindable).

## Results
| Outcome | Immediate (n=25) | Early (n=25) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failures at 1 year | 0 | 0 | — |
| PES at 1 year (≥12 = excellent) | 12.8 ± 1.19 | 12.5 ± 1.36 | 0.362 |

- [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/peitsinis-2025-implant-placement-timing-loading-protocols]] — narrative review ranks immediate placement as carrying the "highest esthetic risk" of the three timing categories (immediate/early/delayed); this trial's comparable 1-year PES (>12 in both arms, p=.362) is a specific counter-example suggesting strict case selection can narrow that general esthetic-risk gradient to non-significance.
- [[immediate-implant/tonetti-2017-immediate-vs-delayed-implant-timing-rct]] — multicenter RCT on an adjacent timing axis (immediate vs. 12-week placement, labeled "delayed" there) found immediate placement produced significantly *worse* esthetics (42% vs 19% inadequate PES, p=.03) and more wound-healing failures; this trial, comparing immediate against a similarly short (4-month) interval, found the opposite direction — comparable-to-slightly-better esthetics for immediate — underscoring that patient selection and grafting protocol, not weeks-of-delay alone, likely drive the immediate-placement esthetic outcome.
