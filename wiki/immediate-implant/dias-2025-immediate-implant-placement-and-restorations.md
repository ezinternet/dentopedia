## Three-line Summary

Scoping review of 28 systematic reviews on immediate implant placement (IIP) and immediate restoration (IR) in single- and multiple-unit fixed dental prostheses, searched across PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane; abstract only (full text not retrieved).

Most reviews addressed anatomical, surgical, and prosthetic factors for single-unit IIP/IR in the esthetic zone, with limited evidence for molars or multiunit prostheses; key anatomical factors were buccal wall integrity, alveolar process dimensions, buccal gap width, and gingival phenotype.

Maps where IIP case-selection criteria are consolidated (anterior single units) versus thin (posterior/molars, multiunit FDPs) — a gap this wiki's molar-septum and full-arch pages partially fill.

## Summary

Abstract-only page. This scoping review is the evidence base commissioned for the ORF IEM 2025 consensus on immediacy (PMID 40900553), mapping 28 systematic reviews on IIP and immediate restoration across anatomical, surgical, and prosthetic domains. It is ingested to document the shape of the criteria evidence — and its posterior/multiunit gaps — rather than to supply effect estimates.

> [!warning] Abstract-only
> Full text not retrieved. No pooled numbers or complete factor taxonomy are available; only abstract-level statements are recorded here.

## Key Contributions

- Maps 28 systematic reviews on IIP/IR in single- and multiple-unit FDPs.
- Organizes case-selection factors into anatomical, surgical, and prosthetic domains.
- Flags limited molar and multiunit-prosthesis evidence relative to anterior single-unit evidence.

## Methodology

- Searches: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane for systematic reviews on IIP/IR in anterior and posterior jaws.
- Bibliometric data and study details extracted; scoping design (no pooled meta-analysis).
- Companion to ORF IEM 2025 consensus (PMID 40900553) and the full-arch narrative review (PMID 40900555).

## Results

- 28 systematic reviews included.
- Key anatomical factors: buccal wall integrity, alveolar process dimensions, buccal gap width, gingival phenotype.
- Surgical factors: flapless vs flapped procedures, implant positioning (abstract truncated).
- Coverage skew toward esthetic-zone single units; limited molar/multiunit evidence.

## Limitations

- Abstract-only; complete results and factor taxonomy unavailable.
- Scoping design maps but does not grade evidence quality or effect sizes.
- Limited posterior/multiunit evidence constrains use as a molar criteria guide.
