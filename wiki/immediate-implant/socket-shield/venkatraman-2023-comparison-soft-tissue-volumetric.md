## Three-line Summary
Parallel-arm RCT (n=22, single maxillary incisor immediate implant, immediate loading) comparing socket shield technique (SST) vs. conventional technique (CT), assessing buccal soft-tissue volumetric change (digital cast superimposition) and Pink Esthetic Score (PES) over 12 months.

Mean 12-month buccal soft-tissue volumetric change: SST −0.152 ± 0.86 mm vs. CT −0.643 ± 0.35 mm (P = .001); PES was significantly higher in the SST group at both 6 months (P = .001) and 12 months (P = .007).

SST produced less volumetric shrinkage of buccal soft tissue and better pink esthetics than the conventional technique at immediately placed and immediately loaded single maxillary incisor implants.

## 세줄요약
평행군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)(n=22, 상악 단일 절치 즉시식립+즉시부하)으로 소켓실드술식 (Socket Shield Technique, SST)과 관습적 술식 (Conventional Technique, CT)을 비교 — 협측 연조직 체적변화(디지털 캐스트 중첩)와 핑크 심미 지수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES)를 12개월간 평가.

12개월 협측 연조직 체적변화: SST군 −0.152 ± 0.86mm vs CT군 −0.643 ± 0.35mm(P=.001); PES는 SST군이 6개월(P=.001)·12개월(P=.007) 모두 유의하게 높았음.

SST는 즉시식립+즉시부하 상악 단일절치에서 관습적 술식 대비 협측 연조직 체적 수축이 적고 핑크 심미도가 우수함.

## Summary
This parallel-arm RCT randomized 22 patients requiring single maxillary incisor replacement to socket shield technique (SST) or conventional technique (CT) at immediate implant placement, with all implants immediately loaded and definitive crowns delivered at 6 months. Using digital-cast superimposition (a volumetric rather than linear/point measure), buccal soft-tissue change at 12 months was significantly smaller in the SST group (−0.152 mm) than the CT group (−0.643 mm, P=.001), and Pink Esthetic Score was significantly higher for SST at both 6 and 12 months. The abstract does not report complications, randomization/allocation-concealment method, or buccal bone (hard-tissue) measurements — only the abstract/landing page was retrievable, not the full text.

## Key Contributions
- One of the few RCTs (rather than case series or retrospective cohorts) directly randomizing SST vs. conventional technique at immediate implant placement
- Uses digital-cast superimposition for quantitative volumetric soft-tissue measurement rather than linear/point recession only
- Shows the SST esthetic advantage (PES) is present by 6 months and sustained to 12 months, not just a short-term artifact

## Methodology
Parallel-arm RCT, n=22 participants requiring single maxillary incisor replacement with an immediate implant. Randomized to SST or CT; all implants immediately loaded; definitive crowns placed 6 months after implant placement. Digital white-light scans of casts taken pre-implant and at 12 months, superimposed to quantify buccal soft-tissue volumetric change. PES assessed pre-implant, at 6 months, and at 12 months. Statistical analysis: Stata 16.0.
