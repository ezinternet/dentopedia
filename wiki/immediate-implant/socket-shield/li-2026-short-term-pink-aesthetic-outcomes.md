## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and Bayesian network meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD420261297690; 21 studies = 15 RCTs + 6 non-randomized controlled trials; 1013 single-tooth implants in the esthetic zone) comparing three socket shield technique (SST) jumping-gap protocols (SST ungrafted, SST-GBR grafted, SST-nJP no gap) against conventional immediate implant placement (IIP ungrafted, IIP-GBR grafted) for short-term (4-12 months) Pink Esthetic Score (PES-SO).

In the full network, IIP was significantly lowest: vs IIP, IIP-GBR MD 1.13 (95% CrI 0.19-2.00), SST MD 2.30 (1.55-3.05), SST-GBR MD 2.57 (1.49-3.57); SST and SST-GBR also beat IIP-GBR (MD 1.17 and 1.45) but did not differ from each other (SST-GBR vs SST MD 0.28, CrI -0.67 to 1.13). SST-nJP ranked top but rested on one small study with very wide CrIs.

Findings are exploratory: transitivity is questionable, the distribution-based MCID (0.619) is unvalidated, only PES-SO at 4-12 months was analyzed (no bone, recession, survival or complication outcomes), and the pattern held in the clinically restricted analysis (12 studies, non-thin labial plates) with some ranking instability.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 베이지안 네트워크 메타분석(21편: RCT 15 + 비무작위 6, 심미부 단일치 임플란트 1013개): 소켓실드 기법(Socket Shield Technique, SST) 3가지 점핑갭 관리(비이식 SST, 골유도재생술 병용 SST-GBR, 갭 없음 SST-nJP)를 기존 즉시식립(비이식 IIP, GBR 병용 IIP-GBR)과 단기(4-12개월) 핑크심미점수(Pink Esthetic Score, PES-SO)로 비교.

전체 네트워크에서 IIP가 유의하게 최저: IIP 대비 IIP-GBR MD 1.13, SST MD 2.30(CrI 1.55-3.05), SST-GBR MD 2.57(CrI 1.49-3.57); SST·SST-GBR는 IIP-GBR보다도 우월(MD 1.17, 1.45)했으나 SST 대 SST-GBR 차이는 없음(MD 0.28, CrI -0.67~1.13). SST-nJP는 순위 1위였으나 소규모 단일 연구 기반으로 신뢰구간이 매우 넓음.

탐색적 결과: 전이성(transitivity) 가정이 불확실하고, 분포기반 MCID(0.619)는 미검증이며, PES-SO 단기 결과만 분석(골소실·퇴축·생존율·합병증 미평가). 임상 제한 분석(얇지 않은 순판 12편)에서도 패턴은 유지되나 순위는 일부 변동.

## Summary

Bayesian NMA of 21 studies (1013 implants) on short-term PES-SO that separates socket shield protocols by jumping-gap management. All SST variants beat ungrafted conventional immediate implants (SST MD 2.30, SST-GBR MD 2.57), and SST/SST-GBR also beat grafted conventional immediate implants (MD 1.17 and 1.45); ungrafted vs grafted SST showed no difference. It refines rather than overturns earlier SST meta-analyses, and the authors label it exploratory.

## Key Contributions

- Splits "socket shield" into three jumping-gap protocols (SST, SST-GBR, SST-nJP) and pairs them with two conventional controls (IIP, IIP-GBR) in one Bayesian network; earlier pairwise reviews pooled all SST variants against one control.
- Pre-specified exploration of transitivity/heterogeneity: a clinically restricted analysis (non-thin labial plate, studies without comparability-related reporting limitations) and a supporting analysis.
- Introduces an exploratory distribution-based MCID for PES-SO (0.5 x pooled SD = 0.619), explicitly flagged as not clinically validated.

## Methodology

- PRISMA + PRISMA-NMA; PROSPERO CRD420261297690. Nine databases (PubMed, Embase, Cochrane, Web of Science, Scopus, CNKI, VIP, Wanfang, SinoMed), January 2010 to January 2026; 9,810 records + 7 from prior reviews, 64 full texts, 21 included.
- Population: adults, immediate implant in the esthetic zone (second premolar to second premolar); excluded labial plate fracture, systemic conditions affecting osseointegration. Sole outcome: PES-SO at 4-12 months.
- RoB 2.0 (15 RCTs: 6 low risk, 9 some concerns) and ROBINS-I (6 NRCTs: all moderate). Certainty by CINeMA. Random-effects Bayesian NMA (R 4.4.3, JAGS, gemtc), node-splitting for inconsistency, funnel plot/Egger for publication bias.

## Results

| Comparison (full network, 21 studies, 1013 implants) | MD in PES-SO (95% CrI) |
|---|---|
| IIP-GBR vs IIP | 1.13 (0.19-2.00) |
| SST vs IIP | 2.30 (1.55-3.05) |
| SST-GBR vs IIP | 2.57 (1.49-3.57) |
| SST-nJP vs IIP | 3.09 (0.95-5.19) |
| SST vs IIP-GBR | 1.17 (0.46-1.95) |
| SST-GBR vs IIP-GBR | 1.45 (0.94-1.93) |
| SST-nJP vs IIP-GBR | 1.96 (0.06-3.89) |
| SST-GBR vs SST | 0.28 (-0.67 to 1.13) |
| SST-nJP vs SST-GBR | 0.52 (-1.32 to 2.38) |

- Ranking: SST-nJP, SST-GBR, SST, IIP-GBR, IIP (SST-nJP informed by a single small study; very wide CrIs).
- Heterogeneity: I2 = 2%, tau = 0.72 (CrI 0.46-1.11), above the exploratory MCID of 0.619; node-splitting P > 0.05 throughout.
- Clinically restricted analysis (12 studies, 430 implants): tau 0.41 (CrI 0.05-0.96), rankings consistent, no pairwise direction reversed; SST-nJP vs IIP-GBR lost significance (mean 1.44, CrI -0.21 to 3.07). Supporting analysis: SST vs SST-GBR order flipped (SUCRA 65.68% vs 73.84%) with wider CrIs, attributed to two small thin-plate studies.
- Seven studies compared only SST-GBR vs IIP-GBR and had reporting limitations that drove heterogeneity; graft material, membrane, shield dimensions and gap width did not materially reduce it.
- CINeMA (restricted analysis): high for IIP vs IIP-GBR, IIP-GBR vs SST, IIP vs SST-GBR; moderate for most others; SST-GBR vs SST-nJP low; SST vs SST-nJP very low. Egger P = 0.16.

## Decision Position

- Presence of the shield, rather than grafting of the jumping gap, appears the dominant short-term PES driver (SST and SST-GBR not distinguishable), while grafting alone (IIP-GBR) also improved PES over ungrafted IIP.
- SST-nJP findings are insufficient to justify a clinical priority recommendation.
- Limitations stated by the authors: transitivity unquantifiable, mixed RCT/NRCT designs, PES-SO only (no bone loss, recession, shield exposure, survival), short follow-up, unvalidated MCID, thin labial plate as a heterogeneity source, small-study effects not excludable.
