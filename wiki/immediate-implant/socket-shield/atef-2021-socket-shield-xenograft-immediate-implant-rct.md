## Three-line Summary

RCT (Atef et al., 2021; n=40, 20 per group, 12-month follow-up) comparing socket shield technique versus xenograft around single immediate implants placed in the esthetic zone.

Socket shield gave significantly less buccal bone resorption (−0.28 vs −0.94 mm) and less buccal mucosal recession (0.18 vs 0.52 mm), with 100% survival in both groups at 12 months.

Results support socket shield for superior buccal-bone and soft-tissue preservation over xenograft at 1 year, though the technique is operator-sensitive and longer-term data are needed.

## 세줄요약

RCT(Atef 등, 2021; n=40, 20명씩, 12개월 추적): 심미부 단일 즉시임플란트에서 소켓실드(Socket Shield Technique, SST) vs 이종골(xenograft) 비교.

SST군이 협측 골 흡수 유의하게 적음(−0.28 vs −0.94 mm), 협측 점막 퇴축도 적음(0.18 vs 0.52 mm); 양군 모두 12개월 생존율 100%.

단기 결과에서 SST가 이종골 대비 협측 골·연조직 보존에 우월하나 술자 의존도가 높고 장기 데이터가 필요함.

## Summary
RCT comparing socket shield technique vs xenograft for buccal bone and soft tissue preservation around single immediate implants in the esthetic zone.

## Key Contributions
- Buccal bone change: socket shield -0.28mm vs xenograft -0.94mm (p<0.05)
- Buccal mucosal recession: socket shield 0.18mm vs xenograft 0.52mm
- Both groups: 100% survival at 12 months

## Methodology
Prospective RCT; n=40 (20 per group); 12-month follow-up.

## Results
Socket shield significantly better buccal bone preservation. Soft tissue recession also reduced. Technique-sensitive: requires experienced operator.
