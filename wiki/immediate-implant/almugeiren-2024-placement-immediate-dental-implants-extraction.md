## Three-line Summary

Meta-analysis (10 RCTs, n=849 patients, 2000–2022): immediate implant placement in apical pathosis sites vs non-infected sites; Saudi Arabia/Riyadh Elm University.

Infected sites: plaque index RR=0.59 (p<0.05), bleeding index RR=0.77 (p<0.05), implant survival OR=2.08 (p<0.05); individual RCT survival rates 92–100%.

Immediate implant placement in apical pathosis is clinically viable when antiseptic environment and proper debridement are ensured.

## Summary

Based on articles retrieved from PubMed, this meta-analysis ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jobcr.2024.03.008)) synthesized 10 RCTs (n=849) comparing immediate implant placement in sites with apical pathosis vs non-infected sites. All three pooled outcomes favored the infected-site approach: plaque index RR=0.59 indicates lower plaque accumulation, bleeding index RR=0.77 indicates lower peri-implant inflammation, and implant survival OR=2.08 indicates favorable odds of implant success. High heterogeneity (I²=83–90%) reflects diverse protocols. Individual RCTs showed consistent results: Crespi (4yr, 98.9% vs 100%), Camara (1yr, no retrograde peri-implantitis in acutely infected sites), Jung (5yr, 100% both groups), Kakar (95.45% with laser decontamination). The authors conclude that immediate dental implant placement in apical pathosis sites is safe and effective when antiseptic protocols are applied, supported by statistically significant meta-analysis results.

## Key Contributions

- Quantified benefit of immediate implantation in infected sites: OR=2.08 for survival (higher than 1 = favorable)
- Demonstrated low plaque (RR=0.59) and bleeding (RR=0.77) indices post-implant in infected sites
- Included Camara et al.: 100 patients with acutely infected periapical sites — no retrograde peri-implantitis at 1 year
- 10 RCTs from 2000–2022 — most comprehensive RCT-based MA on this topic

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR+MA, PRISMA-compliant; Cochrane Collaboration risk of bias tool
- **Search**: MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Cochrane, Web of Sciences; 2000–2023
- **Studies**: 10 RCTs; 849 patients with apical pathosis or non-infected sockets
- **PICO**: P=apical pathosis; I=immediate implantation; C=delayed implantation; O=plaque index, bleeding index, survival rate
- **Statistical**: RevMan, Mantel-Haenszel random bivariate effects; Begg's test publication bias (p=0.45 NS)
