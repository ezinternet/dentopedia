## Three-line Summary

Prospective human in-vivo study (n=18 subjects, 21 immediate implants at premolar/molar sites, J Clin Periodontol 2004) with 4-month surgical re-entry and no membrane or grafting material used.

Buccal bone height was reduced by approximately 56% and lingual/palatal bone by approximately 30% over 4 months; the marginal gap between implant and socket wall filled completely with woven bone confirming osseointegration.

Immediate implant placement does NOT substitute for ridge preservation — this foundational human in-vivo study established the magnitude of buccal bone loss that underpins all subsequent protocols requiring gap grafting and augmentation.

## 세줄요약

전향적 in-vivo 연구 (n=18명, 21개 즉시식립, 소구치·대구치 부위, J Clin Periodontol 2004): 차폐막·골이식 없이 4개월 후 재진입 평가.

협측골 높이는 약 56%, 설측·구개측 골은 약 30% 감소; 임플란트-발치와 벽 사이 간극은 woven bone으로 완전 충전되어 골유착 확인.

즉시식립은 치조제 보존의 대안이 될 수 없다는 결론의 핵심 인간 임상 근거; 협측골 흡수 규모를 수치화해 이후 간극 이식·증대 프로토콜 필요성의 근거를 확립.

## Summary
Prospective human in-vivo study (18 subjects, 21 immediate implants) with 4-month surgical re-entry. No membrane or grafting material was used. Demonstrates that significant buccal bone resorption occurs despite immediate implant placement. Foundational reference establishing buccal wall resorption after immediate implant.

## Key Contributions
- Buccal bone height reduced by ~56% over 4 months
- Lingual/palatal bone height reduced by ~30%
- Marginal gap fills with woven bone — osseointegration confirmed
- Immediate implant does NOT substitute for ridge preservation
- No membrane / no graft: natural healing course documented

## Methodology
- 18 subjects, premolar/molar extraction sites
- No augmentation procedures
- Measurements at implant placement and surgical re-entry (4 months)
- Buccal/lingual/mesial/distal bone height assessed clinically
