## Three-line Summary

Parallel-group RCT (n=220) from a single Chinese hospital comparing 1-year outcomes of immediate vs delayed single-tooth implant placement (Straumann Bone Level Tapered). Primary outcome — 1-year implant survival — was equivalent (95.45% immediate vs 92.73% delayed; χ²=0.736, P=0.391; adjusted OR=1.82, 95% CI 0.56–5.91). Immediate placement was superior across all secondary outcomes: peri-implant health (PD 3.16 vs 3.39 mm; mPLI 0.95 vs 1.21; mSBI 0.85 vs 1.01), aesthetics (PES 12.18 vs 11.34; WES 7.78 vs 7.23), oral health QoL (OHIP-14 3.20 vs 4.15), and patient satisfaction (VAS attachment, colour, chewing; all P<0.05).

## 세줄요약

단일기관 무작위대조임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)(n=220)에서 즉시식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP) vs 지연식립 (Delayed Implant Placement, DIP) 비교; 1년 임플란트 생존율 (1차 결과)은 동등 (95.45% vs 92.73%, P=0.391). 즉시식립군이 치주 지수 (치주낭깊이 Probing Depth, PD·mPLI·mSBI), 핑크심미점수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES)·화이트심미점수 (White Esthetic Score, WES), 구강건강관련 삶의 질 (Oral Health Impact Profile-14, OHIP-14), 환자 만족도 (시각상사척도 Visual Analog Scale, VAS) 모든 2차 결과에서 유의하게 우수. 단일기관 중국 연구·1년 추적·변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 미보고는 한계.

## Background

Implant timing classification (Hämmerle): immediate (Type 1, at extraction) vs delayed (Type 3/4, ≥3 months post-extraction). Prior systematic reviews (including [[garcia-sanchez-2022-immediate-vs-delayed-implant-placement-sr-ma]]) documented survival equivalence but lacked robust patient-reported outcome data. This RCT fills that gap using ID-COSM-aligned outcome set.

## Study Design

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Parallel-group RCT, 1:1 allocation |
| Sample | n=220; 110 immediate, 110 delayed |
| Setting | Single hospital, China |
| Period | January 2022 – October 2023 |
| Implant | Straumann Bone Level Tapered (both groups) |
| Follow-up | 1 year post-functional loading |
| Randomisation | Permuted blocks (size 10), sealed opaque envelopes |
| Blinding | Outcome assessors blinded; no patient/clinician blinding |
| Primary outcome | Implant survival at 1 year |
| Secondary outcomes | PD, mPLI, mSBI, PES, WES, OHIP-14, VAS satisfaction |

Inclusion required: single missing tooth, adequate bone (>500 HU, ≥10 mm height, ≥6 mm width by CBCT), no active periodontitis, non-/light smoker (<10 cigarettes/day). Baseline groups comparable on all parameters (age, sex, BMI, tooth location, cause of loss; all P>0.05).

Both groups used identical Straumann implants with insertion torque ≥35 N·cm. Delayed protocol: 3-month socket healing before implant. Immediate protocol: atraumatic extraction → same-session implant; palatal wall primary stability; Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide (Geistlich) for gap management; buccal flap advanced for tension-free closure.

## Results

### Primary Outcome — 1-Year Implant Survival

| Group | Survived | Failed | Survival Rate | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate | 105/110 | 5 | **95.45%** | 89.7–98.5% |
| Delayed | 102/110 | 8 | **92.73%** | 86.2–96.8% |
| **Difference** | — | — | **Not significant** | χ²=0.736, P=0.391 |

Multivariate adjusted OR = 1.82 (95% CI: 0.56–5.91, P=0.321) — timing not independently associated with survival after controlling for age, sex, and tooth region.

### Secondary Outcomes — Peri-implant Health (1 year)

| Index | Immediate | Delayed | Mean difference | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Probing Depth (mm) | 3.16 ± 0.51 | 3.39 ± 0.59 | −0.23 (−0.39 to −0.07) | 0.005 |
| mPLI | 0.95 ± 0.38 | 1.21 ± 0.54 | −0.26 (−0.40 to −0.12) | <0.001 |
| mSBI | 0.85 ± 0.47 | 1.01 ± 0.35 | −0.16 (−0.27 to −0.05) | 0.004 |

Both groups improved significantly from pre-implantation baseline; immediate group showed greater absolute gains on all three indices.

### Secondary Outcomes — Aesthetics (1 year)

| Score | Immediate | Delayed | Difference | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PES total (max 14) | 12.18 ± 1.13 | 11.34 ± 1.30 | +0.84 (0.51–1.17) | <0.001 |
| WES total (max 10) | 7.78 ± 0.99 | 7.23 ± 1.10 | +0.55 (0.28–0.82) | <0.001 |

Immediate group superior in: labial mucosal curvature, mucosal height, soft tissue colour and texture (PES); restoration shape and surface texture (WES). Papilla heights and labial root protrusion did not differ significantly between groups.

### Secondary Outcomes — QoL and Patient Satisfaction (1 year)

| Measure | Immediate | Delayed | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHIP-14 (lower=better) | 3.20 ± 1.33 | 4.15 ± 1.23 | <0.001 |
| VAS attachment level | 8.12 ± 1.05 | 7.45 ± 1.18 | <0.05 |
| VAS colour match | 8.67 ± 0.89 | 8.15 ± 1.12 | <0.05 |
| VAS masticatory function | 8.89 ± 0.94 | 8.23 ± 1.08 | <0.05 |

## Clinical Interpretation

**Survival equivalence** is the key finding for clinical decision-making: under appropriate patient selection (adequate bone volume, non-smoker, single tooth), immediate implantation does not compromise 1-year survival.

**Soft tissue and aesthetic advantage** of immediate placement (PES, mPLI, mSBI) may reflect preservation of native alveolar bone architecture and gingival contour before post-extraction resorption occurs — consistent with the biological rationale for Type 1 placement.

**Patient-centered outcomes** (OHIP-14, VAS) favour immediate placement, likely due to reduced treatment duration, fewer visits, and maintained aesthetics during healing.

**Caveat**: No marginal bone loss (MBL) data reported — a critical peri-implant endpoint. Long-term (≥3 year) data needed to confirm durability. Results from a single Chinese centre; generalizability to diverse ethnic groups, smoking populations, or esthetic zone cases requires caution.

## Limitations

- Single-centre design (China); limited generalizability
- 1-year follow-up only; no marginal bone loss data
- No CONSORT-compliant patient/clinician blinding (inherent to surgical timing RCT)
- Failure reasons and mode for 13 total failures not detailed
- "Overall aesthetics" VAS domain results not clearly separated in reporting
- Restricted to single missing teeth; does not address multiple-tooth or full-arch scenarios
- Light smokers included (<10 cigarettes/day) but subgroup analysis not provided
