## Three-line Summary
Cross-sectional salivary epithelial cell count study (n=17, University of Manitoba) estimating oral mucosal surface layer turnover time and bacterial load in edentulous mouths via Coulter Counter and anaerobic culture.
Surface layer turnover: every 2.7 h; total bacteria in edentulous mouth: ~1.58 × 10⁹; mean bacterial division time: 1.38 h; salivary flow >0.04 mL/min prevents continuous-culture ecology for fast-growers.
Steady-state calculation assumes uniform mucosal surface area; only edentulous mouths studied; dentate sulcular surfaces not included — likely underestimates total bacterial load.

## Summary
Dawes (2003) used drooled saliva from 17 human subjects to calculate the replacement rate of the oral mucosal epithelial surface layer. By combining the rate of epithelial cell loss in saliva (via Coulter Counter) with known oral mucosal surface area and individual epithelial cell dimensions, the study estimated that the outermost cell layer is replaced every **2.7 hours**. Using bacteria-per-epithelial-cell counts and bacterial swallowing rates, the total oral bacterial load in edentulous individuals was estimated at ~1.58 × 10⁹ with a mean inter-division time of 1.38 h. The work establishes that the oral mucosa is one of the most rapidly renewing epithelial surfaces in the human body, substantially faster than skin (epidermal turnover 14–28 days).

## Key Contributions
- Established the first quantitative estimate of oral mucosal surface layer turnover time: **2.7 h** under steady-state conditions
- Demonstrated that the salivary continuous culture framework can be applied to understand oral microbial ecology
- Calculated edentulous oral bacterial load: ~1.58 × 10⁹; mean division time 1.38 h
- Identified conditions under which the oral salivary phase could act as a bacterial chemostat (flow <0.04 mL/min for fast-growing species)
- Provided a quantitative anchor for understanding oral wound re-epithelialization kinetics

## Methodology
Cross-sectional; n=17 adults (bacterial counts: n=10). Drooled saliva collected and processed:
- **Epithelial cell count**: Coulter Counter (cell/mL)
- **Bacteria per cell**: Toluidine Blue staining, light microscopy
- **Bacterial culture**: Blood Agar, anaerobic 5 days, after dilution in Reduced Transport Fluid
- **Calculation**: (oral mucosal surface area / individual cell area) × cells/mL in saliva × swallowing rate = turnover time

## Results
- Oral mucosal surface layer replacement: **every 2.7 h**
- Total bacteria in edentulous mouth: **~1.58 × 10⁹**
- Mean bacterial inter-division time: **1.38 h**
- Chemostat threshold: salivary flow rate <0.04 mL/min (residual vol 0.8 mL; max division rate 3 h⁻¹)
