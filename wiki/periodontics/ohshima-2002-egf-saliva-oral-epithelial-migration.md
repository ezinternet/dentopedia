## One-line Summary
In vitro study showing that physiologic salivary EGF concentrations (1–10 ng/mL) stimulate oral epithelial DNA synthesis via PI3-K/p38-MAPK/MEK pathways and migration via MEK/TK/PI3-K pathways, with the two processes using distinct signaling routes.

## 한줄요약
구강상피세포주(HO-1-N-1) 시험관 연구: 타액 생리 농도 EGF(1–10 ng/mL)가 증식(PI3-K·p38-MAPK·MEK)과 이동(MEK·TK·PI3-K)을 각각 독립 신호체계로 촉진하며, 두 과정이 서로 다른 경로를 사용함.

## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (oral buccal carcinoma cell line HO-1-N-1, Nihon University) examining physiologic salivary EGF concentrations (1–10 ng/mL) on oral epithelial proliferation and migration pathways.
EGF dose-dependently stimulated DNA synthesis at 1–10 ng/mL; proliferation blocked by PI3-K, p38-MAPK, and MEK inhibitors; migration inhibited most by MEK (→30%), then TK (→40%), then PI3-K (→60%) — demonstrating distinct signaling routes for proliferation vs. migration.
Carcinoma-derived cell line limits generalizability to normal oral mucosa; in vitro only; salivary EGF bioavailability in vivo uncharacterized.

## 세줄요약
시험관 연구(구강 협점막 암종 유래 세포주 HO-1-N-1, 니혼대학교): 타액 생리 농도 EGF(1–10 ng/mL)의 구강상피 증식·이동 신호 경로 탐색.
EGF 1–10 ng/mL 농도 의존적 DNA 합성 촉진; 증식은 PI3-K·p38-MAPK·MEK 억제로 저하; 이동은 MEK 억제(30%로 감소), TK 억제(40%), PI3-K 억제(60%) — 증식·이동이 별개 신호 경로 사용.
암종 유래 세포주는 정상 점막 대표성 한계; 체외 실험; 생체 내 EGF 생물학적 이용률 불확실.

## Summary
Ohshima et al. (2002) used the HO-1-N-1 oral epithelial cell line to elucidate how salivary EGF—at concentrations found in normal human saliva (1–10 ng/mL)—drives wound healing responses. EGF stimulated DNA synthesis dose-dependently in this range; selective inhibition of PI3-K (wortmannin), p38 MAPK (SB203580), or MEK (PD98059) each significantly suppressed proliferation. Cell migration was also accelerated by 1–10 ng/mL EGF. Crucially, the relative contributions of pathways to migration differed: MEK inhibition was most potent (migration reduced to 30%), followed by tyrosine kinase inhibition (40%), then PI3-K and dexamethasone (each 60%). The finding that proliferation and migration share some but not all signaling nodes implies that therapeutic manipulation of one process need not necessarily alter the other.

## Key Contributions
- First mechanistic dissection of EGF signaling specifically at physiologic salivary concentrations in oral epithelium
- Established that 1–10 ng/mL EGF is functionally sufficient for both proliferative and migratory responses
- Demonstrated distinct pathway weighting for proliferation vs. migration (MEK most critical for migration; PI3-K, p38 also needed for proliferation)
- Proposed that salivary EGF serves as an endogenous injury-response mediator for oral wound repair
- Provided pathway specificity data relevant to pharmacologic wound-healing interventions

## Methodology
- Cell line: HO-1-N-1 (oral buccal mucosa squamous cell carcinoma-derived epithelial line)
- EGF doses: 1, 5, 10 ng/mL (physiologic salivary range)
- Proliferation: [³H]-thymidine DNA incorporation assay
- Migration: quantified with selective kinase inhibitors added to EGF-supplemented medium
- Inhibitors: wortmannin (PI3-K), SB203580 (p38 MAPK), PD98059 (MEK), herbimycin A (TK), dexamethasone

## Results
- DNA synthesis: dose-dependent stimulation at 1–10 ng/mL EGF
- Proliferation inhibition: PI3-K ↓ (wortmannin), p38 MAPK ↓ (SB203580), MEK ↓ (PD98059) — all significant
- Migration at 1–10 ng/mL EGF, with inhibitors:
  - PD98059 (MEK): migration → **30%** of control
  - Herbimycin A (TK): migration → **40%**
  - Wortmannin (PI3-K): migration → **60%**
  - Dexamethasone: migration → **60%**
- Conclusion: proliferation and migration are mediated by overlapping but distinct EGF signaling pathways
