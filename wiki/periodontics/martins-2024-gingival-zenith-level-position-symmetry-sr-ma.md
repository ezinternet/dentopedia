## Three-line Summary

PRISMA SR+MA of cross-sectional studies in healthy adults with intact maxillary anterior sextants, assessing gingival zenith (GZ) mesiodistal position, apicocoronal level, and contralateral symmetry (JBI appraisal, GRADE).

GZ was distal / on-axis / mesial in 96% / 3% / 1% of central incisors, 84% / 14% / 1% of lateral incisors, and 43% / 44% / 5% of canines; the lateral-incisor GZ was coronal to the central–canine zenith line in 82%; contralateral level and position were symmetrical.

Distal GZ displacement increases the more anterior the tooth — a reference target for restorative and implant emergence-profile design — but GRADE certainty was very low for every comparison.

GZ 원심/치축/근심 — 중절치 96%/3%/1%, 측절치 84%/14%/1%, 견치 43%/44%/5%; 측절치 GZ는 82%에서 중절치–견치 정점선보다 치관측; 좌우 대칭.

## Summary

The gingival zenith determines where the facial line angles sit in the emergence profile and how the crown's axial inclination reads. This SR+MA pools healthy-dentition cross-sectional data and confirms the textbook pattern: the zenith is distal to the crown's long axis in almost all central incisors, most lateral incisors, and only about half of canines (where it is as often on-axis). The lateral-incisor zenith usually sits slightly coronal to the line joining central and canine zeniths. Left–right symmetry holds. Certainty is very low, so these are design defaults rather than rules.

## Key Contributions

- Pooled prevalence of GZ position categories per tooth type.
- Gradient of distal displacement: central > lateral > canine.
- Lateral GZ coronal to the zenith line in 82%.
- Contralateral symmetry established.

## Methodology

- PVO + PRISMA; cross-sectional studies in healthy adults with complete maxillary anterior sextant.
- JBI analytical cross-sectional checklist; GRADE certainty.
- Study count and pooled n not available in the abstract.

## Results

| Tooth | Distal | On axis | Mesial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Central incisor | 96% | 3% | 1% |
| Lateral incisor | 84% | 14% | 1% |
| Canine | 43% | 44% | 5% |

- Lateral incisor GZ coronal to the zenith line: 82%.
- Contralateral symmetry for level and position.
- GRADE: very low for all comparisons.

**Clinical reading**: when shaping the critical/subcritical contour of an anterior implant crown, place the facial zenith distal to the crown midline for centrals (and usually laterals), near-axial for canines, and keep the lateral zenith slightly coronal to the central–canine line.
