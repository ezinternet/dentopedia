## Three-line Summary

2017 World Workshop consensus (international systematic literature search + Delphi expert process) establishing a new bidimensional classification for periodontitis: Stage I–IV (severity and complexity: CAL, radiographic bone loss, tooth loss, furcation, mobility) and Grade A–C (rate of progression, systemic modifiers — diabetes, smoking — and treatment response).

Stage IV denotes mastication dysfunction from tooth loss attributable to periodontitis; the aggressive periodontitis category was eliminated and absorbed into Stage III/IV grade C; the new system replaces the 1999 chronic/aggressive classification.

The Stage-Grade framework is now the universal clinical language for treatment planning and interdisciplinary communication, with each dimension carrying distinct therapeutic implications — Stages drive surgical/prosthetic complexity and Grade drives systemic risk counseling.

## 세줄요약

2017 치주 세계 워크샵 합의문 (체계적 문헌검색 + Delphi 전문가 합의): 치주염의 이차원 분류 — Stage I–IV(중증도·복잡성: CAL·방사선 골소실·발치 여부·치근이개부·동요도)와 Grade A–C(진행속도·전신 조절 인자 — 당뇨·흡연 — ·치료 반응).

Stage IV는 치주염으로 인한 발치로 저작 기능 장애를 정의; '공격적 치주염' 범주는 폐지돼 Stage III/IV grade C에 흡수; 1999년 만성/공격적 분류 체계 대체.

Stage-Grade 체계는 치료 계획과 다학제 소통의 보편적 임상 언어로, Stage는 외과적·보철적 복잡성을, Grade는 전신 위험도 상담의 근거를 각각 제공함.

## Summary
2017 World Workshop consensus establishing the new Stage (I–IV) and Grade (A–C) classification for periodontitis.

## Key Contributions
- Stage I–IV: severity (CAL, RBL, tooth loss) + complexity (furcation, mobility, vertical bone loss)
- Grade A–C: progression rate, systemic modifiers (diabetes, smoking), treatment response
- Stage IV: mastication compromise; aggressive periodontitis category eliminated

## Methodology
Systematic literature search + international Delphi expert consensus.

## Results
New classification replaces 1999 system. Stage IV: tooth loss attributable to periodontitis + mastication dysfunction. Grading incorporates systemic disease impact.
