## Three-line Summary
Split-mouth RCT (Yonsei University, Korea; n=20 patients with chronic severe periodontitis) comparing flap surgery plus locally delivered minocycline ointment (FM, re-applied at 3 months) against flap surgery alone (FO), with PD, BOP, CAL, recession and plaque index assessed at baseline, 3 and 6 months.
At 6 months FM sites showed greater PD reduction (3.34 ± 0.03 vs 2.62 ± 0.06 mm), BOP reduction (78.01 ± 11.42% vs 50.33 ± 15.01%) and CAL gain (1.88 ± 0.21 vs 1.55 ± 0.13 mm), all p < 0.05.
Authors conclude adjunctive minocycline may benefit surgical protocols; only the abstract was available, so randomization/blinding details, the unusually small PD dispersion, and the 6-month-only follow-up could not be checked.

## Summary
Twenty patients with chronic severe periodontitis were treated in a split-mouth design with flap surgery plus locally delivered minocycline ointment on one side (FM) and flap surgery alone on the other (FO); minocycline was re-applied 3 months after surgery. Both sides improved clinically, but at 6 months the FM sites had a larger PD reduction (3.34 vs 2.62 mm), BOP reduction (78.01% vs 50.33%) and CAL gain (1.88 vs 1.55 mm), p < 0.05. The authors conclude that adjunctive local minocycline may benefit the surgical treatment of chronic severe periodontitis. This is the only minocycline-ointment-with-flap-surgery trial in the wiki; the held minocycline evidence is non-surgical. Split-mouth designs may overstate adjunct effects (per the SR+MA linked below), and the abstract does not describe randomization or blinding.

## Key Contributions
- Extends local minocycline evidence from SRP to flap surgery in chronic severe periodontitis.
- Within-patient comparison with a repeat application at 3 months.
- Reports 6-month gains in PD, BOP and CAL for the minocycline side.

## Methodology
Split-mouth comparative trial (PubMed type: RCT), n=20, chronic severe periodontitis. FM = flap surgery + minocycline ointment (re-applied at 3 months); FO = flap surgery only. PI, PD, BOP, recession and CAL measured at baseline, 3 and 6 months. (Abstract-only.)

## Results
| Outcome (6 months) | FM | FO |
|---|---|---|
| PD reduction | 3.34 ± 0.03 mm | 2.62 ± 0.06 mm |
| BOP reduction | 78.01 ± 11.42% | 50.33 ± 15.01% |
| CAL gain | 1.88 ± 0.21 mm | 1.55 ± 0.13 mm |

Group differences p < 0.05; 3-month values and recession/plaque results are not given in the abstract.
