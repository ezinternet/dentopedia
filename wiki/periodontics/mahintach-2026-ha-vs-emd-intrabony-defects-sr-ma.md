## Summary

Mahintach et al. (Oral Health Prev Dent 2026) conducted a SR+MA comparing hyaluronic acid (HA) versus enamel matrix derivative (EMD) for surgical treatment of intrabony defects, with a non-inferiority framework (margin: 0.5 mm CAL). From 117 screened articles, 3 RCTs met inclusion criteria. HA achieved clinical non-inferiority for CAL gain at 12 months (WMD 0.51 mm, CI −0.08 to 1.11) and for gingival recession (WMD −0.4 mm, I²=0%). However, PPD non-inferiority was not confirmed (WMD 0.71 mm, I²=72.5%), likely reflecting heterogeneity in technique and defect characteristics across studies. The authors conclude that HA may serve as an alternative to EMD for intrabony defects but emphasize the need for further RCTs.

## Key Contributions

- First direct SR+MA comparison of HA vs EMD for intrabony defects
- Non-inferiority design with pre-defined 0.5 mm margin — more rigorous than equivalence testing
- CAL and recession: HA non-inferior; PPD: non-inferiority not established
- Only 3 eligible RCTs — confirms evidence gap for this comparison
- Supports HA as an emerging alternative regenerative adjunct at lower cost than EMD

## Methodology

SR+MA; PRISMA-compliant; databases: PubMed, ScienceDirect, Scopus, Google Scholar, LILACS; inclusion: RCTs comparing HA vs EMD in intrabony defect surgery; outcomes: CAL gain (primary), PPD reduction, gingival recession; random-effects model; WMD + 95% CI; PROSPERO CRD420261301986.

## Results

| Outcome | WMD (HA vs EMD) | 95% CI | I² | Non-inferior? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CAL gain 12 months | 0.51 mm | −0.08 to 1.11 | — | Yes |
| PPD reduction 12 months | 0.71 mm | −0.11 to 1.54 | 72.5% | No |
| Gingival recession 12 months | −0.40 mm | −0.79 to −0.01 | 0% | Yes |

Positive WMD = HA slightly less effective (numerically), but within non-inferiority margin for CAL and recession.
