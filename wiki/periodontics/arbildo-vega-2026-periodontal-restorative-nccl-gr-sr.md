## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420261278151) of 4 parallel RCTs (36–40 patients each, 1–2 y follow-up, all from the Santamaria group in Brazil) comparing CAF+CTG plus NCCL restoration (composite resin or RMGIC) vs CAF+CTG alone for gingival recession associated with non-carious cervical lesions.

No significant difference for GR (MD 0.24 mm, p=0.47), CAL, KTT, KTW, patient/professional esthetics, combined defect height/coverage, RC or complete root coverage (OR 1.09; 80.4% vs 78.6%); restoration significantly improved dentin sensitivity (OR 9.25, 95% CI 2.39–35.77; 92.1% vs 55.3%) but increased PD by 0.47 mm (95% CI 0.14–0.80, p=0.01; still < 3 mm).

GRADE low for all outcomes (very low for professional esthetics) from a single research group — "no difference" is not equivalence; restore the NCCL mainly for DS relief, and pick a color-stable material (nanofilled composite over RMGIC) when esthetics matter.

GR(MD 0.24 mm, p=0.47)·CAL·KTT·KTW·환자/전문가 심미·복합결손 높이/피개율·RC·완전근면피개(CRC, OR 1.09; 80.4% vs 78.6%) 모두 유의차 없음; 상아질 과민(DS)은 복합치료가 유의하게 우수(OR 9.25, 95% CI 2.39–35.77; 92.1% vs 55.3%), 탐침깊이(PD)는 0.47 mm 증가(95% CI 0.14–0.80, p=0.01; 3 mm 미만 유지).

GRADE 전 결과 low(전문가 심미 very low), 단일 연구그룹 — "차이 없음"은 동등성이 아님; NCCL 수복은 주로 DS 완화 목적이며, 심미가 중요하면 색안정성 좋은 수복재(나노필 CR > RMGIC)를 고른다.

## Summary

Gingival recession frequently coexists with a non-carious cervical lesion (a "combined defect", reported in at least half of recession cases). The clinical question is whether the NCCL should be restored as part of root coverage surgery — CAF+CTG over a restored root — or whether the graft alone suffices. This SR+MA updates the earlier Rovai et al. synthesis with RoB 2.0, GRADE and leave-one-out analysis.

Across 12 outcomes, only two differed: **dentin sensitivity** favored the combined approach (OR 9.25), and **probing depth** was 0.47 mm deeper after combined treatment — explained by a long junctional epithelium over the restoration rather than new connective tissue attachment, and remaining within healthy limits (< 3 mm). Root coverage outcomes (GR, RC, CRC, combined defect coverage) and esthetics were not improved or harmed by restoration.

Professional esthetics (MRES) was highly heterogeneous (I² 79%), which the authors attribute to material: RMGI loses color match (up to 44.1% mismatch at 2 years), whereas nanofilled composite integrates better.

## Key Contributions

- Updated SR+MA of the combined GR+NCCL question with GRADE certainty ratings.
- First pooled DS benefit for restoring the NCCL with root coverage (367 more patients per 1,000 free of sensitivity).
- Quantifies the PD cost of subgingival restoration under the flap (+0.47 mm).
- Makes explicit that the whole evidence base is one research group (Santamaria et al.), with two reports from a shared cohort.

## Methodology

- 9 databases incl. grey literature, inception–Dec 2025; RCTs with ≥ 1-year follow-up; healthy adults.
- I = CAF + CTG + composite resin (2 trials) or RMGIC (2 trials); C = CAF + CTG alone.
- RoB 2.0 (κ = 0.95): all "some concerns". Random-effects MD/OR (RevMan 5.3), GRADEpro, leave-one-out; no funnel plot (< 10 studies).
- Canines and premolars only; 18–20 teeth per arm.

## Results

|---|---|---|---|
| GR | MD 0.24 mm (−0.41 to 0.89) | 0.47 | Low |
| PD | **MD 0.47 mm (0.14 to 0.80)**, I² 60% | **0.01** | Low |
| CAL | MD 0.56 mm (−0.18 to 1.30) | 0.14 | Low |
| KTT / KTW | MD 0.13 / −0.04 mm | 0.20 / 0.84 | Low |
| Patient esthetics (VAS) | MD 0.01 | 0.97 | Low |
| Professional esthetics (MRES) | MD −1.11 (−3.44 to 1.22), I² 79% | 0.35 | Very low |
| Dentin sensitivity | **OR 9.25 (2.39–35.77)**; 92.1% vs 55.3% | **<0.01** | Low |
| Combined defect height / coverage | MD 0.23 mm / −6.32% | 0.14 / 0.08 | Low |
| Root coverage | MD 1.61% | 0.61 | Low |
| Complete root coverage | OR 1.09 (0.41–2.91); 80.4% vs 78.6% | 0.87 | Low |

Leave-one-out: PD, MRES and DS were sensitive to individual trials.

## Clinical Takeaway

1. **Restoring the NCCL does not improve root coverage.** GR reduction, RC% and CRC are the same with or without restoration — CAF+CTG does the coverage work.
2. **Restore mainly for dentin sensitivity.** The only clinically meaningful benefit is DS relief (92% vs 55% sensitivity-free), attributed to immediate tubule sealing before the gingiva heals.
3. **Expect slightly deeper sulcus over the restoration** (+0.47 mm, long junctional epithelium) — not periodontal breakdown.
4. **Material matters for esthetics.** Nanofilled composite keeps color better than RMGIC; this inference is narrative (no formal subgroup test).
5. **Shallow recession / minimally invasive preference:** the authors suggest surgery first and deciding on restoration later.
6. **Weak evidence base:** 3 independent cohorts, one group, canines/premolars, ≤ 2 years — treat the conclusions as provisional.
