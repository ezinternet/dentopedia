## Three-line Summary

(1) This is a domestic systematic literature review (n=16 studies) examining the effect of oral hygiene instruction (OHI) as an adjunct to professional and self-performed plaque control/removal on periodontal parameters in Korean adults, children, and elderly populations, spanning studies published from 1993 to 2012.

(2) Repeated OHI using disclosing agents significantly reduced plaque indices (O'Leary plaque index, PHP index, OHI-S index) across all age groups; the most substantial reductions were observed with 4 repeated instruction sessions (up to 74% improvement in PHP index, p<0.001) and the effect was maintained with regular reinforcement visits, though scores regressed after cessation of education.

(3) Combining professional mechanical plaque removal (scaling, root planing) with OHI is more effective than either intervention alone; routine, repeated OHI with disclosing agents is recommended to enhance oral hygiene and reduce periodontal disease burden, but high-quality domestic studies with standardized methodologies are needed to inform dental health insurance policy.

## 세줄요약

(1) 본 연구는 국내에서 발표된 16편의 문헌을 체계적으로 고찰하여, 한국 성인·아동·노인을 대상으로 구강위생교습(Oral Hygiene Instruction, OHI)이 치면세균막관리(dental plaque control) 및 치주지수 개선에 미치는 영향을 분석하였다.

(2) 치면착색제(disclosing agent)를 활용한 반복적 OHI는 모든 연령층에서 치면세균막지수(O'Leary index, PHP index, OHI-S index)를 유의하게 감소시켰으며, 4회 반복 교육 시 최대 74%의 개선효과를 보였다(p<0.001). 교육 종료 후 정기 방문 시 점수는 다소 회귀되었으나 교육 전보다는 유의하게 개선된 상태를 유지하였다.

(3) 전문가 치면세마(professional mechanical plaque removal)와 OHI를 병행하는 것이 단독 시행보다 효과적이며, 정기적이고 반복적인 OHI 시행이 구강위생 향상에 필요하다. 향후 국내 치석제거 건강보험 정책 수립을 위한 근거 중심의 고품질 연구가 요구된다.

## Summary

This systematic review synthesized 16 domestic Korean studies published between 1993 and 2012 evaluating the effectiveness of oral hygiene instruction (OHI) combined with professional and self-performed plaque control/removal on periodontal parameters. Studies included children (preschool, elementary), adults (including workers, patients with chronic diseases), and elderly (nursing home) populations; sample sizes ranged from 27 to 244. The review found that repeated OHI using disclosing agents significantly improved plaque indices (O'Leary index, PHP index, OHI-S index) across all age groups, with 4 repeated sessions yielding the greatest reductions (up to 74% improvement, p<0.001). Combining professional mechanical plaque removal (scaling, root planing) with OHI was more effective than either intervention alone, and regular reinforcement visits were necessary to maintain improvements. Disclosing agent-based individual toothbrushing instruction was more effective than oral lectures or jaw model-based instruction alone (PHP improvement +24.0 points vs +2.8 points, p<0.001). The authors concluded that routine, repeated OHI with disclosing agents is essential for plaque control and periodontal health, but high-quality studies with standardized methodologies are needed to inform evidence-based dental health insurance policy in Korea.

## Key Contributions

- Systematic review of domestic Korean literature (n=16 studies) on plaque control and OHI, covering diverse populations (children, adults, elderly).
- Repeated OHI with disclosing agents significantly improves plaque indices; 4 repeated sessions provides optimal benefit (up to 74% reduction, p<0.001).
- Combining professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR: scaling, root planing) with OHI yields superior outcomes to PMPR alone.
- Disclosing agent-based individual toothbrushing instruction is more effective than oral lecture or model-based instruction alone.
- Maintenance of improved oral hygiene requires regular reinforcement visits; scores regress after education cessation.
- The review highlights the need for high-quality domestic evidence to support dental health insurance policy (e.g., scaling coverage).

## Methodology

This systematic literature review used a narrative synthesis approach, searching five Korean databases (KoreaMed, KMbase, KISS, NDSL, ScienceON) from inception to May 2013, supplemented by hand-searching of key Korean dental journals. Inclusion criteria were original research articles published in Korean academic journals evaluating plaque control outcomes after OHI or oral health education (OHE), including experimental and observational designs with or without gingivitis/periodontitis. Exclusion criteria included abstract-only publications, simple questionnaire surveys, and studies with other interventions (antibiotics, chlorhexidine, flossing, dietary interventions, implants, denture care, etc.). Sixteen domestic studies were included, with sample sizes ranging from 27 to 244 participants. Outcomes included plaque indices (O'Leary, PHP, OHI-S, Quigly-Hein), gingival bleeding, pocket depth, clinical attachment loss, tooth mobility, and halitosis (VSC). No formal risk-of-bias assessment (ROB2, NOS, AMSTAR) was reported; publication bias was considered due to the exclusion of grey literature. No meta-analysis was performed due to heterogeneity in populations, interventions, and outcome measures.
