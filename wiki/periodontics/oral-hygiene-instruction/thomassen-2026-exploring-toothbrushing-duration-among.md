## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42024565180; MEDLINE-PubMed + Cochrane-CENTRAL to Jan 2025) of timed single-brushing exercises in uninstructed healthy adults: 12 observational studies, 16 evaluations, 1246 participants (14 MTB evaluations, 2 PTB evaluations).

Weighted mean brushing time was 96.8 s overall (95% CI 77.10-116.50); manual toothbrush (MTB) 87.3 s (CI 68.50-106.10) vs powered toothbrush (PTB) 151.1 s (CI 142.37-159.83; non-overlapping CIs); MTB duration rose with publication year (R2 = 0.522); GRADE certainty moderate for MTB, limited for PTB.

Uninstructed adults brush manually for ~1.5 min, short of the 2-min guideline, while PTB users exceed 2 min; limits: observational lab-timed studies (Hawthorne effect), mostly European, 1978-2018 data, only 2 PTB evaluations, no formal meta-analysis or plaque outcome.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (PROSPERO CRD42024565180; MEDLINE-PubMed·Cochrane-CENTRAL, 2025년 1월까지)로, 지도받지 않은 건강한 성인의 1회 칫솔질 시간을 측정한 관찰연구 12편·16개 평가·1246명 (수동칫솔 14건, 전동칫솔 2건)을 분석함.

가중평균 칫솔질 시간은 전체 96.8초 (95% 신뢰구간 77.10-116.50), 수동칫솔 (Manual Toothbrush, MTB) 87.3초 vs 전동칫솔 (Powered Toothbrush, PTB) 151.1초 (신뢰구간 비중첩)였고, 수동칫솔 시간은 출판연도에 따라 증가 (R2 = 0.522); GRADE 확실성은 수동칫솔 중등도, 전동칫솔 제한적.

지도받지 않은 성인은 수동칫솔로 약 1.5분 닦아 2분 권고에 못 미치고 전동칫솔 사용자는 2분을 넘김; 한계: 실험실 타이머 측정 관찰연구 (호손 효과), 대부분 유럽, 1978-2018년 자료, 전동칫솔 평가 2건뿐, 정식 메타분석·플라크 결과 없음.

## Summary
This systematic review pooled timed single-brushing exercises in uninstructed healthy adults from 12 observational studies (16 evaluations, 1246 participants). Manual toothbrush users averaged 87.3 s and powered toothbrush users 151.1 s, against a 2-minute guideline. Manual brushing time increased with publication year (R2 = 0.522). Certainty was moderate for MTB and limited for PTB. It measures duration only, not plaque or gingival outcomes, and the values come from observed research settings.

## Key Contributions
- Measured, not self-reported, brushing duration split by MTB vs PTB.
- Publication-year trend in MTB duration (R2 = 0.522).
- GRADE profile with moderate certainty for ~1.5 min MTB duration.

## Methodology
SR per Cochrane Handbook, MOOSE, SWiM (PROSPERO CRD42024565180); MEDLINE-PubMed and Cochrane-CENTRAL to Jan 22, 2025; 3287 records to 12 studies; ROBINS-I (moderate risk of bias); n-weighted mean with 95% CI and simple regression, no formal meta-analysis model. Self-reported data and dental professionals/students excluded.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Overall (16 evaluations, n = 1246) | 96.8 s (95% CI 77.10-116.50) |
| MTB (14 evaluations, n = 1060) | 87.3 s (CI 68.50-106.10) |
| PTB (2 evaluations, n = 186) | 151.1 s (CI 142.37-159.83); abstract states 143 s |
| Range of study means | 33-158 s |
| MTB vs publication year | positive trend, R2 = 0.522 |
| GRADE | MTB moderate; PTB limited |

The paper's discussion cites outside evidence (not held as separate pages here) that 2 min removes more plaque than 1 min with no added benefit at 3 min, and that FDI stresses effective cleaning over a fixed time. Read this page as evidence on how long adults actually brush, not on the optimal duration.
