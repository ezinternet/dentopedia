## Three-line Summary

Parallel-group RCT (n=42 school children aged 6–12 years with gingivitis symptoms and a baseline Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein plaque score >1.5; 21 per group; Yonsei University Dental Hospital and two public health centers, Seoul, Korea) comparing toothbrushing instruction (TBI) with a smart toothbrush and smart mirror (STM) system giving real-time voice feedback from 3D motion tracking versus conventional verbal TBI, with plaque scored at baseline, immediately after TBI (day 0), 1 week and 1 month.

Whole-mouth plaque fell 40.5% with STM versus 40.57% with conventional TBI (mean index 3.31 to 1.88 vs 3.21 to 1.72 at 1 month; time effect p<0.001, no group effect p=0.44; buccal 46.69% vs 43.83%, lingual 31.05% vs 33.89%); the maxillary first molar buccal sub-analysis favored STM numerically but did not reach significance.

STM performed comparably to verbal TBI, not better, so it is a possible alternative rather than an upgrade; limitations are short follow-up, STM used only at the study visit (not at home), small sample, retrospective trial registration, and no gingivitis, caries or socioeconomic data.

## 세줄요약

병행군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 6–12세 학령기 아동 42명, 군당 21명; 치은염 증상 및 기저 터레스키 변형 퀴글리-하인 치태지수 (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein index) >1.5; 연세대학교 치과병원·서울 보건소 2곳): 3차원 동작 인식 실시간 음성 피드백 스마트 칫솔·스마트 거울 (Smart Toothbrush and Smart Mirror, STM) 시스템 칫솔질 교육 (Toothbrushing Instruction, TBI) 대 구두 교육 TBI 비교, 기저·교육 직후 (day 0)·1주·1개월 치태 평가.

전악 치태 감소율은 STM 40.5% 대 구두 TBI 40.57%로 동등 (1개월 지수 3.31에서 1.88 vs 3.21에서 1.72; 시간 효과 p<0.001, 군 효과 p=0.44; 협면 46.69% vs 43.83%, 설면 31.05% vs 33.89%); 상악 제1대구치 협면 하위분석은 STM이 수치상 우세했으나 유의하지 않음.

STM은 구두 TBI보다 우월하지 않고 동등한 수준이라 대체 수단 가능성만 시사; 제한점은 짧은 추적기간, STM을 연구 내원 시에만 사용 (가정 미사용), 소규모 표본, 후향적 임상시험 등록, 치은염·우식·사회경제적 자료 미수집.

## Summary

This parallel-group RCT (BMC Oral Health 2022;22:626) compared a computer-assisted toothbrushing instruction system, the smart toothbrush and smart mirror (STM), with conventional verbal instruction in 42 Korean school children aged 6–12 with gingivitis symptoms. Plaque (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein index) fell by 40.5% with STM and 40.57% with conventional TBI, with a significant time effect (p<0.001) and no group difference (p=0.44). Buccal and lingual reductions also did not differ between groups (p=0.78, p=0.25), and the maxillary first molar buccal surface favored STM only numerically. STM can be considered a comparable alternative to chairside verbal TBI in children, but it was used only at the study visits and followed for one month, so it is not shown to be better or to change gingival or caries outcomes.

## Key Contributions

- Pediatric RCT of a 3D-motion-tracking feedback instruction system versus verbal TBI, with plaque followed to 1 month.
- Equivalent whole-mouth plaque reduction (40.5% vs 40.57%); no evidence that the technology outperforms hygienist instruction.
- Region-level data (buccal, lingual, maxillary first molar buccal) that show where feedback might matter but were not significant.

## Methodology

- **Design**: parallel RCT, coin-flip randomization, blinded examiner (second dental hygienist); CONSORT; retrospectively registered (NCT04627324).
- **Participants**: 42 analyzed (21 per group) of 45 enrolled; age 6–12 (mean 9.64, SD 1.96); gingivitis symptom plus baseline plaque score >1.5; exclusions rampant caries, orthodontic appliances, immunocompromise.
- **Intervention**: STM (smart mirror plus sensor holder; 16 zones; voice feedback; rolling method; 10 s per zone) used at the visit only vs conventional verbal TBI by the same hygienist; identical toothpaste and brushes; both told to brush 3 min three times daily.
- **Outcomes**: primary at least 15% Q-H plaque reduction; secondary whole-mouth, buccal, lingual, maxillary first molar buccal indices at baseline, day 0, 1 week, 1 month; linear mixed model / Mann-Whitney U.
