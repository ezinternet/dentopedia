## Three-line Summary

Case report (n=1, adolescent male) using two-tone plaque-disclosing solution with rolling and Bass brushing instructions and serial follow-up to track self-performed plaque control.

Plaque index (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein) improved from 2.0 (baseline) to 1.8 (12 h), 1.5 (24 h), 1.3 (1 week), 0.8 (4 weeks), and gingival index (Loe and Silness) improved from 2.5 to 2.3, 2.0, 1.9, 1.0 over the same timepoints.

Visual feedback with PDS + individualized brushing reinforcement supported progressive improvement in self-performed plaque control over 4 weeks; long-term maintenance was reported at 6 months by phone.

## Summary

Kamra et al. (Cureus, 2024) reported a single-case experience of using a two-tone plaque-disclosing solution (DPI Alphaplac; old plaque blue, new plaque pink) to enhance oral hygiene instruction in a 17-year-old male with chronic localized gingivitis. After baseline recording of Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein plaque index (2.0) and Loe and Silness gingival index (2.5), rolling and Bass brushing techniques were demonstrated, and a soft-bristled toothbrush with triclosan + sodium monofluorophosphate toothpaste was prescribed. The patient was followed at 12 hours, 24 hours, 1 week, and 4 weeks with repeated PDS application, index recording, and brushing reinforcement. Both indices showed progressive reduction to 0.8 and 1.0 respectively at 4 weeks. A 6-month phone follow-up confirmed continued twice-daily brushing with the instructed technique. The case illustrates how visual feedback from plaque disclosure, combined with individualized demonstration and repeated reinforcement, can support stepwise improvement in self-performed plaque control.

## Key Contributions

- Documented serial plaque and gingival index values at five timepoints (baseline, 12 h, 24 h, 1 week, 4 weeks) under PDS-guided OHI.
- Demonstrated progressive improvement in self-performed plaque control through visual feedback (new vs old plaque differentiation) plus technique correction.
- Reported sustained adherence (twice-daily brushing with instructed technique) at 6-month phone follow-up.

## Methodology

- **Patient**: 17-year-old male with chronic localized gingivitis; no caries, no periodontal pockets; bleeding on probing.
- **Disclosing**: Two-tone plaque-disclosing solution (DPI Alphaplac) — old plaque stained blue, new plaque stained pink.
- **Indices**: Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein plaque index (0-5), Loe and Silness gingival index (0-3).
- **Intervention**: Oral hygiene instruction with rolling and Bass brushing techniques demonstrated; soft-bristled toothbrush, triclosan + sodium monofluorophosphate toothpaste. Brushing pattern observed and corrected at each visit.
- **Follow-up schedule**: Baseline, 12 hours, 24 hours, 1 week, 4 weeks (clinical indices and photographs). 6-month phone call for behavioral follow-up.

## Results

| Timepoint | Plaque index (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein) | Gingival index (Loe and Silness) |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 2.0 | 2.5 |
| 12 hours | 1.8 | 2.3 |
| 24 hours | 1.5 | 2.0 |
| 1 week | 1.3 | 1.9 |
| 4 weeks | 0.8 | 1.0 |

- Progressive reduction observed across both indices over 4 weeks.
- At 6 months (phone follow-up), patient continued brushing twice daily with the instructed technique.
