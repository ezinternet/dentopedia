## Three-line Summary

Single-blind, two-arm RCT (n=30 healthy adults aged 20-39, 15 per arm, Seoul National University, January 2025) comparing about 5 minutes of immersive VR toothbrushing instruction (Meta Quest 3, Bass-method curriculum with a virtual mirror) against one-on-one hygienist instruction using plaque-disclosing agent, all participants receiving professional Airflow cleaning before a 2-week home period.

Mean plaque index (PI, Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein) fell by 0.44 (SD 0.48) in the VR group vs 0.07 (SD 0.26) in controls from baseline to 2 weeks (p=0.012); bleeding on probing was unchanged in both arms (0.05 and 0.04 to same values, p=0.785); attitude (p=0.008) and behavior (p=0.004) scores rose more in VR, knowledge did not (p=0.583); no dizziness was reported.

Promising but preliminary: tiny sample, 2-week follow-up, unblinded examiners, retrospective trial registration, and baseline attitude/behavior scores were lower in the VR arm (behavior 2.53 vs 3.87), so the KAB gains partly reflect catch-up; follow-up behavior scores were nearly identical (4.73 vs 4.53).

## 세줄요약

단일맹검 2군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 건강 성인 20-39세 n=30, 군당 15명, 서울대 치의학대학원, 2025년 1월): 몰입형 가상현실 (Virtual Reality, VR) 칫솔질 교육 (Meta Quest 3, 가상 거울을 활용한 바스법 커리큘럼, 약 5분)을 치면세균막 착색제 (plaque-disclosing agent)를 이용한 치과위생사 1:1 교육과 비교했고, 전원 Airflow 전문가 치면세정 후 2주간 자가 관리.

치면세균막 지수 (Plaque Index, PI)의 기저 대비 2주 감소는 VR군 0.44 (SD 0.48) vs 대조군 0.07 (SD 0.26) (p=0.012); 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)은 양군 모두 변화 없음 (p=0.785); 태도 (p=0.008)·행동 (p=0.004) 점수는 VR군에서 더 증가, 지식은 무변화 (p=0.583); 어지럼증 보고 없음.

유망하나 예비 단계: 표본이 작고 추적 2주, 검사자 비눈가림, 임상시험 사후 등록이며, VR군의 기저 태도·행동 점수가 더 낮아 (행동 2.53 vs 3.87) KAB 개선은 일부 따라잡기 효과일 수 있음 (추적 시 행동 4.73 vs 4.53으로 거의 동일).

## Summary

This single-blind RCT at Seoul National University compared an immersive VR toothbrushing curriculum (Meta Quest 3; Bass technique, posterior molars, brushing sequence, and a virtual-mirror plaque-removal simulation; about 5 minutes) with one-on-one hygienist instruction using a plaque-disclosing agent in 30 healthy adults aged 20-39. After professional Airflow cleaning, participants brushed on their own for 2 weeks. The VR group's mean plaque index reduction from baseline was 0.44 versus 0.07 in controls (p=0.012), while bleeding on probing stayed flat in both arms. Attitude and behavior scores improved more with VR, but the VR arm started lower and follow-up behavior scores were nearly equal (4.73 vs 4.53). With n=30, 2-week follow-up, unblinded examiners and retrospective registration, this is preliminary evidence that interactive VR is at least not inferior to, and possibly better than, disclosing-agent instruction for short-term plaque control.

## Key Contributions
- Active-comparator design: VR versus hygienist instruction with plaque-disclosing agent, a clinically standard method, rather than no-intervention or verbal-only controls.
- Reports objective clinical indices (PI, BOP) together with KAB scores in healthy adults after VR toothbrushing education.
- Describes an interactive (not passive-video) VR curriculum with real-time feedback (plaque gauge falling only with correct 45-degree Bass technique).
- No dizziness or adverse events; high usability (SUS Cronbach alpha 0.91).

## Methodology
- Single-blind (participants only), two-arm parallel RCT, n=30 (15/15), Excel 1:1 randomization; CONSORT 2010; power analysis N=28 (effect size 0.25, 80% power).
- Eligibility: age 20-39, at least 24 natural teeth, no electric toothbrush, no orthodontic appliances, no uncontrolled systemic disease.
- Both arms: about 5 min instruction, brushing without mirror, then Airflow professional cleaning; standardized soft manual brush and 996-ppm fluoride toothpaste; 2 weeks home use with no other aids or dental visits.
- Outcomes: PI (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein, 6 sites/tooth) at baseline, post-education and 2 weeks; BOP at baseline and 2 weeks; KAB questionnaire; DHI and SUS in the VR group.
- Analysis: t-tests, chi-square, repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni.

## Results
| Outcome | Control (n=15) | VR (n=15) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| PI baseline | 2.94 (0.25) | 2.92 (0.34) | - |
| PI after education | 2.34 (0.26) | 2.12 (0.35) | - |
| PI at 2 weeks | 2.87 (0.38) | 2.47 (0.58) | - |
| PI change, baseline to 2 wk | 0.07 (0.26) | 0.44 (0.48) | 0.012 |
| BOP baseline to 2 wk | 0.04 to 0.04 | 0.05 to 0.05 | 0.785 |
| Knowledge | 7.00 to 7.40 | 6.90 to 7.53 | 0.583 |
| Attitude | 37.70 to 38.30 | 35.40 to 39.06 | 0.008 |
| Behavior | 3.87 to 4.53 | 2.53 to 4.73 | 0.004 |

- The Discussion text quotes p=0.024 (PI) and p<0.001 (behavior); the Results tables give 0.012 and 0.004, which are used here.
- Immediate post-education PI improved in both arms; by 2 weeks PI had rebounded (more in controls, 2.87 vs baseline 2.94).
- Caveat on KAB: baseline attitude and behavior were lower in the VR arm, follow-up values were close, and knowledge had a ceiling (about 7 of 8 at baseline).
