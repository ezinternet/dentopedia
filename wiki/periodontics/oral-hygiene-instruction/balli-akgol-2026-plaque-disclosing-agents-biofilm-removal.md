## Three-line Summary

Single-center parallel-group RCT (1:1, NCT06080672; n=124 fourth-year dental students, Istanbul Medipol University) comparing Modified Bass brushing plus plaque-disclosing agent (Mira 2-tone) versus standardized instruction alone, with primary outcome ΔPI baseline→week 1.

Primary outcome between-group difference in ΔPI baseline→week 1 was not significant (0.09, 95% CI −0.05 to 0.24); ΔPI post-brushing, week 1, month 1 and ΔGI week 1, month 1 also showed no significant between-group differences, though both arms improved within-group (Friedman p<0.001).

In high-baseline-proficiency dental students, adding a plaque-disclosing agent conferred no measurable advantage over instruction alone (ceiling effect); benefit likely confined to lay populations with lower oral-health literacy.

## Summary

This single-center parallel-group RCT (NCT06080672, n=124) evaluated whether adding a plaque-disclosing agent (PDA; Mira 2-tone) before Modified-Bass brushing improves Plaque Index (PI) and Gingival Index (GI) compared with standardized instruction alone. The prespecified primary outcome was the between-group difference in ΔPI (baseline→week 1). Both groups improved significantly within-group; the primary outcome showed **no significant between-group difference** in ΔPI (baseline→week 1: 0.09, 95% CI −0.05 to 0.24). Secondary outcomes (ΔPI post-brushing, week 1, month 1; ΔGI week 1, month 1) also showed no significant between-group differences. The authors reject the hypothesis that PDA significantly enhances plaque removal or oral health outcomes in this population. They interpret the null result as a **ceiling effect** (high-baseline oral-hygiene proficiency among fourth-year dental students), suggesting PDA may be more informative/beneficial for lay populations with lower oral-health literacy.

## Key Contributions

- **Negative primary result with reported effect sizes**: quantifies the null between-group difference in ΔPI (baseline→week 1) as 0.09 (95% CI −0.05 to 0.24) and other ΔPI/ΔGI differences as small and non-significant.
- **Covers the "add vs not add" axis**: complementary to [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/sooampon-2026-dc-red-33-erythrosine-plaque-disclosure-rct]] (which compares dye choices) by testing whether adding PDA yields incremental benefit at all.
- **Identifies ceiling effect**: shows that for already-proficient users (dental students), visualization alone does not translate to measurable short-term differences in PI/GI.
- **Reasoned interpretation**: discusses Hawthorne effect, short 1-month horizon, and redirects utility to lower-literacy populations.
- **Transparent limitations**: discloses protocol deviation (OHI-S not collected), residual detection bias risk, and baseline imbalance handled with ANCOVA.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-center, parallel-group RCT (1:1), prospective. Istanbul Medipol University; October 2023 – January 2024.
- **Sample**: 124 fourth-year dental students (20–30 y) from Preventive Dentistry course; convenience sampling. 204 screened, 80 excluded (smoking, orthodontics, pregnancy/breastfeeding, declined); no losses; all analyzed (n=62 each).
- **Randomization**: computer-generated sequence (GraphPad QuickCalcs); SNOSE by independent researcher; allocation opened immediately before intervention by study personnel.
- **Blinding**: outcome assessor (periodontist) blinded; measurements taken only after triple water rinsing to remove dye; no access to allocation list.
- **Intervention**: Both groups received identical standardized Modified-Bass instruction (video + model), instructed to brush twice daily (~2 min), perform interdental cleaning once daily; same toothbrush and toothpaste supplied. Test group additionally stained with **Mira 2-tone Solution (Hager & Werken, Duisburg, Germany)** before brushing and stained areas shown in a mirror (no home PDA).
- **Outcomes**: **Primary**: ΔPI baseline→week 1. **Secondary**: ΔPI baseline→post-brushing, ΔPI baseline→month 1, ΔGI baseline→week 1, ΔGI baseline→month 1. Indices measured with Williams probe on 4 surfaces per tooth (excluding third molars); DMFT collected.
- **Analysis**: Mann–Whitney U / Student t for between-group, Friedman + Bonferroni-adjusted Wilcoxon for within-group; univariate ANCOVA (group, with sex and baseline gum bleeding as covariates) via EMMEANS; p<0.05. Power calculation: 124, assuming 70%→90% plaque removal, α 0.05, power 80%.

## Results

- **Baseline characteristics**: mean age 23.13 ± 1.47 y (55M/69F); groups comparable except baseline self-reported gum bleeding higher in test arm (37.1% vs 16.1%, p=0.015). Baseline GI/PI/DMFT not meaningfully different.
- **Primary endpoint — ΔPI (baseline→week 1)**: control +0.04 ± 0.38 (0.03), test −0.05 ± 0.45 (−0.02); between-group difference **0.09, 95% CI −0.05 to 0.24** (non-significant).
- **ΔGI (baseline→week 1)**: control −0.40 ± 0.48 (−0.46), test −0.44 ± 0.50 (−0.37); between-group difference **0.04, 95% CI −0.14 to 0.21** (non-significant).
- **ΔPI (baseline→post-brushing)**: small, between-group NS; within-group significant PI drop post-brushing in both arms (p<0.01).
- **ΔPI/ΔGI (baseline→month 1)**: between-group NS; GI partially rebounded at month 1 but remained below baseline in both groups; PI rose toward baseline levels by month 1.
- **Index scores at key points**: PI week 1 (0.63 ± 0.43 vs 0.66 ± 0.73; p=0.595), GI week 1 (0.55 ± 0.49 vs 0.38 ± 0.37; p=0.053); all between-group score comparisons non-significant.
- **Adjusted comparisons (ANCOVA)**: test arm had lower adjusted GI at baseline (p=0.047) and week 1 (p=0.009), but not at month 1 (p=0.142); adjusted PI differences at no time point significant (all p>0.05).
- **Within-group**: significant improvements across time in both groups (Friedman p<0.001).
