## Three-line Summary

Randomized clinical trial (Japan; sample size not reported in the abstract) comparing novel OHI, guided by a plaque control record restricted to sites with probing depth (PD) >=4 mm (agPCR, calculated by a dedicated app), against conventional OHI guided by O'Leary's PCR; PD, bleeding on probing (BOP) and periodontal inflamed surface area (PISA) were recorded at baseline and 5-6 months.

PISA converged to a lower value after instruction in the novel OHI group, and the improvement rate of PISA correlated positively with agPCR in the novel OHI group but showed no correlation in the conventional OHI group; the abstract reports no numeric effect sizes, p-values or n.

Directing instruction to deep-pocket plaque sites may improve inflammation control over whole-mouth PCR-based OHI, but the abstract-only evidence (no numbers, no sample size, no blinding details) is insufficient to quantify the benefit.

## 세줄요약

무작위 임상시험(Randomized Clinical Trial, 일본, 표본 수는 초록에 미기재): 탐침 깊이(Probing Depth, PD) 4 mm 이상 부위의 착색 치태만 세는 변형 치태조절기록(agPCR, 전용 앱으로 산출)으로 지도하는 새 구강위생교육(Oral Hygiene Instruction, OHI)과 O'Leary 치태조절기록(Plaque Control Record, PCR) 기반 기존 OHI를 비교했고, PD·탐침 시 출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP)·치주염증표면적(Periodontal Inflamed Surface Area, PISA)을 기저선과 5~6개월 시점에 측정.

새 OHI군에서 교육 후 PISA가 더 낮은 값으로 수렴했고, PISA 개선율과 agPCR이 새 OHI군에서만 양의 상관을 보였다(기존 OHI군은 무상관); 초록에는 수치 효과크기·p값·n이 없음.

전체 구강 PCR 대신 깊은 치주낭 부위의 치태에 초점을 둔 교육이 염증 조절에 더 효과적일 수 있으나, 초록만으로는 수치·표본수·눈가림 정보가 없어 효과 크기를 정량화할 수 없다.

## Summary

This randomized clinical trial from Aichi Gakuin University tested whether directing oral hygiene instruction at plaque on deep-pocket sites (PD >=4 mm) works better than instruction based on O'Leary's whole-mouth plaque control record. The authors argue that stained areas on a plaque chart do not always coincide with deep pockets, so conventional PCR-based instruction can miss the target area. PD, BOP and PISA were measured at baseline and 5-6 months. PISA converged to a lower value after instruction in the novel OHI group, and the PISA improvement rate correlated positively with agPCR only in that group. The evidence here comes from the abstract only; sample size and numeric effects were not retrievable.

## Key Contributions
- Proposes agPCR: a plaque control record counting only plaque-stained surfaces at sites with PD >=4 mm, to avoid 'inattentional blindness' (instruction drifting to stained areas that do not coincide with deep pockets).
- Provides a free application that calculates the agPCR automatically (per the abstract's keywords).
- Reports that the improvement rate of PISA correlated positively with agPCR only in the novel OHI group.

## Methodology
- Randomized clinical trial, two parallel groups: novel OHI (agPCR-based) vs conventional OHI (O'Leary PCR-based).
- Outcomes: PD, BOP and PISA at baseline and 5-6 months later.
- Sample size, randomization/blinding details, inclusion criteria and adjunct treatment: not reported in the abstract.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Effect on PISA | Lower converged PISA after instruction in novel OHI group; no numbers in abstract |
| PISA improvement vs agPCR | Positive correlation (novel OHI); no correlation (conventional OHI) |

Limitations: Abstract-only: no n, effect sizes, confidence intervals or p-values retrievable. Authors' conclusion rests on approximation curves and a correlation; between-group numeric differences are not reported in the abstract. Single-country (Japan) trial; short follow-up (5-6 months); generalizability not assessed.
