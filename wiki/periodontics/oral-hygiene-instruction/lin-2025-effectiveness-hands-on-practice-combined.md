## Three-line Summary

Single-centre RCT (Xiamen, China; n=120 randomized, 113 analysed [57 vs 56]; adolescents 12-18 y in fixed orthodontic treatment): three ~60-min sessions over 12 weeks combining a custom mobile app/videos with typodont hands-on practice (plaque-disclosing feedback, Modified Bass) versus one 30-min verbal session plus pamphlet.

At 3-month follow-up the intervention group had lower plaque index (1.0 vs 1.7; difference -0.7, 95% CI -0.9 to -0.5) and gingival index (0.9 vs 1.5; difference -0.6), higher knowledge scores (17.9 vs 13.8) and lower OHIP-14 (10.8 vs 16.8), all P<0.001, with baseline plaque/gingival indices balanced (2.1 vs 2.0; 1.8 vs 1.7).

The bundled package is confounded with roughly six times more contact time (about 180 vs 30 min) and is unblinded for participants, follow-up is only 3 months, and several reporting features (see Limitations) warrant cautious weighting; treat as supportive, low-certainty evidence that structured practice-based education beats a single verbal session.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 중국 샤먼, 무작위 배정 120명·분석 113명 [57 vs 56], 고정식 교정 중인 12–18세 청소년): 맞춤 모바일 앱·동영상과 치열모형 (typodont) 실습 (치태염색 피드백, 수정 바스법)을 결합한 약 60분×3회 (12주) 교육 vs 30분 1회 구두설명 + 팸플릿.

3개월 추적에서 중재군이 치태지수 (Plaque Index, PI) 1.0 vs 1.7 (차이 −0.7, 95% CI −0.9 ~ −0.5), 치은지수 (Gingival Index, GI) 0.9 vs 1.5, 지식점수 17.9 vs 13.8, 구강건강영향지수 (OHIP-14) 10.8 vs 16.8로 모두 유리 (P<0.001); 기저 PI/GI는 균형 (2.1 vs 2.0; 1.8 vs 1.7).

패키지 효과가 접촉시간 (약 180분 vs 30분)과 뒤섞여 있고 참가자 눈가림이 불가하며 추적이 3개월뿐이고 보고상 의문점이 있어, 구조화된 실습형 교육이 1회 구두설명보다 낫다는 낮은 확실성의 지지 근거로만 가중할 것.

## Summary
This single-centre Chinese RCT randomized 120 adolescents (12–18 y) in fixed orthodontic treatment to a three-session programme (custom app, instructional videos, typodont hands-on practice with plaque-disclosing feedback) or one 30-min verbal session plus pamphlet. Among 113 analysed, the intervention group had lower plaque (1.0 vs 1.7) and gingival (0.9 vs 1.5) indices, higher knowledge and lower OHIP-14 at 3 months (all P<0.001). Because the arms differ in contact time by roughly sixfold and no arm isolates a single component, the result supports "more structured, practice-based education" rather than proving a specific hands-on-plus-multimedia synergy. Reporting oddities (ethics number date, "simulation experiment data" statement, very large effect sizes) argue for cautious weighting.

## Key Contributions
- Adolescent fixed-appliance trial of *education format* (not brush type) with blinded clinical assessors and clinical endpoints (PI, GI) to 3 months.
- Practical protocol: typodont practice with plaque-disclosing feedback, Modified Bass, interdental aids, app-assigned modules, peer problem-solving.

## Methodology
Parallel RCT; stratified computer-generated randomization with sealed envelopes; assessors and statistician blinded, participants/educators not. n=60/60 randomized, 57/56 analysed (retention 94.2%). Outcomes at baseline, 12 weeks and 3-month follow-up: knowledge (20 items), OHIP-14, Silness-Löe PI, Löe-Silness GI; mixed-effects model with group × time interaction.

## Results
| Outcome (3-month) | Intervention | Control | Difference (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | 17.9 ± 1.8 | 13.8 ± 2.5 | 4.1 (3.3 to 4.9) |
| OHIP-14 | 10.8 ± 3.9 | 16.8 ± 5.0 | −6.0 (−7.6 to −4.4) |
| PI | 1.0 ± 0.3 | 1.7 ± 0.5 | −0.7 (−0.9 to −0.5) |
| GI | 0.9 ± 0.3 | 1.5 ± 0.4 | −0.6 (−0.7 to −0.5) |

Baseline PI 2.1 vs 2.0 and GI 1.8 vs 1.7. Self-reported brushing at least twice daily 89.5% vs 62.5%; daily interdental aid use 77.2% vs 42.9%. Satisfaction 93%; no age/gender interaction; no adverse events.

## Limitations
Contact time confounded with format (about 180 vs 30 min); unblinded participants; 3-month horizon; single centre; self-reported behaviours; no registration number given and ethics approval number that appears to postdate the stated enrolment window; data statement mentions "simulation experiment data".
