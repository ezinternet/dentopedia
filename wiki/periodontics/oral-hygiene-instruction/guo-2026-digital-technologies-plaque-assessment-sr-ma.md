## Summary

Guo et al. (J Evid Based Dent Pract 2026) conducted the first SR+MA examining how well digital plaque assessment technologies correlate with conventional reference methods (disclosed plaque indices). From 4981 records, 47 studies were included qualitatively and 16 in meta-analysis. Pooled correlation was strong (Pearson r=0.77, Spearman ρ=0.76). Subgroup analyses found AI-aided technologies performed best (r=0.87) and 3D intraoral scanners showed the strongest overall correlation (ρ=0.91). Use of disclosing agents markedly improved associations (r without agent=0.53 vs with=0.81), suggesting disclosing remains important even for digital workflows. Despite these promising findings, extreme heterogeneity (I²>98%) and very high risk of bias (96% of studies) led to a GRADE very low certainty rating — insufficient to provide definitive clinical recommendations.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA quantifying correlation accuracy of digital plaque assessment vs reference methods
- AI-aided technologies: strongest Pearson r=0.87 — suggests automated image analysis outperforms other digital approaches
- 3D IOS: ρ=0.91 — strong potential for objective plaque coverage mapping in clinical workflow
- Disclosing agents: raise r from 0.53 to 0.81 — continue to add value even with digital systems
- Despite strong correlations: GRADE very low due to heterogeneity and RoB — cannot yet recommend specific digital platform

## Methodology

SR+MA; QUADAS-2; GRADE; PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, Ovid MEDLINE, Cochrane; 4981 records → 47 qualitative, 16 meta-analysis; Fisher's z transformation; pooled Pearson r and Spearman ρ; subgroups: technology type, disclosing agent use, reference method.

## Results

| Technology | Pooled association | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Overall digital | r=0.77 (95%CI 0.64–0.86) | Strong |
| Overall digital | ρ=0.76 (95%CI 0.50–0.89) | Strong |
| AI-aided | r=0.87 | Very strong (subgroup) |
| 3D IOS | ρ=0.91 | Very strong (subgroup) |
| Without disclosing | r=0.53 (0.42–0.64) | Moderate |
| With disclosing | r=0.81 (0.68–0.89) | Strong |

Heterogeneity: I²>98%; RoB: 96% high or unclear; GRADE: very low.
