## Three-line Summary

Multicenter RCT (J Clin Periodontol 2026): 7 German university centers, n=194 adults with periodontitis; Paro-ComPas digital health companion app added to full EFP-guideline periodontal therapy vs standard care.

Primary endpoint (GBI) and all secondary endpoints (BOP, PCR, Feres composite): no statistically significant between-group difference; substantial center heterogeneity observed (Feres endpoint at T2: 37%–89% by center).

Digital companion apps for periodontitis hold intuitive appeal but this large proof-of-concept RCT did not detect a measurable clinical benefit — center-level implementation may matter more than the digital tool itself.

## Summary

Dannewitz et al. (J Clin Periodontol 2026) conducted the largest multicenter RCT evaluating a digital health companion app (Paro-ComPas) as an adjunct to systematic periodontal therapy. Seven German university centers enrolled 194 adults with periodontitis (EFP/AAP 2018) who were randomized to receive standard care ± the Paro-ComPas app featuring an oral hygiene diary, clinical dashboard, educational content, and push reminders. The primary endpoint was the gingival bleeding index (GBI). Confirmatory analyses using mixed-effects regression found no significant time × group interaction for GBI or any secondary outcome (BOP, PCR, Feres composite endpoint). Substantial center heterogeneity was observed — GBI trajectories diverged markedly across centers. The study concludes that digital companion apps do not demonstrably enhance outcomes in a real-world multicenter periodontal treatment setting.

## Key Contributions

- First large RCT (n=194, 7 centers) assessing digital companion over a complete periodontal treatment cycle
- Null result: no significant benefit for GBI (primary), BOP, PCR, or Feres composite
- Center heterogeneity is striking — center-specific context likely mediates digital tool effectiveness
- Proof-of-concept study: negative result informs future digital health trial design (center-specific strategies)
- App engagement data collected — qualitative insights on adherence patterns available for secondary analyses

## Methodology

Multicenter parallel RCT; 7 German centers; Aug 2023–Jan 2025; n=194 periodontitis (EFP/AAP 2018); ≥10 natural teeth; compatible smartphone; intervention = Paro-ComPas app (diary, dashboard, education, reminders); control = standard care (EFP S3 CPG 4-step); primary = GBI; mixed-effects regression; median regression (GBI, BOP); stratified by age (<40 vs ≥40).
