## Three-line Summary

Wang et al. (2026) synthesize evidence showing that regular supportive periodontal care (SPC) and supportive peri-implant care (SPIC) markedly reduce tooth and implant loss, disease progression, and need for surgical re-intervention. Risk-stratified recall intervals — every 3–6 months for moderate-to-high-risk patients, up to 12 months for low-risk — produce significantly better outcomes than irregular attendance. Non-compliance and residual pockets ≥5–6 mm are the dominant predictors of recurrence; AI-assisted imaging and chair-side biomarker testing are emerging adjuncts pending long-term validation.

## 세줄요약

Wang et al. (2026)은 정기적인 지지 치주치료 (Supportive Periodontal Care, SPC)와 지지 임플란트주위 관리 (Supportive Peri-implant Care, SPIC)가 치아 및 임플란트 상실, 질환 진행, 재수술 필요성을 현저히 줄인다는 근거를 종합한다. 중·고위험 환자에서 3–6개월, 저위험 환자는 최대 12개월의 개인화 리콜 간격이 유의하게 더 나은 임상 결과와 연관된다. 비순응 및 잔존 포켓 (Residual Pocket) ≥5–6 mm가 재발 예측인자 (Predictor of Recurrence)의 핵심이며, AI 영상 분석 및 의자측 바이오마커 검사는 유망 기술이나 장기 임상 검증이 선행되어야 한다.

## Summary

This comprehensive narrative review by Wang, Monje, Sculean and colleagues synthesizes the evidence base for supportive periodontal and peri-implant maintenance, published in Periodontology 2000 in 2026. Sourced from PubMed, Embase, and Scopus, the review integrates longitudinal cohort studies, RCTs, systematic reviews, and consensus guidelines.

The central finding is that both 지지 치주치료 (Supportive Periodontal Care, SPC) and 지지 임플란트주위 관리 (Supportive Peri-implant Care, SPIC) significantly reduce the rates of tooth and implant loss, slow the progression of periodontitis and peri-implantitis, and minimize the need for complex surgical re-intervention when patients are enrolled in regular maintenance programs.

Patient non-compliance with scheduled recall visits and the presence of 잔존 포켓 (Residual Pocket) ≥5–6 mm after active therapy are identified as the two principal predictors of disease recurrence — both modifiable targets for clinical intervention.

## Key Contributions

1. **Unified SPC + SPIC framework**: First comprehensive narrative review covering both supportive modalities together, demonstrating parallel evidence trajectories for teeth and implants
2. **Risk-stratified recall intervals**: Quantifies that 3–6 months for moderate-to-high-risk patients and up to 12 months for low-risk patients yields significantly better outcomes
3. **Risk assessment tool profiles**: Reviews 개인화 위험도 평가 (Personalized Risk Assessment, PRA) and 임플란트 질환 위험도 평가 (Implant Disease Risk Assessment, IDRA) as validated algorithms for recall interval determination
4. **Biofilm control modality equivalence**: Establishes that ultrasonic scaling, air-polishing, and 유도 바이오필름 요법 (Guided Biofilm Therapy, GBT) are all effective; none shows superior long-term results
5. **Emerging technology appraisal**: Chair-side biomarker testing and AI-supported image analysis — promising for early disease detection; clinical integration pending long-term validation

## Results

### Risk-Based Recall Interval Evidence

| Risk Profile | Recall Interval | Key Determinants |
|---|---|---|
| Low risk | Up to 12 months | Good compliance, no residual pockets, low systemic burden |
| Moderate risk | 3–6 months | Intermediate bone loss, occasional non-compliance |
| High risk | 3–6 months (toward 3) | Residual pockets ≥5–6 mm, smoking, diabetes, poor compliance history |

### Predictors of Recurrence

- **Primary**: Non-compliance with maintenance schedule
- **Primary**: 잔존 포켓 (Residual Pocket) ≥5–6 mm persisting after active therapy
- Both factors are independently associated with disease progression, tooth loss, and implant loss

### Biofilm Control Approaches

All three modalities demonstrated comparable long-term effectiveness:
- Ultrasonic scaling (conventional instrumentation)
- Air-polishing (subgingival powder delivery)
- 유도 바이오필름 요법 (Guided Biofilm Therapy, GBT) — systematic AIRFLOW + PERIOFLOW + Piezon protocol

No single protocol showed statistically superior long-term results. Selection can be guided by patient anatomy, pocket depth, and clinician preference.

### Emerging Technologies

- **Chair-side biomarker testing**: Targets early inflammatory markers in GCF/saliva; promising for detecting subclinical disease activity before clinical attachment loss occurs
- **AI-supported image analysis**: Assists with detecting bone level changes on periapical radiographs; currently requires expert interpretation; long-term prospective validation pending
