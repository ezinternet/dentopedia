## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (J Evid Based Dent Pract 2024; PRISMA; random-effects REML; Cochrane RoB 5.1.0) of RCTs comparing calcium phosphate (CP) grafts plus GTR versus open flap debridement (OFD): 451 records, 7 studies pooled (5 intrabony, 2 furcation).

Intrabony: WMD for CAL gain 1.536 mm (reported 95% CI -0.933 to 2.139) and PD reduction 1.601 mm (95% CI -0.727 to 2.474), both reported as significant; furcation: CAL gain 1.807 mm (95% CI 0.346-3.268, significant), PD reduction 1.519 mm (95% CI 1.215-4.253, not significant).

Authors conclude CP + GTR is superior to OFD, but the intrabony CIs as printed span zero while labeled significant (source inconsistency), and only 7 studies (2 furcation) contribute — interpret cautiously (abstract-only).

## Summary

Kouhi and colleagues meta-analyzed RCTs comparing calcium phosphate grafts combined with GTR membranes versus OFD in intrabony and furcation defects. Seven of 451 records were pooled (five intrabony, two furcation). Pooled WMDs favored CP + GTR for CAL gain and PD reduction in both defect types, and the authors conclude CP + GTR is superior to OFD. However, the intrabony confidence intervals as printed include zero despite a "significant" label, so the strength of the intrabony claim cannot be confirmed from the abstract.

## Key Contributions

- Separate pooled estimates for CP + GTR in intrabony and furcation defects against OFD
- CAL gain of roughly 1.5-1.8 mm over OFD, comparable to other graft-based regeneration reviews
- Flags the limited furcation evidence (2 studies)

## Methodology

PRISMA-guided electronic and manual search for English-language RCTs of CP + GTR versus OFD in intrabony and/or furcation defects. Outcomes: PD and CAL. Random-effects model (restricted maximum likelihood); quality assessed with Cochrane risk-of-bias tools v5.1.0.
