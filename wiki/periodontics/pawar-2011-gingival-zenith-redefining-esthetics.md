## Three-line Summary

Cast study of 35 young adults (21–30 y): face-bow–mounted casts on the axis-orbital plane; gingival line angle (GLA, gingival line vs midline) by protractor and lateral-incisor-to-gingival-line distance (LID) by caliper.

Left GLA 86.7° ± 4.2° > right 84.6° ± 5.4° (significant; absolute asymmetry 1.7° ± 4.4°) — canine zenith apical to central (GLA <90°), right side higher; mean LID 0.92 ± 0.11; lateral-zenith position vs gingival line reported inconsistently (17% vs 75.7%).

Suggests a canine-apical gingival line and slight directional asymmetry as natural norms; internal inconsistencies and missing tables limit citation.

## 세줄요약

모형 연구 (젊은 성인 35명): 안와축 평면 기준 장착 모형에서 치은선 각도 (GLA)와 측절치 정점–치은선 거리 (LID) 측정.

좌측 GLA 86.7° > 우측 84.6° (유의, 절대 비대칭 1.7°) — 견치 정점이 중절치보다 근단, 우측이 더 높음; LID 평균 0.92; 측절치 위치 비율 17% vs 75.7%로 내부 불일치.

견치 근단 치은선·미세 비대칭을 자연 기준으로 제시하나 수치 불일치·표 누락으로 인용 주의.

## Summary

Pawar and colleagues measured the gingival line — the tangent from central-incisor zenith to canine zenith — against the midline on face-bow-oriented casts. Both sides came out under 90°, meaning the canine zenith sits apical to the central's, and the right side was a couple of degrees "higher" than the left. They argue this subtle asymmetry is natural and that perfect symmetry is not the esthetic goal.

Use with caution. The paper states the lateral incisor's zenith was "below or on (17%)" the gingival line in the abstract but "frequently below (75.7%)" in the discussion, the results tables are not in the retrieved text, the abstract says casts were photographed while the methods say photography was avoided, and one operator did everything. The Martins 2024 SR+MA pooled studies and found contralateral symmetry — this paper is a small dissenting data point on that axis, not a refutation.

## Key Contributions

- GLA measured on 3D-oriented casts.
- Directional asymmetry (right higher) reported.

## Methodology

- 35 subjects; face-bow, Whipmix 2200; protractor and digital caliper; single operator; ANOVA and paired t test.

## Results

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| GLA left | 86.7° ± 4.2° |
| GLA right | 84.6° ± 5.4° |
| Absolute asymmetry | 1.7° ± 4.4° |
| LID | 0.92 ± 0.11 |

- No gender difference.
