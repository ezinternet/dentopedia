## Three-line Summary

JKDA 2025 narrative review (Korean, tertiary-care periodontist) translating EFP S3 and AAP guidelines into a 4-step treatment ladder (OHI/scaling → SRP → surgery → SPT) applicable to Korean clinical practice.

The review outlines a stepwise protocol: Step 1 (OHI, scaling, motivation), Step 2 (SRP for Stage I–III), Step 3 (resective or regenerative surgery for residual pockets), Step 4 (lifelong SPT); diabetes patients with HbA1c <7% show better treatment response, and periodontal therapy improves endothelial function in cardiovascular disease patients.

Korean clinicians should prioritize management of diabetes and cardiovascular comorbidities alongside periodontal treatment and consider specialist co-management (endocrinology, cardiology); SPT is essential for all patients for life.

## 세줄요약

JKDA 2025 종설(중앙보훈병원 치주과 송영우) — EFP S3 가이드라인·AAP 권고를 한국 임상에 적용하는 4단계 치주치료 사다리(OHI/스케일링 → SRP → 수술 → SPT) 제시.

Step 1(OHI·스케일링·동기부여) → Step 2(SRP, Stage I–III) → Step 3(잔존 깊은 포켓 수술) → Step 4(평생 SPT); HbA1c <7% 당뇨 환자에서 치주치료 반응 양호, 치주 치료가 심혈관 환자의 혈관내피기능 개선.

당뇨·심혈관 전신질환 동반 시 내분비·심장내과 협진 우선; 모든 환자에서 SPT는 평생 유지 필수; 전신질환-치주 상호작용은 강한 신호이나 일부 효과는 RCT 수준 입증 부분적.

## Treatment Ladder

| 단계 | 시술 | 적응 |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1 | OHI, scaling, motivation | 모든 환자 |
| Step 2 | SRP (sub-gingival debridement) | Stage I-III |
| Step 3 | Surgical (resective·regenerative) | Stage III-IV, 잔존 deep pocket |
| Step 4 | SPT (Supportive Periodontal Therapy) | 모든 환자 평생 |

## Systemic-Oral Interaction
- **DM**: HbA1c < 7% 환자에서 치주치료 효과 ↑
- **CVD**: 치주염 치료가 endothelial function 개선 (몇 RCT)
- 환자 의뢰의·심장내과·내분비과 협진 강조
