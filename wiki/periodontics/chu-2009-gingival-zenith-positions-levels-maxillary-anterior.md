## Three-line Summary

Clinical cross-sectional study of 240 sites in 20 periodontally healthy young adults (mean age 27.7 y) measuring gingival zenith position (GZP) from the vertical bisected midline and lateral-incisor gingival zenith level (GZL) on diagnostic casts.

All central incisors had a distal GZP (mean 1 mm); lateral incisors deviated a mean 0.4 mm; 97.5% of canines were centred; the lateral-incisor GZL sat ~1 mm coronal to the central–canine zenith line.

These are the canonical reference targets for crown lengthening, root coverage, and anterior restorative/implant soft-tissue design, but come from a small, young, ideal-dentition sample.

## Summary

Chu, Tan, Stappert and Tarnow quantified where the most apical point of the free gingival margin sits on each maxillary anterior tooth. Central incisors consistently have a zenith ~1 mm distal to the crown's bisecting midline, laterals much less (0.4 mm), and canines essentially on-axis. Vertically, the lateral incisor's zenith is about 1 mm coronal to a line drawn between the central and canine zeniths. These numbers became the default targets for periodontal plastic surgery and for shaping provisional restorations around anterior implants.

## Key Contributions

- Operational definitions: GZP (mesiodistal, from VBM) and GZL (apicocoronal, lateral vs zenith line).
- Reference values: 1 mm / 0.4 mm / centred / ~1 mm coronal.

## Methodology

- 20 patients (13 F / 7 M), 240 sites; strict ideal-dentition inclusion (no disease, recession, hypertrophy, papilla loss, spacing, crowding, restorations, attrition).
- Calibrated digital calipers on diagnostic casts.

## Results

| Tooth | GZP finding |
|---|---|
| Central incisor | 100% distal, mean 1 mm |
| Lateral incisor | mean 0.4 mm deviation |
| Canine | 97.5% centred |

- Lateral incisor GZL: ~1 mm coronal to the central–canine zenith tangent.

**Clinical reading**: for an anterior implant provisional, shape the facial critical contour so the zenith falls ~1 mm distal on a central, near-midline on a canine; keep a lateral's margin ~1 mm coronal to its neighbours.
