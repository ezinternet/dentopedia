## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (ADA Council on Scientific Affairs expert panel; PubMed and Embase through July 2014) of randomized controlled trials of scaling and root planing (SRP) with or without adjuncts in chronic periodontitis, with clinical attachment level (CAL) outcomes at 6 months or longer; 72 articles included (abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

SRP alone gave about 0.5 mm average CAL improvement (moderate certainty); adding adjuncts gave an average CAL improvement of 0.2 to 0.6 mm over SRP alone, with moderate-certainty benefit for subantimicrobial-dose doxycycline, systemic antimicrobials, chlorhexidine chips and photodynamic therapy with a diode laser, and low certainty for the other adjuncts, including minocycline microspheres and doxycycline hyclate gel.

Adjunct effects are small (sub-millimeter) and mostly low-certainty, so SRP remains the core treatment; locally delivered minocycline was judged low certainty at the time (2014 search), and this abstract-level summary carries no per-adjunct numbers.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (미국치과의사협회 과학위원회 전문가 패널; PubMed·Embase, 2014년 7월까지): 만성 치주염에서 치석제거술 및 치근활택술 (Scaling and Root Planing, SRP) 단독 또는 보조요법 병용의 6개월 이상 임상 부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 결과를 보고한 무작위 대조 시험 72편 포함 (초록 기반 — 전문 미확보).

SRP 단독으로 평균 약 0.5 mm의 CAL 개선(중등도 확실성); 보조요법 병용 시 SRP 단독 대비 평균 0.2–0.6 mm 추가 개선이며, 저용량 독시사이클린 (Subantimicrobial-dose Doxycycline), 전신 항균제, 클로르헥시딘 칩 (Chlorhexidine chip), 다이오드 레이저 광역동요법 (Photodynamic Therapy, PDT)은 중등도 확실성의 이득, 미노사이클린 마이크로스피어 (Minocycline microspheres)·독시사이클린 하이클레이트 겔 등 나머지는 낮은 확실성.

보조요법 효과는 1 mm 미만으로 작고 대부분 낮은 확실성이므로 SRP가 핵심 치료이며, 국소 미노사이클린은 당시(2014년 검색) 낮은 확실성으로 판정되었다. 초록 수준 요약이라 보조요법별 수치는 없다.

## Summary

abstract-only — full text not retrieved. An ADA Council on Scientific Affairs expert panel ran a systematic review and meta-analysis of 72 articles (RCTs of at least 6 months with CAL outcomes; PubMed and Embase through July 2014) on SRP with or without adjuncts in chronic periodontitis. SRP gave about 0.5 mm average CAL improvement with moderate certainty, and adjunct combinations added 0.2 to 0.6 mm over SRP alone. Moderate-certainty benefit was found for subantimicrobial-dose doxycycline, systemic antimicrobials, chlorhexidine chips and diode-laser photodynamic therapy; other adjuncts, including minocycline microspheres, were low certainty.

## Key Contributions

- Pooled estimate of SRP alone (about 0.5 mm CAL, moderate certainty).
- Range of adjunct add-on effects (0.2 to 0.6 mm CAL) with a certainty rating per adjunct class.
- Evidence base underlying the same panel's ADA clinical practice guideline.

## Methodology

SR+MA; PubMed and Embase through July 2014; English-language RCTs of at least 6 months with CAL outcome; 72 articles; Cochrane Risk of Bias Tool; funnel plots and Egger tests when more than 10 studies; modified US Preventive Services Task Force certainty grading.

## Results

| Outcome | Result (abstract only) |
|---|---|
| SRP alone, average CAL improvement | about 0.5 mm (moderate certainty) |
| SRP plus adjuncts vs SRP alone, CAL | 0.2 to 0.6 mm average improvement |
| Moderate-certainty benefit | subantimicrobial-dose doxycycline; systemic antimicrobials; chlorhexidine chips; diode-laser photodynamic therapy |
| Low-certainty benefit | doxycycline hyclate gel; minocycline microspheres; diode, Nd:YAG and Er lasers |

Per-adjunct pooled estimates were not available from the abstract.
