## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis) of 48 RCTs (2,755 participants, 2010–2024) evaluating adjunctive systemic antibiotics with scaling and root planing (SRP) for Stage III/IV periodontitis; amoxicillin (AMX) + metronidazole (MET) yielded the most consistent improvements: clinical attachment level (CAL) gain 0.47 mm, probing pocket depth (PPD) reduction 0.30 mm, bleeding on probing (BOP) reduction 6.9%.

A 3-day AMX/MET regimen was comparable to 7-day courses with fewer adverse events; evidence for azithromycin and doxycycline was more variable; overall certainty of evidence rated low due to study heterogeneity and PubMed-only search.

Adjunctive AMX/MET significantly enhances SRP outcomes in advanced periodontitis; shorter regimens may minimize antimicrobial resistance risk while preserving efficacy.

## 세줄요약

3일 요법 ≈ 7일 요법, 부작용 감소; 아지스로마이신·독시사이클린 근거 가변적; PubMed 단독 검색·연구 이질성으로 근거 질 낮음(Low).

## Summary

Kondreddy et al. (2025) systematically reviewed 48 RCTs (2,755 participants, 2010–2024) on adjunctive systemic antibiotics with SRP for Stage III/IV periodontitis. AMX/MET showed the most consistent improvements across studies: CAL gain 0.47 mm, PPD reduction 0.30 mm, BOP reduction 6.9%. Metronidazole alone also performed well (CAL 1.12 mm, PPD 1.11 mm, BOP 6.9%) and was an effective option for AMX-allergic patients. Azithromycin and doxycycline showed more variable results. A key finding was that a 3-day AMX/MET course (Cosgarea 2022) achieved comparable CAL and PPD outcomes to 7-day courses with fewer adverse events, supporting shorter regimens for antibiotic stewardship. This SR covers more recent trials (2010–2024) and includes emerging data on duration effects not covered in earlier SRs like Khattri 2020. No formal meta-analysis was performed due to heterogeneity. Overall evidence quality was rated low.

## Key Contributions
- 3-day AMX/MET = 7-day AMX/MET: comparable outcomes, fewer adverse events — supports stewardship
- Metronidazole alone effective for AMX-allergic patients: CAL 1.12 mm, PPD 1.11 mm
- Most recent coverage (2010–2024) with 3 newly published trials not in earlier SRs
- AMX/MET remains the most evidence-supported combination after 48 RCTs
- Low overall evidence quality: heterogeneity, small samples, PubMed-only search

## Results

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amoxicillin + Metronidazole | 389 | 0.47 | 0.30 | 6.90 |
| Metronidazole alone | 71 | 1.12 | 1.11 | 6.90 |
| Azithromycin | 110 | 0.59 | 0.77 | 1.28 |
| Doxycycline | 22 | 0.80 | 1.00 | NR |

- 3-day vs 7-day AMX/MET (Cosgarea 2022): comparable CAL and PPD; fewer adverse events at 3 days
- Overall evidence quality: LOW

## Methodology
- PRISMA; PROSPERO CRD42024554321; PubMed-only search, 2010–2024
- Inclusion: RCTs, adjunctive systemic antibiotics with SRP, Stage III/IV periodontitis
- 48 RCTs, 2,755 participants; no formal meta-analysis
