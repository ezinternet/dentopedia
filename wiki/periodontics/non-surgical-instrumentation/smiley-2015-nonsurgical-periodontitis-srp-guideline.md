## Three-line Summary

ADA evidence-based guideline (SR+MA + expert consensus panel) on nonsurgical treatment of chronic periodontitis, establishing SRP as strongly recommended first-line therapy.

SRP alone reduces PPD 1–2 mm and gains 0.5–1 mm CAL; systemic doxycycline adjunct adds PPD reduction 0.2–0.4 mm and CAL gain 0.3 mm, especially at PPD ≥5 mm; local delivery antimicrobials (minocycline, chlorhexidine chip) also effective at PPD ≥5 mm sites.

Adjunct-antibiotic recommendation is partially outdated — the 2020 EFP S3 guideline restricts systemic antibiotics to stage III grade C/D; this guideline remains valid as the US ADA anchor and for its SRP effect-size figures.

## 세줄요약

만성 치주염 비수술 치료(SRP ± 보조)에 관한 미국치과의사협회(ADA) 근거기반 가이드라인으로, SR+MA 및 전문가 합의 패널을 기반으로 SRP를 1차 치료로 강력 권고.

SRP 단독으로 PPD 1–2 mm 감소·CAL 0.5–1 mm 획득; 전신 독시사이클린 보조 시 PPD ≥5 mm 부위에서 추가 PPD −0.2–0.4 mm, CAL +0.3 mm; 국소 항균제(미노사이클린·CHX 칩)도 PPD ≥5 mm에 유효.

항생제 보조 권고는 2020 EFP S3 가이드라인(3기 grade C/D로 전신 항생제 제한)에 의해 부분 대체되었으나, SRP 효과크기 수치와 미국 가이드라인 근거로서의 가치는 유효.

## Summary
Evidence-based clinical practice guideline on SRP with or without adjuncts for chronic periodontitis.

## Key Contributions
- SRP alone reduces PPD 1–2mm, gains 0.5–1mm CAL
- Systemic doxycycline adjunct: additional PPD reduction 0.2–0.4mm, CAL gain 0.3mm
- Local delivery antimicrobials (minocycline, chlorhexidine chip): effective at PPD ≥5mm sites

## Methodology
Systematic review + meta-analysis + expert consensus panel (ADA).

## Results
SRP is strongly recommended as first-line for chronic periodontitis. Doxycycline adjunct beneficial especially for PPD ≥5mm. SDD (subantimicrobial doxycycline) also supported.
