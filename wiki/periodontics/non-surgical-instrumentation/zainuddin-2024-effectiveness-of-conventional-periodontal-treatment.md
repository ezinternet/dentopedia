## Three-line Summary

Pilot randomized, open, single-blinded trial (n=45; 15 per arm; Universiti Sains Malaysia periodontic clinic; 3-month follow-up) comparing SRP + tetracycline fiber (Periodontal Plus AB), SRP + 2% minocycline HCl gel, and SRP alone in periodontitis patients with pockets of 5 mm or more.

All three arms improved significantly within group at 3 months (PD change 1.79 / 1.46 / 1.74 mm; BOP change 22.15 / 26.42 / 14.76 percentage points for tetracycline / minocycline / control), but repeated-measures ANOVA showed no significant between-group difference for PD, CAL, or BOP (all p>0.05), and SRP alone had the largest CAL gain (1.10 mm).

Local tetracycline or minocycline gave no demonstrated advantage over SRP alone; with n=15 per arm, 3-month follow-up, no microbiology and no sample-size justification, the trial is underpowered and cannot establish equivalence.

## 세줄요약

파일럿 무작위 개방 단일맹검 시험 (n=45, 군당 15명, 말레이시아 이슬람과학대학 치주과 클리닉, 3개월 추적): 5 mm 이상 치주낭이 있는 치주염 환자에서 SRP + 테트라사이클린 파이버 (Tetracycline fiber) vs SRP + 2% 미노사이클린 겔 (Minocycline gel) vs SRP 단독을 비교했다.

세 군 모두 군내에서는 유의하게 개선되었으나(3개월 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 감소 1.79 / 1.46 / 1.74 mm, 탐침 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP) 감소 22.15 / 26.42 / 14.76 %p; 테트라사이클린 / 미노사이클린 / 대조), 반복측정 ANOVA상 PPD·임상 부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL)·BOP 모두 군 간 유의차가 없었고(전부 p>0.05) CAL 개선은 SRP 단독군이 가장 컸다(1.10 mm).

국소 테트라사이클린·미노사이클린이 SRP 단독보다 낫다는 근거는 없으며, 군당 15명·3개월·미생물 검사 없음·표본수 산출 근거 없음으로 검정력이 부족해 동등성도 입증하지 못한다.

## Summary

This pilot randomized trial from Universiti Sains Malaysia assigned 45 adults with periodontitis (mean age about 52 years) equally to scaling and root planing (SRP) alone, SRP plus subgingival tetracycline fiber (Periodontal Plus AB), or SRP plus 2% minocycline HCl gel, treating all pockets of 5 mm or more and re-examining at 1 and 3 months. All arms improved significantly from baseline in probing depth (PD), clinical attachment level (CAL) and bleeding on probing (BOP). The tetracycline arm had the largest PD reduction (1.79 mm at 3 months), the minocycline arm the largest BOP reduction (26.42 percentage points), and the SRP-only arm the largest CAL gain (1.10 mm), but repeated-measures ANOVA found no significant between-group or group-by-time differences. The authors conclude that local tetracycline and minocycline "may improve" healing yet offer no advantage over SRP alone; the small sample and short follow-up limit interpretation.

## Key Contributions

- Direct head-to-head of tetracycline fiber vs minocycline 2% gel vs SRP alone in a single trial.
- No significant between-group difference in PD (F=2.3), CAL (F=0.498) or BOP (F=2.123), all p>0.05.
- Numerically larger CAL gain with SRP alone (1.10 mm) than with either adjunct (0.87 / 0.95 mm).
- Illustrates how an underpowered pilot (15 per arm) fails to confirm the adjunct benefit reported in pooled analyses.

## Methodology

Pilot RCT, blocked randomization with sealed envelopes, Periodontic Clinic of Hospital Universiti Sains Malaysia. n=45 (15 per arm). Inclusion: periodontitis, no periodontal treatment or antibiotics in the prior 3 months; exclusion: pregnancy, bone disease, immunocompromised. SRP (ultrasonic and hand curettes, local anesthesia) of all pockets of 5 mm or more, followed by tetracycline fiber, minocycline HCl 2% gel, or nothing. PD, CAL and BOP% at baseline, 1 and 3 months by one calibrated examiner (ICC/Cronbach alpha 0.78). Repeated-measures ANOVA, alpha 0.05; sphericity violated for CAL and BOP with no correction reported.
