## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated the effectiveness of chlorhexidine chips and gels compared to other local antimicrobials after scaling and root planing in periodontitis patients, including 9 studies (8 RCTs with low risk of bias, 1 non-RCT with moderate risk). Chlorhexidine chips were less effective than other antimicrobials for probing depth at 1 month (MD = 0.58mm, p < 0.00001) and 3 months (MD = 0.50mm, p = 0.001), but more effective for gingival index at 3 months (MD = -0.11mm, p = 0.02). Chlorhexidine gel was less effective for plaque index at 1 month (MD = 0.20mm, p = 0.0008).

Chlorhexidine chips showed superior gingival index improvement, while other antimicrobials (tetracycline, metronidazole, minocycline) showed better probing depth reduction.

## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis compared the effectiveness of chlorhexidine chips and gels versus other local antimicrobials after scaling and root planing (SRP) in periodontitis patients. Nine studies (8 RCTs with low risk of bias, 1 non-RCT with moderate risk) were included. Meta-analysis showed that chlorhexidine chips were less effective than other antimicrobials for probing depth reduction at 1 month (MD = 0.58mm, p < 0.00001) and 3 months (MD = 0.50mm, p = 0.001). However, chlorhexidine chips were more effective for gingival index at 3 months (MD = -0.11mm, p = 0.02). Chlorhexidine gel showed no difference in probing depth (MD = 0.40mm, p = 0.30) or gingival index (MD = -0.04mm, p = 0.83) at 1 month, but was less effective for plaque index (MD = 0.20mm, p = 0.0008). The authors concluded that chlorhexidine chips are superior for gingival index improvement, while other antimicrobials are better for probing depth reduction.

## Key Contributions

- CHX chips inferior to other antimicrobials for PD reduction at 1 month (MD = 0.58mm)
- CHX chips inferior to other antimicrobials for PD reduction at 3 months (MD = 0.50mm)
- CHX chips superior to other antimicrobials for GI improvement at 3 months (MD = -0.11mm)
- No difference in PI between CHX chips and other antimicrobials at 3 months
- CHX gel inferior to other antimicrobials for PI at 1 month (MD = 0.20mm)
- Tetracycline fibers showed best PD reduction; CHX gel showed no significant PD difference

## Methodology

This systematic review and meta-analysis followed PRISMA guidelines with a registered protocol (PROSPERO CRD42022351534). A comprehensive search was conducted in ProQuest, PubMed, EBSCO, ScienceDirect, and Scopus. Eligible studies were clinical trials evaluating chlorhexidine versus other local antimicrobials (metronidazole, tetracycline, minocycline) after SRP in periodontitis patients with probing depth ≥5mm and follow-up ≥30 days. Nine studies were included. Risk of bias was assessed using RoB2 (8 RCTs low risk) and ROBINS-I (1 non-RCT moderate risk). Meta-analysis used mean difference with 95% CI in RevMan 5.4. Heterogeneity was assessed with I².
