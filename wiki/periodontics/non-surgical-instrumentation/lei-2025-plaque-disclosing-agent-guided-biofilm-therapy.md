## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (as titled; the Methods section confusingly states "case-control studies of reporting guidelines have been used"), n=220 enrolled / 205 analyzed (103 control vs 102 observation) patients with chronic periodontitis at Xi'an Jiaotong University, comparing GBT-concept-based oral hygiene guidance against traditional chair-side oral health education over 3 months.

At 3 months the GBT-based OHG group had significantly lower plaque index (31.80±14.06 vs 44.27±15.74, p<0.001), bleeding on probing (23.39±18.53 vs 28.76±16.12, p<0.001), and PD≥4 mm sites (19.18±16.56 vs 26.12±14.91, p=0.002), plus higher self-care efficacy (all three SESS dimensions, p<0.001) and better self-reported oral hygiene behaviors.

Suggests plaque-disclosure-guided GBT oral-hygiene instruction improves plaque control and self-management in chronic periodontitis, but the mislabeled design, single-center retrospective nature, and inconsistent sample sizes/statistics limit causal inference.

만성 치주염 환자 220명(등록; 15명 탈락 → 최종 103명 대조군 vs 102명 관찰군)을 3개월간 전통적 구강위생교육과 가이드 바이오필름 치료(Guided Biofilm Therapy, GBT) 개념 기반 구강위생지도(oral hygiene guidance, OHG)로 나눈 후향 코호트 연구(단 논문 2장은 스스로 "case-control reporting guidelines"라 서술해 설계 표기가 불일치).

3개월 후 관찰군이 대조군보다 치태지수(Plaque Index, PI) 31.80±14.06 vs 44.27±15.74(p<0.001), 탐침시출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP) 23.39 vs 28.76(p<0.001), 치주낭깊이≥4 mm(PD≥4) 19.18 vs 26.12(p=0.002)로 유의하게 낮았고, 자가효능척도(Self-Efficacy Scale for Self-care, SESS) 3개 영역 모두 대조군보다 유의하게 높았다(p<0.001).

치태 착색제 기반 GBT 구강위생지도가 치태조절과 자가관리를 개선함을 시사하나, 설계 오표기·단일기관 후향·불일치한 표본수와 통계로 인과 해석에는 한계가 있다.

## Summary

This single-center study from Xi'an Jiaotong University compared GBT-concept-based oral hygiene guidance (plaque disclosing agent + plaque control record card + patient self-inspection, plus WeChat/telephone continuity of care) against traditional chair-side oral health education in patients with chronic periodontitis. Of 220 enrolled patients, 15 were lost to follow-up, leaving 103 control and 102 observation patients analyzed at 3 months. The GBT-OHG group showed significantly lower plaque index (31.80% vs 44.27%), bleeding on probing (23.39% vs 28.76%), and PD≥4 mm percentage (19.18% vs 26.12%), together with significantly higher self-care efficacy (SESS) across all three dimensions and better self-reported hygiene behaviors. The paper is internally inconsistent about its own design — the title says "retrospective cohort" while the Methods state "case-control studies of reporting guidelines have been used" — and reports n=100 for the observation group in the SESS table, so the causal claim that GBT-based OHG improves outcomes should be treated as hypothesis-generating rather than confirmed.

## Key Contributions

- Applies the GBT concept specifically to **oral hygiene guidance / patient self-care** (plaque disclosure → self-inspection → targeted instruction → home plaque-staining routine), extending GBT beyond the instrumentation step.
- Reports lower PI, BOP, and PD≥4 plus higher self-care efficacy (SESS) with GBT-based OHG versus traditional education at 3 months.
- Demonstrates behavioral improvement (brushing frequency/duration, floss/interdental-brush use, correct brushing technique) in the GBT group.
- Adds structured continuity of care (biweekly WeChat pushes + monthly telephone follow-up).

## Methodology

Retrospective cohort (as titled; §2 inconsistently cites "case-control studies of reporting guidelines"). Single center, October 2020 – October 2022; n=220 enrolled, 15 lost to follow-up, 103 CG vs 102 OG analyzed (SESS table lists OG n=100). CG received traditional chair-side oral health education; OG received GBT-concept OHG (plaque disclosing agent, plaque control record card, supervised on-site brushing, proximal cleaning instruction, home plaque-staining routine, WeChat/telephone follow-up) over 3 months. Outcomes: PI (O'Leary record), BOP%, PD≥4 mm%, SESS (Kakudate; 3 dimensions, 15 items), and an oral-hygiene-behavior questionnaire (Cronbach α=0.915). Statistics: SPSS 20.0, independent-sample t tests and χ² tests, P<0.05 significant; no power calculation, confounder adjustment, effect sizes, or CIs.

## Results

|---|---|---|---|
| Plaque index (%) | 44.27±15.74 | 31.80±14.06 | <0.001 |
| Bleeding on probing (%) | 28.76±16.12 | 23.39±18.53 | <0.001 |
| PD≥4 mm (%) | 26.12±14.91 | 19.18±16.56 | 0.002 |
| SESS — SE-DC | 14.47±4.17 | 23.25±14.47 | <0.001 |
| SESS — SE-B | 13.33±5.26 | 23.76±1.97 | <0.001 |
| SESS — SE-DH | 14.56±3.78 | 23.54±1.37 | <0.001 |
| Brushing ≥3×/day | 25 (24.27%) | 41 (40.20%) | 0.038 |
| Brushing ≥3 min | 34 (45.45%) | 95 (93.14%) | <0.001 |
| Daily floss/interdental brush | 49 (47.57%) | 74 (72.55%) | <0.001 |
| Correct brushing method | 34 (33.01%) | 84 (86.27%) | <0.001 |

Baseline PI, BOP, PD≥4, all SESS dimensions, and demographics were not significantly different between groups (P>.05). The within-group plaque-detection improvement in OG is reported only in Fig. 3 (no numeric values or p-value). Exact p-values are not reported for every comparison.
