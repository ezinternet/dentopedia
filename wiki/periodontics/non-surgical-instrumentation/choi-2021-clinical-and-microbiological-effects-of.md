## Three-line Summary

Pilot parallel-group RCT (Gangneung-Wonju National University Dental Hospital, Korea, April-October 2018; 24 adults with chronic periodontitis who had already been in supportive periodontal therapy (SPT) for more than 1 year, with at least 4 sites of PD of 5 mm or more and BoP above 25%; 12 per arm randomized, 12 test and 10 control analyzed) comparing whole-mouth subgingival debridement plus a single application of 2% minocycline HCl microcapsule gel (Periocline, 20 mg per subject) versus debridement plus saline irrigation, with clinical and real-time PCR assessment at baseline, 1 and 3 months.

Both arms improved from baseline and did not differ: mean PPD 3.55 to 3.37 to 3.42 mm (control) vs 3.35 to 3.27 to 3.36 mm (test), between-group p = 0.476 at 1 month and 0.685 at 3 months; CAL 5.26 to 4.74 mm vs 4.85 to 4.65 mm at 3 months (p = 0.810); BoP 41.03% to 24.98% vs 43.38% to 23.71% at 3 months (p = 0.794); P. gingivalis and F. nucleatum ratios did not change significantly, with only a non-significant tendency to fall in the test arm and rise in the control arm at 1 month.

In maintenance-phase patients the benefit came from the mechanical debridement itself, and a single minocycline ointment application added nothing detectable at 3 months; with 10-12 patients per arm, no sample-size justification, no described allocation concealment or blinding, and only 3 months of follow-up, this shows "no difference detected", not equivalence.

## 세줄요약

소규모 예비 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 한국 강릉원주대학교 치과병원, 2018년 4-10월): 치주 유지관리 치료 (Supportive Periodontal Therapy, SPT)를 1년 이상 받은 만성 치주염 성인 24명 (치주낭 깊이 5 mm 이상 부위 4곳 이상, 탐침출혈 25% 초과; 군당 12명 배정, 시험군 12명·대조군 10명 분석)을 전악 치주낭 내 디브리드먼트 + 2% 미노사이클린 염산염 마이크로캡슐 겔 (Periocline, 1인당 20 mg) 1회 적용군과 디브리드먼트 + 생리식염수 세척군으로 나누어 기저, 1개월, 3개월에 임상·실시간 PCR 평가.

두 군 모두 기저치 대비 개선되었고 군간 차이는 없었다: 평균 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 3.55에서 3.37, 3.42 mm (대조) vs 3.35에서 3.27, 3.36 mm (시험), 군간 p = 0.476 (1개월)·0.685 (3개월); 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 3개월 4.74 vs 4.65 mm (p = 0.810); 탐침출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BoP) 3개월 24.98% vs 23.71% (p = 0.794); 포르피로모나스 진지발리스 (Porphyromonas gingivalis)·푸조박테리움 뉴클레아툼 (Fusobacterium nucleatum) 비율은 유의한 변화가 없었고, 1개월에 시험군은 감소·대조군은 증가하는 비유의적 경향만 보였다.

유지관리 환자에서 개선은 기계적 디브리드먼트 자체에서 왔고 미노사이클린 연고 1회 적용의 추가 효과는 3개월 시점에 확인되지 않았다; 군당 10-12명, 표본수 산출 근거 없음, 배정은닉·눈가림 미기술, 추적 3개월이므로 "차이를 못 찾음"이지 동등성 입증이 아니다.

## Summary

This pilot randomized trial from a Korean university dental hospital tested whether a single subgingival application of 2% minocycline HCl microcapsule gel (Periocline, 20 mg per subject) improves outcomes when added to whole-mouth debridement in 24 chronic periodontitis patients already in supportive periodontal therapy for more than 1 year. Both arms (12 test, 10 control analyzed) improved from baseline in CAL, BoP and the proportion of deep sites, and PPD fell at 1 month, with no significant between-group difference at 1 or 3 months. P. gingivalis and F. nucleatum ratios (real-time PCR) showed only a non-significant tendency to fall in the test arm at 1 month. The result suggests that in stable maintenance patients the benefit comes from debridement itself, but the study is too small and short to exclude a deep-site or longer-term effect.

## Key Contributions

- Maintenance-phase (SPT after more than 1 year) test of a 2% minocycline ointment, a context rarely studied for local antibiotics.
- Pairs clinical outcomes with relative-quantification qPCR of P. gingivalis and F. nucleatum.
- Reports a null added effect at 1 and 3 months (CAL 4.74 vs 4.65 mm, BoP 24.98% vs 23.71% at 3 months, control vs test).

## Methodology

Pilot randomized two-arm study, single examiner, 24 enrolled (12 per arm; 2 control dropouts). Test: debridement + 2% minocycline HCl microcapsule gel (Periocline) into all pockets/sulci, once at baseline; control: debridement + saline irrigation. PPD, CAL, BoP, plaque index, and subgingival paper-strip qPCR at baseline, 1 and 3 months. Friedman/Wilcoxon (Bonferroni, P < 0.017) over time, Mann-Whitney between groups. Randomization method, concealment and blinding not described.

## Results

| Outcome (control vs test) | Baseline | 3 months | Between-group p (3 mo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PPD (mm) | 3.55 vs 3.35 | 3.42 vs 3.36 | 0.685 |
| CAL (mm) | 5.26 vs 4.85 | 4.74 vs 4.65 | 0.810 |
| BoP (%) | 41.03 vs 43.38 | 24.98 vs 23.71 | 0.794 |
| Sites PD 5 mm or more (%) | 12.03 vs 9.18 | 6.76 vs 8.10 | 0.651 |

PPD improved significantly at 1 month (3.37 vs 3.27 mm) but, by Table 3 superscripts, was not distinguishable from baseline at 3 months; CAL, BoP and deep-site proportions stayed improved at 3 months with no 1-to-3-month change. Microbiology: baseline P. gingivalis about 0.01% and F. nucleatum 0.15-0.19% of total bacteria; no significant change or between-group difference, only a non-significant 1-month tendency (decrease in test, increase in control).
