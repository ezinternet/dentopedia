## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 2 systematic reviews aligned with the 2018 World Workshop Classification (WWC) framework for Stage III/IV periodontitis, using AMSTAR-2 quality appraisal and Corrected Covered Area (CCA) for overlap assessment (CCA=52.9% — substantial); AMX+MTZ showed moderate-certainty evidence for greater clinical attachment level (CAL) gain and probing pocket depth (PPD) reduction (Lin et al. 2025, NMA of ~45 RCTs); azithromycin evidence was insufficient for routine use (Jones & Hoyle 2022, lower certainty).

Doxycycline evidence was too sparse for conclusions; Stage IV-specific evidence was absent in the underlying literature; both included SRs had substantial primary-study overlap, limiting independence of conclusions.

Findings support selective, not routine, antibiotic use in Stage III/IV periodontitis; antibiotics should complement effective mechanical treatment — consistent with European Federation of Periodontology (EFP) S3-level clinical practice guidelines.

## Summary

Sesha Reddy Manchala et al. (2026) conducted the first umbrella review restricted to SRs using the 2018 WWC Stage III/IV framework. Only 2 SRs met eligibility from 273 records. Lin et al. (2025, 45 RCTs, NMA) provided moderate-certainty evidence for AMX+MTZ superiority in CAL gain and PPD reduction. Jones & Hoyle (2022, pairwise MA) found insufficient evidence for routine azithromycin use. The CCA formula (N=56 primary studies, r=2 SRs, c=52 unique+shared) yielded 52.9% — indicating the same primary RCTs appear in both reviews, so the two do not provide independent confirmation. Stage IV-specific RCTs were absent in the literature. The authors align findings with EFP S3 guidelines: selective antibiotic use in carefully selected patients, with mechanical treatment as the foundation.

## Key Contributions
- First umbrella review restricted to SRs using 2018 WWC Stage III/IV classification
- CCA=52.9%: substantial primary-study overlap — conclusions not independent between the 2 SRs
- AMX+MTZ: moderate-certainty evidence from NMA (~45 RCTs via Lin 2025)
- Azithromycin: insufficient evidence for routine use
- Stage IV: no dedicated evidence exists — most data are Stage III
- EFP S3 guideline alignment confirmed: selective, patient-centered antibiotic use only

## Methodology
- PRISMA; PROSPERO CRD420261449383
- Databases: Embase, Google Scholar, LILACS, MEDLINE, PubMed, Scopus; June 2018 – June 2026
- Inclusion: SRs/MAs with quantitative meta-analysis, Stage III/IV (2018 WWC), English only
- 273 records → 2 eligible SRs included
- CCA: (N−r)/(rc−r) × 100; N=56, r=2, c=52 → 52.9% substantial overlap
- AMSTAR-2 methodological quality assessment

## Glossary
- **Umbrella review**: SR of SRs; synthesizes evidence across multiple systematic reviews
- **CCA (Corrected Covered Area)**: 52.9% = substantial overlap in primary studies across the 2 SRs
- **AMSTAR-2**: Methodological quality appraisal tool for SRs
- **Stage III/IV (2018 WWC)**: CAL ≥5 mm + bone loss to middle/apical third; Stage IV adds masticatory dysfunction, tooth loss, occlusal collapse
