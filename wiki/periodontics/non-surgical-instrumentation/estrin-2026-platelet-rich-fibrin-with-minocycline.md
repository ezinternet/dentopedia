## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (private practice, Florida; 15 systemically healthy adults with Stage II/III Grade A/B periodontitis recruited, 13 analyzed) comparing, as adjuncts to full-mouth SRP plus laser, PRF mixed with liquid minocycline versus 1 mg minocycline microspheres (Arestin) in 4-6 mm interproximal pockets (one site per arm per patient) over 6 months.

Both arms improved significantly from baseline and did not differ: PD 5.08 to 3.94 mm (PRF + minocycline) vs 4.98 to 3.98 mm (Arestin), between-group p = 0.609; CAL 4.06 to 3.04 vs 4.06 to 2.98 mm, p = 0.789; GR p = 0.096; BOP 100% to 30% vs 100% to 18% (data not shown, not tested); 15.4% of sites in each arm reached residual PD of 3 mm, none below 3 mm.

PRF + minocycline looked comparable to Arestin at 6 months, but with 13 sites per arm, a design not powered for equivalence, an unequal/unclear minocycline dose, laser in both arms, and an author holding PRF intellectual property, "comparable" means "no difference detected", not shown equivalent.

## 세줄요약

분할구강 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 미국 플로리다 개인의원): 전신 건강한 성인 Stage II/III, Grade A/B 치주염 15명 모집(13명 분석); 전악 치석제거·치근활택술 (Scaling and Root Planing, SRP) + 레이저에 더해, 4-6 mm 인접면 치주낭에 PRF에 미노사이클린 (minocycline)을 섞은 군 vs 미노사이클린 1 mg 마이크로스피어 (Arestin) 군을 환자당 1부위씩 배정, 6개월 추적.

두 군 모두 기저치 대비 유의하게 개선되었고 군간 차이는 없었다: 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 5.08에서 3.94 mm (PRF+미노사이클린) vs 4.98에서 3.98 mm (Arestin), 군간 p = 0.609; 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 4.06에서 3.04 vs 4.06에서 2.98 mm, p = 0.789; 치은퇴축 (Gingival Recession, GR) p = 0.096; 탐침출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP) 100%에서 30% vs 100%에서 18% (자료 미제시, 검정 없음); 두 군 모두 15.4% 부위가 잔존 PD 3 mm에 도달했고 3 mm 미만은 없었다.

6개월 시점에서 PRF+미노사이클린은 Arestin과 유사해 보이나, 군당 13부위, 동등성 검정이 아닌 설계, 불균등하거나 불명확한 미노사이클린 용량, 양 군 공통 레이저, PRF 지식재산을 가진 저자라는 한계가 있어 "유사"는 "차이를 못 찾음"이지 동등성 입증이 아니다.

## Summary

Estrin and colleagues (Oral Health & Preventive Dentistry 2026;24:977-984) ran a split-mouth RCT in a Florida private practice comparing two ways of delivering local minocycline after non-surgical periodontal therapy: autologous PRF (Bio-PRF liquid PRF mixed with 0.2 ml minocycline solution, clotted and packed subgingivally) versus the commercial 1 mg minocycline microsphere product Arestin. Fifteen adults with Stage II/III, Grade A/B periodontitis each contributed two interproximal sites (4-6 mm), one per arm; 13 completed the 6-month analysis. All patients received full-mouth SRP with ultrasonic and hand instruments plus adjunctive Fotona laser.

Both arms improved significantly from baseline in PD, CAL and BOP, and the arms did not differ in PD (p = 0.609), CAL (p = 0.789) or GR (p = 0.096). The authors conclude the two adjuncts are comparable in the short term and that the choice may rest on clinician preference, cost, availability and handling. Read critically, the trial cannot say whether either adjunct adds anything to SRP alone (no SRP-only arm, laser in both arms), was powered for superiority with an assumed d = 1.0 rather than for equivalence, did not model within-patient correlation, and reports BOP without data or a test. The Results text also says PRF carried "1 mg" minocycline, while the Methods describe 0.2 ml of a 10 mg/0.5 ml solution (4 mg) mixed into 2 cc PRF with only 2-3 fragments placed, so the delivered dose is unclear.

## Key Contributions

- First reported head-to-head comparison (per the authors) of PRF-delivered minocycline versus Arestin as NSPT adjuncts.
- No between-group difference in PD, CAL or GR at 6 months; both arms improved significantly, with an identical CAL-gain distribution (30.8% under 1 mm; 61.5% 1 to under 2 mm; 7.7% 2 to under 3 mm).
- Residual PD of 3 mm in only 15.4% of sites per arm and none below 3 mm, so pocket closure to shallow depth was uncommon with either adjunct in 4-6 mm interproximal sites.
- Frames PRF as an autologous, synthetic-carrier-free drug-delivery scaffold whose trade-off is venipuncture and chairside preparation versus the ready-to-use Arestin syringe.

## Methodology

- **Design**: split-mouth RCT, computer-generated randomization with allocation concealment until intervention; one trained examiner measured PD, CAL, GR at baseline and 6 months; CONSORT, ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07437209, IRB approved.
- **Participants**: 15 recruited (December 2024 to April 2025), 9 men and 6 women, mean age 46.7 +/- 10.8 y, 6 current smokers; 13 analyzed (1 lost, 1 excluded for 100% plaque at 6 months).
- **Interventions**: all patients full-mouth SRP + Fotona laser; control site Arestin 1 mg; test site PRF (8 min at 700 x g) mixed with 0.2 ml minocycline 10 mg/0.5 ml, clotted 10-20 min, 2-3 fragments placed with a cord packer.
- **Follow-up**: 2 weeks, 3 months (supportive therapy), 6 months.
- **Statistics**: sample size 13 (CAL SD 0.55, d = 1.0, 80% power) plus 20% dropout allowance; Shapiro-Wilk; paired t-test; no GEE or mixed model.
