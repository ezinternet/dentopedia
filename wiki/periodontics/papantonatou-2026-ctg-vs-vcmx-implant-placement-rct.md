## Three-line Summary

3-arm RCT (J Clin Periodontol 2026): 25 patients; CTG vs volume-stable collagen matrix (VCMX) vs no augmentation at implant placement in the maxillary aesthetic zone; 6-month outcomes including clinical, CBCT, histological, and PROMs.

Both CTG and VCMX significantly increased soft-tissue thickness (STT) vs no augmentation; CTG superior to VCMX for thickness gain; histological integration favorable for both; CTG carries higher donor-site morbidity and lower patient willingness to repeat.

VCMX is a clinically effective, less invasive alternative to CTG for soft-tissue augmentation at implant placement, achieving meaningful thickness gains with reduced patient morbidity — though not reaching CTG's superior volume.

## Summary

Papantonatou et al. (J Clin Periodontol 2026) conducted a 3-arm RCT at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki comparing soft-tissue augmentation (STA) at the time of implant placement in the aesthetic zone: subepithelial connective tissue graft (SCTG), volume-stable collagen matrix (VCMX), or no augmentation (control). At 6 months, both CTG and VCMX significantly increased soft-tissue thickness (STT) vs control, with CTG achieving superior thickness. Histological analysis at 3 months showed favorable integration for both materials with adequate vascularization. BBT decreased in all groups. PROMs were comparable for pain between CTG and VCMX, but patient willingness to repeat the procedure was lower in the CTG group (donor-site morbidity). VCMX showed mild inflammation consistent with physiological remodeling.

## Key Contributions

- First 3-arm RCT combining clinical + CBCT + histology + PROMs for STA at implant placement
- Both CTG and VCMX effective for STT gain vs no augmentation
- CTG = superior thickness; VCMX = comparable PROMs, lower morbidity
- Histological evidence of VCMX integration at 3 months — vascularization and remodeling confirmed
- Clinical implication: VCMX is a valid patient-centered alternative when donor morbidity is to be minimized

## Methodology

3-arm parallel RCT; Aristotle University Thessaloniki; maxillary anterior implant, ridge ≤6 mm; n=25 (CTG n=8, VCMX n=9, control n=8); STT by ultrasonography, BBT by CBCT; histological biopsy at 3 months; PROMs at 2 weeks; 6-month follow-up.
