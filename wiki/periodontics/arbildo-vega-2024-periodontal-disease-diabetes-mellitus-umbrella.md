## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (18 systematic reviews, 2009–2023, AMSTAR-2 appraised; 296 primary studies, 17.26% overlap) of the association between periodontal disease (PD) and diabetes mellitus (T1DM/T2DM/GDM).

11 of the included SRs reported a PD–DM association vs 2 reporting none; meta-analytic pooled estimates ranged RR 1.26 (1.12–1.41) to 1.86 (1.25–2.77) and OR 1.69 (0.68–4.21) to 2.59 (2.12–3.15), with diabetics showing worse plaque index, gingival index, CAL, probing depth, and BOP.

High-confidence evidence supports a (likely bidirectional) PD–DM association across all diabetes types, but variable SR reporting quality (13/18 high confidence) and high primary-study overlap temper certainty; hypertension is out of scope.

## 세줄요약

치주질환(Periodontal Disease, PD)과 당뇨병(1형·2형·임신성) 연관을 다룬 18편 체계적 고찰(Systematic Review, SR)의 엄브렐라 리뷰(2009–2023, AMSTAR-2 평가, 원저 296편·중복 17.26%).

포함 SR 중 11편이 PD–DM 연관을, 2편은 무연관을 보고; 메타분석 통합치는 상대위험도(Relative Risk, RR) 1.26(1.12–1.41)~1.86(1.25–2.77), 교차비(Odds Ratio, OR) 1.69(0.68–4.21)~2.59(2.12–3.15) 범위로, 당뇨 환자에서 치태지수·치은지수·임상부착소실(Clinical Attachment Level, CAL)·탐침깊이·탐침시출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP)이 모두 악화.

모든 당뇨 유형에서 PD–DM 연관을 high-confidence로 지지하나, SR 보고 품질 편차(18편 중 13편만 high)와 높은 원저 중복이 확실성을 제한; 고혈압(Hypertension)은 다루지 않음.

## Summary

This umbrella review (Healthcare 2024, PROSPERO CRD42023464043) consolidates 18 systematic reviews published 2009–2023 on the association between periodontal disease and diabetes mellitus, appraising each with AMSTAR-2. Across constituent SRs, 11 reported a positive PD–DM association and 2 reported none; six SRs with meta-analysis yielded pooled relative risks of 1.26–1.86 and odds ratios of 1.69–2.59. Diabetics consistently showed worse periodontal parameters — plaque index, gingival index, clinical attachment level, probing depth, bleeding on probing, and fewer teeth. The authors conclude, with high confidence, that periodontal disease is associated with type 1, type 2, and gestational diabetes, echoing the EFP consensus, while cautioning that only 13 of 18 SRs reached high confidence and that 17.26% primary-study overlap inflates apparent consistency. The paper addresses the diabetes axis only; it does not evaluate hypertension.

## Key Contributions

- Largest and most current AMSTAR-2-graded synthesis of the PD–DM association, correcting prior umbrella reviews that diluted focus by mixing in periodontal-therapy-efficacy studies.
- Parameter-level stratification (plaque index, gingival index, CAL, probing depth, BOP, number of teeth, prevalence) instead of one pooled effect.
- Diabetes-type stratification: T1DM (4 SRs), GDM (3 SRs, all high confidence), general DM (10 SRs, 8 high confidence); one SR found association with T2DM but not T1DM on CAL and probing depth.
- Methodological-quality map: 13 high / 1 low / 4 critically low, flagging AMSTAR-2 critical domains 2, 4, 7, 9, 13, 15 as recurrent weaknesses.

## Methodology

- **Databases**: PubMed, Cochrane, SciELO, Scopus + gray literature (OpenGrey, Google Scholar) to 20 August 2023; PRISMA-P / PRIO-harms reporting.
- **Inclusion**: systematic reviews (± meta-analysis) of the PD–DM association, no time/language limit. Narrative/rapid reviews, primary intervention/observational/preclinical studies, and non-research items excluded.
- **Screening**: 572 → 519 de-duplicated → 12 full-text → +2 (other umbrella reviews) +4 (other) → **18 SRs**. Rayyan; dual independent reviewers with third-reviewer tiebreak at screening, extraction, and quality assessment.
- **Quality**: AMSTAR-2 (Kappa 0.85) in duplicate, graded per Shea et al.
- **Overlap**: 296 primary studies; 17.26% in >1 SR (35 twice, 8 three times, 7 four times). CCA not reported.

## Results

| Parameter | SRs (assoc.) | Meta-analytic effect (range across SRs) |
|---|---|---|
| General association | 11 pos / 2 neg | RR 1.26 (1.12–1.41) → 1.86 (1.25–2.77); OR 1.69 (0.68–4.21) → 2.59 (2.12–3.15) |
| Plaque index | 3 | MD 0.20 (0.18–0.23); SMD 0.54 → 0.71 |
| Gingival index | 2 | SMD 0.46 (0.08–0.84) → 0.63 (0.39–0.87) |
| Clinical attachment level | 4 (1: T2DM only) | MD 0.26 (0.00–0.53) → 1.00 (0.15–1.84); SMD 0.47 → 0.82 |
| Number of teeth | 1 | MD −2.14 (−2.87 to −1.40) |
| Prevalence | 3 | OR 0.19 (0.08–0.37) → 1.85 (1.61–2.11) |
| Probing depth | 3 (1: T2DM only) | MD 0.11 (−0.03–0.25) → 0.46 (0.01–0.91); SMD 0.36 → 0.67 |
| Bleeding on probing | 3 | MD 7.90 (4.24–11.56); SMD 0.32 → 0.65 |

- **Background risk anchor**: poorly controlled diabetes carries an ~86% higher risk of periodontitis vs non-diabetic / well-controlled.
- **Confidence**: 13/18 SRs high, 1 low, 4 critically low.
- **Clinical takeaway**: accurate anamnesis + early diagnosis; least-invasive periodontal management (scaling and root planing, lasers, local antimicrobials).
