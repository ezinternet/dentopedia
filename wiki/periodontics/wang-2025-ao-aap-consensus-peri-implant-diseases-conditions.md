## Three-line Summary

AO/AAP 2024 international consensus (Oak Brook, Illinois; structured discussions 14–16 Aug 2024) synthesizing 8 pre-commissioned systematic reviews into a unified evidence-based framework for peri-implant disease (PID) prevention and management.

Group 1 identifies key risk factors: periodontitis history, smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, poor biofilm control, obesity, implant malposition, unfavorable prosthetics, and thin mucosal phenotype; Group 2 provides a staged treatment algorithm from non-surgical debridement + risk-factor control for mucositis, escalating to individualized non-reconstructive or reconstructive surgery for advanced peri-implantitis.

This consensus supersedes earlier 2017/2018 positions on practical management; it acknowledges that advanced peri-implantitis surgical outcomes remain low-predictability and that some protocol recommendations may still reflect industry influence.

## 세줄요약

AO/AAP 2024 국제 합의(Oak Brook, Illinois; 2024-08-14–16): 임플란트 주위 질환(PID) 예방·관리를 위한 통합 근거기반 framework을 8개 사전 의뢰 SR을 종합해 제시.

Group 1 핵심 위험인자: 치주염 병력·흡연·조절 불량 당뇨·불량 구강위생·비만·임플란트 malposition·부적절 보철·얇은 점막 표현형; Group 2: mucositis는 비외과 데브리망+위험인자 관리로 치료 시작, 진행 peri-implantitis는 결손 형태에 따라 비재건 또는 재건 외과.

2017/2018 이전 합의를 실용적 관리 측면에서 대체하나, 진행 peri-implantitis의 외과 예후 예측성은 여전히 낮고 일부 프로토콜은 산업계 영향 가능성을 인정함.

## Summary
AO/AAP 2024 international consensus (Oak Brook, Illinois) synthesizing 8 systematic reviews into unified evidence-based recommendations for peri-implant disease prevention and management. Published in J Periodontol May 2025.

## Key Contributions
Provides the most current AO/AAP joint position on peri-implant diseases — integrating systemic, behavioral, local, and prosthetic risk factors with a staged treatment algorithm (mucositis → non-surgical → surgical reconstruction). Supersedes earlier 2017/2018 consensus on practical management points.

## Methodology
Eight pre-commissioned systematic reviews grouped into two domains: (Group 1) risk factors for mucositis, peri-implantitis, soft tissue deformities, prosthetic factors and MBL; (Group 2) therapeutic strategies — non-surgical debridement, surface decontamination, surgical interventions (non-reconstructive + reconstructive). Structured consensus discussions Aug 14–16, 2024.

## Results
Key risk factors identified: periodontitis history, smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, poor biofilm control, obesity, implant malposition, unfavorable prosthetics, suboptimal peri-implant soft tissue phenotype. Mucositis: non-surgical debridement + risk factor control effective. Peri-implantitis: starts with mucositis-style approach; advanced cases require surgery (non-reconstructive or reconstructive depending on defect morphology).

## Limitations
Consensus-based — does not generate new primary data. Reflects expert weighting where evidence is mixed. Treatment algorithms still acknowledge low predictability for advanced peri-implantitis surgical outcomes. Some protocol recommendations remain industry-influenced.
