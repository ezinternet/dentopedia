## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (SR+MA; J Periodontal Res 2026; PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO-registered; RCTs searched to April 15, 2025; RoB 2; GRADE) of enamel matrix derivative (EMD) across four indications: 67 RCTs (9 nonsurgical, 41 intrabony, 4 furcation, 13 recession) from 4406 records, 2552 participants, 3521 defects.

In intrabony defects EMD added 1.00 mm PPD reduction (95% CI 0.63-1.38) and 1.14 mm CAL gain (0.83-1.46) over open flap debridement (OFD; 20 RCTs, I2 87%); nonsurgical adjunct gave +0.30 mm PPD but a non-significant +0.21 mm CAL, root coverage and EMD-vs-graft/membrane comparisons were mostly non-significant, and GRADE certainty was very low for every intrabony comparison.

EMD is supported for intrabony regeneration versus OFD, but the review's abstract, Results and Discussion disagree on the EMD plus xenograft effect (see warning), so the graft-combination question is unresolved; furcation, nonsurgical and root coverage use need higher-quality RCTs.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (SR+MA; J Periodontal Res 2026; PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO 등록; 2025-04-15까지 RCT; RoB 2; GRADE): 법랑기질유도체 (Enamel Matrix Derivative, EMD)의 4개 적응증 — 검색 4406건에서 67 RCT (비수술 9, 골내결손 41, 치근이개부 4, 퇴축 13), 참여자 2552명, 결손 3521개.

골내결손에서 EMD는 개방 판막 소파술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD) 대비 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 1.00 mm (95% CI 0.63-1.38), 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 1.14 mm (0.83-1.46) 추가 이득 (20 RCT, I2 87%); 비수술 보조는 PPD +0.30 mm이나 CAL +0.21 mm는 비유의, 치근 피개와 EMD 대 이식재·차폐막 비교는 대부분 비유의, 골내결손 비교 전부 GRADE 근거 확실성 매우 낮음.

OFD 대비 골내결손 재생은 EMD가 뒷받침되나, 이종골 병용 효과는 초록·결과·고찰이 서로 어긋나 (경고 참조) 이식재 병용 질문은 미해결이며, 치근이개부·비수술·치근 피개는 더 높은 질의 RCT가 필요.

> [!warning] Internal discrepancy in the paper (abstract vs Results vs Discussion)
> On EMD plus bone graft versus EMD alone, three sections of the same paper give three versions. **Abstract:** no significant improvement "except greater CAL gain (0.79) when combined with xenografts". **Results 5.4.3:** pooled EMD+BG vs EMD gave PPD +0.26 mm (95% CI -0.02 to 0.53, ns) and CAL +0.33 mm (0.02-0.64, significant); no significant subgroup differences by graft type; some xenograft/alloplast studies showed *reduced* CAL gain vs EMD alone (small, ns); the figure 0.79 does not appear in Results. **Discussion 6.2.2:** states EMD vs EMD+BG "did not lead to significant gains in PPD or CAL", yet also that subgroup differences in CAL by graft type were statistically significant, with EMD + xenograft showing *reduced* CAL gain. These cannot all be true; this page reports the Results values as the primary data and does not adjudicate. Publication bias was strongly suspected for this comparison (GRADE very low).

## Summary

Miron and colleagues first review the biology of EMD: enamel matrix proteins, secreted by Hertwig's epithelial root sheath, deposit on the developing root before cementum forms, and a porcine-derived, amelogenin-rich preparation (EMD) was built to mimic this. Preclinical bench data fix the handling rules: EMD needs roughly 3-5 min to adsorb onto graft particles and blood displaces it (premix at least 5 min, blood-free), and on root surfaces it adsorbs well only on a rinsed, dried (EDTA-conditioned) blood-free surface. Human histology shows EMD persisting on the root up to 4 weeks, with collagen-rich tissue forming at 2-6 weeks.

The systematic review (67 RCTs from 4406 records; 2552 participants, 3521 defects) then shows a clear, if heterogeneous, benefit only for intrabony defects versus OFD (+1.00 mm PPD, +1.14 mm CAL at 12 months). Graft combinations, EMD vs GTR and root coverage comparisons are small or non-significant, GRADE certainty is very low for all intrabony comparisons and low for two recession ones, and most RCTs carry high risk of bias. The paper's own abstract, Results and Discussion disagree on EMD plus xenograft (see warning), so no graft-combination claim should rest on this review alone.

## Key Contributions

- Single pooled estimate of EMD efficacy across nonsurgical, intrabony, furcation and recession indications, with GRADE per comparison
- Intrabony benchmark: +1.00 mm PPD and +1.14 mm CAL versus OFD (20 RCTs, I2 87%)
- Non-resorbable GTR membranes gave 1.08 mm greater PPD reduction than EMD (subgroup); overall EMD vs GTR not significant
- Handling biology: blood-free, dried, conditioned root surface and blood-free premixing with grafts are the practical prerequisites for EMD adsorption
- Documents an unresolved internal inconsistency on EMD + xenograft

## Methodology

PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251088129. PubMed, Scopus and Web of Science to April 15, 2025, plus hand search; RCTs only. Two reviewers screened and extracted independently; RoB 2 by two reviewers; GRADE by one. Site-level weighted mean differences; meta-analysis when at least three studies; random-effects preferred under moderate to high heterogeneity; subgroups by graft and membrane type; funnel plot and Egger test with at least seven studies. Time points: 3 months (nonsurgical) and 12 months (intrabony, furcation, recession). Change-score SDs used an assumed correlation r = 0.7.

- **Subgroups**: BG + EMD vs BG and EMD + BG vs EMD showed no significant subgroup differences by graft type in Results; for EMD vs GTR, non-resorbable membranes gave 1.08 mm greater PPD reduction than EMD while CAL subgroup differences were ns
- **Nonsurgical**: no publication bias on funnel plot or Egger test; Discussion attributes the weak effect partly to difficulty keeping the root dry and blood-free in MINST or flapless protocols (authors' interpretation)
- **Furcation**: 4 RCTs (Class II), no meta-analysis because of heterogeneous adjuncts, techniques and classifications
- **Recession (recession depth)**: CAF vs CAF + EMD ns (4 RCTs); CAF + CTG vs CAF + EMD random-effects -0.20 mm (-0.70 to 0.31), ns, I2 87.3% (common-effect -0.43 mm favored CTG); CAF + CTG vs CAF + CTG + EMD ns (5 RCTs). GRADE very low for CAF vs CAF + EMD and low for the two CTG comparisons
- **Risk of bias**: intrabony 34 high / 3 some concerns / 4 low; most other indications also dominated by high or some-concern ratings

## Limitations

- **Abstract vs Results vs Discussion (EMD + graft)**: the abstract's "except greater CAL gain (0.79) with xenografts" is not found in Results, which report pooled EMD + BG vs EMD CAL +0.33 mm (significant) and no significant subgroup differences; the Discussion says EMD vs EMD + BG gave no significant PPD or CAL gain but also that CAL subgroup differences by graft type were significant (xenograft reduced CAL gain). The three versions conflict and are not reconciled here; discrepancy logged
- Abstract's "no enhancements in CAL" for nonsurgical therapy agrees with the full text (+0.21 mm, ns)
- Most RCTs high risk of bias; very low certainty for every intrabony comparison; very high heterogeneity (I2 up to 90.7%) without meta-regression
- Publication bias strongly suspected for EMD vs EMD + BG
- Secondary outcomes (adverse events, patient-reported outcomes, radiographic bone gain, pocket closure, tooth loss) not analyzed; GRADE by a single assessor; imputed r = 0.7 for change-score SDs
- The membrane-exposure figure (28.8% of defects, five studies) is quoted from Rojas et al., a separate systematic review, and was not re-derived here
- The "gold standard" statement for EMD in intrabony regeneration is the authors' Discussion position, not a pooled result
