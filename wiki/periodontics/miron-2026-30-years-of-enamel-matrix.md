## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (SR+MA; J Periodontal Res 2026; RCTs to April 15, 2025; RoB2) of enamel matrix derivative (EMD) across four indications: 67 RCTs (9 nonsurgical, 41 intrabony, 4 furcation, 13 recession), 2552 participants, 3521 defects.

In intrabony defects EMD added 1.00 mm PPD reduction and 1.14 mm CAL gain over open flap debridement (OFD); EMD plus bone graft added nothing significant except +0.79 CAL with xenografts; root coverage gains were non-significant, nonsurgical adjunct gave +0.30 mm PPD and no CAL benefit.

EMD is supported for intrabony regeneration; evidence for nonsurgical, root coverage and furcation use remains limited and needs higher-quality RCTs (abstract-only: no heterogeneity or bias detail retrieved).

## Summary

Miron and colleagues review the biological basis of EMD, which in preclinical work induces new cementum, periodontal ligament (PDL) and alveolar bone, especially on dry, conditioned root surfaces with minimal blood contamination, and then pool RCTs across four indications. In 67 RCTs (2552 participants, 3521 defects), EMD gave the clearest benefit in intrabony defects versus OFD (+1.00 mm PPD reduction, +1.14 mm CAL gain). Adding EMD to bone grafts did not improve clinical outcomes, except for greater CAL gain (0.79) with xenografts. The authors conclude EMD is one of the few biologics with human histologic evidence of periodontal regeneration, but other indications need further RCTs.

## Key Contributions

- Single pooled estimate of EMD efficacy across nonsurgical, intrabony, furcation and recession indications
- Intrabony benchmark: +1.00 mm PPD and +1.14 mm CAL versus OFD
- Negative finding for EMD plus bone graft (only xenograft combination improved CAL, +0.79)
- Non-resorbable membranes gave 1.08 mm greater PPD reduction than EMD

## Methodology

Random-effects meta-analyses of RCTs searched to April 15, 2025; two reviewers independently screened, extracted data and assessed risk of bias with RoB2. Studies categorized by indication: nonsurgical treatment, intrabony regeneration, furcation regeneration, gingival recession root coverage.

## Results
