## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (Periodontology 2000, 2026): 14 RCTs on adjunctive hyaluronic acid (HA) in regenerative periodontal surgery for intrabony defects — primary outcome PPD reduction; secondary CAL gain, gingival recession, and RDD reduction.

Adjunctive HA reduced PPD by an additional 0.7–1.2 mm and improved CAL by 0.7–1.1 mm vs control (most consistent at 12 months); RDD reduced approximately 0.7–1.0 mm; HA alone or combined with membranes showed significant benefit, but HA combined with bone substitutes showed no added benefit.

EMD comparisons revealed comparable or slightly superior results for EMD; heterogeneity and study quality require cautious interpretation of pooled estimates.

## Summary

This 2026 SR+MA from Wang's group (University of Michigan) is the first to systematically pool clinical and radiographic outcomes of adjunctive hyaluronic acid (HA) use in **regenerative periodontal surgery** for intrabony defects. Fourteen RCTs met inclusion criteria. HA was compared to control (surgery without HA) and in a subset of studies to EMD.

**Core finding**: Adjunctive HA provides a statistically significant, clinically meaningful additional benefit over control in both PPD reduction (0.7–1.2 mm) and CAL gain (0.7–1.1 mm), with effects most consistent at 12 months. Radiographically, RDD decreased by approximately 0.7–1.0 mm more than control, though heterogeneity was notable.

**Critical subgroup**: The benefit is specifically observed when HA is used **alone** or combined with **barrier membranes**. When HA is added to **bone substitute materials**, no additional benefit is detected. This distinction has direct protocol-selection implications.

**EMD head-to-head**: HA does not outperform EMD; EMD results are comparable or slightly superior, consistent with Mahintach 2026 (3 RCTs, HA non-inferior for CAL at 12 months but heterogeneous for PPD).

## Key Results

| Outcome | Additional effect (HA vs control) |
|---|---|
| PPD reduction | ~0.7–1.2 mm |
| CAL gain | ~0.7–1.1 mm |
| RDD reduction | ~0.7–1.0 mm (heterogeneity noted) |

**Subgroup by regenerative protocol**:
- HA alone: **significant benefit**
- HA + barrier membrane: **significant benefit**
- HA + bone substitute material: **no significant added benefit**

**EMD vs HA**: EMD comparable or slightly superior

## Clinical Takeaway

HA is a viable adjunct to periodontal regenerative surgery for intrabony defects when used alone or with membranes. Its addition to bone graft protocols confers no advantage. EMD remains the reference biologic agent. The 14-RCT base is the strongest published evidence for HA in this indication, but heterogeneity requires caution in applying pooled estimates to individual patients.
