## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (18 systematic reviews/meta-analyses, JBI + PRISMA 2020 methodology, PROSPERO CRD42022335209) evaluating the relationship between chronic kidney disease (CKD) and periodontal disease, searched across 5 databases through April 2022.

Findings indicate a bidirectional relationship between CKD and periodontal disease, with periodontal treatment showing potential to improve CKD outcomes, though the quality and heterogeneity of the underlying systematic reviews varied considerably.

Abstract-only source (no PMC full text available): quantitative pooled estimates and SR-by-SR quality appraisal are not captured here; clinical recommendations were generated for medical, dental, and aged-care professionals managing periodontal health in CKD patients.

## Summary

This umbrella review addresses a long-suspected but methodologically fragmented literature: the relationship between chronic kidney disease (CKD) and periodontal disease. Rather than running a new meta-analysis, the authors applied Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) umbrella review methodology and PRISMA 2020 reporting to appraise the quality of 18 existing systematic reviews (out of 371 screened) spanning three sub-questions — association, treatment effect, and quality-of-life impact. The review concludes there is a bidirectional CKD–periodontitis relationship and that periodontal treatment may improve CKD outcomes, while explicitly flagging that the quality and consistency of the underlying systematic reviews is variable. The paper's main deliverable is a set of clinically oriented management recommendations for maintaining periodontal health in CKD patients, aimed separately at medical professionals, dental professionals, and aged-care workers.

## Key Contributions

- Umbrella-level quality appraisal (JBI methodology) of the entire CKD–periodontal disease systematic review literature as of April 2022, rather than a single new systematic review or meta-analysis.
- Structures the evidence into three distinct clinical questions: (1) CKD–periodontitis association, (2) effect of periodontal treatment on CKD outcomes, (3) oral health-related quality of life (OHRQoL) in kidney failure.
- Produces role-specific clinical recommendations (medical professionals, dental professionals, aged-care workers) rather than a single generic conclusion — useful for interdisciplinary CKD care pathways.

## Methodology

- Design: umbrella review of systematic reviews/meta-analyses; JBI umbrella review methodology + PRISMA 2020 reporting.
- Protocol pre-registered on JBI and PROSPERO (CRD42022335209) prior to starting the review.
- Databases: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, Dentistry & Oral Science Source, searched through April 2022.
- Inclusion criteria: systematic reviews/meta-analyses on the relationship between CKD and periodontitis, or the impact of periodontal treatment on CKD outcomes.
- Screening: 371 studies identified → 18 systematic reviews included (10 on association, 5 on periodontal treatment effect, 2 on both, 1 qualitative on OHRQoL).

## Results

- Bidirectional relationship confirmed across the pooled body of 18 systematic reviews between CKD and periodontal disease.
- Periodontal treatment demonstrated potential to improve CKD outcomes in the reviews assessing treatment effect, but effect consistency was limited by underlying study heterogeneity.
- One included systematic review specifically addressed OHRQoL in patients with kidney failure, reinforcing that periodontal/oral disease burden affects quality of life in this population, not just biochemical/renal markers.
- No new pooled effect estimate is generated by this umbrella review itself (abstract-level source; full text would carry SR-by-SR AMSTAR/quality scores and specific numeric findings from the 18 reviews).
