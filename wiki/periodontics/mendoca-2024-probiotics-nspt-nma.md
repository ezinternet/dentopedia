## Three-line Summary

Network meta-analysis of 33 RCTs (1290 patients) comparing probiotic strains as adjuncts to professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR) in periodontitis; Lactobacillus-based interventions showed the most consistent benefits across PPD, CAL, and BOP outcomes.

Short-term PPD reduction reached MD=1.48 mm (95% CI 1.24–1.72) with SRP+Lactobacillus combinations; long-term benefit persisted only with Lactobacillus reuteri (MD=0.80 mm; 95% CI 0.30–1.29, p=0.0016 beyond 3 months); probiotic therapy duration ≤1 month appeared sufficient.

Evidence certainty is very low for 92% of comparisons due to star-network topology (9% direct evidence) and heterogeneity — results point consistently toward L. reuteri as the best-supported adjunct probiotic for periodontitis.

## Background

Periodontitis affects ~50% of the global population and represents the second leading cause of tooth loss after dental caries. While professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR/SRP) is the gold standard for non-surgical management, it does not fully eliminate dysbiotic periodontal biofilm from deep pockets. Probiotics have been proposed as adjuncts to exploit competitive exclusion, antimicrobial compound production (reuterin, bacteriocin), and immunomodulation as mechanisms to restore eubiosis.

Prior systematic reviews reported heterogeneous results for individual probiotic species, with network meta-analysis offering the first opportunity for indirect head-to-head comparison across all studied regimens simultaneously.

## Methods

- **Design**: Systematic review + network meta-analysis (frequentist weighted least-squares model)
- **PROSPERO**: CRD42021250678; PRISMA extension for NMA
- **Search**: MEDLINE, LILACS, Cochrane CENTRAL + grey literature to November 2023
- **Inclusion**: Adults ≥18 years, systemically healthy, untreated periodontitis, RCTs, ≥10 patients, ≥1 month follow-up; NO split-mouth designs, systemic disease, PST patients, children/elderly
- **Outcomes**: Primary — PPD, CAL; Secondary — BOP, PI, CFU
- **Stratification**: Short-term (≤3 months) vs long-term (>3 months)
- **Inconsistency**: SIDE (local) + global incoherence models; no inconsistency detected (I=0% for PPD/CAL)
- **Confidence**: CINeMA framework; 30% of studies had high risk of bias

## Results

### PPD (Short-term ≤3 months)
9 probiotic interventions significantly better than Splac (SRP+placebo):
- **Best**: SRP+*L. reuteri* MD=1.48 mm (95% CI 1.24–1.72, p<0.0001; 95% PdI -0.08 to 3.04)
- **Range**: MD 0.18–1.48 mm across 9 significant interventions

### PPD (Long-term >3 months)
- Only *Lactobacillus* retained significance: MD=0.80 mm (95% CI 0.30–1.29, p=0.0016)
- Sensitivity (therapy ≤1 month): *L. reuteri* MD=1.16 mm (95% CI 1.06–1.25, I²=0%)

### CAL (Short-term)
16 interventions significantly better than Splac:
- Range MD 0.16–1.05 mm
- **Best**: SRP+*Lactobacillus*+multi: MD=1.05 mm (95% CI 1.03–1.07, p<0.0001; I²=0%)

### CAL (Long-term)
- 2 significant: *L. reuteri* MD=0.43 mm (95% CI 0.24–0.62, p<0.0001); *L. rhamnosus* MD=0.32 mm

### BOP (Short-term)
4 combinations significant: MD range 13.26–33.00%

### BOP (Long-term)
4 combinations significant: MD range 5.02–23.31%

### Rankings (P-score)
- *L. reuteri* = best probiotic across all outcomes at all time points (P-score ~1.0 for PPD short-term/BOP/CAL long-term)

### Evidence Quality
- 92% of comparisons: very low certainty (CINeMA)
- Star-network: PPD 9% direct evidence, CAL 8.92%, BOP 13%

## Clinical Takeaway

*Lactobacillus reuteri* (administered as adjunct to SRP) represents the best-supported probiotic for periodontitis in 2024, with clinically meaningful short-term PPD gains (~1.5 mm) and sustained long-term benefits (>3 months). Therapy duration of ≤1 month appears sufficient to capture benefit — longer durations do not add incrementally. However, the very low certainty evidence, star-network topology with heavy reliance on indirect comparisons, and potential pharma funding bias for L. reuteri all warrant caution before adopting as routine practice.
