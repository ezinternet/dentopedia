## Three-line Summary

Single-blind RCT (n=36 completers, fixed-orthodontic gingivitis patients, 3 groups of 12: conventional toothbrush [C-TB] + modified Bass, orthodontic toothbrush [O-TB] + Bass, single-tufted toothbrush [ST-TB] + Solo/circular technique, 3-month follow-up after professional debridement).

By 3 months, ST-TB maintained significantly lower plaque (TQHI) and bleeding on probing (BOP) than both other groups (p<0.001/p<0.05), while O-TB's early plaque advantage over C-TB (seen at weeks 1 and 6) had disappeared; ST-TB also showed lower gingival index (GI) throughout, and posterior teeth scored worse than anterior across all groups.

Small single-center sample (n=36) with unsupervised home brushing and technique bundled to device type (so brush vs. technique effects are not fully separable), but the durability of ST-TB's benefit to 3 months — versus O-TB's fading effect — is a clinically actionable signal for orthodontic-gingivitis patients specifically.

## Summary

This single-blind RCT (Istanbul Okan University, Sept 2023–Apr 2024; NCT06510179) compared three toothbrush types — conventional (C-TB), orthodontic (O-TB, V-groove bristle design), and single-tufted (ST-TB) — each paired with a technique matched to the brush (modified Bass, Bass, and Solo/single-tooth circular respectively) in **36 completers with gingivitis and fixed orthodontic appliances**, following baseline professional mechanical debridement. All groups improved significantly from baseline on TQHI (plaque), GI, and BOP, but the trajectories diverged: O-TB's early (week 1–6) plaque and bleeding advantage over C-TB faded to parity by month 3, whereas ST-TB maintained the lowest plaque, gingival index, and bleeding scores of all three groups at every time point through 3 months. No group showed a significant PPD difference from another at any time point. Posterior teeth consistently scored worse than anterior teeth across all groups and time points, though ST-TB retained its relative advantage regardless of tooth location.

## Key Contributions

- First head-to-head, technique-matched comparison of conventional, orthodontic, and single-tufted toothbrushes specifically in a **fixed-orthodontic gingivitis** population (prior single-tufted-brush studies used periodontally healthy subjects and 72-hour windows).
- Extends the observation period to 3 months, revealing that O-TB's plaque advantage over C-TB is **not durable**, while ST-TB's is.
- First anterior-vs-posterior stratified analysis for toothbrush-type comparisons in this population.
- Provides a concrete alternative signal to the powered-vs-manual literature: **brush design + technique combination** (not just powered vs. manual) can meaningfully affect long-term plaque/gingivitis control in orthodontic gingivitis patients.

## Methodology

- Single-blind, randomized, controlled, parallel-group trial; CONSORT-compliant; ethics approval Istanbul University Faculty of Dentistry (2023/30 Rev-1/681).
- 59 recruited → 36 completers (12/group), 18–35y, systemically healthy, non-smokers, ≥22 bracketed teeth, gingivitis (periodontally healthy or periodontitis patients excluded).
- Sample size via G*Power (f=1.703, 95% CI, 80% power) → minimum n=10/group; 14/group targeted for 10% attrition.
- Baseline professional mechanical debridement (ultrasonic + hand instruments) for all patients before randomization.
- Group-specific oral hygiene instruction:
  - **C-TB**: modified Bass technique (45° bristle angle at gingival margin, rolling motion toward occlusal, repeated occlusal to brackets).
  - **O-TB**: Bass technique (45° angle, short vibrating back-and-forth without lifting bristles).
  - **ST-TB**: Solo/single-tooth circular technique around brackets and gingival margin.
  - All groups also used an interdental brush; other oral hygiene products restricted.
- Outcomes (6 sites/tooth, single calibrated examiner, ICC 0.99): TQHI (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein plaque index), GI (gingival index), BOP (bleeding on probing), PPD (probing pocket depth) at baseline, week 1, week 6, month 3.
- Statistics: Shapiro-Wilk, one-way ANOVA/Kruskal-Wallis (between-group), paired t-test/Wilcoxon (within-group pre-post), two-way mixed-design ANOVA for group×time and group×location×time; α=0.05.

## Results

| Parameter | Week 1–6 | Month 3 |
|---|---|---|
| TQHI (plaque) | O-TB, ST-TB < C-TB | ST-TB < C-TB ≈ O-TB (p<0.001) |
| GI | ST-TB < C-TB, O-TB (all timepoints); C-TB ≈ O-TB | ST-TB lowest |
| BOP | O-TB, ST-TB < C-TB | Only ST-TB < C-TB, O-TB |
| PPD | No significant between-group difference at any time point (p=0.424) | — |

- Baseline: no significant between-group differences on any parameter (p>0.05); no significant age/sex/treatment-duration imbalance.
- Posterior teeth scored significantly worse than anterior on TQHI/GI/BOP across all groups and all time points; ST-TB's advantage held regardless of location, though its own posterior scores were worse than its anterior scores (mirroring all groups).
- No adverse effects reported in any group.
