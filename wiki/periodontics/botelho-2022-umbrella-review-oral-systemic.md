## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 293 systematic reviews with meta-analysis (855 comparisons) evaluating the bidirectional association between oral diseases and systemic noncommunicable diseases (NCDs), registered PROSPERO CRD42022300740.

28 NCDs — including 5 cancer types, diabetes mellitus, cardiovascular disease, rheumatoid arthritis, inflammatory bowel disease, PCOS, obesity, and asthma — showed strong-evidence association with oral disease (predominantly periodontitis); only 10.3% of included meta-analyses reached high/moderate AMSTAR2 quality, but fail-safe-number analysis found 97.5% of suggestive-to-strong associations unlikely to be reversed by future studies.

Clinical implication: periodontal disease is the dominant driver of oral-systemic associations, but almost all evidence is observational — only periodontal-treatment→systemic-inflammation and endodontic-treatment→cardiovascular-disease reached strong intervention-level evidence, so causal treat-to-prevent-NCD claims remain unproven.

## Summary

This landmark umbrella review (systematic review of systematic reviews) synthesizes the evidence base linking oral health to systemic noncommunicable disease (NCD) risk. Searching five databases through December 2021, the authors identified 293 eligible systematic reviews with meta-analysis, encompassing 855 meta-analytic comparisons. Using a four-tier evidence-grading scheme (strong / highly suggestive / suggestive / weak) combined with AMSTAR 2 methodological-quality appraisal and Rosenberg's fail-safe number (FSN), the review grades both directions of the oral-systemic relationship: NCDs as risk factors for oral disease, and oral disease as a risk factor for NCDs, plus the systemic impact of oral treatment interventions.

## Key Contributions

- The most comprehensive evidence-graded map to date of oral-disease↔NCD associations (28 NCDs with strong evidence, spanning cancer, cardiometabolic, rheumatic, and neurodegenerative conditions).
- A reusable evidence-grading framework (strong/highly suggestive/suggestive/weak, per Papadimitriou et al.) combined with FSN robustness testing — useful as a template for judging future SR/MA claims in this wiki.
- Explicit separation of association direction (NCD→oral disease vs. oral disease→NCD) and of intervention-level evidence (oral treatment→systemic outcome), clarifying which claims are causal-adjacent vs. purely observational.

## Methodology

Five databases (PubMed, Cochrane, EMBASE, Web of Science, LILACS) searched to Dec 2021 plus grey literature and manual reference search. 14,861 records → 293 included SR/MA after screening (κ=0.91). AMSTAR 2 used for methodological quality (κ=0.84). Evidence graded strong/highly suggestive/suggestive/weak based on case count (>1000), meta-analytic p-value threshold (≤10⁻³ to ≤10⁻⁶), heterogeneity (I²), 95% prediction interval, and small-study/excess-significance bias. Rosenberg's fail-safe number computed via Meta-Essentials for all nominally significant meta-analyses.

## Results

- 66/855 comparisons (7.7%) reached strong evidence; 157 (18.4%) highly suggestive; 55 (6.4%) suggestive.
- **Periodontal disease → NCD (strong evidence)**: cancer (9 associations), cardiovascular disease (5), diabetes mellitus (3), adverse pregnancy outcomes (2), lower longevity (2), neurodegeneration/dementia (3), PCOS, psoriasis, altered MCH, serum CRP (1 each).
- **NCD → oral disease (strong evidence, 14 NCDs)**: depression, other mental disorders, rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, IBD, NAFLD, PCOS, MRONJ, H. pylori infection, obstructive sleep apnea, stroke, obesity, diabetes mellitus, asthma, special-needs conditions.
- **Intervention evidence**: only periodontal treatment→systemic inflammation and endodontic treatment→CVD reached strong evidence; periodontal treatment showed highly suggestive evidence for CVD, metabolic disorders, adverse pregnancy outcomes, respiratory disease, and systemic inflammation.
- **Quality caveat**: 71.4% of included SR/MA were critically low AMSTAR2 quality; only 10.3% high/moderate.
- **Robustness**: FSN indicates 97.5% of suggestive-to-strong associations are unlikely to be overturned by future null studies (median FSN 51, range 1–87,973).
