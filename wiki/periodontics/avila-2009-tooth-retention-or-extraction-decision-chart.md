## Three-line Summary

Literature-based decision-process article (Journal of Periodontology, 2009) that builds a six-level colour-coded chart for retaining versus extracting a compromised tooth.

The chart weighs individual tooth- and patient-related factors drawn from peer-reviewed English literature and book sources, and frames extraction against implant replacement.

The paper is a reference tool with no reported validation of the chart; do not cite its level thresholds as tested cut-offs.

## 세줄요약

문헌 기반 의사결정 과정 논문 (Journal of Periodontology, 2009) — 상태가 불량한 치아의 보존·발치를 판단하는 6단계 색상 차트를 제시.

영문 동료심사 논문과 교과서에서 얻은 개별 치아·환자 요인을 가중하며, 발치를 임플란트 대체와 대비해 제시.

참조 도구이며 차트에 대한 검증 보고는 없음; 단계별 기준을 검증된 cut-off로 인용하지 말 것.

## Summary

Avila and colleagues (with Misch as co-author) convert the retain-or-extract decision into a structured chart. The contribution is the decision structure itself. Its value for the wiki is as a framework for the first-stage choice before any implant planning.

## Key Contributions

- A six-level colour-coded retain-versus-extract chart.
- Explicit factor-by-factor justification from the literature.

## Methodology

Narrative synthesis of peer-reviewed English articles and book sources; chart constructed and justified factor by factor. No primary data.

## Results

Qualitative chart; no validation numbers in the abstract. The abstract cites the implant literature for a >90% 15-year success benchmark under ideal conditions.
