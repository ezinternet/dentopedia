## Three-line Summary

Single-blinded split-mouth RCT (n=15 patients, 47 vs 48 RT1 multiple-recession teeth, 6-month follow-up, Gazi University, Türkiye) comparing CAF+de-epithelialized gingival graft (DGG) alone vs CAF+DGG+leukocyte-PRF (L-PRF, 3-layer configuration) for multiple adjacent gingival recessions.

Root coverage at 6 months was statistically equivalent between groups (DGG 93.30%±6.97% vs DGG+PRF 95.28%±4.99%, p>0.05, all secondary clinical/periodontal parameters also NS); the DGG+PRF group had significantly less pain (days 6–7) and less discomfort (days 2, 3, 5) postoperatively, with no OHIP-14 or analgesic-use difference.

Adding L-PRF to DGG does not improve root coverage, keratinized tissue width, or gingival thickness over DGG alone — its demonstrated benefit is reduced early postoperative morbidity, not superior root coverage.

## Summary

This single-blinded, split-mouth RCT from Gazi University (Türkiye) asks a narrow but clinically pointed question: once a coronally advanced flap (CAF) is already grafted with a de-epithelialized gingival graft (DGG), does layering leukocyte-PRF (L-PRF) membranes around the graft add any further root-coverage benefit? Fifteen patients with multiple adjacent RT1 gingival recessions received DGG on one side and DGG+3-layer-L-PRF on the contralateral side (6-week stagger), with clinical parameters and patient-reported outcomes tracked to 6 months.

The answer is unambiguous: no. Mean root coverage (RC%) at 6 months was statistically indistinguishable — 93.30%±6.97% (DGG) vs 95.28%±4.99% (DGG+PRF), p>0.05 — and every secondary clinical/soft-tissue parameter (recession depth/width, keratinized tissue width, gingival thickness, PD, CAL, PI, GI, BOP) was likewise non-significant between groups at every follow-up point. **Do not read this as "L-PRF improves root coverage" — it does not, in this design.** What L-PRF did deliver was a patient-experience benefit: significantly lower VAS pain on postoperative days 6–7 and significantly lower VAS discomfort on days 2, 3, and 5, with no difference in bleeding, analgesic consumption, or OHIP-14 scores (the latter likely because OHIP-14 was only sampled at day 14+, missing the early window where the VAS effect actually occurred).

## Key Contributions

- Isolates the marginal effect of L-PRF **when added to an existing autologous graft (DGG)**, as opposed to the more commonly studied PRF-vs-CTG or PRF-vs-bare-CAF comparisons — a distinct and clinically relevant question ("is PRF worth adding on top of a graft I'm already placing?").
- Uses a 3-layer PRF sandwich configuration (membranes above and below the DGG) intended to fully envelop the graft and approximate the periosteum, a specific stacking protocol.
- Captures postoperative morbidity with fine time-resolution (daily VAS pain/bleeding/discomfort + analgesic diary through day 14, plus OHIP-14 at 14 days/3/6 months) — revealing a morbidity benefit that a coarser instrument (OHIP-14 alone) would have missed.
- Demonstrates that root-coverage efficacy and postoperative morbidity are separable outcome axes: an adjunct can fail on one and succeed on the other.

## Methodology

- Split-mouth, single-blinded RCT; CAF per Zucchelli technique (envelope flap, no vertical incisions) on both sides; 15 patients, 47 (DGG) vs 48 (DGG+PRF) teeth; 6-week interval between contralateral surgeries.
- L-PRF: anticoagulant-free venous blood, 2700 rpm × 12 min centrifugation, compressed into membranes; placed as a PRF–DGG–PRF 3-layer sandwich under the flap, secured with 5-0 resorbable sutures.
- Blinded assessor, acrylic-stent-guided measurements (Williams probe), intra-examiner reliability 0.91.
- Primary outcome: RC% = [(preop RD − postop RD)/preop RD] × 100. Secondary: RD, RW, CAL, KTW, GT, PI, GI, BOP, PD, plus OHIP-14 (Turkish version) and daily VAS pain/bleeding/discomfort + analgesic count through day 14.
- Powered for effect size 0.455 at 80%/α=0.05 → 15 patients/group (met).

## Results

- **RC% at T6**: DGG 93.30%±6.97% vs DGG+PRF 95.28%±4.99% — not significant (p=0.382); also NS at T1 (p=0.774) and T3 (p=0.087).
- RD, RW, GT, KTW: significant within-group improvement from baseline in both arms, but **no significant between-group difference** at any time point.
- PI, GI, BOP, PD, CAL: no significant between-group differences at any follow-up; both arms improved similarly within-group.
- **PROMs**: DGG+PRF had significantly lower VAS pain on days 6–7 and significantly lower VAS discomfort on days 2, 3, 5 (p<0.05 each). No between-group difference in bleeding VAS, analgesic tablet count, or OHIP-14 subscales at any time point.
- Bottom line stated by the authors: adjunctive L-PRF with DGG in multiple-recession treatment shows no clinically significant advantage in root coverage but confers a real early-postoperative comfort advantage.
