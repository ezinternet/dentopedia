## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (Periodontol 2000, 2025; PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD420250650648) of RCTs on regeneration of deep intrabony defects (≥3 mm) with ≥5 years follow-up: 1159 records yielded 17 RCTs (33 eligible cohorts in 15 unique trials; 501 defects in 356 patients), mean follow-up 9.32 ± 4.75 y (range 5-20); no trial at low risk of bias, 4 at high risk.

At ≥5 years GTR gave CAL gain 3.27 mm (95% CI 2.90-3.65) and PD reduction 4.04 mm versus baseline; biologics/grafts/both gave CAL 3.21 mm and PD 3.92 mm. GTR versus OFD favored GTR but not significantly (CAL -1.52 mm, 95% CI -3.10 to 0.06, p = 0.0601; PD -0.89 mm, 95% CI -1.99 to 0.22, p = 0.1152); tooth loss was 0.01 (<10 y) vs 0.06 (≥10 y).

Regeneration keeps clinical gains over baseline long term, yet superiority over OFD is not conclusively shown; GRADE certainty was low for CAL and PD (risk of bias, inconsistency) and high for recession, with significant publication bias in regenerative-procedure CAL/PD funnel plots.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (SR+MA; Periodontol 2000, 2025; PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD420250650648): 5년 이상 추적한 깊은 골내결손 (≥3 mm) 재생술 RCT를 검색해 1159건 중 17편 선정 (15개 고유 시험의 적격 코호트 33개; 환자 356명, 결손 501개), 평균 추적 9.32 ± 4.75년 (5-20년); 편향위험 낮음 0편, 높음 4편.

5년 이상에서 조직유도재생술 (Guided Tissue Regeneration, GTR)은 기저 대비 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 3.27 mm (95% CI 2.90-3.65) 획득, 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 4.04 mm 감소; 생물학제제·골이식·병용은 CAL 3.21 mm, PD 3.92 mm. GTR vs 개방 판막 소파술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD)은 GTR 우세 경향이나 유의하지 않음 (CAL -1.52 mm, 95% CI -3.10~0.06, p = 0.0601; PD -0.89 mm, 95% CI -1.99~0.22, p = 0.1152); 치아 상실 비율은 10년 미만 0.01, 10년 이상 0.06.

재생술의 임상 이득은 장기 유지되나 OFD 대비 우월성은 확정되지 않음; 증거 확실성은 CAL·PD 낮음 (편향위험·비일관성), 퇴축 (REC) 높음이며 재생술 CAL·PD 깔때기 도표에 유의한 출판 편향이 있음.

## Summary

Wang and colleagues (University of Michigan) pooled RCTs of regenerative procedures for deep intrabony defects (≥3 mm) with at least 5 years of follow-up. Versus baseline, GTR and biologic/graft-based regeneration produced large, sustained CAL gain and PD reduction; OFD also improved CAL (2.49 mm) and PD (3.55 mm) versus baseline, although its CAL gain was no longer significant in the 20-year subgroup. In the direct GTR-vs-OFD contrast, CAL (-1.52 mm) and PD (-0.89 mm) trended toward GTR, but both confidence intervals include zero. Grafts alone gave significantly less CAL gain than biologics alone or biologics + grafts. Pooled recession was 0.73 mm, and tooth loss rose from 0.01 (<10 y) to 0.06 (≥10 y). Meta-regression tied outcomes to follow-up time, nonresorbable membranes and baseline CAL (CAL) or M-MIST (PD). Certainty was low for CAL and PD and high for REC; the authors conclude long-term superiority over OFD is not conclusively demonstrated.

> [!warning] Abstract printed the GTR-vs-OFD CIs without their sign (resolved 2026-11-22)
> The abstract lists the GTR-vs-OFD intervals as "0.06–3.10" (CAL) and "0.22–1.99" (PD), which would exclude zero and seem to contradict the non-significant p-values. The full-text Results give the signed intervals: CAL -1.52 [-3.10; 0.06] and PD -0.89 [-1.99; 0.22]. Both include zero, consistent with p = 0.0601 and p = 0.1152. The abstract simply dropped the minus signs; there is no CI-vs-p inconsistency. (Direction: negative values = greater gain with GTR in the authors' sign convention.)

## Key Contributions

- First long-term (≥5 y, mean 9.32 y, up to 20 y) pooled estimates for intrabony regeneration, a gap in shorter-follow-up syntheses
- Shows sustained gains versus baseline (CAL 3.2-3.3 mm; PD 3.9-4.0 mm) without loss of effect across 5, 10 and 20 years for GTR
- Qualifies the OFD comparison: point estimates favor GTR but the effect is not significant, and OFD itself also gains CAL/PD versus baseline
- Quantifies tooth loss by follow-up period (0.01 vs 0.06) and flags significant publication bias
- Meta-regression: nonresorbable membrane, follow-up time and baseline CAL (CAL); nonresorbable membrane, follow-up time and M-MIST (PD); baseline REC (REC)

## Methodology

PRISMA-guided review registered in PROSPERO (CRD420250650648, 24 Feb 2025); MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Library and Scopus plus manual search, last run 9 Mar 2025. Eligibility: RCTs in adults with periodontitis, intrabony defects ≥3 mm treated by regenerative surgery, ≥5 years follow-up. Primary outcomes: PD reduction, CAL gain, REC, tooth loss. Random-effects meta-analyses stratified by follow-up period (5, 10, 20 y) or treatment, mixed-effects meta-regression (univariate then multivariate), Egger's test for funnel asymmetry, RoB 2 for bias and GRADE for certainty. No quantitative synthesis was possible for radiographic outcomes, pocket closure or patient-reported outcomes. No funding or conflicts reported.

## Results

### Study selection and characteristics
- 1159 records → 1082 screened → 270 full texts → 253 excluded (follow-up <5 y n = 200; non-RCT n = 42; animal/in vitro n = 11) → 17 RCTs (8 with ≥5 y, 9 with ≥10 y data)
- 9 parallel, 6 split-mouth, 2 mixed designs; 38 arms, of which 5 excluded (4 follow-up cohorts, 1 cohort with extraction/implant) → 33 cohorts, 15 unique trials, 501 defects, 356 patients
- 14 GTR cohorts (178 defects), 6 OFD cohorts (78 defects), biologic/graft cohorts (biologics + grafts 5 cohorts/60 defects; biologics only 4/50; grafts only 3/45); one pooled cohort (23 defects) could not be classified
- All but one trial (India) were European, mostly German; 9 academic, 3 private-practice, setting not reported in 5; all but two were single-center

### Meta-regression
- **CAL**: univariate significant for follow-up time (+0.039 per unit time), modified papilla preservation technique (MPPT), nonresorbable membrane and baseline CAL; in the multivariate model baseline CAL, nonresorbable membrane and follow-up time were confirmed, MPPT was not (estimate -0.678, 95% CI -1.584 to 0.407, p = 0.233)
- **PD**: follow-up time, MPPT and nonresorbable membrane significant univariately; multivariate confirmed follow-up time, modified minimally invasive surgical technique (M-MIST) and nonresorbable membrane
- **REC**: baseline CAL and baseline REC significant univariately; only baseline REC survived multivariate adjustment (baseline CAL p = 0.461)

### Risk of bias and certainty
- RoB 2: no included study at low overall risk; 4 high (3 from randomization, 1 from missing data); all had some concerns for deviations from intended interventions
- GRADE: low certainty for all CAL and PD analyses (risk of bias, inconsistency); high for REC; certainty for tooth retention also described as low

## Limitations

- No trial was at low risk of bias; 4 of 17 at high risk, and all had some concerns about deviations from intended interventions; GRADE certainty low for CAL and PD
- Significant publication bias (Egger) for regenerative-procedure CAL and PD and for tooth loss; GTR-vs-OFD intervals wide and include zero
- Many long-term papers omitted baseline defect features (wall configuration, intrabony depth), limiting adjustment; attrition and survivorship may favor stable cohorts
- OFD had only 6 cohorts, with significant heterogeneity in OFD CAL/PD; pooled cohort in one trial could not be classified by treatment
- Abstract-vs-full-text discrepancies: the abstract dropped the minus signs of the GTR-vs-OFD CIs (see warning above); the Conclusion states CAL and PD show "a nonsignificant trend" versus baseline, contradicting Results where both are significant (3.27 mm, 4.04 mm; p < 0.0001); the Conclusion also calls REC an improvement "in recession reduction" while Results report a pooled recession value of 0.73 mm (the Discussion calls it an increase). Numbers here follow the Results
