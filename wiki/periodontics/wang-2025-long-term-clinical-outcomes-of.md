## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (Periodontol 2000, 2025; PRISMA) of RCTs on regeneration of deep intrabony defects (≥3 mm) with ≥5 years follow-up: 17 RCTs (2004-2022), 501 defects, follow-up 5-20 years; 13 studies with some concerns and 4 at high risk of bias.

At ≥5 years GTR gave CAL gain 3.27 mm and PD reduction 4.04 mm versus baseline; biologics/grafts/both gave CAL 3.21 mm and PD 3.92 mm. GTR versus OFD favored GTR (CAL 1.52 mm, 95% CI 0.06-3.10; PD 0.89 mm, 95% CI 0.22-1.99) but not significantly (p = 0.06; p = 0.115).

Regeneration keeps clinical gains over baseline long term, yet superiority over OFD is not conclusively shown; certainty was low for CAL and PD, high for recession.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (SR+MA; Periodontol 2000, 2025; PRISMA): 5년 이상 추적한 깊은 골내결손 (≥3 mm) 재생술 RCT 17편 (2004-2022), 결손 501개, 추적 5-20년; 편향위험 우려 있음 13편, 높음 4편.

5년 이상에서 조직유도재생술 (Guided Tissue Regeneration, GTR)은 기저 대비 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 3.27 mm 획득, 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 4.04 mm 감소; 생물학제제·골이식·병용은 CAL 3.21 mm, PD 3.92 mm. GTR vs OFD는 GTR 우세 경향 (CAL 1.52 mm, 95% CI 0.06-3.10; PD 0.89 mm, 95% CI 0.22-1.99)이나 유의하지 않음 (p = 0.06; p = 0.115).

재생술의 임상 이득은 장기 유지되나 개방 판막 소파술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD) 대비 우월성은 확정되지 않음; 증거 확실성은 CAL·PD 낮음, 퇴축 (REC) 높음.

## Summary

Wang and colleagues pooled RCTs of regenerative procedures for deep intrabony defects (≥3 mm) with at least 5 years of follow-up (17 RCTs, 501 defects, 5-20 years). Compared with baseline, GTR and biologic/graft-based regeneration produced large, sustained CAL gain and PD reduction. Against OFD, GTR trended toward greater CAL gain and PD reduction but neither reached statistical significance. Meta-regression linked outcomes to follow-up time, surgical technique, membrane type and baseline measurements. Certainty was low for CAL and PD and high for recession; the authors conclude long-term superiority over OFD is not conclusively demonstrated.

## Key Contributions

- Long-term (≥5 y) pooled estimates for intrabony regeneration, a gap in shorter-follow-up syntheses
- Shows sustained gains versus baseline (CAL 3.2-3.3 mm; PD 3.9-4.0 mm)
- Qualifies the OFD comparison: point estimates favor GTR but the effect is not significant long term
- Meta-regression identifies follow-up time, technique, membrane type and baseline severity as modifiers

## Methodology

PRISMA-guided electronic and manual search for RCTs of regeneration in intrabony defects ≥3 mm with ≥5 years follow-up. Primary outcomes: PD reduction, CAL gain, recession depth, tooth loss. Random-effects meta-analyses and meta-regressions; risk of bias: 13 some concerns, 4 high.
