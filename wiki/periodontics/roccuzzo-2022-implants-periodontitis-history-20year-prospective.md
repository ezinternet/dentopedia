## Three-line Summary

20-year prospective cohort (n=112, University of Torino) comparing implant outcomes in patients with vs without a history of treated periodontitis across multiple SPT compliance assessments.

Implant survival was significantly lower in the periodontitis-history group (81.0% vs 91.2%, p<0.05); MBL was greater (2.1 mm vs 1.3 mm); periodontitis history conferred approximately HR ≈2 for implant failure over 20 years.

SPT compliance was the critical modifying factor — non-compliant patients in both groups had dramatically worse outcomes, making regular maintenance essential regardless of periodontal history.

## 세줄요약

20년 전향적 코호트(n=112, 토리노대): 치주염 치료 병력 유무에 따른 장기 임플란트 예후 비교.

치주염 병력군 임플란트 생존율 81.0% vs 비병력군 91.2%(p<0.05); 변연골 손실(MBL) 2.1 mm vs 1.3 mm; 20년간 임플란트 실패 위험비(HR) 약 2배.

SPT 순응도가 결정적 조절 인자 — 두 군 모두에서 순응 불량 환자는 예후가 현저히 불량하여, 치주 병력에 관계없이 정기적 유지 치료가 필수적이다.

## Summary
20-year prospective study showing significantly lower implant survival and greater MBL in periodontitis-history patients, with SPT compliance as critical modifying factor.

## Key Contributions
- 20-year survival: periodontitis-history 81.0% vs non-periodontitis 91.2% (p<0.05)
- MBL: periodontitis group 2.1mm vs control 1.3mm
- SPT non-compliant patients: dramatically worse outcomes in both groups

## Methodology
Prospective cohort; n=112; 20-year follow-up; University of Torino.

## Results
Periodontitis history is significant implant risk factor — hazard ratio approximately 2 for implant failure over 20 years. Regular SPT essential regardless of periodontal history.
