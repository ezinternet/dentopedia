## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (J Evid Based Dent Pract 2026): 18 RCTs (828 patients); adjunctive melatonin to NSPT vs NSPT alone; outcomes at 1, 2, 3, 6 months.

Significant CAL improvement at 2m (MD −1.48 mm), 3m (MD −0.41 mm), 6m (MD −0.68 mm); PPD at 2m (MD −1.80 mm) and 3m; HbA1c in diabetic patients at 2m (MD −1.33%); but prediction intervals cross null — benefit may not be consistent across populations.

Melatonin shows short-term promise as a low-risk adjunct for NSPT, especially in diabetic patients, but substantial heterogeneity limits confident broad recommendation.

## Summary

Abusamak et al. (J Evid Based Dent Pract 2026) conducted the largest SR+MA to date on melatonin as an adjunct to NSPT: 18 RCTs (828 patients), with 16 contributing to quantitative synthesis. Using the HKSJ method with prediction intervals, the meta-analysis showed statistically significant CAL gains favoring melatonin at 2, 3, and 6 months, and PPD reductions at 2 and 3 months. BOP reduction was not significant at any timepoint. A notable finding was HbA1c reduction (MD −1.33%) in diabetic patients at 2 months. However, prediction intervals for both CAL and PPD crossed the null at all timepoints, indicating substantial heterogeneity and that effects may be negligible in some clinical populations. Subgroup analyses by administration route and disease severity did not identify significant effect modifiers.

## Key Contributions

- 18 RCTs (828 patients) — largest quantitative synthesis on melatonin adjunct for perio
- First to report prediction intervals, revealing true effect variability
- HbA1c benefit (−1.33%) at 2 months in diabetic subgroup — clinically relevant signal
- BOP non-significant — anti-inflammatory benefit not consistently reflected in bleeding scores
- GRADE-informed evidence quality rating — certainty is likely low to moderate

## Methodology

SR+MA; 7 databases through April 2025; RCTs comparing NSPT+melatonin vs NSPT alone; primary: CAL and PPD at 1/2/3/6 months; secondary: BOP, GI, PI, HbA1c; HKSJ method + REML; prediction intervals; RoB 2; GRADE.
