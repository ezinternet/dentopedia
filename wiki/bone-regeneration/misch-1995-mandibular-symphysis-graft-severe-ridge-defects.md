## Three-line Summary

Review (Implant Dentistry, 1995) of the mandibular symphysis block graft for repairing severe localized ridge defects before implant placement.

Advantages claimed: easy access, adequate bone quantity, corticocancellous block morphology, low morbidity, minimal resorption, and better density with shorter healing.

The advantages are asserted without comparative data in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Implant Dentistry, 1995) — 임플란트 식립 전 심한 국소 치조제 결손을 수복하는 하악 결합부 블록 골이식.

접근성, 충분한 골량, 피질-해면골 블록 형태, 낮은 이환율, 최소 흡수, 짧은 치유 기간과 나은 골밀도를 장점으로 제시.

장점은 초록에서 비교 자료 없이 주장됨.

## Summary

The symphysis block graft is presented as the workhorse for severe localized defects. The review is an indication paper and does not compare it with other augmentation methods.

## Key Contributions
