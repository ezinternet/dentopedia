## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (Int J Implant Dent 2025, 10 studies, PROSPERO CRD42024628683; 452 sites: BBX 239, PBX 213; MSFA + ARP): primary outcome NFB no significant difference (WMD=1.5, 95% CI −1.46–4.46, p=0.321, I²=44.6%).

All secondary histomorphometric outcomes (residual bone graft, connective tissue) and radiographic outcomes (vertical/horizontal/volume reduction) showed no significant between-group difference.

PBX is clinically comparable to BBX in MSFA and ARP; further well-designed RCTs needed for long-term implant survival and graft resorption data.

## One-line Summary

SR+MA (10 studies, 452 sites) finds no significant difference between porcine (PBX) and bovine (BBX) bone xenografts in new bone formation, residual graft, or ridge dimension changes across MSFA and ARP procedures.

## 한줄요약

SR+MA (10개 연구, 452 sites): MSFA·ARP에서 돼지골(PBX)과 소골(BBX)의 신생골 형성·잔존 이식재·치조제 흡수 모두 유의차 없음 — PBX가 BBX의 임상적 대안임을 지지.

## Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42024628683) compared porcine bone xenograft (PBX) against the widely used bovine bone xenograft (BBX) in two contained bone grafting scenarios: maxillary sinus floor augmentation (MSFA) and alveolar ridge preservation (ARP). Ten studies were included (577 initially screened), covering 239 BBX sites (51 MSFA, 188 ARP) and 213 PBX sites (51 MSFA, 162 ARP). The primary outcome, percentage of newly formed bone (NFB) from six studies covering 202 sites, showed no statistically significant difference between PBX and BBX (WMD=1.5, 95% CI −1.46–4.46, p=0.321) with moderate heterogeneity (I²=44.6%). All secondary outcomes — residual bone graft, connective tissue proportion, vertical height reduction, horizontal width reduction, and volume reduction — were also non-significant between groups. The authors conclude that PBX is clinically comparable to BBX, supporting its use as an alternative particularly in contexts where bovine-derived materials raise cultural or religious concerns.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to directly compare PBX vs BBX specifically within MSFA and ARP (controlled defect environments with similar healing dynamics)
- NFB equivalence confirmed: WMD=1.5 (p=0.321) — the small numerical advantage of PBX is not clinically or statistically meaningful
- All histomorphometric and radiographic secondary outcomes non-significant — consistent across MSFA and ARP subgroups
- Provides SR+MA-level justification for PBX as a BBX alternative where bovine materials are contraindicated (religious/cultural/disease-concern grounds)
- Identifies critical evidence gap: no long-term implant survival, graft resorption, or bone stability data available for PBX vs BBX direct comparison

## Methodology

SR+MA; PRISMA-compliant; prospectively registered (PROSPERO CRD42024628683). Databases: PubMed, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, Scopus (search to April 2025). Included: comparative studies (PBX vs BBX), adult patients, MSFA or ARP, quantitative histomorphometric or radiographic outcomes, peer-reviewed original articles. Excluded: animal studies, reviews, abstracts, other augmentation types (horizontal/vertical GBR excluded to control methodological heterogeneity). Bias assessment: Cochrane ROB 2 for RCTs; Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS) for non-randomized studies. Meta-analysis: weighted mean difference (WMD), 95% CI; heterogeneity by I².

10 studies included: 452 total sites (BBX 239 = MSFA 51 + ARP 188; PBX 213 = MSFA 51 + ARP 162). NFB meta-analysis: 6 studies, 202 sites (101 PBX, 101 BBX).
