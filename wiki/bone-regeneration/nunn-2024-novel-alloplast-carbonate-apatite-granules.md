## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA/Cochrane; MEDLINE, EMBASE, CENTRAL, ClinicalTrials.gov plus hand search to Nov 2023) of 43 publications on carbonate apatite granules (CO3Ap, Cytrans): 5 in vitro, 28 animal, 7 human clinical (all small; 4 sinus-augmentation reports incl. one 3-year follow-up, 2 periodontal-defect studies, 1 three-case series), 3 reviews; a 65-year-old periodontal regeneration case is added.

In rabbit/dog models CO3Ap formed more bone than hydroxyapatite and beta-tricalcium phosphate (14.3-fold and 4.3-fold more mature bone at 4 weeks in rabbit femur) and more early bone than bovine xenograft; in humans sinus-lift height loss was 16.4-27.8% at 12-18 months, new bone 33.8% at 7 months, 100% implant survival at 3 years (n = 13), and bone resorption was 14.2% vs 24.2% for deproteinized bovine bone at 6 months (n = 6 vs 8, p < 0.001).

Evidence is promising but thin: human studies are small and short, comparisons to autogenous bone and xenograft figures are cross-study historical comparisons, no randomized trial exists, and the authors themselves call for multicenter RCTs.

## 세줄요약

체계적 고찰(PRISMA/Cochrane; MEDLINE·EMBASE·CENTRAL·ClinicalTrials.gov + 2023년 11월까지 수기검색): 탄산아파타이트 과립 (Carbonate Apatite granules, CO3Ap, Cytrans) 관련 43편 — 시험관 5·동물 28·사람 임상 7(모두 소규모)·리뷰 3 — 에 65세 환자의 치주재생 증례 1건을 추가.

토끼·개 모델에서 CO3Ap는 수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HAp)·베타-삼칼슘인산염 (beta-Tricalcium Phosphate, β-TCP)보다 많은 골을 형성(토끼 대퇴골 4주 성숙골 14.3배·4.3배)했고 초기 골형성은 우골 이종골 (Bovine xenograft)보다 우수; 사람에서는 상악동거상 후 높이 감소 16.4-27.8%(12-18개월), 7개월 신생골 33.8%, 3년 임플란트 생존율 100%(n = 13), 6개월 골흡수 14.2% vs 단백질 제거 우골 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 24.2%(n = 6 vs 8, p < 0.001).

유망하나 근거가 얇다: 사람 연구가 소규모·단기이고 자가골·이종골과의 비교는 연구 간 과거 수치 비교이며 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)이 없어, 저자 스스로 다기관 RCT를 요구.

## Summary

This PRISMA-guided systematic review evaluates carbonate apatite granules (CO3Ap, Cytrans Granules), a low-crystalline alloplast that is stable at physiologic pH but dissolves at low pH, so osteoclasts can resorb it while osteoblasts lay down new bone. Of 43 publications identified (5 in vitro, 28 animal, 7 human, 3 reviews), animal work consistently showed faster bone formation and graft turnover than hydroxyapatite and beta-TCP, and earlier bone formation than bovine xenograft (differences at later time points were not always significant). In seven small human studies, sinus-floor augmentation with CO3Ap lost 16.4-27.8% of the augmented height by 12-18 months, achieved about 34-37% new bone at 7 months, and gave 100% implant survival at 3 years (n = 13); a 6-month comparison with deproteinized bovine bone mineral favored CO3Ap for resorption (14.2% vs 24.2%, n = 6 vs 8). Periodontal studies combining CO3Ap with FGF-2 or alone showed probing-depth and attachment gains in intrabony defects but little change in Class III furcations. The authors conclude that CO3Ap is a promising alloplast, while acknowledging that randomized multicenter trials are lacking.

## Key Contributions

- First systematic mapping of the carbonate apatite granule literature across in vitro, animal and human designs.
- Compiles all 7 human studies (sinus augmentation, periodontal defects, implant/GBR case series) with quantitative outcomes.
- Presents the proposed osteoclast-mediated resorption plus osteoblast activation mechanism of CO3Ap, which underlies the claim of simultaneous resorption and bone replacement.
- Adds a 65-year-old male periodontal case (tooth 8) in which FGF plus CO3Ap reduced a 10 mm pocket to 4 mm at 6 months, stable at 12 months.

## Methodology

PRISMA/Cochrane-guided systematic review with a PICO focus question comparing CO3Ap against unassisted healing or other grafts (e.g., Bio-Oss). Searches of MEDLINE (PubMed), ClinicalTrials.gov, EMBASE and CENTRAL plus hand search of seven periodontology/implant journals (2012-Nov 2023); two reviewers screened independently with a third resolving disagreements. Ten of 28 animal studies and all human studies are summarized; no meta-analysis was performed and no formal risk-of-bias assessment is described in the retrieved text. The text contains no funding or conflict-of-interest statement although the reviewed material is a commercial product.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Publications identified | 43 (5 in vitro, 28 animal, 7 human, 3 reviews) |
| Rabbit femur, mature bone vs HAp / beta-TCP | 14.3-fold / 4.3-fold at 4 weeks; 7.5-fold / 1.4-fold at 12 weeks |
| Rabbit femur vs Bio-Oss | More new bone at 4 weeks; difference not significant at 8 weeks |
| Beagle mandible (Mano 2019) | 75% new bone at 12 weeks, ridge contour restored (NEOBONE and Bio-Oss insufficient) |
| First-in-human sinus lift (n = 8) | Height 5.2 to 14.0 mm; 11.7 mm at 12 months; resorption 16.4% |
| Two-stage sinus lift (n = 13, 17 implants) | Resorption 19.5% (7 mo) and 27.8% (18 mo); new bone 33.8%, residual graft 15.3% at 7 mo |
| 3-year follow-up of the above | Implant survival 100%; mature new bone 36.8%, residual CO3Ap 16.2% |
| CO3Ap (n = 6) vs DBBM (n = 8) sinus lift, 6 mo | Resorption 14.2% vs 24.2% (p < 0.001) |
| FGF-2 + CO3Ap, severe periodontal defects (n = 10, 36 wk) | No adverse events; significant alveolar bone, CAL, PD and BOP improvement vs baseline (p = 0.003, 0.001, 0.002, 0.016) |
