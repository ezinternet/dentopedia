## Three-line Summary

Collagen — primarily Type I — forms the structural 세포외기질 (Extracellular Matrix, ECM) of bone by templating 수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HA) crystal growth within mineralized fibrils; this review comprehensively maps ~29 collagen types, four source categories (bovine, porcine, marine, recombinant/GEPP), and all major processed forms for bone tissue engineering (골조직공학, BTE). Composite strategies combining collagen with natural polymers (alginate, chitosan, hyaluronan, silk fibroin), synthetic polymers (PCL, PLGA, PVA), metals, and bioceramics (HA, β-TCP) are surveyed, with rhBMP-2/absorbable collagen sponge as the prime FDA-approved example. Key barriers to clinical translation remain mechanical weakness of pure collagen, insufficient vascularization, immune response risk, and a very limited FDA-approved composite portfolio.

## 세줄요약

제1형 콜라겐(Col-I) 중심의 광범위 내러티브 리뷰: 약 29종 콜라겐 유형·4대 공급원(소·돼지·해양·재조합/GEPP)·스캐폴드·스폰지·멤브레인·하이드로젤·나노입자 등 가공 형태를 총망라한다. 천연 고분자(알지네이트·키토산/Col-I/HA·하이알루로난·실크 피브로인)·합성 고분자(PCL·PLGA·PVA)·금속·바이오세라믹(HA·β-TCP)과의 복합 전략을 체계화하며, rhBMP-2/흡수성 콜라겐 스폰지가 FDA 승인 핵심 사례다. 임상 적용 장벽은 기계적 강도 부족·혈관화 미흡·면역반응·승인 복합재 부족이며, 향후 유전자공학적 단백질 고분자(GEPP) 및 나노기술 기반 맞춤형 소재가 돌파구로 제시된다.

## Summary

Collagen is the most abundant protein in the human body (~1/3 of total protein by weight) and constitutes the primary organic component of bone ECM. Its triple-helix structure (Gly-X-Y repeat; proline and hydroxyproline at X/Y positions) confers tensile strength; mature molecules self-assemble into D-banded fibrils whose gap zones template 수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HA) crystal nucleation, yielding both intrafibrillar and extrafibrillar mineralized 콜라겐 (Collagen) — the nanoscale basis of bone's mechanical and biological properties.

Bone repair proceeds through three stages: inflammation (hematoma, cytokine cascade — IL-1, TNF-α, RANKL, VEGF), repair (MSC migration, intramembranous + endochondral ossification), and remodeling (osteoblast–osteoclast coupling to restore lamellar architecture). Col-I released by osteoblasts during ossification provides additional HA nucleation sites. Col-II templates cartilage (endochondral route); Col-IX, X, XI regulate cartilage fibril assembly and calcification interfaces.

Collagen degradation occurs via extracellular 기질금속단백분해효소 (Matrix Metalloproteinases, MMPs) (MMP-1/-2/-3/-8/-9/-13 cleave the triple helix) and cysteine proteases (cathepsin K/L/S — cathepsin K is the major osteoclastic collagenase and a drug target; cathepsin K inhibitors Relacatib, Balicatib, Odanacatib are in development but none FDA-approved). Intracellular pathways (phagocytosis, macropinocytosis, uPARAP-mediated endocytosis, autophagocytosis) handle fragmented collagen via cysteine protease lysosomal degradation.

## Key Contributions

| Form | Mechanism / Application | Key Example |
|---|---|---|
| Scaffolds | 3D porous support; osteoconduction; MSC/osteoblast migration | Collagen + chitosan/HA triple; silk fibroin/collagen 75:25 membranes |
| Membranes | GBR barrier; space maintenance | Crosslinked collagen; fish/carp-derived (marine) |
| Hydrogels | Injectable; hemostatic; sustained drug delivery | VITAGEL® (FDA-cleared); collagen hydrogel in fracture sites ≤5 years |
| Sponges | rhBMP-2 carrier; freeze-dried; customizable pore size | rhBMP-2/ACS (InFUSE — FDA-approved); BMP-2-delivering collagen scaffold |
| Microspheres / Nanoparticles | Controlled release of GFs (BMP, VEGF), drugs, cells | Collagen/HA-integrated into PCL/PLGA scaffolds |

**Composite partners**:
- *Natural polymers*: alginate (alginate-assisted collagen mineralization → enhanced 3D HA structure); chitosan/Col-I/HA (MC3T3-E1 proliferation + osteogenic gene expression dose-dependent on Col-I); hyaluronan (meniscus composite, 109% compression modulus vs natural); silk fibroin (wet-condition mechanical properties + osteoblast adhesion/proliferation).
- *Synthetic polymers*: PCL (FDA-approved, semi-crystalline, widely used); PLGA; PVA — enhanced with collagen to improve cell adhesion and biological activity.
- *Bioceramics*: HA (mimics inorganic bone phase); β-TCP (collagen/β-TCP + BMP-2 graft substitute → robust augmentation).
- *GEPP future*: genetically engineered collagen-binding BMP-2 (CBD-BMP-2) at 0.02 mg/cm³ collagen scaffold achieves posterolateral spinal fusion in rats — ~10× dose reduction vs natural rhBMP-2.

## Methodology

Narrative (non-systematic) review. No defined search protocol, PRISMA reporting, or quality appraisal of cited studies. Evidence base spans in vitro, animal (rat, rabbit), and limited human case series. Orthopedic fracture-repair context predominates; dental/alveolar applications are addressed briefly (nano collagen bone for alveolar ridge preservation).

## Results

- ~29 collagen types identified; Col-I is the dominant BTE material given its bone/tendon/skin distribution, superior biocompatibility, and capacity to support HA mineralization.
- Only a limited number of collagen-based composites have received FDA approval for clinical use (key: rhBMP-2/ACS; VITAGEL® hydrogel).
- Composite limitations summarized: natural polymers → poor cell adhesion, rapid degradation; synthetic polymers → poor biological activity; metals → corrosion/mechanical trade-offs; all → vascularization and immune safety concerns remain.
- Nano collagen artificial bone for alveolar ridge preservation: successful fusion with residual ridge + increased alveolar bone mineral density at 3-month CT follow-up (single case-series level).
- Future: GEPP enables sequence-level molecular control, minimal batch variation, no animal-derived contamination risk — promising for personalized BTE.
