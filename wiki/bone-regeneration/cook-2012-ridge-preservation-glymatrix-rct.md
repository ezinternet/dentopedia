## Three-line Summary

RCT (Cook & Mealey, University of Texas Health Science Center, J Periodontol 84(5):585-594, 2012) comparing two ridge preservation protocols in 44 patients post-extraction: **Protocol 1** (conventional xenograft + non-cross-linked membrane) vs **Protocol 2** (cross-linked collagen sponge with HA + Ossix Plus ribose-glycation membrane), with core biopsy histology at 21 weeks.

**Protocol 2 (GLYMATRIX platform): 47.03% vital bone vs 32.83% (P<0.001)**, zero residual graft vs 13.44% (P<0.001) — the first RCT demonstrating GLYMATRIX cross-linked sponge + membrane combination produces superior histologic healing in socket preservation.

Clinical ridge dimension changes were non-significant between protocols at 21 weeks, suggesting volume differences may require longer follow-up to manifest — histologic superiority precedes macroscopic dimensional advantage.

## 세줄요약

RCT (Cook & Mealey, 텍사스 의대, J Periodontol 2012) — 발치 후 치조제 보존 44명. Protocol 1: 이종골 + 비가교 멤브레인; Protocol 2: 가교 콜라겐 스펀지(HA) + Ossix Plus(GLYMATRIX). 21주 생검.

**활성골 47% vs 33% (P<0.001)**, 잔존 이식재 **0% vs 13% (P<0.001)** — GLYMATRIX 플랫폼 통합 사용 시 조직학적 치유 우위.

치조제 임상 크기 변화 유의차 없음(21주) — 조직학적 우위가 먼저, 부피 차이는 더 긴 추적 필요.

## Clinical Decision Point

> **For socket preservation (ridge preservation)**: GLYMATRIX-platform combination (cross-linked sponge + Ossix Plus) achieves significantly higher vital bone at 21 weeks compared to conventional xenograft protocols. Consider when maximizing bone quality (not just volume) is the priority before implant placement.

## Limitations

- 21-week endpoint; implant outcomes not reported
- Clinical dimension measurement (not CBCT); may lack sensitivity
- Specific product name not listed in abstract (inferred from description)
