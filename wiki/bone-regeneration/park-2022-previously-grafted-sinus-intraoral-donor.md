## Three-line Summary

This case report describes a novel 유도 골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) technique using bone cores harvested from a maxillary sinus previously augmented with 탈단백 소골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 16 years ago.
Histological analysis showed DBBM comprised only 3.6% of total bone volume (1.35 mm³ of 38.83 mm³), while 96.4% was newly-formed vital bone (37.48 mm³); the crushed bone core was used successfully for GBR around a new implant, with stable crestal bone levels at 2-year follow-up.
A previously grafted sinus can serve as a viable intraoral donor site for further bone grafting, as DBBM is largely replaced by new bone over the long term, providing osteogenic potential.

## 세줄요약

본 증례 보고는 16년 전 탈단백 소골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)로 상악동 거상술을 시행받은 부위에서 채취한 골 코어를 사용한 새로운 유도 골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) 기술을 설명합니다.
채취된 골 코어의 조직학적 분석 결과, DBBM이 전체 골 부피의 3.6%(1.35 mm³/38.83 mm³)만을 차지하고 96.4%(37.48 mm³)는 신생골이었으며, 분쇄 골 코어를 사용한 GBR 후 2년 추적에서 안정적인 변연골 수준이 관찰되었습니다.
이전에 이식된 상악동이 장기간에 걸쳐 DBBM을 신생골로 대체하므로 추가 골이식을 위한 구강내 공여부 역할을 할 수 있음을 시사하나, 증례 수가 1명으로 일반화에 한계가 있습니다.

## Summary

Park et al. (2022) present a single case in which bone cores were harvested from a maxillary sinus augmented 16 years earlier with DBBM (Bio-Oss) and repurposed as an autogenous graft for a peri-implant 골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) procedure. 미세 전산화 단층촬영 (Micro-Computed Tomography, micro-CT) and histological analysis of one harvested core revealed that only 3.6% of the total bone volume (1.35 mm³ of 38.83 mm³) consisted of residual DBBM, while 96.4% (37.48 mm³) was newly-formed vital bone — confirming substantial long-term remodeling of the xenograft. The second core was crushed into 1–3 mm fragments, placed over the peri-implant defect, and covered with a resorbable collagen membrane; panoramic radiographs at 2-year follow-up showed stable crestal bone levels. This is a proof-of-concept case demonstrating that long-standing DBBM sinus grafts can be re-harvested as osteogenic intraoral donor material.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that a sinus grafted with DBBM 16 years earlier contains 96.4% vital new bone and only 3.6% residual DBBM by volume (micro-CT).
- Introduces the clinical concept of harvesting previously augmented sinus bone as an intraoral autograft for GBR.
- Histological evidence of active remodeling: vascularized connective tissue, osteoclasts adjacent to residual DBBM particles, mature and immature bone.
- Stable crestal bone levels at 2-year post-prosthesis follow-up using crushed sinus-derived bone core for GBR.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Case report (n=1)
- **Patient**: 63-year-old male; severe chronic periodontitis; bilateral MSA with DBBM 16 years prior
- **Bone harvest**: Two 4.0 mm trephine cores from lateral wall of previously augmented sinus
- **Core 1**: Processed for micro-CT (SkyScan 1173) and H-E histology
- **Core 2**: Crushed to 1–3 mm fragments → GBR graft covered with resorbable collagen membrane
- **Follow-up**: Clinical + panoramic radiograph up to 2 years post-prosthesis
