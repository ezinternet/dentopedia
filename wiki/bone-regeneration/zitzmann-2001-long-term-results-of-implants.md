## Three-line Summary

1. Prospective 5-year study of Brånemark implants with peri-implant defects treated by GBR (Bio-Oss+Bio-Gide or Bio-Oss+Gore-Tex) vs bone-encased controls (75 patients).
2. Cumulative survival 93–97% at 5 years; mean marginal bone loss higher with GBR (1.83 mm Bio-Oss/Bio-Gide, 2.21 mm Bio-Oss/Gore-Tex) than controls (1.73 mm), increasing over time.
3. Keratinized mucosa stable (~3.0–3.16 mm); plaque/inflammation/recession linked more to restoration type than treatment; GBR indicated if vertical defect >2 mm.

## 세줄요약

1. Brånemark 임플란트에서 주위 결손부에 유도골재생(GBR, Bio-Oss+Bio-Gide 또는 Bio-Oss+Gore-Tex)을 시행한 군과 골로 완전 둘러싸인 대조군을 5년간 전향적으로 비교했다(75명).
2. 5년 누적 생존율은 93–97%였고, 변연골소실은 GBR군(Bio-Oss/Bio-Gide 1.83 mm, Bio-Oss/Gore-Tex 2.21 mm)이 대조군(1.73 mm)보다 더 컸으며 시간에 따라 증가했다.
3. 각화점막은 안정적(~3.0–3.16 mm)이었고, 플라크/염증/퇴축은 치료방식보다 보철물 형태와 연관이 컸으며, 수직결손 2 mm 이상에서 GBR이 적응증으로 제시되었다.

## Summary

Zitzmann et al. (2001) prospectively followed implants placed with GBR for peri-implant defects over 5 years. In 112 implants, defects were grafted with deproteinized bovine bone mineral (Bio-Oss) and covered with a resorbable collagen membrane (Bio-Gide); in a split-mouth subset (41 implants), Gore-Tex (ePTFE) was used. Controls were 112 implants entirely surrounded by bone. Implant survival was similar across groups (93–97%), but marginal bone loss was more pronounced at GBR sites and increased over time. Soft tissue parameters were relatively stable, though inflammatory signs correlated more with prosthesis type than GBR. The authors suggest GBR is indicated when the initial vertical defect exceeds ~2 mm.

## Key Contributions

- Long-term (5-year) prospective data for GBR-treated implants compared with bone-encased controls.
- Direct comparison of resorbable (Bio-Gide) vs non-resorbable (Gore-Tex) membranes in a split-mouth design.
- Clinical observation linking peri-implant mucosal changes more to restoration type than to the GBR procedure itself.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective longitudinal study with split-mouth subgroup
- **Participants/implants**: 75 patients; 112 GBR (Bio-Oss+Bio-Gide), 41 split-mouth (Bio-Oss+Gore-Tex), 112 controls (bone-encased)
- **Surgery**: Brånemark implants placed with GBR for defects
- **Follow-up**: 6 months after definitive prosthesis, then annually for 5 years
- **Outcomes**: survival, marginal bone level (MBL), plaque, peri-implant mucosal conditions, keratinized mucosa height (KM), marginal soft tissue level (MSTL)

## Results

| Parameter (5-year) | Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide | Bio-Oss + Gore-Tex | Control (entirely surrounded by bone) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cumulative implant survival | 93–97% | 93–97% | 93–97% |
| Mean marginal bone level (MBL) | 1.83 mm | 2.21 mm | 1.73 mm |
| Keratinized mucosa (KM) | 3.02–3.16 mm (stable) | 3.02–3.16 mm (stable) | 3.02–3.16 mm (stable) |
| Plaque prevalence | ~15% of sites | ~15% of sites | ~15% of sites |
| Notes | MBL increased over time; differences significant among groups | Highest mean MBL among groups | Lowest mean MBL |
