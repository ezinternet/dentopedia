## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Seoul National University, J Periodontal Implant Sci 2026) providing a practical species-selection hierarchy for bone regeneration research: rodent (mechanistic screening) → rabbit (critical-size defects) → dog (intraoral, human-like remodeling) → minipig (late-stage validation, highest human similarity).

Each species has documented advantages and limitations — rodents lack Haversian remodeling and heal faster, rabbits heal faster than humans, dogs raise ethical concerns, and minipigs require large facilities; defect acuity, systemic conditions, and observation period standardization are critical confounders.

This review provides a reference framework for interpreting animal study results from other bone regeneration papers in this wiki, and emphasizes that non-standardized models are the principal barrier to translating pre-clinical GBR findings to clinical practice.

## 세줄요약

서울대학교 골재생 전임상 동물모델 서술적 고찰(J Periodontal Implant Sci 2026): 설치류(기전 스크리닝)→토끼(임계결손)→개(구강내, 인체유사 골재형성)→미니돼지(후기 검증, 최고 유사) 위계 제시.

설치류는 하버시안 재형성 없음·과강한 치유, 토끼는 인체보다 빠른 치유, 개는 윤리 문제, 미니돼지는 대형 시설 요구 — 급성·만성·감염·전신질환 결손 및 관찰기간 표준화가 핵심 교란요인.

본 위키 내 골재생 동물연구 결과 해석의 참조 틀을 제공하며, 비표준화 모델이 전임상 GBR 근거의 임상 번역을 가로막는 주요 장벽임을 강조.

## Summary
Narrative review (Seoul National University, Jungwon Lee) summarizing preclinical animal model selection for bone regeneration research. Provides a practical hierarchy of species — rodent → rabbit → dog → minipig — with species-specific considerations for intraoral defect studies.

## Key Contributions
| Species | Use case | Key limitation |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Rodent | Early screening; mechanistic | No Haversian remodeling; strong healing |
| Rabbit | Medium defects; critical-size threshold | Faster healing than humans |
| Dog | Intraoral defects; human-like remodeling | Declining due to ethics |
| Minipig | Late-stage validation; human-like bone | Large body; strong healing |

- Acute vs chronic, infected vs non-infected, systemic conditions all influence outcomes
- Standardized defect dimensions + observation periods critical for reproducibility
