## Three-line Summary

Animal RCT (6 mongrel dogs, 12 bilateral mandibular buccal dehiscence defects, 5×5×3 mm) with GBR + simultaneous implant placement; randomized to early re-entry at 2 months vs conventional re-entry at 4 months; all euthanized at 5 months for histomorphometric and micro-CT analysis.
No significant differences between groups in new bone area (1.98 vs 0.88 mm², p=0.063), BIC (53.80% vs 54.15%, p>0.999), defect height resolution (68.80% vs 64.80%, p=0.438), or peri-implant mucosal dimensions; early group showed non-significantly greater mucosal volume (75.36 vs 50.05 mm³) and denser collagen maturation.
Early second-stage surgery at 2 months (post-membrane-resorption) does not compromise bone regeneration or osseointegration and may advance soft-tissue maturation, enabling earlier transition to the prosthetic phase.

## 세줄요약

동물 연구 (잡종견 6마리, 12부위 하악 5×5×3 mm 협측 이개열 결손); 동시 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) + 임플란트 식립 후 조기 재진입 (2개월) vs 지연 재진입 (4개월), 식립 5개월 후 조직형태계측 및 마이크로 CT 분석.
신생골 면적 (1.98 vs 0.88 mm², p=0.063), 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) (53.80% vs 54.15%, p>0.999), 결손 해소율 (68.80% vs 64.80%, p=0.438), 임플란트 주위 점막 치수 모두 두 군 간 통계적 유의차 없음; 조기군에서 점막 부피 (75.36 vs 50.05 mm³) 및 교원질 밀도 비유의 우세.

## Summary

This canine study from Yonsei University (Jung group) investigated whether early second-stage surgery following simultaneous GBR and submerged implant placement compromises bone regeneration or peri-implant tissue dimensions. In six mongrel dogs, standardized 5×5×3 mm buccal dehiscence defects received bone-level implants with EDC-crosslinked collagen membrane and BCP (60% HA + 40% β-TCP) bone substitute fixed with titanium bone tacks; healing abutments were connected at either 2 months (early) or 4 months (late) postoperatively, and all specimens were harvested at 5 months. Histomorphometric analysis of five matched pairs (one late-group implant failed due to fibrin incarceration) found no statistically significant differences in any hard-tissue parameter — new bone area, BIC, mineralised tissue area, defect height resolution, or buccal bone thickness — between the two groups. Soft-tissue measurements were consistently numerically higher in the early group (mucosal height 2.65 vs 1.59 mm; 3D mucosal volume 75.36 vs 50.05 mm³), attributed to the longer soft-tissue maturation interval (3 months vs 1 month before harvest), though none reached statistical significance due to the small sample size. The authors concluded that re-entry timing at 2 months — after the resorbable collagen membrane has fully resorbed (~6 weeks) and when the initial scaffold-stabilization role of the membrane is complete — does not disrupt the centripetal bone healing process and may offer a clinical benefit through earlier soft-tissue adaptation.

## Key Contributions

- First direct preclinical comparison of 2-month vs 4-month re-entry timing after simultaneous GBR using histomorphometric and 3D micro-CT endpoints (including volumetric mucosal assessment).
- Confirms that re-entry surgery after complete membrane resorption (~2 months for EDC-crosslinked collagen) does not impair the regenerative compartment or osseointegration — the bone healing process continues undisturbed beneath the implant shoulder.
- Documents a qualitative soft-tissue advantage of early re-entry: denser, more mature, and more complex collagen fiber architecture in the lamina propria of the early group vs a looser fibrous arrangement in the late group.
- One late-group implant failed (fibrin incarceration), suggesting that longer submerged healing is not inherently safer and may carry its own failure risk.
- Supports the biological rationale that the critical GBR protective window is the first ~6 weeks while the membrane is functional, not the entire 4-month healing period traditionally observed.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomized controlled animal experiment (split-mouth); ARRIVE 2.0 compliant; Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee approval (Yonsei Medical Center 2019-0267)
- **Subjects**: 6 male mongrel dogs, 15–24 months, ~15 kg
- **Defect creation**: Mandibular premolar extraction → 8-week healing → bilateral standardized buccal dehiscence defects (5 mm length × 5 mm height × 3 mm depth); decortication around defect periphery
- **Implants**: Bone-level, 3.5 × 8 mm, SLA surface (Superline III, Dentium, Korea); submerged healing with cover screws; primary stability ≥30 N
- **GBR protocol**: EDC-crosslinked collagen membrane (Collagen membrane P, Genoss) + synthetic BCP (Osteon III, Genoss: 60% HA + 40% β-TCP); membrane fixed with 2 titanium bone tacks buccally; periosteal releasing incision; primary closure
- **Groups**: Early (2 months postop, n=6) and Late (4 months postop, n=6); all euthanized 5 months post-implant surgery
- **Re-entry technique**: Minimally invasive mid-crestal incision (<7–8 mm); healing abutments (5.5 mm diameter × 3 mm height); without excessive exposure of augmented area
- **Analysis**: Histomorphometric (ground sections 20–50 μm; Goldner's Trichrome stain; non-decalcified); micro-CT (SkyScan1173); statistics: Wilcoxon signed-rank test (5 matched pairs)

## Results

**Hard tissue:**

| Outcome | Early (2 mo) n=5 | Late (4 mo) n=5 | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| New bone area (mm²) | 1.98 [1.53–2.98] | 0.88 [0.09–1.79] | 0.063 |
| Residual graft area (mm²) | 1.55 [0.98–2.39] | 1.80 [1.12–1.88] | >0.999 |
| Mineralised tissue area (mm²) | 3.78 [2.77–4.99] | 2.10 [1.80–3.36] | 0.063 |
| BIC (%) | 53.80 [40.88–63.10] | 54.15 [45.21–66.02] | >0.999 |
| fBIC from shoulder (mm) | 1.61 [1.53–3.11] | 3.44 [2.57–4.60] | 0.063 |
| BC-S distance (mm) | 1.56 [1.15–2.50] | 1.76 [1.22–2.77] | 0.438 |
| Defect height resolution (%) | 68.80 [50.00–77.00] | 64.80 [44.60–75.60] | 0.438 |

All buccal bone thickness levels 1–5 mm apical: no significant differences. No implant in either group was covered by buccal bone at 1 mm apical to the implant shoulder.

**Soft tissue:**

| Outcome | Early (2 mo) n=5 | Late (4 mo) n=5 | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mucosal thickness at 0 mm (mm) | 2.18 [1.68–2.33] | 1.56 [1.05–1.84] | 0.125 |
| Mucosal thickness at 1 mm (mm) | 1.61 [0.90–1.90] | 0.87 [0.23–1.09] | 0.063 |
| Mucosal thickness at 2 mm (mm) | 0.48 [0–0.91] | 0.00 [0.00] | 0.25 |
| Mucosal height (mm) | 2.65 [1.61–2.75] | 1.59 [0.10–1.81] | 0.063 |
| Junctional epithelium height (mm) | 1.93 [1.02–2.13] | 1.35 [0.98–1.39] | 0.125 |
| 3D mucosal volume (mm³) | 75.36 [42.03–86.19] | 50.05 [41.00–68.47] | 0.313 |

Data presented as median [IQR]. All p-values from Wilcoxon signed-rank test.

**Histology**: Early group — lamellar bone with well-integrated BCP particles; denser and more mature lamina propria collagen; more complex fiber arrangement (parallel + perpendicular to implant). Late group — harmonious bone-graft integration; looser connective tissue with lower fiber density and complexity; junctional epithelium not fully established at harvest (only 1 month after re-entry). Collagen membrane undetectable by 5 months in both groups (fully resorbed).

**Complication**: One implant failure in the late group due to fibrin incarceration; only 5 matched pairs available for statistical analysis.
