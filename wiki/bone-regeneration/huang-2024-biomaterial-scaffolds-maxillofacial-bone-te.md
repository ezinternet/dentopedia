## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Bioactive Materials 33:129-156, 2024; CC BY-NC-ND) organizing maxillofacial bone tissue engineering (BTE) scaffolds along two axes: **physical design** (pore geometry 200–500 µm optimal, interconnectivity, mechanical match) and **biological functionalization** (BMP-2, VEGF, BMSCs/ADSCs, surface bioceramics).

Tables 1–3 compile clinical trial evidence for alveolar bone, jawbone, and periapical defects — Ossix Volumax appears in Table 1 as a ridge augmentation scaffold achieving volume increase.

The review identifies **vascularization** (scaffolds fail at >1 cm defects without pre-vascularization) and **patient-specific 3D printing** (regulatory pathway immature) as the two principal unresolved challenges.

## Scaffold Design Framework

### Physical Axes

| Parameter | Range/Guideline | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Pore size | 200–500 µm | Cell infiltration; vascularization |
| Porosity | >50% | Nutrient exchange |
| Mechanical match | Cancellous ~0.02–0.5 GPa; Cortical 7–30 GPa | Stress shielding / collapse prevention |
| Architecture | Gradient > random for large defects | Mimics cortical-cancellous transition |

### Biological Axes

| Factor/Cell | Key Role | Delivery / Source |
|---|---|---|
| BMP-2 | Osteoinduction | Collagen sponge, 3D-printed scaffold |
| VEGF | Vascularization | Sequential release with BMP-2 |
| BMSCs | Osteoblast progenitors | Iliac crest (gold standard) |
| ADSCs | Comparable osteogenic potential | Liposuction aspirate |
| PDL stem cells | Alveolar-specific progenitors | Extracted tooth root |

## Alveolar Bone Clinical Evidence (Table 1, selected)

| Scaffold | Defect | Outcome | Evidence level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ossix Volumax (collagen) | Ridge augmentation | Volume increase | Case series |
| rhBMP-2/ACS | Alveolar socket | Superior bone fill vs. autograft | RCT |
| DBBM + collagen membrane | Socket preservation | Less ridge resorption | RCT |
| HA/TCP composite | Ridge augmentation | Comparable to autograft | RCT |

## Two Unresolved Challenges

**1. Vascularization**: Above ~1 cm defect size, scaffold ossification fails without pre-vascularization. Current approaches (VEGF co-delivery, channeled architectures) insufficient for routine clinical translation.

**2. Patient-specific 3D printing**: CT/CBCT-to-scaffold pipeline exists technically but regulatory pathway for patient-specific Class III implants remains immature in most jurisdictions.

## Limitations

- Narrative, not systematic — selection bias
- Heterogeneous outcomes across trials; no pooled analysis
- Many trials n < 20 patients
- Limited coverage of periodontal scaffold literature
