## Three-line Summary
Four-arm randomized controlled trial (n=40 patients, University of Zurich) comparing polylactide-coated β-tricalcium phosphate (β-TCP), deproteinized bovine bone mineral with 10% collagen (DBBM-C) covered with a collagen matrix (CM), DBBM-C covered with an autogenous soft-tissue graft (PG), and spontaneous healing (control) for ridge preservation, with cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) at baseline and 6 months.
At 6 months, DBBM-C/CM and DBBM-C/PG markedly limited ridge contraction vs control — worst-case width change 1 mm subcrestal was −1.2/−1.4 mm (−17.4/−18.1%) vs −3.3 mm (−43.3%) for control and −6.1 mm (−77.5%) for β-TCP — and DBBM-C/PG even gained 1.2 mm (8.1%) buccal vertical height; CM and PG covers did not differ (p>0.05).
Under a soft-tissue seal, DBBM-C preserves ridge dimensions far better than spontaneous healing or coated β-TCP, and a collagen matrix seal matches autogenous soft tissue; limitation — abstract-only source, n=40, 6-month horizon.

## 세줄요약
4군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=40명, 취리히 대학교) — 폴락티드 코팅 β-인산삼칼슘 (β-TCP), 탈단백우골-콜라겐 (DBBM-C)+콜라겐 매트릭스 (CM) 피개, DBBM-C+자가 연조직 이식 (PG) 피개, 자연 치유(대조군) 비교, 파생단층촬영 (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT) 기저 및 6개월 측정.
6개월 시 DBBM-C+CM/PG군은 대조군 대비 현저히 적은 치조제 수축 — crest 하방 1 mm 폭 변화는 −1.2/−1.4 mm(−17.4/−18.1%)로 대조군 −3.3 mm(−43.3%), β-TCP −6.1 mm(−77.5%) 대비 우수했고, DBBM-C/PG군은 협측 수직고 +1.2 mm(8.1%) 획득 — CM과 PG 피개는 유의차 없음(p>0.05).
연조직 피개 하의 DBBM-C는 자연 치유·코팅 β-TCP 대비 치조제 치수 보존에 현저히 우수하고 콜라겐 매트릭스 피개는 자가 연조직으로 동등하나, 초록만 확보·n=40·6개월 추적의 한계가 있다.

## Summary
This single-center four-arm randomized controlled clinical trial from the University of Zurich evaluated radiographic alveolar ridge dimensional change 6 months after tooth extraction under four treatments: polylactide-coated β-TCP particles, DBBM-C covered with a collagen matrix, DBBM-C covered with an autogenous soft-tissue graft, and spontaneous healing (control) in 40 patients. CBCT scans taken immediately after treatment and 6 months later showed that both DBBM-C groups underwent markedly less vertical and horizontal ridge change than control, whereas the coated β-TCP group lost the most bone of all arms. DBBM-C/PG gained 1.2 mm (8.1%) of buccal vertical height and 0.3 mm (5.6%) lingually, while control lost 0.6 mm (−10.2%) lingually and 3.3 mm (−43.3%) of width 1 mm below the crest. The collagen matrix and autogenous soft-tissue graft seals over DBBM-C did not differ at any of the three measured levels (p>0.05). Clinically, this trial supports grafting with a slowly resorbing xenograft under a soft-tissue seal, and shows that an acellular collagen matrix can substitute for a palatal soft-tissue graft — but the evidence here rests on published abstract data only.

## Key Contributions
- Provides CBCT-quantified 3D vertical (buccal/lingual) and horizontal ridge change data across three ridge-preservation materials plus spontaneous healing at a uniform 6-month endpoint.
- Demonstrates that DBBM-C under either a collagen matrix or an autogenous soft-tissue seal limits ridge contraction far better than spontaneous healing.
- Shows the sealing material can be an acellular collagen matrix — a palatal autograft adds nothing measurable (p>0.05) — reducing donor-site morbidity for the same dimensional outcome.
- Reports net buccal vertical gain (+1.2 mm, 8.1%) with DBBM-C/PG, i.e. ridge augmentation rather than mere preservation.
- Documents a specific synthetic graft (polylactide-coated β-TCP) underperforming even spontaneous healing on buccal vertical and subcrestal width change, underscoring formulation dependence.

## Methodology
Design: four-arm randomized controlled clinical trial, single center (Clinic of Fixed and Removable Prosthodontics and Dental Material Science / Clinic of Preventive Dentistry, Periodontology and Cariology, Center of Dental Medicine, University of Zurich, Switzerland). Randomization assigned 40 patients to (1) polylactide-coated β-TCP particles, (2) DBBM-C with 10% collagen covered with a collagen matrix (CM), (3) DBBM-C covered with an autogenous soft-tissue graft (PG), or (4) spontaneous healing (control). Outcomes: radiographic vertical (buccal, lingual) and horizontal alveolar ridge changes measured on CBCT obtained after treatment and 6 months later. Funding: university clinics, in part Geistlich Pharma AG and Degradable Solutions AG research grants.

## Results
| Outcome (6 months vs baseline) | Control | β-TCP | DBBM-C/CM | DBBM-C/PG |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical, lingual | −0.6 mm (−10.2%) | — | — | +0.3 mm (+5.6%) |
| Vertical, buccal | — | −2.0 mm (−20.9%) | — | +1.2 mm (+8.1%) |
| Width, 1 mm subcrestal | −3.3 mm (−43.3%) | −6.1 mm (−77.5%) | −1.2 mm (−17.4%) | −1.4 mm (−18.1%) |

- DBBM-C/CM vs DBBM-C/PG: no significant difference at all three levels (p>0.05).
- Most other between-group comparisons reached statistical significance (p<0.05).
- The most accentuated ridge width changes occurred 1 mm below the crest.
- Values are as reported in the published abstract (full-text tables not retrieved).
