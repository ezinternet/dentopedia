## Three-line Summary
Quantified-force RCT (n=12 completers) comparing 5 N vs 30 N compressive force applied to particulate DBBM under a native bilayer collagen membrane during molar open-healing alveolar ridge preservation, with histomorphometry, ISQ, and peri-implant bone level as outcomes.

New bone formation, residual graft ratio, soft-tissue ratio, ISQ, and peri-implant bone level did not differ significantly between force groups (P>0.05); only residual graft particle size was significantly different (P<0.05).

The "more compressive force → more new bone" signal from Cho 2017 did not replicate in the molar DBBM context, suggesting the earlier finding may be material- or site-specific, and a gentle 5 N force is sufficient in practice.

## 세줄요약
압축력 정량 RCT (완료 12명): 대구치 개방 치유 ARP에서 DBBM 입자에 5 N vs 30 N 압축력을 가해 조직형태계측·ISQ·임플란트 주위 골수준을 비교.

신생골·잔존 이식재 비율·연조직 비율·ISQ·임플란트 주위 골수준 모두 군간 유의차 없음 (P>0.05); 잔존 이식재 입자 크기만 유의하게 달랐다 (P<0.05).

Cho 2017의 "압축력 증가→신생골 증가" 소견은 DBBM·대구치 조건에서 재현되지 않아, 재료·부위 의존적 효과일 가능성이 있으며 5 N 압축으로 충분하다.

## Summary
Quantified-force follow-up to cho-2017. In molar ARP with DBBM under a native bilayer collagen membrane, 5 N vs 30 N did not change new bone formation — narrowing where the earlier "more force → more bone" signal applies.

## Key Contributions
- Quantified force (5 N vs 30 N) rather than test/control
- Molar-specific, DBBM + NBCM
- Equivalence finding tempers cho-2017

## Methodology
- RCT; 12 completers; molar open-healing ARP; 5 N vs 30 N on particulate DBBM under NBCM; histomorphometry + ISQ + peri-implant bone level.

## Results
- New bone, residual graft, soft-tissue ratios and ISQ: NS (P>0.05).
- Residual graft particle **size**: significant difference (P<0.05).
- Conclusion: 5 N ≈ 30 N for new bone, implant feasibility, peri-implant bone level.

## Limitations
- Small (n=12); molar-only. Tension with cho-2017 likely due to material/site/force-operationalization differences.
