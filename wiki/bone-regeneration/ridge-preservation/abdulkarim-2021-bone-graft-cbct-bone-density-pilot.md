## Three-line Summary

Retrospective pilot (n=118 implant sites, 66 patients, 38 grafted) correlating preoperative CBCT Hounsfield units (HU) with intraoperative clinical bone quality (Misch D1–D4) in sites with and without prior bone grafting.

In non-grafted sites, HU significantly predicted clinical bone quality (p<0.001); in grafted sites the correlation was non-significant — grafted sites paradoxically showed higher HU but softer clinical quality, especially with xenograft (p=0.02 vs no-graft).

CBCT HU is unreliable for predicting clinical bone quality after bone grafting, particularly after xenograft; CBCT interpretation in post-ARP sites requires caution.

## 세줄요약

후향적 파일럿 (n=118 부위, 66명, 이식재 부위 38개): 술 전 CBCT 하운스필드 단위(Hounsfield Unit, HU)와 술 중 임상 골 질(Misch 분류, D1–D4) 상관관계 — 이식재 유·무 비교.

비이식재 부위: HU가 골 질 유의하게 예측(p<0.001); 이식재 부위: 이식재 종류 불문 상관관계 비유의 — 특히 이종골(Xenograft) 부위에서 HU↑이지만 임상 골 질↓ (p=0.02).

치조제보존술(Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 등 이식재 시술 후 CBCT HU는 임상 골 질의 신뢰할 수 있는 지표가 아님 — 특히 이종골(Bio-Oss형) 이식 부위에서 판독 주의 필요.

## Summary

This retrospective pilot study evaluated whether preoperative CBCT-derived bone density (Hounsfield units) predicts intraoperative clinical bone quality (Misch D1–D4, assessed by tactile sense) differently in implant sites with vs without a history of bone grafting. Among 118 sites in 66 patients, 38 had prior bone grafts. Grafted sites showed paradoxically higher HU but softer clinical quality than non-grafted sites. While HU correlated significantly with clinical bone quality in non-grafted sites (p<0.001), this correlation became non-significant in grafted sites overall. The breakdown by graft type was critical: allograft sites behaved similarly to non-grafted sites (correlation not significantly different, p≥0.07), while xenograft sites showed a significantly different HU–quality relationship (p=0.02). The proposed mechanism is that xenograft particles (e.g., Bio-Oss) persist in the healed socket, artificially elevating CBCT density without a corresponding increase in native bone stiffness or clinical quality.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrated that CBCT HU overestimates clinical bone quality in **post-xenograft** sites — residual xenograft particles inflate radiodensity without reflecting native bone stiffness
- Allograft ARP: HU–quality correlation preserved (allograft resorbs, native bone replaces)
- Xenograft ARP (most common ARP material): HU–quality correlation broken (p=0.02)
- Quantified the magnitude collapse: correlation significant (p<0.001) → non-significant in graft group
- Clinical alarm: a "D2-looking" CBCT site after Bio-Oss ARP may be D3 or D4 tactilely at surgery

## Methodology

Retrospective review of implant surgical records + preoperative CBCT scans, 2017–2019, single US institution (AT Still University/MOSDOH). Inclusion: 273 implants with adequate preoperative CBCT → 118 sites (66 patients, mean age 58). Clinical bone quality: Misch classification (D1–D4) assessed intraoperatively. Radiographic bone density: CBCT HU at site. Regression analysis controlling for jaw location; graft subgroup comparisons.

## Results

| Comparison | Result | p |
|---|---|---|
| Grafted vs non-grafted: clinical quality | Grafted = softer bone quality | 0.003 |
| Grafted vs non-grafted: HU | Grafted = higher HU | <0.001 |
| HU → clinical quality (overall) | Significant positive correlation | ≤0.01 |
| HU → clinical quality (no graft) | Significant (stronger effect) | <0.001 |
| HU → clinical quality (any graft) | **Non-significant** | NS |
| Allograft subgroup vs no-graft | Not significantly different | ≥0.07 |
| Xenograft subgroup vs no-graft | **Significantly different** | 0.02 |
