## Three-line Summary
Double-blind 3-arm RCT (Al-Azhar University, Cairo, n=36 extraction sockets, 12/group, 6-month follow-up) comparing BARP-style collagen sponge + xenograft (GP I) vs. collagen sponge alone (GP II) vs. spontaneous healing (GP III control), with CBCT, soft-tissue, and histomorphometric outcomes at implant-site biopsy.
Both GP I and GP II significantly limited horizontal ridge-width loss at 1 mm vs. control (17.81 ± 3.97% and 19 ± 2.77% vs. 41.79 ± 10.3%, P < 0.001) with no difference between them, but histologically GP I had the highest lamellar bone area% (54.80 ± 6.69 vs. 27.34 ± 5.74 in GP II vs. 22.21 ± 3.66 in control, P = 0.001) and no residual inflammation, while GP II showed more inflammation and GP III the least lamellar bone.
Clinically and radiographically collagen sponge alone performed as well as collagen sponge + xenograft, but the xenograft-containing BARP protocol produced qualitatively better bone (more lamellar, less inflamed) at the histological level — dimensional/radiographic equivalence masked a real histological difference.

## 세줄요약
이중맹검 3군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT, 알아즈하르 대학교 카이로, n=36 발치와, 군당 12개, 6개월 추적): BARP식 콜라겐 스펀지+이종골 (Xenograft, GP I) vs 콜라겐 스펀지 단독 (GP II) vs 자연치유 대조군 (GP III)을 콘빔전산화단층촬영 (Cone Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT)·연조직·조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry)으로 비교.
GP I과 GP II 모두 1mm 지점 수평 치조제 폭 손실을 대조군 대비 유의하게 억제했으나 (17.81 ± 3.97%, 19 ± 2.77% vs 41.79 ± 10.3%, P < 0.001) 두 군 간 차이는 없었음; 조직학적으로는 GP I이 층판골 (Lamellar Bone) 면적비율 최고 (54.80 ± 6.69% vs GP II 27.34 ± 5.74% vs 대조군 22.21 ± 3.66%, P = 0.001)이고 잔존 염증 없음, GP II는 염증 소견 더 많고 대조군이 층판골 최저.
임상·방사선학적으로는 콜라겐 스펀지 단독이 콜라겐+이종골과 동등했으나, 이종골 포함 BARP 프로토콜이 조직학적으로는 더 우수한 골질(층판골 多, 염증 少)을 보여 — 차원적/방사선학적 동등성이 실제 조직학적 차이를 가릴 수 있음을 시사.

## Summary
This double-blind, 3-arm RCT (n=36 extraction sockets, Al-Azhar University, Cairo) tested a novel "Biologically-oriented Alveolar Ridge Preservation" (BARP) protocol — a layered collagen sponge + bovine xenograft sandwich restricted to the coronal socket — against collagen sponge alone and spontaneous healing, evaluating outcomes clinically (keratinized gingiva), radiographically (CBCT ridge width/height at 1/3/5 mm), and histologically (6-month core biopsy at implant placement). Both active treatment groups significantly limited soft- and hard-tissue dimensional change compared to the spontaneous-healing control, with no significant difference between the two active groups on any clinical or radiographic measure. However, histomorphometry separated the groups: the xenograft-containing BARP group (GP I) had significantly more lamellar bone and no residual inflammation, while the collagen-alone group (GP II) showed more woven bone, more residual inflammation, and the control group the least mature bone overall. The authors frame this as evidence that histologic examination can reveal biologically meaningful differences between ARP protocols that dimensional/radiographic measures alone report as equivalent — directly responding to the 2019 EFP/AO systematic review and meta-analysis's conclusion that no single ARP modality is demonstrably superior.

## Key Contributions
- First RCT comparing the BARP layered collagen+xenograft technique against collagen sponge alone and spontaneous healing with combined clinical, radiographic, and histomorphometric endpoints
- Shows clinical/radiographic equivalence between two ARP protocols can coexist with a real histological difference in bone maturity and inflammation
- 6-month (longer than typical 3–4 month) histomorphometric window, argued to better capture vital bone maturation
- Multimodal endpoint set (keratinized gingiva dimensions + CBCT ridge width/height + histomorphometry) in a single trial

## Methodology
Double-blind (patient-blinded; separate blinded examiner per outcome domain), parallel 3-arm RCT, NCT06896097, REC-ME-25-03. n=36 single-rooted maxillary anterior/premolar extraction sockets (ages 20–60), randomized 1:1:1: GP I (collagen sponge + bovine xenograft, BARP-style 3-layer placement), GP II (collagen sponge alone), GP III (spontaneous healing, no graft). All flapless extractions. Primary outcome: CBCT vertical ridge dimensional change. Secondary outcomes: CBCT horizontal ridge width (1/3/5 mm), keratinized gingiva width/thickness, and histomorphometric bone quality (lamellar bone%, woven bone%, residual graft%) from core biopsies taken at 6-month implant placement. Sample size (12/group) powered via G*Power (effect size 0.32, power 90%, α=0.05). Stats: one-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc, chi-square, ICC, Shapiro–Wilk; SPSS v25.
