## Three-line Summary

Mouse knockout study (global and GLI1-conditional Ddr2 deletion, n=6–10 per group) demonstrating that DDR2 collagen receptor is required for maxillary first molar extraction socket healing, assessed by μCT, histology, lineage tracing, and polarized-light collagen analysis over 4 weeks.

Global Ddr2 deletion reduced socket bone volume (BV/TV) to 57% of wildtype at 1 week, 71% at 2 weeks, and 81% at 4 weeks, accompanied by ~30% less cell proliferation, significantly disrupted collagen fibril organization at 1 week, and reduced bone mineral density at all time points; DDR2-lineage cells comprised up to 60% of socket cells at 1 week, predominantly co-localizing with the preosteoblast marker OSX (~80%).

DDR2 functions substantially through GLI1+ skeletal progenitor cells (conditional knockout: 33% BV/TV inhibition vs. 43% for global), and its activating GVMGFO collagen motif is a candidate target for engineered scaffolds to enhance alveolar bone regeneration.

## Summary

DDR2 (Discoidin Domain Receptor 2, DDR2) is a collagen-activated receptor tyrosine kinase expressed in alveolar bone, periodontal ligament, and dental follicle, with known roles in skeletal development and long bone/calvarial repair. This study from Kamath et al. (University of Michigan / Ohio State) is the first to demonstrate DDR2 is required for alveolar bone repair in tooth extraction sockets, using a combination of global knockout (Ddr2LacZ/LacZ), conditional knockout (Gli1-CreERT2;Ddr2fl/fl), and lineage tracing (Ddr2-CreERT2;Rosa26-tdTomato; Gli1-CreERT2;Rosa26-tdTomato) in 4-week-old mice with maxillary first molar extraction.

At all time points assessed (1, 2, 4 weeks post-extraction), global Ddr2 deficiency significantly reduced socket healing: BV/TV was 57%, 71%, and 81% of wildtype at 1, 2, and 4 weeks respectively. Trabecular microarchitecture (thickness, spacing, number) was not significantly different, suggesting a quantitative rather than qualitative bone defect. Cell proliferation (EdU incorporation) was reduced ~30% at 3 days post-extraction with no change in apoptosis. Picrosirius Red polarized-light analysis revealed significantly less organized collagen fibril matrix (lower degree of linear polarization, DoLP) in Ddr2-deficient sockets at 1 week, with gradual normalization over 4 weeks.

Lineage tracing showed DDR2-expressing cells rapidly migrate into sockets and comprise up to 60% of all socket cells at 1 week, with ~80% co-localizing with preosteoblast marker OSX by that time — consistent with a skeletal progenitor cell (SPC) identity. DDR2 and GLI1 lineage cells substantially overlap and share progenitor behavior. Conditional Ddr2 deletion in GLI1+ cells partially phenocopied global knockout (33% vs. 43% BV/TV inhibition), confirming DDR2 functions largely in this hedgehog-pathway SPC population, though residual healing indicates additional DDR2-independent repair mechanisms also operate.

## Key Contributions

- First demonstration that DDR2 collagen receptor is required for alveolar bone repair in extraction sockets
- DDR2+ cells are major skeletal progenitors in socket healing: up to 60% of socket cells at 1 week are DDR2-lineage; ~80% co-localize with OSX (preosteoblast marker)
- DDR2 primarily functions through GLI1+ skeletal progenitor cells (hedgehog pathway)
- Mechanistic role: DDR2 promotes cell proliferation (~30% reduction without it) and collagen fibril organization (disrupted DoLP at 1 week) during alveolar bone repair
- GVMGFO collagen-binding motif (distinct from integrin-binding GFOGER) proposed as scaffold target; PLLA nanofibrous scaffolds with dual GVMGFO/GFOGER peptides may enhance alveolar bone regeneration
- Human relevance: loss-of-function DDR2 mutations cause SMED-SLAC (Spondylo-Meta-Epiphyseal Dysplasia with Short Limbs and Abnormal Calcifications), characterized by severe craniofacial defects and abnormal dentition

## Methodology

**Species / model**: Mus musculus, 4-week-old mice
**Experimental groups**:
- Global knockout: Ddr2LacZ/LacZ (null allele via LacZ knock-in into exon 8) vs. wildtype littermates
- Expression mapping: Ddr2-LacZ knock-in (β-galactosidase reporter)
- Lineage tracing: Ddr2-CreERT2;Rosa26-tdTomato and Gli1-CreERT2;Rosa26-tdTomato
- Conditional knockout: Gli1-CreERT2;Ddr2fl/fl (tamoxifen 80 µg/g × 3 days i.p., extraction 48 h after last injection)

**Procedure**: Maxillary first molar (M1) extraction; sockets harvested at 1 day, 3 days, 1, 2, 4 weeks post-extraction

**Sample sizes**: n=6–10 per group for μCT; ≥3 for histology/IF; approximately equal sexes; sexes pooled (no significant sex differences found)

**Outcome measures**:
- μCT: BV/TV, BMD, trabecular thickness/spacing/number
- Histology: H&E (gross bone fill)
- Collagen organization: Picrosirius Red with micropolarizer array camera — degree of linear polarization (DoLP) and standard deviation of angle of polarization (SD AoP)
- Proliferation: EdU incorporation; Apoptosis: TUNEL
- Immunofluorescence: OSX/SP7 (preosteoblast), BSP/IBSP (osteoblast), DDR2
- Lineage tracing: β-galactosidase and tdTomato co-localization with lineage markers

**Statistics**: GraphPad Prism 10; one- or two-way ANOVA + Fisher's LSD or unpaired t-test; P<0.05

**Limitation**: Juvenile mice (4 weeks) — results may not generalize to adult alveolar bone repair

## Results

| Outcome | Ddr2−/− vs. WT | Time point |
|---|---|---|
| BV/TV | 57% of WT | 1 week |
| BV/TV | 71% of WT | 2 weeks |
| BV/TV | 81% of WT | 4 weeks |
| BMD | Significantly reduced | All time points |
| Trabecular parameters | No significant difference | All time points |
| Cell proliferation (EdU) | ~30% reduction | 3 days post-extraction |
| Apoptosis (TUNEL) | No significant difference | 3 days post-extraction |
| Collagen DoLP | Significantly reduced | 1 week post-extraction |
| DDR2-lineage cells in socket | Up to 60% of total cells | 1 week |
| DDR2-lineage + OSX colocalization | ~80% | 1 week |
| DDR2-lineage + BSP colocalization | ~35% | 7 days |
| Gli1-conditional Ddr2 KO: BV/TV inhibition | ~33% | 1 week |
| Gli1-conditional Ddr2 KO: BMD inhibition | ~24% | 1 week |

Key mechanistic insight: DDR2-dependent collagen matrix organization may activate mechanosensory Hippo pathway (YAP/TAZ nuclear localization), consistent with findings from DDR2's role in heterotopic ossification and cranial suture biology. The GVMGFO epitope on fibrillar collagen activates DDR2 synergistically with β1 integrin-binding GFOGER, suggesting dual-peptide scaffolds as a therapeutic strategy.
