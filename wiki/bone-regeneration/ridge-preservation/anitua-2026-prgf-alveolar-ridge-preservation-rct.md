## Three-line Summary

RCT of 46 patients receiving PRGF (혈소판 풍부 성장인자, PRGF) vs spontaneous healing after aesthetic-zone single-tooth extraction, assessing soft tissue, histomorphometric, and dimensional outcomes at 12 weeks.

PRGF significantly increased new bone formation (median 48.7% vs 36.1%, p=0.024), reduced day-3 pain (p=0.036), and improved soft tissue healing indices on days 3, 5, and 7 post-extraction.

PRGF is a viable biological adjunct for socket management in the aesthetic zone, accelerating both soft tissue closure and histological bone fill, though dimensional mm-values and long-term implant outcomes are not reported in the abstract.

## Summary

According to PubMed ([DOI: 10.1007/s00784-026-06769-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00784-026-06769-z)), this randomized controlled trial by Eduardo Anitua's group evaluated PRGF — a single-spin autologous platelet concentrate — as a socket treatment adjunct versus spontaneous healing in 46 patients undergoing simple tooth extraction in the aesthetic zone with planned subsequent implant placement.

PRGF (혈소판 풍부 성장인자, PRGF) is Anitua's proprietary platelet preparation, distinct from PRF (platelet-rich fibrin) and PRP; it is produced at low speed centrifugation and retains physiological growth factor concentrations in an autologous fibrin matrix.

The trial measured healing at multiple time points: pain and soft tissue quality at 3, 7, and 15 days; histomorphometric bone formation and dimensional ridge changes at 12 weeks via core biopsy and clinical measurement.

## Key Contributions

1. **First ARP-specific PRGF RCT**: Provides RCT-level evidence for PRGF as a socket adjunct in the aesthetic zone, where dimensional preservation is clinically critical.
2. **Histomorphometric quantification**: Bone biopsy at 12 weeks gives direct % new bone formation — PRGF 48.7% (range 31.9–92.3%) vs control 36.1% (15.8–58.9%), p=0.024.
3. **Early soft tissue advantage**: Landry's soft tissue healing index significantly improved on days 3, 5, and 7 — suggesting PRGF facilitates primary epithelialization and soft-tissue closure.
4. **Pain reduction**: Day-3 pain statistically lower in PRGF group (p=0.036), consistent with growth-factor–mediated anti-inflammatory and regenerative effects.
5. **Dimensional stability**: PRGF contributed to improved alveolar ridge dimensional stability at 12 weeks (direction confirmed, mm values not in abstract).

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomized controlled clinical trial, parallel groups
- **n**: 46 patients
- **Inclusion**: Simple-tooth extraction in aesthetic zone + planned implant placement
- **Intervention**: Socket filled with PRGF (autologous) vs spontaneous healing (control)
- **Follow-up**: Days 3, 7, 15 (soft tissue/pain); 12 weeks (biopsy + dimensions)
- **Primary outcomes**: New bone formation % (histomorphometry), alveolar ridge dimensions
- **Secondary outcomes**: Postoperative pain, Landry's soft tissue healing index, inflammation index, adverse events
- **Limitations**: Abstract only — dimensional mm data not extractable; single-center aesthetic-zone focus; no implant-level outcomes reported; blinding details and randomization method not ascertainable from abstract
