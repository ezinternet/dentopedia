## Three-line Summary

n=9 split-mouth RCT; cerabone® plus ± simvastatin 1 mg/0.2 mL saline, 3-month trephine biopsy histomorphometry (University of Zagreb, Croatia).

No significant differences in any histomorphometric parameter: NB/TV 31.0% vs 26.2% (p=0.45), CT/TV 59.1% vs 64.3% (p=0.31), BSM/TV 9.9% vs 9.5% (p=0.91).

Negative result — simple admixture of simvastatin with a slowly resorbing bovine xenograft is biologically safe but histologically neutral at 3 months; translational barrier confirmed in humans.

## 세줄요약

n=9 분할구강 RCT; cerabone® plus ± 심바스타틴(스타틴, Simvastatin) 1mg, 3개월 트레핀 생검 조직형태계측(골-임플란트 접촉률, Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC 아닌 NB/TV).

신생골 비율(NB/TV) 31.0% vs 26.2%(p=0.45), 결합조직 비율(CT/TV) 59.1% vs 64.3%(p=0.31), 잔존 이식재 비율(BSM/TV) 9.9% vs 9.5%(p=0.91) — 어느 지표도 유의차 없음.

부정적 결과 — 이종이식재와의 단순 혼합 방식으로는 초기 무기질화 조직 형성 향상 불가; 서방형(Controlled-Release) 전달 시스템이 필요함을 확인.

## Why Ingested

**Critical negative evidence for local statin adjunct therapy in socket preservation.** This is the first published human histomorphometric split-mouth RCT demonstrating that locally applied simvastatin 1 mg does NOT significantly improve bone formation over cerabone® alone at 3 months. The study provides essential clinical counterbalance to the body of animal and in-vitro statin data, as well as to the optimistic signals in some periodontal RCTs.

Directly complements [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/kommuri-2025-efficacy-of-statins-in-tissue]], which synthesizes statin effects on tissue regeneration, with this first human histomorphometric null result specifically for socket preservation. Also contextualizes findings from [[bone-regeneration/tale-2026-simvastatin-guided-bone-regeneration-sr]], the recent SR on simvastatin-guided bone regeneration, by adding a controlled human biopsy-level data point confirming translational barriers.

Without papers like this, the wiki would present an unduly optimistic view of statin adjuncts based primarily on preclinical data. The negative result here is genuine and clinically relevant: simple admixture ≠ sustained drug delivery.

## Study Design

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Prospective split-mouth clinical pilot RCT |
| n | 9 patients (5F/4M; mean age 56 yr, range 29–79) |
| Control | cerabone® plus alone (Botiss Biomaterials, 0.5–1 mm granules) |
| Experimental | cerabone® plus + simvastatin 1 mg/0.2 mL saline (Calbiochem) |
| Randomization | Computer-generated (randomizer.org); allocation concealed |
| Endpoint | 3-month trephine biopsy (Ø 2 mm, 8 mm depth) |
| Analysis | Paired t-test + Wilcoxon signed-rank; Cohen's d reported |

Inclusion: bilateral non-adjacent tooth extraction (contralateral quadrants, same jaw), systemically healthy (ASA I–II), intact socket walls, no systemic statin use. All sites covered with Jason® collagen membrane; primary closure.

## Key Results

| Outcome | cerabone® Plus | cerabone® + Simvastatin | p | Cohen's d |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NB/TV — Newly formed bone (%) | 31.0 ± 15.5 | 26.2 ± 11.6 | 0.45 | 0.27 |
| CT/TV — Connective tissue (%) | 59.1 ± 12.0 | 64.3 ± 9.4 | 0.31 | −0.36 |
| BSM/TV — Residual graft (%) | 9.9 ± 8.5 | 9.5 ± 9.7 | 0.91 | 0.03 |

No statistically significant differences. Both groups showed comparable healing: CT predominant (~60%), NB ~30%, BSM ~10%. Modest non-significant CT/TV elevation in statin group (~+5%) may reflect transient early fibrovascular/angiogenic activity. No adverse events, no graft complications, no systemic or local statin-related reactions in any patient.

## Mechanistic Interpretation

The null result is pharmacologically coherent:

1. **Rapid drug clearance**: Simple admixture of simvastatin into xenograft provides no controlled release; the drug likely washes out or adsorbs to graft surface before reaching osteoprogenitor cells at effective concentrations.
2. **BMP-2 pathway threshold**: Sustained low-micromolar concentrations required for BMP-2/Ras/PI3K/Akt/ERK osteoanabolic signaling; bolus delivery cannot maintain these levels.
3. **Xenograft barrier**: Dense slowly-resorbing bovine bone (cerabone®) with compact pore architecture restricts drug diffusion to osteogenic cells within the healing compartment.
4. **CT/TV shift**: Not indicative of impaired osteogenesis; statins enhance VEGF/eNOS pathways → early fibrovascular phase is biologically plausible and may precede later mineralization.

## Limitations

- **n=9 only**: Exploratory pilot; wide 95% CIs (NB/TV: −8.7 to +18.4); underpowered for small-to-moderate effects
- No pharmacokinetic data on simvastatin release from cerabone®
- No molecular markers (BMP-2, VEGF, osteocalcin) — mechanistic interpretation limited
- 3-month endpoint only; no long-term follow-up
- Single central 2-mm trephine may miss spatial heterogeneity within socket
- Split-mouth design reduces but does not eliminate site-specific anatomic/vascular confounders

## Clinical Takeaway

Local simvastatin 1 mg + cerabone® is **safe** but **histologically neutral** at 3 months. Clinicians should not expect improved socket preservation outcomes from simple statin admixture with xenograft. Future protocols require controlled-release platforms (hydrogels, polymeric microspheres, electrospun membranes) and optimized dosing before clinical adoption. Larger RCTs with molecular endpoints needed before firm efficacy conclusions.
