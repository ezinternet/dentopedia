## Three-line Summary
Line 1: Split-mouth clinical study (n=13 patients, ≥2 extractions each) comparing collagen plug vs Bio-Oss + Surgicel mesh in extraction sockets.
Line 2: Significant difference in buccolingual wound closure (p=0.045) favoring collagen; pain NRS higher with Bio-Oss overall (p=0.397 over 7 days; p=0.004 on day 5).
Line 3: Collagen supported faster wound healing and decreased pain perception than xenograft bovine bone.

줄2: 협설측 창상 폐쇄에서 유의한 차이(p=0.045)로 콜라겐 우위; NRS 통증은 Bio-Oss 군에서 높았으나 7일간 유의차 없음(5일차 p=0.004).

## Summary
This split-mouth clinical trial compared collagen plug (Collaplug) versus xenograft bovine bone (Bio-Oss) covered with cellulose mesh (Surgicel) for alveolar ridge preservation. The study found significantly better buccolingual wound closure with collagen (p=0.045), and although pain scores were higher with Bio-Oss, the difference was only statistically significant on day 5. Collagen appeared to support faster wound healing and lower pain perception in the immediate post-extraction period.

## Key Contributions
- Direct split-mouth comparison of collagen plug vs xenograft+mesh for ARP
- Concurrent evaluation of soft tissue healing and postoperative pain
- Highlights differential healing kinetics between rapidly resorbing collagen scaffold vs slowly resorbing xenograft

## Methodology
Split-mouth crossover trial with 13 patients requiring ≥2 extractions in same jaw. One socket filled with Collaplug, contralateral/matched socket with Bio-Oss + Surgicel. Wound closure measured at buccolingual and mesiodistal dimensions; NRS pain recorded daily for 7 days, with follow-up at days 3,7,14.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Buccolingual wound closure | p=0.045 (favoring collagen) |
| Mesiodistal wound closure | p=0.204 (ns) |
| NRS pain (overall 7 days) | p=0.397 (ns) |
| NRS pain (day 5) | p=0.004 (favoring collagen) |
