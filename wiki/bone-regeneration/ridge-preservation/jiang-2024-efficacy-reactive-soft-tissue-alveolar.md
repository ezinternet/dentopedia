## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (44 patients recruited, 40 completed; ChiCTR2200061459) sealing extraction sockets **with bone deficiency** with autologous reactive soft tissue (RST) or collagen sponge (CS), both over deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM), and tracking horizontal/vertical ridge dimensions from extraction (T0) to 6 months after ARP (T2), modified Masse healing index at 14 days (T1) and T2, KMW, and biopsy histology at implant placement.

Horizontal ridge gains at 1/3/5 mm below the crest were RST 4.37/5.57/4.16 mm vs CS 3.57/4.66/2.97 mm (authors call them comparable; no p-value given), vertical gains were similar buccal and lingual, and the single significant difference was early soft-tissue healing — HI 6 (RST) vs 7 (CS) at T1, p=0.003 — with no significant difference in KMW change or new-bone proportion.

Both sealants plus DBBM limited ridge resorption in compromised sockets, so on this evidence RST is a **soft-tissue-healing** upgrade rather than a bone-preservation upgrade over collagen sponge — and because the study is non-randomized with no p-values for the dimensional outcomes, that conclusion is about direction, not demonstrated superiority.

## 세줄요약

전향적 코호트 (Prospective Cohort, ChiCTR2200061459, 환자 44명 모집·40명 완료): 골결손이 있는 발치와의 치조제 보존 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP)에서 반응성 연조직 (Reactive Soft Tissue, RST) 대 콜라겐 스펀지 (Collagen Sponge, CS)로 밀봉하되 두 군 모두 탈단백 우골무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)로 충전; 발치 전(T0)과 ARP 6개월 후(T2) 수평·수직 골 차원, 14일(T1)·T2 치유지수 (Healing Index, HI), 각화점막폭 (Keratinized Mucosa Width, KMW), 임플란트 식립 시 생검 조직검사를 추적 — 초록만 인제스트.

치조정 하방 1/3/5 mm 수평 골증가량은 RST 4.37/5.57/4.16 mm vs CS 3.57/4.66/2.97 mm ("비슷함", p값 미제시), 수직 증가량도 협·설측 유사; 유의차는 조기 연조직 치유 하나 — T1 HI 6 (RST) vs 7 (CS), p=0.003 — 이고 KMW 변화·신생골 비율은 차이 없음.

두 재료 모두 DBBM과 함께 골결손 발치와의 흡수를 줄였으므로, 이 근거에서 RST는 **골보존 개선이 아닌 연조직 치유 개선** 선택지다; 비무작위 설계이고 차원 결과의 p값이 없으므로 "방향성"의 결론이지 우월성 입증은 아니다.

## Summary

This prospective cohort (registered ChiCTR2200061459) asks a material-selection question that the socket-seal literature has mostly answered in intact, esthetic-zone sockets: when the socket is **bone-deficient** and must be grafted with DBBM for alveolar ridge preservation (ARP), does it matter whether the sealing layer is autologous **reactive soft tissue (RST)** or a ready-made **collagen sponge (CS)**? Forty-four patients were recruited and 40 completed; all received DBBM fill, with the two arms differing only in the seal.

The outcome battery deliberately spans three tissues. Bone was measured as horizontal dimension at 1, 3 and 5 mm below the crest and as vertical dimension buccal and lingual, from before extraction (T0) to 6 months after ARP (T2). Soft tissue was scored with a modified Masse healing index at 14 days (T1) and at T2, plus keratinized mucosa width (KMW) at T0 and T2. Graft biology was sampled destructively: biopsies taken during implant placement gave new-bone proportion.

The result is a split verdict. Ridge dimensions came out comparable — RST numerically higher at every horizontal level (4.37/5.57/4.16 mm vs 3.57/4.66/2.97 mm at 1/3/5 mm), vertical gains similar on both sides — and neither KMW change nor new-bone proportion differed. The one significant difference was timing of soft-tissue healing: HI 6 for RST vs 7 for CS at 14 days (p=0.003). The authors conclude both sealants plus DBBM limited ridge resorption in bone-deficient sockets, with RST favourable for early healing and "a promising socket sealing material."

What that means at the chair: RST looks like a way to buy faster early mucosal healing, not more millimetres of ridge. Because allocation was not randomized, arm sizes are not reported, and no p-values accompany the dimensional endpoints, the horizontal numbers should be read as a consistent trend in the same direction as the NMA ranking of autologous soft tissue — corroboration, not proof.

## Key Contributions

- Moves the **RST vs collagen sponge** question from the intact/esthetic socket into **bone-deficient sockets requiring DBBM**, the setting where ARP decisions are actually hard.
- Combines **radiographic dimensions + soft-tissue indices + biopsy histology** in one cohort, so the sealant's effect can be attributed to the right tissue compartment.
- Finds the effect **compartment-specific**: early soft-tissue healing improves (HI 6 vs 7, p=0.003) while bone dimensions, KMW change, and new-bone proportion do not differ significantly — a more actionable result than an overall ranking.
- Provides a prospective, registered (ChiCTR2200061459) data point supporting the autologous-soft-tissue-first position that the sealing-material NMA reaches by ranking.
- Extends the same group's RST-vs-ACS work in immediate implants from **open-wound closure** to **graft containment**, tightening the inference that RST's value is mucosal, not osteogenic.

## Methodology

- **Design**: prospective cohort study, registered ChiCTR2200061459; not randomized on the abstract's description.
- **Sample**: 44 patients recruited → 40 completed.
- **Inclusion setting**: teeth with **bone deficiency** undergoing extraction and ARP.
- **Arms**: RST (autologous reactive soft tissue) as socket seal vs CS (collagen sponge) as socket seal; **DBBM graft fill in both**.
- **Timepoints**: T0 before extraction; T1 = 14 days after ARP; T2 = 6 months after ARP.
- **Outcomes**: horizontal bone dimension 1/3/5 mm below crest (T0→T2); vertical bone dimension buccal and lingual (T0→T2); modified Masse healing index (T1, T2); KMW (T0, T2); histology — new bone proportion from biopsies at implant placement.
- **Not recoverable from abstract**: arm sizes, allocation method, statistical tests, RST harvest technique, definition of "bone deficiency," dropout reasons.

## Results

| Outcome | RST | Collagen sponge | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizontal ridge gain, 1 mm below crest | 4.37 mm | 3.57 mm | not reported ("comparable") |
| Horizontal ridge gain, 3 mm below crest | 5.57 mm | 4.66 mm | not reported ("comparable") |
| Horizontal ridge gain, 5 mm below crest | 4.16 mm | 2.97 mm | not reported ("comparable") |
| Vertical bone gain, buccal / lingual | similar | similar | not reported |
| Modified Masse HI, T1 (14 d) | 6 | 7 | **0.003** (better with RST) |
| Modified Masse HI, T2 (6 mo) | not reported | not reported | not reported |
| KMW change, T0→T2 | no significant difference | no significant difference | n.s. |
| New bone proportion (biopsy) | no significant difference | no significant difference | n.s. |

- **Conclusion as published**: both RST and CS with DBBM "effectively reduced ridge resorption in sockets with bone deficiency"; RST showed "a more favorable effect on the early healing of sockets."
- **Scale direction**: the authors treat 6 as better than 7, i.e. lower modified Masse score = better healing in this range.
- **Magnitude caveat**: the horizontal differences are 0.8–1.2 mm in RST's favour with no dispersion or test reported — not interpretable as a significant advantage from the abstract alone.
