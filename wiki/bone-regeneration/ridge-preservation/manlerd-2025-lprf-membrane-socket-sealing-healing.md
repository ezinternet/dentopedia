## Three-line Summary

Prospective 3-arm RCT (n=45 extraction sockets, Mahidol University Bangkok) compared socket sealing with L-PRF membrane, collagen sponge, or spontaneous healing over 21 days.

L-PRF significantly improved soft tissue healing index at day 7 (p=0.002) and reduced pain at day 5 (p=0.026–0.036) vs both comparators, but showed no significant benefit in wound margin distance reduction or wound closure area reduction (L-PRF vs collagen sponge) at any time point.

L-PRF offers short-term symptom relief and faster early epithelial healing appearance but does not accelerate actual wound closure — clinical value is limited to first-week comfort, not enhanced soft-tissue regeneration.

L-PRF는 7일째 연조직 치유지수(p=0.002)와 5일째 통증(p=0.026–0.036)에서 유의하게 우수했으나, 창상 변연 거리 감소율 및 창상 폐쇄 면적 감소율(L-PRF vs 콜라겐 스펀지)에서는 어느 시점에서도 유의한 차이가 없었다.

## Summary

This prospective, randomized, controlled, 3-arm trial from Mahidol University (Bangkok) tested whether leukocyte- and platelet-rich fibrin (L-PRF) membrane sealing of the extraction socket orifice improves soft-tissue healing compared with collagen sponge sealing or spontaneous (unsealed) healing. Forty-five extraction sockets (15/group) were followed for 21 days with wound margin distance measurement, a validated soft-tissue healing index, daily pain diaries, and — as a secondary outcome unique to this trial — digital-scanner-based 3D wound closure area measurement (L-PRF vs collagen sponge only).

L-PRF produced clear **early** benefits: a significantly better Landry healing index at day 7 and significantly lower pain at day 5, versus both collagen sponge and spontaneous healing. However, on the outcomes that most directly reflect actual wound closure — buccolingual/mesiodistal wound margin distance reduction (all three groups) and 3D wound closure area reduction (L-PRF vs collagen sponge) — **no significant differences were found at any follow-up point (days 7, 14, 21)**. The authors' own conclusion is deliberately measured: L-PRF offers short-term symptomatic relief (comfort, reduced inflammation appearance) but does not demonstrate an advantage for accelerating post-extraction soft-tissue regeneration at a clinically relevant level. This negative secondary-outcome finding is the paper's most clinically important takeaway and should not be overshadowed by the positive early healing-index/pain results.

## Key Contributions

- Directly compares L-PRF **alone** (no bone graft) against collagen sponge and spontaneous healing in a general (non-smoker-restricted) extraction population — most prior PRF-ARP RCTs combine PRF with a graft or restrict to special populations (e.g., smokers).
- Introduces a 3D digital wound closure area outcome (TRIOS3 intraoral scan + GOM Inspect STL superimposition) as a more objective secondary measure than 2D probe-based wound margin distance — and this more objective outcome is the one that shows no L-PRF benefit.
- Cleanly separates "soft tissue healing appearance / patient comfort" (L-PRF wins early) from "wound closure kinetics" (no L-PRF advantage) — a distinction blurred in earlier PRF socket-sealing literature.
- Reinforces a mechanistic explanation for collagen sponge's inferior early healing-index/pain performance (citing Guo et al murine data): collagen sponge lacks L-PRF's cellular/growth-factor/fibrin-matrix components and may transiently reduce cell density in the early healing phase.

## Methodology

**Design**: Prospective, randomized, controlled, parallel, 3-arm RCT (Dec 2022–Aug 2024), CONSORT-reported, TCTR20250812006.

**Sample**: 45 extraction sockets randomized 15/group (L-PRF, collagen sponge, spontaneous healing/control); 3 collagen-sponge sites excluded (infection, loss to follow-up) and replaced under the original allocation protocol; final analysis n=48 (3 excluded, no outcome data). Single surgeon performed all extractions; allocation concealed (opaque envelopes, third-party assignment); surgeon and statistician blinded.

**Inclusion/exclusion**: ≥18y, ASA I/II, intact buccal bone plate confirmed intraoperatively; excluded bisphosphonate use, malignancy/radiotherapy history, active infection, uncontrolled periodontal disease, smoking >10 cigs/day, pregnancy/lactation.

**Interventions**:
- **L-PRF**: 10 mL venous blood, Intra-Spin centrifuge 2,700 rpm × 12 min → yellow middle layer compressed 5 min into a membrane, placed over the socket, secured with crisscross suture (extra layer for large sockets).
- **Collagen sponge**: Collaplug (Zimmer Dental) inserted into socket, crisscross suture.
- **Control**: crisscross suture only, socket left unsealed.

**Outcomes**:
- Primary: wound margin distance reduction % (UNC-15 probe, buccolingual + mesiodistal, days 0/7/14/21); Landry soft tissue healing index (1–5, days 7/14/21); postoperative pain (11-point NRS, 4x/day to day 7).
- Secondary (L-PRF vs collagen sponge only): 3D wound closure area reduction % (TRIOS3 scan + STL superimposition, days 0/7/14/21).

**Statistics**: One-way ANOVA + LSD post-hoc (primary), independent t-test (secondary), Fisher's exact/chi-square (categorical covariates); SPSS v18; α=0.05.

## Results

| Outcome | L-PRF | Collagen sponge | Control | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Healing index, day 7 | 3.93 ± 0.70 | 3.20 ± 0.56 | 3.20 ± 0.56 | **0.002** |
| Healing index, day 14/21 | 4.60→5.00 | 4.33→4.73 | 4.13→4.87 | NS (0.077 / 0.359) |
| Pain, day 5 | 0.07 ± 0.26 | 0.63 ± 0.85 | 0.60 ± 0.75 | **0.045** |
| Pain, day 1/3 | lower trend | higher | higher | NS (0.055 / 0.059) |
| Buccolingual wound margin reduction %, day 21 | 66.0 ± 13.9 | 67.2 ± 20.0 | 63.0 ± 24.9 | NS (0.843) |
| Mesiodistal wound margin reduction %, day 21 | 38.2 ± 21.0 | 47.8 ± 18.6 | 42.0 ± 21.0 | NS (0.435) |
| 3D wound closure area reduction %, day 21 (L-PRF vs collagen sponge) | 80.1 ± 10.3 | 80.4 ± 14.0 | — | NS (0.954) |

**Positive findings**: L-PRF significantly better than both comparators on day-7 healing index (p=0.002, 95% CI [0.28,1.18]) and day-5 pain (vs collagen sponge p=0.026; vs control p=0.036). All group differences converge by day 21 (ceiling effect).

**Negative/null findings (secondary outcome and most of the primary wound-margin data)**: No significant difference among any of the three groups in buccolingual or mesiodistal wound margin distance reduction at any timepoint. No significant difference between L-PRF and collagen sponge in 3D wound closure area reduction at any timepoint — L-PRF did **not** outperform the far cheaper, non-autologous collagen sponge on the trial's most objective closure measure.
