## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (n=30 maxillary patients, Plovdiv): socket preservation with FDBA or PRF → CBCT HU at 4 months → ISQ (Osstell) + trephine histomorphometry at implant placement.

CBCT HU significantly correlated with ISQ (vestibulo-oral r=0.392, p=0.032; mesiodistal r=0.407, p=0.026) and strongly with % newly formed bone by histomorphometry (r=0.776, p<0.001).

After FDBA/PRF socket preservation, 4-month CBCT bone density is a significant moderate predictor of primary stability and a strong predictor of true bone regeneration quality.

## 세줄요약

전향적 코호트 (n=30 상악 환자, 플로브디프): 동결건조골(FDBA) 또는 PRF 소켓 보존 → 4개월 CBCT 하운스필드 단위(Hounsfield Unit, HU) → 임플란트 식립 직후 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) + 트레핀 조직계측.

CBCT HU ↔ ISQ: 전정구개 축 r=0.392(p=0.032), 근원심 축 r=0.407(p=0.026); HU ↔ 신생골 비율: r=0.776(p<0.001) — HU가 생물학적 골재생을 반영함을 직접 조직학적으로 검증.

FDBA/PRF 소켓 보존 4개월 후 CBCT 골밀도(HU)는 임플란트 1차 안정성의 유의한 중등도 예측인자이며 조직학적 골재생 질의 강한 예측인자.

## Summary

This prospective cohort study from Medical University of Plovdiv uniquely combined three simultaneous measurements at the same post-socket-preservation implant sites: CBCT bone density (Hounsfield units at 4 months post-ARP), implant stability quotient (ISQ via Osstell Idx, measured immediately post-placement), and histomorphometric % newly formed bone (trephine core, ImageJ analysis). Socket preservation was performed with FDBA or PRF in 30 maxillary patients. CBCT HU showed significant moderate correlations with ISQ on both vestibulo-oral and mesiodistal axes, and a strong correlation with histomorphometric bone formation. The results support CBCT HU as a biologically meaningful proxy for bone regeneration quality after allograft/PRF socket preservation — consistent with Abdulkarim 2021, which showed allograft sites preserve the HU–clinical quality correlation that xenograft sites lose.

## Key Contributions

- Only paper directly correlating post-ARP CBCT HU with both ISQ and histomorphometry in the same sites
- Validates CBCT HU as a biologically grounded metric after FDBA/PRF ARP: strong HU↔%new bone (r=0.776) means HU reflects real bone, not residual graft opacity
- Moderate HU↔ISQ correlation (r≈0.40) establishes HU as a clinically useful but not standalone predictor
- 4-month timing (post-FDBA/PRF) appears sufficient to achieve meaningful bone regeneration measurable by HU
- Consistent with Abdulkarim 2021's allograft-safe finding: FDBA resorbs fully → HU measures native bone

## Methodology

Prospective cohort; n=30 patients, 30 maxillary implants (second premolar to second premolar); Medical University of Plovdiv, Bulgaria. Socket preservation with FDBA or PRF at extraction. CBCT bone density (HU) at 4 months. Implant placement: trephine core harvested for histomorphometry (ImageJ, % newly formed bone); ISQ measured immediately post-placement with Osstell Idx. Correlation analysis.

## Results

| Correlation | r | p | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| CBCT HU ↔ ISQ (vestibulo-oral) | 0.392 | 0.032 | Moderate |
| CBCT HU ↔ ISQ (mesiodistal) | 0.407 | 0.026 | Moderate |
| CBCT HU ↔ % new bone (histomorphometry) | **0.776** | **<0.001** | **Strong** |

HU explains ~16% of ISQ variance (r²≈0.16) — meaningful, but implant design, drill protocol, and cortical engagement also contribute substantially.
