## Three-line Summary

Prospective case series (n=49 patients, 62 extraction sites) evaluating alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) with a sugar-crosslinked collagen/hydroxyapatite (SCLC/HA) sponge under intentional open healing (cross-suture only, no membrane, no primary flap advancement); inclusion required ≥50% residual buccal bone post-extraction.

All 62 sites healed without complication; soft tissue closure by secondary intention at 3–4 weeks; of 54 implant-designated sites, 39 (72%) needed no additional augmentation, 12 (22%) required concurrent lateral GBR, and 3 (6%) staged augmentation; 1-year implant survival was 98.2%; late reentry biopsy (>6 months) showed complete xenograft remodeling.

SCLC/HA with open healing is a viable membrane-free ARP option for sockets with ≥50% residual buccal bone, distinguishing this scaffold from slow-resorbing xenografts (pooled 20.49% residual per Benekou 2025 SR+MA); the 72% augmentation-free rate supports clinical utility, though the uncontrolled case series design precludes comparative conclusions.

## One-line Summary
Prospective case series (n=49 patients, 62 sites) of ARP with SCLC/HA sponge and open healing (cross-suture only, no primary closure or membrane): zero healing complications, 72% required no additional augmentation at implant placement, and 1-year implant survival was 98.2%.

## Summary
Friedmann et al. (Quintessence Int 2026) report a prospective case series evaluating a sugar-crosslinked collagen/hydroxyapatite (SCLC/HA) sponge used for alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) under an intentional open-healing protocol — no primary flap closure and no membrane coverage, with a cross-suture removed at day 7. Inclusion required ≥50% residual buccal bone post-extraction. All 62 sites healed without complication; soft tissue coverage was achieved at 3–4 weeks through secondary intention. At implant placement, 39 of 54 implant-designated sites (72%) required no additional augmentation, 12 (22%) needed concomitant lateral GBR, and 3 (6%) staged augmentation. One-year implant survival was 98.2%. Late-entry biopsy (>6 months) demonstrated complete xenograft remodeling — contrasting with the persistent residual graft content (mean 20.49%) documented for slow-resorbing xenografts in the Benekou 2025 SR+MA. The findings support SCLC/HA open-healing ARP as a minimally invasive, membrane-free option for sockets with adequate residual buccal bone.

## Key Contributions
- First case series for SCLC/HA (sugar-crosslinked collagen/HA) in open-healing ARP, without any membrane or primary closure
- Late-entry complete remodeling of SCLC/HA xenograft — distinguishes this scaffold from slow-resorbing DBBM
- 72% augmentation-free implant placement rate confirms clinically meaningful ridge maintenance
- Soft tissue coverage by secondary intention achievable at 3–4 weeks with cross-suture alone
- Inclusion criterion (≥50% residual buccal bone) operationalizes protocol eligibility

## Methodology
- Design: Prospective case series, single center
- n=49 patients, 62 extraction sites
- Scaffold: SCLC/HA sponge (sugar-crosslinked collagen with hydroxyapatite particles)
- Closure: Cross-suture only, removed at day 7; intentional open healing; no primary flap advancement, no barrier membrane
- Inclusion: ≥50% residual buccal bone height post-extraction
- Primary outcomes: healing complications, augmentation need at implant placement, 1-year implant survival
- Secondary: histological status at late reentry (>6 months)
