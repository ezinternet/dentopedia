## One-line Summary
Multicenter RCT (n=42, 4 Italian clinics, 6 months): DBBM-C + resorbable membrane reduced horizontal ridge loss from 57.7% to 23.0% and buccal vertical loss from 3.0mm to 1.5mm after adjacent tooth extraction.

## 한줄요약
다중기관 RCT (n=42, 6개월): 인접 2개 발치 후 탈단백화 소 뼈 광물 콜라겐 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral with Collagen, DBBM-C) + 흡수성 차폐막으로 수평 치조제 폭 감소를 57.7%→23.0%, 협측 수직 감소를 3.0→1.5mm로 줄임.

## Summary
Tooth extraction causes predictable dimensional ridge reduction, posing challenges for subsequent implant placement. This prospective multicenter RCT enrolled 42 patients requiring removal of two adjacent teeth, randomized to DBBM-C (Bio-Oss Collagen) plus resorbable collagen membrane (Test, n=21) or natural healing (Control, n=21) across 4 Italian private specialist clinics. Ridge dimensions were measured by STL digital models at baseline and 6 months post-extraction. The Test group demonstrated significantly less horizontal ridge reduction (23.0% vs 57.7%, p<0.001) and less vertical buccal (−1.5mm vs −3.0mm, p<0.001) and lingual (−1.3mm vs −2.2mm, p<0.001) loss. Limitations include no histological analysis, 6-month endpoint only, and exclusion of severe socket defects.

## Key Contributions
- Multicenter RCT specifically addressing adjacent tooth extraction — more complex wound healing environment than single-tooth ARP
- STL e-model methodology enables precise 3D dimensional analysis without radiation (vs CBCT)
- Confirms DBBM-C + resorbable membrane as effective across 4 independent specialist practices (external validity for specialist setting)
- Horizontal preservation effect (57.7%→23.0%) is clinically significant for implant placement planning
- Adds to the sparse multicenter ARP RCT evidence base

## Methodology
- Design: Prospective multicenter RCT (4 Italian private specialist clinics)
- Population: 42 patients (21 Test, 21 Control), each with 2 adjacent extraction sites
- Intervention: DBBM-C (Bio-Oss Collagen 250mg, Geistlich) + resorbable collagen membrane (Test); natural healing (Control)
- Measurement: STL digital models from stone casts at baseline and 6 months
- Primary endpoint: Horizontal B-L ridge width % change at 3mm below crest
- Secondary endpoints: Vertical buccal and lingual ridge loss (mm)
- Statistics: Mann–Whitney U for between-group comparisons

## Results
| Dimension | Control | Test | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizontal width reduction (3mm) | 57.7% | 23.0% | <0.001 |
| Buccal vertical loss | −3.0 mm | −1.5 mm | <0.001 |
| Lingual vertical loss | −2.2 mm | −1.3 mm | <0.001 |

Both groups lost ridge volume; Test group significantly preserved more volume at all measurement points. No adverse events requiring early termination were reported.
