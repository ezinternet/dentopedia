## Three-line Summary

In vivo clinical and histological study (n=7, University of Milan) evaluating post-extraction socket preservation with DBBM and non-crosslinked porcine collagen matrix, with 6-month outcomes including dimensional measurements and trephine biopsy histomorphometry.

Socket preservation achieved minimal dimensional loss (horizontal 1.21 mm, vertical 0.46 mm); histomorphometry revealed residual xenograft 31.97%, new bone 16.02%, and connective tissue 50.67% at 6 months, with all sites healing uneventfully.

The small sample (n=7) limits generalizability, but results confirm DBBM + collagen matrix is a viable ARP protocol with predictable early bone formation and acceptable resorption of the xenograft scaffold.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상·조직학 연구 (n=7, 밀라노 대학): 탈단백우골 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) + 비교차결합 돼지 콜라겐막 소켓 보존술, 6개월 치수 측정 및 트레핀 생검 조직형태계측.

발치와 보존 후 수평 흡수 1.21 mm·수직 흡수 0.46 mm로 소실 최소화; 조직형태계측상 잔존 이종골 31.97%, 신생골 16.02%, 결합조직 50.67% — 전 증례 합병증 없이 치유 완료.

n=7 소규모로 일반화에 한계가 있으나, DBBM+콜라겐막의 예측 가능한 초기 골형성 및 허용 가능한 이종골 흡수 속도를 확인했다.

## Summary
Clinical + histological study (n=7, University of Milan) evaluating post-extraction socket preservation with demineralized bovine bone mineral (DBBM) and non-crosslinked porcine collagen matrix. Six-month outcomes showed limited dimensional loss and histomorphometric evidence of bone formation alongside residual xenograft.

## Key Contributions
- Horizontal resorption: 1.21mm (p=0.005)
- Vertical resorption: 0.46mm (p=0.004) — minimal loss
- Histomorphometry at 6 months:
  - Residual xenograft: 31.97±3.52%
  - New bone: 16.02±7.06%
  - Connective tissue: 50.67±8.42%
- Collagen matrix: resorbed physiologically → vascularized connective tissue
- All sockets: uneventful clinical healing
