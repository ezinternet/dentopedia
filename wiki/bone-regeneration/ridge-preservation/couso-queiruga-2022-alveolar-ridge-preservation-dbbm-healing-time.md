## Three-line Summary

A three-arm RCT (n=42, non-molar sites, University of Iowa) randomized patients to 3, 6, or 9 months of healing after 치조정보존 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) with 탈단백우골미네랄-콜라겐 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral with Collagen, DBBM-C) sealed with a porcine collagen matrix (Collagen Matrix, CM), measuring histomorphometric, volumetric, linear, implant-related, and patient-reported outcomes at the time of implant placement.

광화조직 (Mineralized Tissue, MT) proportion increased over time (3 mo: 13.53%; 6 mo: 33.33%; 9 mo: 37.05%), while remaining xenograft material decreased (16.94% → 10.69% → 9.46%), with minimal differences between 6 and 9 months confirming DBBM's low biodegradability; 협측골두께 (Buccal Bone Thickness, BBT) ≤1 mm at baseline was strongly and inversely associated with total and facial alveolar bone volume reduction and soft tissue contour loss.

Implant placement was feasible in all 42 sites with primary stability achieved (median insertion torque 35 Ncm); 16.7% required ancillary 골재생유도술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR), of which 85.7% had thin facial bone (≤1 mm) at baseline, supporting a recommendation for 6-month re-entry in most non-molar ARP sites.

아이오와대학교 치과대학에서 비구치부 발치와에 DBBM-C + 돼지 콜라겐 차단막으로 치조정보존 시행 후 3·6·9개월 치유 집단(각 n=14)으로 무작위 배분하여 조직계측·체적·선형·임플란트 관련·환자보고 결과를 임플란트 식립 시점에 종합 평가하였다.

치유 기간이 길수록 광화조직 비율이 증가(13.53% → 33.33% → 37.05%)하고 잔존 이종골 (Remaining Xenograft Material) 비율이 감소(16.94% → 10.69% → 9.46%)하였으나, 6개월과 9개월 사이의 차이는 미미하여 DBBM의 낮은 생분해성을 재확인하였다.

42개 부위 모두 임플란트 1차 안정성 확보에 성공(중앙값 삽입 토크 35 Ncm)하였으나 16.7%에서 추가 GBR이 필요하였고 그 중 85.7%는 발치 당시 협측골두께 ≤1 mm 부위로, 협측골두께가 골·연조직 위축의 가장 강력한 예측인자임을 체적 차원에서 처음으로 확인하였다.

## Summary

This randomized clinical trial (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03659617) is the first human histologic study evaluating healing of DBBM-C-filled extraction sockets beyond 6 months. The study addressed a critical clinical gap: despite widespread use of DBBM-C (Bio-Oss Collagen, Geistlich) for ARP, no data existed on the optimal healing duration before implant re-entry.

Forty-two patients completed the study at the University of Iowa, randomly allocated to 3, 6, or 9 months of healing. ARP was performed via flapless extraction → socket filling with DBBM-C → socket sealing with a porcine CM (Mucograft Seal, Geistlich) → suturing. At re-entry, a trephine biopsy was harvested prior to implant placement for histomorphometric analysis.

Key findings: (1) MT proportion increases significantly from 3 to 6 months but plateaus between 6 and 9 months; (2) DBBM particles maintain a low biodegradability rate even at 9 months; (3) dimensional atrophy predominantly affects the facial aspect and is significantly worse in the 9-month group for volumetric outcomes; (4) BBT at extraction is the strongest predictor of subsequent ridge and soft tissue loss at a volumetric level — a novel finding. Despite effective ARP, 16.7% of sites still required adjunctive GBR at implant placement, with thin facial bone being the dominant risk factor.

## Key Contributions

1. **First histologic data beyond 6 months** for DBBM-C ARP in humans — establishes that the 6-to-9-month window yields minimal additional MT formation.
2. **BBT as volumetric predictor** — first study to link facial bone thickness at extraction to total/facial bone volume reduction and facial soft tissue contour loss.
3. **Practical re-entry recommendation** — 6 months is appropriate for most cases; 3-month re-entry is feasible but carries higher risk of suboptimal bone density requiring osteotomy undersizing.
4. **GBR rate benchmark** — 16.7% ancillary GBR at implant placement (vs. 32–91% in other DBBM-C ARP studies), attributable to strict thin-bone exclusion and standardized augmentation threshold.
5. **Soft tissue insight** — soft tissue thickness remained virtually unchanged horizontally during healing (unlike unassisted sockets), demonstrated via DICOM-STL superimposition.

## Methodology

- **Design**: 3-arm single-centre RCT; CONSORT-compliant; IRB HawkIRB #201806050
- **Sample**: 79 screened → 42 completed (14/group); median age 57.5 y (24–84); 57.1% female; predominantly maxillary non-molar sites (n=39/42)
- **Groups**: A = 3 months (10 wk); B = 6 months (20 wk); C = 9 months (32 wk)
- **Surgical protocol**: Flapless extraction, DBBM-C fill to alveolar crest height, porcine CM seal, Resolon 6-0 sutures; antibiotics 7 days (amoxicillin or clindamycin)
- **Implant systems**: Straumann BL or Astra Tech; trephine biopsy 2.5 mm ID
- **Histomorphometry**: H&E-stained 5-µm decalcified sections; blinded examiner (H.A.W.); ImageJ; intra-examiner ICC = 0.99; 41 biopsies analysed (1 discarded in Group A)
- **Imaging**: Planmeca Emerald S intraoral scanner + i-CAT Next Gen CBCT (0.3 mm voxel); Romexis v5.2.1 + Geomagic Control X for DICOM-STL superimposition
- **Statistics**: Kruskal-Wallis, Spearman/Pearson, Mann-Whitney-Wilcoxon, linear regression; median + range reporting

## Results

### Histomorphometric outcomes

| Tissue | 3 months (A) | 6 months (B) | 9 months (C) | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mineralized tissue | 13.53% | 33.33% | 37.05% | .094 |
| Non-mineralized tissue | 63.32% | 55.55% | 52.48% | .150 |
| Remaining xenograft | 16.94% | 10.69% | 9.46% | .428 |

Trend significant despite p>.05: progressive MT increase and xenograft decrease consistent across groups; 6-to-9-month plateau well demonstrated.

### Implant-related outcomes

- Primary stability achieved in **all 42 sites**; median insertion torque **35 Ncm** (25–45)
- Ancillary GBR at implant placement: **16.7%** (n=7: A:2, B:1, C:4); buccal dehiscence 3–7 mm depth
- **85.7%** (6/7) of sites needing GBR had BBT ≤1 mm at baseline
- No ancillary soft tissue augmentation required

### Volumetric outcomes (median %; statistically significant between groups)

| Outcome | Group A (3 mo) | Group B (6 mo) | Group C (9 mo) | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total bone volume | −6.36% | −7.94% | −12.45% | .001 |
| Facial bone volume | −8.55% | −10.87% | −14.92% | .002 |
| Lingual bone volume | −4.35% | −5.76% | −8.99% | .070 |
| Total ridge contour | −11.35% | −12.92% | −20% | .005 |
| Facial ridge contour | −13.29% | −14.51% | −28.62% | .006 |

Dimensional atrophy predominantly at the **facial aspect**; lingual changes not statistically different between groups.

### Linear bone outcomes (median mm; no significant between-group differences)

- Horizontal ridge width at 1 mm below crest: A −1.2, B −1.35, C −2.4
- Facial bone height: A −0.6, B −0.5, C −0.4 (similar to lingual: ~0.3–0.5 mm)
- **Linear regression**: BBT at extraction inversely predicts ridge width reduction at 1 mm (p=.02) and 3 mm (p=.008)

### Predictors (regression)

- BBT at extraction → total/facial bone volume reduction: p=.001
- BBT at extraction → total ridge contour: p<.001; facial ridge contour: p=.02
- BBT ≤1 mm: strongest single risk factor for requiring ancillary GBR (85.7% of GBR cases)

### Patient-reported outcomes

- No serious adverse events
- Discomfort progressively decreased; no between-group difference
- Overall satisfaction median: **99/100** (range 77–100)
