## Three-line Summary
Four-arm RCT (n=41 patients, single-center, Athens) comparing FDBA+collagen sponge (A), FDBA+free gingival graft (B), DBBM+FGG (C), and FGG alone (D) for alveolar ridge preservation, with clinical vertical/horizontal measurements at extraction and 4 months.

Groups A, B, C had significantly less bone resorption than FGG-alone group D; FDBA vs DBBM showed no practical difference except a marginal buccal-central vertical advantage for xenograft (adjusted β=1.07mm, 95%CI 0.01–2.10, p=0.05); CS and FGG performed equally as sealing materials over FDBA.

Any bone graft (allograft or xenograft) under a soft-tissue seal outperforms soft-tissue sealing alone; allograft and xenograft are practically interchangeable, and collagen sponge is a viable, less-morbid substitute for FGG as a sealing material over FDBA — but this is a 41-patient, abstract-only source with no retrieved effect sizes beyond the one reported comparison.

## 세줄요약
4군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=41명, 아테네 단일기관) — 동결건조골 동종골 (Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft, FDBA)+콜라겐 스펀지 (Collagen Sponge, CS)(A군), FDBA+유리치은이식 (Free Gingival Graft, FGG)(B군), 탈단백우골 이종골 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)+FGG(C군), FGG 단독(D군) 비교 — 발치 직후·4개월 후 임상 계측.

A·B·C군이 D군(FGG 단독) 대비 유의하게 적은 골흡수를 보였고, FDBA와 DBBM은 실질적 차이 없음(협측 중앙부 수직 골흡수만 이종골이 근소하게 우세: 보정 β=1.07mm, 95%신뢰구간 0.01–2.10, p=0.05), FDBA 위에서 CS와 FGG는 실링재로서 동등한 효과.

동종골이든 이종골이든 이식재를 사용하는 것이 연조직 실링만 하는 것보다 우수하며, 동종골과 이종골은 실질적으로 상호대체 가능하고 콜라겐 스펀지는 FDBA 위 실링재로서 FGG의 이환율 낮은 대안이 될 수 있음 — 단 41명 소규모, abstract만 확보된 자료로 보고된 비교 외 효과크기는 확인 불가.

## Summary
This single-center RCT (n=41) randomized post-extraction sockets to four alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) protocols: freeze-dried bone allograft (FDBA) sealed with collagen sponge (CS), FDBA sealed with free gingival graft (FGG), deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM) sealed with FGG, or FGG alone as a no-graft control. Clinical vertical and horizontal measurements were taken immediately after extraction and at 4 months. All three grafted groups significantly outperformed the FGG-alone control, confirming that grafting — regardless of allograft-vs-xenograft choice — is what drives dimensional preservation, not the soft-tissue seal by itself. Allograft and xenograft performed practically identically except for one marginal, threshold-significance (p=0.05) advantage for xenograft at the buccal-central vertical site. Collagen sponge and free gingival graft were equally effective as sealing materials over FDBA, suggesting CS as a lower-morbidity alternative to FGG when a graft is already in place. The source PDF is a paywalled Springer preview page, so this page is built entirely from the structured abstract — no Methods/Results body text, tables, or additional effect sizes were retrievable.

## Key Contributions
- Direct within-trial RCT comparison of allograft (FDBA) vs xenograft (DBBM) for ARP — most of the literature compares these across separate studies/SRs rather than head-to-head in one design.
- Isolates the sealing-material question (CS vs FGG) while holding the graft material constant (FDBA), a contrast not directly covered by the field's main sealing-material network meta-analysis.
- Confirms, at RCT level, that "any graft beats no graft" while "allograft vs xenograft" and "CS vs FGG" are both practically wash — narrowing the clinically decision-relevant question to cost/morbidity/availability rather than material-driven dimensional outcomes.

## Methodology
Single-center RCT, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens. 41 patients randomized to 4 parallel arms: (A) FDBA+CS, (B) FDBA+FGG, (C) DBBM+FGG, (D) FGG alone. Clinical (not radiographic/CBCT) vertical and horizontal measurements at baseline (immediately post-extraction) and 4 months. Trial registered at clinicaltrials.gov (NCT04934813).

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Grafted groups (A, B, C) vs FGG-alone (D) | Significantly less vertical & horizontal bone resorption in A, B, C |
| FDBA vs DBBM, overall | No practical difference |
| FDBA vs DBBM, buccal-central vertical site | DBBM marginally better: adjusted β=1.07mm, 95% CI 0.01–2.10, p=0.05 |
| CS vs FGG as sealing over FDBA (A vs B) | No significant difference in hard tissue dimensions |
