## Three-line Summary

Single-centre, randomized, partially double-blind proof-of-concept RCT (n=40: ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP 15, β-TCP 15, natural healing 10) comparing a low-dose E. coli-derived recombinant human BMP-2 (rhBMP-2) coating on β-TCP against β-TCP alone and natural healing in single-root extraction sockets, with 6-week biopsy histomorphometry as the primary outcome.

New bone volume density at 6 weeks was 7.72 ± 6.01% with ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP vs 2.96 ± 2.23% with β-TCP alone — significantly lower than both other groups (p<0.05) — and 8.37 ± 6.31% with natural healing; residual material was lower than β-TCP alone (10.90 ± 4.04% vs 15.73 ± 4.52%), and fewer patients needed augmentation at implant placement (36% vs 50% vs 89%, CMH χ²=6.21, p=0.0447).

Safety was comparable across all three arms (adverse events 87% vs 80% vs 80%, CMH p=0.3253; every serum BMP-2 ELISA negative; no bone regeneration outside the socket), but the 6-week observation window, n=40, single-centre design and the negative soft-tissue/contour outcome make this hypothesis-generating for the multi-centre pivotal trials the authors state are still required.

## 세줄요약

단일센터·부분 이중눈가림 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT, 개념증명, n=40: ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP 15명·β-TCP 15명·자연치유 10명) — 대장균 유래 저용량 재조합 사람 골형성단백질-2 (recombinant human Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2, rhBMP-2)를 생체모방 칼슘인산염 (biomimetic calcium phosphate, BioCaP) 코팅으로 삼인산칼슘 (β-tricalcium phosphate, β-TCP)에 실어 단근치 발치와에 충실하고, β-TCP 단독·자연치유 (natural healing)와 비교; 1차 종점은 6주 후 조직형태계측 (histomorphometry) 신생골 (new bone) 부피밀도.

6주 신생골 부피밀도는 ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP 7.72 ± 6.01% vs β-TCP 단독 2.96 ± 2.23%(다른 두 군보다 유의하게 낮음, p<0.05) vs 자연치유 8.37 ± 6.31%; 잔존 이식재 (residual graft) 부피밀도는 β-TCP 단독 대비 낮음(10.90 ± 4.04% vs 15.73 ± 4.52%); 임플란트 식립 시 추가 골증대 (bone augmentation) 필요 비율은 36% vs 50% vs 89% (CMH χ²=6.21, p=0.0447).

안전성은 세 군 간 유의한 차이 없음(이상반응 87%·80%·80%, CMH p=0.3253, 혈청 BMP-2 ELISA 전부 음성, 소켓 외 신생골 생성 없음), 다만 6주 관찰기간·n=40·단일센터 한계와 연조직 윤곽(soft tissue contour) 유의한 차이 부재로 저자들은 다중 센터(multicenter) pivotal 시험이 필요하다고 보고함.

## One-line Summary

First human RCT of low-dose E. coli-derived rhBMP-2-coated β-TCP in extraction sockets: 6-week new bone volume density 7.72% vs 2.96% with β-TCP alone vs 8.37% with natural healing; residual material 10.90% vs 15.73%; augmentation needed at implant placement in 36% vs 50% vs 89% (CMH p=0.0447); no safety signal.

## Summary

This single-centre, partially double-blind proof-of-concept RCT is the first human evaluation of ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP — a low-dose (microgram-range, 126.2 μg rhBMP-2 per g) E. coli-derived recombinant human BMP-2 delivered through a biomimetic calcium phosphate coating on β-TCP granules for sustained, locally limited release. Forty patients requiring implant placement after single-root extraction (EDS-1/EDS-2 sockets) were randomized 3:3:2 to ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP, β-TCP alone, or natural healing, all sockets covered by a double-layer resorbable collagen membrane, with 6-week biopsy histomorphometry as the primary endpoint. The BMP-2-coated graft produced 7.72 ± 6.01% new bone volume density versus 2.96 ± 2.23% with β-TCP alone and 8.37 ± 6.31% with natural healing — i.e. it recovered the early bone-formation deficit of the plain alloplast while leaving less residual material (10.90 ± 4.04% vs 15.73 ± 4.52%) — and it was the only arm that reduced the need for bone augmentation at implant placement (36% vs 50% vs 89%, CMH p=0.0447). Safety was indistinguishable across arms (adverse events 87% / 80% / 80%, p=0.3253; all serum BMP-2 ELISAs negative; no ectopic bone), and no dimensional or soft-tissue advantage was demonstrated beyond the 1 mm crestal width measure. The clinical reading is narrow: a biologically active, well-tolerated graft coating exists, but 6 weeks, n=40 and one centre keep this hypothesis-generating, and the authors state that multi-centre pivotal trials are required.

## Key Contributions

- **First human RCT of the low-dose rhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP platform** — previously only in vitro and canine in vivo (Liu 2013; Wei 2020); this is the first randomized human study with a histomorphometric primary outcome.
- **Isolates the coating's contribution**: β-TCP alone produced markedly less new bone than natural healing (2.96% vs 8.37%), while the rhBMP-2-coated version reached parity (7.72%) with less residual material than the uncoated alloplast (10.90% vs 15.73%) — the growth factor, not the calcium-phosphate carrier, drives early bone formation.
- **Connects histology to the implant-placement endpoint** — augmentation need 36% (coated) vs 50% (β-TCP) vs 89% (natural healing), the only clinical-outcome signal in the trial.
- **Safety at microgram dosing** — no group differences in adverse events, soft-tissue healing score or OHIP-14; all serum BMP-2 ELISAs negative; no bone regeneration outside the socket, i.e. the burst-release safety concern of milligram-dose rhBMP-2 did not appear at this sustained low dose.
- **Documents a preclinical-to-clinical transfer failure** — the >3× new bone versus natural healing reported in critical-sized defect models did not reproduce in human sockets, which heal spontaneously from 2–4 weeks.

## Methodology

- **Design**: single-centre, randomized, controlled, partially double-blind RCT (proof-of-concept); ISO 14155:2020, CONSORT; ChiCTR2000035263; Aug 2020 – Dec 2021, Shanghai Ninth People's Hospital
- **n**: 40 (24 female, 16 male) from 44 screened; no dropouts; FAS/SS = 40, SAS = 37 (3 protocol violations, 1 per arm), PPS for histomorphometry = 36
- **Allocation**: randomized block design, 5 blocks × 8 (3 coated : 3 β-TCP : 2 natural healing, by lot); patients and surgeons blinded for the graft arms only (natural-healing control unblinded); outcome assessors fully blinded
- **Eligibility**: single-root tooth, extraction defect sounding class EDS-1 or EDS-2
- **Procedure**: standardized extraction and socket filling; double-layer resorbable collagen membrane (Bio-Gide) over every socket; 0.12% chlorhexidine rinse twice daily for 7 days; implants placed once primary stability achieved
- **Primary outcome**: new bone volume density (%) in the 6-week trephine biopsy (undecalcified PMMA sections, McNeal's tetrachrome / basic fuchsine / toluidine blue, ImageJ)
- **Secondary outcomes**: unmineralized tissue and residual material volume density (%); CBCT bone width (1/3/5 mm below crest) and buccal/lingual height change; intra-oral soft-tissue surface area and width change; number of augmentation procedures at implant placement
- **Safety outcomes**: soft-tissue healing score, OHIP-14, adverse events, serum BMP-2 (ELISA), blood and urine laboratory tests
- **Statistics**: one-way ANOVA, two-way repeated-measures ANOVA for laboratory data, post-hoc comparisons, CMH test with Fisher exact post-hoc, two-sided p<0.05; power 90% at α=5% (G*Power 3)

## Results

**Histomorphometry at 6 weeks (PPS n=36: 14 / 14 / 8)**

| Outcome | ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP | β-TCP | Natural healing |
|---|---|---|---|
| New bone volume density (%) | 7.72 ± 6.01 | 2.96 ± 2.23 | 8.37 ± 6.31 |
| Residual material volume density (%) | 10.90 ± 4.04 | 15.73 ± 4.52 | — |
| Unmineralized tissue volume density (%) | 81.38 ± 4.81 | 81.32 ± 4.70 | 91.63 ± 6.31 |

- β-TCP alone significantly lower new bone than the other two groups (Figure 2g, p<0.05); the coated graft and natural healing were not reported as significantly different.
- Residual material significantly lower with the coated graft than with β-TCP alone (Figure 2h, p<0.05); unmineralized tissue significantly higher with natural healing than with both grafted arms (Figure 2i, p<0.05).
- Histology: natural-healing sockets formed peripheral trabeculae with central fibrous tissue; β-TCP sockets showed minimal new bone and little material contact; coated sockets showed moderate new bone and bone–material contact in periphery and centre.

- All serum BMP-2 ELISA tests were negative; no serious adverse events; no bone regeneration outside the socket.
- Baseline comparability confirmed for age, sex, smoking status, bone walls, gingival phenotype, defect height, recession height and EDS class (all CMH p>0.49).

**Limitations** — 6-week observation is short for human bone regeneration and insufficient to demonstrate ARP efficacy or long-term safety (a 2-year follow-up has been initiated); n=40, single centre, unblinded natural-healing control; biopsies taken freehand without a surgical guide; soft-tissue contour endpoint negative (attributed to sample size and short follow-up); the socket model is only a proxy for the material's intended use in larger bone defects, which requires multi-centre pivotal trials.
