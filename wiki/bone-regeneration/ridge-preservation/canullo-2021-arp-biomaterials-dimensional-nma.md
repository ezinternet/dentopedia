## Three-line Summary

SR + NMA (88 RCTs, 2805 patients, 3073 sockets; MEDLINE, Scopus, CENTRAL, EMBASE through March 2021; 1740 sockets underwent ARP; pairwise + multidimensional-scale NMA) evaluating dimensional and histomorphometric outcomes of biomaterials for alveolar ridge preservation — the largest ARP materials NMA to date.

All grafting materials significantly reduced horizontal and vertical shrinkage vs spontaneous healing (pairwise MA); NMA ranking identified xenograft (XG) and allograft (AG) — alone or combined with bioactive agents (Bio+AG) — as the most predictable materials for dimensional preservation, while platelet concentrates ranked highest for new bone formation percentage.

XG and Bio+AG minimize dimensional loss but score worst in histomorphometry (bone formation quality), while AG and AG+alloplast combinations achieve comparable dimensional results with better bone formation — a clinically important trade-off for long-term implant prognosis.

## Summary

This 2021 NMA (Canullo et al., Clin Oral Investig) is the largest ARP materials comparison to date, synthesizing 88 RCTs (2805 patients, 3073 sockets) using both pairwise and network meta-analysis. All tested grafting materials significantly reduced horizontal and vertical ridge shrinkage compared to spontaneous healing. The NMA multidimensional ranking showed xenograft and allograft (with or without bioactive agents) as most predictable for dimensional preservation — overturning the "no clear material winner" conclusion of earlier pairwise SRs (avila-ortiz-2019, bassir-2018). Platelet concentrates ranked best for histomorphometric bone formation but were less consistent dimensionally. The critical finding is a dimensional-histomorphometric trade-off: materials that best preserve ridge volume (XG, Bio+AG) produce the least new bone histologically, while those producing the best bone quality (platelet concentrates, AG) show more intermediate dimensional effects. This has direct implications for implant prognosis, as bone quality affects osseointegration and long-term stability.

## Key Contributions

- Largest ARP NMA: 88 RCTs, 2805 patients, 3073 sockets (vs avila-ortiz-2019's 22 RCTs, bassir-2018's 21 studies)
- First head-to-head material ranking via NMA — provides what pairwise SRs could not: explicit comparative ranking
- Identifies XG and AG as most predictable for dimensional preservation — overturns "no clear winner" conclusion
- Reveals dimensional vs histomorphometric trade-off: a key clinical insight absent from earlier SRs
- Platelet concentrates best for new bone formation — supports their use when bone quality is prioritized over volume

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + pairwise and network meta-analysis (NMA)
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, Scopus, CENTRAL, EMBASE (inception to March 2021)
- **Inclusion**: RCTs; ARP after tooth extraction; human patients; comparison vs spontaneous healing or other materials
- **Outcomes (primary)**: Ridge horizontal dimension changes, vertical dimension changes, new bone formation (%)
- **NMA method**: Multidimensional scale ranking (P-scores)
- **Sample**: 88 RCTs, 2805 patients, 3073 sockets; 1740 with ARP; 1432 with membrane coverage
- **Materials**: XG, AG, AP, PC, Bio+AG, AG+AP, and combinations

## Results

**All materials vs spontaneous healing (pairwise MA):** Significant reduction in both horizontal and vertical shrinkage for all grafting materials (p<.05)

**NMA Dimensional Ranking (horizontal + vertical):**
1. Xenograft (XG): highest p-scores for dimensional preservation
2. Bioactive agent + Allograft (Bio+AG): comparable to XG in dimensional outcomes
3. Allograft (AG): similar to XG in clinical comparisons, better histomorphometry
4. AG + Alloplast (AG+AP): consistent across dimensional and bone formation outcomes
5. Platelet concentrates: highly ranked for new bone formation; intermediate dimensional ranking

**Histomorphometric Ranking (new bone formation %):**
- Platelet concentrates ranked best
- XG and Bio+AG ranked worst (most dimensional preservation, least new bone)

**Trade-off conclusion:** XG and Bio+AG → best dimensional, worst histology; AG and AG+AP → balanced; PC → best bone formation, intermediate dimensions
