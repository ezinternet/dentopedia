## Three-line Summary
(Line 1: Split-mouth canine study (12 beagles), bilateral maxillary lateral incisor extraction; immediate vs delayed (6 wk) unilateral unloaded mini-screw placement, ~11 wk evaluation.)
(Line 2: Immediate placement better preserved vertical bone height vs control (12.1±1.4 vs 11.7±1.3 mm, p=0.02); both groups increased peri-screw bone density and osteoid formation vs controls.)
(Line 3: Unloaded mini-screws aid ridge preservation (esp. height if immediate, density if delayed); width unchanged, no increased inflammation.)

## Summary
In a split-mouth canine model, unloaded mini-screws placed in extraction sockets increased peri-screw bone density and histologic bone formation compared with controls. Immediate placement (at extraction) better preserved vertical bone height than delayed placement (6 weeks post-extraction), while delayed placement produced greater density gains around screws. Ridge width was unchanged and inflammation was not increased. The findings suggest mini-screws may be a simple, cost-effective approach to alveolar ridge preservation, particularly in sites where immediate implant placement is not feasible.

## Key Contributions
- **Timing effect**: Immediate > delayed for vertical height preservation.
- **Biologic response**: Unloaded screws induced localized bone formation/density increase regardless of timing.
- **Site specificity**: Maxillary lateral incisor sockets; width less affected than height/density.

## Methodology
- **Design**: Split-mouth RCT-like animal study (beagles), bilateral extraction.
- **Groups**: Immediate (n=6) vs delayed 6-week (n=6); unilateral screw, contralateral control.
- **Assessment**: CBCT (height/width/density at 0,1,2 mm) and histopathology/histomorphometry (inflammation, osteoid score/extension).

## Results
| Outcome | Immediate (screw vs control) | Delayed (screw vs control) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bone height | +0.4±0.3 mm (p=0.023) | +0.1±0.1 mm (n.s.) | Immediate better than delayed (p=0.029) |
| Bone width | n.s. | n.s. | No effect in either group |
| Bone density (0 mm) | +86.5±50.5 Hu (p=0.009) | +183.3±86.3 Hu (p=0.003) | Delayed greater increase vs immediate (p=0.025) |
| Bone density (1 mm) | n.s. | +59.5±10.0 Hu (p=0.027) | Overall increase (p=0.001) |
| Bone density (2 mm) | n.s. | n.s. | No difference |
| Osteoid formation score | 2.7±0.5 vs 0 (p=0.023) | 2.5±0.8 vs 0.2±0.4 (p=0.034) | Both higher vs control; between-group n.s. |
| Osteoid extension (mm^2) | 4.27±0.161 vs 0.037±0.008 (p<0.001) | 6.086±0.803 vs 0.182±0.125 (p=0.033) | Both higher vs control |
