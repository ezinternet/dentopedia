## One-line Summary
Randomized controlled pilot (n=28, 4 months, Australia): DBBM-based ARP in posterior maxillary extraction sites reduced sinus pneumatization (+3.9%→−3.7%, p=0.03) and cut lateral sinus floor elevation need from 89% to 42%.

## 한줄요약
상악 후방 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 파일럿 RCT (n=28, 4개월): 이종골 (DBBM) 충전이 상악동 공기화를 억제하고 (공기화 +3.9%→−3.7%, p=0.03) 측방 상악동 거상 (Sinus Floor Elevation, SFE) 필요율을 89%→42%로 감소.

## Summary
After posterior maxillary tooth extraction, the loss of alveolar bone height allows pneumatization of the maxillary sinus, increasing the need for sinus floor elevation (SFE) before implant placement. This multicenter randomized pilot (n=28; Australia) assigned posterior maxillary extraction sites to unassisted healing, DBBM granules (1–2mm, Bio-Oss), or DBBM-C block (Bio-Oss Collagen), with CBCT volumetric assessment at 4 months. Both DBBM formulations maintained ridge height (Control −39.7%; Test 1 and Test 2 +positive) and significantly reduced sinus pneumatization. The proportion of sites requiring lateral SFE (residual bone height <8mm) dropped from 89% (Control) to 42.8% (Test 1) and 40% (Test 2). This is the first prospective study using 3D volumetric CT sinus analysis to quantify ARP's anti-pneumatization effect.

## Key Contributions
- First prospective RCT demonstrating ARP prevents maxillary sinus pneumatization using 3D volumetric sinus analysis
- Shows that either DBBM formulation (granules or collagen block) halves the need for lateral SFE
- Provides a clinical decision framework: ARP at extraction ↔ prevention of sinus augmentation at implant stage
- Demonstrates equivalence of DBBM granules vs DBBM-C block — no significant difference between formulations
- Adds sinus volume as a novel secondary outcome measure for posterior maxillary ARP studies

## Methodology
- Design: Randomized controlled pilot study
- Population: 28 patients, 28 posterior maxillary extraction sites (~9–10 per group)
- Sites: 2 university clinics + 1 private practice, Brisbane, Australia
- Groups:
  - Control: Unassisted healing
  - Test 1: DBBM granules (Bio-Oss 1–2mm, Geistlich) + collagen membrane
  - Test 2: DBBM-C block (Bio-Oss Collagen, Geistlich) + collagen membrane
- Imaging: CBCT at baseline (pre-extraction) and 4 months
- Primary: Ridge height change (mid-ridge)
- Secondary: 3D sinus volume change, residual bone height (RBH), % sites needing lateral SFE (threshold: RBH <8mm)
- Statistics: One-way ANOVA with Tukey post-hoc

## Results
| Outcome | Control | Test 1 (DBBM) | Test 2 (DBBM-C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-ridge height change | −2.7±0.9mm (−39.7%) | +0.9mm | +1.0mm |
| Sinus volume change | +3.9% | −3.7% (p=0.03) | −2.1% (NS) |
| Residual bone height | 7.9mm | 10.4mm | 10.3mm |
| Lateral SFE needed (%) | 89% | 42.8% | 40% |

Test 1 vs Test 2: No significant difference in any outcome. Both significantly superior to Control for ridge height preservation.
