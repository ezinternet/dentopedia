## Three-line Summary
In a double-blinded RCT (n=20 sockets), allograft + I-PRF showed significantly less radiographic/clinical ridge width loss and more new bone with less residual graft than allograft alone at 3 months.  
Ridge height loss and nonmineralized tissue percentage did not differ significantly between groups.  
Histomorphometry of human biopsies demonstrated improved bone formation and graft remodeling with adjunctive I-PRF.

## 세줄요약
이중맹검 무작위대조시험(n=20)에서 발치와 보존술 시 동종골 + 주사형 혈소판풍부피브린(I-PRF) 병용군이 동종골 단독군보다 3개월째 방사선학적/임상적 치조제 폭 감소가 유의하게 적었고, 신생골 비율은 높고 잔여 이식재 비율은 낮았다.  
치조제 높이 감소와 비광화조직 비율은 두 군 간 유의한 차이가 없었다.  
인체 생검 조직형태측정 결과 I-PRF 병용이 골형성 및 이식재 리모델링에 유리했다.

## Summary
Talebi Ardakani et al. (2024) compared allogenic bone graft + collagen type 1 with or without injectable platelet-rich fibrin (I-PRF) for postextraction alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) in a double-blinded RCT. At 3 months, the I-PRF group showed significantly smaller reductions in radiographic bone width (0.67±0.39 mm vs 1.07±0.40 mm, P=0.038) and clinical bone width (0.82±0.39 mm vs 1.31±0.55 mm, P=0.033), significantly higher new bone formation (60.72%±19.32% vs 44.15%±13.09%, P=0.038), and significantly lower residual graft particles (20.28%±8.14% vs 30.75%±10.24%, P=0.021). No significant differences were observed for radiographic bone height reduction or percentage of nonmineralized tissue. Both treatments were successful with uneventful healing; adjunctive I-PRF improved histological, clinical, and radiographic outcomes in most parameters.

## Key Contributions
- Provides human histomorphometric evidence that I-PRF adjunct to allograft enhances new bone formation and accelerates graft remodeling in ARP.
- Demonstrates clinical/radiographic benefit specifically for ridge width preservation.
- Confirms safety with no inflammation or foreign body reactions observed.

## Methodology
- Design: Double-blinded RCT, parallel groups, 20 single-rooted extraction sockets (n=10 per group).
- Grafts: Mineralized allograft (500–1000 μm) + collagen type 1 ± I-PRF.
- I-PRF preparation: 10 mL venous blood, no anticoagulant, 700 rpm × 3 min, upper 1 mL collected with blunt needle and used immediately.
- Assessments: Pre/post CBCT (ridge width at 3 mm below crest, height), clinical ridge width at re-entry, trephine biopsy (2×4 mm) for histomorphometry (% new bone, residual graft, nonmineralized tissue).
- Blinding: Pathologist and statistician blinded; randomization via Random Allocation Software.
