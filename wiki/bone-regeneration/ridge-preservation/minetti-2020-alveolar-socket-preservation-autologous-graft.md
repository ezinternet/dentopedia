## Three-line Summary

Prospective multicenter pilot study (n=28 patients, 34 post-extractive sockets, 5 Italian dental centers) comparing autologous demineralized dentin/enamel graft (via TT Transformer device) from healthy whole teeth (Group 1, n=19 biopsies) vs. endodontically treated teeth (Group 2, n=13 biopsies) for alveolar socket preservation.

At 4-month histomorphometry, both groups showed comparable new vital bone formation (VB% 20.78% G1 vs 22.89% G2), residual graft (RG% 19.70% G1 vs 17.39% G2), and bone volume (BV% 36.68% G1 vs 39.16% G2), with no statistically significant difference between groups and no inflammation or residual endodontic filling material detected.

Autogenous tooth-derived graft (whether from healthy or root-canal-treated teeth) is biocompatible and produces vital new bone comparable to conventional biomaterials in socket preservation, but this is a small pilot histology study without a control (non-grafted or xenograft-comparator) arm, so efficacy relative to standard bone substitutes remains unquantified.

## 세줄요약

전향적 다기관 파일럿 연구(환자 28명, 발치 후 소켓 34개, 이탈리아 5개 치과센터) — TT Transformer 기기로 제작한 자가 탈회 상아질/법랑질 이식재(autologous demineralized dentin/enamel graft)를 건전 발거치(Group 1, 생검 19개) 대 근관치료된 발거치(Group 2, 생검 13개)에서 치조제보존술(socket preservation)에 사용해 비교.

4개월 후 조직형태계측에서 두 군 모두 유사한 신생 생활골 형성(VB% G1 20.78% vs G2 22.89%), 잔존 이식재(RG% G1 19.70% vs G2 17.39%), 골부피(BV% G1 36.68% vs G2 39.16%)를 보였고, 군간 통계적 유의차 없음, 염증 소견이나 잔존 근관충전재 없음.

자가 치아유래 이식재(건전치·근관치료치 무관)는 생체적합성이 우수하고 기존 골이식재에 준하는 생활골을 형성하지만, 대조군(무이식 또는 이종골 비교군) 없는 소규모 파일럿 조직학 연구라 표준 골이식재 대비 효능은 정량적으로 확인되지 않음.

## Summary

This multicenter pilot study by Minetti et al. (2020) enrolled 28 patients across five Italian dental centers to test whether autologous tooth-derived graft material — demineralized dentin/enamel produced by an automated chairside device (TT Transformer) — performs equivalently whether harvested from healthy whole teeth or from teeth that had previously undergone endodontic (root canal) treatment. Extracted teeth in both groups were cleaned (endodontic filling material removed first in Group 2), cut, and automatically demineralized for 25 minutes, then used to graft 34 post-extractive sockets under a resorbable collagen membrane. At second-stage surgery 4 months later, 32 bone biopsies (19 Group 1, 13 Group 2) were analyzed histomorphometrically for vital bone (VB%), residual graft (RG%), and total bone volume (BV%). No statistically significant differences were found between the whole-tooth and endodontically-treated-tooth groups on any parameter, and no inflammation or residual filling material (gutta-percha, composite, cement) was detected in any specimen.

## Key Contributions

- Provides multicenter human histology evidence that endodontically treated (previously "waste") extracted teeth are histologically as suitable an autologous graft source as healthy whole teeth when processed through the same automated demineralization protocol.
- Confirms biocompatibility of demineralized dentin/enamel autograft: no inflammatory or infective reaction across 32 biopsies, and no residual endodontic materials detected in the graft site.
- Adds early (2020) clinical-histology support to the demineralized-tooth-matrix / autologous dentin graft (ADG) literature synthesized more recently in [[bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]].

## Methodology

- 28 patients (mean age 51.79 ± 5.97 y), 34 post-extractive sockets, Feb–Oct 2018, five Italian dental centers.
- Group 1 (whole healthy teeth, n=20 extracted/19 biopsied) vs. Group 2 (endodontically treated teeth, n=14 extracted/13 biopsied); endodontic filling material stripped from G2 teeth before processing.
- All teeth processed in the TT Transformer/TT grinder device (automated chairside demineralization, 25 min) into a particulate graft, placed in the socket, and covered with a resorbable porcine-pericardium collagen membrane (Bego oss®).
- Bone biopsy via trephine drill at 4-month second-stage surgery; non-decalcified ground-section histology (~40–50 μm) with basic fuchsin/toluidine blue staining and histomorphometric image analysis (IAS 2000) for VB%, RG%, BV%.
- Strict inclusion/exclusion (ASA-1/2 adults, no smoking/diabetes/cancer/HIV/bone-metabolic disease/bisphosphonates/radio-chemotherapy); paired-sample t-test statistics.

## Results

- G1 (whole teeth): BV 36.68% (±8.90%), RG 19.70% (±13.75%), VB 20.78% (±13.29%).
- G2 (endodontically treated teeth): BV 39.16% (±11.51%), RG 17.39% (±7.09%), VB 22.89% (±9.72%).
- No statistically significant difference between groups on any histomorphometric parameter.
- No complications, no inflammation, no residual gutta-percha/composite/cement detected histologically in G2.
- Limitations: no non-grafted or xenograft-comparator control arm; small sample; single 4-month timepoint; unequal/center-imbalanced group sizes; no post-loading implant outcome data — the authors explicitly frame results as preliminary/pilot.
