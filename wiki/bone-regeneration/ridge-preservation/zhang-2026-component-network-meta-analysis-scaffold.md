## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with component network meta-analysis (CNMA) of 31 RCTs (932 participants, 853 extraction sites; PubMed, Embase, CENTRAL, Web of Science to July 2026; 16 RCTs in the horizontal-width network, 14 in the buccal vertical-height network) decomposing alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) into scaffold, platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) and barrier-membrane components.

In additive CNMA for horizontal ridge-width change, barrier membrane had the largest component effect (MD -1.31 mm, 95% CI -2.18 to -0.44), then allograft (-0.77 mm, -1.16 to -0.38) and PRF adjunct (-0.40 mm, -0.66 to -0.14); xenograft (-0.57 mm) and collagen scaffold (-0.42 mm) were non-significant and alloplast was null (0.01 mm).

Estimates were low or very low certainty, with global inconsistency (Q = 19.90, P = 0.006) and sparse nodes, so the results are an evidence-informed component framework, not a definitive treatment hierarchy; no scaffold-by-PRF interaction was demonstrable (imprecise).

## 세줄요약

31개 RCT (참가자 932명, 발치와 853개; 2026-07까지 PubMed·Embase·CENTRAL·Web of Science)를 대상으로 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP)을 이식재·혈소판 풍부 피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)·차단막 (Barrier Membrane) 구성요소로 분해한 구성요소 네트워크 메타분석 (Component Network Meta-Analysis, CNMA).

수평 치조제 폭 변화의 가산형 CNMA에서 차단막이 가장 컸고(MD -1.31 mm, 95% CI -2.18 ~ -0.44), 동종골 (Allograft) -0.77 mm, PRF 보조 -0.40 mm 순이었다; 이종골 (Xenograft) -0.57 mm와 콜라겐 지지체 -0.42 mm는 비유의, 합성골 (Alloplast)은 0.01 mm로 효과 없음.

구성요소 추정치의 확실성은 낮음~매우 낮음이고 전역 비일관성 (Q = 19.90, P = 0.006)과 희소한 노드가 있어 확정적 치료 위계가 아니라 근거 기반 해석 틀로만 읽어야 하며, 이식재×PRF 상호작용은 부정확하여 입증되지 않았다.

## Why Ingested

Retrieved by the bone-graft-material surveillance sweep. Where [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/canullo-2021-arp-biomaterials-dimensional-nma]] ranked complete ARP packages (xenograft and allograft on top), this paper splits the packages into components and attributes the effect to barrier membrane and allograft, with xenograft non-significant at component level, which qualifies that ranking. Its small but significant PRF adjunct effect (-0.40 mm) also sits against the null L-PRF result of [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/alavi-2024-regenerative-grafts-socket-preservation-sr-ma]], so the two are best read together.

## Summary

This systematic review and component network meta-analysis pooled 31 RCTs of alveolar ridge preservation after tooth extraction in adults, coding each arm into eight components (natural healing, allograft, xenograft, alloplast, collagen scaffold, tooth/dentin scaffold, PRF adjunct, barrier membrane). Standard network meta-analysis showed that several scaffold- and membrane-containing strategies reduced horizontal and buccal vertical ridge loss versus natural healing, but several top-ranked nodes rested on a single small trial. Additive CNMA attributed the largest horizontal-width effect to barrier membrane (MD -1.31 mm), then allograft (-0.77 mm) and PRF adjunct (-0.40 mm), with low or very low certainty. Because of global inconsistency and sparse components, the authors present the findings as a component-level interpretive framework rather than a treatment hierarchy.

## Key Contributions

- Decomposes ARP protocols into scaffold, PRF and barrier-membrane components and estimates each independent contribution, rather than ranking packages.
- Horizontal width: membrane -1.31 mm, allograft -0.77 mm, PRF -0.40 mm significant; xenograft -0.57 mm, collagen -0.42 mm non-significant; alloplast 0.01 mm.
- No convincing scaffold-by-PRF interaction (xenograft x PRF 0.28 mm; alloplast x PRF 0.60 mm; collagen x PRF not estimable).
- Additive model most parsimonious (approximate AIC 39.24 vs 42.44 standard NMA, 42.32 interaction CNMA; tau-squared 0.072 vs 0.101 and 0.091), though its residual deviance was higher than the standard NMA.

## Methodology

PRISMA and PRISMA-NMA compliant, PROSPERO CRD420261459356. PubMed, Embase, CENTRAL and Web of Science were searched from inception to July 2026 (3,161 records; 130 full texts; 31 RCTs from 2008-2026, 17 countries or regions, 7 split-mouth and 5 multi-arm trials). The horizontal-width network had 16 studies and 12 nodes; the buccal vertical-height network had 14 studies and 10 nodes. Risk of bias used RoB 2 (6 low, 20 some concerns, 5 high); certainty used CINeMA for standard NMA and a structured narrative approach for CNMA. Analyses used random-effects models in R (netmeta, netcomb). All PRF preparations were pooled into one component, and the membrane component pooled collagen, synthetic, nonresorbable and PRF-derived barriers.

Negative MD means less ridge loss than natural healing (the comparator). Sensitivity analyses (excluding high risk of bias, 4-6 month follow-up window, alternative PRF coding) kept the direction of the membrane, allograft and PRF estimates; the PRF contribution stayed smaller.

## Clinical Insights

- Space maintenance and stabilization (membrane, allograft scaffold) appear to account for more of the dimensional preservation than the biologic PRF adjunct, consistent with the authors' mechanical-stability reading; this is average class-level evidence, not a product recommendation.
- The allograft estimate is the most precise scaffold estimate (largest scaffold evidence volume: 25 intervention occurrences), which should not be read as superiority over xenograft, whose component estimate was merely imprecise (CI -1.20 to 0.06).
- Alloplast showed no detectable independent horizontal-width effect (very low certainty), which tempers its high-ranking combination nodes (alloplast + membrane -1.45 mm).
- Top-ranked complete nodes (dentin graft, allograft + membrane) are single-trial estimates; treat the NMA ranking cautiously.
- The paper does not support choosing PRF according to scaffold type, and cannot show that such interactions are absent.
