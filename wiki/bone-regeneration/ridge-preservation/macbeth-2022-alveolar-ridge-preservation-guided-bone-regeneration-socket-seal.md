## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=42, 14/group) at anterior maxillary extraction sites comparing GBR (Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide membrane), Socket Seal (Bio-Oss + Mucograft collagen matrix), and unassisted healing over 4 months with CBCT outcome assessment.
Both GBR (+0.07±0.83 mm BARH) and SS (+0.65±1.1 mm BARH) significantly prevented buccal ridge height loss versus control (−0.52±0.8 mm; p=0.04 and p=0.005 respectively); GBR significantly reduced implant dehiscence at 4 months (57% vs 85%, p=0.03).
GBR is superior for overall radiographic bone preservation and reducing secondary augmentation at implant placement; SS has higher complication rates (43% collagen matrix breakdown) but comparable vertical preservation.

## Summary

This single-centre RCT enrolled 42 military patients requiring anterior maxillary single-rooted tooth extraction, randomising them to GBR (Bio-Oss xenograft + Bio-Gide collagen membrane), Socket Seal (Bio-Oss + Mucograft Seal collagen matrix), or unassisted healing (control). CBCT images were taken immediately post-extraction and at 4 months. Both ARP techniques significantly prevented buccal alveolar ridge height (BARH) loss compared to control; GBR also significantly preserved palatal alveolar ridge height (PARH) and mid-socket cross-sectional area. Horizontal ridge width decreased similarly across all groups (~2.2–2.4 mm), with no significant between-group difference. At implant placement (4 months), GBR significantly reduced the need for augmentation due to bone dehiscence (57% vs 85% in control; p=0.03). SS was associated with higher rates of collagen matrix breakdown prior to suture removal (43%), while GBR patients more often experienced mucosal colour change and swelling. Both techniques supported 100% prosthetically driven implant placement with adequate primary stability.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT directly comparing GBR vs collagen-matrix Socket Seal (Mucograft Seal) vs control for ARP in anterior maxilla
- Demonstrates GBR superiority in cross-sectional socket area preservation (−4% vs −13% control; p=0.01) and implant dehiscence reduction
- Quantifies SS collagen matrix complication rate (43% partial breakdown) vs free CTG-based SS in earlier literature
- Confirms horizontal ridge width loss (~25–27%) is not significantly mitigated by either ARP technique at 4 months
- Superimposed CBCT with grey-scale profile measurement for alveolar process area — methodologically novel in 2022

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Design | Single-centre, prospective, single-blind RCT (CONSORT) |
| Ethics | UK MOD Research Ethics Committee (643/MODREC/15) |
| Sample | n=42 (14/group); 2015–2019 |
| Population | Military patients 18–55 y; moderate-to-thick phenotype; anterior maxilla (15–25) |
| Intervention A | GBR: Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide membrane, cross-mattress sutures, no primary closure |
| Intervention B | SS: Bio-Oss + Mucograft Seal collagen matrix, single interrupted sutures |
| Control | Gauze pressure only |
| Follow-up | 4 months (CBCT post-extraction baseline + 4-month) |
| Primary outcome | Mid-BARH change (CBCT, OnDemand3D grey-scale profile) |
| Randomisation | Stratified block (premolar/canine covariate), opaque envelopes |
| Antibiotics | Amoxicillin 500 mg pre-op × 5 days |

## Results

| Measurement | GBR | SS | Control | GBR vs Ctrl | SS vs Ctrl |
|-------------|-----|----|---------|-------------|------------|

| Mid-PARH (mm) | +0.86 ± 1.37 | +0.65 ± 1.42 | −0.43 ± 0.83 | p = 0.02 | n.s. |

### Horizontal Dimensions (4-month change)

| Measurement | GBR | SS | Control | ANOVA |
|-------------|-----|----|---------|-------|
| Mid-CARW (5 mm) | −2.17 ± 0.84 mm | −2.36 ± 2.76 mm | −2.30 ± 1.11 mm | p = 0.89 |
| Mid-AARW (10 mm) | −0.96 ± 0.34 mm | −0.86 ± 1.48 mm | −0.82 ± 0.76 mm | n.s. |

| Group | Change | % |
|-------|--------|---|

| Control | −6.93 ± 8.22 | **−13%** |

GBR vs Control: p = 0.01.

### Implant Augmentation at 4 Months

| | GBR | SS | Control |
|--|-----|----|---------|
| Dehiscence requiring GBR | 29% (4/14) | 50% (7/14) | **71% (10/14)** |
| Any augmentation needed | 57% (8/14) | 64% (9/14) | **85% (12/14)** |

GBR vs Control dehiscence: p = 0.03.
