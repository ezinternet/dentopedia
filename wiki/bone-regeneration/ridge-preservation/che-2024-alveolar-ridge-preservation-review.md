## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Korean Dental Association Journal, 2024, Hallym University OMFS) synthesizing current evidence on alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) techniques, graft materials, and socket sealing strategies.

The ideal ARP sequence — granulation removal → appropriate graft → socket sealing — is established as minimizing post-extraction invasiveness at subsequent implant placement; most modalities outperform unassisted healing.

Despite evidence supporting ARP efficacy, clinical guidelines on optimal graft material selection and implant placement timing remain absent, limiting standardized practice.

## 세줄요약

종설 (Narrative Review, 대한치과의사협회지 2024, 한림대학교 구강악안면외과): 발치 후 치조골 흡수 최소화를 위한 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 술기·이식재·소켓 밀봉 현황 합성.

이상적 ARP 시퀀스 — 육아조직 제거 → 적절 이식재 충전 → 소켓 밀봉 — 이 후속 임플란트 식립 시 침습도를 최소화하는 것으로 확립.

ARP 효능을 지지하는 근거에도 불구하고 최적 이식재 선택과 임플란트 식립 시기에 대한 명확한 임상 가이드라인은 아직 없음.

## Summary
Narrative review of alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) techniques, graft materials, and clinical implications. Korean Dental Association journal.

## Key Contributions
Compact ARP procedural and material overview suitable as a reference primer for clinicians considering ARP indications before implant placement.

## Methodology
Literature review. ARP techniques, available graft materials, and socket-sealing options synthesized. Implications for subsequent implant placement discussed.

## Results
Ideal ARP = granulation tissue removal → suitable graft → socket sealing. Demonstrated to minimize invasiveness at subsequent implant placement. Lacks consensus on optimal graft type and implant-placement timing.

## Limitations
Narrative review (not systematic); no quantitative pooling; published guidelines still missing.
