## Three-line Summary

Preclinical RCT (UNESP Brazil, Braz Oral Res 2025; n=72 rats) comparing **Ossix Plus** (GLYMATRIX sugar-glycation cross-linked porcine collagen) vs **Bio-Gide** (non-cross-linked bovine collagen) in 8 mm critical-size rat calvarial defects at 7/15/30/60 days using histometry, IHC (osteocalcin/osteopontin), and micro-CT.

At 30 days Bio-Gide led; at **60 days Ossix Plus reversed, achieving BV 17.33 ± 1.74 vs 8.06 ± 1.26 mm³ (p<0.05)**, BV/TV 45.67% vs 23.69%, and superior Tb.Th and intersection surface — driven by prolonged barrier integrity and M2 macrophage polarization.

Mechanistically, sugar-glycation extends the barrier function window beyond Bio-Gide's degradation point (~30 days), allowing the protected bone-forming environment to persist to 60 days and accumulate greater total bone volume.

## 세줄요약

전임상 RCT (UNESP, Braz Oral Res 2025, n=72) — Ossix Plus(GLYMATRIX 당가교) vs Bio-Gide(비가교) 8mm 흰쥐 두개골 임계결손. **30일엔 Bio-Gide 우세, 60일엔 Ossix Plus 역전**: BV 17.33 vs 8.06 mm³ (p<0.05), BV/TV 45.67 vs 23.69%.

기전: 당가교로 멤브레인이 60일 이상 온전 → Bio-Gide가 ~30일 이후 분해되는 시점에서도 보호 창 지속 + M2 대식세포 분극으로 항염 재생 환경 유지.

임상 함의: **큰 결손·긴 치유** 케이스에서 GLYMATRIX 플랫폼(Ossix Plus/Volumax) 선택 근거.

## Key Results

| Micro-CT (60 days) | Ossix Plus | Bio-Gide | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bone volume (mm³) | **17.33 ± 1.74** | 8.06 ± 1.26 | <0.05 |
| BV/TV (%) | **45.67** | 23.69 | <0.05 |
| Trabecular thickness (mm) | **0.214** | 0.105 | <0.05 |
| Trabecular separation (mm) | 0.285 | 0.357 | NS |

**Histometric reversal**: Bio-Gide > Ossix Plus at day 30 → Ossix Plus > Bio-Gide at day 60.

## Mechanism Summary

```
Days 0–30:
  Bio-Gide (porous, 2-layer) → fast vascularization + cell infiltration → more early bone
  Ossix Plus (non-porous)    → minimal early cell invasion, lower early angiogenesis

Days 30–60:
  Bio-Gide begins enzymatic degradation → barrier function lost
  Ossix Plus intact (sugar-glycation) → protected space maintained
  + M2 macrophage polarization by Ossix Plus → anti-inflammatory environment continues
  → MORE total bone at 60 days
```

## Limitations

- Rat calvaria: non-load-bearing; no graft material (atypical of clinical GBR)
- 60-day max follow-up; remodeling beyond this unknown
- Animal ≠ human healing dynamics
