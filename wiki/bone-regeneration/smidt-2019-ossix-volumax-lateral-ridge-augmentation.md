## Three-line Summary

Single case report (Hebrew University/Hadassah, Quintessence Int 2019) in which a thick, sugar-cross-linked porcine collagen scaffold (Ossix Volumax, GLYMATRIX technology, 1.5–2.0 mm dry) was used as the **sole augmentation material** — without bone substitute — to correct a horizontal buccal crestal ridge deficiency at a posterior maxillary implant site between natural teeth.

At 6 months the scaffold had expanded, integrated, and restored sufficient buccal volume to proceed to final prosthetics; the 2-year result showed a lifelike emergence profile with no re-intervention.

The key practical message is that when implant primary stability is achievable but crestal horizontal tissue volume is deficient (Seibert Class 3), Ossix Volumax alone can replace the conventional "bone substitute + resorbable membrane" GBR stack, eliminating donor-site morbidity compared with connective tissue grafts.

## 세줄요약

단일 증례 (Quintessence Int 2019) — 상악 우측 제2소구치 결손부의 협측 수평결손(Seibert III)을 Ossix Volumax(두꺼운 당가교 돼지 콜라겐 스캐폴드, GLYMATRIX) 단독으로 처리: 뼈 이식재 없음, CTG 없음.

6개월 치유 후 스캐폴드가 팽창·골화하여 협측 부피 회복 → 즉각 보철(지르코니아). 2년 안정.

**적용 조건**: 임플란트 초기 안정성 확보 가능한 경우 + 치조정 수평결손. 큰 골결손에는 이식재 병용 필요.

## The Ossix Volumax Scaffold

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Technology | GLYMATRIX (sugar cross-linking) — same platform as Ossix Plus membrane |
| Material | Porcine tendon collagen |
| Thickness | 1.5–2.0 mm dry; expands ~2× when wetted |
| Resorption | Progressive; ossification begins ~1 month; degradation over up to 6 months |
| Space maintenance | Resists collapse + resists early exposure degradation |

Ossix Volumax is positioned between a standard resorbable membrane (thin, space-maintaining only) and a particulate bone graft (osteoconductive but requires membrane cover). Its thickness provides its own structural support while the collagen scaffold ossifies in situ.

## Procedure Summary

1. Pre-op CBCT: adequate bone height, horizontal crestal deficiency
2. Virtual implant planning (MGuide/MIS Implants), surgical template fabricated
3. Midcrestal incision, full-thickness buccal flap, relaxing cuts for tension-free closure
4. Implant placed; healing abutment connected
5. Ossix Volumax adapted to buccal defect and around healing abutment; secured with internal resorbable mattress sutures
6. Primary closure; unloaded provisional

**Healing**: 6 months → osseointegration confirmed → final zirconia crown

## Clinical Decision Rules (per authors)

**Use Ossix Volumax alone when:**
- Primary stability achievable from existing bone
- Deficiency is horizontal/crestal (Seibert Class 3, buccal concavity)
- Site is between natural teeth (interpositional)
- Patient wants to avoid donor-site harvest

**Add bone substitute when:**
- Complete circumferential defect
- Large vertical component
- Insufficient bone for primary stability (Type C molar socket)

## Limitations

- Single case, no controls, no volumetric measurement
- 2-year follow-up only
- Not compared against conventional GBR
- Indication-specific: cannot generalize to larger defects or edentulous spans
- Quintessence Int not OA; ingested from print PDF
