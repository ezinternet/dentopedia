## Three-line Summary

A comprehensive review classifying bone grafts into natural (autograft, allograft, xenograft) and synthetic (alloplasts: β-TCP, hydroxyapatite, bioactive glass; composites; CAD-CAM personalized) categories, evaluating each across the osteogenesis/osteoinduction/osteoconduction triad. Autografts provide living cells and inductive factors but suffer donor-site morbidity and unpredictable resorption; processed allografts/xenografts offer reliable osteoconduction and volume stability with reduced inductive potential; modern alloplasts achieve comparable outcomes to natural grafts for space maintenance. Composite strategies (autogenous chips + slow-resorbing xenograft/alloplast + membrane/mesh) enhance contour stability in sinus elevation and GBR. Clinical indication mapping: socket preservation (gold standard), sinus lift, lateral/vertical GBR, periodontal intrabony/furcation defects (with biologics), peri-implantitis (limited adjunctive benefit). Future: precision biomimetics integrating intelligent drug delivery, personalized design, rigorous QC.

## Summary

This 2026 narrative review from *Journal of Periodontal Research* provides a comprehensive synthesis of bone graft biomaterials for periodontology and implant dentistry. The authors classify grafts into natural (autograft, allograft, xenograft) and synthetic (alloplasts, composites, CAD-CAM personalized) categories, evaluating each against the fundamental triad of osteogenesis (viable cells), osteoinduction (growth factor signaling), and osteoconduction (scaffold guidance). Autografts remain the biological gold standard combining all three properties but are limited by donor-site morbidity and resorption unpredictability. Processed allografts (FDBA, DBM) and xenografts (DBBM) provide reliable osteoconduction and volume stability with minimal osteoinduction. Modern alloplasts (β-TCP, hydroxyapatite, bioactive glass) achieve outcomes comparable to natural grafts for space maintenance indications. Composite strategies blending autogenous chips (10–30%) with slowly resorbing carriers (xenograft/alloplast) and barrier membranes/meshes enhance contour stability in sinus floor elevation and GBR. Clinical decision mapping covers socket preservation (xenograft gold standard), sinus lift (xenograft/allograft/composites), lateral/vertical GBR (xenograft + membrane, Ti-mesh, composites), periodontal regeneration (allograft/xenograft + EMD/PRF), and peri-implantitis (limited evidence). Translation barriers include batch-to-batch variability, regulatory heterogeneity, and long-term safety gaps. The future lies in precision biomimetics integrating intelligent drug delivery, personalized CAD-CAM design, and rigorous quality control, with cost-effectiveness as the adoption gatekeeper.

## Results

### Classification & Triad Assessment

| Graft Class | Osteogenesis | Osteoinduction | Osteoconduction | Key Characteristics |
|-------------|--------------|----------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Autograft** | ✅ Viable osteoblasts/oprogenitors | ✅ Native BMPs, TGF-β, VEGF | ✅ Calcified matrix scaffold | Gold standard biologically; donor-site morbidity; rapid/unpredictable resorption; harvesting method affects viability |
| **Allograft (FDBA)** | ❌ No viable cells | ⚠️ Limited (demineralized > mineralized) | ✅ Good mineralized matrix | Most used worldwide; no 2nd site; variable processing (fresh-frozen, FDBA, DBM, putty); immunogenicity/resorption trade-offs |
| **Xenograft (DBBM)** | ❌ No viable cells | ❌ Minimal | ✅✅ Excellent (slow resorption, volume stability) | Bovine-derived (Bio-Oss® etc.); hydroxyapatite-like; long-term space maintenance; "gold standard" for socket preservation |
| **Alloplast: β-TCP** | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ Resorbable, osteoconductive | Synthetic; resorption ~3–6 mo; comparable to xenograft in selected indications |
| **Alloplast: Hydroxyapatite** | ❌ | ❌ | ✅✅ Very slow resorption | Non-resorbable/very slow; long-term space maintenance; brittle |
| **Alloplast: Bioactive Glass** | ❌ | ⚠️ Surface reactions (ion release) | ✅ | Silicate/borate/phosphate; Sr-doped enhances osteogenesis; costly, long manufacturing |
| **Composites** | ✅ (autogenous fraction) | ✅ (autogenous + biologics) | ✅✅ (carrier stability) | Autogenous chips (10–30%) + xenograft/alloplast + membrane/mesh; best contour stability |
| **CAD-CAM Personalized** | ❌ (unless cell-seeded) | ⚠️ (peptide-functionalized) | ✅✅ Exact fit | Patient-specific scaffolds; peptide-modified matrices (SmartBonePep); cost/regulatory hurdles |

### Clinical Indication Mapping

| Indication | Preferred Graft(s) | Evidence Level | Notes |
|------------|-------------------|----------------|-------|
| **Socket preservation** | Xenograft (DBBM), allograft, alloplast | High (SR/MA, Cochrane) | Gold standard; space maintenance primary goal |
| **Sinus floor elevation (lateral)** | Xenograft, allograft, composites | High | Volume stability critical; composites enhance contour |
| **Lateral ridge augmentation (GBR)** | Xenograft + membrane, composites | Moderate-High | Tenting screws/Ti-mesh for space making; composite for stability |
| **Vertical ridge augmentation** | Ti-mesh + xenograft/allograft, composites | Moderate | High complication rate; customized CAD-CAM mesh emerging |
| **Periodontal intrabony/furcation** | Allograft/xenograft + biologics (EMD, PRF) | Moderate | Space maintenance needed; biologics for regeneration |
| **Peri-implantitis reconstructive** | Limited evidence | Low | Adjunctive benefit of grafts uncertain; decontamination primary |

### Future Directions & Barriers

| Direction | Description | Barriers |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Precision biomimetics | Intelligent drug delivery (BMP, PDGF, ions), personalized CAD-CAM, peptide matrices | Batch variability, regulatory heterogeneity, long-term safety data gaps |
| SmartBone / SmartBonePep | Collagen-bioactive glass + peptides; osteoinductive | Commercial IP, cost |
| Sr-doped bioactive glass | Sr²⁺ enhances osteogenesis, inhibits osteoclasts | Manufacturing complexity, cost |
| Platelet concentrate combos | i-PRF/CGF + grafts (sticky bone concept) | Protocol standardization |
| Cost-effectiveness focus | Procedural savings → adoption driver | Health economics data scarce |
