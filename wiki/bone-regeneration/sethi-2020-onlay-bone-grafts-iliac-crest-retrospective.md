## Three-line Summary
Retrospective single-center analysis of 173 consecutive patients who received 190 anterior iliac crest onlay grafts for alveolar reconstruction (1992–2017), with 869 implants placed 3 months post-grafting and follow-up up to 274.8 months (mean 109 months).
Overall implant survival rate was 95% ± 2.7% (Kaplan-Meier); survival by deficiency class: IV 95% ± 9.7%, V 96% ± 7.4%, VI 98.7% ± 2.5%; 2/190 grafts (1%) failed before implant placement; seroma was the only donor-site complication (11/173 patients).
Iliac crest onlay grafts remain viable for large jaw defects where intraoral sources are insufficient, with acceptable long-term implant survival despite high loss to follow-up (31.8%).

## Summary
This retrospective analysis reports the largest single-center series of iliac crest onlay grafts for jaw reconstruction prior to implant placement, spanning 25 years (1992–2017) with a mean follow-up of 109 months. Among 173 consecutive patients receiving 190 block grafts, 869 implants were placed 3 months post-grafting. The overall Kaplan-Meier survival estimate of 95% ± 2.7% remained above 92.3% at 200 months, though it dropped to 82% at 250 months with only 22 implants still under observation. Donor-site morbidity was remarkably low, with seroma (11/173) as the only reported complication and all patients returning to normal function. These long-term figures directly reinforce the ICG arm of the McKenna 2022 SR+MA, which pooled data showing ICG survival rates of 88.8% at 120 months — consistent with Sethi's trajectory.

## Key Contributions
- Largest single-center ICG cohort with up to 23-year follow-up (mean 109 months)
- Kaplan-Meier survival curves stratified by Cawood-Howell deficiency class (IV, V, VI)
- Quantified donor-site morbidity: seroma only; gait disturbance and sensory loss not reported
- Graft failure rate before implant placement: 1% (2/190)
- Concurrent procedures (sinus augmentation, nerve lateralization) documented without survival compromise

## Methodology
- Retrospective cohort, single center, 25-year consecutive patient series
- 173 patients, 190 ICG onlay grafts (167 maxilla / 23 mandible), 869 implants
- Defect classified by Cawood-Howell; CT-guided graft harvesting and implant planning
- Implant systems: Osteo-Ti, Ankylos, Xive (multiple manufacturers across study period)
- Kaplan-Meier analysis; Smith-Zarb success criterion
