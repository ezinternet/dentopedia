## 한줄요약

자가혈소판농축물(Autologous Platelet Concentrate, APC) 3세대 — 혈소판풍부혈장(Platelet-Rich Plasma, PRP)·혈소판풍부피브린(Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)·농축성장인자(Concentrated Growth Factor, CGF) — 를 제조 프로토콜·성장인자(TGF-β·BMP·VEGF·PDGF) 조성·세포 구성·임상 적용 측면에서 비교한 서술형 리뷰; 혈소판 농축배율과 성장인자 방출기간이 세대가 진행될수록 증가(PRP 1시간 → PRF 7–10일 → CGF 28일)하며, CGF가 가장 우수한 APC로 제시됨.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (University of Salento/CNR, Genes 2023) comparing the three generations of autologous platelet concentrates (APCs) — platelet-rich plasma (PRP), platelet-rich fibrin (PRF), and concentrated growth factors (CGF) — across preparation protocols, growth-factor composition (TGF-β, BMP, VEGF, PDGF signaling), and clinical applications.

Platelet/growth-factor concentration rises across generations (PRP 3–8× whole blood, A-PRF 17.8×, CGF 15.5×) with progressively longer growth-factor release: PRP releases nearly all content within 1 hour, PRF sustains release 7–10 days, A-PRF up to 14 days, CGF up to 28 days (VEGF peak ~day 14, TGF-β1/BMP-2 peak ~day 21); CGF also retains CD34+/CD45+/CD105+ progenitor-like cells capable of osteoblast and endothelial differentiation.

CGF is framed as the most effective current-generation APC (highest growth-factor content, simplest preparation, sustained delivery kinetics) with the widest clinical evidence in oral surgery/dental implantology, bone-defect regeneration, and facial rejuvenation — but the review is narrative (no PRISMA/systematic search protocol), and conflicting CGF-implant-stability findings (Palermo 2023 positive vs Özveri Koyuncu null) are attributed to technique differences (implant-surface permeation vs socket placement) rather than resolved by pooled evidence.

## 세줄요약

자가혈소판농축물(Autologous Platelet Concentrate, APC) 3세대(PRP·PRF·CGF)를 제조법·성장인자 조성·임상 적용 관점에서 비교한 서술형 리뷰(University of Salento/CNR, Genes 2023).

세대가 진행될수록 혈소판 농축배율(PRP 4–8배 → A-PRF 17.8배 → CGF 15.5배)과 성장인자 방출 지속기간(PRP 1시간 → PRF 7–10일 → CGF 28일, VEGF는 14일·TGF-β1/BMP-2는 21일에 정점)이 늘어나며, CGF는 CD34+/CD45+/CD105+ 전구세포도 함께 함유.

CGF를 가장 우수한 APC로 결론짓지만 서술형 리뷰라 체계적 검색 프로토콜이 없고, 임플란트 안정성에 대한 CGF 효과는 연구마다 상반(Palermo 양성 vs Özveri Koyuncu 무효과) — 저자들은 이를 임플란트 표면 침투 vs 골와 내 배치라는 술식 차이로 설명하되 직접 비교 검증은 없음.

## Summary

This narrative review from the University of Salento and CNR (Italy) synthesizes the biology and comparative characteristics of the three generations of autologous platelet concentrates (APCs) used in regenerative medicine and dentistry: platelet-rich plasma (PRP, 1970s), platelet-rich fibrin (PRF, Choukroun 2000), and concentrated growth factors (CGF, Sacco 2006). It opens with a molecular primer on the four growth-factor families most implicated in platelet-driven tissue regeneration — TGF-β/BMP (Smad signaling), VEGF-A (VEGFR-1/2 → PI3K/Akt/MAPK), and PDGF (tyrosine-kinase receptors) — before comparing how each APC generation's distinct centrifugation protocol (single- vs two-stage vs alternating-speed) shapes its fibrin architecture, platelet/leukocyte retention, growth-factor release kinetics, and resident cell population.

## Key Contributions

- Unifies growth-factor signaling biology (TGF-β/Smad, BMP/RA-Smad, VEGF/PI3K-Akt, PDGF/Src-PLCγ) with the practical APC preparation literature, connecting molecular mechanism to clinical rationale.
- Direct three-way comparison of PRP/PRF/CGF preparation protocols, platelet-concentration fold-increase (PRGF ~3×, PRP 4–8×, A-PRF 17.8×, CGF 15.5×), and growth-factor release duration (PRP ~1 hr bolus; PRF 7–10 days; A-PRF up to 14 days; CGF up to 28 days).
- Characterizes CGF's resident cell population (CD34+/CD45+/CD105+, Oct3/4+/Nanog+) with demonstrated osteoblast and endothelial-progenitor-like differentiation capacity — a cellular mechanism beyond acellular growth-factor delivery.
- Tabulates cross-generation clinical application evidence spanning facial rejuvenation, cartilage (ear/nose) reconstruction, bone-defect repair, dental implantology, and (preliminarily) neural regeneration.

## Methodology

Narrative literature review (no PRISMA flow diagram, database list, or systematic search protocol reported), structured around growth-factor biology, APC-generation comparison (devices, growth-factor release, cellular content, clinical applications), and a brief section on allogeneic (pooled donor) platelet gel treatments for patients who cannot undergo repeated autologous draws.

## Results

| Generation | Platelet fold vs whole blood | Growth-factor release duration | Key notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| PRP (PRGF variant) | ~3× | ~1 hour | Bolus release; supports only initial regeneration phase |
| PRP (standard, L-PRP) | 4–8× | ~1 hour | Unstable matrix disintegrates during culture |
| PRF | — | 7–10 days | More consistent, sustained release than PRP |
| A-PRF | 17.8× | up to 14 days | Softer, denser fibrin clot |
| CGF | 15.5× | up to 28 days | VEGF peak ~day 14; TGF-β1/BMP-2 peak ~day 21–28; retains CD34+/CD45+/CD105+ cells |

**Dental implantology**: PRF increases osteogenic marker expression in periodontal/gingival progenitor cells in vitro and improves implant stability/bone density in vivo. CGF accelerates osseointegration and improves implant stability when the implant surface itself is permeated with CGF before insertion (Palermo et al.; Shetye et al.), but shows no stability benefit when CGF is instead placed in the osteotomy site before implant insertion (Özveri Koyuncu et al.) — a technique-dependent discrepancy not resolved by a direct head-to-head trial.

**Bone-defect regeneration**: CGF and A-PRF outperform PRP in periosteal cell proliferation; CGF shows greater regenerative potential than PRF in early femoral-defect repair (Park et al.); combining CGF with bone substitutes (autograft, chitosan-alginate hydrogel, scaffolds) improves later-stage bone repair.
