## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD42023414686, PRISMA) of 10 studies from 3,291 screened records, evaluating bone expansion techniques for horizontal alveolar ridge augmentation with simultaneous implant placement, without concurrent bone grafting materials (Oral Maxillofac Surg, 2025).

Pooled implant survival 100% (95% CI: 99–100%), complication rate 0% (0–1%), and mean ridge width gain +1.55 mm (95% CI: 0.92–2.17 mm); meta-regression demonstrated a statistically significant time-dependent survival attrition of −0.08%/month (P = 0.03).

Bone expansion is an effective and safe technique for horizontal ridge augmentation without grafting materials, but medium-term follow-up monitoring is warranted given the time-dependent survival signal.

## Summary
This SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD42023414686) evaluated bone expansion techniques for horizontal alveolar ridge augmentation combined with implant placement, without concurrent bone grafting materials. Ten studies from 3,291 screened records were included. Pooled implant survival was 100% (95% CI: 99–100%) with a complication rate of 0% (0–1%). The weighted mean ridge width difference was +1.55 mm. Meta-regression revealed a statistically significant survival decline of 0.08% per month post-placement (P = 0.03), suggesting time-dependent attrition. The authors conclude bone expansion is an efficient and safe technique for horizontal augmentation when grafting materials are not used.

## Key Contributions
- Establishes 100% survival and 0% complication pooled benchmarks for bone expansion + simultaneous implant placement
- Quantifies horizontal ridge gain at +1.55 mm (WMD, 95% CI: 0.92–2.17 mm) — first pooled estimate for graft-free expansion
- Meta-regression time-effect: 0.08%/month survival attrition — highlights need for medium-term follow-up monitoring
- Includes leave-one-out sensitivity analysis and Egger's publication bias test — methodologically robust for the evidence base
- Covers diverse expansion techniques (osteotomes, motorized expanders, OD) under single outcome umbrella

## Methodology
- Design: SR+MA; PRISMA-compliant; PROSPERO CRD42023414686
- Databases: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Scopus
- Inclusion: Human studies using bone expansion for horizontal ridge augmentation without grafting materials, or combined with vertical techniques only
- Final included: n = 10 articles from 3,291 records
- Statistical methods: Random-effects model, weighted mean difference, forest plots, meta-regression, I² heterogeneity, leave-one-out outlier analysis, Egger's test + funnel plots

## Results
- Implant survival: **100%** (95% CI: 99–100%)
- Complication rate: **0%** (95% CI: 0–1%)
- Ridge width gain (WMD): **+1.55 mm** (95% CI: 0.92–2.17 mm)
- Meta-regression (time effect): −0.08%/month (95% CI: −0.15 to −0.009%; P = 0.03)
- Publication bias: Egger's test and funnel plots assessed (results not detailed in accessible text)
