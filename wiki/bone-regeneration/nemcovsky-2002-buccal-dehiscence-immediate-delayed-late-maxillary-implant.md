## One-line Summary

Controlled clinical trial (n=66 patients, 102 implants) comparing buccal dehiscence-defect healing across immediate, delayed, and late maxillary implant placement with collagen membrane + bone graft; delayed placement produced the best defect reduction (height 88.8%, area 95.6%), with spontaneous membrane exposure and multiple simultaneous implant placement independently predicting worse healing.

## Summary

This 2002 controlled clinical trial (Tel Aviv University) compared clinical healing of buccal dehiscence-type defects around maxillary implants placed with bioabsorbable collagen barrier membrane and bone graft under three placement-timing protocols: immediate (at extraction, n=23 implants), delayed (some weeks after extraction, n=39 implants), and late (after complete healing, n=40 implants). Defect height and area were measured at implant placement and again at second-stage surgery. Delayed placement produced significantly better defect reduction than either immediate or late placement on both height (88.8% vs. 77.4% and 75.2%) and area (95.6% vs. 90.2% and 87.6%) metrics. Two additional factors independently predicted healing quality regardless of timing group: spontaneous cover-screw/membrane exposure worsened outcomes, and placing a single implant (rather than multiple implants simultaneously) improved them.

## Key Contributions

- One of the earliest controlled comparisons isolating placement timing itself as the variable driving buccal dehiscence-defect healing, under a fixed membrane+graft protocol
- Establishes delayed placement as empirically superior to both immediate and late placement for this specific outcome — a non-monotonic finding (the middle timing option wins, not either extreme)
- Identifies membrane/cover-screw exposure and multi-implant simultaneous placement as independent negative predictors, foreshadowing the mechanistic graft-containment and closure-tension findings later characterized in ex vivo models (Raabe 2025, Zhou 2025)
- Provides a pre-2000s baseline against which the primary-closure and membrane-fixation-centric literature synthesized in [[overviews/gbr-simultaneous-implant-placement-contour-dehiscence-overview]] can be read

## Methodology

Controlled clinical trial (not randomized), 3 parallel groups by placement-timing protocol. n=66 patients, 102 implants: Group 1 immediate (19 patients/23 implants, site closed with rotated split palatal flap), Group 2 delayed (25 patients/39 implants, site closed with rotated full-thickness palatal flap at extraction, implant placed weeks later), Group 3 late (22 patients/40 implants, complete healing before placement). All sites received bioabsorbable collagen barrier membrane + bone graft; 1–3 proximal implants placed simultaneously per site. Defect height/width measured at placement and at second-stage surgery; area calculated as half-ellipses.

## Results

| Group | n (patients/implants) | Defect height reduction | Defect area reduction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate | 19/23 | 77.4% ± 16.92% | 90.2% ± 9.15% |
| **Delayed** | 25/39 | **88.8% ± 15.29%** | **95.6% ± 8.73%** |
| Late | 22/40 | 75.2% ± 17.99% | 87.6% ± 11.48% |

Between-group differences statistically significant. Spontaneous cover-screw exposure and multiple-implant simultaneous placement each independently associated with significantly worse defect reduction.
