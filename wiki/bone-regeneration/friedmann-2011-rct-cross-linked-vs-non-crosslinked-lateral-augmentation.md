## Three-line Summary

Pilot RCT (University of Witten, J Clin Periodontol 38(7):677-685, 2011) comparing **ribose cross-linked collagen membrane** (RCLM, Ossix Plus, n=17) vs **non-cross-linked** (n=20) for one-stage lateral bone augmentation at peri-implant dehiscence defects with biphasic calcium phosphate (β-TCP/HA), morphometrically assessed at 6 months via customized casts.

RCLM achieved significantly greater **lateral bone gain at crestal level (1.8 vs 0.7 mm, p=0.046)** and **vertical gain (1.1 vs 0.2 mm, p=0.035)**; broader flap-margin measurements converged (3.0 vs 2.1 mm, NS), indicating that cross-linking advantage concentrates in the critical sub-crestal zone.

Soft tissue dehiscence was more common in RCLM (70.5% vs 55%), but RCLM maintained barrier function through exposure events — a key clinical advantage of cross-linking.

## 세줄요약

파일럿 RCT (독일 비텐, JCP 2011) — RCLM (Ossix Plus, n=17) vs 비가교 콜라겐 (n=20), β-TCP 이식재와 함께 임플란트 주위 골열개 측방 증대. 6개월 맞춤 캐스트 형태계측.

치조정 측방 골량 **1.8 vs 0.7 mm (p=0.046)**, 수직 **1.1 vs 0.2 mm (p=0.035)** RCLM 유의 우세. 치조정 수준(가장 중요한 임플란트 결과 지점)에서만 차이 발생 — 넓은 플랩경계 계측은 유의차 없음.

노출(dehiscence) 발생 70.5%에서도 RCLM 장벽 기능 유지 — 가교의 핵심 임상 장점.

## Clinical Decision Point

> **RCLM advantage is crestal-zone specific** — the tightest space where membrane collapse or early degradation most compromises outcomes. For shallow dehiscences or short healing periods, non-cross-linked membranes may perform adequately; for deep defects or anticipated exposure, RCLM is preferred.

## Limitations

- **Pilot**: n=17/20 — underpowered for definitive conclusions
- Cast-based morphometry; no CBCT volumetric data
- Single-blind; JCP not OA (abstract-based ingest)
- One-stage protocol only
