## Three-line Summary

Review (Implant Dentistry, 1993) classifying bone-grafting materials by mode of action and selecting them by defect morphology.

Autogenous bone is osteogenic, osteoinductive and osteoconductive; allografts are osteoinductive and osteoconductive; alloplasts are osteoconductive only. Defects with three or more lost walls need autogenous bone or a small-pore membrane.

The classification is the value here; no clinical outcome data are given, and newer materials are not covered.
