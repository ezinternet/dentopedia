## Three-line Summary

Pilot animal study (5 Labrador dogs enrolled, 1 excluded for post-operative problems → 4 analyzed) using laser Doppler flowmetry (LDF) to measure blood perfusion in full-thickness mucoperiosteal flaps repositioned over titanium implants, with an experimental biodegradable polylactic-acid membrane on one side and the contralateral side as no-membrane control.

The mean LDF value was lower on the membrane side than on the control side for each of the 4 periods studied (measurements taken before surgery, immediately after suturing, and at 24, 48, and 72 h); in the 3 animals whose membranes exposed within 2 weeks, LDF values were lower on the membrane side than the control side.

LDF appears valuable for studying oral mucosal flap perfusion, and reduced relative LDF may be linked to subsequent membrane exposure — but this is an abstract-only pilot in n=4 dogs reporting directional (not numeric) LDF results.

## 세줄요약

래브라도견 5마리 중 1마리 제외 후 4마리를 대상으로 한 파일럿 동물연구 — 상악 소구치 발거 후 전층 점막골막판 (Full-thickness Mucoperiosteal Flap)을 거상하고 각 측에 티타늄 임플란트 4개씩 식립, 한쪽에만 생분해성 폴리락틱산 (Polylactic Acid, PLA) 차단막을 덮어 조직유도재생 (Guided Tissue Regeneration, GTR)을 시행하고 반대측을 무막 대조로 삼았다.

레이저 도플러 유량측정 (Laser Doppler Flowmetry, LDF)로 수술 전·봉합 직후·24·48·72시간에 측정한 결과 막을 덮은 쪽 점막골막판의 평균 LDF 값이 4개 측정 시기 모두에서 대조측보다 낮았고, 2주 내에 막 노출이 생긴 3마리 모두에서 막 쪽 LDF가 대조측보다 낮았다.

점막골막판의 관류 감소가 이후 막 노출과 연관될 수 있음을 시사하지만, 초록만 확보된 n=4 파일럿 연구이며 LDF 결과는 절대 수치 없이 방향성으로만 보고되었다.

## Summary

This pilot dog study tested whether laser Doppler flowmetry (LDF) can evaluate blood perfusion in the mucoperiosteal flap covering a GTR membrane in bone augmentation/implant procedures. Five Labrador dogs were enrolled; one was excluded due to post-operative problems, leaving four analyzed. After maxillary premolar extraction, full-thickness mucoperiosteal flaps were raised, the buccal bone plate was removed, 4 titanium implants were placed on each side, and an experimental biodegradable polylactic-acid membrane was placed over the fixtures on one side with the contralateral side serving as no-membrane control; the flap was then repositioned and sutured. The mean LDF value was lower on the membrane side than on the control side for each of the 4 periods studied, and in the 3 animals whose membranes became exposed within 2 weeks the LDF values were lower on the membrane side. For the periosteal-elevation vascular-compromise question, this is direct hemodynamic evidence that the flap repositioned over an avascular membrane barrier is relatively under-perfused relative to an unmembraned control, and that this perfusion deficit precedes and tracks membrane exposure. Findings are directional only — the abstract reports no absolute LDF values or statistics.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrated the applicability of laser Doppler flowmetry (LDF) for measuring blood perfusion in mucoperiosteal flaps covering GTR/GBR membranes — a non-invasive, direct hemodynamic readout of the flap-over-membrane situation.
- Used a within-animal contralateral-control design (membrane side vs no-membrane side) so each dog served as its own control for flap-perfusion comparison.
- Reported a directional association between reduced relative LDF values in the membrane-covering flap and subsequent exposure of the membrane to the oral environment within 2 weeks.

## Methodology

Pilot experimental animal study in dogs with a bilateral split-side design. Subjects: 5 Labrador dogs initially; 1 excluded due to post-operative problems → n=4 analyzed. Surgery: maxillary premolar extraction → full-thickness mucoperiosteal flap elevation → removal of the buccal bone plate → 4 titanium implants placed on each side → experimental biodegradable polylactic-acid membrane placed over the fixtures on one side (GTR), contralateral side as no-membrane control → flap repositioned and secured with sutures. Outcome: blood perfusion in the flap measured by laser Doppler flowmetry before surgery, immediately after suturing, and at 24, 48, and 72 h postoperatively, with membrane exposure recorded over the early post-operative course.

## Results
