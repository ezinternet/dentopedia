## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PROSPERO-registered) of 5 preclinical studies (rats/dogs) and 22 clinical studies (1,062 patients at final evaluation) on local hyaluronic acid (HyA) application after tooth extraction or for alveolar osteitis (AO) treatment.

Meta-analysis found HyA significantly reduced pain 7 days after surgical lower-third-molar (LM3) removal (effect size 0.32, 95% CI 0.12-0.51, p=0.01) but had no significant effect on early pain, swelling, or trismus at any timepoint, and the bone-formation benefit seen in every individual preclinical study was not confirmed by meta-analysis of BV/TV at 3 months (p=0.42, I²=89.89%).

Clinical implication: HyA may modestly reduce late post-LM3-extraction pain and support soft-tissue socket closure after regular extractions, but evidence for a ridge-preservation/hard-tissue benefit is weak-to-contradictory (one study reported significantly MORE coronal horizontal bone loss with HyA), and overall GRADE certainty is low-to-moderate.

## 세줄요약

PROSPERO 등록 체계적 문헌고찰(SR)+메타분석(MA)으로, 발치 후 또는 치조골염(AO) 치료 목적의 국소 히알루론산(hyaluronic acid, HyA) 적용을 다룬 전임상연구 5편(쥐/개)과 임상연구 22편(최종평가 1,062명)을 통합.

메타분석에서 HyA는 하악 제3대구치(LM3) 수술적 발치 후 7일째 통증을 유의하게 감소시켰으나(effect size 0.32, 95% CI 0.12-0.51, p=0.01) 초기 통증·부종·개구제한(trismus)에는 유의한 효과가 없었고, 전임상 개별연구 전부에서 관찰된 신생골형성 개선효과는 3개월 시점 BV/TV 메타분석에서 확인되지 않음(p=0.42, I²=89.89%).

임상적 의미: HyA는 LM3 발치 후 후기 통증 및 정상 발치 후 연조직 치유에 제한적 도움이 될 수 있으나, 치조제 보존/경조직 효과 근거는 약하거나 상반되며(한 연구는 오히려 치관측 수평골소실 증가 보고), 전체 GRADE 근거 확실성은 낮음-중등도.

## Summary

This systematic review + meta-analysis (Domic 2023, PROSPERO CRD42021266190/CRD42021266183) is the most comprehensive quantitative synthesis to date of hyaluronic acid (HyA) as an adjunct in tooth extraction. It separately analyzes three clinical contexts — surgical lower-third-molar (LM3) removal, non-surgical extraction of regularly erupted teeth, and treatment of alveolar osteitis (AO/dry socket) — plus 5 preclinical (rat/dog) studies on socket bone healing. The headline finding is a statistically significant but narrow benefit: HyA reduces pain specifically at day 7 (not day 2-3) after LM3 removal, with no measurable effect on swelling or trismus at either timepoint. On the hard-tissue side, the review explicitly documents a translation failure — every individual preclinical study showed improved bone formation with HyA, but pooling the 2 studies with comparable BV/TV data at 3 months showed no significant effect, and one clinical ridge-preservation study reported the opposite direction (significantly increased horizontal bone loss with HyA). This makes the paper a caution against extrapolating HyA's preclinical bone-healing signal to a ridge-preservation clinical benefit.

## Key Contributions

- First combined preclinical + clinical systematic review of HyA across the full range of tooth-extraction indications (LM3 surgical removal, regular extraction, AO treatment), rather than restricting to third-molar surgery as prior reviews did.
- Quantitative confirmation that HyA's clinical pain benefit after LM3 removal is time-window-specific: significant only at day 7, not at day 2-3 — refining an earlier systematic review's finding of benefit at both day 3 and day 7 (attributed to inclusion of 2 additional RCTs and exclusion of one non-randomized study that had strongly favored HyA).
- Explicit demonstration that preclinical bone-formation benefit (positive in 5/5 individual studies) does not survive meta-analytic pooling (BV/TV at 3 months, p=0.42, I²=89.89%) — a preclinical-to-clinical translation gap flagged directly in the discussion.
- Documents a clinically important contradictory finding: intra-/post-operative HyA gel applied after ridge preservation (collagen-enriched deproteinized bovine bone mineral + collagen matrix sealing) was associated with significantly MORE coronal horizontal bone loss vs no HyA.
- GRADE certainty ratings applied throughout: low (preclinical BV/TV), moderate (clinical pain, trismus), low (clinical swelling) — signaling that clinical recommendations should remain cautious despite statistically significant point estimates.

## Methodology

- **Search**: Ovid (MEDLINE+CENTRAL), EMBASE, PubMed; searched Oct 2021, updated Apr 2022. 147→90 (dedup)→33 (full-text)→27 included (5 preclinical + 22 clinical).
- **Preclinical inclusion**: randomized/non-randomized controlled experiments, local HyA application (alone or combined) after tooth extraction.
- **Clinical inclusion**: RCT/CT/case series (n≥10), local HyA after extraction or for AO treatment.
- **Risk of bias**: SYRCLE RoB (preclinical), Cochrane RoB 2.0 (RCTs), ROBINS-I (non-randomized).
- **Meta-analysis**: restricted to RCTs; REML heterogeneity + Knapp-Hartung SE adjustment; split-mouth data treated as dependent (r=0.5); chi-square/I² for heterogeneity; STATA/IC 17.0.
- **GRADE**: GRADEpro GDT for all pooled outcomes.

## Results

| Outcome | Timepoint | n RCTs | Effect size | 95% CI | p | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pain (LM3) | 2-3 days | 4 | 0.52 | -0.34 to 1.38 | 0.15 | 0.00% |
| Pain (LM3) | 7 days | 5 | 0.32 | 0.12 to 0.51 | **0.01** | 0.00% |
| Swelling (LM3) | 2-3 days | 2 | -2.08 | -23.73 to 19.58 | 0.44 | 87.94% |
| Swelling (LM3) | 7 days | — | 1.75 | -14.38 to 17.89 | 0.40 | 66.86% |
| Trismus (LM3) | 2-3 days | 3 | 1.31 | -0.65 to 3.26 | 0.10 | 25.86% |
| Trismus (LM3) | 7 days | 5 | 1.08 | -0.97 to 3.12 | 0.22 | 55.99% |
| BV/TV (preclinical) | 3 months | 2 | 9.57 | -86.22 to 105.36 | 0.42 | 89.89% |

- Population: LM3 removal — 603 patients (306 HyA / 370 control); regular extraction — 349 patients (226 HyA / 257 control); AO treatment — 110 patients (90 HyA / 20 control). Preclinical: rats (healthy + diabetic models) and 20 beagle dogs (infected sockets).
- Soft-tissue healing after regular extraction: 3/4 studies showed significantly improved healing with HyA.
- Hard-tissue outcomes after regular extraction: mixed (1 study improved bone formation at 30 days; 1 reduced alveolar-diameter loss to 21 days; 2 found no difference or a significant HyA disadvantage, including increased coronal horizontal bone loss in one ridge-preservation study).
- AO treatment: HyA significantly reduced postoperative pain vs alvogyl in the 1 controlled study.
- Safety: no meaningful adverse events across 27 studies (1 study noted a prolonged but physiologically normal bleeding time).
