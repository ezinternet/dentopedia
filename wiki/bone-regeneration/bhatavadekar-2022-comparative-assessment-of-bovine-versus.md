## Three-line Summary

Randomized prospective cohort study (n=40; 10 per group) comparing bovine-derived xenograft (Cerabone) vs porcine-derived xenograft (MinerOss XP) in two indications — horizontal ridge augmentation (HRA) for veneer contour and extraction socket preservation (ESP) — with 6-month CBCT follow-up.

HRA width rose 5.43 ± 1.82 → 7.75 ± 1.91 mm (bovine) and 5.75 ± 1.64 → 8.75 ± 1.38 mm (porcine), abstract P = .36 at baseline and P = .03 at 6 months; ESP width fell 6.3 ± 1.26 → 5.8 ± 1.21 mm (bovine) and 6.74 ± 1.29 → 6.61 ± 1.56 mm (porcine), abstract P = .16 and P = .01 — with significantly lower dimensional change in the porcine ESP group (Group 4) at 6 months.

Porcine xenograft is a stable, biocompatible osteoconductive alternative that widens graft choice, but n=10/group, unreported allocation concealment and blinding, no histomorphometric numbers, product-specific comparison and abstract-only retrieval keep this supportive rather than decisive evidence.

## 세줄요약

무작위 전향 코호트 연구(RCT 성격, n=40, 군당 10명): 소뼈 유래 이종골 (Bovine Xenograft, Cerabone) 대 돼지뼈 유래 이종골 (Porcine Xenograft, MinerOss XP)을 수평 치조 증강 (Horizontal Ridge Augmentation, HRA)·발치 소켓 보존 (Extraction Socket Preservation, ESP) 두 적응증에서 6개월 CBCT 추적으로 비교.

HRA에서 협설골 폭 (Buccolingual Width)은 소뼈군 5.43 ± 1.82 → 7.75 ± 1.91 mm, 돼지뼈군 5.75 ± 1.64 → 8.75 ± 1.38 mm(초록 보고 p = .36, .03); ESP에서는 소뼈군 6.3 ± 1.26 → 5.8 ± 1.21 mm, 돼지뼈군 6.74 ± 1.29 → 6.61 ± 1.56 mm(p = .16, .01)로, 6개월 시점 돼지뼈 ESP군(4군)의 치수 변화가 유의하게 작았다.

돼지뼈 이종골은 안정적이고 생체적합성이 있는 골전도성 (Osteoconductive) 대체재로 재료 선택지를 늘리지만, 군당 n=10·무작위 배정/눈가림 미기재·조직형태계측 수치 미제시·특정 제품 비교·초록 전용 수집(전문 미확보)으로 보조적 증거의 성격이 강하다.

## Summary

This randomized prospective cohort study (Int J Periodontics Restorative Dent 2022;42(6):789-796; PMID 36305933) compared a bovine-derived xenograft (Cerabone) with a porcine-derived xenograft (MinerOss XP) in two augmentation indications under one protocol: horizontal ridge augmentation (HRA) for veneer contour and extraction socket preservation (ESP), with 40 subjects (10 per group) and 6-month follow-up. Buccolingual ridge width was measured on cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) at baseline and 6 months, and representative histologic core samples were taken from the ESP groups at 6 months.

In HRA, width increased in both arms (bovine 5.43 ± 1.82 → 7.75 ± 1.91 mm; porcine 5.75 ± 1.64 → 8.75 ± 1.38 mm). In ESP, width decreased in both arms, but less in the porcine group (bovine 6.3 ± 1.26 → 5.8 ± 1.21 mm; porcine 6.74 ± 1.29 → 6.61 ± 1.56 mm); the abstract states explicitly that significantly lower dimensional changes were noted in Group 4 (ESP + porcine) at 6 months.

The authors conclude that porcine xenografts "serve as a stable biocompatible osteoconductive bone substitute and expand a clinician's choice of bone grafts in dental applications" — a species-alternative (e.g. for bovine-restricted patients) rather than a superiority claim.

This page is **abstract-only** — the full text was not retrieved (closed access; no open-access copy located), so randomization method, allocation concealment, blinding, sample-size basis, exact statistical tests, baseline characteristics, histomorphometric values and implant outcomes are not available.

## Key Contributions

- Head-to-head comparison of **two xenograft species in the same protocol**: bovine (Cerabone) vs porcine (MinerOss XP), run across **two indications** (HRA for veneer contour, ESP) with one measurement method (CBCT buccolingual width) at a common 6-month endpoint.
- Fills the **horizontal-augmentation gap** in the porcine-vs-bovine literature: the wiki's dedicated SR+MA ([[bone-regeneration/wang-2025-porcine-bovine-xenograft-msfa-arp-sr-ma]]) deliberately excluded horizontal/vertical GBR to control heterogeneity, so no pooled head-to-head evidence existed for HRA before this trial.
- Shows the **indication-dependence** of the comparison: both materials gain width in HRA, while in ESP both lose width and the porcine arm loses significantly less — "interchangeable" cannot be asserted as one statement across indications.
- Adds **histologic core sampling** from both ESP groups at 6 months, giving the study a bone-turnover dimension beyond radiographic width (values not quoted in the abstract).
- Provides a **product-level** data point (Cerabone vs MinerOss XP) for the species-choice question — clinically relevant where bovine-derived material is unacceptable on religious or medical grounds.

## Methodology

- **Design:** randomized prospective cohort study with four parallel groups; PubMed indexes the record as a Randomized Controlled Trial, Journal Article.
- **n:** 40 subjects — 10 in each of Group 1 (HRA + bovine), Group 2 (HRA + porcine), Group 3 (ESP + bovine), Group 4 (ESP + porcine).
- **Materials:** bovine-derived xenograft Cerabone; porcine-derived xenograft MinerOss XP.
- **Indications:** horizontal ridge augmentation for veneer contour; extraction socket preservation.
- **Follow-up:** 6 months.
- **Outcomes:** buccolingual ridge width on CBCT at baseline and 6 months; representative histologic core samples from the ESP groups at 6 months.
- **Not reported in the abstract:** sequence generation, allocation concealment, blinding, sample-size calculation, the statistical test behind each P value, baseline characteristics, and whether the trial was single- or multi-centre.

## Results

| Group (indication + material) | Baseline (mm) | 6 months (mm) | Change (mm, derived) | Abstract P values |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Group 1 — HRA + bovine (Cerabone) | 5.43 ± 1.82 | 7.75 ± 1.91 | +2.32 | P = .36 (baseline pair) / P = .03 (6-month pair) |
| Group 2 — HRA + porcine (MinerOss XP) | 5.75 ± 1.64 | 8.75 ± 1.38 | +3.00 | P = .36 (baseline pair) / P = .03 (6-month pair) |
| Group 3 — ESP + bovine (Cerabone) | 6.3 ± 1.26 | 5.8 ± 1.21 | −0.50 | P = .16 (baseline pair) / P = .01 (6-month pair) |
| Group 4 — ESP + porcine (MinerOss XP) | 6.74 ± 1.29 | 6.61 ± 1.56 | −0.13 | P = .16 (baseline pair) / P = .01 (6-month pair) |

- **Key inferential claim (verbatim from the abstract):** "Significantly lower dimensional changes were noted in Group 4 at 6 months."
- **HRA:** both materials gained buccolingual width; the porcine arm reached the larger absolute width at 6 months (8.75 vs 7.75 mm).
- **ESP:** both materials lost width over 6 months; the porcine arm retained more (6.61 vs 5.8 mm), i.e. less post-extraction resorption.
- **Histomorphometry:** cores were harvested from both ESP groups at 6 months, but **no histomorphometric values are reported in the abstract** — new bone, residual graft and connective tissue proportions are not quotable from this page.
- *Change values are arithmetic derived from the reported means. The P values are reproduced exactly as paired in the abstract, which does not label which comparison each belongs to (between-group at that time point vs within-group change).*

## Limitations

- **Abstract-only ingestion** — full text not retrieved (closed access); methods, statistics and outcomes are limited to what the abstract states.
- **Small arms (10 per group)** — the ESP between-group difference rests on two 10-subject arms, unadjusted for baseline width; statistically significant but fragile.
- **P-value labelling is ambiguous** in the abstract; only the direction of the Group 4 finding is unambiguous.
- **No histomorphometric numbers** despite core sampling from both ESP groups.
- **6-month horizon** — no long-term remodelling, implant survival or late-complication data.
- **Product-level, not species-level** — Cerabone vs MinerOss XP cannot be generalized to all bovine or all porcine xenografts.
- **No allocation concealment / blinding detail** — cannot assess risk of bias beyond what the abstract reveals.
