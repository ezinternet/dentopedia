## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo preclinical study, 60 horizontal bone augmentation (HBA) procedures on 30 fresh pig hemimandibles with uncontained (concavity) defects, testing moderate (FA1) vs. major (FA2) flap advancement, each with vs. without two-pin membrane fixation.

FA2 achieved greater advancement (9.20±0.55mm) at lower flap tension (0.02±0.01N) than FA1 (4.42±0.67mm; 0.09±0.02N), and crestal graft displacement (ΔGMT at L0) was significantly smaller for FA2 (−35.66±30.68%) than FA1 (−44.99±20.80%, p<0.001); two-pin membrane fixation had no significant effect on ΔGMT at any level (p≥0.19).

Achieving greater flap advancement at lower tension — not adding two fixation pins — is the modifiable factor that best preserves crestal graft volume in uncontained defects during primary closure; limitation is the ex vivo model with no healing period.

FA2는 FA1보다 더 큰 전진량(9.20±0.55mm vs 4.42±0.67mm)을 더 낮은 피판 장력(flap tension, 0.02±0.01N vs 0.09±0.02N)으로 달성했고, 치은변연부 이식재 변위(ΔGMT, L0)는 FA2에서 유의하게 작았다(−35.66±30.68% vs −44.99±20.80%, p<0.001); 2-핀 막 고정은 어느 레벨에서도 유의한 효과가 없었다(p≥0.19).

## Summary

This ex vivo preclinical study (30 fresh pig hemimandibles, 60 horizontal bone augmentation (HBA) procedures) isolated the effect of flap-advancement magnitude and its associated flap tension on graft material displacement (ΔGMT) during primary wound closure in guided bone regeneration (GBR), using an uncontained (bone concavity) defect model. Flaps were advanced either moderately (FA1: single periosteal releasing incision, target 4–5mm) or majorly (FA2: additional periosteal scoring, target 9–10mm), each with or without two-pin collagen-membrane fixation, in a 2×2 factorial design. Flap tension was measured directly (spring dynamometer), and ΔGMT was measured via CBCT at nine levels (L0=crest to L8=8mm apical) before and after closure.

FA2 achieved nearly double the advancement of FA1 (9.20±0.55mm vs. 4.42±0.67mm) at roughly a quarter of the tension (0.02±0.01N vs. 0.09±0.02N). This translated into significantly less crestal graft displacement: ΔGMT at L0 was −35.66±30.68% for FA2 vs. −44.99±20.80% for FA1 (p<0.001), with a similar pattern at L4 (p=0.02) but no significant difference at other levels. Two-pin membrane fixation did not significantly reduce ΔGMT at any level or in interaction with FA (p≥0.19 overall; FA×Fixation interaction p=0.56). GEE regression confirmed FA as the only significant independent predictor of ΔGMT (beta=0.46, 95% CI 0.15–0.77, p=0.004), while soft-tissue phenotype (keratinized mucosa width, mucosal thickness) was not significant once tension was accounted for. Clinically, the study suggests that in uncontained defects, achieving a low-tension, well-advanced flap matters more for crestal graft preservation than adding two fixation pins.

## Key Contributions

- First study to isolate flap-advancement magnitude / flap tension itself (rather than defect morphology or fixation) as a driver of GBR graft-material displacement, with flap tension measured directly and objectively (spring dynamometer, N) rather than inferred.
- Tests membrane pin fixation specifically in an uncontained (concavity) defect — most prior fixation-benefit evidence comes from contained/box-shaped defects — and finds two-pin fixation insufficient here, a defect-morphology-dependent boundary condition.
- Rules out soft-tissue phenotype (KMW, mucosal thickness) as an independent predictor of ΔGMT once flap tension is accounted for.
- Provides a quantitative benchmark (−35 to −45% crestal ΔGMT depending on advancement/tension) for the magnitude of "over-contouring" needed during grafting to compensate for closure-induced collapse.

## Methodology

Ex vivo, 2×2 factorial, pig hemimandible model (30 hemimandibles, 60 HBA procedures, n=15/group: FA1−Pins, FA1+Pins, FA2−Pins, FA2+Pins; each mandible received FA1 then FA2 sequentially). Bone concavity (uncontained defect) at the diastema region augmented with particulate DBBM (Bio-Oss) + resorbable collagen membrane (Bio-Gide), ± two non-threaded press-fit pins. Closure performed by a blinded second surgeon. Flap advancement measured via periodontal probe, flap tension via spring dynamometer at a central interrupted suture. GMT measured via CBCT at 9 levels (L0–L8, 1mm increments) before/after closure; primary outcome ΔGMT (%) at L0. Repeated-measures ANOVA (Greenhouse-Geisser corrected) for FA×Fixation×Level; GEE multiple linear regression for FA/fixation/soft-tissue-phenotype predictors. Intra-examiner ICC=0.85.

## Results

**Flap advancement and tension:**

| Group | Advancement (mm) | Tension (N) |
|---|---|---|
| FA1 (moderate) | 4.42 ± 0.67 | 0.09 ± 0.02 |
| FA2 (major) | 9.20 ± 0.55 | 0.02 ± 0.01 |

**ΔGMT (%) at L0 by group (2×2):**

| Group | ΔGMT (%) at L0 |
|---|---|
| FA1 − Pins | −42.3 ± 16.5 |
| FA1 + Pins | −47.6 ± 24.6 |
| FA2 − Pins | −30.6 ± 29.4 |
| FA2 + Pins | −40.7 ± 32.0 |

**Main effects (repeated-measures ANOVA):**

| Factor | F | p |
|---|---|---|
| Flap advancement (FA) | 9.41 | 0.008 |
| Fixation (pins) | 0.32 | 0.58 |
| Level (L0–L8) | 53.4 | <0.001 |
| FA × Fixation | 0.36 | 0.56 |

**GEE regression, significant predictor:** FA2 vs. FA1, beta=0.46 (95% CI 0.15–0.77), p=0.004. KMW, MT6 not significant (p=0.77, 0.15); MT3/MT9 only marginal before tension adjustment (p=0.06 each), non-significant after.
