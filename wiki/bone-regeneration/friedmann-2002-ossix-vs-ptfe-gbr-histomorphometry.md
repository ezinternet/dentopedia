## Three-line Summary

Landmark RCT (Charité/Humboldt University Berlin, Clin Oral Implants Res 13(6):587-594, 2002) — first peer-reviewed comparison of **Ossix** (original GLYMATRIX ribose-crosslinked collagen barrier) vs **e-PTFE** (Gore-Tex, non-resorbable gold standard) for jaw bone augmentation with DBBM in 28 patients, biopsies at 7 months.

Total mineralized bone **42% (Ossix) vs 39% (e-PTFE), non-significant** (NS), with similar unmineralized tissue and DBBM remnants (~14%) in both groups — establishing histomorphometric equivalence between the first resorbable GLYMATRIX product and the gold-standard non-resorbable membrane.

Barrier exposure in both groups had **no effect on histologic outcome** — confirming the fundamental GLYMATRIX durability advantage: unlike conventional resorbable membranes, cross-linked Ossix maintains barrier function through exposure.

## 세줄요약

랜드마크 RCT (베를린 샤리테, COIR 2002) — 흡수성 Ossix (ribose 가교, 현 Ossix Plus 전신) vs 비흡수성 e-PTFE(Gore-Tex), DBBM 이식재, 28명, 7개월 생검.

총 석회화골 **42% vs 39% (유의차 없음)** — 흡수성 가교 콜라겐이 비흡수성 골드스탠다드와 동등한 골질 달성. DBBM 잔존 양쪽 모두 14-15%.

노출 발생해도 조직학적 결과 무영향 — GLYMATRIX 가교의 노출 내성 최초 임상 확인. 이 연구가 Ossix Plus·Ossix Volumax·OSSIX Bone 전체 플랫폼의 임상 근거 출발점.

## Historical Significance

This 2002 study is the **origin of all GLYMATRIX clinical evidence**:

| Year | Paper | GLYMATRIX product | Advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2002** | **Friedmann (this)** | Ossix (original) | Equivalence to e-PTFE established |
| 2011 | Friedmann | Ossix Plus | Superiority over non-CL membrane in lateral augmentation |
| 2012 | Cook & Mealey | Cross-linked sponge + Ossix Plus | Superior socket preservation (47% vs 33% bone) |
| 2019 | Smidt | Ossix Volumax | 1.5-2mm thick scaffold for lateral ridge, no bone graft |
| 2023 | Canullo | OSSIX Bone | HA/collagen sponge for sinus lift, complete ossification |

Note: Sandu Pitaru (Datum Dental co-founder, Ossix developer) is co-author of this landmark paper — confirming the laboratory-to-clinic lineage.

## Limitations

- Small n (28; biopsies: 14 vs 13)
- 7-month histologic endpoint only; implant survival not evaluated
- Ossix = original formulation (pre-Ossix Plus); handling and thickness differ
- Abstract-based ingest; COIR not OA in 2002
