## Three-line Summary

Review (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2001) comparing sulcular, marginal and alveolar mucosal incision designs for symphysis graft harvesting.

Selection factors named are periodontal status, bone loss, root-fenestration risk, keratinized gingiva, gingival-margin restorations and local musculature.

The abstract argues that incision choice is often made on habit; no outcome comparison is given.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2001) — 턱끝 골이식 채취의 세 가지 수평 절개 설계 비교.

선택 요인은 치주 상태, 골소실, 치근 열개 위험, 각화치은, 치은연 수복물, 국소 근육.

절개 선택이 관습에 의존한다고 지적하지만 결과 비교는 없음.

## Summary

Gapski, Wang and Misch frame the symphysis graft as a soft-tissue problem as much as a bone problem, and offer a checklist for choosing the incision. The review is narrative and does not grade the designs against each other.

## Key Contributions

- Side-by-side comparison of three symphysis incision designs.
- Clinical checklist for incision selection.

## Methodology

Narrative literature review.

## Results

Qualitative comparison; no numeric outcomes.
