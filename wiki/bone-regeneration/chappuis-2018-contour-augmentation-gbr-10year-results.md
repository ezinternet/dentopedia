## Summary
This 10-year prospective case series (Buser group, Bern) evaluated early implant placement (Type 2, 6–8 weeks post-extraction) combined with simultaneous contour augmentation via GBR using a 2-layer composite graft in 20 patients at esthetic zone (anterior maxilla). The 2-layer protocol uses autogenous bone chips (inner, osteogenic) and a low-substitution xenograft (outer, volume maintenance) under a biodegradable collagen membrane. At 10 years: 95% success rate, median peri-implant bone loss of 0.35 mm (1–10 year period), facial bone wall thickness increased from 0 mm at surgery to 1.67 mm at 10 years, facial vertical bone loss of only 0.02 mm between 6 and 10 years. PES median was 8 (high esthetic outcome). Key modulating factors: preoperative proximal crest width and soft tissue thickness.

## Key Contributions
- Confirms long-term (10-year) stability of the 2-layer composite GBR protocol for esthetic zone early implant placement
- Facial bone wall rebuilt from zero to clinically meaningful 1.67 mm — demonstrating true bone regeneration
- Extremely low bone loss rate in maintenance phase (6–10 years: 0.02 mm/year) — functionally stable
- Identifies patient-specific modulating factors: narrow proximal crest and thin soft tissue predict less favorable outcomes
- Equivalence testing methodology: robust confirmation of stable bone conditions (null: >0.2 mm/yr difference rejected)

## Methodology
Prospective case series, n=20, single center (University of Bern). Early placement (Type 2, 6–8 weeks post-extraction) + simultaneous 2-layer composite GBR (autogenous bone chips + low-substitution xenograft + collagen membrane). CBCT at pre-op, 6 years, 10 years for facial bone wall assessment. Periapical radiographs for MBL. PES and mucosal recession assessed clinically. Statistical: nonparametric analysis + equivalence testing. Registration: NCT03252106.

## Results
- Success rate: 95% (19/20)
- Peri-implant bone loss (1→10 years): median 0.35 mm
- Facial bone wall thickness: 0 mm (surgery) → 1.67 mm (10 years) — significant increase (p<0.001)
- Facial vertical bone loss (6→10 years): 0.02 mm — clinically negligible
- DIC location: 0.16 mm coronal to implant shoulder — favorable position
- PES (10 years): median 8
- Facial recession (1→10 years): median 0.17 mm — minimal
- Equivalence test: bone change <0.2 mm/yr confirmed (stable conditions)
