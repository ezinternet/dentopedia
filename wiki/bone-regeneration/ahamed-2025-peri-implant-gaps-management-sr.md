## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA-P, PROSPERO CRD42024508852) of 11 RCTs (2013-2024) comparing grafting materials/techniques for managing the peri-implant "jumping gap" after immediate implant placement.

Xenografts and alloplastic grafts consistently outperformed PRF and autogenous bone in preserving buccal/marginal bone; socket-shield technique and immediate provisionalization (Prosthetic Socket Preservation) favored soft-tissue/esthetic preservation; implant stability (ISQ) generally did not differ significantly by graft material.

High between-study heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis/GRADE; allogenic bone grafts were essentially untested (1 of 11 trials, and only as a mixture).

## Summary

This SR addresses a narrow but clinically important question distinct from "immediate implant placement" broadly: once an implant is placed immediately into an extraction socket, how should the residual horizontal gap between the implant surface and the buccal bone plate ("jumping gap"/"peri-implant gap") be managed to preserve bone, soft tissue, and esthetics? Following PRISMA-P/PROSPERO methodology, a search of MEDLINE, Embase, and Cochrane CENTRAL (2013-2024) plus grey literature yielded 11 eligible RCTs, all assessed with RoB 2. No meta-analysis was performed due to high clinical and methodological heterogeneity — the review is a narrative synthesis of individual-study effect directions, not a pooled quantitative estimate.

## Key Contributions

- Focuses specifically on jumping-gap management (not immediate placement generally), consolidating 11 RCTs that individually compare specific grafting materials/techniques.
- Documents a consistent pattern: xenograft/alloplastic bone grafts outperform PRF and autogenous bone for buccal bone and marginal bone preservation across multiple independent trials (Elsheikh 2023, Elbrashy 2022, Sanz 2016).
- Highlights that implant stability (ISQ) is largely insensitive to graft material choice (Samy 2024, Elsheikh 2023, Hammad 2021, Adam 2020 all report ns differences) — suggesting ISQ is driven more by surgical technique/implant design than gap-filling material.
- Surfaces two non-material management levers: socket-shield technique (Atef 2021) and immediate provisionalization / Prosthetic Socket Preservation (PSP) protocols (magnetic mallet, bone condensation, piezo-surgery) as soft-tissue/esthetic-preserving alternatives or adjuncts to grafting.
- Flags PRF's inconsistent evidence — beneficial for bone density in Shaaban Metwally (2021, p=0.039) but inferior to xenograft/alloplast for buccal bone preservation in Elsheikh (2023) and Elbrashy (2022).

## Methodology

- PRISMA-P 2015-compliant, PROSPERO CRD42024508852.
- Databases: MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL (Jan 2013-present) + Google Scholar grey literature + manual reference screening. English-language only.
- PICOS: humans with immediate implant placement + jumping gap; any gap-management intervention vs comparator/control; primary outcomes = soft tissue contour, buccal bone level, esthetics; secondary = implant stability, survival. Eligible designs: RCT, cohort, case-control, 2013-2024; excluded case reports/series <10, reviews/editorials/commentaries.
- Screening: Rayyan, 2 independent reviewers + third-reviewer arbitration.
- Risk of bias: RoB 2 (all 11 included studies were RCTs).
- Flow: 102 → 49 (dedup/eligibility) → 39 sought full text (1 inaccessible) → 27 excluded → **11 included**.

## Results

11 RCTs, follow-up 6-18 months, predominantly maxillary arch (anterior esthetic zone or premolar sites):

| Study | Comparison | Result |
|---|---|---|
| El Ebiary 2023 | xenograft+autogenous vs no graft | higher PES at 6mo (p=0.048) |
| Samy 2024 | MPM vs beta-TCP | MPM better MBL/bone density; ISQ ns |
| Elsheikh 2023 | xenograft/alloplast vs PRF | better buccal bone preservation (p<0.001); ISQ ns |
| Amer 2022 | hyaluronic acid+melatonin gel vs none | better gingival index/probing depth (p<0.05/p=0.001) |
| Elbrashy 2022 | bovine cancellous xenograft vs PRF | better crestal bone maintenance (p=0.002) |
| Hammad 2021 | mixed allograft-xenograft vs xenograft | minimal MBL diff; esthetics ns |
| Shaaban Metwally 2021 | PRF vs non-PRF | higher bone density at 9mo (p=0.039) |
| Atef 2021 | socket shield vs bovine xenograft | better midfacial mucosal preservation (p=0.017) |
| Adam 2020 | NanoBone vs autogenous chin graft | higher marginal bone level; ISQ similar |
| Naji 2019 | flap+graft vs flapless-no-graft | similar horizontal bone healing |
| Sanz 2016 | DBBM-C vs no graft | less horizontal crest resorption (p=0.018-0.029) |

Bottom line: xenograft/alloplastic grafts > PRF/autogenous bone for hard-tissue preservation in most direct comparisons; ISQ largely material-independent; socket shield and PSP/immediate provisionalization protocols are promising non-grafting or adjunctive levers for soft-tissue and esthetic outcomes; allogenic bone (allograft alone) remains essentially untested.
