## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020) of 7 observational studies (8 datasets; 190 patients, 202 healthy controls) measuring masseter stiffness by shear wave elastography or ARFI in bruxism and/or myofascial pain-related TMD.

Masseter stiffness was higher in patients: Hedges g = 1.485 (95% CI 0.763-2.207; I2 = 84.1%); kPa sensitivity analysis MD = 6.16 kPa (95% CI 3.67-8.65; I2 = 84.4%).

GRADE certainty was very low with substantial heterogeneity, so elastography should be interpreted cautiously alongside clinical assessment; absolute values are not interpretable.

## 세줄요약

관찰연구 7편 (8개 데이터셋; 환자 190명, 건강 대조군 202명)을 포함한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR/MA)으로, 이갈이 (Bruxism) 및/또는 근막통 관련 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에서 전단파 탄성초음파 (Shear Wave Elastography, SWE) 또는 음향방사력 임펄스 영상 (Acoustic Radiation Force Impulse, ARFI)으로 교근 (Masseter) 강성을 측정했다.

환자군의 교근 강성이 더 높았다: Hedges g = 1.485 (95% CI 0.763-2.207; I2 = 84.1%), kPa 민감도 분석 평균차 (Mean Difference, MD) = 6.16 kPa (95% CI 3.67-8.65).

근거 확실성 (GRADE)은 매우 낮고 이질성이 커서 절대값 해석은 제한적이며, 탄성초음파는 임상 평가와 함께 신중히 해석해야 한다.

## Summary

This SR/MA (Oral Radiology 2026; PubMed PMID 41874803, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11282-026-00914-w)) asks whether masseter stiffness, measured quantitatively by ultrasound elastography, differs between people with bruxism and/or myofascial pain-related TMD and healthy controls. Seven observational studies (8 datasets, n = 392) were pooled. Stiffness was higher in patients (Hedges g = 1.485), with a consistent kPa-scale difference of 6.16 kPa. Heterogeneity was substantial (I2 ~84%) and GRADE certainty very low, so the finding supports a direction of effect but not absolute reference values or diagnostic thresholds.

What it adds versus the closest held page: [[wiki/tmj/chan-2022-myogenous-tmd-diagnosis-treatment-review]] (narrative review) relies on palpation-based DC/TMD diagnosis; this paper contributes the first pooled, objective imaging-based difference in muscle stiffness. It does not evaluate treatment; treatment-related masseter elasticity change is covered separately in [[wiki/botulinum-toxin/yan-2025-masseter-elasticity-botulinum-toxin-ultrasound]] (a BoNT-A longitudinal study). No held page is superseded.

## Key Contributions

- First pooled estimate of masseter stiffness versus controls in bruxism and/or myofascial pain-related TMD.
- Both standardized (Hedges g) and kPa-based pooled effects, concordant in direction and significance.
- Explicit GRADE rating (very low) and robustness check (leave-one-out).

## Methodology

- PRISMA 2020; PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, OpenGrey to November 2025.
- Observational human studies with SWE or ARFI masseter stiffness and a healthy control group.
- Random-effects SMD with Hartung-Knapp adjustment; kPa MD sensitivity analysis; Newcastle-Ottawa Scale; funnel plot/Egger (limited power); GRADE.
