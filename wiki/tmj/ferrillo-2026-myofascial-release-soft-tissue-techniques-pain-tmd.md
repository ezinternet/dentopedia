## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis; PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251161805, searched to 31 Jan 2026) of 7 RCTs (n=359, 2018-2025) testing myofascial release (MFR) and related soft-tissue techniques for pain in myogenous temporomandibular disorders (TMD).

Most trials reported statistically significant pain reductions (e.g., Serrano-Hernanz VAS 7.2 to 4.9 post-treatment and 4.4 at 3 months vs sham), but only a subset cleared the a priori MCID of 15-22 mm; MFR was not different from oral analgesics (Gurudut, NPRS p=0.066) or from PBM-combined arms (Brochado); RoB 2: 0 low, 6 some concerns, 1 high; GRADE low for pain and mouth function, very low for QoL.

MFR can be considered within a multidisciplinary TMD plan, but effects cannot be attributed to MFR alone because interventions were heterogeneous and often multimodal; low-certainty evidence and sub-MCID effects in several trials mean isolated-MFR RCTs are still needed.

## 세줄요약

근육형 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 통증에 대한 근막이완술 (Myofascial Release, MFR) 및 관련 연조직 기법의 무작위배정 임상시험 7편 (총 359명, PRISMA 2020, 2026년 1월 31일까지 검색) 체계적 문헌고찰이며 메타분석은 시행하지 않았다.

대부분의 시험에서 통계적으로 유의한 통증 감소가 있었으나 (예: Serrano-Hernanz 시각통증척도 7.2에서 치료 직후 4.9, 3개월 4.4, 가짜치료 대비 유의), 사전 설정한 최소 임상적 중요 차이 (Minimum Clinically Important Difference, MCID) 15-22 mm를 넘은 시험은 일부뿐이었고, 비뚤림 위험 (RoB 2)은 낮음 0편·일부 우려 6편·높음 1편, GRADE 확실성은 통증·개구 기능 낮음, 삶의 질 매우 낮음이었다.

MFR은 다학제 TMD 치료의 일부로 고려할 수 있으나 중재가 이질적이고 복합 처치가 많아 MFR 단독 효과로 귀속할 수 없으며, 근거 확실성이 낮고 일부 효과가 MCID 미만이어서 MFR 단독 시험이 더 필요하다.

## Summary

Based on articles retrieved from PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/joor.70271)), this 2026 systematic review (J Oral Rehabil) asks whether myofascial release (MFR) and related soft-tissue techniques reduce pain in adults with myogenous TMD. Seven RCTs (n=359; RDC/TMD or DC/TMD diagnosis) were synthesized narratively; no meta-analysis was done because comparators, techniques, dose, and follow-up differed too much. Interventions included manual therapy, diacutaneous fibrolysis, pressure release, physiotherapy embedded in a splint-plus-education package, soft-tissue mobilization with electro-massage, and manual therapy vs dry needling vs CBT.

## Key Contributions

- First MFR-specific RCT-only SR using RoB 2 and GRADE with an explicit MCID benchmark (15-22 mm on a 100 mm scale).
- Separates statistical from clinical significance: several positive trials did not clearly exceed the MCID.
- Warns against crediting MFR alone, because most protocols were multimodal.

## Methodology

PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420251161805 (registered after screening began, so not fully prospective); PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science to 31 Jan 2026; 857 records, 7 RCTs included; two reviewers; RoB 2 (weighted kappa 0.84); GRADE; narrative synthesis.

## Results

- Risk of bias: 0 low, 6 some concerns, 1 high (Ferreira 2024, outcome measurement). Participants could not be blinded to manual interventions, which alone forces "some concerns" for self-reported pain.
- Certainty (GRADE): low for pain reduction and mouth function; very low for quality of life.
- Brochado 2018 (n=51): MT, PBM, and MT+PBM all lowered VAS by day 14 (MT 4.4 to 1.7; p<0.001) with no between-group difference through day 90.
- Serrano-Hernanz 2023 (n=72): pressure release VAS 7.2 to 4.9 post-treatment and 4.4 at 3 months, significantly better than sham (p<0.001); sham change was below the 1.2 cm threshold.
- Romeo 2024 (n=62): adding physiotherapy to splint plus education gave OR 3.06 (VAS opening, p=0.04) and 6.89 (VAS chewing, p<0.01) for clinically important improvement.
- Espejo-Antunez 2024 (n=46 women): mobilization plus electro-massage beat mobilization alone by -1.59 cm post-treatment and -1.90 cm at 4 weeks, exceeding the MCID.
- Gurudut 2025 (n=34): MFR/massage vs 7 days of chlorzoxazone plus aceclofenac showed no significant between-group NPRS difference (p=0.066).
- Combination with electrotherapy plus MFR improved outcomes, whereas PBM plus MT did not add over MT alone (Brochado).
- Reading caveats: some table entries are internally inconsistent (for example, Ferreira arm sizes do not sum to 60; Leite age duplicates the Brochado row), so trial-level numbers are as printed in the paper.
