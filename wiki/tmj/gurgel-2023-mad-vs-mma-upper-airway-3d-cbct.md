## Three-line Summary

BMI-matched retrospective CBCT cohort (n=17 MAD vs n=17 MMA; T0 to T1 at 6–12 months; 3D upper airway segmentation with ITK-SNAP/3D Slicer) comparing upper airway volume changes from mandibular advancement device vs maxillomandibular advancement surgery.

Both treatments significantly increased superior oropharynx volume, but MMA showed greater gains and was the only treatment to significantly increase inferior oropharynx volume; MAD produced clockwise mandibular rotation (−3.97°) while MMA produced counterclockwise rotation (+2.40°, p<0.001); greater MAD advancement was inversely correlated with superior oropharynx gain.

MAD and MMA expand different airway regions due to opposite mandibular rotation directions; for patients requiring inferior oropharynx expansion (e.g. severe OSA), MMA offers benefits not achievable with MAD; MAD clockwise rotation increases vertical dimension with potential TMJ loading implications.

## 세줄요약

BMI 매칭된 후향적 CBCT 코호트 (MAD 17명 vs MMA 17명; T0→T1 6–12개월; ITK-SNAP/3D Slicer 3D 상기도 분절) — 하악전방이동장치(MAD) vs 상·하악 전방이동술(MMA)의 상기도 용적 변화 비교.

양 치료 모두 상부 구인두 용적 유의하게 증가(MMA > MAD); MMA만 하부 구인두 유의하게 증가; MAD는 시계방향 하악 회전(−3.97°), MMA는 반시계방향(+2.40°, p<0.001); MAD 전진량이 클수록 상부 구인두 증가량이 오히려 역상관.

MAD·MMA는 하악 회전 방향이 반대라 상기도 확장 영역이 다름; 하부 구인두 확장이 필요한 경우(예: 중증 OSA) MAD로 달성 불가; MAD 시계방향 회전은 수직고경 증가로 TMJ 부하에 영향 가능.

## Summary
This BMI-matched retrospective CBCT cohort compared 3D upper airway (UA) changes after mandibular advancement device (MAD, n=17) versus maxillomandibular advancement surgery (MMA, n=17). Both treatments significantly increased superior oropharynx volume, but MMA showed greater gains. Only MMA significantly increased inferior oropharynx volume. A key finding: MAD produces clockwise mandibular rotation (chin down/back, ~−4°) while MMA produces counterclockwise rotation (chin forward/up, ~+3°). In the MAD group, greater mandibular advancement correlated inversely with superior oropharynx gain but directly with inferior gain, suggesting an optimal advancement range.

## Key Contributions
- MAD vs MMA: opposite mandibular rotation directions → different airway regions benefited
- Both: superior oropharynx significantly enlarged; MMA > MAD
- MMA only: inferior oropharynx significantly enlarged
- MAD clockwise rotation: VD increases, potentially relevant for TMJ loading
- Greater MAD advancement ≠ more superior oropharynx gain (inverse correlation)

## Methodology
- Retrospective cohort; BMI/weight/height matched; CBCT at T0 and T1 (6–12 mo)
- UA segmentation: ITK-SNAP + 3D Slicer; standardized head orientation

## Results
