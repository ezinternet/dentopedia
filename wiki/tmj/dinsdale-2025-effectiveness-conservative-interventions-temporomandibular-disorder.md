## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis; 12 studies, 815 participants, mean age 42.2 y, 85% female, mostly myofascial/pain-related TMD) of non-pharmacological conservative interventions on kinesiophobia and pain catastrophizing.

Cognitive behavioural therapy, pain neuroscience education plus exercise, and manual therapy may reduce pain catastrophizing, and pain neuroscience education and manual therapy may improve kinesiophobia (all low to very low certainty); hypnosis and occlusal splinting appeared ineffective for catastrophizing (very low certainty).

Psychologically informed and education-based care look promising for the psychosocial layer of TMD, but certainty is low to very low, so the findings are hypothesis-generating.

## 세줄요약

성인 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD)에서 비약물 보존 치료가 운동공포 (Kinesiophobia)와 통증 파국화 (Pain Catastrophizing)에 미치는 효과를 본 체계적 문헌고찰 (메타분석 없음; 12편, 815명, 평균 42.2세, 여성 85%, 대부분 근막·통증형 TMD).

인지행동치료 (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, CBT), 통증 신경과학 교육 (Pain Neuroscience Education, PNE)+운동, 수기치료가 통증 파국화를, PNE와 수기치료가 운동공포를 줄일 수 있음 (확신도 낮음~매우 낮음); 최면과 교합안정장치는 파국화 감소에 효과 없어 보임 (매우 낮음).

TMD의 심리사회적 층위에는 교육·심리 기반 치료가 유망하나 근거 확신도가 낮아 가설 생성 수준이다.

## Summary

Source: PubMed abstract, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.msksp.2025.103328) (Musculoskelet Sci Pract 2025;77:103328). Pain catastrophizing and kinesiophobia contribute to TMD-related disability. The review searched PubMed, Embase, CINAHL and Cochrane Central for interventional studies of non-pharmacological conservative care in adults with diagnosed TMD, assessed risk of bias with Cochrane RoB 2, synthesized narratively, and graded certainty with a modified GRADE approach.

## Key Contributions

- Shifts the outcome from pain intensity to two psychosocial mediators, which earlier held TMD reviews rarely pooled.
- Ranks intervention classes on those mediators: CBT, PNE plus exercise and manual therapy are the signal carriers; hypnosis and occlusal splinting are not.

## Methodology

Adults with diagnosed TMD; interventional studies; non-pharmacological conservative interventions; RoB 2; narrative synthesis by outcome and intervention; modified GRADE. No meta-analysis.

## Results

- 12 studies, 815 participants (mean age 42.2 y, 85% female, mostly myofascial/pain-related TMD).
- Interventions: CBT, PNE +/- exercise, manual therapy, occlusal splinting, hypnosis.
- Pain catastrophizing: CBT, PNE plus exercise, manual therapy may reduce it (low to very low confidence).
- Kinesiophobia: PNE and manual therapy may improve it (low to very low confidence).
- Hypnosis and occlusal splinting: very low confidence that they are ineffective at reducing catastrophizing compared with other conservative interventions.
- Per-study effect sizes and RoB distribution are not available from the abstract.

## What this adds vs held pages

- [[tmj/ferland-2026-patient-education-self-management-tmd-sr-ma]] (47 RCTs, pain and HRQoL outcomes) does not address kinesiophobia or catastrophizing; this review adds the psychosocial-mediator axis, with a much smaller evidence base (12 studies) and no pooled estimate.
- [[tmj/demelo-2020-manual-therapy-myofascial-pain-tmd-sr]] judged manual therapy on pain only; here manual therapy also shows a possible effect on catastrophizing and kinesiophobia, which narrows its rationale rather than changing its pain conclusion.
- The splint finding is consistent in direction with [[tmj/cimino-2025-counselling-vs-splint-tmd-neck-pain-rct]] (splint added nothing beyond counselling), but a different outcome; not treated as independent confirmation.
- No supersession: no held page's bottom line is overturned.
