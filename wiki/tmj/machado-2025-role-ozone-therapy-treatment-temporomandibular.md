## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (6 databases, searched July 2021, updated November 2023; 315 records, 7 clinical trials included, no meta-analysis) of ozone therapy for articular and muscular temporomandibular disorders (TMD).

Ozone was reported to reduce pain intensity and palpatory pain and to improve pressure pain thresholds and mandibular range of motion; risk of bias (RoB 2) was low in 2 trials, some concerns in 2, and high in 3.

Ozone is a potential adjunct, but the evidence lacks robustness and needs further high-quality randomized controlled trials.

## 세줄요약

관절성·근육성 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에서 오존 치료를 평가한 체계적 문헌고찰 (6개 DB, 2021년 7월 검색·2023년 11월 갱신, 315편 중 임상시험 7편 포함, 메타분석 없음).

오존은 통증 강도·촉진통 감소, 압통역치 (Pressure Pain Threshold, PPT) 및 하악 운동범위 (Range of Motion, ROM) 개선을 보였고, 비뚤림위험 (RoB 2)은 저위험 2편, 일부 우려 2편, 고위험 3편이었음.

오존은 TMD 증상 완화의 잠재적 보조요법이나 근거가 탄탄하지 않아 고품질 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)이 더 필요함.

## Summary

Source: PubMed, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jebdp.2025.102127) (PMID 40716833), abstract level only. TMD affects around 12% of the population. This systematic review asked whether ozone therapy reduces TMD-related symptoms. Six databases were searched (July 15 2021, updated November 17 2023) for clinical trials in Portuguese, English, or Spanish; animal, retrospective, case-report, review, meta-analysis, and non-orofacial ozone studies were excluded. Of 315 articles, 7 were included; RoB 2 rated 2 as low risk, 2 as some concerns, 3 as high risk.

## Key Results

- Ozone applied to articular and muscular TMD was reported to reduce pain intensity and palpatory pain.
- Improved pressure pain thresholds and mandibular range of motion.
- No pooled effect sizes are given in the abstract; no meta-analysis.
- Authors conclude ozone "emerges as a potential therapy" but the evidence lacks robustness.

## Clinical Bottom Line

Ozone is at best an investigational adjunct for TMD: signals of benefit exist in 7 small trials, 3 at high risk of bias, so it should not displace conservative first-line care. Dose, route, and comparator details were not retrieved (abstract only).
