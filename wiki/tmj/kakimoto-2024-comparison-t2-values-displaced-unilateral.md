## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **결론/thesis**: 편측 원판 변위 (unilateral anterior disc displacement, uADD) 환자에서 후방조직 (retrodiscal tissue, 이중판대 (bilaminar zone) 포함)의 T2 이완시간이 상승하며, 증상이 없는 반대측 관절에서도 이미 상승해 있다 — "편측" 변위가 생화학적으로는 양측 병변임을 처음으로 정량 입증.
> - **핵심 수치 — 후방조직 T2 (ms)**: 정상지원자 34.4 ± 3.2 / uADD 반대측 37.8 ± 5.8 / uADD 환측 41.6 ± 7.1 (p < 0.001).
> - **비정복성이 거의 전부를 설명**: 비정복성 원판 변위 (ADDwoR) 환측 43.3 ± 6.8 ms로 최고치; 정복성 (ADDwR)은 정상과 큰 차이 없음(환측 35.8, 반대측 33.9).
> - **관절원판 자체는 덜 민감한 지표**: 원판 T2는 환측에서만 상승(31.5 vs 반대측 28.9), 반대측 효과 없음 — 무혈관·치밀 콜라겐이라 수분 변화에 둔감. 후방조직이 더 빠른 조기 바이오마커.
> - **원판 변위 단독은 약한 유발인자**: 관절 삼출·골관절염·골수부종 (JE/OA/BE)이 없는 하위군(n = 8)은 정상과 T2 차이 없음 → 동반 퇴행성 소견이 신호를 지배.
> - **기전 해석**: 후방조직은 혈관이 풍부하고 (highly vascularized) 쉽게 늘어나며 탄성을 잃어 (loses elasticity) 수분 함량 변화에 민감 → 원판 변위의 보상 기전으로 먼저 변하고, 이 변화가 원판 변화에 선행할 가능성.
> - **임상 takeaway**: uADD 환자에서 **반대측 관절을 간과하지 말 것**; T2 매핑은 통상 형태 MRI가 못 잡는 조기 생화학 변화를 포착. 다만 단면연구라 인과·시간 순서는 미확정.
> - **한계**: 1.5T (3.0T 권장), 연령·성별 불균형, 조직학적 대조 없음, JE/OA/BE 없는 하위군 소표본.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cross-sectional 1.5T MRI T2-mapping study of 65 unilateral anterior disc displacement (uADD) patients (15 with reduction, 50 without) vs 17 healthy volunteers, measuring T2 relaxation time of the articular disc and the retrodiscal tissue (bilaminar zone, superior attachment, inferior attachment ROIs). Retrodiscal-tissue T2 was elevated not only on the affected side (41.6 ± 7.1 ms) but on the clinically unaffected contralateral side (37.8 ± 5.8 ms) vs volunteers (34.4 ± 3.2 ms, p < 0.001), driven almost entirely by the without-reduction subgroup (affected 43.3 ms); the disc itself changed only on the affected side. A subgroup with displacement but no joint effusion / osteoarthritis / bone-marrow edema (n = 8) showed no T2 difference from volunteers — displacement alone is a weak driver and the concomitant degenerative MR findings dominate the biochemical signal.

## 세줄요약

편측 전방 원판 변위 (uADD) 환자 65명(정복성 15, 비정복성 50)과 정상지원자 17명을 1.5T MRI T2 매핑 (T2 mapping)으로 비교한 후향적 단면연구로, 관절원판과 후방조직 (retrodiscal tissue) — 이중판대 (bilaminar zone)·상방·하방 부착부 ROI — 의 T2 이완시간을 측정했다. 후방조직 T2는 환측(41.6 ± 7.1 ms)뿐 아니라 임상적으로 무증상인 반대측(37.8 ± 5.8 ms)에서도 정상지원자(34.4 ± 3.2 ms)보다 유의하게 길었으며(p < 0.001), 이는 거의 전적으로 비정복성 (without-reduction) 군에서 기인했다(환측 43.3 ms). 관절 삼출·골관절염·골수부종이 없는 원판 변위 하위군(n = 8)은 정상지원자와 T2 차이가 없어 — 원판 변위 단독은 약한 유발인자이며 동반 퇴행성 MRI 소견이 생화학적 신호를 지배한다.

## Summary

This study applies quantitative T2 mapping — an MR technique sensitive to tissue water content and collagen-fiber organization, and thus to early degeneration before morphology changes — to the retrodiscal tissue (the bilaminar zone and its superior/inferior attachments) in patients with unilateral anterior disc displacement. Its distinctive design is the within-patient comparison of the symptomatic joint against the same patient's clinically unaffected joint.

Two findings stand out. First, the retrodiscal tissue of the unaffected contralateral joint already carries an elevated T2 signal in without-reduction patients, quantitatively supporting the idea that the two TMJs function as a single mechanical unit and that so-called unilateral disc displacement is rarely biochemically confined to one side. Second, the retrodiscal tissue is a more sensitive early biomarker than the disc itself: the disc, being avascular and densely collagenous, changed only on the affected side, whereas the vascular, elastic, easily-stretched retrodisc responded bilaterally. A subgroup analysis restricted to patients with displacement but no joint effusion, osteoarthritis, or bone-marrow edema found no T2 elevation at all — implying that displacement per se is a weak degenerative driver and the accompanying inflammatory/degenerative MR findings are what the T2 signal mostly tracks.

## Key Contributions

1. **First within-patient (affected vs unaffected side) quantitative T2 comparison** of disc and retrodiscal tissue in unilateral disc displacement.
2. **Contralateral retrodiscal tissue shows elevated T2** in uADDWOR — biochemical evidence of bilateral involvement in nominally unilateral disease.
3. **Retrodiscal tissue > disc as an early biomarker** — the retrodisc registered a contralateral effect the disc did not; mechanistically attributed to its vascularity and elasticity loss.
4. **Displacement alone is a weak T2 driver** — no-JE/OA/BE subgroup indistinguishable from volunteers; degenerative co-findings dominate.
5. **Reduction status stratifies the signal** — uADDWOR markedly higher T2 than uADDWR across all tissues, extending the known ADDWOR–degeneration association to quantitative imaging.

## Methodology

- **Population**: 65 uADD patients (from 260 consecutive TMD patients, 2009–2016) — NorSup disc one side + ADDwR (n=15) or ADDwoR (n=50) the other; partial displacement excluded. 55 F / 10 M, median age 43. Controls: 17 volunteers (bilateral normal disc, 34 joints), median age 26.
- **Imaging**: 1.5T Signa HDxt (GE) + TMJ surface coil. T2 mapping: oblique sagittal FSE, closed mouth, 8 echo times (8.9–71.3 ms), 5:22 min/joint; T2 via Functool.
- **ROIs**: 1 disc + 3 retrodiscal (bilaminar zone, superior attachment, inferior attachment), placed by 2 blinded oral-maxillofacial radiologists (20 y / 12 y). Retrodiscal reliability: intra-rater CV 1.5–5.5%, inter-rater ICC 0.818.
- **MR feature grading**: disc position (Tasaki), joint effusion (Larheim), osteoarthritis (Kirk), bone-marrow edema/osteonecrosis (Larheim).
- **Statistics**: linear regression with GEE (within-patient correlation); multivariable adjustment for age and sex; p < 0.05.

## Results

### Retrodiscal tissue T2 (ms) — the headline measure

| Group | Volunteers | Unaffected side | Affected side |
|---|---|---|---|
| All uADD (n=65) | 34.4 ± 3.2 | 37.8 ± 5.8 (p<0.001 vs healthy) | 41.6 ± 7.1 (p<0.001 vs unaffected & healthy) |
| uADDWR (n=15) | 34.4 ± 3.2 | 33.9 ± 3.8 | 35.8 ± 4.4 |
| uADDWOR (n=50) | 34.4 ± 3.2 | 38.9 ± 5.8 (p<0.001 vs healthy) | 43.3 ± 6.8 (p<0.001 vs every other group) |
| Displacement, no JE/OA/BE (n=8) | 34.4 ± 3.2 | 35.5 ± 3.9 | 34.9 ± 4.5 (ns) |

### Articular disc T2 (ms)

| Group | Volunteers | Unaffected side | Affected side |
|---|---|---|---|
| All uADD (n=65) | 29.3 ± 3.8 | 28.9 ± 4.5 | 31.5 ± 3.8 (p<0.001 vs unaffected) |
| uADDWOR (n=50) | 29.3 ± 3.8 | 29.0 ± 4.1 | 32.0 ± 4.4 (p<0.001 vs unaffected) |
| Displacement, no JE/OA/BE (n=8) | 29.3 ± 3.8 | 29.5 ± 6.1 | 29.3 ± 2.6 (ns) |

- Age/sex adjustment preserved the affected-vs-unaffected retrodiscal difference; some unaffected-vs-volunteer comparisons lost significance, but the core uADDWOR results held.
- Interpretation: longer T2 = more mobile water + disrupted collagen alignment = degeneration; ADDWOR consistently the most degenerate.
