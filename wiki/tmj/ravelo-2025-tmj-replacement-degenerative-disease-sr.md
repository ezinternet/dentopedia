## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (Cochrane Handbook methods, PRISMA reporting; PubMed/Embase/Scopus/Web of Science + gray literature, 1997–July 2024) of total TMJ replacement in 579 participants (of 760 total analyzed) with degenerative and/or inflammatory joint disease — osteoarthritis most frequent, followed by osteoarthrosis, juvenile idiopathic arthritis, and rheumatoid arthritis; ROBINS-I used for risk of bias across the 16 non-randomised included studies, GRADE for certainty of evidence.

Preoperative maximum mouth opening averaged 24.32 ± 5.8 mm with 76.18% of participants reporting moderate-to-severe pain and a soft/liquid diet; postoperatively all participants reported reduced or absent pain, progression to a solid diet, and mouth opening improved to 40.74 ± 3.1 mm; prosthesis replacement due to system failure occurred in only 4.07%. These are uncontrolled before-after outcomes — no included study had a control group or blinding, and all 16 were GRADE-rated low-quality evidence, so the improvement cannot be attributed to the prosthesis with the confidence the raw numbers imply.

The review's most clinically useful finding is a negative one: it was not possible to identify the best timing for joint replacement — not by time since diagnosis, not by time since starting non-surgical treatment, not by number of previous surgeries — leaving the question a clinician actually faces ("when do we stop conserving and operate?") unanswered by the current evidence base.

## 세줄요약

코크란 핸드북(Cochrane Handbook) 방법론과 PRISMA 보고 기준을 따른 체계적 문헌고찰(PubMed·Embase·Scopus·Web of Science + 회색문헌, 1997~2024년 7월)로, 퇴행성 및/또는 염증성 관절질환 환자 579명(전체 분석대상 760명 중)의 전악관절치환술(Total TMJ Replacement, TJR)을 다룸 — 진단은 골관절염(Osteoarthritis)이 가장 흔했고 골관절증(Osteoarthrosis), 소아특발성관절염(Juvenile Idiopathic Arthritis, JIA), 류마티스관절염(Rheumatoid Arthritis, RA) 순; 비무작위 연구 16편의 비뚤림위험(Risk of Bias)은 ROBINS-I, 근거등급(Certainty of Evidence)은 GRADE로 평가.

수술 전 최대 개구량(Maximum Mouth Opening)은 평균 24.32 ± 5.8 mm였고 76.18%가 중등도~중증 통증과 연식/유동식 식이를 보고했으며, 수술 후 전원이 통증 감소·소실과 고형식 회복을 보고했고 개구량은 40.74 ± 3.1 mm로 개선; 시스템 실패로 인한 보철물 재치환은 4.07%에 불과. 다만 이는 대조군 없는 전후비교(Before-After) 결과이며 포함된 16편 전부가 대조군·눈가림 없이 GRADE 낮은 근거등급으로 평가되어, 수치가 시사하는 만큼의 확신으로 개선을 보철물 자체에 귀속시킬 수 없음.

이 리뷰의 가장 유용한 정직한 소견은 음성(negative) 결과다 — 진단 후 경과시간, 비수술적 치료 시작 후 경과시간, 이전 수술 횟수 중 어느 기준으로도 관절치환술의 최적 시행 시점을 특정할 수 없었으며, "언제 보존적 치료를 멈추고 수술해야 하는가"라는 임상의의 실제 질문에 현재 근거는 답하지 못함.

## Summary

According to PubMed, this systematic review ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm14020580); PMID 39860584; PMCID PMC11765714) synthesizes 16 studies (760 participants total) on total TMJ replacement in participants with degenerative and/or inflammatory joint disease of the temporomandibular joint. Conducted per the Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions and reported per PRISMA, the review searched PubMed, Embase, Scopus, and Web of Science from 1997 (chosen because U.S. regulation began requiring TMJ-implant manufacturers to submit premarket safety/efficacy data that year) through July 2024, plus gray literature (Google Scholar, Open Access Theses and Dissertations). Of 579 participants with degenerative and/or inflammatory joint disease specifically (osteoarthritis most frequent, then osteoarthrosis, juvenile idiopathic arthritis, and rheumatoid arthritis), preoperative maximum mouth opening averaged 24.32 ± 5.8 mm (range 18–36.4 mm) and 76.18% presented moderate-to-severe pain with a soft-to-liquid diet. Postoperatively, all participants reported reduced or absent pain, progression to a solid-food diet, and mouth opening improved to 40.74 ± 3.1 mm; only 4.07% of the full 760-participant cohort required prosthesis replacement for system failure.

This wiki's only prior page on total TMJ replacement, [[tmj/guzman-2025-tmj-replacement-customized-prostheses]], is a single-institution n=6 case series with no comparator. This systematic review sits above it in the evidence hierarchy — 16 studies, formal ROBINS-I risk-of-bias assessment, and GRADE certainty rating — and is the page a reader should consult first for the population-level evidence on TJR outcomes, with the case series supplying granular single-center technique and complication detail underneath it.

## Key Contributions

- Synthesizes 16 studies (760 participants total) on total TMJ replacement, the largest and highest-tier (SR with ROBINS-I + GRADE) evidence base in this wiki for alloplastic TJR, above the existing single-center case series [[tmj/guzman-2025-tmj-replacement-customized-prostheses]].
- Quantifies indication-stage severity (mean preop opening 24.32 ± 5.8 mm; 76.18% moderate-to-severe pain, soft/liquid diet) and postoperative outcome (opening 40.74 ± 3.1 mm, universal pain reduction/resolution, diet progression to solids) across a pooled 579-participant subgroup.
- Reports the prosthesis system-failure/replacement rate as low (4.07% of 760), consistent with prior TJR-survival literature (~97% survival, per Lima et al. cited within the source article).
- States a clear negative finding with direct clinical relevance: no reliable predictor of optimal replacement timing (time since diagnosis, time since non-surgical treatment start, or number of prior surgeries) could be identified from the pooled evidence.
- Names juvenile idiopathic arthritis and rheumatoid arthritis among the TJR indications, linking the surgical end-stage to the wiki's inflammatory-arthritis TMJ pages rather than only the osteoarthritis literature.
- Runs and reports a formal ROBINS-I risk-of-bias assessment and GRADE certainty rating for all 16 included non-randomised studies — all rated **low certainty of evidence**, a methodological transparency the underlying case-series literature (including [[tmj/guzman-2025-tmj-replacement-customized-prostheses]]) does not itself provide.

## Methodology

Systematic review per the Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions, reported per updated PRISMA items; PROSPERO registration 602516. PICO: P = participants >15y with degenerative and/or inflammatory TMJ disease; I = total TMJ replacement with prostheses; C = indications and use of TMJ prostheses; O = clinical outcomes ≥12 months post-installation.

Search covered PubMed, Embase, Scopus, and Web of Science (1997–July 2024) plus gray literature (Google Scholar, OATD), no language or study-design restriction. Two independent reviewers (Kappa 0.617), third reviewer for discrepancy resolution; missing data requested from authors (six-week window). Inclusion required human patients >15y, degenerative/inflammatory TMJ disease (≥50% of sample if mixed), joint prosthesis replacement (± concurrent orthognathic surgery), and ≥12-month postoperative follow-up; exclusion for sample <10, animal/cadaver studies, or no full text.

Risk of bias was assessed with the **ROBINS-I** tool (7 domains: confounding, participant selection, exposure measurement, post-exposure interventions, missing data, outcome measurement, outcome reporting) for all 16 non-randomised included studies. Certainty of evidence was assessed with **GRADE** (GRADEpro v7.11.0) per Cochrane/GRADE Working Group criteria. A Cochran Q test found significant heterogeneity across included studies (p = 0.001), which — combined with inconsistent outcome reporting/measurement — precluded meta-analysis; the review is a narrative synthesis only.

## Results

Screening: 687 identified → 242 duplicates removed → 445 title/abstract screened → 23 full-text reviewed → 16 included (7 excluded: 4 not full text, 2 <12-month follow-up, 1 not using total TMJ prostheses as definitive treatment); gray literature 665 screened, 5 full-text reviewed, 0 included.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total participants (all 16 studies) | 760 (78 male, 682 female; mean age 39.7 ± 9.16y) |
| Follow-up range | 12–120 months (10 studies at 2–5y, 4 studies at 5–10y) |
| Prostheses fitted | 1231 total (874 customized, 357 stock); 828 bilateral, 308 unilateral |
| Degenerative/inflammatory disease subgroup | 579/760 (76.18%) — osteoarthritis > osteoarthrosis > JIA > rheumatoid arthritis |
| Preop maximum mouth opening | 24.32 ± 5.8 mm (range 18–36.4 mm) |
| Preop pain distribution (n=579) | 574 moderate–severe, 123 moderate, 21 mild–moderate |
| Prior treatment before TJR (of 619 reporting) | 327 (52.82%) prior surgery; 39 drug-only conservative; 210 (33.92%) no prior treatment |
| System-failure prosthesis replacement | 31/760 (4.07%) |
| Postop mouth opening | 40.74 ± 3.1 mm |
| Postop pain/diet | All participants: reduced/absent pain; diet progressed to solids |
| Orthognathic co-surgery | 7/16 studies, 186 participants (24.47% of sample); 91.65% of these bilateral |
| ROBINS-I overall | 3 studies moderate risk, 2 studies high risk (rest by domain: e.g. 12/16 moderate confounding risk) |
| GRADE certainty | **Low**, all 16 studies (≥1 high-risk domain each) |
| Heterogeneity (Cochran Q) | p = 0.001 — significant; meta-analysis not performed |

Long-term follow-up (>5y) showed only 0.5–1 mm further opening decrease (2–5 mm at ≤5y), neither considered clinically significant; no study reported joint changes or revision need at long-term follow-up. **Design caveat**: every quantitative gain above is a within-group before-after comparison — no included study had a control/comparator arm or blinding of investigators, surgeons, or assessors, and all 16 studies were GRADE-low. **Headline negative finding**: it was not possible to identify the best timing for joint replacement surgery based on time since diagnosis, time since starting non-surgical treatment, or number of previous surgeries.
