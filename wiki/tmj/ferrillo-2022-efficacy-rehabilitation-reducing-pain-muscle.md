## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42021251904; searched to 28 April 2021) of 16 sham- or placebo-controlled RCTs of rehabilitative interventions for painful muscle-related temporomandibular disorders (TMD), pain on a visual analogue scale.

Pooled overall effect size for pain reduction was 1.44 (p<0.0001); laser therapy was the most studied intervention (6 of 16 RCTs, 37.5%).

Rehabilitation might be effective for muscle-related TMD pain, but few RCTs per technique limit technique-specific conclusions; the authors call for caution.

## 세줄요약

근육성 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 통증에 대한 재활 중재를 위약·가짜치료와 비교한 16개 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (PROSPERO CRD42021251904).

통증 감소의 통합 효과크기 (Effect Size, ES)는 1.44 (p<0.0001)였고, 레이저 치료가 16편 중 6편 (37.5%)으로 가장 많이 연구됨.

재활 접근이 근육성 TMD 통증 감소에 효과적일 수 있으나 기법별 RCT 수가 적어 기법별 결론은 제한적이며 해석에 주의가 필요함.

## Summary

Source: PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3233/BMR-210236)), abstract level only. The authors searched PubMed, Scopus and Web of Science from inception to 28 April 2021 for RCTs in patients with painful muscle-related TMD that compared rehabilitative interventions with placebo or sham and reported pain intensity on a visual analogue scale. Of 1997 records screened, 189 full texts were assessed and 16 RCTs were included. The meta-analysis gave an overall effect size of 1.44 (p<0.0001) favoring rehabilitation for pain reduction. Laser therapy was the most frequently studied intervention (6 of 16 RCTs). The authors conclude rehabilitation might be effective but that the small number of RCTs per technique limits synthesis.

## Key Contributions

- Pooled, sham/placebo-controlled estimate for rehabilitation in muscle-related TMD pain (ES 1.44).
- Shows the RCT base is dominated by laser therapy (37.5%).

## Methodology

PROSPERO CRD42021251904. Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science (to 28 April 2021). Population: painful muscle-related TMD. Intervention: rehabilitative approaches. Comparator: placebo or sham. Outcome: pain intensity (VAS). Heterogeneity, risk-of-bias tool and certainty grading are not stated in the abstract.

## Results

| Item | Value |

## What this adds versus the closest held pages

- [[tmj/demelo-2020-manual-therapy-myofascial-pain-tmd-sr]]: that SR (5 RCTs, manual therapy only, no meta-analysis) found MT not superior to counseling or botulinum toxin. This paper differs in design: any rehabilitative approach, sham/placebo comparators only, and a pooled ES. It answers "does rehabilitation beat sham", not "which active treatment is best".
- [[tmj/calixtre-2015-manual-therapy-tmd-pain-rom-sr]]: technique-stratified, GRADE-rated, no pooling; this paper pools across modalities and has no technique-level certainty in the abstract.
- [[tmj/gonzalez-sanchez-2023-tmj-physiotherapy-treatment-sr]]: narrative conclusion that all physiotherapy modalities reduce pain; this paper supplies a pooled number pointing the same direction.

No supersession: this abstract-only paper does not overturn any held page, and its technique-level evidence is thinner than the held technique-stratified SRs.
