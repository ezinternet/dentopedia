## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=109, 89.9% female) of DC/TMD unilateral TMJ arthralgia patients stratified by CBCT condylar subchondral bone alteration (SBA) grade (mild/moderate/severe) and treated with 3 mm resilient splint for 6 months, with JFLS-20 as outcome.

All SBA groups improved significantly (JFLS-sum Δ≈9, p<0.001), but severe SBA patients had worse masticatory outcomes than mild SBA patients after covariate adjustment (JFLS-M p=0.027; JFLS-sum p=0.002 mild vs severe).

CBCT-based SBA grading may identify arthralgia patients with limited splint response who warrant earlier escalation to arthrocentesis/lavage, but the study lacks a control group and psychosocial covariate control.

## Summary

This exploratory retrospective cohort study (n=109) evaluated whether CBCT-graded condylar subchondral bone alteration (SBA) severity predicts 6-month functional outcomes of 3 mm resilient occlusal splint therapy in patients with DC/TMD-diagnosed unilateral TMJ arthralgia. Baseline SBA grade (mild/moderate/severe) did not differ across groups in age, sex, pain intensity, or pain duration. All groups showed significant within-group improvement in JFLS scores and VAS pain at 6 months (p<0.001 throughout). However, after ANCOVA adjusting for baseline covariates, patients with severe condylar SBAs had significantly worse masticatory limitation improvement (JFLS-M, p=0.027) and overall jaw function sum (p=0.002) compared with mild SBA patients. This is the first study to apply a CBCT-based SBA scoring system to predict splint therapy outcomes in TMJ arthralgia.

## Key Contributions

- Establishes CBCT SBA grade as a prognostic variable for occlusal splint therapy response in TMJ arthralgia
- Provides between-group JFLS data: mild SBA achieves JFLS-M improvement of Δ14.7 vs severe SBA Δ8.6 over 6 months
- Identifies a rationale for earlier treatment escalation (e.g., arthrocentesis/lavage) in high-SBA-grade patients who show limited functional improvement at 6 months
- First application of a 4-category CBCT SBA scoring system to functional prognosis in splint therapy context

## Methodology

- **Population**: 109 adults (98F, 11M; mean age 44.3 ± 16.6 yr; range 18–67 yr) with DC/TMD unilateral TMJ arthralgia
- **Intervention**: 3 mm resilient occlusal stabilization splint, 6-month follow-up
- **CBCT classification**: SBA subtypes documented (erosion 88.1%, flattening 55.1%, sclerosis 50.5%, osteophyte 44.0%, resorption 28.4%, cyst 14.7%); collapsed into three severity groups: mild/grade 1 (n=36), moderate/grade 2 (n=42), severe/grade 3–4 (n=31)
- **Outcomes**: JFLS-20 subscales (Mastication, Vertical Jaw Mobility, Verbal/Emotional Expression, Sum); VAS pain (100 mm)
- **Statistics**: Paired t-test (intragroup); ANCOVA adjusting for age, sex, pain intensity, pain duration (intergroup)
- **Limitations**: No control/placebo arm; psychosocial factors (depression, anxiety) not measured; exploratory design

## Results

**Pain reduction** (all groups, 6-month VAS, p<0.001): mild −27.4 mm; moderate −25.1 mm; severe −23.3 mm

**JFLS change (T0→T1), total cohort** (all p<0.001):
| Subscale | T0 | T1 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| JFLS-M (Mastication) | 23.0 ± 11.1 | 10.3 ± 12.1 | −12.6 |
| JFLS-JM (Mobility) | 15.3 ± 7.0 | 8.0 ± 7.6 | −7.3 |
| JFLS-VEE (Verbal/Emotion) | 17.6 ± 13.9 | 10.0 ± 12.4 | −7.5 |
| JFLS-Sum | 18.6 ± 11.5 | 9.5 ± 11.0 | −9.2 |

**JFLS-M by SBA group (T0→T1)**:
- Mild: 25.3→10.6 (Δ14.7)
- Moderate: 25.6→11.8 (Δ13.8)
- Severe: 16.7→8.0 (Δ8.6)

**ANCOVA between-group** (primary finding):
- Severe vs mild SBA: JFLS-M p=0.027 (significantly worse)
- JFLS-sum: mild vs moderate p=0.011; mild vs severe p=0.002; moderate vs severe p>0.05 (NS)
