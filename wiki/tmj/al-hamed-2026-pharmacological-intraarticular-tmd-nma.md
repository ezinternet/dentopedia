## Three-line Summary

SR + NMA (7 databases; 23 RCTs, 1264 patients; Cochrane RoB 2.0; CINeMA certainty) comparing pharmacological treatments — alone or adjunctive — for painful intra-articular TMD (disc displacement and osteoarthritis subgroups analyzed separately).

For TMJ disc displacement: MESNA and hyaluronate (HA) arthrocentesis showed short- and long-term pain significance; for TMJ osteoarthritis: palmitoylethanolamide and avocado-soybean extract (short-term) and glucosamine+HA (long-term pain and mouth opening) were most effective — all low to very low certainty.

No pharmacological treatment can be recommended with confidence for intra-articular TMD; most effect estimates fall below the minimal clinically important difference, and high-quality standardized RCTs remain urgently needed.

## 세줄요약

SR+NMA (7 DB; 23 RCT, 1264명; Cochrane RoB 2.0; CINeMA) — 통증성 관절내 악관절증 (Intra-articular Temporomandibular Disorder, iTMD): 원판 변위 (Disc Displacement, DD)와 악관절 골관절염 (TMJ Osteoarthritis, OA) 소그룹별 약물치료 비교 네트워크 메타분석.

TMJ DD: 단기 여러 약제 통증 유의 감소; MESNA (Sodium 2-mercaptoethanesulfonate)·히알루론산 (Hyaluronate, HA) 관절강세척은 장기(>3개월)에서도 유의. TMJ OA: 팔미토일에탄올아미드 (Palmitoylethanolamide, PEA)·아보카도-콩 불비누화물 단기 통증; 글루코사민 (Glucosamine)+HA 장기 통증·최대개구량 (Maximum Mouth Opening, MMO) 개선 — 전반 근거 확실성 매우 낮음~낮음.

전체 결론: 어떤 약물치료도 신뢰할 수 있게 권고 불가 — 추정치 대부분이 최소 임상 중요차 (Minimal Clinically Important Difference, MCID) 이하; 표준화된 고질 RCT가 시급.

## Summary

This network meta-analysis (Al-Hamed 2026) is the most comprehensive pharmacological treatment NMA for intra-articular TMDs, including 23 RCTs and 1264 patients. It separately analyzed disc displacement (DD) and osteoarthritis (OA) subgroups. For TMJ disc displacement, multiple interventions reduced short-term pain, with MESNA and HA arthrocentesis maintaining effects beyond 3 months. For TMJ OA, palmitoylethanolamide and avocado-soybean unsaponifiables reduced short-term pain, while glucosamine HCl/sulfate combined with HA was the only treatment showing significant long-term effects on both pain and mouth opening. Despite these findings, all certainty ratings were low to very low, and no effect estimate reliably exceeded the MCID threshold. The authors conclude that no pharmacological agent can be confidently recommended, and treatment decisions should remain guided by clinical judgment until higher-quality evidence is available.

## Key Contributions

- Largest NMA (23 RCTs, 1264 patients) specifically targeting intra-articular TMD (DD + OA separately)
- First NMA to stratify pharmacological evidence by TMJ pathological subtype (DD vs OA)
- Identifies MESNA + HA arthrocentesis as potentially effective for long-term disc displacement pain
- Identifies glucosamine + HA as only long-term OA intervention for both pain and mouth opening
- Uniformly low to very low certainty — explicitly calls for standardized RCTs

## Methodology

- **Design**: Frequentist network meta-analysis (NMA)
- **Databases**: 7 databases (inception to publication)
- **Population**: Adults with painful intra-articular TMD — disc displacement and osteoarthritis
- **Interventions**: Pharmacological agents (alone or adjunctive to arthrocentesis/other procedures): MESNA, HA, palmitoylethanolamide, avocado-soybean unsaponifiables, glucosamine HCl, glucosamine sulfate, others
- **Outcomes**: Pain intensity (VAS/NRS), maximum mouth opening (mm)
- **Time**: Short-term ≤3 months, long-term >3 months
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB 2.0
- **Certainty**: CINeMA approach
- **n**: 23 RCTs, 1264 patients
