## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis of 19 human studies (851 records screened) comparing genetic factors between TMD and non-TMD patients, appraised with the JBI checklist (Arch Oral Biol 2024).

Polymorphisms and gene differences were significantly associated with TMD signs and symptoms (myofascial pain, chronic pain, disc displacement); pooled OR for gene polymorphism and TMD development was 2.46 (95% CI 1.93-3.14, p = 0.00001).

Suggests heritable susceptibility contributes to TMD, but the authors call for larger, more diverse samples; this page is built from the PubMed abstract only.

## 세줄요약

851편 중 19편의 인체 연구를 포함해 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 환자와 비환자의 유전 요인을 비교한 메타분석 포함 체계적 문헌고찰 (Arch Oral Biol 2024).

유전자 다형성 (Gene Polymorphism)과 유전자 차이는 근막통·만성통증·관절원판 변위와 유의하게 연관; 다형성과 TMD 발생의 통합 오즈비 (Odds Ratio, OR) 2.46 (95% CI 1.93-3.14, p = 0.00001).

TMD에 유전적 감수성이 기여함을 시사하나 저자들도 더 큰 표본·다양한 집단 검증을 요구; 본 페이지는 PubMed 초록만 근거.

## Summary
According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.archoralbio.2024.106032)), this review searched ScienceDirect, PubMed, Cochrane Library, Dimensions, and Emerald (851 records, 19 included) for human studies comparing genetic factors between TMD and non-TMD participants, using modified PICO criteria and the JBI checklist. Genetic polymorphisms and gene differences significantly influenced TMD signs and symptoms, and polymorphisms influenced TMD onset and progression.

## Key Contributions
- Only genetics-focused TMD synthesis in the wiki; gives a pooled polymorphism OR of 2.46 (1.93-3.14).
- Links genetic factors to myofascial pain, chronic pain, and disc displacement phenotypes.

## Methodology
Five databases; one reviewer selected studies (per abstract); JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist for Non-randomized Experimental Investigations. Heterogeneity, specific genes, and ancestry are not stated in the abstract.

## Results
19 studies included. Significant genetic influence on TMD signs/symptoms; gene polymorphism and TMD development OR 2.46 (1.93-3.14), p = 0.00001. Per-gene estimates not available from the abstract.

## Clinical Notes
A pooled OR of this size indicates association, not a clinically usable predictor; it does not justify genetic testing in practice. It adds a susceptibility layer to the biopsychosocial aetiology described in the general review below. Treatment-page conclusions (splints, manual therapy, botulinum toxin) are unaffected.
