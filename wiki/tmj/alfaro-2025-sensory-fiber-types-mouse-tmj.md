## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **결론/thesis**: 마우스 TMJ 각 조직의 감각신경 섬유 아형 (sensory fiber subtype)을 처음으로 체계적으로 지도화한 연구로, 후방조직 (retrodisc)의 고유한 신경 구성이 통증의 1차 발생원인임을 분자 수준에서 확인했다.
> - **관절원판 (articular disc)은 신경이 없다**: pgp9.5 음성 → 원판 변위 자체는 직접적 통증원이 아님; 임상에서 원판 위치 회복이 통증 치료와 독립적일 수 있는 근거.
> - **관절낭 (joint capsule) 및 전방원판 (anterior disc)**: 펩타이드성 (peptidergic) 통각수용기 (nociceptor) CGRP+ 99% 지배, Htr3a+ 90~100% — 균질한 통각 조성.
> - **후방조직 (retrodisc)의 독특한 구성**: CGRP+ 75%, 비펩타이드성 (non-peptidergic) 통각수용기 MrgprD/A3/C11이 ~20% — 타 조직 대비 현저히 높아 다양한 통증 자극에 반응 가능.
> - **저역치기계수용기 (A-LTMR, low-threshold mechanoreceptor) 전무**: 모든 TMJ 조직에서 parvalbumin+ 및 NFH+CGRP− 섬유 부재 → TMJ의 기계적 자극은 고유감각 (proprioception)이 아닌 통각으로 처리됨.
> - **혈관 주변 신경**: 교감신경 (TH+ ~40%) + 감각신경 (pgp9.5+ ~35%) 이중 지배 → 신경성 염증 (neurogenic inflammation)과 혈관 조절에 관여.
> - **임상 takeaway**: 관절원판 위치(변위 여부)보다 후방조직의 염증·신경 민감화 (sensitization)가 TMD 통증의 직접 기전; CGRP 경로 및 MrgprD/A3/C11 경로가 치료 표적 후보.
> - **한계**: 마우스 모델 — 인간 TMJ와의 직접 외삽 주의; 성별 편향(일부 리포터 계통이 암컷·수컷 한쪽만 사용), 2D IHC의 3D 구조 미포착.

## Three-line Summary

The articular disc is completely devoid of nerve fibers (pgp9.5 negative), confirming that disc displacement per se is not a direct pain generator. The retrodisc is uniquely innervated with ~20% non-peptidergic nociceptors (MrgprD, MrgprA3, MrgprC11) absent elsewhere, superimposed on a 75% CGRP+ peptidergic background — making it the most nociceptively diverse TMJ structure. The complete absence of low-threshold mechanoreceptors (A-LTMRs, PV+ fibers) across all TMJ tissues means that mechanical stimulation of the joint is processed as nociception, not proprioception.

## 세줄요약

관절원판 (articular disc)에는 신경이 전혀 존재하지 않아, 원판 변위 자체가 직접적 통증원이 아님을 확인했다. 후방조직 (retrodisc)에만 비펩타이드성 통각수용기 (non-peptidergic nociceptor) — MrgprD, MrgprA3, MrgprC11 — 이 ~20% 존재하며, 관절낭·전방원판에는 없는 고유한 특성이다. 모든 TMJ 조직에서 저역치기계수용기 (A-LTMR, low-threshold mechanoreceptor)가 부재하여, TMJ에 가해지는 기계적 자극은 고유감각이 아닌 통각으로 처리된다.

## Summary

This 2025 study from UTHSCSA provides the first tissue-resolved map of sensory nerve fiber subtypes in the mouse temporomandibular joint (TMJ), covering the joint capsule, anterior disc, and retrodiscal tissue using immunohistochemistry (IHC) and multiple transgenic reporter mouse lines. The central finding is that each TMJ compartment has a distinct neurochemical identity, with the retrodisc uniquely harboring non-peptidergic nociceptors that are absent everywhere else in the joint.

The work resolves a longstanding question in TMD biology: if the articular disc itself is nerve-free, why does disc displacement cause pain? The answer lies downstream — in the retrodisc, which undergoes compression and inflammation when the disc is anteriorly displaced, and which is the most nociceptively heterogeneous structure in the joint.

## Key Contributions

1. **First comprehensive fiber subtype map per TMJ compartment** — no prior study resolved sensory fiber identity at the level of joint capsule vs. anterior disc vs. retrodisc.
2. **Articular disc is nerve-free** (pgp9.5 negative) — pain in disc displacement TMD is not generated by the disc itself; this has direct implications for treatment rationale.
3. **Retrodisc uniquely harbors ~20% non-peptidergic nociceptors** (MrgprD/A3/C11) — these are entirely absent from the joint capsule and anterior disc.
4. **Absence of A-LTMRs across all TMJ tissues** — no parvalbumin+ fibers, no NFH+CGRP− myelinated mechanoreceptors; the joint lacks non-nociceptive mechanosensation.
5. **Htr3a+ coverage differs markedly**: joint capsule/anterior disc ~99%, retrodisc only ~47% — reflecting serotonin receptor-modulated nociceptive heterogeneity in the retrodisc.
6. **Perivascular dual innervation**: blood vessels are associated with both sympathetic (TH+, ~40%) and sensory (pgp9.5+, ~35%) fibers, implicating autonomic co-regulation of neurogenic inflammation.

## Methodology

- **Model**: Adult C57Bl/6 mice + transgenic reporter lines (CGRP-cre, MrgprD-GFP, MrgprA3-cre, MrgprC11-cre, Htr3a-cre, Sst-cre, PV-cre, each crossed to tdTomato or GFP reporters)
- **Tissue**: Cryosections at 20 μm; joint capsule, anterior disc, and retrodiscal tissue dissected separately
- **IHC markers**: pgp9.5 (pan-neuronal), NFH (myelinated), CGRP (peptidergic), TH (sympathetic), α-SMA (vascular), MrgprD/A3/C11 (non-peptidergic nociceptors), Htr3a (5-HT3a receptor), somatostatin (SST), parvalbumin (PV)
- **Quantification**: Manual fiber counting from ≥3 sections per condition
- **Statistics**: 2-way ANOVA + Bonferroni post-hoc; p<0.05

## Results

### Fiber Distribution Across TMJ Tissues

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Any nerve (pgp9.5+) | + | + | + | **NONE** |
| Myelinated (NFH+) | 28.1 ± 7.6% | 30.3 ± 6.1% | 30.2 ± 12% | — |
| CGRP+ peptidergic | 95.9% | 98.9% | 75.4 ± 9.9% | — |
| Htr3a+ | 99.9% | 90.3% | 46.6 ± 5.5% | — |
| A-LTMR (NFH+CGRP−) | absent | absent | absent | — |
| Parvalbumin (PV+) | absent | absent | absent | — |
| MrgprD+ | <5% | <5% | ~10.8% | — |
| Mrgpr+ (D+A3+C11 combined) | <5% | <5% | ~20% | — |
| Somatostatin (SST+) | <5% | <5% | <5% | — |

| pgp9.5+ perivascular (sensory) | ~35% of vessels | ~35% | ~35% | — |

### Key Interpretive Points

- **Why disc displacement → pain**: The disc itself is silent (no nerves). Displacement compresses the retrodisc, which is richly and diversely innervated. Pain is retrodiscal, not discal.
- **Why retrodisc is most pain-sensitive**: Two nociceptor populations coexist — peptidergic (CGRP+, ~75%) responding to inflammatory mediators like substance P/CGRP, and non-peptidergic (~20%) responding to stimuli like pruritogens (MrgprA3), mechanical damage (MrgprD), and proteases (MrgprC11). No other TMJ compartment has this dual-population architecture.
- **Why mechanical loading hurts**: Complete absence of A-LTMRs (low-threshold touch/pressure receptors) means there is no "quiet" mechanosensation in the TMJ — any mechanical input is routed through nociceptive C-fiber and Aδ-fiber pathways.
- **Therapeutic implications**: CGRP-pathway modulation (e.g., anti-CGRP biologics used in migraine) and MrgprD/A3/C11 targeting are biologically plausible for TMD pain; absence of A-LTMRs means proprioceptive retraining exercises may work through central rather than peripheral mechanisms.
