## Three-line Summary
Retrospective clinical case series, n=6 (all female, mean age 56.5y), severe TMJ osteoarthritis (n=5) or condylar osteochondroma (n=1) treated with unilateral total TMJ replacement using CBCT/MRI-planned customized prostheses (ARTFIX Implants, CPMH Digital).

At ≥24-month follow-up, median jaw function improved 6.5→3.5, dietary intake 8.0→1.5, pain (VAS) 7→2; postoperative interincisal mouth opening averaged 32-33 mm.

One patient required revision surgery at 6 months for condylar screw displacement without long-term functional compromise; small sample size and lack of documented preoperative mouth-opening values limit generalizability.

## Summary
This retrospective case series from the Maxillofacial Surgery Department of Hospital Militar de Santiago (Chile) reports functional outcomes for six female patients (mean age 56.5±13.28y) who underwent unilateral total temporomandibular joint (TMJ) replacement (TJR) with customized, CBCT/MRI-planned prostheses (ARTFIX Implants or CPMH Digital) between 2018-2024. Indications were severe osteoarthritis (n=5) and condylar osteochondroma (n=1); half the patients had a history of prior TMJ surgery. TJR represents the end-stage surgical option in the TMJ treatment ladder — used when conservative management (splints, pharmacotherapy) and minimally invasive procedures (arthrocentesis, arthroscopy) have failed or are not applicable to advanced structural joint destruction. At a minimum 24-month follow-up, all four evaluated clinical parameters improved: jaw function (LTPS), dietary intake (LTPS), and pain (VAS) all showed clinically meaningful median reductions, and postoperative mouth opening averaged 32-33 mm — within the functional range reported by comparable studies. One patient (16.7%) required revision surgery for a displaced condylar screw at 6 months, resolved without functional deficit.

## Key Contributions
- Provides concrete functional-outcome numbers (JF, DI, pain, IMO) for total TMJ replacement with customized prostheses, filling a gap in this wiki's TMJ pages, which previously covered only conservative-to-arthrocentesis-tier management.
- Documents a detailed combined endaural + submandibular surgical approach with CBCT-guided cutting/drilling guides and autologous fat graft interposition.
- Reports a real-world complication (condylar screw displacement, 1/6 patients) and its successful management, useful for informed-consent discussions around TJR.
- Corroborates prior literature (Bhargava, Wolford, Gonzalez-Perez, Ravelo et al.) showing consistent pain/function/mouth-opening gains and high (~97%) prosthesis survival with customized designs, versus higher instability/dislocation risk historically associated with stock devices.

## Methodology
- Retrospective, descriptive case series (no control group, no statistical significance testing) — n=6 female patients treated Sept 2018-Feb 2024.
- Diagnosis via clinical exam plus MRI (soft tissue/internal derangement) and CBCT (osseous structures + prosthesis planning); muscular stabilization achieved preoperatively in all patients.
- Outcome measures recorded preoperatively (1 week-1 month before surgery) and at ≥24-month postoperative follow-up:
  - **IMO** (interincisal mouth opening, mm) via TheraBite Jaw Motion Rehabilitation System
  - **JF** (jaw function, 0-10 Likert-type psychometric scale)
  - **DI** (dietary intake, 0-10 LTPS)
  - **Pain** (0-10 VAS)
- Surgery: general anesthesia; endaural approach (capsulotomy, discectomy, fossa osteotomy) combined with submandibular approach (condylar osteotomy with piezoelectric instrumentation); glenoid component fixed with 4 screws, condylar component with 6-9 locking screws; gentamicin irrigation; autologous abdominal fat graft interposition. Two patients additionally underwent concurrent bimaxillary orthognathic surgery (Le Fort I + BSSO).
- Postoperative protocol: soft diet, cold therapy, chlorhexidine rinse, scheduled multimodal analgesia and antibiotics, kinesiotherapy starting at 1 week.

## Results
- IMO postoperative: mean 32 mm (passive) / 33 mm (gentle force); preoperative IMO not consistently documented across patients.
- JF (LTPS): median 6.5 → 3.5.
- DI (LTPS): median 8.0 → 1.5.
- Pain (VAS): median 7 → 2.
- Complications: 1/6 (16.7%) required revision at 6 months for condylar screw displacement, identified on CBCT, resolved by screw removal via the original approach with full functional recovery; no facial nerve injury, infection, or heterotopic ossification observed in this series.
- Results were consistent with prior TJR literature: Bhargava et al. (JF +4.2 pts, DI +5.6 pts, pain -75%), Wolford et al. (IMO 25.8→36.2 mm, pain reduction p<0.001), Gonzalez-Perez et al. (pain 5.9→1 at 5y), and a systematic review by Ravelo et al. reporting ~97% survival for customized TMJ prostheses.
- Number of prior TMJ surgeries (3/6 patients had ≥1) did not appear to correlate with worse outcomes in this small cohort, contrasting with a larger systematic review (Johnson et al.) that identified prior-arthrotomy count as a prognostic factor — attributed to sample-size limits here.
