## Three-line Summary

NMA (41 RCTs, 2021 adults, myogenic TMD; MEDLINE/Embase/CENTRAL/WoS/GS through Oct 2025) comparing 10 nonpharmacological modalities for pain and maximum mouth opening using frequentist random-effects model and SUCRA rankings.

For pain, PBMT ranked highest (SUCRA 88.9%; SMD vs OST −1.43 [−2.29, −0.56]; SMD vs CON −1.49 [−2.07, −0.91]); MT ranked second (SUCRA 79.9%); occlusal splint and exercise both non-significant vs conventional care; for MMO, MT ranked highest (SUCRA 92.9%; SMD vs CON 2.79 [1.33, 4.26]).

Prioritize PBMT when pain relief is the primary goal and MT when mandibular mobility restoration is primary; exercise and occlusal splints are accessible but show limited short-term pain advantage over conventional care.

## 세줄요약

NMA (41 RCT, 2021명, 근육성 악관절증; 5개 DB, 2025년 10월까지; PROSPERO 등록) — 10가지 비약물 요법을 통증과 최대개구량(MMO)에 대해 네트워크 메타분석으로 비교.

통증: 광생체조절치료 (Photobiomodulation Therapy, PBMT) SUCRA 88.9%(vs 교합안정장치 SMD −1.43 [−2.29, −0.56]; vs 대조군 −1.49 [−2.07, −0.91]); 도수치료 (Manual Therapy, MT) 2위(79.9%); 교합안정장치(Occlusal Splint Therapy, OST)·운동치료(Exercise Therapy, EX)는 대조군 대비 유의차 없음. MMO: MT 1위(SUCRA 92.9%; vs 대조군 SMD 2.79 [1.33, 4.26]), 단 출판 편향 가능성 있어 해석 주의.

임상 결론: 근육성 악관절증에서 통증 완화 목표 → PBMT 우선; 개구량 개선 목표 → MT 우선; 운동·안정장치는 접근성 장점이나 단기 통증 순위 제한적 — 장기 자가관리 맥락에서 의미 있을 수 있음.

## Summary

Network meta-analysis (Zhang 2026) comparing 10 nonpharmacological therapies for adult myogenic TMD. 41 RCTs (2021 participants) from 5 databases through Oct 2025. Photobiomodulation therapy (PBMT) ranked highest for short-term pain (SUCRA 88.9%), demonstrating significant superiority over occlusal splint therapy (SMD −1.43; 95% CI −2.29 to −0.56), exercise therapy, conventional care, and acupuncture. Manual therapy (MT) ranked second for pain (SUCRA 79.9%) and first for maximum mouth opening (SUCRA 92.9%; SMD vs CON 2.79 [1.33, 4.26]). Occlusal splint therapy and exercise therapy were not significantly different from conventional care for pain, though they remain accessible self-management tools. Possible publication bias detected for MMO (Egger p<0.05), so MT's MMO ranking should be interpreted cautiously.

## Key Contributions

- First comprehensive NMA simultaneously ranking 10 nonpharmacological modalities for myogenic TMD
- PBMT significantly outperforms OST, EX, ACU, and CON for pain reduction
- MT shows significant pain and MMO advantages over multiple comparators
- Reframes exercise vs splint debate: both below PBMT/MT for short-term pain
- Inconsistency signal for ELEC vs MT; possible publication bias for MMO outcome

## Methodology

- **Design**: Frequentist random-effects NMA (Stata v17, "network"+"mvmeta")
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL, Web of Science, Google Scholar (inception to Oct 2025)
- **Population**: Adults ≥18y with myogenic TMD
- **Modalities**: PBMT, MT, EX, OST, ACU, ELEC, COMB, CBT, ESWT, CNM
- **Control**: Conventional care / sham / no-treatment
- **Outcomes**: Pain (VAS) and MMO (interincisal distance mm)
- **Time point**: Short-term, at or immediately after intervention completion
- **Ranking**: SUCRA (higher = better relative probability of being best)
- **Risk of bias**: RoB 2.0; 22/41 low risk, 19/41 some concerns
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD420261278458

## Results

**Pain — SUCRA rankings:**

| Treatment | SUCRA (%) | vs CON (SMD [95% CI]) | Significant? |
|---|---|---|---|
| PBMT | 88.9 | −1.49 (−2.07, −0.91) | Yes |
| MT | 79.9 | −1.23 (−1.92, −0.54) | Yes |
| COMB | 72.5 | −1.04 (−2.00, −0.09) | Yes |
| OST | ~37 | −1.06 (−2.96, 0.84) | No |
| EX | ~32 | −0.99 (−3.05, 1.07) | No |
| CBT | 6.9 | −1.57 (−3.66, 0.53) | No |

Key contrasts: PBMT vs OST SMD −1.43 (−2.29, −0.56); MT vs OST SMD −1.16 (−2.10, −0.22)

**MMO — SUCRA rankings:**

| Treatment | SUCRA (%) | vs CON (SMD [95% CI]) | Significant? |
|---|---|---|---|
| MT | 92.9 | 2.79 (1.33, 4.26) | Yes |
| COMB | 75.2 | 1.91 (−0.10, 3.91) | No |
| ELEC | 58.7 | 1.42 (−0.85, 3.70) | No |

Note: MT vs EX significant (2.01 [0.12, 3.90]); Egger p<0.05 for MMO (publication bias possible).
