## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (n=65) at a single Chinese institution compared CAD/CAM occlusal splint alone, splint+manual therapy, and health education alone for ADDwoR (anterior disc displacement without reduction) over 3 months.

The combined splint+manual therapy group was superior to both alternatives at 1 and 3 months on MMO, VAS, and MFIQ (p<0.05); splint alone also outperformed health education.

Non-randomized patient-preference allocation and short follow-up limit causal inference; results support multimodal conservative management but require RCT confirmation.

## 세줄요약

중국 단일기관에서 ADDwoR(환원 없는 전방 관절원판 변위) 환자 65명을 대상으로 CAD/CAM 스플린트 단독, 스플린트+도수치료 병용, 건강교육 단독을 3개월간 후향적 비교.

병용군이 1개월·3개월 시점 모두 최대개구량(MMO, Maximum Mouth Opening), 통증(VAS, Visual Analog Scale), 하악기능장애지수(MFIQ, Mandibular Functional Impairment Questionnaire) 전 항목에서 나머지 두 군보다 유의하게 우월(p<0.05); 스플린트 단독군도 건강교육군보다 우월.

비무작위 환자선호 배정과 3개월 단기 추적의 한계가 있어 인과 추론을 위한 RCT가 필요함.

## Summary

This retrospective observational study evaluated the clinical efficacy of CAD/CAM-fabricated occlusal splints combined with manual therapy (도수치료, Manual Therapy) versus splint alone and health education for patients with ADDwoR (환원 없는 전방 관절원판 변위, Anterior Disc Displacement without Reduction).

Sixty-five adults with MRI-confirmed ADDwoR, persistent symptoms ≥3 months, VAS ≥3.0, and 최대개구량 (MMO, Maximum Mouth Opening) ≤30 mm were treated at Zhejiang Provincial People's Hospital. Allocation was by patient preference (not randomized):

- **Occlusal Splint Group** (n=22): Kovacs Digital Occlusal Splint (KDOS) — iTero intraoral scan → Exocad digital design → SELECT five-axis CAD/CAM milling; worn ≥12 h/day; monthly follow-up
- **Combined Treatment Group** (n=22): KDOS splint + weekly manual therapy (15–20 min/session × 3 months) targeting temporalis, masseter, SCM trigger points, and TMJ joint mobilization with traction and capsular stretching
- **Health Education Group** (n=21): dietary and behavioral counseling only

All groups received baseline health education covering dietary habits, behavioral modification, and self-management.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates additive benefit of manual therapy when combined with CAD/CAM splint therapy for ADDwoR: superior MMO, VAS, and MFIQ at both 1 and 3 months
- Describes the KDOS (Kovacs Digital Occlusal Splint) protocol in sufficient clinical detail for replication (iTero + Exocad + 5-axis milling workflow)
- Confirms that splint alone outperforms health education alone — corroborating conservative device-based management
- Provides power analysis (81–87% power) supporting adequacy of sample for measured outcomes

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective observational, patient preference-based allocation; single-blinded assessment
- **Diagnosis**: DC/TMD criteria; MRI confirmation of non-reducing disc displacement
- **Inclusion**: ADDwoR ≥3 months, VAS ≥3.0, MMO ≤30 mm, MRI-confirmed, complete documentation
- **Exclusion**: Prior TMJ surgery, systemic joint disease (RA/OA), concurrent TMD treatments, severe psychiatric conditions, substance abuse, acute TMJ infection
- **Outcomes**: MMO (mm), VAS (0–10), MFIQ — at baseline, 1 month, 3 months
- **Statistics**: ANOVA; α=0.05; power 81–87%

## Results

| Outcome | Combined Group | Splint Only | Health Education |
|---|---|---|---|
| MMO at 3 months | Significantly superior | Intermediate | Lowest |
| VAS at 3 months | Significantly superior | Intermediate | Highest (worst) |
| MFIQ at 3 months | Significantly superior | Intermediate | Highest (worst) |

- All between-group differences statistically significant at both 1 and 3 months (p<0.05)
- Baseline characteristics comparable across groups (all p>0.05)
- Combined group benefit driven by synergistic effect of neuromuscular mobilization (manual therapy) and biomechanical load redistribution (CAD/CAM splint)
