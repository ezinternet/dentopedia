## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + frequentist network meta-analysis of 52 RCTs in adults with myogenous TMD (M-TMD), comparing counseling, occlusal appliances, manual therapy, laser, dry needling, LA or BTX-A injection, muscle relaxants, hypnosis/relaxation, ozone, and placebo/no treatment (search to August 2018).

Most treatments beat placebo for pain at most follow-ups; short-term (<=5 months) top ranks were manual therapy (83.5%, low certainty), ozone (75.7%, very low), counseling (71.2%, moderate) and occlusal appliances (71.7%, moderate); intermediate-term (>=6 months) were BTX-A (85.8%, very low), counseling (80%, low), occlusal appliances (62.8%, low), hypnosis (50.6%, very low).

Manual therapy is presented as the most effective option, followed by counseling, intramuscular LA and occlusal appliances, but certainty is low to very low for many rankings and the authors urge cautious interpretation.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 빈도론적 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA) 52개 RCT, 성인 근육성 측두하악장애 (Myogenous Temporomandibular Disorders, M-TMD) — 상담치료, 교합장치, 도수치료, 레이저, 건침, 국소마취제 (Local Anesthesia, LA)·보툴리눔독소 A (Botulinum Toxin-A, BTX-A) 주사, 근이완제, 최면·이완, 오존, 위약/무치료 비교 (검색 2018년 8월까지).

대부분의 치료가 위약보다 통증을 유의하게 줄였고, 단기(5개월 이하) 순위는 도수치료 83.5%(낮은 확실성), 오존 75.7%(매우 낮음), 상담치료 71.2%(중간), 교합장치 71.7%(중간); 중기(6개월 이상)는 BTX-A 85.8%(매우 낮음), 상담치료 80%(낮음), 교합장치 62.8%(낮음), 최면 50.6%(매우 낮음).

도수치료가 가장 효과적인 것으로 제시되고 상담치료, LA 근육 내 주사, 교합장치가 뒤따르나, 다수 순위의 근거 확실성이 낮거나 매우 낮아 저자들은 신중한 해석을 권고.

## Summary

This network meta-analysis (Al-Moraissi 2021, Oral Maxillofac Surg) ranked treatments for adult myogenous TMD using 52 RCTs found through August 2018. Compared with placebo, manual therapy, counseling, occlusal splints, BTX-A or LA needling, and dry needling significantly lowered post-treatment pain at most follow-up points. Short-term (<=5 months) the highest-ranked options for pain were manual therapy, ozone, counseling and occlusal appliances; at >=6 months they were BTX-A, counseling, occlusal appliances and hypnosis, mostly at low or very low certainty. The authors conclude manual therapy is the most effective treatment, followed by counseling, intramuscular LA and occlusal appliances, with cautious interpretation advised. Only the abstract was retrieved, so effect sizes and MMO results are not available.

## Key Contributions

- Ranks ten modality groups, including injection, BTX-A, ozone and hypnosis options not covered by the wiki's 2026 nonpharmacological NMA.
- Gives separate short-term and intermediate-term rankings with certainty labels.
- Identifies counseling and occlusal appliances as the only top-four options at both horizons; counseling certainty is moderate short-term and low at >=6 months.

## Methodology

- Adults with M-TMD; RCTs comparing two or more of the listed modalities or placebo/no treatment.
- Primary outcomes: pain and mechanical sensitivity; secondary: maximal mouth opening (MMO).
- Standardized mean difference; frequentist NMA in STATA; Cochrane risk-of-bias tool.
- Search from inception to August 2018.

## Comparison with the closest held page

[[tmj/zhang-2026-nonpharmacological-myogenic-tmd-nma]] (41 RCTs, search to Oct 2025, nonpharmacological only) ranks photobiomodulation first for pain (SUCRA 88.9%), manual therapy second (79.9%), and finds occlusal splint and exercise not significant versus conventional care. This 2021 NMA differs in three ways: it includes injections, BTX-A, ozone and hypnosis; its search ended in 2018, before much of the photobiomodulation literature; and its comparator is placebo/no treatment, where occlusal appliances were significantly better. Both place manual therapy among the top options. The two do not directly conflict (different comparators and time windows), so no contradiction edge or supersession is recorded; for current decisions on nonpharmacological care the 2026 NMA has the later search, while this page is the only held NMA covering BTX-A and injection rankings.
