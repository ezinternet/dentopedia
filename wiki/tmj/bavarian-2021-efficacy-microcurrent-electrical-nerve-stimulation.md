## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of microcurrent electrical nerve stimulation (MENS) for myofascial pain of the masticatory muscles; four independent comparisons from three studies (n = 140) were pooled, with pain on palpation as the primary outcome.

MENS reduced pain by a mean of -0.57 points (95% CI -0.91 to -0.23) versus placebo and other therapies, with high heterogeneity (I2 = 83.7%).

The authors call MENS an effective non-invasive option, but the evidence base is three small studies with marked between-study variability, so the estimate is fragile.

## 세줄요약

근막통이 있는 저작근에 대한 미세전류 전기신경자극 (Microcurrent Electrical Nerve Stimulation, MENS)의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석으로, 3개 연구의 독립 비교 4건 (n = 140)을 합쳤고 일차 결과는 촉진 시 통증 감소.

MENS는 위약 및 다른 치료 대비 평균 통증을 -0.57점 (95% CI -0.91 ~ -0.23) 줄였으나 이질성이 높았음 (I2 = 83.7%).

저자들은 MENS를 효과적인 비침습 치료로 평가하지만, 근거가 소규모 연구 3편이고 연구 간 변동이 커서 추정치는 불안정함.

## Summary

According to PubMed (abstract only; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/08869634.2021.2020006)), this SR+MA evaluated MENS for myofascial pain of the masticatory muscles. Primary outcome was reduction in pain on palpation. Four independent comparisons from three studies (n = 140) were pooled; MENS improved mean pain by -0.57 points (95% CI -0.91 to -0.23; I2 = 83.7%) compared with placebo and other therapies. The authors conclude MENS is an effective non-invasive treatment but note the small number of eligible articles and variability between studies.

## Key Contributions

- Only MENS-specific pooled estimate for masticatory myofascial pain currently held.
- Quantifies the effect at -0.57 points, a modest magnitude with wide CI.

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA; primary outcome pain on palpation of masticatory muscles.
- Included: 4 comparisons, 3 studies, n = 140; comparators placebo and other therapies.
- Not stated in the abstract: databases, registration, risk-of-bias tool, scale, MENS parameters.

## Results

| Outcome | Pooled mean difference | 95% CI | I2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain on palpation | -0.57 points | -0.91 to -0.23 | 83.7% |

High heterogeneity and a pooled comparator mixing placebo with other active therapies limit interpretation.
