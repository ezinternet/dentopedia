## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 38 RCTs comparing conservative (physical therapy, physical agents, pharmacological, occlusal devices), minimally invasive (arthrocentesis), and surgical (arthroscopy, open surgery) treatments in adults with TMD disc displacement.

All seven treatment categories produced meaningful pain reduction and/or improved maximum mouth opening; no single modality was universally superior, and conservative approaches generally matched or approached surgical outcomes.

The findings support a stepwise approach starting with conservative, reversible treatments before escalating to minimally invasive or surgical procedures, though high heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis.

## 세줄요약

성인 턱관절장애(Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 디스크 변위 환자에서 보존적·최소침습적·수술적 치료를 비교한 RCT 38편 체계적 문헌고찰(검색일 2023년 9월).

7개 치료군 모두 통증 감소 및/또는 최대 개구량(Maximum Mouth Opening, MMO) 개선을 보였으나 단일 최우수 치료법은 확인되지 않았다.

높은 이질성으로 메타분석 불가; 보존적·가역적 치료 우선 후 단계적 접근 원칙을 지지한다.

## Summary

Sa et al. (2024) conducted a PRISMA-compliant systematic review of RCTs to compare the full spectrum of TMD disc displacement treatments — from conservative to surgical. Initial search yielded 2,289 records; 38 RCTs were ultimately included after deduplication, screening, and eligibility assessment (kappa agreement 0.719). Risk of bias was assessed using Cochrane RoB 2.

The 38 studies were grouped into seven treatment categories: physical therapy (n=8), physical agents (n=3), pharmacological (n=3), occlusal devices (n=15), arthrocentesis (n=14), arthroscopic surgery (n=4), and open TMJ surgery (n=5). Outcome measures included pain (VAS), MMO, jaw function, joint sounds, and quality of life.

**Key findings across categories:**

| Treatment | Evidence | Key outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Physical therapy (jaw exercises + counseling) | 8 RCTs | Pain ↓ (p<0.05); click discomfort ↓; supervised > home-based |
| High-intensity laser (HILT) | 2 RCTs | Pain ↓ 48%, MMO ↑ 24%; superior to TENS |
| LLLT | 3 RCTs | Effective; combination with splint faster than mono |
| Occlusal devices (stabilization splint, ARA) | 15 RCTs | Most-studied; both types effective; ARA faster for DDwR |
| Arthrocentesis ± injectate | 14 RCTs | Effective for DDwR and DDwoR; hyaluronic acid best injectate |
| Arthroscopic surgery | 4 RCTs | Effective in refractory cases |
| Open joint surgery | 5 RCTs | Good outcomes for pain/function; higher risk |

No meta-analysis was possible due to heterogeneity in populations, outcomes, and follow-up periods.

## Key Contributions

- Broadest RCT-only SR comparing all TMD disc displacement treatment tiers in a single review (up to September 2023)
- Quantified superiority of high-intensity laser therapy (HILT) over TENS: 48% vs 25% pain reduction, 24% vs 10% MMO gain
- Botulinum toxin (BTX) intraoral injection shown faster and equally effective as extraoral (4.86 vs 10.29 min)
- Physical exercise after arthrocentesis reduced pain (VAS difference 1.81) but did not improve MMO range
- Pulsed electromagnetic fields (PEMF) showed no specific treatment effect (p>0.05)
- Hyaluronic acid intra-articular injection showed most significant pain reduction among injectates

## Methodology

- Design: Systematic review (PRISMA); no meta-analysis
- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science (search: 17 September 2023)
- Inclusion: RCTs; adults ≥18 years; TMD disc displacement (DDwR or DDwoR); any intervention; English language
- Exclusion: incomplete randomization, no disc derangement diagnosis, missing full text
- Screening: Rayyan (blind mode); 2 reviewers + 1 TMD specialist
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2 tool

## Results

### Physical Therapy
- Jaw exercises + counseling superior to counseling alone for pain (p=0.041) and click discomfort (p<0.001)
- Supervised exercise program outperforms home-based for pain and MMO (p<0.05)
- Post-arthrocentesis exercise: pain reduction (VAS −1.81, p<0.05) without MMO benefit
- Exercise program non-inferior to stabilization splint (Olbort et al.); faster jaw function recovery with exercise (Haketa et al., p<0.001)
- Craane et al.: physical therapy added no benefit over medical counseling alone

### Physical Agents
- HILT vs TENS: pain reduced 48% vs 25%; MMO increased 24% vs 10% (p<0.05 at 12 weeks)
- HILT, ultrasound, and occlusal splints all effective; pain reduction 0.44–0.66 (normalized units), MMO ↑ 0.19–0.25
- LLLT + BTX injection: faster onset than anterior repositioning appliance (ARA)
- BTX intraoral vs extraoral: equivalent clinical outcome; intraoral faster (4.86 ± 1.53 vs 10.29 ± 2.69 min)
- LLLT + stabilization splint: faster and more effective than either alone
- PEMF: no specific treatment effect (p>0.05)

### Intra-Articular Injections
- 100-patient RCT: hyaluronic acid > tenoxicam ≈ dexamethasone for pain reduction

### Occlusal Devices (15 RCTs)
- Largest treatment category; stabilization splint most studied
- ARA and stabilization splints both effective; ARA faster for acute disc reduction in DDwR

### Arthrocentesis (14 RCTs)
- Effective for DDwR and DDwoR
- Various injectates studied (hyaluronic acid, PRP, corticosteroids, saline)

### Arthroscopy (4 RCTs)
- Effective for refractory disc displacement cases

### Open Joint Surgery (5 RCTs)
- Arthroplasty/arthrotomy for severe or failed-conservative cases; good pain and function outcomes
