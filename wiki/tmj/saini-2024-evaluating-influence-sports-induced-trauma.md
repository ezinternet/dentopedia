## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of observational studies (cohort and cross-sectional; NIH quality tools) comparing temporomandibular disorder (TMD) symptoms between athletes and non-athletes.

Symptoms (myofascial pain, clicking, musculoskeletal symptoms) were comparably prevalent in athletes and non-athletes, and pooled comparisons showed statistically insignificant differences; mouthguard use was associated with a significant reduction in TMD symptoms.

Only the abstract was available, so pooled estimates, study counts and heterogeneity are unknown; sport-specific prevalence differed, but athletic participation per se is not shown to raise TMD risk.

## 세줄요약

관찰연구(코호트·단면연구; NIH 질 평가 도구)를 대상으로 운동선수와 비선수 간 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 증상을 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석.

근막통 (myofascial pain)·관절음·근골격계 증상의 유병률은 선수와 비선수에서 비슷했고 합산 비교에서 통계적으로 유의한 차이가 없었으며, 마우스가드 (mouthguard) 사용 시 TMD 증상이 유의하게 감소했다.

초록만 확보되어 합산 추정치·연구 수·이질성은 알 수 없으며, 종목별 유병률은 달랐으나 운동 참여 자체가 TMD 위험을 높인다고 볼 근거는 제시되지 않았다.

## Summary

According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.archoralbio.2024.106168)), this systematic review and meta-analysis examined whether sports participation, and sports-related trauma, is associated with temporomandibular disorders. Six databases were searched and observational studies were appraised with NIH tools. TMD symptoms such as myofascial pain, clicking and musculoskeletal symptoms were comparably prevalent in athletes and non-athletes, with some contrasting individual studies, and the meta-analyses found statistically insignificant differences. Mouthguard use was associated with a significant reduction in TMD symptoms, and prevalence differed between sports. This page is built from the abstract only.

## Key Contributions

- Null pooled finding: no significant athlete vs non-athlete difference in TMD symptoms.
- Signal that mouthguard use is associated with fewer TMD symptoms.
- Prevalence varies by sport.

## Methodology

- Searches: PubMed, ScienceDirect, Dimensions, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library, ERIC; pre-specified eligibility criteria.
- Quality: NIH tools for observational cohort and cross-sectional studies.
- Number of included studies, sample sizes, effect measure and heterogeneity: not reported in the abstract.

## Results

- Myofascial pain, clicking and musculoskeletal symptoms: comparable prevalence in athletes vs non-athletes; some studies contrasting.
- Meta-analyses: statistically insignificant athlete vs non-athlete differences.
- Mouthguard use: significant reduction in TMD symptoms.
- Different prevalence rates across sports.
- No numeric estimates available (abstract-only).
