## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of controlled trials of deep dry needling (DDN) for chronic masticatory myofascial pain (MMP), paired with a preliminary clinical trial in five consecutive MMP patients (Cranio 2019; published 2019, outside the 5-year surveillance window). Abstract-only.

Of 25 studies screened by title/abstract, only 2 met the inclusion criteria (used for the sample-size calculation); in the five-patient trial, DDN raised the pressure pain threshold (PPT) at myofascial trigger points by 44.6% versus -5.5% with sham (p = 0.04, Wilcoxon).

Suggestive but very low-certainty evidence: the controlled-trial base is only 2 studies and the own trial has n = 5, so it supports a hypothesis of PPT benefit, not a treatment recommendation.

## 세줄요약

저작근 근막통 (Masticatory Myofascial Pain, MMP)에 대한 심부 건침술 (Deep Dry Needling, DDN)의 대조임상시험을 체계적으로 고찰하고, 연속 내원한 MMP 환자 5명의 예비 임상시험을 더한 연구 (Cranio 2019, 2019년 발표로 5년 서베일런스 창 밖; 초록만 확보).

제목·초록 단계 25편 중 포함기준 충족은 2편뿐이었고, 5명 예비시험에서 DDN은 근막 통점 (Myofascial Trigger Point, MTP)의 압통역치 (Pressure Pain Threshold, PPT)를 44.6% 높였다 (가짜 시술 -5.5%; p = 0.04, Wilcoxon).

시사적이지만 근거 확실성은 매우 낮다: 대조시험이 2편, 자체 시험이 n = 5라 PPT 개선 가설을 지지할 뿐 치료 권고의 근거로는 부족하다.

## Summary

Tesch et al. (Cranio 2019; year 2019, outside the 5-year surveillance window) combine a systematic review of controlled trials of deep dry needling (DDN) in chronic masticatory myofascial pain (MMP) with a preliminary clinical trial in five consecutive patients. According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/08869634.2019.1588518)), 25 studies were considered on title and abstract and only 2 met the inclusion criteria. In the five-patient trial, DDN raised the pressure pain threshold (PPT) at myofascial trigger points by 44.6% versus -5.5% with sham (p = 0.04, Wilcoxon). Study type labelled `sr` (no pooled meta-analysis reported in the abstract).

## Key Contributions

- Shows that in 2019 only 2 controlled trials of DDN for MMP met inclusion criteria, i.e. a very thin sham-controlled base.
- Adds a PPT-based signal (+44.6% vs -5.5%) rather than a VAS pain outcome.

## Methodology

Systematic review (25 screened, 2 included) plus a five-patient preliminary trial; three consecutive PPT measurements per trigger point; percentage change compared between DDN side and sham; Wilcoxon test. Search strategy, registration, and risk-of-bias method are not stated in the abstract (full text not retrieved).

## Results

- DDN: PPT +44.6% at MTPs; sham: -5.5%; p = 0.04.
- The 2 eligible trials were used for the sample-size calculation.

Limitations: abstract-only; n = 5; no certainty grading; PPT is a surrogate outcome.
