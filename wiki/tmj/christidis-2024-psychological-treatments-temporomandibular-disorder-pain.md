## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42022320106) of psychological interventions for painful temporomandibular disorders (TMD): 18 RCTs in the narrative synthesis, 6 in the meta-analysis.

Narrative synthesis: psychological treatments appear equivalent to standard treatment for pain; meta-analysis: psychological + standard + manual treatment was significantly better for pain reduction than counselling and standard treatment alone (very-low-quality evidence).

Psychological treatment is a promising add-on rather than a replacement; the meta-analytic signal rests on very-low-certainty evidence from only six RCTs.

## 세줄요약

유병 통증성 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에 대한 심리 치료를 다룬 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 (PROSPERO CRD42022320106): 서술적 종합 RCT 18편, 메타분석 RCT 6편.

서술적 종합에서는 심리 치료가 표준 치료와 통증 면에서 동등해 보였고, 메타분석에서는 심리+표준+수기 치료 병행이 상담+표준 치료보다 통증 감소가 유의하게 컸다 (매우 낮은 근거 확실성).

심리 치료는 대체가 아닌 유망한 추가 치료이며, 메타분석 신호는 RCT 6편에 기반한 매우 낮은 확실성 근거다.

## Summary

According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/joor.13693)), this J Oral Rehabil 2024 review asked whether psychological treatments reduce pain in painful TMD over the short and long term. Eighteen RCTs were synthesized narratively and six were pooled. Psychological options looked equivalent to standard treatment; adding psychological treatment to standard and manual treatment reduced pain more than counselling plus standard treatment, but certainty was very low.

## Key Contributions

- A dedicated synthesis of psychological (e.g., cognitive behavioral) treatments for painful TMD, complementing education/self-management and counselling evidence already held.
- Positions psychological treatment as an equivalent-to-standard option and a promising add-on.

## Methodology

Databases: MEDLINE, CINAHL, EMBASE, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science. RCTs of psychological interventions in TMD. PROSPERO CRD42022320106. Cochrane risk-of-bias tool; GRADE certainty. Abstract-level only; no full text.

## Results

- 18 RCTs included; 6 usable in meta-analysis.
- Narrative synthesis: psychological treatment seems equivalent to standard treatment for painful TMD.
- Meta-analysis: combination of psychological + standard + manual treatment significantly better in pain reduction than counselling plus standard treatment alone (very-low-quality evidence).
- No pooled effect sizes, CIs or I² are available from the abstract.
