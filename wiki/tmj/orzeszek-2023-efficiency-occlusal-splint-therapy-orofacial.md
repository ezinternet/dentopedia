## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA 2020; PubMed, CINAHL, Scopus; searched to June 2022) of 13 RCTs (589 patients) comparing occlusal splints with no treatment, education/counselling, acupuncture, low-level laser, LED, physical therapy, Kinesio Taping, myofunctional therapy and sensorimotor training in orofacial myalgia and myofascial pain.

All 13 trials were at high risk of bias (participants cannot be blinded; allocation concealment unclear in all 13); no meta-analysis was possible, and in most head-to-head comparisons splints performed similarly to the alternatives, with counselling/education in one RCT giving better spontaneous pain relief at 3 months (p=0.034).

The authors conclude evidence is insufficient to show that splints offer an advantage over other interventions or no treatment for muscle pain; high-quality, blinded, larger, long-term trials are needed.

## 세줄요약

근육통 (myalgia) 및 근막통 (Myofascial Pain, MP)에서 교합장치 (Occlusal Splint, OS)를 무치료·교육/상담·침·저출력 레이저 (Low-Level Laser Therapy, LLLT)·발광다이오드 (LED)·물리치료 등과 비교한 RCT 13편 (589명)의 체계적 문헌고찰 (PRISMA 2020; 2022년 6월까지 검색).

13편 모두 비뚤림 위험 높음 (참여자 눈가림 불가, 배정은폐 전부 불명확)이며 메타분석은 불가능했고, 대부분의 직접 비교에서 교합장치는 대안 치료와 비슷했으며 한 RCT에서는 교육군이 3개월 자발통 감소가 더 컸음 (p=0.034).

교합장치가 무치료나 다른 중재보다 우월하다고 볼 근거는 불충분하며, 눈가림을 갖춘 대규모 장기 시험이 필요함.

## Summary

Source: PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-023-02897-0)), BMC Oral Health 2023;23:180. This review differs from broader TMD splint reviews by restricting inclusion to adults with painful masticatory-muscle disorders (RDC/TMD myofascial pain, DC/TMD myalgia/MP, or clear clinical muscle pain) and to RCTs with at least 20 patients. Thirteen RCTs (589 patients) were synthesized narratively by comparator type. Every trial was rated high risk of bias, chiefly because patients cannot be blinded to splint versus other therapy. The authors conclude that evidence is insufficient to establish an advantage of splints over no treatment or other interventions, and that counselling for behavioural change and self-care appears comparable and can be a first-choice approach.

## Key Contributions

- Muscle-pain-only scope, separating myogenous pain from joint and disc disorders.
- Head-to-head map of splint versus acupuncture, laser, LED, physical therapy, Kinesio Taping, myofunctional therapy, sensorimotor training and education.
- Explicit conclusion of insufficient evidence for splint superiority; no pooled estimate and no GRADE rating reported.

## Methodology

- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, CINAHL; English RCTs, January 2010 to June 2022; last search 4 June 2022.
- Population: adults >=18 with muscle pain; groups >=20; exclusions for nonpainful TMD, prior TMD treatment, systemic, neurologic or psychogenic causes, recent facial/neck trauma.
- Two independent reviewers with a third for disputes; Cochrane risk-of-bias tool; narrative synthesis because pain outcomes and statistics were heterogeneous.
- Flow: 474 records, 107 after deduplication, 13 included.
- Internal inconsistency: Results text gives the window end as 1 June 2021; Methods and abstract give 1 June 2022.

## Results

- 589 participants: 276 OS, 108 conservative/counselling, 30 acupuncture, 30 laser, 23 sensorimotor training, 16 Kinesio Taping, 10 myofunctional therapy, 37 manual therapy, 59 LED. Stabilization splints in 207 patients, reflex splints 59, repositioning splints 10. Follow-up 4 weeks to 6 months. Pain measured mainly by VAS.
- Risk of bias: all 13 high overall; allocation concealment unclear in all; random sequence unclear in 8.

| Comparison | Trial (n) | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Acupuncture | Grillo (40), Barrero (20) | Similar short-term pain reduction; no long-term follow-up |
| Low-level laser | Oz (40), Manfredini | Oz: no between-group difference; Manfredini: at 3 weeks VAS fell only with laser (p=0.018), at 6 months all groups improved (laser p=0.001, appliance p=0.002, counselling p=0.048) |
| Sensorimotor training | Giannakopoulos (45) | Pain reduction 53% vs 40%, no between-group difference; training harder to use |
| LED | Costa | Splint + LED better than splint control for pain (p<0.05) |
| Physical therapy | Grootel (72) | Long-term success 51-60% both; Cohen's d 0.86 (physio) vs 1.39 (splint); physio 10.4 weeks shorter |
| Kinesio Taping | Keskinruzgar (34) | Similar to splint in sleep bruxism |
| Myofunctional therapy | Felicio (10 per group) | Better than splint at 120 days |
| Education/counselling | Michelotti (44), Erbasar (40), Conti (51) | Michelotti: education better for spontaneous pain (p=0.034; effect size 0.33); NTI added nothing to counselling (Erbasar p=0.922); Conti: splint gave earlier improvement |
| Prefabricated vs laboratory splint | Ficnar (63) | Mouth-opening gain faster with prefabricated SOLUBrux splint |

- Limitations stated: high bias, heterogeneous outcomes precluding meta-analysis, English-only, baseline pain not characterized, no long-term or large balanced-sex trials.
