## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251029633) of 18 RCTs (n = 1038; 11 muscular, 4 articular, 3 mixed TMD) comparing photobiomodulation (PBM) effects across TMD subtypes.

Pooled effects favored PBM for pain (SMD = -0.84; 95% CI -1.18 to -0.51) and mandibular function (SMD = 0.72; 95% CI 0.38 to 1.05); articular TMD trials showed the most robust and homogeneous effects.

PBM is effective in both muscular and articular TMD, supporting subtype-specific protocols; articular superiority rests on only 4 trials and an abstract-level read (no per-subtype estimates or certainty grades retrieved).

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251029633): 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 18편 (n = 1038; 근육성 11편, 관절성 4편, 혼합 3편)으로 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 아형별 광생체조절치료 (Photobiomodulation, PBM) 효과를 비교.

통증 (SMD = -0.84; 95% CI -1.18 ~ -0.51)과 하악 기능 (SMD = 0.72; 95% CI 0.38 ~ 1.05) 모두 PBM이 유의하게 우수했고, 관절성 TMD 시험에서 효과가 가장 강하고 균질했음.

근육성·관절성 모두 PBM이 유효해 아형별 프로토콜을 뒷받침하나, 관절성 우위는 4편 기반이며 초록 수준이라 아형별 추정치·근거 확실성 등급은 확인 불가.

## Summary

According to PubMed (abstract only; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10103-025-04751-x)), this SR+MA searched PubMed, MEDLINE, LILACS and the Cochrane Library through March 31, 2025 for RCTs of PBM in muscular or articular TMD reporting pain (VAS) and/or maximum mouth opening (MMO). Eighteen RCTs (n = 1038) were included, risk of bias was assessed with Cochrane RoB 2.0, and random-effects models with TMD-subtype subgroups were used. PBM improved pain and function in all subtypes, with the most robust and homogeneous effects in articular TMD. The authors attribute this to PBM's anti-inflammatory properties and recommend subtype-specific protocols.

## Key Contributions

- Direct subtype comparison (muscular vs articular) of PBM, which the myogenic-only NMA [[tmj/zhang-2026-nonpharmacological-myogenic-tmd-nma]] could not provide.
- Pooled pain effect SMD = -0.84 (95% CI -1.18 to -0.51) and function effect SMD = 0.72 (95% CI 0.38 to 1.05).
- Proposes that PBM works better and more consistently in intra-articular conditions (anti-inflammatory mechanism, stated as a hypothesis).

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA, PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251029633.
- Databases: PubMed, MEDLINE, LILACS, Cochrane Library (to 2025-03-31).
- Eligibility: RCTs of PBM in muscular or articular TMD; outcomes VAS pain and/or MMO.
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2.0. Synthesis: random-effects with TMD-subtype subgroup analyses.
- Included: 18 RCTs, n = 1038 (11 muscular, 4 articular, 3 mixed).

## Results

| Outcome | Pooled SMD | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| Pain (VAS) | -0.84 | -1.18 to -0.51 |
| Function (MMO) | 0.72 | 0.38 to 1.05 |

- Significant improvement in pain and function across all subtypes.
- Articular TMD trials: most robust and homogeneous effects.
- Not retrievable from the abstract: per-subtype estimates, heterogeneity (I2), RoB 2.0 distribution, laser parameters (wavelength, dose), follow-up duration, certainty grading.
