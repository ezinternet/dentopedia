## Three-line Summary

Mapping review (OSF-registered osf.io/p3tm5, PRISMA 2020 flow for visualisation only) of PubMed/MEDLINE, Europe PMC and BASE from inception to 7 April 2026, applying a deliberately restrictive rule that an injectable counts as evidence only when its clinical effect can be assessed **independently** of any simultaneous surgical, arthroscopic or immobilisation procedure — which reduced the entire field to **5 primary clinical studies plus 8 secondary sources** (231 records excluded at title/abstract; Cohen's κ = 0.89 and 0.92 at the two screening stages; JBI appraisal rating methodological quality generally low to moderate).

Those 5 studies cover three agents only — **autologous blood** (Machon 2018 double-blind n=40, 2 mL superior joint space + 1 mL periarticular vs 1 mL periarticular alone; Oshiro 2014 n=14, ~3 mL superior compartment + ~2 mL pericapsular ≈ 5 mL, zero recurrences at 1 year; Yoshida 2018 n=21, 18/21 recurrence-free within 36 months), **50% dextrose prolotherapy** (Zhou 2014 n=45, 2 mL 50% dextrose 10 minutes after 2 mL 2% lignocaine into the posterior periarticular tissues at the condylar neck, 41/45 with no further dislocation or subluxation for >6 months) and **5% sodium morrhuate** (Xu 1992 n=105, 0.5 mL per point extra-articularly around the capsule/condylar neck, excellent/good 92.5% at ≥2 years).

No pooling was performed and the conclusion is explicitly negative — the evidence **cannot establish comparative effectiveness or superiority of any injectable modality**, since all three agents work by the same largely theoretical periarticular-fibrosis mechanism while site, volume, session number and baseline joint laxity all vary; the authors also concede that the exclusion rule removed clinically important randomised trials (Hegab 2013, Chhapane 2024) because they paired injection with post-procedural immobilisation.

## 세줄요약

매핑 리뷰 (mapping review, 근거 지도형 설계, OSF 사전등록 osf.io/p3tm5) — 2026년 4월 7일까지 PubMed(MEDLINE)·Europe PMC·BASE에서 inception 이후 검색. 핵심 선별 규칙은 **주사 성분의 효과를 수술·관절경·악관절 고정 (intermaxillary immobilization) 등 동시 비주사 중재와 분리해 평가할 수 있어야 한다**는 것이었고, 그 결과 **1차 임상 연구 5편 + 보조 매핑 문헌 8편**만 남김(제목/초록 제외 231건, Cohen's κ 0.89/0.92, JBI appraisal상 전반적 방법론적 질 낮음~중등).

남은 근거는 세 약제뿐이다 — 자가혈액 주사 (autologous blood injection, ABI): Machon 2018 이중눈가림 n=40(상부 관절강내 2 mL + 관절주변 1 mL vs 관절주변 1 mL 단독, 수치상 우위이나 통계적 유의성 없음), Oshiro 2014 n=14(상부 구획 ~3 mL + 관절주변 ~2 mL ≈ 5 mL, 1년 재발 0건), Yoshida 2018 n=21(36개월 내 18/21 재발 없음); 50% 덱스트로스 프로로테라피: Zhou 2014 n=45(과두경 후방 관절주변 단일 주입, 41/45가 6개월 초과 재탈구·아탈구 없음); 5% sodium morrhuate 경화제: Xu 1992 n=105(관절강내 아닌 관절주변 0.5 mL/점, ≥2년 추적 우수/양호 92.5%).

결론은 명시적으로 부정이다 — 어떤 주사 요법의 비교 효과·우위도 결론 낼 수 없다. 세 약제 모두 "관절주변 섬유화로 과두 과다 이동을 제한"한다는 동일하고 거의 이론적인 기전이며, 주입부위·용량·회수·기준선관절이완성이 연구마다 달라 결과 변동의 원인이 되고, 저자들은 고정 병용 때문에 임상적으로 중요한 무작위 연구(Hegab 2013, Chhapane 2024)가 자기 지도에서 제외됐고 이 대표성 축소도 저작자들이 인정함 — 즉 **급성 복원이 아니라 복원 이후 재발 방지**를 다룬다.

## Summary

This mapping review from the Cracow/Warsaw group (Polish National Medical Institute of the Ministry of the Interior and Administration) asked a deliberately narrow question about recurrent mandibular (temporomandibular joint) dislocation: which injectable intra- or periarticular treatments have a clinical effect that can be read on its own, without simultaneous surgery, arthroscopy or post-procedural mandibular immobilisation? Screening PubMed/MEDLINE, Europe PMC and BASE from inception to 7 April 2026 under that gate left 5 primary clinical studies and 8 secondary sources, appraised with Joanna Briggs Institute checklists and judged generally low-to-moderate in quality, with substantial inter-rater agreement (κ = 0.89 title/abstract, 0.92 full text). The surviving evidence is autologous blood injection (three studies, n = 14, 21, 40; recurrence-free results of 0/14 at one year, 18/21 within 36 months, and a numerically but not statistically significant advantage of intra- plus periarticular over periarticular injection alone), 50% dextrose prolotherapy (one prospective case series, n = 45, 41/45 free of further dislocation or subluxation beyond 6 months after a single 2 mL injection behind 2 mL of lignocaine) and 5% sodium morrhuate sclerosant (one 1992 case series, n = 105, excellent/good 92.5% at two years or longer). No quantitative pooling was performed, and the authors state plainly that the evidence does not allow conclusions about comparative effectiveness or superiority of any injectable modality, noting that all agents are rationalised by the same largely theoretical periarticular-fibrosis mechanism while injection site, volume, session number and baseline joint characteristics vary widely. Their own exclusion rule is the paper's most consequential limitation and they say so: several clinically relevant prospective and randomised studies were set aside because they combined injection with immobilisation, which they acknowledge narrows the clinical representativeness of the map — so this is a map of intervention-specific evidence for **recurrence prevention after reduction**, not of injection protocols as practised, and not of the reduction manoeuvre itself.

## Key Contributions

- **An "isolable effect" gate applied to a whole field**: injections combined with surgery, arthroscopy or immobilisation were excluded unless the injectable contribution could be isolated. This is the paper's real contribution — it explains why the field collapses to 5 studies, and the authors explicitly concede it also removed clinically important randomised trials, which is why they frame the output as a focused intervention-specific map rather than a comprehensive review of injection protocols.
- **A dose/volume/site map that is actually usable chairside**: single posterior periarticular site at the condylar neck, 2 mL of 50% dextrose after 2 mL of 2% lignocaine given 10 minutes earlier (Zhou); superior joint space 2 mL plus 1 mL periarticular versus 1 mL periarticular alone (Machon); ~3 mL superior compartment plus ~2 mL pericapsular ≈ 5 mL, protocol replicated by Yoshida (Oshiro); 0.5 mL per injection point, extra-articular 5% sodium morrhuate (Xu).
- **Separation of "favourable direction" from "comparative evidence"**: recurrence fell in most series, but only Machon is comparative and its intra-plus-periarticular advantage did not reach statistical significance — so the conclusion is a refusal to rank modalities rather than a ranking.
- **A quantified gap statement**: hyaluronic acid and fillers, corticosteroids, botulinum toxin, hydroxyapatite and PRP are supported in this indication only by isolated reports and secondary sources; the recurrent-luxation BoNT studies (Kotimäki 2011, Gilles 2000) were excluded because they were intramuscular rather than intra/periarticular, and PRP/PRF reports were excluded on population grounds.
- **One caution worth carrying into practice**: Xu 1992 reports lower satisfaction when recurrent dislocation was associated with cerebrovascular disease — the only signal in this evidence base pointing at a subgroup modifier.

## Methodology

- **Design**: mapping review (evidence map), prospectively registered (OSF osf.io/p3tm5); PRISMA 2020 flow diagram used **only to visualise selection**, not as a claim of systematic-review-level pooling
- **Search**: PubMed (MEDLINE), Europe PMC, BASE, inception → 7 April 2026, plus reference-list screening and author contact; no language restrictions
- **Eligibility gate**: injectable intra- or periarticular agent intended to stabilise the joint or reduce recurrence, whose effect is evaluable independently of simultaneous non-injectable intervention; RCTs through case reports all eligible, SRs charted as secondary mapping sources, animal/in vitro excluded
- **Included**: 5 primary clinical studies + 8 secondary mapping/reference sources; 231 records excluded at title/abstract screening
- **Screening**: Rayyan, two independent reviewers (J.K., O.J.) blinded at title/abstract and full-text stages after a calibration exercise, consensus on disagreement; Cohen's κ = 0.89 and 0.92
- **Data charting**: structured form piloted and refined, two reviewers charting independently in Google Sheets, terminology harmonised (habitual dislocation/luxation → recurrent mandibular dislocation where repeated episodes were described)
- **Critical appraisal**: Joanna Briggs Institute case-series checklist and revised quasi-experimental checklist (2024); quality judged **low to moderate**
- **Synthesis**: structured descriptive mapping by agent, deposition site, design and outcome; **no quantitative pooling or meta-analysis** given the heterogeneity
- **Outcomes**: recurrence frequency and time to recurrence, maximum interincisal opening, pain scores, jaw function, clicking/sound phenomena, patient-reported outcomes, need for subsequent intervention, adverse events, plus procedural detail (site, volume, sessions)

**Appraisal and mechanism**

- JBI case-series items were mostly "Yes", but inclusion criteria, consecutive/complete enrolment and demographic reporting were Unclear or "No" for Xu 1992 and Yoshida 2018; Machon 2018 (the only quasi-experimental study) had every item Yes except "outcomes measured in a reliable way" (Unclear)
- All three agents are rationalised identically in practice — provoke local inflammation, clot or sclerosing injury, then periarticular fibrosis that limits excessive condylar translation — and the authors label this rationale largely theoretical, with response magnitude potentially depending on site, volume and concentration, number of sessions, baseline joint characteristics and any adjunctive immobilisation

**Exclusions that shape the picture (Table A1)**

- wrong population (general TMD or hypermobility without stratified recurrent-dislocation data): Majumdar 2016, Assiri 2025, Singh 2024, Refai 2011, Simsek 2016, Aishwarya 2024, Triantafillidou 2012
- wrong route: Kotimäki 2011 and Gilles 2000 (intramuscular botulinum toxin, not intra-articular)
- effect not isolable: Yang 1999 (arthroscopic sclerotherapy + suturing), Moussa 2022
- post-procedural immobilisation — the largest single exclusion block, including randomised trials **Hegab 2013** and **Chhapane 2024**, plus Machon 2009, Coser 2015, Candirli 2012/2013, Daif 2010, Hasson 2001, Pandey 2022, Gagnani 2020, Pinto 2009, and others
