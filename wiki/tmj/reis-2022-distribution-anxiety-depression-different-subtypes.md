## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42020150562) of 24 studies (20 pooled; 3678 subjects) comparing anxiety and depression across RDC/TMD or DC/TMD subtypes, with myofascial pain as the comparison group.

Anxiety and depression were more frequently distributed in myofascial pain patients (p = .001), and TMD without myofascial pain had less severe levels than myofascial-pain-only patients (p <= .01), despite moderate-to-high heterogeneity; most individual studies found similar occurrence across subtypes.

Psychological screening appears essential when managing muscular TMD, but the observational design, heterogeneity and instrument dependence limit causal or quantitative inference (abstract-level only).

## 세줄요약

RDC/TMD·DC/TMD로 진단한 측두하악관절장애 (Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD) 아형별 불안·우울 분포를 근막통 (myofascial pain) 군과 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA; 24편, 메타분석 20편, 3678명).

불안·우울은 근막통 환자에서 더 빈번했고 (p = .001), 근막통이 없는 TMD는 근막통 단독군보다 불안·우울 정도가 낮았다 (p <= .01); 이질성은 중등도~높음이며 개별 연구 다수는 아형 간 유사하다고 보고했다.

근육형 TMD 환자에서는 심리 상태 평가가 중요하지만, 관찰연구 기반·이질성·평가도구 의존성 때문에 인과 해석이나 정량적 해석에는 한계가 있다 (초록 수준 정보만 확인).

## Summary

Source: PubMed, [DOI 10.1111/joor.13331](https://doi.org/10.1111/joor.13331) (J Oral Rehabil 2022;49(7):754-767). This PROSPERO-registered, PRISMA 2020 systematic review searched PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus and SciELO (last search 12 March 2021) for studies that diagnosed TMD with RDC/TMD or DC/TMD, measured anxiety and/or depression with validated instruments, and compared at least two TMD subtypes including a myofascial pain group. Of 4086 records, 24 studies were included (3678 subjects) and 20 were meta-analysed. Anxiety and depression were more frequently distributed in myofascial pain patients (p = .001), and TMD without myofascial pain showed less severe anxiety/depression than myofascial-pain-only patients (p <= .01). The authors conclude that psychological assessment is essential for subtype-appropriate management. Only the abstract was available; pooled effect sizes are not reported here.

## Key Contributions

- First pooled subtype-by-subtype comparison of anxiety/depression in TMD (per the abstract's framing that a thorough review was missing).
- Myofascial pain (muscular TMD) carries a higher anxiety/depression burden than other subtypes.
- Assessment instrument type affects measured levels.

## Methodology

PROSPERO CRD42020150562; PRISMA 2020; four databases plus reference lists; RevMan 5.3.5 with random- or fixed-effects models (alpha 0.05); quality by a 10-item prevalence-study appraisal tool and Newcastle-Ottawa scale. 24 studies, 20 in meta-analysis, 3678 subjects.
