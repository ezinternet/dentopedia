## One-line Summary

Single-group pre-post study (n=15 patients, 26 clinically+radiographically confirmed TMJ osteoarthritis joints), Korean journal (JKAOMS); a 6-month home-based physiotherapy program (heat, massage, stretching, mobilization, strengthening) significantly reduced pain (VAS, p=0.001) and improved Helkimo functionality from severe to minimal dysfunction (p=0.001), with minimal rescue-NSAID use and increased ultrasound disc visibility.

## 한줄요약

단일군 전후비교 연구(n=15명, 26개 임상+방사선 확진 TMJ 골관절염 관절), 대한구강악안면외과학회지(JKAOMS) 게재; 6개월 가정 물리치료 프로그램(온열·마사지·스트레칭·관절가동·강화운동)으로 통증(VAS, p=0.001)과 Helkimo 기능지수(중증→경증, p=0.001)가 유의 개선, NSAID 사용은 미미하고 초음파상 관절원판 관찰률도 증가했다.

## Summary

This is a single-group, pre-post experimental (prospective) study from Mexico's National Institute of Rehabilitation, published in the *Journal of the Korean Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons*. It targets a strictly defined TMJ osteoarthritis (OA) subgroup (RDC/TMD Axis I Group IIIb, confirmed by both clinical exam and radiography by two independent readers), distinguishing it from most TMD exercise-therapy literature that pools heterogeneous diagnoses. Fifteen patients (26 joints, all female, median age 57y) completed a fully described 6-month home exercise program — thermotherapy, intra-/extraoral massage, stretching, free joint mobilization, and isometric strengthening — taught in a single supervised session and reinforced with an exercise diary and weekly phone check-ins. Outcomes spanned pain (VAS), function (Helkimo index), and structure (musculoskeletal ultrasound). Both pain and function improved sharply within the first month and were sustained through 6 months, with negligible rescue-analgesic use, supporting home exercise as a low-cost, low-risk first-line conservative option for TMJ OA specifically (not just TMD broadly).

## Key Contributions

- First study (per the authors) to combine pain + Helkimo function + ultrasound structural outcomes in a cohort restricted to dual clinical-and-radiographic-confirmed TMJ OA, rather than mixed TMD.
- Fully replicable 6-component home exercise protocol with adherence tracking (diary + weekly calls), achieving >3 sessions/week sustained adherence through 6 months with zero dropout.
- Demonstrates near-complete pain/function normalization within 1 month (Helkimo severe → free-of-problems), sustained to 6 months, with minimal need for rescue NSAIDs — direct evidence against the assumption that TMJ OA necessarily requires long-term pharmacologic or invasive management.
- Adds ultrasound structural follow-up (disc visibility rose 38.5%→57.7%, opening distance increased) alongside clinical outcomes — most TMD exercise trials report pain/function only.
- Provides OA-subtype-specific evidence directly relevant to the Cochrane TMJ-OA review's gap (that review excluded exercise/physiotherapy for insufficient evidence quality).

## Methodology

- Design: single-group experimental pre-post study (no control arm) — this is a prospective cohort, **not** an RCT; treat effect estimates as susceptible to regression-to-the-mean and no placebo control.
- Cohort: 52 screened → 37 excluded (failed radiographic OA criteria) → 15 patients / 26 joints analyzed; inclusion required RDC/TMD Axis I Group IIIb clinical criteria (pain at rest/opening/movement + crepitus) **and** radiographic confirmation (joint-space reduction, subchondral sclerosis, lateral flattening, condylar surface change, or osteophytes) agreed by two independent observers.
- Intervention (6 months, 3–5×/week, self-administered after one supervised teaching session by two physical therapists): 15-min superficial heat → intraoral massage (10 reps) → extraoral oil massage + neck massage (10 reps) → stretching (mouth-opening and lateral cheek-deviation holds, 10s/5s×3) → free joint mobilization (opening/closing, protrusion/retrusion, laterality, 10 reps each) → isometric strengthening (resisted opening, protrusion, lateralization, molar-bite elevation, 10s/5s×3).
- Rescue medication: acetaminophen up to 1g/6h permitted and logged for VAS >50mm; other OA medications washed out 7 days pre-study.
- Outcome assessment at baseline, 1, 3, 6 months: VAS (palpation/opening/rest), Helkimo index, diagnostic ultrasound (echogenicity, disc visibility, Doppler/synovitis, osteophytes, opening distance).
- Statistics: Wilcoxon (paired), Friedman (within-group over time), Mann-Whitney U, chi-square/Fisher's exact; α=0.05.

## Results

- **Pain (VAS, median mm)**: rest pain fell from 60 (baseline) with a 57.5mm drop by month 1, palpation and opening pain fell by 47.5–51mm in month 1; differences sustained to month 6 (palpation 48, opening 49.5, rest 42.5), overall p=0.001; no significant change detected between months 1–3 or 3–6, but a significant difference between months 1–6.
- **Function (Helkimo index, median total)**: 17 (severe dysfunction) at baseline → 0.25 (free of TMJ problems) at month 1 → 2 (minimal dysfunction) at months 3 and 6 (p=0.001).
- **Structure (ultrasound)**: no change in tissue echogenicity/linearity (i.e., no detectable direct cartilage effect); osteophytes stable at 2/26 joints (7.7%); disc visualization rose from 38.5% to 57.7% of joints by 6 months; mouth-opening distance on ultrasound significantly increased by 6 months.
- **Adherence & safety**: mean 4.52±1.23 sessions/week (3 months), 3.62±0.41 sessions/week (6 months), zero losses to follow-up; rescue acetaminophen use was low and did not change significantly across follow-up (p=0.12) — 20.0% of patients at month 1, 0% at month 3, 26.7% at month 6, median 1.25 days of use.
- **Baseline radiographic burden (26 joints)**: joint-space reduction 92.3%, osteophytes 61.5%, subchondral sclerosis 53.8%, lateral flattening 38.5%, condylar-surface change 26.9%.
