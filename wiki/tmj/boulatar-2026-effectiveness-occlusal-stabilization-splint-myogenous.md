## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA; MEDLINE via PubMed and Web of Science, final search May 2022) of 10 RCTs (539 participants, mean follow-up 6 months) on stabilization splints in myogenous TMD.

Most trials reported symptom improvement versus baseline across pain (10 studies), mouth opening (5), headache (2) and quality of life (4); no pooled estimate is reported in the abstract, and overall evidence quality was low (moderate to high risk of bias, small samples).

Splints appeared effective for signs and symptoms, but low-certainty, baseline-referenced evidence cannot separate splint-specific effect from natural course or placebo; larger prospective studies are needed.

## 세줄요약

근육성 측두하악장애 (Myogenous Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에서 교합안정장치 (Stabilization Splint) 효과를 본 체계적 문헌고찰 (PRISMA; MEDLINE·Web of Science, 최종 검색 2022년 5월), RCT 10편·539명·평균 추적 6개월.

대부분의 시험이 통증(10편)·개구량(5편)·두통(2편)·삶의 질(4편)에서 기저치 대비 호전을 보고했으나, 초록에는 통합 효과추정치가 없고 근거 질은 낮음 (중등도-높은 비뚤림 위험, 소표본).

장치가 효과적으로 보였으나 근거 확실성이 낮고 기저치 대비 호전이라 장치 고유 효과와 자연경과·위약효과를 구분할 수 없음; 대규모 전향 연구 필요.

## Summary

Source: PubMed, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.17219/dmp/194530) (Dent Med Probl 2026;63(2):509-516). This narrative systematic review (no meta-analysis reported) included 10 RCTs with 539 participants and about 6 months mean follow-up. Outcomes: pain reduction (10 studies), mouth opening (5), headache (2), quality of life (4). Most trials improved from baseline; the authors rate overall evidence quality low because of moderate to high risk of bias and small samples, and conclude splints "appeared" effective while calling for large prospective studies.

## Key Contributions

- Subtype-specific (myogenous TMD) framing of stabilization-splint RCT evidence.
- Adds headache and quality-of-life outcomes to the wiki's splint evidence, which mostly tracks pain and mouth opening.

## Methodology

PRISMA-guided; MEDLINE via PubMed and Web of Science; last search May 2022. Comparators, diagnostic criteria, risk-of-bias tool and synthesis method are not given in the abstract.

## Results

- 10 RCTs, n=539, mean follow-up 6 months.
- Improvement versus baseline reported by most trials; no pooled effect sizes in the abstract.
- Overall evidence quality: low.

## What This Paper Adds vs Held Pages

- [[tmj/zhang-2020-occlusal-splint-tmd-rct-sr]] (general TMD, RCTs, GRADE low-moderate) found splints beat no treatment but not non-occluding splints. This SR narrows to myogenous TMD but, from the abstract, rests on baseline-referenced improvement, so it is weaker on the specificity question and does not overturn or supersede Zhang 2020 or [[tmj/singh-2024-occlusal-interventions-tmd-cochrane-sr]] (very low certainty, no advantage over placebo splint).
- [[tmj/cimino-2025-counselling-vs-splint-tmd-neck-pain-rct]] is one RCT showing no added cervical benefit of a splint over counselling; it is compatible with this SR (different question: add-on to counselling, neck outcomes).
- Search ended May 2022, so 2022-2026 trials are not captured.

No supersession and no contradiction: the positive but low-certainty conclusion is consistent with the held conditional-adjunct view.
