## 한국어 핵심요약

> [!summary] 한국어 핵심요약
> - **결론/thesis**: 후방조직 (retrodiscal region)은 균질한 "이중판대 (bilaminar zone)"가 아니라 역학·생화학적으로 뚜렷이 다른 두 부착부 — 후상방 (posterior superior attachment, PSA)·후하방 (posterior inferior attachment, PIA) — 로 나뉘며, 각기 다른 기능을 한다.
> - **abstract-only**: PMC 전문 미확보, 초록의 비율값만.
> - **핵심 수치**: PIA는 PSA보다 강성 2.13배·강도 2.30배, 건조중량당 콜라겐 1.93배·가교 1.78배. 외측 원판은 글리코사미노글리칸 (GAG) 최고(부착부의 1.48–5.39배), 콜라겐 이방성 최고. PSA는 가장 이질적이고 지방 많음.

## Three-line Summary

Structural, tensile, biochemical, and collagen-crosslinking characterization of the two posterior attachments (posterior superior attachment, PSA; posterior inferior attachment, PIA) and the lateral disc in the Yucatan minipig, a standard large-animal TMJ model. The PIA was 2.13× stiffer and 2.30× stronger than the PSA, 1.93× more collagenous by dry weight, and 1.78× more crosslinked; the PSA was the most heterogeneous and fattiest region, while the lateral disc had the highest glycosaminoglycan content (1.48–5.39× the attachments) and the most anisotropic collagen. The attachments are less fibrocartilaginous than the disc but still load-bearing, supporting a division of function — the stiffer PIA anchors the disc to the condyle during articulation while the softer PSA allows translation over the articular eminence. *(abstract-only — full text not retrieved.)*

## Summary

*abstract-only — publisher/PMC full text not retrieved; content below is built from the structured abstract and impact statement.*

The retrodiscal region is often described as a single "bilaminar zone," but anatomically it is two attachments with different tissue-to-tissue connections: a superior lamina toward the temporal bone and an inferior lamina toward the mandibular condyle. This study characterized both — plus the lateral disc — in the Yucatan minipig across structure, tensile mechanics, biochemistry, and collagen crosslinking, to build design targets for tissue-engineered TMJ disc-complex replacements.

The two attachments are quantitatively distinct. The posterior inferior attachment is roughly twice as stiff and twice as strong as the posterior superior attachment, and is nearly twice as collagenous and substantially more crosslinked. The posterior superior attachment is the softest, fattiest, and most heterogeneous of the three regions. The lateral disc stands apart from both, with far higher glycosaminoglycan content and the most anisotropic collagen alignment. The authors read this as a functional division of labor: the stiff inferior attachment tethers the disc to the condyle so they move together, while the compliant superior attachment stretches to let the disc translate forward over the articular eminence during opening — and it recoils to help return the disc on closing.

## Key Contributions

1. Separates the retrodiscal region into PSA and PIA and shows they differ ~2× in stiffness and strength.
2. Functional model: stiff PIA = disc-to-condyle anchor; compliant PSA = permits translation over the eminence.
3. Tissue-engineering design criteria for the TMJ disc complex (per-region moduli, collagen/GAG, crosslink density).
4. Establishes the Yucatan minipig disc-complex baseline.
5. Confirms disc attachments are less fibrocartilaginous than the disc yet still mechanically contributory.

## Methodology

- Yucatan minipig; three locations: PSA, PIA, lateral disc.
- Tensile testing (stiffness, strength); collagen alignment / anisotropy; biochemistry (collagen and GAG per dry weight); collagen crosslinking per dry weight; heterogeneity and fat-vacuole proportion.

## Results

| Comparison | Result |
|---|---|
| PIA vs PSA — tensile stiffness | PIA 2.13× |
| PIA vs PSA — tensile strength | PIA 2.30× |
| Collagen / dry weight | PIA 1.93×, lateral disc 1.91× vs PSA |
| Crosslinking / dry weight | PIA 1.78× vs PSA |
| GAG / dry weight | lateral disc 1.48× vs PIA, 5.39× vs PSA |
| Collagen alignment | lateral disc most anisotropic; attachments primarily mediolateral |
| Heterogeneity / fat vacuoles | PSA highest |
