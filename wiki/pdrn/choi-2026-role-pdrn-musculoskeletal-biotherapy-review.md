## Summary

Choi and Yoo (Int J Mol Sci 2026) synthesize the mechanistic and clinical evidence for PDRN as a biotherapy across three musculoskeletal indications. PDRN operates through two complementary pathways: (1) adenosine A2A receptor (A2AR) agonism, which suppresses NF-κB-mediated inflammation and downregulates TNF-α and IL-1β; and (2) the nucleotide salvage pathway, which provides purines for cell proliferation and DNA repair. Downstream effects include VEGF-mediated angiogenesis and collagen synthesis, making PDRN broadly relevant to any tissue-regeneration context including dentistry. In osteoarthritis, PDRN reduces cartilage degradation and promotes chondrogenic differentiation of mesenchymal stem cells. In tendinopathies (Achilles and rotator cuff), preclinical and early clinical evidence support tissue repair. In spinal radiculopathy, PDRN offers a preliminary non-steroidal alternative. Dosing protocols remain unstandardized across indications.

## Key Contributions

- Consolidated A2AR + nucleotide salvage dual mechanism applicable to dental regenerative contexts (socket healing, peri-implant regeneration, soft tissue augmentation)
- Osteoarthritis: PDRN reduces cartilage matrix degradation and promotes chondrogenesis — shared pathway with alveolar bone regeneration
- Tendinopathy: Achilles and rotator cuff repair via VEGF angiogenesis and collagen synthesis — same molecular drivers as periodontal soft-tissue healing
- Safety: favorable preliminary profile across all musculoskeletal indications; no serious adverse events reported
- Gap: no standardized dosing protocol — large-scale RCTs comparing PDRN to existing treatments needed

## Methodology

Narrative review; scope: osteoarthritis, tendinopathies (Achilles, rotator cuff), spinal radiculopathy; mechanism synthesis from preclinical + clinical studies; no systematic search or formal inclusion criteria.

## Results

| PDRN Mechanism | Effect |
|---|---|
| A2AR agonism (→ cAMP ↑, NF-κB ↓) | Anti-inflammatory: TNF-α ↓, IL-1β ↓ |
| Nucleotide salvage pathway | Cell proliferation, DNA repair |
| VEGF stimulation | Angiogenesis, tissue revascularization |
| Collagen synthesis ↑ | Extracellular matrix repair |

Indication-specific outcomes:
- **Osteoarthritis**: cartilage degradation ↓, chondrogenic MSC differentiation ↑
- **Tendinopathies**: tissue repair confirmed in preclinical + early clinical (Achilles, rotator cuff)
- **Radiculopathy**: preliminary pain reduction; no RCT vs corticosteroids yet
