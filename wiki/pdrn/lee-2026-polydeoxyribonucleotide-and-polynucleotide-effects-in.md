## Three-line Summary

Systematic review without meta-analysis of 38 studies (28 clinical, 10 preclinical) on PDRN/PN injection for musculoskeletal and related neurological pain, from 13,267 records across 10 databases (2010–2026).

Within-group pain (VAS/NRS) and function (WOMAC/MMO) improved, but between-group significance varied by anatomical site and comparator — especially hyaluronic acid (HA) — and GRADE certainty for pain was low to very low in every controlled comparison.

Not a dental trial — TMJ is one of seven sites; heterogeneous designs and incomplete/inconsistent adverse-event reporting preclude meta-analysis and limit generalizability (24/28 clinical studies from Korea).

## Summary

This systematic review synthesized 38 studies (28 clinical, 10 preclinical; published 2010–2026) identified from 13,267 records across 10 databases, evaluating polydeoxyribonucleotide (PDRN) and polynucleotide (PN) injection for musculoskeletal and related neurological pain. It is a cross-domain musculoskeletal review, not a dental trial: the temporomandibular joint (TMJ) is one of seven clinical anatomical sites (3 studies), alongside knee, shoulder, wrist, ankle/foot, elbow, and spine. Pain (visual analog scale, VAS / numeric rating scale, NRS) and function (Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index, WOMAC / maximum mouth opening, MMO) generally improved within groups, but between-group significance was inconsistent and comparator-dependent — PDRN/PN sometimes outperformed hyaluronic acid (HA) or saline, yet not consistently against cross-linked HA or corticosteroids. Preclinical studies support an adenosine A2A receptor (A2AR)–cAMP–PKA–CREB mechanism with M1 suppression, M2 activation, VEGF/FGF/TGF-β1 upregulation, type I collagen formation, and caspase-3/9 inhibition, translating into improved tendon–bone load-to-failure. The authors conclude that benefits are preliminary: GRADE certainty for pain was low to very low in all controlled comparisons, and no serious attributable adverse events were reported although adverse-event assessment and reporting were incomplete and inconsistent.

## Key Contributions

- Integrates PDRN and PN in one systematic review while explicitly distinguishing them by molecular weight (PDRN 50–1,500 kDa; PN >1,500 kDa) and by clinical behavior, correcting their habitual conflation.
- Searches 10 databases across four language groups per PRISMA, with two independent reviewers, design-specific risk-of-bias tools (RoB 2, ROBINS-I, NHLBI, JBI, SYRCLE), and GRADE certainty ratings.
- Refuses to equate within-group baseline improvement with between-group treatment effect; stratifies findings by design strength (controlled / single-arm / case series).
- Maps preclinical A2AR–cAMP–PKA–CREB mechanisms onto clinical functional outcomes and documents the site-specific heterogeneity, HA-comparator inconsistency, and adverse-event reporting gaps — including the small, mixed TMJ evidence base.

## Methodology

- **Design**: systematic review, qualitative synthesis only (no meta-analysis owing to clinical and methodological heterogeneity). Registration: PROSPERO CRD420261363033 and CRD420261363211.
- **Databases**: Ovid-Medline, Ovid-EMBASE, Ovid-AMED, Cochrane, CNKI, CiNii, KoreaMed, KMBASE, OASIS, Science On; search March 2026, no language/year restriction.
- **Flow**: 13,267 records → 10,023 after deduplication → 114 full-text → 38 included (28 clinical, 10 preclinical).
- **Population/outcomes**: adults ≥18 with musculoskeletal or related neurological pain; comparators unrestricted (HA, corticosteroids, placebo, exercise, dextrose). Outcomes: VAS, NRS, WOMAC, MMO, EQ-5D; preclinical molecular, histological, and biomechanical endpoints.

## Results

| Outcome / domain | Finding (from full text) |
|---|---|
| Search yield | 13,267 records → 38 included (28 clinical, 10 preclinical), 2010–2026 |
| Clinical sites | Knee 11 (OA 9), shoulder 4, TMJ 3, wrist 3, ankle/foot 3, elbow 2, spine 2; HA was control in 7 knee studies |
| TMJ — Choi 2026 | PDRN vs 10% hypertonic dextrose (n = 66): VAS 4.70 ± 1.78 → 0.98 ± 1.21, p < 0.001 within group; **no between-group difference** (final VAS p = 0.936); MMO NS |
| TMJ — Jang 2025 | Single-arm pericapsular PDRN (n = 111): NRS 4.36 ± 2.35 → 1.48 ± 1.86; acute Δ −3.74 vs chronic −2.64; MMO improved, no control |
| TMJ — Cenzato 2024 | PN + HA vs physiotherapy (n = 60): VAS −41.7% at T3 (p = 0.006), better than physiotherapy at 12 wk (p < 0.0001); baseline imbalance (p < 0.001); MMO NS, asymmetry significant |
| Knee comparators | Moon 2023 PN > HA (adj p = 0.048) and > cross-linked HA (adj p = 0.016); Yoon 2019 PDRN + HA > HA (p = 0.0001); Dallari 2020 PN + HA vs HA WOMAC NS; Kim T.W. 2023 PN vs HMWHA NS (p = 0.296) |
| Ankle/foot | Kim & Chung 2015 PDRN > placebo only at 12 wk (p < 0.001); Lee 2020 PDRN inferior to corticosteroid at 2 and 6 wk (p = 0.010, 0.016), NS at 6 mo |
| GRADE certainty | Low to very low in all 7 controlled comparisons; none moderate/high |
| Risk of bias | RoB 2: 1/10 low, 2/10 high, rest "some concerns"; SYRCLE: sequence generation/allocation/blinding mostly "unclear" |
| Geography | 24/28 (86%) clinical studies Korea, 4/28 Italy; all 10 preclinical Korea |
| Safety | No serious attributable AEs reported, but AE assessment/reporting incomplete and inconsistent; most preclinical studies did not report AEs |
