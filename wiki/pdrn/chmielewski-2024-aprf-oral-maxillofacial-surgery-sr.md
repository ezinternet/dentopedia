## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (38 articles, PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD42024584161) evaluating A-PRF (1500 rpm × 14 min, no anticoagulants) applications across oral and maxillofacial surgery indications including extractions, free gingival grafts, ridge augmentation, and implant surgery; search cutoff November 2024.

A-PRF consistently demonstrated lower postoperative pain vs L-PRF or blood clot controls, positive effects on graft integration, superior epithelialization of free gingival graft donor sites, and sustained growth factor release for up to 10 days (vs shorter for PRF); included study sizes ranged n=10–102.

A-PRF is a reliable biological adjunct to oral surgical protocols; current evidence is preliminary due to small samples and lack of blinding — well-designed larger RCTs with long follow-up are needed before definitive clinical recommendations.

## 세줄요약

체계적 고찰(38편, PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD42024584161): A-PRF(1500 rpm × 14분, 무항응고제) 적용을 구강·악안면외과 전 분야(발치·유리치은이식·능선 증대·임플란트 수술)에 걸쳐 평가; 검색 마감 2024년 11월.

A-PRF는 L-PRF·혈병 대조군 대비 수술 후 통증 감소, 이식재 통합 개선, 유리치은이식 공여부 상피화 촉진, 성장인자 최대 10일 방출(PRF 대비 우수)을 일관되게 보임; 포함 연구 표본 크기 n=10–102.

A-PRF는 신뢰할 수 있는 생물학적 보조재이나 소표본·비맹검 한계로 권고 강도 제한 — 최종 임상 권고를 위해 대규모 맹검 RCT가 필요.

## Summary
This PRISMA-compliant systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42024584161) evaluated A-PRF applications across oral and maxillofacial surgery indications from 38 included studies published through November 2024. A-PRF (1500 rpm × 14 min centrifugation, no anticoagulants) produces a looser fibrin matrix with more interfibrous space than PRF, enabling sustained growth factor release for up to 10 days. Across included studies (n=10–102 patients), A-PRF consistently demonstrated lower postoperative pain vs L-PRF or blood clot controls, positive effects on graft integration, superior epithelialization of free gingival graft donor sites, and enhanced wound healing. The review concludes A-PRF is a reliable biological adjunct to surgical protocols but recommends well-designed RCTs with larger cohorts and longer follow-up for definitive clinical recommendations.

## Key Contributions
- A-PRF confirmed superior to PRF in growth factor release duration (up to 10 days vs shorter for PRF)
- Lower postoperative pain vs L-PRF or blood clot in multiple study comparisons
- Positive graft integration effect: A-PRF as additive to biomaterials improves outcomes
- Free gingival graft: A-PRF protects donor sites and promotes more efficient epithelialization
- A-PRF generates more PDGF, VEGF, FGF, TGF-β, BMPs, and MMP-9 than PRF due to slower centrifugation

## Methodology
- Design: systematic review (PRISMA); PROSPERO CRD42024584161
- 38 articles included; search cutoff: 11 November 2024
- Study sizes: smallest n=10, largest n=102
- Comparators: L-PRF, blood clot, conventional healing
- Indications: extractions, free gingival grafts, ridge augmentation, implant surgery, maxillofacial procedures
- A-PRF protocol: venous blood in vacuum tubes → 1500 rpm × 14 min (no anticoagulants)

## Results
- Pain: lower in A-PRF vs comparators across multiple studies
- Graft integration: positive effect confirmed as additive to biomaterials
- Free gingival graft donor sites: superior epithelialization vs comparators
- Wound healing: enhanced; soft tissue management and bone preservation noted
- Growth factor release: up to 10 days — more sustained than PRF/L-PRF families
- Limitation: no large-sample blinded RCT; evidence preliminary for most individual indications
