## Three-line Summary
Animal experimental study; 16 New Zealand White rabbits with four 6-mm calvarial defects each, randomized to control versus three commercial polydeoxyribonucleotide (PDRN) formulations added to bone graft and membrane.
Bone volume-to-tissue volume ratio (BV/TV) at 2 weeks was highest with PDRN-A (22.85%±0.88% vs control 18.24%±3.12%); at 6 weeks PDRN-A (25.33%±2.82%) and PDRN-B (23.84%±2.25%) exceeded control (19.33%±1.94%).
First head-to-head formulation comparison under standardized conditions; all three PDRN products showed broadly similar trends with PDRN-A highest, but small sample, early time points, and a non-human model limit clinical extrapolation.

## Summary

This animal experimental study compared three commercially available polydeoxyribonucleotide (PDRN) formulations with a control bone graft/membrane alone for early bone regeneration in a rabbit calvarial defect model. Sixteen New Zealand White rabbits each received four standardized 6-mm calvarial defects, randomized to control, PDRN-A (Zerone Cellvane), PDRN-B (PharmaResearch), or PDRN-C (BR Pharm), with graft blocks soaked in 0.5 mL of PDRN solution for 10 minutes; animals were euthanized at 2 and 6 weeks. Micro-computed tomography (micro-CT) bone volume-to-tissue volume ratio (BV/TV) was significantly higher with PDRN-A at 2 weeks (22.85%±0.88% vs control 18.24%±3.12%) and with PDRN-A and PDRN-B at 6 weeks (25.33%±2.82% and 23.84%±2.25% vs control 19.33%±1.94%), and histomorphometry showed the most new bone with PDRN-A. As the first head-to-head comparison under standardized conditions, it directly addresses the formulation-standardization gap in the PDRN literature, while its small sample, early endpoints, and preclinical model preclude direct clinical extrapolation.

## Key Contributions
- **First head-to-head comparison of three commercial PDRN formulations under identical graft, membrane, defect, and soaking conditions** — directly fills the dose/vehicle/formulation-standardization gap repeatedly flagged in PDRN bone-healing reviews.
- BV/TV advantage for PDRN-A at 2 weeks and for PDRN-A/PDRN-B at 6 weeks versus graft+membrane control, with significant between-group ANOVA at both points (P=0.0019 and P=0.0004).
- Histomorphometric confirmation: PDRN-A had the highest new bone (NB) fraction in the total augmented area at both time points, and all three PDRN formulations exceeded control in the central region at 2 weeks.
- Hypothesis-generating signal that molecular-weight distribution, purification, and raw-material source may produce different biological performance among PDRN products, with PDRN-A trending highest.

## Methodology
- **Design**: Randomized controlled animal experiment; four 6-mm circular calvarial defects per rabbit (n=16 rabbits; 8 per healing period; 8 defects per group per time point).
- **Groups**: Control (Osteon Xeno Collagen graft + Collagen Membrane 2), PDRN-A, PDRN-B, PDRN-C (same graft/membrane plus one of three commercial PDRN products). Graft blocks (6 mm × 4 mm) soaked in 0.5 mL PDRN solution for 10 minutes before placement.
- **Primary outcome**: BV/TV (%) by micro-CT (SkyScan 1173, CTAn; grayscale threshold 55–225 — total mineralized tissue, not new bone alone).
- **Secondary outcome**: Histomorphometry (Masson trichrome, ImageJ) of NB, BG, BG+NB, CT, VS in the total augmented area and a 1.6-mm central region.
- **Statistics**: 1-way ANOVA + Tukey post hoc for between-group comparisons; independent t-test for 2-vs-6-week within-group comparisons; G*Power sample size (Cohen's f=0.75, α=0.05, 90% power); significance P<0.05.
