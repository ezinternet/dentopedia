## Three-line Summary
Mechanistic in-vitro/in-vivo study (HepG2, RAW 264.7, MC3T3-E1 cells; male ICR mice n=30, 6/group, oral PDRN 20/30/40 mg/kg × 5 weeks) of systemic PDRN on longitudinal growth — non-dental cross-domain growth-plate research.
PDRN increased HepG2 IGF-1 mRNA and JAK2/STAT5 phosphorylation (p<0.05), blocked by A2AR antagonist ZM241385; mice given 30/40 mg/kg gained more nose-to-anus and nose-to-tail length with thicker growth plates and higher serum IGF-1 while GH/IGFBP-3 stayed unchanged (p<0.05).
For a dental wiki the value is mechanistic only — systemic longitudinal growth must not be extrapolated to alveolar/dental bone regeneration; oral pharmacokinetics and in-vivo A2AR involvement remain unverified.

## Summary

This is a **non-dental, cross-domain** systemic growth-plate paper: Jeong et al. tested whether oral polydeoxyribonucleotide (PDRN) promotes longitudinal skeletal growth in growing male ICR mice (n=30, 6 per group; 20/30/40 mg/kg daily gavage for 35 days) and probed the mechanism in HepG2 hepatocytes, RAW 264.7 osteoclast precursors and MC3T3-E1 osteoblasts. In HepG2 cells PDRN raised IGF-1 mRNA and JAK2/STAT5 phosphorylation (p<0.05), and the selective 아데노신 A2A 수용체 (Adenosine A2A Receptor, A2AR) antagonist ZM241385 attenuated both responses, supporting A2AR-mediated hepatic signaling. In vivo, 30 and 40 mg/kg increased nose-to-anus and nose-to-tail length gain, proximal tibial growth-plate thickness and serum 인슐린유사성장인자-1 (Insulin-like Growth Factor 1, IGF-1) without changing GH or IGFBP-3, accompanied by improved trabecular/cortical microarchitecture on 미세전산화단층촬영 (Micro-Computed Tomography, micro-CT). The wiki-relevant contribution is mechanistic — A2AR antagonist confirmation of the JAK2-STAT5/IGF-1 axis plus complementary osteoclast/osteoblast marker data — **not a dental indication**: systemic longitudinal growth must not be equated with dental/alveolar bone regeneration, and oral absorption/pharmacokinetics and in-vivo A2AR involvement were not established.

## Key Contributions

- A2AR mediation in the hepatic IGF-1 axis confirmed pharmacologically: ZM241385 (1 µM) suppressed PDRN-induced p-JAK2/p-STAT5 and IGF-1 mRNA in HepG2 cells (p<0.05).
- Oral PDRN for 5 weeks increased systemic length gain, growth-plate thickness and serum IGF-1 **without** altering GH or IGFBP-3 — a GH-sparing/IGF-1-associated pattern.
- Structural correlates: improved tibial/femoral BMD, BV/TV, Tb.Th, Tb.N, Conn.D and reduced Tb.Sp/total porosity on micro-CT.
- Remodeling markers: LPS-induced DC-STAMP/TRAP/CatK suppressed in RAW 264.7; ALP, OCN, OPN, RUNX2, OSX, COL1A1, OPG and IGF-1 up and RANKL down in MC3T3-E1 (p<0.05).
- Extends the in-vitro osteoblast-selectivity finding of [[pdrn/jeon-2026-pdrn-osteoblast-differentiation-osteoclastogenesis]] with an antagonist-based A2AR confirmation and systemic/osteoclast axes.
- **Scope warning**: no dental, alveolar, implant, periodontal or socket-healing endpoint — cross-domain mechanistic reuse only.

## Methodology

- **Design**: mechanistic cell-signaling experiments followed by an in-vivo juvenile-mouse growth model (not a clinical trial); `evidence_level: in-vivo`.
- **In vitro**: HepG2 (human hepatocellular carcinoma) for IGF-1/JAK2-STAT5 and ZM241385 blockade; RAW 264.7 (murine pre-osteoclast, LPS 100 ng/mL) for osteoclast markers; MC3T3-E1 (murine pre-osteoblast, OD medium) for osteogenic markers.
- **PDRN**: salmon-milt derived, >80% purity, 150–400 bp (~97.5–260 kDa); ZM241385 1 µM, 30 min pretreatment.
- **In vivo**: 4-week-old male ICR mice, five groups (normal; PDRN 20/30/40 mg/kg; Humulus japonicus extract 100 mg/kg positive control), N=6/group (total 30), daily oral gavage × 35 days.
- **Readouts**: MTT, qRT-PCR (GAPDH-normalized), Western blot (GAPDH-normalized in vivo), serum IGF-1/IGFBP-3/GH ELISA, serum biochemistry (ALT/AST/BUN/CK/LDH/glucose), high-resolution micro-CT (SkyScan 1276, 10.5 µm voxels).
- **Statistics**: G*Power-derived n=6 (>80% power, α=0.05); one-way ANOVA + Dunnett, two-way repeated-measures ANOVA for body weight; mean ± SEM; p<0.05.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| HepG2 IGF-1 mRNA | Dose-dependent increase, p<0.05; attenuated by ZM241385 (p<0.05) |
| HepG2 JAK2/STAT5 | p-JAK2 and p-STAT5 increased without total-protein change; ZM241385 suppressed both (p<0.05) |
| Hepatic IGF-1 mRNA (in vivo) | Increased, significant at 40 mg/kg vs normal (p<0.05); IGFBP-3 unchanged |
| Hepatic JAK2/STAT5 (in vivo) | Total and phosphorylated JAK2/STAT5 elevated (p<0.05); GAPDH-normalized |
| Body length | N-A and N-T gains higher at PDRN 30 and 40 mg/kg (p<0.05); 30 vs 40 not significantly different; no body-weight change |
| Serum IGF-1 / GH / IGFBP-3 | IGF-1 elevated (p<0.05); GH and IGFBP-3 unchanged |
| Growth plate | Proximal tibial thickness and radiolucent band increased (p<0.05); local IGF-1 and BMP-2 mRNA up (p<0.05) |
| Micro-CT (tibia/femur) | Tibial length and trabecular BMD up; BV/TV, Tb.Th, Tb.N, Conn.D up; Tb.Sp and porosity down (all p<0.05); cortical tibial/femoral BMD markedly higher at 40 mg/kg (no p-value stated) |
| Serum safety panel | ALT, AST, BUN, CK, LDH, glucose — no significant changes |
| RAW 264.7 (LPS) | DC-STAMP, TRAP, CatK induced by LPS (p<0.05) and suppressed by PDRN dose-dependently (p<0.05) |
| MC3T3-E1 (OD) | Viability rescued vs OD; ALP, OCN, OPN, RUNX2, OSX, COL1A1, OPG, IGF-1 up and RANKL down (p<0.05) |

Exact effect magnitudes are figure-based and are not tabulated in the extracted JATS full text; only directions and reported p-values are stated above. **Non-dental cross-domain: these are systemic growth-plate/hepatic outcomes, not dental or alveolar bone-regeneration outcomes.**
