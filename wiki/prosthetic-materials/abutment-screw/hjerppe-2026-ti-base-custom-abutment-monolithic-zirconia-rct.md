## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=51 completing 3 years: Custom n=23, Ti-Base n=28) at University of Zurich compared screw-retained monolithic ZrO₂ single crowns on standardized Ti-Base vs customized Ti abutments; primary outcome: MBL change.
No crown losses in either group; MBL changes comparable at 3 years (p>0.05); 1 minor chipping in Ti-Base (3.6%); patient satisfaction 100% Custom vs 92.9% Ti-Base (p>0.05).
Both abutment types support stable, high-performing monolithic ZrO₂ crowns at 3 years; Ti-Base is a clinically acceptable simpler and cost-effective alternative to custom abutments.

## Summary

This RCT from University of Zurich randomized posterior implant patients to receive screw-retained monolithic ZrO₂ crowns on either standardized Ti-Base or customized titanium abutments. At 3-year interim analysis (51 patients), neither group experienced crown loss. MBL changes from baseline were comparable between groups (p>0.05). Only one minor chipping event occurred (Ti-Base group, 3.6%), with no major technical complications in either group. Patient satisfaction was high in both arms. These 3-year results support Ti-Base as a valid, simpler workflow alternative to custom abutments for screw-retained monolithic zirconia crowns.

## Key Contributions

- 3-year RCT: No significant MBL difference between Ti-Base and customized Ti abutment for MonoZR crowns
- 0 crown losses; minimal technical complications (1 minor chip in Ti-Base group)
- Both approaches achieve comparable patient satisfaction and peri-implant outcomes
- Supports Ti-Base workflow (stock abutment + bonded ZrO₂) as cost-effective alternative
