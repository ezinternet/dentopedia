## Three-line Summary
In-vitro fatigue/monotonic bench study (n=100 lithium disilicate discs bonded to zirconia abutments) comparing 5 groups of screw-access-hole sealing composite thickness (Ctrl/PTFE-only, +0.5mm, +1.0mm, +1.5mm, +2.0mm).

No difference in monotonic fracture load across groups, but cyclic fatigue failure load was significantly higher for 1.5mm and 2.0mm groups vs Ctrl/0.5mm/1.0mm (0.5mm: 1093N = Ctrl: 1120N = 1.0mm: 1127N < 1.5mm: 1426N = 2.0mm: 1307N, p≤0.05).

Clinical implication: more than half of the screw-access hole depth (>1.5mm) should be filled with resin composite to improve fatigue resistance of lithium disilicate crowns cemented over zirconia abutments; abstract-only, no PDF/PMC full text available.

## Summary
This in-vitro bench study evaluated how the thickness of resin composite used to seal the screw-access hole (SAH) of zirconia abutments affects the fatigue behavior of cement-retained lithium disilicate restorations. One hundred lithium disilicate discs were bonded to zirconia abutments and randomized into five sealing-thickness groups (PTFE-only control, +0.5mm, +1.0mm, +1.5mm, +2.0mm resin composite over PTFE tape). While monotonic (single-load) fracture testing showed no significant differences between groups, cyclic fatigue testing revealed that groups filled with 1.5mm or 2.0mm of composite withstood significantly higher fatigue loads (1426N and 1307N respectively) than the control, 0.5mm, and 1.0mm groups (1093–1127N, p≤0.05). The authors conclude that filling more than half of the screw-access hole depth with resin composite meaningfully improves fatigue resistance of zirconia-abutment-supported lithium disilicate restorations — a finding only detectable under cyclic loading, not static monotonic testing.

## Key Contributions
- Establishes a dose-response threshold (>1.5mm composite fill) for screw-access-hole sealing thickness that meaningfully improves fatigue resistance.
- Demonstrates that monotonic testing alone would have missed this effect entirely — only fatigue/cyclic testing detected the significant group differences.

## Methodology
In-vitro bench study: 100 lithium disilicate discs (Ø10mm, 1mm) bonded to zirconia abutments (Ø10mm, 3mm, 2.5mm SAH) across 5 sealing-thickness groups (Ctrl, +0.5/1.0/1.5/2.0mm Tetric N-Ceram Bulk Fill composite over PTFE tape), luted with Multilink N dual-cure resin cement. Monotonic testing (1.0 mm/min, n=5/group) and staircase cyclic fatigue testing (100N × 5000 cycles, +100N every 10,000 cycles, 20Hz, n=15/group) with Kaplan-Meier/Mantel-Cox survival analysis; supplemented by FEA and SEM.
