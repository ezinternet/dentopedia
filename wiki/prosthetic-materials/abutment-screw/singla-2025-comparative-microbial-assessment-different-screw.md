## Three-line Summary
(Line 1: Prospective randomized clinical trial, 35 patients/45 single-tooth screw-retained implants; cotton pellet used as baseline SAH plug, then randomized via lottery method to PTFE tape or cotton soaked in 1% chlorhexidine (Co1%CHX) subgroups, each retrieved and cultured after 1 month.)
(Line 2: Both PTFE and Co1%CHX showed significantly less aerobic/anaerobic bacterial contamination than cotton (e.g., anaerobic positive samples: cotton 95% vs PTFE 50%, p=0.001; cotton 70% vs Co1%CHX 20%, p=0.001); Co1%CHX outperformed PTFE for anaerobic bacteria (20% vs 50% positive, p=0.04), though aerobic difference between PTFE and Co1%CHX was not significant; IL-6 in peri-implant sulcular fluid did not rise significantly with either material over 3 months.)
(Line 3: Clinically, PTFE or cotton dipped in 1% chlorhexidine should replace dry cotton pellets as the SAH plugging material to reduce oral flora colonization and likely halitosis/cacogeusia, though the study did not identify bacterial species and follow-up was limited to 5 months post-restoration.)

## 세줄요약
(줄1: 전향적 무작위 임상연구, 환자 35명/단일치아 나사고정형 임플란트 45개 — 면(cotton)을 기저 SAH 충전재로 사용 후 뽑기(lottery) 방식으로 PTFE 테이프군과 1% 클로르헥시딘(1% Chlorhexidine, CHX) 침지 면(Co1%CHX)군으로 무작위 배정, 1개월 후 회수해 배양)
(줄2: PTFE·Co1%CHX 모두 면(cotton)보다 호기성·혐기성 세균 오염이 유의하게 낮았고(예: 혐기성 양성률 — 면 95% vs PTFE 50%, p=0.001; 면 70% vs Co1%CHX 20%, p=0.001), 혐기성균에서는 Co1%CHX가 PTFE보다 우수(20% vs 50%, p=0.04)했으나 호기성균 차이는 유의하지 않았음; 임플란트주위 열구액(Peri-implant Sulcular Fluid, PISF)의 인터루킨-6(Interleukin-6, IL-6)는 3개월간 양 군 모두 유의한 증가 없음)
(줄3: 임상적으로는 건조 면 대신 폴리테트라플루오로에틸렌(Polytetrafluoroethylene, PTFE) 또는 1% 클로르헥시딘(CHX) 침지 면을 SAH 충전재로 사용해 구강 상주균 집락화·구취/이상미각을 줄일 것을 권고하나, 균종 동정 없이 5개월로 추적기간이 제한된 한계가 있음)

## Summary
This prospective randomized clinical trial compared three screw access hole (SAH) plugging materials — dry cotton pellet, PTFE tape, and cotton soaked in 1% chlorhexidine (Co1%CHX) — in 35 patients with 45 single-tooth screw-retained implants. All implants first received cotton as a baseline plug for 1 month, after which sites were randomized (lottery method) to PTFE or Co1%CHX for a second 1-month period, with retrieved materials cultured for aerobic and anaerobic bacteria. Both PTFE and Co1%CHX showed markedly lower bacterial contamination than cotton, and Co1%CHX outperformed PTFE specifically for anaerobic bacteria, while aerobic differences between the two were not significant. Despite the microbiologic differences, IL-6 levels in peri-implant sulcular fluid (PISF) did not change significantly over 3 months in either group, and no implant failures occurred. The findings support replacing dry cotton with PTFE or 1% CHX-soaked cotton as the SAH plugging material to reduce microbial colonization, though clinical inflammatory consequences within this short follow-up window appear limited.

## Key Contributions
- Head-to-head in vivo comparison of cotton, PTFE, and cotton+1%CHX as SAH plugging materials with quantified colony counts, not just qualitative leakage assessment
- Identifies Co1%CHX as marginally superior to PTFE for anaerobic bacterial control, a comparison not previously well established
- Pairs microbiologic contamination data with a biochemical inflammatory marker (IL-6 in PISF) to test clinical relevance of the microleakage

## Methodology
Prospective, single-center, randomized (lottery method) trial; 35 patients / 45 implants (4 lost to follow-up); all sites received cotton as baseline SAH plug, then randomized to PTFE (n=20) or Co1%CHX (n=20) subgroups. Primary outcome: aerobic/anaerobic bacterial colony counts and positive-sample rates cultured from retrieved plugging material. Secondary outcome: IL-6 in PISF at baseline and 3 months via ELISA.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Aerobic positive samples: Cotton vs PTFE | 90% vs 40% (p=0.01) |
| Anaerobic positive samples: Cotton vs PTFE | 95% vs 50% (p=0.001) |
| Aerobic positive samples: Cotton vs Co1%CHX | 65% vs 15% (p=0.001) |
| Anaerobic positive samples: Cotton vs Co1%CHX | 70% vs 20% (p=0.001) |
| Anaerobic positive samples: Co1%CHX vs PTFE | 20% vs 50% (p=0.04, favors Co1%CHX) |
| Aerobic positive samples: Co1%CHX vs PTFE | 15% vs 40% (p=0.07, NS) |
| IL-6 in PISF, PTFE group (baseline → 3mo) | 256.30±60.94 → 261.30±69.50 pg/ml (p=0.79, NS) |
| IL-6 in PISF, Co1%CHX group (baseline → 3mo) | 227.06±49.63 → 243.99±71.16 pg/ml (p=0.17, NS) |
