## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (n = 36; titanium vs zirconia two-piece implants with titanium-alloy base and abutment screw; three tightening protocols, n = 6 per cell) applying 100,000 cycles of cyclic torsional loading at 10% of the 35 Ncm tightening torque.

Post-loading removal torque (RT-post) was lowest with retightening after 5 min (titanium 27.9 vs 28.9 immediate vs 30.2 Ncm none; zirconia 23.1 vs 24.9 vs 25.4 Ncm), and zirconia was lower than titanium under every protocol (P < 0.05 to P < 0.001), while torque loss (about 15-19%) did not differ between groups.

Under torsional loading retightening did not improve torque maintenance and the titanium system retained torque better than the zirconia system; limited by differing system designs, one system pair, n = 6 per group and a low-load, roughly one-month-equivalent model.

체외 연구(n = 36; 티타늄 vs 지르코니아 2피스 임플란트, 티타늄 합금 베이스·지대주 나사; 조임 프로토콜 3종, 조건당 n = 6)로, 35 Ncm 조임 토크의 10%로 100,000회 반복 비틀림 하중(cyclic torsional loading)을 가했다.

하중 후 제거 토크(RT-post)는 5분 후 재조임군이 가장 낮았고(티타늄 27.9 vs 즉시 재조임 28.9 vs 재조임 없음 30.2 Ncm; 지르코니아 23.1 vs 24.9 vs 25.4 Ncm), 모든 프로토콜에서 지르코니아가 티타늄보다 낮았으나(P < 0.05~0.001) 토크 손실률(약 15~19%)은 군간 차이가 없었다.

## Summary

Hara and colleagues compared abutment-screw torque retention in a titanium (Grade 4) and a zirconia (Y-TZP) two-piece implant system, each with a titanium-alloy base and abutment screw, tightened to 35 Ncm under three protocols: retightening after 5 min (A), immediate retightening (B), and no retightening (C). After 100,000 cycles of cyclic torsional loading (n = 6 per group), removal torque was lowest after 5-minute retightening in both materials, and zirconia systems showed lower removal torque than titanium systems in every condition, although percentage torque loss did not differ. SEM showed screw head and thread wear in all groups, and micro-CT showed slightly smaller screw dimensional change with zirconia. The authors conclude that retightening does not consistently improve torque maintenance under torsion; because the two systems differ in design as well as material, the result describes whole systems rather than intrinsic material behaviour.

## Key Contributions

- Evaluates retightening (previously studied mainly on titanium implants under bending-type loading) under cyclic torsional loading, in titanium and zirconia two-piece implants.
- Retightening after 5 min produced the lowest post-loading removal torque in both materials; immediate retightening vs none differed only for titanium (28.9 vs 30.2 Ncm, P < 0.05).
- Zirconia implants had lower removal torque both before (RT-pre) and after loading, with no difference in torque loss percentage and larger variability.
- Adds SEM and micro-CT (RMS) evidence of screw wear and few-micrometre deformation (6.3 +/- 6.3 um titanium vs 6.1 +/- 2.4 um zirconia; P < 0.05), judged clinically negligible by the authors.

## Methodology

Bench study: Straumann Standard Plus (titanium, 4.1 x 10 mm) vs Straumann PURE Ceramic (Y-TZP, 4.1 x 10 mm); milled zirconia hexagonal-prism superstructure resin-cemented to a titanium-alloy base. Screws tightened to 35 Ncm with a digital torque meter; protocols A/B/C, n = 6 each (36 specimens). Torsional fatigue machine, load 10% of tightening torque, 10 deg/min, 100,000 reciprocating cycles (per ISO/TS 13498, ISO/TR 18130; about one month of function by the authors' estimate). Outcomes: RT-pre (A and B only), RT-post, torque loss % = (RT-pre - RT-post)/RT-pre x 100, SEM, micro-CT STL superimposition RMS. Welch's t-test with Bonferroni, ANOVA/Tukey, Kruskal-Wallis, Mann-Whitney U.

## Results

| TA (Ti, 5 min) | 34.2 +/- 1.0 | 27.9 +/- 0.6 | 18.3 +/- 3.3 |
| TB (Ti, immediate) | 34.9 +/- 0.9 | 28.9 +/- 0.3 | 17.1 +/- 2.3 |
| TC (Ti, none) | - | 30.2 +/- 1.1 | - |
| ZA (Zr, 5 min) | 28.7 +/- 1.1 | 23.1 +/- 1.3 | 19.3 +/- 5.9 |
| ZB (Zr, immediate) | 29.6 +/- 1.8 | 24.9 +/- 1.9 | 15.5 +/- 8.0 |
| ZC (Zr, none) | - | 25.4 +/- 0.5 | - |

- Titanium: TA < TB and TA < TC (P < 0.001), TB < TC (P < 0.05). Zirconia: ZA < ZB (P < 0.05), ZA < ZC (P < 0.001), ZB vs ZC n.s.
- Zirconia < titanium for RT-post under all protocols (TA-ZA P < 0.001, TB-ZB P < 0.05, TC-ZC P < 0.001); TL not significantly different among groups.
- Wear marks on screw heads and threads in all groups by SEM.

## Limitations

Titanium and zirconia groups differ in implant-body design and interface, not only material; single system pair; n = 6 per cell; low load (10% of tightening torque) and 100,000 cycles; dry bench conditions. Retightening conclusions apply to torsional loading and should not be read as overturning retightening under other loading modes.
