## Three-line Summary

In vitro 2x2 factorial study (n=40 internal-hex implants in stainless steel molar models, 10 per group; standard vs 25 µm horizontally reduced hex abutment x standard vs 15 µm-per-side reduced proximal contact on monolithic zirconia crowns) after thermocycling plus 720,000 chewing cycles at 100 N.

Removal torque value (RTV) fell stepwise from control 26.82 ± 0.70 N·cm to reduced-contact only 25.51 ± 0.52, reduced-fit only 24.78 ± 0.89, and both 21.70 ± 0.99 N·cm; all groups differed (P < .05) with a significant fit x contact interaction (P = .001), and misfit had the larger main effect.

Both horizontal misfit and loose proximal contacts accelerate preload loss, but no screw actually loosened in any group; single implant system, internal hex only, rigid steel model and no baseline RTV limit generalization.

## 세줄요약

체외 2x2 요인 연구(내부 육각 임플란트 40개를 상악 우측 제1대구치 스테인리스 모델에 식립, 군당 10개; 표준 vs 25 µm 수평 축소 육각 지대주 x 표준 vs 편측 15 µm 축소 인접면 접촉의 모노리식 지르코니아 크라운), 온도 순환과 100 N 저작 720,000회 후 평가.

제거 토크값(Removal Torque Value, RTV)은 대조군 26.82 ± 0.70 N·cm에서 접촉 축소 25.51 ± 0.52, 적합 축소 24.78 ± 0.89, 둘 다 축소 21.70 ± 0.99 N·cm로 단계적으로 감소; 전 군 간 유의차(P < .05), 적합 x 접촉 교호작용 유의(P = .001), 지대주 적합도의 주효과가 더 컸다.

수평 부적합과 느슨한 인접면 접촉 모두 예압 손실을 가속하지만 어느 군에서도 나사 풀림 자체는 발생하지 않았으며, 단일 임플란트 시스템·내부 육각만·강성 강철 모델·기저 RTV 미측정이 일반화를 제한한다.

## Summary

Gülgezen Aydın and Şahin (J Adv Prosthodont 2026) tested whether a 25 µm horizontal reduction of the internal-hex abutment diagonal and a 15 µm-per-side reduction of the mesial and distal proximal contacts of a monolithic zirconia first-molar crown change abutment screw removal torque after simulated aging. Forty implants (10 per group) in CNC stainless steel models were tightened to 30 N·cm, re-torqued at 10 minutes, cemented with a temporary implant cement, and subjected to thermocycling with 720,000 chewing cycles at 100 N. Control RTV was 26.82 N·cm and the misfit-plus-loose-contact group was 21.70 N·cm (difference -5.12, P < .001). The authors conclude that fabrication-level horizontal misfit and inadequate contacts can lead to torque loss, and recommend checking contacts and screw stability at annual follow-up.

## Key Contributions

- Factorial design isolates horizontal misfit from contact tightness and shows an additive plus interactive effect on RTV.
- Horizontal misfit had the larger effect (partial eta squared .790 vs .679 for contact); in isolation, RS (24.78) was below SR (25.51).
- Links the clinically common proximal contact loss to a screw-side mechanical consequence.
- Recorded no actual screw loosening in any group, so RTV loss here is a preload-loss signal, not a failure.

## Methodology

Stainless steel model of a maxillary right first-molar gap (dimensional deviation 0.2-0.6%); Oxy 4.5 x 10 mm internal-hex implants bonded with adhesive; standard vs supplier-made abutments with a hex diagonal 25 µm narrower; uncoated Ti-6Al-4V screw tightened to 30 N·cm with a digital torque meter and re-torqued at 10 minutes by one operator; milled 3Y-TZP crowns with 0 µm marginal gap, 30 µm internal gap, and contacts verified with 15 µm and 16 µm films; UDMA-based temporary implant cement; chewing simulator (100 N, 1.6 Hz, 720,000 cycles) with 5-55 °C thermocycling; RTV by digital torque meter; two-way ANOVA with Bonferroni (alpha .05). Baseline RTV was not measured.

## Results

| Group (abutment fit / proximal contact) | RTV mean ± SD (N·cm), n=10 |
|---|---|
| Control (standard / standard) | 26.82 ± 0.70 |
| SR (standard / reduced) | 25.51 ± 0.52 |
| RS (reduced / standard) | 24.78 ± 0.89 |
| RR (reduced / reduced) | 21.70 ± 0.99 |

| Effect | Statistic |
|---|---|
| Overall model | F(3,36) = 74.507, P < .001, eta squared .861 |
| Abutment fit | F(1,36) = 135.083, P < .001, partial eta squared .790 |
| Proximal contact | F(1,36) = 76.071, P < .001, partial eta squared .679 |
| Fit x contact interaction | F(1,36) = 12.366, P = .001, partial eta squared .256 |
| RR vs control | mean difference -5.12, SE 0.36, P < .001 |

No screw loosening occurred in any group. Limitations stated by the authors: in vitro rigid model, one internal-hex single-screw cement-retained system, baseline RTV not measured, single operator without guide fixture, and no inter-rater reliability for contact assessment.
