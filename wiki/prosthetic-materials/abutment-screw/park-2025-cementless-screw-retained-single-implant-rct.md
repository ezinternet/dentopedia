## Three-line Summary

Preliminary single-center RCT (CONSORT; 40 implants / 35 patients, ~6-month follow-up) comparing a conventional combined screw-and-cement-retained prosthesis (CSCRP) workflow with a fully digital cementless screw-retained prosthesis (CL-SRP, Osstem DR screw) for posterior single-implant restorations.

The CL-SRP workflow significantly shortened total prosthetic time (588.79 ± 270.32 s vs 1363.15 ± 347.11 s, p < 0.001), impression-taking time (261.58 vs 466.10 s) and placement time (259.21 vs 918.25 s), and showed lower early marginal bone loss (0.47 ± 0.28 mm vs 0.71 ± 0.33 mm, p < 0.05); adjustment time/volume and gingival shape changes did not differ.

Digital cementless workflow improved chairside efficiency and early peri-implant bone preservation with prosthetic accuracy comparable to the conventional protocol, but small n, ~6-month follow-up, and combined test conditions (digital + cementless) limit generalizability; long-term trials are needed.

## 세줄요약

예비 단일기관 무작위대조시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; CONSORT; 40개 임플란트/35명, 약 6개월 추적) — 구치부 단일 임플란트 수복에서 재래식 혼합 나사·시멘트유지 보철(Combined Screw- and Cement-Retained Prosthesis, CSCRP)과 완전 디지털 시멘트리스 나사유지 보철(Cementless Screw-Retained Prosthesis, CL-SRP, 오스템 DR 스크류)을 비교.

CL-SRP군에서 총 보철시간(588.79±270.32초 vs 1363.15±347.11초, p<0.001)·인상채득시간(261.58 vs 466.10초)·장착시간(259.21 vs 918.25초)이 유의하게 짧았고, 조기 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)도 유의하게 낮음(0.47±0.28 mm vs 0.71±0.33 mm, p<0.05); 조정시간·조정량·치은형태 변화는 차이 없음.

디지털 시멘트리스 워크플로우는 보철 정확도를 유지하면서 체어타임 효율과 조기 임플란트 주위 골보존을 개선했으나, 표본 수가 작고(35명) 추적이 짧으며(약 6개월) 디지털화+시멘트리스가 결합된 조건이라 요인 분리가 안 됨 — 장기 연구 필요.

## Summary

This preliminary single-center RCT (40 implants in 35 patients, ~6 months) compared a conventional combined screw-and-cement-retained prosthesis (CSCRP) workflow — PVS impression, custom titanium abutment, zirconia crown intraorally cemented then extraorally cleaned — with a fully digital cementless screw-retained prosthesis (CL-SRP) workflow — prefabricated DR base abutment torqued once, intraoral scanning (TRIOS 3), CAD/CAM monolithic zirconia crown secured by a 20 Ncm prosthetic screw with no cement at any stage. The CL-SRP group showed significantly shorter impression-taking, placement, and total prosthetic times (588.79 vs 1363.15 s total, p < 0.001) and significantly lower early marginal bone loss (0.47 vs 0.71 mm, p < 0.05), while prosthetic accuracy (STL-quantified adjustment volume), adjustment time, and gingival shape changes were equivalent. The authors attribute the MBL difference to the cementless design's one-time abutment (OAOT-style) protocol and the elimination of residual cement, but the combined test conditions and short follow-up preclude isolating the mechanism or claiming long-term superiority.

## Key Contributions

- First randomized clinical data on a fully digital **CL-SRP** (titanium base + CAD/CAM zirconia crown, no cement at any stage) workflow for single posterior implants — the design family previously held in this wiki as FEA only ([[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/lee-2021-cement-vs-cementless-screw-retained-fea]], [[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/lee-2025-screwless-hook-retained-implant-fea]])
- Chairside efficiency quantified: total prosthetic time −57% (d = 2.489), placement time −72% (d = 5.818), impression time −44% (d = 1.890), all p < 0.001
- Early MBL significantly lower with the cementless one-time-abutment protocol (mean diff 0.24 mm, p < 0.05) at equal prosthetic accuracy — consistent with the OAOT rationale
- Both workflows clinically acceptable; no intraoperative/postoperative complications in either group

## Methodology

- **Design**: Preliminary RCT (CONSORT); 1:1 computer-generated allocation; participants and outcome assessors blinded (clinicians not); IRB HALLYM IRB 2024-05-001; KNCT KCT0009159
- **n**: 40 implants / 35 patients (control 20, test 20; one test dropout); G*Power: effect size 1.2, power 0.95, 20% dropout
- **Implants**: Osstem TS III, Ø4.0–5.0 × 8.5–11.5 mm, ~1 mm subcrestal, one-stage, ~12-week healing; prosthetics by two prosthodontists
- **Control (CSCRP)**: PVS impression → custom Ti abutment + zirconia crown cemented with RelyX U200 → extraoral cement cleanup → 30 Ncm abutment screw
- **Test (CL-SRP)**: DR base abutment 30 Ncm once → TRIOS 3 scan → CAD/CAM zirconia crown → 20 Ncm prosthetic screw, no cement
- **Outcomes**: IT/PP/PA times (stopwatch by independent assistant); prosthetic accuracy = adjustment volume via STL superimposition (Geomagic Control X); gingival shape change (µm, 3D superimposition); MBL (periapical radiographs, mesial + distal, ICC 0.88); SPSS 25 (t-test / Mann–Whitney U, α = 0.05)
