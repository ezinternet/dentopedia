## Three-line Summary

Pilot RCT (n=30, 28 completed 3yr): monolithic zirconia RBFPD inlay-retained (IR) vs wing-retained (WR) — IR 3yr survival 100%, WR 93.3% (P=0.317, NS); success IR 72.7% vs WR 86.7% (P=0.379, NS)

One WR failure (unilateral debonding at 3mo); IR design numerically inferior in success but statistically equivalent; pilot sample precludes definitive conclusions

For intact abutment teeth: WR preferred (less invasive); IR appropriate when abutment requires restoration

## Background

Resin-bonded fixed dental prostheses (RBFPDs) offer a maximally conservative option for single tooth replacement. Two retainer designs compete: wing-retained (WR, Maryland bridge) — minimal preparation, thin palatal/lingual wing; inlay-retained (IR) — requires inlay cavity but retains existing tooth structure. This pilot RCT from the Heidelberg group is the first prospective comparison of these two designs in posterior monolithic zirconia.

## Methods

- **Design**: Prospective pilot RCT (ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT01997710)
- **n**: 30 patients (28 completed 3yr follow-up), mandibular second premolar/first molar/second molar
- **Groups**: IR (n=15, mean age 56.4yr, 66.7% male) vs WR (n=15, mean age 45.9yr, 46.7% male)
- **Material**: Monolithic zirconia — Cercon ht (DeguDent)
- **Surface treatment**: Rocatec tribochemical silica coating + Clearfil Ceramic Primer (Kuraray) silanization
- **Cement**: ED Primer + Panavia 21 (Kuraray, self-etching resin)
- **Follow-up**: 1wk, 3mo, 6mo, 1yr, 2yr, 3yr
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier, log-rank test, Fisher's exact test (α=0.05)

## Results

| Outcome | IR | WR | P value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3yr failure-free survival | 100% | 93.3% | 0.317 NS |
| 3yr success | 72.7% | 86.7% | 0.379 NS |

- **1 failure**: WR RBFPD, unilateral debonding at 3 months (counted as failure, not re-cemented)
- **1 withdrawal**: patient with intact IR RBFPD (health reasons, not restoration-related)
- Success criteria based on modified FDI criteria

## Clinical Significance

1. **Both IR and WR zirconia RBFPDs show acceptable 3yr outcomes** for minimally invasive posterior replacement
2. **Wing-retained preferred for intact abutment teeth**: less invasive (no cavity preparation), numerically higher success rate
3. **Inlay-retained appropriate when abutment already needs restoration**: converts necessary restoration into retainer, avoiding additional invasiveness elsewhere
4. **Surface treatment matters**: tribochemical silica coating + silanization + resin cement is current standard for monolithic zirconia adhesive bonding
5. **Pilot limitation**: insufficient power for definitive design comparison — results are directional

## Relation to Lab Data (Waldecker 2026)

The same Heidelberg group published in vitro load-bearing data for the same design comparison [[waldecker-2026-multilayer-zirconia-rbfpd-load-bearing]]:
- IR load-bearing: 684–1216 N
- WR load-bearing: 1025–1617 N
- The higher lab load-bearing capacity of WR does not translate to statistically better clinical outcomes at 3yr

## Limitations

- Pilot study — underpowered
- Heterogeneous replacement sites (premolar + different molar types)
- Age/sex distribution imbalance between groups
- 3yr short-to-medium follow-up
- Single zirconia material/cement system (may not generalize to other protocols)
