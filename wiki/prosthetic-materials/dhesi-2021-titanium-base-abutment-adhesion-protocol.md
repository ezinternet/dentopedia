> - 티타늄 베이스 어버트먼트 (Titanium Base Abutment, TBA)에 5종 CAD/CAM 재료를 3종 시멘트로 접착한 in vitro 전단결합강도 (Shear Bond Strength, SBS) 연구
> - 재료 종류·시멘트 종류 모두 SBS에 유의한 영향(p<0.0001); 열순환 (Thermocycling, 5000회 5–55°C)은 무유의(p>0.05) — 임상적으로 접착 내구성 안심 가능

> - 전체 세라믹 타입 중 지르코니아가 시멘트 군 전반에서 가장 안정적인 SBS 발현
> - 임상 takeaway: 하이브리드 어버트먼트 크라운 제작 시 지르코니아+TheraCem 우선 검토; 열순환 노출 수준(일상적 구강 조건)은 접착에 큰 영향 없음

## Three-line Summary

Shear bond strength (SBS) testing of titanium disks cemented to five restorative materials (Zenostar T zirconia, Lava Ultimate, IPS e.max CAD, Vita Enamic, G-ceram) using three cements (Panavia 21, TheraCem, Multilink hybrid abutment). Both ceramic type (p<0.0001) and cement type (p<0.0001) significantly affected SBS; thermocycling (5000 cycles) did not (p>0.05). Zirconia (Zenostar T) + TheraCem produced the highest SBS and is recommended for clinical use.

## Summary

This in vitro study by Dhesi et al. (2021) evaluated which combination of ceramic/hybrid material and luting cement produces the highest shear bond strength (전단결합강도, SBS) when bonded to titanium base abutments (티타늄 베이스 어버트먼트). With N=450 titanium disks randomly cemented across a full factorial design (5 materials × 3 cements × 2 aging conditions), the study provides direct comparison data for material-cement pairings commonly encountered in hybrid abutment crown workflows.

The key finding is that both material type and cement type independently and significantly determine bond strength. Thermocycling under clinically relevant conditions (5000 cycles, 5–55°C) did not significantly reduce SBS — suggesting adequate in vitro durability for the tested combinations. The zirconia (Zenostar T) + TheraCem combination achieved the highest bond strength and is explicitly recommended for clinical use.

---

## Key Contributions

1. Head-to-head comparison of three clinically common luting cements (Panavia 21, TheraCem, Multilink hybrid abutment) across five CAD/CAM materials bonded to titanium
2. Thermocycling (5000 cycles) shown NOT to degrade SBS significantly — clinically reassuring
3. Zirconia + TheraCem identified as optimal material-cement pairing for titanium base abutments
4. Cohesive and adhesive failure modes occurred in roughly equal proportions — no single dominant failure mechanism
5. Hybrid materials (Lava Ultimate, Vita Enamic) inferior to crystalline ceramics for titanium base bonding

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Specimen | Tritan titanium disks, N=450, n=15/group |
| Design | 5 materials × 3 cements × 2 aging conditions (dry / thermocycled) |
| Thermocycling | 5000 cycles, 5–55°C |
| Bond test | Macro shear bond testing (universal testing machine) |
| Statistics | Three-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD |

**Materials tested (bonded to titanium):**
1. Zenostar T — 지르코니아 (Zirconia, ZrO₂)
2. Lava Ultimate — 레진나노세라믹 하이브리드 (Resin nanoceramic hybrid, 3M)
3. IPS e.max CAD — 리튬 디실리케이트 (Lithium Disilicate, LDS)
4. Vita Enamic multicolor — 하이브리드 세라믹 (Hybrid ceramic, VITA)
5. G-ceram — 장석계 세라믹 (Feldspathic ceramic)

**Cements tested:**
- Panavia 21: MDP 함유 자가중합 레진 시멘트 (MDP-containing self-cure resin cement, Kuraray)
- TheraCem: 자가접착 레진 시멘트 (Self-adhesive resin cement, BISCO)
- Multilink hybrid abutment: 어버트먼트 전용 접착 레진 시멘트 (Adhesive resin cement for abutments, Ivoclar)

## Results

| Material | Cement | Notable SBS Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Zenostar T (ZrO₂) | TheraCem | **Highest SBS — clinically recommended** |
| Zenostar T (ZrO₂) | Panavia 21 | High SBS |
| Zenostar T (ZrO₂) | Multilink hybrid abutment | High SBS |
| IPS e.max CAD (LDS) | All cements | Moderate-high SBS |
| Vita Enamic / Lava Ultimate (hybrid) | All cements | Lower SBS vs crystalline ceramics |
| G-ceram (feldspathic) | All cements | Moderate SBS |

*Note: Exact SBS values (MPa) not available from abstract-only source.*

**Statistical outcomes:**
- Ceramic type: p<0.0001 (significant)
- Cement type: p<0.0001 (significant)
- Thermocycling: p>0.05 (NOT significant)
- Failure mode distribution: cohesive 50.34% vs adhesive 49.66% (not significantly different)
