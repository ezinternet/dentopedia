## Three-line Summary

In vitro (n=16/group): posterior zirconia RBFPD — multilayer 3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ (centric position) > 5Y-PSZ alone in load-bearing; WR design (1025–1617N) > IR (684–1216N); aging had no significant effect

Vertical nesting position within the blank influences load-bearing within the same material — centric > top position for 3Y/5Y material

Clinical recommendation: 3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ multilayer zirconia can proceed to clinical trials; 5Y-PSZ alone should be scrutinized for RBFPD indication

## Background

Multilayer zirconia materials combine 3Y-TZP (high strength) at the core with 5Y-PSZ (translucent) in the outer layers, creating a gradient that improves aesthetics while attempting to maintain adequate strength. For posterior RBFPDs, which are subject to high masticatory forces, the choice of zirconia grade and the position within the milling blank may critically influence load-bearing capacity. This study from the Heidelberg group provides preclinical evidence to guide material selection for the clinical trial reported in [[boemicke-2025-zirconia-rbfpd-inlay-wing-3year-rct]].

## Methods

- **In vitro design**: maxillary first molar replacement RBFPD
- **n**: 16 monolithic RBFPDs per group
- **Retainer designs**: IR (inlay-retained), WR (wing-retained)
- **Materials**:
  - 3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ gradient (labeled 3Y/5Y): zirconia with color/material gradient
  - 5Y-PSZ only (labeled 5Y): monolithic translucent zirconia
- **Nesting positions** (for 3Y/5Y only): center, top of blank
- **Aging**: half of specimens underwent artificial aging (thermocycling + cyclic loading)
- **Load test**: oblique loading to failure (F in Newtons)
- **Statistics**: Welch ANOVA + Dunnett-T3 post hoc (α=0.05)

## Results

### Load-bearing capacity (F, mean range)

| Design | Material / Position | F range (N) |
|---|---|---|
| WR | 3Y/5Y centric | ~1,617 (highest) |
| WR | 3Y/5Y top | intermediate |
| WR | 5Y only | ~1,025 (lowest WR) |
| IR | 3Y/5Y centric | ~1,216 (highest IR) |
| IR | 3Y/5Y top | intermediate |
| IR | 5Y only | ~684 (lowest) |

- **WR consistently > IR** within all material/position groups
- **Material hierarchy**: 3Y/5Y centric > 3Y/5Y top > 5Y only
- **Aging**: no significant effect (P≥0.526) — no debonding or defects after aging
- No decementations observed after artificial aging

## Clinical Significance

1. **3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ gradient material (centric nesting)** provides adequate safety margin for posterior RBFPD → can proceed to clinical trials
2. **5Y-PSZ (pure translucent zirconia)** shows insufficient load-bearing for RBFPD indication, particularly IR design → use with caution or avoid for this application
3. **Nesting position matters**: the same material, cut from a different position in the blank, yields different performance → standardize milling to centric position for RBFPDs
4. **WR design is biomechanically superior** in lab settings, consistent with clinical trend (boemicke-2025: WR 93.3% vs IR 100% survival — note: clinical equivalence despite lab difference)
5. **Aging resistance**: zirconia RBFPD properties do not degrade under simulated aging — reassuring for long-term use

## Limitations

- In vitro only — extrapolation to clinical outcomes requires caution
- Maxillary first molar only
- Monolithic RBFPDs — no veneered alternatives tested
- Limited range of aging protocols
