RCT 13-year follow-up (n=40 implants; 20 customized Zr abutments + all-ceramic crowns vs 20 Ti abutments + metal-ceramic crowns; posterior) showing 100% technical survival of both abutment types and comparable periimplant biological and esthetic outcomes — the longest available RCT data on ceramic vs titanium abutments.

## Summary

This RCT 13-year follow-up study from the University of Zürich compared customized zirconia abutments + all-ceramic crowns (ACC) against customized titanium abutments + metal-ceramic crowns (MCC) in posterior implant-supported single restorations. Twenty patients/abutments per group were randomized at the site level. Of the original 22 patients (40 implants), 15 patients (21 abutments: 13 Zr, 8 Ti) were examined at a mean 13.4-year follow-up, representing a 25% dropout rate.

Both abutment types achieved 100% technical survival. No technical complications occurred on either zirconia or titanium abutments throughout the 13.4-year follow-up. Crown survival was 100% in both groups. Periimplant biological outcomes (PPD, BOP, PCR, bone level) and esthetic outcomes (papilla index, mucosal recession) were similar between groups. The study represents the longest available RCT evidence for ceramic implant abutments, confirming long-term viability of zirconia as an alternative to titanium where esthetics are a priority.

## Key Contributions

- Longest RCT follow-up on ceramic vs titanium abutments (13 years; previously max ~5 years)
- Confirms 100% technical survival of customized zirconia abutments at 13 years — no fractures
- Establishes periimplant biological equivalence of ceramic and titanium abutments long-term
- No ceramic abutment fractures in posterior regions over 13 years supports safe clinical use
- Dropout rate and small final sample limit precision, but direction is clear

## Methodology

**Study design:** RCT 13-year follow-up (single-center, University of Zürich)

**Original allocation:** 20 customized zirconia abutments + all-ceramic cemented crowns (ACC) vs 20 customized titanium abutments + metal-ceramic crowns (MCC); posterior implant sites; randomized per site

**Follow-up:** Mean 13.4 years post-loading; original n=22 patients, 40 implants

**Sample at 13yr:** 15 patients, 21 abutments (13 Zr, 8 Ti); 25% dropout

**Outcomes:** Technical survival (abutment, crown); technical complications; PPD, BOP, PCR, bone level; papilla index (PAP), mucosal recession; descriptive statistics only
