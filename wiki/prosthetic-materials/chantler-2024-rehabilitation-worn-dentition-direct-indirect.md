## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (6 databases; 5009 records, 14 publications: 2 RCTs, 7 prospective, 5 retrospective) of minimally invasive direct vs indirect restorations in worn dentition.

Direct vs indirect manually processed composite showed no significant survival difference (n=2; OR 1.79, 95% CI 0.64-5.05; p=0.270); prospective pooled survival was 99% (direct, n=3) vs 100% (indirect, n=5); lithium disilicate had the fewest failures (1.8%).

Authors suggest indirect ceramics (especially lithium disilicate) for long-term treatment and direct composite as a medium-term solution, but evidence is limited and of varying quality (abstract-level data only).

## Summary

Source: PubMed, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/jerd.13384) (J Esthet Restor Dent 2024;37(3):690-701). This systematic review and meta-analysis asked whether rehabilitating lost tooth structure in dentate patients with worn dentition using indirect restorations, compared with direct ones, affects survival and success. Fourteen publications were analyzed (52% direct, 48% indirect restorations). The pooled comparison of direct composite vs indirect manually processed composite found no significant difference. The authors conclude that indirect restorations, especially lithium disilicate, may offer better survival than direct resin, which is better suited as a medium-term solution.

## Key Contributions

- Quantitative (random-effects) survival comparison of minimally invasive direct vs indirect restorations for worn dentition.
- Lithium disilicate had the lowest failure rate (1.8%).

## Methodology

MEDLINE, Web of Science, Scopus, CENTRAL, VHL and EMBASE were searched without language restriction. PICO: dentate patients with worn dentition; indirect (intervention) vs direct (comparison); survival and success. Random-effects models for survival and prevalence. Included: 2 RCTs, 7 prospective, 5 retrospective studies of varying methodological quality.

## Results

| Outcome | Result |

| Prospective pooled survival, indirect (n=5) | 100% (95% CI 0.99-1.00) |
