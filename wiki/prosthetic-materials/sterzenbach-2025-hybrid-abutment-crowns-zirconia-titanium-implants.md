## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT (n=60, 30/group, Charité-Universitätsmedizin Berlin, DRKS00014866) comparing screw-retained lithium disilicate single implant crowns (SICs) on PEKK-base hybrid abutments over two-piece zirconia implants versus titanium-base hybrid abutments over two-piece titanium implants, followed 3 years.

Zirconia-group cumulative survival was 10.7% lower than titanium (89.3% vs 100%), driven by 3 post-baseline implant losses from incomplete osseointegration (plus 3 early pre-crown implant failures, all revised) — non-inferiority (10% margin) was NOT confirmed, though the Log-Rank test for survival curves was not significant (p=0.068); technical complication rates (modified USPHS) and 12-month PES/WES were statistically similar between groups.

The prosthetic component (crown/abutment) performed comparably in both groups with no crown-level failures, but the lower osseointegration rate of two-piece zirconia implants is a clinically meaningful concern that a non-significant p-value alone would obscure — caution is warranted when selecting zirconia implants for this indication.

## 세줄요약

전향적 RCT(n=60, 군당 30명, 샤리테-베를린대, DRKS00014866) — 투피스 지르코니아 임플란트(two-piece zirconia implant) 위 PEKK(폴리에테르케톤케톤, Polyether Ketone Ketone) 기반 하이브리드 지대주(hybrid abutment)와 투피스 티타늄 임플란트 위 티타늄 기반 하이브리드 지대주에 나사유지형(screw-retained) 리튬디실리케이트(lithium disilicate) 단일 임플란트 크라운(Single Implant Crown, SIC)을 식립해 3년 추적.

지르코니아군 누적 생존율이 티타늄군(100%) 대비 10.7% 낮음(89.3%) — 원인은 크라운 파손이 아닌 임플란트 수준 골유착(osseointegration) 부전(기저선 후 3예, 초기 실패 3예는 모두 재시술 성공); 10% 마진의 비열등성(non-inferiority)은 입증되지 않았으나 Log-Rank 생존곡선 비교는 유의차 없음(p=0.068); 변형 USPHS 기술적 합병증 발생률과 12개월 분홍색 심미 점수(Pink Aesthetic Score, PES)/백색 심미 점수(White Aesthetic Score, WES)는 양군 통계적으로 유사.

보철 구성요소(크라운·지대주) 자체는 양군 모두 준수한 성적을 보였으나(크라운 단독 실패 0건), 지르코니아 임플란트의 낮은 골유착률은 p값만으로는 가려지는 임상적으로 중요한 우려사항 — 해당 적응증에 지르코니아 임플란트를 선택할 때 주의가 필요하다.

## Summary

This is the first RCT to directly compare two-piece **zirconia** and **titanium** dental implants under an identical screw-retained hybrid abutment crown (SIC) protocol — 60 patients (30 per arm), premolar/molar single-tooth replacements, followed to 3 years. Both arms received lithium disilicate crowns bonded to a screw-retained hybrid abutment (PEKK base on zirconia implants; titanium base on titanium implants), placed at second-stage surgery ("one abutment, one time"). The pre-specified primary analysis was a non-inferiority test (10% margin) on cumulative SIC survival.

The zirconia group's cumulative survival (89.3%) was 10.7 percentage points below the titanium group's (100%), and the lower confidence bound (74.6%) fell short of the 82.6% threshold required for non-inferiority — so non-inferiority was **not confirmed**. Critically, none of the failures were crown/prosthetic failures: all six zirconia implant losses (3 early pre-crown, 3 post-baseline) were attributed to incomplete osseointegration (aseptic connective-tissue encapsulation), not to the hybrid abutment crown design. Despite this clinically meaningful gap, the Log-Rank test comparing survival curves was not statistically significant (p=0.068) — the authors explicitly warn that p-value framing alone can obscure a clinically important difference here. Technical complications (modified USPHS), aesthetic outcomes (PES/WES at 12 months), and patient-reported satisfaction were statistically similar between groups.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT isolating **implant material** (zirconia vs titanium) as the sole intentional variable while holding the hybrid-abutment / screw-retained / lithium disilicate crown protocol constant — addressing an evidence gap the EAO position paper and German S3 guidelines had flagged as insufficient for two-piece ceramic implant recommendations.
- Shows the zirconia-implant survival deficit (10.7%, non-inferiority not confirmed) is driven entirely by implant-level osseointegration failure, not by the hybrid abutment or crown — an important attribution distinction for clinicians weighing "is it the implant or the prosthetic design" when zirconia cases fail.
- Demonstrates that technical complication rate (modified USPHS), 12-month PES/WES esthetics, and patient satisfaction are statistically comparable between zirconia and titanium arms — the prosthetic component itself is not the differentiator.
- Explicit methodological caution: a non-significant Log-Rank test (p=0.068) coexisted with a failed non-inferiority analysis on the same survival data — illustrating that the pre-specified, more conservative non-inferiority test (not an incidental significance test) should drive the clinical conclusion.

## Methodology

- Prospective RCT, 1:1 urn randomization, n=60 (34 women), mean age 44 (21-67), Jan 2018-Sep 2019, single center (Dentalzentrum Pankow, Berlin); ethics approval Charité-Universitätsmedizin Berlin (EA4/041/18); registered DRKS00014866; CONSORT-compliant.
- Blinded outcome assessors (clinical follow-up inspection); surgeons unblinded (implants visually distinct).
- Test arm: CERALOG Hexalob two-piece zirconia implant (8 mm, ⌀4.0) + screw-retained PEKK hybrid abutment + bonded lithium disilicate (IPS e.max CAD) crown; gold screw at 15 Ncm.
- Control arm: CAMLOG screw-line titanium implant (9 mm, ⌀4.3) + screw-retained titanium adhesive base + bonded lithium disilicate crown; titanium screw at 25 Ncm.
- Fully digital workflow: CEREC Omnicam full-arch scan → backward planning (Galileos Implant) → 3D-printed guided drilling template; final restoration at 4 months (zirconia) / 3 months (titanium) post-placement, one abutment-one time.
- Primary outcome: cumulative SIC survival to 3 years, non-inferiority design (10% margin), Kaplan-Meier/ITT, Clopper-Pearson CIs, Log-Rank test.
- Secondary outcomes: modified USPHS technical-complication criteria (adapted for screw-retained hybrid abutment SICs) at baseline/6/12/24/36 months; PES/WES at baseline and 12 months; exploratory PROM questionnaire at 36 months (voluntary, unvalidated).
- Powered for 30 patients/arm assuming 95% 5-year titanium survival and a 10% non-inferiority margin (alpha 0.05, power 0.8).

## Results

| Outcome | Titanium (n=30) | Zirconia (n=30, n=28 analyzed) | Statistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-year cumulative survival (ITT) | 100% (0.926-1.0, 90% CI) | 89.3% (0.746-0.97, 90% CI) | Non-inferiority (10% margin) NOT confirmed |
| Log-Rank test, survival curves | — | — | p=0.068 (not significant) |
| Implant losses (cause) | 0 | 6 total: 3 early pre-crown (revised) + 3 post-baseline (osseointegration failure) | All non-osseointegration/aseptic encapsulation |
| Crown-attributable SIC failures | 0 | 0 | None in either group |
| Total technical complications (36 mo) | 2 | 6 | No USPHS parameter significant (all p>0.15) |
| Category-C events (chipping / retention loss) | 0 | 2 chipping + 2 retention loss (all repaired) | — |
| Screw loosening | 1 | 2 | Resolved by screw replacement |
| PES at baseline | mean 7 (SD 2.226) | mean 6.13 (SD 2.074) | p=0.009 (zirconia lower) |
| PES at 12 months | mean 8.38 (SD 1.555) | mean 7.43 (SD 1.950) | p=0.085 (n.s. between groups); both p<0.001 vs baseline |
| WES at 12 months | mean 8.33 | mean 7.65 | p=0.096 (n.s.) |
| PROM overall satisfaction (36 mo, n=24 responders) | 80% very satisfied, 20% satisfied | 77.8% very satisfied, 22.2% satisfied | Low response rate (30-50%), no formal statistics |

Bottom line: the prosthetic/technical/esthetic profile of the hybrid abutment crown is essentially equivalent between implant materials, but zirconia implants carry a clinically meaningful (though not p<0.05) osseointegration-failure risk that drove the non-inferiority analysis to fail.
