## Three-line Summary

SR of 67 studies (4,663 metal-ceramic + 9,434 all-ceramic SCs; PubMed/Embase/CENTRAL 2006–2013; robust Poisson regression) establishing that most all-ceramic single crown materials achieve equivalent 5-year survival to metal-ceramic crowns (gold standard), except feldspathic ceramics.

5-year survival: metal-ceramic 94.7%, leucite/LD glass-ceramic 96.6%, alumina 94.6–96.0%, zirconia 92.1%; feldspathic ceramics significantly inferior (p<0.001); zirconia carries significantly higher chipping of veneering ceramic and loss of retention vs metal-ceramic (p<0.001 each).

All-ceramic SCs using leucite/LD glass-ceramics or oxide ceramics are clinically equivalent to metal-ceramic; zirconia-based SCs achieve comparable survival but should not be a primary option due to high technical complication burden — a recommendation later refined by Pjetursson 2026 (monolithic zirconia).

## 세줄요약

67편 SR(금속도재관 4,663개 + 전세라믹 9,434개, PubMed/Embase/CENTRAL 2006–2013, 포아송 회귀): 도재(feldspathic) 제외 대부분의 전세라믹 단단관은 금속도재관과 5년 생존율 동등.

5년 생존율: 금속도재관 94.7%, 류사이트/LD 글래스 세라믹 96.6%, 알루미나 94.6–96.0%, 지르코니아 92.1%; 도재 세라믹 유의하게 열등(p<0.001); 지르코니아는 베니어 치핑·유지력 상실 유의 증가(p<0.001).

LD/알루미나 등 전세라믹 단단관은 금속도재관과 임상적으로 동등; 지르코니아 단단관은 생존율 동등하나 기술적 합병증 부담으로 1차 선택 비권장 — 2026년 Pjetursson SR에서 모놀리식 지르코니아로 재평가.

## Summary

Sailer et al. 2015 updated the 2007 SR on tooth-supported FDPs to include studies published 2006–2013. The major finding was that most all-ceramic single crown materials have matured to match metal-ceramic survival rates over a 5-year follow-up. The key distinction was material type: leucite/lithium-disilicate glass-ceramics and densely sintered oxide ceramics (alumina, zirconia) achieved equivalent outcomes, while feldspathic ceramics remained significantly inferior, especially in the posterior region. Despite adequate survival, zirconia-based SCs showed a significantly higher burden of technical complications (veneering fracture, loss of retention), leading the authors to advise against their routine use as a primary option. This recommendation was later refined with the emergence of monolithic zirconia designs (Pjetursson 2026).

## Key Contributions

- Established the clinical equivalence of leucite/LD glass-ceramics and oxide ceramics to metal-ceramic SCs (long-debated)
- Defined feldspathic ceramics as posterior-region-limited due to significantly lower survival
- Identified the veneering fracture ("chipping") problem as the primary weakness of zirconia-based SCs
- Provided reference 5-year survival benchmarks used in subsequent SRs (Pjetursson 2026)
- Corrigendum published 2016 (Dental Materials 32:12) to correct minor errors

## Methodology

Systematic review with meta-analysis. PubMed, Embase, CENTRAL search (2006–2013) + 34 studies carried over from 2007 SR. Inclusion: tooth-supported FDP clinical studies, mean follow-up ≥3 years. 67 studies met criteria: 4,663 metal-ceramic + 9,434 all-ceramic SCs. Robust Poisson regression for pooled 5-year rates.

## Results

| Material | 5-year Survival | vs Metal-ceramic |
|---|---|---|
| Metal-ceramic | 94.7% (CI: 94.1–96.9%) | Reference |
| Leucite/LD glass-ceramic | 96.6% (CI: 94.9–96.7%) | Equivalent |
| Glass-infiltrated alumina | 94.6% (CI: 92.7–96%) | Equivalent |
| Densely sintered alumina | 96.0% (CI: 93.8–97.5%) | Equivalent |
| Densely sintered zirconia | 92.1% (CI: 82.8–95.6%) | Equivalent survival, BUT higher complications |
| Feldspathic/silica-based | Significantly lower (p<0.001) | Inferior |

Zirconia: significantly more framework fracture + loss of retention (p<0.001). Posterior region: feldspathic and zirconia had significantly lower survival (p<0.0001). abstract-only — publisher landing page, full text not retrieved.
