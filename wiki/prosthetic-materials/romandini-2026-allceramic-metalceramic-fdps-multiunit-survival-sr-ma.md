## Summary

This SR+MA (Part 2 of a two-part review by the same group) systematically analyzed survival, failure, and complication rates of tooth-supported multi-unit FDPs across 41 prospective and RCT studies published January 2014–December 2024, supplemented with 17 studies from prior systematic reviews covering earlier periods. Using robust Poisson regression, pooled 5-year survival estimates for 600 metal-ceramic and 1,532 all-ceramic FDPs were calculated.

Veneered dense-sintered zirconia FDPs achieved 5-year survival of 92.9%, metal-ceramic 91.3%, glass-infiltrated alumina 88.4%, monolithic dense-sintered zirconia 87.9%, and LD glass-ceramic 82.5%. Only LD was significantly lower than metal-ceramic (p<0.05). The complication-free rate across all materials was 71.0% at 5 years. All-ceramic FDPs showed higher rates of marginal caries and loss of retention compared to metal-ceramic. Framework fractures exceeded 10% at 5 years for LD and glass-infiltrated alumina. Monolithic zirconia had the least chipping of all ceramic options.

The companion paper (Part 1, Pjetursson 2026) examined single crowns; this Part 2 specifically addresses multi-unit FDPs (bridges), filling a critical evidence gap in the multi-unit FDP literature.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA specifically covering multi-unit FDPs from 2014–2024 literature
- Establishes veneered dense-sintered zirconia as the highest-surviving all-ceramic FDP material (92.9% at 5yr, NS vs MC)
- Demonstrates LD FDPs have significantly lower survival in multi-unit vs single-unit scenario (82.5%, p<0.05 vs MC)
- Confirms monolithic zirconia as the best balance of survival + minimal chipping for all-ceramic FDPs
- 71.0% complication-free rate provides baseline for clinical expectation setting

## Methodology

**Study design:** SR+MA (Part 2 of Pjetursson 2026 series)

**Search:** MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL; January 2014–December 2024; supplemented with 17 studies from prior SRs (pre-2014)

**Inclusion:** RCTs and prospective studies on tooth-supported multi-unit FDPs; mean follow-up ≥3 years

**Sample:** 41 studies; 600 metal-ceramic + 1,532 all-ceramic FDPs (n=2,132 total)

**Statistics:** Robust Poisson regression models → pooled 5-year survival estimates per material class

## Results

| Material | 5-Year Survival | vs Metal-Ceramic |
|---|---|---|
| Veneered dense-sintered Zr | 92.9% | NS |
| Metal-ceramic (reference) | 91.3% | — |
| Glass-infiltrated alumina | 88.4% | NS |
| Monolithic dense-sintered Zr | 87.9% | NS |
| LD glass-ceramic | 82.5% | p<0.05 (sig. lower) |

**Complications at 5 years:**
- Complication-free rate: 71.0%
- Framework fractures: LD + glass-infiltrated alumina >10% (highest of all materials)
- Chipping: monolithic Zr had least; veneered Zr intermediate
- All-ceramic vs MC: higher marginal caries + loss of retention
