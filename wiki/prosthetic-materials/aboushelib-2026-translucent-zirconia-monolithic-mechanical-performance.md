## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (n=160 bar specimens; 3Y-PSZ vs 4Y-PSZ; 4 surface treatments: polishing, glass bead abrasion, alumina abrasion, grinding; conventional vs speed sintering; ±2M cycle fatigue) examining how sintering protocol and surface treatment interact to affect flexural strength and reliability.

Glass bead treatment significantly improves 3Y-PSZ strength (p<0.001); grinding significantly reduces both types (p<0.001); speed sintering does not affect 3Y-PSZ (p=0.10) but significantly decreases 4Y-PSZ strength (p<0.001); after fatigue, 3Y-PSZ stable while 4Y-PSZ significantly reduced (p<0.001).

3Y-PSZ is more suitable for high-stress applications and tolerates both speed sintering and cyclic loading; 4Y-PSZ requires careful sintering protocol and surface treatment selection to minimize strength reduction.

## 세줄요약

3Y-PSZ vs 4Y-PSZ 지르코니아 굴곡강도 실험(n=160 막대 시편; 4가지 표면처리; 일반/속성 소결; ±200만 회 피로): 유리구슬 처리 → 3Y-PSZ 강도 유의 증가(p<0.001); 연삭 → 양쪽 모두 유의 감소(p<0.001).

속성 소결은 3Y-PSZ에 무영향(p=0.10)이나 4Y-PSZ 강도 유의 감소(p<0.001); 피로 하중 후 3Y-PSZ 안정, 4Y-PSZ 유의 감소(p<0.001); 최고 Weibull 계수: 연마된 4Y-PSZ(12.8), 유리구슬 3Y-PSZ(12.9).

3Y-PSZ는 고응력 임상 적용 및 속성 소결에 더 적합; 4Y-PSZ는 소결 프로토콜·표면처리 엄격 선택 필요 — 임상 연삭(grinding) 조정 시 강도 손상 주의.

## Summary

Aboushelib and Yousry 2026 provides experimental evidence resolving a clinically important question: can speed sintering be safely used for translucent zirconia? The answer is material-dependent. 3Y-PSZ tolerates speed sintering without significant strength loss, enabling efficient same-day workflows. In contrast, 4Y-PSZ (the more translucent, more esthetic type) shows significant strength reduction with speed sintering — requiring careful protocol selection. Surface treatment findings have direct clinical implications: glass bead abrasion enhances 3Y-PSZ strength (beneficial for adhesive cementation prep), while grinding — which simulates chairside occlusal adjustments — significantly reduces strength in both types. The fatigue data reinforces the fundamental strength-durability difference: 3Y-PSZ remains stable after 2 million cycles while 4Y-PSZ does not. Weibull modulus analysis confirms that polished 4Y-PSZ and glass-bead treated 3Y-PSZ offer the most reliable performance within their respective categories.

## Key Contributions

- Speed sintering validated for 3Y-PSZ (no strength penalty), contraindicated without protocol optimization for 4Y-PSZ
- Glass bead treatment (50 µm) significantly improves 3Y-PSZ strength — preferred surface treatment for adhesive protocols
- Grinding significantly reduces BOTH zirconia types — clinical chairside adjustments should be minimized and followed by polishing
- Fatigue data: 3Y-PSZ more durable (stable after 2M cycles) vs 4Y-PSZ (significant reduction)
- Weibull modulus: highest reliability in polished 4Y-PSZ (12.8) and glass-bead treated 3Y-PSZ (12.9)

## Methodology

In vitro experimental. 160 bar-shaped specimens (3Y-PSZ + 4Y-PSZ). Surface treatments: (1) polishing — control, (2) airborne-particle abrasion with 50 µm glass beads, (3) alumina abrasion, (4) grinding. Sintering: conventional vs speed protocol. Fatigue: half tested initial, half after 2 million cyclic loading. 4-point bending test. Weibull statistics, SEM microstructure analysis. ANOVA + Bonferroni post-hoc, α=0.05.

## Results

| Condition | 3Y-PSZ | 4Y-PSZ |
|---|---|---|
| Glass bead treatment | Significantly improved (p<0.001) | No significant change |
| Alumina abrasion | Intermediate effect | Intermediate effect |
| Grinding | Significantly reduced (p<0.001) | Significantly reduced (p<0.001) |
| Speed sintering | No significant effect (p=0.10) | Significantly decreased (p<0.001) |
| After 2M cyclic fatigue | Stable (p=0.082) | Significantly reduced (p<0.001) |
| Weibull modulus (highest) | Glass bead: 12.9 | Polished: 12.8 |
