## Three-line Summary

Bibliometric analysis of 2051 implant-abutment publications (Web of Science Core Collection, SCI-EXPANDED, 2005–2024; 1906 articles + 145 reviews) using VOSviewer, CiteSpace, bibliometrix, and Pajek.

Both publications and citations followed quadratic growth (R²=0.998 / 0.985; publication–citation r=0.883, P<.01); the USA led volume (462 pubs, H-index 75, 13 502 citations), São Paulo University led institutional output while Zurich/Bern led citation impact, Canullo and Sailer were the key authors, IJOMI + JPD were the top journals, and themes shifted from titanium/stress distribution (2005–2010) → platform switching/FEA (2011–2019) → zirconia/microbial leakage/stability (2020–2024).

This maps *research activity*, not clinical effectiveness — the zirconia and digital-workflow surge reflects evolving clinical priorities and persistent uncertainty (limited long-term zirconia data), NOT established consensus, and must not be read as evidence of what works.

출판·피인용 모두 2차 함수 성장(R²=0.998/0.985; 상관 r=0.883, P<.01); 미국 최다(462편, H-index 75, 피인용 13,502), 상파울루대 출판량 1위·취리히/베른대 피인용 임팩트 1위, 핵심 저자 Canullo·Sailer, 최다 저널 IJOMI·JPD; 주제는 티타늄/응력분포(2005–2010) → platform switching/유한요소분석(2011–2019) → 지르코니아/미생물 누출/안정성(2020–2024)으로 진화.

## Summary

This is a bibliometric map of two decades (2005–2024) of implant-abutment research, built from 2051 Web of Science Core Collection publications and analyzed with the standard scientometric toolchain (VOSviewer, CiteSpace, bibliometrix/Biblioshiny, Pajek). Its value to this wiki is as a **coverage audit**: it shows where the *field* has concentrated its effort, letting the wiki's deep mechanistic abutment pages (screw preload, microgap, emergence profile, hybrid abutments) be located against the overall research terrain — which clusters we already cover and which we have not yet ingested.

Publications and citations both grew quadratically, with a strong publication–citation correlation (r=0.883, P<.01). The intellectual landscape is dominated by the USA (volume and H-index), Brazil (São Paulo University institutional leadership), Germany, and Switzerland (highest citation efficiency via Straumann industry ties). Two Switzerland-based authors anchor the field through complementary pathways — Luigi Canullo (high output on the implant-abutment interface) and Irena Sailer (high-citation zirconia esthetics work).

Thematically, the corpus tells a clear story: an early titanium/stress-distribution focus (2005–2010), a mid-phase platform-switching and finite-element-analysis emphasis (2011–2019), and a recent pivot to zirconia abutments, microbial/bacterial leakage, and connection stability (2020–2024), overlaid with a rising digital-workflow current (CAD-CAM, intraoral scanning, additive manufacturing). Crucially, the authors caution that a keyword surge marks unresolved uncertainty, not consensus — long-term clinical evidence for zirconia abutments and dynamic-loading mechanical data remain sparse.

## Key Contributions

- **First abutment-specific bibliometric map.** Prior implant bibliometrics covered overall implants, overdentures, survival/failure, or prosthetic complications; this isolates the abutment as an independent research domain.
- Quantifies leading countries, institutions, authors, journals, co-citation clusters, keyword clusters, and citation bursts across 2005–2024.
- Frames the field's two dominant currents: the material shift (titanium → zirconia → hybrid) and the digital-workflow surge.

## Methodology

- **Database**: WOSCC / SCI-EXPANDED, searched February 2025.
- **Query**: `TS=("implant abutment*")` → 2351 records → filtered to 2005–2024, articles + reviews, English → **2051 publications** (1906 articles, 145 reviews).
- Two independent screeners with third-reviewer adjudication.
- **Software**: VOSviewer v1.6.20, Pajek v5.16, CiteSpace v6.4.R1 (Pathfinder pruning; co-citation, keyword co-occurrence, burst detection, dual-map overlay), bibliometrix v4.3.0, Excel, SPSS v26 (Pearson correlation). JCR 2025 impact indicators.
- Confidence label `narrative-review` (repo precedent for bibliometric mapping — it is a descriptive scientometric survey, not a graded evidence synthesis).

## Results

**Growth** — Annual output 23 (2005) → 156 (2023/24); cumulative-publication R²=0.9977, citation R²=0.9852; publication–citation r=0.8831 (P<.01); 10.6% annual growth. Corpus: 6312 authors, 29 171 references, 217 sources, 30.38% international co-authorship, 25.2 citations/doc.

**Countries** — USA 462 pubs / H-index 75 / 13 502 cit / TLS 383 (collaboration hub). Brazil 311, Germany 281, Italy 228, Spain 179, China 160, Switzerland 146 (highest 38.52 cit/article), Turkey 119, **Republic of Korea 108 (H-index 35)**, Japan 87. SCPs still predominate though MCPs rose.

**Institutions** — São Paulo (Brazil) 92 pubs (#1 volume); Zurich (2959 cit) and Bern (2351 cit) highest impact; NYU, Washington, Ohio State (USA); G. d'Annunzio (Italy); Geneva, Complutense Madrid; **Seoul National University 40 pubs / H-index 29**.

**Authors** — Dual pathway: Canullo (37 pubs, 1350 cit) vs Sailer (31 pubs, 1931 cit); Piattelli highest TLS (71). Both leaders Switzerland-based.

**Journals** — IJOMI (259 pubs, IF 1.7), J Prosthet Dent (207, IF 4.8), Clin Oral Implants Res (173, IF 5.3, 45 cit/article), CIDRR (97), J Prosthodont (85), and others.

**Reference co-citation clusters** — sealing material (2018), fracture resistance (2012), platform switching (2008/2014), abutment selection (2021), mechanical stability (2018), two-piece zirconia (2004), using titanium (2012), bacterial leakage (2010), dental prostheses (2014). Citation paths flow from "sealing material" toward fracture resistance, platform switching, mechanical stability, and bacterial leakage. Strongest reference burst Jung RE (2008); longest-sustained Canullo L (2010).

**Keyword clusters** — dental implants, fracture resistance, finite element analysis, complication rates, bacterial leakage, translucency, strength, platform switching, titanium implants, implant abutments.

**Temporal shift** — titanium/stress distribution (2005–2010) → platform switching/FEA (2011–2019) → zirconia/microbial leakage/stability (2020–2024). Strongest historical keyword burst "System" (strength 27.31, 2005–2013); recent bursts "zirconia," "stability," "connection," "microbial leakage."

**Limitations** — single database; single standardized query term; excludes non-peer-reviewed literature (where nascent trends often appear first); English-only. Explicitly a picture of research patterns, not clinical effectiveness.
