## Three-line Summary

3D finite element analysis comparing peri-implant stress across two internal connection designs (star tube-in-tube vs Morse+internal hex) × four customized abutment materials (titanium, Co-Cr, Co-Cr-S, zirconia) under axial (200 N) and oblique (100 N at 45°) loading.

Stresses concentrated in crestal cortical bone and were higher under oblique than axial loading; titanium abutments produced the lowest and most stable von Mises stress across all connection–load combinations (peak oblique Co-Cr 99.06 MPa vs titanium lower).

Titanium remains the safest abutment material for stress minimization; FEA assumptions limit direct clinical translation, and the finding should be validated in biomechanical bench or clinical studies.

## 세줄요약

3D 유한요소분석(Finite Element Analysis, FEA): 내부 연결부 두 종류(star tube-in-tube vs Morse+내부 헥스) × 맞춤 어버트먼트 재료 4종(티타늄·Co-Cr·Co-Cr-S·지르코니아) 조합에서 축 방향(200 N) 및 사선(100 N, 45°) 하중 하 변연부 응력 분포 비교.

응력은 치조정 피질골에 집중되고 사선 하중 > 축 방향; 티타늄 어버트먼트가 모든 연결부·하중 조합에서 가장 낮고 안정적인 폰 미제스(von Mises) 응력 (사선 하중 Co-Cr 최대 99.06 MPa vs 티타늄 더 낮음).

응력 최소화 측면에서 티타늄이 가장 안전한 어버트먼트 재료이나, FEA 가정(선형 탄성·이상화 기하·마이크로갭 제외)의 한계로 임상 해석 시 주의 필요.

## Summary
3D finite element analysis comparing peri-implant and implant-component stresses across two internal connection geometries and four abutment materials under axial and oblique loading.

## Key Contributions
Provides FEA evidence ranking abutment-material × connection combinations for implant-supported single crowns, useful when prosthetic material is a free design parameter.

## Methodology
3D FEA. Two implant-abutment connections (S = star tube-in-tube; H = Morse + hex). Four abutment materials. Axial 200 N and oblique 100 N @45° loads. Principal stresses and von Mises analyzed.

## Results
Stresses concentrated in crestal cortical bone. Oblique loading > axial. Highest oblique stress 99.06 MPa (H Co-Cr). Titanium abutments consistently lowest stress. Peak implant von Mises 135 MPa (S Co-Cr oblique).

## Limitations
FEA assumptions (linear elasticity, idealized geometry, no microgaps); single bone-model. Not a clinical outcome study.
