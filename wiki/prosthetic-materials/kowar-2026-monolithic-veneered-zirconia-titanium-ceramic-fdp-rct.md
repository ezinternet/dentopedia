## Three-line Summary

Prospective three-arm RCT (n=49 patients, 63 posterior short-span FDPs) at Brånemark Clinic, Gothenburg, Sweden; randomized to monolithic ZrO₂ (MZ), veneered ZrO₂ (VZ), or titanium-ceramic (TC) on multiunit abutments, 1-year follow-up (3/5 years ongoing).
Prosthetic survival: MZ 100%, VZ 95% (1 framework fracture), TC 95% (1 extensive chipping replaced); chipping rates: MZ 0%, VZ 5%, TC 11.1%; MBLC: MZ −0.18 mm, VZ −0.45 mm, TC −0.15 mm (no significant group differences).
At 1 year, monolithic ZrO₂ demonstrates zero chipping and 100% survival; VZ and TC share comparable technical complication rates; longer follow-up required for definitive material comparison.

## Summary

This prospective three-arm RCT from Brånemark Clinic, Gothenburg randomized 49 patients requiring posterior implant-supported short-span FDPs to monolithic ZrO₂ (MZ, n=23), veneered ZrO₂ (VZ, n=20), or titanium-ceramic (TC, n=20). All prostheses were screw-retained on multiunit abutments (Nobel Biocare). At 1 year, prosthetic survival was 100% (MZ), 95% (VZ, 1 framework fracture above mesial abutment due to close implant spacing requiring framework reduction), and 95% (TC, 1 extensive chipping leading to replacement). MZ showed zero chipping — VZ and TC each had 1–2 events. MBLC and implant survival (99.2% overall) did not differ significantly between groups. MZ's only complication was one screw loosening (4.8%). These 1-year results favor MZ for technical reliability; the study is adequately powered for 5-year outcomes.

## Key Contributions

- First three-arm RCT directly comparing MZ vs VZ vs TC for posterior implant-supported partial FDPs
- MZ achieves 0% chipping at 1 year — only complication is screw loosening (4.8%)
- VZ framework fracture mechanism identified: inadequate mesio-distal space → framework reduction → stress riser
- TC chipping rate (11.1%) consistent with prior SR/MA estimates; VZ rate (5%) at lower end
- MBLC and biological parameters (mucositis, implant survival) comparable across groups at 1 year

## Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Prospective RCT, 1-year (3/5-year follow-ups ongoing) |
| Setting | Brånemark Clinic (Folktandvården Västra Götaland), Gothenburg, Sweden |
| n | 49 patients, 63 FDPs; 59 available at 1-year (MZ 21, VZ 20, TC 18) |
| Implants | Brånemark System MKIII TiUnite + NobelParallel CC TiUnite (Nobel Biocare) |
| FDP design | 2–3 unit, non-cantilevered, short-span; screw-retained on multiunit abutments |
| MZ material | NobelProcera Zirconia Implant Bridge; Nacera Pearl Multi-Shade (3Y-TZP) |
| VZ material | Same ZrO₂ framework; Initial Zr-FS veneering ceramic (GC Europe) |
| TC material | NobelProcera Ti Implant Bridge Grade 2; GC Initial Titanium porcelain |
| Primary | Prosthetic survival + complication rates |
| Secondary | Implant survival, MBLC, biological complications |
| Registry | NCT05296291 |
| Funding | Nobel Biocare Services AG + Västra Götalandsregionen |

## Results

| Outcome | MZ (n=21) | VZ (n=20) | TC (n=18) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prosthetic survival | 100% | 95% (1 framework fx) | 95% (1 extensive chipping) |
| Chipping | 0% | 5% (1) | 11.1% (2) |
| Framework fracture | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| Screw loosening | 4.8% (1) | 0 | 0 |
| Implant survival | 97.7% | 100% | 100% |
| MBLC mean (mm) | −0.18 ± 0.77 | −0.45 ± 1.24 | −0.15 ± 0.77 |
| Peri-implant mucositis | 0% | 10% | 16.7% |

- No statistically significant differences in MBLC or technical/biological complications (all p>0.05)
- VZ framework fracture was a single outlier event attributed to technique (close implant spacing)
- Study was powered for 5-year complication rate differences; 1-year NS is expected by design
