## Three-line Summary

5-year multicenter RCT (n=47 patients, 233 implants) comparing maxillary implant-supported fixed complete dentures (FCDs) supported by four (4-I) versus six (6-I) implants, evaluating marginal bone level (MBL) change, complications, cost, and satisfaction.

Implant survival was 100% (4-I) vs 99.3% (6-I, one early failure); no significant MBL difference between groups at 5 years; the 4-I group showed significantly higher technical complications (16.6% vs 0%) but lower initial and total treatment cost; no peri-implantitis in either group.

The 4-I protocol is non-inferior to 6-I in radiographic MBL after 5 years, with comparable survival and biological/prosthetic complications, higher technical complications, but lower cost — supporting 4-I as a viable maxillary full-arch option; the retrieved PDF was an abstract-only Wiley landing-page printout, so detailed Methods/statistics were not available for this page.

## Summary

This 5-year multicenter randomized controlled trial assessed the non-inferiority of maxillary implant-supported fixed complete dentures (FCDs) supported by four implants (4-I) versus six implants (6-I), in terms of radiographic marginal bone level (MBL) changes. Forty-seven patients were rehabilitated with a total of 233 implants and followed at 1, 3, and 5 years, with clinical parameters (plaque index, bleeding on probing, pocket depth, keratinized mucosa width), periapical radiographs, and maintenance recorded at each visit. Implant survival was excellent in both groups (100% for 4-I, 99.3% for 6-I with one early failure), and MBL did not differ significantly between or within groups at 5 years. Both groups experienced a decrease in keratinized mucosa width and had prosthetic/biological complications, but no peri-implantitis in either group. The 4-I group had a significantly higher rate of technical complications (16.6% vs 0%), while cost analysis favored 4-I for both initial and total treatment costs. Satisfaction diverged by domain: patients/clinicians favored 4-I aesthetically and 6-I functionally (particularly speaking ability at earlier follow-ups). Overall, the trial supports 4-I as non-inferior to 6-I for long-term maxillary full-arch FCD rehabilitation, trading a higher technical-complication rate for lower cost.

## Key Contributions

- Provides 5-year, prospective, multicenter RCT-level evidence (rather than retrospective/registry data) directly comparing 4 vs 6 implants for maxillary FCDs — a long-standing implant-number debate in full-arch rehabilitation.
- Extends the same cohort previously reported at 3 years (Toia et al. 2021), giving longer-term durability data on the non-inferiority conclusion.
- Combines MBL, complication (biological/prosthetic/technical), cost, and satisfaction outcomes within a single trial, offering a multidimensional basis for implant-number decision-making rather than survival/MBL alone.

## Methodology

- Multicenter RCT; patients randomized to 4-I or 6-I groups receiving maxillary implant-supported fixed complete dentures.
- Follow-up visits at 1, 3, and 5 years post-loading; at each visit the FCD was unscrewed, periodontal-probe clinical parameters recorded (plaque index, bleeding on probing, pocket depth, keratinized mucosa width), periapical radiographs taken, and maintenance performed.
- Primary outcome: 5-year radiographic MBL change (4-I vs 6-I, non-inferiority framework).
- Secondary outcomes: incidence of technical, prosthetic, and biological complications; treatment costs; clinician and patient satisfaction.
- Sample: 47 patients, 233 implants total across both groups.

## Results

| Outcome | 4-I | 6-I |
|---|---|---|
| Implant survival | 100% | 99.3% (1 early failure) |
| MBL at 5 years | No significant difference vs 6-I or over time | No significant difference vs 4-I or over time |
| Keratinized mucosa width | Decreased over follow-up | Decreased over follow-up |
| Peri-implantitis | None | None |
| Technical complications | 16.6% (significantly higher) | 0% |
| Treatment cost (initial & total) | Lower | Higher |
| Satisfaction | Preferred aesthetically | Preferred functionally (esp. speaking, early follow-ups) |

Authors' conclusion: FCDs supported by four implants are non-inferior to six implants in terms of 5-year radiographic MBL change, with comparable survival and biological/prosthetic complication rates; 4-I is associated with higher technical complications but reduced overall treatment cost.
