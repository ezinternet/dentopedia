## Three-line Summary

This SR+MA of 8 in vitro studies compared marginal fit of CAD/CAM-fabricated zirconia and PEEK copings. Zirconia demonstrated numerically superior adaptation in 6/8 studies (reported gaps 17–60 µm) versus PEEK (30–108 µm, with milled forms consistently better than pressed/granular). Pooled meta-analysis (5 studies, n=126) showed SMD=1.72 µm (95% CI: −0.46 to 3.90, p=0.12), not statistically significant, with I²=95% heterogeneity; both materials met the clinical acceptability threshold of <120 µm.

## 세줄요약

CAD/CAM 지르코니아(Zirconia)·PEEK 코핑(coping) 변연적합도 (marginal fit)를 비교한 체외 연구 8편의 SR+MA. 지르코니아는 8편 중 6편에서 수치상 우수(17–60 µm 범위), PEEK는 밀링 가공 형태가 프레스·과립형 대비 일관되게 양호. 5편 메타분석(n=126)의 표준화 평균차 (SMD)=1.72 µm, p=0.12로 유의하지 않았고 I²=95%의 극심한 이질성 존재; 양재료 모두 임상적 허용 기준(<120 µm) 충족.

## Summary

Harimkar et al. (2025) conducted a PRISMA-compliant systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO: CRD42024604881) of in vitro studies comparing 변연적합도 (marginal fit) of 지르코니아 (Zirconia, ZrO₂) and 폴리에테르에테르케톤 (Polyetheretherketone, PEEK) copings fabricated by 컴퓨터 보조 설계/제조 (Computer-Aided Design/Manufacturing, CAD/CAM) technology.

**Included studies:** 8 in vitro studies (2019–2025); 4 from India, 4 from Egypt; sample sizes 18–40 per study. Half used extracted human teeth; half used typodont or standardized dies. Five-axis milling systems predominated.

**Primary finding:** Zirconia copings showed superior marginal adaptation in 6/8 studies. Specific examples: Attia 2019 (zirconia 43.98±1.3 µm vs. milled PEEK 45.22±6.09 µm — marginal advantage), Emad 2020 (zirconia 18.39±3.1 µm vs. PEEK 49.88±7.97 µm — large advantage), Emam 2023 (zirconia 48.67±11.98 µm vs. PEEK CAD 108.00±20.08 µm — large advantage). Two studies favored PEEK: Amalorpavam 2021 (PEEK 30.3±5.1 µm vs. zirconia 50.26±16.02 µm) and Chouksey 2023 (PEEK 33.99±8.81 µm vs. zirconia 56.21±15.07 µm).

**Processing-method effect:** Across studies evaluating multiple PEEK forms, CAD/CAM milled PEEK consistently outperformed pressed and granular variants. In Attia 2019: milled PEEK 45.22 µm < pellet PEEK 72.38 µm < granular PEEK 78.69 µm.

**Meta-analysis:** Pooled from 5 eligible studies (n=126 total, 63 per group). SMD = 1.72 µm (95% CI: −0.46 to 3.90), p=0.12 — not statistically significant. Heterogeneity: I²=95%, Tau²=5.76, Chi²=79.78 (df=4), p<0.00001. Funnel plot showed no strong evidence of publication bias; minor asymmetry attributed to methodological heterogeneity.

**Clinical acceptability:** All 8 studies reported marginal gaps for both materials below the 120 µm threshold universally regarded as clinically acceptable for cemented restorations.

**Risk of bias:** 2/8 studies low risk (Attia 2019, Amalorpavam 2021); 6/8 moderate risk. Most common deficiencies: absent sample randomization (6/8), no blinding of outcome assessors (5/8).

## Key Contributions

1. **Synthesis resolving conflicting individual studies:** pooled 8 studies that individually showed contradictory results into a unified framework, demonstrating that the apparent contradiction (zirconia vs. PEEK superiority) depends heavily on methodology and processing conditions.
2. **Processing method matters for PEEK:** milled PEEK is not equivalent to pressed or granular PEEK — a distinction with direct clinical implications for material specification.
3. **Heterogeneity quantification:** I²=95% signals that existing in-vitro methodology is too variable to support definitive material rankings; standardized protocols are required.
4. **Both materials are clinically viable:** all included studies confirmed <120 µm marginal gaps for both materials under controlled in-vitro conditions.

## Methodology

- **Study design**: PRISMA-compliant SR+MA; PROSPERO CRD42024604881
- **Search period**: 2016–2025; PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar, Embase, EBSCOHost
- **Eligibility**: In vitro studies; CAD/CAM fabrication; both zirconia AND PEEK; quantitative marginal gap (mean±SD)
- **Measurement techniques across included studies**: 전계 방출 주사전자현미경 (Field Emission Scanning Electron Microscopy, FE-SEM) (n=2), 실체현미경 (Stereomicroscopy) (n=2), digital microscopy+ImageJ (n=2), 3D triple-scan/PolyWorks (n=1), handheld 40× microscope (n=1)
- **Statistical method**: RevMan; SMD; random-effects model (I²>50%); 이질성 (Heterogeneity) assessed by I² statistic and Chi²
- **Risk of bias**: QUIN tool (9 domains): clarity of aim, sample size justification, preparation standardization, randomization, blinding, operator consistency, outcome measurement, statistical reporting, limitations acknowledgment

**Meta-analysis summary (5 studies, n=126):**
- SMD = 1.72 µm (95% CI: −0.46 to 3.90)
- p = 0.12 (not significant)
- I² = 95% (substantial heterogeneity)
- Direction: numerically favored PEEK in the pooled estimate, contrasting with 6/8 individual studies favoring zirconia — artifact of which 5 studies were poolable and direction of SMD coding

**Key threshold:** Clinical acceptability cut-off = 120 µm; all studies for both materials remained below this threshold.
