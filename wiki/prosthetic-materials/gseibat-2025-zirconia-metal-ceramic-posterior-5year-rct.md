## Three-line Summary

5-year prospective RCT comparing translucent zirconia (4Y–5Y-TZP) vs metal-ceramic posterior crowns fabricated in a digital workflow.

5-year survival was equivalent (zirconia 96% vs metal-ceramic 97%) and USPHS clinical acceptability was similar, but chipping occurred in 3 metal-ceramic crowns versus zero in the zirconia group.

Translucent monolithic zirconia fabricated digitally eliminates veneer chipping risk while maintaining equivalent survival — supporting its use as the default posterior crown material.

## Summary
5-year prospective RCT comparing translucent zirconia vs metal-ceramic posterior crowns fabricated in a digital workflow.

## Key Contributions
- 5-year survival: zirconia 96% vs metal-ceramic 97% (equivalent)
- Chipping: metal-ceramic 3 events vs zirconia 0
- USPHS criteria evaluation: both groups similar clinical acceptability

## Methodology
Prospective RCT; 5-year follow-up; posterior crowns in digital workflow.

## Results
Translucent zirconia (4Y-5Y-TZP) eliminates chipping risk vs metal-ceramic while maintaining equivalent survival. Digital workflow suitable for both materials.
