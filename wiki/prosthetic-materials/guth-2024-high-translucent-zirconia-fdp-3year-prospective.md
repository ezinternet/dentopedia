Prospective pilot (N=22, posterior 3-unit FDPs from 5Y-PSZ Lava Esthetic, 3yr) showing 100% survival with zero mechanical complications and all FDI criteria acceptable — first published clinical evidence for monolithic high-translucent cubic zirconia in multi-unit FDPs.

## Summary

This prospective single-arm pilot study at LMU Munich evaluated 22 posterior 3-unit FDPs fabricated from high-translucent shade-graded 5Y-PSZ zirconia (Lava Esthetic, Solventum Dental Solutions) over a 3-year follow-up. Restorations were milled on a 5-axis CAD/CAM system (Ceramill Motion 2, Amann Girrbach) and cemented with air abrasion (50 μm alumina) followed by RelyX Universal self-adhesive resin cement.

At 3 years, survival was 100% (21/21 evaluated; 1 dropout for relocation). No mechanical complications occurred — zero chipping, fractures, or loss of retention. Three biological complications arose (endodontic treatment, occlusal sensitivity, cervical caries), all successfully managed. All FDI criteria for esthetic, functional, and biological parameters were rated clinically acceptable or better. PPD remained stable at 2.2–2.8 mm. Glaze layer wear was visible in 16 restorations after 2 years — expected and not classified as failure.

This represents the first published clinical data on 5Y-PSZ cubic zirconia in multi-unit FDPs. The material had previously been limited to single crowns due to concerns about its lower fracture resistance vs 3Y-TZP, but the monolithic design with appropriate connector dimensions offset this.

## Key Contributions

- First prospective clinical data on 5Y-PSZ (cubic, high-translucent) zirconia for multi-unit posterior FDPs
- Confirms zero mechanical complications at 3 years — monolithic design eliminates chipping risk
- Material characterization: Lava Esthetic — 800 MPa flexural strength, 1,200 HV, 216 GPa elastic modulus
- Validates air abrasion (50 μm Al₂O₃) + RelyX Universal cement protocol for 5Y-PSZ FDPs
- Worst-case survival (1 dropout counted as failure): 95.5% at 3 years

## Methodology

**Study design:** Prospective single-arm pilot study

**Ethics:** UE No 19–0243; LMU Munich

**Sample:** 22 patients, 22 posterior 3-unit FDPs (44 abutment teeth); 18 maxillary, 26 mandibular; 4 FDPs at P1-M1, 18 at P2-M2

**Material:** Lava Esthetic 5Y-PSZ (Solventum); 800 MPa flexural strength, 1,200 HV, 216 GPa elastic modulus; shade-graded; inherent fluorescence

**Fabrication:** CAD design → 5-axis milling (Ceramill Motion 2, Amann Girrbach) → sintering → stain and glaze

**Cementation:** Air abrasion 50 μm Al₂O₃ at 0.1 MPa + RelyX Universal dual-cure self-adhesive resin cement (no chemical conditioning)

**Follow-up:** Baseline (4 wks), 1yr, 2yr, 3yr; modified FDI criteria; PPD; radiographs; patient satisfaction questionnaire

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| 3-year survival | 100% (21/21) |
| Worst-case survival | 95.5% (1 dropout counted as failure) |
| Mechanical complications | 0 (zero chipping, fractures, loss of retention) |
| Biological complications | 3/21 (14.3%; ~4.76%/yr) |
| FDI criteria | All acceptable or better |
| PPD | 2.2–2.8 mm (stable) |

**Biological complications:** endodontic treatment of abutment (preparation trauma; FDP remained in service), occlusal sensitivity (resolved with adjustment), cervical caries (GIC filling; asymptomatic). All successfully managed.

**FDI observations:** glaze layer wear visible in 16/21 restorations after 2 years (expected; not classified as failure); marginal adaptation showed slight decline within "just perceptible" range at 3yr.
