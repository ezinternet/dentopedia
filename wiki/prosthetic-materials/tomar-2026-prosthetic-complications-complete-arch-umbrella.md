## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (7 systematic reviews, ≥5-year follow-up, PRISMA+JBI methodology) of implant-supported complete arch fixed prostheses (metal-ceramic, metal-resin, veneered zirconia, monolithic zirconia; screw- or cement-retained; 4–8 implants/arch).

Prosthetic survival 89.5–100% and implant survival 95.2–99.5% over 5–15 years; screw loosening 5–15%, veneer chipping 15–35% (significantly higher in veneered vs monolithic zirconia, P<.05), framework fractures <5%, peri-implantitis 4–18% (higher with cement retention); implant number (4 vs ≥5) did not significantly affect survival or bone loss (P>.05).

Screw-retained and monolithic zirconia designs show the most favorable long-term complication profile, but the synthesis is qualitative (no new pooled meta-analysis) and constrained by AMSTAR-2 confidence ranging low-to-high across the 7 included reviews.

## 세줄요약

엄브렐러 리뷰 (5년 이상 추적관찰 체계적고찰 7편, PRISMA+JBI 방법론) — 임플란트 지지 완전 궁 고정성 보철물 (금속-세라믹, 금속-레진, 비니어드/모놀리식 지르코니아; 나사 또는 시멘트 유지; 궁당 임플란트 4–8개) 대상.

보철물 생존율 89.5–100%, 임플란트 생존율 95.2–99.5% (5–15년); 나사 풀림 5–15%, 비니어 치핑 15–35% (비니어드 지르코니아가 모놀리식보다 유의하게 높음, P<.05), 프레임워크 파절 <5%, 임플란트주위염 4–18% (시멘트 유지 시 더 높음); 임플란트 개수(4개 대 5개 이상)는 생존율·골소실에 유의한 영향 없음 (P>.05).

나사 유지 및 모놀리식 지르코니아 디자인이 장기적으로 가장 우수한 합병증 프로파일을 보이나, 종합은 정성적 서술(새로운 메타분석 미실시)이며 포함된 7편의 AMSTAR-2 신뢰도가 낮음~높음으로 혼재해 제약이 있다.

## Summary

This umbrella review synthesizes 7 systematic reviews (published 2009–2019, each requiring ≥5-year mean follow-up) evaluating prosthetic complications, implant survival, and biological outcomes of implant-supported complete arch fixed prostheses. Frameworks assessed include metal-ceramic, metal-resin, veneered zirconia, and monolithic zirconia, retained by screws or cement, supported by 4–8 implants per arch. Prosthetic survival ranged 89.5–100% and implant survival 95.2–99.5% over 5–15 years, confirming long-term reliability of this treatment approach. Technical complications (screw loosening 5–15%, veneer chipping 15–35% in veneered zirconia, framework fracture <5%) and biological complications (peri-implantitis 4–18%, marginal bone loss 1.2–1.5 mm) were common but generally manageable. Notably, veneered zirconia showed significantly more chipping than monolithic zirconia (P<.05), and cement retention was consistently linked to higher rates of peri-implant biological complications than screw retention. The number of supporting implants (4 vs ≥5) did not significantly affect implant survival, prosthetic survival, or marginal bone loss (P>.05), supporting the predictability of reduced-implant-count ("All-on-4"-type) protocols when biomechanical principles are respected. AMSTAR-2 quality of the 7 included reviews ranged from low (1 review) to moderate (5 reviews) to high (1 review), and primary-study overlap (CCA) was moderate at 10.8%.

## Key Contributions

- Restricts inclusion to SRs with **≥5-year follow-up**, addressing the fragmented, short-follow-up bias of prior complete-arch complication literature (24 candidate reviews were excluded specifically for <5-year follow-up).
- Quantifies technical complication ranges across the full arch: screw loosening 5–15%, veneer chipping 15–35% (veneered zirconia), framework fracture <5%.
- Quantifies biological complications: peri-implantitis 4–18% (higher with cement retention), marginal bone loss 1.2–1.5 mm at 5–10 years.
- Statistically confirms material effect: veneered zirconia chips significantly more than monolithic zirconia (P<.05); metal-ceramic and metal-resin show comparable performance (P=.38).
- Statistically confirms implant-number effect is null: 4 vs ≥5 implants shows no significant difference in implant survival, prosthetic survival, or marginal bone loss (P>.05).
- Provides an AMSTAR-2 quality map (Table 4) and CCA overlap quantification (10.8%, moderate) for the 7 included reviews, enabling weighting of the strength of each underlying conclusion.

## Methodology

- PRISMA 2020 + JBI umbrella review guidance; protocol registered on PROSPERO (CRD420251143650).
- Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Cochrane Library, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar, LILACS (Jan 2000–Aug 2025).
- Inclusion: SRs/meta-analyses of implant-supported complete arch fixed or removable prostheses, ≥5-year follow-up, reporting prosthesis/implant survival, technical or biological complications, and/or patient-reported outcomes.
- 547 records identified → 195 screened → 93 full-text assessed → **7 systematic reviews included** (PRISMA 2020 flow diagram).
- Quality appraisal via AMSTAR-2 (16 domains, overall confidence rating); overlap quantified via corrected covered area (CCA) = 10.8%.
- Synthesis is narrative/qualitative — pooled estimates and P values were extracted verbatim from primary reviews where reported; no new quantitative meta-analysis was performed.

## Results

| Outcome | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Prosthetic survival | 89.5–100% | 5–15 years |
| Implant survival | 95.2–99.5% | 5–15 years |
| Screw loosening | 5–15% | technical complication |
| Veneer chipping (veneered zirconia) | 15–35% | significantly > monolithic zirconia (P<.05) |
| Framework fracture | <5% | metal-ceramic ≈ metal-resin (P=.38) |
| Peri-implantitis | 4–18% | higher with cement retention |
| Marginal bone loss | 1.2–1.5 mm | 5–10 years; unaffected by implant number |
| Implant number (4 vs ≥5) | no significant difference | survival and MBL (P>.05) |

AMSTAR-2 confidence of the 7 included reviews: 1 high (Wong 2018), 5 moderate (Daudt Polido 2018, de Luna Gomes 2019, Harder 2009, Lima 2018, Papaspyridakos 2013), 1 low (Kwon 2014). Publication bias formally testable in only 2 of 7 reviews (Egger test, both P>.05).
