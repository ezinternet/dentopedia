## Three-line Summary

A meta-analysis of 9 in vitro studies found milled zirconia prostheses have significantly better marginal fit than 3D-printed ones (MD = −13.12 µm; P < .001), while internal adaptation is equivalent between techniques (P = .30). Both methods produce clinically acceptable restorations. High heterogeneity (I² ≈ 92%) and exclusive reliance on in vitro single-crown data limit generalizability.

## 세줄요약

9편의 체외 연구를 메타분석한 결과, 밀링 지르코니아가 3D 프린팅보다 변연적합도가 통계적으로 유의하게 우수하며(MD = −13.12 µm, P < .001), 내부적합도는 두 기법 간 차이가 없었다(P = .30). 두 기법 모두 임상 허용 기준을 충족했다. 높은 이질성(I² ≈ 92%)과 단일 크라운 중심의 체외 설계가 결론의 한계다.

## Summary

This SR+MA (Sengottaiyan et al., 2026) is the first pooled quantitative comparison of 변연적합도 (marginal fit) and 내부적합도 (internal adaptation) for 지르코니아 완전 피개 고정성 보철물 (zirconia complete coverage fixed dental prostheses) fabricated by 절삭 가공 (milling, subtractive manufacturing) versus 3D 프린팅 (3D printing, additive manufacturing).

Of 2,205 records screened across five databases, 9 in vitro studies met inclusion criteria. The majority examined single crowns; one study addressed a 4-unit 고정성 치아 보철물 (fixed dental prosthesis, FDP).

**Clinical acceptability**: Both techniques produced clinically acceptable marginal fit and internal adaptation, meaning neither is contraindicated based on fit accuracy alone.

**Key distinction**: Milling holds a statistically significant marginal fit advantage, but the absolute difference (~13 µm) is modest and both groups fall within the commonly cited clinical threshold of < 120 µm.

## Key Contributions

1. **Milling superiority for marginal fit** — pooled MD = −13.12 µm favoring milling (95% CI: −20.21 to −6.03; P < .001)
2. **Internal adaptation equivalence** — no significant difference (MD = −3.26 µm; P = .30)
3. **High heterogeneity** — I² = 91.98% (marginal fit) and 85.93% (internal adaptation); caution required in applying pooled estimates
4. **Limited FDP data** — only 1 of 9 studies included multi-unit prostheses; most evidence is single-crown

## Results

### Marginal Fit (변연적합도)

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean Difference | −13.12 µm (milling better) |
| 95% CI | −20.21 to −6.03 µm |
| P-value | < .001 |
| I² | 91.98% (high heterogeneity) |

Milled zirconia prostheses produced statistically significantly smaller marginal gaps. Both groups remain within the clinically acceptable range.

### Internal Adaptation (내부적합도)

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean Difference | −3.26 µm |
| 95% CI | −9.41 to 2.88 µm |
| P-value | .30 (NS) |
| I² | 85.93% (high heterogeneity) |

No significant difference between fabrication techniques.

### Clinical Interpretation

- Both milling and 3D printing produce **clinically acceptable** fit for zirconia crowns
- The ~13 µm marginal fit advantage of milling is statistically real but clinically modest
- High heterogeneity (I² > 75%) means pooled estimates should be interpreted with caution; study-to-study variation is large
- 3D printing materials/hardware evolve rapidly — older included studies may underestimate current 3D printing accuracy
