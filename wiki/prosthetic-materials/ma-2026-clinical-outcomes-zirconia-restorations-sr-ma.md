## Background

Zirconia has become the dominant all-ceramic material for fixed prosthodontics due to its high flexural strength and biocompatibility. However, clinical performance varies substantially by restoration type, adhesive protocol, and follow-up duration. Prior SRs were limited by restoration type or short follow-up. This SR+MA covers all zirconia restoration types in natural teeth with up to 13+ years follow-up, providing the most comprehensive current benchmark.

## Methods

- **Search**: PubMed, Cochrane, Embase, Web of Science (up to January 2026)
- **Inclusion**: clinical studies ≥1-year follow-up, zirconia restorations in natural teeth
- **Included**: 66 studies (2008-2025)
- **Risk of bias**: RoB 2 (RCTs), Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS, observational), Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI, other)
- **Evidence certainty**: GRADE
- **Outcomes**: pooled survival, success, biological/mechanical complication rates

## Results

### Survival Rates (pooled)

| Restoration Type | Pooled Survival | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Single Crown (SC) | 92.9–98.7% | ≤10 yr |
| Fixed Dental Prosthesis (FPD) | 77.6% | 10–13 yr |
| Resin-Bonded FPD (RBFPD) | 97.4% | 10–15 yr |
| Inlay-Retained FPD (IRFPD) | 89.0% | 10 yr |
| Cantilever FPD (CFPD) | 50.0–52.6% | 9–13 yr |

### Success Rates (pooled)

| Restoration Type | Pooled Success | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| SC | 46.1% | 10 yr |
| FPD | 40.1% | 10–13 yr |
| RBFPD | 84.3% | 10–15 yr |
| IRFPD | 70.3% | 10 yr |
| CFPD | 12.5–22.6% | 9–13 yr |

Note: Low success rates reflect cumulative complication burden, not restoration loss.

### Complication Rates

| Restoration | Biological | Mechanical | Timepoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC | 12.1% | 53.9% | 10 yr |
| FPD | 19.4% | 46.7% | 10–13 yr |
| RBFPD | 3.3% | 8.0% | 10–15 yr |
| IRFPD | 21.4% | 14.3% | 3 yr |
| CFPD | 50.0% | 37.5% | 9 yr |

- **Biological**: secondary caries, pulpitis, apical periodontitis
- **Mechanical**: chipping (veneering ceramic), debonding

### Surface Treatment — APA+MDP Protocol

| Restoration | Survival (APA+MDP) | Success (APA+MDP) |
|---|---|---|
| SC | 98.6% | 97.7% |
| IRFPD | 98.1% | 70.1% |

APA (airborne-particle abrasion) + MDP-containing primer currently represents the evidence-based adhesive surface treatment for zirconia.

### Evidence Quality
- Risk of bias: low to moderate (RoB 2, NOS, JBI)
- GRADE: **very low certainty** for all restoration types
- Interpretation: directional signals only; definitive claims require higher-certainty evidence

## Clinical Significance

1. **RBFPD is the most conservative and complication-resistant zirconia fixed option** for single tooth replacement when abutments are intact (97.4% survival at 10-15yr, lowest complication rates)
2. **IRFPD (Maryland bridge with inlay retainer)**: acceptable 10yr survival (89%) but lower than wing-retained RBFPD; suitable when abutment teeth already need restorations
3. **CFPD should be used with caution** — survival drops to 50% at 9-13yr with high complication burden
4. **APA+MDP is the recommended surface treatment protocol** for all zirconia adhesive applications
5. **Success ≠ Survival**: high mechanical complication rates (especially chipping in SC/FPD) explain the wide gap between survival and success

## Limitations

- GRADE very low certainty across all types
- Heterogeneity in study designs, preparation protocols, cement systems
- Long-term data for IRFPD limited
- Rapid evolution of zirconia materials (4Y-, 5Y-TZP) may not be reflected in included studies
