## Three-line Summary
Cochrane systematic review (2018 update, original 2016) searching for RCTs evaluating any intervention for treating post-extraction bleeding (PEB) — **zero RCTs met inclusion criteria**.
No randomised controlled trial evidence exists for effectiveness of any available intervention (local haemostats, suturing, systemic agents, combinations) for treating PEB.
Clinicians must rely on clinical experience; well-designed RCTs conforming to CONSORT are urgently needed.

## Summary
This Cochrane Intervention Review (update of the 2016 version) assessed the effects of interventions for treating post-extraction bleeding (PEB), defined as bleeding continuing beyond 8–12 hours after dental extraction. The review searched multiple databases up to 24 January 2018 (Cochrane Oral Health Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, CINAHL, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP). **No randomised controlled trials met the inclusion criteria.** The authors categorised interventions into local (surgical: suturing; non-surgical haemostatics: gauze, oxidised cellulose, gel foam, thrombin, collagen fleeces, tranexamic acid mouthwash, fibrin glue, chitosan-based dressings, bone wax, etc.) and systemic (antifibrinolytics, vitamin K, etc.), as well as combinations. Despite the wide array of techniques used in clinical practice, **there is no RCT evidence for the effectiveness of any intervention** for treating PEB. Primary outcomes (bleeding cessation, blood loss, time to control, patient-reported pain/discomfort, cost, adverse events) had no data. The authors conclude that clinicians must rely on clinical experience, and **well-designed, adequately powered RCTs conforming to CONSORT are urgently needed** to inform practice.

## Key Contributions
- 발치 후 출혈(PEB) 치료에 대한 **RCT 근거가 전무함**을 코크란 수준에서 공식 확인 — '근거 부재(evidence gap)'를 명시한 드문 리뷰
- PEB 정의(Lockhart 4기준: 12시간 지속, 환자 재내원/응급실행, 거대 혈종/반상출혈, 수혈/입원 필요) 및 분류(1차/반응성/2차 지연출혈, Abdullah 경도/중등도/중증) 체계적 정리
- 임상 현장 쓰이는 모든 국소 지혈제·봉합·전신약물·복합요법을 범주화해 나열 — 향후 RCT 설계 시 비교군 설정 참고용
- **근거 공백(evidence gap) 명시** — CONSORT 준수 고품질 RCT 긴급 필요성 강조

## Methodology
- **Design**: Cochrane systematic review (Intervention Review) — standard Cochrane methodology
- **Databases**: Cochrane Oral Health Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE Ovid (1946–2018), Embase Ovid (2015–2018), CINAHL EBSCO (1937–2018), ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP
- **Selection**: RCTs evaluating any intervention for PEB; any age/sex/tooth type; comparison vs other intervention, placebo, or no treatment
- **n**: **0 RCTs included** (no studies met criteria)
- **Outcomes**: Primary — bleeding (blood loss amount, complete cessation assessed clinically, time to control); patient-reported pain/discomfort; treatment cost; adverse events
- **Analysis**: No meta-analysis possible; narrative summary of evidence gap; GRADE not applicable (no studies)
