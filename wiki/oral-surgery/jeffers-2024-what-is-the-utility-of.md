## Three-line Summary

Evidence summary (emergency medicine, 2024) answering whether point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) can separate soft-tissue abscess from cellulitis, built from four systematic reviews and meta-analyses of POCUS for diagnosing abscess.

The abstract reports no pooled numbers; it concludes that POCUS "can reliably differentiate abscess and cellulitis and assist with management" of skin and soft-tissue infections.

Non-dental skin/soft-tissue evidence from an abstract only; the figures behind the conclusion were not retrieved, so it cannot be used to quote accuracy values.

## 세줄요약

응급의학 근거 요약(2024): 연부조직 농양과 봉와직염을 POCUS로 구분할 수 있는지를, POCUS 농양 진단을 다룬 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 4편으로 정리.

## Summary

In the emergency department, cellulitis and abscess often cannot be told apart by history and exam alone, and POCUS has been proposed as an aid. This summary draws on four systematic reviews and meta-analyses and concludes POCUS is useful for the distinction.

## Key Contributions

- Brings the skin and soft-tissue POCUS literature (four SR/MAs) into one conclusion on abscess vs cellulitis.

## Methodology

- Abstract only: the evidence is four systematic reviews and meta-analyses; no further method is stated.

## Results

- Conclusion without numbers: POCUS can reliably differentiate abscess and cellulitis and assist management.

## Limitations (reader's appraisal)

- Abstract only; no accuracy figures. Skin and soft-tissue infection, not odontogenic; do not transfer to facial or dental infections without the dental data ([[oral-surgery/frid-2025-diagnostic-accuracy-and-clinical-impact]] found lower accuracy in that setting).
