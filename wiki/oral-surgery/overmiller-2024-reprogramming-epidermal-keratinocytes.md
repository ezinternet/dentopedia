## One-line Summary

Transgenic mouse model with ectopic epidermal PITX1 expression shows that PITX1 is a master determinant of oral keratinocyte identity — driving proliferation, migration, and faster full-thickness wound healing — confirmed by scRNA-Seq and Xenium spatial transcriptomics.

## Summary

Oral wounds heal faster and with less scarring than skin wounds. This study identifies PITX1 — a homeodomain transcription factor expressed in oral epithelium but absent from skin epidermis — as a key driver of this difference. Using an inducible transgenic mouse model, PITX1 was expressed ectopically in epidermal keratinocytes. Single-cell RNA-Seq and Xenium in situ spatial transcriptomics of control skin, PITX1 skin, and buccal mucosa revealed that PITX1 drives skin keratinocytes to adopt an oral-like transcriptional state, with increased expression of activated KC genes (KRT6A, KRT16, S100A8) that are constitutively expressed in oral mucosa. PITX1 skin had significantly increased KC proliferation (PCNA) and migration in vitro and in vivo, and full-thickness wounds healed faster at days 2–8 post-injury. PITX1 also recruited neutrophils to the skin via pro-inflammatory keratinocyte-derived signals, establishing a pro-wound-resolution immune environment. CUT&Tag-Seq confirmed PITX1 directly binds and activates wound-healing and keratinization pathway genes.

## Key Contributions

- PITX1 is highly expressed in oral epithelium but **undetectable in skin epidermis** — oral-specific identity factor
- Ectopic PITX1 drives oral-like pseudotime gene expression in skin KCs; higher expression of ALDH1A3, KRT6A, KRT16, S100A8/A9, CRABP2 — all direct PITX1 CUT&Tag targets
- PITX1 skin KCs are **more proliferative and migratory** than control — matching the intrinsic properties of oral KCs
- **Full-thickness wound healing accelerated** in PITX1 mice (days 2–8): greater granulation tissue area and IFE migration distance at day 4
- Tissue-wide effect: fibroblasts, immune cells, and intercellular signaling networks all shift toward a buccal-mucosa-like state
- Alopecia occurs as an off-target consequence: PITX1 disrupts hair follicle stem cell renewal

## Methodology

**Design**: Tet-On TRE-/-rtTA inducible transgenic mice fed doxycycline chow for 6 weeks; n=8 control + 8 PITX1 for scRNA-Seq; n=7 buccal mucosa pools; total 331,572 Xenium cells analyzed. Full-thickness 6 mm excisional dorsal wounds, tracked days 0–35. CUT&Tag-Seq for PITX1 + H3K4me3 occupancy. In vitro scratch and proliferation assays.
