## Three-line Summary

Abstract-only case report of a 62-year-old man presenting to the emergency department with airway compromise from a floor-of-mouth hematoma after implant surgery in the edentulous anterior mandible, caused by two malpositioned implants perforating the lingual cortical plate. Computed tomography angiography showed floor-of-mouth elevation with airway occlusion. Awake fiberoptic intubation was performed immediately, the two malpositioned implants were removed, and extubation occurred after 3 days.

## 세줄요약

62세 남성이 무치악 전방 하악 임플란트 수술 중 발생한 구강저 (Floor of the Mouth, FOM) 혈종으로 응급실에 내원하여 기도 폐쇄를 보인 단일 증례 보고이다. 전산화단층촬영 혈관조영술 (Computed Tomography Angiography, CTA)에서 구강저 상승과 기도 폐쇄, 두 개의 부정위치 임플란트로 인한 설측 피질골 천공이 확인되었다. 즉시 각성 굴곡기관지경 삽관 (Awake Fiberoptic Intubation)을 시행하고, 부정위치 임플란트 두 개를 제거한 뒤 3일 후 발관하였다.

## Summary

A single case report: a 62-year-old man developed a floor-of-mouth hematoma with airway compromise following dental implant placement in the edentulous anterior mandible. Two malpositioned implants had perforated the lingual mandibular cortical plate. CTA showed elevation of the floor of the mouth with occlusion of the airway. Management consisted of immediate awake fiberoptic intubation, subsequent removal of the two malpositioned implants, and extubation after 3 days.

## Key Contributions

- Documents a rare but life-threatening complication: floor-of-mouth hematoma with airway obstruction after anterior mandible implant surgery.
- Identifies lingual cortical plate perforation by malpositioned implants as the causative mechanism.
- Demonstrates successful airway rescue via awake fiberoptic intubation with implant removal and 3-day intubation.

## Methodology

Single case report; no comparison group or controlled protocol. Abstract only.
