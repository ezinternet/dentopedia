## One-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (n=36 T2DM patients, 72 extractions) — hyaluronic acid gel accelerated socket healing at D7/D14 but by D21 both HA and control groups reached complete soft-tissue closure (healing index 4, socket fully closed) without antibiotics.

## Summary

Ruggiero et al. 2024 is a split-mouth RCT from the University of Turin enrolling 36 T2DM patients with a history of diabetes complications (nephropathy, neuropathy, retinopathy, cardiopathy). Each patient underwent bilateral homologous tooth extraction; one site received topical HA gel (3×/day for 7 days) and the contralateral site received no treatment. Follow-up at days 3, 7, 14, and 21 assessed healing index (modified Landry 1–4) and socket closure dimensions (OV/MD diameter, socket depth). HA significantly improved healing index at D7 (p=0.01) and D14 (p=0.02) and socket closure at D3/D7/D14, but the key finding is that by D21 all sockets regardless of treatment achieved complete healing (p=1). This establishes 21 days as the outer boundary for soft-tissue epithelial closure in T2DM patients with active complications, even without intervention. No infections or antibiotic prescriptions occurred in either group.

## Key Contributions

- Provides 3/7/14/21-day granular healing timeline for T2DM post-extraction socket — **D21 = complete epithelial closure outer boundary** even in complicated diabetics without treatment.
- HA reduces the number of patients requiring D21 to achieve excellent healing: 1 of 36 (HA) vs. 10 of 36 (control) needed the full 21 days.
- Challenges routine antibiotic prophylaxis for well-managed T2DM extractions — 0 infections in either group.
- 100% of patients preferred HA for future extractions; 89% noted more bleeding in untreated site.

## Methodology

Single-center split-mouth RCT (CONSORT 2010); n=36 T2DM patients with prior diabetes complication history; bilateral homologous non-impacted extractions; HA gel (sodium hyaluronate + amino acids, aminogam) 3×/day for 7 days vs. no treatment. Blinded examiners (Cohen's kappa 0.78–0.80 agreement). Outcomes: healing index (modified Landry 1–4 for secondary intention), OV/MD socket diameter, socket depth, VAS, patient questionnaire. Follow-up: D0, D3, D7, D14, and D21 (if needed). Statistics: Mann-Whitney or Student's t-test, chi-square/Fisher; p<0.05 significant.

## Results

| Timepoint | Healing Index | Socket Closure |
|---|---|---|
| D3 | NS (p=0.08) | p=0.04 (HA better) |
| D7 | p=0.01 (HA better) | p=0.001 (HA better) |
| D14 | p=0.02 (HA better) | p=0.001 (HA better) |
| D21 | p=1 (equal, 100% both groups) | p=1 (equal) |

- **Excellent healing (index=4) at D14**: HA 97.2% vs. control 72.2% (p=0.004)
- **D21 complete healing**: 100% both groups
- **Antibiotics needed**: 0 in both groups
- **VAS pain**: HA significantly lower at D3, D7, D14; equal at D21
