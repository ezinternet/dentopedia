## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (case-illustration based; Cebrian-Carretero et al., 2014) of implant-guided surgery for dental rehabilitation in patients with maxillomandibular reconstruction using microvascular free flaps (fibula, iliac crest, scapula) after oncologic resection.

Guided surgery is argued to be essential — not optional — enabling implant positioning in the highest-quality bone regions of the flap, avoiding osteosynthesis hardware conflicts, and achieving prosthetically-driven planning despite extreme anatomic constraints.

No quantitative outcome synthesis; conclusion is based on narrative rationale and case illustrations — limitations include the absence of controlled data on guided vs freehand outcomes in free-flap jaws.

## 세줄요약

서술적 문헌고찰(Narrative Review; 증례 기반; Cebrian-Carretero et al., 2014) — 종양 절제 후 미세혈관 유리피판술(Microvascular Free Flap; fibula·장골능·견갑골)로 상하악 재건된 환자에서 임플란트 가이드 수술(Implant-Guided Surgery)의 역할 검토.

Guided surgery는 선택이 아닌 필수 — 피판 내 골 조건 최적 부위 선택·골접합재료(Osteosynthesis Hardware) 회피·극심한 해부학적 제약 하 보철 중심 계획 수립을 가능하게 함.

정량 결과 종합 없음; 서술적 근거와 증례 예시에 기반 — 유리피판 악골에서 guided vs freehand 방식의 대조 데이터 부재가 핵심 한계.

## Summary
Narrative review on guided implant surgery for dental rehabilitation in patients reconstructed with microvascular free flaps after maxillomandibular oncologic resection.

## Key Contributions
Articulates why guided surgery is essential (not optional) in free-flap-reconstructed jaws: bone variability + osteosynthesis hardware + biomechanical demands require pre-planning.

## Methodology
Narrative review summarizing free flap options (fibula, iliac crest, scapula) and the planning workflow for implant-guided surgery in this context.

## Results
Guided surgery enables (1) implant positioning in the highest-quality bone regions, (2) avoidance of osteosynthesis hardware, (3) prosthetically-driven planning despite anatomic constraints.

## Limitations
Narrative review; case-illustration based; no quantitative outcome synthesis.
