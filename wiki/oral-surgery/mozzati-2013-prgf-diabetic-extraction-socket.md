## One-line Summary

Split-mouth study (34 T1DM patients) — PRGF significantly improved socket Healing Index and reduced residual volume days 3–14 vs. spontaneous healing; poorly controlled DM (glycemia ≥240 mg/dL) showed worst outcomes; 21-day follow-up provides epithelialization reference data for insulin-dependent diabetics.

## Summary

Mozzati et al. 2013 is a retrospective split-mouth study of 34 T1DM (insulin-dependent) patients requiring bilateral homologous tooth extractions. One socket was treated with PRGF (Anitua protocol), the contralateral received spontaneous healing. Assessments at days 3, 7, 14, and 21 measured Healing Index, residual socket volume, VAS, and complications. PRGF produced significantly smaller residual socket volumes and better Healing Indices from days 3 to 14. Patients with glycemia ≥240 mg/dL formed a distinct subgroup with worse outcomes regardless of PRGF. Key clinical takeaway: in T1DM, PRGF accelerates socket epithelialization and tissue maturation; glycemic control is the primary determinant of healing quality, not just PRGF presence.

## Key Contributions

- First clinical RCT-level evidence for PRGF efficacy specifically in T1DM post-extraction sockets with day-by-day (3/7/14/21) granular follow-up.
- Establishes glycemia ≥240 mg/dL as a clinically relevant threshold: distinct healing impairment beyond this level vs. lower glycemia, regardless of PRGF.
- 21-day observation window provides reference for expected epithelialization timeline in T1DM patients.
- HbA1c and End Organ Disease Score identified as covariates — multi-organ complication burden predicts healing capacity.

## Methodology

Retrospective split-mouth design; 34 T1DM patients (January–December 2012); bilateral homologous extraction in one appointment; PRGF (Anitua protocol) vs. spontaneous healing; 4 follow-up checkups over 21 days. Healing Index (soft-tissue closure score), residual socket volume (indirect epithelialization surrogate), VAS pain, complications, patient questionnaire. Covariates: glycemia, HbA1c, End Organ Disease Score, smoking. Statistics: Wilcoxon, Mann-Whitney, chi-square/Fisher.

## Results

- **Healing Index D3–D14**: PRGF significantly better than control (group significance)
- **Residual socket volume D3–D14**: PRGF significantly smaller (faster closure)
- **Glycemia ≥240 mg/dL subgroup**: Worse Healing Index AND smaller socket volume decrease — distinct from lower glycemia subgroup
- **Patient questionnaire**: 100% preference for PRGF
- **Complications**: No major complications implied
