## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-center cohort (n=111 enrolled, 90 analyzed; children 1–17 y with acute maxillofacial odontogenic infection) testing handheld POCUS (Philips Lumify) read by ultrasound-naive oral-maxillofacial-surgery residents after ~10 h training, to separate abscess from cellulitis.

Sensitivity 72.97% (95% CI 57.02–84.60), specificity 73.58% (60.42–83.56), PPV 65.85%, NPV 79.59%, accuracy 73.33% (66/90); institutional admissions fell from 60.0 to 19.5/year after POCUS adoption (RR 0.33, 95% CI 0.25–0.42), an associational pre/post comparison.

Moderate accuracy only — an adjunct to the clinical exam, not a stand-alone test; asymmetric reference standard (I&D for abscess vs 7-day clinical resolution for cellulitis) risks verification bias, and deep-space infections were excluded.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 전향 코호트(등록 111명, 분석 90명; 1–17세 급성 악안면 치성감염): 초음파 경험이 없는 구강악안면외과 레지던트가 약 10시간 교육 후 휴대용 POCUS(Philips Lumify)로 농양과 봉와직염을 감별.

민감도 72.97%(95% CI 57.02–84.60), 특이도 73.58%(60.42–83.56), 양성예측도 65.85%, 음성예측도 79.59%, 정확도 73.33%(66/90); 도입 후 연간 입원 60.0→19.5건(RR 0.33, 95% CI 0.25–0.42, 연관성만 확인되는 전후 비교).

정확도는 중등도이며 임상진찰의 보조수단이지 단독 판단 도구가 아님; 농양은 I&D, 봉와직염은 7일 임상경과로 확정하는 비대칭 기준표준이라 검증편향 가능, 심부공간 감염은 제외됨.

## Summary

The management fork in an acute odontogenic facial infection is cellulitis (diffuse soft-tissue inflammation, treated with antibiotics and removal of the source) versus abscess (a loculated collection, treated with incision and drainage). In children this is hard to call by exam alone. This study asked whether a pocket-size ultrasound probe, used by residents with no prior ultrasound experience, can make that call at the bedside.

## Key Contributions

- Prospective test of handheld POCUS for the abscess-vs-cellulitis decision specifically in odontogenic infection.
- Full 2×2 data (TP 27, FP 14, TN 39, FN 10) are reported, so the figures are checkable.
- Pairs accuracy with an institutional outcome (admission rate), with the associational limitation stated by the authors.

## Methodology

- Single center (Rambam, Israel); enrolment Mar 2021–Dec 2022; convenience sample of 111 children aged 1–17 y.
- Operators: OMFS residents (years 1–5), ~10 h training (mostly didactic), no supervised patient-scanning quota; not blinded to the clinical exam.
- B-mode, linear L12-4 probe, ≥2 orthogonal planes, dynamic compression. Reading: abscess / cellulitis / ambiguous.
- Reference standard: abscess = purulence at I&D; cellulitis = full resolution on antibiotics alone at 7 days. No CT/MRI.
- Admissions: Poisson regression, 2017–2021 vs Jan 2022–Apr 2025.

## Results

- 90 analyzed (14 ambiguous scans, 1 missing age, 6 indeterminate outcome excluded). Reference standard: 37 abscess (41.1%), 53 cellulitis (58.9%).
- Sensitivity 72.97% (27/37), specificity 73.58% (39/53), PPV 65.85% (27/41), NPV 79.59% (39/49), accuracy 73.33% (95% CI 63.38–81.38).
- False positives (14): small hypoechoic areas without pus or that resolved on antibiotics. False negatives (10): small/deep collections under edema, or poor cooperation; all drained later, none developed airway compromise or deep-space infection.
- Admissions 60.0/y → 19.5/y, RR 0.33 (95% CI 0.25–0.42), p < 0.001; no difference in length of stay by POCUS result among the 60 admitted.
- Authors' reading: NPV ~80% may help rule out abscess in lower-risk cases only together with the clinical exam; a positive scan needs clinical correlation because about one-third of positives had no pus.

## Limitations (reader's appraisal)

- Verification bias is acknowledged by the authors: a small early abscess that improves on antibiotics is counted as cellulitis.
- No blinding, single center, learning-curve effect, jaw not analyzed, deep spaces excluded; the admissions result is a pre/post comparison and cannot show causation.

## Clinical Insights

- 봉와직염과 농양의 구분은 "농이 고여 있는가"의 문제이며, 이 코호트에서 봉와직염 53명은 항생제만으로 해결, 농양 37명은 전원 I&D로 농 확인 (Frid 2025). [확인]
- 보유 근거로부터의 추론: 초음파 음성(NPV ~80%)이라도 5건 중 1건은 농양일 수 있으므로, 항생제 중 재평가와 재스캔 기준이 필요하다. 이 수치는 소아·휴대용·초심자 조건에 한정되며 성인이나 숙련자에게 옮겨 쓰지 않는다.
