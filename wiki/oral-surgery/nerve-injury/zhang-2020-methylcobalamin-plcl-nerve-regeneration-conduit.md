## Three-line Summary

Animal study (rat, 10 mm sciatic nerve gap) evaluating PLCL nerve guidance conduits loaded with methylcobalamin (MeCbl) at 5%, 15%, and 25% concentrations.

15% MeCbl-PLCL conduit yielded the best outcome: largest axon diameter, thickest myelinated fibers, sustained 21-day drug release, and enhanced Schwann cell proliferation/elongation in vitro.

Local sustained delivery of MeCbl via biodegradable PLCL conduit is a viable strategy to enhance peripheral nerve regeneration without systemic supplementation.

## Summary

Zhang et al. (2020) fabricated poly(D,L-lactide-co-caprolactone) (PLCL) films loaded with methylcobalamin (MeCbl) at three concentrations (5%, 15%, 25% wt%) and rolled them into tubular nerve guidance conduits (NGCs). MeCbl is the active coenzyme form of vitamin B12, known to support Schwann cell function and axonal regeneration.

**In vitro findings**: MeCbl-loaded PLCL films enhanced Schwann cell proliferation and elongation, and Neuro2a neuronal cell proliferation, in a concentration-dependent manner. MeCbl was released sustainably from the films for up to 21 days.

**In vivo findings**: NGCs were implanted to bridge a 10 mm sciatic nerve gap in rats. The 15% MeCbl-PLCL conduit showed the most satisfactory nerve regeneration — the largest axon diameter and thickest myelinated nerve fibers — along with recovery of both motor and sensory function.

The study establishes proof-of-concept for local, controlled MeCbl delivery via a biodegradable polymer conduit as an alternative or adjunct to autograft repair for peripheral nerve injuries.

## Key Contributions

1. **Fabrication method**: PLCL films loaded with MeCbl rolled and sutured into tubular NGCs — a simple, scalable approach.
2. **Sustained release**: MeCbl released continuously for 21 days from PLCL, providing a prolonged neurotrophic microenvironment.
3. **Optimal dose identified**: 15% MeCbl loading balances drug delivery and structural integrity; outperforms 5% and 25% in nerve regeneration metrics.
4. **Dual in vitro/in vivo validation**: Both Schwann cell biology and rat nerve gap model confirm efficacy.

## Methodology

- **Material**: PLCL + MeCbl composite films at 5/15/25 wt%; rolled/sutured to form ~10 mm conduits.
- **In vitro**: Schwann cell and Neuro2a cell culture; proliferation (cell viability assay); morphology.
- **In vivo**: Rat sciatic nerve, 10 mm amputation gap; NGC bridging; assessment of motor/sensory function (gait analysis, toe-spread reflex), histology, and histomorphometry (axon diameter, myelin thickness).
- **Duration**: Standard rodent nerve regeneration follow-up (typically 8–16 weeks, not specified in abstract).
