## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (8 studies — 3 RCTs + 5 cohorts, 1,488 teeth) comparing coronectomy versus total mandibular third molar extraction, with GRADE evaluation and trial sequential analysis.

Coronectomy significantly reduces IAN injury (Peto OR 0.23, 95% CI 0.13–0.39, p<0.0001; TSA-conclusive fourfold protection); dry socket and infection rates do not differ between techniques.

Root-retrieval re-operation is needed in only 1.2% (95% CI 0.0–4.4%), reframing root migration as usually benign and making coronectomy the preferred technique when IAN proximity is confirmed.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (8편 — RCT 3편 + 코호트 5편, 1,488치): 하악 제3대구치에서 치관절제술(coronectomy) 대 완전 발치를 GRADE·TSA까지 적용한 방법론적으로 가장 강력한 합성 연구.

치관절제술이 하치조신경(IAN) 손상을 Peto OR 0.23(95% CI 0.13–0.39, p<0.0001, TSA 확정적)으로 약 4배 감소; 건성치조염·감염은 유의한 차이 없음.

잔존 치근 회수를 위한 재수술률은 1.2%에 불과해, 치근 이동은 대개 양성 경과로 임상적 우려가 낮다.

## Summary

This 2026 meta-analysis is the methodologically strongest coronectomy-vs-total-extraction synthesis to date. Using Peto odds ratios (appropriate for the rare-event IAN injury), HKSJ-adjusted random-effects CIs, GRADE, and trial sequential analysis, it shows coronectomy cuts IAN injury roughly fourfold (Peto OR 0.23) and — critically — TSA confirms the evidence is *conclusive*, not just statistically significant. Dry socket and infection do not differ between techniques. Root migration is frequent but pooled re-operation for root retrieval is only 1.2%. The paper reinforces, rather than overturns, the 2016 anchor: same direction of effect, far higher methodological rigor.

## Key Contributions

- Conclusive (TSA-confirmed) effect estimate for IAN-injury reduction.
- Demonstrates equivalence for dry socket and infection.
- Low pooled re-operation rate (1.2%) reframes root migration as usually benign.

## Methodology

- PRISMA; PROSPERO CRD420261305351; PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Cochrane.
- 8 studies (3 RCTs, 5 cohorts), 1488 teeth; RoB 2.0 + ROBINS-I; GRADE.
- Random-effects REML + HKSJ; Peto OR for rare events; Freeman-Tukey arcsine for single-arm proportions; subgroup/meta-regression; TSA.

## Results

- IAN injury: Peto OR **0.23** (95% CI 0.13–0.39, p<0.0001), TSA-conclusive.
- Dry socket: RR 0.68, p=0.22 (NS).
- Infection: RR 0.87, p=0.71 (NS).
- Re-operation (root retrieval): **1.2%** (95% CI 0.0–4.4%).
