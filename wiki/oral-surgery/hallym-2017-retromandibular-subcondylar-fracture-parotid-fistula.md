## Summary

This case report describes the management of a subcondylar fracture via a retromandibular approach, complicated by a post-operative parotid gland fistula. The retromandibular approach provides excellent exposure of the subcondylar region while minimizing risk to the facial nerve (particularly the marginal mandibular branch). However, the approach carries an inherent risk of parotid gland/duct injury. In this case, a parotid fistula developed post-operatively. Conservative management — pressure dressing, antibiotics, and anticholinergic medication — was attempted initially but failed. Surgical ligation of the parotid duct successfully resolved the fistula without recurrence at follow-up. The authors discuss the trade-off between facial nerve safety (favoring retromandibular approach) and parotid injury risk, and propose a stepwise management algorithm for post-operative parotid fistulas.

## Key Contributions

- Clinical demonstration of retromandibular approach for subcondylar fracture ORIF
- Stepwise management protocol for post-operative parotid fistula (conservative → surgical)
- Discussion of facial nerve preservation vs. parotid injury risk balance

## Methodology

- **Design**: Case report (single patient)
- **Diagnosis**: Subcondylar fracture (하악두하 골절)
- **Surgical approach**: Retromandibular approach (후하악 접근법)
- **Fixation**: Open reduction internal fixation (개방 정복 내고정)
- **Complication**: Parotid gland fistula (이하선 누공) post-operatively
- **Fistula management sequence**:
  1. Conservative: pressure dressing, systemic antibiotics, anticholinergics
  2. Surgical (after conservative failure): parotid duct ligation
- **Outcome assessment**: Facial nerve function, fistula resolution, fracture healing
