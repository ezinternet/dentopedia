## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-center study (India; n=20 patients, 50 odontogenic fascial-space infections) comparing grayscale ultrasonography (9 MHz linear probe) with 1.5-T MRI, confirmed by percutaneous aspiration or incision-and-drainage microbiology.

Ultrasonography identified 42/50 involved spaces (84%) versus MRI 50/50 (100%); both matched for all superficial spaces, while ultrasonography missed every deep space (sublingual, masticatory, pharyngeal); both classified 16 cases as cellulitis and 4 as abscess.

Ultrasonography is a cheap, radiation-free tool for staging superficial space infection (cellulitis vs abscess) but cannot replace MRI for deep spaces; small sample and a reference standard that is itself imaging limit certainty.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 전향 연구(인도; 환자 20명, 치성 근막공간 감염 50개): 그레이스케일 초음파(9 MHz 선형 프로브)와 1.5T MRI를 비교하고, 경피 흡인 또는 절개배농 검체의 균배양으로 확인.

초음파는 50개 중 42개(84%), MRI는 50개 전부(100%) 검출; 표재 공간은 두 방법이 일치했으나 초음파는 심부 공간(설하·저작·인두)을 전부 놓침; 봉와직염 16건·농양 4건 분류는 두 방법이 동일.

초음파는 표재 공간 감염의 병기(봉와직염 vs 농양) 판정에 저렴하고 방사선이 없는 도구지만 심부 공간은 MRI를 대체하지 못함; 표본이 작고 기준표준이 영상 자체라 확실성에 한계.

## Summary

Odontogenic infection that leaves the tooth spreads into fascial spaces and can become cellulitis or an abscess. Some authors drain only abscesses and treat cellulitis with antibiotics; others drain both, and clinical diagnosis of the two is often difficult. This study checks whether ultrasonography, which is cheap and radiation-free, can stand in for MRI in locating the infection and staging it.

## Key Contributions

- Same-patient ultrasonography vs MRI comparison for odontogenic fascial-space infection.
- Ultrasonographic four-stage scheme by echogenicity: edematous change (isoechoic), cellulitis (hyperechoic), pre-abscess (mixed), abscess (anechoic).
- Clear superficial-vs-deep boundary for what ultrasonography can see.

## Methodology

- 20 patients, 50 fascial spaces; 9 MHz linear probe, transverse and axial scans; 1.5-T MRI, axial and coronal, compared with the uninfected side.
- Confirmation by microbiology of aspirated or drained material; SPSS.

## Results

- Ultrasonography 42/50 spaces (84%), MRI 50/50 (100%). All superficial spaces (buccal 15, canine 7, submasseteric 14, submandibular 6) were seen by both; deep spaces (sublingual 3, masticatory 4, pharyngeal 1) were seen only by MRI.
- Stage classification identical: cellulitis 16, abscess 4. The four abscesses were drained and pus confirmed them; the 16 cellulitis cases got antibiotics alone.
- Polymicrobial flora, aerobes 65.7% of growth.

## Limitations (reader's appraisal)

- n=20, one center, only four abscesses; MRI is the comparator, so ultrasonography accuracy is relative to another imaging test.
- The paper's own numbers conflict: "all 40 superficial spaces" in the text vs 42 in Table 1, and PPV 90% beside specificity 100%. Use the counts in the tables, not the derived percentages. (Logged as a source-data issue.)
- No blinding is described.

## Clinical Insights

- 근막공간 치성감염의 병기를 초음파로 부종→봉와직염→농양 전 단계→농양으로 나눌 수 있고, 표재 공간(협·견치·교근하·악하)에서는 MRI와 일치했다 (Ghali 2021). [확인]
- 보유 근거로부터의 추론: 설하·저작·인두 같은 심부 공간이 의심되면 초음파 음성은 배제 근거가 못 된다. 이 경우 MRI/CT와 상급병원 이송 판단으로 넘어간다 ([[oral-surgery/gargava-2022-deep-neck-space-infection-150-cases]]).
