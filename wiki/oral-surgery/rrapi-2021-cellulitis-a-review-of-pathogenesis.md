## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Med Clin North Am, 2021, dermatology) of pathogenesis, diagnosis and management of cellulitis.

Cellulitis is a common skin infection with rising hospitalizations and costs; there is no gold standard diagnostic test; exam typically shows poorly demarcated unilateral erythema with warmth and tenderness, and history plus exam can narrow the differential and avoid unnecessary admission; antibiotic choice follows history, risk factors, severity and likely organism.

Abstract only: the pathogenesis section itself is not in the abstract, and the review concerns skin infection generally, not odontogenic facial infection.

## Summary

The review summarizes how cellulitis presents (poorly demarcated, unilateral, red, warm, tender), why it is hard to diagnose (no gold standard, many mimickers), and how antibiotic choice is made.

## Key Contributions
