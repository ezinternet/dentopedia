## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 3 split-mouth RCTs (81 patients, 162 mandibular third molars; non-smoking healthy patients, >=6 months follow-up) comparing bovine xenograft plus collagen membrane with spontaneous healing at the distal aspect of the second molar.
Pooled (2 trials, 73 third molars): probing depth (PPD) gain MD 2.36 mm (95% CI 0.69-4.03; P=0.005; I2=97%) and clinical attachment level (CAL) gain MD 2.52 mm (0.96-4.09; P=0.002; I2=95%) favoured xenograft; alveolar bone level (ABL) and adverse events did not differ.
Only 3 trials (1 low risk of bias) with very high heterogeneity, so the benefit is suggestive rather than robust; larger, longer, better-designed RCTs are needed.

## Summary

This PRISMA-based systematic review and meta-analysis asked whether guided bone regeneration with bovine xenograft prevents periodontal defects at the distal aspect of the mandibular second molar after surgical removal of the third molar. Three split-mouth RCTs (81 patients, 162 third molars) were included from 795 records. Pooling two trials (73 third molars), xenograft plus collagen membrane gave greater probing depth gain (MD 2.36 mm) and clinical attachment gain (MD 2.52 mm) than spontaneous healing, whereas alveolar bone level change and postoperative complications did not differ. With only one trial at low risk of bias and I2 of 95-97%, the authors advise caution and call for larger, longer RCTs.

## Key Contributions
- First review to isolate bovine xenograft versus spontaneous healing for this indication.
- Quantifies a periodontal benefit (PPD and CAL gain of roughly 2.4-2.5 mm) of xenograft plus collagen membrane without a detectable rise in complications.
- In the one trial comparing xenograft with and without membrane, the membrane arm was better for PPD and CAL.

## Methodology
PubMed, Cochrane Library and Scopus searched in April 2020 plus a manual search of nine journals; two independent reviewers (kappa = 1); Cochrane risk-of-bias tool; random-effects meta-analysis in RevMan 5.3 with the third molar as the unit in split-mouth trials; outcomes PPD, CAL, ABL and adverse events at >=6 months (12 months in pooled trials).

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Studies / patients / third molars | 3 RCTs / 81 analysed (98 enrolled, 17 dropouts) / 162 |
| PPD gain, xenograft + membrane vs spontaneous healing | MD 2.36 (95% CI 0.69 to 4.03), P=0.005, I2=97% |
| CAL gain | MD 2.52 (95% CI 0.96 to 4.09), P=0.002, I2=95% |
| ABL change | no significant difference |
| Adverse events | no significant difference (not pooled; 3 infections grafted vs 1 control in one trial) |
| Risk of bias | 1 low, 1 unclear, 1 high |

## Limitations
Three trials, small sample, one low-risk-of-bias trial and very high heterogeneity; no pooled data for xenograft without membrane; one trial used a locally marketed xenograft; all trials performed scaling and root planing, which may confound; the conclusion is hypothesis-strengthening, not definitive.
