## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (n=30) placed simvastatin 10 mg on Gelfoam in one mandibular third-molar socket and saline-soaked Gelfoam in the contralateral control socket. Gray-level histographic bone density was significantly higher in simvastatin sockets at every measurement point (1, 4, 8, 12 weeks; P = .001), corroborated by CBCT at 12 weeks. Local simvastatin appears to accelerate post-extraction osseous regeneration as a low-cost adjunct.

## 세줄요약

하악 제3대구치 양측 발치 환자 30명 split-mouth RCT: 한쪽 발치와에 심바스타틴 (Simvastatin) 10 mg + Gelfoam, 반대쪽에 생리식염수 + Gelfoam 배치. 발치 후 1·4·8·12주 방사선 농도(gray-level) 측정 결과 심바스타틴군에서 모든 시점 유의하게 높음(P = .001); 12주 CBCT도 가속 골재생 확인. 저비용·간편한 국소 심바스타틴이 발치와 골재생 (Socket Healing) 촉진에 효과적임을 시사.

## Study Design

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Split-mouth, single-blind, single-center RCT |
| n | 30 patients (60 sockets: 30 study, 30 control) |
| Age | 18–40 years |
| Intervention | Gelfoam + simvastatin 10 mg (study) vs Gelfoam + normal saline (control) |
| Primary outcome | Mean gray-level histographic values (intraoral periapical radiograph) |
| Time points | 1, 4, 8, 12 weeks |
| CBCT | Obtained for every 5th patient at 12 weeks (n ≈ 6) |
| Follow-up | 3 months |

## Key Results

- **Bone density (gray values):** Significantly higher in simvastatin sockets at 1, 4, 8, and 12 weeks (P = .001)
- **CBCT:** Substantiated accelerated bone regeneration in simvastatin sockets at 12 weeks
- **Mechanism cited:** Simvastatin promotes osteoblastic activity, inhibits osteoclastic activity, supports osteoblast differentiation via bone morphogenetic protein (BMP) pathway
- **Clinical conclusion:** Local simvastatin application is a cost-effective and simple approach to hasten osseous regeneration

## Limitations

- Single-center, single-blinded (patients blinded, not operators)
- CBCT restricted to every 5th patient — not whole-cohort volumetric data
- No follow-up beyond 12 weeks; implant-site suitability not assessed
- Absolute gray-level values not reported in abstract; no power calculation available
- abstract-only — full text not retrieved

## Why Ingested

Directly investigates local simvastatin (Simvastatin) 10 mg application after mandibular third molar (제3대구치) extraction — provides RCT-level evidence that this low-cost statin accelerates socket bone density (발치와 골밀도) recovery, complementing the broader SR evidence base in [[bone-regeneration/tale-2026-simvastatin-guided-bone-regeneration-sr]].
