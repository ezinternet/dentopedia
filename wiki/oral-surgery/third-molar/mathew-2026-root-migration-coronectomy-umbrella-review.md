## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA; 13 systematic reviews encompassing 178 primary studies) of root migration after mandibular third molar coronectomy — prevalence, magnitude, timing, determinants, and clinical significance (abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

Migration prevalence ranged from 6% to nearly 100%; it began within 3-6 months, was fastest in the first postoperative year, and stabilised thereafter, with mean distances of 2-4 mm; younger age, female sex, and specific root/impaction characteristics predicted greater migration, and excessive displacement occasionally caused root exposure, pain, and secondary surgery.

Authors frame "controlled root migration" and propose a conceptual (unvalidated) risk stratification model to personalise follow-up and minimise secondary interventions; the abstract reports no pooled estimate, AMSTAR 2 appraisal, or overlap statistics.

## 세줄요약

하악 제3대구치 치관절제술(coronectomy) 후 치근 이동(root migration)의 유병률·크기·시기·결정인자·임상적 의미를 다룬 우산형 리뷰(PRISMA; 체계적 고찰 13편, 일차연구 178편; 초록만 확보 — 전문 미확보).

이동 유병률은 6%~거의 100%로 편차가 컸고, 술 후 3-6개월 내 시작해 첫 1년에 가장 빠르며 이후 안정화, 평균 이동 거리 2-4 mm; 젊은 연령·여성·특정 치근/매복 특성에서 이동이 크고, 과도한 이동은 드물게 치근 노출·통증·2차 수술과 연관.

저자들은 "조절된 치근 이동(controlled root migration)" 개념과 개념적(미검증) 위험 층화 모델을 제안하나, 초록에는 통합 추정치·AMSTAR 2 평가·중복도 지표가 없어 위험 층화 모델은 임상 적용 전 검증이 필요하다.

## Summary

Abstract-only — full text not retrieved. This umbrella review (13 systematic reviews, 178 primary studies) synthesises what is known about root migration after mandibular third molar coronectomy. Migration is common (6% to nearly 100%), starts at 3-6 months, is fastest in year one, then stabilises, with mean distances of 2-4 mm. Younger age, female sex, and specific root/impaction characteristics predict more migration. Most migrated roots stay asymptomatic, but excessive displacement occasionally causes root exposure, pain, and secondary surgery. The authors propose "controlled root migration" and a conceptual risk stratification model.

## Key Contributions

- Isolates root migration (not just re-intervention) as the unit of analysis at umbrella level.
- Timeline benchmark: onset 3-6 months, peak year one, plateau afterwards.
- Predictor list (age, sex, root/impaction features) and a conceptual, unvalidated risk model.

## Methodology

PRISMA-guided umbrella review of systematic reviews on coronectomy root migration; appraisal tool and overlap method not stated in the abstract. Graded `sr` (not `sr+ma`): no new pooled estimate is reported.

## Results

- 13 SRs, 178 primary studies; prevalence 6% to ~100%.
- Mean migration 2-4 mm; onset 3-6 months; stabilises after ~1 year.
- Excess displacement → occasional root exposure, pain, secondary surgery.
