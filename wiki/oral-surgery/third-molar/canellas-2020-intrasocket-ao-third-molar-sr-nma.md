## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and network meta-analysis evaluated the efficacy of different intrasocket interventions immediately after mandibular third molar surgery to prevent alveolar osteitis (AO), including 37 RCTs (6175 surgeries in 4716 patients). Pairwise meta-analysis showed intrasocket interventions significantly reduced AO incidence (RR = 0.39, p < 0.0001, 95% CI: 0.30 to 0.52). Platelet-rich fibrin (OR = 0.28, 95% CI: 0.10 to 0.71) and chlorhexidine 0.2% gel (OR = 0.52, 95% CI: 0.32 to 0.95) were significantly more effective than placebo.

There is moderate evidence supporting the use of platelet-rich fibrin or chlorhexidine 0.2% gel in the socket for prevention of alveolar osteitis after mandibular third molar surgery.

## Summary

This systematic review and network meta-analysis evaluated the efficacy of intrasocket interventions to prevent alveolar osteitis (AO) after mandibular third molar surgery. Thirty-seven randomized controlled trials involving 6175 surgeries in 4716 patients were included. Pairwise meta-analysis showed that intrasocket interventions significantly reduced AO incidence compared to placebo or no treatment (RR = 0.39, p < 0.0001). Network meta-analysis identified platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) as significantly more effective than placebo (OR = 0.28, 95% CI: 0.10 to 0.71), as was chlorhexidine 0.2% gel (OR = 0.52, 95% CI: 0.32 to 0.95). Other effective interventions included eugenol paste, recombinant bovine growth factor, colloidal silver gelatin sponge, and acellular dermal matrix. The authors concluded that there is moderate evidence to support the use of PRF or chlorhexidine 0.2% gel for AO prevention after mandibular third molar surgery.

## Key Contributions

- Intrasocket interventions reduce alveolar osteitis risk by 61% (RR = 0.39)
- PRF is the most effective intervention for AO prevention (OR = 0.28)
- Chlorhexidine 0.2% gel is effective for AO prevention (OR = 0.52)
- PRF and chlorhexidine gel have large numbers of included studies and patients
- Moderate evidence supports PRF or chlorhexidine 0.2% gel use in clinical practice

## Methodology

This systematic review and network meta-analysis followed PRISMA guidelines. A comprehensive search was conducted in EMBASE, Cochrane Library, MEDLINE/PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, LILACS, and grey literature up to September 2019. Eligible studies were RCTs evaluating intrasocket interventions immediately after mandibular third molar extraction to prevent alveolar osteitis. Thirty-seven RCTs were included. Risk of bias was assessed using the Cochrane RoB tool with excellent agreement (kappa = 0.80). Pairwise meta-analysis and network meta-analysis were performed. Heterogeneity was assessed with I².
