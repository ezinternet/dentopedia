## Three-line Summary

Single case report (emergency department, USA): a 31-year-old man with recurrent right periorbital and malar swelling, where contrast maxillofacial CT showed preseptal cellulitis without a defined abscess but bedside ultrasound found a small abscess close to the globe.

A specialist then performed incision and drainage; he received IV antibiotics for 2 days and was discharged improved without complication (n=1; no accuracy statistics).

Illustrates that physical exam can miss an occult abscess and that ultrasound can add information when CT does not, especially near the eye and nose; one case, authors' interpretation, and no stated dental source.

## Summary

Cellulitis can be managed medically with antibiotics, whereas an abscess needs incision and drainage, so telling them apart spares cellulitis patients an invasive procedure. Near the eye and nose the cost of a missed abscess is higher because of venous communication with the cavernous sinus. The report uses one patient to argue for adding bedside ultrasound to the exam.

## Key Contributions

- States the exam-based definitions: cellulitis = erythema, edema, warmth; abscess suspected on a tender fluctuant area within it.
- Frames the facial "danger zone" as the reason diagnostic accuracy matters more there.
- One case where ultrasound found an abscess that contrast CT did not.

## Methodology

- Narrative case report with a brief background on soft-tissue ultrasound; no study design, no accuracy analysis.

## Results

- Case: afebrile, WBC 9.3, CRP 2.07; right periorbital and malar erythema and edema with a pustule. CT: preseptal cellulitis, no abscess. Bedside ultrasound: small abscess near the globe. Treated with I&D plus 2 days of IV antibiotics; discharged improved.

## Limitations (reader's appraisal)

- n=1, no comparator counts; "accurate" is the authors' claim, not a measured result.
- No dental source is stated, so this is a facial soft-tissue infection case, not an odontogenic one; use it as an illustration of the cellulitis/abscess decision near the orbit, not as dental evidence.

## Clinical Insights

- 안와·비강 인접 안면 감염에서는 농양 유무가 처치(I&D)를 가르고, 이 증례에서 CT는 농양을 못 보여 줬지만 초음파는 보였다 (Lewis 2014, 증례 1건). [확인]
- 보유 근거로부터의 추론: 위험 삼각부에서 CT 음성만으로 농양을 배제하지 않는다는 주의 신호로만 쓰고, 영상 간 우열 근거로 인용하지 않는다.
