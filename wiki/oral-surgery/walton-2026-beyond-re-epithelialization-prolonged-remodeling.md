## One-line Summary
Mouse hard-palate punch-excision model: re-epithelialization completes by 14 days, but Wnt-responsive stem cell restoration and full differentiation marker recovery require 5–6 months of post-wound remodeling.

## 한줄요약

## Summary
Walton et al. (2026) used a mouse hard-palate 2 mm circular full-thickness punch-excision model to track oral mucosal wound healing at nine time points spanning PSD1 through 6 months post-wounding (6MPW). Re-epithelialization was histologically complete by PSD14, but cell proliferation, apoptosis, stem cell composition, keratin expression, and epigenetic marks did not return to homeostatic levels until 5–6 MPW. Critically, Wnt-responsive stem cells (Axin2+), tracked by CreERT2 lineage tracing, were strikingly depleted from the regenerated epithelium between PSD14 and 2MPW and only recovered by 5MPW. Stress keratins K6 and K17 remained elevated until 2MPW, whereas the differentiation markers K10 and filaggrin reappeared only at 6MPW. These findings establish that oral wound healing operates in two distinct phases: rapid re-epithelialization (≤14 days) followed by prolonged cellular remodeling (months) required to reconstitute true epithelial homeostasis.

## Key Contributions
- Distinguishes re-epithelialization (gap closure, ≤14 days) from homeostasis restoration (5–6 months): two biologically separate phases
- First demonstration that Wnt-responsive (Axin2+) stem cells are transiently depleted from regenerated oral epithelium after wound closure, with recovery at 5MPW
- Stress keratin K6/K17 elevation persists to 2MPW, revealing that histological closure underestimates functional healing state
- Characterizes epigenetic remodeling (H3K27ac, H2AK119ub, H3K27me3) across the full post-wound timeline
- Documents sustained immune infiltration (CD45+, CD3+ T cells) to 6MPW, implicating prolonged mucosal immunity in remodeling

## Methodology
- **Model**: C57BL/6 male mice; 2 mm circular full-thickness punch excision of hard palate mucosa
- **Lineage tracing**: Axin2CreERT2 × Rosa26-tdTomato (tamoxifen-induced labeling of Wnt-responsive cells)
- **Timepoints**: PSD1, PSD4, PSD7, PSD14, 1MPW, 2MPW, 3MPW, 5MPW, 6MPW
- **Assays**: H&E histology; immunofluorescence (Ki67, cleaved caspase-3, K6, K17, K10, filaggrin, CD45, CD3, tdTomato); CUT&RUN epigenomics (H3K27ac, H2AK119ub, H3K27me3); qRT-PCR (p53, p21, K6, K17, K10)
- **Sample size**: 3–5 mice per timepoint; two independent experimental replicates

## Results
| Parameter | Baseline | Peak change | Return to baseline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proliferation (Ki67+) | — | PSD4 | ~2MPW |
| Apoptosis (Cl-Casp3+) | — | PSD14→2MPW ↑ | >2MPW |
| Re-epithelialization | — | complete PSD14 | — |
| Axin2+ stem cells | Present | Depleted PSD14–2MPW | 5MPW |
| Stress keratins K6/K17 | Low | Elevated to 2MPW | 5–6MPW |
| Differentiation K10/filaggrin | Present | Lost early | 6MPW |
| Immune infiltrate (CD45+/CD3+) | Low | Rising to 6MPW | Not yet normal |
| H3K27ac (active mark) | — | Decreased PSD14–2MPW | — |

- Apoptosis driver: p53 and p21 mRNA upregulation tracked apoptosis elevation
- Epigenetic pattern: polycomb-mediated suppression (H2AK119ub, H3K27me3) transiently elevated during remodeling phase
