Narrative review (Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Clinics of North America, 2017) of treatment considerations and controversies in odontogenic infection and its extension into the head and neck.

The abstract says the general principles have not changed but timing of treatment sequences and techniques has been modified by advances in diagnostic methods, bacterial genetics and antibiotic use; keywords include cellulitis, abscess, biofilm, imaging and deep neck space infection.

## Summary

Odontogenic infection and its spread into the head and neck remain central to oral and maxillofacial surgery and to hospital referral. The review revisits where management has shifted, driven by better diagnostics, bacterial genetics and antibiotic practice.
