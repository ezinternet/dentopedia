## One-line Summary

Mouse gingival wound healing study showing IRX1 is a primed oral epithelial stem cell marker in the basal and junctional epithelium that activates SOX9 and EPGN/EGF signaling to drive KC proliferation and migration; Irx1 haploinsufficiency delays re-epithelialization by 3–7 days.

## Summary

The gingiva heals faster and with less scarring than skin. This study identifies IRX1 (Iroquois Homeobox 1) as a transcription factor expressed in the basal and immediate suprabasal cells of the gingival epithelium (GE) and junctional epithelium (JE), as well as in mesenchymal stroma niches. Using Irx1 heterozygous (Het) knock-in mice with a gingival biopsy-punch wound model, the authors found that haploinsufficiency of Irx1 significantly delays wound closure: while WT mice show near-complete re-epithelialization at 1 dpi, Het mice have largely open wounds at 3 dpi and incomplete closure at 7 dpi. RNA-Seq of regenerated epithelium at 3 dpi revealed a diminished wound-activated KC signature in Het mice, with downregulation of KC migration, KC differentiation, and EGF signaling pathways. Mechanistically, IRX1 directly activates the SOX9 promoter (ChIP-confirmed) in the transient amplifying suprabasal layer. EPGN, a KC-produced EGF ligand at the regenerative front, is also IRX1-dependent and its exogenous addition rescues KC migration when IRX1 is inhibited. IRX1 is not expressed in skin epidermis but is present in hair follicle bulge, paralleling the oral-specific basal expression of PITX1.

## Key Contributions

- First demonstration of **IRX1 expression in gingival epithelial basal stem cell layer** (JE + GE basal + immediate suprabasal) and in STRO-1+ mesenchymal niches in human and murine gingiva
- IRX1 is **oral-epithelium-specific** (absent from skin epidermis; present in HF bulge) — confirms a pattern shared with PITX1
- Irx1 Het mice: **wound area 0.31 mm² vs 0.08 mm² at 1 dpi** (WT); full closure delayed to day 7
- IRX1→**SOX9** activation confirmed by luciferase reporter + ChIP in OCCM30 cells; SOX9 marks TA cells in suprabasal layer
- **EPGN** (EGF ligand) expression at the regenerative front is IRX1-dependent; EPGN addition rescues IRX1-knockdown cells
- Irx1 Het RE shows psoriatic KC phenotype: absent Loricrin, prolonged KRT6A/SOX2 in all RE layers = defective terminal differentiation
- Lineage tracing (Krt14CreERT2 × Rosa26mTmG) shows defective stratification in Het mice: GFP-labeled basal cells fail to migrate into wound area at 3 dpi

## Methodology

**Design**: Irx1 Het (haploinsufficient) vs WT adult mice (10–16 weeks); gingival wound ø0.75 mm biopsy punch in front of right first molar; sacrifice at 1/3/7 dpi; H&E, IF (IRX1, KRT5, KRT14, SOX2, KRT6A, Loricrin, EPGN, SOX9), lineage tracing, total RNA-Seq at 3 dpi. In vitro: LS-8 oral epithelial cells with IRX1 OE/inhibition, scratch + proliferation assays, EPGN rescue. Human: 2 gingiva samples, IF only.
