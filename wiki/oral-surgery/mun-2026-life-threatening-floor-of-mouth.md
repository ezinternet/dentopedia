## Three-line Summary

A 59-year-old male with a severely resorbed edentulous mandible developed sudden intraoral bleeding and sublingual swelling causing tongue elevation and impending airway obstruction after computer-guided implant placement. Emergency neck CT and postdischarge CBCT showed lingual cortical perforation at the #31 implant site with suspected vascular injury; only the #31 implant was displaced lingually relative to the digital plan, suggesting deviation during freehand insertion after fully guided osteotomy. Emergency nasotracheal intubation with conservative management led to progressive hematoma resolution.

## 세줄요약

## Summary

Single case report of a life-threatening floor-of-mouth (FOM) hematoma after computer-guided implant placement in a severely resorbed edentulous mandible. The event occurred despite guided surgery; imaging identified lingual cortical perforation at the #31 site with suspected vascular injury, and plan comparison suggested deviation of only that implant during freehand insertion after guide removal. Airway was secured by emergency nasotracheal intubation; conservative management led to resolution.

## Key Contributions

- Life-threatening FOM hematoma can occur even with guided implant surgery in severely resorbed mandibles.
- Mucosa-supported guides without auxiliary fixation plus freehand implant insertion after guide removal may contribute to positional deviation in completely edentulous cases.
- Thorough CBCT assessment and preparedness for airway intervention are essential to prevent catastrophic outcomes.

## Methodology

Single case report. 59-year-old male, severely resorbed edentulous mandible, mucosa-supported static surgical guide, six planned implants. Diagnosis via emergency neck CT and postdischarge CBCT; positional deviation assessed by direct comparison with the original digital surgical plan from the referring clinic.

## Results

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| Implant site with complication | #31 (lingual cortical perforation) |
| Other implants | 5 of 6 consistent with plan |
| Suspected cause of deviation | Freehand insertion after fully guided osteotomy / guide removal |
| Clinical presentation | Sudden intraoral bleeding, sublingual swelling, tongue elevation, impending airway obstruction |
| Management | Emergency nasotracheal intubation + conservative management |
| Outcome | Progressive hematoma resolution |
