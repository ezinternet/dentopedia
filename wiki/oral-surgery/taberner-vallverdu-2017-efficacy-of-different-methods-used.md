## 세줄요약

2005–2015년 문헌 24편을 포함해 치조골염(dry socket, alveolar osteitis, 치조골염) 예방 방법과 위험인자를 분석한 체계적 고찰(SR, Systematic Review)이다.

국소 클로르헥시딘(chlorhexidine, 클로르헥시딘) 또는 PRP(Platelet-Rich Plasma, 혈소판 풍부 혈장)는 치조골염 위험 감소와 연관되었고, 하악 제3대구치 수술 후 예방적 항생제는 합병증 예방 효과가 없다고 보고되었다.

연령·감염 병력·발치 난이도는 공통 위험인자로 제시된 반면 흡연(smoking)은 위험인자 합의가 없었던 바, 흡연과 발치 후 합병증의 관계는 연구 설계·정의·집단에 따라 달리 관찰될 수 있음을 함께 고려해야 한다.

## Summary

This systematic review synthesized evidence on prevention strategies and risk factors for alveolar osteitis (dry socket). Across 24 included studies, local chlorhexidine and PRP were reported to reduce dry socket risk, whereas antibiotics did not prevent postoperative complications after lower third molar surgery. Patient age, prior infection, and extraction difficulty were consistent risk factors; smoking did not show consensus as a risk factor in this evidence set.

## Key Contributions

- Combined appraisal of preventive interventions and risk factors for dry socket.
- Highlights chlorhexidine/PRP as preventive options and questions routine antibiotic prophylaxis for this indication.
- Provides nuanced risk-factor synthesis showing non-consensus for smoking in the included 2005–2015 literature.

## Methodology

- Search: Cochrane and PubMed-MEDLINE (2005–2015, English), predefined terms for dry socket/prevention/risk factors.
- Inclusion: clinical studies with ≥30 patients; exclusions: case reports/nonhuman studies.
- Evidence grading: SIGN criteria.
- Included evidence: 24 articles (9 prospective, 2 retrospective, 13 clinical trials).

## Results

- Preventive associations: local chlorhexidine or PRP reduced dry socket likelihood.
- Antibiotics: no prevention of postoperative complications after lower third molar surgery.
- Consistent risk factors: age, previous infection, extraction difficulty.
- No consensus: smoking, gender, menstrual cycles.
