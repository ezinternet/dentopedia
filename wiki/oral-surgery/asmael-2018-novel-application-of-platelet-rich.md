## Three-line Summary

RCT in 20 smoker male patients (40 trans-alveolar extraction sockets, intra-patient comparison) evaluating PRF versus natural healing in extraction sockets.

PRF improved soft tissue healing quality/reduced inflammation (P = 0.0035), but pain (VAS 0.65 vs 1.8, P = 0.1511), epithelization (P = 0.7134), and dry socket prevention/socket closure showed no significant differences.

In smokers, PRF may enhance soft-tissue healing after extraction, while evidence for pain reduction or alveolar osteitis prevention remains inconclusive in this small trial.

## 세줄요약

흡연 남성 20명(발치와 40개)을 대상으로 한 임상시험(RCT, Randomized Controlled Trial)에서 혈소판 풍부 피브린(PRF, Platelet-Rich Fibrin) 적용군과 자연 치유군을 비교했다.

PRF군은 연조직 치유 질 향상/염증 감소(P = 0.0035)를 보였으나, 통증(VAS, Visual Analog Scale, 시각통증척도 0.65 vs 1.8, P = 0.1511), 상피화(P = 0.7134), 치조골염(dry socket, alveolar osteitis) 예방·발치와 폐쇄에서는 유의차가 없었다.

흡연자 발치와에서 PRF의 연조직 치유 보조 가능성을 시사하나, 표본 크기와 일부 비유의 결과로 인해 임상적 일반화에는 추가 연구가 필요하다.

## Summary

This randomized clinical study evaluated platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) as an adjunct for extraction-socket healing in smokers. Using an intra-patient comparison (40 sockets in 20 smoker males), PRF significantly improved soft tissue healing quality and reduced inflammation versus natural healing, but did not significantly improve pain scores, epithelization, or prevent dry socket/socket non-closure outcomes as reported in the abstract.

## Key Contributions

- Direct interventional evidence in smokers undergoing tooth extraction (a population at higher complication risk).
- Demonstrates possible benefit of PRF for soft-tissue healing/inflammation control in smoker extraction sockets.
- Clarifies limits of observed benefit (no significant effect on pain, epithelization, dry socket prevention, socket closure).

## Methodology

- Participants: 20 smoker male patients (18–72 years), multiple extractions.
- Sample: 40 teeth extracted via trans-alveolar extraction.
- Intervention/control: PRF-treated sockets vs naturally healing sockets within patients.
- Outcomes (abstract): soft tissue healing/inflammation, VAS pain, epithelization (dental caliper), dry socket prevention/socket closure.

## Results

- Soft tissue healing/inflammation: significantly better with PRF (2-tailed P = 0.0035).
- Pain (VAS): 0.65 (PRF) vs 1.8 (control), P = 0.1511 (NS).
- Epithelization: P = 0.7134 (NS).
- Dry socket prevention and socket closure: no significant difference reported.
