## Three-line Summary

Longitudinal cohort (302 periodontitis + 113 healthy adults, monitored bi-monthly ×1 year without treatment, then 6 months post-NSPT); progressors (P2: ≥3 sites progressed) had distinctly elevated salivary IFNγ, IL-6, VEGF, IL-1β, MMP-8, IL-10, and OPG vs. stable and healthy; serum analytes were NOT associated with progression.

NSPT significantly reduced salivary IFNγ, IL-6, IL-8, IL-1β, MMP-8, IL-10, OPG, MMP-9 and serum CRP, MMP-8, MMP-9, MPO — demonstrating both local and systemic anti-inflammatory effects of periodontal treatment.

The dissociation between salivary (progression-sensitive) and serum (progression-insensitive) biomarkers positions saliva as the primary monitoring medium for periodontitis activity, with IFNγ, IL-1β, and MMP-8 as key progression signals.

## Background

Identifying patients at risk of periodontitis progression before irreversible attachment loss occurs is a central challenge in periodontics. Salivary biomarkers (cytokines, matrix metalloproteinases) reflect local inflammatory activity and are collectible non-invasively, but no longitudinal study had previously characterized which specific analytes distinguish progressors from stable patients while tracking on a monthly timescale. This NIH-funded study at Penn/Harvard/Forsyth is the largest longitudinal periodontal biomarker cohort to date.

## Methods

- **Design**: Longitudinal observational cohort; bi-monthly assessments ×12 months (NO treatment); then NSPT at 12 months + reassessment at 18 months
- **Sample**: 415 adults (113 healthy H; 302 periodontitis P)
- **Progression classification at 12 months**:
  - P0: no sites progressed
  - P1: 1–2 sites with progression
  - P2: ≥3 sites with progression (defined as ≥2mm CAL loss)
- **Biomarker panel**: 10 salivary analytes + 5 serum analytes
- **Platform**: Luminex multiplex immunoassay
- **Statistics**: Linear mixed models (log-transformed analyte levels); adjusted for covariates

## Results

### Progression Signature (P2 vs P0/P1/H)

**Salivary biomarkers elevated in P2** (all p<0.05):
- IFNγ (interferon-gamma)
- IL-6 (interleukin-6)
- VEGF (vascular endothelial growth factor)
- IL-1β (interleukin-1 beta)
- MMP-8 (matrix metalloproteinase-8, collagenase-2)
- IL-10 (interleukin-10)
- OPG (osteoprotegerin)

**Serum biomarkers**: NOT significantly associated with progression — key finding demonstrating local vs. systemic disconnect.

### Post-NSPT Biomarker Changes

| Biomarker type | Significantly reduced after NSPT |
|---------------|----------------------------------|
| Salivary (8) | IFNγ, IL-6, IL-8, IL-1β, MMP-8, IL-10, OPG, MMP-9 |
| Serum (4) | CRP, MMP-8, MMP-9, MPO |

### Interpretation

- IL-10 and OPG elevation in progressors may represent failed counter-regulatory attempts (anti-inflammatory responses unable to halt destruction)
- VEGF elevation suggests enhanced angiogenesis and tissue permeability in progressing periodontal lesions
- NSPT's systemic anti-inflammatory effect (serum CRP/MMP reduction) supports the "focal infection" model: treating local periodontal disease reduces systemic inflammatory burden

## Clinical Takeaway

For monitoring periodontitis activity and identifying high-risk patients:
1. **Saliva, not serum**: Serum biomarkers do not track local periodontal progression — clinical monitoring and salivary assays are necessary
2. **Priority biomarkers**: IFNγ, IL-1β, MMP-8 in saliva are the most informative progression signals
3. **NSPT benefit is measurable both locally and systemically**: Motivates treatment in patients with systemic inflammatory comorbidities (CVD, DM)
4. **Future implementation**: Luminex is not yet point-of-care; panel assays requiring laboratory processing limit routine clinical deployment
