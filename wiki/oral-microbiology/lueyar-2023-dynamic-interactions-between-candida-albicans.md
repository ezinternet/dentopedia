## Three-line Summary

In-vitro eight-species supragingival biofilm study (Zürich model, 3 experiments × triplicate, CFU on selective agar + FISH/CLSM) examining how individual streptococcal species affect *C. albicans*.

Progressive streptococcal richness lowered *C. albicans* CFU (*S. gordonii* + *S. mutans* yielded the lowest counts, p<0.01); *S. mutans* + a single mitis-group species induced hyphal form, while multiple mitis species co-existing reverted *C. albicans* to yeast.

Community complexity governs *C. albicans* morphology switching — clinical relevance is that disrupted oral-streptococcal ecology may tip *C. albicans* toward the invasive hyphal form.

연쇄상구균 종 다양성 증가 → *C. albicans* CFU 감소 (*S. gordonii* + *S. mutans* 최저, p<0.01); *S. mutans* + mitis군 단일종 → 균사형 (hypha) 유도, mitis 다종 공존 → 효모형 (yeast) 회귀.

## Summary

Lueyar et al. used the reproducible Zürich in vitro "supragingival" biofilm model on saliva-conditioned hydroxyapatite discs to dissect how individual oral streptococci — rather than "streptococci" as a bulk category — affect the pathogenic yeast *Candida albicans*. Nine biofilm compositions were built from a four-species non-streptococcal basic community, adding *S. mutans* and individual mitis-group species (*S. oralis*, *S. oralis* subsp. *tigurinus*, *S. gordonii*) up to a full eight-species biofilm. Species were quantified on selective agar (BIGGY for Candida) and visualized by FISH/CLSM. The more streptococcal species present, the lower the *C. albicans* CFU, with the *S. gordonii* + *S. mutans* combination producing the lowest fungal counts and the eight-species biofilm the strongest reduction (p < 0.0001). Strikingly, *S. mutans* plus a single mitis species induced the invasive filamentous (hyphal) form of *C. albicans*, whereas co-existence with several mitis species pushed it back to the yeast form — implicating inter-streptococcal competition in fungal morphology control. This is, to the authors' knowledge, the first eight-species model to include *S. oralis* subsp. *tigurinus*.

## Key Contributions

- **Species-resolved cross-kingdom data**: identifies *S. gordonii* + *S. mutans* as the combination that most suppresses *C. albicans* CFU; *S. oralis* / *S. oralis* subsp. *tigurinus* + *S. mutans* produced no significant CFU change.
- **Community-complexity → morphology switch**: single mitis species (with *S. mutans*) drives hyphae; multiple mitis species drive yeast — a novel observation attributed to competition.
- **First eight-species model including *S. oralis* subsp. *tigurinus*.**
- Reiterates the **CFU/biomass decoupling** caveat: once *C. albicans* filaments, CFU underestimates true candidal biomass.

## Methodology

- **Model**: Zürich supragingival biofilm on saliva-conditioned HA discs; anaerobic 37 °C; 16 h in mFUM + 0.3% glucose, then 48 h in mFUM + 0.15% glucose + 0.15% sucrose (~64 h total), dip-washed 3×/day.
- **Compositions**: 4-species basic (no streptococci) → stepwise + *S. mutans* + individual mitis species → 8-species.
- **Quantification**: CBA agar (total/MSG/*S. mutans* CFU) and BIGGY agar (*C. albicans* CFU); MSG identified by α-hemolysis.
- **Imaging**: FISH probes MUT590-Cy5 (*S. mutans*), MIT447-FAM (mitis group), Caal1249-Cy3 (*C. albicans*), SYTO40 total DNA; Leica SP5 CLSM ×63, Imaris 9.6 3-D reconstruction.
- **Stats**: 3 experiments × triplicate; two-way ANOVA + Tukey, α = 0.05.

## Results

| Biofilm | Streptococcal addition (to basic) | Effect on C. albicans |
|---|---|---|
| Basic (4 sp.) | none | Yeast form; reference CFU |
| + S. mutans + S. oralis | mitis: S. oralis | No significant CFU change |
| + S. mutans + S. oralis subsp. tigurinus | mitis: S. oralis subsp. tigurinus | No significant CFU change |
| + S. mutans + S. gordonii | mitis: S. gordonii | **Significant CFU reduction (p < 0.01); lowest of all mitis combos** |
| Eight-species | all mitis + S. mutans | **Strongest CFU reduction (p < 0.0001); yeast form** |

- Progressive CFU decline with increasing streptococcal richness.
- *C. albicans* presence did not significantly change total or MSG CFU counts.
- Morphology: yeast (no streptococci) → hyphae (*S. mutans* + one mitis species) → yeast (*S. mutans* + several mitis species).
- Spatial: MSG near the pellicle/base; *C. albicans* in the middle/upper biofilm.
- Candidal biomass (Imaris) showed no significant differences (large variance) — the authors caution CFU is unreliable once filamentation occurs.
