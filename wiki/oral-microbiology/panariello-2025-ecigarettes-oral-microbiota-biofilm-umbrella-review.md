## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD420251025639, AMSTAR 2-appraised) synthesizing 10 systematic reviews/meta-analyses (4–98 primary studies each) comparing oral biofilm/plaque accumulation and oral microbiota composition among e-cigarette (ENDS) users, traditional cigarette smokers, and non-smokers.

E-cigarette users consistently showed a higher plaque index than non-smokers (generally less severe than traditional smokers) plus microbial dysbiosis — increased cariogenic (S. mutans, Lactobacilli), periodontal-pathogenic (P. gingivalis, F. nucleatum), and opportunistic (Candida spp.) organisms — linking vaping to elevated risk of caries, periodontitis, peri-implantitis, and oral candidiasis.

Nine of ten included reviews were AMSTAR 2 "moderate" (mainly incomplete exclusion-list reporting), no formal cross-review meta-analysis was performed, and most underlying primary evidence is cross-sectional — so treat this as a consistent-direction dysbiosis/biofilm signal, not a quantified dose-response verdict, and counsel vaping patients with the same monitoring rigor as smokers.

## 세줄요약

우산형 체계적 고찰(umbrella review, PRISMA·PROSPERO 등록·AMSTAR 2 품질평가) — 전자담배(e-cigarette, ENDS) 사용자·궐련 흡연자·비흡연자의 구강 치태(biofilm)/치태지수(plaque index)와 구강 미생물총(oral microbiota) 구성을 비교한 10편의 SR/MA(개별 연구 4~98편)를 종합.

전자담배 사용자는 비흡연자 대비 일관되게 높은 치태지수를 보였고(궐련 흡연자보다는 대체로 덜 심함), 우식원성균(S. mutans, Lactobacilli)·치주병원균(P. gingivalis, F. nucleatum)·기회감염균(Candida spp.) 증가로 우식·치주염·임플란트주위염(peri-implantitis)·구강칸디다증 위험 상승과 연관됨.

10편 중 9편이 AMSTAR 2 "중등도" 품질(주로 제외연구 목록 미보고)이었고 리뷰 간 정량적 재메타분석은 수행되지 않았으며 기저 근거 대부분이 단면연구이므로, 정량적 용량-반응 결론이 아니라 "방향성이 일관된 dysbiosis/biofilm 신호"로 해석하고 vaping 환자에게는 흡연자와 동일한 수준의 구강 모니터링을 권고할 것.

## Summary

This umbrella review consolidates 10 independently published SR/MAs (June 2025 cutoff, PROSPERO-registered, AMSTAR 2-graded) into a single evidence map of how e-cigarette (ENDS) use affects oral biofilm accumulation and microbiota composition relative to traditional smoking and non-smoking. Across the included reviews, e-cigarette use tracked with a consistently elevated plaque index and a coherent set of dysbiotic microbial shifts — more cariogenic, periodontal-pathogenic, and opportunistic organisms — translating into elevated clinical risk for caries, periodontitis, peri-implantitis, and oral candidiasis, though generally at a lesser magnitude than combustible cigarette smoking. Methodological quality of the underlying reviews was moderate-to-high, with the field's dominant weakness being incomplete exclusion-list reporting rather than critical flaws.

## Key Contributions

- First umbrella-level synthesis pairing oral biofilm/plaque outcomes AND oral microbiota composition outcomes for e-cigarette exposure in one review.
- Quantifies the evidence base: 10 SR/MAs (398 initial records → 10 final), AMSTAR 2 quality 9 "moderate" / 1 "high", none low/critically-low.
- Identifies specific taxa implicated in vaping-associated dysbiosis (S. mutans, Lactobacilli, P. gingivalis, F. nucleatum, Candida spp.) and candidate mechanisms (nicotine-promoted bacterial adhesion/EPS synthesis, reduced salivary lysozyme/lactoferrin, elevated IL-10/IL-12/TNF-α, microbial shifts within 24h of exposure).
- Confirms a dose/severity gradient: e-cigarette effects on plaque/periodontal/peri-implant parameters are directionally similar to but generally milder than traditional cigarette smoking.

## Methodology

PRISMA-guided umbrella review; PECO question (Population: e-cig users/cigarette smokers/non-smokers; Exposure: e-cigarettes/ENDS; Comparison: non-smokers vs. traditional smokers; Outcome: biofilm/plaque index and oral microbiota composition). Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library, Scopus (search to 12 March 2025, studies 2015–2025, no language restriction) plus hand-searched reference lists. Dual independent screening/extraction with third-reviewer arbitration. Quality appraisal via AMSTAR 2. Synthesis was narrative/qualitative — no formal re-meta-analysis across the 10 included reviews (design/outcome heterogeneity precluded pooling).

## Results

- **Screening**: 398 records → 387 after dedup → 10 SR/MAs included (18 excluded as non-SR/MA, 357 off-topic, 2 commentaries).
- **Plaque/biofilm**: consistently higher plaque index in e-cig users vs. non-smokers across most reviews (Shabil 2024, Vámos 2024, Youssef 2023, Figueredo 2021, Yang 2020); generally lower than traditional smokers (Thiem 2023, Yang 2020, Vámos 2024).
- **Microbiota**: increased cariogenic bacteria (S. mutans, Lactobacilli — Zięba 2024); enrichment of P. gingivalis and F. nucleatum linked to periodontal disease risk; higher Candida spp. prevalence (Ralho 2019); overall oral microbiome alterations documented across multiple reviews (Tattar 2025, Camoni 2023).
- **Clinical downstream risk**: worse peri-implant clinical/radiographic/inflammatory parameters (Vámos 2024, Youssef 2023, Yang 2020); increased self-reported gingivitis (Camoni 2023); enamel-detrimental effect of flavored e-liquids (Camoni 2023); worsening caries over 6 months in daily users (Yang 2020, the sole "high"-AMSTAR-2 review).
- **Quality**: 9/10 reviews "moderate" AMSTAR 2 (mainly incomplete excluded-study lists, limited grey-literature search); 1/10 (Yang 2020, 98 studies) "high."
