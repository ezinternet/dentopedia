## Three-line Summary

Secondary analysis of an RCT (86 Stage II/III periodontitis subjects: SRP alone vs. SRP + intensive antiplaque [0.12% CHX ×3 months + interdental cleaners ×6 months]; 30 healthy references) evaluating subgingival microbiome trajectories toward a modeled healthy plane (HPL) by 16S rRNA sequencing; only the SRP+antiplaque group showed statistically significant reduction in distance to the HPL at 6 months.

Individual responses were highly variable in both groups; random forest analysis identified **baseline microbiome composition as a stronger predictor of microbiome treatment response than treatment type** — the pre-treatment dysbiosis pattern outperforms treatment modality in predicting eubiosis recovery.

This finding supports a microbiome-guided personalized periodontal therapy paradigm: the question "who is likely to respond?" may require microbiome profiling before treatment, not just clinical staging.

## Background

Non-surgical periodontal therapy (NSPT/SRP) reduces pathogenic biofilm load, but clinical outcomes (BOP, PD reduction) do not fully predict microbiome normalization. Whether adjunctive antiplaque measures (chlorhexidine rinse, interdental cleaning) additionally shift the subgingival microbiome toward a healthy composition — and whether that shift is treatment-dependent or patient-dependent — was unknown. This study introduces a healthy plane (HPL) model: the subgingival microbiome structure of 30 healthy reference subjects is modeled mathematically, providing a community-level benchmark to quantify dysbiosis and track eubiosis recovery.

## Methods

- **Design**: Secondary analysis of RCT (not primary microbiome trial)
- **Groups**: SRP alone vs. SRP + antiplaque (0.12% CHX rinse ×3 months + interdental cleaners ×6 months)
- **Sample**: 86 Stage II/III periodontitis subjects + 30 healthy references
- **Sequencing**: 16S rRNA gene; genus-level resolution
- **Outcome**: Distance to healthy plane (HPL) at 6 months
- **Prediction analysis**: Random forest (predictors of microbiome treatment response)

## Results

- **SRP+antiplaque**: Significant reduction in HPL distance at 6 months (p<0.05)
- **SRP alone**: NOT a statistically significant reduction in HPL distance
- **Both groups**: High individual variability — only a fraction achieved health-direction change
- **Random forest**: Baseline microbiome composition = stronger predictor of response than treatment type

## Clinical Implications

1. **Antiplaque adjuncts matter microbially**: Adding CHX and interdental cleaning to SRP produces a measurable microbiome shift toward health that SRP alone does not reliably achieve.
2. **Individual response variability**: Average group effects mask large individual differences — some SRP-alone patients improve dramatically; some SRP+antiplaque patients do not.
3. **Microbiome-guided stratification**: If baseline microbiome predicts response, screening patients' subgingival microbiome composition before treatment could identify who needs intensified protocols.
4. **Limitation — abstract only**: These interpretations derive from the published abstract; full methods and species-level data are not available.
