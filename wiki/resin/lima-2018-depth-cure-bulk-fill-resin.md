## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 33 studies found that bulk-fill resin composites (BFRCs) achieve acceptable depth of cure when LCU irradiance is ≥1000 mW/cm² and exposure time is ≥20 seconds. Acceptable thresholds are bottom/top microhardness ratio ≥0.8 and degree of conversion ≥50%; results are material-dependent when these parameters are not met. Polywave LCUs are useful but not essential for alternative-photoinitiator BFRCs.

## 세줄요약

## Background

Bulk-fill resin composites allow placement in increments up to 4–5 mm, reducing clinical time compared to conventional 2 mm incremental technique. However, adequate depth of cure is critical to ensure mechanical properties and marginal integrity throughout the restoration depth. This SR aimed to synthesize evidence on depth of cure of BFRCs and identify the LCU parameters required for successful polymerization.

## Methods

- **Design:** Systematic review
- **Databases:** PubMed/Medline, Embase, Scopus, ISI Web of Science
- **Search period:** October 2016 – May 2017
- **Inclusion:** Studies assessing depth of cure by microhardness test and/or degree of conversion (DC)
- **Studies found:** 742; **included:** 33
- **Outcome measures:** Bottom/top microhardness ratio; degree of conversion (%)

## Key Findings

### Microhardness (bottom/top ratio threshold: ≥0.8)

| Outcome | Number of studies |
|---|---|
| Acceptable ratio (≥0.8) for ALL materials | 10 |
| Material-dependent results | 9 |
| Non-satisfactory (<0.8) | 2 |

### Degree of Conversion (threshold: ≥50%)

| Outcome | Number of studies |
|---|---|
| Acceptable DC (≥50%) for ALL BFRCs | 11 |
| Material-dependent results | 8 |

### LCU Parameters Across Studies

- **Irradiance range:** 650–1330 mW/cm²
- **Exposure time range:** 5–60 seconds

## Critical Thresholds (Clinical Imperative)

> **Favorable depth of cure requires:**
> - LCU irradiance **≥1000 mW/cm²**
> - Exposure time **≥20 seconds**
> Both conditions must be met simultaneously.

LED devices displaying ≥1000 mW/cm² and ≥20 seconds are **imperative** for successful polymerization of MOST BFRCs.

## Polywave LCUs

Polywave LCUs (broad-spectrum, covering camphorquinone and alternative photoinitiators such as Ivocerin/BAPO) are **useful but NOT essential** for polymerizing alternative photoinitiator-containing BFRCs. Standard monowave devices with adequate irradiance and time remain effective for most materials.

## Clinical Implications

1. **LCU verification is mandatory** before bulk-fill placement — always confirm the device outputs ≥1000 mW/cm² with a radiometer.
2. **Minimum 20-second exposure** per increment; do not reduce exposure time even with high-intensity units unless validated for that specific BFRC.
3. **Material-dependent variability** exists — some BFRCs underperform even with adequate LCU parameters; follow manufacturer guidelines.
4. **Polywave LCU** is not required but may improve outcomes for non-CQ photoinitiator BFRCs.
