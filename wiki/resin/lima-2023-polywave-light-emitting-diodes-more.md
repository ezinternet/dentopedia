## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 18 studies comparing polywave vs monowave LED photoactivation of resin composites, adhesives, and cements containing alternative photoinitiators (mainly TPO). Polywave LED improved degree of conversion (DC) in 9/18 studies and microhardness in 7/18 studies; flexural strength showed no differences between lamp types. Evidence quality is LOW due to high heterogeneity and risk of bias in 11/18 studies.

## 세줄요약

18편 SR: 대체 광개시제(주로 TPO) 함유 레진의 폴리웨이브 vs 모노웨이브 LED 광중합 비교. 폴리웨이브 LED가 중합전환도(Degree of Conversion, DC)를 9/18 연구에서, 미세경도(Microhardness)를 7/18 연구에서 개선; 굴곡강도(Flexural Strength)는 차이 없음. 근거 수준 낮음(11편 고위험 비뚤림).

## Background

Camphorquinone (CQ) is the most widely used photoinitiator in dental resin-based materials, with an absorption peak around 468 nm, well-matched to conventional monowave (blue) LED curing lights. Alternative photoinitiators—particularly TPO (diphenyl-2,4,6-trimethylbenzoyl phosphine oxide, absorption peak ~380–400 nm)—have been incorporated into newer materials to reduce yellowing and improve depth of cure. However, monowave blue LEDs do not effectively activate TPO, raising the question of whether polywave LED curing lights (emitting both violet ~405 nm and blue ~460 nm) provide meaningful clinical benefit.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Systematic review (SR)
- **Population**: In vitro studies of resin composites, adhesives, and dental cements
- **Intervention**: Polywave LED photoactivation
- **Comparator**: Monowave LED photoactivation
- **Outcomes**: Degree of conversion (DC), microhardness, flexural strength
- **Studies included**: 18
- **Alternative photoinitiator focus**: TPO (diphenyl-2,4,6-trimethylbenzoyl phosphine oxide)

## Key Findings

### Degree of Conversion (DC, 중합전환도)

- Polywave LED superior in **9/18 studies** (50%)
- No significant difference in remaining 9/18 studies
- Overall trend: 11 studies showed some DC improvement with polywave

### Microhardness (미세경도)

- Polywave LED superior in **7/18 studies** (39%)
- No significant difference in 11/18 studies

### Flexural Strength (굴곡강도)

- **No differences** between polywave and monowave across all 18 studies
- Mechanical integrity not influenced by lamp type when using alternative photoinitiators

### Evidence Quality

- Overall evidence quality: **LOW**
- 11/18 studies rated high risk of bias
- High heterogeneity among included studies

## Clinical Implications

1. **Polywave LEDs provide partial advantage** for TPO-containing resins—specifically for degree of cure and surface hardness, but not mechanical strength
2. **Flexural strength is lamp-type independent**—regardless of photoinitiator or lamp type, mechanical properties appear unaffected by the curing light spectrum
3. **Material selection matters**: The benefit of polywave LEDs depends on the specific material's photoinitiator composition; check manufacturer specifications
4. **Low evidence quality** means these conclusions should be interpreted cautiously pending higher-quality RCTs or more homogeneous in vitro protocols

## Limitations

- Only abstract available (source_collection: pubmed-abstract); detailed methodology not fully verifiable
- High heterogeneity and risk of bias reduce conclusion reliability
- Predominantly in vitro studies; clinical translation uncertain
- TPO-centric; other alternative photoinitiators (Ivocerin/phenyl-bis(2,4,6-trimethylbenzoyl)-phosphine oxide, PPD) underrepresented

## Glossary

- **폴리웨이브 LED (Polywave LED)**: 광범위 스펙트럼 LED 광중합기 (380–500 nm); 여러 광개시제 활성화 가능
- **모노웨이브 LED (Monowave LED)**: 협대역 스펙트럼 LED (440–480 nm); 캠퍼퀴논(Camphorquinone, CQ) 최적화
- **TPO (Diphenyl-2,4,6-trimethylbenzoyl phosphine oxide)**: 흡수 피크 ~380–400 nm의 대체 광개시제; 바이올렛 광원이 필요
- **중합전환도 (Degree of Conversion, DC)**: 중합 중 C=C 이중결합이 C-C로 전환된 비율; 높을수록 중합 양호
- **미세경도 (Microhardness)**: 중합도를 반영하는 표면 경도 측정값
- **굴곡강도 (Flexural Strength)**: 굽힘 변형에 대한 저항; 광개시제 유형에 영향을 덜 받음
