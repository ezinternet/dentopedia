## Three-line Summary

Experimental self-adhesive resin cements (SARCs) containing 0–5 wt% bioactive glass (BAG) were tested against TheraCem and RelyX U200 on human dental pulp stem cells (DPSCs). All groups showed no significant cytotoxicity (MTT, 24–72 h), while BAG-modified groups (BG2–BG5) significantly upregulated late odontogenic markers DMP1 and BSP and promoted mineralized nodule formation — optimally at 1–2 wt% BAG. TheraCem showed the lowest mineralization (ARS) of all groups throughout the study, despite its calcium silicate composition.

## 세줄요약

BAG 0–5 wt% 함유 실험용 SARC를 TheraCem·RelyX U200과 비교해 치수줄기세포(DPSC) 생체적합성을 평가했다. MTT(24–72 h) 세포독성은 전 군 유의차 없었고, BAG 함유군(BG2–BG5)은 후기 상아질 분화 마커인 상아질 기질 단백질 1 (DMP1)과 골 시알로단백질 (BSP)을 유의 상향하고 광물화 결절 형성을 촉진(최적 1–2 wt%)했다. TheraCem은 칼슘 실리케이트 성분 특성에도 불구하고 모든 군 중 ARS 기준 광물화가 가장 낮았다.

## Summary

Self-adhesive resin cements (자가접착형 레진 시멘트, SARC) are widely used luting materials that simplify cementation by eliminating the separate etch-prime-bond steps of conventional adhesives. However, their acidic functional monomers raise concerns about pulp compatibility, particularly in deep cavity preparations with thin residual dentin.

This study by Kwon et al. (2026) incorporated bioactive glass (바이오액티브 글라스, BAG) at 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 wt% into experimental SARCs and evaluated the effects of their eluates on human 치수줄기세포 (Dental Pulp Stem Cells, DPSC) viability, 상아질 형성 분화 (Odontogenic Differentiation), and 광물화 (Mineralization). Two commercially available SARCs — TheraCem (TC; BISCO) and RelyX U200 (RU; 3M ESPE) — served as controls.

**Key conclusions:**
1. BAG incorporation up to 5 wt% does not compromise short-term DPSC viability (null hypothesis 1 accepted)
2. BAG significantly enhances late-stage odontogenic differentiation and mineralization (null hypotheses 2 & 3 rejected)
3. The optimal BAG bioactive window is **1–2 wt%** for mineral nodule formation — higher concentrations show a paradoxical decrease despite greater gene expression
4. TheraCem consistently demonstrates the **worst mineralization performance** of all groups tested

## Key Contributions

1. First study to systematically compare 0–5 wt% BAG-modified SARCs against commercial controls on DPSC odontogenic differentiation endpoints
2. Identifies 1–2 wt% BAG as an optimal bioactive concentration window — non-linear biphasic dose-response rather than simple dose-dependency
3. Challenges TheraCem's "bioactive" positioning: its calcium silicate composition does not translate to superior DPSC mineralization support in this model
4. Mechanistic insight: BAG-derived Ca²⁺ and silicate ions promote differentiation at moderate levels; excessive concentrations may cause hyperalkalinity (>pH 9.0) that suppresses alkaline phosphatase (ALP) activity and inhibits calcium phosphate precipitation

## Methodology

- **Model**: In-vitro eluate model per ISO 10993-12 (disk 5 mm × 2 mm; surface area/volume ratio 3 cm²/mL; 37°C, 24 h extraction)
- **Test groups**: BG0, BG1, BG2, BG3, BG4, BG5 (0–5 wt% BAG; experimental Mediclus formulations), TheraCem (TC), RelyX U200 (RU)
- **Cell line**: Human DPSCs (passage 5; CefoBio, Seoul)
- **Assays**:
  - MTT cytotoxicity at dilutions 1:2 and 1:4, timepoints 24 h and 72 h
  - qRT-PCR: early markers (DSPP, RUNX2, COL1) and late markers (DMP1, BSP) at days 3, 7, 14
  - Immunofluorescence (IF): DMP1 and BSP protein on day 7 (ImageJ mean gray value quantification)
  - Alizarin Red S (ARS) mineralization staining: days 14, 21, 28 (O.D. 560 nm)
- **Statistics**: One-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc; n=3; p<0.05

## Results

### Cell Viability (MTT)

No statistically significant differences observed among any group (BG0–BG5, TC, RU) at 24 h or 72 h, at either dilution (1:2 or 1:4). A slight decline at 72 h vs 24 h was attributed to general eluate exposure effects, not material-specific cytotoxicity.

### Gene Expression — Odontogenic Differentiation Markers

| Marker | Type | BG1–BG2 | BG3–BG5 | TheraCem | RelyX U200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DSPP | Early | Sporadic ↑ (BG1 d7*) | Sporadic ↑ | Comparable to OM | Comparable to OM |
| RUNX2 | Early | Sporadic ↑ (BG2 d14*) | Sporadic ↑ (BG3,5 d14*) | Comparable | Comparable |
| COL1 | Early | No sig change | BG4,5 ↑ d14* | Comparable | Comparable |
| DMP1 | **Late** | BG2 ↑ d3,7*; BG1 ↓ d7* | BG3–5 ↑ d7*; all BGs ↑ d14* | ↑ d14 only | ↑ d14 only |
| BSP | **Late** | BG2 ↑↑ d3,7* | BG3–5 ↑↑ d3,7* (BG5 highest) | No sig diff vs OM | No sig diff vs OM |

*p<0.05 vs OM group

Early markers: no consistent or dose-dependent trend. Late markers: systematic and sustained upregulation in BG2–BG5, particularly BSP which showed concentration-related tendency (BG3–BG5 > BG2).

### Protein Expression — Immunofluorescence (Day 7)

BG2–BG5: significantly higher DMP1 and BSP fluorescence intensity than OM group (p<0.05). TC and RU: comparable to OM (no significant difference). Pattern: DMP1 showed elongated fibrillar distribution (extracellular matrix role); BSP showed punctate cytoplasmic localization (vesicular storage prior to secretion).

### Mineralized Nodule Formation (ARS Staining)

Progressive increase from days 14→28 in all groups. Ranking at day 28:

**OM (positive control) > BG1 ≈ BG2 > BG3–BG5 > RU > TC ≈ GM (negative control)**

- TC group: lowest O.D. values at all time points, significantly below GM at day 14 (p<0.05)
- BG1–BG2: highest among BG groups at day 28 — paradoxically better than BG3–BG5 despite lower BSP mRNA
- Proposed mechanism: excessive Ca²⁺/silicate ions at higher BAG concentrations create hyperalkaline microenvironment → disrupts intracellular homeostasis, reduces ALP activity, impedes calcium phosphate precipitation

### Mechanistic Interpretation: Biphasic BAG Dose-Response

```
BAG wt%:  0      1      2      3      4      5
Gene (BSP): ─────  ↑      ↑↑     ↑↑↑    ↑↑↑↑   ↑↑↑↑↑  (monotonic ↑)
Mineralization: ─  ↑↑↑   ↑↑↑    ↑↑     ↑      ↑      (peaks at 1–2 wt%)
```

Discrepancy explained by: high BAG → high ionic burst → initial gene transcription driven; but excess ions → hyperalkalinity, osmotic stress → impaired post-transcriptional processing → reduced matrix protein secretion → less mineralization despite high mRNA.
