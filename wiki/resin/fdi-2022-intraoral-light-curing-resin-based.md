## Three-line Summary

The FDI 2022 policy statement formally recognizes that narrow-spectrum LED and laser 광중합 장치 (Light-Curing Unit, LCU)s may fail to activate alternative 광중합 개시제 (Photoinitiator, PI)s used in modern 레진 기반 재료 (Resin-Based Material, RBM)s, creating a clinically underrecognized polymerization failure risk. Manufacturers of both LCUs and light-curable materials are required under ISO 10650:2018 and ISO 4049:2019 to provide complete spectral and irradiance specifications. Professional education on correct LCU technique — tip position, angulation, movement, and exposure time — is identified as an essential but insufficiently practiced element of clinical care.

## 세줄요약

FDI 2022 정책 성명서는 협소 스펙트럼 LED·레이저 LCU가 캄포퀴논 이외의 신형 광개시제를 활성화하지 못할 수 있음을 공식 인정한 국제 치과연맹 문서다. ISO 10650:2018(LCU)·4049:2019(레진 재료)에 따라 제조사는 파장·조사도·노출 시간·재료 두께 등 완전한 스펙트럼 사양을 레이블에 표기해야 한다. 팁 위치·각도·이동·노출 시간에 관한 임상가 교육이 장기 수복 성공의 핵심으로 강조된다.

## Summary

This is an official FDI World Dental Federation policy statement, adopted at the FDI General Assembly in Sydney (September 2021) and published in the International Dental Journal in 2022. It constitutes the highest-level professional consensus guidance on intraoral light curing currently available.

The document addresses a widespread but underrecognized clinical problem: the growing mismatch between LCU emission spectra and the absorption spectra of alternative photoinitiators (beyond camphorquinone) incorporated into newer RBMs. Traditional quartz-tungsten-halogen (QTH) LCUs emit broad spectra that activate most photoinitiators; modern narrow-spectrum LED and laser LCUs may not.

## Key Contributions

- **Spectral incompatibility formally recognized**: Narrow-spectrum LED/laser LCUs may not activate all photosensitizers in modern RBMs using alternative photoinitiators beyond camphorquinone — identified as an "often unrecognized problem."
- **Mandatory manufacturer disclosure (ISO compliance)**:
  - Light-curable material manufacturers: disclose required wavelength range, irradiance, exposure time, and maximum RBM thickness (ISO 4049:2019).
  - LCU manufacturers: disclose emitted radiant power, radiant exitance, irradiance loss over distance, emission spectrum, active tip area, light transmission, and beam uniformity (ISO 10650:2018).
- **Clinical education requirement**: Professional guidance and education on tip position, movement, angulation, and exposure time are explicitly identified as needed but currently inadequate.
- **Research advocacy**: FDI supports further research on LCU safety and efficiency.
- **Eye protection**: Appropriate eye protection is required for safe LCU use.

## Methodology

FDI consensus process; adopted by the FDI General Assembly at the 2021 World Dental Congress. Policy statement format — no primary experimental data. Based on the best available scientific evidence at the time of adoption.

## Results

**Core FDI policy positions:**

1. Light-curable material manufacturers must comply with ISO 4049:2019: provide specific wavelengths, required irradiance, exposure time, and maximum RBM thickness for assured adequate polymerization.
2. LCU manufacturers must comply with ISO 10650:2018: provide radiant power, radiant exitance, irradiance-distance curves, emission spectrum, active tip area, beam uniformity, and transmission data.
3. Further research on LCU safety and efficiency is supported and encouraged.

**Key principles:**
- Adequate light curing is important but commonly underestimated in clinical practice.
- Correct LCU selection and use is essential for patient/operator safety and long-term restoration success.

**Definitions standardized by this document:**

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| 조사도 (Irradiance, mW/cm²) | Radiant power received per unit area; equals exitance at 0 mm from tip |
| 복사 발산도 (Radiant exitance, mW/cm²) | Radiant power emitted per unit area |
| 발광 스펙트럼 (Emission spectrum, nm) | Wavelength range emitted by light source |
| 광 빔 균일성 (Light beam uniformity) | Homogeneity of irradiance and spectral power across beam |
| 광중합 개시제 (Photoinitiator) | Component activated by specific visible wavelength to initiate RBM polymerization |
| 광감응제 (Photosensitizer) | Component reacting with accelerator to produce reactive species; camphorquinone is typical |
