## Three-line Summary
PRISMA-guided comprehensive review of 38 studies (selected from 819) mapping endogenous opioid counter-system modulation of orthodontic-induced dental pulp neurogenic inflammation (PubMed/Scopus/ISI, through December 2024).
β-endorphin, enkephalins, dynorphin, SST, and endocannabinoids act via MOR/DOR/KOR and anti-inflammatory cytokines (IL-10, TGF-β) to suppress SP/CGRP-driven neurogenic inflammation; moderate forces (56–180 g) are compensated, while severe forces (>180 g) overwhelm antinociceptive capacity.
Clinically, reactivation intervals of ~40–50 days allow opioid system recovery; forces >180 g risk asymptomatic irreversible pulpitis or necrosis.

## 세줄요약
PRISMA 기반 포괄적 문헌고찰; 819편 중 38편 선정(PubMed/Scopus/ISI); 교정력에 의한 치수 신경원성 염증 (Neurogenic Inflammation, NI)의 내인성 오피오이드 조절 기전 분석.
β-엔도르핀·엔케팔린·다이노르핀·소마토스타틴 (Somatostatin, SST)·엔도카나비노이드가 뮤-오피오이드 수용체 (Mu-Opioid Receptor, MOR)/델타-오피오이드 수용체 (Delta-Opioid Receptor, DOR)/카파-오피오이드 수용체 (Kappa-Opioid Receptor, KOR) 및 IL-10, TGF-β를 통해 물질 P (Substance P, SP)/칼시토닌 유전자 관련 펩티드 (Calcitonin Gene-Related Peptide, CGRP) 유발 신경원성 염증을 억제; 교정력 56–180 g은 보상되나 180 g 초과 시 항통증 용량 초과 위험.
임상적으로 중등도 교정력(56–180 g) + 재활성 간격 약 40–50일이 오피오이드 시스템 회복에 필요하며, 과도한 교정력은 무증상 비가역적 치수염·괴사를 유발할 수 있다.

## Summary
This PRISMA- and AMSTAR-guided comprehensive review systematically mapped the endogenous opioid antinociceptive counter-system that suppresses orthodontic-induced neurogenic pulpal inflammation. From 819 identified records, 38 studies were selected covering eight opioid peptide categories: β-endorphin (12 studies), SST (7), methionine-enkephalin (5), endocannabinoids (5), leu-enkephalin (3), dynorphin (3), orphanin (3), and anti-inflammatory cytokines (1). The review establishes that moderate orthodontic forces (56–180 g) trigger compensatory pulp immune-cell secretion of β-endorphin and enkephalins that hyperpolarize Aδ/C nociceptors via GIRK K⁺ channels, while severe forces (>180 g) may overwhelm this capacity, leading to clinically silent irreversible pulpitis or necrosis. Receptor heterodimerization (MOR–NK1, MOR–DOR, CB1–DOR) is proposed as the mechanistic basis for inter-patient pain variability during orthodontic treatment.

## Key Contributions
- First PRISMA/AMSTAR review specifically mapping the endogenous opioid counter-system in orthodontic pulpal neurogenic inflammation
- Establishes force-threshold framework: moderate 56–180 g (opioid system compensates) vs. severe >180 g (overwhelms antinociceptive capacity)
- Identifies 8 opioid peptide categories across 38 studies with quantitative breakdown (β-endorphin 12, SST 7, methionine-enkephalin 5, endocannabinoids 5, leu-enkephalin 3, dynorphin 3, orphanin 3)
- Maps peripheral and central antinociceptive pathways: pulp immune cells → MOR on Aδ fibres → GIRK hyperpolarization (peripheral); locus coeruleus/dorsal raphe/PAG → caudal nucleus inhibition (central)
- Explains receptor heterodimerization (MOR–NK1, MOR–DOR, CB1–DOR) as mechanistic basis for variable pain experience among orthodontic patients
- Proposes ~40–50 day reactivation intervals as clinically appropriate to allow opioid system recovery

## Methodology
- **Design**: PRISMA + AMSTAR-guided comprehensive literature review
- **Databases**: PubMed (304), Scopus (305), ISI Web of Science (210), manual search (21) = 840 total; 572 after deduplication; 38 final
- **Inclusion criteria**: Reviews/clinical trials on dental pulp × endogenous opioids; cell-line studies on orthodontic opioid expression; Q1–Q4 SCImago-indexed English journals meeting AMSTAR/PRISMA standards
- **Exclusion**: Non-pulp/non-opioid focus; pharmacological opioid interventions; non-indexed journals; poor methodological transparency
- **Search period**: Up to December 2024; two independent reviewers + arbitration by third reviewer
- **Search terms**: "dental pulp," "tooth pulp," "opioids," "neurogenic inflammation," "orthodontic movement" combined with each opioid peptide keyword

## Results
| Opioid / System | Studies (n) | Key finding |
|---|---|---|
| β-endorphin | 12 | Most studied; pulp immune cells secrete β-endorphin → MOR on Aδ fibres → GIRK K⁺ hyperpolarization → antinociception; increases at 24 h and 7 days |
| Somatostatin (SST) | 7 | SST-immunoreactive fibres in human dental pulp; pre-synaptic inhibition of SP/CGRP via SST2 receptors |
| Methionine-enkephalin | 5 | Rapidly depleted then upregulated proportionally to force intensity; DOR agonist |
| Endocannabinoids | 5 | Anandamide, 2-AG from immune cells; CB1 in trigeminal ganglion, CB2 in immune cells; severe/long-term forces may upregulate CB1 mRNA |
| Leu-enkephalin | 3 | DOR agonist; role in peripheral antinociception |
| Dynorphin | 3 | KOR agonist; complex excitatory/inhibitory effects depending on receptor and context |
| Orphanin | 3 | Nociceptin/orphanin FQ system; ORL1 receptor |
| Anti-inflammatory cytokines | 1 | Opioids → M2 macrophage polarization → IL-10, TGF-β → restrict neurogenic inflammation; IL-10/TGF-β–opioid loop promotes pulp mineralization |

**Force-threshold clinical framework:**
| Force range | Opioid system response | Pulp outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Moderate (56–180 g) | Compensates; β-endorphin/enkephalin upregulated | Asymptomatic; reversible neurogenic inflammation |
| Severe (>180 g) | Overwhelmed; central pathways recruited | Risk of asymptomatic irreversible pulpitis or necrosis |

**Receptor mechanism**: Gαi/o-coupled MOR/DOR/KOR → inhibit adenylyl cyclase → ↓cAMP → GIRK K⁺ opening → hyperpolarization; also modulate Nav, inhibit PKA → ↓p38/ERK → ↓proinflammatory cytokines.
