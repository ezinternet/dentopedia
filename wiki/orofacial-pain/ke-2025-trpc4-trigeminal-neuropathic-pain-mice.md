## Three-line Summary

Animal (in-vivo) study, adult male/female C57BL/6 mice (n=6/group per behavioral cohort), using a chronic constriction of the unilateral infraorbital nerve (CION) model of trigeminal neuropathic pain (TNP).

TRPC4 mRNA/protein rose in the trigeminal ganglion (TG) after CION; TRPC4 knockdown (shRNA) or antagonist ML204 attenuated mechanical allodynia/spontaneous pain/cold allodynia, while agonist Englerin A reproduced pain-like behavior in naive mice via Ca²⁺ influx → ERK/P38 phosphorylation → ATF2 activation (confirmed by co-immunoprecipitation of p-P38/ATF2).

Identifies TRPC4-Ca²⁺-ERK/P38-ATF2 as a druggable TG pathway for TNP, but findings are preclinical (rodent, non-selective pharmacological tools ML204/Englerin A also affect TRPC5) and require confirmation with more specific TRPC4 modulators before clinical translation.

## 세줄요약

동물(생체내) 연구, 성체 C57BL/6 마우스(암수, 행동실험 그룹당 n=6), 편측 안와하신경 만성압박술 (Chronic Constriction of the unilateral Infraorbital Nerve, CION) 모델을 이용한 삼차신경병증성 통증 (Trigeminal Neuropathic Pain, TNP) 연구.

CION 후 삼차신경절 (Trigeminal Ganglion, TG)에서 TRPC4 mRNA/단백질 발현이 증가했고, TRPC4 shRNA 넉다운 또는 길항제 ML204는 기계적 이질통(Mechanical Allodynia)·자발통·냉이질통을 완화한 반면, 작용제 Englerin A는 나이브 마우스에서 칼슘(Ca²⁺) 유입 → ERK/P38 인산화 → ATF2 활성화 경로(공동면역침전으로 p-P38/ATF2 직접결합 확인)를 통해 유사 통증행동을 유발했다.

TRPC4-Ca²⁺-ERK/P38-ATF2 경로가 TNP의 새로운 치료 표적임을 제시하나, 전임상(설치류) 결과이며 사용된 약리학적 도구(ML204/Englerin A)가 TRPC5에도 작용해 특이성이 낮다는 한계가 있어 임상 적용 전 더 선택적인 TRPC4 조절제로 재검증이 필요하다.

## Summary

This mouse study establishes Transient Receptor Potential Canonical 4 (TRPC4) — a Ca²⁺-permeable, non-selective cation channel — as a driver of trigeminal neuropathic pain (TNP) using the chronic constriction of the unilateral infraorbital nerve (CION) model. Bioinformatic screening of a GEO trigeminal-neuropathic-pain transcriptome, cross-referenced against CTD/GeneCards neuropathic-pain gene sets and refined by LASSO regression, singled out TRPC4 among four candidate genes. TRPC4 mRNA and protein rose progressively in the trigeminal ganglion (TG) from Day 14–28 post-CION, localized exclusively to TG neurons (NeuN⁺, not glial GS⁺) and concentrated in the V2 branch territory, spanning NF200⁺ (39.4%), CGRP⁺ (37.0%), and IB4⁺ (12.2%) afferent subtypes. Both pharmacological blockade (antagonist ML204, local and systemic) and genetic knockdown (TG-targeted shRNA) of TRPC4 attenuated CION-induced mechanical allodynia, spontaneous pain, and cold allodynia, while the TRPC4 agonist Englerin A reproduced all three pain phenotypes when injected into the TG of naive mice. Mechanistically, TRPC4 activation drove intracellular Ca²⁺ influx (confirmed by calcium imaging in TRPC4-overexpressing HEK293 cells and primary TG neurons, abolished by TRPC4 knockout or calcium chelation with BAPTA-AM), which selectively activated ERK and P38 (not JNK, PI3K, Akt, or mTOR), culminating in phosphorylation of the transcription factor ATF2 — a direct p-P38–ATF2 protein interaction was confirmed by co-immunoprecipitation. This is the first report implicating TRPC4 in TNP specifically, adding a new cation-channel mechanistic axis (Ca²⁺ influx → MAPK → transcription factor) alongside chloride-channel and T-type calcium-channel mechanisms already characterized in other orofacial pain contexts.

## Key Contributions

- First evidence for a causal TRPC4 role in trigeminal neuropathic pain (TNP), distinct from the more studied TRPA1/TRPV1 channels.
- Demonstrates both necessity (TRPC4 antagonism/knockdown reduces CION-induced pain) and sufficiency (TRPC4 agonism alone reproduces pain-like behavior in naive mice).
- Maps TRPC4 distribution in the trigeminal ganglion (TG): neuron-specific (not glial), enriched in the V2 branch, spanning Aβ (NF200⁺), peptidergic C/Aδ (CGRP⁺), and non-peptidergic C-fiber (IB4⁺) neuron classes.
- Delineates the full mechanistic cascade: TRPC4 → intracellular Ca²⁺ influx → ERK/P38 phosphorylation (selectively, not JNK/PI3K/Akt/mTOR) → direct p-P38–ATF2 binding → ATF2 phosphorylation/activation.
- Validates findings across pharmacological (ML204 antagonist, Englerin A agonist), genetic (TG-targeted AAV-shRNA), and biophysical (calcium imaging, Co-IP) methods, and replicates key findings in female mice.

## Methodology

- Mouse model: CION (intraoral infraorbital nerve ligation) in adult C57BL/6 mice (male + female cohorts), n=6/group for behavioral endpoints.
- Behavioral readouts (Day 3–28): Von Frey mechanical allodynia threshold, non-evoked nociceptive (facial grooming) behavior, acetone-induced cold allodynia.
- Pharmacology: TRPC4 antagonist ML204 (local subcutaneous 10 μg and systemic IP 2 mg/kg/day) and agonist Englerin A (intra-TG stereotactic injection, dose range 0.25–25 μg, 2.5 μg used for mechanistic work).
- Genetic knockdown: AAV-delivered Trpc4 shRNA stereotactically injected into the TG (vs. scramble control), 4-week recovery.
- Bioinformatic candidate-gene selection: GEO TNP transcriptome DEGs (limma) intersected with CTD/GeneCards neuropathic-pain gene sets, refined by LASSO regression to 4 candidates, TRPC4 selected.
- In vitro: primary TG neuron culture and HEK293 (wild-type, TRPC4-KO, TRPC4-overexpression) cell models; calcium imaging (Fura-4 AM / Cal-590) with and without the calcium chelator BAPTA-AM; Western blot for the PI3K-Akt-mTOR and MAPK (ERK/P38/JNK) pathways plus ATF2/p-ATF2; immunofluorescence colocalization (TRPC4 with NeuN/GS/NF200/CGRP/IB4); co-immunoprecipitation of p-P38 and ATF2.

## Results

- TRPC4 (not other Trpc family members) mRNA/protein significantly increased in TG after CION (p<0.05 mRNA, p<0.001 protein; sustained Day 14–28); replicated in female mice.
- Local and systemic ML204 significantly reduced CION-induced mechanical allodynia, spontaneous pain, and cold allodynia (p<0.05–0.001); local effect was side-specific to the injured (ipsilateral) side; repeated systemic dosing delayed but did not fully prevent pain-like behavior.
- Intra-TG Englerin A dose-dependently reproduced mechanical allodynia, spontaneous pain, and cold allodynia in naive mice within 1–24 h (p<0.05–0.001 vs. vehicle); effect plateaued between 2.5 μg and 25 μg.
- Englerin A significantly raised intracellular Ca²⁺ (ΔF/F0) only in TRPC4-transfected HEK293 cells and primary TG neurons (p<0.001), not in TRPC4-KO cells or controls.
- Englerin A/TRPC4 overexpression activated p-Akt, p-ERK, p-P38, p-JNK (p<0.05–0.001) but not p-PI3K/p-mTOR; effect abolished by TRPC4 knockout and blunted by BAPTA-AM calcium chelation.
- In vivo, CION and intra-TG Englerin A both significantly elevated p-ERK, p-P38, ATF2, p-ATF2 (p<0.01–0.001) with no change in PI3K/Akt/mTOR/JNK — implicating the ERK/P38 branch specifically, not JNK or PI3K-Akt.
- Co-immunoprecipitation confirmed direct p-P38–ATF2 protein interaction in primary TG neurons; P38 inhibitor SB202190 blocked Englerin A-induced ATF2 activation.
- TG-targeted TRPC4 shRNA significantly rescued all three CION- and Englerin A-induced pain phenotypes and normalized TRPC4/p-ERK/p-P38/p-ATF2/ATF2 expression (p<0.05–0.001), with no baseline pain-behavior effect in sham animals.
- Limitation: pharmacological tools (ML204, Englerin A) are not TRPC4-selective (also modulate TRPC5); animal-only evidence; downstream ATF2 targets (e.g., VIP/NPY via ATF2/c-Jun) not directly tested in TG.
