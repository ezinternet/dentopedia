## One-line Summary
Animal study (OVX + fracture mouse model): propranolol + PTH synergistically increased bone mass (BV/TV +93% vs vehicle) by reversing NE-mediated suppression of PTH osteogenesis via the Bmal1/RUNX2 axis.

## 한줄요약
동물연구(난소절제 골절 마우스): 프로프라놀올+PTH 병용이 BV/TV +93% 달성; 노르에피네프린 (Norepinephrine, NE) → 베타 아드레날린 수용체 (β-Adrenergic Receptor, βAR) → Bmal1 억제 경로를 차단해 PTH 골동화 효과 증폭.

## Three-line Summary

Animal study (OVX C57BL/6 mouse with tibia fracture; 4 groups: vehicle, iPTH, propranolol, PTH+propranolol; 4-week treatment); outcomes: μCT (BV/TV, Tb.Th, Tb.N), TRAP staining, calcein double labeling (MAR), ELISA (NE, OCN, CTX-I), in vitro BMSC experiments with siRNA Bmal1 knockdown.

PTH+propranolol increased trabecular BV/TV by 93% vs vehicle (PTH alone +54%); post-fracture sympathetic hyperactivation raises NE → βAR → Bmal1↓ → RUNX2↓, suppressing PTH osteoanabolic effect; propranolol reverses NE-induced RANKL↑ and RUNX2↓ by restoring Bmal1 expression.

Identifies Bmal1 as the critical mediator between sympathetic signalling and PTH osteogenesis; suggests β-blocker + PTH combination as a bone regeneration strategy; relevant to implant osseointegration in hypertensive patients where chronic sympathetic tone may suppress bone formation.

## 세줄요약

동물연구(난소절제 C57BL/6 암컷, 경골 골절; vehicle·iPTH·프로프라놀올·PTH+프로프라놀올 4군, 4주); 미세전산화단층촬영(Micro-CT)·ELISA(NE)·석회화 이중표지·골수기질세포(Bone Marrow Stromal Cell, BMSC) 시험관 실험·Bmal1 소형간섭RNA(siRNA) 녹다운.

PTH+프로프라놀올: BV/TV +93%(PTH 단독 +54%); 골절 후 교감신경 과활성→NE↑→βAR→Bmal1↓→RUNX2↓로 PTH 골동화 억제; 프로프라놀올이 RANKL↓·RUNX2↑ 복원 — Bmal1이 교감신경-PTH 골형성 간 중개 분자임을 최초 규명.

고혈압 환자 만성 교감신경 항진이 임플란트 골유착을 억제할 수 있는 기전과, β차단제가 이를 보상할 수 있음을 시사; β차단제+PTH 병용 전략의 동물 근거.

## Summary
This study from Peking University used an OVX + tibia fracture mouse model to investigate propranolol's ability to potentiate PTH's anabolic bone effects. Fracture induced sympathetic stress (elevated serum NE), which activated osteoblast β-adrenergic receptors and suppressed PTH-induced osteogenesis. Propranolol reversed this suppression: PTH alone increased femoral trabecular BV/TV by 54%, while PTH + propranolol increased it by 93%. The mechanistic axis was identified as NE → βAR → BMAL1 suppression and RANKL upregulation in osteoblasts, both reversed by propranolol. BMAL1 knockdown (siRNA) blocked the propranolol rescue effect, confirming BMAL1 as the key circadian clock mediator.

## Key Contributions
- Demonstrates that post-fracture sympathetic activation (elevated NE) antagonizes PTH anabolism
- Propranolol + PTH: ~80% enhancement of PTH effect on trabecular bone mass and fracture healing
- Identifies NE → βAR → BMAL1 suppression as the mechanistic bridge between sympathetic tone and PTH resistance
- Distinguishes reversible βAR blockade (propranolol) from β2AR gene knockout — important distinction for clinical translation
- BMAL1 (circadian clock gene) proposed as pharmacological target for bone loss post-fracture

## Methodology
OVX female C57BL/6 mice (12 weeks), tibia fracture 2 months post-OVX. Groups: iPTH (80 μg/kg/day SC), propranolol (0.5 g/L drinking water), PTH+propranolol, vehicle. Duration: 4 weeks. Outcomes: μCT (femur, L5, callus), TRAP/OCN histology, calcein labeling (MAR), ELISA (serum OCN, CTX-I, NE), rotarod. In vitro: BMSCs with iPTH ± NE ± propranolol; Bmal1-siRNA knockdown; conditioned media osteoclastogenesis assay.

## Results
Femur trabecular BV/TV (vs vehicle): propranolol +mild significant; PTH +54%; PTH+propranolol +93%. L5: PTH +26%, PTH+propranolol +47%. In vitro: NE suppressed iPTH-induced ALP, ARS, RUNX2, Bmal1 expression; propranolol rescued all. NE enhanced RANKL, propranolol attenuated. Bmal1-siRNA blocked propranolol rescue on RUNX2 and osteoblast differentiation. Fracture callus BV/TV: PTH+propranolol > PTH alone. No adverse effects (body weight, liver/kidney, rotarod) over 4 weeks.
