## Three-line Summary

StatPearls narrative textbook chapter (NCBI Bookshelf, last update 2023-03-17) — basic physiology of bone remodeling: two cell lineages (osteoclast/osteoblast) plus osteocytes (mechanotransduction), governed by Wolff's Law of load adaptation, regulated hormonally (PTH, estrogen, calcitonin, GH, glucocorticoids, thyroid).

Key principles: osteoclasts resorb then osteoblasts deposit in a coupled cycle maintaining plasma calcium homeostasis; PTH/glucocorticoids raise RANKL and lower OPG (OPG = decoy receptor), estrogen deficiency accelerates turnover with resorption outpacing formation; drugs act on the RANK-RANKL axis (denosumab), farnesyl pyrophosphate synthase (bisphosphonates), SERM (raloxifene), and calcitonin.

Clinical relevance for dentistry: this is the biology that underpins osseointegration and peri-implant bone turnover; disease states (osteoporosis, hyperparathyroidism, Paget, osteopetrosis) and antiresorptive drugs explain implant survival risk and MRONJ-adjacent pharmacology.

## 세줄요약

StatPearls 교과서 장(NCBI Bookshelf, 2023-03-17 마지막 개정) — 골개조(Remodeling)의 기본 생리: 두 세포 계통(파골세포·조골세포)과 골세포(기계적 감지·신호 전달), Wolff 법칙에 따른 하중 적응, 호르몬(PTH·에스트로겐·칼시토닌·성장호르몬·글루코코르티코이드·갑상선호르몬)에 의한 조절.

핵심 원리: 파골세포 흡수→조골세포 형성의 결합 순환으로 혈장 칼슘 항상성을 유지; PTH·글루코코르티코이드는 RANKL↑·OPG(decoy 수용체)↓, 에스트로겐 결핍은 재건 대비 흡수 우위의 고전환을 유발; 약물은 RANK-RANKL 축(데노수맙)·farnesyl pyrophosphate synthase(비스포스포네이트)·SERM(랄록시펜)·칼시토닌에 작용.

치과적 의미: 이 생리가 골유착(Osseointegration)·임플란트 주위 골대사(Peri-implant Bone Turnover)의 기반이며, 골다공증·부갑상선항진증·Paget·대리석골병과 항흡수 약물이 임플란트 생존 위험·항흡수약 관련 약물학을 설명한다.

## Summary

This StatPearls chapter (NCBI Bookshelf, last updated 2023-03-17) is a narrative physiology textbook reference for bone remodeling — the continuous resorption-and-deposition cycle that maintains skeletal structural integrity and plasma calcium/phosphorus homeostasis. It is not a clinical study (no cohort, no n, no abstract) and reports no quantitative benchmarks; its value is consolidating the canonical biology: hematopoietic-lineage osteoclasts resorb bone (Howship lacunae), mesenchymal osteoblasts deposit collagen/mineral and become lining cells, osteocytes, or undergo apoptosis, and osteocytes act as mechanotransduction conductors of Wolff's Law load adaptation. Hormonal regulation (PTH→RANKL, estrogen-mediated IL-6 blockade, calcitonin, GH/IGF, glucocorticoid RANKL↑/OPG↓, thyroid hormone) is mapped with its pharmacology (bisphosphonates, denosumab, raloxifene, calcitonin) and disease consequences (osteoporosis, hyperparathyroidism, Paget disease, osteopetrosis). For implant dentistry the chapter is the physiological substrate of osseointegration and peri-implant bone turnover, and the mechanistic basis for antiresorptive drug effects relevant to implant survival and MRONJ-adjacent pharmacology.

## Key Contributions

- Single consolidated physiology reference for bone remodeling: resorption (osteoclast) → reversal (macrophage-lineage) → formation (osteoblast), with osteocytes as the most abundant bone cell and the mechanotransduction conductor (Wolff's Law).
- Complete endocrine regulation map: PTH (RANKL-mediated, CaSR negative feedback), estrogen (IL-6 blockade; deficiency → turnover with resorption outranking formation), calcitonin (osteoclast inhibition), GH/IGF (net formation), glucocorticoids (RANKL↑/OPG↓ + osteoblast apoptosis), thyroid hormone (turnover rate).
- RANKL/OPG axis as the central molecular switch: OPG is a decoy receptor in the TNF superfamily; denosumab is its clinical anti-RANKL antibody counterpart.
- Clinical pharmacology and pathology bridge: bisphosphonate (farnesyl pyrophosphate synthase), raloxifene (SERM), and disease states (osteoporosis, hyperparathyroidism primary/secondary/tertiary, Paget, osteopetrosis) — directly relevant to implant risk stratification.

## Results

| Aspect | Content |
|---|---|
| Cell types | Osteoclast = resorptive, hematopoietic (multinucleated, lysosomal enzymes + H⁺, Howship lacunae); osteoblast = formative, mesenchymal (collagen/mineral deposition; fate → lining cell / osteocyte / apoptosis); osteocyte = most abundant, mechanotransduction |
| Wolff's Law | ↑load → strengthen spongy then cortical architecture; ↓load → weaken; duration·magnitude·rate of forces govern integrity |
| RANKL/OPG | RANKL (raised by PTH, glucocorticoids) → osteoclast activity; OPG = decoy receptor blocks RANKL-RANK; denosumab = anti-RANKL mAb |
| Estrogen deficiency | Accelerated bone turnover, resorption outpaces formation; IL-6 production/action blocked by estrogen; osteoclast survival thrives in deficiency |
| Disease states | Osteoporosis (most common metabolic skeletal disorder; wrist/hip/spine; menopause + aging), hyperparathyroidism (primary/secondary/tertiary), Paget (disorganized resorption + woven-bone response, pathologic fracture), osteopetrosis (osteoclast failure → ↑mass, weak integrity) |
| Drug mechanisms | Bisphosphonates (farnesyl pyrophosphate synthase inhibition → osteoclast detachment; non-N types cause osteomalacia), calcitonin (osteoclast inhibition, ↑renal Ca excretion), raloxifene (SERM, ↓osteoclast survival), denosumab (RANKL blockade) |
| Markers/imaging | Formation: ALP, B-ALP, osteocalcin, PICP, PINP; resorption: OHP, PYD, DPD, BSP, TR-ACP; scintigraphy (Tc-99m-MDP), DEXA |
