## One-line Summary
Animal + in vitro study: Ang II activates osteoclasts via RANKL induction in osteoblasts (AT1 receptor-mediated); olmesartan completely blocks this pathway; AT1a knockout mice show no ovariectomy-induced bone loss, establishing RAAS as a bone-remodelling regulator.

## 한줄요약
동물+시험관 연구: Ang II가 조골세포에서 RANKL 발현 유도 → 파골세포 (Osteoclast) 활성화 → 골밀도↓; olmesartan (ARB)으로 완전 차단; AT1a 수용체 결핍 마우스는 난소절제 후 골 소실 없음 — 레닌-안지오텐신-알도스테론계 (RAAS)의 골 대사 조절 기초 메커니즘.

## Summary
This Osaka University study established the mechanistic link between the renin-angiotensin system and bone resorption. In vitro, Angiotensin II (Ang II) dose-dependently induced RANKL expression in osteoblasts, driving TRAP+ osteoclast formation; olmesartan (ARB) and MEK inhibitors completely blocked both RANKL induction and osteoclastogenesis. In a rat ovariectomy model, Ang II infusion accelerated bone density loss and increased urinary deoxypyridinoline; olmesartan attenuated these effects. AT1a receptor-deficient mice showed no bone volume decrease after OVX + nephrectomy, confirming that AT1 receptor signalling is the required pathway. These findings provide the mechanistic foundation for why RAAS inhibitors (ACEi, ARB) protect bone, directly explaining clinical observations in Saravi 2021 and Solomon 2011.

## Key Contributions
- Established Ang II → RANKL → osteoclast activation as a functionally significant bone-resorptive pathway
- Demonstrated that ARB (olmesartan) and MEK inhibition completely block this pathway
- AT1a knockout mouse confirms AT1 receptor specificity — not off-target effects
- Provided mechanistic basis for epidemiological findings showing ARB fracture protection
- Identified osteoblasts as the intermediary cell expressing RANKL in response to Ang II

## Methodology
Three complementary experimental models:
1. In vitro: bone marrow-derived mononuclear cells + Ang II (1×10⁻⁶ M) ± olmesartan or MEK inhibitors; TRAP-positive osteoclast quantification; RANKL mRNA in osteoblasts
2. Rat OVX model: Ang II infusion (200 ng/kg/min) ± olmesartan; bone density (DXA), TRAP activity, urinary deoxypyridinoline
3. Hypertensive rat + OVX: olmesartan treatment; bone density + TRAP
4. AT1a receptor-deficient mice + OVX + 5/6 nephrectomy: bone volume by microCT vs wild-type

## Results
- Ang II: significant increase in TRAP+ osteoclasts in vitro; blocked by olmesartan and MEK inhibitors (p<0.05)
- Ang II: significant RANKL upregulation in osteoblasts; completely blocked by olmesartan
- OVX + Ang II: ↑ TRAP activity, ↓ bone density, ↑ urinary deoxypyridinoline vs OVX alone
- OVX + olmesartan: attenuated bone density decrease and TRAP activity in hypertensive rats
- AT1a−/− mice: no bone volume decrease vs wild-type after OVX + nephrectomy
