## Three-line Summary
Narrative review (92 references, no pooled patient data) of the classification and treatment of 식편압입 (food impaction), organizing the field into four classification bases (etiology, direction, extent, contact relationship) and vertical vs horizontal treatment tracks plus an implant-specific section.
Cites implant-neighbor food impaction in >40% of patients and up to 66.9% after single-crown repairs, horizontal-type share 9.7%, Newell's occlusal adjustment with 80% symptomatic improvement, a 0.06 mm gap threshold, and 3–6-month recall after each recurrent-impaction repair.
Provides the classification-and-treatment taxonomy missing from the wiki's PCL-focused pages; as a narrative review it pools no efficacy data and flags personalized planning and longitudinal evidence as the field's main gaps.

## Summary
This narrative review is the only paper in the wiki that directly addresses the *classification* of 식편압입 (food impaction), consolidating four historical classification bases — etiology (Hirschfeld's five mechanisms), direction (vertical/horizontal/mixed), extent (extensive ≥3 quadrants / partial 1–2 quadrants / limited 2–4 teeth), and contact relationship (static/dynamic/mixed) — and mapping each to its clinical advantages and limitations. It then develops complementary treatment algorithms: vertical impaction via restoring interproximal contact (including the novel 근·원심 조절형 크라운 (Mesiodistal Adjustable, MDA crown)), occlusal adjustment, contact-zone adjustment, tooth/restoration form correction, and adjacent/opposing-tooth management; horizontal impaction via periodontal therapy, gingival papilla reconstruction, tooth-loss restoration, and oral-habit improvement. A dedicated implant section addresses 근접접촉 소실 (Proximal Contact Loss, PCL)-driven impaction, recommending screw retention to enable porcelain addition, broad-flat abutment contacts near the gingiva, immediate implantation for papilla preservation, and 3–6-month follow-up after each repair because recurrence accelerates. Clinically it serves as the classification-and-treatment backbone for the category, but its own limitations section concedes insufficient personalized planning, recurrent cases without predictive assessment, and a lack of longitudinal validation.

## Key Contributions
- **Four-axis classification synthesis** — the first classification-level organization in this wiki category: etiology (Hirschfeld, five mechanisms), direction (vertical/horizontal/mixed with horizontal = 9.7% of cases), extent (Zheng Dize: extensive ≥3 quadrants / partial 1–2 / limited 2–4 teeth), and contact relationship (Fang Bisong: static/dynamic/mixed, with two dynamic-pathogenesis mechanisms — irreversible mesial-displacement misalignment and absent food spillage pathways).
- **Vertical treatment taxonomy** — five directions with specific methods and indications (interproximal-contact restoration incl. MDA crown and fiber-reinforced/flowable resin + cerclage wire; occlusal reshaping with food-overflow grooves; contact-zone orthodontic/restorative adjustment; form correction via composite vs ceramic vs orthodontics; adjacent/opposing-tooth management).
- **Horizontal treatment taxonomy** — periodontal therapy, gingival papilla reconstruction (immediate-denture, soft-tissue graft, GTR, CAD/CAM abutments), tooth-loss restoration (implants/bridge/denture), and oral-habit modification.
- **Implant-specific prevention guidance** — screw retention for cleanable porcelain addition, recurrence-accelerates-after-each-repair insight with 3–6-month recall, immediate implantation for papilla preservation, and anti-impaction abutment contouring.

## Methodology
Narrative review — no systematic search protocol, databases, or patient-level data; synthesis of 92 references including Chinese-language epidemiological and clinical reports. Evidence is review-level opinion citing external figures (e.g., implant food impaction >40% per Wong et al.; 66.9% after single crowns; 17.5% of failed fixed-denture repairs; 45% 5-year periodontal incidence in recurrent impaction). No pooled efficacy outcomes.

## Results
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Implant-neighbor food impaction prevalence | >40% of patients (Wong et al.); up to 66.9% after single-crown implant repairs |
| Horizontal-type share of cases | 9.7% (Peng 2005) |
| Failed fixed-denture restorations attributable to food impaction | 17.5% |
| 5-year periodontal-disease incidence with recurrent impaction | 45% |
| Newell occlusal-adjustment symptomatic improvement | 80% |
| Interproximal gap threshold for excessive occlusal-contact area | >0.06 mm |
| Recall interval after recurrent-impaction repair | every 3–6 months |
| Extent-based classification (Zheng Dize) | extensive ≥3 quadrants / partial 1–2 / limited 2–4 teeth (limited = most common clinically) |
