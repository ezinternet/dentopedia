## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical observational study (41 patients, 15 procedures) measuring aerosol number concentration (0.5–20 μm aerodynamic particle sizer) with triplicate phantom-head controls to fingerprint the aerosol source.

The 3-in-1 air+water syringe generated aerosol during 75.3% of its use time (air-only 24.8%; water-only produced none), but its size distribution matched the non-salivary instrument source — unlike high/slow-speed drilling, which produced unexplained salivary aerosol (P<0.002).

The three-way syringe is a real aerosol generator, yet its aerosol is instrument (waterline) derived rather than salivary, so its infection risk is governed by DUWL water quality — reinforcing that clean syringe water is the control point.

## 세줄요약

전향 임상 관찰연구(환자 41명, 시술 15종)로 에어로졸 수농도(0.5–20 μm 공기역학 입자계수기, APS)를 삼중 팬텀헤드 대조와 비교해 에어로졸 발생원을 크기분포 지문으로 규명.

3-in-1 air+water 시린지는 사용시간의 75.3% 동안 에어로졸 발생(air만 24.8%, water만은 미검출)했으나 크기분포가 비타액성 기구 발생원과 일치 — 타액성 에어로졸이 나온 고속·저속 드릴(P<0.002)과 대비.

쓰리웨이 시린지는 실제 에어로졸 발생원이지만 그 에어로졸은 기구(수관 물) 유래이지 타액 유래가 아님 → 감염 위험은 DUWL 수질이 좌우, 즉 "깨끗한 시린지 물"이 통제 지점.

## Summary

Dudding et al. (PLoS One 2022), part of the AERATOR study, measured aerosol from **real dental patients** (not just manikins) with an aerodynamic particle sizer in a low-background room able to resolve a single cough. For each of 41 patients they ran the **3-in-1 (air/water, three-way) syringe** in three modes — air 30 s, water 30 s, air+water 30 s — plus a range of periodontal, oral-surgery and orthodontic procedures, and compared every aerosol size distribution against a triplicate **phantom-head control** to separate instrument-generated aerosol from salivary-contaminated aerosol.

The key finding for the three-way syringe: **air+water use aerosolised heavily (aerosol present 75.3% of use time)**, air-only much less (24.8%), and **water-only produced no detectable aerosol at all**. But the emitted aerosol's size-distribution fingerprint **matched the phantom-head (instrument-only) control**, meaning the aerosol is derived from the instrument/waterline spray, not from resuspended saliva. This contrasts sharply with high- and slow-speed drilling, whose patient distributions diverged from controls (P<0.002), signalling genuine salivary aerosol. Practically: the three-way syringe spreads whatever is in the DUWL water into the air, so its cross-infection risk is a **water-quality problem** — which ties it directly to the syringe-water contamination data.

## Key Contributions

1. **Quantified the three-way syringe as an aerosol source** in patients: air+water 75.3%, air 24.8%, water-only 0% of use time.
2. **Source attribution by size-distribution fingerprinting**: syringe aerosol is instrument-derived (non-salivary); only high/slow drilling showed salivary aerosol.
3. **Reframed AGP as a continuum**, not a binary AGP/non-AGP label.
4. Confirmed 9 of 15 procedures (including 3-in-1 water only) produced no aerosol above background.

## Methodology

- TSI 3321 aerodynamic particle sizer (0.5–20 μm); 3D-printed funnel 22 cm from nasion at 45° (11 o'clock).
- Each patient: 3-in-1 air 30 s, water 30 s, air+water 30 s to all teeth; high-volume aspiration 300 L/min (60 L/min Yankauer for oral surgery).
- Triplicate phantom-head controls; size distributions compared by mode number, width log(σ), peak Dp,c via unpaired t-test, Bonferroni p<0.002.

## Results

| Procedure | % procedure time aerosol above background |
|---|---|
| 3-in-1 **water only** | 0 (none detected) |
| Ultrasonic scaling | 12.7% |
| 3-in-1 **air only** | 24.8% |
| High-speed drilling | 40.1% |
| Slow-speed drilling | 49.9% |
| Surgical drilling | 55.6% |
| **3-in-1 air + water** | **75.3%** (abstract cites 42.9% overall) |

- High-speed drilling produced the most aerosol (median 118.38 /cm³).
- Instrument-matched (non-salivary): ultrasonic scaling, 3-in-1 air/air+water, surgical drilling.
- Salivary (unexplained) aerosol: high- and slow-speed drilling (P<0.002).

## Clinical Takeaways

- The **three-way syringe in air+water mode is a bona fide aerosol generator** — treat it as an AGP for PPE/ventilation purposes.
- Because that aerosol is **waterline-derived**, the mitigation is upstream: keep DUWL/syringe water clean (see [[infection-control/dang-2022-assessment-microbiota-diversity-dental-unit]], [[infection-control/ji-2018-three-key-factors-influencing-bacterial]]).
- High-volume evacuation and adequate ventilation remain relevant; water-only bursts are the low-aerosol option when air is not needed.
