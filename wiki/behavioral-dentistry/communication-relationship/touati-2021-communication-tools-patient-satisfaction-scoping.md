## 한줄요약
스코핑 리뷰(10편, 2000–2020): 심미 치과 커뮤니케이션 도구(구두·시각·디지털)는 모두 환자 만족·치료 수락률·SDM 품질에 긍정적; 표준화 도구 부재가 비교 연구를 막음.

## Three-line Summary
Scoping review of 10 studies (6,678 screened) on communication tools in esthetic dentistry from 2000–2020. All 10 studies found their chosen tools — verbal, visual (wax-up/mock-up), or digital (2D/3D smile simulation, augmented reality) — positively impacted at least one of: patient satisfaction, treatment acceptance, SDM quality, information retention, or care quality. No standardized SDM communication tool exists in dentistry, limiting cross-study comparison.

## 세줄요약
심미 치과 커뮤니케이션 도구 스코핑 리뷰(6,678편 중 10편 포함, 2000–2020). 구두·시각(납형/목업)·디지털(2D/3D 시뮬레이션, AR) 도구 모두 환자 만족·치료 수락·SDM 품질·정보 보유 중 ≥1개에 긍정적 효과. 치과 특화 표준 SDM 도구가 없어 연구 간 비교가 불가능.

## Summary
Touati et al. (2021) conducted a scoping review following PRISMA-ScR guidelines to assess whether communication tools used in esthetic dentistry — verbal, visual, or digital — affect patient satisfaction. From 6,678 records, 10 clinical studies (n range: 26–250) were included covering prosthodontics, orthodontics, and orthognathic surgery. All 10 studies found communication tools positively influenced at least one outcome: patient satisfaction, dentist-patient relationship, information retention, treatment acceptance, quality of care, or treatment outcome. Digital tools (2D/3D smile simulation, AR) enabled pre-treatment expectation alignment. The key clinical implication is that additional communication beyond verbal explanation — especially visual or digital — consistently improves patient experience and acceptance. The main gap: no validated, standardized SDM tool exists for dentistry, preventing systematic implementation and research comparison.

## Key Contributions
- Established across 10 studies that communication tools in esthetic dentistry universally improve ≥1 patient outcome
- Categorized tool types: verbal (all), written (some), visual mock-up (some), digital 2D/3D simulation (emerging)
- Linked communication quality to treatment acceptance — expectation alignment before treatment reduces post-treatment dissatisfaction
- Identified critical gap: no standardized patient decision aid (PDA) for SDM in dentistry
- Provided framework for developing and evaluating future SDM tools in dental practice

## Methodology
Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR). Databases: Medline, Embase, Cochrane, Web of Science, 2000–2020. Inclusion: clinical studies, esthetic dentistry, patient communication tools, patient satisfaction, English, ≥10 participants. 6,678 records → 53 full-texts → 10 included. Data extracted in 13 categories per study. Studies classified by satisfaction assessment method (structured vs. semi-structured questionnaire).

## Results
10/10 included studies demonstrated positive effect of communication tools. Outcome domains improved across studies: patient satisfaction (all), patient-dentist relationship (majority), information retention, treatment acceptance, quality of care, treatment outcome. Digital tools showed the most consistent benefit for pre-treatment expectation alignment. Specialties: prosthodontics (3), orthodontics (3), orthognathic surgery (2), mixed (2). No study reported negative consequences of enhanced communication.
