## Three-line Summary
Prospective single-arm clinical study (n=80) compared conventional verbal consultation alone vs. DSD-assisted consultation in esthetic dentistry. All Likert-scale domains (understanding of procedures, duration, cost, limitations; confidence in outcome and dentist) improved significantly (p<0.001) after digital smile simulation. Treatment acceptance rate was 85%; 95% felt communication improved; 98.8% would recommend DSD consultation.

## 세줄요약
전향적 단일군 연구(n=80): 구두 설명 후 DSD 시뮬레이션을 추가했을 때 모든 이해도·자신감 도메인이 유의하게 상승(p<0.001). 결과 시각화 점수 상승폭이 가장 컸다(2.5→4.8). 치료 수락률 85%, 전체 만족 96.3%, 추천 의향 98.8%.

## Summary
Qadiri et al. (2026) evaluated whether Digital Smile Design (DSD) adds clinical value beyond standard verbal consultation in esthetic dental practice at Najran University, Saudi Arabia. Eighty patients seeking esthetic treatment received a conventional consultation followed by DSD simulation (Smile Designer Pro software). Pre- and post-DSD assessments using Likert scales revealed significant improvement in all dimensions: clarity of procedures (2.8→4.5), understanding of cost (3.0→4.2), confidence in expected result (3.0→4.6), and ability to visualize outcome (2.5→4.8, all p<0.001). Eighty-five percent of patients accepted the proposed treatment following DSD. The study establishes that visualization tools that allow patients to see their expected result — rather than imagine it — meaningfully raise both understanding and willingness to proceed.

## Key Contributions
- Highest treatment acceptance metric in esthetic dentistry consultation literature: 85% acceptance with DSD
- "Ability to visualize final result" showed largest gain (2.5→4.8), suggesting this is the key mechanism
- Understanding of treatment cost improved significantly (3.0→4.2) — directly relevant to 비보험 치료 상담
- 88.8% felt more involved in treatment planning — confirms SDM-enabling role of DSD
- All 5 VAS satisfaction domains (communication, understanding, confidence, excitement, involvement) rated positively by >88% of participants

## Methodology
Prospective single-arm pre-post study. n=80 (age 18–60; 67.5% female; 52.5% aged 31–50). Setting: Restorative Dentistry Dept, Najran University, Saudi Arabia. Intervention: conventional consultation → Smile Designer Pro DSD simulation → re-assessment. Instruments: 5-point Likert (understanding, confidence) and 10-point VAS (satisfaction). Statistics: paired t-test, Cohen's d; p<0.05 threshold. Sample size calculated by G*Power (d=0.4, α=0.05, power=80%).

## Results
Understanding overall: 2.9±0.9 → 4.4±0.6 (p<0.001). Confidence in expected smile: 3.0±1.1 → 4.6±0.5 (p<0.001). Certainty to proceed: 3.2±1.2 → 4.4±0.7 (p<0.001). Ability to visualize: 2.5±1.0 → 4.8±0.3 (p<0.001). Treatment acceptance: 85% (68/80). Overall satisfaction: 96.3%. Would recommend DSD consultation: 98.8%. Reasons for non-acceptance (15%): not reported in detail by study.
