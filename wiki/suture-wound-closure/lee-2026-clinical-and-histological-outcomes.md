## Three-line Summary
Pilot split-mouth RCT (n=19 participants, 2 palatal donor sites each) randomized to allogenic amnion-chorion membrane (ACM, test) vs collagen dressing (CLD, control) after free gingival graft (FGG) harvest, evaluated clinically and histologically at 4, 10, 14, 21, 28, 45, and 60 days post-op.
No significant differences were found between ACM and CLD in wound area reduction, epithelialization, color change, pain, sensibility, epithelium/keratin thickness, inflammatory cell/fibroblast/blood vessel counts, or collagen density at any time point.
ACM was comparable, not superior, to collagen dressing for palatal FGG donor site healing — both are equally viable dressing options; registered at ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT03713073).

## Summary
This pilot split-mouth randomized controlled trial enrolled 19 participants requiring free gingival graft (FGG) procedures, using their paired right and left palatal donor sites to compare an allogenic amnion-chorion membrane (ACM) against a standard collagen dressing (CLD) for donor-site wound protection. Clinical outcomes (wound area, epithelialization, color match, bleeding, pain, sensibility) and histomorphometric measures from biopsies (epithelium/keratin thickness, inflammatory cells, fibroblasts, blood vessels, collagen density) were assessed at seven timepoints from day 4 to day 60 post-operatively. No statistically significant differences emerged between the two dressings on any clinical or histological measure at any timepoint. The authors conclude ACM is comparable, not superior, to collagen dressing — a null/equivalence-leaning result rather than evidence of ACM's superiority as a biologic dressing. As a pilot trial (n=19), the study is likely underpowered to detect small-to-moderate between-group differences, and the retrieved text lacks numeric tables and full methodology detail (abstract-only ingestion).

## Key Contributions
- Split-mouth (within-patient) design directly pitting a biologic placental-membrane dressing (ACM) against the collagen-dressing standard of care at the palatal FGG donor site
- Joint clinical + histomorphometric evaluation (biopsy at baseline and day 10) across a 60-day observation window
- Registered trial (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03713073) with no reported conflicts of interest

## Methodology
Pilot, split-mouth RCT; 19 participants, 38 palatal donor sites (one ACM, one CLD per patient, randomized). Outcomes: wound area size, epithelialization, color match, bleeding/hemostasis, pain, sensibility (clinical) and epithelium/keratin thickness, inflammatory cell/fibroblast/blood vessel counts, collagen density (histomorphometric, from day-0 and day-10 biopsies). Evaluated at days 4, 10, 14, 21, 28, 45, and 60 post-op.
