## Three-line Summary
In-vivo fluorescein angiography study in 4 healthy adult mongrel dogs: buccal and lingual full-thickness envelope flaps were reflected in mandibular quadrants (intrasulcular incisions, first premolar to first molar), immediately readapted, and closed with horizontal mattress sutures in one quadrant vs direct interrupted sutures in the contralateral quadrant.
The simple act of raising the mucoperiosteal envelope flap initiated significant vascular trauma — flap-circulation reductions vs presurgical baseline were statistically significant for at least 3 days and persisted 7 days at interproximal sites, while extravascular dye diffusion recovered sooner and covered a significantly greater flap area than intracapillary circulation early in healing.
Clinical implication: even an uncomplicated envelope flap leaves a perfusion-compromised healing window over the first postoperative week; abstract-only record (truncated at 250 words — the mattress-vs-interrupted comparison results were cut off), dog model, n=4.

## 세줄요약
건강한 성인 잡종견(mongrel dog) 4마리의 하악 사분면에서 협측·설측 전층 봉투판 (Full-thickness Envelope Flap) 을 거상 후 즉시 복원하고, 수평 매트리스 봉합 (Horizontal Mattress Suture) 과 단순결절봉합 (Direct Interrupted Suture) 을 대향 사분면에 각각 적용하여 형광혈관조영술 (Fluorescein Angiography) 로 술전 및 술후 1·3·7·10·14·21일 치은 혈류를 측정한 생체내 동물실험.
점막골막판 (Mucoperiosteal Flap) 거상만으로도 유의한 혈관 손상이 시작되어 판막 혈류가 술전 기준치 대비 유의하게 감소 — 최소 3일, 치간 (interproximal) 부위에서는 7일까지 지속; 혈관외 확산 (extravascular diffusion) 은 모세관내 순환보다 빨리 회복되어 초기 치유기에 유의하게 더 넓은 판막 면적을 덮음.
임상적 의미: 합병증 없는 봉투판 수술 후에도 첫 술후 주가 관류 저하 창이므로 이 시기의 봉합·판막 취급이 중요; 초록이 250단어에서 절단되어 봉합법 간 비교 결과는 미확보, 개(dog) 모델 n=4.

## Summary
This in-vivo animal study used fluorescein angiography, recorded with a simple photographic system, to measure gingival circulation in 4 healthy adult mongrel dogs before and after mucoperiosteal flap surgery. Full-thickness buccal and lingual envelope flaps were reflected in the mandibular quadrats after intrasulcular incisions from the first premolar to the first molar, immediately readapted, and closed primarily with horizontal mattress sutures in one quadrant and direct interrupted sutures in the contralateral quadrant of each dog; dye fluorescence at three interproximal and two mid-buccal sites per flap was quantified by computerized planimetry on postoperative days 1, 3, 7, 10, 14, and 21. The central finding is that the simple act of raising the flap initiates significant vascular trauma: flap-circulation reductions relative to presurgical baseline were statistically significant for at least 3 days and persisted 7 days at interproximal sites. Extravascular dye diffusion recovered sooner and extended over a significantly greater flap area than intracapillary circulation during early healing. Clinically this frames the first postoperative week as a hypoperfusion window even after an uncomplicated envelope-flap procedure — though the retrieved abstract is truncated at 250 words, so the mattress-vs-interrupted comparison results and all numeric values are unavailable, and the measured endpoint is flap (soft-tissue) circulation rather than alveolar-bone perfusion.

## Key Contributions
- Direct in-vivo demonstration that raising a mucoperiosteal envelope flap — before any grafting, membrane, or releasing incision — initiates significant vascular trauma in the flap.
- Quantified time course of the perfusion deficit: significant flap-circulation reduction lasting ≥3 days overall and 7 days at interproximal sites.
- Separated intracapillary circulation from extravascular dye diffusion as two recoverable signals, showing diffusion recovers sooner and covers a significantly greater flap area early in healing.
- Within-dog contralateral-quadrant design (horizontal mattress vs direct interrupted) controlling for between-animal variability.
- Methodological template: photographic fluorescein angiography + computerized planimetry for longitudinal flap-perfusion measurement.

## Methodology
- **Design**: in-vivo animal experimental study, contralateral-quadrant (within-dog) design, each dog serving as its own control with presurgical baseline.
- **Subjects**: 4 healthy adult mongrel dogs.
- **Surgery**: intrasulcular incisions from mandibular first premolar to first molar; buccal and lingual full-thickness envelope flaps reflected; immediate readaptation; horizontal mattress closure in one quadrant, direct interrupted closure in the contralateral quadrant.
- **Measurement**: fluorescein angiography of in-vivo gingival circulation recorded photographically pre-surgery and on days 1, 3, 7, 10, 14, 21.
- **Sites**: 3 interproximal + 2 mid-buccal sites per flap.
- **Quantification**: computerized planimetry of intracapillary and diffusion extent of dye fluorescence.
- **Statistics**: paired t-test (longitudinal vs baseline); Student t-test (cross-sectional: suture technique; interproximal vs mid-buccal per day).
