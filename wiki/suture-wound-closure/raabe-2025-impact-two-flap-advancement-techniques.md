## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo preclinical study (porcine hemimandible model), 68 GBR procedures on 34 fresh pig hemimandibles, comparing two flap-advancement techniques — modified periosteal releasing incision (MPRI) vs. mucosal detachment technique (MDT) — each with and without periosteal suturing (PS), on graft material displacement during primary wound closure.

Periosteal suturing significantly reduced graft material displacement at the implant-platform level (ΔGMT −14.0% ± 11.1% with PS vs. −33.2% ± 16.9% without, p < 0.001), while flap-advancement technique (MPRI vs. MDT) and soft-tissue phenotype (keratinized mucosa width, flap thickness) had no significant effect on displacement or on flap advancement itself.

Clinicians should not expect the choice between MPRI and a pouch-style MDT to protect graft material during closure — membrane stabilization by periosteal mattress sutures is the variable that matters; limitation: ex vivo pig-mandible model without postoperative loading, swelling, or healing dynamics.

## 세줄요약

돼지 반측하악골(hemimandible) 모델을 이용한 사체(ex vivo) 전임상 연구로, 총 34개 반측하악골에 68건의 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)을 시행해 두 피판전진술식 — 변형 골막이완절개 (Modified Periosteal Releasing Incision, MPRI) vs 점막박리술식 (Mucosal Detachment Technique, MDT) — 을 골막봉합 (Periosteal Suturing, PS) 유무와 함께 비교했다.

골막봉합은 임플란트 플랫폼 수준에서 이식재 변위를 유의하게 감소시켰으나(ΔGMT −14.0%±11.1%(PS) vs −33.2%±16.9%(무PS), p<0.001), 피판전진 술식(MPRI vs MDT)과 연조직 표현형(각화점막폭·피판두께)은 이식재 변위나 피판전진량 자체에 유의한 영향을 미치지 않았다.

임상적으로는 MPRI와 파우치형 MDT 중 어느 술식을 택하든 이식재 보호 효과 차이는 기대하기 어렵고, 골막 매트리스 봉합을 통한 막 안정화가 관건이다; 한계는 술후 부하·부종·치유 과정이 없는 돼지 사체 모델이라는 점.

## Summary

This ex vivo preclinical study (34 fresh pig hemimandibles, 68 GBR procedures, randomized 2×2 design) isolated two independent variables affecting graft material displacement during primary wound closure in guided bone regeneration: the flap-advancement **technique** used to achieve tension-free closure (modified periosteal releasing incision, MPRI, vs. mucosal detachment technique/periosteal pouch flap, MDT), and periosteal suturing (PS) for membrane stabilization. CBCT-measured graft material thickness (GMT) was tracked at 7 levels from the implant platform (L0) to 6 mm apical (L6), before and after primary closure standardized to a 0.1 N flap-tension threshold. PS significantly reduced graft displacement at the crestal levels (L0–L2) regardless of flap technique, while flap-advancement technique and soft-tissue phenotype (keratinized mucosa width, flap thickness) had no measurable effect on either graft stability or the amount of advancement achieved. Both MPRI and MDT achieved statistically equivalent flap advancement (~8.3 mm) sufficient for primary closure.

## Key Contributions

- Isolates flap-advancement **technique** (not just advancement magnitude) and periosteal suturing (PS) as two independently randomized variables affecting intraoperative graft displacement — the first study to test both together.
- PS significantly reduces graft displacement at the crestal/implant-platform level (L0–L2) independent of which flap-advancement technique is used — no significant MPRI-PS vs. MDT-PS or MPRI+PS vs. MDT+PS interaction at any level.
- Counters the assumption that pouch-style flaps (MDT) better contain graft material than a full-thickness flap with periosteal releasing incision (MPRI): both achieved equivalent advancement (~8.3 mm) and equivalent graft protection.
- Confirms soft-tissue phenotype (KMW, flap thickness) does not predict flap advancement achieved or graft displacement magnitude in this model.
- Establishes a standardized, clinically motivated 0.1 N flap-tension threshold (spring dynamometer) as the operational definition of adequate primary-closure advancement.

## Methodology

Randomized 2×2 factorial ex vivo design: 34 fresh pig hemimandibles (30-week-old, from a local butcher), 68 total GBR procedures at standardized 2-walled box defects (8 × 6 × 3 mm) at the second-premolar site, split into 4 groups of 17 (MDT+PS, MDT−PS, MPRI+PS, MPRI−PS). A dental implant (SPI Element 4.0 × 11 mm, Thommen Medical) was placed centrally per defect. Sites were augmented with particulate DBBM (Bio-Oss) overcontoured 2 mm and covered with a native collagen membrane (Bio-Gide); PS sites additionally received absorbable vertical mattress sutures (Novosyn 5-0) connecting the periosteum to the lingual flap. Flap advancement was standardized to a ≤ 0.1 N closure tension (spring dynamometer). CBCT (before/after primary closure) measured graft material thickness (GMT) at 7 apico-crestal levels (L0–L6, 1 mm increments from the implant platform); ΔGMT (mm and %) was computed. Keratinized mucosa width and flap thickness (3/6/9 mm) were recorded pre-surgically. Intra-examiner reliability (ICC) = 0.94. Statistics: Kruskal–Wallis, Wilcoxon–Mann–Whitney, Spearman correlation.

## Results

**Flap advancement**: MPRI 8.3 ± 1.1 mm vs. MDT 8.3 ± 1.5 mm (no significant difference).

| Comparison | Level | ΔGMT | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| MPRI vs. MDT (technique) | L0 | −24.3% ± 13.7% vs. −23.0% ± 14.3% | ≥ 0.085 (all levels L0–L6, n.s.) |
| No PS vs. PS (membrane stabilization) | L0 | −33.2% ± 16.9% vs. −14.0% ± 11.1% | < 0.001 |
| No PS vs. PS | L1 | −21.3% ± 14.3% vs. −8.5% ± 7.0% | < 0.001 |
| No PS vs. PS | L2 | −11.5% ± 11.0% vs. −5.4% ± 8.9% | 0.041 |
| No PS vs. PS | L3–L6 | — | ≥ 0.12 (n.s.) |
| MPRI−PS vs. MPRI+PS | L0 | −35.1% ± 14.9% vs. −13.5% ± 12.4% | < 0.001 |
| MDT−PS vs. MDT+PS | L0 | −31.4% ± 18.8% vs. −14.6% ± 9.9% | 0.020 |
| MPRI−PS vs. MDT−PS / MPRI+PS vs. MDT+PS | all levels | — | ≥ 0.267 / ≥ 0.54 (n.s. — PS effect is technique-independent) |

**Phenotype**: KMW 6.8 ± 0.9 mm; FT3/FT6/FT9 = 1.6 ± 0.5 / 1.0 ± 0.6 / 0.8 ± 0.3 mm. No significant association with ΔGMT (p ≥ 0.238) or FA (p ≥ 0.264).

**Bottom line**: PS matters for graft stability at the crest; flap-advancement technique choice (MPRI vs. MDT) and soft-tissue phenotype do not.
