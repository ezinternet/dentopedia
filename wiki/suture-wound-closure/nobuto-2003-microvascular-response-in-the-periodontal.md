## Three-line Summary

Experimental mucoperiosteal flap surgery in adult beagle dogs (India ink vascular injection at postoperative days 5, 7, 14, 21, 28, and 42; light microscopy plus scanning electron microscopy of acrylic-resin vascular corrosion casts) tracking the periodontal ligament (PDL) vascular response after flap elevation.

From day 5 the PDL vascular plexus formed new vessels toward the bone and root sides, with resorption of the alveolar bone proper initiating primarily around Volkmann's canal openings; judged by new-vessel quantity and bone resorption the PDL space was widest around day 14, after which the complicated vascular architecture gradually organized into a mesh structure from day 21, osteogenesis encircled the new vessels, and the alveolar bone proper recovered a flat morphology.

Clinical implication: PDL angiogenesis and the subsequent alveolar bone resorption may temporarily reduce tooth-supporting function and cause postoperative mobility — beagle-dog model, ingested from the PubMed abstract only.

## 세줄요약

성견 비글 실험 연구 — 점막골막판 (Mucoperiosteal Flap) 박리 수술 후 치주인대 (Periodontal Ligament, PDL) 혈관총의 재혈관화 과정을 술 후 5·7·14·21·28·42일에 인도잉크 (India ink) 혈관주입 광학현미경 관찰과 아크릴수지 혈관부식주조 (vascular corrosion cast) 주사전자현미경 (Scanning Electron Microscope, SEM) 관찰로 추적함.

술 후 5일차부터 PDL 혈관총이 골측·치근측으로 신생혈관을 형성하며 볼크만관 (Volkmann's Canal) 개구부 주변에서 치조골 고유층 (alveolar bone proper) 흡수가 시작되었고, 신생혈관량·골흡수 기준 PDL 강 폭경이 가장 넓은 시점은 술 후 14일차이며 21일차부터 혈관이 망상구조로 재조직화되고 골형성이 신생혈관을 둘러싸며 치조골 고유층이 평탄한 형태로 회복됨.

점막골막판 박리 시 치은-골막 혈관총 (gingivo-periosteal vascular plexus)과 PDL 혈관총이 치조골을 관통하는 순환로와 단절되지만 PDL 고유의 미세순환계가 혈관신생 (angiogenesis)으로 능동적 치유를 주도함 — 다만 동반되는 일과성 골흡수로 치아 지지기능이 일시적으로 저하되어 술후 치아동요 (tooth mobility)가 초래될 수 있다고 제안; 비글 동물모델이며 초록만 확보한 한계.

## Summary

This beagle-dog experimental study examined what mucoperiosteal flap elevation does to the periodontal ligament (PDL) vasculature: at elevation, the gingivo-periosteal vascular plexus and the PDL vascular plexus are severed from the circulatory tracts that pass through alveolar bone. India-ink-injected histological specimens at postoperative days 5, 7, 14, 21, 28, and 42 (light microscopy) and acrylic-resin vascular corrosion casts (scanning electron microscopy) were used to trace the restoration of this circulation. The PDL plexus responded with new-vessel formation toward both the bone and root sides from day 5, accompanied by resorption of the alveolar bone proper that initiated primarily around the Volkmann's canal openings — and judged from the quantity of new vessels and bone resorption, the PDL space was widest on day 14. From day 21 the complicated vascular architecture gradually organized into a mesh structure, osteogenesis encircled the newly formed vessels, and the alveolar bone proper recovered to a flat morphology. The authors conclude that active wound healing after flap elevation is driven by angiogenesis from the PDL, which possesses its own microcirculatory system — but that this angiogenesis and the subsequent alveolar bone resorption might temporarily reduce tooth-supporting function and cause postoperative mobility.

## Key Contributions

- Documents the vascular severance event underlying flap morbidity: elevation of the mucoperiosteal flap severs the connection of the gingivo-periosteal vascular plexus and the PDL vascular plexus with the circulatory tracts that pass through alveolar bone.
- Provides the time course of PDL-side restoration of circulation: new vessels toward the bone and root sides from day 5; a complicated vascular architecture during the day 7–14 resorption phase; gradual organization into a mesh structure from day 21.
- Links the vascular response to bone remodeling and clinical function: bone resorption of the alveolar bone proper initiates around the Volkmann's canal openings, the PDL space is judged widest around day 14, and later osteogenesis encircles the new vessels and restores a flat alveolar bone proper — implying a transiently reduced tooth-supporting function and postoperative mobility.

## Methodology

- **Design**: experimental animal study — mucoperiosteal flap surgery in adult beagle dogs (evidence level: animal).
- **n**: adult beagle dogs — exact count not stated in the retrieved abstract.
- **Observation time points**: postoperative days 5, 7, 14, 21, 28, 42.
- **Methods**: histological specimens prepared after injecting India ink into the blood vessels, examined under a light microscope; vascular corrosion cast specimens of the PDL (acrylic resin injection) observed by scanning electron microscopy (SEM).
- **Outcomes**: PDL vascular plexus restoration (new-vessel formation, vascular architecture) and alveolar bone remodeling (resorption onset, osteogenesis, PDL space width).

## Results

| Postoperative interval | PDL vascular response | Alveolar bone response |
|---|---|---|
| Day 5 | New blood vessels formed toward the bone side and root side | Resorption of the alveolar bone proper initiated, primarily around the opening of the Volkmann's canal |
| Days 7–14 | New-vessel formation on the bone and root sides; complicated vascular architecture | Bone resorption of the alveolus accompanied the vessel formation; PDL space width judged greatest on day 14 (from quantity of new vessels and bone resorption) |
| Days 21–42 | Vascular architecture gradually organized and transformed into a mesh structure | Osteogenesis initiated and encircled the newly formed vessels; the alveolar bone proper recovered to a flat morphology |

No quantitative vessel counts, PDL-space widths (mm), or statistical tests are reported in the abstract — all findings are qualitative/descriptive histology.
