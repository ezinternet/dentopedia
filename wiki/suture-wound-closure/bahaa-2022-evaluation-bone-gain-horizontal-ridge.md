## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=40 partially edentulous mandible patients, 4 groups of 10; Cairo University, Egypt) comparing four flap-advancement techniques for horizontal titanium-mesh GBR — periosteal releasing incision (PRI), double flap incision (DFI), modified periosteal releasing incision (MPRI), coronally advanced lingual flap (CALF) — on clinical and radiographic bone gain.

CALF exhibited the highest mean clinical bone gain (4.12 ± 1.37 mm) and radiographic bone gain (3.54 ± 1.65 mm); PRI the lowest of both (2.60 ± 1.36 mm clinical, 2.06 ± 1.11 mm radiographic); differences significant across groups (P < 0.001 both outcomes). Flap advancement correlated positively with radiographic bone gain (P = 0.003); mesh exposure area/percentage correlated inversely with clinical bone gain (P = 0.022 / 0.017).

Plain PRI is the weakest of the four techniques tested for bone gain in horizontal Ti-mesh GBR, and CALF the strongest; clinical implication is that flap-advancement technique choice measurably affects regenerative outcome, not just closure success — but abstract-only ingestion means the DFI/MPRI numeric detail is unavailable.

## 세줄요약

RCT (하악 부분무치악 환자 n=40, 4개 군 각 10명; 카이로 대학교, 이집트)로 티타늄 메시 수평 GBR에서 4가지 피판전진술식 — PRI, DFI, MPRI, CALF — 을 임상적·방사선학적 골증대량으로 비교.

CALF군이 임상적 골증대(4.12±1.37mm)·방사선학적 골증대(3.54±1.65mm) 모두 최고, PRI군이 둘 다 최저(임상 2.60±1.36mm, 방사선 2.06±1.11mm); 두 지표 모두 군간 유의차(P<0.001). 피판전진량은 방사선학적 골증대와 양의 상관(P=0.003); 메시노출 면적·비율은 임상적 골증대와 음의 상관(P=0.022/0.017).

수평 티타늄 메시 GBR에서 단순 PRI가 골증대 면에서 4개 기법 중 가장 약하고 CALF가 가장 강함 — 피판전진 기법 선택이 폐쇄 성공뿐 아니라 실제 골재생 결과에도 영향을 미친다는 임상적 함의이나, 초록만 확보되어 DFI·MPRI 구체 수치는 확인 불가.

## Summary

This randomized clinical trial (Cairo University, Egypt) is the only 4-arm head-to-head RCT in the wiki comparing plain periosteal releasing incision (PRI) directly against three named alternative flap-advancement techniques — double flap incision (DFI), modified periosteal releasing incision (MPRI), and coronally advanced lingual flap (CALF) — for horizontal ridge augmentation using titanium mesh, on both clinical and radiographic bone-gain outcomes. Forty patients with a partially edentulous mandible were randomized into four groups of 10. CALF produced the greatest clinical and radiographic bone gain; PRI produced the least; both differences were statistically significant across the four groups. Correlation analysis linked greater flap advancement to greater radiographic bone gain, and greater mesh exposure (area and percentage) to less clinical bone gain — tying the closure-quality literature (mesh exposure) directly to the regenerative outcome. Abstract-only ingestion: the abstract reports the CALF-highest/PRI-lowest extremes with omnibus P-values but does not disclose the specific numeric bone-gain values for the DFI and MPRI groups.

## Key Contributions

- The only 4-arm **head-to-head RCT** in the wiki's flap-advancement cluster testing PRI, DFI, MPRI, and CALF against each other in a single trial on the same bone-gain outcome.
- Establishes a comparative **ranking** for bone gain — CALF highest, PRI lowest — where prior wiki pages tested at most 2 techniques or provided only a taxonomic (non-comparative) framework.
- Links **mesh exposure** (area and percentage) inversely to clinical bone gain and flap advancement positively to radiographic bone gain, connecting closure-quality metrics to the regenerative endpoint rather than treating them as separate literatures.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial (RCT), single-center (Faculty of Dentistry, Cairo University).
- **Population**: 40 patients with a partially edentulous mandible requiring horizontal ridge augmentation with titanium mesh.
- **Groups**: 4 arms, n=10 each, randomly allocated — PRI, DFI, MPRI, CALF.
- **Primary outcome**: clinical bone gain (mm). **Secondary outcome**: radiographic bone gain (mm).
- **Additional analysis**: correlations among clinical/radiographic bone gain, flap advancement, mesh exposure area, mesh exposure percentage, pain, and swelling.

## Glossary

- **PRI (periosteal releasing incision)**: a horizontal incision through the periosteum at the base of a reflected mucoperiosteal flap that releases its inelastic attachment to bone, allowing coronal advancement for tension-free primary closure. Reference/conventional technique; weakest for bone gain in this trial.
- **DFI (double flap incision)**: a flap-advancement technique using two incision layers to advance the flap; compared here as an alternative to plain PRI.
- **MPRI (modified periosteal releasing incision)**: a modification of the conventional PRI technique, tested here as a distinct arm from plain PRI.
- **CALF (coronally advanced lingual flap)**: technique advancing the lingual flap coronally (rather than relying on buccal advancement alone) to achieve primary closure; strongest for bone gain in this trial.
