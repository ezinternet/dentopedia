## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2007) of botulinum toxin type A for dynamic facial wrinkles in the perioral, glabellar and forehead regions.

Response is expected only where wrinkles are caused by hyperfunctional muscles; wrinkles from aging, gravity, photodamage, trauma or scarring are not amenable.

Case selection, dosing and complication management are presented as decisive; the abstract reports no numeric outcomes.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2007) — 입주위·미간·이마 동적 주름에 대한 A형 보툴리눔 독소 사용 검토.

근육 과기능에 의한 주름에서만 반응을 기대할 수 있으며, 노화·중력·광손상·외상·반흔에 의한 주름은 치료 대상이 아님.

증례 선택, 용량, 합병증 관리가 핵심이며 초록에 정량 결과는 없음.

## Summary

This review gives an implant-practice clinician an overview of botulinum toxin for facial macroesthetics. Its most useful claim for case selection is that treatment targets muscle-driven wrinkles only.

## Key Contributions

- Case-selection rule: only muscle-hyperfunction wrinkles respond.
- Consolidated overview of pathogenesis, technique and controversy.

## Methodology

Narrative review; no systematic search reported in the abstract.

## Results

Qualitative; no efficacy or safety figures in the abstract.
