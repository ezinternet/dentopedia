## Three-line Summary

Systematic review without meta-analysis of 10 randomized clinical trials (PROSPERO CRD420251178977; five databases searched to 22 Dec 2025; RoB-2 and GRADE) of intramuscular botulinum toxin type A (BoNT-A) for myogenous temporomandibular disorders (TMD).

In placebo-controlled trials BoNT-A did not consistently beat saline, while trials in refractory myofascial pain reported larger pain reductions (for example about 50% with higher pressure pain threshold); effects were generally described as lasting 8 to 24 weeks, total doses of 40 to 100 U showed no consistent dose-response, and adverse events were mostly mild and transient.

Most trials carried some risk-of-bias concerns and protocols were heterogeneous (pooling judged inappropriate), so BoNT-A is not supported as first-line care and may be considered only in selected refractory cases after failed conservative therapy.

위약 대조 시험에서 BoNT-A는 생리식염수 대비 일관된 우월성을 보이지 못했고, 난치성 근막통 (refractory myofascial pain) 시험에서는 더 큰 통증 감소(약 50% 감소, 압통역치 증가)가 보고됨; 효과는 대체로 8~24주 지속, 총 용량 40~100 U 범위에서 일관된 용량-반응 없음, 이상반응은 대부분 경미·일과성.

## Summary
According to PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13005-026-00637-7), Head Face Med 2026), this PROSPERO-registered systematic review included 10 RCTs of BoNT-A for myogenous TMD and synthesized them qualitatively, with no meta-analysis because of clinical and methodological heterogeneity. Pain fell in both BoNT-A and control arms; in placebo-controlled trials BoNT-A did not consistently beat saline, whereas refractory myofascial pain trials showed larger gains. Effects were generally reported to last 8 to 24 weeks, peaking at about 8 to 16 weeks. The review concludes that BoNT-A is not a first-line treatment and may be considered in selected refractory cases after conservative therapy fails.

## Key Contributions
- Focuses specifically on duration of effect and dosage parameters, which earlier reviews treated only in passing.
- RCT-only evidence base with RoB-2 and outcome-wise GRADE (details in Supplementary Material 2, not retrieved).
- Separates refractory myofascial pain populations (larger BoNT-A benefit) from broader myogenous TMD (no consistent superiority).

## Methodology
Five databases (MEDLINE/PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, LILACS) searched to 22 December 2025; two independent reviewers; 179 records, 155 screened after 24 duplicates, 36 full texts, 10 RCTs included. Population by RDC/TMD or DC/TMD. Primary outcomes pain (VAS/NRS) and time to relapse; secondary MMO, tenderness, adverse events. Risk of bias with RoB-2: most trials "some concerns", none high.

## Results
| Question | Finding |
|---|---|
| Pain vs saline | no consistent BoNT-A superiority in placebo-controlled trials; both arms improved |
| Refractory myofascial pain | greater reduction with BoNT-A (about 50%, higher PPT), maintained up to 6 months (De la Torre Canales 2024) |
| Vs splint, acupuncture, biofeedback | pain reduced in both arms, no consistent differences (Results text) |
| Mandibular function | improved in BoNT-A and control arms; similar to saline |
| Duration | 8 to 24 weeks; peak 8 to 16 weeks, then gradual decline; longer claims only from observational/case reports |
| Dose | 40 to 100 U total; no consistent dose-response; units formulation-specific |
| Adverse events | transient weakness, smile asymmetry, headache, fatigue, reduced EMG activity; no serious or permanent events |

Trials included: Foscaldo 2025 (n=40), Yurttutan 2019 (73), De la Torre Canales 2024 (28), 2021 (54) and 2022 (80), Minston 2024 (45), Sitnikova 2024 (57), Montes-Carmona 2021 (60), Reeve 2024 (75), Kim 2023 (21).

**Source inconsistencies.** The Results text says placebo-controlled trials showed no significant difference "across all included studies", yet the Discussion and Table 2 report BoNT-A superiority over placebo in refractory myofascial pain trials; Yurttutan 2019 is reported as BoNT-A superior to splint alone in Table 2 but as no consistent difference vs conservative therapies in the text. Treat the refractory-subgroup benefit as reported but unpooled.
