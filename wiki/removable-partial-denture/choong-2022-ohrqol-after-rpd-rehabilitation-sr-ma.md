## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (13 eligible studies, 8 pooled; MEDLINE/EMBASE/CENTRAL to March 2022; PROSPERO CRD42022328606) of OHRQoL change before and after RPD rehabilitation in partially edentulous adults, with moderate-to-serious risk of bias across included studies.

OHIP-14 improved at 3 months (222 participants, MD −12.0, 95% CI −16.1 to −7.9) and 6 months (101 participants, MD −10.5) at **very low** certainty, but at 12 months the OHIP-14 estimate was no longer statistically significant (62 participants, MD −12.7, 95% CI −26.1 to 0.6, p = 0.06), while OHIP-49 at 12 months did reach significance (87 participants, MD −34.8) at low certainty.

RPD rehabilitation appears to improve quality of life for up to six months on very low certainty evidence, and the twelve-month picture is inconclusive and instrument-dependent — the benefit should be presented as real but short-horizon and not yet durable-proven.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (적격 13편 중 8편 병합; MEDLINE·EMBASE·CENTRAL 2022년 3월까지; PROSPERO CRD42022328606): 부분무치악 성인에서 가철성 국소의치 (Removable Partial Denture, RPD) 장착 전후의 구강건강관련 삶의 질 (Oral Health-Related Quality of Life, OHRQoL) 변화를 종합했고, 포함 연구의 비뚤림 위험은 중등도~심각 수준이었다.

구강건강영향지수 14문항 (Oral Health Impact Profile-14, OHIP-14)은 3개월 (222명, 평균차 Mean Difference, MD = −12.0)과 6개월 (101명, MD = −10.5)에 개선됐으나 **확실성 등급은 very low**였고, **12개월에는 유의성이 사라졌다** (62명, MD = −12.7, 95% CI −26.1 ~ 0.6, p = 0.06); 반면 OHIP-49는 12개월에 유의했다 (87명, MD = −34.8, low certainty).

RPD는 **6개월까지는** 삶의 질을 개선하는 것으로 보이나 근거 확실성이 very low이고 12개월 시점은 결론이 나지 않았으며 측정도구에 따라 갈린다 — 환자에게는 "좋아지지만 단기 지평이며 장기 지속은 아직 입증 안 됨"으로 설명해야 정확하다.

## Summary

This is the paper that puts a clock on the consent conversation. The clinical habit is to promise that a partial denture will improve how the patient feels; this meta-analysis says that promise is supported for about six months, on very low certainty evidence, and becomes inconclusive at twelve.

The twelve-month null deserves careful reading, because it is easy to misreport in both directions. The point estimate at 12 months (MD −12.7) is **larger** than the significant estimate at 6 months (−10.5). It fails significance because the pooled sample collapses from 222 participants at 3 months to 62 at 12, widening the confidence interval across zero. This is an **underpowered** result, not a demonstrated disappearance of benefit. Saying "quality of life stops improving after a year" over-reads it; saying "the benefit is proven durable" over-reads it the other way. The honest statement is that the evidence runs out.

The instrument dependence is the second useful finding: OHIP-49 remains significant at 12 months where OHIP-14 does not. The longer questionnaire has more items and, here, more participants (87 vs 62) — which again points at power rather than at a real divergence in what patients experience.

For the selection decision this matters because [[removable-partial-denture/walter-2021-shortened-dental-arch-15year-rct]] removed prosthetic survival as a discriminator. When the tie-breaker becomes how the patient feels, this review tells us the tie-breaker itself rests on very low certainty ground.

## Key Contributions

1. **Time-course rather than snapshot**: 3, 6 and 12-month estimates separately pooled.
2. **The 12-month OHIP-14 null** (p = 0.06) — the finding that distinguishes this from the standard summary.
3. **Instrument dependence** documented (OHIP-49 significant where OHIP-14 is not).
4. **Explicit GRADE certainty** attached to each estimate, which most prosthodontic reviews omit.

## Methodology

Prospectively registered systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42022328606) with random-effects meta-analysis. MEDLINE, EMBASE, CENTRAL to 29 March 2022. Adults ≥18 years, OHRQoL measured before and after rehabilitation with RPDs of any type and design. Cochrane risk-of-bias tools; GRADE certainty. Thirteen eligible, eight pooled.

## Results

| Instrument · timepoint | n | MD | 95% CI | p | Certainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OHIP-14, 3 months | 222 | −12.0 | −16.1 to −7.9 | <0.001 | Very low |
| OHIP-14, 6 months | 101 | −10.5 | −16.4 to −4.6 | <0.001 | Very low |
| **OHIP-14, 12 months** | 62 | −12.7 | **−26.1 to 0.6** | **0.06 (NS)** | — |
| OHIP-49, 12 months | 87 | −34.8 | −41.9 to −27.7 | <0.001 | Low |

Lower OHIP scores are better, so negative mean differences denote improvement.

**Limitations.** All estimates very low or low certainty; the authors state there is insufficient evidence on the effect of RPD treatment on OHRQoL. Sample size falls 222 → 101 → 62, making the 12-month null underpowered rather than a demonstrated absence of effect. Before-after designs without comparator cannot separate the prosthesis from regression to the mean. Abstract-only ingest: I², subgroup analyses by design and GRADE downgrade reasons unavailable. "RPDs of any type and design" pools cast frameworks with interim acrylic dentures.
