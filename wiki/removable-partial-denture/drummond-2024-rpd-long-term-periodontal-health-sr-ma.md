## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (46 studies, 4,359 prostheses, 4,072 participants, mean age 60 ± 5.2 years; PRISMA, RoB 2.0 / ROBINS-I / GRADE) asking whether long-term RPD use is deleterious to the remaining dentition.

Five-year prosthesis survival was 95.1% for cast-clasp RPDs and 91.7% for telescopic-crown-retained RPDs with no difference between designs (P = .71); abutment teeth carried a higher 5-year extraction risk (OR = 1.99, 95% CI 1.32–3.01) and nonvital abutments higher still (HR = 2.961, 95% CI 2.023–4.335), while tooth mobility (P = .98) and probing depth (P = .50) were unchanged and only gingival index rose (MD = 0.477).

The prosthesis survives well and the periodontium is largely stable, so the long-term cost of an RPD concentrates in the abutment teeth — especially nonvital ones — reframing case selection around abutment vitality and prognosis rather than around the denture itself.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (46편, 보철 4,359개, 4,072명, 평균 60 ± 5.2세; PRISMA·RoB 2.0·ROBINS-I·GRADE): 장기간의 가철성 국소의치 (Removable Partial Denture, RPD) 사용이 잔존치에 해로운가를 물었다.

5년 보철 생존율은 주조 클라스프 RPD 95.1%, 이중관 (telescopic crown) 유지 RPD 91.7%로 설계 간 차이가 없었고 (P = .71), 지대치는 5년 발치 위험이 유의하게 높았으며 (교차비 Odds Ratio, OR = 1.99, 95% CI 1.32–3.01) 실활 지대치는 더 높았다 (위험비 Hazard Ratio, HR = 2.961, 95% CI 2.023–4.335); 반면 치아 동요도 (P = .98)와 탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) (P = .50)는 변하지 않았고 치은지수 (Gingival Index, GI)만 상승했다 (평균차 Mean Difference, MD = 0.477).

보철은 잘 버티고 치주조직도 대체로 안정적이므로, RPD의 진짜 장기 비용은 **지대치**에 — 특히 실활 지대치에 — 몰려 있다. 케이스 선택의 축을 의치 자체가 아니라 지대치의 생활력과 예후로 옮겨야 한다.

## Summary

This is the quantitative baseline for the RPD selection conversation. Its value is not that it shows RPDs "work" — it is that it separates two things that clinical folklore fuses. The **prosthesis** does well (≈95% at five years, and the retention design barely matters). The **periodontium adjacent to it** is not destroyed either: mobility and probing depth are statistically unchanged, and the only measurable deterioration is gingival inflammation, which is a plaque-control finding rather than an attachment-loss finding.

What does deteriorate is the abutment tooth's survival, at roughly double the odds by five years, and a nonvital abutment nearly triples the hazard. That relocates the entire risk conversation. The question at planning time is not "will this denture damage the mouth" but "which teeth am I about to make abutments, and are they vital and periodontally sound enough to carry that role for a decade."

The paper cannot say whether an alternative treatment would have done better, because it has no comparator arm — for that axis the wiki relies on the randomised SDA-versus-RPD literature.

## Key Contributions

1. **Pools prosthesis survival and abutment/periodontal outcomes in one review**, so benefit and cost are directly comparable.
2. **Design does not drive survival**: cast-clasp vs telescopic-crown differ by 3.4 percentage points at five years and the difference is not significant (P = .71).
3. **Abutment vitality is the modifiable risk multiplier**: HR ≈ 2.96 for nonvital teeth.
4. **Dissociates inflammation from attachment loss**: gingival index rises while mobility and probing depth do not.

## Methodology

PRISMA-guided systematic review. Six databases plus grey literature searched April 2024, no language or follow-up restriction; only clinical studies of long-term RPD use included. Risk of bias by RoB 2.0 and ROBINS-I; certainty by GRADE. Meta-analyses for survival and periodontal health (α = .05). 5,577 records screened → 46 included. Overall risk of bias low to moderate.
