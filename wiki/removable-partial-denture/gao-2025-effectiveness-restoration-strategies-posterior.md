## Summary
This retrospective study from West China Hospital of Stomatology compared two implant restoration strategies in patients missing both first (M1) and second (M2) molars: simultaneous M1+M2 implant placement (Group 1, n=195 regions) versus M1-only restoration applying the shortened dental arch concept (Group 2, n=88 regions). Implant survival (HR 1.66, p=0.567) and complication-free survival (HR 1.21, p=0.678) were statistically equivalent. However, M1-only restoration was significantly more cost-effective (1.7x lower cumulative costs), showed no peri-implantitis cases versus 16.2% in the M1+M2 group over three years, and was a protective factor against marginal bone loss >0.5 mm. Patient-reported chewing ability did not differ significantly between groups for soft or hard foods.

## Key Contributions
- Extends SDA concept to implant-based treatment: M1-only implantation is clinically equivalent to full posterior arch restoration with significantly lower risk and cost
- Peri-implantitis incidence of 0% vs 16.2% (p=0.043) at 3 years is a clinically compelling safety advantage
- Provides cost-effectiveness data: M1-only implant strategy costs 1.7x less (p<0.001), supporting SDA as an economic and biologic choice
- PROMs show no functional disadvantage, aligning with the SDA concept's claim that posterior extension beyond the first molar is not functionally necessary
- Challenges routine M2 implantation in SDA contexts and supports a "less is more" implant philosophy

## Methodology
- **Design:** Retrospective cohort study
- **n:** 247 patients, 283 posterior regions; both M1 and M2 missing
- **Group 1:** M1+M2 simultaneous implant restoration (n=195 regions)
- **Group 2:** M1-only implant restoration (n=88 regions)
- **Outcomes:** Implant survival (Cox regression), complication-free survival, cumulative cost, peri-implant conditions, MBL (radiological), PROMs (chewing, pain, discomfort, food impaction)
- **Follow-up:** ≥3 years
- **Statistical models:** Cox proportional hazards (survival); β-coefficients (cost, MBL)

## Results
- **Implant survival:** HR 1.66 (95% CI 0.29–9.50, p=0.567) — no significant difference
- **Complication-free survival:** HR 1.21 (95% CI 0.49–3.04, p=0.678) — NS
- **Cost:** M1-only 1.7x lower (β=−14.40, p<0.001)
- **Peri-implantitis (3yr):** 0% (M1-only) vs 16.2% (M1+M2), p=0.043
- **MBL >0.5 mm:** M1-only protective (β=0.34, p=0.012)
- **PROMs — chewing:** NS between groups for soft and hard foods
- **Post-surgical pain:** Lower in M1-only group
