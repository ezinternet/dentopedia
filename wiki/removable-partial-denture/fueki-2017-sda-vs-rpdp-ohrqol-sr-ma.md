## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (MEDLINE + Cochrane, up to November 2016) of 2 RCTs + 1 non-RCT (N=516) comparing shortened dental arch (SDA) vs removable partial dental prosthesis (RPDP) or implant-supported fixed partial dental prosthesis (IFPDP) on oral health-related quality of life (OHRQoL) measured by OHIP.

Meta-analysis of 2 RCTs showed no statistically significant OHRQoL difference between SDA and RPDP at 6 months (SWMD=0.24) or 12 months (SWMD=0.40); single non-RCT comparing SDA vs IFPDP was insufficient for a definitive conclusion (SWMD favoured IFPDP slightly at −0.59 at 6mo, −0.67 at 12mo, both NS).

SDA is as feasible as RPDP from an OHRQoL perspective; the SDA vs IFPDP comparison is underpowered and inconclusive — both treatment strategies are clinically acceptable for patients where SDA is indicated.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (MEDLINE + Cochrane, ~2016년 11월); RCT 2편+비RCT 1편(N=516) — 단축치열 (Shortened Dental Arch, SDA) vs 가철성 국소의치 (Removable Partial Dental Prosthesis, RPDP) 또는 임플란트 고정성 국소의치 (Implant-supported Fixed Partial Dental Prosthesis, IFPDP)의 구강건강관련 삶의 질 (Oral Health-related Quality of Life, OHRQoL) 비교.

SDA vs RPDP 메타분석: 6개월 SWMD=0.24(NS), 12개월 SWMD=0.40(NS) — 유의한 차이 없음; SDA vs IFPDP 단일 비RCT만 존재 — 6개월 −0.59(NS), 12개월 −0.67(NS), 결론 불가.

OHRQoL 관점에서 SDA는 RPDP와 동등하게 실행 가능; SDA vs IFPDP 비교는 증거 불충분 — 양 전략 모두 적응증 환자에게 임상적으로 허용 가능.

## Study Overview

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| Design | Systematic review + meta-analysis |
| Search | MEDLINE + Cochrane Central Register; 1980–November 2016; 2 independent reviewers |
| Included studies | 2 RCTs + 1 non-RCT; N = 516 participants total |
| Comparators | SDA vs RPDP; SDA vs IFPDP |
| Outcome | OHRQoL via OHIP (Oral Health Impact Profile) — all studies used OHIP |
| Statistics | Random effects model; standardised weighted mean difference (SWMD) |

## Key Results

### SDA vs RPDP (2 RCTs — pooled meta-analysis)

| Timepoint | SWMD | p | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 months | 0.24 | NS | No significant difference |
| 12 months | 0.40 | NS | No significant difference |

**Positive SWMD = SDA slightly worse; but non-significant — no clinically meaningful difference.**

### SDA vs IFPDP (1 non-RCT only — narrative; not pooled)

| Timepoint | SWMD | p | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 months | −0.59 | NS | IFPDP trend toward better; insufficient evidence |
| 12 months | −0.67 | NS | IFPDP trend toward better; insufficient evidence |

**Negative SWMD = IFPDP slightly better; single small non-RCT — no definitive conclusion.**

## Evidence Gap

This SR was published in 2017 and searched up to November 2016. Notably excluded by timing:
- **McKenna 2018** (PMID 30307966) — 2yr RCT published after search cutoff; would have been the 3rd RCT in SDA vs RPD comparison; its results show SDA significantly superior in elderly patients
- **Walter 2021** (PMID 33713361) — 15yr RCT; not yet available at search time

The IFPDP comparison remains critically underpowered even by 2026 — no high-quality RCT has been published comparing SDA to implant-supported fixed restorations.

## Clinical Implications

- SDA delivers **equivalent OHRQoL to RPDP** at 6–12 months — supports SDA as a simpler, non-removable alternative where indicated.
- **IFPDP comparison unresolved**: the trend toward better OHRQoL with IFPDP is consistent with clinical intuition but cannot be acted on from this evidence base alone.
- This SR establishes the baseline: subsequent RCTs (McKenna 2018) have since extended the evidence to show SDA superiority over RPD in elderly patients — moving the field beyond "equivalent."

## Limitations

- Only 3 studies met inclusion criteria — small evidence base at time of publication
- Maximum follow-up 12 months — no long-term OHRQoL data
- IFPDP comparison severely underpowered (n=1 non-RCT)
- OHRQoL only — no functional outcomes, survival, or cost-effectiveness
