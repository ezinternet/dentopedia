## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRIOR-guided overview of reviews; 466 records → 9 SRs included; AMSTAR-2 quality appraisal; Corrected Covered Area overlap index) synthesizing the evidence on mouthguards for preventing dentofacial injuries in athletes.

Mouthguards significantly reduce dentofacial injuries — particularly the high-consequence avulsions and fractures — with custom-made (lab-fabricated) designs outperforming stock and boil-and-bite on protection and comfort; effects on athletic performance are mixed.

The overall conclusion is limited by methodological variability and low AMSTAR-2 quality across several underlying reviews, but the prevention benefit is consistent enough to support mandatory-use policies and athlete education.

## Summary

Mouthguards are the only intervention that prevents traumatic dental injuries before they occur, making this the prevention arm of the dental-trauma category. This AMSTAR-2-appraised umbrella review synthesizes nine systematic reviews and concludes that mouthguards significantly reduce dentofacial injuries — particularly the high-consequence avulsions and fractures handled elsewhere in this category — with custom-made (lab-fabricated) designs outperforming stock and boil-and-bite on protection and comfort. Effects on athletic performance are mixed. The authors call for mandatory-use policies and athlete education, while flagging that the underlying SR base is methodologically variable and often low quality.

## Key Contributions

- Umbrella-level (top-of-pyramid) prevention statement synthesizing 9 SRs.
- Establishes custom-made > stock / boil-and-bite for protection and comfort.
- Transparently flags low AMSTAR-2 quality across several underlying reviews.

## Methodology

- PRIOR-guided overview-of-reviews; databases: MEDLINE, EMBASE, CINAHL, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane + grey literature.
- 466 records → 9 SRs included.
- Dual-reviewer extraction; AMSTAR 2 (quality); Corrected Covered Area (CCA) overlap index.

## Results

- Mouthguards significantly reduce dentofacial injuries (notably avulsions, fractures).
- Custom-made: superior protection and comfort.
- Athletic-performance effect: mixed; some signal of improved occlusion/neuromuscular alignment.
- Conclusion strength limited by methodological variability and low quality of several SRs.
