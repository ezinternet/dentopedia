## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA 2020) of 19 cross-sectional studies from 10 countries (5,752 participants) assessing awareness and knowledge of tooth avulsion emergency management in dental vs non-dental populations; narrative synthesis only due to high heterogeneity.
Dental professionals/students averaged ~50% correct knowledge; non-dental populations (parents, teachers) averaged 15–20% — willingness to replant 2–37%, milk as medium 2.3–15.2%, <30-min window 14–33%; a 30-min educational session raised Kuwaiti teachers' storage media knowledge from 4% to 86%.
Global knowledge gap is critical among first-responders; targeted educational interventions integrated into school health programs and community campaigns can substantially improve outcomes.

## Summary

This PRISMA 2020-compliant systematic review synthesized 19 cross-sectional studies (5,752 participants, 10 countries) on awareness and knowledge of tooth avulsion emergency management. No meta-analysis was performed due to heterogeneity in populations, outcome definitions, and instruments. Dental professionals and students showed higher theoretical awareness (~50% correct overall), but detailed protocol knowledge — optimal replantation window, ideal storage media, splinting duration — was inconsistent even in trained populations. Non-dental groups (parents, teachers, emergency physicians) averaged 15–20% correct, with willingness to attempt immediate reimplantation ranging from 2% to 37%. Milk was identified as a suitable medium by only 2.3–15.2% of parents. Educational interventions consistently demonstrated substantial gains (4%→86% storage media knowledge after a 30-min session).

## Key Contributions

- Establishes ~50% vs ~15–20% knowledge disparity between dental and non-dental populations globally (10 countries, 5,752 participants)
- Milk as medium: only 2.3–15.2% of parents — most widespread practical first-aid knowledge gap
- Even dental professionals lack detailed protocol knowledge: HBSS identified by 40.1%, splinting 7–14 days by 26.7%
- Brief 30-min educational session can raise storage media knowledge from 4% to 86% (Kuwait teachers)
- Most non-dental populations have no structured education in dental trauma management; this represents a preventable prognosis gap

| Domain | Pre | Post |

### Summary Gap

- **Dental**: ~50% average correct knowledge; main gaps = storage media specifics, splinting duration, immediate replantation protocol
- **Non-dental**: ~15–20% average; main gaps = awareness replantation is possible, correct storage, time urgency
- Preparedness gap is greatest in South Asian countries (India, Pakistan) vs moderate in Middle East
