## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional survey of 444 Korean dentists (45.9% adjusted response rate from a random sample of 1,000 dentists registered with the Korean Dental Association), measuring burnout with the Maslach Burnout Inventory–Human Services Survey (MBI-HSS).

High emotional exhaustion (EE) in 41.2%, high depersonalization (DP) in 55.9%, and low personal accomplishment (PA) in 41.4%; higher burnout was associated with younger age, male gender, absence of occupational calling, and unwillingness to reselect dentistry as a career.

The only Korea-specific primary burnout dataset in this wiki — subscale-cutoff prevalence runs markedly higher than pooled global SR+MA figures, though the two scoring conventions are not directly comparable; occupational calling and career satisfaction are proposed as the actionable management levers.

## Summary

This cross-sectional survey examined burnout among Korean dentists using the Maslach Burnout Inventory–Human Services Survey (MBI-HSS) embedded in a custom-designed and validated questionnaire, mailed to a random sample of 1,000 dentists drawn from the 13,207 registered with the Korean Dental Association (KDA); 444 responses were analyzed (45.9% adjusted response rate). High emotional exhaustion (EE) was found in 41.2% of respondents, high depersonalization (DP) in 55.9%, and low personal accomplishment (PA) in 41.4% — all measured against MBI-HSS subscale cutoffs, a scoring convention distinct from the "3-subscale-positive full syndrome" (~13%) definition used in the wiki's pooled SR+MA references. Bivariate and multivariate analysis linked higher burnout to younger age, male gender, absence of a sense of occupational calling, and unwillingness to reselect dentistry as a career. As the only Korea-specific primary burnout dataset in this wiki, it speaks — for dentists only, at single-study evidence strength — to the East Asian subgroup gap explicitly flagged as unanalyzed in [[professional-wellbeing/long-2023-prevalence-professional-burnout-dentists-systematic]]'s geographic breakdown; its markedly higher subscale rates versus the pooled global figures should be read as a distinct national data point, not a replication of the same statistic.

## Key Contributions
- The only Korea-specific primary burnout dataset in this wiki — every other prevalence figure held here is a pooled multi-country SR+MA estimate
- Subscale-level prevalence using MBI-HSS cutoffs (EE 41.2%, DP 55.9%, reduced PA 41.4%) — substantially higher than the pooled global SR+MA figures (EE 28%, DP 18%, full syndrome 13% in Moro 2022), though the scoring conventions are not directly comparable
- Identifies actionable/modifiable determinants — occupational calling and willingness to reselect dentistry — alongside fixed demographic factors (younger age, male gender)
- National sampling frame (random draw from all 13,207 KDA-registered dentists) gives Korea-wide representativeness, unlike single-clinic convenience samples

## Methodology
- Cross-sectional survey; random sample of 1,000 dentists drawn from 13,207 KDA-registered dentists; 444 analyzed (45.9% adjusted response rate)
- Instrument: MBI-HSS (emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, personal accomplishment subscales) plus sociodemographic/job-related items
- Bivariate analysis followed by multivariate analysis to isolate factors independently associated with each subscale
