## Summary
Revilla-León et al. conducted a PRISMA-compliant SR+MA across five databases, including 34 studies split between virtual cast accuracy (n=17) and restoration fit (n=17). For inlay preparations, Primescan demonstrated the best trueness (12.29 µm, p<0.001), substantially outperforming Omnicam (69.34 µm) and exceeding TRIOS 3 (38.39 µm). Restoration type significantly influenced digital cast accuracy: overall inlay trueness (27.47 µm) was markedly better than onlay trueness (64.15 µm). Despite these virtual cast differences, the impression method (IOS vs conventional) did not significantly affect the marginal discrepancy of fabricated inlay and onlay restorations (p=0.06, though I²=99% limits interpretation). Conversely, internal discrepancy was significantly higher for conventionally fabricated restorations (p<0.001, I²=72%). Insufficient data precluded conclusions for veneer restorations.

## Key Contributions
- Primescan demonstrates the best IOS trueness for inlay preparations (12.29 µm) — a ~3× advantage over TRIOS 3 and ~6× over Omnicam
- Restoration type (inlay > onlay) is a stronger accuracy determinant than IOS brand — consistent with preparation-geometry-dominant finding of sanioglu-2026
- IOS and conventional impressions are equivalent for marginal discrepancy of inlay/onlay restorations — IOS does not compromise marginal fit
- Conventional impressions show higher internal discrepancy — potential advantage of digital workflow for inlay fit
- Veneer restoration accuracy with IOS: insufficient evidence — research gap identified

## Methodology
SR+MA. Five databases (PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane) + manual search. 34 studies: 17 virtual cast accuracy, 17 restoration fit. Quality: Joanna Briggs Institute critical appraisal. Outcomes: (1) trueness/precision of digital casts; (2) marginal and internal discrepancy of fabricated restorations. Meta-analysis on IOS subgroups for inlay trueness; meta-analysis on marginal and internal discrepancy (IOS vs conventional). Random-effects model; heterogeneity via I².

## Results
**Virtual cast trueness by IOS (inlay subgroup):**
- Primescan: **12.29 µm** (p<0.001)
- CS3500: 28.90 µm (p<0.001)
- TRIOS 3: 38.39 µm (p<0.001)
- TRIOS (unspecified): 52.96 µm (p<0.001)
- Omnicam: 69.34 µm (p<0.001)

**Overall virtual cast (pooled):**
- Inlay trueness: **27.47 µm** (p<0.001); precision: 19.88 µm (p<0.001)
- Onlay trueness: **64.15 µm** (p<0.001); precision: 19.69 µm (p<0.001)

**Restoration fit:**
- Marginal discrepancy: NS (p=0.06, I²=99%); high heterogeneity limits conclusion
- Internal discrepancy: significantly higher with conventional method (p<0.001, I²=72%)
