## Three-line Summary

A prospective 10.6-year follow-up (up to 15.4 years) of 30 three-unit zirconia ceramic IRFDPs found a 10-year cumulative survival rate of 89% and success rate of 70.4%, with veneering ceramic chipping (20.1%) as the dominant complication. The modified design used a short retainer-wing bevel within enamel bonded with phosphate monomer resin after air-abrasion. These results position zirconia IRFDPs as a clinically acceptable minimally invasive option for posterior single-tooth replacement.

## Summary

Chaar et al. (2023) report one of the longest prospective follow-up studies for posterior 인레이 유지형 고정성 치과 보철물 (Inlay-Retained Fixed Dental Prosthesis, IRFDP) fabricated from 3몰% 이트리아 안정화 정방정계 지르코니아 (3Y-TZP). Over a mean 10.6 ± 1.5 years (range to 15.4 years), 30 three-unit fixed-fixed IRFDPs placed in 30 patients achieved a 10-year cumulative survival rate of 89% by 카플란-마이어 분석 (Kaplan-Meier Analysis), with 4 total failures.

The modified retainer design incorporated a short retainer-wing bevel preparation confined within enamel at both buccal and oral aspects — a refinement intended to improve retention and distribute stress while preserving tooth structure. 프레임워크 (Framework) zirconia was milled from 3Y-TZP and veneered with 장석 세라믹 (Feldspathic Ceramic) on the pontic. Luting followed 공기 연마 (Air-Abrasion) of intaglio surfaces with an 인산염 모노머 레진 시멘트 (Phosphate Monomer Resin Cement).

The 10-year cumulative success rate (complication-free) was 70.4%, with 18 of 30 IRFDPs free of any complication for up to 15.4 years. The dominant complication was 치핑 (Chipping) of the veneering ceramic (20.1%). Failures included deep secondary caries with loss of retention (n=2), repeated debonding with enamel fractures (n=1), and generalized progressive periodontitis (n=1).

## Key Contributions

- Establishes 89% 10-year survival and 70.4% success rate for a modified zirconia IRFDP design
- Documents up to 15.4 years of complication-free service in 18/30 cases
- Identifies veneering ceramic chipping as the primary clinical liability of this design
- Characterizes the four distinct failure modes and their relative frequencies
- Supports zirconia IRFDP as a minimal-preparation alternative to conventional three-unit fixed partial dentures (FPDs)

## Methodology

**Study design:** Prospective clinical study; annual recall (initial recall at 6–12 months post-placement).

**Sample:** 30 patients, 30 × 3-unit fixed-fixed IRFDPs replacing:
- 7 premolars: 4 maxillary, 3 mandibular
- 23 first molars: 15 maxillary, 8 mandibular

**Prosthetic protocol:**
- Preparation: inlay principles + modified short retainer-wing bevel within enamel (buccal + oral)
- Framework: 3Y-TZP, CAD/CAM milled
- Veneering: feldspathic ceramic on pontic
- Luting: air-abrasion of intaglio → phosphate monomer resin cement

**Analysis:** Kaplan-Meier survival and complication rates; mean observation time 10.6 ± 1.5 years.

## Results

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean follow-up | 10.6 ± 1.5 years |
| Maximum follow-up | 15.4 years |
| 10-year cumulative survival rate | **89%** |
| Total failures | 4 |
| 10-year cumulative success rate | **70.4%** |
| Complication-free cases | 18/30 |
| Veneering ceramic chipping rate | **20.1%** |

**Failure breakdown:**
| Cause | n |
|---|---|
| Deep secondary caries / loss of retention | 2 |
| Repeated debonding with enamel fractures | 1 |
| Generalized progressive periodontitis | 1 |

**Clinical interpretation:** The 89% survival rate is comparable to published data for resin-bonded FDPs and ceramic IRFDPs at 10 years, though the 70.4% success rate reflects a meaningful complication burden driven predominantly by ceramic chipping. The feldspathic veneer on the pontic appears to be the weak link; monolithic zirconia designs may reduce chipping risk in future iterations.
