## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PubMed/Embase/Cochrane) comparing clinical performance of onlays/partial crowns vs full crowns for restoring posterior teeth across published RCTs and prospective studies.

Five-year survival rates were statistically equivalent — onlay/partial crown 93.5% vs full crown 94.2% — while onlays required substantially less tooth structure removal (key minimally invasive advantage), but showed higher fracture complication rates.

Onlays are a viable minimally invasive alternative to full crowns with equivalent 5-year survival; material selection (lithium disilicate or zirconia preferred) and occlusal design are critical to controlling fracture risk.

## 세줄요약

구치부 온레이/부분관 vs 완전관의 임상 성능을 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(Systematic Review+Meta-Analysis, SR+MA) (PubMed/Embase/Cochrane).

5년 생존율 통계적 동등 — 온레이/부분관 93.5% vs 완전관 94.2%; 온레이는 치질 삭제량이 현저히 적은 최소침습(Minimally Invasive Restoration, MIR) 이점을 가지나 파절 합병증 빈도 더 높음.

5년 생존율 동등으로 온레이는 완전관의 유효한 대안; 파절 위험 관리를 위해 재료 선택(리튬 디실리케이트·지르코니아 권장) 및 교합 설계가 중요.

## Summary
SR+MA comparing clinical performance of onlays/partial crowns vs full crowns for restoring posterior teeth.

## Key Contributions
- 5-year survival: onlay/partial crown 93.5% vs full crown 94.2% — no significant difference
- Onlay/partial crowns: substantially less tooth structure removal (key MIR advantage)
- Fracture complications more common in onlays; material and occlusal design critical

## Methodology
PubMed/Embase/Cochrane SR+MA.

## Results
Survival rates equivalent. Onlays offer MIR advantage with equal survival. Higher fracture rate in onlays warrants careful material selection (lithium disilicate or zirconia preferred).
