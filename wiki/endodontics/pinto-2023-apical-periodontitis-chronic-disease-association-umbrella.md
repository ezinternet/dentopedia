## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (AMSTAR-2 quality appraisal) of 9 systematic reviews (2016-2021, k=3-21 primary studies each) evaluating the association between apical periodontitis (AP) and chronic systemic diseases — cardiovascular disease (CVD), diabetes mellitus (DM), HIV, chronic liver disease (CLD), blood disorders, osteoporosis, autoimmune disease.

Positive association found between AP and CVD (moderate evidence; one meta-analysis: RR=1.38 for CVD diagnosis with chronic endodontic infection) and between AP and DM (limited evidence); no association with HIV; positive association with blood disorders, CLD, osteoporosis, and autoimmune disease at moderate evidence quality; included SRs ranged "low" to "high" AMSTAR-2 quality with substantial heterogeneity.

Evidence for all associations remains inconclusive/observational-only — periodontitis and glycemic control are major uncontrolled confounders across the primary literature, and most included SRs did not search grey literature or report primary-study funding sources.

## 세줄요약

AMSTAR-2 기반 우산리뷰(umbrella review) — 근단치주염 (Apical Periodontitis, AP)과 만성전신질환의 연관성을 평가한 체계적 문헌고찰(SR) 9편(2016-2021년, 개별 SR당 원저 3-21편)을 질평가.

심혈관질환 (Cardiovascular Disease, CVD)·당뇨병 (Diabetes Mellitus, DM)과 AP 간 양성 연관성 확인(각각 중등도·제한적 근거수준; 한 메타분석에서 만성근관감염 시 CVD 진단 위험비 RR=1.38), HIV는 연관성 없음, 혈액질환·만성간질환 (Chronic Liver Disease, CLD)·골다공증·자가면역질환은 중등도 근거수준의 양성 연관성.

모든 연관성이 관찰연구 기반의 미확정 상태이며, 치주염과 혈당조절이 주요 미통제 교란변수로 작용 — 향후 잘 설계된 전향적 연구와 염증 바이오마커 평가가 필요함(임상적 한계).

## Summary

This umbrella review — the "endodontic medicine" survey of the field — appraises the methodological quality of every systematic review through September 2022 that evaluated a link between apical periodontitis (AP) and a chronic systemic disease, restricting inclusion to SRs that performed a valid risk-of-bias assessment of their primary studies (a stricter bar than two earlier umbrella reviews on this topic, which admitted SRs without proper RoB tools). Nine SRs met that bar, spanning cardiovascular disease (CVD, n=6 SRs), diabetes mellitus (DM, n=4), and single SRs each on HIV, chronic liver disease (CLD), blood disorders, osteoporosis, and autoimmune disease. Using AMSTAR-2, the authors graded 1 SR "high" quality, 6 "moderate," and 2 "low." The synthesized conclusion: AP shows a positive association with CVD and DM (evidence quality "limited" for both), no association with HIV, and positive associations with blood disorders, CLD, osteoporosis, and autoimmune disease at "moderate" evidence quality — but every underlying primary study is observational, so none of these associations can be read as causal.

## Key Contributions

- Establishes the current state of "endodontic medicine" evidence in one place: 7 chronic disease categories mapped against AP association strength and evidence quality.
- Enforces a stricter inclusion bar (valid RoB assessment required) than prior umbrella reviews on the same topic, excluding SRs that only used OCEBM evidence-level grading (not an RoB tool).
- AMSTAR-2 grading exposes a repeatable methodological gap across the field: most included SRs skipped grey-literature/study-register searches (AMSTAR-2 Item 4) and failed to report primary-study funding sources (Item 10).
- Flags periodontitis and glycemic control as the two most important uncontrolled confounders running through the AP-systemic-disease literature.

## Methodology

- PRISMA-guided umbrella review, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42020189979).
- Seven databases searched (PubMed, VHL, Scopus, Cochrane Library, Embase, Web of Science, Open Grey), no date/language restriction, search current to September 2022.
- Two independent reviewers for screening/extraction, third reviewer for disagreements.
- Included only SRs that (a) evaluated AP-chronic disease association and (b) performed a valid risk-of-bias assessment of primary studies.
- AMSTAR-2 (16-item checklist) used to grade each included SR: high / moderate / low / critically-low quality.

## Results

| Disease | # SRs | Association direction | Evidence quality | Pooled effect (where meta-analyzed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cardiovascular disease (CVD) | 6 | Positive | Moderate | RR=1.38 (95% CI 1.06-1.80) for CVD dx with chronic endodontic infection (Koletsi et al., overall evidence "very low"); 67% higher risk of negative RCT outcome in CVD patients (Aminoshariae et al., moderate certainty); RR=1.20 (95% CI 0.79-1.82) for AP→CVD development (Aminoshariae et al., low certainty) |
| Diabetes mellitus (DM) | 4 | Positive | Limited | Higher prevalence of radiolucent periapical lesions in root-filled teeth of diabetics (Gupta et al.); no association with delayed healing established; Ríos-Osório et al. all-positive but 9/10 studies high RoB |
| HIV | 1 (shared with CVD/DM SR) | No association | — | 0/3 studies positive |
| Blood disorders | 1 | Positive | Moderate | High RoB in the single primary study |
| Chronic liver disease (CLD) | 1 | Positive | Moderate | High RoB in the single primary study |
| Osteoporosis | 1 | Positive | Moderate | High RoB in the single primary study |
| Autoimmune disease (T1DM, RA, IBD) | 1 (7 primary studies) | Positive (mostly non-significant individually) | Moderate | 4/7 studies low RoB, 3/7 moderate RoB |

Overall AMSTAR-2 grading of the 9 included SRs: 1 "high," 6 "moderate," 2 "low." No pooled cross-SR meta-analysis was possible due to heterogeneity in outcomes and methodology.
