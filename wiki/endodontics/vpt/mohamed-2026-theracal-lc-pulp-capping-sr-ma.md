## Summary

Mohamed et al. (BMC Oral Health 2026) synthesized 11 RCTs (2015–2025) comparing TheraCal LC — a light-cured, resin-modified calcium silicate material — against calcium hydroxide, Biodentine, and MTA for IPC and DPC in permanent teeth, with follow-up from 6 to 36 months. TheraCal LC consistently demonstrated comparable clinical success rates and satisfactory dentin bridge formation relative to all comparators, with low pain scores throughout follow-up. Risk of bias was "some concerns" in most studies. This provides medium-term evidence confirming TheraCal LC as a clinically viable alternative, with light-cured handling as a practical advantage over MTA and Biodentine.

## Key Contributions

- Dedicated SR+MA for TheraCal LC across both IPC and DPC in permanent teeth (11 RCTs)
- Confirms clinical equivalence to MTA, Biodentine, and calcium hydroxide at 6–36 months
- Establishes low pain profile and dentin bridge formation as reproducible outcomes
- Identifies light-cured handling as a practical benefit that may favor TheraCal in clinical workflows
- Flags that long-term data (>36 months) remains a gap

## Methodology

SR+MA; PRISMA 2020; databases: PubMed, Scopus, WoS, CENTRAL (up to August 2025). Inclusion: RCTs comparing TheraCal LC vs calcium hydroxide, Biodentine, or MTA in IPC or DPC of permanent teeth, 6–36 month follow-up. 606 records → 11 eligible RCTs. ROB-2 assessment.
