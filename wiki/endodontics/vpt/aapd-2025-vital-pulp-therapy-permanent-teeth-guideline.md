## Three-line Summary

First standalone AAPD 2025 consensus guideline dedicated to vital pulp therapy (VPT) in permanent teeth — covering DPC, partial pulpotomy, and full pulpotomy across mature and immature permanent teeth, based on a systematic literature review.

Expanded indication: VPT may be considered even for symptomatic irreversible pulpitis in select permanent teeth; MTA/Biodentine are first-choice materials for DPC (≥90% 5-year success in mature teeth) and full pulpotomy (80–90% success), avoiding root canal treatment in appropriate cases.

The guideline marks a paradigm shift toward pulp preservation in permanent teeth that previously would have been routinely referred for root canal treatment, contingent on accurate pre-treatment pulpal diagnosis and adequate coronal seal.

## 세줄요약

영구치 VPT 전용 최초 AAPD 2025 합의 가이드라인 — 체계적 문헌 고찰 기반; 성숙·미성숙 영구치의 DPC·부분 치수절단·완전 치수절단 포함.

확대된 적응증: 선택적 영구치에서 증상성 비가역 치수염에도 VPT 고려 가능; DPC(성숙치 5년 성공률 ≥90%)·완전 치수절단(80–90%)의 1차 재료로 MTA/Biodentine 권고.

종전에 근관치료로 의뢰되던 영구치에서 치수 보존으로의 패러다임 전환을 공식화하며, 정확한 술전 치수 진단과 충분한 관상부 밀폐가 전제 조건.

## Summary
First standalone AAPD guideline on vital pulp therapy for permanent teeth: indications, protocols, and outcomes.

## Key Contributions
- First AAPD guideline dedicated to VPT in permanent teeth
- Expanded indication: VPT may be considered for symptomatic irreversible pulpitis in select permanent teeth
- MTA/Biodentine: first-choice materials for DPC and pulpotomy in permanent teeth

## Methodology
Evidence-based guideline; systematic literature review.

## Results
Mature permanent tooth DPC with MTA: 5-year success ≥90%. Full pulpotomy for irreversible pulpitis: 80–90% success. Avoids root canal treatment in select cases.
