## Three-line Summary
Cochrane SR+MA (2018 update, RCTs only) comparing pulp treatment modalities — indirect pulp capping (IPC), direct pulp capping (DPC), pulpotomy, and pulpectomy — for extensive caries in primary teeth across multiple follow-up periods.

MTA pulpotomy achieved approximately 93% 24-month success; formocresol (FC) pulpotomy yielded 85–92% success (acceptable but less biocompatible); IPC without complete caries removal showed ~90% success in reversible pulpitis cases.

MTA is preferred over formocresol for pulpotomy due to superior biocompatibility; IPC is a valid conservative alternative when pulpitis is clearly reversible, avoiding more invasive pulp removal.

## 세줄요약
코크란 체계적 문헌고찰(Cochrane SR+MA)(2018 개정판, RCT만 포함): 유치 광범위 우식에서 간접치수복조(Indirect Pulp Capping, IPC)·직접치수복조(DPC)·치수절단술·치수절제술을 비교.

MTA 치수절단술 24개월 성공률 약 93%; 포름크레솔(Formocresol, FC) 치수절단술 85–92%(허용 범위이나 생체적합성 열위); IPC는 가역성 치수염 선택증례에서 성공률 ~90%.

MTA가 포름크레솔보다 생체적합성이 우수해 치수절단술에 선호되며, 가역성 치수염이 명확한 경우 IPC가 보수적 선택지로 유효하다.

## Summary
Cochrane review comparing pulp treatment modalities (IPC, DPC, pulpotomy, pulpectomy) for extensive caries in primary teeth.

## Key Contributions
- MTA pulpotomy: 24-month success ~93% across studies
- FC pulpotomy: 85–92% success — still acceptable but biocompatible alternatives preferred
- IPC without complete caries removal: ~90% success in selected cases

## Methodology
Cochrane SR+MA; RCT only; 2018 update.

## Results
MTA pulpotomy superior to FC in biocompatibility and comparable in success rates. IPC valid option in reversible pulpitis cases. FC acceptable when alternatives unavailable.
