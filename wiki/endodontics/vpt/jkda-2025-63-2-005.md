## Three-line Summary
Narrative review (Song MJ, NHIS Ilsan Hospital, JKDA 2025) addressing vital pulp therapy (VPT) decision-making in immature permanent teeth, where pulp preservation is essential for continued root development and apex closure.

Traditional Ca(OH)₂ is associated with weak hard tissue formation and unpredictable long-term prognosis; contemporary hydraulic calcium silicate cements (HCSC) — MTA, Biodentine, BioRoot RCS — are recommended based on multiple RCT evidence, with apexogenesis preferred over apexification/REP when viable pulp tissue remains.

This Korean-language review applies the 2025 AAPD guideline framework to the Korean clinical context, highlighting the important distinction between pulp preservation (apexogenesis via VPT) and pulp-absent strategies (apexification, REP).

## 세줄요약
내러티브 리뷰(송민주, 국민건강보험 일산병원, 대한치과의사협회지(JKDA) 2025): 미성숙 영구치에서 치수 보존이 치근 발육(Apexogenesis)과 근단 폐쇄에 필수임을 강조.

전통적인 수산화칼슘(Calcium Hydroxide, Ca(OH)₂)은 경조직 형성 불량 및 장기 예후 불확실; 수경성 칼슘 규산염 시멘트(Hydraulic Calcium Silicate Cement, HCSC) — MTA, Biodentine, BioRoot RCS — 가 다수 RCT를 근거로 권장됨.

AAPD 2025 가이드라인을 한국 임상 환경에 적용하였으며, 생활 치수가 남아 있을 때 치수보존술(Apexogenesis)을 치근단 폐쇄술(Apexification)·재생근관치료(Regenerative Endodontic Procedure, REP)보다 우선 선택하도록 안내.

## Summary
미성숙 영구치에서 vital pulp 보존이 치근 발육·apex closure에 핵심. 전통적 Ca(OH)2의 prognosis 한계를 hydraulic calcium silicate cement (HCSC) 신재료로 극복하는 contemporary approach.

## Treatment Decision

| 임상 상황 | 시술 | 재료 |
|---|---|---|
| Pulpal exposure, no symptoms | Direct pulp capping | MTA, Biodentine |
| Pulpitis (reversible), partial | Partial pulpotomy (Cvek) | MTA, Biodentine |
| Pulpitis 진행, immature | Full pulpotomy | MTA, Biodentine |
| Pulp necrosis, immature | Apexification (단계적) or REP | MTA apical plug |
| Open apex with pulp 생활 | Apexogenesis (VPT) | MTA, Biodentine |

## Materials Comparison
- **Ca(OH)2**: 전통, 저렴, hard tissue formation 약함
- **MTA**: gold standard, dentinal bridge, discoloration risk
- **Biodentine**: 신속 설정, less discoloration
- **BioRoot RCS**: bioactive, root canal sealer 변형

## Strengths and Caveats
