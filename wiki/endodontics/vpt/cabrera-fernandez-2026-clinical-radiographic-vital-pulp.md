## One-line Summary
SR+MA of 9 RCTs: TheraCal LC (RM-CSM) achieves comparable clinical-radiographic success to NRM-CSMs at all timepoints, but dentin bridge formation is significantly lower at 360 days (RR 0.85, 95% CI 0.76–0.95); NRM-CSMs remain the biological gold standard.

## Summary
This systematic review and meta-analysis compared resin-modified calcium silicate-based materials (RM-CSMs), principally TheraCal LC, against conventional non-resin-modified CSMs (NRM-CSMs: Biodentine, ProRoot MTA, iRoot BP Plus, CEM) for vital pulp therapy (VPT) in permanent teeth. Nine RCTs met inclusion criteria. Pooled clinical-radiographic success showed no significant differences at 90, 180, or 360 days. However, dentin bridge formation at 360 days was significantly lower with TheraCal LC (RR 0.85, 0.76–0.95, p=0.006, I²=0%). This is mechanistically explained by leaching of unpolymerized resin monomers (PEGDMA, Bis-GMA), which impair odontoblast differentiation and cause cellular apoptosis. TheraCal PT had only one eligible RCT and could not be meta-analysed. GRADE certainty was rated low, driven by methodological limitations and imprecision.

## Key Contributions
- First class-level SR+MA comparing all RM-CSMs vs NRM-CSMs across the full VPT spectrum (IPC, DPC, pulpotomy)
- Short-term clinical success is equivalent — RM-CSMs are not inferior for symptom control and radiographic healing
- Dentin bridge formation is the discriminating outcome: significantly worse with RM-CSMs at 360 days
- Mechanistic link confirmed: monomer leaching (PEGDMA, Bis-GMA) → ROS ↑ → mitochondrial dysfunction → impaired dentinogenesis
- TheraCal PT data too sparse for meta-analysis; preliminary data suggest still inferior to NRM-CSMs for dentinogenesis despite lower monomer leach vs LC

## Methodology
PRISMA 2020; Medline, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science (inception to October 2025). Population: permanent teeth, deep caries, vital pulp diagnosis (reversible or symptomatic irreversible pulpitis). Intervention: RM-CSMs; comparator: NRM-CSMs. Outcomes: combined clinical-radiographic success (primary) and dentin bridge formation (secondary). Nine RCTs included; random-effects meta-analysis (RevMan 5.4, Mantel-Haenszel RR). Quality: RoB 2 (most "some concerns"; 2 high-risk); GRADE = low.

TheraCal LC vs Biodentine subgroup (360d): RR 0.86 (0.71–1.03) — consistent direction, CI crosses 1. DPC subgroup (360d): RR 0.90 (0.76–1.08) — trend favoring NRM, not significant.
