## Three-line Summary

AAPD 2024 evidence-based consensus guideline on vital pulp therapy (VPT) for primary teeth — superseding the 2017 edition — covering indirect pulp capping (IPC), direct pulp capping (DPC), and pulpotomy indications, materials, and protocols.

MTA is the first-choice pulpotomy material (~93% 24-month clinical/radiographic success); formocresol remains acceptable (85–92%) when biocompatible alternatives are unavailable; for DPC, MTA or Biodentine is preferred over Ca(OH)₂; DPC is not recommended in primary teeth with irreversible pulpitis.

The guideline provides age- and tooth-specific decision pathways that guide which VPT procedure to perform (IPC vs DPC vs pulpotomy) based on clinical and radiographic diagnosis, making material selection contingent on correct pre-operative pulpal diagnosis.

## Summary
AAPD 2024 guideline on vital pulp therapy for primary teeth: IPC, DPC, and pulpotomy indications, materials, and protocols.

## Key Contributions
- IPC: remove near-pulp caries to firm dentin; MTA/Ca(OH)₂/Biodentine acceptable
- DPC: small exposure (<1mm²); MTA or Biodentine preferred over Ca(OH)₂
- Pulpotomy: MTA ≥ formocresol; FC still acceptable but biocompatible alternatives preferred

## Methodology
Evidence-based guideline; systematic review base; 2024 edition (supersedes 2017).

## Results
MTA pulpotomy: 24-month success ~93%. FC pulpotomy still acceptable (85–92%) when MTA unavailable. DPC not recommended for primary teeth with reversible pulpitis only.
