## Three-line Summary
PRISMA systematic review of in vitro studies (IJERPH 2022) comparing plant-derived chelating agents with EDTA for smear-layer removal from instrumented root canals, motivated by interest in more biocompatible alternatives.

Among herbal agents, quixabeira, Morinda citrifolia (noni), oregano extract, and neem consistently outperformed other herbals, but EDTA remained the benchmark standard comparator in most studies.

The evidence base is entirely in vitro and heterogeneous; herbal agents are promising adjuncts rather than established EDTA replacements, and clinical translation requires further study.

## 세줄요약
PRISMA 체계적 문헌고찰(IJERPH 2022) — 생체적합 대안으로 주목받는 식물 유래 킬레이트제와 EDTA의 근관 도말층(Smear Layer) 제거 효과 비교(in vitro 연구 한정).

허브 약제 중 quixabeira, Morinda citrifolia(noni), 오레가노 추출물, 님(neem)이 기타 허브 대비 일관되게 우수했으나, EDTA가 대부분 연구에서 표준 비교 기준(Benchmark)으로 유지됨.

근거가 전부 in vitro이고 이질성이 높아 임상 적용에는 추가 연구가 필요하며, 현재는 EDTA 보조제 수준으로 평가됨.

## Summary
This systematic review evaluated whether plant-derived (herbal) chelating agents can match EDTA for removing the smear layer from instrumented root canals — a question driven by interest in more biocompatible alternatives. Among herbals, quixabeira, Morinda citrifolia (noni), oregano extract, and neem performed better than other herbal agents, but EDTA generally remained the reference standard. The evidence is in vitro and heterogeneous, so herbal agents are promising adjuncts rather than established EDTA replacements.

## Key Contributions
- Ranks herbal chelating alternatives for smear-layer removal.
- Benchmarks them against EDTA.

## Methodology
PRISMA systematic review of in vitro smear-layer-removal studies comparing herbal agents with EDTA.

## Results
Quixabeira, Morinda citrifolia, oregano, and neem best among herbals; EDTA a strong comparator overall.
