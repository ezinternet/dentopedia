## Three-line Summary
RCT-only systematic review (7 RCTs, Frontiers in Oral Health 2022, search to Jan 2021) evaluating irrigating solutions and activation methods used in clinical endodontics, using Cochrane RoB2 quality assessment.

Activation methods (PUI, XP-endo Finisher, EndoVac) consistently outperformed conventional needle irrigation for biofilm reduction; NaOCl vs CHX showed broadly comparable antimicrobial efficacy; combining NaOCl with HEDP or MTAD provided no additional antimicrobial benefit over NaOCl alone.

The evidence base is limited to 7 heterogeneous RCTs, restricting definitive conclusions, but the strong activation advantage over conventional needle irrigation is the most consistent and clinically actionable finding.

## 세줄요약
근관 세정액·활성화법 임상 RCT만 포함한 체계적 고찰(Frontiers in Oral Health 2022; RCT 7편; Cochrane RoB2 평가; 검색 2021년 1월).

활성화 기법(수동초음파세정 Passive Ultrasonic Irrigation, PUI; XP-endo Finisher; EndoVac)이 기존 주사기 세정 대비 바이오필름 감소에서 일관되게 우수; NaOCl vs CHX는 항균력 동등; NaOCl + HEDP/MTAD 병용은 항균력 추가 이점 없음.

RCT 7편으로 근거가 제한적이지만, 활성화 기법의 기존 주사기 대비 우위는 가장 일관되고 임상적으로 활용 가능한 결론.

## Summary
RCT-only systematic review (7 RCTs, search to Jan 2021) on irrigating solutions and activation methods in clinical endodontics. Activation methods consistently outperform conventional needle irrigation (CNI) for biofilm reduction. Combining NaOCl with HEDP or MTAD adds no antimicrobial advantage over NaOCl alone.

## Key Findings

| Comparison | Finding |
|---|---|
| NaOCl + HEDP vs NaOCl alone | **No** antimicrobial advantage |
| NaOCl + MTAD vs NaOCl alone | **No** antimicrobial advantage |
| NaOCl vs CHX | Broadly comparable antimicrobial efficacy |
| CNI vs PUI / XP-endo / EndoVac | **Activation significantly better** for biofilm reduction |

## Activation Methods Included
- **PUI** (Passive Ultrasonic Irrigation): strongest evidence
- **XP-endo Finisher**: effective, thermomechanical activation
- **F-file**: sonic-based
- **EndoVac** (ANP): superior apical delivery

## Design Strength
- RCT-only inclusion → higher internal validity vs mixed-design reviews
- Cochrane RoB2 tool for quality assessment
- Limitation: only 7 RCTs — small evidence base; heterogeneous protocols
