## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review + embedded frequentist network meta-analysis (NMA) of 14 systematic reviews (48 unique deduplicated primary studies, CCA overlap = 13.46%) evaluating guided tissue regeneration (GTR) adjuncts in surgical endodontic treatment.

The combination of expanded polytetrafluoroethylene (e-PTFE) membrane + hydroxyapatite ranked first (SUCRA = 84.4%; OR vs. control = 0.13, 95% CI 0.02-0.98; estimated NNT ≈ 5), followed by CGF + xenogenic Bio-Oss collagen graft (SUCRA = 73.2%) and collagen membrane + bovine-derived hydroxyapatite (SUCRA = 70.8%); combination therapies consistently outranked single-component interventions, and no-GTR control ranked lowest (SUCRA = 12.2%).

Despite the clear ranking hierarchy, only 5/27 underlying effect estimates reached Class III (suggestive) credibility and none reached Class I/II — CINeMA certainty was mostly moderate-to-low, so the ranking is clinically informative but not yet definitive pending head-to-head RCTs.

## 세줄요약

14개 체계적 문헌고찰(원 연구 48편, 중복도(Corrected Covered Area, CCA) 13.46%)을 재추출해 시행한 우산 리뷰(umbrella review) + 빈도주의 네트워크 메타분석(network meta-analysis, NMA)으로, 근관수술(surgical endodontic treatment) 시 유도조직재생(guided tissue regeneration, GTR) 보조재료의 효과를 비교했다.

확장 폴리테트라플루오로에틸렌(e-PTFE) 막 + 수산화인회석(hydroxyapatite, HA) 병용이 1위(SUCRA=84.4%; 대조군 대비 OR=0.13, 95% CI 0.02-0.98; NNT≈5)를 기록했고, 농축성장인자(concentrated growth factor, CGF)+이종골 Bio-Oss 콜라겐(SUCRA=73.2%), 콜라겐막+우골유래 HA(SUCRA=70.8%)가 뒤를 이었으며, 복합요법이 단일요법보다 일관되게 우수했고 무처치 대조군이 최하위(SUCRA=12.2%)였다.

다만 27개 효과추정치 중 Class III(시사적) 근거는 5개뿐이고 Class I·II는 전무했으며 CINeMA 확실성도 대체로 중등도-저등도였으므로, 이 서열은 임상적으로 유용한 참고이나 확정적 지침은 아니며 상위 조합 간 RCT 직접비교가 필요하다.

## Summary

Surgical endodontic treatment (apical/periapical microsurgery) is performed when nonsurgical retreatment fails to resolve periapical pathology, but predictable healing remains difficult in large or through-and-through lesions. GTR — barrier membranes with or without bone grafts — has been borrowed from periodontics to try to bias the healing cascade toward true bone regeneration rather than fibrous repair. Prior systematic reviews on GTR in apical surgery were fragmented and discordant, with differing eligibility criteria, outcome definitions, and imaging modalities, and — critically — substantial overlap in the primary studies they drew from (later confirmed at CCA = 13.46%, "high").

This umbrella review addresses that fragmentation with a three-stage design: (1) identify and appraise all eligible systematic reviews/meta-analyses (14 found, from 61 initial records across PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science to July 2025); (2) quantify overlap among them using the GROOVE/CCA method to justify re-extraction rather than pooling review-level numbers; (3) re-extract all 48 unique deduplicated primary studies and run a fresh frequentist random-effects NMA comparing 16 distinct GTR-related interventions head-to-head (direct + indirect evidence), ranked by SUCRA.

The result is a clean hierarchy: membrane+graft (or membrane+biologic) combinations dominate the top of the ranking, single-component strategies occupy the middle, and no-GTR control performs worst of all 16 nodes. But the umbrella-review credibility layer (a 7-domain Class I-IV framework applied to the 27 underlying effect estimates) and the CINeMA certainty layer applied to the NMA comparisons both flag that most of this evidence is weak/moderate — so the ranking should guide, not dictate, clinical material selection.

## Key Contributions

- First study to combine an umbrella-review overlap correction (CCA/GROOVE) with a primary-study-level NMA specifically for GTR in surgical endodontics, avoiding the double-counting that plagued prior fragmented reviews.
- Produces an actionable 16-node SUCRA hierarchy rather than a series of disconnected pairwise comparisons.
- Explicitly separates "which material ranks highest" (NMA/SUCRA) from "how much should we trust that" (Class I-IV credibility grading + CINeMA certainty) — a methodological discipline often missing from single meta-analyses.
- Quantifies the clinical benefit of the top intervention in absolute terms (NNT ≈ 5 for e-PTFE + hydroxyapatite over no-GTR control, assuming ~70% baseline control success), translating an OR into something directly usable chairside.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Umbrella review (PRISMA 2020) with embedded frequentist random-effects NMA; protocol not prospectively registered.
- **Search**: MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, inception-July 2025; 61 records → 47 duplicates removed → 14 unique records, all 14 retained after full-text review.
- **PICO**: surgical endodontic patients; GTR barrier membranes/biomaterials vs. no-GTR or alternative GTR materials; clinical + radiographic success (symptom absence + periapical healing per Rud and Molven / Gutmann and Harrison / modified PENN CBCT criteria), ≥12-month follow-up for the primary NMA dataset.
- **Overlap correction**: Corrected Covered Area (CCA) via GROOVE tool = 13.46% ("high" overlap) across 14 reviews/48 primary studies → triggered re-extraction at the primary-study level.
- **Credibility grading (umbrella layer)**: 7-domain framework (significance of summary/largest-study estimate, N > 1000, I² > 50%, Deeks' small-study test, 95% prediction interval, excess significance test) → Class I (convincing) to IV (weak), applied to 27 effect estimates.
- **Review-level risk of bias**: ROBIS tool, 4 domains, two independent reviewers + third-reviewer arbitration.
- **NMA**: Stata 17, frequentist random-effects model; ORs with 95% CIs; SUCRA rankograms/radial plots; league table; loop-specific inconsistency + net heat plots; comparison-adjusted funnel plots for publication bias.
- **Certainty of NMA estimates**: CINeMA (GRADE-based), 6 domains (within-study bias via Cochrane RoB 2.0, across-study bias, indirectness, imprecision, heterogeneity, incoherence).

## Results

- 14 systematic reviews → 48 unique deduplicated primary studies (CCA = 13.46%, "high" overlap). 7/14 reviews at high ROBIS risk of bias; 7/14 at low risk.
- Credibility of 27 effect estimates: 0 Class I, 0 Class II, 5 Class III (suggestive), 22 Class IV (weak) — no comparison reached convincing/highly suggestive evidence.
- **SUCRA ranking (16 nodes, top → bottom)**:
  1. e-PTFE + hydroxyapatite — SUCRA 84.4%; OR vs. control 0.13 (95% CI 0.02-0.98); estimated ARD +21% (95% CI +1% to +38%), NNT ≈ 5 (assuming ~70% control baseline success)
  2. CGF + xenogenic Bio-Oss collagen graft — SUCRA 73.2%; OR vs. control 0.26 (95% CI 0.07-0.97); NNT ≈ 6
  3. Collagen membrane + bovine-derived hydroxyapatite — SUCRA 70.8%; OR vs. control 0.33 (95% CI 0.22-0.50); NNT ≈ 7
  4. Hydroxyapatite alone — SUCRA 67.8%
  5. CGF + hydroxyapatite — SUCRA 67.4%; OR vs. control 0.22 (95% CI 0.01-4.02, wide/imprecise)
  - Mid-tier: collagen membranes alone, APCs alone, Bio-Oss/collagen-based bone grafts alone — moderate, variable efficacy, dependent on protocol/patient factors.
  - Bottom tier: calcium sulphate graft, bio-resorbable polylactic acid membrane, collagen membrane (Ossix-plus).
  - Lowest (rank 16): control (no GTR) — SUCRA 12.2%; ~8-fold higher odds of unfavorable outcome vs. e-PTFE + hydroxyapatite (OR 7.72, 95% CI 1.02-58.53).
- Consistency: all loop-specific inconsistency factors < 0.15 (network largely coherent); net funnel plot symmetrical (minimal small-study/publication bias signal).
- CINeMA certainty: only two comparisons rated "high confidence" — collagen membrane + bovine-derived hydroxyapatite vs. control, and collagen membranes vs. e-PTFE membranes. Most other comparisons, including several involving the top-ranked e-PTFE + HA node, were "moderate confidence"; APC/CGF-vs-collagen or platelet-based comparisons scored lower due to imprecision.
