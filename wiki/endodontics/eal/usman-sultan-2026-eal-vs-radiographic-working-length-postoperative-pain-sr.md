## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO-registered) of 4 RCTs (n=414, single-visit root canal treatment, vital teeth across Egypt, Pakistan, India, Turkey) designed with postoperative pain as the primary endpoint for standalone EAL vs standalone radiographic working length determination.

No statistically significant difference was found in postoperative pain incidence, intensity (VAS/verbal), analgesic consumption, or pain resolution time at any observation point from 4 h to 7 days; one small three-arm trial (n=60, symptomatic teeth) showed lower early pain at 6–48 h with a hybrid EAL+radiograph (SLC) method.

Method choice (EAL vs radiograph) can be driven by patient context — radiation sensitivity, complex anatomy, ALARA — without penalizing pain outcomes; very low evidence certainty from all four trials limits generalizability.

## Summary
Systematic review (qualitative synthesis, no meta-analysis) of 4 RCTs comparing **standalone** 전자근단측정기 (Electronic Apex Locator, EAL) vs **standalone** 방사선 (radiographic) 근관장측정 (working length determination, WLD), with **postoperative pain as the primary outcome**. Across 414 patients in single-visit root canal treatment (mostly vital teeth), there was no statistically significant difference in postoperative pain incidence, intensity (VAS or 4-point verbal rating scale), analgesic consumption, or time to pain resolution at any time point from 4 h to 7 days. Pain was low-to-moderate and resolved within 72 h in both groups; no flare-ups or serious adverse events. One small three-arm trial (Saha, n=60, symptomatic irreversible pulpitis) showed significantly lower early pain (6–48 h) with a hybrid 동시근관장조절 (Simultaneous Length Control, SLC) method combining EAL + radiography. Evidence certainty is **very low** (all 4 trials "some concerns" on Cochrane RoB 2.0, mainly randomization/blinding/subjective pain assessment).

## Key Contributions
- First SR restricted to RCTs directly comparing standalone EAL vs standalone radiographic WLD with **postoperative pain as the primary endpoint** — narrows the scope left open by prior SRs (Kaur et al.: accuracy/obturation focus, pain secondary, extracted teeth; Kumar et al.: conflated hybrid + standalone methods).
- Establishes clinical equivalence in postoperative pain → method choice can be driven by patient context (radiation sensitivity such as pregnancy/pediatric, complex anatomy, ALARA, resource availability) without penalizing pain outcomes.
- Surfaces a hybrid (EAL + radiograph, SLC) signal for reduced early pain in symptomatic teeth — hypothesis-generating, needs confirmation.

## Methodology
- PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420251247626.
- Databases: PubMed, Cochrane CENTRAL, ScienceDirect (Jan 2000 – Nov 17, 2025); English, human, peer-reviewed RCTs; no grey literature.
- PICOS: permanent teeth needing RCT; EAL (any generation) vs conventional/digital radiograph; primary outcome postoperative pain (VAS/NRS/verbal scale).
- 368 records → 330 after dedup → 22 full-text → **4 RCTs** (Kara Tuncer & Gerek; Khan; Naeem; Saha).
- Cochrane RoB 2.0 (5 domains). No meta-analysis (heterogeneity in pain scales, tooth types, follow-up).

## Results
| Outcome | Finding | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Postoperative pain (4 h–7 d) | EAL ≈ radiographic, low-to-moderate | No significant difference (all 4 RCTs) |
| Naeem cumulative VAS | EAL 0.96±1.25 vs DRG 0.73±1.37 | p=0.29 (NS) |
| Khan 48 h VAS | EAL 4.27±0.48 vs DRG 4.35±0.39 | NS |
| Kara Tuncer & Gerek 48 h (4-pt) | EAL 1.26±0.31 vs DRG 1.20±0.31 | p>0.05 |
| Hybrid SLC (Saha, n=60, symptomatic) | Lower early pain at 6/24/48/72 h | Significant at select early points (single small trial) |
| Analgesic intake | EAL ≈ radiographic | NS (Naeem p=0.529; Saha NS) |
| Pain resolution time | 24–26 h to ~1.35–3.88 d | NS by method |
| Safety | No flare-ups / serious AEs | — |
| Certainty | Very low (all "some concerns" RoB 2.0) | — |

- n = 414 across 4 RCTs (2014–2024; Egypt/Pakistan/India/Turkey), sample sizes 54–220, ages 14–60, mostly single-rooted (Naeem: multi-rooted molars).
- EAL: third-generation DentaPort ZX, Root ZX, Propex Pixi; radiographic arms digital sensors. All single-visit, rotary NiTi, NaOCl + EDTA, rescue analgesia only.
