## Summary

This in-vitro preliminary study compared the cutting efficiency of three commercial nickel-titanium (NiTi) glide path instruments — PathFile (multi-file system), One G (single-file), and ProGlider (single-file) — using standardized Dentalike artificial teeth. The mesiobuccal root canals of mandibular molars were prepared with each glide path system (or no glide path as control), weighed before and after at 0.1mg resolution, and subsequently shaped with WaveOne files while measuring torque generation. One-way ANOVA with Tukey post-hoc test (α=0.05) was used for statistical analysis.

The ProGlider group demonstrated significantly greater weight reduction (cutting efficiency) compared to other groups (p<0.05). However, no significant differences were found in maximum torque or total stress generation during subsequent WaveOne shaping among any groups, including the no-glide-path control. Micro-CT examination revealed discrepancies in root canal size among the Dentalike specimens, raising concerns about the reliability of this artificial tooth model for standardized testing. The authors caution that geometric design differences among glide path instruments affect cutting efficiency, but the clinical relevance is limited by model standardization issues.

## Key Contributions

- Direct comparison of three commercial glide path NiTi systems (multi-file vs. single-file)
- Simultaneous assessment of cutting efficiency (weight loss) and shaping torque
- Identification of standardization limitations in Dentalike artificial teeth (micro-CT verified)
- Data on single-file (One G, ProGlider) vs. multi-file (PathFile) glide path approaches

## Methodology

- **Design**: In-vitro preliminary study
- **Specimens**: Dentalike artificial teeth (mandibular molar, 2nd mesiobuccal canal)
- **Groups**:
  1. PathFile (multi-file glide path)
  2. One G (single-file glide path)
  3. ProGlider (single-file glide path)
  4. No glide path control (direct WaveOne)
- **Procedure**: Glide path → weight measurement (0.1mg) → WaveOne shaping with torque recording
- **Analysis**: One-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc, significance α=0.05

## Results

| Outcome | PathFile | One G | ProGlider | No Glide Path |
|---------|----------|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Weight reduction (mg) | Intermediate | Intermediate | **Highest (p<0.05)** | Lowest |
| Max torque (WaveOne) | No sig. diff. | No sig. diff. | No sig. diff. | No sig. diff. |
| Total stress (WaveOne) | No sig. diff. | No sig. diff. | No sig. diff. | No sig. diff. |

- ProGlider showed significantly superior cutting efficiency
- Glide path establishment did not significantly reduce shaping torque/stress vs. no glide path
- Dentalike canal size variability confirmed by micro-CT
