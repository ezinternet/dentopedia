## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and frequentist network meta-analysis of in vitro studies (48 trials, 2,724 extracted single-rooted human teeth; 5 databases, Jan 2000–Sep 2023; INPLASY2023100034) comparing vertical fracture resistance of roots filled with six obturating systems and four techniques against unfilled prepared (positive control) and untreated (negative control) roots.

By SUCRA, Resilon/Epiphany ranked highest among systems (80.8%), then GP/iRoot SP (55.5%), GP/MTA-Plus (47.9%), GP/AH-Plus (47.4%), GP/AH-26 (45.9%) and GP/ZOE (12.3%); Resilon/Epiphany beat GP/AH-Plus (SMD 0.77, 95% CI 0.10–1.45) and GP/ZOE (SMD 1.64, 0.47–2.80), the calcium-silicate systems did not differ significantly from the resin systems, and no technique differed significantly from another (single cone SUCRA 70.2%; Thermafil did not beat the unfilled control).

Bench-only, load-to-fracture evidence with 3/48 studies blinding the examiner, 14/48 at medium risk of bias, a loop-specific inconsistency in the technique network and small calcium-silicate arms (72 and 144 teeth); the top-ranked Resilon/Epiphany is off the market, so the data support only a weak pro-single-cone/calcium-silicate lean and cannot be extrapolated to clinical fracture rates.

## 세줄요약

체외 연구의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 빈도론적 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA) — 48개 시험, 발거된 단근 인간 치아 2,724개; 5개 데이터베이스 (2000년 1월–2023년 9월; INPLASY2023100034); 6가지 충전 시스템과 4가지 충전 술식의 수직 치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 저항성을 미충전 형성 근관 (양성 대조)·미처치 치근 (음성 대조)과 비교.

누적순위곡선하면적 (Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking Curve, SUCRA)상 시스템 순위는 Resilon/Epiphany 80.8% > GP/iRoot SP 55.5% > GP/MTA-Plus 47.9% > GP/AH-Plus 47.4% > GP/AH-26 45.9% > GP/ZOE 12.3%; Resilon/Epiphany가 GP/AH-Plus (표준화 평균차 SMD 0.77, 95% CI 0.10–1.45)와 GP/ZOE (SMD 1.64, 0.47–2.80)보다 우수, 칼슘실리케이트계와 레진계 간 유의차 없음, 술식 간 유의차 없음 (단일 콘 술식 SUCRA 70.2%; Thermafil은 미충전 대조를 넘지 못함).

체외 파절하중 실험만으로 구성되고 (검사자 눈가림 3/48편, 중간 편향위험 14/48편), 술식 네트워크에 loop 불일치가 있으며 칼슘실리케이트군 표본이 작고 (72·144개), 1위 Resilon/Epiphany는 시판 중단 — 단일 콘·칼슘실리케이트계로의 약한 경향만 시사하며 임상 파절률로 외삽할 수 없다.

## Summary
This systematic review and frequentist network meta-analysis (China Medical University, BMC Oral Health 2024) pools 48 in vitro load-to-fracture studies on 2,724 extracted single-rooted human teeth to rank how well six obturating systems and four techniques preserve vertical root fracture (VRF) resistance. All filled roots fell below untreated controls, and most filled arms beat the prepared-but-unfilled positive control. Resilon/Epiphany ranked first (SUCRA 80.8%) but is no longer marketed; GP/iRoot SP (55.5%) and GP/MTA-Plus (47.9%) ranked marginally above GP/AH-Plus (47.4%) and GP/AH-26 (45.9%) without significant contrast, and GP/ZOE ranked last among systems (12.3%). No technique differed significantly from another, although single cone ranked highest (70.2%) and Thermafil failed to beat the unfilled control. The evidence is bench-only with modest bias control and one inconsistent loop, so it is hypothesis-grade for clinical practice.

## Key Contributions
- First NMA ranking VRF resistance across six obturating systems and four techniques (authors' claim).
- Quantifies that filling the canal restores part of the strength lost to preparation (GP/AH-Plus vs positive control SMD 0.78; Resilon/Epiphany SMD 1.55) but never reaches untreated-root strength.
- Shows calcium-silicate sealers are not statistically distinguishable from resin sealers on this bench outcome (small arms: 72 and 144 teeth), and that GP/ZOE is the weakest system.
- Finds no significant between-technique difference, so technique choice is not supported as a stand-alone VRF lever on this evidence.

## Methodology
Five databases (PubMed, Embase, ScienceDirect, Web of Science, Cochrane) Jan 2000–Sep 2023, English only; 5,193 records to 48 studies (systems) and 46 (techniques); closed-apex single-rooted human teeth; two independent reviewers; modified Tan 9-item risk-of-bias tool (34 low, 14 medium, 0 high; only 3/48 blinded the examiner); random-effects SMD NMA in Stata with global and loop-specific inconsistency tests and SUCRA ranking; no GRADE rating applied. Protocol INPLASY2023100034.

Global inconsistency was not detected (P = 0.47 systems, 0.86 techniques) but the single cone-Thermafil-warm vertical loop was inconsistent (P < 0.001); funnel plots looked symmetric. Authors attribute Resilon/Epiphany's rank to a self-etch "monoblock" bond and calcium-silicate ranking to bioactivity and wettability, and single cone's rank to low wall pressure; these are mechanistic explanations offered in the discussion, not tested outcomes.

## Clinical interpretation (wiki reasoning, not from the paper)
This is bench evidence on load-to-fracture, so it should not be quoted as a clinical VRF rate. Held evidence suggests VRF risk in root-filled teeth is driven mainly by lost tooth structure and loading ([[cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]]; [[cracked-tooth/liao-2021-vertical-root-fracture-non-endodontically-treated]], which says condensation force alone is rarely the direct cause). That fits the null between-technique contrasts here. The practical reading is that sealer and technique should be chosen on sealing, healing and handling grounds (see the clinical obturation pages below), with VRF resistance at most a weak tie-breaker favouring avoidance of heavy spreader pressure and of ZOE-based fills.
