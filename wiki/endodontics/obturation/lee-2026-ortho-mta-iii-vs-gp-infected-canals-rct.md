## Three-line Summary

RCT (IEJ 2026): 120 teeth, infected root canals with apical periodontitis; Ortho MTA III (bioceramic) vs GP+AH Plus; 30-month median follow-up.

Comparable success rates — loose criteria 88.24% vs 83.02%, strict criteria 64.71% vs 58.49%; no significant difference between obturation methods; predictors of failure: older age, larger periapical lesion size, higher PAI score.

Ortho MTA III is a viable alternative to GP+AH Plus for infected canal treatment with equivalent long-term outcomes.

## Summary

Lee et al. (IEJ 2026) conducted a parallel-arm RCT comparing Ortho MTA III bioceramic sealer versus conventional GP+AH Plus for orthograde obturation in infected root canals with apical periodontitis (120 teeth, 30-month median follow-up). Success rates using loose criteria were 88.24% (Ortho MTA III) vs 83.02% (GP+AH Plus); strict criteria 64.71% vs 58.49% — neither difference was statistically significant. Predictors of failure were patient age, periapical lesion size, and PAI score, rather than the obturation material choice. This provides long-term RCT evidence supporting bioceramic sealers as equivalent to conventional GP for infected canal treatment.

## Key Contributions

- Long-term RCT (30-month) directly comparing Ortho MTA III vs GP+AH Plus for infected canals with apical periodontitis
- Establishes material equivalence — choice of bioceramic vs conventional GP does not significantly affect success
- Identifies patient/lesion factors (age, lesion size, PAI score) as more important determinants of outcome than material choice
- Loose vs strict criteria success gap (88% vs 65%) highlights the importance of defining success thresholds in future studies

## Methodology

Parallel-arm RCT; 120 teeth with infected root canals and apical periodontitis. Intervention: Ortho MTA III bioceramic sealer for obturation. Control: GP lateral condensation + AH Plus sealer. 30-month median follow-up. Primary outcomes: healing success by loose (periapical healing or reduction of lesion) and strict criteria (complete radiographic healing). Predictor analysis for failure variables. Journal: International Endodontic Journal (IEJ).
