## One-line Summary
SR+MA (84 studies, 11,965 samples) comparing obturation techniques found no significant differences at ≤12 months; at 24 months CLC and CB modestly outperform SC; outcomes converge beyond 3 years — operator expertise matters more than technique choice.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (84개 연구, 11,965 표본): 충전 기법 간 단기 성공률 차이 없음; 24개월 시 CLC·CB가 SC보다 소폭 우수(∆5–7.5%); 3년 이후 수렴 — 기법 선택보다 술자 숙련도·증례 선택이 장기 성공에 더 중요.

## Summary
This largest SR+MA to date (84 studies, 11,965 samples, PROSPERO CRD42024524608) compared clinical and radiographic success rates of cold lateral condensation (CLC), warm vertical compaction (WVC), warm lateral compaction (WLC), single-cone (SC), and carrier-based (CB) obturation techniques in both primary root canal treatments (RCTs) and retreatments (re-RCTs). Overall success rates were high and comparable at 6 months (87.1%) and 12 months (87.2%) without technique-specific differences. A modest but statistically significant difference emerged at 24 months, where CLC (+5.0%, p=0.021) and CB (+7.5%, p=0.011) outperformed SC in primary RCTs. Beyond 3 years, success declined to 84.9% with no significant technique differences — suggesting convergence of outcomes in the long term. Retreatment success was consistently lower than primary treatment. Bioceramic sealers with SC technique showed no clear clinical superiority over conventional approaches. GRADE evidence certainty was low to very low throughout, with high heterogeneity, underscoring that operator expertise and case selection outweigh technique choice.

## Key Contributions
- No significant outcome differences among obturation techniques at ≤12 months follow-up
- At 24 months: CLC and CB showed modest superiority over SC (differences not sustained at >3 years)
- Retreatment success consistently lower than primary treatment at all time points: 92.9% (6m) → 77.0% (12m) → 83.5% (24m) → 73.7% (>3y)
- Bioceramic sealers + SC technique: no demonstrated clinical benefit over traditional approaches
- Operator expertise and case selection identified as greater determinants of success than obturation technique

## Methodology
SR+MA following PRISMA guidelines. Databases: PubMed, Cochrane Library, ScienceDirect + grey literature. Included studies: RCTs and cohort studies with ≥6 months follow-up, ≤25% dropout, permanent teeth, documented technique and material. Risk of bias: ROBINS-I (cohort), RoB 2.0 (RCTs). Statistical analysis: mixed-effects meta-regression reporting beta coefficients with 95% CI. Subgroup analyses by technique (CLC, WVC, WLC, SC, CB), follow-up duration (6m/12m/24m/>3y), and treatment type (primary/retreatment).
