## One-line Summary
Systematic review (30 studies, 1988–2025) of nonsurgical retreatment for persistent AP: favorable prognosis consistently linked to absent/small preoperative lesion, adequate obturation and coronal seal, specialist operator, and contemporary techniques (CBCT, dental microscope, NiTi rotary, ultrasonic irrigation); strict CBCT criteria show lower healing rates than 2D radiographs.

## 한줄요약
체계적 문헌고찰 (30개 연구, 1988–2025): 비수술적 재근관치료의 예후 향상 인자는 ①술전 병소 없음/소, ②적절한 충전+치관 밀폐, ③전문의 시술, ④현대 술식(CBCT·현미경·NiTi·초음파); CBCT strict 기준 성공률은 2D 방사선보다 낮음.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 30 studies (RCTs and cohorts, ≥10 teeth each) on nonsurgical retreatment for persistent apical periodontitis, spanning 37 years of literature (January 1988 – September 2025) across five databases plus grey literature.

Heterogeneity in outcome definitions, imaging modalities and follow-up intervals precluded meta-analysis, but four prognostic factors held consistently across studies — absence of a preoperative periapical lesion, smaller lesion size, adequate obturation and coronal seal, and a specialist operator — and CBCT-based strict criteria again produced systematically lower healing rates than 2D radiographs.

Contemporary armamentarium (CBCT, operating microscope, NiTi rotary instrumentation, ultrasonic irrigation, thermoplastic obturation) has improved retreatment predictability enough that nonsurgical retreatment should be prioritized over extraction in primary endodontic failure; the synthesis is narrative only, so no pooled effect estimate is available.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR): 지속성 치근단치주염 (Persistent Apical Periodontitis)에 대한 비외과적 재치료를 다룬 30편(무작위대조시험 및 코호트, 각 10치 이상)을 5개 데이터베이스와 회색문헌에서 37년치(1988년 1월~2025년 9월) 수집.

결과 정의·영상 방식·추적 기간의 이질성으로 메타분석은 불가했으나, 네 가지 예후인자가 연구 전반에서 일관됐다 — 술전 치근단 병소 없음, 작은 병소 크기, 적절한 근관충전과 치관부 밀폐, 전문의 술자. CBCT 엄격기준은 여기서도 2D 방사선보다 체계적으로 낮은 치유율을 보였다.

현대 장비(CBCT, 수술현미경, 니켈티타늄 (Nickel-Titanium, NiTi) 회전기구, 초음파 세척, 열가소성 근관충전)가 재치료 예측성을 충분히 끌어올렸으므로, **1차 근관치료 실패 시 발치보다 비외과적 재치료를 우선**해야 한다 — 다만 서술적 종합이라 통합 효과추정치는 없다.

## Summary
This systematic review (PRISMA 2020) covered 37 years (1988–Sep 2025) of evidence on nonsurgical endodontic retreatment (NS-ReTx) for persistent AP. Thirty studies (RCTs and cohort studies with ≥10 teeth) were included. Narrative synthesis was used due to heterogeneity in outcome definitions, imaging modalities (2D vs CBCT), and follow-up intervals that precluded meta-analysis. Key finding: strict radiographic criteria (complete resolution), especially when CBCT is used, consistently yield lower apparent success rates than loose criteria or 2D imaging. Despite variability in reported rates, four prognostic factors were consistently favorable across studies: absence of preoperative periapical lesion, smaller lesion size, adequate obturation quality with adequate coronal seal, and specialist operator. Contemporary techniques — CBCT for planning, dental operating microscope, NiTi rotary files for removal, ultrasonic irrigation, thermoplastic obturation — have measurably improved NS retreatment predictability. NS retreatment should be prioritized over extraction in cases of primary endodontic failure where appropriate.

## Key Contributions
- 37-year synthesis: outcome data from 1988–2025, capturing the full transition from pre- to post-modern technique era
- Consistent finding across all studies: CBCT strict criteria reveal more residual disease than 2D radiographs
- Four invariant prognostic factors across all time periods and imaging modalities
- Contemporary techniques (microscope, NiTi, CBCT, ultrasonic irrigation) each linked to measurably improved retreatment predictability
- Clinical recommendation: NS retreatment should be offered before extraction in primary endodontic failure

## Methodology
Systematic review (PRISMA 2020). Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Scopus, Web of Science + grey literature. Period: January 1988–September 2025. Inclusion: RCTs and cohort studies with ≥10 teeth, reporting periapical healing after NS retreatment. Primary outcome: periapical healing (radiograph and/or CBCT). Secondary: tooth survival, prognostic factors. Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2 (RCTs), ROBINS-I (cohort), Newcastle-Ottawa (observational). Narrative synthesis (no meta-analysis possible).

## Results

**Success rate range (across included studies):**
- Variable by imaging modality and criteria
- Strict CBCT criteria: generally lower than 2D or loose criteria
- Quantitative pooling not possible due to heterogeneity

**Consistent prognostic factors (favorable):**

| Factor | Direction |
|---|---|
| Preoperative periapical lesion | Absent → better |
| Periapical lesion size | Smaller → better |
| Obturation quality | Adequate → better |
| Coronal seal | Adequate → better |
| Operator type | Specialist → better |

**Contemporary technique contributions:**
- CBCT: improved lesion detection and access planning
- Dental operating microscope: improved direct visualization
- NiTi rotary files: improved efficiency of prior material removal
- Ultrasonic irrigation: improved canal disinfection
- Thermoplastic obturation: improved sealing
