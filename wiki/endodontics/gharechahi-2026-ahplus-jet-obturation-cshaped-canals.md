## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study on 30 3D-printed replicas of C1-configuration C-shaped canals (from a CBCT-scanned mandibular molar), comparing cold lateral compaction (CLC), warm vertical compaction (WVC), and warm vertical hybrid compaction (HC), all obturated with AH Plus Jet sealer.

WVC produced the lowest void percentage in the middle (1.03%) and coronal (0.62%) thirds and the shortest obturation time (168 s vs 482.5 s for CLC and 341 s for HC, p<0.001); apical-third void differences were statistically significant (WVC 1.45% vs CLC/HC ~1.00%, p=0.036) but the effect size was small (0.218), indicating negligible clinical impact.

WVC is the most efficient and best-adapting technique for AH Plus Jet in C1 C-shaped canals, but findings come from resin 3D-printed replicas (no dentinal tubules, no periapical tissue) and require validation in long-term clinical trials.

## 세줄요약

CBCT로 확인된 하악대구치 C1형 C-shaped 근관을 3D 프린팅한 레플리카 30개(체외 실험)에 AH Plus Jet 실러로 냉측방가압(Cold Lateral Compaction, CLC), 온측방가압(Warm Vertical Compaction, WVC), 온혼합가압(Warm Vertical Hybrid Compaction, HC) 세 충전법을 비교.

WVC가 중간·치관 부위 미세공극(void, %)이 가장 낮고(각 1.03%, 0.62%) 충전시간도 가장 짧음(168초 vs CLC 482.5초, HC 341초, p<0.001); 근단부 공극 차이는 통계적으로 유의(WVC 1.45% vs CLC/HC 약 1.00%, p=0.036)했으나 효과크기가 작아(0.218) 임상적 의미는 미미.

AH Plus Jet과 함께 사용 시 WVC가 C1형 C-shaped 근관에서 효율성과 충전 적합도 면에서 우수하나, 상아세관이 없는 레진 3D 프린팅 레플리카 기반 연구라는 한계가 있어 장기 임상시험으로 검증이 필요.

## Summary

This in-vitro laboratory study evaluated three obturation techniques — cold lateral compaction (CLC), warm vertical compaction (WVC), and warm vertical hybrid compaction (HC) — using **AH Plus Jet** sealer (Dentsply De Trey), a premixed-syringe reformulation of the epoxy-resin gold-standard AH Plus, in standardized 3D-printed replicas of **C1-configuration C-shaped canals**. A single mandibular molar with confirmed C1 anatomy (per Fan et al. classification) was CBCT-scanned and 3D-printed into 30 replicas (n=10/group, power-analysis-derived). Replicas were sectioned into apical, middle, and coronal thirds and imaged under a stereomicroscope to quantify gutta-percha %, sealer %, and void % per third, alongside obturation time. WVC achieved the lowest void percentage in the middle and coronal thirds and the shortest obturation time; CLC achieved the best apical adaptation but progressively worsened toward the coronal third; apical void differences, while statistically significant, had a small effect size judged clinically negligible.

## Key Contributions

- First reported evaluation (per the authors) of **AH Plus Jet** specifically in **C-shaped canal** anatomy, extending prior AH Plus/sealer comparisons that used conventional round canals.
- Standardizes anatomical variability via **3D-printed replicas** of a single CBCT-confirmed C1 tooth, isolating obturation-technique effects from tooth-to-tooth morphological variation.
- Reports **void %, gutta-percha %, sealer %, and obturation time together** by canal third, distinguishing statistically significant differences from clinically meaningful ones via effect size.
- Rejects the study's null hypothesis for middle/coronal thirds (technique matters) while showing apical-third differences, though significant, are clinically negligible (small effect size).

## Methodology

- **Sample origin**: 15 human mandibular molars with fused roots screened by CBCT (Planmeca ProMax 3D Classic, 0.125 mm slices); one tooth with confirmed C1 configuration selected.
- **Canal preparation**: working length established, decoronated to 10 mm; shaped with M3 Pro Gold rotary to #30/0.04 (350 rpm, 1–2 Ncm); anti-curvature filing (H-files #15–25) at the isthmus; 1% NaOCl between files; final irrigation 17% EDTA + saline.
- **3D-printed replicas**: high-resolution (75 μm) CBCT reconstruction (Planmeca Romexis 6.2) printed at 4K on an AccuFab-L4K printer (SG100 resin); power analysis (α=0.05, power=0.8) → n=10/group, 30 replicas total.
- **Obturation**: AH Plus Jet sealer with CLC, WVC, or HC, by a calibrated endodontist under senior supervision; fill time recorded; 1-week incubation (37 °C, 100% humidity) for sealer setting.
- **Sectioning/imaging**: CNC-sectioned into apical (2 mm), middle (5 mm), coronal (7 mm) thirds; stereomicroscope (32×) + DinoCapture 2.0 quantified gutta-percha %, sealer %, void % per section.
- **Statistics**: one-way ANOVA + LSD post-hoc (SPSS v23.0), p<0.05, with effect sizes reported per comparison.

## Results

| Metric | WVC | CLC | HC | P | Effect size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gutta-percha, apical | 73.46±7.29 | 84.12±5.76 | 78.21±2.56 | <0.001 | 0.405 |
| Gutta-percha, middle | 92.82±1.72 | 89.85±3.58 | 89.72±0.76 | 0.009 | 0.294 |
| Gutta-percha, coronal | 97.89±0.38 | 94.02±1.24 | 95.87±0.93 | <0.001 | 0.766 |
| Sealer, apical | 25.09±7.24 | 15.38±5.77 | 20.77±2.41 | 0.002 | 0.364 |
| Sealer, middle | 6.14±1.85 | 7.90±3.59 | 8.81±0.66 | 0.053 (NS) | 0.196 |
| Sealer, coronal | 1.47±0.39 | 3.02±1.13 | 3.39±0.79 | <0.001 | 0.525 |
| Void, apical | 1.45±0.55 | 1.00±0.21 | 1.00±0.43 | 0.036 | 0.218 |
| Void, middle | 1.03±0.37 | 2.24±0.39 | 1.46±0.38 | <0.001 | 0.652 |
| Void, coronal | 0.62±0.11 | 2.95±0.36 | 0.72±0.31 | <0.001 | 0.940 |

- **CLC**: best apical adaptation (highest gutta-percha %, lowest sealer %) but void % increases progressively toward the coronal third — the opposite pattern from WVC.
- **WVC**: significantly higher gutta-percha % and lower void % in middle/coronal thirds (attributed to injectable gutta-percha flow into fins/isthmuses); lowest sealer % coronally.
- **HC**: intermediate performance; auxiliary cones gave only negligible improvement in apical quality over WVC/CLC.
- **Obturation time**: CLC 482.50±6.20 s (slowest) > HC 341.00±19.12 s > WVC 168.00±24.29 s (fastest); all pairwise p<0.001.
- **Bottom line**: apical void differences are statistically significant (p=0.036) but small effect size (0.218) → clinically negligible; middle/coronal void differences are both statistically and clinically significant (effect sizes 0.652, 0.940) → WVC preferred for those regions and for procedural efficiency. Authors call for long-term clinical validation before generalizing.
