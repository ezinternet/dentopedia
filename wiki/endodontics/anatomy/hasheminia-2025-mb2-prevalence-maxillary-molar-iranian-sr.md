## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (29 Iranian-population studies, PROSPERO CRD42024542862): pooled MB2 prevalence is 60% (95% CI 53–67%) in maxillary first molars and 33% (25–42%) in second molars, including the first meta-analysis incorporating Persian-language databases (Magiran, SID, IranMedex).

Detection method is the dominant driver of heterogeneity (I²=97%): microscope detects MB2 in 78% of first molars vs only 15% by periapical radiograph — a 5× difference underlining how method choice shapes apparent prevalence.

Clinically, the Iranian first-molar MB2 prevalence (60%) is consistent with global figures, and the 5× method gap strongly supports using DOM as the standard of care when MB2 is suspected or missed.

SR+MA(이란인 29개 연구, PROSPERO 등록): 상악 제1대구치 MB2 유병률 60%(95% CI 53–67%), 제2대구치 33%(25–42%) — 페르시아어 DB 포함 최초 메타분석.

검출 방법이 이질성(I²=97%)의 주요 원인: 현미경 78% vs 치근단 방사선 15%(5배 차이) — 측정법이 유병률 수치를 좌우.

임상 의미: 이란인 제1대구치 MB2 60%는 전 세계적 수치와 일관; MB2 의심/미발견 시 DOM이 표준으로 뒷받침됨.

## Summary
Systematic review and meta-analysis of 29 studies on 제2근심협측 근관 (MB2) prevalence in 이란인 (Iranian population). PROSPERO-registered (CRD42024542862). MB2 prevalence: 60% (95% CI 53–67%) in 상악 제1대구치 and 33% (95% CI 25–42%) in 상악 제2대구치. Detection rates vary dramatically by method: 현미경 (microscope) highest (78%/61%), 치근단 방사선 (periapical radiograph) lowest (15%/4%). Heterogeneity extremely high (I²=97%) in all subgroups, driven primarily by detection method differences.

## Key Contributions
- Population-specific reference: Iranian MB2 prevalence benchmarks
- Quantifies detection method gap: microscope vs X-ray for MB2 (5× difference)
- First SR+MA including Persian-language literature (Magiran, SID, IranMedex)

## Methodology
- PRISMA SR+MA; 6 databases; through May 2024; JBI quality appraisal; STATA18; inverse variance method
