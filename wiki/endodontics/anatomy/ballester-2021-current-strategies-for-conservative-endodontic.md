## Three-line Summary

PRISMA systematic review and meta-analysis of 33 studies comparing four endodontic access cavity (AC) designs — traditional (TradAC), conservative (ConsAC), ultraconservative (UltraAC), and truss (TrussAC) — on fracture resistance, canal detection, procedural errors, and treatment time.

ConsAC/TrussAC achieve significantly higher fracture resistance than TradAC only when all marginal ridges are preserved (P < 0.05); the advantage disappears with one or more ridge lost (P > 0.05); ConsAC without operating microscope and ultrasonic troughing significantly increases risk of missed canals (P < 0.05).

Clinical implication: minimize AC extent as far as treatment quality allows; marginal ridge integrity and diagnostic visualization equipment are the two mandatory moderators for selecting a conservative design.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 (임상연구 33편): 네 가지 근관접근동 (Access Cavity, AC) 설계 — 전통적(TradAC) vs 보존적(ConsAC) vs 초보존적(UltraAC) vs 트러스(TrussAC) — 의 파절저항성·근관 발견율·수술상 오류·처치시간 비교.

핵심 결과: ConsAC/TrussAC는 **모든 변연능선 (Marginal Ridge)이 보존된 경우에만** TradAC 대비 파절저항성 유의 우월(P < 0.05); 능선 1개 이상 상실 시 유의차 소멸(P > 0.05); ConsAC는 치과수술용현미경 (Dental Operating Microscope, DOM)·초음파 트로프 없이 사용하면 미발견 근관 위험 유의 증가(P < 0.05).

임상 권고: 치료 질 손상 없는 최소 접근동 유지 — 변연능선 보존 여부·진단 시인성 장비가 설계 선택의 두 가지 핵심 조절변수.

## Summary

This PRISMA-compliant systematic review and meta-analysis synthesized 33 clinical and in vitro studies (screened from 356 records) to evaluate four endodontic access cavity designs across biomechanical and clinical outcome domains. The central finding is that the fracture resistance advantage of conservative designs is conditional: ConsAC and TrussAC outperform TradAC only when all marginal ridges remain intact, an advantage that disappears once ridges are sacrificed for restorative reasons. Equally important, ConsAC without a dental operating microscope and ultrasonic troughing significantly increases the risk of leaving canals undetected, undermining the very rationale for minimally invasive access. TrussAC was judged insufficiently evidenced for routine adoption, and UltraAC was restricted to carefully selected single-canal scenarios. The authors propose a decision-making protocol anchored on two gatekeeping questions: Are the marginal ridges preserved? Is magnification-assisted visualization available?

## Key Contributions

- First meta-analysis directly comparing fracture resistance and canal detection across all four major AC design families (TradAC, ConsAC, UltraAC, TrussAC).
- Establishes marginal ridge preservation as the critical moderator of mechanical advantage: benefit present with intact ridges, absent with ridge loss — previously implicit but never meta-analytically confirmed.
- Quantifies the diagnostic risk of ConsAC: significantly higher missed-canal rate without DOM + ultrasonic troughing (P < 0.05), making magnification a prerequisite rather than an option.
- Provides evidence-based grading of design readiness: ConsAC conditionally supported, TrussAC insufficiently evidenced, UltraAC limited-indication only.
- Proposes a structured decision-making protocol (ridge status + visualization equipment) to guide practitioner selection.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA 2009)
- **Databases**: PubMed, Web of Science
- **Included studies**: 33 articles (in vitro biomechanics, clinical RCTs, retrospective cohorts; screened from 356 records)
- **Risk of bias**: Moderate (global assessment); heterogeneity precluded full pooling of some outcomes (qualitative synthesis for those)
- **Primary outcomes**: Fracture resistance (N), root canal detection rate
- **Secondary outcomes**: Cleaning/disinfection efficacy, apical debris extrusion, procedural errors, root canal filling quality, treatment time
- **Meta-analysis significance level**: P = 0.05

## Results

### Fracture Resistance

| Comparison | Marginal Ridge Status | Result |
|---|---|---|
| ConsAC vs TradAC | All ridges intact | ConsAC significantly higher (P < 0.05) |
| TrussAC vs TradAC | All ridges intact | TrussAC significantly higher (P < 0.05) |
| ConsAC vs TradAC | ≥1 ridge lost | No significant difference (P > 0.05) |
| TrussAC vs TradAC | ≥1 ridge lost | No significant difference (P > 0.05) |
| UltraAC vs TradAC | Various | Strength advantage in some studies; insufficient evidence for routine recommendation |

### Canal Detection

| Condition | Canal Detection Risk |
|---|---|
| ConsAC without DOM + ultrasonic troughing | Significantly higher missed-canal rate (P < 0.05) |
| ConsAC with DOM + ultrasonic troughing | Comparable to TradAC |

### Other Outcomes

- **Procedural errors**: No significant difference across designs with competent technique
- **Apical debris extrusion**: No significant difference
- **Root canal filling/sealing**: Comparable across designs when visibility maintained
- **Treatment time**: Conservative designs may add minimal additional time (not quantified)
