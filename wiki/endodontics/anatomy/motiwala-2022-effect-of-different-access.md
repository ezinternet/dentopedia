## Three-line Summary
Systematic review and network meta-analysis of 14 in vitro studies (10 studies, n=456 extracted molars) ranking traditional endodontic cavities (TEC), conservative endodontic cavities (CEC), truss, and ninja access designs by fracture resistance using SUCRA and CINeMA/GRADE confidence framework.

Conservative endodontic cavities ranked second best (SUCRA 51.4%, MD vs intact: −365.59 N [95% CI −759.02, 27.83]), while traditional cavities ranked last (SUCRA 15.3%, MD: −927.52 N [95% CI −1304.80, −550.24]), representing ~562 N additional fracture load preserved with CEC over TEC.

Clinical bottom line: in vitro evidence favors CEC for structural preservation of endodontically treated teeth; overall confidence is low-to-moderate and all studies are laboratory-based, so clinical long-term outcome data are needed.

## Summary
This 2022 systematic review and network meta-analysis from Aga Khan University Hospital synthesizes 14 in vitro comparative studies to rank endodontic access cavity designs by their effect on fracture resistance of endodontically treated teeth. The network meta-analysis on 10 molar studies (n=456 teeth) used random-effects frequentist modeling with SUCRA ranking and the CINeMA framework for confidence-in-evidence grading. Conservative endodontic cavities preserved significantly more fracture resistance than traditional cavities (MD difference ~562 N), though the CEC advantage over intact teeth did not reach statistical significance (95% CI crossed zero). Truss cavities ranked intermediate (SUCRA 40.2%), while ninja cavities had insufficient data for network inclusion. Overall confidence across all comparisons was low to moderate due to publication bias, heterogeneity, small sample sizes, and the intrinsic indirectness of in vitro endpoints; no clinical long-term fracture or survival data exist to validate these laboratory findings.

## Key Contributions
- First network meta-analysis applying SUCRA ranking and formal CINeMA/GRADE confidence grading to compare multiple endodontic access designs (TEC, CEC, truss, ninja) simultaneously
- Quantifies the structural advantage of CEC over TEC: ~562 N additional fracture load preserved (MD: −365.59 vs −927.52 relative to intact baseline)
- Demonstrates TEC's fracture resistance reduction is statistically significant (95% CI excludes zero), while CEC's reduction vs intact teeth is not (CI includes zero)
- Establishes hierarchy of access cavity designs ranked by SUCRA: intact (85.4%) > CEC (51.4%) > truss (40.2%) > TEC (15.3%)

## Methodology
- **Design**: Systematic review + frequentist network meta-analysis of in vitro controlled studies
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, LILACS, OpenGrey + hand search (through September 2020)
- **Screening**: 844 articles screened → 14 studies included in systematic review; 10 molar studies (n=456 teeth) entered the NMA
- **Inclusion**: In vitro studies, extracted human teeth, endodontically treated, comparing fracture toughness/resistance across ≥2 access designs
- **Exclusion**: Single-rooted teeth in main network; clinical data; non-English publications
- **Outcomes**: Primary = fracture resistance (N or MPa, load-to-fracture); secondary = biomechanical properties
- **Risk of bias**: Modified RoB tool for in vitro studies — overall moderate
- **Evidence confidence**: CINeMA framework (GRADE-adapted for NMA); overall low-to-moderate across comparisons

## Results

### Fracture Resistance by Access Design (vs Intact Teeth Baseline)

| Access Design | MD (95% CI) vs Intact | SUCRA | Rank | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intact teeth (reference) | 0 [baseline] | 85.4% | 1st | — |
| Conservative endodontic cavity (CEC) | −365.59 [−759.02, 27.83] | 51.4% | 2nd | Not significant (CI crosses zero) |
| Truss access cavity | −548.78 [−918.29, −179.27] | 40.2% | 3rd | Significant |
| Traditional endodontic cavity (TEC) | −927.52 [−1304.80, −550.24] | 15.3% | 4th | Significant |
| Ninja cavity | — insufficient data — | — | — | Not estimable |

**CEC vs TEC estimated difference**: ~+562 N fracture load preserved with CEC (−365.59 − (−927.52) = +561.93 N).

### Evidence Confidence (CINeMA/GRADE)
- Overall: **low to moderate** across all pairwise comparisons
- Main downgrade reasons: publication bias (funnel asymmetry), inconsistency (high I²), indirectness (all in vitro), small samples per comparison arm
