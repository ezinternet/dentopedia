## Three-line Summary

In vivo study (n=60 patients undergoing maxillary first molar RCT, India): MB2 detection rose stepwise from naked-eye explorer 20% (12/60) → dental operating microscope (DOM) 55% (33/60) → DOM + ultrasonic trough 70% (42/60), with the difference across stages highly significant (p<0.001).

The ultrasonic trough technique removes a 3-mm groove ultrasonically from the MB1 orifice toward the palatal canal under continuous DOM visualization, exposing dentin bridges that conceal the MB2 orifice.

Limitation: no CBCT gold standard was used, so the 70% figure represents clinical detection rate rather than true population prevalence — Camacho-Aparicio 2022 with CBCT showed 86% sensitivity and 79% true prevalence using the same escalating protocol.

## 세줄요약

In vivo 연구(상악 제1대구치 근관치료 60명, 인도): MB2 발견율 육안 20% → DOM 55% → DOM+초음파 트로프 70%(p<0.001).

초음파 트로프 술기: MB1 근관구에서 구개 근관 방향으로 3mm 홈을 DOM 하 초음파로 절삭 → MB2 근관구를 덮는 상아질 다리(dentin bridge) 제거.

한계: CBCT gold standard 없음 — 70%는 임상 탐지율이지 진성 유병률이 아님; Camacho-Aparicio 2022(CBCT 사용)에서 동일 프로토콜로 민감도 86%·유병률 79% 확인.

## Summary
In vivo study (60 patients, maxillary first molar RCT) evaluating sequential escalation of MB2 detection tools. Each step — naked eye explorer → dental operating microscope (DOM) → DOM + ultrasonic trough — significantly increases detection. Overall detection rate 70% with full protocol.

## Detection Results (Cumulative)

| Method | MB2 Detected | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Naked eye (explorer) | 12 / 60 | **20%** |
| + DOM | 33 / 60 | **55%** |
| + DOM + Ultrasonic trough | 42 / 60 | **70%** |

- p<0.001 (ROC curve analysis across methods)

## Technique: Ultrasonic Trough
- 3-mm groove cut ultrasonically from MB1 orifice toward palatal canal
- Removes dentin bridges hiding MB2 orifice
- Examined under DOM magnification throughout
