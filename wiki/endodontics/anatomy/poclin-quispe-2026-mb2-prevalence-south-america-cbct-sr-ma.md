## Summary

Poclin-Quispe et al. (Front Dent Med 2026) synthesized 23 CBCT studies from South American populations to determine MB2 canal prevalence in maxillary molars. The pooled estimate was 59% overall, with first molars at 66% and second molars at 41%. This regional CBCT-based SR+MA complements global prevalence data and reinforces that MB2 is a common finding in South American patients — present in roughly two-thirds of first molars. CBCT is the detection reference standard across all included studies.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA on MB2 prevalence specific to South American populations using CBCT
- Pooled 59% prevalence (23 studies) — substantial population-specific estimate
- First molar (66%) > second molar (41%) differential — clinically important for case anticipation
- Strengthens the evidence for routine MB2 search in clinical endodontic practice
- Adds to global literature on population-specific MB2 variation

## Methodology

SR+MA; 23 CBCT studies from South America (specific countries not detailed in abstract); outcome: MB2 canal prevalence (%) in maxillary first and second molars. Journal: Frontiers in Dental Medicine.
