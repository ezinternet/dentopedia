## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42024564150) of 6 systematic reviews on guided endodontics (static navigation, SN / dynamic navigation, DN; 2 of the 6 included meta-analyses), quality-appraised with AMSTAR 2.

Pooled evidence shows high success rates for computer-aided navigation — apex-location success 96.8% (SN, endodontic microsurgery) and root-canal-location success 98.1% (access cavities, no significant SN-vs-DN difference), with SN ~27x more effective than conventional endodontic microsurgery; average drill-tip deviation was 0.46 mm.

AMSTAR 2 scores ranged from moderate (10/16) to high (14.5/16) quality; evidence is dominated by case reports/series with limited follow-up, so further high-quality RCTs are needed before routine clinical adoption.

## Summary

This umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42024564150, PRISMA-guided) synthesizes 6 systematic reviews (2 with formal meta-analysis) evaluating whether guided endodontics — static navigation (SN, pre-planned 3D-printed CBCT-derived templates) or dynamic navigation (DN, real-time optical tracking) — improves endodontic treatment success versus conventional freehand techniques. Across the pooled evidence, guided endodontics consistently achieved success rates above 95% for locating calcified canals, performing access cavities, and executing endodontic microsurgery, with SN roughly 27 times more effective than conventional endodontic microsurgery in one meta-analysis. A recurring precision trade-off was a mean drill-tip deviation of ~0.46 mm, attributable to guide design rather than operator skill, which can limit suitability in complex anatomy (e.g., C-shaped canals). Methodological quality of the 6 underlying SRs (via AMSTAR 2) ranged from moderate (10/16) to high (14.5/16), and most supporting evidence is case-report/case-series level with limited follow-up — so the authors call for high-quality RCTs before guided endodontics becomes routine standard of care.

## Key Contributions

- Consolidates systematic-review-level (not just primary-study-level) evidence on guided endodontics into a single AMSTAR 2-appraised umbrella review — the first of its kind on this topic.
- Provides pooled quantitative benchmarks: SN apex-location success 96.8% (CI 93.0–100%) in endodontic microsurgery; overall root-canal-location success 98.1% (CI 95.7–100%) in access cavities, with no significant SN-vs-DN difference (98.5% vs 94.5%, p=0.451).
- Quantifies SN's advantage over conventional endodontic microsurgery at ~27x (OR 27.7, 95% CI 11.3–68.1, p<0.0001).
- Identifies a consistent precision limitation (mean drill-tip deviation 0.46 mm) replicated across multiple independent reviews (Zehnder 2016, Connert 2017, Nayak 2018, Pena-Bengoa 2023), and links it to guide design rather than operator technique.
- Surfaces methodological-quality heterogeneity across the field via item-level AMSTAR 2 scoring (weakest items: funding-source disclosure of included primary studies, and listing/justifying excluded studies).

## Methodology

- **Design**: Umbrella review of SRs/MAs; registered PROSPERO CRD42024564150; PRISMA-guided.
- **Search**: PubMed, Scopus, EBSCOhost, Cochrane (2016–July 2024) + manual and grey-literature search.
- **Screening**: Two independent reviewers for title/abstract and full-text screening; third reviewer resolved disagreements.
- **Included evidence types (within source SRs)**: RCTs, randomized clinical trials, case series, case reports, English language.
- **Excluded**: non-SR reviews, non-English articles, in vitro/ex vivo studies, randomized experimental (in vitro) studies, animal studies, narrative reviews, expert opinions, editorials.
- **Quality tool**: AMSTAR 2 (16-item checklist; Yes/Partial Yes/No/N/A), classifying reviews as high/moderate/low/critically-low quality.
- **PRISMA flow**: 112 records identified → 87 duplicates + 2 automation-excluded + 2 other-reason excluded → 11 full-text assessed → 5 excluded (2 pilot, 3 in vitro) → 6 SRs included (2 with meta-analysis).

## Results

| Included SR (year) | AMSTAR 2 | Meta-analysis | Key finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pena-Bengoa 2023 | 10.5/16 (moderate) | No | High success locating calcified canals (only 2/45 studies failures); average drill-tip deviation 0.46 mm |
| Zubizarreta-Macho Jan 2021 | 14/16 (high) | Yes (7 studies) | SN apex-location success 96.8% (CI 93.0–100%); 27x more effective than conventional microsurgery (OR 27.7, 95% CI 11.3–68.1, p<0.0001); no heterogeneity (I²=2.4%) |
| Zubizarreta-Macho Mar 2021 | 14.5/16 (high) | Yes (13 studies) | Root-canal-location success 98.1% (CI 95.7–100%); SN 98.5% vs DN 94.5% (NS); in vitro 96.2% vs in vivo 100% |
| Moreno-Rabie 2020 | 11.5/16 (moderate) | No | Guided access cavity/surgery highly accurate; "acceptable" precision = canal locatable + apical healing at follow-up (Buchgreitz 2019, n=50) |
| Vasudevan 2022 | 12.5/16 (high) | No (heterogeneity) | DN mean 3D deviation 0.67 mm coronal / 0.9 mm apical, 2.50° angular; fewer iatrogenic errors; treatment time 11.5 sec–<45 min |
| Poirazi 2023 | 10/16 (moderate) | No | Guided fiber-post removal: 100% success, no fractures/root perforations |

**Bottom line**: Guided endodontics (SN and DN) is consistently accurate (generally >95% success) across calcified-canal location, access-cavity preparation, endodontic microsurgery, and post removal, substantially outperforming freehand technique, but at the cost of a small guide-design-dependent drill-tip deviation (~0.46 mm) and an evidence base still weighted toward case reports/series rather than RCTs.
