## Summary

Bai et al. (J Dent 2026) synthesized 19 studies (11 with extractable data for MA) to characterize where accessory canals are distributed along the root length and determine the optimal root-end resection depth during apicoectomy. Within apical 2mm, 75.8% of molar accessory canals are captured. However, anterior teeth require a 3mm resection level to include >95% of their accessory canals. This refines the traditional blanket 3mm resection guideline: molars can be managed with 2mm in most cases, while anteriors justify the full 3mm to comprehensively address lateral canals near the apex.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA providing tooth-type-specific accessory canal distribution data for root-end resection planning
- 2mm resection captures 75.8% of molar apical accessory canals — supports more conservative resection in molars
- 3mm resection validated for anteriors (>95% capture) — confirms classic guideline anatomically for anterior teeth
- Differentiates molar vs anterior requirements, enabling tooth-type-specific surgical planning

## Methodology

SR+MA; 19 studies included (11 for quantitative synthesis). Outcomes: proportion of accessory canals within apical 2mm and 3mm from root tip, by tooth type. Detection methods across studies: microCT, histology, clearing-staining techniques. Journal: Journal of Dentistry.
