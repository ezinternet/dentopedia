## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42023461029, PRISMA 2020) of 7 systematic reviews (2020–2024, 63 unique RCTs, 5 with meta-analysis) comparing bioceramic vs. epoxy resin-based sealers for postoperative pain after primary non-surgical root canal treatment, with de novo random-effects pooling of RCT-level data extracted from the included reviews.

Pooled analysis found a small but statistically significant reduction in pain favoring bioceramic sealers at 24h (SMD = −0.15, 95% CI −0.28 to −0.03, p<0.05) and 48h (SMD = −0.29, 95% CI −0.42 to −0.16, p<0.001), with no differences beyond 48h, in analgesic consumption (RR = 0.91–0.92), or flare-up incidence (RR = 0.97); evidence overlap across reviews was moderate (CCA ≈ 12%) and AMSTAR-2 quality ranged from moderate to low.

Clinically, the pain reduction with bioceramic sealers is statistically real but too small to be clinically meaningful, so sealer selection should rest on handling/biocompatibility/retrievability properties rather than expected postoperative discomfort.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review, SR) 7편(2020–2024년, 고유 무작위대조시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 63편, 그중 5편 메타분석 포함)을 대상으로 원발성 비외과적 근관치료(Non-Surgical Root Canal Treatment, NSRCT) 후 바이오세라믹(bioceramic) 실러와 에폭시레진(epoxy resin) 실러 간 술후통증(postoperative pain)을 비교한 우산리뷰(umbrella review, PROSPERO CRD42023461029, PRISMA 2020)로, 포함된 SR에서 추출한 RCT 수준 자료로 자체 랜덤효과모델 메타분석(random-effects meta-analysis)을 새로 수행했다.

풀링 분석 결과 바이오세라믹 실러가 24시간(표준화평균차, Standardized Mean Difference, SMD = −0.15, 95% 신뢰구간 −0.28~−0.03, p<0.05)과 48시간(SMD = −0.29, 95% CI −0.42~−0.16, p<0.001)에 통계적으로 유의하게 작은 통증 감소를 보였으나, 48시간 이후·진통제 사용(위험비, Risk Ratio, RR = 0.91–0.92)·급성발작(flare-up) 발생률(RR = 0.97)에서는 차이가 없었고, 연구 간 근거 중복도(Corrected Covered Area, CCA)는 중간 수준(약 12%), AMSTAR-2 방법론 질은 중간~낮음이었다.

임상적으로 바이오세라믹 실러의 통증 감소는 통계적으로는 실재하나 임상적 의미를 갖기엔 너무 작아, 실러 선택은 술후통증 예상보다는 재료의 조작성·생체적합성·재처치 가능성 등에 근거해야 한다.

## Summary

This umbrella review is the first to focus specifically on postoperative pain differences between bioceramic (calcium silicate-based) and epoxy resin-based root canal sealers, synthesizing 7 systematic reviews (2020–2024, 63 unique RCTs, 5 with meta-analysis) identified from 569 initial records after PRISMA 2020-compliant screening. Unlike a purely narrative umbrella synthesis, the authors extracted RCT-level effect data from the constituent reviews and ran their own random-effects meta-analyses, yielding pooled SMDs for pain at 24h and 48h plus pooled RRs for analgesic use and flare-ups. The headline finding is a small, statistically significant reduction in early postoperative pain with bioceramic sealers (SMD −0.15 at 24h, −0.29 at 48h) that disappears by 72h/7 days and does not extend to analgesic consumption or flare-up incidence. Moderate evidence overlap (CCA ≈ 12%) across the 7 reviews and AMSTAR-2 quality of moderate-to-low temper confidence, and GRADE certainty tops out at moderate for any outcome. The authors explicitly frame the effect as real but clinically negligible, echoing (and now quantifying) the same bottom line that [[zamparini-2026-postop-pain-endodontic-instrumentation-irrigation-obturation-umbrella]] reached narratively for the obturation domain of its broader 3-procedure umbrella review.

## Key Contributions

- First umbrella review dedicated exclusively to the bioceramic-vs-epoxy-resin sealer postoperative-pain question, rather than treating sealer type as one sub-question within a broader procedural umbrella review (contrast with [[zamparini-2026-postop-pain-endodontic-instrumentation-irrigation-obturation-umbrella]]).
- Performs genuine de novo random-effects meta-analysis on RCT-level data pooled across the 5 quantitative constituent SRs (not simply re-reporting each SR's own pooled estimate), producing new time-point-specific SMDs at 6h, 24h, 48h, and 72h/7d.
- Quantifies inter-review evidence overlap via Corrected Covered Area (CCA ≈ 11.7% average, moderate) and dual-appraises with AMSTAR-2 (review methodology) + GRADE (outcome certainty).
- Meta-regression testing visit protocol, pulp status, and pain-scale type as moderators found none significant (all p>0.3), suggesting the small BC-favoring effect is stable across these clinical/methodological subgroups rather than being driven by a particular study design.
- Robustness confirmed via REML vs. Paule–Mandel estimators, Knapp–Hartung adjustment, and Peto sensitivity analysis for the binary endpoint — conclusions unchanged across all checks.

## Methodology

PRISMA 2020 + Cochrane Handbook + JBI umbrella-review manual, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42023461029). PICOS: adults undergoing primary single-visit NSRCT; bioceramic (calcium silicate-based, e.g. EndoSequence BC, iRoot SP, TotalFill BC, Endoseal MTA) vs. epoxy resin-based (predominantly AH Plus) sealers; postoperative pain via validated scales (VAS/NRS) at 24h/48h (primary) or >48h/analgesic intake/flare-up (secondary); systematic reviews and meta-analyses of RCTs/quasi-RCTs/CCTs, English-language only. Searched PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Embase, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, LILACS, Google Scholar plus grey literature (OpenGrey, SIGLE, OpenThesis), January 2000–November 2024. Dual independent screening with consensus/third-party adjudication. AMSTAR-2 for review-level quality; risk-of-bias tools extracted from constituent reviews (Cochrane RoB-2, ROBINS-I, JBI). Random-effects (DerSimonian–Laird) meta-analysis of RCT-level SMDs/RRs pooled from constituent reviews (each primary trial counted once per outcome/time point); heterogeneity via I² (low <25%, moderate 25–75%, high >75%); GRADE certainty per outcome; CCA for evidence overlap; sensitivity analyses (REML, Paule–Mandel, Knapp–Hartung, Peto) and meta-regression (visit protocol, pulp status, pain scale) for robustness. Software: RevMan 5.4.1, Stata v16, R (meta package).

## Results

- **Search**: 569 records → 424 after deduplication → 75 full-text assessed → 7 SRs included (5 with meta-analysis, 1 qualitative-only), covering 63 unique RCTs published 2020–2024 (Iran, Italy, India, Brazil, Turkey).
- **Pain at 24h**: pooled SMD = −0.15 (95% CI −0.28 to −0.03, p<0.05), I² = 0% (5 RCTs pooled from Mekhdieva 2021, Monteiro 2022, Eren 2022, Supare 2024).
- **Pain at 48h**: pooled SMD = −0.29 (95% CI −0.42 to −0.16, p<0.001), I² = 0% (4 RCTs pooled from Sponchiado 2021, Monteiro 2022, Supare 2024).
- **Pain at 6h**: SMD = −0.12 (95% CI −0.38 to 0.14) — slightly favors bioceramic, GRADE low certainty.
- **Pain at 72h/7 days**: SMD = −0.03 (95% CI −0.16 to 0.10) — no difference, GRADE moderate certainty.
- **Analgesic consumption (24h)**: RR = 0.91 (95% CI 0.78–1.06) — no difference, GRADE low certainty.
- **Flare-up incidence**: RR = 0.97 (95% CI 0.74–1.26) — no difference, GRADE low certainty.
- **Evidence overlap**: CCA ≈ 11.7% average across the 7 reviews (moderate — each review contributed largely independent primary data).
- **AMSTAR-2 quality**: 1 review high quality, 4 moderate quality, 1 high risk of bias (no explicit study-selection/extraction methodology).
- **Meta-regression**: no significant moderator effects for visit protocol, pulp status, or pain-assessment scale (all p>0.3) at 24h, 48h, or the composite outcome.
- **Certainty ceiling**: no outcome reached GRADE-high certainty; moderate for 24h/48h/>72h pain estimates, low for 6h pain, analgesic use, and flare-up incidence.
- **Bottom line**: bioceramic sealers offer a statistically significant but clinically negligible early (≤48h) pain reduction; no advantage persists beyond 48h or extends to analgesic use/flare-ups.
