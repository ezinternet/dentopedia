## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42024590194) of two SRMAs (Khadmi 2025, n=2936 teeth across 47 trials; Faghihian 2022, n=341 across 7 trials) comparing rotary versus manual instrumentation for primary-tooth pulpectomy, focused specifically on cleaning effectiveness and postoperative pain.

Rotary instrumentation showed better obturation quality (root OR=1.66, teeth OR=2.62, voids OR=4.29) and significantly lower postoperative pain at 6h/24h/48h (OR=3.37/2.23/2.27) versus manual files in the higher-quality included review; GRADE certainty was Low for cleaning effectiveness and Moderate for postoperative pain.

Only 2 eligible SRMAs existed (low review overlap, CCA=0.15) and obturation-material heterogeneity (ZOE vs Metapex/Ca(OH)2) was not stratified, so the clinical advantage of rotary systems should be interpreted cautiously pending standardized pediatric endodontic outcome sets.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(umbrella review, PRISMA·PROSPERO CRD42024590194)로, 유치 발수(pulpectomy)에서 회전형(rotary) 대 수동(manual) 기구조작을 비교한 2편의 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(SR+MA)(Khadmi 2025, 47개 임상시험·치아 2,936개; Faghihian 2022, 7개 임상시험·341개)을 세정효율(cleaning effectiveness)·술후통증(postoperative pain) 두 종점 중심으로 종합했다.

회전형 기구가 충전(obturation) 질(근관 단위 OR=1.66, 치아 단위 OR=2.62, 공극(voids) OR=4.29)과 6·24·48시간 술후통증(OR=3.37/2.23/2.27, 고품질 리뷰 기준)에서 우수했으며, 근거등급(GRADE)은 세정효율 Low, 술후통증 Moderate로 평가되었다.

포함 가능한 SRMA가 단 2편(리뷰 간 중복도 CCA=0.15로 낮음)이었고 충전재(ZOE vs Metapex/Ca(OH)2) 이질성이 층화분석되지 않아, 표준화된 소아 근관치료 결과지표 세트가 확립되기 전까지는 회전형 기구의 임상적 우위를 신중히 해석해야 한다.

## Summary

This umbrella review asked a narrow, clinically pointed question: among rotary vs manual instrumentation in primary-tooth pulpectomy, what does the highest-level systematic evidence say specifically about cleaning effectiveness and postoperative pain — the two outcomes most directly tied to procedural success and patient (child) comfort? A PRISMA-guided, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42024590194) search across six databases plus grey literature (through 2025-05-26) screened 287 records down to just 2 eligible SRMAs (Khadmi et al. 2025 and Faghihian et al. 2022), both meta-analyzing RCTs on primary teeth. Because of clinical and statistical heterogeneity between the two reviews, no new meta-analysis was performed by the umbrella review itself; instead, findings were synthesized narratively and appraised with AMSTAR-2 (methodological quality of each included SRMA) and GRADE (certainty of evidence per outcome), plus a corrected covered area (CCA) calculation to quantify how much the two reviews' primary studies overlapped.

The higher-quality review (Khadmi et al., AMSTAR-2 High) found rotary instrumentation associated with better obturation quality (root-level OR=1.66, tooth-level OR=2.62, fewer voids OR=4.29) and significantly reduced postoperative pain at 6h, 24h, and 48h (ORs 3.37, 2.23, 2.27 respectively, all favoring rotary/pain-absence). The lower-quality review (Faghihian et al., AMSTAR-2 Low) found rotary produced more optimum fills (OR=3.53) but did not significantly reduce over- or underfilling risk, and did not assess pain at all. GRADE certainty landed at Low for cleaning effectiveness (driven by inconsistency and imprecision across just 2 studies) and Moderate for postoperative pain (based on the single Khadmi et al. RCT-pooled estimate, with no serious limitations flagged on the assessed domains).

A recurring theme in the discussion is that obturation material — chiefly ZOE, with Metapex (iodoform-Ca(OH)2) and ZOE+Ca(OH)2 combinations appearing in a subset of underlying trials — was never stratified as a variable in either included SRMA, despite plausibly confounding both the cleaning-quality and pain outcomes attributed to instrumentation type. The authors also note that CCA=0.15 nominally suggests "low overlap," but in practice all 7 Faghihian-included primary studies were a subset of Khadmi's 47, meaning the two reviews are not fully independent confirmations of the same signal.

## Key Contributions

- Narrows a previously broad umbrella-review question (Patnana et al.'s general rotary-vs-manual pediatric endodontic outcomes) down to the two outcomes most directly tied to clinical success and patient comfort: cleaning effectiveness and postoperative pain.
- Layers AMSTAR-2 (review-level methodological quality), GRADE (outcome-level evidence certainty), and CCA (primary-study overlap between included reviews) together — a more rigorous triangulated appraisal than a single-tool umbrella review.
- Explicitly surfaces obturation-material heterogeneity (ZOE vs Metapex vs combinations) as an unstratified confound in the underlying trial base, flagging a concrete direction for future primary research and secondary analysis.

## Methodology

- PRISMA-guided umbrella review of systematic reviews, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42024590194).
- Search: PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, Google Scholar, OpenGrey; final search executed 2025-05-26; filtered to English, SR/MA study type, human studies.
- PICOS eligibility: children undergoing primary-tooth pulpectomy; rotary vs manual/hand instrumentation; outcomes = cleaning effectiveness (via obturation quality/void presence) and postoperative pain (6h/12h/24h intervals); design = SRMAs of RCTs on primary teeth only (excluded in vitro studies, narrative reviews, permanent-tooth studies).
- Screening via Rayyan by two independent reviewers (title/abstract → full text), disagreements resolved by a third reviewer; standardized Excel data extraction.
- AMSTAR-2 (16 domains, 7 critical) rated each included SRMA's methodological confidence; GRADE (5 domains: risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias) rated certainty of evidence per outcome.
- Planned quantitative meta-analysis was abandoned due to heterogeneity across the 2 included SRMAs; a CCA heatmap quantified primary-study overlap between them instead.

## Results

- **Study flow**: 287 records → 22 duplicates removed, 51 auto-excluded → 214 screened → 42 full-text assessed → 2 SRMAs included.
- **Khadmi et al. 2025** (47 trials, n=2,936 teeth, age 3–12y; AMSTAR-2 High): Cleaning effectiveness — Root OR=1.66 (95% CI 1.25–2.21, p=0.0005), Teeth OR=2.62 (95% CI 2.00–3.44, p<0.00001), Voids OR=4.29 (95% CI 2.34–7.87, p<0.00001), all favoring rotary. Postoperative pain (pain-absence, favoring rotary) — 6h OR=3.37 (95% CI 2.40–4.74), 24h OR=2.23 (95% CI 1.40–3.54), 48h OR=2.27 (95% CI 1.29–3.99). No significant difference in clinical success at 3/6 months.
- **Faghihian et al. 2022** (7 trials, n=341, age 3–9y; AMSTAR-2 Low): Optimum obturation OR=3.53 (95% CI 1.79–6.97) favoring rotary; overfilling OR=0.58 and underfilling OR=0.45, both non-significant. Rotary reduced instrumentation time by 1.79 minutes vs manual. Postoperative pain not assessed. All trials at high risk of bias (no allocation concealment).
- **Review overlap**: CCA=0.15; all 7 Faghihian primary studies were also captured within Khadmi et al.'s 47.
- **GRADE certainty**: Cleaning effectiveness = Low (serious inconsistency + imprecision, only 2 studies). Postoperative pain = Moderate (1 study, Khadmi et al., no serious limitations on assessed domains).
- Obturation materials were predominantly ZOE, with Metapex and ZOE+Ca(OH)2 combinations in a subset of trials — not stratified in either SRMA's analysis, per the umbrella review's discussion.
