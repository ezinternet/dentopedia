## Three-line Summary
Retrospective chart review of 1031 teeth (2355 canals) treated by postgraduate students using ProTaper Universal over 5 years at a Brazilian reference center.
Overall fracture incidence was 4.4% per tooth and 1.9% per canal; mandibular arch had significantly more fractures (66.7% vs 33.3%, P<0.01) and apical third accounted for 84.4% of fractures.
Mandibular molars showed highest rates (8.8–9.6%); operator experience (once-monthly postgraduate training) was the key driver vs prior lower rates in full-time residency settings.

## Summary
This retrospective clinical study evaluated ProTaper Universal instrument fracture incidence across 1031 teeth (2355 canals) treated by postgraduate endodontic students at a Brazilian university reference center over a 5-year period. The overall fracture rate was 4.4% per tooth and 1.9% per canal—substantially higher than rates reported in studies with full-time residents (Wolcott 2.4%, Di Fiore 0.41%). Fractures were significantly concentrated in the mandibular arch (66.7% vs 33.3%, P<0.01) and the apical third of canals (84.4% vs 15.6%, P<0.01). Mandibular first and second molars had the highest tooth-level incidence (8.8% and 9.6%), with 80% of mandibular molar fractures occurring in mesial roots. The authors attribute the elevated fracture rates primarily to operator inexperience, as Brazilian postgraduate students treat endodontic cases only once monthly compared to full-time US residency training. Limitations include the retrospective design, single-center population, conventional (non-heat-treated) NiTi only, and lack of operator experience quantification.

## Key Contributions
- Largest single-system retrospective fracture incidence dataset for ProTaper Universal (1031 teeth, 2355 canals)
- Statistically confirmed mandibular arch predominance (66.7%, P<0.01) and apical-third predominance (84.4%, P<0.01)
- Identified operator training frequency (once-monthly postgraduate vs full-time residency) as key explanatory variable for inter-study rate discrepancies
- Zero fractures in coronal third; 93.3% of all fractures in molars

## Methodology
- **Design**: Retrospective chart/radiograph review
- **Population**: 1031 teeth, 2355 canals treated by postgraduate endodontic students
- **Period**: March 2009 – March 2014 (5 years)
- **Setting**: Single Brazilian university reference center (Universidade Estadual de Londrina)
- **Instrument**: ProTaper Universal (conventional NiTi) — SX, S1, S2, F1–F5
- **Motor**: X-Smart (Dentsply Maillefer), 16:1 handpiece, 250 rpm, manufacturer torque limits
- **Glide path**: Manual Flexo-file #15
- **Instrument reuse**: Maximum 4 uses; discarded if worn/fractured/defects at 4× magnification; complex/severe curvature = single-use
- **Statistics**: Fisher's exact test, significance level α=0.01
