## Three-line Summary
Prospective cohort study at Fırat University (Turkey) evaluating instrument fracture in 2,168 teeth treated by 66 fourth-year, 72 fifth-year, and 16 postgraduate students using K-files and Endoart Expert Gold reciprocating NiTi files.
NiTi reciprocating files fractured at ~3× the rate of hand files (59 vs 20 fractures); overall fracture rate 3.6% (79/2,168), highest in molars (6.9%) and apical third (72.1%); no significant difference by operator education level (p=0.051).
Curvature strongly predicts fracture (moderate 44.3%, severe 38% of fractures); apical fractures mostly left in situ (84.2%), coronal fractures mostly retrieved (66.7%); single-institution student operator cohort limits generalizability.

## 세줄요약
피라트 대학교(튀르키예) 전향적 코호트 연구로, 4학년·5학년·대학원생이 K-파일과 Endoart Expert Gold 왕복 NiTi 파일을 사용해 2,168개 치아 치료 시 기구 파절 발생률을 조사함.
NiTi 왕복 파일이 수기 파일 대비 약 3배 더 많이 파절(59 vs 20); 전체 파절률 3.6%(79/2,168), 대구치(6.9%)와 근단 3분의 1(72.1%)에서 최다; 술자 교육 수준 간 유의한 차이 없음(p=0.051).
만곡도가 파절 강력 예측(중등도 44.3%, 심한 38%); 근단 파절은 주로 방치(84.2%), 치관부는 주로 회수(66.7%); 단일 기관 학생 술자 코호트로 일반화 제한됨.

## Summary
This prospective study at Fırat University Faculty of Dentistry (Turkey) investigated the prevalence and management of endodontic instrument fracture across three operator education levels: fourth-year students (n=66), fifth-year students (n=72), and postgraduate residents (n=16). A total of 2,168 permanent teeth undergoing primary endodontic treatment were included (October 2022–February 2023). All operators used Mani K-files for hand instrumentation and Endoart Expert Gold #25 reciprocating NiTi files (heat-treated) with an Endoart WISMY endomotor. Postgraduates established a manual glide path with #10/15 K-files before rotary use, while undergraduates prepared to #20 K-file apically before switching to the reciprocating file. Canal curvature was measured via the Schneider method on periapical radiographs using AutoCAD. The overall instrument fracture rate was 3.6% (79/2,168), with NiTi reciprocating files fracturing at approximately three times the rate of hand files (59 vs 20). Fracture rates did not differ significantly by education level (p=0.051), though postgraduates treated more cases per operator (75.3 vs 8.2 vs 5.7) with a numerically lower fracture rate. Molars had a significantly higher fracture rate (6.9%) than premolars (2.2%) or incisors (0.9%). The apical third was the most common fracture site (72.1%). Curvature was a strong predictor: moderate (44.3%) and severe (38%) curvatures accounted for the majority of fractures. Fracture management varied by level: apical fragments were predominantly left in situ (84.2%), middle third fragments were mostly left (63.2%) or bypassed (31.6%), while coronal fragments were mostly retrieved (66.7%). As curvature severity increased, the likelihood of leaving the fragment increased (mild 50% left → severe 96.7% left → ultra-severe 100% left).

## Key Contributions
- Largest prospective dataset (2,168 teeth) on instrument fracture across predoctoral and postgraduate education levels in a single clinical setting
- Direct head-to-head comparison of reciprocating heat-treated NiTi (Endoart Expert Gold) vs hand K-file fracture rates under identical clinical conditions
- Fracture management decisions stratified by fracture level (coronal/middle/apical) and canal curvature severity using standardized Schneider measurement
- Quantification of the "curvature-fracture-management" triad: as curvature increases, fracture risk rises and retrieval feasibility drops sharply

## Methodology
- **Design**: Prospective cohort study (ethical approval 2022/12-29, Fırat University)
- **Period**: October 2022 – February 2023
- **Population**: 2,168 permanent teeth (primary endodontic treatment only; retreatments and primary teeth excluded)
- **Operators**: 66 fourth-year students (373 teeth), 72 fifth-year students (590 teeth), 16 postgraduate residents (1,205 teeth)
- **Instruments**: Hand K-files #10/15/#20 (Mani); Reciprocating NiTi Endoart Expert Gold #25 (İnci Dental, heat-treated) driven by Endoart WISMY endomotor
- **Protocol**: Postgraduates — manual glide path #10/15 K-file → Endoart Expert Gold #25; Undergraduates — apical preparation to #20 K-file → Endoart Expert Gold #25; balanced force technique for manual instrumentation
- **Curvature assessment**: Schneider method on periapical radiographs via AutoCAD; categories: mild (<10°), moderate (≥10–<25°), severe (≥25–<45°), ultra-severe (≥45°)
- **Fracture localization**: Dental operating microscope (Zumax)
- **Retrieval method**: Ultrasonic tips
- **Outcomes**: Fracture incidence by operator level, tooth type, arch, canal, fracture level, curvature; management method (retrieval, bypass, left in situ)

## Results

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| **Total fractures** | 79 (3.6%) — 59 NiTi reciprocating, 20 hand files |
| **Fracture rate by education** (p=0.051) | Postgraduate: 3.2% (38/1,205); 4th year: 2.4% (9/373); 5th year: 5.4% (32/590) |
| **Fracture rate by arch** (p=0.922) | Maxilla: 3.6% (39); Mandible: 3.7% (40) |
| **Fracture rate by tooth type** (p<0.001) | Incisor: 0.9% (6/654); Premolar: 2.2% (15/677); **Molar: 6.9% (58/837)** |
| **Fracture level** | Apical: 72.1%; Middle: 19.0%; Coronal: 8.9% |
| **NiTi vs hand file fracture ratio** | NiTi reciprocating ~3× hand file rate |
| **Management by fracture level** (p<0.001) | Apical: left 84.2%, bypass 15.8%, retrieval 0%; Middle: left 63.2%, bypass 31.6%, retrieval 5.3%; Coronal: retrieval 66.7%, bypass 33.3%, left 0% |
| **Management by curvature** (p=0.028) | Mild: bypass 41.7%, left 50%; Moderate: left 65.7%, bypass 28.6%; Severe: left 96.7%, bypass 3.3%; Ultra-severe: left 100% |
| **Curvature distribution of fractured cases** | Moderate 44.3%, Severe 38%, Mild/Ultra-severe remainder |
| **Mean broken fragment length** | Incisor 2.42 mm, Premolar 3.4 mm, Molar 3.63 mm |
| **Cases referred to specialist clinic** | 20 cases from 4th/5th year students |
| **Cases per operator (mean)** | Postgraduate: 75.3; 5th year: 8.2; 4th year: 5.7 |
