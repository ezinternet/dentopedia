## Three-line Summary
Ex-vivo study using single-rooted extracted teeth inoculated with E. faecalis, comparing apical bacterial extrusion during glide-path preparation with three different file kinematics: manual stainless-steel K-file, One G continuous rotary, and WaveOne Gold Glider reciprocation.

All three glide-path techniques produced measurable apical bacterial extrusion beyond the apical foramen, demonstrating that even the glide-path stage (before main shaping) carries a biological cost relevant to postoperative flare-up risk.

The study complements geometric transportation data by adding the biological dimension, but is limited to single-species E. faecalis, single-rooted teeth, and no in vivo flare-up endpoint.

## 세줄요약
E. faecalis 접종 단근치(Single-rooted Teeth) 발치치아 ex-vivo 연구 — 수기 K-file, One G 연속 회전형, WaveOne Gold Glider 왕복형 3가지 글라이드패스 기구별 치근단 세균 밀출(Apical Bacterial Extrusion) 비교.

세 가지 글라이드패스 기법 모두 치근단 공(Apical Foramen) 너머로 측정 가능한 세균 밀출 발생 — 주 성형 이전 단계(글라이드패스)에서도 생물학적 비용이 존재하며 술후 flare-up 위험과 연관됨.

기하학적 근관 이동(Transportation) 연구의 생물학적 보완 근거이나, 단균종·단근치·in vivo flare-up 결과 없음의 한계.

## Summary
Comparative ex-vivo study quantifying apical bacterial extrusion after glide path preparation with three different kinematics: manual stainless-steel K-file, continuous-rotary One G, and reciprocating WaveOne Gold Glider. All techniques produced measurable apical extrusion of E. faecalis, with relevance to flare-up risk and post-operative pain.

## Key Contributions
- Quantifies the biological cost (apical bacterial extrusion) of the glide-path stage
- Compares three different file kinematics (manual vs continuous rotary vs reciprocation)
- Complements geometric transportation studies that ignore the biological dimension

## Methodology
- Design: ex-vivo experimental
- Specimens: single-rooted extracted teeth inoculated with E. faecalis
- Three glide-path groups (manual K, One G, WaveOne Gold Glider)
- Outcome: number of bacteria extruded beyond the apical foramen

## Results
- All instrumentation techniques produced measurable apical extrusion
- Specific between-group values and significance: see PDF tables

## Limitations
- Single bacterial species (E. faecalis) — multi-species biofilm not modeled
- Single-rooted teeth; multi-rooted molars not represented
- No in vivo flare-up endpoint
