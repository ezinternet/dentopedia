## Three-line Summary
Prospective clinical study by 3 calibrated specialists treating 750 maxillary and mandibular molars (2691 root canals) with WaveOne Gold single-file reciprocating system over 12 months.
Fracture incidence data reported for 1104 WaveOne Gold instruments used (38 small, 750 primary, 228 medium, 88 large) in canals with curvature <45°.
WaveOne Gold heat-treated reciprocating system shows low fracture incidence in experienced hands for single-session molar treatments with moderate curvature.

## Summary
This prospective clinical study evaluated the fracture incidence of WaveOne Gold reciprocating single-file instruments during root canal treatment of maxillary and mandibular molars. Three experienced and calibrated specialists treated 750 molars (2691 canals) with curvatures less than 45° over a 12-month period, using a standardized single-session protocol. A total of 1104 WaveOne Gold instruments were used across all cases. The study provides valuable real-world clinical data on the fracture resistance of heat-treated (gold-wire technology) NiTi instruments in molar teeth when used by experienced operators.

## Key Contributions
- First prospective clinical study reporting fracture incidence of WaveOne Gold files in molar teeth
- Large clinical sample: 750 molars, 2691 canals, 1104 instruments over 12 months
- Standardized protocol by 3 calibrated specialists minimizes operator variability
- Single-session treatment protocol reflecting modern clinical practice
- Clear curvature inclusion criteria (<45° by Schneider method) for reproducibility

## Methodology
- **Design**: Prospective clinical study
- **Operators**: 3 experienced, calibrated endodontic specialists
- **Sample**: 750 maxillary and mandibular molars (2691 root canals)
- **Inclusion criteria**: Canal curvature <45° (Schneider method)
- **Intervention**: WaveOne Gold single-file reciprocating system
- **Protocol**: Single-session treatments, standardized technique
- **Instruments used**: 1104 total (38 small, 750 primary, 228 medium, 88 large)
- **Follow-up**: 12 months
