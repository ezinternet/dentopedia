## Three-line Summary
In vitro cyclic fatigue trial (n=240, 80 per type) testing ProTaper Universal (conventional NiTi), ProTaper Next (M-wire), and ProTaper Gold (gold-wire heat-treated) under four sterilization protocols (as received; 10× pre-test autoclave; 25/50/75% life partial use then autoclave; same use without autoclave) with one-way ANOVA + Tukey post hoc.

At baseline, ProTaper Gold outperformed Universal and Next (p<0.05); 10 pre-test autoclave cycles significantly increased number of cycles to failure (NCF) for both PTN and PTG; autoclaving a fresh ProTaper Gold was significantly better than autoclaving a partially used one for NCF.

The findings confirm heat-treated alloys (M-wire, gold-wire) benefit from autoclaving and provide a within-brand generational comparison directly applicable to sterilization and single-use vs multi-use policy decisions.

## 세줄요약
ProTaper Universal(기존 NiTi), ProTaper Next(M-wire), ProTaper Gold(gold-wire 열처리) 3세대 비교 in vitro 주기적 피로 시험(군당 n=80; 4가지 멸균 프로토콜; one-way ANOVA + Tukey 사후검정).

기준치에서 PTG > PTN > PTU 피로 저항성(p<0.05); 10회 사전 고압증기멸균은 PTN·PTG 모두 파절 전 회전수(NCF) 유의하게 증가; 새 기구 멸균이 부분 사용 후 멸균보다 PTG NCF 높음.

열처리 합금(M-wire, gold-wire)은 멸균으로 강화되며, 단일 사용 vs 재사용 정책과 멸균 순서(사용 전 멸균이 유리)에 직접 적용 가능한 동일 브랜드 세대별 근거 제공.

## Summary
Head-to-head comparison of three ProTaper generations under four sterilization protocols. ProTaper Gold (gold-wire) was the most fatigue-resistant out of the box. Autoclaving (10 cycles) before testing significantly raised the number of cycles to failure for both PTN and PTG. When files were pre-fatigued and then autoclaved, PTN still gained NCF compared to its non-sterilized counterpart.

## Key Contributions
- Within-brand generational comparison (Universal → Next → Gold)
- Quantifies the strengthening effect of autoclaving on heat-treated NiTi
- Tests sterilization timing (before use vs after partial use)

## Methodology
- Design: in vitro
- Sample: 240 files (80 per type) × 4 subgroups × 20
- Subgroups: (1) as received, (2) 10× pre-test autoclave, (3) 25%/50%/75% NCF use + autoclave then fatigue test, (4) same use without autoclave
- Statistics: one-way ANOVA + Tukey post hoc; α=0.05

## Results
- Baseline (G1): PTG > PTU and PTN (p<0.05)
- 10× pre-autoclave (G2): NCF significantly increased for PTN and PTG (p<0.05)
- Partial use then autoclave (G3) vs no autoclave (G4): PTN G3 > G4 (p<0.05)
- PTG G2 > G3 and G4 (p<0.05): autoclaving a fresh file is better than autoclaving a partially used file

## Limitations
- Single canal geometry
- No correlation with clinical fracture rate
- ProTaper-only — other heat-treated systems out of scope
