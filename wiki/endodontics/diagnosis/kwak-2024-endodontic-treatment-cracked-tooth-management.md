## Three-line Summary

Narrative practitioner review (KDA Journal 2024, Korean) addressing the indications and timing of endodontic treatment in cracked tooth management, based on author clinical experience and literature.

The central algorithm is staged: assess fracture extent, place a temporary restoration, re-evaluate symptoms, then decide on root canal treatment versus final prosthesis versus extraction — avoiding premature or unnecessary RCT.

Many cracked teeth require no endodontic intervention if symptoms resolve after stabilization, positioning this review as a decision framework to reduce over-treatment in cracked tooth syndrome.

## 세줄요약

균열치 관리에서 근관치료의 적응증과 시점을 다룬 KDA 한국어 임상 총설(2024)로, 저자의 임상 경험 및 문헌에 근거한다.

핵심 알고리즘은 단계별 접근: 균열 범위 평가 → 임시 cementation → 증상 재평가 → 근관치료·최종 보철·발거 결정 순으로, 불필요한 RCT를 회피하는 것이 목표다.

임시 고정 후 증상이 소실되는 균열치는 근관치료 없이 관리 가능하며, 이 총설은 균열치 증후군에서 과잉치료를 줄이기 위한 의사결정 프레임워크를 제공한다.

## Summary
Narrative practitioner-oriented review (Korean Dental Association) on the decision logic for endodontic treatment in cracked tooth management. Emphasizes a staged approach to avoid premature/unnecessary RCT.

## Key Contributions
Clarifies that "cracked tooth = endo" is an overuse pattern; offers a staged decision algorithm via temporary cementation as a diagnostic step.

## Methodology
Narrative clinical commentary based on author experience and literature.

## Results
Staged protocol: assess fracture extent → temporary cementation → re-evaluate symptoms → decide endo vs prosthesis vs extraction. Many cases need no endo if symptoms resolve.

## Limitations
Narrative review without quantitative outcomes. Practice-pattern based.
