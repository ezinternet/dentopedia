## Three-line Summary

Case-control histological/immunohistochemical study of human periapical tissue (n=50: 25 radicular cysts [RCs], 25 periapical granulomas [PGs]; plus n=4 healthy third-molar pulp controls) from Cairo University Endodontics department.

PGs showed more severe inflammatory infiltration (median score 4) than RCs (median score 3, p<0.001); both TGF-β1 and CD68 immunoexpression were significantly higher in PGs and RCs vs control (p<0.001), and significantly higher in PGs than RCs (both p<0.001), with a negative TGF-β1–CD68 correlation in both groups (p<0.05).

Clinically, distinct TGF-β1/CD68 immunoprofiles support histology-based typing of periapical lesions and mark PGs as the more actively macrophage-driven inflammatory lesion; small control group (n=4) and cross-sectional design limit causal/temporal inference.

## Summary

AbuBakr et al. (2025) conducted a case-control histological and immunohistochemical (IHC) study comparing 25 periapical granulomas (PGs) and 25 radicular cysts (RCs), obtained during periapical surgery at Cairo University, against 4 healthy third-molar dental pulp controls. Histological scoring showed PGs had more severe inflammatory infiltration (median score 4, dominated by macrophages, plasma cells, and lymphocytes) than RCs (median score 3), with the control group showing no inflammation. IHC and morphometric quantification of TGF-β1 (형질전환성장인자-베타1) and CD68 (대식세포 표지자) demonstrated that both markers were significantly elevated in PGs and RCs relative to control pulp (p<0.001 for both), and — the key finding — significantly higher in PGs than RCs for both markers (p<0.001 for both). A significant negative correlation between TGF-β1 and CD68 expression was found within both lesion groups (much stronger in PGs, r=-0.88, than RCs, r=-0.14; p<0.05), which the authors interpret as reflecting TGF-β1's dual role — initially recruiting macrophages, then later suppressing macrophage activity as inflammation matures. The findings reinforce that PGs represent a more actively macrophage-driven inflammatory lesion than the more chronic/stable RCs, adding molecular evidence to the histopathological distinction between the two lesion types.

## Key Contributions

- Quantifies histological inflammatory severity differences between PGs (severe, median score 4) and RCs (moderate, median score 3), p<0.001.
- First study to jointly quantify and correlate TGF-β1 and CD68 area-percentage immunoexpression across PG, RC, and healthy-pulp-control groups.
- Shows both markers are significantly higher in PGs than RCs (not just higher than control), supporting a mechanistic distinction beyond simple "inflamed vs less inflamed."
- Identifies a significant negative TGF-β1–CD68 correlation, especially strong in PGs (r=-0.88), proposed to reflect a temporal shift from TGF-β1-driven macrophage recruitment to TGF-β1-driven immunosuppression.

## Methodology

Case-control design: 25 human RCs and 25 human PGs from patients (age 20–40, systemically healthy) undergoing periapical surgery for chronic periapical lesions unresponsive to routine endodontic therapy at Cairo University Faculty of Dentistry; control = healthy dental pulp from 4 patients undergoing extraction of sound third molars. Diagnosis by histopathologic + clinical/radiographic criteria (RC = nonkeratinized stratified squamous epithelial lining around a fluid-filled cavity; PG = granulomatous inflammatory tissue without epithelium). H&E staining with blinded 4-point inflammatory scoring (10 fields, ×400). IHC for TGF-β1 (clone TB21) and CD68 (clone KP1) with DAB chromogen; morphometric area-% quantification via Leica Quin 500 image analyzer (10 fields, ×400). Statistics: ANOVA + post hoc Tukey (morphometry), Kruskal-Wallis (histology scores), Pearson correlation (marker relationships), significance p≤0.05.

## Results

| Marker | Control (n=4) | RC (n=25) | PG (n=25) | p-value (across groups) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inflammatory score (median) | 1 (none) | 3 (moderate) | 4 (severe) | <0.001 |
| TGF-β1 (mean area % ± SD) | 0.142 ± 0.057 | 25.43 ± 1.335 | 52.75 ± 1.369 | <0.001 (all pairwise) |
| CD68 (mean area % ± SD) | 0.148 ± 0.066 | 11.938 ± 1.136 | 17.15 ± 1.016 | <0.001 (all pairwise) |

Pearson correlation TGF-β1 vs CD68: PGs r = -0.8803 (p<0.05), RCs r = -0.1389 (p<0.05) — negative in both groups, markedly stronger in PGs. RCs also showed positive TGF-β1/CD68 staining in the cyst-lining epithelial cells themselves, not just stroma.
