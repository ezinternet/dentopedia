## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42023460388; PRISMA 2020) of 39 studies (49 reports, 2,215 patients, 2,305 teeth; searches to June 2024) on intentional replantation (IR) — deliberate extraction, extraoral treatment, and reinsertion of a tooth — for teeth with periapical periodontitis, assessing overall prognosis and prognostic factors via the QUIPS tool.

Pooled success rate was 0.78 (95% CI 0.70–0.84) and pooled survival rate 0.89 (95% CI 0.85–0.93) — but **31 of the 39 included studies were judged high risk of overall bias and the other 8 moderate; not a single study was low risk**, and every outcome carried GRADE certainty of low or very low. The 89% figure also hides a real time-decay: annual pooled survival was 0.98 at 3 months, 0.91 at 1 year, and only 0.63 at 8 years.

Two prognostic factors reached statistical significance: age (survival rate ratio, SRR, 0.73, 95% CI 0.53–0.99, p=0.048 — younger patients, <25y, had higher survival, but the CI nearly touches 1, a fragile signal) and combined periodontal-endodontic lesions (CPEL) (SRR 0.80, 95% CI 0.67–0.95, p=0.001 — CPEL lowered survival, a firmer and mechanistically coherent signal, since CPEL means the periodontal attachment IR depends on is already compromised). This review does not compare IR against extraction plus implant — it is explicitly framed as "a final strategy to preserve teeth," and that comparison should not be read into the numbers.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review, SR)+메타분석(Meta-Analysis, MA)(PROSPERO CRD42023460388; PRISMA 2020) — 근단치주염(periapical periodontitis) 치아에 대한 의도적 재식(Intentional Replantation, IR: 발치 후 구강외 처치·재식립)의 전체 예후 및 예후인자를 다룬 연구 39편(보고서 49편, 환자 2,215명, 치아 2,305개; 2024년 6월까지 검색)을 QUIPS 도구로 편향위험 평가하며 종합.

통합 성공률 0.78(95% 신뢰구간(Confidence Interval, CI) 0.70–0.84), 통합 생존율 0.89(95% CI 0.85–0.93)였으나 **39편 중 31편이 전체 편향위험 high risk, 나머지 8편도 moderate — low risk는 단 한 편도 없었고**, 모든 결과의 GRADE 근거확실성이 low 또는 very low였다. 89% 수치의 이면엔 실제 시간에 따른 하락이 있다: 연간 통합 생존율 3개월 0.98 → 1년 0.91 → 8년 0.63.

통계적으로 유의한 예후인자는 두 가지: 연령(생존율비(Survival Rate Ratio, SRR) 0.73, 95% CI 0.53–0.99, p=0.048 — 25세 미만이 생존율 높음, 단 CI가 1에 근접해 취약한 신호)과 치주-치수 복합병변(Combined Periodontal-Endodontic Lesion, CPEL)(SRR 0.80, 95% CI 0.67–0.95, p=0.001 — CPEL 존재 시 생존율 저하, 재식이 의존하는 치주 부착 자체가 이미 손상된 병변이므로 기전적으로도 타당한 더 확실한 신호). 본 리뷰는 IR을 발치+임플란트와 비교하지 않는다 — "치아를 보존하기 위한 최종 전략(final strategy)"으로 명시적으로 규정되었을 뿐, 그 비교를 수치에 덧입혀 읽으면 안 된다.

## Summary

According to PubMed, this systematic review and meta-analysis ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-025-06583-1); PMID 40713650; PMCID PMC12297747) by Zhang and colleagues at Sichuan University's West China Hospital of Stomatology set out to quantify the prognosis of intentional replantation (IR) — extraction, extraoral treatment, and reinsertion of a tooth into its original socket — specifically for teeth with periapical periodontitis. IR sits at the extraction threshold: it is indicated when nonsurgical root canal therapy, retreatment, or apical surgery have failed or are contraindicated, and it is explicitly framed by the authors as "a final strategy to preserve teeth" rather than a routine option.

Searching ten databases and two trial registers to June 2024 (nine databases are explicitly named in the Methods text, against "ten" stated in the Abstract — a minor inconsistency in the source article), the authors identified 39 eligible studies (49 reports; 2,215 patients, 2,305 teeth), predominantly one-armed retrospective cohorts (24) and case series (8), from China (13), Korea (9), Japan (4), and elsewhere. Meta-analysis using random-effects models produced a pooled success rate of 0.78 (95% CI 0.70–0.84) and pooled survival rate of 0.89 (95% CI 0.85–0.93). **These headline numbers must be read together with the quality appraisal: 31 of the 39 studies were judged high risk of overall bias by the QUIPS tool, the remaining 8 moderate, and none low risk** — every pooled estimate in this review rests entirely on moderate-to-high-risk evidence, and GRADE certainty was rated low or very low for every outcome, including the two statistically significant prognostic factors.

A second caveat sits inside the headline survival figure itself: the review's own annual breakdown shows pooled survival declining from 0.98 at 3 months to 0.91 at 1 year to just 0.63 at 8 years — meaning the single pooled 89% estimate aggregates across highly variable follow-up durations and should not be read as "89% of replanted teeth remain in the mouth long-term." Of eight tested prognostic factors, two reached significance: younger age (<25y, SRR 0.73, 95% CI 0.53–0.99, p=0.048, a marginal finding whose CI nearly crosses 1) and absence of combined periodontal-endodontic lesions (CPEL) (SRR 0.80, 95% CI 0.67–0.95, p=0.001, a firmer finding drawn from only 2 high-risk-of-bias studies). Sinus tract, jaw position, probing pocket depth, extraoral time, splint method, and root-end filling material showed no significant association with survival. The authors explicitly call for standardized IR protocols and large-sample cohort studies, and note the absence of such cohorts as a key limitation of the current evidence base.

## Key Contributions

- **First page in this wiki on intentional replantation (IR)** — filling a gap directly relevant to treatment-planning decisions at the extraction threshold: what else can be tried before extraction and implant placement.
- **Largest and most methodologically rigorous synthesis of IR prognosis to date** for periapical periodontitis: 39 studies, 2,305 teeth, with formal QUIPS risk-of-bias appraisal and GRADE certainty rating — features the authors note prior IR systematic reviews lacked (one included zero studies due to overly strict inclusion criteria; another analyzed success rate only with no prognostic-factor analysis; a third had prognostic factors but no proper statistical analysis).
- **Distinguishes success from survival as separate pooled outcomes** (0.78 vs 0.89) rather than conflating "the tooth is still there" with "the tooth actually healed" — the gap between the two is the fraction of replanted teeth that survive without full periapical/periodontal healing.
- **Surfaces a real time-decay hidden inside the pooled survival figure**: 0.98 at 3 months → 0.91 at 1 year → 0.63 at 8 years, meaning the commonly quoted ~89% survival substantially overstates what should be expected at longer follow-up.
- **Quantifies two prognostic factors with explicit fragility labeling**: age (marginal, very-low certainty) and CPEL (firmer, low certainty, but derived from only 2 high-risk studies) — both downgraded by the authors' own GRADE assessment.
- **The risk-of-bias finding is itself the most important result**: 31/39 high risk, 8/39 moderate, 0/39 low risk — a literature base for which no low-risk study currently exists.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis of prognostic studies. PICOS — Population: periapical periodontitis (incl. abscess/granuloma); Intervention: intentional replantation; Comparator: any or none; Outcomes: success/survival at various times, prognostic factors, complications; Study designs: RCTs, nonrandomized cohort studies (prospective/retrospective), case-control, case series ≥10 teeth.
- **Exclusions**: in vitro/animal studies, case reports <10 teeth, deciduous teeth, teeth with vertical root fracture/developmental groove, follow-up <6 months, abstract/protocol-only reports, ongoing studies.
- **Search**: to June 5, 2024, across (per Abstract) ten databases and two trial registers (the Methods text names nine databases explicitly — Ovid MEDLINE, Ovid Embase, Ovid EBM Reviews, Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, Inspec, KCI-KJD, SciELO, SinoMed-CBM — plus ICTRP and ClinicalTrials.gov). No language or date restriction; citation tracking performed.
- **Selection & extraction**: two independent reviewers with a third-reviewer arbiter for disagreements, at every stage (screening, full-text assessment, data extraction, risk-of-bias judgment, GRADE rating).
- **Risk of bias**: QUIPS (Quality In Prognosis Studies) tool, 6 domains, each rated low/moderate/high; overall rating = least-favourable domain.
- **Outcome definitions** (radiographic + clinical, explicit in the protocol): Survival = asymptomatic + radiographic lesion decreased in size. Success = asymptomatic + normal function + no attachment loss + radiographic lesion resolved with clear/continuous periodontal ligament. Ankylosis = fused root-to-bone with loss of periodontal ligament space. Failure = persistent symptoms, unchanged/enlarging radiolucency, mobility beyond grade I or uncontrolled pocketing, or inflammatory root resorption → extraction.
- **Synthesis**: Stata 18 (random-effects pooled proportions for success/survival/ankylosis); RevMan 5.4 (random-effects survival rate ratio, SRR, with 95% CI for prognostic factors, where comparable outcomes existed). Subgroup analysis via chi-square (α=0.05); sensitivity analyses where feasible. Heterogeneity via Cochrane's chi-square (α=0.1) and I² statistic. Certainty via GRADE (two independent raters).

## Results

| Outcome | Pooled estimate (95% CI) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Success rate | 0.78 (0.70–0.84) | Random-effects; distinct from survival |
| Survival rate (pooled) | 0.89 (0.85–0.93) | Aggregates across all follow-up durations |
| Survival at 3 months | 0.98 | Annual breakdown behind the pooled figure |
| Survival at 1 year | 0.91 | " |
| Survival at 8 years | 0.63 | Real decline with longer follow-up |
| Ankylosis incidence | 0.38 (0.09–0.72) | Very wide CI |
| SRR — age (<25y vs older) | 0.73 (0.53–0.99), p=0.048 | GRADE: very low; CI nearly crosses 1 |
| SRR — CPEL (presence lowers survival) | 0.80 (0.67–0.95), p=0.001 | GRADE: low; only 2 studies, both high risk of bias |
| SRR — sinus tract | 0.87 (0.76–1.00), p=0.005 | Paper itself groups this as "not significant" despite p=0.005 — an internal inconsistency in the source article |
| SRR — jaw (maxillary vs mandibular) | 0.97 (0.90–1.04), p=0.35 | Not significant |
| SRR — probing pocket depth | 0.95 (0.79–1.15), p=0.63 | Not significant |
| SRR — extraoral time | 0.93 (0.78–1.10), p=0.40 | Not significant |
| SRR — splint method | 1.08 (0.66–1.78), p=0.75 | Not significant |
| SRR — root-end filling material | 1.03 (0.81–1.31), p=0.81 | Not significant; high heterogeneity in material choice |
| Risk of bias (QUIPS, overall) | 31/39 high, 8/39 moderate, 0/39 low | No low-risk study exists in this literature |

**Bottom line**: IR shows a high pooled success/survival rate on paper, but that evidence rests entirely on moderate-to-high-risk-of-bias studies with low/very-low GRADE certainty, and the pooled 89% survival figure understates the decline seen at longer follow-up (63% by 8 years). Of the two statistically significant prognostic factors, older age is a fragile signal and combined periodontal-endodontic lesions (CPEL) a firmer one. The review does not compare IR to extraction and implant placement.
