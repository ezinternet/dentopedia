## Summary
This systematic review and meta-analysis examined outcomes of regenerative endodontic procedures (REPs) across varying degrees of root maturity using traditional meta-analysis, dose-response meta-analysis (DRMA), and trial sequential analysis (TSA). Ten studies (508 teeth) were included. Pooled clinical success was 90% and tooth survival 98% — both robust across the full range of apical diameters. However, complete apical closure occurred in only 53% and pulp vitality recovery in only 21% of cases, with substantial heterogeneity. Exploratory DRMA suggested a negative association between apical diameter and closure rate, while clinical success remained stable regardless of maturity. A key interpretive finding: high clinical success and survival should not be equated with true pulp-dentin regeneration, as most REPs achieve periapical healing rather than pulp tissue restoration.

## Key Contributions
- REP clinical success (90%) and tooth survival (98%) are reliable across all apical maturity levels — supports broad indications for REP
- Apical closure (53%) and pulp vitality recovery (21%) are far lower and highly variable — genuine biological regeneration is the exception, not the rule
- DRMA: larger apical diameter associated with lower apical closure rate (~70% → ~30% across diameter range); clinical success unaffected
- TSA confirms sufficient evidence for clinical success and survival; apical closure and vitality remain underpowered for definitive conclusions
- Standardization of vitality assessment methods (cold test, EPT, laser Doppler) is urgently needed for cross-study comparability

## Methodology
Systematic review + meta-analysis (DerSimonian-Laird random effects) + DRMA (2-stage GLS, restricted cubic splines, 3 knots) + TSA. Databases: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Scopus, Cochrane CENTRAL (to July 2025). Inclusion: permanent teeth with necrotic pulps, REP of any type, ≥5 teeth, any study design except in vitro/animal. Quality: RoB 2.0 for RCTs (generally low risk), NOS 5–8/9 for observational studies. Registration: INPLASY202640005.

## Results
| Outcome | n teeth | Pooled rate | 95% CI | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical success | 496 | 90% | 83–95% | 40.3% |
| Tooth survival | 453 | 98% | 95–99% | 0% |
| Complete apical closure | 196 | 53% | 36–70% | 79.6% |
| Pulp vitality recovery | 224 | 21% | 7–49% | 69.2% |

DRMA findings:
- **Clinical success**: stable >80% across all apical diameters; no significant dose-response
- **Apical closure**: approximately 70% at narrow diameters → ~30% at wider diameters (exploratory, few data points)
- **Pulp vitality**: insufficient data for reliable dose-response modelling
- Subgroup by material (exploratory): blood clot ~90% regardless of diameter; FGF showed steeper decline with wider apex
