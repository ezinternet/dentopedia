## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD42019124402, Iran Endod J 2022) pooling 20 systematic reviews (13 descriptive SR + 7 meta-analyses, AMSTAR-2 assessed) on regenerative endodontic therapy in necrotic, immature-root permanent teeth.

Overall success rates ranged 50–98% and survival rates 94–100%; symptom resolution reached 100% in 75% of included SRs, and dentin thickening/root length gain were reported as advantages largely exclusive to regenerative therapy over apexification.

The review's own limitation analysis flags that all 20 included SRs conflated success/survival definitions inconsistently (100% of them) and used heterogeneous outcome parameters (80%), so pooled percentages should be read as ranges, not precise point estimates.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD42019124402, Iran Endod J 2022)로 비활력·미성숙 치근 영구치에서 재생근관치료(Regenerative Endodontic Treatment, RET)에 관한 20편의 체계적 문헌고찰(기술적 SR 13편 + 메타분석 7편, AMSTAR-2 평가)을 종합.

전체 성공율은 50~98%, 생존율은 94~100%로 보고되었으며, 증상 소실은 포함 SR의 75%에서 100% 달성, 상아질벽 비후·치근 길이 증가는 근단형성술 (Apexification) 대비 RET에서 거의 배타적으로 관찰되는 이점으로 정리됨.

이 리뷰 자체의 한계 분석에서, 포함된 20편 전부(100%)가 성공/생존 정의를 불일치하게 혼용했고 80%가 이질적인 결과 지표를 사용했음을 지적하므로, 풀링된 수치는 정확한 점추정치가 아닌 범위(range)로 해석해야 한다.

## Summary

Iran Endod J 2022 umbrella review (JBI methodology, PRISMA + PROSPERO-registered). Pools 20 SRs/MAs (2012–2020 publication window) on RET effectiveness and success factors in necrotic-pulp, immature-root permanent teeth. AMSTAR-2-graded quality: 55% high, 30% moderate, 15% low. Reports overall success 50–98%, survival 94–100%, and factor-specific outcomes (symptom resolution, lesion healing, apical closure, dentin thickening, root length gain, pulp sensibility recovery). Notably quantifies methodological weaknesses shared across the 20 included SRs (e.g., inconsistent success/survival definitions in 100%).

## Key Contributions

- Umbrella-level (SR-of-SRs) synthesis combining **effectiveness** and **success-factor** evidence for RET, not just survival/success rates alone.
- AMSTAR-2 quality stratification of the 20 included SRs: 11 high (55%), 6 moderate (30%), 3 low (15%) quality.
- Structured, quantified limitations table (Table 5) — a meta-methodological contribution showing how prevalent specific SR weaknesses are across the RET literature (e.g., 100% conflate survival/success; 80% inconsistent success definitions; 65% no registered protocol).
- Clear conceptual separation of survival (tooth retained) vs success (survival + functional/health restoration), mapped against what each included SR actually measured.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Umbrella review, Joanna Briggs Institute methodology, PRISMA-compliant, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42019124402), IRB-approved (University of Antioquia).
- **PICO**: P = necrotic pulp + incomplete root development (all ages); I/C = different RET types; O = effectiveness and success factors (symptoms, root length, dentin wall thickness, apical closure, sensitivity, other clinical/demographic variables).
- **Search**: PubMed-Medline, Scopus, LILACS, EMBASE, Cochrane SR Database + Google Scholar/manual search (grey literature). No date filter; English/Portuguese/Spanish only.
- **Screening**: Two independent reviewers, third-reviewer arbitration. 398 → 309 (post-dedup) → 45 full-text → 20 included.
- **Appraisal**: PRISMA checklist + AMSTAR-2 (16 items, pilot-tested at 90% concordance); per-SR risk of bias and heterogeneity extracted where reported.
- **Synthesis**: Descriptive/narrative only — no re-pooling of primary-study data across the 20 SRs; results reported as ranges/percentages.

## Results

| Metric | Finding |
|---|---|
| Records → included | 398 → 309 (dedup) → 45 (full-text) → **20 SRs/MAs included** (2012–2020) |
| AMSTAR-2 quality | High 55% (n=11), Moderate 30% (n=6), Low 15% (n=3) |
| General success rate | **50–98%** across SRs |
| General survival rate | **94–100%** across SRs |
| Symptom resolution = 100% | 75% of SRs (15/20) |
| Lesion size reduction 50–100% | 55% of SRs (11/20) |
| Apical closure >80% | 30% of SRs (6/20) |
| Dentin wall thickening 80–100% | 30% of SRs (6/20) |
| Root length increase reported | 45% of SRs (9/20), range 0–100% |
| Pulp sensibility recovery | 10% of SRs (2/20), range 13–80% |
| Follow-up range | 1–48 months |

Comparative context from Discussion: Ca(OH)₂ apexification survival ~88%; MTA apexification survival 97–100%; RET survival 94–98% (up to 48 months). Root-length gain and dentin-wall thickening are described as near-exclusive advantages of RET over apexification (apexification success on these two parameters typically <10%).

Most common shared limitations across the 20 included SRs: survival/success definitional conflation (100%), inconsistent success parameters (80%), small original-study sample sizes (75%), no registered methodology protocol (65%), heterogeneous regeneration protocols/materials (60%).
