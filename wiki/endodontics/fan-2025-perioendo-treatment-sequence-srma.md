## One-line Summary
SR+MA (16 studies, 1,468 cases) on combined periodontal-endodontic lesions: endodontic-first treatment significantly improved probing depth (MD -1.07mm), CAL gain (+0.99mm), and tooth survival (RR 1.22) vs periodontal-first or periodontal-only approaches, though very high heterogeneity warrants cautious interpretation.

## Summary
This SR+MA evaluated the influence of treatment sequence on outcomes in combined periodontal-endodontic lesions (PELs). Sixteen studies (10 RCTs, 6 nRCTs, n=1,468) comparing endodontic-first versus periodontal-first or periodontal-only approaches were included. The endodontic-first strategy was associated with significantly greater probing depth reduction (MD -1.07 mm, p<0.0001), clinical attachment level gain (MD +0.99 mm, p<0.0001), and tooth survival/success (RR 1.22, p=0.001). Subgroup analysis revealed that region (Chinese studies showed larger effects), follow-up duration, and use of regenerative materials were major sources of heterogeneity. Caution is warranted given very high I² for both PD (98.4%) and CAL (91.5%) outcomes, and significant publication bias for PD. True combined PELs versus secondary involvement were often inconsistently classified across studies, further limiting generalizability.

## Key Contributions
- Endodontic-first approach statistically superior to periodontal-first in PD, CAL, and survival
- Chinese studies subgroup: larger effects (PD MD -1.39mm, CAL MD 1.35mm) with lower intra-group heterogeneity
- Region, follow-up, and regenerative materials: key heterogeneity sources
- Publication bias detected for PD (Egger's p=0.003); not for CAL (p=0.733)
- First SR+MA to include both English and Chinese databases for PEL treatment sequence

## Methodology
PRISMA guidelines. Databases: MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL, Web of Science, Scopus + Chinese databases (CNKI, Wanfang, Weipu) + grey literature. Included: RCTs and non-randomized controlled studies reporting PD, CAL, and tooth survival in adult PEL patients. Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2.0 (RCTs) and ROBINS-I (nRCTs). Statistical: mean difference (MD), risk ratio (RR), random-effects model; Benjamini-Hochberg correction.
