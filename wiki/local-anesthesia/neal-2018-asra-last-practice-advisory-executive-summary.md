## Three-line Summary

ASRA Third Practice Advisory on LAST (Reg Anesth Pain Med 2018; interim update from 2010 advisory) — authoritative guideline for prevention, recognition, and treatment of local anesthetic systemic toxicity (국소마취제 전신독성, LAST), covering updated ILE mechanisms, epidemiology, and clinical recommendations.

Three ILE mechanisms now recognized: rapid partitioning (lipid sink), direct inotropy, post-conditioning; trend toward delayed LAST presentation mirroring increased ultrasound guidance adoption; new risk factors — small patient size, sarcopenia; increasing events outside hospitals and among non-anesthesiologists.

Supersedes 2010/2012 advisory; updated again in 2023 (Fourth Advisory) but this 2017/2018 version remains the most widely cited in dental LAST literature through 2025.

## 세줄요약

ASRA 제3차 LAST 실무지침 (Reg Anesth Pain Med 2018; 2010 판 중간 개정) — LAST 예방·인식·치료의 권위적 가이드라인; ILE 메커니즘 3종 업데이트, 역학, 임상 권고.

ILE 메커니즘: 빠른 분배(지질 싱크) + 직접 강심 효과 + 후처리(post-conditioning); 지연 발현 추세(초음파 가이드·국소침윤·지속주입 증가 반영); 신규 위험인자: 소체형·근감소증; 병원 외·비마취과 의사 사례 증가.

2010/2012 판을 대체; 2023 제4차 개정판 발행되었으나 치과 LAST 문헌에서는 본 2017/2018 판이 2025년까지 가장 많이 인용됨.

## ILE Mechanisms (Updated from 2010)

### 1. Rapid Partitioning (Lipid Sink)
- Lipid droplets create a hydrophobic compartment in blood
- Lipophilic LA molecules partition into lipid phase, reducing free drug at cardiac/CNS receptors
- Well-established; efficacy correlates with drug lipophilicity (log P)

### 2. Direct Inotropy
- ILE has positive inotropic effects independent of drug sequestration
- Restores cardiac ATP production (mitochondrial direct effect)
- Reverses sodium channel blockade caused by bupivacaine
- Nitric oxide inhibition → vasoconstriction supports blood pressure

### 3. Post-Conditioning
- ILE modifies ischemia-reperfusion signaling after cardiac arrest
- GSK-3β phosphorylation → inhibits apoptosis in cardiomyocytes
- Reduces cell death during resuscitation

## Epidemiology Updates

- **Lower frequency** at some registries vs previous reports — but still significant in community practice
- **Delayed presentation** increasingly common:
  - Ultrasound guidance → fewer intravascular injections → slower toxicity onset
  - Local infiltration → slower systemic uptake
  - Continuous infusions → gradual accumulation
- **New risk factors**: small patient size, sarcopenia (reduced volume of distribution → higher effective dose per kg)
- **Setting shift**: increasing LAST outside hospitals; increasing involvement of non-anesthesiologists

## Prevention Recommendations

1. **Use minimum effective dose** — calculate by patient weight
2. **Aspiration before injection** — especially IANB (15.3% positive rate per Bakır 2026)
3. **Incremental injection** — inject in small aliquots, pause between
4. **Ultrasound guidance** where applicable — reduces intravascular placement
5. **Choose appropriate agent** — lower lipophilicity = lower cardiotoxicity risk
6. **Know patient risk factors**: extremes of age, small body size, sarcopenia, hepatic/renal impairment

## Recognition — LAST Prodrome Sequence

1. Perioral numbness, metallic taste, tinnitus
2. Dizziness, visual disturbance
3. Agitation → altered consciousness
4. Seizures
5. Cardiovascular: arrhythmia → hypotension → cardiac arrest

**Important**: delayed presentation means symptoms may appear minutes to hours after injection, not only immediately.

## Treatment Protocol (ILE)

| Step | Protocol |
|---|---|
| Immediate | Stop injection; call for help; airway management |
| ILE bolus | 20% lipid emulsion 1.5 mL/kg IV over 1 min |
| ILE infusion | 0.25 mL/kg/min × 30–60 min |
| If collapse persists | Repeat bolus × 1–2 |
| Maximum total | 10–12 mL/kg |
| ACLS | Standard ACLS; avoid propofol as ILE substitute |
| Benzodiazepines | For seizures (preferred over propofol) |

## Clinical Insights

1. **Delayed presentation** is the most important clinical update from this advisory — clinicians must monitor patients for 30+ minutes after LA injection for LAST signs, not just immediately.

2. **Sarcopenia as risk factor** has direct dental relevance — elderly edentulous patients undergoing implant placement or denture adjustment injections may have higher effective mg/kg doses than body weight alone suggests.

3. **Non-anesthesiologist setting**: dental offices are among the primary non-hospital LAST venues. This guideline's recognition of the setting shift directly calls for better LAST preparedness in dental practice.

4. **Liposomal bupivacaine**: limited data at time of this advisory — unclear LAST risk profile compared to standard bupivacaine.
