## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 16 RCTs (Front Dent Med 2025; PROSPERO CRD42025638427) comparing anesthetic protocols for symptomatic irreversible pulpitis (SIP) in mandibular teeth — the most clinically challenging anesthesia scenario in dentistry.

Modified protocols (articaine, supplemental infiltration, volume increase, additives) achieve 3.34× higher success than conventional IANB with 2% lidocaine (OR = 3.34; 95% CI: 2.49–4.48; high-quality evidence; I² = 0%); IANB outperforms buccal infiltration (OR = 4.03); 4% articaine shows highest success (OR = 4.18, moderate certainty) followed closely by 2% lidocaine (OR = 3.94, moderate certainty).

The empirical evidence that the clinical default (2% lidocaine IANB alone) is systematically suboptimal for SIP — the failure rate of 12–65% is not random noise but a systematic limitation that modified protocols overcome.

## 세줄요약

16개 RCT SR+MA (Front Dent Med 2025) — 하악 치아 증상성 비가역성 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP) 국소마취 (Local Anesthesia, LA) 프로토콜 비교; 임상적으로 가장 어려운 마취 시나리오.

변형 프로토콜 (아티카인·보조 침윤·용량 증가·첨가제)은 표준 하악 신경 차단 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 2% 리도케인 대비 3.34배 성공률 (OR = 3.34; 95% CI: 2.49–4.48; 고질 증거; I² = 0%); IANB > 협측 침윤 (Buccal Infiltration, BI) (OR = 4.03); 4% 아티카인 가장 높은 성공 (OR = 4.18, 중간 확신) — 2% 리도케인 (OR = 3.94)과 CI 겹침.

2% 리도케인 단독 IANB의 SIP 실패율 12–65%는 우연이 아닌 체계적 한계이며, 변형 프로토콜로 극복 가능함을 입증.

## Study Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Systematic review and meta-analysis |
| PROSPERO | CRD42025638427 |
| Included RCTs | 16 (mandibular SIP) |
| Reference comparator | Conventional IANB with 2% lidocaine |
| Primary outcome | Anesthetic success rate |

## Key Quantitative Results

### Primary outcome — All 16 RCTs
| Model | OR | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Random-effects | **3.34** | 2.49–4.48 | < 0.001 |
| Fixed-effects | 3.79 | 3.23–4.44 | < 0.001 |
| Heterogeneity | I² = 0% | P = 0.52 | Low |

### Subgroup: Anesthesia technique
| Technique | OR | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IANB** | **4.03** | 3.38–4.81 | < 0.001 |
| Buccal infiltration (BI) | Lower | — | — |

### Subgroup: Anesthetic agent (GRADE quality)
| Agent | OR | 95% CI | GRADE |
|---|---|---|---|
| **4% articaine** | **4.18** | 2.85–6.16 | Moderate |
| **2% lidocaine** | **3.94** | 2.42–6.44 | Moderate |

Note: CI ranges overlap between articaine and lidocaine — no statistically significant difference between agents.

## Clinical Significance

- Conventional treatment (OR denominator) = IANB with 2% lidocaine alone: failure rate ~12–65%
- Modified protocols achieve OR 3.34–4.18 — a profound improvement in SIP management
- Articaine mechanism: thiophene ring → higher lipid solubility → better tissue penetration; in acute pulpitis, diffusion advantage partially counteracted by "bone barrier collapse—high clearance—low threshold blockage" triple effect
- Practical implication: consider articaine BI after IANB for SIP, or supplemental IOI/ILI

## Publication Bias

Egger test P = 0.461 > 0.05 → no significant publication bias; funnel plot symmetric.
