## Three-line Summary

Prospective, randomised, double-blinded RCT in 126 mandibular molar patients with symptomatic irreversible pulpitis and confirmed failed primary IANB (85% failure rate), randomised 2×2 to aceclofenac/placebo premedication × articaine/mepivacaine intraligamentary injection.
Aceclofenac + 4% articaine intraligamentary achieved 87.1% success vs 66.7% (placebo + articaine), 60.6% (aceclofenac + mepivacaine), and 53.1% (placebo + mepivacaine); articaine groups were significantly superior to mepivacaine groups (p<0.05); aceclofenac augmented efficacy only in the articaine group (p=0.048, NSD for mepivacaine p=0.595).
Best-evidence supplemental protocol for failed IANB in SIP is aceclofenac premedication + 4% articaine with 1:100,000 epinephrine intraligamentary injection; mepivacaine without epinephrine remains inferior even with premedication.

## 세줄요약

불가역성 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP) 하악 대구치에서 1차 하악 치조신경 차단 마취 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 실패(85%) 후 126명을 2×2 무작위 이중맹검으로 배정 — 아세클로페낙 (Aceclofenac)/위약 × 아티카인 (Articaine)/메피바카인 (Mepivacaine) 치주인대 주사 (Intraligamentary Injection, IL).
아세클로페낙 + 4% 아티카인 치주인대주사 87.1% 성공, 위약 + 아티카인 66.7%, 아세클로페낙 + 메피바카인 60.6%, 위약 + 메피바카인 53.1%; 아티카인군이 메피바카인군보다 유의하게 우수(p<0.05), 아세클로페낙은 아티카인군에서만 유의미한 향상(p=0.048).
실패한 IANB의 최선 보완 프로토콜: 아세클로페낙 전처치 + 4% 아티카인 (1:100,000 에피네프린) 치주인대주사 — 에피네프린 없는 메피바카인은 전처치 추가에도 열등함.

## Summary

This prospective, randomised, double-blinded RCT enrolled 126 mandibular molar patients with symptomatic irreversible pulpitis (SIP) whose primary IANB failed (85% failure rate confirmed). Using a 2×2 factorial design, patients received either aceclofenac 100 mg or placebo 15 minutes before the IANB, and after IANB failure received supplemental intraligamentary injection with either 4% articaine 1:100,000 epinephrine or 3% plain mepivacaine. The best combination — aceclofenac premedication plus articaine IL injection — achieved 87.1% success, nearly 34 percentage points above the worst combination (placebo + mepivacaine at 53.1%). Articaine consistently outperformed mepivacaine regardless of premedication status, and aceclofenac provided a significant additive benefit only when paired with articaine. The study establishes a clear first-line salvage protocol for the hot tooth scenario and implicates epinephrine-mediated vasoconstriction and peripheral NSAID action as the two independent augmentation axes.

## Key Contributions

- 2×2 factorial design isolates NSAID premedication and anesthetic agent effects independently
- Aceclofenac selectively augments articaine-based IL injection — no benefit with plain mepivacaine (p=0.595)
- Confirms 85% primary IANB failure rate in SIP mandibular molars (consistent with 70–81% literature range)
- Overall supplemental IL success 66.7%, rising to 87.1% with optimized protocol
- Establishes that epinephrine-containing solution (articaine) > plain solution (mepivacaine) for IL supplemental anesthesia in inflamed pulp

## Methodology

**Design**: Prospective, randomised, parallel, double-blind RCT — CTRI/2023/08/056538
**Setting**: JSS Dental College and Hospital, Mysore, India (Sep 2023 – Feb 2024)
**n**: 126 patients (from 148 with confirmed lip numbness after primary IANB; 22 had IANB success and were excluded)
**Primary IANB**: 1.8 mL 2% lidocaine 1:80,000 epi (Halsted); 15 min wait
**IL injection**: 0.6 mL per root × 3 pulses (0.2 mL each), 30-G 12-mm needle, pressure syringe
**Outcomes**: 헤프트-파커 시각통증척도 (Heft-Parker VAS) ≤54 mm throughout access and instrumentation = success
