## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=110; India; Feb–Jun 2025) comparing 2% lignocaine + adrenaline 1:80,000 vs 2% plain lignocaine during tooth extraction: adrenaline group showed significant post-extraction increases in SBP (+12.69%, 138.20 vs 126.75 mmHg), DBP (+10.60%), blood glucose (+14.63%, 112.85 vs 101.90 mg/dL), and pulse rate (+15.84%, 88.40 vs 78.45 bpm) — all p<0.001.

Multiple regression confirmed adrenaline use as the dominant predictor of all physiological changes (ΔSystolic β=11.45mmHg, Δglucose β=10.20mg/dL, ΔHR β=9.85bpm); age significantly predicted greater BP response; gender and BMI were not significant.

Adrenaline-containing lidocaine is effective for hemostasis and prolonged anesthesia but warrants careful patient selection and monitoring — particularly in elderly, hypertensive, diabetic, or cardiovascular-compromised patients — while plain lidocaine produces minimal systemic impact.

## 세줄요약

무작위 대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=110; 인도; 2025) — 2% 리그노케인 + 아드레날린 (에피네프린, Epinephrine) 1:80,000 vs 순수 리그노케인 2% 발치 비교: 에피 군에서 발치 직후 수축기혈압 (Systolic Blood Pressure, SBP) +12.69% (138.20 vs 126.75mmHg), 이완기혈압 (Diastolic Blood Pressure, DBP) +10.60%, 혈당 +14.63% (112.85 vs 101.90mg/dL), 맥박수 +15.84% (88.40 vs 78.45bpm) — 모두 p<0.001.

중회귀분석에서 에피네프린 사용이 모든 생리적 변화의 가장 강력한 예측인자(Δ수축기 β=11.45mmHg, Δ혈당 β=10.20mg/dL, Δ맥박 β=9.85bpm); 연령이 혈압 반응의 유의한 추가 예측인자(노인→반응 큼); 성별·BMI는 무의미.

에피 함유 리도케인은 지혈·마취 연장에 효과적이지만 노인·고혈압·당뇨·심혈관 취약환자는 신중 선택 및 모니터링 필수; 순수 리도케인은 전신적 영향 최소.

## Study Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Prospective, randomized comparative clinical study |
| N | 110 (55/group) |
| Population | ASA I–II, 18–60 years, simple tooth extraction |
| Institution | Siddharth Multi Speciality Dental Hospital, Vijayawada, India |
| Period | February–June 2025 |
| Group A | Lignocaine 2% + adrenaline 1:80,000 |
| Group B | Lignocaine 2% plain (no vasoconstrictor) |
| Volume | ≤2 mL (standardized) |
| Measurements | Baseline → 10min post-anesthesia → post-extraction |

Note: 1:80,000 concentration is higher than standard 1:100,000; may overestimate magnitude vs standard formulation.

## Key Results

| Parameter | Group A (epi) | Group B (plain) | % change A | % change B | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Systolic BP (mmHg) | 138.20±10.45 | 126.75±8.45 | +12.69% | +2.93% | <0.001 |
| Diastolic BP (mmHg) | 87.10±8.15 | 79.85±7.10 | +10.60% | +1.85% | <0.001 |
| Blood glucose (mg/dL) | 112.85±12.34 | 101.90±10.55 | +14.63% | +4.19% | <0.001 |
| Pulse rate (bpm) | 88.40±7.20 | 78.45±6.10 | +15.84% | +3.36% | <0.001 |

Baseline values were comparable between groups (all p>0.05).

## Mechanism

Adrenaline → α-1 adrenoceptor stimulation → vasoconstriction → ↑BP
Adrenaline → β-1 stimulation → ↑HR, ↑cardiac output
Adrenaline → glycogenolysis + gluconeogenesis → ↑blood glucose
These effects are transient but clinically important in susceptible patients.

## Clinical Implications for Special Patients

| Patient Group | Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Hypertension | Significant BP elevation (11.45mmHg systolic) | Monitor BP; consider plain lidocaine if uncontrolled |
| Diabetes | +14.63% blood glucose (β=10.20mg/dL) | Monitor glucose; use minimum effective dose |
| Elderly | Age is significant BP predictor | Increase monitoring; lower epi dose if possible |
| Cardiovascular disease | Combined BP + HR elevation | Consult physician; cardiac monitoring if needed |

## Limitations

- Single-center India study
- 1:80,000 concentration (not standard 1:100,000) — slightly higher epi dose
- Short follow-up; no return-to-baseline monitoring
- Did not separate stress-induced vs pharmacological effects
