## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=100; India; 2025) comparing 4% articaine (1:100,000 epi) vs 2% lignocaine (1:80,000 epi) for IANB in patients with symptomatic irreversible pulpitis (SIP) in permanent mandibular first molars: articaine demonstrated significantly faster onset (4.3 vs 7.8 min, p<0.001), markedly longer duration (120.8 vs 64.9 min, p<0.001), and superior pain control (SF-MPQ 1.09 vs 1.63, p<0.001).

Key finding: 2% lignocaine onset in SIP is **7.8 minutes** — approximately 50% longer than healthy-tissue IANB onset (~5 min) — confirming that periapical inflammation extends onset time and reduces anesthetic depth through tissue pH alteration and nociceptor sensitization.

Articaine's dual structural advantage (thiophene ring + ester side chain: 1.5× lipid solubility vs lidocaine, plasma esterase hydrolysis) overcomes the inflammatory tissue barrier more effectively, making it the preferred agent for IANB in endodontic cases with SIP.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 무작위 대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=100; 인도; 2025) — 하악 제1대구치 증상성 비가역성 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP) 환자에서 하악신경차단 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 시 4% 아티카인 (1:100,000 에피) vs 2% 리그노케인 (1:80,000 에피) 비교: 아티카인에서 발현시간 현저히 짧음 (4.3 vs 7.8분, p<0.001), 지속시간 현저히 김 (120.8 vs 64.9분, p<0.001), 통증 낮음 (단축형 맥길통증질문지 (Short-Form McGill Pain Questionnaire, SF-MPQ) 1.09 vs 1.63, p<0.001).

핵심 발견: SIP에서 2% 리그노케인 IANB 발현시간 **7.8분** — 건강치아 대비 (~5분) 약 50% 지연 — 염증조직의 pH 저하가 이온화 분율↑ → 막 투과성↓ → 발현 지연을 실증.

아티카인의 이중 구조 이점(티오펜환 + 에스테르기: 지질용해도 리도케인 대비 1.5배, 혈장에스테라제 가수분해)이 SIP 염증장벽을 더 효과적으로 극복하여 근관치료 IANB의 선호 약제.

## Study Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Prospective, double-blind RCT |
| N | 100 (50/group) |
| Population | ASA I, 18–30y, SIP in mandibular first molar, no periapical pathology |
| Institution | Regional Dental College, Guwahati, India |
| Intervention A | 2% lignocaine + 1:80,000 epinephrine (1.8mL) |
| Intervention B | 4% articaine + 1:100,000 epinephrine (1.7mL) |
| Technique | IANB; 20% benzocaine topical 1 min prior |
| Anesthetic confirmation | EPT reading ≥80 (no response) = profound pulpal anesthesia |
| Pain assessment | SF-MPQ after caries removal and access cavity preparation |

## Key Results

| Parameter | Lignocaine 2% (1:80k epi) | Articaine 4% (1:100k epi) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onset (min) | **7.8 ± 0.8** | 4.3 ± 0.4 | <0.001 |
| Duration (min) | 64.9 ± 3.0 | **120.8 ± 5.2** | <0.001 |
| SF-MPQ pain score | 1.63 ± 0.640 | **1.09 ± 0.258** | <0.001 |

## Clinical Significance: Onset Time in SIP vs Healthy Tissue

| Context | Lidocaine IANB Onset | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy adults | ~5.17 min | Panya 2025 ([[panya-2025-lidocaine-mepivacaine-ianb-comparison-rct]]) |
| SIP (inflamed tissue) | **7.8 min** | This study (Chakraborty 2025) |
| Children (unbuffered lidocaine) | ~1.92 min | Suresh 2026 ([[suresh-2026-buffered-articaine-lidocaine-children-onset]]) |

**SIP extends lidocaine IANB onset by ~50%** relative to healthy tissue. Mechanism:
- Inflamed tissue pH ↓ (4.5–5.5 vs normal 7.4) → lidocaine ionization ↑ → membrane impermeability ↑
- Prostaglandin-sensitized nociceptors lower the pain threshold even under partial block
- Increased vascularity and blood flow increase anesthetic clearance

## Why Articaine Performs Better in SIP

1. **Thiophene ring**: replaces benzene in lidocaine → 1.5× higher lipid solubility → better penetration despite low tissue pH
2. **Ester side chain**: plasma esterase hydrolysis in addition to hepatic → faster clearance, lower systemic toxicity
3. **Lower pKa (7.8 vs 7.9)**: marginally more non-ionized base form at any pH
4. **Higher concentration (4% vs 2%)**: 2× the drug per unit volume → overcomes diffusion limitations in inflammatory tissue

## Limitations

- Lignocaine arm used 1:80,000 epi (not standard 1:100,000) — higher epi concentration may extend lignocaine duration, but onset advantage for articaine remains valid
- Single-center; India-specific patient demographics
- Age 18–30 only; does not cover middle-aged or elderly patients
- Mandibular first molar only; maxillary or other teeth not evaluated
- No separate analysis of anesthetic failure (EPT response after IANB)
- Relatively small n per group for categorical outcomes
