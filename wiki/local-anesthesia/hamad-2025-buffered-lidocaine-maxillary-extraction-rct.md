## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=100; Kurdistan, Iraq; 2024–2025) comparing buffered 2% lidocaine (pH ~7.4, 0.18mL 8.4% NaHCO3 per 1.8mL carpule) vs conventional 2% lidocaine (pH ~4.2) for maxillary posterior tooth extraction via supraperiosteal infiltration: buffered lidocaine significantly reduced injection pain (VAS 2.8 vs 4.2, p<0.01), shortened onset (2.3 vs 4.7 min, p<0.01), and extended duration (45.8 vs 36.4 min, p<0.01).

No significant difference in reinjection rate (4% vs 8%, p=0.678), confirming buffering improves comfort and pharmacodynamics but does not eliminate anesthetic failure.

Buffering protocol is simple (bedside NaHCO3 addition, immediate use), low-cost, and broadly applicable — particularly beneficial for anxious patients and periapical pathology cases where faster onset and extended duration improve procedural experience.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 무작위 대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=100; 쿠르디스탄, 이라크; 2024–2025) — 완충 2% 리도케인 (pH ~7.4, 8.4% 탄산수소나트륨 (Sodium Bicarbonate, NaHCO3) 0.18mL + 카풀 (carpule) 1.8mL) vs 표준 리도케인 (pH ~4.2) 상악 후방부 발치 침윤마취 비교: 완충군에서 주사 통증 시각 통증 척도 (Visual Analogue Scale, VAS) 유의하게 낮음 (2.8 vs 4.2, p<0.01), 발현 시간 51% 단축 (2.3 vs 4.7분, p<0.01), 지속 시간 26% 연장 (45.8 vs 36.4분, p<0.01).

재주사율 차이 없음 (4% vs 8%, p=0.678) — 완충이 편안함·약동학 개선하지만 마취 실패 자체는 제거 못함.

완충 프로토콜은 간단(침상에서 NaHCO3 즉석 혼합), 저비용으로 불안 환자 및 치근단 병소 발치 케이스에서 특히 유익.

## Study Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Double-blind, prospective RCT |
| N | 100 (50/group) |
| Population | ASA I–II, 18–60y, maxillary posterior extraction with periapical lesion |
| Institution | Hospital dental clinic, Kurdistan region, Iraq |
| Period | September 2024 – April 2025 |
| Group A | 2% lidocaine + 1:100,000 adrenaline, pH ~4.2 |
| Group B | 2% lidocaine + 1:100,000 epi + 8.4% NaHCO3 (1:10 ratio), pH ~7.3–7.4 |
| Technique | Supraperiosteal infiltration, 27G, 1.8mL, 1mL/min, single blinded surgeon |
| Ethics | Kurdistan Board of Medical Specialties No. 440, 29-08-2024 |

## Key Results

| Outcome | Group B (buffered) | Group A (conventional) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| VAS injection pain | **2.8 ± 0.7** | 4.2 ± 0.9 | **<0.01** |
| Onset time (min) | **2.3** | 4.7 | **<0.01** |
| Duration (min) | **45.8 ± 7.6** | 36.4 ± 8.2 | **<0.01** |
| Reinjection rate | 4% | 8% | 0.678 (NS) |

## Buffering Protocol

```
0.18 mL of 8.4% NaHCO3 + 1.8 mL standard lidocaine carpule (1:10 ratio)
Mix immediately before injection
Result: pH ~7.3-7.4 (vs conventional ~4.2)
```

**Mechanism of improvement:**
- pH 4.2 → tissue irritation → nociceptor activation → injection pain
- pH 7.4 → more uncharged base form (Henderson-Hasselbalch) → faster axon membrane penetration → earlier onset
- Less acidic vasodilation → epinephrine retains vasoconstriction → prolonged duration

## Limitations

- Maxillary supraperiosteal only; mandibular IANB results mixed in literature
- Fixed NaHCO3 ratio (optimal ratio unknown)
- Non-vital teeth with periapical lesions — may not generalize to vital teeth extraction
- VAS-only outcome; no objective physiological measures
