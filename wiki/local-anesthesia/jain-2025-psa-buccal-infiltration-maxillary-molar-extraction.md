## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=150 ASA I; India; 2025) comparing posterior superior alveolar (PSA) nerve block vs buccal infiltration for maxillary second and third molar extraction, both using 2% lidocaine 1:100,000 epi (1.8mL): no significant difference in intraoperative VAS pain (2.11 vs 2.02, p=0.71), VDS scores (p=0.96), or need for supplemental anesthesia (10.7% vs 12.0%, p=0.74).

PSA block showed 4.0% complication rate (hematoma, trismus, positive aspiration) vs 0% for buccal infiltration (p=0.03) — PSA's anatomical proximity to the pterygoid venous plexus creates irreducible aspiration and hematoma risk not present with infiltration technique.

Buccal infiltration is the preferred technique for routine maxillary molar extraction: equivalent anesthesia, no aspiration risk, simpler technique, better tolerated — both using standard 2% lidocaine 1:100,000 epi dosing.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 무작위 대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=150 ASA I; 인도; 2025) — 상악 제2·제3대구치 발치 시 후상치조신경차단 (Posterior Superior Alveolar Nerve Block, PSA Block) vs 협측 침윤마취 (Buccal Infiltration) 비교, 양군 모두 2% 리도케인 1:100,000 에피 (1.8mL): 수술 중 시각 통증 척도 (VAS) (2.11 vs 2.02, p=0.71)·언어통증척도 (Verbal Descriptor Scale, VDS) (p=0.96)·보충마취 필요율 (10.7% vs 12.0%, p=0.74) 모두 차이 없음.

PSA 차단 합병증 4.0% (혈종·개구제한·양성 흡인) vs 침윤 0% (p=0.03) — PSA의 익돌정맥총 (Pterygoid Venous Plexus) 근접으로 인한 흡인·혈종 위험은 침윤마취에는 없음.

상악 대구치 발치 표준 술기는 협측 침윤마취: 동등한 마취 효과 + 합병증 없음 + 더 단순한 술기 + 더 나은 환자 내성 — 양군 모두 동일 리도케인 용량 사용.

## Study Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Prospective, double-blind RCT |
| N | 150 (75/group) |
| Population | ASA I, healthy adults, non-surgical maxillary second/third molar extraction |
| Intervention A | PSA nerve block, 1.8mL 2% lidocaine 1:100,000 epi |
| Intervention B | Buccal infiltration, 1.8mL 2% lidocaine 1:100,000 epi |
| Primary outcomes | VAS, VDS, supplemental anesthesia rate |
| Secondary outcomes | Complications, rescue analgesic use within 3h |

## Key Results

| Outcome | PSA Block (n=75) | Buccal Infiltration (n=75) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| VAS intraoperative | 2.11 ± 1.21 | 2.02 ± 1.30 | 0.71 (NS) |
| VDS | comparable | comparable | 0.96 (NS) |
| Supplemental anesthesia | 10.7% | 12.0% | 0.74 (NS) |
| **Complications** | **4.0%** | **0%** | **0.03** |
| Analgesic use 3h | comparable | comparable | 0.48 (NS) |

PSA complications: hematoma, trismus, positive aspiration (all in PSA group only).

## Clinical Recommendation

**Preferred technique:** Buccal infiltration for routine maxillary second/third molar extraction.

Rationale:
1. Equivalent pain control (no advantage to PSA block)
2. No aspiration risk (avoids pterygoid venous plexus proximity)
3. Simpler technique (lower learning curve, consistent results)
4. Better patient tolerance (less intimidating injection)

**Both techniques use identical dosing:** 1.8mL 2% lidocaine 1:100,000 epinephrine.
