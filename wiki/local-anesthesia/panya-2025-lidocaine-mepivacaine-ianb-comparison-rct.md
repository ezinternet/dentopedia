## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=62 healthy dental students; BMC Oral Health 2025; Chulalongkorn University, Thailand) comparing 2% lidocaine vs 2% mepivacaine — both with 1:100,000 epinephrine — for IANB, the most commonly used anesthetic in Asian dental practice.

No statistically significant differences in onset (5.17 vs 5.52 min, p=0.435), duration (214 vs 234 min, p=0.073), or 10-minute success rate (78.6% vs 91.2%, p=0.161); both agents produce comparable anesthetic profiles in healthy young adults under conventional IANB technique.

Provides the empirical baseline for 2% lidocaine 1:100,000 epi IANB success rate in healthy adults (78.6%), establishing the floor against which SIP failure rates must be contextualized; also confirms mepivacaine superiority claims lack evidence in controlled conditions.

## 세줄요약

무작위 이중맹검 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=62 건강한 치대생; BMC Oral Health 2025; 쭐라롱콘 대학, 태국) — 2% 리도케인 vs 2% 메피바카인 (각 1:100,000 에피네프린) 하악 신경 차단 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 직접 비교.

발현 (5.17 vs 5.52분, NS)·지속 (214 vs 234분, NS)·10분 내 성공률 (78.6% vs 91.2%, NS) 모두 유의차 없음; 건강한 젊은 성인 IANB에서 두 약제 동등.

2% 리도케인 1:100,000 에피 IANB의 건강 성인 기준선 성공률 78.6% 실측 제공 — 증상성 비가역성 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP) 실패율 맥락화의 기준점; 메피바카인 우월성 주장에 근거 없음 확인.

## Study Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Double-blind, randomized, pilot comparative trial |
| N | 62 (lidocaine n=28, mepivacaine n=34) |
| Population | Healthy dental students, 19–21 years |
| Institution | Faculty of Dentistry, Chulalongkorn University, Thailand |
| Intervention | 2% lidocaine 1:100,000 epi vs 2% mepivacaine 1:100,000 epi; 1.5 mL IANB |
| Operators | Calibrated 4th-year dental students under supervision |
| Trial registry | TCTR20250106002 |

## Key Results

| Outcome | Lidocaine (n=28) | Mepivacaine (n=34) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onset time (min) | 5.17 ± 1.96 (95% CI: 4.41–5.93) | 5.52 ± 1.57 (95% CI: 4.97–6.07) | **0.435 (NS)** |
| Duration (min) | 214.25 ± 47.52 (95% CI: 195.82–232.68) | 234.32 ± 39.02 (95% CI: 220.71–247.93) | **0.073 (NS)** |
| Success ≤10 min | 22/28 = **78.6%** (95% CI: 60.5–89.8%) | 31/34 = **91.2%** (95% CI: 77.0–96.9%) | **0.161 (NS)** |

Adverse events: 1 transient headache (mepivacaine group); 0 (lidocaine group); both well tolerated (98.4% no adverse events).

## Limitations

- Underpowered (n=62 from planned 250); cannot claim formal equivalence
- Exclusively healthy young adults; not generalizable to older/systemic patients
- No painful procedure (not tested under actual clinical pain stimulus)
- Duration by self-report only — subjective
- Multiple student operators → inter-operator variability
