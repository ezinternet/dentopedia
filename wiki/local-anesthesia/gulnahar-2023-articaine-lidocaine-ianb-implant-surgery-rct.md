## Three-line Summary

Largest RCT comparing 4% articaine vs 2% lidocaine for IANB in posterior mandibular implant surgery (n=577 patients, 1185 implants; two university centers, Turkey, 2019–2022): no significant difference in VAS pain during injection (p=0.778) or surgery (p=0.302), and no difference in lip numbness time (3.06 vs 2.96 min, p=0.678).

Despite articaine group having implants placed anatomically closer to the mandibular canal (2.28 vs 2.45mm, p<0.05) and requiring significantly more release incisions (20.3% vs 12.3%, p<0.05), pain perception was identical — confirming IANB technique quality, not agent choice, is the primary success determinant.

Both agents provide adequate anesthesia for posterior mandibular implant placement via IANB; agent selection should be based on cost, availability, and risk profile (4% articaine carries higher paresthesia risk at higher concentration than 2% lidocaine).

## 세줄요약

하악 후방부 임플란트 수술에서 하악신경차단 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 시 4% 아티카인 vs 2% 리도케인 최대 규모 무작위 대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=577, 1185 임플란트; 터키 2개 대학병원, 2019–2022): 주사 시 시각 통증 척도 (Visual Analogue Scale, VAS) (p=0.778)·수술 중 VAS (p=0.302)·입술 무감각 시간 (p=0.678) 모두 유의차 없음.

아티카인 군이 하치조신경관 (Mandibular Canal, MC)에 더 가까이 임플란트를 식립했고 (2.28 vs 2.45mm, p<0.05) 이완절개 (Release Incision) 더 많이 시행했음에도 (20.3% vs 12.3%, p<0.05) 통증 인식 동일 — IANB 성공의 핵심은 약제 선택이 아니라 술기 정확도임을 확인.

하악 후방부 임플란트 IANB에서 두 약제 모두 적절한 마취 제공; 약제 선택은 비용·이용가능성·리스크 프로파일(4% 아티카인: 고농도로 인한 지각이상 위험 더 높음)로 판단.

## Study Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Prospective, double-center RCT (CONSORT 2010) |
| N | 577 analyzed (634 enrolled; 57 excluded for requiring 2nd injection) |
| Population | ASA I–II patients receiving ≥1 posterior mandibular implant |
| Implants | 1185 dental implants total |
| Institution | Pamukkale University + 1 other university hospital, Turkey |
| Period | Feb 2019 – Mar 2022 |
| Intervention A | 4% articaine HCl + 0.006 mg/mL epi (Ultracain D-S, Sanofi) |
| Intervention B | 2% lidocaine HCl + 0.0125 mg/mL epi (Jetokain, Adeka) |
| Technique | IANB, 27-G 50mm needle, full ampoule injected |
| Ethics | Pamukkale University EC (E-60116787-020-201307) |

## Key Results

| Outcome | Articaine (n=252) | Lidocaine (n=325) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| VAS injection pain | median 1 (0–4) | median 1 (0–8) | 0.778 (NS) |
| VAS surgical pain | median 1 (0–3) | median 1 (0–7) | 0.302 (NS) |
| Lip numbness time | 2.96 ± 3.09 min | 3.06 ± 3.22 min | 0.678 (NS) |
| Canal-implant distance | 2.28 ± 0.75 mm | 2.45 ± 0.86 mm | **p<0.05** |
| Release incision | 20.3% (51/252) | 12.3% (40/325) | **p<0.05** |

Exclusion rate: ~9% per group required 2nd injection (25 articaine, 32 lidocaine) — excluded to eliminate confounding.

## Clinical Interpretation

**Why did articaine require more release incisions?** Articaine's theoretical superior bone penetration may have influenced surgeon decisions about flap management, but this did not translate to pain benefit. For IANB, direct nerve proximity is the primary mechanism — bone diffusion (articaine's hypothetical advantage) is irrelevant.

**Correlation finding:** Pain during injection predicted pain during surgery in both groups. Pre-injection anxiety management and atraumatic technique are clinically important.

**pKa comparison:** Articaine 7.8 vs lidocaine 7.9 — minimal difference; does not explain meaningful clinical variation.
