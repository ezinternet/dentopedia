## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=100; India; 2025–2026) comparing buffered 4% articaine (1:100,000 epi, pH-adjusted with 0.18mL 8.4% NaHCO3 in 1:10 ratio) vs non-buffered 2% lidocaine (1:100,000 epi) for dental injections in children aged 6–12 years: buffered articaine produced 37.6% faster onset (71.98 vs 115.28 s, p<0.001), lower injection pain (VAS 2.25 vs 3.93, p<0.001), and better behavioral cooperation (Frankl 3.18 vs 2.48, p<0.001).

Pulpal success rates were equivalent (84% vs 82%, p=1.000), confirming that buffered articaine's comfort and onset advantages do not come at the cost of clinical effectiveness; prolonged soft-tissue anesthesia (159 vs 138 min, p<0.001) did not increase lip/cheek biting (12% vs 16%, p=0.773).

Non-buffered lidocaine onset in children is ~1.92 min — far shorter than adult IANB (~5–8 min) due to pediatric mandibular anatomy (thinner cortex, higher porosity, shorter diffusion distance); buffered articaine reduces this further to ~1.20 min, critical for maintaining cooperation in the anxious waiting window.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 무작위 대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=100; 인도; 2025–2026) — 소아(6–12세) 완충 4% 아티카인 (Buffered Articaine) (1:100,000 에피, 8.4% 탄산수소나트륨 (NaHCO3) 0.18mL 혼합, 1:10) vs 비완충 2% 리도케인 (Non-buffered Lidocaine) (1:100,000 에피) 비교: 완충 아티카인에서 발현 37.6% 빠름 (71.98 vs 115.28초, p<0.001), 주사통증 낮음 (시각 통증 척도 (Visual Analogue Scale, VAS) 2.25 vs 3.93, p<0.001), 행동협조도 우수 (Frankl 척도 3.18 vs 2.48, p<0.001).

치수마취 성공률 동등 (84% vs 82%, p=1.000); 연조직 지속시간은 완충 아티카인이 더 길었으나 (159 vs 138분, p<0.001) 입술/볼 깨물기 차이 없음 (12% vs 16%, p=0.773).

소아에서 비완충 리도케인 발현 ~1.92분 — 성인 IANB (~5–8분)보다 훨씬 짧음 (소아 피질골 얇음·골공극 높음·확산거리 짧음); 완충 아티카인은 ~1.20분으로 단축, 불안 대기시간 최소화에 임상적으로 중요.

## Study Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Prospective, double-blind, parallel-group RCT (CONSORT 2010) |
| N | 100 (50/group), zero attrition |
| Population | ASA I, 6–12y, first-time dental attendees, moderate baseline anxiety (Frankl 2–4) |
| Institution | Single academic center, India |
| Registration | CTRI/2025/03/083198 |
| Group A | Buffered 4% articaine + 1:100,000 epi (0.18mL 8.4% NaHCO3 + 1.62mL Septanest) |
| Group B | Non-buffered 2% lidocaine + 1:100,000 epi (Xicaine 1.8mL) |
| Technique | 30G needle, 1 mL/min, 60s topical 10% lidocaine spray prior |
| Onset measure | Seconds from needle withdrawal to confirmed numbness (verbal + probing) |

## Buffering Protocol

```
Add: 0.18 mL of 8.4% NaHCO3
To:  1.62 mL articaine 4% + 1:100,000 epi cartridge
     (≈ 1:10 sodium bicarbonate:anesthetic ratio)
Mix: Invert 5–6 times
Result: pH raised from 3.5 to ~6.5–7.0
Use:  Immediately after mixing
```

**Why pH buffering works:**
- Commercial articaine pH 3.5–4.5 → ionized (charged) form predominates → poor membrane traversal
- After buffering pH → 6.5–7.0 → non-ionized (lipid-soluble base) form ↑ → faster neural membrane penetration
- Effect amplified by articaine's lower pKa (7.8) and thiophene ring's superior lipid solubility

## Pediatric vs Adult Onset Context

| Population | Non-buffered Lidocaine Onset | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Adults healthy (IANB) | ~5.17 min | Normal cortical thickness, diffusion distance |
| Adults SIP (IANB) | ~7.8 min | Inflamed tissue pH↓ extends ionization |
| Children 6–12y (various) | **~1.92 min** | Thin cortex, high porosity, short diffusion distance |
| Children buffered articaine | **~1.20 min** | pH optimization + superior articaine pharmacokinetics |

## Clinical Recommendations (Pediatric)

1. **Buffered articaine is preferred** for anxious or uncooperative children when faster onset reduces the waiting window
2. **Inform parents** of prolonged soft-tissue numbness (~159 min) to prevent inadvertent lip/cheek trauma post-procedure
3. **Pulpal success is equivalent** — switch to buffered articaine does not compromise clinical effectiveness
4. **Caution**: pharmacokinetic benefits (buffering + agent) cannot be disentangled in this study design

## Limitations

- Inter-agent comparison (articaine vs lidocaine + buffering vs non-buffering) — cannot isolate single variable
- Single academic center; India demographics
- First-time attendees only with moderate anxiety — may not represent all pediatric populations
- Maxillary vs mandibular not stratified in primary analysis
- One-week follow-up only
- Categorical outcomes (reinjection, adverse events) underpowered
