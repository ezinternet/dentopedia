## Three-line Summary

Special JKDA article introducing the 2022 Korean sedation guideline (consensus document, not a primary research study), targeting moderate sedation across the medical system including non-anesthesiologist dental practitioners with 15 PICO-structured recommendations.

Topics covered include provider training, drug/equipment requirements, patient selection, fasting, adult/pediatric drug comparison, intraoperative monitoring (respiratory, cardiovascular, sedation depth), pediatric respiratory complication management, and discharge criteria; no primary outcomes are reported.

The guideline explicitly addresses the risk of unintentional deep-sedation progression from moderate sedation, and its existence is the institutional counterpoint to the paired position paper calling for a still-absent Korean local-anesthesia guideline.

## Summary

This special article introduces the new Korean sedation guideline (published 2022) to a general dental audience. The guideline targets moderate sedation across the medical system — including non-anesthesiologist providers — and addresses the realistic risk of unintentional progression to deep sedation. It is structured around 15 PICO questions and covers provider training, drug/equipment requirements, patient selection, referral thresholds, fasting, adult/pediatric usage, drug comparison, intraoperative monitoring (respiratory, cardiovascular, sedation depth), pediatric respiratory complication management, and discharge criteria.

## Key Contributions

- Surfaces the 15 PICO question structure of the 2022 Korean sedation guideline.
- Frames sedation as a continuum (moderate → deep), with explicit monitoring and rescue requirements for the transition.
- Aimed at non-anesthesiologist dental practitioners — clarifies institutional and referral expectations.

## Methodology

Special-article format (clinical guideline introduction). Not a primary research study.

## Results

No primary outcomes — this is a guideline overview. For numerical thresholds (e.g., monitoring intervals, fasting windows, discharge criteria), refer to the underlying 2022 Korean sedation guideline.
