## Three-line Summary

Phase 1 pharmacokinetic trial (n=16, 4 treatments; Anesth Prog 2008) measuring lidocaine/epinephrine PK after intraoral injection with vs without phentolamine mesylate reversal — the only published dental-specific carpule-level pharmacokinetic study.

Phentolamine (alpha-adrenergic antagonist) blocks epinephrine's vasoconstriction, slightly increasing Cmax and delaying Tmax of lidocaine; t½, CL, and Vd are similar across all treatment groups; 4-carpule dose (144 mg lidocaine) yields Cmax well below the 6 μg/mL toxicity threshold.

Provides empirical validation that standard dental doses (1–4 carpules of 2% lidocaine/epinephrine) produce systemic concentrations with a large safety margin before LAST onset — with the critical caveat that inadvertent intravascular injection bypasses this safety margin entirely.

## 세줄요약

1상 약동학 시험 (n=16, 4 치료군; Anesth Prog 2008) — 구강 내 주사 후 펜톨아민 (Phentolamine) 가역화 유무에 따른 리도케인 약동학; 치과 카폴 수준 PK를 다룬 유일한 공개 연구.

펜톨아민(α차단제)이 에피네프린 혈관수축 제거 → Cmax 소폭 증가·Tmax 지연; t½·CL·Vd는 전 군 유사; 4카폴(144 mg 리도케인) Cmax는 독성역치(6 μg/mL) 훨씬 하회.

표준 치과 용량(1–4카폴)은 LAST 발생 전 큰 안전 마진 — **단, 혈관 내 주입(intravascular injection)은 이 안전 마진을 즉각 무력화**.

## Study Design

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Study type | Phase 1 pharmacokinetic trial |
| N | 16 subjects |
| Setting | Dental (intraoral injection) |
| Treatments | 4 groups (crossover) |
| PK parameters | Cmax, Tmax, AUC₀-last, AUC∞, t½, CL, Vd |

## Treatment Groups

| Group | Description |
|---|---|
| **1L1P** | 1 carpule lidocaine/epi → 30 min → 1 carpule phentolamine submucosal |
| **1Piv** | 1 carpule phentolamine IV |
| **4L2P** | 4 carpules lidocaine/epi → 30 min → 2 carpules phentolamine submucosal |
| **4L** | 4 carpules lidocaine/epi, no phentolamine |

**1 carpule = 1.8 mL of 2% lidocaine with 1:100,000 epinephrine = 36 mg lidocaine**

## Key Pharmacokinetic Findings

### Phentolamine Effect on Lidocaine PK:
- **Tmax**: Later in 4L2P vs 4L — phentolamine delays peak concentration (blocks epi vasoconstriction → faster initial absorption from mucosal site → lidocaine redistributes)
- **Cmax**: Slightly higher in 4L2P vs 4L (phentolamine increases systemic absorption by removing vasoconstriction)
- **t½, CL, Vd**: Similar across 1L1P, 1Piv, 4L2P groups — phentolamine does not affect lidocaine metabolism or distribution
- **Interpretation**: phentolamine accelerates systemic absorption of residual LA from injection site — mechanism is alpha-blockade removing epinephrine-mediated vasoconstriction

### Dental Dose Context:
| Dose scenario | Lidocaine (mg) | mg/kg (70 kg adult) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 carpule | 36 mg | 0.51 mg/kg |
| 4 carpules | 144 mg | 2.06 mg/kg |
| FDA max (7 mg/kg) | 490 mg | 13.6 carpules |
| ASRA dental limit | ~6.6 mg/kg | ~12–13 carpules |

**At 4 carpules (2 mg/kg), Cmax is substantially below the 6 μg/mL mild toxicity threshold** — confirming the wide safety margin for standard dental doses in non-compromised adults.

## Why Intravascular Injection Is the Real Danger

This study measures normal mucosal absorption. The critical danger in LAST is **inadvertent intravascular injection**:
- Normal absorption: LA distributes over minutes → Tmax 10–30 min → gradual rise
- Intravascular: entire dose enters bloodstream as bolus → immediate peak → LAST onset in 30–60 seconds
- A single carpule (36 mg) injected IV produces ~0.5 mg/L in a 70 kg patient — well below 6 μg/mL
- But if any factor reduces systemic clearance (hepatic impairment, low volume of distribution), even intravascular injection of standard doses can cause LAST

## Clinical Insights

1. **Standard dental doses are safe by absorption kinetics** — Cmax from 4 carpules is far below 6 μg/mL in a healthy adult. The safety margin is not the concern for normal injection.

2. **Aspiration is the key intervention** — this study does not address intravascular injection because its safety design excluded it. Aspiration before every cartridge is the primary LAST prevention tool.

3. **Phentolamine reversal**: increases systemic lidocaine Cmax slightly — not clinically significant at therapeutic doses, but clinicians should know the pharmacological mechanism. This does NOT mean phentolamine is contraindicated; the magnitude of Cmax increase is small and still far below toxicity thresholds.

4. **Dental PK baseline**: this is the reference for what "normal" dental lidocaine concentrations look like — essential context for interpreting case reports of dental LAST, where doses are often described in number of carpules.
