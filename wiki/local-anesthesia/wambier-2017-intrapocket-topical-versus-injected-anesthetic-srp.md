## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 6 RCTs (from 976 screened) comparing intrapocket topical anesthetic gel vs injected local anesthetic for pain control during scaling and root planing (SRP) in adults.

Injected anesthetic produced lower pain intensity (P=.03) and required less rescue anesthetic (P<.0001) than intrapocket topical gel; however, the risk of developing pain and patient preference were similar between groups.

Intrapocket topical gel remains a clinically reasonable, less-invasive option for SRP despite the edge injectable anesthetic holds on pain intensity — the lack of patient-preference difference matters practically.

## 세줄요약

성인 치석제거·치근활택술(Scaling and Root Planing, SRP) 중 통증 조절에서 intrapocket 표면마취 겔 vs 주사마취를 비교한 6편 무작위대조시험 메타분석(976편 중 선별).

주사마취가 통증 강도(P=.03)와 구제(rescue) 마취 필요도(P<.0001) 모두 낮았으나, 통증 발생 위험과 환자 선호도는 양군 간 차이 없음.

주사마취가 통증 강도에서 우세하지만 침습성이 낮은 intrapocket 겔도 환자 선호가 동등해 SRP 시 합리적인 대안으로 고려할 수 있다.

## Summary
This systematic review with meta-analysis evaluated pain control during scaling and root planing (SRP) in adults, comparing intrapocket topical anesthetic gel against injectable local anesthetic. From 976 articles, 6 RCTs entered qualitative synthesis (4 low, 2 unclear risk of bias). Injected anesthetic produced lower pain intensity and less need for rescue anesthetic, but patients did not prefer one over the other and the overall risk of developing pain was comparable — meaning intrapocket topical gel remains a reasonable, less-invasive option for SRP despite injection's edge on pain intensity.

## Key Contributions
- First SR+MA directly comparing intrapocket topical vs injected anesthesia for SRP.
- Separated outcomes: pain intensity, rescue-anesthetic need, patient preference, risk of pain.
- Frames the efficacy-vs-invasiveness trade-off relevant to hygienist-led periodontal therapy.

## Methodology
SR+MA. 6 databases, RCTs only, comparing intrapocket vs injectable anesthetic during SRP. Primary outcome: risk/intensity of pain. Quality: Cochrane risk-of-bias tool; meta-analysis restricted to low/unclear-risk studies. 976 identified → 6 in qualitative synthesis.

## Results
Pain intensity: injected < topical gel (P=.03). Rescue anesthetic: injected < topical (P<.0001). Patient preference: no difference (P=.09). Risk of developing pain: similar between injected and topical. [확인]
