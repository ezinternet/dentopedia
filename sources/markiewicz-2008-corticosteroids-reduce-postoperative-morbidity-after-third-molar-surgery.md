## Why Ingested

Landmark 2008 SR+MA (12 pooled RCTs) on perioperative corticosteroids for third-molar postoperative sequelae — the highest evidence-tier paper in the wiki's growing corticosteroid/third-molar cluster. Its pooled pain result (no significant benefit) directly disagrees with the newer single RCT [[wiki/drug/analgesics/tamgadge-2025-preoperative-dexamethasone-third-molar-pain-swelling-trismus]] (dexamethasone significantly reduced pain, p<0.001), even though both agree CS reduces edema/swelling and improves trismus/mouth-opening — a genuine, worth-flagging split on the pain outcome specifically.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review and meta-analysis of 12 RCTs testing perioperative corticosteroids (CS, yes/no) on edema, trismus, and pain after third molar (M3) removal, assessed at early (1–3 days) and late (>3 days) postoperative periods.

CS significantly reduced edema (early SMD 1.4, 95% CI 0.6–2.2, P<.001; late SMD 1.1, 95% CI 0.1–2.0, P=.03) and trismus (early WMD 4.1 mm, 95% CI 2.8–5.5 mm, P<.001; late WMD 2.7 mm, 95% CI 0.8–4.6 mm, P=.005), but pain was not significantly different between groups at either timepoint (early WMD 0.4 VAS, P=.07; late WMD 0.5 VAS, P=.4).

Perioperative CS gives a mild-to-moderate reduction in edema and better range of motion after M3 surgery, but the pooled evidence does not support a significant pain benefit — this abstract-only source (publisher landing page, no retrievable full text) limits verification of heterogeneity/study-level detail.

## 세줄요약
12개 RCT를 풀링한 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA) — 사랑니(Third Molar, M3) 발치 후 코르티코스테로이드(Corticosteroid, CS) 주위수술기 투여가 부종·개구제한(Trismus)·통증에 미치는 영향을 조기(1–3일)·후기(>3일)로 나눠 평가.

CS 투여군에서 부종은 조기(표준화평균차, Standardized Mean Difference, SMD 1.4, 95% 신뢰구간 0.6–2.2, P<.001)·후기(SMD 1.1, 95% CI 0.1–2.0, P=.03) 모두 유의하게 감소, 개구제한도 조기(가중평균차, Weighted Mean Difference, WMD 4.1mm, 95% CI 2.8–5.5mm, P<.001)·후기(WMD 2.7mm, 95% CI 0.8–4.6mm, P=.005) 모두 유의하게 개선; 통증은 조기·후기 모두 군간 유의차 없음(조기 WMD 0.4 VAS, P=.07; 후기 WMD 0.5 VAS, P=.4).

주위수술기 CS 투여는 부종 감소·개구량 개선에는 경도~중등도 효과가 있으나 통증에는 유의한 효과 없음 — 본 소스는 초록만 확보된 출판사 랜딩페이지(전문 미확보)라 개별 연구·이질성(heterogeneity) 세부 검증에 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions
- One of the first meta-analyses to pool perioperative-corticosteroid trials in third-molar surgery separately by early (1–3d) vs late (>3d) postoperative windows, for three distinct outcomes (edema, trismus, pain).
- Establishes that the CS effect is NOT uniform across outcomes: robust, statistically significant benefit for edema and trismus, but no significant average benefit for pain — a nuance often lost when CS is described generically as "reduces postoperative morbidity."
- Uses random-effects models and metaregression (rather than fixed-effect pooling), appropriate given expected clinical heterogeneity across drugs/doses/routes/timing among the 12 included trials.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (random-effects models; metaregressions for early and late assessments).
- **Databases**: Not stated in the extracted abstract (full-text Methods section — including which databases, search dates, and inclusion/exclusion criteria — was not retrievable; see Limitations).
- **n**: 12 trials met inclusion criteria (patient-level n not stated in abstract).
- **Predictor variable**: perioperative corticosteroid exposure (yes vs no).
- **Outcomes**: edema (SMD), trismus (WMD), pain (WMD, VAS) — each assessed at early (1–3 days) and late (>3 days) postoperative periods.

Overall conclusion (authors' own words): "perioperative administration of corticosteroids produces a mild to moderate reduction in edema and improvement in range of motion after M3 removal" — notably, the conclusion sentence itself does not claim a pain benefit, consistent with the non-significant pooled pain results.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **This source is abstract-only.** The PDF is a browser-printed publisher landing page (joms.org "/abstract" URL visible in the footer of every page, "Get full text access — Log in, subscribe or purchase" banner present) — not the full-text article. Confirmed via PubMed MCP that this article (PMID 18718396) has no PMC full-text deposit. All numbers above come from the structured abstract; heterogeneity statistics (I², Q-test), the list of specific included trials, risk-of-bias assessment, databases searched, and search dates are NOT available from this source.
- The paper's own reference list (74 entries) contains numerous individually-plausible candidate RCTs (e.g., Skjelbred 1982, Bystedt 1985, Beirne 1986, Graziani 2006, Leone 2007, Markovic 2007, Mico-Llorens 2006, Ordulu 2006, Pedersen 1985, Montgomery 1990), but the abstract does not enumerate which 12 were actually pooled — do not assume any specific subset without the full text.
- Currency: this meta-analysis only pools trials available up to ~2007–2008; it necessarily does not include any post-2008 corticosteroid RCTs (e.g., the newer dexamethasone and prednisolone trials now also in this wiki).
- Patient-level n (total subjects across the 12 trials) is not stated in the retrieved abstract.

## 6. Related Work
- [[drug/analgesics/tamgadge-2025-preoperative-dexamethasone-third-molar-pain-swelling-trismus]]: newer single-drug RCT (dexamethasone vs placebo, split-mouth, n=60). Agrees with this paper's edema/trismus direction, but its pain result (significant reduction, p<0.001 at day 2 and day 7) genuinely conflicts with this paper's pooled null pain finding — flagged as a `contradicts` edge scoped to the pain outcome only.
- [[drug/analgesics/bakri-2024-prednisolone-prescribing-styles-third-molar]]: newer RCT comparing two prednisolone dosing *styles* (both arms received CS, no placebo arm) — not a direct CS-vs-no-CS test, so not directly comparable to this paper's pooled effect, but its finding that tapered dosing improves swelling/trismus (with no pain difference between dosing styles) is broadly consistent with this paper's edema/trismus > pain effect-size pattern.
- [[drug/analgesics/satpathi-2024-corticosteroids-dentistry-review]]: general narrative review of corticosteroid use across dental specialties; its brief claim that "dexamethasone 4–8 mg pre-extraction reduces post-surgical edema" is independently reinforced by this paper's quantitative pooled edema result.

## 7. Glossary
- **SMD (Standardized Mean Difference)**: effect-size measure for edema, expressed in pooled standard-deviation units to combine studies using different edema-measurement scales.
- **WMD (Weighted Mean Difference)**: effect-size measure for trismus (mm of mouth opening) and pain (VAS), used when studies share a common measurement unit.
- **VAS (Visual Analog Scale)**: patient-reported pain-intensity scale, typically 0–10 or 0–100.
- **M3**: Third molar (wisdom tooth).
- **Random-effects model**: meta-analytic pooling method that assumes true effect size varies across studies (vs a fixed-effect model assuming one common true effect) — appropriate given expected heterogeneity in CS drug/dose/route/timing across the 12 trials.
- **Metaregression**: regression technique used within the meta-analysis to explore how study-level covariates (e.g., CS drug/dose/timing) relate to effect-size variation across trials.
