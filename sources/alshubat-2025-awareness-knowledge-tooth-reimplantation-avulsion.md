## Why Ingested

치아 탈구 (Tooth Avulsion) 응급 관리에서 가장 큰 예후 결정 요인인 "즉시 재식 vs 올바른 보관"은 최초 목격자(보호자·교사·응급의)의 행동에 달려 있다. 그런데 실제 그들이 얼마나 알고 있는지를 체계적으로 종합한 근거가 위키에 없었다. 이 SR은 10개국 5,752명을 포괄한 19편 단면 연구를 종합해 의치 vs 비치과 집단 간 인식 격차를 정량화하고 교육 중재 효과를 제시한다. 임상에서 외상 아동 내원 시 보호자 교육·응급 지침 설명의 근거로 직접 활용 가능.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA 2020) of 19 cross-sectional studies (5,752 participants, 10 countries) assessing awareness and knowledge of tooth avulsion emergency management across dental and non-dental populations; no meta-analysis due to substantial heterogeneity.
Dental professionals/students showed ~50% average correct knowledge; non-dental populations (parents, teachers) averaged 15–20% — willingness to replant 2–37%, milk as medium 2.3–15.2%, <30-min window 14–33%; a brief 30-min educational session raised Kuwaiti teachers' storage media knowledge from 4% to 86%.
A global knowledge gap persists especially among first-responders, underscoring the need for targeted educational interventions integrated into school health programs and community campaigns.

## 세줄요약

19편 단면 연구 (5,752명, 10개국) SR — PRISMA 2020, 이질성으로 메타분석 미실시; 치아 탈구 (Tooth Avulsion) 응급처치 인식·지식을 치과 vs 비치과 집단별로 종합.
치과 집단 평균 정답률 ~50%, 비치과 집단 (부모·교사) ~15–20% — 즉시 재식 의향 2–37%, 우유를 보관 매체로 아는 비율 2.3–15.2%, 30분 이내 재식 시간 인지 14–33%; 쿠웨이트 교사 30분 강의로 보관 매체 정답률 4%→86%.
비치과 집단(최초 목격자)의 인식 격차가 임계적 — 학교 건강 프로그램·지역사회 인식 캠페인에 치아 외상 관리 모듈 통합이 시급하다.

## Key Contributions

- First SR to systematically quantify dental vs non-dental knowledge gap across 10 countries with 5,752 participants
- Establishes ~50% vs ~15–20% knowledge disparity between dental and lay populations
- Milk as storage medium: only 2.3–15.2% of parents across studies — most widespread practical knowledge gap
- Demonstrates 30-min educational intervention can raise key knowledge domains 4→86% (Kuwait teachers)
- Documents specific knowledge gaps even among dental professionals: splinting duration (26.7%), HBSS identification (40.1%)

## Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Systematic review (PRISMA 2020; no meta-analysis) |
| Databases | PubMed, ScienceDirect, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar |
| Date range | Inception to October 2025 |
| Studies included | 19 cross-sectional studies |
| Participants | 5,752 total; 10 countries |
| Quality tool | Modified Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS, 0–10) |

## Key Results

### Dental Populations (6 studies)

| Outcome | Range |
|---|---|
| Aware avulsed tooth can be replanted | 66–87.6% |
| Recommends immediate replantation | 37.1% (Brazil GDPs) |
| Identifies milk/saline as suitable medium | 36.1–83.4% |
| Knows <30-min replantation window | 41.6–63.9% |
| Handles by crown | 39.1–45% |
| Knows 7–14 day flexible splinting | 26.7% (Yemen) |

### Non-dental Populations (13 studies)

| Outcome | Range |
|---|---|
| Aware avulsed tooth can be replanted | 23.2–79% |
| Willing to attempt immediate replantation | 2–37% |
| Identifies milk as suitable medium | 2.3–15.2% of parents |
| Knows <30-min replantation window | 14–33% |
| Oman EPs (theoretical awareness) | 72% |
| Turkey EPs (would replant) | 28% |

### Educational Intervention (Al-Asfour 2008, Kuwait)

| Domain | Pre-training | Post-training |
|---|---|---|
| General knowledge | 39% | 97% |
| Correct storage medium | 4% | 86% |
| Extra-oral time awareness | 1% | 74% |

## Limitations

- Mostly cross-sectional surveys; self-reported knowledge may overestimate actual competence
- High methodological heterogeneity across studies (populations, definitions, instruments)
- Majority of evidence from Middle East and South Asia; limited geographic diversity
- English-language restriction may introduce language bias
- No meta-analysis (narrative synthesis only)
- No long-term outcome data (tooth survival after reimplantation)

## Glossary

- **Tooth Avulsion (치아 탈구)**: Complete displacement of a tooth from its socket due to traumatic injury; one of the most severe dental traumatic injuries
- **IADT (International Association of Dental Traumatology, 국제 치아 외상학회)**: Body setting evidence-based guidelines for dental trauma management, including avulsion
- **HBSS (Hank's Balanced Salt Solution)**: Isotonic, pH-balanced solution recognized as the ideal storage medium for avulsed teeth; rarely available outside dental/medical settings
- **Extra-oral dry time (구외 건조 시간)**: Time spent outside the mouth without moisture; >60 min dry dramatically worsens PDL viability and prognosis
- **PDL (Periodontal Ligament, 치주인대)**: Fibrous connective tissue attaching tooth root to alveolar bone; viability of remaining PDL cells is the key determinant of successful reimplantation
- **NOS (Newcastle-Ottawa Scale)**: Quality assessment tool for observational studies
