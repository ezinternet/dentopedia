## Why Ingested

구독 전용 저널(abstract-only)이지만 208개 임플란트라는 큰 단일 코호트로 치유 기간 ISQ 궤적과 영향 인자(부위·길이·직경·성별·표면)를 한 번에 기술하는 유일한 데이터셋이라 추가. "수개월 치유 후에도 낮은 ISQ" 질문 맥락에서 치유 후반기 부위별 최저치(후상악)와 길이 무영향·직경 영향이라는 인자 프로파일을 제시해, 기존 치유기 안정성 추이 페이지 [[implants/isq/shokri-2013-primary-secondary-stability-rfa-mandible]]를 네 부위·SLA/SLActive 포함으로 확장하고 ISQ 영향인자 종합 [[implants/isq/huang-2020-isq-clinical-significance-literature-review]]를 독립 데이터로 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study of 208 Straumann implants (164 standard SLA + 44 SLActive) in all four jaw regions, ISQ measured with the Osstell mentor at placement and during the healing period.

ISQ increased significantly during healing; posterior maxilla had the lowest ISQ (except at initial measurement); implant length had no significant influence (P > .05), while at the second measurement 4.8 mm diameter and male sex were significantly higher, and SLActive was transiently higher than SLA (P = 0) but equal at final measurement.

Repeated ISQ measurement of a specific implant has some diagnostic benefit — healing-period stability is best judged over time and by region, not by a single reading.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상연구: 모든 악궁 부위에 식립된 Straumann 임플란트 208개(표준 SLA 164개 + SLActive 44개)를 대상으로 Osstell mentor로 식립 시와 치유 기간 중 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)를 측정.

치유 중 ISQ가 유의하게 증가; 후상악이 최저(초회 측정 제외); 임플란트 길이는 유의 영향 없음(P > .05), 두 번째 측정에서 남성·4.8mm 직경이 유의하게 높았고(SLActive도 일시적으로 유의 높음, P = 0) 최종 측정에선 표면 간·4.8 vs 4.1mm 간 차이 없음.

반복 ISQ 측정이 진단적 이득을 가지며 치유 중 안정성은 단회 측정이 아니라 시간·부위 맥락으로 판단해야 함.

- 208 Straumann 임플란트(표준 SLA 164 + SLActive 44)를 네 악궁 부위로 나누어 치유 기간 중 ISQ 추이를 보고한 대규모 단일 코호트.
- 부위·길이·직경·성별·표면(표면처리, SLA vs SLActive)의 영향 인자 프로파일 제시: 후상악 최저, 길이 무영향, 4.8mm 직경 유의 우위, 두 번째 측정에서 남성 높음.
- 반복 ISQ 측정이 특정 임플란트의 진단적 이득을 가진다는 결론.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective clinical study (abstract-level detail only)
- **n**: 208 Straumann implants (164 standard SLA, 44 SLActive), all jaw regions
- **Measurement**: Osstell mentor, at implant placement and during the healing period
- **Factors analyzed**: jaw region, implant length, implant diameter, sex, surface type (SLA vs SLActive)
- **Patient count / exact timepoints / statistical model**: not stated in abstract

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Healing trend**: ISQ value ranges showed a significant increase during the healing period.
- **Region**: posterior maxilla had the lowest ISQ values (except for the initial measurement); no significant difference among anterior mandible, posterior mandible, and anterior maxilla.
- **Length**: no significant influence on ISQ (P > .05).
- **Sex**: second measurement significantly higher in men than in women (P < .05).
- **Diameter**: second measurement significantly higher at 4.8 mm than the others; at final measurement no significant difference between 4.8 and 4.1 mm, both higher than 3.3 mm (P < .05).
- **Surface (SLA vs SLActive)**: no significant difference at insertion; SLActive significantly higher at the second measurement (abstract reports P = 0); no significant difference at final measurement.
- **Conclusion**: repeated ISQ measurements of a specific implant have some diagnostic benefit.

## 6. Related Work

- [[implants/isq/shokri-2013-primary-secondary-stability-rfa-mandible]]: same-era weekly RFA tracking of 15 mandibular implants showing a week-4 stability dip and partial recovery; Guler's net-increase-over-healing finding is consistent with the recovery phase of that trajectory at much larger n and wider regional coverage.
- [[implants/isq/gomez-polo-2016-length-diameter-bone-quality-stability]]: independently reported length-null and diameter-relevant ISQ effects; Guler replicates the length-null but the two differ in diameter direction at later timepoints (Gomez-Polo: narrower higher secondary ISQ; Guler: wider higher final ISQ) — mild tension, likely range-dependent.
- [[implants/isq/vilchez-2025-sla-slactive-split-mouth-rct]]: split-mouth RCT finding no SLA/SLActive ISQ difference at placement or 8 weeks; Guler's transient mid-healing SLActive advantage that washes out by the final measurement is broadly consistent at full-text level.
- [[implants/isq/huang-2020-isq-clinical-significance-literature-review]]: review ranked implant length/diameter among ISQ factors with limited/inconsistent evidence; Guler's large cohort weights that assessment (length null, diameter significant).
- [[implants/isq/monje-2025-implant-design-stability-early-healing-rct]]: 8-timepoint early-healing ISQ monitoring; complementary serial-measurement methodology.

## 7. Glossary

- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: dimensionless stability index on a 1–100 scale derived from resonance frequency analysis; higher = greater stability.
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis)**: non-invasive method that measures a transducer's resonance frequency to quantify stiffness of the bone–implant interface.
- **SLA (Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched)**: Straumann's conventional roughened titanium surface.
- **SLActive**: chemically modified hydrophilic version of the SLA surface.
- **Osstell mentor**: RFA (공명주파수분석) 측정 장치; ISQ 값으로 안정성을 표시.
