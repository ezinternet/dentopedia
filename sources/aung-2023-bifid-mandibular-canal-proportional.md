## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/radiology/wamasing-2018-imaging-bifid-mandibular-canal]]는 단일기관 MRI 기반 서술적 연구(n=154, BMC 6.4%)로 이분 하악관(Bifid Mandibular Canal, BMC)의 발생률을 다뤘으나 표본이 작고 병변 의뢰 환자군에 국한됐다. 본 논문(Aung 2023)은 40편·17,714명 환자·31,973 반측하악골을 통합한 비례 메타분석(proportional meta-analysis)으로, CT/CBCT 기반 BMC 유병률(환자수준 20.7%, 반측하악골수준 14.3%)에 대한 정량적 pooled anchor를 제공해 기존 페이지의 근거 무게를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + proportional meta-analysis of 40 CT/CBCT studies (17,714 patients; 31,973 hemi-mandibles) quantifying the prevalence, laterality, sex distribution, and dimensions of bifid mandibular canal (BMC).

Pooled patient-level BMC prevalence was 20.7% (95% CI 15.9–26%) and hemi-mandible-level prevalence 14.3% (95% CI 10.7–18.3%); bilateral symmetry occurred in 22.8% of BMC-positive patients; mean accessory-canal length was 12.14 mm and mean diameter 1.54 mm; prevalence was significantly higher in males and on the right side.

Clinically, roughly 1 in 5 CT-imaged patients and 1 in 7 hemi-mandibles harbor a BMC — relevant to inferior alveolar nerve block failure and implant/surgical injury risk — but very high heterogeneity (I² ≈ 99%), moderate overall risk of bias (JBI 54.69%), and detected funnel-plot asymmetry (publication bias) in the prevalence estimates temper precision.

## 세줄요약

40편의 CT/CBCT 연구(환자 17,714명, 반측하악골 31,973례)를 통합한 체계적 문헌고찰 + 비례 메타분석(proportional meta-analysis)으로 이분 하악관(Bifid Mandibular Canal, BMC)의 유병률·좌우 비대칭·성별 분포·크기를 정량화했다.

통합 환자수준 BMC 유병률은 20.7%(95% CI 15.9–26%), 반측하악골수준 유병률은 14.3%(95% CI 10.7–18.3%)였고, BMC 양성 환자 중 22.8%가 양측성이었으며, 평균 부속관 길이는 12.14mm, 평균 직경은 1.54mm였다. 남성과 우측에서 유병률이 유의하게 높았다.

임상적으로 CT 촬영 환자 약 5명 중 1명, 반측하악골 약 7개 중 1개에서 BMC가 관찰되어 하치조신경(inferior alveolar nerve) 마취 실패·임플란트/수술 손상 위험과 관련되지만, 매우 높은 이질성(I² ≈ 99%), 중등도의 전반적 비뚤림 위험(JBI 평균 54.69%), 그리고 유병률 추정치에서 확인된 깔때기그림 비대칭(출판 편향)이 정밀도를 제한한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First proportional meta-analysis to separately pool **patient-level** (20.7%) and **hemi-mandible-level** (14.3%) prevalence of bifid mandibular canal (BMC), across 40 CT/CBCT studies spanning multiple continents and three competing classification systems (Naitoh, Norje, Langlais).
- Quantifies bilateral symmetrical distribution (22.8%) and pooled dimensions of the accessory canal (mean length 12.14 mm, mean diameter 1.54 mm) — previously only reported per-study, not pooled.
- Subgroup/moderator analyses: sex (male > female), laterality (right > left), continent (Europe > Asia > America for patient-level; Africa > Europe > Asia > America for hemi-mandible-level), classification system (Naitoh's > Norje's > Langlais's), reliability-testing status, and CBCT voxel size (positively associated with hemi-mandible-level prevalence).
- Explicit methodological-quality appraisal (JBI critical appraisal tool for prevalence studies) and publication-bias assessment (Doi plot, LFK index, trim-and-fill), rare additions in this literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Literature search (PubMed, Google Scholar, ResearchGate, ProQuest, Scopus, LILACS; inception–April 2022; no language/date restriction) combining BMC-related and CT/CBCT-related search terms. Forty studies met eligibility for both quality appraisal and quantitative synthesis. Outcomes extracted: patient-level and hemi-mandible-level BMC counts, bilateral symmetry, mean length/diameter of accessory canals (continuous), sex, laterality, continent, classification system used, CBCT voxel size, and reliability-testing status. Meta-analysis performed in MetaXL (proportions, random-effects) and Meta-Essentials (continuous outcomes via generic inverse-variance, random-effects). Heterogeneity assessed via I²; subgroup analyses stratified by sex, laterality, continent, classification system, and reliability testing; sensitivity analysis excluded studies without inter/intra-examiner reliability testing; moderator analysis regressed CBCT voxel size against prevalence. Publication bias assessed via funnel plot, Doi plot, and LFK index, with trim-and-fill for the diameter outcome. Methodological quality scored with the JBI critical appraisal tool for prevalence studies (8 items retained).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Patient-level BMC prevalence: 20.7% (95% CI 15.9–26%; range 1–67%; I²=99%), n=17,239 patients (36 studies).
- Hemi-mandible-level BMC prevalence: 14.3% (95% CI 10.7–18.3%; range 1–46%; I²=99%), n=31,603 hemi-mandibles (38 studies).
- Bilateral symmetry: 22.8% (95% CI 16.3–30%) of BMC-positive patients (29 studies, n=2,416).
- Mean accessory-canal length: 12.14 mm (95% CI 10.08–14.21 mm); mean diameter: 1.54 mm (95% CI 1.27–1.82 mm).
- Sex: male 22.6% vs female 18.9% patient-level prevalence (p=0.000074).
- Laterality: right 14% vs left 12.6% hemi-mandible-level prevalence (p=0.023).
- Continent (patient-level): Europe 26.5% > Asia 18.8% > America 13.9%; (hemi-mandible-level): Africa 32.8% > Europe 17.2% > Asia 13.3% > America 7.8%.
- Classification system (hemi-mandible-level): Naitoh's 16.9% > Norje's 11.3% > Langlais's 1.6% (p<0.00001) — attributed partly to Naitoh's inclusion of coronal-plane buccolingual canals.
- Overall JBI quality score: 54.69% (moderate risk of bias); sample-size calculation was the weakest domain (absent in 90% of studies).
- Publication bias: major funnel-plot asymmetry detected for both patient-level and hemi-mandible-level prevalence (LFK index 2.66 and 2.78); no asymmetry for bilateral symmetry.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Very high heterogeneity (I²≈98–99%) even after subgroup stratification by classification system; moderate average risk of bias (JBI 54.69%) driven mainly by absent sample-size calculation and inconsistent reliability testing (only ~50% of studies performed inter/intra-examiner agreement testing); detected publication bias in prevalence estimates; only CT/CBCT-based studies were pooled (MRI, considered the soft-tissue gold standard, was excluded from quantitative synthesis, so this SR+MA cannot directly resolve the CT-vs-MRI detection gap noted in [[wiki/radiology/wamasing-2018-imaging-bifid-mandibular-canal]]); sparse data for African/Australian populations. Authors call for future pooled sensitivity/specificity analysis of CBCT against MRI as reference standard.

## 7. Glossary

- **Bifid Mandibular Canal (BMC)**: an anatomic variant in which the mandibular canal branches into two or more accessory canals from its main trunk.
- **Naitoh's / Norje's / Langlais's classification**: three competing radiographic classification systems for BMC subtypes, differing mainly in imaging plane (2D vs 3D) and inclusion of buccolingual-type canals.
- **JBI critical appraisal tool**: Joanna Briggs Institute checklist for assessing methodological quality/risk of bias in prevalence studies.
- **Doi plot / LFK index**: graphical/quantitative tools for detecting funnel-plot asymmetry (publication bias) as an alternative to Egger's test, suited to proportion meta-analyses.
- **Trim-and-fill**: a method for estimating and adjusting for missing studies in a funnel plot to correct for suspected publication bias.
