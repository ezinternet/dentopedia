## Why Ingested

The wiki already holds this author group's later, broader umbrella review [[wiki/implants/giok-2026-factors-implant-failure-umbrella-review]] (overall implant failure, search to June 2024) and a larger, independent 49-SR umbrella review specifically on peri-implantitis risk factors, [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/tarce-2025-peri-implantitis-risk-assessment-pira-part1]] (search to August 2021). This 2024 paper (Giok, search to October 2023) sits between them: it is the same author group's earlier, peri-implantitis-specific predecessor to their 2026 implant-failure review, and its search window is later than Tarce's cutoff, so it independently corroborates — rather than duplicates — Tarce's finding that periodontitis history and smoking are the two best-evidenced peri-implantitis risk factors, from a partially non-overlapping meta-analysis pool (12 articles / 41 meta-analyses, none reaching "convincing" credibility).

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of meta-analyses of observational studies (PROSPERO CRD42024512408) — 12 articles describing 41 unique meta-analyses on peri-implantitis risk factors, searched PubMed/Scopus/Cochrane/Epistemonikos from inception to October 2023.

Of 41 associations (24 significant), presence of periodontitis (OR 3.84, 95% CI 2.58-5.72) and cigarette smoking (RR 2.07, 95% CI 1.41-3.04) graded "highly suggestive"; 8 further associations (diabetes mellitus, hyperglycaemia, lack of prophylaxis, chronic periodontal disease history, anterior-jaw implant location, OPG gene polymorphisms, lack of keratinized mucosa) graded "suggestive"; none reached "convincing."

Periodontitis history and smoking are the most robustly evidenced peri-implantitis risk factors and should inform preoperative consent and monitoring; abstract-only page (paywalled Elsevier full text not retrieved), so per-study data and full bias assessment are not captured here.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantitative + qualitative umbrella review restricted specifically to peri-implantitis as the outcome (versus broader implant-failure or general peri-implant-disease reviews), synthesizing 41 meta-analytic associations from 12 source articles.
- Applies a formal credibility-grading scheme (convincing / highly suggestive / suggestive / weak / nonsignificant) with explicit bias assessment across all included meta-analyses.
- Identifies periodontitis history and cigarette smoking as the only two associations reaching "highly suggestive" credibility, with a further 8 associations at "suggestive" level.
- Frames the highly-suggestive/suggestive risk factors as candidates for the preoperative informed-consent process, not just academic risk stratification.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review of meta-analyses of observational studies, registered PROSPERO CRD42024512408.
- **Search**: PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, Epistemonikos, from inception to October 2023.
- **Screening**: 5002 publications → 51 full-text articles assessed for eligibility → 12 included articles describing 41 unique meta-analyses.
- **Grading**: Standard umbrella-review credibility tiers (convincing / highly suggestive / suggestive / weak / nonsignificant), incorporating bias assessment (methodology consistent with the credibility-ceiling approach used in related umbrella reviews in this wiki, e.g. Tarce 2025 and Giok 2026).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only retrieval: individual meta-analysis characteristics, forest plots, heterogeneity/prediction-interval data, and the full bias-assessment detail are not captured in this source page.
- As with sibling umbrella reviews in this wiki (Tarce 2025, Giok 2026), no association reached "convincing" credibility, underscoring that peri-implantitis risk-factor evidence remains short of the highest confidence tier despite plausible biological mechanisms.
- The authors explicitly call for more high-quality primary studies to upgrade "suggestive" associations (diabetes, keratinized mucosa width, implant location, OPG polymorphisms, etc.).

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: A systematic review of existing meta-analyses/systematic reviews on a topic, synthesizing evidence one level above individual meta-analyses.
- **Credibility grading (convincing/highly suggestive/suggestive/weak/nonsignificant)**: A tiered framework (per Ioannidis-style umbrella-review methodology) assessing the robustness of an association across sample size, heterogeneity, bias, and effect-size consistency.
- **OPG (osteoprotegerin) gene polymorphisms**: Genetic variants in the OPG gene, part of the RANK/RANKL/OPG bone-remodeling pathway, studied here as a candidate genetic risk factor for peri-implantitis.
- **Keratinized mucosal width**: The band of keratinized tissue surrounding an implant, hypothesized to protect against peri-implant inflammation when adequate.
