## Why Ingested

Severe localized ridge defects are the main reason for autogenous block grafting, and the `bone-regeneration` folder needs the symphysis-graft technique source. This Misch and Misch 1995 review describes the symphysis block graft for localized repair before implant placement, and it pairs with the incision-design review in [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/abdulkarim-2021-bone-graft-cbct-bone-density-pilot]] context.

## Three-line Summary

Review (Implant Dentistry, 1995) of the mandibular symphysis graft for repairing severe localized ridge defects before implant placement — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The technique is credited with easy access, adequate bone quantity, a corticocancellous block morphology, low morbidity and minimal resorption, with improved bone density and shorter healing than other repair methods.

The abstract asserts these advantages without citing comparative data; graft-resorption and survival figures are not given.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Implant Dentistry, 1995) — 임플란트 식립 전 심한 국소 치조제 결손 수복을 위한 하악 결합부 (symphysis) 골이식 — 초록만 수집 (전문 미확보).

접근성, 국소 수복에 충분한 골량, 피질-해면골 블록 형태, 낮은 이환율, 최소 흡수, 다른 방법보다 나은 골밀도와 짧은 치유 기간을 장점으로 제시.

초록은 이 장점들을 비교 자료 없이 주장하며, 흡수율과 생존율 수치는 없음.
