## Why Ingested
상악 구치부 저밀도 골에서 표면 처리(SAE vs 친수성 SAE)가 ISQ 안정성 궤적에 미치는 영향을 16주간 추적한 RCT. 기존 [[implants/isq/andersson-2019-rfa-factors-5year-neoss-survival]]이 다루지 않은 친수성 표면의 ISQ ≥70 도달 속도 차이(HR 2.24)를 직접 제공한다.

## One-line Summary
RCT (n=64 implants, 21 patients, posterior maxilla) showing hydrophilic SAE surface achieves ISQ ≥70 2.24× faster than standard SAE over 16 weeks.

## 한줄요약
상악 구치부 RCT (64개 임플란트, 21명): 친수성 SAE 표면이 일반 SAE보다 ISQ ≥70 도달 2.24배 빠름 (Cox HR 2.24, CI 1.62–3.11).

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Clinical Implant Dentistry and Related Research, 2017;19(5):770–775
- Published: 2017 (received Feb 2017, accepted May 2017)
- Implant system: Neodent Drive CM (SAE = Neoporos; hydrophilic SAE = Acqua), 4.3 × 10 mm conical compressive
- ISQ device: Osstell (Integration Diagnostics AB, Göteborg, Sweden) with Smartpeg type A3
- ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT02134743
- Funding/COI: Not reported; Neodent implants used but no explicit sponsorship declared

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT isolating surface chemistry (SAE vs hydrophilic SAE) with identical implant macro-geometry in posterior maxilla
- Hydrophilic surface shows significantly higher ISQ from week 5 onward (ANOVA p<0.01)
- Cox regression quantifies acceleration: HR 2.24 (95% CI 1.62–3.11) for reaching ISQ ≥70
- 100% survival at 1-year follow-up for both surfaces — ISQ advantage does not translate to survival difference in this small sample
- Establishes that hydrophilicity shortens the "ISQ dip" window (weeks 1–5) in low-density posterior maxilla

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Single-centre split-mouth RCT (University of São Paulo Dental School), Dec 2013–Jul 2015
- **Population**: 21 healthy non-smoking patients (ASA I), age 27–64 (mean 49 ± 3.6), 8M/13F; posterior maxilla with ≥8mm bone height, ≥6.3mm ridge width, D3/D4 density
- **Implants**: 64 implants — 32 SAE (Neoporos) and 32 hydrophilic SAE (Acqua), same 4.3×10mm conical compressive design
- **Randomisation**: Site-level (not patient-level), Research Randomizer simple block of 64; even = control (SAE), odd = test; surgeon and assistant not blinded, patient and data collector blinded
- **ISQ measurement timepoints**: T0 (immediate post-surgery), T1 (1w), T2 (2w), T3 (3w), T4 (5w), T5 (8w), T6 (12w), T7 (16w); buccal + mesial, average recorded
- **Statistical methods**: ANOVA repeated measures + Greenhouse-Geisser, Kaplan-Meier with ISQ ≥70 cutoff, Cox regression for time-to-ISQ≥70 hazard ratio

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Primary ISQ trajectory**: Hydrophilic > SAE from T5 (8 weeks) to T7 (16 weeks); ANOVA p<0.01
- **ISQ range**: SAE 42–81; hydrophilic SAE 32.5–82.5
- **Time-to-ISQ≥70**: Hazard ratio 2.24 (95% CI 1.62–3.11, Cox regression p<0.01) favoring hydrophilic
- **Mean insertion torque**: SAE 36.06 ± 5.99 Ncm; hydrophilic 34.66 ± 6.94 Ncm (no significant difference)
- **Dropout/failure**: 0 dropouts at 16 weeks; 1 patient dropout and 2 implants lost to follow-up at 1 year; 100% survival both groups at 1 year
- **Complication note**: No implant failures; no peri-implant complications reported

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single-centre, single-surgeon study limits external validity
- Split-mouth design risks contamination bias (immune response, healing factors shared between sites)
- Only ISQ ≥70 used as surrogate endpoint; no clinical loading outcomes beyond survival at 1 year
- D3/D4 bone only — results may not apply to D1/D2 bone where ISQ differences could be smaller
- Neodent implant-specific; generalizability to other implant systems unclear
- Small n limits power to detect survival differences

## 7. Glossary
- **SAE**: Sandblasted, Acid-Etched (standard hydrophobic titanium surface)
- **Hydrophilic SAE (Acqua)**: Same SAE microstructure but chemically modified to enhance wettability (stored in isotonic NaCl solution to preserve hydrophilicity)
- **ISQ ≥70**: Arbitrary threshold used in Kaplan-Meier survival analysis, not a validated clinical loading decision cutoff in this study
- **HR (hazard ratio)**: 2.24 means hydrophilic implants reach ISQ ≥70 at 2.24× the "rate" of standard SAE implants at any given time point
- **D3/D4 bone**: Lekholm & Zarb classification for low-density posterior maxillary bone
