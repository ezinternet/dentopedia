## Why Ingested

기존 [[orofacial-pain/korczeniewska-2022-pathophysiology-post-traumatic-trigeminal-neuropathic]]의 말초 캐스케이드(이온채널 재편·위성신경교세포 감작)는 Aβ/Aδ/C 섬유를 언급하지만 그 위에 작동하는 구체적 분자 마커·후성유전 스위치를 제시하지 않는다. 본 논문(Guan 2025)은 CBLN2라는 단일 유전자를 Aδ/Aβ/C-fiber LTMR에 명시적으로 매핑하고, TET3 매개 프로모터 탈메틸화라는 새로운 후성유전 기전으로 삼차신경병증통증(TNP) 말초감작을 설명해 그 공백을 채운다.

## Three-line Summary

Animal (mouse) study: partial infraorbital nerve transection (pIONT) model of trigeminal neuropathic pain, combined with cross-species (human/mouse) single-nucleus RNA-seq of trigeminal ganglion (TG) cell atlases and in vitro (ND7/23, Neuro-2a) mechanistic assays.

CBLN2 is a marker gene predominantly expressed in Aδ-LTMR (human NF3 56.3%, mouse Aδ-LTMR ~70%) with broader Aβ/cLTMR expression in mice; CBLN2 mRNA/protein rise sustainedly in TG after pIONT (peak day 3–10, persisting to day 21), driven by TET3-mediated promoter CpG demethylation, and both Cbln2 and Tet3 knockdown attenuate mechanical allodynia; exogenous CBLN2 increases TG neuron excitability and pro-inflammatory cytokines (Cxcl1/9/10, Cxcr2, Il-6) via ERK/MAPK signaling, blocked by the MEK inhibitor PD98059.

Clinical implication: identifies a novel epigenetic (TET3-CBLN2-ERK axis) drug target for peripheral sensitization in trigeminal neuropathic pain, but evidence is confined to a rodent nerve-injury model (mostly male + limited female replication), with human translational relevance of the murine methylation/expression pattern unconfirmed.

## 세줄요약

동물(마우스) 연구: 삼차신경병증통증(Trigeminal Neuropathic Pain, TNP) 부분 안와하신경 절단(pIONT) 모델과, 사람·마우스 교차종 단일세포핵 RNA-seq(snRNA-seq) 삼차신경절(Trigeminal Ganglion, TG) 세포지도 분석 및 시험관내(ND7/23, Neuro-2a) 기전 실험을 결합.

세레벨린 2 (Cerebellin 2, CBLN2)는 Aδ 저역치 기계수용체(Aδ-LTMR)에 우세 발현하는 마커 유전자(사람 NF3 56.3%, 마우스 Aδ-LTMR 약 70%)이며 마우스에서는 Aβ/cLTMR에도 넓게 발현; pIONT 후 TG에서 CBLN2 mRNA/단백이 지속 상승(3–10일 정점, 21일까지 지속)하는데 이는 텐-일레븐 전좌효소 3 (Ten-Eleven Translocation 3, TET3) 매개 프로모터 CpG 탈메틸화가 원인이며, Cbln2·Tet3 넉다운 모두 기계적 이질통(Mechanical Allodynia)을 완화; 외인성 CBLN2는 ERK/MAPK 신호를 통해 TG 뉴런 흥분성과 염증성 사이토카인(Cxcl1/9/10, Cxcr2, Il-6)을 증가시키며 MEK 억제제(PD98059)로 차단됨.

임상적 의미: 삼차신경병증통증 말초감작에 대한 새로운 후성유전(TET3-CBLN2-ERK 축) 치료 표적을 제시하나, 근거는 설치류 신경손상 모델(주로 수컷, 암컷은 일부만 재확인)에 국한되며 사람에서의 메틸화/발현 패턴 번역 가능성은 미확인.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. First characterization of cerebellin (CBLN) family gene expression in peripheral sensory ganglia (trigeminal ganglion) — prior CBLN/pain literature was restricted to CNS (spinal cord, central amygdala).
2. Cross-species (human/mouse) TG single-nucleus atlas mapping shows CBLN2 is a cell-type marker gene predominantly expressed in Aδ-LTMR (human) and Aδ/Aβ/cLTMR (mouse), with upregulation confirmed in an inflammatory-soup (IS) migraine model but not a cortical-spreading-depression (CSD) model.
3. Establishes a causal TET3 → CBLN2 promoter demethylation → CBLN2 overexpression epigenetic cascade in trigeminal neuropathic pain (TNP), via MSP/bisulfite sequencing and Tet3 siRNA knockdown.
4. Demonstrates CBLN2 is functionally required for both initiation (concurrent knockdown) and maintenance (delayed day-7 knockdown) phases of pIONT-induced allodynia.
5. Links CBLN2 to a downstream ERK-dependent mechanism: increased TG neuron excitability (reduced rheobase, increased AP firing) and upregulation of pro-inflammatory chemokines/cytokines (Cxcl1, Cxcr2, Cxcl9, Cxcl10, Il-6), both reversible with the MEK inhibitor PD98059.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Animal model**: ICR mice (male, 6–8 weeks), partial infraorbital nerve transection (pIONT, ~50% ION ligated + transected distal to ligation) vs sham surgery, following IASP guidelines.
- **Bioinformatics**: integrated cross-species snRNA-seq analysis of TG cell atlases (human + mouse), resolving 15 transcriptionally distinct clusters (NF1/NF2/NF3/cLTMR etc.); reanalysis of published murine TG datasets (8 integrated datasets) and bulk RNA-seq post-pIONT; comparison across two migraine models (inflammatory soup [IS] and cortical spreading depression [CSD]).
- **Behavioral**: mechanical allodynia via von Frey filaments (0.02 g, 0.16 g/0.60 g), blinded scoring 0–5 (facial withdrawal/wipes) on ipsilateral vibrissal pad.
- **Molecular**: qRT-PCR, Western blot (anti-CBLN2, anti-ERK/pERK), ELISA, immunofluorescence for CBLN2/pERK localization; DNA methylation analysis via methylation-specific PCR (MSP) and bisulfite sequencing PCR (BSP) targeting an 11-CpG-site region of the Cbln2 promoter (CpG island −905 to −465).
- **Loss-of-function**: intra-TG injection of Cbln2- and Tet3-targeting siRNA (screened in ND7/23 and Neuro-2a cells respectively), administered either concurrent with pIONT (initiation phase) or at day 7/10 post-pIONT (maintenance phase).
- **Gain-of-function / pathway dissection**: intra-TG injection of recombinant CBLN2 (rCBLN2) protein in naïve mice; MEK inhibitor PD98059 pretreatment to test ERK-dependency; whole-cell current-clamp electrophysiology on acutely isolated TG neurons (rheobase, AP firing frequency, membrane properties).
- **Sex comparison**: primary experiments in male mice, with key CBLN2/ERK/allodynia findings replicated in female mice (Supplementary Fig. 2) showing no significant sex difference.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- CBLN2 (mouse Aδ-LTMR): 66.6–69.5% of cluster cells expressing (human NF3/Aδ: 56.3%); no sex dimorphism in Aδ-LTMR expression (male 69.5% vs female 72.9%).
- pIONT-induced Cbln2 mRNA upregulation in TG: day 1 = 2.6±0.37-fold, day 3 = 3.4±0.79-fold, day 10 = 3.6±1.14-fold (all p<0.001 vs sham), persisting at day 21 = 2.0±0.44-fold (p<0.001); CBLN2 protein up 1.9-fold (Western, day 10, p<0.01) and 1.3-fold (ELISA, day 10, p<0.05).
- Cbln2 siRNA knockdown: intra-TG injection reduced Cbln2 to 55.5–59.7% of NC levels; alleviated mechanical allodynia both when given concurrent with pIONT and when given at day 7 post-pIONT (established/maintenance phase).
- Cbln2 promoter demethylation: MSP and BSP both showed significantly reduced CpG methylation in pIONT vs sham TG (p<0.05 and p<0.01, respectively); parallel Tet1/Tet2/Tet3 qRT-PCR showed only Tet3 (and not Tet1/Tet2) significantly increased post-pIONT.
- Tet3 siRNA knockdown (day 10 post-pIONT): attenuated mechanical allodynia (p<0.001) and reduced both Tet3 and Cbln2 mRNA (p<0.01 each) — establishing the causal TET3→CBLN2 link.
- rCBLN2 intra-TG injection: induced facial mechanical allodynia dose-dependently; reduced rheobase current and increased AP firing frequency in TG neurons (p<0.01–0.001).
- ERK activation: rCBLN2 induced pERK elevation in vitro (1.7-fold at 15 min, 1.8–2.1-fold at 30–60 min, p<0.01) and in vivo (1.2-fold at 3h, 1.9-fold increase in pERK⁺ neuron proportion, p<0.001).
- MEK inhibition (PD98059): attenuated CBLN2-induced allodynia and neuronal hyperexcitability; suppressed CBLN2-induced pro-inflammatory cytokine induction by 21–42% (Cxcl1, Cxcr2, Cxcl9, Cxcl10, Il-6 rose 2.4–37.9-fold in vitro and 3.2–358.2-fold in vivo with rCBLN2 alone).
- Findings replicated in female mice for CBLN2-induced pERK activation and PD98059 rescue of allodynia, with no obvious sex difference reported.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Entirely rodent (mouse) evidence; human relevance of the murine CBLN2 methylation/expression pattern is explicitly flagged by the authors as requiring validation — human TG shows CBLN2 restricted mainly to NF3/Aδ-LTMR (56.3%) whereas mouse shows broader NF2/Aβ (48.0%) and cLTMR (39.1%) expression, a cross-species divergence the authors caution against over-interpreting for therapeutic targeting.
- CBLN2's TG-neuron interaction partner/receptor is not identified (unlike its established GluD1/GluD2 receptor interactions in the CNS).
- Direct TET3 binding to the Cbln2 promoter CpG island is inferred from parallel expression/knockdown data, not directly demonstrated (e.g., no ChIP or hMeDIP data reported in this extract); authors state this as needed future work.
- Systemic ERK inhibition carries known off-target risk; authors note localized delivery strategies would be needed for translational development.
- Primary cohort is male mice; female replication is present but limited to a subset of endpoints (Supplementary Fig. 2), not the full experimental cascade (e.g., methylation assays not repeated in females per this extract).

## 6. Related Work

- Builds on prior CNS-restricted cerebellin/pain literature (CBLN1-GluD1 in central amygdala and spinal dorsal horn) by extending cerebellin biology to a peripheral sensory ganglion for the first time.
- Complements [[orofacial-pain/korczeniewska-2022-pathophysiology-post-traumatic-trigeminal-neuropathic]]'s peripheral PTNP cascade (chemokines, ion channel remodeling, satellite glial cell activation) by supplying a specific molecular marker (CBLN2) and an upstream epigenetic switch (TET3) operating within that cascade.
- Complements [[orofacial-pain/ke-2025-trpc4-trigeminal-neuropathic-pain-mice]] as a second ion-channel/molecular-signaling axis (ERK-dependent, downstream of CBLN2) demonstrated with bidirectional (knockdown + gain-of-function) causal evidence in the same pIONT mouse model family.

## 7. Glossary

- **CBLN2 (Cerebellin 2)**: synaptic-organizer glycoprotein family member; here identified as a cell-type marker gene for Aδ/Aβ-LTMR neurons in the trigeminal ganglion, upregulated after nerve injury.
- **TET3 (Ten-Eleven Translocation 3)**: DNA demethylase enzyme ("eraser") that oxidizes 5-methylcytosine, permitting gene activation; drives Cbln2 promoter demethylation in this model.
- **pIONT (partial Infraorbital Nerve Transection)**: mouse model of trigeminal neuropathic pain via partial ligation + transection of the infraorbital nerve branch.
- **LTMR (Low-Threshold Mechanoreceptor)**: sensory neuron subtype (Aβ, Aδ, C-fiber classes) responding to innocuous mechanical stimuli; implicated in allodynia when sensitized.
- **snRNA-seq (single-nucleus RNA sequencing)**: transcriptomic profiling method used here for cross-species TG cell atlas construction.
- **MSP/BSP (Methylation-Specific PCR / Bisulfite Sequencing PCR)**: assays for quantifying DNA CpG methylation status.
- **ERK/MAPK (Extracellular signal-Regulated Kinase / Mitogen-Activated Protein Kinase)**: intracellular signaling pathway; downstream effector of CBLN2 driving neuronal hyperexcitability and cytokine induction here.
- **PD98059**: pharmacological MEK inhibitor used to block ERK activation.
- **rCBLN2 (recombinant CBLN2)**: exogenous CBLN2 protein used for gain-of-function intra-TG injection experiments.
