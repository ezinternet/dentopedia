## Why Ingested

기존 [[oral-surgery/nerve-injury/juodzbalys-2011-ian-injury-implant-etiology-treatment-review]]의 ≥2 mm 안전 거리 권고가 CBCT·디지털 플래닝 시대에도 유효한지 의문. 본 SR+MA (Peña-Cardelles 2025)는 임플란트-하악관 거리(0–1 / 1–2 / ≥2 mm / 관 침범)를 계층화해 신경감각변화(NA) 발생률을 정량화하고, 2 mm가 아닌 **1 mm**가 안전할 수 있다는 갱신 근거를 제시해 고전적 2 mm 규칙을 refine한다. 또한 [[oral-surgery/nerve-injury/reus-2024-neuropathic-injury-dental-implant-prevalence-sr]]의 하악 임플란트 신경병증 유병률 데이터와 거리-종속적 위험 구배를 상호 보강하고, [[oral-surgery/nerve-injury/li-2022-ian-position-nerve-injury-sr-ma]]의 IAN 위치–신경손상 SR-MA를 확장하며, [[overviews/ian-nerve-injury-management-overview]] synthesis의 안전거리·예방 분기를 갱신하는 입력.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA; 14 observational studies; meta-analytic subset ~1636 patients) evaluating neurosensory alteration (NA) incidence stratified by implant-apex-to-mandibular-canal distance.

NA incidence was 0% at ≥2 mm and 0% at 1–2 mm, but jumped to 68% at 0–1 mm and 53% when the implant intruded into the canal; intra-canal intrusion produced hypoesthesia in 42% and hyperesthesia/neuropathic pain in 46%.

A 1 mm apex-to-canal distance may be sufficient to avoid NA — updating the classic 2 mm rule — but the estimate rests on heterogeneous retrospective studies with very wide confidence intervals, so prospective RCTs are needed.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (PRISMA; 관찰연구 14편; 메타분석 대상 약 1636명) — 임플란트 첨부–하악관(mandibular canal) 거리별로 신경감각변화(Neurosensory Alteration, NA) 발생률을 계층화 평가.

NA 발생률은 ≥2 mm에서 0%, 1–2 mm에서 0%였으나 0–1 mm에서 68%, 관 침범 시 53%로 급증; 관 내 침범은 저감각(hypoesthesia) 42%, 과감각/신경병증통증(hyperesthesia/neuropathic pain) 46%를 유발.

임플란트 첨부–하악관 **1 mm** 거리가 NA 예방에 충분할 수 있어 고전적 2 mm 규칙을 갱신하지만, 근거는 이질적·후향적 연구와 매우 넓은 신뢰구간에 기반하므로 전향적 RCT 확인이 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to **stratify NA incidence by discrete implant-apex-to-mandibular-canal distance bands** (0–1, 1–2, ≥2 mm, plus canal intrusion) rather than reporting a single lumped incidence.
- Provides a machine-usable distance→risk gradient: a sharp threshold effect between the 0–1 mm band (68% NA) and the 1–2 mm band (0%).
- Separately meta-analyzes **NA type** (hypoesthesia vs hyperesthesia/neuropathic pain) by distance band — canal intrusion uniquely carries substantial hyperesthesia/neuropathic pain risk (46%).
- Argues the classic **2 mm safety margin (Misch & Crawford 1990; Bartling 1999)** can be refined to **1 mm** in the CBCT/digital-planning era, on a much larger pooled sample (1636 patients) than Bartling's original 94.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **PICO**: In patients receiving implants (P), how does implant-to-mandibular-canal distance (I/C) affect NA development (O)?
- **Inclusion**: human RCTs, controlled trials, prospective/retrospective cohorts, cross-sectional, case-control, case series (>20 cases), case reports, observational; English; last 10 years.
- **Exclusion**: animal/cadaver/lab studies, prior SRs/MAs, duplicates, books, letters, commentaries.
- **Screening funnel**: 1254 records → 771 (10-yr) → 761 (English) → 667 (human) → 496 (de-duplicated) → 58 full text → **14 included**.
- **Meta-analysis**: SPSS v28; fixed-/random-effects by heterogeneity; forest plots. Distance groups 0–1 / 1–2 / ≥2 mm plus intra-canal intrusion. NA categorized per IASP as hypoesthesia vs hyperesthesia/neuropathic pain.
- **Risk of bias**: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale — 5 good, several medium, remainder low quality.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Overall NA incidence by distance (meta-analysis, ~1636 patients):**

| Implant-apex to canal | NA incidence | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 2 mm | 0% | 0.00–0.00 |
| 1–2 mm | 0% | 0.01–0.02 |
| 0–1 mm | 68% | 0.09–1.28 |
| Intrusion into canal | 53% | 0.13–0.82 |

**NA type by distance:**

| Distance | Hypoesthesia | Hyperesthesia / neuropathic pain |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 2 mm | 0% | 0% |
| 1–2 mm | 0% | 0% |
| 0–1 mm | 58% (CI 0.06–1.11) | 10% (CI 0.1–0.3) |
| Canal intrusion | 42% (CI 0.42–1.26) | 46% (CI 0.16–1.08) |

- Both the 0–1 mm and intrusion groups differed significantly from the 1–2 mm and ≥2 mm groups.
- IAN is the most commonly injured nerve, followed by the lingual nerve. Numbness was the single most common symptom (91% in one series).
- Recovery narrative: ~80% regain normal sensation within 6 months, 91% within 1 year; in one cohort 23/1065 had disturbance at 1 month, all resolved by 13 months.
- Demographics: nerve injury more common in women (one study: women 3.29× more likely than men; older women >60 most affected).
- Planning: in one study 76% of NA cases had relied on periapical/panoramic radiographs rather than virtual/CBCT planning. Even with CBCT planning, injury occurred via 0.86 ± 0.5 mm canal intrusion — underscoring computer-assisted implant surgery (CAIS) to prevent plan-to-placement deviation.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Heterogeneous, largely retrospective** included studies; NA definitions/reporting varied across studies, limiting comparability.
- **Very wide confidence intervals** for the 0–1 mm (0.09–1.28) and intrusion bands — the point estimates (68%, 53%) are directional, not precise; some CIs exceed 1.0, reflecting small pooled study counts per band.
- Meta-analysis conducted on a reduced number of studies.
- The 1 mm recommendation is a refinement, not a validated replacement of the 2 mm rule; the authors explicitly call for prospective RCTs.

## 7. Glossary

- **IAN (Inferior Alveolar Nerve, 하치조신경)**: mandibular sensory nerve running in the mandibular canal; most commonly injured nerve in implant surgery.
- **Mandibular canal (하악관)**: bony canal transmitting the IAN; the reference structure for implant safety-distance measurement.
- **NA (Neurosensory Alteration, 신경감각변화)**: altered sensation encompassing hypoesthesia, hyperesthesia, paresthesia, dysesthesia, and neuropathic pain.
- **Hypoesthesia (저감각)**: reduced sensation/numbness.
- **Hyperesthesia (과감각)**: heightened/painful sensation, often with neuropathic pain.
- **Neuropraxia / Axonotmesis / Neurotmesis**: Seddon nerve-injury grades from reversible conduction block to complete severance.
- **Safety distance (안전 거리)**: recommended minimum implant-apex-to-canal distance (classically 2 mm; refined to 1 mm here).
- **CBCT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, 콘빔전산화단층촬영)**: 3D imaging gold standard for mandibular canal localization.
- **CAIS (Computer-Assisted Implant Surgery, 컴퓨터 보조 임플란트 수술)**: guided translation of virtual plan to placement to minimize deviation.
