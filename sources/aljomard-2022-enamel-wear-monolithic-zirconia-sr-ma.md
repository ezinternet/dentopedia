## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (25 in-vitro studies met SR criteria; 3 included in meta-analysis at 50N, 240–250k cycles) of enamel wear against monolithic zirconia restorations (J Esthet Restor Dent 2022;34(3):473–489).

Enamel wear against monolithic zirconia was within statistically accepted levels in the meta-analysis; polished monolithic zirconia caused significantly less enamel wear than glazed zirconia across studies.

Surface texture (polishing vs. glazing) is the essential determinant of enamel wear against monolithic zirconia — clinicians should polish rather than glaze monolithic zirconia occlusal surfaces; the small meta-analysis base (3 studies) limits generalizability.

## 세줄요약

단일체(monolithic) 지르코니아 수복물에 대한 법랑질 마모 SR+MA(in-vitro 25편 SR 기준; 메타분석 50N·240–250k cycle 기준 3편 포함; J Esthet Restor Dent 2022;34(3):473-489).

메타분석 결과 단일체 지르코니아 대비 법랑질 마모는 통계적 허용 수준 이내; 연마(polishing)된 지르코니아가 유약(glazing) 처리 지르코니아보다 법랑질 마모를 유의하게 적게 일으킴.

표면 처리 방법(연마 vs 유약)이 법랑질 마모의 핵심 결정 인자 — 임상적으로 단일체 지르코니아 교합면은 유약보다 연마를 권장; 메타분석 포함 연구 3편으로 일반화 한계 있음.

## 1. Key Results
- 3968 articles screened; 25 met SR criteria; 3 included in meta-analysis (50N, 240-250k cycles)
- Meta-analysis: enamel wear against monolithic Zr = within statistically accepted levels
- Polished monolithic Zr: less enamel wear than glazed
- Conclusion: surface texture (polish vs glaze) is essential determinant of enamel wear
- Published online 2021; volume 2022
