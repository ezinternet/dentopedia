SR+MA (in vitro), J Prosthet Dent 2026. 63 studies included; search January 2015–September 2025, PubMed/WoS/Embase/Cochrane. Outcome: microtensile bond strength (µTBS) effect of collagen cross-linkers on resin-dentin interface.

Five cross-linker types evaluated: aldehyde-, chitosan-, riboflavin-, polyphenol-, and carbodiimide-based. All five enhanced immediate or long-term bonding strength under specific conditions. Most consistent effects: pretreatment with etch-and-rinse (E&R) adhesive system. Evidence for chitosan- and siloxane-based cross-linkers is limited and reinforcing effects unconfirmed.

Efficacy depended on: cross-linker type, application mode, adhesive strategy. Random-effects model, standardized mean difference (SMD) with 95% CI.

GRADE evidence: low to very low. Most studies: medium bias. Significant publication bias in aged subgroups of aldehyde- and polyphenol-based pretreatments.

Conclusion: Certain collagen cross-linkers may enhance resin-dentin bonding; effectiveness context-dependent. High heterogeneity and low evidence quality warrant cautious interpretation. Clinical translation premature.
