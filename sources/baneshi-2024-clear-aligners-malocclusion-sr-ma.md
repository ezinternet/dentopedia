## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 21 RCTs (n=970) comparing clear aligners vs fixed appliances in simple non-extraction malocclusions; RoB-2 applied; evidence quality low-to-moderate.

No significant difference in occlusal outcomes (ABO-OGS, LII, PAR); CA significantly better on plaque index (MD −0.76), gingival index (MD −0.61), bleeding index (MD −0.71), and 6-month QoL (MD −4.37).

CA is equivalent to fixed appliances for occlusal correction in simple malocclusions and shows a consistent periodontal/QoL advantage, but the evidence is limited to non-extraction cases and most RCTs carry unclear risk of bias.

## 2. Key Contributions

- RCT-only restriction (RoB 2) raises evidence bar vs mixed-design SRs.
- Quantifies periodontal advantage of CA with pooled effect sizes.
- Confirms occlusal-outcome equivalence in simple non-extraction cases.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

EMBASE/Medline/Cochrane/LILACS/WoS/EBMR to Oct 2024; RCTs only; RoB 2. 600 records -> 21 RCTs, 970 participants. MA (RevMan 5.4) feasible for secondary outcomes only.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Occlusal outcome (ABO-OGS, LII, PAR): no significant CA vs FA difference.
- Plaque index MD -0.76 (95% CI -1.14, -0.38); gingival index MD -0.61 (-0.78, -0.44); bleeding index MD -0.71 (-0.92, -0.49) — all favor CA.
- 6-month QoL MD -4.37 (-6.93, -1.80) favoring CA.
- CA also favored for chairside time, pain, root resorption.
- Evidence quality: low (periodontal) to moderate (QoL).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- 14/21 unclear RoB, only 1 low; MA limited to secondary outcomes.
- Mostly simple non-extraction cases; complex malocclusion under-studied. Abstract-only ingest.
