## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Cureus 2023; Saudi Arabia) synthesizing the current and emerging applications of artificial intelligence subfields (machine learning, deep learning, artificial neural networks, robotics) in dental implantology including diagnostics, treatment planning, and surgical guidance.

AI-driven deep learning image analysis enhances implant placement precision from CBCT and panoramic data; AI-driven data analytics enables patient-specific treatment strategies and improved predicted success rates; key applications span implant site assessment, position planning, and complication prediction.

AI holds promise for personalizing and streamlining implant workflows, but limited standardization across AI models warrants clinical caution before routine adoption.

## 세줄요약

서술적 리뷰 (Cureus 2023; 사우디아라비아) — 치과 임플란트학에서 인공지능(Artificial Intelligence, AI) 하위 분야(기계학습, 딥러닝, 인공신경망, 로보틱스)의 현황·전망 종합.

딥러닝 영상 분석이 CBCT·파노라마 기반 임플란트 식립 정밀도 향상; AI 데이터 분석으로 환자 맞춤 치료 전략·예측 성공률 개선; 임플란트 부위 평가·위치 계획·합병증 예측이 주요 적용 영역.

AI는 임플란트 워크플로우 개인화·효율화 잠재성 있으나, AI 모델 간 표준화 부족으로 임상 적용에 주의 필요.

## 1. Key Results
- Narrative review; AI subfields: ML, DL, ANNs, robotics
- AI-driven image analysis and deep learning: enhance precision of implant placement
- AI-driven data analytics: patient-specific treatment strategies, improved success rates
- Key applications: implant site assessment, position planning, complication prediction
- AI promises personalized, efficient oral healthcare transformation

## 2. Clinical Implications
- AI image analysis can support implant position planning from CBCT/panoramic data
- Patient data integration enables risk stratification before treatment
- Limited standardization across AI models — clinical caution warranted
- Continued AI evolution expected to reshape implant dentistry workflow
