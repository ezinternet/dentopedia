## Why Ingested

Adds the evidence-based peer-reviewer's lens on the dentist-burnout literature — this JEBDP commentary critically appraises and endorses Moro 2022's headline emotional-exhaustion finding (~28%), anchoring the appraisal layer of [[overviews/dentist-burnout-mental-health-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Commentary/critical appraisal (JEBDP 2023) by Afrashtehfar and Jurado appraising Moro et al. 2022 SR+MA, which meta-analyzed 31 studies on burnout/emotional exhaustion (EE) in the dental profession.

The commentary endorses the headline finding that EE prevalence among dental professionals is approximately 28%, confirms MBI as the gold standard burnout measurement tool, and validates the EE-dominant subscale signal without contributing new primary data.

No new evidence is added; value lies in independent editorial endorsement of the Moro 2022 SR+MA conclusions — clinicians should treat this as supporting weight for existing evidence, not an independent data source.

## 4. Key Results (Restated from Moro 2022)
- 37 studies narrative / 31 meta-analyzed
- MBI subscales (EE, DP, PA) used as the diagnostic framework
- Affirms the high prevalence finding and the emotional exhaustion-dominant pattern

## 5. Limitations
- Adds no new primary evidence
- Reviewer perspective only — same conclusions as the underlying SR
