## Why Ingested

Earlier (16-study) domestic-literature precursor to [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/choi-2017-dental-plaque-control-oral-hygiene-education-korea-sr]] by the same first author — kept for provenance and its OHI-specific framing even though the 2017 review's broader 22-study synthesis supersedes it (partial).

## Three-line Summary

(1) This is a domestic systematic literature review (n=16 studies) examining the effect of oral hygiene instruction (OHI) as an adjunct to professional and self-performed plaque control/removal on periodontal parameters in Korean adults, children, and elderly populations, spanning studies published from 1993 to 2012.

(2) Repeated OHI using disclosing agents significantly reduced plaque indices (O'Leary plaque index, PHP index, OHI-S index) across all age groups; the most substantial reductions were observed with 4 repeated instruction sessions (up to 74% improvement in PHP index, p<0.001) and the effect was maintained with regular reinforcement visits, though scores regressed after cessation of education.

(3) Combining professional mechanical plaque removal (scaling, root planing) with OHI is more effective than either intervention alone; routine, repeated OHI with disclosing agents is recommended to enhance oral hygiene and reduce periodontal disease burden, but high-quality domestic studies with standardized methodologies are needed to inform dental health insurance policy.

## 세줄요약

(1) 본 연구는 국내에서 발표된 16편의 문헌을 체계적으로 고찰하여, 한국 성인·아동·노인을 대상으로 구강위생교습(Oral Hygiene Instruction, OHI)이 치면세균막관리(dental plaque control) 및 치주지수 개선에 미치는 영향을 분석하였다.

(2) 치면착색제(disclosing agent)를 활용한 반복적 OHI는 모든 연령층에서 치면세균막지수(O'Leary index, PHP index, OHI-S index)를 유의하게 감소시켰으며, 4회 반복 교육 시 최대 74%의 개선효과를 보였다(p<0.001). 교육 종료 후 정기 방문 시 점수는 다소 회귀되었으나 교육 전보다는 유의하게 개선된 상태를 유지하였다.

(3) 전문가 치면세마(professional mechanical plaque removal)와 OHI를 병행하는 것이 단독 시행보다 효과적이며, 정기적이고 반복적인 OHI 시행이 구강위생 향상에 필요하다. 향후 국내 치석제거 건강보험 정책 수립을 위한 근거 중심의 고품질 연구가 요구된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- This systematic review synthesized 16 domestic Korean studies on dental plaque control and oral hygiene instruction (OHI) published between 1993 and 2012.
- Repeated OHI using disclosing agents (e.g., erythrosine dye) significantly improved plaque control indices (O'Leary index, PHP index, OHI-S index) across all age groups (preschoolers, elementary school children, adults, elderly).
- Combining professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR: scaling, root planing) with OHI yielded greater reductions in plaque and gingival bleeding indices than PMPR alone.
- The most effective education protocol involved 4 repeated OHI sessions at 2-week to 4-week intervals; effect sizes ranged from 38% to 74% reduction in plaque indices (p<0.001).
- Maintenance of improved oral hygiene required regular reinforcement visits; plaque scores regressed toward baseline after education cessation but remained significantly better than pre-education levels.
- Disclosing agent-based individual toothbrushing instruction was more effective than instruction using only oral lectures or jaw models.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design:** Systematic literature review (narrative synthesis) of domestic Korean studies; no meta-analysis performed.
- **Databases & search window:** 5 Korean databases recommended by the National Evidence-based Healthcare Collaborating Agency (NECA): KoreaMed (http://www.koreamed.org), KMbase (http://kmbase.medric.or.kr), KISS (http://kiss.kstudy.com), NDSL (http://www.ndsl.kr), ScienceON (http://society.kisti.re.kr). Supplementary hand-searching of key Korean dental journals. Search period: inception to May 2013.
- **Inclusion criteria:** Original research articles published in Korean academic journals; studies evaluating plaque control after OHI/OHE (oral health education); studies assessing plaque index changes, microbial examinations, toothbrushing efficacy, or continuous oral health maintenance programs; studies with or without gingivitis/periodontitis; experimental and observational designs.
- **Exclusion criteria:** Abstract-only publications; simple questionnaire surveys without clinical outcomes; studies with other intervention types (e.g., antibiotics, chlorhexidine, flossing, dietary interventions, implants, denture care, arthritis, pregnancy, Helicobacter pylori, xerosis, malocclusion, halitosis, whitening, fluoride rinses, herbal therapy) were excluded from the Cochrane library search results but domestic database results were screened separately; non-English literature excluded from international database searches.
- **Included studies:** 16 domestic studies (total participants per study ranged from 27 to 244).
- **Quality assessment / bias evaluation:** Publication bias considered (grey literature not included); no formal ROB2, NOS, or AMSTAR assessment reported (narrative review approach described).
- **Outcomes:**
  - Primary: Plaque indices (O'Leary Plaque Index, PHP index, OHI-S index, Quigly-Hein Plaque Index, Plaque Control Record)
  - Secondary: Gingival bleeding index (BOB, sulcus bleeding), periodontal pocket depth, clinical attachment loss, tooth mobility, tooth loss, halitosis (VSC), oral hygiene knowledge/practice
- **Statistics:** Descriptive synthesis; effect sizes reported as pre-post differences, percent reductions; p-values from paired t-tests, repeated-measures ANOVA, ANCOVA, chi-square tests; no meta-analytic pooling performed.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Plaque index reduction (children, n=39, 12 months) —** S-OHI decreased from 2.28 at baseline to 1.44 at 6 months (1.58-fold reduction) and to 1.14 at 12 months (1.61-fold reduction, p<0.001) in the continuous oral health care group; DMFT index was 1.4-fold lower in the management group vs control (p<0.001) (Son et al., 2012).
- **Plaque index reduction (elderly, n=105, 3 months) —** O'Leary plaque index decreased from 72.6 to 53.6 (19.0 points, 26.4% reduction) in the caregiver-mediated intervention group vs 1.2% increase in control group (p<0.001); tongue coating decreased 31.5%, denture contamination decreased 30.2% (Kim et al., 2010).
- **Plaque index reduction (adults, n=54, 4 weeks) —** OHI-S decreased from 2.87 to 1.13 (1.74-point reduction); modified PHP index decreased from 0.50 to 0.34 (0.16 reduction); VSC halitosis decreased from 76.97 to 51.56 ppb (25.41 reduction) (Lee et al., 2009).
- **Education frequency effect (adults, n=173, 2 years) —** O'Leary plaque index improved from 36→56→69 (3 repeated sessions) and 30→42→52→61 (4 repeated sessions); scores regressed after education cessation but remained significantly better than baseline (Uhm et al., 2009).
- **Repeated instruction effect (adults, n=30, 6 weeks) —** O'Leary plaque index decreased from 59.3 to 21.2 (38% reduction, p<0.001); gingival bleeding index (BOB) decreased from 3.76 to 1.90 (p<0.001) (Kim, 2011).
- **Education method comparison (adults, n=150, 1 month) —** PHP index improvement: oral model group +2.8 points (p=0.142); hands-on instruction group +11.6 points (p<0.05); disclosing agent group +24.0 points (p<0.001). Disclosing agent-based individual instruction was most effective (Ahn et al., 2012).
- **Toothbrushing method comparison (adults, n=27, 4 weeks) —** All three toothbrushing groups (modified Bass, Watanabe, self-toothbrushing) showed significant reductions in GI and PI over 4 weeks; no significant difference between methods (Jeong et al., 2011).
- **Long-term periodontal maintenance (adults, n=46, 3 years) —** O'Leary plaque index decreased 1.5% (Year 1), 4.2% (Year 2), 6.5% (Year 3); pocket depth 4–6mm decreased from 4.5 to 3.14 (Year 1), 4.59 to 3.83 (Year 2), 4.55 to 3.42 (Year 3); bleeding on probing decreased across 3 years (Shin et al., 2001).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- The review included only 16 domestic studies, severely limiting the evidence base and generalizability; most studies had small sample sizes (27–244 participants).
- Heterogeneous study designs (RCTs, quasi-experimental, pre-post) and variable outcome measures precluded quantitative meta-analysis.
- No formal risk-of-bias assessment (e.g., ROB2, NOS) was conducted; publication bias may exist as grey literature and unpublished studies were not included.
- Short follow-up durations (4 weeks to 12 months) limit conclusions about long-term maintenance of plaque control benefits.
- Lack of standardized education protocols and dosing (frequency, duration, reinforcement schedules) across studies makes it difficult to identify an optimal OHI regimen.
- No patient-reported outcomes (e.g., OHRQoL, satisfaction) or cost-effectiveness analyses were included; quality of life measures were not systematically assessed.
- The review did not include international studies for direct comparison, and domestic studies used varied indices (O'Leary, PHP, OHI-S) making cross-study comparisons challenging.
- No meta-analysis was possible due to heterogeneity in populations, interventions, and outcome measures.

## 6. Related Work

- **Axelsson & Lindhe (1981)** — Long-term (6-year) controlled oral hygiene program significantly reduced caries and periodontal disease progression in adults; foundational evidence for professional plaque control + OHI.
- **Axelsson, Nystrom & Lindhe (2004)** — 30-year maintenance program demonstrated sustained tooth retention and reduced periodontal disease with regular professional plaque control and OHI.
- **Needleman et al. (2005)** — Systematic review of professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR) concluded that PMPR without OHI is less effective; repeated OHI provides similar benefits to PMPR alone.
- **van der Weijden & Hioe (2005)** — Systematic review of self-performed mechanical plaque removal in adults with gingivitis found that manual toothbrushing with OHI and professional scaling reduced plaque and bleeding indices over ≥6 months.
- **Hoogstraten & Moltzer (1983)** — Dental health instruction improved knowledge, attitude, and behavior, but sustaining behavioral change required ongoing reinforcement.

## 7. Glossary

- **Dental plaque (치면세균막, 치태)**: A biofilm of microorganisms and their products adhering to tooth surfaces; cannot be removed by oral self-cleansing and requires mechanical removal.
- **Oral Hygiene Instruction (OHI, 구강위생교습)**: Educational interventions (verbal, model-based, or with disclosing agents) aimed at improving toothbrushing technique and plaque control.
- **Oral Health Education (OHE, 구강보건교육)**: Broader educational interventions including oral hygiene instruction, dietary advice, and preventive behaviors.
- **PHP index (Patient Hygiene Performance index, 구강환경관리능력지수)**: A plaque scoring system assessing the extent of debris on six tooth surfaces; lower scores indicate better oral hygiene.
- **O'Leary Plaque Index (O'Leary plaque control record)**: The percentage of tooth surfaces with detectable plaque; lower percentages indicate better plaque control.
- **OHI-S index (Simplified Oral Hygiene Index, 간이구강위생지수)**: A simplified index combining debris and calculus scores to assess oral hygiene status.
- **Professional Mechanical Plaque Removal (PMPR, 전문가 기계적 치면세균막관리)**: Removal of plaque and calculus by dental professionals using hand instruments, sonic/ultrasonic scalers, or rotary devices.
- **Disclosing agent (치면착색제)**: A dye (e.g., erythrosine) applied to tooth surfaces to visualize plaque, used as an educational tool to improve toothbrushing efficacy.
