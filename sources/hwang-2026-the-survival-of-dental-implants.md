## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (1694 implants in 806 women aged 55 years or older, follow-up of at least 12 months; propensity score matching; Firth logistic regression with patient-level clustering, linear mixed model for marginal bone loss, Kaplan-Meier curves) comparing anti-resorptive drug (ARD: bisphosphonates or denosumab) users with non-ARD controls — abstract-only ingest.

Implant survival was 99.13% (bisphosphonates), 98.08% (denosumab) and 98.24% (control), with no significant differences in implant failure or marginal bone loss between groups; diabetes mellitus, alcohol consumption and overdenture prosthesis were associated with significantly increased failure risk.

Implant rehabilitation appears feasible in post-menopausal women on ARD, but caution is advised for high-dose or long-term therapy and the risk linked to invasive procedures needs further evaluation; effect sizes and design details are not available from the abstract.

## 세줄요약

후향적 코호트 (55세 이상 여성 806명, 임플란트 1694개, 추적 12개월 이상; 성향점수매칭, 환자 단위 군집을 반영한 Firth 로지스틱 회귀, 선형 혼합모형, Kaplan-Meier 곡선) — 골흡수억제제 (anti-resorptive drug, ARD; 비스포스포네이트·데노수맙) 사용군과 비사용 대조군 비교 (초록만 수집).

임플란트 생존율 (implant survival rate)은 비스포스포네이트 99.13%, 데노수맙 98.08%, 대조군 98.24%였고 임플란트 실패와 변연골 소실 (marginal bone loss)에 군간 유의한 차이는 없었음; 당뇨병, 음주, 오버덴처 보철은 실패 위험 증가와 유의하게 연관.

폐경 후 여성의 ARD 복용 중에도 임플란트 수복은 가능해 보이나 고용량·장기 투여 시 주의가 필요하고 침습적 시술 관련 위험은 추가 평가가 필요함; 효과 크기와 설계 세부는 초록만으로는 확인 불가.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Retrospective cohort study. [ABSTRACT-ONLY — full text not retrieved]
- **Journal**: Clinical Implant Dentistry and Related Research 2026
- **PMID**: 41562427 · **DOI**: [10.1111/cid.70122](https://doi.org/10.1111/cid.70122)
- **Population**: 806 women aged 55 years or older with osteoporosis; 1694 implants; follow-up at least 12 months.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Compares implant outcomes in bisphosphonate users, denosumab users and non-ARD controls within one post-menopausal cohort.
- Reports risk factors for failure within this population (diabetes mellitus, alcohol consumption, overdenture prosthesis).
- Concludes implant-based rehabilitation is feasible in women on ARD for osteoporosis.

## 3. Methodology

- Exposure groups: bisphosphonates, denosumab, or non-ARD control.
- Variables: demographic, ARD-related, implant/prosthesis-related factors, and marginal bone loss.
- Propensity score matching to balance group characteristics.
- Firth logistic regression with patient-level clustering; linear mixed model for longitudinal bone change; Kaplan-Meier curves for survival trends.

## 4. Key Results

| Group | Implant survival |
|---|---|
| Bisphosphonates | 99.13% |
| Denosumab | 98.08% |
| Control (non-ARD) | 98.24% |

- No significant differences in implant failure or marginal bone loss between groups.
- Diabetes mellitus, alcohol consumption and overdenture prosthesis were associated with significantly increased risk of implant failure.
- Group sizes, odds ratios, confidence intervals and ARD dose/duration data are not given in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations

- Retrospective design.
- Abstract-only: per-group n, effect estimates, and dose/duration stratification are unavailable.
- Authors note that the risk of failure associated with invasive procedures needs further evaluation, and advise caution with high-dose or long-term ARD therapy.
