## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT study (n=1,200 Saudi maxillary molars: 600 first molars + 600 second molars; single center, King Abdulaziz University) assessing MB2 prevalence and PMB1-PMB2 distance.

MB2 prevalence was 46.7% in first molars (significantly higher in females, p=0.020) and 17.7% in second molars (no sex difference, p=0.457); mean PMB1-PMB2 distance was 1.87±0.42 mm (first molar) and 1.24±0.76 mm (second molar).

Saudi MB2 prevalence is lower than Chilean (~70%/47%) and Iranian SR averages, supporting ethnic variability; single-center retrospective design and unreported voxel size limit precision.

## 세줄요약

후향적 CBCT 연구 (사우디 상악 대구치 1,200개: 제1대구치 600개+제2대구치 600개) — MB2 유병률과 PMB1-PMB2 거리 측정.

제1대구치 MB2 유병률 46.7% (여성↑, p=0.020), 제2대구치 17.7% (성별 유의차 없음, p=0.457); PMB1-PMB2 평균 거리 제1대구치 1.87±0.42 mm, 제2대구치 1.24±0.76 mm.

사우디 MB2 유병률은 칠레(~70%/47%)·이란 SR 평균보다 낮아 민족적 변이 지지; 단일 기관·후향적 설계·voxel 크기 미보고로 정밀도 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 사우디 1,200 CBCT primary data (1MM 600 + 2MM 600)
- 1MM 46.7%, 2MM 17.7% — 다른 인구 대비 낮은 prevalence
- PMB1-PMB2 거리 정량 (1MM 1.87±0.42 mm, 2MM 1.24±0.76 mm)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Retrospective CBCT chart review
- Standardization: pulpal floor에서 1 mm apical 측정
- Chi-square sex/tooth-type comparison, α=0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 1MM MB2 prevalence: 46.7% (p=0.020, female↑)
- 2MM MB2 prevalence: 17.7% (p=0.457, sex-NS)
- PMB1-PMB2: 1MM 1.87±0.42 mm, 2MM 1.24±0.76 mm
- PMB2-PP: 1MM 0.74±0.21 mm, 2MM 0.43±0.18 mm
- Overall: 386/1,200 (32.2%) maxillary molars positive

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective single-center
- CBCT resolution (voxel size 미명시) — micro-MB2 누락 가능
- Operator inter-rater reliability 미보고
- 사우디 인구 단일 — 다른 중동/북아프리카 인구 비교 후속 필요
