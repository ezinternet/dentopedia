## Why Ingested

[[wiki/periodontics/barootchi-2024-autologous-platelet-concentrates-root-coverage]]의 네트워크 메타분석은 CAF+PRF가 CAF **단독** 대비 근면피복(root coverage)을 유의하게 증가시킨다(+6.12%)고 보고했으나, 그 비교의 대조군은 이식편이 없는 CAF뿐이었다. 본 split-mouth RCT(Corekci 2025)는 이미 이식편(DGG)이 존재하는 상태에서 L-PRF를 추가했을 때의 효과를 직접 검증해, "PRF의 근면피복 이득이 이식편 유무와 무관하게 성립하는가"라는 공백을 메운다 — 답은 아니오였다(RC% 무의미 차이, 대신 조기 통증·불편감만 유의 감소). PRF의 임상적 가치가 어디에서 발현되는지(피복률 vs 이환율)를 재구획하는 근거.

## Three-line Summary

Single-blinded split-mouth RCT (n=15 patients, 47 vs 48 RT1 multiple-recession teeth, 6-month follow-up, Gazi University, Türkiye) comparing CAF+de-epithelialized gingival graft (DGG) alone vs CAF+DGG+leukocyte-PRF (L-PRF, 3-layer configuration) for multiple adjacent gingival recessions.

Root coverage at 6 months was statistically equivalent between groups (DGG 93.30%±6.97% vs DGG+PRF 95.28%±4.99%, p>0.05, all secondary clinical/periodontal parameters also NS); the DGG+PRF group had significantly less pain (days 6–7) and less discomfort (days 2, 3, 5) postoperatively, with no OHIP-14 or analgesic-use difference.

Adding L-PRF to DGG does not improve root coverage, keratinized tissue width, or gingival thickness over DGG alone — its demonstrated benefit is reduced early postoperative morbidity, not superior root coverage.

## 세줄요약

단일맹검 split-mouth RCT(환자 15명, RT1 다발성 치은퇴축 치아 47 vs 48개, 6개월 추적, 튀르키예 Gazi 대학) — 치관변위판막(CAF)+탈상피화 치은이식편(De-epithelialized Gingival Graft, DGG) 단독 vs CAF+DGG+백혈구혈소판풍부섬유소(Leukocyte Platelet-Rich Fibrin, L-PRF, 3층 배치) 비교.

6개월 근면피복률(Root Coverage, RC%)은 두 군 간 통계적으로 동등(DGG 93.30%±6.97% vs DGG+PRF 95.28%±4.99%, p>0.05, 기타 임상지표도 모두 NS); DGG+PRF군은 술후 통증(6–7일)·불편감(2·3·5일)이 유의하게 낮았으나 OHIP-14·진통제 사용량은 차이 없음.

DGG에 L-PRF를 추가해도 근면피복률·각화조직폭·치은두께는 개선되지 않으며, 입증된 이득은 우수한 피복률이 아니라 술후 조기 이환율(통증·불편감) 감소이다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Periodontal & Implant Science (Korean Academy of Periodontology), published online 2025-05-19
- **Design**: Single-blinded, split-mouth randomized controlled trial; CONSORT-compliant; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06509867 (registered retrospectively)
- **Ethics**: Gazi University Faculty of Dentistry Clinical Research Ethics Committee (approved 2018-12-20); Turkish Ministry of Health Stem Cell Transplants Scientific Board approval; Declaration of Helsinki 1975/2013
- **Population**: 15 patients (4 women, 11 men) with multiple adjacent RT1 (Cairo type 1) gingival recessions ≥2 mm depth, KTW <3 mm, systemically healthy, non-smokers, no active periodontal disease; enrolled 2019-05 to 2021-04
- **Intervention (split-mouth)**: one side CAF+DGG (DGG group, 47 teeth); contralateral side (6-week interval) CAF+DGG+3-layer L-PRF (DGG+PRF group, 48 teeth)
- **Sample size**: powered for effect size 0.455, 80% power, α=0.05 → 15 patients/group
- **Follow-up**: T0 (baseline), T1 (1 month), T3 (3 months), T6 (6 months); PROMs also at T14 (14 days)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly tests whether L-PRF adds root-coverage benefit **on top of an existing autologous graft (DGG)** — a distinct question from PRF-vs-CTG or PRF-vs-CAF-alone comparisons dominating the literature (e.g., network meta-analyses using CAF-alone as reference).
- Uses a **3-layer PRF configuration** (membranes both beneath and above the DGG) intended to fully envelop the graft and approximate the periosteum — a specific stacking protocol distinct from single-layer or PRF-over-SCTG designs reported elsewhere.
- Pairs clinical outcomes with **dual patient-reported instruments** (OHIP-14 + daily VAS for pain/bleeding/discomfort + analgesic diary) across the full first postoperative week, capturing morbidity granularity that OHIP-14 alone (administered only at day 14/month 1/3/6) would have missed.
- Demonstrates a **dissociation between root-coverage efficacy and postoperative morbidity** as two independent axes on which an adjunct (L-PRF) can succeed or fail separately — L-PRF failed to move RC%/KTW/GT but succeeded on days 2–7 pain/discomfort.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- CAF per Zucchelli technique, envelope-style flaps without vertical incisions; recipient site prepared, then treatment-assignment envelope opened intraoperatively (concealed allocation).
- L-PRF: 2 × 9 mL anticoagulant-free venous blood tubes, centrifuged 2700 rpm × 12 min, clot pressed into membranes ("PRF BOX", Process, Nice, France); assembled as PRF–DGG–PRF sandwich, no extraoral suturing, secured with 5-0 resorbable sutures under the coronally advanced flap (vertical double-crossed suture closure).
- Blinded outcome assessor (clinician C.G.) recorded clinical measurements using acrylic stents with fixed notches (mesio/mid/disto-buccal) + Williams periodontal probe; intra-examiner reliability 0.91 (5 calibration patients, repeat exam 1 week apart).
- Statistics: SPSS v20.0; Kruskal-Wallis for between-group, Wilcoxon signed-rank for within-group/time comparisons; significance p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome RC%** (mean±SD): DGG — T1 98.79±2.12%, T3 94.72±5.98%, T6 93.30±6.97%; DGG+PRF — T1 98.99±1.62%, T3 97.80±3.07%, T6 95.28±4.99%. Between-group p: T1 0.774, T3 0.087, T6 0.382 — **not significant at any time point**.
- RD, RW, GT, KTW: all improved significantly from baseline within each group but showed **no significant between-group difference** at any follow-up (all p>0.05).
- PI, GI, BOP, PD, CAL: no significant between-group differences at any time point; both groups improved similarly within-group over 6 months.
- **PROMs**: DGG+PRF group had significantly lower VAS pain on postoperative days 6 and 7, and significantly lower VAS discomfort on days 2, 3, and 5 (all p<0.05). Bleeding VAS and analgesic tablet counts did not differ between groups at any time point. OHIP-14 subscales showed no significant between-group differences across follow-up.
- Overall interpretation stated by the authors: L-PRF's demonstrated value in this design is **morbidity reduction, not root-coverage or soft-tissue-dimension superiority**.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=15 patients; single-center, single-operator) limits generalizability and power for secondary/subgroup effects.
- Graft dimensions and palatal donor mucosal thickness were not recorded, despite PRF membrane thickness plausibly affecting total graft thickness, tissue nutrition, and flap perfusion.
- Terminal-tooth status adjacent to the surgical site (a known wound-healing modifier) was neither recorded nor analyzed.
- OHIP-14 was administered only at day 14, 3, and 6 months — missing the early-postoperative window (days 1–13) where the VAS data show the PRF-associated difference actually occurs, likely explaining the "no OHIP-14 difference" null finding.
- Retrospective trial registration (ClinicalTrials.gov, after enrollment window) is a reporting-quality caveat.
- Authors call for longer follow-up to clarify long-term dimensional stability of the DGG±PRF grafts.

## 6. Related Work

- Cited network meta-analysis (not otherwise identified in-text, but consistent with [[wiki/periodontics/barootchi-2024-autologous-platelet-concentrates-root-coverage]]) establishing CAF+PRF > CAF alone for mean root coverage (+6.12%) — this trial's null RC% result is compatible with that NMA once the comparator is DGG (already a graft), not bare CAF.
- Meta-analyses cited in-text reporting PRF's superior PROMs vs SCTG for pain/discomfort in single and multiple CAF procedures — reinforced here with a graft-controlled (DGG-matched) comparison.
- Prior DGG-vs-SCTG and T-PRF-vs-SCTG comparative literature cited showing comparable RC% but occasionally divergent CAL/KTW findings, attributed to heterogeneity in adjunctive biomaterials and surgical technique across studies.

## 7. Glossary

- **DGG (De-epithelialized Gingival Graft, 탈상피화 치은이식편)**: free gingival graft harvested with its epithelium subsequently excised ex vivo, leaving a thick, dense connective-tissue graft with a more stable architecture than SCTG.
- **L-PRF (Leukocyte Platelet-Rich Fibrin, 백혈구혈소판풍부섬유소)**: second-generation autologous platelet concentrate prepared by anticoagulant-free centrifugation, releasing growth factors (PDGF-AB, TGF-β1, VEGF) over ~10 days; used here as a 3-layer membrane sandwich around the DGG.
- **RC% (percentage of Root Coverage, 근면피복률)**: [(preoperative recession depth − postoperative recession depth) / preoperative recession depth] × 100; this trial's primary outcome.
- **RT1 (Cairo type 1)**: gingival recession classification with no interproximal attachment loss — the population eligible for complete root coverage.
- **OHIP-14 (Oral Health Impact Profile-14)**: 14-item patient-reported instrument measuring negative oral-health impact across physical, psychological, and social domains.
- **CAF (Coronally Advanced Flap, 치관변위판막)**: the base surgical flap technique (Zucchelli et al.) onto which DGG ± L-PRF was grafted in both study arms.
