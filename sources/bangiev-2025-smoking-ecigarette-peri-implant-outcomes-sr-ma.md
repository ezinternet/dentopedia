## Why Ingested

Most recent (2025) SR+MA comparing three smoking modalities on peri-implant MBL; extends Vámos 2024 (network MA through April 2023) with literature through October 2025 and adds inflammatory biomarker quantification (IL-1β, TNF-α). Provides updated MBL effect sizes and the first explicit e-cigarette vs cigarette comparison with biomarker data. See [[wiki/implants/mbl/vamos-2024-nicotine-containing-products-peri-implant-network-ma]] for the Bayesian network MA.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (literature through October 2025) comparing cigarette smoking, waterpipe smoking, and e-cigarette vaping vs non-smokers on peri-implant outcomes (MBL, plaque, BOP) and inflammatory cytokines (IL-1β, TNF-α); random-effects models.

Cigarette: MBL +2.16 mm, plaque +5.22 units; Waterpipe: MBL +1.90 mm, plaque +6.31 units; E-cigarette: MBL +0.83 mm, plaque +1.61 units — all vs NS; all three modalities paradoxically reduced BOP (nicotine-induced vasoconstriction); risk gradient CS > WP > EC > NS.

All three smoking types significantly worsen peri-implant MBL and inflammation despite bleeding suppression; e-cigarettes cause less bone loss than cigarettes/waterpipe but still +0.83 mm vs NS — not a safe alternative.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (2025년 10월까지 문헌): 궐련(CS, Cigarette), 물담배(WP, Waterpipe), 전자담배(EC, E-Cigarette) vs 비흡연(NS, Non-Smoker), 임플란트주위 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss)·염증지표 비교.

CS: MBL +2.16mm, 플라크 +5.22; WP: MBL +1.90mm, 플라크 +6.31; EC: MBL +0.83mm, 플라크 +1.61 — 모두 유의; 역설적으로 3군 모두 탐침 시 출혈(BOP, Bleeding on Probing) 감소(니코틴 유발 혈관수축); 위험 순서 CS > WP > EC > NS.

3가지 흡연 방식 모두 MBL 및 염증 악화; 전자담배는 궐련·물담배보다 MBL 적으나 비흡연 대비 +0.83mm — 안전한 대안 아님.

## Key Contributions

- Most recent SR+MA on smoking-type comparison (through Oct 2025)
- Quantifies e-cigarette MBL effect (+0.83 mm) — not previously clearly established
- Adds inflammatory biomarker data: CS highest IL-1β (+239.63 pg/mL), EC elevated IL-1β (+170 pg/mL) and TNF-α (+17.6 pg/mL)
- Paradoxical BOP reduction confirmed across all three modalities (vasoconstriction)
- Risk gradient: CS > WP > EC > NS explicitly quantified

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA; PRISMA guidelines; random-effects models
- Search: PubMed/MEDLINE; October 2015 to October 2025
- Inclusion: Adults with dental implants; smoking vs non-smoking comparison; any smoking modality
- Outcomes: MBL (primary), plaque index, BOP, IL-1β, TNF-α
- Statistics: Mean differences (MD) for MBL/cytokines; standardized mean differences (SMD) for plaque and BOP

## Key Results

| Smoking type | MBL vs NS | Plaque index vs NS | BOP vs NS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cigarette (CS) | +2.16 mm | +5.22 units | Decreased (vasoconstriction) |
| Waterpipe (WP) | +1.90 mm | +6.31 units | Decreased |
| E-cigarette (EC) | +0.83 mm | +1.61 units | Decreased |

### Inflammatory cytokines vs NS
- Cigarette: IL-1β +239.63 pg/mL (highest)
- E-cigarette: IL-1β +170 pg/mL, TNF-α +17.6 pg/mL
- Waterpipe: cytokine values reported in full text

## Limitations

- PubMed only (single database) — may underrepresent non-English literature
- Heterogeneity expected across studies (different definitions of smoking quantity, implant types, follow-up)
- Lower-impact journal compared to Vámos 2024 (Nicotine & Tobacco Research)
- BOP reduction (vasoconstriction) is a recognized masking effect — bone loss may understate true disease burden

## Glossary

- **BOP paradox**: Nicotine causes vasoconstriction → reduced gingival bleeding on probing despite active inflammation; masks true disease severity
- **IL-1β**: Interleukin-1β — key pro-inflammatory cytokine in peri-implant disease; elevated in smokers
- **TNF-α**: Tumor necrosis factor-alpha — pro-inflammatory cytokine contributing to osteoclast activation and bone loss
- **Waterpipe (shisha)**: Device where tobacco-containing mixture is smoked through water; often perceived as safer but associated with high MBL
