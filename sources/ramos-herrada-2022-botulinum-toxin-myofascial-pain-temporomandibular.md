## Why Ingested

Fills the pre-2023 botulinum toxin (Botulinum Toxin, BoNT) evidence gap for myofascial pain specifically: a GRADE- and RoB 2-appraised systematic review of 8 RCTs (n=314) whose low-to-medium certainty verdict frames the later single trials [[botulinum-toxin/kim-sr-2023-effect-botulinum-toxin-masticatory-muscle]] and [[botulinum-toxin/minston-2025-effect-pain-following-botulinum-toxin]], and the 2-year cohort [[botulinum-toxin/abdulrahman-2026-botulinum-toxin-myofascial-pain-masseter-temporalis]]. Also sits under the broader [[tmj/zhu-2020-btx-painful-tmd-sr-ma]] and [[tmj/saini-2024-botulinum-toxin-tmd-sr-ma]] syntheses.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis stated in the abstract; searched to February 2021; 900 records retrieved, 8 RCTs, n=314, ages 18-75, mostly women) of botulinum toxin for refractory myofascial pain related to temporomandibular disorders.

Authors conclude that low doses of botulinum toxin are effective for refractory TMD myofascial pain; RoB 2.0 rated 7 of 8 trials "some concerns" and only 1 low risk, and GRADE certainty was medium to low. (abstract-only)

Clinical reading: supports botulinum toxin only as an option for refractory cases, with the certainty caveat; no pooled effect size is available from the abstract.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Myofascial-pain-specific SR restricted to RCTs, with both RoB 2.0 and GRADE appraisal
- Databases: PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, Cochrane Library, LILACS; grey literature via Google Scholar, ClinicalTrials.gov, OpenGrey
- No language or year restriction; search to February 2021

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 900 studies retrieved, 8 RCTs selected, 314 patients (18-75 y, predominantly women)
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2.0; certainty: GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- RoB 2.0: 7 studies "some concerns", 1 low overall risk
- Conclusion: low doses of botulinum toxin effective for refractory myofascial pain associated with TMD
- GRADE certainty: medium to low
- Doses, pain scores, follow-up durations and effect sizes are not reported in the abstract

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract only; no numerical outcomes, doses, muscles injected or comparator details available
- Search ended February 2021; later RCTs (e.g. Kim 2023, Minston 2025) are not covered
- "Low doses effective" rests on medium-to-low certainty and mostly some-concerns trials
