## Why Ingested

하악 제3대구치 (Mandibular Third Molar, M3) 발치 시 발생하는 하치조신경 손상 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Injury, IANI) 및 설신경 손상 (Lingual Nerve Injury, LNI) 등 합병증의 실제 임상 발생율 및 삶의 질(Quality of Life, QoL) 영향을 전향적 연구로 파악하고자 함. 기존 문헌고찰 [[oral-surgery/baek-2010-literature-review-altered-sensation-mandibular]] 및 [[oral-surgery/juodzbalys-2011-ian-injury-implant-etiology-treatment-review]]에서 제시한 외과적 합병증 수치를 임상 코호트로 대조 및 보강함.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort study of 163 patients undergoing surgical extraction of impacted mandibular third molars (M3) to assess postoperative discomfort, complications, and neurosensory deficits.

Transient lingual nerve injury (LNI) occurred in 4 patients (2.45% of cases; reported as 2%), resolving completely within 12 weeks, while no cases of inferior alveolar nerve paresthesia (IANP), dry socket, or postoperative infection were reported.

Although surgical extraction is a safe procedure with low long-term morbidity, adequate preoperative risk assessment and patient education regarding potential neurosensory deficits are critical.

## 세줄요약

매복 하악 제3대구치 (Mandibular Third Molar, M3) 외과적 발치를 시행한 환자 163명을 대상으로 수술 후 불편감, 합병증, 신경감각 결손을 평가한 전향적 코호트 연구임.

일시적인 설신경 손상 (Lingual Nerve Injury, LNI)은 4명(2.45%, 본문 내 2%로 기술)에서 발생하여 12주 이내에 완전히 회복되었으며, 하치조신경 감각이상 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Paresthesia, IANP)이나 건성 발치와 (Alveolar Osteitis / Dry Socket, AO), 술 후 감염 사례는 보고되지 않음.

외과적 발치는 장기적인 합병증 발생률이 낮은 안전한 시술이나, 잠재적인 신경감각 저하 및 심리적 영향에 대한 철저한 사전 위험 평가와 환자 교육이 필수적임.

## 2. Key Contributions

* **Complication Profiling**: Establishes a prospective profile of postoperative discomfort (pain, swelling, trismus) and neurosensory disturbances following mandibular third molar (M3) surgery.
* **Transient Neurological Deficit Quantification**: Reports a 2.45% (reported as 2%) incidence of lingual nerve injury (LNI) with 100% recovery within 12 weeks. Confirms 0% incidence of inferior alveolar nerve paresthesia (IANP) in this cohort.
* **Psychological & Life Satisfaction Correlation**: Highlights that although LNI or IANP may not heavily limit physical functions, they impose a severe negative psychological impact, higher depression levels, and reduced life satisfaction, especially in patients over 40.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

* **Study Design**: Prospective clinical study.
* **Patient Population**: 163 patients (108 males, 55 females) requiring surgical extraction of impacted mandibular M3. Most patients were aged 25–30 years (48%) and <25 years (33%).
* **Surgical Protocol**: Standard surgical osteotomy and tooth sectioning under local anesthesia.
* **Assessments at 1-Week Follow-up (Suture Removal)**:
  * *Pain*: Visual Analogue Scale (VAS, 0–10).
  * *Swelling*: Thread-measurement of facial landmarks (tragus to corner of mouth, tragus to pogonion, lateral corner of eye to angle of mandible).
  * *Trismus*: Interincisal mouth opening difference (preoperative vs. postoperative, in mm).
  * *Wound Healing*: Poor, fair, or good approximation.
  * *Complications*: Alveolar osteitis (dry socket), infection, hemorrhage, and syncope.
  * *Neurosensory Testing (for suspected deficits)*: Two-Point Discrimination (TPD) threshold test, Pinprick (PP) pain perception test, and Light Touch (LT) tactile test using a cotton stick. Follow-up for 6 months if deficits were identified.
* **Statistical Analysis**: Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Test (significance level α = 0.05).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

* **Pain Scale (VAS) at Day 7**:
  * *No Pain (VAS 0)*: 32%
  * *Mild Pain (VAS 1–3)*: 66%
  * *Moderate Pain (VAS 4–6)*: 1%
  * *Severe Pain (VAS 7–9)*: 1%
* **Trismus & Swelling**:
  * Mean difference in interincisal mouth opening was 8.067 mm, showing a statistically significant reduction postoperatively (p < 0.001).
  * Facial swelling measurements showed a statistically significant increase at day 7 compared to baseline (p < 0.001).
* **Nerve Injuries**:
  * *Lingual Nerve Injury (LNI)*: 4 patients (2.45% of 163; 2 males, 2 females). All 4 cases were temporary and resolved completely by 12 weeks.
  * *Inferior Alveolar Nerve Injury (IANI)*: 0 patients (0%).
* **Other Complications**:
  * Dry socket (AO): 0%
  * Postoperative infection: 0%
  * Postoperative bleeding: 0%
  * Intraoperative syncope: 2 patients (resolved immediately, no postoperative sequelae).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

* **Single-Center Cohort**: The population is drawn from a single academic department, potentially limiting generalizability compared to multi-center private practice settings.
* **Follow-up Rate**: Of the 4 patients with LNI, 1 patient was lost to follow-up, though the remaining 3 showed complete resolution within 12 weeks.
* **Predictive Value limitations**: The study notes that Pell and Gregory Class II (96%) and vertical/mesioangular angulations (63%) dominated the sample, which could skew difficulty index and complication rates compared to horizontally impacted M3.

## 7. Glossary

* **Mandibular Third Molar (M3)**: 하악 제3대구치. The last teeth to erupt in the dental arch, frequently impacted due to lack of space.
* **Lingual Nerve Injury (LNI)**: 설신경 손상. Damage to the lingual nerve causing sensory loss or alteration in the anterior two-thirds of the tongue.
* **Inferior Alveolar Nerve Paresthesia (IANP)**: 하치조신경 감각이상. Persistent altered sensation (numbness, tingling) in the lower lip, teeth, and chin.
* **Alveolar Osteitis (AO)**: 건성 발치와. Also known as dry socket; painful inflammation of the alveolar bone after premature loss of the blood clot.
* **Visual Analogue Scale (VAS)**: 시각 아날로그 척도. A subjective scale used to measure pain intensity from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst pain).
* **Two-Point Discrimination (TPD)**: 이점 판별 검사. A neurosensory test measuring the distance at which two distinct tactile points are recognized.
