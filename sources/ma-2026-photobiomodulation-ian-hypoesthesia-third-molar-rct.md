## Why Ingested

Only RCT head-to-head comparing laser acupuncture (LA) vs direct photobiomodulation (PBM) along the IAN trajectory for post-extraction IAN hypoesthesia — directly informs adjunct treatment protocols for iatrogenic IAN paresthesia. Extends the photobiomodulation arm of [[overviews/ian-nerve-injury-management-overview]] with RCT-level dosimetry (808 nm, 9 J/site, 15 alternate-day sessions), a delivery-route question the SR-level evidence in [[oral-surgery/nerve-injury/keykha-2024-iatrogenic-paresthesia-mandibular-treatment-sr]] could not resolve.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled trial (n=60) in adults with post-extraction IAN hypoesthesia lasting 7 days to 12 weeks; three arms: laser acupuncture (LA), direct PBM along IAN trajectory, or sham — all with background mecobalamin 0.5 mg TID × 30 days.

Both active arms improved significantly over sham: TPD change at day 30 was −3.2 mm (LA) and −2.6 mm (direct PBM) vs −0.8 mm (sham); SWME improved ≥1 grade in 85%, 80%, and 25% respectively; VAS numbness decreased 60% (LA), 48% (PBM), and 12% (sham).

LA and direct PBM produced comparable neurosensory recovery at matched dosimetry (808 nm, 9 J/site, every other day × 15 sessions); LA was associated with greater patient-reported numbness reduction, suggesting an additive central neuromodulatory effect from acupoint targeting.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Photobiomodulation, Photomedicine, and Laser Surgery (SAGE/SPIE)
- **Impact Factor**: 1.4 (5-year: 2.0)
- **Study type**: Randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled clinical trial
- **Sample size**: 60 participants completed intervention
- **Population**: Adults 18–60 years with unilateral IAN hypoesthesia ≥7 days and ≤12 weeks post-mandibular third molar extraction
- **Registration**: Not specified in extracted text

## 2. Key Contributions

- First double-blind RCT directly comparing laser acupuncture vs anatomical-trajectory PBM for post-extraction IAN hypoesthesia
- Demonstrates both strategies superior to sham under matched 808 nm dosimetry (9 J/site)
- LA associated with greater VAS numbness reduction (60% vs 48%), suggesting acupoint-mediated central neuromodulation beyond local PBM effects
- No adverse events in either active arm — confirms safety of both protocols at tested parameters
- Day-60 follow-up confirms sustained benefit

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Intervention protocol:**
- Laser: 808 nm diode, continuous wave, 100 mW, beam area 1.0 cm²
- Dosimetry: 90 s/site → 9 J/site (9 J/cm²), every other day, 15 sessions total
- All groups: mecobalamin 0.5 mg TID × 30 days (background neurotropic vitamin)

**LA arm:** Fixed 5+2 acupoint prescription — ST4, ST6, ST7, CV24, Jiachengjiang (EX-HN19) [local/face points] + LI4, PC6 [distal points]

**Direct PBM arm:** Irradiation along the external projection of the IAN trajectory (anatomical course from mental foramen to angle of mandible)

**Sham arm:** Identical setup with inactive device

**Outcome measures:**
- Primary: Two-point discrimination (TPD) and Semmes–Weinstein monofilament examination (SWME) at day 30
- Secondary: VAS numbness, additional sensory tests, day-60 follow-up

**Reporting standards:** CONSORT 2010, STRICTA (acupuncture-specific extension)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | LA | Direct PBM | Sham |
|---|---|---|---|
| TPD change at day 30 (mm) | −3.2 ± 1.8 | −2.6 ± 1.7 | −0.8 ± 1.1 |
| SWME ≥1 grade improvement | 85% | 80% | 25% |
| VAS numbness decrease | 60% | 48% | 12% |

- Group × time interaction p < 0.01 for both TPD and SWME
- Improvements largely maintained at day-60 follow-up
- No treatment-related adverse events in either active group

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-center study; generalizability unknown
- n=60 (20/arm) — modest sample; larger trials needed
- Mecobalamin background treatment in all arms may have elevated baseline recovery rate in sham
- Only short-term follow-up (60 days); long-term recovery trajectories not characterized
- LA acupoint selection was fixed; individualized traditional protocols not tested
- Sham control used inactive device — patient blinding efficacy not formally assessed

## 7. Glossary

- **IAN**: Inferior alveolar nerve
- **PBM**: Photobiomodulation (formerly LLLT — low-level laser therapy)
- **LA**: Laser acupuncture — delivery of PBM to traditional acupuncture points
- **TPD**: Two-point discrimination — clinical neurosensory test
- **SWME**: Semmes–Weinstein monofilament examination — graded filaments testing tactile threshold
- **VAS**: Visual Analog Scale
- **Mecobalamin**: Active form of vitamin B12 (methylcobalamin); neurotropic agent used in peripheral nerve recovery
- **EX-HN19 (Jiachengjiang)**: Extra point below CV24, used for mandibular/mental nerve disorders
- **ST4, ST6, ST7**: Stomach meridian points on the face around the mandible
- **LI4, PC6**: Distal acupoints (large intestine 4, pericardium 6) used for systemic analgesic/neuromodulatory effects
