## Why Ingested

[[implants/mbl/gupta-2019-platform-switching-crestal-bone-loss-sr]] attributes this wiki's single most specific numeric claim in the platform-switching (PS) evidence cluster — that a 0.45 mm per-side abutment-diameter reduction is "sufficient" to avoid peri-implant crestal bone loss — to this paper, but only secondhand via Gupta's Table 1. This ingest sources that claim independently from the primary study itself (PubMed structured abstract, since no PMC full text is available), so the wiki no longer relies on a single citing intermediary for its most quotable PS number. It also gives this wiki's PS cluster its earliest primary controlled-comparison data point (submitted/published 2007, before [[implants/mbl/lazzara-2006-platform-switching-new-concept]]'s foundational 2006 observation had been followed by controlled trials).

## Three-line Summary

Prospective, non-randomized comparative clinical trial in 15 patients: 14 wide-diameter implants restored with platform-switched (PS) abutments (test, 5 mm implant / 4.1 mm abutment, i.e. 0.45 mm per-side offset) vs 8 regular-diameter implants restored with traditional (non-platform-switched, PM) abutments (control), followed 1 year after final restoration.

Mean crestal bone height at 1-year follow-up: -0.22 mm ± 0.53 mm (PS/test) vs -2.02 mm ± 0.49 mm (control) — significant at baseline and follow-up (P ≤ .0001); mean bone-level *change* from baseline to 1 year was -0.12 mm ± 0.40 mm (test) vs -0.29 mm ± 0.34 mm (control), significant by ANCOVA (P ≤ .0132).

Authors conclude platform switching limits crestal resorption and that a 0.45 mm per-side abutment reduction "seems sufficient" to avoid peri-implant bone loss — but this is a single small non-randomized trial (n=14 test vs 8 control implants, one offset magnitude tested, 1-year follow-up only), so the specific "0.45 mm is sufficient" threshold has not been dose-response tested against other offset magnitudes within this study.

## 세줄요약

전향적 비무작위 비교임상시험, 환자 15명: 광경 임플란트 14개는 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS) 지대주로 수복(시험군, 임플란트 5mm/지대주 4.1mm, 편측 0.45mm 오프셋), 표준경 임플란트 8개는 전통적(비-PS) 지대주로 수복(대조군), 최종 보철 장착 후 1년 추적.

1년 추적 시 평균 치조정골 높이: 시험군(PS) -0.22mm±0.53mm vs 대조군 -2.02mm±0.49mm (기저치·추적 모두 P≤.0001로 유의); 기저치→1년 골수준 변화량은 시험군 -0.12mm±0.40mm vs 대조군 -0.29mm±0.34mm(ANCOVA P≤.0132로 유의).

저자들은 플랫폼 스위칭이 치조정골 흡수를 제한하며 **지대주 편측 0.45mm 축소가 골소실 방지에 "충분한 것으로 보인다"**고 결론지었으나, 이는 소규모 단일 비무작위 시험(시험 임플란트 14개 vs 대조 8개, 오프셋 크기 1종만 시험, 1년 추적)이라 이 논문 자체 내에서 다른 오프셋 크기와의 용량-반응 검증은 이루어지지 않았다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: J Oral Maxillofac Surg. 2007 Jul;65(7 Suppl 1):33-9.
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.joms.2007.03.024
- **Institution**: Department of Operative Dentistry and Periodontology, Albert Ludwigs University, Freiburg, Germany (Hürzeler); private practice co-authors (Fickl, Zuhr, Wachtel)

## 2. Key Contributions
- One of the earliest controlled clinical (non-randomized) comparisons of platform-switched vs traditionally-restored implants with quantified 1-year radiographic bone-level outcomes.
- Provides the specific offset-magnitude number (0.45 mm per side, 5 mm implant / 4.1 mm abutment) that later reviews (e.g. Gupta 2019) cite as evidence toward a minimum "sufficient" mismatch threshold.
- Distinguishes baseline crestal bone height (already lower/better in the PS group at time of final restoration, -0.09 mm vs -1.73 mm) from subsequent bone-level *change* over the following year — i.e. most of the between-group difference at 1 year reflects a baseline difference already present, with a smaller but still statistically significant additional divergence over the observation year.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective, controlled clinical trial; test group (implant diameter dictated PS vs non-PS restoration, not stated as randomized) — `evidence_level: prospective`, not RCT.
- **n**: 15 patients; 14 wide-diameter implants (PS/test group), 8 regular-diameter implants (traditional abutment/control group)
- **Measurement**: standardized digital radiographs at time of final restoration placement and at 1-year follow-up; marginal peri-implant bone levels measured mesially and distally per implant via digital image analysis
- **Outcomes**: crestal bone height at baseline and 1 year; bone-level change from baseline to 1 year (ANCOVA)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Timepoint | PS / test group | Non-PS / control group | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline (final restoration) | -0.09 mm ± 0.65 mm | -1.73 mm ± 0.46 mm | P ≤ .0001 |
| 1-year follow-up | -0.22 mm ± 0.53 mm | -2.02 mm ± 0.49 mm | P ≤ .0001 |
| Change, baseline→1yr | -0.12 mm ± 0.40 mm | -0.29 mm ± 0.34 mm | ANCOVA P ≤ .0132 |

Offset tested: 0.45 mm per side (5 mm implant / 4.1 mm abutment) in the test group; 0 mm (matched diameter) in control.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (14 test vs 8 control implants; 15 patients total) — likely underpowered relative to the effect claimed.
- Group assignment tracked implant diameter (wide-diameter → PS, regular-diameter → non-PS), not stated as randomized — `prospective` non-randomized comparative design, confounding by implant diameter/site cannot be excluded.
- Only 1-year follow-up — no data on whether the observed difference is stable long-term (contrast with later 3–5 year RCTs in this wiki's PS cluster, e.g. [[implants/mbl/enkling-2013-platform-switching-bone-level-3year-rct]], [[implants/mbl/messias-2019-platform-switching-mbl-5year-rct]]).
- Only a single offset magnitude (0.45 mm/side) was tested — the "sufficient" claim is not a dose-response finding within this study; it is an inference from one data point compared against literature ranges of prior bone loss (1.5–2 mm cited from other long-term studies).
- Abstract-only ingest (no PMC full text, no PDF obtained) — Methods/Results detail beyond the structured abstract (e.g. implant system, exact site distribution, statistical test names beyond ANCOVA) is [미검증] and not available to this wiki.

## 6. Related Work

- strietzel-2015: independent, later, higher-rigor SR+MA (22 studies) that reinforces the same directional PS-vs-PM bone-preservation conclusion with pooled effect sizes — see [[implants/mbl/strietzel-2015-platform-switching-mbl-sr-ma]].

## 7. Glossary
- **Platform switching (PS)**: use of a prosthetic abutment narrower than the implant's coronal platform diameter, shifting the implant-abutment junction (IAJ) horizontally inward, away from the crestal bone.
- **Platform matching / non-platform-switched (PM)**: abutment diameter matches the implant platform diameter (no horizontal offset) — the control condition here.
- **Per-side offset / mismatch**: half the difference between implant platform diameter and abutment diameter; here (5 mm − 4.1 mm) / 2 = 0.45 mm.
- **ANCOVA**: analysis of covariance — used here to test between-group difference in bone-level change while accounting for baseline covariates.
- **Crestal bone height**: radiographically measured distance from a fixed reference point (typically implant shoulder) to the first bone-to-implant contact, at mesial and distal surfaces.
