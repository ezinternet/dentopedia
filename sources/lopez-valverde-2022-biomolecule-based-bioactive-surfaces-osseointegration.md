## Why Ingested
Existing [[wiki/implants/surface/smeets-2016-implant-surface-modifications-osseointegration]] flags protein/growth-factor (BMP-2, PDGF) coatings as "experimental" without quantifying the evidence; this SR+MA (26 preclinical studies, 1,109 implants) is the first meta-analytic attempt to pool BIC outcomes across biomolecule-coating categories (peptide, BMP, ECM/collagen) and quantifies just how weak that evidence base is — very-high heterogeneity (I²=99%), publication bias, and no significant pooled advantage over uncoated controls except a single favorable BMP-subgroup result.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review and meta-analysis (INPLASY-registered, PRISMA) of 26 preclinical animal studies (1,109 titanium implants; peptide/BMP/ECM-collagen biomolecule coatings vs uncoated etched controls) pooling bone-to-implant contact (BIC) at 4 weeks post-placement.
Random-effects meta-analysis (RevMan 5.4.1) found only one result favorable to coating (in the BMP subgroup); across grouped studies there was no significant difference between coated and uncoated implants, with very high heterogeneity (I²=99%) and funnel-plot evidence of publication bias.
Biomolecule surface coatings may favor osseointegration in early healing stages qualitatively, but the meta-analytic evidence is inconclusive and heterogeneous — long-term, better-standardized studies (and eventually clinical trials) are needed before any clinical recommendation.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First meta-analysis (not just narrative review) pooling BIC outcomes across biomolecule-based bioactive surface modifications on titanium dental implants, using RevMan random-effects modeling.
- Classifies the biomolecule-coating literature into three categories — peptides (5 studies), BMPs (14 studies), and ECM/collagen products (7 studies) — and tabulates animal model, implantation site, follow-up, and BIC/BA/BD findings for each of the 26 included studies.
- Applies SYRCLE risk-of-bias tool (animal-study adapted Cochrane RoB) and ARRIVE 23-item reporting-quality checklist (mean score 16.5 ± 1.5/23) to the included preclinical literature, exposing systematic underreporting of animal-welfare items (3Rs, adverse events, rationale for animal model — 0/26 studies reported these).
- Quantifies the near-total absence of pooled meta-analytic benefit: across 26 studies, only one BMP-subgroup comparison favored the coated surface; the funnel plot shows publication-bias asymmetry.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (INPLASY202260076, PRISMA-guided), preclinical animal studies only
- **Databases**: PubMed/MEDLINE, Web of Science, EMBASE (searched to May 2022), plus gray literature/cross-referencing
- **n**: 26 included studies (from 10,697 initial hits → 84 title/abstract-screened → 26 full-text included); 1,109 implants total
- **Outcomes**: Primary — bone-to-implant contact (BIC), measured at 4 weeks post-placement in all 26 studies; secondary — bone area (BA, 6 studies), bone density (BD, 9 studies)
- **Statistics**: RevMan 5.4.1, random-effects model (chosen a priori for expected methodological heterogeneity), mean difference (MD)/SD effect size, I² > 50% deemed significant heterogeneity, α = 0.05, funnel plot for publication bias

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 26 studies / 1,109 implants; follow-up ranged 2–16 weeks except Bae et al. (84 weeks, rat tibia, Type I collagen/GA coating).
- Coating categories: peptides (5 studies — e.g., RGD-peptide, biomimetic P-15, vitronectin-derived peptide), BMPs (14 studies — rhBMP-2 dominant, also rhBMP-7, rhGDF-5), ECM/collagen (7 studies — Type I collagen ± crosslinking agents, ± chondroitin sulfate).
- Pooled BIC meta-analysis at 4 weeks: heterogeneity **very high, I²=99%**; only one comparison (within the BMP subgroup) statistically favored the coated surface; overall, no significant difference between coated and uncoated (SLA-type etched) controls.
- Funnel plot showed graphic asymmetry consistent with publication bias.
- ARRIVE reporting-quality mean 16.5 ± 1.5/23; items on animal-model rationale, 3Rs, and adverse events were reported by 0/26 studies; animal accommodation/handling reported by only 5/26.
- SYRCLE risk-of-bias: random sequence generation most often reported (60%); blinding of participants/personnel and outcome assessors were the least-reported domains, yielding high/unclear risk of bias across most included studies.
- Individual highlights: Wikesjö et al.'s three rhBMP-2 dog/monkey studies were inconsistent — one non-human-primate study found accelerated bone formation, one canine dose-response study found rhBMP-2 induced osseointegration but with BIC values significantly *higher in the uncoated control*; Ramazanoglu et al. similarly found no BIC increase with rhBMP-2+rhVEGF biomimetic coating despite improved peri-implant bone density.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Very high statistical heterogeneity (I²=99%) across pooled studies — different animal models (dog, pig, rabbit, rat, monkey, goat), implantation sites (jaw, tibia, femur, calvaria), and follow-up windows (2–84 weeks) undermine pooling validity.
- Meta-analysis restricted to only three biomolecule-coating categories (peptides/BMPs/ECM); most studies used combined rather than single coatings, complicating attribution.
- Discrepant follow-up periods and animal numbers across studies.
- Only BIC was chosen as the osseointegration outcome measure, even though several studies also reported BA/BD/other parameters.
- All evidence is preclinical/animal — authors explicitly state results "do not necessarily reflect the human clinical reality"; no human clinical trials were eligible for inclusion.
- Publication bias evident on funnel plot.

## 7. Glossary
- **BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact)**: histomorphometric percentage of implant surface in direct contact with mineralized bone; the primary outcome pooled in this meta-analysis.
- **BMP (Bone Morphogenetic Protein)**: TGF-β superfamily growth factor that induces osteogenic differentiation of mesenchymal stem cells; rhBMP-2 was the most common coating tested (9/26 studies).
- **ECM (Extracellular Matrix) coating**: coatings based on Type I collagen and related matrix macromolecules (fibronectin, laminin, chondroitin sulfate) that provide structural/biochemical cues for cell adhesion and osteogenic differentiation.
- **RGD peptide (Arg-Gly-Asp)**: short amino-acid sequence recognized by cell-surface integrin receptors; used to biofunctionalize implant surfaces to promote osteoblast adhesion.
- **SYRCLE RoB tool**: an animal-study-adapted version of the Cochrane risk-of-bias tool, used to assess methodological quality of the included preclinical studies.
- **I² (heterogeneity statistic)**: percentage of total variability across studies attributable to between-study heterogeneity rather than chance; I²=99% in this meta-analysis indicates the pooled BIC estimate should be interpreted with strong caution.
