## Why Ingested

탈감작제 도포 후 dentin bonding agent의 결합강도가 떨어지는지에 대한 controversial 질문을 SR로 정리. [[resin-bonding/cotert-2024-desensitizing-agents-zirconia-bond-strength]] 단일 실험을 상위 근거로 묶고, [[overviews/adhesive-bonding-strategies-overview]]의 표면처리 단락에 연결.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (23 in vitro studies, human extracted teeth, 2010–2020; PubMed, Cochrane, Google Scholar; Cochrane RoB assessment) evaluating how prior desensitizing agent application affects subsequent dentin bonding agent bond strength.

The effect was agent- and protocol-dependent: some desensitizers reduced bond strength, others preserved or increased it, with no uniform direction across the included studies.

The controversial and heterogeneous results mean no universal recommendation can be made; clinicians should consider specific agent pairings and application timing when combining desensitization and adhesive bonding.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(in vitro 23편, 사람 발거치아, 2010–2020; PubMed·Cochrane·Google Scholar; 코크란 비뚤림위험 평가) — 탈감작제 도포 후 dentin bonding agent 결합강도에 미치는 영향.

효과는 탈감작제 종류·도포 프로토콜에 따라 결과가 엇갈림: 결합강도 감소, 보존, 증가가 제제별로 혼재하여 일관된 방향성 없음.

결과 상충으로 보편적 권고 불가 — 탈감작과 접착을 병용할 때는 구체적 제제 조합과 도포 시점을 고려해야 함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- PICO-structured SR isolating desensitizer effect on dentin-adhesive bond strength.
- Modified Cochrane RoB assessment of 23 in vitro studies.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PubMed, Cochrane, Google Scholar; 2010–2020; in vitro human-tooth bond-strength studies after desensitizer application.
- 146 identified → 23 included; customized extraction; modified Cochrane Collaboration tool.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Effect is agent- and protocol-dependent; some desensitizers reduce, some preserve or increase bond strength — no uniform direction.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- In vitro only; heterogeneous adhesives/desensitizers; controversial net effect.

## 6. Related Work
- Umbrella for single-study SBS experiments such as the zirconia desensitizer study.
