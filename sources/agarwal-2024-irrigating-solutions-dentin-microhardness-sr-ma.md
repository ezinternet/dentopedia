## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (44 studies assessed, PRISMA-compliant) examining how endodontic irrigating solutions — especially NaOCl, EDTA, and chelating agents — affect root-canal dentin microhardness measured by Vickers/Knoop indentation.

Irrigating solutions reduce dentin microhardness; magnitude depends on concentration and on testing methodology (load, dwell time, sectioning direction), making direct cross-study comparisons difficult.

Standardization of in-vitro microhardness testing protocols is urgently needed before clinical implications regarding structural integrity of root dentin can be reliably drawn.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA(44편 평가, PRISMA): NaOCl·EDTA·킬레이트제 등 근관세정제가 Vickers/Knoop 압입법으로 측정한 상아질 미세경도에 미치는 영향 정량 분석.

세정제는 상아질 미세경도를 감소시키며, 그 크기는 농도와 측정 방법론(하중·접촉시간·절단 방향)에 따라 달라져 연구 간 직접 비교가 어려움.

근관 상아질 구조적 완전성에 대한 임상적 결론을 도출하려면 in vitro 미세경도 측정 프로토콜의 표준화가 선행되어야 함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Meta-analyzes irrigant effects on dentin microhardness.
- Highlights methodological moderators (sample prep, load, dwell time, sectioning).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PRISMA SR+MA; 44 studies assessed with consistent quality/RoB; microhardness (Vickers/Knoop) outcomes pooled with moderator discussion.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Irrigating solutions (esp. NaOCl, EDTA, chelators/acids) reduce dentin microhardness; magnitude depends on concentration and on testing methodology.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Heterogeneous indentation methods/loads; in vitro; standardization needed.

## 6. Related Work
- Mechanical-property counterpart to FTIR chemistry review and chelator SR.

## 7. Glossary
- Microhardness: Vickers/Knoop indentation resistance, proxy for dentin mineral integrity.
