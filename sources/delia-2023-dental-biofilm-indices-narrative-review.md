## Three-line Summary

Narrative review summarizing dental plaque/biofilm indices used to evaluate home oral hygiene effectiveness, classifying them into non-quantitative (area/numerical, gingival thickness, dichotomous) and quantitative (weight, planimetric, QLF, automated, 3D-coordinate) methods.

Non-quantitative indices (Plaque Index/PlI, Quigley-Hein/Turesky-modified QHPI, Navy Plaque Index and its RMNPI/MNPI modifications, O'Leary Plaque Control Record, OHI/OHI-S) remain the most widely used because they need no special instruments; quantitative methods (QLF, automated RGB/HSI photographic analysis, 3D coordinate-measuring machine) offer greater objectivity but require dedicated equipment and are largely confined to research.

Clinicians/researchers must select the index matching their purpose (epidemiologic survey vs clinical trial vs chairside motivation) since only general findings are comparable across different indices, and ad hoc index modification should be avoided; a Full-Mouth Plaque Score of 20-25% is an acceptable maintenance threshold, tightening to ≤15% before regenerative periodontal surgery.

## 세줄요약

가정용 구강위생 관리 효과를 평가하는 치태/바이오필름 지수들을 정리한 서술적 문헌고찰로, 비정량적(면적/수치, 치은연 두께, 이분법) 방법과 정량적(중량, planimetric, QLF, automated, 3D 좌표) 방법으로 분류한다.

비정량적 지수(PlI, Quigley-Hein/Turesky 변형 QHPI, Navy Plaque Index 및 그 변형인 RMNPI/MNPI, O'Leary Plaque Control Record, OHI/OHI-S)는 특수 장비가 필요 없어 가장 널리 쓰이며, 정량적 방법(QLF, 자동화 RGB/HSI 사진분석, 3D 좌표측정기)은 객관성은 높지만 전용 장비가 필요해 연구 영역에 국한된다.

역학조사·임상시험·환자 동기부여 등 목적에 맞는 지수를 선택해야 하며(서로 다른 지수 간에는 일반적 경향만 비교 가능, 임의 변형 지양), Full-Mouth Plaque Score 20-25%가 유지관리 허용 임계값이고 재생 치주수술 전에는 ≤15%로 더 엄격히 관리해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides a single, organized taxonomy of dental plaque/biofilm indices, splitting them first by **objectivity** (non-quantitative vs quantitative) and then by **scoring criterion** (area/extent, thickness, weight, fluorescence, presence/absence, 3D volume).
- Clarifies the historical lineage of the most-cited indices: Ramfjord Plaque Component (1959) → Shick-Ash modification; Green-Vermillion OHI (1960) → OHI-S/DI-S → Glass Index; Quigley-Hein PI (1962) → Turesky modification (1970, TQHPI); Navy Plaque Index (1973) → Elliott modification (MNPI) and Rustogi Modified Navy Plaque Index (RMNPI).
- Explains the mechanism and history of disclosing agents (iodine, Bismarck brown, erythrosine, methylene blue, basic fuchsin, two-tone solutions) as the practical prerequisite for applying most non-quantitative indices.
- States explicit Full-Mouth Plaque Score (FMPS) clinical thresholds: 20-25% acceptable for periodontal maintenance; ≤15% (plaque-free at the surgical site) required before regenerative periodontal surgery.
- Surveys emerging quantitative/objective techniques (QLF, automated RGB/HSI/"yellow-pixel" photographic analysis, CMM-based 3D plaque quantification) and assesses their present-day clinical feasibility versus research-only utility.
- Lists five ideal-index criteria clinicians/researchers should weigh when choosing among competing indices: simplicity, minimal time/instrumentation, clearly defined reproducible criteria, amenability to statistical analysis, and uniform sensitivity across the scale.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative (non-systematic) literature review. No PRISMA flow, explicit search strategy, or quality-appraisal tool is reported; the authors synthesize and classify indices described across the periodontal/preventive-dentistry literature since the mid-20th century, organized under two summary tables (non-quantitative indices; quantitative indices) referenced in the original PDF/HTML (not reproduced in the plain-text full text retrieved via PMC).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

No original clinical data are generated (this is a methods-taxonomy review, not a primary study). Key synthesized findings:
- Non-quantitative indices dominate clinical/research use because they require no special instruments, at the cost of subjectivity (inter/intra-examiner variability).
- Quantitative indices (weight-based, planimetric, QLF, automated, 3D-CMM) trade convenience for objectivity/reproducibility but remain largely confined to research settings due to equipment and training demands.
- Dichotomous indices (e.g., O'Leary Plaque Control Record) are best suited to chairside patient motivation; severity-graded indices (e.g., TQHPI, RMNPI) are best suited to clinical trials and epidemiologic surveys.
- FMPS 20-25% = acceptable maintenance threshold; FMPS ≤15% (plaque-free surgical site) = threshold before regenerative periodontal surgery, since lower biofilm burden correlates with greater clinical attachment gain.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative (not systematic) review — no reproducible search strategy, no risk-of-bias/quality appraisal of the underlying primary sources for each index's validation.
- Does not perform head-to-head statistical comparison of index performance (sensitivity, inter-examiner reliability) across studies; conclusions on "most used" indices are qualitative impressions rather than a quantified survey.
- Original summary tables and schematic figures (referenced in-text as "listed in" / "shown in") were not captured in the plain full text retrieved via PMC; readers needing the exact tabulated index-by-index comparison should consult the published PDF/HTML version.
- The authors flag that findings from different plaque indices are only broadly comparable, not interchangeable — a methodological caution rather than a resolved limitation.

## 6. Related Work

- Ramfjord SP (1959) — Plaque Component of the Periodontal Disease Index (foundational six-teeth index).
- Shick RA, Ash MM — modification limiting Ramfjord's index to buccal/lingual gingival-third surfaces.
- Greene JC, Vermillion JR (1960) — Oral Hygiene Index (OHI) and its Simplified (OHI-S)/Debris (DI-S) derivatives.
- Quigley GA, Hein JW (1962) — Quigley-Hein Plaque Index; modified by Turesky et al. (1970) into the widely used TQHPI.
- Navy Plaque Index (1973); modified by Elliott (MNPI) and by Rustogi et al. (RMNPI) — both used in interdental-cleaning efficacy RCTs in this wiki (e.g., [[wiki/interdental-cleaning/sawan-2022-super-floss-water-flosser-orthodontic-rct]]).
- O'Leary TJ et al. (1972) — Plaque Control Record, the archetypal dichotomous (presence/absence) index.
- Yeganeh et al. — 3D coordinate-measuring-machine (CMM) plaque quantification method.

## 7. Glossary

- **Biofilm**: spatially organized, metabolically integrated microbial community embedded in an extracellular polymeric matrix.
- **Disclosing agent**: dye (e.g., erythrosine, two-tone solution) that selectively stains dental plaque to increase visual contrast for scoring or patient education.
- **RMNPI (Rustogi Modified Navy Plaque Index)**: a further 9-area-per-tooth subdivision of the Navy Plaque Index, emphasizing gingival-margin-adjacent deposits.
- **TQHPI (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein Plaque Index)**: weighted buccal+oral full-dentition plaque score; the most commonly used index in plaque-removal clinical trials.
- **FMPS (Full-Mouth Plaque Score)**: percentage of examined tooth sites showing detectable plaque, used as a single-value oral-hygiene summary metric.
- **QLF (Quantitative Light-induced Fluorescence)**: optical method exploiting natural/plaque-associated (porphyrin) fluorescence to detect and quantify biofilm and early demineralization.
- **Ramfjord teeth**: six selected teeth (16, 21, 24, 36, 41, 44) used as a representative subset for partial-mouth periodontal/plaque indices.
