## Three-line Summary

Multicenter (Berne/Witten/Bonn) 3-year RCT: 2-piece bone-level implant randomized to platform switching (PS) vs platform matching (PM) abutment, follow-up at 1, 2, 3 years with standardized periapical radiographs.

PS group showed significantly less crestal bone loss over 3 years vs PM; benefit maintained through 36-month follow-up; no difference in implant survival between groups.

3-year RCT extends prior short-term evidence on PS advantage; addresses controversy in the literature; confirms PS clinical benefit beyond 12-month follow-up.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 3-year follow-up extending beyond the typical 12-month studies
- Randomized controlled trial design with PS vs PM groups
- Platform switching: significantly less crestal bone loss
- Multi-center university collaboration (Berne, Witten, Bonn)
- Addresses controversy around platform switching clinical evidence

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Randomized clinical trial. Two-piece dental implants randomized to PS or PM abutment configurations. Follow-up at 1, 2, and 3 years post-loading. Radiographic bone level changes measured on standardized periapical radiographs.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- PS group: significantly less crestal bone loss over 3 years vs PM
- Benefit of platform switching maintained through 36-month follow-up
- No significant difference in implant survival between groups
- Results extend and confirm short-term PS advantages to 3 years

## 7. Glossary
- **PS**: Platform switching — use of smaller-diameter abutment on larger implant collar
- **PM**: Platform matching — abutment matches implant platform diameter
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss — radiographic bone level change around implant
- **TL**: Tissue-level implant — transmucosal component integral to implant
- **BL**: Bone-level implant — implant platform at crestal bone level
