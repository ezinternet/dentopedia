## Why Ingested

한국 대학병원(서울대학교 분당병원)에서 2.5 mm/3.0 mm 일체형 협경 임플란트 (narrow-diameter implant, NDI) 40개를 평균 10.5년 추적한 후향적 연구로, 실패 2개(보철 후 3개월의 조기 골유착 실패 1개, 118개월의 후기 치주염 1개)의 시기 구분과 한국 실사용 장기 데이터를 제공한다. 3년 추적 한국 NDI 연구를 장기로 연장한다. → [[implants/short-narrow/kim-2023-clinical-outcome-of-narrow-diameter]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center study at Seoul National University Bundang Hospital (Korea; implants 2007-2022, at least 6 years of follow-up required) of one-piece 2.5 mm and 3.0 mm narrow-diameter implants (Osstem MS SA narrow ridge): 25 patients, 40 implants, mean 10.5 years (range 6.1-14.0) of function.

Survival was 95.1% at the implant level and 96.0% at the patient level; two 2.5 mm implants failed in one patient with uncontrolled diabetes and poor hygiene (one osseointegration failure 3 months after immediate loading, one peri-implantitis 118 months after loading), and all 3.0 mm implants survived.

Mean marginal bone loss was 0.44±0.57 mm and only guided bone regeneration (GBR) at placement was significantly associated with it (p=0.046); the sample is very small and patient ethnicity is not stated in the text (Korean only implied by the setting).

## 세줄요약

서울대학교 분당병원(한국)에서 2007-2022년 식립한 Osstem MS SA 일체형 2.5 mm/3.0 mm 협경 임플란트 (narrow-diameter implant, NDI)를 6년 이상 추적한 후향적 단일기관 연구: 환자 25명, 임플란트 40개, 보철 후 평균 10.5년(범위 6.1-14.0).

생존율은 임플란트 기준 95.1%, 환자 기준 96.0%이며, 실패 2개는 모두 조절되지 않은 당뇨와 불량한 구강위생을 가진 한 환자의 2.5 mm 임플란트로 즉시 부하 3개월 후 골유착 실패 1개와 118개월 후 치주염 1개였고 3.0 mm는 전부 생존했다.

평균 변연골 소실 (marginal bone loss, MBL)은 0.44±0.57 mm였고 식립 시 골유도재생술 (guided bone regeneration, GBR)만 유의한 관련을 보였다 (p=0.046); 표본이 매우 작고 환자 인종은 본문에 명시되지 않았다(한국 환자는 기관 위치로만 추정).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Long-term (mean 10.5 years, range 6.1-14.0) Korean data for one-piece 2.5 mm and 3.0 mm NDIs used in fixed prostheses (single crowns, cantilevers, bridges) in anterior and posterior areas.
- Survival 95.1% (implant level) and 96.0% (patient level); 2.5 mm NDIs 93.8%, 3.0 mm NDIs 100.0%.
- Separates early from late failure: one failure 3 months after loading, one 118 months after loading.
- Mean MBL 0.44±0.57 mm; only GBR at placement was significantly associated with MBL (p=0.046).
- Two prosthetic complications (one decementation, one veneer chipping).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective study, single center, IRB No. B-2411-938-104 (SNUBH), consent waived, Declaration of Helsinki.
- **Population**: patients treated for edentulous areas with MS SA narrow ridge implants (Osstem Implant) from 2007 to 2022. Lengths 10, 11.5, 13, 15 mm; diameters 2.5 and 3.0 mm; all one-piece, tapered, sandblasted and acid-etched (SA) surface. Excluded: observation under 6 years, lost to follow-up, adjacent natural teeth extracted during follow-up.
- **Variables**: age, sex, jaw, site (anterior/posterior), placement timing after extraction (immediate/delayed), placement depth (subcrestal/equicrestal/supracrestal), GBR, diameter, loading timing (immediate/early/delayed, cut-offs at 1 week and 8 weeks), prosthesis type (splinted/non-splinted), opposing dentition (natural teeth including fixed prosthesis, implant prosthesis, removable prosthesis).
- **Radiographs**: periapical, recall every 6 months or 1 year; MBL = change in distance from the alveolar crest to the fixture platform between baseline (immediately after surgery) and final follow-up, calibrated to fixture length, mean of mesial and distal sides.
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier survival; univariate and multivariate logistic regression for MBL (the dichotomization of MBL used as the outcome is not described in the retrieved text); R 4.1.0; p<0.05.
- **Failure definition**: not explicitly defined in the text; the two failures are described as implant removal (osseointegration failure; peri-implantitis).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Authors: only patients with more than 6 years of follow-up were analysed, limiting data; retrospective design with insufficient bone quality and quantity data at placement; larger long-term prospective studies, especially for NDIs under 3.0 mm, are needed.
- Exclusion of patients with under 6 years of follow-up or loss to follow-up creates survivorship selection; failures before 6 years in patients who were then lost would not be captured.
- Very small sample: 25 patients, 40 implants, 2 failures in a single patient (clustered); 2.5 mm vs 3.0 mm survival comparison and risk factor analyses are underpowered. The 2.5 mm group size is not given in the text (the 93.8% figure is consistent with 15 of 16 survivors, an inference, not a statement in the text).
- Source-data issue: 2 failures of 40 implants is 95.0%, while the text reports 95.1% (probably the Kaplan-Meier estimate; method not stated). The text also counts the replacement implant in the survival calculation without clarifying whether it is one of the 40.
- Implant distribution is unbalanced (35 of 40 mandibular, 32 of 40 anterior), limiting generalization to maxillary and posterior sites.
- Both failures arose in a patient with uncontrolled diabetes and poor hygiene; the single-patient clustering means no implant-level inference about risk factors is possible.
- Patient ethnicity/nationality is not stated; Korean setting only.

## 7. Glossary

- **NDI (Narrow-Diameter Implant, 협경 임플란트)**: implant of 3.5 mm diameter or less in the paper's definition; here 2.5 and 3.0 mm.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골 소실)**: radiographic change in crestal bone level around the fixture.
- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 골유도재생술)**: bone augmentation with barrier membrane at the implant site.
- **One-piece implant (일체형 임플란트)**: fixture and abutment fabricated as a single unit.
