## Why Ingested

수직 유도골재생(Vertical Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)에서 자가골(Autogenous Bone, AB) + 탈단백소골(Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 복합이식재는 표준이지만, 이를 만들려면 **제2 수술부위(second surgical site)** 가 필요하고 이것이 사실상 유일한 실클리닉 결함이다. 이 RCT는 백혈구-혈소판풍부피브린(Leukocyte-Platelet-Rich Fibrin, L-PRF) 골블록을 그 대체재로 직접 비교하는 무작위(split-mouth) 대조 시험이며, [[wiki/overviews/bone-graft-material-selection-matrix-overview]]가 정리한 "골이식재 선택은 용적 안정성 목표·비용·공여부 이환으로 결정된다"는 clinical bottom line과 "공여부 회피 시 동종골/합성골(또는 동종골 블록)"이라는 해법을 **수직 GBR 영역에서 정면으로 검증**한다. 동시에 [[wiki/overviews/autogenous-bone-graft-donor-site-selection-overview]]가 정리한 "제2 수술부위 자체를 없애는" 흐름에, 채취를 통째로 생략하는 **혈소판 유래 골블록**이라는 세 번째 축을 추가한다. 생물학적 근거 측면에서는 [[wiki/bone-regeneration/cho-2026-prf-bone-regeneration-mechanisms-scoping-review]]가 PRF를 "독립 구조 이식재가 아닌 생물학적 보조제"로 규정하는데, 이 RCT는 그 부정형 명제를 클리닉에서 직접 시험한다. 또한 [[wiki/bone-regeneration/idiri-2023-prf-xenograft-sinus-ridge-augmentation-sr]]의 "PRF는 통상 비교군보다 우월하지 않다"는 SR 결론을 이종골이 아닌 자가골+DBBM 복합을 상대로 한 무작위(split-mouth) RCT 수준에서 재확인한다.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized, double-blind, split-mouth clinical trial (6 patients, 12 sites) comparing a leukocyte-platelet-rich fibrin (L-PRF) bone block graft against a composite graft mixture of 50% autogenous bone (AB) with 50% deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM) for vertical guided bone regeneration (GBR), with a dense polytetrafluoroethylene (d-PTFE) membrane used at both test and control sites before implant placement.

Vertical bone height (VBH) gain did not differ significantly between arms at any timepoint: 4.6 ± 3.0 mm (test) vs 5.2 ± 2.7 mm (control) at implant placement, and 3.0 ± 2.8 mm vs 3.8 ± 2.6 mm one year after implant loading (P = .96); complications were minimal and comprised one implant loss due to infection in a test site.

The authors read the L-PRF bone block as a potentially viable alternative that could reduce the need for harvesting bone from a second surgical site, but with 6 patients and no significant difference at any timepoint this is feasibility evidence rather than demonstrated equivalence — the authors themselves call for larger sample sizes.

## 세줄요약

무작위·이중눈가림(split-mouth) 임상시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (환자 6명, 부위 12개): 임플란트 식립 전 수직 유도골재생(Vertical Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)에서 백혈구-혈소판풍부피브린(Leukocyte-Platelet-Rich Fibrin, L-PRF) 골블록 이식재를 대조군인 자가골(Autogenous Bone, AB) 50% + 탈단백소골(Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 50% 복합이식재와 비교했고, 양군 모두 조밀 폴리테트라플루오로에틸렌(Dense Polytetrafluoroethylene, d-PTFE) 막을 사용했다.

수직골높이(Vertical Bone Height, VBH) 증가량은 **어느 시점에서도 군간 유의차가 없었다** — 임플란트 식립 시점 시험군 4.6 ± 3.0 mm vs 대조군 5.2 ± 2.7 mm, 임플란트 하중 1년 후 시험군 3.0 ± 2.8 mm vs 대조군 3.8 ± 2.6 mm(P = .96). 합병증은 최소였고 시험군 1예에서 감염에 의한 임플란트 상실이 발생했다.

저자들은 L-PRF 골블록을 **제2 수술부위 골 채취를 줄일 수 있는 유력한 대안**으로 제시하나, 환자 6명이고 전 시점 유의차가 없으므로 이는 "동등성 증명"이 아니라 "실행가능성(feasibility)" 수준의 근거다 — 저자들도 후속 대규모 연구 필요성을 명시했다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **First head-to-head RCT of an L-PRF bone block used as the sole vertical GBR graft** against the conventional composite (50% AB + 50% DBBM) under a d-PTFE membrane — the exact comparison this wiki did not hold.
2. **Vertical bone height measured radiographically (CBCT) at two postoperative timepoints (9 and 25 months)** with a split-mouth design, so each of the 6 patients serves as their own control.
3. **Follow-up carried past implant loading** ("up to 1 year of follow-up after implant loading") — the only vertical-GBR trial here that reports the grafted site after the implant has been loaded.
4. **Quantifies post-loading remodeling of the gained bone** in both arms (VBH gain falls from 4.6/5.2 mm at implant placement to 3.0/3.8 mm one year after loading).
5. **Addresses donor-site burden head-on**: the stated rationale for the L-PRF block is that it "could be a viable alternative to the composite graft, potentially reducing the need for harvesting bone from a second surgical site."

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: randomized, double-blind, split-mouth clinical trial
- **n**: 6 patients requiring bilateral vertical GBR before implant placement (12 sites)
- **Test site**: leukocyte-platelet-rich fibrin (L-PRF) bone block graft
- **Control site**: composite graft mixture of 50% autogenous bone (AB) with 50% deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM)
- **Barrier**: a dense polytetrafluoroethylene (d-PTFE) membrane was used at **both** test and control sites — the trial therefore compares graft materials under one fixed membrane protocol, not membrane strategies
- **Outcome**: primary outcome measure = vertical bone height (VBH) gain, assessed by CBCT at 9 and 25 months postoperative
- **Clinical landmarks**: VBH gain reported "at implant placement" and "one year after implant loading"; the abstract does not explicitly map the 9- and 25-month CBCT readings onto these two moments
- **Not reported in the abstract**: allocation concealment and sequence generation, sample-size calculation, exact defect morphology and baseline defect height, L-PRF preparation protocol (spin speed/RCF, block dimensions), implant system and number of implants per site, donor-site morbidity measurement, histomorphometric/mineralized-tissue outcome, implant survival beyond the single loss, funding and conflicts of interest

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Timepoint | Test (L-PRF bone block) | Control (50% AB + 50% DBBM) | Reported significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| At implant placement | VBH gain 4.6 ± 3.0 mm | VBH gain 5.2 ± 2.7 mm | no significant difference at any timepoint |
| 1 year after implant loading | VBH gain 3.0 ± 2.8 mm | VBH gain 3.8 ± 2.6 mm | P = .96 |

- The abstract states there was **no significant difference in VBH gain between test and control sites at any time point**; the only p-value printed is P = .96, attached to the one-year post-loading figures.
- **Complications**: "minimal" — one implant loss due to infection in a test site.
- Both arms lose roughly 1.4–1.8 mm of the earlier bone gain between implant placement and one year after loading (test 4.6 → 3.0 mm; control 5.2 → 3.8 mm) — i.e. the numeric difference between arms never materializes.
- No donor-site morbidity data, no implant-survival table and no histomorphometry are reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract only.** No full text was retrievable, so allocation concealment, PRF preparation protocol, defect characterization, sample-size basis, donor-site morbidity outcomes, implant-level survival and funding are unknown here.
- **Very small sample (6 patients, 12 sites).** A non-significant difference in a trial this size cannot support an equivalence or non-inferiority claim — the abstract reports no confidence intervals, no power calculation and no non-inferiority margin.
- **Radiographic endpoint only.** VBH gain is a CBCT measurement; the abstract describes no histomorphometric or mineralized-tissue outcome, so "bone gain" here means height, not bone quality or vital bone fraction.
- **Confounded by a single protocol.** Because both arms used a d-PTFE membrane, the result is conditional on that non-resorbable membrane and cannot be extrapolated to collagen or titanium-mesh GBR.
- **The claimed advantage is not measured.** The stated rationale — avoiding a second surgical site — is not backed by any donor-site morbidity or patient-reported outcome in the abstract; it is asserted as a potential, not demonstrated.
- **Author-stated next step**: future studies with larger sample sizes are needed to confirm these findings and to explore the biologic benefits of integrating the L-PRF bone block into bone graft materials for oral implantology.
- **Citation-date discrepancy**: the abstract citation line reads "2026 May 6" while the ingest frontmatter date is 2026-05-01; both refer to the same 2026 May issue (46(3):360-376).

## 7. Glossary

- **L-PRF**: leukocyte-platelet-rich fibrin — a leukocyte- and platelet-rich autologous platelet concentrate, prepared by centrifugation, here compressed into a bone block.
- **VBH**: vertical bone height — the CBCT-measured vertical gain of the augmented ridge; the primary outcome of this trial.
- **d-PTFE**: dense polytetrafluoroethylene — a non-resorbable barrier membrane used at both test and control sites.
- **AB**: autogenous bone — the patient's own bone; gold-standard graft material but it requires a second surgical site.
- **DBBM**: deproteinized bovine bone mineral — an osteoconductive bovine xenograft.
- **Split-mouth**: each patient contributes both a test and a control site, so the patient is their own control.
- **Composite graft**: here a 1:1 mixture of autogenous bone and DBBM.
