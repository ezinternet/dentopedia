## Why Ingested

Plaque disclosing agents are an everyday chairside and at-home caries-prevention tool, yet the wiki held no evidence on their staining effect on restorative materials — only on plaque-detection efficacy ([[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/sooampon-2026-dc-red-33-erythrosine-plaque-disclosure-rct]]) and on restoration color change from other extrinsic factors ([[tooth-whitening/vidal-2022-resin-composite-color-change-bleaching]]). This in vitro study supplies the missing material-science axis: two-tone solution (Mira-2-Ton) produced clinically unacceptable ΔE00 in every material tested, while the erythrosine gel (ProPind) stayed clinically acceptable.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro bench study of 80 disc-shaped specimens (4 restorative materials: 3 resin composites + 1 giomer, n=20 each, 10 per disclosing agent) assessing immediate color change after a single plaque-disclosing-agent application via CIEDE2000.

Mira-2-Ton two-tone solution caused clinically unacceptable (ΔE00 > 1.8) color change in all four materials, highest on Charisma Diamond (7.18 ± 2.65) and lowest on Z250 (2.98 ± 1.13), whereas the erythrosine-based ProPind gel stayed perceptible-but-acceptable (ΔE00 < 1.8) in every group (1.35–1.63) with no material differences (p>0.05).

Clinicians should favour gel/erythrosine disclosing agents over low-molecular-weight two-tone solutions to protect aesthetic longevity of restorations; the single-application, no-saliva, no-polishing protocol limits extrapolation.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 실험실 연구 — 4종 수복재료(레진 복합체 3종 + 자이오머 1종), 재료당 20개·플라그 착색제당 10개의 원반 시편(n=80)에 단회 착색제를 도포한 직후 CIEDE2000로 색변화 측정.

Mira-2-Ton two-tone 용액은 4종 모두에서 임상적 허용 불가(ΔE00 > 1.8) 색변화를 유발 — Charisma Diamond 최대 7.18 ± 2.65, Z250 최소 2.98 ± 1.13; 반면 에리스로신 기반 ProPind 젤은 전 군에서 인지 가능하나 임상적 허용(ΔE00 < 1.8, 1.35–1.63) 수준이었고 재료 간 차이 유의성 없음(p>0.05).

임상적으로는 저분자량 two-tone 용액보다 젤/에리스로신 제형 착색제를 선택하는 것이 수복물의 심미 수명 유지에 유리하나, 단회 도포·타액 부재·폴리싱 없음 프로토콜이라 일반화에는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First comparison of two plaque-disclosing-agent *delivery forms/chemistries* (two-tone solution vs erythrosine gel) against the color stability of four restorative materials using CIEDE2000 with perceptibility (0.8) and acceptability (1.8) thresholds.
- Demonstrates that the two-tone solution Mira-2-Ton produced clinically unacceptable (ΔE00 > 1.8) discoloration in **all** tested materials, while the erythrosine-based gel ProPind stayed clinically acceptable — i.e. the dye system and delivery form, not the material alone, drove the outcome.
- Attributes material-ranking differences to resin-matrix composition (TCD-DI-HEA in Charisma Diamond; TEGDMA in Beautifil II; hydrophobic Bis-EMA in Z250; Bis-MPEPP in Estelite) and to colorant molecular weight (erythrosine 879.9 g/mol vs CI 45410 785.7 and CI 42090 797.9 g/mol).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro laboratory study (bench).
- **Sample size**: G*Power 3.1.9.4 — effect size 0.45, α 0.05, power 0.80 → 80 disc specimens (8 mm diameter, 2 mm thickness), 20 per material.
- **Materials (all A2 shade)**: Z250 (3M ESPE; micro-hybrid, matrix Bis-GMA/UDMA/Bis-EMA, zirconia-silica filler); Charisma Diamond (Heraeus Kulzer; nanohybrid, matrix TCD-DI-HEA/UDMA, barium aluminum fluoride glass); Estelite Universal Flow Medium (Tokuyama; supra-nano flowable, matrix Bis-GMA/Bis-MPEPP/TEGDMA/UDMA, spherical silica-zirconia); Beautifil II (Shofu; giomer, matrix Bis-GMA/TEGDMA, S-PRG F-Br-Al-Si glass).
- **Specimen prep**: stainless-steel mold, Mylar strip + glass slide, LED-B curing 20 s (radiometer ≥1,200 mW/cm²), Sof-Lex polishing (medium → fine → superfine, 20 s each), stored in distilled water 37°C for 24 h before baseline.
- **Disclosing agents**: Mira-2-Ton (Hager & Werken; aqua, sodium benzoate, potassium sorbate, C.I. 45410, C.I. 42090; fluid) to 10 specimens/material; ProPind gel (Promida; water, stabilizer, glycerin, erythrosine; gel) to the other 10. Both applied 10 s, rinsed under running water 30 s, air-dried.
- **Color measurement**: VITA Easyshade Compact spectrophotometer, 3 readings under standard lighting against a white background, calibrated before each measurement; CIE L*a*b* recorded, ΔE00 computed by CIEDE2000. Thresholds: perceptible > 0.8, unacceptable > 1.8.
- **Statistics**: IBM SPSS v27.0; Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality; One-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc (normal data), Kruskal-Wallis (non-normal), Mann-Whitney U (between agents); p<0.05.
- **Outcomes**: primary — ΔE00 per material per agent; secondary — ΔL*, Δa*, Δb* components.

- All groups showed perceptible change (ΔE00 > 0.8).

- Mann-Whitney U: ΔE00 with Mira-2-Ton significantly higher than with ProPind (p<0.05).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single application of the disclosing agent only; no polishing after application (deliberate, to mimic at-home use) — so post-polishing recovery was not assessed.
- No saliva or artificial saliva immersion during application, omitting the salivary pellicle that facilitates extrinsic staining in vivo; the authors argue short-term saliva exposure has limited effect (citing Uchimura et al.).
- Short-term/immediate measurement only; no surface roughness measurement, though roughness is proposed as a mechanism.
- In vitro design cannot capture the oral environment's glycoprotein–pellicle–stainant interactions; the authors call for extended-duration in vitro and in vivo clinical studies across delivery forms and dyes.

## 7. Glossary
- **ΔE00 (CIEDE2000)**: advanced color-difference metric used to quantify color change; perceptibility threshold 0.8, acceptability threshold 1.8 in this study.
- **CIELAB (L*a*b*)**: three-dimensional color space; L* lightness, a* red-green, b* yellow-blue.
- **Giomer**: composite containing S-PRG (surface pre-reacted glass-ionomer) filler; here Beautifil II.
- **Two-tone disclosing agent**: solution combining two dyes to differentiate old vs new plaque; here Mira-2-Ton (C.I. 45410 phloxine O + C.I. 42090 brilliant blue).
- **Erythrosine**: red disclosing dye (molecular weight 879.9 g/mol); the sole dye in ProPind gel.
- **Mira-2-Ton**: Hager & Werken fluid two-tone disclosing solution tested here.
- **ProPind**: Promida erythrosine-based plaque indicator gel tested here.
- **Perceptibility / Acceptability threshold**: ΔE00 > 0.8 (visually perceptible) / > 1.8 (clinically unacceptable) color change.
