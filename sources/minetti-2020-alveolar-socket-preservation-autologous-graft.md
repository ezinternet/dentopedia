## Why Ingested

Reference mined from [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]], which cites demineralized tooth matrix (autologous dentin graft, ADG) as one of seven bone-substitute categories but relies on secondary summary; this is the primary multicenter human histology data (Minetti's group, one of the original ADG clinical teams) directly comparing whole-tooth vs. endodontically-treated-tooth graft subtypes for socket preservation, giving the Bubalo review's ADG claims a traceable clinical source.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective multicenter pilot study (n=28 patients, 34 post-extractive sockets, 5 Italian dental centers) comparing autologous demineralized dentin/enamel graft (via TT Transformer device) from healthy whole teeth (Group 1, n=19 biopsies) vs. endodontically treated teeth (Group 2, n=13 biopsies) for alveolar socket preservation.

At 4-month histomorphometry, both groups showed comparable new vital bone formation (VB% 20.78% G1 vs 22.89% G2), residual graft (RG% 19.70% G1 vs 17.39% G2), and bone volume (BV% 36.68% G1 vs 39.16% G2), with no statistically significant difference between groups and no inflammation or residual endodontic filling material detected.

Autogenous tooth-derived graft (whether from healthy or root-canal-treated teeth) is biocompatible and produces vital new bone comparable to conventional biomaterials in socket preservation, but this is a small pilot histology study without a control (non-grafted or xenograft-comparator) arm, so efficacy relative to standard bone substitutes remains unquantified.

## 세줄요약

전향적 다기관 파일럿 연구(환자 28명, 발치 후 소켓 34개, 이탈리아 5개 치과센터) — TT Transformer 기기로 제작한 자가 탈회 상아질/법랑질 이식재(autologous demineralized dentin/enamel graft)를 건전 발거치(Group 1, 생검 19개) 대 근관치료된 발거치(Group 2, 생검 13개)에서 치조제보존술(socket preservation)에 사용해 비교.

4개월 후 조직형태계측에서 두 군 모두 유사한 신생 생활골 형성(VB% G1 20.78% vs G2 22.89%), 잔존 이식재(RG% G1 19.70% vs G2 17.39%), 골부피(BV% G1 36.68% vs G2 39.16%)를 보였고, 군간 통계적 유의차 없음, 염증 소견이나 잔존 근관충전재 없음.

자가 치아유래 이식재(건전치·근관치료치 무관)는 생체적합성이 우수하고 기존 골이식재에 준하는 생활골을 형성하지만, 대조군(무이식 또는 이종골 비교군) 없는 소규모 파일럿 조직학 연구라 표준 골이식재 대비 효능은 정량적으로 확인되지 않음.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Alveolar Socket Preservation with Different Autologous Graft Materials: Preliminary Results of a Multicenter Pilot Study in Human
- **Authors**: Minetti E, Giacometti E, Gambardella U, et al.
- **Journal**: Materials (MDPI), 2020, 13(5):1153
- **DOI**: 10.3390/ma13051153 | **PMID**: 32150927 | **PMCID**: PMC7084996
- **Study type**: Prospective multicenter pilot study (histological/histomorphometric)
- **Setting**: Five dental centers in Italy, Feb–Oct 2018; ethics approval University of Chieti (protocol N°1869, 12/12/2018)

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the earliest multicenter human histology datasets directly comparing graft material derived from **whole (vital) teeth** vs. **endodontically treated (non-vital) teeth** processed with the same automated demineralization device (TT Transformer).
- Demonstrates that prior endodontic treatment does not appear to compromise the tooth's suitability as an autologous graft source — both groups produced comparable vital bone with no residual gutta-percha/cement/composite detected histologically.
- Supports the broader premise (echoed in [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]]) that extracted teeth — including previously "waste" endodontically treated teeth — are a viable, no-cost autologous graft source with hydroxyapatite/type-1-collagen composition similar to bone.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Sample**: 28 patients (10 M / 18 F, mean age 51.79 ± 5.97 y), 34 post-extractive sockets, five Italian dental centers, Feb–Oct 2018.
- **Groups**: Group 1 (G1) = healthy whole teeth (n=20 extracted, 19 biopsied); Group 2 (G2) = teeth extracted after endodontic root canal treatment (n=14 extracted, 13 biopsied).
- **Processing**: extracted teeth cleaned with diamond drill; G2 teeth had filling materials (gutta-percha, composite, etc.) removed first; both groups cut into 5-mm samples and processed in the TT grinder device (TT Tooth Transformer srl, Milan) for automated single-use demineralization (25 min).
- **Graft placement**: particulate demineralized dentin/enamel graft placed in socket, covered with a resorbable porcine-pericardium collagen membrane (Bego oss®).
- **Follow-up**: second-stage implant-site bone biopsy via trephine drill at 4 months.
- **Histology**: non-decalcified ground sections (~40–50 μm), basic fuchsin/toluidine blue staining, light/polarized microscopy; histomorphometry (IAS 2000 software) for vital bone % (VB), residual graft % (Graft/RG), and bone volume % (BV).
- **Inclusion/exclusion**: adults ≥18y, ASA-1/ASA-2, non-smokers (≥6 months), no diabetes/cancer/HIV/bone-metabolic disease/immunosuppression/bisphosphonates/radio-chemotherapy.
- **Stats**: paired-sample t-test (pre–post), SPSS v11.5.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 32 of 34 grafted sites biopsied at 4 months (19 G1, 13 G2); no complications, no infection signs during healing.
- **G1 (whole teeth)**: BV 36.68% (±8.90%), residual graft (RG) 19.70% (±13.75%), vital bone (VB) 20.78% (±13.29%).
- **G2 (endodontically treated teeth)**: BV 39.16% (±11.51%), RG 17.39% (±7.09%), VB 22.89% (±9.72%).
- No statistically significant difference between G1 and G2 on any histomorphometric parameter.
- Histology: partially resorbed dentin/enamel graft particles surrounded by new bone layers in all specimens; no inflammatory or infective reaction; no residual gutta-percha, composite, or cement detected in G2 samples.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No non-grafted control or xenograft/allograft comparator arm — cannot quantify efficacy advantage over standard biomaterials, only intra-group (whole vs. RCT tooth) equivalence.
- Small sample (28 patients, 32 biopsies), single time point (4 months), pilot/preliminary study design as stated by authors.
- Group sizes unequal (19 vs 13 biopsies) and imbalanced by center; no long-term (post-implant-loading) outcome data.
- Authors call for further studies to clarify comparative advantages of tooth-derived autologous graft vs. current biomaterials.

## 6. Related Work

- Cited (indirectly, via later reviews) as foundational human clinical/histologic evidence for autologous dentin graft (ADG) in [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]], which frames demineralized tooth matrix as one of seven bone-substitute categories for alveolar ridge augmentation.
- Builds on earlier animal/mechanistic work establishing demineralized dentin matrix osteoinduction (Bang 1967; Bessho 1991) and chairside autologous dentin matrix protocols (Kim 2017; Minamizato 2018) cited in the paper's own introduction.

## 7. Glossary

- **TT Transformer**: automated chairside medical device (TT Tooth Transformer srl, Milan) that cleans, grinds, and demineralizes extracted teeth into a particulate graft material.
- **ADG (Autologous Dentin Graft)**: graft material derived from a patient's own extracted tooth (dentin ± enamel), demineralized to expose bone morphogenetic proteins (BMPs) and collagen.
- **VB% (Vital Bone percentage)**: histomorphometric measure of newly formed living bone within the biopsy specimen.
- **RG% / Graft% (Residual Graft percentage)**: histomorphometric measure of remaining (not-yet-resorbed) graft particles.
- **BV% (Bone Volume percentage)**: histomorphometric measure of total mineralized bone volume in the specimen.
- **Socket preservation**: surgical grafting of a post-extraction socket to limit the natural three-dimensional (vertical/horizontal) alveolar bone resorption that follows tooth loss.
