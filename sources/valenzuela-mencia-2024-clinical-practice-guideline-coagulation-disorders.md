## Why Ingested

Adds a national oral-surgery society guideline (SECIB, Spain) to a category dominated by systematic reviews: it gives PICO-level, graded recommendations for extractions and implants in VKA, heparin, DOAC and hereditary-coagulopathy patients, complementing [[drug/anticoagulants/moldovan-2023-anticoagulant-oral-surgery-bleeding-sr]] and [[drug/anticoagulants/madrid-2009-anticoagulants-oral-implant-therapy-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Clinical practice guideline (Spanish Society of Oral Surgery, SECIB; Med Oral Patol Oral Cir Bucal 2024) answering 15 PICO questions on extractions and implants in patients with coumarin, heparin, DOAC or hereditary coagulation disorders, using literature from December 2011 to December 2021.

Two PICO questions received a grade C recommendation and the rest grade D (mostly expert opinion, limited evidence without control groups); the main recommendations are to continue the oral anticoagulant for simple extractions and implants with local hemostatic measures, with post-extraction bleeding risk appearing similar to healthy patients at INR up to 2.2.

Evidence is weak throughout (few acenocoumarol and DOAC studies, mostly retrospective); INR above 3 or multiple extractions and hereditary coagulopathies need physician or haematologist input, and the authors call for controlled trials with representative samples.

## 세줄요약

스페인 구강외과학회(SECIB) 임상진료지침(Clinical Practice Guideline, CPG, 2024): 쿠마린·헤파린·직접작용 경구 항응고제(Direct Oral Anticoagulant, DOAC)·유전성 응고장애 환자의 발치·임플란트에 대한 15개 PICO 질문, 2011.12–2021.12 문헌 기반.

15개 중 2개는 권고등급 C, 나머지는 등급 D(대부분 전문가 의견); 단순 발치·임플란트에서는 항응고제 유지와 국소 지혈 병행이 핵심이며, 쿠마린 환자의 발치 후 출혈 위험은 INR 2.2 이하에서 건강한 환자와 유사해 보인다고 기술.

근거 수준이 전반적으로 낮고(아세노쿠마롤·DOAC 연구 부족, 후향 연구 위주) INR 3 초과·다수 발치·유전성 응고장애는 주치의·혈액내과 협진이 필요하며, 저자들은 대조군을 둔 대규모 시험을 요구.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 15 PICO questions across coumarin (acenocoumarol/warfarin), heparin, DOAC and hereditary coagulopathies, for extractions and implants.
- Grades exactly as stated: Grade C for PICO 1 (INR >3 or several extractions: contact physician) and PICO 8 (do not stop anticoagulant in simple extractions; use local hemostatic measures); all others Grade D.
- Specific points: no INR-related excess risk up to 2.2; DOAC extraction at plasma trough with dose delayed at least 4 hours afterwards (expert opinion, Grade D); no pharmacological hemostatic adjunct needed for coumarin or heparin patients (Grade D).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Method follows the Spanish NHS CPG methodological manual (GuiaSalud); MEDLINE (PubMed) and EMBASE; English, French, Spanish; December 2011 to December 2021.
- Appraisal tools: SIGN checklists, OSTEBA sheets for case series, AGREE for guidelines; recommendation grades A-D (SIGN / Oxford CEBM).
- Panel included oral surgeons, haematologists and oral medicine experts; methodological support from a methodology expert and a health librarian.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Coumarin + extraction: risk similar to healthy when INR does not exceed 2.2; INR >3 or several extractions -> contact the physician (Grade C).
- Heparin bridging appears to raise bleeding versus continuing the anticoagulant; local measures should suffice (Grade D).
- DOAC + extraction: increased bleeding versus healthy individuals (Grade D); DOAC + implants: can be treated safely, intraoperative bleeding possibly slightly greater, postoperative similar (Grade D).
- Implants on coumarin or heparin: continuation does not significantly increase haemorrhage; suspension or heparin substitution not justified (Grade D).
- Local measures with most evidence: sutures, collagen or cellulose sponges, gauze soaked in tranexamic acid (Grade C); older patients warrant local measures (Grade C).
- Hereditary coagulopathies: insufficient evidence; implants may be placed (risk appears lower than extractions); individual evaluation with haematology (Grade D).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Evidence limited by lack of control groups, small samples and mostly retrospective DOAC studies; few acenocoumarol studies although it is the most used coumarin in Spain.
- Most recommendations are expert opinion (Grade D); hereditary-coagulopathy evidence is case series only.
- The published text lists the questions as 1-12, 14 and 15 (no item 13) while stating 15 PICO questions; the PubMed title (coagulation disorders) also differs from the title in the ingest briefing (anticoagulant/antiplatelet treatment); the text as retrieved covers coumarins, heparin and DOACs and does not present a separate antiplatelet section.
- Future work: RCTs with control groups, INR 2-4 populations, patients on combined anticoagulant plus antiplatelet therapy, and larger DOAC cohorts.
