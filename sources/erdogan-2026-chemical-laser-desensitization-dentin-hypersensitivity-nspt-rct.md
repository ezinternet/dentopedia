## Three-line Summary

Three-arm RCT (52 randomized, 48 analyzed, Stage III/IV periodontitis) comparing Gluma, Er,Cr:YSGG laser, and Gluma+laser for dentin hypersensitivity after non-surgical periodontal therapy (NSPT), with VAS and Schiff scale to 6 months.

All three arms significantly reduced VAS and Schiff scores (P<0.05), with the largest gain by month 2 and slight recurrence at 6 months; no significant intergroup difference at any time point (P>0.05).

Chemical and laser desensitization are comparable for post-NSPT DH, and combining them adds no measurable benefit; patients should be warned of symptom recurrence and need for maintenance or retreatment (abstract-only; n=16/group is small).

## 세줄요약

3군 RCT(무작위 배정 52명·분석 48명, Stage III/IV 치주염)로 비수술 치주치료(NSPT) 후 상아질 과민증에 대해 Gluma, Er,Cr:YSGG 레이저, 병용을 비교하고 VAS·Schiff를 6개월까지 평가.

세 군 모두 VAS·Schiff가 유의하게 감소(P<0.05), 2개월까지 개선이 가장 크고 6개월에 약간 재발; 모든 시점에서 군간 유의 차이 없음(P>0.05).

NSPT 후 DH에서 화학제와 레이저는 동등하고 병용의 추가 이득도 없음; 재발 가능성과 유지·재치료 필요성을 환자에게 설명해야 함(초록 기반, 군당 n=16으로 소규모).

## 1. Document Information

Erdoğan G, Açıkgöz-Alparslan E, Büyükgöze-Dindar M. Am J Dent 2026 Oct;39(5):226-232. PMID 42881521. DOI not available from PubMed response (doi: null). Trakya University, Edirne, Turkey. Abstract-only.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Tests desensitizers in a specific, common clinical setting: DH arising after NSPT in Stage III/IV periodontitis.
- Three-arm design isolates the combination effect (Gluma + Er,Cr:YSGG) against each single modality.
- Follows patients to 6 months and reports recurrence, not just early response.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

62 screened; 52 with VAS >4 and Schiff 2-3 randomized to Gluma (n=18), Er,Cr:YSGG laser (n=17), or combination (n=17). Assessments at baseline, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 2 months, 6 months. Four dropouts; 48 analyzed (16 per group). α=0.05. Laser parameters, Gluma protocol, and statistical tests are not given in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

All modalities significantly reduced VAS and Schiff over time (P<0.05). DH was highest right after NSPT and fell progressively, most markedly through month 2. Slight recurrence at 6 months but still below baseline. No significant intergroup difference at any evaluation for either outcome (P>0.05).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract-only: effect sizes, laser settings, allocation concealment, blinding, and placebo/negative control are not verifiable. No untreated control, so natural resolution of post-NSPT DH cannot be separated from treatment effect (my reading, not stated in the abstract). Small groups (16 each) limit power to detect between-group differences, so "no difference" may reflect underpowering. Follow-up ends at 6 months with recurrence already visible.

## 7. Glossary

- NSPT: non-surgical periodontal therapy (scaling and root planing).
- Schiff Cold Air Sensitivity Scale: examiner-rated 0–3 response to air blast.
- Gluma: glutaraldehyde + HEMA desensitizer.
- Er,Cr:YSGG: erbium, chromium-doped yttrium-scandium-gallium-garnet laser.
