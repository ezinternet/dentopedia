## Why Ingested

[[periodontics/chu-2009-gingival-zenith-positions-levels-maxillary-anterior]]의 측절치 GZL 기준과 같은 축이다. PMC 전문이 있으나 표 본문이 누락되어 있고 수치가 내부적으로 불일치한다.

## Three-line Summary

Clinical cast study of 35 young adults (21–30 y) at one Indian dental college: face-bow–mounted maxillary casts oriented to the axis-orbital plane; gingival line angle (GLA, gingival line vs maxillary midline) measured with a flexible protractor and lateral-incisor-to-gingival-line distance (LID) with a digital caliper.

Left GLA 86.7° ± 4.2° was significantly greater than right 84.6° ± 5.4° (mean absolute asymmetry 1.7° ± 4.4°), i.e. canine zenith apical to central incisor zenith (GLA <90°) with the right side higher; mean LID 0.92 (± 0.11, unit not legible); lateral zenith position relative to the gingival line reported inconsistently (abstract "below or on (17%)" vs discussion "frequently below (75.7%)").

Supports a canine-apical gingival line and a small directional asymmetry as natural-esthetic norms, but internal numeric inconsistencies, missing tables, and single-operator measurement limit citation.

## 세줄요약

임상 모형 연구: 인도 단일 치과대학 젊은 성인 35명(21–30세), 안궁으로 안와축 평면 기준 교합기 장착한 상악 모형에서 치은선 각도 (Gingival Line Angle, GLA; 치은선–상악 정중선)와 측절치 정점–치은선 거리 (LID) 측정.

좌측 GLA 86.7° ± 4.2° > 우측 84.6° ± 5.4° (유의), 평균 절대 비대칭 1.7° ± 4.4° — 견치 정점이 중절치 정점보다 근단 (GLA <90°), 우측이 더 높음; LID 평균 0.92 (± 0.11, 단위 판독 불가); 측절치 정점 위치는 초록 "아래 또는 선상 17%" vs 고찰 "대부분 아래 75.7%"로 불일치.

견치 근단 치은선과 미세한 방향성 비대칭을 자연 심미 기준으로 제시하나, 수치 불일치·표 누락·단일 측정자로 인용 시 주의.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Indian Society of Periodontology, 2011;15(2):135-138
- **Study type**: Cross-sectional cast measurement study
- **PMID / PMCID**: 21976837 / PMC3183664
- **Artifact**: PMC full text (Tables 1–2 bodies not present in retrieved text)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Measures GLA on face-bow–oriented casts (avoids 2D photographic flattening, per authors).
- Reports a directional left–right asymmetry (right side higher).
- Defines LID sign convention: positive = lateral zenith coronal to gingival line.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 35 young adults, 21–30 y, no dental deformity or prior periodontal/surgical/orthodontic treatment of maxillary anteriors.
- Alginate impressions, stone casts, face-bow transfer, Whipmix 2200 semi-adjustable articulator.
- Zeniths marked with pencil; midline and gingival line drawn; GLA traced with protractor onto transparent sheet; LID by digital vernier caliper.
- Single operator for impressions and measurements.
- ANOVA for gender/handedness; paired t test for left vs right.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- GLA left 86.7° ± 4.2°, right 84.6° ± 5.4° (left significantly greater).
- Mean absolute GLA asymmetry 1.7° ± 4.4° (discussion: 1.68°, "2%").
- LID mean 0.92 ± 0.11 (source character garbled; unit presumably mm).
- No gender differences in GLA or LID (discussion).
- Lateral zenith vs gingival line: abstract "below or on (17%)"; discussion "frequently below the gingival line (75.7%)".

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Internal inconsistency**: the 17% vs 75.7% statements cannot both describe the same proportion; Table 2 (which would resolve it) is absent from the retrieved text. With the LID sign convention (positive = coronal) and a positive mean LID of 0.92, "frequently below" is also hard to reconcile.
- **Method wording**: abstract says casts were "photographed"; methods say photography was avoided.
- Small (n=35), young, single-centre sample; single operator, no reliability statistics.
- "Randomly selected" not described.

## 6. Related Work

- Chu 2009 (GZP/GZL reference); Martins 2024 SR+MA (symmetry); Kolte 2019 (gingival zenith angle by age/sex).

## 7. Glossary

- **GLA**: angle between the gingival line (central–canine zenith tangent) and the maxillary midline.
- **LID**: distance from lateral-incisor zenith to the gingival line.
- **Axis-orbital plane**: reference plane used to orient casts via face-bow.
