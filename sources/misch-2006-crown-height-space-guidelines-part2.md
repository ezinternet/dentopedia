## Why Ingested

Crown-height space (CHS) decides how much restorative room remains for a single-implant crown, and the wiki's emergence-angle and marginal-bone-loss pages ([[implants/soft-tissue/misch-2025-abutment-height-emergence-angle-peri-implant-bone-loss]]) assume an abutment-height framework that this ICOI panel helped define. Part 2 (Misch 2006) extends the Part 1 guidelines, so both parts are ingested together.

## Three-line Summary

Consensus conference panel report (ICOI, Las Vegas, Jun 2004; multi-author expert panel; no formal consensus on most issues) — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

Part 2 summarizes the general guidelines that emerged on crown-height space for implant dentistry, noting that a single opinion was not reached on most issues.

Because the panel explicitly did not reach consensus on most items, cite the guidelines as expert opinion, not as settled evidence; the abstract gives no numeric thresholds.
