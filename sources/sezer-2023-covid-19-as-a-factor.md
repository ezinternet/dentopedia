## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-institution study (Erciyes University Faculty of Dentistry) of 1228 patients who received 4841 implants between March 11, 2020 and April 01, 2022, testing whether COVID-19 and patient- and implant-related factors were associated with early implant failure (univariate and multivariate GEE logistic regression at the implant level).

Early implant failure occurred in 3.1% of implants and 10.4% of patients; smokers had higher odds than nonsmokers (OR 2.140, 95% CI 1.438-3.184, p < 0.001) and short implants (8 mm or less) had higher odds than long implants (12 mm or more) (OR 2.089, 95% CI 1.290-3.382, p = 0.003).

COVID-19 had no significant effect on early implant failure; the evidence is retrospective and based on the abstract only, so the early-failure time window, other covariate estimates and COVID-19 effect size are not verifiable here.

조기 임플란트 실패율은 임플란트 기준 3.1%, 환자 기준 10.4%; 흡연자가 비흡연자보다 높은 오즈 (OR 2.140, 95% CI 1.438-3.184, p < 0.001), 짧은 임플란트 (8 mm 이하)가 긴 임플란트 (12 mm 이상)보다 높은 오즈 (OR 2.089, 95% CI 1.290-3.382, p = 0.003).

COVID-19는 조기 임플란트 실패에 유의한 영향이 없었다; 후향적 연구이며 초록 기반이므로 조기 실패의 기간 정의, 그 외 공변량 추정치, COVID-19 효과 크기는 확인할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Large single-institution cohort (1228 patients, 4841 implants) placed during the COVID-19 period (March 11, 2020 - April 01, 2022).
- Reports early implant failure at both levels: 3.1% implant-level, 10.4% patient-level.
- Smoking (OR 2.140, 95% CI 1.438-3.184, p < 0.001) and short implants of 8 mm or less versus 12 mm or more (OR 2.089, 95% CI 1.290-3.382, p = 0.003) were associated with higher early failure risk.
- COVID-19 had no significant effect on early implant failure.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective study; Erciyes University Faculty of Dentistry (country not stated in the abstract).
- **Population**: 1228 patients, 4841 implants.
- **Variables**: COVID-19, age, gender, smoking, diabetes, irradiation, chemotherapy, osteoporosis, implant system, location, implant characteristics.
- **Statistics**: univariate and multivariate generalized estimating equation (GEE) logistic regression at the implant level.
- **Early failure definition**: not stated in the abstract; time window not verifiable.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only: early-failure time window, number of failures, COVID-19 effect size, and estimates for other covariates are not available.
- Retrospective single-institution design; confounding and period effects are possible.
- Country, COVID-19 status ascertainment and number of COVID-19 patients are not given in the abstract.

## 7. Glossary

- **Early implant failure (조기 임플란트 실패)**: failure before prosthetic loading; exact window not given in the abstract.
- **GEE (Generalized Estimating Equation, 일반화추정방정식)**: regression handling clustered implants within patients.
- **OR (Odds Ratio, 오즈비)**: relative odds of the outcome.
