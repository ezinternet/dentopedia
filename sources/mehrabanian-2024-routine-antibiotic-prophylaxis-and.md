## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 7 RCTs (1,859 participants, 3,014 implants) evaluating whether antibiotic prophylaxis reduces early dental implant failure in systemically healthy patients, using GRADE assessment and Cochrane Risk of Bias (RoB-2).

Meta-analysis found no statistically significant reduction in early implant failure with antibiotic prophylaxis (RR 0.66, 95% CI 0.30–1.47); number needed to treat (NNT) = 143, indicating minimal clinical benefit in healthy patients.

The study concludes routine antibiotic prophylaxis should be avoided in medically fit implant patients given very limited benefit and antimicrobial resistance concerns.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Establishes NNT=143 — requires treating 143 healthy patients to prevent 1 early implant failure
- Confirms RR 0.66 (95% CI 0.30–1.47) — not statistically significant
- Reinforces antimicrobial stewardship: routine use not justified in medically fit patients
- GRADE framework: low-quality evidence for most outcomes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Databases: PubMed (MEDLINE), Cochrane Library, Web of Science; search to October 1, 2023
- Inclusion: RCTs comparing antibiotic prophylaxis vs no antibiotics/placebo in systemically healthy implant patients
- Exclusion: medically compromised patients, mini-implants, immediate placement in infected sites, high-ROB studies
- 1,267 initial records → 7 RCTs included
- Quality: 4 high quality, 3 moderate quality by RoB-2
- Random-effects model, I² heterogeneity

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Early implant failure (RR) | 0.66 (95% CI 0.30–1.47), p=NS |
| NNT | 143 implants |
| Evidence quality (GRADE) | Low |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Same 7-RCT dataset as momand-2024 — not independent confirmation
- Short follow-up (early failure only; no long-term outcomes)
- Excludes compromised patients — cannot generalize to immunosuppressed or diabetic patients
- Low GRADE quality limits confidence in estimates

## 6. Related Work and Context
- Same dataset as momand-2024: parallel confirmation of NNT=143 and non-significance
- Contrasts with esposito-2026 Cochrane (RR 0.34, NNT=19) — different inclusion criteria explain divergence

## 7. Glossary
- **Early implant failure**: Loss before or just after prosthetic loading
- **NNT (Number Needed to Treat)**: 143 = marginal clinical relevance
- **RR (Relative Risk)**: 0.66 = 34% relative reduction, not statistically significant here

## Why Ingested

Provides NNT quantification (143) for antibiotic prophylaxis at implant placement in healthy patients — directly addresses stewardship decision; companion to momand-2024 with same dataset, reinforcing the evidence base.
