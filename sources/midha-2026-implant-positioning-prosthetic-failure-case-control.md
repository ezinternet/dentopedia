## Why Ingested

No held paper provided CBCT-measured implant positioning parameters (angulation, A-P spread, cantilever-to-A-P ratio, inter-implant distance) directly correlated with prosthetic failure in a matched case-control design. This fills the biomechanical placement parameter gap with quantified thresholds: failed cases showed 14.2mm cantilever vs 10.4mm controls, and C/AP ratio 1.42 vs 0.89.

[[implants/full-arch/midha-2026-implant-positioning-prosthetic-failure-case-control]]
## Three-line Summary

Retrospective matched case-control study (30 failure vs 60 controls), CBCT assessment of mesiodistal angulation, buccolingual angulation, vertical position, A-P spread, cantilever length, C/AP ratio, inter-implant distance in full-arch fixed implant-supported prostheses.

Failure cases: mesiodistal angulation 18.4° vs 12.1°; buccolingual 15.7° vs 10.3°; cantilever 14.2 vs 10.4mm; C/AP ratio 1.42 vs 0.89 (all p<0.001); greater A-P spread and inter-implant distance protective; most common complications: screw loosening 43.3%, framework fracture 36.7%, veneering fracture 20%.

Implant angulation and cantilever extension are the strongest positioning predictors of prosthetic failure; greater A-P spread and inter-implant distance are protective — prosthetically driven placement is the key prevention strategy.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantified implant positioning thresholds associated with prosthetic failure via CBCT
- C/AP ratio threshold: 1.42 (failure) vs 0.89 (control) — guideline approximately <1.0
- Cantilever length threshold: 14.2mm (failure) vs 10.4mm (control) — guideline approximately <11mm
- Mesiodistal angulation: 18.4° vs 12.1° — <15° appears protective
- Complication spectrum: screw loosening 43.3%, framework fracture 36.7%, veneering fracture 20%
