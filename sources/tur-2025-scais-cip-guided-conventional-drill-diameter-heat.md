## Why Ingested

The most methodologically rigorous in vitro comparison of S-CAIS vs conventional implant preparation thermal output: CNC-automated apparatus eliminates operator variability, allowing isolation of technique-inherent thermal differences by drill diameter and depth. The key finding — that S-CAIS is NOT globally hotter than CIP and that the critical risk scenario is limited to the 3.5mm large-diameter drill at 12mm depth with external irrigation — refines the clinical safety message for guided surgery and feeds directly into the `implants/osteotomy-thermal` synthesis alongside [[wiki/implants/osteotomy-thermal/gokce-uckun-2025-guided-surgery-cold-irrigation-temperature]].

## Three-line Summary

Standardized in vitro study (Medical University of Vienna; 240 preparations, CNC-automated intermittent protocol) comparing static computer-assisted implant surgery (S-CAIS) vs conventional implant preparation (CIP) thermal output across three stainless steel drill diameters (2.2, 2.8, 3.5 mm), two depths (10, 12 mm), and two irrigation conditions using composite BoneSim bone blocks with thermocouple arrays at 1 mm and 2 mm from the osteotomy at multiple depths.

Significant technique differences emerged only with the 3.5 mm drill: S-CAIS generated significantly lower ΔT than CIP without irrigation at 10 mm depth (15.05 vs 17.36°C, p=0.0115), but significantly higher ΔT than CIP with external irrigation at 12 mm depth (9.14 vs 6.00°C, p=0.0101); smaller diameters (2.2, 2.8 mm) showed no significant S-CAIS vs CIP difference.

External irrigation universally prevented 47°C threshold exceedance regardless of technique; S-CAIS is not thermally inferior to CIP overall, but its closed-guide geometry impairs deep-cooling efficiency specifically when using large-diameter drills at extended depths.

## 세줄요약

빈의과대학교 표준화 체외 연구(총 240회 골절개, CNC 자동화 간헐적 프로토콜) — 정적 컴퓨터보조 임플란트 수술 (Static Computer-Assisted Implant Surgery, S-CAIS) vs 기존 임플란트 준비 (Conventional Implant Preparation, CIP) 열 발생 비교; 스테인리스강 드릴 직경 3종(2.2·2.8·3.5 mm), 깊이 2종(10·12 mm), 관개 유무, 복합 BoneSim 골블록 + 다층 열전대 배열.

유의한 술식 차이는 3.5 mm 드릴에서만 발생: 무관개 10 mm 깊이에서 S-CAIS가 CIP보다 유의하게 낮은 ΔT(15.05 vs 17.36°C, p=0.0115), 외부 관개 12 mm 깊이에서는 S-CAIS가 유의하게 더 높은 ΔT(9.14 vs 6.00°C, p=0.0101); 소직경(2.2·2.8 mm)은 두 술식 간 유의한 차이 없음.

외부 관개는 술식에 관계없이 47°C 골괴사 임계값 초과를 완전히 예방하며; S-CAIS가 전반적으로 열적으로 불리하지 않으나, 대직경 드릴을 심부까지 사용할 때 폐쇄형 가이드 기하구조가 냉각 효율을 저해하는 특정 위험 시나리오가 존재함.

## Summary

Standardized in vitro study (Medical University of Vienna) comparing static computer-assisted implant surgery (S-CAIS) vs conventional implant preparation (CIP) in automated, intermittent protocol. Standardized bovine bone samples (BoneSim composite, type-2 bone density, 3mm cortical + 15mm cancellous), rectangular shape 56×18×10mm. CNC material testing apparatus (Lloyd LS1) for reproducible drilling. Stainless steel twist drills (Straumann PRO: 2.2, 2.8, 3.5mm). Multiple type-K thermocouple sensor arrays at 1mm and 2mm from osteotomy, depths 3/6/9/12/15mm. 240 total preparations: 10 repetitions per constellation.

Variables: 3 drill diameters × 2 depths (10, 12mm) × 2 irrigation modes (with/without external) × 2 techniques (S-CAIS vs CIP).

Key temperature findings (ΔT°C, 10mm drilling, without irrigation):
- 2.2mm: S-CAIS 13.06 vs CIP 13.62 (NS)
- 2.8mm: S-CAIS 16.70 vs CIP 16.54 (NS)
- 3.5mm: **S-CAIS 15.05 vs CIP 17.36** (p=0.0115 — CIP significantly higher)

12mm drilling, with external irrigation:
- 3.5mm: **S-CAIS 9.14 vs CIP 6.00** (p=0.0101 — S-CAIS significantly higher)

Overall pattern:
- Significant differences only with 3.5mm drills; smaller diameters (2.2, 2.8mm): S-CAIS and CIP statistically equivalent
- External irrigation dramatically reduces all temperatures regardless of technique
- Deeper drilling (12mm > 10mm) → higher temperatures invariably
- Maximum temperature depth shifts deeper with irrigation (cooling of superficial layers)

Critical: All temperatures exceeded 47°C threshold only WITHOUT irrigation; with external irrigation, all preparations safely below threshold.

Conclusion: S-CAIS is NOT globally hotter than CIP — it depends on drill diameter, depth, and irrigation. The primary concern is the 3.5mm large-diameter drill in S-CAIS at 12mm depth with external irrigation where guided geometry impairs cooling efficiency.

Limitations: in vitro only; room temperature conditions, no physiological vascularity; closed-guide only; only 10mm and 12mm depths tested.
