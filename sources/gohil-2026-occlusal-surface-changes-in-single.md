## Why Ingested

Retrospective clinical comparison of occlusal wear and antagonist enamel wear in single-unit versus three-unit implant-supported zirconia restorations (임플란트 지지 지르코니아 보철물, ISZRs) using digital scan superimposition over 12 months. Provides in-vivo wear data for both restoration types — a clinical anchor for the dental-materials/zirconia wear literature.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/gohil-2026-occlusal-surface-changes-in-single]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective observational study (Cureus 2026; n=120: 64 single-ISZRs, 56 three-ISZRs; posterior sites; 12-month follow-up) quantifying occlusal surface wear and antagonist enamel wear via 3D digital scan superimposition (Geomagic Control X).

At 12 months, single-ISZRs showed less occlusal wear (14.6 ± 3.4 vs 19.8 ± 4.1 μm; p<0.001) and less antagonist enamel wear (15.2 ± 3.8 vs 19.7 ± 4.5 μm; p<0.001); overall complication rate was also lower (9.4% vs 25.0%; p=0.022).

Restoration configuration (single vs three-unit) is significantly associated with wear magnitude and complication rate; findings support careful occlusal adjustment and regular maintenance especially for multi-unit ISZRs.

## 세줄요약

후향적 관찰연구 (Cureus 2026; n=120: 단관 지르코니아 임플란트 보철물 (single-ISZR) 64개, 3본 지르코니아 임플란트 보철물 (three-ISZR) 56개; 구치부; 12개월 추적) — 3D 디지털 스캔 중첩(Geomagic Control X)으로 교합면 마모 및 대합치 에나멜 마모 정량화.

12개월 시점 교합면 마모 (Occlusal Wear): 단관 14.6 ± 3.4 μm vs 3본 19.8 ± 4.1 μm (p<0.001); 대합치 에나멜 마모 (Antagonist Enamel Wear): 15.2 ± 3.8 vs 19.7 ± 4.5 μm (p<0.001); 총 합병증률: 9.4% vs 25.0% (p=0.022).

보철물 구성(단관 vs 3본)은 마모량과 합병증률 모두에 유의한 영향을 미치며, 특히 3본 지르코니아 임플란트 보철물에서 교합 조정·표면 마무리·정기 유지관리가 중요함.

## Key Contributions
- First clinical study directly comparing occlusal wear magnitude between single-unit vs three-unit ISZRs using 3D scan superimposition
- Demonstrates that restoration configuration significantly influences in-vivo wear trajectory
- Provides reference wear values (μm) for single and three-unit zirconia over 12 months
- Confirms higher complication burden for three-unit ISZRs in a real-world posterior population

## Methodology
- **Design**: Retrospective observational cohort (record review)
- **Sample**: 120 records (64 single-ISZR, 56 three-ISZR) from posterior premolar/molar sites
- **Implant system**: NobelActive (Nobel Biocare); restorations via Nobel Procera (full-contour zirconia)
- **CAD/CAM**: 3Shape Dental System 2019; sintered full-contour monolithic zirconia
- **Digital scanning**: 3Shape TRIOS 3 intraoral scanner; full-arch STL datasets
- **Superimposition software**: Geomagic Control X 2018.0
- **Outcome**: vertical occlusal surface height loss (μm); antagonist enamel wear; complications
- **Follow-up**: baseline (delivery), 6 months, 12 months
- **Statistics**: independent-samples t-test (Welch's correction), Pearson's chi-square / Fisher's exact; Cohen's d effect sizes
- **Reliability**: intra-examiner ICC 0.94; inter-examiner ICC 0.92

## Key Results
- **Baseline wear**: single 2.1 ± 0.8 vs three 2.3 ± 0.9 μm (p=0.204, NS)
- **6-month wear**: 8.4 ± 2.1 vs 11.2 ± 2.8 μm (p<0.001; d=-1.14)
- **12-month wear**: 14.6 ± 3.4 vs 19.8 ± 4.1 μm (p<0.001; d=-1.39)
- **Δ baseline→12 months**: 12.5 ± 3.2 vs 17.5 ± 3.8 μm (p<0.001; d=-1.43)
- **Antagonist enamel wear at 12 months**: 15.2 ± 3.8 vs 19.7 ± 4.5 μm (p<0.001; d=-1.09)
- **Occlusal adjustment need**: higher in three-ISZR at all time points (NS individually)
- **Surface roughness** (Rough/degradation): 18.8% (single) vs 32.1% (three) (p=0.091, NS)
- **Chipping**: 4.7% vs 12.5% (p=0.186); **fracture**: 1.6% vs 5.4% (p=0.338); **debonding**: 3.1% vs 7.1% (p=0.416)
- **Overall complication rate**: 9.4% vs 25.0% (p=0.022; φ=0.21)

## Limitations
- Retrospective design; cannot establish causality
- 12-month follow-up only; long-term wear trajectory unknown
- Single center; modest sample size; generalizability limited
- Could not control occlusal force, chewing behavior, surface-finishing details retrospectively
- No formal repeated-measures / group × time interaction analysis
- Restoration configuration compared, not different zirconia materials
