## Why Ingested

투명교정(CAT)을 이용한 상악 대구치 원심이동(molar distalization)은 [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/bhate-2025-cat-maxillary-molar-distalization-class-ii-sr]]가 보여주듯 anchorage loss(고정원 손실) 위험이 내재된 술식인데, 이 논문은 그 부작용 중 하나인 상악 전치부 개방형 치은유두 공간(Open Gingival Embrasure, OGE — 일명 black triangle)의 발생률(17.3%)과 술전 예측인자(나이, ANB angle, U1-NA angle, anteroposterior overlap)를 처음으로 이 특정 임상 시나리오에 한정해 규명한 후향적 연구. 술전 리스크 스크리닝에 바로 쓸 수 있는 회귀 모델(AUC 0.839)을 제공해 임상적 gap을 메운다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center cohort (n=75 of an initial 630-patient pool) of permanent-dentition patients undergoing sequential bilateral maxillary molar distalization (2-4 mm/side) with Invisalign clear aligners, evaluated for post-treatment open gingival embrasures (OGE) of the maxillary central incisors via the Jemt papilla index.

OGE incidence was 17.3% (13/75); multivariate logistic regression identified age (OR 1.17, 95% CI 1.07-1.30), anteroposterior overlap (OR 2.41, 95% CI 1.05-5.54), ANB angle (OR 2.31, 95% CI 1.08-4.94), and U1-NA angle (OR 1.39, 95% CI 1.03-1.89) as independent pre-treatment predictors, with the combined model showing good discrimination (ROC AUC 0.839, 95% CI 0.72-0.96).

Gingival biotype and amount of anterior tooth retraction were NOT significant predictors (contrary to hypothesis); small sample (n=75, only 13 OGE events) limits EPV robustness and the model needs external multicenter validation before clinical deployment as a pre-treatment screening tool.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일기관 코호트 연구(초기 630명 중 75명) — 영구치열기 환자에서 Invisalign 투명교정장치로 시행한 순차적 양측 상악 대구치 원심이동(측당 2-4mm) 후 Jemt 유두지수로 평가한 상악 중절치 개방형 치은유두(Open Gingival Embrasure, OGE, 일명 black triangle) 발생.

OGE 발생률 17.3%(13/75); 다변량 로지스틱 회귀에서 나이(OR 1.17), 전후방 중첩(anteroposterior overlap, OR 2.41), ANB각(OR 2.31), U1-NA각(OR 1.39)이 독립적 술전 예측인자로 확인(모두 P<0.05), 예측모델 판별력 우수(ROC AUC 0.839).

치은생물형(gingival biotype)과 전치부 후방견인량은 가설과 달리 유의하지 않았음; 표본 크기 작고(n=75, OGE 사례 13건) EPV 기준 부족해 외부 다기관 검증이 필요한 예비적 결과.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to specifically investigate pre-treatment risk factors for OGE in patients undergoing sequential maxillary molar distalization with clear aligners (as opposed to mixed-appliance or general orthodontic OGE literature).
- Establishes a clinically usable, all-pre-treatment-variable prediction model (age, anteroposterior overlap, ANB, U1-NA) with good discriminatory power (AUC 0.839) that could inform pre-treatment risk counseling and treatment planning.
- Reports a specific incidence figure (17.3%) for this precise clinical scenario (sequential distalization, non-extraction, clear aligners), distinguishing it from broader fixed-appliance OGE literature (22-58% per prior studies) and from general clear-aligner OGE rates (25.7-38% per Wang et al., Yang et al.).
- Proposes a biomechanical explanation specific to sequential (V-shaped) distalization: anterior teeth initially serve as anchorage, then undergo reciprocal retraction as space closes, potentially compressing interradicular alveolar bone crest and widening the interproximal-contact-point-to-alveolar-crest distance.
- Uses strict exclusion criteria (no pre-existing diastema/OGE, no periodontitis, no IPR, no restorations in anterior maxilla) to isolate distalization-specific mechanical/biological OGE risk from confounding pre-existing periodontal disease.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: retrospective single-center cohort, Department of Orthodontics, Tianjin Stomatological Hospital, patients treated June 2014-September 2024.
- Population: 75 patients selected from an initial pool of 630; permanent dentition stage; treated with sequential maxillary molar distalization using Invisalign clear aligners; bilateral molar distalization limited to 2-4 mm per side; complete pre/post-treatment records (digital casts, lateral cephalograms, intraoral/extraoral photographs). Each patient treated by a single orthodontist with >10 years experience.
- Exclusion criteria: pre-treatment diastema or OGE in maxillary anterior region; history of maxillary anterior extraction or congenital absence; poor oral hygiene/gingivitis/periodontitis in maxillary anterior region; existing restorations in maxillary anterior teeth; history of interproximal reduction (IPR) in maxillary anterior teeth; secondary orthodontic treatment; inability to attend follow-up; systemic disease (e.g., diabetes).
- Outcome measurement: OGE assessed via Jemt papilla-filling index (0-4 scale) from standardized pre/post-treatment frontal intraoral photographs; OGE group = scores 0-2, non-OGE group = scores 3-4. Assessor blinded to clinical data and group allocation. Intra-observer reliability: 15% of samples re-measured after 1 month; ICC ≥ 0.885, Kappa ≥ 0.824 ("excellent").
- Gingival biotype classified as thin vs. thick (Seibert & Lindhe 1998 concept), assessed visually.
- 17 candidate pre-treatment factors evaluated across 3 domains: sociodemographic (medical records), malocclusion characteristics (cephalometric measurements via Dolphin Imaging v11.95; digital model measurements via OrthoCAD v5.9.1.50 — anteroposterior overlap, transverse overlap, rotation, overjet, overbite, crowding, ANB, MP-SN, MP-FH, U1-SN, U1-NA, U1-L1), orthodontic treatment characteristics (clear aligner treatment plan, e.g., anterior teeth retraction amount).
- Statistics: Chi-square for categorical variables; normality via Kolmogorov-Smirnov/Shapiro-Wilk; Levene's test for variance homogeneity; independent-samples t-test or Welch's t-test for normal continuous data, Mann-Whitney U for non-normal data. Univariate logistic regression screened candidates (P<0.1 threshold for entry into multivariate model); multivariate logistic regression for independent associations (OR, 95% CI). Multicollinearity checked via VIF (<5) and tolerance (>0.2). Model fit: Hosmer-Lemeshow test. ROC curve for discriminatory power. SPSS v21.0 (IBM Corp).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Sample: 75 patients, 12 male (16.0%), 63 female (84.0%); mean age 25.32 ± 9.44 years. Gingival biotype: 34 thick (37.3%), 41 thin (62.7%) [note: text has a labeling inconsistency in one passage but Table 1 confirms 34 thick/37.3%, 41 thin — wait, re-check: paper text states "34 patients (37.3%) were classified as thick biotype and 41 patients (62.7%) as thin biotype" while Table 1 shows thin n=28 total (25+3) and thick n=47 total (37+10); these totals don't perfectly reconcile in the source manuscript — flagged as an internal inconsistency in this "article in press" version].
- OGE incidence: 13/75 = 17.3%; non-OGE 62/75 (82.7%).
- Categorical comparisons: no significant association between OGE and gender (P=0.974) or gingival biotype (P=0.242).
- Continuous variable comparisons: no significant intergroup differences in overjet, overbite, crowding level, anteroposterior overlap, transverse overlap, rotation, anterior teeth retraction, ANB, MP-SN, MP-FH, U1-SN, U1-NA, or U1-L1 individually by univariate group comparison (all P>0.05) — EXCEPT age, which differed significantly (non-OGE median 21.00 [IQR 17.00-29.00] vs. OGE 34.00 [IQR 23.00-40.50] years, P=0.005).
- Univariate logistic regression (P<0.1 candidates for multivariate model): age (OR 1.26, 95% CI 1.09-1.46), overjet (OR 0.35, 95% CI 0.12-1.02), anteroposterior overlap (OR 2.97, 95% CI 0.89-9.91), ANB (OR 3.65, 95% CI 1.22-10.91), U1-NA angle (OR 1.52, 95% CI 0.98-2.35), U1-L1 angle (OR 1.41, 95% CI 1.05-1.89).
- Multivariate logistic regression (independent predictors, P<0.05): age (OR 1.17, 95% CI 1.07-1.30; each +1 year → 17% higher odds of OGE), anteroposterior overlap (OR 2.41, 95% CI 1.05-5.54; each +1 mm → 141% higher risk), ANB angle (OR 2.31, 95% CI 1.08-4.94; each +1° → 2.31-fold higher odds), U1-NA angle (OR 1.39, 95% CI 1.03-1.89; each +1° → 1.39-fold higher odds).
- ROC analysis: AUC 0.839 (95% CI 0.72-0.96) for the combined 4-factor model — "good discriminatory power."
- Mean age by group: non-OGE 23.69 ± 8.30 years vs. OGE 33.08 ± 10.96 years (~9-year difference cited in Discussion, though Table 2 reports median/IQR rather than mean±SD for age as a non-normally distributed variable).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Author-stated limitations:
- Small sample size relative to Events Per Variable (EPV) criterion (only 13 OGE events for a multivariate model with several candidate predictors).
- Extent of sequential distalization varied among patients; use of auxiliary anchorage (e.g., mini-implants/TADs) was not categorized as a covariate, potentially confounding results.
- OGE evaluated via 2D intraoral photographs and the Jemt index only — may introduce measurement error versus 3D imaging or periapical radiographs.
- Gingival biotype assessed by visual inspection only (operator-dependent, no probe transparency or periodontal probing) — may explain the null finding for biotype versus prior literature (e.g., Al-Zarea et al., Gul et al. found thin biotype strongly predictive).
- Single-center, single-ethnicity (Chinese) sample with 84% female — limits generalizability to other populations/appliance systems.
- No external validation cohort — the ROC/AUC figure is derived and evaluated on the same development sample (internal validation only).

Additional limitations identified by this summary:
- Retrospective design with historical selection (75 of 630 screened) risks selection bias not fully quantified (flow chart referenced as Figure 1 but not reproduced in extracted text).
- The mechanistic explanation for age, ANB, and U1-NA associations (accelerated bone remodeling failure, compensatory lingual retraction in skeletal Class II) is explicitly stated by the authors as speculative, not directly confirmed by imaging data.
- Apparent inconsistency in gingival-biotype counts between the prose (34 thick/41 thin) and Table 1 subgroup totals (47 thick/28 thin when non-OGE and OGE columns are summed) suggests a possible transcription or table-labeling error in this pre-publication "article in press" version; should be checked against the final published version when available.

## 6. Related Work

- Yang et al. — OGE incidence in maxillary/mandibular incisors significantly higher with clear aligners (35.0%/38.0%) than fixed appliances (18.0%/24.0%).
- Wang et al. — non-extraction clear aligner cases: OGE 25.7% maxillary incisors, 40.3% mandibular incisors.
- Jung et al. — OGE incidence 51% maxillary incisor region, 64% mandibular incisor region after general orthodontic treatment.
- Burke et al. — 41.9% post-treatment OGE incidence among 500 adolescent orthodontic patients with maxillary central incisor crowding.
- Kurth & Kokich — 38% prevalence of OGE after adult orthodontic treatment.
- An et al. — each 1 mm lingual movement of maxillary central incisors increased OGE risk 1.18-fold.
- Kandasamy et al. — labial tooth movement may also elevate OGE risk via gingival margin thinning/recession.
- Mahasneh et al. — OGE incidence 2% when interproximal-contact-point-to-alveolar-bone-crest distance ≤5 mm, increasing incidence as distance increases (radiographic correlate cited to explain the anteroposterior-overlap finding).
- Al-Zarea et al.; Gul et al. — thin gingival biotype as a primary OGE risk factor in general orthodontic populations (contrasts with this study's null finding).
- Cui et al. (2025, BMC Oral Health) — risk factors for OGE after clear aligner treatment (retrospective study); closely related population, referenced regarding crowding and OGE association.

## 7. Glossary

- OGE (Open Gingival Embrasure): pathological triangular space between adjacent teeth where the interdental papilla fails to fill the gingival embrasure; colloquially "black triangle."
- Jemt index: 0-4 scale scoring interdental papilla fill (0 = absent papilla/flat gingival curve; 4 = papillary hyperplasia beyond the interdental space).
- ANB angle: angle between A-point, Nasion, B-point; cephalometric measure of anteroposterior skeletal jaw discrepancy (larger ANB → more skeletal Class II tendency).
- U1-NA (angle/mm): angulation/linear position of the upper central incisor relative to the NA line — reflects incisor inclination/protrusion.
- U1-L1: angle between upper and lower incisors (interincisal angle).
- MP-SN, MP-FH: mandibular plane angles relative to Sella-Nasion and Frankfort Horizontal planes (vertical skeletal pattern).
- Anteroposterior overlap: measure of localized sagittal crowding/overlap in the anterior region (measured via OrthoCAD digital models).
- ICP-ABC distance: distance from the interproximal contact point to the alveolar bone crest; a radiographic predictor of papilla fill capacity (Tarnow-type relationship, cited via Mahasneh et al.).
- Sequential (V-shaped) molar distalization: distalization strategy initiated at bilateral second molars, progressing mesially tooth-by-tooth.
- EPV (Events Per Variable): methodological criterion for adequate sample size in regression modeling relative to number of outcome events.
- VIF (Variance Inflation Factor): multicollinearity diagnostic in multivariate regression.
