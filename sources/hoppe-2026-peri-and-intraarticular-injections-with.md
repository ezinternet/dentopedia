## Why Ingested

위키 주제인 급성 악관절 탈구의 복원 (reduction of acute TMJ dislocation) 에서 복원은 1단계이고 **재발 방지가 2단계**인데, [[tmj/prechel-2018-the-treatment-of-temporomandibular-joint]]의 자가혈액 주사 (autologous blood injection, ABI) ~80% 무증상·프로로테라피 (prolotherapy) 91% 재발 無 근거는 크기가 각각 ≤30명인 케이스시리즈에서 나왔고, [[tmj/abrahamsson-2020-treatment-of-temporomandibular-joint-luxation]]는 재발성 주사 요법을 RCT 5편으로만 다룬다. 이 논문은 **주사 성분 효과가 동시 비주사 중재로부터 분리(isolable)될 수 있는** 전제만으로 근거를 다시 골라 재발성 악관절 탈구 (recurrent mandibular dislocation) 주사 요법을 표적화했고, 최종적으로 **1차 임상 연구 5편(ABI 3·덱스트로스 프로로테라피 1·5% sodium morrhuate 1)** 만 남겼다는 것이 핵심 — 즉 이 주제 영역에서 실제로 비교 가능한 무기절 (non-arthroscopic)·무고정 (non-immobilization) 주사 근거가 이 5편뿐이라는 **정량적 허점 정량화**가 [[tmj/hoppe-2025-management-of-recurrent-temporomandibular-joint]]의 소아 재발 관리 SR(포함 9편)과 나란히 읽혀야 한다.

> **프레이밍 주의**: 이 논문은 급성 탈구의 복원 (reduction) 자체가 아니라 **복원 이후 재발 방지 (recurrence prevention)** 를 다룬다. 주입부위(관절강내 vs 관절주변)와 약제 선택에 대한 근거 공백 지도이며, Hipocratic 수기 복원의 효과는 다루지 않는다.

## Three-line Summary

Mapping review (evidence-mapping/scoping design, prospectively registered on OSF as osf.io/p3tm5, PRISMA 2020 flow used only to visualise selection) searching PubMed/MEDLINE, Europe PMC and BASE from inception to 7 April 2026, with a deliberately restrictive eligibility rule — the injectable component's clinical effect had to be assessable **independently** from any simultaneous surgical, arthroscopic or immobilisation procedure — that reduced the field to **5 primary clinical studies plus 8 secondary mapping/reference sources**, with substantial reviewer agreement (Cohen's κ = 0.89 at title/abstract, 0.92 at full text) and JBI appraisal rating methodological quality as generally low to moderate.

The 5 included studies cover only three agents: **autologous blood** (Machon 2018 double-blind n=40, 2 mL superior joint space + 1 mL periarticular vs 1 mL periarticular only; Oshiro 2014 n=14, ~3 mL superior compartment + ~2 mL pericapsular ≈ 5 mL, 0 recurrence at 1 year; Yoshida 2018 n=21, 18/21 recurrence-free within 36 months), **50% dextrose prolotherapy** (Zhou 2014 n=45, 2 mL 50% dextrose after 2 mL 2% lignocaine, posterior periarticular at the condylar neck, 41/45 free of further dislocation/subluxation for >6 months) and **5% sodium morrhuate** (Xu 1992 n=105, 0.5 mL per point extra-articularly, excellent/good 92.5% at ≥2 years).

No quantitative pooling was performed and the conclusion is explicitly negative: the evidence **does not permit conclusions on comparative effectiveness or superiority of any injectable modality** — all agents act by the same largely theoretical route of inducing periarticular fibrosis to limit condylar translation, and the authors note that clinically important prospective and randomised studies (e.g. Hegab 2013, Chhapane 2024) were excluded precisely because they combined injection with post-procedural immobilisation, which they concede narrows the clinical representativeness of their own map.

## 세줄요약

매핑 리뷰 (mapping review, 근거 지도형 스코핑 설계) — OSF 사전등록(osf.io/p3tm5), PRISMA 2020 흐름도는 선별 시각화 용도로만 사용, 2026년 4월 7일까지 PubMed(MEDLINE)·Europe PMC·BASE 3개 DB를 inception부터 검색. 핵심 선별 규칙은 **주사 성분의 임상 효과를 수술·관절경·악관절 고정 (intermaxillary immobilization) 같은 동시 비주사 중재와 분리하여 평가할 수 있어야 한다**는 것이었고, 그 결과 **1차 임상 연구 5편 + 보조 매핑·참고확인 문헌 8편**만 남음(제목/초록 단계 제외 231건, Cohen's κ=0.89 / 전문 단계 0.92, JBI 평가에서 전반적 방법론적 질은 낮음~중등).

남은 5편이 다루는 약제는 단 세 가지뿐이다 — 자가혈액 주사 (autologous blood injection, ABI): Machon 2018 이중눈가림 n=40(상부 관절강내 2 mL + 관절주변 1 mL vs 관절주변 1 mL 단독, 수치상 우위이나 통계적 유의성 없음), Oshiro 2014 n=14(상부 구획 ~3 mL + 관절주변조직 ~2 mL, 1년 재발 0건), Yoshida 2018 n=21(36개월 내 18/21 재발 없음); 50% 덱스트로스 프로로테라피 (dextrose prolotherapy): Zhou 2014 n=45(2% 리그노카인 2 mL 10분 후 50% 덱스트로스 2 mL를 과두경 후방 관절주변에 단일 주입, 41/45가 6개월 초과 재탈구·아탈구 없음); 5% sodium morrhuate 경화제: Xu 1992 n=105(관절강내가 아닌 관절주변 0.5 mL/점, ≥2년 추적에서 우수 94·양호 3·불량 8 → 우수/양호 92.5%).

정량적 통합(메타분석)은 수행하지 않았고 결론은 명시적으로 **부정**이다 — 어떤 주사 요법의 비교 효과·우위도 결론 낼 수 없다. 세 약제 모두 "관절주변 섬유화를 유도해 과두 과다 이동을 제한"이라는 거의 이론적인 동일 기전으로 설명되며, 저자들은 임상적으로 중요한 전향성·무작위 연구들(예: Hegab 2013, Chhapane 2024)이 주사 후 악관절 고정 병행 때문에 제외되었고 이 자체가 지도 (map) 의 임상적 대표성을 좁혔다고 인정함 — 따라서 본 논문은 급성 복원 (reduction) 이 아니라 **복원 이후 재발 방지 (recurrence prevention)** 를 다룬다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **An "isolable effect" eligibility gate applied to an entire field**: injections combined with surgery, arthroscopy, or post-procedural mandibular immobilisation were excluded *unless* the injectable component could be isolated. This single design decision is the paper's real contribution — it is why the field collapses to 5 studies, and the authors openly concede it also removed clinically important randomised trials.
- **A dose/volume/site map of the surviving evidence**, which is the practically usable output: single-site posterior periarticular 2 mL of 50% dextrose after 2 mL lignocaine (Zhou); superior joint space 2 mL + 1 mL periarticular vs 1 mL periarticular alone (Machon); ~3 mL superior compartment + ~2 mL pericapsular ≈ 5 mL (Oshiro, replicated protocol in Yoshida); 0.5 mL per injection point extra-articular 5% sodium morrhuate (Xu).
- **Explicit separation of "favourable direction" from "comparative evidence"**: most studies report reduced recurrence, but only one study is comparative (Machon), and its numerical advantage for intra- plus periarticular injection did not reach statistical significance — so the paper's conclusion is that no modality can be ranked.
- **A quantified statement of the gap**: evidence for hyaluronic acid/fillers, corticosteroids, botulinum toxin, hydroxyapatite and PRP in this indication rests on isolated reports and secondary sources; the BoNT studies for recurrent luxation were excluded because they were intramuscular (Kotimäki 2011, Gilles 2000) rather than intra/periarticular, and PRP/PRF reports were excluded on population grounds.
- **Mechanism framing that is explicitly labelled theoretical**: ABI → local inflammatory response, clot, periarticular fibrosis; hypertonic dextrose → controlled inflammatory/reparative response; sodium morrhuate → sclerosant-induced fibrosis. The authors state response magnitude may depend on site, volume/concentration, number of sessions, baseline joint characteristics and adjunctive immobilisation — all of which vary across the mapped studies.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: mapping review (evidence map), prospectively registered; PRISMA 2020 flow diagram for selection visualisation only; descriptive structured mapping synthesis, **no quantitative pooling or meta-analysis**
- **Databases**: PubMed (MEDLINE), Europe PMC, BASE — inception to 7 April 2026, plus reference-list screening and author contact
- **Search string**: ("temporomandibular joint" OR "TMJ" OR temporomandibular OR mandibular OR mandible) AND (dislocation OR … OR recurrent*) AND (inject* OR "intra-articular" OR "peri-articular" OR injection) AND (dextrose OR "autologous blood" OR "platelet rich plasma" OR PRP OR "hyaluronic acid" OR corticosteroid* OR "botulinum toxin" OR hydroxyapatite OR filler* OR fibrin OR biologic*)
- **Screening**: Rayyan, two independent reviewers (J.K., O.J.) at title/abstract and full-text stages after a calibration exercise; 231 records excluded at title/abstract; disagreements by consensus
- **n**: 5 primary clinical studies (Machon 2018, Oshiro 2014, Xu 1992, Yoshida 2018, Zhou 2014) + 8 secondary mapping/reference sources (Abrahamsson 2020, Cecílio 2019, Renapurkar & Laskin 2018, Nagori 2018, Chęciński 2023, Turosz 2024, Zhou 2025, Hoppe 2025)
- **Critical appraisal**: Joanna Briggs Institute checklists (case series; revised quasi-experimental checklist 2024), two reviewers
- **Outcomes charted**: recurrence frequency and time to recurrence, maximum interincisal/mouth opening, pain scores, jaw function, clicking/sound phenomena, patient-reported outcomes, need for subsequent intervention, adverse events, and procedural details (site, volume, number of sessions)
- **Terminology harmonisation**: "habitual dislocation"/"habitual luxation" reclassified under recurrent dislocation where the clinical description indicated repeated episodes

**Exclusion table (Table A1) highlights** — why the field looks so thin: wrong population (general TMD or hypermobility without stratified recurrent-dislocation data: Majumdar 2016, Assiri 2025, Singh 2024, Refai 2011, Simsek 2016, Aishwarya 2024, Bindra 2015, Triantafillidou 2012); wrong route for BoNT (intramuscular, not intra-articular: Kotimäki 2011, Gilles 2000); effect not isolable (Yang 1999 arthroscopic sclerotherapy + suturing; Moussa 2022); protocol only (Yoshioka 2016); conference abstract (Machon 2015); and a long list of post-procedural **mandibular immobilisation** exclusions — Kato 2007, Chhapane 2024, Hange 2020, Candirli 2012, Ertas 2022, Gupta 2013, Gagnani 2020, Hasson 2001, Matsumoto 2015, Daif 2010, **Hegab 2013**, Koparal 2016, Shah 2022, Pinto 2009, Machon 2009, Coser 2015, Pandey 2022, Candirli 2013.

**Bottom line (Conclusions)**: evidence "remains preliminary and highly heterogeneous"; studies evaluated autologous blood, dextrose and sclerosing agents and generally reported reduced recurrence; "the current evidence does not permit conclusions regarding comparative effectiveness or support broad clinical recommendations."

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Stated**: heterogeneity across design, population, agent, technique, site, follow-up and outcome definitions precluded quantitative synthesis; most primary studies small and non-comparative; inconsistent terminology (recurrent vs habitual dislocation vs subluxation vs hypermobility); the immobilisation exclusion narrowed clinical scope and may have introduced selection bias.
- **Stated**: no high-quality comparative trials of injectables versus surgery or standardised conservative care; prospective controlled studies with standardised protocols and clearly defined recurrent-dislocation populations are needed.
- **Additional observation (editorial quality)**: the Discussion contains duplicated sentences (the "Consequently, despite generally favorable reported outcomes…" clause appears twice in §4.1), a copy-editing artefact that does not change any result but is worth noting when citing.
- **Not answered by this paper**: whether intraarticular and periarticular routes are genuinely equivalent — the single comparative study (Machon) was underpowered to separate them, so the route question stays open.

## 7. Glossary

- **Mapping review (evidence map)**: a systematic search and structured charting of an entire evidence field when studies are too sparse and heterogeneous for quantitative comparison; characterises agents, targets, designs and outcomes rather than pooling them
- **Isolable treatment effect**: the eligibility principle of this paper — an injectable counts as evidence only if its clinical contribution can be separated from any simultaneous surgical, arthroscopic or immobilisation procedure
- **Autologous blood injection (ABI)**: injection of the patient's own venous blood, ~2–5 mL total, into the superior joint space and/or periarticular tissues, intended to provoke clot and periarticular fibrosis that limits excessive condylar translation
- **Dextrose prolotherapy**: injection of hypertonic dextrose (50% in the included study) into periarticular tissues to provoke a controlled inflammatory and reparative response
- **Sodium morrhuate**: a sclerosant injected extra-articularly around the TMJ capsule/condylar neck to induce periarticular fibrosis
- **Recurrent vs habitual dislocation**: overlapping terms for repeated episodes; this review harmonised "habitual dislocation/luxation" into recurrent mandibular dislocation where the description indicated repeated episodes
