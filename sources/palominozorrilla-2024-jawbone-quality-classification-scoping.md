## One-line Summary
Scoping review (442 titles → 17 relevant studies): Lekholm & Zarb classification dominates (13/17 studies); no study validated diagnostic accuracy or reproducibility of its classification — a critical evidence gap.

## 한줄요약
범위 고찰 (442편 → 17편): 렉홀름-자르브(Lekholm & Zarb) 분류가 지배적(13/17); 그러나 단 한 편도 분류 체계의 진단 정확도나 재현성을 검증하지 않음 — 핵심 근거 공백.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Documents prevalence of classification systems across implant planning/placement literature
- Finds Lekholm & Zarb used in 13/17 studies; Misch, UCLA, Trisi & Rao in 4
- Identifies critical gap: assessment methods "often briefly described or not described at all"
- Only 5/17 studies assessed observer performance; 3/17 assessed diagnostic accuracy
- None validated reproducibility of their classification — systematic methodological weakness
- Calls for a classification system that integrates bone tissue characteristics, assessment method description, diagnostic accuracy, and observer performance

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Scoping review — PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science
- Search terms: bone quality, bone quantity, bone density, CBCT/cone-beam CT
- 3 reviewers: title screening → abstract → full text
- n=442 titles → 32 full text → 17 relevant articles
- Categorized: classification systems used, assessment methods, observer performance, diagnostic accuracy

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 13/17 articles used Lekholm & Zarb classification (most common)
- 4/17 proposed modifications to Lekholm & Zarb
- 4 other classifications: Misch (D1-D4), UCLA, Trisi & Rao
- Bone quality assessment methods: HU (CT/CBCT), ITV, RFA (ISQ), subjective drilling
- Observer performance reported in 5/17 studies
- Diagnostic accuracy reported in 3/17 studies
- None validated reproducibility of their specific classification

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Scoping review — no meta-analysis possible (heterogeneous classification systems)
- Year-limited: published data up to 2024
- No formal quality assessment of included studies
- Calls for new validated classification integrating: CBCT-derived parameters + ITV + ISQ + observer reproducibility

## 6. Related Work
- Lekholm & Zarb 1985: original 4-type bone quality classification (Type I-IV)
- Misch: D1-D4 classification based on cortical/trabecular density
- Shemtov-Yona 2021 (PLoS ONE): quantitative meta-analysis of jawbone geometry for classification — companion paper
- Rosas-Díaz 2024, 2026: PITV classification development for intraoperative bone quality assessment

## 7. Glossary
- **Lekholm & Zarb (1985)**: 4-type bone classification based on cortical/trabecular ratio and density from radiograph + drilling sensation
- **Misch classification (D1-D4)**: density-based bone classification in edentulous areas
- **UCLA classification**: University of California Los Angeles bone quality classification variant
- **Trisi & Rao**: histomorphometry-based bone density classification
- **HU (Hounsfield Unit)**: CT radiodensity unit used for quantitative bone density measurement
