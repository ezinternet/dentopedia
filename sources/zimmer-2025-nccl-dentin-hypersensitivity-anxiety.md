## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional study (n=157, age 18–45 yr) found 상아질 과민증 (Dentin Hypersensitivity, DH) prevalence of 84.7% and 비우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesions, NCCL) prevalence of 49.7% in young adults. Anxiety (Beck Anxiety Inventory) was independently associated with higher DH prevalence, alongside NCCL, whitening toothpaste use, and post-meal brushing as risk factors. Soft/extra-soft toothbrush use and oral health knowledge were protective against DH.

## 세줄요약

157명(18–45세) 횡단면 연구에서 상아질 과민증 (DH) 유병률 84.7%, 비우식성 치경부 병소 (NCCL) 유병률 49.7% 확인. 불안(Beck 불안 척도)이 DH 유병률 상승과 독립적으로 연관되었으며, 미백 치약과 식후 즉시 칫솔질도 위험인자로 확인됨. 부드러운/초부드러운 칫솔 사용과 구강보건 지식은 DH 유병률을 낮추는 보호인자로 나타남.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Analytical cross-sectional study
- **Sample**: 157 participants, age 18–45 years
- **Data collection**: Clinical examination + self-report questionnaire (lifestyle, daily habits, Beck Anxiety Inventory)
- **Statistical method**: Poisson regression models

## Key Findings

- **DH prevalence**: 84.7%
- **NCCL prevalence**: 49.7%
- **Risk factors for DH**: NCCL (both ≥1 tooth and >4 teeth per individual), anxiety signs, whitening toothpaste use, brushing immediately after meals
- **Protective factors for DH**: Soft/extra-soft toothbrush use, oral health knowledge
- **Novel finding**: Individuals with anxiety signs had higher DH prevalence — psychological factor independently associated with DH

## Why Ingested

First study to link anxiety (Beck Anxiety Inventory) to both DH and NCCL prevalence in a clinical sample. Provides novel psychological risk factor angle for NCCL/DH etiology discussion. High prevalence figures (84.7% DH, 49.7% NCCL) in young adults (18–45 yr) serve as epidemiological reference values.

## Limitations

- Cross-sectional design: cannot establish causality
- Single-center (implied by sample size)
- Abstract-only source; full methodology details not available
- DH diagnosis method not specified in abstract
