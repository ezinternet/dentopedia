## Why Ingested

This network meta-analysis of 37 RCTs is the most comprehensive ranking of intrasocket interventions for alveolar osteitis prevention after mandibular third molar extraction, identifying platelet-rich fibrin (OR=0.28) and CHX 0.2% gel (OR=0.52) as best options. It provides a hierarchical evidence base directly applicable to surgical decision-making alongside [[oral-surgery/third-molar/daly-2022-local-interventions-management-alveolar-osteitis]].

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and network meta-analysis evaluated the efficacy of different intrasocket interventions immediately after mandibular third molar surgery to prevent alveolar osteitis (AO), including 37 RCTs (6175 surgeries in 4716 patients). Pairwise meta-analysis showed intrasocket interventions significantly reduced AO incidence (RR = 0.39, p < 0.0001, 95% CI: 0.30 to 0.52). Platelet-rich fibrin (OR = 0.28, 95% CI: 0.10 to 0.71) and chlorhexidine 0.2% gel (OR = 0.52, 95% CI: 0.32 to 0.95) were significantly more effective than placebo.

There is moderate evidence supporting the use of platelet-rich fibrin or chlorhexidine 0.2% gel in the socket for prevention of alveolar osteitis after mandibular third molar surgery.

## 세줄요약

본 체계적문헌고찰 및 네트워크 메타분석은 하악 제3대구치(사랑니) 발치 후 치조골염(AO, dry socket) 예방을 위한 다양한 치조내 중재법의 효과를 평가하였으며, 37개의 무작위대조시험(RCT, 6175건 수술, 4716명 환자)을 포함하였다. 쌍별 메타분석에서 치조내 중재법은 AO 발생률을 유의하게 감소시켰고(RR = 0.39, p < 0.0001, 95% CI: 0.30 ~ 0.52), 혈소판-농축 피브린(PRF, OR = 0.28, 95% CI: 0.10 ~ 0.71)과 클로르헥시딘 0.2% 젤(OR = 0.52, 95% CI: 0.32 ~ 0.95)은 위약보다 유의하게 효과적이었다.

중등도 근거는 하악 제3대구치 수술 후 치조골염 예방을 위해 치조 내 혈소판-농축 피브린 또는 클로르헥시딘 0.2% 젤 사용을 지지한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Intrasocket interventions significantly reduce alveolar osteitis incidence after mandibular third molar surgery (RR = 0.39, p < 0.0001).
- Platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) significantly reduces AO compared to placebo (OR = 0.28, 95% CI: 0.10 to 0.71).
- Chlorhexidine 0.2% gel significantly reduces AO compared to placebo (OR = 0.52, 95% CI: 0.32 to 0.95).
- Eugenol paste, recombinant bovine growth factor, colloidal silver gelatin sponge, and acellular dermal matrix also showed significant efficacy.
- Moderate evidence supports PRF or chlorhexidine 0.2% gel as effective intrasocket interventions.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- Databases: EMBASE, Cochrane Library, MEDLINE/PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, LILACS, and grey literature. Searched up to September 2019.
- PRISMA compliance: Yes.
- Inclusion criteria: RCTs evaluating intrasocket interventions immediately after mandibular third molar extraction to prevent alveolar osteitis.
- Included: 37 RCTs (6175 mandibular third molar surgeries in 4716 patients).
- Risk of bias: Assessed using Cochrane RoB tool. Kappa = 0.80 (excellent agreement).
- Data synthesis: Pairwise meta-analysis + network meta-analysis. Risk ratio (RR) and odds ratio (OR) with 95% CI.
- Heterogeneity: Assessed via I².

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Alveolar osteitis incidence (overall)** — RR = 0.39 (95% CI: 0.30 to 0.52, p < 0.0001)
- **Platelet-rich fibrin vs placebo** — OR = 0.28 (95% CI: 0.10 to 0.71)
- **Chlorhexidine 0.2% gel vs placebo** — OR = 0.52 (95% CI: 0.32 to 0.95)
- **Eugenol paste vs placebo** — OR = 0.06 (95% CI: 0.00 to 0.77)
- **Recombinant bovine growth factor vs placebo** — OR = 0.07 (95% CI: 0.00 to 0.97)
- **Colloidal silver gelatin sponge vs placebo** — OR = 0.05 (95% CI: 0.00 to 0.74)
- **Acellular dermal matrix vs placebo** — OR = 0.04 (95% CI: 0.00 to 0.74)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- More than 80% of included studies compared only two interventions, limiting direct comparisons.
- Wide variety of definitions and diagnostic criteria for alveolar osteitis across studies.
- Network meta-analysis assumptions (transitivity, consistency) may not be fully satisfied.
- Publication bias cannot be ruled out.
- Small sample sizes in some studies.
- Lack of standardized protocols for intrasocket interventions.

## 7. Glossary

- **AO (Alveolar Osteitis)**: 치조골염 — "dry socket"으로도 불리는 발치 후 합병증, 혈병 소실과 동반된 통증
- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)**: 혈소판-농축 피브린 — 자가 혈액에서 제조한 2세대 혈소판 농축물, 성장인자 다량 함유
- **RR (Risk Ratio)**: 위험비 — 두 군 간 사건 발생 위험의 비율
- **OR (Odds Ratio)**: 교차비 — 두 군 간 사건 발생 오즈의 비율
- **Network Meta-analysis**: 네트워크 메타분석 — 직접 및 간접 비교를 통해 여러 중재법을 동시에 평가하는 방법
- **Mandibular Third Molar**: 하악 제3대구치 — 사랑니
