## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD42023414686, PRISMA) of 10 studies from 3,291 screened records, evaluating bone expansion techniques for horizontal alveolar ridge augmentation with simultaneous implant placement, without concurrent bone grafting materials (Oral Maxillofac Surg, 2025).

Pooled implant survival 100% (95% CI: 99–100%), complication rate 0% (0–1%), and mean ridge width gain +1.55 mm (95% CI: 0.92–2.17 mm); meta-regression demonstrated a statistically significant time-dependent survival attrition of −0.08%/month (P = 0.03).

Bone expansion is an effective and safe technique for horizontal ridge augmentation without grafting materials, but medium-term follow-up monitoring is warranted given the time-dependent survival signal.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA exclusively on bone expansion (without concurrent bone grafting materials) for horizontal ridge augmentation
- Meta-regression demonstrates time-dependent survival decline: 0.08%/month post-placement (P = 0.03)
- Pooled ridge width gain benchmark: +1.55 mm for procedures using osseodensification-type expansion
- Establishes survival benchmark of 100% (99–100%) and complication rate 0% (0–1%) across 10 studies
- Qualitative + quantitative synthesis with leave-one-out outlier analysis and Egger's publication bias testing

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA, PROSPERO-registered)
- Inclusion: Human original articles using different bone expansion procedures for horizontal ridge augmentation, without bone grafting materials or combined with vertical techniques
- Exclusion: Studies using grafting materials exclusively, vertical augmentation only
- Records identified: 3,291; Final included: 10 articles
- Analysis: Random-effects model, weighted mean difference, forest plots, meta-regression, leave-one-out influence analysis, Egger's test, funnel plots
- Heterogeneity: I² assessment

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Implant survival rate: 100% (95% CI: 99–100%)
- Complication rate: 0% (95% CI: 0–1%)
- Ridge width gain (weighted mean difference): +1.55 mm (95% CI: 0.92–2.17 mm)
- Meta-regression: survival probability decreases 0.08%/month (95% CI: 0.009–0.15%; P = 0.03)
- Note: Survival decrease per month is a statistical signal, not a clinically alarming absolute number given the 100% pooled estimate

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- PDF is a Springer paywall preview; detailed included-study table not accessible
- Only 10 articles included — limited by strict exclusion of grafting-combined procedures
- Geographic clustering and study heterogeneity likely
- Short follow-up in most included studies limits long-term survival interpretation

## 7. Glossary
- **Bone expansion**: Technique using incremental osteotomes or OD drills to laterally compress alveolar bone without removing it, increasing ridge width
- **Weighted mean difference (WMD)**: Meta-analytic effect size for continuous outcomes across studies with different scales
- **Leave-one-out analysis**: Sensitivity test to identify influential/outlier studies in meta-analysis
