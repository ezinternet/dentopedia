## Three-line Summary

Abstract-only SR+MA of endodontically treated cracked teeth (15 studies; 9 in time-anchored quantitative synthesis; all at serious risk of bias, very low certainty).

Pooled 4–5-year survival was 82.2% (95% CI 67.7–91.1%; I² 89.5%; 7 cohorts); two cohorts reported 10-year survival of 54% and 66%, and 55% at 15 years in the Singapore cohort; deep probing (aHR 2.48 for 4–5 mm, 3.19 for ≥6 mm) was the persistent adverse marker.

Prognosis should be time-dependent and individualized: survival is often acceptable at 4–5 years but declines thereafter, and the pooled estimate is highly heterogeneous.

## 1. Document Information

- Ruan C, Wang L. J Endod 2026 (e-pub 2026-10-12). DOI 10.1016/j.joen.2026.10.002; PMID 42863660. No PMCID — abstract-only — full text not retrieved.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Time-anchored reassessment: pooled 4–5-year survival 82.2%, with longer horizons (10/15 yr) reported narratively.
- Integrates deep crack-associated probing with radicular extension, periapical disease, multiple cracks, age, and lack of definitive cuspal coverage as risk indicators.
- Explicitly states probing depth is "not a newly identified factor" — the contribution is quantifying its persistence.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PubMed/Europe PMC, Embase, Web of Science through 2026-10-03; longitudinal cohorts of endodontically treated permanent cracked teeth, ≥1 yr follow-up; primary outcome tooth survival; two independent reviewers. Published 4–5-yr proportions pooled on logit scale (REML random effects, modified Knapp-Hartung); longer horizons and prognostic factors narrative; ROBINS-E-informed risk of bias.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 15 studies included; 9 in quantitative synthesis; 7 cohorts in the 4–5-yr pooled estimate.
- Pooled 4–5-yr survival 82.2% (95% CI 67.7–91.1%), I² = 89.5%.
- 10-yr survival 54% and 66% (two independent cohorts); 55% at 15 yr (Singapore cohort).
- Longest cohort: adjusted extraction hazard 2.48 (4–5 mm probing), 3.19 (≥6 mm).
- All studies serious risk of bias; certainty very low.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract-only: study-level data, individual cohort identities, and sensitivity analyses were not available to us. The abstract's own limitations: serious risk of bias in all studies, very low certainty, extreme heterogeneity (I² 89.5%), few cohorts at 10–15 yr (narrative only, two cohorts). The 2.48/3.19 aHRs match those on our [[cracked-tooth/chan-2025-survival-endodontically-treated-cracked-teeth]] page, so the long-horizon figures are likely not independent of that cohort (abstract does not name it explicitly beyond "Singapore cohort"/"longest cohort").
