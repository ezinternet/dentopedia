## Three-line Summary

In-vitro material-science study (Clin Oral Investig 2026, Turku/SCUT) synthesizing a polymerizable radical inhibitor PBHT (a methacrylate-functional BHT analog) and adding it at 1–3 wt% to a UDMA/Bis-EMA/TEGDMA composite: PBHT significantly lowered polymerization shrinkage stress (max stress-rate falling from 47.5 to 13.1 ×10⁻³ MPa·s at 3 wt%) where non-polymerizable BHT did not, while leaching far less inhibitor (1%PBHT 132 vs 1%BHT 549 µg/g), with 1 wt% PBHT giving the best overall balance.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Synthesizes a **polymerizable inhibitor (PBHT)** — a methacrylate-functional analog of the ubiquitous composite stabilizer BHT — to combine shrinkage-stress control with low leachability.
- Demonstrates that **gel-point delay** (not volumetric-shrinkage reduction) is the operative lever: PBHT slows polymerization and lowers shrinkage stress; volumetric shrinkage barely changes (only at 3 wt%).
- Shows the copolymerizing inhibitor **leaches ~4× less** than free BHT at matched concentration, addressing BHT's cytotoxicity/elution concern.
- Identifies a matrix-dependent caveat: in a **UDMA-rich matrix**, non-polymerizable BHT loses its stress-reducing effect (trapped by rapid gelation), whereas PBHT still works.

## 3. Methodology

- **PBHT synthesis**: 2,6-di-tert-butyl-4-hydroxymethylphenol (HBHT) + 2-(methacryloyloxy)ethyl isocyanate (MEI), DBTL catalyst; 90% yield; confirmed by FT-IR + 1H-NMR.
- **Composites**: UDMA 69.3 / Bis-EMA(04) 19.8 / TEGDMA 9.9 / CQ 0.5 / EDB 0.5 (wt%), silanated glass filler at resin:filler 1:3; BHT or PBHT added at 1/2/3 wt%; no-extra-inhibitor control.
- **Outcomes**: DC (ATR-FTIR, Elipar DeepCure-L 1600 mW/cm², 40 s); volumetric shrinkage (Archimedes density); shrinkage stress (FRC-rod cantilever, 1.5 mm, 3 min); FS/FM (ISO 4049 3-pt bend); fracture toughness (SENB, ISO 20795-2); WS/SL (ISO 4049:2019, 7 d); leachable inhibitor (GC-MS SIM).
- **Stats**: two-way ANOVA + Tukey/Sidak, α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results

- **Shrinkage stress**: PBHT significantly reduced SS (p<0.05); BHT did NOT (p>0.05) in this UDMA-rich matrix. Max shrinkage-stress rate (×10⁻³ MPa·s): control 47.5 → 3%BHT 21.8 → **3%PBHT 13.1**; 1%PBHT 30.8.
- **Volumetric shrinkage**: only 3 wt% PBHT reduced VS (mainly via lower DC).
- **Conversion**: all DC >60%; only 3 wt% PBHT significantly lower (66.4% vs 68.7% control, Δ2.3%). PBHT delayed polymerization more than BHT at 2–3 wt%.
- **Mechanical**: FS, FM, fracture toughness comparable to control (except 3%BHT higher FM) — no deterioration.
- **Leachable inhibitor (µg/g)**: 1%BHT 549 / 2%BHT 1102 / 3%BHT 2105 vs **1%PBHT 132 / 2%PBHT 290 / 3%PBHT 717** — PBHT leaches far less.
- **WS/SL**: WS up only at 3 wt%; both inhibitors raise SL, but PBHT < BHT at matched conc; all within ISO 4049 limits (<40 / <7.5 µg/mm³).
- **Optimal**: 1 wt% PBHT (best comprehensive balance).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In-vitro; no cytotoxicity, water-storage aging, wear, or tooth-restoration performance tested.
- Shrinkage measured at 5 min (early-stage; post-cure shrinkage continues).
- Higher-concentration (2–3 wt%) groups mainly mechanistic, not clinical.
- Direct monomer-elution quantification and oxygen-inhibition interaction not done.

## 7. Glossary

- **Shrinkage stress (SS)**: stress at the composite–tooth interface when polymerization contraction is resisted by bonding; drives microleakage, cusp deflection, post-op sensitivity.
- **Gel point**: conversion at which the network becomes rigid; before it, stress relaxes via chain rearrangement — delaying it lowers final SS.
- **BHT (butylated hydroxytoluene)**: common non-polymerizable radical inhibitor/antioxidant in composites; effective but leachable and cytotoxic in excess.
- **PBHT**: 2,6-di-tert-butyl-4-(methacryloyloxy-ethyl-carbamate-methyl)phenol — the synthesized polymerizable (copolymerizing) BHT analog.
- **Degree of conversion (DC)**: fraction of C=C double bonds reacted; too low harms mechanical properties.
- **Water sorption / solubility (WS/SL)**: ISO 4049 durability metrics; limits <40 / <7.5 µg/mm³.
