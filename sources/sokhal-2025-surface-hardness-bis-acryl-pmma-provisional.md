## Why Ingested

사용자의 "아크릴레진 임시치아 재료(SNAP 제품)" 요청에서 SNAP 논문은 PMC 전문이 없어(초록만), PMC 전문판 파트너로 이 논문을 ingest. 아크릴(PMMA) vs bis-acryl 임시수복재의 **표면경도(Vickers)** 근거로, SNAP 논문 [[prosthetic-materials/givens-2007-marginal-adaptation-color-stability-provisional]](변연적합·색안정성)가 다루지 못한 기계적 물성 축을 채운다. 두 편이 짝을 이뤄 "전통 아크릴은 변연적합·색안정은 대등하나 경도는 bis-acryl보다 낮다"는 임상 결론을 완성. 기존 [[prosthetic-materials/tooth-preparation/alqarawi-2024-digital-taper-finish-line-temporary-crown-retention]](임시치관 유지력)와도 연결.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro Vickers-hardness study (Cureus 2025, n=40/group, 160 total) of four provisional crown-and-bridge materials: three bis-acryl composites (3M Protemp 4, Dentsply Integrity, Vericom Care C&B) all had significantly higher surface hardness than the PMMA self-cure acrylic (Pyrax SC-10) — ranking Protemp 4 (20.54 VHN) > Integrity (19.80) > Vericom (18.55) > Pyrax (16.65), all pairwise p<0.01.

- **Design**: In-vitro comparative Vickers hardness study (ADA spec. no. 27 dies)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Head-to-head **surface hardness** ranking of three bis-acryl vs one PMMA provisional under standardized ADA-spec conditions (n=40/group, well-powered).
- Confirms the material-class rule: **bis-acryl > PMMA** for hardness, attributed to cross-linked matrix + inorganic filler.
- Translates to a clear clinical split: bis-acryl for **long-term/high-stress** provisionals; PMMA for **short-term**, adjustable, low-cost use.

## 3. Methodology

- 160 specimens (40/group) in stainless-steel dies per ADA spec no. 27 (30×10×2 mm); type III stone three-pour flasking.
- **Groups**: 1 = 3M Protemp 4 (bis-acryl, bifunctional MA); 2 = Dentsply Integrity (bis-acryl, multifunctional MA); 3 = Vericom Care C&B (dual-cure bis-acryl); 4 = Pyrax SC-10 (PMMA self-cure).
- Prep per manufacturer: bis-acryls auto-mixed (Garant 10:1, press 30 psi/5 min) or extruded + light-cured 40 s/post-cure 10 min (Vericom); PMMA hand-mixed 2:1, self-cure 15 min.
- **Vickers**: HV-1000B, 25 g load, 15 s, 2 indentations/specimen ≥2 mm apart; VHN = 1.8544·F/d².
- Sample size via G*Power (95% power, f=0.40); SPSS v25, one-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD, p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results

| Group | Material | Type | Mean hardness (VHN) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3M Protemp 4 | Bis-acryl | **20.54 ± 0.69** (highest) |
| 2 | Dentsply Integrity | Bis-acryl | 19.80 ± 0.47 |
| 3 | Vericom Care C&B | Dual-cure bis-acryl | 18.55 ± 1.36 |
| 4 | Pyrax SC-10 | PMMA (self-cure) | **16.65 ± 0.66** (lowest) |

- One-way ANOVA p<0.01; all pairwise comparisons significant (Tukey, p≤0.001).
- Largest gap Protemp 4 vs Pyrax (MD 3.89, CI 3.39–4.39); smallest significant gap Protemp 4 vs Integrity (MD 0.74).
- Ranking: **Protemp 4 > Integrity > Vericom > Pyrax (PMMA)**.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In-vitro; no thermocycling / mechanical loading / oral-environment simulation.
- Only **surface hardness** measured — not flexural strength, fracture toughness, marginal integrity, or color stability (the last covered by the paired [[prosthetic-materials/givens-2007-marginal-adaptation-color-stability-provisional]]).
- Single operator; only 4 commercial products; fabrication/storage/aging effects not explored.
- Does not include SNAP specifically; PMMA class is represented by Pyrax SC-10.

## 7. Glossary

- **Provisional (temporary) restoration**: interim crown/bridge protecting the prepared tooth until the definitive prosthesis.
- **Bis-acryl composite**: bi-/multifunctional methacrylate provisional resin with inorganic filler; cross-linked, higher hardness/strength.
- **PMMA (polymethyl methacrylate)**: traditional acrylic provisional; affordable, easy to adjust, lower hardness/wear resistance.
- **Vickers Hardness Number (VHN)**: indentation hardness from a diamond pyramid indenter; VHN = 1.8544·F/d².
- **ADA specification no. 27**: standard governing polymer-based restorative material specimen dimensions/testing.
