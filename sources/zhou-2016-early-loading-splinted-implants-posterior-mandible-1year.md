## Why Ingested
하악 구치부 OsseoSpeed TX 임플란트 스플린팅의 조기 부하 12개월 기초 데이터. [[sources/liu-2019-early-loading-splinted-implants-posterior-mandible-3year]]의 동일 코호트 초기 보고로, 조기 부하 프로토콜의 1년 실현가능성을 확립한다.

## Three-line Summary
Prospective multicenter case series across three Chinese centers enrolled 45 patients with Kennedy class I or II mandibular edentulism (107 OsseoSpeed TX implants, one-stage placement) and delivered early-loaded splinted fixed prostheses at 6–8 weeks, with 12-month follow-up.
At 12 months, implant survival was 100% and mean marginal bone change was +0.08 ± 0.41 mm (slight gain, patient level), with maintained soft tissue health and no dropouts.
This study provides the 12-month baseline data for the same cohort extended to 3 years by Liu 2019, confirming early splinted loading feasibility in posterior mandible, though the single-arm design limits evidence hierarchy.

## 세줄요약
줄1: 전향적 다기관 증례 연구 (Prospective Multicenter Case Series) — 3개 중국 기관, Kennedy Class I·II 하악 무치악 환자 45명(임플란트 107개, OsseoSpeed TX), 단일 단계 식립 후 6–8주 조기부하 (Early Loading, EL) 스플린팅 고정성 보철물, 12개월 추적.
줄2: 임플란트 생존율 (Implant Survival Rate) 100%(107/107); 12개월 변연골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 변화 +0.08 ± 0.41 mm(경미한 골 증가); 연조직 건강 유지, 탈락자 없음.
줄3: Liu 2019 동일 코호트의 1년 기초 데이터로 하악 구치부 스플린팅 조기부하의 실현 가능성을 확인했으나, 대조군 없는 단일군 설계로 근거 수준이 낮다.

## One-line Summary
Prospective multicenter case series (n=45 subjects, 107 implants): 100% survival at 12 months with early loading; mean MBL +0.08 ± 0.41 mm (bone gain) and healthy soft tissue.

## 한줄요약
전향적 다기관(n=45, 임플란트 107개): 12개월 생존율 100%; 변연골 +0.08mm 경미한 증가, 연조직 건강 — 하악 구치부 스플린팅 조기 부하 가능성 최초 확인.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Periodontology, 2016;43(3):298–304
- **Institutions**: Three centers, China
- **Funding**: None (no conflicts)
- **Follow-up**: 12 months after loading (6 and 12-month timepoints)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First multicenter report of early loading protocol (6–8 weeks) for splinted OsseoSpeed TX implants in posterior mandible
- Demonstrates 100% 1-year survival with negligible marginal bone change (slight gain)
- Establishes the baseline cohort data that Liu 2019 follows to 3 years
- Kennedy class I or II edentulism in mandible — clinically common indication

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective multicentre case series
- **n**: 45 subjects (Kennedy class I or II mandibular edentulism), 107 implants
- **Implant system**: OsseoSpeed TX (fluoride-modified, tapered apex, Dentsply)
- **Protocol**: One-stage, 2–3 implants per edentulous region; screw-retained splinted fixed prosthesis at 6–8 weeks
- **Follow-up**: 6 and 12 months after loading
- **Statistical unit**: Patient level (mean overall implants/patient)
- **Data pooling**: Three centers combined

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Implant survival**: 100% (107/107)
- **MBL change**: +0.08 ± 0.41 mm (bone gain, patient level)
- **Soft tissue health**: maintained (no significant probing depth changes or bleeding issues)
- **Dropouts**: None at 12-month endpoint

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No control (no delayed loading comparison group)
- Single arm, case series design — low evidence hierarchy
- All patients Chinese — limited generalizability
- 12-month follow-up is short for definitive assessment
- All implants same brand/surface — results may be surface-specific

## 6. Related Work
- Liu 2019: 3-year follow-up of same cohort
- Anitua 2019: immediate loading short implants (different approach)
- Jofre 2010 (mini-implant splinting): different implant type but splinting principle

## 7. Glossary
- **Kennedy Class I/II**: Bilateral (Class I) or unilateral (Class II) posterior edentulism classification
- **OsseoSpeed TX**: Dentsply Sirona implant with fluoride-modified nanostructure surface
- **Early loading**: Prosthetic loading at 6–8 weeks post-placement
