## Why Ingested

The wiki had no primary cohort that stratified alcohol exposure into light / moderate / heavy and reported peri-implantitis incidence per stratum — the meta-analytic pages could only report pooled odds. This retrospective is the dose-gradient source behind those pooled estimates, and it is the page that supplies the concrete percentages (11.5% vs 46.2%) worth quoting chairside.

→ [[implants/survival/bhatia-2024-alcohol-consumption-implant-success-retrospective]]

## Three-line Summary

Single-centre retrospective cohort (tertiary care centre, implants placed 2010–2022; mean age 59 ± 11.3 years; sample size not reported in the article) relating alcohol intake recorded within 3 months of placement to implant outcome and peri-implantitis.

Peri-implantitis incidence was lowest in light/moderate drinkers (11.5%) and highest in heavy drinkers (46.2%), with moderate intake associated with a 79.1% reduction (P = 0.0365) and light intake a 51.3% reduction (P = 0.026) versus abstainers, while heavy drinking was significant in the opposite direction (P = 0.0001).

The dose gradient (heavy worse, light/moderate seemingly better) reproduces the pattern the 2026 meta-analysis later pooled — but with no reported cohort size, no confounder adjustment table, and a single centre, it is a hypothesis-generating data point, not a risk estimate.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 후속 관찰 연구 (3차 의료기관, 2010–2022년 식립, 평균 연령 59 ± 11.3세, **논문에 표본 수 기재 없음**): 식립 후 3개월 내 음주력을 조사해 임플란트 결과 및 주위염과 연관 분석.

주위염 발생률은 저·중등도 음주군 11.5%, 과량 음주군 46.2%였고, 비음주 대비 중등도 음주는 79.1% 감소 (P = 0.0365), 저용량 음주는 51.3% 감소 (P = 0.026), 과량 음주는 반대 방향으로 유의 (P = 0.0001).

용량 구배 자체는 이후 메타분석이 풀링한 패턴과 같지만, 표본 수 미기재·교란보정표 부재·단일기관이므로 위험 추정치가 아니라 가설생성용 근거로만 인용해야 함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reports peri-implantitis **by alcohol stratum** rather than as a single drinker/non-drinker variable: light/moderate 11.5% vs heavy 46.2% — the widest dose gap of any single cohort in this wiki's alcohol set.
- Gives the timing signal that is clinically useful: **most peri-implantitis occurred within the first two years** after placement (median time to event 31.3 ± 22.2 months).
- Supplies the primary-study layer under the pooled heavy-drinking OR 2.78 of [[implants/survival/boroomand-2026-alcohol-consumption-dental-implant-failure-sr-ma]] and the pooled OR 2.07 of [[implants/peri-implantitis/galarraga-vinueza-2025-peri-implant-disease-risk-factors]].
- Independently reproduces the awkward inverse association at low dose, which is the single most misreadable number in this literature.

## 3. Methodology

- **Setting**: tertiary care centre; implants inserted between 2010 and 2022, analysed as a retrospective cohort.
- **Exposure**: alcohol intake information collected from health maintenance records **within 3 months of implant placement**, classified into light / moderate / heavy / none.
- **Outcomes**: implant success/failure and peri-implantitis at follow-up visits.
- **Statistics**: logistic regression with Wald test per parameter; results as odds ratios with 95% confidence; significance P < 0.05.
- **Sample**: the article reports mean age at placement (59 ± 11.3 years) but **never states the number of patients or implants** — see Limitations.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Stratum | Peri-implantitis incidence | Effect vs abstainers | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 11.5% (joint with moderate) | −51.3% | 0.026 |
| Moderate | 11.5% (joint with light) | −79.1% | 0.0365 |
| Heavy | 46.2% | Increased | 0.0001 |

- Mean age at implant placement: 59 ± 11.3 years.
- Median time to peri-implantitis: 31.3 ± 22.2 months; the majority of events occurred within 2 years of placement.
- The light/moderate figure (11.5%) is reported jointly in the abstract, so the two strata cannot be separated from the available text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Sample size is not reported** anywhere in the accessible article text — patient and implant counts, and therefore the precision of every percentage above, cannot be verified. Do not compute a confidence interval for this study.
- Single centre, retrospective, record-based exposure ascertainment; alcohol intake in the 3-month post-placement window is a proxy for habitual intake, not a measured dose.
- No adjustment table is reported, so the inverse light/moderate association cannot be separated from the healthy-drinker confounders (socioeconomic status, dental attendance, smoking).
- The light and moderate groups are pooled in the headline percentage, hiding whether the effect is monotonic.
- Outcome definitions (peri-implantitis case definition, implant success criteria) are not specified in the deposited text.

## 7. Glossary

- **Peri-implantitis**: inflammatory disease of the tissues around an implant with progressive bone loss.
- **Retrospective cohort**: outcome and exposure are assembled from existing records after the fact; association only.
- **Light / moderate / heavy**: the study's own intake strata; cut-offs are not reported in the accessible text.
- **Wald test**: a regression coefficient test used here to judge whether each parameter's effect differs from zero.
- **OR (odds ratio)**: ratio of odds of the outcome between exposure groups; values < 1 here indicate lower odds than abstainers.
