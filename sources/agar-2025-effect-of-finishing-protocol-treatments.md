## Why Ingested

Serves the **post-adjustment finishing-protocol** axis of the 2026-11-12 `zirconia-occlusal-adjustment` surveillance topic: once an occlusal contact is adjusted, the clinician must choose polished vs glazed vs stain+glaze — this paper quantifies what that choice does to long-term color stability (ΔE) and translucency (TP) of super translucent (ST) zirconia under simulated brushing wear. It complements the mechanical/wear side already held in [[dental-materials/zirconia/steiner-2024-zirconia-conditioning-glazing-enamel-wear]] and [[dental-materials/zirconia/aflatoonian-2025-deglazing-hydrothermal-aging-high-translucent-zirconia]] with the **optical** outcome that those pages do not measure.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (60 ST zirconia plates, Vita YZ ST shade A1, 7×11×2 mm; 3 finishing protocols n=20 each — Polished, Glazed, Chroma+Glaze) measuring CIELAB/CIEDE2000 color difference and translucency parameter (TPab, TP00) before vs after 10,000 brushing cycles (200 g, 1.5 Hz: ~1 year of oral hygiene).

Finishing protocol significantly affected translucency (TPab H=43.48/18.67; TP00 H=46.79/21.09, all p<0.001 — Chroma+Glaze lowest, TPab 4.71–4.8) and color change (ΔEab H=13.21, p=0.0014; ΔE00 H=9.14, p=0.0104), with Chroma+Glaze showing the smallest shift (median ΔE00 ≈ 0.33) and Polished the largest (ΔEab = 1.02); no significant within-group TP change after brushing (all p>0.64).

All groups stayed below the perceptibility threshold (1.8–2.6), so clinically the protocol mainly trades **color stability + masking (stain+glaze)** against **higher translucency (polish/glaze)**; limitation — single brand, no thermocycling, no Ra/gloss measured.

## 세줄요약

In-vitro 연구: ST 지르코니아 (super translucent zirconia) 60개(Vita YZ ST A1, 7×11×2 mm), 마무리 프로토콜 3군(연마/글레이징/Chroma+Glaze) 각 n=20 — 10,000회 칫솔질(200 g, 1.5 Hz, 약 1년 분량) 전후로 색차(ΔEab, ΔE00)와 반투명도 (translucency parameter, TP) 측정.

마무리 프로토콜이 반투명도(TPab H=43.48/18.67, TP00 H=46.79/21.09, 모두 p<0.001; Chroma+Glaze 최저 TPab 4.71–4.8)와 색차(ΔEab H=13.21, p=0.0014; ΔE00 H=9.14, p=0.0104)에 유의한 영향 — 색차 최소는 Chroma+Glaze(ΔE00 중앙값 ≈0.33), 최대는 연마군(ΔEab = 1.02); 칫솔질 전후 군내 TP 변화는 유의하지 않음(모두 p>0.64).

모든 군이 지각역치(perceptibility threshold, 1.8–2.6) 이내여서, 임상적으로는 **색 안정성·차폐(스테인+글레이즈)** 와 **높은 반투명도(연마/글레이즈)** 의 맞교환; 한계 — 단일 브랜드, 열순환 없음, 표면거칠기 (surface roughness, Ra)·광택 미측정.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies the **optical** price of the finishing choice (polish vs glaze vs stain+glaze) on ST zirconia — the axis clinical adjustment protocols usually leave unmeasured
- **Chroma + Glaze gives the best color stability**: median ΔE00 ≈ 0.33, significantly lower than Polished (ΔEab adj p=0.00059; ΔE00 adj p=0.0076) but not significantly lower than Glazed alone (p=0.359 / p=0.717)
- **Chroma + Glaze costs translucency**: lowest TPab (4.71–4.8) and lowest TP00 vs both Glazed and Polished, i.e. the stain layer buys masking at the price of light transmission
- Brushing wear (~1 year simulated) did **not** significantly change TP within any group (all p>0.64) — the protocol, not the wear, dominated translucency
- Polished surface was the least optically stable (highest ΔEab = 1.02, highest variability), consistent with the literature that unglazed zirconia discolors more after abrasion

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 60 rectangular plates 7×11×2 mm milled from a Vita YZ ST A1 disc (CAD/CAM), sintered 1530 °C (VITA SMART.FIRE ADVANCED), thickness verified with digital caliper (±0.01 mm)
- Groups (n=20): **P** = zirconia polisher (Komet ZrO2) + Polish All-in-One paste, 2 min; **G** = Vita Lumex Unique 370 glaze, vacuum-fired 750 °C/1 min; **CG** = Vita Lumex Unique 330 Red-Brown Chroma A stain + Vita Lumex AC 370 glaze, no polishing after
- Color: VITA Easyshade V spectrophotometer (recalibrated each set), 3 readings per specimen, against **white and black** backgrounds, one operator
- Metrics: ΔEab (CIELAB) and ΔE00 (CIEDE2000) baseline→post-wear; TPab and TP00 = white-vs-black color difference
- Wear: custom mechanical brushing simulator, 10,000 cycles, medium bristle (Aquafresh Clean & Flex), Aquafresh Active Fresh toothpaste, 200 g load, 1.5 Hz → ~1 year of clinical hygiene
- Sample size: G*Power 3.1, mixed-model ANOVA, f=0.25, α=0.05, power 0.80 → ≥18 needed; 60 used
- Statistics: Shapiro–Wilk showed non-normality (all p<0.001) → Kruskal–Wallis + Mann–Whitney U (Bonferroni) for TP, Dunn post hoc (Bonferroni) for ΔE, Wilcoxon signed-rank within groups; α=0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Phase | Kruskal–Wallis |
|---|---|---|
| TPab | before | H=43.48, p<0.001 |
| TPab | after | H=18.67, p<0.001 |
| TP00 | before | H=46.79, p<0.001 |
| TP00 | after | H=21.09, p<0.001 |
| ΔEab (baseline→post) | — | H=13.21, p=0.0014 |
| ΔE00 (baseline→post) | — | H=9.14, p=0.0104 |

- Translucency, pairwise: CG < G (p<0.0001 before; p<0.001 after) and CG < P (p<0.0001; p=0.0147 for TP00 / p=0.0152 for TPab); **G vs P not different** (p>0.20)
- Within-group before/after TP: no significant change in any group (all p>0.64)
- Color difference, pairwise: CG < P (ΔEab adj p=0.00059; ΔE00 adj p=0.0076); CG vs G (p=0.359 / p=0.717) and G vs P (p=0.215 / p=0.257) not significant
- Magnitudes: CG median ΔE00 ≈ 0.33 (clinically imperceptible); P highest ΔEab = 1.02; all means below perceptibility threshold (1.8–2.6), acceptability threshold 2.25–3.7
- Discussion notes polished zirconia showed the **greatest increase in translucency after brushing**, attributed to uncontrolled surface roughening

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In-vitro only; single ST zirconia brand (Vita YZ ST), single shade (A1), no thermocycling — no saliva, pH fluctuation, or dietary chromogens
- **Surface roughness (Ra) and gloss were not measured**, so the optical changes cannot be tied to microtopography; intaglio/bonding-surface conditioning also not assessed (etched zirconia morphology not evaluated)
- Staining and glazing were always combined in the CG arm → individual contributions of chroma vs glaze cannot be separated
- Internal inconsistency: the Discussion states TP "decreased across all finishing protocol groups", while the Results report no significant within-group TP change (all p>0.64) — treat the decrease as descriptive/trend rather than tested
- Simulated brushing = one year; long-term durability of stains/glazes under thermal cycling and pH variation left to future work; clinical confirmation needed

## 7. Glossary

- **ST zirconia (초투명 지르코니아, super translucent zirconia)**: high-translucency monolithic zirconia positioned between 3Y-TZP strength and glass-ceramic esthetics
- **Finishing protocol (마무리 프로토콜)**: the final surface treatment of a restoration — polishing, glazing, or staining followed by glazing
- **ΔEab / ΔE00 (색차)**: CIELAB and CIEDE2000 color-difference metrics; perceptibility threshold ~1.8–2.6, acceptability ~2.25–3.7
- **Translucency parameter (반투명도, TP)**: color difference of the same specimen over white vs black backgrounds (TPab = CIELAB, TP00 = CIEDE2000 basis); higher TP = more light transmission
- **Chroma (크로마 스테인)**: extrinsic staining layer applied before glazing to adjust value/chroma
- **Glaze (글레이즈)**: low-firing glass layer sealing the zirconia surface
