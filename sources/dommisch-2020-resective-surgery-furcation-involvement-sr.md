## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (EFP S3 Clinical Practice Guidelines supplement, J Clin Periodontol 2020;47 Suppl 22): 7 studies (from 683 screened), 667 patients, 2,021 teeth with class II or III furcation involvement (FI) undergoing resective surgery (root amputation/resection, root separation, tunnelling) compared with SRP or open flap debridement (OFD).

Tooth survival ranged 38–94.4% for root amputation/resection/separation (highly variable), 62–67% for tunnelling, 63–85% for OFD, and 68–80% for SRP; 1,515 teeth survived 4 to 30.8 years; class II FI showed better outcomes than class III.

The review concludes that SRP and OFD may achieve similar survival rates as resective surgery for class II and III FI — resective approaches are not clearly superior to non-surgical/less-invasive management.

## 1. Document Information

- **DOI**: 10.1111/jcpe.13241
- **PMID**: 31912534
- **Study design**: Systematic review (no formal meta-analysis due to high heterogeneity)
- **Search period**: January 1998 – December 2018, 3 electronic databases
- **Funding**: Authors and their institutions (independent)
- **Context**: Part of the EFP S3 2020 Clinical Practice Guidelines for the treatment of periodontitis

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic review focusing exclusively on resective surgery (root amputation/resection, root separation, tunnelling) for class II and III FI
- Provides survival data pooled across 4–30.8-year follow-up: 1,515/2,021 teeth survived
- Shows SRP and OFD achieve comparable tooth survival to resective surgery for class II–III FI
- Establishes that class II FI responds better to any treatment than class III
- Highlights extreme heterogeneity in outcome data (38–94.4% survival range for resective approaches) — indicates patient selection, operator skill, and defect morphology as major effect modifiers
- Contributes to EFP S3 guideline conclusion that resective surgery is not mandated over conservative management for most FI cases

## 4. Key Results

### Tooth Survival by Treatment

| Treatment | Survival range | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Root amputation / resection / separation | 38–94.4% | 4–30.8 years |
| Tunnelling | 62–67% | — |
| OFD | 63–85% | — |
| SRP | 68–80% | — |
| All treated teeth (overall) | 1,515/2,021 survived | 4–30.8 years |

- Class II FI: better outcomes with all treatment modalities vs class III
- Data "very heterogeneous" regarding follow-up duration and FI distribution
- No superiority of resective surgery over SRP or OFD demonstrated

### Conclusion

Within the limits of available data, SRP and OFD may result in similar survival rates as root amputation/resection, root separation or tunnelling for class II and III FI.

## 5. Limitations

- Only 7 studies met inclusion criteria from 683 screened — limited evidence base
- High heterogeneity in follow-up periods (4 to 30.8 years) and FI distribution across studies
- No meta-analysis possible due to data heterogeneity
- Outcome measure: tooth survival (long-term clinical composite); shorter-term clinical outcomes (PAL gain, PPD) are secondary and sparse
- Search cutoff December 2018 — studies after that not captured
- Spectrum of operator expertise and patient maintenance programs not standardized across studies

## 7. Glossary

- **Furcation Involvement (FI, 치근이개부 침범)**: Destruction of periodontium at the furcation of multi-rooted teeth; classified as class I (early horizontal, <3 mm), II (horizontal ≥3 mm, not through-and-through), III (through-and-through)
- **Root amputation/resection (근절단술)**: Surgical removal of one or more roots of a multi-rooted tooth while retaining the crown
- **Root separation (근분리술)**: Sectioning of a multi-rooted tooth into single-rooted units, each retaining its own crown portion
- **Tunnelling (터널링)**: Surgical creation of an accessible space through the furcation of a grade III FI tooth for cleaning
- **SRP (Scaling and Root Planing, 치석제거 및 치근활택술)**: Non-surgical periodontal debridement
- **OFD (Open Flap Debridement, 개방성 판막 변연 절제술)**: Periodontal flap surgery with debridement but without regenerative or resective procedures
- **EFP S3 (European Federation of Periodontology S3 Guidelines, EFP S3 임상진료지침)**: Highest-evidence-level clinical practice guidelines for periodontitis treatment (2020 version)
