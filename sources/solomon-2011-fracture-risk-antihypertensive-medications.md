## Why Ingested
Largest human cohort (n=376,061) quantifying differential fracture risk by antihypertensive drug class — independently confirms ARB and thiazide bone-protective effects suggested by Saravi 2021 ISQ data.
→ [[overviews/hypertension-antihypertensive-implant-outcomes-overview]]

## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort of 376,061 Medicare hypertension patients: ARB (HR 0.76) and thiazide diuretics (HR 0.85) significantly reduced fracture risk vs CCB; BB, ACEi, and loop diuretics did not differ significantly.

## 한줄요약
후향적 코호트 (n=376,061 Medicare 수혜자): ARB (HR 0.76) 및 치아자이드 (HR 0.85)가 CCB 대비 골절 위험 유의하게 낮음 — 가장 큰 n 인간 코호트에서 약물 클래스별 골 보호 효과 확인.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 376,061 Medicare beneficiaries with hypertension beginning single-drug antihypertensive therapy; adjusted Cox proportional hazards regression with CCB as reference group; osteoporotic fracture events captured via healthcare utilisation claims data; overall fracture rate 35.2/1000 person-years.

ARB significantly reduced fracture risk vs CCB (HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.68–0.86); thiazide diuretics also protective (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.76–0.97); loop diuretics had the highest fracture rate (49.0/1000 person-years); beta-blockers and ACEi showed no significant fracture risk difference from CCB.

Largest human cohort confirming drug-class specificity of antihypertensive bone effects — ARB and thiazide protective, loop diuretics harmful; aligns with Saravi 2021 (RAAS protective, BB null) and Shimizu 2008 (ARB mechanistic basis); informs implant patient risk stratification by antihypertensive drug class.

## 세줄요약

Medicare 수혜자 후향코호트(n=376,061); 단일 항고혈압약 시작 환자; 칼슘채널차단제 (CCB)를 기준군으로 한 보정 콕스 비례위험 회귀분석; 의료비 청구 데이터로 골다공증 골절 포착; 전체 골절률 35.2/1000인·년.

ARB: 골절 위험 유의하게 낮음 (위험비 (Hazard Ratio, HR) 0.76, 95% 신뢰구간 0.68–0.86); 치아자이드: HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.76–0.97); 루프이뇨제: 최고 골절률(49.0/1000인·년); 베타차단제·안지오텐신전환효소억제제(ACEi): 유의한 차이 없음.

최대 규모 인간 코호트에서 항고혈압약 클래스별 골 보호 효과 이질성 확인 — ARB·치아자이드 보호, 루프이뇨제 해로움; Saravi 2021(RAAS 보호·BB 무효)·Shimizu 2008(ARB 기전)과 일치; 고혈압 환자 임플란트 위험 계층화에 적용 가능.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Largest human cohort (n=376,061) comparing fracture risk across all AHT drug classes
- ARB and thiazide independently reduce fracture risk vs CCB reference — largest-n evidence to date
- Loop diuretics carry highest fracture risk (fracture rate 49.0/1000 person-years)
- BB and ACEi show no significant fracture risk reduction — aligns with Saravi 2021 null result for BB on ISQ
- Provides epidemiological grounding for drug-class specificity of bone effects

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Retrospective cohort
- Population: n=376,061 Medicare beneficiaries, diagnosis of hypertension, no prior AHT prescription in 365 days, beginning single-drug AHT therapy
- Follow-up: health care utilization data for osteoporotic fracture events
- Analysis: Adjusted Cox proportional hazards regression; reference group = CCB users
- Covariates: relevant comorbidities and comedications accessible in claims data

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Overall fracture rate: 35.2/1000 person-years (95% CI 34.4–36.1)
- Thiazide diuretics: lowest rate 28.5/1000 (95% CI 25.4–31.9)
- Loop diuretics: highest rate 49.0/1000 (95% CI 46.1–52.1)
- ARB vs CCB: HR 0.76 (95% CI 0.68–0.86) — significant
- Thiazide vs CCB: HR 0.85 (95% CI 0.76–0.97) — significant
- Loop diuretic, BB, ACEi vs CCB: NS

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Claims data only — cannot adjust for bone mineral density, smoking, alcohol, fall risk
- CCB as reference may not be ideal (CCB itself may affect bone)
- Residual confounding (AHT users may differ systematically in diet, exercise, other medication use)
- Only fracture events as proxy for bone health — no BMD or microarchitecture data

## 6. Related Work
- Saravi 2021: RAAS inhibitors → ISQ +3.59 (BB null result aligned with this study)
- Aşantoğrl 2024: mandibular FD improvement across AHT classes including ARB
- Shimizu 2008: mechanistic basis for ARB bone protection (olmesartan blocks Ang II → RANKL pathway)

## 7. Glossary
- HR: Hazard Ratio; values <1.0 indicate reduced fracture risk vs reference (CCB)
- Person-years: composite of number of subjects and length of follow-up
- CCB (Calcium Channel Blocker): reference group for Cox regression comparisons
