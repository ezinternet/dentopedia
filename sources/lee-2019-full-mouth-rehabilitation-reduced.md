## Why Ingested

Demonstrates an integrated digital dentistry workflow — CBCT-guided implant surgery, Arcus Digma jaw-movement recording, CAD/CAM PMMA provisionals, and double-scan zirconia final restorations — applied to a complex case of reduced occlusal vertical dimension (OVD). Extends [[wiki/digital-workflow/fouda-2025-accuracy-digital-workflow-implant-fullarch]] by showing clinical translation of the digital full-arch pipeline in a partially dentate patient where OVD recovery adds an extra decision layer.

## Three-line Summary

(Line 1: Case report — single 78-year-old Korean female with severely reduced OVD due to multiple tooth loss and generalized attrition, treated at Seoul National University Prosthodontics.)
(Line 2: OVD raised 2.5 mm guided by freeway space 7 mm, Willis measurement, and phonetics; 10 implants placed with CBCT-fabricated surgical guides; CAD/CAM PMMA provisionals produced without chairside occlusal adjustment; final full-arch zirconia restorations achieved canine guidance right / group function left.)
(Line 3: Digital workflow — particularly the scan-merge of provisional + abutment models — eliminated intraoral occlusal adjustment at final delivery; night-guard prescribed for long-term protection; follow-up duration not specified.)

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: J Korean Acad Prosthodont 2019;57(4):456-66
- **DOI**: 10.4047/jkap.2019.57.4.456
- **Institution**: Department of Prosthodontics, School of Dentistry, Seoul National University, Seoul, Republic of Korea

## 2. Key Contributions
- Systematic OVD determination protocol combining freeway space (~7 mm), Willis anthropometric measurement, and phonetic assessment — resulting in 2.5 mm of OVD raise at the canine zenith level
- Full digital implant planning pipeline: CBCT + provisional model IOS scan merged in Implant Studio → OneGuide surgical guide → fully guided multi-implant surgery in partially dentate arch
- Occlusal surface generation via N.A.T. (Naturgemäße Aufwachs-Technik) wax-up + N.F.R. (Natural Functional Reconstruction) with articulator condylar parameters derived from Arcus Digma 2 electronic jaw movement recording
- Double-scan CAD/CAM workflow: provisional wax-up scan + abutment scan merged to replicate desired occlusal morphology and anterior guidance in PMMA provisionals, then transferred to final zirconia with no chairside adjustment
- Hybrid occlusal scheme: mutual protection (canine guidance) on natural tooth side, group function on implant-supported side — to distribute lateral loads away from single cantilever implant canine

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Case report
- **n**: 1 patient (78-year-old female)
- **Setting**: University hospital prosthodontics department, Seoul, Republic of Korea
- **Treatment sequence**: extraction → OVD assessment → gothic arch CR recording → Arcus Digma mandibular movement recording → diagnostic wax-up → crown-lengthening surgery (intentional RCT #24–26) → implant planning (Implant Studio) → surgical guide fabrication (OneGuide, Osstem) → guided implant surgery (10 implants: #14,15,16,23,27,35,36,37,46,47) → free gingival graft + apically repositioned flap (mandible) → impressions + provisional restorations II (CAD/CAM PMMA) → 2-month provisional stabilization → final zirconia restorations (posterior first, then anterior)
- **Outcomes**: OVD restoration, occlusal stability, functional and esthetic satisfaction, absence of postoperative complications at case-report endpoint

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- OVD increase: 2.5 mm (at canine zenith reference)
- Freeway space at habitual occlusion: ~7 mm → adequate freeway space confirmed at new OVD
- Implants placed: 10 (with fully guided OneGuide system)
- PMMA provisional delivery: no intraoral occlusal adjustment required at seating
- Provisional monitoring: 2 months — occlusion stable, functionally and esthetically satisfactory
- Final occlusal scheme: right side — canine guidance (mutual protection), left implant canine — group function
- Final zirconia cementation: posterior (full zirconia) with FujiCEM 2 (resin-reinforced GIC); anterior (cut-back zirconia + porcelain) with Variolink (resin cement)
- Postoperative panoramic and TMJ radiographs: no pathologic findings

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single case report — no control, no randomization, n=1; findings are not generalizable as efficacy evidence
- Follow-up duration not explicitly stated; long-term survival, MBL, and occlusal stability not reported
- Gothic arch apex / habitual closure coincided in this patient — success of CR determination may not generalize to patients with larger habitual-closure deflections
- Patient had mild osteoporosis (non-medicated); potential bone quality confound for implant osseointegration not assessed
- Night-guard prescribed but compliance and protective efficacy not evaluated

## 7. Glossary
- **OVD (Occlusal Vertical Dimension, 수직고경)**: Vertical height of the lower face when teeth are in maximum intercuspation
- **Freeway space (자유공극)**: Difference between rest vertical dimension and OVD; normal ~2–4 mm
- **Willis measurement (Willis 안면 계측)**: Anthropometric method comparing pupil-to-mouth-corner distance with lower face height for OVD estimation
- **Gothic arch tracing (고딕 아치 기록)**: Intraoral tracing device to locate centric relation (CR) as the apex of the arrow-head tracing
- **CR (Centric Relation, 중심위)**: Most retruded, reproducible position of the condyles in the glenoid fossa
- **Arcus Digma 2**: Electronic jaw-movement recording device (KaVo); records sagittal condylar guidance angle and lateral condylar guidance angle for articulator programming
- **N.A.T. (Naturgemäße Aufwachs-Technik)**: Anatomical wax-up technique producing natural tooth morphology layer by layer
- **N.F.R. (Natural Functional Reconstruction)**: Technique using articulator movement to generate functional wear facets on wax-up surfaces
- **OneGuide (Osstem)**: Fully guided, tooth- and mucosa-supported surgical template system for CBCT-planned implant placement
- **Implant Studio (3Shape)**: Software platform for implant position planning using CBCT + IOS data merge
- **T-Scan**: Digital occlusal analysis system measuring relative force magnitude and timing across teeth
- **Double-scan technique**: Workflow scanning the provisional restoration on diagnostic cast + the prepared abutment cast separately, then merging both datasets to transfer provisional morphology into CAD design
- **PMMA (Polymethyl methacrylate, 폴리메틸메타크릴레이트)**: Acrylic resin block used for CAD/CAM-milled provisional restorations
- **FujiCEM 2**: Resin-reinforced glass-ionomer cement (GC) used for posterior zirconia crown cementation
- **Variolink**: Resin cement (Ivoclar) used for esthetic anterior zirconia restoration bonding
- **Group function occlusion (군기능교합)**: Lateral occlusal scheme where multiple teeth on the working side contact simultaneously during excursion; preferred on implant-supported canines to distribute lateral forces
- **Mutual protection occlusion / Canine guidance (상호보호교합·견치유도)**: Posterior teeth protect anterior teeth in CR; anterior teeth guide posteriors off contact in excursion; preferred on natural tooth canine side
