## Three-line Summary

Preclinical animal study (UNESP, Brazil, Braz Oral Res 2025; n=72 Wistar rats) comparing **Ossix Plus** (sugar-glycation cross-linked porcine collagen membrane, GLYMATRIX) vs **Bio-Gide** (non-cross-linked bovine collagen) vs blood clot (negative control) in 8 mm critical-size rat calvarial defects at 7, 15, 30, and 60 days, using histometry, immunohistochemistry (OC, OP), and micro-CT.

At 30 days Bio-Gide had the most bone formation; at **60 days Ossix Plus reversed this, achieving a bone volume (BV) of 17.33 ± 1.74 mm³ vs Bio-Gide 8.06 ± 1.26 mm³ (p<0.05)** and superior BV/TV (45.67% vs 23.69%), Tb.Th (0.214 vs 0.105 mm), and intersection surface.

The authors attribute Ossix Plus's delayed but superior performance to sugar-glycation cross-linking prolonging the barrier function (maintains structural integrity through 60 days while Bio-Gide begins degrading), allowing a longer protected healing window; Ossix Plus showed less early vascularization but greater M2 macrophage polarization, creating a more sustained anti-inflammatory regenerative environment.

## 세줄요약

전임상 동물연구 (UNESP, Braz Oral Res 2025, n=72) — Ossix Plus(당가교 돼지 콜라겐) vs Bio-Gide(비가교 소 콜라겐) 8mm 흰쥐 두개골 임계결손. 30일엔 Bio-Gide 우세, 60일엔 **Ossix Plus 역전** (BV 17.33 vs 8.06 mm³, p<0.05).

기전: 당가교가 멤브레인 수명을 연장(60일에도 구조 유지) → 더 긴 보호 창 → 더 많은 최종 골량. Bio-Gide는 초기(7-30일) 혈관화·골형성 빠르나 30일 후 분해.

임상 함의: **큰 결손·긴 치유 기간이 필요한 경우** Ossix Plus(GLYMATRIX) 플랫폼이 Bio-Gide보다 유리. 소결손·단기 치유엔 Bio-Gide도 충분.

## 3. Proposed Mechanism

**Why Ossix Plus wins long-term despite slower start:**

1. **Barrier durability**: Sugar-glycation (GLYMATRIX) resists enzymatic degradation; membrane intact at 60 days, whereas Bio-Gide begins degrading after ~30 days
2. **M2 macrophage polarization**: Ossix Plus favors anti-inflammatory M2 phenotype → sustained regenerative environment (per Radenkovic et al.)
3. **Slower but sustained vascularization**: Less early angiogenesis than Bio-Gide, but stable regenerative milieu longer
4. **Space maintenance prolonged**: The non-porous Ossix Plus maintains space compression-free through 60 days

**Bio-Gide faster initially because:**
- Porous two-layer structure allows immediate vascularization and cell infiltration
- Faster resorption → rapid early bone formation but protected window closes sooner

## 4. Limitations

- Rat calvaria not load-bearing; cannot translate directly to jaw/ridge augmentation
- No graft material — clinically most GBR uses bone substitute + membrane
- Animal model; human healing dynamics may differ
- Short follow-up (60 days maximum); long-term remodeling not assessed
