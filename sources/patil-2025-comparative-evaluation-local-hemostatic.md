## Why Ingested

[[drug/anticoagulants/guardieiro-2023-chitosan-cellulose-hemostasis-dapt-rct]] and [[drug/anticoagulants/tang-2025-chitosan-antibacterial-hemostatic-sponge-extraction]] both evaluate chitosan as a single hemostatic agent; this RCT provides the first head-to-head comparison of four distinct local agents (Botroclot, chitosan, adrenaline, tranexamic acid) against gauze in healthy patients, quantifying the relative hemostatic speed of each with identical technique.

## One-line Summary

Parallel-group RCT (n=60 healthy molar extractions) demonstrating Botroclot achieves the shortest hemostasis time (0.87 min), followed by TXA (0.93 min) and chitosan (1.13 min), all significantly faster than pressure gauze (1.80 min; p=0.001).

## 한줄요약

건강 환자 대구치 발치 RCT (n=60): Botroclot(0.87분) > TXA(0.93분) > 키토산(1.13분) > 에피네프린(1.38분) > 거즈(1.80분) 순으로 지혈 빠르고, Botroclot·TXA·키토산이 거즈보다 유의하게 우수(p=0.001).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First 5-arm RCT directly comparing Botroclot (hemocoagulase), chitosan, adrenaline, tranexamic acid, and saline gauze under identical extraction conditions in healthy patients
- Botroclot showed the fastest hemostasis; TXA and chitosan were statistically equivalent to each other and superior to gauze (p=0.001)
- Adrenaline was not statistically different from gauze (p=0.027 only vs gauze); its vasoconstrictive effect showed time-dependent correlation with bleeding time (r=−0.57, p=0.018)
- Chitosan and Botroclot showed no significant correlation between baseline bleeding time and observation time, suggesting mechanism-independent speed
- No postoperative rebleeding or adverse events in any group across all 60 patients

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Parallel-group RCT; 5 arms, n=12 per arm; Feb–Apr 2025, Dhule, India
- **Population**: Systemically healthy, aged 20–50 years; first/second molar extraction indicated for periodontal, carious, or traumatic reasons; normal BT, CT, INR, CBC required
- **Exclusion**: Anticoagulant/antiplatelet therapy, bleeding disorders, systemic illness, allergy to any test agent
- **Randomization**: Computer-generated sequence (R v4.3.2); allocation concealment via SNOSE; blinding of assessors and patients (operator unblinded due to application differences)
- **Interventions**: G1 Botroclot (hemocoagulase 0.2 CU + CHX 0.1%); G2 Celox™ chitosan; G3 adrenaline 1:1,000; G4 TXA (Tranexa); G5 saline-soaked gauze — all applied passively without external pressure
- **Primary outcome**: Observation time (OT) = minutes to complete hemostasis
- **Secondary**: Postoperative rebleeding at 30 min; baseline BT/CT correlation with OT
- **Statistics**: One-way ANOVA, Tukey post-hoc, Pearson correlation; significance p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Agent | Mean OT ± SD (min) | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| Botroclot | 0.87 ± 0.33 | 0.65–1.08 |
| TXA | 0.93 ± 0.39 | 0.68–1.18 |
| Chitosan | 1.13 ± 0.27 | 0.96–1.30 |
| Adrenaline | 1.38 ± 0.25 | 1.23–1.54 |
| Gauze (control) | 1.80 ± 0.41 | 1.54–2.06 |

- ANOVA F=15.45, p=0.001 (highly significant overall)
- Post-hoc significant: Botroclot vs Adrenaline (p=0.004), Botroclot vs Gauze (p=0.001), Chitosan vs Gauze (p=0.001), TXA vs Gauze (p=0.001), TXA vs Adrenaline (p=0.014)
- Not significant: Botroclot vs TXA (p=0.992), Botroclot vs Chitosan (p=0.331), Chitosan vs TXA (p=0.593)
- No postoperative rebleeding in any group

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small per-arm sample (n=12); underpowered for subgroup analyses (sex, age)
- Healthy patients only — results not directly applicable to anticoagulated or bleeding-disordered patients
- Operator blinding not feasible; assessor-blinded design partially mitigates this
- Single-center, single-operator design
- No long-term healing assessment

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary

- **Botroclot**: hemocoagulase derived from Bothrops atrox snake venom (batroxobin/BTX) + 0.1% chlorhexidine; cleaves fibrinogen → fibrin monomers without requiring clotting factors
- **Observation time (OT)**: interval from hemostatic agent application to complete cessation of visible bleeding
- **BT/CT**: bleeding time (Ivy method) / clotting time (capillary tube method)
