## Why Ingested

The wiki already holds [[complete-denture/milic-lemic-2025-single-two-implant-mandibular-overdenture-umbrella-review]], an umbrella review comparing single- vs two-implant mandibular overdenture *designs* (SIMO vs TIMO) on implant survival/MBL/satisfaction. Sharka 2019 asks a different, higher-level question — conventional complete dentures (CCDs) vs implant-retained overdentures (IRODs) as a whole, on OHRQoL and satisfaction — so it fills a gap one level up in the decision tree (treatment-modality choice, not implant-count-within-overdenture choice). It also predates and provides historical/foundational context for the newer, larger synthesis in [[overviews/complete-denture-digital-overdenture-overview]], which already states "IFCD vs IOD PRO equivalent" from more recent SR+MA-level papers; Sharka's earlier (2019) umbrella-of-umbrella-reviews evidence base is a useful check on whether that equivalence/superiority claim has held over time.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella systematic review (JBI methodology) of 8 systematic reviews (6 SR-only, 1 SR+MA, 1 MA) evaluating oral health-related quality of life (OHRQoL) and patient satisfaction in edentulous patients treated with conventional complete dentures (CCDs) vs implant-retained overdentures (IRODs).

IRODs showed superiority over CCDs on both OHRQoL and satisfaction outcomes across the included reviews (evidence level 1+ to 1- per SIGN); the advantage was most pronounced when patients specifically wanted implant treatment or could not adapt to CCDs.

Financial factors and individual adaptive capability moderate patient tolerance of both prosthesis types; this is an abstract-only ingest (no PMC/PDF full text available, Wiley paywalled) so per-review breakdown and quality-appraisal detail could not be extracted.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Applies the Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) umbrella-review methodology to synthesize existing systematic reviews (rather than primary studies) comparing CCDs and IRODs on patient-reported OHRQoL/satisfaction outcomes.
- Searched Medline, Google Scholar, and Cochrane Library, plus PROSPERO for ongoing/recent reviews — a multi-database umbrella-review search strategy.
- Graded the included reviews' evidence level using the Scottish Intercollegiate Guidelines Network (SIGN) framework (range: 1+ to 1-).
- Concludes IRODs are superior to CCDs on OHRQoL and satisfaction, with the effect size moderated by patient treatment-demand and adaptive capability — introducing a patient-centered/behavioral qualifier rather than a blanket superiority claim.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella systematic review (review of systematic reviews), JBI methodology.
- **Databases**: Medline, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library; PROSPERO searched for ongoing/recently completed reviews.
- **Inclusion criterion**: Systematic reviews (with or without meta-analysis) reporting OHRQoL and/or patient satisfaction as outcomes for edentulous patients treated with CCDs and/or IRODs.
- **Quality/evidence grading**: SIGN levels of evidence applied to each of the 8 included reviews (range 1+ to 1-).
- No PRISMA flow diagram, AMSTAR score, or per-review numeric data were retrievable from the abstract alone (full text not accessible).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 8 reviews included in data synthesis: 6 SR without meta-analysis, 1 SR+MA, 1 MA.
- Evidence level across included reviews: SIGN 1+ to 1-.
- Overall direction of effect: IRODs superior to CCDs for both OHRQoL and patient satisfaction.
- Effect modifiers reported: patient demand for implant treatment, inability to adapt to CCDs, financial factors, and individual adaptive capability.
- No pooled effect sizes, confidence intervals, or per-domain OHIP scores are available from the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingest**: full text is paywalled (Wiley); no PMC copy exists. Per-review AMSTAR/SIGN item-level detail, PRISMA flow, and any quantitative pooled estimates are not extractable from this record.
- Umbrella-of-reviews design inherits the limitations of its 8 constituent reviews (heterogeneous inclusion criteria, mixed SR/SR+MA/MA designs, and only one meta-analysis among the eight).
- 2019 publication — predates several newer SR+MA-level papers already in the wiki on IFCD/IOD equivalence (see [[overviews/complete-denture-digital-overdenture-overview]]); should be read as historical/foundational context rather than the most current quantitative estimate.

## 7. Glossary

- **OHRQoL**: Oral Health-Related Quality of Life — patient-reported impact of oral health/disease/treatment on daily functioning and wellbeing, commonly measured with instruments like OHIP (Oral Health Impact Profile).
- **CCD**: Complete Conventional Denture — full removable denture with no implant support.
- **IROD**: Implant-Retained Overdenture — removable prosthesis retained/stabilized by one or more dental implants.
- **JBI methodology**: Joanna Briggs Institute framework for conducting systematic and umbrella reviews.
- **SIGN levels of evidence**: Scottish Intercollegiate Guidelines Network grading scale (1++ to 4) for study/review quality; 1+ and 1- denote well-conducted to lower-quality meta-analyses/SRs of RCTs.
- **Umbrella review**: A review that synthesizes multiple existing systematic reviews on a topic, rather than primary studies.
