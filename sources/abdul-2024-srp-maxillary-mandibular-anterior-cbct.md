## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT cross-sectional study (Diagnostics 2024) assessing sagittal root position (SRP) of both maxillary and mandibular anterior teeth in the alveolar bone — a rare combined-arch dataset from an Iraqi subpopulation.

SRP class distributions and root-to-bone angulations were reported for both arches; mandibular anterior teeth showed distinct SRP patterns compared to maxillary, with direct implications for immediate implant placement trajectory planning.

Mandibular anterior SRP data fills a key gap — most existing evidence covers maxillary teeth only — expanding pre-surgical anatomical assessment for esthetic-zone immediate implant cases in both arches.

## 세줄요약

후향적 CBCT 횡단 연구 (Diagnostics 2024): 상악 및 하악 전치의 시상근 위치 (Sagittal Root Position, SRP) 분류를 단일 코호트에서 함께 분석한 드문 양악 데이터셋.

상악·하악 전치 모두에서 SRP 등급 분포 및 치근-골 각도 보고; 하악 전치는 상악과 다른 SRP 패턴을 보여 즉시 식립 계획 시 궤적 설계에 영향.

하악 전치 SRP 데이터 부재를 보완 — 기존 근거는 상악 중심 → 심미 영역 즉시 식립 시 양악 모두 CBCT 기반 개별 평가 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
One of the few datasets that explicitly applies SRP classification to mandibular anterior teeth, useful for the less-studied mandibular esthetic-zone IIP.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Retrospective CBCT review. SRP class and root-to-bone angulation measured for maxillary and mandibular anterior teeth.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Prevalence distributions reported for both arches. Mandibular anterior teeth often show distinct patterns vs maxillary, with implications for trajectory planning.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
Cross-sectional anatomy-only; single population.

## 7. Glossary
- **IIP:** Immediate Implant Placement — placement at the time of tooth extraction.
- **IIPP:** Immediate Implant Placement and Provisionalization — IIP combined with same-visit provisional restoration.
- **SRP:** Sagittal Root Position — Kan I/II/III/IV classification of root-to-bone relationship.
- **CBCT:** Cone-Beam Computed Tomography.
- **PES/WES:** Pink/White Esthetic Score — esthetic outcome indices for anterior implants.
- **EMBL:** Early Marginal Bone Loss.
