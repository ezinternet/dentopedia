## Why Ingested

Addresses the combined mechanical + hydrothermal aging interaction — a gap in the LTD literature where most studies use chemical aging alone. Directly relevant to the clinical failure mechanism in [[dental-materials/zirconia/koenig-2024-ltd-monolithic-zirconia-5year-prospective]] where both LTD and masticatory loads are present.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (3 generations of dental zirconia: 1st-gen VITA YZ, 2nd-gen INCORIS TZI, 3rd-gen KATANA UTML; combined aging: hydrothermal 134°C/20h + step-stress mechanical fatigue): continuous HT+fatigue cycling reduced VITA YZ fatigue strength (516±38 MPa), while an alternating HT-fatigue protocol INCREASED it (730±58 MPa).

KATANA UTML (3rd-gen, high cubic) showed no differences between aging protocols and no t→m transformation — confirming cubic-dominant composition provides combined aging resistance.

INCORIS TZI showed highest fatigue strength (810±76 MPa) and survival cycles (147,000) after combined aging — the best combined mechanical + LTD performer among tested materials.

## 세줄요약

지르코니아 3세대(1세대 VITA YZ, 2세대 INCORIS TZI, 3세대 KATANA UTML), 복합 에이징(수열 134°C/20h + 단계응력 기계피로): 연속 HT+피로가 VITA YZ 피로강도 감소(516±38 MPa), 교대 프로토콜은 오히려 증가(730±58 MPa).

KATANA UTML(3세대, 고큐빅): 에이징 프로토콜 간 차이 없고 t→m 미발생 — 큐빅 위주 조성의 복합 에이징 저항 확인.

INCORIS TZI가 복합 에이징 최고 성능: 810±76 MPa, 147,000 사이클 — 임상적 기계+LTD 복합 열화에 최강.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to use alternating hydrothermal-mechanical fatigue protocol (AFA) to simulate cyclical intraoral aging
- Demonstrates paradoxical strength INCREASE with alternating protocol vs continuous aging for 1st-gen zirconia
- Confirms 3rd-generation (high-translucency/cubic-dominant) zirconia resistance to both chemical and mechanical combined aging
- Provides Kaplan-Meier survival analysis for clinical fatigue data interpretation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 3 zirconia types (1st, 2nd, 3rd generation commercial products)
- 3 aging protocols: CF (control, fatigue only), AF (134°C/20h HT → fatigue), AFA (alternating: 4×5h HT intercalated with fatigue)
- Step-stress mechanical fatigue: biaxial piston-on-3-balls setup, 100 MPa initial + 50 MPa steps/10,000 cycles, 20 Hz
- Kaplan-Meier + Mantel-Cox (α=0.05) + Weibull analysis
- Fractography: stereo + SEM + XRD on fractured specimens

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Material | Protocol | Fatigue Strength (MPa) | Cycles |
|---|---|---|---|
| VITA YZ (1st gen) | AF (continuous) | 516 ± 38 | — |
| VITA YZ (1st gen) | AFA (alternating) | 730 ± 58 | — |
| INCORIS TZI (2nd gen) | AF | 810 ± 76 | 147,000 |
| KATANA UTML (3rd gen) | AF/AFA | No difference | No t→m |

- Fracture origin in all specimens: tensile side at pre-existing defects
- KATANA UTML: no t→m even after 134°C/20h aging — cubic dominance confirmed
- INCORIS TZI: highest fatigue strength + survival rate after combined aging

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In-vitro only; no clinical follow-up
- Stepstress approach accelerates failure artificially
- Only tested 3 materials; newer ultra-translucent zirconias not included

## 7. Glossary

- **Step-stress approach (단계응력법)**: 하중 단계적 증가로 피로 수명 단시간 측정; 임상 피로 가속화
- **AFA protocol (교대 프로토콜)**: HT 에이징과 기계피로를 교대 반복 — 현실 구강 주기 모사
- **Kaplan-Meier**: 생존분석법; 피로파절 발생 시간 분포를 그룹 간 비교
