## Why Ingested

Evaluates cold atmospheric pressure plasma (CAPP) as a surface treatment for CAD-CAM zirconia prior to resin cementation, comparing it against sandblasting, laser, and MDP primer combinations. Directly relevant to clinical bonding decisions for zirconia restorations.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/kamis-2023-cold-plasma-zirconia-resin-cement-bond]]

## Three-line Summary

Cold atmospheric pressure plasma (CAPP) alone does not significantly improve shear bond strength (SBS) of resin cement to zirconia, but CAPP combined with 10-MDP primer (PPR) yields significantly higher SBS than untreated controls. Sandblasting + primer (SPR) and laser + plasma + primer (LPPR) groups also outperformed untreated controls. CAPP increases zirconia surface wettability (lower contact angle), which facilitates MDP primer penetration and bonding.

## Document Information

- **Journal:** American Journal of Dentistry (Am J Dent)
- **Volume/Issue:** 36(5): 251-259
- **PMID:** 37865813
- **DOI:** Not available
- **Study design:** In vitro, 9-group comparative study
- **Material:** Vita YZ-HT zirconia discs (n=189, 21 per group)
- **Resin cements tested:** RelyX Ultimate Clicker (adhesive), TheraCem (self-adhesive)

## Key Contributions

1. Demonstrates that CAPP alone improves wettability (contact angle reduction) but is insufficient to significantly increase SBS without a chemical primer.
2. Shows PPR (plasma + primer) achieves SBS comparable to or exceeding SPR (sandblasting + primer) in the TheraCem group.
3. Provides surface roughness (Ra) and contact angle data alongside SBS, clarifying the mechanism: wettability, not roughness alone, is the key mediator for plasma's benefit.
4. Establishes that adding laser to plasma + primer (LPPR) does not provide a meaningful additive benefit over PPR alone.

## Methodology

- **Specimens:** 189 Vita YZ-HT zirconia discs, sintered and polished
- **Groups (n=21 each):**
  1. Untreated (U)
  2. Sandblasting (S) — 50 µm Al₂O₃
  3. Laser (L)
  4. Plasma (P) — cold atmospheric pressure plasma (CAPP)
  5. Primer (PR) — 10-MDP primer only
  6. Sandblasting + Primer (SPR)
  7. Laser + Primer (LPR)
  8. Plasma + Primer (PPR)
  9. Laser + Plasma + Primer (LPPR)
- **Cements:** RelyX Ultimate Clicker (adhesive resin cement) OR TheraCem (self-adhesive resin cement)
- **Measurements:** Surface roughness (Ra), contact angle (wettability), shear bond strength (SBS)
- **Statistical analysis:** Comparative analysis across groups

## Key Results

- **Surface roughness (Ra):** S, PR, SPR, PPR, and LPPR groups showed significantly greater Ra than the untreated group; L and P alone did not significantly increase Ra.
- **Contact angle (wettability):** S, P, and L groups showed significantly lower contact angles vs. U, indicating improved wettability.
- **Shear bond strength:** SPR, PPR, and LPPR groups were significantly greater than U; CAPP alone (P group) did not significantly differ from U.
- **Cement comparison:** TheraCem self-adhesive cement showed different response patterns vs. RelyX adhesive cement across groups.
- **Conclusion:** CAPP improves wettability but requires MDP primer to translate into improved bond strength.

## Limitations

- In vitro study only; no aging/thermocycling data for long-term durability assessment.
- Single zirconia brand (Vita YZ-HT, 3Y-TZP); generalizability to other zirconia compositions (4Y, 5Y multilayer) unclear.
- DOI not available in PubMed metadata; source verified by PMID 37865813.
- Abstract-only source; quantitative SBS values not extractable from abstract.

## Glossary

- **CAPP (Cold Atmospheric Pressure Plasma):** Non-thermal plasma generated at atmospheric pressure and near room temperature; produces reactive oxygen/nitrogen species (ROS/RNS) that modify surface chemistry and wettability without significant heat damage.
- **10-MDP (10-methacryloyloxydecyl dihydrogen phosphate):** Functional monomer in zirconia primers that forms chemical bonds with the ZrO₂ surface via phosphate ester linkage.
- **SBS (Shear Bond Strength):** Mechanical test measuring resistance to shear forces at the cement–substrate interface.
- **Ra (Arithmetic mean surface roughness):** Average deviation of surface profile from the mean line; higher Ra indicates greater micromechanical interlocking potential.
- **Contact angle:** Angle formed between a liquid droplet and the solid surface; lower contact angle = better wettability = improved adhesive penetration.
- **TheraCem:** Self-adhesive resin cement (BeautiCem/TheraCem brand) that bonds without separate adhesive application.
- **RelyX Ultimate Clicker:** Adhesive resin cement (3M ESPE) requiring separate adhesive/primer step.
