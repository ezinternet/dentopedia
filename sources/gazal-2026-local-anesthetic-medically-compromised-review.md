## Three-line Summary

Comprehensive narrative review (35 studies, 2000–2025; Saudi J Anaesth 2026) synthesizing evidence on local anesthetic selection across nine systemic disease categories in dental patients, introducing a structured condition-specific clinical decision table as the first unified reference of its kind.

Key recommendations: lidocaine for pregnancy (Category B) and diabetes; mepivacaine for cardiovascular/thyroid patients; articaine for renal/hepatic impairment (85–90% plasma metabolism); prilocaine avoided in pregnancy (felypressin uterotonic, fetal methemoglobinemia); bupivacaine avoided in elderly/cardiac; chloroprocaine for amide allergy.

Evidence is heterogeneous (8 observational, 1 RCT, 18 reviews, 8 case reports/guidelines); no large RCTs in multi-comorbid populations — emerging strategies include buffered LA, low-epinephrine articaine, and ultrasound-guided injection.

## 세줄요약

종합 서술적 리뷰 (35편, 2000–2025; Saudi J Anaesth 2026) — 전신질환별 최적 치과 국소마취제 선택 지침; 9개 질환 카테고리에 걸친 최초의 통합 임상결정표 제시.

핵심 권고: 임신·당뇨 = 리도케인 (Lidocaine, Category B); 심혈관·갑상선 = 메피바카인 (Mepivacaine, 바소콘스트릭터 없이); 신장/간장 = 아티카인 (Articaine, 혈장대사 85–90%); 임신에서 프릴로카인 (Prilocaine)+펠리프레신 금기; 아미드 알레르기 = 클로로프로카인 (Chloroprocaine, 에스터).

근거 이질성 높음 (대규모 RCT 없음); 버퍼드 마취제·저농도 에피네프린·초음파 유도 기술 등 신흥 전략 서술.
