## Why Ingested
기존 [[implants/isq/lages-2018-isq-insertion-torque-correlation-sr]] 리뷰가 IT-ISQ 상관관계를 분석했지만, 임상 결과(생존율·골흡수)별 IT 임계값 구간을 직접 비교한 전향적 데이터가 없었음. 이 논문은 G1 <10 / G2 10–30 / G3 30–45 / G4 ≥45 Ncm 4개 구간의 1년 임상 결과를 제공하며, G3 역설(과토크 임계 직하 구간에서 최악의 생존율)을 처음으로 임상 데이터로 기록한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective 1-year clinical trial of 62 narrow implants (ø2.9mm) in 31 atrophic edentulous mandibles, grouped by insertion torque into four ranges: <10, 10–30, 30–45, and ≥45 Ncm.

G4 (IT ≥45 Ncm) achieved the highest survival rate (95%) and maintained the most stable ISQ; G3 (IT 30–45 Ncm) had the worst survival (64.3%), producing a paradox contradicting linear torque-stability assumptions.

IT did not significantly influence marginal bone loss at 12 months; instead, bone remodeling and cytokine behavior were primarily driven by loading and patient factors, not torque magnitude alone.

## 세줄요약

전향적 1년 임상시험(세경 임플란트 62개, 위축 무치악 하악 31명): 삽입토크(삽입 토크, IT)를 <10, 10–30, 30–45, ≥45 Ncm 4구간으로 분류.

G4(IT ≥45 Ncm) 생존율 95%로 최고, ISQ도 가장 안정적; G3(30–45 Ncm) 생존율 64.3%로 최저 — 선형 토크-안정성 가정에 반하는 역설.

IT는 12개월 변연골 소실에 유의한 영향 없음; 골개조·사이토카인 반응은 토크 크기보다 하중·환자 요인이 주도.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First clinical trial to stratify narrow-diameter implants (NDI) into four discrete IT ranges and track survival/ISQ/cytokines over 12 months
- Identifies G3 paradox: IT 30–45 Ncm yields worst outcomes (64.3% survival), not the lowest IT group
- Demonstrates G4 (≥45 Ncm) produces highest ISQ and positive marginal bone remodeling despite theoretical overcompression concerns
- Shows TNF-α negatively correlates with IT; IL-1β increases with time regardless of IT (loading-driven, not torque-driven)
- Challenges consensus that "optimal IT = 32 Ncm" for atrophic edentulous mandible patients

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Prospective longitudinal clinical trial (1 year)
- **Setting:** Federal University of Pelotas, Brazil (June 2014 – June 2015)
- **Population:** 31 fully edentulous patients with atrophic mandibles (mean age 66.9 years; 21F/10M)
- **Implants:** 62 NDI (ø2.9×10mm Facility® NeoPoros, Neodent), placed between mental foramina
- **IT grouping (manual wrench):**
  - G1: IT <10 Ncm (n=12 implants)
  - G2: IT ≥10 and ≤30 Ncm (n=16)
  - G3: IT >30 and <45 Ncm (n=14)
  - G4: IT ≥45 Ncm (n=20)
- **Loading:** Conventional — Equator abutments + overdentures at 3 months
- **Outcomes:** ISQ (Osstell), PD, GI, PI, BOP, CP, IL-1β, TNF-α (PICF), MBL (panoramic radiograph), survival/success (Misch/Papaspyridakos criteria)
- **Follow-up:** 1, 3, 6, 12 months
- **Statistics:** Shapiro-Wilk, chi-square, Kruskal-Wallis, mixed-effects regression, Kendall τ, Kaplan-Meier

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Survival/Success rates (Kaplan-Meier, 12 months):**
| Group | IT range | n implants | Survival/Success |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | <10 Ncm | 12 | 75% (3 failures at 2 months) |
| G2 | 10–30 Ncm | 16 | 81.3% (3 failures) |
| G3 | 30–45 Ncm | 14 | 64.3% (5 failures) |
| G4 | ≥45 Ncm | 20 | 95% (1 failure at 4 months) |

**ISQ:** G2 and G4 maintained higher ISQ throughout; G1 and G3 showed lower and more variable ISQ. G4 had smallest ISQ dip from baseline (high torque = minor relative instability).

**MBL:** No significant difference between groups at baseline or 12 months. G4 showed only group with positive MBC (+0.29 mm), but not significant.

**Cytokines:**
- IL-1β: rose progressively regardless of IT; peaked at 12 months (~140% above month-1 levels)
- TNF-α: negatively correlated with IT (r = negative); highest in G1 at month 12 (low IT → greater inflammatory response)
- G4 had higher IL-1β at month 3 (p=0.015)

**IT–ISQ correlation:** Weak positive (Rk = 0.252, P=0.01) — IT is a weak predictor of primary ISQ

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample; IT groups unequal in size (post-hoc grouping, not randomized allocation)
- Only narrow-diameter implants (2.9mm) — results may not extrapolate to standard-diameter implants
- Manual wrench for IT measurement lacks precision compared to electronic torquemeters
- G3 worst-outcome finding may reflect confounders (bone type distribution, surgical technique variation at intermediate torques)
- Single operator for surgery; single implant type; atrophic mandible only
- 1-year follow-up insufficient to assess long-term MBL trends
- No histologic analysis to confirm bone necrosis mechanism in G3 failures

## 6. Related Work
- Khayat et al. 2013: IT ≥70 Ncm did not harm osseointegration (contradicts concern about overtorque)
- Marconcini et al. 2018: IT ≥50 Ncm → greater bone resorption and mucosal recession (mandible), especially at 3 years
- Norton 2017: Low IT (5–20 Ncm) → 96.7–100% survival; challenges need for high torque
- Berardini SR 2016: No significant difference in MBL or survival between high vs low IT groups
- Verrastro-Neto et al. 2018: Low IT → enhanced VEGF and OPG at day 7 (angiogenesis-favoring)

## 7. Glossary
- **IT (Insertion Torque, 삽입 토크):** Rotational resistance during implant insertion, measured in Ncm
- **NDI (Narrow-Diameter Implant, 세경 임플란트):** Implant diameter <3.0mm
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient, 임플란트 안정성 지수):** RFA-derived stability score 1–100
- **PICF (Peri-Implant Crevicular Fluid, 임플란트 주위 열구액):** Fluid sampled from peri-implant sulcus for cytokine analysis
- **MBL/MBC:** Marginal Bone Level / Marginal Bone Change
- **G3 paradox:** Intermediate-high torque (30–45 Ncm) producing worst clinical outcomes despite exceeding "optimal 32 Ncm" threshold
- **Osseocompression (골압축):** Bone compression by high-torque implant threading, hypothesized to risk ischemia/necrosis in thick cortical bone
