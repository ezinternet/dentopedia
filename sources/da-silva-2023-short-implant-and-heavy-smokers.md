## Why Ingested

감염 소켓 즉시식립 클러스터의 기존 근거([[immediate-implant/infected-socket/colak-2023-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-retrospective]], [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/de-oliveira-neto-2019-immediate-dental-implants-placed-into]])는 감염 유무 자체의 효과에 초점을 두는데, 이 후향 코호트는 만성 치근단 치주염 발치와 215개를 비감염 208개와 함께 다변량(GEE)으로 분석해 감염 외의 환자·임플란트 수준 실패 예측인자(중흡연, 짧은 임플란트)를 정량화한다. 감염 부위 식립 시 감염 여부 외에 무엇을 조정해야 하는지 위키에 근거를 더하려고 인제스트 (2026-09-29 감염 소켓 서베일런스 배치).

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (single private clinic, Rio de Janeiro, 2006–2018; single surgeon): 423 immediate implants in 186 patients, 215 placed into sockets of teeth with chronic apical periodontitis (CAP) and 208 into sockets without it; mean follow-up 39.4 months.

Overall implant survival 91% (385/423): 88.8% (191/215) in CAP sockets vs 93.3% (194/208) in non-CAP sockets, a difference that was not significant (bivariate p=0.111; GEE adjusted OR 2.36, 95% CI 0.70–7.97, p=0.167), whereas smoking >20 cigarettes/day (adjusted OR 7.66, 95% CI 1.56–37.49, p=0.012) and short implants of 6.0–8.5 mm (adjusted OR 14.06, 95% CI 2.67–73.95, p=0.002) independently predicted failure.

Chronic apical periodontitis was not a significant failure predictor here, but the CIs are wide (only 5 of 26 short implants failed; 3 of 11 heavy smokers), acute or purulent infection was not analyzed, and non-random retrospective records with no lesion-size data limit inference — prefer longer implants and address heavy smoking before immediate placement in infected sites.

## 세줄요약

후향 코호트 (브라질 리우데자네이루 단일 개인치과, 2006–2018, 단일 술자): 환자 186명·즉시식립 임플란트 423개, 그중 215개는 만성 치근단 치주염 (Chronic Apical Periodontitis, CAP) 치아의 발치와, 208개는 CAP 없는 발치와에 식립; 평균 추적 39.4개월.

전체 임플란트 생존율 91% (385/423): CAP 발치와 88.8% (191/215) vs 비CAP 93.3% (194/208)로 유의차 없음 (이변량 p=0.111; GEE 보정 OR 2.36, 95% CI 0.70–7.97, p=0.167). 반면 하루 20개비 초과 흡연 (보정 OR 7.66, 95% CI 1.56–37.49, p=0.012)과 짧은 임플란트 6.0–8.5 mm (보정 OR 14.06, 95% CI 2.67–73.95, p=0.002)는 독립적 실패 예측인자.

이 연구에서 CAP는 유의한 실패 예측인자가 아니었으나 신뢰구간이 넓고 (짧은 임플란트 26개 중 5개, 중흡연자 11명 중 3명 실패), 급성·화농성 감염은 분석되지 않았으며 후향 기록 기반이라 병소 크기 정보도 없다 — 감염 부위 즉시식립 시 더 긴 임플란트를 선택하고 중등도 이상 흡연을 먼저 관리할 것.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: J Clin Exp Dent 2023;15(11):e874-81 (Periodontology section); received 12/12/2022, accepted 25/09/2023.
- DOI 10.4317/jced.60238; PMID 38074166; PMCID PMC10699762.
- Setting: private clinic, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil; records from September 2006 to December 2018; ethics approval CAAE 03928818.5.0000.5284.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Multivariable (GEE logistic) analysis of failure in 423 immediate implants, with chronic apical periodontitis sockets (n=215) contrasted with non-infected sockets (n=208).
- Identifies >20 cigarettes/day and short implants (6.0–8.5 mm) as independent failure predictors regardless of chronic apical periodontitis.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Retrospective cohort from medical records and imaging; all surgeries by one specialist; extraction with luxators/forceps, thorough curettage of granulation tissue, saline irrigation; antibiotic therapy after surgery in all patients, antibiotics before surgery only when needed (e.g. mitral valve prolapse or physician request).
- Inclusion: single or multiple immediate implants, single- or multi-rooted teeth with or without chronic apical periodontitis and periodontal disease, minimum 12 months follow-up after loading, smokers and patients with monitored chronic systemic disease. Exclusion: previously healed sites, history of implant loss/failure, incomplete records.
- Chronic apical periodontitis defined as a radiolucency clearly associated with the root apex on periapical, panoramic or CBCT images; inter-examiner agreement on 50 random images kappa=0.810.
- Sample size (G*Power, w=0.30, power 90%, alpha 0.05): 158 sites. Statistics: chi-square/Fisher/Mann-Whitney bivariate tests; variables with p<0.20 entered a forward stepwise GEE multiple logistic model (clustering of implants within patient); SPSS 21.
- Failure: implant removed by the operator or spontaneously lost; early failure = before prosthetic loading (before first disconnection of provisional when immediately loaded).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 423 implants, 186 patients over 147 months, mean follow-up 39.4 months; mean age 57.1 years (SD 11.37, 176 records). Survival 91% (385) by implant, 91.4% by patient; 93.4% at first 12 months; 300 implants (70.9%) reviewed at 84 months.
- Chronic apical periodontitis sockets: 24/215 failed (88.8% survival) vs 14/208 in non-infected sockets (93.3%); bivariate p=0.111; GEE adjusted OR 2.36 (95% CI 0.70–7.97), p=0.167.
- Smoking >20 cigarettes/day: 3/11 failed (27.3%) vs 9/131 non-smokers (6.9%); adjusted OR 7.66 (1.56–37.49), p=0.012; <=20 cigarettes/day OR 1.31 (0.26–6.58), p=0.745.
- Short implants (6.0–8.5 mm): 5/26 failed (19.2%) vs 33/397 long (9.0–18.0 mm, 8.3%); bivariate p=0.059; adjusted OR 14.06 (2.67–73.95), p=0.002.
- No association with age (p=0.812), sex (p=0.317), systemic condition (p=0.410), diameter (p=0.295), region (p=0.498), insertion torque (p=0.596), prosthesis type (p=0.656) or immediate loading (82 immediately loaded, 9 failed [11.0%], p=0.482).
- Peri-implant status: 71 peri-implantitis cases, 10 failed (14.1%); 102 mucositis cases, all survived; peri-implant condition and prosthetic adaptation (both p<0.0001) were excluded from the GEE for multicollinearity.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Incomplete or patient-reported records; no data on bone volume, grafting/membranes, healing mode (submerged vs transmucosal), plaque index, other habits, prior periodontal condition, apical lesion size or oral hygiene.
- Infected group is chronic apical periodontitis only (radiographic diagnosis); acute infection, purulence and periodontal-origin infection are not analyzed as separate strata.
- Wide CIs from few events (38 failures overall; 26 short implants, 11 heavy smokers); a single operator limits generalizability. Authors call for prospective studies with pre-determined protocols and maintenance programs.
- The paper's running header/abstract wording ("infected sockets") refers throughout to chronic apical periodontitis.
