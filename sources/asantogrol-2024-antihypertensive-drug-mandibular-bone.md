## Why Ingested
First human radiographic study measuring mandibular bone quality directly in antihypertensive drug users — fills the mechanism arm of the hypertension-implant evidence base where only animal studies existed.
→ [[overviews/hypertension-antihypertensive-implant-outcomes-overview]]

## One-line Summary
Retrospective case-control (n=230+230): all antihypertensive drug classes produced significantly higher fractal dimension in mandibular bone vs controls (p<0.001), indicating denser trabecular architecture favourable for osseointegration.

## 한줄요약
후향적 증례-대조 (n=230+230): 전 항고혈압약 클래스에서 하악골 프랙탈 차원 (FD)이 대조군보다 유의하게 높음 (p<0.001) — 임플란트 골유착에 유리한 치밀 골 구조 직접 증거.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First human study linking antihypertensive medication use to direct mandibular bone quality improvement via radiographic fractal analysis
- Higher fractal dimension (FD) confirmed across all drug classes (BB, ACEi, ARB, CCB, combination)
- Thiazide diuretics showed significance only in cortical bone ROI — drug-class specific trabecular vs cortical pattern
- Provides clinical-translational link from animal mechanistic studies (Huang 2024, Uchibori 2020) to human bone
- Mandibular ROI methodology directly applicable to implant site assessment

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Retrospective case-control
- Population: n=230 antihypertensive drug users + n=230 sex-matched normotensive controls
- Method: Fractal dimension (FD) analysis via ImageJ on panoramic radiographs
- 5 ROIs: 4 trabecular (ROI 1–4) + 1 cortical (ROI 5) in mandibular regions
- Subgroups by drug class: BB, ACEi, ARB, CCB, thiazide diuretic, combination therapy
- Statistical: comparison of mean FD between groups

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Overall: Mean FD significantly higher in AHT group vs controls (p<0.001) for ALL ROIs
- BB subgroup: higher FD vs controls (all ROIs, p<0.001)
- ACEi subgroup: higher FD vs controls (all ROIs, p<0.001)
- ARB subgroup: higher FD vs controls (all ROIs, p<0.001)
- CCB subgroup: higher FD vs controls (all ROIs, p<0.001)
- Combination therapy: higher FD vs controls (all ROIs, p<0.001)
- Thiazide diuretic: significant only in ROI 5 (cortical bone, p<0.001); trabecular ROIs NS
- Interpretation: higher FD = more complex, denser trabecular bone = better osseointegration substrate

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design; cannot establish causality
- No implant outcome data — bone quality inferred, not correlated with actual osseointegration
- Panoramic FD is 2D approximation of 3D bone quality (CBCT would be more precise)
- Drug dosage and duration not controlled

## 6. Related Work

- Uchibori 2020, Huang 2024: animal mechanistic studies (β-AR → RANKL/SOST pathway)
- Wu 2016: clinical implant failure outcomes in AHT users

## 7. Glossary
- FD (Fractal Dimension): mathematical measure of structural complexity; higher FD = denser, more interconnected trabecular architecture
- ROI: Region of Interest; 5 standardised mandibular locations on panoramic radiograph
- ImageJ: open-source image analysis software used for FD calculation
