## Why Ingested

Provides comprehensive synthesis of evidence on long-term supportive periodontal and peri-implant maintenance — recall intervals, risk stratification, protocol design, and emerging technologies (AI, biomarkers). Directly extends [[manresa-2018-supportive-periodontal-therapy-cochrane-sr]] into the peri-implant dimension and consolidates evidence on personalized risk tools (PRA, IDRA) relevant to clinical workflow.

## Three-line Summary

Wang et al. (2026) synthesize evidence showing that regular supportive periodontal care (SPC) and supportive peri-implant care (SPIC) markedly reduce tooth and implant loss, disease progression, and need for surgical re-intervention. Risk-stratified recall intervals — every 3–6 months for moderate-to-high-risk patients, up to 12 months for low-risk — produce significantly better outcomes than irregular attendance. Non-compliance and residual pockets ≥5–6 mm are the dominant predictors of recurrence; AI-assisted imaging and chair-side biomarker testing are emerging adjuncts awaiting long-term clinical validation.

## 세줄요약

Wang et al. (2026)은 정기적인 지지 치주치료(SPC)와 지지 임플란트주위 관리(SPIC)가 치아 및 임플란트 상실, 질환 진행, 재수술 필요성을 현저히 줄인다는 근거를 종합한다. 중·고위험 환자는 3–6개월, 저위험 환자는 최대 12개월 간격의 개인화 리콜이 유의하게 더 나은 임상 결과를 보인다. 비순응과 잔존 포켓 ≥5–6 mm가 재발의 주요 예측인자이며, AI 영상 분석 및 의자측 바이오마커 검사는 유망하나 장기 임상 검증이 선행되어야 한다.

## Key Contributions

1. Establishes that both SPC and SPIC are independently effective in reducing tooth/implant loss when maintained regularly
2. Quantifies risk-stratified recall intervals: 3–6 months (moderate-to-high risk), up to 12 months (low risk)
3. Profiles two validated risk assessment algorithms: PRA (Personalized Risk Assessment) and IDRA (Implant Disease Risk Assessment)
4. Identifies non-compliance and residual pockets ≥5–6 mm as dominant predictors of periodontal/peri-implant recurrence
5. Critically appraises emerging adjuncts: chair-side biomarker testing and AI-supported image analysis — promising but still requiring long-term validation
6. Demonstrates equivalence of biofilm control modalities (ultrasonic scaling, air-polishing, guided biofilm therapy) with no single protocol showing superior long-term outcomes

## Key Results

| Risk Level | Recall Interval | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Low risk | Up to 12 months | Longitudinal cohort evidence |
| Moderate risk | 3–6 months | Consensus + longitudinal data |
| High risk | 3–6 months (closer to 3) | RCT and cohort evidence |

**Biofilm control methods**: Ultrasonic scaling, air-polishing, guided biofilm therapy (GBT) — all effective; no single protocol demonstrated superior long-term results.

**Predictors of recurrence**:
- Non-compliance with maintenance schedule
- Residual pockets ≥5–6 mm

**Emerging technologies**:
- Chair-side biomarker testing: promising for early disease detection; pending long-term clinical validation
- AI-supported image analysis: promising for early detection; clinical integration guided by expert interpretation pending validation

**Clinical relevance**: Personalized supportive regimens (regular recalls + risk-based protocol selection + emerging technology integration) substantially enhance longevity and stability of teeth and implants while reducing long-term healthcare costs.

## Glossary

- **SPC (Supportive Periodontal Care)**: Regular professional maintenance for periodontitis patients post-active therapy
- **SPIC (Supportive Peri-implant Care)**: Analogous maintenance for peri-implant tissues around implants
- **PRA (Personalized Risk Assessment)**: Algorithm stratifying individual periodontal risk based on multiple clinical/behavioral factors
- **IDRA (Implant Disease Risk Assessment)**: Algorithm for peri-implant disease risk stratification
- **GBT (Guided Biofilm Therapy)**: Systematic biofilm removal protocol combining AIRFLOW, PERIOFLOW, and Piezon instrumentation
- **Residual Pocket**: Probing depth ≥5–6 mm persisting after active periodontal therapy; major recurrence predictor
