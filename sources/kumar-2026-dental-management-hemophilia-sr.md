## Why Ingested

혈우병 환자 치과 처치에서 TXA 항섬유소분해제의 역할을 체계적으로 정리한 첫 SR. 기존 위키의 [[drug/anticoagulants/bergo-2026-topical-tranexamic-acid-powder-dentistry]] 및 [[drug/anticoagulants/kaddah-2024-tranexamic-acid-gelfoam-warfarin]]이 일반 항응고 환자를 다루는 반면, 이 SR은 응고인자 결핍(혈우병)에서의 TXA 용량·프로토콜을 특이적으로 정리한다.

## One-line Summary

SR of 23 studies (inception–March 2025) on dental management in congenital hemophilia: TXA/EACA antifibrinolytics significantly reduce factor infusion requirements; 30–50% factor correction is standard; infiltration anesthesia safe without factor, nerve block requires factor correction.

## 한줄요약

혈우병 치과관리 SR (23편, ~2025): TXA/EACA 항섬용제가 응고인자 추가 주사 필요성을 유의하게 줄임; 표준 인자교정 30–50%; 침윤마취 안전, 하치조신경블록은 인자교정 필수.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Synthesizes dental management protocols for 23 studies across UK, USA, Europe, Asia, Australia, Africa
- Establishes TXA + single pre-op factor dose as sufficient for many dental procedures (including some mild hemophilia cases without factor)
- Confirms infiltration anesthesia safe without factor coverage; IAN block requires factor correction
- Documents key TXA dosing regimens across studies: 1g TID × 5 days oral; 20 mg/kg IV; 5% mouthwash/compress protocols
- Highlights global variability in protocols and absence of universally accepted guidelines

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA); no meta-analysis possible due to heterogeneity.
**Population**: 23 studies — congenital hemophilia A or B (mild/moderate/severe); acquired hemophilia and inhibitor-positive cases excluded.
**Inclusion**: RCTs, cohort studies, case-control, case reports; all dental procedures.
**Quality assessment**: JBI critical appraisal checklist; overall quality low-to-moderate.
**Key limitation**: Majority retrospective; non-uniform reporting; meta-analysis not possible.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Factor correction standards
- **Standard target**: 30–50% factor activity correction pre-procedure (WFH guidelines)
- **Formula**: Factor dose (IU) = body weight (kg) × desired rise (%) / 2 (for FVIII); ×1 (for FIX)

### TXA dosing protocols across studies
| Study | Protocol | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| Forbes 1972 (RCT) | 1g TID × 5 days oral, starting 2h pre-op | Hemophilia A + B extractions |
| Tavenner 1972 | TXA + blood/plasma; TXA superior to EACA | Extractions |
| Bjorlin 1973 | 25 mg/kg q6h oral | Single factor dose + extraction |
| Zanon 2000 | 20 mg/kg IV → oral TID × 7 days; TXA-soaked gauze 30–60 min | +fibrin sponge + suture |
| Frachon 2005 | TXA compress 10 min q1h (Day 1) → q2h (Day 2) → q3h (Day 3) | Reduces hospital stay to 24h |
| Hewson 2011 | 5% TXA mouthwash + 1g TID oral + 5% TXA socket flood + Surgicel | Severe hemophilia B, 3rd molar |
| Raso 2022 | Oral TXA 1g TID; mild hemophilia — factor not always needed | Mild hemophilia A only |
| Fribourg 2024 | TXA prescribed TID × 10 days post-op | Routine oral surgery |

### Anesthesia safety
- Infiltration with adrenaline (1:100,000 or 1:200,000): **safe without factor correction**
- Inferior alveolar nerve block (IAN): **requires factor correction** due to deep intramuscular penetration risk
- General anesthesia: safe in PwH with appropriate factor cover

### Local hemostatic agents reported
- Surgicel (oxidized cellulose), Gelfoam, thrombin powder: used across studies
- TXA-soaked gauze/compress: widely reported
- Fibrin glue (Tisseel): used for high-risk/severe cases

### Key clinical outcomes
- Post-extraction bleeding incidence: 11.9% per intervention (Zanon 2000 retrospective, Japan cohort)
- Emicizumab prophylaxis + TXA only (no additional factor): successful in severe hemophilia A case (Aruche 2022)
- Mouth splints significantly reduced post-extraction bleeding risk (Japan cohort n=151 extractions)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Most included studies are case reports/series with low-to-moderate quality
- Non-uniform reporting of factor dose, extraction type, procedure complexity
- Pediatric vs adult dosages not comparable due to weight-based dosing
- No meta-analysis possible; results not generalizable
- Future need: prospective cohort studies with standardized protocols by hemophilia severity

## 6. Related Work

- Forbes 1972 (PMID legacy): First RCT of TXA in hemophilia dental extraction; TXA 1g TID × 5 days reduces blood loss
- Pedersen Sindet 1988: TXA mouthwash vs EACA — TXA superior for gingival bleeding
- Lee 2005 (RCT): TXA mouthwash vs factor replacement for dental scaling — no significant difference
- WFH Guidelines: Standard reference for factor dosing in hemophilia

## 7. Glossary

- **PwH**: People with hemophilia
- **FVIII/FIX**: Factor VIII (hemophilia A) / Factor IX (hemophilia B)
- **EACA**: Epsilon-aminocaproic acid (older antifibrinolytic, more side effects than TXA)
- **EHL/SHL/UHL**: Extended/standard/ultra-long half-life factor concentrates
- **Emicizumab**: Bispecific monoclonal antibody, non-factor hemostatic therapy for hemophilia A
- **WFH**: World Federation of Hemophilia
