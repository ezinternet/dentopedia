## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case series, 107 REPLICATE (NDI Berlin) root-analogue implants (RAIs) placed in one German private practice (Sept 2016–Aug 2019), 31 RAIs in 28 patients available for full clinical/radiographic/esthetic follow-up.

Survival 94.4% (mean follow-up 18.9 ± 2.4 months); mean marginal bone loss (MBL) 1.20 ± 0.73 mm at follow-up, with a statistically significant slowdown in MBL progression after loading (p = 0.013) vs. before loading; combined 4-domain success rate (implant/soft-tissue/prosthetic/patient-satisfaction) only 64.5%, driven down mainly by implant-level (80.8%) and marginal-integrity/prosthetic (83.3%) shortfalls despite excellent soft-tissue (96.8%) and patient-satisfaction (90.0%) results.

Clinically, immediate RAI placement did not reduce MBL versus literature benchmarks for conventional screw-shaped immediate implants and showed weaker marginal fit, so the authors — writing as the manufacturer (NDI Berlin) ceased operations mid-study — conclude RAIs are not yet justified for routine use at their ~15% cost premium, despite favorable esthetics and soft-tissue stability.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest reported single-center clinical series of REPLICATE root-analogue implants (RAIs) to date (107 placed, 31 followed up), with independent (non-surgeon) follow-up examination and independent radiographic MBL measurement to reduce bias.
- Applies a comprehensive 4-domain implant-success framework (implant level, peri-implant soft tissue, prosthetic level via PES/WES + modified USPHS, patient satisfaction via VAS) rather than survival alone, revealing that RAIs can have high survival (94.4%) but much lower combined success (64.5%) — a distinction often collapsed in smaller case reports.
- Empirically shows MBL progression significantly slows after prosthetic loading vs. the healing phase (p=0.013), mirroring the biology of conventional screw-shaped immediate implants.
- Tests 12 candidate predictors of MBL (gender, age, region, arch, material, implant/root/abutment length, surface area, bone quality, surgical difficulty, root number) and finds none statistically significant — a null result relevant to risk stratification.
- Documents a real-world commercial failure signal: manufacturer NDI Berlin ceased operations during the study (31 Jan 2020), which the authors explicitly interpret alongside the modest clinical benefit as an indicator RAIs were "not yet convincing."

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective case series; STROBE-informed reporting for observational studies.
- **Setting**: Single private oral-surgery practice, Berlin, Germany; surgeries by one experienced surgeon (D.H.); follow-up exams by an independent second examiner (M.B.); MBL radiographic measurements by an independent, external private lecturer — three-way role separation to reduce bias.
- **Cohort**: 107 RAIs (REPLICATE Immediate Tooth Replacement System, NDI Berlin) placed Sept 2016–Aug 2019 in patients selected for non-smoking status, no bone-affecting medication, non-inflamed periodontium, intact bone, and visible periodontal gap on CBCT. Only 28 of the treated patients (31 RAIs) consented to full follow-up (wide referral network across Germany limited recall); remaining survival status confirmed by phone/referring-dentist records.
- **Implant system**: One-piece hybrid (titanium endosseous body + glass-fused zirconia abutment) or one-piece all-zirconia RAI, subtractively milled from CBCT DICOM + intraoral STL data merged pre-extraction; macro/micro surface retentions for primary stability; intra-bony portion intentionally undersized vs. the native root to avoid bone compression.
- **Surgical protocol**: Atraumatic extraction (Benex Extraction System where feasible, ~83% reported success), Try-In fit verification, PRGF (Plasma Rich Growth Factors) wetting of implant surface/socket voids, hammer/mallet seating, adhesive temporary protective cover (TPC) on adjacent teeth with ~0.6 mm load-protection gap. Healing checked at 3–6 months before final impressions/restorations.
- **Outcome framework** (4 superordinate domains, per a 2012 systematic review methodology):
  1. Implant level — radiographic MBL at surgery (T0), loading (T1), follow-up (T2) via ImageJ using manufacturer reference points; Albrektsson-style criteria (<1.5 mm year-1 loss, <0.2 mm/year thereafter, no pain/mobility/infection).
  2. Peri-implant soft tissue — modified plaque index (mPI) and bleeding index (mBI, Mombelli), keratinized gingiva (KG) width ≥1.5 mm.
  3. Prosthetic level — PES/WES (Pink/White Esthetic Score, threshold ≥6) and modified USPHS 7-criterion A/B/C/D rating.
  4. Patient satisfaction — Visual Analogue Scale (VAS, 0–100%) for esthetics and chewing ability, ≥80% = success.
- **Statistics**: SciPy (Python); paired t-test on MBL-gradient intervals; Welch's t-tests for subgroup comparisons (gender, region, arch, material, difficulty, root number); Pearson correlation for continuous predictors; α = 0.05.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, single-center, single-surgeon design with no control/comparison arm — comparisons to screw-shaped implant literature are indirect (benchmark citation, not head-to-head).
- Only 31/107 placed RAIs (29%) received full protocol follow-up; selection/attrition bias likely (patients lost to a wide, dispersed referral network).
- Non-standardized 2D periapical radiographs without customized holders — MBL measurement subject to projection artifact.
- Short observation window (<2 years mean); authors explicitly call for mid- to long-term prospective, controlled studies.
- PES/WES tool was originally validated for esthetic-zone implants compared to contralateral natural teeth, but only 41.9% of this cohort's RAIs were in the esthetic zone and many contralateral "control" teeth were themselves restored — potential false-positive/negative bias in the esthetic score.
- Manufacturer discontinuation (NDI Berlin, 31 Jan 2020) during the study limits any assumption of continued availability/comparability for future systems; the authors treat this as circumstantial evidence the concept was "not yet convincing" commercially.

## 7. Glossary

- **RAI (Root-Analogue Implant)**: Custom, patient-specific implant milled to replicate the exact external morphology of the extracted tooth root, for immediate placement into the fresh socket.
- **REPLICATE Immediate Tooth Replacement System**: NDI Berlin's commercial RAI product (2013–2020), combining CBCT DICOM + intraoral STL data for pre-extraction subtractive manufacturing.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: Radiographically measured loss of crestal bone height around the implant, tracked at surgery/loading/follow-up timepoints.
- **JIAP (Junction between Implant and Abutment Portions)**: The RAI's rough-to-polished transition zone, analogous to the microgap/platform of screw-shaped implants; positioned mostly 1 mm subcrestal in this cohort.
- **PES/WES (Pink and White Esthetic Score)**: Standardized 0–10 (each) scoring of peri-implant soft-tissue ("pink") and crown ("white") esthetics; combined score ≥6 typically deemed clinically acceptable per component in this study's threshold.
- **Modified USPHS criteria**: A/B/C/D grading system (Alpha=excellent to Delta=irreparable) applied to 7 restoration parameters (fracture, roughness, marginal integrity, contour, esthetics, discoloration).
- **PRGF (Plasma Rich Growth Factors)**: Autologous blood-derived growth-factor concentrate used here to wet implant surfaces/fill peri-implant voids at placement.
