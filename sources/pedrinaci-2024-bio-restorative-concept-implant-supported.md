## Three-line Summary

Narrative review/concept article (J Esthet Restor Dent 2024) proposing the "bio-restorative concept" for implant-supported restorations (ISR), integrating periodontal, surgical, and prosthetic variables through digitally assisted diagnosis and planning.

The framework combines (A) anatomical site characteristics and (B) restorative variables—including digital tooth arrangement, soft/hard tissue and peri-implant phenotype assessment, implant selection/3D position, supra-platform components, and a surgical plan that respects or modifies the phenotype—to align biologic and prosthetic goals.

Clinical implication: a comprehensive digital planning workflow may improve predictability, efficiency, and long-term esthetic/functional/biologic outcomes of ISRs, though this is a conceptual synthesis rather than comparative clinical-effectiveness evidence.

## 세줄요약

개념적 고찰(Pedrinaci et al. 2024, J Esthet Restor Dent) — 임플란트 지지 수복(implant-supported restoration, ISR)에서 치주·외과·보철 변수를 디지털 계획으로 통합하는 "bio-restorative concept"을 제안.

해부학적 부위 특성(연·경조직/임플란트 주위 표현형)과 수복 변수(디지털 치아 배열, 임플란트 선택·3차원 위치, 상부구조/출현윤곽, 표현형 보존·개선 수술 계획)를 가상 환경에서 동시에 평가해 생물학적·보철적 목표를 정렬하는 프레임워크를 제시한다.

임상적 의미: 디지털 기반 통합 계획이 ISR의 예측성·효율·장기 심미/기능/생물학적 결과 향상에 도움될 수 있으나, 비교 임상효과를 입증한 연구가 아닌 개념 종합임을 고려해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Defined a **bio-restorative concept** linking biologic (phenotype/surgical) and restorative (prosthetic) planning via digital workflows.
- Outlined key planning elements: digital tooth arrangement, soft/hard tissue and peri-implant phenotype assessment, implant selection/macro-design/3D positioning, supra-platform components, and phenotype-respecting/modifying surgical strategy.
- Emphasized that ISR success depends on long-term outcomes of the implant–prosthetic complex and integrated pre-extraction/placement planning.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Narrative/conceptual review and framework proposal (not a systematic review/meta-analysis).
- **Approach**: Synthesizes evolution from surgically driven to prosthetically driven to digitally integrated planning; presents checklist-like key elements for diagnosis, treatment planning, and sequencing.
- **Core components**: (A) anatomical site characteristics; (B) implant restorative variables assessed simultaneously in a virtual planning environment.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- No pooled quantitative clinical outcomes; primary "results" are conceptual recommendations and planning principles.
- Highlights importance of peri-implant phenotype dimensions/components (e.g., keratinized mucosa width, mucosal thickness, supracrestal tissue height, bone thickness) in planning and outcomes (as discussed in the framework).
- Advocates digital superimposition of scans/DICOM and intended restoration to guide 3D implant positioning and emergence/profile design.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Conceptual article: lacks primary comparative data and effect-size estimates; recommendations depend on underlying cited evidence and clinical judgment.
- Applicability varies by site, phenotype, and selected implant placement/loading protocols.
- Future work implied: clinical validation and outcome studies of the integrated bio-restorative workflow.

## 7. Glossary
- **ISR (implant-supported restoration)**: Prosthetic restoration supported by dental implants.
- **Peri-implant phenotype**: Morphologic and dimensional characteristics of soft and hard tissues surrounding an implant/restoration (e.g., KMW, mucosal thickness, supracrestal tissue height, bone thickness).
- **Digital tooth arrangement**: Virtual design of intended tooth position/form used to guide restorative and implant planning.
- **Supra-platform components**: Prosthetic/abutment components coronal to the implant platform influencing emergence profile and tissue response.
