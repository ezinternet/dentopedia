## Why Ingested

Foundational systematic review establishing the evidence base for soft tissue augmentation at implant sites and partially edentulous areas, informing the [[implants/soft-tissue/tavelli-2021-soft-tissue-phenotype-modification-peri-implant-nma]] network meta-analysis and [[implants/soft-tissue/oh-2024-keratinized-mucosa-augmentation-functioning-implants-sr-ma]] SR+MA that extend these conclusions. Published as part of the EAO 2014 Consensus Conference supplement, making it a landmark clinical consensus document.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 9 keratinized mucosa (KM) and 11 soft tissue volume studies around implants and partially edentulous sites found APF/vestibuloplasty + FGG/SCTG to be the best-documented method for KM width gain (1.4–3.3 mm), with statistically superior outcomes over controls (p < 0.05). Collagen matrix (CM) showed less KM gain but reduced surgery time and patient morbidity compared to SCTG in two RCTs. SCTG was the best-documented approach for soft tissue volume gain and improved aesthetics (papilla fill, marginal mucosal levels) at immediate implant sites.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 주변 및 부분 무치악 부위의 연조직 증강 효능을 평가한 체계적 문헌고찰로, 각화점막(KM) 관련 9편·연조직 두께 관련 11편을 포함. 치조정접근 판막/전정성형술(APF/V) + 유리치은이식(FGG)/상피하 결합조직이식(SCTG) 병용이 KM 폭 1.4–3.3 mm 증가로 가장 효과적이었으며 대조군 대비 통계적으로 유의한 우위(p < 0.05). 콜라겐 매트릭스(CM)는 KM 증가량이 적지만 수술 시간·환자 이환율에서 SCTG보다 유리하며, SCTG는 연조직 두께 및 심미적 결과(유두 충전, 변연점막 수준)에서 최선.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Systematic Review (EAO 2014 Consensus Conference Supplement)
- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Periodontology, 2014;41 Suppl 15:S77-91
- **Institution**: Clinic of Fixed and Removable Prosthodontics and Dental Material Science, University of Zurich, Zurich, Switzerland
- **Search database**: MEDLINE
- **Inclusion**: Human studies; augmentation of KM and soft tissue volume around implants and partially edentulous areas
- **Studies included**: 9 (KM augmentation) + 11 (soft tissue volume)
- **Meta-analysis**: Not performed due to heterogeneity between studies

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Established APF/vestibuloplasty + FGG/SCTG as the gold standard for KM width augmentation at implant sites (1.4–3.3 mm gain)
2. Demonstrated statistically significant superiority of APF/V + FGG/SCTG over controls (APF/V alone; no treatment; p < 0.05)
3. Identified CM as a viable alternative with lower morbidity at the cost of reduced KM gain (supported by 2 RCTs)
4. Confirmed SCTG as the best-documented method for soft tissue volume gain and aesthetic improvement at immediate implant sites
5. Highlighted lack of homogeneity in outcomes, precluding meta-analysis — flagging the methodological gap later addressed by Tavelli 2021 NMA

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review following MEDLINE search
- **Heterogeneity**: High across included studies; no meta-analysis performed
- **Outcome domains**:
  - KM width change (mm)
  - Soft tissue volume change
  - Surgery time
  - Patient morbidity
  - Aesthetics (papilla fill, marginal mucosal level)
- **Comparators**: APF/V alone, no treatment, autogenous grafts (FGG, SCTG), collagen matrix, allogenic dermal matrix
- **Study designs included**: RCTs and observational studies (mixed); 2 RCTs specifically cited for CM vs. SCTG morbidity comparison

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Keratinized Mucosa Augmentation (9 studies)

| Intervention | KM Gain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| APF/V + FGG or SCTG | 1.4–3.3 mm | Best documented; p < 0.05 vs controls |
| APF/V alone | Less gain | Control; statistically inferior |
| APF/V + CM | Less than FGG/SCTG | Lower morbidity; less surgery time (2 RCTs) |

### Soft Tissue Volume (11 studies)

| Intervention | Outcome | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SCTG | Best documented for volume gain | Superior aesthetics at immediate implant sites |
| SCTG vs non-grafted | Better papilla fill + higher marginal mucosal levels | Aesthetic advantage |
| CM | Less data vs SCTG for volume | — |

### Patient Morbidity / Surgery Time

- CM statistically significantly better than SCTG for surgery time and patient morbidity (p < 0.05, 2 RCTs)
- Trade-off: less KM gain with CM

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No meta-analysis due to heterogeneity — quantitative synthesis not possible
- Mixed study designs limit strength of evidence
- Limited number of RCTs, especially for volume outcomes
- Aesthetic outcomes not standardized across studies
- Need for long-term follow-up data
- Subsequent work (Tavelli 2021 NMA, Oh 2024 SR+MA) addressed the heterogeneity gap through network meta-analytic approaches

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| APF/V | Apically Positioned Flap / Vestibuloplasty — surgical technique to widen keratinized tissue zone |
| FGG | Free Gingival Graft — autogenous epithelialized graft from palate |
| SCTG | Subepithelial Connective Tissue Graft — autogenous connective tissue graft from palate |
| CM | Collagen Matrix — xenogeneic or alloplastic collagen scaffold as graft substitute |
| KM | Keratinized Mucosa — keratinized tissue band around implants/teeth |
| EAO | European Association for Osseointegration — sponsoring organization of the 2014 Consensus Conference |
