## Why Ingested

Korean-language (Yonsei, Advanced General Dentistry) clinical review of the 2017 AAP/EFP World Workshop classification, written from the practitioner's viewpoint — directly complements the primary sources [[wiki/periodontics/caton-2018-classification-scheme-periodontal-periimplant-diseases]] (workshop overview) and [[wiki/periodontics/papapanou-2018-periodontitis-classification-consensus-2017]] (periodontitis workgroup consensus). Especially valuable for Korean-speaking clinicians: it carries the operational details (stage/grade, case definitions, the ≤3 mm vs ≤4 mm "clinically healthy" paradox) in Korean with the original English terms preserved.

## Three-line Summary

Review article (J Korean Acad Adv Gen Dent 2020;9:83–96, Yonsei) of the 2017 World Workshop classification of periodontal and peri-implant diseases and conditions, written to remedy the diagnostic imprecision and clinical applicability problems of the long-standing 1999 classification.

Periodontitis is now staged I–IV (severity/complexity, with furcation involvement alone able to raise a case to stage III/IV) and graded A/B/C (rate of progression: assume B, seek evidence for A or C, then modify by risk factors such as smoking or HbA1c); conditions are defined by explicit case definitions, and the review details the clinical-health/gingivitis boundary (probing depth ≤3 mm with <10% BOP for gingivitis vs ≤4 mm without bleeding for clinical health in treated-periodontitis patients — a deliberate paradox to avoid over-treating stable sites).

Peri-implant section distinguishes peri-implant mucositis from peri-implantitis and maps the mucogingival/gingival-recession and phenotype concepts; the review adds a periodontal-biological-phenotype discussion for gingival health.

## 세줄요약

연세대 통합치의학과 팀이 2017 AAP/EFP 월드워크숍 신분류(1999년 분류의 진단 부정확성·임상 적용 어려움 개선)를 임상의 관점에서 정리한 한국어 리뷰 (J Korean Acad Adv Gen Dent 2020;9:83–96).

치주염은 stage I–IV(중증도·복잡성; 2·3급 이개부 병변만으로도 stage III/IV 상향)와 grade A/B/C(진행 속도: 일단 B로 가정→A/C로 바꿀 근거 탐색→흡연·혈당 등 위험인자로 수정)로 분류하고, 각 질환에 case definition을 도입.

구강건강·치은염 경계를 세부화(치은염=탐침 깊이 ≤3 mm·<10% BOP; 단 성공적 치주치료 후 임상적 건강은 ≤4 mm·무출혈로 정의해 안정 부위의 과잉치료 방지); 임플란트주위 점막염 vs 임플란트주위염 구분, 치은퇴축·치주 표현형 개념도 정리 — primary source 2편(caton-2018, papapanou-2018)의 한국어 임상 해설로 사용.
