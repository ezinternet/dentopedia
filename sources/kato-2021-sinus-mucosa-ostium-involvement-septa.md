## Why Ingested

Extends the ostium-patency risk story around sinus augmentation: this CBCT study tracks the transient post-sinus-floor-elevation Schneiderian swelling that peaks at 1 week (partially obstructing the infundibulum/ostium) and regresses by 9 months, and asks whether interfering septa modulate that risk. Reinforces the mucosal-thickening literature in [[sinus-lift/lateral/akbari-2022-maxillary-sinus-mucosal-thickening-ridge-height]] and complements the membrane-thickness LSFE work in [[sinus-lift/lateral/khiabani-2024-sinus-membrane-thickness-lsfe-mucosal]].

## Three-line Summary


Retrospective CBCT study (15 septa + 15 control sinuses, lateral-window SFE): post-op mucosal swelling peaks at 1 week — obstructing 3/15 infundibula in the septa group vs 5/15 in controls — and fully regresses by 9 months with no residual obstruction;

septa presence did not worsen ostium involvement (though it caused 4 perforations vs 0).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to specifically document whether the **presence of interfering septa** modifies the dimensional variation of the Schneiderian mucosa and **ostium/infundibulum involvement** over time after lateral-window maxillary sinus floor elevation.
- Confirms the **transient nature of post-SFE mucosal swelling**: a virtual thickening peaks at 1 week and regresses to near-baseline by 9 months, with all infundibular obstructions resolving.
- Counter-intuitive finding: **septa did NOT worsen ostium involvement** — controls (no septa) showed a tendency to greater mucosal thickening and more infundibular obstructions (5 vs 3). Hypothesis: septa may physically limit the spread of edema toward the palatal wall/ostium.
- Reaffirms septa as a **perforation risk** — 4 perforations (26.7%) in the septa group vs 0 in controls; all small and managed with a collagen membrane.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Secondary retrospective analysis of records from RCTs performed at University Corporation Rafael Núñez, Cartagena de Indias, Colombia (ethics protocol # CURN-0002-).
- **Sample**: 88 sinuses of 75 patients screened → 15 sinuses (12 patients, mean age 55.2 ± 9.4 y) with interfering septa met inclusion; 15 randomly selected septa-free sinuses (15 patients, mean age 57.8 ± 8.1 y) served as controls. 10 sinuses right / 20 left.
- **Inclusion (parent RCTs)**: edentulous posterior maxilla; residual crest height ≤4 mm; ≥21 y; good general health; not pregnant. **Additional criteria**: lateral-access SFE; septa interfering with surgery (septa group); good-quality CBCT at T0 (pre-op), T1w (1 week), T9m (9 months).
- **All cases**: lateral window approach. Grafts: 10 sinuses/group OsteoBiol Gen-Os (250–1000 µm) + OsteoBiol Evolution 0.3 mm collagen membrane; 5 sinuses/group Cerabone granulate (1.0–2.0 mm) + Collprotect collagen membrane.
- **CBCT device**: 3D Accuitomo 170 (J. Morita); measurements in i-Dixel 2.0.
- **Coronal-view measures**: MSO-to-sinus-floor distance, MSO diameter, infundibulum length, number of infundibula out of patency, sinus mucosa thickness (T0); plus distance between ostium and edema (T1w, T9m). **Lateral view**: vertical edema extension above (+)/below (−) the base-of-nose Z-axis.
- **Septa**: height, ostium-to-septum distance, Irinakis classification (Class I buccal-lingual, II mesial-distal, III horizontal, IV combination).
- **Statistics**: Mann–Whitney (septa vs control); Wilcoxon (between periods, exploratory). SPSS v19.0.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Baseline (T0)** — no significant septa-vs-control differences:
- Septa mean height 6.3 ± 2.4 mm (min 2.9, max 9.8); mainly molar region, 9.6 ± 4.9 mm distal to ostium. Irinakis: 11 Class I, 1 Class II, 3 Class IV. 10 sinuses one septum, 3 two septa, 2 three septa.
- MSO-to-sinus-base distance: 33.2 ± 3.9 mm (septa) vs 32.6 ± 4.6 mm (control).
- MSO diameter: 1.7 ± 0.4 mm (septa) vs 2.1 ± 0.8 mm (control).
- Infundibulum length: 8.5 ± 1.3 mm (septa) vs 9.2 ± 2.1 mm (control). **No infundibular obstruction pre-op** in either group.
- Mucosa thickness: 1.1 ± 0.7 mm (septa) vs 1.0 ± 0.7 mm (control).

**1 week (T1w)** — peak swelling:
- MSO diameter narrowed to 1.0 ± 0.6 mm (septa) and 1.0 ± 0.8 mm (control).
- Sinus mucosa width increased to 6.7 ± 7.8 mm (septa) and 8.2 ± 7.0 mm (control). *Discussion reports the mean mucosal height increase as **5.7 mm (septa) and 7.1 mm (control)**; abstract cites these figures.*
- Distance from edema extension to ostium: 11.8 ± 10.7 mm (septa) vs 7.5 ± 8.8 mm (control) (p = 0.310) — i.e., edema ~4.3 mm closer to the ostium in controls.
- **Infundibular obstructions: 3/15 (20%) septa vs 5/15 (33.3%) control.** Not statistically significant.

**9 months (T9m)** — regression:
- MSO diameter returned to baseline in both groups; mucosa width normalized in septa group. Control mucosa remained ~1.2 mm thicker than T0 (driven by a single asymptomatic case).
- Only significant septa-vs-control differences at T9m: mucosa thickness (p = 0.028) and mucosa area (p = 0.029) in coronal view.
- Edema-to-ostium distance decreased in both coronal and lateral views. **No infundibular obstructions in either group; all sinuses pathology-free.**

**Perforations**: 4 (26.7%) in septa group (≈1, 2, 3, and 3×5 mm), all managed with subjacent collagen membrane; **0 in controls**. No unexpected complications.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **2-dimensional analysis** on CBCT and **retrospective design**.
- CBCT cannot reliably distinguish real mucosal thickness from edema-driven ("virtual") thickening.
- **Small sample** (15 + 15) — likely underpowered; several between-group tendencies (mucosal thickening, edema proximity to ostium) did not reach significance and might with a larger cohort.

## 6. Related Work

- Cited a retrospective series of 79 lateral-window SFEs: interfering septa in ~48% of sinuses; 17/38 perforations (44.7%) at septa sinuses vs 1 at septa-free sinuses.
- Cited a clinical CBCT study where pre-op mucosa was 1.9 mm with 7/53 infundibula out of patency; post-op mucosa rose to ~4 mm with 16 obstructed, normalizing by 7.5 months (5 still obstructed).
- Experimental monkey study: post-SFE edema/clot regressed within a month.
- Irinakis septa classification adopted for orientation categorization.

## 7. Glossary

- **MSO (maxillary sinus ostium)**: natural drainage opening of the maxillary sinus into the middle meatus.
- **Infundibulum**: the funnel-shaped channel through which the maxillary sinus drains toward the ostium; loss of patency ("out of patency") = obstruction of this drainage path.
- **Schneiderian membrane**: the sinus mucosa lining the maxillary sinus.
- **Virtual sinus mucosa thickening**: apparent CBCT thickness increase that is actually post-op clot/edema, not true mucosal hypertrophy.
- **Septum (sinus septa)**: bony wall/ridge within the maxillary sinus; interferes with membrane elevation and raises perforation risk.
- **Irinakis classification**: septa orientation categories — Class I buccal-lingual, II mesial-distal, III horizontal, IV combination.
- **Lateral-access (lateral window) SFE**: sinus floor elevation via a bony antrostomy in the lateral sinus wall.
