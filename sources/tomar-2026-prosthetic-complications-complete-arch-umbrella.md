## Why Ingested

Umbrella review synthesizing 7 systematic reviews (≥5-year follow-up) of prosthetic and biological complications in implant-supported complete arch fixed prostheses — fills a gap in the wiki's single-crown-focused prosthetic-materials coverage by extending screw-vs-cement and material (monolithic vs veneered zirconia) complication comparisons to the full-arch level. Reinforces [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/khurshid-2025-screw-vs-cement-crown-complications]] (screw-retained favorable biological-complication profile) and [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/laumbacher-2021-zirconia-prosthetic-restorations-long-term-sr]] (veneered zirconia chipping > monolithic zirconia) at a higher evidence tier and in the full-arch rather than single-unit context.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA + JBI methodology; 7 systematic reviews, ≥5-year follow-up, published 2009–2019, covering RCTs/prospective/retrospective primary studies) of implant-supported complete arch fixed prostheses (metal-ceramic, metal-resin, veneered zirconia, monolithic zirconia; screw- or cement-retained; 4–8 implants per arch).

Prosthetic survival 89.5–100% and implant survival 95.2–99.5% over 5–15 years; screw loosening 5–15%, veneer chipping 15–35% (significantly higher in veneered vs monolithic zirconia, P<.05), framework fractures <5%, peri-implantitis 4–18% (higher with cement retention), marginal bone loss 1.2–1.5 mm; implant number (4 vs ≥5) did not significantly affect survival or bone loss (P>.05).

Clinically, screw-retained and monolithic zirconia designs show the best long-term technical/biological complication profile; corrected covered area (CCA) overlap was 10.8% (moderate), and AMSTAR-2 confidence in included reviews ranged low (1) to moderate (5) to high (1), so conclusions are qualitative/narrative (no new pooled meta-analysis) and constrained by primary-review heterogeneity.

## 세줄요약

엄브렐러 리뷰 (PRISMA+JBI 방법론; 5년 이상 추적관찰 체계적고찰 7편, 2009–2019년 발표, RCT·전향적·후향적 1차 연구 포함) — 임플란트 지지 완전 궁 고정성 보철물 (금속-세라믹, 금속-레진, 비니어드 지르코니아, 모놀리식 지르코니아; 나사 또는 시멘트 유지; 궁당 임플란트 4–8개)의 보철·생물학적 합병증 종합.

보철물 생존율 89.5–100%, 임플란트 생존율 95.2–99.5% (5–15년); 나사 풀림 5–15%, 비니어 치핑 15–35% (비니어드 지르코니아가 모놀리식보다 유의하게 높음, P<.05), 프레임워크 파절 <5%, 임플란트주위염 4–18% (시멘트 유지 시 더 높음), 변연골소실 1.2–1.5mm; 임플란트 개수 (4개 대 5개 이상)는 생존율·골소실에 유의한 영향 없음 (P>.05).

임상적으로 나사 유지 및 모놀리식 지르코니아 디자인이 장기 기술적·생물학적 합병증 프로파일이 가장 우수하며, 포함된 리뷰 간 중복도(corrected covered area, CCA)는 10.8%(중등도), AMSTAR-2 신뢰도는 낮음(1편)~중등도(5편)~높음(1편)으로 혼재해 결론은 정성적/서술적 종합(새로운 메타분석 미실시)이며 1차 리뷰 간 이질성에 제약받음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review restricting inclusion to systematic reviews reporting complete arch implant-supported prosthesis outcomes with a **minimum 5-year follow-up**, addressing fragmentation and inconsistent follow-up duration/outcome reporting across primary SRs.
- Quantifies the long-term technical complication landscape: screw loosening (5–15%), veneer chipping (15–35% in veneered zirconia, significantly higher than monolithic zirconia, P<.05), framework fractures (<5%).
- Quantifies biological complications: peri-implantitis (4–18%, higher with cement retention), marginal bone loss (1.2–1.5 mm at 5–10 years), not influenced by implant number.
- Confirms high long-term reliability: implant survival 95.2–99.5%, prosthetic survival 89.5–100% across 5–15 years.
- Formally tests and reports that implant number (4 vs ≥5) does not significantly affect implant survival, prosthetic survival, or marginal bone loss (P>.05) — supports cost/morbidity-reducing 4-implant ("All-on-4"-type) protocols when biomechanical principles (cantilever control, occlusal load distribution) are respected.
- Applies corrected covered area (CCA) overlap quantification (10.8%, moderate) to justify inclusion of all 7 reviews despite some primary-study overlap, and AMSTAR-2 quality grading of each included review (1 high, 5 moderate, 1 low confidence).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Conducted per **PRISMA 2020** and **Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) umbrella review guidance**; protocol prospectively registered on **PROSPERO (CRD420251143650)**.
- Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Cochrane Library, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar, LILACS; searched January 2000 – August 2025.
- Inclusion: SRs/meta-analyses of clinical studies on implant-supported complete arch fixed or removable prostheses with **≥5-year follow-up**, reporting prosthesis survival, implant survival, technical complications (screw loosening, framework fracture, veneer chipping), biological complications (peri-implantitis, marginal bone loss), and/or patient-reported outcomes.
- Exclusion: no primary clinical data, follow-up <5 years, case reports/series only, in vitro/animal-only studies. 24 reviews were excluded specifically for <5-year follow-up (e.g., Bidra 2017, Saleh 2023, Del Fabbro 2022 — see Table 2 of source).
- Screening: EndNote deduplication → Rayyan title/abstract and full-text screening by 2 independent reviewers (S.T., N.A.), third reviewer (G.V.) as arbiter for disagreements.
- Data extraction: standardized form (first author, year, journal, objective, number/type of primary studies, patient/prosthesis characteristics, follow-up, prosthesis type, retention method, material, outcomes); only ≥5-year data extracted when reviews reported both short- and long-term results.
- Quality appraisal: **AMSTAR-2** (16 domains) per review, with overall confidence rating (high/moderate/low/critically low), independently by 2 reviewers with consensus resolution.
- Overlap quantification: **corrected covered area (CCA)** method — 10.8% (moderate overlap), confirming each review contributed largely distinct data.
- Synthesis: **qualitative/narrative**, not pooled quantitatively across reviews; where meta-analyses were reported within a primary SR, pooled estimates/P values were extracted verbatim (no new meta-analysis performed by the authors of this umbrella review).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Search yield**: 547 records identified → 195 screened after dedup/exclusion → 93 full-text assessed → **7 systematic reviews** included (PRISMA 2020 flow diagram, Fig. 1).
- **Included reviews** (Table 3): Daudt Polido 2018 (implant number), de Luna Gomes 2019 (implant number, JPD), Harder & Kern 2009 (CAD-CAM vs conventional), Kwon 2014 (hybrid prosthesis survival), Lima 2018 (mandibular implant number), Papaspyridakos 2013 (mandibular complete arch survival), Wong 2018 (metal-ceramic vs all-ceramic prosthodontic complications).
- **Prosthetic survival**: 89.5–100% (some sources in-text state 89.6% vs abstract's 89.5%) over 5–15 years.
- **Implant survival**: 95.2–99.5% (abstract states 95.2–99.2%; body text states 95.4–99.5% — minor internal inconsistency in the source PDF).
- **Technical complications**: overall prevalence 5–33%; screw loosening 5–15%; veneer chipping 15–35% in veneered zirconia (significantly > monolithic zirconia, P<.05); framework fractures <5%; metal-ceramic vs metal-resin comparable (P=.38).
- **Biological complications**: peri-implantitis 4–18% (higher with cement retention); marginal bone loss 1.2–1.5 mm at 5–10 years, unaffected by implant number.
- **Implant number**: 4 vs ≥5 implants — no significant difference in implant survival, prosthetic survival, or marginal bone stability (P>.05).
- **AMSTAR-2 quality** (Table 4): Wong 2018 = high confidence; Daudt Polido 2018, de Luna Gomes 2019, Harder 2009, Lima 2018, Papaspyridakos 2013 = moderate; Kwon 2014 = low. Publication bias formally assessed (Egger test) in only 2 of 7 reviews (both P>.05, no significant bias); several reviews lacked protocol registration or excluded-study lists.
- **Patient-reported outcomes**: consistently favorable (OHRQoL, esthetics, function) across all prosthetic designs/implant numbers, though only reported qualitatively in a subset of reviews.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Conclusions are dependent on the methodological rigor of the 7 included reviews, which varied from low to high AMSTAR-2 confidence; 1 review rated low confidence.
- Findings are synthesized **qualitatively**, not via new quantitative pooling — the umbrella review reports ranges rather than a single pooled effect estimate.
- Moderate primary-study overlap (CCA=10.8%) may reduce independence of the underlying evidence base despite reviews addressing distinct outcomes.
- Publication bias was assessable in only 2 of 7 reviews; most could not be evaluated due to incomplete reporting.
- Included reviews span a wide publication range (2009–2019) and heterogeneous study designs (RCTs, prospective and retrospective cohorts), limiting direct comparability.
- Authors call for future long-term standardized studies reporting outcomes separately by arch (maxillary vs mandibular) and stratified by prosthetic material/design.
- Minor internal numeric inconsistencies exist between the abstract and body text for prosthetic/implant survival ranges (e.g., 89.5% vs 89.6%; 99.2% vs 99.5%) — flagged here for transparency; both ranges are within the same clinical ballpark (~90–100% prosthetic, ~95–99.5% implant survival).

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: A systematic review that synthesizes findings from multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a topic, rather than primary studies directly.
- **Corrected covered area (CCA)**: A method quantifying the degree of overlap of primary studies across multiple systematic reviews being combined in an umbrella review.
- **AMSTAR-2**: "A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews" (version 2) — 16-domain critical appraisal tool for systematic review methodological quality, yielding an overall confidence rating (high/moderate/low/critically low).
- **PROSPERO**: International prospective register of systematic review protocols.
- **Screw-retained vs cement-retained prosthesis**: Retention method for implant-supported prostheses; screw-retained allows retrievability but risks screw loosening/access-related esthetics, cement-retained improves esthetics/occlusion but risks excess-cement peri-implant disease and limited retrievability.
- **Monolithic vs veneered zirconia**: Monolithic zirconia frameworks are milled as a single homogeneous material (no separate veneering porcelain layer), reducing chipping risk compared with veneered zirconia (zirconia core + layered ceramic veneer).
- **Marginal bone loss (MBL)**: Radiographic measure of peri-implant crestal bone level change over time, a key biological-complication indicator.
