## Why Ingested

얇은 협측골(BBT <1.5mm)은 즉시 임플란트 식립 후 치조제 흡수의 주요 위험 인자임에도, 해당 표현형 대상 소켓 실드 기술(SST) 특정 RCT는 기존 14편 SST 문헌에 없었음. [[immediate-implant/gharpure-2022-socket-shield-conventional-implant-sr-ma]] 및 [[immediate-implant/lin-2022-socket-shield-technique-systematic-review-meta]] 등 기존 SR에 통합될 새 RCT로, 얇은 협측골 표현형을 가진 환자에서 SST의 치조제 보존 우월성을 CBCT로 직접 측정.

## One-line Summary

RCT (Med Oral 2026): 40 patients, BBT <1.5 mm; socket shield technique produced significantly less horizontal and buccal vertical ridge resorption and less volumetric bone loss vs immediate implant placement alone at 1 year on CBCT superimposition.

## 한줄요약

RCT (Med Oral 2026): 40명, BBT <1.5mm; 소켓 실드 기술이 1년 CBCT 중첩 기준 즉시 임플란트 단독 대비 수평·협측 수직 치조제 흡수 및 부피 골 소실이 유의하게 적음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First RCT specifically powered for thin buccal bone phenotype (BBT <1.5 mm) comparing SST vs IIP
- Used CBCT superimposition for volumetric analysis — more comprehensive than horizontal point measurements alone
- Demonstrated significantly greater marginal bone stability with SST from prosthetic loading onward
- Supports SST as a "predictable strategy" for anatomically compromised sites (thin buccal bone)
- Fills critical gap in existing SST literature which typically did not stratify by buccal bone thickness

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Study type: Parallel-arm RCT
- Participants: 40 patients with single-rooted extraction sites and thin buccal bone (BBT <1.5 mm)
- SST group: Socket shield technique + immediate implant placement
- IIP group: Immediate implant placement alone
- Outcome measurements: CBCT superimposition at baseline and 1 year; standardized periapical radiographs at implant placement, prosthetic loading, and 1-year follow-up
- Outcomes: Horizontal ridge changes (mm), buccal vertical bone loss (mm), volumetric changes (mm³), marginal bone levels (MBL, mm)
- Statistics: ANCOVA adjusting for baseline values

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Horizontal ridge reduction (1 year):**
- SST: significantly less than IIP (p<0.001) — specific values in full text

**Buccal vertical bone loss (all levels evaluated):**
- SST: significantly less than IIP (p<0.001)

**Volumetric analysis:**
- IIP: significantly greater overall bone loss (p<0.001)

**Marginal bone loss:**
- At implant placement: no significant difference between groups
- At prosthetic loading: SST significantly better (p<0.001)
- At 1-year follow-up: SST significantly better (p<0.001)

**Conclusion:** SST superior to IIP for ridge dimensional preservation and MBL stability in thin buccal bone sites

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-center study with 40 patients; limited statistical power for subgroup analyses
- 1-year follow-up; longer-term MBL stability and implant survival not addressed
- Specific volumetric values not published in abstract; full text (non-PMC) required for detailed numbers
- Results specific to thin buccal bone phenotype (<1.5mm) — may not generalize to standard phenotype
- Surgical technique variation between operators not reported

## 7. Glossary

- **BBT**: Buccal bone thickness — thin phenotype defined as <1.5 mm
- **SST**: Socket shield technique — retention of buccal root fragment to preserve periosteum-PDL-bone complex
- **IIP**: Immediate implant placement — placement into fresh extraction socket without shield
- **CBCT superimposition**: Volumetric overlay of CBCT scans to measure 3D dimensional changes
- **ANCOVA**: Analysis of covariance — adjusts group comparisons for baseline values
