## Three-line Summary

RCT 13-year follow-up (originally 22 patients, 40 implants: 20 customized zirconia abutments + all-ceramic crowns vs 20 customized titanium abutments + metal-ceramic crowns in posterior regions; University of Zürich; mean follow-up 13.4 years; 15 patients/21 abutments examined at 13yr) reporting 100% technical survival of both abutment types and 100% crown survival.

Biological outcomes (PPD, BOP, PCR, bone level) and esthetic outcomes (MG, papilla index) were similar between zirconia and titanium abutment groups at 13 years; dropout rate 25% (patient level); no technical complications on abutments.

Zirconia abutments demonstrate comparable 13-year technical survival and periimplant health outcomes to titanium abutments, confirming long-term viability of ceramic abutments for single implant-borne posterior restorations.

## 세줄요약

RCT 13년 추적(n=22명, 40임플란트: 맞춤형 지르코니아 어버트먼트+전세라믹 단관 20개 vs 티타늄 어버트먼트+MC 단관 20개, 구치부, 취리히대학; 13.4년 평균 추적; 15명 21어버트먼트 검사): 양쪽 모두 어버트먼트·크라운 기술적 생존율 100%.

생물학적(PPD·BOP·PCR·골 수준) + 심미적(MG·유두 지수) 결과 양군 유사; 탈락율 25%(환자 수준); 어버트먼트 기술적 합병증 없음.

## One-line Summary

RCT 13-year follow-up (n=40 implants; 20 zirconia + 20 titanium abutments; posterior) showing 100% technical survival of both abutments, 100% crown survival, and similar periimplant biological and esthetic outcomes — longest available RCT data on ceramic vs Ti abutments.

## 한줄요약

RCT 13년 추적(n=40임플란트, 구치부 지르코니아 vs 티타늄 어버트먼트): 양쪽 어버트먼트·크라운 기술적 생존율 100%, 임플란트 주변 생물학적·심미적 결과 유사 — 현재까지 가장 긴 세라믹 어버트먼트 RCT.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Longest RCT follow-up data on ceramic (zirconia) vs titanium abutments (13 years)
- Confirms 100% technical survival of customized zirconia abutments at 13 years
- Establishes periimplant biological equivalence of ceramic and titanium abutments long-term
- No ceramic abutment fractures observed over 13 years in posterior regions
- Supports zirconia abutments as a durable alternative where titanium esthetics are insufficient

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design:** RCT 13-year follow-up
- **Original sample:** 22 patients, 40 implants; posterior regions
- **Allocation:** 20 customized zirconia abutments + all-ceramic cemented crowns (ACC) vs 20 customized titanium abutments + metal-ceramic crowns (MCC); random assignment per site
- **Follow-up:** Mean 13.4 years post-loading
- **Sample at 13yr:** 15 patients, 21 abutments (13 Zr, 8 Ti)
- **Dropout:** 25% (patient level)
- **Outcomes:** Survival, technical complications; PPD, BOP, PCR, bone level, papilla index (PAP), mucosal thickness, recession
- **Analysis:** Descriptive statistics

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Zirconia | Titanium |
|---|---|---|
| Technical survival (abutments) | 100% | 100% |
| Crown survival | 100% | 100% |
| PPD | Similar | Similar |
| BOP | Similar | Similar |
| PCR | Similar | Similar |
| Bone level | Similar | Similar |
| Papilla index | Similar | Similar |

No technical complications on abutments in either group. Similar periimplant biological and esthetic outcomes at 13 years.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Dropout rate 25% (22 → 15 patients): survival estimates may be optimistic
- Small sample (21 abutments at 13yr)
- Descriptive statistics only — underpowered for inferential comparisons
- Customized abutments; results may not generalize to prefabricated/Ti-base systems
- No comparison with Ti-base + zirconia crown combination (current standard)
