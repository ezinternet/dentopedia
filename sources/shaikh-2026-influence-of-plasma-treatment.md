## Why Ingested
First PROSPERO-registered SR+MA synthesizing all evidence on plasma treatment for zirconia bonding (14 studies, SMD 3.04 vs untreated). Provides the quantitative evidence base that [[dental-materials/zirconia/kamis-2023-cold-plasma-zirconia-resin-cement-bond]] represents as a single study, and directly contextualizes the surface conditioning umbrella framework in [[dental-materials/zirconia/nasri-2025-surface-conditioning-zirconia-bonding-umbrella]].

## One-line Summary
Systematic review + meta-analysis of 14 in vitro studies (search through Jan 2026): plasma surface treatment significantly improves zirconia shear bond strength vs untreated (SMD 3.04; 95% CI 1.76-4.32) and is comparable to sandblasting; best performance with CAP + 10-MDP primer combination.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (14개 연구, 검색일 2026-01): 플라즈마 처리가 지르코니아 전단결합강도를 미처리 대비 유의하게 향상(SMD 3.04; 95% CI 1.76-4.32); 공기분사연마(Airborne Particle Abrasion, APA)와 동등; 냉온 대기압 플라즈마(Cold Atmospheric Plasma, CAP) + 10-MDP 프라이머 병용이 최고 효과.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First PROSPERO-registered SR+MA quantifying plasma effect on zirconia bonding (14 studies, 2016-2024)
- Meta-analysis: plasma vs untreated SMD 3.04 (95% CI 1.76-4.32, p<0.00001) — statistically significant; plasma vs sandblasting SMD 0.89 (ns), sensitivity analysis SMD 1.49 (p=0.01)
- Identifies CAP + 10-MDP primer as synergistic protocol with best immediate and aged bond strength
- Documents long-term durability advantage: Bunz 2021 showed best 250-day aging performance with plasma + APA + universal adhesive
- Identifies Kim 2024 as an outlier (plasma alone reduced SBS in 3Y-TZP, but increased in (Y,Nb)-TZP) — composition dependence
- Concludes plasma does not alter bulk zirconia structure or induce phase transformation (unlike APA)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: SR+MA, PRISMA 2020, QUIN risk-of-bias tool
- Inclusion: in vitro studies on dental zirconia, plasma surface treatment, quantitative bond strength or hydrophilicity outcomes
- 14 studies included (all in vitro); published 2016-2024; 10 countries
- Meta-analysis: RevMan 5.4, random-effects inverse-variance model, SMD + 95% CI
- Two comparisons: (1) plasma vs untreated; (2) plasma vs sandblasting
- Sensitivity analysis: exclusion of Kim 2024 (directionally discordant) for plasma vs sandblasting

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Comparison | Studies | n/group | SMD | 95% CI | p | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plasma vs untreated | 9 | 130 | 3.04 | 1.76-4.32 | <0.00001 | 89% |
| Plasma vs sandblasting (primary) | 6 | 80 | 0.89 | -0.41-2.19 | 0.18 (ns) | 90% |
| Plasma vs sandblasting (sensitivity) | 5 | — | 1.49 | 0.34-2.63 | 0.01 | 87% |

High heterogeneity across all analyses (I²≥87%) due to plasma type, gas, exposure, comparator variability.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- All 14 studies in vitro — no clinical studies
- Language restriction (English only) may have introduced bias
- High heterogeneity limits pooled estimate interpretation
- Funnel plot / Egger's test not possible (fewer than 10 studies per comparison)
- Standardized plasma protocols needed (gas composition, power, time, distance)
- Clinical studies on long-term zirconia restoration outcomes lacking

## 6. Related Work
- Kamis 2023: Single in vitro study showing CAP benefits (now part of this SR's evidence base)
- Nasri 2025: Umbrella review on surface conditioning — plasma SR fills a gap it identified
- Ito 2016: First study showing plasma ≈ APA while preserving crystal structure (seminal finding)
- Bunz 2021: Key study showing plasma + APA + universal adhesive = best long-term bond durability
- Kim 2024: Outlier showing plasma alone reduced SBS for 3Y-TZP; composition-dependent effect

## 7. Glossary
- **CAP**: Cold Atmospheric Plasma (냉온 대기압 플라즈마) — plasma at near room temperature and atmospheric pressure
- **APPJ**: Atmospheric Pressure Plasma Jet
- **SMD**: Standardized Mean Difference — effect size metric for meta-analysis
- **10-MDP**: 10-methacryloyloxydecyl dihydrogen phosphate — phosphate monomer primer for zirconia
- **QUIN**: Quality Assessment Tool for In Vitro Studies
