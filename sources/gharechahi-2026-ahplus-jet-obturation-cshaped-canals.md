## Why Ingested

PubMed-full-text monitoring catch for **AH Plus Jet**, the newer premixed-syringe reformulation of the epoxy-resin gold-standard AH Plus, tested here for the first time (per the authors) in C-shaped canal anatomy. Extends the wiki's existing C-shaped-canal prevalence evidence in [[wiki/endodontics/anatomy/tulegenova-2025-cshaped-canal-second-mandibular-premolar-sr-ma]] (which quantifies *how often* C-shaped canals occur but says nothing about how to obturate them) by adding the obturation-technique dimension, and it extends the AH Plus material cluster seeded by [[wiki/endodontics/kunam-2024-wettability-ahplus-ceraseal-guttaflow]] and [[wiki/endodontics/shim-2025-retrieval-ahplus-bioceramic-ceraseal-retreatment]] into a new, anatomically challenging context.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study on 30 3D-printed replicas of C1-configuration C-shaped canals (from a CBCT-scanned mandibular molar), comparing cold lateral compaction (CLC), warm vertical compaction (WVC), and warm vertical hybrid compaction (HC), all obturated with AH Plus Jet sealer.

WVC produced the lowest void percentage in the middle (1.03%) and coronal (0.62%) thirds and the shortest obturation time (168 s vs 482.5 s for CLC and 341 s for HC, p<0.001); apical-third void differences were statistically significant (WVC 1.45% vs CLC/HC ~1.00%, p=0.036) but the effect size was small (0.218), indicating negligible clinical impact.

WVC is the most efficient and best-adapting technique for AH Plus Jet in C1 C-shaped canals, but findings come from resin 3D-printed replicas (no dentinal tubules, no periapical tissue) and require validation in long-term clinical trials.

## 세줄요약

CBCT로 확인된 하악대구치 C1형 C-shaped 근관을 3D 프린팅한 레플리카 30개(체외 실험)에 AH Plus Jet 실러로 냉측방가압(Cold Lateral Compaction, CLC), 온측방가압(Warm Vertical Compaction, WVC), 온혼합가압(Warm Vertical Hybrid Compaction, HC) 세 충전법을 비교.

WVC가 중간(1.03%)·치관(0.62%) 부위 미세공극(void, %)이 가장 낮고 충전시간도 가장 짧음(168초 vs CLC 482.5초, HC 341초, p<0.001); 근단부 공극 차이는 통계적으로 유의(WVC 1.45% vs CLC/HC 약 1.00%, p=0.036)했으나 효과크기가 작아(0.218) 임상적 의미는 미미.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First reported evaluation (per the authors) of **AH Plus Jet** — the premixed-syringe reformulation of AH Plus with adjustable delivery tips — specifically in **C-shaped canal** anatomy.
- Standardizes anatomical variability by using **3D-printed C1-configuration replicas** derived from a single CBCT-scanned mandibular molar, isolating the effect of obturation technique from tooth-to-tooth anatomical variation.
- Compares three techniques (CLC, WVC, HC) on both **sealing quality (void %/gutta-percha %/sealer % by third)** and **obturation time**, a clinically relevant efficiency metric rarely reported together with void analysis.
- Rejects the null hypothesis for middle/coronal thirds: technique materially affects filling quality with this sealer, though apical differences are statistically significant but clinically negligible (small effect size).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Sample origin**: 15 human mandibular molars with fused roots screened by CBCT (Planmeca ProMax 3D Classic, 0.125 mm slice thickness); one tooth with confirmed **C1 configuration** (per Fan et al. classification) selected.
- **Canal preparation**: working length via three #10 K-files; decoronated to 10 mm root length; shaped with M3 Pro Gold rotary to #30/0.04 (350 rpm, 1–2 Ncm torque); anti-curvature filing with H-files #15–25 in the isthmus; 1% NaOCl between files; final irrigation 17% EDTA (10 mL) + saline (2 mL).
- **3D-printed replicas**: high-resolution (75 μm) CBCT scan reconstructed in Planmeca Romexis 6.2, exported as .stl, printed at 4K resolution on an AccuFab-L4K printer with SG100 resin. Power analysis (α=0.05, power=0.8) determined **n=10 replicas per group**; 30 replicas total.
- **Obturation**: AH Plus Jet (Dentsply De Trey) sealer with CLC, WVC, or HC, performed by a calibrated endodontist under senior-endodontist supervision; fill time recorded per replica; samples incubated 1 week (37 °C, 100% humidity) to allow sealer setting.
- **Sectioning/imaging**: CNC-cut into thirds at 2 mm (apical), 5 mm (middle), 7 mm (coronal) from the apex using a 0.3 mm disc; stereomicroscope imaging at 32×; DinoCapture 2.0 software quantified gutta-percha %, sealer %, and void % per section.
- **Statistics**: one-way ANOVA with LSD post-hoc pairwise comparisons (SPSS v23.0), significance threshold p<0.05, effect sizes reported per comparison.

| Sealer, middle | 6.14±1.85 | 7.90±3.59 | 8.81±0.66 | 0.053 (NS) | 0.196 |
| Sealer, coronal | 1.47±0.39 | 3.02±1.13 | 3.39±0.79 | <0.001 | 0.525 |
| Void, apical | 1.45±0.55 | 1.00±0.21 | 1.00±0.43 | 0.036 | 0.218 |
| Void, middle | 1.03±0.37 | 2.24±0.39 | 1.46±0.38 | <0.001 | 0.652 |
| Void, coronal | 0.62±0.11 | 2.95±0.36 | 0.72±0.31 | <0.001 | 0.940 |

- **CLC**: highest gutta-percha % and lowest sealer % in the apical third (best apical adaptation), but progressively worse (higher void %) toward the coronal third — the opposite pattern from WVC.
- **WVC**: significantly higher gutta-percha % in middle and coronal thirds, lowest void % in those thirds, and lowest sealer % in the coronal third — attributed to injectable gutta-percha flow.
- **Treatment time**: CLC 482.50±6.20 s (slowest) > HC 341.00±19.12 s > WVC 168.00±24.29 s (fastest); all pairwise differences p<0.001.
- Apical void differences, while statistically significant (p=0.036), had a small effect size (0.218) — authors interpret this as **clinically negligible**. Middle/coronal void differences had medium-to-large effect sizes (0.652, 0.940) — clinically meaningful.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In-vitro design precludes assessment of long-term clinical/biological performance under dynamic physiological conditions.
- Only **C1 configuration** was tested; results may not generalize to C2/C3 morphologies.
- Periodontal ligament and periapical tissue pressures were not simulated, so sealer extrusion could not be evaluated.
- 3D-printed resin replicas differ from natural dentin in thermal conductivity and mechanical properties (no dentinal tubules), which may alter sealer penetration, setting behavior, and heat transfer during warm compaction techniques.
- Biological responses (periapical tissue irritation/inflammation) were not assessed.
- Authors explicitly call for validation in long-term clinical trials before changing practice.

## 7. Glossary

- **C-shaped canal** — a root canal configuration (most common in mandibular 2nd molars/premolars) with a ribbon-like, fused cross-section instead of discrete round canals; classified C1 (continuous C-shape at the orifice), C2, C3 per Fan et al.
- **AH Plus Jet** — premixed-syringe, adjustable-tip reformulation of AH Plus epoxy-resin sealer (Dentsply De Trey), designed to simplify delivery and reduce operator variability vs hand-mixed AH Plus.
- **CLC (Cold Lateral Compaction)** — spreader + accessory gutta-percha cones compacted laterally at room temperature.
- **WVC (Warm Vertical Compaction)** — heat-softened gutta-percha (down-pack + injectable backfill) compacted vertically; promotes flow into fins/isthmuses.
- **HC (Warm Vertical Hybrid Compaction)** — combines auxiliary gutta-percha cones with warm vertical compaction, aiming to merge thermal flow with lateral-compaction control.
- **Void %** — proportion of the sectioned canal cross-section area not occupied by gutta-percha or sealer, measured via stereomicroscope image analysis (DinoCapture 2.0).
- **Effect size** — magnitude-of-difference statistic reported alongside p-values here to distinguish statistically significant but clinically trivial differences (e.g., apical void, effect size 0.218) from clinically meaningful ones (e.g., coronal void, effect size 0.940).
