## Why Ingested

위키의 측방 상악동거상술 페이지들은 이식재 종류 비교(동종골 vs 이종골)에 집중해 있고([[sinus-lift/lateral/galindomoreno-2022-maxillary-sinus-floor-augmentation-comparing]], [[sinus-lift/lateral/keldal-2026-trabecular-bone-remodelling-following-lateral]]), **광생물조절(Photobiomodulation, PBM) 즉 저출력레이저치료(Low-Level Laser Therapy, LLLT)를 병용한 상악동거상술**은 보유 근거가 전무했다. 본 2×2 요인설계 무작위 대조 시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)은 6개월 조직생검과 CBCT를 함께 본 첫 페이지로, LLLT 병용의 임상적 이득이 없다는(오히려 경도 염증세포침윤만 유의하게 많다는) 탐색적 결과를 기록한다.

## Three-line Summary

Exploratory 2×2 factorial randomized clinical trial (single centre, Wrocław Medical University, Poland, 2023–2024; 20 patients, four groups of n=5) of lateral-window maxillary sinus augmentation with allogeneic (0.5 mm cortico-cancellous) vs xenogeneic (0.25–1.0 mm spongious) graft, each with or without adjunctive LLLT (635 nm, 6 J/cm², 1 min, days 0/3/7), assessed by CBCT bone gain and 6-month trephine-biopsy histology.

Bone gain was achieved in every site (implant placement in all 20): 7.53 ± 3.32 mm with LLLT vs 7.02 ± 2.00 mm without (NS), and 7.68 ± 2.77 mm with allograft vs 6.87 ± 2.67 mm with xenograft (NS); trabecular bone was present in all samples, and the only significant histological difference was more frequent mild inflammatory cell infiltrates in LLLT sites (60% vs 10%, p = 0.029).

Both graft classes were biocompatible and effective at 6 months, but adjunctive 635 nm LLLT produced no measurable clinical or radiological benefit in this underpowered exploratory cohort — and the trial reports semiquantitative histology scores rather than histomorphometry, no interaction analysis, and no sample-size calculation.

## 세줄요약

탐색적 2×2 요인설계 무작위 대조 시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 폴란드 브로츠와프 의과대학 단일기관, 2023–2024년, 20명, 4군 각 n=5): 측방창 상악동거상술에서 동종골(Allograft, 0.5 mm 피질-해면골 과립) 대 이종골(Xenograft, 0.25–1.0 mm 해면골 과립)에 저출력레이저치료(Low-Level Laser Therapy, LLLT; 635 nm, 6 J/cm², 1분, 수술 직후·3일·7일) 병용 여부를 더한 4군 비교, 평가는 원추형빔전산화단층촬영(Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT) 골증가량과 6개월 트레파인 생검 조직학.

전 군에서 골증가 달성(20명 전원 임플란트 식립): LLLT 군 7.53 ± 3.32 mm vs 대조군 7.02 ± 2.00 mm(유의차 없음), 동종골 7.68 ± 2.77 mm vs 이종골 6.87 ± 2.67 mm(유의차 없음); 전 표본에서 소주골 확인, 유일한 유의차는 LLLT 군의 경도 염증세포침윤 빈도(60% vs 10%, p = 0.029).

두 이식재 모두 6개월 시점 생체적합성·효과가 양호했으나 635 nm LLLT 병용은 임상·방사선학적 이득이 측정되지 않았고, 이는 표본수가 작은 탐색적 연구이며 조직형태계측 대신 반정량 점수만 쓰고 상호작용 분석·표본수 계산이 없다는 한계를 함께 읽어야 함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides the wiki's **first histological + CBCT evidence on adjunctive LLLT in lateral sinus augmentation** — a question the held pages (graft-class comparisons) do not address at all.
- Runs a **2×2 factorial** head-to-head of allogeneic vs xenogeneic graft *and* LLLT vs no LLLT in the same trial, so both factors are randomized and allocation-concealed (sealed opaque envelopes, 1:1:1:1).
- Pairs **6-month trephine biopsy histology with CBCT bone gain in the same patients** — histological verification that radiographically augmented height corresponds to real trabecular bone rather than fibrous tissue.
- Reports a **counter-intuitive direction of harm signal**: mild inflammatory cell infiltrates were significantly *more* frequent in LLLT-treated sites (60% vs 10%, p = 0.029), though not linked to worse organization or bone gain.
- Publishes **complete LLLT dosimetry** (635 nm, 0.5 cm² beam, 6 J/cm², 1 min continuous, three sessions) — the parameter set most sinus-lift LLLT reports leave incomplete; the authors themselves flag that 635 nm (visible red) penetrates less than near-infrared.
- Honestly labels the work **exploratory/hypothesis-generating**, states no sample-size calculation was performed (ethical limits on biopsy collection) and that interaction/four-group analyses were not run.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Design**: single-centre, parallel-group, exploratory 2×2 factorial RCT; histological and radiological outcomes predefined as secondary endpoints of the parent anti-HLA trial.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| n | 20 patients, 4 groups × n = 5 |
| Groups | G1 xenograft; G2 xenograft + LLLT; G3 allograft; G4 allograft + LLLT |
| Randomization | simple randomization by an independent investigator (A.B.) not performing surgery; sealed opaque envelopes opened after enrolment |
| Allocation ratio | 1:1:1:1 |
| Inclusion | age ≥ 18; partial edentulism posterior maxilla; **RBH < 5 mm**; ridge width > 7 mm; ≥ 2 mm keratinized tissue height |
| Exclusion | plaque index > 20%; pregnancy/lactation; previous sinus surgery in the area; active sinus infection or large cysts; systemic contraindications (uncontrolled metabolic disease, immunosuppression, severe cardiovascular disease, head/neck radiotherapy) |

**Graft materials**

| Group factor | Material | Particle size | Manufacturer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allogeneic | cortico-cancellous bone, granules | 0.5 mm | Biobank, Lieusaint, France |
| Xenogeneic | spongious bone, granules | 0.25–1.0 mm | Geistlich, Wolhusen, Switzerland |

Both groups: lateral antrostomy with DASK® sinus kit (Dentium, Suwon, Korea), 8 mm diamond drill window under saline irrigation, membrane elevation, graft fill, resorbable collagen membrane coverage (Bio-Gide®, Geistlich), 5/0 nylon closure. Graft volume determined intraoperatively and **not quantitatively recorded**.

**LLLT (photobiomodulation) protocol** — full parameters, as reported:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Device | diode laser, Lasotronix® (Piaseczno, Poland) |
| Wavelength | **635 nm** (visible red) |
| Beam diameter / spot | **0.5 cm²** |
| Energy density | **6 J/cm²** |
| Exposure time | **1 min** per session, continuous mode |
| Delivery | intraoral, tip over the osteotomy site and overlying soft tissue |
| Schedule | immediately after surgery + postoperative days **3 and 7** (3 sessions total) |
| Rationale | authors place it within the common intraoral therapeutic range 630–660 nm, 4–10 J/cm² [ref 12] |

**Radiology**: CBCT (Planmeca Viso® G7; FOV 300 × 200 mm), Romexis 6.0. RBH and postoperative bone height measured at the planned implant site on standardized cross-sections perpendicular to the crest; **BG = postoperative bone height − RBH**. Assessed by a blinded investigator not involved in surgery, at baseline and 6 months.

**Histology**: at implant placement 6 months post-augmentation, a cylindrical biopsy (new bone + graft) was taken with a Khoury–Meisinger trephine (Hager & Meisinger, Neuss, Germany), fixed in 4% formalin, decalcified in 10% EDTA, paraffin-embedded, cut at 4 µm, H&E stained, read at 20× on an Olympus BX41 by **two blinded authors** (A.P., P.D.) with consensus on discrepancies.

**Semiquantitative scoring (0–3 / 1–3, no histomorphometry)**

| Parameter | Scale |
|---|---|
| Inflammatory cell infiltrates (ICI) | 0 = <1 ICI/HPF; 1 = <3; 2 = <5; 3 = <8 per HPF |
| Trabecular bone-to-connective tissue ratio (TB/CT) | 1 = trabecular predominates; 2 = comparable amounts; 3 = loose connective tissue predominates |
| Bone morphology (BM) | 1 = numerous large bone areas, few small islands; 2 = moderate; 3 = scarce large areas, numerous small islands |

**Statistics**: STATISTICA 13.3; non-parametric tests (Mann–Whitney U) because of small n and non-normal distributions; categorical variables by independence tests; α = 0.05. Comparisons made **separately for each main factor only** (graft type; LLLT) — no interaction term and no four-group analysis; no formal sample-size calculation.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Course**: all 20 patients tolerated surgery, no major complications, all attended follow-up and received implants. All groups reached bone gain sufficient for implant placement.

**Demographics** (Table 1 not available in retrieved text): G1 and G3 mostly male, G2 and G4 female-predominant; mean age ~45 y (G1, G2), ~52 y (G3), slightly over 59 y (G4); differences not statistically significant.

**Residual bone height at baseline (mm, mean ± SD)**

| Comparison | Group 1 | Group 2 | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| LLLT vs no LLLT | 2.80 ± 1.25 | 2.31 ± 1.76 | NS |
| Allograft vs xenograft | 2.89 ± 1.76 | 2.00 ± 1.21 | NS |

Baseline anatomy was comparable, i.e. the graft-type comparison started with *higher* RBH in the xenograft-allocated factor.

**CBCT bone gain at 6 months (mm, mean ± SD)**

| Comparison | Test factor | Other factor | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| LLLT | 7.53 ± 3.32 | 7.02 ± 2.00 | +0.51 mm, NS; greater variance in LLLT |
| Graft type | 7.68 ± 2.77 (allograft) | 6.87 ± 2.67 (xenograft) | +0.81 mm for allograft, NS |

Note the direction conflict: bone gain was *higher* in the allograft factor despite the allograft factor having *lower* baseline RBH — the authors do not model this.

**Histology (all 20 samples contained bone tissue)**

| Parameter | Finding | Statistics |
|---|---|---|
| Inflammatory cell infiltrates | scores predominantly 0–1, **no sample above 2**; mild infiltrates in **60% of LLLT sites vs 10% of controls** | **p = 0.029** (only significant result) |
| TB/CT ratio | most samples scored "comparable amounts" of trabecular bone and loose connective tissue (intermediate regeneration) | NS between groups |
| Bone morphology | mixed larger mineralized areas + smaller trabecular structures = intermediate-stage regeneration at 6 months; scores consistent across groups | NS; qualitative "more uniform trabeculation" in LLLT (80% vs 70%) |

**Authors' interpretation**: the inflammatory finding is *not* read as a harmful pro-inflammatory effect but as altered healing kinetics / ongoing remodeling at 6 months; the absence of an LLLT benefit is attributed partly to the sinus's already highly vascular, osteoconductive environment (ceiling effect) and partly to **limited penetration depth of 635 nm red light** into the deep grafted compartment versus near-infrared wavelengths.

**Comparison numbers cited from other studies (Discussion, not this trial's data)**: Mazor et al. RBH ~2.9 ± 0.9 mm with bone gain 10.1 ± 0.9 mm; Tajima et al. bone height 4.28 ± 1.00 → 11.8 ± 1.67 mm.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Small n (5/group)**: no sample-size calculation (declared as an ethical constraint of bone biopsy collection); no interaction or four-group analysis, so the 2×2 design is analysed as two separate one-factor comparisons.
- **No histomorphometry and no micro-CT**: histology is semiquantitative ordinal scoring (ICI, TB/CT, BM), so new-bone %, residual graft % and bone-to-implant contact are not reported. This blocks pooling with the histomorphometric literature held in the wiki (e.g. PRF/BCP sinus meta-analyses).
- **Single 6-month time point**: sinus augmentation is a long-term remodeling process; no implant stability, ISQ, survival or loading data.
- **LLLT dosimetry limits**: single 635 nm red wavelength (3 sessions; days 0/3/7); near-infrared would penetrate the grafted compartment better; LLLT parameter heterogeneity across the literature is acknowledged. Whether the inflammatory signal is a dose effect or a chance finding in n=20 cannot be separated.
- **No immunohistochemistry**, so the phenotype of the observed infiltrates is unknown.
- **Graft volume not recorded** — determined intraoperatively by cavity size, a plausible source of bone-gain variability.
- **Data-scope limitation of this ingest**: the retrieved PMC text omits Tables 1–4 and the reference list, so per-group (G1–G4) values and exact test statistics beyond the reported p values are not available on this page. No briefing figure was used; all numbers above are read from the article prose.

## 6. Related Work

- **Allograft vs xenograft in the sinus, tissue level** — Galindo-Moreno et al. 2022 (split-mouth RCT, bovine vs porcine xenograft with autogenous bone): no histomorphometric difference; consistent with the present null graft-type result.
- **LLLT as sinus-lift adjunct, histomorphometric** — the authors cite a split-mouth study of 8 participants (FDBA + PRF) reporting significantly greater new bone and radiological density in irradiated sites, and a 660/808 nm study reporting higher bone content/maturation in xenograft sinuses, alongside a 620 ± 2 nm study finding no difference in inflammation, bone quality or collagen maturity — i.e. the LLLT literature is small, heterogeneous and contradictory.
- **Radiographic remodeling of allograft vs xenograft** — Keldal et al. 2026 (fractal dimension, allograft vs xenograft) reports convergence by 6 months, matching the present absence of a 6-month radiographic difference.
- **Graft-class hierarchy** — Somngam et al. 2025 network meta-analysis (autograft > allograft > BCP > TCP > xenograft for new bone) and Pogacian-Maier 2024 (allograft vs DBB, no pooled difference) set the expectation against which this trial's +0.81 mm allograft trend should be read as noise, not signal.
- **Barrier membrane** — both arms used a resorbable collagen membrane over the window (Bio-Gide), so membrane-related effects are held constant (cf. Jabbari 2024 / Starch-Jensen 2019 on window coverage).

## 7. Glossary

- **LLLT (Low-Level Laser Therapy)**: low-dose laser/photobiomodulation using red or near-infrared light to stimulate mitochondrial ATP production, cytokine modulation and fibroblast/osteoblast proliferation without thermal damage.
- **Photobiomodulation (PBM)**: the broader term for light-based cellular modulation; LLLT is one delivery form.
- **RBH (Residual Bone Height)**: pre-augmentation vertical bone height beneath the sinus floor; <5 mm here was an inclusion criterion.
- **BG (Bone Gain)**: postoperative bone height minus RBH on CBCT cross-sections at the planned implant site.
- **ICI (Inflammatory Cell Infiltrates)**: inflammatory cells per high-power field, scored 0–3 in this study.
- **TB/CT (Trabecular Bone-to-Connective Tissue ratio)**: semiquantitative maturation score (1 = bone predominates → 3 = connective tissue predominates).
- **BM (Bone Morphology)**: semiquantitative score of large mineralized areas versus small bone islands.
- **2×2 factorial design**: two randomized factors (graft type; LLLT) crossed into four cells, permitting main-effect comparisons with half the patients of four separate trials.
