## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (103 patients, 103 internal-hex implants of 4.6 mm diameter with regular/horizontally matching connections) comparing 1-year crestal bone loss in untreated thin mucosa, thin mucosa thickened with an allogenic membrane, and naturally thick mucosa.

One-year crestal bone loss was 1.65 ± 0.08 mm mesial / 1.81 ± 0.06 mm distal in untreated thin tissue (A, n=34), 0.31 ± 0.05 / 0.34 ± 0.05 mm after membrane thickening (B, n=35), and 0.44 ± 0.06 / 0.47 ± 0.07 mm in naturally thick tissue (C, n=34); A vs B and A vs C were significant (p = .000) both mesially and distally, while B vs C was not (p = .166 mesial, p = .255 distal).

Thin mucosa drives early crestal bone loss even around matching-connection implants, and allogenic-membrane thickening reduces resorption to the level seen in naturally thick tissue — a soft-tissue route to crestal preservation independent of platform switching.

## 세줄요약

전향적 대조 임상시험(환자 103명, 직경 4.6 mm 내측 육각·수평 일치 연결 임플란트 103개) — 치료하지 않은 얇은 점막, 동종이식막(Allogenic Membrane)으로 두껍게 한 얇은 점막, 선천적으로 두꺼운 점막에서 1년 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss)을 비교.

1년 골소실: A군(얇은 점막, n=34) 근심 1.65 ± 0.08 mm·원심 1.81 ± 0.06 mm, B군(막으로 두껍게, n=35) 0.31 ± 0.05·0.34 ± 0.05 mm, C군(두꺼운 점막, n=34) 0.44 ± 0.06·0.47 ± 0.07 mm; A-B·A-C 간 유의(p = .000), B-C 간 비유의(근심 p = .166, 원심 p = .255).

임상 함의: 수평 일치 연결 임플란트에서도 얇은 점막이 초기 변연골 소실을 유발하며, 동종이식막으로 연조직을 두껍게 하면 자연적 두꺼운 점막 수준까지 골소실을 줄일 수 있다 — 플랫폼 스위칭(PS, Platform Switching)과 별개의 연조직 경로.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly tests whether thickening thin peri-implant mucosa with an allogenic membrane reduces crestal bone loss around horizontally matching (non-platform-switched) regular-connection implants.
- Holds implant geometry constant — all 103 implants were 4.6 mm internal-hex with a regular connection — so the compared variable is soft-tissue thickness, not connection design.
- Shows thickened thin tissue (group B: 0.31/0.34 mm) becomes statistically indistinguishable from naturally thick tissue (group C: 0.44/0.47 mm), both far below untreated thin tissue (group A: 1.65/1.81 mm).
- Supplies a soft-tissue-modification counterpart to the wiki's two established crestal-bone strategies: the horizontal offset of platform switching and the vertical microgap/collar position.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective controlled clinical trial; patients assigned to groups according to gingiva thickness (not randomized)
- **Databases**: none — primary clinical trial
- **n**: 103 patients receiving 103 internal hex implants, 4.6 mm diameter, regular connection; group A thin tissue n=34, group B thin tissue thickened with allogenic membrane n=35, group C thick tissue n=34
- **Surgical approach**: groups A and C one-stage; group B two-stage
- **Radiographic assessment**: after implant placement, 2 months after healing, after restoration, and after 1-year follow-up
- **Outcomes**: crestal bone loss calculated mesially and distally; significance threshold 0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | Tissue | n | 1-year mesial bone loss | 1-year distal bone loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Thin, untreated (one-stage) | 34 | 1.65 ± 0.08 mm | 1.81 ± 0.06 mm |
| B | Thin, allogenic-membrane thickening (two-stage) | 35 | 0.31 ± 0.05 mm | 0.34 ± 0.05 mm |
| C | Naturally thick (one-stage) | 34 | 0.44 ± 0.06 mm | 0.47 ± 0.07 mm |

- A vs B and A vs C: significant (p = .000) both mesially and distally.
- B vs C: not significant mesially (p = .166) or distally (p = .255).
- Authors' conclusion: thin mucosal tissues may cause early crestal bone loss, their thickening with allogenic membrane may significantly reduce bone resorption, and implants in naturally thick soft tissues experience minor bone remodeling.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 1-year follow-up reported in the retrieved text; no longer-term crestal stability data.
- Group allocation was by gingiva thickness rather than randomization, and group B also differed in surgical approach (two-stage vs one-stage in A and C) — membrane thickening and staging are confounded.
- The retrieved artifact is a Wiley HTML-print rendering; author affiliation/institution and some methodological detail are absent from its extracted text.
- No patient-reported, esthetic, or soft-tissue-graft stability outcomes appear in the retrieved text.

## 6. Related Work

- Linkevicius 2015 (this paper): matching-connection implants; soft-tissue thickening as the modifier of crestal bone loss.
- Terzioglu 2024: independent RCT showing supracrestal tissue height ≤ 3 mm with equicrestal placement maximizes MBL in platform-switched implants — same thin-tissue risk direction, different connection geometry.
- Schwarz 2013 (Camlog consensus): connection type and microgap/collar position as crestal-bone variables; this paper instead fixes the connection and varies the soft tissue.
- Platform switching synthesis overview (2026): the horizontal-offset route to MBL preservation, to which this paper contributes the matching-connection counterpart.

## 7. Glossary

- **Horizontally Matching Connection**: implant-abutment connection in which the abutment diameter matches the implant platform (regular connection), as opposed to platform switching.
- **Allogenic Membrane**: donor-derived membrane used here to thicken thin peri-implant mucosa.
- **Crestal Bone Loss**: radiographic vertical reduction of the marginal bone crest around an implant, measured here mesially and distally.
- **Internal Hex**: internal hexagonal implant-abutment connection geometry; the connection used in all 103 implants of this trial.
- **One-Stage / Two-Stage Approach**: single-surgery placement with transmucosal healing (one-stage) versus submerged placement with a subsequent uncovering surgery (two-stage).
