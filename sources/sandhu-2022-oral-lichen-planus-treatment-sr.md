## Why Ingested

Adds a cost-aware comparative-efficacy lens to OLP therapy that the efficacy-only Cochrane review [[oral-medicine/lodi-2020-oral-lichen-planus-corticosteroids-cochrane]] omits, and complements [[oral-medicine/srivastava-2022-oral-lichen-planus-diagnosis-management-review]].

## Three-line Summary

PRISMA systematic review (BMC Oral Health 2022) comparing both reported efficacy and direct treatment cost across oral lichen planus (OLP) interventions — topical corticosteroids, calcineurin inhibitors (tacrolimus), and systemic agents — representing one of the few OLP reviews to jointly evaluate cost alongside efficacy.

Topical corticosteroids delivered favorable efficacy at relatively low cost; tacrolimus was effective but more expensive; however, cost reporting across the included literature was inconsistent, preventing formal cost-effectiveness conclusions.

The combination of heterogeneous/incomplete cost data and underlying trial quality limitations means all conclusions are qualified; standardized cost reporting in future OLP trials is required before definitive value-based guidance can be given.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA 체계적 문헌고찰 (BMC Oral Health 2022) — 구강 편평태선 (Oral Lichen Planus, OLP) 치료제(국소 코르티코스테로이드·칼시뉴린 억제제·전신약물)의 보고된 효능과 직접 치료비용을 동시에 비교한 몇 안 되는 리뷰 중 하나.

국소 코르티코스테로이드는 비교적 낮은 비용에 양호한 효능을 제공했고, 타크로리무스(Tacrolimus)는 효과적이나 더 비쌌으나, 포함된 문헌에서 비용 보고가 일관되지 않아 공식적인 비용-효과 결론 도출이 불가했음.

비용 데이터의 이질성·불완전성과 기초 임상시험의 근거 질 한계가 겹쳐 모든 결론이 한정적이며, 가치 기반의 OLP 치료 지침을 위해서는 향후 임상시험에서 표준화된 비용 보고가 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the few OLP reviews to jointly weigh efficacy and direct treatment cost.
- Provides a comparative framework across topical corticosteroids, calcineurin inhibitors, and systemic agents.
- Surfaces cost as an under-reported decision variable in OLP management.
- Confirms corticosteroids as the practical first-line on efficacy-cost grounds.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Systematic literature search of OLP intervention studies reporting efficacy and/or cost; qualitative synthesis of comparative efficacy and cost data.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Topical corticosteroids deliver favorable efficacy at relatively low cost; calcineurin inhibitors (tacrolimus) effective but more expensive.
- Cost reporting across the literature is inconsistent, limiting formal cost-effectiveness conclusions.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Heterogeneous, often incomplete cost reporting; no formal cost-effectiveness modeling; efficacy evidence limited by underlying trial quality.
