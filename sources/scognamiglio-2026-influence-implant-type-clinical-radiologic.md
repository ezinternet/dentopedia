## Why Ingested
Direct 3-year retrospective comparison of Straumann Tissue-Level (TL) vs BLX implants in private practice — the first wiki paper to specifically compare TL against BLX (fully-tapered modern BL design), adding real-world practice data to the existing RCT/SR base. Extends [[overviews/tissue-level-vs-bone-level-implant-outcomes]] with a modern BLX platform and a private practice setting not covered by [[implants/mbl/pirc-2026-comparative-analysis-one-piece-tissue-level]].

## One-line Summary
Retrospective private practice analysis (n=87 patients, 146 implants; 73 TL vs 73 BLX Straumann, 3-year follow-up) found TL implants had 100% survival vs 98% for BLX, with TL showing statistically superior mesial bone levels at 3 years (Δ=0.21mm, p=0.041), though baseline group differences (more GBR and immediate placements in BLX) limit causal interpretation.

## 한줄요약
사설 치과의원 후향적 분석 (n=87명/146 임플란트; 티슈 레벨 [Tissue Level, TL] 73개 vs BLX 73개, 3년): TL 생존율 (Survival Rate, SR) 100% vs BLX 98%, TL의 근심 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 이 3년 시점에 유의하게 우수 (Δ=0.21mm, p=0.041) — 단 BLX 군에 더 많은 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) 및 즉시 식립이 포함되어 인과 해석 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First direct comparison of TL vs BLX Straumann implants in private practice setting
- TL showed bone gain at mesial surface between 2-3yr (0.08mm, p=0.018) — recovery after initial 6-month remodeling
- BLX showed significant distal bone loss over 3yr (0.15mm, p=0.038)
- BLX had 3 failures, 3 chipping events, 1 mucositis; TL had 0 complications — all complications in BLX group
- KM (Keratinized Mucosa) >2mm especially beneficial for BLX: smaller bone level alterations

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Retrospective, non-interventional, cross-sectional single-center private practice study
- n: 87 patients, 146 implants (73 TL Straumann Standard Plus SLA vs 73 BLX Straumann SLActive)
- Time points: 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 3 years
- Radiographic assessment: standardized periapical radiographs at each time point
- Statistical threshold: p < 0.05
- **Key baseline confounds**: BLX group had significantly more GBR (56 vs 17), more anterior maxilla placement, and all 24 immediate placements were in BLX group; TL predominantly placed in posterior mandible

|---|---|---|---|
| Survival rate (3 yr) | 100% | 98% (3 failures) | — |
| Peri-implantitis (3 yr) | 0 | 0 | NS |
| Mesial bone gain (2→3yr) | +0.08mm (p=0.018) | Not reported | — |
| Mesial MBL difference at 3yr | Better by 0.21mm | — | p=0.041 |
| Distal bone loss over 3yr | Not significant | 0.15mm (p=0.038) | — |
| BLX complications total | 0 | 3 chipping + 1 mucositis + 3 failures | — |
| MBL vs Albrektsson limit | <0.15mm for both | within limits | Both compliant |

- Initial TL bone loss at 6 months (distal), then recovery and gain at 2-3yr
- BLX: no early bone loss, progressive late bone loss at distal
- KM >2mm especially protective for BLX outcomes

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Non-interventional retrospective — groups differ significantly at baseline (BLX: more GBR, more anterior, all immediate placements; TL: predominantly posterior mandible)
- Cannot rule out confounding from site-specific factors (GBR, immediate placement)
- Single private practice center (Mendrisio, Switzerland) — generalizability limited
- 3-year maximum follow-up — bone remodeling patterns may diverge further
- Small n per group (73 each) — no multivariable adjustment reported

## 6. Related Work
- Pirc 2026: 15-17yr RCT (1-piece TL vs 2-piece BL); TL MBL 0.08mm vs BL 1.53mm
- Scognamiglio adds BLX-specific data (modern fully-tapered BL) vs traditional 2-piece BL in Pirc
- IAC position (supragingival in TL, at/below crest in BLX) identified as key mechanism

## 7. Glossary
- **TL**: Tissue-Level implant (Straumann Standard Plus SLA) — IAC supragingival
- **BLX**: Bone-Level X implant (Straumann SLActive) — fully tapered, self-cutting; IAC at/below crest
- **IAC**: Implant-Abutment Connection position
- **KM**: Keratinized Mucosa
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Level (radiographic)
- **GBR**: Guided Bone Regeneration
- **Albrektsson limit**: <0.15mm/year MBL after first year — success criterion
