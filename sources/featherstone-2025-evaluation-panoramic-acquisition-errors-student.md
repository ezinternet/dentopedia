## Why Ingested

This study is the direct training-population counterpart to the clinical-setting audit we already hold in [[wiki/radiology/lingam-2023-common-errors-subjective-quality-panoramic]] — the same panoramic error taxonomy applied longitudinally to 52 predoctoral students across four formative plus one summative assessment, quantifying which positioning errors persist despite instruction and whether error count predicts diagnostic adequacy.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective review of 263 panoramic exposures (Dentsply Sirona Orthophos SL 2D/3D) made by 52 third-year (D3) predoctoral students across 4 formative assessments + 1 final summative assessment (East Carolina University, 2023–2024), grading 13 technical error categories.

Most common errors: chin up 41.02%, head rotation 24.84%, tongue placement 17.19%, chin down 16.36%, head tilt 9.91%, foreign objects 9.14%; error probabilities showed no statistically significant change across assessments, and only 4/263 images were nondiagnostic.

Students reached diagnostic competence (all passed the summative assessment first attempt), but Frankfort-plane, midsagittal-plane, and spinal-slumping errors trended upward at the SA — marking them as priority teaching targets and showing error count alone does not predict diagnostic adequacy.

## 2. Key Contributions
- **First longitudinal (learning-curve) error analysis** of predoctoral panoramic acquisition — 4 FAs + final SA, versus prior single-time-point studies
- **Quantified error-type distribution** in a supervised student setting: 383 errors across 263 exposures, with a tiered frequency ranking (chin up ≫ rotation > tongue > chin down)
- **Error quantity ≠ diagnostic inadequacy**: over 80% of exposures had 1–4 errors, yet only 4/263 (1.5%) were nondiagnostic — severity, not count, drives diagnostic adequacy
- **Identified persistent teaching targets**: Frankfort (chin up/down), midsagittal, and spinal errors persisted and rose at the independent SA (zero assistance), isolating them from errors that improve with instruction (e.g., exposure)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: retrospective analysis of radiographs from scheduled D3 clinical assessments
- **Subjects**: 52 third-year students (class of 2023–2024); patients from the Comprehensive Care Dental Clinic (predominantly adults >18 yr)
- **Device**: Dentsply Sirona Orthophos SL 2D/3D; 90 kV, 160 mA, ~14 s; settings adjustable for body habitus
- **Assessments**: 4 FAs (assistance from licensed dental assistants/radiology technologists allowed) + 1 SA (independent, no assistance); 51/55/53/52/52 exposures = 263 total
- **Taxonomy**: 13 errors — Frankfort plane (chin up/down), midsagittal (rotation/tilt), focal trough (forward/backward), tongue placement, foreign object, spinal slumping, patient motion, over/underexposure, lead apron artifact
- **Raters**: 2 dental radiology technologists (>20 yr each, calibrated biannually); Cohen's kappa 0.95 on 10 random images
- **Stats**: Clopper–Pearson 95% CI for error probabilities; SCORE method for between-assessment change; SAS 9.4M8; p<0.05 significant

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single D3 class (n=52), single institution, single device — generalizability limited
- FA allowed auxiliary assistance while the SA did not → possible bias (mitigated: assistance sought decreased over time)
- SA error increase speculated (not measured) to reflect exam stress/anxiety — a questionnaire study proposed
- Variability in students' prior experience; no stratification by experience or learning style
- Retrospective design; intervals between assessments varied with patient assignment

## 7. Glossary
- **Frankfort plane**: line connecting the inferior orbital rim to the superior margin of the external auditory meatus; deviation (chin up/down) flattens or over-curves the occlusal plane
- **Midsagittal plane**: midline plane; rotation magnifies one side, tilt displaces condylar heights asymmetrically
- **Focal trough**: the curved zone of sharp focus in panoramic imaging; forward/backward placement shrinks or enlarges anterior teeth
- **Nondiagnostic image**: radiograph whose errors preclude diagnostic interpretation — requires retake
- **Formative vs summative assessment**: practice (feedback, assistance allowed) vs final independent skills test
- **Clopper–Pearson interval**: exact binomial 95% confidence interval for error probabilities
- **SCORE method**: statistical approach used to compare error-pattern changes across assessments
