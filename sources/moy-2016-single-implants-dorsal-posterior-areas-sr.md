## Three-line Summary

Moy et al. (2016) systematically reviewed 36 studies (1985–2014) on single implants in posterior quadrants (molar and premolar regions) of the maxilla and mandible. Immediate placement yielded 96.9% survival and 0.85 mm mean bone loss; delayed placement yielded 96.8% survival and 0.55 mm bone loss. No significant difference in survival was found between placement timing protocols; success rates were slightly higher for delayed loading.

## 세줄요약

Moy 등(2016)은 구치부 단일 임플란트 36편(1985–2014)을 체계적으로 고찰했다. 즉시 식립 생존율 96.9%·골소실 0.85 mm, 지연 식립 생존율 96.8%·골소실 0.55 mm였다. 생존율은 두 군 간 유의한 차이가 없었으며, 성공률은 지연 로딩 프로토콜에서 소폭 높았다.

## Study Design

Systematic review. Three databases searched (MEDLINE/PubMed, Ovid MEDLINE, Cochrane CENTRAL) for publications 1985–2014. 141 articles reviewed; 36 met inclusion criteria.

## Population

Single missing teeth in posterior quadrants (maxilla and/or mandible) replaced with implant-supported dental prostheses. Includes molar and premolar regions.

## Key Outcomes

### Immediate implant placement (standalone group)
- Survival rate: 96.9%
- Success rate: 100%
- Mean bone loss: 0.85 mm

### Delayed implant placement (standalone group)
- Survival rate: 96.8%
- Success rate: 94.1%
- Mean bone loss: 0.55 mm

### Head-to-head comparison (immediate vs delayed)
| Outcome | Immediate | Delayed |
|---|---|---|
| Survival rate | 96.8% | 96.3% |
| Success rate | 85.8% | 93.3% |
| Mean bone loss | 0.57 ± 0.57 mm | 0.55 ± 0.37 mm |

## Conclusions

- Single molar implants are a viable treatment option for replacing single missing teeth in posterior quadrants of maxilla and mandible.
- No significant difference in survival rates between immediately placed and delayed implant placement.
- Success rates slightly higher with delayed loading protocols than immediate loading protocols.
