## Why Ingested
발치 후 소켓에 L-PRF 적용이 통증 감소·연조직 치유를 개선한다는 초기 임상 근거. [[pdrn/chmielewski-2024-aprf-oral-maxillofacial-surgery-sr]] A-PRF SR 및 [[oral-surgery/dinkova-2025-local-hemostasis-oral-surgery-review]] 지혈제 리뷰에서 인용되는 L-PRF 효과의 직접 임상 데이터 원천.

## One-line Summary
Prospective split-mouth RCT (n=26, 108 extractions) showing L-PRF socket filling reduced postextraction pain (VAS 3.2 vs 4.1) and improved soft tissue healing index at 7 days compared to natural clot healing.

## 한줄요약
전향적 분구강 RCT(n=26, 108발치): L-PRF 소켓 충전이 대조군 대비 수술 후 통증 감소(VAS 3.2 vs 4.1) 및 7일 연조직 치유 지수 개선을 보임.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: BioMed Research International, Volume 2015, Article ID 369273
- DOI: 10.1155/2015/369273
- Study period: January 2012–July 2013, University of Naples Federico II
- Funding: Not reported; open access (Creative Commons Attribution License)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First split-mouth RCT demonstrating L-PRF reduces postextraction pain vs natural socket healing
- Quantified pain reduction: VAS 3.2±0.3 (L-PRF) vs 4.1±0.1 (control) across 24–96h
- Evaluated modified Healing Index (mHI) 4–12 scale at 3,7,14,21 days
- Demonstrated L-PRF practical feasibility in daily private practice (low cost, simple preparation)
- L-PRF releases growth factors (PDGF-AB, TGF-β, VEGF) for >7 days vs PRP's 3-day dissolution

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: prospective single-blind randomized split-mouth clinical trial
- N: 26 patients (9 males, 17 females; mean age 53±4 years); 108 total extractions
- Inclusion: nonsmokers/light smokers (<5/day), no systemic disease, bilateral paired extractions
- Randomization: coin toss for side allocation (L-PRF vs natural healing)
- Intervention: L-PRF clot placed into test socket; control socket allowed natural clot formation
- Suture: 3/0 Vicryl; no full-thickness flap elevation; buccal/lingual walls preserved
- Outcomes: VAS pain (24, 48, 72, 96h); modified Healing Index at 3,7,14,21 days

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Mean postextraction pain: VAS 3.2±0.3 (L-PRF) vs 4.1±0.1 (control)
- Modified Healing Index at 7 days: 4.8±0.6 (L-PRF) vs 5.1±0.9 (control)
- Statistically reduced early inflammation adverse effects in L-PRF group
- No dry socket reported in L-PRF group (exact n not stated for control)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single-blind only (surgeon not blinded)
- Small sample (n=26); light smoker inclusion may affect healing outcomes
- Modified Healing Index difference (4.8 vs 5.1) clinically modest
- No long-term (>21 day) follow-up; socket volumetric/bone regeneration not assessed
- Anesthesia: mepivacaine (no epinephrine) — limits generalizability to vasoconstrictor use

## 7. Glossary
- L-PRF (Leukocyte- and Platelet-Rich Fibrin): 2nd-generation platelet concentrate, solid fibrin matrix, no anticoagulant, centrifuged ~12min at 2700 rpm
- VAS (Visual Analogue Scale): 1–10 pain scale
- modified Healing Index (mHI): 4–12 scale for soft tissue wound healing assessment
- PDGF-AB, TGF-β, VEGF: growth factors released from L-PRF α-granules promoting neoangiogenesis and healing
