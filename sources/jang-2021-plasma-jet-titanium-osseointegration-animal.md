## Three-line Summary

In vivo study in 6 mongrel dogs (41 implants: 21 NTAPPJ-treated, 20 control) placed in both maxilla and mandible, evaluated at 4 or 8 weeks with bone volume (BV) and bone-to-implant contact (BIC) as endpoints.

Non-thermal atmospheric pressure plasma jet (NTAPPJ) treatment significantly increased BV and BIC at 4 weeks (p<0.05), but the differences diminished at 8 weeks as control-group values caught up.

The benefit of NTAPPJ is concentrated in the critical early healing period; long-term predictability beyond 8 weeks was not demonstrated in this animal model.

## 세줄요약

mongrel 개 6마리(임플란트 41개: NTAPPJ 처리 21·대조 20)를 대상으로 상·하악 모두에 식립 후 4주 또는 8주에 골량(BV)과 골-임플란트 접촉률(BIC)을 평가한 in vivo 연구이다.

비열 대기압 플라즈마 제트(NTAPPJ) 처리군이 4주에서 BV·BIC 모두 유의하게 높았으나(p<0.05), 8주에는 대조군이 따라잡아 두 군 간 차이가 줄었다.

플라즈마 이득은 초기 치유 결정적 시기에 집중되며, 8주 이후 장기 예측성은 이 동물 모델에서 입증되지 않았다.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: Materials 2021, 14(8):1942
- DOI: 10.3390/ma14081942
- Study type: in vivo animal (mongrel dog)
- Device: non-thermal atmospheric pressure plasma jet (NTAPPJ)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Clean demonstration of early-only benefit (4 wk significant, 8 wk converges).
- Maxilla + mandible placement; BV and BIC endpoints.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 41 implants (21 NTAPPJ, 20 control) in maxilla/mandible of 6 mongrel dogs; 4 or 8 weeks.
- BV and BIC by region of interest; Wilcoxon rank-sum test.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 4 weeks: NTAPPJ significantly higher BV and BIC than control (p<0.05).
- 8 weeks: differences less significant — control caught up.
- Conclusion: NTAPPJ aids the critical initial healing/stability period.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Benefit transient (early only); animal model.
- Long-term predictability inferred, not directly shown.
