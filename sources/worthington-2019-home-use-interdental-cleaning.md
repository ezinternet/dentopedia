## Why Ingested

Wen et al. 2026 (RCT on the effect of dental floss *sequence* — pre/mid/post-brushing — on plaque removal and periodontal health)의 reference list를 mining하다가 발견한 근거 논문. Wen 2026이 다루는 "치실을 언제 쓰느냐"라는 질문은 애초에 "치실이 toothbrushing 대비 효과가 있는가"라는 더 근본적인 질문 위에 서 있는데, 이 Cochrane SR+MA (35 RCTs, n=3929)가 바로 그 근본 질문에 대한 가장 포괄적인 근거다. 특히 이 리뷰의 결론(flossing 효과는 low-certainty이고 clinical importance가 불확실할 수 있음)은 [[wiki/interdental-cleaning/jung-2025-flossing-performance-plaque-removal]]가 최근에 "올바른 치실 술식도 치태 제거를 유의하게 개선하지 못한다"고 보고한 것과 같은 방향을 가리켜, 치실의 낮은 효능이 개별 연구의 우연이 아니라 device 수준의 일관된 신호임을 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Cochrane systematic review + meta-analysis of 35 RCTs (n=3929 adults) comparing home-use interdental cleaning devices (floss, interdental brushes, wooden/rubber cleaning sticks, oral irrigators) plus toothbrushing versus toothbrushing alone, and devices against each other.

Low- to very-low-certainty evidence suggests floss and interdental brushes may reduce gingivitis and/or plaque more than toothbrushing alone, with interdental brushes possibly outperforming floss; evidence for cleaning sticks and oral irrigators was limited and inconsistent, and no trial assessed interproximal caries.

Overall evidence certainty is low-to-very-low and effect sizes may not be clinically important — this is a foundational, frequently-cited "floor" reference for interdental device efficacy debates in this wiki's category.

## 세줄요약

Cochrane 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(35개 RCT, 성인 n=3929)으로, 가정용 치간청소용품(치실, 치간칫솔, 나무/고무 치간자극기, 구강세정기)을 칫솔질에 추가한 경우와 칫솔질 단독을 비교하고, 용품 간 비교도 수행.

Low~very low 확실성 근거에서 치실과 치간칫솔이 칫솔질 단독보다 치은염 및/또는 치태를 더 감소시킬 수 있으며 치간칫솔이 치실보다 우월할 가능성이 있음; 치간자극기와 구강세정기 근거는 제한적·비일관적이었고 어떤 연구도 치간 우식을 평가하지 않음.

전반적 근거 확실성이 낮아 효과크기가 임상적으로 중요하지 않을 수 있음 — 이 위키의 치간청소 카테고리에서 자주 인용되는 기초 근거(baseline reference) 문헌.

- **Journal**: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2019;4(4):CD012018
- **Publication date**: 2019-04-10
- **DOI**: 10.1002/14651858.CD012018.pub2
- **PMID**: 30968949 / **PMCID**: PMC6953268
- **Study type**: Cochrane systematic review + meta-analysis (Cochrane Oral Health group)
- **Retraction status**: Not retracted; standard Cochrane SR (verify prior to citing that no later Cochrane update has superseded it, as Cochrane reviews are periodically updated)

## 2. Key Contributions

- The most comprehensive pooled synthesis to date (at time of ingest) of RCT evidence on home interdental cleaning devices (floss, interdental brushes, wooden sticks, rubber/elastomeric sticks, oral irrigators) added to toothbrushing.
- Establishes GRADE-rated certainty levels (mostly low to very low) for each device comparison, providing a calibrated "how much should we trust this" baseline that later, higher-quality trials in this wiki (e.g. water-flosser RCTs) can be measured against.
- Explicitly documents an evidence gap: no included trial measured interproximal caries, and few assessed periodontitis — most outcomes are gingivitis/plaque surrogate markers only.
- Head-to-head secondary comparisons (interdental brush vs floss; oral irrigator vs floss; sticks vs brushes) give relative-effectiveness signal beyond the toothbrushing-alone baseline.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Cochrane systematic review with random-effects meta-analyses (mean differences / standardised mean differences).
- **Search**: Cochrane Oral Health's Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, CINAHL (to 16 Jan 2019); trial registries searched for ongoing studies; no language/date restriction.
- **Eligibility**: RCTs comparing toothbrushing + home-use interdental device vs toothbrushing alone or vs another device, minimum 4 weeks duration.
- **Data handling**: ≥2 review authors independently screened, extracted, and assessed risk of bias and GRADE certainty; interproximal-surface indices extracted preferentially.
- **Included studies**: 35 RCTs, 3929 randomised adults. High risk of performance bias throughout (blinding of device use not feasible); only 2 trials otherwise low risk of bias overall.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Floss vs toothbrushing alone**: gingivitis (GI) reduced at 1 month, SMD -0.58 (95% CI -1.12 to -0.04; 8 trials, 585 participants); low certainty. Bleeding/plaque results inconsistent (very low certainty).
- **Interdental brush vs toothbrushing alone**: gingivitis (GI) reduced at 1 month, MD -0.53 (95% CI -0.83 to -0.23; 1 trial, 62 participants), very low certainty; plaque reduced, SMD -1.07 (95% CI -1.51 to -0.63; 2 trials, 93 participants), low certainty.
- **Wooden sticks**: bleeding reduced at 3 months (MD -0.25, 1 trial, 24 participants), plaque not reduced; very low certainty.
- **Rubber/elastomeric sticks**: plaque reduced at 1 month (MD -0.22), gingivitis not reduced; very low certainty, tiny samples (n=12-30).
- **Oral irrigators**: gingivitis (GI) reduced at 1 month (SMD -0.48, 4 trials, 380 participants) but not sustained at 3-6 months; no plaque or bleeding-site benefit demonstrated over toothbrushing alone.
- **Device-vs-device**: interdental brushes may reduce gingivitis more than floss at 1 and 3 months (low certainty); oral irrigation may reduce gingivitis more than flossing at 1 month; sticks vs brushes/floss showed no clear superiority either way.
- **Safety**: no severe adverse events attributable to any device; no group differences in minor effects (e.g. gingival irritation).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Overall evidence certainty low to very low across nearly all comparisons — driven by unblindable interventions, small trials, and heterogeneous outcome measurement.
- No trial measured interproximal caries; periodontitis (vs gingivitis surrogate) rarely assessed — the review cannot speak to hard clinical endpoints.
- Most participants had low baseline gingival inflammation, limiting generalisability to periodontitis populations.
- Follow-up mostly short-term (1-6 months); long-term/maintenance effects unknown.
- Authors explicitly call for future trials to use the (then-new) 2018 periodontal disease classification and to run long enough to capture periodontitis and caries outcomes.

## 6. Related Work

- Reinforced by later, more granular device-vs-device RCTs in this wiki (e.g. water flosser vs interdental brush, water flosser vs floss SR) that refine this review's coarse "device vs toothbrushing alone" comparisons.
- Reinforced by [[wiki/interdental-cleaning/jung-2025-flossing-performance-plaque-removal]], which independently shows that even technically correct flossing does not substantially improve plaque removal — consistent with this review's low-certainty, possibly-not-clinically-important floss effect sizes.
- See [[wiki/overviews/interdental-cleaning-devices-synthesis]] for the wiki's cross-paper synthesis of the interdental-cleaning device evidence base, of which this Cochrane review is a foundational input.

## 7. Glossary

- **GI (Gingival Index)**: Löe-Silness index (0-3 scale) scoring gingival inflammation severity.
- **Quigley-Hein Index**: plaque index (0-5 scale) commonly used to score dental plaque accumulation.
- **SMD / MD**: standardised mean difference / mean difference — meta-analytic effect-size measures pooling across studies with differing outcome scales (SMD) or the same scale (MD).
- **GRADE**: framework (high/moderate/low/very low) for rating certainty of evidence in a systematic review.
- **Interproximal / interdental**: the space between adjacent teeth, not reachable by toothbrush bristles alone.
