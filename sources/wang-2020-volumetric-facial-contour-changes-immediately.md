## Why Ingested

This RCT provides 3D volumetric analysis of facial ridge changes after immediate implant placement with and without immediate provisionalization—a companion study to [[sources/chan-2019-randomized-controlled-trial-compare-aesthetic]] that extends the same RCT's findings by measuring morphometric soft-tissue contour preservation using digital model registration. Directly addresses whether immediate provisionalization improves esthetic volume retention in the esthetic zone.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized controlled trial (RCT), n=40 subjects with unrestorable maxillary anterior/premolar teeth receiving immediately-placed single implants; compared 12-month 3D facial ridge changes between provisional crown (test) and healing abutment (control) arms.

Linear 3D spatial resorption ranged from 0.1–0.7 mm with no significant intergroup difference; volumetric soft-tissue contour change differed significantly at 12 months, test 11.9% versus control 17.4% (P = 0.04) in favor of immediate provisionalization.

Immediate provisionalization showed superior volumetric soft-tissue preservation at the mid-facial margin and 2–6 mm above the crest (esthetic concern area), though linear marginal resorption measurements were similar between groups; suggests esthetic benefit independent of classical marginal-recession metrics.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First RCT to quantify 3D volumetric facial ridge contour changes using digital model voxel-based registration comparing immediate provisionalization versus healing abutment at immediately placed implants in the esthetic zone
- Demonstrated significant volumetric soft-tissue preservation benefit with immediate provisionalization at 12 months (11.9% vs 17.4% volume change, P = 0.04) despite similar linear resorption patterns between groups
- Identified that esthetic volume retention occurs specifically at the mid-facial margin and 2–6 mm coronal to crest—the clinically visible esthetic zone—independent of marginal-level recession measurements
- Provided evidence that immediate provisionalization confers a volumetric/morphometric esthetic advantage that may not be captured by traditional 2D clinical metrics (marginal level, papilla height)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized controlled trial (RCT), parallel assignment
- **Sample Size**: 40 participants (unrestorable maxillary anterior or premolar teeth; target population for esthetic single-implant replacement)
- **Inclusion Criteria**: Hopeless maxillary anterior or premolar tooth requiring extraction; sufficient bone for immediate implant placement without additional augmentation
- **Randomization**: Random allocation to test (immediate provisional crown) or control (standard healing abutment) immediately after implant placement
- **Intervention Groups**:
  - **Test (n=20)**: Immediate non-occluding provisional crown placed directly after implant insertion
  - **Control (n=20)**: Standard healing abutment without restoration
- **Prosthodontic Phase**: Permanent restoration fabrication and delivery at 4 months post-implant placement
- **Follow-up Timeline**: Clinical and 3D model measurements at baseline (pre-operative), 4 months (crown delivery), and 12 months (final follow-up)
- **Primary Outcomes**:
  - Linear 3D spatial resorption measured at multiple levels (2 mm-intervals above implant platform)
  - Volumetric soft-tissue contour change (%) in facial ridge region
  - Buccal bone plate/crest dimensional changes
- **Secondary Outcomes**: Implant survival, radiographic bone remodeling, buccal/lingual contour stability
- **3D Measurement Method**: Voxel-based registration of digital models (DICOM data or STL intraoral-scan/casts); compared preoperative baseline to 4-month and 12-month time points; calculated linear and volumetric deviations by region

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Test (Prov. Crown) | Control (Healing Ab.) | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean linear 3D spatial resorption (mm) | 0.1–0.7 | 0.1–0.7 | No difference |
| Volumetric soft-tissue change at 12 mo (%) | 11.9% | 17.4% | **P = 0.04** (favors test) |
| Mid-facial margin/2–6 mm above crest preservation | Better | Less preservation | P = 0.04 |
| Buccal bone thickness changes | Modest | Modest | No significant difference |
| Implant survival at 12 mo | Similar | Similar | Not reported as significantly different |

**Summary**: While linear/spatial resorption was similar between groups, the volumetric analysis revealed significantly greater facial ridge contour preservation in the test (immediate provisionalization) group at 12 months. This volumetric benefit was concentrated in the mid-facial and supracrestal zone (2–6 mm above crest), the zone most visible clinically and critical to esthetic outcomes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Relatively small sample size (n=40, n=20 per arm) limits statistical power for subgroup analysis
- Single center with limited demographic and geographic diversity
- Provisional crown design specified as non-occluding; results may not generalize to occluding provisional restorations or different crown morphologies
- 3D measurement/registration method (intraoral scans vs casts vs DICOM) not specified in abstract; potential registration accuracy variance
- Follow-up limited to 12 months; longer-term (3–5 year) volumetric stability unknown
- No subgroup analysis by biotype (thick vs thin gingival), implant position (central vs lateral), or gender
- Radiographic assessment of vertical crestal bone resorption not emphasized (though may have been collected as secondary outcome, cf. Chan 2019 publication)

## 7. Glossary

- **Immediate Implant Placement (IIP)**: Placement of an endosseous implant into an extraction socket immediately following tooth removal, without waiting for socket healing/mineralization
- **Provisional Crown / Temporary Restoration**: Non-permanent restoration placed immediately or shortly after implant insertion; provides occlusal function and soft-tissue contour guidance during osseointegration and final prosthetic fabrication
- **Healing Abutment**: Transmucosal component placed on implant body to facilitate soft-tissue contours and protect implant platform during osseointegration, without a crown or restoration on top
- **3D Volumetric Change (%)**: Percentage change in soft-tissue volume in a defined facial ridge region, calculated by voxel-based registration of pre-operative and post-operative 3D models; expresses volume loss or gain as a percentage of baseline volume
- **Linear 3D Spatial Resorption**: Direct measurement of ridge contour shift in linear distance (millimeters) at specific measurement planes, typically 2 mm-intervals apical to the alveolar crest
- **Mid-Facial Margin**: The most facially prominent aspect of the soft-tissue contour at the implant-crown interface, clinically and esthetically critical
- **Voxel-Based Registration**: Automated 3D alignment of two volumetric image datasets (DICOM scans or 3D-scanned models) to calculate point-cloud deviation and volumetric change between time points
