## Why Ingested

Largest comprehensive narrative review (64 studies) on cracked tooth treatment outcomes up to 2022, covering monitoring, restorative, and endodontic approaches with evidence tables. Provides the foundational synthesis that later SR+MA by [[wiki/cracked-tooth/zhang-2024-cracked-teeth-treatment-outcomes-sr-ma]] extends with pooled quantitative data. Complements [[wiki/cracked-tooth/li-2021-cracked-tooth-syndrome-etiology-review]] (etiology/diagnosis focus) with a treatment-outcomes lens.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of 64 studies (35 observational, 17 case series, 6 in vitro, 4 interventional, 2 SR) from multi-database search up to February 2022 on permanent cracked tooth treatment outcomes.
Cracked teeth with NP/RP show 80–93% pulp survival with direct/indirect composite restorations; monitoring is viable for asymptomatic teeth (80% stable at 3 years); endodontic outcomes comparable to non-cracked teeth (84–88% SR-level survival) when paired with cuspal-coverage restorations.
No consensus on crack line management or timing; treatment should be individualized with full-crown protection strongly favored post-RCT and direct restorations acceptable for vital cracked teeth with cuspal coverage.

## 세줄요약

2022년 2월까지 발표된 64편(관찰연구 35, 증례 17, 실험 6, 중재 4, 체계적 문헌고찰 2)을 포함한 균열치 (Cracked Tooth) 치료 결과에 관한 포괄적 서술 문헌고찰.
정상 치수 (Normal Pulp, NP) 또는 가역적 치수염 (Reversible Pulpitis, RP) 단계의 균열치는 직·간접 복합레진 수복 시 치수 생존율 80–93%; 무증상 균열치는 3년간 80% 안정적 경과(모니터링 가능); 근관치료 (Root Canal Treatment, RCT) 후 완전 크라운 장착 시 체계적 문헌고찰 수준 생존율 84–88%(비균열치와 동등).
균열선 처리 및 치료 시점에 관한 합의 없음; 치료는 증례별 개별화, RCT 후 반드시 크라운 보호 강력 권고, 활성치수 균열치는 교두 피개 직접수복 허용.

## Key Contributions

- First comprehensive narrative review synthesizing monitoring, restorative, and endodontic data in one framework
- Distinguishes single-stage vs. multiple-stage restorative approaches with outcome tables
- Confirms monitoring viability: 80% of asymptomatic teeth remain stable at 3 years; symptom decrease (23%) is twice as common as increase (10%) at 1 year
- Documents Iowa Index (Krell & Caplan) as prognostic tool: periradicular diagnosis + distal marginal crack + probing ≥5 mm
- Establishes that full-crown restoration after RCT strongly protects survival (97% vs 68% without crown, Nguyen Thi & Jansson)
- Notes endodontic outcomes for cracked teeth are comparable to non-cracked root-filled teeth when appropriately restored

## Methodology

- Design: Comprehensive narrative review
- Databases: MEDLINE (Ovid), Embase (Ovid), Scopus, Web of Science + grey literature; supplemented by hand-searching
- Search period: up to February 22, 2022 (additional searches Sep 2021, Feb 2022)
- Inclusion: Clinical follow-up studies, laboratory studies, SR/MAs on permanent cracked teeth in English
- Selection: Two reviewers independently (title/abstract → full-text), disagreements by consensus
- PRISMA flow: 1318 initial → 679 screened → 85 full-texts → 64 included
- Article types: 35 observational, 17 case series/reports, 6 in vitro, 4 interventional, 2 systematic reviews

## Key Results

### Monitoring vs. Restoring
- Ferracane et al. (n=2858): 64% monitored; 80% remain on monitoring at 3 years; tooth survival >98%
- Hilton et al. (n=1850): symptom decrease 23%, increase 10% over 1 year without treatment
- Monitoring favorable when: barely visible/incipient cracks, no compromised structure, no symptoms

### Vital Cracked Teeth (NP/RP) — Pulp Survival
| Study | Restoration | Pulp survival |
|---|---|---|
| Opdam et al. (n=41, 7yr) | Direct composite ± cuspal coverage | 93% |
| Abbott & Leow (n=100, 5yr) | Glass ionomer → Crown/onlay | 80% |
| Banerji et al. (n=151, 3mo interim) | Composite splint → definitive | 93% |
| Signore et al. (n=43, 6yr) | Indirect composite onlays | 93% |
| Wu et al. (n=199, 3yr) | Orthodontic band → Crown | 71% (overall), 81% (with crown) |
- No significant difference between direct composite with/without cuspal coverage (Opdam, 7yr), but higher failure rate without cuspal coverage

### Endodontically Treated Cracked Teeth (CT-RCT) — Survival
| Study | n | Follow-up | Survival/Success |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leong SR | — | 5yr (estimated) | 84% survival |
| Olivieri SR | — | 1yr (estimated) | 88% survival, 82% success |
| Nguyen Thi & Jansson (n=200, 4.5yr) | 200 | 5yr | 68% overall; 97% with full crown |
| Davis & Shariff (n=65, 2.8yr) | 65 | 2–4yr | 96.6% survival, 90.6% success |
| Malentacca et al. (n=87, 5.6yr) | 87 | — | 68% survival, 53% success |

### Prognostic Factors (Negative)
- Deep periodontal pockets (≥5 mm): Iowa Index component (Krell & Caplan)
- Periapical lesion: 27.5% lower success (Chen et al.); 11% lower 1-year success (Krell & Caplan)
- Distal marginal crack: Iowa Index component
- Terminal abutment: 8% higher extraction risk (Leong SR, not significant); 96% higher risk (Ng et al., mixed)
- Crack without crown after RCT: 11-fold higher extraction risk (Nguyen Thi & Jansson)

### Crack Line Management
- Complete removal: reduces bacterial biofilm, allows pulp vitality testing, risks pulp exposure (Abbott & Leow: 2/100 pulp exposed)
- Partial removal: preferred to avoid iatrogenic damage; trace until fine craze, no probe separation, or flowable composite liner
- Leave in situ + crown splinting: 21% required RCT within 6 months (Krell & Rivera) — may not be sufficient alone
- FEA: crack removal + composite core + crown gives significantly superior fracture resistance vs crown alone (Kim et al.)

## Limitations

- Heterogeneous study designs, sample sizes, and outcome definitions — precludes meta-analysis
- Majority are observational (high potential bias); few RCTs
- Short follow-up in most studies (< 3 years)
- No standardized protocol for crack line management across studies
- Iowa Index uses outdated diagnostic terminology (degrades clinical utility)
- Gap in evidence: intra-operative prognostic variables (irrigation, apical patency, inter-appointment flare-up) not explored

## Glossary

- **NP/RP** (Normal Pulp / Reversible Pulpitis): pulpal diagnoses where vital pulp preservation is the treatment goal
- **CT-VDP** (Cracked Tooth–Vital Dental Pulp): cracked tooth in which pulp remains vital; managed with restorative ± monitoring
- **CT-RCT** (Cracked Tooth–Root Canal Treatment): cracked tooth requiring endodontic intervention
- **Iowa Index**: prognostic index by Krell & Caplan combining periradicular diagnosis + distal marginal crack + probing ≥5 mm
- **Cuspal coverage**: restoration design (onlay, crown) that splints all cusps to prevent crack propagation under occlusal load
- **DCS** (Direct Composite Splint): supra-coronal direct composite applied as interim treatment (Banerji et al.) to brace cracked segments before definitive restoration
