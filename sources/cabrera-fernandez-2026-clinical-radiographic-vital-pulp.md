## Why Ingested
기존 [[endodontics/vpt/mohamed-2026-theracal-lc-pulp-capping-sr-ma]]와 [[endodontics/vpt/elmsmari-2026-decision-ready-evidence-vital-pulp]]는 TheraCal 재료를 VPT 맥락에서 다루지만, resin-modified vs non-resin-modified CSM 클래스 전체를 비교하는 SR+MA는 없었음 — 특히 상아교 형성(dentin bridge formation)에서의 차이가 핵심 임상 gap.

## One-line Summary
SR+MA of 9 RCTs: RM-CSMs (TheraCal LC) show comparable short-term clinical success to NRM-CSMs but significantly lower dentin bridge formation at 360 days (RR 0.85, 95% CI 0.76–0.95).

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (9 RCTs): 레진함유 칼슘실리케이트(RM-CSM, TheraCal LC)는 단기 임상 성공률은 NRM-CSM과 동등하나 360일 상아교 형성이 유의하게 열등(RR 0.85, CI 0.76–0.95) — 조작성 우선 시 RM-CSM 허용, 생물활성 극대화 시 NRM-CSM.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA directly comparing RM-CSM (TheraCal LC) vs NRM-CSM across full VPT spectrum (IPC, DPC, pulpotomy) with 360-day dentin bridge formation outcome
- Comparable clinical-radiographic success at 90d, 180d, and 360d — no statistically significant difference
- Significantly lower dentin bridge formation with TheraCal LC at 360 days (p=0.006)
- Mechanistic explanation: unpolymerized resin monomer leaching (PEGDMA, Bis-GMA) → cellular apoptosis, impaired odontoblast differentiation
- TheraCal PT data insufficient for quantitative synthesis (only 1 RCT)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis; PRISMA 2020; random-effects (RevMan 5.4, Mantel-Haenszel)
- **Databases**: Medline, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science (inception to October 2025)
- **Population**: Permanent teeth, deep caries, vital pulp diagnosis (reversible ± symptomatic irreversible pulpitis)
- **Intervention**: RM-CSM (TheraCal LC, TheraCal PT) for IPC, DPC, or pulpotomy
- **Comparator**: NRM-CSMs (Biodentine, ProRoot MTA, NeoMTA, iRoot BP Plus, CEM)
- **Outcomes**: Primary — combined clinical + radiographic success at 3–36mo; Secondary — dentin bridge formation
- **Studies included**: 9 RCTs (TheraCal LC: 8; TheraCal PT: 1 excluded from meta-analysis)
- **Quality**: RoB 2 (most = some concerns; 2 studies = high risk); GRADE = low certainty

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Timepoint | RR (RM vs NRM) | 95% CI | I² |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90 days (overall success) | 1.01 | 0.94–1.07 | 15% |
| 180 days (overall success) | 0.97 | 0.90–1.04 | 0% |
| 360 days (overall success) | 0.89 | 0.69–1.13 | 69% |
| 180 days (dentin bridge) | 0.99 | 0.90–1.08 | 0% |
| **360 days (dentin bridge)** | **0.85** | **0.76–0.95** | **0%** |

- DPC subgroup (360d): RR 0.90 (0.76–1.08) — trend favoring NRM, not significant
- TheraCal LC vs Biodentine (360d): RR 0.86 (0.71–1.03) — consistent direction

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Conclusions apply only to TheraCal LC — insufficient evidence for TheraCal PT, BioCal-CAP, Oxford ActiveCal CP
- Short follow-ups; most studies <2 years
- Operator blinding impossible (inherent limitation of material-comparison trials → all studies "some concerns")
- Only permanent teeth included — cannot extrapolate to primary dentition
- Future: standardized RCTs ≥3–5 years comparing all RM-CSM formulations including Bis-GMA-free materials

## 6. Related Work
- Mohamed 2026 (same journal topic): TheraCal LC comparable to MTA/Biodentine for success rates in IPC/DPC
- Elmsmari 2026 (NMA): hydraulic CSMs beat Ca(OH)₂ regardless of resin modification
- Peskersoy et al. (2021): narrative synthesis excluded from meta-analysis — TheraCal LC showed progressive decline vs Biodentine/MTA Plus over time (consistent with dentin bridge finding)

## 7. Glossary
- **RM-CSM**: Resin-modified calcium silicate-based material (e.g., TheraCal LC, TheraCal PT)
- **NRM-CSM**: Non-resin-modified calcium silicate-based material (e.g., Biodentine, ProRoot MTA)
- **PEGDMA / Bis-GMA**: Unpolymerized resin monomers that leach from RM-CSMs → cytotoxicity via ROS, mitochondrial disruption
- **Dentin bridge formation**: Reparative tertiary dentin deposited at exposure site; proxy for pulp healing quality
