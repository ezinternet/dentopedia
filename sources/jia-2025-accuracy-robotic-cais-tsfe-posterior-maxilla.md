## Why Ingested

Posterior maxilla implant placement direction is constrained above by the Schneiderian membrane — any angular error propagates directly to membrane perforation risk. This paper is the only PMC-accessible case series quantifying angular deviation (1.25°) and membrane perforation rate (2.5%) for posterior maxilla TSFE implants (80% delayed), filling the maxillary posterior placement direction gap in the wiki. Complements [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/decker-2024-clinical-radiographic-transcrestal-sinus-augmentation]] on TSFE outcomes with the robotic accuracy dimension.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case series (n=40 patients/40 implants, posterior maxilla, 80% delayed placement, RBH 4–9 mm) evaluating robotic computer-assisted implant surgery (r-CAIS, Remebot) combined with manual transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE) using Summers osteotomes at a single Chinese university center.

Angular deviation was 1.25° ± 0.61°, global coronal/apical deviations 0.50/0.54 mm; Schneiderian membrane perforation rate was 2.5% (1/40), substantially lower than the 7.2% reported for osteotome-only TSFE; only timing (early vs delayed) significantly affected positional deviations in LMM.

r-CAIS with a 1-mm sinus floor safety margin maintained high directional accuracy in the posterior maxilla; angular deviation under 2° with no other adverse events supports robotic guidance as a safe approach for the anatomically constrained posterior maxillary implant site.

## 세줄요약

후향적 케이스시리즈(n=40, 후방 상악, 80% 지연식립, 잔존 치조골 높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 4–9 mm): 로봇 컴퓨터지원 임플란트 수술 (Robotic Computer-Assisted Implant Surgery, r-CAIS, Remebot)과 Summers 오스테오톰 방식의 치조정접근 상악동 거상 (Transcrestal Sinus Floor Elevation, TSFE)을 병용.

각도 편차 1.25°±0.61°, 전반적 관상/첨단 편차 0.50/0.54 mm; 슈나이데리안 막 천공률 2.5%(1/40) — 오스테오톰 단독 TSFE 7.2% 대비 현저히 낮음; 선형혼합모델(LMM)에서 식립 타이밍(조기 vs 지연)만 편차에 유의한 영향.

상악동 저부 1-mm 안전 마진 + r-CAIS 조합은 해부학적으로 제한된 후방 상악에서 각도 정확도(≤2°)와 막 안전성을 동시에 달성함.

## Key Contributions

- First r-CAIS clinical series (n=40) specifically for TSFE + delayed posterior maxillary implants
- Angular deviation 1.25° — the most precise reported for r-CAIS + TSFE; compares favorably to d-CAIS (Wu et al.)
- Sinus membrane perforation 2.5% vs 7.2% (Stacchi meta-analysis, osteotome only) — robotic precision as safety mechanism
- 1-mm sinus floor safety margin during robotic osteotomy = operationalized safe direction protocol
- LMM: timing (early vs delayed) the only significant deviation predictor; bone quality, implant size, registration method: NS

## Methodology

- **Design**: retrospective case series, single center, STROBE guidelines; Ethics KY-QT-20250004 (Xi'an Jiaotong Univ)
- **Robot**: Remebot (trueness 156 µm, precision 33 µm); RemebotDent surgical planning software
- **CBCT**: Orthophos XG, 110 kV/5 mA, 0.3 mm voxel; preoperative + immediate postoperative
- **Workflow**: prosthetic-driven planning → 1 mm safety margin below sinus floor → robotic osteotomy → manual TSFE (Summers osteotomes) → robotic implant placement
- **Registration**: CBCT (n=19) or IOS/STL overlay (n=21); marker fixed to adjacent teeth
- **Accuracy**: planned vs placed superimposition (RemebotDent v1.0); global + lateral + vertical deviations at coronal + apical levels, angular deviation; ICC intra-examiner 0.96
- **Statistics**: Shapiro-Wilk, descriptive statistics; LMM with 7 fixed factors; SPSS 27.0 + GraphPad Prism 9.5.1

## Key Results

**Accuracy:**

| Metric | Mean ± SD | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| Global coronal deviation | 0.50 ± 0.22 mm | 0.43–0.57 |
| Global apical deviation | 0.54 ± 0.21 mm | 0.47–0.60 |
| **Angular deviation** | **1.25° ± 0.61°** | 1.06°–1.45° |
| Lateral coronal | 0.33 ± 0.14 mm | 0.28–0.37 |
| Lateral apical | 0.37 ± 0.14 mm | 0.33–0.42 |
| Vertical coronal | 0.18 ± 0.38 mm | 0.06–0.30 |
| Vertical apical | 0.17 ± 0.38 mm | 0.05–0.30 |

**Complications:**
- Sinus membrane perforation: 2.5% (1/40) — Case XIX (#17, Type III, delayed, RBH 7 mm, CBCT registration)
- No postoperative infection, wound dehiscence, early implant failure (6-month follow-up)

**LMM subgroup analysis (7 factors):**
- Timing (early vs delayed): significant (p<0.05) on coronal and apical deviations
- Implant position, arch side, bone quality, implant type, size, registration method: NS (p>0.05)

**Patient mix:** 32 delayed (80%), 8 early; 30 molars (#16/17/26/27), 10 premolars (#14/15/24/25)

## Limitations

- Single-center case series, no control group; direct comparison (r-CAIS vs freehand/d-CAIS/s-CAIS) not possible
- n=40 limits statistical power; LMM timing result may be sample-size artifact
- Bone quality assessed by HU on CBCT (Misch classification) — inaccuracy acknowledged
- Follow-up 6 months only; no long-term implant survival or peri-implant outcome data
- Robotic arm restricted to osteotomy and placement only; TSFE performed freehand
- r-CAIS adds extra time, cost, steep learning curve; restricted mouth opening is a contraindication
