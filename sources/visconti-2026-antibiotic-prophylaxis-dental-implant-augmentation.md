Cochrane Protocol (CD016385): 임플란트 + 동시 골증대술 환경에서의 항생제 예방투여 근거 종합 예정. 기존 [[implants/peri-implantitis/isola-2025-impact-and-efficacy-of-systemic]] 임플란트주위염 항생제 SR과 연계해 임플란트 감염 예방-치료 스펙트럼 커버리지 확장. 완성된 리뷰가 출판되면 이 프로토콜 페이지를 갱신한다.

## One-line Summary
Cochrane Protocol (2026): to evaluate systemic prophylactic antibiotics vs no antibiotics for implant placement with concomitant bone augmentation procedures — no results yet.

## 한줄요약
코크란 프로토콜 (2026): 골증대술 동반 임플란트 식립 시 전신 예방적 항생제 (Prophylactic Antibiotic)의 편익·위해 평가 예정 — 결과 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First Cochrane protocol specifically addressing antibiotic prophylaxis for the bone-augmented implant subgroup
- Distinguishes from existing review on non-augmented implant placement (gap: augmented cases higher risk)
- Establishes standardized comparison framework: pre-op single dose vs extended post-op regimens vs no antibiotics
- Includes GBR procedures (resorbable/non-resorbable membrane) and sinus lift as augmentation types
- Statistical unit: participant (not implant), minimizing unit-of-analysis errors

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Cochrane SR of parallel-group RCTs, minimum 3-month follow-up, ≥10/arm
- Databases: CENTRAL, MEDLINE (from 1946), Embase (from 1980) + WHO ICTRP + ClinicalTrials.gov
- No language/publication year restrictions
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 1 tool (7 domains: sequence generation, allocation concealment, blinding × 2, incomplete data, selective reporting, other)
- Synthesis: Mantel-Haenszel random-effects, RR + 95% CI
- Certainty: GRADE (summary of findings tables for 4 comparisons)
- Subgroup analyses for implant failures: augmentation type, horizontal vs vertical GBR, resorbable vs non-resorbable membrane

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Protocol — no results yet. Outcomes to be assessed:
- Critical (at longest follow-up ≤1yr): implant failure, prosthetic failure
- Important (within 3 months): postoperative infection, adverse events (GI, allergic)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Protocol stage — results dependent on eligible RCT availability
- One existing RCT found no benefit from systemic antibiotics in healthy patients with simultaneous GBR
- Conflicting findings in prior literature — unclear whether current RCT evidence is sufficient for meta-analysis

## 7. Glossary
- 항생제 예방투여 (Prophylactic Antibiotic): 감염 발생 전 투여하는 예방 목적 항생제
- 골유도재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR): 차폐막 ± 골이식재를 이용한 골결손 재생 술식
- 흡수성 차폐막 vs 비흡수성 차폐막 (Resorbable vs Non-resorbable Membrane): 생분해 여부에 따른 GBR 차폐막 분류
