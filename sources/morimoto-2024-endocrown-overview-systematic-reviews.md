## Why Ingested

An overview of systematic reviews on 엔도크라운 (endocrown, one-piece endodontic crown), synthesizing survival/success data across 9 SRs. Establishes 5-year benchmark evidence for ceramic and indirect resin endocrowns, identifies evidence gaps for zirconia/metal options, and frames limitations of the current SR landscape.

→ [[inlay/morimoto-2024-endocrown-overview-systematic-reviews]]

## Three-line Summary

Overview of 9 systematic reviews on one-piece endodontic crowns (endocrowns) in posterior teeth, assessing survival, success, and clinical outcomes through June 2024.
Ceramic and indirect resin endocrowns show high 5-year survival/success rates comparable to conventional crowns with posts; loss of retention is the primary failure mode with similar rates for molars and premolars.
Firm conclusions are precluded by low methodological quality in most SRs, very high study overlap, and absence of evidence for zirconia/metal materials and patient-reported outcomes.

## 세줄요약

후구치 비활력 치아 수복용 단일 구조 엔도크라운 (endocrown) 관련 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR) 9편을 종합한 개요 연구.
세라믹 (ceramic) 및 간접 레진 (indirect resin) 엔도크라운은 5년 생존율·성공률이 높고 기존 포스트 보조 완전 크라운과 동등하며, 주요 실패 원인은 유지력 상실 (retention loss)로 대구치·소구치 간 차이 없음.
대부분 SR의 방법론적 질이 낮고 연구 중복이 매우 높아 확정적 결론 도출 불가; 지르코니아 (zirconia)·금속 (metal) 재료 및 환자 보고 결과 (Patient-Reported Outcome Measures, PROMs) 에 대한 SR은 없음.

## Key Contributions

1. First overview-level synthesis of SR evidence on one-piece endodontic crowns (엔도크라운)
2. Demonstrates 5-year high survival/success for ceramic and indirect resin types (comparable to conventional crowns + posts)
3. Identifies loss of retention as the dominant failure mode, with no significant difference between molars and premolars
4. Highlights absence of any SR addressing zirconia/metal endocrowns or PROMs
5. Concludes that more primary clinical trials—not more SRs—are needed given very high study overlap and low quality of existing SRs

## Methodology

- Systematic search across four electronic databases + manual search through June 2024
- Inclusion: SRs addressing clinical studies of 1-piece endodontic crowns (엔도크라운) or endocrowns in posterior teeth
- Exclusion criteria: non-SR designs, non-clinical studies
- Screening: 468 articles identified → 9 SRs included
- Risk of bias assessed with ROBIS tool; methodological quality with AMSTAR-2
- Overlap of included primary studies quantified

## Key Results

| Outcome | Finding |
|---|---|
| 5-year survival/success (ceramic + indirect resin) | High; comparable to conventional crowns with posts |
| Main failure mode | Loss of retention |
| Molar vs. premolar failure rates | Similar |
| Zirconia endocrowns | Evidence limited; no SR found |
| Metal endocrowns | Evidence limited; no SR found |
| PROMs | No SR found addressing patient-reported outcomes |
| AMSTAR-2 quality | Low or critically low in most SRs |
| ROBIS risk of bias | Low for 2 of the 3 best-evidence SRs |
| Study overlap | Very high |
| SRs included (from 468 screened) | 9 |

## Limitations

- Low or critically low methodological quality in the majority of included SRs (AMSTAR-2)
- Very high overlap of primary studies across SRs (redundant evidence base)
- Heterogeneity in preparation design, operative steps, materials, and follow-up duration
- No evidence from SRs on zirconia, metal endocrowns, or PROMs
- Cannot provide firm conclusions due to lack of high-quality primary clinical trials
- Authors explicitly call for primary RCTs rather than further SRs

## Glossary

- **엔도크라운 (endocrown, one-piece endodontic crown)**: Single indirect restoration combining crown and post-and-core function; retains within pulp chamber; minimally invasive alternative for endodontically treated posterior teeth
- **개요 연구 (overview of systematic reviews)**: Second-order synthesis—meta-research that systematically collects and appraises existing SRs rather than primary studies
- **ROBIS**: Risk Of Bias In Systematic reviews assessment tool
- **AMSTAR-2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews—version 2; rates methodological quality
- **환자 보고 결과 (Patient-Reported Outcome Measures, PROMs)**: Outcomes assessed directly by patients (pain, satisfaction, function) rather than clinicians
- **유지력 상실 (retention loss)**: Debonding or loss of cementation without fracture of the restoration or tooth
