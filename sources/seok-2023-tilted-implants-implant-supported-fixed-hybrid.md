## Why Ingested

The wiki's tilted-vs-axial evidence is dominated by pooled SR/MA data ([[implants/full-arch/del-fabbro-2022-full-arch-tilted-axial-implants-sr-ma]]: axial MBL significantly lower than tilted, P<.0001) plus one 3.5-year All-on-Four cohort reporting the same direction; this paper reports the opposite direction with individual-implant granularity — MBL *decreased* monotonically as vertical angulation rose (0.76 mm at >20° vs 2.17 mm at <10°) over a mean 10.5 years of loading in fixed hybrid prostheses. It is worth holding as the strongest available counter-example, and as the only page in the wiki that analyses bone-graft status (simultaneous vs delayed) inside fixed hybrid prostheses.

## Three-line Summary

Single-centre retrospective cohort (21 fully edentulous patients, 137 implants placed 2003–2010 at Seoul National University Bundang Hospital, mean 10 years 6 months after loading, ≥7-year minimum follow-up) of fixed hybrid prostheses, analysed by implant vertical angulation, bone-graft status and Osstell ISQ.
Marginal bone resorption fell as angulation increased (initial 1.21 → 0.87 → 0.33 mm and final 2.17 → 1.27 → 0.76 mm for <10°, 10–20°, >20°; P=0.005 and P=0.003), overall survival was 94.9% but success only 60.6%, and grafted sites lost more bone (2.36 vs 1.39 mm, P=0.003) with lower success (47.5% vs 71.0%, P=0.005).
Intentional tilting did not harm — and in this cohort appeared to help — the supporting implants, but the >20° subgroup is only 19 implants with uncorrected multiple comparisons and non-standardised radiography, so the finding is hypothesis-generating against the pooled SR+MA consensus rather than a refutation of it.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 후향적 코호트 (완전 무치악 21명, 임플란트 137개, 2003–2010년 식립, 부하 후 평균 10년 6개월, 최소 7년 추적) — 고정 하이브리드 보철 (Fixed Hybrid Prosthesis)을 임플란트 수직 경사도·골이식 여부·오스텔 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)로 분석.

경사도가 커질수록 변연골흡수 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)가 감소 (초기 1.21 → 0.87 → 0.33 mm, 최종 2.17 → 1.27 → 0.76 mm; 각 10° 미만·10–20°·20° 초과; P=0.005·P=0.003), 전체 생존율 94.9%이나 성공률은 60.6%에 그쳤고, 골이식 부위는 더 많은 골소실 (2.36 vs 1.39 mm, P=0.003)과 낮은 성공률 (47.5% vs 71.0%, P=0.005)을 보임.

의도적 경사식립은 지지 임플란트에 해롭지 않았고 이 코호트에서는 오히려 유리했으나, 20° 초과군이 19개에 불과하고 다중비교 보정이 없으며 방사선 계측이 표준화되지 않아 — 통합 SR+MA 결론에 반하는 가설생성적 근거로 다루어야 함.

## Key Contributions

- Provides individual-implant-level, 10.5-year data on fixed hybrid prostheses (few anterior implants + posterior cantilevers) — a prosthesis type that long-term implant literature usually omits in favour of All-on-4.
- Reports a **monotonic inverse relation between implant vertical angulation and marginal bone resorption** — highest-angle implants (>20°) lost roughly one-third the bone of near-axial implants (<10°) — the opposite direction to Del Fabbro 2022 SR+MA and to Szabó 2022 All-on-Four cohort data.
- Separates **positioning strategy from grafting**: implants placed without bone graft had ~1 mm less final bone resorption (1.39 vs 2.36 mm, P=0.003) and a 23.5-point higher success rate (71.0% vs 47.5%, P=0.005), arguing that tilting to find native bone beats grafting to allow axial placement in this indication.
- Shows that **secondary (not primary) ISQ** carries the prognostic signal: secondary ISQ <60 was associated with 100% complication occurrence (P=0.01) and 3.48 mm final MBL (P=0.04), whereas primary ISQ was unrelated to any outcome.
- Documents how low "success" can fall under strict ICOI/Pisa criteria (60.6%) when "survival" is 94.9% — a useful reminder that success-rate comparisons across studies are criterion-dependent.

## Methodology

**Design**: single-centre retrospective review of patients treated 2003–2010, followed ≥7 years after first delivery of the fixed hybrid prosthesis.

**Inclusion**: implant surgery for full-mouth fixed hybrid prosthesis at Seoul National University Bundang Hospital + ≥7 years follow-up after prosthesis delivery.

**Exclusion**: lost follow-up before prosthesis delivery; implants failing before first delivery (early osseointegration failure); implants placed but not incorporated into the final prosthesis design.

**Sample**: n=21 patients, **137 implants**. Systems used (not compared): Ti-Unite (Nobel Biocare), Osstem US & TS II, AVANA II (Osstem Implant), CMI (Neobiotech). Diameters 3.3 mm (n=8), 3.75 mm (n=5), 4 mm (n=99), 5 mm (n=25); lengths 10 mm (n=9), 11.5 mm (n=62), 13 mm (n=59), 15 mm (n=7).

**Marginal bone resorption**: intraoral peri-apical radiographs (Heliodent Sirona; Sirona Dental Systems) analysed with a PACS program (INFINIT Co.). Distance from implant shoulder to the most coronal bone-to-implant contact measured mesially and distally; mean of the two = implant MBL. Baseline = radiograph immediately after surgery; **initial MBL** = ~1-year post-surgery radiograph vs baseline; **final MBL** = last follow-up radiograph vs baseline.

**Angulation grouping** (radiographic, angle between alveolar ridge line and fixture long-axis bisector): Group 1 ≤10° (n=88, 64.2%); Group 2 >10° to <20° (n=30, 21.9%); Group 3 ≥20° (n=19, 13.9%).

**Bone graft**: with graft (n=57) vs without (n=80); within grafted, simultaneous placement (n=56) vs delayed placement after graft healing (n=5). Materials used but not compared: auto tooth bone graft, Bio-Oss (Geistlich), Biocera-F (BIOTEM), Orthoblast II (Isotis Orthobiologics); membranes Bio-Gide (Geistlich), OSSIX plus (Orapharma), Bio-ARM (ACE Surgical Supply).

**Stability**: Osstell ISQ kit (resonance frequency analysis) in 101 implants; primary stability immediately after installation, secondary at second-stage surgery/healing-abutment connection; split at ≥60 vs <60.

**Outcome definitions**: survival (ICOI "Health Scale for Dental Implants", 2007 — satisfactory + compromised survival) and success; biological and prosthetic complications tabulated (peri-implantitis, infection, nerve injury, wound dehiscence, thread exposure; screw loosening/fracture, fixture fracture, food impaction, crown/abutment fracture, occlusal change, pontic failure, denture fracture).

**Statistics**: IBM SPSS v25.0. One-way ANOVA for MBL vs angulation/graft/stability; chi-square for complications, survival and success; Mann–Whitney for the delayed-vs-simultaneous and stability comparisons because group sizes were very unequal. No multiplicity correction, no multivariable model, no a-priori sample-size calculation reported.

## Key Results

**Overall** (n=137 implants): initial MBL 1.01 ± 1.13 mm; final MBL 1.80 ± 1.99 mm; complications 52.6%; survival 94.9%; success 60.6%. Mean follow-up 10 years 6 months after loading.

| Group | n | Initial MBL (mm) | Final MBL (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group 1, <10° | 88 | 1.21 ± 1.18 | 2.17 ± 2.25 |
| Group 2, 10–20° | 30 | 0.87 ± 1.06 | 1.27 ± 1.25 |
| Group 3, >20° | 19 | 0.33 ± 0.54 | 0.76 ± 0.80 |

### Dimension subgroups (descriptive, non-significant interaction)

## Limitations

**Stated by the authors**: retrospective design; many different implant systems from several manufacturers (production changes over decades make within-brand pooling unreliable); marginal bone resorption measured on peri-apical radiographs without a repositionable film holder, specified technique or automatic measuring system (citing Benn 1990); the one-piece prosthesis makes it impossible to attribute an upper-structure prosthetic complication to a specific implant, so a computerised stress analysis is suggested for future work.

**Inferred by this review**:

- **Small, unbalanced subgroups.** Group 3 (the headline finding) is 19 implants; the delayed-placement arm is 5 implants; secondary-ISQ <60 is 5 implants. Three of the paper's most quoted claims rest on n≤19 with chi-square or Mann–Whitney tests.
- **No multiplicity correction or multivariable model.** Roughly a dozen subgroup comparisons are run at α=0.05; a P=0.04 (secondary ISQ vs final MBL) in that setting is fragile. Angulation, graft status, ISQ and implant position are mutually confounded and never adjusted for each other.
- **Angulation is not randomised and is confounded by site.** Tilted implants are, by construction, the posterior implants placed to avoid the sinus or nerve — i.e. in native basal bone — whereas near-axial <10° implants include anterior sites and sites where axial placement required grafting. "Tilting reduces MBL" and "native bone beats grafted bone" may be one finding seen twice.
- **Angulation-dependent measurement bias.** Reading the shoulder-to-bone distance on a 2D peri-apical image of a deliberately tilted fixture is angle-sensitive; no correction for beam angulation or magnification calibration per implant is described. This is a plausible mechanism for exaggerating the difference in Group 3.
- **Patient demographics absent.** Age, sex, smoking, diabetes, periodontal history, prosthesis design (cantilever length, number of implants per arch) and arch distribution are not reported, so the cohort cannot be risk-adjusted and the reader cannot judge how the "success" failures distributed across patients (21 patients × clustered implants = non-independence; the analysis treats implants as independent units).
- **Success criterion drives the low 60.6%.** Strict ICOI/Pisa success thresholds, including radiographic bone-loss criteria, mechanically punish the same implants already flagged for high MBL; "success" and "MBL" are therefore not independent outcomes, and the striking success gradient across angulation groups partly restates the MBL gradient.
- **No control/comparison group outside the cohort**, no time-to-event analysis (survival is crude, not Kaplan–Meier), and failure timing is not modelled.
- **Internal numerical inconsistency**: the Abstract and Results text give Group 1 initial MBL as **1.19 mm**, while Table 2/Table 4 give **1.21 ± 1.18 mm**. The Tables are treated as authoritative here.
- **ISQ is not available for 36 of 137 implants** (101 recorded), and it is not stated how those 36 were selected — a potential selection bias in the stability analysis.
- **Generalisability**: single Korean tertiary hospital, 2003–2010 implant generations, one-piece metal-resin hybrid design; conclusions may not transfer to contemporary monolithic zirconia full-arch protocols.

## Related Work

- [[implants/full-arch/del-fabbro-2022-full-arch-tilted-axial-implants-sr-ma]] — the head-on counterpoint: 24 studies, 5,594 tilted vs 5,611 axial implants, ≥3 years, axial MBL significantly **lower** than tilted (P<.0001); this paper finds the reverse with 19 high-angle implants.
- [[implants/full-arch/szabo-2022-all-on-four-tilted-distal-implants-mbl]] — individual-implant All-on-Four cohort reaching the opposite conclusion (tilted > axial MBL at every time point), and the closest design match to this study.
- [[implants/full-arch/lin-2018-clinical-performance-tilted-versus-axial]] — SR of 42 studies finding no survival/MBL difference; this paper agrees on survival (100% in both tilted groups) but not on MBL.
- [[implants/full-arch/mehta-2021-tilted-axial-implants-edentulous-maxilla-sr-ma]] — All-on-4-type maxillary SR+MA reporting equivalence (RR 1.00 survival, MD −0.02 mm MBL); partially superseded by Del Fabbro 2022.
- [[implants/mbl/del-fabbro-2014-marginal-bone-axial-tilted-sr-ma]] — the founding SR+MA that established no MBL difference at 1 year and ≥3 years; the historical baseline this paper's direction-of-effect argument has to be read against.
- [[implants/full-arch/de-araujo-nobre-2025-mandibular-all-on-four-20-25yr]] — the longest All-on-4 follow-up in the wiki (20–25 years, 181 patients) and a reference point for absolute MBL magnitudes (20-year 1.71 mm) against this study's 1.80 mm at 10.5 years.
- [[implants/isq/tisci-2026-isq-it-mbl-survival-sr-ma]] — SR+MA concluding ISQ is not an independent predictor of MBL or survival; this paper claims secondary ISQ is a prognostic indicator, which is the same tension in a different dataset.
- [[implants/full-arch/uesugi-2026-investigation-of-the-long-term]] — long-term all-on-four cohort showing that late failures are driven by anatomical/systemic factors rather than angulation, a useful counterweight to reading angulation as the causal variable here.

## Glossary

- **Fixed hybrid prosthesis (FHP)** — one-piece implant-supported full-arch prosthesis with few anterior implants (≥6 maxilla, ≥4 mandible) and posterior cantilevers; also called hybrid or metal-resin full-arch prosthesis.
- **Vertical angulation** — angle between the alveolar ridge line and the fixture's long-axis bisector, measured on peri-apical radiographs; here grouped ≤10°, 10–20°, ≥20°.
- **Marginal bone resorption (MBR/MBL)** — radiographic loss of bone height from the implant shoulder to the most coronal bone-to-implant contact, averaged over mesial and distal surfaces.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)** — 1–100 resonance-frequency-analysis index of implant stability; Osstell convention: ≥70 high, 60–69 medium, <60 low.
- **Primary vs secondary stability** — primary = mechanical stability at installation; secondary = stability after biological osseointegration, measured at second-stage surgery.
- **ICOI/Pisa "Health Scale for Dental Implants"** — 2007 consensus criteria separating *survival* (satisfactory/compromised survival) from *success* (including radiographic bone-level and symptom requirements), which is why success here (60.6%) is far below survival (94.9%).
