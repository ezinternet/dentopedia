## Three-line Summary

Comprehensive narrative review of complications of open (lateral window) and closed (transcrestal) sinus lifting procedures — classification and management guidance from the University Complutense Madrid group.
Hemorrhage is identified as a specific intraoperative complication of open sinus lifting; membrane perforation (~20–25%) is the most common; additional specific complications include chronic rhinosinusitis, ostium blockage by overfilling, and hemorrhage.
New technologies (piezoelectric, hydraulic sinus lift, reamer burs) are proposed to reduce complications but supporting evidence remains insufficient; thorough preoperative CBCT and clinical evaluation are essential.

- Systematic classification of open and closed sinus lift complications
- Names hemorrhage as a specific (less frequent but significant) complication of open sinus lifting
- Covers both intraoperative and postoperative complications in a single reference

## 4. Key Results (from abstract)

**Open sinus lifting complications:**
- Most common: Schneiderian membrane perforation (~20–25% incidence)
- General oral surgery complications: swelling, hematoma
- Specific (less frequent): hemorrhage, chronic rhinosinusitis, ostium blockage by overfilling — these may compromise graft/implant viability

**Closed sinus lifting complications:**
- Membrane perforation
- Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV)
- Implant displacement into sinus cavity

**New technologies reviewed** (piezoelectric, hydraulic sinus lift, reamer burs): proposed to reduce complications, but evidence for effectiveness/safety still lacking

**Key recommendation**: Detailed medical history + thorough radiographic + clinical examination essential before any sinus augmentation; most appropriate technique for the specific case characteristics + surgeon's experience/skills must be matched.

## 8. Glossary

- **SMP (Schneiderian Membrane Perforation)**: Perforation of the sinus membrane during osteotomy — most common complication (~20–25%).
- **BPPV (Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo, 양성 발작성 두위 현기증)**: Specific complication of transcrestal sinus lift.
- **Ostium blockage**: Complication from overfilling — sinus natural drainage blocked.
- **Piezoelectric device (피에조전기 기구)**: Ultrasonic cutting tool selectively cutting calcified tissue, proposed to reduce membrane perforation and vascular injury.
