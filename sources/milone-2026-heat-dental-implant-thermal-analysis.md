## Why Ingested
임플란트 삽입(positioning) 중 발생하는 열을 적외선 열화상 카메라로 정량화한 in vitro 연구 — [[overviews/osteotomy-drilling-heat-determinants-irrigation-overview]] 및 [[overviews/dense-bone-osteotomy-heat-reimplantation-overview]]에서 드릴링 단계 이후 임플란트 삽입 단계의 열 거동을 보완하는 근거.

## Three-line Summary
In vitro thermal imaging study (n=24 titanium implants, FLIR A40 infrared camera, D3-equivalent resin) isolates the implant placement (screwing) phase — not drilling — as an independent source of heat generation.
Highest temperatures occurred with manual screwing, no saline lubrication, and undersized osteotomy; saline irrigation significantly reduced surface temperatures; water accumulation degraded thermographic measurement accuracy.
Complements drilling-phase heat literature by establishing that placement mechanics independently risk exceeding the 47°C/1-min osteonecrosis threshold, and argues for individualized protocol design (lubrication, micromotor vs manual, preparation size).

## 세줄요약
In vitro 열화상 연구(n=24, FLIR A40, D3 유사 레진): 드릴링이 아닌 임플란트 삽입(나사 조임) 단계를 독립적인 발열원으로 정량화.
수동 삽입 + 생리식염수 없음 + 언더사이즈 준비 조합에서 최고 온도 상승; 생리식염수 윤활이 표면 온도를 유의하게 낮춤; 수분 축적이 열화상 정확도를 저하.
드릴링 단계 연구의 공백을 채우며, 삽입 역학이 독립적으로 47°C/1분 골괴사 임계치를 위협할 수 있음을 확립 — 개인화 프로토콜(윤활, 모터 vs 수동, 준비 크기) 설계 필요성 제시.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Characterizes thermal behavior specifically during the implant placement (screwing) phase — not drilling phase
- Compares manual vs micromotor screwing, with vs without physiological saline lubrication, different preparation sizes
- Shows lubrication (saline) significantly reduces implant surface temperature during placement
- Notes water buildup affected measurement accuracy (thermographic limitation)
- Calls for development of personalized drilling/placement protocols based on patient factors

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: In vitro, controlled experiment
- Implants: n=24 titanium dental implants (Diagram, Schutz-Dental GmbH, Rosbach, Germany)
- Substrate: resin D3 bone-like blocks (mimicking D3 bone mechanical properties)
- Variables: screwing mode (manual vs micromotor), presence/absence of physiological saline, preparation size (normal vs undersized), number of revolutions
- Measurement: FLIR A40 microbolometric infrared camera — surface temperature of implant during screwing
- Protocols: 24 total placements across different protocol combinations

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Highest temperature rises: manual screwing without saline, undersized preparation
- Lubrication (saline): significantly reduced surface temperature
- Undersized preparation increased friction and therefore heat generation
- Micromotor: different thermal profile vs manual — specifics not extracted from available text
- Water buildup: affected thermographic measurement accuracy (limitation)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- In vitro only — resin blocks do not replicate biological heat dissipation, vascular heat exchange, cortical bone behavior
- n=24 — small sample per protocol combination
- Temperature measured on implant surface, not at bone-implant interface
- FLIR accuracy affected by water/saline accumulation
- No biological outcomes measured
- Authors recommend further in vivo studies and personalized protocol development

## 6. Related Work
- Osteotomy drilling heat literature (Timon 2019, Jung 2021, Chauhan 2018): focus on drilling phase; this study complements by addressing placement phase
- Critical temperature threshold: 47°C for 1 min (Eriksson & Albrektsson 1983) — reference standard
- Bernabeu-Mira 2020, Di Fiore 2018: continuous vs intermittent drilling temperature

## 7. Glossary
- **FLIR A40**: microbolometric infrared thermographic camera — detects infrared radiation to measure temperature non-invasively
- **D3 bone equivalent resin**: synthetic substrate replicating D3 bone mechanical properties (thin cortical layer, coarse trabecular bone)
- **Undersized preparation**: osteotomy smaller than implant diameter — increases friction during insertion
- **Microbolometric**: FLIR sensor type — measures infrared radiation without cooling requirement
