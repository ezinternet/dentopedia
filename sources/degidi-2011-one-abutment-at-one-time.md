## Why Ingested

초기 전환점 논문으로, 원-어버트먼트-원-타임(One-Abutment-One-Time, OAOT) 질문에 수평적 골리모델링(horizontal bone remodeling) 결과와 골하(Subcrestal) 테이퍼드(Tapered) 임플란트 즉시 수복 맥락을 추가한다. 이후 [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]]의 풀링된 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 이득을 해석할 때 수직 MBL은 유의차가 없었으나 수평 골소실은 탈착군에서만 유의했다는 점이 이득의 범위를 한정한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical study of 24 patients receiving 48 immediately restored, subcrestal tapered implants (3.5 mm diameter), comparing abutment removal at 24 weeks (12 patients) with the one-abutment-at-one-time protocol (12 patients).
All 48 implants osseointegrated and were clinically stable at 6 months; vertical bone healing showed no significant between-group difference, while a small but significant horizontal bone loss above the implant platform occurred between 6 months and 1 year only in the removal-control group.
In this narrow indication, non-removal of the surgical abutment significantly reduced horizontal bone remodeling; exact bone values and p-values are not reported in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

전향적 대조 임상시험(Prospective Controlled Clinical Study; 환자 24명, 즉시 수복·골하 테이퍼드 임플란트 48개, 직경 3.5 mm) — 24주에 지대주 탈착(abutment disconnection) 군 12명 대 원-어버트먼트-원-타임(One-Abutment-One-Time, OAOT) 군 12명 비교.

임플란트 48개 모두 6개월에 골유착(Osseointegration) 및 임상적 안정성 확보; 수직적 골치유는 군 간 유의차 없음, 임플란트 플랫폼 상방 수평 골소실은 6개월~1년 사이 탈착 대조군에서만 작지만 유의하게 발생.

이 제한적 적응증에서 수술 시 지대주 비탈착이 수평 골리모델링을 유의하게 감소 — 구체적 골소실 수치와 p값은 초록에 미기재.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Early prospective controlled comparison of the OAOT protocol against standard abutment removal at 24 weeks in immediately restored, subcrestally placed tapered implants.
- Separates the vertical bone-healing endpoint (no significant between-group difference) from the horizontal crestal-remodeling endpoint (small but significant loss above the platform in the removal group between the 6-month and 1-year visits).
- Reports a radiographic follow-up schedule extending to 36 months in a defined, narrow clinical indication (partial posterior mandibular edentulism).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective clinical (controlled) study; 24 consecutively treated patients, each receiving two immediately restored 3.5 mm diameter tapered implants in healed sites (48 implants total). Allocation method is not described in the abstract.
- **Groups**: 12 patients underwent the standard prosthetic protocol (abutments removed at 24 weeks, impressions made directly on the implant platform); 12 patients underwent the OAOT protocol (impressions made of the abutments using snap-on abutment copies).
- **Restoration**: Implants immediately splinted with a temporary restoration placed to avoid occlusal contact; final restoration delivered approximately 6 months after implant insertion.
- **Outcomes**: Vertical and horizontal bone changes assessed by periapical radiographs immediately after surgery and at 6, 12, 24, and 36 months.
- **Setting**: Partial posterior mandibular edentulism, healed sites, subcrestal tapered implants.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Osseointegration/stability**: All implants osseointegrated and were clinically stable at the 6-month follow-up.
- **Vertical bone healing**: No statistically significant difference between the two groups (no numeric values or p-values reported in the abstract).
- **Horizontal bone change**: A small but significant horizontal bone loss in the hard tissue portion over the implant platform was evidenced between the 6-month and 1-year follow-up in the control (removal) group (no numeric values or p-values reported in the abstract).
- **Authors' conclusion**: Non-removal of an abutment placed at surgery results in a statistically significant reduction of horizontal bone remodeling around immediately restored, subcrestally placed tapered implants in partial posterior mandibular edentulism.

## 7. Glossary

- **OAOT (One-Abutment-One-Time)**: Protocol in which the definitive/surgical abutment is placed at implant surgery and left in place rather than removed and replaced during prosthetic steps.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: peri-implant crestal bone level change, here assessed in vertical and horizontal dimensions on periapical radiographs.
- **Subcrestal placement**: implant platform positioned below the surrounding crestal bone level.
- **Tapered implant**: implant with a conical (tapered) body shape; 3.5 mm diameter in this study.
- **Snap-on abutment copy**: temporary replica snapped onto the in-place abutment to make an impression without removing the abutment.
- **Horizontal bone remodeling**: change in the hard tissue portion over the implant platform, distinct from vertical bone-level change.
- **Osseointegration**: direct bone-implant contact with clinical stability; achieved in all implants at 6 months in this study.
