## Why Ingested

Held pages describe cracked tooth (CT) vs vertical root fracture (VRF) mainly by clinical features and treat CBCT as a selective, last-resort aid; this paper supplies the CBCT bone-loss pattern (angular vs J-shaped defect) and probing-depth cut-off that separate the two entities. It reinforces the probing-depth (<6 mm for CT) and selective-CBCT statements in [[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2025-position-statement-longitudinal-cracks-fractures]] and adds a diagnostic-imaging angle to [[wiki/cracked-tooth/hernando-2026-diagnostic-methods-dental-cracks-systematic-review]]. ## Three-line Summary

Retrospective records-based comparison of 95 patients (95 teeth) with a visually confirmed longitudinal fracture (cracked tooth only or VRF); 54 teeth had both periapical (PA) radiographs and CBCT plus probing depth and clinical images, read by 2 independent examiners for bone-defect pattern (none, angular, J-shaped, combined).

CBCT had 4.4 times the odds of detecting bony defects suggestive of longitudinal fracture compared with PA radiographs. VRF teeth were associated with indirect restorations, deep probing (>6 mm), absent cortical plate and J-shaped defects (P < .05); cracked teeth with direct restorations, shallow probing (<6 mm), intact cortical plate and angular defects (P < .001); radicular extension of the fracture line correlated with deep probing and J-shaped defects (P < .05).

CBCT bone-loss pattern plus probing depth can likely differentiate cracked teeth from VRF before intervention (angular defect suggests a crack; J-shaped defect, probing >6 mm and cortical-plate loss suggest VRF); limitations are the retrospective design and the abstract-only record (per-group n and effect sizes not available).

## 세줄요약

후향적 기록 기반 비교 연구로, 임상적으로 파절선이 확인된 종축 파절 환자 95명(95치아; 균열치 또는 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)) 중 54치아에서 치근단방사선사진 (Periapical radiograph, PA)과 콘빔 CT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT), 치주낭 깊이를 모두 확보해 2명의 검사자가 골결손 양상(없음·각형·J형·복합형)을 판독.

CBCT는 PA 대비 종축 파절을 시사하는 골결손 검출 odds가 4.4배; VRF는 간접수복·깊은 치주낭(>6 mm)·피질골판 소실·J형 결손과 연관 (P < .05), 균열치는 직접수복·얕은 치주낭(<6 mm)·피질골판 유지·각형 결손과 연관 (P < .001); 파절선의 치근 방향 연장은 깊은 치주낭 및 J형 결손과 상관 (P < .05).

CBCT 골소실 양상과 치주낭 깊이로 중재 전에 균열치와 VRF를 감별할 수 있을 가능성이 있다(각형 결손은 균열 시사, J형 결손·>6 mm·피질골판 소실은 VRF 시사); 한계는 후향적 설계와 초록만 확보된 점(군별 n과 효과크기 미확인).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Identifies CBCT bone-loss patterns (angular vs J-shaped defect) associated with cracked teeth vs VRF.
- Quantifies CBCT's advantage over PA for detecting bony defects of longitudinal fracture (4.4x odds).
- Links clinical predictors (restoration type, probing depth, cortical plate status) to the fracture type.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: retrospective review of patient records; fracture confirmed by clinical visualization of the fracture line.
- **n**: 95 patients/teeth; 54 teeth with complete PA + CBCT + clinical data (probing depth, clinical images).
- **Outcomes**: bone-defect pattern (no defect / angular / J-shaped / combined) on PA and CBCT by 2 independent examiners; presence of buccal plate; probing depth; restoration type; radicular extension of the fracture line.
- **Statistics**: Cohen kappa (inter-examiner; PA vs CBCT), Pearson chi-square, Fisher exact test, Bonferroni-adjusted post hoc testing (P < .05).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- CBCT: 4.4 times the odds of detecting bony defects suggestive of longitudinal fracture vs PA.
- VRF: indirect restorations, probing >6 mm, absent cortical plate, J-shaped defect (P < .05).
- Cracked teeth: direct restorations, probing <6 mm, intact cortical plate, angular defect (P < .001).
- Radicular extension of the fracture line correlated with deep probing and J-shaped defects (P < .05).
- Kappa values, per-group counts and sensitivity/specificity are not reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective record-based design with confirmation by visualization of the fracture line (selection toward teeth where the line was visible).
- Only 54 of 95 teeth had full imaging; per-group numbers unknown from the abstract.
- Abstract-only: methods detail, kappa values and effect sizes not verified against the full text.

## 7. Glossary
- **Angular defect**: angular (vertical) bone defect on CBCT, here associated with a cracked tooth.
- **J-shaped defect**: J-shaped bone-loss pattern along the root on CBCT, here associated with VRF.
- **VRF (vertical root fracture)**: longitudinal fracture extending into the root.
- **CBCT**: cone-beam computed tomography, a 3D radiographic technique.
- **PA radiograph**: periapical radiograph, 2D intraoral film/sensor image.
