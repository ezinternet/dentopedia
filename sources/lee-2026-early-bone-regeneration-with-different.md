## Three-line Summary
Animal experimental study; 16 New Zealand White rabbits, four 6-mm calvarial defects each, randomized to control vs three commercial polydeoxyribonucleotide (PDRN) formulations with bone graft and membrane.
At 2 weeks bone volume-to-tissue volume ratio (BV/TV) was highest with PDRN-A (22.85%±0.88% vs control 18.24%±3.12%); at 6 weeks PDRN-A (25.33%±2.82%) and PDRN-B (23.84%±2.25%) exceeded control (19.33%±1.94%).
Formulations showed broadly similar trends with PDRN-A highest on histomorphometry; small sample, early time points only, and non-human model limit direct clinical extrapolation.

## 2. Key Contributions
- **First head-to-head comparison of three commercially available PDRN formulations under standardized conditions** — directly addresses the repeatedly flagged dose/vehicle/formulation-standardization gap in the PDRN preclinical literature; most prior studies tested a single product.
- Standardized graft geometry (6 mm diameter × 4 mm height blocks) and a single soaking protocol (0.5 mL PDRN solution, 10 min) so that formulation — not graft volume or soaking time — was the variable compared.
- Combined micro-computed tomographic (micro-CT) and histomorphometric endpoints at two early healing points (2 and 6 weeks), with separate analysis of the total augmented area and the biologically challenging central region.
- Found broadly similar trends across formulations, with PDRN-A showing the highest new bone (NB) values — hypothesis-generating evidence that molecular-weight distribution, purification, and raw-material source may translate into different biological performance.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized, controlled animal experiment; four 6-mm circular defects created per rabbit calvarium with a 6-mm trephine bur and allocated to four groups.
- **Animals**: 16 New Zealand White rabbits, 9–20 months old, ~3.0 kg; 8 euthanized at 2 weeks and 8 at 6 weeks; 8 defects per group per time point.
- **Groups**: Control = Osteon Xeno Collagen bone graft material (BG) + Collagen Membrane 2 (Genoss); PDRN-A = same graft/membrane + PDRN from Zerone Cellvane; PDRN-B = + PDRN from PharmaResearch; PDRN-C = + PDRN from BR Pharm. Graft blocks immersed in 0.5 mL of the corresponding PDRN solution for 10 minutes before placement.
- **Primary outcome**: BV/TV (%) measured by micro-CT (SkyScan 1173, CTAn). Grayscale threshold 55–225 (8-bit, 0–255) could not distinguish NB from residual BG, so BV/TV reflects total mineralized tissue rather than NB alone.
- **Secondary outcomes**: Histomorphometry (Masson trichrome–stained 6–8 µm sections, ImageJ) of NB, BG, BG+NB, fibrovascular connective tissue (CT), and void space (VS) in two regions of interest — total augmented area and a 1.6-mm-wide central region — reported both as area (mm²) and fraction (%).
- **Statistics**: G*Power sample size (1-way ANOVA, Cohen's f=0.75, α=0.05, 90% power → 8 defects/group). Shapiro–Wilk normality (all P>0.05); between-group comparisons by 1-way ANOVA followed by Tukey multiple comparisons; within-group 2-vs-6-week comparisons by independent t-test; significance at P<0.05.

| Control | 18.24±3.12 (a) | 19.33±1.94 (a) | 0.4177 (ns) |
| PDRN-A | 22.85±0.88 (b) | 25.33±2.82 (b) | 0.0439 |
| PDRN-B | 20.55±2.14 (a,b) | 23.84±2.25 (b) | 0.0385 |
| PDRN-C | 20.20±1.60 (a,b) | 22.51±2.88 (a,b) | 0.0733 (ns) |
| Between-group ANOVA P | 0.0019 | 0.0004 | — |

- At 2 weeks PDRN-A significantly exceeded control; PDRN-B and PDRN-C were intermediate and not significantly different from either control or PDRN-A.
- At 6 weeks both PDRN-A and PDRN-B significantly exceeded control; PDRN-C was intermediate and not significantly different from any group.
- BV/TV increased significantly over time only in PDRN-A and PDRN-B.

**Histomorphometry — total augmented area (NB%, mean±SD):**
- 2 weeks: PDRN-A 10.87±3.03 vs control 6.50±2.11 (significant); PDRN-B 9.59±3.74 and PDRN-C 9.60±1.58 intermediate (ANOVA P=0.0240).
- 6 weeks: PDRN-A 12.38±1.73 vs control 7.18±3.34 (significant); PDRN-B 10.16±1.87 and PDRN-C 10.27±2.93 intermediate (ANOVA P=0.0041).
- 2 weeks BG+NB%: PDRN-A 29.26±4.11 vs control 24.88±2.48 (significant; ANOVA P=0.0272).
- 6 weeks CT%: significantly lower in PDRN-A (52.56±4.13) than control (61.79±8.93; ANOVA P=0.0389).
- NB area increased significantly over time only in PDRN-A (1.42±0.35 → 1.81±0.27 mm², P=0.0298).

**Histomorphometry — central region (NB%, mean±SD):**
- 2 weeks: control 0.25±0.20, PDRN-A 0.64±0.10, PDRN-B 0.59±0.19, PDRN-C 0.49±0.15 — all three PDRN groups significantly above control (ANOVA P=0.0002).
- 6 weeks: PDRN-A 1.44±0.19 and PDRN-C 1.17±0.25 significantly above control 0.81±0.31; PDRN-B 1.11±0.23 intermediate (ANOVA P=0.0003).
- Central NB% increased significantly from 2 to 6 weeks in every group, including control — a time-dependent healing response, so treatment effects were interpreted mainly from early intergroup differences.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Relatively small sample size (n=8 defects/group/time point) may have reduced power to detect subtle intergroup differences.
- Observation restricted to early healing (2 and 6 weeks); bone maturation and remodeling continue beyond this window.
- Rabbit calvarial defect findings cannot be directly extrapolated to human clinical conditions.
- Defects within an animal are not fully independent (four per rabbit); the analysis did not account for clustering.
- micro-CT grayscale threshold could not separate NB from residual BG, so BV/TV represents total mineralized tissue, not NB alone.
- The 2- vs 6-week comparisons are cross-sectional (different animals), not longitudinal; unexpectedly greater residual graft readings at 6 weeks were interpreted cautiously as sampling/sectioning variability rather than true graft gain.
- Formulation composition/physicochemical characterization was not fully reported; authors call for experimental and clinical studies to identify the optimal formulation and the clinical relevance of inter-formulation differences.

## 7. Glossary
- **PDRN**: Polydeoxyribonucleotide — DNA fragments (from salmon/trout sperm) that act via adenosine A2A receptor activation, VEGF upregulation, and anti-inflammatory effects.
- **BV/TV**: Bone Volume-to-Tissue Volume ratio — micro-CT metric of mineralized tissue fraction within the defect; here includes both new bone and residual graft.
- **micro-CT**: Micro-Computed Tomography — 3D radiographic imaging used to quantify BV/TV (SkyScan 1173, CTAn; grayscale threshold 55–225).
- **NB**: Newly formed bone — mineralized bone with osteocytes in lacunae, distinguished histologically from graft material.
- **BG**: Bone graft material — Osteon Xeno Collagen xenograft blocks (6 mm × 4 mm).
- **BG+NB**: Combined mineralized tissue — sum of residual graft and new bone area/fraction.
- **CT**: Fibrovascular connective tissue — non-mineralized fibrous and vascular tissue in the defect.
- **VS**: Void space — unoccupied space between graft particles or tissues.
- **Total augmented area / central region**: Histomorphometric ROIs — the whole augmented area and a 1.6-mm-wide strip centered on the defect.
- **Tukey post hoc**: Multiple-comparisons test following 1-way ANOVA; different superscript letters denote significant intergroup differences at P<0.05.
