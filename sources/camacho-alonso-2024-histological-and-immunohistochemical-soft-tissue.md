## Why Ingested

오목형 (Concave) vs 원통형 (Cylindrical) 지대주의 임플란트주위 연조직 (Peri-implant Soft Tissue) 차이를 조직학·면역조직화학 (Immunohistochemistry, IHC)으로 직접 측정한 다기관 분할구강 (Split-mouth) 무작위대조시험(RCT)이다. → [[implants/seijas-naya-2025-concave-straight-abutment-bone-rct]]의 오목 지대주 골 보호 효과에 대한 **조직학적 기전**을 제공하고, 오목/협소 지대주의 임상적 무차이를 보고한 [[overviews/abutment-emergence-profile-peri-implant-tissue-overview]]의 형태 레버 논쟁에 생물학적 엔드포인트를 추가한다.

## Three-line Summary

Multicenter split-mouth double-blind RCT, 74 implants in 37 patients (cylindrical vs concave 3-mm abutments), 12-week healing, four Spanish universities; histology, immunohistochemistry and FESEM of retrieved abutments.

Concave abutments showed greater total soft-tissue height (3.57 ± 0.28 vs 2.95 ± 0.27 mm, p<0.001) and barrier-epithelium extension (2.46 ± 0.17 vs 1.89 ± 0.21 mm, p<0.001), more transverse/perpendicular collagen fibers (23.76 ± 5.86 vs 15.68 ± 4.57%, p<0.001), and lower inflammatory/vascular intensity (1.05 ± 0.78 vs 1.97 ± 0.68, p<0.001).

Concave geometry extends the barrier epithelium without increasing abutment height and produces a better-oriented, less inflamed soft-tissue seal in thick phenotypes — but 3-month, 2D, single-design evidence cannot be extrapolated to thin tissue or long-term peri-implant health.

## 세줄요약

다기관 분할구강 이중맹검 무작위대조시험(RCT), 환자 37명·임플란트 74개(원통형 vs 오목형 3 mm 지대주), 12주 치유, 스페인 4개 대학; 조직학·면역조직화학(IHC) 및 회수 지대주 주사전자현미경(FESEM) 분석.

오목형 군에서 연조직 총 높이(3.57±0.28 vs 2.95±0.27 mm, p<0.001)와 장벽상피 연장(2.46±0.17 vs 1.89±0.21 mm, p<0.001)이 유의하게 컸고, 교원섬유의 횡단/수직 배열 비율(23.76±5.86 vs 15.68±4.57%, p<0.001)이 높았으며 염증·혈관 강도(1.05±0.78 vs 1.97±0.68, p<0.001)는 유의하게 낮음.

오목형 형태는 지대주 높이를 늘리지 않고도 장벽상피를 연장해 두꺼운 점막 표현형에서 더 잘 정렬되고 염증이 적은 연조직 봉쇄를 만든다 — 다만 3개월·2차원·단일 디자인 근거라 얇은 점막이나 장기 예후로 확대 해석할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First multicenter human RCT to evaluate **histological and immunohistochemical** peri-implant soft-tissue response to concave vs cylindrical abutment macrogeometry (rather than radiographic bone endpoints).
- Shows that the greater soft-tissue height around concave abutments is driven by **barrier-epithelium extension**, not by a statistically significant increase in supracrestal connective tissue (p=0.069).
- Demonstrates a more favorable **collagen-fiber orientation** (more transverse/perpendicular fibers) and lower T/B-cell inflammatory infiltration around concave abutments, providing a biological mechanism for earlier MBL findings.
- FESEM of retrieved abutments shows significantly more tissue remnants on concave surfaces (45.12 ± 3.03 vs 42.47 ± 1.32%, p=0.040), with no difference in biofilm or clean-surface area.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Multicenter, split-mouth, double-blind randomized clinical trial with 2 parallel arms (4 Spanish universities; March 2019–June 2022).
- **n**: 37 patients (26 men, 11 women; mean age 64.38 ± 13.72 years), 74 implants / 74 soft-tissue samples; sample size powered for a 30 µm fiber-thickness difference (80% power, α=0.05).
- **Implants/abutments**: Galimplant IPX bone-level, internal conical connection, platform switching, equicrestal placement; 4/4.5/5 mm diameter, 8–14 mm length. Both arms used 3-mm-high machined anodized titanium abutments with identical connection and surface; cylindrical = rotational aesthetic straight, concave = rotational slim. Abutments inserted at surgery at 30 Ncm (one-abutment-one-time).
- **Population**: Periodontally healthy, thick phenotype (baseline mucosal thickness 3.79 ± 1.25 mm; ≥3 mm supracrestal soft tissue and ≥2 mm keratinized mucosa required), ≥3 months healed extraction sockets, no regeneration.
- **Outcomes at 12 weeks**: Histometric soft-tissue dimensions (PM–AM total height, PM–aJE barrier epithelium, aJE–AM supracrestal connective tissue); collagen fiber orientation under polarized light; IHC inflammatory/vascular score (CD3, CD79a, CD68, myeloperoxidase, CD31; 0–3 scale); FESEM biofilm/clean/tissue-remnant percentages; Misch implant success.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 20.0; chi-square, paired t-test; p ≤ 0.05. Patients and statistician blinded; histology examiner not blinded.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Cylindrical | Concave | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total soft-tissue height (PM–AM), mm | 2.95 ± 0.27 | 3.57 ± 0.28 | <0.001 |
| Barrier epithelium (PM–aJE), mm | 1.89 ± 0.21 | 2.46 ± 0.17 | <0.001 |
| Supracrestal connective tissue (aJE–AM), mm | 1.03 ± 0.16 | 1.11 ± 0.17 | 0.069 (NS) |
| Transverse/perpendicular collagen, % | 15.68 ± 4.57 | 23.76 ± 5.86 | <0.001 |
| Longitudinal/parallel collagen, % | 84.32 ± 4.57 | 76.24 ± 5.86 | <0.001 |
| Inflammatory/vascular intensity (0–3) | 1.97 ± 0.68 | 1.05 ± 0.78 | <0.001 |
| Submucosal tissue remnants on abutment, % | 42.47 ± 1.32 | 45.12 ± 3.03 | 0.040 |
| Biofilm coverage, % | 2.40 ± 0.44 | 1.91 ± 0.48 | NS |
| Clean titanium surface, % | 55.17 ± 1.07 | 52.35 ± 0.65 | NS |

- Inflammatory/vascular intensity was greatest in the lower portion of the barrier epithelium and supracrestal connective tissue for **both** abutment types.
- All implants met Misch success criteria; no failures reported.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Short follow-up (3 months) and 2-dimensional histological analysis only.
- Results apply only to subcrestal bone-level implants with conical internal connection, platform switching, and this specific abutment design/surface.
- All patients were periodontally healthy with abundant bone and soft tissue — no thin-phenotype, periodontitis, or compromised-site data.
- Center effect and patient demographics were not modeled; examiner was not blinded; no patient-reported outcomes.

## 7. Glossary

- **Barrier epithelium (PM–aJE)**: Epithelial portion of the peri-implant seal from the mucosal margin to the apical extension of the junctional epithelium; ~1.5–2 mm.
- **Supracrestal connective tissue (aJE–AM)**: Connective-tissue portion between the apical junctional epithelium and the apical limit of the mucosa; ~1–1.5 mm.
- **Collagen fiber orientation**: Percentage of fibers running parallel vs transverse/perpendicular to the abutment surface; transverse orientation is interpreted as a mechanically stable, functional seal.
- **FESEM**: Field-emission scanning electron microscopy, used here to quantify biofilm, exposed titanium and tissue remnants on retrieved abutments.
- **Split-mouth design**: Each patient receives both abutment types, so each subject serves as their own control.
