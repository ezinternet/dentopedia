## Why Ingested

Requested to fill the gap in PSAA intraoperative hemorrhage management evidence; this CBCT-based study of 1152 sinuses provides the most detailed lateral-window-height–specific PSAA localization framework to date, directly extending the anatomy data already held in [[sinus-lift/lateral/gulec-2025-psaa-cbct-maxillary-sinus-anatomy]].

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT analysis of 1152 maxillary sinuses (Romanian cohort) classifying PSAA location against a 4-class lateral window height framework.
PSAA detected in 68.1%; most commonly submucosal (48.6%), mean sinus-floor distance 8.3±4.3 mm; large windows (>11 mm) significantly associated with intraosseous course (χ²=19.11, p=0.004; Cramer's V=0.111).
Preoperative CBCT-based anatomical assessment is essential for individualized lateral window planning to minimize vascular complications.

## 세줄요약

루마니아 코호트 1152개 상악동을 대상으로 측방창 높이 4분류 체계에 따라 후상치조동맥 (Posterior Superior Alveolar Artery, PSAA) 위치를 분석한 후향적 콘빔CT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT) 연구.
PSAA 검출률 68.1%; 점막하형 (submucosal) 48.6% 최다, 동저 평균거리 8.3±4.3 mm; 대형창 (>11 mm) 에서 골내형 (intraosseous) PSAA 유의하게 증가 (p=0.004).
측방 상악동거상술 (Lateral Sinus Augmentation, LSA) 계획 시 개인화된 창 디자인을 위해 CBCT 기반 혈관해부 평가가 필수적이다.

## Key Contributions

- Novel 4-class radiographic classification correlating lateral window vertical position to PSAA localization risk
- Largest Romanian-cohort CBCT study: n=1152 MS (547 male, 605 female)
- Demonstrates that large windows (>11 mm) are significantly more likely to encounter intraosseous PSAA
- PSAA mean distance from sinus floor: 8.30 mm (SD 4.31) — consistent with existing data
- Sex difference noted: PSAA detection 72.4% (male) vs. 64.1% (female)
- Provides mean PSAA-sinus floor distance stratified by window class: low risk <3mm → 2.0mm; small window (3-8mm) → 5.9mm; medium (8-11mm) → 9.4mm; large (>11mm) → 14.2mm

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective observational cross-sectional, STROBE guidelines
- **n**: 1152 maxillary sinuses from CBCT scans acquired for routine diagnostic purposes
- **Institution**: Municipal Emergency Hospital, Timisoara, Romania
- **Equipment**: Planmeca VISOG7 CBCT (110 kV, 8 mA, FOV 8×15 cm / 13×15 cm, voxel 0.15 mm)
- **Software**: Romexis 6.4.2.49 acquisition; OnDemand3D (Cybermed) analysis
- **PSAA classification**: Class 1 intraosseous / Class 2 submucosal / Class 3 extraosseous
- **Window height classification**: Class 1 (<3mm), Class 2 (3-8mm small), Class 3 (8-11mm medium), Class 4 (>11mm large)
- **Statistics**: χ² test for independence; Cramer's V; hierarchical loglinear analysis (sex)
- **Inclusion**: Age ≥18, complete MS visualization, no trauma/surgery/pathology/active infection history
- **Exclusion**: Fully edentulous maxilla, artifacts, incomplete FOV, MS pathology

|---|---|---|

| 8-11mm (medium) | 216/216 | 25.9% |
| >11mm (large) | 160/160 | 35.0% ↑ |

- **Mean PSAA-sinus floor distance**: 8.30 mm (95% CI 8.00–8.60)
- **Chi-square**: χ²(6)=19.11, p=0.004; association significant but weak (V=0.111)
- **Large window intraosseous residual**: +2.3 (significantly overrepresented)
- **Sex interaction**: Three-way loglinear analysis — sex did not modify window-PSAA relationship (p=0.332)
- **Detection by sex**: Male 72.4%, Female 64.1% (descriptive only, mechanism unclear)

## Limitations

- Retrospective design; CBCT acquired for routine diagnosis, not sinus lift planning
- Dental status (dentate/edentulous) not analyzed as dedicated variable
- Bilateral sinus inclusion — partial statistical dependence between pairs
- Window classification is exploratory/study-specific, not validated externally
- No correlation with intraoperative findings or clinical outcomes
- Intra-rater reliability not evaluated

## Glossary

- **PSAA (Posterior Superior Alveolar Artery, 후상치조동맥)**: Branch of maxillary artery supplying lateral maxillary sinus wall and Schneiderian membrane; principal bleeding risk in lateral window SFE
- **Alveolar antral artery (치조동맥)**: Anastomosis between PSAA and infraorbital artery within lateral sinus wall
- **Intraosseous course (골내형 경로)**: PSAA canal completely encased within cortical bone of lateral wall — highest hemorrhage risk as disruption requires bone removal
- **Submucosal course (점막하형 경로)**: PSAA running immediately beneath Schneiderian membrane — elevated with membrane during SFE, most common pattern
- **Lateral window height classification**: Study-specific 4-class radiographic framework correlating window vertical position to PSAA encounter risk
