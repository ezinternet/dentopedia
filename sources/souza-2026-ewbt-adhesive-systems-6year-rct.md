## Why Ingested

Longest-term (6-year) RCT of the ethanol-wet bonding technique (EWBT) in NCCLs. The wiki's only prior clinical EWBT evidence was [[resin-bonding/mortazavi-2012-ethanol-wet-bonding-clinical-trial]] (36 NCCLs, 12 months), which used EWBT with the hydrophobic bond bottle of OptiBond FL only and concluded "equal performance". This trial's EB arm (EWBT + hydrophobic bond bottle only, same concept) failed at 6 years (48.7% retention vs 88.9% control, log-rank p=0.008), while EWBT with hydrophilic adhesives gave no benefit — reversing the short-term optimism and supplying the long-term clinical arm that [[resin-bonding/forville-2024-moist-dentin-adhesive-systems-reevaluation]] and [[overviews/resin-dentin-bond-durability-degradation-overview]] lacked for EWBT.

## Three-line Summary

Double-blind (patient + evaluator) RCT, Brazil (ReBEC RBR-5hncr3): 148 NCCLs in 67 adults randomized at restoration level to NE (water-wet + 3-step E&R Scotchbond Multi-Purpose), EMP (EWBT + Scotchbond MP), EB (EWBT + Scotchbond MP hydrophobic Bond only), EU (EWBT + Single/Scotchbond Universal); 100% ethanol 60 s; follow-up 6 years (72–83 months).

6-year retention (ITT, LOCF): NE 88.9%, EU 81.1%, EMP 77.8%, EB 48.7%; Kaplan-Meier log-rank p=0.008 overall, EB inferior to NE (p=0.030), EU (p=0.005), EMP (p=0.015); no difference among the three hydrophilic protocols; EB already inferior from 6 months (p<0.01).

EWBT adds no longevity benefit when hydrophilic adhesives are used and makes a purely hydrophobic bonding resin clinically unacceptable; limitations are restoration-level analysis without cluster adjustment, LOCF imputation (may underestimate failure), 72.9% recall, and unblinded operator.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검(환자·평가자) RCT, 브라질: 성인 67명의 비우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 148개를 수복 단위로 4군 무작위 배정 — NE(수분 습윤 + 3단계 산부식-수세 (Etch-and-Rinse, E&R)), EMP(에탄올 습윤 접착술 (Ethanol-Wet Bonding Technique, EWBT) + 3단계 E&R), EB(EWBT + 소수성 본딩 레진 단독), EU(EWBT + 유니버설 접착제); 100% 에탄올 60초, 6년(72–83개월) 추적.

6년 유지율(ITT): NE 88.9%, EU 81.1%, EMP 77.8%, EB 48.7%; 카플란-마이어 로그순위 검정 p=0.008, EB만 나머지 3군 모두보다 유의하게 열등(p=0.030/0.005/0.015), 친수성 3군 간 차이 없음; EB는 6개월부터 이미 열등.

친수성 접착제에서는 EWBT가 수명 개선 효과가 없고, 순수 소수성 레진과 결합하면 임상적으로 부적합 — 한계는 환자 내 군집효과 미보정, 마지막 관측값 이월 (LOCF) 대치로 실패율 과소추정 가능, 회수율 72.9%, 술자 비맹검.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First 6-year clinical data on EWBT; prior clinical EWBT studies followed 6–24 months.
- Direct head-to-head of EWBT combined with adhesives of differing hydrophilicity (3-step E&R with primer, universal adhesive, hydrophobic bond only) against a water-wet control.
- Shows EWBT effectiveness depends on adhesive hydrophilicity: no benefit with hydrophilic systems; harmful with an undiluted hydrophobic bonding resin.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design:** randomized, double-blind (patients and two calibrated evaluators blinded; operator not blinded) controlled trial; randomization by envelope at restoration level; "no repetition" rule — each lesion in a patient got a different protocol; allocation order by predefined arch-position sequence.
- **Sample size:** equivalence design, α=5%, power 80%, 96% success, 15% equivalence limit → 30/group; 35/group planned; 148 enrolled (NE 36, EU 37, EMP 36, EB 39).
- **Participants:** adults ≥18 y with 1–4 NCCLs in canines/premolars, ≥1 mm depth and height, occlusal antagonists; excluded severe parafunction without splint, periodontal disease, non-vital/carious teeth. 121 screened (262 lesions) → 67 patients, 148 restorations. Baseline characteristics homogeneous (≈64% wedge-shaped; ~77% working-side).
- **Protocol:** pumice prophylaxis; no bevel; 37% phosphoric acid 15 s; EWBT groups: 100% ethanol actively applied 60 s, excess air-dried.
  - NE: Scotchbond Multi-Purpose primer (dry 5 s) + bond actively 20 s, air 5 s, light-cure 10 s.
  - EMP: ethanol + same MP primer + bond.
  - EB: ethanol + MP bond (third step) only.
  - EU: ethanol + universal adhesive (Methods name "Scotchbond Universal"; Table 1/abstract "Single Bond Universal"; contains 10-MDP, HEMA) actively 20 s.
  - Composite Filtek Z350 XT, incremental; Radii-Cal LED 900 mW/cm²; finishing after 7 days.
- **Evaluation:** modified USPHS at baseline (1 week), 6 mo, 18 mo, 6 y (72–83 months, window stretched by COVID-19). Calibration kappa 0.61.
- **Statistics:** restoration as unit; ITT with LOCF; Kaplan-Meier + log-rank (dropouts right-censored); Fisher's exact per time point; Friedman + Wilcoxon (Bonferroni) intra-group. No cluster adjustment (acknowledged).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Recall:** 42 patients / 108 restorations examined at 6 years (72.9%); all 148 in ITT.

**Retention (primary outcome)**

| Group | Baseline | 6 mo | 18 mo | 6 y |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NE (water-wet + 3-step E&R) | 100% | 100% | 100% | **88.9%** (4 lost) |
| EU (EWBT + universal) | 100% | 97.3% | 94.6% | **81.1%** (7 lost) |
| EMP (EWBT + 3-step E&R) | 100% | 97.2% | 94.4% | **77.8%** (8 lost) |
| EB (EWBT + hydrophobic Bond only) | 97.4% | 76.3% | 60.5% | **48.7%** text / 47.4% Table 4 (20 lost) |

- Overall 6-year survival 73.6% (109/148); 39 restorations lost.
- Fisher's exact among groups: p>0.05 baseline; p<0.01 at 6 mo, 18 mo, 6 y.
- Kaplan-Meier log-rank overall p=0.008; pairwise EB vs NE p=0.030, vs EU p=0.005, vs EMP p=0.015; no significant difference among NE, EU, EMP.
- All groups declined significantly within-group over time (Friedman p<0.01).

**Secondary outcomes at 6 years (success = Alpha)**

| Criterion | NE | EU | EMP | EB | p (6 y) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marginal discoloration | 58.3% | 54.1% | 41.7% | 35.9% | 0.179 |
| Marginal adaptation | 50.0% | 37.8% | 38.9% | 23.1% | 0.111 |
| Surface texture | 61.1% | 56.8% | 47.2% | 33.3% | NS at all times |
| No post-op sensitivity | 83.3% | 75.7% | 77.8% | 46.2% | <0.01 (NE > EB) |

- EB worse for discoloration at 6 mo (p=0.049) and 18 mo (p<0.01), and marginal adaptation at 6 mo (p=0.022) and 18 mo (p<0.01); differences lost significance at 6 years as all groups degraded.
- No secondary caries or anatomic-form change in any surviving restoration.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Randomization and analysis at restoration level; patients contribute to multiple groups; no cluster (within-patient) adjustment.
- LOCF imputation assumes no change after last visit — likely underestimates failure.
- 72.9% recall; 6-year window 72–83 months (COVID-19).
- Operator not blinded; single centre; kappa 0.61 only moderate.
- Internal inconsistencies: EB n=39 (Results text, Table 3, 48.7%) vs n=38 in Table 4 (18/38 = 47.4%); the universal adhesive is named both "Scotchbond Universal" and "Single Bond Universal"; one Discussion paragraph says "the etch-and-rinse strategy showed a more pronounced reduction", which contradicts the reported data (NE control best at 88.9%).
- The EB arm used an undiluted commercial hydrophobic bond; ethanol-solvated experimental hydrophobic primers (the original EWBT concept) are not commercially available and were not tested.
- Briefing figures matched the paper's text values; the 47.4% Table 4 variant noted above.

## 7. Glossary

- **EWBT** — ethanol-wet bonding technique: replacing residual water in etched dentin with ethanol before adhesive application to allow hydrophobic monomer infiltration.
- **NE / EMP / EB / EU** — non-ethanol control / EWBT + Scotchbond Multi-Purpose / EWBT + MP Bond only / EWBT + universal adhesive.
- **NCCL** — non-carious cervical lesion.
- **USPHS (modified)** — Alpha/Bravo/Charlie clinical rating criteria for restorations.
- **LOCF** — last observation carried forward imputation.
- **ITT** — intention-to-treat.
