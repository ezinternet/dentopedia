## Why Ingested

임플란트 실패 원인을 early/late failure로 나눈 classical narrative 분류 체계를 확보하기 위해 인제스트. 최근 확보한 [[implants/giok-2026-factors-implant-failure-umbrella-review]] (25개 메타분석 종합 umbrella review)와 같은 주제를 다루지만, 본 논문은 정량적 근거등급 대신 6개 기존 분류체계(Nallaswamy, Rosenberg, Esposito, Truhlar/Tonetti-Schmid, Matukas, El Askary)를 종합한 **early/late working classification**을 제시하는 서술적(narrative) 종합이라 근거수준은 낮지만 임상 교육·차트리뷰용 분류 틀로서 별도 가치가 있음. giok-2026이 정량적 위험인자 근거를 대체하므로 kate-2016 본문에 partial supersession 배너 추가.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (concise review article, no formal systematic search protocol) proposing a consolidated "early vs. late" working classification of dental implant failure, synthesizing six prior classification schemes (Nallaswamy, Rosenberg et al., Esposito et al., Truhlar/Tonetti-Schmid, Matukas, El Askary et al.).

Early failures are attributed to implant selection (e.g., 9.5% failure for 7 mm implants vs. 3.8% for other lengths), bone type (35% of failures in porous trabecular bone per Jaffin & Berman 1991), surgical/prosthetic errors, and overheating (5.8% loss, Salonen 1993); late failures are attributed to host factors (diabetes: 85% success rate, Fiorellini 2000), smoking (11.3% vs. 4.8% failure in nonsmokers), bruxism (41% vs. 12% loss in parafunction, Glauser et al.), radiotherapy (~70% success), and peri-implantitis/retrograde infection.

Clinical implication: implant failure is multifactorial and the proposed early/late × etiology/personnel/failure-mode/biological-cause matrix is a useful chairside triage framework, but this is a low-evidence-weight narrative synthesis of secondary citations (not a systematic review/meta-analysis) — numeric claims should be cross-checked against primary studies or higher-tier syntheses such as [[implants/giok-2026-factors-implant-failure-umbrella-review]].

## 세줄요약

서술적 종합(narrative review, 공식 체계적 검색 프로토콜 없음) — 기존 6개 임플란트 실패 분류체계(Nallaswamy, Rosenberg 등, Esposito 등, Truhlar/Tonetti-Schmid, Matukas, El Askary 등)를 종합해 "조기(early) vs 후기(late)" 통합 작업 분류(working classification)를 제안.

조기 실패는 임플란트 선택(7mm 임플란트 9.5% 실패 vs 기타 길이 3.8%), 골질(다공성 소주골에서 실패의 35%, Jaffin & Berman 1991), 수술·보철 오류, 과열(5.8% 소실, Salonen 1993)에 기인; 후기 실패는 숙주 요인(당뇨병: 성공률 85%, Fiorellini 2000), 흡연(비흡연자 4.8% vs 흡연자 11.3% 실패), 이갈이/브럭시즘(부기능 시 41% vs 12% 소실, Glauser 등), 방사선치료(성공률 약 70%), 주위염(peri-implantitis)/역행성 감염에 기인.

임상적 의미: 임플란트 실패는 다인성(multifactorial)이며 제안된 early/late × 병인/책임자/실패양상/생물학적원인 매트릭스는 체어사이드 분류 틀로 유용하나, 본 논문은 체계적 문헌고찰이 아닌 2차 인용 중심 저근거 서술 종합이므로 수치는 원저 또는 [[implants/giok-2026-factors-implant-failure-umbrella-review]] 같은 상위 근거로 교차검증 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Compiles and diagrams six prior implant-failure classification systems (Nallaswamy; Rosenberg et al.; Esposito et al.; Truhlar and Tonetti-Schmid; Matukas; El Askary et al.) side by side.
- Proposes a new consolidated "working classification" splitting failures into **early** (intraoperative or within 3 months) vs. **late** (postoperative, after 3 months), each cross-classified by etiology, personnel responsible, failure mode, and biological cause.
- Assembles a large set of secondary-cited numeric findings (implant length/diameter, bone type, host disease, smoking, bruxism, radiotherapy) into one narrative table (Table 1).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Narrative/concise review article — no PRISMA flow, no stated database search strategy or inclusion/exclusion criteria; synthesizes prior reviews and individual primary studies cited secondarily.
- **Databases**: Not reported (not a systematic review).
- **n**: N/A — narrative synthesis of ~34 references (prior classification papers + primary clinical studies).
- **Outcomes**: Qualitative classification of implant failure causes; no pooled effect estimates.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 7 mm implants: 9.5% failure vs. 3.8% for all other lengths (Bahat 1993, posterior maxilla, n=732 implants).
- All 5 mm-diameter implants: 2%–3% failure; double implants: 1%–2% (Bahat & Handelsman 1996).
- Porous trabecular bone: 35% of all implant failures (Jaffin & Berman 1991, 5-year analysis).
- Bone overheating (47°C for >1 min): 5.8% of implants lost to osseointegration failure (Salonen et al. 1993).
- Diabetic patients: 85% implant success rate (Fiorellini et al. 2000); duration of diabetes correlates with more failures (Olson et al. 2000).
- Patients aged 60–70: higher implant-failure risk vs. patients <40 (Moy et al. 2005).
- Smokers: 11.3% failure vs. 4.8% in nonsmokers (cited secondary source); 4.2% (321/7680 implants) failed due to smoking including passive smoking, Israel cohort 1999–2008 (Twito & Sade).
- Bruxers: 41% implant loss vs. 12% in non-parafunctional patients among 127 immediately loaded implants in 41 patients (Glauser et al.).
- Radiotherapy patients: ~70% implant success rate reported (Jacobsson et al. 1988 showed increasing implant loss over time with radiation).
- Abutment screw loosening complication rate: 7%, overall implant failure rate 4.6% (Robert L. Simon 2003).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No systematic search methodology, PRISMA flow, or quality/risk-of-bias appraisal of the underlying primary studies — all numeric claims are single-study point estimates cited secondarily, several from studies now 20–30+ years old.
- No meta-analytic pooling; contradictory or overlapping classification schemes are juxtaposed rather than reconciled with modern evidence-grading.
- No discussion of implant surface technology, digital workflow, or peri-implantitis surgical management, which have evolved substantially since 2016.
- The proposed working classification (Table 1) has not been externally validated for inter-rater reliability or prospective prognostic use.

## 6. Related Work
- giok-2026 (umbrella review, 25 meta-analyses/35 associations): supplies GRADE/credibility-tier quantification of many factors this paper only cites narratively (smoking, bone quality, immediate loading).
- yari-2023 (single-center retrospective cohort): multivariate early-failure predictors partially overlapping with this paper's "early failure" bucket.
- fan-2024, naseri-2020 (smoking-focused SR+MA): quantify the smoking association this paper cites via older single studies (11.3% vs. 4.8%).

## 7. Glossary
- **Osseointegration**: Direct structural and functional connection between living bone and the surface of a load-bearing implant.
- **Peri-implantitis**: Inflammatory process affecting the soft and hard tissues around an osseointegrated implant, causing progressive loss of supporting bone.
- **Retrograde (apical) peri-implantitis**: Periapical implant lesion developing shortly after implant insertion while the coronal bone-implant interface remains normal; rare cause of failure.
- **Crown-root ratio**: Ratio of the length of a restoration/superstructure above the bone to the implant length within bone; unfavorable ratios (e.g., short implants) increase biomechanical failure risk.
