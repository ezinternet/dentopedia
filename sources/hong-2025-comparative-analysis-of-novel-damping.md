## Why Ingested

신형 감쇠능 분석 (damping capacity analysis, DCA) 장비 AnyCheck와 The Trust를 구강 내 실제 임상에서 Periotest와 비교한 한국 병원 후향적 연구로, 부하 전 이차 안정성 평가 도구의 일치도(r = -0.870 vs -0.473)와 신뢰도(ICC 0.94-0.98)를 제공한다. 조기 실패 평가에서 안정성 측정 도구의 한계(기준 장비와의 일치도일 뿐 실패 예측 타당도는 아님)를 이해하는 데 쓰인다. → [[implants/isq/hong-2025-comparative-analysis-of-novel-damping]] · [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective chart analysis at Ilsan Hospital (Korea) of 37 patients and 58 implants (secondary-stability check before occlusal loading, on average 91 days after placement) comparing two new damping capacity analysis (DCA) devices, AnyCheck (DCA-A) and The Trust (DCA-T), against Periotest (DCA-P) as the reference, with 3 readings per implant per device.

Accuracy versus Periotest: Spearman r = -0.870 for AnyCheck (R2 0.828) versus r = -0.473 for The Trust (R2 0.379; Z = 4.28, p < 0.05); The Trust improved to r = -0.70 (R2 0.6067) after excluding values above 90. Reliability was excellent for all three devices (ICC 0.98 Periotest, 0.96 AnyCheck, 0.94 The Trust) with no significant between-device difference.

AnyCheck accuracy was higher in the maxilla than the mandible (p = 0.0037) and lower at site #30 than #20 (p = 0.0010); The Trust showed no significant subgroup differences. The sample is small, Periotest is itself the reference rather than a histologic or outcome standard, and the paper contains several internal numerical inconsistencies, so the findings show agreement between devices, not diagnostic validity for predicting implant failure.

일산병원에서 37명 58개 임플란트(부하 전 이차 안정성 평가, 식립 후 평균 91일)를 후향적으로 분석해, 신형 감쇠능 분석 (damping capacity analysis, DCA) 장비인 AnyCheck (DCA-A)와 The Trust (DCA-T)를 기준 장비 Periotest (DCA-P)와 비교했다(임플란트당 장비별 3회 측정).

Periotest 대비 정확도는 AnyCheck가 Spearman r = -0.870 (R2 0.828), The Trust가 r = -0.473 (R2 0.379)로 AnyCheck가 유의하게 높았고(Z = 4.28, p < 0.05), The Trust는 90 초과 값을 제외하면 r = -0.70 (R2 0.6067)로 개선되었다. 신뢰도 (intraclass correlation coefficient, ICC)는 세 장비 모두 우수(Periotest 0.98, AnyCheck 0.96, The Trust 0.94)했고 장비 간 유의한 차이는 없었다.

AnyCheck 정확도는 상악이 하악보다 높았고(p = 0.0037) #30 부위가 #20보다 낮았다(p = 0.0010); The Trust는 하위 변수별 유의한 차이가 없었다. 표본이 작고 Periotest 자체가 기준이며 논문 내 수치 불일치가 여럿 있어, 이 결과는 장비 간 일치도이지 임플란트 실패 예측의 진단 타당도가 아니다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Chairside comparison of three damping capacity analysis devices in 58 implants (37 patients): Periotest (DCA-P, reference), AnyCheck (DCA-A), The Trust (DCA-T).
- Accuracy against Periotest: AnyCheck Spearman r = -0.870 (R2 0.828) vs The Trust r = -0.473 (R2 0.379); Z-test 4.28 (r) and 4.24 (R2), p < 0.05. The Trust after excluding values above 90: r = -0.70, R2 0.6067.
- Reliability (ICC of 3 readings): Periotest 0.98, AnyCheck 0.96 (95% CI 0.95-0.98), The Trust 0.94 (0.90-0.96); no significant between-device difference.
- Position effects: AnyCheck accuracy higher in maxilla than mandible (p = 0.0037), lower at #30 than #20 (p = 0.0010), higher when DCAV-P above -5.5 (p = 0.0124); Periotest reliability lower in mandible and at #40 vs #10.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective chart analysis, single center, 37 patients and 58 implants measured in November 2023; IRB NHIMC 2024-05-002, consent waived. The ethics text labels the study "prospective" and retrospective in the same paragraph.
- **Timing**: before occlusal loading, mean 91 days (SD 41.91) after placement; delayed when secondary surgery was done or readings were insufficient for loading (PTV < 0).
- **Protocol**: 3 readings per device per implant, 10-second intervals, healing abutment attached, device parallel to the ground, buccal strike, manufacturer correction values for abutment height; devices used sequentially.
- **Devices (Table 5)**: Periotest 153 g, 16 taps, 1.2 N, about 4 s; AnyCheck 100 g, 6 taps, 0.9 N, about 3 s, scale 0-99; The Trust 80 g, 7 taps, 0.6-0.8 N, about 3 s, scale 0-90.
- **Variables**: anterior/posterior, arch, quadrant (#10 right maxilla, #20 left maxilla, #30 left mandible, #40 right mandible), healing abutment height/diameter, fixture diameter/length, period (90 days), bone graft, DCAV-P magnitude (-5.5).
- **Statistics**: Spearman correlation of mean readings; Fisher z-test for subgroup differences; ICC with 95% CI overlap for reliability differences; sample size by PASS 11 (reference r = 0.777, power 0.90, alpha 0.05, minimum 25); SPSS 23.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small retrospective single-center sample; small subgroups; authors call for larger and more diverse samples. Clustering of multiple implants per patient is not addressed in the text.
- Periotest serves as reference, so results show agreement with another damping device, not validity against histology, ISQ or clinical failure; authors note some apparent regional accuracy loss may reflect true Periotest differences.
- The Trust saturates at 90; exclusion above 90 was a post hoc sensitivity step; the methods also mention truncation at 90 for AnyCheck but no such result is reported.
- Reliability differences judged by 95% CI overlap (conservative, no exact test); authors suggest alternative designs to verify osseointegration with The Trust.
- In-paper inconsistencies: ethics text says prospective and retrospective; Table 4 total Periotest ICC CI 0.78-0.99 vs text 0.97-0.99; R2 values are not the squares of the Spearman r (0.870 squared is 0.757 vs 0.828; 0.473 squared is 0.224 vs 0.379); p = 0.0039 for r = -0.870 exceeds p = 0.0002 for r = -0.473 at the same n; Introduction gives The Trust force as under 3 N vs 0.6-0.8 N in Table 5. ## 7. Glossary

- **DCA (Damping Capacity Analysis, 감쇠능 분석)**: stability assessment from the contact time/damping of a percussion strike on the implant or abutment.
- **PTV (Periotest Value)**: Periotest output from -8 (rigid) to +50 (non-integrated); lower is more stable.
- **DCAV-P / DCAV-A / DCAV-T**: values from Periotest / AnyCheck / The Trust.
- **ICC (Intraclass Correlation Coefficient, 급내상관계수)**: reproducibility of repeated readings.
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis, 공명주파수 분석)**: ISQ-based stability measurement requiring a transducer.
- **Secondary stability (이차 안정성)**: biological stability from bone remodeling around the implant.
