## Three-line Summary

Nested case-control study with risk-set sampling comparing 550 failed implants in 328 subjects against 1156 control implants in 502 subjects (mean follow-up 59.07 months) to identify patient-related risk factors for implant failure.

Odds of failure were elevated with periodontitis (OR 12.82, 95% CI 7.12-23.08), smoking (OR 2.70, 1.60-4.57), immunosuppressive medication (OR 2.75, 1.31-5.76) and type 2 diabetes (OR 1.70, 1.11-2.61); stage III-IV periodontitis was associated with failure independent of diabetes and smoking (OR 1.64, 1.24-2.18), and adherence to periodontal maintenance reduced failure risk.

Early failure was associated with maxillary placement, short length (10 mm or less) and smoking, but the abstract gives no early-failure definition, rate or effect sizes; evidence is retrospective and abstract-only.

## 세줄요약

위험집합 표집 방식의 내포 사례-대조군 연구로, 실패 임플란트 550개(대상자 328명)와 대조 임플란트 1156개(대상자 502명)를 평균 59.07개월 추적하여 환자 관련 위험인자를 분석.

실패 오즈는 치주염 (OR 12.82, 95% CI 7.12-23.08), 흡연 (OR 2.70, 1.60-4.57), 면역억제제 복용 (OR 2.75, 1.31-5.76), 제2형 당뇨병 (OR 1.70, 1.11-2.61)에서 증가; III-IV기 치주염은 당뇨·흡연과 독립적으로 실패와 연관 (OR 1.64, 1.24-2.18)되었고 치주 유지관리 순응은 실패 위험을 낮춤.

조기 실패는 상악 식립, 짧은 길이(10 mm 이하), 흡연과 연관되었으나 초록에는 조기 실패의 정의·비율·효과크기가 없음; 후향적 설계이며 초록 기반.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Large implant-level case-control sample with patient-record covariates.
2. Periodontitis (OR 12.82) and T2DM (OR 1.70) quantified as failure risk factors; stage III-IV periodontitis independently associated (OR 1.64) after accounting for T2DM and smoking.
3. Immunosuppressive medication, osteoporosis and statin use added as risk factors; periodontal-maintenance adherence protective.
4. Early-failure signal: maxilla, short implants (10 mm or less), smoking.

## 3. Methodology

Nested case-control design with a risk-set sampling scheme. Cases were failed implants (550 in 328 subjects); controls were 1156 implants in 502 subjects. Patient-related factors were collected from patient records. Univariate analysis gave ORs with 95% CI; multiple logistic regression evaluated covariates and independent risk factors. The early-failure definition (time window) is not reported in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results

- Smoking OR 2.70 (95% CI 1.60-4.57); T2DM OR 1.70 (1.11-2.61); periodontitis OR 12.82 (7.12-23.08); immunosuppressive medication OR 2.75 (1.31-5.76).
- Stage III and IV periodontitis OR 1.64 (1.24-2.18), independent of T2DM and smoking.
- Multivariate independent risk factors: periodontitis history, smoking, osteoporosis, statin use; adherence to periodontal maintenance significantly reduced failure risk.
- Early failure associated with maxillary placement, short length (10 mm or less) and smoking; no early-failure rate or effect sizes given.
- Authors' conclusion: the findings substantiate the association of T2DM and periodontitis with increased implant failure risk; immunosuppressive medication, smoking and patient compliance also matter.

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract-only: setting, early-failure definition and rate, adjusted ORs and covariate definitions are not verifiable.
- Retrospective record-based data; case-control design yields odds ratios, not incidence.
- The univariate periodontitis OR (12.82) and the stage III-IV OR (1.64) derive from different comparisons; the abstract does not specify the reference groups.
- Handling of clustering of implants within subjects is not described.

## 7. Glossary

- **Nested case-control (내포 사례-대조군)**: controls sampled from the cohort at risk when each case occurs (risk-set sampling).
- **OR (Odds Ratio, 오즈비)**: ratio of the odds of failure between exposed and unexposed.
- **T2DM (Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, 제2형 당뇨병)**: systemic metabolic risk factor studied.
- **Periodontal maintenance (치주 유지관리)**: scheduled supportive periodontal care after therapy.
- **Early implant failure (조기 임플란트 실패)**: failure before prosthetic loading; time window not defined in the abstract.
