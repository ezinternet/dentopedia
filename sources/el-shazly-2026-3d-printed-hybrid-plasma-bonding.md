## Three-line Summary

In vitro micro-shear bond strength study (Sci Rep 2026;16:11237) testing 5 surface treatment combinations on 3D-printed hybrid ceramic (Saremc Print Crowntec): non-thermal atmospheric pressure plasma (NTAP) alone, 50 µm sandblasting, 110 µm sandblasting, 50 µm sandblasting + NTAP, and 110 µm sandblasting + NTAP; bonded with universal adhesive and composite.

Combined 50 µm sandblasting + NTAP (SP50) produced the highest bond strength (2.70 ± 0.49 MPa), significantly higher than sandblasting alone; NTAP alone achieved 2.35 MPa (comparable to sandblasting); all values were low (2.12–2.70 MPa).

3D-printed hybrid ceramics are a challenging substrate for adhesive bonding; combining fine sandblasting with NTAP plasma is the best current strategy but the low absolute bond strengths indicate this material class requires further adhesive protocol optimization.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 미세전단결합강도 연구(Sci Rep 2026;16:11237) — 3D 프린팅 하이브리드 세라믹(Saremc Print Crowntec)에 비열 대기압 플라즈마(Non-Thermal Atmospheric Pressure Plasma, NTAP) 단독·50 µm 샌드블라스팅·110 µm 샌드블라스팅·50 µm SB+NTAP·110 µm SB+NTAP 5가지 표면처리를 비교.

50 µm SB+NTAP(SP50)군이 2.70 ± 0.49 MPa로 가장 높음(p<0.05 vs SB 단독); NTAP 단독 2.35 MPa로 샌드블라스팅과 유사; 전 군 2.12–2.70 MPa의 낮은 절대값.

3D 프린팅 하이브리드 세라믹은 접착이 어려운 기질 — 미세 샌드블라스팅+NTAP 병용이 현재 최선이나 낮은 절대 결합강도는 추가 프로토콜 최적화 필요를 시사.

## 1. Key Results
- 5 groups: NTAP alone, 50µm SB, 110µm SB, SB50+NTAP, SB110+NTAP
- SP50 (50µm SB + NTAP): 2.70 ± 0.49 MPa (highest, p<0.05 vs S110 and S50)
- NTAP alone: 2.35 MPa — comparable to sandblasting
- All values relatively low (2.12–2.70 MPa) vs conventional zirconia/ceramic bonding
- 3D-printed hybrid ceramics: challenging substrate for adhesive bonding
- Thermocycling: performed before testing
