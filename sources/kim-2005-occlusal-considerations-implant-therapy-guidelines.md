## Why Ingested

Occlusion for implants is a core clinical decision, and the wiki's occlusion folder lacks an implant-specific guideline source. This Kim et al. 2005 review offers clinical guidelines with a biomechanical rationale, and it states explicitly that no evidence-based, implant-specific occlusal concept exists. That statement should be recorded beside the other occlusion sources. Related wiki pages: [[occlusion/occlusion]], [[overviews/implant-occlusion-loading-biomechanics-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Review with clinical guidelines (Clinical Oral Implants Research, 2005; biomechanical rationale for implant occlusion) — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

Overload factors named are large cantilevers, parafunction, improper occlusal design and premature contacts; the authors recommend keeping implant occlusion within physiologic limits to support long-term success.

The authors state there is no evidence-based implant-specific occlusal concept, so the guidelines should be treated as rationale-based recommendations rather than tested rules.
