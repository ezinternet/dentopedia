## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-institution cohort at Wonkwang University Dental Hospital (Korea; nationality/ethnicity of patients not stated in the text, Korean setting only implied by the institution): 604 implants in 326 patients placed between the mental foramina (June 2010 - June 2023), with a mean observation of 44.38 months (range 1 day to 163 months).

Overall cumulative survival was 97.68% (14 implants failed; failure = implant loss or removal, denominator implants); log-rank curves were lower for immediate loading (chi-square 12.71, df 2), screw-retained prostheses (26.44, df 2) and cantilevered prostheses (5.25, df 1), and in Cox analysis only conventional versus immediate loading was significant (HR 0.00, 95% CI 0.000-0.509), a degenerate estimate the authors attribute to few events.

Timing is partly given: 3 of the 5 failed immediate-loading implants and 4 of the 5 failed screw-retained implants failed during the temporary prosthesis phase, but the text gives no overall early-versus-late split, no other effect sizes, and sex, age, smoking, diabetes, osteoporosis, hypertension, diameter, length and grafting were not associated with failure.

## 세줄요약

한국 원광대학교치과병원 단일기관 후향적 코호트 (환자의 국적·인종은 본문에 명시되지 않았고 기관 소재지로만 한국 환자임이 암시됨): 2010년 6월-2023년 6월 양쪽 이신경공(mental foramen) 사이 하악 전치부에 식립한 임플란트 604개 (환자 326명), 평균 관찰 44.38개월 (1일-163개월).

전체 누적 생존율 (cumulative survival rate) 97.68% (실패 14개, 실패 정의는 임플란트 소실 또는 제거, 분모는 임플란트 수); 로그순위 검정에서 즉시 부하 (immediate loading), 나사 유지형 보철, 캔틸레버 (cantilever) 보철의 생존율이 낮았고, Cox 분석에서는 즉시 부하 대비 통상 부하만 유의했으나 (HR 0.00, 95% CI 0.000-0.509) 사건 수 부족으로 불안정한 추정치이다.

즉시 부하 실패 5개 중 3개, 나사 유지형 실패 5개 중 4개가 임시 보철 단계에서 실패했으나 전체 조기-후기 실패 구분은 본문에 없고, 성별·나이·흡연·당뇨·골다공증·고혈압·직경·길이·골이식은 실패와 유의한 관련이 없었다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Korean single-center dataset restricted to the mandibular anterior region (between the mental foramina), including partially edentulous patients and five prosthesis types (single crown, fixed partial denture, hybrid denture, implant overdenture, implant-assisted removable partial denture).
- Overall survival 97.68% (14 failures of 604 implants), with prosthetic parameters (loading protocol, opposing dentition, prosthesis type, retention type, cantilever) analyzed by Kaplan-Meier and log-rank.
- Immediate loading, screw retention and cantilevered design had lower survival curves; conventional loading had lower failure hazard than immediate loading in Cox regression.
- Failure timing in part: 3 of 5 failed immediate-loading implants (60%) and 4 of the 5 failed screw-retained implants failed during the temporary prosthesis phase.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, single center; IRB WKDIRB202401-01.
- **Population**: 631 implants in 343 patients placed June 2010 - June 2023 (first 2010-06-01, last 2023-06-27) by oral and maxillofacial surgeons, periodontists and residents. Excluded: insufficient records, implants splinted to the posterior region, uncontrolled diabetes, head and neck radiotherapy. Final: 326 patients, 604 implants (all restored, at least one follow-up visit).
- **Failure definition**: implant loss or removal (criteria after Cochran et al.: persistent or irreversible mobility, pain or infection). Survival time = placement date to failure or last follow-up. Denominator is implants.
- **Loading protocol (after Weber et al.)**: conventional more than 2 months after placement; early 1 week to 2 months; immediate within 1 week.
- **Variables**: age, sex, implant diameter/length/manufacturer, smoking, systemic conditions (diabetes, osteoporosis, hypertension), grafting, loading protocol, occlusal material, opposing dentition, prosthesis type, retention type, cantilever, follow-up.
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier, log-rank across groups, Cox regression, SPSS 25, significance p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Implants / patients analyzed | 604 / 326 |
| Observation period | 1 day to 163 months; mean 44.38 months |
| Failures / overall cumulative survival | 14 / 97.68% |
| Loading protocol (log-rank) | immediate lower than conventional and early; chi-square 12.71, df 2, p<0.05 |
| Opposing dentition (log-rank) | not significant; chi-square 2.04, df 2, p>0.05 |
| Prosthesis type (log-rank) | not significant; chi-square 4.25, df 4, p>0.05 |
| Retention type (log-rank) | screw-retained lower than cement-retained and attachment; chi-square 26.44, df 2, p<0.05 |
| Cantilever (log-rank) | with cantilever lower; chi-square 5.25, df 1, p<0.05 |
| Cox: conventional vs immediate loading | HR 0.00, 95% CI 0.000-0.509 (significant) |

- Failure timing: of the 5 failed immediate-loading implants, 3 (60%) failed during the temporary prosthesis phase; 4 of the 5 failed screw-retained implants failed during the temporary restoration phase. The text gives no month windows and no overall early-versus-late (before versus after final prosthesis) split; per-implant failure details are in tables not present in the retrieved text.
- Not associated with failure in Cox analysis: sex, age, smoking, diabetes, osteoporosis, hypertension, bone grafting, implant diameter and length, prosthetic material, opposing dentition, prosthesis type, retention type, cantilever (authors cite small events per category). No failures occurred in patients with osteoporosis, or in those aged 30 and under or 31-50.
- Failures were most common with 3.4-4.0 mm diameter implants and in the 51-70 year group, but HRs were unstable (extreme values) because events were few.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Few events (14) spread over many categories; HR values are extreme or unstable (e.g. HR 0.00 for conventional versus immediate loading), so effect sizes should not be read quantitatively.
- Screw-retained group included a temporary implant restoration cylinder, so retention-type comparison is confounded by temporary-phase failures; 4 of the 5 screw-retained failures occurred in the temporary phase, suggesting impaired initial stability.
- Periodontal disease, oral hygiene and bone quality not recorded.
- Variable follow-up (mean 44.38 months); retrospective design, confounding by indication likely (loading choice, prosthesis design not randomized).
- In-paper inconsistency: the Discussion says survival curves differed by prosthesis type and rejects the first null hypothesis, whereas Results and Conclusion report no significant difference by prosthesis type (p>0.05); the significant differences were for loading, retention type and cantilever. Also, the Cox section reports only loading protocol as significant, so smoking, short implants and immediate placement were not confirmed as risk factors here.
- Authors recommend a larger sample and exclusion of temporary cylinders in future analyses.

## 7. Glossary

- **Cumulative survival rate (누적 생존율)**: Kaplan-Meier proportion of implants not removed over time.
- **Immediate / early / conventional loading (즉시 / 조기 / 통상 부하)**: prosthetic loading within 1 week / 1 week to 2 months / after more than 2 months.
- **Cantilever (캔틸레버)**: prosthesis extension beyond the last supporting implant.
- **Temporary prosthesis phase (임시 보철 단계)**: interval with provisional restoration before the final prosthesis.
- **HR (Hazard Ratio, 위험비)**: relative event rate in the Cox model.
