## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 12 prospective and retrospective clinical studies (search December 2006–December 2021) evaluating whether preventive antibiotic therapy reduces sinus graft infection or dental implant failure rates following maxillary sinus elevation surgeries.

The only retrospective study comparing antibiotic vs no antibiotic showed no significant difference for implant failure; the only RCT comparing antibiotic duration (surgery day only vs 7 additional postoperative days) found no significant difference in sinus infection rate between protocols.

Evidence is insufficient to support or oppose routine antibiotic prophylaxis for sinus elevation, nor to recommend any specific protocol over another.

## 세줄요약

12편 임상연구(전향적·후향적) SR: 상악동 거상(Maxillary Sinus Elevation) 수술 시 예방적 항생제 투여가 동(洞) 이식 감염 및 임플란트 실패를 줄이는지 평가 (검색기간 2006년 12월–2021년 12월).

항생제 사용 vs 미사용을 비교한 유일 후향적 연구에서 임플란트 실패 유의 차이 없음; 항생제 기간(수술 당일만 vs 7일 추가) 비교 유일 RCT에서 동 감염률 군 간 유의 차이 없음.

상악동 거상 시 예방적 항생제 사용 여부 및 최적 프로토콜에 관한 근거 불충분 — 사용 지지도, 불사용 지지도 불가.

- **Study type**: Systematic review (no meta-analysis possible); 12 studies

## 2. Key Contributions
- First systematic review specifically focused on antibiotic prophylaxis in sinus elevation procedures
- Covers both implant failure and sinus graft infection as outcomes
- Conclusion: insufficient evidence to establish any recommendation
- Identified key evidence gap: no RCT comparing antibiotic vs placebo for sinus elevation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Databases: MEDLINE (PubMed), Web of Science, Scopus, LILACS, OpenGrey
- Search period: December 2006 – December 2021
- Inclusion: prospective and retrospective comparative clinical studies, ≥50 patients, English
- Exclusion: animal studies, SRs/MAs, case reports, letters
- 12 studies met inclusion criteria
- Descriptive synthesis only (insufficient data for meta-analysis)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Antibiotic vs no antibiotic** (1 retrospective study): No statistically significant difference in implant failure; sinus infection data not reported
- **Short vs long antibiotic course** (1 RCT — surgery day only vs 7 postoperative days): No significant difference in sinus infection rate between groups
- **Quality of evidence**: Very limited — only 1 retrospective comparison and 1 RCT available

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No RCT directly comparing antibiotic vs placebo for sinus elevation procedures
- Only 12 studies met criteria; most did not have a comparator group
- Cannot recommend specific antibiotic type, dose, or duration
- Need for well-designed RCTs with adequate power and standardized protocols

## 6. Related Work and Context
- Addresses a common but under-studied clinical question in implant dentistry
- Aligns with broader antimicrobial stewardship trend: insufficient evidence to mandate prophylaxis

## 7. Glossary
- **Sinus elevation / Sinus floor augmentation**: Surgical procedure to increase vertical bone height in posterior maxilla for implant placement
- **Sinus graft infection**: Infection of the bone grafting material placed in the maxillary sinus
- **NNT**: Not calculable from available evidence in this review
