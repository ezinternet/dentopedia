## Why Ingested

Surveillance pick (geriatric-dentistry). It adds the first meta-analytic estimate (pooled OR = 2.05 for dry mouth) to the narrative claim in [[geriatric-dentistry/halasabalu-kalgeri-2026-polypharmacy-oral-health-elderly]] that medication count drives xerostomia, and complements [[geriatric-dentistry/kumar-2025-nutritional-status-dry-mouth-older-adults]] (dry mouth in older adults) and [[geriatric-dentistry/li-2024-prevalence-oral-frailty-older-adults]] (oral frailty; here polypharmacy was not associated with oral frailty in two studies).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD420251003698; MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane; final search 17 April 2025, updated 22 December 2025) of 23 studies (21 cross-sectional, 2 cohort; predominantly low risk of bias on the Newcastle-Ottawa Scale) on multimorbidity, polypharmacy and oral conditions in adults aged 75 years or older.

Pooled analysis of two studies showed polypharmacy (more than 5 medications) was significantly associated with dry mouth (OR = 2.05, 95% CI 1.31-3.22, I2 = 0%), and polypharmacy was consistently associated with poorer Oral Health Assessment Tool (OHAT) scores; multimorbidity meta-analyses were non-significant and heterogeneous (edentulism OR = 1.91, 95% CI 0.24-15.13, I2 = 86%; dry mouth OR = 1.87, 95% CI 0.50-7.00, I2 = 85%).

Dry mouth should be assessed before treatment planning and after new medications, but the polypharmacy-dry mouth pooled estimate rests on only two self-report studies, and evidence for teeth number, edentulism and oral function is inconsistent; no study evaluated management targeting multimorbidity or polypharmacy.

## 세줄요약

75세 이상 노인에서 다중이환 (Multimorbidity)과 다약제복용 (Polypharmacy)이 구강 상태와 맺는 연관성을 본 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA); 23편 (횡단 21편, 코호트 2편)을 포함했고 뉴캐슬-오타와 척도 (Newcastle-Ottawa Scale, NOS) 상 대부분 낮은 편향 위험.

2편을 합친 분석에서 다약제복용 (5종 초과)은 구강건조 (Dry mouth)와 유의한 연관 (OR = 2.05, 95% CI 1.31-3.22, I2 = 0%)을 보였고 구강건강평가도구 (Oral Health Assessment Tool, OHAT) 점수 악화와도 일관되게 연관; 다중이환 메타분석은 비유의적이고 이질성이 컸음 (무치악 OR = 1.91, 95% CI 0.24-15.13, I2 = 86%; 구강건조 OR = 1.87, 95% CI 0.50-7.00, I2 = 85%).

치료계획 전과 새 약물 시작 후 구강건조를 평가할 만하나, 합산 추정치는 자가보고 연구 2편에 불과하고 잔존치 수·무치악·구강기능 근거는 일관되지 않으며 다중이환·다약제복용 관리 중재를 평가한 연구는 없었음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled polypharmacy (more than 5 medications) vs dry mouth: OR = 2.05, 95% CI 1.31-3.22, I2 = 0% (2 studies, both self-reported dry mouth).
- Polypharmacy consistently associated with poorer OHAT scores; multimorbidity/comorbidity (CCI, FCI) not consistently associated with OHAT or dry mouth.
- Suggests polypharmacy effect on dry mouth and hyposalivation may be partly independent of multimorbidity.
- No study evaluated management of multimorbidity/polypharmacy to improve oral outcomes.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PECO question; MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Cochrane Library; search 17 April 2025, updated 22 December 2025; no filters.
- Inclusion: adults aged 75 or older (or mean age >= 75), >= 10 participants per group.
- 3433 records identified (PubMed 1944; Embase 1041; CENTRAL 448); 1118 duplicates removed; 2876 screened; 202 full texts; 185 excluded (age n = 105, design n = 49, reviews n = 18, outcomes n = 11, protocols n = 2); 23 included (17 from search, 6 from additional sources).
- Designs (section 3.2): 20 cross-sectional (abstract says 21 cross-sectional and 2 cohort), 2 prospective cohort (6 months, 3 years), 1 retrospective. Populations: 12 care-dependent, 10 community-dwelling, 1 mixed.
- Risk of bias: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale; RevMan 5.4.1; OR with 95% CI; heterogeneity by Tau2, Chi2, I2; funnel plots planned only if more than 10 studies.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Meta-analyses (4 studies total): multimorbidity vs edentulism OR = 1.91 (0.24-15.13), I2 = 86%; multimorbidity vs dry mouth OR = 1.87 (0.50-7.00), I2 = 85%; polypharmacy vs dry mouth OR = 2.05 (1.31-3.22), I2 = 0%.
- Islas-Granillo 2019: edentulism 55.3% with vs 32.7% without multimorbidity.
- Syrjala 2011: low salivary flow group averaged 6.5-7.4 medications vs 4.1-4.3 with normal flow.
- Discussion cites a dose-dependent pattern: 11 or more, 7-10, 4-6 medications gave 3.34, 2.07, 1.38 times the odds of dry mouth vs 0-3 medications (from an external study cited by the authors).
- Longitudinal studies (Tan 2020, 3 years, n = 30,995; Klotz 2020, 6 months, n = 114) found no association with extractions, restorations, DMF-T or OHAT; Tan reported opioid use associated with more extractions.
- Polypharmacy (6 or more medications) and more than 3 systemic diseases associated with oral candidiasis (Pedersen 2015).
- NOS: 18 of 21 cross-sectional studies low risk of bias; both cohorts moderate (score 5 each).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Mostly cross-sectional, limiting causal inference; about half of studies in institutionalised or hospitalised populations.
- Heterogeneous definitions of multimorbidity/polypharmacy limited pooling; meta-analyses had few studies (2 each) and substantial heterogeneity; publication bias not assessable.
- The polypharmacy-dry mouth pooling used self-reported dry mouth in two studies only.
- Future: longitudinal cohorts in adults 75 or older; identify drug combinations driving xerostomia.
- Note: the text states 20 cross-sectional studies in section 3.2 and 21 in the abstract and risk-of-bias section (internal inconsistency in the source).

## 7. Glossary

- Multimorbidity: two or more long-term chronic conditions (WHO definition cited).
- Polypharmacy: concurrent use of five or more medications; 10 or more is excessive polypharmacy.
- CCI / FCI: Charlson Comorbidity Index (17 conditions weighted by one-year mortality) / Functional Comorbidity Index (18 equally weighted conditions).
- OHAT: Oral Health Assessment Tool.
- PECO: Population, Exposure, Comparison, Outcome.
