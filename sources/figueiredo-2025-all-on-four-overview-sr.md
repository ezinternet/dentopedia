## Why Ingested

Existing wiki pages on All-on-Four report individual SR/MA findings, but no umbrella review synthesizing multiple SRs on this topic was present. This PROSPERO-registered overview of 8 SRs (2013–2021) using AMSTAR-2 + ROBIS is the highest evidence layer currently available for comparing All-on-4 to conventional full-arch approaches and directly addresses the gap in [[wiki/implants/full-arch/del-fabbro-2022-full-arch-tilted-axial-implants-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (overview of SRs) of 8 systematic reviews (4,468 records screened) evaluating the All-on-Four concept against other implant-supported full-arch rehabilitation approaches; AMSTAR-2 and ROBIS used for quality and bias assessment; PROSPERO-registered (CRD42023471446).

All-on-Four cumulative survival rate (CSR) ranged 94.8%–99.3%; mean marginal bone loss (MBL) ranged 1.1–1.5 mm; no included SR reported statistically significant inferiority of All-on-Four versus approaches using more implants.

Conclusion is limited by the evidence base: most included SRs were rated as low methodological quality on AMSTAR-2 and high risk of bias on ROBIS, so the overall evidence quality remains low.

## 세줄요약

누적 생존율(Cumulative Survival Rate, CSR) 94.8–99.3%·변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 1.1–1.5 mm를 보고한 8편의 체계적 문헌고찰(SR)을 포함한 산형 리뷰(umbrella review); 4,468건 스크리닝, AMSTAR-2·ROBIS로 질 평가.

포함된 8편 SR 중 어느 것도 All-on-Four가 더 많은 임플란트를 사용하는 접근법에 비해 통계적으로 유의미하게 열등하다고 보고하지 않음.

대부분의 포함 SR이 AMSTAR-2 기준 낮은 방법론적 질, ROBIS 기준 높은 비뚤림 위험을 가져 전체적인 근거 수준은 낮다는 한계가 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First PROSPERO-registered umbrella review specifically comparing All-on-Four against multi-implant full-arch approaches using formal quality appraisal (AMSTAR-2 + ROBIS).
- Provides the highest-level synthesis currently available for this comparison, aggregating 8 SRs spanning ~8 years of research.
- Establishes that no SR has demonstrated statistically significant inferiority of All-on-Four, while explicitly flagging the methodological weakness of the underlying evidence base.
- Identifies key outcomes (CSR 94.8–99.3%, MBL 1.1–1.5 mm) as the benchmark range for All-on-Four in systematic evidence.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Search strategy**: Comprehensive search across 10 databases (unspecified in abstract), including grey literature; two independent reviewers at all stages.
- **Inclusion**: Systematic reviews (with or without meta-analysis) reporting on All-on-Four versus other full-arch implant rehabilitation methods.
- **Outcomes of interest**: Number of implants, loading protocol, marginal bone loss (MBL), cumulative survival rate (CSR).
- **Quality assessment**: AMSTAR-2 (methodological quality of included SRs), ROBIS (risk of bias in systematic reviews).
- **Synthesis**: Narrative synthesis (no pooled meta-analysis across SRs, appropriate given heterogeneity).
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD42023471446 (prospective registration).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | All-on-Four range |
|---|---|
| Cumulative Survival Rate (CSR) | 94.8% – 99.3% |
| Mean Marginal Bone Loss (MBL) | 1.1 mm – 1.5 mm |
| SRs reporting AO4 inferiority | 0 of 8 |

- 8 SRs included, published between 2013 and 2021.
- No SR in the included pool reported statistically significant differences indicating All-on-Four is inferior to approaches using more implants.
- Most included SRs: high risk of bias (ROBIS) and low methodological quality (AMSTAR-2).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Underlying evidence quality is low**: Most of the 8 included SRs had high risk of bias and low methodological quality — the umbrella review cannot compensate for primary-level weaknesses.
- **Narrative synthesis only**: No pooled numerical comparison across SRs; heterogeneity in included SR definitions and outcomes precludes meta-analytic synthesis.
- **Included SRs up to 2021**: The umbrella search captured literature through 2021, so more recent SRs and RCTs are not represented.
- **Outcome heterogeneity**: Variability in how CSR and MBL were defined and measured across included SRs limits direct comparability of ranges.
- **Grey literature included**: While strengthening the search, grey literature inclusion may introduce unpublished or lower-quality sources.

## 7. Glossary

- **CSR** (Cumulative Survival Rate, 누적 생존율): proportion of implants or prostheses surviving to a defined follow-up endpoint.
- **MBL** (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골소실): radiographic loss of crestal bone around the implant.
- **AMSTAR-2**: A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews — 16-item quality appraisal checklist for SRs.
- **ROBIS**: Risk Of Bias In Systematic reviews — tool for assessing risk of bias in SRs across four domains.
- **PROSPERO**: International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews.
- **Umbrella review**: a systematic review of systematic reviews.
