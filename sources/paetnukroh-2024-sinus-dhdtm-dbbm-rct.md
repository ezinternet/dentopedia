## Why Ingested

No prior wiki page addresses dpDTM (deproteinized human Demineralized Tooth Matrix) as a graft material for lateral sinus floor augmentation. This RCT directly compares dpDTM against the DBBM benchmark used in [[sinus-lift/lateral/alkandari-2025-bcp-dbbm-sinus-elevation-sr-ma]], filling the evidence gap on human-derived tooth-matrix grafts for sinus procedures and adding 3D volumetric CBCT data that [[sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]] identifies as lacking.

## Three-line Summary

RCT (24 patients) compared volumetric bone changes and implant stability after lateral sinus augmentation with dpDTM vs DBBM and simultaneous implant placement.

Volumetric graft reduction was comparable (dpDTM 120.33 mm³ vs DBBM 108.51 mm³, p=0.690); ISQ values increased similarly to ~70 in both groups; mesial graft height reduction was greater in dpDTM (−1.08 mm vs −0.58 mm, p=0.04).

dpDTM showed comparable performance to DBBM in overall graft volume and implant stability, suggesting it as a viable alternative for sinus floor augmentation, though the 0.5 mm greater mesial height reduction warrants longer follow-up.

## 1. Document Information

- **Full title**: Three-dimensional volumetric assessment and stability of simultaneously placed implant following sinus floor augmentation with deproteinized human demineralized tooth matrix or deproteinized bovine bone mineral: a randomized controlled clinical trial
- **Authors**: Nattha Paetnukroh, Narit Leepong, Srisurang Suttapreyasri
- **Institution**: Prince of Songkla University, Thailand
- **Journal**: Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (Oral Maxillofac Surg), 28:1353–1363
- **Year**: 2024
- **DOI**: 10.1007/s10006-024-01263-w
- **Study type**: Randomized controlled trial (RCT)
- **N**: 24 patients (12 dpDTM, 12 DBBM)
- **Follow-up**: 6 months post-augmentation

## 2. Key Contributions

- First direct 3D volumetric CBCT comparison of dpDTM vs DBBM for lateral sinus floor augmentation with simultaneous implant placement
- Demonstrates comparable graft volumetric stability between a human tooth-derived (dpDTM) and a bovine-derived (DBBM) substitute
- Provides ISQ trajectory data for both materials from placement to 6 months
- Identifies a statistically significant but clinically uncertain difference in mesial graft height reduction (dpDTM > DBBM)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized controlled clinical trial; parallel-group; single center (Prince of Songkla University, Thailand)
- **Randomization**: Block randomization into dpDTM (n=12) or DBBM (n=12)
- **Procedure**: Lateral window sinus floor augmentation with simultaneous implant placement
- **Graft materials**:
  - dpDTM: deproteinized human demineralized tooth matrix (autologous/allogeneic human tooth-derived)
  - DBBM: deproteinized bovine bone mineral (Bio-Oss equivalent)
- **Imaging**: CBCT at immediate post-op and 6 months; 3D volumetric analysis of graft reduction
- **Stability**: Resonance frequency analysis (RFA) / ISQ at placement and 6 months
- **Graft height measurement**: Sagittal and coronal views at mesial, mid, and distal positions
- **Statistical analysis**: Paired and unpaired t-tests, Mann-Whitney U test; significance at p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | dpDTM | DBBM | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volumetric reduction (mm³) | 120.33 | 108.51 | 0.690 (NS) |
| ISQ at 6 months | ~70 | ~70 | NS |
| Mesial graft height reduction (mm) | −1.08 | −0.58 | 0.04* |
| Average height reduction range (mm) | −0.59 to −0.93 | −0.55 to −0.82 | >0.05 (NS) |

- Overall graft volume reduction was statistically comparable between groups
- ISQ values increased similarly in both groups across the 6-month follow-up
- Only mesial graft height showed a statistically significant difference (dpDTM > DBBM)
- No significant differences in mid or distal graft height reductions

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Short follow-up**: 6 months only; long-term remodeling and survival data absent
- **Small sample size**: n=24 (12 per group); underpowered for secondary outcomes
- **Single center**: Generalizability limited
- **No histologic evaluation**: Bone quality and maturation not assessed
- **Clinical significance uncertain**: The 0.5 mm mesial height difference between groups may not be clinically meaningful
- Future studies should include histomorphometry, longer follow-up (≥1 year), and larger multicenter cohorts

## 7. Glossary

- **dpDTM**: Deproteinized human Demineralized Tooth Matrix (탈단백화 인간 탈회 치아 기질) — human tooth-derived graft processed to remove organic protein and mineral components
- **DBBM**: Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral (탈단백화 우골 광물) — xenograft derived from bovine cancellous bone; Bio-Oss is the reference product
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient (임플란트 안정성 지수) — dimensionless value 0–100 from resonance frequency analysis; higher = greater stability
- **CBCT**: Cone-Beam Computed Tomography (콘빔 CT) — 3D imaging modality for volumetric graft assessment
- **Volumetric reduction**: Decrease in graft volume between immediate post-op and 6-month CBCT; proxy for graft resorption/remodeling
- **RFA**: Resonance Frequency Analysis (공명 주파수 분석) — non-invasive method for measuring implant stability via ISQ
