## Three-line Summary

Randomized double-blind RCT (n=77, 140 NCCLs, 6yr) comparing bulk-fill (Filtek Bulk Fill Posterior) vs nanofill (Filtek Z350 XT) in NCCLs with 1.5mm or 3mm OGD, all bonded with Clearfil SE Bond. Retention rates were 85.7–91.4% across all groups with no statistically significant differences (Log-rank p=0.961). Bulk-fill performs equivalently to nanofill at 6 years regardless of lesion extension (OGD).

## Study Design

- **Design**: Randomized, parallel, double-blind clinical trial
- **Registration**: NCT (implied by RCT reporting standards)
- **Participants**: 77 participants (19 dropouts: 2 died, 11 dropped, 6 uncontactable); recall rate 75.3%
- **Lesions**: 140 NCCLs stratified by occlusogingival distance (OGD)
- **Follow-up**: 6 years (range 66–94 months due to COVID-19 delays)

## Groups

| Group | OGD | Material | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.5mm-C | 1.5 mm | Nanofill (Filtek Z350 XT) | 35 |
| 1.5mm-B | 1.5 mm | Bulk-fill (Filtek Bulk Fill Posterior) | 35 |
| 3mm-C | 3.0 mm | Nanofill (Filtek Z350 XT) | 35 |
| 3mm-B | 3.0 mm | Bulk-fill (Filtek Bulk Fill Posterior) | 35 |

- **Adhesive (all groups)**: Clearfil SE Bond (2-step self-etch)

## Key Results

### Retention
- 1.5mm-C: 85.7%
- 1.5mm-B: 91.4%
- 3mm-C: 91.4%
- 3mm-B: 91.4%
- Kaplan-Meier Log-rank p = 0.961 (no significant differences)

### Secondary Outcomes (FDI criteria)
- **Marginal adaptation**: 1.5mm-B, 3mm-C, 3mm-B showed statistically significant increase in marginal discrepancy at 6yr (p<0.01); 1.5mm-C remained stable
- **Marginal staining**: 41.42% of restorations showed minor staining at 6yr; no significant group differences
- **Surface texture**: 72 restorations rated Bravo at 6yr; no significant group differences
- **Caries recurrence**: None observed at 6yr
- **Postoperative sensitivity**: None observed at 6yr
- **Anatomic form alteration**: None observed at 6yr

## Conclusions

Bulk-fill composite demonstrated similar clinical performance to nanofilled composite over 6 years in NCCL restorations. Occlusogingival distance (lesion extension) does not significantly influence the clinical performance of either material. Clearfil SE Bond combined with both materials showed acceptable long-term performance.

## Clinical Implications

- Bulk-fill (single-increment technique) can replace the incremental technique in NCCLs, simplifying the procedure and potentially saving chair time without compromising 6-year longevity.
- Lesion depth/extension (OGD 1.5mm vs 3mm) should not drive material selection based on clinical performance data.
- Minor marginal discrepancy increases in three of four groups warrant monitoring but do not compromise retention.
