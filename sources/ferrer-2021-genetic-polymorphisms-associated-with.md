## Three-line Summary

abstract-only — full text not retrieved

Systematic review (PRISMA, PubMed/ScienceDirect/Scopus, cutoff August 2019) of the association between **early implant failure** and genetic polymorphisms; 225 records screened → 24 full-text read → 10 met inclusion → 5 studies carried the polymorphism–EIF association.

Five studies reported associations for promoter-region variants of three matrix metalloproteinase (MMP) genes — **G-1607GG of MMP 1, C-799T of MMP 8, and -77 A>G of MMP 13** — which alter transcriptional activity, linking the effect to inflammation and extracellular-matrix degradation.

The authors themselves state the genetic axis is still exploratory: they frame establishing a polymorphism–EIF relationship as necessary groundwork for **precision medicine** rather than as an established risk prediction (no pooled effect estimate, no GRADE, and no gene other than MMP 1/8/13 named in the abstract).

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(PRISMA, PubMed/ScienceDirect/Scopus, 2019년 8월까지) — 조기 임플란트 실패(EIF)와 유전자 다형성의 연관성을 분석; 225편 검색 → 전문 24편 → 포함기준 충족 10편 → 다형성–EIF 연관을 보고한 5편 선정.

5편이 세 개 기질금속단백분해효소(matrix metalloproteinase, MMP) 유전자의 프로모터부 변이 연관을 보고 — **MMP 1의 G-1607GG, MMP 8의 C-799T, MMP 13의 -77 A>G** — 전사활성 변화를 통해 염증·세포외기질 분해에 영향.

저자 스스로 유전 축은 아직 탐색 단계라고 명시 — 통합 효과추정치도 GRADE도 없고, 초록에 명명된 유전자는 MMP 1/8/13뿐이며, 다형성–EIF 관계 규명을 치과 정밀의학(precision medicine)의 토대로 규정.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic framing of EIF as having a possible **intrinsic (genetic) component** — failures occurring under appropriate conditions in patients without local or systemic contraindications.
- Consolidates the sparse literature into **five studies** that link named promoter-region polymorphisms (MMP 1/8/13) to early implant failure.
- Proposes the mechanistic pathway: promoter variants → altered **transcriptional activity** → effects on **inflammation and extracellular-matrix degradation**.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: systematic review (no meta-analysis / no pooled estimate).
- **Guidelines**: PRISMA.
- **Databases**: PubMed, ScienceDirect, Scopus.
- **Search terms**: "Dental implant" AND "Polymorphism".
- **Cutoff date**: August 2019.
- **Risk of bias / quality**: risk of bias, methodologic quality, and heterogeneity of the included studies were analysed.
- **Screening flow**: 225 records → 24 full-text read → 10 met inclusion criteria → 5 studies chosen for the polymorphism–EIF association.
- **n**: 5 studies (reporting the specific polymorphism associations).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Genes/variants named in the abstract:

| Gene | Variant | Region |
|---|---|---|
| MMP 1 | G-1607GG | promoter |
| MMP 8 | C-799T | promoter |
| MMP 13 | -77 A>G | promoter |

- All three are **promoter-region** variants → alter cellular transcriptional activity → effects observed in **inflammation and extracellular-matrix degradation**.
- No pooled odds ratio/risk ratio, no confidence intervals, and no quantitative effect sizes are reported in the abstract.
- Evidence strength as stated by the authors: the relationship is a **necessary groundwork for precision medicine**, not an established predictive test.
- Search yielded 225 articles; 24 selected for full reading; 10 met inclusion; **5 studies** cited the polymorphism–EIF association.
- No other genes (e.g., IL-1, TNF-α, OPG/RANKL) are named in the abstract — only MMP 1, MMP 8, MMP 13.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only **five studies** underpinned the association — a very thin evidence base.
- No meta-analysis / no pooled effect size was performed; risk of bias and heterogeneity were assessed but not quantitatively synthesized in the abstract.
- Abstract-only retrieval — the list of the other five included-but-unassociated studies, the individual study designs/ORs, and the quality-appraisal details are not available.
- The authors position the field as pre-precision-medicine: relationship establishment is the current task, not clinical implementation.

## 6. Related Work

- scoffier-2026 (aetiology scoping review): classifies EIF aetiology into local, systemic (including **host genetic susceptibility**), and surgical domains — this paper fleshes out the genetic limb that scoffier names only in passing.
- yari-2023 / chrcanovic-2016 / manzano-2016: local, environmental, and systemic EIF risk-factor studies — the non-genetic counterpart evidence.

## 7. Glossary

- **Polymorphism**: a DNA sequence variant (here, in a gene promoter) present in the population.
- **MMP (matrix metalloproteinase)**: a family of zinc-dependent proteases that degrade extracellular matrix; MMP 1/8/13 are collagenases, implicated in inflammation and matrix turnover.
- **Promoter region**: the regulatory DNA stretch that controls how strongly a gene is transcribed.
- **Early implant failure (EIF)**: loss of the implant before osseointegration is complete / before prosthetic loading.
- **Precision medicine**: tailoring prevention/treatment to individual (here, genetic) characteristics.
