## Why Ingested

Foundational systematic review establishing the evidence base and uncertainty around thermal bone necrosis thresholds. The 47°C/1 min value used throughout implant literature is explicitly examined here against 25 animal studies (455 animals) and shown to be the lower bound of a range, not a single validated threshold. Provides the most rigorous multi-species in vivo synthesis available for this critical safety parameter.

→ [[implants/osteotomy-thermal/kniha-2020-temperature-threshold-bone-necrosis-sr]]

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (Materials 2020; PROSPERO CRD42019141867; MEDLINE/Cochrane/Embase/WoS through Nov 2019): 25 in vivo animal studies, 455 animals (224 rats, 117 rabbits, 70 emus, 41 sheep, 10 dogs), no meta-analysis due to heterogeneity.

Thermal bone necrosis threshold ranged **47–55°C for 1 min** across studies; no single clear threshold confirmed; Eriksson 1983's 47°C/1min is the lower bound; cancellous bone more susceptible than cortical bone; at 50°C/1min cancellous BIC significantly decreased while cortical BIC did not.

Clinical implication: the 47°C/1min safety limit used in practice reflects the lower bound of a broad range; the threshold is not a sharp boundary but a probabilistic zone, and bone quality (cancellous vs cortical) modulates the risk substantially.

## 세줄요약

체계적 고찰 (Systematic Review, SR) (Materials 2020; PROSPERO CRD42019141867; MEDLINE/Cochrane/Embase/WoS, 2019년 11월까지): 동물 대상 생체 내 연구 25편, 455마리(쥐 224, 토끼 117, 에뮤 70, 양 41, 개 10), 이질성으로 메타분석 미시행.

열 골괴사 (Thermal Osteonecrosis) 임계값은 연구마다 **47–55°C/1분** 범위로 분산 — 단일 명확한 임계값 없음; Eriksson 1983의 47°C/1분이 하한선; 해면골 (Cancellous Bone)이 피질골 (Cortical Bone)보다 열 손상에 더 취약; 50°C/1분에서 해면골 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 유의하게 감소, 피질골 BIC는 무의미.

임상적 의미: 현행 47°C/1분 기준은 광범위한 범위의 **하한선**이며, 뚜렷한 경계선이 아닌 확률적 위험 구간; 골 질 (해면골 vs 피질골) 에 따라 위험도 크게 달라짐.

## 4. Key Results

### Warm threshold
- 44°C/1 min: no bone damage
- 47°C/1 min: first tissue reactions (hyperemia) — Eriksson & Albrektsson 1983 (lower bound)
- 48°C/1 min: osteocyte apoptosis in calvarial study; 5 weeks to regain bone formation (Yoshida 2009)
- 48–50°C/1 min: likely heat boundary from most studies, consistent with Lundskog (50°C/30s)
- 50°C/1 min: bone resorption + dead osteocytes in some studies; cancellous BIC significantly decreased; cortical BIC unchanged
- 55°C/1 min: one study stated this as threshold (Rouiller 1953)
- 180–190°C for a few seconds: no bone necrosis (Arnoldi 2012) — time dominates duration-temperature product

### Cold threshold
- 3.5–1°C: histologically proven bone necrosis (max 0.7 mm), emu/cadaver model
- −10°C to −20°C: inevitable surrounding skin and bone necrosis

### BIC after thermal treatment (implant studies)
- 38.9°C/4 min: BIC 43.1% vs 45% control — no significant difference
- 50°C/1 min: cancellous BIC significantly decreased; cortical BIC unchanged
- 60°C/1 min: cancellous BIC 25.42% vs 38.05% (significant); cortical BIC 27.23% vs 31.94% (not significant)

### Infrabony pockets
- 60°C/1 min: cancellous pockets 3.11 SD 0.33 vs 1.21 SD 0.16 (significant); cortical 1.07 vs 0.56 (not significant)
- Bone quality (iliac crest > mandible) influenced pocket depth

## 5. Conclusions
No clear single threshold value. Range: **47–55°C/1 min** for warm stimuli. Cancellous > cortical susceptibility. 47°C/1 min remains the recommended upper limit for clinical practice (conservative lower-bound), but is insufficient as a sole safety guideline; bone type must be considered. Authors call for further preclinical work before clinical application of thermo-explantation.

## 6. Limitations
- High heterogeneity (species, bone sites, devices, measurement methods) — no meta-analysis possible
- Not all studies used thermocouples; infrared thermography captures surface only
- None of the studies are human in vivo — extrapolation to humans uncertain
- Laser threshold data: insufficient to define a clear threshold
