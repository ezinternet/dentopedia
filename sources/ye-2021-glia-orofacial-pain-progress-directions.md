## Why Ingested

Narrative review covering glial cell contributions to orofacial pain across four conditions — migraine/headache, TMD, dental pulp injury, and HNC pain. Provides the mechanistic framework (SGC connexin 43, p38MAPK, glutamine synthetase) that connects peripheral sensitization at the trigeminal ganglion to central sensitization, essential context for understanding why endodontic pain can persist beyond the pulp. See [[orofacial-pain/kim-2024-trigeminal-sensory-system-orofacial-pain]].

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of glial cell (Schwann cells, satellite glial cells, microglia, astrocytes) contributions to orofacial pain in four conditions: migraine/headache, TMD, dental pulp injury, and head and neck cancer pain. Peripheral glia (SGCs, Schwann cells) modulate trigeminal ganglion neuronal excitability via connexins, pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-1β, PGE2), and gap junctions; central glia (microglia, astrocytes) drive sensitization in the trigeminal nucleus. Key therapeutic targets include connexin 43, COX-2/PGE2 axis, p38MAPK in microglia, and glutamine synthetase in astrocytes.

## 세줄요약

신경계 외 비신경세포(슈반세포·위성신경절세포·미세아교세포·성상교세포)가 두통·TMD·치수손상·두경부암 통증에서 구강안면 통증을 증폭·지속시키는 기전을 종설. 말초의 SGC는 connexin 43·PGE2·IL-1β로 삼차신경절 뉴런을 감작시키며, 중추의 성상교세포·미세아교세포는 글루타민 합성효소·p38MAPK·TNF-α로 삼차신경핵 중추감작을 유지함. 치수 내 슈반세포는 손상 방어·줄기세포 저장소 역할도 하며, 교세포 기반 치료제(connexin 차단제·fluorocitrate·ibudilast)의 임상적 가능성을 논의.

## Key Findings

### Epidemiology
- Chronic orofacial pain affects ~22% of American adults
- Migraine affects >1 billion adults globally
- TMD afflicts ~33% of the population, peak 20–40 years old
- HNC: >650,000 cases and 330,000 deaths annually worldwide; up to 85% of patients report pain at diagnosis

### Glial Cell Types and Markers
- **PNS glia**: Satellite glial cells (SGCs) in trigeminal ganglia (TG), Schwann cells in peripheral nerve
- **CNS glia**: Microglia and astrocytes in trigeminal nucleus (TN/TNC)
- Key markers: S100B, GFAP (Schwann cells, SGCs, astrocytes); Iba1, CD11b/OX42 (microglia)

### Headache/Migraine
- SGC activation in TG: CGRP from neurons activates adjacent SGCs → cytokine/chemokine release
- Astrocytes: S100B elevated in serum in migraine patients; FHM2 mutations in Atp1a2 impair astrocyte Na+/K+ ATPase → reduced glutamate clearance → CSD facilitation
- Tonabersat (gap junction modulator, inhibits CSD and neuronal-SGC signaling) shows some clinical efficacy for migraine with aura
- Ibudilast (cAMP PDE inhibitor, glial modulator) did NOT improve chronic migraine or reduce opioid use in MOH — negative clinical trial

### TMD
- CFA injection into TMJ activates SGCs within hours: ↑GFAP, ↑connexin 43, ↑PGE2, ↑IL-1β, ↑Nav1.7
- P2Y2 receptor on SGCs potentiated by CGRP → downstream sensory hyperactivity; P2Y2 blockade reduces hyperalgesia
- Connexin 43 selective inhibition reduces CFA-induced GFAP, IL-1β, Nav1.7 upregulation and hyperalgesia
- Central: microglia (Iba1) activated at 24h, astrocytes (GFAP) consistently at >3 days post-CFA — astrocytes more important for persistence
- Astrocyte inhibitor L-α-aminoadipate reduces stress-induced hyperalgesia; minocycline (microglia inhibitor) ineffective in chronic stress model → astrocyte-specific role
- Non-CFA models (mouth opening, tooth movement, masseter ligation, chronic stress) also show peripheral + central glial activation

### Dental Pulp Injury
- Schwann cells in pulp: myelinating (root) and non-myelinating (coronal periphery, S100B+/GFAP+)
- Caries encroachment → expanded then degraded pulpal glial network; may protect pulp from bacteria; induces M2 macrophage phenotype
- Primary tooth exfoliation: Wallerian-like axon die-back + Schwann cell proliferation/dedifferentiation
- Pulpal Schwann cells = reservoir of multipotent mesenchymal stem cells → can differentiate into odontoblasts (dentin secretion)
- Dental pulp stem cells can also differentiate into Schwann cells → peripheral nerve regeneration potential
- TG after pulp injury: GFAP ↑ in SGCs adjacent to injured AND adjacent non-injured teeth → explains ectopic/diffuse tooth pain
- SGC-released IL-1β sensitizes neighboring neurons; connexin 43 mediates spread to uninjured TG neurons
- TNC after pulp injury: ↑GFAP (astrocytes), ↑glutamine synthetase activity → central sensitization; ↑Iba1 (microglia) ipsilateral; p38MAPK inhibitors block sensitization
- Persistent post-RCT pain may be mediated by TG/TN glial pathological changes

### Head and Neck Cancer (HNC)
- Walker 256B breast cancer model in rat vibrissa: microglia and astrocyte activation in ipsilateral TNC, but NOT SGC activation → CNS-predominant glial response
- Oral cancer cells in rat gingiva: SGC activation in TG ipsilateral to tumor
- Cancer microenvironment mediators activate/sensitize primary afferents contributing to cancer pain

## Methods / Scope
- Narrative review (not systematic, no formal risk-of-bias assessment)
- Covers molecular/cellular findings mostly from rodent models
- Four pain conditions: headache, TMD, dental pulp injury, HNC
- Behavioral phenotyping in orofacial region more challenging than hind limb models

## Therapeutic Targets Discussed
| Target | Condition | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Connexin 43 | TMD, pulp injury | Selective inhibition → reduced SGC activation |
| COX-2/PGE2 axis | TMD | COX-2 inhibitors → reduced Nav1.7, hyperalgesia |
| P2Y2 receptor | TMD | Pharmacological blockade → reduced hyperalgesia |
| p38MAPK | Headache, pulp injury | Inhibitors → reduced central sensitization |
| Glutamine synthetase | Pulp injury | Activity reduction → reduced central sensitization |
| L-α-aminoadipate | TMD | Astrocyte/SGC inhibitor (preclinical) |
| Fluorocitrate | TMD | SGC activation blocker → reduced Nav1.7 |
| Tonabersat | Headache (migraine with aura) | Gap junction modulator; some RCT efficacy |
| Ibudilast | Headache | Glial modulator; NO efficacy in chronic migraine RCT |
| Minocycline | TMD (stress model) | Microglia inhibitor; ineffective in astrocyte-dominant model |
