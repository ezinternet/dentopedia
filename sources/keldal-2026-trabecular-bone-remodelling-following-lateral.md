## Why Ingested

측방접근 상악동거상술 (Lateral Sinus Floor Elevation, LSFE)의 동종골 (Allograft) vs 이종골 (Xenograft) 비교는 위키에서 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry)·임플란트 생존율 축으로만 다뤄져 있었고, 파노라마 방사선 기반 프랙탈 차원 (Fractal Dimension, FD)이라는 비침습 대리지표로 1년 리모델링 궤적을 비교한 연구는 없었다. 본 논문은 [[sinus-lift/lateral/galindomoreno-2022-maxillary-sinus-floor-augmentation-comparing]] 가 보여준 "이식재 종류 간 조직학적 동등성"을 방사선 골소주 복잡도 축에서 재검토하고, [[sinus-lift/lateral/li-2023-dbbm-particle-size-sinus-floor-elevation]] 가 다룬 입자 크기(양 군 모두 0.5–1.0 mm)를 통제한 상태의 시간경과 데이터를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective comparative study (Tokat Gaziosmanpaşa University, Türkiye; 100 systemically healthy patients, 50 allograft vs 50 xenograft, both 0.5–1.0 mm particles, treated 2015–2023) measuring trabecular fractal dimension (FD) on panoramic radiographs by the White–Rudolph box-counting method immediately postoperatively and at 3 months, 6 months and 1 year after lateral window sinus augmentation.

FD changed significantly over time in both groups (allograft p=0.021; xenograft p<0.001), with allograft significantly higher immediately postoperatively (1.3005 vs 1.2505, p=0.010) and at 3 months (1.2705 vs 1.1949, p<0.001, Cohen's d=0.747), but no difference at 6 months (1.3061 vs 1.2900, p=0.107) or 1 year (1.3054 vs 1.3011, p=0.751).

Radiographic trabecular complexity converged by 6 months despite distinct early kinetics (xenograft dipped more steeply at 3 months and recovered faster), but FD is a texture surrogate only — with 2D panoramic imaging, JPEG storage, no histology and no implant or clinical outcomes, this is not evidence of biological or clinical equivalence.

## 세줄요약

후향적 비교 연구 (Tokat Gaziosmanpaşa University, 터키; 전신적으로 건강한 100명, 동종골 50명 vs 이종골 50명, 양 군 모두 0.5–1.0 mm 입자, 2015–2023년 치료): 측방접근 상악동거상술 (Lateral Sinus Floor Elevation, LSFE) 직후·3개월·6개월·1년 파노라마 방사선에서 White–Rudolph 박스계수법으로 프랙탈 차원 (Fractal Dimension, FD)을 측정.

FD는 양 군 모두 시간에 따라 유의하게 변했고 (동종골 p=0.021; 이종골 p<0.001), 동종골 군이 술후 직후 (1.3005 vs 1.2505, p=0.010)와 3개월 (1.2705 vs 1.1949, p<0.001, Cohen's d=0.747)에 유의하게 높았으나 6개월 (1.3061 vs 1.2900, p=0.107)과 1년 (1.3054 vs 1.3011, p=0.751)에는 차이가 없었다.

초기 리모델링 궤적은 달랐지만 (이종골이 3개월에 더 크게 하락 후 빠르게 회복) 6개월에 방사선적 골소주 복잡도가 수렴했으며, FD는 2차원 파노라마·JPEG·조직학 및 임상 결과 부재라는 한계를 가진 텍스처 대리지표이므로 생물학적·임상적 동등성의 근거로 볼 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First (within this wiki's sinus-lift collection) head-to-head comparison of **allograft vs xenograft** trabecular remodelling in lateral window sinus augmentation using **fractal dimension (FD)** on panoramic radiographs across four time points (postoperative, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year).
- Describes **distinct early remodelling kinetics**: allograft showed a shallow, statistically modest dip at 3 months (only 3-month vs 6-month significant after Bonferroni), whereas xenograft showed a deep, highly significant 3-month trough and recovered rapidly — yet both reached statistically indistinguishable FD by 6 months and 1 year.
- Provides **convergence evidence for a radiographic texture endpoint**: FD 1.3061 vs 1.2900 at 6 months (p=0.107) and 1.3054 vs 1.3011 at 1 year (p=0.751) — i.e. early between-material differences do not persist.
- Explicitly frames FD as an **indirect surrogate** of microarchitecture (not mineral density, porosity or elasticity) and warns that convergence in FD ≠ biological or clinical equivalence.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective screening of records 2015–2023 at a single centre; 600 sinus lift sites reviewed, 100 patients included and split by graft material (Group 1 allograft n=50; Group 2 xenograft n=50). Staged protocol — implants placed ~6 months after augmentation.
- **Graft materials**: Allograft — Atident® (Ankara, Türkiye), particle size 0.5–1.0 mm. Xenograft — Cerabone® (Botiss Biomaterials GmbH, Zossen, Germany), particle size 0.5–1.0 mm. Lateral window covered with a porcine-pericardium resorbable collagen membrane (Jason®, botiss) in all cases. No autogenous bone, platelet concentrates, hyaluronic acid or other bioactive additives.
- **Surgery**: Standardized lateral window protocol (crestal incision + two vertical releasing incisions, full-thickness flap, round bur window, sinus curettes for Schneiderian membrane elevation), local anesthesia 4% articaine with 1:100,000 epinephrine, 3-0 silk sutures; operators were departmental surgeons with ≥5 years experience, protocol unchanged across the study period.
- **Inclusion**: Age ≥18; residual bone height <5 mm posterior maxilla; allograft OR xenograft used exclusively; postoperative + 3-month + 6-month + 1-year panoramic radiographs available; no systemic disease/medication affecting bone metabolism.
- **Exclusion**: Missing/insufficient postoperative radiographs; poor-quality panoramas; smoking; diabetes mellitus or inadequate/unknown glycemic control; abnormal or unavailable vitamin D; alcohol consumption; parafunctional habits; systemic disease/bone-active medication; use of additional graft materials, platelet concentrates, hyaluronic acid, autogenous bone or other bioactive materials; intraoperative complications including Schneiderian membrane perforation.
- **Imaging**: Single digital panoramic unit (Veraviewepocs 2-D, J. Morita, Kyoto, Japan), 67 kVp, 5 mA, 7.4 s, automatic exposure by patient size, standardized patient positioning (Frankfort horizontal parallel to floor).
- **Fractal analysis**: ImageJ (NIH, Bethesda, USA), White & Rudolph box-counting method — Gaussian blur (σ=35 px), subtract from original, add gray value 128, binarize at threshold 128, sequential erode/dilate for noise, invert, skeletonize, then Fractal Box Count; FD = slope of the log–log plot of box count vs box size. ROI fixed at 50×50 px in the central augmented sinus region, anchored to fixed anatomical references (roots of bordering teeth in dentate patients, maxillary tuberosity in edentulous patients, vertical reference = native sinus floor) and repositioned by the identical landmark relationship on each serial radiograph. Native bone, Schneiderian membrane and radiopaque artifacts excluded from the ROI.
- **Blinding/reliability**: Two oral and maxillofacial surgeons (≥5 years experience) measured each image independently, blinded to graft type and time point; ICC(2,1), two-way random effects, absolute agreement.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 27.0; Shapiro–Wilk for normality; Friedman test for intragroup temporal change, followed by Wilcoxon signed-rank with Bonferroni correction (six pairwise comparisons → threshold p<0.0083); Mann–Whitney U or independent-samples t-test for intergroup comparisons depending on distribution, Cohen's d as effect size. Two-tailed p<0.05 for all other tests.
- **Sample size**: G*Power 3.1.9.7, based on prior sinus-augmentation FD data, assumed Cohen's d=0.6, α=0.05, power 80% → 45 patients/group minimum; inflated to 50/group for ~10% dropout.
- **Outcome**: FD (unitless fractal dimension) at four time points; primary comparison = between-material difference at each time point and within-material change over time.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Demographics**: age 56.10±9.38 (allograft) vs 57.60±9.97 years (xenograft), Mann–Whitney U=1083.500, Z=−1.149, p=0.250; sex M/F 31/19 (62.0%/38.0%) vs 36/14 (72.0%/28.0%), χ²=1.131, df=1, p=0.288; medians 57.50 (36–73) vs 60.00 (30–74).
- **FD descriptive values**:

|---|---|---|
| Postoperative | 1.3005±0.0897 (1.3227; 1.0203–1.4644) | 1.2505±0.1056 (1.2674; 1.0065–1.4716) |
| 3 months | 1.2705±0.0975 (1.2752; 1.0604–1.4737) | 1.1949±0.1046 (1.2231; 0.9034–1.4558) |
| 6 months | 1.3061±0.0903 (1.3342; 1.0569–1.4433) | 1.2900±0.0759 (1.3026; 1.0913–1.4265) |
| 1 year | 1.3054±0.0948 (1.3190; 0.9683–1.4448) | 1.3011±0.0909 (1.3146; 1.0226–1.4429) |

- **Intragroup over time**: allograft Friedman χ²=9.720, df=3, p=0.021; xenograft Friedman χ²=56.688, df=3, p<0.001. Bonferroni-corrected pairwise p-values — allograft: post vs 3 mo 0.029, post vs 6 mo 0.508, post vs 1 yr 0.881, 3 mo vs 6 mo **0.001**, 3 mo vs 1 yr 0.018, 6 mo vs 1 yr 0.534 (only 3 mo vs 6 mo passes p<0.0083). Xenograft: post vs 3 mo **<0.001**, post vs 6 mo **0.005**, post vs 1 yr **<0.001**, 3 mo vs 6 mo **<0.001**, 3 mo vs 1 yr **<0.001**, 6 mo vs 1 yr 0.025 (all significant except 6 mo vs 1 yr).
- **Intergroup**: postoperative allograft higher (1.3005 vs 1.2505, Mann–Whitney U, p=0.010); 3 months allograft higher (1.2705 vs 1.1949, independent-samples t-test, p<0.001, Cohen's d=0.747, 95% CI 0.340–1.151 — medium-to-large); 6 months p=0.107; 1 year p=0.751 (both Mann–Whitney U, non-significant).
- **Interobserver reliability**: allograft ICC 0.802–0.842 across all time points (95% CI 0.68–0.91); xenograft ICC 0.856 (postoperative), 0.804 (3 months), 0.744 (6 months; 95% CI 0.59–0.85, moderate), 0.794 (1 year).
- **Direction of the curve**: both groups dipped at 3 months then rose by 6 months; xenograft reached its lowest point at 3 months (1.1949, the lowest value in the study) and recovered to 1.3011 by 1 year, essentially matching allograft's 1.3054.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective design plus **two-dimensional panoramic radiography** — no volumetric information, superimposition of anatomical structures can reduce fractal sensitivity; the authors state this "inherently limits the strength of the conclusions."
- **No histological/histomorphometric validation** — FD findings are radiographic texture only.
- Images stored as **JPEG** rather than lossless TIFF; FD is reported to be relatively robust to lossy compression and all images were processed identically, but a minor effect on grayscale texture cannot be excluded.
- **ROI reproducibility** across serial panoramas is imperfect: despite anatomical anchoring, projection geometry, magnification and patient-positioning variability allow minor ROI shifts, and FD is sensitive to ROI position and acquisition parameters.
- Graft volume (cc) per patient not standardized; implant stability, insertion torque and postoperative complications not available from the retrospective records.
- Authors' call for prospective studies with larger samples, **CBCT-based fractal analysis and histomorphometric validation**, correlating FD with histological parameters.

## 6. Related Work

- Sancar et al. — fractal assessment of ossification after open sinus lift; progressive reduction in xenograft radiographic density between months 3 and 9 attributed to graft resorption before trabecular reorganization; the present xenograft curve partly agrees (lowest FD early) but recovers earlier, from 6 months.
- Molon et al. — increased FD six months after sinus augmentation, linked to new trabecular formation; pattern consistent with the present results.
- Heo et al. — biphasic FD after bilateral sagittal split osteotomy (initial decrease, then gradual increase on panoramic radiographs); Çolak et al. — early postoperative FD reduction at osteotomy and condylar regions with recovery by 6–12 months.
- Studies comparing autograft vs xenograft with mean gray values — density decreased in both groups at 6 months without a significant between-group difference.
- Prior work on grafted regions reporting lower FD than native bone; and reports of positive correlation between pre-implant FD and post-implant ISQ, and between osteoclast number and FD.
- Within this wiki: [[sinus-lift/lateral/galindomoreno-2022-maxillary-sinus-floor-augmentation-comparing]] (allograft-type vs xenograft histology at 6 months), [[sinus-lift/lateral/li-2023-dbbm-particle-size-sinus-floor-elevation]] (particle size within DBBM), [[sinus-lift/lateral/mordenfeld-2010-msfa-dpbb-biopsies-11year]] (DPBB persists as scaffold at 11 years), [[sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]] (graft-class equivalence for implant survival).

## 7. Glossary

- **FD (Fractal Dimension)**: unitless index of trabecular complexity derived from the slope of a log–log box-count/size regression; higher values = more complex/branching radiographic trabecular network, lower values = simpler or immature/resorbed structure.
- **Box-counting method**: grid-overlay technique in which the number of boxes containing trabecular structures is counted at progressively smaller box sizes.
- **White and Rudolph method**: standardized image-processing sequence for FD of jaw trabeculae (blur, subtract, binarize, erode/dilate, skeletonize) used here.
- **ROI (Region of Interest)**: here a fixed 50×50-pixel square in the central augmented sinus region, anchored to stable anatomical landmarks.
- **LSFE (Lateral Sinus Floor Elevation)**: sinus augmentation via a lateral antral wall window.
- **ICC (Intraclass Correlation Coefficient)**: reliability statistic; ICC(2,1) with absolute agreement used for interobserver agreement.
- **Cohen's d**: standardized mean difference effect size (0.2 small, 0.5 medium, 0.8 large).
- **Bonferroni correction**: alpha adjustment for multiple pairwise comparisons; here six comparisons → p<0.0083.
