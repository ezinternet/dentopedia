## Why Ingested
This split-mouth clinical study compares collagen plug (Collaplug) versus xenograft bovine bone (Bio-Oss) with cellulose mesh for alveolar ridge preservation, reporting wound healing and pain outcomes relevant to [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/saliba-2020-collagen-xenograft-bovine-bone-inserted]].

## Three-line Summary
Line 1: Split-mouth clinical study (n=13 patients, ≥2 extractions each) comparing collagen plug vs Bio-Oss + Surgicel mesh in extraction sockets.
Line 2: Significant difference in buccolingual wound closure (p=0.045) favoring collagen; pain NRS higher with Bio-Oss overall (p=0.397 over 7 days; p=0.004 on day 5).
Line 3: Collagen supported faster wound healing and decreased pain perception than xenograft bovine bone.

## 세줄요약
줄1: 분할구강 임상연구(n=13명, 환자당 발치치아 2개 이상) — 콜라겐 플러그(Collaplug) vs Bio-Oss + Surgicel 비교.
줄2: 협설측 창상 폐쇄에서 유의한 차이(p=0.045)로 콜라겐 우위; NRS 통증은 Bio-Oss 군에서 높았으나 7일간 유의차 없음(5일차 p=0.004).
줄3: 콜라겐이 이종골(Bio-Oss) 대비 빠른 창상 치유와 낮은 통증을 보였다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Split-mouth comparison of collagen plug vs xenograft + mesh for socket preservation
- Evaluated both soft tissue healing (wound closure) and pain perception

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Split-mouth clinical trial
- **Sample**: n=13, ≥2 extractions per patient
- **Materials**: Collaplug (bovine collagen type I) vs Bio-Oss + Surgicel
- **Outcomes**: Buccolingual/mesiodistal wound closure; NRS pain for 7 days
- **Follow-up**: Days 3, 7, 14

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Buccolingual closure: p=0.045 favoring collagen
- Mesiodistal closure: p=0.204
- Pain NRS: Bio-Oss higher (p=0.397 overall; p=0.004 day 5)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=13)
- Short-term follow-up (14 days), no long-term ridge dimensional data
- Subjective pain reporting
