## Why Ingested

gingival zenith 후속 서베일런스. 직전 인제스트한 [[implants/farronato-2020-implant-abutment-connection-buccal-zenith-rct]]가 "연결부 설계 → 협측 정점 연조직"을 1년 단면으로 본 반면, 이 RCT는 원뿔·평면·플랫폼 스위칭 3종 연결부의 **점막 정점 위치 변화를 5년** 추적했다. 정점 변화는 세 군 모두 미미해, 연결부가 정점 안정성의 주요 레버라는 해석을 제한한다. 원뿔 연결의 골 이점은 [[implants/mbl/stacchi-2023-marginal-bone-changes-platform-switched-conical]]와 같은 방향.

## Three-line Summary

Multicenter 5-year RCT of 141 subjects randomized to conical (conus, n=48), flat-to-flat (n=49), or platform-switched (n=44) implant–abutment interfaces for anterior maxillary single teeth in healed ridges with immediate provisionalization; 33/28/27 implants evaluable at 5 years.

Marginal bone change: conical −0.16 ± 0.45 mm vs flat-to-flat −0.92 ± 0.70 mm vs platform-switched −0.81 ± 1.06 mm (P<.0005); peri-implant mucosal zenith change was minimal in all groups (−0.10 to +0.08 mm, P>.60); BoP 16–19% with no difference; papillae changes positive and similar; 6 PS and 8 flat-to-flat implants failed, mostly within 3 months.

Conical interface preserved bone better, but zenith position stayed stable regardless of interface — mucosal stability did not track bone differences over 5 years.

변연골 변화: 원뿔 −0.16 ± 0.45 mm vs 평면 −0.92 ± 0.70 mm vs PS −0.81 ± 1.06 mm (P<.0005); 임플란트주위 점막 정점 (Mucosal Zenith) 변화는 전 군 미미 (−0.10~+0.08 mm, P>.60); 탐침시출혈 (BoP) 16–19% 무차이; 유두 변화 양(+)·유사; PS 6개·평면 8개 실패(대부분 3개월 내).

## 1. Document Information

## 2. Key Contributions

- Longest-held RCT comparing three implant–abutment interface designs on esthetic-zone mucosal zenith.
- Shows bone-level differences by interface without corresponding zenith differences.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 141 subjects; anterior maxillary single teeth; healed ridges; immediate provisionalization.
- Groups: conical (conus), flat-to-flat, platform-switched.
- Clinical, photographic, radiographic assessment at 6 months and annually to 5 years.
- Outcomes: marginal bone level change, mucosal zenith location, papilla lengths, plaque index, BoP.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Interface | Placed | Evaluated at 5 y | MBL change (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conical | 48 | 33 | −0.16 ± 0.45 (−1.55 to 0.65) |
| Flat-to-flat | 49 | 28 | −0.92 ± 0.70 (−2.90 to 0.20) |
| Platform-switched | 44 | 27 | −0.81 ± 1.06 (−3.35 to 1.35) |

- MBL difference P<.0005.
- Zenith change minimal (−0.10 and +0.08 mm reported), P>.60.
- BoP 16–19% of surfaces, P>.81.
- Failures: 6 PS, 8 flat-to-flat (mostly <3 months).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only.
- Heavy attrition (141 → 88 evaluable, ~38% loss), unequal by group.
- Interfaces belong to different implant systems; abutment height/profile not described in abstract.
- Abstract reports zenith change values as "0.10 mm and +0.08 mm" without assigning them to groups.

## 6. Related Work

- Earlier reports on comparable three-interface anterior RCT designs: 3-year (IJOMI 2019, PMID 30695089) and multicenter comparative trial (IJOMI 2015, PMID 26009913) — not held; relationship to this cohort not verified from the abstract.
- Farronato 2020 (connection angle and buccal-zenith mucosal dimensions).

## 7. Glossary

- **Conus interface**: conical implant–abutment connection.
- **Mucosal zenith change**: apicocoronal shift of the most apical point of the facial peri-implant mucosa over time.
