## Why Ingested

## Three-line Summary

- ADA/Evidence-Based Clinical Practice Guideline chairside distillation (©2024, FDA/HHS U01FD007151) for TEMPORARY pharmacologic management of toothache (symptomatic pulpitis, pulp necrosis with symptomatic apical periodontitis, or acute apical abscess) when definitive dental treatment is not immediately available.
- All recommendations are Conditional: in-visit short-acting local anesthetic (very low certainty); first-line post-visit NSAID alone or NSAID + acetaminophen, escalating by ADDING (Option 1) or REPLACING (Option 2) with acetaminophen-opioid combination at lowest dose/fewest tablets/duration rarely >3 days; acetaminophen-only pathway when NSAIDs contraindicated (low certainty); optional benzocaine 10–20% and supplemental long-acting anesthetics (bupivacaine/articaine).
- Scope gate dominates every page element: recommendations are a bridge only until pulpectomy, nonsurgical root canal treatment, incision for drainage, or extraction/referral within 2–3 days — opioids require informed consent, PDMP review, and extreme caution in adolescents aged 12–<17 years.

## 세줄요약

- ADA 근거기반 임상실무 지침(©2024, FDA/HHS U01FD007151)의 체어사이드 요약본으로, 확정 치과치료(pulpectomy·비수술적 근관치료·절개배농·발치)가 즉시 불가한 상황의 치통(증상성 치수염, 치수괴사+증상성 근첨치주염, 급성 근첨농양)의 **잠정 약리관리**를 다룬다.
- 권고는 전부 Conditional(조건부): 내원 중 단시간 작용 국소마취제(근거확신도 very low), 내원 후 1차는 NSAID 단독 또는 NSAID+아세트아미노펜, 불충분 시 옵션1은 추가(add)·옵션2는 교체(replace)로 아세트아미노펜-오피오이드 병합(최소유효용량·최소 정·3일 이내), NSAID 금기면 아세트아미노펜 경로(근거확신도 low), 벤조카인 10–20%와 장시간 작용 국소마취제(부피바카인·아티카인)는 선택 보조.
- 적용 범위 게이트가 모든 것을 지배한다: 2–3일 내 확정치료·의뢰 전까지의 다리(bridge)일 뿐이며, 오피오이드는 informed consent·PDMP 확인 필수, 12–<17세 청소년엔 오남용 위험으로 극도 주의(caregiver consent 권고).

## 2. Key Contributions

1. A one-page operational algorithm converting the guideline panel's recommendations into a chairside decision flow: definitive-treatment-available gate → in-visit relief → short-term adjunct → post-visit analgesic line of therapy → escalation branches.
2. Explicit dual first-line options with distinct escalation logic: Option 1 (NSAID alone) escalates by **ADDING** acetaminophen plus an acetaminophen-opioid combination tablet; Option 2 (NSAID + acetaminophen) escalates by **REPLACING** the initial prescription with NSAID + acetaminophen + acetaminophen-opioid combination.
3. A dedicated contraindicated branch (acetaminophen-only pathways) and safety rails: maximum daily doses, opioid prescribing limits (lowest effective dose, fewest tablets, rarely >3 days), no routine delayed just-in-case opioid prescriptions.
4. Seven good practice statements covering counseling, scope reminder, history review, minimum effective dosage, storage/disposal, PDMP review, and substance use disorder care.
5. GRADE interpretation tables for strength (Strong vs Conditional implications for patients/clinicians/policy makers) and certainty (High/Moderate/Low/Very Low).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Document architecture: numbered footnoted recommendations (scope note, toothache definition, age bands, max doses, co-prescribing exclusions, informed consent, cumulative-acetaminophen alert, contraindication definition per 2011 FDA guidance for industry, LA-as-complement note), a GRADE legend, the Guideline Panel Recommendations list (Recs 1–4 with sub-recommendations 2.1–2.4), Good Practice Statements, and a clinical pathway diagram.
- The parent guideline's methodology (GRADE — Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation) is inherited: each recommendation carries a strength (all are Conditional here) and a certainty-of-evidence rating (Low or Very Low).
- The pathway diagram encodes decision nodes: "No definitive dental treatment immediately available?" → implement temporary management vs definitive treatment; "Are NSAIDs contraindicated?" Yes/No branch; Option 1/Option 2 first-line columns with second-line escalation boxes.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Recommendations (all Conditional):

| # | Recommendation | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Short-acting local anesthetic for immediate pain relief (e.g., 2% lidocaine + 1:100,000 epinephrine or 4% articaine + 1:100,000 epinephrine) | Very low |
| 2 | Post-visit non-opioid analgesics as first-line therapy instead of opioid analgesics | Low |
| 2.1 | Initiate with NSAID alone (400 mg ibuprofen or 440 mg naproxen sodium) OR NSAID + acetaminophen (e.g., 500 mg) | Low |
| 2.2 | If NSAID alone inadequate: ADD 325 mg acetaminophen PLUS acetaminophen-opioid combination (e.g., 5–7.5 mg hydrocodone or 5 mg oxycodone); lowest effective dose, fewest tablets, shortest duration rarely exceeding three days | Low |
| 2.3 | If NSAID + acetaminophen inadequate: REPLACE initial prescription with NSAID + 325 mg acetaminophen + acetaminophen-opioid combination; same opioid limits | Low |
| 2.4 | When NSAIDs contraindicated: acetaminophen alone at full therapeutic dose (e.g., 1,000 mg) OR 325 mg acetaminophen + acetaminophen-opioid combination; same opioid limits | Low |
| 3 | Extended temporary management: supplemental 0.5% bupivacaine + 1:200,000 epinephrine by block or infiltration OR 4% articaine + 1:100,000/1:200,000 epinephrine infiltration | Very low |
| 4 | Short-term temporary management: topical benzocaine 10% or 20% vs no topical benzocaine | Low |

Safety constants across all branches: maximum daily doses 2,400 mg ibuprofen / 1,100 mg naproxen sodium / 4,000 mg acetaminophen; acetaminophen-opioid combination pills contain both drugs (total acetaminophen ≤4,000 mg/day); routine delayed just-in-case opioid prescriptions NOT recommended.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Every recommendation is Conditional with Low or Very Low certainty — direct high-certainty evidence for this temporary-management scenario is sparse; the panel leans on extrapolated analgesic trial data.
- Applicability restricted to the no-definitive-treatment window; the guide explicitly does not replace definitive care and instructs patients to call if pain fails to lessen or referral within 2–3 days is impossible.
- Opioid-containing branches carry population-level misuse risk (especially adolescents/young adults after even a single prescription), mitigated by consent, storage/disposal counseling, and PDMP review rather than eliminated.
- Combination-product dosing requires arithmetic vigilance (acetaminophen appears in both standalone and combination tablets).

## 7. Glossary

- **Definitive dental treatment**: pulpectomy, nonsurgical root canal treatment, incision for drainage of abscess, or tooth extraction.
- **Toothache (guideline definition)**: symptomatic pulpitis (reversible, or symptomatic irreversible with/without symptomatic apical periodontitis) OR pulp necrosis with symptomatic apical periodontitis OR acute apical abscess.
- **Temporary management**: pharmacologic strategies that temporarily alleviate pain until definitive treatment/referral is in place.
- **Delayed just-in-case prescription**: an opioid prescription given routinely "for breakthrough pain" — NOT recommended.
- **PDMP**: Prescription Drug Monitoring Program (state database of controlled-substance prescriptions).
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation — framework behind the Strength (Strong/Conditional) × Certainty (High/Moderate/Low/Very Low) ratings.
