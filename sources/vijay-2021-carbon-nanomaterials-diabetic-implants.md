## Why Ingested
Narrative review proposing carbon-nanomaterial-modified biomimetic implant surfaces (drug delivery + antibacterial + anti-inflammatory) as a materials-side solution to the osseointegration-failure burden of diabetes in implantology — the wiki currently holds the *clinical problem* side ([[implants/survival/james-2024-success-rates-of-dental-implants]] SR, [[implants/survival/andrade-2021-survival-rate-peri-implant-evaluation]] T2DM SR+MA) and point applications of surface treatment in diabetics ([[implants/surface/krishna-2025-uv-photofunctionalization-diabetic-implant-stability-rct]]), but no carbon-nanomaterial/biomimetic-surface strategy page. Extends the nanoparticle-coating coverage of [[implants/surface/rehner-2025-dental-implants-nanoparticle-coatings-review]] into carbon nanomaterials specifically.

## Three-line Summary
(L1) Narrative review (QUT, Nanomaterials 2021) of carbon-nanomaterial-modified biomimetic dental implant surfaces as a strategy to counter implant failure in diabetic patients — bioinspired nanopatterned surfaces, polydopamine coatings, and graphene/GO, carbon nanotubes, nanodiamonds, carbon dots.
(L2) CNM coatings offer three mechanisms: sustained local insulin/drug delivery via π-π stacking (10 U topical insulin safe without altering blood glucose), contact-mediated antibacterial action (e.g., dragonfly-wing nanopillars lysing E. coli), and anti-inflammatory/immunoregulatory effects (GO-coated titanium raised osteogenic marker gene expression).
(L3) Preclinical/in-vitro evidence only — no clinical trials; carbon nanomaterial safety, coating durability, and translation to diabetic patients remain unresolved, so clinical decisions cannot rest on this tier alone.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Frames dental implant failure in diabetes as a material-surface problem addressable by biomimetic, carbon-nanomaterial-functionalized implant coatings.
- Consolidates bioinspired antibacterial surfaces (cicada/dragonfly-wing nanopillar topography disrupting bacterial membranes) as a coating rationale for implants.
- Proposes CNM coatings as a triple-function platform: local insulin/drug delivery (π-π stacking, non-covalent), antibacterial, and anti-inflammatory/immunoregulatory.
- Reviews polydopamine (PDA) as a mussel-inspired universal binding layer that anchors graphene oxide, growth factors (bFGF, BMP-2), and drug carriers to titanium.
- Tabulated implant surface coatings (Table 2) that improve osseointegration across in-vitro/in-vivo studies (TNT, hydroxyapatite ± Si, chitosan-gold NP, laser-deposited TiO2 NP, Al2O3 NP, PDA, AuNP-miR204, calcium phosphate).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Narrative review (non-systematic)
- **Scope**: Carbon nanomaterial applications in dentistry; bioinspired/biomimetic implant surface strategies; implant failure in diabetes; topical insulin wound-healing evidence
- **Sources**: Non-systematic literature survey (animal + in-vitro studies primarily); key cited evidence includes Javed & Romanos 2009 (SR on diabetes/glycemic control & osseointegration), Naujokat SR 2016, Chrcanovic 2014 (diabetes & oral implant failure SR), Monje 2017 (SR+MA diabetes & peri-implant diseases)
- **Outcomes**: Narrative synthesis of mechanisms (drug delivery, antibacterial, anti-inflammatory) rather than pooled effect estimates

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Topical insulin 10 U safe without changing blood glucose; sustained-release insulin promoted wound healing by balancing inflammatory cytokines (Kaur et al., AgNP-sustained 50 µL insulin).
- Diabetes experimental model showed decreased bone-implant contact; restored by insulin therapy, which promoted osteoblastic matrix/osteoid comparable to controls.
- Malekzadeh et al.: insulin-coated titanium discs released insulin in controlled fashion, benefiting mineralization.
- TNT surface under diabetic (DM) conditions showed good biocompatibility, pro-osteogenic activity, lower ROS and higher total SOD (Yang et al.) — less hyperglycemia-driven osteogenesis inhibition.
- GO-coated titanium surface (Su et al.): increased osteogenic-marker gene expression + immunoregulatory effect on osteogenesis.
- AntagomiR204-conjugated gold NPs in PLGA restored miR204 misexpression and promoted osteogenesis in diabetic rats.
- Bioinspired nanopatterned surfaces (dragonfly-wing) caused mechanical rupture/lysis of E. coli (SEM-confirmed).
- No clinical trials of CNM-coated dental implants in diabetic patients reported.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Narrative review without systematic search or risk-of-bias assessment — mechanism-oriented, not an evidence synthesis.
- Evidence body is almost entirely in-vitro and animal; no clinical or RCT data on CNM-coated implants in diabetics.
- CNM safety, immunotoxicity risk, long-term coating durability/degradation, and sterilization implications are not addressed.
- No manufacturing/regulatory pathway or standardized evaluation of CNM implant coatings proposed.

## 7. Glossary
- **BIC**: Bone-to-Implant Contact (골-임플란트 접촉률) — histomorphometric measure of osseointegration; reduced in diabetes, restored with insulin.
- **CNM**: Carbon Nanomaterial (탄소나노물질) — graphene/graphene oxide, carbon nanotubes, nanodiamonds, carbon quantum dots/carbon dots.
- **GO**: Graphene Oxide (산화그래핀) — oxidized graphene used for coatings, drug/gene delivery, and osteoimmunomodulation.
- **PDA**: Polydopamine (폴리도파민) — mussel-inspired catechol/amine adhesive coating that anchors biomolecules and nanoparticles to titanium.
- **TNT**: Titania Nanotube (티타니아 나노튜브) — TiO2 nanotube surface with antioxidant/pro-osteogenic effect under high glucose.
- **π-π stacking**: Non-covalent aromatic stacking used to load drugs (e.g., insulin) onto carbon nanomaterials for local delivery.
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient (임플란트 안정성 지수) — RFA-based stability measure (related surface-treat-diabetic outcomes).
