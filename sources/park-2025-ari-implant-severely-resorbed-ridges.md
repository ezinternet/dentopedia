## Why Ingested
두 증례에서 ARi® 피질골 고정형 임플란트 (Basal Cortical Implant)가 심한 상악 전치부 치조제 흡수 부위에 사용되어 2년 추적에서 안정된 결과를 보고했다. 전치부 임플란트 주위 연조직 관리 전략([[implants/soft-tissue/wang-2024-mrarf-modified-roll-envelope-apf-implant]])을 보완하는 피질골 고정형 임플란트 디자인의 임상 적용 근거를 제공하며, 피질골 고정임에도 골이식의 필요성이 지속됨을 명시한다.

## Three-line Summary
Case report of 2 patients with severely resorbed anterior maxillary alveolar ridges treated with the ARi® basal cortical implant system (4.0–4.1 × 9.0–10.0 mm), combined with BCP bone grafting and collagen membranes in both cases; one case additionally received a VIP flap for soft tissue augmentation.
Both implants achieved a primary fixation torque of 40 N/cm² at placement; at the 2-year follow-up, peri-implant tissues were stable with satisfactory esthetic outcomes in both cases.
Basal cortical anchorage provides improved primary stability in severely resorbed sites but does not eliminate the need for bone augmentation in the anterior esthetic zone.

## 세줄요약
상악 전치부 심한 치조제 흡수 환자 2명에서 ARi® 피질골 고정형 임플란트 (Basal Cortical Implant) 4.0–4.1 × 9.0–10.0 mm를 이인산칼슘 (Biphasic Calcium Phosphate, BCP) 골이식재·콜라겐 차폐막 (Collagen Membrane)과 병용; 1증례에 혈관경 개재 골막 피판 (Vascularized Interpositional Periosteal flap, VIP flap) 추가.
초기 고정 토크 (Initial Fixation Torque) 40 N/cm²; 2년 추적 시 임플란트 주위 조직 (Peri-implant Tissue) 안정 및 심미 결과 양호.
피질골 고정은 흡수된 치조제에서 초기 안정성을 향상시키나, 전치부 심미 부위에서 골이식의 필요성을 완전히 제거하지 못함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First published clinical case documentation of the ARi® Implant System in severely resorbed anterior maxillary alveolar ridges
- Demonstrates that basal cortical anchorage achieves 40 N/cm² primary fixation torque in sites where cancellous bone volume is severely compromised
- Establishes that adjunctive BCP bone grafting + collagen membrane remains necessary even with basal cortical implants in the anterior esthetic zone
- VIP flap combined with BCP grafting shown as an effective composite augmentation strategy for anterior soft tissue volume and ridge contouring
- 2-year panoramic follow-up shows stable peri-implant bone without evidence of progressive marginal bone loss

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective case report (2 cases, 3 implants)
- **n**: 2 patients; 3 implants placed (2 ARi® implants + 1 conventional Blue Diamond implant as control in Case 1)
- **Implant (ARi®)**: MegaGen Implant Co., Ltd.; dimensions 4.0 × 9.0 mm and 4.1 × 10.0 mm; XPEED® nanostructured calcium-incorporating surface; Magic Cuff® abutment connection with micro-groove design
- **Augmentation**: BCP (Bone Matrix I, MegaGen) + collagen membrane in both cases; VIP flap rotation from palatal connective tissue in Case 1; Lyoplant collagen membrane (Aesculap AG) in Case 2
- **Follow-up**: 24 months; panoramic radiographs annually; 6-monthly clinical assessments
- **Outcomes**: Primary stability (insertion torque), peri-implant tissue health, esthetic outcome, radiographic bone level

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Case 1** (58M, heavy smoker): Missing #11 and #21 (3 months post-loss from periodontal disease); extraction of #22 planned. ARi® 4.0 × 9.0 mm placed at #22; Blue Diamond 4.1 × 10.0 mm at #11. Both achieved 40 N/cm² insertion torque. VIP flap + BCP + collagen membrane applied. At 2 years: stable peri-implant tissues; laminate veneer added to address residual esthetic concerns (papillary recession at lateral incisor).
- **Case 2** (50M, non-smoker): Missing #11 (5 months post-loss from periodontal disease). ARi® 4.1 × 10.0 mm at #11; 40 N/cm² insertion torque. BCP + Lyoplant collagen membrane applied. At 2 years: stable prosthesis; extended transmucosal pathway noted as hygiene challenge.
- Key biomechanical rationale: ARi® thread design engages basal cortical bone, distributing occlusal forces efficiently and potentially reducing crestal bone stress.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Very small sample (n=2): findings not generalizable
- No quantitative MBL measurements reported (panoramic assessment only — no calibrated measurements)
- Relatively short follow-up (2 years): long-term bone stability and implant survival unknown
- Significant conflict of interest: first three authors (Park K.-B., An H.-W., Park K.-O.) are affiliated with MegaGen Implant Co., Ltd., the manufacturer of ARi®
- Papillary recession management remains an unresolved esthetic challenge in the anterior region
- Extended transmucosal pathway in apically placed implants may complicate long-term oral hygiene
- Future multicenter RCTs comparing ARi® vs conventional implants ± GBR required to validate outcomes

## 7. Glossary
- **ARi® Implant**: Basal cortical implant system by MegaGen; threaded body designed for basal cortical bone anchorage; integrated XPEED® surface and Magic Cuff® connection
- **XPEED®**: Nanostructured, calcium-incorporating surface treatment; enhances early osteoblastic activity, accelerates apatite formation, and improves bone-to-implant contact (BIC)
- **Magic Cuff®**: Cylindrical intermediary abutment connection on ARi®; smooth surface analogous to machined collar; micro-groove for peri-implant soft tissue stability
- **VIP flap**: Vascularized interpositional periosteal flap — palatal connective tissue is incised and rotated to augment anterior labial soft tissue volume
- **BCP**: Biphasic calcium phosphate — synthetic bone graft substitute composed of hydroxyapatite and β-tricalcium phosphate; used for ridge contouring
- **Basal cortical bone**: Dense cortical bone apical to the alveolar crest; higher mineral density and resistance to resorption compared to cancellous alveolar bone; used for primary implant anchorage in severely resorbed ridges
