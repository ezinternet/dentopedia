## Three-line Summary

A systems-engineering review framing the oral cavity as a bioreactor to explain the microbial, metabolic, and mass-transport mechanisms that drive volatile sulfur compound production in halitosis.

## 세줄요약

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Bioreactor framework**: Formalizes the oral cavity as a dynamic bioreactor governed by microbial consortia, mass transport, enzymatic kinetics, and microenvironmental growth conditions.
2. **Biofilm modeling**: Addresses heterogeneous structural organization of oral biofilms and fundamental transport/metabolic processes at the biofilm-bulk fluid interface.
3. **VSC flux regulation**: Explains how interfacial processes regulate the flux of volatile compounds (VSC and other volatile organic compounds, VOC) into oral air.
4. **Dysbiosis link**: Explores associations between halitosis and gingivitis, periodontitis, and periodontal abscesses via microbial dysbiosis.
5. **Management overview**: Reviews contemporary strategies including mechanical debridement, antimicrobial agents, and probiotics.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Halitosis is predominantly intra-oral in origin; periodontal disease, tongue coating, and xerostomia are primary sources.
- Proteolytic anaerobes in tongue dorsum and periodontal pockets produce VSC via degradation of sulfur-containing substrates.
- Biofilms are structured; mass transport and enzymatic kinetics within the biofilm matrix determine VSC output.
- Modeling approaches are described as necessary to elucidate dynamics at biofilm interfaces.

## 7. Glossary

- **휘발성 황화합물 (Volatile Sulfur Compounds, VSC)**: H₂S, CH₃SH 등 구강 악취의 주요 원인 물질
- **생물반응기 (Bioreactor)**: 미생물이 기질을 대사산물로 전환하는 반응 용기; 이 논문에서는 구강을 이에 비유
- **설태 (Tongue Coating)**: 혀 등면의 미생물·상피세포·음식물 잔류물로 구성된 막
- **미생물 불균형 (Microbial Dysbiosis)**: 구강 미생물 군집의 병적 불균형
- **단백분해 혐기성균 (Proteolytic Anaerobes)**: 단백질을 분해하여 황화합물을 생성하는 혐기성 세균
