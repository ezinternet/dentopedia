## Why Ingested

[[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/tiskratok-2026-tooth-colored-abutment-peri-implant-soft-tissue-sr]] synthesize zirconia/PEEK vs titanium abutment soft-tissue responses over a broad 136-article (in-vitro + in-vivo) base, and [[wiki/dental-materials/zirconia/davoudi-2025-zirconia-abutments-biological-mechanical-esthetic]] umbrella-review the same comparison across biological/esthetic/mechanical domains. This 2015 SR/MA is ingested as the focused, controlled-clinical counterpart: it restricts to same-patient direct zirconia-vs-titanium RCT/CCT comparisons and is the source that isolates soft-tissue color as the only outcome quantitatively poolable, with an explicit "no obvious advantage of either material" bottom line.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 9 studies (11 papers: 4 randomized clinical trials and 5 prospective controlled clinical trials) comparing zirconia (Zr) vs titanium (Ti) abutments directly within the same patient, identified by electronic and hand searches of English-language journals through December 1, 2014.

Meta-analysis was feasible only for soft tissue color, which was significantly superior for Zr abutments; no statistically significant Zr-vs-Ti differences were found for soft tissue recession, probing depths, bleeding on probing, marginal bone level, or patient-reported outcome, and one study reported significantly higher pink esthetic score (PES) at Zr implants with Zr abutments.

The authors conclude that the research does not support any obvious advantage of Ti or Zr abutments over each other, while noting a significant tendency for Zr abutments to evoke a better color response of peri-implant mucosa and superior PES esthetics — the retrieved abstract reports no numeric effect sizes or p-values.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석(Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR/MA) — 2014년 12월 1일까지 영문 학술지에 대한 전자·수기 검색으로 찾아낸 9개 연구(11편 논문: 무작위 대조 시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 4편 + 전향적 대조 임상시험(Controlled Clinical Trial, CCT) 5편)에서 같은 환자 내 지르코니아(Zirconia, Zr)·티타늄(Titanium, Ti) 지대주(abutment)를 직접 비교.

메타분석은 연조직 색상에만 가능했으며 Zr 지대주가 유의하게 우수; 연조직 퇴축·탐침깊이(Probing Depth, PD)·탐침 시 출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP)·변연골수준(Marginal Bone Level, MBL)·환자 보고결과(Patient-Reported Outcome, PRO)에서는 유의한 차이 없음, 1편의 연구에서는 Zr 임플란트에 Zr 지대주가 금속 임플란트·Ti 지대주보다 분홍심미점수(Pink Esthetic Score, PES)가 유의하게 높음.

임상 함의: 어느 소재가 명백히 우위라고 볼 근거는 없으나 Zr 지대주가 임플란트 주위 점막 색상 반응과 PES 심미 결과에서 더 좋을 경향이 유의하다는 결론 — 인용된 초록에는 수치적 효과크기·p값이 제시되지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Restricts eligibility to studies that directly compared Zr and Ti abutments **in the same patient**, removing between-patient confounding from the material comparison.
- Reports that, among eight pre-specified outcome measures, only soft tissue color was homogeneous enough for a meta-analysis — and that this single pooled outcome significantly favored Zr.
- Provides a qualitative (non-pooled) across-outcome verdict of equivalence for recession, probing depth, bleeding on probing, marginal bone level, and patient-reported outcome.
- Flags the esthetic signal beyond color: one study's significantly higher PES for Zr abutments on Zr implants versus metal implants with Ti abutments.
- Concludes with a deliberately restrained bottom line — no obvious advantage of either material overall — while acknowledging a significant Zr tendency for mucosal color and PES esthetics.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: systematic review with meta-analysis where feasible; qualitative synthesis otherwise.
- **Search**: electronic and hand searches of English-language journals, through December 1, 2014.
- **Inclusion**: only randomized clinical trials (RCTs) and prospective controlled clinical trials (CCTs) showing a direct comparison between Zr and Ti abutments in the same patient.
- **n**: 9 relevant studies reported across 11 papers — 4 RCTs and 5 CCTs.
- **Outcome measures (8)**: (1) soft tissue color; (2) soft tissue recession; (3) peri-implant probing; (4) bleeding on probing; (5) esthetic indexes; (6) patient-reported outcome; (7) marginal bone level; (8) biological complications.
- **Analysis**: meta-analysis applied only to soft tissue color; heterogeneity in study design, statistical methods, and reported results precluded pooling the remaining outcomes, which were analyzed qualitatively.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only artifact**: the retrieved `.txt` is a PubMed abstract (no PMC record, no PDF); no numeric effect estimates, confidence intervals, p-values, I² statistics, or risk-of-bias appraisal are available for the color meta-analysis, so the "significantly superior" claim cannot be quantified here.
- Pooling was feasible for a single outcome only; the other seven outcomes rest on qualitative synthesis across designs that the authors themselves judged heterogeneous in study design, statistical methods, and reported results.
- Search was limited to English-language journals through December 1, 2014, so later evidence (e.g., the 2023 umbrella review and 2026 SR already held in the wiki) is not reflected in this review's conclusion.
- The within-patient direct Zr-vs-Ti abutment inclusion criterion improves internal validity but narrows the evidence base to 9 studies; the PES-esthetic signal comes from a single study and mixes implant material (Zr vs metal implant) with abutment material.
- The abstract gives no detailed account of abutment design, mucosal thickness, follow-up duration, or restoration type, all of which can modify soft-tissue color; these cannot be assessed from the retrieved artifact.

## 6. Related Work

- Tiskratok 2026 ([[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/tiskratok-2026-tooth-colored-abutment-peri-implant-soft-tissue-sr]]): 136-article SR on tooth-colored abutments (mainly Zr and PEEK) vs Ti, spanning in-vitro and in-vivo evidence; shares the Zr-favorable soft-tissue direction but is much broader than this review's controlled clinical head-to-heads.
- Davoudi 2025 ([[wiki/dental-materials/zirconia/davoudi-2025-zirconia-abutments-biological-mechanical-esthetic]]): umbrella review of 14 SR/MAs through March 2023; similarly finds biological parameters comparable and esthetic indices trending favorable for Zr, while extending the comparison to mechanical complications and survival.
- Humm 2023 ([[wiki/prosthetic-materials/humm-2023-zirconia-titanium-abutments-13year-rct]]): 13-year RCT follow-up finding 100% technical survival and comparable biologic/esthetic outcomes for Zr vs Ti abutments — consistent with this review's no-difference verdict outside color.
- Canullo 2020 ([[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/canullo-2020-titanium-abutment-surface-peri-implant-tissue-ma]]): MA on titanium abutment surface characteristics and peri-implant tissue — a different modifier (surface) of the same tissue response addressed here by material.

## 7. Glossary

- **Zr (Zirconia, 지르코니아)**: white ceramic abutment material; in this review associated with significantly better soft tissue color and a PES esthetic trend.
- **Ti (Titanium, 티타늄)**: metallic abutment material serving as the comparator throughout the review.
- **RCT (Randomized Controlled Trial, 무작위 대조 시험)**: one of the two eligible designs (4 of 9 studies).
- **CCT (Controlled Clinical Trial, 대조 임상시험)**: prospective controlled design; the other eligible design (5 of 9 studies).
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score, 분홍심미점수)**: esthetic index for peri-implant soft tissue; significantly higher for Zr abutments in one included study.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level, 변연골수준)**: radiographic bone level around the implant; no significant Zr-vs-Ti difference in this review.
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing, 탐침 시 출혈)**: soft-tissue inflammation measure; no significant Zr-vs-Ti difference.
- **PRO (Patient-Reported Outcome, 환자 보고결과)**: one of the eight pre-specified outcomes; no significant Zr-vs-Ti difference.
- **Heterogeneity (이질성)**: variability in study design, statistical methods, and reported results that limited meta-analysis to the color outcome.
