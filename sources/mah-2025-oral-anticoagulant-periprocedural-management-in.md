## Why Ingested

Prospective national observational survey (PRADCO, NCT 03150303) of 523 anticoagulated patients (345 DOAC, 178 VKA) undergoing invasive oral procedures with 30-day follow-up; provides washout/interruption durations and bleeding outcomes by drug class. → [[drug/anticoagulants/mah-2025-oral-anticoagulant-periprocedural-management-in]]

## Three-line Summary

Prospective noninterventional national study (n=523, mean age 74) of long-term oral anticoagulant patients undergoing invasive oral surgery with 30-day follow-up; no thromboembolic events occurred, and all 62 bleedings were local and managed with local hemostasis.
Bleeding incidence was 3-fold higher when DOACs were continued vs VKAs continued (15.97 vs 5.92 events per 100 person-months, P=.003), whereas short DOAC discontinuation (median 2 days) yielded bleeding rates comparable to continued VKAs (6.25 vs 5.92, P=.746).
Results support brief preoperative DOAC interruption (last dose the morning before the procedure) for invasive oral procedures.

## 세줄요약

전국적 전향적 관찰 연구(평균 74세, n=523)로 구강 외과 시술을 받는 장기 경구 항응고제 환자를 30일 추적; 혈전색전증은 없었고 62건 출혈 모두 국소 출혈로 국소 지혈로 관리됨.
DOAC 지속 시 출혈 발생률은 VKA 지속 대비 3배 높았으나(15.97 vs 5.92/100 person-month, P=.003), DOAC 단기 중단(중앙값 2일)은 VKA 지속과 유사한 출혈률을 보였음(6.25 vs 5.92, P=.746).
침습적 구강 시술에서는 DOAC을 시술 전날 아침 마지막 복용 후 단기간 중단하는 것이 권장됨.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Oral anticoagulant periprocedural management in patients undergoing an oral, dental implant or periodontal surgery: a prospective national observational survey.
- **Journal**: Research and Practice in Thrombosis and Haemostasis, 2025.
- **Design**: Prospective, noninterventional, national, multicenter cohort (PRADCO, NCT 03150303), 25 sites in France, July 2017–Dec 2019.
- **Sample**: 523 patients (345 DOAC, 178 VKA); mean age 73.8; recruitment stopped early for funding reasons (target 2000).
- **Follow-up**: 30 days (periprocedural period = 5 days pre- to 30 days post-procedure).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First prospective study dedicated to periprocedural anticoagulant management in invasive oral procedures with 30-day follow-up.
- No thromboembolic events recorded; all bleedings local, managed by dentist.
- DOAC continuation associated with 3-fold higher 30-day bleeding risk vs VKA continuation (OR 3.13 for all bleedings).
- Short DOAC discontinuation (median 2.0 days) yielded bleeding rates comparable to continued VKAs (6.25 vs 5.92 events/100 PM, P=.746).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Inclusion: adults ≥18 on long-term oral anticoagulants (≥1 month), referred for invasive oral procedure (extractions, implants, bone grafting, sinus lift, mucosal/bone surgery).
- Anticoagulants: VKAs (acenocoumarol, fluindione, warfarin); DOACs (dabigatran, rivaroxaban, apixaban, edoxaban).
- Discontinuation definition: VKA withdrawal ≥3 days pre-procedure (no bridging); DOAC withdrawal ≥1 day pre-procedure (last dose no later than morning before procedure).
- Continuation categories: usual dose; VKA with LMWH bridging; VKA temporary dose lowering; DOAC switched to LMWH.
- Outcomes adjudicated by blinded Clinical Events Committee; bleeding classified by ISTH (major, CRNMB, minor).
- Procedures categorized low/high bleeding risk per SFCO classification.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Global bleeding incidence: 11.85 (95% CI 9.24–15.21) events/100 PM; 9.56% patients with ≥1 bleeding within 30 days.
- 62 bleedings in 50 patients: 43 (69.4%) CRNMB, 19 (30.6%) minor; no major bleeding.
- Timing: ~30.6% within 24h, 59.7% days 2–7, 9.7% days 7–30.
- DOAC vs VKA overall: 15.07 vs 5.62 events/100 PM (P=.003).
- DOAC continuation: 15.97 events/100 PM vs VKA continuation: 5.92 (P=.003); 12.78% vs 4.73% patients (P=.005).
- Interrupted DOAC: 6.25 events/100 PM; interrupted VKA: 0 events.
- DOAC discontinuation vs VKA continuation: 6.25 vs 5.92 events/100 PM (P=.746); CRNMB 6.25 vs 4.14 (P=.95).
- Median washout: DOAC 2.00 (2.00; 2.00) days; VKA 3.00 (2.00; 4.00) days (P=.008).
- Multivariable: DOAC continuation OR 3.13 (1.43–6.89) for all bleedings; DOAC discontinuation OR 1.15 (0.23–5.80). Male gender associated with higher bleeding (P=.02).
- No significant difference in bleeding risk between high-risk and low-risk oral procedures.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Recruitment stopped early (523 vs planned 2000); few discontinuations → wide CIs.
- No thromboembolic events observed → could not assess TE risk factors.
- Possible underreporting of minor bleeding.
- Observational, non-randomized; anticoagulant management left to dentist discretion.
- Industry funding (Bristol Myers Squibb, Daiichi Sankyo) unrestricted grant.

## 6. Related Work

- Compared with Brennan et al. (continued DOACs vs warfarin, similar bleeding) — present study found 3-fold higher risk with continued DOACs.
- Consistent with PAUSE study and GIHP group: omit DOAC day before procedure.
- References systematic reviews/meta-analyses on dental extractions under DOACs/VKAs.

## 7. Glossary

- **DOAC**: Direct Oral Anticoagulant — dabigatran, rivaroxaban, apixaban, edoxaban.
- **VKA**: Vitamin K Antagonist — acenocoumarol, fluindione, warfarin.
- **CRNMB**: Clinically Relevant Non-Major Bleeding — ISTH classification; bleeding requiring medical intervention or unscheduled physician contact.
- **LMWH**: Low-Molecular-Weight Heparin — used for bridging.
- **PM**: Person-Month — incidence rate denominator.
