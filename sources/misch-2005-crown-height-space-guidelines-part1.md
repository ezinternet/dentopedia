## Three-line Summary

Consensus conference panel report (ICOI, Las Vegas, Jun 2004; multi-author expert panel; general guidelines but no single opinion on most issues) — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

Part 1 summarizes the first set of general guidelines on crown height space from the 2004 ICOI conference.

Treat as expert opinion; the abstract gives no numeric thresholds and the panel did not reach consensus on most items.
