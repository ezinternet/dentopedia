## Why Ingested

Held pages treat CBCT in cracked-tooth work only as a selective, last-resort aid ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2025-position-statement-longitudinal-cracks-fractures]]) or as a tool that cannot reliably show the fracture line itself ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]]). This radiology-department review is the only held paper organised around CBCT for all five AAE cracked-tooth types and the device-, patient- and evaluator-related factors that change diagnostic accuracy, so it reinforces the CBCT-limitation statements and adds the imaging-side detail the diagnostic SR ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/hernando-2026-diagnostic-methods-dental-cracks-systematic-review]]) lacks. Surveillance ingest (PMC record, abstract only).

## Three-line Summary

Narrative literature review plus clinical case experience (Nanjing Stomatological Hospital radiology department) on CBCT diagnosis of the five American Association of Endodontists (AAE) cracked-tooth types, of which vertical root fracture (VRF) is one; no n or quantitative pooling is reported in the abstract.

Because cracks and fractures vary and overlap, some narrow VRF root fractures cannot be seen on CBCT while some wide crown cracks can; diagnosis is influenced by CBCT device-related, patient-related and evaluator-related factors, and the review also contrasts root fractures with lateral canals and with external root resorption on CBCT images.

CBCT is useful but not definitive for crack/fracture diagnosis; strategies targeting device settings, patient factors and evaluator training can improve accuracy, but the abstract gives no sensitivity/specificity figures (abstract-only record).

## 세줄요약

내러티브 문헌고찰 + 임상 경험(난징구강병원 영상의학과)으로, 미국근관치료학회 (American Association of Endodontists, AAE) 분류 균열치 5유형(수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 포함)에서 콘빔 CT (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT)의 진단 가치를 다룸; 초록에는 n이나 정량 통합 수치가 없음.

균열·파절의 변이와 중첩 때문에 VRF의 좁은 치근 파절선은 CBCT로 안 보일 수 있고 반대로 넓은 치관부 균열은 보일 수 있음; 진단은 장치 요인·환자 요인·판독자 요인의 영향을 받으며, 측방 근관 및 외흡수 (External Root Resorption)와 치근 파절의 CBCT 감별점도 비교함.

CBCT는 유용하지만 확정적 도구는 아니며, 장치 설정·환자 요인·판독자 교육으로 정확도를 높일 수 있으나 초록에는 민감도/특이도 수치가 없음 (초록만 확보).

## 7. Glossary
- **AAE five types**: American Association of Endodontists cracked-tooth classification (craze line, fractured cusp, cracked tooth, split tooth, vertical root fracture).
- **VRF**: vertical root fracture, a longitudinal fracture beginning in the root.
- **CBCT**: cone-beam computed tomography, 3D dental imaging.
- **Lateral canal**: accessory canal that can mimic a root fracture line on CBCT.
- **External root resorption**: loss of root surface that can mimic a fracture/bone defect on CBCT.
