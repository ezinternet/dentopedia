## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (Materials 2021;14:3920) evaluating chemical bonding stability to cubic 5Y-PSZ zirconia using MDP primer, silane, and tribochemical silica coating (CoJet) combinations with aging (water storage and thermocycling).

MDP primer alone significantly improved bond strength over untreated controls; CoJet tribochemical silica coating + silane + MDP achieved the highest bond-strength stability at 6 months; aging degraded bonds more in non-combined treatments.

Cubic (5Y-PSZ) zirconia responds to chemical bonding strategies similarly to tetragonal zirconia, supporting the use of combined surface-treatment protocols to maximize durable adhesion.

## 세줄요약

인비트로 연구(Materials 2021;14:3920): 고투명 입방형(cubic, 5Y-PSZ) 지르코니아에 MDP 프라이머·실란·CoJet 트리보케미컬 실리카 코팅 조합의 접착강도 안정성을 노화 처리(수분 저장·온도순환) 후 평가.

MDP 프라이머 단독도 대조군 대비 접착강도 유의 향상; CoJet + 실란 + MDP 조합이 6개월 후 최고 안정성; 단독 처리군은 노화 시 접착 저하 더 심함.

입방형 5Y-PSZ도 정방형 지르코니아와 유사하게 화학 접착에 반응 — 복합 표면처리 프로토콜을 통해 내구성 있는 접착 확보 가능.

## 1. Key Results
- Cubic 5Y-PSZ zirconia tested (high-translucency type)
- Chemical treatments: MDP primer, silane, combination with tribochemical silica coating
- CoJet + silane + MDP: highest bond strength stability at 6 months
- MDP primer alone: significant improvement over untreated
- Aging (water storage, thermocycling): bond degradation greater in non-combined treatments
