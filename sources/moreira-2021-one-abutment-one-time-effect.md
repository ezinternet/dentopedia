## Why Ingested

A rigorous double-blind RCT that found **no significant MBL advantage** for OAOT — the null result inside [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]] (tabulated there as "Moreira 2021", 53 implants, 0.14 vs 0.21 mm, p=0.33). Keeping the primary report alongside the positive trials prevents the OAOT narrative from being read as uniformly favorable, and it directly tests the recommendation made on indirect grounds in [[implants/peri-implantitis/lin-2025-influence-of-prosthetic-designs]].

## Three-line Summary

Prospective, controlled, randomised, double-blind two-centre trial in 53 platform-switched posterior implants (patients could receive 1–4 implants); definitive abutment placed at surgery and never removed (DA) versus healing abutment disconnected/reconnected three times (PA), with 12-month follow-up.

Mean bone loss was 0.14 ± 0.18 mm (DA) versus 0.23 ± 0.29 mm (PA) from surgery to 6 months and 0.14 ± 0.21 versus 0.21 ± 0.27 mm from 6 to 12 months — both non-significant, with total 12-month loss also non-significant (p=0.330); survival 100%, mean insertion ISQ 80.9.

The one-abutment-one-time protocol was at least **equivalent**, not demonstrably superior, to repeated healing-abutment manipulation in healed posterior sites.

## 세줄요약

전향적·대조·무작위·이중맹검 2기관 시험(플랫폼 스위칭 후방부 임플란트 53개, 환자당 1~4개): 수술 시 최종 지대주를 장착 후 제거하지 않은 군(DA) vs 치유 지대주를 3회 탈착·재장착한 군(PA), 12개월 추적.

수술~6개월 평균 골소실은 DA 0.14 ± 0.18 mm vs PA 0.23 ± 0.29 mm, 6~12개월은 0.14 ± 0.21 vs 0.21 ± 0.27 mm로 모두 유의차 없음; 12개월 총 골소실도 비유의(p=0.330), 생존율 100%, 수술 당일 평균 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 80.9.

치유된 후방부 골수준 식립에서 원-어버트먼트-원-타임(One-Abutment-One-Time, OAOT)은 반복 탈착보다 최소한 **동등**하며, 명백한 우위는 입증되지 않음.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Materials. 2021;14:4179
- **DOI**: 10.3390/ma14154179
- **Institution**: Institute of Oral Implantology and Prosthodontics, Faculty of Medicine, University of Coimbra, Portugal
- **Study type**: Prospective, controlled, randomized, double-blind clinical trial (two centres)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Double-blind design in a literature where most OAOT trials are unblinded — reduces measurement bias on the radiographic primary outcome.
- Tests the protocol in **healed posterior sites**, where the evidence was explicitly unclear, rather than the more common immediate/single-tooth setting.
- Demonstrates non-inferiority-style result: OAOT at least equivalent, but no statistically detectable superiority.
- ISQ ≥50 required for randomisation; mean 80.9 at surgery — high primary stability context.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective double-blind randomized controlled trial, 2 centres; ethics approval CE-048/2018 (University of Coimbra, June 2018).
- **Population**: Partially edentulous adults ≥18, posterior maxilla/mandible (FDI 4–7), ≥4 months post-extraction healing, 1–4 platform-switched implants per patient with ISQ ≥50 at surgery.
- **Groups**: DA (definitive abutment at implant surgery, never removed) vs PA (healing abutment disconnected/reconnected three times before definitive rehabilitation); abutment heights 1, 2 or 3 mm chosen by mucosal height.
- **Outcomes**: Primary = vertical bone level change at 6 and 12 months; secondary = implant mobility, BOP, probing depth, plaque index.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 12 months of follow-up — too short for peri-implantitis incidence.
- Implants pooled at patient level with 1–4 per patient; unit-of-analysis handling not fully reported.
- Non-significant difference does not prove equivalence without a pre-specified non-inferiority margin — the authors' "at least equivalent" phrasing is a cautious reading, not a formal equivalence claim.
- Abutment height varied (1–3 mm) by mucosal thickness, adding a design variable alongside the protocol variable.

## 7. Glossary
- **DA (Definitive Abutment)**: final abutment connected at surgery and never removed.
- **PA (Healing/Provisional Abutment)**: abutment removed and reconnected during healing and prosthetic procedures.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: resonance-frequency-based implant stability measure; ≥50 used here as primary-stability threshold.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: vertical peri-implant crestal bone change.
