## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (n=36 patients, 60 implants, D3/D4 bone) comparing ISQ stability transition from primary to secondary stability between a nano-superhydrophilic bioactive surface (test) and a traditional machined surface (control) over 45 days.

The bioactive surface maintained stable ISQ throughout (74.57→74.78→74.97, no significant change), while the traditional surface showed a significant ISQ dip at 30 days (77.12→73.33, p=0.01) and 45 days (73.44, p=0.03); 26/30 bioactive vs 23/30 traditional implants reached ISQ ≥70 for early loading, and 6 control implants required 180-day delayed loading.

In poor-quality bone (D3/D4), bioactive nano-superhydrophilic surfaces prevent the primary-to-secondary stability dip, enabling earlier loading protocols compared with traditional surfaces.

## 세줄요약

전향적 대조 임상시험 (n=36, 임플란트 60개, D3/D4 골질): 나노초친수성(nano-superhydrophilic) 생활성 표면(검사군) vs 기존 가공면(대조군)의 1차→2차 안정성 전환 과정의 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ, Implant Stability Quotient) 변화 비교.

생활성 표면은 전 관찰기간 동안 ISQ 안정 유지(74.57→74.97, 유의한 변화 없음); 기존 표면은 30일(p=0.01)·45일(p=0.03)에 유의한 ISQ 저하(77.12→73.33); ISQ ≥70 도달: 생활성 26/30 vs 기존 23/30, 기존 표면 6개 임플란트는 180일 지연 부하 필요.

D3/D4 저질 골에서 나노초친수성 생활성 표면은 안정성 저하(stability dip)를 방지해 조기 부하 프로토콜 적용을 가능하게 한다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Investigations 2024; 28:372
- **Study type**: Prospective controlled clinical trial

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrates bioactive surface prevents ISQ stability dip in D3/D4 bone
- Traditional surface: significant ISQ decrease at T30 (p=0.01) and T45 (p=0.03) vs baseline
- Bioactive surface: no significant ISQ change at any timepoint — smoother primary→secondary stability transition
- Torque density (Ncm/sec) more predictive than IT or ISQ for early bone density

## 3. Key Results
- Test (bioactive): ISQ 74.57→74.78→74.97 (T0→T30→T45); no significant change
- Control (traditional): ISQ 77.12→73.33→73.44; significant decrease T30/T45 vs baseline
- ISQ ≥70 threshold: 26/30 test vs 23/30 control → earlier loading protocol in test group
- 6 control implants required 180-day delayed loading due to low ISQ
