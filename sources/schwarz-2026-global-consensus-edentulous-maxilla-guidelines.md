## Why Ingested

최초의 상악 무치악 재활 글로벌 컨센서스 지침의 메인 문서로, 34개 핵심 결과물(Core Outcome Set)을 기반으로 36개 컨센서스 권고안을 정의한다. 기존 [[implants/full-arch/fiorellini-2026-global-consensus-edentulous-maxilla-group3]]의 그룹 3 보고서와 함께 상악 무치악 재활의 전체 프레임워크를 완성.

## Three-line Summary

This S2k-level guideline developed 36 consensus-based recommendations mapped to a Core Outcome Set (COS) of 34 critically important outcomes (10 PROs, 22 ClinROs, 2 subjective ClinROs) through a three-round Delphi process with 105 experts from 26 countries. The guideline covers all phases of care: patient selection, diagnostics, surgical/prosthetic treatment, complication management, and maintenance. It emphasizes shared decision-making, prosthetically driven planning, immediate protocols when feasible, and risk-based maintenance.

## 세줄요약

S2k 수준 지침으로, 26개국 105명 전문가의 3라운드 델파이 과정을 통해 34개 핵심 결과물(COS: 10개 PROs, 22개 ClinROs, 2개 주관적 ClinROs)에 매핑된 36개 컨센서스 권고안을 개발. 환자 선정·진단·외과적·보철적 치료·합병증 관리·유지의 모든 단계를 포괄하며, 공유 의사결정·보철 주도 계획·위험 기반 유지·가능 시 즉시 프로토콜을 강조. 임플란트 치료에 COS를 통합한 최초의 글로벌 지침.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First global guideline for edentulous maxilla rehabilitation integrating a Core Outcome Set
- 36 consensus-based recommendations covering all treatment phases
- Clinical workflow checklist and decision trees for practical implementation
- Emphasizes PROs alongside traditional ClinROs

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: S2k-level guideline (AWMF framework)
- **Process**: Multi-phase (8 SRs + 6 surveys → Delphi → nominal group → plenary vote)
- **Delphi participants**: 105 experts (3 rounds)
- **Evidence base**: 8 systematic reviews + 6 structured surveys

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 36 consensus recommendations formulated
- COS: 34 outcomes (10 PROs, 22 ClinROs, 2 subjective ClinROs)
- All recommendations reached consensus
- Clinical workflow checklist + decision trees provided

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Implementation varies by regional healthcare systems
- Long-term outcomes and maintenance protocols need more evidence
- PRO measurement standardization still evolving
- Cost-effectiveness analysis not included

## 7. Glossary
- **COS (Core Outcome Set)**: 핵심 결과물 세트 (standardized set of outcomes for clinical research)
- **PRO (Patient-Reported Outcome)**: 환자 보고 결과
- **ClinRO (Clinician-Reported Outcome)**: 임상가 보고 결과
- **Delphi Process**: 델파이 방법 (iterative expert consensus process)
