## Why Ingested

위키에는 좁은 직경 임플란트(Narrow-Diameter Implant, NDI)의 *직경* 축 근거(구치부 고정성 보철: [[implants/pachiou-2025-narrow-diameter-implants-fixed-posterior]])와 *하중 시기(Loading Timing)* 축 근거([[immediate-implant/gjelvold-2021-immediate-delayed-loading-single-tooth-5year-rct]], [[implants/liu-2021-immediate-delayed-loading-overdenture-mbl-sr-ma]])가 각각 존재하지만, **두 축이 교차하는 지점** — "좁은 임플란트가 즉시 하중을 견디는가?" — 을 다룬 페이지는 없었다. 본 SR+MA (Pachiou 2026, 9편 정성/7편 정량)는 정확히 그 상호작용을 공급하며, 같은 저자의 pachiou-2025(구치부 NDI)를 하중 프로토콜 축으로 확장한다. NDI는 즉시 하중 시 1차 안정성 확보가 관건이므로 임상 의사결정에 직접적 근거가 된다.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (9 comparative studies qualitative, 7 meta-analyzed; NDIs ≤3.5 mm, 2.5–3.5 mm) comparing immediate vs early/delayed loading of narrow-diameter implants supporting fixed restorations (single crowns, FDPs, full-arch); search to January 2026, PROSPERO CRD420250654797, GRADE.

Implant failure did not differ between loading protocols short-term (<5 y: 4 studies/808 implants, RR 1.41, 95% CI 0.66–3.00, I²=0%) or long-term (≥5 y: 4 studies/502, RR 1.10, 95% CI 0.49–2.47, I²=0%); short-term MBL, biological (RR 0.81, 95% CI 0.10–6.66) and prosthetic complications were comparable — but all certainty LOW to VERY LOW.

Immediate loading of NDIs appears a viable, time-efficient option for fixed restorations in well-selected cases; a signal of higher long-term biological complications with immediate loading (2 observational studies, very low certainty) is hypothesis-generating and mandates careful case selection and monitoring.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (정성 9편, 정량 7편; NDI ≤3.5 mm, 2.5–3.5 mm) — 고정성 보철(단일크라운·FDP·full-arch)을 지지하는 좁은 직경 임플란트의 즉시 하중 대 조기/지연 하중을 비교; 검색 2026년 1월까지, PROSPERO CRD420250654797, GRADE 적용.

임플란트 실패는 하중 프로토콜 간 차이 없음 — 단기(<5년: 4편/808개, RR 1.41, 95% CI 0.66–3.00, I²=0%), 장기(≥5년: 4편/502개, RR 1.10, 95% CI 0.49–2.47, I²=0%); 단기 변연골소실(MBL)·생물학적(RR 0.81, 0.10–6.66)·보철적 합병증 모두 비슷하나 근거 확실성은 LOW~VERY LOW.

NDI 즉시 하중은 적절히 선택된 증례에서 시간 효율적 옵션으로 유효; 다만 장기 생물학적 합병증이 즉시 하중군에서 더 많다는 신호(관찰연구 2편, very low)는 가설 생성 수준 — 신중한 증례 선택·장기 모니터링 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to isolate the **loading-timing axis** for NDIs (≤3.5 mm) supporting *fixed* restorations, requiring direct within-study comparison of immediate vs early/delayed loading.
- Pooled implant-failure risk ratios show **no difference by loading protocol** in both short- and long-term windows, with I²=0%.
- Flags a **long-term biological-complication signal** (higher with immediate loading, 2 observational studies, 30 events) as the key evidence gap — hypothesis-generating only.
- Applies GRADE, transparently rating all outcomes LOW (survival) to VERY LOW (MBL, biological, prosthetic complications) certainty.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA 2020 SR+MA; Cochrane Handbook methodology.
- **PICO**: Partially edentulous patients with fixed NDI-supported restorations (P); immediate loading (I); early or delayed loading (C); implant survival (primary O), biological/prosthetic complications (secondary O).
- **NDI definition**: diameter ≤3.5 mm (included studies span 2.5–3.5 mm).
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Scopus + manual reference screening, OpenGrey, ClinicalTrials.gov; up to January 10, 2026.
- **Eligibility**: RCTs and non-randomized clinical studies (prospective/retrospective), case series ≥10 patients, English, directly comparing immediate vs early/delayed loading within the same study; removable restorations excluded.
- **Loading categories**: immediate vs early/delayed (delayed and conventional grouped together).
- **Risk of bias**: RoB 2 (RCTs), ROBINS-I V2 (non-randomized); two independent reviewers.
- **Statistics**: Random-effects; RRs (with implant failure as the event) for dichotomous outcomes, MDs for MBL; RCTs and non-randomized studies analyzed separately, pooled only when ≥3 comparable studies per design category; otherwise narrative. GRADE for certainty.
- **Yield**: 9 studies in qualitative synthesis, 7 in meta-analysis. Included studies (Table 1): Henn 2025, Kim 2024, Anitua 2020, Slagter 2020, Altinci 2016, MacLean 2016, Malo 2011, Degidi 2009 (RCT), Degidi 2008.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Implant failure, short-term (<5 y)**: 4 studies, 808 implants — RR 1.41 (95% CI 0.66–3.00), I²=0%. No significant difference. GRADE LOW.
- **Implant failure, long-term (≥5 y)**: 4 studies, 502 implants — RR 1.10 (95% CI 0.49–2.47; p=0.73), I²=0% (Q=0.88, p=0.83). No significant difference. GRADE LOW.
- **MBL, short-term (<5 y)**: 3 studies, 345 implants (91 IL / 254 E-DL) — narrative only; comparable between protocols. GRADE VERY LOW. Long-term MBL: only 1 study → narrative.
- **Biological complications, short-term (<5 y)**: 3 non-randomized studies, 795 implants (316 IL / 479 E-DL); only 2 events total — RR 0.81 (95% CI 0.10–6.66; p=0.71), I²=0%. No difference; extremely wide CI. GRADE VERY LOW.
- **Biological complications, long-term (≥5 y)**: 2 studies, 242 implants (95 IL / 147 E-DL), 30 events — narrative; **both studies suggest higher incidence with immediate loading**; <3 studies so not pooled. Hypothesis-generating.
- **Prosthetic complications, short-term (<5 y)**: 3 studies (2 non-randomized, 1 RCT), 817 implants (328 IL / 489 E-DL), 5 events — narrative; comparable. GRADE VERY LOW. Long-term: 1 study → narrative.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Few studies per outcome/design category → most secondary outcomes narrative, not pooled; publication bias not formally assessable (<10 studies per outcome).
- Predominance of non-randomized studies with moderate-to-serious ROBINS-I risk (confounding, missing outcome data) → survival certainty capped at LOW.
- Very low event counts for biological/prosthetic complications → wide CIs, imprecision.
- Long-term biological-complication signal rests on only 2 observational studies — not conclusive.
- Heterogeneous NDI diameters (2.5–3.5 mm), restoration types (single crown/FDP/full-arch), and jaw regions pooled together.
- Future need: adequately powered RCTs with standardized biological-complication definitions and ≥5-year follow-up.

## 6. Related Work

- Extends the same group's posterior-region NDI SR+MA (Pachiou 2025) onto the loading-timing axis.
- Complements loading-timing evidence derived largely from overdentures / single crowns (Liu 2021, Gjelvold 2021) by focusing on NDIs under fixed restorations.
- Consistent with NDI vs standard-diameter equivalence literature (Cao 2023 TiZr single crowns; Coskunses 2021 full-arch).

## 7. Glossary

- **NDI (Narrow-Diameter Implant)**: dental implant with diameter ≤3.5 mm; here 2.5–3.5 mm.
- **Immediate loading (IL)**: functional/occlusal loading of the implant within days of placement.
- **Early/delayed loading (E-DL)**: loading after a healing interval (early = weeks; delayed/conventional = ≥2–3 months); grouped together here.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: change in peri-implant crestal bone level over time.
- **RR (Risk Ratio)**: here the relative risk of implant *failure* between loading groups (RR>1 favors comparator).
- **GRADE**: system rating certainty of evidence (High → Very Low).
- **ROBINS-I / RoB 2**: risk-of-bias tools for non-randomized and randomized studies.
