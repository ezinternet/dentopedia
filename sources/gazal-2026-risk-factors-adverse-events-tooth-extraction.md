## Why Ingested

Narrative review quantifying condition-specific adverse event rates for tooth extraction in medically compromised patients, with explicit safe clinical thresholds (HbA1c <9%, INR ≤3.5, BP <180/110 mmHg) and a stepwise safety ladder framework. Extends the anticoagulant-focused evidence base (→ [[drug/anticoagulants/inchingolo-2024-anticoagulation-therapy-dental-practice-sr]]) to a broader multi-condition risk stratification model including diabetes, hypertension, asthma/COPD, and immunosuppression.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of adverse event risks during tooth extraction across five major medical comorbidities (DM, anticoagulation, hypertension, asthma/COPD, immunosuppression), synthesizing prevalence data and perioperative complication rates from the literature. Key quantitative findings: DM patients face 10-15% intraoperative hypoglycemia risk; anticoagulated patients have 8-12% postoperative bleeding vs 2-3% controls; immunosuppressed/transplant patients reach up to 20% postoperative infection. The review proposes evidence-based clinical thresholds and a structured safety ladder as decision tools for pre-, intra-, and postoperative risk management.

## Key Contributions

- Quantifies intraoperative hypoglycemia risk in diabetic dental patients: 10-15% of those with DM (8-12% of dental patients)
- Documents anticoagulated patient postoperative bleeding: 8-12% vs 2-3% in non-anticoagulated controls
- Estimates perioperative hypertensive blood pressure elevations in 20-25% of hypertensive patients (up to 30% of adults)
- Reports asthma/COPD bronchospasm risk: 3-6% of affected patients (7-10% of dental patient pool)
- Establishes immunosuppressed/transplant postoperative infection ceiling: up to 20%
- Proposes validated clinical cut-offs: HbA1c <9%, INR ≤3.5, BP <180/110 mmHg as safe-to-proceed thresholds
- Provides five structured tables covering medical classifications, safe clinical thresholds, emergency management protocols, referral criteria, and a stepwise safety ladder

## Glossary

- **Safety ladder**: Structured stepwise clinical protocol for pre-, intra-, and postoperative risk management in medically compromised patients
- **Risk stratification**: Classification of patients by complication risk level to guide treatment modification and referral decisions
- **HbA1c**: Glycated hemoglobin; proxy for 3-month glycemic control; threshold <9% used in this review as safe-to-proceed cutoff for dental surgery
- **INR (International Normalized Ratio)**: Standardized measure of anticoagulation intensity; threshold ≤3.5 cited as safe for dental extraction
- **Bronchospasm**: Acute airway constriction; a known intraoperative risk in asthma/COPD patients under stress or with certain medications
- **Immunosuppression**: Pharmacologic or disease-mediated reduction of immune function; elevates postoperative infection risk to ≤20% in transplant patients
