## Three-line Summary

Practice guideline (American College of Chest Physicians, Chest 2022; 43 PICO questions; GRADE) on perioperative management of patients on long-term vitamin K antagonist (VKA), heparin bridging, direct oral anticoagulant (DOAC) or antiplatelet therapy who need an elective surgery/procedure.

The multidisciplinary panel issued 44 recommendations, of which two are strong: against heparin bridging in atrial fibrillation, and continuation of VKA for pacemaker/ICD implantation; separate recommendations cover minor procedures including dental.

The abstract states that substantial new evidence has emerged since the 2012 guideline, but uncertainty remains for the majority of perioperative questions; dental-specific recommendations are not detailed in the abstract (abstract-only ingest).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Addresses 43 PICO questions in four categories: VKA, heparin bridging in VKA patients, DOAC, antiplatelet drugs.
- 44 recommendations graded strong or conditional on high/moderate/low/very low certainty (GRADE).
- Two strong recommendations: against heparin bridging in atrial fibrillation; continue VKA in pacemaker/ICD implantation.
- Separate recommendations for minor procedures: dental, dermatologic, ophthalmologic, pacemaker/ICD implantation, GI (endoscopic).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Multidisciplinary panel; GRADE methodology for clinical practice guidelines; PICO-based. Details of searches and panel process are not in the abstract.

## 7. Glossary

- VKA: vitamin K antagonist, mainly warfarin.
- Heparin bridging: perioperative heparin (typically LMWH) during VKA interruption.
- DOAC: direct oral anticoagulant.
- GRADE: Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development, and Evaluation.
- PICO: Patients-Interventions-Comparators-Outcomes.
