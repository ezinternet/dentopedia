## Why Ingested

"국소의치 해드리면 삶의 질이 좋아진다"는 상담 문장의 **유통기한**을 재는 유일한 메타분석이라 넣었다. [[wiki/removable-partial-denture/gotfredsen-2021-removable-partial-prosthesis-periodontitis-sr]]가 "OHRQoL은 개선되나 SDA·고정성 보철보다 작다"고 서술적으로 말한 것을 본 논문은 **시점별 수치**로 분해한다 — 3·6개월엔 유의하나 12개월엔 OHIP-14 기준으로 유의성이 사라진다. [[wiki/removable-partial-denture/walter-2021-shortened-dental-arch-15year-rct]]가 보철 성공률로는 두 전략을 못 가른다고 했으므로 환자보고결과가 결정 근거로 올라오는데, 그 근거의 확실성 등급이 very low라는 사실 자체를 위키가 들고 있어야 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (13 eligible studies, 8 pooled; MEDLINE/EMBASE/CENTRAL to March 2022; PROSPERO CRD42022328606) of OHRQoL change before and after RPD rehabilitation in partially edentulous adults, with moderate-to-serious risk of bias across included studies.

OHIP-14 improved at 3 months (222 participants, MD −12.0, 95% CI −16.1 to −7.9) and 6 months (101 participants, MD −10.5, 95% CI −16.4 to −4.6) at **very low** certainty, but at 12 months the OHIP-14 estimate was no longer statistically significant (62 participants, MD −12.7, 95% CI −26.1 to 0.6, p = 0.06), while OHIP-49 at 12 months did reach significance (87 participants, MD −34.8) at low certainty.

RPD rehabilitation appears to improve quality of life for up to six months on very low certainty evidence, and the twelve-month picture is inconclusive and instrument-dependent — so the benefit should be presented to patients as real but short-horizon and not yet durable-proven.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (적격 13편 중 8편 병합; MEDLINE·EMBASE·CENTRAL 2022년 3월까지; PROSPERO CRD42022328606): 부분무치악 성인에서 가철성 국소의치 (Removable Partial Denture, RPD) 장착 전후의 구강건강관련 삶의 질 (Oral Health-Related Quality of Life, OHRQoL) 변화를 종합했고, 포함 연구의 비뚤림 위험은 중등도~심각 수준이었다.

구강건강영향지수 14문항 (Oral Health Impact Profile-14, OHIP-14)은 3개월 (222명, 평균차 Mean Difference, MD = −12.0, 95% CI −16.1 ~ −7.9)과 6개월 (101명, MD = −10.5)에 개선됐으나 **확실성 등급은 very low**였고, **12개월에는 유의성이 사라졌다** (62명, MD = −12.7, 95% CI −26.1 ~ 0.6, p = 0.06); 반면 OHIP-49는 12개월에 유의했다 (87명, MD = −34.8, low certainty).

RPD는 **6개월까지는** 삶의 질을 개선하는 것으로 보이나 그 근거의 확실성이 very low이고, 12개월 시점은 결론이 나지 않았으며 사용한 측정도구에 따라 갈린다 — 환자에게는 "좋아지지만 단기 지평이며 장기 지속은 아직 입증 안 됨"으로 설명해야 정확하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First meta-analytic time-course of OHRQoL after RPD, rather than a single post-treatment snapshot.
- Documents the **loss of statistical significance at 12 months on OHIP-14** — the finding that distinguishes this from the usual "dentures improve quality of life" summary.
- Exposes **instrument dependence**: OHIP-49 remains significant at 12 months where OHIP-14 does not.
- Attaches explicit GRADE certainty (very low / low) to each estimate, which most prosthodontic reviews omit.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Systematic review with random-effects meta-analysis, prospectively registered (PROSPERO CRD42022328606). Databases: MEDLINE, EMBASE, CENTRAL to 29 March 2022. Inclusion: adults ≥18 years, OHRQoL assessed before and after rehabilitation with RPDs of any type and design. Quality appraised with Cochrane risk-of-bias tools; certainty by GRADE. Thirteen studies eligible, eight pooled.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Instrument · timepoint | n | MD | 95% CI | p | Certainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OHIP-14, 3 months | 222 | −12.0 | −16.1 to −7.9 | <0.001 | Very low |
| OHIP-14, 6 months | 101 | −10.5 | −16.4 to −4.6 | <0.001 | Very low |
| **OHIP-14, 12 months** | 62 | −12.7 | **−26.1 to 0.6** | **0.06 (NS)** | — |
| OHIP-49, 12 months | 87 | −34.8 | −41.9 to −27.7 | <0.001 | Low |

Note the 12-month OHIP-14 point estimate (−12.7) is *larger* than the significant 6-month estimate; it fails significance because n falls to 62 and the interval widens, not because the effect reverses.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Every pooled estimate is **very low or low certainty**; the authors state outright there is insufficient evidence on the effect of RPD treatment on OHRQoL.
- Sample sizes collapse over time (222 → 101 → 62), so the 12-month null is under-powered rather than a demonstrated absence of effect. This distinction matters and is easy to misreport.
- Moderate-to-serious risk of bias across included studies; before-after designs without a comparator arm cannot separate the prosthesis from regression to the mean or attention effects.
- Ingested **abstract-only**: heterogeneity statistics, subgroup analyses by denture design, and GRADE downgrade reasons unavailable.
- Pools "RPDs of any type and design", so a well-designed cast framework and an acrylic interim denture may sit in the same estimate.

## 7. Glossary

- **OHRQoL (구강건강관련 삶의 질, Oral Health-Related Quality of Life)**: patient-reported construct covering function, pain, psychological and social impact of oral conditions.
- **OHIP-14 / OHIP-49 (구강건강영향지수, Oral Health Impact Profile)**: 14- and 49-item versions of the standard OHRQoL questionnaire; **lower scores are better**, so a negative mean difference denotes improvement.
- **MD (평균차, Mean Difference)**: pooled difference between post- and pre-treatment scores.
- **GRADE certainty (근거 확실성 등급)**: rating of how much confidence to place in an effect estimate; "very low" means the true effect is likely substantially different.
- **PROSPERO**: international prospective register of systematic reviews.
