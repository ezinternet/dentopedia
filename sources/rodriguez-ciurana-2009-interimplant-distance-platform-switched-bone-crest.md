## Three-line Summary
Prospective radiographic study (n=37 patients, 41 pairs of adjacent implants placed <3 mm apart) evaluating whether platform switching changes the bone-resorption pattern between closely spaced adjacent implants, compared to the pre-platform-switching 3 mm rule established by Tarnow et al. 2000.

Mean vertical bone resorption was 0.62 mm and mean horizontal resorption was 0.60 mm; the interproximal bone peak extended coronally 0.24 mm beyond the imaginary line connecting the two implant-abutment interfaces (IAJ-to-IAJ line). (Separately, per Gupta 2019's review of this same paper: four abutment/implant diameter pairs — 4.1/4.8, 5.0/5.8, 4.1/5.0, 3.4/4.1 mm — giving a per-side offset of 0.35–0.45 mm, with bone resorption M 0.7±0.57mm / D 0.55±0.52mm.)

Platform switching appears to preserve a measurable bone peak above the IAJ-to-IAJ line even when adjacent implants are placed <3 mm apart, i.e., closer than Tarnow's classic 3 mm guideline for non-platform-switched implants — but this is a single prospective study without a non-PS control arm within the same paper, so it does not by itself prove PS "rescues" close spacing.

## 세줄요약
전향적 방사선 연구(n=37명, <3mm 간격으로 식립된 인접 임플란트 41쌍) — 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS)이 인접 임플란트 간 골소실 양상을 Tarnow 등 2000의 (PS 이전) 3mm 룰과 비교해 바꾸는지 평가.

평균 수직 골소실 0.62mm, 평균 수평 골소실 0.60mm; 치간 골첨부가 두 임플란트-지대주 접합부(IAJ)를 잇는 가상선보다 관측방향으로 0.24mm 더 유지됨. (별도로, Gupta 2019 리뷰가 정리한 이 논문의 임플란트/지대주 직경 조합 4종 — 4.1/4.8, 5.0/5.8, 4.1/5.0, 3.4/4.1mm — 은 편측 오프셋 0.35–0.45mm, 골소실 근심 0.7±0.57mm/원심 0.55±0.52mm.)

플랫폼 스위칭은 인접 임플란트 간격이 Tarnow의 3mm 기준보다 좁아도(<3mm) IAJ 간 가상선 위로 측정 가능한 골첨부를 유지하는 것으로 보이나, 이 논문 자체엔 비-PS 대조군이 없는 단일 전향 연구라 "PS가 좁은 간격을 구제한다"는 결론을 이 논문만으로 확정할 수는 없음.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: The International Journal of Periodontics & Restorative Dentistry 2009;29(2):141-51
- **PMID**: 19408476
- **Institution**: Not stated in abstract; lead/senior author affiliation listed as "headquarters@borgroup.net" (BOR Group, Barcelona) per PubMed record; co-author Dennis P. Tarnow (author of the 2000 3mm-rule paper this study follows up on)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First (or among the first) prospective radiographic evaluations of inter-implant crestal bone height specifically for platform-switched implants placed closer than the classic 3 mm inter-implant distance (IID) threshold.
- Introduces the IAJ-to-IAJ imaginary line as the reference for measuring how much interproximal bone peak is preserved above the level connecting the two implants' abutment junctions — a distance-specific metric to complement (not replace) simple vertical/horizontal resorption values.
- Per Gupta 2019's review, also reports its own abutment/implant diameter-offset dataset across four diameter-pair combinations, adding to the pooled evidence on per-side offset magnitude (0.35–0.45 mm) used across the platform-switching literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective radiographic study (MeSH: "Prospective Studies")
- **n**: 41 pairs of adjacent implants placed <3 mm apart, in 37 patients
- **Outcomes**: Vertical bone resorption, horizontal bone resorption, interproximal bone-peak height relative to the IAJ-to-IAJ imaginary line

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Pairs / patients | 41 pairs / 37 patients, IID <3 mm |
| Mean vertical bone resorption | 0.62 mm |
| Mean horizontal bone resorption | 0.60 mm |
| Bone peak above IAJ-to-IAJ line | 0.24 mm (coronal preservation) |
| (Gupta 2019, offset dataset) diameter pairs | 4.1/4.8, 5.0/5.8, 4.1/5.0, 3.4/4.1 mm |
| (Gupta 2019, offset dataset) per-side offset | 0.35–0.45 mm |
| (Gupta 2019, offset dataset) bone resorption | M 0.7±0.57 mm, D 0.55±0.52 mm |

Reference point: Tarnow et al. 2000 (pre-PS, external hex) reported crestal bone loss of 1.04 mm at IID ≤3 mm vs 0.45 mm at IID >3 mm, with ~1.34–1.40 mm lateral bone loss per implant shoulder.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only the PubMed abstract was retrieved — no PMC full text, no independently verified DOI; full methodology (implant systems, connection type, exact per-patient breakdown, statistical testing) not confirmed beyond what the abstract states.
- No non-platform-switched control arm reported within the abstract itself, limiting direct causal attribution of the preserved bone peak to platform switching per se (contrast with Tarnow 2000's own ≤3 mm non-PS cohort, which showed larger 1.04 mm crestal loss — a cross-study, not within-study, comparison).
- The offset/diameter-pair data cited here comes from a secondary source (Gupta 2019's table), not independently verified against Rodríguez-Ciurana 2009's own full text.

## 7. Glossary
- **Inter-implant distance (IID)**: horizontal distance between the shoulders/platforms of two adjacent implants.
- **Platform switching (PS)**: using an abutment with a smaller diameter than the implant platform, shifting the microgap inward.
- **IAJ (implant-abutment junction)**: the interface between implant platform and abutment; here used as the reference line for measuring interproximal bone-peak preservation between two adjacent implants.
- **Offset magnitude**: the per-side (radial) reduction in abutment diameter relative to implant platform diameter under platform switching, typically reported in mm.
