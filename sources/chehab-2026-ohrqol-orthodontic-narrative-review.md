## Why Ingested

Fills PRO/OHRQoL gap: most comprehensive up-to-date (inception–Dec 2025) narrative review of 140 studies synthesizing OHRQoL trajectory across orthodontic treatments with specific aligner vs FA comparisons. Covers 3-phase model, pain kinetics, and long-term recovery. → [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/chehab-2026-ohrqol-orthodontic-narrative-review]]

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of 140 studies (inception to December 2025) mapping OHRQoL across all orthodontic treatment phases using a 3-phase trajectory model: initial adaptation (impairment), stabilization (improvement), and completion/retention (recovery). Clear aligners outperform fixed appliances on OHRQoL in 65–75% of comparative studies, with significantly less impact in comfort, aesthetics, and social domains. Pain peaks at 24–48 hours after each aligner change and resolves within 4–7 days; overall OHRQoL decline during aligner treatment is only 8–12%.

## 2. Key Findings

- 3-phase OHRQoL trajectory: (1) initial impairment phase — highest impact from pain/discomfort; (2) stabilization — gradual improvement as adaptation occurs; (3) completion/retention — near-baseline or improved vs pre-treatment
- CA vs FA comparisons: CA superior in 65–75% of comparative studies (comfort, aesthetics, social impact, speech)
- Pain kinetics (87% of studies): peak 24–48h post-aligner change, resolves 4–7 days
- OHRQoL decline during CA treatment: 8–12% (minimal) vs higher with FA
- Speech: transient impact (days to weeks); resolves as tongue adapts
- Social impact: CA significantly less disruptive (aesthetics-driven)
- Retention phase: OHIP-14 returns to pre-treatment values or better in both CA and FA

## 3. Methodology

Narrative review (not systematic; no PROSPERO registration). Database search not specified in detail. 140 included studies. Narrative synthesis by treatment phase and outcome domain. Risk of bias not formally assessed (narrative design).

## 4. Clinical Implications

Patients should be counseled about the 24–48h pain cycle with each new aligner tray; over-the-counter analgesics timed before/after change can mitigate OHRQoL impact. The 8–12% OHRQoL decline is reassuring and can be communicated at treatment planning.

## 5. Limitations

Narrative review — no formal systematic search, no meta-analysis, no risk-of-bias assessment. Subject to selection and confirmation bias. Heterogeneity of included studies not quantified.
