## Why Ingested

Systematic review and narrative evolution of graftless lateral sinus lift from 1997 to 2011 — describes the blood-clot-only mechanism, Schneiderian membrane osteogenic capacity, and reviews all long-term studies available at time of publication; foundational context for [[sinus-lift/lateral/dongo-2018-lateral-sinus-floor-graftless-meta-analysis]] and [[sinus-lift/lateral/hegde-2016-sinus-augmentation-membrane-elevation-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with narrative surgical technique description (PubMed 1997–November 2011) examining graftless lateral sinus floor elevation using blood clot only in human clinical studies with ≥1 year follow-up, covering the evolution from Tatum's 1976 concept through 2011 evidence and characterizing the Schneiderian membrane's osteogenic mechanism.

All identified studies reported high implant survival rates for the graftless lateral technique; the technique was found to be cost-effective and less time-consuming than grafted lateral SFE; the Schneiderian membrane's periosteum-like deep layer provides osteoprogenitor cells that colonize the blood clot scaffold under the elevated membrane.

Graftless lateral SFE relying solely on the blood clot as a natural autologous scaffold is a reliable and cost-effective alternative to grafted approaches, eliminating graft material cost, donor site morbidity, and the long-term bone resorption seen with some graft substitutes.

## 세줄요약

혈병(blood clot)만을 이용하는 이식재 없는 측방 상악동거상술(graftless lateral SFE)을 대상으로 한 체계적 문헌고찰 + 수술 기법 기술(PubMed 1997–2011, 인간 임상 연구 ≥1년 추적); 1976년 Tatum 개념부터 슈나이더 막(Schneiderian membrane) 골형성 기전까지 서술.

포함된 모든 연구에서 높은 임플란트 생존율 보고; 이식재 없는 술식이 이식재 사용 술식 대비 비용·시간 절감; 슈나이더 막의 골막 유사 심층부에서 골전구세포가 혈병 기질로 이동해 신생골을 형성하는 기전 규명.

혈병만으로 충분한 자가 기질을 제공하는 무이식 측방 상악동거상술은 이식재 비용·공여부 이환율·장기 흡수 문제를 배제하는 신뢰 가능한 대안임.

## One-line Summary

Review (PubMed search 1997–2011): graftless lateral SFE (blood clot only) is cost-effective and reliable; high implant survival rates across all included studies; technique relies on Schneiderian membrane osteogenic capacity and blood clot as natural scaffold.

## 한줄요약

서술적 고찰+체계적 문헌 검색(1997–2011): 무이식 측방 상악동거상술은 비용 효율적이고 신뢰 가능; 포함된 모든 연구에서 높은 임플란트 생존율; 슈나이더 막의 골형성 능력과 혈병이 핵심 기전.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: International Journal of Dentistry, Volume 2012, Article ID 105483
- **DOI**: 10.1155/2012/105483
- **Institution**: Uppsala University, Department of Plastic & Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery
- **Study design**: Systematic review + narrative description of surgical technique evolution
- **Search**: PubMed, 1997–November 2011
- **Inclusion**: Clinical human studies, ≥1 year follow-up; no bone graft/substitute; no osteotome technique; lateral approach only

## 2. Key Contributions

- Describes evolution from Tatum's 1976 concept to modern graftless lateral SFE
- Identifies Schneiderian membrane as having periosteum-like osteogenic capacity (deep layers, microvascular cells)
- Blood clot under elevated membrane serves as autologous scaffold for osteoprogenitor migration
- Relative few long-term studies found; technique described as reliable given available evidence
- All included studies show high implant survival rates for graftless technique
- Advantages: lower cost (no graft material), shorter procedure time, no donor site morbidity

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Search**: PubMed free text: "sinus" and "implant"
- **Inclusion**: Clinical human studies; ≥1 year follow-up; graftless lateral sinus lift (no graft or bone substitute); minimum follow-up 1 year
- **Exclusion**: Studies using bone graft or substitutes in relation to sinus lift; osteotome technique studies
- **Process**: Two authors (CR, AT); not blinded; titles+abstracts then full text

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- All investigated studies show high implant survival rates for graftless technique
- Technique cost-effective and less time-consuming than grafted lateral SFE
- Mechanism: blood clot provides scaffold; Schneiderian membrane osteogenic capacity drives new bone
- Bone resorption in grafted sinus is a significant drawback not applicable to graftless approach
- Limited long-term studies as of 2011; need for more robust evidence noted

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small number of eligible long-term studies as of search date (1997–2011)
- Not a formal meta-analysis; qualitative synthesis only
- No standardized outcome measures across studies
- Lack of randomization in included studies acknowledged
- Subsequent Dongo 2018 meta-analysis provides stronger statistical synthesis

## 6. Related Work

- Dongo 2018: SR+MA of lateral graftless SFE; 11 studies, 660 implants; survival 97.5%
- Hegde 2016: SR on direct (lateral) sinus elevation without graft; broader scope including animal studies
- Del Fabbro 2025: SR of 49 RCTs for sinus augmentation materials — establishes grafted benchmarks

## 7. Glossary

- **Schneiderian membrane (슈나이더 막)**: sinus floor mucoperiosteum; has osteogenic capacity from deep periosteum-like layer
- **Blood clot scaffold (혈병 기전)**: autologous clot under elevated membrane provides matrix for osteoprogenitor cell migration and bone formation
- **Graftless lateral SFE (무이식 측방 상악동거상술)**: lateral window approach without bone substitute or autograft — blood clot only
