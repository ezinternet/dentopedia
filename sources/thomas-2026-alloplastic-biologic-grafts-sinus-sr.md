## Why Ingested
상악동거상술 이식재 선택 근거 강화를 위해 인제스트. 기존 [[sinus-lift/lateral/alkandari-2025-bcp-dbbm-sinus-elevation-sr-ma]]가 BCP vs DBBM의 개별 비교라면, 이 논문은 autograft·allograft·xenograft·alloplast 4종을 망라한 SR로 임상 이식재 선택의 위계 구조를 제공한다.

## One-line Summary
SR of 18 studies comparing alloplastic vs biologic grafts in MSFA: autograft showed highest NBF, xenograft superior volumetric stability, alloplasts viable but inferior in regenerative potential; all types achieved >90% implant survival.

## 한줄요약
18개 연구 SR: 상악동거상술 이식재 비교 — 자가골이 신생골 형성 최고, 이종골이 부피 안정성 우수, 합성골이식재는 생물학적 이식재보다 재생 잠재력 열등하지만 임플란트 생존율 90% 이상으로 허용 가능.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR to compare alloplastic vs biologic grafts using GRADE evidence grading in MSFA context
- Demonstrates autograft superiority in NBF (41.7%) with quantitative benchmarks vs alloplast (30.3%) and xenograft (24.9%)
- Establishes that xenografts outperform alloplasts in volumetric stability (103% vs 66% per Schmitt)
- Confirms >90% implant survival regardless of graft type — suggests survival is not the discriminating variable
- Identifies alloplastic grafts as appropriate alternative when donor-site morbidity or immunological risk is a concern

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Systematic review (PRISMA), no meta-analysis due to heterogeneity
- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, EBSCO, Google Scholar, ClinicalTrials.gov, Cochrane
- Included studies: 18 (13 RCTs + 5 non-randomized cohorts)
- Sample sizes: 5–56 patients per study; follow-up 5 months to 2 years
- Graft types compared: autogenous bone, FDBA/MCBA (allografts), DBBM/anorganic bovine (xenografts), HA/β-TCP/BCP/bioactive glass (alloplasts), composite grafts
- Surgical technique: predominantly lateral window sinus lift ± barrier membrane
- Outcome measures: NBF %, residual graft %, volumetric stability, bone height gain, implant survival, complications
- Risk of bias: RoB 2.0 (RCTs); ROBINS I V2 (non-randomized); GRADE for evidence certainty

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**New bone formation (NBF):**
- Autograft: 41.7% (Schmitt) / 36.8% (Danesh-Sani) — highest
- Allograft (FDBA): 35.4% (Schmitt)
- Alloplast (BCP/β-TCP): 30.3% (Schmitt) / 28.2% (Danesh-Sani)
- Xenograft (DBBM): 24.9% (Schmitt) — note: DBBM low own NBF % but scaffold retention

**Residual graft content:**
- Alloplast (BCP): 25.4% vs allograft (FDBA) 12.5% (Kolerman)
- Alloplast: 32.9% vs autograft 4.8% (Danesh-Sani)

**Volumetric stability:**
- Xenograft: 103% (Schmitt)
- Allograft: 112% (Schmitt)
- Alloplast: 66% (Schmitt) — significantly inferior

**Implant survival rates:**
- Consistently >90% across all graft types
- Lindgren: alloplast 96.8% = xenograft 96.8% (success rate: xenograft 95.7% vs alloplast 91.7%)
- Mordenfeld: alloplast 91.7% = xenograft 91.3%; marginal bone loss lower in xenograft (1.0 vs 1.4 mm)
- Onișor-Gligor: higher implant failure in alloplasts (7.7%) vs autografts (1.9%)

**GRADE certainty:** Low for NBF, graft resorption, and implant survival with alloplasts

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- High heterogeneity prevents meta-analysis; evidence certainty rated low (GRADE)
- Majority studies <5 year follow-up for alloplastic materials
- High risk of bias in 2 RCTs (randomization, allocation concealment)
- Small sample sizes in many included studies
- Future: large-scale RCTs with standardized protocols; longitudinal >5 years; bioactive/nanostructured alloplastics

## 7. Glossary
- **NBF**: New Bone Formation — % of histomorphometric section occupied by newly formed bone
- **DBBM**: Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral — xenograft (e.g., Bio-Oss)
- **BCP**: Biphasic Calcium Phosphate — alloplast (e.g., 60% HA + 40% β-TCP)
- **β-TCP**: Beta-tricalcium phosphate — resorbable alloplast
- **FDBA**: Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft — allogeneic graft
- **MCBA**: Mineralized Cortical Bone Allograft
- **RoB 2.0**: Cochrane Risk of Bias tool for RCTs
- **ROBINS I V2**: Risk of Bias In Non-randomized Studies of Interventions
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development, and Evaluation

## Three-line Summary

- Systematic review (PRISMA, PROSPERO; 18 studies — 13 RCTs + 5 cohorts; sample sizes 5–56 patients; follow-up 5 months–2 years) comparing alloplastic grafts (BCP, β-TCP, HA, bioactive glass) versus biologic grafts (autograft, allograft, xenograft) in maxillary sinus floor augmentation.
- Autograft yielded the highest new bone formation (NBF 41.7%), followed by allograft (35.4%), alloplast (30.3%), and xenograft (24.9%); xenograft showed superior volumetric stability (103% vs alloplast 66%); all graft types achieved >90% implant survival (GRADE low certainty).
- Alloplastic grafts are a valid clinical alternative when donor-site morbidity or infection risk precludes biologics, but carry inferior regenerative potential and volumetric stability; implant survival alone is not a sufficient discriminator of material choice.

## 세줄요약

- 체계적 문헌 고찰(Systematic Review, SR; PRISMA, PROSPERO; 18편 — 무작위 대조 시험(RCT) 13편 + 코호트 5편; 추적 5개월–2년): 합성골이식재(Alloplast: BCP·β-TCP·HA·생활성 유리) vs 생물학적 이식재(자가골·동종골·이종골) 비교, 상악동저 거상술(Maxillary Sinus Floor Augmentation, MSFA) 맥락.
- 자가골이 신생골 형성(New Bone Formation, NBF) 최고(41.7%), 이어 동종골(35.4%)·합성골(30.3%)·이종골(24.9%); 이종골이 부피 안정성 가장 우수(103% vs 합성골 66%); 전 이식재군 임플란트 생존율 90% 이상(GRADE 낮음).
- 합성골이식재는 공여부 이환율 또는 감염 위험이 있을 때 유효 대안이나, 재생 잠재력과 부피 안정성이 열등; 임플란트 생존율 단독으로는 재료 선택의 결정 인자로 불충분.
