## Why Ingested

측방창 (Lateral Window) 상악동거상술의 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry) 근거는 이종골 (Xenograft, DBBM) 중심으로 축적되어 있고, **동종골 (Allograft) + 이상성 인산칼슘 (Biphasic Calcium Phosphate, BCP = 30% HA/70% β-TCP) + 자가 PRF** 3중 복합이식재를 6개월 시점에 대규모 생검 (n=60) 으로 정량한 인체 자료는 위키에 없었다. 특히 [[sinus-lift/lateral/motimath-2026-xenograft-prf-sinus-histomorphometric]] 가 DBBM+PRF 축에서 보고한 신생골 (New Bone, NB) 31.2–42.8%·잔존 이식재 (Residual Graft, RG) 28.4–41.6% 와 직접 비교 가능한 수치를 제공하며, 잔존골 높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 2.31 mm 라는 중증 위축 조건과 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 상관까지 함께 보고한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-arm descriptive histomorphometric study (42 patients / 60 lateral-window sinus augmentation sites, Vietnam; residual bone height ≤4 mm, mean 2.31 ± 0.93 mm) using a 2:1 composite of corticocancellous allograft and 30% HA/70% β-TCP plus autologous PRF, with 6-month trephine core biopsies.

At 6 months: newly formed bone 31.28 ± 17.71% (95% CI 26.70–35.85), residual graft material 15.48 ± 12.33% (12.29–18.68), connective tissue 53.23 ± 18.74% (48.38–58.07); no inflammation or necrosis, mature lamellar bone with Haversian systems and active osteoblast/osteoclast remodeling; ISQ rose 58.20 ± 6.47 → 74.85 ± 7.68 at 6 months, with ISQ1 correlating strongly with residual bone height (r = 0.626) and inversely with connective tissue (r = −0.627).

Histology supports biocompatibility and osseoconductive integration of the allograft/β-TCP-HA/PRF composite in severely atrophic maxillae, but the single-arm design (no comparator, one time point, 2-D area fraction from 3-mm cores) makes the result descriptive — the individual contribution of allograft, biphasic ceramic and PRF cannot be separated.

## 세줄요약

전향적 단일군 기술적 조직형태계측 연구 (베트남, 42명 / 60개 측방창 상악동거상술 부위, 잔존골 높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) ≤4 mm, 평균 2.31 ± 0.93 mm): 동종골 (Allograft) : 30% 수산화인회석/70% β-삼인산칼슘 (30% HA/70% β-TCP) 2:1 복합이식재에 자가 혈소판풍부피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF) 을 더해 이식하고 6개월째 트레핀 생검.

6개월 조직형태계측 — 신생골 (New Bone, NB) 31.28 ± 17.71% (95% CI 26.70–35.85), 잔존 이식재 (Residual Graft, RG) 15.48 ± 12.33% (12.29–18.68), 결합조직 (Connective Tissue, CT) 53.23 ± 18.74% (48.38–58.07); 염증·괴사 소견 없음, 하버스관 (Haversian Canal) 을 갖춘 성숙 층판골 (Lamellar Bone) 과 활발한 골모세포/파골세포 리모델링; 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 는 58.20 ± 6.47 → 6개월 74.85 ± 7.68 로 상승.

이식재의 생체적합성과 골유도성 골전도 통합을 지지하나, 대조군 없는 단일군·단일 시점·3 mm 코어의 2차원 면적률이라는 한계 때문에 동종골·이상성 인산칼슘 (Biphasic Calcium Phosphate, BCP)·PRF 각 성분의 기여도는 분리할 수 없는 기술적 (descriptive) 근거다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides the largest single-protocol histomorphometric series in the wiki for a **three-component composite graft** — corticocancellous allograft : 30% HA/70% β-TCP at 2:1 plus autologous PRF — in severely atrophic posterior maxillae (60 biopsies, mean RBH 2.31 mm).
- Quantifies a **low residual graft fraction at 6 months (15.48%)**, i.e. the β-TCP/HA component is substantially resorbed/replaced by vital bone by 6 months, in contrast to DBBM series where residual xenograft persists.
- Reports the **whole tissue budget** (NB + RG + CT ≈ 100%) rather than NB alone, exposing that connective tissue/marrow occupies the majority of the grafted volume (53.23%) — the component that also drives the negative ISQ correlation.
- Pairs histology with **implant-level stability**: ISQ 58.20 → 74.85 at 6 months, and shows ISQ1 tracks residual bone height (r = 0.626) and CT fraction (r = −0.627) more strongly than vital bone fraction (r = 0.405).
- Documents **region-specific (Southeast Asian / Vietnamese) histological data**, arguing that nutritional and metabolic differences from European/North American cohorts may shift graft remodeling.
- Reports a sex difference in residual graft material (female 18.19% vs male 11.42%, p = 0.036) with no difference in new bone (p = 0.932).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective, single-arm (no comparator), specimen-level analysis; registered retrospectively.
- **Setting / period**: single centre, Can Tho University of Medicine and Pharmacy Hospital, Vietnam; January 2022 – February 2024.
- **Inclusion**: residual bone height ≤4 mm, prosthetic space ≥5 mm, consenting adults requiring lateral-window sinus floor augmentation.
- **Exclusion**: acute/chronic maxillary sinusitis, bleeding disorders, diabetes mellitus, bone metabolic disease, periodontal disease, malignancy, anticoagulant/NSAID/glucocorticoid use, smokers, pregnancy.
- **n**: 42 patients → **60 grafted sites** (some patients contributed >1 site); analyses performed at specimen level; clustering acknowledged but no multilevel model applied.
- **Sample size**: n = Z²·p(1−p)/d² with Z = 1.96, p = 9.09% (from a previous study), d = 0.0231.
- **Imaging**: CBCT cross-sections perpendicular to the ridge; RBH measured by two calibrated examiners.
- **Surgery**: crestal incision, piezotome lateral window, Schneiderian membrane elevation; graft = **2:1 allograft (corticocancellous bone, HC Biologics, USA) : synthetic substitute (30% HA/70% β-TCP, Osteon II, Dentium, Korea)** + autologous PRF; prophylactic amoxicillin/clavulanate 1 g, 2% lidocaine with epinephrine 1:100,000.
- **PRF protocol**: Choukroun; venous blood in glass tubes without anticoagulant, 2700 rpm × 12 min (DM0412, DLAB); clot compressed into membranes in a PRF box; **two PRF membranes per site** mixed into the graft.
- **Biopsy**: at **6 months**, 3 mm trephine core from the planned implant site immediately before implant placement.
- **Processing**: 10% buffered formalin → EDTA decalcification → paraffin → ~5 μm sections → H&E.
- **Histomorphometry**: **three non-consecutive sections** (coronal, middle, apical) per core; ImageJ polygon segmentation; two blinded independent examiners with prior intra/inter-examiner reproducibility checks. Non-mineralized area (including vascular/marrow spaces) classified as connective tissue; processing artifacts excluded.
- **Outcomes**: % newly formed bone (NB), % residual graft (R), % connective tissue (CT); histologic quality descriptors; ISQ at placement (ISQ1) and at 6 months (ISQ2); complications.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 26.0; Shapiro–Wilk + Levene; descriptive mean ± SD, 95% CI, ranges; independent t-test by sex; Pearson (or Spearman when non-normal) correlations; exploratory one-way ANOVA across tissue components; compositional dependency and proportional-sum problem explicitly flagged as limiting inference; two-tailed, α = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Cohort**: 42 patients / 60 sites; age 53.57 ± 12.90 y (23–72); 16 male (38.1%), 26 female (61.9%); RBH 2.31 ± 0.93 mm (0.80–3.90) — severely atrophic by design.
- **Histomorphometry at 6 months (n = 60 cores)**:

| Component | Mean ± SD (%) | 95% CI | Range (%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Newly formed bone (NB) | 31.28 ± 17.71 | 26.70–35.85 | 2.87–72.19 |
| Connective tissue (CT) | 53.23 ± 18.74 | 48.38–58.07 | 10.70–90.22 |
| Residual graft material (R) | 15.48 ± 12.33 | 12.29–18.68 | 0.00–45.09 |

- **Qualitative histology**: residual particles consistently enveloped by newly formed bone and marrow; abundant vascularization; **no acute/chronic inflammatory infiltrate, no foreign-body reaction, no necrosis**; newly formed lamellar bone with osteocytes in lacunae and Haversian canals (mature secondary trabecular bone); graft periphery showed a "seamless mineralized interface" with numerous osteoblast/osteoclast pairs (ongoing remodeling); central portions of residual particles had empty osteocyte lacunae.
- **Sex comparison (independent t-test)**: NB 31.03 ± 19.83 (M) vs 31.44 ± 16.43 (F), p = 0.932; R 11.42 ± 9.92 vs 18.19 ± 13.18, **p = 0.036**; CT 57.53 ± 19.03 vs 50.35 ± 18.25, p = 0.148.
- **Compositional correlations (Pearson)**: NB vs R r = −0.262 (p = 0.043); NB vs CT r = −0.772 (p < 0.0001); R vs CT r = −0.412 (p = 0.001) — authors caution these may partly be mathematical (components sum to 100%).
- **Implant stability**: ISQ1 58.20 ± 6.47 (44–70) → ISQ2 at 6 months 74.85 ± 7.68 (61–94).
- **ISQ1 correlations (Spearman)**: RBH0 r = 0.626 (p < 0.001); NB % r = 0.405 (p = 0.001); CT % r = −0.627 (p < 0.001).
- **Complications / survival**: 3 intraoperative Schneiderian membrane perforations (5.0%), all managed intraoperatively; no postoperative infection; no early implant failures; **100% implant and prosthetic survival at 1 year**.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Single-arm, no comparator** — cannot attribute outcomes to allograft vs β-TCP/HA vs PRF, and cannot claim superiority/equivalence vs xenograft, autograft or other substitutes; the authors themselves label the findings descriptive rather than causal.
- **PRF applied to every site** — its independent effect is unmeasurable in this design; future RCTs should include ±PRF arms.
- **Single time point (6 months)** — no resorption/formation kinetics; time-series histology (3/6/12 months) plus micro-CT suggested.
- **2-D area fraction from 3 mm cores** — small-core sampling may not represent the whole grafted sinus; volumetric imaging recommended.
- **Compositional data** (NB + R + CT = 100%) — correlations and the one-way ANOVA are exploratory only; no compositional transformation (e.g. log-ratio) applied.
- **Clustering** — 60 sites from 42 patients analysed at specimen level without a multilevel/cluster-adjusted model.
- **Retrospective trial registration** (NCT07186166, 30 Aug 2025) for a study enrolling from Jan 2022.
- Single centre, single operator pool, single manufacturer combination; generalizability to other HA:β-TCP ratios or PRF preparations is untested.

## 7. Glossary

- **LWSFA**: Lateral Window Sinus Floor Augmentation — sinus floor elevation through an antral wall window, indicated when residual bone height <5 mm.
- **RBH**: Residual Bone Height — crest-to-sinus-floor vertical distance on CBCT; ≤4 mm here (mean 2.31 mm).
- **β-TCP**: β-tricalcium phosphate — faster-resorbing synthetic calcium phosphate; here paired with HA as a 30/70 biphasic ceramic (Osteon II).
- **HA**: hydroxyapatite — slowly resorbable mineral phase providing mechanical/space-maintaining scaffold.
- **BCP**: Biphasic Calcium Phosphate — a controlled HA:β-TCP mixture; ratio governs resorption rate and ion release.
- **PRF**: Platelet-Rich Fibrin — autologous fibrin matrix with platelets, leukocytes and growth factors (PDGF, VEGF, TGF-β), prepared per the Choukroun protocol.
- **NB / RG / CT**: Newly formed Bone / Residual Graft material / Connective Tissue — the three mutually exclusive histomorphometric compartments summing to 100%.
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient — resonance-frequency-analysis stability index (1–100); ISQ1 at placement, ISQ2 at 6 months.
- **Haversian canal**: central vascular canal of an osteon; its presence in newly formed bone indicates mature secondary (lamellar) bone.
- **ImageJ polygon segmentation**: manual region-of-interest tracing used here to assign area fractions to NB, RG and CT on each section.
