## Why Ingested

ICAR:RS 2016은 비강부비동염(ARS, CRS, CRSwNP/CRSsNP, RARS, AECRS, 소아 RS)의 진단·병태생리·치료를 140개 주제로 정리한 최초의 다국적 합의문서로, 치성상악동염(ODS)·상악동거상술 합병증·치과 임플란트 관련 부비동염을 이해하는 근거 베이스가 된다. 기존 [[oral-medicine/odontogenic-sinusitis/psillas-2020-odontogenic-maxillary-sinusitis-comprehensive-review]]의 ODS 종설이 ICAR:RS의 치성 감염 파트(V.B.1.e)를 인용·확장하고 있어 본 원문 합의문을 직접 인제스트해 1차 근거로 삼는다.

## Three-line Summary

International Consensus Statement (ICAR:RS) by 75 global rhinology experts synthesizing evidence-based reviews with recommendations (EBRRs) for 140+ topics across all rhinosinusitis subtypes (ARS, RARS, CRSsNP, CRSwNP, AECRS, pediatric RS).
Evidence grades assigned per topic using Oxford LOE system; aggregate grades predominantly C (low-level evidence), highlighting significant gaps in high-quality RCTs and meta-analyses for most RS management decisions.
Provides the first comprehensive, critically appraised evidence compendium for RS, establishing baseline recommendations while explicitly identifying research priorities to upgrade the evidence base.

## 세줄요약

전 세계 75명의 비과 전문가가 참여한 국제합의문서(ICAR:RS)로, 140개 이상의 주제에 대해 근거기반 리뷰 및 권고(EBRR)를 수행해 급성/만성/재발성/소아 비강부비동염 전체를 아우른다.
각 주제별 Oxford LOE로 증거등급을 매겼으나 종합 등급이 대다수 C등급(저수준 근거)에 머물러, 대부분의 RS 진료 결정에 대한 고품질 RCT·메타분석 부재를 명확히 드러낸다.
RS 진단·병태생리·치료의 첫 번째 포괄적 근거 총서를 제공하면서, 향후 연구 우선순위를 제시해 근거 수준을 끌어올릴 방향을 명시한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **First global consensus on rhinosinusitis** integrating allergy and rhinology perspectives with 75 authors from 19 countries across 6 continents.
2. **Structured EBRR methodology**: 140+ clinical questions addressed via evidence-based reviews with recommendations, using anonymous iterative writing and consensus review — minimizing expert opinion bias.
3. **Comprehensive subtype coverage**: ARS, RARS, CRSsNP, CRSwNP, AECRS, pediatric RS, and special considerations (immunodeficiency, cystic fibrosis, pregnancy, etc.).
4. **Evidence grading transparency**: Each recommendation tagged with Oxford Level of Evidence (LOE) and aggregate Grade of Evidence (A–D), revealing that most RS recommendations rest on Grade C evidence (level 3–4 studies).
5. **Odontogenic sinusitis recognition**: Section V.B.1.e explicitly addresses odontogenic infections as contributing factor for ARS (aggregate Grade C), citing periapical abscess–sinus microbiological concordance and implant penetration data.
6. **Knowledge gaps map**: Section XIII catalogs specific research deficiencies (e.g., ARS antibiotic RCTs, CRS biologics phenotyping, pediatric RS definitions) to guide future high-impact studies.
7. **Clinical utility**: Diagnostic criteria, medical therapy algorithms (INCS, antibiotics, saline, steroids), surgical indications (ESS), and postoperative care — all evidence-graded.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Panel composition**: 75 primary authors (rhinology/otolaryngology/allergy leaders) + 46 contributing authors; multidisciplinary international representation.
- **Topic development**: 140+ clinical questions formulated across 13 sections (Definitions, Burden, ARS, RARS, CRSsNP, CRSwNP, AECRS, Surgery, Pediatric, Special Considerations, Knowledge Gaps).
- **Evidence review process**:
  - Systematic literature search per topic (PubMed, Cochrane, Embase)
  - Evidence-based reviews (EBRs) where evidence insufficient for recommendations
  - Evidence-based reviews with recommendations (EBRRs) where sufficient evidence existed
  - Oxford Centre for Evidence-Based Medicine LOE system (1a–5) applied to each study
  - Aggregate Grade of Evidence (A: consistent level 1; B: consistent level 2–3; C: level 4–5; D: expert opinion)
- **Consensus process**: Stepwise anonymous writing → iterative review by all authors → final consensus on recommendations and evidence grades.
- **Limitations acknowledged**: Not a meta-analysis; not a clinical practice guideline (CPG); does not define standard of care; recommendations context-dependent on evidence robustness.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Section | Topics Covered | Key Evidence Grade Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **III. Definitions** | RS subtypes (ARS, ABRS, AVRS, CRSsNP, CRSwNP, RARS, AECRS, pediatric RS) | Consensus definitions (Grade D) |
| **IV. Burden** | Prevalence (CRS ~10–15% US), QoL impact (SNOT-22), economic cost ($60B+ US) | Level 2b–3b (Grade B–C) |
| **V. ARS** | Diagnosis (clinical, duration >10d), viral vs bacterial differentiation, odontogenic factor (Grade C), antibiotics (Grade B for ABRS), INCS, saline | Mixed B–C |
| **VI. RARS** | Definition (≥4 episodes/yr), workup (CT, allergy, immune), medical/surgical management | Mostly Grade C–D |
| **VII. CRSsNP** | Pathophysiology (biofilm, barrier, osteitis), diagnostics (CT Lund-Mackay, endoscopy), medical therapy (INCS Grade A, antibiotics Grade C, saline Grade B), surgery (ESS Grade B) | A for INCS; B–C for rest |
| **VIII. CRSwNP** | Endotypes (Type 2 vs non-Type 2), biologics (anti-IL4/5/13 emerging), medical (INCS Grade A, systemic steroids Grade B), surgery (ESS Grade B), recurrence | A for INCS; B–C for biologics/surgery |
| **IX. AECRS** | Definition, microbiology, treatment (antibiotics + INCS + saline) | Grade C–D |
| **X. Surgery** | ESS indications, techniques (uncinectomy, maxillary antrostomy, frontal/sphenoid/ethmoid), navigation, outcomes (SNOT-22 improvement), complications | Grade B for ESS efficacy |
| **XI. Pediatric RS** | Definitions (adenoiditis overlap), diagnostics, medical (INCS Grade B, antibiotics Grade C), adenoidectomy Grade B, ESS Grade C | B–C |
| **XII. Special Considerations** | Immunodeficiency, CF, ciliary dyskinesia, AERD, fungal RS, pregnancy, aspirin desensitization | Mostly Grade C–D |
| **XIII. Knowledge Gaps** | 30+ specific research priorities (e.g., ARS antibiotic RCT, CRS endotype-driven biologics, pediatric definitions, odontogenic RS prospective studies) | — |

**Notable dental-relevant findings**:
- Odontogenic ARS: Aggregate Grade C (6 level-4 studies); oroantral fistula only independent predictor (Bomeli 2009); periapical abscess–sinus microbiological concordance demonstrated.
- Implant penetration <3 mm not associated with RS (Abi Najm 2013, n=70, 20-yr retrospective).
- Microbiology of odontogenic RS: anaerobic organisms more prevalent.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Evidence base predominantly low-level**: Aggregate Grade C for most recommendations; few Level 1a/1b studies.
- **Not a CPG**: Lacks stakeholder review, cost-effectiveness analysis, patient preference integration required for guidelines.
- **Heterogeneity within RS subtypes**: CRS especially variable (symptom burden, imaging extent, treatment history) — single recommendations cannot capture all phenotypes.
- **Rapidly evolving biologics landscape**: CRSwNP biologics (dupilumab, omalizumab, mepolizumab) evidence post-dates this 2016 consensus.
- **Odontogenic RS evidence gap**: Only 6 level-4 studies; no prospective studies; ICAR:RS explicitly calls for "well-designed investigation into the role of odontogenic infections in ARS" (Section XIII).
- **Pediatric RS definitions inconsistent**: Adenoiditis vs RS distinction remains problematic.
- **No formal cost-effectiveness or health-economic modeling** included.

## 6. Related Work

- **EPOS 2012/2020** (European Position Paper on Rhinosinusitis and Nasal Polyps) — parallel European consensus; ICAR:RS provides North American/allergy perspective with explicit evidence grading.
- **Rosenfeld 2015 AAO-HNSF Clinical Practice Guideline: Adult Sinusitis** — CPG derived from systematic review; ICAR:RS broader (all subtypes, pediatric, surgery) but not a CPG.
- **Fokkens 2020 EPOS2020** — updated European consensus with endotype-driven CRSwNP management.
- **Psillas 2020** [[oral-medicine/odontogenic-sinusitis/psillas-2020-odontogenic-maxillary-sinusitis-comprehensive-review]] — comprehensive ODS review citing ICAR:RS odontogenic section.
- **Lin 2024** [[oral-medicine/odontogenic-sinusitis/lin-2024-odontogenic-maxillary-sinusitis-expert-consensus]] — expert consensus on ODS management referencing ICAR:RS.
- **Kim 2019** [[oral-medicine/odontogenic-sinusitis/kim-2019-definition-management-odontogenic-maxillary]] — ODS definition/management framework.

## 7. Glossary

- **ICAR:RS**: International Consensus Statement on Allergy and Rhinology: Rhinosinusitis
- **EBRR**: Evidence-Based Review with Recommendations
- **EBR**: Evidence-Based Review (insufficient evidence for recommendations)
- **LOE**: Level of Evidence (Oxford CEBM 2011: 1a–5)
- **Aggregate Grade of Evidence**: A (consistent level 1), B (consistent level 2–3), C (level 4–5), D (expert opinion)
- **ARS**: Acute Rhinosinusitis (<4 weeks)
- **ABRS**: Acute Bacterial Rhinosinusitis
- **AVRS**: Acute Viral Rhinosinusitis
- **CRS**: Chronic Rhinosinusitis (>12 weeks)
- **CRSsNP**: CRS without nasal polyps
- **CRSwNP**: CRS with nasal polyps
- **RARS**: Recurrent Acute Rhinosinusitis (≥4 episodes/year)
- **AECRS**: Acute Exacerbation of Chronic Rhinosinusitis
- **ODS**: Odontogenic Sinusitis (maxillary sinusitis of dental origin)
- **ESS**: Endoscopic Sinus Surgery
- **INCS**: Intranasal Corticosteroid Spray
- **SNOT-22**: Sino-Nasal Outcome Test-22 (QoL instrument)
- **Lund-Mackay**: CT staging score for sinus opacification (0–24)
- **AERD**: Aspirin-Exacerbated Respiratory Disease
- **MCC**: Mucociliary Clearance
- **OMC**: Ostiomeatal Complex
