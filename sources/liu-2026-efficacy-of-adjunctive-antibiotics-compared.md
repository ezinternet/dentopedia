## Why Ingested
New 2026 antibiotic meta-analysis that sits between the non-surgical SR [[implants/peri-implantitis/grusovin-2022-antibiotics-non-surgical-peri-implantitis-ma]] (6 RCTs) and the surgical SR [[implants/peri-implantitis/antonoglou-2025-do-systemic-antibiotics-offer-benefits]]; it extends the RCT pool to 22 trials and directly compares local vs systemic routes, but its mixed control arms and I² of 91–95% on the key outcomes need careful reading.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD420261328879; five databases to 15 January 2026) of 22 RCTs, 1028 patients (487 antibiotic, 541 non-antibiotic), testing adjunctive local or systemic antibiotics added to mechanical debridement for peri-implantitis against mechanical debridement alone or plus another non-antibiotic adjunct (probiotic, photodynamic therapy, chlorhexidine, enamel matrix derivative, iodine glycerin).

Pooled benefit was modest: PPD WMD −0.69 mm (95% CI −1.04 to −0.33; I² = 91%), CAL −0.55 mm (−1.07 to −0.04; I² = 95%), plaque score −4.07 (−6.80 to −1.33; I² = 0%), BOP −12.45 percentage points (−24.13 to −0.77; I² = 94%); bone level (WMD 0.03 mm, P = 0.39) and mPLI (P = 0.09) did not differ, and local and systemic routes gave similar PPD gains (−0.68 vs −0.70 mm).

Authors conclude the evidence does not support routine antibiotic use; key limitations are very high heterogeneity, pooled heterogeneous controls, no stratification by surgical vs non-surgical setting, unreported smoking/dose/duration data, and sparse adverse-event and resistance reporting.

## 세줄요약
체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA; PROSPERO CRD420261328879; 5개 데이터베이스, 2026년 1월 15일까지) — 22편 RCT, 환자 1,028명 (항생제군 487명, 비항생제군 541명). 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis)에서 기계적 변연청결 (Mechanical Debridement, MD)에 국소·전신 항생제를 추가한 효과를, MD 단독 또는 MD + 다른 비항생제 보조요법 (프로바이오틱스, 광역학요법, 클로르헥시딘 등)과 비교했다.

통합 이득은 작았다: 탐침 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) WMD −0.69 mm (95% CI −1.04 ~ −0.33; I² = 91%), 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) −0.55 mm (−1.07 ~ −0.04; I² = 95%), 치태 점수 (Plaque Score, PS) −4.07 (−6.80 ~ −1.33; I² = 0%), 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP) −12.45%p (−24.13 ~ −0.77; I² = 94%). 골 수준 (Bone Level, BL)은 WMD 0.03 mm (P = 0.39)로 차이가 없었고, 국소·전신 경로의 PPD 이득은 비슷했다 (−0.68 vs −0.70 mm).

저자들은 근거가 상시 사용을 뒷받침하지 않는다고 결론짓는다. 핵심 한계는 매우 높은 이질성, 이질적 대조군 통합, 외과/비외과 미구분, 흡연·용량·기간 자료 부재, 이상반응·내성 보고 빈약이다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: PLOS One 2026 (PONE-D-26-18120)
- **DOI**: 10.1371/journal.pone.0352311
- **Institution**: Not stated in the retrieved full text (included trials span 12 countries per the authors)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Pools 22 RCTs of adjunctive antibiotics for peri-implantitis with a predefined local vs systemic subgroup contrast, a larger RCT pool than the earlier non-surgical antibiotic SR of 6 RCTs.
- Pools heterogeneous non-antibiotic controls (debridement alone, plus probiotic, plus antimicrobial photodynamic therapy, plus chlorhexidine, enamel matrix derivative or iodine glycerin) in one primary comparison.
- Reports that local and systemic routes were comparable for PPD (between-subgroup P = 0.95, I² = 0%), while PS improved significantly only with local antibiotics.
- Finds no bone-level benefit, and recommends antibiotics only as selected adjuncts rather than routine therapy.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR + MA of RCTs, PRISMA 2020; Cochrane Risk of Bias tool; GRADEpro for certainty.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Scopus, Cochrane Library, plus ClinicalTrials.gov, inception to 15 January 2026; no language restriction.
- **Eligibility**: peri-implantitis defined as bone loss ≥2 mm + PPD ≥5 mm + BOP positive; antibiotic + debridement vs non-antibiotic control; RCTs only.
- **n**: 7,984 records identified, 2,086 duplicates removed (5,898 left), 5,544 discarded as ineligible types, 296 more excluded on manual screening, 22 RCTs included; 1028 patients.
- **Antibiotics used**: minocycline, amoxicillin, metronidazole, azithromycin, doxycycline (tetracycline ointment in one trial).
- **Statistics**: WMD with 95% CI; random effects (DerSimonian-Laird) when I² > 50%, otherwise fixed; Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman check; univariate meta-regression (region, age, male proportion, follow-up, year, sample size) for PPD and BOP; sequential leave-one-out sensitivity for PPD; Egger test.
- **Outcomes**: BL, BOP, PPD, CAL, mPLI, PS.

| BOP (% sites) | 11 | −12.45 (−24.13, −0.77), P = 0.04 | I² = 94% |
| Plaque score (%) | 9 | −4.07 (−6.80, −1.33), P = 0.004 | I² = 0% |
| CAL (mm) | 7 | −0.55 (−1.07, −0.04), P = 0.04 | I² = 95% |
| mPLI | 7 | −0.07 (−0.16, 0.01), P = 0.09 | I² = 26% |

- Route subgroups, PPD: local −0.68 (−1.14, −0.22), P = 0.004, I² = 93%; systemic −0.70 (−1.25, −0.15), P = 0.01, I² = 83%; between-subgroup P = 0.95.
- Route subgroups, PS: local −6.57 (−11.10, −2.04), P = 0.004; systemic −2.63 (−6.07, 0.80), P = 0.13. mPLI: systemic −0.39 (−0.68, −0.09), P = 0.01; local −0.05 (−0.13, 0.04), P = 0.31. BOP: local −18.23 (−37.41, 0.96), P = 0.06; systemic −4.47 (−15.22, 6.27), P = 0.41. CAL: local −0.26 (−0.69, 0.16), P = 0.23; systemic −0.94 (−2.02, 0.15), P = 0.09.
- Antibiotic + debridement vs debridement alone: CAL −0.84 (−1.55, −0.13), P = 0.02; PPD −0.73 (−1.19, −0.27), P = 0.002; PS −3.94 (−7.14, −0.74), P = 0.02; BOP −18.64 (−38.91, 1.63), P = 0.07.
- Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman: PPD MD −0.686, P = 0.0009; BOP MD −12.46, P = 0.0445; CAL MD −0.552, P = 0.0675 (not significant under this method).
- Follow-up pattern as described by the authors: short-term gains in BOP/PS/PPD, mid-term attachment gain, 12-month CAL and PPD benefit.
- Meta-regression: no covariate explained PPD heterogeneity (R² 0.00%); for BOP region explained R² = 42.03% but P > 0.05; residual I² > 89%.
- Sensitivity (leave-one-out, PPD): result stable. Egger test P = 0.62.
- GRADE: certainty for PPD rated high by the authors (details in supplement, not in the retrieved text).
- Adverse events: only 6 of 22 trials gave detailed data; gastrointestinal disturbance in 4–38% with systemic amoxicillin, metronidazole or azithromycin; local pain or soreness in up to 31% after local minocycline; no life-threatening events.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Very high heterogeneity (I² 91–95% for PPD, BOP, CAL) not explained by meta-regression.
- Control arms pooled across debridement alone, probiotics, photodynamic therapy, chlorhexidine, enamel matrix derivative and iodine glycerin; a pre-specified sensitivity analysis excluding active-control trials is announced in the Methods, but its result is not reported in the retrieved text.
- The text does not separate surgical from non-surgical debridement contexts, although the Introduction criticises earlier reviews for mixing them.
- Smoking: of 22 trials, 10 reported it, 8 used it only as exclusion, 4 did not mention it; no smoking subgroup analyses. Antibiotic dose and duration, implant surface, baseline severity and diabetes were not analysable.
- Diagnostic thresholds varied across trials, and older trials may have used looser definitions.
- Antibiotic resistance was not monitored in any included trial; microbiological outcomes inconsistent.
- BOP and PS are expressed as percentage-point differences; the Conclusion section names "systemic antibiotics" although the pooled analysis covers local and systemic routes.
- Authors call for long-term trials with standardised diagnostic criteria, adverse-event reporting and resistance monitoring.

## 6. Related Work
- Grusovin 2022: earlier non-surgical antibiotic SR (6 RCTs); reported larger PPD gain with systemic than local antibiotics, whereas this review finds similar PPD gains across routes.
- Antonoglou 2025: surgical-setting SR of systemic antibiotics; found short-term success benefit that vanished at ≥3 years.
- Herrera 2023 (EFP S3 guideline): cautions against routine systemic antibiotics, in line with this review's conclusion.
- Baus-Domínguez 2023 and other 2022–2025 meta-analyses cited in the Introduction as conflicting (surgical: local helped, systemic did not; pooled: systemic improved PPD and bone gain).

## 7. Glossary
- **WMD**: weighted mean difference; pooled difference between groups in the original units (mm, or percentage points for BOP and PS).
- **I²**: percentage of variability across trials due to heterogeneity rather than chance.
- **PS / mPLI**: plaque score (percentage of positive sites) / modified plaque index.
- **Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman**: random-effects variance adjustment giving more conservative confidence intervals.
