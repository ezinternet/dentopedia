## Three-line Summary

Kumari et al. (2026) registered a Cochrane protocol to systematically review the benefits and harms of non-invasive management strategies for molar-incisor hypomineralization (MIH) in children aged 6–14 years. This represents the first Cochrane-level initiative specifically targeting MIH, a condition characterised by enamel mineralisation defects affecting first permanent molars and potentially incisors, with uncertain aetiology and limited high-quality evidence for management.

The protocol covers a broad range of non-invasive interventions including fluoride varnish, gel, foam, and toothpaste; casein phosphopeptide-amorphous calcium phosphate (CPP-ACP); silver diamine fluoride (SDF); fissure sealants; resin infiltration; and desensitising agents. Primary outcomes will include caries increment, sensitivity reduction, enamel breakdown prevention, and patient-reported outcomes. As of the protocol stage, no results are available.

The clinical significance of this protocol lies in its potential to provide structured evidence for a condition currently managed largely by expert opinion and extrapolation from general caries prevention evidence. The 6–14 age window captures the critical period when first permanent molars erupt and when non-invasive strategies may prevent or delay invasive restorative intervention in affected teeth.

## 세줄요약

쿠마리 등(2026)은 소아(6–14세)의 구치절치석회화부전(Molar-Incisor Hypomineralization, MIH) 비침습 관리 전략의 편익과 위해를 체계적으로 평가하기 위한 코크란 프로토콜을 등록했다. MIH에 특화된 최초의 코크란 수준 근거 종합 시도로, 현재 임상 관리가 주로 전문가 의견과 일반적 우식 예방 근거의 외삽에 의존하는 상황을 반영한다.

검토 예정 비침습 중재에는 불소 바니시/젤/폼/치약, 카세인 포스포펩타이드-비결정성 인산칼슘(Casein Phosphopeptide-Amorphous Calcium Phosphate, CPP-ACP), 불화디암민은(Silver Diamine Fluoride, SDF), 치면열구전색제(Fissure Sealant), 레진 침투법(Resin Infiltration), 지각과민처치제가 포함된다. 현재 프로토콜 단계로 결과는 없다.

제1대구치 맹출 임계 시기인 6–14세 구간에서의 비침습 관리 성공 여부가 침습적 수복 치료 필요성을 결정하므로 임상적 중요성이 높다. 이 프로토콜이 완성되면 MIH 비침습 관리의 최고 수준 근거가 될 것이다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First Cochrane protocol specifically targeting non-invasive MIH management
- Population defined as children 6–14 years — the critical clinical window for first permanent molar eruption and management
- Covers range of non-invasive agents: fluoride products, CPP-ACP, SDF, sealants, resin infiltration
- Will provide structured evidence synthesis to guide clinical decisions in a condition currently managed by expert opinion

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Population: children 6–14 years with MIH (≥1 first permanent molar affected)
- Interventions: fluoride varnish/gel/foam/toothpaste; CPP-ACP; SDF; fissure sealants; resin infiltration; desensitizing agents
- Status: awaiting RCT identification and data extraction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Protocol — no results yet.
Outcomes to be assessed: caries increment, sensitivity reduction, enamel breakdown prevention, patient-reported outcomes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Protocol stage
- RCT evidence for MIH-specific management is sparse and heterogeneous
- MIH diagnosis criteria have varied across studies — standardization needed

## 7. Glossary
- 구치절치석회화부전 (Molar-Incisor Hypomineralization, MIH): 전신적 원인에 의한 1개 이상 제1대구치의 에나멜 무기화 결함; 절치 동반 가능
- CPP-ACP (카세인 포스포펩타이드-비결정성 인산칼슘, Casein Phosphopeptide-Amorphous Calcium Phosphate): 재광화 촉진 제품 (Tooth Mousse, GC)
- 비침습 관리 (Non-invasive Management): 삭제 없이 치아를 관리하는 접근법 — 불소, 실런트, 재광화제 사용
