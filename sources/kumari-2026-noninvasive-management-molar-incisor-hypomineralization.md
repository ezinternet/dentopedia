## Why Ingested
MIH (구치절치석회화부전, Molar-Incisor Hypomineralization) 비침습 관리에 대한 최초 Cochrane Protocol. 기존 [[caries/worthington-2024-sdf-preventing-managing-dental-caries]] SDF 근거와 연계해 비침습 우식 관리 스펙트럼 확장. MIH는 소아 치의학에서 임상적으로 중요한 미해결 과제.

## One-line Summary
Cochrane Protocol (2026, Kumari et al.): to assess benefits and harms of non-invasive management strategies for MIH in children aged 6–14 years — no results yet.

## 한줄요약
코크란 프로토콜 (2026): 소아(6–14세) 구치절치석회화부전 (Molar-Incisor Hypomineralization, MIH) 비침습 관리 전략 효과·위해 평가 예정 — 결과 없음.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2026 (Protocol)
- DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD016369
- PMID: 42454625 | PMC: PMC13370776
- Status: Protocol — full review pending

## 2. Key Contributions
- First Cochrane protocol specifically targeting non-invasive MIH management
- Population defined as children 6–14 years — the critical clinical window for first permanent molar eruption and management
- Covers range of non-invasive agents: fluoride products, CPP-ACP, SDF, sealants, resin infiltration
- Will provide structured evidence synthesis to guide clinical decisions in a condition currently managed by expert opinion

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Population: children 6–14 years with MIH (≥1 first permanent molar affected)
- Interventions: fluoride varnish/gel/foam/toothpaste; CPP-ACP; SDF; fissure sealants; resin infiltration; desensitizing agents
- Status: awaiting RCT identification and data extraction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Protocol — no results yet.
Outcomes to be assessed: caries increment, sensitivity reduction, enamel breakdown prevention, patient-reported outcomes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Protocol stage
- RCT evidence for MIH-specific management is sparse and heterogeneous
- MIH diagnosis criteria have varied across studies — standardization needed

## 7. Glossary
- 구치절치석회화부전 (Molar-Incisor Hypomineralization, MIH): 전신적 원인에 의한 1개 이상 제1대구치의 에나멜 무기화 결함; 절치 동반 가능
- CPP-ACP (카세인 포스포펩타이드-비결정성 인산칼슘, Casein Phosphopeptide-Amorphous Calcium Phosphate): 재광화 촉진 제품 (Tooth Mousse, GC)
- 비침습 관리 (Non-invasive Management): 삭제 없이 치아를 관리하는 접근법 — 불소, 실런트, 재광화제 사용
