## Why Ingested

Provides a mechanistic narrative review of how hyperlipidemia disrupts osseointegration (Wnt/β-catenin, PPARγ, RANKL pathways) and systematically catalogs statin-based local delivery strategies (direct coating, hydrogels, nanoparticles) tested in animal models. Directly extends [[implants/survival/aghaloo-2019-systemic-diseases-medications-implant-osseointegration-sr]], which identifies hyperlipidemia as a risk factor but does not detail surface-modification countermeasures.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (MEDLINE/EMBASE, 1985–2023) examining hyperlipidemia's effects on peri-implant bone biology and cataloging implant modification strategies to restore osseointegration.
Hyperlipidemia suppresses osteoblast differentiation via Wnt/β-catenin inhibition and upregulated PPARγ, promotes osteoclastogenesis via RANKL, and elevates ROS; hypercholesterolemia was linked to peri-implantitis and implant failure (OR = 5.1) in one two-year clinical study.
Simvastatin surface coating on roughened titanium consistently promotes osseointegration in hyperlipidemic animal models, though drug loading efficiency is low; advanced delivery vehicles (PLGA microspheres, hydrogels, nanoparticles, cerium oxide coatings) show promise but lack dental-implant-specific clinical validation.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Mechanistic synthesis**: Maps three interlocking signaling axes by which hyperlipidemia impairs osseointegration — (a) Wnt/β-catenin inhibition (LDL↑, HDL↓ → MSC → adipocyte bias, osteoblast suppression via BMP2-Wnt); (b) PPARγ upregulation by oxidized LDL → suppresses Runx2/Osx, promotes osteoclast differentiation; (c) NF-κB and ROS-driven pro-inflammatory milieu (IL-6, TNF-α, RANKL) accelerating peri-implant bone resorption.
2. **Titanium-specific hazard**: TiO₂ surface oxidizes circulating LDL to oxLDL, amplifying local osteoclast activation and inflammation in hyperlipidemic patients — an underappreciated implant material–host interaction.
3. **Three-route strategy framework**: Categorizes local drug delivery into (a) peri-implant injection, (b) implant surface modification, and (c) bone grafting material modification — with statin-based approaches reviewed for each.
4. **Simvastatin evidence summary**: Direct roughened-surface coating (micro-arc oxidation, mesoporous TiO₂, biomimetic calcium phosphate) promotes osseointegration in hyperlipidemic animal models; alginate hydrogel and nanohydroxyapatite carriers extend release duration.
5. **Emerging platform**: Cerium oxide nano-catalytic coating reduces ROS, protects osteoblasts under oxidative stress, and improves BMP/TGF-β-mediated bone formation — proposed as a promising antioxidant strategy specific to hyperlipidemia.
6. **Clinical signal**: A two-year clinical follow-up found hypercholesterolemia independently associated with peri-implantitis and implant failure (OR = 5.1, P = 0.046), contrasting with a retrospective cohort reporting no influence — highlighting the need for controlled prospective data.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Narrative (non-systematic) review; no PRISMA, no formal risk-of-bias assessment.
- **Animal models cited**: New Zealand rabbits, Sprague-Dawley rats, mice — high-fat diet-induced hyperlipidemia; implants placed in femur/tibia/jaw.
- **Outcome measures**: Bone–implant contact (BIC), peri-implant bone volume/fill rate, push-out strength, histomorphometry, micro-CT.
- **Drug delivery routes reviewed**: Local injection (fluvastatin+PGA gel, PLGA microspheres); surface coating (simvastatin on micro-arc oxidized surface, mesoporous TiO₂, calcium phosphate, alginate hydrogel, nanohydroxyapatite); bone graft modification (HPMC granules, PLGA-PEG nanoparticle bioceramic scaffold, methylcellulose gel).
- **Limitation acknowledged**: No systematic selection or quality grading of included studies; evidence base is predominantly preclinical animal experiments.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Intervention | Model | Key finding |
|---|---|---|
| Simvastatin, micro-arc oxidized surface | Hyperlipidemic rat | Improved BIC vs uncoated Ti |
| Mesoporous TiO₂ + simvastatin | In vitro / animal | Mesopores enhance drug loading and osseointegration |
| Fluvastatin+PGA gel, local injection | Rat tibia | Superior histomorphometry and push-in strength vs control |
| PLGA microspheres + fluvastatin | Animal | Improved osseointegration and bone mechanical properties |
| Vitamin D surface coating | Hyperlipidemic model | Reduced alveolar bone loss, but promotion effect "very limited" |
| Cerium oxide coating | Osteoporotic/oxidative-stress model | Reduced ROS, protected osteoblast differentiation, BMP/TGF-β upregulation |
| Hypercholesterolemia (clinical) | 2-yr follow-up humans | OR 5.1 for peri-implantitis/failure (P = 0.046) |
| Hyperlipidemia (clinical cohort) | Retrospective | No influence on implants (contradictory result) |

**Key pathways**:
- LDL↑/HDL↓ → Wnt/β-catenin inhibition → BMP2 ↓ → osteoblast ↓
- PPARγ↑ → Runx2/Osx ↓ + osteoclast ↑
- TiO₂ + LDL → oxLDL → osteoclast activation + ROS ↑
- NF-κB → IL-6, IL-10, TNF-α, RANKL; macrophage M1 polarization

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All surface modification and local injection data from animal models; no RCT or clinical cohort specifically targeting hyperlipidemic implant patients.
- Clinical evidence contradictory (one positive, one null association with failure).
- Drug loading efficiency of direct simvastatin coating on roughened Ti is low; uneven distribution complicates BIC interpretation.
- Genetic engineering approaches (miR-29a-3p, lncRNA) raise ethical barriers to clinical application.
- Cerium oxide and advanced nanoparticle systems (PLGA-PEG, hydrogels) not yet tested in dental-implant–specific models under hyperlipidemia.
- Authors recommend: delayed loading, high initial stability compression, antibiotic prophylaxis, and pre-surgical lipid control as interim clinical guidance.

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Osseointegration (골유착, OI) | Direct structural and functional integration of implant surface with surrounding bone, without fibrous tissue ingrowth |
| HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor (스타틴) | Drug class that blocks cholesterol synthesis; promotes osteogenesis as pleiotropic effect |
| Simvastatin (심바스타틴) | Most studied statin for implant surface modification; activates Ras-PI3K/Akt/MAPK, upregulates BMP-2, Runx2 |
| Wnt/β-catenin pathway | Key osteogenic signaling cascade inhibited by elevated LDL/reduced HDL |
| PPARγ | Nuclear receptor upregulated by oxidized LDL; suppresses osteoblast transcription factors Runx2/Osx |
| RANKL/OPG axis | Osteoclast regulation system; statins upregulate OPG and downregulate RANKL to reduce bone resorption |
| Micro-arc oxidation (MAO) | Electrochemical surface treatment creating rough TiO₂ layer with micropores suitable for drug loading |
| Cerium oxide (CeO₂, 세리아) | Nano-catalytic antioxidant; scavenges ROS, promotes osteoblast activity, modulates macrophage M1/M2 balance |
| BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact, 골-임플란트 접촉률) | Histomorphometric measure of osseointegration quality |
| oxLDL (산화 저밀도 지단백) | Oxidized LDL; activates osteoclasts and promotes inflammatory cytokine release |
