## Why Ingested

zenith sweep 중 임플란트 협측 정점 (buccal zenith)의 연조직 높이·두께를 직접 측정한 유일한 RCT다. 임플란트-지대주 연결 각도(5° 원뿔 vs 45°)라는 레버를 다뤄, 지대주 표면·분리 횟수·형태를 비교한 [[overviews/abutment-emergence-profile-peri-implant-tissue-overview]]에 "연결부 설계" 축을 추가하고, 원뿔 연결 비교 문헌 [[implants/mbl/stacchi-2023-marginal-bone-changes-platform-switched-conical]]의 연조직 측면을 보완한다. 5° 군 임플란트가 국산(MegaGen AnyRidge)이라 원내 임상과도 직접 관련된다.

## Three-line Summary

Single-centre RCT (Italy, 2011–2013) of 104 patients / 188 bone-level, platform-switched implants in healed ridges randomized (coin toss, per implant) to a 5° conical internal-hex connection (Anyridge, MegaGen; n=125) or a 45° internal-hex connection (Core, Kristal; n=63); after 12 months of provisionalization, buccal-zenith mucosal height (MH) and thickness (MT) were measured digitally on scanned stone casts.

5° vs 45°: MH 3.32 ± 0.12 vs 2.70 ± 0.16 mm; MT 4.37 ± 0.16 vs 3.93 ± 0.18 mm; MT/MH ratio 1.50 ± 0.88 vs 1.81 ± 1.20 (overall 1.61); ratio significantly associated with group (p=0.047) and sector (p=0.015).

The 5° conical connection supported taller and thicker buccal-zenith mucosa, but the analysis (Pearson correlation, implant-level, no clustering adjustment, unbalanced groups, posterior-dominant sample, retrospective registration) makes this hypothesis-generating rather than practice-changing.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 RCT (이탈리아, 2011–2013): 환자 104명·골수준 플랫폼 스위칭 임플란트 188개(치유된 치조제)를 5° 원뿔 내부육각 연결 (AnyRidge, MegaGen; 125개) vs 45° 내부육각 연결 (Core, Kristal; 63개)에 동전 던지기로 임플란트 단위 무작위 배정; 12개월 임시수복 후 석고모형 스캔에서 협측 정점 (Buccal Zenith) 점막 높이 (Mucosal Height, MH)·두께 (Mucosal Thickness, MT) 디지털 계측.

5° vs 45°: MH 3.32 ± 0.12 vs 2.70 ± 0.16 mm, MT 4.37 ± 0.16 vs 3.93 ± 0.18 mm, MT/MH 비 1.50 ± 0.88 vs 1.81 ± 1.20(전체 1.61); 비율은 군(p=0.047)·부위(p=0.015)와 유의 연관.

5° 원뿔 연결에서 협측 정점 점막이 더 높고 두꺼웠으나, 통계(피어슨 상관·임플란트 단위·군집 미보정)·군 불균형·구치부 편중·사후 등록으로 가설 생성 수준.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health, 2020;20(1):53
- **Study type**: Randomized controlled trial (parallel, implant-level randomization)
- **Registration**: NCT04160689 (retrospectively registered 2019-11-13)
- **Ethics**: University of Insubria #826–0034086
- **Artifact**: PMC full text (JATS via PubMed MCP)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Extends the Nozawa (2006) height/thickness concept to a two-connection comparison at the buccal zenith.
- Reports higher MH and MT and a lower MT/MH ratio with a 5° conical connection than with a 45° internal hex.
- Provides a digital cast-superimposition method for measuring peri-implant mucosal dimensions.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Population**: partially edentulous adults 18–90 y, healed ridges (≥6 months post-extraction), good hygiene; excluded heavy smokers (>20/day), drugs causing gingival overgrowth, sites needing bone augmentation or soft-tissue grafting.
- **Allocation**: coin toss per implant (patients could have implants in both arms); both systems platform-switched; all placed at bone level (non-bone-level placements excluded from analysis).
- **Prosthetics**: screw-retained provisional for 12 months; individualized transfer impression copying the provisional emergence profile; type IV stone casts.
- **Measurement**: scan bodies on analogues, desktop scanner (3Shape D700), superimposition in Geomagic (three-point + best fit), sections along implant axis in GOM Inspect.
  - MH: vestibular shoulder of analogue → upper gingival margin of supra-implant tissue (along implant axis).
  - MT: vestibular shoulder → external mucosa point perpendicular to the implant axis.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 17; Pearson correlation between MH, MT, MT/MH, connection, sector, gender; α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | 5° conical (n=125) | 45° hex (n=63) |
|---|---|---|
| MH (mm) | 3.32 ± 0.12 | 2.70 ± 0.16 |
| MT (mm) | 4.37 ± 0.16 | 3.93 ± 0.18 |
| MT/MH | 1.50 ± 0.88 | 1.81 ± 1.20 |

- Overall: MT 4.22 mm, MH 3.11 mm, MT/MH 1.61 ± 1.0.
- MT/MH significantly correlated with group (p=0.047) and sector (p=0.015); group–sector correlation not significant (p=0.075).
- Sample: 104 patients (64 F / 40 M; mean age 66, range 43–88); sectors S1 upper anterior 15.4%, S2 lower anterior 1.1%, S3 upper posterior 34.0%, S4 lower posterior 49.5%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Statistics**: group differences were tested with Pearson correlation rather than a between-group test; implants clustered within patients (188/104) with no clustering adjustment.
- **Dispersion inconsistency**: the ± values for MH/MT (0.12–0.18 mm) are implausibly small for SDs next to ratio SDs of 0.88–1.20 — likely standard errors; not stated.
- **Ratio direction**: the text calls the ratio "MT/MH" in Results but "MH/MT" in the abstract/conclusions; the authors interpret a lower MT/MH as better, a reasoning step (via Nozawa) rather than an outcome measured.
- **Allocation**: coin-toss per implant produced 125 vs 63; no allocation concealment or examiner blinding described; retrospective registration.
- **Population**: ~84% posterior implants; only 29 upper anterior — esthetic-zone inference is limited.
- **Endpoint**: single 12-month cross-sectional measurement on stone casts; no baseline tissue thickness, recession, or MBL outcome.
- **Abutment height**: chosen per soft-tissue thickness (authors acknowledge confounding with MBL).
- **Systems**: two whole implant systems differ in more than connection angle (macro-design, platform switch magnitude), so the effect cannot be attributed to connection angle alone.

## 6. Related Work

- Nozawa 2006 (height/width ratio 1:1.5 around flat-to-flat internal hex; not held).
- Authors' pilot study (32 Anyridge implants: MH 3.44, MT 3.29 mm).
- Platform switching / conical connection MBL literature.

## 7. Glossary

- **Buccal zenith**: most coronal point of the buccal peri-implant mucosa, here measured along the implant axis.
- **MH / MT**: mucosal height / mucosal thickness at the buccal zenith.
- **Nozawa ratio**: peri-implant mucosal height-to-width relationship.
