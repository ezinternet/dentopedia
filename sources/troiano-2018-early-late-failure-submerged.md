## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis with trial sequential analysis (TSA) of 11 prospective randomized and non-randomized controlled studies directly comparing submerged vs non-submerged implant healing without immediate loading, searched in PubMed, Scopus, Embase and Web of Science.

Non-submerged healing carried a small but higher rate of early implant failure (+2%); late failure (after 6 months from placement) did not differ between the two protocols, but TSA judged the power of that evidence not high; at 1 year from loading, marginal bone level appeared slightly better preserved with non-submerged healing, though with a very small effect size (0.13 mm).

This is the wiki's only pooled and trial-sequenced estimate for the healing-protocol contrast, but the early-failure penalty is an absolute 2% difference, immediate-loading studies were excluded by design, and the MBL signal is explicitly underpowered — full text not retrieved here, so pooled effect measures and confidence intervals are unavailable.

## 세줄요약

체계적 고찰 + 메타분석 + 시험순차분석(Trial Sequential Analysis, TSA) — PubMed·Scopus·Embase·Web of Science에서 즉시 로딩을 제외하고 매몰형(Submerged) 대 비매몰형(Non-submerged) 치유를 직접 비교한 전향적 무작위·비무작위 대조군 연구 11편.

비매몰형 치유에서 조기 임플란트 실패(Early Implant Failure, EIF)율이 2% 더 높았음. 만기(Late) 실패(식립 6개월 이후)는 두 방식에서 차이가 없었으나 TSA 기준 근거의 검정력은 낮았고, 보철 로딩 1년 시점 주위골높이(Marginal Bone Level, MBL)는 비매몰형이 다소 보존에 유리했으나 효과크기(Effect Size)가 매우 작음(0.13 mm).

위키가 보유한 매몰 vs 비매몰 대조에 대한 유일한 풀링·TSA 추정치이나, 조기 실패 페널티는 절대값 2%에 불과하고 즉시 로딩 연구는 설계상 제외되었으며 MBL 근거는 저자 스스로 검정력 부족을 명시 — 전문 미확보 상태로 작성되어 풀링 효과크기·신뢰구간은 확인 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **First pooled + trial-sequenced estimate in this wiki of the submerged vs non-submerged healing contrast on implant failure.** The healing-protocol question in [[wiki/overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]] previously rested only on retrospective cohorts; this is the SR+MA stratum the overview itself flagged as missing.
- **Quantifies the early-failure penalty and assigns it to a specific arm**: non-submerged healing, +2%. The umbrella review [[wiki/implants/survival/giok-2026-factors-implant-failure-umbrella-review]] grades the submerged-vs-nonsubmerged contrast as high-certainty from RCTs but, being abstract-only, never says *which* arm carries the risk; this paper names it.
- **Early vs late failure are defined and analysed separately** (before vs after 6 months from placement) — the definitional clarity that the retrospective cohorts held here lack (e.g. Guarnieri uses "before or at abutment connection", Wåhlberg uses any event within year 1).
- **Reports a countervailing MBL signal against its own primary direction** — non-submerged healing preserved marginal bone *better* at 1 year (effect size 0.13 mm) — and explicitly labels the evidence power low. The paper is honest that its secondary outcome does not follow the failure outcome.
- **Applies trial sequential analysis and lets it downgrade a null**: the "no difference in late failure" result is reported together with a TSA verdict that the power of that evidence is not high, i.e. the authors distinguish absence of evidence from evidence of absence.
- **Excludes immediate loading by eligibility design**, which makes it a clean two-stage/non-loaded contrast but bounds how far the finding travels to current one-stage immediate-loading practice.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis + trial sequential analysis (SR+MA+TSA).
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, EMBASE, Web of Science (four databases; no Cochrane Library or Embase-only grey-literature channel is mentioned in the abstract).
- **Eligibility**: prospective randomized **and** non-randomized controlled studies addressing a **direct** comparison of submerged vs non-submerged implant healing, **without immediate loading**.
- **n**: 11 studies met the inclusion criteria.
- **Outcomes**: (a) early implant failure — before 6 months from implant placement; (b) late implant failure — after 6 months from placement; (c) marginal bone level (MBL) changes, reported at 1 year from implant load.
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane Collaboration Tool for Randomized Clinical Trials. (Applied to a body that also includes non-randomized trials — the abstract does not explain how.)
- **Synthesis**: meta-analysis performed, and the **power of the meta-analytic findings determined by trial sequential analysis** — the TSA is not a sensitivity annex but a co-primary analytic step used to qualify the pooled conclusions.
- **Design-mixing note (inference)**: pooling randomized and non-randomized controlled studies in one meta-analysis without stratification is implied by the abstract's eligibility wording; this is a standard source of pooled-estimate dilution, but the paper may have stratified in the full text (unverifiable here).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Result (as reported in the abstract) |
|---|---|
| Studies included | 11 |
| Early implant failure (<6 months from placement) | **Small higher rate (2%) with non-submerged healing**; abstract reports the difference as a rate, not as an OR/RR, and gives no CI |
| Late implant failure (>6 months from placement) | **No difference** between submerged and non-submerged healing |
| Late-failure evidence power (TSA) | **Not high** — TSA downgrades the null result |
| MBL at 1 year from implant load | **Non-submerged healing may better preserve marginal bone**; effect size **0.13 mm** (very small) |
| MBL evidence power | **Low** (authors' own words) |
| Immediate loading | Excluded by eligibility — no data on one-stage immediate loading |
| Authors' conclusion | Non-submerged implants carry a 2% higher early-failure risk; MBL results "seem to favour non-submerged healing, although with a very small effect size" |

**No p-values, confidence intervals, heterogeneity statistics or forest-plot values appear in the abstract.** The "2%" is reproduced here exactly as the abstract frames it — an absolute rate difference, not a relative effect estimate.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingest (retrieval limit).** No full text: per-study n, search strategy, pooled effect measure and CI, I², publication-bias testing, and the risk-of-bias results are all unavailable to this wiki. Obtaining the full text would materially upgrade this page — it is the concrete future-work item.
- **Small evidence base** — 11 studies, and pooled early-failure events for a 2% difference are necessarily few; TSA was applied precisely because of this, and its verdict on the late-failure comparison was "not high".
- **The primary result is clinically small.** A 2% absolute difference in early failure is at the edge of what changes chairside behaviour for a patient; the abstract does not supply an NNT, so the absolute per-patient implication cannot be computed here (inference).
- **The secondary outcome runs the other way.** Non-submerged healing preserved MBL *better* (0.13 mm). A reader who treats this paper as "submerged is better" has read half of it; the paper's own framing is that the failure and bone-level findings disagree.
- **The null on late failure is underpowered, not demonstrated.** TSA explicitly says the power is not high — this must not be cited as evidence that late failure is unaffected by healing protocol.
- **Immediate loading excluded.** The finding does not transfer directly to immediate-loading/one-stage protocols, which are where much contemporary practice sits.
- **Randomized and non-randomized trials pooled together** (inferred from the abstract's eligibility wording); randomization status is not stratified in the reported results.
- **Cochrane RCT risk-of-bias tool applied to a mixed-design body** without an explanation in the abstract (inference).
- **2018 evidence cutoff.** Roughly eight years of trial and long-term reporting have accumulated since; whether the 2% early-failure penalty has shrunk with modern surfaces, grafting and loading protocols is not assessable from this page (inference — requires a newer search).

## 7. Glossary

- **Submerged healing (매몰형 치유, 잠매몰)**: implant fully covered by the mucoperiosteal flap at placement, with the restorative connection deferred to a second surgery — the classic two-stage protocol.
- **Non-submerged healing (비매몰형 치유)**: implant left exposed at placement via a healing abutment (one-stage/transmucosal), with no second operation to close the flap over it.
- **Early implant failure (조기 임플란트 실패, EIF)**: loss of the implant before osseointegration completes — defined in this paper as failure **within 6 months of placement**.
- **Late implant failure (만기 임플란트 실패)**: failure **after 6 months from placement**, i.e. after the osseointegration window has closed; mechanistically a different event (peri-implantitis, overload) from early failure.
- **Trial sequential analysis (시험순차분석, TSA)**: a meta-analysis extension that adds the required-information-size and sequential-boundary lines, telling the reader whether the accumulated evidence has crossed the threshold that makes the pooled estimate reliable — or whether it is still compatible with random variation.
- **Marginal bone level (주위골높이, MBL)**: radiographic bone height measured relative to the implant platform/shoulder, the standard outcome for peri-implant bone change; reported here at 1 year from loading.
- **Cochrane Collaboration Tool for Randomized Clinical Trials**: the risk-of-bias instrument used to appraise the included trials.
