## Why Ingested

조직수준 (Tissue-Level) 임플란트에서 보철물 장착 후 18개월간 변연골 변화에 지대주/보철 인자(출현각, 경점막 칼라 높이)가 영향을 주는지를 직접 본 다기관 전향 연구다. 골수준 (Bone-Level) 임플란트에서 지대주 높이가 핵심 인자라는 [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]]·[[implants/mbl/tajti-2024-less-marginal-bone-loss]]의 결론이 조직수준에서는 그대로 옮겨지지 않음을 보여주고, 조직수준에서 출현각 연관이 없었다는 [[implants/peri-implantitis/katafuchi-2018-restoration-contour-is-a-risk]]를 독립 코호트로 재확인하며, 얇은 점막이 초기 골소실을 키운다는 [[implants/mbl/linkevicius-2009-reaction-crestal-bone-around-implants]]의 흐름과 맞닿는다. → [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]]

## Three-line Summary

Multicenter prospective study (six clinical centers; 47 patients/47 single posterior-mandibular tissue-level implants analysed, 46 functioning at 18 months) placed at three depths by vertical mucosal thickness (thin 2 mm subcrestal, medium 1 mm subcrestal, thick equicrestal), tracking marginal bone from crown delivery (T1) to 18 months of loading (T2) with multiple linear regression.

From T1 to T2 mean marginal bone resorption was 0.14 ± 0.56 mm overall (Group 1 0.41 ± 0.59, Group 2 0.08 ± 0.56, Group 3 bone gain 0.07 ± 0.43 mm), with no significant association with age, gender, smoking, periodontitis history, mucosal thickness, torque, collar height, emergence angle or crown width; from placement (T0) to T2, resorption was 1.05 ± 0.58 / 0.37 ± 0.64 / 0.25 ± 0.70 mm in thin/medium/thick mucosa, and thin mucosa (p = 0.007) and smoking (p = 0.028) were the significant predictors.

Around tissue-level implants the prosthetic variables tested did not drive bone change after crown delivery — the bone is largely committed during unsubmerged healing, so depth should be matched to mucosal thickness at surgery; limited by n = 47, one mandibular site, 2D periapical radiographs and 18 months.

## 세줄요약

다기관 전향 연구 (Multicenter Prospective Study, 6개 임상센터) — 후방 하악 단일 조직수준 (Tissue-Level) 임플란트 47명(18개월 시점 기능 중 46개)을 수직 점막 두께에 따라 3단계 깊이(얇음 2 mm 골하방·보통 1 mm 골하방·두꺼움 골정상)로 식립하고, 보철물 장착 (T1)부터 하중 18개월 (T2)까지 변연골 변화 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)를 다중 선형회귀로 분석.

T1–T2 평균 변연골 흡수는 전체 0.14 ± 0.56 mm (1군 0.41 ± 0.59, 2군 0.08 ± 0.56, 3군은 0.07 ± 0.43 mm 골 증가)였고 연령·성별·흡연·치주염 병력·점막 두께·식립 토크·경점막 칼라 높이 (Transmucosal Collar Height, TCH)·출현각 (Emergence Angle, EA)·크라운 근원심 폭과 유의한 연관이 없었으며, 식립 시점 (T0)–T2 흡수는 얇은/보통/두꺼운 점막에서 1.05 ± 0.58 / 0.37 ± 0.64 / 0.25 ± 0.70 mm로 얇은 점막 (p = 0.007)과 흡연 (p = 0.028)이 유의한 예측인자.

조직수준 임플란트에서는 검사한 보철 변수가 크라운 장착 후 골 변화를 좌우하지 않았다 — 골 변화는 비매몰 치유기에 대부분 결정되므로 수술 시 점막 두께에 맞춘 식립 깊이가 중요하며, 다만 n = 47·하악 단일 부위·2차원 치근단 방사선·18개월이라는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Continues a previously published 5-month trial (reference 20 in the paper; same patients) to 18 months of functional loading: the study isolates the post-crown period (T1 to T2) from the pre-crown healing period (T0 to T1).
- Tested prosthetic variables for tissue-level implants directly: crown mesio-distal dimension (CMDD), emergence angle (EA) and transmucosal collar height (TCH, >2 mm vs ≤2 mm). None was significantly associated with T1–T2 bone change.
- Reports that thin mucosa and smoking, not prosthetic design, were the significant predictors of total bone resorption from placement (T0) to 18 months (T2).
- Classifies bone change into bone remodeling (BR, confined to the machined collar) vs bone loss (BL, apical to the collar with rough-surface exposure): BL in 0/16, 1/15 and 7/15 implants in Groups 1/2/3.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Multicenter prospective study (STROBE), continuation of a 5-month clinical trial; NCT05363306. Each patient contributed one implant (most mesial if several).
- **Population**: 49 consecutively enrolled patients, partially edentulous, single implant in native posterior mandible; 2 Group 1 patients lost to follow-up after prosthesis delivery → 47 implants/47 patients (25 male, 22 female; mean age 58.3 ± 12.2 y, range 34–85). One Group 2 implant failed after 9 months of loading (2.1% failure at 18 months); 46 functioned at T2 (Group 1: 16; Group 2: 15; Group 3: 15).
- **Key inclusion/exclusion**: age >18; ≥3 mm keratinized mucosa width; ≥6 mm bone width and ≥9 mm height above the mandibular canal; no augmentation; healed crest (≥6 months); uncontrolled diabetes (HbA1c >7.5%), full-mouth plaque score >25%, untreated periodontal disease, peak insertion torque >60 Ncm excluded.
- **Implant**: i-Smart tissue-level (i-Res, Lugano), 3 mm-high convergent machined transmucosal collar, moderately rough endosseous surface, external hexagon connection; length 8 or 10 mm; one-stage unsubmerged healing for 5 months with a healing cap, no provisional crown.
- **Groups by vertical mucosal thickness** (measured after buccal flap elevation): Group 1 thin (<2.5 mm), implant 2 mm subcrestal; Group 2 medium (2.5–3.5 mm), 1 mm subcrestal; Group 3 thick (>3.5 mm), equicrestal.
- **Prosthesis**: single-unit screw-retained metal-ceramic crown (cobalt-chromium based), connected directly to the collar; crown margin at platform level or on the collar at the prosthodontist's discretion; screw 30 Ncm; recall every 6 months.
- **Radiographs**: long-cone paralleling periapicals with patient-specific bite jigs at placement (T0), crown delivery (T1) and 18 months of loading (T2); same X-ray generator (60 kV, 7 mA). Peri-implant bone level (PBL) = distance from the most apical point of the crown margin to the most coronal bone-to-implant contact, mesial and distal; two calibrated blinded examiners (Cohen's kappa 87.2% intra-, 81.6% inter-examiner within ±0.1 mm).
- **Prosthetic variables at T1**: CMDD (≥10 mm vs <10 mm), EA (angle between implant long axis and tangent from the collar–rough-body junction along the crown profile), TCH (>2 mm vs ≤2 mm).
- **Statistics**: SPSS 26; one patient/implant as unit; 15 patients per group for 80% power assuming a 0.3 mm (±0.25 mm) difference; one-way ANOVA + Tukey; multiple linear regression (enter method); p < 0.05.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Authors: small sample (adequate for the statistics but one region, the posterior mandible); 18 months post-loading only, no long-term or peri-implantitis data; 2D periapical radiographs only (no buccal/lingual bone, no 3D emergence profile); other prosthetic factors (crown-to-implant ratio, occlusal table width, cusp inclination) not analysed; multicenter operator variability in surgery, radiography and prosthetic design.
- The authors recommend larger, more diverse samples and 3D imaging such as CBCT.
- Internal inconsistency in the retrieved text: the Results report a significant T1–T2 difference between Group 1 and Group 3 (p = 0.041), while the Abstract and Conclusion say no significant differences between groups from T1 to T2. Both are transcribed above.
- Table 4 codes smoking with smokers as reference and a positive coefficient for non-smokers (0.580), whereas the text says smoking is positively associated with resorption; the sign convention of the dependent variable is not stated in the retrieved text — not resolved here.
- Group 1 smokers 6/16 vs Group 2 2/16 (Table 1): smoking and mucosal-thickness group are not balanced, which the paper does not discuss. [derived from Table 1 counts]
- Thin mucosa is defined as <2.5 mm for grouping, whereas the Introduction describes thin mucosa as ≤2 mm.

## 7. Glossary
- **EMBL (Early Marginal Bone Loss)**: non-infective, non-progressive bone loss around the implant neck from placement to the first year of prosthetic function.
- **T0 / T1 / T2**: implant placement / crown delivery / 18 months of functional loading.
- **BR (Bone Remodeling) vs BL (Bone Loss)**: BR = bone change confined to the machined transmucosal collar; BL = resorption apical to the collar with rough-surface exposure.
- **TCH (Transmucosal Collar Height)**: distance from the bone crest to the most apical point of the crown margin at T1 (dichotomized >2 mm / ≤2 mm).
- **EA (Emergence Angle)**: angle between the implant long axis and the tangent along the crown emergence profile from the collar–rough-body junction.
- **STH (Supracrestal Tissue Height)**: vertical soft-tissue dimension (sulcus, junctional epithelium, connective tissue) sealing the transmucosal component.
