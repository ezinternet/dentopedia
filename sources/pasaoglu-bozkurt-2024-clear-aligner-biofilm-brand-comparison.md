## Why Ingested
First head-to-head microbiological comparison of 6 clear aligner brands (Invisalign, Clarity, ClearCorrect, Smartee, Orthero, Graphy) for time-dependent biofilm formation — provides brand-level caries/periodontal risk data absent from clinical outcome studies.

## Three-line Summary
In vitro time-course study comparing biofilm formation and microbial adhesion (S. mutans + L. acidophilus) on 6 clear aligner systems at 0, 24, 48, 72, 96, 120, 168, and 240 h; two-way repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni T2 post-hoc.

ClearCorrect showed significantly more bacterial formation than Smartee at 120, 168, and 240 h (p<0.05); Graphy showed more biofilm than Smartee at 168 h (p<0.05); combined S. mutans + L. acidophilus formed more biofilm on Graphy than on Invisalign at 120 and 168 h (p<0.05); Smartee consistently lowest biofilm among all brands.

All 6 aligner materials support elevated biofilm formation over extended wear periods; Smartee (China) performed best microbiologically; Invisalign was not the worst performer — Graphy showed more combined biofilm than Invisalign at peak time points.

## 세줄요약
6개 CA 브랜드(Invisalign·Clarity·ClearCorrect·Smartee·Orthero·Graphy)에 대해 S. mutans + L. acidophilus 바이오필름 형성을 0–240시간 추적 비교; 이원 반복측정 분산분석 + Bonferroni T2 사후검정.

ClearCorrect > Smartee (120·168·240시간, p<0.05); Graphy > Smartee (168시간, p<0.05); S. mutans+L. acidophilus 혼합 기준 Graphy > Invisalign (120·168시간, p<0.05); Smartee가 전 브랜드 중 일관적으로 최저 바이오필름.

모든 브랜드에서 장시간 사용 시 바이오필름이 현저히 증가; Smartee가 미생물학적으로 가장 우수; Graphy는 Invisalign보다 혼합 바이오필름이 많아 최악 순위에 위치.

## 1. Document Information
- **Type**: In vitro comparative microbiological study
- **Journal**: American Journal of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics (AJO-DO)
- **Year**: 2024 (published October 2024)
- **Institution**: Istanbul Aydin University + University of Sydney
- **Evidence level**: In vitro (microbiology)
- **Note**: Abstract-only (no PMC full text available)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First biofilm time-course comparison across 6 commercially diverse aligner brands
- Time-series resolution (0–240h) shows that brand differences emerge at 120+ hours of use
- Identifies Smartee as the lowest biofilm brand; Graphy as worst for combined S. mutans + L. acidophilus
- Elevates caries/periodontal risk assessment from generic CA concern to brand-specific consideration

## 3. Methodology
- Organisms: Streptococcus mutans (ATCC 25175) and Lactobacillus acidophilus (ATCC 4356)
- Timepoints: 0, 24, 48, 72, 96, 120, 168, 240 h
- Brands tested: Invisalign, Clarity (3M), ClearCorrect (Straumann), Smartee (China), Orthero (Turkey), Graphy (South Korea)
- Statistics: Two-way repeated measures ANOVA (Greenhouse-Geisser) + Bonferroni T2 post-hoc
- Significance: p<0.05

## 4. Key Results
- **ClearCorrect > Smartee** at 120 h, 168 h, 240 h (p<0.05)
- **Graphy > Smartee** at 168 h (p<0.05)
- **Graphy > Invisalign** for combined S. mutans + L. acidophilus at 120 h and 168 h (p<0.05)
- **Smartee**: consistently lowest biofilm among all brands
- All 6 materials showed increasing biofilm over time; no brand maintained consistently low biofilm at 240 h

## 5. Limitations
- Abstract only; detailed material characterization and exact CFU counts not available from abstract
- In vitro — does not account for saliva, toothbrushing, pH cycling, or patient wear duration
- Single-lab study (Istanbul Aydin University)

## 6. Related Work
- Complements chemical color stability data (Kobkiatkawin 2025, Salvatori 2025) with microbiological risk data
- Brand-specific biofilm risk adds dimension to aligner oral hygiene counseling

## 7. Glossary
- **S. mutans**: primary cariogenic bacterium; ATCC 25175 reference strain
- **L. acidophilus**: secondary caries-associated bacterium; ATCC 4356 reference strain
- **CFU**: colony-forming units (assumed measure though not specified in abstract)
