## Why Ingested
In vitro study examining the correlation between insertion torque (IT) and ISQ values, and the effect of thread initiator use — directly extends [[implants/isq/gil-2016-rfa-insertion-torque]] (same study) and informs clinical ISQ/IT decision thresholds for loading protocols.

## Three-line Summary
- In vitro study (n=140 implants in 10 bovine ribs, bone quality type II) comparing ISQ (Osstell) and insertion torque with vs without thread initiator.
- Thread initiator reduced mean insertion torque (24.36±14.88 N vs 35.30±10.51 N) but did not significantly affect ISQ (69.14±8.39 vs 72.17±5.76); correlation IT-ISQ was moderate with thread initiator (r=0.587, p<0.05) and weak/non-significant without (r=0.193, p=0.11).
- Using a thread initiator lowers insertion torque without compromising primary stability (ISQ), suggesting IT and ISQ reflect different aspects of primary stability.

## 세줄요약
- 소골 type II에서 140개 임플란트를 이용해 스레드 개시자(thread initiator) 사용 유무에 따른 삽입 토크(IT)와 ISQ(Osstell) 상관관계를 비교한 in vitro 연구
- 스레드 개시자 사용 시 평균 IT가 유의하게 감소(24.36→35.30 N)했으나 ISQ는 유의한 차이 없음; IT-ISQ 상관계수는 개시자 사용 시 0.587(p<0.05), 미사용 시 0.193(p=0.11)
- 스레드 개시자는 초기 고정(ISQ)을 저하시키지 않으면서 삽입 토크를 낮추며, IT와 ISQ는 초기 고정의 서로 다른 차원(전단력 vs 굽힘 저항)을 반영함을 시사

## 2. Key Contributions
- Quantified the effect of thread initiator on both insertion torque and ISQ in a controlled bovine rib model
- Demonstrated that thread initiator significantly reduces insertion torque without reducing ISQ values
- Showed that the IT-ISQ correlation is meaningful only when thread initiator is used (r=0.587 vs r=0.193)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro experimental study
- **n**: 140 implants (Essential® Cone, Klockner, 4mm×10mm) placed in 10 bovine ribs (bone quality type II per Lekholm & Zarb)
- **Groups**: Group A (thread initiator used, n=70) vs Group B (no thread initiator, n=70)
- **Outcomes**: Insertion torque (manual torque wrench), ISQ (Osstell ISQ, two perpendicular measurements per implant)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Group A (Thread Initiator) | Group B (No Thread Initiator) | p-value / Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean Insertion Torque | 24.36 ± 14.88 N | 35.30 ± 10.51 N | Significant reduction |
| Mean ISQ | 69.14 ± 8.39 | 72.17 ± 5.76 | NS |
| Pearson r (IT vs ISQ) | 0.587 | 0.193 | p<0.05 vs p=0.11 |
| ISQ < 60 (Group A) | 12.00 ± 2.33 N | — | p<0.004 vs Group A ISQ≥60 |
| ISQ ≥ 60 (Group A) | 25.95 ± 10.08 N | — | p<0.001 vs Group A ISQ<60 |
| IT < 30 N (Group A) | 66.97 ± 8.41 ISQ | — | p<0.0008 vs Group A IT≥30 |
| IT ≥ 30 N (Group A) | 74.97 ± 4.91 ISQ | — | p<0.0005 vs Group B IT≥30 |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- In vitro bovine rib model (type II bone) — limited generalizability to human clinical scenarios with varied bone qualities
- Single implant system (Klockner Essential Cone) and single operator
- Manual torque wrench insertion may introduce operator variability
- No long-term osseointegration or survival outcomes assessed

## 6. Related Work
- Degidi et al. 2010 (r=0.247, 514 Xive implants): positive but low correlation
- Degidi et al. 2012 (r=0.218, 4135 XiVE implants): similar low correlation
- Turkyilmaz et al. 2006 (Spearman r=0.583, 142 Brånemark): correlation similar to Group A
- Kahraman et al. 2009 (r=0.78 at surgery): higher correlation in clinical setting
- Park et al. 2012 (r=0.427): correlation with max IT at surgery

## 7. Glossary
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: 1–100 scale from Osstell RFA; higher = more stable
- **Insertion Torque (IT)**: Moment of force to seat implant; measured in Ncm (here reported in N)
- **Thread Initiator**: Tap-like instrument used to cut threads in osteotomy before implant placement
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis)**: Non-invasive measurement of implant stability via resonance frequency
- **Lekholm & Zarb Type II**: Dense cortical bone with coarse trabecular bone core
