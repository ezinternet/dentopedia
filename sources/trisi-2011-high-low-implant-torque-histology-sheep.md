## Why Ingested
[[implants/isq/marconcini-2018-high-insertion-torque-bone-resorption-rct]]에서 ≥50 Ncm → 골흡수↑라는 임상 근거가 생겼으나, 이 현상이 **압박괴사(골압축 허혈괴사, Osseocompression Necrosis)** 때문인지 조직학적으로 검증한 연구가 위키에 없었음. Trisi 2011은 양 하악에서 HT(평균 110 Ncm) vs LT(10 Ncm)를 6주간 조직학·생체역학적으로 비교한 **유일한 동물조직학 연구**로, "과토크 = 압박괴사" 가설을 직접 검증해 반박함.

## Three-line Summary

Controlled animal histologic study (양 하악, 5마리, 임플란트 40개, 6주) comparing very high insertion torque (삽입 토크, HT = mean 110 Ncm) versus low torque (LT = mean 10 Ncm) using split-mouth design with sacrifice at 1–6 weeks.

HT implants showed significantly greater bone apposition and removal torque than LT at every timepoint; critically, **no bone necrosis was observed** in the HT group despite 110 Ncm in dense cortical bone.

RFA/ISQ was insensitive to these histologic and biomechanical differences, and HT produced a transient primary-stability dip at day 7 that self-corrected — evidence against the "overtorque → osseocompression necrosis" hypothesis.

## 세줄요약

대조군 동물 조직학 연구(양 하악, 5마리, 임플란트 40개, 6주): split-mouth 설계로 초고삽입토크(삽입 토크, HT = 평균 110 Ncm) vs 저토크(LT = 평균 10 Ncm)를 1~6주 희생 시점별 비교.

HT군이 모든 시점에서 골형성·제거토크 유의하게 큼; 110 Ncm의 치밀 피질골에서도 **골괴사는 관찰되지 않음**.

RFA/ISQ는 이 조직학적·생체역학적 차이를 감지하지 못했고, HT군은 7일째 일시적 1차 안정성 저하 후 자연 회복 — "과토크→골압축 괴사" 가설에 반하는 근거.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal:** The International Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Implants, Vol. 26, Issue 4, pp. 837–849
- **Publication date:** 2011
- **Model:** Sheep (hybrid), mandible (extraoral approach); 5 animals, 40 implants
- **Funding/COI:** Biomaterials Clinical Research Association, Lucca, Italy (Trisi P affiliation); potential industry link

## 2. Key Contributions
- First histologic study to directly test whether very high insertion torque (mean 110 Ncm) causes peri-implant bone necrosis — **conclusion: it does not**
- Demonstrates that HT in dense cortical bone produces significantly *more* bone apposition than LT at every healing timepoint (1, 2, 3, 4, 6 weeks)
- Identifies a critical biomechanical phenomenon: **HT causes a significant transient drop in primary stability at 7 days** — the early "dip" well known in ISQ curves, more pronounced with high torque
- LT group achieves significant stability increase after 4 weeks (secondary stability) while HT starts high and dips then recovers
- Demonstrates that RFA/ISQ is **insensitive** to these histologic and biomechanical changes — cannot detect the early stability drop or the magnitude of bone apposition
- Provides the mechanistic basis for high-IT immediate loading protocols: high torque → high initial bone contact → no necrosis → early loading possible

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Controlled animal experiment (split-mouth)
- **Animals:** 5 hybrid sheep; extraoral mandibular approach
- **Implants:** 40 tapered-screw-form implants; 4 per side per animal
  - HT group (test): mean 110 Ncm (dense cortical bone)
  - LT group (control): mean 10 Ncm (contralateral side)
- **Sacrifice timepoints:** 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6 weeks post-placement (n=1 animal per timepoint)
- **Outcomes:**
  - Removal torque (biomechanical stability)
  - Resonance frequency analysis (ISQ)
  - Histology + histomorphometry (bone apposition, bone-implant contact, necrosis assessment)
- **Key histologic endpoint:** Presence or absence of osteocyte lacunar emptying (bone necrosis marker), woven bone formation, cortical bone remodeling

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Bone apposition (histomorphometry):**
- HT > LT at ALL timepoints (1, 2, 3, 4, 6 weeks) — significantly higher bone formation around high-torque implants

**Removal torque:**
- HT > LT consistently — mechanical bone-implant bonding superior in HT group throughout healing

**Primary stability (ISQ / RFA):**
- HT group: significant **drop in primary stability at day 7** post-placement — bone remodeling dip
- LT group: significant stability **increase at 4 weeks** (secondary stability achievement)
- RFA could not distinguish these histologic differences — insensitive to the biology

**Bone necrosis:**
- **NO bone necrosis observed in HT group** (110 Ncm) in dense cortical sheep mandible
- No osteocyte lacunar emptying, no avascular zones attributed to compression

**Conclusion of authors:** High implant insertion torque in dense cortical bone does not induce bone necrosis or implant failure; instead it increases primary stability critical for immediate loading.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Animal model (sheep mandible); cortical bone anatomy differs from human jaw sites
- Very small n per timepoint (1 animal = 8 implants sacrificed per week)
- No histologic quantification of inflammatory cells or vascular density
- HT value (110 Ncm) exceeds most clinical torque limits — may not represent typical clinical "overtorque" scenarios (45–70 Ncm range)
- Short follow-up (6 weeks); long-term remodeling and BIC not assessed
- Potential COI: Trisi affiliated with private research association; results consistently favor high-torque protocols
- Split-mouth design: contralateral control reduces confounders but systemic/vascular factors shared

## 6. Related Work
- Marconcini 2018 [[implants/isq/marconcini-2018-high-insertion-torque-bone-resorption-rct]]: contradicts Trisi — clinical RCT shows IT ≥50 Ncm → greater MBL and recession at 3 years; discrepancy may reflect short-term (Trisi, 6 weeks) vs long-term (Marconcini, 3 years) remodeling
- Faot 2019 [[implants/isq/faot-2019-insertion-torque-mandibular-overdenture]]: reinforces Trisi — G4 ≥45 Ncm best clinical outcomes; no harm
- Lemos 2020 SR+MA [[implants/isq/lemos-2020-high-insertion-torque-dental-implants-sr-ma]]: reinforces — no significant MBL or survival difference in clinical pooled data

## 7. Glossary
- **Osseocompression Necrosis (골압축 괴사):** Hypothesized bone death from ischemia secondary to excessive compressive stress from high-torque implant insertion; this study found no evidence of it at 110 Ncm
- **Osteocyte lacunar emptying (골세포 소강 공동화):** Histologic marker of bone necrosis — empty lacunae indicate osteocyte death; not observed in HT group
- **Removal torque (제거 토크):** Force required to unscrew the implant from bone; surrogate for mechanical bone-implant integration
- **HT (High Torque, 고삽입토크):** Mean 110 Ncm in this study — extreme value, exceeds typical clinical overtorque range
- **LT (Low Torque, 저삽입토크):** Mean 10 Ncm — below typical clinical minimum for stable insertion
- **Transient stability dip:** Drop in ISQ/removal torque at ~7 days post-insertion during bone remodeling phase; more pronounced in HT group but self-corrects
