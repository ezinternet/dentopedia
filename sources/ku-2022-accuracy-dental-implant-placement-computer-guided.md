## Why Ingested

Quantifies guided-surgery error in a real cohort *and* reports the arm most accuracy studies silently drop — the 42.7% of implants where the printed guide could not be used at all — which is the missing denominator in guided-accuracy meta-analyses → [[digital-workflow/nava-2026-guided-surgery-immediate-implant-accuracy-nma]] and [[digital-workflow/tomar-2025-comparing-clinical-outcomes-guided-freehand]].

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort with CBCT superimposition (BMC Oral Health 2022;22:8, Seoul National University Bundang Hospital; 34 patients, 89 implants, mean follow-up 17.3 months) measuring the deviation between virtually planned and actually placed implants.

Guided implants deviated 2.69 ± 1.00 mm at the entrance point and 5.52 ± 2.80° in angulation, versus 14.53 ± 6.64 mm and 11.75 ± 4.39° in the 38 implants (42.7%) that abandoned the guide; within the guided group the flapless approach was more accurate than the flapped approach (2.22 vs 3.18 mm, P < 0.001; 4.27° vs 6.82°, P = 0.001).

Accuracy is conditional: the guide helps only in the minority of cases where it fits and engages bone, and abandoning it is decided mainly by insufficient primary stability (19/38), anatomic risk (9/38), dehiscence during drilling (4/38) and poor stent fit (6/38).

## 세줄요약

CBCT 중첩을 이용한 후향 코호트 (BMC Oral Health 2022;22:8, 분당서울대; 환자 34명, 임플란트 89개, 평균 17.3개월): 컴퓨터 유도 수술 (Computer-guided Surgery)의 계획-실제 위치 편차 측정.

가이드 사용군의 입구점 편차 2.69 ± 1.00 mm·각도 5.52 ± 2.80° vs 가이드를 포기한 38개(42.7%)에서 14.53 ± 6.64 mm·11.75 ± 4.39°; 가이드군 내에서는 무피판 (Flapless)이 유피판 (Flapped)보다 정확 (2.22 vs 3.18 mm, P<0.001; 4.27° vs 6.82°, P=0.001).

정확성은 조건부 — 가이드가 적합하고 골에 물릴 때만 유효하며, 포기 결정의 주된 이유는 초기 안정성 부족(19/38)·해부학적 위험(9/38)·천공(4/38)·스텐트 적합 불량(6/38).

## Key Contributions
- Reports a real-world guided-accuracy figure (2.69 mm entrance deviation) alongside the guide-abandonment rate (38/89 implants, 42.7%) in the same cohort.
- Shows flapless guided placement was *more* accurate than flapped placement within the guided group — the opposite of what tissue-support reasoning might suggest, and coupled with significantly higher primary ISQ (72.69 vs 55.84).
- Itemizes the reasons guides fail in practice: insufficient primary stability 19, anatomic risk (inferior alveolar canal proximity) 9, dehiscence during drilling 4, poor stent fitness 6.
- Documents the shift in case mix implied by guide abandonment — the drop-out group was older (65.8 vs 61.9 years).

## Methodology
Retrospective cohort, single centre; patients planned with CBCT-based computer-guided surgery. Three groups:
- **Group 1 (flapped guided)** — n = 25 implants
- **Group 2 (flapless guided)** — n = 26 implants
- **Drop-out group** — n = 38 implants where the plan changed mid-surgery and the fabricated guide was not used (flap conversion or freehand)

Accuracy measured by superimposing postoperative CBCT on the planned position: deviation distance at the implant entrance point and angular deviation of the fixture axis. Primary stability by ISQ. Demographics compared across groups; independent t-tests for deviations and ISQ.

## Key Results

| Group | Entrance deviation (mm) | Angular deviation (°) | Primary ISQ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drop-out (n=38) | 14.53 ± 6.64 | 11.75 ± 4.39 | 66.49 ± 15.18 |
| Guided, all (n=51) | 2.69 ± 1.00 | 5.52 ± 2.80 | 64.43 ± 18.26 |
| Group 1 flapped (n=25) | 3.18 ± 0.89 | 6.82 ± 2.71 | 55.84 ± 20.47 |
| Group 2 flapless (n=26) | 2.22 ± 0.88 | 4.27 ± 2.30 | 72.69 ± 10.97 |

P-values: flapped vs flapless — 0.001 (distance, abstract reports P < 0.001), P = 0.001 (angulation), P = 0.001 (ISQ). Drop-out vs guided — P < 0.001 for both deviations, P = 0.578 for ISQ.

**Cohort.** 89 implants in 34 patients (19 male, 15 female; mean age 62.1 years); all implants survived over a mean 17.3 months with marginal bone loss within 0.5 mm.

**Guide abandonment causes (n=38).** Insufficient primary stability 19; risk of anatomic damage (inferior alveolar canal proximity) 9; bone dehiscence during drilling 4; poor stent fitness 6.

**Demographics.** Drop-out group older (65.8 ± 9.3 vs 61.9 ± 10.8 years); within the guided group the flapped subgroup was younger (59.7 vs 63.9 years, P < 0.001) — a potential confound for the flapped-vs-flapless comparison.

## Limitations
Retrospective, single-centre, modest sample. The flapped-vs-flapless comparison is confounded: group assignment followed clinical necessity (flapped cases were younger and had much lower primary ISQ, i.e. harder sites), so "flapless is more accurate" may reflect case selection rather than approach. The paper's own group labeling is inconsistent — the abstract assigns flapless to group 1 while the tables and results assign flapped to group 1 — so readers must follow the tables. No blinding of deviation measurement is described. Follow-up (17.3 months) addresses survival only, not the prosthetic consequence of up to 3 mm of deviation.

## Glossary
- **Computer-guided surgery:** static guided implant placement using a CBCT-derived, printed surgical guide with drill sleeves.
- **Flapless approach:** implant placement without mucoperiosteal flap elevation.
- **Entry-point deviation:** 3D distance between planned and actual implant entrance position (mm).
- **Angular deviation:** angle between planned and actual implant long axis (°).
- **Stent fitness:** how well the surgical guide seats on the dental arch.
- **ISQ:** Implant Stability Quotient.
