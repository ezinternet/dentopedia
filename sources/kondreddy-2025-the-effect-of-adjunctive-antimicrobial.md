## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 48 RCTs (2,755 participants, 2010–2024) evaluating adjunctive systemic antibiotics with scaling and root planing (SRP) for stage III/IV periodontitis; amoxicillin + metronidazole yielded the most consistent improvements: CAL gain 0.47 mm, PPD reduction 0.30 mm, BOP reduction 6.9%.

A 3-day antibiotic regimen provided comparable outcomes to 7-day courses with fewer adverse events; evidence for azithromycin and doxycycline was more variable; overall certainty of evidence rated low due to study heterogeneity.

Adjunctive AMX/MET significantly enhances SRP outcomes in advanced periodontitis; shorter regimens may minimize resistance risk while preserving efficacy.

## 세줄요약

48편 RCT SR(2,755명, 2010–2024): 치석제거·치근활택(SRP) + 보조적 전신 항생제 조합, Stage III/IV 치주염 임상 결과 평가; 아목시실린(AMX) + 메트로니다졸(MET) 조합이 가장 일관된 개선: CAL 획득 0.47 mm, PPD 감소 0.30 mm, 출혈지수(BOP) 감소 6.9%.

3일 항생제 요법이 7일 요법과 유사 효과이면서 부작용 적음; 아지스로마이신·독시사이클린 근거는 가변적; 연구 이질성으로 인해 전반적 근거 질 낮음(Low).

AMX/MET 보조요법이 중증 치주염의 SRP 결과를 유의하게 개선; 단기 요법이 항생제 내성 위험 최소화하면서 효능 유지 가능.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Covers recent evidence (2010–2024), including three newly published trials
- AMX/MET remains the most evidence-supported combination for adjunctive SRP
- 3-day regimen comparable to 7-day: clinically important for stewardship
- Metronidazole alone also effective: CAL 1.12 mm, PPD 1.11 mm — option for AMX-allergic patients
- Low overall evidence quality: heterogeneity, small samples, variable designs

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PRISMA; PubMed-only search, 2010–2024
- Inclusion: RCTs, adjunctive systemic antibiotics in SRP, stage III/IV periodontitis
- Exclusion: surgical interventions only, insufficient follow-up
- 48 RCTs, 2,755 participants

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Antibiotic | n | CAL gain (mm) | PPD reduction (mm) | BOP reduction (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amoxicillin + Metronidazole | 389 | 0.47 | 0.30 | 6.90 |
| Metronidazole alone | 71 | 1.12 | 1.11 | 6.90 |
| Azithromycin | 110 | 0.59 | 0.77 | 1.28 |
| Doxycycline | 22 | 0.80 | 1.00 | NR |

- 3-day AMX/MET = 7-day AMX/MET: comparable CAL and PPD, fewer adverse events (Cosgarea 2022)
- Overall evidence quality: LOW (heterogeneity, small samples)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- PubMed-only search may miss trials in other databases
- No meta-analysis: effect size estimates pooled informally
- Short follow-up in many studies
- Low evidence quality limits confidence in numerical estimates
- Future: standardized protocols, larger multicenter trials

## 6. Related Work and Context
- Builds on Khattri 2020 (adjunctive AMX/MET SR)
- Zhao 2021 found 500/500 mg optimal dose; Kondreddy 2025 adds 3-day duration data

## 7. Glossary
- **CAL (Clinical Attachment Level)**: Primary periodontal outcome; 0.47 mm gain with AMX/MET
- **PPD (Probing Pocket Depth)**: 0.30 mm reduction with AMX/MET
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing)**: Inflammation marker; 6.9% reduction with AMX/MET

## Why Ingested

Provides the most recent (2024) summary of adjunctive antibiotic outcomes in SRP, including 3-day vs 7-day duration evidence directly relevant to prescribing decisions.
