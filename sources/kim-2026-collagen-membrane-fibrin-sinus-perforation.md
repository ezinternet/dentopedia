## Why Ingested

The existing wiki covers perforation *incidence* and *risk factors* ([[sinus-lift/lateral/soares-2024-sinus-membrane-perforation-repair-review]] SR+MA, [[sinus-lift/lateral/koleilat-2023-prf-collagen-sinus-membrane-repair]]) but lacks a dedicated technical note on the specific handle-design modification for collagen membrane placement combined with fibrin glue stabilization of the bony window. This case report (Kim 2026) adds an actionable surgical technique variant applicable even in medically compromised patients (osteoporosis/denosumab), complementing the repair-strategy evidence base.

## Three-line Summary

Line 1: Technical note and single case report of a modified sinus membrane perforation repair technique using a resorbable collagen membrane cut with a custom "handle" extension and fibrin glue, in a 71-year-old female with osteoporosis and denosumab history undergoing lateral sinus augmentation.
Line 2: The handle enabled precise intrasinus membrane positioning and prevented inward displacement during bone graft packing; fibrin glue mixed with graft and rhBMP-2 formed a cohesive putty mass and stabilized the lateral bony window without tacks; graft height was maintained at 13.3 mm (from 15.2 mm post-op) at 6 months with no complications.
Line 3: The technique simplifies perforation management, reduces risk of graft displacement, and may offer advantages in anticoagulated or bone-fragile patients, though evidence is limited to a single case with no control.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Handle-design collagen membrane:** A resorbable collagen membrane (Bio-Gide) is cut to include a tab/"handle" extension that protrudes through the sinus window, allowing controlled manipulation and exact intrasinus positioning; the handle is trimmed flush after membrane seating.
2. **Inward-displacement prevention:** During bone graft packing, the handle allows the surgeon to maintain membrane position against the perforation site, preventing the graft from pushing the membrane into the sinus.
3. **Fibrin glue multi-function:** Fibrin glue is used in two ways: (a) mixed with synthetic graft + rhBMP-2 to create a cohesive putty-like mass that packs without crumbling, and (b) applied to the outer surface of the repositioned bony window to stabilize it without titanium tacks.
4. **Applicability in medically compromised patients:** The denosumab history and osteoporosis of the patient meant standard cortical-screw fixation was avoided; fibrin-glue window stabilization provided an alternative.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Surgical sequence:** Preoperative CBCT confirming pneumatization; lateral window osteotomy using piezosurgery; membrane elevation with detection of 5–7 mm perforation; additional elevation to reduce functional perforation to 3–4 mm; custom collagen membrane with handle inserted and draped over perforation; synthetic graft (Bio-Oss + rhBMP-2) mixed with fibrin glue; packed incrementally; handle trimmed; bony window repositioned and secured with fibrin glue; primary closure.
- **Imaging follow-up:** Periapical radiographs + CBCT at 6 months.
- **Implant:** Placed simultaneously at site #26.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Initial perforation size | 5–7 mm |
| Size after additional elevation | 3–4 mm |
| Graft height immediately post-op | 15.2 mm |
| Graft height at 6 months | 13.3 mm (12.5% resorption) |
| Sinus complications | None |
| Graft leakage | None |
| Window fixation method | Fibrin glue (no tacks) |
| Implant stability | Clinically stable at 6 months |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single case report — no control group, no comparative data
- No long-term follow-up beyond 6 months
- No histologic evaluation of graft incorporation
- Technique requires familiarity with piezosurgery and membrane handling
- rhBMP-2 use adds cost and regulatory considerations
- Generalizability to larger perforations or different patient populations unknown

## 7. Glossary

- **Schneiderian membrane (슈나이더막):** The periosteal lining of the maxillary sinus; prone to perforation during elevation.
- **Fibrin glue (피브린 접착제):** A two-component biologic adhesive (thrombin + fibrinogen) used to seal, stabilize, or bond tissue planes.
- **rhBMP-2 (재조합 인간 골형성단백질-2, recombinant human Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2):** Osteoinductive growth factor used to enhance bone formation.
- **Handle design (손잡이 디자인):** Author-coined term for a collagen membrane cut with a protruding tab to allow intrasinus manipulation without direct instrument contact with the membrane body.
- **Lateral bony window (측방 골창):** The cortical bone flap raised during lateral sinus augmentation; repositioned after graft packing.
- **Bio-Gide:** Brand name resorbable bilayer collagen membrane (porcine-derived) used for GBR and sinus perforation repair.
- **Bio-Oss:** Brand name deproteinized bovine bone mineral used as a volume-stable sinus graft material.
- **Denosumab (데노수맙):** RANKL inhibitor (anti-resorptive) used for osteoporosis; relevant for MRONJ risk stratification and bone quality assessment.
