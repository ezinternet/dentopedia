## Why Ingested

The wiki has no existing paper covering the systemic-disease association side of apical periodontitis (근단치주염, apical periodontitis, AP) — current endodontic diagnosis pages (e.g. [[wiki/endodontics/diagnosis/karamifar-2020-endodontic-periapical-lesion-an-overview]]) cover etiology/imaging/treatment of periapical lesions but not their bidirectional link to chronic systemic disease. This umbrella review (AMSTAR-2 grading of 9 systematic reviews) fills that gap and anchors a new "endodontic medicine" knowledge cluster for future ingests on AP-CVD, AP-diabetes, and AP-autoimmune disease papers.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (AMSTAR-2 quality appraisal) of 9 systematic reviews (2016-2021, k=3-21 primary studies each) evaluating the association between apical periodontitis (AP) and chronic systemic diseases — cardiovascular disease (CVD), diabetes mellitus (DM), HIV, chronic liver disease (CLD), blood disorders, osteoporosis, autoimmune disease.

Positive association found between AP and CVD (moderate evidence; one meta-analysis: RR=1.38 for CVD diagnosis with chronic endodontic infection) and between AP and DM (limited evidence); no association with HIV; positive association with blood disorders, CLD, osteoporosis, and autoimmune disease at moderate evidence quality; included SRs ranged "low" to "high" AMSTAR-2 quality with substantial heterogeneity.

Evidence for all associations remains inconclusive/observational-only — periodontitis and glycemic control are major uncontrolled confounders across the primary literature, and most included SRs did not search grey literature or report primary-study funding sources.

## 세줄요약

AMSTAR-2 기반 우산리뷰(umbrella review) — 근단치주염 (Apical Periodontitis, AP)과 만성전신질환의 연관성을 평가한 체계적 문헌고찰(SR) 9편(2016-2021년, 개별 SR당 원저 3-21편)을 질평가.

심혈관질환 (Cardiovascular Disease, CVD)·당뇨병 (Diabetes Mellitus, DM)과 AP 간 양성 연관성 확인(각각 중등도·제한적 근거수준; 한 메타분석에서 만성근관감염 시 CVD 진단 위험비 RR=1.38), HIV는 연관성 없음, 혈액질환·만성간질환 (Chronic Liver Disease, CLD)·골다공증·자가면역질환은 중등도 근거수준의 양성 연관성.

모든 연관성이 관찰연구 기반의 미확정 상태이며, 치주염과 혈당조절이 주요 미통제 교란변수로 작용 — 향후 잘 설계된 전향적 연구와 염증 바이오마커 평가가 필요함(임상적 한계).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review restricting inclusion to SRs that performed a **valid risk-of-bias assessment** of primary studies (a stricter bar than 2 prior umbrella reviews on CVD-AP and DM-AP outcome, which included SRs lacking proper RoB tools).
- Consolidates AP's association evidence across **7 chronic disease categories** in one place: CVD, DM, HIV, chronic liver disease, blood disorders, osteoporosis, autoimmune disease.
- Applies AMSTAR-2 (16-item checklist) systematically to grade each of the 9 included SRs as high/moderate/low quality, exposing consistent methodological gaps (missing grey-literature search, missing funding-source reporting, unjustified study-design inclusion criteria).
- Identifies periodontitis and glycemic control as under-addressed confounders across the primary-study literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PRISMA-guided umbrella review; PROSPERO-registered protocol (CRD42020189979).
- Search: PubMed, Virtual Health Library (VHL), Scopus, Cochrane Library, Embase, Web of Science, Open Grey — no date/language restriction, search run through September 2022.
- Two independent reviewers (K.P.P., C.M.A.F.) for screening/extraction, third reviewer (E.J.N.L.S.) for disagreement resolution.
- Inclusion: systematic reviews evaluating AP-chronic disease association AND performing a valid risk-of-bias assessment of primary studies. Excluded: narrative/critical reviews, SRs without RoB assessment (including those using only OCEBM evidence-level grading, which is not a RoB tool), primary studies, case reports, protocols/abstracts-only.
- Quality tool: AMSTAR-2 (16 items, yes/partial-yes/no responses), completed via the AMSTAR online checklist; final categorization high/moderate/low/critically-low.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 933 records retrieved → 525 after dedup → 20 full-text assessed → **9 SRs included** (2016-2021).
- Diseases covered across the 9 SRs: CVD (n=6 SRs), DM (n=4), HIV/osteoporosis/CLD/blood disorders/autoimmune disease (n=1 each).
- AMSTAR-2 grading: 1 SR "high" quality (Gupta et al., DM), 6 SRs "moderate," 2 SRs "low."
- 5/9 SRs performed their own meta-analysis. Key pooled effect sizes reported within those SRs:
  - Koletsi et al.: RR=1.38 (95% CI 1.06-1.80, p=0.008) for CVD diagnosis with chronic endodontic infection — but overall evidence graded "very low" (serious RoB in most primary studies).
  - Aminoshariae et al. (CVD, RCT-outcome direction): 67% higher risk of negative root-canal-treatment outcome in CVD patients — moderate certainty, low RoB.
  - Aminoshariae et al. (AP→CVD development direction): RR=1.20 (95% CI 0.79-1.82) — low certainty, high RoB.
  - Gupta et al. (DM): higher prevalence of radiolucent periapical lesions in root-filled teeth of diabetics vs healthy; no established association with delayed healing.
  - Ríos-Osório et al. (DM): all 10 included studies positive, but 9/10 high RoB → inconclusive, DM "may be" a risk factor.
- Bottom-line associations: positive AP-CVD (moderate evidence), positive AP-DM (limited evidence), no AP-HIV association, positive AP-blood disorders/CLD/osteoporosis/autoimmune disease (moderate evidence).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No pooled quantitative synthesis across the 9 included SRs was possible (high heterogeneity in outcomes/methodology) — this is itself an umbrella review of qualitative synthesis, not a re-meta-analysis.
- All underlying evidence is observational (case-control, cross-sectional, cohort); causality cannot be established.
- Major uncontrolled confounders flagged across primary studies: periodontitis status, glycemic control severity in DM, CD4+/viral load in HIV, endotoxin/cytokine levels in CLD, vascular-cell alterations in blood disorders, smoking (shared risk factor for AP/CVD/DM/osteoporosis), alcohol (shared risk factor for CLD/osteoporosis), and genetic predisposition.
- Authors call for prospective cohort studies with inflammatory biomarker measurement, and animal studies where RCTs are unethical.
- Most included SRs failed AMSTAR-2 items on grey-literature search and primary-study funding-source reporting — a repeatable methodological weakness the authors flag for future SR authors in this space.

## 6. Related Work

- Supersedes/updates two prior narrower umbrella reviews (on CVD-AP and DM-RCT-outcome specifically) that included SRs without valid risk-of-bias assessment.

## 7. Glossary

- **Apical periodontitis (AP, 근단치주염)**: inflammatory lesion of periradicular tissue resulting from root-canal microbial infection.
- **Umbrella review (우산리뷰)**: a review that systematically collects and appraises multiple existing systematic reviews on a topic, rather than primary studies.
- **AMSTAR-2**: "A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews," 16-item instrument for critically appraising SR/MA methodological quality.
- **Risk of bias (RoB, 비뚤림위험)**: systematic error in a study's design/conduct/analysis that can distort its result.
- **CVD**: cardiovascular disease. **DM**: diabetes mellitus. **CLD**: chronic liver disease.
- **PROSPERO**: international prospective register of systematic review protocols.
