## Why Ingested

The held vertical-root-fracture (VRF) pages describe CBCT as a selective, last-resort aid whose fracture-line detection is unreliable in root-filled teeth ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]], [[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2025-position-statement-longitudinal-cracks-fractures]]) and show CBCT bone-pattern value ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/alaugaily-2022-cbct-patterns-bone-loss-clinical]]), but none states a formal imaging-modality sequence. This Korean adaptation guideline supplies that sequence (periapical first, small-FOV CBCT only if inconclusive) and reinforces the selective-CBCT stance with a graded, Delphi-agreed recommendation.

## Three-line Summary

Practice-guideline development study (Korean Clinical Imaging Guidelines adaptation methodology): 20 guidelines retrieved (Ovid-Medline, Embase, GIN, NGC, KoreaMed, KMbase, KoMGI; 2000 to Sept 2023), 3 included, supplemented by 22 recent primary/secondary studies, for one key question (periapical radiography vs CBCT in chewing-pain patients with suspected VRF).

Two recommendations were finalized by a 6-member two-round Delphi panel (Rec 1: grade A, evidence level II, mean agreement 8.0 [SD 1.2]; Rec 2: grade B, evidence level II, mean 7.6 [SD 1.6]): periapical radiography is the preferred first imaging method, and small-FOV CBCT may be considered only when intraoral radiographs are insufficient.

CBCT use in root-filled teeth is constrained by beam-hardening/streak artifacts (pooled CBCT sensitivity in filled teeth 72.76% [SD 18.73], range 30-92%), only 1 of 3 source guidelines scored above 50 on AGREE II "Rigor of development", and tooth-site/root-number-specific advice could not be given for lack of literature.

## 세줄요약

진료지침 개발 연구(한국 임상영상진료지침 (Korean Clinical Imaging Guidelines, K-CIG) 적응 방법론): 20편의 지침을 검색(2000-2023.9)해 3편을 채택하고 최신 연구 22편을 보충하여, "저작통 + 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 의심 환자에서 치근단방사선사진 (Periapical radiography, PA) 대비 콘빔 CT (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT)" 단일 핵심질문을 다룸.

전문가 6인 2회 델파이 합의로 권고 2개 확정(권고 1: 등급 A, 근거수준 II, 평균 합의도 8.0 [SD 1.2]; 권고 2: 등급 B, 근거수준 II, 평균 7.6 [SD 1.6]): PA를 1차 영상으로 선호하고, 구내방사선사진으로 정보가 부족할 때만 소조사야 (small field of view, FOV) CBCT를 고려.

근관충전치에서는 빔경화 (beam hardening)·줄무늬 인공물로 CBCT 사용이 제한됨(충전치 CBCT 민감도 평균 72.76% [SD 18.73], 범위 30-92%); 채택된 3개 지침 중 AGREE II 개발의 엄격성 영역 50점 초과는 1편뿐이며, 치아 위치·치근 수별 권고는 근거 부족으로 제시하지 못함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First Korean evidence-based clinical imaging guideline (K-CIG) for teeth with suspected VRF.
- Establishes a modality sequence: periapical radiography first; small-FOV CBCT only if intraoral imaging is non-diagnostic; CBCT confirmation unnecessary when VRF is already visible on a periapical film (high PA specificity, low false-positive rate).
- Documents the conflict in the recent literature on CBCT in obturated teeth (artifact-limited vs resolution-dependent) and names voxel size (0.2-0.3 mm suggested), detector type (flat panel better than image-intensifier/CCD) and scanner model as modifiers.
- Lists radiologic features of VRF: visible fracture line, fragment separation, space between root filling and canal wall, vertical bone loss, diffuse/halo or J-type periradicular radiolucency.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Guideline adaptation (K-CIG methodology of Choi et al.); development committee, working group, 6-member consensus group (nominated by 5 societies); PICO key question (Population: chewing-pain patients with suspected VRF; Intervention: CBCT; Comparator: periapical radiographs; Outcome: diagnostic possibility of VRF).
- **Databases**: Ovid-Medline, Elsevier-Embase, Guideline International Network (GIN), National Guideline Clearinghouse (NGC; offline since July 2018, searched to June 2018), KoreaMed, KMbase, KoMGI; 2000 to September 2023.
- **n**: 20 guidelines retrieved, 17 excluded at title/abstract, 1 at full text, 1 added by hand search, 3 included; 22 recent primary/secondary studies for the evidence table (experimental, observational, SR/MA).
- **Quality**: K-AGREE II (two reviewers, 1-7 scale; threshold >=50 in "Rigor of Development"); recommendation grades A/B/C/I and evidence levels I-IV; two-round anonymous Delphi (1-9 agreement scale).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Selected guidelines and AGREE II scores: Kullman 2012 (dental radiography after dento-alveolar trauma) 33; Radiation Protection No. 172, Cone beam CT for dental and maxillofacial radiology (Horner 2012) 90; Mallya 2015 (Evidence and Professional Guidelines for Appropriate Use of CBCT) 31. Only the Radiation Protection No. 172 guideline passed 50; the other two were retained under the K-CIG exception for scarce guidelines (acceptability/applicability judged "generally yes" for all three).
- Delphi result: Rec 1 grade A, evidence level II, mean 8.0 (SD 1.2, CV 0.16); Rec 2 grade B, evidence level II, mean 7.6 (SD 1.6, CV 0.26). The paper's text does not print the recommendation wording separately from the abstract; mapping of grades to Rec 1 (periapical first) and Rec 2 (small-FOV CBCT if inconclusive) follows the abstract and is inferred.
- Cited accuracy figures (from included studies, discussed in the paper): in vitro CBCT vs PA sensitivity 0.752 vs 0.242 (0.2 mm voxel, unfilled teeth); nine-angle 2D images sensitivity 28% / specificity 33% vs CBCT 55% / 82% in another study; diagnostic odds ratio in unfilled teeth CBCT 94.26 vs PA 14.42, with no significant difference (PA slightly better) in filled teeth; CBCT sensitivity with gutta-percha/metal posts 72.76 (SD 18.73)% and specificity 75.44 (SD 18.26)%.
- Technique points: multiple PA angulations (minimum three films differing by 15 degrees) and X-ray beam aligned within +/-4 degrees of the fracture line; reducing CBCT projections from 180 to 60 did not change root-fracture diagnosis (Mora et al.).
- Prevalence context: VRF in endodontically treated teeth 3.7-30.8% (cited).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 3 source guidelines, 2 of them scoring below 50 on AGREE II rigor; evidence base is mostly in vitro (experimental) studies on extracted teeth.
- No recommendation by tooth location (maxillary/mandibular, anterior/posterior) or root number; no cost-effectiveness analysis beyond a qualitative note on Korean insurance coverage.
- Recent studies conflict on CBCT performance in root-filled teeth; the guideline is scheduled for periodic update.
- Source-data issue: the dose paragraph cites i-CAT effective doses of "75.3 and 110.5 mSv" vs panoramic "13 mSv" (ratio 5.8-8.5); the unit is almost certainly microsievert (uSv), as mSv would be implausible. Reproduced as printed and flagged.

## 7. Glossary
- **K-CIG**: Korean Clinical Imaging Guidelines, an adaptation methodology (NECA with radiology societies) for building imaging guidelines from existing guidelines.
- **AGREE II**: Appraisal of Guidelines for Research and Evaluation II; "Rigor of Development" domain >=50 used as the inclusion cut-off.
- **Delphi method**: anonymous iterative expert rating (here 1-9 agreement scale, 2 rounds, 6 experts).
- **VRF**: vertical root fracture, a longitudinal root fracture most often in root-filled teeth.
- **FOV**: field of view; small-FOV CBCT limits volume and dose.
- **Beam hardening**: artifact from dense materials (gutta-percha, metal posts) that can mimic or mask fracture lines on CBCT.
