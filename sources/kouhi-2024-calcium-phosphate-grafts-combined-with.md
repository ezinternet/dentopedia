## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (J Evid Based Dent Pract 2024; PRISMA; random-effects REML; Cochrane RoB 5.1.0) of RCTs comparing calcium phosphate (CP) grafts plus GTR versus open flap debridement (OFD): 451 records, 7 studies pooled (5 intrabony, 2 furcation).

Intrabony: WMD for CAL gain 1.536 mm (reported 95% CI -0.933 to 2.139) and PD reduction 1.601 mm (95% CI -0.727 to 2.474), both reported as significant; furcation: CAL gain 1.807 mm (95% CI 0.346-3.268, significant), PD reduction 1.519 mm (95% CI 1.215-4.253, not significant).

Authors conclude CP + GTR is superior to OFD, but the intrabony CIs as printed span zero while labeled significant (source inconsistency), and only 7 studies (2 furcation) contribute — interpret cautiously (abstract-only).

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (SR+MA; J Evid Based Dent Pract 2024; PRISMA; REML 랜덤효과; Cochrane RoB 5.1.0): 칼슘포스페이트 (Calcium Phosphate, CP) 이식재 + 조직유도재생술 (GTR) vs 개방 판막 소파술 (OFD) RCT — 451편 중 7편 통합 (골내결손 5, 치근이개부 2).

골내결손: 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득 WMD 1.536 mm (보고된 95% CI -0.933 to 2.139), 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 감소 1.601 mm (95% CI -0.727 to 2.474)로 모두 유의하다고 보고; 치근이개부: CAL 획득 1.807 mm (95% CI 0.346-3.268, 유의), PD 감소 1.519 mm (95% CI 1.215-4.253, 비유의).

저자는 CP + GTR이 OFD보다 우월하다고 결론내렸으나, 골내결손 CI가 0을 포함한 채 유의로 표기된 원문 불일치가 있고 7편 (치근이개부 2편)뿐이라 신중한 해석 필요 (초록 전용).

## 2. Key Contributions

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA, PRISMA protocol, random-effects (REML)
- **Search**: electronic and manual; English-language RCTs
- **n**: 451 records screened; 7 studies in meta-analysis (5 intrabony, 2 furcation)
- **Outcomes**: PD, CAL; risk of bias with Cochrane tools v5.1.0
