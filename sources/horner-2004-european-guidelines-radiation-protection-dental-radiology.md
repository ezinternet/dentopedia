## Three-line Summary

European Commission Radiation Protection 136, developed by University of Manchester for the EU, synthesizes dose data, age-stratified risk, and evidence-graded referral criteria for all dental radiographic modalities, implementing Directives 96/29/Euratom and 97/43/Euratom.
Key dose benchmarks: intraoral 1–8.3 µSv, panoramic 3.85–30 µSv, cephalometric 2–3 µSv, mandibular CT 364–1,202 µSv; age risk multipliers: ×3 for under-10s, ×0.3 for age 50–80.
Provides legally enforceable ED-graded requirements alongside A–C evidence-graded recommendations covering justification, equipment optimization, quality assurance, and staff protection across the EU dental primary-care setting.

## 세줄요약

유럽위원회(European Commission) 방사선방호 지침 No.136 — 맨체스터대학교가 EU를 위해 개발한 치과 방사선 선량·연령별 위험도·근거등급 의뢰기준 종합서로, EU 기본안전기준 지령(Euratom Directive) 96/29 및 97/43을 구현함.
핵심 선량: 구내방사선(Intraoral Radiography) 1–8.3 µSv, 파노라마(Panoramic Radiography) 3.85–30 µSv, 두부규격(Cephalometric Radiography) 2–3 µSv, 하악 컴퓨터단층촬영(CT) 364–1,202 µSv; 연령 위험도 배수: 10세 미만 ×3, 50–80세 ×0.3.
촬영 정당성(Justification)·장비 최적화·품질보증(Quality Assurance, QA)·직원방호에 걸쳐 법적 강제(ED등급) + 근거등급(A~C) + 국가권고(NR) 3층 권고 체계를 EU 1차 치과진료 현장에 제공.

## Key Contributions

- First EU-wide comprehensive radiation protection guideline for dental radiology implementing both Euratom Directives simultaneously
- Tiered evidence grading: ED (legally required Euratom Directive articles), A (SR/RCT), B (cohort/case-control), C (case series/expert opinion), NR (national recommendations)
- Detailed effective dose table (Table 2.3): intraoral 1–8.3 µSv, panoramic 3.85–30 µSv, lateral ceph 2–3 µSv, mandibular CT 364–1,202 µSv
- Age-stratified stochastic risk multipliers (Table 2.2): <10 yrs ×3, 10–20 ×2, 20–30 ×1.5, 30–50 ×0.5, 50–80 ×0.3, 80+ negligible
- Full chapter 3 referral criteria covering caries (children/adults), orthodontics, periodontics, endodontics, new adult patients, edentulous patients, implantology, oral surgery, pregnancy
- Diagnostic Reference Levels (DRLs) for all major modalities with survey data from multiple EU member states
- Quality standards tables for bitewing, periapical, panoramic, and cephalometric radiography
- Staff protection guidance: ALARA in practice, distance rules, controlled area classification, facility design

## Methodology

- **Design**: Evidence-based guideline development; systematic literature review + expert panel consensus
- **Grading system**: ED (Euratom Directive), A (SR/RCT), B (cohort/case-control), C (case series/expert opinion), NR (national recommendations)
- **Scope**: All dental radiographic modalities in EU primary care dental practice
- **Panel**: 9-member multidisciplinary expert group (oral and maxillofacial imaging, radiology, statistics, EU member states)
- **Mandate**: Update and extend Radiation Protection 81 (1995) under contract to University of Manchester

## Key Results

**Effective dose benchmarks (Table 2.3):**

| Modality | Effective Dose |
|---|---|
| Intraoral (bitewing/periapical) | 1–8.3 µSv |
| Panoramic | 3.85–30 µSv |
| Lateral cephalometric | 2–3 µSv |
| CT scan mandible | 364–1,202 µSv |

**Stochastic risk by age at exposure (Table 2.2):**

| Age group | Risk multiplier |
|---|---|
| <10 years | ×3 |
| 10–20 years | ×2 |
| 20–30 years | ×1.5 |
| 30–50 years | ×0.5 |
| 50–80 years | ×0.3 |
| 80+ years | negligible |

**Dental radiology's EU context**: accounts for ~⅓ of all radiological examinations in the European Union.

**Key referral criteria findings:**
- Caries diagnosis in children: bitewing radiographs at intervals dictated by caries risk status, not routine
- New adult patients: individualized assessment, not routine full-mouth series
- Implantology: cross-sectional imaging (CT) required for pre-operative bone assessment; panoramic as baseline
- Endodontics: periapical radiographs at key stages (pre-op, working length, pre-condensation, post-op, review)

**Equipment optimization priorities:**
- E-speed or F-speed film; rectangular collimation for intraoral
- Filtration minimum 2.5 mm Al equivalent at 70 kV
- Digital receptors: CCD/PSP offer dose reduction but require own QA protocols
- Leaded aprons: limited protective benefit; thyroid collar of limited additional value for intraoral

**Staff dose limits (Chapter 6, from Directive 96/29):**
- Occupational workers: 20 mSv/year (averaged over 5 years); maximum 50 mSv in any single year
- Members of public: 1 mSv/year
- 2m rule: occupational exposure effectively negligible at >2m from X-ray tube during exposure

## Limitations

- Published 2004; CBCT as emerging technology was not yet addressed (limited discussion only; pre-dates widespread clinical CBCT adoption)
- No systematic review of intraoral digital receptors vs film — digital section relies on available survey data
- National variations across EU member states not fully harmonized (NR category acknowledges this)
- No individual EU member state enforcement audit
- CT dose data ranges reflect 1990s–early 2000s equipment; current CT dose management may differ

## Glossary

- **ALARA** (As Low As Reasonably Achievable): Core radiation protection principle — minimize dose while achieving diagnostic objectives; foundational to all EU recommendations in this document
- **Effective dose** (µSv/mSv): Radiation dose weighted for tissue radiosensitivity and radiation type; allows modality comparison
- **Diagnostic Reference Level (DRL)**: Investigation level for patient dose (not dose limit); doses exceeding DRL trigger review of justification and technique
- **Directive 96/29/Euratom**: EU basic safety standards for workers and public; mandates dose limits and ALARA
- **Directive 97/43/Euratom (Medical Exposures Directive)**: Requires justification, optimization, and practitioner training for all medical X-ray exposures
- **ED grading**: Euratom Directive — legally binding requirement that must be implemented by all EU Member States
