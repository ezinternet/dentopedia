## Why Ingested

No held paper provided CSSA (Cross-Sectional Surface Area) threshold values for predicting fracture risk in monolithic zirconia full-arch prostheses from a large private practice dataset. This fills the critical design-parameter gap with statistically significant connector CSSA and posterior prosthetic space thresholds (≥80mm², >10mm height) for fracture prevention.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study of 140 monolithic zirconia full-arch IFCDPs (digital workflow, private practice), up to 8yr follow-up; survival assessment plus CSSA and posterior linear height measurement correlated with fracture occurrence; 6 fractures (3 maxilla, 3 mandible).

95.7% survival (mean 3yr, range 1–8yr); non-fractured maxillary CSSA 80.5mm² vs fractured 62.5mm² (Δ17.9mm², p<0.001); mandibular: 79.4 vs 54mm² (Δ25.4mm², p<0.001); posterior height non-fractured maxillary 10.58 vs fractured 9mm (p=0.018); mandibular: 11.89 vs 9.55mm (p<0.001).

Clinical thresholds: connector CSSA ≥80mm² and posterior prosthetic space >10mm during CAD design to prevent fracture in monolithic zirconia IFCDPs.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest single private practice dataset for monolithic zirconia full-arch (140 jaws, up to 8yr)
- First study to define CSSA threshold ≥80mm² as the fracture-prevention design target for both maxilla and mandible
- Posterior prosthetic space >10mm threshold with statistical significance for both arches
- 95.7% survival from a private practice setting (vs academic centers) adds generalizability
- Equal maxillary and mandibular fracture distribution (3+3)

## 4. Key Results

### Survival

- **IFCDPs fractured**: 6/140 (3 maxilla, 3 mandible)
- **Survival rate**: 95.7%
- **Mean follow-up**: 3yr (range 1–8yr)

### CSSA Thresholds

| Arch | Non-fractured CSSA | Fractured CSSA | Difference | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maxilla | 80.5 mm² | 62.5 mm² | **Δ17.9 mm²** | <0.001 |
| Mandible | 79.4 mm² | 54.0 mm² | **Δ25.4 mm²** | <0.001 |

### Posterior Prosthetic Space Thresholds

| Arch | Non-fractured height | Fractured height | Difference | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maxilla | 10.58 mm | 9.00 mm | **Δ1.58 mm** | 0.018 |
| Mandible | 11.89 mm | 9.55 mm | **Δ2.34 mm** | <0.001 |

### Design Thresholds (Recommended)

- Connector CSSA: **≥80 mm²**
- Posterior prosthetic space (linear height): **>10 mm**

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract only — details on implant number, angulation, arch distribution, fracture location, follow-up distribution not available
- Mean follow-up only 3yr (1–8yr range) — survivorship bias possible
- Private practice: single-provider technique may not generalize universally
- No bruxism subgroup analysis reported
