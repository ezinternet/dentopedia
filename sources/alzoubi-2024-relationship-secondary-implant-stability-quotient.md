## Why Ingested

Answers the "low secondary ISQ at the post-healing timepoint" question directly for a uniform oxidized (TiUnite) implant system: 3.5 mm platform, <35 Ncm insertion torque, failed primary stability, and two-stage surgery each scored significantly lower secondary ISQ at superstructure connection — yet the cohort mean stayed high (77.30), extending [[wiki/implants/isq/gomez-polo-2016-length-diameter-bone-quality-stability]]'s diameter-significant/length-null direction into the post-healing phase and refining [[wiki/implants/isq/shiffler-2016-implant-length-diameter-location-isq]]'s null diameter finding (which tested only 4.3/5.0 mm) by isolating the 3.5 mm platform as the driver. Its multivariate regression aligns with [[wiki/implants/isq/huang-2017-multivariate-regression-isq-prediction]]: platform diameter was the only significant predictor of pre-restoration (secondary) ISQ.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective chart review (Kuwait University Dental Center, 2016–2020) of 135 patients with 305 anodically oxidized (TiUnite) tapered NobelReplace implants placed by a single surgeon, relating secondary ISQ measured at superstructure connection (mean 20.3 ± 8.8 weeks post-placement) to 7 implant- and protocol-related factors via group comparisons and multivariate linear regression.

Univariate, platform diameter (P<0.0001; 3.5 mm ≈72 vs 4.3 mm ≈78 vs 5.0 mm ≈81 patient-level), surgical protocol (one vs two-stage, P=0.006), insertion torque (<35 vs >35 Ncm, P=0.001) and primary stability (achieved vs not, P=0.001) each showed significant secondary ISQ differences; in the regression only platform diameter remained a significant positive predictor (patient-level B=5.25, P<0.0001, adjusted R²=0.25; implant-level B=4.28, P<0.0001, R²=0.11); cohort mean secondary ISQ 77.30 ± 7.22 (patient-level) and 77.15 ± 7.40 (implant-level).

Wider platforms increase secondary stability before final restoration — clinically useful for soft-bone sites and for reaching ISQ thresholds such as ≥65 for immediate loading — while length, loading protocol, and (at patient level) simultaneous grafting showed no significant effect; generalization is limited by the retrospective single-surgeon, single-implant-system design.

## 세줄요약

쿠웨이트단대 구강클리닉 2016–2020 후향 차트리뷰(환자 135명, 산화피막 TiUnite tapered 노벨리플레이스 임플란트 305개, 단일 술자): 상부구조 연결 시점(식립 후 평균 20.3 ± 8.8주)의 이차 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)와 7개 임플란트·프로토콜 관련 인자의 관계를 집단비교와 다변량 선형회귀로 평가.

단변량에서 플랫폼 직경(P<0.0001, 3.5mm≈72 대 4.3mm≈78 대 5.0mm≈81), 술식(1단계 대 2단계, P=0.006), 삽입토크(<35 대 >35 Ncm, P=0.001), 일차안정성(달성 대 미달성, P=0.001)이 각각 이차 ISQ 유의 차이; 다변량 회귀에서는 플랫폼 직경만 유의 양의 예측인자(환자단위 B=5.25, P<0.0001, 조정 R²=0.25; 임플란트단위 B=4.28, R²=0.11)였고, 코호트 평균 이차 ISQ는 77.30 ± 7.22.

더 넓은 플랫폼이 상부구조 연결 전 이차안정성을 높이므로(연조직골 부위·즉시하중 ISQ 문턱 ≥65 달성에 유용), 길이·하중 프로토콜·(환자단위) 동시골이식은 유의 영향 없음 — 단, 후향·단일술자·단일임플란트시스템 설계로 일반화는 제한적.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Heliyon 2024;10(20):e39156 (published 2024-10-10)
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e39156
- **Institution**: Kuwait University Health Sciences Center / Kuwait University Dental Center, Kuwait; Imam Abdulrahman bin Faisal University, Saudi Arabia

## 2. Key Contributions

- First regression-based isolation of implant platform diameter as the **only** significant positive predictor of secondary (post-healing) ISQ for a uniform oxidized (TiUnite) implant system at superstructure connection — patient-level B=5.25, implant-level B=4.28, both P<0.0001.
- Demonstrates that factors significant in univariate comparisons (insertion torque, primary stability, surgical protocol) all drop out of the multivariate model once patient-level covariates are adjusted for — comparative-only designs overstate their role.
- Well-powered, confound-controlled cohort (target 270 implants/100 patients, actual 305/135) with a single surgeon and single implant design/surface, removing operator- and design-related variance.
- Documents that the diameter effect is driven by the 3.5 mm platform (3.5 vs both larger diameters significant; 4.3 vs 5.0 not), which reconciles an earlier null diameter finding in the same implant system (Shiffler et al.).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective chart review of implant patients (2016–2020), STROBE-compliant observational cohort; approval VDR/EC/3676/2020 + IRB-2021-02-494.
- **Inclusion**: age 21–90, ASA 1–2, Smoker/non-smoker, normoglycemic (HbA1C <5.7%) or controlled diabetic (HbA1C <8%), RFA recorded at superstructure connection; exclusion of extensive pre-placement grafting, significant healing-compromising conditions, head/neck radiation.
- **n**: 135 patients / 305 implants (all TiUnite anodically oxidized, tapered NobelReplace, conical connection; same PI placed all).
- **RFA**: Osstell IDx (3rd generation), read by the PI at superstructure connection; mean 20.29 ± 8.83 weeks post-placement (median 18; range 7–55).
- **Factors (7)**: implant length (8.0/10.0/11.5/13.0 mm), platform diameter (3.5/4.3/5.0 mm), loading protocol (immediate/delayed), surgical protocol (one/two stage), simultaneous grafting (yes/no), primary stability (achieved = no detectable mobility at insertion, or not), insertion torque (<35 vs >35 Ncm).
- **Outcomes**: secondary ISQ, analyzed at patient level (one implant blindly selected per patient by masking the ISQ column) and implant level. Statistics: Mann–Whitney U / Kruskal–Wallis; hierarchical multivariate linear regression with block 1 = patient covariates (age, gender, smoking, diabetes, healing time, implantation site) and block 2 = implant-/protocol-related factors. Sample size via G*Power (type I 5%, effect 0.15, power 80%). SPSS 26; GraphPad Prism 9.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Mean secondary ISQ: patient-level 77.30 ± 7.22; implant-level 77.15 ± 7.40.

Patient-level ISQ by platform diameter (P<0.0001 overall; 3.5 vs 4.3 P=0.0002; 3.5 vs 5.0 P<0.0001; 4.3 vs 5.0 P=0.06 NS):

| Platform diameter | Patient-level N (%) | Patient ISQ (SD) | Implant-level n (%) | Implant ISQ (SD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.5 mm | 40 (30%) | 72.09 (6.46) | 71 (23%) | 72.78 (7.72) |
| 4.3 mm | 48 (36%) | 78.00 (6.75) | 141 (46%) | 77.82 (6.88) |
| 5.0 mm | 47 (35%) | 81.01 (5.61) | 93 (31%) | 79.46 (6.52) |

Other comparisons (patient-level; implant-level in parentheses):
- Implant length: NS (P=0.07; implant 0.59)
- Loading protocol immediate vs delayed: NS (P=0.28; 0.12)
- Grafting: NS patient-level (P=0.33); significant implant-level, lower ISQ in grafted sites (78.46 vs 76.27, P=0.01)
- Surgical protocol one- vs two-stage: 79.54 vs 76.42, P=0.006 (implant 78.71 vs 76.53, P=0.002)
- Insertion torque >35 vs <35 Ncm: 78.36 vs 74.59, P=0.001 (implant 77.65 vs 76.03, P=0.007)
- Primary stability achieved vs not: 77.97 vs 73.43, P=0.001 (implant 77.52 vs 74.88, P=0.003)

Multivariate regression (final model, block 2, patient-level): implant diameter only significant predictor B=5.25, P<0.0001, adjusted R²=0.25 (~25% of ISQ variance); implant-level B=4.28, P<0.0001, adjusted R²=0.11. Insertion torque (B=0.10, P=0.95), primary stability (B=3.07, P=0.15), implant length (−0.12, P=0.80), surgical/loading protocol and grafting all non-significant in the adjusted model.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, single center, single expert surgeon, single implant system/surface — limits generalization to other operators, protocols, and implant designs.
- Uneven subgroup sizes (e.g. delayed vs immediate loading 73/27%, grafting 59%) and inclusion of smokers (18%) and diabetics (16%) mainly to reach the target sample size.
- RFA measures stiffness of the implant–bone unit, not bone-to-implant contact (BIC); ISQ is not a direct measure of osseointegration, and the model explains only ~25% (patient) / 11% (implant) of ISQ variance.
- Data not deposited in a public repository; additional prospective multi-system comparisons of ISQ predictors are warranted.

## 6. Related Work

- Huang H. et al. 2017 (PLoS One; multivariate regression, 557 implants, 2 systems): implant diameter the only general pre-restoration (T2) ISQ predictor — direct agreement with the present platform-diameter finding.
- Huang H. et al. 2016 (Ther Clin Risk Manag): at T2 both diameter (β=0.414) and insertion torque (β=0.150) predicted ISQ — in the present adjusted model torque is no longer significant.
- Shiffler et al. 2016 (same NobelReplace TiUnite system): only 4.3/5.0 mm diameters tested, reported no diameter effect — the 3.5 mm platform, untested there, drives the difference here.
- Zix et al. 2008 (SLA Straumann): secondary ISQ affected only by diameter (3.3 vs 4.1/4.8), higher ISQ for wider implants, length irrelevant — consistent.
- gomez-polo-2016 (prospective, InHex): diameter and bone type drive IT/primary ISQ, length null — shared diameter-significant/length-null axis, here extended to the secondary timepoint.
- TiUnite surface background: in vivo series showing accelerated early coupled bone formation/remodeling and reduced proinflammatory response vs machined implants (Omar 2010/2011; Lennerås 2015).

## 7. Glossary

- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: 1–100 scale derived from resonance frequency analysis; higher values = higher implant stability.
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis)**: non-invasive technique using a piezoelectric transducer and magnetic peg; measures the stiffness of the implant–bone junction, not BIC.
- **Primary stability (일차안정성)**: mechanical anchorage at installation, from frictional contact between implant and host bone.
- **Secondary stability (이차안정성)**: biological anchorage established through osseointegration (new bone formation and remodeling) over healing.
- **Superstructure connection**: the time the abutment/final restoration is connected to the implant (here, the secondary ISQ registration point).
- **TiUnite**: anodically oxidized (spark anodization) titanium surface with a thick (1–10 μm), porous, moderately rough, crystalline, phosphorus-containing oxide layer.
- **BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact)**: histologic proportion of the implant surface in direct contact with bone; RFA does not correlate with it.
