## Why Ingested

교흔(morsicatio buccarum/cheek biting)에 특화된 유병률·감별 데이터가 필요해 인제스트. [[oral-medicine/normal-variants/gupta-2023-prevalence-distribution-oral-mucosal-nepal]]이 normal variant 전반(Fordyce granules, linea alba)의 인구 기반 유병률을 제공한다면, 본 논문은 frictional keratosis·tobacco pouch keratosis·morsicatio buccarum 3종의 white lesion을 직접 비교해 교흔이 linea alba의 연속선상 병소임을 명확히 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective clinical study of 144,056 South Indian dental patients (2019-2023) identified 5,613 (3.89%) with an innocuous keratotic white lesion — frictional keratosis (71.65%), tobacco pouch keratosis (27.38%), or morsicatio buccarum (0.9%) — all significantly more common in men, distinguishing these benign entities from oral potentially malignant white lesions.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: Cureus, 2024;16(1):e52199
- Publication date: 2024-01-13
- Institution: Saveetha Dental College and Hospitals, Chennai, India; ethics approval IHEC/SDC/UG-2157/23/OMED/303
- Conflicts of interest: none declared

## 2. Key Contributions

- Direct three-way epidemiological comparison of frictional keratosis, tobacco pouch keratosis, and morsicatio buccarum (MB) in a single large cohort (144,056 screened, 5,613 cases).
- Clarifies MB as a continuation of linea alba along the occlusal biting plane, arising from chronic cheek biting rather than a discrete point irritant (as in frictional keratosis).
- Reports age- and gender-specific distributions useful for clinical differentiation without biopsy.

## 3. Methodology

- Retrospective clinical study using DIAS (Digital Information and Archiving Software) records, Saveetha Dental College, June 2019-June 2023.
- Inclusion: clinically identifiable irritant/trauma source (frictional keratosis), history of SLT/quid use (tobacco pouch keratosis), or history of cheek biting (MB); diagnosis confirmed by treatment response.
- Exclusion: no clinical response to conservative management after 4 weeks, or coexisting OPMD.
- Statistics: SPSS v26.0, chi-square test with post hoc comparisons; Shapiro-Wilk normality test.

## 4. Results

- Total 5,613/144,056 patients (3.89% prevalence) had one of the three lesions; mean age 37.28y (range 18-78y), approximately normally distributed.
- Frictional keratosis: n=4,026 (71.65%), mean age 26.28y, 72.5% male / 27.5% female.
- Tobacco pouch keratosis: n=1,537 (27.38%), mean age 44.53y, 93.55% male / 6.45% female.
- Morsicatio buccarum (MB): n=54 (0.9%), mean age 29.34y, 75.92% male / 24.08% female.
- Chi-square test for gender association within lesion category: p=0.01; linear-by-linear association gender×lesion type: p=0.034 (both significant, male predominance across all three).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-center (unicentric) South Indian population — limited generalizability.
- Diagnosis based on clinical exam + treatment response, not histopathological confirmation.
- Authors suggest future studies comparing treatment modalities and healing rapidity.

## 7. Glossary

- **Frictional keratosis**: benign hyperkeratotic white patch from mechanical trauma (sharp tooth, appliance, toothbrush); non-scrapable, resolves after removing the irritant.
- **Tobacco pouch keratosis**: white/grey velvety patch at smokeless-tobacco (SLT) contact site from chronic quid-related frictional irritation; resolves in 5-6 weeks after habit cessation.
- **Morsicatio buccarum (MB)**: keratotic white line along the occlusal/bite plane from chronic cheek biting; continuation of linea alba; may relate to anxiety, psychiatric conditions, or rare syndromes (Lesch-Nyhan, Riley-Day).
- **DIAS**: Digital Information and Archiving Software (institutional dental record system).
