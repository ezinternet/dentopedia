## Why Ingested

Provides 1-year clinical data for the Straumann® 2.9 mm BLT Roxolid/SLActive implant (Category 2 NDI per Schiegnitz classification) in anterior single-tooth gaps — fills the specific product-level evidence gap that SR+MA [[wiki/implants/survival/schiegnitz-2018-narrow-diameter-implants-systematic-review]] identified for 2.5–3.3 mm two-piece NDIs.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-arm multicenter PMCF study (n=41, 5 German centers) evaluating the Straumann® 2.9 mm bone-level tapered (BLT) Roxolid®/SLActive implant for single-tooth replacement at maxillary lateral and mandibular incisor sites over 12 months.
Implant survival 92.7% (95%-CI 79.0–97.6%) at 1 year; success rate 89.7% (95%-CI 75.8–97.1%); mean relative MBL −0.3 mm (±0.42); PES 11.6/14 at 12 months; 3 implant losses all before provisional crown insertion; no fractures.
The 2.9 mm two-piece BLT implant is safe and clinically viable for narrow anterior gaps, but the 92.7% 1-year survival is modest compared to broader NDI SR+MA benchmarks and warrants longer follow-up.

## Key Contributions

- First multicenter prospective clinical data specifically for the Straumann® 2.9 mm BLT Roxolid® implant in anterior single-tooth gaps
- Applied the updated Schiegnitz (2021) NDI classification (Category 2: 2.5–<3.3 mm two-piece) — distinguishes this two-piece 2.9 mm implant from Category 1 mini-implants
- Reported 0 implant fractures over 12 months despite 2.9 mm diameter in Roxolid® alloy — supports fracture resistance of Ti-Zr alloy
- Documented PES 11.6/14 at 12 months — adequate esthetic soft tissue outcome
- 34.1% of placements required simultaneous bone augmentation — quantifies real-world augmentation burden for this site/diameter combination

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective, single-arm, multicenter, post-market clinical follow-up (PMCF), STROBE-compliant
- **Implant**: Straumann® BLT ∅ 2.9 mm SC, Roxolid®, SLActive (lengths: 10/12/14 mm)
- **Sites**: Maxillary lateral incisors (FDI 12/22) and all mandibular incisors (31–42)
- **n**: 41 patients, 41 implants; 38 completed the study
- **Enrollment**: October 2015 – December 2019 (Germany)
- **Primary outcome**: Implant survival at 12 months (Kaplan–Meier)
- **Secondary**: Implant success, Pink Esthetic Score (PES, 0–14), marginal bone level change (periapical X-ray), PI, mSBI, PPD, adverse events
- **Success criteria**: Survival + absence of pain/foreign body sensation/dysesthesia + no peri-implant infection with suppuration + no mobility + no radiolucency
- **Bone augmentation**: Simultaneous in 14/41 (34.1%); lateral 10, contour 4; resorbable collagen membrane in 11/14 (78.6%)
- **Bone quality**: Type II 24.4%, Type III 65.9%, Type IV 9.8%
- **Smokers**: 12.2% current (<10 cig/day); 80.5% non-smokers

## Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Implant survival at 12 months | **92.7%** (95%-CI 79.0–97.6%) |
| Implant success at 12 months | **89.7%** (95%-CI 75.8–97.1%) |
| Implant losses | 3/41 (all before provisional crown insertion) |
| Implant fractures | 0 |
| PES at 6 months | 11.2 (95%-CI 10.5–11.9) |
| PES at 12 months | 11.6 (95%-CI 11.1–12.1) |
| Relative MBL change at 12 months | −0.3 mm (±0.42; range 0.45 to −1.54 mm) |
| Absolute MBL at final crown insertion | −1.1 mm (±0.62) |
| mSBI at 12 months | 0.0–0.2 (distal highest) |
| PPD median at 12 months | 2.0 mm (range 1–4 mm) |

- Primary stability achieved in 40/41 (97.6%) — one unstable implant had uneventful course
- 80.5% submerged healing; 19.5% transgingival
- All 3 implant losses occurred before provisional crown; 1 additional case of persistent dysesthesia at 12 months
- Most common implant sites: FDI 12 (43.9%) and 22 (41.5%)

## Limitations

- Single-arm design — no control group (no direct comparison to SDI or wider NDI)
- Short follow-up: 12 months only; no long-term fracture or MBL data
- Small sample size (n=41); 92.7% survival with wide CI (79.0–97.6%)
- Predominantly anterior mandibular/maxillary incisor sites — limited generalizability to other positions
- High bone augmentation rate (34.1%) increases procedure complexity and confounds outcomes
- Bone type III/IV in 75.7% — soft bone majority, which may influence survival
- Post-market clinical follow-up (PMCF) design limits internal validity

## Glossary

- **BLT (Bone-Level Tapered)**: Two-piece tapered implant design with crestal bone-level platform; allows flexible prosthetic options
- **Roxolid®**: Straumann proprietary Ti-Zr alloy (83–87% Ti, 13–17% Zr) with higher fatigue resistance than cpTi
- **SLActive**: Hydrophilic sandblasted/acid-etched surface modification to accelerate osseointegration
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score)**: 7-parameter soft tissue esthetic assessment (0–14); ≥10 = good esthetic outcome
- **PMCF**: Post-Market Clinical Follow-up — regulatory requirement for medical devices after CE marking
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level)**: Radiographic distance from implant shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact
