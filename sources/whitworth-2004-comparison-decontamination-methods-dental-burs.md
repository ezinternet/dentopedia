## Three-line Summary

In-vitro decontamination assay study on 30 used dental burs plus experimentally contaminated unused burs (Streptococcus sanguis NCTC 7863 alone, or combined with blood/saliva/blood+saliva), comparing six pre-sterilisation cleaning techniques.

Microbial counts on used burs ranged 0–6.92×10^4 CFU/mL with multiple pathogenic species identified; autoclaving alone was not sufficient to sterilise dental burs, manual scrubbing in air was significantly less effective than manual scrubbing under water (p<0.001), and washer-disinfectors were the most effective pre-sterilisation cleaning method.

Clinical implication: pre-sterilisation cleaning (ideally washer-disinfector, or enzymic soak immediately after use) is a mandatory prerequisite step before autoclaving dental burs — autoclave sterilization cannot be relied upon alone; abstract-only record (no PMC full text retrieved).

## 세줄요약

사용된 치과용 버 30개 및 실험적으로 오염시킨(Streptococcus sanguis 단독 또는 혈액·타액·혈액+타액 병용) 새 버를 대상으로 6가지 멸균전 세척법을 비교한 in-vitro 오염제거 평가 연구.

사용된 버의 미생물 수는 0~6.92×10^4 CFU/mL 범위였고 다수의 병원성 균종이 확인되었으며, 오토클레이브 단독으로는 버 멸균에 불충분했고, 공기 중 수동 스크러빙은 수중 수동 스크러빙보다 유의하게 덜 효과적이었으며(p<0.001) washer-disinfector가 가장 효과적인 멸균전 세척법이었다.

임상적 의미: 치과용 버를 오토클레이브하기 전 멸균전 세척(이상적으로는 washer-disinfector, 또는 사용 직후 효소세제 침지)이 필수 선행 단계다 — 오토클레이브만으로는 멸균을 보장할 수 없다; 본 기록은 초록만 확보한 abstract-only 자료다(PMC 전문 미확보).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Introduced a novel assay system for quantitatively comparing pre-sterilisation cleaning technique efficacy on dental burs.
- Characterised the microbial burden (bacteria and fungi) present on clinically used dental burs after cavity preparation.
- Established experimentally that autoclaving alone does not sterilise contaminated dental burs — organic debris and biofilm must be removed beforehand.
- Ranked six pre-sterilisation cleaning techniques (manual scrubbing in air, manual scrubbing under water, enzymic agents, washer-disinfectors, among others) by log reduction in contamination achieved.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Thirty dental burs contaminated during clinical cavity preparation were collected and analysed for microbiological total viable counts and identification of bacterial/fungal species present.
- To simulate clinically contaminated burs under controlled conditions, unused dental burs were inoculated with a culture of *Streptococcus sanguis* NCTC 7863, either alone or combined with blood, saliva, or a mixture of blood and saliva.
- Contaminated burs were then subjected to six pre-sterilisation cleaning techniques.
- Log reduction in contamination achieved by each cleaning method was assessed and compared.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Microbial count from used dental burs ranged from 0 to 6.92 × 10^4 CFU/mL.
- Many potentially pathogenic bacterial and fungal species were identified on used burs.
- Autoclaving alone was not sufficient to sterilise dental burs.
- Manual scrubbing in air was significantly less effective than manual scrubbing under water (p<0.001).
- The most effective method of pre-sterilisation cleaning for dental burs was a washer-disinfector.
- Enzymic agents were found suitable for soaking contaminated dental burs immediately after clinical use.

## 7. Glossary

- **Washer-disinfector**: An automated device that combines mechanical washing with thermal or chemical disinfection to clean and decontaminate reusable medical/dental instruments prior to sterilisation.
- **Enzymic agent**: A cleaning solution containing enzymes (e.g., proteases) designed to break down organic debris (blood, saliva, tissue) on instruments, typically used as an immediate post-use soak.
- **Pre-sterilisation cleaning**: The mechanical/chemical removal of organic and inorganic debris from an instrument before it undergoes a sterilisation process (e.g., autoclaving); necessary because sterilisation processes do not reliably penetrate or remove gross organic soil.
- **Log reduction**: A logarithmic (base-10) measure of the decrease in the number of viable microorganisms achieved by a decontamination process.
