## Why Ingested

이 논문은 [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]] narrative review가 인용한 hyaluronic acid (HyA, 히알루론산) 보조재의 "상반된 결과"를 뒷받침하는 원전 SR+MA다. Bubalo 2026이 HyA를 "일부 연구에서 crestal bone loss 증가"로 짧게 언급한 것과 달리, 본 논문(Domic 2023)은 5편의 전임상 연구 + 22편의 임상 연구(최종평가 1,062명)를 정량적으로 통합해 HyA의 발치와(extraction socket) 치유 효과를 부위·적응증별로(하악 제3대구치 수술적 발치 vs 정상 맹출치 발치 vs 치조골염 치료) 세분화한다. 특히 HyA가 신생골형성(bone formation)에는 메타분석에서 유의성이 없었고, 일부 연구에서는 치조제 수평골소실이 오히려 증가했다는 결과가 Bubalo review의 "상반된 결과" 언급의 근거가 된다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PROSPERO-registered, PRISMA) of 5 preclinical studies (rats/dogs) and 22 clinical studies (1,062 patients at final evaluation) evaluating local hyaluronic acid (HyA) application after tooth extraction or for alveolar osteitis (AO/dry socket) treatment.

Meta-analysis showed HyA significantly reduced pain 7 days after surgical lower-third-molar (LM3) removal (effect size 0.32, 95% CI 0.12-0.51, p=0.01) vs control, but had no significant effect on early (2-3 day) pain, swelling, or trismus at any timepoint; preclinical bone-formation benefit seen in every individual study was not confirmed in meta-analysis of bone volume/tissue volume (BV/TV) at 3 months (p=0.42, high heterogeneity I²=89.89%).

Clinical implication: HyA may modestly reduce late post-LM3-removal pain and aid soft-tissue socket closure after regular extractions, but evidence for a hard-tissue/ridge-preservation benefit is weak-to-contradictory (one study reported significantly MORE coronal horizontal bone loss with HyA), and overall certainty of evidence is low-to-moderate (GRADE) due to small study numbers and inconsistent HyA product/dosing reporting.

## 세줄요약

PROSPERO 등록·PRISMA 기반 체계적 문헌고찰(SR)+메타분석(MA)으로, 발치 후 또는 치조골염(AO, dry socket) 치료를 위한 국소 히알루론산(hyaluronic acid, HyA) 적용을 다룬 전임상연구 5편(쥐/개)과 임상연구 22편(최종평가 1,062명)을 통합.

메타분석 결과 HyA는 하악 제3대구치(LM3) 수술적 발치 후 7일째 통증을 유의하게 감소시켰으나(effect size 0.32, 95% CI 0.12-0.51, p=0.01) 초기(2-3일) 통증·부종·개구제한(trismus)에는 유의한 효과가 없었고, 전임상 개별연구 전부에서 관찰된 신생골형성 개선효과는 3개월 시점 골부피/조직부피비(BV/TV) 메타분석에서는 확인되지 않음(p=0.42, 높은 이질성 I²=89.89%).

임상적 의미: HyA는 LM3 발치 후 후기 통증 및 정상 맹출치 발치 후 연조직 치유에 제한적 도움을 줄 수 있으나, 경조직/치조제 보존 효과에 대한 근거는 약하거나 상반되며(한 연구는 HyA군에서 오히려 치관측 수평골소실 증가 보고), 연구 수가 적고 HyA 제품/용량 보고가 비일관적이어서 전체 근거 확실성(GRADE)은 낮음-중등도.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First comprehensive combined preclinical + clinical systematic review of hyaluronic acid (HyA) across the full spectrum of tooth-extraction contexts: surgical lower-third-molar (LM3) removal, non-surgical extraction of regularly erupted teeth, and alveolar osteitis (AO/dry socket) treatment.
- Quantitative meta-analysis (5 RCTs) demonstrating a statistically significant reduction in pain 7 days after LM3 removal with HyA vs control (negative control or placebo/carrier), while explicitly showing no benefit at 2-3 days, and no benefit for swelling or trismus at either timepoint.
- Preclinical bone-formation meta-analysis (BV/TV at 3 months, 2 studies) that failed to confirm the positive effect seen in every individual preclinical study — a rare instance of the review explicitly flagging the preclinical-to-clinical translation gap.
- Documents a directly contradictory clinical bone finding: one study reported significantly increased coronal horizontal bone loss with post-extraction HyA gel application (3x/day for 1 week onto a collagen matrix following ridge preservation), directly relevant to HyA's role (or lack thereof) as a ridge-preservation adjunct.
- GRADE certainty ratings applied to all meta-analyzed outcomes: low (preclinical BV/TV), moderate (clinical pain, trismus), low (clinical swelling).

## 3. Methodology

- **Databases**: Ovid (MEDLINE + CENTRAL), EMBASE, PubMed; searched 14 Oct 2021, updated 7 Apr 2022.
- **Screening**: 147 records → 90 after dedup → 33 full-text → 5 preclinical + 22 clinical included after excluding 6 for product-type/design mismatch. Full-text screening agreement kappa = 1.0 (100%); final enrollment agreement kappa = 0.61 (84.9%).
- **Preclinical inclusion**: randomized/non-randomized controlled experiments, local HyA (alone or combined) applied after extraction of ≥1 tooth.
- **Clinical inclusion**: RCT, controlled trial, or case series (n≥10), local HyA application after extraction or for AO treatment; English or German language; full text available; clinical/radiographic/histological data reported.
- **Risk of bias**: SYRCLE RoB tool (preclinical), Cochrane RoB 2.0 (RCTs), ROBINS-I (non-randomized trials).
- **Statistics**: Pairwise meta-analysis restricted to RCTs with comparable design; REML heterogeneity (τ²) with Knapp-Hartung SE adjustment; split-mouth data treated as dependent (correlation=0.5); chi-square test for heterogeneity (p<0.1 significant), I² for extent; 95% prediction interval when ≥3 studies; STATA/IC 17.0.
- **GRADE**: GRADEpro GDT software for certainty-of-evidence grading of all meta-analyzed outcomes.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Study population**: Preclinical — 2 rat studies (Holtzman/Wistar, healthy and diabetic models), 3 beagle dog studies (20 dogs, infected extraction sockets). Clinical — LM3 removal: 603 patients (306 HyA sites, 370 control); regular tooth extraction: 349 patients (226 HyA, 257 control); AO treatment: 110 patients (90 HyA, 20 control).

**Meta-analysis results**:
| Outcome | Timepoint | n RCTs | Effect size | 95% CI | p | Heterogeneity (I²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pain (LM3) | 2-3 days | 4 | 0.52 | -0.34 to 1.38 | 0.15 (NS) | 0.00% |
| Pain (LM3) | 7 days | 5 | 0.32 | 0.12 to 0.51 | **0.01 (sig.)** | 0.00% |
| Swelling (LM3) | 2-3 days | 2 | -2.08 | -23.73 to 19.58 | 0.44 (NS) | 87.94% |
| Swelling (LM3) | 7 days | — | 1.75 | -14.38 to 17.89 | 0.40 (NS) | 66.86% |
| Trismus (LM3) | 2-3 days | 3 | 1.31 | -0.65 to 3.26 | 0.10 (NS) | 25.86% |
| Trismus (LM3) | 7 days | 5 | 1.08 | -0.97 to 3.12 | 0.22 (NS) | 55.99% |
| BV/TV (preclinical) | 3 months | 2 | 9.57 | -86.22 to 105.36 | 0.42 (NS) | 89.89% |

- Individual-study level: all 5 preclinical studies found significantly better bone-formation results with HyA (histologic/radiologic/immunohistochemical), independent of socket condition (healthy/infected) — but this did not survive pooling.
- Clinical soft-tissue healing (regularly erupted teeth): 3 of 4 studies found significantly improved soft-tissue healing with HyA.
- Clinical hard-tissue/ridge outcomes (regularly erupted teeth): mixed — 1 study improved bone formation at 30 days, 1 study reduced alveolar-diameter loss to 21 days, 2 studies found no difference or a significant HyA disadvantage (including the ridge-preservation study with increased coronal horizontal bone loss).
- AO treatment: 1 controlled study found HyA significantly reduced postoperative pain vs alvogyl; the second study had no control arm.
- Safety: HyA generally well tolerated; only 1 study reported prolonged (but physiological) bleeding time; no other adverse events reported across 27 studies.
- Risk of bias: preclinical quality scores 20-40% (SYRCLE); RCTs mostly "some concerns" (13) or "low risk" (5) per RoB 2.0.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small number of comparable RCTs per outcome/timepoint (2-5 studies), limiting statistical power and precluding meta-analysis for several outcomes (e.g., AO rate).
- Substantial heterogeneity for swelling and BV/TV outcomes (I² 66-90%), meaning pooled estimates for these outcomes should be interpreted cautiously.
- HyA product heterogeneity poorly reported: concentration unreported in 5/27 studies, cross-linking status unreported in 16/27 studies — precludes any dose-response or formulation-specific conclusion.
- No study assessed healing at the distal aspect of the second molar (the site most associated with post-LM3-extraction periodontal defects).
- Authors explicitly call for future SRs to separately analyze parallel-arm vs split-mouth designs and for more standardized HyA product/dosage reporting.

## 6. Related Work

- Updates/extends a prior systematic review on HyA in third-molar surgery that found pain reduction at both day 3 and day 7 (discrepancy attributed to 2 additional included RCTs and exclusion of one strongly-favorable non-randomized study in the present review).
- Relevant to [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]] as the primary quantitative source behind that review's brief, qualitative mention of "inconsistent/contradictory findings" for HyA as a ridge-augmentation adjunct.

## 7. Glossary

- **HyA (Hyaluronic Acid, 히알루론산)**: glycosaminoglycan with anti-inflammatory, anti-edematous, osteoinductive, and pro-angiogenic properties, used topically as a wound-healing adjunct.
- **LM3 (Lower Third Molar, 하악 제3대구치)**: mandibular wisdom tooth; surgical removal is the most common context for HyA adjunct trials in this review.
- **AO (Alveolar Osteitis, 치조골염/dry socket)**: painful post-extraction complication from lysis of the blood clot, occurring in 20-35% of surgical LM3 extractions.
- **BV/TV (Bone Volume/Tissue Volume, 골부피/조직부피비)**: radiographic/histomorphometric measure of new bone formation used as the main preclinical outcome.
- **PICOS**: Population, Intervention, Comparison, Outcomes, Study design — systematic review question framework.
- **SYRCLE RoB / RoB 2.0 / ROBINS-I**: risk-of-bias assessment tools for animal studies, RCTs, and non-randomized studies respectively.
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation — certainty-of-evidence framework.
