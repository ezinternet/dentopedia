## Why Ingested

Primary phantom measurement of fetal dose across all dental modalities (intraoral, panoramic, ceph, CBCT) with and without shields — the source behind dental fetal-dose numbers cited in Gamba 2024.

→ [[radiology/benavides-2023-patient-shielding-dentomaxillofacial-radiography]] — same conclusion on shielding
→ [[radiology/horner-2004-european-guidelines-radiation-protection-dental-radiology]] — 1 mSv public/fetal benchmark

## Three-line Summary

Anthropomorphic female phantom study (Helsinki; DMFR 2016) estimating upper-bound fetal and breast doses for intraoral, panoramic, cephalometric and CBCT examinations with and without lead shields (abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

Upper fetal doses were 0.009–6.9 µGy without shielding and 0.005–2.1 µGy with lead shields; breast doses 0.602–75.4 µGy unshielded.

Unshielded fetal doses were <1% of the 1 mSv annual public limit, so fetal and breast shielding was judged irrelevant — pregnancy is never a reason to avoid or postpone a clinically justified dental radiograph.

## 세줄요약

여성 인체모형 팬텀 연구(헬싱키, DMFR 2016) — 구내·파노라마·세팔로·CBCT 촬영 시 납 차폐 유무에 따른 태아·유방 선량 상한 추정 (초록만 — 전문 미확보).

태아 선량 상한: 차폐 없이 0.009–6.9 µGy, 납 차폐 시 0.005–2.1 µGy. 유방 선량은 차폐 없이 0.602–75.4 µGy.

차폐 없는 태아 선량도 공중 연간 한도 1 mSv의 1% 미만이므로 태아·유방 차폐는 의미 없다고 판단 — 임신은 적응증 있는 치과촬영을 피하거나 미룰 이유가 되지 않는다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Covers all four dental modalities in one phantom set-up.
- Highest unshielded fetal dose 6.9 µGy (<1% of 1 mSv).
- Shielding reduces relative dose but is clinically irrelevant at these absolute levels.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Anthropomorphic female phantom with dosimeters at fetal and breast positions; intraoral, panoramic, cephalometric and CBCT exposures with and without lead shields (abstract-level detail only).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Site | Unshielded | Shielded |
|---|---|---|
| Fetus (upper estimate) | 0.009–6.9 µGy | 0.005–2.1 µGy |
| Breast | 0.602–75.4 µGy | 0.002–10.4 µGy |

## 7. Glossary

- Tissue reaction (deterministic effect) — threshold effect (pregnancy loss, malformation, microcephaly, intellectual disability)
- Stochastic effect — probability scales with dose, no threshold assumed (childhood cancer)
- mGy / µGy / nGy — absorbed dose (1 mGy = 1,000 µGy = 1,000,000 nGy); for X-rays 1 mGy ≈ 1 mSv
