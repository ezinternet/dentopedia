## Why Ingested

Every page we hold on 무이식 측방접근 (Graftless Lateral Approach) judges the outcome vertically — graft height gain, endo-sinus bone, survival ([[sinus-lift/lateral/dongo-2018-lateral-sinus-floor-graftless-meta-analysis]], 97.5% pooled survival over 660 implants). This is the first page in the wiki that measures the *horizontal* price of the same procedure (mean −2.08±1.06 mm crestal width at 6 months, 100% short-term survival) and ties it to a preoperative CBCT variable — 측방골벽 두께 (Lateral Wall Thickness) <1 mm vs >1 mm.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center CBCT study (35 consecutive sinuses/patients, Xiamen Stomatological Hospital, Sept 2023–Dec 2024) of lateral sinus elevation with collagen sponges only (no bone graft) plus simultaneous implant placement, testing whether preoperative lateral wall thickness (LWT) predicts horizontal crestal bone change at 6 months.

Crestal width fell from 10.97±2.63 to 8.85±2.42 mm (change 2.08±1.06 mm); LWT <1 mm lost significantly more width than LWT >1 mm (2.45±0.97 vs 1.54±0.98 mm, p=0.01), with a medium negative correlation between LWT and width change (r=−0.47, p=0.004) and between residual bone height (RBH) and width change (r=−0.36, p=0.03).

Graftless lateral sinus lift is vertically safe (100% short-term survival, no complications) but costs ~2 mm of horizontal crestal bone — a dimension no vertical endpoint captures — and thin walls (<1 mm) plus low RBH mark the high-remodeling group; retrospective design, n=35, and no power calculation limit inference.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일기관 콘빔 전산화단층촬영 (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT) 연구 (연속 35 증례/환자, Xiamen Stomatological Hospital, 2023-09–2024-12): 콜라겐 스펀지만 사용한 무이식 측방접근 (Graftless Lateral Approach) + 동시 임플란트 식립에서 술전 측방골벽 두께 (Lateral Wall Thickness, LWT) 가 6개월 수평 치조정 골 변화를 예측하는지 평가.

치조정 골폭 (Crestal Bone Width) 은 10.97±2.63 → 8.85±2.42 mm (변화 2.08±1.06 mm); LWT <1 mm 군이 >1 mm 군보다 유의하게 더 많이 감소 (2.45±0.97 vs 1.54±0.98 mm, p=0.01), LWT 와 골폭 변화의 중등도 음의 상관 (r=−0.47, p=0.004), 잔존골 높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 와도 음의 상관 (r=−0.36, p=0.03).

무이식 측방 상악동거상술은 수직적으로는 안전 (단기 생존율 100%, 합병증 0) 하지만 수평 치조정 골을 약 2 mm 잃는다 — 수직 지표로는 안 잡히는 축 — 이며, 얇은 골벽 (<1 mm) + 낮은 RBH 가 고위험군; 후향적 설계·n=35·검정력 계산 없음이 해석의 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First study to test **horizontal** crestal bone remodeling after graftless lateral sinus elevation with simultaneous implants — the literature's endpoints had been vertical (graft height, endo-sinus bone, survival) and volumetric.
- Quantifies the price of the procedure in the horizontal axis: mean 2.08±1.06 mm crestal width loss at 6 months (range 0.24–4.35 mm) despite 100% short-term implant survival and zero complications.
- Proposes **LWT as a preoperative CBCT prognostic variable**, with a clinically usable cut-off (1 mm): <1 mm walls remodeled 0.91 mm more than >1 mm walls (2.45 vs 1.54 mm, p=0.01).
- Extends the same inverse logic to **RBH** (r=−0.36, p=0.03): the more resorbed the ridge, the more horizontal bone is lost — i.e. the already-compromised site is the one that remodels most.
- Mechanistic framing: flap elevation disrupts mucoperiosteal blood supply; thick cortical wall resists osteoclastic remodeling and has more marrow space/vascularity, thin wall is mechanically and biologically more vulnerable.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective review of all consecutive lateral sinus lift procedures performed by one experienced surgeon, 1 Sept 2023 – 31 Dec 2024.
- **n**: 50 procedures screened → 15 excluded (bone graft n=8, membrane perforation n=1, lateral window border <3 mm from sinus floor n=3, missing CBCT n=3) → **35 cases (35 patients)** analyzed.
- **Inclusion**: RBH <5 mm; lateral sinus elevation with collagen sponges (no bone graft); simultaneous implant placement; both immediate-postoperative and 6-month CBCT available.
- **Surgery**: mid-crestal + two vertical incisions, full-thickness flap; 8×8 mm bony window with diamond bur (DASK, Dentium) 3–5 mm above estimated sinus floor; membrane reflected; Prichard periosteal elevator against the median wall; **six saline-soaked collagen sponges per site (20×20×5 mm, Jinling)**; no other graft; window covered with compressed non-soaked collagen; 4-0 nylon closure. No antibiotic prophylaxis intra-operatively; post-op antibiotics + analgesics.
- **CBCT protocol**: same scanner (Newtom, Italy), 100 kVp / 20 mA, 3.2 s, voxel 0.2 mm³; BlueSky Bio software, multiplanar reconstruction. All values are means of **three consecutive slices** (to damp implant artifact); two calibrated examiners, inter-examiner reliability by kappa.
- **Measurements**: crestal width at the center of the implant site on a line perpendicular to the implant axis through the coronal platform, bisecting the alveolar crest mesiodistally; **LWT measured ~10 mm above the sinus floor** (3–4 mm RBH + 7 mm); RBH in the same reference frame; bone density by Misch D-I–D-IV.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 27; Shapiro-Wilk normality; Student t-test for continuous variables by LWT group (>1 mm vs <1 mm); Chi-square for bone density; Pearson correlation for LWT/RBH vs crestal width change, with bone-density subgroup analysis; α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Baseline (Table I, n=35)**: age 42.5±11.2 y; 21 male (60%); molar 24 (68.5%), premolar 11 (31.5%); RBH 3.86±1.22 mm (range 2.03–4.7); LWT 1.07±0.45 mm (range 0.41–2.37); crestal width 10.97±2.63 mm pre-op → 8.85±2.42 mm at 6 months; width change 2.08±1.06 mm (range 0.24–4.35); D-II 21 (60%), D-III 14 (40%); implant diameter 3.3–4.5 mm, length 7–11 mm; no complications; implant survival 100%.

**LWT subgroups (Table II)**

| Variable | LWT >1 mm (n=14) | LWT <1 mm (n=21) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-op RBH (mm) | 3.93±1.37 | 3.81±1.15 | 0.80 |
| Pre-op LWT (mm) | 1.53±0.31 | 0.76±0.17 | <0.0001 |
| Pre-op crestal width (mm) | 11.09±2.99 | 10.89±2.43 | 0.84 |
| Post-op crestal width (mm) | 9.55±2.40 | 8.39±2.37 | 0.17 |
| **Change in crestal width (mm)** | **1.54±0.98** | **2.45±0.97** | **0.01** |
| D-II / D-III | 10 (71.4%) / 4 (28.6%) | 11 (52.4%) / 10 (41.6%) | 0.16 |

Groups were balanced at baseline on RBH, crestal width and density — the width-change difference is not explained by starting anatomy.

**Correlations with crestal width change (Table III)**

| Sample | Variable | r | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entire sample | LWT | −0.47 | 0.004 |
| Entire sample | RBH | −0.36 | 0.03 |
| D-II subgroup | LWT | −0.35 | 0.12 |
| D-II subgroup | RBH | −0.26 | 0.24 |
| D-III subgroup | LWT | −0.30 | 0.29 |
| D-III subgroup | RBH | −0.42 | 0.13 |

Negative sign = thinner wall / lower RBH → larger width loss. Subgroup correlations kept the direction but lost significance (n shrank). **Internal inconsistency to note**: Table I reports mean LWT 1.07±0.45 mm, while the Discussion states 0.91±0.43 mm (compared against Monje et al.'s 1.31 mm). The paper does not reconcile the two figures.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design — no causal inference.
- No delayed-placement control group, so the contribution of *simultaneous* implant placement cannot be separated from the graftless protocol.
- n=35, no power calculation (explicitly justified as first study on the topic); bone-density subgroups underpowered.
- CBCT measurements vulnerable to implant artifact; follow-up imaging not standardized across centers, precluding volumetric reconstruction.
- Patient factors with known remodeling influence (soft tissue thickness, sinus anatomy, systemic health, smoking) were not collected.
- Authors call for prospective designs, larger samples, standardized CBCT protocols, long-term follow-up, and trials of adjuncts (simultaneous horizontal grafting, flap-design modification, minimally invasive window preparation) in the thin-wall/low-RBH high-risk group.

## 6. Related Work
- Dongo 2018 (SR+MA, 11 studies, 660 implants): graftless lateral SFE pooled survival 97.5%, bone gain 5.7 mm — pure vertical/survival endpoint; Wu 2026 supplies the missing horizontal axis.
- Abullais 2024 (CBCT, 150 sinuses): population LWT reference values and their regional (premolar vs molar) and sex variation — the anatomical distribution Wu 2026 dichotomizes at 1 mm.
- Park 2023 (retrospective, 3 y): vertical graft height and resorption after lateral vs crestal SFE (~1 mm resorption, mostly year 1) — the vertical counterpart to Wu's horizontal loss.
- Monje et al. (cited by the authors, not held in the wiki): LWT 1.31 mm reference mean.

## 7. Glossary
- **LWT (Lateral Wall Thickness)**: buccal bony wall thickness of the maxillary sinus, measured here ~10 mm above the sinus floor; the osteotomy substrate and, per this study, a remodeling predictor.
- **RBH (Residual Bone Height)**: crest-to-sinus-floor bone height; inclusion required <5 mm (transcrestal contraindication).
- **Crestal bone width**: mesiodistal horizontal dimension of the alveolar crest at the implant platform level; measured at the implant site center.
- **Graftless lateral approach**: lateral window elevation with collagen sponges only, no particulate graft — the Schneiderian membrane and sponges provide the scaffold.
- **Misch D-I–D-IV**: bone density classification from CBCT (D-I dense cortical to D-IV fine trabecular); D-II/D-III dominate the posterior maxilla.
