## Why Ingested

기존 신경재생 페이지들은 도관(conduit) + 약리학적 보조제 축(PLCL+메틸코발라민 [[wiki/oral-surgery/zhang-2020-methylcobalamin-plcl-nerve-regeneration-conduit]], 은행잎 추출물 [[wiki/oral-surgery/lin-2007-ginkgo-biloba-nerve-regeneration-rat]])을 다뤄왔다. 본 논문(Zheng 2025)은 세포치료 축을 추가한다 — 치과에서 chairside로 얻을 수 있는 자가유래 세포인 치수줄기세포 (Dental Pulp Stem Cells, DPSC)가 미토콘드리아 전달을 통해 슈반세포 (Schwann Cell) 파이롭토시스 (Pyroptosis)를 완화한다는 구체적 기전을 제시하며, 원숭이(1년) + 랫드 안면신경손상 (Facial Nerve Injury, FNI) 모델 + in-vitro 3단 검증을 거쳤다.

## Three-line Summary

Preclinical mechanistic study — crab-eating macaque facial-nerve-transection model (n=12, 1-year follow-up), rat facial nerve injury (FNI) crush model (n=16), and in-vitro RSC96 Schwann-cell pyroptosis co-culture — evaluating dental pulp stem cells (DPSCs) for nerve regeneration.

DPSC-loaded nerve conduits improved long-term myelin/axon regeneration and CMAP recovery vs bare conduit in macaques (approaching autograft); in the rat FNI model and in vitro, DPSCs reduced Schwann-cell pyroptosis and mitochondrial ROS by transferring functional mitochondria via tunneling nanotubes (TNTs), a transfer amplified by Schwann-cell TNFα → NF-κB signaling in DPSCs, and TNFα-preconditioned DPSCs showed superior efficacy.

Purely preclinical (macaque/rat/in-vitro only, no human data) — DPSC cell therapy for facial nerve injury is a mechanistic proof-of-concept, not a clinical option today; dental pulp (extracted third molars) is highlighted as a practical, low-immunogenicity autologous cell source for future translation.

## 세줄요약

전임상 기전연구 — 게잡이원숭이 안면신경절단 모델(n=12, 1년 추적), 랫드 안면신경손상(FNI) 압박모델(n=16), RSC96 슈반세포 파이롭토시스 공동배양(in vitro) 3단 구조로 치수줄기세포(DPSC)의 신경재생 효과를 평가했다.

원숭이에서 DPSC 탑재 신경도관이 대조(Neurolac® 단독) 대비 장기 수초·축삭 재생과 CMAP 회복을 유의하게 개선(자가이식에 근접); 랫드 FNI 및 in vitro에서는 DPSC가 터널링 나노튜브(TNT)를 통해 기능성 미토콘드리아를 슈반세포로 전달함으로써 파이롭토시스와 미토콘드리아 ROS를 감소시켰고, 이 전달은 슈반세포 유래 TNFα→DPSC NF-κB 신호로 증폭되며 TNFα 전처리 DPSC가 더 우수한 효과를 보였다.

완전한 전임상 단계(원숭이/랫드/in vitro만, 인체 데이터 없음) — 안면신경손상에 대한 DPSC 세포치료는 기전적 개념증명이지 현재 임상 옵션이 아니며, 발치한 사랑니에서 얻는 치수는 향후 임상전환을 위한 실용적·저면역원성 자가세포원으로 제시된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. First demonstration that DPSCs rescue Schwann-cell pyroptosis specifically through intercellular mitochondrial transfer via tunneling nanotubes (TNTs), rather than paracrine signaling alone.
2. Establishes that transferred-mitochondria functional integrity (not mere transfer occurrence) is necessary for the protective effect — shown via mitochondrial-DNA-deficient ρ⁰-DPSCs that transfer normally but fail to rescue.
3. Identifies a feed-forward paracrine loop: pyroptotic Schwann cells secrete TNFα → activates NF-κB in DPSCs → increases mitochondrial biogenesis and TNT-mediated transfer → TNFα-preconditioned DPSCs show superior therapeutic efficacy in vivo.
4. Longest-duration (1-year) non-human-primate validation of DPSC-loaded nerve conduits for facial nerve regeneration in the literature at time of publication, approaching autograft-level functional recovery.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Macaque model**: 12 adult crab-eating macaques, 5 mm facial nerve defect, randomized to Neurolac® conduit alone, autologous nerve graft, or Neurolac® + DPSC injection (7.5×10⁷ cells/mL) at the proximal conduit. 12-month endpoint: H&E, Ki67 IHC, TEM (myelin/axon thickness, G-ratio), electrophysiology (CMAP amplitude/conduction velocity).
- **Rat FNI model**: 16 male SD rats, forceps-crush facial nerve injury, DPSC (5×10⁵ cells) or TNFα-preconditioned DPSC (20 ng/mL TNFα × 12 h) injected at injury site. Functional scoring (blink reflex, whisker movement, facial symmetry) at days 3/7/14/21/42, double-blind. TEM at 2 weeks; IF/WB for pyroptosis markers (GSDMD, cleaved-Caspase1, IL1β, S100, MPZ) and mitochondrial ROS (MitoSox) at 4 days.
- **In vitro**: RSC96 Schwann cells pyroptosis-induced with LPS (1 μg/mL, 24 h) + ATP (2 mM, 24 h) → LA-RSC96. Pharmacologic tools: Mito-TEMPO (mitochondrial ROS scavenger), Cytochalasin B (TNT blocker), Bay 11-7082 (NF-κB inhibitor), TNFα-neutralizing antibody. Direct-contact vs transwell co-culture to isolate contact-dependent mechanism. Mito-mCherry (DPSC) + CFSE (RSC96) labeling for confocal/flow-cytometry visualization and quantification of mitochondrial transfer. ρ⁰-DPSCs (mtDNA-depleted via ethidium bromide) to test dependence on functional mitochondria. RNA-seq/GO/KEGG comparing RSC96 vs LA-RSC96 secretome pathways.
- Statistics: two-tailed Student's t-test (2-group) or one-way ANOVA (multi-group); p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Macaque (12 mo): DPSCs@Neurolac® > Neurolac® alone in cell proliferation (Ki67), myelin/axon thickness, reduced G-ratio, and CMAP amplitude/conduction velocity (Neurolac® alone showed NO detectable CMAP on the injured side); DPSC group approached but did not fully match autograft.
- Rat FNI: DPSC treatment accelerated functional recovery; reduced GSDMD, cleaved-Caspase1, IL1β; reduced mitochondrial ROS (MitoSox); attenuated pyroptosis/ferroptosis/necroptosis marker upregulation, most strongly for pyroptosis markers.
- In vitro: Mito-TEMPO alone alleviated pyroptosis (establishing mitochondrial ROS as causal); DPSC co-culture required direct contact (not transwell) for full protective effect; mitochondrial transfer confirmed via TNTs (F-actin), increased over time, higher into pyroptotic vs non-pyroptotic cells, sharply increasing after 8 h coincident with caspase-1 suppression at 16 h; ATP alone did not trigger transfer.
- ρ⁰-DPSCs: transferred mitochondria normally but failed to rescue pyroptosis/mitochondrial dysfunction — functional mitochondria are required, not just transfer.
- Cytochalasin B (TNT blocker): abolished transfer and protective effect.
- TNFα/NF-κB axis: LA-RSC96 cells secrete TNFα (RNA-seq/KEGG/ELISA/WB/qPCR); TNFα-neutralizing antibody or Bay 11-7082 (NF-κB inhibitor) blocked NF-κB activation in DPSCs and reduced TNT formation/mitochondrial transfer/protective effect; exogenous TNFα enhanced transfer and NF-κB activation.
- TNFα-preconditioned DPSCs: increased mitochondrial biogenesis markers (PGC1α, NRF1, TFAM), function markers (COX4, TOM20, mt-Co2), antioxidant capacity (CAT, SOD1), mitophagy markers (Parkin, PINK1); improved in-vivo functional recovery, thicker myelin, reduced pyroptosis vs untreated DPSCs.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Entirely preclinical: no human trial or clinical translation data. DPSC facial-nerve cell therapy is not an available clinical option.
- Cross-species model (human DPSCs used in rat and macaque hosts) — authors note mechanisms are conserved but call for species-matched models for deeper translational insight.
- In-vivo mitochondrial transfer was inferred from in-vitro tracing (Mito-mCherry/CFSE) and pharmacologic block (Cytochalasin B, ρ⁰-DPSC); the paper explicitly calls for direct in-vivo confirmation using fluorescent labeling, electron microscopy, isotope tracing, and genetic markers.
- Long-term safety (tumorigenicity) only assessed to 12 months in macaques; broader safety/dose-response data would be needed before clinical development.
- Specific cytoskeletal regulators of TNT formation (e.g., TNFα-induced TNFAIP2 pathway reported in endothelial cells) were not directly investigated here and are flagged as needing further study.

## 7. Glossary

- **DPSC (Dental Pulp Stem Cells, 치수줄기세포)**: multipotent mesenchymal stem cells of neural-crest origin isolated from dental pulp (commonly extracted third molars); low immunogenicity, pro-angiogenic, anti-inflammatory/antioxidant properties.
- **Schwann cell (슈반세포)**: glial cell of the peripheral nervous system that forms the myelin sheath and supports axonal regeneration after nerve injury.
- **Pyroptosis (파이롭토시스, 세포파이롭토시스)**: inflammatory programmed cell death mediated by Caspase-1/NLRP3 inflammasome activation and GSDMD pore formation, releasing pro-inflammatory cytokines (e.g., IL1β).
- **FNI (Facial Nerve Injury, 안면신경손상)**: injury to the facial nerve (cranial nerve VII), modeled here by forceps crush in rats.
- **TNT (Tunneling Nanotube, 터널링 나노튜브)**: F-actin-based membranous intercellular channel enabling direct transfer of organelles (including mitochondria) between adjacent cells.
- **ROS (Reactive Oxygen Species, 활성산소종)**: reactive oxygen-derived molecules; mitochondrial ROS accumulation drives NLRP3 inflammasome activation and pyroptosis.
- **CMAP (Compound Muscle Action Potential)**: electrophysiological measure of motor nerve conduction/muscle response used to assess functional nerve recovery.
- **ρ⁰-DPSC**: mitochondrial-DNA-depleted DPSCs generated via chronic ethidium bromide exposure; used to test dependence of the therapeutic effect on functional (not merely transferred) mitochondria.
- **G-ratio**: ratio of axon diameter to total fiber diameter (including myelin), an index of myelination adequacy — lower G-ratio indicates thicker relative myelin.
