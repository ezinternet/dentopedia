## One-line Summary
Multicenter RCT (J Clin Periodontol 2026): 7 German centers, n=194; Paro-ComPas digital companion app as adjunct to periodontal therapy showed no statistically significant improvement in gingival bleeding index or any secondary outcome vs standard care alone.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First multicenter RCT (7 centers) evaluating a digital companion app over a full periodontal treatment cycle
- n=194 adults with periodontitis; largest digital health perio intervention trial
- No significant between-group difference in primary (GBI) or any secondary outcome (BOP, PCR, Feres composite)
- Substantial center-level heterogeneity — digital tools may need context-specific implementation
- Proof-of-concept study — digital companion concept not disproven, but benefit was not detectable at group level

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Multicenter parallel-group RCT; Paro-ComPas project (EFP/G-BA funded)
- Setting: 7 German university dental centres; August 2023–January 2025
- n: 194 adults with periodontitis (EFP/AAP 2018 classification); ≥10 natural teeth; compatible smartphone
- Intervention: Paro-ComPas app (oral hygiene diary, clinical dashboard, educational content, reminder push notifications)
- Control: Standard periodontal therapy (EFP S3 CPG-aligned, 4-step protocol)
- Primary outcome: Gingival Bleeding Index (GBI) at baseline, re-evaluation, and during SPC
- Secondary: plaque control record (PCR), BOP, Feres composite endpoint
- Statistical: mixed-effects regression; median regression for GBI and BOP; stratified by age (<40 vs ≥40), center
- Target sample size: 200 (80% power for 20 pp GBI difference)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Primary outcome (GBI): No statistically significant time × group interaction
- Secondary outcomes (BOP, PCR, Feres composite): No significant between-group differences
- Substantial center heterogeneity: GBI at Centre 7 increased (9.4→12.2%); most centres decreased
- Feres composite endpoint at T2 ranged widely: 37.0% (Centre 10) to 88.9% (Centre 4)
- Exploratory subgroup: patients with high baseline GBI (>10%) showed non-overlapping CIs (descriptive only)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Center heterogeneity undermines pooled conclusions — center-specific implementation strategies needed
- Proof-of-concept trial under Article 82 MDR — not designed for regulatory approval
- App engagement and usage data collected but efficacy not established
- Longer follow-up and behavioral engagement metrics needed

## 6. Related Work
- mHealth interventions meta-analyses show short-term plaque/bleeding benefits — this RCT provides a more rigorous full-cycle test

## 7. Glossary
- GBI: Gingival Bleeding Index
- BOP: Bleeding on probing
- PCR: Plaque control record
- SPC: Supportive periodontal care (maintenance)
- Feres composite: composite periodontal success endpoint (PPD, BOP, CAL criteria)
- EFP S3 CPG: European Federation of Periodontology S3 Clinical Practice Guideline
