## Why Ingested

골이식재(bone-graft-material) 서베일런스에서 확보한 2026년 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 구성요소 네트워크 메타분석 (Component Network Meta-Analysis, CNMA). 기존 [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/canullo-2021-arp-biomaterials-dimensional-nma]]이 "완성된 치료 패키지"의 순위를 매긴 것과 달리, 이식재·PRF·차단막을 구성요소로 분해해 각각의 독립 기여를 추정한다. 특히 [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/alavi-2024-regenerative-grafts-socket-preservation-sr-ma]]의 "L-PRF는 자연 치유와 차이 없음"과 PRF 보조 효과(-0.40 mm)를 대비해 읽을 수 있다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with component network meta-analysis (CNMA) of 31 RCTs (932 participants, 853 extraction sites; PubMed, Embase, CENTRAL, Web of Science to July 2026; 16 RCTs in the horizontal-width network, 14 in the buccal vertical-height network) decomposing alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) into scaffold, platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) and barrier-membrane components.

In additive CNMA for horizontal ridge-width change, barrier membrane had the largest component effect (MD -1.31 mm, 95% CI -2.18 to -0.44), then allograft (-0.77 mm, -1.16 to -0.38) and PRF adjunct (-0.40 mm, -0.66 to -0.14); xenograft (-0.57 mm) and collagen scaffold (-0.42 mm) were non-significant and alloplast was null (0.01 mm).

Estimates were low or very low certainty, with global inconsistency (Q = 19.90, P = 0.006) and sparse nodes, so the results are an evidence-informed component framework, not a definitive treatment hierarchy; no scaffold-by-PRF interaction was demonstrable (imprecise).

## 세줄요약

31개 RCT (참가자 932명, 발치와 853개; 2026-07까지 PubMed·Embase·CENTRAL·Web of Science)를 대상으로 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP)을 이식재·혈소판 풍부 피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)·차단막 (Barrier Membrane) 구성요소로 분해한 구성요소 네트워크 메타분석 (Component Network Meta-Analysis, CNMA).

수평 치조제 폭 변화의 가산형 CNMA에서 차단막이 가장 컸고(MD -1.31 mm, 95% CI -2.18 ~ -0.44), 동종골 (Allograft) -0.77 mm, PRF 보조 -0.40 mm 순이었다; 이종골 (Xenograft) -0.57 mm와 콜라겐 지지체 -0.42 mm는 비유의, 합성골 (Alloplast)은 0.01 mm로 효과 없음.

구성요소 추정치의 확실성은 낮음~매우 낮음이고 전역 비일관성 (Q = 19.90, P = 0.006)과 희소한 노드가 있어 확정적 치료 위계가 아니라 근거 기반 해석 틀로만 읽어야 하며, 이식재×PRF 상호작용은 부정확하여 입증되지 않았다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First component-level (CNMA) decomposition of ARP into scaffold type, PRF adjunct and barrier membrane, estimating each independent contribution instead of ranking complete protocols.
- Barrier membrane and allograft carried the favorable component-level estimates for horizontal ridge-width preservation; the PRF adjunct effect was statistically detectable but small (~0.4 mm).
- Interaction CNMA found no convincing evidence that the PRF effect depends on scaffold type (xenograft x PRF 0.28 mm; alloplast x PRF 0.60 mm, both imprecise).
- The additive model was the most parsimonious (approximate AIC 39.24 vs 42.44 for standard NMA and 42.32 for interaction CNMA), although its residual deviance was higher than that of the standard NMA.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review with random-effects standard NMA (complete strategies as nodes) followed by additive and interaction CNMA (R packages netmeta and netcomb); PRISMA and PRISMA-NMA; PROSPERO-registered.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science, inception to July 2026 (3,161 records, 1,294 duplicates removed, 130 full texts).
- **n**: 31 RCTs (2008-2026, 17 countries or regions; 932 participants, 853 sites; 7 split-mouth, 5 with 3 or more arms). 16 RCTs in the horizontal-width network (12 nodes), 14 in the buccal vertical-height network (10 nodes).
- **Components (8 coded)**: natural healing/blood clot (reference), allograft, xenograft, alloplast, collagen-based scaffold, tooth/dentin-derived scaffold, PRF adjunct (all PRF preparations pooled), barrier membrane (collagen, synthetic, nonresorbable or PRF-derived).
- **Outcomes**: primary horizontal ridge-width change and buccal vertical ridge-height change (mm; negative MD = less loss); secondary histomorphometry, patient-reported outcomes and adverse events (descriptive only).
- **Appraisal**: RoB 2 (6 low risk, 20 some concerns, 5 high); CINeMA for standard NMA, structured narrative certainty for CNMA.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Standard NMA, horizontal width vs natural healing: dentin graft -2.07 mm (95% CI -3.17 to -0.97; one study, 14 sites), xenograft + membrane -1.78 mm (-2.57 to -0.99), allograft + membrane -1.78 mm (-3.06 to -0.50; one study, 5 sites), dentin + PRF -1.52 mm, alloplast + membrane -1.45 mm, allograft + PRF -1.28 mm (-1.93 to -0.63), allograft alone -0.77 mm (-1.28 to -0.27); PRF alone -0.36 mm (-0.75 to 0.03), collagen -0.46 mm and alloplast + PRF -0.29 mm were non-significant.
- Standard NMA, buccal vertical height: allograft + membrane -3.03 mm (-4.86 to -1.20), dentin + PRF -2.49 mm (-3.85 to -1.12), xenograft + membrane -2.23 mm (-3.53 to -0.93).
- Additive CNMA, horizontal width (certainty): membrane -1.31 mm (-2.18 to -0.44; P = 0.003; low), allograft -0.77 mm (-1.16 to -0.38; P < 0.001; low), PRF -0.40 mm (-0.66 to -0.14; P = 0.003; low), xenograft -0.57 mm (-1.20 to 0.06; very low), collagen -0.42 mm (-1.07 to 0.23; very low), alloplast 0.01 mm (-0.51 to 0.53; very low).
- Model comparison: tau-squared 0.072 (additive) vs 0.101 (standard NMA) vs 0.091 (interaction).
- Global inconsistency for horizontal width: between-design Q = 19.90, P = 0.006; node-splitting found no significant local disagreement.
- Sensitivity analyses (excluding high risk of bias, 4-6 month window, alternative PRF coding): direction of membrane/allograft/PRF estimates preserved.
- Histomorphometry (16 studies): scaffold strategies generally showed more new bone or hard-tissue maturation than spontaneous healing, but no quantitative synthesis was possible.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Sparse and uneven component co-occurrence (membrane occurred only in combination, 0 standalone vs 18 combined), limiting separation of component effects from the packages in which they were tested.
- Heterogeneous component classes (membranes, scaffolds, PRF preparations): estimates are class averages, not product- or protocol-specific.
- Global inconsistency, possible transitivity violations (socket morphology, buccal-wall integrity, follow-up), unresolved split-mouth unit-of-analysis issues, and no formal publication-bias testing.
- Several top-ranked nodes rest on a single small trial (dentin graft; allograft + membrane).
- The additive model assumes component effects sum; absence of a significant interaction means insufficient evidence, not evidence of no interaction.
- Future work: factorial or adequately powered multi-arm RCTs, standardized outcome reporting, baseline socket characteristics, separate description of scaffold, membrane and biologic components.

## 6. Related Work
- Canullo 2021: ARP biomaterials NMA of complete strategies; the present CNMA decomposes the same question to component level.
- Alavi 2024: SR+MA of biologics for socket preservation, reporting no L-PRF benefit over natural healing.
- Del Fabbro 2022: NMA of sealing materials, relevant to the barrier-membrane component.

## 7. Glossary
- **CNMA (Component Network Meta-Analysis)**: NMA that splits multicomponent interventions into components and estimates each one's contribution, here with additive and interaction models.
- **ARP (Alveolar Ridge Preservation)**: socket-grafting or sealing after extraction to limit dimensional loss.
- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)**: autologous platelet concentrate (L-PRF, A-PRF, i-PRF, T-PRF, e-PRF) used as adjunct or membrane.
- **MD (Mean Difference)**: here negative values mean less ridge dimensional loss than the comparator.
- **CINeMA**: framework for rating confidence in network meta-analysis results.
