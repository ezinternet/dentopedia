## Why Ingested
골유착(Osseointegration) 생물학의 기원과 Brånemark의 역할을 서술한 역사 리뷰로, [[implants/surface/abraham-2014-historical-perspective-dental-implant-surfaces-treatments]]가 4,500년 임플란트 역사와 표면처리 계보를 다루는 것과 달리, Rawat 2024는 Brånemark 개인의 연구 궤적(1950년대 토끼 경골 실험 → 1965년 첫 인간 식립 → 1982년 토론토 학회)과 BAHA·의지(肢) 등 치과 외 영향까지 집중 조명한다.

## Three-line Summary
Narrative review, single-institution (Subharti Dental College), 2024, based on PubMed/Google Scholar literature search: recounts Per-Ingvar Brånemark's (1929–2014) scientific journey and legacy in implantology.
First human mandibular osseointegrated implants placed 1965 (Gösta Larsson, lasted 40 years); 1982 Toronto Conference introduced the Brånemark System globally; over 100,000 patients have since benefited from BAHA cochlear devices built on the same principle.
The paper documents the historical lineage from titanium-chamber animal experiments to Nobel Biocare commercialization, framing osseointegration as the unifying paradigm across dental, orthopedic, and otologic prosthetics.

## Key Contributions
- Documents Brånemark's name change (Persson → Brånemark, meaning "who breaks ground") and his trajectory from anatomy professor to implantology pioneer
- Identifies the foundational rabbit-fibula microcirculation experiment (NIH-funded) as the precursor to osseointegration discovery
- Records first human implant surgery date (1965, Gösta Larsson) and its longevity (40 years)
- Traces the commercialization chain: Bofors AB → Nobel Biocare; Brånemark Osseointegration Centre (BOC) founded 1989, Gothenburg
- Covers non-dental spin-offs: BAHA cochlear system (1999, 100,000+ patients), orthopedic joint replacements with osseointegration, transcutaneous prosthetic limbs (Rickard and PI Brånemark collaboration)

## Methodology
- Study type: Narrative/historical literature review
- Databases searched: PubMed, Google Scholar
- No statistical analysis; qualitative synthesis of primary and secondary sources
- Review timeline: submitted 11/07/2024, accepted 11/10/2024

## Key Results
- 1953–1960s: in vivo titanium-chamber experiments in rabbit tibia/fibula; bone-marrow repair studies
- 1965: First human mandibular implants (4 implants, complete arch fixed prosthesis, Gösta Larsson)
- 1970: Swedish National Board of Health and Welfare authorized Brånemark dental implants
- 1981: Brånemark founded biomedical firm in Sweden (later Nobel Biocare)
- 1982: Toronto Conference — Brånemark System introduced to North America and globally
- Mid-1970s: BAHA prototype (Anders Tjellström team); product commercialized 2005 (Cochlear Bone Anchored Solutions), 100,000+ patients
- Awards: Söderberg Prize (Mini Nobel, 1992), Medal for Technical Innovation (Swedish Engineering Academy, 1992), Harvard School of Dental Medicine Medal, 30+ honorary posts in Europe/North America

## Glossary
- **Osseointegration**: Direct structural and functional connection between living bone and a load-bearing implant surface (Brånemark's term, ~1952)
- **BAHA (Bone Anchoring Hearing Aid)**: Cochlear implant system using osseointegration to conduct sound through bone to the inner ear; derived from Brånemark's principles
- **TOFA (Transcutaneous Osseointegration for Amputees)**: Orthopedic application of osseointegration for bone-anchored limb prostheses; distinct from dental use
- **Toronto Conference (1982)**: Landmark meeting where Brånemark System was first formally presented to North American dental community
- **Brånemark System**: Machined titanium screw-root implant system commercialized by Nobel Biocare; basis of modern implantology
