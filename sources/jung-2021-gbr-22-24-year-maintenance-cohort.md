## Why Ingested

Longest prospective GBR maintenance cohort (22–24 years) comparing resorbable collagen (Bio-Gide) + DBBM vs nonresorbable e-PTFE + DBBM vs pristine bone controls. Directly addresses the evidence gap for GBR-specific long-term surveillance protocols. Complements [[wiki/implants/survival/gurbanov-2024-implants-grafted-nongrafted-sites-sr]] (≥10yr SR) and [[wiki/overviews/supportive-peri-implant-therapy-maintenance-overview]] (SPiT framework) with GBR-specific 23.5-year data.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (original RCT 1994–1996, n=72 patients, 265 implants) with 22–24 year follow-up (median 23.5 years, 39 patients, 147 implants). Implant survival: resorbable collagen+DBBM (BG) 89.3%, nonresorbable e-PTFE+DBBM (GT) 90.2%, pristine bone controls (CT) 93.8% — no significant difference (frailty model p=0.79). Marginal bone levels stable and comparable across groups (BoneL: BG −2.3±1.4mm, GT −3.0±1.5mm, CT −2.3±1.5mm; BoneLbucc: BG −3.0±1.9mm, GT −3.5±2.2mm, CT −2.6±1.8mm). Smoking significantly reduced survival (p=0.012). Yearly recalls first 5 years, then private maintenance — documents real-world long-term GBR surveillance pattern.

## 세줄요약

1994–1996년 시작된 전향적 RCT(72명, 265개 임플란트)의 22–24년 추적 관찰(중앙값 23.5년, 39명 147개 임플란트). 흡수성 콜라겐막+DBBM(BG) 89.3%, 비흡수성 e-PTFE+DBBM(GT) 90.2%, 대조군(CT) 93.8%로 군간 생존율 유의차 없음(frailty model p=0.79). 변연골레벨(BoneL, BoneLbucc) 모든 군에서 안정적·비교 가능. 흡연만 생존율 유의 저하(p=0.012). 초기 5년 연 1회 recall 후 개별 유지관리로 전환된 실제 GBR 장기 서베일런스 패턴 기록.

## Key Contributions

- Longest prospective GBR maintenance cohort (22–24 years) with controlled comparison (resorbable vs nonresorbable membrane + DBBM vs pristine bone)
- Documents real-world surveillance protocol: yearly recalls ×5 years → private practice maintenance
- Implant survival comparable between GBR and non-GBR sites at 23.5 years (89.3–93.8%)
- 3D CBCT analysis of buccal/oral bone dimensions at 23.5 years — rare long-term 3D data
- Smoking confirmed as significant negative survival factor (p=0.012)
- Technical complications documented: 84% crown survival, 20.8% ceramic chipping, 1.9% abutment screw loosening at 23.5 years
- Peri-implant mucosal condition worse in GT (e-PTFE) group (PC score 2.2 vs 1.0 BG, 0.9 CT)
- Suppuration history higher in GT (34.8% vs 11.8% BG, 14.3% CT)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective randomized controlled clinical trial (original 1994–1996) with long-term follow-up (2018–2019)
- **Original population**: 72 patients (54 women), mean age at baseline 75.4±15.7 years, 265 implants (256 Brånemark, 8 Biomed 3i, 1 IMZ)
- **Groups**: BG = resorbable collagen membrane (Bio-Gide) + DBBM (Bio-Oss) (n=112 implants); GT = nonresorbable e-PTFE (Gore-Tex) + DBBM (Bio-Oss) (n=41); CT = pristine bone, no GBR (n=112)
- **Follow-up protocol**: Yearly recalls at clinic for first 5 years post-loading → referred to private offices for regular maintenance
- **Follow-up examination (2018–2019)**: Health questionnaire, clinical parameters (PI, PPD, BOP, KM, MSTL, PC), technical outcomes, 2D periapical radiographs (long-cone paralleling), 3D CBCT (bucco-oral sections)
- **Radiographic analysis**: BoneL (mesial/distal, implant shoulder reference), BoneLbucc/BoneLoral (CBCT, implant shoulder reference), buccal bone thickness at IS-1/3/5/7mm
- **Statistics**: Mixed models (patient random factor), Kaplan-Meier + frailty proportional hazard Cox model for survival, Spearman correlations
- **Retention**: 39/72 patients (54.2%), 147/265 implants (55.5%); 23 died (31.9%), 10 lost to follow-up (13.9%)

## Key Results

### Implant Survival (23.5 years median)
| Group | Implants (n) | Survival Rate | Frailty Model |
|---|---|---|---|
| BG (collagen+DBBM) | 68 | 89.3% | p=0.79 (NS) |
| GT (e-PTFE+DBBM) | 23 | 90.2% | — |
| CT (pristine bone) | 56 | 93.8% | — |

- **Smoking**: Significant negative effect (p=0.012), no interaction with treatment
- Age, follow-up length, location, jaw: NS

### Clinical Parameters (23.5 years)
| Parameter | BG (n=58) | GT (n=21) | CT (n=50) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PI (%) | 18.0±30.0 | 6.0±12.0 | 21.0±34.0 |
| PPD (mm) | 2.9±1.1 | 3.3±1.1 | 3.1±1.1 |
| BOP (%) | 34.0±35.0 | 38.0±35.0 | 41.0±37.0 |
| PC (0–4) | 1.0±1.3 | 2.2±1.6 | 0.9±1.2 |
| KM (mm) | 3.6±2.9 | 3.7±3.3 | 2.9±3.1 |
| MSTL (mm) | −0.4±2.0 | −0.4±2.3 | −0.3±1.7 |
| Sites with ≥1mm recession | 16.3% | 5.4% | 14.1% |
| Pain reported | 1.5% | 0% | 15% |
| Suppuration history | 11.8% | 34.8% | 14.3% |

### Radiographic Outcomes
| Measure | BG | GT | CT | Intergroup p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BoneL (mm) | −2.3±1.4 | −3.0±1.5 | −2.3±1.5 | 0.768 (NS) |
| BoneL loss from baseline | 2.1±1.4 | 2.5±1.5 | 2.0±1.4 | — |
| BoneLbucc (mm) | −3.0±1.9 | −3.5±2.2 | −2.6±1.8 | 0.755 (NS) |
| BoneLoral (mm) | −2.3±1.6 | −2.9±1.5 | −2.2±2.1 | 0.319 (NS) |

- Intragroup: all significant bone loss over time (p<0.0001)
- Buccal bone thickness: BG 1.2±0.9mm (IS-1mm), 1.7±1.0mm (IS-3mm), 2.2±1.1mm (IS-5mm), 2.7±1.2mm (IS-7mm)

### Correlations (Spearman)
- KM, MSTL vs BoneLbucc: all very weak (<0.2 for BG/GT, <0.25 for CT)
- Initial KM vs BoneLbucc: 0.1 (BG), −0.4 (GT), 0.3 (CT)
- Initial MSTL vs BoneLbucc: 0.3 (BG), −0.3 (GT), 0.0 (CT)

### Technical Outcomes
- Abutment survival: 76.1%
- Crown survival: 84.0%
- Framework fracture: 2.7%
- Ceramic chipping: 20.8%
- Abutment screw loosening: 1.9%
- Color mismatch: 30.3%
- USPHS: Anatomical form Alpha 76.4%, Surface texture Bravo 57.3%, Marginal integrity Alpha 93.8%

### Patient Satisfaction
- Mean VAS: 97.5±5.4% (24/33 patients = 100% satisfaction)

## Limitations

- Heterogeneous group sizes (BG 68, GT 23, CT 56 implants at follow-up)
- No a priori power analysis
- Implant-based analysis (patients had up to 8 study implants) — clustering accounted by mixed models/frailty
- Intraoral radiographs not standardized
- CBCT buccal bone <0.5mm may be undetectable (artifacts)
- Missing baseline defect size/shape data
- 45.8% patient attrition (deaths + lost to follow-up) over 23.5 years

## Glossary

- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 골유도재생술)**: 차폐막과 골 이식재로 골 결손 재생
- **BG (Bio-Gide group)**: 흡수성 콜라겐막(Bio-Gide) + DBBM(Bio-Oss) GBR 그룹
- **GT (Gore-Tex group)**: 비흡수성 e-PTFE막(Gore-Tex) + DBBM(Bio-Oss) GBR 그룹
- **CT (Control group)**: GBR 없이 깨끗한 골에 식립된 대조군
- **BoneL (Vertical Marginal Bone Level, 수직 변연골 레벨)**: 임플란트 숄더 기준 근원심 골 레벨
- **BoneLbucc (Buccal Bone Level, 협측 골 레벨)**: CBCT 상 협측 골-임플란트 첫 접촉점 레벨
- **MSTL (Midfacial Soft Tissue Level, 협측 연조직 레벨)**: 임플란트/어버트먼트 접합부 기준 협측 연조직 위치 (음수=노출)
- **PC (Peri-implant Mucosal Condition, 임플란트주위 점막 상태)**: 0=건강 ~ 4=과형성
- **Frailty model**: 이질적 위험을 가진 집단의 생존 분석을 위한 콕스 모델 확장
