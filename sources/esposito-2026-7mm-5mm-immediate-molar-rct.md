## Why Ingested
대구치 발치 후 즉시 식립 시 광폭 직경(7mm)과 통상 직경(5mm)의 1년 결과를 비교한 다기관 RCT. 기존 [[immediate-implant/checchi-2017-wide-diameter-immediate-post-extractive]]의 초기 데이터를 보완하며, 광폭 임플란트의 합병증 위험(7mm그룹 4건 vs 5mm그룹 0건, p=0.047)을 직접 수치화한다.

## One-line Summary
Multicenter RCT (n=39, 5 centres, 1-year) found no significant difference in implant survival, MBL, or esthetics between 7mm and 5mm diameter immediate post-extraction molar implants, but a significantly higher minor complication rate in the 7mm group (21.1% vs 0%, p=0.047).

## 한줄요약
다기관 RCT (39명, 5개 센터, 1년): 대구치 즉시 식립에서 7mm vs 5mm 직경 임플란트는 생존율·MBL·PES 차이 없으나, 7mm에서 경미 합병증 유의하게 더 많음 (21.1% vs 0%, p=0.047).

- Journal: International Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Implants, 2026 Jun 26 (ahead of print)
- PMID: 42360900; DOI: 10.11607/jomi.11536
- Centres: 5 centres (Korea + Europe); affiliations include Young-Jin Cho, Byoung-Kyou Park, Jung-Hyun Kim (Korea), Esposito/Bocari/Greco/Visconti (Europe), Dong-Wook Chang
- Implants: BioHorizons Camlog 7.0mm (wide) vs 5.0mm (conventional); gaps grafted with bone substitute; definitive zirconia crowns at 4 months
- Funding/COI: Not reported in abstract

## 2. Key Contributions
- First adequately powered multicenter RCT directly comparing 7mm vs 5mm immediate molar implants with 1-year outcomes
- No statistically significant difference in survival (1 failure in 5mm group vs 0 in 7mm), MBL, or PES at 1 year
- Significant disadvantage for 7mm: 4 complications in 4 patients (21.1%) vs 0 in 5mm group (p=0.047)
- Marginal bone loss was numerically greater in 7mm (0.83±1.56mm vs 0.45±1.19mm) but not significant (p=0.396)
- Authors recommend larger RCTs before adopting wide-diameter immediate molar implants routinely

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Multicenter parallel-arm RCT; 5 centres; randomisation 1:1
- **Population**: 39 patients needing single immediate post-extraction molar implant; 19 in 7mm group, 20 in 5mm group; no dropouts
- **Intervention**: Immediate post-extraction implant placement (7mm or 5mm diameter, BioHorizons Camlog); bone substitute graft for gaps; 4-month healing before definitive zirconia crown
- **Primary outcome**: Implant failure at 1 year after loading
- **Secondary outcomes**: Complications, patient satisfaction, MBL change, pink esthetic score (PES)
- **Follow-up duration**: 1 year after loading (≈16 months post-extraction)
- **Statistics**: Comparison of proportions (95% CI, Fisher's exact), comparison of means; p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Implant failure**: 0/19 (7mm) vs 1/20 (5mm); difference 5.0% (95% CI −4.6% to 14.6%); P=1.000
- **Complications**: 4/19 (21.1%) in 7mm vs 0/20 (0%) in 5mm; difference 21.1% (95% CI 2.7% to 39.4%); P=0.047 ✱ significant
- **MBL at 1 year**: 7mm: 0.83±1.56mm; 5mm: 0.45±1.19mm; mean diff 0.39mm (95% CI −0.53 to 1.30); P=0.396
- **PES at loading**: 7mm 5.84±2.34; 5mm 6.06±4.04; P=0.847
- **PES at 1 year**: 7mm 9.16±2.22; 5mm 9.21±2.88; P=0.950
- Nature of complications not detailed in abstract

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=39) — underpowered for survival outcome; failure difference 5% not significant but clinically meaningful
- Only 1-year follow-up — long-term MBL and peri-implant health unknown
- Abstract-only data available; full paper not retrieved — complication details, implant design specifics, and patient selection criteria not fully accessible
- PubMed PDF only; no access to full-text result tables or radiographic data
- Authors explicitly state larger RCTs are needed

## 7. Glossary
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level)**: Radiographic peri-implant crestal bone height change from loading; positive value = bone loss
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score)**: 7-item assessment of peri-implant soft tissue esthetics (max 14); higher = better esthetics
- **Wide-diameter implant**: Conventionally defined as ≥5mm; in this study 7mm is considered "wide" vs 5mm "conventional"
- **Immediate post-extraction implant**: Placed within the same surgical appointment as tooth extraction (Type 1 per ITI classification)
