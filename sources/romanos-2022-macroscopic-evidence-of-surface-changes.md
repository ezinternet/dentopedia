## Why Ingested

Bench evidence on implant thread-surface damage (titanium abrasion, zirconia thread-tip microfractures) after a single insertion/removal cycle in dense bone, which complements the bone-side microdamage question raised in [[implants/osseodensification/wang-2017-effects-of-condensation-on-peri]] and the insertion-load discussion in [[implants/isq/nascimento-2024-measurement-bone-deformation-insertion-torque]].

## Three-line Summary

In vitro pilot study: four implant systems (Astra Tech, Nobel Biocare, Straumann titanium; Zeramex zirconia), 6 implants per group (10-11 mm long, 4.0-4.5 mm diameter), inserted into and removed from dense bovine bone disks representing type II bone density.

All groups showed surface changes: titanium implants showed abrasion at the thread flanks, and zirconia implants showed microfractures at the thread tips (Cochran Q test used; numeric results not in the abstract).

After insertion and removal in dense bone, thread flanks and tips of both titanium and zirconia implants develop visible surface damage; this is a small bovine-bone pilot, and clinical consequences are not tested in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

체외 (In vitro) 예비연구: 4개 임플란트 시스템 (티타늄 3종, 지르코니아 Zeramex 1종), 군당 6개 (길이 10-11 mm, 직경 4.0-4.5 mm)를 제II형 골밀도에 해당하는 치밀한 소 (bovine) 골 디스크에 식립 후 제거.

모든 군에서 표면 변화가 관찰되었다: 티타늄 임플란트는 나사산 측면 (Thread flank)의 마모 (Abrasion), 지르코니아 임플란트는 나사산 끝 (Thread tip)의 미세골절 (Microfracture) (Cochran Q 검정 사용, 수치는 초록에 없음).

치밀골에서 식립·제거 시 티타늄과 지르코니아 모두 나사산 측면·끝에 육안으로 보이는 표면 손상이 생긴다; 소 골 기반 소규모 예비연구이며 임상적 결과는 초록만으로 검증되지 않는다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Tests whether current implant designs show visible surface damage after insertion and removal in dense bone, and whether titanium and zirconia are comparable.
- Describes material-specific damage patterns: abrasion at thread flanks (titanium) vs microfractures at thread tips (zirconia).
- Frames titanium and zirconium wear as contributors to mechanical complications.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Experimental in vitro pilot study
- **Groups**: Astra Tech (Dentsply Sirona), Nobel Biocare, Straumann (Institut Straumann), Zeramex (Dentalpoint); 6 implants each
- **Substrate**: dense bovine bone disks, type II density
- **Outcomes**: presence/absence and type of damage at coronal, middle and apical regions; 5x clinical-magnification evaluation plus higher magnification
- **Statistics**: Cochran Q test for binary dichotomous data

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- All groups showed surface changes.
- Titanium: abrasion at thread flanks. Zirconia: microfractures at thread tips.
- No per-region frequencies or p-values in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only ingest; numeric results not captured.
- Pilot design, bovine bone, single insertion/removal cycle; clinical significance of the damage is not addressed in the abstract.

## 7. Glossary
- **Abrasion**: material loss from a surface by frictional contact.
- **Microfracture**: small crack or chip, here at zirconia thread tips.
- **Cochran Q test**: nonparametric test for binary outcomes across related groups.
- **Type II bone**: dense bone density class used for the bovine disks.
