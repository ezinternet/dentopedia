## Why Ingested

[[wiki/drug/antibiotics/teoh-2021-oral-antibiotic-empirical-acute-dentoalveolar-infections]]는 배농만 확보되면 좁은 스펙트럼(페니실린 계열)이 급성 치성감염에 동등하게 효과적이라 결론짓지만, 실제 배양·감수성 데이터는 없었다. 본 논문은 10년간 302균주의 실제 배양 결과를 제시해 그 빈틈을 채우며, viridans streptococcus의 penicillin G 감수성이 최근 5년간 68.4%→52.0%로 하락하는 반면 3세대 cephalosporin은 ~94% 유지된다는 한국 3차병원 실제 데이터를 더해 경험적 항생제 선택에 미묘한 수정을 가한다(입원이 필요한 중증 근막공간농양 맥락).

## Three-line Summary

10-year retrospective chart review (n=192 hospitalized patients, 302 bacterial strains, single Korean tertiary center, 2013–2022) of pus cultures and antibiotic sensitivity testing after incision and drainage for odontogenic fascial-space abscess.

Viridans streptococcus was the most frequent isolate (51.7%, rising from 41.8% in 2013–2017 to 60.9% in 2018–2022); its sensitivity to penicillin G fell from 68.4% to 52.0% over the decade while sensitivity to 3rd-generation cephalosporins (cefotaxime/ceftriaxone) stayed ~94–95%; Klebsiella pneumoniae showed rising resistance and was more frequent in deep-neck-space abscesses.

Penicillin-only empirical regimens are increasingly inadequate for hospitalized odontogenic fascial-space abscess; culture/sensitivity testing is particularly important when infection extends to deep neck spaces, where Gram-negative aerobic resistance is higher.

## 세줄요약

10년 후향적 차트 리뷰(입원 환자 n=192명, 균주 302주, 한국 단일 3차병원, 2013–2022): 치성 근막공간농양(odontogenic fascial-space abscess) 절개배농 후 농양 배양·항생제 감수성 검사.

Viridans streptococcus(비리단스 연쇄상구균)가 가장 흔한 균주(51.7%, 2013–2017년 41.8%→2018–2022년 60.9%로 증가); penicillin G 감수성은 10년간 68.4%→52.0%로 하락한 반면 3세대 세팔로스포린(cefotaxime·ceftriaxone)은 ~94–95% 유지; Klebsiella pneumoniae(폐렴막대균)는 저항성이 증가했고 심경부공간(deep neck space) 감염에서 더 흔함.

입원이 필요한 치성 근막공간농양에서 페니실린 단독 경험적 요법은 점점 부적절해지고 있으며, 특히 심경부공간까지 확산된 경우 그람음성 호기성균 저항성이 높아 배양·감수성 검사가 중요함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides 10-year real-world culture and antibiotic-sensitivity trend data (2013–2022, n=192 patients, 302 strains) for hospitalized odontogenic fascial-space abscess in a Korean population — an evidence gap that pure SR-of-outcome-studies (e.g. Teoh 2021) cannot fill.
- Quantifies a temporal shift: Gram-positive aerobic bacteria (chiefly viridans streptococcus) rose from 56.8%→72.4% of isolates while Gram-negative anaerobes fell from 23.3%→12.8%, plausibly reflecting increased pre-hospital broad-spectrum antibiotic use at primary clinics before tertiary referral.
- Documents declining penicillin-class sensitivity in viridans streptococcus alongside maintained high sensitivity to 3rd-generation cephalosporins, directly informing empirical antibiotic choice.
- Separately characterizes Klebsiella pneumoniae behavior in deep-neck-space abscesses (higher frequency, rising multi-drug resistance, but relatively preserved piperacillin/tazobactam sensitivity 88.9%).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: retrospective chart review of patients admitted to the Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Dankook University Hospital 2013–2022 for odontogenic fascial-space abscess.
- Inclusion: patients who underwent pus culture + antibiotic sensitivity testing following incision and drainage. Exclusion: negative culture results, contaminated samples (>3 species detected).
- Specimen collection: swabbing method (sterile culture swab), not needle aspiration — chosen for consistency across the full 10-year window (few aspiration samples available).
- Diagnosis of fascial-space abscess via clinical exam + enhanced CT, dental origin confirmed via dental history/panoramic/periapical imaging.
- Fascial spaces classified as primary (canine, buccal, submental, sublingual, submandibular, infratemporal), secondary (adjacent to masticatory muscles), and deep neck (lateral pharyngeal, retropharyngeal).
- Split into two 5-year cohorts (2013–2017, n=79; 2018–2022, n=113) for temporal comparison. Statistics: chi-square test for isolate/sensitivity ratio comparisons between cohorts; one-way ANOVA for isolate differences across fascial-space groups; SPSS v28; p<0.05 significant.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 192 patients, 302 strains (mean 1.57 strains/patient). Aerobic 73.8%, anaerobic 26.2%. Gram-positive 73.2%, Gram-negative 26.8%.
- Most frequent isolates overall: viridans streptococcus 51.7%, Prevotella spp. 16.9%, Klebsiella pneumoniae 5.6%, Streptococcus anginosus group 4.6%, Peptostreptococcus spp. 4.6%.
- Most-affected fascial spaces: submandibular 22.7%, submasseteric 14.7%, pterygomandibular 13.1%, buccal 9.1%, sublingual 8.9%. 66.1% of patients had ≥2 spaces involved.
- Temporal shift (2013–2017 → 2018–2022): Gram-positive aerobic bacteria 56.8%→72.4% (significant increase); Gram-negative anaerobic bacteria 23.3%→12.8% (significant decrease); viridans streptococcus 41.8%→60.9% (significant increase); Prevotella spp. 22.6%→11.5% (significant decrease).
- Deep-neck-space abscesses vs primary/secondary: significantly lower Gram-positive aerobic %, significantly higher Gram-negative aerobic %; lower viridans streptococcus, relatively higher Klebsiella pneumoniae.
- Viridans streptococcus antibiotic sensitivity: penicillin G 60.4%, ampicillin 80.5%, cefotaxime 93.5%, ceftriaxone 95.5%, linezolid 100.0%, vancomycin 99.4%, levofloxacin 96.1%; erythromycin resistance 31.2%, clindamycin resistance 27.6%; tetracycline sensitivity 44.2% / resistance 47.4%. Penicillin G sensitivity fell significantly from 68.4% (2013–2017) to 52.0% (2018–2022); no other significant temporal changes.
- Klebsiella pneumoniae antibiotic profile: 100.0% resistant to ampicillin; 83.3% sensitive to amoxicillin/clavulanic acid; resistance to cefazolin (1st-gen) 22.2%, cefoxitin (2nd-gen) 5.6%, cefotaxime/ceftazidime (3rd-gen) 16.7%/22.2%, cefepime (4th-gen) 5.6%; piperacillin/tazobactam sensitivity 88.9%. Sensitivity declined across most agents from 2013–2017 (100% except ampicillin) to 2018–2022 (e.g. amoxicillin/CA 75.0%, cefazolin 66.7%, cefotaxime/ceftazidime 66.7%).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Swabbing method (not aspiration) was used for all 10 years for methodological consistency, but is disadvantageous for culturing anaerobic strains — the paper itself notes a comparator aspiration-method study found ~3.3 strains/patient (vs 1.57 here), roughly double, implicating under-detection of anaerobes.
- Patients without adequate culture/sensitivity testing were excluded, shrinking and potentially biasing the sample.
- Low-frequency strains (S. anginosus group, Peptostreptococcus spp.) had too few samples to assess reliable temporal trends.
- Single-center, single-country (Korea) — antibiotic resistance patterns are known to vary geographically and with local prescribing practices; findings may not generalize outside this population/era.
- Retrospective design; no standardized empirical-antibiotic protocol tested prospectively against outcomes.
- Future: larger multi-center samples, aspiration-based collection to better capture anaerobic strains, prospective validation of a culture-informed empirical regimen.

## 6. Related Work

- Dahlén (cited): fusobacterium/streptococcus group most common in pulp-origin infection; Prevotella/Porphyromonas/Fusobacterium/Peptostreptococcus common in periodontal-origin infection.
- Hirai et al. (cited): S. anginosus group 100% sensitive to 2nd–4th-gen cephalosporins, 71% to ampicillin — consistent with this study's cephalosporin-favoring pattern.
- Heim et al. 2021 (cited, 206 patients): similar Prevotella/Fusobacterium frequency to this study.
- Lee & Kanagalingam 2011, Yun et al. 2021, Chang et al. 2013 (cited): deep-neck-space abscess microbiology comparators, including the Klebsiella-in-deep-neck-space association also seen here.

## 7. Glossary

- Viridans streptococcus (비리단스 연쇄상구균): alpha-hemolytic Streptococcus group including the Streptococcus anginosus group (S. anginosus, S. constellatus, S. intermedius), commonly implicated in odontogenic infection of pulpal origin.
- Fascial space abscess (근막공간농양): purulent infection spreading into anatomic fascial planes of the head/neck, classified as primary, secondary, or deep neck depending on spread.
- Deep neck space infection (심경부공간감염): infection extending to the lateral pharyngeal or retropharyngeal space, at risk for airway obstruction, necrotizing fasciitis, mediastinitis.
- Empirical antibiotic therapy (경험적 항생제 요법): antibiotic selection made before culture/sensitivity results are available, based on typical causative-organism patterns.
- 3rd-generation cephalosporin (3세대 세팔로스포린): e.g. cefotaxime, ceftriaxone — beta-lactam antibiotics with expanded Gram-negative coverage relative to earlier generations.
- Swabbing vs aspiration method (도말법 대 흡인법): specimen-collection techniques for pus culture; swabbing is more aerobe-favoring due to unavoidable air contact, aspiration better preserves anaerobic strains.
