## Why Ingested

Lin 2025 ([[sinus-lift/transcrestal/lin-2025-schneiderian-membrane-biomechanical-transcrestal-fea]])의 FEA가 hydraulic pressure TSFE의 역학적 우위를 수치로 제시함. 이 논문은 동일한 CAS-Kit 시스템의 **임상 결과**를 15환자 20임플란트 후향 시리즈로 검증하며, RBH ≥2mm 하한까지 확장한 실제 데이터를 제공한다.

## One-line Summary

Retrospective case series (n=15, 20 implants): CAS kit hydraulic TSFE + tissue-level implants achieved 9.6±2.4mm bone gain, 0 implant failures, and 0 complications at 9-month follow-up with minimum RBH of 2mm.

## 한줄요약

후향적 케이스 시리즈(n=15, 20임플란트): CAS-Kit 수압식 경치조골 상악동거상 (TSFE) + 조직 레벨 임플란트 → 평균 골이득 9.6±2.4mm, 임플란트 실패 0, 합병증 0(9개월 추적, 최소 RBH 2mm).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Extends CAS-Kit minimum RBH indication down to **2mm** (historically 3mm or 5mm for transcrestal).
- Largest single-center retrospective series with tissue-level implants + CAS-Kit: 15 patients, 20 implants, mean follow-up 19.5±6.4 months.
- Bone gain 9.6±2.4mm; membrane elevation settled from 13.8±1.8mm (placement) to 13.0±1.6mm (9m) — minimal remodeling.
- Bone above implant tip: 3.4±1.7mm at placement → 3.0±1.2mm at 9m (clinically stable).
- 100% implant and prosthetic survival; no membrane perforations reported intraoperatively.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective case series, single center (San Pietro Hospital, Rome), 2021–2024.
- **Patients**: 15 of 36 candidates; 21 excluded (16 missing follow-up panoramic, 3 moved, 2 health emergencies).
- **Inclusion**: RBH ≥2mm, posterior maxilla, partially edentulous, age ≥18.
- **Exclusion**: Sinus pathology, heavy smoking (≥10 cigs/day), untreated periodontitis, FMBS/FMPI >25%, ASA III/IV, head-neck irradiation <5y, bisphosphonate therapy.
- **Implant**: PRAMA (Sweden & Martina) tissue-level implants, 2.8mm convergent neck (UTM surface, ZirTi alloy, BOPT design); seating torque ≥25 Ncm.
- **Graft**: Bio-Oss 0.25–1mm (anorganic bovine bone) in all cases.
- **Follow-up**: Panoramic radiography at placement and 9 months (crown delivery).
- **Statistics**: Wilcoxon signed-rank test (non-parametric), p<0.05. Implant = statistical unit.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Retrospective design**: selection bias; 21/36 (58%) excluded, predominantly due to missing panoramic at crown delivery — survivorship concern.
- **Small sample**: n=15/20 implants; insufficient for subgroup analysis by RBH or bone density.
- **Panoramic only**: not CBCT — measurement accuracy lower than volumetric imaging.
- **No control group**: no comparison to lateral approach or other TSFE techniques.
- **Short follow-up**: 9-month radiographic endpoint; long-term implant survival unestablished.
- Future: CBCT-based measurement, RCT with larger cohorts, head-to-head vs lateral approach.

## 6. Related Work

- **Lin 2025** (FEA): CAS-Kit hydraulic pressure generates lowest von Mises stress and requires least force at 5mm elevation — this study confirms no membrane perforations in clinical practice.
- **Gatti 2018** (PMID 29889923): 2-year prospective CAS-Kit series (n=35 patients, 49 elevations), no failures — larger and longer but older protocol without tissue-level implants.
- **Wang 2018** (PMID 30522205): 1204 implants CAS-KIT vs conventional transalveolar — confirms CAS-Kit advantage in perforation rate.
- **Yassin Alsabbagh**: CAS-Kit vs balloon vs conventional osteotome — CAS-Kit superior safety.

## 7. Glossary

- **CAS-Kit** (Crestal Approach Sinus): rounded drill series + hydraulic lifter + bone carrier; OSSTEM system for TSFE
- **PRAMA implant**: tissue-level implant with 2.8mm convergent neck (UTM surface), BOPT design (Sweden & Martina)
- **BOPT**: Biologically Oriented Preparation Technique — supragingival chamfer prep adapting to soft tissue
- **Overall membrane elevation**: panoramic height = RBH + implant length + bone above apex (combined graft column height)
- **STROBE**: Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology (reporting guideline used)

## Three-line Summary

- Retrospective single-center case series (n=15 patients, 20 implants, Rome 2021–2024) evaluating CAS-Kit hydraulic transcrestal sinus floor elevation combined with PRAMA tissue-level implants, with minimum residual bone height of 2mm and 9-month radiographic follow-up.
- Mean bone gain was 9.6±2.4mm; membrane elevation settled from 13.8±1.8mm at placement to 13.0±1.6mm at 9 months (−0.8mm, p=0.000); 100% implant and prosthetic survival; zero membrane perforations or complications.
- CAS-Kit hydraulic transcrestal sinus floor elevation is feasible down to residual bone height 2mm with tissue-level implants, extending the conventional 3–5mm threshold; minimal membrane remodeling and stable bone above the implant tip support long-term osseointegration.

## 세줄요약

- 후향적 케이스 시리즈(n=15, 20임플란트, 로마 2021–2024): CAS-Kit 수압식 치조정접근 상악동 거상(Transcrestal Sinus Floor Elevation, TSFE) + PRAMA 조직-레벨(tissue-level) 임플란트, 최소 잔존골 높이(Residual Bone Height, RBH) 2mm 조건, 9개월 방사선 추적.
- 평균 골이득 9.6±2.4mm; 막 거상 높이 식립 시 13.8±1.8mm → 9개월 13.0±1.6mm(−0.8mm, p=0.000); 임플란트·보철 생존율 100%; 막 천공·합병증 0.
- CAS-Kit 수압식 TSFE는 RBH 2mm까지 안전하게 적용 가능 — 기존 3–5mm 기준을 하향 확장; 막 리모델링 최소 및 임플란트 상방 골 안정 확인.
