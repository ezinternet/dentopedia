## Why Ingested

기존 bone-biology 페이지들은 대부분 *기계적 부하* 기전(교합 저하·[[bone-biology/xu-2016-sclerostin-wnt-occlusal-hypofunction-alveolar-bone]], type-H 혈관신생·[[bone-biology/chen-2023-occlusal-force-alveolar-bone-type-h-angiogenesis]])과 잔존치조제 흡수 병태([[bone-biology/kondo-2022-current-perspectives-residual-ridge]]) 축에 몰려 있고, *노화(aging)* 축을 조직 원리로 정리한 페이지가 없다. 노화는 임플란트를 가장 많이 받는 환자군인데, 노령 치조골 생물학을 hallmarks-of-aging 틀로 정리하면 치주염 감수성·치유 지연·peri-implant 골을 하나로 묶는 organizing framework가 된다. 이 Perio 2000 narrative review는 그 축을 채운다. 동물 대응 근거인 [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/shin-2026-aging-socket-healing-rat-jeonbuk]](노령 rat에서 발치와 치유 저하)의 상위 기전 리뷰로도 짝을 이룬다.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Periodontology 2000, Univ. of Pittsburgh) organizing age-related bone biology as a framework linking periodontal disease (PD), impaired healing, and peri-implant bone in older adults; PubMed literature search, no meta-analysis.

Aging degrades bone via matrix change (AGE crosslinks up to 10× higher, ~80% strength loss age 20→80), inflammaging + cellular senescence/SASP (3× more senescent cells in old mice; SA-β-gal+ periodontal ligament cells drive horizontal bone loss), immunosenescence (blunted neutrophil phagocytosis/NETs, M1-shifted macrophages, Th1/Th17 expansion → osteoclast activity), osteocyte TGF-β dysregulation, and osteoprogenitor (SSC/MSC) exhaustion with a shift from osteogenesis to adipogenesis (↑RANKL, ↓OPG); parabiosis/chimera rejuvenation confirms the aging immune milieu itself impairs bone repair.

Increased age alone is NOT a contraindication to implants or regeneration, but osteoporosis/aging effects on peri-implant bone remain unresolved and the aging effect on alveolar-bone healing and osseointegration specifically is largely unstudied — a large translational-target table (senolytic/metformin/rapamycin/PTH/melatonin/aspirin etc.) is preclinical.

## 세줄요약

Periodontology 2000 종설(피츠버그대) — 노화 골 생물학을 hallmarks-of-aging 틀로 정리해 치주염(PD)·치유 저하·peri-implant 골을 노령 환자에서 하나로 연결. PubMed 문헌검색 기반, 메타분석 없음.

노화는 기질(AGE 가교 최대 10배↑, 20→80세 강도 약 80%↓), inflammaging + 세포노화/SASP(노령 마우스 senescent 세포 3배, SA-β-gal+ 치주인대세포가 수평 골흡수 유발), 면역노화(중성구 탐식·NET 저하, M1 대식세포, Th1/Th17 확장 → 파골세포 활성), 골세포 TGF-β 신호 교란, 골전구세포(SSC/MSC) 고갈로 골형성→지방생성 편향(RANKL↑, OPG↓)을 통해 골을 악화. parabiosis/키메라 실험으로 노령 면역환경 자체가 골재생을 저해함을 확인.

고령 자체는 임플란트·재생술의 금기가 아니나, 골다공증/노화가 peri-implant 골에 미치는 영향은 미해결이며 특히 치조골 치유·osseointegration에 대한 노화 영향은 거의 미연구 — 대규모 중개 표적 표(senolytic/metformin/rapamycin/PTH/melatonin/aspirin 등)는 전임상 단계.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Narrative review article (Periodontology 2000, 2026; DOI 10.1111/prd.70035, open access CC-BY).
- **Authors**: Dalia Rasheed Issa (also Kafrelsheikh Univ., Egypt), Giuseppe Intini, Daniel Clark — Dept. of Periodontics & Preventive Dentistry and Center for Craniofacial Regeneration, Univ. of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine (Clark & Intini also School of Medicine / UPMC Hillman Cancer Center).
- **Funding**: NIH K08DE029505, R01DE032696. No conflict declared; no new data.
- **Scope**: Age-related changes to (alveolar) bone matrix, mineral, bone cells, immune cells, and osteoprogenitor cells, and their implications for periodontal disease (PD) and implant dentistry, with emphasis on osteoprogenitor stem cells and translational rejuvenation targets.
- **Method**: PubMed literature search focused on known age-related pathological processes affecting bone in peri-implant and periodontal disease. Not systematic; no quantitative synthesis.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Applies the **hallmarks-of-aging** paradigm (López-Otín) specifically to alveolar/craniofacial bone, connecting PD susceptibility, impaired healing/regeneration, and peri-implant bone in one framework.
2. Frames PD as an **age-related chronic inflammatory condition** (47% of US adults; 68% of those ≥65) whose osteolytic bone loss is amplified by inflammaging and cellular senescence.
3. Consolidates the **osteoprogenitor (SSC/MSC) aging** literature — the paper's declared "special emphasis" — including the osteogenesis→adipogenesis shift, and a large summary Table 3 of translational modulators.
4. Provides two literature tables on **clinical implant outcomes vs. age** (Table 1) and **osteoporosis vs. implant outcomes** (Table 2), showing the evidence is mixed/unresolved.
5. Repeatedly flags the **peri-implant knowledge gap**: most mechanistic aging-bone work is orthopedic; the effect of aging on alveolar-bone healing and osseointegration specifically is under-studied.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Conceptual review structured by biological level. Sections: (2) aging bone matrix & mineral; (3) osteoimmunological changes with age; (4) aging bone cells; (5) aging of osteoprogenitor cells; (6) translational strategies to modulate osteoprogenitors; (7) conclusion. Evidence drawn largely from mouse models (fracture-healing chimera/parabiosis, ovariectomized rodents, aged-mouse periodontitis) plus human observational studies for the clinical implant/osteoporosis tables. Mechanistic claims are model-based; clinical claims are associational.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Bone matrix & mineral (Sec 2).**
- Bone strength falls markedly with age — up to **~80% lower strength in an 80-yr-old vs 20-yr-old**; increased fragility/fracture risk.
- **Non-enzymatic crosslinking via advanced glycation end-products (AGEs)** increases with age; AGE content **up to 10× higher** in older bone. AGE crosslinks form mid-fibril, restrict collagen deformation, reduce toughness and impair loading capacity (relevant to occlusal/implant load; exaggerated in diabetes).
- Mineral changes: carbonate-for-phosphate substitution in hydroxyapatite, altered crystallinity/crystal length → predicted decreased stiffness (full effects unclear).
- **Osteoporosis**: alveolar BMD cannot be measured by DXA (heterogeneous mineralized tissue/teeth); surrogate radiographic measures (mandibular cortical width, densitometry) correlate with axial-skeleton BMD. Systemic BMD associates with alveolar crest height and CAL, but the **2017 World Workshop** concluded osteoporosis was associated with alveolar bone loss but NOT with other PD clinical parameters. Osteoporosis↔implant success is **not clear** (Table 2: some studies no effect, others ↑crestal bone loss/failure); osteoporosis alone is **not a contraindication**.

**Osteoimmunology / inflammaging (Sec 3).**
- **Inflammaging** = age-related chronic low-grade inflammation (↑IL-6, CRP, IFN-γ, TNF-α), contributing to PD, osteoporosis, atherosclerosis, insulin resistance, T2DM.
- **Cellular senescence + SASP** (chemokines, cytokines, prostaglandins, ROS) mirrors inflammaging mediators; senescent cells resist apoptosis and accumulate (**~3× more in old vs young mice**; identifiable by p16^Ink4a). Clearing p16+ cells delays age-associated disease and extends lifespan in mice. **SA-β-gal+ senescent cells accumulate in aged periodontium and associate with significant horizontal bone resorption** (Ikegami 2023 — senescent periodontal ligament cells trigger inflammation).
- **Immunosenescence**: neutrophils show reduced phagocytosis and reduced NET formation with age → impaired microbial control. Macrophages shift toward pro-inflammatory **M1** with a distinct age-related transcriptional signature during bone healing; inhibiting/preventing macrophage recruitment improved PD severity and fracture healing in old mice. **Thymic involution** → fewer naïve T cells, blunted TCR response; **CD4+ bias toward Th1 over Th2**; **Th17 expansion** (Th17 promotes osteoclast activity) → osteolysis.
- Human experimental gingivitis: **older subjects mount a more severe inflammatory response at equal plaque levels**.
- **Aging immune milieu impairs regeneration independent of bone-intrinsic aging**: bone-marrow **chimera** (young HSCs into old mouse) and **parabiosis** (shared young circulation) both **improved fracture healing** in old mice — youthful circulation rejuvenates repair via β-catenin modulation. Peri-implant analog is unstudied.

**Aging bone cells (Sec 4).**
- Osteoblasts (4–6% of bone cells), osteocytes (>90%), osteoclasts (hematopoietic, resorptive).
- Old vs young: **↓osteogenesis genes (RUNX2, OPN, OC)**; **↑osteoclastogenesis with ↑RANKL and ↓OPG**; ~10× higher p16^Ink4a/p21^Cip1 senescence markers across osteoblasts/osteoclasts/osteocytes in old mice.
- Estrogen deficiency (postmenopausal) → ↑osteoclast activity, ↓osteoblast survival, ↑pro-inflammatory cytokines.
- **Osteocyte aging** disrupts resorption–formation coupling via **TGF-β–dependent signaling dysregulation**: ↓perilacunar/canalicular remodeling genes (Mmp13, Acp5, Ctsk), ↓toughness, ↑stiffness. Osteocyte-specific TGF-β receptor knockout in young mice reproduces the aged phenotype.

**Osteoprogenitor aging (Sec 5).** MSC (broad/heterogeneous) vs SSC (skeletal-lineage-restricted) distinguished. Aged osteoprogenitors show ↑ROS, mitochondrial degradation, DNA damage, telomere shortening, senescence (↑p16^INK4a, SA-β-gal), apoptosis resistance, cell-cycle arrest, ↑SASP — driving a **decline in osteogenic differentiation (↓RUNX2, ALP, BSP, OPN, OC) and a shift toward adipogenesis (↑aP2/FABP4, ↑PPAR-γ)** = marrow-fat accumulation at bone's expense.

**Translational targets (Sec 6, Table 3 — mostly preclinical/in-vitro).** Modulating the extracellular environment (young serum/marrow rejuvenates MSCs; low oxygen tension/HIF-1 → ↑RUNX2, BMP-9, Wnt), hormones (17β-estradiol reverses SSC impairment; PTH/PTHG2 → ↑osteoblast, ↓adipogenic differentiation via cAMP-PKA; melatonin → p38-MAPK/ERK/Wnt, ↑BIC in animals, ↓crestal bone loss in humans per one meta-analysis), natural compounds (celastrol, resveratrol → ↓marrow adipocytes, ↓peri-implant bone loss in diabetic rats), drugs (metformin → autophagy/AMPK, ↑osseointegration in OVX rats; aspirin → ↑RUNX2/ALP, ↑periodontal regeneration; rapamycin → autophagy, reverses PD-induced alveolar bone loss in aged mice; simvastatin → ↓adipogenesis, ↑osseointegration interface), growth factors (BDNF), H₂S (Ca²⁺-GSK3β/β-catenin), autophagy induction (Optn), epigenetic (KDM4B, HDAC6), and anti-miR-155-5p.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Narrative (non-systematic) review** — no risk-of-bias appraisal, no pooled estimates; selection and interpretation are author-driven.
- Most mechanistic aging-bone evidence is **orthopedic/rodent**; direct alveolar-bone and especially **peri-implant/osseointegration** aging data are sparse — repeatedly stated as an open gap.
- Clinical implant-vs-age and osteoporosis-vs-implant evidence is **mixed/unresolved** (Tables 1–2 include both null and positive-risk findings).
- Nearly all Table 3 rejuvenation strategies are **in vitro or animal**; no human efficacy for aged alveolar bone/implant osseointegration is established.
- Aging inflammatory dysregulation cannot easily be isolated from concurrent stem-cell/matrix aging in vivo (addressed only partially by chimera/parabiosis designs).

## 7. Glossary

- **Inflammaging** — age-related chronic low-grade systemic inflammation (↑IL-6, CRP, IFN-γ, TNF-α) predisposing to PD, osteoporosis, metabolic disease.
- **Cellular senescence / SASP** — irreversible cell-cycle arrest; senescence-associated secretory phenotype = pro-inflammatory secretome (cytokines, ROS, prostaglandins) driving tissue dysfunction; p16^Ink4a / SA-β-gal markers.
- **Immunosenescence** — age-related decline in immune competence (thymic involution, ↓naïve T cells, blunted neutrophil phagocytosis/NETs).
- **AGEs** — advanced glycation end-products; non-enzymatic collagen crosslinks that stiffen and embrittle bone matrix, accumulate with age and in diabetes.
- **SSC vs MSC** — skeletal stem cell (skeletal-lineage-restricted) vs mesenchymal stem cell (broad multipotent); both used for osteoprogenitors.
- **RANKL / OPG** — pro-osteoclastogenic ligand vs its decoy receptor; aging raises RANKL and lowers OPG → net resorption.
- **Perilacunar/canalicular remodeling** — osteocyte-mediated local matrix maintenance (Mmp13, Acp5, Ctsk), dysregulated by aging TGF-β signaling.
- **Parabiosis / chimera models** — surgically shared circulation / marrow transplant used to isolate the aging immune contribution to bone repair.
