## Why Ingested

Systematic review of esthetic outcomes across timing protocols for immediate implant placement and loading in single-tooth implants. Companion review in the same ITI Consensus Conference special issue as [[immediate-implant/pommer-2021-maxillary-single-tooth-timing-protocols-sr-ma]] — while Pommer evaluates survival and marginal bone resorption outcomes, Francisco specifically evaluates esthetic outcomes (soft-tissue dimensional changes and esthetic indices) across the same placement and loading timing axes, providing the esthetic-outcome complement to Pommer's bone-level focus.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis of 18 RCTs (8549 screened, 72 full-text assessed) on timing of implant placement (immediate/early/delayed) and loading (immediate/early/conventional/delayed) in single-tooth implants, evaluated using soft-tissue and esthetic indices (midfacial recession, papilla level, Pink Esthetic Score, White Esthetic Score, Papilla Index).

No statistically significant differences found in esthetic outcomes between placement-timing groups (immediate vs. early vs. delayed) at 1-, 2-, or 10-year follow-up; loading timing (immediate vs. conventional loading) showed no impact on midfacial mucosal margin or esthetic indices in short- and medium-term follow-up.

Both immediate and early implant placement protocols demonstrated stable esthetic treatment outcomes over time; timing of loading protocol does not appear to influence esthetic outcomes in the available evidence.

## 세줄요약

단일치아 임플란트에서 식립타이밍(즉시/조기/지연식립)과 부하타이밍(즉시/조기/종래/지연부하) 영향을 평가한 체계적고찰 메타분석; 18개 RCT 포함(8549개 선별, 72개 전문 평가), 연조직 및 심미지수(mid-facial recession, papilla level, Pink Esthetic Score, White Esthetic Score, Papilla Index) 사용.

식립타이밍 그룹 간(즉시 vs. 조기 vs. 지연) 심미 결과에 유의한 차이 없음(1년·2년·10년 추적); 부하타이밍(즉시 vs. 종래부하)도 mid-facial mucosal margin 및 심미지수에 영향 미치지 않음(단기·중기 추적).

즉시 및 조기식립 프로토콜 모두 장시간 안정적인 심미 결과 제시; 현재 근거로는 부하프로토콜의 타이밍이 심미 결과에 영향을 미치지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Comprehensive SR/MA systematically comparing esthetic outcomes across placement-timing (immediate vs. early vs. delayed) AND loading-timing (immediate vs. early vs. conventional vs. delayed) combinations in single-tooth implants — the esthetic outcome complement to bone-level/survival SRs.
- Restricts scope to studies reporting objective esthetic measures: midfacial recession, papilla level, Pink Esthetic Score, White Esthetic Score, Papilla Index.
- Demonstrates equivalence (non-inferiority) of immediate and early placement timing for esthetic outcomes, contrary to clinical intuition that earlier healing time improves tissue stability.
- Provides evidence that loading protocol alone does not drive esthetic outcome differences in single-tooth implant restoration.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review with meta-analysis.
- **Databases**: Electronic search across four databases until June 2020; Cochrane Risk of Bias Tool applied.
- **Inclusion criteria**: RCTs with minimum 10 patients, 1-year follow-up, reporting dimensional changes of peri-implant mucosa (midfacial recession, papilla level) and/or esthetic indices.
- **Screening**: 8549 articles initially identified; 72 full-text articles assessed for eligibility; 18 studies included (9 evaluating placement-timing outcomes, 9 evaluating loading-timing outcomes).
- **Outcomes**: Midfacial recession, papilla level, Pink Esthetic Score (PES), White Esthetic Score (WES), Papilla Index.
- **Statistical method**: Meta-analyses conducted on comparable trials; heterogeneous findings tabulated.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Comparison | Studies | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate vs. delayed placement (midfacial recession) | Multiple | Heterogeneous findings; no consistent difference at 1–2 years |
| Immediate vs. early placement (recession, papilla level) | Multiple | No differences at 1–2 years |
| Conventional vs. immediate loading (midfacial margin change) | Multiple | Not statistically significant at 1-year follow-up |
| Pink Esthetic Score across timing groups | All included | No differences between groups |
| White Esthetic Score across timing groups | All included | No differences between groups |
| Papilla Index across timing groups | All included | No differences between groups |
| Long-term esthetic stability (1–10 years) | All groups | Immediate and early placement stable at 1-, 2-, and 10-year follow-up |

**Key null finding**: Loading protocol timing (immediate vs. conventional) did not significantly influence esthetic outcomes in short- and medium-term follow-up.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Heterogeneous outcome reporting**: Included trials varied widely in esthetic indices measured and timing of measurement, limiting meta-analytic pooling.
- **Small trial numbers**: Only 9 trials evaluated placement timing and 9 evaluated loading timing separately; insufficient power for sub-group analysis by timing combination.
- **Short follow-up in many trials**: Although the review aimed for ≥1 year, few trials provided 2–10 year data, limiting long-term esthetic stability conclusions.
- **Surgical technique variability**: Confounding by surgical approach (flap vs. flapless), implant macrogeometry, and soft-tissue grafting techniques not systematically controlled.
- **Provisionalization protocol not controlled**: Immediate provisionalization (temporary crown) may influence soft-tissue remodeling independent of placement/loading timing; most trials did not isolate this variable.
- **Publication bias risk**: No mention of systematic assessment of publication bias; small negative trials less likely published.

## 7. Glossary

- **Immediate Implant Placement (IP)**: Implant placement into a fresh extraction socket within 8 weeks post-extraction.
- **Early Implant Placement (EP)**: Implant placement 8 weeks to ~4 months post-extraction.
- **Delayed Implant Placement (DP)**: Implant placement ≥4–6 months post-extraction, in healed bone.
- **Immediate Loading (IL)**: Functional prosthetic load applied at time of implant placement.
- **Early Loading (EL)**: Functional load applied within 2–8 weeks of placement.
- **Conventional/Delayed Loading (DL)**: Load applied ≥3 months post-placement after osseointegration.
- **Midfacial Recession**: Apical shift of the buccal gingival margin relative to the initial position; measured in millimeters from fixed reference (CEJ, implant shoulder).
- **Papilla Level**: Vertical distance from the implant-crown contact point to the height of the inter-proximal soft tissue; measured in millimeters.
- **Pink Esthetic Score (PES)**: Seven-item index assessing gingival contour, soft-tissue margin, soft-tissue texture, and gingival color (Fürhauser et al. 2005; 0–2 per item, max 14).
- **White Esthetic Score (WES)**: Tooth crown esthetic assessment: form, outline, contact, color, translucency, and surface texture (Belser et al. 2009; 0–2 per item, max 10).
- **Papilla Index**: Score assessing inter-proximal papilla completeness on mesial and distal aspects (Jemt 1997; scale 0–4).
