## Why Ingested

Early PRISMA-guided SR+MA that quantified the platform-switching MBL benefit and the dose–response with implant–abutment mismatch (≥0.4 mm), cited as the origin of the PS effect size that later SRs refined → [[implants/mbl/strietzel-2015-platform-switching-mbl-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA; searched to 15 March 2010; 10 controlled trials, 1,239 implants) comparing marginal bone-level change and implant failure of platform-switched vs platform-matched implants.

Platform switching reduced marginal bone loss (MD −0.37 mm; 95% CI −0.55 to −0.20; P<0.0001) with no difference in implant failure (RR 0.93; 95% CI 0.34–2.95; P=0.89); an implant–abutment diameter difference ≥0.4 mm was associated with a more favorable bone response and bone resorption was inversely related to the mismatch.

Supports PS for bone preservation, but the included set is small and early (to 2010); the authors call for long-term, well-conducted RCTs.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (SR+MA; PRISMA 지침, 2010년 3월 15일까지 검색; 대조 임상시험 10편, 임플란트 1,239개): 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS) 임플란트와 플랫폼 매칭 (Platform Matching, PM) 임플란트의 변연골 수준 변화와 임플란트 실패율을 비교.

PS는 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)을 유의하게 감소 (MD −0.37 mm; 95% CI −0.55~−0.20; P<0.0001), 임플란트 실패율은 차이 없음 (RR 0.93; 95% CI 0.34–2.95; P=0.89); 임플란트-지대주 직경 차 ≥0.4 mm에서 골반응이 더 양호했고 골흡수는 mismatch 정도에 반비례.

PS의 골보존 효과를 지지하나 포함 연구가 적고 이른 시기 (2010년까지)이며, 저자들도 장기 고품질 RCT가 필요하다고 명시.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: J Periodontol 2010;81(10):1350-66
- **DOI**: 10.1902/jop.2010.100232
- **Institution**: School of Dentistry, University of Otago, Dunedin, New Zealand

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled MBL benefit of PS over PM: −0.37 mm
- No difference in implant failure
- Dose–response: larger implant–abutment mismatch → less bone resorption (subgroup ≥0.4 mm better)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: SR+MA, PRISMA
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Oral Health Group Trials Register, CENTRAL, UK National Research Register, ANZCTR, DARE, CPCI; hand search; authors contacted
- **n**: 10 studies, 1,239 implants
- **Outcomes**: radiographic marginal bone-level change; implant failure; MD and RR with 95% CI

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- MBL: MD −0.37 mm (95% CI −0.55 to −0.20), P<0.0001, favoring PS
- Failure: RR 0.93 (95% CI 0.34–2.95), P=0.89
- Subgroup: mismatch ≥0.4 mm → more favorable bone response

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest; risk-of-bias and heterogeneity details not reviewed
- Search ended March 2010; superseded in scope by larger later SR+MAs
- Authors: validity of the concept needs long-term, well-conducted RCTs

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Platform switching (PS) | Abutment narrower than the implant platform, shifting the junction inward |
| Platform matching (PM) | Abutment and implant platform of equal diameter |
| MD | Mean difference |
| RR | Risk ratio |
