## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/isq/stoilov-2023-macrodesign-length-diameter-bone-quality-isq]]가 "직경 > 길이, 저밀도골에서 차이 감소"를 PU 폼 블록으로 보였는데, 본 in-vitro 연구(Barikani 2013)는 실제 골밀도 유사 인공 골블록(D1/D3)에서 동일한 length×diameter×bone-quality 상호작용을 더 이른 시기(2013)에 확인한 선행 근거로, 상호작용 효과(길이는 저밀도골에서만 유의)의 재현성을 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (60 Nobel Biocare Replace Select TiUnit Tapered implants, 2 lengths × 3 diameters, placed into artificial bone blocks analogous to D1 and D3 bone types) measuring primary stability by resonance frequency analysis (RFA/ISQ, Osstell mentor).

D1 bone gave significantly higher ISQ than D3 (p≤0.001); implant length had no effect on ISQ in D1 bone but significantly increased ISQ in D3 bone (e.g., WP 13mm vs 10mm mean ISQ difference 9.60, p=0.005 in D3); narrow-platform (3.4mm) implants showed significantly lower ISQ than regular (4.3mm) or wide (5mm) platform implants (p≤0.007), with no difference between regular and wide platforms (p≥0.215).

Clinical implication: in low-quality (D3) bone, increasing implant length and avoiding narrow-diameter implants both meaningfully raise primary stability, supporting a combined length+diameter optimization strategy for immediate-loading candidates in soft bone; in vitro artificial-bone-block design (no biological remodeling) is the key limitation.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 연구 (Nobel Biocare Replace Select TiUnit Tapered 임플란트 60개, 길이 2종×직경 3종, D1/D3 유사 인공 골블록에 식립) 후 공진주파수분석(Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA)으로 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 측정.

D1 골이 D3 골보다 ISQ 유의하게 높음(p≤0.001); 임플란트 길이는 D1 골에서는 ISQ에 영향 없었으나 D3 골에서는 유의하게 ISQ 증가(예: WP 13mm vs 10mm 평균차 9.60, D3에서 p=0.005); 협측좁은(Narrow Platform, NP, 3.4mm) 임플란트는 일반형(Regular Platform, RP, 4.3mm)·광폭형(Wide Platform, WP, 5mm)보다 유의하게 낮은 ISQ(p≤0.007), RP-WP 간 차이는 없음(p≥0.215).

임상적 의미: 저밀도(D3) 골에서는 임플란트 길이 증가와 협폭(NP) 임플란트 회피 모두 1차 안정성 향상에 유의미하므로, 연조직골 즉시부하 후보에서 길이·직경 동시 최적화 전략을 지지; 인공 골블록 기반 in-vitro 설계(생물학적 골 리모델링 미반영)가 핵심 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly tested the interaction between implant length, implant diameter, and bone type/quality on ISQ — an interaction that prior single-factor studies (e.g., Bischof et al., Degidi et al.) had not analyzed.
- Showed implant length's effect on ISQ is bone-quality-dependent: no effect in dense (D1) bone, significant positive effect in soft (D3) bone.
- Showed narrow-platform (3.4mm) implants are the clearest ISQ-negative factor regardless of length, especially pronounced in D3 bone.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: in vitro experimental study, artificial bone blocks
- **Materials**: 60 Nobel Biocare Replace Select TiUnit Tapered implants; 2 lengths (10mm, 13mm) × 3 diameters (3.4mm NP, 4.3mm RP, 5mm WP); 2 bone-type analog blocks (D1, D3); 12 groups of n=5
- **Outcome measure**: ISQ via Osstell mentor (RFA) immediately after placement
- **Statistics**: univariate ANOVA for interaction testing; T-test with Bonferroni correction (bone type × length); Tukey's HSD post-hoc (diameter effect); significance α=0.05

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro artificial bone blocks only — no biological bone remodeling, blood supply, or patient variability captured.
- Only two bone-density analogs tested (D1, D3); D2/D4 not evaluated.
- Single implant system (Nobel Biocare Replace Select TiUnit Tapered) — findings may not generalize to other macro-designs (see [[implants/isq/stoilov-2023-macrodesign-length-diameter-bone-quality-isq]] for macro-design as an independent driver).
- No long-term/secondary stability or osseointegration outcome measured (ISQ only at placement).

## 6. Related Work

- Bischof et al. (2004): found length/diameter had no significant ISQ effect, but excluded bone type IV patients — potential explanation for divergence from this study's D3 finding.
- Ostman et al. (2006): wide-platform implants → higher ISQ in posterior sites; longer implants → lower ISQ (opposite length direction to this study, attributed to heat generation from longer drilling).
- Degidi et al. (2012, 4,135 implants): found only weak correlation between RFA and implant length/diameter; did not analyze the bone-quality interaction that this study specifically targets.
- Winter et al. (FEA study): implant length positively correlated with stability only at lower bone stiffness — consistent direction with this study's D3 finding.
- [[implants/isq/stoilov-2023-macrodesign-length-diameter-bone-quality-isq]]: 2023 in-vitro PU-foam study finding diameter > length as the dominant ISQ driver, with macro-design (tapered vs parallel) as an independent factor — complements this 2013 study's bone-quality × length interaction.

## 7. Glossary

- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: 1–100 scale output of resonance frequency analysis quantifying implant primary/secondary stability.
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis)**: non-invasive method (Meredith technique) using a transducer attached to the implant/abutment to assess bone-to-implant contact stiffness.
- **NP/RP/WP**: Narrow Platform (3.4mm) / Regular Platform (4.3mm) / Wide Platform (5mm) implant diameter classes.
- **D1/D3 bone type**: Misch/Lekholm-Zarb bone density classification analogs; D1 = dense cortical, D3 = thin cortical with low-density trabecular core.
