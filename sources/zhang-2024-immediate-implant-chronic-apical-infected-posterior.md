## Three-line Summary

5-year prospective-style clinical observation (n=36 patients, 41 simple taper retentive implants), Nanchang University 4th Affiliated Hospital, China; immediate implant placement in posterior teeth with chronic apical infection after thorough debridement.

Implant survival 97.6% (1/41 failed, mobility); marginal bone resorption -0.74±1.03 mm (placement→crown) then a further -0.21±0.70 mm (crown→5yr, NS); 1 case of peri-implantitis, no mechanical complications, good patient satisfaction.

Long-term (5-year) data support that immediate implantation after meticulous debridement of chronic apically infected posterior sockets achieves stable outcomes, expanding indications for immediate implantation — abstract-only source (original Chinese-language article; PMC full text body not retrievable).

## 세줄요약

5년 임상관찰 (n=36 환자, 41개 단순 원추형 유지 임플란트), 중국 난창대학교 제4부속병원; 후치부 만성 치근단감염 (chronic apical infection) 치아에 철저한 소파 후 즉시식립.

임플란트 생존율 97.6% (41개 중 1개 동요도로 실패); 변연골 흡수 -0.74±1.03 mm (식립→보철장착), 이후 5년까지 추가 -0.21±0.70 mm (NS); 임플란트주위염 1례, 기계적 합병증 없음, 환자 만족도 양호.

5년 장기 데이터가 만성 근첨감염 후치부 발치와의 철저한 소파 후 즉시식립이 안정적 결과를 낸다는 근거를 보강 — 원문이 중국어라 초록만 확보(PMC 본문 텍스트 미제공).

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the few **5-year** (mid/long-term) follow-ups specifically on immediate implantation in chronic apically infected **posterior** sites — most infected-socket IIP literature ([[immediate-implant/infected-socket/munoz-camara-2020-immediate-implants-acute-periapical-infected]], [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/colak-2023-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-retrospective]]) is 1–2 year and/or anterior/esthetic-zone weighted
- Reports a two-phase marginal bone resorption pattern: larger early resorption (placement→crown, -0.74 mm) followed by a small, statistically non-significant further loss over the following 5 years (-0.21 mm) — consistent with early remodeling stabilizing rather than progressive bone loss
- No significant difference in resorption by mesial/distal, maxilla/mandible, tooth position, or implant length — suggests posterior-region chronic apical infection does not create site-specific risk once debrided

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Single-center clinical observation, 5-year follow-up post-crown-restoration
- **n**: 36 patients, 41 simple taper retentive implants, posterior teeth with chronic apical infection
- **Protocol**: thorough debridement of the chronic infected alveolar socket, immediate implant placement
- **Assessments**: marginal bone resorption (radiographic) at placement, at crown restoration, and at 5-year post-restoration; peri-implant soft tissue health; mechanical complications; patient satisfaction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Implant survival (5 yr) | 97.6% (40/41; 1 failure, mobility) |
| MBL, placement → crown restoration | -0.74 ± 1.03 mm (p<0.05) |
| MBL, crown restoration → 5 yr | -0.21 ± 0.70 mm (NS, p>0.05) |
| Peri-implantitis | 1 case |
| Mechanical complications | 0 |
| Site-specific resorption difference (mesial/distal, jaw, tooth position, implant length) | NS for all |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingestion: PMC full-text body was empty and the original article is Chinese-language, so methodological detail (debridement protocol specifics, antibiotic regimen, implant system, statistical methods) beyond the structured abstract is not captured in this wiki page
- No control/comparator group (non-infected posterior IIP) reported in the retrievable abstract — cannot itself establish equivalence, only within-group stability over time
- Single-center, moderate sample size (n=41 implants)
