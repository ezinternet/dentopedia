## Why Ingested

Animal-model counter-evidence on bone condensation: it tests the assumption that condensing bone improves interfacial density and primary stability, which bears on condensing/osteotome-type protocols such as [[implants/osseodensification/tian-2019-alveolar-ridge-expansion-osseodensification-osteotome]] and the osteotome arm of [[implants/osteotomy-thermal/slete-2018-histomorphometric-comparison-three-osteotomy-techniques]].

abstract-only — full text not retrieved

## Three-line Summary

Murine oral implant model: osteotomies in healed maxillary extraction sites made by drilling alone vs drilling followed by stepwise condensation with tapered osteotomes (group sizes not stated in the abstract).

Condensation raised interfacial bone density (significant change in bone volume/total volume and trabecular spacing) but produced microfractures and osteoclast activity on postimplant day 1, very high interfacial strains and marginal bone resorption, with no improvement in implant stability.

Condensation did not positively contribute to implant stability in this animal model; extrapolation to humans is not tested in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

생쥐 구강 임플란트 모델: 치유된 상악 발치와에서 드릴링만 vs 드릴링 후 테이퍼드 골압축기 (Osteotome)로 단계적 압축 (군별 n은 초록에 없음).

압축은 계면 골밀도를 높였으나 (골체적/전체체적, 골소주 간격 유의한 변화) 식립 1일째 미세골절과 파골세포 활성, 매우 높은 계면 변형률과 변연골 흡수를 유발했고 임플란트 안정성은 개선되지 않았다.

이 동물 모델에서 골 압축 (Condensation)은 임플란트 안정성에 기여하지 않았으며, 사람으로의 외삽은 초록만으로는 검증되지 않는다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Tests two assumptions, that condensation densifies interfacial bone and that it improves primary stability, which the authors say lack substantiating data.
- Multiscale analysis: finite element modeling, mechanical testing and immunohistochemistry across the osseointegration period.
- Concludes condensation does not positively contribute to implant stability.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Murine oral implant model, controlled comparison of two osteotomy techniques
- **Groups**: drilling vs drilling plus stepwise condensation with tapered osteotomes
- **n**: not stated in the abstract
- **Outcomes**: bone volume/total volume, trabecular spacing, microfractures, osteoclast activity, interfacial strain (finite element), implant stability, marginal bone resorption

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Interfacial bone density increased with condensation (significant change in BV/TV and trabecular spacing); no numeric values in the abstract.
- Day 1: microfractures and osteoclast activity at the condensed interface.
- Very high interfacial strains and marginal bone resorption; no improvement in implant stability.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only ingest; numeric effect sizes and sample sizes not captured.
- Murine model; human applicability not addressed in the abstract.
