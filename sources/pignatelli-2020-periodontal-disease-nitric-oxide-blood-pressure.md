## Why Ingested

This narrative review introduces a clinically important paradox: chlorhexidine mouthwash—widely recommended for preprocedural rinsing and ICU oral hygiene—may raise systemic blood pressure by 2–3.5 mmHg through disruption of oral nitrate-reducing bacteria essential to the nitric oxide vasodilatory pathway. This systemic risk is especially relevant for hypertensive patients and provides a critical counterpoint to the otherwise unconditional promotion of CHX mouthwash in studies like [[infection-control/elzein-2021-chlorhexidine-povidone-iodine-mouthwash-salivary-sars-cov-2-rct]].

## Three-line Summary

This narrative review explores the link between oral health, specifically the abundance of nitrate-reducing bacteria and periodontal disease, and systemic blood pressure (BP) via the nitric oxide (NO) pathway. Endogenous NO, a key vasodilator, is supplemented by the "nitrate-nitrite-NO pathway," where dietary nitrates (e.g., from vegetables) are reduced to nitrite by oral commensal bacteria on the tongue and then to NO in the stomach and circulation.

Disruption of this pathway by antiseptic mouthwashes (e.g., chlorhexidine) or tongue cleaning can reduce salivary nitrite production, leading to increased BP. Conversely, periodontal disease, characterized by pathogenic bacteria and inflammation, is associated with endothelial dysfunction and hypertension, although the specific role of nitrate-reducing bacteria in this relationship requires further study.

Restoring a healthy oral microbiome, potentially through probiotics or dietary nitrate supplementation, may offer a novel therapeutic strategy to enhance NO bioavailability and manage hypertension.

항균 구강 세정액(예: 클로르헥시딘) 또는 혀 세척에 의한 이 경로의 교란은 타액 아질산염 생성을 감소시켜 혈압 상승을 초래할 수 있습니다. 반대로, 병원성 박테리아와 염증을 특징으로 하는 치주 질환은 내피 기능 장애 및 고혈압과 관련이 있지만, 이 관계에서 질산염 환원 박테리아의 특정 역할은 추가 연구가 필요합니다.

프로바이오틱스 또는 식이 질산염 보충을 통해 건강한 구강 미생물을 복원하는 것은 NO 생체 이용률을 향상시키고 고혈압을 관리하기 위한 새로운 치료 전략이 될 수 있습니다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reviews the critical role of the enterosalivary nitrate-nitrite-NO pathway in systemic blood pressure regulation.
- Identifies oral commensal bacteria (e.g., Veillonella, Actinomyces, Haemophilus, Neisseria) as essential for nitrate reduction and NO bioavailability.
- Highlights that antiseptic mouthwashes (particularly chlorhexidine) can disrupt this pathway, leading to a measurable increase in blood pressure (e.g., by 2-3.5 mmHg).
- Links periodontal disease and its associated pathogens to endothelial dysfunction and hypertension.
- Proposes probiotic therapy as a potential future strategy to restore the oral microbiome and manage hypertension.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Narrative Review.
- The review synthesizes existing literature to discuss the interplay between dietary nitrate, oral nitrate-reducing bacteria, the NO pathway, and blood pressure.
- It specifically examines the impact of antiseptic mouthwash on this pathway and the association between periodontitis and hypertension.
- The paper references and discusses results from various study types, including RCTs, observational studies, and in vitro research.
- It outlines the biochemical pathways of NO synthesis (eNOS, iNOS, nNOS) and the nitrate-nitrite-NO pathway.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome (Mouthwash effect on BP) —** 0.2% chlorhexidine mouthwash twice daily for 7 days reduced salivary NO release by almost 50% and increased systolic and diastolic BP by 2-3.5 mmHg.
- **Primary outcome (Mouthwash effect on nitrite) —** Use of 0.2% chlorhexidine resulted in a significant reduction in salivary and plasma nitrite with a concomitant rise in salivary and plasma nitrate concentrations.
- **Primary outcome (Periodontitis and Hypertension) —** A strong positive association was found between increased subgingival colonization by specific pathogens (A. actinomycetemcomitans, P. gingivalis, T. forsythia, T. denticola) and prevalent hypertension.
- **Primary outcome (Dietary nitrate effect) —** NO3 supplementation increases plasma concentration of NO2 and reduces systemic BP in older (70-79 years) but not young (18-22 years) participants.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- As a narrative review, it is subject to selection bias and does not provide a quantitative synthesis of evidence.
- The correlation between nitrate-reducing bacteria and hypertension has been poorly studied and requires more direct investigation.
- The exact mechanisms by which specific periodontal pathogens contribute to hypertension are not fully understood.
- The effects of different types of mouthwashes on the oral microbiome and BP are not uniformly reported; some studies show no effect.
- More research is needed on the use of probiotics to restore the oral microbiome and enhance NO bioavailability as a therapeutic strategy.
- The potential cytotoxic effects of nitrite, particularly the formation of N-nitrosamines, are not fully addressed.

## 7. Glossary

- **NO (Nitric Oxide)**: 산화질소. 혈관 확장을 유도하고 혈압을 조절하는 중요한 신호 전달 분자입니다.
- **NOS (NO Synthase)**: 산화질소 합성효소. L-아르기닌으로부터 NO를 생성하는 효소 패밀리입니다 (eNOS, nNOS, iNOS).
- **Nitrate-Nitrite-NO Pathway**: 질산염-아질산염-산화질소 경로. 식이 질산염이 구강 박테리아에 의해 아질산염으로, 그리고 NO로 전환되는 생물학적 경로입니다.
- **eNOS (Endothelial NOS)**: 내피 세포에서 발견되며 혈관 긴장도와 내피 기능을 조절하는 NO 합성효소입니다.
- **CPC (Cetylpyridinium Chloride)**: 세틸피리디늄 클로라이드. 구강 세정액에 사용되는 항균제입니다. 본 검토에서는 질산염 환원을 방해할 수 있는 물질 중 하나로 언급됩니다.
- **Enterosalivary Cycle**: 장-타액 순환. 식이 질산염이 흡수되어 타액으로 분비되고 구강 박테리아에 의해 아질산염으로 환원되는 과정입니다.
