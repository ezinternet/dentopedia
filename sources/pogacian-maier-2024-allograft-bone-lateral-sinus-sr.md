## Why Ingested

The wiki already held graft-class comparisons for lateral sinus augmentation that barely covered allograft — [[sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]] pooled DBBM, autogenous and synthetic grafts, and [[bone-regeneration/abushama-2025-xenograft-allograft-bone-graft-implant]] had only 1 sinus study in its xenograft-vs-allograft synthesis. No existing sinus-lift page found by QMD/grep is dedicated to allograft (FDBA/DFDBA) in the lateral window approach; this SR fills that gap, surfaced by the bone-graft-material (allograft/xenograft/alloplast) surveillance sweep.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA, STROBE-based risk of bias, RevMan random-effects) of 7 clinical studies (4 prospective, 3 retrospective; 238 patients) using allograft (FDBA, DFDBA) in lateral-window sinus floor elevation versus xenograft (DBB), alloplast (BCP) or autogenous bone; 321 records retrieved, 20 full texts assessed.

Pooled FDBA vs DBB mean bone volume at 6–9 months: WMD −0.17 [−0.69, 0.36], p = 0.53, I² = 81%; pooled implant survival FDBA vs autogenous bone at 6–12 months: RR 1.00 [0.96, 1.05], p = 1.00, I² = 0% — no detectable difference in either comparison.

Allograft is a usable sinus-graft option, but the evidence base is small, observational-heavy, short in follow-up and thin on histology and complications, so superiority over other graft classes cannot be concluded.

## 세줄요약

상악동 측방접근 거상술에서 동종골 (Allograft: 동결건조골이식재 FDBA, 탈회동결건조골이식재 DFDBA)을 이종골 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone, DBB)·합성골 (Biphasic Calcium Phosphate, BCP)·자가골과 비교한 임상연구 7편(전향 4, 후향 3; 환자 238명)의 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR).

6–9개월 골용적 합산 FDBA vs DBB 가중평균차 (Weighted Mean Difference, WMD) −0.17 [−0.69, 0.36], p = 0.53 (I² = 81%); 6–12개월 임플란트 생존율 FDBA vs 자가골 위험비 (Risk Ratio, RR) 1.00 [0.96, 1.05], p = 1.00 (I² = 0%) — 두 비교 모두 차이 없음.

동종골은 상악동 이식재로 사용 가능하나, 소수의 관찰연구 위주·짧은 추적기간·조직학/합병증 자료 부족으로 다른 이식재 대비 우월성은 결론 내릴 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- SR focused on allograft (FDBA/DFDBA) in the lateral window sinus lift, restricted to studies with head-to-head comparison vs DBB, BCP or autogenous bone.
- Quantitative synthesis restricted to two pairs: bone volume (FDBA vs DBB) and implant survival (FDBA vs autogenous bone); neither showed a significant difference.
- Documents the thin evidence base: only 7 eligible comparative studies, observational-heavy design (4 prospective, 3 retrospective), histomorphometry in only 2 studies.
- Concludes allograft can be used in lateral sinus lift but that long-term, controlled trials are needed to define its advantages.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review with meta-analysis of two outcome pairs; PRISMA and Cochrane Handbook; Cohen's kappa for reviewer agreement = 0.95.
- **Databases**: PubMed (116), Web of Science (96), Scopus (109) up to October 2023; grey literature (OpenGrey, ClinicalTrials) and hand search of 16 journals; English only.
- **Selection**: 321 retrieved → 266 after de-duplication → 20 full texts → 7 included (published 2013–2023; Belgium, Iran, Israel, Brazil, Turkey, Italy).
- **Population / intervention / comparison**: adults with atrophic posterior maxilla, systemically healthy; allograft + lateral sinus lift vs autogenous, xenograft or alloplastic graft + lateral sinus lift. Study types allowed: RCT, prospective CCT, prospective/retrospective studies.
- **Outcomes**: implant survival rate (ISR), marginal bone loss, bone volume, new bone formation, biological/prosthetic complications, radiological/histological assessment.
- **Risk of bias**: STROBE framework (rated "good" for all included studies; Table 3).
- **Statistics**: RevMan 5.4, random-effects, 95% CI; RR (Mantel–Haenszel) for ISR, WMD (inverse variance) for bone measures; I² thresholds <30% low, 30–60% moderate, >60% substantial.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Included: 7 studies, 238 patients (129 male, 109 female); 4 prospective + 3 retrospective. All used a lateral window with a resorbable collagen membrane; implants placed simultaneously or 6–9 months after grafting.
- Graft groups reported: DFDBA n = 67, FDBA n = 76, DBB n = 128, BCP n = 13, autogenous bone n = 22 (the unit — patients vs sites — is not stated; the counts sum to more than 238).
- Bone volume (6–9 months), FDBA vs DBB: WMD −0.17 [−0.69, 0.36], p = 0.53, I² = 81% (high heterogeneity). Measurement unit not stated in the text.
- Implant survival (6–12 months), FDBA vs autogenous bone: RR 1.00 [0.96, 1.05], p = 1.00, I² = 0%.
- Individual-study detail from the Discussion: one study with 3.6-year mean follow-up reported implant survival of 81/84 (DFDBA) vs 106/107 (DBB); one implant failed to osseointegrate in the FDBA group at 6 months in another study; a 2-year study found no failures in either FDBA or DBB group.
- Histology (2 studies, not poolable): FDBA vs autogenous bone / allogeneic femoral head in 15 patients gave similar overall results; FDBA reported superior to BCP in a 13-patient bilateral split-mouth study.
- Prosthetic loading reported in 3 studies only (crowns 50.5%, fixed partial dentures 22.4%, overdentures 23.4% in one).
- Authors cite other work (not re-analyzed here): lateral vs transcrestal approach did not change ISR in an earlier meta-analysis; graftless lifts give lower height gain but similar ISQ.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Very few RCTs; mix of 4 prospective and 3 retrospective studies; only 7 studies and 238 patients in total.
- Included studies gave limited or no data on ISR, marginal bone loss, biological and prosthetic complications, implant type, loading time and follow-up.
- Follow-up short (6–12 months for the pooled outcomes); histological data limited to 2 studies.
- High heterogeneity (I² = 81%) for the bone-volume pool; bone-volume unit and comparator for each pooled study not stated in the text.
- Internal inconsistency: Results assign bone volume to FDBA vs DBB and survival to FDBA vs autogenous bone, but the Discussion describes the survival data as FDBA vs DBB and the bone-volume data as FDBA vs autogenous bone. The Results/abstract version is used throughout this wiki entry.
- Risk of bias assessed with STROBE (a reporting checklist, not a risk-of-bias tool); no GRADE certainty rating.
- Authors call for longer-follow-up RCTs comparing allograft with xenogeneic, alloplastic and autogenous bone.

## 7. Glossary

- **FDBA (Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft)**: 동결건조 동종골; mineralized human cadaveric bone, primarily osteoconductive.
- **DFDBA (Demineralized Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft)**: 탈회 동결건조 동종골; acid-demineralized allograft exposing bone matrix proteins.
- **DBB (Deproteinized Bovine Bone)**: 탈단백 소 이종골; xenograft with slow resorption and good volume stability.
- **BCP (Biphasic Calcium Phosphate)**: 이상성 인산칼슘; hydroxyapatite/β-TCP alloplast.
- **WMD / RR**: weighted mean difference for continuous outcomes; risk ratio for dichotomous outcomes such as implant survival.
- **STROBE**: reporting guideline for observational studies, used here (unconventionally) as the quality appraisal framework.
