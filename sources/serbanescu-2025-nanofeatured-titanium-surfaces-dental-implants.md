## Why Ingested
Systematic evaluation (PRISMA 2020, 25 studies, GRADE-appraised) of whether nanofeatured/nanocoated titanium surfaces beat contemporary microrough (e.g., SLA) controls on osseointegration — directly nuances the wiki's nanoscale/hierarchical-roughness narrative in [[overviews/implant-surface-comparison]] §4 and [[implants/surface/hasegawa-2020-meso-micro-nano-rough-titanium-surface]]: early gains are consistently reproduced, but the SR finds no clinical evidence of durable advantage over microrough titanium once healing consolidates (convergence by 3–6 months, GRADE certainty low for patient-important outcomes).

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review (PRISMA 2020, no meta-analysis; 25 studies: 4 human clinical [2 RCT, 2 non-randomized], 10 in vivo animal, 1 in vitro, 10 combined in vitro/in vivo) comparing nanocoated/nanostructured titanium implant surfaces (TiO2 nanotubes, HA/Ca-P, Ag, Sr, Zn/Mg, graphene/nanospikes) against conventional microrough (chiefly SLA/machined) titanium controls.
21/25 studies favored the nanosurface on ≥1 osseointegration metric (e.g., Ko et al. 2024 RCT: ISQ ~76.5 vs 71.3 at 2 months, ~79.1 vs 73.4 at 4 months, both p<0.01/p<0.0001; Li et al. 2023 dog model: BIC 76.6% vs 44.1% at 12 weeks for Sr-loaded vs plain TiO2-nanotube); gains are largest at 2–4 weeks and commonly converge with microrough controls by 8–12 weeks; GRADE certainty for patient-important clinical outcomes (MBL, survival) is low to very low.
Nanofeatures accelerate early osseointegration and can add antibacterial activity without cytotoxicity, but no consistent long-term (≥12-month) survival/marginal-bone-loss advantage over contemporary microrough titanium is demonstrated — the authors call for adequately powered, preregistered ≥3–5-year head-to-head RCTs.

## 세줄요약
체계적 문헌고찰(PRISMA 2020, 메타분석 미시행; 연구 25편: 인체임상 4편[RCT 2, 비무작위 2], 동물생체내 10편, 시험관내 1편, 병합 10편) — 나노코팅/나노구조화 티타늄 표면(TiO2 나노튜브, HA/인산칼슘, 은(Ag), 스트론튬(Sr), 아연/마그네슘(Zn/Mg), 그래핀/나노스파이크)을 기존 마이크로거칠기(주로 SLA/기계가공) 티타늄 대조군과 비교.
25편 중 21편이 최소 1개 골유착 (Osseointegration) 지표에서 나노 표면 우위(예: Ko 등 2024 RCT — 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 2개월 ~76.5 vs 71.3, 4개월 ~79.1 vs 73.4, 모두 p<0.01/p<0.0001; Li 등 2023 개 모델 — 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 12주 76.6% vs 44.1%[Sr첨가 vs 순수 TiO2나노튜브]); 격차는 2–4주에 최대, 8–12주면 대조군과 수렴하는 경우多; 환자-중요 임상결과(변연골소실, 생존율) 대상 GRADE 확신도는 낮음~매우낮음.
나노 표면은 초기 골유착을 가속하고 세포독성 없이 항균 효과를 더할 수 있으나, 현대 마이크로거칠기 티타늄 대비 장기(≥12개월) 생존율·변연골소실 우위는 입증되지 않음 — 저자들은 사전등록된 ≥3–5년 head-to-head RCT가 필요하다고 결론.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Antibiotics (Basel) 2025;14(12)
- **DOI**: 10.3390/antibiotics14121191
- **Institution**: multi-institutional Romanian group (Carol Davila University of Medicine and Pharmacy, Bucharest and collaborators)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First systematic evaluation spanning clinical + in vivo animal + in vitro nanofeatured-titanium-vs-microrough-control osseointegration literature with a unified risk-of-bias battery (RoB2/ROBINS-I/RoBDEMAT/OHAT/SYRCLE + CAMARADES/CRIME-Q/MIRIBEL/ARRIVE 2.0 reporting checklists)
- GRADE-appraised human clinical outcomes: certainty low (MBL, soft-tissue indices) to very low (infection, survival) — insufficient to claim clinical superiority of nanocoated over uncoated implants
- Narrative synthesis by coating class (TiO2 nanotubes, HA/Ca-P, Ag, Sr, Zn/Mg, graphene/nanospikes, inert UNCD) shows early-phase acceleration is the consistent signal, not durable superiority
- Identifies a time-course pattern: nano-vs-control differences peak at 2–4 weeks and narrow/converge by 8–12 weeks in most (not all) models

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review, PRISMA 2020-compliant; no meta-analysis (methodologic heterogeneity across designs, outcome definitions/timepoints, missing variance data, measurement platforms, and surface characterization)
- **Databases**: PubMed-Medline, Scopus, Web of Science (Jan 2016–Aug 2025), plus supplementary Elicit/Semantic Scholar search
- **n**: 1171 records → 924 screened (title/abstract) → 357 full-text assessed → 25 included (4 human clinical [2 RCT/2 non-randomized], 10 in vivo animal, 1 in vitro, 10 combined in vitro/in vivo)
- **Outcomes**: bone-to-implant contact (BIC, 17/25 studies), bone density/volume (~10 studies), removal/push-out torque (4 studies), ISQ (clinical subset), osteogenic gene expression/ALP (in vitro), antimicrobial/biofilm assays, cytotoxicity
- **RoB tools**: Cochrane RoB 2 (RCTs, "some concerns"), ROBINS-I (non-randomized, moderate–serious), RoBDEMAT (in vitro), OHAT Tier 1–3 (animal; 0 Tier 1, 4 Tier 2, 13 Tier 3) with SYRCLE sensitivity check; CAMARADES/CRIME-Q/MIRIBEL/ARRIVE 2.0 reporting-quality checklists (descriptive, not weighted)
- **Certainty**: GRADE applied to human comparative studies only; animal/in vitro evidence treated as biological plausibility, not graded

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 21/25 included studies reported nanocoated implants better on ≥1 osseointegration metric; 2 studies unequivocally null (Scarano 2020 silver-ion TiO2, BIC/bone density n.s.; Hall 2019 anodized abutment, no stability/bone advantage); 2 mixed/partial (Chappuis 2018 minipig — early gain, BIC equalized by 8 weeks; Ko 2024 human RCT — ISQ gain, similar bone remodeling)
- Ko et al. 2024 (RCT, HA-nanocoated SLA): ISQ ~76.5 vs 71.3 at 2 months (p<0.01), ~79.1 vs 73.4 at 4 months (p<0.0001); similar radiographic bone levels between arms
- Memon et al. 2025 (clinical, AgNP coating): BIC and bone density significantly higher (p=0.013 / p=0.012, 0.006); IL-6/CRP significantly lower (p=0.015); in vitro P. aeruginosa adhesion reduced (p=0.009)
- Li et al. 2023 (dog, Sr-loaded nanotextured vs TiO2-nanotube control): BIC 76.6% vs 44.1% at 12 weeks; bone volume fraction 54.9%±9.97 vs 28.8%±5.9
- Rousseau et al. 2021 (sheep, micro/nanofeatured): BIC 79.3%/86.4% at 4/13 weeks vs 68.3%/74.8% control — persistent advantage, one of the few models that did not converge
- Toscano et al. 2024 (osteoporotic rat, HA nanoparticle "ZiHa"): reverse torque 4.75±2.22 vs 1.5±0.58 N·cm (p=0.03), ~3-fold increase
- Yu et al. 2017 (rabbit, Zn/Mg co-implanted Ti): highest peak push-out force at 12 weeks vs Zn-only/Mg-only/pure Ti (p<0.05); ~40–50% reduction in P. gingivalis/F. nucleatum/S. mutans colony counts
- Gil & Sanz 2025 (nanospike topography): BIC 54% vs 41%; 70–90% bactericidal efficiency against a 4-strain oral panel, without sacrificing osseointegration
- GRADE: MBL/soft-tissue-index certainty low (~75 total trial participants, below optimal information size for MID ≈0.2–0.3 mm at SD ≈0.4–0.6 mm); infection/survival certainty very low (rare events, short follow-up, only 2 trials)
- No cytotoxicity, genotoxicity, or systemic-toxicity signal reported across any of the 25 studies

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No meta-analysis performed — narrative synthesis only, due to design/outcome/timepoint heterogeneity and inconsistent variance reporting
- Most animal studies OHAT Tier 2–3 (none Tier 1) — randomization/allocation-concealment/blinding frequently unreported, risking inflated histomorphometric gains
- Clinical evidence base is small (4 studies total, ~75 participants across RCTs) and short-follow-up (up to ~4–6 months); no study followed patients beyond 1 year
- Cross-nanocoating comparisons are indirect (no head-to-head trials between different nanocoating types within the review)
- Authors call for preregistered, adequately powered ≥3–5-year RCTs against a single contemporary microrough benchmark, with harmonized timepoints (BIC/ISQ at 2, 4, ≥12 weeks; MBL at 6 and 12 months) and standardized multiscale surface characterization (topography, chemistry, wettability, roughness, ion release)

## 6. Related Work
- hasegawa-2020 (meso-micro-nano hierarchical titanium surface): this SR's early-gains-but-uncertain-long-term-durability pattern is a broader, evidence-synthesis-level qualifier on the kind of single-study hierarchical/nanoscale-roughness enthusiasm hasegawa-2020 represents
- ballo-2011, gaviria-2014 (implant surface physicochemical/biological reviews): general surface-science background this SR narrows into a focused nanofeature-vs-microrough osseointegration comparison

## 7. Glossary
- **BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact)**: histomorphometric percentage of implant surface directly apposed to bone, the primary osseointegration outcome in this review
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: unitless 1–100 resonance-frequency-analysis (RFA) measure of implant stability used longitudinally during healing
- **OHAT Tier**: Office of Health Assessment and Translation risk-of-bias-derived confidence tier (1–3) applied to animal studies, rolling up domain judgments
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development, and Evaluation — framework for rating certainty of evidence for patient-important outcomes
- **TiO2 nanotubes**: ordered nanoporous titanium dioxide structures created by anodization, used as a surface-modification and drug/ion-delivery platform
- **CAMARADES / CRIME-Q / MIRIBEL / ARRIVE 2.0**: reporting-quality checklists for animal/preclinical experimental literature (used descriptively, not for weighting evidence)
