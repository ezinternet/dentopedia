## Why Ingested

Directly relevant to zirconia surface treatment protocols for veneering ceramics. Quantifies tetragonal-to-monoclinic (t→m) phase transformation induced by different grinding and polishing regimens in 3Y-TZP using both Bragg-Brentano and grazing incidence XRD, revealing a subsurface concentration gradient. Also measures surface free energy (SFE) components critical for ceramic-zirconia bonding.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/regulska-2021-analysis-of-the-surface-condition]]

## Three-line Summary

Mechanical surface treatment (grinding and polishing) of 3Y-TZP zirconia induces tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation detectable by XRD, with polishing producing less monoclinic phase than grinding. Neither grit size (120 vs. 500) nor wet/dry cooling significantly affected the degree of transformation, but grazing incidence XRD revealed a subsurface monoclinic concentration gradient. Surface free energy (total 37–43 mJ/m²) showed no significant inter-group differences, with the apolar component dominating, suggesting that bonding of veneering ceramics relies primarily on apolar adhesion during firing.

## 세줄요약

3Y-TZP 지르코니아의 기계적 표면 처리(연삭·연마)는 XRD로 확인되는 정방정→단사정 상전이(t→m 전이, Tetragonal-to-Monoclinic Phase Transformation)를 유발하며, 연마군에서 연삭군보다 단사정상 함량이 낮다. 입자 크기(120 vs. 500) 및 습식/건식 냉각 여부는 전이 정도에 유의미한 차이를 주지 않았으나, 경사입사 X선 회절법(Grazing Incidence XRD)으로 표면에서 심부로 갈수록 단사정상 농도 구배가 존재함을 확인했다. 표면 자유 에너지(Surface Free Energy, SFE) 총값(37–43 mJ/m²)은 군 간 유의차 없었으며, 비극성 성분이 극성 성분보다 높아 소성 과정에서 세라믹과의 결합이 비극성 접착에 의존함을 시사한다.

## Document Information

- **Study type**: In vitro laboratory study
- **Sample**: 15 cylindrical 3Y-TZP samples (Ceramill Zi, Amann Girrbach; sintered 1450 °C/2 h)
- **Groups**:
  - A: Coarse grinding, dry (grit 120)
  - B: Coarse grinding, wet (grit 120)
  - C: Fine grinding, dry (grit 500)
  - D: Fine grinding, wet (grit 500)
  - E: Polishing
  - F: Milled reference (no surface treatment)
- **Instruments**: PANalytical Empyrean XRD (Co anode, λ=1.79 Å); JEOL JSM-6610LV SEM-EDS; contact angle goniometry

## Key Contributions

1. Demonstrates that polishing produces less t→m transformation than grinding, while wet vs. dry cooling and grit size (120 vs. 500) have no significant effect.
2. Uses grazing incidence (GI) XRD alongside Bragg-Brentano to reveal a **subsurface monoclinic concentration gradient** — the phase front is confined to near-surface layers, deeper with coarser grits.
3. Provides SFE component data (polar: 8–11 mJ/m², apolar: 27–36 mJ/m², total: 37–43 mJ/m²) across all treatment types — no significant inter-group differences found.
4. Confirms that the apolar (dispersive) component dominates SFE, explaining why veneering ceramics bond better during high-temperature firing (apolar liquid conditions) than at room temperature.

## Methodology

- XRD performed in both **Bragg-Brentano (θ-θ) geometry** and **grazing incidence geometry** (5° incident angle) to distinguish bulk vs. surface-layer phase content.
- Rietveld method used for quantitative phase analysis via High Score Plus software + ICDD PDF4+ database.
- Penetration depth calculated: Bragg-Brentano averages the whole illuminated volume; GI geometry at 5° probes near-surface layers specifically.
- SFE calculated by Owens-Wendt model using contact angles of water (polar) and diiodomethane (apolar), 3 µL drops.
- SEM-EDS (20 kV) for surface topography and chemical composition verification.

## Key Results

| Group | Monoclinic % (B-B scan) | Monoclinic % (GI scan) |
|---|---|---|
| F (milled reference) | ~0% | ~0% |
| A (coarse dry, 120) | moderate | higher |
| B (coarse wet, 120) | moderate | higher |
| C (fine dry, 500) | moderate | higher |
| D (fine wet, 500) | moderate | higher |
| E (polished) | lowest among treated | lower than grinding |

- **GI consistently higher than B-B** in all treated samples → monoclinic concentrated at surface, gradient toward core.
- **Contact angles**: water 67–79°, diiodomethane 47–61° (all θ < 90° → wettable surfaces).
- **SFE**: polar 8–11 mJ/m², apolar 27–36 mJ/m², total 37–43 mJ/m². No significant differences across groups.
- **SEM**: Coarser grits → more developed surface with deeper fissures; water cooling → less surface damage vs. dry.
- **EDS**: Chemical composition unchanged across groups (typical YSZ values).

## Limitations

- Small sample size (n=15 total, n=3 per group) limits statistical power.
- No flexural strength testing — SFE and XRD data alone cannot predict bond failure.
- No sandblasting group included, limiting comparison to the most clinically common pre-veneering protocol.
- No aging/thermocycling data to assess long-term monoclinic stability.
- Grazing incidence depth resolution limited by the fixed 5° incidence angle; variable-angle studies would better map the gradient profile.

## Glossary

- **t→m 전이 (Tetragonal-to-Monoclinic Phase Transformation)**: 정방정→단사정 상전이; 체적 팽창 약 4%, 균열 전파 억제에 유리하나 과도하면 계면 응력 발생
- **XRD (X-ray Diffraction, X선 회절법)**: 결정상 정성·정량 분석법
- **Grazing Incidence XRD (경사입사 X선 회절법)**: 낮은 입사각(여기 5°)으로 표면층만 선택적 분석
- **Bragg-Brentano geometry**: 대칭 θ-θ 주사 — 조사 체적 전체의 평균 위상 함량 제공
- **SFE (Surface Free Energy, 표면 자유 에너지)**: Owens-Wendt 모델로 극성·비극성 성분 분리 계산
- **Owens-Wendt model**: 두 가지 액체(극성+비극성)의 접촉각으로 SFE 성분을 분리하는 모델
- **3Y-TZP**: 3 mol% 이트리아 안정화 정방정 지르코니아
