## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of controlled trials of deep dry needling (DDN) for chronic masticatory myofascial pain (MMP), paired with a preliminary clinical trial in five consecutive MMP patients (Cranio 2019; published 2019, outside the 5-year surveillance window). Abstract-only.

Of 25 studies screened by title/abstract, only 2 met the inclusion criteria (used for the sample-size calculation); in the five-patient trial, DDN raised the pressure pain threshold (PPT) at myofascial trigger points by 44.6% versus -5.5% with sham (p = 0.04, Wilcoxon).

Suggestive but very low-certainty evidence: the controlled-trial base is only 2 studies and the own trial has n = 5, so it supports a hypothesis of PPT benefit, not a treatment recommendation.

## 세줄요약

저작근 근막통 (Masticatory Myofascial Pain, MMP)에 대한 심부 건침술 (Deep Dry Needling, DDN)의 대조임상시험을 체계적으로 고찰하고, 연속 내원한 MMP 환자 5명의 예비 임상시험을 더한 연구 (Cranio 2019, 2019년 발표로 5년 서베일런스 창 밖; 초록만 확보).

제목·초록 단계 25편 중 포함기준 충족은 2편뿐이었고, 5명 예비시험에서 DDN은 근막 통점 (Myofascial Trigger Point, MTP)의 압통역치 (Pressure Pain Threshold, PPT)를 44.6% 높였다 (가짜 시술 -5.5%; p = 0.04, Wilcoxon).

시사적이지만 근거 확실성은 매우 낮다: 대조시험이 2편, 자체 시험이 n = 5라 PPT 개선 가설을 지지할 뿐 치료 권고의 근거로는 부족하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reviews controlled trials of deep dry needling (DDN) for chronic masticatory myofascial pain (MMP).
- Uses the 2 eligible trials to compute the sample size for its own preliminary trial.
- Reports the local pressure pain threshold (PPT) at myofascial trigger points (MTPs) as the outcome.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Systematic review: 25 studies considered on title/abstract; 2 met inclusion criteria. Keywords indicate SciELO and LILACS searching (regional databases); the abstract does not state the full search strategy, registration, or risk-of-bias tool.
- Preliminary clinical trial: five consecutive MMP patients; mean of three consecutive PPT measurements at MTPs; percentage change compared between experimental side (DDN) and sham; Wilcoxon test.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- DDN increased PPT at MTPs by 44.6% versus -5.5% with sham (p = 0.04).
- Authors conclude patients with chronic MMP treated with DDN of MTPs showed increased PPT on the experimental side.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only; full text not retrieved, so design details (blinding, side allocation, DDN protocol, follow-up) are unknown.
- Only 2 of 25 screened studies were eligible; n = 5 in the own trial; a Wilcoxon p = 0.04 at n = 5 is fragile.
- No pooled estimate or certainty grading in the abstract; PPT is a surrogate, not a patient-reported pain outcome.
