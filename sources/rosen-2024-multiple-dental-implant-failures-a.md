## Why Ingested

Early and repeated implant failure is a core theme of the wiki; this Vienna referral-center cohort restricts inclusion to patients who already lost two or more implants and reports retention time plus smoking, age, implant length and surgical-protocol risk factors, adding a "multiple failure" patient phenotype to the early-failure literature. → [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]] · [[implants/survival/fan-2024-smoking-early-implant-failure-sr-ma]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center study (Academy of Oral Implantology, Vienna, Austria; 2004-2022) of 371 patients (median age 63 years) who lost 2 or more of at least 2 placed implants: 3,141 implants, of which 1,090 failed, with a median retention time of 108.11 months (about 9 years).

Shorter retention was associated with nicotine use (P < 0.001), age < 50 and > 70 years (P < 0.001), maxillary location (P = 0.05), transgingival healing (P < 0.001), no provisional restoration (P = 0.035) and short implants (P < 0.001); patients with multiple failures showed cluster behaviour.

Abstract-only: effect sizes (HR/OR), the time window defining early failure and the multivariate model are not reported in the retrieved text, so the findings are hypothesis-generating for a highly selected multiple-failure population.

## 세줄요약

오스트리아 빈 구강임플란트학회 (Academy of Oral Implantology)의 2004-2022년 후향적 단일기관 연구: 임플란트 2개 이상 식립 후 2개 이상 실패한 환자 371명 (중앙 연령 63세), 임플란트 3,141개 중 1,090개 실패, 중앙 유지기간 108.11개월 (약 9년).

유지기간 단축과 연관된 요인은 니코틴 사용 (P < 0.001), 50세 미만 및 70세 초과 (P < 0.001), 상악 위치 (P = 0.05), 경점막 치유 (transgingival healing, P < 0.001), 임시 보철 없음 (P = 0.035), 짧은 임플란트 (P < 0.001); 다중 실패 환자는 군집 (cluster) 양상을 보였다.

초록 기반이라 효과 크기 (HR/OR), 조기 실패의 시간 기준, 다변량 모형은 확인되지 않으며, 선택된 다중 실패 집단에 대한 가설 생성 수준의 결과이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Cohort restricted to patients with multiple failures (at least 2 failed implants of at least 2 placed), a population rarely isolated in survival studies.
- Reports implant retention time (median 108.11 months) rather than a simple survival percentage.
- Evaluates patient-, implant-, anatomical- and surgical-level factors together (age, sex, medical history, medication, smoking, alcohol, implant properties, anatomy, surgical protocol).
- Describes cluster behaviour of failures within patients.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective study; descriptive plus univariate and multivariate analysis; significance level 0.05.
- **Setting**: Academy of Oral Implantology, Vienna, Austria; patients treated 2004-2022.
- **Population**: patients with placement of at least two implants and failure of two or more; 371 patients (178 men, 193 women, median age 63 years), equal to 3.14% of all patients treated since 2004.
- **Sample**: 3,141 implants; 1,090 failures, stated as 59.01% of all failed implants at the institution.
- **Early failure definition**: not stated in the abstract (no time window available).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Patients / implants | 371 / 3,141 |
| Failed implants | 1,090 |
| Median retention time | 108.11 months |
| Nicotine use | shorter retention, P < 0.001 |
| Age < 50 and > 70 years | shorter retention, P < 0.001 |
| Maxillary location | shorter retention, P = 0.05 |
| Transgingival healing | shorter retention, P < 0.001 |
| No provisional restoration | shorter retention, P = 0.035 |
| Short implant length | shorter retention, P < 0.001 |
| Periodontitis-related tooth loss | no tendency to early failure (reported P > 0.001); longer retention per conclusion |

- Conclusion statement: smoking, short implant length, single-stage surgery and immediate loading were associated with higher failure risk; age 50-70 years and tooth loss due to periodontitis were associated with longer retention time.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only: no effect sizes, confidence intervals, group sizes, multivariate model terms or early-failure time window are available.
- Retrospective, single referral center; the cohort is selected for repeated failure (3.14% of patients), so results cannot be generalized to routine patients and there is no comparison group of non-failing patients.
- Failures cluster within patients, so implant-level P values may overstate independence unless clustering was modeled (not verifiable here).
- Internal inconsistencies in the abstract: periodontitis P reported as "P > 0.001" (likely a typographical error); "single-stage surgery" and "immediate loading" appear as risk factors in the conclusion but not in the results list, while "transgingival healing" and "no provisional restoration" appear only in the results list; the cohort is defined by at least 2 failures yet 1,090 failures across 371 patients implies about 2.9 failed implants per patient.

## 7. Glossary

- **Retention time (유지기간)**: time an implant remains in situ before removal.
- **Transgingival healing (경점막 치유)**: non-submerged healing with the implant exposed through the mucosa.
- **Cluster behaviour (군집 양상)**: failures concentrated within the same patients.
- **Provisional restoration (임시 보철)**: temporary prosthesis placed during healing.
