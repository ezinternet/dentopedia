## Why Ingested
5년 추적 RCT로 bone-level 임플란트에서 매몰형 vs 비매몰형(transmucosal) 치유 프로토콜을 직접 비교. [[implants/mbl/aal-2025-marginal-bone-loss-single]] 및 [[implants/survival/troiano-2018-early-late-failure-submerged]]의 근거 기반을 장기 단일치아 보철 맥락에서 보강.

## One-line Summary
5-year RCT (n=40 enrolled, 30 completed) comparing submerged vs transmucosal bone-level implants for single-tooth replacement found 100% survival in both groups and no significant differences in crestal bone levels, soft tissue, or patient-reported outcomes.

## 한줄요약
5년 RCT (n=30 완료) — bone-level 단일치아 임플란트에서 매몰형 vs 비매몰형 치유 프로토콜 간 생존율(양군 100%)·변연골·연조직·환자 만족도 모두 유의차 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 5-year RCT evidence that bone-level implants with platform switching and conical IAC produce equivalent outcomes regardless of submerged vs transmucosal healing
- MBL from baseline to 5 years: submerged 0.59 (0.92) mm vs transmucosal 0.78 (1.03) mm — numerically transmucosal higher but statistically NS
- 100% implant survival rate in both groups through 5 years — no protocol-specific failure signal
- Only one peri-implantitis case (one patient), not protocol-specific
- Patient satisfaction high in both groups

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Subset analysis of a multicentre randomized clinical trial (single-blind)
- n enrolled: 40 patients; n completing 5 years: 30
- Implant: Single bone-level implant, platform switching, conical IAC, non-molar sites
- Groups: Submerged (cover screw) vs transmucosal (healing abutment) healing protocol
- Follow-up: Implant placement to 5 years
- Outcomes: Crestal bone level change (radiographic), clinical parameters, patient-reported outcomes
- Statistics: Not extractable from abstract

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Implant survival: 100% both groups
- MBL baseline → 5yr: Submerged 0.59 ± 0.92 mm; Transmucosal 0.78 ± 1.03 mm (NS)
- No significant differences in any investigated variable between groups
- Peri-implantitis (≥2 mm bone change + BOP): only 1 patient
- Patient satisfaction: high in both groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- n=30 completers — underpowered for detecting small between-group differences
- Subset analysis of a multicenter RCT — potential selection bias
- Non-molar sites only — cannot generalize to molar sites with higher occlusal load
- Single-blind design
- Abstract only (full text not retrieved via PMC)

## 6. Related Work
- Troiano 2018 SR+MA: pooled early-failure penalty +2% on non-submerged; this RCT shows 100% survival (no failures) — consistent with "no late-failure difference"
- Moustafa Ali 2018 SR+MA: MBL favors submerged by 0.12 mm; this paper shows 0.19 mm transmucosal-higher gap — directionally consistent, numerically similar
- Aal 2025 RCT: non-submerged mandibular SIRMO 0.27 vs submerged 0.43 mm MBL — same direction reversed (S worse in SIRMO context)
- Verma 2024 RCT: short-term (3-month) MBL favoring non-submerged

## 7. Glossary
- **IAC**: Implant-abutment connection (conical = Morse taper-style)
- **Platform switching**: Implant wider than abutment connection, preserves crestal bone
- **BOP**: Bleeding on probing
- **Peri-implantitis**: ≥2 mm crestal bone change + BOP (Flores-Guillen definition)
