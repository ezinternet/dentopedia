## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (ADA Council on Scientific Affairs expert panel; PubMed and Embase through July 2014) of randomized controlled trials of scaling and root planing (SRP) with or without adjuncts in chronic periodontitis, with clinical attachment level (CAL) outcomes at 6 months or longer; 72 articles included (abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

SRP alone gave about 0.5 mm average CAL improvement (moderate certainty); adding adjuncts gave an average CAL improvement of 0.2 to 0.6 mm over SRP alone, with moderate-certainty benefit for subantimicrobial-dose doxycycline, systemic antimicrobials, chlorhexidine chips and photodynamic therapy with a diode laser, and low certainty for the other adjuncts, including minocycline microspheres and doxycycline hyclate gel.

Adjunct effects are small (sub-millimeter) and mostly low-certainty, so SRP remains the core treatment; locally delivered minocycline was judged low certainty at the time (2014 search), and this abstract-level summary carries no per-adjunct numbers.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (미국치과의사협회 과학위원회 전문가 패널; PubMed·Embase, 2014년 7월까지): 만성 치주염에서 치석제거술 및 치근활택술 (Scaling and Root Planing, SRP) 단독 또는 보조요법 병용의 6개월 이상 임상 부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 결과를 보고한 무작위 대조 시험 72편 포함 (초록 기반 — 전문 미확보).

SRP 단독으로 평균 약 0.5 mm의 CAL 개선(중등도 확실성); 보조요법 병용 시 SRP 단독 대비 평균 0.2–0.6 mm 추가 개선이며, 저용량 독시사이클린 (Subantimicrobial-dose Doxycycline), 전신 항균제, 클로르헥시딘 칩 (Chlorhexidine chip), 다이오드 레이저 광역동요법 (Photodynamic Therapy, PDT)은 중등도 확실성의 이득, 미노사이클린 마이크로스피어 (Minocycline microspheres)·독시사이클린 하이클레이트 겔 등 나머지는 낮은 확실성.

보조요법 효과는 1 mm 미만으로 작고 대부분 낮은 확실성이므로 SRP가 핵심 치료이며, 국소 미노사이클린은 당시(2014년 검색) 낮은 확실성으로 판정되었다. 초록 수준 요약이라 보조요법별 수치는 없다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: J Am Dent Assoc 2015 Jul;146(7):508-24.e5
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.adaj.2015.01.028
- **PMID**: 26113099
- **Institution**: American Dental Association Council on Scientific Affairs expert panel (USA)
- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (abstract only — full text not retrieved)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled SRP effect: about 0.5 mm average CAL improvement (moderate certainty).
- Adjuncts added 0.2 to 0.6 mm CAL over SRP alone across the range of adjunct combinations.
- Four adjuncts judged beneficial with moderate certainty: systemic subantimicrobial-dose doxycycline, systemic antimicrobials, chlorhexidine chips, photodynamic therapy with a diode laser; the others (incl. doxycycline hyclate gel, minocycline microspheres, diode, Nd:YAG and Er lasers) low certainty.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: SR + meta-analysis by an ADA Council on Scientific Affairs expert panel.
- **Databases**: PubMed (MEDLINE) and Embase; English-language RCTs through July 2014.
- **Eligibility**: RCTs of SRP with or without adjuncts, CAL outcome, at least 6 months duration.
- **n**: 72 articles.
- **Appraisal**: Cochrane Risk of Bias Tool; funnel plots and Egger tests when more than 10 studies; modified US Preventive Services Task Force method for overall certainty.
- **Adjuncts covered**: systemic antimicrobials; subantimicrobial-dose doxycycline; locally delivered chlorhexidine chips, doxycycline hyclate gel, minocycline microspheres; photodynamic therapy with diode laser, diode laser, Nd:YAG, Er lasers.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Abstract-level only (full text not retrieved): SRP about 0.5 mm average CAL improvement (moderate certainty); SRP plus adjuncts 0.2 to 0.6 mm average CAL improvement over SRP alone; 4 adjuncts moderate-certainty benefit, the rest low certainty. Per-adjunct pooled estimates, heterogeneity and publication-bias results are not available from the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only the abstract was read; no per-adjunct estimates, I-squared, or risk-of-bias results available.
- Search ended July 2014 and was English-only; later trials of locally delivered minocycline are not included (see [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/soysa-2025-the-long-term-efficacy-of]]).
- The panel's clinical recommendations are in the associated guideline, not this paper.

## 7. Glossary

- **SRP**: scaling and root planing, mechanical subgingival debridement.
- **CAL**: clinical attachment level, periodontal attachment measure.
- **Subantimicrobial-dose doxycycline**: low-dose systemic doxycycline acting as a host modulator rather than an antibiotic.
- **Minocycline microspheres**: locally delivered sustained-release minocycline placed in the pocket after SRP.
