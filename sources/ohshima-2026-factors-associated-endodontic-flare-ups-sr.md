## Why Ingested

No existing wiki page synthesizes risk factors for endodontic flare-ups with pooled relative risk estimates. This SR/MA (n = 24,320 cases, 15 studies) provides GRADE-evaluated evidence on which clinical factors meaningfully elevate flare-up risk — directly applicable to pre-treatment patient counselling and case risk stratification. The intracanal medicaments page ([[ordinola-zapata-2022-intracanal-medicaments-status-directions]]) is tangentially related via inter-appointment management, but no dedicated flare-up risk page exists.

## Three-line Summary

A systematic review and meta-analysis of 15 studies (24,320 cases) found an overall severe endodontic flare-up (급성 악화, Flare-Up) incidence of 2.83%. Preoperative spontaneous pain (RR = 5.83) and percussion pain (RR = 3.45) were the strongest independent predictors; female sex, mandibular location, non-vital pulp, periapical lesion, multiple-visit treatment, and re-treatment were also significant. Age, tooth type, canal number, instrumentation technique, and preoperative analgesic use were not significantly associated.

## 세줄요약

15편 연구(24,320증례)의 체계적 고찰 및 메타분석(Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR/MA) 결과 근관치료 후 심한 급성 악화(Endodontic Flare-Up)의 전체 발생률은 2.83%였다. 술전 자발통(Spontaneous Pain, 비교위험도 Relative Risk, RR = 5.83)과 타진통(Percussion Pain, RR = 3.45)이 가장 강력한 독립 예측 인자였으며, 여성, 하악 위치, 비생활치(Non-vital Pulp), 치근단 병소(Periapical Lesion), 다회방문 치료, 재치료도 유의미한 위험인자였다. 연령, 치아 유형, 근관 수, 기구조작 기법, 술전 진통제 사용은 유의미한 관련성이 없었다.

## Key Contributions

1. **Pooled incidence**: Overall severe flare-up rate of 2.83% across 24,320 cases — provides a clinically grounded baseline for informed consent.
2. **Quantified risk hierarchy**: Spontaneous pain (RR = 5.83) > percussion pain (RR = 3.45) > non-vital pulp, periapical lesion, re-treatment, multiple-visit, mandibular location, female sex.
3. **Null findings clarified**: Age, tooth type, canal count, instrumentation technique, and preoperative analgesics were not significant — challenges some clinical assumptions.
4. **GRADE certainty**: Overall evidence certainty rated low, flagging the need for prospective study designs.

---

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis
- **Search**: PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus from inception to September 2025
- **Eligible study types**: Observational and interventional studies evaluating risk factors for flare-up after root canal treatment
- **Definition of flare-up**: Severe postoperative pain and/or swelling requiring unscheduled intervention
- **Risk of bias tools**: ROBINS-I (non-randomized); Cochrane RoB 2 (RCTs)
- **Certainty of evidence**: GRADE approach
- **Included studies**: 15 studies, 24,320 cases

## Limitations

- Overall certainty of evidence is low (GRADE), driven by study heterogeneity and non-randomized designs
- Further well-designed prospective studies needed to strengthen the evidence base
- Abstract-only ingest; effect sizes for non-spontaneous-pain factors not available from abstract

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Endodontic flare-up | Severe postoperative pain and/or swelling after root canal treatment requiring unscheduled intervention |
| RR (Relative Risk) | Ratio of flare-up probability in exposed vs unexposed group |
| GRADE | Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation — certainty-of-evidence framework |
| ROBINS-I | Risk Of Bias In Non-randomized Studies of Interventions |
| RoB 2 | Cochrane Revised risk of bias tool for randomized trials |
| Re-treatment | Secondary root canal treatment of a previously treated tooth |
