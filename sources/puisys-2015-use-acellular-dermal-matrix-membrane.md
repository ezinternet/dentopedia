## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/bassetti-2016-soft-tissue-augmentation-second-stage-sr]]의 이차수술(second-stage surgery) 시점 연조직 증대술과 [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/tommasato-2024-autogenous-graft-versus-collagen-matrices]]의 무세포 진피 기질 (Acellular Dermal Matrix, ADM) 두께 증가 근거를, 얇은 수직 연조직(≤2 mm)에서 AlloDerm을 매몰형 식립 시 적용한 1차 증례시리즈(40명)로 보강하고 수직 두께라는 측정 축으로 확장하기 위해 인제스트.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective case series of 40 patients (42 implants) with thin vertical soft tissue (≤2 mm), augmented with acellular dermal matrix (AlloDerm) membranes during submerged implant placement and re-evaluated at 3-month second-stage surgery.
Mean vertical soft tissue thickness increased from 1.54 ± 0.51 mm to 3.75 ± 0.54 mm — a mean gain of 2.21 ± 0.85 mm (P < 0.001); all 40 allografts healed successfully.
ADM appears effective for vertical soft-tissue thickening in the submerged-placement setting, but the uncontrolled single-arm design and abstract-only record limit the strength of inference.

## 세줄요약

수직 연조직 두께 2 mm 이하인 환자 40명(임플란트 42개)에서 매몰형(submerged) 식립 시 무세포 진피 기질 (Acellular Dermal Matrix, ADM) 막(AlloDerm)으로 증대술을 시행하고 3개월 후 이차수술(second-stage surgery)에서 평가한 전향적 증례시리즈.

평균 수직 연조직 두께가 1.54 ± 0.51 mm에서 3.75 ± 0.54 mm로 증가해 평균 2.21 ± 0.85 mm의 두께 증가를 보였고(P < 0.001), 40개 동종이식편(allograft) 모두 성공적으로 치유됨.

매몰형 식립 시 ADM의 수직 두께 증대 효과를 시사하나, 대조군 없는 단일군 설계와 초록 전용 기록 때문에 인과 추론의 강도는 제한적임.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Evaluated acellular dermal matrix (ADM) specifically for **vertical** soft-tissue thickness gain during **submerged (two-stage) implant placement** — a timing/goal combination distinct from keratinized-mucosa-width augmentation at uncovering.
- Prospective case series with an explicit thin-phenotype entry criterion (vertical soft tissue thickness ≤2 mm) and a homogeneous implant system (BioHorizons Tapered Laser Lok, internal hex, laser-modified surface).
- Used a simple reproducible clinical endpoint — augmented soft-tissue thickness measured with a periodontal probe at abutment connection — and reported both pre/post absolute values and the derived gain.
- Reported 100% graft healing (40/40 allografts) at the 3-month second-stage evaluation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective, single-arm case series (no control group).
- **Setting**: Submerged implant placement with simultaneous ADM membrane augmentation; healing abutments connected at 3 months.
- **n**: 40 patients (15 males, 25 females; mean age 42.5 ± 1.7 years); 40 ADM-derived allogenic membranes (AlloDerm, BioHorizons, Birmingham, AL, USA); 42 laser-modified surface internal hex implants (BioHorizons Tapered Laser Lok, Birmingham, AL, USA).
- **Eligibility**: Thin vertical soft tissue thickness of 2 mm or less.
- **Outcome**: Vertical soft-tissue thickness measured with a periodontal probe after abutment connection at 3 months; gain in vertical soft-tissue volume calculated.
- **Statistics**: Mann-Whitney U-test; significance set at 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Result (abstract verbatim) |
|---|---|
| Allograft healing | All 40 allografts healed successfully |
| Thickness before augmentation | 1.54 ± 0.51 mm SD (range 0.5–2.0 mm; median 1.75 mm) |
| Thickness at 3 months post-placement | 3.75 ± 0.54 mm SD (range 3.0–5.0 mm; median 4.0 mm) |
| Difference between medians | Statistically significant, P < 0.001 (Mann-Whitney U-test) |
| Mean increase in soft tissue thickness | 2.21 ± 0.85 mm SD (range 1.0–4.5 mm; median 2.0 mm) |

Stated conclusion: "acellular dermal matrix membrane can be successfully used for vertical soft tissue augmentation."

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only**: PubMed abstract only; no PMC record and no PDF obtained. No site-level data, complications, or attrition details are available.
- **No control group**: Single-arm case series — no autograft (connective tissue graft/free gingival graft) or untreated comparator, so superiority and causality cannot be established.
- **Short follow-up**: Outcome assessed at 3 months only; no data on long-term stability of the gained thickness, keratinized mucosa width, or peri-implant health.
- **Measurement**: Thickness measured with a periodontal probe at a single time point, not with profilometry or three-dimensional imaging.
- **Unit of analysis unclear**: 42 implants in 40 patients; the abstract does not state how multiple implants per patient were handled.
- **Statistical choice**: A Mann-Whitney U-test (unpaired) is reported for a within-subject pre/post comparison; the abstract does not justify this choice.
- **Future work**: Controlled comparisons against autogenous grafts with longer follow-up would be needed to position ADM within the treatment hierarchy.

## 7. Glossary

- **ADM (Acellular Dermal Matrix)**: Human dermis-derived allogenic membrane processed to remove cellular components while retaining the collagen extracellular matrix scaffold; here used as AlloDerm.
- **AlloDerm**: Trade name of the acellular dermal matrix used in this study (BioHorizons, Birmingham, AL, USA).
- **Submerged (two-stage) implant placement**: Placement of the implant below the mucosa with a cover screw, leaving it unloaded and buried until a second-stage surgery connects the healing abutment (here at 3 months).
- **Second-stage surgery (abutment connection / implant uncovering)**: Surgical exposure of a submerged implant to connect a healing abutment; the time point at which augmentation was measured.
- **Vertical soft tissue thickness**: The apicocoronal dimension of the peri-implant soft tissue, as distinct from keratinized mucosa width (horizontal dimension).
- **Mann-Whitney U-test**: Nonparametric rank-based test for differences between two independent groups; the test reported here for the pre/post thickness comparison.
