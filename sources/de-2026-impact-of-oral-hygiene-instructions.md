## Why Ingested

This 2026 RCT asks the question the existing mucositis evidence leaves open: how much of peri-implant mucositis resolution comes from patient-performed hygiene alone versus professional instrumentation. It reinforces [[implants/peri-implantitis/mauriello-2026-peri-implant-mucositis-adjunctive-narrative-review]] (professional mechanical removal as the backbone, with the added value of instrumentation quantified here) and extends [[implants/peri-implantitis/bishti-2025-interproximal-devices-peri-implant-mucositis-sr-ma]] (interproximal cleaning devices) with trial data on interdental brushing under instruction, feeding the Primary Prevention: Mucositis Management section of [[overviews/peri-implantitis-treatment-modalities-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Randomized controlled trial (56 randomized, 28 per arm; 48 analyzed: 23 OHI-only patients/66 implants vs 25 OHI + mechanical instrumentation patients/52 implants) at a university periodontology department in Spain, testing whether oral hygiene instructions (OHI) alone can resolve peri-implant mucositis (PM) over 3 months; the trial was not powered for superiority or non-inferiority.
Disease resolution (modified bleeding index, mBI, <= 0.16) at 3 months was 39.1% (OHI) vs 56% (OHI + MI) at the patient level and 36.4% vs 67.3% at the implant level; both arms reduced mBI significantly (p < 0.001) with no significant between-group difference in 3-month mBI (p = 0.408), and compliance was the strongest predictor of resolution (OR = 11.4, p = 0.004).
OHI alone resolves PM in a substantial minority, mainly in compliant patients, but adding professional mechanical instrumentation yields higher resolution; history of periodontitis, posterior implant position and poor accessibility worsen outcomes; limitations are the 3-month follow-up, self-reported compliance and a sample size based on a subjective 50% assumption.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Implants Research 2026
- **DOI**: 10.1111/clr.70098
- **Institution**: Department of Periodontology, Universitat Internacional de Catalunya (UIC), Barcelona, Spain
- **Registration**: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06535958; CONSORT-reported; ethics ref. PER-ECL-2023-07; conducted September 2024 to February 2025

## 2. Key Contributions
- Reports the absolute proportion of patients achieving PM resolution with OHI alone (39.1% patient level; 36.4% implant level), a patient-centered figure rather than a superiority comparison.
- Quantifies the added benefit of professional mechanical instrumentation: implant-level resolution 67.3% (OHI + MI) vs 36.4% (OHI alone).
- Identifies compliance (interproximal cleaning at least once daily), history of periodontitis and posterior implant position as prognostic factors.
- All implants whose prosthesis was modified for hygiene access (16 prostheses, minor contour reduction) achieved resolution.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective, parallel-group, comparative-effectiveness RCT, 3-month follow-up; computer-generated permuted blocks of four, sealed opaque envelopes opened after OHI; per-protocol analysis. Not designed to test superiority, non-inferiority or equivalence.
- **Population**: Patients with >= 1 titanium implant in function >= 1 year, PM defined as bleeding on gentle probing (0.20 N) at > 1 site, no bone loss beyond initial remodelling (max 2 mm if no baseline radiograph), > 1 mm keratinized mucosa; non-smokers or light smokers (< 10 cigarettes/day); controlled diabetes (HbA1c < 7) allowed; untreated periodontitis, pregnancy, recent PM treatment or antibiotics within 3 months excluded.
- **Interventions**: All received individualized OHI (twice-daily cleaning with low-abrasive toothpaste; cylindrical/conical interproximal brushes). Control arm (OHI + MI): supra- and submucosal debridement with ultrasonic plastic tip and plastic curettes, rubber-cup polishing. Test arm: OHI only.
- **n**: 56 randomized (28 per arm); 48 analyzed (118 implants).
- **Outcomes**: Primary: disease resolution, mBI <= 0.16 (per EFP S3 guideline) at 3 months. Secondary: mBI, modified plaque index (mPI), suppuration on probing, probing depth, recession, full-mouth plaque/bleeding indices (FMPI/FMBI), radiographs, subgingival bacteria at baseline (T1), 1 month (T2), 3 months (T3). Blinded calibrated examiners (ICC 0.87-0.98).
- **Sample size**: 28 per arm, assuming 66.6% resolution with OHI + MI and >= 50% with OHI alone (alpha 5%, beta 10%), plus 15% dropout.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Analyzed: 48 patients (24 male, 24 female; mean age 63.6, SD 11.4); 45/48 (93.8%) had a history of periodontitis; 33 (68.8%) compliant; 8 dropouts.
- Disease resolution at 3 months, patient level: 39.1% (95% CI 19.7%-61.5%) OHI vs 56% (95% CI 34.9%-75.6%) OHI + MI.
- Disease resolution at 3 months, implant level: 36.4% (95% CI 24.8%-48.0%) OHI vs 67.3% (95% CI 54.6%-80.1%) OHI + MI.
- mBI decreased significantly in both groups (p < 0.001); 3-month median mBI 0.40 (IQR 0.17-0.94) OHI vs 0.17 (IQR 0.17-0.29) OHI + MI (p = 0.408); median reduction -1.02 (OHI) vs -1.58 (OHI + MI).
- mPI fell significantly in both groups without between-group difference (patient level p = 0.977; implant level p = 0.973); FMPI reduction was greater with OHI + MI (p < 0.001).
- Suppuration was rare: 98.1% (OHI + MI) and 95.4% (OHI) of implants suppuration-free at 3 months. Median probing depth at 3 months 2.67 mm (OHI) vs 2.83 mm (OHI + MI), p = 0.261.
- Microbiology: P. gingivalis fell from 21/25 to 12/25 (OHI + MI) and 16/23 to 7/23 (OHI); no significant group differences for any species.
- Patient-level predictors: compliance OR = 11.4 (95% CI 2.19-59.2, p = 0.004), success 63.6% in compliant vs 13.3% in non-compliant patients; higher FMPI and FMBI at 3 months predicted lower resolution.
- Implant-level: OHI-only OR = 0.28 (95% CI 0.11-0.71, p = 0.007); compliance OR = 9.43 (p < 0.001); posterior location trend (p = 0.056); history of periodontitis associated with higher final mBI (p = 0.010).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 3 months of follow-up; not extended to avoid progression to peri-implantitis in non-resolving OHI-only patients.
- Sample size rested on a subjective 50% resolution assumption without prior data; the trial is exploratory, not a superiority or non-inferiority test.
- Implants were not randomized (unit of randomization = patient); variable number of implants per patient (1 to 7) may affect implant-level results.
- Compliance was self-reported; per-protocol analysis; baseline imbalance in FMPI and implant position (posterior implants more frequent in the OHI-only arm).
- Source text inconsistency: the abstract assigns 52 implants to OHI and 66 to OHI + MI, whereas the body text and the implant-level rates (36.4% and 67.3%) correspond to 66 OHI and 52 OHI + MI implants; this page follows the body text.

## 6. Related Work
- Herrera 2023 (EFP S3 guideline): source of the mBI <= 0.16 success definition used here.
- Aimetti 2019 and Verket 2023: reported 30.9%-34.5% resolution after instrumentation, comparable to the OHI-only arm here.
- Tutuncuoglu 2022: toothbrush plus irrigator or interdental brush resolved PM at 12 weeks.
- Romandini 2021: interdental brush use protective against peri-implant disease (OR 0.27).
- Nicola 2024: initial FMBI below 25% predicted partial resolution.

## 7. Glossary
- **OHI**: oral hygiene instructions (individualized behavioral phase, here with interproximal brushes).
- **MI**: mechanical instrumentation (ultrasonic plastic-tip and plastic-curette debridement plus polishing).
- **mBI**: modified bleeding index (Mombelli); resolution defined as mBI <= 0.16.
- **PM**: peri-implant mucositis, inflammation of peri-implant mucosa without supporting bone loss.
- **FMPI/FMBI**: full-mouth plaque / bleeding index.
