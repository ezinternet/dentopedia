## Why Ingested

First CBCT-exclusive SR+MA directly comparing external apical root resorption (External Apical Root Resorption, EARR) magnitude between clear aligner and fixed appliance groups using only quantitative linear CBCT measurements — closing the imaging-heterogeneity gap present in prior reviews. Complements [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/butsabul-2024-clear-aligner-root-resorption-cbct-sr-ma]] (which quantified absolute aligner-induced EARR without a fixed-appliance comparator) and provides a PROSPERO-registered SR+MA counterpart to the RCT finding of [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/bespalez-neto-2026-root-resorption-clear-aligner-ai-cbct]].

## One-line Summary

SR+MA (6 CBCT-only comparative studies; 392 patients) — clear aligner therapy produces significantly less apical root shortening than fixed appliances (MD = −0.50 mm; 95% CI −0.79 to −0.21; p<0.001; I²=60.8%; GRADE low).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to restrict inclusion exclusively to CBCT-based linear root length measurements (mm) for CA-vs-FA EARR comparison, eliminating projection error from 2D radiographic pooling.
- Pooled evidence of 6 studies (392 patients) shows statistically significant and directionally consistent advantage for clear aligners (MD −0.50 mm favoring CA).
- Extraction-protocol subgroup (k=1, descriptive only) showed larger absolute difference (MD −0.95 mm); non-extraction/mixed subgroup (k=5) MD −0.41 mm remained significant.
- Leave-one-out sensitivity analysis confirmed effect robustness — pooled MD ranged −0.41 to −0.60 mm regardless of which study was excluded.
- GRADE certainty rated low, appropriately reflecting non-randomized study designs and residual confounding by case complexity.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD420261320269; PRISMA 2020)
- **Search**: PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, CENTRAL, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP, Google Scholar — inception to January 2026; English-only
- **Inclusion**: Comparative clinical studies reporting CBCT-based linear root length change (mm) in CA and FA groups; human subjects; no year restriction
- **Exclusion**: Non-CBCT or 2D-only imaging, no comparator group, non-extractable data, reviews/commentaries
- **Participants**: 6 studies, 392 patients; mixed extraction/non-extraction protocols; various malocclusion types
- **Risk of bias**: ROBINS-I tool (no RCTs met inclusion; no RoB 2 applied); most studies: moderate risk; one pilot study: serious risk; none critical
- **Statistical method**: Random-effects model (DerSimonian–Laird); effect size MD = CA − FA (negative = less EARR with CA); heterogeneity by Cochran Q + I²
- **Subgroup analyses**: Extraction vs non-extraction/mixed protocols (pre-specified)
- **Sensitivity**: Leave-one-out; excluding serious-risk study; excluding extraction-only study

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Primary outcome — CBCT linear EARR (mm), CA vs FA:**
- Pooled MD = **−0.50 mm** (95% CI −0.79 to −0.21; p<0.001)
- Heterogeneity: I² = 60.8% (moderate); Cochran Q significant
- Direction favored CA in 5 of 6 studies; one study showed near-null difference

**Subgroup — Extraction protocol:**
- Non-extraction/mixed (k=5): MD = −0.41 mm (95% CI −0.69 to −0.13; I²=52.5%)
- Extraction (k=1): MD = −0.95 mm (95% CI −1.42 to −0.48) — single study, descriptive only

**Sensitivity analyses:**
- Excluding serious-risk study: MD = −0.53 mm (95% CI −0.81 to −0.25; I²=55%)
- Excluding extraction-only study: MD = −0.41 mm (95% CI −0.69 to −0.13; I²=52.5%)
- Leave-one-out range: −0.41 to −0.60 mm — all significant

**Reporting bias:** Egger test p=0.28 (non-significant but underpowered with k<10)

**GRADE**: Low certainty (non-randomized designs; residual confounding; moderate heterogeneity; imprecision from small study count)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 6 studies eligible; insufficient for meta-regression of heterogeneity sources (treatment duration, movement type, CBCT parameters)
- All included studies non-randomized — residual confounding by case complexity and selection bias (CA often used in less complex cases) cannot be excluded
- Extraction-protocol subgroup effectively single-study; no valid pooled inference
- CBCT acquisition heterogeneity (voxel size, field of view, landmark protocols) contributes unexplained variance
- Unit-of-analysis inconsistency across studies (tooth-level vs patient-level averaging) may inflate precision in some primary studies
- No universally accepted minimal clinically important difference (MCID) threshold for orthodontic EARR — clinical weight of −0.50 mm is context-dependent
- Future needs: prospective RCTs with standardized mechanics and CBCT protocols; distributional outcomes (proportion exceeding clinically meaningful thresholds); within-patient clustering adjustment; core outcome sets

## 6. Related Work

- Butsabul 2024 (CBCT SR+MA, absolute CA EARR, no FA comparator): incisor −0.56 mm; maxillary central −0.74 mm
- Bespalez-Neto 2026 (RCT, AI-3D CBCT surface, CA vs FA): overall median −0.72 mm, no significant CA-vs-FA difference
- Ronchi Lemos 2026 (SR+MA, mixed 2D/3D, adverse effects): linear −0.33 mm / volume −4.37 mm³ (CA absolute)
- Yassir 2022 (umbrella review of 18 SRs): noted lower EARR risk with CA vs FA — consistent direction
- Hassan 2024 (TNF-α prospective, CA vs FA): FA generates higher sustained inflammatory cytokine load, mechanistically supporting less EARR with CA

## 7. Glossary

- **EARR (External Apical Root Resorption, 외부 유치단 흡수)**: Irreversible loss of cementum and dentin at root apex during orthodontic tooth movement
- **MD (Mean Difference, 평균 차이)**: CA − FA; negative = less EARR with clear aligners
- **I² (I-squared, 이질성 통계량)**: 60.8% = moderate heterogeneity
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation; certainty of evidence framework
- **ROBINS-I**: Risk of Bias in Non-randomized Studies of Interventions tool
- **PROSPERO**: International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews
- **DerSimonian–Laird**: Standard random-effects estimator for meta-analysis τ²
- **PDL (Periodontal Ligament, 치주인대)**: Fibrous tissue between root and alveolar bone; site of orthodontic force transduction and EARR initiation

## Three-line Summary

- PROSPERO-registered SR+MA (CRD420261320269; 6 CBCT-exclusive comparative studies; 392 patients) comparing external apical root resorption (EARR) in clear aligner versus fixed orthodontic appliance groups using only quantitative CBCT linear measurements.
- Pooled random-effects meta-analysis yielded MD = −0.50 mm (95% CI −0.79 to −0.21; p<0.001; I²=60.8%) favoring clear aligners; non-extraction/mixed subgroup (k=5) MD = −0.41 mm; leave-one-out sensitivity confirmed robustness (−0.41 to −0.60 mm); GRADE certainty low.
- Clear aligner therapy produces statistically significantly less apical root shortening than fixed appliances on CBCT measurement; the effect is robust to sensitivity analysis but GRADE-low certainty from non-randomized designs means this evidence is exploratory rather than clinically definitive.
