## Why Ingested

New randomized clinical evidence (2026) on filling the fixture–socket gap at immediate implant placement with deproteinized bovine bone + hyaluronic acid (cerabone plus), quantifying early stability (ISQ at 4 months) and CBCT bone density / buccal bone preservation (6 months). Reinforces [[wiki/immediate-implant/gap-grafting/sanz-2017-bone-graft-gap-immediate-implant-rct]] (gap grafting preserves buccal bone) and [[wiki/immediate-implant/gap-grafting/zaki-2021-bone-substitute-materials-immediate-implant-sr-ma]] with fresh primary data, and adds the HA-modified xenograft variable to the gap-grafting literature.

## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=16 immediate implants, 8 per group) in medically healthy adults (21–40 yr) with non-restorable single-rooted maxillary anterior/premolar teeth, comparing immediate implant placement (IIP) alone vs IIP + natural bovine bone with hyaluronic acid in the fixture–socket gap.

Study group showed higher 4-month implant stability quotient (ISQ) (72.25 ± 2.66 vs 64.88 ± 1.36, p<0.001) and higher 6-month CBCT bone density (1163.38 ± 112.64 vs 1034.13 ± 71.19 HU, p=0.016); buccal bone width reduction was 3.88% vs 15.99% (no significant between-group buccal width difference).

Combined deproteinized bovine bone + hyaluronic acid grafting at IIP favors early implant stability and buccal bone preservation, but the design cannot isolate HA's independent contribution (no DBB-only arm) and follow-up is short — considered preliminary.

## 세줄요약

RCT (n=16 즉시식립 임플란트, 8/군): 건강한 21–40세 성인의 수복불가 단근 상악 전치·소구치에서 즉시식립 단독 vs 즉시식립 + 천연 우골·히알루론산(cerabone plus) 간극 이식 비교.

연구군이 4개월차 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) (72.25 ± 2.66 vs 64.88 ± 1.36, p<0.001) 및 6개월차 CBCT 골밀도 (1163.38 vs 1034.13 HU, p=0.016) 유의 우위를 보였고, 협측골 폭 감소는 3.88% vs 15.99%로 작았다(군간 폭 차는 유의하지 않음).

우골+HA 복합 이식이 초기 안정성·협측골 보존에 유리하나, 탈단백 우골(Deproteinized Bovine Bone, DBB) 단독 대조군 부재로 HA 고유 효과 분리가 불가능하고 추적이 단기여서 예비적(pilot) 근거로 해석해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First randomized clinical study evaluating natural bovine bone combined with hyaluronic acid specifically for gap grafting at immediate implant placement (IIP).
- Demonstrates combined graft improves early secondary stability (4-month ISQ crossing the ~70 threshold associated with predictable loading) vs ungrafted IIP.
- Reports CBCT bone density and buccal bone width trajectories at 6 months, with markedly smaller buccal bone loss (3.88% vs 15.99%) in the grafted group.
- Highlights that the immediate bone-density advantage reflects graft radiopacity/crystallinity rather than biological success — longitudinal density change is the meaningful signal.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Parallel-group randomized clinical trial (CONSORT-compliant), two arms
- **Setting**: Suez Canal University, Egypt; Ethics approval REC/544/2022; trial NCT07199725 (registered retrospectively, Sep 2025)
- **n**: 16 implants in 16 patients, 8 per group (G*Power: effect size 0.39, partial η² 0.13, power 80%, α=0.05)
- **Groups**: Group 1 control = IIP only; Group 2 study = IIP + natural bovine bone (DBB) with hyaluronic acid, cerabone plus (botiss biomaterials GmbH), hydrated to a sticky bone and packed into the marginal (jumping) gap
- **Inclusion**: medically healthy, 21–40 yr, non-restorable single-rooted maxillary anterior or premolar, no periapical lesions. Exclusion: uncontrolled systemic disease, vertical/horizontal bone defects, heavy smoking, bruxism/parafunction, poor hygiene/advanced periodontitis
- **Surgery**: atraumatic extraction (periotomes), osteotomy 700–900 rpm, 40 Ncm torque, copious irrigation; Frontier implants (Global Medical Implants Co, Barcelona); collagen sponge cover + 3/0 vicryl
- **Randomization/blinding**: research randomizer (randomizer.com), concealment via sealed opaque envelopes by an independent investigator; clinical and radiographic examiners blinded to allocation
- **Outcomes**: primary = implant stability (ISQ via Osstell, at surgery and 4 months); secondary = CBCT (Carestream) peri-implant bone density (2-mm zone, HU, OnDemand3D virtual implant method) and buccal bone width (coronal third) at immediate and 6 months
- **Analysis**: unpaired Student's t-test per time point, chi-square/Fisher as appropriate, SPSS v26, two-tailed p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **ISQ immediate**: control 50.75 ± 4.50 vs study 55.25 ± 4.33, p=0.061 (NS)
- **ISQ 4 months**: control 64.88 ± 1.36 vs study 72.25 ± 2.66, p<0.001 (within-group change 27.84% vs 30.77%, both p<0.001)
- **Bone density immediate (HU)**: 880.63 ± 76.18 vs 1075.88 ± 97.51, p=0.001
- **Bone density 6 months (HU)**: 1034.13 ± 71.19 vs 1163.38 ± 112.64, p=0.016 (within-group +17.43% vs +8.13%, p<0.001 / p=0.001)
- **Buccal bone width immediate (mm)**: 2.94 ± 1.09 vs 3.09 ± 1.58, p=0.831 (NS)
- **Buccal bone width 6 months (mm)**: 2.47 ± 1.07 vs 2.97 ± 1.61, p=0.468 (NS); within-group reduction 15.99% (t=8.74, p<0.001) vs 3.88% (t=3.247, p=0.014)
- **Age**: control 29.88 ± 5.08 vs study 33.00 ± 6.55 yr, p=0.305 (NS)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (16 implants) and short follow-up (4–6 months); no long-term survival or peri-implant tissue outcomes
- CBCT gray values are device- and parameter-dependent and not absolute Hounsfield units; density values are relative indicators of mineralized tissue
- Cannot isolate the independent contribution of hyaluronic acid — no DBB-only (third) arm; outcomes may reflect DBB osteoconduction, HA biological activity, or synergy
- The immediate bone-density difference likely reflects graft radiopacity/crystallinity rather than biological success
- Trial retrospectively registered
- Authors call for adequately powered future RCTs with a DBB-only comparator and longer follow-up

## 7. Glossary

- **Immediate implant placement (즉시식립)**: placement of an implant into a fresh extraction socket (type 1 timing)
- **Fixture–socket gap (jumping gap, 간극/틈새)**: the dimensional discrepancy between the implant fixture and the extraction socket wall; a biologically sensitive region where graft material can be placed
- **Deproteinized bovine bone (탈단백 우골, DBB)**: bovine xenograft bone-substitute material with osteoconductive properties; "natural bovine bone" in this paper
- **Hyaluronic acid (히알루론산, HA)**: extracellular-matrix glycosaminoglycan with anti-inflammatory, angiogenic and cell-viability effects used to support bone regeneration
- **Implant Stability Quotient (임플란트 안정성 지수, ISQ)**: resonance frequency analysis (RFA, Osstell) measure of implant stability; higher values indicate a more stable bone–implant interface
- **Hounsfield units (하운스필드 단위, HU)**: CT/CBCT gray-scale density units; in CBCT, device-dependent relative values rather than absolute units
