## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (Periodontology 2000, 2026): 14 RCTs on adjunctive hyaluronic acid (HA) in regenerative periodontal surgery for intrabony defects — primary outcome PPD reduction; secondary CAL gain, gingival recession, and radiographic defect depth (RDD) reduction.

Adjunctive HA reduced PPD by an additional 0.7–1.2 mm and improved CAL by 0.7–1.1 mm vs control (most consistent at 12 months); RDD reduction was approximately 0.7–1.0 mm; subgroup analysis showed significant benefit with HA alone or combined with membranes, but no added benefit when combined with bone substitute materials.

Comparisons with enamel matrix derivative (EMD) showed comparable or slightly superior results for EMD; heterogeneity and study quality require cautious interpretation.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (Periodontology 2000, 2026): 치조내 골결손 치주 재생 수술에서 히알루론산 (Hyaluronic Acid, HA) 보조 적용을 다룬 14 RCT 통합 — 1차 결과: 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 감소; 2차: 임상 부착 수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득·치은 퇴축·방사선학적 결손 깊이 (Radiographic Defect Depth, RDD) 감소.

HA 보조군은 대조군 대비 PPD 약 0.7–1.2 mm 추가 감소, CAL 0.7–1.1 mm 추가 획득 (12개월 시 가장 일관); RDD 약 0.7–1.0 mm 추가 감소; 서브그룹: HA 단독 또는 차폐막 병용 → 유의한 이점, 골이식재 병용 → 추가 이점 없음.

에나멜 기질 단백질 (Enamel Matrix Derivative, EMD) 비교 시 EMD가 동등하거나 약간 우세; 이질성·연구 질이 높아 해석 주의.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to pool clinical and radiographic outcomes of adjunctive HA in regenerative periodontal surgery for intrabony defects
- Quantifies additional PPD reduction (0.7–1.2 mm) and CAL gain (0.7–1.1 mm) over control at 12 months
- Subgroup finding: benefit only when HA used alone or with membranes — not with bone substitutes
- Radiographic benefit (RDD): 0.7–1.0 mm additional reduction (with heterogeneity)
- EMD head-to-head: EMD comparable or slightly superior to HA
- 14 RCTs included — largest evidence base for HA in periodontal regeneration to date

## 3. Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | SR + MA, random-effects models |
| Primary outcome | PPD reduction |
| Secondary outcomes | CAL gain, gingival recession, RDD reduction |
| Population | Adult patients with intrabony defects undergoing regenerative periodontal surgery |
| Intervention | Adjunctive HA (various forms) |
| Comparators | Control (surgery without HA), EMD |
| Included studies | 14 RCTs |
| Subgroup analyses | Regenerative protocol: HA alone / HA + membrane / HA + bone substitute |
| Statistical method | Random-effects meta-analysis with subgroup analyses |

## 4. Key Results

| Outcome | Additional effect (HA vs control) | Consistency |
|---|---|---|
| PPD reduction | ~0.7–1.2 mm | More consistent at 12 months |
| CAL gain | ~0.7–1.1 mm | More consistent at 12 months |
| RDD reduction | ~0.7–1.0 mm | Heterogeneity noted |

**Subgroup (regenerative protocol)**:
- HA alone: significant benefit
- HA + membrane: significant benefit
- HA + bone substitute: no significant added benefit

**EMD comparison**: EMD comparable or slightly superior results vs HA

## 5. Limitations

- Heterogeneity noted in radiographic outcomes (RDD)
- Study quality considerations noted — cautious interpretation warranted
- Various HA formulations/concentrations may contribute to heterogeneity

## 7. Glossary

- **HA (Hyaluronic Acid, 히알루론산)**: Naturally occurring glycosaminoglycan with anti-inflammatory, anti-bacterial, and pro-regenerative properties; used as a filler/adjunct in periodontal surgery
- **Intrabony defect (치조내 골결손)**: Periodontal bone defect extending apically below the alveolar crest between the root surface and alveolar bone
- **PPD (Probing Pocket Depth, 치주낭 깊이)**: Distance from gingival margin to the base of the periodontal pocket
- **CAL (Clinical Attachment Level, 임상 부착 수준)**: Distance from CEJ to the base of the pocket; primary measure of periodontal destruction
- **RDD (Radiographic Defect Depth, 방사선학적 결손 깊이)**: Radiographic measure of intrabony defect depth
- **EMD (Enamel Matrix Derivative, 에나멜 기질 단백질)**: Protein extract from developing porcine teeth (Emdogain); established regenerative agent for intrabony defects
