## Why Ingested

위키에 급성 악관절 탈구 (acute temporomandibular joint dislocation) 관련 근거 기반 국제 합의 권고안 (international consensus recommendation)이 전무했다. [[tmj/prechel-2018-the-treatment-of-temporomandibular-joint]]는 독일 단일국가 S3 지침이고, [[tmj/abrahamsson-2020-treatment-of-temporomandibular-joint-luxation]]는 RCT-only SR이다. 이 논문은 유럽 12개국 22명 전문가가 수정 델파이법 (modified Delphi method)으로 합의한 첫 번째 국제 권고안으로, 독일 지침을 국제 표준으로 확장하면서 "엄지는 하악 외사선에 얹어라", "손목축법은 LoE Ib~IV 근거에도 ESTMJS 경험 부족으로 히포크라테스 우선"처럼 독일 지침과의 차이점을 명시한다 — 술기 선택 근거와 단계적 치료 알고리즘이 한 문서에 정리된 유일한 국제 자료다.

## Three-line Summary

International consensus recommendation based on a modified Delphi procedure conducted by the European Society of TMJ Surgeons (ESTMJS): 20/44 members completed preliminary (blinded) grading of 30 draft recommendations (derived from the German S3 guideline, AWMF 007-063) and 22/44 participated in the final consensus meeting (Marburg, May 2019); literature base updated to February 2021 for publication, encompassing 230 papers; preliminary vs. final SoC differed significantly (p=0.0005), but all 24 final recommendations were accepted at **strong consensus (>95%)**.

The 24 final recommendations cover: (a) uniform nomenclature (fixed vs. non-fixed; one-time/recurrent/habitual; acute/chronic [>4 weeks]/longstanding); (b) diagnosis — history and physical examination sufficient for non-traumatic cases, X-ray optional except to exclude fracture; (c) treatment — immediate manual reduction for acute dislocation, Hippocratic method preferred (wrist pivot method not adopted due to ESTMJS inexperience), thumbs on the oblique line not on molars, one-side-at-a-time vs. bilateral left to physician preference; if manual reduction fails, try with muscle relaxants/analgesics/sedation/GA; for recurrent/habitual, non-surgical first (ABI best evidence LoE Ib, BTX off-label but potential indication), then minimally invasive, then surgical (eminectomy / restrictive procedures / capsulorrhaphy).

Key divergence from the German S3 guideline: the Delphi process produced a significantly different initial distribution of grades of recommendation, requiring moderated discussion before unanimous consensus was reached; the ESTMJS members explicitly declined to adopt the unilateral-first sequence and wrist pivot method as recommendations.

## 세줄요약

유럽 악관절외과학회 (European Society of Temporomandibular Joint Surgeons, ESTMJS) 수정 델파이법 국제 합의 권고안 — 독일 S3 지침 30개 초안을 기반으로, 예비 투표 20명(12개국)·최종 투표 22명이 참여, 2021년 2월까지 문헌 업데이트(총 230편) 반영 후 24개 최종 권고안을 **강한 합의 (strong consensus, >95%)** 로 채택. 예비투표와 최종투표 간 합의 강도가 유의미하게 달랐다(p=0.0005).

용어 표준화 3축(자기복원 여부·발생 시간·지속 기간)과 진단·치료 권고 21개를 확정함 — 급성 비외상성 탈구는 병력·신체검사만으로 진단 가능, X선 불필수(이상 증상 시 고려), 즉시 수동복원 (manual reduction)에서 히포크라테스법 (Hippocratic method) 우선·엄지는 외사선 (oblique line) 위 거치·단측/양측 선택은 술자 재량; 복원 실패 시 근이완제·진통제·진정·전신마취 (general anesthesia, GA); 재발성에는 자가혈액 주사 (autologous blood injection, ABI, 근거수준 LoE Ib)·보툴리눔 독소 (botulinum toxin, BTX, 허가외 사용이나 잠재적 적응증으로 포함)·경화요법 (sclerotherapy), 이후 관절융기절제술 (eminectomy)·제한술 (restrictive procedures)·관절낭봉합 (capsulorrhaphy) 순.

독일 S3 지침과의 핵심 차이: 손목축법 (wrist pivot method)은 LoE Ib~IV 근거에도 ESTMJS 경험 부족(2/20만 지지)으로 표준 권고에서 제외됨 — "히포크라테스법 우선" 근거가 문헌 우위보다 학회 임상경험에 무게를 둔 선택이라는 점을 명시함. 이 문서는 법적 구속력 없는 임상 지침이나 저자들은 모든 임상 의사에게 따르도록 강력히 권고한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **First international evidence + consensus-based recommendation for TMJ dislocation management**: to the authors' knowledge, the first internationally produced document combining systematic literature review and formal Delphi consensus for this condition.
- **Establishes uniform international nomenclature**: three-axis classification (self-reducibility: fixed vs. non-fixed; time course: one-time / recurrent / habitual; duration: acute / chronic >4 weeks / longstanding) — addresses a documented global heterogeneity in terminology.
- **Documents divergence between national guidelines and international expert voting**: preliminary Delphi vote differed significantly from the German S3 guideline (p=0.0005), highlighting that European specialist practice is more heterogenous than the German standard implies.
- **Explicitly positions the wrist pivot method**: despite LoE Ib evidence, the ESTMJS declined to adopt it as a standard recommendation due to collective inexperience — making transparent how consensus can lag evidence in specialist communities.
- **Formalizes the treatment ladder**: non-surgical → minimally invasive (ABI best evidence LoE Ib, BTX included as off-label potential indication) → surgical (eminectomy / restrictive / capsulorrhaphy), providing a structured escalation framework absent from pure evidence summaries.
- **Introduces an internationalized "oblique line" technique detail**: recommends thumbs on the mandibular oblique line (not on molars or with bite blocks) to prevent biting injury — a specific technical modification from German practice, endorsed by the ESTMJS.

## 3. Methodology

- **Consensus method**: modified Delphi procedure following AWMF S3 guideline rules
- **Draft source**: 30 recommendations from the German S3 guideline (AWMF 007-063, 2016), translated to English by A.N. (the German guideline coordinator)
- **Grading system**: Oxford CEBM levels of evidence (LoE I–V, ± quality modifiers); Grades of Recommendation (GoR): A = "shall" (strong, highest LoE), B = "should" (non-emphasized, LoE II+), 0 = "may" (open, LoE III–V)
- **Preliminary vote**: blinded grading by 20/44 ESTMJS members from 12 countries, 6 weeks prior to GA; independent monitor processed suggestions
- **Final vote**: 22/44 ESTMJS members at general assembly, Marburg, May 2019; moderated discussion and live voting (Wilcoxon signed-rank test for GoR/SoC comparisons)
- **Strength of consensus**: strong consensus >95%; consensus >75–95%; majority >50–75%; no consensus ≤50%
- **Literature search**: initial 104 articles from German guideline searches (PubMed/Cochrane/Embase/ZB Med, 2014–2016); updated 2019 (+34 articles) and 2020–Feb 2021 (+92 articles) → **230 total**
- **Final outcome**: 8 recommendations discarded, 2 newly added → **24 final recommendations** (12 unchanged at strong consensus; 6 modified to reach strong consensus; 4 changed GoR to reach strong consensus); GoR distribution: A=1, B=15, 0=8
- **Statistical analysis**: Wilcoxon signed-rank test comparing German guideline vs. preliminary vs. final SoC and GoR

## 4. The 24 Final Recommendations (Condensed)

**Examinations (Rec. 1–3)**
1. Non-traumatic first-episode dislocation: diagnosis based on history + physical examination (inspection, palpation) if symptoms sufficiently indicative. [GoR 0]
2. X-rays not mandatory for standard cases; consider with atypical symptoms or history of facial trauma; may be indicated post-acute for pathogenesis assessment. [GoR B / 0]
3. Optional additional examinations may be indicated post-acute for pathogenesis and treatment planning. [GoR 0]

**Treatment (Rec. 4–22)**
4. Attempt manual reduction first in non-traumatic dislocations; earlier = better success. [GoR B]
5. Use Hippocratic method as the primary technique; ESTMJS members are not experienced with alternative methods described in literature (e.g. wrist pivot). [GoR B]
6. Reduction may be performed one side at a time or bilaterally — left to physician preference. [GoR 0]
7. Place thumbs on the oblique line (not on molars, not with bite blocks) to prevent bite injury. [GoR B]
8. Stabilize patient's head if seated. [GoR 0]
9. Initial attempt may be without medications. [GoR 0]
10. If unsuccessful, retry with muscle relaxants and/or analgesics; if still failing, use sedation or GA. [GoR B]
11. In patients with infectious diseases, dementia, etc., unilateral extraoral reduction may also be used. [GoR 0]
12. Post-reduction bandage may be used for stabilization. [GoR 0]
13. For recurrent, longstanding, or habitual dislocations, consider immobilization/securing methods. [GoR B]
14. Non-surgical methods should fail before minimally invasive or open surgical intervention. [GoR A — the sole GoR A in the document]
15. BTX treatment for recurrent dislocation remains off-label; manufacturer warnings shall be observed. [GoR B]
16. BTX should nonetheless be included as a potential indication for recurrent dislocations. [GoR B]
17. If non-surgical methods fail, especially in longstanding dislocations, consider surgical methods. [GoR B]
18. Surgical treatment indication in recurrent dislocation: only after failure of non-surgical and minimally invasive therapy. [GoR B]
19. Persisting dislocations: individualized approach across the full range of surgical methods. [GoR B]
20. After surgery: soft diet for a few days, avoid wide mouth opening. [GoR B]
21. After ABI or capsular ligament surgery: consider immobilization (limit maximum opening, not rigid fixation). [GoR B]
22. Secondary damage (e.g. anterior open bite): individualized functional/reconstructive/orthognathic surgery. [GoR 0]

**Meta-recommendations (Rec. 23–24)**
23. Treatment should begin as early as possible to limit degenerative changes and increase chances of conservative/minimally invasive success. [GoR B]
24. Best treatment depends on pathogenesis, age, co-morbidities, compliance, care structures — individualized based on thorough history and physical examination. [GoR B]

## 5. Key Results and Statistics

- Preliminary vs. final SoC: p=0.0005 (Wilcoxon signed-rank) — preliminary heterogeneous, final unanimous (SoC=100% for all 24)
- GoR distribution: GoR A: 1/24 (4.2%), GoR B: 15/24 (62.5%), GoR 0: 8/24 (33.3%)
- Draft proposals: 30 → discarded 8, added 2 → 24 final
- Consensus trajectory: recommendations with consensus in preliminary → 57% unchanged, 14% modified to strong consensus, 29% discarded; "approved by majority" → 46% unchanged, 38% modified, 15% discarded; "no consensus" → 1/3 survived with GoR/wording change, 2/3 discarded
- Literature base: 104 (original German search) + 34 (2019 update) + 92 (2020-2021) = 230 papers

## 6. Limitations

- ESTMJS ≠ representative sample: member list is essentially a volunteer group without formal national mandates; not all national European OMFS societies are represented.
- 22/44 members voted at the GA (~50% quorum); non-participating members' positions unknown.
- Not formally approved by national European societies at time of publication — stated as a next step.
- Preliminary vote subject to social influence at the GA (unblinded results, peer pressure) despite independent moderation.
- Literature base uses the same search as the German 2016 guideline, updated but not fully reprospective to PRISMA standards.

## 8. Glossary

- **ESTMJS** (European Society of Temporomandibular Joint Surgeons): European-level specialist society for TMJ surgery; author of this guideline
- **Modified Delphi method** (수정 델파이법): iterative expert-consensus procedure with blinded preliminary vote, moderated face-to-face discussion, and final voting
- **GoR A / B / 0** (권고등급): "shall" (Grade A, strong) / "should" (Grade B, recommended) / "may" (Grade 0, open decision), based on LoE + expert opinion
- **Oblique line** (외사선): bony ridge on the lateral surface of the mandibular ramus — ESTMJS recommended thumb placement for Hippocratic reduction to prevent bite injury
- **ABI** (autologous blood injection, 자가혈액 주사): injection of the patient's own blood into the superior joint space and/or pericapsular tissue to provoke sclerosis and reduce recurrence; LoE Ib per Hegab 2013 RCT
- **Longstanding dislocation** (장기 탈구): defined by degenerative adaptive changes in/around the joint rather than duration per se; distinguished from "chronic" (>4 weeks) in the ESTMJS nomenclature
