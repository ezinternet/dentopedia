## Why Ingested

기존 [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/smiley-2015-nonsurgical-periodontitis-srp-guideline]]는 국소 투여 항균제(미노사이클린·클로르헥시딘 칩)가 PPD >=5 mm 부위에서 효과적이라고만 요약하는데, 본 RCT는 미노사이클린 마이크로스피어(MM)를 해당 깊은 부위에만 도포·3개월 재도포하는 프로토콜의 실제 이득 크기(전악 PD/CAL 무차이, >=5 mm 부위만 소폭 개선)와 타액 병원균 11종 변화를 직접 제시해 그 조건을 한정(refines)한다. [[periodontics/john-2017-network-meta-analysis-studies-included]]의 "보조요법 CAL 이득 ~0.3 mm, 보조요법 간 우열 없음" 크기와도 일치해 이를 보강한다. 단일 논문 기록은 [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/arnett-2023-effect-of-scaling-and-root]]에 있다.

## Three-line Summary

Single-site, parallel-group RCT (University of Minnesota; n=70 randomized 35/35, Stage II–IV Grade B periodontitis, non-smokers, >=8 sites with PD >=5 mm) comparing full-mouth ultrasonic + hand SRP alone vs SRP plus minocycline HCl microspheres (MM) placed in PD >=5 mm sites at baseline and again after the 3-month maintenance visit, with 6-month follow-up and saliva qPCR quantification of 11 periodontal pathogens.

Whole-mouth PD and CAL did not differ at 6 months (PD 3.49 to 2.57 vs 3.57 to 2.67 mm, p=0.73; CAL p=0.77), and the cumulative salivary bacterial-burden score fell similarly in both arms; in baseline PD >=5 mm sites SRP+MM gave modestly greater 6-month change (PD 5.40 to 3.34 vs 5.42 to 3.54 mm, p<0.0001; CAL 5.48 to 3.60 vs 5.54 to 3.81 mm, p=0.002; BOP 48.8% to 7.6% vs 45.5% to 11.0%, p=0.001), plus greater drops in 6 of 11 pathogens at 1 month and 4 of 11 (F. nucleatum, P. intermedia, C. rectus, E. corrodens) at 6 months, but not in P. gingivalis.

The benefit is site-specific, small (~0.15–0.2 mm extra PD/CAL change at deep sites) and confounded with the 3-month MM re-application; unblinded single-operator assessment, saliva (not site) sampling with a proprietary test, and no described multiplicity adjustment across 11 pathogens x 3 visits limit confidence, and the authors themselves advise weighing cost/risk per patient.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 평행군 RCT(미네소타대; 무작위 70명, 35/35, Stage II–IV Grade B 치주염, 비흡연, PD >=5 mm 부위 8개 이상) — 초음파+수기 치석제거·치근활택술(SRP) 단독 vs SRP + 미노사이클린 HCl 마이크로스피어 (Minocycline HCl Microspheres, MM)를 PD >=5 mm 부위에 기저시 및 3개월 유지관리 직후 재도포, 6개월 추적, 타액 qPCR로 치주병원균 11종 정량.

전악 PD·CAL은 6개월에 군간 차이 없음(PD 3.49→2.57 vs 3.57→2.67 mm, p=0.73; CAL p=0.77), 타액 누적 세균부하 점수도 양 군 비슷하게 감소; 기저 PD >=5 mm 부위에서는 SRP+MM이 6개월에 소폭 더 큰 개선(PD 5.40→3.34 vs 5.42→3.54 mm, p<0.0001; CAL 5.48→3.60 vs 5.54→3.81 mm, p=0.002; BOP 48.8%→7.6% vs 45.5%→11.0%, p=0.001), 병원균은 1개월에 11종 중 6종, 6개월에 4종(F. nucleatum, P. intermedia, C. rectus, E. corrodens)이 더 크게 감소했으나 P. gingivalis는 차이 없음.

이득은 깊은 부위 한정·소폭(PD/CAL 추가 개선 약 0.15–0.2 mm)이고 3개월 MM 재도포와 분리되지 않으며, 비맹검 단일 술자 평가·부위가 아닌 타액 검체·독점 검사법·11종x3시점 다중비교 보정 미기술이 한계 — 저자도 환자별 비용/위험 대비 판단을 권고.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Adds a species-resolved salivary qPCR layer (11 pathogens plus a cumulative bacterial-burden score) to a local-antibiotic RCT.
- Tests MM confined to PD >=5 mm sites with a 3-month re-application.
- Clinical benefit concentrated in deep sites; whole-mouth PD/CAL unchanged at 6 months.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: single-site parallel RCT, block randomization (size 4), ITT, mixed-effects models (gamma/log link for pathogens).
- **n**: 70 randomized (35/35); 64 completed (86% SRP alone, 97% SRP+MM retention); 787 records prescreened, 701 ineligible, 16 declined/screen-fail.
- **Intervention**: ultrasonic + hand SRP all quadrants; MM at PD >=5 mm sites at baseline (594 sites) and after 3-month maintenance (566 sites, 95%).
- **Outcomes**: PD, CAL, BOP, GI (UNC probe, 6 sites/tooth); saliva qPCR of 11 pathogens at baseline, 1, 3, 6 months.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

See the wiki page Results table. Headline: >=5 mm sites at 6 months PD 5.40 to 3.34 vs 5.42 to 3.54 mm (p<0.0001), CAL 5.48 to 3.60 vs 5.54 to 3.81 mm (p=0.002), BOP 48.8% to 7.6% vs 45.5% to 11.0% (p=0.001); whole-mouth PD p=0.73, CAL p=0.77; 6 of 11 pathogens lower at 1 month, 4 of 11 at 6 months; P. gingivalis and cumulative burden score not different.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Unblinded single-operator examiner; no patient-reported outcomes; single center, predominantly older Caucasian men.
- Saliva sampling with a proprietary test is not site-specific; no multiplicity adjustment described for 11 pathogens x 3 visits.
- Repeat MM application confounded with initial placement.
- Authors call for work on GCF/serum biomarkers (IL-1, IL-6, TNF-alpha, MMP-8), systemic markers (hsCRP, HbA1c), and test sensitivity/specificity for precision periodontics.

## 6. Related Work

- Teles et al. and Goodson et al. (cited in the paper): repeated MM may lower pathogens; red-complex decrease at 1 month after SRP+MM.
- ADA guideline (Smiley 2015) and the NMA of John 2017: see wiki page.

## 7. Glossary

- **MM**: minocycline HCl microspheres, a locally delivered sustained-release antibiotic adjunct to SRP (FDA-approved for pocket depth reduction).
- **SRP**: scaling and root planing.
- **PD / CAL / BOP / GI**: probing depth / clinical attachment level / bleeding on probing / Loe gingival index.
- **Bacterial burden SCORE**: 0–100 cumulative, risk-weighted sum of log10 pathogen quantities (11 analytes).
