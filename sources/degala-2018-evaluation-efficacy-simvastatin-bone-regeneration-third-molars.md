## Why Ingested

First split-mouth human RCT testing local simvastatin in third molar extraction sockets, providing direct clinical evidence for the bone-anabolic effect of local statin application. Complements the animal SR [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/kommuri-2025-efficacy-of-statins-in-tissue]] and the negative histomorphometric RCT [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/katanec-2026-histomorphometric-evaluation-of-socket-preservation]].

## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (n=30) evaluating local simvastatin 10 mg in Gelfoam vs saline control following bilateral mandibular third molar extraction.
Gray-level bone density values were significantly higher in simvastatin sockets at all time points (1, 4, 8, 12 weeks; P=.001), with CBCT confirming accelerated regeneration.
Positive result but limited by single-center design, single-blinding, and CBCT only for every fifth patient.

## Summary

abstract-only — full text not retrieved

Split-mouth RCT (n=30, ages 18–40) evaluating local application of simvastatin 10 mg on post-extraction bone regeneration after bilateral mandibular third molar removal. Each patient underwent two surgical sessions; one socket received Gelfoam soaked with simvastatin, the other Gelfoam soaked with normal saline (control). Bone density was measured by mean gray-level histographic values on intraoral periapical radiographs at 1, 4, 8, and 12 weeks. CBCT obtained for every fifth patient at 12 weeks.

## Key Findings

- Gray-level histographic values significantly higher in simvastatin sockets at all time points: 1, 4, 8, 12 weeks (P = .001; n=30 sockets each group)
- CBCT analysis corroborated accelerated bone regeneration in study sockets at 12 weeks
- Simvastatin mechanism cited: promotes osteoblastic activity, inhibits osteoclastic activity, supports osteoblast differentiation via BMP
- Conclusion: local simvastatin is "cost-effective and simple" to stimulate osseous regeneration post-extraction

## Methods

- Design: randomized, split-mouth, single-blinded, single-center RCT
- Population: 30 patients, 18–40 years, requiring bilateral surgical extraction of mandibular third molars
- Intervention: Gelfoam + simvastatin 10 mg vs Gelfoam + normal saline
- Outcomes: pain, postoperative swelling, bone density (gray-level histography); CBCT for every 5th patient
- Follow-up: 3 months (12 weeks)

## Limitations (from abstract)

- Single-center, single-blinded
- CBCT only for every fifth patient (n=6 of 30), not full cohort
- No long-term follow-up beyond 3 months
- Abstract only; effect sizes (absolute gray-level values) not reported here
