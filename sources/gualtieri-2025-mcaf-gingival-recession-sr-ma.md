## Why Ingested

Provides a 2025 SR+MA specifically comparing modified coronally advanced flap (mCAF) adjunctive techniques for multiple adjacent gingival recession (MAGR) treatment — addresses a distinct surgical scenario not covered by [[periodontics/cairo-2025-root-coverage-single-grd-sr]] (which covers single GRDs), establishing the superiority of sCTG over substitutes for MAGR.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (17 studies in SR; 9 in MA) comparing mCAF + sCTG vs. alternative adjuncts (collagen matrix, xenogeneic ADM, PRF, EMD) for multiple adjacent gingival recession defects; mCAF+sCTG was superior for complete root coverage and keratinized tissue width increase.

Primary outcome meta-analysis: CRC — OR=1.70 (95% CI 1.18–2.44, p=0.004); ΔKTW — SMD=0.37 (95% CI 0.11–0.63, p=0.005) both favoring mCAF+sCTG; high-evidence superiority of sCTG over XADM for CRC (p=0.002) and ΔKTW (p=0.0001).

Long-term evidence remains limited; sCTG is the evidence-supported gold standard for MAGR, though EMD and double-blade-scalpel harvested sCTG show promise in early data.

## 세줄요약

다발성 인접 치은퇴축(MAGR) 치료에서 mCAF + sCTG vs 대체 재료(CM, XADM, PRF, EMD)를 비교한 SR+MA(SR 17편, MA 9편); mCAF+sCTG가 완전근면피개율(CRC) 및 각화치은폭(KTW) 증가에서 우위.

CRC: OR=1.70(95% CI 1.18–2.44, p=0.004); ΔKTW: SMD=0.37(95% CI 0.11–0.63, p=0.005) — sCTG>XADM: CRC(p=0.002), ΔKTW(p=0.0001) 고근거 수준 우위.

장기 근거는 제한적 — sCTG가 MAGR의 근거 기반 표준 치료이나 EMD 및 double-blade sCTG는 추가 연구 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA focused specifically on mCAF (envelope design) adjunctive techniques for MAGR.
- Establishes hierarchy: sCTG > XADM > CM > PRF for MAGR treatment.
- Highlights long-term evidence gap — only 1 RCT showed long-term stable CTG results.
- Identifies EMD and double-blade-scalpel sCTG as promising alternatives needing more data.

## 3. Methodology

- **Search**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Cinahl Complete (2013–2025)
- **Inclusion**: RCTs, ≥5 patients, ≥6-month follow-up, MAGR treatment, mCAF adjunctive techniques
- **Risk of bias**: RoB-2; GRADEPro GDT for evidence level assessment
- **MA software**: RevMan5.4

## 4. Key Results

**Included**: 17 studies in SR; 9 in MA (mCAF+sCTG vs alternatives)

**CRC (complete root coverage)**:
- mCAF+sCTG vs alternatives: OR=1.70 (95% CI 1.18–2.44, p=0.004) — HIGH evidence
- sCTG > XADM: p=0.002 — HIGH evidence
- sCTG > CM: ΔKTW p=0.0006 — LOW evidence

**ΔKTW (keratinized tissue width change)**:
- mCAF+sCTG vs alternatives: SMD=0.37 (95% CI 0.11–0.63, p=0.005) — HIGH evidence
- sCTG > XADM: p=0.0001 — HIGH evidence

**Root Coverage Esthetic Score (RES)**: No statistically significant differences between techniques

**Promising alternatives**: mCAF+EMD and mCAF+sCTG (double blade scalpel) — limited LOW evidence

## 5. Limitations

- Heterogeneity in follow-up periods and distribution of techniques
- Limited long-term data (only 1 RCT with long-term stable CTG results)
- Most evidence in short- and medium-term follow-up
- Not all mCAF technique combinations had sufficient data for MA

## 6. Glossary

- **mCAF**: Modified coronally advanced flap (envelope design)
- **MAGR**: Multiple adjacent gingival recession-type defects
- **sCTG**: Subepithelial connective tissue graft (gold standard donor tissue)
- **XADM**: Xenogeneic acellular dermal matrix
- **CM**: Collagen matrix
- **PRF**: Platelet-rich fibrin
- **EMD**: Enamel matrix derivatives
- **CRC**: Complete root coverage
- **ΔKTW**: Change in keratinized tissue width
- **RES**: Root coverage esthetic score
- **RCPPS**: Root coverage periodontal plastic surgery
