## Why Ingested
근관치료 성공률 향상 서베일런스. 비수술적 치료 실패 후 시행하는 근단 수술(periapical surgery)의 장기 성공률 및 예후 인자를 197개 치아·평균 45개월 추적으로 평가한 후향적 코호트. 전신질환이 근단 수술 성공에 미치는 영향을 처음으로 다변량 분석한 논문이며, 기존 위키 [[endodontics/he-2026-hemostatic-agents-periapical-surgery-sr-ma]]의 수술 관련 지식을 예후 관점으로 보완.

## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=197 teeth, mean follow-up 45.2 months) of endodontic surgery with CEM cement: 94.9% overall success; systemic disease was the sole significant predictor of failure (HR=5.87), with no significant tooth-related or lesion-related predictors.

## 한줄요약
후향적 코호트 (197개 치아, 평균 45.2개월 추적): CEM 시멘트 근단 수술 성공률 94.9%; 전신질환만이 유일한 유의 실패 예측 인자(HR=5.87, p=0.010) — 병소 크기·치관-근관 비율·근단 절제 길이는 유의하지 않음.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: BMC Oral Health, Volume 26, 2026
- DOI: 10.1186/s12903-026-07945-z
- PMC: PMC13037221
- Single endodontist, single tertiary center (Shahid Beheshti University, Tehran, Iran)
- Study period: 2011–2024 (13 years)
- STROBE-compliant retrospective cohort

## 2. Key Contributions
- 94.9% long-term success for CEM-based endodontic surgery — exceeds amalgam/IRM historical rates, comparable to MTA (91.6%)
- First multivariate demonstration that systemic disease (ASA ≥II) is the sole independent predictor
- Systemic disease: 14% failure rate vs 2.6% in healthy patients (HR=5.87)
- Lesion size, crown-root ratio, root-end preparation depth, tooth type, post presence: NOT independently significant
- Failure-free survival at 1/3/5 years: 96.4% / 92.7% / 87.3%

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Retrospective observational cohort (STROBE)
- Population: 197 teeth (169 patients), persistent AP unresponsive to prior orthograde therapy
- Inclusion: ≥6 months radiographic follow-up after endodontic surgery
- Surgical protocol: root-end resection + ultrasonic root-end preparation + CEM root-end fill (single endodontist)
- Outcome assessment: Rud/Molven classification (healed = success; diseased = failure)
- Statistical analysis: Kaplan-Meier survival; Cox proportional hazards regression (multivariate)
- Intra-observer agreement: excellent (κ=1.00; ICC=0.83)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Overall success:** **94.9%** (187/197 teeth)

**Failure-free survival:**
- 1 year: 96.4%
- 3 years: 92.7%
- 5 years: 87.3%

**Multivariate Cox regression — significant predictor:**
- Systemic disease (ASA ≥II): HR = **5.87** (95% CI: 1.52–22.68; p=0.010)
  - Systemic disease failure rate: 14% vs 2.6% (healthy)

**NOT significant predictors:**
- Lesion size (<5mm / 5–10mm / >10mm): p>0.05
- Crown-root ratio (≤1 vs >1): p>0.05
- Root-end preparation depth: p>0.05
- Tooth type (anterior/premolar/molar): p>0.05
- Intracanal post presence: p>0.05
- Age, sex, arch, smoking: p>0.05

**Mechanism (CEM):** Forms hydroxyapatite + releases Ca²⁺/PO₄³⁻ → osteoblastic differentiation + cementogenesis

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single center, single operator — limits external generalizability
- Retrospective design — selection bias possible
- Systemic disease coded categorically (ASA I vs II+) — dose-response unclear
- No control arm (e.g., NS retreatment comparison)
- Future need: prospective multicenter study, diabetes/immunocompromise subgroup analysis

## 7. Glossary
- CEM: Calcium-enriched mixture cement — bioceramic root-end filling material developed in Iran
- Endodontic surgery / Periapical surgery: Apicoectomy + root-end resection + root-end filling
- Rud/Molven classification: Standard outcome criteria for endodontic surgery (healed/diseased)
- ASA: American Society of Anesthesiologists physical status classification
- AGEs: Advanced glycation end-products — accumulate in hyperglycemia, reduce osteoblast activity
- MMP/TIMP: Matrix metalloproteinase/tissue inhibitor balance — altered in systemic inflammation
