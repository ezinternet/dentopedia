## Why Ingested

The llm-wiki [[orthodontics/clear-aligner]] coverage focuses on clinical efficacy, biomechanics, and patient-centred outcomes. This scoping review adds a uniquely absent dimension: the full environmental life-cycle burden of clear aligner therapy — plastic waste generation, microplastic release, greenhouse gas emissions, and sustainability strategies (circular economy, 3D printing, recycling). Directly relevant to growing global SDG-alignment pressures on orthodontic practice.

## Three-line Summary

This PRISMA-ScR scoping review synthesized 13 studies (2014–2024) on the environmental impact of clear aligner therapy (CAT), identifying five themes: materials/manufacturing, waste/recycling, microplastics, carbon emissions, and sustainability strategies. Over 25 million aligners are discarded annually; PET-G, TPU, and PP polymers persist in landfills up to 100 years, release microplastics during degradation, and generate greenhouse gases across the production-to-disposal life cycle; direct 3D printing (DLP) life-cycle assessment shows reduced material and energy burden compared to conventional thermoforming. Current recycling programs lack closed-loop capability; circular economy approaches and biodegradable polymers are proposed but constrained by limited infrastructure and insufficient high-quality LCA evidence.

## 세줄요약

이 범위 검토 (Scoping Review, SR)는 2014–2024년 사이 출판된 13편을 종합해 투명교정장치 치료 (Clear Aligner Therapy, CAT)의 환경 영향을 5가지 주제(재료·제조, 폐기물·재활용, 미세플라스틱, 탄소배출, 지속가능성 전략)로 정리했다. 매년 2,500만 개 이상의 교정장치가 폐기되며, 폴리에틸렌 테레프탈레이트 글리콜 (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol, PET-G)·열가소성 폴리우레탄 (Thermoplastic Polyurethane, TPU) 등 석유계 고분자는 매립 시 최대 100년 잔류하고 미세플라스틱 (Microplastics)·온실가스 (Greenhouse Gas, GHG)를 방출한다. 직접 3D 프린팅 (Digital Light Processing, DLP)이 열성형법보다 생애주기평가 (Life Cycle Assessment, LCA) 부담이 낮다는 초기 증거가 있으나 폐쇄루프 (closed-loop) 재활용 체계가 부재하고 고질적 증거 부족으로 지속가능한 교정 임상 전환에는 추가 연구가 필요하다.

## Key Contributions

1. First comprehensive PRISMA-ScR mapping of environmental evidence for clear aligner therapy across five sustainability themes.
2. Quantifies scale: >25 million aligners discarded annually; PET trays persist in landfills up to 100 years.
3. Documents LCA evidence that direct 3D printing (DLP) reduces raw material consumption, electricity, and waste vs conventional thermoforming.
4. Maps CAT environmental challenges to UN SDGs 3, 9, 12, and 13.
5. Identifies critical evidence gap: no standardized LCA methodology or comprehensive carbon-footprint studies for CAT.

## Methodology

- **Framework**: PRISMA extension for scoping reviews (PRISMA-ScR); Population-Concept-Context (PCC)
- **Population**: Patients undergoing clear aligner therapy
- **Concept**: Environmental sustainability (materials, manufacturing, waste, recycling, carbon emissions)
- **Context**: Orthodontic practice and the manufacture-use-disposal cycle of clear aligners
- **Screening**: Two independent reviewers at title/abstract and full-text stages; disagreements resolved by consensus
- **Data extraction**: Standardized form; descriptive thematic synthesis

## Key Results

### PRISMA flow
- Records identified: 293
- Duplicates removed: 137
- Screened: 156
- Sought for retrieval: 73
- Included: 13

### Five major themes

| Theme | Studies (n) | Main finding | Evidence gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Materials & manufacturing | 9 | Conventional thermoplastics (PET-G, PP, PC, TPU) dominate; 3D printing (DLP) and shape-memory polymers may reduce waste | Limited LCA evidence |
| Waste management & recycling | 8 | 4Rs widely recommended; >25M aligners/year discarded; PET persists 100 years in landfill | No closed-loop recycling; limited infrastructure |
| Microplastics | 6 | Polymer degradation → environmental microplastic contamination; landfill & incineration both problematic | No standardized quantification methods |
| Carbon emissions | 7 | Manufacturing, transport, disposal all contribute GHG; digital workflows may reduce footprint | Few comparative LCA/carbon-footprint studies |
| Sustainability strategies | 8 | Circular economy, digital workflows, biodegradable materials proposed | Limited real-world implementation and evaluation |

### Key data points
- Discarded aligners per year: **>25 million**
- Landfill persistence of PET trays: **up to 100 years**
- Incineration of PET-G releases **benzene** (carcinogen); polyurethane releases **tetrahydrofuran** (suspected carcinogen)
- DLP direct 3D printing: lower raw material consumption, electricity use, and manufacturing waste vs thermoforming (1 LCA study)
- Polymer Hazard Index for microplastic exposure in dental settings: **minor to medium** health risk
- Most common microplastic polymer found in dental units: **polyethylene terephthalate**

## Limitations

- English-language publications only; five databases only
- 13 studies with heterogeneous designs (mostly narrative reviews and laboratory investigations, not primary environmental assessments)
- Scoping methodology: no meta-analysis
- Inter-reviewer agreement statistics not calculated
