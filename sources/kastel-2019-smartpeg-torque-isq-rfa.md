## Three-line Summary

In vitro bovine rib study (30 XiVE S implants, D1 bone, 4 examiners) evaluating whether SmartPeg tightening torque affects ISQ readings; hand tightening (2–11 Ncm) and machine tightening (5 Ncm) produced no significant difference (mesial p=0.343, buccal p=0.890).

Per-Ncm torque increase caused only 0.09 (mesial) and 0.02 (buccal) ISQ decrease — clinically negligible; null hypothesis that finger tightening negatively impacts ISQ accuracy was rejected.

Manual SmartPeg tightening is clinically reliable regardless of examiner skill level; no torque-controlled device needed for routine RFA measurement.

## 세줄요약

체외 소늑골 연구 (XiVE S 30개, D1 골질, 술자 4명): 스마트팩 조임 토크가 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)에 미치는 영향 평가; 수동 조임 (2–11 Ncm)과 기계 조임 (5 Ncm) 간 유의차 없음 (근심 p=0.343, 협측 p=0.890).

Ncm당 조임 토크 증가에 따른 ISQ 감소는 근심 0.09, 협측 0.02에 불과 — 임상적으로 무시 가능; 손가락 조임이 ISQ 측정 정확도에 부정적 영향을 미친다는 근무가설은 기각됨.

수동 스마트팩 조임은 술자 숙련도에 관계없이 임상적으로 신뢰할 수 있음; 공명주파수분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) routine 측정에 별도의 토크 조절 장치 불필요.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: International Journal of Implant Dentistry 5:42 (2019)
- DOI: 10.1186/s40729-019-0195-1
- Design: In vitro, 3 fresh bovine ribs (D1 bone), 30 XiVE S implants (ø3.8 × 11 mm), 4 examiners
- Institution: Bad Dürkheim, Germany; University of Cologne; Goethe University Frankfurt

## 2. Key Contributions
- Rejected null hypothesis that finger tightening negatively impacts ISQ accuracy
- Established that SmartPeg tightening torque variation (2–11 Ncm hand vs 5 Ncm machine) does not significantly alter ISQ readings
- Validated manual SmartPeg placement across different examiner skill levels
- Demonstrated per-Ncm torque increase causes negligible ISQ change (0.09 mesial, 0.02 buccal)

## 3. Methodology
- **Implants**: 30 XiVE S self-tapping screw (ø3.8 × 11 mm, Dentsply Sirona), internal hexagon connection
- **Bone**: 3 fresh bovine ribs, D1 quality, same animal, stored airtight/humid/cool
- **Placement**: Single experienced surgeon (IK), standard protocol, all inserted at 50 Ncm
- **Test group**: 4 examiners (2F/2M, varying experience) hand-tightened SmartPeg Type 45
- **Control group**: SmartPegs mechanically tightened to 5 Ncm (Meg Torq device, Megagen)
- **Torque measurement**: Removal torque with BTG36N Analog Torque Meter (Tohnichi)
- **RFA**: Osstell IDx, mesial/buccal directions, probe 1 mm from SmartPeg at 90°
- **Statistics**: ANOVA, Pearson r, significance p<0.05

## 4. Key Results
| Parameter | Hand tightening (2–11 Ncm) | Machine tightening (5 Ncm) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mesial ISQ | 79.76 ± 2.11 | Similar | 0.343 (NS) |
| Buccal ISQ | 77.98 ± 2.84 | Similar | 0.890 (NS) |
| ISQ range | 71–85 | Similar | — |
- Per Ncm increase: ISQ decreased 0.09 (mesial), 0.02 (buccal)
- Full range (2–11 Ncm): ISQ decreased only 0.81 (mesial), 0.18 (buccal)
- 147 measurements (1 SmartPeg fractured)
- Buccal values consistently lower (thinner bone wall)
- All ISQ >70 (high primary stability)
- Removal torque: 79–93% of insertion torque

## 5. Limitations
- In vitro bovine rib — may not replicate in vivo soft tissue effects
- Single implant system (XiVE S, internal hexagon) — not transferable to other connections
- D1 bone only — does not address low-density bone
- Only SmartPeg Type 45 tested
- 1 SmartPeg fractured (supports manufacturer single-use recommendation)
