## Why Ingested

Symphysis (chin) bone harvest is a common autogenous graft source for implant sites, and the `bone-regeneration` folder has no source on the soft-tissue side of that procedure. This Gapski et al. 2001 review compares the three horizontal incision designs and the clinical findings that should guide the choice.

## Three-line Summary

Literature review (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2001) of incision design for symphysis graft harvesting — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The three horizontal incisions (sulcular, marginal, alveolar mucosal) are compared for advantages, disadvantages, indications and contraindications, with periodontal status, bone loss, keratinized gingiva and restorations named as selection factors.

The abstract argues that incision choice is often made on experience or habit rather than on interfacing factors; no outcome comparison is reported.

문헌 고찰 (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2001) — 턱끝 (symphysis) 골이식 채취를 위한 절개 설계 — 초록만 수집 (전문 미확보).

세 가지 수평 절개 (sulcular, marginal, alveolar mucosal) 를 장단점·적응증·금기증 측면에서 비교하며, 치주 상태, 골소실, 각화치은, 치은연 수복물을 선택 요인으로 제시.
