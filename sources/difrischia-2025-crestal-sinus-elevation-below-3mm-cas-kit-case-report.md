## Why Ingested

Ceruso 2025([[sinus-lift/transcrestal/ceruso-2025-crestal-hydraulic-sinus-lift-cas-kit-retrospective]])의 최소 RBH 2mm 확장 데이터를 개별 케이스 수준에서 뒷받침. 이 논문은 RBH 3.6mm/2.5mm라는 **기존 금기 수준** 케이스에서 CAS-Kit 수압법의 단계별 술식(0.5cc 분할 주입, 임시 sealant 전략)을 상세 기록한다.

## One-line Summary

Case report (1 patient, 2 implants, 68F): CAS-Kit hydraulic TSFE at RBH 3.6/2.5mm achieved 14.1/11.4mm bone gain, 30 Ncm torque, and stable bone at 3-year follow-up with no complications.

## 한줄요약

케이스 리포트(68세 여성, RBH 3.6/2.5mm): CAS-Kit 수압식 TSFE → 골이득 14.1/11.4mm, 삽입토크 30 Ncm, 3년 안정 — 기존 최소 RBH 기준(4mm) 이하 케이스의 가능성 제시.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Documents CAS-Kit TSFE success at **RBH 2.5mm** — below the widely cited 3mm minimum and the 4mm general threshold.
- Detailed step-by-step dual-site hydraulic injection protocol with **temporary sealing** of first site during second-site injection to prevent pressure leak.
- Explains high torque (30 Ncm) despite minimal bone via three mechanisms: (1) favorable anatomy with medial/distal bone; (2) under-preparation of implant site; (3) tapered macro-texture implant.
- 3-year radiographic stability confirmed (bone height maintained).
- No intraoperative/postoperative complications; Valsalva maneuver confirmed membrane integrity.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Patient**: 68-year-old female, non-smoker, systemically healthy, missing teeth 1.6 and 1.7.
- **Preoperative imaging**: OPT + CBCT; RBH 3.6mm (site 1.6) and 2.5mm (site 1.7); no sinus pathology.
- **Implants**: PRAMA (Sweden & Martina), 3.8mm × 10mm, tissue-level, ZirTi/BOPT design.
- **Graft**: Bio-Oss 0.25–1mm, 0.5cc per site (total 1cc).
- **Hydraulic injection**: Sterile saline, 0.5cc increments; total 2.0cc (both sites) + additional 1.0cc at site 1.7.
- **Torque**: ~30 Ncm both implants.
- **Prosthetics**: Screw-retained PFM single crowns at 3 months post-surgery.
- **Follow-up**: 3 years (bone height stable by panoramic).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Site 1.6 | Site 1.7 |
|---|---|---|
| Preoperative RBH | 3.6 mm | 2.5 mm |
| Postoperative bone height | 17.8 mm | 13.8 mm |
| Bone gain | **14.1 mm** | **11.4 mm** |
| Insertion torque | ~30 Ncm | ~30 Ncm |
| Intraoperative complications | None | None |
| 3-year radiographic stability | Stable | Stable |

- Saline injection: 0.5cc lifts membrane ~3mm; stepwise up to 2.5cc total for complete detachment.
- Valsalva test confirmed membrane integrity post-elevation.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **n=1**: Cannot support clinical guidelines; only proof-of-concept.
- **Panoramic only for follow-up**: volumetric bone measurement inaccurate.
- **Highly selected patient**: favorable anatomy, non-smoker, healthy — not generalizable to high-risk profiles.
- **Single operator**: reproducibility unknown.
- Future: prospective cohort with systematic RBH <3mm cases to define selection criteria.

## 6. Related Work

- **Ceruso 2025**: retrospective series with same CAS-Kit + PRAMA implant protocol; n=15/20 implants (RBH down to 2mm).

## 7. Glossary

- **Valsalva maneuver**: patient forced expiration against closed glottis — if membrane perforated, air exits nose confirming perforation
- **Under-preparation**: final implant diameter larger than last drill diameter → compresses bone laterally for higher primary stability
- **Tenting effect**: implant apex displacing graft upward, maintaining space under elevated membrane
- **UTM surface**: Ultra-low surface roughness microtextured surface on PRAMA implant convergent neck (soft tissue integration)
