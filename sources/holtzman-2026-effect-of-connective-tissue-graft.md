## Why Ingested

The held reconstructive peri-implantitis RCTs ([[implants/peri-implantitis/soldini-2025-reconstructive-therapy-peri-implantitis-rct]], [[implants/peri-implantitis/hakkers-2026-reconstructive-peri-implantitis-3wall-4wall-rct]]) and the GBR network meta-analysis ([[implants/peri-implantitis/ramanauskaite-2023-guided-bone-regeneration-periimplantitis-defects-nma]]) all test reconstruction against non-reconstruction, and none asks whether a soft-tissue adjunct improves the reconstructive procedure itself. This is, per its authors, the first RCT of reconstructive peri-implantitis treatment with vs without a subepithelial connective tissue graft (sCTG), and it links the keratinized-mucosa risk-factor literature ([[implants/peri-implantitis/mahardawi-2023-lack-keratinized-mucosa-peri-implantitis-sr-ma]]) to a treatment-stage intervention.

## Three-line Summary

Dual-center RCT (Rome, Italy and Belgrade, Serbia; n=32 patients, 16 per arm; 12-month follow-up, no dropouts) comparing GBR plus subepithelial connective tissue graft (test) vs GBR alone (control) for surgical treatment of peri-implantitis, stratified by baseline keratinized mucosa (KM <2 mm vs ≥2 mm).

At 12 months CAL gain was greater with CTG (3.21 ± 1.57 mm vs 1.65 ± 1.28 mm; p = 0.022; primary outcome) and keratinized mucosa width gain was greater (2.25 ± 2.89 mm vs 0.26 ± 1.49 mm; p = 0.010); PPD reduction favored CTG but did not reach significance (3.25 ± 1.59 vs 1.97 ± 1.23 mm; p = 0.052); disease resolution was not different (EFP criteria 62.5% vs 56.3%, p = 0.719).

CTG adds clinical attachment and keratinized mucosa gain but did not change the binary disease-resolution endpoint at 1 year; with n=32, a single 12-month follow-up, and no histology, whether the soft-tissue benefit translates into long-term implant health remains unproven.

## 세줄요약

이중 기관 무작위대조시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)(이탈리아 로마·세르비아 베오그라드; 환자 32명, 군당 16명; 12개월 추적, 탈락 없음): 임플란트주위염 수술치료에서 골유도재생술(Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) + 상피하 결합조직이식(subepithelial Connective Tissue Graft, CTG)(시험군) vs GBR 단독(대조군) 비교, 기저 각화점막(Keratinized Mucosa, KM) <2 mm / ≥2 mm로 층화.

12개월 임상부착수준(Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득량은 CTG군이 더 컸고(3.21 ± 1.57 mm vs 1.65 ± 1.28 mm; p = 0.022; 일차 결과), 각화점막 폭(KMW) 증가도 더 컸다(2.25 ± 2.89 mm vs 0.26 ± 1.49 mm; p = 0.010). 탐침깊이(Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 감소는 CTG군에서 더 컸으나 유의하지 않았고(3.25 ± 1.59 vs 1.97 ± 1.23 mm; p = 0.052), 질환 해소율은 차이가 없었다(EFP 기준 62.5% vs 56.3%, p = 0.719).

CTG는 부착 획득과 각화점막 증가를 더하지만 1년 시점 이진 질환 해소 지표는 바꾸지 못했다. n=32, 12개월 추적 1회, 조직학 부재로 연조직 이점이 장기 임플란트 건강으로 이어지는지는 입증되지 않았다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT (per the authors) of reconstructive peri-implantitis treatment with vs without a subepithelial connective tissue graft.
- CTG added to GBR increased CAL gain and keratinized mucosa width gain at 12 months but did not change disease resolution.
- Stratified randomization by baseline KM (<2 mm vs ≥2 mm) with no group-by-stratum interaction on disease resolution.
- Reports both EFP and Derks composite disease-resolution definitions on the same cohort.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Multicenter (two centers), parallel-group, 1:1 RCT, CONSORT 2025 reporting; stratified randomization (KM <2 mm vs ≥2 mm) with concealed allocation revealed only after flap reflection; blinded clinical and radiographic examiners.
- **n**: 32 patients (16 test, 16 control), all completed 12 months. Mean age 56.9 ± 12.3 years; 9 smokers (28.1%); implants in function 2.19 ± 0.82 years.
- **Inclusion**: At least one implant with PPD ≥6 mm plus profuse BoP/suppuration and radiographic bone loss beyond early remodeling; loaded ≥1 year. Exclusions included smoking >20 cigarettes/day, uncontrolled diabetes (HbA1c ≥7%), implants with ≥60% bone loss or mobility, previously treated implants, FMBS >25%, FMPS >20%, and purely supraosseous defects.
- **Control (GBR)**: full-thickness flap, degranulation with titanium curettes, titanium brush, erythritol air-polishing (0.3%), saline irrigation; collagenated deproteinized bovine bone mineral block (Bio-Oss Collagen) and resorbable membrane (Biogide); coronally advanced flap.
- **Test (GBR + CTG)**: same protocol plus an extraorally de-epithelialized palatal graft (~1.5 mm thick) sutured 1 mm apical to the buccal flap margin with two horizontal mattress sutures; no "poncho" occlusal grafting.
- **Perioperative care**: amoxicillin-clavulanic acid 1 g twice daily (clindamycin if penicillin-allergic) starting 3 days before surgery for 10 days total; 0.2% chlorhexidine rinses for 2 weeks; maintenance every 3 months.
- **Outcomes**: primary CAL at 12 months; secondary PPD, BoP, plaque index, KMW, mucosal thickness (MT), buccal recession (REC), suppuration, radiographic marginal bone level (baseline and 12 months), defect morphology (Monje 2019), disease resolution (EFP composite and Derks composite), early wound healing index, surgical time.
- **Statistics**: nonparametric (Mann-Whitney U, Brunner-Langer model, Wilcoxon with Bonferroni); sample size 12 per group for 80% power, 16 planned for 30% dropout.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome (change T0 to T2, 12 months) | CTG + GBR | GBR alone | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAL gain (mm) | 3.21 ± 1.57 | 1.65 ± 1.28 | 0.022 |
| PPD reduction (mm) | 3.25 ± 1.59 | 1.97 ± 1.23 | 0.052 |
| KMW change (mm) | 2.25 ± 2.89 | 0.26 ± 1.49 | 0.010 |
| Mean bone level, baseline (mm) | 3.72 ± 1.49 | 2.92 ± 1.30 | 0.188 (between groups) |
| Mean bone level, 12 months (mm) | 1.05 ± 0.90 | 1.63 ± 1.19 | 0.320 (between groups) |
| Disease resolution, EFP criteria | 62.5% | 56.3% | 0.719 (OR 1.30, 95% CI 0.32-5.33) |
| Disease resolution, Derks criteria | 62.5% | 50.0% | 0.477 (OR 1.67, 95% CI 0.41-6.82) |
| Surgical time, mean (min) | 68.5 ± 26.0 | 60.3 ± 15.8 | not significant overall |

- Both groups improved significantly over time (CAL, PPD, BoP, suppuration, radiographic bone level; each p < 0.001 for the overall time effect in the text); most improvement occurred by 6 months and was stable to 12 months.
- Bone level: both groups improved significantly (test p < 0.001, control p = 0.001) with a significant time-by-group interaction (p < 0.001), but neither the baseline nor the 12-month between-group comparison was significant.
- KMW: the control group showed no significant change; the test group rose from T0 to T1 (p = 0.012) and again to T2 (p = 0.009), but the between-group comparison of absolute KMW at T2 was not significant (p = 0.915).
- Buccal recession stayed below 1 mm in both groups with no between-group difference; mucosal thickness rose in the test group at 6 months (p = 0.027) then fell by 12 months (p = 0.012), with no between-group difference at any time.
- Stratified disease resolution: EFP 57.1% vs 50.0% (KM <2 mm) and 66.7% vs 62.5% (KM ≥2 mm); Derks 57.1% vs 37.5% (KM <2 mm; OR 2.22, p = 0.450) and 66.7% vs 62.5% (KM ≥2 mm); group-by-stratum interactions not significant (p = 0.942 EFP, p = 0.674 Derks).
- Defects were mostly Monje class IIIc, followed by IIIb and Ic, distributed evenly between groups (p = 0.697); defect class was not associated with disease resolution.
- Early healing: EHI scores did not differ between groups at 1 or 2 weeks; no suppuration, necrosis, or membrane exposure was recorded. In the KM <2 mm stratum, surgery took significantly longer with CTG (p = 0.040).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=32) and 12-month follow-up only; the 24-month follow-up is planned for a later publication.
- The PPD difference narrowly missed significance (p = 0.052); the authors argue it may be underpowered, whereas the disease-resolution difference (62.5% vs 56.3%) is small enough that more patients are unlikely to change it.
- No histology to confirm the nature of the gained keratinized tissue; KMW was measured by roll technique without iodine staining.
- Surgeon (unblinded) scored early healing; groups were not powered for the disease-resolution endpoint, and intra-text and table values for some secondary outcomes are not fully consistent (for example, the control-group CAL T0-T2 p-value is given as 0.048 in a table and p < 0.001 in the text).
- Graft was positioned apically on the buccal flap; a poncho configuration may behave differently for recession.
- Superimposition of STL and DICOM data was not available to track thickness change.

## 6. Related Work
- Soldini 2025 and Hakkers 2026: reconstruction vs non-reconstruction RCTs with disease resolution of about 44% in both arms; this trial's GBR-only control arm reached 56.3% (EFP) and 50.0% (Derks), with milder baseline disease than Derks 2022.
- Ramanauskaite 2023 (GBR NMA): ranks reconstructive strategies; does not include a soft-tissue adjunct comparison.
- Mahardawi 2023 SR/MA: lack of KM as a peri-implantitis risk factor, the rationale for augmenting KM at the time of treatment.
- Derks 2022, Monje 2022, Schwarz 2010/2014: cited by the authors as comparators for resolution rates and CTG with reconstruction (not held in the wiki).

## 7. Glossary
- **CAL**: clinical attachment level, measured from the prosthetic margin to the pocket bottom.
- **sCTG**: subepithelial connective tissue graft harvested from the palate.
- **KMW**: keratinized mucosa width (mm), measured by the roll technique.
- **DBBM-C**: collagenated deproteinized bovine bone mineral.
- **EFP / Derks composite**: two definitions of disease resolution (EFP: BoP ≤1 site, no suppuration, PPD ≤5 mm, no progressive bone loss; Derks: implant retained, no BoP/suppuration, PPD <5 mm, recession ≤1 mm).
