## Three-line Summary

Korean MOHW ordinance (공포·시행 2026-07-09) under Medical Service Act §37, plus the KDCA notice (2025-11-24), governing radiation staff (방사선 관계 종사자) at clinics operating diagnostic X-ray devices including dental intraoral, panoramic and dental CT units.

Staff limits (Annex 3): effective dose ≤50 mSv/year and ≤100 mSv per 5 years, lens 150 mSv/y, skin/hands/feet 500 mSv/y; TLD badge read at least every 3 months; health exam every 2 years and before first duty; KDCA issues a caution notice when a TLD quarter exceeds 5 mSv.

Neither the ordinance, its Annex 3, nor the KDCA notice contains any pregnancy-specific dose limit (full-text search, 0 hits for 임신) — pregnant dental staff fall under the general limit unless the clinic adopts a stricter internal target.

## 세줄요약

보건복지부령(2026-07-09 시행), 의료법 제37조 근거 — 치과 구내·파노라마·치과용 CT를 포함한 진단용 방사선 발생장치를 쓰는 의료기관의 방사선 관계 종사자 관리 규정(+질병관리청 「진단용 방사선 안전관리 규정」 고시).

별표 3 선량한도: 유효선량 연 50 mSv·5년 100 mSv, 수정체 연 150, 피부·손·발 연 500 mSv. TLD는 3개월마다 1회 이상 측정, 건강진단 2년마다(첫 종사 전 포함). TLD 분기 5 mSv 초과 시 질병관리청 주의통보.

규칙·별표 3·고시 어디에도 임신 종사자 전용 선량한도가 없다(전문 검색 "임신" 0건) — 치과 임신 직원은 일반 한도를 적용받으며, 더 엄격한 기준은 원내 목표로 정해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Korean legal annual effective-dose limit for diagnostic radiation staff is 50 mSv/year (≤100 mSv/5 y) — numerically different from the EU 20 mSv/year cited in Horner 2004.
- No pregnancy-specific provision in the ordinance, Annex 3, or the KDCA notice (verified by full-text search).
- Article 18 exemption: clinics using only intraoral dental X-ray units (panoramic and cephalometric units excluded) with ≤10 mA·min/week total workload are exempt from badge dosimetry, radiation area, safety officer and health-exam articles — a clinic with a panoramic or dental CT unit is not exempt.
- Safety measures for over-limit staff are explicitly "change of workplace or shortened working hours".

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Item | Provision |
|---|---|
| Effective dose limit | ≤50 mSv/year and ≤100 mSv/5 years (Annex 3) |
| Lens equivalent dose | ≤150 mSv/year |
| Skin, hands, feet | ≤500 mSv/year |
| Pregnancy-specific limit | **None** |
| Dosimeter wear | Mandatory during any exposure-risk work (§12-4) |
| TLD read interval | ≥1 per 3 months (film: monthly) (§4-5) |
| Health examination | Every 2 years + before first duty (§13) |
| Caution notice | TLD >5 mSv per quarter (KDCA notice §20) |
| Field investigation | >20 mSv/quarter, >50 mSv/year, or >100 mSv/5 y (KDCA notice §23) |
| Radiation area | ≥0.3 mSv/week, partitioned, signed, public entry restricted (§2-5, §9) |
| Over-limit measure | Health exam; change of workplace or shorter hours (§8-3) |
| Exemption | Intraoral-only (no pano/ceph) ≤10 mA·min/week (§18) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Extract covers staff-dose articles only; registration, inspection-agency and fee articles omitted. Annex 3 last amended 2010-01-22. The rule is a legal floor, not a statement of biological risk.
