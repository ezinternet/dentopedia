## Why Ingested
핵심 기초 수치 논문: 구강점막 상피세포 (Oral Mucosal Epithelial Cell) 표면층이 얼마나 빨리 교체되는지 측정. 기존 위키 [[periodontics/drukala-2005-comparison-of-proliferation-and-motile]]에서 구강 각질형성세포 (Oral Keratinocyte)의 증식·이동 능력을 다루는데, Dawes 2003은 그 생리적 기저 속도를 타액 분석으로 독립적으로 추정한 foundational paper.

## One-line Summary
Cross-sectional salivary epithelial cell count study (n=17) estimating oral mucosal surface layer turnover every 2.7 h and ~1.58×10⁹ bacteria in an edentulous mouth.

## 한줄요약
타액 내 상피세포 수를 Coulter Counter로 계측한 관찰 연구(n=17): 구강점막 표면층은 매 2.7시간마다 교체되고, 무치악 구강 내 세균 수는 약 15.8억 개.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Archives of Oral Biology, Vol. 48, No. 5, pp. 329–336, May 2003
- Institution: Department of Oral Biology, University of Manitoba, Winnipeg, Canada
- Funding: Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't

## 2. Key Contributions
- First quantitative estimate of oral mucosal epithelial turnover time using salivary cell counts: **2.7 h/surface layer**
- Derived bacterial load in edentulous mouth: ~1.58 × 10⁹ bacteria
- Mean bacterial division time: 1.38 h under steady-state conditions
- Demonstrated the salivary phase can act as bacterial continuous culture only for fast-growing organisms (<0.04 mL/min flow rate threshold)
- Established a quantitative framework linking epithelial shedding rate to oral microbial ecology

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Cross-sectional, observational
- Subjects: 17 adults; bacterial counts obtained from 10 subjects
- Method: Drooled saliva collected → epithelial cells/mL counted in Coulter Counter → adherent bacteria/cell counted (Toluidine Blue staining) → bacterial counts on Blood Agar (anaerobic, 5 days, Reduced Transport Fluid)
- Calculation: Known oral mucosal surface area + individual epithelial cell area + salivary loss rate → surface layer replacement time; Known cell count × bacteria/cell + swallowing rate → total bacterial load

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Oral mucosal surface layer turnover: every 2.7 h** (steady-state calculation)
- Total bacteria in edentulous mouth: ~1.58 × 10⁹
- Mean inter-bacterial-division time: 1.38 h
- Threshold for continuous culture for fast-growers: flow rate <0.04 mL/min with residual volume 0.8 mL and maximum division rate 3 h⁻¹

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Calculation relies on assumed oral mucosal surface area — subject to inter-individual variation
- Only edentulous mouths studied; dentate mouth adds tooth/sulcular surfaces with different shedding dynamics
- Steady-state assumption may not hold during disease, inflammation, or xerostomia

## 6. Related Work
- Drukala 2005 — proliferation/motility of oral vs skin keratinocytes
- Glim 2013 — oral mucosal wound healing comparison (oral faster than dermal)

## 7. Glossary
- **Coulter Counter**: particle sizing instrument measuring cell count and volume
- **Reduced Transport Fluid (RTF)**: anaerobic transport medium preserving viable bacteria
- **Turnover time**: interval for complete replacement of the surface epithelial cell layer
