## Why Ingested

유지장치 착용 순응도에 대한 오디오비주얼+리마인더 개입의 효과를 평가한 RCT로, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/chong-2025-patient-experiences-cat-scoping-review]]가 지적한 환자 순응도 문제에 유지단계 개입 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

This RCT with 52 participants assessed audiovisual instructions with weekly electronic reminders vs verbal instructions alone on Hawley retainer wear compliance over 6 months. Mean daily wear time was 14.9h (±4.9) at T1 and 14.3h (±5.4) at T2; the audiovisual group showed significantly better compliance at 6 months (p=0.033) but not at 3 months (p=0.065). No significant differences in gingival or plaque index scores were found between groups.

## 세줄요약

52명 참가자를 대상으로 오디오비주얼 지시+주간 전자 알림 대 구두 지시 alone의 Hawley 유지장치 착용 순응도를 6개월간 평가한 RCT. 일일 평균 착용시간은 T1에서 14.9시간(±4.9), T2에서 14.3시간(±5.4); 오디오비주얼 그룹이 6개월 시점에서 유의하게 높은 순응도(p=0.033)를 보였으나 3개월 시점에서는 NS(p=0.065). 치은·플라크 지수는 두 그룹 간 유의한 차이 없음.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Scientific Reports 2023;13:8543
- **DOI**: 10.1038/s41598-023-35686-4
- **Institution**: Zhengzhou University, University of Otago, University of Pittsburgh

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT to combine audiovisual integration with weekly electronic reminders for retainer compliance
- Demonstrates long-term (6-month) effectiveness of multimodal behavioral intervention
- Objectively measured wear time using TheraMon microsensor

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial
- **n**: 52 participants (mean age 26.1y)
- **Outcomes**: Retainer wear time (TheraMon), gingival index, plaque index, patient experience

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Mean wear time at T1 (3mo): 14.9 ± 4.9 h/day
- Mean wear time at T2 (6mo): 14.3 ± 5.4 h/day
- 3mo compliance: NS between groups (p=0.065)
- 6mo compliance: audiovisual group significantly better (p=0.033)
- Gingival index: NS (p=0.165)
- Plaque index: NS (p=0.173)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Self-reporting bias potential despite objective sensor measurement
- Single-center study
- Cultural factors may influence compliance generalizability
- Long-term retention effects beyond 6 months unknown

## 7. Glossary
- **TheraMon**: Microsensor-equipped retainer for objective wear-time measurement
- **Hawley Retainer**: Wire-and-acrylic removable retention appliance
- **Audiovisual Integration**: Multimodal (auditory + visual) information delivery method
- **Hawthorne Effect**: Behavior change due to awareness of being observed
