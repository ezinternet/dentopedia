## Why Ingested

First focused review of the peri-implant bone response to excessive occlusal load (search window 1991–2011) — the historical anchor for the wiki's occlusal-overload cluster. Its bottom line ("evidence is poor, causation unclear, biologically-acceptable limit unknown") is the 2013 baseline that the newer systematic reviews [[occlusion/mojaver-2025-occlusal-overload-peri-implant-health-sr]] (80-study synthesis, association now quantified at 0.65–3.0 mm MBL) and [[occlusion/di-fiore-2022-periimplant-bone-loss-overload-occlusal-analysis]] (7 clinical studies, no standardized overload threshold) extend — and it qualifies the firmer pro-correlation stance of [[occlusion/misch-2005-occlusal-trauma-peri-implant-bone-loss-literature]].

## Three-line Summary

Literature review of English-language Medline and Wiley Online Library papers (January 1991–December 2011) reporting peri-implant bone conditions under excessive occlusal loading with a controlled oral hygiene regime.

Evidence on the peri-implant bone response to overload was limited and of poor quality, with conflicting animal studies and an unknown biologically-acceptable load limit, but consistently higher peri-implant bone remodeling activity around implants subjected to high loading forces.

Whether overload causes marginal bone loss or total loss of osseointegration in already osseointegrated implants therefore remains unproven — occlusal force management rests on biomechanical rationale, not an evidence-based threshold (abstract-only source; full text not retrieved).

## 세줄요약

문헌고찰 (literature review) — Medline·Wiley Online Library 영문 문헌(1991–2011) 중 구강위생 조절 하에 과도한 교합부하 (excessive occlusal load)를 받은 임플란트 주위 골 조건을 보고한 실험·고찰 연구를 검토.

교합 과부하 (occlusal overload)에 대한 주위골 (peri-implant bone) 반응 근거는 수준이 낮고 동물연구 결과가 상충하며 생물학적 허용 한계는 미상이나, 고부하 임플란트 주위에서 골재형성 (bone remodeling) 활성 증가는 일관되게 관찰.

이미 골결합 (osseointegration)된 임플란트에서 과부하가 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)이나 골결합 상실을 초래하는지는 입증되지 않음 — 임플란트 교합 조정은 근거 기반 임계값이 아닌 생체역학적 근거에 의존 (초록 전용 원문, 전문 미확보).

## 2. Key Contributions
- First review dedicated to the peri-implant bone response of *already osseointegrated* implants under excessive occlusal load, covering 1991–2011.
- Concludes the evidence base is limited and of poor quality, with animal experimental studies giving conflicting results.
- Names the unknown "biologically-acceptable" occlusal load limit as the central knowledge gap for overload causation.
- Reports consistently higher remodeling activity of peri-implant bone around implants subjected to high loading forces.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Literature (narrative) review
- **Databases**: Medline, Wiley Online Library
- **Search window**: January 1991 – December 2011, English language
- **Inclusion criteria**: Experimental or review papers reporting peri-implant bone conditions of dental implants submitted to excessive occlusal loading in the presence of a controlled oral hygiene regime
- **n**: number of included studies not stated in the available abstract
- **Outcomes**: peri-implant bone response — marginal bone loss, loss of osseointegration, bone remodeling activity

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only source (PubMed HTML printout) — included-study count, per-study details, and tables could not be retrieved.
- Search window ends December 2011: predates modern quantitative/digital occlusal analysis studies and the 2022/2025 systematic reviews now held in the wiki.
- Heterogeneous and conflicting animal experimental evidence precludes causal inference; identifying the biological overload threshold is stated as future work.

## 6. Related Work
- mojaver-2025: newer systematic review (narrative SR, 80 studies) that extends this review's "limited evidence" baseline with a quantified overload–MBL association (0.65–3.0 mm).
- di-fiore-2022: newer systematic review restricting evidence to quantitative occlusal-analysis studies; still finds no standardized overload threshold — consistent with this review's "limit unknown".
- misch-2005: earlier narrative review arguing a positive correlation between occlusal trauma and peri-implant bone loss; this review qualifies that stance as unproven.
- podaropoulos-2016 / podaropoulos-2020: dog overloading models of the type whose conflicting results this review reports.

## 7. Glossary
- **Occlusal overload (교합 과부하)**: occlusal forces exceeding what the implant–bone unit physiologically accommodates.
- **Osseointegration (골결합)**: direct structural and functional contact between living bone and an implant surface.
- **Marginal bone loss (MBL, 변연골소실)**: resorption of crestal bone around an implant neck.
- **Peri-implant bone remodeling (주위골 골재형성)**: cycles of bone resorption and formation in response to mechanical load.
- **Biologically-acceptable limit**: the load threshold above which bone breakdown is hypothesized to begin — explicitly unknown in this review.
