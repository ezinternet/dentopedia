## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (66 studies, 2008-2025): zirconia SC survival 92.9–98.7% at ≤10yr; IRFPD 89.0% at 10yr; CFPD only 50–52% at 9-13yr — highest complication burden

APA+MDP surface treatment yielded superior short-term SC (98.6%) and IRFPD survival (98.1%); debonding and chipping are dominant mechanical complications

GRADE certainty: very low — findings are directional signals, not definitive benchmarks

## 2. Key Contributions
- Most comprehensive recent SR+MA covering all zirconia restoration types in natural teeth
- IRFPD 10yr survival 89%, success 70.3% — acceptable but lower than SC/RBFPD
- CFPD should be used with caution (50–52% survival, high complication rates)
- APA+MDP surface treatment protocol: SC survival 98.6%/success 97.7%; IRFPD survival 98.1%/success 70.1%
- Chipping and debonding are leading mechanical complications; secondary caries and pulpitis are biological

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Electronic search: PubMed, Cochrane, Embase, Web of Science (up to Jan 2026)
- Inclusion: clinical studies ≥1-year follow-up, zirconia restorations in natural teeth
- 66 studies included (2008-2025)
- Risk of bias: RoB 2, Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS), Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI)
- Evidence certainty: GRADE approach
- Outcomes: survival, success, biological/mechanical complication rates

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Survival rates (pooled)
| Restoration | Survival | Timepoint |
|---|---|---|
| SC | 92.9–98.7% | ≤10 yr |
| FPD | 77.6% | 10–13 yr |
| RBFPD | 97.4% | 10–15 yr |
| IRFPD | 89.0% | 10 yr |
| CFPD | 50.0–52.6% | 9–13 yr |

### Success rates (pooled)
| Restoration | Success | Timepoint |
|---|---|---|
| SC | 46.1% | 10 yr |
| FPD | 40.1% | 10–13 yr |
| RBFPD | 84.3% | 10–15 yr |
| IRFPD | 70.3% | 10 yr |
| CFPD | 12.5–22.6% | 9–13 yr |

### Complications at 10yr
- SC: biological 12.1%, mechanical 53.9%
- FPD: biological 19.4%, mechanical 46.7%
- RBFPD: biological 3.3%, mechanical 8.0% (best profile)
- IRFPD: biological 21.4%, mechanical 14.3% (at 3yr only)
- CFPD: biological 50.0%, mechanical 37.5% (at 9yr)

### APA+MDP surface treatment
- SC: survival 98.6%, success 97.7%
- IRFPD: survival 98.1%, success 70.1%

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- GRADE: very low certainty for all restoration types
- Heterogeneity across studies
- Limited long-term data for IRFPD
- Predominant risk: low-to-moderate bias (RoB 2, NOS, JBI)

## 7. Glossary
- SC: Single Crown
- FPD: Fixed Dental Prosthesis (conventional 3-unit bridge)
- RBFPD: Resin-Bonded Fixed Dental Prosthesis (Maryland bridge)
- IRFPD: Inlay-Retained Fixed Dental Prosthesis
- CFPD: Cantilever Fixed Dental Prosthesis
- APA: Airborne-Particle Abrasion (sandblasting)
- MDP: 10-methacryloyloxydecyl dihydrogen phosphate (bonding monomer)
- GRADE: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation
