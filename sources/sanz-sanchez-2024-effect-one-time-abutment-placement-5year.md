## Why Ingested

Sanz-Sánchez 2024는 Abayov 2026 SR+MA 포함 7편 중 유일한 5년 추적 RCT다. Ríos-Santos 2020 1년 결과(PMID 31955495, 같은 연구팀)의 장기 연장 보고서이며, 위키에 이미 있는 [[implants/mbl/rios-santos-2020-one-abutment-one-time-rct]] 페이지를 5년으로 갱신하는 역할을 한다. 장기 통계(DIB 0.53 vs 0.97mm; p=0.063)는 SR+MA에서는 NS이지만 임상적 경향으로서 중요하다.

## Three-line Summary

5-year follow-up of a parallel RCT (60 implants, 30 patients) comparing OAOT vs conventional repeated-abutment protocol in bone-level platform-switched implants in the posterior region; peri-implant bone level (DIB) change from surgery to 5 years was the primary outcome.

Mean distance implant shoulder to bone (DIB) at 5 years: 0.53 mm (OAOT) vs 0.97 mm (conventional; p = 0.063); implant survival 100% in both groups; soft tissue parameters (probing depth, BOP, KT width) comparable between groups at 5 years.

No statistically significant 5-year bone preservation benefit for OAOT, though numerical trend favored OAOT by 0.44 mm; this is the longest follow-up OAOT RCT included in Abayov 2026 SR+MA and provides the only long-term data point for the pooled estimate.

## 세줄요약

5년 추적 평행 무작위 대조 임상시험 (임플란트 60개, 환자 30명): 1어버트먼트-1회(OAOT, One-Abutment-One-Time) vs 반복 탈착 프로토콜, 골수준 플랫폼 스위칭(PS) 임플란트; 주요 결과: 임플란트 어깨부터 골까지 거리(DIB, Distance Implant shoulder to Bone) 변화.

5년 DIB: OAOT 0.53mm vs 대조군 0.97mm (p=0.063; NS); 임플란트 생존율 양군 100%; 탐침 깊이·출혈·각화치은 폭 5년 시 그룹 간 차이 없음.

5년에서 OAOT의 통계적 유의 골보존 효과 미확인; 수치적 경향(0.44mm 차이)은 OAOT 방향; Abayov 2026 SR+MA의 유일한 5년 데이터 포인트.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Longest follow-up OAOT RCT in literature (5 years)
- Extension of Ríos-Santos 2020 1-year results (same cohort)
- DIB at 5yr: OAOT 0.53 mm vs 0.97 mm conventional (Δ = 0.44 mm; p = 0.063, NS)
- 100% implant survival in both groups at 5 years
- Soft tissue stability: PD, BOP, KT width comparable at 5 years
- Only long-term anchor for Abayov 2026 SR+MA pooled effect

## 3. Methodology
- Design: parallel RCT; Complutense University of Madrid
- Population: 30 patients (60 implants; posterior region; partially edentulous)
- Implants: bone-level PS implants; Ankylos C/X (Dentsply Sirona)
- OAOT: definitive abutment at surgery; never removed at 5yr
- Control: repeated abutment disconnections at surgery, impression, delivery
- Primary outcome: DIB (distance from implant shoulder to bone contact), T0→5yr
- Secondary: PD, BOP, KT width, recession, implant survival

## 4. Key Results
- DIB at 5 years: 0.53 mm (OAOT) vs 0.97 mm (conventional; p = 0.063)
- Numerically favors OAOT by 0.44 mm but does not reach statistical significance
- Implant survival: 100% in both groups
- PD, BOP, KT width: comparable between groups at 5 years
- No peri-implantitis cases reported in either group

## 5. Limitations
- p = 0.063 — below conventional threshold; may be underpowered for 5yr endpoint
- Single center; 30 patients, 60 implants — low for 5yr attrition
- Ankylos C/X system only; results may not generalize to all conical-connection systems
- No blinding possible (OAOT vs repeat disconnection is clinically apparent)

## 7. Glossary
- **DIB**: Distance from implant shoulder to first bone contact (임플란트 어깨부터 제1골접촉점까지 거리)
- **OAOT**: One-Abutment-One-Time (1어버트먼트-1회)
- **PS**: Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭)
- **BOP**: Bleeding on probing (탐침 시 출혈)
- **KT**: Keratinized tissue (각화치은)
