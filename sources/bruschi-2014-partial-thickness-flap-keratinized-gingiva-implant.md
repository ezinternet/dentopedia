## Why Ingested

Demonstrates a graft-free partial thickness flap technique for simultaneous keratinized mucosa augmentation at implant placement, with 4-year stability data — directly supports the [[implants/soft-tissue/keratinized-mucosa-peri-implant-health-overview]] evidence base for soft-tissue management strategies around implants.

## Three-line Summary

1. A partial thickness flap raised from residual keratinized mucosa was apically repositioned at the time of implant placement in 85 patients (131 implants) to increase both the width and thickness of peri-implant keratinized tissue without grafting.
2. Mean keratinized mucosa width was 7.26 ± 2.01 mm at 1 year and 7.37 ± 2.12 mm at 4 years, demonstrating stable clinical gain across the follow-up period.
3. Implant survival was 87.79% at 4 years; the technique allowed immediate tissue adaptation with no need for a separate augmentation procedure.

## 세줄요약

1. 임플란트 식립 시 잔존 각화점막에서 부분층 판막을 거상해 치조정 방향으로 재위치시키고, 골막 봉합으로 고정함으로써 이식재 없이 각화점막 폭경과 두께를 동시에 증가시켰다.
2. 각화점막 폭경은 1년 7.26 ± 2.01 mm, 4년 7.37 ± 2.12 mm로 추적 기간 내내 안정적으로 유지되었다.
3. 4년 임플란트 생존율 87.79%; 별도의 증대술 없이 식립과 동시에 연조직을 교정할 수 있음을 입증한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Describes a reproducible partial thickness flap (PTF) design for augmenting peri-implant keratinized mucosa (KM) at the time of implant placement
- Demonstrates 4-year stability of KM gains without connective tissue grafts or free gingival grafts
- Reports outcomes stratified by implant diameter, providing clinically actionable data
- Establishes baseline-to-4-year trajectory: KM width maintained within ≈0.1 mm of 1-year levels

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Study design**: Prospective clinical study with 4-year follow-up  
**Sample**: 85 patients, 131 implants (103 mandibular, 28 maxillary)  
**Technique**:
- Pre-surgical measurement of residual keratinized mucosa width (buccal-palatal) with periodontal probe
- Palatal or lingual incision to raise a partial thickness flap carrying all residual keratinized tissue
- Implant placement in edentulous ridge
- Apical repositioning of flap secured with loose periosteal sutures
- Healing by secondary intention on the donor area

**Outcome measures**: KM levels at baseline, 6 months, 1 year, and annually through 4 years; implant survival rate  
**Stratification**: By implant diameter

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Timepoint | Mean KM Width |
|---|---|
| 1-year follow-up | 7.26 ± 2.01 mm |
| 4-year follow-up | 7.37 ± 2.12 mm |

- KM gain confirmed in **all cases** — no regression reported
- Levels remained stable over time (1-year to 4-year difference ≈ +0.11 mm)
- Implant survival rate at 4 years: **87.79%** (115/131 implants)
- Technique provides simultaneous correction of KM width and thickness at time of placement

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-center private practice setting (Rome, Italy); generalizability uncertain
- No control group with standard full-thickness flap or graft techniques for direct comparison
- Implant survival rate of 87.79% is lower than typically reported benchmarks — loss causes not detailed in abstract
- Abstract-only ingest: implant diameter stratification data not extractable
- No data on pocket depth, bleeding on probing, or marginal bone level changes

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Partial Thickness Flap (PTF) | Flap raised through the connective tissue layer, leaving periosteum on the bone; preserves blood supply to the bone |
| Keratinized Mucosa (KM) | Band of gingiva/mucosa with a keratinized epithelium; resists mechanical trauma and microbial penetration |
| Apical Repositioning | Suturing a flap coronally from its origin to displace the mucogingival junction apically and expose more keratinized tissue |
| Secondary Intention Healing | Wound closure without primary flap coverage; granulation tissue fills the exposed connective tissue bed |
| Periosteal Suture | Suture anchored to the periosteum to secure a repositioned flap in its new position |
