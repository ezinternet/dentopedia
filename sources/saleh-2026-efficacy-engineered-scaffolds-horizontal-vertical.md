## Why Ingested

Surveillance hit (bone-graft-material topic): the first SR+MA to pool patient-specific CAD/CAM scaffolds (mostly titanium mesh, plus PEEK, 3D hydroxyapatite and a customized allogeneic bone block) for horizontal and vertical augmentation, reporting volumetric gain and complication rates with GRADE. It extends [[implants/vertical-ridge-augmentation/ragucci-2025-customized-titanium-mesh-vertical-ridge-augmentation-sr-ma]] (CAD/CAM vs conventional Ti mesh) and turns the descriptive workflow map in [[bone-regeneration/elrefaei-2025-3d-printed-scaffolds-ridge-augmentation]] into pooled numbers; its ~22% complication rate is a counterweight to the 10.8% in [[implants/vertical-ridge-augmentation/sabri-2024-titanium-mesh-bone-augmentation-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD42024611887; 13 studies = 6 single-center RCTs + 7 prospective case series, 241 patients, 202 defects; 9 studies pooled) of patient-specific CAD/CAM "engineered scaffolds" for staged horizontal and vertical ridge augmentation; 9 of 13 used customized titanium mesh.

Pooled bone gain at 6-9 months was 4.96 mm horizontal (95% CI 4.48-5.44), 5.64 mm vertical (5.26-6.02) and 1307 mm3 volumetric (1000-1700), but overall complication rate was 21.7% (early 24.3%, late 16.5%); meta-regression linked light smoking (about -2.4 mm horizontal) and expert surgeons (+2.42 mm) to outcome, and GRADE certainty was moderate for linear gain but low for volume and complications.

Customized scaffolds regenerate substantial bone in all dimensions, but the high complication rate makes results unpredictable, so use should be restricted to selected cases (non-smokers, experienced operators); evidence is dominated by titanium mesh, with few RCTs and short follow-up.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA; 단일기관 RCT 6편 + 전향적 증례군 7편, 환자 241명, 결손 202개, 정량합성 9편)으로 환자맞춤형 CAD/CAM 스캐폴드(engineered scaffold)를 이용한 2단계 수평·수직 치조제 증대술을 분석했고, 13편 중 9편이 맞춤형 티타늄 메쉬 (titanium mesh)였다.

6-9개월 시점 합산 골 증가량은 수평 4.96 mm, 수직 5.64 mm, 체적 1307 mm3였지만 전체 합병증률은 21.7% (조기 24.3%, 후기 16.5%)였고, 메타회귀에서 경도 흡연(수평 약 -2.4 mm)과 숙련 술자(+2.42 mm)가 결과와 연관되었으며 GRADE 근거 확실성은 선형 골 증가량 '중등도', 체적·합병증은 '낮음'이었다.

맞춤형 스캐폴드는 모든 방향에서 상당한 골 증가를 얻지만 합병증이 많아 예측성이 낮으므로 비흡연자·숙련 술자 등 선택된 증례에 한정해야 하며, 근거는 티타늄 메쉬 중심이고 RCT가 적고 추적기간이 짧다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First pooled analysis of patient-specific engineered scaffolds (CAD/CAM Ti mesh, PEEK shells, 3D hydroxyapatite blocks, customized allogeneic bone block) for horizontal and vertical ridge augmentation, with volumetric (CBCT) gain as a third outcome.
- Quantifies complications by timing (early < 4 weeks vs late >= 4 weeks) and resorbable-membrane use.
- Meta-regression of patient and operator factors (smoking, surgeon experience, defect type, arch, graft material) with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR adjustment.
- Applies GRADE per outcome: moderate for horizontal and vertical gain, low for volumetric gain and complications.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA, PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD42024611887; random-effects (REML) pooling, Cochran's Q and I2 for heterogeneity, Egger's test, mixed-effects meta-regression (study as random intercept).
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed), EMBASE (OVID), Scopus, Cochrane CENTRAL, plus Web of Science citation tracking, ClinicalTrials.gov, OpenGrey, theses database and hand search.
- **n**: 16,539 records identified, 10,509 screened after de-duplication; 13 studies (15 articles) included; 9 studies pooled. 241 patients (mean age 47.1 years across 11 studies), 202 defects (12.4% horizontal, 27.2% vertical, 60.4% combined); all studies used staged protocols.
- **Interventions**: 9 titanium-based devices, 2 PEEK shells, 1 3D hydroxyapatite block, 1 customized allogeneic bone block (CABB). Graft: autogenous + xenograft mixture in 11 studies (84.6%); 2 studies used only particulate bovine bone or allograft particulate.
- **Outcomes**: horizontal, vertical and volumetric bone gain; early/late complication rates; PROMs; histology/bone density.
- **Risk of bias**: RoB 2 for RCTs, ROBINS-I V2 for non-randomized studies.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Horizontal gain (3 studies) | 4.96 mm (4.48-5.44); with resorbable membrane 5.64 mm (5.02-6.26, 55 cases) vs without 2.48 mm (1.85-3.11, 22 cases) |
| Vertical gain (5 studies, 145 cases) | 5.64 mm (5.26-6.02); without membrane 6.36 mm (5.96-6.76) vs with membrane 4.26 mm (3.62-4.90) |
| Volumetric gain (5 studies, 83 cases, CAD/CAM Ti mesh) | 1307 mm3 (1000-1700); with membrane 1715 mm3 vs without 900 mm3 |
| Overall complication rate | 21.7% (16.4-27.0) |
| Early complications (123 cases) | 24.3% (17.8-30.8); no membrane 25.6% vs membrane 21.4% (not significant) |
| Late complications (113 cases) | 16.5% (7.3-25.7); no membrane 18.0% vs membrane 11.1% (not significant) |
| Histomorphometry | 28.6% to 79.6% regenerated bone at 4-6 months (heterogeneous); 34.82% at 6 months in combined defects |
| Meta-regression, horizontal | Light smokers -2.4 mm (p = 0.031); expert surgeons +2.42 mm vs graduate students (p = 0.021) |
| Meta-regression, vertical (multivariable) | Combined vs horizontal defect +3.11 mm (p = 0.001); light smoking -0.78 mm (p = 0.01) |
| Meta-regression, volumetric (multivariable) | Combined defect +720 mm3; autogenous particles + xenograft +745 mm3 vs 3D HA + xenograft; maxilla lower; RM + PRF -850 mm3 |
| Risk of bias | 3 of 6 RCTs low risk; no non-randomized study low risk (3 moderate, 4 serious) |
| GRADE | Moderate (horizontal, vertical gain); low (volumetric, early, late complications) |

Context from the Discussion: complication rates exceed prior SR values for horizontal (17.8%) and vertical GBR (11-16%), and customized meshes did not clearly lower exposure compared with conventional meshes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only one RCT compared a scaffold (CAD/CAM Ti mesh) against a current gold standard (Ti-reinforced d-PTFE mesh); most studies were single-arm.
- Evidence imbalance: 9 titanium studies vs 2 PEEK vs 1 3D hydroxyapatite vs 1 CABB; material subgroups are exploratory.
- Few studies per pooled outcome (3 horizontal, 5 vertical, 5 volumetric); the resorbable-membrane subgroup findings conflict between outcomes (membrane associated with more horizontal and volumetric gain but less vertical gain).
- Short follow-up, no long-term stability data, PROMs reported in only two studies; surgeon experience could not be quantified (case counts unavailable).
- Publication bias could not be formally assessed (rated unclear). Inferred: some numbers differ between Abstract, Results and Discussion (e.g., follow-up "6-9" vs "4-10" months; horizontal membrane subgroup 55 vs 32 cases), and a few meta-regression table columns look internally inconsistent, so treat subgroup values as approximate.
- Future work: larger RCTs, bioactive scaffold development, standardized reporting, implant-level and patient-centered outcomes, cost-benefit analysis.

## 7. Glossary
- **Engineered scaffold**: patient-specific CAD/CAM-designed barrier or block (titanium mesh, PEEK, polylactic acid, hydroxyapatite, allogeneic bone) for bone regeneration.
- **CABB**: customized allogeneic bone block, milled from an allogeneic blank to fit the defect.
- **HARA/VARA (-SI/-ST)**: horizontal/vertical alveolar ridge augmentation with simultaneous or staged implant placement.
- **Early / late complication**: complication occurring < 4 weeks / >= 4 weeks after surgery (e.g., exposure, infection).
- **GRADE**: framework rating certainty of evidence as high, moderate, low or very low.
- **REML**: restricted maximum likelihood estimator for between-study variance in random-effects meta-analysis.
