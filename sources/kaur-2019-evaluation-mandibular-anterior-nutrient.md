## Why Ingested

만성질환(하이퍼텐션·당뇨)과 하악 전치부 영양관(nutrient canal, NC) 방사선 소견의 연관성을 다룬 topical sweep의 일환으로 인제스트. 본 논문(Kaur 2019, n=600 3군 비교)은 [[radiology/hasan-2022-prevalence-nutrient-canals-mandibular]]가 나중에 재확인하는 "NC 유병률이 전신질환(DM/HT/치주염) 존재 시 증가한다"는 다중질환 연관성의 초기 데이터 포인트다 — 두 연구 모두 NC 증가와 전신질환 사이의 양(positive)의 연관성을 독립적으로 보고한다.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional comparative radiographic study (n=600; 200 hypertension, 200 type 2 diabetes mellitus, 200 healthy controls, age 21-60) evaluating nutrient canals (NCs) on mandibular anterior intraoral periapical radiographs (#33-#43) using the paralleling technique.

NC presence was highest in the diabetes group (93.5%), followed by hypertension (88.5%), and lowest in healthy controls (44.5%); total NC count was also highest in the hypertension group (394, 65.5%) vs diabetes (356, 59.3%) vs controls (138, 23%), with both disease groups significantly higher than controls (P<0.05) but no significant difference between the two disease groups.

NCs were predominantly interradicular rather than periapical/beyond-root in all groups, and no significant correlation was found between NC presence and disease duration/severity or patient gender — supporting NC count as an adjunct (not standalone) radiographic marker prompting further systemic workup, but with a key limitation that periodontitis-related NC changes were not separately excluded.

## 세줄요약

하악 전치부 구내 치근단 방사선사진(#33-#43, paralleling technique)에서 영양관(Nutrient Canal, NC)을 평가한 3군 비교 단면연구(n=600; 고혈압 200명, 제2형 당뇨병 200명, 건강대조군 200명, 21-60세).

NC 존재율은 당뇨군에서 가장 높았고(93.5%), 고혈압군(88.5%), 건강대조군(44.5%) 순이었으며; 총 NC 개수는 고혈압군(394개, 65.5%)이 당뇨군(356개, 59.3%)·대조군(138개, 23%)보다 많았고, 두 질환군 모두 대조군 대비 유의하게 높았으나(P<0.05) 두 질환군 간 유의차는 없었다.

NC는 모든 군에서 치근단 주변보다 치근 사이(interradicular)에 더 많이 위치했고, NC 존재와 질환 이환기간·중증도·성별 간 유의한 상관관계는 없었다 — NC 개수를 전신질환 추가 검사를 유도하는 보조적(단독 아님) 방사선학적 지표로 활용 가능하나, 치주염 관련 NC 변화를 별도로 배제하지 않은 것이 핵심 한계다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly compares NC incidence, count, and location across three groups (hypertension, DM, healthy controls) in a single cross-sectional design (n=600), rather than comparing a single disease group against historical/pooled controls.
- Uses a strict exclusion protocol (excludes patients with both HT+DM, other systemic disease, severe bone loss/crowding, edentulism, periapical pathology, pregnancy, poor-quality films) to isolate the HT/DM-specific signal from confounding etiologies of NC visibility.
- Reports both NC presence (per-patient binary) and total NC count (per-group aggregate), plus location (interradicular vs beyond-root), allowing a more granular read of the systemic-disease/NC relationship than presence-only designs.
- Finds no correlation between NC presence and disease duration or severity — an important negative finding tempering the marker's prognostic (vs merely diagnostic-adjunct) value.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: cross-sectional comparative radiographic study, 3 parallel groups.
- **Population**: n=600, 21-60 years; Group I hypertension (n=200, mean age 40.72y, mean duration 3.29y); Group II type 2 DM (n=200, mean age 45.03y, mean duration 3.20y); Group III healthy controls (n=200, mean age 42.05y).
- **Diagnostic thresholds**: hypertension = BP >120/80 mmHg (auscultatory method); diabetes = RBS >=200 mg/dL or FBS >=126 mg/dL.
- **Exclusions**: co-morbid HT+DM, other systemic disease, severe bone loss/crowding of lower anteriors, edentulism/missing lower anterior teeth, periapical pathology, pregnancy, poor-quality radiographs.
- **Imaging**: IOPAR of mandibular anterior region (#33-#43) via paralleling technique (XCP holder), Care Stream 2100CS unit, ICRP-compliant protection.
- **Outcome assessment**: two independent oral & maxillofacial radiologists (blinded), scoring presence/absence, count, and location (interradicular vs beyond apex) of radiolucent NC lines.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 20.0; Chi-square for intergroup comparison; Cohen's kappa for interobserver reliability; Pearson correlation (r) for duration/severity vs NC count; significance P<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Interobserver reliability: substantial-to-near-perfect agreement (kappa 0.635-0.87 across measures), P<0.01 for all kappa tests — indicating negligible interobserver bias.
- NC presence by group (observer 1): Group II (DM) 187/200 = 93.5%; Group I (HT) 177/200 = 88.5%; Group III (control) 89/200 = 44.5%. HT group 1.9x controls; DM group 2.1x controls.
- Total NC count (observer 1): Group I 394 (65.5%); Group II 356 (59.3%); Group III 138 (23%) — out of 888 total NCs across all 600 patients.
- Both disease groups significantly exceeded controls (Chi-square, P<0.05); HT vs DM difference not significant (P>0.05).
- Location: NCs predominantly interradicular in all groups (Group I 187/200 between roots vs 13 beyond; Group II 190/200 vs 10; Group III 190/200 vs 10); no significant intergroup difference in location distribution (P>0.005).
- No significant gender difference in NC presence in any group (P>0.005), though males showed numerically more NCs overall (40.3% vs 35.2%).
- No significant correlation between NC presence and HT duration (r=0.024, P=0.74) or DM duration (r=0.05, P=0.48).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Cross-sectional design cannot establish causality between HT/DM and NC formation, only association.
- Single-institution sample (Rajasthan, India) limits generalizability across ethnicities (paper notes NCs are more common in Afro-Caribbean populations elsewhere in the literature).
- Only per-patient duration/severity correlation was tested; no multivariate adjustment for other confounders (e.g., periodontal status, calcium deficiency) despite excluding overt periodontal bone loss.
- Two-dimensional IOPAR imaging (vs CBCT) limits spatial resolution and may under/over-count overlapping NCs.
- Authors explicitly call for larger-sample studies to substantiate radiographic NC presence/count as a diagnostic aid for HT/DM screening.

## 7. Glossary

- **Nutrient canal (NC)**: radiolucent linear channel in alveolar bone through which blood vessels/nerves pass; classified by Lovett into Types I-III by width/length.
- **IOPAR**: intraoral periapical radiograph.
- **Paralleling technique**: intraoral radiographic technique using a film/sensor holder (XCP) positioned parallel to the tooth's long axis to minimize geometric distortion.
- **RBS / FBS**: random blood sugar / fasting blood sugar.
- **Cohen's kappa**: statistic measuring interobserver agreement beyond chance.
