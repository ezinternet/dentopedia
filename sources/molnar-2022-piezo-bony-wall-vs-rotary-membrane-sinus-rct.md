## Why Ingested

측방 상악동거상술에서 창 폐쇄 방식(골벽 재위치 vs 콜라겐막 피개)을 조직형태계측까지 포함해 직접 비교한 임상연구는 위키에 없었다 — [[sinus-lift/lateral/jabbari-2024-does-collagen-membrane-coverage-offer]]는 막 피개 vs **비피개**였고, [[sinus-lift/lateral/schiavon-2022-bone-lid-technique-lateral-sinus]]의 골개(Bone Lid) 근거는 임상연구 2편뿐이었다. 본 전향적 무작위 임상·조직학 연구(RCT, 40명)는 동일한 이종골에 골벽 재위치와 막 피개를 맞붙여 6개월 생검으로 신생골 비율이 동등함을 보여, "막이 우월하다"는 통념과 동물실험의 골개 우위 신호를 인체 조직학 수준에서 한정한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective randomized clinical and histological study (n=40, 20 per arm; Semmelweis University, Budapest; NCT04811768) of lateral maxillary sinus augmentation with a sintered bovine xenograft, comparing piezosurgical window preparation with bony wall repositioning (BW) against rotary preparation with resorbable collagen membrane coverage (CM), with 6-month trephine core biopsies.

Histomorphometry of 29 evaluable biopsies showed no significant between-group difference — connective tissue BW 39.2 ± 9.0% vs CM 37.9 ± 8.5%, residual bone substitute 32.9 ± 6.3% vs 31.8 ± 8.8%, newly formed bone 27.8 ± 11.2% vs 30.3 ± 4.5% — and surgery duration, membrane perforations (6/20 vs 7/20) and VAS/hematoma/edema scores were likewise not significantly different (all p > 0.05).

Window closure with the retrieved autologous bony wall performs like collagen membrane coverage, but the two arms confound the closure method with the osteotomy instrument (piezo vs rotary), the membrane advantage over an *uncovered* window cannot be assumed to hold here, and 11 of 40 biopsies were lost to histological processing.

## 세줄요약

전향적 무작위 임상·조직학 연구 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 40명, 군당 20명, 세멜바이스 대학, 부다페스트; NCT04811768): 소결 우골 이종골 (Xenograft)을 사용한 측방 상악동거상술에서 피에조서저리 (Piezosurgery)로 채취한 골벽 재위치 (Bony Wall Repositioning, BW)와 회전기구 절제 후 콜라겐막 피개 (Collagen Membrane, CM)를 6개월 트레핀 (Trephine) 코어 생검 조직형태계측으로 비교.

조직형태계측 분석 가능한 생검 29개에서 군 간 유의차 없음 — 결합조직 (Connective Tissue, CT) BW 39.2 ± 9.0% vs CM 37.9 ± 8.5%, 잔류이식재 (Bone Substitute Material, BSM) 32.9 ± 6.3% vs 31.8 ± 8.8%, 신생골 (New Bone, NB) 27.8 ± 11.2% vs 30.3 ± 4.5%; 수술 시간·점막 천공 (6/20 vs 7/20)·시각통증척도 (Visual Analogue Scale, VAS)·혈종·부종도 모두 유의차 없음 (p > 0.05).

골벽 재위치는 콜라겐막 피개와 대등한 결과를 보였으나 두 군이 창 폐쇄법과 절제 기구(피에조 vs 회전기구)를 동시에 바꿔 교란되었고, "비피개" 대비 막의 우위를 이 설계로 일반화할 수 없으며, 생검 40개 중 11개가 조직처리 문제로 탈락했다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First clinical RCT we hold that isolates **window-closure method** (repositioned autologous bony wall vs native collagen membrane) while holding the graft constant (same sintered bovine xenograft) and adds **6-month human histomorphometry** — the exact evidence type the authors state had not been reported for either closure method.
- Reports a **null result in both domains**: no significant clinical difference (surgery time, window/mucosa preparation time, perforations, VAS, hematoma, edema) and no significant histological difference (CT, residual BSM, NB), with tissue fractions nearly identical in rank order (CT > BSM > NB in both arms).
- Documents **complete reintegration of the repositioned bony wall in all 20 BW cases**, and BSM particles embedded in native bone at the window in the CM group — i.e. no dislocation/failure of the bone-wall technique.
- Adds a **within-group observation** the between-group comparison hides: in the BW arm, connective tissue fraction was significantly higher than newly formed bone (p < 0.01), suggesting the reinserted wall may shift tissue distribution slightly toward soft tissue via connective tissue carried in the plate's interspaces.
- Contradicts the common clinical preconception that piezosurgery is slower than rotary preparation — piezo was non-significantly *faster* on every recorded time parameter.
- Provides a perforation-rate data point (6/20 = 30% BW; 7/20 = 35% CM) that is explicitly higher than prior reports, attributed to a short 6-week minimum post-extraction healing window.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective randomized controlled clinical and histological study; parallel groups; computer-generated randomization; single operator [BM] performed all surgeries; clinical scoring by investigator [ZSP]; histological/histomorphometric analysis by two independent investigators [AJ, MB].
- **n**: 40 patients (BW n = 20, CM n = 20), ages 18–70. Baseline age 51 (SD 9) vs 48 (SD 8); FMPS 15 (SD 4) vs 14 (SD 4); FMBS 17 (SD 2) vs 15 (SD 3).
- **Inclusion**: ≥1 missing maxillary premolar/molar; crestal bone width ≥7 mm; residual bone height ≤5 mm on CBCT; FMPS and FMBS <20%; compliant.
- **Exclusion**: smoking; clinically relevant systemic disease (diabetes, rheumatism, cancer); untreated periodontitis; systemic steroids or bisphosphonates; acute/chronic inflammation; prior endoscopic sinus surgery or sinus floor elevation; GBT/GTR at the site; extraction within 6 weeks.
- **Surgical protocol**: split-thickness flap (midcrestal + single mesial vertical incision); 3D guided surgery stents for implant positioning and window mapping; 7 × 7 mm quadratic window 5 mm apical to the bony crest.
  - **BW**: piezosurgery; window lifted outward, repositioned after grafting.
  - **CM**: 3 mm rotary diamond burs at 400 RPM; bony wall completely eliminated; resorbable porcine collagen membrane over the window.
- **Graft**: particulate bovine xenograft, 0.5–1.0 mm, both groups. Two-layer closure (horizontal mattress + single interrupted, Dafilon 4/0 and 5/0); sutures out at 14 days; amoxicillin/clavulanic acid 3 × 625 mg × 7 days (clindamycin 3 × 300 mg if allergic); diclofenac up to 3 × 50 mg; 0.2% chlorhexidine twice daily × 2 weeks.
- **Re-entry at 6 months**: core biopsy via crestal approach at the planned implant site with 3.2 mm outer / 2.6 mm inner trephine; then 4.1 × 10 mm Straumann Tissue Level RN implants by guided surgery; uncovering and screw-retained FPD restoration 3 months later.
- **Histology**: 4% formalin 24 h → PBS → graded alcohol → paraffin → 3–5 µm sections (Cut 6062 rotation microtome) → HE, Masson trichrome, von Kossa (figure legends also reference Masson Goldner).
- **Histomorphometry**: ×10 digitized total scans; total implant area (TIA) outlined; NFB, BSM and CT fractions divided by TIA to yield percentages.
- **Outcomes**: primary = total duration of sinus floor augmentation surgery; secondary = duration of window preparation, duration of sinus mucosa preparation, VAS (day 0, 1, 2, 3), hematoma and edema (0–3 scale), and histomorphometric NFB/BSM/CT percentages.
- **Statistics**: paired t-test for within-group treatment durations; Levene's test + independent-samples t-test for between-group means; chi-squared for perforation frequency; ANOVA with Brown-Forsythe for histomorphometry (GraphPad Prism 8.0.2); significance p ≤ 0.05 (*), highly significant p ≤ 0.01 (**); data as mean ± SD.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Fraction | BW (n=13) | CM (n=16) | Between-group |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connective tissue (CT) | 39.2 ± 9.0% | 37.9 ± 8.5% | No meaningful difference |
| Residual BSM | 32.9 ± 6.3% | 31.8 ± 8.8% | No meaningful difference |
| Newly formed bone (NB) | 27.8 ± 11.2% | 30.3 ± 4.5% | No meaningful difference |

Within-group: in BW, CT was significantly higher than NB (** p < 0.01, Fig. 6). Rank order identical in both arms: CT > BSM > NB.

**Clinical course**: all 40 augmentations successful, no significant arterial injury or intraoperative bleeding, uneventful healing. All perforations ≤5 mm and covered with a collagen membrane, with treatment success in every case. At 6 months CBCT showed sufficient hard tissue in all cases; ≥7 mm ridge width at re-entry and no additional grafting needed in any case. Complete reintegration of the repositioned bony wall in all BW cases. 40 implants placed; all osseointegrated at re-entry + 2 months (per Discussion) and restored.

**Qualitative histology**: comparable tissue reaction and BSM integration in both arms — new bone attached to granule surfaces throughout the grafted area; osteoblast-lined new bone matrix in CT-covered areas (bone growth still in progress); macrophages and single multinucleated giant cells at granule surfaces, osteoclasts in the neighbourhood, no inflammatory infiltrate in intergranular connective tissue, and no signs of biodegradation or phagocytosis of the BSM.

**Authors' interpretation**: closure of the access window by the retrieved bony wall or by a native collagen membrane yields comparable bone augmentation; piezosurgery with bony window repositioning is a membrane-free alternative. They also note their histology found no bone growth originating from the reimplanted bony wall (contrary to Tawil et al.) and attribute this to crestal biopsy retrieval rather than lateral-wall biopsy.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Confounded design (author-acknowledged)**: BW received piezosurgery and CM received rotary instrumentation, so closure method and osteotomy instrument cannot be separated; the paper cannot attribute any difference (or equivalence) to one factor.
- **Split-thickness flap** (author-acknowledged) limits comparability with studies using full-thickness mucoperiosteal flaps, which may affect bony-window healing.
- **Sinus width not accounted for** (author-acknowledged): the lateral–medial wall distance may affect graft healing but was not considered in randomization or analysis.
- **Short-term only** (author-acknowledged): 6-month histology plus early implant follow-up; no long-term graft stability, survival or MBL data.
- **Biopsy loss**: only 29 of 40 core biopsies were evaluable (11 discarded for lack of measurable material or processing difficulty), and the arms became unbalanced (13 vs 16); the CM new-bone SD (±4.5%) is much tighter than BW (±11.2%), which with n = 13/16 leaves the null result underpowered rather than proven equivalent — no a-priori sample-size calculation or equivalence margin is reported.
- **High perforation rate** (30–35%) versus prior literature, which the authors attribute to a short post-extraction healing interval (majority 6–12 weeks) and unlevelled interradicular septa; all were ≤5 mm, so the cohort does not test large perforations.
- **Reporting gaps in the retrieved text**: no CONSORT flow diagram, no allocation-concealment or blinding description for the clinical examiner/surgeon beyond named roles, no per-patient implant survival table, and no correction for multiple comparisons.
- **Missing baseline/radiographic outcome table**: augmented bone height or graft volume by group is not reported numerically; 3D graft volume change is not analysed.

## 6. Related Work

- Barone et al. [44] — covered vs non-covered lateral windows: collagen membrane did not substantially increase vital bone over 6 months but reduced connective tissue proliferation; the present study's near-identical CT fractions fit a "membrane has little to add once the window is closed" reading.
- Johansson et al. [35] — bone-to-implant contact after lateral augmentation with autologous graft + bony wall repositioning vs collagen membrane vs autologous graft alone: no differences, consistent with the present null.
- Ohayon et al. [22] — with vs without membrane coverage: membrane reduced overall postoperative complication rate (the one direction in which the present data show only non-significant trends favouring BW).
- Tawil et al. [40] — reported bone growth starting from the repositioned bony wall with the same xenograft; the present crestal biopsies did not reproduce it, which the authors attribute to biopsy location.
- Geminiani et al. [26] — suggested piezo preparation is slower; the present timings trend the other way (non-significant).
- Barbeck et al. [47] and Rothamel et al. [49] — same sintered bovine BSM: 31.63 ± 5.69% new bone (preclinical + clinical) and 25.8–49.6% new bone in one-/two-stage sinus floor elevation, bracketing the ~28–30% seen here.

## 7. Glossary

- **BW (bony wall group)**: test arm in which the piezosurgically harvested 7 × 7 mm bony window is repositioned as an autologous barrier (also called bone lid / bony window repositioning).
- **CM (collagen membrane group)**: control arm in which the window is drilled away with rotary burs and the antrostomy covered by a resorbable porcine collagen membrane (collprotect).
- **BSM (bone substitute material)**: here cerabone, a high-temperature-sintered particulate bovine xenograft, 0.5–1.0 mm, osteoconductive and slowly resorbing.
- **NFB / NB (newly formed bone)**: bone matrix deposited on and between graft granules during healing, measured as % of the total implant area.
- **TIA (total implant area)**: the outlined cross-sectional area of the entire core biopsy, used as the denominator for all histomorphometric fractions.
- **Piezosurgery**: ultrasonic micro-vibration osteotomy with a selective-cutting tip, intended to spare soft tissue (Schneiderian membrane) while cutting mineralized bone.
- **VAS (visual analogue scale)**: patient-rated pain/discomfort score recorded on the day of surgery and days 1–3.
- **Split-thickness flap**: flap raised leaving periosteum on the bone (vs full-thickness mucoperiosteal), used here for all cases.
- **FMPS / FMBS**: full-mouth plaque score / full-mouth bleeding score, both required <20% for inclusion.
