## Why Ingested
지그마틱 임플란트 vs 골이식 후 통상 임플란트를 직접 비교한 최초 Cochrane SR (pub4). 기존 [[implants/full-arch/abdunabi-2019-immediately-loaded-maxillary-full-arch-sr]]의 로딩 프로토콜 근거와 연계해 고도 골흡수 상악 재활 의사결정 근거 보강.

## One-line Summary
Cochrane SR (2 RCTs, n=71+20, 3yr): zygomatic implants probably reduce implant failures and time to rehabilitation vs bone-grafted conventional implants but probably increase complications; drilling technique (piezoelectric vs rotary) makes little difference in outcomes.

## 한줄요약
코크란 SR (RCT 2편, 3년): 지그마틱 임플란트 (지그마 임플란트, Zygomatic Implant)는 골이식 통상 임플란트 대비 임플란트 실패를 줄이고 재활 기간을 단축하지만(1.3일 vs 444일) 합병증은 증가시킴.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First Cochrane SR directly comparing zygomatic implants vs conventional implants in augmented bone with RCT evidence
- Quantifies time-to-rehabilitation difference: zygomatic 1.3 days vs grafting 444 days (moderate certainty)
- Confirms moderate-certainty evidence for reduced implant failures with zygomatic approach
- Establishes moderate-certainty evidence for increased complication rate with zygomatic approach
- Finds no clinically meaningful difference between piezoelectric and rotary drilling for site preparation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Cochrane SR of RCTs
- Search: CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, 2 trial registries; searched November 2025
- Included: 2 RCTs with ≥1yr follow-up, ≥10 per arm
  - Parallel RCT: n=71, zygomatic (immediate load) vs delayed conventional + grafting
  - Split-mouth RCT: n=20, piezoelectric vs rotary drills
- Both studies 3-year follow-up; both European centres
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2 tool; generally low; one outcome (patient satisfaction) high risk (missing data)
- Synthesis: narrative (too few studies for meta-analysis); GRADE for certainty

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Zygomatic vs grafted conventional (3yr):**
- Implant failures: OR 0.23 (95% CI 0.06–0.96) — moderate certainty
- Prosthetic failures: OR 0.29 (0.05–1.56) — low certainty
- Complications: OR 4.53 (1.47–13.98) — moderate certainty (~80% vs ~50%)
- Patient satisfaction: MD 0.40 (-3.05 to 3.85) — low certainty (no difference)
- Time to rehab: MD −442.98 days (−518.64 to −367.32) — moderate certainty

**Piezoelectric vs rotary drills (3yr):**
- Prosthetic/implant failures: OR 1.00 (0.02–50.40) — low certainty
- Complications: OR 1.50 (0.25–8.99) — low certainty
- Operative time: MD −9.15 min (−10.41 to −7.89) — rotary faster — moderate certainty

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 2 small RCTs; serious imprecision throughout
- Comparison 2 (zygomatic vs subperiosteal) had no eligible RCTs
- Short 3-year follow-up; long-term survival/complication data lacking
- Cannot determine which complication types drive the OR 4.53
- More large well-designed RCTs needed

## 7. Glossary
- Zygomatic implant (지그마 임플란트, Zygomatic Implant): 30–52 mm titanium screw anchored in zygomatic bone (cheekbone), bypassing the atrophic maxilla
- Severely atrophic maxilla: insufficient residual bone for conventional implant anchorage
- Piezoelectric surgery (압전 수술, Piezoelectric Surgery): ultrasonic bone cutting using vibrating tips — selective for bone, potentially less thermal damage
