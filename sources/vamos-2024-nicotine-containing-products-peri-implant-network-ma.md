## Why Ingested

First network meta-analysis comparing four nicotine-containing product categories (cigarette, waterpipe, e-cigarette, smokeless tobacco) against non-smokers on MBL and peri-implant outcomes. Fills the smoking risk-factor gap in the MBL wiki — the wiki has no smoking-specific MBL paper. See [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/giok-2024-risk-factors-peri-implantitis-umbrella]] for peri-implantitis risk umbrella context.

## Three-line Summary

Network SR+MA (39 observational studies; 32 in Bayesian network MA) comparing peri-implant outcomes across cigarette smokers (CS), waterpipe (WP), e-cigarette (EC), smokeless tobacco (ST), and non-smokers (NS); Bayesian random-effects model; Nicotine & Tobacco Research 2024.

Nonsmokers had the smallest MBL; CS: MD +1.34 mm (95% CrI 0.85–1.79), WP: MD +1.58 mm (95% CrI 0.84–2.35), ST: MD +2.53 mm (95% CrI 1.20–3.87) vs NS — all significant; EC: MD +0.52 mm (CrI −0.33 to 1.36) vs NS — not significant; NS ranked 1st for all secondary outcomes.

Risk gradient: ST > WP > CS > EC > NS for MBL; e-cigarettes do not significantly elevate MBL vs non-smokers; but all nicotine products worsen peri-implant plaque and inflammation.

## 세줄요약

네트워크 SR+MA (39편 관찰연구; 32편 베이지안 네트워크 MA): 궐련흡연(CS, Cigarette Smoker)·물담배(WP, Waterpipe)·전자담배(EC, E-Cigarette)·무연담배(ST, Smokeless Tobacco)·비흡연(NS, Non-Smoker) 5군 임플란트주위 결과 비교.

비흡연군 MBL 최소; CS +1.34mm (CrI 0.85–1.79), WP +1.58mm (CrI 0.84–2.35), ST +2.53mm (CrI 1.20–3.87) vs NS — 모두 유의; EC +0.52mm (CrI −0.33~1.36) vs NS — **비유의**; NS 모든 2차 결과 1위.

MBL 위험 순서: 무연담배 > 물담배 > 궐련 > 전자담배 > 비흡연; 전자담배는 MBL에 통계적 유의성 없음; 전체 니코틴 제품이 임플란트주위 염증·플라크 악화.

## Key Contributions

- First network MA directly comparing 4 nicotine product categories + NS for peri-implant outcomes
- Establishes risk gradient: ST > WP > CS > EC > NS for MBL
- E-cigarettes: not significantly worse than NS for MBL (clinically important null)
- Smokeless tobacco: highest MBL impact (MD +2.53 mm) — highest-risk product
- Bayesian approach allows probabilistic ranking across 5 groups simultaneously
- CINeMA tool used for certainty of evidence assessment

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA; Bayesian network meta-analysis; PRISMA guidelines
- Search: PubMed, EMBASE, Web of Science, CENTRAL; up to April 2023
- Language: English only
- Inclusion: Observational studies; adults with dental implants; 5 nicotine groups
- n: 39 studies (qualitative); 32 studies in network MA
- Primary outcome: MBL
- Secondary: PPD, plaque index, BOP, modified plaque index, PPD >4mm, gingival index, PISF volume, TNF-α, IL-1β
- Statistics: Bayesian random-effects network MA; credible intervals (CrI); QUIPS and CINeMA for bias/certainty

## Key Results

### MBL vs Non-Smokers (Bayesian network MA)

| Product | MD vs NS | 95% CrI | Significant? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cigarette (CS) | +1.34 mm | 0.85–1.79 | **Yes** |
| Waterpipe (WP) | +1.58 mm | 0.84–2.35 | **Yes** |
| Smokeless tobacco (ST) | +2.53 mm | 1.20–3.87 | **Yes** |
| E-cigarette (EC) | +0.52 mm | −0.33–1.36 | No |
| Non-smoker (NS) | Reference | — | — |

- MBL risk ranking: ST > WP > CS > EC > NS (NS ranked 1st for lowest MBL)
- Subgroup analyses by smoking habit, implant duration, maintenance: no ranking changes
- Secondary outcomes: NS ranked 1st for all; EC showed inferior parameters to NS in several (not all) outcomes

## Limitations

- Observational studies only — inherent confounding by oral hygiene, maintenance compliance
- English-language restriction
- Heterogeneity of nicotine product definitions (cigarettes per day, waterpipe type)
- Limited data on e-cigarettes and ST (smaller subgroups, wider CrIs)
- Publication to April 2023 — later e-cigarette data not included

## Glossary

- **Network meta-analysis (NMA)**: MA that compares multiple treatments simultaneously via direct and indirect evidence through a connected network
- **Bayesian credible interval (CrI)**: Probabilistic interval for the true effect (analogous to 95% CI in frequentist statistics)
- **Smokeless tobacco (ST)**: Non-combusted tobacco products (chewing tobacco, snuff, snus)
- **CINeMA**: Confidence in Network Meta-Analysis — tool for rating certainty of NMA evidence
- **PISF**: Peri-implant sulcular fluid — a measure of local peri-implant inflammation
