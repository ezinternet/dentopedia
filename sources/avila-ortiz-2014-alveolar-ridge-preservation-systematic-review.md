## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (J Dent Res 2014, University of Iowa) of RCTs comparing alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) vs extraction alone at non-molar sites, quantifying dimensional change at multiple ridge surfaces.

ARP significantly preserved buccolingual width (+1.89 mm, 95% CI 1.41–2.36) and midbuccal height (+2.07 mm, 95% CI 1.03–3.12); subgroup analyses showed flap elevation + membrane + xenograft/allograft were associated with superior preservation; distal height was the only outcome that did not reach significance.

ARP is effective for preserving ridge dimensions; the combination of flap elevation, membrane, and xenograft/allograft is the optimal protocol, but results apply only to non-molar sites.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (J Dent Res 2014, 아이오와대학교) — 비구치부 발치 후 치조제보존술(ARP) 대 발치만 시행 간 골 치수 변화를 다면적으로 정량화한 무작위대조시험 메타분석.

ARP 시행 시 협설 폭 +1.89 mm (95% CI 1.41–2.36), 협측 정중 높이 +2.07 mm (95% CI 1.03–3.12) 유의하게 보존; 피판 거상+차폐막+이종/동종골 조합이 하위군 분석에서 최우수; 원심 높이만 유의성 미달.

ARP는 골 치수 보존에 효과적이며 피판+차폐막+이종/동종골 조합이 최적 프로토콜이나, 결과는 비구치부에 국한됨.

## 1. Key Results
- Multiple electronic databases; RCTs only; non-molar teeth
- ARP vs extraction alone:
  - Buccolingual width: +1.89mm (95%CI: 1.41–2.36; p<.001)
  - Midbuccal height: +2.07mm (95%CI: 1.03–3.12; p<.001)
  - Midlingual height: +1.18mm (95%CI: 0.17–2.19; p=.022)
  - Mesial height: +0.48mm (95%CI: 0.18–0.79; p=.002)
  - Distal height: +0.24mm (NS; p=.102)
- Subgroup analyses: flap elevation, membrane use, xenograft/allograft = superior midbuccal and midlingual height preservation
- University of Iowa

## 2. Clinical Implications
- ARP is effective; all dimensions improved except distal height
- Flap elevation + membrane + xenograft/allograft = optimal ridge preservation protocol
- Non-molar sites only — results may not generalize to molar extraction
