## Why Ingested

기존 orofacial-pain 카테고리의 이온채널 축은 염소이온 (Chloride, Cl⁻) 채널([[orofacial-pain/wilke-2020-chloride-underrated-ion-nociceptors]], [[orofacial-pain/price-2009-chloride-regulation-pain-pathway]])과 T형 칼슘채널 ([[orofacial-pain/pathirathna-2005-t-type-calcium-gaba-neurosteroids]])로 구성되어 있었다. 본 논문(Ke 2026, CION 마우스 모델)은 TRPC4라는 **양이온(Ca²⁺ 투과성 TRP) 채널**을 삼차신경절 (Trigeminal Ganglion, TG) 통증 기전에 추가하여 이온채널 축을 확장한다. 또한 임상적 삼차신경병증을 다룬 [[oral-medicine/trigeminal-neuralgia/alkhutari-2023-post-traumatic-trigeminal-neuropathy-endodontic-sr]] (치과치료 후 외상성 삼차신경병증)에 분자기전 근거를 제공하며, [[overviews/bms-orofacial-pain-neuropathic-mechanism-overview]] 종합에 새로운 채널 후보를 편입시킨다.

## Three-line Summary

Animal (in-vivo) study, adult male/female C57BL/6 mice (n=6/group per behavioral cohort), using a chronic constriction of the unilateral infraorbital nerve (CION) model of trigeminal neuropathic pain (TNP).

TRPC4 mRNA/protein rose in the trigeminal ganglion (TG) after CION; TRPC4 knockdown (shRNA) or antagonist ML204 attenuated mechanical allodynia/spontaneous pain/cold allodynia, while agonist Englerin A reproduced pain-like behavior in naive mice via Ca²⁺ influx → ERK/P38 phosphorylation → ATF2 activation (confirmed by co-immunoprecipitation of p-P38/ATF2).

Identifies TRPC4-Ca²⁺-ERK/P38-ATF2 as a druggable TG pathway for TNP, but findings are preclinical (rodent, non-selective pharmacological tools ML204/Englerin A also affect TRPC5) and require confirmation with more specific TRPC4 modulators before clinical translation.

## 세줄요약

동물(생체내) 연구, 성체 C57BL/6 마우스(암수, 행동실험 그룹당 n=6), 편측 안와하신경 만성압박술 (Chronic Constriction of the unilateral Infraorbital Nerve, CION) 모델을 이용한 삼차신경병증성 통증 (Trigeminal Neuropathic Pain, TNP) 연구.

CION 후 삼차신경절 (Trigeminal Ganglion, TG)에서 TRPC4 mRNA/단백질 발현이 증가했고, TRPC4 shRNA 넉다운 또는 길항제 ML204는 기계적 이질통(Mechanical Allodynia)·자발통·냉이질통을 완화한 반면, 작용제 Englerin A는 나이브 마우스에서 칼슘(Ca²⁺) 유입 → ERK/P38 인산화 → ATF2 활성화 경로(공동면역침전으로 p-P38/ATF2 직접결합 확인)를 통해 유사 통증행동을 유발했다.

TRPC4-Ca²⁺-ERK/P38-ATF2 경로가 TNP의 새로운 치료 표적임을 제시하나, 전임상(설치류) 결과이며 사용된 약리학적 도구(ML204/Englerin A)가 TRPC5에도 작용해 특이성이 낮다는 한계가 있어 임상 적용 전 더 선택적인 TRPC4 조절제로 재검증이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to implicate TRPC4 (a Ca²⁺-permeable, non-selective cation TRP channel) in trigeminal neuropathic pain (TNP), as opposed to the more commonly studied TRPA1/TRPV1.
- Bioinformatic funnel from GEO trigeminal-neuropathic-pain transcriptome (87 up/116 down DEGs) → intersection with CTD/GeneCards neuropathic-pain gene sets (16 candidates) → LASSO regression (4 top genes) → TRPC4 selected for its understudied role in TNP and importance of ion channels in neuropathic pain pathophysiology.
- Establishes TRPC4 as both necessary (shRNA knockdown, ML204 antagonism reduce CION-induced pain) and sufficient (Englerin A agonism reproduces pain-like behavior in naive mice) for TNP.
- Localizes TRPC4 to TG neurons only (not satellite glial cells; colocalizes with NeuN but not glutamine synthetase, GS), predominantly in the V2 branch territory, distributed across small/medium/large neuron subtypes and NF200⁺/CGRP⁺/IB4⁺ afferent classes.
- Mechanistically links TRPC4-mediated Ca²⁺ influx to activation of ERK and P38 (but not JNK, PI3K, Akt, or mTOR) in the MAPK pathway, and demonstrates direct protein-protein interaction between phosphorylated P38 (p-P38) and the transcription factor ATF2 by co-immunoprecipitation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Nerve injury model**: CION via intraoral approach — 6-0 silk ligation of the left infraorbital nerve (ION) in anesthetized C57BL/6 mice (10 weeks, 23–25 g); sham = incision without nerve damage.
- **Behavioral assays** (Day 3–28 post-surgery, n=6 mice/group): Von Frey up-down method for mechanical allodynia (head withdrawal threshold, HWT); non-evoked nociceptive behavior (facial grooming/rubbing duration in a plexiglass cylinder); cold hypersensitivity (acetone evaporative cooling, grooming duration).
- **Pharmacology**: TRPC4 antagonist ML204 — local subcutaneous injection into the ipsilateral upper lip (10 μg/5 μL) on day 14 post-CION, and systemic intraperitoneal administration (2 mg/kg/day, Day 0–11 starting Day 14 post-CION). TRPC4 agonist Englerin A (EA) — direct intra-TG stereotactic injection at 3 doses (0.25 μg, 2.5 μg, 25 μg); 2.5 μg selected for subsequent mechanistic experiments (no additional benefit at 25 μg).
- **Genetic knockdown**: CMV-shRNA adeno-associated virus (AAV; titer >5×10¹² vg/mL) stereotactically injected into the left TG of 6-week-old mice (coordinates AP −4.5, ML 1.6, DV 6.40 mm), 4-week recovery before behavioral testing; scramble shRNA as negative control.
- **Bioinformatics**: GEO trigeminal-neuropathic-pain RNA-seq dataset reprocessed (FPKM→TPM), DEGs via `limma` (p<0.05, |log2FC|>0.5, no multiple-comparison correction), cross-referenced against CTD and GeneCards (keyword: "neuropathic pain," "migraine headache," "trigeminal neuropathic pain"), Venn intersection → LASSO regression for final candidate-gene shortlist.
- **In vitro systems**: Primary trigeminal ganglion sensory neuron culture (papain/Dispase II/Collagenase II dissociation, Percoll gradient purification, NGF-supplemented B27 medium); HEK293 wild-type and TRPC4-knockout (KO) cell lines; TRPC4 overexpression via plasmid transfection (jetPRIME).
- **Calcium imaging**: Fura-4 AM (TG neurons) or Cal-590 (HEK293 cells) fluorescent indicators, confocal/TIRF microscopy, ΔF/F0 quantification before/after Englerin A exposure; calcium chelator BAPTA-AM (10 μM, 6 h) used to test Ca²⁺-dependence of downstream signaling.
- **Molecular assays**: Western blot for TRPC4, p-Akt/Akt, p-PI3K/PI3K, p-mTOR/mTOR, p-ERK/ERK, p-P38/P38, p-JNK/JNK, p-ATF2/ATF2 (normalized to GAPDH); RT-qPCR for Trpc1–7 family mRNA; immunofluorescence (TRPC4 with NeuN/GS/NF200/CGRP/IB4; ATF2/p-P38/β3-tubulin via tyramide signal amplification); co-immunoprecipitation (Co-IP) of ATF2 and p-P38 in primary TG neurons.
- **Statistics**: Shapiro-Wilk normality test; Student's t-test or Mann-Whitney U (two groups); one-way/two-way ANOVA with Tukey's post hoc (≥2 groups); GraphPad Prism 9.0; α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **CION model validation**: Mechanical allodynia threshold, non-evoked nociceptive time, and cold allodynia all significantly worsened vs. sham from Day 3–28 (p<0.05 to p<0.001, two-way repeated-measures ANOVA); reproduced in female mice with comparable TRPC4 upregulation, addressing known sex-difference concerns in trigeminal neuralgia epidemiology.
- **TRPC4 upregulation**: TRPC4 (not other Trpc family members) mRNA and protein significantly increased in TG at Day 28 post-CION (p<0.05 mRNA; p<0.001 protein, n=6/group); increase sustained from Day 14 to Day 28; concentrated in the V2 branch region.
- **TRPC4 distribution**: ~26.7% small, 36.3% medium, 37% large TG neuron somata were TRPC4⁺; colocalization with NF200 (39.4%), CGRP (37.0%), IB4 (12.2%) — spanning Aβ, peptidergic C/Aδ, and non-peptidergic C-fiber populations.
- **Necessity/sufficiency**: Local and systemic ML204 significantly reduced CION-induced mechanical allodynia, spontaneous pain, and cold allodynia (p<0.05–0.001 vs. CION+vehicle) but did not fully prevent pain emergence with repeated systemic dosing; local ML204 effect was side-specific (ipsilateral only). Intra-TG Englerin A (dose-dependent, 0.25–25 μg) reproduced all three pain-like phenotypes in naive mice within 1–24 h (p<0.05–0.001 vs. vehicle).
- **Calcium/MAPK signaling**: Englerin A significantly increased ΔF/F0 Ca²⁺ signal only in TRPC4-transfected HEK293 cells (not controls, p<0.001) and in primary TG neurons; this drove significant increases in p-Akt, p-ERK, p-P38, p-JNK (p<0.05–0.001) but not p-PI3K/p-mTOR. Effects abolished in TRPC4-KO HEK293 cells and blunted by calcium chelation (BAPTA-AM), confirming Ca²⁺-dependence.
- **ATF2 pathway confirmation**: In vivo, CION and intra-TG Englerin A both significantly elevated p-ERK, p-P38, ATF2, and p-ATF2 in TG (p<0.01–0.001) with no change in PI3K/Akt/mTOR/JNK — implicating ERK/P38 (not JNK or PI3K-Akt) specifically. Co-IP confirmed direct p-P38–ATF2 protein interaction; P38 inhibitor SB202190 blocked Englerin A-induced ATF2 activation.
- **TRPC4 shRNA rescue**: TG-targeted TRPC4 shRNA significantly reduced CION- and Englerin A-induced mechanical allodynia, spontaneous pain, cold allodynia, and normalized TRPC4/p-ERK/p-P38/p-ATF2/ATF2 expression (p<0.05–0.001), with no baseline pain-behavior difference in sham groups (scramble vs. shRNA), supporting target specificity over off-target TRPC5 effects seen with pharmacological tools.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Purely preclinical: mouse CION model only; no human trigeminal neuralgia/neuropathy tissue or clinical correlation.
- Pharmacological tools (ML204 antagonist, Englerin A agonist) are not TRPC4-selective and also modulate TRPC5 — the shRNA knockdown experiments partially address this but do not fully resolve isoform selectivity for future drug development.
- Repeated systemic ML204 delayed but did not entirely prevent pain-like behavior emergence, suggesting incomplete blockade or compensatory mechanisms with long-term nerve injury — authors flag long-term dosing strategy as an open question.
- Mechanism of TRPC4-triggered intracellular Ca²⁺ release from organelles (endoplasmic reticulum, lysosomes) vs. extracellular influx not fully dissected in vivo (only inferred from in vitro ACSF conditions).
- Downstream targets of ATF2 beyond hypothesized VIP/NPY (via ATF2/c-Jun heterodimer, extrapolated from prior DRG literature) not directly tested in this TG model — flagged as future work.
- Discrepancy with a prior report of decreased Trpc4 mRNA in DRG after sciatic spared nerve injury (SNI) is discussed but not experimentally reconciled; attributed to TG-vs-DRG anatomical/functional differences and CION-vs-SNI injury-model differences — the authors call for direct TG-vs-DRG comparison studies across injury types.
- No behavioral dose-response beyond 2.5 μg vs 25 μg Englerin A (plateau observed but underlying receptor kinetics not characterized).

## 6. Related Work

- Cites prior TRPC channel-pain literature: TRPC5 in inflammatory pain (pulpitis, arthritis, CFA models), TRPC6 in chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy, TRPC4 in general neuropathic pain, morphine-induced tolerance/hyperalgesia, migraine, and visceral pain.
- Builds on established MAPK-neuropathic pain literature (ERK in central sensitization across chronic nerve compression, spinal injury, spinal nerve injury, morphine hypersensitivity models; P38 inhibitor SB203580 efficacy in inflammatory/bone-cancer/incision pain and prior CION-TNP tongue-pain studies).
- References ATF2's established role in dorsal root ganglion (DRG)/spinal dorsal horn neuropathic pain maintenance, and ATF2/c-Jun heterodimer regulation of VIP/NPY in DRG neurons of neuropathic rats.
- Contrasts with a prior study reporting decreased Trpc4 mRNA in DRG after sciatic nerve spared nerve injury (SNI) — discussed as a TG-vs-DRG and CION-vs-SNI model difference rather than a direct contradiction.
- Notes existing TRPC4 pharmacological inhibitors under development for other diseases (Pico145, HC-070, ML204) relevant to epilepsy, migraine, pulmonary arterial hypertension, and arthritis-associated vascular inflammation.

## 7. Glossary

- **TRPC4**: Transient Receptor Potential Canonical 4 — a non-selective, Ca²⁺-permeable cation channel of the TRPC1/4/5 subfamily.
- **CION**: Chronic Constriction of the unilateral Infraorbital Nerve — a surgical mouse model of trigeminal neuropathic pain via silk-suture ligation of the infraorbital nerve.
- **TG**: Trigeminal Ganglion — the sensory ganglion housing cell bodies of primary afferent neurons innervating the face/oral cavity via V1–V3 branches.
- **ATF2**: Activating Transcription Factor 2 — a MAPK-phosphorylated transcription factor implicated in neuropathic pain gene regulation.
- **ERK / P38 / JNK**: Extracellular signal-regulated kinase, P38, and c-Jun N-terminal kinase — the three branches of the MAPK signaling family.
- **ML204**: A pharmacological TRPC4 (and TRPC5) antagonist.
- **Englerin A (EA)**: A natural-product pharmacological TRPC4 (and TRPC5) agonist.
- **Co-IP**: Co-Immunoprecipitation — assay for detecting direct protein-protein interaction.
- **HWT**: Head Withdrawal Threshold — behavioral readout of the Von Frey mechanical allodynia test.
- **shRNA**: short hairpin RNA — used here (via AAV) for TG-targeted gene knockdown of Trpc4.
