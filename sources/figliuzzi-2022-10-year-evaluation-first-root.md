## Three-line Summary

Single case report (n=1), 50-year-old healthy female with a fractured maxillary second premolar, followed for 10 years after immediate post-extraction placement of a CT/CAD-CAM/DMLS-fabricated Ti-6Al-4V root analogue implant (RAI).

At the 10-year follow-up, crestal bone resorption was 0 mm, probing pocket depth was within normal range (2.5 mm), no bleeding on probing, no gingival recession, no increased mobility, and the patient reported full functional/esthetic satisfaction throughout.

This is the longest-documented follow-up in the RAI literature and supports long-term dimensional stability of additively-manufactured RAIs, but as a single case (n=1) it requires confirmation from larger series before informing general clinical practice.

단일 증례보고(n=1), 상악 제2소구치 파절이 있던 50세 건강한 여성 환자로, CT/CAD-CAM/DMLS로 제작한 Ti-6Al-4V 치근유사형 임플란트(Root Analogue Implant, RAI)를 즉시발치식립 후 10년간 추적관찰했다.

10년 추적관찰 시점에서 치조정골 흡수는 0mm, 탐침깊이(Probing Pocket Depth, PPD)는 정상범위(2.5mm), 탐침시출혈(Bleeding On Probing, BOP) 없음, 치은퇴축 없음, 동요도 증가 없이 전 기간 동안 기능적·심미적으로 환자 만족도가 높았다.

RAI 문헌 중 가장 긴 추적관찰 사례로 적층제조(Additive Manufacturing, AM) 기반 RAI의 장기적 치수안정성을 뒷받침하지만, 단일증례(n=1)이므로 일반 임상 적용을 위해서는 더 큰 규모의 연구로 재확인이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reports the 10-year follow-up of what the authors describe as the first-in-human root analogue implant (RAI) fabricated using CT-derived 3D models, CAD/CAM, and direct metal laser sintering (DLMS) in Ti-6Al-4V.
- Provides the longest documented clinical follow-up in the RAI evidence base, serving as a historical anchor for additive-manufacturing-based custom root-analogue implants.
- Demonstrates that a socket-matched, non-screwed implant geometry (mimicking the extracted root anatomy) can maintain dimensional stability of peri-implant hard and soft tissue over a decade.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Implant fabrication**: A root analogue implant was designed from 3D models derived from CT-based radiographic processing of the pre-extraction alveolus/root anatomy, then manufactured in Ti-6Al-4V using direct metal laser sintering (DLMS), a laser-based additive manufacturing technique that fuses titanium microparticles layer by layer. DLMS allows control of porosity, pore interconnectivity, size, shape, and distribution — theoretically promoting bone ingrowth/vascularization beyond conventional plasma-sprayed surfaces.
- **Patient**: A single healthy 50-year-old female with a fractured maxillary second premolar; no systemic disease, non-smoker, average lifestyle. Verbal informed consent obtained.
- **Surgical protocol**: Atraumatic, conservative extraction preserving alveolar wall integrity and periosteal vascularization; RAI immediately inserted into the intact post-extraction socket (not screwed — positioned to match socket geometry), with platform switching, respecting biological width.
- **Restoration**: Metal-ceramic restoration (a metal-free crown would not adequately adhere to the custom implant); cemented with GC Fuji I glass ionomer cement.
- **Follow-up schedule**: T0 (implant placement) plus reviews at 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, and 10 years — clinical exam (observation, palpation, percussion, probing) and periapical intraoral radiographs using the parallel technique with customized resin bite registration jigs (GXIO-770 Gendex unit).
- **Outcome measures**: Probing pocket depth (PPD), bleeding on probing (BOP), gingival recession, mobility, and radiographic crestal bone level (distance from bone crest base to implant shoulder).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Crestal bone resorption at 10 years: **0 mm** (measured radiographically, base of bone crest to implant shoulder).
- PPD: 2.5 mm (within normal limits).
- BOP: absent.
- No gingival recession, no increased mobility.
- No pathological symptoms reported by the patient at any recall (1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 10 years).
- Patient-reported outcome: high functional and esthetic satisfaction sustained throughout follow-up.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single case (n=1) — the authors explicitly acknowledge this limits generalizability and state the results "require further confirmation."
- No comparison group / no standardized statistical analysis (case report design).
- Verbal-only informed consent documentation.
- Organizational/production limitation noted: DLMS-based custom RAI fabrication requires multiple design phases, limiting current scalability versus stock implant systems; authors speculate broader availability of 3D printing technology may address this in the future.

## 7. Glossary

- **RAI (Root Analogue Implant)**: A custom-shaped dental implant fabricated to mimic the external anatomy of the extracted natural tooth root, designed to be press-fit into the post-extraction socket rather than screwed into osteotomy-prepared bone.
- **DLMS (Direct Laser Metal Sintering)**: Additive manufacturing process using a focused laser to fuse metal (titanium) microparticles layer-by-layer to build a 3D object from a computer-generated model; also referred to as DMLS (Direct Metal Laser Sintering).
- **Platform switching**: Implant-abutment interface design where the abutment diameter is smaller than the implant platform diameter, associated with reduced crestal bone loss.
- **PPD (Probing Pocket Depth)**: Distance from the gingival margin to the base of the probeable sulcus/pocket, a periodontal/peri-implant health measure.
- **BOP (Bleeding On Probing)**: Presence of bleeding upon gentle periodontal probing, an indicator of tissue inflammation.
