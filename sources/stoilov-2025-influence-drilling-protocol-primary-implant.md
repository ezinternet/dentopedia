## Why Ingested

Direct follow-up to this group's own 2023 PU-foam macro-design study ([[wiki/implants/isq/stoilov-2023-macrodesign-length-diameter-bone-quality-isq]]), and the first page ingested here that actually *tests* the drilling adaptations that page could only recommend — it overturns that page's "under-preparation in hard bone" takeaway (D1 needs over-preparation) while confirming its diameter-over-length finding. It also sharpens the "all four alternatives consistently raise primary stability" line held in [[wiki/implants/osteotomy-thermal/el-kholey-2019-drilling-technique-low-density-bone-sr]] by showing no protocol reaches target torque at 10 PCF.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro bench study (390 groups × 6 sites = 2340 osteotomies per bone density, N = 2340) comparing 6 drilling protocols against insertion torque (IT) in rigid polyurethane foam at 35/25/15/10 PCF (D1–D4), across 3 SIC macro-designs × 4 diameters (3.4–5.2 mm) × 3 lengths (7.5–14.5 mm).

Drilling protocol altered IT highly significantly at every density (D1 F₁,₃₉₈ = 115.5; D2 F₂,₄₅₃ = 850; D3 F₂,₆₇₁ = 924; D4 F₂,₄₈₅ = 241; all p < 0.001), with under-sizing raising and over-sizing lowering IT; optimal-window (25–50 Ncm) attainment went from 3–4/10 to 7/10 for SICmax and SICtapered in D1, but was 0/10 for *every* implant and protocol at 10 PCF.

Clinical implication is density-conditional rather than universal: over-prepare D1 to avoid compression necrosis and >80 Ncm motor-stop, under-prepare small D2 diameters, prefer tapered wide implants in D3, and treat D4 as beyond the reach of drilling modification alone. The paper cannot support or overturn any clinical outcome claim — it is single-system, PU-foam, torque-only bench data with an internally inconsistent target window (25–50 Ncm in Methods vs 25–30 Ncm in all figure captions).

## 세줄요약

In vitro 벤치 연구 (390군 × 6사이트 = 골밀도별 2,340개 골절개, N = 2340) — 강경질 폴리우레탄 폼(Polyurethane Foam, PU) 35/25/15/10 PCF(치밀도, Pounds per Cubic Foot)에서 6가지 드릴링 프로토콜이 삽입토크(Insertion Torque, IT)에 미치는 영향을 3종 거시설계 × 4직경(3.4–5.2 mm) × 3길이(7.5–14.5 mm)로 평가.

모든 골밀도에서 드릴링 프로토콜이 IT에 매우 유의한 영향을 미쳤음(적정범위 25–50 Ncm 도달률 기준 D1·D2 SICmax/SICtapered가 3–4/10 → 7/10으로 향상, 그러나 10 PCF에서는 모든 임플란트·프로토콜이 0/10).

임상적 시사점은 보편적이지 않고 골밀도별 조건부임 — D1은 압괴괴사(>80 Ncm 모터 정지) 방지를 위해 과대직경 준비(Overpreparation), D2는 소직경에 언더사이즈(Undersized) 준비, D3는 넓은 테이퍼형, D4는 드릴링 조정만으로는 부족하고 보조기법 필요. 단일 임플란트 시스템·PU 폼·삽입토크만 측정한 벤치 데이터이며 목표 토크 범위가 본문(25–50)과 도표(25–30) 간 일관되지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Systematic in vitro comparison of six drilling protocols (standard, no-crestal-drill, over-prepared ×2, under-prepared ×2) crossed with four bone densities, three macro-designs, four diameters and three lengths.
- Establishes drilling protocol as a highly significant determinant of insertion torque at *every* bone density (all p < 0.001), with under-sizing > standard > over-sizing as the direction of effect.
- First density-resolved demonstration that drilling modification has essentially no useful effect in very soft bone (10 PCF / D4): 0/10 implant–protocol combinations reached the target torque.
- Quantifies the D1 over-torque hazard: 5.2 mm SICmax and SICtapered exceeded the 80 Ncm motor ceiling and could not be fully seated.
- Concludes with explicit per-density surgical recommendations (over-prepare D1, under-prepare small D2 diameters, tapered + wide in D3, conservative approach in D3/D4).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro bench study (ASTM F1839-08 rigid polyurethane foam standard material; single-layer blocks to mimic the immediate-placement scenario where stability derives from apical engagement rather than crestal cortex).
- **n**: 390 groups × 6 implants = 2340 osteotomies per bone density; N = 2340 as reported. Six implants per group.
- **Implants**: SIC invent (Basel, Switzerland) — SICace (parallel, 7.5 mm) and SICmax (parallel, 9.5 mm) and SICtapered (tapered, 13 mm); at larger diameters lengths extend to 14.5 mm. Diameters 3.4, 3.7, 4.0, 4.2, 4.5, 4.7, 5.0, 5.2 mm. All zirconia-blasted + acid-cleaned SICmatrix, Sa = 1.0 µm.
- **Bone densities**: 35 PCF (D1), 25 PCF (D2), 15 PCF (D3), 10 PCF (D4).
- **Drilling protocols**: S = standard drill, 100% depth, crestal drill, no tap; S2 = standard drill, crestal drill omitted; O1 = next-larger drill, 100% depth; O2 = next-larger drill + tap drill; U1 = standard drill at 70% depth, no crestal drill; U2 = previous-smaller drill, 100% depth, no crestal drill.
- **Preparation**: CNC-controlled milling machine (Pro©, Mekanika, Anderlecht, Belgium) with original SIC drills at 300 rpm — machine-prepared osteotomies to remove manual angle/depth error.
- **Insertion**: torque-controlled surgical motor + contra-angle (W&H Implantmed Plus / WS-75L) at 25 rpm by an experienced implantologist; peak IT recorded; 80 Ncm motor ceiling — implants that hit it before full seating were measured for protrusion and **excluded** from statistics.
- **Outcomes**: primary = peak insertion torque (Ncm); secondary = proportion of configurations attaining the pre-defined optimal IT window; <25 Ncm and >50 Ncm classified as critical.
- **Statistics**: descriptive frequency distribution of IT vs thresholds; linear mixed models per bone density with implant specification as random factor and implant design + preparation protocol as fixed factors; log-transformation of IT where needed; Bonferroni correction; Satterthwaite effect estimates; α = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Protocol effect on insertion torque, linear mixed models (all p < 0.001):

| Bone density (PCF / type) | Test statistic | p |
|---|---|---|
| 35 (D1) | F₁,₃₉₈ = 115.5 | < 0.001 |
| 25 (D2) | F₂,₄₅₃ = 850 | < 0.001 |
| 15 (D3) | F₂,₆₇₁ = 924 | < 0.001 |
| 10 (D4) | F₂,₄₈₅ = 241 | < 0.001 |

Direction of effect, consistent across densities: **under-sized protocols > standard > over-sized protocols.**

Proportion of the 10 implant configurations per design reaching the optimal IT window:

| Density | Design | Standard (S) | Modified protocol | Modified protocol detail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 35 PCF (D1) | SICace | 4/10 | 4/10 | over-preparation (O1/O2) |
| 35 PCF (D1) | SICmax | 3/10 | 7/10 | over-preparation (O1/O2) |
| 35 PCF (D1) | SICtapered | 4/10 | 7/10 | over-preparation (O1/O2) |
| 25 PCF (D2) | SICace | 4/10 | 4/10 (U1) / 4/10 (O1) | under- and over-preparation equal |
| 25 PCF (D2) | SICmax | 4/10 | 5/10 (U1) / 3/10 (O1) | under-preparation best, over-preparation worst |
| 15 PCF (D3) | SICace | 0/10 | 1/10 (S2) / 2/10 (U1) / 2/10 (U2) | crestal-drill omission minimal help |
| 15 PCF (D3) | SICmax | 2/10 | 3/10 (S2) / 3/10 (U1) / 3/10 (U2) | diameter, not protocol, drives IT |
| 10 PCF (D4) | all designs | 0/10 | 0/10 | no protocol reached the window |

Per-density operative findings:

| Density | Protocol finding | Clinical reading |
|---|---|---|
| D1 (35 PCF) | Over-preparation reduced IT in all groups; exception 5.2 mm SICmax/SICtapered exceeded 80 Ncm → motor stop, incomplete seating | Over-preparation protects bone from compression necrosis, but large diameters need a pre-planned anti-overtorque strategy |
| D2 (25 PCF) | Over-preparation (O1) reduced IT consistently; under-preparation (U1) was the *only* way to clear >25 Ncm at 3.7–4.2 mm; 5.2 × 14.5 mm only reached a physiologically acceptable torque *with* over-preparation | Split rule: under-prepare small diameters, standard suffices for wide ones, over-prepare the widest to avoid over-torque |
| D3 (15 PCF) | Undersized osteotomies gave only slight IT gains; omitting the crestal drill helped in only 3 subgroups (SICace 4.5 × 9.5, SICmax 4.7 × 9.5, SICtapered 4.2 × 13); diameter↔IT positive, length↔IT not consistent; tapered > parallel but **not** significant | No drilling trick rescues D3; choose wide tapered implants instead |
| D4 (10 PCF) | Larger diameter had minimal effect; under-preparation only slightly raised IT for SICmax and SICtapered; nothing reached the window | Drilling modification alone is exhausted — needs adjunctive strategies |

Macro-geometry: differences between the three designs were minor at every density. Parallel-walled (SICace, SICmax) equalled or exceeded the tapered SICtapered in D4, and SICmax tended to exceed SICtapered under standard preparation; SICtapered was slightly elevated in several D2 configurations and slightly higher in D3 (non-significant).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- PU foam is not bone: no cortical/cancellous differentiation, no vascularity, no biological healing response, no BIC or osseointegration endpoint — IT is the only outcome.
- Single implant family (SICace/SICmax/SICtapered) from one manufacturer; results may not transfer to other macro-designs or thread geometries.
- Single-layer blocks deliberately remove crestal cortical support, so conclusions model apical-engagement stability only.
- Implants hitting the 80 Ncm motor ceiling were **excluded** from the statistical analysis — a non-random exclusion that biases the D1/D2 large-diameter estimates downward and inflates apparent protocol benefits.
- **Internal inconsistency in the target window**: Methods §2.4 defines optimal IT as 25–50 Ncm, while all figure captions and the green bands are labelled 25–30 Ncm. All "/10 attainment" figures above inherit that ambiguity.
- **Internal inconsistency on D1 direction**: the Abstract states under-preparation improved torque in dense bone (D1, D2), but D1 was prepared only with over-preparation protocols and the Conclusions recommend over-preparation in D1.
- Ten configurations per design (one per length × diameter pairing per protocol) is a small denominator for the attainment fractions; the 2/10 vs 3/10 differences in D3 should not be over-read.
- No heat, no thermal necrosis measurement, no cortical perforation or fracture risk, no clinical outcome.

- Stoilov 2023 (J. Funct. Biomater. 14:469): own predecessor — identical implants, densities and metrics under standard drilling only; this paper adds the protocol arm.
- El-Kholey 2019 (SR): four alternative drilling strategies in low-density bone, all raising primary stability.
- Yu 2022 (SR+MA): cross-technique ISQ comparison, under-drilling MD +11.43 ISQ.
- Tabassum 2021 (ex vivo goat): lateral vs axial undersizing as separable compression vectors.
- Sakai 2023: deep-learning model to pre-select the drilling protocol from CBCT — the preoperative-decision problem this bench work cannot solve.
- Chrcanovic 2017 (SR+MA): bone quality/quantity and implant failure — the clinical outcome none of this bench work reaches.

## 7. Glossary
- **PCF (Pounds per Cubic Foot)**: density unit for the polyurethane foam bone simulant; higher = denser. 35/25/15/10 PCF are mapped to Lekholm–Zarb D1/D2/D3/D4 in this study.
- **IT (Insertion Torque, 삽입토크)**: rotational resistance recorded by the surgical motor during implant seating; peak value used as the primary stability metric here.
- **Optimal IT window**: manufacturer/clinical threshold for immediate loading — stated as 25–50 Ncm in the Methods, 25–30 Ncm in figure legends.
- **Over-/under-sizing (Over-/Undersized preparation)**: using a drill larger or smaller than the manufacturer's final drill so the implant body overfills or underfills the osteotomy, changing radial bone compaction.
- **Crestal drill omission (S2)**: skipping the coronal/crestal step drill so the coronal osteotomy stays wider than nominal.
- **Macro-design**: implant body geometry — parallel-walled (SICace, SICmax) vs tapered (SICtapered).
- **Compression necrosis**: ischemia from excessive radial bone compaction, the mechanism the authors invoke to justify over-preparation in D1.
