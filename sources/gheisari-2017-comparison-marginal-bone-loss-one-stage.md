## Why Ingested
RCT comparing one-stage vs two-stage surgical approaches for marginal bone loss (MBL) around Astra Tech implants. Extends the evidence base on surgical protocol MBL outcomes alongside [[wiki/implants/mbl/verma-2024-comparison-bone-loss-submerged-nonsubmerged]], which found submerged (cover screw) protocol minimized early MBL vs non-submerged healing abutments at 3 months. This 2017 RCT provides longer follow-up (6 months post-loading) and a different implant system (Astra Tech), testing whether the MBL difference persists through functional loading.

## Three-line Summary
Randomized clinical trial comparing crestal bone loss at mesial/distal surfaces of 310 Astra Tech implants placed via one-stage (170 implants, 90 patients) or two-stage (140 implants, 50 patients) surgery, with 6-month post-loading follow-up using parallel periapical radiography.
Mean MBL was 0.760±0.04 mm (one-stage) vs 0.842±0.04 mm (two-stage); difference not statistically significant (p>0.05). Maxilla (0.860 mm) vs mandible (0.729 mm) also showed no significant difference.
One-stage surgery offers comparable bone preservation with reduced treatment time and patient discomfort, supporting its use as a viable alternative to two-stage approach.

## 세줄요약
무작위 임상시험으로 아스트라 테크 임플란트 310개(140명)를 one-stage(170개, 90명) vs two-stage(140개, 50명) 수술법으로 나누고, 기능적 부하 6개월 후 평행 근단파노라마 방사선사진으로 근위/원위면 변연골소실(MBL) 비교.
One-stage 군 **0.760±0.04 mm**, two-stage 군 **0.842±0.04 mm**로 수치상 one-stage가 적었으나 **통계적 유의차 없음**(p>0.05). 상악(0.860 mm) vs 하악(0.729 mm)도 유의차 없음.
수술 시간·환자 불편감 감소 측면에서 **one-stage가 two-stage의 대안으로 타당**하며, 골보존 측면에서 열등하지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Prospective RCT directly comparing one-stage vs two-stage surgical approaches for MBL with 6-month post-loading follow-up
- Large sample: 310 implants in 140 patients (ASA I, non-smokers, no augmentation needed)
- Standardized radiographic measurement using implant length as reference for magnification correction
- Single operator, single implant system (Astra Tech) controlling for surgical and implant variables

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial (parallel groups, unequal allocation)
- **n**: 140 patients (100 female, 40 male, age 18-65), 310 implants
- **Implant system**: Astra Tech (Dentsply Implants, Sweden), cylindrical implants
- **Groups**: One-stage (healing abutment placed at surgery, n=170 implants, 90 patients) vs Two-stage (cover screw, primary closure, n=140 implants, 50 patients)
- **Outcomes**: Marginal bone loss (mesial + distal) measured on parallel periapical radiographs at baseline (post-surgery) and 6 months post-loading using Adobe Photoshop CS5 with implant length as reference
- **Statistics**: Independent t-test (SPSS PASW 18), significance p<0.05
- **Follow-up**: Baseline radiograph immediately post-op; prosthetic loading at 3 months; final radiograph at 6 months post-loading
- **Inclusion**: ASA I, non-smokers, no periodontal disease, no bone/soft tissue augmentation, fixed prosthesis plan

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Unequal group allocation (170 vs 140 implants) — not a balanced randomization
- Relatively short follow-up (6 months post-loading); long-term MBL trajectory unknown
- Single-center, single-operator, single implant system (Astra Tech cylindrical) limits generalizability
- Radiographic measurement via 2D periapical radiographs with manual magnification correction (Photoshop) — less precise than CBCT
- No reporting of confidence intervals, only mean ± SD
- No soft tissue parameters assessed (gingival index, probing depth, keratinized mucosa width)
- No power calculation or sample size justification reported

## 6. Related Work
- Verma 2024: 3-arm RCT (submerged/cover screw vs non-submerged anatomical vs esthetic healing abutment) at 3 months; submerged had least MBL (0.18 mm CBCT) — [[wiki/implants/mbl/verma-2024-comparison-bone-loss-submerged-nonsubmerged]]
- Siadat et al. 2012/2013 (refs 22, 23): Compared one-stage vs two-stage crestal bone loss at 1 year; no significant difference
- Wenström et al. 2000 (ref 25): 5-year follow-up, submerged vs non-submerged; no difference in bone level changes related to surgical approach
- Dias et al. (ref 24): Early loading stimulates bone remodeling

## 7. Glossary
- **One-stage surgery (원스테이지 수술, One-stage)**: 치유어버트먼트를 수술 시 장착하고 2차 수술 없이 진행하는 방식 (non-submerged healing)
- **Two-stage surgery (투스테이지 수술, Two-stage)**: 픽스처를 골 아래에 묻고 커버스크류로 덮은 뒤 2차 수술로 힐링어버트먼트 장착 (submerged healing)
- **Marginal bone loss/MBL (변연골소실, Marginal Bone Loss)**: 임플란트 식립 후 치조골 정점 부위의 골 흡수량
- **Astra Tech implant system (아스트라 테크 임플란트 시스템)**: 덴츠플라이 임플란트(Dentsply Implants, Sweden) 제품군, 본 연구에선 cylindrical design 사용
- **Parallel periapical radiography (평행 근단파노라마 방사선사진, Parallel periapical radiography)**: 방사선원-치아-필름 위치를 평행하게 맞춘 표준화된 근단 방사선 촬영 기법
- **Functional loading (기능적 부하)**: 보철물이 장착되어 저작력이 전달되기 시작하는 시점
