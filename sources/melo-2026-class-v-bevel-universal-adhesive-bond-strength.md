## Why Ingested
Requested during a question on whether an enamel bevel is needed in cervical (Class V) composite restorations. The held clinical meta-analysis found no clinical influence of beveling ([[resin-bonding/mahn-2015-bonding-parameters-cervical-restorations-ma]]); this bench study tests the same question mechanically (push-out bond strength, universal adhesive, ER vs SE, bulk-fill vs conventional) and adds an adhesive-strategy datapoint alongside the clinical universal-adhesive NCCL evidence (e.g. [[resin-bonding/assis-2023-acid-etching-universal-adhesive-nccl-sr-ma]]). → [[resin-bonding/melo-2026-class-v-bevel-universal-adhesive-bond-strength]]

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study of 80 bovine-incisor blocks with simulated conical Class V cavities (enamel margins, internal dentin walls), 8 groups of n=10 in a 2 x 2 x 2 design: 1-mm enamel bevel vs none, universal adhesive (Ambar Universal APS, 10-MDP, pH ~2.57) in etch-and-rinse vs self-etch mode, conventional nanohybrid vs bulk-fill composite; outcome was push-out bond strength after 24 h (three-way ANOVA, Tukey).

Bevel did not affect bond strength (p=0.177); etch-and-rinse beat self-etch (p=0.042; cell means 9.85-13.7 vs 9.95-12.3 MPa) and bulk-fill beat conventional composite (p<0.001); no interactions (p>0.05). All cell means lay within 9.85-13.7 MPa.

Within this bench model, beveling gave no bond-strength gain, so it should be reserved for esthetic needs; the adhesive-strategy gap was small and, like the rest, is a 24-h bovine-tissue result without aging or failure-mode analysis that cannot be extrapolated directly to clinical practice.

## 세줄요약

소 절치 (bovine incisor) 블록 80개에 원추형 5급 와동 (법랑질 변연, 내면 상아질)을 형성한 시험관 내 연구 (In vitro): 법랑질 사면 (Bevel) 유무 × 유니버설 접착제 (Universal Adhesive) 산부식-수세 (Etch-and-Rinse, ER) 대 자가산부식 (Self-Etch, SE) × 일반 복합레진 대 벌크필 복합레진 (Bulk-fill Composite)의 2×2×2 설계, 군당 n=10, 24시간 후 푸시아웃 결합강도 (Push-out Bond Strength) 측정.

사면은 결합강도에 영향이 없었고 (p=0.177), ER이 SE보다 유의하게 높았으며 (p=0.042), 벌크필이 일반 복합레진보다 유의하게 높았다 (p<0.001); 상호작용은 없었다 (p>0.05). 전체 셀 평균은 9.85-13.7 MPa 범위였다.

이 실험 모델에서는 사면 형성이 결합강도를 높이지 못했으므로 심미 목적이 있을 때만 시행하는 것이 합리적이다; 다만 24시간·소 치아·노화 및 파절양상 분석 없는 결과라 임상에 직접 외삽할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First study (per the authors) to test a universal adhesive plus bulk-fill composite in simulated Class V cavities spanning both enamel and dentin, in a single factorial design.
- Tests marginal bevel as a bond-strength variable alongside adhesive strategy and composite type; bevel had no main effect and no interaction.
- Supports etch-and-rinse application of a universal adhesive and a bulk-fill composite as the highest-bond-strength combination in this model.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro factorial (2 x 2 x 2), random allocation to 8 groups, n=10 each (sample size calculated in Bioestat 5.0, power 0.8, alpha 0.05).
- **Substrate**: 80 blocks (7 x 7 mm) from the cervical buccal surface of bovine incisors, thymol 0.5% storage; flattened to ~1 mm enamel + ~1 mm dentin; cracked or defective blocks excluded.
- **Cavity**: conical, 2.0 mm deep, 1.8 mm diameter at widest (enamel level), bur #2130; bur replaced every 5 preparations; single calibrated operator; metallic holder kept bur axis perpendicular.
- **Bevel**: freehand cone bur #4130 at ~45 degrees to enamel surface, ~1.0 mm extension, enamel only; bevel dimensions measured per specimen and added to bonded area (A_total = A_cavity + A_bevel).
- **Adhesive**: Ambar Universal APS (FGM), 10-MDP, ultra-mild pH ~2.57. ER: 37% phosphoric acid 15 s on enamel and dentin, rinse 15 s, blot; SE: no etching (no selective enamel etching). Active application 10 s x 2 layers, air 10 s, cure 10 s at 1,400 mW/cm2.
- **Composites**: conventional nanohybrid Opallis in two 1-mm increments vs bulk-fill Opus Bulk Fill APS (non-flowable) in one 2-mm increment; Mylar strip + 500 g for 30 s; cure 20 s; 24 h in distilled water at 37 C; finishing with Sof-Lex discs.
- **Test**: push-out on universal testing machine at 0.5 mm/min (3-mm base hole, larger cavity diameter opposite load direction); MPa = failure load (N) / total bonded area.
- **Statistics**: Shapiro-Wilk p=0.09, Levene p=0.432; three-way ANOVA + Tukey, alpha 5% (JAMOVI).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- In vitro, 24-h storage only; no thermocycling, mechanical loading, or aging.
- Bovine tissue; all cavities had enamel margins only, whereas real cervical lesions often have dentin/cementum cervical margins.
- Push-out bonded area includes the bevel area in the denominator, so a bevel adding enamel bonding surface is partly normalized away; bevel was freehand (extent measured per specimen).
- Single universal adhesive and single composite per category; SE mode without selective enamel etching, which the authors themselves note favors ER.
- ER vs SE difference was statistically significant but small in absolute terms (authors' words: relatively small, especially with conventional composite).
- Bulk-fill is more translucent, which may limit esthetic use in the opaque cervical region.
- Authors recommend failure-mode analysis and long-term evaluation; findings are not to be extrapolated directly to clinical practice.

## 6. Related Work
- Mahn 2015 (meta-analysis of cervical restorations): clinical trials found no influence of enamel beveling; this bench result agrees in direction.
- Patanjali 2019 (cited in the paper): bevel + self-etch reduced microleakage in rectangular buccal slot cavities; discrepancy attributed to cavity geometry, bevel instrument, and materials.
- Clinical 36-month study cited by the authors: universal adhesive in ER mode outperformed SE mode in NCCLs even with selective enamel etching.
- Sibling ingests on cervical bevel: [[resin-bonding/schroeder-2015-enamel-bevel-cervical-composite-retention-sr-ma]], [[resin-bonding/da-costa-2013-enamel-bevel-nccl-composite-rct]].

## 7. Glossary
- **Push-out bond strength**: load to dislodge a restoration from a through-cavity divided by bonded wall area; loads all cavity walls at once.
- **Marginal (cavosurface) bevel**: angled removal of enamel at the cavity margin, ~45 degrees here, 1 mm extension.
- **Universal (multi-mode) adhesive**: adhesive usable in etch-and-rinse, self-etch, or selective-etch mode; here with 10-MDP.
- **Bulk-fill composite**: resin composite placed in thick (here 2 mm) single increments with lower polymerization shrinkage stress.
- **C-factor**: ratio of bonded to free surfaces; high in Class V cavities, raising shrinkage stress.
