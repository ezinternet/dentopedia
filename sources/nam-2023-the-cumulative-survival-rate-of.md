## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-centre cohort (Seoul National University Dental Hospital, Dept. of Periodontology; 151 patients, 490 Oneplant micro-threaded-neck implants placed 2006-2010, followed >10 years to December 2020) estimating Kaplan-Meier cumulative survival rate (CSR) and Cox risk factors.

10 of 490 implants failed (2.0%); overall 10-year CSR 97.9%; multivariate Cox identified only 2-stage surgery as a significant risk factor (HR 4.769, p=0.039, 95% CI 1.081-21.033); sex, age, diameter, length, fixture type, location, timing, GBR, sinus graft and restoration type were not significant.

Micro-threaded implants showed predictable long-term survival, but with only 10 events the Cox model is unstable (many degenerate CIs), smoking/diabetes were not analysed, and no non-micro-thread comparator existed.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: The cumulative survival rate of dental implants with micro-threads in the neck: a retrospective study over 10 years
- **Authors**: Nam DH, Cho YD, Kim S (analysis by three periodontists: DHN, YDC, SK)
- **Journal**: Journal of Periodontal & Implant Science (DOI 10.5051/jpis.2203240162)
- **PMID**: 37336523 | **PMCID**: PMC10901680
- **Study type**: Retrospective cohort (STROBE; IRB No. S-D20210027, informed consent waived)
- **Setting**: Department of Periodontology, Seoul National University Dental Hospital (SNUDH), Korea

## 2. Key Contributions

1. 10-year (>10 years after prosthesis installation) Kaplan-Meier CSR of 97.9% for a micro-threaded neck, bone-level implant (Oneplant, Warantec, Seoul).
2. Cause-of-failure breakdown for all 10 failures (4 fixture fractures, 3 peri-implantitis, 2 failed osseointegration with fibrous encapsulation, 1 unknown).
3. Cox model: 2-stage surgery HR 4.769 (p=0.039) as the sole significant factor.

## 3. Methodology

- Records and radiographs of patients receiving Oneplant implants during 2006-2010 at SNUDH; follow-up from surgery to December 2020. Inclusion: one or more Oneplant implants, >10 years follow-up after prosthesis installation, sufficient records/radiographs.
- Survival counted for cases classed as implant success, satisfactory survival, or compromised survival under Misch's criteria (ICOI 2008 definition).
- Surgery by an experienced periodontist; 2-stage surgery if insertion torque <30 N·cm, otherwise 1-stage. GBR beforehand or simultaneous; sinus augmentation and immediate placement as needed.
- Three Oneplant types: external hexagon, internal octagon, one-body; sandblasted large-grit acid-etched surface, micro-thread at the neck.
- SPSS 25.0; Kaplan-Meier CSR; chi-square and multiple Cox proportional hazards (backward stepwise) with age, sex, diameter, length, fixture type, location, procedure, timing, GBR, sinus graft, restoration type; significance p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results

- **Sample**: 151 patients (mean age 53 years, range 21-80; 60.2% male), 490 implants; 290 external (59.2%), 166 internal (33.9%), 34 one-body (6.9%); 62.6% maxillary, 87.1% posterior; 306 one-stage (62.4%) vs 184 two-stage (37.6%); 12 immediate (2.4%); GBR 75 (15.3%); lateral sinus 38 (7.8%), crestal sinus 8 (1.7%); restoration: single 89 (18.2%), splinted 338 (69.0%), bridge 63 (12.9%).
- **Survival**: 10/490 failed (2.0%); overall CSR 97.9%.
- **Fixture type**: text states external 98.2% and internal 97.6% (p=0.670); Table 1 lists external 97.6% (7/290 failed), internal 98.2% (3/166), one-body 100% (0/34). Text and table are reversed for external vs internal (reproduced as-is).
- **Cox**: 2-stage vs 1-stage HR 4.769 (p=0.039; 95% CI 1.081-21.033); CSR 97.3% (2-stage; 5/184) vs 98.4% (1-stage; 5/306). Splinted crown HR 0.265 (p=0.094; 95% CI 0.056-1.255), not significant. Female vs male: p=0.141, HR 0.269 (95% CI 0.047-1.544), not significant. Length 13 mm vs 8.5 mm: p=0.092. Many other CIs are degenerate (e.g., 0.000 to >1E+50) because those categories had zero events.
- **Failure causes (Table 2)**: 4 fixture fractures, all internal-type implants (3 at 4 years, 1 at 9 years); 3 peri-implantitis (9 years); 2 fibrous encapsulation (6-7 months); 1 unknown (8 years). Several failures had hypertension; one had diabetes.

## 5. Limitations

- Retrospective, single-centre records review; authors state other clinical parameters (e.g., marginal bone loss, success) were not evaluated.
- Smoking (status, cessation, quantity) and systemic disease/glycaemic control were not analysed (authors' stated limitation).
- No comparison with implants lacking micro-threads, so the micro-thread effect cannot be isolated.
- Only 10 failure events: Cox estimates have very wide or degenerate CIs; 2-stage result (CI 1.081-21.033) is imprecise and likely confounded by indication (2-stage used when torque <30 N·cm or poor bone/grafting).
- Inclusion required >10 years of records, which may exclude lost-to-follow-up patients (censoring/selection bias; inferred from methods).
- Survival defined to include "compromised survival", which the authors acknowledge may yield higher rates than earlier reports.
- Conclusion sentence says other factors "were significantly associated"; Results state p>0.05 (not significant) — apparent typo.

## 7. Glossary

- **CSR**: cumulative survival rate (Kaplan-Meier).
- **GBR**: guided bone regeneration.
- **Micro-thread**: fine threads at the implant neck intended to reduce shear stress and marginal bone loss.
- **Misch criteria / ICOI 2008**: grades implant success, satisfactory survival, compromised survival, failure.
