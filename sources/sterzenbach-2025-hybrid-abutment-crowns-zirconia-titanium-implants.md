## Why Ingested

첫 RCT로 two-piece 지르코니아 임플란트 본체 vs 티타늄 임플란트 본체를 동일한 screw-retained hybrid abutment crown(PEKK/티타늄 base + lithium disilicate crown) 프로토콜에서 직접 비교했다는 점에서 신규성이 있다 — 지르코니아군 누적 생존율이 티타늄 대비 10.7% 낮아 non-inferiority가 입증되지 않았고, 원인이 보철 실패가 아닌 임플란트 수준의 골유착 부전(3예)이었다는 점이 핵심. 기존 [[prosthetic-materials/hosseini-2022-zirconia-metal-implant-premolar-5year-rct]]는 지대주·크라운 재료(지르코니아 vs 금속-세라믹)를 비교한 연구로 임플란트 본체 재료 자체를 다루지 않아 직접 모순은 아니지만, "지르코니아=비열등"이라는 인상을 줄 수 있는 기존 지르코니아 낙관론에 대한 중요한 대비 사례로 함께 읽어야 한다. 어버트먼트 유지력 측면에서는 [[prosthetic-materials/selvi-2025-custom-stock-abutment-fatigue-sem]]과 함께 hybrid/screw-retained abutment의 기계적 신뢰성 논의를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT (n=60, 30/group, Charité-Universitätsmedizin Berlin, DRKS00014866) comparing screw-retained lithium disilicate single implant crowns (SICs) on PEKK-base hybrid abutments over two-piece zirconia implants versus titanium-base hybrid abutments over two-piece titanium implants, followed 3 years.

Zirconia-group cumulative survival was 10.7% lower than titanium (89.3% vs 100%), driven by 3 post-baseline implant losses from incomplete osseointegration (plus 3 early pre-crown implant failures, all revised) — non-inferiority (10% margin) was NOT confirmed, though the Log-Rank test for survival curves was not significant (p=0.068); technical complication rates (modified USPHS) and 12-month PES/WES were statistically similar between groups.

The prosthetic component (crown/abutment) performed comparably in both groups with no crown-level failures, but the lower osseointegration rate of two-piece zirconia implants is a clinically meaningful concern that a non-significant p-value alone would obscure — caution is warranted when selecting zirconia implants for this indication.

## 세줄요약

전향적 RCT(n=60, 군당 30명, 샤리테-베를린대, DRKS00014866) — 투피스 지르코니아 임플란트 위 PEKK 기반 하이브리드 지대주(hybrid abutment)와 투피스 티타늄 임플란트 위 티타늄 기반 하이브리드 지대주에 나사유지형(screw-retained) 리튬디실리케이트 단일 임플란트 크라운(SIC)을 식립해 3년 추적.

지르코니아군 누적 생존율이 티타늄군(100%) 대비 10.7% 낮음(89.3%) — 원인은 크라운 파손이 아닌 임플란트 수준 골유착 부전(기저선 후 3예, 초기 실패 3예는 모두 재시술 성공); 10% 마진의 비열등성(non-inferiority)은 입증되지 않았으나 Log-Rank 생존곡선 비교는 유의차 없음(p=0.068); 변형 USPHS 기술적 합병증 발생률과 12개월 PES/WES는 양군 통계적으로 유사.

보철 구성요소(크라운·지대주) 자체는 양군 모두 준수한 성적을 보였으나(크라운 단독 실패 0건), 지르코니아 임플란트의 낮은 골유착률은 p값만으로는 가려지는 임상적으로 중요한 우려사항 — 해당 적응증에 지르코니아 임플란트를 선택할 때 주의가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First RCT to directly compare cumulative survival and technical complications of all-ceramic screw-retained SICs on two-piece **zirconia implants** versus two-piece **titanium implants** of comparable dimensions (prior evidence gap explicitly noted; EAO position paper / German S3 guidelines had refrained from a recommendation on two-piece ceramic implants due to insufficient clinical evidence).
- Demonstrates that when implant material is the sole intentional variable (same hybrid-abutment / screw-retained / lithium disilicate crown design across arms), the zirconia group fails non-inferiority on 3-year cumulative survival due to implant-level osseointegration failure, not prosthetic/crown failure.
- Uses modified USPHS criteria (adapted from Spies et al., re-modified for screw-retained hybrid abutment SICs) plus PES/WES aesthetic scoring and an exploratory PROM survey — giving a fairly complete picture (survival + technical + esthetic + patient-reported) in a single RCT.
- Highlights a statistics-vs-clinical-significance tension: Log-Rank test on survival curves was NOT significant (p=0.068), while the non-inferiority CI analysis (which is the pre-specified primary analysis) clearly failed the 10% margin — illustrating how p-value framing alone can obscure a clinically important between-group difference.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective RCT, 1:1 urn randomization (60 lottery tickets from a sealed box), unblinded for surgeons (visually distinct implant systems) but blinded outcome assessors (KR, KA, HK) for follow-up clinical inspection.
- **Population**: 60 participants (34 women), mean age 44 years (range 21-67), January 2018-September 2019. Premolar/molar single-tooth gap ≥7.5 mm, residual bone height ≥10 mm (no augmentation needed), ≥4-month post-extraction healing.
- **Exclusions**: heavy smokers (>10 cig/day), substance abuse, uncontrolled systemic disease, bone-metabolism-affecting medications (bisphosphonates, corticosteroids, monoclonal antibodies, TKIs, methotrexate), head/neck irradiation history, coagulopathy/anticoagulation, poor oral hygiene, untreated periodontal disease, bruxism with distinctive wear, material allergy, pregnancy/lactation.
- **Interventions**:
  - Test (zirconia) group: CERALOG Hexalob two-piece zirconia implant (8 mm, ⌀4.0; CAMLOG Biotechnologies) + screw-retained PEKK hybrid abutment + bonded lithium disilicate (LS2) crown (IPS e.max CAD, Ivoclar); gold screw torqued to 15 Ncm.
  - Control (titanium) group: CAMLOG screw-line titanium implant (9 mm, ⌀4.3) + screw-retained titanium adhesive base + bonded LS2 crown (IPS e.max CAD); titanium screw torqued to 25 Ncm.
- **Digital workflow**: full-arch CEREC Omnicam scan → InLab design software → Galileos Implant backward planning → 3D-printed (V-Print SG resin) semi-navigated drilling template. Intraoperative scan via CEREC scan body (titanium group) or a fabricated resin splint on a printed model (zirconia group, no manufacturer scan body available).
- **Timing**: final restoration at 4 months (zirconia) / 3 months (titanium) post-implant placement — "one abutment, one time" concept at second-stage surgery.
- **Sample size**: powered for non-inferiority (assumed 95% 5-year titanium survival, 10% non-inferiority margin, alpha 0.05, power 0.8) → 30 per group.
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier / intention-to-treat for primary cumulative survival; Clopper-Pearson 90%/95% CI for non-inferiority; Log-Rank test for survival curve equality; Fisher's exact, exact Mann-Whitney U, exact Wilcoxon for secondary USPHS/PES/WES comparisons; SPSS v29.
- **Follow-up schedule**: USPHS assessment at 6 weeks post-crown (baseline), then 6, 12, 24, 36 months; PES/WES at baseline and 12 months; PROM questionnaire (unvalidated, author-developed) mailed at 36 months (voluntary).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Cumulative survival (3 years, ITT)**: Titanium 100% (30/30); Zirconia 89.3% (Clopper-Pearson 90% CI two-sided 0.746-0.97) — a 10.7-percentage-point gap.
- **Non-inferiority verdict**: NOT confirmed. Required zirconia lower-CI bound >82.6% (100% − 10% margin's one-sided 95% lower bound logic based on titanium 92.6% worst case); actual zirconia lower bound was 74.6% < 82.6%.
- **Log-Rank test (survival curve equality)**: p=0.068 — not statistically significant, despite the non-inferiority analysis failing.
- **Implant-level failure causes**: 3 early pre-crown zirconia implant failures (revised successfully, treatment completed on schedule) + 3 post-baseline zirconia implant losses (2 in year 1, 1 at 30 months), all due to aseptic connective-tissue encapsulation indicating incomplete osseointegration — zero crown-attributable SIC failures in either group. 2 zirconia-group patients also lost to follow-up.
- **Technical complications (modified USPHS, 36 months)**: no category-D (unacceptable/replacement) events in either group. Category C (repair needed): zirconia group only — 2 chipping events (no clinical impact) + 2 loss-of-retention-to-adhesive-base events (successfully re-cemented). Category B rates (titanium/zirconia): anatomic form 23%/20%; aesthetic/colour 37%/40%; occlusal closure colour 43%/48%; occlusal marginal gap 27%/32% — no significant between-group difference on any USPHS parameter (all p>0.15). Screw loosening: 1 titanium, 2 zirconia (all resolved by screw replacement). Total technical complications: 2 (titanium) vs 6 (zirconia).
- **PES**: baseline zirconia mean 6.13 (SD 2.074) significantly lower than titanium mean 7 (SD 2.226) for clinical-acceptability proportion (p=0.009); by 12 months no significant between-group difference (p=0.085); both groups improved significantly from baseline (titanium 8.38±1.555, zirconia 7.43±1.950; both p<0.001).
- **WES** (12 months): zirconia 7.65 vs titanium 8.33 — no significant difference (p=0.096).
- **PROMs (36 months, voluntary, n=24 responders — 50% titanium, 30% zirconia response rate)**: high overall-satisfaction ("very satisfied"+"satisfied" ≈100%) in both groups across comfort, appearance, chewing function, taste, fit; low response rate precluded formal statistical comparison.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=60, 30/group) limits generalizability and statistical power to detect between-group differences beyond the primary non-inferiority endpoint.
- Unequal dropout between groups (2 lost to follow-up + 3 implant losses concentrated in zirconia arm) may bias comparative outcomes.
- Findings are specific to the tested materials/components (CERALOG Hexalob zirconia implant, PEKK hybrid abutment, CAMLOG screw-line titanium implant, IPS e.max CAD LS2 crowns) and cannot be generalized to other zirconia implant systems.
- PES/WES aesthetic follow-up limited to 12 months — no longitudinal esthetic data beyond that point.
- PROMs lacked a baseline measurement and had a low (30-50%) 3-year response rate, limiting interpretability.
- Authors explicitly flag that statistical non-significance (Log-Rank p=0.068) should not be read as equivalence — the pre-specified non-inferiority analysis (the primary, more conservative test) failed, and the clinically important difference (implant loss confined to the zirconia arm) is the more decision-relevant signal.
- No implant-level micromotion/ISQ or histologic data were reported to directly explain *why* zirconia osseointegration failed in the 3 post-baseline cases — this remains an open mechanistic question the RCT was not designed to answer.

## 6. Related Work

- Compares directly with Spies et al. (source of the original USPHS-based technical-complication criteria for cemented one-piece zirconia implant crowns, modified here for screw-retained hybrid abutment SICs).
- Cites prior reports of zirconia implant osseointegration failure within 24 months (Jank & Hochgatterer; Koller et al.; Brunello et al.; Hossain et al.; Padhye et al.) as consistent with this study's findings.
- Cites Mohseni et al. and Naumann et al. for prior data on titanium-implant lithium-disilicate hybrid abutment crown survival/technical complications, described as broadly consistent with this study's titanium-arm results.
- Notes sparse existing head-to-head evidence directly comparing implant *materials* in two-piece screw-retained systems (Neugebauer et al.), and that EAO/German S3 guidelines had declined to recommend two-piece ceramic implants pending better evidence (Thiem et al.) — this RCT is framed as addressing that gap.
- In this wiki: contrasts with [[prosthetic-materials/hosseini-2022-zirconia-metal-implant-premolar-5year-rct]] (a different comparison — zirconia vs metal-ceramic *abutment/crown* material on presumably shared implant substrate, not implant-body material — so not a direct contradiction, but relevant context for zirconia-implant optimism); complements [[prosthetic-materials/selvi-2025-custom-stock-abutment-fatigue-sem]] on hybrid/screw-retained abutment mechanical reliability.

## 7. Glossary

- **SIC (Single Implant Crown)**: an implant-supported crown restoring a single missing tooth.
- **PEKK (Polyether Ketone Ketone)**: a high-performance thermoplastic polymer used here as a hybrid abutment base material bonded to a ceramic implant, chosen for an elasticity modulus closer to bone (stress-shielding mitigation) versus rigid ceramic or metal.
- **Hybrid abutment (crown)**: a screw-retained restoration combining a metal or polymer (PEKK/PEEK) base/abutment adhesively bonded to an all-ceramic (here lithium disilicate) crown, allowing damage-free retrievability without cementation.
- **Non-inferiority trial**: a trial design testing whether a new treatment (zirconia implants) is not worse than a reference (titanium implants) by more than a pre-specified margin (10% cumulative survival here), rather than testing for superiority.
- **Clopper-Pearson confidence interval**: an exact (non-approximated) method for computing binomial proportion confidence intervals, used here for the cumulative survival rate CIs.
- **Modified USPHS criteria**: an A-D graded clinical evaluation scale (adapted from the United States Public Health Service system) assessing restoration quality parameters (ceramic fracture, surface texture, anatomic form, aesthetics, occlusal marginal gap, connection/retention), modified in this study for screw-retained hybrid abutment ceramic crowns.
- **PES / WES (Pink/White Aesthetic Score)**: 0-10-point composite indices (Fürhauser et al.; Belser et al.) scoring peri-implant soft tissue (pink) and crown (white) esthetics; ≥6 considered clinically acceptable.
- **One abutment-one time**: a protocol placing the definitive abutment/prosthetic component at second-stage surgery without an intervening healing abutment exchange, intended to minimize soft-tissue disruption.
- **Aseptic connective tissue encapsulation**: fibrous (non-osseous) tissue interposed between implant and bone in the absence of infection, indicating a failure of osseointegration.
