## Why Ingested

기존 상악동 자연공(ostium) 관련 페이지들([[sinus-lift/lateral/kim-2019-maxillary-implants-otolaryngologist-perspective]] — ostium patency가 임플란트 안전성을 결정, [[sinus-lift/lateral/kato-2021-sinus-mucosa-ostium-involvement-septa]] — 술후 ostium 침범의 시간 경과)은 ostium이 "왜 중요한지"와 "술후 어떻게 변하는지"를 다루지만, "술전에 몇 mm까지 안전하게 거상해도 되는지"를 계산해주는 정량적 기준이 없었다. 본 논문(Vaddi 2021)은 CBCT 상 ostium-to-floor 높이에서 점막비후 높이를 뺀 **safe height** 공식(치아 있는 경우 ~27.05mm, 무치악 ~23.40mm)을 제시해 그 공백을 메운다. [[sinus-lift/lateral/akbari-2022-maxillary-sinus-mucosal-thickening-ridge-height]]의 점막비후-치조제높이 상관관계를 임상 계산식으로 확장(extends)하는 관계.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT study (200 sinonasal complexes from 112 patients: 100 dentate, 100 edentulous) evaluating the available height for maxillary sinus augmentation without compromising ostium/ostiomeatal-complex drainage.

Mean height from sinus floor to ostium was 31.40±5.04 mm (dentate) vs 29.50±4.41 mm (edentulous); subtracting mean mucosal-thickening height yields a calculated safe augmentation height of ~27.05 mm (dentate) and ~23.40 mm (edentulous); ostium was located near the first molar region in 47% of sinuses.

Single-site retrospective study with excellent inter-rater reliability (Krippendorff α 0.89–0.98) but no standardized safe-height guideline existed before this study; findings support routine preoperative CBCT measurement of ostium-to-floor height minus mucosal thickening for sinus augmentation planning.

## 세줄요약

후향적 CBCT 연구 (112명 환자, 200개 부비동-비강 복합체: 유치악 100·무치악 100)로 상악동공(ostium)/골성구멍복합체(Ostiomeatal Complex, OMC) 배액을 침범하지 않고 상악동거상술(Sinus Augmentation)이 가능한 여유 높이를 평가.

동저-자연공 평균 높이는 유치악 31.40±5.04mm, 무치악 29.50±4.41mm; 여기서 점막비후 평균 높이를 뺀 **안전 거상 높이**는 유치악 약 27.05mm, 무치악 약 23.40mm로 산출; 자연공은 47%에서 제1대구치 부위에 위치.

단일기관 후향적 연구(관찰자간 신뢰도 Krippendorff α 0.89–0.98로 우수)이나 이전까지 표준화된 안전높이 지침이 없었다는 한계; 상악동거상술 술전 CBCT에서 "동저-자연공 높이 − 점막비후 높이" 계산을 일상적으로 적용할 근거를 제공한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Proposes a calculable **"safe height"** for sinus augmentation — sinus-floor-to-ostium height minus mucosal-thickening height — where no standardized protocol previously existed.
2. Reports normative CBCT morphometrics of the maxillary sinus and ostium separately for dentate vs. edentulous patients (200 sinonasal complexes, 112 patients).
3. Localizes the ostium to the first molar region in 47% of sinuses, useful for surgical orientation during lateral-window osteotomy planning.
4. Documents sex-based differences: females have greater alveolar ridge height, males have greater sinus-floor-to-ostium height (both p<0.05).
5. Demonstrates excellent inter-rater reliability (Krippendorff α 0.89–0.98) for the measurement protocol, supporting its reproducibility in clinical use.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective CBCT review, single academic center (2017–2018 archive).
- **Sample**: 112 patients (56 M / 56 F), 200 sinonasal complexes — 100 dentate (age 16–85y) + 100 edentulous (age 36–91y); each side scored individually (not averaged per patient).
- **Scanner**: 3D Accuitomo CBCT (J Morita), 140×100mm or 170×120mm FOV, 90 kVp/7 mA, 250 µm voxel.
- **Reference view**: coronal section through the ostiomeatal complex, analyzed in Invivo 5.0 (Anatomage).
- **Measurements**: (1) sinus top width, (2) sinus middle width, (3) sinus-floor-to-ostium height, (4) alveolar/residual ridge height, (5) mucosal-thickening height.
- **Raters**: 2 oral radiology residents (calibrated), reviewed by a board-certified radiologist; Krippendorff α for inter-rater reliability.
- **Exclusions**: age <15y, developmental sinus variation, prior sinonasal surgery/grafting/augmentation, non-mucosal-thickening sinus pathology.
- **Statistics**: unpaired t-test, dentate-vs-edentulous and male-vs-female comparisons, α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Dentate | Edentulous | Sig. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top width | 8.88±4.90 mm | 8.35±4.32 mm | ns |
| Middle width | 22.20±4.22 mm | 21.20±3.67 mm | p<0.05 (dentate higher) |
| Sinus floor → ostium height | 31.40±5.04 mm | 29.50±4.41 mm | — |
| Alveolar/residual ridge height | 7.17±3.74 mm | 7.59±4.10 mm | p<0.05 (dentate higher) |
| Mucosal thickening prevalence | 64% | 52% | — |
| Mucosal thickening magnitude | 4.05±3.19 mm | 6.40±6.18 mm | p<0.05 (edentulous higher) |
| **Calculated safe augmentation height** | **~27.05 mm** | **~23.40 mm** | (floor→ostium minus mean MT) |

- Ostium located in the first-molar region in 47% of sinuses.
- Ridge height significantly higher in females (p<0.05); sinus-floor-to-ostium height significantly higher in males (p<0.05); no sex difference in top/middle width.
- Inter-rater reliability (Krippendorff α): sinus measurements 0.98, mucosal-thickening height 0.89, ridge height 0.95 — all "excellent" (0.80–1.0).
- Prior literature comparison: floor-to-ostium height reported elsewhere ranged 28.60 mm (El-Anwar, CT) to 33.97 mm (Butaric, MRI coronal) — this study's 29.5–31.4 mm falls within that range.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single university-based site, retrospective design, limited generalizability.
- No pre-/post-augmentation comparison — the "safe height" formula is derived from baseline anatomy only, not validated against actual surgical outcomes.
- 2D coronal-plane linear measurements of a 3D structure; ostium diameter/patency graded qualitatively elsewhere in the literature (e.g., obstruction thresholds) but not quantified here.
- Authors explicitly call for a larger prospective study with pre- and post-augmentation radiographs to validate the safe-height calculation against real complication rates.

## 7. Glossary

- **Ostium (maxillary sinus ostium)**: the natural drainage opening of the maxillary sinus into the middle meatus; mean diameter ~2.4 mm.
- **Ostiomeatal complex (OMC)**: the common drainage pathway through which the maxillary, frontal, and anterior ethmoid sinuses drain; obstruction predisposes to sinusitis.
- **Pneumatization**: physiological expansion of the maxillary sinus into surrounding bone, accelerated after tooth loss.
- **Sinus augmentation / sinus lift**: surgical elevation of the sinus floor with bone grafting to gain implant-placement height in the posterior maxilla.
- **Schneiderian membrane**: the ciliated mucosal lining of the maxillary sinus responsible for mucociliary clearance through the ostium.
- **Safe height (this study's construct)**: sinus-floor-to-ostium height minus mucosal-thickening height — the maximum elevation that avoids blocking the ostium.
