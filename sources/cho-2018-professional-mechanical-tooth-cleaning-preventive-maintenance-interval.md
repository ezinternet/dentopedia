## Why Ingested

Prospective Korean study giving an empirical answer to a question [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/jo-2008-management-of-dental-biofilm-through]] argues for on principle — that recall/management interval should track the patient's own plaque-control skill rather than a fixed default. Also complements [[periodontics/farooqi-2015-periodontal-maintenance-recall-interval-sr]]'s general SPT recall-interval evidence with PMTC-specific Korean data (1-month interval optimal before self-care skill is established; degrades by 3 months).

## Three-line Summary

A prospective interventional study in 33 healthy adults receiving weekly professional mechanical tooth cleaning (PMTC) for 4 visits followed by 3‑ and 6‑month maintenance visits. Plaque control score (O’Leary index) and gingival index (Löe & Silness) were measured at each visit.

At the 4th weekly visit, plaque control score improved from 38.98 to 57.57 (p<0.001) and gingival index decreased from 1.55 to 0.85 (p<0.001). At 3‑month follow‑up, scores worsened (plaque 46.33, GI 1.15; p<0.05), but both remained improved vs baseline. At 6‑month follow‑up, plaque score was 48.93 (p<0.05 vs baseline) and GI was 1.01 (p<0.05 vs 3‑month).

The optimal maintenance interval based on participant questionnaire responses (54.5% chose 1 month) and the finding that hygiene ability declined at 3 months suggests a 1‑month interval when proper self‑care skills are not yet established. Use of phase‑contrast microscopy and evaluation index scores showed motivational potential.

## 세줄요약

건강한 성인 33명을 대상으로 주간 전문가 치면세정술 (Professional Mechanical Tooth Cleaning, PMTC)을 4회 방문 동안 시행하고, 이후 3개월 및 6개월 유지관리 방문을 진행한 전향적 중재 연구. 각 방문에서 치면세균막지수 (O’Leary index)와 치은염지수 (Löe & Silness index)를 측정하였다.

4번째 주간 방문에서 치면세균막관리점수는 38.98에서 57.57로 향상되었고 (p<0.001), 치은염지수는 1.55에서 0.85로 감소하였다 (p<0.001). 3개월 추적관리 시 점수는 악화되었으나 (치면세균막 46.33, 치은염 1.15; p<0.05), 두 지표 모두 기준치보다는 개선된 상태를 유지하였다. 6개월 추적관리 시 치면세균막점수는 48.93 (기준 대비 p<0.05), 치은염지수는 1.01 (3개월 대비 p<0.05)이었다.

참가자 설문 응답 (54.5%가 1개월 선택)과 3개월 시점에서 위생 관리 능력이 저하된 결과를 바탕으로, 올바른 자가관리 능력이 확립되지 않은 상태에서의 적정 관리주기는 1개월로 제안되었다. 위상차현미경과 평가점수 산출이 동기부여에 효과적인 것으로 나타났다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstration that 4 weekly PMTC sessions significantly improved plaque control and gingival health in healthy adults.
- Showed that self‑care ability declined at 3‑month follow‑up, suggesting a 1‑month maintenance interval is more appropriate when oral hygiene skills are not fully established.
- Identified use of phase‑contrast microscopy and feedback of evaluation index scores as potential motivational tools for improving oral hygiene.
- Found that participants with greater interest in dental health had better plaque control scores.
- Reported that 54.5% of participants recommended a 1‑month maintenance interval after completing the program.
- Confirmed that a 6‑month follow‑up showed partial recovery of gingival health compared to 3‑month, indicating benefits of re‑education.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design:** Prospective interventional study with repeated measures.
- **Participants:** 33 healthy adults (15 male, 18 female; mean age 40s; 84.9% college educated). Recruited via dental clinic website. Excluded those with multiple cavities requiring endodontic treatment or advanced periodontitis with alveolar bone loss.
- **Intervention:** All participants received initial scaling and rotary toothbrushing instruction. Thereafter, PMTC with low‑speed handpiece, EVA tip, and rubber cup was performed weekly from Visit 1 to Visit 4, along with toothbrushing education. Follow‑up visits at 3 months and 6 months.
- **Motivational aids:** At Visit 1, phase‑contrast microscopy was used to visualize oral bacteria. At follow‑up visits, evaluation index scores were provided verbally and via text message.
- **Outcomes:** Plaque control score (O’Leary index) assessed on 4 surfaces of 6 selected teeth. Gingival index (Löe & Silness) assessed on 4 surfaces per tooth.
- **Statistics:** Paired t‑test for within‑group changes over visits; independent t‑test for group comparisons (interest in dental health, perceived gum health); one‑way ANOVA for motivation method comparisons. PASW Statistics v.18.0 used.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome — Plaque control score:** Increased from 38.98±15.28 at baseline to 57.57±10.09 at Visit 4 (p<0.001). Dropped to 46.33±15.75 at 3‑month follow‑up (p<0.05 vs Visit 4), but remained higher than baseline (p<0.05). At 6‑month follow‑up: 48.93±15.63 (p<0.05 vs baseline; p>0.05 vs 3‑month).
- **Primary outcome — Gingival index:** Decreased from 1.55±0.34 at baseline to 0.85±0.26 at Visit 4 (p<0.001). Increased to 1.15±0.26 at 3‑month follow‑up (p<0.001 vs Visit 4), then slightly decreased to 1.01±0.22 at 6‑month follow‑up (p<0.001 vs 3‑month).
- **Interest in dental health:** ‘Interested’ group had significantly better plaque control scores than ‘not interested’ group (p<0.05).
- **Motivation method:** Phase‑contrast microscopy showed trend toward improving plaque scores and gingival index at Visit 1 (scores not reported); evaluation index showed similar trend at follow‑ups (p>0.05, not significant).
- **Recommended interval:** 1 month (54.5%), 3 months (39.4%), 6 months (6.1%).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample size (n=33) limits generalizability.
- Short study duration may not reflect long‑term maintenance outcomes.
- Lack of a control group (no comparison with standard scaling or no‑intervention arm).
- The motivational effect of phase‑contrast microscopy and evaluation index was not statistically significant (p>0.05).
- Attrition or loss to follow‑up not reported.
- Future research should examine individualized maintenance intervals based on patients' oral hygiene skill acquisition and attitude change over longer periods.

## 6. Related Work

- Han et al. (2008) — Professional tooth cleaning and plaque control instruction effects according to subject characteristics; reported decline in scores at 3‑month follow‑up.
- Eom et al. (2009) — Enhancement of plaque control score following individualized repeated instruction; similar decline at 3 months.
- Jo and Min (2010) — Repeated toothbrushing education improves plaque control ability.
- Grace et al. (1994) — Emphasized patient re‑education and motivation for behavior modification.
- Keyes and Rams (1983) — Recommended phase‑contrast microscopy for rapid identification of microbial targets and patient motivation.

## 7. Glossary

- **PMTC (Professional Mechanical Tooth Cleaning)**: 전문가 치면세정술. 저속 핸드피스와 러버컵을 이용한 전문가 구강 세정술.
- **Plaque Control Score (O’Leary index)**: 치면세균막관리점수. 6개 치아의 4면에서 세균막 부착 여부를 평가하여 점수화.
- **Gingival Index (Löe & Silness index)**: 치은염지수. 치은 염증을 4부위(근심, 원심, 협측, 설측)에서 0~3점으로 평가.
- **Phase‑contrast microscope**: 위상차현미경. 구강 내 세균 활성도를 시각화하여 환자 동기부여에 사용.
- **Evaluation index**: 평가점수. 구강위생관리 평가 결과를 환자에게 피드백하여 자가 관리 동기부여.
