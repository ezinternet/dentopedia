## Why Ingested

위키의 위축악(severe atrophic jaw) 재건 사다리는 현재 골이식(graft) → 짧은 임플란트(short implant) → 경사·풀아치(tilted/full-arch)로 이어지고, 수직골증대(vertical ridge augmentation)가 공격적 옵션으로 배치돼 있다. 그러나 재이식·재수술을 피하려는 **무이식(graftless) salvage 단계**인 맞춤형 골막하 임플란트(customized subperiosteal implant, CSI)는 위키에 전무했다. 본 SR+MA(Cosola 2026)는 이 빈칸을 채우며, 특히 "우수한 단기 생존율이 장기적으로 *연조직* 실패로 붕괴한다"는 패턴을 보여주는데 — 이는 [[implants/soft-tissue]]와 [[overviews/peri-implant-soft-tissue-dehiscence-prevention]]가 가르치는 연조직 우선(soft-tissue-primacy) 교훈과 동일하다. 기존 위축악 대안인 [[implants/barausse-2024-4mm-short-implants-posterior-atrophic-8year]], [[sinus-lift/lateral/chaware-2021-short-vs-long-implant-sinus-graft-sr-ma]], [[implants/del-fabbro-2022-full-arch-tilted-axial-implants-sr-ma]], 그리고 종합 결정 페이지 [[overviews/short-implant-vs-sinus-augmentation-decision]] · [[overviews/vertical-ridge-augmentation-overview]]에 salvage rung을 대비시키는 근거로 활용.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis of 11 studies (RCTs, cohorts, case series; 268 patients, 369 implants) on modern customized CAD/CAM 3D-printed titanium subperiosteal implants (CSI) for severely atrophic jaws, searched to November 2025 (PRISMA, PROSPERO-registered, random-effects pooling).

Short-term survival was high (97.8% at ≤3-year follow-up) but the overall pooled survival was 92.4% with substantial heterogeneity driven by long-term data — the single 6-year study reported survival falling to 54.1%; soft tissue–related complications (implant exposure, dehiscence) were the most common adverse event and the main cause of late failure.

Authors conclude CSI give predictable short-term rehabilitation but survival alone overestimates clinical success; they should be a salvage option for carefully selected patients — this is an abstract-only page (JOMS full text paywalled, not retrieved).

## 세줄요약

심하게 위축된 악골에 쓰는 최신 맞춤형 CAD/CAM 3D 프린팅 티타늄 골막하 임플란트(CSI)를 다룬 SR+MA로, 2025년 11월까지 검색해 11편(RCT·코호트·증례군; 환자 268명, 임플란트 369개)을 random-effects로 통합했다.

단기 생존율은 높았으나(≤3년 97.8%) 전체 통합 생존율은 92.4%로 장기 데이터가 주도하는 상당한 이질성을 보였고 — 유일한 6년 추적 연구에서는 생존율이 54.1%까지 떨어졌으며, 연조직 관련 합병증(임플란트 노출·열개)이 가장 흔한 부작용이자 후기 실패의 주원인이었다.

저자들은 CSI가 단기 재건에는 예측 가능하나 생존율만으로는 임상 성공을 과대평가하며, 신중히 선별된 환자의 salvage 옵션으로 써야 한다고 결론지었다 — 본 페이지는 초록 전용(JOMS 전문은 페이월, 미확보)이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First meta-analytic pooling (to Nov 2025) of the **re-emerged, additively manufactured** generation of subperiosteal implants — distinct from the discredited mid-20th-century cast-frame subperiosteal implants that fell out of use for high failure/infection.
- Positions CSI as a **graftless alternative** to extensive bone augmentation for severe jaw atrophy (Cawood–Howell class V–VI territory), enabling immediate fixed rehabilitation.
- Quantifies the **survival-vs-time collapse**: high ≤3-year survival that is not sustained long-term.
- Identifies **soft tissue complications (exposure, dehiscence)** — not osseointegration failure — as the dominant adverse event and main driver of late failure.

## 7. Glossary

- **Subperiosteal implant**: a custom metal framework that rests **on** the residual bone under the periosteum (not embedded within bone like an endosseous implant), transmitting load via transmucosal posts.
- **CSI / AMSJI**: Customized Subperiosteal Implant / Additively Manufactured Subperiosteal Jaw Implant — the modern CAD/CAM, 3D-printed (direct metal laser sintering) generation.
- **Graftless rehabilitation**: fixed prosthetic rehabilitation of an atrophic jaw without bone augmentation.
- **Dehiscence / exposure**: soft tissue breakdown exposing the implant framework — here the leading late-failure mechanism.
- **Cawood–Howell classification**: staging of edentulous jaw resorption; severe atrophy = classes V–VI.
