## Why Ingested

CBCT-based assessment of bone formation 6 months after graft-free OSFE with IPL/RBH/ESBG correlation — preliminary clinical data complementing [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/duan-2017-graft-free-maxillary-sinus-floor-elevation-sr-ma]] and [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/yu-2021-implant-protrusion-length-nongrafting-osfe]].

## One-line Summary

Preliminary study (n=31 implants/27 patients): significant 6-month bone formation after graft-free OSFE (mean ESBG 1.8 mm); IPL positively correlated with ESBG; RBH negatively correlated with ESBG; 100% survival.

## 한줄요약

전향적 예비 연구(31개 임플란트/27명): 무이식 OSFE 6개월 후 유의한 신생골 형성(평균 상악동내 골 증가량 (Endo-Sinus Bone Gain, ESBG) 1.8mm); IPL이 ESBG와 양의 상관관계; RBH는 ESBG와 음의 상관관계; 생존율 100%.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: International Journal of Implant Dentistry, 5:27
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s40729-019-0181-7
- **Institution**: Srinakharinwirot University, Bangkok, Thailand
- **Study design**: Retrospective-prospective mixed design (clinical and CBCT data from cases with history of OSFE without graft)
- **n**: 31 implants in 27 patients (areas 14–17 and 24–27)
- **Follow-up**: 6 months post-implantation

## 2. Key Contributions

- Mean RBH before surgery: 7.14 ± 1.07 mm; at 6 months: 8.95 ± 1.17 mm
- Significant increase in new bone formation at 6 months (p<0.05)
- Mean ESBG: 1.8 ± 0.79 mm; mean IPL: 2.02 ± 0.73 mm
- Significant positive correlation between IPL and ESBG (p<0.05)
- Negative correlation between RBH and ESBG (higher preop RBH → less ESBG)
- Percentage of bone formation relative to IPL decreases as IPL increases
- 100% implant survival rate at 6 months

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Selection**: Implants with prior OSFE without grafting; CBCT pre-op and at 6 months
- **Measurements**: RBH, new bone level, ESBG (= new bone height - RBH after surgery), IPL
- **Statistics**: Paired sample t-test (pre vs post bone levels); Pearson correlation (IPL vs ESBG; RBH vs ESBG)
- **CBCT**: Pre-operative + 6-month post-op radiographic evaluation

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Pre-op RBH | 7.14 ± 1.07 mm |
| Post-op bone level (6mo) | 8.95 ± 1.17 mm |
| Mean ESBG | 1.8 ± 0.79 mm |
| Mean IPL | 2.02 ± 0.73 mm |
| IPL–ESBG correlation | Positive (p<0.05) |
| RBH–ESBG correlation | Negative |
| Survival rate | 100% |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (31 implants, 27 patients); preliminary study
- Short follow-up (6 months); no long-term ISR or MBL data
- Mixed retrospective-prospective design; no randomization
- No comparison arm (with graft)
- Single institution, single ethnicity (Thai)

## 6. Related Work

- Yu 2021: 3–9 year retrospective, 105 implants; IPL <4mm recommended; optimal ESBG range
- Albash 2023: TSFE without graft, 34 implants; IPL-IBG correlation confirmed
- Duan 2017: SR+MA of graft-free transcrestal; mean survival 91.8%

## 7. Glossary

- **OSFE (Osteotome Sinus Floor Elevation, 골절도 상악동저 거상술)**: transalveolar sinus elevation using osteotomes (Summers technique)
- **ESBG (Endo-Sinus Bone Gain, 상악동내 골 증가량)**: net increase in sinus bone height after SFE
- **RBH (Residual Bone Height, 잔존골 높이)**: pre-operative bone height below sinus floor
- **IPL (Implant Protrusion Length, 임플란트 돌출 길이)**: implant length extending above sinus floor into sinus
