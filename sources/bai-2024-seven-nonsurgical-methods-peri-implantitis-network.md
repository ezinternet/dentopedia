## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/peri-implantitis/mauriello-2026-peri-implant-mucositis-adjunctive-narrative-review]]가 peri-implant mucositis(PM)의 화학·약물 보조요법을 서술적으로 리뷰했다면, 본 network meta-analysis(NMA, 네트워크 메타분석)는 laser·PBMT·PDT·SA·probiotics·LA·APP 7개 비외과적 보조요법을 SUCRA 순위로 정량 비교해 peri-implantitis(PI)와 PM 양쪽에서 어떤 조합이 PPD·BoP·PLI·CAL·MBL 각 지표에 가장 유리한지 근거를 보강한다. [[implants/herrera-2023-prevention-treatment-peri-implant-efp]] (EFP S3 가이드라인)의 전신항생제 신중 사용 권고와도 직접 연결된다.

## Three-line Summary

Network meta-analysis (frequency random-effects model) of 33 RCTs (2004–2023; PubMed/Embase/Web of Science/Cochrane search to July 2023; PROSPERO CRD42023447616) comparing 7 non-surgical adjuncts — laser, PBMT, PDT, systemic antibiotics (SA), probiotics, local antimicrobials (LA), air-powder polishing (APP) — each combined with mechanical debridement (MD), versus MD alone, in peri-implantitis (PI) and peri-implant mucositis (PM).

For PI, PBMT+MD ranked best for pocket probing depth (PPD) reduction (SUCRA 75.3%), and SA+MD ranked best for clinical attachment level (CAL, SUCRA 87.4%, SMD 2.20) and marginal bone loss (MBL, SUCRA 99.9%, SMD 3.92) versus MD alone; for PM, probiotics+MD ranked best for PPD (SUCRA 100%) and plaque index (PLI, SUCRA 83.2%), and SA+MD ranked best for bleeding on probing (BoP, SUCRA 88.1%, SMD 0.77).

Rankings differ by outcome and by disease stage (PI vs PM), so treatment choice should be individualized per clinical indicator rather than a single "best" adjunct; evidence remains limited by few large direct-comparison trials, heterogeneous follow-up (3–12 months), and small CAL/MBL study numbers, and the authors caution that SUCRA rankings should still defer to current guidelines (e.g., EFP S3 advises against routine systemic antibiotics).

## 세줄요약

네트워크 메타분석(33편 RCT, 2004–2023, PROSPERO 등록)이 laser·PBMT·PDT·전신항생제(SA)·프로바이오틱스·국소항균제(LA)·에어파우더폴리싱(APP) 7개 비외과적 보조요법을 기계적 세정(MD)과 병용해 peri-implantitis(PI)와 peri-implant mucositis(PM)에서 비교했다.

PI에서는 PBMT+MD가 PPD 개선 1위(SUCRA 75.3%), SA+MD가 CAL(SUCRA 87.4%, SMD 2.20)과 MBL(SUCRA 99.9%, SMD 3.92) 개선 1위였고, PM에서는 프로바이오틱스+MD가 PPD(SUCRA 100%)·PLI(SUCRA 83.2%) 개선 1위, SA+MD가 BoP 개선 1위(SUCRA 88.1%, SMD 0.77)였다.

지표·질환 단계별로 최적 보조요법이 달라 개별화된 선택이 필요하며, 직접비교 대규모 RCT 부족·추적기간 이질성(3~12개월)·CAL/MBL 연구 수 부족이 한계이고 최신 가이드라인(EFP S3의 전신항생제 신중사용 권고 등)과 함께 해석해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First network meta-analysis (NMA) to compare 7 non-surgical adjunctive therapies simultaneously — laser, photobiomodulation therapy (PBMT), photodynamic therapy (PDT), systemic antibiotics (SA), probiotics, local antimicrobials (LA), air-powder polishing (APP) — each combined with mechanical debridement (MD), against MD alone, across both peri-implantitis (PI) and peri-implant mucositis (PM).
- Unlike prior pairwise meta-analyses that focused mainly on PPD and BoP, this NMA additionally analyzes plaque index (PLI), clinical attachment level (CAL), and marginal bone loss (MBL) — indicators of antimicrobial efficacy and hard/soft tissue healing.
- Produces SUCRA (surface under the cumulative ranking area) rankings that let clinicians pick individualized adjuncts by outcome and by disease stage (PI vs PM) rather than a single blanket recommendation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA/Cochrane Handbook–compliant systematic review + frequentist random-effects network meta-analysis (STATA 17.0, Network package).
- **Search**: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Library through July 25, 2023, plus manual reference cross-checking.
- **PICO**: Adults (≥18y) with ≥1 implant diagnosed with PI or PM per the 2018 Classification of Periodontal and Peri-implant Diseases; intervention = any non-surgical adjunct + MD; comparator = MD alone; outcomes = PPD (primary), BoP/PLI/CAL/MBL (secondary); RCTs only, ≥5 patients/group, ≥3-month follow-up.
- **Exclusions**: animal/in-vitro studies, surgical approaches, systemic-disease subjects, all-smoker cohorts, unclear/missing outcome data, non-RCTs.
- **Screening**: 2,244 records identified → 887 duplicates removed → 1,299 excluded at title/abstract → 58 full-text reviewed → **33 RCTs included** (published 2004–2023).
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB tool; 10 studies low overall risk, 4 high risk, remainder some risk. 29 studies detailed randomization method, 20 described allocation concealment.
- **Statistics**: SMD with 95% CI; I² heterogeneity thresholds 25/50/75% (mild/moderate/high); random-effects model when I²≥50%; consistency evaluated via global inconsistency test, node-splitting (local), and loop inconsistency factor (IF); SUCRA (0=worst, 1=best) for ranking; sensitivity analysis excluding poor/fair-quality trials; funnel plots for publication bias.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Study distribution by modality (33 RCTs)**: MD+laser (8), MD+PBMT (2), MD+PDT (3), MD+SA (6), MD+probiotics (4), MD+LA (10), MD+APP (3). Outcome reporting: PPD (32 studies), BoP (15), PLI (9), CAL (7), MBL (6).

**Peri-implantitis (PI):**
- **PPD reduction (32 RCTs)**: no significant global inconsistency (p=0.71). Vs MD alone: SA+MD (SMD 1.45, 95% CI 0.12–2.78) and LA+MD (SMD 1.30, 95% CI 0.03–2.56) statistically significant. SUCRA ranking topped by **PBMT+MD (75.3%)**.
- **CAL changes (7 studies, 5 treatments)**: vs MD alone, only SA+MD significant (SMD 2.20, 95% CI 0.38–4.02). SUCRA topped by **SA+MD (87.4%)**.
- **MBL changes (6 RCTs, 3 treatments)**: significant global inconsistency (p=0). SA+MD vs MD (SMD 3.92, 95% CI 2.90–4.93), APP+MD vs MD (SMD 2.05, 95% CI 1.24–2.87), SA+MD vs APP+MD (SMD 1.86, 95% CI 0.56–3.17), SA+MD vs laser+MD (SMD 4.24, 95% CI 3.15–5.33), APP+MD vs laser+MD (SMD 2.38, 95% CI 1.47–3.28) all significant. SUCRA topped by **SA+MD (99.9%)**.
- BoP and PLI for PI were reported by too few studies (1–2 treatment arms) to meaningfully NMA.

**Peri-implant mucositis (PM):**
- **PPD reduction**: significant global inconsistency (p=0). Vs MD alone: PBMT+MD (SMD 1.11, 95% CI 0.38–1.84), SA+MD (SMD 1.03, 95% CI 0.02–2.03), LA+MD (SMD 3.59, 95% CI 2.43–4.76), APP+MD (SMD 3.70, 95% CI 2.58–4.82) all significant; probiotics+MD vs MD (SMD 0.37, 95% CI −0.23–0.97) was **not** significant despite ranking first by SUCRA. SUCRA topped by **probiotics+MD (100%)**.
- **BoP reduction (7 RCTs, 4 treatments)**: SA+MD (SMD 0.77, 95% CI 0.27–1.28) and probiotics+MD (SMD 0.60, 95% CI 0.14–1.06) significant vs MD alone. SUCRA: **SA+MD (88.1%)** top, probiotics+MD (75.7%) second.
- **PLI reduction (6 RCTs, 4 treatments)**: no pairwise comparison statistically significant. SUCRA topped by **probiotics+MD (83.2%)**.

**Publication bias**: funnel plots roughly symmetric with most effect points near the top → low risk of publication bias, results considered relatively reliable.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Few large-sample RCTs available for direct head-to-head comparison, limiting precision of some network estimates.
- Follow-up windows pooled across 3/6/12 months despite some treatments (e.g., Nd:YAG laser) showing early-but-not-late significance — a source of potential bias.
- Small number of studies contributing to CAL (7) and MBL (6) outcomes limits confidence in those rankings.
- Could not assess additional clinically important outcomes: implant survival rate, pain scores, swelling grades.
- Substantial heterogeneity across included RCTs in study design, patient populations, diagnostic criteria, treatment protocols, follow-up duration, and outcome measurement — authors caution definitive conclusions are premature.
- Authors explicitly note that despite SUCRA rankings, systemic antibiotic use is discouraged by the 2023 EFP S3-level clinical practice guideline (Herrera et al. 2023) for peri-implant disease due to patient/public-health considerations; probiotics may be considered adjunctive for PM per the same guideline.
- Future work: more high-quality RCTs, especially direct multi-arm comparisons between adjunctive modalities, and inclusion of implant-survival/patient-reported outcomes.

## 6. Related Work

- Barbato et al. (conventional meta-analysis; ref. [54] in this paper) — found SA+MD superior for PPD in PI, but did not include PBMT and other modalities, partly explaining divergence from this NMA's PBMT+MD PPD ranking.
- Ren et al. 2017 (meta-analysis of low-level laser therapy adjunct to non-surgical periodontal therapy; ref. [53]) — corroborates PBMT's favorable effect on PPD, consistent with this NMA's PI PPD finding.
- Vives-Soler et al. 2020 (systematic review, probiotics + non-surgical periodontal therapy for chronic periodontitis; ref. [57]) — parallels this NMA's PM probiotics findings.
- Herrera et al. 2023 EFP S3-level clinical practice guideline (ref. [56]) — informs the clinical caveat on systemic antibiotic use and probiotics for PM despite this NMA's SUCRA rankings; already in wiki as [[implants/herrera-2023-prevention-treatment-peri-implant-efp]].
- [[implants/peri-implantitis/mauriello-2026-peri-implant-mucositis-adjunctive-narrative-review]] — narrative review of chemical/pharmaceutical PM adjuncts (chlorhexidine, local antibiotics, sodium hypochlorite, probiotics, bioactive agents); its conclusion of "no consistent statistically significant added benefit over PMPR alone" for probiotics is consistent with this NMA's PM PPD finding that probiotics+MD, despite ranking SUCRA-first, was not statistically significant vs MD alone.

## 7. Glossary

- **PI (Peri-implantitis, 임플란트 주위염)**: inflammatory disease of peri-implant tissues with progressive supporting bone loss.
- **PM (Peri-implant mucositis, 임플란트 주위점막염)**: inflammation of peri-implant mucosa without bone loss.
- **MD (Mechanical debridement, 기계적 세정)**: primary non-surgical treatment removing plaque/calculus from implant surfaces.
- **PBMT (Photobiomodulation therapy, 광생체조절요법)**: low-level laser therapy enhancing microcirculation and cell proliferation.
- **PDT (Photodynamic therapy, 광역동요법)**: photosensitizer + specific-wavelength laser generating reactive oxygen species (ROS) to kill bacteria.
- **SA (Systemic antibiotics, 전신항생제)**: e.g., metronidazole, amoxicillin, azithromycin, tetracycline.
- **LA (Local antimicrobials, 국소항균제)**: e.g., chlorhexidine chips, doxycycline, minocycline, sodium hypochlorite.
- **APP (Air-powder polishing, 에어파우더폴리싱)**: glycine/erythritol-based powder + compressed air biofilm removal.
- **PPD (Pocket probing depth, 치주낭깊이)**: primary outcome; distance from mucosal margin to pocket base.
- **BoP (Bleeding on probing, 탐침시출혈)**: soft-tissue inflammation marker.
- **PLI (Plaque index, 치태지수)**: biofilm accumulation marker.
- **CAL (Clinical attachment level, 임상부착수준)**: distance from implant-abutment junction to pocket base; epithelial reattachment marker.
- **MBL (Marginal bone loss, 변연골소실)**: radiographic bone-level change; long-term implant-health indicator.
- **SUCRA (Surface under the cumulative ranking area)**: 0–100% probability-based treatment ranking metric from NMA (100% = best).
- **NMA (Network meta-analysis, 네트워크 메타분석)**: statistical method combining direct + indirect evidence across multiple treatment comparisons.
- **SMD (Standardized mean difference, 표준화평균차)**: effect-size measure; Cohen's criteria: ≥0.8 large, 0.5–0.8 medium, 0.2–0.5 small, <0.2 trivial.
- **IF (Inconsistency factor)**: measures disagreement between direct and indirect evidence in a closed treatment loop.
