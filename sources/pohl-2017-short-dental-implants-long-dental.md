## Why Ingested

This paper provides 3-year randomized controlled data comparing 6 mm short implants vs 11–15 mm long implants with sinus floor elevation in the posterior maxilla with 5–7 mm residual bone height. It extends the same multicentre trial reported as Part 1 (1 year) in [[implants/short-narrow/thoma-2015-short-implants-sinus-floor-elevation]] and is relevant to ongoing discussion about short implants avoiding sinus augmentation.

## Three-line Summary

Study type/context: Multicentre RCT in posterior maxilla with 5–7 mm residual bone height randomized to 6 mm short implants (GS) or 11–15 mm long implants + sinus grafting (GG).  
Primary result with numbers: At 3 years, implant survival 100% in both groups (94/101 patients, 129/137 implants); marginal bone level change from implant placement to FU-3 was −0.43 ± 0.58 mm (GG) and −0.44 ± 0.56 mm (GS), no between-group difference.  
Clinical implication/key limitation: Both approaches show high survival at 3 years in this indication; differences in PPD observed at FU-3 (p=0.035) with no clear clinical advantage demonstrated.

## 세줄요약

연구 유형/맥락: 잔존 골높이 5–7 mm의 상악 구치부에서 6 mm 단축 임플란트(GS)와 11–15 mm 장기 임플란트 + 상악동거상(GG)을 무작위 비교한 다기관 RCT.  
주요 결과(수치): 3년 추적 시 양군 생존율 100% (94/101 환자, 129/137 임플란트). 임플란트 식립 시점부터 FU-3까지 변연골 수준 변화는 GG −0.43 ± 0.58 mm, GS −0.44 ± 0.56 mm으로 군간 유의차 없음.  
임상적 시사점/한계: 잔존골 5–7 mm에서 6 mm 단축 임플란트와 상악동거상을 동반한 장기 임플란트 모두 3년간 높은 생존율을 보이며, 본 연구 범위 내에서는 두 치료법이 대안이 될 수 있음.

- Study design: Multicentre randomized controlled clinical trial

## 2. Key Contributions

- 3-year data from the same trial as Thoma et al. 2015 (Part 1) comparing short (6 mm) vs long (11–15 mm) + sinus floor elevation.
- Demonstrates 100% implant survival in both groups at 3 years under controlled conditions.
- Reports marginal bone level changes and clinical parameters (PPD, BoP, PCR) with between-group comparisons.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Patients: 101 patients with partial edentulism in posterior maxilla, residual bone height 5–7 mm
- Randomization: short implants 6 mm (GS) vs long implants 11–15 mm simultaneously with sinus grafting (GG)
- Timing: implant placement (IP), loading with single crowns at 6 months (PR), yearly follow-up
- Outcomes: implant survival, marginal bone level changes (MBL), probing pocket depth (PPD), bleeding on probing (BoP), plaque accumulation (PCR), adverse effects
- Analysis: descriptive + non-parametric comparison between groups

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Enrolled 101 patients, 137 implants placed; FU-3: 94 patients, 129 implants examined
- Implant survival: 100% in both groups
- MBL at FU-3: 0.45 mm (GG) vs 0.44 mm (GS), p>0.05
- MBL change IP→FU-3: −0.43 ± 0.58 mm (GG), −0.44 ± 0.56 mm (GS)
- MBL change PR→FU-3: −0.25 ± 0.58 mm (GG), −0.1 ± 0.54 mm (GS) (significant loss in GG, not in GS)
- PCR/BoP similar between groups at FU-3; PPD differed between groups (p=0.035)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-crown restorations in selected indication (residual height 5–7 mm); generalizability limited
- Relatively short observation (3 years); longer-term data needed
- Industry funding (Dentsply Sirona)

## 7. Glossary

- 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC): 임플란트 표면과 직접 접촉하는 골조직의 비율
- 변연골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL): 임플란트 경부에서 변연골까지의 거리
- 상악동저거상술 (Sinus Floor Elevation, SFE): 상악동저를 상승시켜 골이식재와 함께 임플란트 식립 공간 확보
- 단축 임플란트 (Short Dental Implant): 일반적으로 길이 6–8 mm 정도로 정의되는 임플란트
