## Why Ingested

Serves the 2026-11-12 `zirconia-occlusal-adjustment` surveillance topic from its manufacturing-tool side: it quantifies how far grinding-tool wear (a single diamond bur set taken from fresh to 3% remaining life across 32 units) can degrade the marginal gap of a zirconia-containing glass-ceramic (ZLS) crown before fit leaves the clinically tolerable range — the bench analogue of asking "how much grinding-induced error is tolerable" in the adjustment workflow. Anchors that answer to pages we already hold on the same outcome: [[dental-materials/ceramic/omar-2026-marginal-gap-additive-subtractive-nanoceramic]] (milling vs printing against the same 120 µm limit) and [[dental-materials/zirconia/shely-2024-self-adhesive-cements-zirconia-lithium]] (same group, same ZLS + Primescan + typodont protocol, cement instead of bur wear as the variable).

## Three-line Summary

In vitro SEM study: 32 Zirconia-reinforced Lithium Silicate (ZLS, Celtra Duo) single crowns ground consecutively on one CEREC MC XL 4-axis unit with a single diamond bur set (Step Bur 12S + Cylinder Pointed Bur 12S), split into 4 groups of n=8 by grinding sequence so bur life fell from 73% remaining (after crown 8) to 3% (after crown 32); marginal gap measured at 4 sites × 3 measurements per crown at ×250 after temporary cementation with 50 N static load.

Total mean marginal gap did not differ significantly between wear groups — 47.20 ± 5.16 µm (Group 1), 44.62 ± 7.23 µm (Group 2), 54.14 ± 10.02 µm (Group 3), 48.53 ± 7.87 µm (Group 4); F(3,28) = 2.143, p = 0.117 — and no surface reached significance (distal p = 0.277, mesial p = 0.732, palatal p = 0.251, buccal p = 0.084).

All gaps (44.6–54.1 µm) sat far below the McLean–von Fraunhofer 120 µm clinical limit, so a standard bur set appears safe for at least 32 ZLS units; but the non-monotonic pattern (the most worn group fitted better than Group 3) plus n=8 warns that this is a "no detectable harm by 32 units" result, not proof that wear is irrelevant beyond that point.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to isolate **cumulative diamond-bur wear** as the independent variable for **marginal gap** (not 3D trueness/precision) of ZLS single crowns — the earlier Avrahami work on the same material/system measured global 3D superimposition accuracy.
- Provides a **wear-decay curve tied to the machine's own wear readout**: 73% → 57% → 30% → 3% remaining bur life at crowns 8, 16, 24, 32, i.e. the exact interval at which Dentsply Sirona recommends bur replacement for ZLS.
- Empirically validates the **32-unit replacement interval** for ZLS on a 4-axis unit against the 120 µm clinical acceptability threshold, with all group means under 55 µm.
- Contradicts the **linear wear-deterioration expectation** reported for lithium disilicate (Tan & Dudley): fit did not degrade monotonically, suggesting the electronic bur measurement (EBM) wear-compensation algorithm offsets wear within this window.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Abutment**: one machine-prepared plastic typodont maxillary right canine (Nissin UR31C Jacket) — 1.2 mm circumferential shoulder, 6° axial convergence, 2 mm incisal reduction; scanned with Primescan, exported as STL, one master design in CEREC SW 5.1.3.
- **Virtual design parameters**: radial spacer 90 µm, incisal spacer 90 µm, proximal/incisal/dynamic contact strength 25 µm, radial minimum thickness 1000 µm, incisal minimum thickness 1500 µm, margin thickness 50 µm, margin ramp width 50 µm, margin ramp angle 60°.
- **Grinding**: 32 crowns from ZLS blocks on CEREC MC XL with one fresh bur set (Step Bur 12S + Cylinder Pointed Bur 12S), ground consecutively; 4 groups of n=8 by sequence (1–8, 9–16, 17–24, 25–32) representing progressive wear.
- **Cementation**: non-eugenol temporary cement (Temp-Bond NE Unidose, KaVo Kerr) under 50 N constant axial static load (Lutron FG-20KG) until set; crown removed with GC pliers and abutment cleaned with 96% ethanol between measurements.
- **Measurement**: SEM (JSM-IT100, JEOL) at ×250; mid-buccal, mid-palatal, mid-mesial, mid-distal; 3 measurements per site = 12 per crown.
- **Statistics**: Kolmogorov–Smirnov normality; one-way ANOVA across groups, α = 0.05; G*Power sample-size justification — n=8/group detects Cohen's d = 0.626 at 80% power.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group (crowns) | Bur life after group | Total mean marginal gap (TMMG) |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 (1–8) | 73% | 47.20 ± 5.16 µm |
| Group 2 (9–16) | 57% | 44.62 ± 7.23 µm (lowest) |
| Group 3 (17–24) | 30% | 54.14 ± 10.02 µm (highest) |
| Group 4 (25–32) | 3% | 48.53 ± 7.87 µm |

- One-way ANOVA on TMMG: **F(3,28) = 2.143, p = 0.117** → null hypothesis (no wear effect) accepted.
- Per-surface ANOVA: distal p = 0.277, mesial p = 0.732, palatal p = 0.251, buccal p = 0.084 — no surface-specific wear effect (distal and palatal were numerically larger but not significantly).
- Normality: Kolmogorov–Smirnov p > 0.05 for all study variables.
- **All values remained far under the 120 µm clinical limit** (McLean & von Fraunhofer), with the range spanning only 44.62–54.14 µm — comparable to SEM-measured lithium disilicate crowns at 42–43 µm in the recent literature.
- Non-linear pattern flagged by the authors: Group 3 (30% life) worst, Group 4 (3% life) better — attributed to material variability, minor calibration shifts, or simply that 32 crowns of wear is insufficient to degrade performance.
- Comparator cited: Tan & Dudley — 12 LDS crowns per group, 4-axis 100.40 µm vs 5-axis 101.08 µm (no difference between units), but **linear** gap increase with bur ageing (Trios 3 scanner, removable adhesive, stereomicroscope).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro: no oral environment, no thermal/mechanical cycling, no saliva, no occlusal function.
- Single typodont abutment, single material (ZLS/Celtra Duo), single IOS (Primescan), single grinding system (CEREC MC XL) — generalizability to 5-axis units, zirconia or lithium disilicate is untested.
- Wear data came only from the machine's readout; burs were never inspected visually or measured dimensionally, so "wear %" is a system-reported proxy.
- n=8 per group, powered only for d=0.626 — small true wear effects would be missed.
- Authors' proposed next steps: larger samples to locate the absolute bur failure point, and other ceramic materials.

## 6. Related Work

- **Avrahami et al. (same group, cited [17])** — progressive bur wear significantly affected 3D trueness/precision of ZLS crowns, though deviations stayed clinically acceptable. This paper shifts the outcome from global accuracy to marginal adaptation and finds no significant wear effect.
- **Tan & Dudley [19]** — sequential milling of lithium disilicate crowns; 4-axis 100.40 µm vs 5-axis 101.08 µm, with steady precision loss as burs aged; differences vs this study attributed to material (LDS vs ZLS), scanner (Trios 3 vs Primescan), cement (removable adhesive vs Temp-Bond) and microscope (stereo vs SEM).
- **Dentsply Sirona EBM algorithm [32]** — touch-trigger probing of Step Bur and Cylinder Pointed Bur against a calibration pin before each cycle to correct axial length and radial diameter; offered as the mechanism explaining the absence of linear degradation.
- **Systematic review on bur degradation [28]** — adverse effect on surface quality and dimensional accuracy, magnitude depending on material and milling system; most included studies assessed global accuracy, not marginal fit.
- **Marginal-fit literature across CAD/CAM materials [15,29,30]** — discrepancies generally 40–120 µm, driven by preparation design, cement spacer and manufacturing system; SEM is the most sensitive of the measurement methods (vs silicone replica, micro-CT).

## 7. Glossary

- **ZLS (Zirconia-reinforced Lithium Silicate)** — glass-ceramic with a lithium metasilicate/lithium orthophosphate crystalline phase in a glassy matrix, reinforced with ~10% tetragonal zirconia fillers (Celtra Duo); lithium metasilicate crystallites ~1 µm.
- **TMMG (Total Mean Marginal Gap)** — mean of all 12 SEM marginal-gap measurements per crown, averaged within group.
- **Marginal gap** — vertical distance between the restoration margin and the preparation finish line; >120 µm associated with cement dissolution, microleakage, plaque accumulation and secondary caries.
- **Trueness / precision** — deviation from true geometry / reproducibility of repeated measurements (3D superimposition metrics, distinct from marginal gap).
- **EBM (Electronic Bur Measurement)** — CEREC's touch-trigger bur-wear probing and compensation routine run before each grinding cycle.
- **Step Bur 12S / Cylinder Pointed Bur 12S** — the CEREC MC XL dual-bur set: stepped bur for the intaglio surface, pointed cylinder bur for external anatomy.
- **Typodont** — standardized plastic tooth model used here as the machine-prepared abutment.
