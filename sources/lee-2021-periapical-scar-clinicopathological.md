## Why Ingested

Periapical scar (PS) is the benign fibrous healing variant that presents as a persistent radiolucency after adequate endodontic therapy — clinically indistinguishable from treatment failure without histopathology. This paper provides the only systematic clinicopathological series (n=7) with explicit 5-criterion clinical diagnosis rules, histopathological confirmation, and a proposed biological mechanism (cortical plate loss → connective tissue colonization precedes osteoblasts). Critical for surveillance protocol: PS must be on the differential for any stable, well-defined periapical radiolucency after adequate RCT. 0/7 cases were suspected as PS before histopathology, underscoring the diagnostic gap this paper helps close.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective clinicopathological series (Taiwan, J Dent Sci 2021): 7 histopathology-confirmed periapical scars identified from 445 periapical lesion biopsies (prevalence 1.6%); all showed persistent well-defined radiolucency, 86% asymptomatic, lesion size 3–10 mm (mean 5.6 mm); 0/7 suspected as PS clinically before surgery.

Histopathology: dense fibrous collagenous tissue with absent or minimal inflammation; hyalinization in long-standing cases; 1/7 contained amalgam particles from prior retrograde filling confirmed by polarized microscopy.

Five clinical criteria proposed for PS diagnosis without histopathology: (1) adequate root canal/retrograde filling, (2) no symptoms/signs, (3) no root fracture + healthy periodontium, (4) well-defined radiolucency stable in size long-term, (5) well-performed periapical surgery — when all 5 present, conservative monitoring is appropriate.

## 세줄요약

후향적 조직병리 연구 (대만, J Dent Sci 2021): 치근단 병소 생검 445건 중 치근단반흔 (Periapical Scar, PS) 7건 확인 (유병률 1.6%); 전례 지속적·경계 명확 방사선 투과상, 86% 무증상, 병소 크기 3–10 mm (평균 5.6 mm); 0/7에서 수술 전 PS 임상 의심 없었음.

조직병리: 염증 없거나 최소 만성염증을 동반한 치밀 섬유콜라겐 조직 (Fibrous Collagenous Tissue); 장기 경과 시 유리화 (Hyalinization); 1건에서 편광현미경으로 아말감 입자 확인.

임상 진단 5기준 제시 (조직병리 없이 PS 추정): ① 적절한 근관충전/역충전, ② 무증상·무소견, ③ 치근파절 없음·건강한 치주조직 (방사선 투과상 제외), ④ 크기 변화 없는 경계 명확 투과상 장기 지속, ⑤ 수술적 치근단 수술 적절 시행 — 5기준 충족 시 보존적 경과관찰 가능.

## Key Contributions

- Largest systematic clinicopathological PS series with explicit inclusion criteria (histopathological confirmation required)
- PS prevalence: 1.6% of periapical lesions (7/445) in biopsy series — lower end vs Grung 1990 (9% of surgical cases)
- 100% misdiagnosis rate pre-histopathology: 5/7 called radicular cyst, 2/7 called granuloma; 0/7 suspected PS
- 5-criterion clinical diagnostic framework enabling PS identification without surgical biopsy
- Proposed mechanisms: cortical plate loss → connective tissue colonization precedes osteoblasts; excess type III collagen, TGF-β overproduction, MMP deficiency
- Amalgam foreign body finding: large particles → minimal inflammation; small granules → mild chronic inflammation
- Clinical implication: stable periapical radiolucency after adequate RCT ≠ treatment failure; PS warrants conservative monitoring, not retreatment
