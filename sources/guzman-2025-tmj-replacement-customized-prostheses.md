## Why Ingested

Existing TMJ pages in this wiki (e.g. [[wiki/tmj/kim-2023-treatment-tmj-osteoarthritis]], [[wiki/tmj/song-2023-understanding-tmj-osteoarthritis-pathophysiology]]) cover conservative-to-surgical management up through arthrocentesis/arthroscopy for TMJ osteoarthritis, but the wiki had no page on total joint replacement (TJR/alloplastic reconstruction) — the end-stage surgical tier used when conservative and minimally-invasive options fail. This case series (n=6, customized ARTFIX/CPMH prostheses, 24-month follow-up) fills that gap with concrete functional-outcome numbers (jaw function, dietary intake, pain, mouth opening) and complication data, extending the wiki's TMJ treatment ladder to its final rung.

## Three-line Summary
Retrospective clinical case series, n=6 (all female, mean age 56.5y), severe TMJ osteoarthritis (n=5) or condylar osteochondroma (n=1) treated with unilateral total TMJ replacement using CBCT/MRI-planned customized prostheses (ARTFIX Implants, CPMH Digital) at Hospital Militar de Santiago, 2018-2024.

At ≥24-month follow-up, median jaw function improved 6.5→3.5, dietary intake 8.0→1.5, pain (VAS) 7→2; postoperative interincisal mouth opening averaged 32-33 mm.

One patient required revision surgery at 6 months for condylar screw displacement without long-term functional compromise; small sample size and lack of documented preoperative mouth-opening values limit generalizability.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Reports functional outcomes (jaw function, dietary intake, pain, mouth opening) for total TMJ replacement (TJR) with customized (patient-specific) prostheses, a treatment tier beyond arthrocentesis/arthroscopy and above conservative management.
- Uses a structured Likert-type psychometric scale (LTPS) for jaw function and dietary intake, plus VAS for pain, all measured pre- and postoperatively (≥24-month minimum follow-up).
- Details the full surgical protocol: endaural + submandibular combined approach, CBCT/MRI-based digital prosthesis planning (ARTFIX Implants, CPMH Digital), autologous abdominal fat graft interposition, and postoperative kinesiotherapy protocol.
- Documents a single complication (condylar screw displacement) and its successful revision, contributing real-world complication-rate data.
- Contextualizes results against prior TJR literature (Bhargava et al., Wolford et al., Gonzalez-Perez et al., Ravelo et al. systematic review reporting ~97% survival).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective, descriptive case series; no control group, no statistical hypothesis testing (explicitly acknowledged as a limitation).
- **Population**: 6 female patients, mean age 56.5±13.28y, treated Sept 2018-Feb 2024. Severe osteoarthritis (n=5, 83.3%) or condylar osteochondroma (n=1, 16.7%). 50% had ≥1 prior TMJ surgery. Laterality: 55.6% left, 44.4% right.
- **Preoperative workup**: Clinical exam + MRI (soft tissue/internal derangement) + CBCT (osseous structures, prosthesis planning). Muscular stabilization achieved before surgery in all cases.
- **Outcome measures** (preop vs. ≥24-month postop):
  - Interincisal mouth opening (IMO), measured with TheraBite Jaw Motion Rehabilitation System.
  - Jaw function (JF): 0-10 Likert-type psychometric scale (LTPS; Bhargava et al. scale).
  - Dietary intake (DI): 0-10 LTPS.
  - Pain: 0-10 visual analog scale (VAS).
- **Surgical protocol**: General anesthesia; endaural approach to TMJ (capsulotomy, discectomy, fossa/condylar cutting guides, piezoelectric osteotomy) combined with ipsilateral submandibular approach; glenoid component fixed with 4 screws, condylar component with 6-9 locking screws; gentamicin irrigation; autologous abdominal fat graft interposition; layered closure. Two patients also underwent bimaxillary orthognathic surgery (Le Fort I + BSSO) concurrently.
- **Postoperative protocol**: Soft/pureed diet, cold therapy, chlorhexidine rinse, scheduled analgesia (metazamole, ketorolac, paracetamol) and antibiotics (cefazolin), kinesiotherapy starting 1 week postop.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- IMO postoperative: mean 32 mm (passive) / 33 mm (with gentle force); preoperative IMO not consistently documented.
- JF (LTPS): median 6.5 (preop) → 3.5 (postop).
- DI (LTPS): median 8.0 (preop) → 1.5 (postop).
- Pain (VAS): median 7 (preop) → 2 (postop).
- Complications: 1/6 patients (16.7%) required revision surgery at 6 months for condylar screw displacement; screw removed under general anesthesia via the same approach, full functional recovery achieved; no facial nerve injury, infection, or heterotopic ossification reported.
- Benchmarking against literature: comparable to Bhargava et al. (JF +4.2 pts/54.5%, DI +5.6 pts/55.6%, pain -75%, IMO +30.48 mm in ankylosis), Gonzalez-Perez et al. (pain 5.9→1 at 5y, IMO +0.7 cm/20%), Wolford et al. (IMO 25.8→36.2 mm, pain -3 pts p<0.001), and Ravelo et al. systematic review (~97% survival for customized TMJ prostheses).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design, very small sample (n=6), no control/comparator group.
- No statistical significance testing performed (explicitly descriptive study).
- Preoperative IMO values were not consistently documented, preventing direct pre/post comparison for mouth opening.
- Minimum follow-up 24 months but not uniform across patients; longer-term (e.g., 10-14y) durability not assessed in this cohort (cited from other studies instead).
- Two different commercial prosthesis systems (ARTFIX Implants, CPMH Digital) were pooled without separate subgroup analysis.
- Authors recommend larger cohorts, extended follow-up, and standardized measurement protocols for IMO/JF/pain in future studies.

## 7. Glossary
- **TJR (Total Joint Replacement)**: Alloplastic reconstruction replacing both the condylar and fossa/glenoid components of the TMJ with a prosthesis.
- **IMO (Interincisal Mouth Opening)**: Maximum vertical distance between upper and lower incisal edges, a standard mandibular range-of-motion measure.
- **LTPS (Likert-type Psychometric Scale)**: 0-10 patient-reported scale used here for jaw function and dietary intake.
- **VAS (Visual Analog Scale)**: 0-10 patient-reported pain intensity scale.
- **CBCT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography)**: 3D imaging used as the gold standard for osseous TMJ assessment and prosthesis planning.
- **ARTFIX Implants / CPMH Digital**: Commercial customized (patient-specific) TMJ prosthesis systems used in this series.
