## Why Ingested

무치악 환자의 OVD(교합수직고경) 결정 방법 중 인체계측(anthropometric) 분야의 근거를 보강하기 위해 인제스트. 이 위키는 이미 [[wiki/complete-denture/goyal-2026-anthropometric-methods-vertical-dimension-occlusion-sr-ma]] (11개 인체계측법 SR+MA, thumb length r≈0.63, I²≈99%, "adjunct 전용" 결론)을 보유하고 있다. 본 RCT는 그 SR의 스코프 안에 들어가는 단일 primary study로, 시지(index finger, 2D) 길이 대 Sn-Me 안면거리 상관성을 무치악 환자 80명 RCT로 직접 검증하고, 나아가 실제 의치 제작에 적용해 1주 만족도(VAS)까지 측정한 드문 사례다. 근거계층상 goyal 2026 SR의 "adjunct only, 이질성 매우 높음" 결론을 뒤집지 않으며, 오히려 그 SR이 지적한 population-dependence(파키스탄 단일 기관 표본)를 실증한다.

According to PubMed, the source article is "Correlation of index finger length to vertical dimensions of occlusion for edentulous patients and their satisfaction: a randomized controlled trial" (Scientific Reports 13:7414, 2023; PMID 37150797; PMCID PMC10164741), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-33722-x). Trial registration NCT05153213.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized controlled trial (n=80 edentulous patients randomized, 71 analyzed after 9 lost to follow-up; Dow University of Health Sciences, Pakistan) correlating right index-finger (2D) length with the Sn-Me (subnasion-to-menton) facial distance used as occlusal vertical dimension (OVD), then fabricating complete dentures on the correlated OVD in the experimental group vs. conventional method in the control group.

Finger and facial measurements were significantly greater in males than females; strong, positive, statistically significant Spearman correlations were found in both sexes (explicit figure recoverable: female r=0.966, p<0.001); at 1-week follow-up, 97.0% of the experimental (index-finger) group reported satisfaction on an 8-item VAS vs. 84.2% of the control group, though no variable reached statistical significance for satisfaction association.

The authors themselves conclude index-finger length is an "adjunct method" for OVD, not a standalone standard — consistent with the wiki's governing anthropometric SR+MA, and the 1-week satisfaction endpoint and single-population sample limit how far this correlation and satisfaction result can be generalized.

## 세줄요약

무치악 환자 80명 무작위배정, 71명 완료(9명 탈락) RCT(파키스탄 Dow University of Health Sciences) — 우측 시지(index finger, 2D) 길이와 Sn-Me(비하점-이하점) 안면거리(OVD로 사용)의 상관관계를 검증하고, 실험군은 그 상관 기반 OVD로, 대조군은 통상법으로 총의치를 제작.

남성이 여성보다 손가락·안면 길이 모두 유의하게 큼; 양측 성별 모두 강한 양의 유의 상관(회수 가능한 수치: 여성 r=0.966, p<0.001); 1주 추적에서 실험군(시지법) 97.0% 만족(8문항 VAS) vs 대조군 84.2%, 단 만족도와 유의하게 연관된 변수는 없었음.

저자 스스로도 시지 길이법을 OVD의 "adjunct(보조) 방법"으로 결론지어 위키의 근거 상위 문헌인 인체계측 SR+MA와 일치하며, 1주라는 짧은 만족도 추적 기간과 단일 집단(파키스탄) 표본은 일반화 범위를 제한한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Extends the index-finger(2D)-to-OVD anthropometric correlation, previously established mostly in **dentate** populations, to **completely edentulous** patients through a proper RCT design rather than a cross-sectional correlation study.
- Rare pairing of an anthropometric correlation study with a **downstream clinical outcome** — dentures were actually fabricated on the correlated OVD and patient satisfaction (VAS) measured, rather than stopping at correlation alone.
- Reports **sex-stratified** measurements (finger and facial length both significantly greater in males) consistent with androgen-driven anthropometric dimorphism.
- Uses a simple, low-cost instrument (digital vernier caliper) with no radiographic or specialized equipment, framed as suited to low-resource settings.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Parallel-group RCT (CONSORT), Department of Prosthodontics, Dow University of Health Sciences, Karachi, Pakistan; IRB approval IRB-1189/DUHS/Approval/2019/32; trial registration NCT05153213 (registered 2021-12-10).
- **Sample**: 250 screened → 170 excluded → 80 randomized (coin toss by principal investigator, single-blind to group) into experimental (anthropometric/index-finger OVD) and control (conventional OVD) groups, 40 per arm. 71 completed (9 lost to follow-up): 38 control, 33 experimental. Age 30-80 y eligible; analyzed sample mean age 58.2 +/- 7.4 y (range 45-77); 25 male (35.2%), 46 female (64.8%).
- **Inclusion**: completely edentulous, both sexes, males without a beard (to allow chin landmark access), willing to consent.
- **Exclusion**: partial dentition; maxillofacial/myofascial disorders or orthognathic surgery history; finger deformity/disfigurement; head/neck neurological problems; bony defects/spicules; nose or chin deformity; TMJ disorders; bulky chin.
- **Measurements**: right index-finger (2D) length via digital vernier caliper (Kangalu; fingertip to metacarpophalangeal crease, hand supine); Sn-Me distance via Willis gauge with posterior occlusal plane set to the ala-tragus (Camper) line via Fox plane. Denture fabrication followed standard protocol; experimental-group OVD was set from the correlated index-finger value.
- **Outcome measurement**: 8-item Visual Analog Scale (VAS) questionnaire (each 0-10, total 0-80, cutoff 40) at 1-week post-insertion follow-up, covering wear consistency, comfort, mastication pain, swallowing ease, gagging, facial appearance, functional satisfaction, and perceived need for replacement.
- **Statistics**: SPSS v16.0; Shapiro-Wilk normality; Mann-Whitney U (sex differences in Sn-Me and 2D length); Spearman's rho by sex (Sn-Me vs. 2D correlation); Fisher's exact test (categorical variables vs. satisfaction); alpha = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Sample flow**: 80 randomized (40/40) → 71 analyzed (38 control, 33 experimental) after 9 lost to follow-up.
- **Sex differences**: 2D length and Sn-Me distance both statistically significantly greater in males than females (exact male 2D and Sn-Me means from the study's own dataset: 2D 70.12 +/- 7.08 mm; Sn-Me 69.73 mm; female 2D 68.97 +/- 3.72 mm — precise female Sn-Me mean not recoverable from the retrieved text, see deviation note below).
- **Correlation (first hypothesis)**: strong, positive, statistically significant Spearman correlation between Sn-Me and 2D length in **both** sexes — first null hypothesis rejected. The only explicit coefficient recoverable from the retrieved full text is for females: **r = 0.966, p < 0.001**. The male-specific coefficient was reported only in a table that did not transfer through extraction (see deviation note).
- **Satisfaction (second hypothesis)**: 8-item VAS, cutoff 40/80. Overall satisfaction 90.1% (64/71). By sex: males 88.0%, females 91.3%. By group: **control 84.2%, experimental (index-finger method) 97.0%**. No variable reached statistical significance for association with satisfaction (Fisher's exact) — second null hypothesis rejected (patients were not, on aggregate, dissatisfied).
- **Follow-up window**: satisfaction assessed once, at **1 week** post-insertion only.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work (author-stated, plus wiki context)

- **Author-stated**: small, single-population (Pakistani) sample — racial/ethnic anthropometric variation must be considered before generalizing; only 1-week satisfaction follow-up, other concerns could emerge later; impression technique and occlusal scheme for tooth arrangement not controlled as satisfaction covariates; 9/80 (11%) lost to follow-up.
- **Wiki context (see interpretation caveats in the wiki page)**: a statistically significant group-level correlation (even r=0.966 in females) is not the same as validated individual-patient predictive accuracy — the paper reports correlation, not agreement/limits-of-agreement (no Bland-Altman analysis is reported in the retrieved text). A 1-week satisfaction endpoint is far too short to capture OVD-related complications, which the wiki's [[wiki/complete-denture/taylor-2021-longevity-complete-dentures-sr-ma]] shows accumulate over years (failure 5% at <=2 yr rising to 41% at >=10 yr). This primary study sits inside the scope of, and does not overturn, [[wiki/complete-denture/goyal-2026-anthropometric-methods-vertical-dimension-occlusion-sr-ma]]'s "adjunct only, I² up to 99%" verdict on anthropometric OVD methods.

## 6. Related Work

- Sits inside the scope of the wiki's governing anthropometric OVD synthesis, [[wiki/complete-denture/goyal-2026-anthropometric-methods-vertical-dimension-occlusion-sr-ma]] (SR+MA of 11 anthropometric methods, 13 studies, 3,275 subjects; thumb length strongest overall r≈0.63, I²≈99%, high risk of bias, "adjunct only" conclusion).
- Extends the anthropometric-methods catalog of [[wiki/complete-denture/fayad-2025-vertical-dimension-determination-prosthodontics-review]] and [[wiki/complete-denture/alhajj-2017-occlusal-vertical-dimension-complete-denture-review]] from correlation-only evidence to an RCT with a downstream clinical (satisfaction) outcome.
- The 1-week satisfaction endpoint should be read against [[wiki/complete-denture/taylor-2021-longevity-complete-dentures-sr-ma]]'s finding that complete-denture failure accumulates over years, not weeks.

## 7. Glossary

- **Occlusal vertical dimension (OVD / VDO)**: facial height (nose-base to chin-base) with teeth in maximal intercuspation; here, standardized as Sn-Me and measured with a Willis gauge.
- **Sn-Me (Subnasion-Menton)**: anatomical facial landmarks (base of nasal septum to base of chin) used as the standard/reference OVD measurement in this trial.
- **Index finger (2D)**: the second digit; anthropometric literature abbreviates fingers 2D (index) through 5D (little finger).
- **Anthropometric method (for OVD)**: non-invasive external body/facial measurement proposed as a correlated proxy for OVD when intraoral/pre-extraction records are unavailable.
- **Visual Analog Scale (VAS)**: patient-reported outcome scale (here, 8 items, 0-10 each, 0-80 total, cutoff 40) used to assess denture satisfaction.
