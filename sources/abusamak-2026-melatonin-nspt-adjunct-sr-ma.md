## One-line Summary
SR+MA (J Evid Based Dent Pract 2026): 18 RCTs (828 patients); adjunctive melatonin to NSPT significantly improved CAL at 2–6 months and PPD at 2–3 months, but wide prediction intervals limit confidence in consistent clinical benefit.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (J Evid Based Dent Pract 2026): 18 RCTs (828명); 멜라토닌 보조 NSPT, CAL 2–6개월·PPD 2–3개월에서 유의하게 개선; 그러나 넓은 예측구간으로 임상 일관성 제한.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA, 18 RCTs (828 patients), 7 databases through April 2025 — melatonin as adjunct to nonsurgical periodontal therapy in periodontitis patients.

Adjunctive melatonin significantly improved CAL at 2 months (MD −1.48 mm), 3 months (MD −0.41 mm), and 6 months (MD −0.68 mm); PPD reduced at 2 and 3 months; HbA1c improved in diabetic subgroup at 2 months (MD −1.33%).

Wide prediction intervals crossing null at all timepoints indicate substantial heterogeneity; clinical benefit may not be consistent across populations despite statistically significant pooled effects.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA, 18편 RCT (828명), 7개 데이터베이스 2025년 4월까지 — 치주염 환자 NSPT 보조 멜라토닌 효능 종합.

멜라토닌 보조 NSPT는 CAL을 2개월(MD −1.48 mm)·3개월·6개월에 유의하게 개선; PPD는 2–3개월에 감소; 당뇨 환자 HbA1c 2개월 시점 MD −1.33% 개선.

모든 시점에서 예측구간이 영가설을 포함하는 이질성 확인 — 통계적 유의성에도 임상 일관성 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Largest meta-analysis (18 RCTs, 828 patients) on melatonin as NSPT adjunct
- First to calculate prediction intervals — showing that true effect may be negligible in some populations
- CAL gain significant at 2m (MD −1.48 mm), 3m (MD −0.41 mm), 6m (MD −0.68 mm)
- PPD reduction significant at 2m (MD −1.80 mm) and 3m (MD −0.29 mm) but not 6m
- HbA1c benefit in diabetic patients at 2m (MD −1.33%) — potential adjunct for diabetic perio patients
- BOP reduction not statistically significant at any timepoint

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR+MA
- Databases: 7 databases (through April 2025)
- Inclusion: RCTs comparing NSPT + melatonin vs NSPT alone in periodontitis patients
- Primary outcomes: CAL gain and PPD reduction at 1, 2, 3, 6 months
- Secondary: BOP, GI, PI at multiple timepoints; HbA1c at 2 months (diabetic subgroup)
- Statistical method: Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman (HKSJ) with REML; prediction intervals; RoB 2; GRADE
- n: 18 RCTs, 16 contributing to quantitative synthesis; 828 patients

| CAL gain | 3 months | −0.41 mm | −0.69 to −0.13 | Yes |

| PPD reduction | 1 month | 0.19 mm | 0.04 to 0.34 | Yes (favors control) |
| PPD reduction | 2 months | −1.80 mm | −2.49 to −1.12 | Yes |
| PPD reduction | 3 months | −0.29 mm | −0.53 to −0.05 | Yes |

| HbA1c (diabetic) | 2 months | −1.33% | −1.48 to −1.18 | Yes |
| BOP | All timepoints | — | — | No |

Prediction intervals for CAL and PPD crossed null at all timepoints — heterogeneity is substantial.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Prediction intervals cross null — effects may be negligible in some settings
- Subgroup analyses (administration route, disease severity) showed no significant effect modification
- Further high-quality RCTs with standardized protocols and longer follow-up needed

## 6. Related Work
- Melatonin's anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and osteogenic properties are established in preclinical work
- HbA1c benefit suggests particular relevance for diabetic periodontitis patients

## 7. Glossary
- NSPT: Nonsurgical periodontal therapy (scaling and root planing)
- CAL: Clinical attachment level
- PPD: Probing pocket depth
- BOP: Bleeding on probing
- GI: Gingival index; PI: Plaque index
- HKSJ: Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman method (conservative for small k meta-analyses)
- GRADE: Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development, and Evaluation
