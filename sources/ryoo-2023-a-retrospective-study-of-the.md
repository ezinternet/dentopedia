## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-centre cohort at Seoul National University Dental Hospital (implants placed March 2006 to April 2011, followed more than 10 years): 247 RESTORE resorbable-blast-media (RBM) implants in 86 patients, analysed with Kaplan-Meier and Cox regression.

Cumulative survival was 92.5% with 17 late failures at a mean 6.88 years (10 fixture fractures, 6 peri-implantitis, 1 screw fracture); male sex (multivariate HR 14.004, 95% CI 1.840-106.600, p=0.011) and overdenture support (HR 109.345, 95% CI 9.245-1,293.229, p<0.001) were the significant risk factors.

The RBM implant showed favourable 10-15-year survival, but the overdenture estimate rests on only 2 implants in 1 patient and the sex effect has very wide CIs, so both should be read as hypothesis-generating.

## 세줄요약

서울대학교치과병원 단일기관 후향 코호트(2006년 3월~2011년 4월 식립, 10년 이상 추적): 환자 86명의 RESTORE 흡수성 블라스트 표면 (resorbable blast media, RBM) 임플란트 247개를 카플란-마이어 (Kaplan-Meier) 및 콕스 회귀 (Cox regression)로 분석했다.

누적 임플란트 생존율 (cumulative survival rate) 92.5%, 실패 17개는 평균 6.88년에 발생(고정체 파절 10, 임플란트주위염 6, 나사 파절 1)했으며, 남성(다변량 HR 14.004, 95% CI 1.840-106.600, p=0.011)과 피개의치 지지(HR 109.345, 95% CI 9.245-1,293.229, p<0.001)가 유의한 위험인자였다.

10~15년 생존율은 양호했으나 피개의치는 환자 1명의 임플란트 2개에 근거하고 성별 효과의 신뢰구간이 매우 넓어 가설 생성 수준으로 해석해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Long-term (10-15 years) survival data for the RESTORE RBM implant (Keystone Dental), for which beyond-10-year data were described as scarce.
- Failure-mode breakdown: fixture fracture was the leading cause of late failure.
- Univariate and multivariate Cox analysis of patient, implant, surgical and prosthetic variables.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Dental records and radiographs of patients who had implants placed between March 2006 and April 2011 at Seoul National University Dental Hospital were reviewed; follow-up ran until 2022 using the most recent appointment date. Initially 360 implants in 134 patients were evaluated; inclusion required one or more RESTORE RBM implants, and implants with under 10 years of follow-up after prosthetic rehabilitation or with insufficient records or radiographs were excluded, leaving 247 implants in 86 patients. Variables: follow-up, sex, age, location, ITI placement type, diameter, length, prosthesis type, GBR and/or sinus floor elevation (SFE), periodontitis treatment history, hypertension, diabetes, chronic kidney disease. Kaplan-Meier for cumulative survival; univariate Cox, then multivariate Cox for variables with p<0.05 (SPSS 26.0). Failure was implant removal.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Population: age 19-74 (mean 57.9); 127 implants (51.4%) in males and 120 (48.6%) in females; 111 mandibular (44.9%), 136 maxillary (55.1%); 218 posterior (88.3%); 4.0 mm diameter 209 (84.6%); 11.5 mm length 162 (65.6%); bridges 178 (72.1%), single crowns 67 (27.1%), overdenture 2 (0.8%); GBR 82 (33.2%); SFE lateral 34, crestal 10; 198 implants (80.2%) in patients with periodontitis history; hypertension 74, diabetes 26, CKD 5 implants.
- Cumulative survival 92.5%; 17 implants removed at a mean 6.88 years. Table 2 lists 10 fixture fractures, 6 peri-implantitis and 1 screw fracture (the text percentages 55.82%/35.29% and 55.56%/38.89% do not match each other or the counts).
- Sex: CSR 88.3% (14 failures in 127) male vs 96.7% (3 in 120) female; univariate HR 4.542 (95% CI 1.305-15.807, p=0.017); multivariate HR 14.004 (95% CI 1.840-106.600, p=0.011).
- Overdenture: CSR 0% (2 of 2 failed); univariate HR 15.498 (3.105-77.357, p=0.001); multivariate HR 109.345 (9.245-1,293.229, p<0.001).
- Not significant: age (p=0.074), placement type, jaw, anterior/posterior, diameter, length, GBR/SFE, single crown, periodontitis history (CSR 92.3% vs 93.6%), hypertension (97.3% vs 90.5%), diabetes (85.7% vs 93.5%), CKD.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, single-centre design with record-based data; 113 of 360 implants (and 48 of 134 patients) excluded for follow-up or record reasons, so attrition bias is possible.
- Only 17 events and sparse strata (overdenture 2 implants/1 patient; some CIs span 0 to infinity), so estimates are unstable.
- Multiple implants per patient are analysed without stated clustering adjustment; confounding by occlusal load or prosthesis design is not controlled. The authors acknowledge the small overdenture sample.
- Late failures only; early failures before loading are not described.

## 7. Glossary

- RBM: resorbable blast media surface, blasted with resorbable calcium phosphate then passivated.
- CSR: cumulative survival rate (Kaplan-Meier).
- ITI type 1-4: placement timing after extraction (immediate; 4-8 wk; 12-16 wk; over 16 wk).
