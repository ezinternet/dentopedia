## Why Ingested
In vitro pilot directly answering "Can the Cimara SiC grinding bur replace or augment APA for zirconia bonding?" — a clinically relevant negative finding: APA (37.0 MPa) significantly outperformed SiC bur (20.8 MPa) after 5,000 thermocycles. Complements [[dental-materials/zirconia/kamis-2023-cold-plasma-zirconia-resin-cement-bond]] and the surface treatment evidence base aggregated in [[overviews/ceramic-bonding-hf-silane-contamination-overview]] by establishing that SiC bur ceramic repair protocols do not transfer to primary zirconia cementation.

## One-line Summary
In vitro pilot (n=30 4Y-PSZ specimens, 5,000 thermocycles): APA (37.0 MPa) produced significantly higher SBS than SiC bur alone (20.8 MPa) or APA + SiC bur (24.9 MPa); SiC bur cannot be recommended as alternative or adjunct to APA.

## 한줄요약
인비트로 파일럿 (n=30, 4Y-PSZ, 5,000회 열순환): 공기분사연마 (Airborne Particle Abrasion, APA, 37.0 MPa)가 탄화규소 버(SiC bur, 20.8 MPa)나 APA+SiC(24.9 MPa) 대비 유의하게 높은 전단결합강도; SiC 버는 대안·보조수단으로 권장 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First study isolating the SiC bur contribution in Cimara repair system for primary zirconia cementation
- Demonstrates APA superiority is maintained after thermocycling (5,000 cycles)
- Negative finding: SiC bur addition after APA actually worsens bond vs APA alone (24.9 vs 37.0 MPa)
- Hypothesis: SiC bur disrupts APA-created surface topography unfavorably
- 4Y-PSZ material choice (monolithic, multilayer) reflects clinically realistic scenario
- Large effect size (η²=0.762) with only n=30 confirms robust treatment difference

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: In vitro pilot study
- Zirconia: Copra Supreme Symphony 4Y-PSZ (Whitepeaks); sintered at 1630°C/120 min
- n=30 (10 per group); sample size from prior work (SD=7 MPa assumption, Δ=10 MPa, 80% power)
- Groups: (1) APA: 110-μm Al₂O₃, 2 bar, 10 mm, 10 s; (2) SiC bur: 7,000 rpm, dry, 10 strokes; (3) APA + SiC bur
- Cementation: Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus + PANAVIA V5 (MDP-containing)
- Configuration: zirconia-resin cement-zirconia (symmetric; single zirconia-cement interface not isolated)
- Aging: 5,000 thermocycles (5±2°C to 55±2°C, 60 s/cycle)
- SBS test: Instron 4465, 1 mm/min, knife-edge blade
- Failure mode: digital microscope ×35

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Group | n | Mean SBS (MPa) | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| APA | 10 | 37.00 | 32.70 | 41.10 |
| APA + SiC bur | 10 | 24.86 | 18.28 | 33.56 |
| SiC bur | 10 | 20.77 | 9.98 | 24.75 |

- APA vs SiC bur: p<0.001
- APA vs APA+SiC: p<0.001
- SiC bur vs APA+SiC: p>0.05 (ns)
- η²=0.762, ω²=0.737 (large effect)
- All groups: mixed failure (10-90% cement remnants); no macroscopic zirconia damage

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No untreated zirconia control
- Small pilot (n=10/group)
- Zirconia-zirconia configuration; not tooth-zirconia
- Cement layer thickness not measured
- Surface roughness / topography not assessed
- No SEM analysis
- External validity: results from repair-system protocol not directly applicable to standard cementation

## 6. Related Work
- Kirmali et al.: No short-term difference SiC bur vs CoJet for repair; but short-term only
- Libecki et al.: After 150 days + 37,500 thermocycles, SiC bur group declined to near zero; APA maintained
- Janda et al.: Complete Cimara repair system showed high SBS but cannot isolate SiC contribution
- PANAVIA V5 / Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus: MDP-containing system — standard recommendation for zirconia

## 7. Glossary
- **APA**: Airborne Particle Abrasion (공기분사연마) with Al₂O₃ particles
- **SiC bur**: Silicon Carbide grinding bur (Cimara, VOCO GmbH) — from ceramic repair system
- **4Y-PSZ**: 4 mol% yttria partially stabilized zirconia polycrystal
- **MDP**: 10-methacryloyloxydecyl dihydrogen phosphate — phosphate monomer primer
- **η²**: Eta squared — effect size measure (0.762 = very large effect)
