## Why Ingested

SR+MA directly comparing marginal bone loss (MBL) outcomes across all three restoration-timing protocols (IR/L, EL, CL) for both immediate-placement and delayed-placement implants. Provides level-1 evidence that loading timing does not drive MBL, redirecting protocol selection toward other clinical factors.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 11 studies (8 RCTs, 2 CCTs, 1 retrospective; searched to November 2011) comparing MBL between immediate restoration/loading (IR/L), early loading (EL), and conventional loading (CL) for both immediately placed and delayed-placed implants in four group comparisons. Meta-analysis of the largest group (IR/L + delayed placement vs CL + delayed placement, n=6 studies) showed only 0.09 mm MBL difference (95% CI −0.27 to 0.09; P=0.33), with no significant differences in any of the other three comparisons. Conclusion: restoration timing does not affect implant MBL; protocol selection should be driven by factors other than bone preservation.

## 세줄요약

수복 타이밍(즉시 수복·부하/조기 부하/기존 부하)이 임플란트 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)에 미치는 영향을 검토한 SR+MA (11편, 검색 2011년 11월까지); 최대 그룹 메타분석(즉시수복/부하 + 지연식립 vs 기존부하 + 지연식립, 6편)에서 MBL 차이 0.09 mm (95% CI −0.27 ~ 0.09, P=0.33)로 유의하지 않음. 나머지 3개 비교군 모두 유의차 없음 → 수복 타이밍은 MBL에 영향을 주지 않으며, 프로토콜 선택 기준은 MBL이 아닌 다른 임상 요소여야 함.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA synthesizing MBL across all three loading-timing categories (IR/L, EL, CL) with placement timing (IP vs DP) analyzed separately
- Meta-analysis group 1 (largest, n=6 studies): MBL difference 0.09 mm — clinically and statistically non-significant
- Consistent null result across all four pairwise comparison groups
- Identified moderate-to-high risk of publication bias in the RCT pool
- Explicitly redirects protocol choice away from MBL preservation to other clinical considerations

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis
- **Databases**: 3 electronic databases + hand search; searched until November 2011
- **Initial search**: 1,640 articles; 27 full texts evaluated; 11 studies qualified
- **Study types**: 8 RCTs, 2 CCTs, 1 retrospective
- **Inclusion**: Human clinical studies; MBL comparison between ≥2 loading protocols; ≥12-month follow-up; ≥10 implants per group
- **Placement**: Both immediate (IP) and delayed (DP) placement included, analyzed separately
- **Risk of bias**: Eight RCTs at moderate-to-high risk of publication bias

## Limitations

- Abstract-only — full statistical heterogeneity values, individual study data, forest plots, and sensitivity analyses not available
- Small pool per comparison (1–6 studies per group); low power to detect small differences
- Publication bias risk moderate-to-high in the RCT subset
- Searched only to November 2011; evidence base has grown substantially since
