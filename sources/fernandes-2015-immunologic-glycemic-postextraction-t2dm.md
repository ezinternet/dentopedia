## Why Ingested

Prospective case-control study directly addressing the question of whether T2DM glycemic control and neutrophil dysfunction increases postextraction complication risk — fills the oral-surgery evidence gap alongside [[oral-surgery/yang-2022-wound-healing-diabetes-tooth-extraction]] and [[oral-surgery/mozzati-2013-prgf-diabetic-extraction-socket]].

## One-line Summary

Prospective case-control (53 T2DM + 29 controls): despite impaired neutrophil function and poor glycemic control, no increase in postextraction complications; T2DM per se is not a risk factor for complications; antibiotic prophylaxis not indicated based solely on diabetic status.

## 한줄요약

전향적 증례-대조군(53명 제2형 당뇨 + 29명 대조): 호중구 기능 저하·불량 혈당 조절에도 불구하고 발치 후 합병증 증가 없음; 당뇨 자체 또는 혈당 조절은 합병증 위험 인자가 아님; 당뇨 상태만으로 예방적 항생제 투여 불필요.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of the American Dental Association (JADA), Vol. 146, Issue 8, P592-599, August 2015
- **DOI**: 10.1016/S0002-8177(15)00345-1
- **Institution**: University of São Paulo School of Dentistry, Brazil
- **Study design**: Prospective case-control study
- **n**: 53 T2DM patients + 29 controls (no T2DM)
- **Follow-up**: 3, 7, 21, and 60 days post-extraction

## 2. Key Contributions

- No increase in postoperative complications in T2DM vs controls despite impaired neutrophil function
- No association between delayed wound healing at day 21 and glycemic control (HbA1c)
- No association between delayed healing and reduced neutrophil function
- At day 60: all alveolar sockets were completely epithelialized with no signs of infection in both groups
- Practical implication: antibiotic prophylaxis not indicated based solely on diabetic status or glycemic control level

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Enrollment**: T2DM patients undergoing extraction of 1 erupted tooth (no periapical infection, no periodontal pockets >4mm)
- **Exclusion**: Smokers; periodontal pockets >4mm
- **Glycemic assessment**: HbA1c at time of extraction
- **Immunologic profile**: Neutrophil function measured at time of extraction
- **Assessment**: Signs and symptoms at 3, 7, 21, and 60 days post-surgery
- **Outcomes**: Healing pattern, incidence of postextraction complications

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Finding |
|---|---|
| Postop complications (T2DM vs control) | No significant difference |
| Delayed epithelialization at day 21 | Not associated with HbA1c level |
| Delayed healing vs neutrophil function | No association |
| Day 60 status | 100% epithelialization, no infection in both groups |
| Antibiotic recommendation | Not indicated based solely on DM status |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only accessible in PDF (full text behind paywall in the copy)
- Small sample (53 T2DM); single tooth extraction limit
- No severe/Type 1 DM included
- No HbA1c subgroup analysis published in available text
- Larger sample studies needed to confirm findings

## 6. Related Work

- Yang 2022: Narrative review — day 7 most pronounced healing difference; glycemic control is primary risk stratifier
- Mozzati 2013: PRGF in diabetic socket — improved wound healing intervention
- Ruggiero 2024: RCT hyaluronic acid in diabetic extraction socket (JPIS 2024)
- Andrade 2021: Similar design but CKF/HD vs controls — parallel population

## 7. Glossary

- **HbA1c (당화혈색소)**: glycated hemoglobin; reflects 3-month average blood glucose control
- **Neutrophil dysfunction (호중구 기능 저하)**: impaired chemotaxis and phagocytosis in diabetic patients
- **Antibiotic prophylaxis (예방적 항생제)**: preventive antibiotic administration before procedure
