## Why Ingested

기존 위키에는 베체트병(Behçet's disease)을 재발성 아프타 구내염(RAS)의 감별진단 항목으로만 언급한 페이지([[oral-medicine/aphthous-stomatitis/gasmi-2021-oral-aphthous-pathophysiology-treatment]])만 있고, 베체트병 자체의 구강 병인론·구강위생-전신중증도 상관관계·치과 치료 타이밍을 다루는 전용 페이지가 없었다. 본 review(Mumcu & Fortune, 2021, *Frontiers in Medicine*)는 이 공백을 메우며, 구강 미생물총(특히 *Streptococcus sanguinis*/*mutans*)·치주 상태·치과 내원 이력이 베체트병 중증도의 매개변수(mediator)임을 제시해 [[oral-medicine/aphthous-stomatitis/gasmi-2021-oral-aphthous-pathophysiology-treatment]]의 감별진단 프레임을 병인론 수준으로 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review synthesizing the aetiological role of oral health (oral microbiome, innate/adaptive immunity, periodontal status) in Behçet's disease (BD) pathogenesis and disease course.

Poor periodontal health and plaque accumulation are associated with more severe BD (especially in males); a structured 1-month dental/periodontal intervention reduced recurrent oral ulcer frequency at 6 months despite an early flare from mechanical trauma.

Clinical implication: dental treatment should be deferred during active BD flares (trauma-triggered exacerbation), but proactive periodontal treatment and oral hygiene during quiescence is an under-acknowledged adjunct to systemic BD management; evidence is narrative-review level (mechanistic + cohort/intervention synthesis, not a single RCT).

## 세줄요약

구강 미생물총·선천/획득 면역·치주 상태가 베체트병(BD) 병인론과 질병 경과에 미치는 역할을 종합한 내러티브 리뷰.

치주 상태 불량·치태 축적은 (특히 남성에서) 더 중증의 BD와 연관; 1개월 구조화된 치과·치주 치료 개입은 기계적 외상으로 인한 초기 악화에도 불구하고 6개월 시점 재발성 구강궤양 빈도를 감소시킴.

임상적 의미: BD 활동기에는 치과 치료(기계적 외상으로 인한 악화 위험)를 연기해야 하지만, 관해기의 능동적 치주치료·구강위생 관리는 전신 BD 관리의 과소평가된 보조 수단 — 근거 수준은 단일 RCT가 아닌 기전+코호트/개입연구 종합인 내러티브 리뷰 수준.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Frames oral health as a bidirectional aetiological factor in Behçet's disease (BD), not merely a downstream manifestation — poor periodontal health and oral dysbiosis actively trigger and sustain systemic disease activity via microbial (*Streptococcus sanguinis*/*mutans*), innate (neutrophil NETs, TLR2/4, S100A12), and adaptive (γδ T cells, IL-1/TNF-α polymorphisms) immune pathways.
- Synthesizes the authors' own salivary/gut microbiome studies showing site-specific dysbiosis (ulcer vs. non-ulcer sites) in BD vs. RAS, and demonstrates that dental/periodontal treatment stabilises the salivary microbiome toward a more symbiotic pattern.
- Reports a structured dental/periodontal intervention (1-month treatment period, 6-month follow-up) showing reduced recurrent oral ulcer frequency despite an early trauma-triggered flare — direct evidence for "treat the oral focus" as adjunctive BD management.
- Provides practical dental-visit guidance: defer treatment during active BD flares (mechanical trauma exacerbates disease via epithelial breach); emphasizes regular recall tailored to individual risk, and screening questions (reason for last dental visit, hygiene habits) as clinical red-flag indicators.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review (not systematic); synthesizes epidemiologic, immunologic, microbiome and interventional literature on oral health in BD, substantially drawing on the corresponding author's (Mumcu) own prior cohort and microbiome studies from Turkey/UK BD clinics.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Poor periodontal scores, plaque accumulation, gingival inflammation, and tooth loss are more common in BD patients than healthy controls; plaque accumulation is a risk factor for more severe disease course, confirmed at 5-year follow-up via mediation analysis (male gender → periodontal disease → BD severity).
- Country-level oral health disparity: UK BD patients had higher filled-teeth counts, tooth-brushing frequency and dental visit frequency than Turkish BD patients, who had lower dental service utilisation and poorer oral hygiene.
- Dental/periodontal intervention (1 month) → oral ulcer activity initially rose (days 1–2, mechanical trauma) then fell significantly by 6 months vs. pre-treatment and vs. untreated control group.
- Serum mannose-binding lectin (MBL) <100 ng/ml (innate immunity deficit) correlates with elevated *S. mutans* colonisation and more severe disease course.
- Pharmacologic oral ulcer control: BD-specific mouthwash (Triorasol) outperforms betamethasone mouthwash; colchicine + prophylactic benzathine penicillin outperforms colchicine alone; azithromycin and minocycline show immunomodulatory benefit; apremilast (PDE4 inhibitor) reduces ulcers within the first week.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Narrative (non-systematic) review; heavily weighted toward the senior author's own Turkish/UK cohort data rather than an independent, exhaustive literature synthesis. No pooled effect sizes or formal risk-of-bias assessment. Authors call for further work characterising salivary/gut microbiome biodiversity across geographic/genetic contexts to clarify mechanisms.

## 7. Glossary

- **BD**: Behçet's disease — chronic multisystem inflammatory vasculitis with recurrent oro-genital ulceration
- **HLA-B51**: Human leukocyte antigen allele strongly associated with BD genetic susceptibility
- **MBL**: Mannose-binding lectin, an innate immune host-defence protein
- **NETs**: Neutrophil extracellular traps
- **RAS**: Recurrent aphthous stomatitis
