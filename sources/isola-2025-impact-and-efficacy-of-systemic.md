## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 18 studies (9 RCTs pooled) evaluating systemic antibiotics (SA) as adjuncts to non-surgical or surgical treatment of peri-implant mucositis (PM) and peri-implantitis (PI); SA did not benefit PM; for PI, significant reductions in bleeding on probing (p=0.007) and probing pocket depth (p=0.004) at 12 months were observed with adjunctive SA.

However, no significant improvement in marginal bone level (MBL) was found; only 2 studies reported significant microbiological effects (metronidazole reducing P. gingivalis and T. forsythia up to 12 months); evidence remains heterogeneous and inconclusive for long-term outcomes.

Adjunctive systemic antibiotics offer only limited clinical benefits for PI — confined to soft tissue parameters with no significant bone-level effect — and are not supported for PM; further high-quality, long-term studies needed.

## 세줄요약

18편 연구(RCT 9편 메타분석) SR+MA: 치주위염(Peri-implant Mucositis, PM)·임플란트 주위염(Peri-implantitis, PI) 치료 시 보조적 전신 항생제(SA) 임상·미생물 결과 평가; SA는 PM에 추가 임상적 편익 없음; PI에서 보조 SA가 12개월 출혈지수(BOP, p=0.007) 및 치주낭 깊이(PPD, p=0.004) 유의 감소.

그러나 변연골수준(MBL) 유의 개선 없음; 미생물 효과는 2편 연구에서만 메트로니다졸이 12개월까지 P. gingivalis·T. forsythia 유의 감소; 근거 이질적, 장기 결과 불명확.

보조적 전신 항생제는 PI의 연조직 파라미터에만 제한적 임상 효과 — 골수준 효과 없음, PM에는 미지지; 고품질 장기 연구 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Differentiates SA effects in PM vs PI: no benefit for PM; limited soft-tissue benefit for PI
- Establishes bone level (MBL) as unaffected by SA adjunct — critical finding
- Identifies metronidazole as the only SA with demonstrated microbiological effects (2 studies)
- Highlights heterogeneity and limited evidence quality as barriers to firm recommendations
- Uses ID-COSM consensus outcome set for consistency

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PRISMA; PROSPERO CRD420251059056
- Included: RCTs and non-RCTs evaluating SA as adjuncts to non-surgical PM treatment, or non-surgical/surgical PI treatment
- Risk of bias: RoB2 (RCTs), MINORS (non-RCTs)
- 18 qualitative studies; 9 RCTs in meta-analysis
- Outcomes: BOP, PPD, MBL, microbiological outcomes (P. gingivalis, T. forsythia, A. actinomycetemcomitans)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Condition | Outcome | p-value | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| PM | All clinical outcomes | p>0.05 | SA: no significant benefit |
| PI | BOP at 12 months | p=0.007 | SA: significant reduction |
| PI | PPD at 12 months | p=0.004 | SA: significant reduction |
| PI | MBL | p>0.05 | SA: no significant improvement |
| PI | Microbiological (metronidazole) | Significant | P. gingivalis + T. forsythia reduction up to 12 mo (2 studies only) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Heterogeneity of included studies: variable SA regimens, PI severity, treatment protocols
- Only 9 RCTs available for quantitative analysis
- Short-term outcomes (12 months) — no long-term bone/implant survival data
- Concerns: antimicrobial resistance, systemic side effects, lack of microbial standardization
- Need: standardized SA protocols, long-term RCTs, ID-COSM outcome compliance

## 6. Related Work and Context
- Builds on antonoglou-2025 (systemic antibiotics in PI treatment): consistent finding of limited bone benefit
- Aligns with stewardship concerns: SA resistance risks vs marginal soft-tissue benefit
- Microbiological rationale for SA in PI: targets specific anaerobes (P. gingivalis, T. forsythia)

## 7. Glossary
- **PM (Peri-implant Mucositis)**: Inflammation limited to peri-implant soft tissues; reversible; no bone loss
- **PI (Peri-implantitis)**: Inflammation with progressive marginal bone loss around implant
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level)**: Radiographic measure of bone support; primary implant survival indicator
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing)**: Soft-tissue inflammation marker; improved with SA in PI
- **P. gingivalis / T. forsythia**: Key peri-implant pathogens targeted by metronidazole

## Why Ingested

Provides the most current SR+MA evidence on systemic antibiotics specifically for peri-implant diseases (PM and PI separately), including the first comprehensive microbiological outcome data — directly relevant to clinical decision-making in implant maintenance.
