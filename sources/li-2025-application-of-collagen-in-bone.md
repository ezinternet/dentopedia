## Why Ingested

Comprehensive narrative review of collagen biology and all major delivery forms (scaffolds, sponges, membranes, hydrogels, microspheres/nanoparticles) for bone tissue engineering (골조직공학, BTE). Provides the mechanistic and materials-science backbone for collagen-based 유도골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) membrane studies already in the wiki (e.g., [[bone-regeneration/wessing-2018-gbr-collagen-membranes-particulate-graft-sr-ma]], [[bone-regeneration/bujda-2026-carp-collagen-membrane-gbr-rat-femoral]]).

## Three-line Summary

Collagen — primarily Type I — is the most abundant protein in the human body (~1/3 of total protein mass) and forms the structural ECM of bone via mineralized collagen fibrils templating hydroxyapatite (수산화인회석, HA) crystal growth. This review synthesizes ~29 known collagen types, their sources (bovine, porcine, marine, recombinant), synthesis/degradation pathways, and all major processed forms — scaffolds, sponges, membranes, hydrogels, microspheres/nanoparticles — along with composite strategies combining collagen with natural polymers, synthetic polymers, metals, and bioceramics. Only a limited number of collagen-based composites have received FDA approval for clinical use; key barriers remain mechanical weakness, limited biological stability, immune response risk, and inadequate vascularization.

## 세줄요약

콜라겐(특히 제1형)은 체내 단백질의 약 1/3을 차지하며 미네랄화된 피브릴을 통해 수산화인회석(HA) 결정핵 형성을 유도, 골조직의 구조적 토대를 형성한다. 본 리뷰는 약 29종의 콜라겐 유형, 소·돼지·해양·재조합 공급원, 합성·분해 경로, 스캐폴드·스폰지·멤브레인·하이드로젤·마이크로스피어/나노입자 등 주요 가공 형태, 그리고 천연/합성 폴리머·금속·바이오세라믹과의 복합 전략을 종합한다. FDA 승인을 받은 임상용 콜라겐 복합재는 극히 제한적이며, 기계적 강도·생물학적 안정성·면역반응·혈관화 부족이 핵심 장벽으로 남아 있다.

## Key Contributions

1. **Collagen type taxonomy**: Enumerates ~29 types; Col-I (dominant in bone/tendon/skin), Col-II (cartilage), Col-III (skin/vessels), Col-IV (basement membrane), and minor bone-matrix types (Col-VI, IX, X, XI) with distinct spatial roles during ossification and remodeling.
2. **Sources landscape**: Bovine and porcine = traditional Col-I sources; marine (fish, shellfish) = low immunogenicity alternative; plant/microbial and genetically engineered protein polymers (GEPP) = emerging batch-consistent, animal-derivative-free platforms.
3. **Forms for BTE**: Scaffolds (porous 3D support), membranes (GBR barrier), hydrogels (injectable/hemostatic — e.g., FDA-cleared VITAGEL®), sponges (rhBMP-2 carrier, freeze-dried), microspheres/nanoparticles (drug/GF controlled release).
4. **Composite strategies**: Natural polymers (alginate, chitosan/Col-I/HA triples, hyaluronan, silk fibroin); synthetic polymers (PCL, PLGA, PVA); metals (enhanced corrosion resistance + mechanical strength); bioceramics (HA, β-TCP — mimics inorganic bone phase).
5. **Mechanism of bone repair**: Three-stage cascade (inflammation → repair → remodeling); Col-I guides HA crystallization, provides BTE scaffold for osteoblast/MSC migration; rhBMP-2 delivered via absorbable collagen sponge enhances osteogenesis.
6. **GEPP future direction**: Genetically engineered collagen allows precise molecular control (sequence-level), monodisperse batches, minimal contamination risk; enables low-dose CBD-BMP-2 delivery (0.02 mg/cm³) for spinal fusion in rats.

## Methodology

Narrative review — literature synthesis without systematic search protocol or PRISMA reporting. No defined inclusion/exclusion criteria. Scope: collagen biology (types, sources, synthesis/degradation) + all processed forms + composite materials for bone tissue engineering and regenerative medicine. References span basic science to preclinical animal models to limited clinical examples.

## Key Results

- ~29 collagen types identified; Col-I is the dominant ECM component of bone and the most used BTE material.
- Triple-helix (Gly-X-Y repeat) structure with D-banding; gap zones serve as nucleation sites for nano-HA crystals — basis for intrafibrillar vs extrafibrillar mineralization.
- Collagen degradation: extracellular (matrix metalloproteinases [MMPs] — MMP-1, -2, -3, -8, -9, -13; cysteine proteases: cathepsin K/L/S); intracellular (phagocytosis, macropinocytosis, receptor-mediated endocytosis via uPARAP, autophagocytosis).
- Cathepsin K inhibitors (Relacatib, Balicatib, Odanacatib) under investigation for osteoporosis; no FDA-approved drugs yet.
- FDA-approved: rhBMP-2 + absorbable collagen sponge (InFUSE/ACS) for bone formation; VITAGEL® collagen hydrogel as hemostatic agent.
- Composite limitations: poor cell adhesion and rapid degradation (natural polymers); unsatisfactory biological activity (synthetic polymers); mechanical weakness and immune/vascular concerns for clinical translation.
- Nano collagen bone used for alveolar ridge preservation showed successful fusion and increased alveolar bone mineral density at 3 months post-implantation (case series).

## Limitations

- Narrative (non-systematic) review: no protocol, no quality appraisal of cited studies, cherry-picking risk.
- Heavy preclinical bias: most cited evidence is in vitro or animal model; human clinical data scarce.
- No meta-analytic data synthesis; no effect sizes reported.
- Orthopedic (fracture repair) framing predominates; dental/alveolar-specific applications are brief.
- Regulatory status of composites varies by country; "FDA-approved" statements pertain to US context only.

## Glossary

- **BTE** — Bone Tissue Engineering (골조직공학)
- **Col-I** — Collagen Type I (제1형 콜라겐)
- **ECM** — Extracellular Matrix (세포외기질)
- **GEPP** — Genetically Engineered Protein Polymers (유전자공학적 단백질 고분자)
- **GBR** — Guided Bone Regeneration (유도골재생)
- **HA** — Hydroxyapatite (수산화인회석)
- **MMPs** — Matrix Metalloproteinases (기질금속단백분해효소)
- **MSC** — Mesenchymal Stromal/Stem Cells (중간엽 줄기세포)
- **PCL** — Polycaprolactone (폴리카프로락톤)
- **PLGA** — Poly(lactic-co-glycolic acid) (폴리락트산-글리콜산 공중합체)
- **rhBMP-2** — Recombinant Human Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2 (재조합 인간 골형성단백질-2)
- **β-TCP** — β-Tricalcium Phosphate (β-삼인산칼슘)
