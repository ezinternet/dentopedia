## Why Ingested

근관치료 치아 라미네이트 비니어 적용 가능성에 대한 가장 최신(2025) 후향적 데이터. 기존 위키 [[veneers/gresnigt-2019-ceramic-laminate-veneers-ids-11year]]와 함께 ETT 생존율을 생활치와 비교한 중장기 임상 근거 쌍을 구성하며, OR=1.68(ETT 실패 위험)과 dentin exposure 3단계 층화 OR(3.47/4.67) 수치를 위키에 추가.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center study (JERD 2025; 189 patients, 672 lithium disilicate ceramic laminate veneers, single operator, 1–15 years, Strasbourg); dentin exposure categorized as GA1 (enamel only, n=290), GA2 (<30% dentin, n=306), GA3 (>30% dentin, n=76); ETT n=38 included; Kaplan-Meier with Odds Ratio analysis.

15-year cumulative estimated survival 96% (observed 98.4%); dentin exposure >30% significantly increased failure risk (GA1 vs. GA3 p=0.033, OR=4.67); ETT survival 95.0% vs. vital 96.0% (p=0.289, NOT significant, OR=1.68); newer amine-free dual-cure cement trended toward better performance (99.6% vs. 95.5%).

Enamel preservation is the primary survival determinant for ceramic laminate veneers; ETT is not a significant clinical contraindication by survival criteria but warrants careful case selection given OR=1.68, and esthetic complications from discoloration require dedicated management.

## One-line Summary

Retrospective study (n=672 lithium disilicate veneers, 189 patients, up to 15 years) found 96% cumulative 15-year survival; dentin exposure >30% significantly increased failure risk (OR=4.67), while endodontic treatment did not significantly affect survival (ETT 95% vs. vital 96%, p=0.289, OR=1.68).

## 한줄요약

후향적 연구 (672개 LDS 비니어, 189명, 최장 15년): 15년 누적 생존율 96%; 상아질 노출 >30%에서 실패 위험 OR=4.67(유의), 근관치료 치아는 유의차 없음(95% vs. 96%, p=0.289, OR=1.68).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest single-operator retrospective on ceramic LV with 15-year follow-up (n=672)
- Quantified dentin exposure risk in 3 tiers (GA1/GA2/GA3) with OR values: 3.47 (any dentin) and 4.67 (>30% dentin)
- Confirmed ETT is not a significant predictor of LV failure (p=0.289, OR=1.68 only)
- Demonstrated newer amine-free dual-cure cement (GC LinkForce) outperformed older VL system (99.6% vs. 95.5%)
- Esthetic complications in ETT linked to underlying discoloration — a masking challenge, not a structural failure

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design**: Single-center retrospective clinical study
- **Period**: July 2001 – January 2023
- **Population**: 189 patients (137 women, 52 men), mean age 47 (range 18–90)
- **Sample**: 672 ceramic LVs on incisors and canines (maxilla + mandible)
- **Material**: Exclusively lithium disilicate glass-ceramic (IPS e.max Press; Ivoclar Vivadent)
- **Operators**: Single clinician (OE), single ceramist (DW), independent examiner (CW)
- **Dentin exposure categories** (assessed via post-etching photographs + ImageJ):
  - GA1: 0% dentin (enamel only) — 290 teeth (43.1%)
  - GA2: <30% dentin — 306 teeth (45.5%)
  - GA3: >30% dentin — 76 teeth (11.3%)
- **ETT status**: 38 NVT (6%), 634 VT (94%)
- **Preparation technique**: APT (Aesthetic Pre-evaluative Temporary) mock-up driven protocol
- **Cement systems**:
  - 2001–2015: ExciTE F DSC + Variolink II (VL)
  - 2015–2023: G-Premio BOND + G-CEM LinkForce (GC)
- **Follow-up**: 1–180 months (mean 5.98 years)
- **Survival definition**: Restoration in situ (failure = rebonding or replacement)
- **Statistics**: Kaplan–Meier, Log-Rank (Mantel-Cox), Odds Ratios; SPSS v29

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Overall observed survival**: 98.4% (11 failures out of 672)
- **15-year cumulative estimated survival**: 96%
- **Survival by dentin exposure**:
  - GA1 (enamel only): 96.7% estimated, 2 failures (99.3% observed)
  - GA2 (<30% dentin): 95.3% estimated, 5 failures (98.4% observed)
  - GA3 (>30% dentin): 93.9% estimated, 4 failures (84.7% observed)
  - GA1 vs. GA3 statistically significant (p=0.033)
- **Odds ratios for failure**:
  - Any dentin exposure: OR=3.47
  - >30% dentin (GA3): OR=4.67
  - ETT (non-vital): OR=1.68
- **ETT survival**: NVT 95.0% vs. VT 96.0% (p=0.289, NOT significant)
- **Preparation design**: Butt-margin vs. incisal overlap — no significant difference (p=0.214)
- **Cement system**: VL 95.4% vs. GC 99.3% (no statistically significant difference)
- **Failure types**: Fracture/chipping 5 (0.75%), debonding 1, tooth loss 1, esthetic 3, sensitivity 1

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-center, single-operator design limits generalizability
- 2D photographs for dentin exposure quantification (no palatal surface captured)
- Small NVT subgroup (n=38) limits statistical power for ETT comparisons
- No formal USPHS-based success rate analysis at each recall
- Retrospective design limits control of confounding variables

## 7. Glossary

- **APT** — Aesthetic Pre-evaluative Temporary: mock-up technique guiding conservative depth reduction
- **GA1/GA2/GA3** — Dentin exposure tiers: 0% / <30% / >30%
- **NVT/VT** — Non-Vital Teeth (ETT) / Vital Teeth
- **IDS** — Immediate Dentin Sealing (not used in this study; DDS protocol with immediate photopolymerization used instead)
- **OR** — Odds Ratio for failure
