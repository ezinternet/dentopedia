## Why Ingested

Maps the clinical and preclinical evidence on vitamin D in peri-implant and periodontal contexts — complements host-modulation adjunct series in this category; addresses the specific role of Vitamin D deficiency (>1 billion affected globally) in implant failure and periodontal disease progression, with novel topical/coating delivery strategies.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (SANRA guidelines, PubMed-only) synthesizing in vitro, animal, and clinical evidence on vitamin D in peri-implant healing and periodontal disease; vitamin D deficiency is consistently linked to impaired osseointegration, reduced bone-to-implant contact (BIC), and increased marginal bone loss (MBL).

Critical dose-response finding: hypovitaminosis D → worse implant survival/MBL, but hypervitaminosis D (>70 ng/mL) → 21.1-fold increased peri-implant bone loss; periodontal benefits include upregulation of RUNX2/ALP/OCN in PDL cells, reduced RANKL/TNF-α/IL-1, and clinical improvement (PPD, CAL) in T2DM+periodontitis patients given 60,000 IU/week × 8 weeks.

Evidence is predominantly preclinical; no standardized dosing protocol can yet be defined, and the predominance of animal and observational studies limits direct clinical translation.

## 세줄요약

비타민 D의 임플란트 주위 치유 및 치주질환에 대한 내러티브 리뷰; 비타민 D 결핍은 골유착(osseointegration) 저하, 골-임플란트 접촉률(BIC) 감소, 변연골 소실(MBL) 증가와 일관되게 연관됨.

혈청 비타민 D >70 ng/mL(과잉)→ 임플란트 주위 골소실 21.1배 증가; 치주 수준에서는 RUNX2/ALP/OCN 발현 증가, RANKL/TNF-α/IL-1 감소, T2DM+치주염 환자에서 60,000 IU/주×8주 보충 후 PPD·CAL 유의 개선.

전임상 연구 편중, 표준 용량/프로토콜 미확립 — 임상 적용 전 추가 RCT 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Distinguishes systemic vs. topical/local vitamin D delivery strategies for implant osseointegration.
- Documents dose-response U-curve: deficiency (<20 ng/mL) AND excess (>70 ng/mL) both impair peri-implant outcomes.
- Maps molecular mechanisms: vitamin D → RUNX2, ALP, OPN, OCN upregulation; RANKL/TNF-α/IL-1 downregulation.
- Identifies clinical translational gap: most high-quality implant evidence is animal-based.

## 3. Key Results — Peri-Implant

- Vitamin D surface coating on implants → +10% BIC at 12 weeks (Salomó-Coll 2016, animal)
- Vitamin D deficiency → reduced BIC + impaired cortical bone formation; supplementation 2400 IU/kg × 8 weeks → improved peri-implant bone (Dvorak 2012, animal)
- Hypervitaminosis D (>70 ng/mL) → 21.1-fold increased peri-implant bone loss risk (Cheng 2024, human retrospective)
- Sufficient serum vitamin D → lowest MBL at 12 months vs deficient/insufficient groups (Tabrizi 2022, cohort)

## 4. Key Results — Periodontal

- Periodontitis patients had serum 25(OH)D <50 ng/mL vs healthy (Laky 2017, case-control)
- Vitamin D deficiency → elevated MMP-9 in GCF, linked to periodontitis severity (Yildirim 2025)
- Vitamin D treatment in PDL cells → upregulation RUNX2, ALP, OCN, mineral nodule formation (Pereira 2024)
- Animal model: 25-hydroxyvitamin D3 injection × 8 weeks → reduced RANKL, IL-1, TNF-α, limited alveolar bone loss (Han 2019)
- Clinical: 60,000 IU/week × 8 weeks in T2DM+periodontitis → significant improvement in PI, BI, PD, CAL (RamaPrabha 2023)

## 5. Limitations

- Narrative review only (no systematic search, no meta-analysis)
- Single database (PubMed)
- High heterogeneity of study designs, supplementation protocols, baseline vitamin D levels
- Predominance of preclinical studies
- No standardized outcome measures; optimal dosage undefined

## 6. Glossary

- **BIC**: Bone-to-implant contact ratio
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss
- **25(OH)D / calcidiol**: Inactive storage form of vitamin D; measured in serum
- **Calcitriol**: Active form of vitamin D (1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D)
- **RUNX2**: Runt-related transcription factor 2 (osteoblast differentiation marker)
- **ALP**: Alkaline phosphatase (mineralization marker)
- **OCN/OPG**: Osteocalcin / osteoprotegerin (bone markers)
- **RANKL**: Receptor activator of NF-κB ligand (osteoclast activation signal)
- **PDL**: Periodontal ligament
