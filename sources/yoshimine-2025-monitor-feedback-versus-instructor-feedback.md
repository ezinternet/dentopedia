## Why Ingested

CPR 교육 방법론 RCT — 치과 학생 대상 BLS 훈련에서 모니터 피드백 vs 교관 피드백의 효과를 비교한 유일한 무작위대조시험. 기존 [[drug/medical-emergencies/khattak-2026-clinical-readiness-knowledge-attitude-bls]] (지식·태도 단면조사)와 [[drug/medical-emergencies/tayebi-hillali-2026-competence-semi-automated-external-defibrillator]] (AED 실기평가) 에 교육 개입 효과 근거를 추가한다.

## One-line Summary

RCT (n=34, two groups) found that monitor feedback improves CPR chest compression depth while instructor feedback improves compression rate, suggesting a complementary hybrid training approach for dental students.

## 한줄요약

무작위대조시험 (n=34, 2군) — 모니터 피드백은 흉부압박 (Chest Compression) 깊이를 개선하고 교관 피드백은 압박 속도를 개선, 치과대학 BLS 훈련에 상호보완적 하이브리드 방식 필요.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: BMC Medical Education, Vol. 25, No. 1, p. 918 (2025)
- Affiliation: Department of Dental Anesthesiology, Kagoshima University, Japan
- Funding/COI: Not reported in abstract

## 2. Key Contributions

- First RCT specifically comparing monitor vs. instructor feedback for BLS in dental students in a post-pandemic context
- Quantifies that monitor feedback selectively improves CC depth but not rate
- Instructor feedback selectively improves CC rate but not depth
- Identifies mechanism-specific feedback requirements for CPR quality components
- Provides evidence base for self-directed BLS training models during pandemic restrictions

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized controlled trial, pre-post test design
- **Population**: Dental students (n=40 enrolled, 34 analyzed; 17 per group after attrition exclusion)
- **Intervention**: Monitor feedback group — CPR training using simulator with real-time metric display, no instructor
- **Control**: Instructor feedback group — traditional face-to-face instructor-guided CPR training
- **Outcomes**: CC depth, CC rate, CC fraction, tidal volume; pre-test vs. post-test comparison
- **Setting**: Kagoshima University dental school, Japan (COVID-19 period)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **CC depth**: Significantly improved in monitor feedback group post-training (p < 0.05); monitor feedback superior to instructor for depth
- **CC rate**: Significantly improved in instructor feedback group post-training (p < 0.05); instructor feedback superior to monitor for rate
- **CC fraction and tidal volume**: No significant between-group differences reported in abstract
- **Implication**: Neither feedback type alone achieves comprehensive CPR quality; depth and rate require different feedback channels

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=34 after attrition from 40); single center
- Short follow-up — no retention testing beyond immediate post-training
- Only dental students in Japan; generalizability to other dental schools uncertain
- Does not assess actual resuscitation outcomes or skill retention at 6–12 months
- Pandemic context may not represent normal educational conditions

## 6. Related Work

- Yoshimine et al. is among the first to isolate feedback modality effects in dental BLS training
- Broader BLS literature (non-dental) shows combined monitor+instructor feedback outperforms either alone
- khattak-2026: knowledge/attitude baseline data for dentists in Saudi Arabia (cross-sectional)
- tayebi-hillali-2026: AED practical competence in dental students (before/after evaluation)

## 7. Glossary

- **BLS**: Basic Life Support — airway management + chest compressions + AED
- **CC depth**: Chest compression depth (AHA target ≥5 cm)
- **CC rate**: Chest compression rate (AHA target 100–120/min)
- **CC fraction**: Proportion of time hands-on during resuscitation (target ≥60%)
- **Monitor feedback**: Real-time metric display from a CPR simulator manikin without instructor
