## Why Ingested

A key clinical question when planning implants in ridge-deficient sites is whether prior bone grafting compromises long-term outcomes. This SR is the only synthesis with a ≥10-year follow-up threshold explicitly comparing implant survival rate (ISR) in grafted vs non-grafted sites, extending the short-to-medium-term evidence in [[wiki/bone-regeneration/wessing-2018-gbr-collagen-membranes-particulate-graft-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA) of clinical studies with ≥10-year follow-up, searching 6 databases through December 2020 / May 2024; 3 controlled studies (n = 34–96 patients each) included.
Implant survival rate (ISR) ranged 96–100% in grafted sites and 91.6–94% in non-grafted sites across all 3 studies; all studies had low risk of bias (ROBINS-I).
Grafted and non-grafted implant sites show similar long-term ISR; however, meta-analysis was not feasible due to high heterogeneity and only 3 eligible studies were identified.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic review to specifically require ≥10-year follow-up as inclusion criterion for grafted-vs-non-grafted implant survival comparisons
- Documents that implants placed in guided bone regeneration (GBR) or autogenous-bone-grafted sites achieve long-term ISR of 96–100%, comparable to non-grafted controls (91.6–94%)
- Highlights a critical evidence gap: only 3 eligible studies exist in the entire literature; none conducted prior sample-size estimation, and meta-analysis is precluded by heterogeneity
- Uses PRISMA guidelines and ROBINS-I risk-of-bias tool with OCEBM evidence-level classification

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review (SR) without meta-analysis, PRISMA-compliant
- **Databases**: PubMed/MEDLINE, EMBASE, Scopus, ISI Web of Knowledge, OVID, Google Scholar — searched without language/time restriction through December 2020 (initial search) and May 2024 (updated)
- **Inclusion criteria**: Controlled clinical studies, adult patients (≥18 yr), implant placement in grafted sites (maxilla and/or mandible), ≥10-year follow-up, comparison vs implants without GBR, ISR + clinicoradiographic status
- **Excluded**: Case reports, case series, letters, reviews, in-vitro/ex-vivo/in-vivo experimental, animal studies
- **n**: 3 studies included; participants per study: 34–96; total implants: 225–306 per study
- **Risk of bias**: ROBINS-I tool; OCEBM evidence levels 3–4 assigned to included studies
- **Primary outcome**: Implant survival rate (ISR) at ≥10 years

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Study | Design | n (grafted / control) | Follow-up | ISR — Grafted | ISR — Non-grafted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daubert et al | Cross-sectional case-control | 59 (GBR) / 37 (no GBR) | 10 yr | 100% | 91.6% |
| De Moraes et al | Retrospective case-control cohort | 22 (autogenous) / 20 (no graft) | 10 yr | 96% | 94% |
| Roccuzzo et al | Non-randomised case-control | 19 (GBR) / 15 (no GBR) | 10 yr (est.) | Not separately reported | — |

- Mean age range across studies: 47.2–67.6 years
- All 3 studies rated **low risk of bias** (ROBINS-I)
- Evidence levels: OCEBM Level 3 (Roccuzzo et al) and Level 4 (Daubert et al, De Moraes et al)
- Meta-analysis was **not possible** due to high heterogeneity among included studies
- None of the studies conducted prior sample-size estimation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 3 studies met the strict ≥10-year inclusion criterion — extremely sparse evidence base
- All included studies are non-randomised controlled designs (OCEBM Level 3–4) — no RCTs with ≥10-year follow-up exist for this comparison
- High heterogeneity among studies precluded meta-analysis
- No prior sample-size estimation in any included study — underpowering likely
- Confounders (smoking, systemic health, oral hygiene) not uniformly controlled across studies
- Well-designed, adequately powered, long-term prospective trials are urgently needed

## 7. Glossary

- **ISR (Implant Survival Rate, 임플란트 생존율)**: Proportion of implants remaining in situ and functional at a defined follow-up point, regardless of complications
- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 골유도재생)**: Surgical technique using barrier membranes +/− graft materials to regenerate alveolar bone deficiencies prior to or concurrent with implant placement
- **ROBINS-I**: Risk of Bias In Non-randomised Studies of Interventions — validated tool for bias assessment in observational/non-RCT clinical studies
- **PRISMA**: Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses — reporting guideline for systematic reviews
- **OCEBM**: Oxford Centre for Evidence-Based Medicine — classification system assigning evidence levels 1 (highest) to 5 (expert opinion) based on study design
- **High heterogeneity (높은 이질성)**: Substantial variation in populations, interventions, comparators, or outcomes across included studies that precludes pooling in a meta-analysis
