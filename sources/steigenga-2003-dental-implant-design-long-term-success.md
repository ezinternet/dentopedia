## Why Ingested

Implant macrogeometry and thread design are general design questions with no dedicated subcategory in `implants`. This Steigenga 2003 review links design elements to osseointegration quality and long-term success, and the wiki needs it as the design-level companion to the bone-quality sources.

## Three-line Summary

Review (Implant Dentistry, 2003) of how implant biomechanical design (geometry, material, surface, thread) affects osseointegration quality and long-term success — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The authors argue there is no single "optimal" design, and that strength, interfacial stability and load transfer can be engineered through materials, surfaces and thread design.

The abstract states that limited information is available on how thread design influences overall success, so design claims should be read as engineering rationale rather than outcome-based rankings.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Implant Dentistry, 2003) — 임플란트 생체역학 설계 (형상, 재료, 표면, 나사산) 가 골유착 품질과 장기 성공에 미치는 영향 — 초록만 수집 (전문 미확보).

단일한 "최적" 설계는 없으며, 재료·표면·나사산 설계를 통해 강도, 계면 안정성, 하중 전달을 공학적으로 최대화할 수 있다고 주장.

나사산 설계가 전체 성공에 미치는 영향에 대한 정보가 제한적이라고 명시하므로, 설계 주장은 결과 기반 순위가 아니라 공학적 근거로 읽을 것.
