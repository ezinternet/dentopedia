## Why Ingested

Clinically validates the digital functionally generated path (FGP) technique using Patient Specific Motion (PSM) via IOS to pre-emptively eliminate eccentric occlusal interferences in CAD-CAM monolithic zirconia crowns before milling — directly relevant to reducing chairside adjustment and protecting zirconia integrity.

→ [[digital-workflow/saad-2024-reliability-of-the-digital-functionally]]

## Three-line Summary

A within-patient RCT (n=13) compared CAD-CAM zirconia crowns adjusted using buccal interocclusal record alone (Group I) versus buccal plus lateral interocclusal record via IOS Patient Specific Motion (Group II). Group II crowns showed significantly lower maximum pressure during lateral excursion (20.62 ± 3.38% vs 26.00 ± 4.95%, p<0.0001) and at maximum intercuspation (7.23 ± 1.59% vs 8.08 ± 1.50%, p=0.001). Incorporating lateral mandibular motion into CAD software pre-milling reduces occlusal interferences and preserves more crown volume compared to post-milling intraoral adjustment.

## 세줄요약

환자 내 비교 무작위 임상시험(n=13)으로 협측 교합기록만 사용한 군(Group I)과 협측+측방 교합기록(IOS 환자특이운동, PSM)을 함께 사용한 군(Group II) 간 단일 모놀리식 지르코니아 크라운을 비교하였다. Group II는 측방 편심 운동 시 최대압력(20.62 ± 3.38% vs 26.00 ± 4.95%, p<0.0001)과 최대교두감합위(MI) 압력(7.23 vs 8.08%, p=0.001) 모두 유의하게 낮았다. 밀링 전 교합조정(디지털 기능적발생경로 기법)은 의장내 조정을 줄이고 지르코니아 크라운의 기계적·미적 특성을 보존하는 데 유리하다.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that capturing lateral mandibular excursion with IOS (Medit i500, PSM/functionally generated path) and importing into Exocad for virtual interference removal significantly reduces post-cementation occlusal contact forces
- Group II (buccal + lateral record) crowns showed 20.7% less lateral excursion pressure and 10.5% less MI pressure vs Group I (buccal only)
- Crown volume in Group II was significantly smaller (157.63 vs 160.36 mm³, p=0.001), indicating effective pre-milling material removal — preserving zirconia surface integrity vs post-milling grinding
- Digital FGP workflow using IOS eliminates need for intraoral articulating-paper-based occlusal adjustment, which is limited by poor visibility, saliva, and marking imprecision
- Occlusense (60-micron sensor, 60 Hz, wireless) validated as equivalent to T-Scan for this application

## Methodology

- **Participants**: 13 patients, maxillary first premolar endodontically treated, all-ceramic preparation (1.5 mm occlusal, 1 mm chamfer margin, equigingival finish line), canine-guided occlusion, Class I
- **Scanning**: Medit i500 IOS (v2.2) — complete arch + buccal bite registration (BBR) + lateral excursion recording (working side, canine contact endpoint)
- **CAD design**: Exocad v3.1 Rejeka — cement gap 50 μm, 0 mm clearance; Group I: static occlusion (MI) only; Group II: MI + lateral excursion with Exocad intersection cutting tool
- **Milling/sintering**: Zenostar zirconia (Ivoclar Vivadent), Ceramill milling (Amann Girrbach), same furnace/protocol for all crowns
- **Cementation**: Zinc oxide temporary cement; silicon index for standardized seating; 2-minute rest between recordings; blind protocol (patients unaware of crown type)
- **Outcome measurement**: Occlusense digital occlusal analyzer — maximum pressure during lateral mandibular movement (working side) and MI; crown volume by Meshmixer (surface area × center-of-mass height)
- **Statistics**: Shapiro-Wilk normality, paired t-test and Wilcoxon Sign Rank test, p ≤ 0.05; IBM SPSS v23

## Key Results

| Outcome | Group I (buccal only) | Group II (buccal+lateral) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max pressure at lateral excursion (mean ± SD, %) | 26.00 ± 4.95 | 20.62 ± 3.38 | <0.0001* |
| Pressure at MI (mean ± SD, %) | 8.08 ± 1.50 | 7.23 ± 1.59 | 0.001* |
| Crown volume (mean ± SD, mm³) | 160.36 ± 15.94 | 157.63 ± 14.45 | 0.001* |

- Null hypothesis (no significant difference between groups) was **refuted**
- Group II crowns exhibited statistically significantly fewer and lower occlusal interferences on working-side lateral excursion
- Smaller crown volume in Group II reflects successful pre-milling interference removal, not material failure

## Limitations

- Small sample (n=13), single operator, single-center (Alexandria University)
- Exclusively maxillary first premolar single crowns — generalizability to anteriors, long-span FPDs, or other positions untested
- Lateral interocclusal records capture only initial (MI) and final (canine contact) points of excursion; intermediate path not fully captured — manual adjustment of remaining portion may still be needed
- Assessor was the provider (not independent); blind protocol limited to patient side
- Occlusense sensor positioning variability (dental arch shape, centering) can affect recordings; centering tray techniques recommended in future studies
- Lateral mandibular movement recorded as video (full path), which improves on point-capture methods but was not compared to full pantographic/jaw-tracking

## Glossary

- **디지털 기능적발생경로 (Digital Functionally Generated Path, FGP)**: IOS로 측방 하악 운동 경로를 기록하여 CAD 소프트웨어 내에서 편심 교합 간섭을 가상으로 제거하는 기법
- **환자특이운동 (Patient Specific Motion, PSM)**: 개인의 하악 운동을 IOS로 직접 캡처하는 3Shape/compatible 기능; FGP의 디지털 구현
- **최대교두감합위 (Maximum Intercuspation, MI)**: 최대 교두 접촉이 이루어지는 하악 위치; 보철 설계의 정적 기준점
- **교합 간섭 (Occlusal Interference)**: 편심 운동 시 보철물이 대합치에 조기 접촉하는 현상; 치아 이동, 마모, 보철 파절을 유발할 수 있음
- **Occlusense**: Dr. Jean Bausch GmbH의 60마이크론 압전 센서 기반 디지털 교합 분석 장치; 아이패드 무선 연동, 초당 60프레임 측정
- **몰리식 지르코니아 (Monolithic Zirconia)**: 도재 미소부 없이 지르코니아 블록 전체를 그대로 사용한 단일구조 보철; 연삭 시 상전이(tetragonal→monoclinic)에 취약
