## Three-line Summary

Two case reports (n=2 patients) of immediate post-extraction provisional prosthetic components used to support and transfer anterior soft-tissue contours: one immediate implant with a chairside customized healing abutment, one tooth-supported ovate-pontic fixed dental prosthesis (FDP).
Case 1 (59-year-old man) placed a 4.1 × 10-mm implant ~4 mm apical to the gingival margin with xenograft and left the customized abutment unadjusted for 3 months; Case 2 (66-year-old man) delivered a PMMA ovate pontic extending ~3 mm into the socket with 0.25 g Bio-Oss + 13 × 25-mm Bio-Gide, modified at 4 weeks; both showed relatively maintained labial contours on 1-month Exocad superimposition.
Contour assessment was visual rather than quantitative, follow-up was ~1 month, and both cases were grafted — so maintenance cannot be attributed solely to the provisional components; longer-term follow-up is needed.

## 세줄요약

발치 직후 즉시 프로비저널 보철물로 전치부 연조직 윤곽을 지지·전달한 2증례 보고 (환자 2명): 1예는 즉시 임플란트 식립(Immediate Implant Placement)+맞춤형 치유 지대주(Customized Healing Abutment), 1예는 치아지지성 난형 pontic(Ovate Pontic) 고정성 보철물(Fixed Dental Prosthesis, FDP).
증례 1(59세 남)은 치은연변 약 4 mm 근첩에 4.1×10-mm 임플란트를 식립하고 이종골이식(Xenograft) 후 맞춤형 지대주를 3개월간 무조정 유지; 증례 2(66세 남)는 소켓 내 약 3 mm 연장 난형 pontic에 0.25 g Bio-Oss·13×25-mm Bio-Gide 치조제 보존(Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 후 즉시 장착하고 4주째 프로비저널을 수정.
최종 지르코니아(Zirconia) 보철 후 1개월 Exocad 중첩에서 협측 연조직 윤곽 비교적 유지 — 다만 시각적 평가에 그치고 추적 ∼1개월·양 증례 모두 골이식 병행으로 윤곽 유지를 프로비저널 단독 효과로 귀속 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Applies one shared principle across two different prosthetic designs: a provisional component placed immediately after extraction, before soft-tissue collapse, acts as a scaffold and the established contour is then transferred to the definitive prosthesis.
- Case 1: chairside flowable-resin customized healing abutment with a concave subcritical contour (to minimize pressure and leave healing space) and a slightly under-contoured critical contour (to avoid premature marginal compression); left undisturbed through 3 months of osseointegration, with emergence profile developed only in the provisional phase.
- Case 2: digitally designed, milled polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA) ovate-pontic FDP (~3 mm into the extraction socket) delivered immediately after atraumatic extraction plus ridge preservation; modified at 4 weeks (subgingival resin addition, distal reduction, mesial addition) to correct gingival asymmetry and insufficient papilla.
- Digital contour-transfer workflow: superimposition of intraoral scans (provisional in place, provisional itself, and scan body / abutment teeth) to carry the subgingival contour into the definitive zirconia prostheses.
- Pre-extraction and post-delivery scans aligned on stable references (nonmobile teeth, adjacent hard tissue) in Exocad for visual labial-contour comparison.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Two case reports (n = 2 patients, 2 anterior sites), single center; no control group, no randomization.
- **Case 1**: 59-year-old man, fractured crown of the maxillary left central incisor with a crack extending toward the root → atraumatic extraction + immediate implant placement. Gingival margin level matched the contralateral central incisor; preoperative cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) deemed the site suitable. A surgical guide positioned the implant ~4 mm apical to the gingival margin for a favorable emergence profile.
- **Case 1 restorations**: 4.1 × 10-mm BlueDiamond implant (MegaGen Implant Co., Ltd., Daegu, Korea); labial gap grafted with Bio-Oss xenograft (Geistlich Pharma, Wolhusen, Switzerland); temporary abutment + chairside flowable-resin customized healing abutment; customized impression coping → working cast → screw-retained provisional, with resin added to the critical contour; definitive screw-retained zirconia implant-supported FDP.
- **Case 2**: 66-year-old man with a buccal fistula and periapical lesion at the maxillary left lateral incisor; removal of the existing splinted FDP revealed deep palatal root caries of the maxillary right central incisor → extraction of that incisor, replacement of the FDP (maxillary right lateral incisor to maxillary left central incisor), endodontic retreatment, and a crown for the maxillary left lateral incisor.
- **Case 2 restorations**: tooth preparation and intraoral scan performed before extraction; digitally designed PMMA ovate-pontic FDP (~3 mm into socket); after atraumatic extraction, ridge preservation with 0.25 g Bio-Oss + 13 × 25-mm Bio-Gide collagen membrane (Geistlich Pharma); immediate provisional delivery; definitive zirconia FDP + single crown.
- **Outcome assessment**: Exocad software (Exocad GmbH, Darmstadt, Germany) superimposition of pre-extraction vs. 1-month post-definitive-delivery intraoral scans, aligned on nonmobile teeth and adjacent hard tissue; visual comparison of labial soft-tissue contour. No validated quantitative measurement.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Item | Case 1 (implant) | Case 2 (pontic) |
|---|---|---|
| Patient / site | 59-year-old man, maxillary left central incisor | 66-year-old man, maxillary right central incisor |
| Implant | 4.1 × 10 mm BlueDiamond, guided, ~4 mm apical to gingival margin | None (tooth-supported FDP) |
| Graft | Bio-Oss xenograft in labial gap | 0.25 g Bio-Oss + 13 × 25-mm Bio-Gide |
| Provisional component | Chairside flowable-resin customized healing abutment (concave subcritical, slightly under-contoured critical), unadjusted for 3 months; then screw-retained provisional with resin added to critical contour | Milled PMMA FDP with ovate pontic ~3 mm into socket, delivered immediately; resin added subgingivally at 4 weeks, pontic shifted mesially |
| Definitive prosthesis | Screw-retained zirconia implant-supported FDP | Zirconia FDP + single crown |
| Contour outcome (Exocad superimposition, 1 month after definitive delivery) | Relatively well-maintained labial soft-tissue contour | Relatively well-maintained labial soft-tissue contour around pontic site |

- No quantitative contour measurements, effect sizes, or p-values were reported — the assessment was explicitly a "supportive visual assessment."
- Soft-tissue contour was clinically stable at 3 months in Case 1; gingival asymmetry and insufficient papilla at 4 weeks in Case 2 prompted provisional modification, after which contour and papilla improved.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only two cases, and the prosthetic approaches differed between them.
- Errors from intraoral scanning, scan-data alignment, and soft-tissue measurement cannot be excluded; contour change was assessed mainly by visual evaluation of superimposed scans rather than validated quantitative measurements.
- Follow-up limited to ~1 month after definitive prosthesis delivery — only early outcomes evaluable.
- Bone grafting was performed in both cases and may itself have preserved ridge dimensions and labial contour; the relative contributions of grafting and prosthetic soft-tissue support cannot be separated.
- Longer-term follow-up needed to evaluate biologic and mechanical stability.

## 7. Glossary

- **Emergence Profile (형성 프로파일)**: contour of the abutment/provisional as it emerges through the soft tissue, used to shape the peri-implant or pontic soft tissue.
- **Customized Healing Abutment (맞춤형 치유 지대주)**: patient-specific abutment, here flowable resin fabricated chairside, shaped to support peri-implant soft tissue during healing.
- **Critical / Subcritical Contour (임계 / 아임계 윤곽)**: critical contour shapes the marginal gingiva at the tissue level; subcritical contour lies deeper and provides space for soft-tissue healing without pressure.
- **Ovate Pontic (난형 pontic)**: egg-shaped pontic designed to extend partially into the extraction socket to support and guide pontic-site soft-tissue healing.
- **Alveolar Ridge Preservation (치조제 보존, ARP)**: grafting of the extraction socket to limit post-extraction ridge dimensional change.
- **Exocad Superimposition (Exocad 중첩)**: software alignment of serial intraoral scans on stable references for visual comparison of soft-tissue contour change.
