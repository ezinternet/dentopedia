## Why Ingested

투명교정 부착물(attachment)의 형태·배치·접착에 대한 종합적 시스테마틱 리뷰로, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/nucera-2022-composite-attachments-clear-aligners-sr]]가 제시한 부착물 효율 근거를 더 넓은 26편으로 체계화한 업데이트다.

## Three-line Summary

This comprehensive SR analyzed 26 articles on composite attachments for orthodontic aligner treatment, covering attachment bonding (4 studies) and movement efficacy (22 studies). Attachments significantly improve orthodontic movement expression and aligner retention, with specific tooth sites identified where attachments have better efficacy. The review provides evidence-based guidance on optimal attachment placement, shape, and bonding protocols.

## 세줄요약

투명교정 부착물의 형태·배치·접착에 대한 26편의 종합 SR로, 접착(4편)과 이동 효율(22편)을 분석. 부착물은 교정 이동 표현과 교정기 보존을 유의하게 향상시키며, 특정 치아 위치에서 부착물 효율이 더 좋은 것으로 확인. 최적의 부착물 배치·형태·접착 프로토콜에 대한 근거 기반 지침을 제공.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Most comprehensive SR to date on aligner attachments (26 studies)
- Identifies optimal tooth sites for attachment placement by movement type
- Provides evidence-based guidance on attachment shape, size, and bonding
- Distinguishes between attachment bonding protocols and movement efficacy

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review
- **Databases**: 6 databases (searched Dec 2022)
- **n**: 26 articles (4 bonding, 22 movement efficacy)
- **PROSPERO**: CRD42022383276

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Attachments significantly improve movement expression vs no attachments
- Specific sites identified for optimal efficacy per movement type
- Attachment design influences treatment outcome predictability
- Bonding protocol affects attachment retention during treatment

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Heterogeneity in attachment designs across studies
- Limited clinical RCTs (mostly in-vitro/FEA)
- Manufacturer-specific attachment protocols not fully comparable
- Long-term attachment performance data limited
