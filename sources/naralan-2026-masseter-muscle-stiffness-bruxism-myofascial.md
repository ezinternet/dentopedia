## Why Ingested

Palpation-based diagnosis of myogenous TMD, as described in [[wiki/tmj/chan-2022-myogenous-tmd-diagnosis-treatment-review]], is subjective; this SR/MA adds the first pooled estimate of an objective imaging marker (masseter stiffness by elastography). It also gives a pre-treatment reference frame for [[wiki/botulinum-toxin/yan-2025-masseter-elasticity-botulinum-toxin-ultrasound]], which tracks elasticity after BoNT-A.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020) of 7 observational studies (8 datasets; 190 patients, 202 healthy controls) measuring masseter stiffness by shear wave elastography or ARFI in bruxism and/or myofascial pain-related TMD.

Masseter stiffness was higher in patients: Hedges g = 1.485 (95% CI 0.763-2.207; I2 = 84.1%); kPa sensitivity analysis MD = 6.16 kPa (95% CI 3.67-8.65; I2 = 84.4%).

GRADE certainty was very low with substantial heterogeneity, so elastography should be interpreted cautiously alongside clinical assessment; absolute values are not interpretable.

## 세줄요약

관찰연구 7편 (8개 데이터셋; 환자 190명, 건강 대조군 202명)을 포함한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR/MA)으로, 이갈이 (Bruxism) 및/또는 근막통 관련 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에서 전단파 탄성초음파 (Shear Wave Elastography, SWE) 또는 음향방사력 임펄스 영상 (Acoustic Radiation Force Impulse, ARFI)으로 교근 (Masseter) 강성을 측정했다.

환자군의 교근 강성이 더 높았다: Hedges g = 1.485 (95% CI 0.763-2.207; I2 = 84.1%), kPa 민감도 분석 평균차 (Mean Difference, MD) = 6.16 kPa (95% CI 3.67-8.65).

근거 확실성 (GRADE)은 매우 낮고 이질성이 커서 절대값 해석은 제한적이며, 탄성초음파는 임상 평가와 함께 신중히 해석해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First pooled quantification of masseter stiffness (elastography) versus healthy controls in bruxism and/or myofascial pain-related TMD.
- Reports both a unit-free (Hedges g) and a clinically readable (kPa) pooled difference.
- Grades certainty explicitly (very low).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PRISMA 2020; PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, OpenGrey from inception to November 2025.
- Eligibility: observational human studies, masseter stiffness by SWE or ARFI, healthy control group.
- Primary: random-effects SMD (Hedges g) with Hartung-Knapp adjustment. Sensitivity: MD in kPa after unit harmonization.
- Risk of bias: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale; Egger's test and funnel plot (limited power, < 10 datasets); leave-one-out analyses; GRADE.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 7 studies / 8 datasets; 190 patients vs 202 controls (n = 392).
- Primary: Hedges g = 1.485; 95% CI 0.763-2.207; p = 0.00183; I2 = 84.1%.
- Sensitivity: MD = 6.16 kPa; 95% CI 3.67-8.65; p = 0.000628; I2 = 84.4%.
- Leave-one-out: findings robust. GRADE: very low.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Substantial heterogeneity (I2 ~84%); very low certainty.
- Egger's test underpowered with < 10 datasets.
- Observational, cross-sectional comparisons only; no diagnostic accuracy or treatment-response data in the abstract.
- Abstract-only ingest: subgroup details (bruxism vs TMD, device, scan protocol) not available.

## 7. Glossary

- Shear wave elastography (SWE): ultrasound method estimating tissue stiffness from shear wave speed.
- ARFI: acoustic radiation force impulse imaging, an elastography variant.
- Hedges g: bias-corrected standardized mean difference.
- Hartung-Knapp adjustment: variance correction for random-effects meta-analysis with few studies.
- GRADE: framework rating certainty of evidence.
