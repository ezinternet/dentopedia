## Why Ingested

The wiki's 46-paper synthesis [[overviews/clear-aligner-indications-limitations]] builds its extraction-biomechanics axis (axis 1, the "Roller Coaster Effect" and CoS-leveling-failure findings) mostly on retrospective cohorts — [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/zhuo-2026-the-roller-coaster-effect]] (n=65) and [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/tang-2025-evaluating-the-effectiveness-of-clear]] (n=60) — plus one RCT-tier comparison of complex/extraction outcomes, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/jaber-2023-clear-aligners-complex-extraction-vs-fixed-sr]], which compared PAR index between clear aligners and fixed appliances but did not measure tooth-movement accuracy. This paper is a **split-mouth RCT** directly testing whether a palatal power arm improves canine-retraction accuracy during first-premolar extraction space closure — the same movement (uncontrolled canine tipping/rotation during distalization) implicated in the Roller Coaster Effect and CoS undercorrection — raising the evidence tier for a specific biomechanical countermeasure (power arm) rather than just documenting the problem. It also extends [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/nucera-2022-composite-attachments-clear-aligners-sr]] (attachment biomechanics improving anterior torque/rotation) and [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/cao-2025-clear-aligner-biomechanics-finite-element-analysis-sr]] (auxiliaries rebalancing crown/root stress) with clinical-trial evidence for one specific auxiliary design.

## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT, 18 adults (21 randomized, 3 dropouts) undergoing maxillary first-premolar-extraction in-house clear aligner (IHCA) treatment, comparing a palatal power arm (PA) side vs. control (C) side for final canine retraction accuracy using equivalence testing (TOST, Holm–Bonferroni correction).

All three canine linear movements (distalization, extrusion-intrusion, buccolingual) were equivalent between PA and control (90% CI within ±0.5 mm), but canine rotation (mean diff 5.22°, 90% CI 1.48–8.95°) and distal crown tipping (mean diff 1.67°, 90% CI −0.44–3.78°) were NOT equivalent — exceeding the ±1.5° margin — with the PA side showing less tipping/rotation than control; anchorage-tooth (premolar/molar) linear outcomes were mostly equivalent but angular outcomes were mostly inconclusive, and both sides showed anchorage loss signs (mesial tipping, relative intrusion).

Clinical implication: a palatal power arm may improve angular control (tipping/rotation) of the canine during aligner-based extraction space closure without changing linear distalization accuracy, but the study is underpowered (post-hoc power 68.8% for the primary outcome) and does not demonstrate anchorage preservation — findings are IHCA-specific and may not generalize to commercial aligner systems.

## 세줄요약

split-mouth RCT, 성인 18명(21명 무작위배정 중 3명 탈락), 상악 제1소구치 발치 후 in-house 투명교정장치(IHCA)로 최종 견치 원심이동 시 구개측 파워암(palatal power arm, PA) vs 대조측(C) 비교, 동등성검정(TOST, Holm–Bonferroni 보정).

견치 선형이동(원심이동·정출입·협설) 3항목 모두 PA-대조 간 동등(90% CI가 ±0.5mm 이내)했으나, 견치 회전(평균차 5.22°, 90% CI 1.48–8.95°)과 원심경사(distal tipping, 평균차 1.67°, 90% CI −0.44–3.78°)는 ±1.5° 한계를 초과해 **동등성 미입증**(PA측이 tipping·rotation 더 적음); 고정원 치아(소구치·대구치)는 선형지표 대부분 동등하나 각도지표는 대부분 미확정, 양측 모두 고정원 소실 징후(근심경사·상대적 압하) 관찰.

임상적 함의: 구개측 파워암은 발치 공간폐쇄 시 견치의 각도 조절(경사·회전)을 개선할 가능성이 있으나 원심이동 자체의 정확도에는 영향 없고, 본 연구는 통계적 검정력이 부족(1차 결과지표 post-hoc power 68.8%)하며 고정원 보존 효과는 입증 못함 — IHCA 특이적 결과로 상용 투명교정 시스템에 일반화 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First RCT to directly compare power-arm-assisted vs. non-power-arm canine retraction accuracy in extraction cases using clear aligners — the wiki's prior power-arm/anchorage discussion (`nucera-2022`, `cao-2025`, `lei-2026`) was mechanistic/FEA/narrative, not clinical-trial.
- Split-mouth design controls for pretreatment/patient-level confounders (each patient serves as own control, one side PA, one side control), strengthening causal inference over the retrospective cohorts (`zhuo-2026`, `tang-2025`) used elsewhere in the wiki's extraction-biomechanics evidence.
- Introduces a multi-stage in-house aligner (IHCA) protocol with interim rescanning/replanning every 3 mm of canine movement, contrasting with the one-stage digital setup typical of commercial (Invisalign) studies — argued by the authors to reduce cumulative tracking error in extraction space closure.
- Uses a rigorous equivalence-testing framework (TOST + Holm–Bonferroni step-down correction across 3 linear/3 angular outcomes per tooth) rather than simple paired t-tests, explicitly distinguishing "no significant difference" from "clinically equivalent."

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective, single-center, split-mouth RCT, 1:1 allocation, no blinding (power arm visibility precludes it). CONSORT 2010-compliant.
- **Population**: Adults >18y requiring maxillary first-premolar extraction, enrolled 2020–2023; 21 randomized, 18 analyzed (85.7%, 3 dropouts). Mean age 23.46±4.62y, 17 female/1 male. 6 Angle Class I, 12 Class II. Mean maxillary crowding −3.42±1.50 mm, overjet 6.10±1.69 mm.
- **Intervention**: Both sides received identical IHCA staging/attachment/aligner-sequence protocol (three-sided prism attachments on canines, 2nd premolars, 1st molars, lateral incisors). PA side additionally had a prefabricated palatal power arm bonded to canine (12 mm) and ipsilateral 1st molar (8 mm), connected by a superelastic power chain (80–100 g force, replaced q6–8wk). Control side: aligner force alone, no power arm/chain.
- **Aligner fabrication**: PET-G thermoplastic (0.5 mm then 0.75 mm sequential sheets), thermoformed via pressure molding (Biostar). Canine moved 3 mm per stage (0.5 mm/model) with model reprinting via Meshmixer-prepared hollow-base 3D-printed models.
- **Outcome measurement**: iTero intraoral scans at pretreatment and after final canine aligner completion. Virtual (predicted) vs. actual (achieved) models superimposed via GOM Inspect Suite (palatal rugae/vault reference, Geometric Element + local best-fit methods). 3 linear (mesio-distal, bucco-lingual, extrusion-intrusion) + 3 angular (tip, rotation, torque) measurements per canine, 2nd premolar, 1st molar.
- **Statistics**: Primary outcome = canine distalization. Equivalence margins pre-specified: ±0.5 mm (linear), ±1.5° (angular), based on prior aligner-accuracy literature and clinical detectability thresholds. TOST (Two One-Sided Tests) with 90% CI, Holm–Bonferroni step-down correction (m=3 per tooth/domain family) for multiplicity. Sample size: 14 pairs needed for 80% power (detecting 0.31 mm mean paired difference, SD 0.27 mm); 21 recruited for attrition buffer.

- Difference test: canine rotation deviation (actual−predicted) was significantly smaller on PA side (−0.89±7.50°) than control (−6.10±6.15°), mean difference 5.22±9.11° — i.e., control-side canines rotated substantially more than planned, PA-side canines tracked closer to plan.
- Post-hoc power for primary outcome (canine distalization equivalence): 68.8% — study likely underpowered, especially for angular outcomes.
- Dahlberg's error analysis: all measurement discrepancies <0.5 mm / <0.5° (acceptable measurement precision).
- No harms observed in any participant.
- Consistent with prior Invisalign extraction-case findings (Dai, Ren, Feng — cited but not held in this wiki): canines under-distalize but over-tip/rotate/extrude vs. plan; posterior anchor teeth show more mesial movement/tipping/intrusion than planned — suggesting these biomechanical tendencies are common across clear aligner systems, not IHCA-specific.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=18 analyzed), single population (mild crowding, mild canine asymmetry, premolar-extraction cases only) — enhances internal validity but limits generalizability and power.
- No blinding possible (power arm is visually obvious) — mitigated by independent outcome assessor and standardized protocol, but performance/detection bias not eliminated.
- Palatal-rugae-based superimposition can be affected by rugae remodeling during anterior retraction; no skeletal anchorage (TADs) used, so posterior "stationary" reference teeth may themselves have moved, potentially biasing best-fit superimposition.
- Outcomes assessed only at completion of canine retraction (not full anterior/space-closure treatment) — cannot speak to full Roller-Coaster-Effect-relevant endpoints (posterior open bite) assessed in `zhuo-2026`.
- IHCA-specific: material (PET-G 0.5–0.75 mm), staging (multi-stage with interim rescanning), and attachment design differ from commercial systems (e.g., Invisalign multilayer polyurethane, one-stage setup) — authors explicitly caution against generalizing to commercial aligners.
- Authors recommend larger prospective trials with varied materials/wear protocols and CBCT-based 3D setup/superimposition for improved precision.

## 7. Glossary

- **IHCA**: In-house clear aligners — clinician-fabricated (vs. commercial/outsourced) aligner system.
- **PA / C**: Palatal power arm side / Control side (split-mouth design arms).
- **TOST**: Two One-Sided Tests — statistical procedure for equivalence testing.
- **Holm–Bonferroni step-down**: multiple-comparison correction controlling family-wise error rate across ranked p-values.
- **GOM Inspect Suite**: 3D metrology software used for virtual-vs-actual model superimposition and deviation measurement.
- **Roller Coaster Effect**: named phenomenon (see `zhuo-2026`) of posterior open bite from uneven anterior/posterior contact during aligner-based extraction space closure.
- **Center of resistance**: the point in a tooth-root system about which a force produces pure translation without rotation; aligner force applied below it produces uncontrolled tipping, which a power arm (raising the force application point) is intended to counteract.
