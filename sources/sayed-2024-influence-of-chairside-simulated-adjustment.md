## Why Ingested

Clinically important in vitro study on the effect of chairside grinding/polishing protocols and chlorhexidine mouthwash on the color stability and translucency of two preshaded multilayered monolithic zirconia (MLZ) ceramics. Directly informs zirconia crown adjustment and postoperative mouthwash prescription decisions.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/sayed-2024-influence-of-chairside-simulated-adjustment]]

## Three-line Summary

Two preshaded multilayered monolithic zirconia ceramics (Nacera Pearl Multi-Shade [PSZ/3Y-TZP] and Ceramill Zolid FX Multilayer [FSZ]) differed in color at baseline despite identical Vita shade designations. Chlorhexidine gluconate (ChG) mouthwash immersion for 14 days produced clinically perceptible color changes (ΔE = 1.0–3.3) and increased contrast ratio (reduced translucency) in both ceramics regardless of adjustment kit used. Glazed MLZ specimens showed the least color and translucency change, supporting the recommendation to complete all chairside adjustments prior to final glazing.

## 세줄요약

사전착색 다층 모놀리식 지르코니아(NPM: PSZ, CZM: FSZ) 두 종류는 동일한 Vita 쉐이드로 제작되었음에도 기준선 색상이 유의하게 달랐다. 클로르헥시딘 구강 세정제 2주 침지 시 두 도재 모두 임상적으로 인지 가능한 색상 변화(ΔE = 1.0–3.3)와 투명도 저하가 발생하였으며, 조정 키트 종류와 무관하였다. 최종 광택처리(글레이즈)된 시편에서 색상 및 투명도 변화가 가장 적었으므로, 모든 임상 조정은 최종 글레이즈 전에 완료해야 한다.

## Key Contributions

1. First study to investigate ChG mouthwash effect on preshaded (not colored/stained) multilayered MLZ after simulated chairside adjustment
2. Demonstrates that two preshaded MLZ from the same Vita shade designation differ significantly in baseline color (PSZ vs FSZ composition difference)
3. Quantifies that NPM (PSZ) is more sensitive to third-party adjustment kits than CZM (FSZ)
4. Confirms that glazed MLZ offers superior color and translucency stability against ChG staining
5. Clinically actionable recommendation: adjust MLZ before final glazing if ChG is to be prescribed

## Methodology

**Materials tested:**
- NPM: Nacera Pearl Multi-Shade (PSZ, Y₂O₃ 4–6 mol%, shade A-light = Vita A1+A2)
- CZM: Ceramill Zolid FX Multilayer C20-HT (FSZ, Y₂O₃ 8.5–9.5 mol%)

**Specimen preparation:**
- Disc-shaped specimens: 1.2 ± 0.2 mm thick × 14 mm diameter (ISO 6872-2015)
- CAD/CAM milled dry; sintered per manufacturer protocol
- All specimens glazed to simulate clinical setting (n = 72 each ceramic)

**Adjustment protocols:**
- Diamond bur (40 grit) grinding at 160,000 rpm, unidirectional
- Finishing/polishing: manufacturer-recommended kits (Diasynt Plus + Diacera for NPM; SUN for CZM) vs. third-party (swapped kits)
- Polishing: 60 s, two-stage perpendicular technique; ultrasonic cleaning in triple-distilled water

**Immersion media (n = 12/group):**
- Artificial saliva (control)
- 2% chlorhexidine gluconate mouthwash (Avohex): 10 min × twice daily × 14 days

**12 groups total:** 6 per ceramic (glazed/Kit1/Kit2 × saliva/ChG)

**Outcome measures:**
- Color: CIELAB colorimeter (ΔE); thresholds — CAT/CPT ΔE ≥ 3.3; clinically perceptible ΔE 1.0–3.3
- Translucency parameter (TP): white/black backing comparison
- Contrast ratio (CR): Yb/Yw; threshold Y ≥ 0.07 = clinically detectable translucency reduction
- Statistical analysis: one-way ANOVA + post hoc LSD; Shapiro-Wilk normality; p < 0.05

## Key Results

**Baseline color difference:**
- NPM and CZM differed significantly in color (L*, a*, b*) at baseline despite same Vita A1+A2 shade
- No significant baseline difference in translucency parameter (TP)

**Effect of adjustment kit:**
- NPM: significant color increase (ΔE 0.33→0.43 in saliva; 2.74→2.90 in ChG) when adjusted with third-party kit
- CZM: compatible with both manufacturer-recommended and third-party kits in saliva (no significant color difference between Gp CDS/Gp CSS); less sensitive to third-party kits

**Effect of chlorhexidine immersion:**
- All groups (regardless of ceramic type or adjustment kit): clinically perceptible color changes (ΔE = 1.0–3.3) on the CAT/CPT scale
- All groups: increased CR (Y ≥ 0.07) indicating clinically detectable translucency reduction
- Both ceramics' TP differed significantly between ChG-immersed groups except glazed specimens
- Glazed specimens: minimal to no color/TP change in both ceramics after ChG immersion

**Saliva-immersed groups:**
- Color changes ΔE ≤ 1.00 (not clinically appreciable) in all saliva groups
- Ceramic type and adjustment kit influenced color (significant ANOVA between Gp NGS/Gp CGS and Gp NSS/Gp CSS)

## Limitations

1. In vitro study; clinical conditions (intraoral temperature cycling, masticatory load, pH variation) not replicated
2. Only two MLZ ceramics and one mouthwash (2% ChG) investigated — results not generalizable to all MLZ
3. Color measurement used CIELAB formula; CIEDE2000 is a more comprehensive and perceptually uniform tool
4. Disc specimens; clinical crown geometry not represented
5. Limited to 2-week immersion; long-term effects unknown

## Glossary

- **MLZ**: Monolithic zirconia — fully contoured zirconia restoration without veneering porcelain
- **PSZ**: Partially stabilized zirconia (Y₂O₃ 4–6 mol%); 3Y-TZP class; higher strength, lower translucency
- **FSZ**: Fully cubic stabilized zirconia (Y₂O₃ 8.5–9.5 mol%); higher translucency
- **CAT/CPT**: Clinical Acceptability/Perceptibility Threshold (ΔE ≥ 3.3 = clinically acceptable; ΔE 1.0–3.3 = perceptible by skilled operator)
- **TP**: Translucency parameter — color difference measured over black vs white backgrounds
- **CR**: Contrast ratio = Yb/Yw; Y ≥ 0.07 = clinically detectable translucency reduction
- **ChG**: Chlorhexidine gluconate
- **LTD**: Low-temperature degradation — tetragonal→monoclinic phase transformation in 3Y-TZP upon water exposure
- **NPM**: Nacera Pearl Multi-Shade (product name, Doceram Medical GmbH)
- **CZM**: Ceramill Zolid FX Multilayer (product name, Amann Girrbach)
