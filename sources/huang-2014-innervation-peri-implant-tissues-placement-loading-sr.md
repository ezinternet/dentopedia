## Why Ingested

임플란트 주위 신경지배 SR — [[huang-2014-innervation-peri-implant-tissues-placement-loading-sr]] 페이지의 1차 근거; 로딩 프로토콜과 임플란트 주위 조직 신경재분포의 관계를 다루는 희소한 SR.

## Three-line Summary

A systematic review (10 studies from MEDLINE/Cochrane/EMBASE; up to July 2013) investigated the effect of implant placement and loading protocols on peri-implant innervation in hard and soft tissues. All 10 studies confirmed sensory innervation around osseointegrated implants; loaded implants showed significantly higher nerve fibre density compared to unloaded implants and extraction sites without implantation. Evidence was insufficient to distinguish innervation patterns between immediate and delayed placement or loading protocols due to high risk of bias and study heterogeneity.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 식립 및 로딩 프로토콜이 임플란트 주위 조직 신경지배 (Peri-implant Innervation)에 미치는 영향을 다룬 체계적 문헌고찰 (10편, 2013년 7월까지). 10편 모두 골유착 임플란트 주변의 감각 신경섬유를 확인했으며, 로딩된 임플란트 주위의 신경섬유 밀도 (Nerve Fibre Density)가 무부하 임플란트 및 비식립 발치와에 비해 유의하게 높았다. 즉시·지연 식립/로딩 간 신경분포 패턴 차이는 높은 비뚤림 위험 (Risk of Bias, RoB)과 연구 이질성으로 인해 결론 내리기 어려움.

## Objective

Systematically review available literature on the influence of dental implant placement and loading protocols on peri-implant innervation. PEO question: Is there, and if so, what is the effect of time between tooth extraction and implant placement or implant loading on neural fibre content in peri-implant hard and soft tissues?

## Search Strategy

- Databases: MEDLINE, Cochrane, EMBASE, Web of Science, LILACS, OpenGrey
- Hand searching performed
- Coverage: up to July 2013

## Inclusion / Screening

- 683 titles retrieved
- 10 articles fulfilled inclusion criteria
  - 5 evaluating innervation of peri-implant epithelium
  - 5 evaluating sensory function in peri-implant bone

## Study Quality

- 3 studies: medium quality
- 7 studies: low quality
- High risk of bias noted across included studies

## Key Findings

1. **Innervation pattern confirmed**: All 10 studies reported sensory innervation around osseointegrated implants — at the bone-implant interface or in peri-implant epithelium — with a particular, distinct innervation pattern.

2. **Loading effect (significant)**: Compared to unloaded implants or extraction sites without implantation, a significantly higher density of nerve fibres around loaded dental implants was confirmed.

3. **Placement/loading timing (insufficient evidence)**: Insufficient evidence to distinguish between innervation patterns following immediate and delayed implant placement and loading protocols.

4. **Peri-implant epithelium component**: Five studies specifically addressed innervation of peri-implant epithelium; five addressed sensory function in peri-implant bone.

## Conclusions

- Peri-implant tissues have a distinct innervation pattern in both hard and soft tissues
- Implant loading appears to increase nerve fibre density in peri-implant tissues
- Placement timing (immediate vs delayed) and loading timing cannot be differentiated in terms of innervation outcomes based on current evidence
- Evidence gaps: high risk of bias, variability in study design and loading protocols, inconsistency across studies
- Research needs: more RCTs, longer observation periods, standardised protocols

## Clinical Relevance

Osseoperception — sensory feedback transmitted through osseointegrated implants — may be modulated by loading status. The finding that loading significantly increases peri-implant nerve fibre density has implications for understanding the proprioceptive/osseoperceptive capacity of implant-retained prostheses compared to unloaded or recently placed implants.
