## Why Ingested

Single-center RCT directly comparing 피개나사 (Cover Screw) and 치유지대주 (Healing Abutment) at bone-level single implants, reporting 치조정 변연골 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) and peri-implant soft-tissue outcomes at 6 months with a fully digital workflow; extends and reinforces the existing [[overviews/implant-placement-protocol-submerged-one-stage-one-piece-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

RCT of 66 bone-level PrimeTaper implants in 45 patients randomized to healing abutment (HA, n=34) or cover screw submerged (CS, n=32) protocol with 6-month follow-up using a fully digital workflow and definitive zirconia crowns. Both groups achieved 100% implant survival and comparable MBL at 6 months; however, the HA group showed markedly less bleeding — approximately 75% of implants had no BuPA at follow-up (p<0.001). Healing abutment protocol avoids flap re-entry, simplifies the clinical sequence, and is equally effective to the submerged cover-screw approach.

## 세줄요약

## Key Contributions

1. **Comparable MBL at 6 months** — both cover screw and healing abutment protocols yield statistically equivalent marginal bone-level changes at 6 months in bone-level single implants.
2. **Soft-tissue advantage of healing abutment** — HA group had ~75% of implants with no BuPA at follow-up (p<0.001), substantially reducing bleeding during prosthetic procedures.
3. **No flap re-entry required** — HA group avoids a second surgical procedure, streamlining the clinical workflow and reducing patient burden.
4. **Digital workflow integration** — provisional and definitive zirconia crowns delivered at 3–4 months using a fully digital protocol with no compromise to outcomes.
5. **100% survival in both arms** — no implant failures; one mucositis case in the HA group (minor).

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomized controlled clinical trial (RCT)
- **Sample**: 66 PrimeTaper implants (Dentsply, Mannheim, Germany) in 45 healthy adult patients
- **Groups**: Healing abutment group (n=34 implants); Cover screw submerged group (n=32 implants)
- **Placement**: Bone-level crestal placement
- **Workflow**: Fully digital — provisional and definitive zirconia crowns at 3–4 months
- **Follow-up**: Baseline, 3 months, 6 months
- **Outcomes**: BuSP (Bleeding upon Scanbody positioning), BuPA (Bleeding upon Prosthetic Abutment application), Mombelli modified plaque score, MBL (radiographic), prosthetic complications
- **Baseline**: Groups balanced at baseline

## Key Results

| Outcome | Healing Abutment | Cover Screw | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant survival | 100% | 100% | NS |
| BuPA (≈75% absent) | ~75% no BuPA | Significantly higher BuPA | <0.001 |
| MBL at 6 months | Stable, comparable | Stable, comparable | NS |
| Plaque score (Mombelli) | Improved over time | Improved over time | NS |
| Mucositis | 1 case | 0 cases | — |
| Flap re-entry | Not required | Required (2nd stage) | — |

Both techniques yielded stable peri-implant tissues. Healing abutment protocol reduced bleeding during prosthetic procedures and eliminated the need for second-stage surgery.

## Limitations

1. Short follow-up (6 months) — long-term MBL trajectories beyond prosthetic loading are not captured.
2. Single implant system (PrimeTaper/Dentsply) — generalizability to other bone-level implants uncertain.
3. Single institution — center-specific digital workflow may not represent general practice.
4. Abstract-only — precise MBL values and statistical details for MBL comparison not available from abstract.
5. Small sample size per arm (n≈32–34) limits power for secondary outcomes.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Cover Screw (피개나사) | A flat screw placed on the implant at surgery; implant is then covered by a sutured mucoperiosteal flap (submerged/2-stage healing). Second surgery required to uncover implant. |
| Healing Abutment (치유지대주, HA) | A transmucosal abutment placed at implant surgery, allowing healing through the gingiva (non-submerged/1-stage). No second surgery needed. |
| BuSP | Bleeding upon Scanbody positioning — bleeding when the digital scan body is placed on the implant; surrogate for peri-implant tissue health at first prosthetic appointment. |
| BuPA | Bleeding upon Prosthetic Abutment application — bleeding when the final abutment is seated; reflects mucosal inflammation at time of prosthetic delivery. |
| MBL (Marginal Bone Level / 치조정 변연골) | Radiographic level of crestal bone relative to implant reference point; key indicator of peri-implant bone stability. |
| Submerged vs Non-submerged | Submerged = cover screw under flap (2-stage); non-submerged = healing abutment through mucosa (1-stage, transmucosal healing). |
