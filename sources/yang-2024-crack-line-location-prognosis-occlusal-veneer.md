## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study (n=80) evaluating the prognostic significance of crack line location (Type I: within occlusal veneer preparation boundaries vs Type II: beyond preparation boundaries) on pulp vitality and tooth survival after occlusal veneer treatment.

Crack line location relative to the preparation boundary was a significant predictor of pulp vitality outcomes; Type II cracks (extending beyond prep boundaries) showed inferior prognosis compared to Type I, providing a clinically actionable classification for treatment planning decisions.

The study supports using crack line location — determined at the time of veneer preparation — as a stratification factor for counseling patients about pulp risk and expected prognosis when choosing occlusal veneer as the restorative approach for cracked teeth.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상 연구(n=80): 교합 비니어 삭제 후 균열선 위치(Type I: 삭제 경계 내 vs Type II: 경계 초과)가 치수 활력 및 치아 생존 예후에 미치는 영향 평가.

균열선이 삭제 경계를 초과하는 Type II가 Type I 대비 치수 활력 예후 열등 — 교합 비니어 치료 계획 시 임상적으로 활용 가능한 균열 분류 기준 제시.

균열선 위치를 비니어 삭제 시점에 확인해 환자 상담 및 치수 위험도 층화에 활용할 것을 권고.

## One-line Summary
Prospective study (n=80): crack line location relative to occlusal veneer preparation boundaries (Type I within vs Type II beyond) significantly predicts pulp vitality outcomes after occlusal veneer treatment.

## 한줄요약
전향적 연구(n=80): 교합 비니어 삭제 경계 대비 균열선 위치(Type I 내부 vs Type II 초과)가 치수 활력 예후의 유의한 예측인자.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Introduces Type I/II crack classification based on relationship to occlusal veneer preparation boundary
- First prospective study linking crack line location to pulp vitality prognosis post-veneer treatment
- Provides actionable intraoperative classification for treatment planning and patient counseling
- Demonstrates that occlusal veneer preparation itself reveals prognostically important crack extent information
- Supports tooth-conserving occlusal veneer as viable option when crack is within Type I boundary

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Prospective clinical study
- Sample: n=80 (cracked teeth receiving occlusal veneer preparation)
- Classification: Type I = crack line within occlusal veneer preparation boundaries; Type II = crack line extends beyond preparation boundaries
- Primary outcome: Pulp vitality status post-treatment
- Secondary outcomes: Tooth survival, patient-reported outcomes
- Follow-up duration: Not specified in abstract

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Type II cracks: inferior pulp vitality prognosis vs Type I
- Crack line location (Type I/II) = significant predictor of pulp outcome (P<.05 implied)
- Specific survival rates, AUC, or OR not extractable from abstract

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: specific effect sizes and follow-up duration not available
- Single-center study; generalizability unknown
- Type I/II classification requires clear intraoperative visualization — operator dependent
- No randomization — observational design subject to selection bias

## 7. Glossary
- Type I crack: crack line entirely contained within the occlusal veneer preparation boundary
- Type II crack: crack line extending beyond the preparation boundary
- Occlusal veneer: conservative full-occlusal restoration removing enamel/minimal dentin without full crown preparation
