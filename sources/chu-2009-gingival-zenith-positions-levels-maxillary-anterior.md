## Why Ingested

치은 정점 (Gingival Zenith) 관련 위키 gap을 메우는 sweep의 기초 문헌이다. 임상에서 가장 많이 인용되는 "중절치 1 mm 원심 / 측절치 0.4 mm / 견치 중앙 / 측절치 1 mm 치관측" 수치의 원출처로, 출현윤곽 설계 목표값을 제공해 [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/gomez-meda-2021-esthetic-biological-contour-emergence-profile]]의 Zone 3(임계 윤곽) 설계를 구체화한다. 같은 저자군의 [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chu-2015-flapless-postextraction-socket-implant-placement-part2]]와 연결된다.

## Three-line Summary

Clinical cross-sectional study of 240 sites in 20 periodontally healthy young adults (13 F, 7 M; mean age 27.7 y) measuring gingival zenith position (GZP) from the vertical bisected midline and lateral-incisor gingival zenith level (GZL) on diagnostic casts.

All central incisors had a distal GZP (mean 1 mm); lateral incisors deviated a mean 0.4 mm; 97.5% of canines had a centred GZP; the lateral-incisor GZL sat about 1 mm coronal to the line joining central and canine zeniths.

These values serve as reference targets for crown lengthening, root coverage, and anterior restorative/implant soft-tissue design, though derived from a small, young, ideal-dentition sample.

중절치 GZP는 전부 원심(평균 1 mm), 측절치 평균 0.4 mm 편위, 견치 97.5%는 중앙; 측절치 GZL은 중절치–견치 정점 연결선보다 약 1 mm 치관측.

치관연장술·치근피개·전치부 보철/임플란트 연조직 설계의 기준값이나, 소수·젊은·이상적 치열 표본에서 나온 값.

## 1. Document Information

## 2. Key Contributions

- Defines GZP relative to the VBM of each maxillary anterior tooth.
- Defines GZL of the lateral incisor relative to the central–canine zenith tangent line.
- Supplies the widely quoted 1 mm / 0.4 mm / centred / 1 mm-coronal reference values.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 20 healthy patients, 240 sites; mean age 27.7 y.
- Inclusion: no periodontal disease, recession, or gingival hypertrophy; no papilla loss, spacing, crowding, restorations, or incisal attrition.
- GZP measured mediolaterally from VBM with calibrated digital calipers; GZL measured apicocoronally from a tangent line drawn between adjacent GZPs on casts.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Central incisor: 100% distal GZP, mean 1 mm.
- Lateral incisor: mean deviation 0.4 mm.
- Canine: 97.5% centred along the long axis.
- Lateral incisor GZL: ~1 mm coronal to the central–canine zenith line.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest; SDs and per-tooth distributions not retrieved.
- Small (n=20), young, ideal-dentition sample — limited generalizability to older or restored dentitions.
- Cast-based caliper measurement; examiner calibration not reported in the abstract.

## 6. Related Work

- Martins 2024/2025 SR+MA pooled later cross-sectional studies and reproduced the same distal gradient (very-low certainty).

## 7. Glossary

- **GZP**: gingival zenith position (mesiodistal, from VBM).
- **GZL**: gingival zenith level (apicocoronal, of the lateral incisor relative to the zenith line).
- **VBM**: vertical bisected midline of the clinical crown.
