## Why Ingested

2026-09-29 흡연과 조기 임플란트 실패(early implant failure) 서베일런스에서 확보. 흡연 메타분석 [[implants/survival/fan-2024-smoking-early-implant-failure-sr-ma]]과 단일센터 위험인자 코호트 [[implants/survival/yari-2023-risk-factors-early-implant-failure]]에 대해, 3개 전문센터 1,875명 규모에서 흡연·전신질환·알레르기를 함께 보정한 다변량 추정치를 추가한다. 특히 흡연이 조기 실패에서는 비유의하고 조기 합병증에서만 유의했다는 점이 핵심.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective multicenter cohort (3 Swedish specialist centers; 1,875 patients, 4,670 implants; 2007 and 2017 cohorts): patient-level multivariable logistic regression of early (first-year) implant failure and complications.

Early failure was independently associated with exposed implant threads (OR 3.47), number of implants per patient (OR 1.27 per implant) and food allergy (OR 2.64); smoking was not significant for failure (OR 1.61, 95% CI 0.88-2.94, p=0.12) but was for early complications (OR 2.30, 95% CI 1.61-3.29).

Diabetes, cardiovascular and autoimmune disease were not associated with failure; interpretation is limited by retrospective self-reported data, binary disease coding and substantial missing bone-status data.

## 세줄요약

후향적 다기관 코호트 (스웨덴 전문센터 3곳; 환자 1,875명, 임플란트 4,670개; 2007·2017 코호트): 환자 단위 다변량 로지스틱 회귀로 1년 이내 조기 실패·합병증 위험인자 분석.

조기 실패는 노출된 임플란트 나사선 (OR 3.47), 환자당 식립 임플란트 수 (임플란트 1개당 OR 1.27), 음식 알레르기 (OR 2.64)와 독립적으로 연관; 흡연은 조기 실패에서 비유의 (OR 1.61, 95% CI 0.88-2.94, p=0.12)였고 조기 합병증에서만 유의 (OR 2.30, 95% CI 1.61-3.29).

당뇨·심혈관·자가면역질환은 실패와 무관했으나, 후향적 자기보고 자료·이분화된 질환 변수·많은 골상태 결측으로 해석에 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Patient-level multivariable analysis in ~1,900 patients across two treatment years (2007, 2017), adding disease and allergy variables (cardiovascular, diabetes, autoimmune, food/antibiotic/metal allergy) to the Part I variable set.
- Separates early failure from early complications; smoking predicts only the latter.
- Reports food allergy as a failure factor (OR 2.64), extending the case-control observation of Olsson Malm et al. cited by the authors.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective cohort, three specialist centers, Västra Götaland, Sweden; cohorts treated in 2007 and 2017.
- **Definition**: early implant failures and complications = within the first year.
- **Sample**: 1,949 treated, 74 excluded (3.8% in text; abstract states 3.7%; reports not found 60, wrong year 14) plus 2 zygoma implants; 1,875 patients / 4,670 implants analysed (2007: 799 pts / 2,473 implants; 2017: 1,076 pts / 2,287 implants).
- **Statistics**: SPSS 28; Fisher's exact/permutation tests between cohorts; univariate screen p<0.20; multivariable logistic regression p<0.05; all inference at patient level, implant-level data descriptive.
- **Failure model covariates** (Table 3): diseases, smoking, bone quality, implants per patient, bone augmentation, exposed threads, sinus perforation, diabetes, food allergy, antibiotic allergy, cardiovascular disease.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Early failure, patient level: 23 (2.9%) in 2007 vs 40 (3.7%) in 2017 (p>0.30); implant level 26 (1.1%) vs 56 (2.4%) (p<0.001).
- Early complications: 56 (7.0%) vs 145 (13.5%) patients (p<0.001).
- Smoking prevalence 15.4% (2007) vs 14.6% (2017).

Multivariable ORs for early failure (95% CI; p):

| Variable | OR | 95% CI | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exposed threads | 3.47 | 1.56-7.71 | 0.0023 |
| Number of implants (per implant) | 1.27 | 1.15-1.41 | <0.001 |
| Food allergy | 2.64 | 1.15-6.06 | 0.022 |
| Smoking | 1.61 | 0.88-2.94 | 0.12 |
| Diseases (any) | 1.46 | 0.84-2.52 | 0.18 |
| Bone quality (per unit) | 1.50 | 0.91-2.47 | 0.12 |
| Bone augmentation | 1.25 | 0.63-2.48 | >0.30 |
| Sinus perforation | 3.41 | 0.68-16.98 | 0.13 |
| Diabetes | 1.84 | 0.81-4.19 | 0.15 |
| Antibiotic allergy | 1.33 | 0.64-2.77 | 0.45 |
| Cardiovascular disease | 1.31 | 0.76-2.25 | 0.34 |

Early complications: smoking 2.30 (1.61-3.29); no preoperative antibiotics 1.92 (1.33-2.78); implants per patient 1.21 (1.13-1.30); exposed threads 4.46 (2.59-7.69); sinus perforation 8.19 (2.45-27.35); food allergy 1.92 (1.08-3.40); metal allergy 2.45 (1.08-5.57); all p<0.05.

Jaw/region (maxilla vs mandible; incisor/premolar/molar) not significant for failure or complications (p>=0.25). Implant-level failure 2.0% maxilla vs 1.3% mandible (descriptive).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, self-reported history and surgeon documentation; disease severity not captured; diseases dichotomized.
- Bone quality, bone volume and primary stability incompletely reported (missing 20.7-57.7% by variable and cohort).
- 2007 documentation less detailed, so complications may be underestimated in 2007.
- Sinus perforation CI very wide (few events). Only 63 patients with failure limits the failure model; the smoking CI for failure includes 1.
- Text says 3.8% excluded, abstract 3.7%; text prose labels the 3.47 OR as "bone perforations and exposed implant threads" while Table 3 lists exposed threads only (table value used). Text p-values for molar/incisor region printed as ">0.001" (typo; interpreted as <0.001).

## 7. Glossary

- **Early implant failure**: implant loss within the first year (this study's definition).
- **Exposed implant threads**: implant threads not covered by bone at placement (dehiscence/fenestration).
- **Early complications**: first-year complications not necessarily causing implant loss.
- **Patient-level analysis**: unit of analysis is the patient (any failed implant), accounting for clustering.
