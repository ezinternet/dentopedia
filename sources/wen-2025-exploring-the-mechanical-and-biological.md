## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Int J Oral Sci 2025) integrating periodontal ligament (PDL) macro- and micro-structure, mechanical properties, and mechanotransduction under orthodontic and occlusal loading.

PDL is heterogeneous (collar vs furcation region, buccal vs lingual, cervical/middle/apical), viscoelastic/poroelastic, and its properties depend on tooth position, loading frequency, and temperature; compression mainly drives osteoclast differentiation while tension drives osteogenesis via YAP, PIEZO1, TRPV4, ncRNA and PI3K/AKT pathways.

The authors argue structure, mechanics, and mechanotransduction form one interdependent network, and that ECM microstructure effects on cell fate remain under-studied.

## 세줄요약

치주인대 (Periodontal Ligament, PDL)의 거시·미세 구조, 기계적 특성, 기계신호전달 (Mechanotransduction)을 통합한 내러티브 리뷰 (Int J Oral Sci 2025).

PDL은 부위(치경부 collar vs 근분지부, 협측 vs 설측, 치경부·중간·근단)별로 이질적이고 점탄성·다공탄성을 보이며, 압박력은 주로 파골세포 분화를, 인장력은 골형성 분화를 YAP·PIEZO1·TRPV4·비코딩 RNA·PI3K/AKT 경로로 유도한다.

구조-역학-기계신호전달이 상호의존적 네트워크를 이룬다고 정리하며, 세포외기질 (Extracellular Matrix, ECM) 미세구조가 세포 운명에 미치는 영향은 추가 연구가 필요하다고 결론.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Summarizes macrostructure (PDL width 0.15–0.38 mm, narrowest in the middle third of the root; fiber groups: dentinogingival, transseptal, alveolodental subgroups) and microstructure (multi-branched fiber networks on FIB/SEM tomography; collar region rich in type VI collagen and LOX).
- Reviews mechanical properties and what modifies them (region, tooth position, age in rats, loading type/frequency, temperature) and constitutive models (linear elastic to visco-hyperelastic, porous hyper-elastic, damage models) plus FEA.
- Tabulates mechanotransduction mechanisms of PDL cells under compression and tension (Table 2), and argues for an integrated structure-mechanics-mechanotransduction view.

## 3. Structure and Composition
- Collagen type I dominant; human PDL fibers/fibrils ~3.1 um and 90 nm, rat 1.7 um and 70 nm; Sharpey's fibers 1-2 um diameter.
- Compression of PDL vessels on the compressive side causes local hypoxia and bone resorption.
- Under load the PDL space narrows macroscopically, but microscopically fibers are stretched rather than compressed (reduced fluid phase).
- A narrowed PDL space disrupts the gradual property transition at the interfaces and raises the elastic modulus, which may create a new load-bearing site and potentially bone-cementum fusion.

## 4. Mechanical Properties
- Collar region: stiffer and more viscoelastic under low-frequency loading; furcation region: lower stiffness and compression modulus, higher poroelasticity under high-frequency loading.
- Buccal PDL modulus higher than lingual; middle third stiffer/more viscous than gingival third; central-incisor storage/loss moduli about twice those of lateral incisors under compression.
- Loss factor 0.04-0.40 in compression vs 0.04-0.08 in tension; loss factor higher at 37 C than 25 C; storage modulus rises with frequency (plateau reported between 5 and 10 Hz in one study).
- Elastic modulus about 9.64 x 10^-4 GPa quoted from FEA/experimental studies; FEA accuracy depends on Poisson's ratio and tension-compression asymmetry.
- Many data come from rodent, porcine, or cadaver samples; species differences flagged by the authors.

## 5. Mechanotransduction (Table 2 and text)
- Compression: reduced proliferation (MIR31HG downregulation); YAP promotes osteogenesis under intermittent but osteoclastogenesis (via GDF15) under static compression; PIEZO1 in osteoclastogenesis/apoptosis; TRPV4 activation in PDLSCs; M1 macrophage polarization via H2S/STAT1, PDLSC autophagy/AKT, and YAP-TEAD; ANXA3 exosomes promote osteoclast differentiation via ERK; excessive compression linked to IL-6 and Th17 polarization with root resorption risk.
- Tension: effects depend on magnitude, frequency, and duration; lower tensile forces favor osteogenesis; 0.1-0.7 Hz increasing frequency enhances PDLSC osteogenesis (EYA1, SALL1); downregulation of miR-34a/miR-146a (CELF3), lncRNA SNHG8/EZH2, miR-129-5p/BMP2-Smad; Nrf2, PI3K/AKT, ROCK-TAZ, YAP/WNT5A/FZD4, PDGF-BB/JAK2/STAT3, autophagy, PIEZO1.
- 3D culture, nanofiber/porous scaffolds, and nano-grooves elicit stronger or more osteogenic responses than 2D/microstructures.

## Limitations
- Narrative review; much of the mechanotransduction evidence is rodent or in vitro (Table 2).
- Mechanical-property values come from heterogeneous methods, species, and samples.
- Conclusion states pathways remain complex and not fully understood.
