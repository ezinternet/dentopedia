## Three-line Summary
3D finite element analysis (BMC Oral Health 2025, DOI 10.1186/s12903-025-07122-8) comparing stress distribution in cortical bone, implant, and components across two internal connection geometries (S = star tube-in-tube; H = Morse + hex) and four abutment materials under axial (200 N) and oblique (100 N, 45°) loading.

Stress concentrated in crestal cortical bone and was greater under oblique than axial loading; titanium abutments consistently generated the lowest stress values; the highest oblique cortical stress was 99.06 MPa (H Co-Cr) and peak implant von Mises stress 135 MPa (S Co-Cr oblique).

Titanium abutments are the most stress-favorable material across both connection geometries; oblique loading is the clinically relevant worst-case scenario — however FEA assumptions (linear elasticity, idealized geometry, no microgaps, single bone model) limit direct clinical extrapolation.

## 세줄요약
3D 유한요소분석(BMC Oral Health 2025): 2가지 내부연결 기하구조(S=스타 튜브인튜브; H=모르스+육각)와 4가지 지대주 재료를 축방향(200 N) 및 사방향(100 N, 45°) 하중에서 피질골·임플란트·구성요소 응력분포 비교.

응력은 치조정 피질골에 집중되고 사방향이 축방향보다 큰 응력 유발; 티타늄 지대주가 두 연결형 모두에서 가장 낮은 응력 — 최고 사방향 피질골 응력 99.06 MPa(H Co-Cr), 최고 임플란트 von Mises 응력 135 MPa(S Co-Cr 사방향).

티타늄 지대주가 응력 분산 측면에서 가장 유리하며, 임상적으로 편심 하중(사방향)이 더 큰 위험을 초래 — 단 FEA의 선형탄성·이상화 가정으로 임상 직접 적용에는 주의가 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
Provides FEA evidence ranking abutment-material × connection combinations for implant-supported single crowns, useful when prosthetic material is a free design parameter.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
3D FEA. Two implant-abutment connections (S = star tube-in-tube; H = Morse + hex). Four abutment materials. Axial 200 N and oblique 100 N @45° loads. Principal stresses and von Mises analyzed.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Stresses concentrated in crestal cortical bone. Oblique loading > axial. Highest oblique stress 99.06 MPa (H Co-Cr). Titanium abutments consistently lowest stress. Peak implant von Mises 135 MPa (S Co-Cr oblique).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
FEA assumptions (linear elasticity, idealized geometry, no microgaps); single bone-model. Not a clinical outcome study.
