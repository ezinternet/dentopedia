## Why Ingested

This SR and evidence map provides critical context for the [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/charoenrat-2025-clear-aligner-anterior-open-bite-molar-intrusion-sr-ma|charoenrat 2025 efficacy SR]] by explicitly mapping the *mechanism* (dentoalveolar vs. skeletal) behind the ~3 mm overbite improvement reported in adult anterior open bite treated with clear aligners. It adds GRADE certainty appraisal (very low across all outcomes) and a descriptive evidence map classifying dentoskeletal changes as direct, indirect, or insufficient — establishing that current evidence supports dentoalveolar compensation but not predictable skeletal correction. Essential for the [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/clear-aligner|clear aligner overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Ten retrospective studies (n=421) of clear aligner therapy for adult anterior open bite yielded a pooled overbite increase of 3.07 mm (primary, 5 studies) and 2.89 mm (extended sensitivity, 7 studies), both with substantial heterogeneity (I²=93–96%) and very low GRADE certainty. Reported dentoskeletal changes were dominated by anterior dentoalveolar compensation (incisor extrusion/retroclination) with variable, protocol-dependent posterior vertical change; predictable skeletal correction was not established. Nine of ten studies had serious risk of bias (ROBINS-I), and comparative evidence showed clear aligner therapy and skeletal anchorage-based posterior intrusion operate via different mechanisms.

## 세줄요약

성인 전방 개방 교합 (Anterior Open Bite, AOB) 환자 421명을 다룬 10개 후향적 연구를 종합한 결과, 투명교정 (Clear Aligner Therapy, CAT) 후 과개교합 개선량 (Overbite Improvement)은 주요 분석 3.07 mm, 민감도 분석 2.89 mm였으나 이질성이 매우 높고 (I²=93–96%) GRADE 근거 확실성은 전 지표에서 매우 낮음이었다. 치아치조 변화 (Dentoalveolar Changes) — 특히 절치 돌출 (Incisor Extrusion) 및 후방 경사 — 가 가장 일관되게 보고되었으며, 예측 가능한 골격 교정 (Skeletal Correction)은 입증되지 않았다. 골격 정박 (Skeletal Anchorage) 기반 구치부 압하 (Posterior Intrusion) 프로토콜과 투명교정은 기전적으로 동등하지 않으므로 임상 적용 시 구분이 필요하다.

## Key Findings

### Quantitative synthesis
- **Primary analysis** (5 completed-treatment cohorts, n=206): pooled overbite increase **3.07 mm** (95% CI 2.46–3.69 mm), I²=93%
- **Extended sensitivity** (7 studies, n=322): **2.89 mm** (95% CI 2.21–3.57 mm), I²=96%
- Range across individual studies: 1.50 mm (Burashed 2023, attachment-protocol cohort) to 4.26 mm (Arwa 2025, CBCT-based)

### Descriptive evidence map — dentoskeletal changes
- **Anterior dentoalveolar** (most consistent): incisor extrusion and/or retroclination across multiple studies; however, extrusion is recognized as one of the less predictable aligner movements
- **Posterior vertical** (variable): some molar intrusion reported (Moshiri, Harris, Arwa); Chamberland showed TAD-based protocol produced more intrusion than CAT; Garnett showed no significant posterior change
- **Mandibular autorotation** (secondary, limited): reported in subset as consequence of anterior/posterior dentoalveolar change; small magnitude without skeletal anchorage
- **Mandibular plane angle**: reduction in some studies (especially skeletal AOB phenotypes, Suh 2022); tempered by cephalometric superimposition variability

### GRADE certainty — all outcomes: Very Low
Main downgrade reasons: serious risk of bias, substantial heterogeneity, indirectness, imprecision

### Comparative findings
- Steele 2022 (vs miniplate): CAT achieved dentoalveolar correction; miniplate produced more direct posterior intrusion
- Chamberland & Nataf 2024 (vs TAD double-arch): TAD group showed greater mandibular counterclockwise rotation
- Withayanukonkij 2023 RCT (vs miniscrew intrusion, 6-mo): different mechanisms confirmed
- **Conclusion**: CAT and skeletal anchorage-based protocols are NOT mechanistically equivalent

## Methodology Details

- Search: PubMed, EMBASE, Scopus, Cochrane Library (Jan 2010–Apr 2026)
- PRISMA compliance, PROSPERO registered
- Risk of bias: ROBINS-I (9 nonrandomized); RoB 2 (1 RCT) → 9 serious, 1 some concerns
- Evidence map classification: direct / indirect / insufficient for mechanistic interpretation
- Random-effects inverse-variance meta-analysis; heterogeneity via tau², chi², I²
- GRADE appraisal for 5 key outcomes

## Clinical Implications

1. ~3 mm overbite improvement in selected adult AOB cases is plausible but should be interpreted as a descriptive estimate, not a reliable individual prediction
2. Anterior dentoalveolar compensation is the primary pathway; programmed extrusion may not be fully expressed in all patients
3. Posterior vertical control is possible but protocol-dependent — should not be treated as equivalent to skeletal anchorage-based intrusion
4. Case selection is critical: mild-to-moderate AOB → CAT dentoalveolar camouflage may suffice; severe skeletal AOB → consider TAD/orthognathic surgery
5. Future studies need: prospective comparative designs, standardized phenotyping, component-level outcomes, blinded cephalometric/3D evaluation, long-term retention endpoints
