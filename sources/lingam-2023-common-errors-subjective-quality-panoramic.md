## Why Ingested

기존 wiki의 ghost image 페이지들([[radiology/kim-2024-real-ghost-pseudo-ghost-images-panoramic]], [[radiology/ramos-2016-ghost-images-metal-objects-panoramic]])은 ghost image의 형성 기전과 개별 증례(case-report)에 집중돼 있어, "실제 임상 코호트에서 ghost image / artifact가 얼마나 흔한가"에 대한 유병률 근거가 없었다. 본 2,629장 cross-sectional 연구는 ghost image(error 10)를 10개 positioning error 중 하나로 정량화해(전체 2.2%, 소아 5–12세에서 유의하게 높음, P=0.001) 기존 기전·증례 페이지를 유병률로 보강한다. 파노라마 판독 시 ghost/artifact를 실제 병변으로 오인하지 않도록 하는 실무 근거.

## One-line Summary

Cross-sectional audit of 2,629 digital panoramic radiographs (Orthophos XG, Riyadh): 77.2% had ≥1 positioning error, only 32.8% error-free; ghost/reflected images (error 10) were the least frequent error overall (~2.2%) but significantly concentrated in 5–12-year-old children (P=0.001), while ~15% of images were diagnostically unacceptable (exceeding the ≤10% benchmark).

## 한줄요약

디지털 파노라마 2,629장 단면 감사(사우디 리야드): 77.2%가 자세오류 1개 이상, 무오류는 32.8%뿐이며, ghost/reflected image(오류 10)는 전체적으로 가장 드물지만(~2.2%) 5–12세 소아에서 유의하게 몰림(P=0.001), 진단 불가 영상은 ~15%로 권고 기준(≤10%)을 초과.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies the frequency of 10 categorized panoramic positioning errors in a large real-world cohort (n=2,629), including ghost/reflected image artifacts.
- Provides a rare **prevalence** figure for ghost images (error 10 ≈ 2.2% overall), complementing the mechanism/case-report literature.
- Shows ghost images are **age-dependent** — significantly more common in 5–12-year-old children (P=0.001), plausibly linked to thyroid-collar / lead-apron and accessory positioning (Figure 1: thyroid collar ghost).
- Documents that ~15% of images were diagnostically unacceptable — above the ≤10% quality benchmark — with excellent images well below the ≥70% target.
- Near-perfect interexaminer agreement (kappa = 0.998) across two experienced radiologists.

## 3. Methodology

- 2,629 orthopantomograms selected from 3,900 new patients (Aug 2020 – Jun 2021), all ages (pediatric 5–12 to elderly >60).
- Excluded: developmental abnormality, maxillofacial fracture, orthognathic surgery, tumors/cysts. First image only when repeats existed.
- Two technicians (matched 8-yr experience); two radiology specialists (>15 yr) evaluated on monitor.
- Errors categorized into 10 types (Table 1). Quality graded on a 3-point NRPB-consistent scale: Excellent / Diagnostically acceptable / Unacceptable.
- Interobserver reliability re-tested after 1 month; kappa statistic; SPSS v25.0, frequency test.

## 4. Key Results

- **Error-free (excellent): 32.8%** (863/2,629); **≥1 error: 77.2%** (1,766).
- **Most common error: Error 8** — tongue not against palate → radiolucent airspace obscuring maxillary roots (avg 17.9%; cf. AL-Faleh 81.8%).
- **Least common error: Error 10 (ghost/reflected images)** — avg **2.2%**.
- **Ghost mechanism (as stated)**: object/anatomic structure between the X-ray source and the center of rotation, dense enough to attenuate the beam → magnified reflected image on the contralateral side; jewelry, hair clips, lead apron, thyroid collar, and bony structures are typical causes; unilateral jewelry can be mistaken for radio-opaque disease.
- **Age association (significant)**: Error 3 P=0.024, Error 8 P=0.027, **Error 10 P=0.001** (ghost concentrated in 5–12-year-olds, ~48% of ghost cases). Errors 1–9 predominate in 19–59-year-olds.
- **Quality**: Excellent 32.1% · Diagnostically acceptable 49.05% · **Diagnostically unacceptable 15.08%** (benchmark ≤10%).
- **Reliability**: kappa = 0.998. No gender difference.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single institution, single device (Orthophos XG); Riyadh cohort — external validity limited.
- Patient clinical condition (swelling, facial asymmetry, physical constraints) not accounted for; unavoidable positioning errors inflate the "unacceptable" percentage.
- Ghost images quantified only as an error tally, not by object type or diagnostic-consequence severity.
- Only positioning/artifact errors; no dose or focal-trough (magnification) measurement.

## 7. Glossary

- **Orthopantomogram (OPG) / Panoramic radiograph**: tomographic image of the full dentition and jaws using a moving X-ray source and detector, displaying objects within a focal trough (image layer).
- **Ghost image**: a reflected, magnified, blurred artifact projected on the contralateral side, formed when a radiopaque object lies between the X-ray source and the machine's center of rotation.
- **Focal trough / image layer**: the 3-D zone of sharp focus; objects outside it are distorted, magnified, or ghosted.
- **Center of rotation**: the axis of the rotating source–detector assembly; object position relative to it governs ghost-image formation.
- **Diagnostically unacceptable**: NRPB quality grade in which errors preclude reliable diagnostic use; target ≤10% of images.
- **Kappa (κ)**: chance-corrected inter-rater agreement statistic (0.998 here = near-perfect).
