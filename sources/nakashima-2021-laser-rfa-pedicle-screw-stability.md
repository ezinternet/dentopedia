## Why Ingested
레이저 기반 RFA 시스템을 척추 나사못(pedicle screw)에 적용해 ISQ값·삽입 토크·pull-out force와의 상관관계를 정량적으로 보여준 in-vitro/cadaveric 연구다. [[overviews/rfa-isq-measurement-mechanism]] 및 [[implants/isq/debruyne-2017-isq-laser-vibrometry-resonance-frequency]] 와 함께 RFA 물리적 기반을 보강한다; 레이저 비접촉 RFA → ISQ 선형 상관(R=0.981)이 핵심 기여.

## One-line Summary
Cadaveric and model bone study establishing laser-based RFA as a non-contact quantitative method for pedicle screw fixation, with RF correlating linearly with ISQ (R=0.981) and logarithmically with pull-out force (R=0.931).

## 한줄요약
척추 나사못 고정력 평가용 레이저 공진주파수 분석(RFA) 시스템 개발; 모형골에서 공진주파수(RF)와 ISQ 선형 상관 R=0.981, pull-out force와 R=0.931 확인.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Established first laser-based RFA system for non-contact, high-speed pedicle screw stability assessment
- Demonstrated linear correlation between RF and ISQ (R=0.981, p<0.001) in model bone
- Demonstrated logarithmic correlation between RF and pull-out force (R=0.931) and peak torque (R=0.931) in model bone
- Showed cadaveric vertebrae correlations are weaker but still significant (R=0.716–0.811 for peak torque)
- Extended RFA application from dental implants to orthopedic spine surgery

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: In-vitro (model bone) + cadaveric (human vertebrae)
- Pedicle screw inserted into human cadaver vertebral pedicles or polyurethane model bone
- Laser RFA: non-contact vibration induction and measurement via laser Doppler vibrometry
- Conventional comparisons: pull-out force (POF), peak insertion torque, Osstell ISQ (magnetic dental RFA)
- Correlation analysis: best-fit linear and logarithmic approximations
- Cadaveric n: not specified in extracted text; model bone n: not specified

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Model bone study:**
- RF vs peak torque: R=0.931, p<0.001 (logarithmic)
- RF vs POF: R=0.931, p<0.001 (logarithmic)
- RF vs ISQ: R=0.981, p<0.001 (linear)

**Cadaveric vertebrae study:**
- RF vs peak torque: logarithmic R=0.716, linear R=0.811, p<0.001
- RF vs POF: logarithmic R=0.644, linear R=0.548, p<0.05

**ISQ–RF relationship (referenced):**
- ISQ=0 → RF ~3000 Hz; ISQ=100 → RF ~8000 Hz

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Cadaveric correlations weaker than model bone (R=0.548–0.811 vs 0.931–0.981)
- Model bone does not fully replicate biological bone variability
- No clinical in-vivo data — intraoperative validation pending
- Laser system not yet commercially available for dental/orthopedic use
- Pedicle screw geometry differs from dental implant geometry; direct numerical translation requires caution

## 6. Related Work
- Osstell ISQ (Osstell, Integration Diagnostics) uses magnetic pulse RFA for dental implants
- Dental RFA range: ~3000–8000 Hz corresponding to ISQ 0–100
- Prior dental RFA literature: pull-out force / insertion torque correlations established in dental implants

## 7. Glossary
- Laser RFA: non-contact resonance frequency analysis using laser to induce and detect vibration
- Pedicle screw fixation (PSF): spinal instrumentation surgery screw anchored to vertebral pedicle
- POF (Pull-out Force): destructive measure of screw fixation strength
- RF (Resonance Frequency): natural vibration frequency of an implant-bone complex
- ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient): proprietary Osstell 0–100 scale derived from RF
