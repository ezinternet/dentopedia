## Why Ingested

Peri-implantitis prevalence evidence in the wiki is built almost entirely on titanium cohorts ([[implants/peri-implantitis/diaz-2022-what-is-the-prevalence]], [[implants/peri-implantitis/reis-2025-peri-implant-disease-prevalence-world-workshop-criteria]]), while zirconia implant pages ([[implants/surface/mohseni-2024-clinical-outcomes-zirconia-implants]]) cover survival and marginal bone loss but not peri-implantitis as the primary outcome. This is the first dedicated SR+MA on peri-implantitis prevalence around zirconia implants, filling that gap from the 2026-10 peri-implantitis surveillance batch.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD420251273821; RCTs and cohort studies; searches to February 2026; RoB 2 / ROBINS-I) of 19 studies (687 patients, 995 implants: 891 zirconia implants, 104 titanium implants; mean follow-up about 49 months) estimating implant-level peri-implantitis prevalence around zirconia implants (ZIs) and comparing ZIs with titanium implants (TIs).

Pooled implant-level prevalence around ZIs was 2% (95% CI 0%-6%; I2 = 79.3%) at follow-up <=3 years, 2% (0%-8%; I2 = 38.9%) at >3 to 5 years, and 8% (0%-23%; I2 = 82.1%) at >5 years (study-level range 0%-33.3%); five RCTs (109 ZIs, 106 TIs) showed no significant ZI-vs-TI difference (RR 1.66; 95% CI 0.48-5.71; p = 0.42; I2 = 0%).

Low short- to mid-term prevalence with a rising trend after 5 years, but GRADE certainty is low to very low (heterogeneous peri-implantitis definitions, wide prediction intervals up to 0%-67% beyond 5 years, few RCT events), so the non-significant RCT result is not evidence of equivalence.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (PROSPERO CRD420251273821; RCT·코호트; 2026년 2월까지 검색; RoB 2 / ROBINS-I) — 19편 (환자 687명, 임플란트 995개: 지르코니아 임플란트 891개, 티타늄 임플란트 104개; 평균 추적 약 49개월)으로 지르코니아 임플란트 (Zirconia Implant, ZI) 주위 임플란트 수준 임플란트 주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 유병률을 추정하고 티타늄 임플란트 (Titanium Implant, TI)와 비교.

ZI의 통합 임플란트 수준 유병률: 추적 3년 이하 2% (95% CI 0%-6%; I2 = 79.3%), 3-5년 2% (0%-8%; I2 = 38.9%), 5년 초과 8% (0%-23%; I2 = 82.1%) (개별 연구 범위 0%-33.3%); RCT 5편 (ZI 109개, TI 106개)에서 ZI와 TI 간 유의차 없음 (RR 1.66; 95% CI 0.48-5.71; p = 0.42; I2 = 0%).

단기~중기 유병률은 낮고 5년 이후 증가 경향이나, GRADE 근거 확실성은 낮음~매우 낮음 (진단 정의 이질성, 5년 초과 예측구간 0%-67%, RCT 사건 수 적음) — RCT의 비유의 결과를 동등성의 근거로 읽어서는 안 된다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First dedicated quantitative synthesis of implant-level peri-implantitis prevalence around zirconia implants, stratified by follow-up duration.
- Pooled RCT comparison with titanium implants (5 RCTs, 109 ZIs vs 106 TIs) showing no significant difference.
- Explicit caution that pooled estimates are not definitive epidemiological figures because case definitions, follow-up, and maintenance protocols vary.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA, PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD420251273821 registered before study selection; random-effects (generic inverse variance) with cluster adjustment (ICC 0.07), prediction intervals, leave-one-out and critical-risk-of-bias exclusion sensitivity analyses.
- **Databases**: PubMed/MEDLINE, Cochrane CENTRAL, Embase, Web of Science to February 2026; OpenGrey and ProQuest gray literature.
- **n**: 19 studies (2015-2025); 687 patients; 995 implants (891 ZIs, 104 TIs); 5 RCTs, 12 prospective cohorts, 2 retrospective; 14 of 19 one-piece, 4 two-piece, 1 both; follow-up 12 to 144 months (mean about 49).
- **Eligibility**: ZIs in function at least 12 months; no uniform PD or MBL cutoff imposed; studies restricted to high-risk populations excluded.
- **Outcomes**: Implant-level peri-implantitis prevalence by follow-up (FQ-1); ZI vs TI (FQ-2); PD, BOP, PI, MBL descriptively (FQ-3).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Pooled prevalence: <=3 years 2% (95% CI 0%-6%; prediction interval 0%-22%; I2 = 79.3%); >3 to 5 years 2% (0%-8%; PI 0%-19%; I2 = 38.9%); >5 years 8% (0%-23%; PI 0%-67%; I2 = 82.1%).
- Individual study implant-level prevalence ranged 0% to 33.3% (highest: a 120-month prospective cohort, 33.3%).
- ZI vs TI, 5 RCTs: RR 1.66 (95% CI 0.48-5.71; p = 0.42; I2 = 0%; prediction interval 0.11-25).
- Descriptive means around ZIs: PD 2.98 mm (range 1.7-3.94), BOP 28.4% (0.2%-89.6%), MBL 1.28 mm (0-3.09), PI 23.9% (0%-54.6%).
- Patient-level prevalence not meta-analyzed (inconsistent reporting).
- Risk of bias: RCTs "some concerns"; most observational studies moderate; 4 studies critical. Excluding critical-risk studies gave comparable estimates without reducing heterogeneity. Egger intercept 2.49 (95% CI -0.58 to 5.55; p = 0.13). GRADE low to very low.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Heterogeneous peri-implantitis definitions and older success criteria; sensitivity analysis by diagnostic criteria not feasible.
- Mostly one-piece ZIs, mostly single crowns; overdentures and prosthetic design not assessable; mucositis poorly reported.
- Only 104 titanium implants in the dataset; RCT comparison has few events and wide CIs, so non-significance is not equivalence.
- Future: long-term comparative studies with standardized definitions, patient- and implant-level reporting, and described maintenance protocols.

## 6. Related Work
- Diaz 2022 and Reis 2025: titanium-dominated overall prevalence anchors (patient level about 19.5% / 25.0%; implant level 12.53% / 18.0%) in different populations and definitions, so not directly comparable.
- Mohseni 2024: ZI survival and MBL SR+MA that this paper extends to peri-implantitis.

## 7. Glossary
- **ZI / TI**: zirconia implant / titanium implant.
- **Prediction interval**: expected range of true effect in a new study; much wider than the CI here.
- **ICC**: intraclass correlation coefficient used for cluster adjustment (0.07).
