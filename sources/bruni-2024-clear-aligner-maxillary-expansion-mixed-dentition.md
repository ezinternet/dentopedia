## Why Ingested

기존 [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/de-la-rosa-gay-2025-expansion-predictability-clear-aligner]] 및 [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/fonseca-planells-2026-clear-aligner-maxillary-expansion-growing]]가 성장기·전반적 투명교정 확장 예측성을 다루지만, **혼합치열(mixed dentition, 유치+영구치 혼재)** 특정 집단에서 상악 치조성 확장(dentoalveolar expansion)의 tooth-region별 정확도를 정량화한 prospective 근거가 부족했다. 본 Korean Journal of Orthodontics 논문(Bruni 2024, n=40, 평균 8.7세)은 유견치·유구치·영구대구치 각 구간의 cusp/gingival level 정확도(87.7%→67.9%)와 회귀 estimate(계획 1 mm당 임상 0.58–0.72 mm 발현)를 제시해, 소아 조기개입 확장 시 overcorrection 계획의 정량 근거를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective interventional cohort (n=40 mixed-dentition children, mean age 8.7y, transverse discrepancy ≤5 mm) treated with Invisalign First evaluating maxillary dentoalveolar expansion accuracy vs the ClinCheck virtual plan, region-stratified (deciduous canine / first deciduous molar / permanent first molar) at cusp and gingival levels.

Expansion accuracy decreased anterior→posterior: inter-canine 87.7% (cusp) / 82.7% (gingival), inter-deciduous-molar 84.9% / 80.5%, inter-permanent-molar 77.8% / 67.9%; regression estimate ≈0.58–0.72 mm clinical expression per 1 mm planned (overall 80.2% of target).

Clear aligners give predictable dentoalveolar (not orthopedic/sutural) maxillary expansion in growing children, but molar expansion is largely buccal crown tipping — plan overcorrection (≈7 mm ClinCheck to obtain 5 mm clinically at permanent molar) and avoid CAT when a true orthopedic effect is required.

## 세줄요약

혼합치열 소아 40명(평균 8.7세, 횡적 부조화 ≤5 mm)을 인비절라인 퍼스트로 치료한 전향적 코호트로, ClinCheck 가상계획 대비 상악 치조성 확장의 정확도를 유견치·제1유구치·제1대구치 구간별(교두/치은 레벨)로 비교했다.

확장 정확도는 전방→후방으로 감소(견치간 87.7%/82.7%, 유구치간 84.9%/80.5%, 대구치간 77.8%/67.9%); 회귀 estimate상 계획 1 mm당 임상 0.58–0.72 mm 발현(전체 목표의 80.2%).

투명교정은 성장기 소아에서 (봉합 열개가 아닌) 치조성 상악 확장을 예측가능하게 얻지만 대구치는 대부분 협측 치관경사(crown tipping)라 overcorrection이 필요(영구대구치 5 mm 임상확장 위해 ClinCheck 약 7 mm 계획)하고, 진정한 정형효과가 필요하면 CAT 대신 RME를 선택해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies **region-specific predictability** of clear-aligner maxillary dentoalveolar expansion in mixed dentition (not adults, not permanent dentition) — a gap in the CAT-expansion literature.
- Establishes a clear **anterior→posterior accuracy gradient** (87.7% canine cusp → 67.9% permanent-molar gingival).
- Provides a practical **overcorrection rule**: regression estimate ≈0.6 mm clinical per 1 mm planned → plan ~7 mm ClinCheck to achieve ~5 mm clinical at the permanent molar.
- Demonstrates that molar "expansion" with aligners is dominated by **buccal crown tipping**, not bodily translation (cusp-level accuracy > gingival-level accuracy, especially at molars).

## 3. Methodology

- **Sample**: 61 recruited → 40 analyzed (18 M, 22 F), mean age 8.72 ± 1.17 y; consecutive referrals, Sept 2020–Mar 2022.
- **Inclusion**: mixed dentition with transverse maxillary deficiency, inter-arch posterior discrepancy ≤ 5 mm.
- **Appliance**: Invisalign First Phase I (multilayer aromatic thermoplastic polyurethane/co-polyester; ~0.57–0.64 mm thick), attachments only — no IPR, no extractions.
- **Protocol**: ClinCheck sequential dentoalveolar expansion — permanent molars buccalized first (rest of arch as anchorage), then deciduous molars/canines; 0.15 mm extrusion + 2° extra buccal root torque per stage as overcorrection; weekly aligner change; checked every 4 weeks; mean 9 ± 1 months, 6 ± 2 visits.
- **Measurement**: T0 (initial IOS), T1/2 (virtual plan final stage), T1 (final IOS) STL files in Geomagic Control X; inter-dental distances at cusp and gingival (gingival zenith) levels — deciduous canine (anterior), first deciduous molar (middle), first permanent molar (posterior).
- **Statistics**: ICC for reproducibility (threshold >0.9); Student's t test (planned vs obtained, per tooth); multiple regression (estimate = mm clinical per mm planned); Kravitz percent-accuracy formula. Power: min n=22 for 80% power, 95% CI.

## 4. Key Results

- **Reproducibility**: ICC 0.90–0.95 (excellent).
- **Accuracy (planned vs achieved)**:

  | Region | Cusp level | Gingival level |
  |---|---|---|
  | Inter-deciduous canine | 87.7% | 82.7% |
  | Inter-first deciduous molar | 84.9% | 80.5% |
  | Inter-first permanent molar | 77.8% | 67.9% |

- **Regression estimate (mm clinical per 1 mm planned)**: canine 0.65 (cusp & gingival); deciduous molar 0.72 (cusp) / 0.58 (gingival); permanent molar 0.61 (cusp) / 0.66 (gingival).
- **Significance (planned vs obtained)**: significant differences at inter-canine gingival, inter-deciduous-molar cusp & gingival, and permanent-molar gingival; permanent-molar **cusp** expansion showed **no significant** difference from plan.
- **Overall**: mean dentoalveolar expansion = 80.2% of planned target; ~3.5 mm clinical at permanent molar and >4 mm at first deciduous molar achieved with a single aligner set.

## 5. Limitations

- **Patient compliance not objectively measured** (self-reported wear charts only), despite CAT being highly compliance-dependent.
- Arch-width data analyzed per tooth pair **in isolation** — adjacent-tooth tracking, rotation, torque, and vertical movements not accounted for, which affect aligner fit and expression.
- **No untreated control** and no long-term stability/relapse data (dentoalveolar expansion has variable retention).
- Operators highly experienced (>10 y) at two-operator single center → limited generalizability to average practice.
- Author-level affiliations are Italian (Turin); population is European mixed-dentition children — findings may not transfer directly to other craniofacial patterns.

## 7. Glossary

- **Maxillary transverse constriction (MTC)** — reduced transverse dimension of the maxilla; presents as posterior crossbite, crowding, dark buccal corridors.
- **Dentoalveolar expansion** — arch widening via tooth + alveolar-bone movement (crown tipping ± bodily), without opening the mid-palatal suture (contrast: orthopedic RME).
- **Rapid maxillary expansion (RME) / Slow maxillary expansion (SME)** — high-load sutural (orthopedic) vs low-load mostly dentoalveolar expansion approaches.
- **ClinCheck** — Align Technology's virtual treatment-planning software; represents the intended force system, not a guaranteed simulation of outcome.
- **Buccal root torque** — palatal-root/buccal-crown moment applied to convert tipping toward bodily movement; here 2° extra per stage as overcorrection.
- **Kravitz percent-accuracy formula** — standard method to express achieved-vs-predicted tooth movement as a percentage (≤100%).
