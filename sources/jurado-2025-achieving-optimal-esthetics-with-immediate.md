## Why Ingested

Held pages on immediate implants in periapically infected sites ([[immediate-implant/infected-socket/munoz-camara-2020-immediate-implants-acute-periapical-infected]]) and on soft-tissue grafting at immediate implants ([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/torra-moneny-2024-connective-tissue-grafts-immediate-implant-sr-ma]]) contain no worked clinical example of a combined immediate-implant + veneer smile-zone workflow. This case report supplies one. It was also checked for a live wiki question: it is NOT usable as evidence for immediate placement in acute purulent infection, because the paper documents only chronic abscesses and no pus at extraction.

## Three-line Summary

Single case report (n=1, 30-year-old woman, Biomimetics 2025): two non-restorable, endodontically treated maxillary central incisors with chronic periapical abscess were extracted atraumatically and flaplessly, the abscess cavities were curetted and irrigated with saline and chlorhexidine, and two immediate implants (3.5 x 11.5 mm) were placed with a 3D-printed guide plus a palatal autogenous soft tissue graft, followed by feldspathic laminate veneers on the other visible teeth.

Provisional restorations were recontoured at 10 weeks and replaced at 3 and 4 months; at the 3-year follow-up the patient was satisfied, no signs of peri-implant disease were noted and radiographs showed osseointegrated implants. No quantitative outcomes (bone level, PES, recession) are reported.

The paper documents no pus at surgery, no antibiotic regimen and no bone graft, so it cannot inform the acute-infection question; it is a technique illustration with n=1 and no comparator.

## 세줄요약

증례보고 1건 (n=1, 30세 여성, Biomimetics 2025): 근관치료·포스트가 있는 상악 중절치 2개(만성 치근단 농양, 수복 불가)를 비외상적·무판 발치하고, 농양강을 소파 후 생리식염수와 클로르헥시딘으로 세척한 뒤 3D 프린팅 가이드로 즉시 임플란트 2개(3.5 x 11.5 mm)를 식립, 구개 자가 연조직 이식을 병행하고 나머지 미소부위에는 장석질 라미네이트 비니어를 시행.

논문에는 발치 시 농(pus) 기록, 항생제 처방, 골이식이 모두 없어 급성 감염 즉시식립 질문에는 답할 수 없다; n=1·비교군 없는 술식 예시.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Describes a workflow combining two adjacent immediate central-incisor implants with feldspathic laminate veneers on the remaining smile-zone teeth (first molar to first molar), which the authors state has few clinical reports.
- Workflow elements: digital planning (CBCT + intraoral scan, Exocad), diagnostic wax-up and mock-up, atraumatic flapless extraction, guided implant placement, palatal autogenous soft tissue graft, screw-retained non-occluding provisionals used to shape the soft tissue, custom impression posts, zirconia-titanium-base custom abutments, bonding under dental dam.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Patient.** 30-year-old woman; chief complaint "some exudated tissue appearing in the upper anterior area" and dislike of her smile. History: endodontic treatment and crowns on both maxillary central incisors 5 years earlier; extraction of both maxillary first premolars followed by orthodontics 10 years earlier.

**Diagnosis.** Clinical, photographic, radiographic and CBCT evaluation: both maxillary incisors diagnosed with chronic periapical abscesses; grade II mobility; long metal posts; apical resorption; therefore non-restorable. Incisal wear of right canine, right lateral incisor and left canine; stained old composite on incisal edge of left lateral incisor. The abstract states the patient's complaint included "periapical infections with purulent exudate at the apex of her central incisors"; the Case Report section itself records "exudated tissue" and a diagnosis of "chronic periapical abscess". Neither section describes a fistula, swelling, pain, pus at extraction, or any systemic sign.

**Options offered.** Lateral-to-lateral fixed prosthesis, removable partial denture, implants; porcelain or composite veneers for the smile zone. Patient chose implants (immediate rather than two-stage, to reduce the number of surgeries) and porcelain veneers.

**Surgery.** Diagnostic scans, facebow, printed models, wax-up, mock-up; implant planned in Exocad and a surgical guide printed. Atraumatic extraction with periotome and forceps, vertical force only. Sockets cleaned with curettes; "the abscess cavity was irrigated with saline solution and chlorohexidine to flush out all the debris and blood." Two implants (Helix GM, Neodent, 3.5 mm x 11.50 mm) placed through the guide. An autogenous soft tissue graft harvested from the palate was positioned on the facial surface of the left-side implant with polyethylene sutures. GM temporary abutments with screw-retained non-occluding interim crowns. Not reported: antibiotic prophylaxis or therapy, timing of extraction relative to any infection episode, insertion torque or ISQ, socket-gap management or bone graft, buccal plate status.

**Provisionalization.** Follow-ups at 2, 4, 7, 10 weeks and 3 and 4 months; provisionals recontoured at week 10, new sets at 3 and 4 months; contour accepted at 4 months.

**Final restorations.** Minimally invasive veneer preparation (Brasseler kit); customized impression posts copying the provisional contours; PVS impression; zirconia abutments on titanium bases (tooth-shaped with space for a facial veneer); hand-crafted feldspathic porcelain veneers; dry and paste try-in; bonding under dental dam (right to left second molar) with HF 9.5% 60 s, silane 60 s, universal adhesive, sandblasting 29 um, phosphoric acid 32% 15 s, resin cement; occlusal guard provided.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Single-patient outcome only: patient and clinicians satisfied with contour at 4 months; patient pleased with shape, contour and shade of the final restorations; at 3-year follow-up "no signs of peri-implant diseases were noted" and radiographs (Figure 14e) show osseointegrated central-incisor implants.
- No numerical outcomes (marginal bone level, mucosal recession, PES/WES, probing depth) are reported.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Authors: single case report with small sample size; larger series with the same approach and longer follow-up needed; other ceramics (zirconia, hybrid) should be tested; future research should identify the most effective immediate implant protocols for positioning the facial bone crest in patients with defective labial bone plates, and explore long-term mucosal stability.
- Appraisal (Claude reading, not stated by the authors): the infection-relevant details a reader would need (pus at extraction, antibiotic use, socket healing, buccal plate integrity) are absent, and the abstract's "purulent exudate" is not corroborated by the case narrative, which describes chronic abscesses. The abstract's stated result ("can be successfully paired") is a generalization from one patient. One author (Villalobos-Tinoco) was a private-practice dentist on this case.

## 6. Related Work

The Discussion cites, as background: Lang 2012 SR of immediate implants (46 studies, 98.4% survival), Assery 2020 22-year case series (36 implants, 97.2% survival, 1.61 mm marginal bone loss), Torra-Moneny 2024 and De Angelis 2021 SR/MAs on soft tissue grafts at immediate implants, Gargallo-Albiol 2020 SR/MA on guided placement, Beier 2012 (318 porcelain veneers: 94.4% at 5 y, 93.5% at 10 y, 82.9% at 20 y), and a Cochrane review on rubber dam.

## 7. Glossary

- 비외상적 발치 (Atraumatic extraction): vertical-force, flapless extraction with periotome to preserve socket walls and papillae.
- 즉시식립 (Immediate implant placement): implant placed at the same visit as extraction.
- 라미네이트 비니어 (Laminate veneer): thin bonded ceramic facial restoration.
- 만성 치근단 농양 (Chronic periapical abscess): long-standing apical infection, here diagnosed clinically and on CBCT.
