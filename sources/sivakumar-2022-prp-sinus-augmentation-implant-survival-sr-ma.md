## Why Ingested

측방 상악동거상술에서 혈소판 유래 골증강제(PRP·PRF·PRGF)가 임플란트 생존에 미치는 영향에 대해 최근 인제스트한 [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/zhang-2025-platelet-bone-enhancers-dbbm-sinus]](16편 SR+MA, RR=1.03 NS)는 DBBM 병용 한정 결과였다. 본 Sivakumar 2022 SR+MA는 이식재 종류를 자가골·이종골로 한정하지 않고 PRP-only RCT 6편(781 implants)을 직접 풀링해 "PRP가 임플란트 생존율을 개선하는가"라는 좁은 질문에 독립적으로 답한다 — pooled OR 0.84 (95% CI 0.37–1.91, NS)로 Zhang 2025의 null 결과를 재확인(reinforces)하며, DBBM 한정이 아닌 더 넓은 이식재 스펙트럼에서도 동일 결론이 성립함을 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42020184501) of 6 RCTs (188 patients, 781 implants) comparing platelet-rich plasma (PRP) plus bone graft versus bone graft alone in maxillary sinus augmentation prior to implant placement.

Pooled estimate for implant survival was OR 0.84 (95% CI 0.37–1.91, I2=0%) — no statistically significant difference between PRP and control groups; subgroup analysis (split-mouth vs. parallel-group design) also showed no significant difference (p=0.45, I2=0%).

The effect of PRP on dental implant survival in sinus augmentation remains uncertain; risk of bias from selective outcome reporting was rated "some concern" in 3 of 6 included trials, underscoring the need for better-designed, protocol-uniform RCTs.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(PRISMA, PROSPERO 사전등록): 상악동거상술 시 골이식재 단독 대비 혈소판풍부혈장(Platelet-Rich Plasma, PRP) 병용을 비교한 RCT 6편(환자 188명, 임플란트 781개)을 통합.

임플란트 생존율에 대한 통합추정치는 OR 0.84 (95% 신뢰구간 0.37–1.91, I2=0%)로 PRP군과 대조군 간 통계적으로 유의한 차이 없음; split-mouth vs parallel-group 하위군 분석에서도 유의차 없음(p=0.45).

상악동거상술에서 PRP가 임플란트 생존율을 개선한다는 근거는 불확실하며, 포함 연구 6편 중 3편에서 선택적 결과보고에 대한 비뚤림 위험이 "일부 우려"로 평가되어 향후 PRP 제조·이식 프로토콜이 통일된 잘 설계된 RCT가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to quantitatively pool implant-count-level (not patient-count-level) implant survival data comparing PRP vs. no-PRP in sinus augmentation, correcting a gap identified in a prior systematic review that pooled by patient number and missed 2 eligible RCTs.
- Pools split-mouth and parallel-group study designs as separate subgroups (per Lesaffre et al. methodological recommendation) to avoid the confidence-interval distortion that arises from ignoring within-patient correlation in split-mouth trials.
- Applies a design-effect adjustment (assumed correlation coefficient 0.07) for the 3 split-mouth studies before pooling.
- Directly compares its findings against 5 prior systematic reviews on PRP/bone formation/implant survival, situating discordant PRP literature (some reviews found benefit, most found none) in one head-to-head evidence table.
- Concludes PRP's effect on implant survival is "uncertain," a more conservative and precisely-scoped conclusion than earlier reviews that either overreached (claiming PRP benefit from patient-level pooling) or underpowered the question (qualitative-only synthesis).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA-guided systematic review + meta-analysis; PROSPERO-registered (CRD42020184501).
- **PICO**: Population = patients undergoing sinus augmentation with implant placement; Intervention = bone graft + PRP; Comparison = bone graft alone; Outcome = dental implant survival (≥6 months follow-up).
- **Inclusion**: RCTs only, minimum mean follow-up 6 months, no language restriction.
- **Exclusion**: non-randomized trials, retrospective studies, case series/reports, animal studies, reviews, conference abstracts, duplicate-cohort publications.
- **Databases**: PubMed, CENTRAL, Cochrane registry, journal databases, Scopus, searched to April 2021; manual reference-list search of included full-texts and reviews.
- **Screening**: 2 independent reviewers for title/abstract and full-text screening; 3rd reviewer (M.M.B.) adjudicated disagreements. Inter-examiner reliability kappa = 0.94 (95% CI 0.92–0.96).
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB tool for RCTs (5 domains; rated low risk / some concern / high risk) by 2 independent reviewers (I.S., S.A.).
- **Statistics**: R software v4.1.1 (meta package). Odds ratio (OR) with 95% CI for dichotomous implant-survival data. Mantel-Haenszel fixed-effect model. Split-mouth studies adjusted for design effect (assumed intra-patient correlation r=0.07). Hartung-Knapp estimator for CI adjustment. Heterogeneity via Cochran's Q and I2 (I2 <25% low, 25–75% moderate, >75% considerable).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Search yield**: 1,914 electronic + 5 hand-search records → 1,100 after dedup → 24 full-text assessed → 6 RCTs included for qualitative and quantitative synthesis.
- **Sample**: 188 patients, 781 implants total (411 intervention/PRP arm, 370 control arm); publication years 2008–2017; follow-up 6–24 months (typical), up to 5 years.
- **Graft type**: 3 studies used autografts, 3 used xenografts, combined with PRP in the intervention arm.
- **Design mix**: 3 split-mouth, 3 parallel-group.
- **Implant survival rate**: intervention 93.3%–100% (weighted mean 98.05%); control 96.2%–100% (weighted mean 97.25%).
- **Implant failures**: 12/411 (intervention) vs. 14/370 (control).
- **Pooled meta-analysis (overall)**: OR 0.84, 95% CI 0.37–1.91, I2=0% (abstract; body text of the article also reports OR 0.80 for the same overall pooled estimate — the two values appear inconsistently within the article, but the CI, I2, and "no significant difference" conclusion are identical wherever stated).
- **Subgroup**: split-mouth OR 1.40 (95% CI 0.52–3.76) favoring control; parallel-group OR 0.28 (95% CI 0.06–1.19) favoring intervention; test for subgroup difference p=0.45 (not significant), I2=0%.
- **Risk of bias**: 3 studies "low risk," 3 studies "some concern" (driven mainly by selective outcome reporting — no pre-specified analysis plan available).
- **Most common complication**: sinus membrane perforation/dehiscence, followed by sinusitis and peri-implant infection.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 6 eligible RCTs (188 patients) — underpowered for a rare binary event (implant failure) with wide confidence intervals (0.37–1.91) that cannot rule out either a clinically meaningful benefit or harm.
- Heterogeneous PRP preparation protocols across included studies (blood volume, single vs. double spin, centrifugation force/time, activating agent — thrombin vs. calcium chloride vs. unspecified) — no consensus formulation, so "PRP" as an intervention is not standardized across the pooled trials.
- Implant success/survival criteria inconsistently reported: only 1 study used Buser's criteria, 2 defined their own, 3 reported none — undermines comparability.
- Loss-to-follow-up reported by only 2 of 6 studies.
- No study reported prosthetic-related failure causes (overload, poor design); only surgical factors captured.
- Authors explicitly flag insufficient evidence to judge PRP's effect specifically in immediate vs. delayed implant placement protocols — future RCTs should target this comparison with a uniform PRP preparation protocol.
- Bias from selective outcome reporting rated "some concern" for half the included trials, weakening confidence in the null result.

## 6. Related Work

The paper situates itself against 5 prior systematic reviews on PRP/bone formation/implant survival, noting that: (a) one prior review pooled implant failure by patient number (not implant number) and missed 2 eligible RCTs; (b) another found adequate support for PRP in sinus augmentation generally but insufficient evidence specifically for implant survival; (c) several found no significant PRP effect on implant survival/bone formation (consistent with this review); (d) one concluded PRP might have a positive effect. The authors cite Lee 2008 (histomorphometric study, 97 implants, 100% survival with PRP+multiple graft materials), Khairy (RCT, PRP improved bone density at 6 months, p=0.041), and Karaca (100% 1-year / 83% 5-year success with PRP) as examples of the positive-signal primary literature this SR+MA statistically reconciles into a null pooled estimate. Related in-house: [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/zhang-2025-platelet-bone-enhancers-dbbm-sinus]] (broader PRP/PRF/PRGF SR+MA restricted to DBBM, also null on implant survival, RR=1.03) and [[wiki/overviews/dbbm-bone-substitute-overview]] (synthesis placing platelet-adjunct evidence in the DBBM decision framework).

## 7. Glossary

- **PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma)**: autologous blood-derived platelet concentrate (5–10x whole-blood platelet concentration) used as a graft adjunct to release growth factors (PDGF, TGF-β) and promote hard/soft-tissue healing.
- **OR (Odds Ratio)**: ratio of odds of implant failure in the PRP group vs. control group; OR <1 favors PRP (lower failure odds), OR >1 favors control.
- **Split-mouth design**: within-patient comparison (e.g., PRP on one side, control on the contralateral side) requiring adjustment for intra-patient correlation before pooling with parallel-group trials.
- **Mantel-Haenszel method**: fixed-effect meta-analytic pooling method for dichotomous outcome data.
- **I2 statistic**: measure of between-study heterogeneity (percentage of total variation due to heterogeneity rather than chance).
- **PROSPERO**: international prospective register of systematic reviews, used here for protocol pre-registration (CRD42020184501).
- **Buser's criteria**: established clinical criteria for assessing dental implant success/osseointegration.
