## Three-line Summary

Comparative prospective study (Chu, Tarnow et al. 2020) evaluating a variable platform-switched subcrestal angle-correction implant for immediate tooth replacement therapy (ITRT) in postextraction sockets, comparing this implant geometry against a reference implant to assess whether axial angulation correction reduces unfavorable socket perforation during uniaxial placement.

The angle-correction implant showed favorable soft-tissue stability and esthetic outcomes and a reduced incidence of unfavorable socket perforation events compared to the reference group through approximately 12 months of follow-up.

Clinical implication: angle-correction geometry can help reconcile prosthetic-driven implant positioning with bony socket constraints in immediate placement scenarios; however, the study is not a true RCT (comparative prospective design), has industry involvement, and medium-term follow-up only limits conclusions about long-term peri-implant bone maintenance.

## 세줄요약

비교 전향 연구(Chu, Tarnow 등 2020): 즉시 치아 교체 요법(Immediate Tooth Replacement Therapy, ITRT) 발치 와에서 가변 플랫폼전환(Variable Platform-Switched) 치조정하 각도수정 임플란트(Angle-Correction Implant)를 평가, 단축 식립 시 소켓 천공을 줄이는지 비교군과 비교; 약 12개월 추적.

각도수정 임플란트는 참조 임플란트 대비 불리한 소켓 천공 발생 감소 및 양호한 연조직 안정성·심미 결과를 보임.

보철 중심 식립 위치와 골성 소켓 제약을 절충하는 각도수정 기하 구조의 임상적 유용성을 시사; 단, 진정한 무작위 배정이 아닌 전향 비교 설계·산업계 연관·중기 추적으로 장기 임플란트주위 골 유지에 대한 결론이 제한됨.

## 2. Key Contributions
Validates angle-correction implant geometry as a tool to reconcile prosthetic-driven positioning with bony-housing constraints in postextraction sockets.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Comparative prospective design. ITRT cases with the angle-correction implant vs reference. Clinical and radiographic outcomes through ~12 months.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Favorable soft-tissue stability and esthetic outcomes with the test implant. Reduced incidence of unfavorable socket perforation events.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
Industry-aligned implant; not a true RCT; medium-term follow-up.

## 7. Glossary
- **IIP:** Immediate Implant Placement — placement at the time of tooth extraction.
- **IIPP:** Immediate Implant Placement and Provisionalization — IIP combined with same-visit provisional restoration.
- **SRP:** Sagittal Root Position — Kan I/II/III/IV classification of root-to-bone relationship.
- **CBCT:** Cone-Beam Computed Tomography.
- **PES/WES:** Pink/White Esthetic Score — esthetic outcome indices for anterior implants.
- **EMBL:** Early Marginal Bone Loss.
