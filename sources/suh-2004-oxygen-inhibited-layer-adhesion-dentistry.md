## Why Ingested

Foundational in-vitro investigation of the oxygen-inhibited layer (OIL) role in resin bonding — directly relevant to adhesive selection, especially self-etching all-in-one adhesives used with dual-cure or self-cure cements.

→ [[resin-bonding/suh-2004-oxygen-inhibited-layer-adhesion-dentistry]]

## Three-line Summary

Suh (2004) conducted four in-vitro experiments at Bisco Inc. to investigate whether the oxygen-inhibited layer (OIL) of cured resin composites is necessary for bonding with subsequent composite resin increments, using shear bond strength testing, microtensile bond strength testing, UV-Vis camphorquinone (CQ) decomposition spectroscopy, and FTIR degree of conversion (DoC) analysis. This represented a foundational investigation into the role of OIL in adhesive dentistry at a time when the clinical significance of OIL was debated.

The critical finding was that bond strength was not significantly different between samples cured with and without an oxygen-inhibited layer, suggesting OIL is not necessary for bonding composite to composite. However, the study identified a clinically significant exception: the OIL of simplified self-etching all-in-one adhesives is acidic (due to unpolymerised acid monomers), which interferes with the amine activator in self-curing and dual-curing composites, rendering such adhesives incompatible with self-cured or dual-cured resin cements without additional neutralisation steps, oxygen-blocking layers, or dual-cure compatible adhesive selection.

The practical clinical implication—that all-in-one (single-bottle) self-etch adhesives used for indirect restorations bonded with dual-cure or self-cure luting cements require specific compatibility management—became a foundational principle of adhesive cementation. This paper's mechanistic data complements Gauthier 2005 (OIL physical chemistry) and Lim 2022 (glycerin-based OIL elimination) by anchoring the clinical consequence side of the OIL knowledge axis.

## 세줄요약

수(2004)는 경화된 레진 복합체의 산소억제층(Oxygen-Inhibited Layer, OIL)이 후속 레진 접합에 필수적인지를 규명하기 위해 전단결합강도·미세인장결합강도·자외선-가시광선 분광법·FTIR 중합전환도(Degree of Conversion, DoC) 등 4가지 시험관 내 실험을 시행했다.

핵심 발견: OIL 유무에 따른 결합강도는 유의한 차이가 없어 복합레진-복합레진 접합에 OIL이 필수적이지 않음이 확인됐다. 그러나 중요한 예외: 단일 용기형 자기부식 접착제(all-in-one adhesive)의 OIL은 미중합 산성 단량체를 포함해 산성 환경을 형성하며, 이중중합·자기중합 레진 시멘트의 아민 활성제를 억제해 접합 불량을 초래한다.

이 연구의 임상적 함의는 간접 수복물을 이중중합 또는 자기중합 시멘트로 접착할 때 all-in-one 자기부식 접착제를 단독 사용하면 안 된다는 접착 도재 세공의 근본 원칙을 확립했다. 이 발견은 가티에 2005(OIL 물리화학)·임 2022(글리세린 OIL 제거) 논문과 함께 OIL 지식 축의 임상 결과 측면에 해당한다.

## Summary

Suh (2004) investigated whether the oxygen-inhibited layer (OIL) of cured resin composites is necessary for bonding with subsequent composite increments. Four experiments were conducted at Bisco Inc.: shear bond strength (BisCover/D/E Resin on Renew composite), microtensile bond strength (composite cured in air vs nitrogen), camphorquinone (CQ) decomposition by UV-Vis spectroscopy, and degree of conversion (DoC) by FTIR.

Key findings:
- Bond strength was NOT significantly different between samples with and without OIL
- CQ photoinitiator was depleted/decomposed within the OIL
- Degree of conversion of post-cured OIL was lower than control

Conclusion: OIL is not necessary for bonding with composite resin. However, with simplified self-etching all-in-one adhesives, the OIL is acidic, which interferes with self-curing reactions and renders these adhesives incompatible with self-cured or dual-cured composite cements without additional neutralization steps.

## Methods

1. Shear bond strength: BisCover and D/E Resin placed on Renew composite disks, cured with/without OIL. Light-Bond composite cured over resin in gelatin capsule; tested after 2h water soak at 37°C (Instron Model 4466).
2. Microtensile bond strength: Renew A2 substrates cured in air or nitrogen. Light-Core placed on each, sectioned into beams, stressed to failure.
3. CQ decomposition: Experimental CQ resin between glass plates irradiated at intervals, absorption spectrum measured (Cary 50 Bio UV-Vis Spectrometer).
4. Degree of conversion: Mylar-strip CQ resin pre-cured in air at different intervals, post-cured under nitrogen; FTIR (Spectrum 1000).

## Clinical Implication

Self-etching all-in-one adhesives leave an acidic OIL that inhibits the amine activator in self-cured composites → not compatible with dual-cure or self-cure resin cements without additional steps (separate bonding agent, oxygen-blocking layer, or dual-cure compatible adhesive).
