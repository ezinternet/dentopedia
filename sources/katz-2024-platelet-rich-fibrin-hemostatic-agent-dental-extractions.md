## Why Ingested

[[drug/anticoagulants/guardieiro-2023-chitosan-cellulose-hemostasis-dapt-rct]] and [[drug/anticoagulants/swissdentj-2026-gelatin-sponge-tranexamic-acid-anticoagulated]] cover chitosan, cellulose, gelatin sponge and tranexamic acid as local hemostatics in anticoagulated extraction, but the wiki had no systematic review of autologous PRF in this setting ([[oral-surgery/dinkova-2025-local-hemostasis-oral-surgery-review]] only mentions platelet concentrates narratively). This SR fills that gap and positions PRF against those comparators.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42024562289; no meta-analysis) of 11 clinical studies (7 RCTs, 1 non-randomized controlled study, 3 uncontrolled series; 864 patients, at least 1148 teeth) on autologous platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) as a local hemostatic after dental extraction in patients on anticoagulants or antiplatelet drugs.

All studies reported only mild-to-moderate bleeding, mostly controlled by local compression; PRF outperformed stitches alone, dry gauze and cellulose plugs, showed no significant bleeding difference versus chitosan, tranexamic acid or gelatin sponge, and achieved hemostasis more slowly than chitosan (2-3 min in both chitosan-comparison RCTs that reported it).

PRF is a feasible fully autologous option, but heterogeneous PRF protocols, few controlled trials, no analysis of extraction extent, and only one small DOAC RCT (Kyyak 2023) mean its hemostatic effect, especially under direct oral anticoagulants, cannot yet be quantified.

## 세줄요약

항응고제·항혈소판제 복용 환자의 발치 후 지혈제로서 자가 혈소판풍부섬유소 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)를 평가한 체계적 문헌고찰 (PRISMA, PROSPERO 등록, 메타분석 없음); 임상연구 11편 (무작위 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 7, 비무작위 대조 1, 무대조 3), 환자 864명·최소 1148개 치아.

전 연구에서 경도~중등도 출혈만 보고되었고 대부분 국소 압박으로 해결; PRF는 봉합만 한 경우·마른 거즈·셀룰로오스 플러그보다 우수했고, 키토산·트라넥삼산 (Tranexamic Acid, TXA)·젤라틴 스펀지와는 출혈 차이가 유의하지 않았으며, 지혈 시간은 키토산보다 느렸다 (키토산 비교 RCT 두 편 모두 양군 2-3분 내 안정).

PRF는 완전 자가유래 대안으로 실행 가능하나, PRF 제조 프로토콜의 이질성·대조군 RCT 부족·발치 범위 미분석, 직접경구항응고제 (Direct Oral Anticoagulant, DOAC)는 소규모 RCT 1편뿐이라 지혈 효과의 크기를 정량화하기 어렵다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- PRF-only systematic review restricted to anticoagulated or antiplatelet patients, updating two earlier reviews (Filho 2021, 3 studies; Campana, 6 studies mixing PRF and PRP).
- Comparator-stratified synthesis: PRF better than stitches, dry gauze, cellulose; no significant bleeding difference vs chitosan, tranexamic acid, gelatin; slower than chitosan.
- Drug-class breakdown: 4 antiplatelet, 4 VKA, 3 DOAC, 1 unspecified.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PubMed, EMBASE, Cochrane Library, SCOPUS to 1 June 2024; German/English; autologous PRF only; 1782 records, 789 after de-duplication, 13 full texts, 11 included; kappa 0.822.
- RoB 2: 5 low, 6 moderate. No pooling because of heterogeneity.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 864 patients, at least 1148 teeth; 7 RCTs (2 split-mouth in text; Giudice, Brancaccio, Kyyak are described as split-mouth in the table), 1 non-randomized controlled, 3 uncontrolled.
- Only mild-to-moderate bleeding, mostly resolved by compression; chitosan arrested bleeding faster (both stabilized in 2-3 min).
- VKA mean INR 2.28, 2.4, 3.16 across the three warfarin studies; two bleeds at INR 3.7 in Sammartino.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Heterogeneous PRF protocols and comparators; three studies without controls; single small DOAC RCT (n=21); extraction extent not analyzed.
- Source inconsistencies (PRF vs tranexamic acid result; study counts by drug class; 789 vs 1782) noted on the wiki page.
- Authors call for RCTs with standardized PRF protocols and control sites, stratified by drug and extraction extent.

## 6. Related Work

- Local hemostatic comparisons (chitosan, cellulose, gelatin sponge, tranexamic acid) in anticoagulated/antiplatelet extraction.
- Continue-versus-interrupt evidence for DOAC/VKA in dentoalveolar surgery.

## 7. Glossary

- **PRF**: platelet-rich fibrin, autologous fibrin clot made from centrifuged blood without added anticoagulant; L-PRF (leukocyte-PRF) and A-PRF/A-PRF+ (advanced) differ in centrifugation.
- **INR**: international normalized ratio, VKA monitoring value.
- **VKA / DOAC**: vitamin K antagonist / direct oral anticoagulant.
