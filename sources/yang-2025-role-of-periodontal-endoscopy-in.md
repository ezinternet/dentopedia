## Why Ingested

The held vertical-root-fracture (VRF) pages stop at CBCT: [[wiki/cracked-tooth/kim-2024-developing-evidence-based-clinical-imaging]] gives the imaging sequence (periapical first, small-FOV CBCT only if needed) and [[wiki/cracked-tooth/shokri-2024-is-cone-beam-computed-tomography]] shows CBCT still misses roughly 3 in 10 VRFs, with lower sensitivity in root-filled teeth. None addresses what to do when a tooth has VRF-type signs (isolated deep pocket, sinus tract) but CBCT shows no fracture line. This prospective clinical cohort supplies a chairside next step (periodontal endoscopy with methylene-blue staining) and a measured effect on the extract / flap / root-canal decision, complementing the laboratory-stage optical option in [[wiki/endodontics/diagnosis/alkhani-2026-optical-coherence-tomography-detection-dental]].

## Three-line Summary

Single-centre prospective cohort (Xi'an Jiaotong University, July 2022 to June 2023): 112 analysed teeth with suspected VRF (S-VRF; isolated pocket >6 mm plus swelling, sinus tract or occlusal pain, no definite fracture line on CBCT; 90.2% root-filled), all examined with periodontal endoscopy after scaling and methylene-blue staining, and treatment plans (flap surgery 56, root canal treatment 48, extraction 11) then re-assigned.

VRF was confirmed in 70/112 teeth (62.5%); endoscopy detected 59 (sensitivity 84.3%, 95% CI 75.0-91.1%; specificity and PPV 100%; NPV 79.25%; AUC 0.92), and the endoscopy-based re-plan avoided planned flap surgery in 36/56 (64.3%) and planned root canal (re)treatment in 19/45 (42.2%); interobserver kappa rose from 0.444 (CBCT alone) to 0.842 (CBCT + endoscopy).

Periodontal endoscopy looks like a useful chairside adjunct after a CBCT-negative work-up, but a positive finding is far more reliable than a negative one (11 of 70 VRFs missed, mainly premolars with narrow or interproximal access), and the design (convenience sample, 62.5% VRF prevalence, endoscopy findings steering the treatment that verified them, 1-year follow-up) limits generalisation.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 전향적 코호트 (Xi'an Jiaotong University, 2022.7-2023.6): 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 의심치 (Suspected VRF, S-VRF; 6 mm 초과 단독 치주낭 + 부종·누공·교합통, 콘빔 CT (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT)상 확정 파절선 없음; 90.2% 근관치료치) 분석 112치아에 스케일링·메틸렌블루 염색 후 치주내시경 (Periodontal endoscopy)을 시행하고 치료계획(치주판막수술 56·근관치료 48·발치 11)을 재배정.

VRF는 70/112 (62.5%)에서 확인되었고 치주내시경은 59개를 검출 (민감도 84.3%, 95% CI 75.0-91.1%; 특이도·양성예측도 100%; 음성예측도 79.25%; 곡선하면적 (Area Under the Curve, AUC) 0.92); 내시경 기반 재계획으로 계획된 판막수술 36/56 (64.3%)과 근관 (재)치료 19/45 (42.2%)를 피함; 관찰자 간 일치도 (Cohen's kappa)는 CBCT 단독 0.444 → CBCT+내시경 0.842.

CBCT 음성 이후 chairside 보조 수단으로 유망하나 양성 소견의 신뢰도가 음성 소견보다 훨씬 높고 (70개 중 11개 VRF 누락: 주로 접근이 좁거나 인접면인 소구치), 편의표본·VRF 유병률 62.5%·내시경 소견이 이를 검증하는 치료를 좌우한 설계·1년 추적이라는 한계로 일반화에 제약이 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First held clinical study of periodontal endoscopy as a decision aid for CBCT-negative suspected VRF, reporting diagnostic accuracy and the change in treatment plan.
- Defines a standardised chairside protocol: anaesthesia, endoscope sheath into the pocket, observe, scale/root-plane, stain with methylene blue, re-observe; a continuous blue line longer than 2 mm on screen is read as VRF.
- Quantifies avoided exploratory procedures (flap surgery 36/56, root canal (re)treatment 19/45) and improved examiner agreement (kappa 0.444 to 0.842).
- Describes three concrete miss mechanisms (narrow top / wide bottom bone defect blocking sheath entry; fracture on the buccal line angle running to the interproximal surface; shallow early crack beyond probe resolution).
- Proposes using periodontal endoscopy as an adjunct to CBCT in S-VRF with an isolated pocket, before choosing extraction, flap surgery or root canal treatment.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective single-centre cohort / diagnostic-accuracy study, convenience sampling; ethics approval and written consent.
- **Population**: 112 patients, 115 S-VRF teeth enrolled (3 lost to follow-up, 112 analysed); 45 male, 67 female, mean age 44.6 +/- 13.4 years; molars 86 (76.8%), premolars 25 (22.3%), anterior 1; 101 (90.2%) previously root-filled. Inclusion: age 18-70, recurrent swelling / sinus tract / occlusal pain, isolated pocket >6 mm (UNC-15), no definite fracture line on two-plane CBCT (NewTom VGi). Exclusion: HbA1c >7.5% or uncontrolled hypertension, generalised periodontitis (>=30% sites CAL >=5 mm), existing root fracture or iatrogenic crack, pregnancy/lactation.
- **Pre-endoscopic plan** (two endodontists, based on filling, restoration and periodontal status): flap surgery (S, n=56), root canal treatment / retreatment (R, n=48), extraction (E, n=11).
- **Index test**: periodontal endoscope (P100, Bangwo) by periodontists with >10 years' experience; interpreted jointly by one periodontist and one endodontist; results then used to reassign treatment.
- **Reference standard**: VRF confirmed by microscopic crack identification after extraction/root amputation, flap exploration under microscope (methylene-blue stained), crown removal or pulp-chamber/canal exploration, or clinical failure within a 1-year follow-up.
- **Outcomes**: reduction in unnecessary root canal treatment and surgery, sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, AUC, inter/intra-examiner kappa, logistic regression of risk factors.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Convenience sampling at one tertiary hospital; VRF prevalence 62.5% is not representative of S-VRF in general practice (NPV and the avoided-treatment percentages depend on this prevalence).
- Only 9.8% were non-root-filled teeth; results mainly apply to root-filled teeth with an isolated deep pocket.
- No blinded independent comparator: all teeth had endoscopy, and the "CBCT-based" comparator is the pre-endoscopic plan assigned by two endodontists, not a separately measured CBCT accuracy. Every tooth was CBCT-negative for a definite fracture by inclusion, so the "49.1% accuracy gain" is partly built into the design.
- Verification differs by arm: endoscopy-positive teeth were extracted and examined microscopically, whereas endoscopy-negative root canal teeth were confirmed only by canal exploration and 1-year follow-up, so later-revealed VRFs could be undercounted; follow-up is limited to 1 year (3-year and 5-year follow-up planned).
- The device has real limits: apical, interproximal, furcation and complex-pocket fractures are poorly reached; operator skill (spatial awareness, periodontal experience) is required; equipment cost and optical-component damage from heat sterilisation.
- Source-data issues (reproduced as stated, not corrected): (a) abstract reports 39.58% (19/48) avoided root canal treatment, whereas the body reports 42.2% (19/45); (b) specificity is written as 59/59 although 59 is the count of endoscopy-positive teeth (implied true negatives 42/42); (c) Table 1 lists probing depth as 100% >=8 mm while inclusion required >6 mm; (d) the Discussion states VRF was more prevalent in females and that root canal treatment "accelerates" VRF, but Table 1 shows no significant association for sex (p=0.367) or endodontic treatment (p=0.07), so these statements are not supported by the study's own table.

## 7. Glossary
- **S-VRF (suspected vertical root fracture)**: tooth with VRF-type signs (isolated deep pocket, sinus tract, occlusal pain) but no definite fracture line on CBCT.
- **Periodontal endoscopy**: fibre-optic cold-light probe with magnified video, inserted in the periodontal pocket to view the root surface.
- **Methylene-blue staining**: dye applied to the root surface so that crack lines appear as continuous blue lines.
- **ETT / NETT**: endodontically treated / non-endodontically treated teeth.
- **NPV / PPV**: negative / positive predictive value; both depend on disease prevalence.
