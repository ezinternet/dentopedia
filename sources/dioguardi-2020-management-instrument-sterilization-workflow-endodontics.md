## Why Ingested

The wiki's only existing sterilization-workflow page, [[infection-control/patino-marin-2025-sterilization-disinfection-dental-practices]], is a broad narrative review with no systematic search protocol and gives general dental-practice sterilization parameters (autoclave 121–132°C/≥15min) without endodontic-specific guidance. This PRISMA-guided SR+MA (Dioguardi 2020) fills that gap with quantitative meta-analytic comparisons (autoclave vs glutaraldehyde vs glass-bead) specific to endodontic instrumentation, and adds three clinically actionable details the narrative review lacks: (1) a validated minimum autoclave parameter for endodontic instruments (≥120°C for ≥30 min, distinct from the general 121–132°C/15min figure), (2) an explicit NiTi rotary-file reuse-cycle limit (≤5 autoclave cycles) grounded in cyclic-fatigue/torsional-resistance data, and (3) a dedicated chemical-disinfection protocol for non-autoclavable single-use root-filling materials (gutta-percha/resin cones), which the narrative review does not address at all.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA, 130 eligible studies from PubMed/Scopus, 40-year search window) evaluating sterilization/disinfection methods for endodontic instruments and root-filling materials.

Meta-analysis found autoclaving superior to glutaraldehyde (I²=0%, fixed-effects) and glutaraldehyde superior to glass-bead sterilization (I²=73%, random-effects); autoclaving at minimum 120°C for ≥30 min is confirmed as the most effective method, with NiTi instrument reuse limited to ≤5 autoclave cycles and non-autoclavable gutta-percha/resin cones requiring chemical disinfection (NaOCl 2–5.25% or chlorhexidine 2%).

Clinically, a structured presterilization ultrasonic/washer-disinfector cleansing step is essential before final sterilization regardless of method, and glutaraldehyde is discouraged for routine outpatient dental use due to its 8–12h exposure requirement and toxicity.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(PRISMA, PubMed/Scopus 130편, 40년 검색창)으로 근관치료 기구·근관충전재의 멸균/소독 방법을 평가.

메타분석 결과 오토클레이브가 글루타르알데히드보다 우수(I²=0%, 고정효과모형), 글루타르알데히드가 유리구슬 멸균보다 우수(I²=73%, 랜덤효과모형); 최소 120°C·30분 이상 오토클레이브가 최적 방법으로 확인되었고, NiTi 파일 재사용은 오토클레이브 5회 이하로 제한, 열처리 불가능한 거타퍼차/레진 콘은 화학적 소독(NaOCl 2–5.25% 또는 클로르헥시딘 2%) 필요.

임상적으로는 최종 멸균 방법과 무관하게 초음파세척기/세척소독기를 이용한 사전세척 단계가 필수이며, 글루타르알데히드는 8–12시간 노출·독성 문제로 외래 진료 일상 사용에는 권장되지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. First systematic quantitative synthesis (meta-analysis) specifically comparing sterilization methods (autoclave vs glutaraldehyde vs glass-bead) for endodontic instruments.
2. Establishes a structured, phase-by-phase endodontic instrument-reprocessing protocol: presterilization cleansing → chemical disinfection of non-autoclavable materials → final sterilization.
3. Quantifies mechanical degradation of endodontic files under repeated autoclaving and proposes a concrete reuse-cycle limit for NiTi instruments.
4. Documents that standard sterilization/disinfection methods only partially inactivate prions, and that WHO-suggested prion-inactivation protocols are impractical for routine clinical use.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PRISMA-guided systematic review. Search on PubMed and Scopus (22 Jan 2019 – 1 Feb 2019, updated 4 Feb 2019) using keyword combinations: "endodontic sterilization" (PubMed 316, Scopus 236), "endodontic autoclave" (37, 50), "decontamination dental burs" (11, 6), "sterilization dental burs" (60, 25), "gutta-percha cones sterilization" (27, 24), "gutta-percha points sterilization" (12, 14). Included peer reviews, original research, systematic clinical studies, and reviews on endodontic sterilization from the last 40 years, English language. Screening by two independent reviewers (κ = 0.675) with third/fourth reviewer arbitration; Newcastle-Ottawa scale for risk of bias; Review Manager 5.3 for meta-analysis. Two quantitative comparisons: (1) autoclave vs glutaraldehyde; (2) glutaraldehyde vs glass-bead sterilizers.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 845 records identified (PubMed + Scopus) → 761 after year restriction → 130 eligible studies after screening/deduplication.
- Autoclave vs glutaraldehyde meta-analysis: low heterogeneity (I²=0%), fixed-effects model, result favors autoclaving.
- Glutaraldehyde vs glass-bead meta-analysis: high heterogeneity (I²=73%), random-effects model, result favors glutaraldehyde over glass-bead.
- Ultrasonic cleaning outperforms manual/containerized cleaning by ~5% higher measured cleanliness for non-containerized instruments.
- Autoclaving at minimum 120°C for ≥30 min identified as most efficient for total destruction of bacteria/viruses/spores; UV light (240–280nm) ineffective for hollow/shadowed surfaces; glass-bead sterilization (45s at 240°C) ineffective; CO2 laser comparable to autoclave but rarely used clinically.
- Cutting-capacity reduction in reused instruments: 20% after 7 autoclave cycles in one study; 1–12% reduction after 5–10 cycles in another; NiTi files may show improved fatigue resistance after autoclaving (thermal-treatment effect) while steel files show reduced torsional resistance. Recommendation: limit NiTi reuse to ≤5 autoclave cycles.
- Non-autoclavable single-use materials (gutta-percha/resin cones): disinfect with NaOCl 2–5.25% (5–10 min), chlorhexidine 2% (1–2 min), or peracetic acid 1–2% (2.5–5 min); glutaraldehyde requires 8–12h and is not recommended for outpatient use.
- Burs arrive nonsterile from manufacturers; autoclaving is the most effective sterilization system; ultrasonic/washer-disinfectors alone cannot remove all interblade detritus.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Meta-analysis limited to two specific pairwise comparisons (autoclave vs glutaraldehyde; glutaraldehyde vs glass-bead) — no direct autoclave vs glass-bead pooled comparison.
- High heterogeneity (I²=73%) in the glutaraldehyde vs glass-bead comparison limits confidence in that specific effect estimate.
- Search restricted to PubMed/Scopus and English-language publications; may miss non-English or grey literature.
- Prion inactivation remains an unresolved practical gap — WHO-suggested protocols are corrosive/time-prohibitive for routine clinical workflows.

## 7. Glossary

- **NiTi**: Nickel-titanium alloy, used for rotary/reciprocating endodontic instruments.
- **I² (I-squared)**: Statistic quantifying heterogeneity across meta-analyzed studies (0% = no heterogeneity, higher = more).
- **Glass-bead sterilizer**: Chairside dry-heat device using heated glass beads (~240°C) for rapid small-instrument sterilization; found ineffective in this review.
- **Newcastle-Ottawa scale**: Risk-of-bias assessment tool for non-randomized/observational studies used in systematic reviews.
