## Why Ingested

Seminal FEA study explaining the biomechanical mechanism by which sinus floor cortex contact improves implant stability. Establishes the dose-response relationship between implant apex penetration depth and axial resonance frequency — the theoretical basis for ISQ improvement in [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/han-2016-primary-stability-bicortical-posterior-maxilla]] and the clinical ISQ gap in [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/bhave-2023-unicortical-bicortical-anchorage-posterior-maxilla]].

## Three-line Summary

3D FEA, 10 posterior maxilla models; Group 1 (n=6): alveolar ridge 10–14 mm, varying distance from implant apex to sinus floor cortex; Group 2 (n=4): fixed ridge 11 mm, varying sinus floor cortex thickness (0.5–2.0 mm); 4 mm × 10 mm Nobel Biocare implant; 129 N at 30°.
Bicortical implantation (apex through sinus floor) redirected stress from crestal cortex to sinus floor cortex; implant displacement and axial resonance frequency improved linearly as apex penetration depth increased; buccolingual (RFA/ISQ-relevant) resonance frequency was NOT significantly affected by sinus floor contact.
Sinus floor cortex improves axial stability and reduces displacement, especially for immediate loading; the ISQ benefit of bicortical fixation may be smaller than clinical studies suggest because buccolingual RF is largely unaffected by sinus floor engagement.

## 세줄요약

3D FEA 10모델; 임플란트 첨단과 상악동저 피질골 간격 변화(10–14 mm 골 높이) + 피질골 두께 변화(0.5–2.0 mm); 4×10 mm Nobel Biocare; 129 N 30°.
쌍겹질 임플란트에서 상악동저 피질골이 치조정 피질골 응력 대신 응력 부담; 첨단 침입 깊이 ↑ → 변위 ↓ · 축방향 공명주파수 ↑ (선형); 협설방향 공명주파수(RFA/ISQ)는 유의 변화 없음.
상악동저 피질골은 축방향 안정성·즉시 하중에 유리; ISQ(협설 RFA)에 대한 쌍겹질 이점은 임상 연구 결과보다 작을 수 있음 — 협설 방향은 상악동저 피질골에 둔감.

## Key Contributions

- First FEA isolating the implant apex–sinus floor relationship, all other variables held constant
- Bicortical implantation shifts stress FROM crestal cortex TO sinus floor cortex — reduces neck-level bone stress
- Axial resonance frequency increases linearly with sinus floor cortex penetration depth
- Buccolingual resonance frequency (= what Osstell/ISQ measures) NOT significantly changed by sinus floor contact
- Sinus floor cortex thickness (0.5–2.0 mm) modulates sinus floor stress but minimally affects crestal cortex
- Immediate loading benefits most from sinus floor engagement — displacement reduction is most dramatic

## Methodology

- **Models**: 10 FE models of posterior maxilla with sinus membrane (0.3 mm)
  - Group 1: alveolar ridge 10–14 mm, implant apex distance to sinus floor varied
  - Group 2: alveolar ridge 11 mm fixed, sinus floor cortex thickness 0.5–2.0 mm
- **Interface conditions**: (1) full osseointegration (conventional loading), (2) frictional contact μ=0.3 (immediate loading)
- **Load**: 129 N, 30° posterior and 30° sagittal incline
- **Outputs**: von Mises stress distribution, implant displacement (neck + apex), axial resonance frequency, buccolingual resonance frequency

## Key Results

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| Bicortical (apex through cortex) vs distant apex: crestal cortex stress | **Lower** in bicortical — sinus floor absorbs stress |
| Immediate loading: bicortical implant displacement vs other | **Significantly smaller** |
| Axial RFA with increasing penetration depth | **Linear increase** |
| Buccolingual RFA (= ISQ direction) | Not significantly affected by sinus floor contact |
| Sinus floor cortex stress: bicortical immediate loading | 73.44 MPa (higher than crestal 58.69 MPa) |
| Sinus membrane von Mises: immediate loading, apex in cortex | Increased but clinically tolerable |

## Limitations

- Standard Nobel Biocare implant (4×10 mm) only; no ultrashort models
- Simplified geometry (uniform bone heights, no real anatomy)
- Does not model bone formation around apex after OSFE
- Buccolingual vs axial RFA distinction needs clinical validation — Osstell measures buccolingual primarily

## Glossary

- **Axial resonance frequency**: Vibration mode along implant long axis (apex-to-crown); improved by sinus floor cortex contact
- **Buccolingual resonance frequency**: Lateral vibration mode — what Osstell ISQ predominantly measures; insensitive to sinus floor contact per this study
- **Frictional contact (μ=0.3)**: Models immediate loading before osseointegration; allows minor micromotion
