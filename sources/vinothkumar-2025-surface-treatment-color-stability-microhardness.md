## Why Ingested

Directly compares four OIL-management strategies (glycerin, Mylar strip, finishing/polishing, no treatment) on color stability and surface microhardness of nanohybrid composites — the first study in this wiki to quantify the practical trade-off between glycerin and polishing for OIL elimination in direct composite.
→ [[resin/aromaa-2018-delayed-post-curing-oxygen-inhibition]]

## Three-line Summary

In vitro, n=240 discs of two nanohybrid enamel-shade composites (G-aenial A'CHORD, Beautifil II Enamel), comparing four OIL-control treatments: glycerin (G), Mylar strip (M), finishing+polishing (FP), and no treatment (NT) on ΔE (spectrophotometer) and Vickers microhardness (VHN). FP achieved the highest hardness; M showed the least color change (ΔE); glycerin occupied an intermediate position on both outcomes; G-aenial A'CHORD outperformed Beautifil II on both metrics. Finishing and polishing is the superior strategy for surface hardness; Mylar strip or glycerin better preserves color; neither strategy eliminates the need to choose based on clinical priority.

## 세줄요약

나노하이브리드 에나멜 쉐이드 복합레진 2종(G-aenial A'CHORD vs Beautifil II Enamel), 총 240개 시편을 대상으로 산소억제층(Oxygen-Inhibited Layer, OIL) 처리법 4가지(글리세린·마일러 스트립·연마·무처리) 비교. 표면 경도(Vickers Hardness Number, VHN)는 연마(FP)가 최고; 색 안정성(ΔE)은 마일러 스트립(M)이 가장 낮은 ΔE(최고 안정); 글리세린은 양 지표에서 중간 위치. G-aenial A'CHORD가 두 지표 모두 Beautifil II 대비 우수했으며, OIL 처리 선택은 경도와 색 중 어느 쪽을 우선시하느냐에 따라 달라진다.

## Key Contributions

- First direct 4-way comparison of OIL-elimination strategies on **both** color stability and microhardness simultaneously
- FP (finishing/polishing) is superior for VHN; Mylar strip is superior for color stability
- Glycerin, while widely recommended, does not outperform polishing on hardness or Mylar on color
- Material brand matters: G-aenial A'CHORD (GC) showed lower ΔE and higher VHN than Beautifil II Enamel (Shofu) regardless of treatment
- Provides evidence base for clinician decision-making between OIL strategies based on clinical priority (aesthetic vs mechanical)

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | In vitro, controlled experiment |
| Composites | G-aenial A'CHORD (AC) and Beautifil II Enamel (BT) |
| Specimen type | Disc-shaped (n=240 total; 60/composite/outcome × 4 subgroups of 15) |
| OIL treatments | Glycerin (G), Mylar strip (M), Finishing+polishing (FP), No treatment (NT) |
| Outcome 1 | ΔE (color difference) via spectrophotometer |
| Outcome 2 | VHN (Vickers microhardness) |
| Statistics | One-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc; p=0.001 significance threshold |

## Key Results

### Vickers Microhardness (VHN)

| Treatment | Ranking | Note |
|---|---|---|
| FP (finishing+polishing) | **1st (highest)** | Both AC and BT; Tukey p=0.001 |
| G (glycerin) | 2nd–3rd | Intermediate |
| M (Mylar strip) | 2nd–3rd | Intermediate |
| NT (no treatment) | **Lowest** | Both groups |

- AC showed significantly higher mean HV than BT across all subgroups

### Color Stability (ΔE)

| Treatment | Ranking | Note |
|---|---|---|
| M (Mylar strip) | **Least ΔE (best)** | AC: one-way ANOVA p=0.001; BT: p=0.001 |
| G (glycerin) | BT: also low ΔE | M and G comparable in BT group |
| FP (finishing+polishing) | High ΔE | Paradoxically, polishing increased color change |
| NT (no treatment) | — | Comparison baseline |

- AC showed significantly lower ΔE than BT (better color stability) across treatments

## Limitations

- In vitro — no oral aging, thermal cycling, or salivary exposure
- Single curing protocol; no variation in light intensity or exposure time
- Short-term color assessment; long-term color stability not assessed
- No mechanical fatigue or wear testing
- Two specific composites only; generalizability limited
- No analysis of OIL thickness or degree of conversion

## Glossary

- **OIL (Oxygen-Inhibited Layer)**: Incompletely polymerized surface layer (4–40 µm) on resin composites exposed to atmospheric oxygen during photopolymerization
- **VHN (Vickers Hardness Number)**: Surface microhardness; proxy for degree of polymerization
- **ΔE**: Color difference metric; lower = better color stability; ΔE<3.3 is often cited as clinically acceptable threshold
- **Nanohybrid composite**: Composite resin with nanoparticle + hybrid filler combination for improved surface finish
- **Mylar strip**: Polyester film placed over uncured composite surface to exclude oxygen during curing
