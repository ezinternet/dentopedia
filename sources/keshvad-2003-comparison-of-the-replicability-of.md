## Why Ingested

Supplies the original three-method head-to-head dataset behind the centric-relation (CR) technique evidence we already hold: the de Moraes Melo Neto 2021 SR lists a "Keshvad & Winstanley" study (n = 14, bimanual > chin-point) among its six included studies — matching this paper's author pair and sample size — so this page contributes the primary data, including a Gothic-arch arm, that the SR summarized at technique level. It also complements [[wiki/complete-denture/huang-2026-evaluation-of-condylar-position-displacement]], which asks a different question (CR–maximum intercuspation position (MIP) displacement) whereas this paper asks which CR method is most *repeatable* over time.

## Three-line Summary

In-vivo method-comparison study in 14 healthy adults (7 men, 7 women; mean age 26.61 +/- 4.20 years) comparing the repeatability of three centric relation (CR) recording methods — bimanual manipulation with a jig, chin-point guidance with a jig, and Gothic arch tracing — using a mechanical three-dimensional (3D) mandibular position indicator mounted on a Denar D4A articulator.

Within-subject variability spanned 0.03 mm (left-side z axis, bimanual) to 1.6 mm (left-side y axis, Gothic arch); by F test the bimanual method was the most consistent (reported as 10.11, p = 1, to 0.438, p = 0.005 times less variation than Gothic arch) and chin-point guidance fell between the two.

For CR jaw-relation records in dentate, non-TMD subjects, bimanual manipulation gave the most repeatable condylar positioning and Gothic arch the least — but this is an abstract-only report of a small healthy-volunteer sample, with no per-axis data tables available for verification.

## 세줄요약

생체내(in-vivo) 술식비교 연구, 건강한 성인 14명(남 7명·여 7명, 평균 연령 26.61 +/- 4.20세)에서 중심위(Centric Relation, CR) 기록법 3가지 — 지그(Jig)를 이용한 양수조작(Bimanual Manipulation), 지그를 이용한 턱끝유도(Chin-Point Guidance), 고딕아치 추적(Gothic Arch Tracing) — 의 재현성을 교합기(Articulator, Denar D4A)에 장착한 기계적 3차원(3-Dimensional, 3D) 하악위치 지시기(Mandibular Position Indicator)로 비교.

피험자 내 변동성은 0.03 mm(양수조작법 좌측 z축)에서 1.6 mm(고딕아치법 좌측 y축)까지였고, F검정(F-test)에서 양수조작법이 가장 일관적이었다(고딕아치법보다 10.11배, p = 1 ~ 0.438배, p = 0.005 적은 변동으로 기재), 턱끝유도법은 두 술식의 중간.

치아가 있고 측두하악장애(Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD)가 없는 성인에서 중심위 기록 시 양수조작법이 가장 재현성이 높고 고딕아치법이 가장 낮았으나, 초록 기반 소표본 건강 지원자 연구이며 축별 원자료 표가 없어 검증이 제한된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly compares three routinely used CR registration techniques — bimanual manipulation with a jig, chin-point guidance with a jig, and Gothic arch tracing — within the same subjects.
- Describes a mechanical three-dimensional mandibular position indicator that resolves mandibular position on the x (anteroposterior), y (superoinferior), and z (mediolateral shift) axes on an articulator.
- Reports a within-method repeatability ranking: bimanual most consistent, Gothic arch least consistent, chin-point guidance intermediate.
- Samples CR four times per subject at baseline, 1 hour, 1 day, and 1 week at approximately the same time of day, addressing short-term temporal repeatability.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: In-vivo method-comparison / repeatability study in healthy volunteers (not a patient-outcome trial).
- **Population**: 14 healthy adult volunteers (7 males, 7 females), mean age 26.61 +/- 4.20 years, with no history of extractions, temporomandibular joint dysfunction, or orthodontic treatment.
- **Mounting**: Accurate casts mounted on a Denar D4A articulator via facebow transfer and a maximum intercuspation silicone registration record.
- **Measurement**: A purpose-built mechanical 3D mandibular position indicator mounted on the articulator; three spatial axes — x (anteroposterior), y (superoinferior), z (mediolateral shift).
- **Recording schedule**: Each CR method recorded four times per subject — baseline, 1 hour, 1 day, and 1 week, at approximately the same time of day.
- **Data extraction**: Records transferred to the articulator; data read with a stereomicroscope modified to accept the mandibular position indicator.
- **Analysis**: F test used to identify the least-variable (most repeatable) method.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Within-subject variability range**: 0.03 mm (left-side z axis, bimanual method) to 1.6 mm (left-side y axis, Gothic arch method).
- **F-test comparison**: the bimanual method was the most consistent, showing between 10.11 (p = 1) and 0.438 (p = 0.005) times less variation than the Gothic arch method (the least consistent). Reproduced verbatim; the pairing of the larger ratio with p = 1 and the smaller with p = 0.005 is internally counterintuitive and is flagged, not corrected.
- **Ranking**: bimanual > chin-point guidance > Gothic arch for repeatability; the chin-point method's repeatability was "somewhere between the other 2 methods."
- **Conclusion (authors)**: of the three methods, bimanual manipulation positioned the condyles in the temporomandibular joint with more consistent repeatability, whereas Gothic arch was the least consistent.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only — full text not retrieved.** Only the structured abstract was available; no Methods-detail, Results tables, figures, or per-subject/per-axis data could be inspected.
- The abstract *does* report numeric results (variability range and F-test ratios), but does not report a full per-method × per-axis variability table, confidence intervals, or subject-level data; the two F-test ratio/p-value pairings are printed as given and cannot be resolved from the abstract.
- Small sample (n = 14) of healthy, dentate, non-TMD volunteers — no edentulous patients, so direct applicability to complete-denture prosthodontics is limited.
- Repeatability (consistency of repeated records) is not accuracy relative to an anatomic CR reference; the abstract makes no accuracy claim.
- Operator skill, jig-based deprogramming, and time-of-day standardization are not quantified in the abstract beyond "approximately the same time of day."

## 7. Glossary

- **Centric relation (CR)**: maxillomandibular relationship independent of tooth contact, used as a repeatable condylar reference for prosthetic jaw-relation records.
- **Bimanual manipulation**: guided CR technique using both operator hands to manipulate and stabilize the mandible, here with an anterior jig.
- **Chin-point guidance**: guided CR technique guiding the mandible from the chin with one hand, here with a jig.
- **Gothic-arch tracing**: graphic CR method recording mandibular border movements to locate the arrowhead/apex position.
- **Mandibular position indicator**: mechanical device mounted on an articulator that transduces mandibular position into three spatial axes (x, y, z).
- **F test**: statistical test comparing variances across methods; used here to identify the least-variable (most repeatable) CR method.
