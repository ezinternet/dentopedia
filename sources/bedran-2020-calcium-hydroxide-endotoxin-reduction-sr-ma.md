## Why Ingested

이 endotoxin 배치에서 calcium hydroxide(CH) intracanal medication의 LPS 감소 효과를 정량화하는 핵심 SR+MA. [[endodontics/irrigation/ordinola-zapata-2022-intracanal-medicaments-status-directions]]의 CH 한계 서술과 [[endodontics/irrigation/rossi-fedele-2023-irrigation-dressing-apical-periodontitis-sr-ma]]의 medicament 효과 평가를 endotoxin endpoint로 확장하며, "CH는 endotoxin을 감소시키나 완전 제거는 못 한다"는 배치 thesis의 정량적 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (J Endod 2020; 9 studies qualitative, 7 meta-analyzed) examining whether calcium hydroxide (Ca[OH]₂) intracanal medication reduces endotoxin/LPS in infected root canals, with subgroup analysis by formulation, irrigant, and presence of added antimicrobial substance.

Ca(OH)₂ significantly reduced LPS both before CMP (SMD −1.087, 95% CI −1.453 to −0.721, p<0.001, I²=58.7%) and after CMP (SMD −0.919, 95% CI −1.156 to −0.682, p<0.001, I²=24.7%); adding an antimicrobial substance raised the incidence of LPS reduction from 61.7% to 98.9%.

Ca(OH)₂ does not eliminate endotoxin completely regardless of irrigant used, certainty of evidence is very low due to heterogeneous formulations and mostly pre-post designs without control groups.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA(J Endod 2020; 정성 9편, 메타 7편) — 감염 근관에서 수산화칼슘(CH) 근관내 첩약의 LPS/endotoxin 감소 효과를 항균제 첨가 유무·세정액 종류별 서브그룹으로 분석.

CH는 화학기계적 성형 전후 모두 LPS를 유의하게 감소(SMD −1.09 / −0.92, p<0.001)시켰으며, 항균제 첨가 시 LPS 감소 발생률 98.9% vs 미첨가 61.7%.

그러나 세정액과 무관하게 LPS를 완전 제거하지 못하고, 근거 확실성은 매우 낮아(GRADE) — CH를 "endotoxin 제거 도구"로 과신해서는 안 됨.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Does Calcium Hydroxide Reduce Endotoxins in Infected Root Canals? Systematic Review and Meta-analysis
- **Authors**: Natália Rocha Bedran, Patricia Nadelman, Marcela Baraúna Magno, Andréa Vaz Braga Pintor, Lucianne Cople Maia, Laura Guimarães Primo (UFRJ, Brazil)
- **Journal**: Journal of Endodontics 2020;46(11):1545–1558
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.joen.2020.07.030 (locator: J Endod 2020;46(11):1545-1558; S0099-2399(20)30582-3)
- **Study type**: Systematic review + meta-analysis

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies the LPS-reduction effect of calcium hydroxide (Ca[OH]₂) as intracanal medication (ICM), with and without an added antimicrobial substance (AS).
- Subgroup analysis by Ca(OH)₂ formulation, presence of AS, irrigant solution during chemomechanical preparation (CMP), and incidence of LPS reduction.
- GRADE certainty assessment.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Searches up to June 2020. Included clinical and experimental studies comparing LPS amount before vs after Ca(OH)₂ ICM in infected root canals.
- Risk of bias: RoB 2 / ROBINS-I / NIH pre-post tool. 9 studies qualitative, 7 in meta-analysis (3 low RB, 1 moderate, 2 high, 3 "some concerns").
- Random-effects meta-analysis of standardized mean difference (SMD); GRADE for certainty.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Ca(OH)₂ (±AS) reduced mean LPS **before CMP**: SMD = −1.087 (CI −1.453 to −0.721), p<0.001, I²=58.7%.
- **After CMP**: SMD = −0.919 (CI −1.156 to −0.682), p<0.001, I²=24.7%.
- By irrigant: reduction before CMP SMD = −1.053 (CI −1.311 to −0.795); after CMP SMD = −0.938 (CI −1.147 to −0.729).
- Incidence of LPS reduction: 98.9% with AS vs 61.7% without AS.
- Conclusion: Ca(OH)₂ reduces endotoxin but cannot eliminate LPS completely, independent of irrigant; **very low** certainty of evidence.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Very low GRADE certainty; heterogeneity in formulations, irrigants, and LPS quantification.
- Few low-RB studies; mostly pre-post designs without control group.
- Cannot isolate Ca(OH)₂ effect from concurrent CMP/irrigation.

## 7. Glossary

- **ICM**: intracanal medication.
- **CMP**: chemomechanical preparation.
- **AS**: antimicrobial substance added to Ca(OH)₂ (e.g., chlorhexidine).
- **SMD**: standardized mean difference.
- **LPS / endotoxin**: lipopolysaccharide, Gram-negative virulence factor; LAL-assay quantified.
