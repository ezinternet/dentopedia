## Why Ingested
Prospective clinical study (12 months, 571 ProTaper Next files) quantifying fracture/deformation rates by file size in specialist endodontic practice. Directly extends [[endodontics/shaping/plotino-2019-protaper-next-cyclic-fatigue]] and [[endodontics/shaping/bui-2018-protaper-next-cyclic-fatigue]] (in-vitro cyclic fatigue) with real-world clinical data, and informs single-use recommendations for smaller files (X1 17.04).

## Three-line Summary
Prospective 12-month cohort study in a university endodontics clinic; 571 ProTaper Next (M-Wire) files discarded after clinical use by 6 specialist endodontists using X-Smart motors (300 rpm, 4-5.2 Ncm).
Overall defect rate 13.83% (fracture 7.53%, unwinding/curving remainder); X1 17.04 files had 19.87% fracture rate (32/161) and 31.67% total defects; cyclic fatigue accounted for 79.07% of fractures.
Smaller ProTaper Next files (especially X1 17.04) show high clinical fracture/deformation rates supporting single-use policy; all files should be inspected at ≥10× magnification before each use.

## 세줄요약
전향적 12개월 코호트 연구로 대학병원 치수치료과에서 6명의 전문의가 사용 후 폐기한 571개 ProTaper Next (M-Wire) 파일을 분석함.
전체 결함률 13.83% (파절 7.53%, 언와인딩/굴곡 등 나머지); X1 17.04 파일이 파절률 19.87% (32/161), 총 결함률 31.67%로 가장 높았고, 파절의 79.07%가 순환 피로(cyclic fatigue) 기전.
작은 ProTaper Next 파일(특히 X1 17.04)은 임상 파절/변형률이 높아 일회용 사용 원칙 권장; 모든 파일은 사용 전 ≥10배 확대경 검사 필수.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical and Experimental Dentistry 2018;10(11):e1120-e1125
- **DOI**: 10.4317/jced.54910
- **Institution**: University of Santiago de Compostela, Spain

## 2. Key Contributions
- First prospective clinical study quantifying ProTaper Next fracture/deformation rates by file size after real-world use
- X1 17.04 files: 19.87% fracture rate, 31.67% total defect rate — substantially higher than larger files
- Cyclic fatigue (flexural fatigue) is the dominant fracture mechanism (79.07%) in clinical use
- Fragment sizes 1.29–4.27 mm (from original 25 mm); smaller fragments may go undetected without magnification
- Evidence-based support for single-use recommendation for smaller ProTaper Next instruments

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective observational cohort study over 12 months
- **Setting**: Exclusive endodontics clinic, University of Santiago de Compostela
- **n**: 571 ProTaper Next files collected after clinical use
- **Operators**: 6 endodontists trained in ProTaper Next protocol
- **Equipment**: X-Smart motor (300 rpm, torque 4-5.2 Ncm)
- **Protocol**: Glide path with K-files #08, 10, 15, 20; preparation started with X1 (17.04) and finished with file matching apical gauging; all instruments used at full working length; irrigation with 5.25% NaOCl and 17% EDTA
- **Usage limits**: Max 2 molars with wide canals per file; curved/narrow/calcified canals — discard after each molar
- **Inspection**: Stereomicroscope examination before each use; length measured with digital caliper; defects classified as fracture, unwinding, curving/bending

- **Total defects**: 13.83% of all discarded files (79/571)
- **Fracture type** (stereomicroscope analysis of 43 fractured files):
  - Flexural fatigue (cyclic fatigue): 79.07% (34 files)
  - Torsional: 20.93% (9 files)
- **Fragment size**: 1.29–4.27 mm (original length 25 mm); cyclic fatigue fragments generally larger
- **Fracture location**: Mostly apical third
- **p-values**: Chi-square and z-test used (exact p-values not reported in abstract)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single-center study with 6 specialist operators — limited generalizability to general practitioners
- ProTaper Next (M-Wire alloy) only — not generalizable to other file systems/alloys
- Survivor bias: only discarded files analyzed; files not discarded (survived) not included
- No denominator of total files used (only discarded files collected)
- 12-month collection period may not capture long-term fatigue accumulation patterns
- Exact number of uses per file not tracked (only maximum recommended limits)

## 6. Related Work
- **Plotino et al. 2019** ([[endodontics/shaping/plotino-2019-protaper-next-cyclic-fatigue]]): In-vitro cyclic fatigue comparison; ProTaper Next superior to WaveOne Gold and ProTaper Universal
- **Bui et al. 2018** ([[endodontics/shaping/bui-2018-protaper-next-cyclic-fatigue]]): In-vitro cyclic fatigue resistance of ProTaper Next vs other systems
- **Al-Hadlaq et al. 2010**: M-Wire alloy has better cyclic fatigue resistance than conventional NiTi
- **Shen et al. 2009**: Small files should be single-use
- **Inan et al.**: Mtwo study showing smaller instruments more prone to distortion/separation

## 7. Glossary
- **ProTaper Next (프로테이퍼 넥스트, PTN)**: M-Wire 열처리 니켈-티타늄 합금 회전 파일 시스템; 비대칭 단면(swaggered cross-section) 설계
- **M-Wire (M-와이어)**: 열처리로 탄성 한계와 순환 피로 저항성 향상시킨 NiTi 합금
- **Cyclic fatigue (순환 피로, 굴곡 피로)**: 반복적 굽힘 응력으로 인한 점진적 손상 후 파절; 임상 파절의 주된 기전
- **Torsional fracture (비틀림 파절)**: 회전 저항이 토크 한계를 초과할 때 발생하는 파절
- **Unwinding (언와인딩, 풀림)**: 파일 나선 구조의 풀림 변형; 파절 전 선행 현상
- **Glide path (글라이드 패스, 근관 경로 확보)**: 초기 손파일(#08-20)로 근관 경로 형성 후 회전 파일 진입
- **Working length (작업 길이, WL)**: 근관장까지 파일 진입 길이; 모든 파일이 full working length 사용됨
