## Three-line Summary

Scoping review of 28 systematic reviews on immediate implant placement (IIP) and immediate restoration (IR) in single- and multiple-unit fixed dental prostheses, searched across PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane; abstract only (full text not retrieved).

Most reviews addressed anatomical, surgical, and prosthetic factors for single-unit IIP/IR in the esthetic zone, with limited evidence for molars or multiunit prostheses; key anatomical factors were buccal wall integrity, alveolar process dimensions, buccal gap width, and gingival phenotype.

The evidence map identifies where IIP case-selection criteria are consolidated (anterior single units) and where they remain thin (posterior/molars, multiunit FDPs) — a gap the current wiki's molar-septum and full-arch pages partially fill.

대부분의 고찰은 심미부 단일치 IIP/IR의 해부학적·술적·보철적 인자를 다루었고, 구치부나 다장치 보철 근거는 제한적이었음; 핵심 해부학 인자는 협측 벽 온전성, 치조돌기 크기, 협측 간극 폭, 치은 표현형임.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Maps the evidence base for IIP and immediate restoration across single-unit and multiple-unit fixed dental prostheses (FDPs).
- Separates **anatomical, surgical, and prosthetic** factor domains as the organizing axes for IIP/IR case selection.
- Quantifies the scope: 28 systematic reviews included, with bibliometric data and study details extracted.
- Flags the **evidence gap**: molar and multiunit-prosthesis IIP/IR is under-represented relative to anterior single-unit esthetic-zone evidence.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Electronic searches in PubMed, Embase, and Cochrane for systematic reviews on IIP and IR in anterior and posterior jaw regions.
- Bibliometric data and study details extracted from included reviews; no new primary data or pooled effect estimates (scoping design).
- This is one of the companion evidence reviews behind the ORF IEM 2025 consensus on immediacy (PMID 40900553), alongside the full-arch narrative review (PMID 40900555).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **28 systematic reviews** included.
- **Key anatomical factors** reported: buccal wall integrity, alveolar process dimensions, buccal gap width, gingival phenotype.
- **Surgical factors** commonly addressed: flapless vs flapped procedures, implant positioning (abstract truncated beyond this point).
- **Coverage skew**: most reviews focused on single-unit IIP/IR in the esthetic zone; limited evidence for molars or multiunit prostheses.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest: full text, complete results tables, and the review's full factor taxonomy were not available, so no quantitative synthesis can be reported here.
- Scoping design maps evidence rather than grading it; it does not itself establish effect sizes or certainty.
- The authors explicitly note limited evidence for molars and multiunit prostheses, which constrains use as a posterior/multiunit criteria guide.

## 7. Glossary

- **IIP** — Immediate Implant Placement (즉시식립).
- **IR** — Immediate Restoration (즉시수복).
- **FDP** — Fixed Dental Prosthesis (고정성 보철물).
- **Scoping review** — 범위고찰; maps the breadth/type of evidence without pooled effect estimation.
- **Buccal gap width** — the horizontal discrepancy between implant and buccal socket wall (협측 간극).
