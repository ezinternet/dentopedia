## Why Ingested

ZLS (지르코니아 강화 리튬실리케이트, Zirconia-reinforced Lithium Silicate) 세라믹(Vita Suprinity)의 소성 횟수 및 산 에칭 조건이 자가접착 복합레진시멘트(TheraCem)의 인장결합강도(Tensile Bond Strength, TBS)에 미치는 영향을 정량 평가한 연구. ZLS 세라믹의 접합 최적화 프로토콜 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

A controlled in-vitro study assessed how firing cycle number and HF etching parameters (duration and concentration) affect TBS of TheraCem self-adhesive composite cement to Vita Suprinity ZLS ceramic. Etching duration (45 s optimal, 4.83 MPa peak) and firing cycles (1 cycle highest at 4.00 MPa; as-machined lowest at 3.29 MPa) significantly affected TBS, while etchant concentration (p = 0.31) did not. Clinicians should prioritize adequate etching duration over concentration when cementing ZLS restorations with TheraCem.

## 세줄요약

Vita Suprinity ZLS 세라믹에 대한 TheraCem 자가접착 복합레진시멘트의 TBS를 소성 횟수·HF 에칭 조건별로 정량 비교한 시험관 연구. 에칭 시간(45초 최고 4.83 MPa)과 소성 횟수(1회 최고 4.00 MPa; 비소성 최저 3.29 MPa)는 TBS에 유의미한 영향을 미쳤으나, 에칭 농도(p = 0.31)는 유의미하지 않았다. ZLS 수복물 접착 시 에칭 시간이 농도보다 더 중요한 변수임을 시사한다.

## Key Contributions

1. Quantified TBS of TheraCem to ZLS under a matrix of firing cycle × etching conditions
2. Demonstrated etching duration (not concentration) is the critical variable for TBS
3. Showed 1 firing cycle outperforms multiple cycles or no firing for TBS
4. Established 45 s as the peak etching duration for TheraCem–Suprinity bond

## Methodology

- **Specimens**: 53 Vita Suprinity discs (12.5 × 14 × 2 mm)
- **Cement**: TheraCem (self-adhesive composite cement; BeautiCem SA equivalent protocol)
- **Variables**:
  - Firing cycles: as-machined (0), 1, 2, 3 cycles
  - Etching duration: 20, 45, 60 s (HF acid)
  - Etchant concentration: varied levels (specific concentrations not stated in abstract)
- **Bonded substrate**: titanium rods (for tensile loading)
- **Test**: Tensile bond strength (TBS)
- **Statistics**: Least-square means linear regression + Tukey HSD post-hoc test (α = 0.05)

## Key Results

| Variable | Condition | TBS (MPa) |
|---|---|---|
| Etching duration | 45 s (peak) | 4.83 |
| Etching duration | 20 s (lowest) | 3.02 ± 0.28 |
| Firing cycles | 1 cycle (highest) | 4.00 ± 0.71 |
| Firing cycles | As-machined / 0 (lowest) | 3.29 ± 0.53 |

- Etching duration: p < 0.001 (significant)
- Firing cycles: p < 0.001 (significant)
- Etchant concentration: p = 0.31 (not significant)

## Limitations

- Abstract-only source — exact HF concentrations, etching agent brand, and surface pretreatment details not available
- Single cement (TheraCem); results may not generalize to other adhesive/resin cements
- In-vitro titanium-rod bonding setup does not replicate intraoral dentin/enamel substrate
- No aging or thermocycling reported in the abstract
- n = 53 total specimens across multiple subgroups limits per-cell power

## Glossary

- **ZLS (지르코니아 강화 리튬실리케이트, Zirconia-reinforced Lithium Silicate)**: A glass-ceramic incorporating ZrO₂ for enhanced strength; CAD/CAM milled then crystallization-fired
- **TBS (인장결합강도, Tensile Bond Strength)**: Force per unit area to separate bonded interfaces in tension
- **TheraCem**: Self-adhesive composite resin cement (BISCO); no separate bonding agent required
- **Firing cycle (소성 주기)**: Crystallization heat-treatment step for CAD/CAM glass ceramics
- **HF etching (불산 에칭, Hydrofluoric Acid Etching)**: Selective dissolution of the glassy phase to create micro-roughness for micromechanical interlocking
