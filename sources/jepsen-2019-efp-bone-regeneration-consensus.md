## Why Ingested

Foundation EFP consensus (15th European Workshop on Periodontology, Group 4) establishing evidence-based thresholds for lateral GBR, vertical ridge augmentation, peri-implantitis GBR, and sinus floor augmentation — provides the evidentiary backbone for the bone-regeneration category alongside the ITI-GBR and Global Consensus frameworks ([[wiki/bone-regeneration/cucchi-2019-iti-gbr-consensus]], [[wiki/bone-regeneration/brunello-2026-global-consensus-maintenance]]).

## Three-line Summary

Consensus statement from 20 European experts (EFP 15th Workshop, 2019) synthesizing four systematic reviews on alveolar ridge defect regeneration — lateral/vertical bone augmentation, peri-implantitis bone defects, and lateral window sinus augmentation.
Lateral bone augmentation before implant placement is predictable for ridge width gain; vertical ridge augmentation is effective but carries high complication rates; peri-implantitis defect reconstruction shows radiographic but not clinical benefit in few RCTs; lateral window sinus augmentation is reliable long-term.
All four procedures are effective for ridge reconstruction, but demanding techniques and complication risk must be weighed against patient needs.

## 세줄요약

유럽치주학회 (European Federation of Periodontology, EFP) 15차 워크샵 그룹4가 골증대술 4가지 영역을 종합한 컨센서스 성명 (20인 전문가, 체계적 문헌고찰 4편 기반).
수평 골증대술 (Lateral Bone Augmentation, LBA)은 임플란트 전 예지성 있는 술식; 수직 골증대술 (Vertical Ridge Augmentation, VRA)은 효과적이나 합병증 발생률이 높음; 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)로 치료한 치주염성 골결손은 방사선학적 이득만 확인; 측방접근 상악동 거상 (Lateral Window Sinus Floor Augmentation, LWSFA)은 장기적으로 신뢰 가능.
네 가지 술식 모두 효과가 입증됐으나 기술적 난이도와 합병증 위험이 높다.

## Key Contributions

- Synthesizes four systematic reviews (lateral GBR, vertical GBR, peri-implantitis GBR, sinus augmentation) into a single consensus document
- Confirms lateral bone augmentation (prior to implant) as a predictable, evidence-based procedure for horizontal deficiency
- Identifies high complication rates as the primary caveat for vertical ridge augmentation
- Highlights the scarcity of RCTs on reconstructive peri-implantitis management; radiographic bone gain supported but not probing depth/clinical attachment changes
- Validates lateral window sinus floor augmentation as reliable long-term for edentulous maxilla

## Methodology

- **Type**: Consensus statement based on four systematic reviews
- **Workshop**: 15th European Workshop on Periodontology, Group 4
- **Topics covered**:
  1. Lateral bone augmentation with implant placement at a later stage
  2. Vertical bone augmentation
  3. Reconstructive treatment of peri-implantitis-associated defects
  4. Long-term results of lateral window sinus augmentation
- **Evidence synthesis**: Expert discussions + systematic review evidence; no individual patient data

## Key Results

- Lateral bone augmentation: predictable for ridge width gain prior to implant placement (substantial body of evidence)
- Vertical ridge augmentation: effective in many studies for deficient ridge treatment, but **high complication rates**
- Peri-implantitis GBR: meta-analyses show radiographic bone gain benefit; **no significant benefit for clinical outcomes** (probing depth, CAL)
- Lateral window sinus floor augmentation: **reliable long-term** procedure for partially and fully edentulous maxilla

## Limitations

- Consensus-level evidence limited by quality/quantity of primary studies available (2016–2018 literature base)
- Peri-implantitis RCT evidence sparse at time of writing
- Long-term sinus data based on retrospective/observational designs
- Expert consensus can introduce opinion bias despite systematic review foundation

## Glossary

- **LBA (Lateral Bone Augmentation)**: Horizontal ridge widening procedure using GBR membranes ± graft materials prior to or simultaneous with implant placement
- **VRA (Vertical Ridge Augmentation)**: Procedures to increase vertical bone height, including block grafts, GBR with rigid membranes (d-PTFE/Ti-mesh)
- **EFP (European Federation of Periodontology)**: European professional body for periodontology hosting the European Workshop on Periodontology
- **Lateral window sinus floor augmentation**: Sinus lift via lateral bony window approach to elevate Schneiderian membrane and place graft material
- **Peri-implantitis GBR**: Use of barrier membranes ± graft to reconstruct bone defects around failing implants
