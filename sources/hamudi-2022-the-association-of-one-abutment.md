## Why Ingested

Medium-term (3-year) randomised clinical evidence for the definitive-abutment protocol on platform-switched conical implants — one of the seven RCTs pooled in [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]] (listed there as "Hamudi 2021", 42 implants, 0.35 vs 0.57 mm, p=0.005), but with its own full primary report and its own explicit caution that the difference is clinically doubtful. That caution is the counterweight to the stronger single-study signals in the same meta-analysis.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective randomised clinical study of 21 patients receiving two adjacent non-submerged platform-switched conical implants; definitive multiunit abutments connected at surgery and never removed (DEF) versus healing abutments disconnected/reconnected 3–5 times (HEA), followed for 3 years.

Survival and success were 100% with no bone loss >1.9 mm; 3-year marginal bone loss was −0.35 ± 0.69 mm (DEF) versus −0.57 ± 0.80 mm (HEA), a statistically significant difference, while peri-implant mucositis occurred in 38.1% (DEF) and 41.9% (HEA).

Immediate definitive-abutment connection reduced bone loss after repeated disconnections, but the authors judged the between-group difference minimal and its clinical relevance doubtful.

## 세줄요약

전향적 무작위 임상연구(환자 21명, 인접 비매몰 플랫폼 스위칭 원뿔 연결 임플란트 2개씩): 수술 시 장착 후 탈착하지 않은 최종 다Units 지대주(DEF) vs 치유 지대주를 보철 단계에서 3~5회 탈착·재장착(HEA), 3년 추적.

생존율·성공률 100%, 1.9 mm 초과 골소실 없음; 3년 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 DEF −0.35 ± 0.69 mm vs HEA −0.57 ± 0.80 mm로 유의한 차이, 임플란트주위점막염(Peri-implant Mucositis)은 DEF 38.1% vs HEA 41.9%.

즉시 최종 지대주 장착이 반복 탈착보다 골소실을 줄였으나, 저자는 군 간 차이가 작아 임상적 유의성은 의심스럽다고 명시적으로 유보.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: J Clin Med. 2022;11:74 (published online 24 Dec 2021)
- **DOI**: 10.3390/jcm11010074
- **Institution**: Tel Aviv University, Goldschleger School of Dental Medicine, Israel
- **Study type**: Prospective randomised clinical study (split-mouth allocation of two adjacent implants)

## 2. Key Contributions
- 3-year medium-term follow-up in a literature dominated by 6–12 month OAOT studies.
- Platform-switched conical-connection implants only — the exact niche later isolated by the 2026 SR+MA.
- Quantifies mucositis as well as MBL: 38.1% vs 41.9% at 3 years, no significant separation.
- Authors' own conclusion explicitly dampens the clinical importance of a statistically significant 0.22 mm MBL difference.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective clinical study; consecutive patients treated 2015–2017 by two experienced operators; each patient's two adjacent implants randomly assigned to DEF or HEA (within-patient comparison).
- **Population**: 21 patients (6 men, 15 women; mean age 66.23 ± 9.35 at placement); ≥21 years, full-mouth plaque score <25%, treated/stable periodontitis, ≥8 mm length and ≥3.75 mm width, ≥2 mm buccal/lingual wall, ≥2 mm keratinized gingiva.
- **Outcome**: Periapical radiographic marginal bone level at baseline (surgery), 4–7 months (prosthetic delivery), 1 year and 3 years; plus survival, success, mucositis.
- **Groups**: DEF = definitive multiunit abutments never removed; HEA = healing abutments disconnected and reconnected 3–5 times during the prosthetic phase.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome at 3 years | DEF (never removed) | HEA (3–5 disconnections) | Comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marginal bone level | −0.35 ± 0.69 mm | −0.57 ± 0.80 mm | significant difference |
| Implant survival / success | 100% / 100% | 100% / 100% | no implant lost, no bone loss >1.9 mm |
| Peri-implant mucositis | 38.1% | 41.9% | — |

- Cross-check: [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]] tabulates this trial as "Hamudi 2021" with 42 implants, DEF 0.35 mm vs HEA 0.57 mm and p=0.005; the primary paper's abstract reports only "significant statistical difference" without a numeric p-value.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (21 patients / 42 implants) from a two-operator single-centre setting.
- Paper describes itself variously as a "prospective clinical study" and "prospective clinical study with random assignment"; randomisation is at the two-implant patient level, so independence is limited.
- 3-year endpoint still short for peri-implantitis incidence; mucositis rates were high in both arms.
- Authors acknowledge the 0.22 mm difference is of doubtful clinical relevance — statistical ≠ clinical significance.

## 7. Glossary
- **DEF (Definitive multiunit abutment)**: final abutment connected at surgery and left in place.
- **HEA (Healing Abutment)**: provisional transmucosal component removed/reconnected during prosthetic procedures.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: peri-implant crestal bone level change measured on periapical radiographs.
- **PS (Platform Switching)**: diameter-reduced abutment relative to the implant platform.
