## Why Ingested

[[tmj/valesan-2021-tmd-prevalence-sr-ma]] gives TMD prevalence (painful TMD ~11–13%) but does not split orofacial pain by ICOP-type origin. This 2026 meta-analysis adds pooled prevalence for myofascial, TMJ, dentoalveolar and cranial-nerve orofacial pain, giving the epidemiological denominator for the TMD pain treatment pages.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of observational studies (MEDLINE and Scopus, 2004–2024) estimating global prevalence of orofacial pain by ICOP-type origin.

Pooled prevalence: myofascial pain 20.60%, myofascial plus TMJ pain 11.95% (31 studies, 522,056 observations), TMJ pain alone 9.51%, dentoalveolar 27.46%, cranial nerve 7.98%.

Substantial methodological heterogeneity and inability to apply ICOP directly to the included studies mean estimates need cautious interpretation.

## 세줄요약

관찰연구 대상 SR+MA (MEDLINE, Scopus, 2004–2024)로 구안면통증 국제분류 (International Classification of Orofacial Pain, ICOP) 기준 기원별 세계 유병률을 추정.

통합 유병률: 근막 구안면통증 20.60%, 근막통+측두하악관절 (Temporomandibular Joint, TMJ) 통증 11.95% (31편, 522,056건), TMJ 통증 단독 9.51%, 치조·관련구조 27.46%, 뇌신경 7.98%.

연구 간 방법론적 이질성이 크고 ICOP을 직접 적용하지 못해 해석에 주의 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled global prevalence for five orofacial pain categories mapped to ICOP-type origin.
- Shows ICOP could not be applied directly to existing epidemiologic literature; calls for standardized criteria and reporting guidelines.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Systematic search of MEDLINE and Scopus for observational studies published 2004–2024. Pooled prevalence with 95% CI. Details beyond the abstract (risk-of-bias tool, model, registration) not retrieved.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Category | Pooled prevalence (95% CI) | Studies / observations |
|---|---|---|
| Myofascial orofacial pain | 20.60% (9.71–38.50) | 5 / 3,395 |
| Myofascial + TMJ pain | 11.95% (8.85–15.96) | 31 / 522,056 |
| TMJ pain alone | 9.51% (6.01–14.73) | 13 / 21,407 |
| Dentoalveolar and related structures | 27.46% (22.83–32.63) | 66 / 548,782 |
| Cranial nerve lesions or diseases | 7.98% (3.28–18.18) | 2 / 9,735 |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Substantial methodological heterogeneity across studies; ICOP classification not directly applicable; myofascial (5 studies) and cranial nerve (2 studies) estimates rest on few studies with wide CIs. Authors call for standardized criteria and reporting guidelines.
