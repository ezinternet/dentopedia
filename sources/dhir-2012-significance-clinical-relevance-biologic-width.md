## Why Ingested

Provides the classic peri-implant biologic-width framework that underpins many marginal-bone pages: Gargiulo 2.04 mm baseline, peri-implant measurements (Glauser, Kan), microgap-driven bone loss (Hermann), Ericsson's ICT concept, and the mechanical rationale of platform switching. Complements [[wiki/implants/mbl/salimi-2011-platform-switching-current-results]] and [[wiki/implants/mbl/gupta-2019-platform-switching-crestal-bone-loss-sr]] on the mechanism side, and links the tooth-side biologic width concept ([[wiki/periodontics/hamasni-2017-biologic-width-restorative-dentistry]], [[wiki/periodontics/padilla-avallos-2026-supracrestal-tissue-attachment-biologic-width]]) to implant dentistry.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (J Interdiscip Dent 2012;2(2):84–91) of the biologic width concept around dental implants: structure, function, influencing factors, and its role in crestal bone loss and surgical technique choice.

Around implants the biologic width is larger than around natural teeth (two-piece > one-piece > natural teeth); measured 4–4.5 mm (Glauser, one-piece mini implants) and 6.17/3.63/5.93 mm mesial/midfacial/distal (Kan, bone sounding); the implant–abutment microgap location (~2 mm below crest) is the main determinant of crestal bone level (Hermann), with crest bone resorbing about 2 mm below the microgap.

Crestal bone loss factors: biologic seal formation (6 weeks), microgap/inflammatory cell infiltrate (1.5–2 mm resorption), occlusal overload (progressive), crest module design; platform switching horizontally repositions the biologic width (min 3 mm soft tissue for seal) and confines the ICT <90°.

## 세줄요약

치아 주위 생물학적 폭경(Gargiulo 2.04 mm) 개념을 임플란트로 확장한 내러티브 리뷰 (J Interdiscip Dent 2012): 구조·기능·영향인자·변연골소실 기전·수술법 선택.

임플란트 주위 생물학적 폭경은 치아보다 큼 (2-piece > 1-piece > 치아); 실측 Glauser 4–4.5 mm (1-piece mini), Kan bone sounding 6.17/3.63/5.93 mm (근심/협측/원심); 미세극(microgap) 위치(~치조정 하방 2 mm)가 골높이 결정 (Hermann), 미세극 하방 약 2 mm까지 골흡수.

변연골소실 인자: 생물학적 봉쇄 형성(6주), 미세극·염증세포침윤(1.5–2 mm 흡수), 교합과부하(진행성), crest module 설계; 플랫폼 스위칭은 생물학적 폭경을 수평 재배치(연조직 3 mm 최소)해 ICT를 <90°로 국한.
