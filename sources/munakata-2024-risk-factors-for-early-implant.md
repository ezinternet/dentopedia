## Why Ingested

조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure)를 장기 생존과 다른 기전("골유착 획득 실패")으로 규정하고, 골이식 술식(치조제 보존, 치조제 증대, 상악동 거상)별 위험인자와 이식재 선택을 정리한 서술적 고찰이다. 이번 조기 실패 배치에서 기존 코호트·SR/MA 수치를 하나의 틀로 묶는 해석 프레임을 제공한다. → [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review arguing that early implant failure (variously defined as loss before the superstructure, within 6 months, or up to 1 year after prosthesis) is a failure to achieve osseointegration with different risk factors from late failure, and that most reported early-failure risk factors are bone augmentation related (technique, graft material, surgeon skill, postoperative complications) rather than systemic or implant-design factors.

Cited studies report early failure associated with bone grafting or GBR (e.g., OR 9.45 in the maxilla; OR 1.50 in a meta-analysis), surgeon skill (HR 5.13), sinus augmentation (86.1% vs 96.4% survival), and crestal sinus lift with membrane perforation; all numbers are quoted from other studies, since no systematic search or pooling was performed here.

The authors conclude that graft material should be chosen by new bone formation and residual graft rates rather than CBCT appearance, and that non-resorbable xenograft (especially bovine bone) should not be used alone under loading, with large thread exposure, or in sinus augmentation, although they state they could not establish an appropriate graft material.

## 세줄요약

조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure; 보철물 장착 전, 식립 후 6개월, 또는 보철 후 1년 등 정의가 통일되지 않음)를 장기 실패와 다른 "골유착 획득 실패"로 보고, 보고된 위험인자 다수가 전신질환이나 임플란트 설계가 아니라 골이식 관련 요인(술식, 이식재, 술자 숙련도, 술후 합병증)이라고 논증하는 서술적 고찰이다.

인용된 연구들에서 조기 실패는 골이식·유도골재생술 (guided bone regeneration, GBR) (상악 OR 9.45, 메타분석 OR 1.50), 술자 숙련도 (HR 5.13), 상악동 거상 (생존율 86.1% 대 96.4%), 치조정 접근 상악동 거상의 막 천공과 연관되었으며, 모든 수치는 타 연구의 인용이고 자체적인 체계적 검색이나 통합 분석은 없다.

저자들은 CBCT 소견이 아니라 신생골 형성률과 잔존 이식재 비율로 이식재를 선택해야 하며, 비흡수성 이종골(특히 소 유래 골)을 단독으로 하중 부위, 큰 나사산 노출, 상악동 거상에 쓰지 말라고 결론짓지만, 적절한 이식재를 확립하지는 못했다고 스스로 밝힌다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Frames early implant failure as "failure to achieve osseointegration" (often with postoperative infection), distinct from late failure by peri-implantitis or overload.
- Notes that no universal definition exists; periods used include (1) placement to superstructure, (2) placement to 6 months, (3) placement to 1 year after superstructure.
- Collates early-failure risk factors from cited cohorts, with emphasis on bone augmentation, surgeon skill and postoperative complications.
- Reviews graft material behaviour (new bone formation, residual graft) for alveolar ridge preservation (ARP), ridge augmentation and sinus augmentation, and gives practical cautions on xenograft use.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Narrative review. No search strategy, databases, eligibility criteria, risk-of-bias appraisal, or PRISMA flow is described in the text.
- **Structure**: Introduction; early vs late failure and reimplantation; risk factors; relation with bone augmentation; ARP; ridge augmentation (vertical/horizontal); sinus augmentation (lateral/crestal); graft material characteristics; conclusions.
- **Early-failure definition used by the authors**: no single definition; three period types listed (see above). The cited studies each use their own window.

- **Authors' conclusions**: choose graft by NBF volume and residual graft rate histologically, not CBCT appearance; xenograft alone should not be used for ARP under load, ridge augmentation during waiting, large thread exposure (beyond about 2-3 mm), or sinus augmentation; healing period matters; they could not establish an appropriate bone graft material.
- **Practical suggestions in text**: avoid augmentation where possible (narrow implants, site change, angled placement); skills to convert crestal to lateral approach; consider lift amount (averaging 4-5 mm limit) not only residual height.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative design with no stated search or selection method, so selective citation cannot be excluded; evidence level rests on the cited studies' mix of designs and eras (1980s-2010s per the authors).
- The authors acknowledge that early-failure definitions differ and no consensus on risk factors exists; they also state they could not establish an appropriate graft material.
- Retrieved text lacks title, author list, date, tables and reference list; bracketed citations are empty, so attribution of each number to its original paper relies on author names in the sentences. Metadata here was taken from the task listing.
- Minor in-text inconsistencies: the review says "xenografts and alloplasts have the lowest survival rates after 7 months" for residual graft and later describes graft "survival rate" as residual graft proportion, which is an unusual use of "survival"; one sentence labels xenogeneic and homologous bone under "autogenous bone". Pesce quote "xenografts (7.30%)" for 6-month resorption duplicates the figure for existing bone volume with ARP (7.30 mm) in a different study; interpret with caution.
- Future work: understand the characteristics of new materials (carbonate apatite, calcium carbonate, calcium phosphate) before use.

## 7. Glossary

- **Early implant failure (조기 임플란트 실패)**: loss before or shortly after prosthesis; window varies by study.
- **ARP (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, 치조제 보존술)**: graft or other material placed in an extraction socket to limit resorption.
- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 유도골재생술)**: barrier-membrane-assisted bone augmentation.
- **NBF (New Bone Formation, 신생골 형성)** and **RG (Residual Graft, 잔존 이식재)**: histologic outcomes used to compare graft materials.
- **Crestal vs lateral approach (치조정 접근 / 측방 접근)**: two routes for sinus floor elevation.
