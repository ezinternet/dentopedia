## Why Ingested

Complements the existing maxilla-only SR+MA [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/xianggang-2026-predictability-maxillary-transverse-changes-invisalign]] by covering both maxillary and mandibular arches across a much larger evidence base (23 studies, 1391 patients vs 6/233). Also extends the single-cohort findings of [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/de-la-rosa-gay-2025-expansion-predictability-clear-aligner]] with pooled meta-analytic estimates and multivariable regression identifying independent predictors of success.

## One-line Summary

SR+MA (23 studies, 1391 patients) finding 75.5% maxillary and 80.6% mandibular transverse expansion predictability with Invisalign in permanent dentition, with younger age, SmartTrack (LD30) material, and mandibular arch as independent predictors of higher accuracy.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to evaluate Invisalign expansion predictability for both maxillary and mandibular arches simultaneously in permanent dentition
- Multivariable regression identifying three independent predictors: younger age (−1.1% pts/year, p=0.001), SmartTrack/LD30 material (+32.7% pts vs EX30, p<0.001), mandibular arch (+13.1% pts vs maxilla, p=0.001)
- Confirms expansion is predominantly buccal crown tipping: gingival-margin predictability ~13.5 percentage points lower than cusp-level, with gingival ~83% of cusp predictability
- Meta-regression showing recent publications report lower predictability (β=−0.150, p=0.010), possibly reflecting improved methodological rigor and larger samples
- GRADE very low certainty and significant publication bias documented (Egger p<0.001), with likely overestimation in pooled values

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020); PROSPERO registered
- **Search**: PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science; up to November 2025; no language restriction
- **Included studies**: 23 articles (20 retrospective, 3 prospective), 1391 patients; 21 eligible for meta-analysis
- **Inclusion (PICOS)**: Permanent dentition patients treated with Invisalign requiring transverse expansion; planned vs achieved expansion ratio reported quantitatively
- **Exclusion**: Mixed/primary dentition, syndromic patients, surgically assisted expansion, qualitative-only outcomes
- **Primary outcome**: Predictability = (achieved expansion / planned expansion) × 100; measured at cusp-tip and, in subset, gingival margin
- **Statistical methods**: Random-effects models with REML and Sidik–Jonkman adjustment; logit transformation of proportions; meta-regressions (publication year, age, arch, tooth type, material, change interval, planned/achieved expansion); multivariable regression; hierarchical clustering; Shapiro-Wilk normality; Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons
- **RoB assessment**: ROBINS-I v2; 4 Low, 15 Moderate, 4 Serious RoB

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Maxillary arch (average 75.5%):**
| Tooth group | Pooled predictability | 95% CI | I² |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canines (13–23) | 76% | 66–85% | Very high (I²=90.9%) |
| 1st premolars (14–24) | 81% | 73–87% | >84% |
| 2nd premolars (15–25) | 83% | 76–89% | >84% |
| 1st molars (16–26) | 78% | 67–87% | High |
| 2nd molars (17–27) | 78% | 22–93% | 94.7% |

**Mandibular arch (average 80.6%):**
| Tooth group | Pooled predictability | 95% CI | I² |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canines (33–43) | 86% | 74–93% | 84.6% |
| 1st premolars (34–44) | 86% | 69–95% | >86% |
| 2nd premolars (35–45) | 88% | 75–95% | >86% |
| 1st molars (36–46) | 87% | 68–96% | High |
| 2nd molars (37–47) | 73% | 12–98% | 90.2% |

**Meta-regression findings (significant):**
- Publication year: β=−0.150, p=0.010; R²=4.28% (more recent → lower predictability)
- Patient age: β=−0.069, p=0.016; R²=5.6% (~50% reduction in odds per decade)
- Achieved expansion magnitude: β=0.534, p=0.002, R²=7.18% (greater achieved → higher predictability)

**Multivariable model (R²=0.524, p<0.001):**
- Age: −1.1% pts/year (p=0.001)
- LD30 vs EX30: +32.7% pts (p<0.001)
- Mandible vs maxilla: +13.1% pts (p=0.001)
- Change interval and tooth type: not significant

**GRADE**: Very low certainty for both arches (downgraded for RoB and high heterogeneity)

**Publication bias**: Egger p<0.001; Begg p<0.001; funnel plot asymmetric — likely overestimation in pooled values

**95% Prediction intervals**: Very wide (~4–99% across tooth groups), indicating future cohorts may experience low to near-complete expression

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High between-study heterogeneity (I²>84% in most groups) limits generalizability of pooled estimates
- Most included studies retrospective; lack of randomized designs
- Several studies lacked standard deviations, requiring use of proportions and logit transformation
- Significant publication bias suggests underrepresented studies with lower predictability
- Age-related findings restricted to adult range; cannot extrapolate to growing patients
- Aligner-change interval findings inconclusive due to within-study variation in protocols
- Future need: prospective, pre-registered studies with uniform measurement protocols; CBCT-based alveolar boundary assessment for large planned expansions

## 6. Related Work

- Ma & Wang and Caruso et al.: anterior-to-posterior predictability gradient in maxillary arch
- Lin et al.: expansion feasible across broad adult age range within alveolar bone limits
- Condò et al. and Caruso et al.: LD30 (SmartTrack) superior elastic memory and force delivery vs EX30
- Karsli et al. and FEA studies: Invisalign primarily produces buccal crown tipping; stress concentrated at cervical crown third
- International Delphi consensus on clear aligner therapy: confirmed tipping-dominant mechanism and need for overcorrection

## 7. Glossary

- **LD30 / SmartTrack**: Invisalign aligner material introduced 2013; polyurethane-based with superior elastic memory and force control vs older EX30
- **EX30**: Older Invisalign aligner material (co-polyester); higher stiffness but greater stress relaxation
- **Logit transformation**: ln[p/(1−p)] applied to predictability proportions to stabilize variances for meta-analysis
- **ROBINS-I v2**: Risk of Bias In Non-randomized Studies of Interventions, version 2
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development and Evaluations; certainty of evidence framework
- **95% Prediction Interval (PI)**: Range expected to contain the true effect in a future study; much wider than CI due to between-study heterogeneity
- **Buccal crown tipping**: Rotation of the tooth crown outward around the cervical fulcrum without equivalent root movement; primary mechanism of Invisalign expansion
- **Bodily expansion**: Parallel translation of the entire tooth (crown + root); harder to achieve with aligners
- **ClinCheck**: Align Technology's proprietary software for digital planning of Invisalign treatment
