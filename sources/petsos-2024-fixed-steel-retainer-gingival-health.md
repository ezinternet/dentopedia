## Why Ingested

고정 스틸 유지장치가 치은 건강에 미치는 영향에 대한 RCT로, 교정 유지(retention) 단계에서의 치주 건강 근거를 제공하며, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/gazzani-2026-mixed-dentition-2year-stability]]의 유지 안정성 관점과 함께 유지단계 임상 결정에 활용.

## Three-line Summary

This RCT evaluated the effect of fixed steel retainers vs removable vacuum-formed retainers on gingival health in 37 orthodontically treated patients over 12 months. REC and PPD showed no significant differences between groups; 73.3% of fixed retainer patients maintained healthy gingiva at 12 months vs 88.2% in the control group. Gingival recessions were clinically non-relevant at all time points.

## 세줄요약

고정 스틸 유지장치 vs 제거식 진공형 유지장치가 하악 전치 치은 건강에 미치는 영향을 37명 환자에게 12개월간 추적한 RCT. REC와 PPD에서 그룹 간 유의한 차이 없었고, 고정 유지장치군 73.3%가 12개월 시점에서 치은 건강 유지(대조군 88.2%). 치은 퇴축은 모든 검사 시점에서 임상적으로 관련성 낮음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT directly comparing fixed steel vs removable vacuum-formed retainers on gingival health
- Demonstrates that fixed retainers maintain acceptable gingival health (73.3% healthy at 12 months)
- Gingival recessions remain clinically irrelevant with both retainer types

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized clinical controlled trial
- **n**: 37 patients (test: n=15, control: n=17)
- **Outcomes**: PPD, REC, BOP, plaque index, gingival index at BL, 6mo, 12mo

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- REC: no significant pairwise differences between groups
- PPD: no significant pairwise differences between groups
- Gingival health at 12mo: test 73.3%, control 88.2% (inter-group NS)
- Intra-group: control group BL→6mo improvement significant (p=0.043)
- Recessions: clinically non-relevant at all time points

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=37)
- Single-center study
- 12-month follow-up may be insufficient for long-term retainer effects
- Only mandibular anterior teeth evaluated
