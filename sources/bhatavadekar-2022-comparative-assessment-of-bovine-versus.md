## Why Ingested

The wiki's current porcine-vs-bovine xenograft statement is a pooled equivalence claim from [[wiki/bone-regeneration/wang-2025-porcine-bovine-xenograft-msfa-arp-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 10 studies, 452 sites), and that review explicitly excluded horizontal and vertical guided bone regeneration (GBR) to control methodological heterogeneity — so no head-to-head bovine/porcine evidence existed here for horizontal ridge augmentation (HRA). This RCT fills that gap with a same-indication bovine (Cerabone) vs porcine (MinerOss XP) comparison in HRA *and* extraction socket preservation (ESP), and it also gives an RCT-level, indication-specific read on the "porcine ≈ bovine" interchangeability stance taken in [[wiki/overviews/dbbm-bone-substitute-overview]], where porcine is the recommended tie-breaker for bovine-restricted (halal) patients.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized prospective cohort study (n=40; 10 per group) comparing bovine-derived xenograft (Cerabone) vs porcine-derived xenograft (MinerOss XP) across two indications — horizontal ridge augmentation (HRA) for veneer contour and extraction socket preservation (ESP) — with 6-month CBCT follow-up.

HRA: buccolingual width rose 5.43 ± 1.82 → 7.75 ± 1.91 mm (bovine) and 5.75 ± 1.64 → 8.75 ± 1.38 mm (porcine), with the abstract reporting P = .36 at baseline and P = .03 at 6 months; ESP: width fell 6.3 ± 1.26 → 5.8 ± 1.21 mm (bovine) and 6.74 ± 1.29 → 6.61 ± 1.56 mm (porcine), with P = .16 and P = .01 respectively, and the abstract states explicitly that significantly lower dimensional changes were noted in Group 4 (ESP + porcine) at 6 months.

Porcine xenograft behaved as a stable, biocompatible osteoconductive substitute and widens the clinician's graft options, but n=10 per group, no reported allocation concealment/blinding/sample-size basis, no histomorphometric values, product-specific comparison (Cerabone vs MinerOss XP) and abstract-only retrieval limit how far the claim can be pushed.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First head-to-head comparison of a **bovine-derived** (Cerabone) and a **porcine-derived** (MinerOss XP) xenograft across **two augmentation indications in one protocol** — horizontal ridge augmentation for veneer contour and extraction socket preservation — with a common 6-month CBCT measurement method.
- Supplies the **horizontal-augmentation** comparison that the wiki's porcine-vs-bovine SR+MA ([[wiki/bone-regeneration/wang-2025-porcine-bovine-xenograft-msfa-arp-sr-ma]]) deliberately excluded as a heterogeneity-control criterion, i.e. a gap that pooled evidence could not fill.
- Reports an **indication-dependent behaviour**: both materials gained width in HRA, whereas in ESP both lost width but the porcine group lost significantly less at 6 months — so "interchangeable" is not a single-claim statement across indications.
- Adds histologic core sampling from the ESP groups at 6 months, giving the study a histomorphometric dimension beyond the radiographic width comparison (values are not reported in the abstract).
- Authors' conclusion: porcine xenograft "serve[s] as a stable biocompatible osteoconductive bone substitute and expand[s] a clinician's choice of bone grafts in dental applications" — a species-alternative argument (e.g. for bovine-restricted patients) rather than a superiority claim.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design:** randomized prospective cohort study; PubMed indexes it as a Randomized Controlled Trial. Four parallel groups, 10 subjects each (40 total).
- **Groups:** Group 1 = HRA + bovine; Group 2 = HRA + porcine; Group 3 = ESP + bovine; Group 4 = ESP + porcine.
- **Indications:** horizontal ridge augmentation (HRA) for veneer contour; extraction socket preservation (ESP).
- **Follow-up:** 6 months.
- **Primary measurement:** buccolingual ridge width measured on cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) at baseline and at 6 months.
- **Histology:** representative histologic core samples taken from the ESP groups at 6 months.
- **Not reported in the abstract:** randomization sequence generation, allocation concealment, blinding of operator/assessor, sample-size calculation, statistical test used for each P value, baseline demographic/medical data, and whether the design was single- or multi-centre.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group (indication + material) | Baseline width (mm) | 6-month width (mm) | Change (mm, derived) | Abstract P values |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Group 1 — HRA + bovine (Cerabone) | 5.43 ± 1.82 | 7.75 ± 1.91 | +2.32 | P = .36 (baseline pair) / P = .03 (6-month pair) |
| Group 2 — HRA + porcine (MinerOss XP) | 5.75 ± 1.64 | 8.75 ± 1.38 | +3.00 | P = .36 (baseline pair) / P = .03 (6-month pair) |
| Group 3 — ESP + bovine (Cerabone) | 6.3 ± 1.26 | 5.8 ± 1.21 | −0.50 | P = .16 (baseline pair) / P = .01 (6-month pair) |
| Group 4 — ESP + porcine (MinerOss XP) | 6.74 ± 1.29 | 6.61 ± 1.56 | −0.13 | P = .16 (baseline pair) / P = .01 (6-month pair) |

- **Explicit abstract statement:** "Significantly lower dimensional changes were noted in Group 4 at 6 months" — i.e. the porcine ESP group showed the least dimensional change of the four groups.
- **HRA reading:** both materials produced a substantial horizontal width gain; the porcine group reached the larger absolute width at 6 months (8.75 vs 7.75 mm).
- **ESP reading:** both materials showed buccolingual width loss after 6 months; the porcine group retained more width (6.61 vs 5.8 mm).
- **Histomorphometry:** representative core samples from the ESP groups at 6 months were taken, but **no histomorphometric values are reported in the abstract** — new bone, residual graft and connective-tissue proportions cannot be quoted from this ingest.
- *Change values are simple arithmetic derived from the reported means; the P values are reproduced exactly as paired in the abstract, which does not label which comparison each P belongs to (between-group at that time point vs within-group change).*

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingestion** — full text not retrieved (closed access). Randomization method, allocation concealment, blinding, sample-size basis, exact statistical tests, baseline characteristics, implant outcomes and complication data are all unavailable.
- **Small groups** — 10 subjects per group, 40 overall; the ESP comparison rests on two 10-subject arms, so the reported between-group difference in dimensional change is statistically significant but fragile and unadjusted for baseline width.
- **Ambiguous P-value labelling** — the abstract attaches one P value to each pair of numbers (baseline and 6 months) without naming the comparison; the directional statement ("significantly lower dimensional changes in Group 4") is the only unambiguous inferential claim.
- **No histomorphometric numbers** — despite core sampling from both ESP groups, no new bone / residual graft / connective tissue data are quoted, so the "bone turnover" half of the study question is unanswered at page level.
- **6-month horizon** — no long-term remodelling, implant survival or late-complication data; the SR+MA in the wiki notes the same evidence gap for porcine-vs-bovine comparisons.
- **Product-specific** — Cerabone vs MinerOss XP is a comparison of two products, not of the species categories "bovine" and "porcine" in general; conclusions cannot be transferred to other xenografts without a direct head-to-head.
- **Future work needed:** adequately powered RCTs reporting both radiographic and histomorphometric outcomes with allocation concealment and blinding, longer follow-up, and implant-outcome endpoints.

## 7. Glossary

- **Xenograft (이종골)** — graft material derived from a non-human species, processed for osteoconductive scaffolding.
- **Bovine bone xenograft, BBX (소뼈 유래 이종골)** — deproteinized bovine bone used as a particulate bone substitute (here: Cerabone).
- **Porcine bone xenograft, PBX (돼지뼈 유래 이종골)** — deproteinized porcine bone used as a particulate bone substitute (here: MinerOss XP).
- **Horizontal ridge augmentation, HRA (수평 치조 증강)** — widening of an atrophic alveolar ridge to permit prosthetic contour (veneer) correction.
- **Extraction socket preservation, ESP (발치 소켓 보존)** — grafting of the extraction socket at the time of tooth removal to limit post-extraction ridge resorption.
- **Buccolingual ridge width (협설골 폭)** — the buccolingual dimension of the alveolar ridge, measured here on CBCT.
- **Osteoconductive (골전도성)** — material acts as a scaffold that supports osteoconduction, i.e. bone grows on/along the graft surface without itself being osteoinductive.
- **Cone-beam computed tomography, CBCT** — cross-sectional imaging used here for buccolingual width measurement.
- **Histomorphometry (조직형태계측)** — quantitative histology of bone, typically partitioning area into new bone, residual graft and connective tissue.
- **Residual graft (잔존 이식재)** — graft material still identifiable in the specimen at follow-up, i.e. not yet replaced by host bone.
