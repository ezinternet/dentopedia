## Three-line Summary

RCT, n=30 (7–12세 혼합치열기 아동, Angle Class I 구치관계 + 고립성 위-Class III 전치부 반대교합), 투명교정(Group A, n=15) vs Z-spring 장치(Group B, n=15) 비교.

전치부 반대교합은 전원 교정 성공; 치료기간은 Z-spring군이 유의하게 짧음(48.4±27일 vs 96.3±22.7일, p<.05); Group A는 절치·치은부 치열궁 깊이가 유의 증가(각 2.6mm, 1.17mm, p<.001)한 반면 Group B는 유의 변화 없음; 삶의 질(COHIP-SF-19)은 양군 유사(p>.05).

임상적 함의: 두 장치 모두 전치부 반대교합 교정에 효과적이나 이동 양상이 다르다(Z-spring은 더 큰 치아 경사이동tipping 유발 가능, 투명교정은 경사·정렬·치체이동bodily movement을 유도) — abstract-only이므로 세부 방법론·이탈률 등은 본문 확인 전까지 불명.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First RCT (identified in this wiki's holdings) directly comparing clear aligners against a conventional fixed interceptive appliance (Z-spring) for isolated dental anterior crossbite in the mixed dentition.
- Quantifies treatment duration difference (Z-spring markedly faster) alongside cephalometric (U1–NA angle, overjet) and model-analysis (arch depth) outcomes for both appliance types.
- Adds objective quality-of-life measurement (COHIP-SF-19) to the growing-patient clear-aligner literature, an outcome rarely reported in the wiki's existing pediatric/interceptive holdings.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Thirty patients aged 7–12 years with Angle Class I molar occlusion and isolated pseudo-Class III anterior crossbite were randomly assigned 1:1 to clear aligners (Group A, n=15) or Z-spring appliance (Group B, n=15). Outcomes assessed: treatment duration, cephalometric changes, dental-cast (model) analysis, and OHRQoL via COHIP-SF-19. Randomization procedure, blinding, and statistical methods beyond significance thresholds are not available from the abstract alone.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Crossbite correction**: successful in all patients (both groups).
- **Treatment duration**: Group B (Z-spring) 48.4 ± 27 days vs Group A (clear aligner) 96.3 ± 22.7 days (p < .05) — Z-spring roughly twice as fast.
- **Cephalometrics**: U1–NA angle increased 5.9° (Group A) vs 7.7° (Group B); overjet increased 4 mm (Group A) vs 4.2 mm (Group B) (both within-group p < .01). Between-group ΔT1–T0 differences were not significant (p > .05).
- **Model analysis**: Group A showed significant increases in incisal (2.6 mm) and gingival (1.17 mm) arch depth (p < .001); Group B showed no significant arch-depth change (p > .05).
- **OHRQoL (COHIP-SF-19)**: comparable between groups (p > .05).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingest**: full text not retrieved (PMC returned empty body). Sample recruitment details, randomization/allocation-concealment method, blinding, dropout/attrition, exact cephalometric landmark protocol, and full statistical model are unknown pending full-text retrieval.
- Small sample (n=15/arm) typical of interceptive-appliance RCTs; generalizability beyond isolated dental (non-skeletal) pseudo-Class III crossbite is untested.
- No long-term stability/relapse follow-up reported in the abstract.

## 7. Glossary

- **Anterior crossbite (AC)**: one or more maxillary anterior teeth occluding lingual to their mandibular counterparts.
- **Pseudo-Class III**: a dental/functional anterior crossbite mimicking skeletal Class III appearance but without true skeletal discrepancy (often with a forward mandibular functional shift).
- **Z-spring (ZS) appliance**: a removable appliance with a Z-shaped spring used to procline/tip individual maxillary incisors labially to correct anterior crossbite.
- **U1–NA angle**: cephalometric measure of maxillary central incisor inclination relative to the NA line.
- **COHIP-SF-19**: Child Oral Health Impact Profile-Short Form-19, a validated pediatric oral health-related quality of life (OHRQoL) instrument.
