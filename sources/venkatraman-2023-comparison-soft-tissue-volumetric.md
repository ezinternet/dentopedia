## Why Ingested

Part of the implant-iip-loading surveillance batch (Wave 3); provides an individual-trial data point on socket-shield technique (SST) vs. conventional immediate implant placement, directly relevant to [[wiki/immediate-implant/socket-shield/lu-2025-socket-shield-conventional-aesthetic-meta]]'s pooled SST-vs-conventional comparison.

## Three-line Summary
Parallel-arm RCT (n=22, single maxillary incisor immediate implant, immediate loading) comparing socket shield technique (SST) vs. conventional technique (CT), assessing buccal soft-tissue volumetric change (digital cast superimposition) and Pink Esthetic Score (PES) over 12 months.

Mean 12-month buccal soft-tissue volumetric change: SST −0.152 ± 0.86 mm vs. CT −0.643 ± 0.35 mm (P = .001); PES was significantly higher in the SST group at both 6 months (P = .001) and 12 months (P = .007).

SST produced less volumetric shrinkage of buccal soft tissue and better pink esthetics than the conventional technique at immediately placed and immediately loaded single maxillary incisor implants.

SST는 즉시식립+즉시부하 상악 단일절치에서 관습적 술식 대비 협측 연조직 체적 수축이 적고 핑크 심미도가 우수함.

## Key Contributions
- One of the few RCTs (rather than case series) directly randomizing SST vs. conventional technique at immediate implant placement
- Uses digital cast superimposition for quantitative volumetric soft-tissue measurement, not just linear/point recession
- Reports PES at three time points (baseline, 6mo, 12mo), showing the SST advantage is present by 6 months and sustained to 12

## Methodology
Parallel-arm RCT, n=22 participants requiring single maxillary incisor replacement with an immediate implant. Randomized to SST or CT; all implants immediately loaded; definitive crowns at 6 months. Digital white-light scans of casts taken pre-implant and at 12 months, superimposed to quantify buccal soft-tissue volumetric change. PES assessed pre-implant, 6mo, and 12mo. Analysis: Stata 16.0.

## Key Results
| Outcome | SST | CT | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buccal soft-tissue volumetric change (12mo) | −0.152 ± 0.86 mm | −0.643 ± 0.35 mm | .001 |
| PES at 6 months | Higher | Lower | .001 |

## Glossary
- **SST (Socket Shield Technique)**: retaining a thin buccal root fragment ("shield") at immediate implant placement to preserve the buccal bone plate and soft-tissue contour, instead of removing the entire root (conventional technique).
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score)**: composite index scoring peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics (papilla presence, soft-tissue contour, level, color, texture) against a reference tooth.
- **Buccal soft-tissue volumetric change**: 3D loss/gain of soft-tissue volume on the facial aspect of the ridge, measured by digital-cast superimposition rather than linear point measurement.
