## Why Ingested

[[tmj/desai-2021-low-level-laser-therapy-tmj-pain]] is a single abstract-only placebo-controlled RCT of LLLT; the wiki lacked a broader synthesis of the LLLT RCT literature (37 trials, 2000-2020) showing how often LLLT actually beats sham. This qualitative SR fills that gap and gives the pre-2021 backdrop to the quantitative [[tmj/candido-do-prado-2025-differential-effectiveness-photobiomodulation-muscular-articular]] and [[tmj/zhang-2026-nonpharmacological-myogenic-tmd-nma]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review without meta-analysis (J Med Life 2021) of 37 RCTs (2000-June 2020; PubMed and Science Direct only) testing low-level laser therapy (LLLT) in patients with temporomandibular disorders.

18 studies found LLLT efficacious for TMD pain, 12 found it equal to placebo or comparator, and 4 reported mixed effects; no pooled estimate was computed, and laser parameters varied widely (632.8-1064 nm; 1.5-112.5 J/cm2; 4-20 sessions).

The authors call LLLT a promising non-invasive option, but the evidence is heterogeneous and only two databases were searched, so they themselves state there is still no conclusive validation for or against LLLT.

## 세줄요약

측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 환자에서 저출력레이저치료 (Low-Level Laser Therapy, LLLT)를 평가한 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 37편(2000~2020년 6월, PubMed·Science Direct 2개 DB)의 메타분석 없는 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR; J Med Life 2021).

18편은 LLLT가 TMD 통증에 효과적, 12편은 위약·대조군과 동등, 4편은 결과가 엇갈림; 통합 효과크기는 산출하지 않았고 레이저 파라미터 편차가 큼 (632.8~1064 nm, 1.5~112.5 J/cm2, 4~20회).

저자들은 LLLT를 유망한 비침습 치료로 평가하나 이질성이 크고 DB가 2개뿐이라 LLLT 찬반의 결정적 근거는 여전히 없다고 스스로 밝힘.

- Source of text: PubMed / PMC (full text retrieved via PubMed MCP)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Qualitative mapping of 37 LLLT RCTs in TMD, including wavelength, dose, power, session number, site and comparator for each trial.
- Tallies trial verdicts: 18 LLLT efficacious for pain, 12 similar to placebo/control/other intervention, 4 varied.
- Documents the heterogeneity of laser parameters as the likely source of contradictory trial results.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PICO: TMD patients; LLLT; placebo or other interventions (occlusal splints, analgesics, TENS, botulinum toxin); efficacy.
- Search Jan 2000 - June 2020 in PubMed and Science Direct plus hand-searching. Inclusion: human RCTs, English, at least 10 subjects. Exclusion: non-randomized or crossover studies, animal studies, systemic disease (RA, fibromyalgia) or non-TMD pain.
- Primary outcome: pain intensity (VAS); secondary: mouth opening, excursions, PPT, EMG, noises, masticatory efficiency, psychological aspects.
- Risk of bias with the Robvis visualization tool; no meta-analysis; no registration reported in the text.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 37 RCTs included. Risk of bias: 33 (89.18%) low risk or some concerns, 4 high risk.
- Lasers: GaAlAs in 19 studies, GaAs in 5, Nd:YAG/diode/red/infrared 2 each. Wavelength 632.8-1064 nm; dose 1.5-112.5 J/cm2; power 1.76-500 mW; total sessions 4-20 (most commonly 8, 12, or 10); most studies 2 sessions/week.
- Verdicts: 18 studies LLLT efficacious, 12 equal to placebo/control/other, 4 varied; 2 studies reported improved psychological/emotional aspects.
- Seven studies used an active comparator (botulinum toxin A, TENS, ibuprofen, needling, occlusal splints, physiotherapy/drug protocol); e.g., LLLT acted faster than botulinum toxin A for pain with no significant mouth-opening change (Carli 2016), and equaled occlusal splints (Oz 2010).
- Laser acupuncture was proposed as an alternative needing less chair time (Madani 2020); PBM plus manual therapy added nothing over either alone (Brochado 2018).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only PubMed and Science Direct searched; English only; authors call for an SR with meta-analysis and more databases.
- Large disparity in dose, power, wavelength, number and frequency of applications, and patient samples.
- Text-level inconsistencies: the review claims "no potential publication bias" from a Robvis risk-of-bias plot (which assesses study bias, not publication bias); a few table rows lack sample size or laser parameters; the verdict counts (18+12+4) do not map row-by-row to the table.
- No quantitative synthesis or certainty grading; no protocol registration reported.

## 7. Glossary

- Low-level laser therapy (LLLT): non-thermal, photochemical laser light for analgesia and tissue healing.
- Photobiomodulation (PBM): current term for LLLT-type light therapy.
- RDC/TMD: Research Diagnostic Criteria for TMD.
- VAS: visual analog scale for pain.
- PPT: pressure pain threshold.
- Robvis: NIHR risk-of-bias visualization tool.
