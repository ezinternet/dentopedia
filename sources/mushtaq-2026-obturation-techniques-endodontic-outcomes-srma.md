## Why Ingested
근관치료 성공률 향상 서베일런스의 일환. 충전 기법(CLC, WVC, SC, CB)과 재료가 치료 결과에 미치는 영향을 84개 연구·11,965개 표본으로 비교한 대규모 SR+MA로, 기존 위키 [[endodontics/obturation/elmsmari-2025-single-cone-bioceramic-clinical-success-sr-ma]] 및 [[endodontics/obturation/song-2022-sealer-based-obturation-epoxy-calcium-silicate-rct]]의 결과를 장기 비교 관점으로 보강한다.

## One-line Summary
SR+MA (n=11,965 samples, 84 studies) comparing CLC, WVC, SC, and CB obturation techniques found no significant differences at ≤12 months but modest advantages for CLC and CB over SC at 24 months; outcomes converged beyond 3 years, with operator expertise outweighing technique.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (84개 연구, 11,965 표본): 충전 기법 간 단기(≤12개월) 성공률 차이 없음; 24개월 시점에서 CLC·CB가 SC보다 소폭 우수하나 3년 이후 수렴, 술자 숙련도가 기법보다 중요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 84 studies (11,965 samples) — largest SR+MA comparing obturation techniques for endodontic outcomes
- Bioceramic sealers used with SC technique showed no clear clinical benefit over conventional sealers/techniques
- 24-month window: CLC +5.0% (p=0.021) and CB +7.5% (p=0.011) success rate over SC in primary RCTs
- Beyond 3 years: success rates converge among WVC, WLC, CLC, SC, and CB (no significant differences)
- Retreatment success consistently lower than primary treatment at all time points

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA)
- Population: Permanent teeth undergoing primary RCT or re-RCT
- Techniques compared: Cold lateral condensation (CLC), warm vertical compaction (WVC), warm lateral compaction (WLC), single-cone (SC), carrier-based (CB)
- Follow-up: minimum 6 months; subgroups at 6m, 12m, 24m, >3 years
- Risk of bias: ROBINS-I (cohort) and RoB 2.0 (randomized); GRADE for evidence certainty
- Statistical method: Mixed-effects meta-regression (beta coefficients with 95% CI)
- Databases: PubMed, Cochrane Library, ScienceDirect + grey literature

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Primary treatment success rates:**
- 6 months: 87.1% overall; no significant differences among techniques
- 12 months: 87.2% overall; no significant differences
- 24 months: 92.0% overall; CLC vs SC β=5.0% (p=0.021); CB vs SC β=7.5% (p=0.011)
- >3 years: 84.9%; no significant differences among WVC, WLC, CLC, SC, CB

**Retreatment (re-RCT) success rates:**
- 6 months: 92.9%
- 12 months: 77.0%; SC marginally higher than CLC (p=0.045)
- 24 months: 83.5%; CB significantly outperformed WVC (p=0.004)
- >3 years: 73.7%; no significant differences between CLC and CB

**GRADE evidence quality:** Low to very low — particularly for long-term outcomes; high heterogeneity throughout.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Low to very low GRADE certainty; high heterogeneity limits interpretability
- Most included studies had short (<2 years) follow-up
- Operator expertise, case selection, and study design heavily influence outcomes — confounders not controlled
- Bioceramic sealers have not demonstrated clear clinical superiority over traditional sealers
- Need for well-powered, multicenter, long-term RCTs with standardized outcome reporting

## 7. Glossary
- CLC: Cold lateral condensation — classical technique with gutta-percha cones and spreader
- WVC: Warm vertical compaction — thermoplasticized GP, better 3D adaptation
- SC: Single-cone technique — one master cone + bioceramic sealer (hydraulic condensation)
- CB: Carrier-based — thermoplasticized GP on a carrier (Thermafil type)
- Re-RCT: Nonsurgical endodontic retreatment
