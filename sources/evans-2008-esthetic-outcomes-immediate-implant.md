## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case series evaluating esthetic outcomes of 42 consecutive non-adjacent single-unit immediate implants with mean 18.9-month follow-up (range 6–50 months) in Clinical Oral Implants Research 2008. Primary outcome was crown height change (midfacial recession). Participants included both buccal-platform and lingual-platform immediate placements.

Mean crown height change was 0.9±0.78 mm (p=0.000, highly significant). Buccal shoulder positioning demonstrated 3-fold greater recession (1.8±0.83 mm) compared to lingual shoulder (0.6±0.55 mm, p=0.000), making shoulder location the dominant esthetic risk factor. Thin biotype showed trend toward more recession (1.0±0.9 mm) but not statistically significant (p=0.187) compared to thick biotype (0.7±0.57 mm).

Implant shoulder position (buccal versus lingual) is the key esthetic risk factor determining midfacial recession after immediate implant placement. Lingual or palatal positioning provides strong protection. Immediate implant placement demands precise three-dimensional positioning and careful case selection, particularly in anterior esthetic zones where recession is most visible.

## 세줄요약

42개 연속 단일 단위 즉시식립의 심미 결과 평가 후향적 사례집합 (COIR 2008), mean 18.9개월 추적(6~50개월). 주요 결과: crown height change(midfacial recession). 협측·구개측 platform 위치 모두 포함.

Crown height change 평균 0.9±0.78 mm (p=0.000). 협측 shoulder 위치는 구개측(0.6±0.55 mm)보다 3배 더 큰 퇴축(1.8±0.83 mm, p=0.000)으로 shoulder 위치가 심미 위험도 핵심. 얇은 생체형은 퇴축 경향(1.0±0.9 mm)이나 두꺼운 생체형(0.7±0.57 mm)과 통계적 유의 아님(p=0.187).

임플란트 shoulder 위치(협측 vs 구개측)가 즉시식립 후 협측 퇴축을 결정하는 핵심 심미 위험도. 구개측/구개 위치는 강력한 보호. 즉시식립은 정밀한 3D 위치결정 및 신중한 사례 선택 필수, 특히 퇴축이 눈에 띄는 전방 심미 부위.

## 1. Key Results
- 42 non-adjacent single-unit immediate implants; mean function 18.9 months (range 6–50 months)
- Implant platform: 4.1mm or 4.8mm diameter
- Crown height change (recession): 0.9±0.78mm highly significant (p=0.000)
- Buccal shoulder position: 1.8±0.83mm recession vs lingual shoulder: 0.6±0.55mm (p=0.000) — 3× more
- Thin biotype: 1.0±0.9mm vs thick biotype: 0.7±0.57mm (NS, p=0.187)
- No difference between implant systems (p=0.837)

## 2. Clinical Implications
- Shoulder position (buccal vs lingual) is dominant esthetic risk factor
- Lingual/palatal implant positioning strongly protective against recession
- Thin biotype = trend toward more recession but not statistically significant
- Immediate implant placement demands precise 3D positioning and careful case selection
