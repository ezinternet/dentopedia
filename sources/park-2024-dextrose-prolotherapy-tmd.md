## Why Ingested

TMJ prolotherapy(하악관절 증식치료)는 기존 위키의 TMD 보존적 치료(스플린트, arthrocentesis, BTX)가 실패한 난치성(refractory) 환자에게 적용하는 대안적 minimally-invasive 옵션인데, 본 위키에 관련 임상 근거가 없었다. 본 연구(Park 2024, SNUBH, n=19 retrospective)는 dextrose prolotherapy의 원발 임상 데이터로, 같은 기관에서 나온 서술적 리뷰 [[pdrn/ku-2025-prolotherapy-temporomandibular-joint-disorders]]가 인용하는 핵심 실증 근거다. 또한 기존 [[tmj/kim-2023-treatment-tmj-osteoarthritis]]가 다루는 conservative-treatment-refractory 환자군에 대한 후속 대안 치료로 연결된다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (Seoul National University Bundang Hospital, n=19 TMD patients, Jan 2022–Dec 2023) with chronic TMJ pain refractory to conventional treatment (splint, botulinum injection, intra-articular dexamethasone/hyaluronic acid, arthrocentesis), treated with hypertonic (10%) dextrose prolotherapy injected at retrodiscal attachment, anterior disc attachment, lateral capsule, masseter origin, and stylomandibular ligament, up to 3 sessions 2–3 weeks apart.

Mean NRS pain dropped from 5.7 at baseline to 1.7 after completing all sessions (13/19 completed 3 sessions); mean maximum mouth opening increased from 34.5 mm to 38.8 mm; outcomes were positive regardless of whether the disorder originated from soft-tissue (muscle/capsule/disc, group A) or bone-related (osteoarthritis, group B) causes; no serious complications, only transient injection-site pain and brief facial nerve weakness.

Small, uncontrolled, single-surgeon retrospective series (no comparator arm, subjective NRS outcome, single expert operator with 35 years' experience) — supports dextrose prolotherapy as a low-cost, low-risk option for refractory chronic TMD, but efficacy needs confirmation in a controlled prospective design.

## 세줄요약

후향적 연구 (서울대학교병원 분당서울대병원, n=19명 TMD 환자, 2022년 1월–2023년 12월): 스플린트·보툴리눔 주사·관절강 내 덱사메타손/히알루론산 주사·관절세정술(arthrocentesis) 등 기존 보존적 치료에 반응하지 않는 만성 통증 환자에게 고장성(10%) 덱스트로스 (dextrose)를 retrodiscal 부착부, 전방 원판 부착부, 외측 관절낭, 교근 기시부, 경상하악인대 부위에 최대 3회(2–3주 간격) 주입.

NRS 통증 점수 기저 5.7 → 전 세션 완료 후 1.7로 감소(19명 중 13명 3세션 완료); 최대 개구량 34.5mm → 38.8mm 증가; 연조직 원인군(A군)과 골성/골관절염군(B군) 모두 유사한 개선; 중대 합병증 없음, 경미한 주사부위 통증과 일과성 안면신경 약화만 관찰.

단일기관·단일술자(35년 경력)의 대조군 없는 소규모(n=19) 후향적 연구로 주관적 NRS에 의존 — 난치성 만성 TMD에 대한 저비용·저위험 대안으로서의 근거를 제공하지만, 대조군을 갖춘 전향적 연구로 확증이 필요함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First (in this wiki) primary clinical evidence for TMJ dextrose prolotherapy as a treatment for chronic, treatment-refractory TMD
- Defines a concrete 5-point injection protocol (retrodiscal attachment tissue, anterior disc attachment tissue, superior/inferior lateral capsule, masseter origin at zygomatic arch, stylomandibular ligament insertion) with a standardized 10% dextrose solution recipe
- Stratifies patients into group A (soft-tissue origin: muscle/capsule/disc disorders, Japanese Society for TMJ types I–III) vs group B (bone-related, type IV osteoarthritis) and shows both groups improve similarly
- Reports a dose-response-like pattern across up to 3 sessions (NRS 5.7 → 4.0 → 2.5 → 1.7)
- Documents a low, self-limiting complication profile (transient injection-site pain, brief facial nerve weakness within 2–3 hours)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective chart review, single center (SNUBH), single expert operator (35+ years' experience)
- **Period**: January 2022 – December 2023
- **Sample**: n=19 (6 male, 13 female; mean age 43, range 18–74); 11/19 had parafunctional habits (bruxism/clenching)
- **Inclusion**: TMD-related pain unresponsive to conventional treatment (splint therapy, botulinum injection to masseter/temporalis, intra-articular dexamethasone or hyaluronic acid, TMJ arthroscopy-arthrocentesis); voluntary NRS reporting
- **Exclusion**: diabetes mellitus, TMJ tumor/severe condylar ankylosis, prior TMJ open surgery, systemic NSAID/steroid use, need for psychiatric treatment
- **Diagnosis framework**: Japanese Society for the Temporomandibular Joint (2001) 5-category system — type I muscle disorder, II capsule-ligament disorder, III disc disorder, IV degenerative/osteoarthritis, V psychiatric (excluded); group A = types I–III (n=10), group B = type IV (n=9)
- **Injection technique**: auriculotemporal nerve block (2 mL 2% lidocaine, no vasoconstrictor) + topical EMLA cream; prolotherapy solution = dextrose 20% : lidocaine 4% : saline in 2:1:1 ratio → final 10% dextrose; 1 cc per injection point; 26-gauge 22-mm needle
- **Schedule**: up to 3 sessions, 2–3 week intervals, continuation based on compliance/response; other TMD interventions discontinued during treatment/follow-up
- **Outcome measures**: 0–10 NRS for pain/dysfunction (pain, opening limitation <35mm, hypermobility >50mm, joint sounds), maximum mouth opening (MMO), subjective satisfaction; assessed 2–3 weeks post-treatment
- **Statistics**: Mann-Whitney U test (group A vs B comparison), SPSS ver. 27.0

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **NRS trajectory (n=19 at baseline)**: 5.7 (baseline) → 4.0 (after session 1, n=19) → 2.5 (after session 2, n=15) → 1.7 (after session 3, n=13)
- **Session completion**: 13/19 (68.4%) completed all 3 sessions; 2/19 (10.5%) stopped after 2 sessions with improvement; 4/19 (21.1%) stopped after 1 session with improvement
- **MMO**: initial dip from 34.5 mm → 33.5 mm after session 1, then recovery/increase to 38.8 mm by final follow-up
- **Group A (soft-tissue, n=10) vs Group B (bone-related/osteoarthritis, n=9)**: both groups showed improved pain scores and MMO; outcomes positive regardless of symptom origin
- **Safety**: no serious complications; minor transient injection-site pain and ipsilateral facial nerve weakness resolving within 2–3 hours
- Overall patient satisfaction reported as positive

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=19), single center, single operator — limited generalizability
- No control/comparator group (e.g., saline sham, splint-only continuation) — cannot isolate prolotherapy-specific effect from natural fluctuation or regression to the mean
- Outcome relies on subjective NRS without validated composite TMD instrument (e.g., RDC/TMD); symptom types (pain vs opening limitation vs joint sound) not separately analyzed, only overall discomfort
- Retrospective design — no pre-registered protocol, potential selection/recall bias
- Diabetes mellitus excluded a priori (dextrose contraindication), so generalizability to diabetic TMD patients is unknown
- Authors call for further study to optimize injection points and to tailor protocols per TMD subtype

## 7. Glossary

- **Prolotherapy** (증식치료): regenerative injection therapy that induces a controlled inflammatory response to stimulate fibroblast proliferation and tissue repair in ligaments/tendons
- **Hypertonic dextrose** (고장성 포도당): concentrated glucose solution (here 10%) that causes osmotic cell-wall lysis, triggering fibroblast recruitment and growth-factor release (PDGF, TGF-β, CTGF, EGF)
- **NRS** (Numerical Rating Scale, 숫자평정척도): 0–10 patient-reported pain/dysfunction scale
- **MMO** (Maximum Mouth Opening, 최대개구량): interincisal distance at maximal unassisted mouth opening, in mm
- **Retrodiscal tissue** (후원판조직): vascularized connective tissue posterior to the TMJ articular disc, a common site of TMD-related pain
- **Stylomandibular ligament** (경상하악인대): ligament limiting excessive mandibular protrusion/hypermobility, a group B (osteoarthritis/hypermobility) injection target
