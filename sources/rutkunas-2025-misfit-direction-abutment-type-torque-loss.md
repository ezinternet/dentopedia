## Why Ingested

The wiki's misfit evidence is clinical and long-term ([[implants/katsoulis-2017-misfit-implant-prosthesis-clinical-outcomes-sr]], [[implants/jokstad-2014-3d-misfit-implant-prosthesis-long-term]]: misfit raises screw complications, with a concern band around 134–169 µm) and its torque-loss evidence is single-component bench work ([[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/lee-2025-abutment-screw-design-torque-loss-fatigue]]). This factorial bench study adds a controlled 100 µm horizontal-vs-vertical misfit comparison on a 3-unit zirconia FDP with reverse-torque readout after 1.2 million thermomechanical cycles, and a mesial-vs-distal implant-position effect on preload. → [[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/rutkunas-2025-misfit-direction-abutment-type-torque-loss]]

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro factorial study (N = 80 CAD-CAM zirconia 3-unit FDPs on two Conelog implants, n = 10 per group; non-engaging/non-engaging, non-engaging/engaging and engaging/engaging Ti-bases; 0 µm control vs 100 µm horizontal or vertical misfit; 1.2 × 10^6 thermomechanical cycles at 49 N) measuring reverse torque and maximum failure load (Fmax).

Engagement configuration did not affect Fmax or reverse torque, and 100 µm misfit in either direction did not change reverse torque; horizontal misfit lowered Fmax versus control (P < .001), and distal implants showed lower reverse torque than mesial (13.5 ± 0.5 vs 18.6 ± 0.5 Ncm; P < .001).

Implant position (distal) and horizontal misfit were the relevant risk factors rather than Ti-base engagement, but the single zirconia/implant system, low 49 N load, and several internal inconsistencies in the reported tables limit generalizability.

## 세줄요약

시험관 내 요인설계 연구 (CAD-CAM 지르코니아 3본 고정성 보철 80개, 임플란트 2개 [Conelog], 군당 n = 10; 비결합형/비결합형·비결합형/결합형·결합형/결합형 Ti-base; 0 µm 대조 vs 100 µm 수평 또는 수직 부적합; 49 N로 1.2 × 10^6회 열기계 순환)로 역회전력 (reverse torque)과 최대 파절하중 (Fmax)을 측정.

Ti-base 결합 형태는 Fmax와 역회전력에 영향이 없었고, 어느 방향이든 100 µm 부적합은 역회전력을 바꾸지 않았다. 수평 부적합만 Fmax를 대조군보다 낮췄고 (P < .001), 원심 임플란트의 역회전력이 근심보다 낮았다 (13.5 ± 0.5 vs 18.6 ± 0.5 Ncm; P < .001).

관건은 Ti-base 결합 형태가 아니라 임플란트 위치 (원심)와 수평 부적합이었으나, 단일 지르코니아·임플란트 시스템, 낮은 49 N 하중, 보고된 표들의 내부 불일치가 일반화를 제한한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Combines abutment engagement (Ti-base anti-rotation) and misfit orientation (horizontal vs vertical, 100 µm) in one factorial design with reverse-torque measurement under fatigue; earlier work cited by the authors examined these factors separately.
- Reports a mesial-vs-distal implant-position effect on reverse torque (distal lower).
- Identifies horizontal misfit, not vertical, as the factor reducing Fmax, with fractures concentrated at the distal connector/pontic region.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In-vitro factorial bench study with thermomechanical aging.
- **Sample size**: Pilot (n = 5; effect size d = 0.848) → G*Power computed n = 9, raised to 10 per group; N = 80 frameworks.
- **Model**: Two Conelog Screw-Line implants (Ø4.3 × 13 mm, 7.5° internal connection), 22 mm between implant centers, placed 10° to each other in a Frasaco model (mandibular second premolar to second molar span). Digital impressions (Trios 4); full-contour 3Y-TZP (Katana HTML Plus) frameworks with 1 mm buccal feldspathic veneer; mesial connector 14.8 mm2, distal 18.8 mm2.
- **Groups**: N-N and N-E screw-retained; E-E cement-retained (zirconia abutments, Panavia V5). Misfit: 0 µm control cast, 100 µm horizontal cast, 100 µm vertical cast (micromechanical stand + splinted copings). Eight experimental cells: N-N and N-E each at control/vertical/horizontal, E-E at control/vertical only.
- **Aging**: 5–55 °C thermocycling (120 s dwell), 1.2 × 10^6 cycles at 49 N and 1.67 Hz (Zurich Chewing Simulator), estimated by the authors as 4–5 years of function. Screws tightened to 20 Ncm.
- **Outcomes**: Reverse torque (Mark-10 M7i) at T0 and T1 (new screws used at T1); Fmax in a universal testing machine (1 mm/min); failure mode by digital microscope (40x).
- **Statistics**: Two-way ANOVA with Tukey for Fmax; mixed-model ANOVA for reverse torque with implant position; α = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Survival through aging: all specimens survived without fracture except one N-E vertical-misfit specimen with a broken screw (Fmax n = 9 in that cell).
- Fmax (Table 1, mean ± SD, N): control N-N 1828.06 ± 473.63, N-E 1758.70 ± 255.76, E-E 1284.54 ± 283.71; vertical N-N 2119.74 ± 453.71, N-E 1816.18 ± 327.39 (n = 9), E-E 1563.26 ± 404.36; horizontal N-N 1509.20 ± 179.64, N-E 1557.25 ± 283.67.
- Engagement effect on Fmax: F = 1.12, P = .294; engagement × misfit interaction F = 0.92, P = .421; misfit factor F = 6.37, P < .001, driven by horizontal groups vs control (P < .001); vertical vs control P > .05.
- Reverse torque (mixed ANOVA): implant position significant (mesial > distal; abstract: 18.6 ± 0.5 vs 13.5 ± 0.5 Ncm); engagement P = .318; misfit P = .267; no significant interactions. Some T1 values exceeded T0 (e.g., control N-N mesial 21.3 → 25.7 Ncm), attributed by the authors to friction/new screws.
- Failure mode (Table 3): cracks most often began at the pontic-distal connector junction; transversal distal-connector fractures occurred mainly in N-N control and vertical groups (none in N-E or E-E); fossa-to-distal-connector fractures were common in N-E and E-E, especially under horizontal misfit.
- Conclusion as stated: horizontal and vertical misfit up to 100 µm did not compromise removal torque; horizontal misfit reduced fracture strength.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single zirconia material and single implant system; only 100 µm misfit in two orientations; low (49 N) cyclic load at the bottom of the chewing-force range, so bruxism or heavy loads may be underestimated.
- E-E (engaging) groups are cement-retained while N-N/N-E are screw-retained, so "engagement" is confounded with retention mode, and E-E has no horizontal-misfit cell and no reverse-torque data.
- Internal inconsistencies noted on reading the text: (a) the abstract attributes P = .421 to the engagement effect on fracture strength and reverse torque, whereas the Results give .421 as the engagement × misfit interaction (engagement P = .294 for Fmax, .318 for torque); (b) the abstract states no Fmax difference "across zirconia specimen groups ... under various misfit conditions" while the Results report a significant horizontal-misfit effect; (c) Table 2 reports n = 4 and n = 6 for N-N torque cells although n = 10 per group is stated; (d) four columns of Table 3 (V N-N, V N-E, C N-N, C E-E) sum to 11 failures against n = 10; (e) the abstract mentions seven test groups plus one control, while the Methods list eight cells; (f) the T0/T1 timing is described ambiguously (T0 "after thermomechanical loading" and T1 "after fatigue testing"). No effect of engagement on Fmax is claimed although control E-E Fmax (1284.54 N) is numerically lowest.
- Authors call for different zirconia generations, veneering protocols, implant-abutment designs, wider misfit ranges, and long-term clinical trials.

## 6. Related Work
- Katsoulis 2017 ([[implants/katsoulis-2017-misfit-implant-prosthesis-clinical-outcomes-sr]]): clinical SR linking misfit to screw complications; this bench study finds no torque effect at 100 µm.
- Jokstad 2014 ([[implants/jokstad-2014-3d-misfit-implant-prosthesis-long-term]]): screw-complication group had higher misfit (169 vs 134 µm); 100 µm here sits below that concern band.
- Lee 2025 ([[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/lee-2025-abutment-screw-design-torque-loss-fatigue]]): torque loss under cyclic fatigue for different screw designs.
- Heckmann et al. and Nissan et al. (cited by the authors, not held in the wiki): cement- vs screw-retained bone-loss versus survival findings.

## 7. Glossary
- **Reverse torque (removal torque)**: torque needed to loosen a tightened screw; a proxy for remaining preload.
- **Engaging / non-engaging Ti-base**: titanium abutment base with or without an anti-rotation hex/star feature at the implant interface.
- **Horizontal / vertical misfit**: lateral displacement versus axial gap of the framework relative to implant positions (100 µm each here).
- **Fmax**: maximum load to failure in a universal testing machine.
- **FDP**: fixed dental prosthesis.
