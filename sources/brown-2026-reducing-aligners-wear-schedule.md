## Why Ingested

This RCT directly addresses whether reducing the clear aligner (투명교정 장치, CAT) change interval from 7 days to 3 days improves predictability of tooth movement. Wear schedule is listed as a leading compliance variable in [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/albaqshi-2025-invisalign-predictability-factors-compliance-sr]] and acceleration strategies are reviewed in [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/sirri-2026-physical-adjunctive-interventions-clear-aligner-rct-sr]]. This RCT supplies direct per-protocol deviation data (mm/°) for the 3-day vs 7-day comparison, filling the gap between SR-level evidence and per-tray activation tradeoffs.

## Three-line Summary

A 2-arm RCT (n=38 completers; 45 randomized) compared a 3-day protocol (0.11 mm/1° activation per tray) with a conventional 7-day protocol (0.25 mm/2° per tray) for mandibular incisor tooth movement in Class I crowding cases. Linear deviations were clinically equivalent (both within 0.5 mm ABO threshold); angular deviations on teeth 31/41 reached statistical significance but remained under the 2° clinical threshold. The 3-day group required significantly more refinements and clinical visits without gains in predictability or treatment duration.

## 세줄요약

투명교정 장치 (Clear Aligner, CAT) 교체 주기를 7일에서 3일로 단축한 RCT (완료 38명): 3일 프로토콜은 트레이당 활성화량을 0.11 mm/1°으로 절반 이하로 줄였고, 7일 프로토콜은 0.25 mm/2°를 사용했다. 선형 이동 편차는 두 군 모두 ABO 임상 기준치 (0.5 mm) 이내로 동등했으나, 하악 절치 31·41번의 각도 편차가 통계적으로 유의했고(p<0.05) 임상 기준치(2°)는 초과하지 않았다. 3일 군에서 재정렬 (Refinement) 횟수와 내원 횟수가 유의하게 많아, 교체 주기 단축이 예측도나 치료 기간을 개선하지 못하며 마모 시간(Wear Duration)이 교정력 효율의 핵심임을 시사한다.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Two-arm, parallel-group, single-blinded, single-center prospective RCT
- **Period**: October 2023 – March 2025
- **Setting**: University orthodontic clinic
- **Registration**: ClinicalTrials.gov (number not reported in abstract)
- **Randomization**: Fixed block randomization; concealed sequentially numbered envelopes
- **Blinding**: Outcome assessors blinded to group allocation (single-blind)

## Population

- n = 45 randomized; n = 38 completed (7 excluded for non-compliance)
- Age: 18–30 years (adults)
- Inclusion: Class I malocclusion, moderate-to-severe mandibular anterior crowding
- Exclusion: Not specified in abstract (non-compliance led to 7 dropouts)

## Interventions

| Parameter | 3-day protocol | 7-day protocol |
|---|---|---|
| Change interval | 3 days | 7 days |
| Linear activation per tray | 0.11 mm | 0.25 mm |
| Angular activation per tray | 1° | 2° |

## Primary Outcome

Deviation between predicted and achieved mandibular incisor tooth movement after the first aligner series, measured across 3 linear and 3 angular dimensions.

## Results

**Linear deviations**
- Comparable between groups
- Both within ABO clinical threshold of 0.5 mm

**Angular deviations**
- Tooth 31: statistically significant deviation (p = 0.040)
- Tooth 41: statistically significant deviation (p = 0.003)
- Tooth 41 buccolingual inclination: p = 0.005
- None exceeded the 2° clinical threshold

**Clinical efficiency**
- 3-day group: significantly more refinements and clinical visits
- Overall treatment duration: no significant difference between groups
- Final occlusal outcomes: no significant difference
- Adverse effects: none reported

## Authors' Conclusions

Both protocols produced comparable occlusal outcomes. The 3-day protocol required more refinements and visits without improved predictability. Wear duration plays a key role in aligner effectiveness — reducing tray wear time does not compensate for reduced per-tray activation.

## Limitations (from abstract)

- Abstract-only; sample size n=38 completers is modest
- Single-center design limits generalizability
- ClinicalTrials.gov registration number not reported in abstract
- Restricted to mandibular anterior teeth / Class I cases; may not generalize to posterior segments or Class II/III
