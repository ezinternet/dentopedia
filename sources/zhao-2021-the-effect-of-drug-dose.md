## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 11 RCTs evaluating optimal dose and duration of adjunctive amoxicillin plus metronidazole (AMX/MET) to full-mouth scaling and root planing (FMSRP) in periodontitis, with primary outcome of clinical attachment level (CAL) gain.

Higher dose (500/500 mg) AMX/MET produced significantly greater CAL gain and probing pocket depth (PPD) reduction at 3 and 6 months compared with lower doses; duration (3–10 days) made no significant difference in outcomes, with minimal between-group adverse event difference.

Evidence supports 500/500 mg AMX/MET as the optimal dose adjunct to FMSRP for 6-month stability; duration recommendation awaits further evidence.

## 세줄요약

11편 RCT 메타분석: 전악 치석제거·치근활택(Full-Mouth Scaling and Root Planing, FMSRP)에 보조적으로 사용하는 아목시실린(Amoxicillin, AMX) + 메트로니다졸(Metronidazole, MET)의 최적 용량·기간 평가, 주결과 임상 부착 수준(Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득.

고용량(500/500 mg) AMX/MET가 저용량 대비 3·6개월 CAL 획득 및 치주낭 깊이(Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 감소에서 유의하게 우월; 복용 기간(3–10일) 간 차이는 미미하며 부작용도 군 간 최소 차이.

6개월 안정 효과를 위해 500/500 mg AMX/MET가 최적 용량으로 지지됨; 기간 권고안은 근거 부족으로 결론 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Establishes 500/500 mg as the optimal AMX/MET dose for adjunctive periodontal therapy
- Demonstrates no significant benefit from extending duration beyond 3 days (up to 10 days)
- Supports stable 6-month clinical attachment gain with high-dose AMX/MET
- Adverse event risk difference was minimal between treatment and control groups

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Electronic search across 4 databases + manual search of 4 journals
- Inclusion: RCTs comparing AMX/MET adjunct to FMSRP vs FMSRP alone, ≥3 months follow-up
- 11 studies eligible for analysis
- Primary outcome: CAL gain; Secondary: PPD reduction, adverse events
- Follow-up: 3 and 6 months

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **High dose (500/500 mg)**: Significant CAL gain + PPD reduction vs lower doses at 3 and 6 months
- **Duration comparison (3 vs 7 vs 10 days)**: Minimal differences — no recommendation possible
- **Adverse events**: Risk difference minimal between AMX/MET and FMSRP-alone groups
- **Conclusion**: 500/500 mg AMX/MET + FMSRP provides stable improvement at 6 months

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Limited number of trials per dose subgroup
- Short follow-up (≤6 months); long-term outcomes unknown
- Cannot recommend specific duration due to limited evidence
- Heterogeneity in patient populations and disease severity

## 6. Related Work and Context
- Builds on the established evidence that AMX/MET adjunct improves periodontal outcomes
- Addresses the clinical question of which dose to use — a gap in prior SRs

## 7. Glossary
- **FMSRP (Full-Mouth Scaling and Root Planing)**: Complete subgingival debridement within ≤24 h
- **AMX/MET (Amoxicillin + Metronidazole)**: Most-studied antibiotic combination for adjunctive periodontal therapy
- **CAL (Clinical Attachment Level)**: Primary measure of periodontal status; gain indicates improvement
- **PPD (Probing Pocket Depth)**: Depth of periodontal pocket; reduction indicates treatment success

## Why Ingested

Directly addresses optimal dosing of the most evidence-supported antibiotic combination for periodontal therapy — a clinical decision point not resolved by prior systematic reviews.
