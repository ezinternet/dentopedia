## Three-line Summary

Narrative conceptual paper (IJPRD 2010) that redefines "contour" for implant restorations by naming two zones of the abutment/crown — the critical contour and the subcritical contour.

States that overcontouring generally positions the gingival margin apically and undercontouring has the opposite effect, and that altering either zone can change gingival margin level, papilla height, gingival architecture, labial alveolar profile, and gingival color.

Foundational vocabulary for emergence-profile design; expert opinion without quantitative data in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

서술적 개념 논문 (IJPRD 2010): 임플란트 지대주/치관을 임계 윤곽 (Critical Contour)과 하임계 윤곽 (Subcritical Contour) 두 구역으로 정의해 임플란트 보철의 "윤곽" 개념을 재정의.

과풍융 (overcontour)은 대체로 치은연을 근단으로, 저풍융 (undercontour)은 반대로 이동시킨다고 제시; 두 구역 수정이 치은연 높이·치간유두 높이·치은 형태·순측 치조 윤곽·치은 색조를 바꿀 수 있음.

출현윤곽 설계의 기초 용어 원출처이나, 초록상 정량 데이터 없는 전문가 의견.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Introduces the critical contour / subcritical contour zones of the implant abutment and crown.
- Restates the over/undercontour rule: overcontour → apical gingival margin; undercontour → opposite.
- Lists soft-tissue features each zone can modify: gingival margin level, papilla height, gingival architecture, labial alveolar profile, gingival color.
- Notes the abutment can effect the transition from round implant head to cervical tooth anatomy "provided there is sufficient running room".

## 7. Glossary

- **Critical contour**: zone of abutment/crown immediately apical to the gingival margin.
- **Subcritical contour**: zone apical to the critical contour, toward the implant platform.
- **Running room**: vertical distance from implant platform to gingival margin available for contour transition.
