## Why Ingested

제3대구치 발치 후 비약물적 보조요법(PRF·오존치료·LLLT·Kinesio taping 등) 근거를 통합한 엄브렐라 리뷰. 기존 [[oral-surgery/pattnayak-2024-prf-prp-third-molar-extraction-sr]]는 PRF vs PRP 국소 비교에 그치지만, 본 리뷰(Firoozi 2022)는 network meta-analysis로 7개 비약물적 개입을 서로 순위화해 PRF·오존이 최상위임을 정량 근거로 보강한다. [[oral-surgery/gonzalez-2025-low-level-laser-third-molar-complications-sr-ma]]의 LLLT 효과를 "상대적으로 약함"이라는 맥락으로 재확인.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42022326584, PRISMA 2020) of 19 systematic reviews / 10 meta-analyses with quantitative pain synthesis, network meta-analysis ranking 7 nonpharmacological complementary interventions after third molar surgery.

Ozone therapy was most effective for early pain (SMD −0.84) and PRF/derivatives most effective for late pain (SMD −1.03); LLLT was least effective for early pain (SMD −0.32); surgical drainage least effective for late pain.

Findings limited by unclear risk of bias in most included reviews and lack of a standardized application protocol for each complementary intervention — clinical adoption should await further high-quality RCTs.

## 세줄요약

엄브렐라 리뷰(PROSPERO 사전등록, PRISMA 2020): 제3대구치 발치 후 통증에 대한 19편 체계적 문헌고찰/10편 메타분석 정량 통합, network meta-analysis로 7가지 비약물적 보조요법 순위화.

초기 통증에는 오존치료가 최우수(SMD −0.84), 후기 통증에는 PRF/유도체가 최우수(SMD −1.03); LLLT는 초기 통증에서 가장 효과 낮음(SMD −0.32); 수술 배액은 후기 통증에서 가장 효과 낮음.

포함된 리뷰 대부분에서 비뚤림 위험이 불명확하고 개입별 표준 프로토콜이 부재해, 임상 routine 도입 전 추가 고품질 RCT 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review to apply network meta-analysis to rank nonpharmacological complementary interventions for post-third-molar-surgery pain head-to-head, rather than pairwise.
- Systematic overlap handling via corrected covered area (CCA) with a defined tie-breaking algorithm (ROBIS quality → recency/RCT count → sensitivity analysis for low-overlap reviews).
- Separates early (48-72h) vs. late (5-7 day) pain outcomes, revealing that different interventions dominate at different time points.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PROSPERO-registered, PRISMA 2020-compliant umbrella review of systematic reviews with quantitative meta-analysis (RCTs only; no restriction on language/date).
- Searches: MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase (to 20 June 2022) + Google Scholar (top 100) + hand search.
- Two independent reviewers screened/extracted; third reviewer resolved conflicts.
- ROBIS tool for risk-of-bias (3 phases, 4 domains in phase 2).
- CCA for pairwise overlap (0-5% slight to >15% very high); Jadad's algorithm for discordance resolution.
- Effect sizes converted to standardized mean difference (SMD) + SE; frequentist network meta-analysis (fixed-effect, "netmeta" R package).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Intervention | Early pain SMD (95% CI) | Late pain SMD (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|
| Ozone therapy | −0.84 (−1.09, −0.59) — large | −0.72 (−0.99, −0.45) — moderate-large |
| PRF / derivatives | moderate | −1.03 (−1.56, −0.50) — large |
| Kinesio taping | moderate | not significant late |
| Surgical drainage | moderate | −0.13 (−0.38, 0.12) — smallest/NS |
| LLLT | −0.32 (−0.49, −0.15) — smallest | minimal |

- Sensitivity analysis (lowest-overlap, most-recent reviews) confirmed PRF derivatives (new centrifugation protocols: A-PRF, L-PRF) and ozone therapy as top performers for both early and late pain.
- A-PRF > L-PRF for early pain (higher monocyte content → faster vascularization); L-PRF > A-PRF for late pain (denser, slower-dissolving structure, prolonged socket protection).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- 12/19 included meta-analyses had high risk of bias for eligibility criteria; heterogeneous quality across the evidence base.
- No standardized protocol exists for ozone administration or PRF preparation — centrifugation speed/time materially changes outcome.
- Split-mouth RCT designs risk pain-side cross-contamination; authors recommend ≥7-day washout between surgical sides in future trials.
- Recommend future RCTs standardize peri-operative analgesic/antibiotic regimens and control for patient age (≥25y affects healing/pain).

## 7. Glossary

- **CCA (Corrected Covered Area)**: quantifies the overlap of primary studies across multiple systematic reviews on the same topic.
- **ROBIS**: risk-of-bias assessment tool specific to systematic reviews (as opposed to primary studies).
- **SMD (Standardized Mean Difference)**: effect-size metric allowing pooling across studies using different pain scales.
- **A-PRF / L-PRF**: advanced-PRF / leukocyte-PRF, distinguished by centrifugation protocol (speed/time), affecting cellular/cytokine content and dissolution rate.
