## Why Ingested

사용자 요청으로 PA(periapical, 구내치근단) artifact 근거를 보강. 기존 위키의 파노라마 artifact/오류 페이지들([[radiology/lingam-2023-common-errors-subjective-quality-panoramic]] 파노라마 오류 유병률, [[radiology/kim-2024-real-ghost-pseudo-ghost-images-panoramic]], [[radiology/ramos-2016-ghost-images-metal-objects-panoramic]])은 모두 파노라마 중심이라, **구내(PA/bitewing) 디지털 영상의 기술적 artifact** 근거가 비어 있었다. 본 5만 장 retrospective 감사는 PSP 영상 artifact를 operator/plate/scanning 3범주로 정량화하고, 특히 **재촬영의 80.8%가 PA**임을 보여 lingam-2023(파노라마)와 짝을 이루는 구내판(intraoral) 유병률 근거가 된다. Dioguardi 2025(해석적 optical artifact)와 함께 PA artifact를 기술적/해석적 두 축으로 커버.

## One-line Summary

Retrospective audit (World J Clin Cases 2022, n=50,000 intraoral PSP radiographs, Riyadh): 7.1% required retakes for image artifacts, of which periapical images were 80.8%; operator errors dominated (70.4% of retakes; cone cut the single most common at 39.5%), followed by plate errors (bite marks 40.3%) and scanning errors (delayed scanning 39.9%), with near-perfect reader agreement (kappa 0.99).

## 한줄요약

후향적 감사(World J Clin Cases 2022, 구내 PSP 영상 5만 장, 리야드): 7.1%가 artifact로 재촬영, 그중 치근단(Periapical, PA) 영상이 80.8%. 술자오류가 재촬영의 70.4%(단일 최다는 cone cut 39.5%), 이어 판(plate) 오류(교흔 bite mark 40.3%)·스캐닝 오류(지연 스캐닝 39.9%); 판독자 일치도 kappa 0.99.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Rare **prevalence** figures for intraoral **PSP (photostimulable phosphor) digital-radiograph artifacts** — a receptor-specific gap vs the well-documented film literature.
- Shows **periapical images bear the brunt** of artifact-driven retakes (80.8%).
- A usable **3-category taxonomy** (operator / plate / scanning) with subtype frequencies and troubleshooting.
- Frames artifacts as a **dose problem** — each retake is avoidable additional radiation.

## 3. Methodology

- 50,000 intraoral digital radiographs (PA + bitewing), April 2018–April 2020; Gendex Expert DC (7 mA/65 kVp), Soredex DIGORA Optime PSP + laser scanner.
- Retakes auto-logged (MIPACS toolkit); two experienced OMF radiologists screened all retakes; 1,000 re-read at 2 weeks for reliability. IRB approved.
- Frequencies/percentages (SPSS v23); interobserver kappa.

## 4. Key Results

- **Retakes**: 3,550/50,000 = **7.1%**; kappa **0.99**.
- **By image type**: PA **2,869 (80.8%)**, bitewing 518 (14.6%), blank 163 (4.6%).
- **Categories**: operator 2,500 (70.4%), plate 685 (19.3%), scanning 365 (10.3%).
- **Operator subtypes**: cone cut 39.5% (988), improper placement 30.4%, projection geometry 22.4%, unexposed 4.5%, packet movement 1.6%, reversed image 0.8%, overexposed 0.6%, bending 0.3%.
- **Plate subtypes**: bite marks 40.3% (276), image size reduction 25.4%, scratches 19.6%, contamination 6.5%, coat peeling 5.7%, damage 2.3%, fingerprints 0.1%.
- **Scanning subtypes**: delayed scanning 39.9% (145), white lines 37.2%, blank 13.4%, black lines 5.5%, double image 4.1%.
- **Most common overall**: bite marks, image size reduction, scratches, delayed scanning.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single institution, single PSP system (Soredex DIGORA Optime) — brand/receptor generalizability limited.
- Retrospective; artifacts counted from retakes only (subclinical artifacts on accepted images not captured).
- No CCD/CMOS comparison arm; no dose quantification.
- Descriptive prevalence, not a diagnostic-accuracy or interpretive-artifact study (see the paired Dioguardi 2025).

## 7. Glossary

- **PSP (photostimulable phosphor storage plate)**: reusable cordless indirect digital receptor forming a latent image read by a laser scanner; film-like but bend/scratch-prone.
- **Cone cut**: unexposed (clear) area where the X-ray beam missed part of the receptor — a collimation/aiming operator error.
- **Delayed scanning artifact**: latent-image fading from ambient-light exposure before scanning → non-uniform/bright image.
- **Bite marks**: plate deformation/indentation from patient biting the plate through its disposable sleeve.
- **Reversed (mirror) image**: PSP plate placed backwards, producing a flipped, often faint herringbone-like image.
- **Retake rate**: fraction of exposures repeated due to unacceptable quality; a dose/quality metric.
