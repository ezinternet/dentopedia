## Why Ingested

Canonical CBCT-based 4-arm RCT from the Zürich group comparing DBBM-C under two different soft-tissue covers vs coated β-TCP vs spontaneous healing — the primary radiographic dimensional evidence behind the "graft under a seal preserves the ridge" consensus our ARP pages summarize. Independently confirms [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/el-sioufi-2023-clinical-evaluation-different-alveolar]]'s "any graft under a seal ≫ seal alone" finding and its sealing-material null (CM ≈ autogenous PG here, CS ≈ FGG there), and supplies the coated-β-TCP underperformance data point that describes the "greater resorption versus xenograft" end of the range [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/pezzella-2026-beta-tcp-alveolar-ridge-preservation-sr]] reports.

## Three-line Summary
Four-arm randomized controlled trial (n=40 patients, University of Zurich) comparing polylactide-coated β-tricalcium phosphate (β-TCP), deproteinized bovine bone mineral with 10% collagen (DBBM-C) covered with a collagen matrix (CM), DBBM-C covered with an autogenous soft-tissue graft (PG), and spontaneous healing (control) for ridge preservation, with cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) at baseline and 6 months.
At 6 months, DBBM-C/CM and DBBM-C/PG markedly limited ridge contraction vs control — worst-case width change 1 mm subcrestal was −1.2/−1.4 mm (−17.4/−18.1%) vs −3.3 mm (−43.3%) for control and −6.1 mm (−77.5%) for β-TCP — and DBBM-C/PG even gained 1.2 mm (8.1%) buccal vertical height; CM and PG covers did not differ (p>0.05).
Under a soft-tissue seal, DBBM-C preserves ridge dimensions far better than spontaneous healing or coated β-TCP, and a collagen matrix seal matches autogenous soft tissue; limitation — abstract-only source, n=40, 6-month horizon.

## 세줄요약
4군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=40명, 취리히 대학교) — 폴락티드 코팅 β-인산삼칼슘 (β-TCP), 탈단백우골-콜라겐 (DBBM-C)+콜라겐 매트릭스 (CM) 피개, DBBM-C+자가 연조직 이식 (PG) 피개, 자연 치유(대조군) 비교, 파생단층촬영 (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT) 기저 및 6개월 측정.
6개월 시 DBBM-C+CM/PG군은 대조군 대비 현저히 적은 치조제 수축 — crest 하방 1 mm 폭 변화는 −1.2/−1.4 mm(−17.4/−18.1%)로 대조군 −3.3 mm(−43.3%), β-TCP −6.1 mm(−77.5%) 대비 우수했고, DBBM-C/PG군은 협측 수직고 +1.2 mm(8.1%) 획득 — CM과 PG 피개는 유의차 없음(p>0.05).
연조직 피개 하의 DBBM-C는 자연 치유·코팅 β-TCP 대비 치조제 치수 보존에 현저히 우수하고 콜라겐 매트릭스 피개는 자가 연조적으로 동등하나, 초록만 확보·n=40·6개월 추적의 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- CBCT-based 3D quantification of vertical (buccal/lingual) and horizontal ridge dimensional changes 6 months after extraction across four ridge-preservation modalities and spontaneous healing.
- DBBM-C covered with either a collagen matrix or an autogenous soft-tissue graft reduced vertical and horizontal ridge change vs spontaneous healing at 6 months.
- Soft-tissue graft coverage (PG) produced no significant advantage over collagen matrix coverage (CM) over DBBM-C (p>0.05 at all three levels) — a soft-tissue autograft is not required for dimensional preservation.
- DBBM-C/PG showed net ridge *gain* on the buccal side (+1.2 mm, 8.1%) and lingual side (+0.3 mm, 5.6%) at 6 months.
- Polylactide-coated β-TCP performed worst in this comparison — greater buccal vertical loss (−2.0 mm, −20.9%) and 1 mm subcrestal width loss (−6.1 mm, −77.5%) than spontaneous healing — highlighting formulation dependence of synthetic grafts.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Four-arm randomized controlled clinical trial, single center (University of Zurich)
- **n**: 40 patients (four randomly assigned treatment modalities)
- **Interventions**: (1) polylactide-coated β-TCP particles; (2) DBBM-C covered with a collagen matrix (CM); (3) DBBM-C covered with an autogenous soft-tissue graft (PG); (4) spontaneous healing (control)
- **Outcomes**: Radiographic vertical (buccal, lingual) and horizontal ridge dimensional changes; CBCT scans after treatment (baseline) and 6 months later
- **Funding**: Predominantly university clinics, in part research grants of Geistlich Pharma AG (Wolhusen) and Degradable Solutions AG (Schlieren); authors declare no conflicts of interest

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only source — full text, per-level measurement tables, and randomization/blinding details not retrievable from the publisher landing-page print.
- n=40 patients across four arms and a single 6-month radiographic endpoint; no histologic outcomes, no longer-term remodeling data.
- Radiographic (CBCT) measures only — soft-tissue and clinical/esthetic outcomes not reported in the extractable text.
- The β-TCP arm tested one specific polylactide-coated formulation; results may not generalize to other β-TCP products (consistent with the heterogeneous evidence later reported for β-TCP in ARP).
- Partly industry-funded (Geistlich Pharma AG — DBBM manufacturer; Degradable Solutions AG), though the authors declare no conflicts of interest.

## 6. Related Work
- el-sioufi-2023: independent 4-arm RCT (Athens) whose "any graft under a seal ≫ seal alone" and sealing-material null (CS ≈ FGG) independently corroborate this trial's DBBM-C/CM ≈ DBBM-C/PG > control result.
- pezzella-2026: β-TCP ARP systematic review whose "variable results — greater resorption versus xenograft" range includes this trial's coated-β-TCP data point.
- pickert-2022: CBCT-only SR+MA of ARP dimensional outcomes — the outcome modality (CBCT ridge dimensional change) and xenograft-advantage finding this RCT contributed to.
- bassir-2018, avila-ortiz-2014: SR+MAs quantifying ARP-vs-extraction-alone dimensional benefit consistent with this trial's control comparison.
- jung-2018: Zürich-group narrative review framing post-extraction timing windows in the esthetic zone — same group's later framework building on this evidence base.

## 7. Glossary
- **ARP (Alveolar Ridge Preservation)**: procedures to minimize post-extraction dimensional ridge change for later implant site development.
- **DBBM-C**: deproteinized bovine bone mineral with 10% collagen (xenograft).
- **β-TCP**: β-tricalcium phosphate — synthetic bone substitute; here a polylactide-coated particulate formulation.
- **Collagen matrix (CM) cover**: acellular collagen matrix used as a soft-tissue seal over the graft.
- **PG (palatal graft)**: autogenous soft-tissue graft harvested from the palate used as a seal over the graft.
- **CBCT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography)**: 3D radiographic imaging used to measure ridge dimensional changes.
