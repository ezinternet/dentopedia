## Three-line Summary

Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants 2003;18(1):31-39: 35 patients, 35 HA-coated immediate implants + immediate provisionals, maxillary anterior, 1yr — 100% survival

MBL -0.26mm

midfacial recession -0.55mm; patients highly satisfied.

## 세줄요약

상악 전치부 즉시 식립·즉시 가철식 보철의 1년 전향적 연구 (IJOMI 2003): 35명, 35개 HA 코팅 임플란트

12개월 생존율 100%, 한계골 소실 -0.26mm (근심)·-0.22mm (원심), 치은 퇴축 -0.55mm

환자 만족도 100% — 심미대를 선택한 빠른 심미 회복과 높은 만족도.

## 1. Key Results
- 35 patients (8 men, 27 women; mean age 36.5yr); maxillary anterior immediate implant + immediate provisionalization
- 35 threaded HA-coated implants; definitive restoration at 6 months
- Evaluations: implant placement, 3, 6, 12 months
- 12-month survival: 100% osseointegrated
- MBL: -0.26±0.40mm mesial; -0.22±0.28mm distal (statistically significant but within clinical range)
- Midfacial gingival level change: -0.55±0.53mm
- Mesial papilla: -0.53±0.39mm; distal papilla: -0.39±0.40mm
- Patient satisfaction: 100% very satisfied; none noticed gingival level changes
