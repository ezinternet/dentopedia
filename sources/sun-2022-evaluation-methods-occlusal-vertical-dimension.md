## Why Ingested

The wiki's occlusal vertical dimension (OVD) cluster is complete-denture-centric ([[complete-denture/alhajj-2017-occlusal-vertical-dimension-complete-denture-review]], fayad-2025, goyal-2026) — none of them carries a clinically actionable workflow for the **dentate/partially edentulous full-mouth rehabilitation** patient. This Korean prosthodontics narrative review closes that gap: it reclassifies all OVD evaluation methods into a two-stage clinical workflow (preclinical articulator determination → intraoral verification with provisionals), quantifies a safe increase range, and supplies Korean-population reference values (Park 2005 vestibular distances; Korean anterior crown lengths).

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (J Korean Acad Prosthodont 2022, ~50 references from 1928–2021) synthesizing the immutability-vs-variability debate on occlusal vertical dimension (OVD), the clinical evidence for changing it, and five families of OVD evaluation methods, reclassified into a two-stage clinical workflow for full-mouth rehabilitation.

Physiologic OVD is a range rather than a fixed point; the stomatognathic system adapts to OVD increases within physiologic limits (symptoms self-limited within 1–2 weeks across the cited series), and OVD reduction per se is not a proven TMD cause; a ≤5 mm anterior increase is concluded to be safe and predictable, bounded by the ~20 mm pure hinge-rotation range from centric relation.

Every single evaluation method is empirical with no consensus gold standard, so the authors propose determining the provisional increase preclinically on the articulator from vestibular-distance reference values (Fayz 21.3/16.7/34.0 mm; Korean Park 20.8/17.3/35.0 mm) with a re-recorded centric relation, then verifying intraorally with provisionals (freeway space, speech, swallowing, esthetics, symptom surveillance) — and note that Korean- and ethnicity-specific normative data are still scarce.

## 세줄요약

대한치과보철학회지 2022 서사 리뷰(참고문헌 약 50편, 1928–2021): 교합 수직 고경 (Occlusal Vertical Dimension, OVD)의 불변성 vs 가변성 논쟁, 수직 고경 변경의 임상 근거, 5가지 평가법군을 정리하고 전악 구강 회복을 위한 2단계 임상 워크플로로 재분류.

생리적 수직 고경은 고정점이 아닌 범위이며, 악구강계 (stomatognathic system)는 생리적 한계 내의 수직 고경 증가에 적응함 (인용 연구들에서 증상은 1–2주 내 자기제한적); 수직 고경 감소 자체가 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD)의 원인이라는 증거는 없음; 전치부 기준 ≤5 mm 거상은 안전·예측 가능하며, 중심위 (Centric Relation, CR)에서 순수 회전 운동 범위 약 20 mm가 이론적 상한.

어느 평가법도 단일 표준이 없으므로, 교합기에서 전정간 거리 참고값 (Fayz 21.3/16.7/34.0 mm; 한국인 Park 20.8/17.3/35.0 mm)과 재채득한 중심위로 전임상 단계에서 잠정 거상량을 정하고, 임시 보철 (provisional prosthesis) 장착 후 자유공극 (Freeway Space)·발음·연하·심미·증상 감시로 구내 검증하는 2단계 워크플로를 제안; 한국인·인종별 규범 데이터 부족이 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Critical synthesis of the **OVD immutability vs variability** debate: the dentoalveolar compensatory mechanism (secondary dentin, continuous eruption, masticatory muscle adaptation) can preserve OVD against wear, but dentin wears ~6× faster than enamel and compensation declines with age — current concept accepts physiologic OVD as a **range**, making OVD change clinically defensible.
- Summarizes the **clinical evidence for OVD increase** across the Christensen/Carlsson/Dahl/Gross/Ormianer series: initial symptoms are self-limited (1–2 weeks), balanced occlusion explains the discrepancy between studies, and long-term (3–11 yr) adaptation is documented.
- Proposes a **five-category taxonomy** of OVD evaluation methods: (1) physiologic rest position + freeway space, (2) quantitative measurements and records (facial proportions, cephalometric, vestibular distances), (3) phonetics, (4) swallowing, (5) patient subjective evaluation.
- Reclassifies those methods into a **two-stage clinical workflow**: preclinical articulator-based determination of the provisional increase (vestibular-distance values, CR re-record with Lucia jig/Leaf gauge/Gothic arch tracer) followed by intraoral verification with provisionals (freeway space, speech, swallowing, esthetics, symptom surveillance).
- Quantifies clinical boundaries: **≤5 mm anterior increase** is safe and predictable in appropriate indications; theoretical maximum is the ~20 mm pure hinge-rotation range from CR; interarch space ≥~4 mm enables comprehensive restoration.
- Provides **Korean-population reference data**: Park 2005 vestibular distances (maxillary 20.8 / mandibular 17.3 / total 35.0 mm) and Korean average anterior crown lengths (maxilla 11.0/9.8/10.5 mm; mandible 8.7/9.1/10.5 mm for central/lateral/canine).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Narrative (non-systematic) literature review; no PRISMA flow, no database list, no risk-of-bias appraisal.
- **Scope**: ~50 references spanning 1928 (Turner & Fox) to 2021 (Goldstein et al. OVD Best Evidence Consensus); English and Korean prosthodontic literature.
- **Structure**: (1) definitions; (2) immutability vs variability of OVD; (3) clinical evidence for OVD change; (4) five-category classification of evaluation methods with reliability appraisal; (5) clinical reclassification into preclinical determination vs intraoral verification; (6) conclusion and research needs.
- **Outcomes discussed**: OVD/VDR/freeway-space relationships, vestibular-distance norms, closest speaking space, adaptation/TMD evidence, increase-amount limits, Korean normative values.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**OVD variability evidence**
- Crothers & Sandham 1993: mean 8.3 mm tooth-structure loss, 5.1 mm compensated by dentoalveolar mechanism → net 3.2 mm facial-height loss.
- Dentin wear rate ~6× enamel under normal functional load (Burak 1999, cited).
- Reduced OVD ↔ TMD: no statistically established causal link (Moreno-Hay & Okeson 2015; Pullinger — posterior tooth loss is the risk factor).

**Adaptation to OVD increase (cited series)**
| Study | Design | Increase | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Christensen 1970 | 20 dentate + 22 edentulous, molar overlays, 3–7 d | molar-region | Symptoms (bruxism, muscle pain) — weaker but more frequent in dentate; attributed by the review to occlusal instability, not VD per se |
| Carlsson 1979 | 6 dentate adults, canine-to-molar splints, balanced, 7 d | full-arch splint | Initial symptoms resolved in 1–2 d; all adapted with new rest position |
| Dahl & Krogstad 1982/1985 | 20 worn dentition, Dahl appliance | partial bite-raising | Transient discomfort; adapted; crowns 3 yr later without TMD symptoms |
| Gough & Setchell 1999 | 45 patients, Dahl-type appliance | localized | 94% reported no discomfort |
| Gross & Ormianer 1994 / Ormianer & Gross 1998 | 6 severe-wear patients, fixed provisionals → final | fixed prosthodontic | Speech/muscle discomfort resolved 1–2 wk; stable at 2 yr |
| Ormianer & Palty 2009 | 30 tooth- and implant-supported patients | 3–5 mm (premolar) | All adapted over 3–11 yr; no muscle or speech problems |

**Freeway space / rest position**
- Niswonger 1934: 200 dentate subjects — 87% had ~3 mm freeway space.
- Pleasure 1951: VDR − 3 mm = OVD (applied to dentate as well).
- Goldstein 2021 consensus: measurement methods inconsistent; individual freeway space ranges from <1 mm to ~10 mm (means mostly 2–4 mm); rest position is a range that changes with age and decreases in parallel with OVD loss (Abduo & Lyons 2012) — rest + 3 mm is unreliable in pathologic occlusion.

**Vestibular distance norms (mm)**
| Source | Maxillary vestibule→incisal | Mandibular vestibule→incisal | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| McGrane 1949 (100 dentate) | 22 | 18 | 40 |
| Ellinger 1968 (radiographic) | 20 | 16.33 | — |
| Fayz 1987 (overbite considered) | 21.3 | 16.7 | 34.0 |
| Park 2005 (Korean adults) | 20.8 | 17.3 | 35.0 (deepest points) |

**Facial/cephalometric references**
- Willis 1930: outer canthus→labial commissure = subnasale→chin.
- McGee 1947: pupil→stomion = glabella→subnasale = subnasale→gnathion.
- Hull & Junghans 1968: upper:lower facial height = 43:57 (cephalometric).
- Orthlieb 2000: OVD estimation formula from gonial angle.

**Phonetics**
- Silverman 1953: closest speaking space (S sound) is constant over life and individual-specific — usable for pre-extraction records.
- Pound 1977: S-space should stay within 1–2 mm.
- M (bilabial) guides the mandible toward rest position but hides tooth relationship; F/V (labiodental) reflect maxillary incisal length/position; Swerdlow 1964 judged phonetics more reliable than other methods.

**Clinical boundaries**
- Pure hinge rotation from CR ≈ 20 mm (average) — theoretical maximum OVD increase.
- ≤5 mm anterior increase: safe and predictable in appropriate indications.
- Interarch space ~4 mm: sufficient for comprehensive restorative treatment (Abduo & Lyons).
- Korean average anterior crown length (mm): maxilla central 11.0 / lateral 9.8 / canine 10.5; mandible 8.7 / 9.1 / 10.5.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review: no systematic search strategy, inclusion criteria, or quality/bias appraisal — conclusions are author synthesis, not graded evidence.
- The cited clinical series are small, mostly uncontrolled and non-randomized, with short follow-up (the authors state this explicitly); splint-based studies differ from fixed-prosthodontic reality.
- No coverage of digital/3D OVD-transfer methods (paper predates their wider adoption) — a gap later reviews address.
- Korean normative data rest on a single 2005 study; the authors call for ethnicity-, age-, and sex-stratified datasets and for more quantitative preclinical methods.

## 7. Glossary

- **Occlusal vertical dimension (OVD / VDO)**: vertical jaw relation at maximum intercuspation.
- **Vertical dimension of rest (VDR)**: vertical jaw relation at physiologic rest position (minimal masticatory muscle activity).
- **Freeway space**: VDR − OVD; the interocclusal clearance at rest (Niswonger: ~3 mm in 87% of dentate subjects; individual range <1–10 mm).
- **Closest speaking space**: minimal maxillo-mandibular distance during sibilant ("S") phonation — individual-constant, used as an OVD guide (Silverman).
- **Dentoalveolar compensatory mechanism**: secondary dentin formation + continuous tooth eruption + masticatory muscle adaptation that offsets tooth wear and preserves OVD.
- **Centric relation (CR)**: condyle–disc assembly position against the posterior slope of the articular eminence, independent of tooth contact.
- **Lucia jig / Leaf gauge / Gothic arch tracer**: devices used to re-record CR at an increased vertical dimension.
- **Vestibular distance (전정간 거리)**: distance between maxillary and mandibular labial vestibules — an anthropometric OVD reference.
