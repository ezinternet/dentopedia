## Why Ingested

The wiki's cracked-tooth cluster treats vertical root fracture (VRF) only as the "usually extract" endpoint of the longitudinal-fracture spectrum ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2025-position-statement-longitudinal-cracks-fractures]] splits it into VRF-NRFT vs VRF-RFT but gives little diagnostic detail) and holds no page that characterizes VRF epidemiology, signs, and radiographic patterns side by side for non-endodontically vs endodontically treated teeth. This review extends that consensus framework with the clinical/radiographic detail, and its treatment tables are the case-report layer that [[wiki/resin-bonding/corbella-2025-surgical-techniques-vertical-root-fractures-sr]] later appraised systematically; it also gives the background for the etiology finding in [[wiki/endodontics/shaping/lee-2026-residual-pericervical-apical-dentine-vertical]].

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (MEDLINE/PubMed/Google Scholar, English-language literature 1978–2021, 106 articles included, no formal risk-of-bias appraisal) comparing vertical root fracture (VRF) in non-endodontically treated teeth (VRFNETT) with endodontically treated teeth (VRFETT) across prevalence, demography, diagnosis, etiology, clinical/radiographic features and treatment.

VRFETT prevalence is reported as 2–25%; most VRFs occur after age 40 (86.79% in one series), VRFNETT patients are older (mean 69 vs 56 years in one series) and more often male and Chinese; deep narrow probing is present in 64–93% of VRFs, a coronally positioned sinus tract in 35–42%, and 13–14% show no radiographic lesion; surgical repair series are small case reports (e.g., 4-META/MMA-TBB replantation survival 88.5% at 12 months falling to 59.3% at 60 months).

Diagnosis needs a combination of probing, bite test, multi-angle radiographs, selective CBCT (limited by intracanal metal and radiopaque fillings) and, when doubt remains, exploratory surgery; evidence is descriptive and extraction remains the default, so long-term prognosis studies of repair techniques are still needed.

## 세줄요약

내러티브 리뷰 (MEDLINE/PubMed/Google Scholar, 1978–2021 영문 문헌, 106편 포함, 편향위험 평가 없음) — 비근관치료치 (Non-Endodontically Treated Teeth) 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)과 근관치료치의 VRF를 유병률·인구학·진단·병인·임상/방사선 소견·치료 면에서 비교.

근관치료치 VRF 유병률은 2–25%로 보고; VRF 대부분이 40세 이후 발생 (한 시리즈 86.79%), 비근관치료치 VRF 환자는 더 고령 (평균 69세 vs 56세, 한 시리즈)이며 남성·중국계에서 더 흔함; 좁고 깊은 치주낭 탐침은 VRF의 64–93%, 치관측 누공 (Sinus Tract)은 35–42%, 방사선상 병소 없음은 13–14%; 수복 보고는 소규모 증례 (예: 4-META/MMA-TBB 의도적 재식립 생존율 12개월 88.5% → 60개월 59.3%).

탐침·교합검사 (Bite Test)·다각도 방사선·선택적 콘빔CT (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT; 근관 내 금속·방사선불투과성 충전물에 의해 제한)·불확실 시 탐색적 외과술의 조합이 필요하며, 근거는 기술적 (descriptive) 수준이고 발치가 기본값이라 수복술식의 장기 예후 연구가 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Side-by-side comparison of VRFETT and VRFNETT (Table 3), arguing that non-endodontically treated VRF is a distinct, possibly under-diagnosed entity whose symptoms mimic endodontic or periodontal disease.
- Consolidates clinical-sign frequencies (Table 1, 9 series, 30–227 teeth each) and radiographic-feature frequencies (Table 2) from the primary VRF series.
- Gives a stepwise diagnostic flowchart (Figure 3) ending in exploratory surgery as the gold standard for visual confirmation.
- Tabulates published repair attempts (Tables 4–6: CO2 laser, calcium hydroxide, fragment removal, resin/GIC re-cementation, bioceramic sealing, intentional replantation).
- Raises genetic/developmental predisposition (patients with multiple VRFs in different teeth) and betel-quid/hard-food chewing habits as hypotheses for the Chinese-population predominance of VRFNETT.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Narrative review (self-described); not a PRISMA systematic review.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, PubMed, Google Scholar; English-language articles 1978–2021.
- **Inclusion / exclusion**: systematic reviews, retrospective cohorts, demographic research, clinical studies, case reports and case series included; in vitro, ex vivo and animal studies excluded. Two researchers screened titles, abstracts and full texts independently; disagreements resolved by discussion.
- **n**: 106 articles included. Some illustrative cases come from NTUH patients (approved by the NTUH Ethics Committee; no cohort data reported).
- **Outcomes**: prevalence, demography, tooth/root distribution, diagnostic methods, etiology/predisposing factors, clinical features, radiographic characteristics, treatment options.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Prevalence**: VRF in endodontically treated teeth about 3.69–25% (several studies 2–5%); the spread is attributed to diagnostic difficulty and loss to recall. Non-endodontically treated VRF is mainly reported in Chinese patients: 40% of 315 VRFs (one survey) and 80% of 51 VRFs (another) were in non-endodontically treated teeth.
- **Demography**: VRFETT shows no clear gender preference; VRFNETT is more common in males (proposed: stronger masticatory force, harder diet). Most VRFs occur at 30–69 years; 86.79% of patients were older than 40 in one series. Mean age 69 (non-endodontically treated) vs 56 (endodontically treated) in one series.
- **Distribution**: VRFETT mainly maxillary premolars and mandibular molars (mesial roots); VRFNETT mainly first molars (maxillary and mandibular) in Chinese cohorts, typically severely attrited with minimal restorations. Roots with small mesiodistal width and oval/flattened cross-section are most susceptible.
- **Clinical signs**: coronally positioned sinus tract 35–42% (up to 60% in one series, Table 1); deep narrow periodontal pocket 64–93% (Table 1 range 40–93% across series); a gutta-percha point traced through a sinus tract running parallel to the PDL strongly suggests VRF; bite test (Tooth Slooth, rubber wheel) reproduces biting pain.
- **Radiographic signs**: displaced fragment, radiolucent line in canal, space between canal wall and filling/post, PDL widening, periradicular halo (37–75% in the series reporting it, Table 2), angular defect, step-like defect, V-shaped defect, resorption along the fracture line, canal-space widening; 13–14% of VRFs show no detectable radiolucency.
- **CBCT**: helpful for VRFNETT; in endodontically treated teeth, metal posts and radiopaque fillings produce artifacts, one in vivo study judged high-resolution CBCT non-diagnostic, and micro-CT at 9 µm voxel is far finer than clinical CBCT; no consensus on CBCT accuracy for VRFETT.
- **Etiology**: iatrogenic (over-preparation, excessive obturation force — though stress studies suggest condensation force alone is rarely the direct cause — excessive post-space preparation) plus predisposing factors (loss of internal/remaining tooth structure, oval canal/root with thin proximal dentin, age-related dentin sclerosis, implant-protective occlusion loading adjacent teeth, heavy chewing habits such as betel quid and bones).
- **Treatment series (Tables 4–6)**: 4-META/MMA-TBB bonded intraoral repair 9/11 good vs extraoral replantation 9/12 good (Sugaya, 23 teeth, 22–33 months); extraoral replantation survival 83.3% at 12 months and 36.3% at 24 months (Hayashi 2002, n=20) and 88.5% / 69.2% / 59.3% at 12 / 36 / 60 months (Hayashi 2004, n=26); silver glass-ionomer plus GTR failed in all 6 teeth (Selden); MTA retrograde fill with collagen membrane healed in 4 teeth at 8–24 months (Floratos); most others are single-tooth reports with follow-up from 3 months to 10 years.
- **Prevention**: conservative instrumentation and flare, minimal passive force for fillings/posts/restorations, fiber posts (modulus similar to dentin) cited as lowering fracture and improving premolar survival, night guards for bruxism/clenching.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative design: no PRISMA flow, no risk-of-bias appraisal, no pooled estimates; study selection from three databases including Google Scholar is not reproducible.
- Treatment evidence is almost entirely single-case reports and small case series with heterogeneous methods and follow-up; the review itself calls for long-term prognosis studies.
- Prevalence ranges mix different denominators (extracted teeth, failed endodontic cases, recall cohorts) and the Chinese-population predominance of VRFNETT is attributed to habits/genetics without causal evidence.
- Sex-difference and masticatory-force explanations are speculative; the statement that fiber posts reduce root fracture rests on cited studies rather than new data.
- Table column layouts for some rows (e.g., Von Arx and Bosshardt in Tables 1–2) were ambiguous in PDF text extraction, so only unambiguous values are quoted above.

## 7. Glossary

- **VRF (Vertical Root Fracture)**: longitudinally oriented fracture of the root.
- **VRFETT / VRFNETT**: VRF in endodontically treated teeth / in non-endodontically treated teeth.
- **Coronally positioned sinus tract**: draining tract near the gingival margin rather than the apex, typical of VRF.
- **Halo radiolucency**: periradicular rarefaction visible around the lateral (or opposite) root surface, a common radiographic VRF sign.
- **4-META/MMA-TBB resin**: dentin-bonding adhesive resin (Super-Bond family) used to re-bond fracture fragments, usually with intentional replantation.
- **Intentional replantation**: extraction, extraoral repair and immediate re-insertion of the tooth.
