## Why Ingested

Adds the only sham/placebo-controlled, pooled-estimate SR+MA on rehabilitation for muscle-related TMD pain to a cluster that is otherwise narrative or non-pooled: [[wiki/tmj/demelo-2020-manual-therapy-myofascial-pain-tmd-sr]] and [[wiki/tmj/calixtre-2015-manual-therapy-tmd-pain-rom-sr]] (no meta-analysis) and [[wiki/tmj/gonzalez-sanchez-2023-tmj-physiotherapy-treatment-sr]] (narrative synthesis). Source: PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3233/BMR-210236)).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42021251904; PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science to 28 April 2021) of 16 sham- or placebo-controlled RCTs of rehabilitative interventions for painful muscle-related temporomandibular disorders (TMD), pain measured on a visual analogue scale.

Pooled overall effect size for pain reduction was 1.44 (p<0.0001); laser therapy was the most studied intervention (6 of 16 RCTs, 37.5%).

Rehabilitation might be effective for muscle-related TMD pain, but few RCTs per technique limit technique-specific conclusions, so the authors urge caution.

## 세줄요약

근육성 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 통증에 대한 재활 중재를 위약·가짜치료와 비교한 16개 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (PROSPERO CRD42021251904, 2021년 4월 28일까지 검색).

통증 감소의 통합 효과크기 (Effect Size, ES)는 1.44 (p<0.0001)였고, 레이저 치료가 16편 중 6편 (37.5%)으로 가장 많이 연구됨.

재활 접근이 근육성 TMD 통증 감소에 효과적일 수 있으나 기법별 RCT 수가 적어 기법별 결론은 제한적이므로 해석에 주의가 필요함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled estimate for rehabilitation versus placebo/sham in painful muscle-related TMD (ES 1.44).
- Maps the trial base: laser therapy dominates (6 of 16 RCTs).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PRISMA-style SR+MA, PROSPERO CRD42021251904. Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, inception to 28 April 2021. Eligible: RCTs in patients with painful muscle-related TMD; rehabilitative interventions; placebo or sham comparators; pain intensity by visual analogue scale. 1997 records screened at title/abstract, 189 full texts assessed, 16 RCTs included.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Overall ES 1.44 (p<0.0001) favoring rehabilitation for pain reduction. 6 of 16 RCTs (37.5%) studied laser therapy. Per-technique estimates, heterogeneity and risk-of-bias results are not in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract-only: heterogeneity (I2), risk of bias, certainty of evidence and subgroup estimates were not available. Authors note the low number of RCTs per conservative technique impairs synthesis by technique.

## 7. Glossary

- Muscle-related TMD: TMD with masticatory muscle pain (myogenous/myofascial).
- Effect size (ES): standardized pooled difference in pain between rehabilitation and sham/placebo.
- Sham: inactive simulated treatment used as control.
- VAS: visual analogue scale for pain intensity.
