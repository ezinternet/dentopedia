## Three-line Summary

Narrative review + personal case series (ENT/otorhinolaryngology perspective, Acta Otorhinolaryngologica Italica 2007) on iatrogenic benign paroxysmal positional vertigo following dental and maxillo-facial surgery.

Authors describe several personal cases of BPPV they judge to be of iatrogenic origin based on temporal relationship to dental/maxillofacial surgical procedures, framed within the broader post-traumatic pathophysiologic model of BPPV (otoconia dislodgement from vibration/positional trauma during surgery).

No causal statistics or incidence figures are given (abstract-only source) — the paper's contribution is establishing dental/maxillofacial surgery as a recognized surgical-trauma risk category for BPPV in the ENT literature, while explicitly cautioning that a definitive cause-effect link cannot be established from case-level temporal association alone.

저자들은 수술과의 시간적 연관성에 근거해 의인성으로 판단한 BPPV 개인 증례 여러 건을 기술하며, BPPV의 외상후(post-traumatic) 병태생리 모델(수술 중 진동·체위 변화로 인한 이석(otoconia) 이탈) 틀 안에서 논의.

발생률·통계 수치는 제시되지 않음(초록만 확보) — 이 논문의 기여는 치과·구강악안면외과 수술을 BPPV의 수술적 외상(surgical trauma) 위험군으로 이비인후과 문헌에 자리매김시킨 것이며, 시간적 연관성만으로는 명확한 인과관계를 확정할 수 없다는 한계를 저자 스스로 명시.

## 7. Glossary
- **BPPV (Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo)**: 양성돌발성두위현훈 — transient vertigo triggered by specific head-position changes, caused by displaced otoconia (calcium carbonate crystals) within the semicircular canals of the inner ear.
- **Iatrogenic**: 의인성 — caused by medical/surgical treatment itself.
- **Otoconia**: 이석 — calcium carbonate crystals in the utricle/saccule that, when dislodged, cause BPPV.
