## Why Ingested
Quantifies aging + adhesive-choice effects on dentin bond strength; supports [[resin-bonding/brkanovic-2023-universal-adhesive-systems-dentin-bond]].

## Three-line Summary

In vitro factorial study (University of Zagreb) comparing shear bond strength (SBS) of multiple universal adhesive systems to dentin under baseline and thermocycled (aged) conditions at one year.

Both thermocycling (p=0.018) and adhesive system choice (p=0.001) significantly affected dentin SBS; systems were statistically comparable at one-year storage.

Absolute SBS values do not directly translate clinically, but the factorial design separates the independent contributions of aging and adhesive brand — relevant for adhesive selection and durability prediction.

## 세줄요약

여러 범용 접착제 시스템을 상아질 전단결합강도(SBS) 기준으로 기저 및 열순환(1년) 조건에서 비교한 인 비트로 요인설계 연구(자그레브 대학).

열순환(p=0.018)과 접착제 종류(p=0.001) 모두 상아질 SBS에 유의한 영향; 1년 보관 후에는 시스템 간 차이가 비교 가능한 수준으로 수렴.

절대 SBS 수치는 임상 직결 해석이 어렵지만, 요인설계로 노화와 접착제 선택의 독립적 기여를 분리했다는 점이 접착제 선택 근거로 유용.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Separates aging vs adhesive-choice contributions to dentin bond strength.
- One-year durability context.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
In-vitro dentin shear bond strength across universal adhesive systems; thermocycling; one-year storage; factorial statistics.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Thermocycling p=0.018; adhesive-system p=0.001; comparable SBS at one year.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
In-vitro; absolute values do not directly translate clinically.

## 7. Glossary
- SBS: shear bond strength.
- Universal adhesive: adhesive usable in E&R, SE, or selective-etch modes.
