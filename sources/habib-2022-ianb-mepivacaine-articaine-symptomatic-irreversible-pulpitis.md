## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=330) comparing 2% mepivacaine vs 4% articaine for inferior alveolar 신경 차단 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) success in mandibular molars with 증상성 비가역 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP).

Overall IANB success rates were similarly low: 35.8% (mepivacaine) vs 41.2% (articaine); relative risk of failure 1.09 [95% CI: 0.92–1.30], not statistically significant; supplemental anesthesia needed in 43.6% vs 38.2% respectively (NS).

Preoperative pain level and patient age predicted need for supplemental anesthesia; agent choice alone does not resolve the hot-tooth anesthesia problem.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 무작위대조시험(n=330): 하악 대구치의 증상성 비가역 치수염 (Symptomatic Irreversible Pulpitis, SIP) 환자에서 2% 메피바카인 (Mepivacaine) vs 4% 아티카인 (Articaine) 하치조신경 차단 (Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block, IANB) 성공률 비교.

IANB 성공률은 두 군 모두 낮았음: 메피바카인 35.8% vs 아티카인 41.2%; 실패 상대위험도 1.09 [95% CI: 0.92–1.30], 유의차 없음; 보조마취 필요율도 43.6% vs 38.2%로 유의차 없음.

술전 통증 수준 및 환자 연령이 보조마취 필요성과 연관; 약제 선택만으로는 "뜨거운 치아 (hot tooth)" 마취 문제를 해결할 수 없음.

## Why Ingested

Large double-blind RCT (n=330) establishing that neither mepivacaine nor articaine reliably achieves 하치조신경 차단 (IANB) success in hot tooth — both fail 59–64% of the time. Directly quantifies the clinical problem of IANB failure in SIP and identifies preoperative pain level and age as predictors for supplemental anesthesia need. Likely included in the alkandari-2024 SR+MA on the same clinical question.
