## Why Ingested
SDA 개념의 적응증 근거를 강화하기 위해 인제스트. 기존 [[removable-partial-denture/mckenna-2018-sda-vs-rpd-older-patients-rct]] 및 [[removable-partial-denture/walter-2021-shortened-dental-arch-15year-rct]]의 배경 SR로 활용 가능하며, SDA 환자에서 어떤 보철 전략이 잔존치 생존을 가장 잘 지지하는지 체계적으로 검토한 첫 SR이다.

## One-line Summary
SR of 10 RCT/prospective/cohort studies concluding there is insufficient evidence to recommend one tooth replacement strategy over another in adults with shortened dental arches (J Dent, 2018).

## 한줄요약
단축치열궁 성인 환자에서 10편(RCT 3편 포함)을 체계 검토한 결과, 어느 보철 전략도 우월하다는 충분한 근거가 없음 (J Dent 2018).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Systematic review of RCTs and non-randomised controlled trials
- **Search databases:** Medline (OVID), EMBASE (OVID), Cochrane CENTRAL
- **Period:** Not specified; published 2018
- **Inclusion:** Adult patients with SDA (3–5 posterior occluding pairs per side); tooth replacement studies
- **Exclusion:** Not specified in abstract
- **Included studies (n=10):**
  - Multicentre RCT Germany (4 publications + 1 pilot)
  - McKenna RCT UK/Ireland (2 publications)
  - Prospective cohort Denmark
  - Prospective cohort Netherlands
- **Outcomes:** Survival rates (prostheses, teeth), OHRQoL, risk of tooth loss

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Primary:** Insufficient evidence to recommend one tooth replacement strategy over another
- **RCT findings:** Heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis
- **RPD evidence:** Mixed; some data on tooth loss risk vs FDP
- **SDA concept:** OHRQoL with SDA comparable to prosthetically restored patients in some studies
- **Limitation note:** Too few good-quality RCTs; heterogeneous reporting

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Insufficient RCTs for meta-analysis (heterogeneous data, non-standard reporting)
- Most evidence from European cohorts — may not generalise to other populations
- CONSORT non-compliance limits data pooling
- Future: standardised outcome reporting, more long-term RCTs

## 7. Glossary
- **SDA:** Shortened Dental Arch — dentition ending at premolars or first molars (≥3–5 posterior occluding pairs per side)
- **ESDA:** Extreme SDA — ≤2 posterior occluding pairs per side
- **OHRQoL:** Oral health-related quality of life
- **PROSPERO:** International Register of Systematic Reviews (registration number CRD42017064851)
