## Why Ingested

The largest direct clinical test of the question "does the bony access window in lateral SFE need covering at all?" — 131 sinuses in 105 patients with three window treatments (collagen barrier, repositioned bone fragment, untreated) and 4.3 ± 1.4 years of CBCT follow-up. It supplies the human, long-term numbers that the animal-dominated bone-lid synthesis [[sinus-lift/lateral/schiavon-2022-bone-lid-technique-lateral-sinus]] and the RCT-only membrane-coverage review [[sinus-lift/lateral/starch-jensen-2019-sinus-augmentation-barrier-membrane-window]] both lack, and its perforation-risk model reinforces the smoking finding of [[sinus-lift/lateral/wang-2023-smoking-schneiderian-membrane-perforation-sinus]].

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-surgeon cohort (105 patients, 131 sinuses, 266 implants, residual bone height 2.7 ± 0.9 mm) comparing three treatments of the bony access window in lateral sinus floor elevation — collagen barrier (CB, 33 sinuses), repositioned bone fragment (RW, 24) and untreated (UT, 64) — with 4.3 ± 1.4 years of CBCT follow-up.

Window treatment had no significant effect on radiographic window healing (morphology, corticalization), augmented bone height change or marginal bone level change (all p > 0.05); implant survival was 100% in CB and UT and 96.9% in RW (2 failures in one patient), augmented bone height loss was 1.3–1.4 mm in every group, and marginal bone level change averaged 0.1 ± 0.5 mm.

Leaving the window untreated healed as well as covering it, which argues against routine barrier placement over the lateral window when the sinus membrane is intact — although the retrospective, non-randomized design and absence of histology limit the strength of that conclusion, and the paper reports 41.2% membrane perforation with smoking (OR 6.84) as the dominant modifiable risk factor.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일술자 코호트 (환자 105명, 상악동 131개, 임플란트 266개, 잔존골 높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) 2.7 ± 0.9 mm) — 측방접근 상악동거상술 (Lateral Sinus Floor Elevation, LSFE)의 골성 접근창 (Bony Access Window) 처리 3가지, 즉 콜라겐 차폐막 (Collagen Barrier, CB) 33개·골편 재위치 (Repositioned Window, RW) 24개·무처치 (Untreated, UT) 64개를 평균 4.3 ± 1.4년 추적한 연구.

창 처리 방법은 창부위 방사선학적 치유 (형태·피질화), 이식골 높이 변화, 변연골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 변화 어디에도 유의한 영향을 주지 않았고 (모두 p > 0.05), 임플란트 생존율은 CB·UT 100%, RW 96.9% (1명에서 2개 실패), 이식골 높이 감소는 전 군 1.3–1.4 mm, MBL 변화는 평균 0.1 ± 0.5 mm였다.

슈나이더막 (Schneiderian Membrane)이 온전할 때 창을 덮지 않아도 덮은 경우와 치유 결과가 같아 일상적 차폐막 적용을 정당화하지 못하지만, 후향적·비무작위 설계와 조직학적 평가 부재가 근거 강도를 제한하며, 점막 천공률 41.2%에 대해 흡연이 가장 큰 조절 가능 위험인자 (교차비 (Odds Ratio, OR) 6.84)로 확인되었다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First clinical study to compare **three** window-handling strategies (collagen barrier vs repositioned bone fragment vs no treatment at all) in the same lateral-SFE cohort, rather than the usual two-group membrane-vs-no-membrane design.
- Reports that an untreated bony access window heals with a flat, fully corticalized surface at 4.3 ± 1.4 years — 73.4% full corticalization in the untreated group, versus 90.9% (CB) and 85.3% (RW), a difference that did not reach significance — with no graft particle scattering out of the sinus cavity in any group.
- Quantifies long-term augmented bone height shrinkage for three biphasic calcium phosphate (BCP) ratios: 1.3 ± 2.2 mm (CB, 4.8 ± 1.4 y), 1.4 ± 1.8 mm (RW, 3.6 ± 1.2 y), 1.4 ± 1.6 mm (UT, 4.4 ± 1.4 y) — all below 1.5 mm, with remodelling concentrated between prosthesis insertion and last follow-up.
- Identifies smoking (OR 6.84) and low residual bone height (OR 0.64 per +1 mm) as significant predictors of Schneiderian membrane perforation, in a cohort where 54/131 sinuses (41.2%) perforated yet none developed abnormal sino-nasal symptoms.
- Provides a large-sample (266 implants) confirmation that the window treatment itself does not drive implant survival or marginal bone stability, narrowing the remaining justification for a barrier to the perforated-membrane / graft-containment scenario.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort (chart + CBCT review), single experienced periodontist, patients treated January 2016 – December 2020 in a private clinic.
- **n**: 105 patients / 131 sinuses / 266 implants. Patient-level groups: CB 29, RW 28, UT 48. Sinus-level groups: CB 33, RW 24, UT 64. Implant-level: CB 72, RW 64, UT 130.
- **Inclusion**: good general health, > 20 years, edentulous posterior maxilla with sinus pneumatization, residual bone height < 5 mm, adequate oral hygiene. **Exclusion**: systemic contraindications (uncontrolled metabolic disease, head/neck radiotherapy or chemotherapy), local sino-nasal contraindications, untreated periodontal disease, heavy smoking (> 10 cigarettes/day).
- **Surgery**: 2.0 g amoxicillin 1 h pre-op; full-thickness flap; round bur outline with greenstick fracture of the bony window (window ≈ 15–20 mm mesio-distal × 6–10 mm apico-coronal); membrane elevation with sinus curettes (DASK kit, Dentium); synthetic BCP graft (Osteon 1/2/3, Genoss) and simultaneous implant placement (Implantium, Dentium) in all cases; sub-crestal placement avoided when RBH < 2 mm.
- **Groups**: CB = cross-linked collagen membrane (Genoss Collagen Membrane) extending 2–3 mm beyond the window margin; RW = detached bone fragment repositioned; UT = no treatment of the window.
- **Imaging timepoints**: T0 (pre-op), T1 (immediately post-op), T2 (final prosthesis insertion), T3 (last follow-up); CBCT superimposed in OnDemand3D, bone height measured along the implant long axis on matched sagittal sections.
- **Outcomes**: implant survival; radiographic healing of the window (morphology flat/protruded/depressed, corticalization full/partial, protrusion extent); change in augmented bone height (T1–T2, T1–T3, T2–T3); marginal bone level change (T2–T3, mesial/distal averaged); sinus membrane perforation.
- **Covariates**: sex, diabetes mellitus (DM), cardiovascular disease, smoking, window treatment, bone-substitute material, age, implant diameter and length, sinus width, sinus angle, lateral wall thickness, residual bone height at T0.
- **Statistics**: SAS 9.4; mixed binomial logistic model (perforation), mixed multinomial logistic model (window healing), mixed linear models (bone height, marginal bone level), patient- and implant-level clustering accounted for; α = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Cohort**: age 62.1 ± 11.1 years, 62 male / 43 female, 77 non-smokers, 28 smokers, follow-up 4.3 ± 1.4 years; implant length 10.2 ± 0.6 mm, diameter 4.5 ± 0.4 mm.
- **Implant survival**: 100% (CB), 96.9% (RW), 100% (UT). Two failures in one RW patient from loss of osseointegration, both before prosthesis insertion; 264/266 implants stable thereafter. Too few events for statistical modelling of failure.
- **Augmented bone height**: RBH 2.7 ± 0.9 mm at T0 → 17.4 ± 2.7 mm at T1 → 16.4 ± 2.8 mm at T2 → 16.0 ± 2.9 mm at T3. Height loss 1.3 ± 2.2 mm (CB), 1.4 ± 1.8 mm (RW), 1.4 ± 1.6 mm (UT) — all < 1.5 mm; window treatment not significant (p > 0.05).
- **Window morphology** (flat / protruded): CB 60.6% / 33.3%, RW 82.4% / 17.6%, UT 68.8% / 28.1%; depressed rare, none in RW. Protrusion extent 0.4 ± 0.6 (CB), 0.4 ± 0.7 (RW), 0.5 ± 0.7 mm (UT). No particle scattering outside the sinus; unintegrated particles rarely seen.
- **Corticalization**: full in 90.9% (CB), 85.3% (RW), 73.4% (UT); only two sites lacked corticalization (one CB, one UT). Window treatment not significant (p > 0.05) for morphology or corticalization.
- **Marginal bone level**: 0.1 ± 0.5 mm overall (mesial 0.1 ± 0.4, distal 0.2 ± 0.6). DM and window treatment were statistically significant (p < 0.05) in the model, but all changes were < 0.5 mm — judged clinically negligible.
- **Sinus membrane perforation**: 54/131 sinuses (41.2%), tears 2–4 mm, managed by elevating with a Prichard periosteal elevator and folding the membrane over the tear without a collagen patch; no post-healing sino-nasal symptoms. Significant predictors: sex OR 0.23 (female vs male), smoking OR 6.84 (vs non-smoker), residual bone height OR 0.64 per +1.0 mm (p < 0.05).
- **Other significant covariates**: morphology was affected by sex, DM, smoking, implant length and diameter, sinus angle and width, lateral wall thickness and RBH at T0. Protrusion extent was affected by DM and sinus width. Height change T1–T3 was less with a thicker lateral wall (B 0.58, 95% CI 0.09–1.08, p = 0.0211); height change T2–T3 was greater with Osteon 3 than Osteon 2 (B −0.48, 95% CI −0.84 to −0.11, p = 0.0108).
- **Graft materials**: all BCP — Osteon 1 = 70% HA + 30% β-TCP; Osteon 2 = 30% HA + 70% β-TCP; Osteon 3 = 60% HA + 40% β-TCP.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective and non-randomized: group allocation followed clinical judgement, so selection bias cannot be excluded (baseline RBH differed — 2.3 mm CB vs 3.2 mm RW).
- No histology or re-entry; healing of the window and graft integration were assessed on CBCT only, whose resolution cannot resolve bone-substitute particles at the cortical level.
- Window size was not standardized (15–20 × 6–10 mm), varying with implant number and local anatomy.
- All surgeries by one experienced surgeon — good internal consistency, reduced external validity.
- Unbalanced group sizes (UT 64 sinuses vs RW 24) and only 2 implant failures — underpowered for survival analysis; authors explicitly note that failure risk factors, including window treatment, could not be modelled.

## 6. Related Work

- **Schiavon 2022** (bone lid SR+MA): pooled animal data favouring the repositioned bone lid over resorbable membrane for new bone formation, with only two small clinical studies (all < 15 months) — the present study is the long-term clinical test of exactly that comparison.
- **Starch-Jensen 2019** (SR+MA, 6 RCTs): no survival or new-bone difference with vs without lateral-window barrier coverage; the present cohort extends that conclusion to a longer horizon and adds a third arm (repositioned fragment).
- **Barone 2013 / Imai 2020** (cited within): two-group clinical comparisons (membrane vs no membrane) showing no difference in new bone formation (30.7% vs 28.1%) or height/area change (0.6 vs 0.8 mm) — superseded in scale by this 131-sinus cohort.
- **Ohayon et al.** (cited within): reported graft displacement of 0.5 ± 0.4 mm with a barrier vs 3.8 ± 3.1 mm without at 6 months, a discrepancy the authors attribute to their much longer follow-up allowing displaced material to resorb.
- **Molnár 2022** (cited within): re-entry histology at 6 months showing integrated bone fragments and particles embedded in new bone — the direct-observation counterpart the present radiographic study could not provide.
- **Wang 2023** (smoking → perforation SR+MA): independently supports the OR 6.84 smoking effect found here.

## 7. Glossary

- **Bony access window (lateral window)**: the osteotomy in the lateral wall of the maxillary sinus created to reach and elevate the Schneiderian membrane in lateral SFE.
- **Lateral sinus floor elevation (LSFE)**: SFE via a lateral antrostomy, indicated mainly when residual bone height ≤ 4–5 mm.
- **Collagen barrier (CB)**: cross-linked resorbable collagen membrane placed over the window and 2–3 mm onto the surrounding bone, following guided bone regeneration logic.
- **Repositioned window / bone lid (RW)**: the detached lateral-wall bone fragment returned to its original position as an autologous barrier.
- **Untreated (UT)**: window left uncovered, healing by blood clot and spontaneous corticalization.
- **Corticalization**: radiographic appearance of a continuous cortical bone layer over the healed window, scored full or partial on CBCT.
- **Biphasic calcium phosphate (BCP)**: synthetic graft of hydroxyapatite (HA) and beta-tricalcium phosphate (β-TCP); the HA:β-TCP ratio governs resorption speed vs dimensional stability.
- **Residual bone height (RBH)**: crest-to-sinus-floor height before elevation; the primary determinant of approach (lateral vs transcrestal) and a perforation risk factor.
- **Mixed (multilevel) model**: regression that accounts for clustering of multiple sinuses/implants within patients.
- **Marginal bone level (MBL) change**: crestal bone loss measured on the mesial and distal aspects between prosthesis insertion (T2) and last follow-up (T3).
