## Three-line Summary

Single-blind, randomized, parallel clinical trial (n=30 active fixed-orthodontic patients, King Saud University, Riyadh) comparing water jet flossing (n=18) vs conventional interdental flossing (n=12), each combined with manual toothbrushing, over 2 weeks.

Both groups significantly reduced Plaque Index and Gingival Bleeding Index from baseline to day 14 (interdental: PI −16.13%, BI −23.57%, both p≤0.01; water jet: PI −21.87%, BI −32.29%, both p<0.0001); the between-group difference favored water jet flossing numerically but was not statistically significant (PI p=0.279, BI p=0.172).

Water jet flossing and interdental flossing are both effective adjuncts to toothbrushing for orthodontic patients with no significant superiority of one over the other; small sample size (n=30, unequal groups 12 vs 18) and short 2-week follow-up limit generalizability and preclude long-term/gingival-index-grade conclusions.

## 세줄요약

단일맹검 무작위 병렬(parallel) 임상시험(교정 고정장치 활성 치료 중인 환자 n=30, 사우디 킹사우드대): 워터젯 플로싱(water jet flosser, n=18) vs 전통적 치간치실(interdental floss, n=12)을 칫솔질과 병용해 2주간 비교.

두 군 모두 기저치 대비 치태지수(PI)와 치은출혈지수(BI)가 유의하게 감소(치실군: PI −16.13%, BI −23.57%, 모두 p≤0.01; 워터젯군: PI −21.87%, BI −32.29%, 모두 p<0.0001); 군간 비교에서는 워터젯이 수치상 우세했으나 통계적으로 유의하지 않음(PI p=0.279, BI p=0.172).

워터젯 플로싱과 치간치실 모두 교정 환자의 칫솔질 보조 도구로 효과적이며 어느 한쪽의 유의한 우월성은 없음; 소규모 표본(n=30, 12 vs 18 불균형)과 짧은 2주 추적기간이 일반화 가능성을 제한하고 치은지수 등급 변화 등 장기적 결론은 도출할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of only a handful of published RCTs directly comparing water jet flossing vs conventional interdental flossing specifically in patients undergoing active fixed-orthodontic treatment.
- Uses three validated periodontal indices (Bleeding Index, Plaque Index, Gingival Index grading 0–3) recorded at six representative FDI teeth (16, 12, 24, 36, 32, 44) at baseline and 2 weeks.
- Reinforces (via an independent parallel-design RCT) the broader interdental-cleaning literature's conclusion that oral irrigators are non-inferior — but also non-superior — to string floss in orthodontic patients.
- Reports both between-group (water jet vs floss) and within-group (baseline vs 2-week) comparisons, distinguishing "both effective" from "neither superior."

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: single-blind, randomized, parallel clinical study; clinician performing outcome assessment was blinded to group allocation.
- **Setting**: Orthodontic Clinics, King Saud University, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.
- **Sample size**: calculated via n = [(Zα/2+Zβ)×{2(Sd)}]²/Δ² using mean BI difference of 25 and SD 22.79, 80% power, α=5% → 15 subjects/group planned (30 total); actual randomization yielded an unequal split (12 interdental floss vs 18 water jet).
- **Inclusion**: healthy adults ≥18 y, ≥24 natural teeth (excluding 3rd molars), <5 mm anterior crowding/spacing with adequate overjet/overbite, adequate manual dexterity, good periodontal health with no urgent treatment need.
- **Exclusion**: immunologic/inflammatory/liver disease, diabetes, pregnancy, dexterity-limiting conditions, recent gingiva-altering medications (anti-sialagogues, steroids, antibiotics), active periodontal disease, removable orthodontic appliances, no mouthwash use.
- **Intervention**: all participants given soft manual toothbrush + fluoridated toothpaste, brushing 2×/day for 2 minutes; assigned intervention (water jet flosser or interdental floss) used once daily at night; no other hygiene aids permitted (no mouthwash, toothpicks). Verbal/visual/written instruction given at baseline and reinforced on day 14.
- **Outcome measures**: Bleeding Index (BI, presence/absence of bleeding on probing across 4 tooth surfaces), Plaque Index (PI, presence/absence of supragingival plaque across 4 surfaces), Gingival Index (GI, 0–3 grade scale) — all recorded at 6 FDI-numbered index teeth (16, 12, 24, 36, 32, 44) at baseline and day 14.
- **Statistics**: SPSS v26; descriptive statistics (median, IQR); Mann-Whitney U (between-group) and Wilcoxon signed-rank (within-group) for non-normally distributed PI/BI; Fisher's exact test for GI grade distribution; significance threshold p≤0.05.

|---|---|---|---|
| PI baseline (median %) | 37.50 | 36.45 | 0.932 |
| PI 2-week (median %) | 21.37 | 14.58 | 0.279 |
| BI baseline (median %) | 47.91 | 5.61 pt higher (mean diff) | 0.518 |
| BI 2-week (median %) | 20.83 | 15.62 | 0.172 |
| PI change baseline→2wk (median % diff) | 16.13 (p=0.010) | 21.87 (p<0.0001) | — |
| BI change baseline→2wk (median % diff) | 23.57 (p=0.002) | 32.29 (p<0.0001) | — |

- GI grades: interdental group 83.3%→91.7% grade 2 (16.7%→8.3% grade 3); water jet group 94.4%→100% grade 2 (5.6%→0% grade 3) — no statistically significant within- or between-group GI change.
- Both interventions produced statistically significant within-group reductions in PI and BI from baseline to 2 weeks; between-group differences (favoring water jet numerically) did not reach significance for either PI or BI.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small total sample (n=30) with unequal group allocation (12 vs 18) despite equal-group sample size calculation, reducing statistical power for between-group comparisons.
- Short 2-week follow-up — cannot assess long-term periodontal outcomes or sustained compliance.
- Single-center, single ethnic/geographic population (Saudi orthodontic clinic patients) limits generalizability.
- No long-term GI-grade shift was captured; possible floor effect since most participants started at GI grade 2.
- Authors call for larger, longer-duration, more diverse-population trials to confirm water jet flossing's specific benefit, comfort, and compliance advantages in orthodontic patients.

## 6. Related Work

- Abdellatif et al. 2021 — split-mouth RCT, both floss and water flosser reduced plaque in fair-to-poor oral hygiene patients.
- Xu et al. 2023 — water flossing + brushing outperformed brushing alone for gingival/sulcus bleeding index in gingivitis patients.
- Lyle et al. 2020 — water flosser + electric toothbrush outperformed electric toothbrush alone (n=70).
- Goyal et al. 2018 — manual brush + water flosser outperformed manual brush alone over 4 weeks for bleeding/gingival health.
- Worthington et al. 2019 (Cochrane-type SR) — oral irrigation adjunct to brushing improves outcomes vs brushing alone.
- Sharma et al. 2008 — similar bleeding reduction from water flosser and regular flossing in orthodontic adolescents; water flosser surpassed floss/brushing alone for whole-mouth/interproximal plaque at 2–4 weeks.
- Mazzoleni et al. 2019 — dissenting finding: dental water jet did not significantly enhance home oral hygiene efficacy in multi-bracket orthodontic patients.
- Sawan et al. (already in wiki as [[interdental-cleaning/sawan-2022-super-floss-water-flosser-orthodontic-rct]]) — super floss vs water flosser equally effective overall, water flosser superior at distal molar interproximal surface.
- Caccianiga et al. 2022 — oral irrigator + sonic toothbrush combination may reduce caries/gingivitis risk in orthodontic patients.

## 7. Glossary

- **Water jet flosser / oral irrigator**: a device delivering a pulsating pressurized water stream to dislodge interdental plaque and debris (e.g., Waterpik-type devices).
- **Interdental (string) flossing**: mechanical removal of interproximal plaque using threaded dental floss.
- **Plaque Index (PI)**: presence/absence scoring of supragingival plaque on tooth surfaces.
- **Gingival Bleeding Index (BI)**: presence/absence of bleeding upon periodontal probing.
- **Gingival Index (GI)**: 0–3 grading of gingival inflammation severity (0=normal, 3=severe with spontaneous bleeding tendency).
- **FDI tooth numbering system**: two-digit international tooth notation used to specify the index teeth examined (16, 12, 24, 36, 32, 44).
- **CONSORT**: Consolidated Standards of Reporting Trials — reporting guideline framework followed for the trial flow diagram.
