## Three-line Summary

Three-period randomized crossover within-subject trial (n = 24, mean age 59.0 years; ISRCTN49105064) in patients with a fully dentate maxilla and unilateral distal-extension loss of two or three mandibular teeth, comparing a conventional metal-clasp RPD, a unilateral nonmetal clasp denture, and a shortened dental arch (no prosthesis), each used for two-week periods.

Both the unilateral nonmetal clasp denture (P = .002) and the shortened dental arch (P = .049) outperformed the conventional RPD on total OHIP, with medium and small effect sizes; the nonmetal clasp denture and SDA were equivalent to each other, both showed medium effects in the oral function dimension, and the nonmetal clasp denture was distinctly better on orofacial appearance than either conventional RPD (P = .001) or SDA (P = .006).

For a few unilaterally missing posterior teeth, the conventional clasp denture was the least-preferred of the three options tested and doing nothing was not inferior to it — while sex significantly modified the SDA result (P = .033), so patient-level preference should be elicited rather than assumed.

## 세줄요약

3기간 무작위 교차 (crossover) 동일피험자 내 대조 임상시험 (n = 24, 평균 59.0세; ISRCTN49105064): 상악은 완전 유치악이고 하악에 편측 원심 결손 2~3치가 있는 환자에서 통상 금속 클라스프 국소의치 · 편측 비금속 클라스프 의치 (Nonmetal Clasp Denture, NMCD) · 단축치열 (Shortened Dental Arch, SDA, 무보철)을 각 2주씩 사용시켜 비교했다.

비금속 클라스프 의치 (P = .002)와 단축치열 (P = .049) 모두 총 구강건강영향지수 (Oral Health Impact Profile, OHIP)에서 통상 국소의치보다 우수했고 (각각 중간·작은 효과크기), NMCD와 SDA는 서로 동등했으며, 둘 다 **구강기능** 영역에서 중간 효과를 보였고, NMCD는 **구강안면 외모** 영역에서 통상 국소의치 (P = .001)·SDA (P = .006) 양쪽보다 뚜렷이 우수했다.

편측 소수 구치 결손에서는 **통상 클라스프 의치가 세 선택지 중 가장 선호되지 않았고, 아무것도 하지 않는 것이 그보다 못하지 않았다** — 다만 성별이 SDA 결과를 유의하게 조절했으므로 (P = .033), 환자별 선호를 가정하지 말고 물어야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Within-subject crossover** — each participant experiences all three options, removing between-patient confounding that every other comparison in this category carries.
- Includes **doing nothing (SDA)** as a randomised arm, not merely as an observational comparator.
- Separates OHIP **dimensions**, showing the nonmetal denture's advantage is concentrated in appearance while function is shared with SDA.
- Detects a **sex effect** on the SDA result, a patient-level modifier rarely reported.
- Addresses the small unilateral distal-extension case — the commonest real-world RPD indication.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Three-period randomized crossover, within-subject controlled trial at a private practice. Twenty-four participants (mean 59.0 years; 7 men 62.6 [45–85], 17 women 57.5 [24–75]), all fully dentate in the maxilla with unilateral distal-extension loss of two or three mandibular teeth. Allocated to one of six sequence groups; each prosthesis (or nothing) used for two-week periods. OHIP questionnaire completed at the end of each intervention. Differences estimated with a mixed-effects model (α = .05).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **n = 24** at a single private practice — small, and the sex split (7 men / 17 women) makes the sex-effect finding fragile.
- **Two-week exposure periods.** Two weeks captures initial acceptance, not adaptation, maintenance burden, or the abutment consequences that accrue over years. A conventional cast RPD may be disadvantaged by being judged before adaptation.
- **Scoring-direction ambiguity**: the abstract reports *higher* total OHIP scores for the arms it concludes were *better*, which is inverted relative to the standard OHIP convention where lower is better. The instrument variant is not recoverable from the abstract. **The conclusion statement (NMCD and SDA better than conventional RPD) is what this page carries forward**, not the raw score direction.
- Washout period, carry-over testing and dropout are not reported in the abstract.
- Nonmetal clasp dentures are generally considered interim rather than definitive prostheses in many guidelines; short-term preference should not be read as an endorsement for long-term use.
- Applies specifically to **2–3 unilaterally missing mandibular teeth** with an opposing complete dentition; do not extrapolate to bilateral or extensive loss.

## 7. Glossary

- **Crossover trial (교차설계 임상시험)**: each participant receives all interventions in randomised sequence, serving as their own control.
- **NMCD (비금속 클라스프 의치, Nonmetal Clasp Denture)**: removable denture with thermoplastic resin clasps instead of cast metal, chosen mainly for esthetics.
- **SDA (단축치열, Shortened Dental Arch)**: deliberately leaving posterior teeth unreplaced.
- **OHIP dimensions (OHIP 영역)**: oral function, orofacial appearance, orofacial pain, psychosocial impact.
- **Mixed-effects model (혼합효과모형)**: regression accommodating repeated measures within participants.
