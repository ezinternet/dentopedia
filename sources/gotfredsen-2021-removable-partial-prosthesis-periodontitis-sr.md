## Why Ingested

RPD 선택 결정에서 가장 자주 인용되는 **금기 논리** — "치주염 병력이 있으면 국소의치는 남은 치아를 망친다" — 를 직접 검증한 유일한 워크숍급 문헌고찰이라 spine으로 넣었다. [[wiki/removable-partial-denture/drummond-2024-rpd-long-term-periodontal-health-sr-ma]]가 일반 인구에서 치주 지표가 대체로 안정적임을 보였다면, 본 리뷰는 **가장 취약한 하위집단(치주염 환자)** 에서 같은 질문을 던지고 비교군을 넷(무처치·단축치열·고정성 보철·RPD)으로 세운다. [[wiki/removable-partial-denture/walter-2021-shortened-dental-arch-15year-rct]]가 무작위 비교로 못 다룬 치주 위험 축을 채운다. 위키의 치주 축([[wiki/overviews/periodontics-treatment-ladder]])과 수복 축을 잇는 연결점이기도 하다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (EFP workshop supplement; literature 1966–2020, PRISMA, RoB 2.0 + Newcastle-Ottawa) asking whether removable dental prostheses (RDPs) are more efficacious than no treatment, a shortened dental arch (SDA), or tooth-supported fixed dental prostheses (FDPs) in partially edentulous patients with periodontitis.

Two retrospective studies found RDPs raised tooth-loss risk relative to FDPs in patients with a periodontitis history, while prospective studies found RDPs could be maintained long-term without significant periodontal destruction; heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis, several studies showed increased plaque accumulation, and RDPs improved OHRQoL less than SDA or FDP treatment.

The authors conclude there is no strong evidence that RDPs *per se* cause periodontal destruction or tooth loss — the risk travels with plaque control and maintenance rather than with the appliance, which makes supportive periodontal therapy, not avoidance of the RPD, the operative decision.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (유럽치주학회 European Federation of Periodontology, EFP 워크숍 특집; 1966–2020 문헌, PRISMA·RoB 2.0·Newcastle-Ottawa): 치주염이 있는 부분무치악 환자에서 가철성 국소의치 (Removable Dental Prosthesis, RDP)가 무처치·단축치열 (Shortened Dental Arch, SDA)·치아지지 고정성 보철 (Fixed Dental Prosthesis, FDP)보다 유효한가를 물었다.

후향 연구 2편에서는 치주염 병력 환자에서 RDP가 FDP 대비 치아상실 위험을 높였으나, 전향 연구들에서는 RDP를 장기간 유지해도 유의한 치주 파괴가 없었다; 이질성 때문에 메타분석은 불가했고, 여러 연구에서 RDP가 **치태 축적을 증가**시켰으며, 구강건강관련 삶의 질 (Oral Health-Related Quality of Life, OHRQoL) 개선폭은 SDA·FDP보다 작았다.

저자 결론은 **"RDP 자체가 치주 파괴나 치아상실을 일으킨다는 강한 근거는 없다"** 는 것이다. 위험은 장치가 아니라 **치태 조절과 유지관리**를 따라 움직이므로, 임상 결정은 "국소의치를 피한다"가 아니라 "치주 유지치료를 붙인다"가 된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Frames the RPD question with **four comparators at once** (no treatment / SDA / FDP / RDP) rather than the usual two.
- Directly tests the most common contraindication argument in the highest-risk subgroup and does not find strong support for it.
- Separates *appliance* risk from *plaque* risk — the increased plaque accumulation is consistent across studies even where periodontal destruction is not.
- Places RDP below SDA and FDP on OHRQoL gain, which matters when outcomes are otherwise similar.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PRISMA-guided systematic review commissioned for an EFP workshop supplement. Electronic search covering 1966–2020. Two authors independently screened for eligibility. Risk of bias assessed with RoB 2.0 (randomised) and the Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (observational). Data heterogeneity precluded quantitative synthesis, so findings are reported narratively by comparator and outcome domain.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Domain | Finding | Evidence base |
|---|---|---|
| Tooth loss vs FDP | RDP increased risk in patients with periodontitis history | 2 retrospective studies |
| Periodontal destruction over time | RDP maintainable without significant destruction | Prospective studies |
| Plaque accumulation | Increased with RDP | Several studies |
| Masticatory efficiency / nutrition | Limited effect | Narrative |
| OHRQoL | Improved, but less than SDA; less than FDP incl. resin-bonded FDP | Narrative |
| Quantitative pooling | **Not possible** (heterogeneity) | — |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **No meta-analysis**: every comparison above is narrative, so no effect size or confidence interval is attached to "increased risk of tooth loss."
- The tooth-loss signal rests on **two retrospective studies**, the weakest design in the review, while the reassuring signal comes from prospective studies — a discordance the abstract does not resolve.
- Ingested **abstract-only**: included-study count, follow-up ranges, and per-study risk-of-bias judgements are unavailable.
- Confounding by indication is intrinsic: patients who receive RDPs rather than FDPs typically have fewer or weaker remaining teeth to begin with.
- Maintenance intensity is not standardised across included studies, yet it is the variable the conclusion implicitly rests on.

## 7. Glossary

- **RDP (가철성 국소의치, Removable Dental Prosthesis)**: removable prosthesis replacing missing teeth; used here interchangeably with RPD.
- **FDP (고정성 보철, Fixed Dental Prosthesis)**: tooth-supported fixed bridge; includes resin-bonded designs.
- **SDA (단축치열, Shortened Dental Arch)**: deliberately ending the arch at the premolars.
- **OHRQoL (구강건강관련 삶의 질, Oral Health-Related Quality of Life)**: patient-reported construct usually measured with OHIP.
- **Newcastle-Ottawa Scale**: risk-of-bias instrument for observational studies.
