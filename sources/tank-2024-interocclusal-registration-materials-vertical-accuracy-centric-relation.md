## Why Ingested

위키에 interocclusal/bite registration **재료**를 다룬 페이지가 없었다 — 폐구 시 어떤 교합채득재가 수직 오차를 가장 적게 만드는지는 매일 chairside에서 마주치는 질문인데 근거가 비어 있었다. 본 임상 연구(Tank 2024, n=30, 치아상태 dentate 20–45세)는 alu wax·hard wax·polyvinylsiloxane(Jetbite)·bis-acrylic resin(Luxabite) 4종을 centric relation(CR) 채득 시 수직 정확도로 직접 비교해 그 공백을 메운다. [[occlusion/kattadiyil-2021-relationship-centric-occlusion-maximal-intercuspal]]가 centric occlusion(CO)과 maximal intercuspal position(MIP)이 대부분 일치하지 않는다는 근거를 제시했다면, 본 논문은 그 CR을 실제로 "재료로" 채득하는 행위 자체가 수직 방향으로 얼마나 오차를 더하는지를 정량화한다.

**주의 (본문에 그대로 반영)**: JPBS 부록(Suppl 4) 3페이지 논문 — 본지보다 동료심사가 약한 포맷. 초록은 수치 없이 형용사(“가장 낮음/높음”)만 제공해 PMC 전문에서 직접 수치를 채굴해야 했고, 채굴 결과도 **왼쪽 표(Table 1)만 완전하게 생존**했다 — 오른쪽 표는 PMC 전문 추출 과정에서 유실되어 정성적 서술("MIP 최저, hard wax 최고")만 남았다. `## Results`에서 이 비대칭을 숨기지 않고 명시한다.

## Three-line Summary

Clinical cross-sectional study (n=30 dentate subjects, 20–45 y) comparing four interocclusal registration materials — alu wax, hard wax, polyvinylsiloxane (PVS, Jetbite), and bis-acrylic resin (Luxabite) — for vertical accuracy in recording centric relation (CR), against a direct-closure maximum intercuspation (MIP) baseline, on a Hanau Wide Vue articulator with Lucia jig stabilization.

On the recovered left-side data (Table 1), mean vertical readings were MIP 48.05±9.01, ALU 54.28±8.98, HARD 58.48±9.42, JET 50.24±8.95, LUX 50.11±8.94 (unit not stated in text, presumed mm); computed discrepancy from MIP was largest for hard wax (+10.43) and alu wax (+6.23), smallest for Jetbite (+2.19) and Luxabite (+2.06), all left-side intergroup differences p<0.05. Right-side numeric data were not recoverable from the extracted full text — only the qualitative statement survives (lowest MIP, highest hard wax).

Waxes distorted the vertical relationship most; PVS and bis-acrylic resin distorted least — the paper's framing is that closure onto any record material and its subsequent removal (bite release) inherently introduces vertical error, not just that materials differ in quality.

## 세줄요약

임상 횡단연구(치열이 있는 20–45세 성인 30명) — alu wax, hard wax, 폴리비닐실록산(Polyvinylsiloxane, PVS, Jetbite), bis-acrylic 레진(Luxabite) 4종의 교합채득재를 중심위(Centric Relation, CR) 채득 수직 정확도 기준으로 비교. 기준은 재료 없이 직접 폐구한 최대교두감합위(Maximum Intercuspation, MIP), Hanau Wide Vue 교합기·Lucia jig 사용.

복구된 왼쪽 데이터(Table 1)에서 평균값은 MIP 48.05±9.01, ALU 54.28±8.98, HARD 58.48±9.42, JET 50.24±8.95, LUX 50.11±8.94(단위 본문 미기재, 추정 mm); MIP 대비 산출 편차는 hard wax(+10.43)·alu wax(+6.23)에서 가장 크고 Jetbite(+2.19)·Luxabite(+2.06)에서 가장 작았다(왼쪽 군간 비교 모두 p<0.05). 오른쪽 수치표는 PMC 전문 추출에서 유실되어 복구 불가 — "MIP 최저, hard wax 최고"라는 정성적 서술만 남는다.

왁스류가 수직 방향 왜곡이 가장 컸고 PVS·bis-acrylic 레진이 가장 작았다 — 논문의 논지는 재료 자체의 우열뿐 아니라 채득재 위에서 폐구했다가 다시 제거(bite release)하는 행위 자체가 본질적으로 수직 오차를 만든다는 것.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Fills a materials-level gap**: the wiki previously had no page comparing interocclusal registration *materials* head-to-head for vertical accuracy — only occlusal-contact-marking methods (carbon paper, IOS, articulating paper) were covered.
2. **Frames error as inherent to the act of registration, not just material choice**: closure onto any record material plus its bite release measurably shifts the vertical reading versus direct MIP closure — even the "best" materials (Jetbite, Luxabite) showed nonzero discrepancy from MIP.
3. **Class-level ranking recoverable from Table 1 (left side only)**: wax (hard > alu) distorts most; PVS (addition silicone) and bis-acrylic resin distort least, both far closer to true MIP than either wax.
4. **Reconciles brand vs. material class**: Jetbite = polyvinylsiloxane (PVS, addition silicone); Luxabite = bis-acrylic resin; alu wax and hard wax are two distinct wax formulations, not one "wax" category. The keyword list's "lucia jig" is the anterior-stop device used for all four registrations, not a fifth material.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Clinical, within-subject comparative study, 4 materials × 30 subjects, each subject providing all 4 records.
- **Sample**: 30 dental students/staff volunteers, 20–45 years, no gender restriction, dentate (population is NOT edentulous — findings should not be extrapolated to complete-denture jaw-relation records without separate validation).
- **Materials compared** (brand → material class):
  - Group 1 — Alu wax (double thickness) — **wax**
  - Group 2 — Hard wax — **wax**
  - Group 3 — Jetbite® (premixed auto-mix cartridge) — **polyvinylsiloxane (PVS, addition silicone)**
  - Group 4 — Luxabite® (4:1 dual-cartridge auto-mix) — **bis-acrylic resin**
- **Procedure**: interocclusal record taken in centric relation with a Lucia jig as anterior stabilizer for all four materials; casts mounted with maximum intercuspation on a Hanau Wide Vue articulator; vertical distance between two predefined points measured with a digital vernier caliper; the difference between each material's reading and the direct-closure MIP reading computed per side (left/right) per subject.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 22.0; α = 0.05; Shapiro–Wilk normality; descriptive mean ± SD; intergroup comparison via Mann–Whitney U and one-way ANOVA followed by Fisher's LSD post-hoc.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Left side (Table 1 — fully recovered from PMC full text):**

| Group | Mean | SD | Discrepancy vs. MIP (computed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIP (direct closure, no material) | 48.05 | 9.01 | — (baseline) |
| ALU wax | 54.28 | 8.98 | +6.23 |
| HARD wax | 58.48 | 9.42 | +10.43 |
| JET (Jetbite, PVS) | 50.24 | 8.95 | +2.19 |
| LUX (Luxabite, bis-acrylic) | 50.11 | 8.94 | +2.06 |

Unit is not stated anywhere in the retrieved full text; the instrument is a "digital vernier caliper," conventionally read in mm — treat the mm assumption as unverified. The "discrepancy vs. MIP" column is a value computed for this wiki page (material mean − MIP mean), not a number printed in the source table itself.

- All left-side intergroup comparisons: statistically significant (p<0.05) via Fisher's LSD, **except** hard wax-vs-MIP, Jetbite-vs-MIP, and Luxabite-vs-MIP, which were **not** significantly different from MIP (only alu wax differed significantly from MIP on both sides per the text).
- MIP right-vs-left comparison: not significantly different (p=0.84) — the baseline itself was consistent across sides, supporting internal validity of the comparison.
- **Right side**: numeric table not recovered from the PMC extraction (see Why Ingested caveat) — text states only that MIP had the lowest mean and hard wax the highest, with a statistically significant one-way ANOVA intergroup result. No SD or effect size available for the right side.
- **Overall ranking (accuracy, left-side numbers + right-side qualitative agreement)**: Jetbite ≈ Luxabite (least distortion) > MIP-adjacent < Alu wax < Hard wax (most distortion).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **3-page supplement article** — format carries a weaker peer-review signal than a full-length JPBS article; no discussion of blinding, examiner calibration, or sample-size justification is present in the retrieved text.
- **Numeric right-side data unrecoverable** from this extraction — the page's clinical claims about the right side rest on qualitative wording only ("lowest in MIP, highest in hard wax"), not a reported mean/SD/p-value.
- **Unit of the vertical-distance parameter is never stated** in the retrieved text.
- **n=30, dentate, 20–45 y** — a young, dentate, presumably periodontally healthy population; do not generalize to edentulous jaw-relation records, geriatric patients, or patients with reduced periodontal support / mobile teeth, where record-material behavior may differ.
- Article does not report an a priori power calculation, examiner blinding, or number of examiners taking the caliper readings.
- **Future work**: replicate with the right-side table intact (re-extract from the publisher PDF/HTML rather than PMC's JATS conversion), report actual measurement units, and extend to edentulous jaw-relation registration materials — see the companion in-vitro study below for the same question outside a mouth.

## 6. Related Work

- **Rani et al. 2024** (companion paper, in-vitro, J Pharm Bioallied Sci 2024;16(Suppl 1):S745–S747, DOI 10.4103/jpbs.jpbs_990_23) — asks the same "which bite-registration material distorts least vertically" question in vitro; found polyether (Ramitec) best, elastomers > Aluwax. Directionally agrees (elastomer-type materials beat wax) but disagrees on which specific material tops the ranking (polyether there vs. PVS/bis-acrylic here) — worth reading together rather than treating either as definitive on brand-level ranking.
- Prior literature cited in the Discussion (uncited by name in the extracted text) attributes wax's poor performance to high viscosity limiting fine surface detail capture, and PVS's high accuracy to addition-polymerization curing without by-products (minimal dimensional change).

## 7. Glossary

- **CR** — Centric relation, the condyle-seated jaw position recorded in this study.
- **MIP** — Maximum (habitual) intercuspation; here used as the no-material direct-closure baseline.
- **Lucia jig** — Anterior deprogramming/stabilization device used during registration for all four material groups; a device, not a registration material.
- **PVS** — Polyvinylsiloxane (addition silicone); Jetbite is a brand-name PVS product.
- **Bis-acrylic resin** — Auto-mixed dual-cartridge resin material; Luxabite is a brand-name bis-acrylic product.
- **Alu wax / hard wax** — Two distinct wax formulations used as registration materials (not a single "wax" class).
