## Why Ingested

Teaching-clinic cohort (University of Bergen, 1,005 implants, 2011–2018) that supplies three signals under-represented in the held early-failure set: an **age effect** (older → lower early-failure risk, HRR 0.86 per decade), a **mandible-vs-maxilla site gradient quantified within the same jaw**, and a **consecutive-implant recurrence effect** (first-implant failure → HRR 1.82 for the next implant). It also reports early-failure rates by **implant system** (Straumann/Nobel Biocare/Astra Tech). Reinforces the age/male/smoking axis in [[implants/survival/yari-2023-risk-factors-early-implant-failure]] and the site-specific absolute-rate approach of [[implants/survival/fouda-2020-impact-alveolar-bone-sites]]. An abstract-only consequence: teaching-clinic operator (resident) effect is unquantified. Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants is paywalled (not in PMC) → abstract-based (abstract-only).

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective teaching-clinic cohort (Section of Oral Surgery and Oral Medicine, University of Bergen, January 2011–December 2018; 1,005 dental implants) reporting prevalence of and risk indicators for early implant failure (implant lost before functional loading).

54 implants failed early (EFR 5.4%; functional loading obtained for 94.6%); increased hazard for smokers, men, and younger patients (each 10-year age increase reduced failure risk by 14%, HRR 0.86, P=.037), anterior maxilla failed more than posterior maxilla (7.79% vs 3.29%, HRR 0.47, P=.041), posterior mandible failed more than posterior maxilla (HRR 3.68, P=.005), a prior implant failure raised the next implant's risk (HRR 1.82), and system EFRs differed (Straumann 5.2%, Nobel Biocare 7.2%, Astra Tech 2.5%).

The authors conclude the overall early failure rate was low despite significant patient- and implant-related differences; abstract-only ingest means the implant-system difference cannot be separated from confounding by operator/site/era, and no confidence intervals are reported.

## 세줄요약

조기 실패 54개 (조기 실패율 (Early Failure Rate, EFR) 5.4%, 94.6%는 기능 부하 도달); 흡연자·남성·젊은 환자에서 위험 증가 (10세 연령 증가 시 실패 위험 14% 감소, Hazard Ratio (HRR) 0.86, P=.037), 전방 상악이 후방 상악보다 실패율 높았고 (7.79% vs 3.29%, HRR 0.47, P=.041), 후방 하악이 후방 상악보다 높았으며 (HRR 3.68, P=.005), 첫 임플란트가 실패하면 다음 임플란트도 실패할 가능성이 높았고 (HRR 1.82), 임플란트 시스템별 EFR 차이 (Straumann 5.2%, Nobel Biocare 7.2%, Astra Tech 2.5%)가 있었다.

저자들은 환자·임플란트 인자 차이에도 전체 조기 실패율 (5.4%)은 낮았다고 결론짓지만, 초록만 수집해 시스템별 차이가 술자·부위·시기 교란과 분리 불가하고 신뢰구간은 보고되지 않았다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reports an **age effect inverted** relative to typical late-failure cohorts: younger patients at higher risk of early failure (each +10 years → 14% lower risk, HRR 0.86).
- Quantifies a **within-jaw site gradient** in the maxilla (anterior > posterior, 7.79% vs 3.29%) and a **mandible-vs-maxilla** posterior comparison (posterior mandible > posterior maxilla, HRR 3.68).
- Identifies a **carryover/recurrence signal**: failure of the first implant raises the hazard for the consecutive implant (HRR 1.82).
- Provides early-failure rates by **implant system** in one teaching-clinic population (Straumann 5.2%, Nobel Biocare 7.2%, Astra Tech 2.5%).
- Confirms the male and smoker signals in an independent, non-US teaching-clinic cohort.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: retrospective cohort of all patients receiving a dental implant at one university teaching clinic.
- Period/inclusion: implants placed January 2011–December 2018 (Section of Oral Surgery and Oral Medicine, University of Bergen).
- Data sources: operation logbooks and patient records.
- Outcome definition: a failed implant = implant lost before functional loading (early failure).
- Analysis: survival/hazard modelling; associations reported as hazard ratios (HRR) with P-values.
- n: 1,005 dental implants.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Early failure: 54/1,005 implants — EFR 5.4%; functional loading obtained for 94.6%.
- Increased hazard for early failure: smokers, men, and younger patients.
  - Age: each 10-year increase reduced failure risk by 14% — HRR 0.86 (P=.037).
  - Site (maxilla): anterior maxillary implants > posterior maxillary implants — 7.79% vs 3.29%, HRR 0.47 (P=.041).
  - Site (jaw): posterior mandible > posterior maxilla — HRR 3.68 (P=.005).
  - Recurrence: if the first implant failed, the consecutive implant was more likely to fail — HRR 1.82.
- Implant system (proportion of placed implants / EFR): Straumann 53.4% / 5.2%; Nobel Biocare 30.3% / 7.2%; Astra Tech 16.3% / 2.5%.
- No confidence intervals are reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single teaching clinic; implants placed by supervised trainees, so **operator experience** is an unquantified confounder (residents' learning curve plausibly contributes to failure).
- Retrospective, record/logbook-based data; no prospective protocol.
- **Abstract-only ingest** — full multivariable model, CIs, and covariate definitions not verifiable here; the implant-system EFR differences may reflect confounding by site, era, indication, or operator rather than material/design.
- The direction of the HRR values (e.g., 0.47 for anterior-vs-posterior maxilla, 0.86 for age) needs care: HRR is expressed against a reference category whose identity is defined only in the full text.
- Age effect (younger = higher early-failure risk) is opposite to several late-failure cohorts and is not explained mechanistically in the abstract.

## 6. Related Work

- Multivariable early-failure risk models in other cohorts (Yari 2023; Chrcanovic 2016) — consistent male/smoking signals, different site/age framing.
- Site-specific absolute early-failure rates SR+MA (Fouda 2020) — this cohort's within-maxilla and posterior jaw comparisons complement the pooled quadrant picture.
- Risk-factor syntheses for early implant failure (Manzano 2016; Borba 2017) — teaching-clinic context.
- Smoking as the most consistent modifiable early-failure driver (Fan 2024 SR+MA).

## 7. Glossary

- **Early implant failure**: loss of the implant before functional loading (this study's definition).
- **EFR (early failure rate)**: proportion of placed implants lost before loading.
- **Functional loading**: point at which the implant-supported prosthesis is brought into occlusal function.
- **HRR (hazard rate ratio)**: ratio of failure hazards between groups in the survival model; the paper's label for hazard ratio.
- **Anterior maxilla / posterior maxilla / posterior mandible**: standard implant site divisions used here to compare failure rates.
- **Consecutive implant**: the next implant placed in the same patient after a prior implant failure.
