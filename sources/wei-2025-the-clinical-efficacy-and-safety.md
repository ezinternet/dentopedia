## Why Ingested
First human randomized trial of the low-dose ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP platform — the same rhBMP-2/biomimetic-calcium-phosphate delivery system that until now existed only as canine preclinical work ([[bone-regeneration/jiang-2020-bmp2-calcium-phosphate-alveolar-orthodontic]]) — and the first human histomorphometric head-to-head that shows *which* socket-graft material explains the null pooled ARP-vs-spontaneous-healing estimate ([[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/benekou-2025-histological-arp-vs-spontaneous-healing-sr-ma]]). It also tests, as a primary endpoint, the rhBMP-2 class claim that currently rests on pooled RCTs ([[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/alavi-2024-regenerative-grafts-socket-preservation-sr-ma]]) and the AAP position that biologics improve histomorphometry without improving ridge dimensions ([[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/suarez-lopez-del-amo-2022-efficacy-biologics-alveolar-ridge]]).

## One-line Summary
Proof-of-concept RCT (n=40) of low-dose E. coli-derived rhBMP-2-coated β-TCP in extraction sockets: 6-week new bone volume density 7.72% vs 2.96% with β-TCP alone vs 8.37% with natural healing; residual material 10.90% vs 15.73%; augmentation needed at implant placement in 36% vs 50% vs 89% (CMH p=0.0447); no safety signal.

## 한줄요약
저용량 대장균 유래 rhBMP-2 코팅 β-TCP 골대체재의 개념증명 무작위대조시험 (n=40): 6주 신생골 부피밀도 7.72% vs β-TCP 단독 2.96% vs 자연치유 8.37%, 잔존 이식재 10.90% vs 15.73%, 임플란트 식립 시 추가 골증대 필요 36% vs 50% vs 89% (CMH p=0.0447), 안전성 차이 없음.

## Three-line Summary

Single-centre, randomized, partially double-blind proof-of-concept RCT (n=40: ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP 15, β-TCP 15, natural healing 10) comparing a low-dose E. coli-derived recombinant human BMP-2 (rhBMP-2) coating on β-TCP against β-TCP alone and natural healing in single-root extraction sockets, with 6-week biopsy histomorphometry as the primary outcome.

New bone volume density at 6 weeks was 7.72 ± 6.01% with ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP vs 2.96 ± 2.23% with β-TCP alone — significantly lower than both other groups (p<0.05) — and 8.37 ± 6.31% with natural healing; residual material was lower than β-TCP alone (10.90 ± 4.04% vs 15.73 ± 4.52%), and fewer patients needed augmentation at implant placement (36% vs 50% vs 89%, CMH χ²=6.21, p=0.0447).

Safety was comparable across all three arms (adverse events 87% vs 80% vs 80%, CMH p=0.3253; every serum BMP-2 ELISA negative; no bone regeneration outside the socket), but the 6-week observation window, n=40, single-centre design and the negative soft-tissue/contour outcome make this hypothesis-generating for the multi-centre pivotal trials the authors state are still required.

## 세줄요약

단일센터·부분 이중눈가림 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT, 개념증명, n=40: ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP 15명·β-TCP 15명·자연치유 10명) — 대장균 유래 저용량 재조합 사람 골형성단백질-2 (recombinant human Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2, rhBMP-2)를 생체모방 칼슘인산염 (biomimetic calcium phosphate, BioCaP) 코팅으로 삼인산칼슘 (β-tricalcium phosphate, β-TCP)에 실어 단근치 발치와에 충실하고, β-TCP 단독·자연치유 (natural healing)와 비교; 1차 종점은 6주 후 조직형태계측 (histomorphometry) 신생골 (new bone) 부피밀도.

6주 신생골 부피밀도는 ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP 7.72 ± 6.01% vs β-TCP 단독 2.96 ± 2.23%(다른 두 군보다 유의하게 낮음, p<0.05) vs 자연치유 8.37 ± 6.31%; 잔존 이식재 (residual graft) 부피밀도는 β-TCP 단독 대비 낮음(10.90 ± 4.04% vs 15.73 ± 4.52%); 임플란트 식립 시 추가 골증대 (bone augmentation) 필요 비율은 36% vs 50% vs 89% (CMH χ²=6.21, p=0.0447).

안전성은 세 군 간 유의한 차이 없음(이상반응 87%·80%·80%, CMH p=0.3253, 혈청 BMP-2 ELISA 전부 음성, 소켓 외 신생골 생성 없음), 다만 6주 관찰기간·n=40·단일센터 한계와 연조직 윤곽(soft tissue contour) 유의한 차이 부재로 저자들은 다중 센터(multicenter) pivotal 시험이 필요하다고 보고함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **First human trial of the low-dose rhBMP-2 delivery platform.** ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP (126.2 μg rhBMP-2 per g BioCaP/β-TCP, 10.4 μm coating thickness, 0.25–1.00 mm granules) had previously only been characterized in vitro and in vivo preclinically (Liu 2013; Wei 2020); this is the first randomized human evaluation with a histomorphometric primary endpoint.
- **Explains why β-TCP alone under-performs at 6 weeks.** β-TCP alone yielded 2.96 ± 2.23% new bone vs 8.37 ± 6.31% with natural healing, whereas adding the rhBMP-2 coating brought the graft arm to parity with natural healing (7.72 ± 6.01%) while leaving less residual material than β-TCP alone (10.90 ± 4.04% vs 15.73 ± 4.52%) — i.e. the *coating*, not the calcium-phosphate carrier, carries the early bone-formation effect.
- **Connects histology to the clinical implant endpoint.** Augmentation was required at implant placement in 36% of the ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP group vs 50% (β-TCP) vs 89% (natural healing) (CMH χ²=6.21, p=0.0447) — the only clinical-outcome signal in the trial.
- **Safety at microgram dosing.** No group differences in adverse events (87% vs 80% vs 80%, CMH p=0.3253), soft-tissue healing score or OHIP-14; every serum BMP-2 ELISA was negative and no bone regeneration was seen outside the socket — the pre-specified safety concern with milligram-dose rhBMP-2/burst release was not reproduced at this sustained low-dose release.
- **Negative transfer finding.** The preclinical result of >3× new bone versus natural healing in critical-sized defects did **not** transfer to human extraction sockets (which heal spontaneously from 2–4 weeks), which the authors attribute to defect classification, healing period and species differences.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: single-centre, randomized, controlled, partially double-blind RCT (proof-of-concept); ISO 14155:2020 + CONSORT
- **n**: 40 patients (24 female, 16 male) screened from 44 → enrolled; no loss to follow-up, no intervention discontinuation
- **Allocation**: randomized block design, 5 blocks × 8 subjects (3 ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP : 3 β-TCP : 2 natural healing, by drawing lots)
- **Blinding**: patients and surgeons blinded for the two graft arms; natural-healing control unblinded (procedurally impossible); outcome measurement and analysis fully blinded
- **Eligibility**: single-root tooth meeting extraction indications, defect sounding classified EDS-1 or EDS-2 (Caplanis, Lozada & Kan 2005)
- **Intervention**: standardized extraction + socket filling, then double-layer resorbable collagen membrane (Bio-Gide) over every socket (grafted and control); 0.12% chlorhexidine rinse twice daily for 7 days
- **Follow-up**: 5 visits over 6 weeks; CBCT + intra-oral scan immediately post-surgery and at 6 weeks; trephine biopsy at week 6 (2.3 mm diameter × 6 mm height, socket centre, ≥1.5 mm bone walls preserved in all directions)
- **Primary outcome**: new bone volume density (%) in the 6-week biopsy (undecalcified PMMA embedding, McNeal's tetrachrome/basic fuchsine/toluidine blue, ImageJ)
- **Secondary outcomes**: unmineralized tissue volume density (%); residual material volume density (%); CBCT bone width (1/3/5 mm below crest) and buccal/lingual height change; intra-oral scan soft-tissue surface area and width change; number of bone augmentation procedures at implant placement
- **Safety outcomes**: soft-tissue healing score (Afat 2019), OHIP-14, adverse events (application-site pain, swelling, haemorrhage, dental/oral discomfort), serum BMP-2 (ELISA), blood and urine laboratory tests
- **Analysis sets**: FAS and SS = 40; SAS = 37 (3 excluded for protocol violations, 1 per arm); PPS (histomorphometry) = 36 (1 natural-healing patient excluded for insufficient biopsied tissue)
- **Statistics**: one-way ANOVA (histomorphometry, soft-tissue score, OHIP-14, demographics, CBCT/intra-oral changes), two-way repeated-measures ANOVA (laboratory data), post-hoc comparisons, CMH test for categorical data with Fisher exact post-hoc, GraphPad Prism 9, two-sided, p<0.05
- **Power**: G*Power 3, α 5%, power 90%, two-tailed independent samples t-test, effect size from prior preclinical data (Liu 2013); enrolled n exceeded the calculated minimum to allow for dropouts (none occurred)

- New bone volume density: β-TCP alone significantly lower than the other two groups (Figure 2g; *p<0.05). The ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP and natural-healing arms were not reported as significantly different.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **6-week observation window** is short for human bone regeneration; the authors state the trial duration was insufficient to demonstrate alveolar ridge preservation efficacy and long-term safety. A 2-year follow-up study has been initiated.
- **Proof-of-concept scale**: n=40, single centre, 3 protocol violations excluded from FAS, 1 patient excluded from histomorphometry for insufficient tissue, and the natural-healing control could not be blinded.
- **No surgical guide for biopsy** — trephine samples were taken freehand rather than under a virtual surgical guide, which the authors identify as a source of sampling variability.
- **Negative soft-tissue outcome** — no significant difference in soft-tissue contour change; the authors attribute this to limited sample size and the short 6-week period.
- **Preclinical-to-clinical transfer failure** — the >3× new bone formation reported in critical-sized defect models did not reproduce in human sockets, so the platform's efficacy in larger defects remains unproven.
- **Generalisation** — socket preservation is only a proxy model; the material's purpose extends to general bone defect repair, which requires larger multi-centre pivotal trials.

## 7. Glossary

- **ErhBMP-2/BioCaP/β-TCP** — E. coli-derived recombinant human BMP-2 incorporated in a biomimetic calcium phosphate coating on β-TCP granules (126.2 μg/g); microgram-range, locally limited, sustained release
- **BioCaP** — biomimetic calcium phosphate coating (mean thickness 10.4 μm by SEM)
- **β-TCP** — beta-tricalcium phosphate, a resorbable calcium-phosphate alloplast
- **EDS-1 / EDS-2** — extraction defect sounding classification of the socket wall defect (Caplanis 2005); inclusion required EDS-1 or EDS-2
- **New bone volume density (%)** — histomorphometric fraction of the biopsy occupied by newly formed bone
- **Residual material volume density (%)** — fraction of the biopsy still occupied by the graft material
- **Unmineralized tissue volume density (%)** — fraction occupied by non-mineralized (fibrous/immature) tissue
- **FAS / SAS / PPS / SS** — full analysis set (40) / sensitivity analysis set (37) / per-protocol set for histomorphometry (36) / safety set (40)
- **OHIP-14** — Oral Health Impact Profile-14, patient-reported oral quality-of-life instrument
- **CMH test** — Cochran–Mantel–Haenszel test for comparison of categorical data across groups
