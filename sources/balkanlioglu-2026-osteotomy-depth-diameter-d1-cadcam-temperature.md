## Why Ingested

Osteotomy geometry (depth and diameter) directly governs heat transfer in D1-like dense cortical bone — the highest-risk scenario for thermal osteonecrosis. This study provides effect-size quantification (η²) missing from earlier biomechanical reviews such as [[implants/osteotomy-thermal/chauhan-2018-biomechanical-factors-heat-generation-osteotomy]], establishing depth as the dominant variable with a CAD/CAM-standardized thermocouple methodology.

## Three-line Summary

A 3×3 in vitro factorial study in bovine D1-like cortical bone found that osteotomy depth (η²=0.82) overwhelmingly dominates intraosseous temperature rise compared with drill diameter (η²=0.17). All 90 osteotomies remained below the classical 47°C threshold when continuous irrigation was applied. Each 1-mm depth increase corresponded to approximately +0.63°C (R²=0.785), a clinically actionable linear rule.

## 세줄요약

치밀 피질골(D1형)에서 골절제 깊이(η²=0.82)가 드릴 직경(η²=0.17)보다 골내 최고온도(Tmax)를 압도적으로 결정함을 3×3 교차설계 시험관 연구로 입증했다. 지속 세척 조건에서 전 90개 군이 47°C 임계값을 초과하지 않았다. 깊이 1mm 증가당 약 +0.63°C(R²=0.785)의 선형 상승 규칙을 제시하여 임상적 계획 기준으로 활용 가능하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Depth dominance quantified**: Osteotomy depth explains η²=0.82 (82%) of variance in Tmax — the largest effect-size estimate for any single drilling parameter reported in this category to date.
2. **Diameter secondary**: Drill diameter explains η²=0.17 (17%), statistically significant but clinically secondary.
3. **Linear depth rule**: Each 1-mm depth increase ≈ +0.63°C Tmax (r=0.886, R²=0.785, p<0.001) — a simple planning heuristic.
4. **CAD/CAM thermocouple standardization**: 3D-printed surgical guides placed both K-type thermocouples at exactly 1 mm lateral to the osteotomy wall at the apical region, eliminating placement variability that confounds earlier thermocouple studies.
5. **Safety margin confirmed under irrigation**: Tmax remained 33.3–41.7°C (all below 47°C) with continuous irrigation across all 90 osteotomies in the densest cortical model, supporting current irrigation protocols.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Bone model**: Fresh bovine femoral cortical bone blocks — selected as a D1-like dense cortical analog. No cancellous component; represents worst-case clinical scenario for thermal load.

**Factorial design**: 3 (final drill diameters: 3.5, 4.0, 4.5 mm) × 3 (depths: 6.5, 11, 15 mm) = 9 groups × 10 osteotomies = **n=90 total**.

**Thermocouple placement**: Two K-type thermocouples per osteotomy, positioned via 3D-printed (CAD/CAM) surgical guides at 1 mm lateral from the osteotomy wall at the apical zone. This standardization is the methodological novelty.

**Irrigation**: Continuous external irrigation applied throughout.

**Analysis**: Two-way ANOVA with Tukey post-hoc tests; Pearson correlation (r); simple linear regression (R²).

**Outcome measure**: Maximum intraosseous temperature (Tmax) only — cumulative thermal dose (CEM43) not measured.

**Clinical implication**: At 15mm depth (the deepest tested), Tmax reached 41.7°C — still below 47°C with irrigation. Without irrigation or in less-controlled conditions, the depth gradient of +0.63°C/mm would matter more.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **In vitro only**: Bovine cortical bone lacks vascular heat dissipation; Tmax in vivo may be lower due to blood perfusion.
- **D1 model only**: Results reflect maximal cortical scenario. Softer (D3/D4) bone with less cortical heat generation is not addressed.
- **Peak temperature only**: Cumulative thermal dose (CEM43 metric) was not assessed; a brief high peak may be less damaging than sustained moderate heat.
- **Single drill system**: Results may not generalize across drill geometries (flute design, tip angle, coating).
- **Interaction significant but modest**: The diameter×depth interaction (F=11.36) is statistically significant but the effect is smaller than main effects — not explored in depth.
- **Future work needed**: In vivo validation, inclusion of cancellous bone models, CEM43 measurement, and multi-system drill comparison.

## 7. Glossary

- **Tmax**: Maximum intraosseous temperature recorded during drilling
- **η² (partial eta squared)**: Effect size measure (0–1); η²=0.82 = large/dominant effect
- **K-type thermocouple**: Nickel-alloy thermocouple for temperature measurement; common in bone heat studies
- **CAD/CAM surgical guide**: Computer-aided design/manufacturing guide; here 3D-printed to standardize thermocouple placement
- **47°C threshold**: Classical Eriksson & Albrektsson (1983) bone necrosis threshold at 1-minute exposure
- **D1 bone**: Densest Misch bone quality classification — homogeneous dense cortical; typical of anterior mandible
- **CEM43**: Cumulative equivalent minutes at 43°C — thermal dose metric not used in this study
