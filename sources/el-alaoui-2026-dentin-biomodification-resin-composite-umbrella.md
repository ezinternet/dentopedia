## Why Ingested

`resin-bonding` 카테고리(78편)에 아직 umbrella review가 없어, 흩어진 dentin biomodifier(collagen crosslinker, MMP inhibitor, NTAP, ethanol-wet bonding 등) 개별 SR들을 하나의 taxonomy로 종합하는 최상위 근거로 인제스트. 기존 [[resin-bonding/kiuru-2021-mmp-inhibitors-dentin-bonding-sr-ma]]의 MMP-inhibitor 메커니즘을 20종 biomodifier 전체 분류(natural/physical/chemical) 안에 위치시켜 scope를 확장하고, [[resin-bonding/zhou-2025-collagen-crosslinkers-naocl-dentin-bond-strength-sr-ma]]의 NaOCl 결합강도 저하 결과와 일관됨을 재확인.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 9 systematic reviews (7 meta-analyses, 175 primary studies, PROSPERO CRD42024588804) synthesizing 20 dentin biomodification agents (natural/physical/chemical) across 51 experimental setups for their effect on resin-dentin bond durability of composite restorations.

Non-thermal atmospheric plasma (NTAP) was the most consistently beneficial physical agent long-term (SMD up to 3.28); grape seed extract and chitosan improved long-term bond strength among natural agents; sodium hypochlorite (NaOCl) and hypochlorous acid (HOCl) both significantly reduced bond strength as chemical agents. Overlap among included SRs was slight (CCA = 1.52%).

Only scrubbing and air abrasion are currently affordable/chairside-ready physical biomodification techniques; NTAP is promising but limited by equipment cost and workflow integration, and chemical deproteinizers (NaOCl especially) should be avoided as routine dentin pretreatment before bonding.

## 세줄요약

레진-상아질 결합 내구성에 대한 20종 dentin biomodifier(천연/물리/화학)를 9편의 SR(7편 메타분석 포함, 175개 원저 연구)로 종합한 umbrella review.

비열 대기압 플라즈마(NTAP)가 물리적 agent 중 장기적으로 가장 일관되게 결합강도를 향상시켰고, grape seed extract·chitosan은 천연 agent 중 장기 결합강도를 개선; NaOCl·HOCl은 화학적 agent로서 결합강도를 유의하게 저하시켰다.

현재 임상 적용 가능한 물리적 전처치는 scrubbing과 air abrasion뿐이며, NTAP는 장비 비용·워크플로우 문제로 제한적이고, NaOCl 계열 화학적 deproteinization은 상아질 전처치로 권장되지 않는다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic taxonomy of dentin biomodifiers (natural / physical / chemical) specifically for resin-composite bond durability, spanning 20 agents and 51 experimental setups extracted from 9 SRs.
- Quantifies overlap between included SRs via Corrected Covered Area (CCA = 1.52% overall; slight), but flags a very-high pairwise overlap (57.9%) between the two NTAP-focused SRs as a caveat.
- Cross-tabulates biomodifier agent × bond-strength test type × adhesive strategy (self-etch vs etch-and-rinse) to expose testing-method heterogeneity across the field.
- Time-stratifies evidence into short- (<3mo), medium- (3-12mo), and long-term (≥12mo) bond strength outcomes, the most common clinically relevant unit used across the underlying SRs (immediate and 12-month being the most frequently reported points).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Umbrella review per PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD42024588804. PICOS: Population = resin-composite restorations; Intervention = dentin biomodifier pretreatment; Comparator = no pretreatment; Outcome = bond durability; Study design = SRs of in vitro studies (with/without meta-analysis).

Search: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, up to October 2024, no date/language restriction. Eligible: SRs evaluating bond durability of direct resin-composite to dentin of adult human teeth after biomodifier pretreatment; excluded SRs on structurally anomalous teeth or non-composite materials (sealants, luting cements, fiber posts).

Data extraction duplicated by 2 reviewers (disagreements resolved by 3rd author). Adhesive strategies harmonized to self-etch vs etch-and-rinse. Overlap quantified via GROOVE citation matrix + CCA (Pieper et al thresholds: slight 0-5%, moderate 6-10%, high 11-15%, very high >15%). Risk of bias of included SRs assessed with ROBIS (3 phases, 4 domains + overall judgement).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 486 records → 9 SRs included (7 meta-analyses incl. 1 network meta-analysis) → 175 primary studies (19 duplicated across SRs).
- Overall CCA = 0.0152 (1.52%, slight); pairwise CCA between the 2 NTAP SRs = 57.9% (very high — biases NTAP-specific conclusions).
- ROBIS: 50% of SRs low overall risk of bias, 30% unclear, 20% high; bias increasingly concentrated in later review stages (synthesis/appraisal) vs early stages (eligibility criteria: 100% low risk).
- 20 biomodifiers across 51 setups; µTBS used in 68.62% of setups (dominant test), SBS 15.68%, µSBS 11.76%.
- Best-performing agents by category: natural — grape seed extract (long-term MD = 13.88, I²=46%) and chitosan (medium/long-term SMD = -1.24 under etch-and-rinse, I²=50%); physical — NTAP (long-term SMD up to 3.28 for He-NTAP, I²=97%); chemical — ethanol-wet bonding (immediate SMD = -1.41, low heterogeneity) and DMSO (long-term stability, etch-and-rinse, not dose-dependent).
- Worst-performing agents: NaOCl and HOCl (chemical deproteinizers) both significantly reduced bond strength; dentin desensitizers also reduced bond strength (SMD = 0.96, I²=80%).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No pooled quantitative umbrella-level meta-analysis was possible — heterogeneity in outcome metrics (MD vs SMD), testing methods, and effect directions forced a qualitative synthesis instead.
- Several agents (electric current, ozone, glycolic acid, warm air blow) had no reported bond-strength outcome data in the underlying SRs — evidence gaps, not negative findings.
- In vitro aging periods (short/medium/long-term) are analytical groupings that do not directly predict clinical longevity in the oral cavity.
- Very-high pairwise overlap between the 2 NTAP SRs (CCA 57.9%) means NTAP's apparent superiority may be partly inflated by shared primary-study inclusion.
- Authors call for in-vivo and clinical studies to validate in vitro biomodifier findings before clinical protocol adoption.

## 7. Glossary

- **µTBS**: micro-tensile bond strength — the dominant bond-strength test method used across included SRs (68.62% of setups).
- **CCA (Corrected Covered Area)**: overlap metric between systematic reviews sharing primary studies; formula accounts for duplicate inclusion count, index publications, and number of SRs.
- **ROBIS**: Risk of Bias in Systematic reviews tool (3-phase: relevance, review-process concerns across 4 domains, overall judgement).
- **NTAP**: non-thermal atmospheric plasma — partially ionized gas at atmospheric pressure/temperature that generates reactive species to enhance dentin wettability.
- **PAC**: proanthocyanidin — polyphenol class responsible for grape seed extract's collagen cross-linking / MMP-2 inhibition effect.
- **DMSO**: dimethyl sulfoxide — solvent used both as a standalone bonding-stability agent and as a carrier for incorporating natural plant extracts into dentin pretreatment.
- **Hybrid layer**: the resin-infiltrated, demineralized collagen zone at the resin-dentin interface whose stability determines bond durability.
