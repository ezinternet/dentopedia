## Why Ingested
Biocompatibility evidence for dual-cure self-adhesive cements; supports [[resin/deniz-2025-dual-cure-self-adhesive-cement-cytotoxicity]] and [[resin/theracem-bisco-product-reference]].

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (Akdeniz University, J Prosthet Dent 2025; L929 murine fibroblasts, MTT assay, 48-h DMEM extracts at multiple dilutions and incubation times) comparing cytotoxicity of three dual-polymerizing self-adhesive resin cements — Calibra Universal, SpeedCem Plus, and TheraCem Ca — under light-cure and autopolymerization modes.

All three cements significantly reduced cell viability; light-cured specimens showed significantly less cytotoxicity than autopolymerized specimens for all three materials; SpeedCem Plus was least cytotoxic overall followed by TheraCem Ca.

Findings are limited to an extract-based single-cell-line in-vitro model; clinical pulp-tissue response correlation and comparisons across more cements and exposure conditions are needed.

## 세줄요약

인 비트로 연구 (아크데니즈대학, J Prosthet Dent 2025; L929 마우스 섬유아세포, MTT 분석, 48시간 DMEM 추출물, 다양한 희석 농도·배양 시간) — 3종의 이중중합 자가접착 수지 시멘트(Calibra Universal·SpeedCem Plus·TheraCem Ca)를 광중합·자가중합 모드로 비교했다.

3종 모두 세포 생존율을 유의하게 감소시켰으며, 광중합 시편이 자가중합 시편보다 세포독성이 유의하게 낮았다. SpeedCem Plus가 전체적으로 세포독성이 가장 낮았고, 그 다음이 TheraCem Ca였다.

추출물 기반 단일 세포주 인 비트로 모델의 한계가 있으며, 임상 치수 반응과의 상관관계와 더 많은 시멘트·노출 조건 비교가 필요하다.

## 1. Document Information
J Prosthet Dent 2025; 134(1): 178.e1–178.e7. PII S0022-3913(25)00208-2. PMID 40118683. Published online 2025-03-20. Akdeniz University, Türkiye.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Shows polymerization mode (light vs auto) is a major cytotoxicity determinant.
- Comparative biocompatibility ranking incl. TheraCem Ca.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
In-vitro. L929 fibroblasts; MTT assay. Cements: Calibra Universal, SpeedCem Plus, TheraCem Ca. Light vs autopolymerization; 48-h DMEM extracts; multiple dilutions/incubation times.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
All cements significantly ↓cell viability. Light-cured viability > autopolymerized. Least cytotoxic: light-cured SpeedCem Plus, then TheraCem Ca. Composition + activation mode both matter.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
In-vitro single-cell-line model; extract-based MTT; clinical pulp-response correlation needed.

## 6. Related Work
Self-adhesive cement cytotoxicity literature; polymerization degree-of-conversion studies.

## 7. Glossary
- MTT assay: tetrazolium-based cell-viability test.
- L929: murine fibroblast cell line for cytotoxicity testing.
- Dual-polymerizing: cement curable by light and/or self-cure.
