## One-line Summary
RCT (BMC Oral Health 2026): 30 patients, conventional vs Summers' osteotome vs osseodensification in posterior maxilla — no statistically significant ISQ difference at placement, 3m, or 6m, but numerical trend favored OD; all 100% survival.

## 한줄요약
RCT (BMC Oral Health 2026): 30명, 후방 상악 3가지 골삭제술 비교 — ISQ 통계적 유의차 없음(p NS), 수치적으로 OD 소폭 높고 osteotome 소폭 낮음; 전 그룹 6개월 100% 생존.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: BMC Oral Health, 2026;26
- DOI: 10.1186/s12903-026-08858-7 | PMID: 42393673 | PMC: PMC13352673
- Setting: AB Shetty Memorial Institute of Dental Sciences, Nitte University, Karnataka, India
- CTRI: CTRI/2021/09/036513 (retrospectively registered)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct 3-arm RCT comparing conventional / Summers' osteotome / OD ISQ in the same patient pool
- No statistically significant ISQ differences at any time point (placement, 3m, 6m)
- Numerical trend: OD marginally higher, osteotome marginally lower
- All techniques adequate for immediate provisionalization in posterior maxilla
- Caution: underpowered (n=10/group) — may have missed real differences (Type II error)
- Same implant system across all groups (Straumann BLT SLActive 4mm×10mm) controls confounders

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: 3-arm RCT, exploratory
- n=30 patients: Group I conventional (n=10), Group II Summers' osteotome (n=10), Group III OD (n=10)
- Implant: Straumann Bone Level Tapered, SLActive, 4mm×10mm
- Non-submerged with immediate provisional (no functional loading)
- RFA (Osstell) at placement, 3m, 6m
- Statistics: one-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc; repeated measures ANOVA for intragroup

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- ISQ at placement: OD > conventional > osteotome (numerically) — no statistical significance
- ISQ at 3 months: similar pattern, no statistical significance
- ISQ at 6 months: all three groups converged — no statistical significance
- 100% survival at 6 months all groups
- No implant failures, no major complications

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- n=10 per group — critically underpowered (acknowledged by authors)
- Single center, India
- Retrospectively registered CTRI — methodology concern
- 6-month follow-up only
- All implants same type (4mm×10mm) — cannot generalize to other dimensions
- Need: adequately powered multicenter RCT (n≥30/group)

## 6. Related Work
- OD vs conventional SR+MA (Marzorati 2026): OD significantly higher ISQ and IT pooled
- Umbrella review (Tomar 2026): OD generally superior for primary stability but heterogeneity
- Summers' osteotome: established technique, especially for crestal sinus floor elevation

## 7. Glossary
- OD: Osseodensification (골밀도화) — Densahbur clockwise-compacting drills (Versah)
- ISQ: Implant Stability Quotient — Osstell RFA (0–100)
- Summers' osteotome: bone-displacement/condensation technique using hand-driven osteotomes
- RFA: Resonance frequency analysis — contactless stability measurement
