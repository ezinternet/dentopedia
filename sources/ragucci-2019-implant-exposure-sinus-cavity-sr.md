## Why Ingested

Systematic review quantifying implant survival and complication rates when implants penetrate the sinus cavity (with or without membrane punch-through) — covers both lateral and transcrestal routes, provides evidence base for [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/duan-2017-graft-free-maxillary-sinus-floor-elevation-sr-ma]] and informs complication risk framework.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane; 8 studies, 493 implants, mean follow-up 52.7 months) of implant survival and complication rates when implants penetrate the sinus cavity without bone grafting, encompassing both lateral and transalveolar techniques.

Weighted mean survival was 95.6%; clinical complications occurred in 3.4% of cases (epistaxis most common) and radiographic complications in 14.8% (Schneiderian membrane thickening most common); penetration depth ≤4 mm vs >4 mm showed no statistically significant difference for either survival (p=0.403) or clinical complications.

Implant penetration into the sinus cavity without grafting yields clinically acceptable survival and complication profiles; the 4 mm depth threshold does not constitute a hard safety boundary for implant placement decisions, though a trend toward higher radiographic complications at >4 mm warrants monitoring.

## 세줄요약

임플란트가 상악동 내로 돌출될 때의 생존율과 합병증을 평가한 체계적 문헌고찰(MEDLINE·EMBASE·Cochrane, 8편, 493개 임플란트, 평균 52.7개월 추적); 측방 및 경치조정 접근법을 이식재 없이 시행한 연구를 대상으로 함.

가중 평균 생존율 95.6%; 임상 합병증 3.4%(비출혈 최다)·방사선 합병증 14.8%(슈나이더 막 비후 최다); 돌출 깊이 ≤4mm vs >4mm에서 생존율(p=0.403)·임상 합병증 모두 유의차 없음.

이식재 없이 상악동 내로 임플란트를 돌출시켜도 임상적으로 허용 가능한 생존율과 합병증 수준을 유지하며, 4mm 깊이 기준은 절대적 안전 경계가 아님; 다만 >4mm 시 방사선 합병증 경향 증가는 추적 관찰 필요.

## One-line Summary

SR (8 studies, 493 implants, 52.7 months): weighted mean survival 95.6% for implants penetrating sinus; clinical complications 3.4% (epistaxis most common), radiographic 14.8% (membrane thickening); penetration depth (≤4 vs >4 mm) does not significantly affect survival or clinical complications.

## 한줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (8편, 493개 임플란트, 추적 52.7개월): 상악동 내로 돌출된 임플란트 가중평균 생존율 95.6%; 임상 합병증률 3.4% (비출혈 최다), 방사선 합병증률 14.8% (막 비후 최다); 관통 깊이 (≤4 vs >4mm)는 생존율·임상합병증에 유의 영향 없음.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: International Journal of Implant Dentistry, 5:6
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s40729-019-0157-7
- **Institution**: International University of Catalonia, Barcelona; University of Michigan
- **Study design**: Systematic review (MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane)
- **Search date**: Up to January 2018
- **n**: 8 studies, 493 implants, mean follow-up 52.7 months
- **Inclusion**: ≥6 months follow-up; implant placement perforating sinus floor without regenerative procedure and graft material (lateral or transalveolar technique)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Weighted mean survival rate: 95.6% (IC 95%)
- Penetration ≤4 mm vs >4 mm: p=0.403 (NS) for survival
- Clinical complication rate: 3.4%; most common = epistaxis; no significant difference by penetration depth
- Radiographic complication rate: 14.8%; most common = Schneiderian membrane thickening
- Radiographic complications: 5.29% at ≤4 mm penetration vs 29.3% at >4 mm (p=0.301, NS — borderline)
- No serious sinus infection or implant failure attributable to sinus penetration in included studies

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Databases**: MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Oral Health Group
- **Language**: No restriction
- **Study types**: Reporting implant survival when penetrating sinus without graft/regenerative procedure
- **Outcome**: Primary = survival rate; Secondary = clinical complications, radiographic complications
- **Statistical**: Weighted mean ± IC 95%; chi-square for depth group comparison
- **Bias**: High risk in most studies due to retrospective designs and small samples

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High heterogeneity across studies; meta-analysis not performed
- Small sample sizes; mostly retrospective
- Mix of lateral and transalveolar approaches limits technique-specific conclusions
- Unclear definition of "sinus penetration" across studies
- No RCT evidence; need longer follow-up

## 7. Glossary

- **IPL (Implant Protrusion Length, 임플란트 돌출 길이)**: length of implant penetrating into sinus cavity above sinus floor
- **Epistaxis (비출혈)**: nosebleed; most common clinical complication from sinus penetration
- **Schneiderian membrane (슈나이더 막)**: sinus floor mucoperiosteum; thickening on CBCT = most common radiographic sign
