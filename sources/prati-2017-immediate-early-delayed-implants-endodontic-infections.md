## Why Ingested

감염 소켓 즉시식립 문헌에서 "급성 감염 = 즉시식립 금기" 기준의 근거를 제시하는 임상 의사결정 프로토콜 논문. [[wiki/immediate-implant/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]]가 인용하는 논문군으로, 만성 vs 급성 병변에 따른 식립 타이밍 결정을 구조화된 볼로냐대 프로토콜로 검증. 급성 농양에서 즉시식립을 피하고 2개월 후(조기식립 Early)를 선택한 결과 오히려 24개월 변연골 소실이 가장 적음을 보임.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (n=85 patients, 131 flapless transmucosal ZirTi implants), University of Bologna; pre-extractive endodontic diagnosis determined timing (Immediate/Early/Delayed); 2-year follow-up.

100% survival all groups; MBL at 24 months — Early: 0.48 ± 0.72 mm (best), Immediate: 0.78 ± 0.70 mm, Delayed: 1.02 ± 1.01 mm; Early vs Delayed significant (p=0.0001), Immediate vs Delayed NS (p=0.088).

Acute periapical abscess is a contraindication to immediate placement; Early placement (8-12 weeks after abscess resolution) achieves the lowest MBL — superior to both immediate and delayed timing.

## 세줄요약

전향적 코호트 (n=85명, 131 임플란트), 볼로냐대학교; 발치전 근관 진단이 식립 타이밍 결정 기준; 2년 추적.

생존율 100% (전 군); 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 24개월 — 조기 0.48 mm (최소), 즉시 0.78 mm, 지연 1.02 mm; 조기 vs 지연 유의 (p=0.0001), 즉시 vs 지연 NS.

급성 치근단 농양 = 즉시식립 금기; 농양 해소 후 8–12주 조기식립이 변연골 보존 최적.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Implant Dentistry 26(5):654-663, 2017
- **DOI**: 10.1097/ID.0000000000000666
- **Institution**: University of Bologna (Endodontic Clinical Section + Laboratory of Biomaterials), 2 private offices; Italy
- **Study period**: January 2011 – January 2017
- **Ethical approval**: not required (no experimental procedures per authors)
- **Implant system**: ZirTi blasted surface, tulip platform-switching design

## 2. Key Contributions

- Formalized clinical decision algorithm: acute abscess (tumor/rubor/dolor/calor + fistula + pus) → Early; chronic periapical lesion + intact cortex → Immediate; healthy bone → Delayed
- Demonstrated Early placement (ITI Type 2) produces less MBL than both Immediate (Type 1) and Delayed (Type 4) at 24 months
- All flapless transmucosal — eliminates second re-entry surgery as confounding variable
- Multilevel analysis accounting for patient/implant/time levels; controlled for gender, age, location
- First Bologna Endodontic Department protocol with ZirTi surface published at 2-year follow-up

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Non-randomized prospective cohort (treatment timing not randomized — ethics precluded randomizing acute vs chronic infection groups)
- **n**: 85 patients, 131 implants; mean age 54.6 ± 11.8 years; 46 women, 39 men; 8 light smokers (10-20 cig/day, distributed evenly)
- **Groups by timing**:
  - Immediate (ITI Type 1, n=29 implants): chronic periapical lesion, no acute signs, intact cortical bone
  - Early (ITI Type 2, n=29 implants): acute periapical abscess resolved → 8-12 weeks post-extraction
  - Delayed (ITI Type 4, n=73 implants): extraction site healed → 10-12 months post-extraction
- **Implants**: ZirTi (zirconia-oxide blasted) surface; tulip platform-switching; flapless transmucosal placement
- **Gap fill (immediate group)**: corticocancellous porcine bone in socket gaps
- **Loading**: 3 months post-insertion (provisional resin crown); definitive ceramic crown ~2 weeks later
- **MBL measurement**: periapical radiographs at T1, T3, T6, T12, T24 (months); calibrated to implant dimensions; single-blind assessment
- **Statistics**: multilevel linear regression; Friedman + Wilcoxon; Kruskal-Wallis; Bonferroni correction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | n implants | MBL T24 (mm) | 95% CI | vs Delayed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate | 29 | 0.78 ± 0.70 | 0.20 to -1.37 | -0.129 (p=0.088 NS) |
| Early | 29 | 0.48 ± 0.72 | -0.006 to -0.961 | **-0.400 (p=0.0001)** |
| Delayed | 73 | 1.02 ± 1.01 | 0.61 to -1.43 | reference |

Location effect: Maxilla shows 0.20 mm less MBL than mandible at T24 (p=0.001).

Survival: 100% (all groups); 1 dropout (early group, non-smoker, excluded after 6 months).

Complications: zero wound infections, osteitis, bone graft sequestration, or implant loosening. One mucositis episode (abutment screw loosening) — resolved with CHX.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Non-randomized (by design; ethical constraint)
- Only 2-year follow-up; authors note longer studies needed (Schropp 10-year data cited as reassurance)
- Single clinical team (standardized protocol but reduces generalizability)
- Mandible and maxilla not stratified in group analysis

## 7. Glossary

- **ITI Classification (timing)**: Type 1=Immediate; Type 2=Early (4-8 weeks / 8-12 weeks); Type 3=Early (12-16 weeks); Type 4=Delayed (>6 months)
- **ZirTi**: Zirconia-oxide blasted titanium surface (high roughness/wettability implant surface)
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: Distance from implant reference point (shoulder) to first bone-to-implant contact on periapical radiograph
- **Platform-switching**: Tulip-shaped abutment smaller than implant platform, redirects bone remodeling stresses
- **Flapless transmucosal**: No mucoperiosteal flap raised; implant pierces through gingiva directly; reduces trauma
