## Why Ingested
Surveillance sweep on bone graft materials (allograft/xenograft/alloplast). The wiki already holds a larger, later review of implant survival in autogenous versus allogeneic blocks ([[wiki/bone-regeneration/donkiewicz-2021-survival-rates-implants-autogenous-allogeneic]]) that reports only implant-level survival; this earlier, narrower SR/MA adds block-level survival, block complications and histomorphometric composition for allogeneic blocks alone, and its implant survival (97.36%) is consistent with that review's 97.66%. It also supplies the block-form allograft evidence behind the cancellous-allograft question raised by [[wiki/bone-regeneration/rao-2026-allogeneic-bone-plug-versus-dbbm-gbr-pilot-rct]].

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA; 7 non-randomized clinical studies, searched to April 2019; 234 patients, 323 allogeneic bone blocks, 501 implants, >=12 months follow-up, Newcastle-Ottawa scale scores 2-4 of 9) of allogeneic bone block grafts for atrophic-ridge augmentation before implant placement.

Pooled block survival was 94.52% (random effects; I2 = 56.3%), pooled implant survival 97.36% (fixed effects; I2 = 0%; range 95.2-100%); block exposure was the commonest complication, newly formed bone was 31-42% and residual graft 14-40% in the three studies with histomorphometry.

Allogeneic blocks look like a feasible alternative to autogenous blocks (no donor-site morbidity), but there was no usable allogeneic-versus-autogenous comparison, protocols were unstandardized and there were no randomized trials, so the conclusion rests on low-grade, short-to-medium-term evidence.

## 세줄요약
체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (PRISMA 준수; 2019년 4월까지 검색, 비무작위 임상연구 7편; 환자 234명, 동종골 블록 (Allogeneic Bone Block) 323개, 임플란트 501개, 추적 12개월 이상, Newcastle-Ottawa 척도 2~4점)으로, 위축된 치조제의 임플란트 전 증강에 쓰인 동종골 블록의 성적을 평가했다.

통합 블록 생존율 (Block Survival Rate)은 94.52% (변량효과 모형, I2 = 56.3%), 통합 임플란트 생존율은 97.36% (고정효과 모형, I2 = 0%, 범위 95.2~100%)였고, 블록 노출 (Block Exposure)이 가장 흔한 합병증이었다. 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry)을 보고한 3편에서 신생골은 31~42%, 잔존 이식재는 14~40%였다.

동종골 블록은 공여부 이환이 없는 자가골 블록 (Autogenous Bone Block)의 대안으로 보이나, 동종골 대 자가골의 직접 비교 자료가 없고 프로토콜이 표준화되지 않았으며 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)이 없어 근거 수준은 낮고 추적 기간은 짧거나 중간 정도에 그친다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Meta-analytic pooling of block-level survival (94.52%) and implant-level survival (97.36%) for allogeneic bone blocks across 7 clinical studies.
- Collects secondary outcomes in one place: horizontal gain 1.65 +/- 0.14 mm to 4.69 mm, block resorption 0.4-0.5 mm or 5.39 +/- 2.18%, marginal bone loss 0.5 +/- 0.5 mm (one study), complications of blocks and implants.
- Documents histomorphometric composition (new bone 31-42%, residual graft 14-40%, marrow/connective tissue 19.3-53.7%) and the clearly demarcated necrotic interface seen with allogeneic versus autogenous blocks.
- Identifies the lack of standardization (healing time 3-6 months, screw vs titanium plate fixation, with/without membrane and particulate, implant design) as the main barrier to conclusions.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review with meta-analysis, PRISMA-guided; adapted PIO question (no comparator), no protocol registration stated in the retrieved text.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed), Cochrane CENTRAL, Scopus, Web of Science, plus manual journal search; to April 2019; English and Spanish.
- **Selection**: 372 records, 66 duplicates removed, 52 full texts read, 7 studies included (inter-reviewer kappa 0.955). Inclusion: human studies, n > 5, follow-up >= 12 months, systemically healthy patients.
- **Risk of bias**: Newcastle-Ottawa scale (one study 2 points, two studies 3 points, four studies 4 points).
- **Statistics**: Survival with 95% CI in Comprehensive Meta-Analysis v3; fixed vs random effects chosen by Cochran Q and I2.
- **n**: 234 patients (18-80 years; 63 men, 140 women, one study without age/sex), 323 blocks, 501 implants.
- **Outcomes**: Primary - block survival and implant survival; secondary - complications, bone gain, resorption, marginal bone level, histology/histomorphometry.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Block survival (pooled) | 94.52% (random effects; Q = 13.726, df 6, p = 0.033, I2 = 56.286%); one study 79.31%, others >= 90% |
| Implant survival (pooled) | 97.36% (fixed effects; Q = 4.143, p = 0.657, I2 = 0%); range 95.2-100%; 501 implants |
| Follow-up | minimum 12 months, longest 96 months; implants placed after 3-6 months healing |
| Horizontal bone gain | 1.65 +/- 0.14 mm to 4.69 mm (3 studies) |
| Vertical gain | 5.15 mm post-surgery to 2.92 mm at 1 year (one study) |
| Block resorption | 0.4-0.5 +/- 0.5 mm (2 studies) or 5.39 +/- 2.18% (1 study); no standardized method |
| Marginal bone loss | 0.5 +/- 0.5 mm at 37 +/- 17 months (one study) |
| Block complications | 28 blocks exposed and 9 osseointegration failures (6 studies); one study: 4 blocks lost (3 prosthetic trauma, 1 iatrogenic dislocation) |
| Implant complications | 11 implants failed to osseointegrate; 3 lost within first month |
| Histomorphometry (3-4 studies) | new bone 31-42%; residual graft 14-40%; marrow/connective tissue 19.3-53.7% |

Discussion-level comparisons cited from other studies (not pooled by this review): fresh-frozen allograft block resorption 46% vs autograft 28%; residual graft 28.9% allogeneic vs 19.5% autologous; new vital bone not significantly different; multinucleated giant cells minimal (0.82 +/- 2.97 per mm2); marginal bone loss 0.38 mm (allogeneic) vs 0.15 mm (autologous), not significant.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 7 non-randomized studies (NOS 2-4); no RCTs; the comparative allogeneic-vs-autogenous group could not be analyzed because of short follow-up and heterogeneous results.
- Substantial heterogeneity in block survival (I2 = 56.3%); no standardization of healing time, fixation, membrane/particulate use or implant design; most studies did not report block location, resorption or marginal bone loss.
- Internal numerical inconsistencies in the retrieved text: the abstract states 13 failed blocks (4.02%) while the pooled survival of 94.52% implies about 5.5% failure; the discussion cites 347 blocks and 30 exposures (4.32%) versus 323 blocks and 28 exposures in the results; sex counts (63 + 140) do not sum to 234 patients.
- Search limited to a narrow two-term strategy and English/Spanish; only systemically healthy patients; search ended April 2019, so later allogeneic-block studies are not covered.
- Authors call for long-term data and standardized protocols.

## 6. Related Work
- Donkiewicz 2021: larger later SR of 100 studies comparing autogenous and allogeneic blocks (implant survival 96.23% vs 97.66%).
- Rao 2026: pilot RCT of a partially demineralized allogeneic cancellous bone plug vs DBBM (particulate/plug form, not block).
- Benic 2014: horizontal augmentation review framing GBR vs block grafts.

## 7. Glossary
- **Allogeneic bone block (동종골 블록)**: processed human bone from a tissue bank, shaped as a block and fixed to the ridge to gain width or height.
- **Block survival rate**: proportion of grafted blocks that remain integrated and allow implant placement during follow-up.
- **Newcastle-Ottawa scale (NOS)**: star-based risk-of-bias tool for non-randomized studies (maximum 9).
- **I2**: percentage of variability across studies due to heterogeneity rather than chance.
- **Histomorphometry**: quantitative histology of biopsies (new bone, residual graft, soft tissue percentages).
