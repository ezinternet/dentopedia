## One-line Summary
mBio perspective: dentists account for ~10% global antibiotic prescriptions and are uniquely positioned in AMR response, yet dentistry remains absent from most national AMR action plans and lacks unified prescribing guidelines.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Dentists prescribe ~10% of global antibiotics — a significant systemic AMR lever
- AMR stewardship insufficiently represented in dental education curricula worldwide
- Dentistry is absent from most national AMR Action Plans (NAPs) — a systemic policy failure
- Four-pillar action framework proposed: (1) unified prescribing guidelines, (2) integration into NAPs, (3) targeted clinician education, (4) oral resistome surveillance
- "One Health" framing: dental AMR is part of the human-animal-environment nexus

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: invited perspective/commentary (mGem); narrative synthesis of evidence
- Not a systematic review — no formal inclusion criteria
- Draws on global antibiotic prescribing data, AMR policy frameworks, dental education literature

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Dentists: ~10% of global antibiotic prescriptions
- Dentistry rarely integrated into national AMR surveillance or action plans
- Dental AMR education: insufficient emphasis in curricula and competency standards
- Oral resistome: largely unmapped — surveillance gap
- Proposed solutions: unified international guidelines, NAP integration, oral resistome surveillance

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Perspective article: no primary data — all claims are narrative synthesis
- Global figures cited are estimates; exact prescribing share varies by country
- Authors acknowledge data gaps in oral resistome and stewardship intervention efficacy

## 6. Related Work
- Complements Thompson 2023 (dental antibiotic stewardship core outcome set)
- Aligns with Baker et al.'s prior work on oral microbiome and AMR
- Positions dentistry within the WHO global AMR action plan context

## 7. Glossary
- AMR: Antimicrobial Resistance
- NAP: National AMR Action Plan
- One Health: integrated approach to human-animal-environment health interdependence
- Oral resistome: the totality of antibiotic resistance genes harbored in the oral microbiome
- mGem: mBio's invited Perspective format for concise, opinionated synthesis
