## Why Ingested

`orofacial-pain` 카테고리는 지금까지 이온채널/신경전달물질 수준(Cl⁻ homeostasis, T-type Ca²⁺ 채널, neurosteroid modulation)의 좁은 기전 논문 3편만 보유하고 있었다 — [[orofacial-pain/wilke-2020-chloride-underrated-ion-nociceptors]], [[orofacial-pain/price-2009-chloride-regulation-pain-pathway]], [[orofacial-pain/pathirathna-2005-t-type-calcium-gaba-neurosteroids]]. 이 논문(Kim 2024)은 삼차신경 감각계 (Trigeminal Sensory System) 전체를 해부학·시냅스 전달·중추감작 (Central Sensitization) 관점에서 통합 리뷰한 시스템 수준 프레임이며, 세 이온채널 논문이 다루는 세부 분자기전이 실제로 어느 핵산·시냅스(Sp5O/Sp5I/Sp5C, LTP/LTD)에서 작동하는지 상위 지도를 제공한다. 또한 [[overviews/bms-orofacial-pain-neuropathic-mechanism-overview]] 종합페이지가 다루는 4개 임상표현형(신경병증성 통증, 삼차신경통, 두통, 구강작열감증후군) 전체를 한 논문 안에서 다루므로 해당 overview 갱신의 핵심 근거가 되며, 임상측면에서는 [[oral-medicine/khan-2023-trigeminal-neuralgia-therapeutic-approach-sr]]의 치료적 접근이 기대는 기저 병태생리(혈관압박→탈수초화→ephaptic transmission→중추감작)를 상세히 뒷받침한다.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review synthesizing the anatomy, cellular composition, and synaptic physiology of the trigeminal sensory system (trigeminal ganglion, mesencephalic/principal/spinal trigeminal nuclei, paratrigeminal nucleus) and relating it to four orofacial pain conditions.

Central sensitization in the spinal trigeminal nucleus (particularly Sp5C↔Sp5O/Sp5I intersubnuclear pathways) — driven by NMDA-receptor- and mGluR-dependent LTP, microglial activation, and reduced GABAergic/glycinergic inhibition — underlies orofacial neuropathic pain, trigeminal neuralgia's continuous-pain phase, headache chronification, and burning mouth syndrome (Aδ-fiber cooling-inhibition loss unmasking C-fiber burning).

No original data (narrative review); clinical implication is that four seemingly distinct orofacial pain syndromes converge mechanistically on a shared trigeminal-nuclei central-sensitization pathway, suggesting shared therapeutic targets (NMDA/mGluR/microglia) rather than fully independent disease processes.

## 세줄요약

삼차신경절 (Trigeminal Ganglion, TG), 중뇌핵 (Mesencephalic Nucleus, Me5)·주감각핵 (Principal Sensory Nucleus, Pr5)·삼차신경척수핵 (Spinal Trigeminal Nucleus, Sp5)·부삼차신경핵 (Paratrigeminal Nucleus, Pa5)의 해부학·세포구성·시냅스전달을 정리하고 4가지 구강안면통증 (Orofacial Pain)과 연결한 내러티브 리뷰.

Sp5C↔Sp5O/Sp5I 간 핵간연결(intersubnuclear pathway)에서 NMDA 수용체·mGluR 의존성 장기강화 (Long-Term Potentiation, LTP), 미세아교세포 (Microglia) 활성화, GABA/glycine 억제성 전달 감소로 유발되는 중추감작 (Central Sensitization)이 신경병증성통증·삼차신경통 지속통·두통 만성화·구강작열감증후군 (Burning Mouth Syndrome, BMS, Aδ 냉각억제 소실→C섬유 작열통 노출)의 공통 기전.

원저 데이터 없는 내러티브 리뷰이므로 근거수준은 제한적이나, 서로 다른 4개 임상증후군이 삼차신경핵 중추감작이라는 공유 경로로 수렴한다는 시사점은 NMDA/mGluR/미세아교세포 표적 치료의 공통 근거를 제공.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates the cellular organization of the entire trigeminal sensory system — trigeminal ganglion (type A/B/C neurons, CGRP/substance P/TRPV1/glutamate content) plus four brainstem nuclei (Me5, Pr5, Sp5 [Sp5O/Sp5I/Sp5C], Pa5) — into a single anatomical/physiological reference.
- Details the brainstem parasympathetic circuits that ride along the trigeminal branches (ciliary, pterygopalatine, otic, submandibular ganglia), an often-overlooked autonomic layer relevant to trigeminal-autonomic cephalalgias and lacrimation/salivation symptoms.
- Maps intersubnuclear synaptic connections within Sp5 (Sp5O↔Sp5I↔Sp5C, ascending/descending, AMPA/NMDA excitatory and GABA/glycine inhibitory) and shows how LTP/LTD at these synapses (mGluR1/5-, NMDA-dependent, VGCC-mediated) constitute a "multilevel filtering" gate for nociceptive input.
- Links wind-up (homosynaptic, C-fiber-driven) and central sensitization (heterosynaptic, receptive-field expansion, spontaneous activity) as sequential phenomena within Sp5O/Sp5C, tying them to clinical findings in painful TMD (altered temporal summation).
- Synthesizes four orofacial pain phenotypes — orofacial neuropathic pain, trigeminal neuralgia (TN), headache (migraine/TTH/TACs), and burning mouth syndrome (BMS) — through the shared lens of peripheral/central sensitization in the trigeminal system, rather than treating them as unrelated diagnoses.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative literature review (not a systematic review or meta-analysis); no PRISMA protocol, no formal search strategy or study-selection criteria reported. Structure: (1) anatomical/physiological description of trigeminal ganglion and each brainstem nucleus, drawing on rodent, cat, monkey, and human anatomical/electrophysiological studies; (2) synaptic transmission and plasticity section covering excitatory (AMPA/kainate/NMDA/mGluR) and inhibitory (GABA/glycine) mechanisms and LTP/LTD phenomena, largely from animal brain-slice electrophysiology; (3) four disease-specific sections (orofacial neuropathic pain, TN, headache, BMS) each reviewing peripheral and central mechanistic evidence from a mix of animal models and human clinical/QST/biopsy studies.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Not a primary-data study — no benchmarks or effect sizes. Key qualitative findings reported from the literature synthesized in the review:

- **Trigeminal ganglion**: ~50% of neurons CGRP+, ~15% substance P+, 32–45% glutamatergic; type A cells → tooth pulp afferents, type B cells → corneal afferents.
- **Sp5 intersubnuclear LTP**: Sp5C→Sp5O LTP is NMDA-receptor-dependent (2 Hz stimulation) or NMDA-independent via group I mGluRs (Gq-coupled, L-type VGCC/TRPC-mediated); Sp5C→Sp5I theta-burst LTP is coupled to GABAergic inhibitory LTP (NMDA/NO-cGMP-mediated).
- **Trigeminal neuralgia**: no vascular compression found in ~25% of TN patients (about half of these attributable to MS/tumor); V2/V3 branch involvement common (single branch ~60%, both ~35%), V1 rare (~4%); female:male ratio ~2–3:1; rat infraorbital-nerve-constriction model shows Nav1.3 upregulation, Nav1.7 downregulation, Kv7.2 upregulation in TG neurons; SCN8A (Nav1.6) mutation reported in one TN patient.
- **Burning mouth syndrome**: prevalence 0.1–3.9%, higher in postmenopausal women aged 50–70; reduced intraepithelial nerve fiber density on tongue biopsy; increased TRPV1/NGF/P2X3 immunostaining; QST shows Aδ-fiber (cooling) hypofunction more prevalent than C-fiber hypofunction, supporting a "loss of cold-mediated inhibition of heat/burning" model; no validated animal model of BMS currently exists.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- The review explicitly notes the pathophysiology of TN, headache central mechanisms, and BMS remain "poorly understood" in several places — much of the evidence is from animal (rodent/cat/monkey) electrophysiology and anatomy, with human correlation often indirect (imaging, QST, biopsy) rather than direct.
- No animal model of BMS exists, limiting mechanistic dissection of that specific condition (acknowledged explicitly by the authors as a gap).
- As a narrative (non-systematic) review, there is no explicit search methodology, risk-of-bias assessment, or quantitative synthesis — findings are authors' curated synthesis, susceptible to selection bias.
- The peritrigeminal nucleus is explicitly excluded because its connections and functions are unknown — an acknowledged incompleteness in the trigeminal sensory nuclei taxonomy covered.

## 6. Related Work

The review's disease-specific sections engage with the broader ion-channel/neurotransmitter literature on peripheral (VGSC/VGCC/TRP) and central (NMDA/mGluR/GABA) sensitization mechanisms that intersects with prior wiki holdings on chloride homeostasis in nociceptors, T-type calcium channel/neurosteroid modulation, and clinical trigeminal neuralgia management (see wikilinks in Why Ingested). ## 7. Glossary

- **Trigeminal Ganglion (TG, 삼차신경절)**: Pseudounipolar sensory neuron cell body cluster in the middle cranial fossa; branches into V1 (ophthalmic), V2 (maxillary), V3 (mandibular).
- **Mesencephalic Nucleus (Me5, 중뇌핵)**: Brainstem nucleus containing proprioceptive afferent somata from masticatory/extraocular muscle spindles and periodontal receptors.
- **Principal Sensory Nucleus (Pr5, 주감각핵)**: Processes discriminative touch/pressure/temperature from the face; projects to contralateral thalamic VPM/Po, analogous to dorsal column nuclei.
- **Spinal Trigeminal Nucleus (Sp5, 삼차신경척수핵)**: Divided into oralis (Sp5O), interpolaris (Sp5I), caudalis (Sp5C, "medullary dorsal horn"); Sp5C is the principal nociceptive-processing subnucleus.
- **Paratrigeminal Nucleus (Pa5, 부삼차신경핵)**: Integrates orofacial somatic and visceral (upper GI) nociceptive input via calbindin D28k neurons.
- **Central Sensitization (중추감작)**: Heterosynaptic increase in CNS neuron responsiveness after repetitive stimulation; manifests as spontaneous activity, receptive-field expansion, and stimulus hyperresponsiveness.
- **Wind-up (와인드업)**: Homosynaptic progressive enhancement of neuronal response during repeated C-fiber stimulation (Mendell & Wall).
- **Trigeminal Neuralgia (TN, 삼차신경통)**: Paroxysmal unilateral facial pain, often from vascular compression → focal demyelination → ephaptic transmission between Aβ touch fibers and pain fibers.
- **Burning Mouth Syndrome (BMS, 구강작열감증후군)**: Chronic burning oral dysaesthesia without visible lesions; linked to trigeminal small-fiber neuropathy and Aδ-fiber cooling-inhibition loss.
- **Trigeminal Autonomic Cephalalgias (TACs, 삼차신경자율신경두통)**: Headache group with autonomic symptoms mediated by the trigeminal-autonomic reflex via the superior salivatory nucleus (SSN) and sphenopalatine ganglion (SPG).
