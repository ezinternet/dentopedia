## Why Ingested

[[dental-erosion/kanzow-2016-etiology-pathogenesis-dental-erosion]]가 GERD·섭식장애 등 위험군을 질적으로만 기술했는데, 본 SR+MA는 위험군별 침식성 치아마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW) 유병률을 정량화해 그 틀을 수치로 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with random-effects meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42021270150; 9 databases to April 2024; 148 observational studies from 4403 retrieved) of erosive tooth wear (ETW) prevalence across seven risk groups.

Pooled prevalence ranged from 30% (legal drugs and medications) to 67% (drugs and alcohol); GERD 54.1%, eating disorders 65%, special diets 65.9%, acidic beverages 40%, occupational and sports 51%.

Every risk group showed elevated ETW, supporting targeted prevention and monitoring; subgroup pooling was limited by heterogeneity of wear indices (abstract-level only).

## 세줄요약

침식성 치아마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW) 위험군 7종을 대상으로 한 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review)과 메타분석 (Meta-analysis): 9개 데이터베이스, 4403편 중 관찰연구 148편 포함.

합동 유병률은 약물 복용군 30%에서 약물·알코올군 67%까지였고, 위식도역류질환 (Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease, GERD) 54.1%, 섭식장애 65%, 특수 식이 65.9%, 산성 음료 40%, 직업·스포츠 51%.

모든 위험군에서 ETW가 높아 예방적 관리와 정기 모니터링이 필요하나, 지수 이질성 때문에 일부 군은 하위분석이 불가했고 본 요약은 초록 수준이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies ETW prevalence across seven risk groups in one review (148 studies).
- Shows elevated ETW in every risk group.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Nine databases plus grey literature, through April 2024; PROSPERO CRD42021270150.
- Observational studies of children and adults in risk groups reporting ETW prevalence; no date or language limits.
- Quality appraisal: Joanna Briggs Institute Prevalence Data Critical Appraisal Tool.
- Random-effects meta-analysis, general and subgroup.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 4403 retrieved; 148 included.
- Prevalence: legal drugs and medications 30%; acidic beverages 40%; occupational and sports 51%; GERD 54.1%; eating disorders 65%; special diets 65.9%; drugs and alcohol 67%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only; CIs, I-squared, risk-of-bias results, and per-group study counts not seen.
- Authors note heterogeneity of wear indices prevented subgroup analysis for some groups.
- Prevalence of a cross-sectional outcome, not a comparison with non-risk controls (as stated in the abstract, risk groups showed "higher" prevalence; comparator not described).
