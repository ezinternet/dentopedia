## Why Ingested

Directly addresses the clinical question of what surface finishing procedure (polishing vs. glaze) is best after grinding zirconia restorations — a common intraoral adjustment scenario. Provides quantified color stability (ΔE00 by CIEDE2000), translucency (TP00), and opalescence (OP) outcomes across four surface-treatment conditions after red wine immersion, filling a specific evidence gap in the `dental-materials/zirconia` collection.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/soares-2024-optical-properties-and-color-stability]]

## Three-line Summary

This in vitro study compared four surface-treatment conditions (control/as-sintered, grinding only, grinding+polishing, grinding+glaze) on 3Y-TZP zirconia discs (Zenostar T) after 18-day red wine immersion. Grinding alone produced clinically unacceptable color change (ΔE00 > 1.8) at both 9 and 18 days, while glaze application yielded the most color-stable surface; polishing reduced color change relative to grinding-only but performed worse than glaze. Translucency (TP00) and opalescence (OP) were statistically unaffected by surface treatment or immersion across all groups.

## Key Contributions

1. **Grinding-only produces clinically unacceptable color change**: Gr group ΔE00 = 2.65 (9 days) and 2.94 (18 days) — both above the clinical unacceptability threshold of 1.8 (Paravina 2015 criteria).
2. **Glaze is superior to polishing for post-grinding color recovery**: Gr+Gl ΔE00 = 0.65 at 9 days (below the perceptibility limit of 0.8), rising to an acceptable 1.66 at 18 days. Gr+Pol ΔE00 = 1.29 (9d) and 1.65 (18d), both perceptible but acceptable.
3. **Polishing reduces but does not match glaze performance**: The OptraFine polishing kit lowered Ra from 2.36 (Gr) to 1.34 μm, while glaze reduced it to 0.48 μm, approaching the control value of 0.17 μm.
4. **Surface roughness moderately predicts color change** (Spearman r = 0.42, p = 0.02): smoother surfaces accumulate fewer pigments.
5. **Translucency and opalescence preserved across all conditions**: Neither TP00 nor OP differed significantly between surface treatments or time points; all ΔTP00 values were within clinically acceptable limits (< 2.62 threshold).

## Methodology

- **Specimen fabrication**: Pre-sintered Zenostar T blocks sectioned, polished to Ø12 mm × 0.8 mm, sintered at 1450°C for 2 h.
- **Groups (n=8 each)**:
  - Ctrl: as-sintered, no further treatment
  - Gr: grinding with #4219 diamond bur (90–120 μm grit), oscillatory motion, 170,000 rpm
  - Gr+Pol: grinding + OptraFine Polishing Kit (two diamond rubbers + nylon brush with diamond paste)
  - Gr+Gl: grinding + Ivocolor glaze (fired at 710°C, vacuum, 45°C/min ramp)
- **Surface roughness**: Mitutoyo SJ-410 profilometer, ISO 4287:1997 (Ra, Rz), 6 measurements/specimen.
- **SEM**: Vega3 Tescan, 5000× magnification, gold-sputtered.
- **Color measurement**: SP60 spectrophotometer (X-Rite), CIE L*a*b* on gray/black/white backgrounds with glycerol coupling.
- **Color metrics**: ΔE00 by CIEDE2000; TP00 (translucency parameter) from black vs white background; OP (opalescence) = (aB* − aW*) + (bB* − bW*).
- **Thresholds**: ΔE00 > 0.8 perceptible, > 1.8 unacceptable (Paravina 2015); ΔTP00 > 0.62 perceptible, > 2.62 unacceptable (Salas 2018).
- **Statistics**: Repeated-measures ANOVA/Tukey for ΔE00/TP00/OP; Kruskal-Wallis for Ra/Rz; Spearman correlation for roughness vs. color.

## Key Results

| Group | ΔE00 9d | ΔE00 18d | Ra (μm) median | Rz (μm) median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ctrl | 1.19 | 1.34 | 0.17 | 1.30 |
| Gr | 2.65 | 2.94 | 2.36 | 13.20 |
| Gr+Pol | 1.29 | 1.65 | 1.34 | 8.33 |
| Gr+Gl | 0.65 | 1.66 | 0.48 | 2.54 |

- Gr was the only group with clinically unacceptable ΔE00 (> 1.8) at both time points.
- Gr+Gl was the only group at ΔE00 < 0.8 (sub-perceptibility) at 9 days.
- After 18 days, Ctrl, Gr+Pol, and Gr+Gl all converged to perceptible-but-acceptable values.
- TP00 and OP: no significant differences among groups or across time (p = 0.180 and p = 0.259 respectively).
- Roughness–color correlation: moderate (r = 0.42, p = 0.02).

## Limitations

- Single staining solution (red wine only), one pH value — does not reflect polychromatic dietary exposure.
- No resin cement layer, which would also absorb pigments in clinical conditions.
- In vitro, no aging beyond staining (no thermocycling, mechanical loading).
- Small sample (n = 8/group); single zirconia brand tested (Zenostar T / 3Y-TZP only).

## Glossary

- **3Y-TZP (3Y-테트라지르코니아)**: 3 mol% yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal — translucent monolithic zirconia used in conservative-thickness posterior restorations.
- **ΔE00 (색차)**: CIEDE2000 color difference formula; clinically perceptible > 0.8, unacceptable > 1.8.
- **TP00 (투광성 파라미터)**: Translucency parameter calculated from black and white background measurements.
- **OP (유백광)**: Opalescence parameter; difference in a*/b* coordinates between black and white backgrounds.
- **Ra (산술평균 거칠기)**: Arithmetical mean surface roughness (ISO 4287).
- **Rz (최대 높이 거칠기)**: Maximum height of the roughness profile (ISO 4287).
- **LTD (저온열화)**: Low-temperature degradation / hydrothermal aging — tetragonal→monoclinic phase transformation in humid/acidic environments.
- **t-m 상전이**: Tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation induced by grinding/polishing stress.
