## Why Ingested
First study to validate the Simple (S) clinical image quality evaluation chart that KAOMFR developed for non-radiologists — the P (professional) chart it is compared against is the manual grading basis that [[radiology/ameli-2025-automated-quality-evaluation-dental-panoramic]] automates. It supplies inter-rater reliability data for the same item taxonomy (artifacts, coverage area, positioning, density/contrast, overall quality) that [[radiology/lingam-2023-common-errors-subjective-quality-panoramic]] audited on clinical panoramics — and shows which chart items are too unreliable for subjective grading (artifacts, coverage area, patient movement).

## Three-line Summary
Instrument-comparison study: 10 evaluators (5 oral and maxillofacial radiologists [OMFR], 5 general dentists [G]) scored 285 clinical panoramic radiographs twice, 7 days apart, with both the KAOMFR professional (P) and simple (S) image-quality evaluation charts, to compare reliability, correlation, scores, time, and preference.

The S chart produced similar evaluation scores at roughly one-third shorter evaluation time (OMFR: 96.67 s P vs 62.93 s S, diff 33.74 s, p<0.001), the item correlations were overall lower than the P chart's (P 0.373–0.884 vs S 0.231–0.801), and 80% of evaluators (all 5 general dentists) preferred the S chart.

Inter-rater reliability was excellent (ICC single measures 0.929 OMFR / 0.870 G), but patient movement agreement was weak in both charts (r=0.373 P, r=0.231 S) — the S chart needs revision of its artifacts, coverage area, and patient movement items; the study is cross-sectional and its assigned S-chart weighting was imposed post hoc.

## 세줄요약
평가 도구 비교 연구: 치과 영상의학 전문의(Oral and Maxillofacial Radiologist, OMFR) 5명·일반 치과의사(General dentist, G) 5명, 총 10명의 평가자가 임상 파노라마 방사선 사진(Panoramic Radiograph) 285장을 대한구강악안면방사선학회(KAOMFR)의 전문가용(P) 및 간편형(S) 영상평가표로 각각 7일 간격으로 2회 평가해 신뢰도·상관·점수·시간·선호도를 비교했다.

S표는 비슷한 평가 점수를 얻으면서도 평가 시간이 약 1/3 짧았고(OMFR: P 96.67초 vs S 62.93초, 차 33.74초, p<0.001), 항목 상관은 P표보다 전반적으로 낮았으며(P 0.373–0.884, S 0.231–0.801), 평가자 80%(일반 치과의사 전원 포함)가 S표를 선호했다.

평가자 간 신뢰도는 우수했지만(단일측정 ICC 0.929 OMFR / 0.870 G), 환자 움직임 항목은 두 표 모두에서 일치가 낮아(r=0.373 P, r=0.231 S) S표의 인공물(Artifact)·촬영범위(Coverage Area)·환자 움직임 항목 개정이 필요하며, S표 가중치는 사후 부여된 것이라는 단면적 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First validation of the KAOMFR **Simple (S) chart** — previously created but never evaluated in use; shows it yields similar scores with markedly shorter evaluation time
- Reliability of the Professional (P) chart across OMFR vs general-dentist raters: inter-rater ICC single measures 0.929 (OMFR) / 0.870 (G), intra-rater ≥0.722 / ≥0.694
- Item-level correlation map of both charts: weak agreement isolated to **artifacts (r=0.428–0.495), coverage area (r=0.434 P-chart), patient movement (r=0.231–0.373)** — the items needing S-chart revision
- Preference evidence: 80% chose the S chart (100% of general dentists), citing ease of item selection and time saving — support for routine clinic quality control by non-radiologists

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: cross-sectional instrument-comparison (reliability/repeatability study) on archived clinical panoramic radiographs (IRB-approved, re-use of a previous study's image set)
- **Participants**: 10 evaluators — OMFR group n=5 (selected from 20 who had 4–30 yrs experience) and G group n=5 general dentists (1–3 yrs experience, recruited via online announcement)
- **Images**: n=285 panoramic radiographs, randomly mixed, not from a single imaging system (multi-device, multi-imaging-function — stated advantage for generalizability)
- **Instruments**: KAOMFR P chart and S chart; S chart had no per-item scores, so points were assigned to match the P chart as closely as possible. P: identification 12, artifacts 4, coverage area 6, patient positioning 18 + patient movement 4, density/contrast/sharpness 46, overall image quality grade 10 (=100). S: identification 12, coverage area 6, artifacts 4, patient movement 4, head positioning error 18, density and contrast 46, overall image quality grade 10 (=100)
- **Procedure**: order-balanced crossover to control learning effects (subgroups OMFR-A n=3, OMFR-B n=2, G-A n=2, G-B n=3; A = P then S, B = S then P); ≤50 images evaluated per evaluator per day; full second round repeated after 7 days for reliability
- **Outcomes**: inter-/intra-rater reliability, item-wise Pearson correlations (P–S, group-wise, chart-wise), evaluation time, evaluation scores, chart preference (questionnaire)
- **Statistics**: Pearson correlation, paired t-test, IBM SPSS Statistics 26.0; significance p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Reliability**: inter-rater single measures 0.929 (OMFR) and 0.870 (G), p<0.001; intra-rater 0.722–0.955 (OMFR) and 0.694–0.967 (G), p<0.001 — acceptable-to-excellent
- **Correlation (Pearson r)**: group → total score 0.871 (OMFR) / 0.663 (G); chart → total score 0.884 (P) / 0.801 (S). Highest blanket agreement on density/contrast/sharpness and overall quality; patient movement lowest in both charts (P 0.373, S 0.231)
- **Evaluation time**: OMFR 96.67±58.55 s (P) vs 62.93±50.11 s (S), diff 33.74 s (t=60.50, p<0.001); G 71.13±30.13 s (P) vs 45.28±17.61 s (S), diff 25.85 s (t=59.07, p<0.001)
- **Scores**: total (P vs S) OMFR 67.30±13.90 vs 64.27±14.74 (diff 3.03, p<0.001); G 67.36±13.33 vs 65.98±14.91 (diff 1.37, p<0.001). OMFR showed NO significant P–S difference for coverage area (p=1.00), patient movement (p=0.07), overall quality (p=0.42). Largest single-item gap: density/contrast/sharpness (diff 2.18 OMFR, 0.76 G)
- **Preference**: P chart 20% (n=2, both OMFR), S chart 80% (n=8; OMFR n=3, G n=5); no general dentist preferred P. Top reasons for S: facility (ease of selecting items) n=7 and time-saving n=7, then clarity n=4 and simplicity n=4

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Cross-sectional single-topic (panoramic) validity; no external criterion — correlations are internal (P vs S, chart vs chart), not against a gold-standard diagnostic reference
- S chart scoring weights were assigned post hoc by the researchers, not by KAOMFR — results partly depend on that imposed weighting
- G group had far less experience (1–3 yrs) — group differences may confound chart effects
- Identified low-reliability items (artifacts, coverage area, patient movement) should be revised/upgraded in the S chart; future work points to deep-learning automation of the P chart to remove the evaluator-time burden shown here

## 6. Related Work
- Ameli 2025: automated YOLOv8 classification of the same four quality criteria (artifacts, coverage, positioning, contrast/density); the manual chart reliability data here explain its differential accuracy per criterion
- Lingam 2023: prevalence audit of panoramic positioning errors (n=2,629) — the error taxonomy this chart-based grading formalizes
- Elkhateeb 2022: PSP image-plate artifact prevalence — prevalence context for the low-reliability artifacts chart item
- Ruston et al. 1999 (BDJ, cited): 1,813 panoramics from 41 UK clinics — 33% unacceptable, evidence for national standards; Korean P/S charts are the local instrument for equivalent control

## 7. Glossary
- **OMFR (Oral and Maxillofacial Radiologists)**: 전공 구강악안면방사선 전문의 group in this study (n=5, 4–30 yrs experience)
- **G (General dentists)**: 방사선 비전공 일반 치과의사 evaluator group (n=5, 1–3 yrs experience)
- **P chart**: Professional clinical image quality evaluation chart (KAOMFR) — detailed professional tool
- **S chart**: Simple clinical image quality evaluation chart (KAOMFR) — developed for frequent use by non-radiologists in clinics
- **KAOMFR**: Korean Academy of Oral and Maxillofacial Radiology
- **Focal trough (upper layer)**: 파노라마에서 예리하게 촬영되는 타원형 초점 구역 — 벗어나면 확대·흐림 발생
- **KAMI**: Korean Institute for Accreditation of Medical Imaging — CT/MRI/유방촬영은 체계적 QC하지만 파노라마는 미포함된 한국 상황
