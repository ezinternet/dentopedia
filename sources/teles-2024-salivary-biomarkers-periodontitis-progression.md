## Why Ingested

Longitudinal cohort (302 periodontitis + 113 healthy, 1 year without therapy) identifying salivary cytokine signatures specific to disease progression — provides high-quality clinical evidence for salivary biomarker utility in risk stratification and monitoring, complementing [[oral-microbiology/regueira-iglesias-2024-salivary-microbiome-periodontitis-diagnostic]] (microbiome) with proteomics/cytokine biomarker data.

## Three-line Summary

Longitudinal study monitoring 302 periodontitis and 113 healthy adults bi-monthly for 1 year without therapy, then 6 months post-NSPT; progressing patients (P2: ≥3 sites progressed) showed distinctly elevated salivary IFNγ, IL-6, VEGF, IL-1β, MMP-8, IL-10, and OPG relative to stable and healthy participants.

Serum analytes were NOT associated with progression — only salivary biomarkers distinguished progressors; NSPT significantly reduced salivary IFNγ, IL-6, IL-8, IL-1β, MMP-8, IL-10, OPG, MMP-9 and serum CRP, MMP-8, MMP-9, MPO.

The pro-inflammatory salivary milieu characterizing progressors (not reflected in serum) supports saliva as the primary diagnostic medium for periodontitis activity monitoring, with the specific cytokine panel (IFNγ, IL-1β, MMP-8) as key progression biomarkers.

혈청보다 타액이 치주염 진행 반영에 더 민감 — 타액이 치주 활성 모니터링의 주요 진단 매체임을 지지.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Salivary and serum inflammatory biomarkers during periodontitis progression and after treatment
- **Authors**: Teles FRF et al. (University of Pennsylvania, Harvard, Forsyth Institute)
- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Periodontology. 2024;51(12):1619-1631
- **PMID**: 39104016 | **PMCID**: PMC11651721 | **DOI**: 10.1111/jcpe.14048
- **Funding**: NIH Extramural
- **Study type**: Longitudinal observational cohort + post-NSPT assessment
- **Full text**: PMC open access

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest longitudinal study linking salivary cytokine profiles to periodontitis progression trajectories.
- Establishes that serum biomarkers are NOT useful for tracking local periodontal progression.
- Identifies a 7-cytokine salivary panel specific to progressors: IFNγ, IL-6, VEGF, IL-1β, MMP-8, IL-10, OPG.
- Demonstrates NSPT's systemic anti-inflammatory effect (CRP, MPO, MMPs in serum).

## 3. Methodology

- **Design**: Longitudinal cohort; bi-monthly assessments × 12 months (no treatment); then NSPT at 12 months + 6-month reassessment
- **Sample**: Periodontally healthy (H; n=113) and periodontitis patients (P; n=302)
- **Progression classification**: P0 (no sites progressed), P1 (1-2 sites), P2 (≥3 sites)
- **Biomarkers**: 10 salivary (IFNγ, IL-6, IL-8, VEGF, IL-1β, MMP-8, IL-10, OPG, MMP-9, + 1 more) + 5 serum (CRP, MMP-8, MMP-9, MPO, + 1)
- **Platform**: Luminex multiplex
- **Statistics**: Linear mixed models (log-transformed levels)

## 4. Key Results

**Progression signature (P2 vs P0/P1/H):**
- Elevated (p<0.05): Salivary IFNγ, IL-6, VEGF, IL-1β, MMP-8, IL-10, OPG
- Serum analytes: NOT associated with progression

**After NSPT:**
- Saliva reduced: IFNγ, IL-6, IL-8, IL-1β, MMP-8, IL-10, OPG, MMP-9 (all p<0.05)
- Serum reduced: CRP, MMP-8, MMP-9, MPO (all p<0.05)
- Clinical improvement confirmed

## 5. Limitations

- Observational; no intervention in monitoring phase
- Luminex platform — not point-of-care
- Saliva collection standardization needed for clinical implementation
- Limited amount of progression per patient → serum may miss local events

## 6. Glossary

- **P0/P1/P2**: Progression trajectory classification (0 = stable, 1 = 1-2 sites, 2 = ≥3 sites)
- **MMP-8/MMP-9**: Matrix metalloproteinases (collagenase, gelatinase — tissue destruction markers)
- **OPG**: Osteoprotegerin (decoy receptor for RANKL — bone protection)
- **VEGF**: Vascular endothelial growth factor
- **CRP**: C-reactive protein (systemic inflammation marker)
- **MPO**: Myeloperoxidase (neutrophil activation marker)
- **Luminex**: Bead-based multiplex immunoassay platform
- **NSPT**: Non-surgical periodontal therapy (SRP)
