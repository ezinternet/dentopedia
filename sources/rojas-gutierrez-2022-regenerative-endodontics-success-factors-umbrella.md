## Why Ingested

동일 배치에서 재생근관치료(Regenerative Endodontic Treatment, RET) 관련 umbrella review 3편을 함께 인제스트 중이다. 본 논문(Rojas-Gutiérrez 2022, Iran Endod J)은 13편의 기술적 SR + 7편의 메타분석(AMSTAR-2 평가)을 풀링해 RET의 전반적 성공/생존율과 성공 인자(증상 소실·병소 치유·치근 발육·상아질벽 비후·감각 회복)를 정리한 가장 포괄적인 우산리뷰로, 개별 SR인 [[endodontics/regenerative/panda-2022-regenerative-apexification-immature-teeth-sr-ma]]와 [[endodontics/regenerative/torabinejad-2017-ret-mta-apical-plug-sr-ma]]의 결과를 상위 메타 수준에서 재확인·통합하는 역할을 한다. 같은 배치의 다른 두 우산리뷰(DOI 10.22037/iej.v19i4.46292 — 미성숙치 치수괴사 관리 apexification vs RET 초점; DOI 10.3390/ijerph18020754 — REP 프로토콜 자체에 초점)와는 focus가 달라 중복이 아니다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD42019124402, Iran Endod J 2022) pooling 20 systematic reviews (13 descriptive SR + 7 meta-analyses, AMSTAR-2 assessed) on regenerative endodontic therapy in necrotic, immature-root permanent teeth.

Overall success rates ranged 50–98% and survival rates 94–100%; symptom resolution reached 100% in 75% of included SRs, and dentin thickening/root length gain were reported as advantages largely exclusive to regenerative therapy over apexification.

The review's own limitation analysis flags that all 20 included SRs conflated success/survival definitions inconsistently (100% of them) and used heterogeneous outcome parameters (80%), so pooled percentages should be read as ranges, not precise point estimates.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD42019124402, Iran Endod J 2022)로 비활력·미성숙 치근 영구치에서 재생근관치료(RET)에 관한 20편의 체계적 문헌고찰(기술적 SR 13편 + 메타분석 7편, AMSTAR-2 평가)을 종합.

전체 성공율은 50~98%, 생존율은 94~100%로 보고되었으며, 증상 소실은 포함 SR의 75%에서 100% 달성, 상아질벽 비후·치근 길이 증가는 apexification 대비 RET에서 거의 배타적으로 관찰되는 이점으로 정리됨.

이 리뷰 자체의 한계 분석에서, 포함된 20편 전부(100%)가 성공/생존 정의를 불일치하게 혼용했고 80%가 이질적인 결과 지표를 사용했음을 지적하므로, 풀링된 수치는 정확한 점추정치가 아닌 범위(range)로 해석해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella-level synthesis (as of publication) specifically pooling SR/MA evidence on both **effectiveness** (success/survival rates) and **success factors** (symptom resolution, lesion healing, apical closure, dentin thickening, root length increase, pulp sensibility recovery) of regenerative endodontic therapy.
- Applied AMSTAR-2 quality appraisal to 20 included SRs/MAs, classifying 11 (55%) high, 6 (30%) moderate, 3 (15%) low quality — giving readers a quality-weighted view of the underlying evidence base.
- Compiled a structured limitations table (Table 5) quantifying how many of the 20 included SRs share each methodological weakness (e.g., 100% conflate success/survival; 80% use inconsistent success definitions), which is itself a useful meta-methodological finding for the field.
- Explicitly distinguished "survival" (tooth retained) from "success" (survival + functional/health restoration) and mapped which outcome each included SR actually measured.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review per Joanna Briggs Institute methodology; PICO — Population: necrotic-pulp, incomplete-root-development teeth (all ages); Intervention/Comparator: different regenerative endodontic therapy types; Outcome: effectiveness and success factors.
- **Search**: PubMed-Medline, Scopus, LILACS, EMBASE, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews + grey literature (Google Scholar, manual reference-list search). No date restriction; languages English/Portuguese/Spanish.
- **Screening**: Two independent reviewers (title/abstract), third reviewer arbitrated disagreements. 398 records → 309 after dedup → 264 excluded → 45 full-text reviewed → 20 included.
- **Quality appraisal**: PRISMA checklist + AMSTAR-2 (16-item), pilot-tested on 5 articles with 90% concordance. Risk-of-bias and heterogeneity extracted per included SR when reported by that SR.
- **Synthesis**: Descriptive/narrative synthesis only (no re-pooling of primary data across the 20 SRs); results presented as ranges/percentages per SR in structured tables.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Metric | Range across included SRs |
|---|---|
| General success rate | 50–98% |
| General survival rate | 94–100% |
| Symptom resolution = 100% | 15/20 SRs (75%) |
| Lesion size reduction 50–100% | 11/20 SRs (55%) |
| Apical closure >80% | 6/20 SRs (30%) |
| Dentin wall thickening 80–100% | 6/20 SRs (30%) |
| Root length increase (reported) | 9/20 SRs (45%), range 0–100% |
| Pulp sensibility recovery | 2/20 SRs (10%), range 13–80% |
| Follow-up span | 1–48 months |

AMSTAR-2: 11 high (55%) / 6 moderate (30%) / 3 low (15%) quality. Cochrane Collaboration Tool was the most-used risk-of-bias instrument (10/20 SRs, 50%); Newcastle-Ottawa Scale in 3 (15%).

Traditional apexification comparison (from Discussion, citing primary literature within included SRs): calcium hydroxide apexification survival ~88%; MTA apexification survival 97–100%; RET survival 94–98% (up to 48-month follow-up). Root length gain and dentin wall thickening were reported as near-exclusive advantages of RET over apexification (apexification success rates for these parameters typically <10%).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Author-stated limitations (Table 5, n=20 included SRs):
- 100% of included SRs did not clearly distinguish survival from success.
- 80% used inconsistent success-definition parameters across studies.
- 75% included small-sample-size original studies.
- 65% lacked a registered/documented methodology protocol.
- 60% pooled heterogeneous regeneration protocols (scaffolds, materials).
- 40% flagged general lack of methodological rigor / called for more RCTs.
- 40% used inconsistent risk-of-bias methodology.
- 20% restricted to English-language literature with no grey-literature search.

Umbrella-review-level limitations (author-acknowledged): findings depend on the methodological quality of the included SRs rather than primary studies directly; geographic representation skewed to Europe/Asia/North America with only Brazil representing Latin America (possible "iceberg effect"); this review's own search may have omitted non-English or non-indexed literature.

Future work recommended: more rigorous RCTs on regenerative endodontic techniques (basic and modified protocols), further study of growth-factor/stem-cell approaches, standardized success/survival definitions across the field, and investigation of genotype/phenotype effects on healing/prognosis.

## 7. Glossary

- **RET (Regenerative Endodontic Treatment/Therapy)**: Biologically-based procedures aiming to regenerate pulp-dentin complex function in necrotic, immature teeth.
- **Apexification**: Traditional technique (calcium hydroxide or MTA/biomaterial apical barrier) to induce apical closure without true root regeneration.
- **AMSTAR-2**: 16-item critical appraisal tool for systematic reviews of healthcare interventions.
- **PROSPERO**: International prospective register of systematic review protocols.
- **Umbrella review**: A systematic review that synthesizes findings from multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a related topic.
- **MTA (Mineral Trioxide Aggregate)**: Bioactive cement used as an apical barrier/plug material in apexification and as a cervical seal in RET.
- **PRP/PRF**: Platelet-rich plasma / platelet-rich fibrin — blood-derived scaffolds used to deliver growth factors during regenerative procedures.
