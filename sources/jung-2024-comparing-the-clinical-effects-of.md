## Why Ingested

Requested as part of an ingest sweep for the Dongkook "Minoclin" product (2% minocycline HCl periodontal ointment): this is one of very few clinical studies that tests a 2% minocycline ointment head-to-head against drainage alone and against local doxycycline in an acute periodontal abscess, a setting the wiki's local-tetracycline evidence did not cover. ## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case series (single periodontist, single Korean clinic, 2019–2020; 63 of 83 screened patients) of acute periodontal abscesses at molars (PD ≥6 mm, pus, bone loss) treated with drainage plus one of three adjuncts chosen by patient preference: saline irrigation alone (control, n=20), 2% minocycline ointment (TM, n=21), or 300 mg/mL doxycycline solution (TD, n=22), repeated at 1 week and followed to 4 weeks.

At 4 weeks, abscess-side probing depth (PD) fell 1.09 mm (control), 1.88 mm (TM) and 2.88 mm (TD), and abscess-side bleeding on probing (BOP) fell 45.00%, 73.02% and 95.45%; both antibiotic groups beat control (TM vs control PD P=0.024, BOP P=0.009) and TD beat TM (PD P<0.001, BOP P=0.031), while plaque, recession and tooth mobility (Periotest value, PTV) did not differ between groups.

Allocation was by patient choice with no randomization or blinding, one examiner, 4-week follow-up and no microbiology, so the ranking (and especially the doxycycline-over-minocycline claim, from a compounded non-commercial solution) is hypothesis-generating; all three arms improved, including saline-only.

## 세줄요약

후향적 증례군 연구(치주전문의 1인·국내 치과의원 1곳, 2019–2020; 선별 83명 중 63명): 대구치 급성 치주농양(치주낭 깊이 ≥6 mm, 농, 골소실)을 배농 후 환자 선택에 따라 생리식염수 세척만(대조군, n=20), 2% 미노사이클린 연고(TM, n=21), 300 mg/mL 독시사이클린 용액(TD, n=22)으로 치료하고 1주 후 동일 처치를 반복, 4주까지 추적.

4주 시점 농양측 치주낭 깊이(PD) 감소량은 대조 1.09 mm·TM 1.88 mm·TD 2.88 mm, 농양측 탐침 시 출혈(BOP) 감소는 45.00%·73.02%·95.45%; 두 항생제군 모두 대조군보다 우수(TM vs 대조 PD P=0.024, BOP P=0.009)했고 TD가 TM보다 우수(PD P<0.001, BOP P=0.031); 치태·치은퇴축·치아동요도(Periotest value, PTV)는 군간 차이 없음.

환자 선택 배정이며 무작위·눈가림 없음, 평가자 1명, 추적 4주, 미생물 검사 없음이라 군간 순위(특히 시판 제품이 아닌 조제 용액인 독시사이클린이 미노사이클린보다 우월하다는 결과)는 가설 생성 수준이며, 생리식염수만 쓴 군도 모두 호전되었다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct comparison of three adjuncts after drainage of acute periodontal abscess: saline only vs 2% minocycline ointment vs 300 mg/mL doxycycline irrigation.
- Both local tetracycline-class adjuncts improved PD and BOP at the abscess side beyond drainage + saline alone at 4 weeks; doxycycline 300 mg/mL outperformed 2% minocycline ointment on PD, BOP and CAL.
- Reports abscess-specific effect sizes (deepest-site PD, abscess-side PD/BOP) rather than whole-mouth means.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective case series, Jan 2019–Dec 2020, one periodontist. 83 patients treated, 63 eligible (33 men, 30 women; mean age 46.2 y). Group assignment by patient preference after explanation of options.
- **Inclusion**: afebrile (36.4–36.7 C); first/second maxillary or mandibular molar with acute pain; PD ≥6 mm; pus; radiographic bone loss; ovoid buccal/lingual swelling.
- **Exclusion**: drugs inducing gingival changes, uncontrolled hypertension/diabetes/heart disease, current smoker or ex-smoker ≤10 y, pregnancy/lactation, tetracycline allergy, non-periodontal abscess (endodontic, crack/fracture, foreign body), spread to adjacent teeth, antibiotics within 1 month before/after, post-treatment anti-inflammatories, cervical lymphadenitis, different medication at visits 1 and 2.
- **Groups**: control n=20 (saline irrigation 5 min); TM n=21 (saline irrigation, then 2% minocycline ointment, excess removed); TD n=22 (300 mg/mL doxycycline prepared by dissolving doxycycline capsule powder [100 mg/capsule] in saline at 40 C, applied 5 min, then saline-irrigated).
- **Protocol**: Visit 1 (baseline): local anesthesia, drainage via pocket, supragingival calculus removal, assigned adjunct. Visit 2 (1 week): full-mouth root planing, same adjunct repeated. Visit 3 (4 weeks): examination, plaque control.
- **Outcomes**: primary PD; secondary BOP, plaque index (PI), gingival recession (GR), clinical attachment level (CAL), tooth mobility by Periotest value (PTV). Single examiner, UNC-15 probe, 6 sites per tooth; abscess-side (3 sites) PD/BOP and deepest PD also analyzed.
- **Stats**: patient-level; paired t-test, 1-way ANOVA, repeated-measures ANOVA, linear mixed model for abscess-side PD/BOP, Bonferroni post hoc, alpha 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Baseline: no significant between-group differences in PD, BOP, PI, GR, CAL. Most patients had generalized stage 3 periodontitis; 17/19/20 per group had never-treated periodontitis. All sites were posterior molars.

| Outcome (visit 1 to visit 3) | Control | TM (2% minocycline) | TD (doxycycline 300 mg/mL) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean PD, whole tooth (mm) | 6.97 to 6.22 (-0.75) | 6.66 to 5.41 (-1.25) | 7.03 to 5.29 (-1.74) |
| Abscess-side PD (mm) | 9.40 to 8.31 (-1.09) | 8.98 to 7.10 (-1.88) | 9.51 to 6.63 (-2.88) |
| Abscess-side BOP (%) | 100 to 55.00 (-45.00) | 100 to 26.98 (-73.02) | 100 to 4.55 (-95.45) |
| Mean BOP, whole tooth (%) | 80.83 to 39.17 | 83.33 to 20.63 | 78.79 to 6.82 |
| Deepest PD (mm) | 9.99 to 8.35 | 9.47 to 7.34 | 10.13 to 6.90 |
| Mean CAL (mm) | 7.43 to 7.03 (-0.40) | 6.91 to 6.05 (-0.86) | 7.38 to 6.07 (-1.31) |
| PTV | 28.28 to 18.50 | 27.04 to 17.74 | 25.25 to 13.57 |

- Between-group tests (linear mixed model, abscess side): PD control vs TM P=0.024, control vs TD P<0.001, TD vs TM P<0.001; BOP control vs TM P=0.009, control vs TD P<0.001, TD vs TM P=0.031.
- Whole-tooth mean PD change: TD larger than control (P<0.001) and TM (P=0.010).
- Deepest-site PD change, visit 1 to 2: control 0.52, TM 0.77, TD 1.30 mm (control vs TD P<0.001; TM vs TD P=0.015; control vs TM P=0.528). Visit 2 to 3: control 1.02, TM 1.35, TD 1.92 mm; only control vs TD significant (P=0.001).
- CAL gain: TD 1.31 mm vs control 0.40 mm (P<0.001) and vs TM 0.86 mm (P=0.002).
- No significant between-group differences in PI, GR or PTV change; all within-group indices improved significantly by 4 weeks, including control.
- At visit 2, mild spontaneous or biting pain persisted in 5 (control), 3 (TM) and 4 (TD) patients; none at visit 3.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective, non-randomized; allocation by patient choice (selection bias/confounding), no blinding, one examiner who was also the treating clinician; single clinic.
- Short follow-up (4 weeks); no microbiological assessment (authors' stated limitations).
- Small groups (20-22); patient-level analysis; multiple outcomes with limited multiplicity control beyond Bonferroni post hoc.
- Doxycycline arm used a clinic-compounded 300 mg/mL solution (stated as 100 mg capsule powder dissolved in saline), not a commercial product; concentration, stability and reproducibility are unverified here. Minocycline arm used a commercial 2% ointment (brand not named in the text). The two arms also differ in vehicle, contact time and application method (5-min irrigation vs ointment left in pocket), so the comparison is not drug-vs-drug at equal delivery.
- Text inconsistencies: BOP scoring is described as 0 = bleeding within 10 s and 1 = no bleeding (apparently inverted); results text cites "Table 1" for several analyses that are in Tables 2-4.
- Generalized stage 3 periodontitis patients, posterior molars, non-smokers only; extrapolation to other presentations (anterior, smokers, diabetics) is unsupported.
- Authors call for longer follow-up and microbiological studies.

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary
- **TM group**: drainage + saline irrigation + 2% minocycline ointment.
- **TD group**: drainage + saline irrigation + 300 mg/mL doxycycline solution.
- **sidePD / sideBOP**: mean PD/BOP at the 3 sites on the abscess-bearing (buccal or lingual) side.
- **deepPD**: deepest PD per tooth.
- **PTV**: Periotest value, tooth mobility (lower = firmer).
