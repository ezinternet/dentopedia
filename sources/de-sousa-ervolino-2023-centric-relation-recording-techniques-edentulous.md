## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/complete-denture/de-moraes-melo-neto-2021-centric-relation-condylar-reproducibility-sr]]는 완전치열·비TMD 환자를 대상으로 한 6편의 저질 연구를 검토하며 "무치악 환자 데이터가 없고, 그래픽법(고딕아치) 대 생리적 기법 간 직접 비교가 없다"는 두 가지 근거 공백을 명시적으로 지적했다. 본 논문은 정확히 그 공백을 메운다 — 무치악 환자(n=12) 대상, 구외/구내 고딕아치 추적(그래픽법)과 연하(deglutition)·전두엽 조작(FMTE, 생리적/유도법)을 교합기 상 과두위치 재현성으로 직접 비교한다. de Moraes Melo Neto CL이 두 논문의 공저자로 겹친다 — 같은 연구팀이 자신들의 SR이 지적한 공백을 임상연구로 후속 검증한 형태.

## Three-line Summary

Observational cross-sectional study (n=12 edentulous individuals) comparing four centric relation (CR) recording techniques — extraoral gothic arch (EOGA), intraoral gothic arch (IOGA), deglutition (D), and frontal manipulation with tongue elevation (FMTE) — each repeated 3× by the same operator, reproducibility assessed via tri-dimensional condylar-housing displacement on a semi-adjustable articulator.

Deglutition showed significantly greater anteroposterior displacement (right 3.78±0.69 mm, left 3.45±0.74 mm) than the other three techniques (EOGA 3.00±0.00/3.00±0.00, FMTE 2.81±0.52/2.82±0.79, IOGA 2.90±0.65/3.12±0.44 mm; ANOVA p<0.001, F=11.396); IOGA (340.40±163.41 s) and EOGA (285.93±133.84 s) took substantially longer than D (86.00±34.33 s) and FMTE (101.33±36.72 s) (ANOVA p<0.001, F=21.118).

Authors conclude graphic recordings (gothic arch) show better reproducibility and accuracy, though the reported numbers show FMTE numerically lowest and EOGA/IOGA/FMTE close together — the clear separation is deglutition being worst, not graphic methods being clearly best.

## 세줄요약

무치악 개인 12명 대상 관찰 단면연구 — 구외 고딕아치(EOGA), 구내 고딕아치(IOGA), 연하법(Deglutition, D), 전두부 조작+혀거상법(Frontal Manipulation with Tongue Elevation, FMTE) 4가지 중심위(Centric Relation, CR) 기록 기법을 같은 술자가 각 3회 반복, 반조절성 교합기 상 과두(condyle) 하우징의 3차원 변위로 재현성 평가.

연하법이 다른 세 기법 대비 유의하게 큰 전후방 변위를 보였고(우측 3.78±0.69mm, 좌측 3.45±0.74mm; ANOVA p<0.001, F=11.396; EOGA/FMTE/IOGA는 2.8~3.1mm대), 소요시간은 IOGA(340.40±163.41초)·EOGA(285.93±133.84초)가 D(86.00±34.33초)·FMTE(101.33±36.72초)보다 유의하게 길었다(ANOVA p<0.001, F=21.118).

저자들은 그래픽 기록(고딕아치)이 재현성·정확도가 더 낫다고 결론지었으나, 실측치상 FMTE가 수치상 가장 낮고 EOGA/IOGA/FMTE 세 기법이 근접해 있어 — 명확한 분리는 "그래픽이 우월"이 아니라 "연하법만 유의하게 열등"이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Fills a gap explicitly named by a prior SR from an overlapping author team ([[wiki/complete-denture/de-moraes-melo-neto-2021-centric-relation-condylar-reproducibility-sr]]): the first direct comparison of graphic (gothic arch) vs. physiologic/guided (deglutition, FMTE) CR recording techniques in edentulous subjects.
- Rare **four-way** comparison (EOGA, IOGA, D, FMTE) in the same cohort with the same operator, each technique repeated 3× — controls for inter-technique confound better than pairwise designs.
- Times each technique to seconds — a chairside-relevant outcome largely absent from other CR-reproducibility literature: graphic tracing (EOGA/IOGA, 286–340 s) costs roughly 3× the chair time of deglutition/FMTE (86–101 s).
- Nuance the authors' own headline claim: "graphic recordings showed better reproducibility" is supported only insofar as deglutition is significantly worse than the other three (D vs EOGA/IOGA/FMTE); FMTE is numerically the **lowest** anteroposterior displacement of all four techniques (2.81/2.82 mm), so graphic tracing is not shown to beat FMTE — the paper's own data do not cleanly support "graphic > all others," only "graphic and FMTE > deglutition."

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Observational cross-sectional study, single operator.
- **Sample**: 12 fully edentulous individuals.
- **Techniques** (4, each performed on every subject): extraoral gothic arch (EOGA) tracing, intraoral gothic arch (IOGA) tracing, deglutition (D), frontal manipulation with tongue elevation (FMTE).
- **Repetition**: each technique repeated 3× on the same subject, same time of day, 30-minute interval between techniques.
- **Outcome — reproducibility**: tri-dimensional displacement of the condylar housing (mandibular condyle) relative to the condylar guide wall (glenoid cavity) on a semi-adjustable articulator, measured anteroposterior (AP), mediolateral (ML), and superior-inferior (SI), per side.
- **Outcome — time**: seconds elapsed from the start of each technique to wax occlusion fixation.
- **Statistics**: two-way ANOVA + Tukey for AP displacement (technique × side); one-way ANOVA + Tukey for time; Kruskal-Wallis for ML/SI displacement across techniques; Mann-Whitney for side (right vs. left) comparison. α<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Anteroposterior (AP) displacement, mm (mean ± SD)**

| Technique | Right side | Left side |
|---|---|---|
| Deglutition (D) | **3.78 ± 0.69** | **3.45 ± 0.74** |
| Extraoral gothic arch (EOGA) | 3.00 ± 0.00 | 3.00 ± 0.00 |
| Intraoral gothic arch (IOGA) | 2.90 ± 0.65 | 3.12 ± 0.44 |
| Frontal manipulation + tongue elevation (FMTE) | 2.81 ± 0.52 | 2.82 ± 0.79 |

Technique factor significant, ANOVA α<0.001, F=11.396. D differed significantly from the other three; EOGA, IOGA, FMTE were not clearly separated from each other in the reported summary statistics.

**Time spent, seconds (mean ± SD)**

| Technique | Time |
|---|---|
| Intraoral gothic arch (IOGA) | **340.40 ± 163.41** |
| Extraoral gothic arch (EOGA) | 285.93 ± 133.84 |
| Frontal manipulation + tongue elevation (FMTE) | 101.33 ± 36.72 |
| Deglutition (D) | 86.00 ± 34.33 |

Technique factor significant, ANOVA α<0.001, F=21.118. Graphic techniques (EOGA, IOGA) took ~3× longer than functional/guided techniques (D, FMTE).

**Mediolateral / superior-inferior displacement**: assessed via Kruskal-Wallis (technique) and Mann-Whitney (side); numeric values not given in the abstract.

**Authors' conclusion**: "Graphic recordings showed better reproducibility and accuracy of the position of the centric relation."

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest — full text not retrieved (paywalled, no OA copy). ML/SI displacement values, exact p-values for pairwise comparisons, and full discussion/limitations sections are not available from the abstract alone.
- Very small sample (n=12) for a four-technique, three-repetition design.
- **EOGA reported as exactly 3.00 ± 0.00 mm on both sides** — a zero standard deviation across 12 subjects × 3 repeats each is implausible for a continuous biological measurement and suggests a measurement floor, instrument resolution limit, or rounding artifact in how AP displacement was captured/reported for that technique. Treat this cell with caution; it may understate true EOGA variability.
- The authors' summary conclusion ("graphic recordings showed better reproducibility") is not cleanly supported by the reported AP means — FMTE is numerically the lowest, and EOGA/IOGA/FMTE are close together; the only clearly separated technique is deglutition (worst). This distinction was not qualified in the abstract's conclusion sentence.
- Single operator — no inter-operator reliability data.
- Reproducibility metric (condylar-housing-to-glenoid-wall displacement in the articulator, mm scale ~2.8–3.8 mm) is not on the same scale as split-cast discrepancy metrics used elsewhere in this literature (e.g., ~0.15–0.86 mm in [[wiki/complete-denture/singh-2026-nick-notch-intraoral-gothic-arch-jaw-relation]]) — direct cross-study magnitude comparison is not valid.

This study did not test a static bite record, and uses a different, coarser-resolution metric (~2.8–3.8 mm condylar-housing displacement), so the two rankings are not directly comparable — flagged as an open question for a future overview rather than resolved here.

## 7. Glossary

- **Centric relation (CR)**: reproducible, bone-braced maxillomandibular reference position with the condyles in the most anterior-superior position against the articular eminences.
- **Extraoral gothic arch (EOGA) tracing**: functional graphic CR technique using a forward-projecting extraoral tracing platform; arrowhead apex marks CR.
- **Intraoral gothic arch (IOGA) tracing**: functional graphic CR technique using an intraoral tracing device near the hinge axis.
- **Deglutition (D)**: CR recording during the act of swallowing, exploiting reflexive retruded mandibular positioning.
- **Frontal manipulation with tongue elevation (FMTE)**: guided CR technique combining operator frontal manipulation of the mandible with patient tongue elevation.
- **Condylar housing displacement**: the tri-dimensional discrepancy between the mandibular condyle position recorded by a technique and the wall of the condylar guide (glenoid cavity) on a semi-adjustable articulator — this study's reproducibility metric.
