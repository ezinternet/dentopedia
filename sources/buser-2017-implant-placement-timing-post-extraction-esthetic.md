## Three-line Summary

ITI narrative review (Periodontology 2000 2017) tracing the historical evolution of implant placement timing post-extraction in esthetic single-tooth sites — from pioneer era (1975–1989) through the 2013 ITI selection-criteria framework.

Without patient selection, immediate (Type I) placement carries a 20–30% risk of >1 mm midfacial recession; applying strict criteria (intact facial wall + medium-thick biotype) substantially reduces this risk, while early placement (Type II/III) offers lower esthetic risk for borderline anatomical situations.

All timing protocols achieve >95% implant survival, so the critical clinical decision is esthetic outcome risk stratification — not survival — when choosing between immediate, early, and late placement.

## 세줄요약

즉시·조기·지연 식립 시점의 역사적 발전(1975–1989 개척기 → 2013 ITI 선택기준 확립)을 추적한 ITI 서술 리뷰(Periodontology 2000 2017).

환자 선택 없이 즉시 식립(Type I)을 시행하면 >1 mm 순면 퇴축 위험이 20–30%에 달하며, 엄격한 기준(순면 골벽 온전 + 중등도·두꺼운 조직 생체형) 적용 시 이 위험이 유의하게 감소; 조기 식립(Type II/III)은 경계 해부학에서 더 낮은 심미 위험 제공.

모든 식립 시점에서 생존율 >95%를 달성하므로 핵심 임상 결정은 생존율이 아닌 심미 결과 위험 분류에 있음.

## 1. Key Results
- Historical analysis: pioneer (1975-89) → trial & error (1990-2003) → ITI classification (2003) → risk factor identification (2008) → selection criteria (2013)
- ITI Type I (immediate): highest esthetic risk; requires intact facial wall + medium-thick biotype
- ITI Type II (early, 4-8wk): soft tissue healed; reduced recession risk; recommended when biotype/bone borderline
- ITI Type III (early, 12-16wk): partial bone fill; best for compromised sockets
- ITI Type IV (late, >6mo): healed ridge; most predictable but requires bone augmentation often
- Esthetic complications: 20-30% risk of >1mm midfacial recession for Type I without selection criteria
- After 2008: proper selection criteria (intact wall + thick biotype) reduces this risk significantly
- All survival rates >95% regardless of timing

## 2. Risk Factors Identified
- Thin tissue biotype
- Facial malposition of implant (must be 2mm palatal from future crown margin)
- Thin (<1mm) or damaged facial bone wall
- High smile line
