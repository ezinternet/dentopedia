## Why Ingested

한국 대학병원(강릉원주대학교치과병원 치주과)의 20년 단일기관 후향 자료로, 실패한 임플란트를 같은 부위에 다시 식립(재식립)한 경우의 생존율과 재실패 위험인자를 보고한다. 한국 환자군의 임플란트 실패·장기 생존 문헌을 보강하며 조기 실패 위험 종합 페이지와 연결된다. → [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]

## Three-line Summary

Single-center Korean retrospective study (Gangneung-Wonju National University Dental Hospital, Department of Periodontics; December 1999 - March 2021): of 10,666 implants in 4063 patients, 259 failed implants in 170 patients were reviewed and 80 second-time-placed (re-implanted) implants met the inclusion criteria.

The 1-year survival of second-time-placed implants was 88.1%; the abstract names smoking (HR 5.066, p = 0.081), implant surface (HR 18.776, p < 0.01) and timing of reimplantation (HR 0.086, p < 0.01) as risk factors for refailure, though the smoking p-value does not reach 0.05.

Authors conclude that re-implanted implants survive less well than first-time-placed implants and that causes of the earlier failure should be analyzed and modifiable factors controlled before reimplantation; this summary rests on the abstract only, and patient nationality is not stated in the text (Korean setting implied by the institution).

## 세줄요약

강릉원주대학교치과병원 치주과의 한국 단일기관 후향 연구 (1999년 12월 - 2021년 3월): 임플란트 10,666개(환자 4063명) 중 실패 259개(환자 170명)를 검토했고 재식립 (second-time-placed) 임플란트 80개가 포함 기준을 충족.

재식립 임플란트의 1년 생존율은 88.1%; 초록은 흡연 (hazard ratio, HR 5.066, p = 0.081), 임플란트 표면 (HR 18.776, p < 0.01), 재식립 시기 (HR 0.086, p < 0.01)를 재실패 위험인자로 제시하나 흡연의 p값은 0.05에 미치지 못함.

저자는 재식립 임플란트의 생존율이 최초 식립 임플란트보다 낮다고 결론짓고 재식립 전 이전 실패 원인 분석과 조절 가능한 위험인자 관리를 권고; 초록 기반이며 환자 국적은 본문에 명시되지 않고 기관 소재지로만 한국임을 알 수 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of few studies focused on the outcome of re-implanted implants (the authors state few studies have examined this).
- Reports 1-year survival of second-time-placed implants (88.1%) and Cox-type hazard ratios for smoking, implant surface and reimplantation timing.
- Practical message: analyze the cause of the first failure and control modifiable factors before reimplantation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective cohort from clinical and radiographic records; survival analysis for implants meeting inclusion criteria.
- **Setting**: Department of Periodontics, Gangneung-Wonju National University Dental Hospital, Korea; implants placed December 1999 - March 2021.
- **Population**: 10,666 implants in 4063 patients overall; 259 failed implants in 170 patients evaluated; 80 second-time-placed implants met inclusion criteria (patient count for the 80 not given in the abstract).
- **Factors evaluated**: patient-, surgical-, implant- and prosthesis-related.
- **Failure/refailure definition and the exact inclusion criteria**: not given in the abstract. Early-versus-late failure timing is not reported in the abstract; only 1-year survival is given.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Item | Result |

| Failed implants evaluated | 259 in 170 patients (of 10,666 implants in 4063 patients) |
| Included second-time-placed implants | 80 |
| 1-year survival, second-time-placed | 88.1% |
| Smoking | HR 5.066, p = 0.081 |
| Implant surface | HR 18.776, p < 0.01 |
| Timing of reimplantation | HR 0.086, p < 0.01 |

- The abstract states that survival of second-time-placed implants was lower than that of first-time-placed implants (no comparison figure given).
- The abstract lists smoking among risk factors although p = 0.081; the direction of effect for surface category and timing category (reference groups) is not given.
- Denominator: the survival rate and HRs are implant-based (n = 80); the overall 259/10,666 corresponds to about 2.4% failure, calculated here from the stated counts, not reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only: reference categories for implant surface and reimplantation timing, follow-up length, definition of failure, inclusion criteria, and confidence intervals are unavailable. The very large surface HR (18.776) from 80 implants implies few events and likely wide intervals (inference, not reported).
- Retrospective single-center design.
- Smoking HR is labelled a risk factor at p = 0.081 (not significant at 0.05); treat as a trend.
- Only 1-year survival reported; no early/late failure split.

## 7. Glossary

- **Re-implantation (재식립)**: placing a new implant after a previous implant failed and was removed.
- **Second-time-placed implant**: an implant placed for the second time at a site of prior failure.
- **HR (Hazard Ratio, 위험비)**: relative event rate in survival analysis.
- **Refailure (재실패)**: failure of the re-implanted implant.
