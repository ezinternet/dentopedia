## Why Ingested

The wiki holds TMD treatment reviews that mention psychological comorbidity only in passing (e.g. [[tmj/alowaimer-2024-noninvasive-therapies-tmj-sr]], [[tmj/chan-2022-myogenous-tmd-diagnosis-treatment-review]]) and single studies of stress/sleep ([[tmj/jeon-2024-chewing-difficulty-tmd-tooth-loss]], [[tmj/albadi-2026-sleep-disorders-tmj-pain-dysfunction]]), but no pooled estimate of anxiety/depression by TMD subtype. This SR+MA fills that gap for the myogenous (myofascial pain) subtype.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42020150562) of 24 studies (20 pooled; 3678 subjects) comparing anxiety and depression across RDC/TMD or DC/TMD subtypes, with myofascial pain as the comparison group.

Anxiety and depression were more frequently distributed in myofascial pain patients (p = .001), and TMD without myofascial pain had less severe levels than myofascial-pain-only patients (p <= .01), despite moderate-to-high heterogeneity; most individual studies found similar occurrence across subtypes.

Psychological screening appears essential when managing muscular TMD, but the observational design, heterogeneity and instrument dependence limit causal or quantitative inference (abstract-level only).

## 세줄요약

RDC/TMD·DC/TMD로 진단한 측두하악관절장애 (Temporomandibular Disorder, TMD) 아형별 불안·우울 분포를 근막통 (myofascial pain) 군과 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA; 24편, 메타분석 20편, 3678명).

불안·우울은 근막통 환자에서 더 빈번했고 (p = .001), 근막통이 없는 TMD는 근막통 단독군보다 불안·우울 정도가 낮았다 (p <= .01); 이질성은 중등도~높음이며 개별 연구 다수는 아형 간 유사하다고 보고했다.

근육형 TMD 환자에서는 심리 상태 평가가 중요하지만, 관찰연구 기반·이질성·평가도구 의존성 때문에 인과 해석이나 정량적 해석에는 한계가 있다 (초록 수준 정보만 확인).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled comparison of anxiety and depression between myofascial pain and other TMD subtypes.
- Shows that the choice of psychological instrument affects assessed levels.
- Argues for psychological assessment as part of subtype-specific management.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PROSPERO-registered, PRISMA 2020. PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, SciELO (last search 12 March 2021) plus reference lists. Inclusion: RDC/TMD or DC/TMD diagnosis; validated anxiety/depression instruments; at least two TMD subtypes with myofascial pain as comparison group. RevMan 5.3.5, random- or fixed-effects. Quality: 10-item prevalence-study tool and Newcastle-Ottawa scale.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 4086 records; 24 included; 20 in meta-analyses; 3678 subjects.
- Anxiety and depression more frequent in myofascial pain (p = .001); moderate-to-high heterogeneity.
- TMD without myofascial pain less severe than myofascial-pain-only (p <= .01).
- Most individual studies: similar occurrence/severity across subtypes, but average prevalence higher in muscular TMD diagnoses.
- Pooled effect sizes, CIs and I-squared values were not available in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract-only; full text not retrieved, so pooled estimates and risk-of-bias details are unknown. Moderate-to-high heterogeneity; instrument type influences results; observational data cannot establish direction of association (distress as cause vs consequence of pain).
