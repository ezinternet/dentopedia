## Why Ingested

기존 [[drug/anticoagulants/izzetti-2024-doac-bleeding-management-tooth-extraction-prospective]] (전향 코호트)의 "DOAC 비중단 + 국소지혈" 결론을 Xa 인자 억제제 전용 무작위 분할구강 설계로 보강하고, 국소 지혈제 비교 근거([[drug/anticoagulants/swissdentj-2026-gelatin-sponge-tranexamic-acid-anticoagulated]])에 자가 혈소판풍부피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF) 대 젤라틴 스펀지 (Gelatine Sponge, GS)라는 직접 비교 RCT를 더한다. Source: PubMed ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00784-023-05317-3)).

## Three-line Summary

Prospective, double-blind, randomized split-mouth trial (Mainz, n=21 patients on FXa inhibitors, 42 extraction sockets) comparing PRF plug vs gelatine sponge without stopping the anticoagulant.

Mild oozing occurred at 28 of 42 sites (67%), 24 stopped within 30 min of gauze pressure and the longest within 1.5 h; no hemorrhage or delayed bleeding, and no PRF vs GS difference (all p>0.05).

PRF and GS are both acceptable socket inserts with sutures; FXa inhibitors need not be interrupted for simple extractions, but n=21, pooled drugs and no no-agent control limit inference.

## 세줄요약

전향적 이중맹검 무작위 분할구강 시험(마인츠, Xa 인자 억제제 복용 환자 21명, 발치와 42개)으로, 항응고제를 중단하지 않고 혈소판풍부피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)과 젤라틴 스펀지 (Gelatine Sponge, GS)를 비교했다.

42부위 중 28부위(67%)에서 경미한 스며나옴이 있었고 24건은 거즈 압박 30분 내, 최장 1.5시간 내 지혈되었으며, 출혈 합병증이나 지연 출혈은 없었고 PRF와 GS 간 차이도 없었다(모두 p>0.05).

PRF와 GS 모두 봉합과 함께 사용할 수 있는 지혈 삽입재이며 단순 발치에서 Xa 인자 억제제 (Factor Xa inhibitor) 중단은 불필요하나, 21명·약제 합산 분석·무처치 대조군 부재로 해석에 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First split-mouth randomized comparison of PRF vs a hemostatic sponge specifically in patients on direct FXa inhibitors (rivaroxaban, apixaban, edoxaban), with the drug continued.
- Time-resolved bleeding assessment (0, 30 min, 1 h, 1.5 h, 24 h, day 7) with blinded assessor.
- Descriptive signal that posterior mandibular sockets, age >85 y and higher-dose regimens drove the longest oozing.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: prospective, double-blind, randomized split-mouth; sealed-envelope allocation; investigator 2 placed inserts, investigator 1 (blinded) assessed bleeding.
- Patients: FXa-inhibitor monotherapy, normal coagulation; two contralateral teeth of the same jaw; exclusions included hematologic disease, antiresorptives, teeth needing osteotomy or flap, sinus communication.
- Test: PRF plug (A-PRF tubes, 1200 rpm, 177 g, 8 min, 10 ml blood per matrix); control: Hygitec gelatine sponge. Both sockets curetted and closed with cross-mattress Vicryl 3/0 sutures; articaine 4% + epinephrine 1:200,000; drug taken as usual.
- Bleeding graded by duration of gauze tamponade (none, 30 min, 1 h, 1.5 h); severity mild/moderate/severe.
- Statistics: 42 sites (21 + 21); paired t-test, Kruskal-Wallis; p <= 0.05; drug subgroups descriptive only.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Patients: mean age 71 ± 2.8 y (45-89), 11 F / 10 M, 3 smokers; rivaroxaban 9, apixaban 7, edoxaban 5; indications AF 9, DVT 10, PE 2.
- Oozing at 28 sites: 24 stopped at 30 min, 2 at 1 h, 2 at 1.5 h; no hemorrhage, no bleeding after 1.5 h or at day 7; PRF vs GS no difference (all p>0.05).
- By drug: events rivaroxaban 18, apixaban 14, edoxaban 10 (p=0.15 t-test; p=0.45 Kruskal-Wallis); both 1.5-h cases were apixaban.
- Location: mandible 18 vs maxilla 10 events (p=0.0008 t-test; p=0.02 KWT); posterior 16 vs anterior 12 (p=0.03 t-test; p=0.23 KWT).
- Time since last FXa dose not associated with bleeding (p=0.1875); no creatinine correlation (max 1.28 mg/dl; not tested statistically).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (21 patients), so drug subgroups (5-9 patients) descriptive only; three FXa inhibitors pooled.
- No control socket without any hemostatic agent, so absolute benefit of either insert is not measured; both arms also had sutures.
- Simple extractions only (no osteotomy, flap or sinus involvement); no severe renal impairment; few elderly and smokers.
- PRF requires blood draw and added chair time.
- Source inconsistency: title/abstract say single-tooth removal, but each patient had two extractions (one per side); PubMed labels it a randomized controlled trial while the title says clinical prospective randomized split-mouth study.

## 7. Glossary

- Factor Xa inhibitor: direct oral anticoagulant blocking factor Xa (rivaroxaban, apixaban, edoxaban).
- PRF (platelet-rich fibrin): autologous fibrin-platelet clot from centrifuged patient blood, used as a socket insert.
- Split-mouth design: each patient receives both interventions on contralateral sides, serving as own control.
- Gelatine sponge (GS): absorbable hemostatic sponge, the comparator "gold standard".
- Oozing: mild bleeding controlled by local pressure.
