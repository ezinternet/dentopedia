## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (Nanchang, China; 2021-2024; 3,533 simple taper retentive implants in 1,681 patients): early failure occurred in 53 implants (1.5% implant level) and 49 patients (2.9% patient level).

In multivariate analysis, smoking (OR=2.148, P=0.021), anterior mandible site (OR=3.669, P=0.006) and posterior maxilla site (OR=2.191, P=0.033) were risk factors; simultaneous sinus floor elevation was significant only in univariate analysis.

Smoking and these two sites should be weighed in preoperative planning; this summary rests on the abstract only (full text not retrieved).

## 세줄요약

후향적 코호트 (중국 난창, 2021-2024; 환자 1,681명의 단순 테이퍼 유지형 임플란트 3,533개): 조기 실패 53개 (임플란트 단위 1.5%), 49명 (환자 단위 2.9%).

다변량 분석에서 흡연 (OR=2.148, P=0.021), 하악 전치부 (OR=3.669, P=0.006), 상악 구치부 (OR=2.191, P=0.033)가 위험인자; 동시 상악동 거상술은 단변량에서만 유의.

- Sinus floor elevation lost significance after adjustment.

## 3. Methodology

- **Design**: retrospective cohort, cases January 2021 - June 2024 with complete medical records; implant is the unit of analysis.
- **Exposures**: patient (sex, age, smoking, hypertension, diabetes), implant (length, diameter, surface treatment), surgical (site, timing, simultaneous sinus floor elevation, simultaneous bone augmentation).
- **Analysis**: univariate followed by multivariate analysis (model type and covariates not stated in the abstract).
