## Why Ingested

The wiki holds several **pairwise** CTG/FGG-vs-collagen-matrix comparisons signal-by-signal ([[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/rios-osorio-2025-xcm-vs-ctg-fgg-implant-soft-tissue-sr-ma]], [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/dadlani-2024-efficacy-xenogeneic-collagen-matrices-augmenting]], [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/montero-2022-soft-tissue-substitutes-vs-autogenous-keratinized-mucosa-sr]]) but no rank-ordering of all five materials against each other; this network meta-analysis (NMA) supplies that head-to-head ranking at 180/360 days and is the natural sibling of [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/tavelli-2021-soft-tissue-phenotype-modification-peri-implant-nma]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + network meta-analysis (23 studies in the qualitative synthesis, 16 studies — 11 RCTs and 5 CCTs — in the quantitative NMA; N = 573 sites) comparing autogenous soft tissue grafts (CTG, FGG) with collagen matrices (ADM, xCM, VCMX) for peri-implant soft tissue augmentation, with dimensional thickness change and keratinized mucosa width (KMW) as primary outcomes.
CTG was the best material for increasing peri-implant soft tissue thickness at both 180 and 360 days; ADM gave good buccal thickness increase mainly in the first three months, and vestibuloplasty + FGG was the most effective technique for KMW augmentation after 180 days (FGG best for KMW up to 90 days).
Clinical bottom line: CTG remains the reference for thickness gain and FGG for keratinized width, while ADM and VCMX are acceptable thickness alternatives with lower patient morbidity; recommendations are constrained by the limited number of included studies and high heterogeneity.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 주위 연조직 증대에서 자가이식(CTG·FGG)과 콜라겐 매트릭스(ADM·xCM·VCMX)를 비교한 체계적 고찰 + 네트워크 메타분석(NMA, 질적 23편·양적 16편 — RCT 11·CCT 5, N = 573 부위).
결합조직이식 (CTG, Connective Tissue Graft)이 180·360일 모두 연조직 두께 증가에서 최상위 재료였고, 무세포 진피 기질 (ADM, Acellular Dermal Matrix)은 술 후 3개월 이내 협측 두께 증가에 양호했으며, 전정성형술 + 유리치은이식 (FGG, Free Gingival Graft)이 180일 후 각화점막폭 (KMW, Keratinized Mucosa Width) 증대에 가장 효과적이었다 (90일까지는 FGG 단독이 최상).
임상적 하한선: 두께 확보는 CTG, 각화폭 확보는 FGG가 기준이며 ADM·용적안정형 콜라겐 매트릭스 (VCMX, Volume-Stable Collagen Matrix)는 이환율이 낮은 두께 대안이나, 포함 연구 수 제한과 높은 이질성 때문에 결론의 확실성은 제한적이다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First NMA comparing **all five** peri-implant soft tissue materials/techniques head-to-head: autogenous grafts (CTG, FGG) versus matrices (ADM, xCM = xenogeneic collagen matrix, VCMX = volume-stable collagen matrix).
- Separates the two clinical goals that pairwise reviews often conflate: **thickness** (CTG wins, both at 180 and 360 days) versus **keratinized mucosa width** (FGG/vestibuloplasty+FGG wins), showing no single material dominates both.
- Time-dependent ranking: ADM performs well only in the early (first 3 months) buccal thickness window, while vestibuloplasty + FGG only takes the KMW lead after 180 days (FGG best up to 90 days).
- Adds patient-centered framing: ADM and VCMX offer lower patient morbidity as thickness alternatives to autogenous harvest.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review + network meta-analysis (NMA), following a pre-specified electronic database search.
- **Inclusion**: Randomized and non-randomized controlled trials (RCTs and CCTs), parallel or split-mouth design, treating ≥10 patients.
- **Interventions compared**: CTG, FGG, ADM, xCM, VCMX (plus vestibuloplasty + FGG technique).
- **n**: 23 studies in the qualitative analysis; 16 studies (11 RCTs + 5 CCTs) in the quantitative analysis; N = 573 sites evaluated in the NMA.
- **Primary outcomes**: soft tissue thickness dimensional changes and keratinized mucosa width (KMW) changes.
- **Secondary outcomes**: (a) patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs); (b) volumetric changes; (c) surgical operating time; (d) other periodontal measurements.
- **Statistics**: Network meta-analysis (multivariate random-effects meta-regression framework — STATA/network-plot references cited).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Comparison / outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Soft tissue thickness, 180 days | CTG ranked best material |
| Soft tissue thickness, 360 days | CTG ranked best material |
| Buccal thickness, first ~3 months | ADM showed good results |
| KMW, up to 90 days | FGG best graft for increasing keratinized mucosa |
| KMW, after 180 days | Vestibuloplasty + FGG most effective technique |
| Lower-morbidity thickness alternatives | ADM, VCMX |
| Evidence base | 23 studies qualitative; 16 studies (11 RCT 5 CCT, N = 573 sites) quantitative NMA |

Reported conclusion: "While CTG demonstrated better performance in all the comparison and FGG showed to be the best graft to increase keratinized mucosa up to 90 days, ADM and VCMX may be used to increase soft tissue horizontal thickness with lower patients' morbidity."

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Limited number of included studies for the network.
- High heterogeneity among included studies — number of patients, treatment sites, surgical techniques, outcome measures, and follow-up durations all varied.
- Abstract-only ingest: full text (network geometry, SUCRA/ranking probabilities, effect estimates with 95% CIs, PROM and operative-time results) was not retrievable from the PDF, so no numeric effect sizes or p-values could be transcribed.
- Authors explicitly state that recommendations for future studies are mandatory.

## 7. Glossary
- **CTG (Connective Tissue Graft)**: autogenous subepithelial connective tissue harvested from the palate; reference material for increasing peri-implant soft tissue thickness.
- **FGG (Free Gingival Graft)**: autogenous full-thickness palatal graft; most effective single graft for creating keratinized mucosa width.
- **ADM (Acellular Dermal Matrix)**: allogeneic processed dermal matrix used as a soft tissue substitute.
- **xCM / XCM (Xenogeneic Collagen Matrix)**: porcine-derived collagen matrix (e.g. Mucograft-type), non-cross-linked substitutive matrix.
- **VCMX (Volume-Stable Collagen Matrix)**: cross-linked, volume-stable porcine collagen matrix (e.g. Fibro-Gide-type) designed for horizontal thickness gain.
- **KMW (Keratinized Mucosa Width)**: apico-coronal width of keratinized mucosa around an implant; ≥2 mm is the commonly cited threshold for peri-implant health.
- **NMA (Network Meta-Analysis)**: meta-analysis that combines direct and indirect comparisons to rank multiple interventions simultaneously.
