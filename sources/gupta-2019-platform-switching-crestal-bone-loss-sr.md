## Why Ingested

Directly addresses a gap the wiki's platform-switching cluster had never closed: how large a horizontal implant-abutment mismatch is actually needed for the bone-preservation effect. [[overviews/platform-switching-marginal-bone-preservation-synthesis]] synthesizes 9 papers on PS-vs-PM outcome magnitude but none of them report or compare the offset dimension itself — this paper's Table 1 compiles actual abutment/implant diameter pairs across 9 primary studies, and cites Hürzeler et al. 2007's explicit claim that a 0.45 mm per-side abutment reduction is sufficient to avoid peri-implant bone loss.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative literature review (self-labeled "systematic review" but no PRISMA flow, no formal risk-of-bias tool, no meta-analysis; PubMed + Google Scholar search, 2005–2017) compiling human clinical studies, FEA studies, and case reports on platform switching (PS) and crestal bone loss.

All 9 tabulated human comparative studies favored PS over platform-matching (PM); Hürzeler et al. 2007 (cited primary study) reported PS MBL 0.22 mm vs PM 2.02 mm and concluded a 0.45 mm per-side abutment-diameter reduction is sufficient to prevent peri-implant bone loss; compiled per-side mismatches across studies cluster mostly at 0.25–0.45 mm, with one outlier study using 1.0 mm.

No original data of its own — a narrative aggregation of case reports and small primary studies, most using the Biomet 3i implant system (6 of 9), so generalizability across implant-abutment connection designs is limited; abstracts and full-text for some cited primary studies (Hürzeler 2007, Canullo & Rasperini 2007, Rodríguez-Ciurana 2009) are not yet independently ingested into this wiki.

## 세줄요약

서술형 문헌 리뷰(자칭 "체계적 문헌고찰"이나 PRISMA 흐름도·공식 비뚤림위험 도구·메타분석 없음; PubMed+Google Scholar, 2005–2017) — 플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching, PS)과 치조정골 소실 관련 인체 임상연구·유한요소분석(FEA)·증례를 정리.

정리된 인체 비교연구 9편 전부 PS가 플랫폼 매칭(Platform Matching, PM)보다 우수; 인용된 원저 Hürzeler 2007은 PS군 변연골소실(MBL) 0.22mm vs PM 2.02mm 보고, **지대주를 편측 0.45mm씩 줄이면 임플란트주위 골소실 방지에 충분**하다고 결론; 여러 연구의 편측 미스매치 값은 대부분 0.25–0.45mm에 몰려 있고 한 연구는 1.0mm의 이례적 큰 값을 사용.

독자적 데이터는 없는 증례·소규모 원저 나열형 리뷰이며 9편 중 6편이 Biomet 3i 임플란트 시스템이라 다른 연결부 설계로의 일반화는 제한적; 여기 인용된 핵심 원저(Hürzeler 2007·Canullo & Rasperini 2007·Rodríguez-Ciurana 2009)는 이 위키에 아직 독립적으로 인제스트되지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Table 1 compiles actual abutment/implant diameter pairs (mm) across 9 primary human comparative studies — the only wiki-held source with per-study offset dimensions rather than just PS-vs-PM outcome numbers.
- Surfaces Hürzeler et al. 2007's explicit numeric claim: 0.45 mm per-side abutment reduction is "sufficient" to avoid peri-implant crestal bone loss — the closest thing in this wiki's evidence base to a stated minimum-effective-mismatch threshold.
- Separately reports Rodríguez-Ciurana et al. 2009's inter-implant distance data (41 adjacent-implant pairs, <3 mm spacing): mean vertical bone resorption 0.62 mm, horizontal 0.60 mm, bone peak extending coronally 0.24 mm beyond the imaginary IAJ-to-IAJ line — a distinct "distance" question (implant-to-implant spacing) from the abutment-mismatch question.
- Compiles 5 FEA reports (July 2009 cutoff) classifying implant abutment-joint types into butt-joint vs taper-joint designs, diagonal loads tested 15–30° / 10–250 N.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Narrative literature review labeled "systematic review" in the title; methods section states inclusion/exclusion criteria and a PubMed + Google Scholar search 2005–2017, but no PRISMA flow diagram, no formal risk-of-bias/quality appraisal tool, and no meta-analysis (no pooled effect size) are reported.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Google Scholar.
- **n**: 9 human comparative studies tabulated (Table 1); 5 FEA studies discussed narratively.
- **Outcomes**: Crestal/marginal bone loss (vertical and horizontal), by PS vs PM abutment configuration; secondarily inter-implant distance and FEA stress distribution.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Not a true systematic review by PRISMA standards despite the title — no independent dual-reviewer screening described, no formal risk-of-bias tool, no meta-analysis/pooled estimate.
- 6 of 9 tabulated studies use the Biomet 3i system specifically — the offset values and outcomes may not generalize to other implant-abutment connection geometries (e.g., internal conical vs external hex).
- The 0.45 mm "sufficient" claim (Hürzeler 2007) is a single-study conclusion carried forward by this review, not independently replicated within the review's own dataset — no dose-response analysis across the compiled mismatch values was performed by the review authors themselves.
- Longest follow-up cited is ~2 years; no long-term (5+ year) offset-magnitude data.

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary
- **PLS/PS**: Platform Switching — using a narrower-diameter abutment on a wider-diameter implant platform.
- **PM**: Platform Matching — abutment and implant platform diameters equal (conventional restoration).
- **IAJ**: Implant-Abutment Junction.
- **Per-side mismatch**: half the difference between implant platform diameter and abutment diameter — the radial horizontal offset distance, i.e. (implant Ø − abutment Ø) / 2.
- **FEA**: Finite Element Analysis, a computational stress-distribution modeling method.
