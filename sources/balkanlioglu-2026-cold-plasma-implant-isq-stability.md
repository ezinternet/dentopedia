## One-line Summary
Within-patient RCT (BMC Oral Health 2026): 10 patients, 40 implants — all three cold plasma protocols (vacuum, argon jet, cold atmospheric) showed +8.3–10.0 ISQ units at 56 days vs untreated control (all p<0.001); pilot study, n=10.

## 한줄요약
Within-patient RCT (BMC Oral Health 2026): 10명 40개 임플란트 — 콜드 플라즈마 3종(진공·아르곤·대기) 모두 56일 ISQ 대조대비 +8.3~10.0 단위 우위(p<0.001); 파일럿 연구, n=10.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: BMC Oral Health, 2026;26
- DOI: 10.1186/s12903-026-08679-8 | PMID: 42226275 | PMC: PMC13505121
- Setting: Kahramanmaras Sutcu Imam University, Turkey
- Trial: NCT07348770 (retrospectively registered Dec 2025)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First within-patient RCT directly comparing 3 cold plasma protocols vs untreated for early ISQ
- All 3 plasma types: significantly more favorable ISQ trajectory vs control (all p<0.001 at day 56)
- Vacuum plasma: +10.0 ISQ; argon jet: +8.9; cold atmospheric: +8.3 vs control
- No adverse events; 100% retention at 56 days
- Key mechanism: cold plasma increases titanium surface wettability and protein adsorption → promotes osseointegration
- Pilot study — effect direction consistent, magnitude substantial, but n=10 warrants replication

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Prospective, single-center, randomized within-patient controlled trial
- n=11 patients enrolled; 1 excluded (insufficient primary stability); n=10 final
- 40 implants: 10 per group (control/vacuum/argon jet/cold atmospheric plasma)
- Each patient: 4 implants, one per group (non-anatomically mirrored sites)
- Plasma applied 60 seconds before insertion
- ISQ: Osstell, at baseline, day 14, 28, 56
- Statistics: nested linear mixed-effects model

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Time | Vacuum vs control | Argon vs control | Cold atm vs control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 14 | Increasing trend | Increasing trend | Increasing trend |
| Day 28 | Control shows transient dip | Plasma groups rise | — |
| Day 56 | +10.0 ISQ (p<0.001) | +8.9 ISQ (p<0.001) | +8.3 ISQ (p<0.001) |

Baseline ISQ comparable across groups (70.6–70.9).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- n=10 patients — critically small (pilot)
- Retrospectively registered — methodology concern
- Sites not anatomically mirrored — site variability uncontrolled
- 56-day endpoint — long-term osseointegration not assessed
- No clinical outcomes (MBL, survival) — only ISQ surrogate
- Need: larger multicenter RCT, standardized site characteristics, 12-month ISQ + clinical endpoints

## 6. Related Work
- Cold plasma mechanism: reactive oxygen/nitrogen species (RONS) → oxidize surface contaminants → increase surface energy → better protein adsorption → faster osseointegration
- Existing in-vitro evidence: cold plasma improves cell adhesion and proliferation on Ti surfaces
- Sandblasted/acid-etched (SLA) surfaces remain gold standard — plasma may be applied on top of any surface

## 7. Glossary
- Cold plasma: partially ionized gas at near room temperature; delivers RONS without thermal damage
- Vacuum plasma: argon-based discharge in low-pressure chamber
- Cold atmospheric plasma (CAP): operates at ambient pressure; suitable for chairside use
- ISQ: Implant Stability Quotient; within-patient: each patient is their own control
