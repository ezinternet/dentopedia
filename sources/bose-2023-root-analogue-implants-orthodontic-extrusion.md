## Why Ingested

Reference-mined from [[wiki/immediate-implant/alqutaibi-2026-root-analog-dental-implants]] — a 28-study SR on root-analog implants (RAIs) that explicitly names this Böse 2023 paper as its **only retrospective comparative study**, and the only study in the whole SR that directly pits an RAI against a non-implant alternative (forced orthodontic extrusion, FOE) rather than against conventional screw-type implants. This fills a specific evidence gap the SR could not synthesize on its own (no meta-analysis was possible; RAI-vs-FOE is a single head-to-head study, not a pooled comparison), and gives the RAI-vs-conventional decision framework a socket-preservation-alternative comparator.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective comparative study (n=40 patients; 20 restorations supported by root-analogue implants [RAIs, REPLICATE™ System, milled titanium/zirconia-root + zirconia-abutment] vs 20 restorations of severely damaged natural roots after forced orthodontic extrusion [FOE], 10 premolars + 10 anterior teeth per group) at Charité–Universitätsmedizin Berlin, evaluated with the Functional Implant Prosthodontic Score (FIPS, 5 domains, max 10) at mean follow-up 18.4±5.7 months (RAI) vs 43.9±16.4 months (FOE).

Both groups showed 100% survival, but FOE scored significantly better than RAIs on marginal bone loss (p<0.01, both raters), interdental papillae/interproximal contacts (p<0.05, one rater) and mucosa quality/quantity (p<0.02, one rater); composite FIPS means were approximately 9.2/8.8±1.1/1.2 (FOE) vs 7.4/7.7±1.3/1.5 (RAIs) per the body/discussion text (the abstract states the opposite attribution — see note below).

Clinical implication: FOE (root preservation via forced extrusion) appears more bone- and soft-tissue-preserving than immediate RAI placement in this small retrospective cohort, and the authors suggest FOE could serve as a socket-preservation strategy before eventual conventional implant placement — but the retrospective design, unequal follow-up durations between groups, single-operator/single-examiner treatment (with independent-rater bias partially mitigated), and small sample (n=20/group) limit generalizability.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to directly compare RAI-supported restorations against restorations of natural roots preserved via forced orthodontic extrusion (FOE) — two divergent strategies for the same clinical problem (a deeply destroyed, otherwise-extractable tooth).
- Applies the Functional Implant Prosthodontic Score (FIPS; interproximal, occlusion, design, mucosa, bone — each 0–2, max 10) to both implant-retained and non-implant (natural-root) restorations, adapting the bone sub-score for FOE via segmental radiographic superimposition (10-segment root-length division).
- Reports FOE technique parameters in granular detail: mean extrusion 3.50±0.87 mm over mean 17.88±10.98 days, retention period mean 130.60±89.12 days (minimum 8 weeks), fiber-post extrusion anchor + adjacent-tooth bonding.
- Documents RAI fabrication detail for the REPLICATE™ System: CBCT/STL-superimposed CAD design, milled Y-TZP (all-ceramic, n=4) or titanium-root/Y-TZP-abutment hybrid (n=16) components, PRGF (plasma rich growth factors) socket/implant wetting, Temporary Protective Cover (TPC) load protection with ~0.6 mm protective gap.
- Post-hoc power analysis (G*Power, n=20/group, α=0.05, effect size 1.17) reports 97% power, supporting that the observed between-group differences are unlikely to be a sample-size artifact despite the small n.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Groups**: n=20 RAI-supported single crowns (10 premolar + 10 anterior) vs n=20 FOE-restored single crowns (10 premolar + 10 anterior); 40 cases total, all retrospectively selected from available complete datasets.
- **RAI inclusion**: non-smokers; no bone-metabolism-affecting medication; non-inflammatory surrounding soft/hard tissue; intact bone compartments around the tooth.
- **FOE inclusion**: need for restoration of a deeply destroyed tooth with two adjacent teeth present; probing depth ≤2 mm; defect violating biologic width and/or missing ferrule; prospective crown-to-root ratio ≤1; tooth mobility ≤1; single-tooth restoration planned. Molars, suspected hypercementosis/ankylosis, and vertical root fractures excluded from FOE.
- **RAI fabrication**: impression → type IV plaster cast → laboratory-scanned STL → DICOM/STL superimposition by manufacturer specialist → milled CAD/CAM TPC + implant + abutment; all-ceramic parts = sintered Y-TZP; hybrid parts = titanium (grade IV) root bonded to Y-TZP abutment via biocompatible glass-oven process; macro/micro retentions added via corundum blasting + etching. Benex Extraction-System used for atraumatic vertical tooth removal; conventional extraction as fallback. Try-In verified fit before RAI placement; PRGF used to wet implant surface/alveolus; TPC semi-adhesively bonded to adjacent tooth/teeth for load protection (~0.6 mm gap).
- **FOE protocol**: fiber-reinforced composite extrusion pin bonded to root surface at widest diameter; anchor post bonded to adjacent tooth; elastics (>0.5 N) drive occlusal-direction extrusion; concurrent supra-crestal fibrectomy + scaling/root planing; weekly control visits, elastics changed twice daily by patient; retention (composite bonding to adjacent teeth) for ≥8 weeks after target extrusion reached; post-extrusion endodontic revision, glass-fiber post placement where indicated, composite core build-up, single crown.
- **Outcome measure**: FIPS (5 domains, 0–2 each, max 10) scored independently by two raters (M.W.H.B. for RAI follow-up, M.B. for FOE follow-up and treatment) from photographs (papillae/design/mucosa), floss-checked interproximal contacts, shimstock-checked occlusion, and radiographs (bone). Bone sub-score for FOE used 10-segment radiographic superimposition of pre-/post-treatment films; RAI bone loss computed via rule-of-three scaling against known implant length.
- **Statistics**: SciPy (Python); Krippendorff's alpha for inter-rater reliability (McHugh's strict interpretation bands); Mann–Whitney U test for ordinal FIPS comparisons between groups; significance threshold p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Follow-up**: RAI 18.4±5.7 months; FOE 43.9±16.4 months (substantially longer FOE follow-up — a key limitation given bone remodeling is time-dependent).
- **Demographics**: RAI patients mean age 55.9±14.0y (70% female); FOE patients mean age 47.3±18.5y (45% female).
- **Survival**: 100% in both groups over the observation period.
- **Complications**: ceramic chipping in 1 restoration per group; orthodontic relapse in 3 FOE-extruded teeth (15% of the FOE group) — consistent with prior literature flagging relapse as FOE's chief complication.
- **FIPS composite** (body/discussion-text attribution): FOE 9.2/8.8±1.1/1.2 (raters M.B./M.W.H.B.) vs RAI 7.4/7.7±1.3/1.5 — FOE scored higher (better).
- **Significant sub-domain differences (favoring FOE)**: bone loss p<0.01 (both raters, consistent — described as "moderate" [RAI] to "almost perfect" [FOE] inter-rater reliability for this domain); interproximal (papillae/contacts) p<0.05 (one rater, M.B., only); mucosa quality/quantity p<0.02 (one rater, M.B., only, with only "weak" inter-rater reliability on this domain — authors flag possible subjective bias).
- **Occlusion**: scored 2/2 (perfect) for every restoration in both groups — no statistical comparison possible on this domain.
- Authors' interpretation: FOE appears to better preserve marginal bone and peri-restorative soft tissue than immediate RAI placement in this cohort, though the much shorter RAI follow-up (18.4 vs 43.9 months) means RAI bone loss could still be under-reported relative to its true long-term trajectory.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Retrospective design**, no randomization, cases selected from available complete datasets only (selection bias risk).
- **Unequal follow-up duration** between groups (18.4 vs 43.9 months) confounds direct bone-loss comparison — the paper itself flags that RAI bone loss might appear worse partly *because* it wasn't followed as long, or the comparison is simply not apples-to-apples.
- **Single-operator/single-examiner design**: all RAI treatments by author D.H., all RAI follow-ups by M.W.H.B.; all FOE treatments and follow-ups by M.B. — despite independent dual-rating and Krippendorff's alpha calculation, own-procedure-scored-better bias is explicitly acknowledged by the authors (weak inter-rater reliability, 0.40–0.59 band, specifically on "mucosa").
- **Small sample** (n=20/group, n=10 per tooth-position subgroup) — though post-hoc power was 97% for the observed effect size, subgroup (premolar vs anterior) analyses were not reported separately.
- **No standardized radiograph holders** — 2D radiographic bone-loss assessment without customized bite-registration devices, a recognized methodological limitation shared with much of the RAI/FOE literature.
- **Abstract vs. body-text discrepancy** (data-quality note, not a finding of the study): the published abstract states FIPS scores 9.2/8.8 = RAIs and 7.4/7.7 = FOE, while the Results/Discussion body states the opposite (9.2/8.8 = FOE, 7.4/7.7 = RAIs). This source page follows the body-text attribution because it is internally consistent with the paper's own narrative (FOE significantly outperforms RAI on bone/interproximal/mucosa, so FOE should carry the higher composite score) — but readers consulting the original abstract alone should be aware of this apparent publisher/typesetting inconsistency.
- Authors call for further long-term, larger-sample data on both RAI and FOE success/survival/functional/esthetic outcomes, and note FOE may have an underexplored role as a socket-preservation step before eventual conventional implant placement.

## 6. Related Work

- Cites the RAI historical lineage: Hodosh et al. 1969 (PMMA Dental Polymer Implant Concept), early-1990s beagle-dog titanium root-copy experiments (88% osseointegration), Strub/Kohal "Re-Implant System" (48% survival at 2002 follow-up), "BioImplant Concept" zirconia RAIs (92% survival at 2 years), CBCT-enabled prefabricated RAIs (94.4% survival, 18.9±2.4 months).
- Cites prior FOE literature establishing the technique (first described 1973) and its main documented complication (orthodontic relapse) across multiple appliance approaches (fixed arch wire/elastics, removable appliances, RPDs, complete dentures).
- References a prospective 1-year RAI comparative study reporting higher survival for milled titanium RAIs vs milled zirconia RAIs vs DLMS-manufactured RAIs — directly relevant to the material/fabrication stratification also discussed in [[wiki/immediate-implant/alqutaibi-2026-root-analog-dental-implants]].

## 7. Glossary

- **RAI (Root-Analogue Implant)**: Custom implant milled/fabricated to replicate the exact external root morphology of an extracted tooth, for immediate placement into the extraction socket without a screw-thread form.
- **FOE (Forced Orthodontic Extrusion)**: Orthodontic movement of a tooth in the occlusal direction using anchored elastics/appliances, used to re-establish biologic width and expose sound tooth structure (ferrule) on a severely damaged tooth without surgical crown lengthening.
- **FIPS (Functional Implant Prosthodontic Score)**: A 5-domain (interproximal, occlusion, design, mucosa, bone), 0–10-point clinical/radiographic/esthetic scoring system, originally developed for implant-retained restorations and here adapted for non-implant (FOE) restorations.
- **Y-TZP**: Yttria-stabilized tetragonal polycrystalline zirconia, the ceramic used for RAI abutments (and all-ceramic RAI roots) in this study.
- **PRGF**: Plasma Rich Growth Factors, an autologous blood-derived preparation used to wet the RAI surface/socket at placement.
- **Krippendorff's alpha**: A statistical measure of inter-rater reliability used to assess agreement between the two independent FIPS evaluators.
