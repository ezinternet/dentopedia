## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/mbl/pico-2019-abutment-height-implant-depth-thin-mucosa]]는 얇은 점막에서만 지대주 높이를 시험했고 식립 깊이도 함께 바꿨다. 본 클러스터 RCT는 얇은 점막과 두꺼운 점막 모두에서 모든 임플란트를 골정상 식립해 지대주 높이(1 mm 대 3 mm) 단독 효과와 점막 두께와의 상호작용을 동시에 평가하므로, 후향적 근거인 [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]]의 짧은 지대주 불리 효과를 무작위 배정으로 재확인하고 [[implants/mbl/linkevicius-2009-reaction-crestal-bone-around-implants]]류 점막 두께 연구와의 관계를 따지는 데 필요해 추가한다.

## Three-line Summary

Cluster randomized controlled trial (70 platform-switched internal-hex implants placed equicrestally; thin mucosa <=2.0 mm vs thick mucosa >2.0 mm) assigning 1-mm (short) or 3-mm (long) prosthetic abutments after 3 months of submerged healing, with radiographic marginal bone loss (MBL) followed to 12 months of loading.

At 12 months of loading 66 implants were functioning (two dropouts, two failures; 97% survival); mean MBL versus placement (T0) was 0.59-0.80 mm in the short-abutment groups and 0.28-0.37 mm in the long-abutment groups, a significant difference irrespective of vertical mucosal thickness, with most resorption in the first 6 months after loading, particularly around short abutments.

In these equicrestal platform-switched implants, abutment height rather than mucosal thickness drove MBL (abstract-only; no exact p-values, CIs or per-group sample sizes available).

## 세줄요약

클러스터 무작위 대조시험 (Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial) — 골정상 (Equicrestal) 식립한 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS) 내부 육각 임플란트 70개를 점막 두께 얇음(<=2.0 mm)·두꺼움(>2.0 mm) 두 군으로 나누고, 3개월 매몰 치유 후 1 mm(짧은) 또는 3 mm(긴) 지대주 (Abutment)를 배정해 부하 12개월까지 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)을 방사선으로 추적.

부하 12개월에 66개 기능 중 (탈락 2, 실패 2; 생존율 97%); 식립 시점 (T0) 대비 평균 MBL은 짧은 지대주군 0.59-0.80 mm, 긴 지대주군 0.28-0.37 mm로 수직 점막 두께와 무관하게 유의한 차이였고, 골흡수는 부하 후 첫 6개월에 가장 컸으며 특히 짧은 지대주에서 두드러졌다.

골정상 식립 PS 임플란트에서는 점막 두께가 아니라 지대주 높이가 MBL을 좌우했다 (초록만 확보 — 정확한 p값·신뢰구간·군별 표본수는 미확인).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First clinical study (per the authors' objective statement) to evaluate the simultaneous effect of abutment height and vertical mucosal thickness on MBL.
- Crosses abutment height (1 mm short vs 3 mm long) with mucosal thickness (thin <=2.0 mm vs thick >2.0 mm) in implants all placed equicrestally, so placement depth is not co-varied.
- Reports a per-height MBL range at 12 months of loading and the time course (greatest resorption in the first 6 months after loading).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Cluster randomized controlled trial; radiographs at implant placement (T0), restoration delivery (T1), 6 months (T2) and 12 months (T3) of loading.
- **Implants**: 70 platform-switched implants with internal hex, placed equicrestally.
- **Groups**: Patients grouped by vertical mucosal thickness, thin (<=2.0 mm) vs thick (>2.0 mm). After three months of submerged healing, abutments of 1 mm (short) or 3 mm (long) were randomly assigned for single-crown screwed restorations in both groups.
- **Outcome**: Marginal bone loss (MBL) on radiographs.
- **Not available in the abstract**: implant system, group sizes, randomization/concealment/blinding details, radiographic method, statistical test, exact p-values, confidence intervals, SDs.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- After 12 months of loading, 66 implants were functioning (two dropouts, two failures) — 97% survival rate.
- Mean MBL at T3 compared with T0: 0.59 to 0.80 mm in short-abutment groups; 0.28 to 0.37 mm in long-abutment groups.
- Differences were statistically significant irrespective of vertical peri-implant mucosal thickness.
- The MBL pattern over time showed the greatest bone resorption in the first 6 months after loading, particularly around implants with short abutments.
- Authors' conclusion: platform-switched implants restored with short abutments present greater MBL than identical implants with long abutments, without significant mucosal-thickness effects.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only**: no tables, SDs, CIs, exact p-values, per-group n, or risk-of-bias detail.
- The abstract reports a range across the two thickness subgroups per abutment height, not a separate value for each of the four cells.
- 12-month post-loading horizon only; all implants were placed equicrestally, so the finding does not address supracrestal or subcrestal placement.
- Abutment height was assigned after 3 months of submerged healing in a platform-switched internal-hex design; generalization to other connections is not addressed in the abstract.

## 7. Glossary

- **Biological width** — the supracrestal soft-tissue dimension that establishes around an implant, accompanied by marginal bone remodeling.
- **Equicrestal placement** — implant platform flush with the crestal bone.
- **Platform switching (PS)** — abutment narrower than the implant platform.
- **T0-T3** — radiographs at implant placement, restoration delivery, 6 and 12 months of loading.
