## Why Ingested

This SR+MA provides the highest-level synthesis of mouthwash efficacy against SARS-CoV-2 oral viral load, finding that only PVP-I is significantly effective in pooled analysis—while CHX and CPC are not—directly challenging the conclusions of individual RCTs and refining clinical recommendations. It supersedes individual positive trials for CHX (e.g., [[infection-control/elzein-2021-chlorhexidine-povidone-iodine-mouthwash-salivary-sars-cov-2-rct]]) and CPC at the population-evidence level, making it the decisive reference for evidence-based mouthwash selection in dental infection control.

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated the effects of mouthwashes on SARS-CoV-2 viral load reduction in the oral cavity, including 9 clinical trials (low risk of bias). Meta-analysis showed that 1% povidone-iodine mouthwash was effective (MD = 3.61, 95% CI: 1.03 to 6.19), while cetylpyridinium chloride (MD = 0.61, 95% CI: -1.03 to 2.25) and chlorhexidine gluconate (MD = -0.04, 95% CI: -1.20 to 1.12) were not effective against SARS-CoV-2.

PVP-I mouthwash may be recommended for reducing SARS-CoV-2 viral load in the oral cavity before and during dental procedures.

## 세줄요약

본 체계적문헌고찰 및 메타분석은 구강 내 SARS-CoV-2 바이러스 부하 감소에 대한 구강세정제의 효과를 9개의 임상시험(낮은 비뚤림 위험)을 통해 평가하였다. 메타분석 결과, 1% 포비돈-아이오딘(PVP-I) 구강세정제는 효과적이었고(MD = 3.61, 95% CI: 1.03 ~ 6.19), 세틸피리디늄 염화물(CPC, MD = 0.61, 95% CI: -1.03 ~ 2.25)과 클로르헥시딘 글루코네이트(CHX, MD = -0.04, 95% CI: -1.20 ~ 1.12)는 SARS-CoV-2에 효과적이지 않았다.

PVP-I 구강세정제는 치과 시술 전후 구강 내 SARS-CoV-2 바이러스 부하 감소를 위해 권장될 수 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- 1% povidone-iodine mouthwash significantly increases Ct value (MD = 3.61), indicating viral load reduction.
- CPC mouthwash shows no significant effect on SARS-CoV-2 viral load (MD = 0.61, p = 0.47).
- CHX mouthwash shows no significant effect on SARS-CoV-2 viral load (MD = -0.04, p = 0.95).
- PVP-I effect sustained at 5 min, 1h, 2h, 3h, and 6h after rinsing.
- PVP-I mouthwash recommended for pre-procedural use in dental settings during COVID-19 pandemic.
- All included studies had low risk of bias (modified Downs and Black score ≥5).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs, non-RCTs, and quasi-experimental studies.
- Databases: PubMed, EMBASE, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Central. Searched up to July 20, 2022. Grey literature searched (Google Scholar, MedRxiv, clinicaltrials.gov).
- PRISMA compliance: Yes (PRISMA 2020).
- Inclusion criteria: Patients diagnosed with COVID-19 (any age/gender). Mouthwash use as intervention. Comparator: no mouthwash. Outcomes: Ct value change or viral load change.
- Excluded: Reviews, letters, technical notes, in vitro, animal studies, conference papers, studies without Ct/viral load data.
- Included: 9 studies (all low risk of bias using modified Downs and Black checklist).
- Risk of bias: Modified Downs and Black checklist (score ≥5 = low risk). Scores: 5 studies score 6, 1 study score 7, 3 studies score 8.
- Data synthesis: Meta-analysis using RevMan 5.4.1. Random-effects model. Subgroup analysis by mouthwash type and time points.
- GRADE: Moderate certainty (downgraded for heterogeneity).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **PVP-I vs control (Ct value)** — MD = 3.61 (95% CI: 1.03 to 6.19, p = 0.006, I² = 0%)
  - 5 min subgroup: MD = 1.67 (95% CI: -7.86 to 11.20, p = 0.73)
  - 1h subgroup: MD = 4.69 (95% CI: -0.49 to 9.87, p = 0.08)
  - 2h subgroup: MD = 6.06 (95% CI: 0.88 to 11.24, p = 0.02)
  - 3h subgroup: MD = 2.47 (95% CI: -2.20 to 7.13, p = 0.30)
  - 6h subgroup: MD = 0.50 (95% CI: -6.84 to 7.84, p = 0.89)
- **CHX vs control (Ct value)** — MD = -0.04 (95% CI: -1.20 to 1.12, p = 0.95)
- **CPC vs control (Ct value)** — MD = 0.61 (95% CI: -1.03 to 2.25, p = 0.47)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample sizes in some included studies.
- Heterogeneity in mouthwash type, diagnostic kit, specimen type (saliva/nasopharynx/oropharynx), and time of testing.
- Limited number of clinical trials (only 9 studies) evaluating this topic.
- Publication bias assessment not reliable due to small number of included studies.
- Long-term effects of mouthwashes on viral load not evaluated.
- Need for more high-quality RCTs with standardized protocols.
- Only 5 studies were included in meta-analysis; 4 were descriptive only.

## 7. Glossary

- **Ct (Cycle Threshold)**: 주기 역치값 — RT-PCR에서 검출 신호가 나타나기까지의 증폭 주기 수. Ct값이 높을수록 바이러스 부하가 낮음
- **PVP-I (Povidone-Iodine)**: 포비돈-아이오딘 — 수용성 요오드포르, 광범위 항균 및 항바이러스 효과
- **SARS-CoV-2**: 중증급성호흡기증후군 코로나바이러스-2 — COVID-19의 원인 바이러스
- **CHX (Chlorhexidine)**: 클로르헥시딘 — 광범위 항균 구강세정제
- **CPC (Cetylpyridinium Chloride)**: 세틸피리디늄 염화물 — 양이온 계면활성제 항균제
- **ACE2 (Angiotensin-Converting Enzyme 2)**: 안지오텐신전환효소 2 — SARS-CoV-2가 숙주 세포에 결합하는 수용체
- **MD (Mean Difference)**: 평균 차이 — 두 군 간 평균값 차이
