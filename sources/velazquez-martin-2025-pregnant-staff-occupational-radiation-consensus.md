## Three-line Summary

Joint position paper (2025) of six Spanish interventional cardiology/radiology societies on pregnant workers exposed to ionizing radiation, reviewing fetal thresholds, published doses to pregnant interventionalists, EURATOM/Spanish law and protection measures.

Fetal tissue-reaction thresholds start at 50–100 mGy (pre-implantation) and ~200 mGy (malformations), while no published pregnant interventionalist exceeded the 1 mSv fetal limit — 50–100× below harmful doses; 1 mSv in gestation adds ~0.008% to the spontaneous 4% malformation and 0.07% childhood-cancer risk.

Pregnancy does not automatically exclude a worker (EURATOM Art. 10); the decision to continue should be the worker's own, informed and supported with a monthly abdominal dosimeter and 0.25 mm lead vest+skirt (overlapping 0.5 mm over the abdomen).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pregnancy ≠ automatic exclusion: EURATOM Art. 10 requires evaluating working conditions, not removing the worker.
- Fetal limit 1 mSv (ICRP/EURATOM) vs 5 mSv (NCRP) — the 1 mSv limit is the conservative one.
- No published pregnant interventionalist fetal dose >1 mSv; risk increment at 1 mSv ≈0.008%.
- Practical measures: monthly abdominal dosimeter (real-time if available), 0.25 mm lead apron with overlapping skirt, worker-led decision.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Expert consensus/position paper of six Spanish societies; narrative review of radiobiology, published occupational dose series in pregnant interventionalists, and European/Spanish regulation.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Pre-implantation (0–2 wk) threshold | 50–100 mGy (all-or-none) |
| Organogenesis malformation threshold | 200 mGy |
| Severe intellectual disability (8–15 wk) | 60–310 mGy |
| IQ loss | 20 points per 100 mGy |
| Fetal dose limit (ICRP/EURATOM) | 1 mSv for remainder of pregnancy |
| Fetal dose limit (NCRP) | 5 mSv |
| Pregnant interventionalists, published | All ≤1 mSv (most background) |
| Risk added by 1 mSv in gestation | ~0.008% over spontaneous (malformation 4%, childhood cancer 0.07%) |
| Spanish radiodiagnostic workers | Mean 0.6 mSv/y; 96% of monitored workers <1 mSv |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Interventional cath-lab context (higher scatter than dentistry); dose data from small surveys and observational series; few offspring-outcome studies; Spanish/EU legal framing.

## 7. Glossary

- Tissue reaction (deterministic effect) — threshold effect (pregnancy loss, malformation, microcephaly, intellectual disability)
- Stochastic effect — probability scales with dose, no threshold assumed (childhood cancer)
- mGy / µGy / nGy — absorbed dose (1 mGy = 1,000 µGy = 1,000,000 nGy); for X-rays 1 mGy ≈ 1 mSv
