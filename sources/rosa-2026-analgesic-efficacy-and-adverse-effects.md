## Why Ingested

From a 2026-11-09 surveillance triggered by a chairside question: a patient vomited after amoxicillin/clavulanate + loxoprofen + mosapride, and the wiki lacked evidence on nausea/vomiting with postoperative dental analgesics. This SR+MA quantifies nausea (18%, NNH 11) with paracetamol + codeine after third molar surgery, a relevant NSAID-alternative regimen. ## Three-line Summary

Meta-analytic systematic review of 6 RCTs (582 patients) of paracetamol + codeine versus placebo or other analgesics for postoperative pain after third molar surgery.

The combination significantly reduced pain and delayed rescue medication (OR=0.45, 95% CI 0.30-0.68), but adverse events were common: drowsiness 25% (NNH=7), nausea 18% (NNH=11), dizziness 12% (NNH=15).

It is effective especially when NSAIDs are contraindicated, but clinicians should anticipate sedation and gastrointestinal symptoms and counsel patients accordingly; only the abstract was available.

## 세줄요약

제3대구치 발치 후 통증에서 파라세타몰+코데인 병용을 위약 또는 다른 진통제와 비교한 무작위대조시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 6편(582명)의 메타분석 체계적 문헌고찰.

병용요법은 통증을 유의하게 줄이고 구제약 필요 시점을 늦췄으나(OR=0.45, 95% CI 0.30-0.68), 졸림 25%(NNH=7), 오심 18%(NNH=11), 어지럼 12%(NNH=15) 등 이상반응이 흔했다.

비스테로이드성 소염진통제 (NSAID) 금기 환자에게 특히 유효하나 진정·위장관 증상 위험을 고려해 환자 교육이 필요하며, 초록만 확보되어 세부 검증에는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Pools adverse events of paracetamol + codeine in the third molar model with NNH values
- Reports rescue-medication delay (OR=0.45, 95% CI 0.30-0.68)
- Positions the combination for patients contraindicated for NSAIDs

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis, random-effects model
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Library
- **n**: 6 RCTs, 582 patients
- **Outcomes**: Pain reduction, rescue medication use, adverse effects (OR, NNT/NNH)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Rescue medication / pain | OR=0.45 (95% CI 0.30-0.68) |
| Drowsiness | 25%, NNH=7 |
| Nausea | 18%, NNH=11 |
| Dizziness | 12%, NNH=15 |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only; risk of bias, heterogeneity, comparators, and doses are not reported in the available text
- Only 6 RCTs; pooled adverse-event rates are not stated per arm
