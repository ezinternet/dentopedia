## Why Ingested

Important **negative result** for CBCT periapical lesion characterization: grey density values cannot differentiate cystic from solid lesions (AUC 0.44 — chance level). Anchors the "what CBCT *cannot* do" axis alongside [[endodontics/diagnosis/pitcher-2017-cbct-periapical-cyst-decision-tree]], which shows that **volume** (not grey density) is the useful CBCT predictor. Together these two papers define the operative limits of CBCT for pre-surgical cyst screening.

→ [[endodontics/diagnosis/almadi-2020-cbct-grey-density-periapical-lesion]]

## Three-line Summary

abstract-only — full text not retrieved

Cross-sectional study (n=57 patients, biopsy gold standard, King Saud University) testing whether CBCT adjusted grey density values can distinguish periapical cystic from solid lesions: AUC 0.44 (P=0.45), accuracy 0.54, sensitivity 1.00, specificity 0.075 — not better than chance.

Reliability of grey density measurement itself was excellent (intra- and inter-examiner); the diagnostic failure is fundamental to the signal, not measurement noise — grey density simply does not encode the cyst/granuloma distinction.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly tests the clinical hypothesis that CBCT grey density differentiates cystic from solid periapical lesions — and definitively refutes it.
- AUC 0.44: statistically no better than random classification (95% CI includes 0.5; P=0.45).
- Measurement reliability is excellent — the negative result is not an artefact of poor technique.
- Eliminates grey density as a viable CBCT diagnostic criterion for cyst pre-operative screening.

## 3. Methodology

- n=57 patients with periapical lesions; retrievable small or medium FOV CBCT images + biopsy samples
- Gold standard: two oral and maxillofacial pathologists, independent histopathological diagnosis (cystic vs solid)
- Measurement: minimum adjusted grey density of each lesion, two independent examiners, duplicate readings
- Statistics: intra/inter-examiner ICC; ROC with AUC; sensitivity/specificity/accuracy at optimal cutoff
