## Why Ingested

조직수준 임플란트 (Tissue-level implant)에서 수직 점막 두께 (Vertical mucosal thickness)에 맞춰 식립 깊이를 조절하면 보철 전 치조정상방 조직 높이 (Supracrestal Tissue Height, STH) 형성기의 초기 변연골흡수와 처리 표면 노출이 어떻게 달라지는지를 3단계 깊이로 직접 비교한 전향적 연구다. 얇은 점막에서 골흡수가 크다는 → [[implants/mbl/linkevicius-2009-reaction-crestal-bone-around-implants]]의 결과를 조직수준 임플란트·깊이 조절 설계에서 재확인하고, STH와 식립 깊이를 함께 다룬 → [[implants/mbl/terzioglu-2024-effect-supracrestal-tissue-height-marginal]]의 범위를 조직수준 임플란트로 넓힌다.

## Three-line Summary

Multi-center prospective observational study (STROBE; NCT05363306), 49 patients / 49 tissue-level implants (3-mm machined transmucosal collar, 4.1 mm) placed in the posterior mandible at depth adapted to vertical mucosal thickness (thin <2.5 mm: collar 2 mm subcrestal, n=18; medium 2.5–3.5 mm: 1 mm subcrestal, n=16; thick >3.5 mm: equicrestal, n=15), radiographic follow-up to 5 months before prosthesis delivery.

Mean marginal bone resorption at 5 months (T2 ΔPBL) was 0.66 ± 0.49, 0.32 ± 0.41 and 0.22 ± 0.52 mm in the thin, medium and thick groups (ANOVA p = 0.025; at 3 months p = 0.184, not significant); bone loss exposing the treated implant surface occurred in 0/18, 1/16 and 8/15 implants (unexposed in 100%, 93.7% and 53.3%).

Thin mucosa still showed the most resorption despite deeper placement, but depth adaptation prevented early exposure of the rough implant surface; the 5-month, single-site (posterior mandible), 2D-radiograph design has no equicrestal thin-mucosa control and excludes the prosthetic phase.

## 세줄요약

다기관 전향적 관찰연구(STROBE, NCT05363306), 환자 49명·조직수준 임플란트 (Tissue-level implant) 49개(3 mm 기계가공 경점막부, 직경 4.1 mm), 하악 구치부, 수직 점막 두께에 맞춰 식립 깊이를 조절(얇음 <2.5 mm: 경점막부 2 mm 치조정하방 n=18; 중간 2.5–3.5 mm: 1 mm 하방 n=16; 두꺼움 >3.5 mm: 치조정 수준 n=15), 보철물 장착 전 5개월까지 방사선 추적.

5개월 평균 변연골흡수 (T2 ΔPBL)는 얇음·중간·두꺼움 군에서 각각 0.66 ± 0.49, 0.32 ± 0.41, 0.22 ± 0.52 mm (ANOVA p = 0.025; 3개월 시점은 p = 0.184로 비유의); 처리 표면을 노출시키는 골소실은 각각 0/18, 1/16, 8/15개(비노출 100%, 93.7%, 53.3%).

얇은 점막에서는 더 깊게 식립해도 골흡수가 가장 컸지만 깊이 조절이 거친 임플란트 표면의 조기 노출을 막았다; 5개월·하악 구치부 한정·2차원 방사선 측정이며 치조정 수준으로 식립한 얇은 점막 대조군이 없고 보철 단계는 제외되어 일반화에 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Prospective, three-depth comparison of tissue-level implants (3-mm machined transmucosal collar) placed 2 mm subcrestal / 1 mm subcrestal / equicrestal according to measured vertical mucosal thickness (<2.5 / 2.5–3.5 / >3.5 mm), evaluated before prosthesis delivery to exclude prosthetic confounders.
- Splits peri-implant bone level (PBL) change into **bone remodeling (BR)** (resorption around the 3-mm collar, implant partly sunk below crest) and **bone loss (BL)** (resorption exposing the rough implant body below the collar), following the Linkevicius distinction cited by the authors.
- Reports that depth adaptation avoided early exposure of the treated surface in 100% (thin), 93.7% (medium) and 53.3% (thick, equicrestal) of implants, and that most marginal changes occurred within 3 months of exposure to the oral environment.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Multi-center observational prospective study, six centers, STROBE; one implant per patient (most mesial implant evaluated if several).
- **n**: 54 patients enrolled; 4 group 2 patients missed follow-up and 1 group 3 patient was excluded for peak insertion torque >60 Ncm; **49 patients/49 implants analyzed** (25 men, 24 women; mean age 56.7 ± 11.8 years, range 34–85): group 1 n=18, group 2 n=16, group 3 n=15. No significant between-group difference in age, gender, smoking or periodontitis history (p > 0.05).
- **Implants**: i-Smart (i-Res) tissue-level, 3-mm convergent machined transmucosal component, moderately rough body, external hex; all 4.1 mm diameter, length 8 or 10 mm; one-stage unsubmerged protocol with healing cap; no removable prosthesis during healing; prosthetic rehabilitation after 5 months.
- **Groups (by vertical mucosal thickness measured after buccal flap reflection)**: group 1 thin <2.5 mm — treated part 2 mm below crest; group 2 medium 2.5–3.5 mm — 1 mm below crest; group 3 thick >3.5 mm — equicrestal.
- **Inclusion highlights**: partially edentulous posterior mandible, pristine healed bone (≥6 months), ≥6 mm crest width and ≥9 mm height above the mandibular canal, ≥3 mm keratinized mucosa width, no augmentation; FMPS ≤25%; peak insertion torque ≤60 Ncm.
- **Radiographs**: customized bite-jig long-cone paralleling periapicals at T0 (placement), T1 (3 months), T2 (5 months, before impression); PBL = distance from most coronal point of the implant platform to most coronal bone-to-implant contact (mesial and distal averaged); two calibrated examiners blind to mucosal thickness; Cohen's κ intra-/inter-examiner 87.2% / 81.6% (linear measurements within ±0.1 mm).
- **Statistics**: patient as unit; one-way ANOVA; simple linear regression with coincidence analysis of regression lines; α = 0.05; sample of 15 per group planned to detect a 0.3 mm (±0.25) MBL difference at 80% power.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Depth of placement was assigned by mucosal thickness, so thickness and apico-coronal position are fully confounded; there is no thin-mucosa group placed at equicrestal level and no non-adapted comparator, and the authors state that depth could affect bone remodeling irrespective of mucosal thickness.
- Only 5 months of healing; the prosthetic phase, abutment height and loading effects (which the authors say can strongly influence later bone change) are outside the study.
- Mucosal thickness was probed after flap reflection (soft tissue is deformable); authors state no evidence yet shows ultrasonic methods are superior.
- Posterior mandible, specific inclusion/exclusion criteria, single implant system, 2D radiographs (no buccal/lingual information), not randomized.
- Authors suggest thick-mucosa sites could also benefit from slight subcrestal positioning (53.3% exposure at equicrestal) and note subcrestal placement may not suit every implant–abutment connection.

## 7. Glossary

- **Supracrestal tissue height (STH)**: height of the soft-tissue barrier (epithelium + connective tissue attachment) coronal to the bone crest.
- **Tissue-level implant**: implant with a machined transmucosal collar (here 3 mm) so the implant–abutment interface lies coronal to bone, with no microgap at crest level.
- **Peri-implant bone level (PBL)**: radiographic distance between the most coronal implant-platform point and the most coronal bone-to-implant contact; an increase indicates resorption.
- **Bone remodeling (BR)**: resorption around the machined collar when the implant is partially sunk below the crest.
- **Bone loss (BL)**: resorption that exposes the treated (rough) implant body below the collar.
- **Early marginal bone loss (EMBL)**: non-infective crestal bone remodeling within the first year of function.
