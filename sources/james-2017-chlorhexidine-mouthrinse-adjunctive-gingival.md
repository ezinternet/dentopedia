## Why Ingested

Originally ingested from the PubMed abstract only; upgraded on 2026-10-11 to the full Cochrane text (194-page PDF) because the abstract-level page had overstated two points: that the 0.05%, 0.12% and 0.2% concentrations are all "effective" (only five trials tested 0.05%/0.06%, and their gingivitis effect was unclear) and that CHX is a well-supported gingivitis adjunct (the authors rate the GI reduction as not clinically relevant). Reinforces and qualifies [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/figuero-2019-adjunctive-antiplaque-chemical-agents-gingivitis]] and [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/dasilveira-2026-subgingival-irrigation-chemical-agents-nspt-sr-ma]] as the core evidence for chemical antiseptic adjuncts.

## Three-line Summary

Cochrane SR+MA of 51 parallel-group RCTs (5,345 participants analysed in the relevant arms; 6,995 across all arms) testing chlorhexidine (CHX) mouthrinse used for at least 4 weeks as an adjunct to mechanical oral hygiene, searched to 28 September 2016; 50 of 51 trials were at high risk of bias.

At 4 to 6 weeks CHX reduced plaque by SMD 1.45 (95% CI 1.00 to 1.90; 12 trials, 950 participants; high certainty) and the Gingival Index (GI, 0 to 3) by 0.21 (95% CI 0.11 to 0.31; 10 trials, 805 participants; high certainty) in people whose mean GI was about 1, a change the authors judge not clinically important, while extrinsic tooth staining rose (SMD 1.07, 95% CI 0.80 to 1.34; moderate certainty).

CHX is indicated for particular clinical situations for short periods only: no concentration or rinsing frequency was shown to be better, the effect in moderate to severe gingivitis (mean GI 1.1 to 3) is unknown, and staining, taste disturbance and mucosal effects must be weighed, especially for long-term use.

## 세줄요약

코크란 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA): 기계적 구강위생의 보조제로 4주 이상 사용한 클로르헥시딘 (Chlorhexidine, CHX) 가글을 평가한 평행군 무작위 대조 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 51편 (해당 군 5,345명, 전체 6,995명), 2016년 9월 28일까지 검색; 50편이 높은 비뚤림 위험 (risk of bias).

4~6주에서 치태 (plaque)는 SMD 1.45 감소 (95% CI 1.00~1.90; 12편 950명; 고확실성), 치은지수 (Gingival Index, GI, 0~3)는 0.21 감소 (95% CI 0.11~0.31; 10편 805명; 고확실성)였으나 평균 GI가 약 1인 경도 염증군이라 저자들은 임상적으로 의미 없다고 평가; 외인성 치아 착색 (extrinsic tooth staining)은 SMD 1.07 증가 (중등도 확실성).

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Plaque**: large reduction at 4 to 6 weeks (SMD 1.45) and at 6 months (high certainty). Roughly half a unit on the 0 to 3 Silness and Löe Plaque Index and three quarters of a unit on the 0 to 5 Turesky modification of the Quigley and Hein Index.
2. **Gingivitis**: GI reduced by 0.21 (95% CI 0.11 to 0.31) at 4 to 6 weeks and by about 0.20 at 6 months in people with mild inflammation (mean GI about 1); judged not clinically important. Gingival bleeding showed a moderate effect, equivalent to a 7% fall in the proportion of sites with bleeding at 4 to 6 weeks and 4% at 6 months.
3. **Evidence gap**: too few data to estimate the effect in moderate or severe gingivitis (mean GI 1.1 to 3).
4. **Concentration and frequency**: no evidence that one concentration is more effective; most trials rinsed twice daily so frequency could only be assessed for GI at 6 months (no difference). Only five trials used 0.05% or 0.06%; their plaque effect was clear but their gingivitis effect was not clear or consistent.
5. **Adverse effects**: extrinsic staining (moderate certainty, large effect); calculus inconclusive at 1 to 3 months and increased at 6 months; taste disturbance (11 trials), oral mucosal effects (13 trials), burning sensation or tongue (9 trials).

## 3. Methodology

- **Eligibility**: parallel-group RCTs of CHX mouthrinse used for at least 4 weeks in children and adults as an adjunct to mechanical oral hygiene (toothbrushing with or without floss or interdental aids, optionally professional cleaning), compared with mechanical hygiene alone, placebo or control rinse. Participants could have gingivitis, periodontitis, be healthy, or have medical conditions or special care needs.
- **Included**: 51 studies; 16 two-arm, 22 three-arm, 10 four-arm, 2 five-arm; 38 studies reported gingival inflammation, 34 gingival bleeding, 48 plaque.
- **Participants**: 6,995 overall, 5,345 in relevant arms (20 to 852 per study); 6 studies in children aged 8 to 16; adults 17 to 94 years; 8 studies included smokers (11% to 41%). Mean baseline GI across 21 studies about 1.09 (range 0.53 to 1.87).
- **Interventions**: 0.2% in 10 studies, 0.12% in 29, 0.1% in 2, 0.05% or 0.06% in 4; usually 10 to 15 mL twice daily for 30 to 60 seconds. Duration 4 to 6 weeks (15 studies) up to 24 months (1 study). Rinsing was unsupervised in 38 studies; 36 studies gave a baseline prophylaxis.
- **Analysis**: two reviewers screened, extracted and rated risk of bias independently; MD for the same scale, SMD for different scales, RR for dichotomous outcomes; random-effects models throughout; GRADE for the summary of findings.

- GI subgroup: 8 studies (466 participants) vs placebo or control rinse and 2 studies (339) vs no rinse; no difference between control types (P = 0.33). Control-group mean GI 0.93 (range 0.17 to 1.4).
- Risk of bias: 50 of 51 studies at high risk (blinding unlikely to hold because CHX stains and causes visible mucosal change; 42 studies high risk for performance or detection bias; 37 high risk of selective reporting). GRADE was not downgraded for risk of bias or heterogeneity for gingivitis and plaque because further research is judged very unlikely to change the estimate; staining was downgraded once.
- Funding: 19 trials funded directly by industry; only Zimmer 2015 stated the funder had no role in the report.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Most GI data come from people with low gingivitis (mean GI about 1), so the clinical relevance of a 0.21-unit change is unclear and the effect in moderate or severe gingivitis (GI 1.1 to 3) cannot be estimated. The 0 to 3 GI scale is not linear, and a low mean can mask a few severely inflamed sites.
- Many bleeding indices were used, preventing an estimate of bleeding reduction in severe inflammation.
- Almost all trials were at high risk of bias (inadequate blinding, poor reporting; 22 predated CONSORT 1996); heterogeneity was considerable for gingivitis.
- Rinse frequency could hardly be analysed (most trials used twice daily); only five trials tested 0.05% or 0.06% even though low-concentration products are marketed for long-term daily use. Trials of those products are needed.
- Authors changed some protocol criteria (types of studies and participants); only 13 trials gave a sample size calculation.
- Authors call for expert consensus on which gingivitis indices to use, and for evidence-based guidance on which patients should receive short-term CHX.

## 7. Glossary

- **GI (Gingival Index)**: Löe and Silness index, 0 to 3, higher is more inflamed.
- **SMD (standardised mean difference)**: effect expressed in standard deviations, used when trials report different scales.
- **CHX**: chlorhexidine (digluconate), a broad-spectrum bisbiguanide antiseptic; effect depends on substantivity, its retention on oral surfaces.
- **Extrinsic tooth staining**: brown surface discolouration that can be removed by professional scaling and polishing.
- **GRADE**: system grading certainty of evidence as high, moderate, low or very low.
