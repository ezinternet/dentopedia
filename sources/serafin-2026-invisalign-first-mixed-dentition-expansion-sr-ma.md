## Why Ingested

[[orthodontics/clear-aligner/bruni-2024-clear-aligner-maxillary-expansion-mixed-dentition]] provides prospective region-stratified accuracy data for maxillary Invisalign First expansion in 40 mixed-dentition children, but a pooled estimate across studies covering both arches was missing. This Angle Orthodontist SR+MA (Serafin 2026, 9 studies, PROSPERO CRD420251083929) is the first meta-analysis to report population-level predictability for both maxillary (65%) and mandibular (71%) expansion with Invisalign First in mixed dentition, and uniquely shows the absence of a dose-response between planned expansion amount and achieved predictability — a critical planning assumption to correct.

## One-line Summary

SR+MA (9 studies, 8 meta-analyzed, PROSPERO CRD420251083929) of Invisalign First for dental arch expansion in mixed dentition: pooled predictability 65% maxilla / 71% mandible, no significant association between planned expansion amount and achieved predictability; GRADE low (maxilla) / moderate (mandible).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to pool predictability for Invisalign First expansion in mixed dentition across both maxillary and mandibular arches
- Mandibular arch (71%, GRADE moderate) showed higher predictability than maxillary arch (65%, GRADE low)
- Tooth-level gradient: deciduous canines most predictable (mandible 75%), permanent first molars least (maxilla 58%)
- No dose-response: planned expansion amount had no significant association with achieved predictability in any subgroup — overcorrection ratios are not modified by target magnitude
- Sensitivity analysis confirmed robust findings; no evidence of publication bias (Egger's and Begg's tests; funnel plot)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: systematic review and meta-analysis, PRISMA guidelines, PROSPERO CRD420251083929
- Databases searched up to July 2025
- Eligibility: studies evaluating Invisalign First for dental arch expansion in mixed dentition
- Primary outcome: predictability = ratio of achieved to planned expansion (%)
- Meta-analysis method: proportions meta-analysis using binomial modeling on logit scale, performed separately for maxillary and mandibular arches
- Secondary: correlation analyses and univariate linear regression to assess planned expansion vs achieved predictability
- Risk of bias: ROBINS-I tool
- Publication bias: funnel plot, Egger's test, Begg's test
- Certainty of evidence: GRADE framework
- 9 studies in systematic review, 8 in meta-analysis

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Maxillary arch** (GRADE: low):
- Pooled predictability: **65%**
- By tooth: permanent first molars **58%** (lowest) → deciduous canines **70%** (highest)

**Mandibular arch** (GRADE: moderate):
- Pooled predictability: **71%**
- By tooth: deciduous canines **75%** (highest peak)

**Dose-response (correlation and regression)**:
- No significant association between amount of planned expansion and achieved predictability in any subgroup or arch

**Publication bias**: no evidence found; sensitivity analysis confirmed robustness

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- GRADE certainty only low to moderate — heterogeneity likely across included studies
- Only Invisalign First evaluated; no direct comparison with other CA systems or conventional expanders (e.g., RME)
- Abstract-only data extraction for some included studies limits subgroup depth
- Long-term retention and relapse outcomes after mixed dentition expansion not addressed
- Mandibular expansion literature is less developed; fewer included studies for that arch

## 6. Related Work

- Bruni 2024 (DOI 10.4041/kjod24.082): prospective n=40 Invisalign First, maxillary accuracy 80.2% overall, anterior→posterior gradient
- Fonseca Planells 2026 (DOI 10.1186/s40510-026-00620-7): SR+MA comparing CA vs conventional expanders in growing patients — CA produces significantly less transverse gain
- Gazzani 2026 (DOI 10.3390/children13020298): 2-year Invisalign First stability in mixed dentition without retainer — most expansion stable

## 7. Glossary

- **Predictability**: ratio of achieved to planned expansion (%), the primary outcome metric
- **Invisalign First**: Align Technology's phase-I clear aligner system designed for mixed dentition (6–10 y)
- **Logit scale**: log-odds transformation applied to proportions for binomial meta-analysis; back-transformed to % for reporting
- **ROBINS-I**: Risk Of Bias In Non-randomised Studies of Interventions (Cochrane risk-of-bias tool for observational studies)
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation — certainty framework (very low / low / moderate / high)
- **PROSPERO**: International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews (CRD420251083929)
