## Why Ingested

무열개 수술법 (Flapless Implant Surgery) 하에서 즉시 하중 (Immediate Loading, IL) vs 지연 하중 (Delayed Loading, DL)의 연조직 심미 결과를 비교한 초기 임상 연구로, 연조직 파라미터(PPI·WKM·ML)에 대한 하중 시기 효과의 근거를 제공한다. [[wiki/implants/loading-protocol/esposito-2013-loading-times-dental-implants-cochrane]]의 IL≈DL 결론을 전치부 flapless 맥락에서 보강하는 독립적 임상 증거.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective comparative clinical study (n=20, 10/group) in partially edentulous anterior maxilla using flapless implant surgery; immediate loading (single-piece, metal-ceramic crown day 14) vs delayed loading (two-piece, loaded at 4 months).
IL success 80% vs DL 90% (NS); mPI, mBI, ML, WKM showed no intergroup difference; PPI at Day 60 significantly higher in IL (mean 2.00 vs 1.00, p<0.05), with greater PPI change Day 60–180 in IL.
Flapless surgery enhances peri-implant esthetics regardless of loading protocol, but small n (10/group), short 6-month follow-up, and single-piece vs two-piece implant confounding limit generalizability.

## 세줄요약

전치부 부분 무치악 환자 20명을 대상으로 무열개 수술법 (Flapless Implant Surgery) 하에서 즉시 하중 (Immediate Loading, IL, n=10) vs 지연 하중 (Delayed Loading, DL, n=10)의 연조직 파라미터를 0·60·120·180일 시점에 비교한 전향적 비교 임상 연구.
성공률 IL 80% vs DL 90% (유의차 없음); 변연연조직 위치 (ML), 각화점막 폭 (Width of Keratinized Mucosa, WKM), 변형치태지수 (Modified Plaque Index, mPI), 변형출혈지수 (Modified Bleeding Index, mBI) 모두 그룹 간 차이 없음; Day 60 유두충전지수 (Papilla Presence Index, PPI)는 IL이 유의하게 높았으나 (p<0.05) 이후 DL이 더 빠르게 개선.
무열개 수술법은 즉시·지연 하중 모두에서 연조직 심미 향상에 효과적이나, 소표본·단기 추적·임플란트 종류 혼재라는 한계가 결론의 적용 범위를 제한함.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Indian Society of Periodontology, Vol 14, Issue 4, Oct–Dec 2010, pp. 245–251
- **DOI**: 10.4103/0972-124X.76930
- **Institution**: Department of Periodontology and Implantology, Meenakshi Ammal Dental College and Hospital, Chennai, India
- **Study type**: Prospective comparative clinical study
- **Submission / Accepted**: 11-11-2009 / 06-12-2010

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that flapless implant surgery produces comparable soft-tissue outcomes (mPI, mBI, ML, WKM) between IL and DL protocols at 180 days
- Shows PPI (papilla index) trajectory differs between groups: IL group recovers papilla earlier (higher PPI Day 60) while DL group has greater PPI gain from Day 60 to 180
- Supports flapless technique as esthetically viable regardless of loading timing in anterior maxilla
- Reports 6-month success rates: IL 80%, DL 90% with no statistically significant difference

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective, randomized-allocation comparative clinical study
- **Participants**: 20 partially edentulous patients, anterior maxillary region (premaxilla), natural teeth mesial and distal to edentulous space
- **Exclusions**: Uncontrolled diabetes, bleeding disorders, osteoporosis, radiotherapy, smoking, chronic periodontitis, bruxism
- **Group I (IL, n=10)**: Single-piece implants (LifeCare Devices), flapless placement, acrylic provisional day 0, metal-ceramic crown day 14
- **Group II (DL, n=10)**: Two-piece implants (LifeCare Devices), flapless placement, healing cap, permanent crowns at 4 months
- **Presurgical protocol**: Full-mouth scaling/root planing, OPG + IOPA, surgical stent
- **Outcomes**: mPI, mBI, PPI (Jemt index), ML (marginal level of soft tissue), WKM (Schiller's iodine staining)
- **Time points**: Day 0 (baseline), Day 60, Day 120, Day 180
- **Statistics**: Mann-Whitney U test (intergroup), Wilcoxon signed rank test (intragroup)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Group I (IL) | Group II (DL) | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Success rate | 80% (2 failures) | 90% (1 failure) | NS |
| mPI, mBI | No intergroup difference across all time points | — | NS |
| ML (marginal level) | No intergroup difference | — | NS |
| WKM | No intergroup difference | — | NS |
| PPI Day 60 | Mean 2.00 | Mean 1.00 | p<0.05 (significant) |
| PPI change Day 60→180 | Lower change | Greater change | Significant |

- IL group failures: attributed to poor bone quality assessment and suspected occlusal overload
- Both groups showed statistically significant improvement in WKM and PPI within groups (intragroup) over time

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample size (n=10/group) limits statistical power for secondary outcomes
- Short 6-month follow-up — long-term bone remodeling and esthetic stability unknown
- Single-piece (IL) vs two-piece (DL) implant design confounds the loading protocol comparison
- Non-split-mouth design; no blinding described
- Single institution, single operator; not a true RCT (no allocation concealment, blinding, or CONSORT compliance described despite "random" terminology)
- Unable to assess bone quality intraoperatively in flapless approach — may contribute to IL failures

## 7. Glossary

- **Flapless implant surgery (무열개 임플란트 수술)**: Implant placement without elevation of mucoperiosteal flaps; tissue punch only
- **PPI / Papilla Presence Index (유두충전지수)**: Jemt 0–4 scale measuring presence and completeness of interproximal papilla around implant restorations
- **WKM / Width of Keratinized Mucosa (각화점막 폭)**: Width of peri-implant keratinized tissue, measured with Schiller's potassium iodide staining
- **ML / Marginal Level of soft tissue (연조직 변연부)**: Distance of peri-implant mucosal margin from the reference line connecting adjacent teeth free gingival margins
- **mPI / Modified Plaque Index (변형치태지수)**: 0–3 scale; 0=no plaque, 3=abundant plaque
- **mBI / Modified Bleeding Index (변형출혈지수)**: 0–3 scale; 0=no bleeding, 3=heavy bleeding
