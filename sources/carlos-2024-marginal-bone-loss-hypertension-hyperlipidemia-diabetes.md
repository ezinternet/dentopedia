## Summary

Cross-sectional, record-based study (UNLV dental clinics, 2012–2022; 1,310 records screened → 57 patients/165 implants meeting criteria; predominantly elderly ≥65yr = 79%; IRB exempt). Evaluated MBL correlation with HTN, hyperlipidemia (HL), and diabetes mellitus (DM).

MBL prevalence: HTN 78.95% (p<0.001), HL 73.68% (p<0.001), DM 40.35% (NS, p=0.145). Combined HTN+HL: highest MBL incidence (29.82%, p=0.036). HTN medications and statins both statistically associated with MBL (p<0.001 each). Polypharmacy strongly correlated: ≥5 medications group had highest MBL burden.

**Critical interpretation note**: the MBL association with antihypertensive medications likely reflects polypharmacy confounding in multimorbid elderly patients — not a direct drug-mediated effect on bone loss. DM did not reach significance, which may reflect varying glycemic control in T2DM.

**Key limitations**: very small sample (n=57), cross-sectional design (cannot establish causation), record-based (dependent on documentation accuracy), no CBCT, single examiner, lack of MBL quantification.
