## Three-line Summary

Narrative short communication review (J Indian Soc Periodontol 2013) summarizing the concept, mechanism, and clinical rationale of platform switching for peri-implant bone preservation.

Platform switching displaces the microgap away from crestal bone and redistributes biomechanical stress circumferentially, reducing marginal bone resorption; clinical evidence up to 2013 consistently supported its use.

Platform switching is a simple, effective technique for long-term peri-implant bone maintenance; however, as a narrative review it does not provide quantitative pooled estimates.

## 세줄요약

플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching, PS)의 개념·기전·임상 근거를 개관한 서술적 단보 (J Indian Soc Periodontol 2013).

PS는 미세간극(microgap)을 치조정 골에서 멀리 이동시키고 응력을 분산시켜 변연골 흡수를 줄임; 2013년까지 임상 근거는 일관되게 PS의 골 보존 효과를 지지.

단순하고 효과적인 장기 변연골 유지 술식이나, 서술적 고찰로 정량적 메타분석 수치는 제공하지 않는 한계.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Platform switching: use of smaller-diameter abutment on larger implant collar
- Mechanism: displaces microgap away from crestal bone
- Effect: reduces circumferential bone resorption
- Clinical evidence to 2013: consistently supports PS for bone preservation
- Simple, effective technique for long-term peri-implant bone maintenance

## 7. Glossary
- **PS**: Platform switching — use of smaller-diameter abutment on larger implant collar
- **PM**: Platform matching — abutment matches implant platform diameter
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss — radiographic bone level change around implant
- **TL**: Tissue-level implant — transmucosal component integral to implant
- **BL**: Bone-level implant — implant platform at crestal bone level
