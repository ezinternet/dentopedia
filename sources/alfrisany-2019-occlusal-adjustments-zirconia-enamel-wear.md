## Three-line Summary

In-vitro wear study (J Adv Prosthodont 2019;11:162–8) testing 2 Y-TZP brands (Lava, Bruxzir) under 5 surface conditions (polished, ground, repolished, glazed, porcelain-veneered) against artificial enamel (steatite) antagonists over 500,000 mastication cycles at 80 N.

Polished zirconia caused significantly more steatite (enamel) wear (0.09 mm³) than glazed (0.02 mm³) or porcelain-veneered (0.02 mm³) surfaces; glazed zirconia roughness increased after simulation due to partial removal of the glaze layer.

Intraoral polishing did not adequately reduce roughness after grinding; material surface condition outweighs finishing technique — clinically, glazed or porcelain-veneered surfaces best protect the opposing enamel.

## 세줄요약

인공 에나멜(steatite) 대항치 대상 in vitro 마모 연구(J Adv Prosthodont 2019;11:162–8): Y-TZP 2종(Lava, Bruxzir) × 5가지 표면 조건(연마·삭제·재연마·유약·도재부착) × 80 N 하중 50만 사이클.

연마(polished) 지르코니아가 유약(glazed, 0.02 mm³)·도재부착(PVZ, 0.02 mm³)보다 steatite 마모(0.09 mm³) 유의하게 많음; 유약 지르코니아는 씹기 시뮬레이션 후 글레이즈층 일부 제거로 거칠기 증가.

구강 내 연마로 거칠기 충분히 감소 불가; 표면 조건이 마무리 기법보다 중요 — 임상적으로 유약 또는 도재부착 표면이 대합 에나멜 보호에 가장 유리.

## 1. Key Results
- 2 Y-TZP brands (Lava, Bruxzir); 5 conditions: CPZ (control polished), GRZ (ground), RPZ (repolished), GZ (glazed), PVZ (porcelain-veneered)
- 500,000 cycles; 80N; artificial enamel (steatite) antagonist
- No brand effect (p=0.216); no brand effect on steatite loss (p=0.064)
- GZ roughness INCREASED after chewing simulation (glaze layer partially removed)
- Steatite loss: CPZ 0.09mm³ > PVZ 0.02mm³ = GZ 0.02mm³
- Polished zirconia caused MORE wear on artificial enamel than glazed or porcelain-veneered
- Intraoral polishing system did not reduce roughness adequately
