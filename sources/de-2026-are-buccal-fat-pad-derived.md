## Why Ingested

위키에는 구강내 줄기세포 (Stem Cell) 기반 골재생의 **전임상 축**은 [[bone-regeneration/huang-2024-biomaterial-scaffolds-maxillofacial-bone-te]] (Bioactive Materials 2024, 서술적 리뷰 — scaffold 중심), [[periodontics/host-modulation/liu-2019-periodontal-bone-ligament-cementum-regeneration]] (SR, PDL·백악질·뼈 복합 재생)로 잡혀 있고, 자가골 **공여부** 선택 축은 [[overviews/autogenous-bone-graft-donor-site-selection-overview]] (7편 종합)로 정리돼 있으나 — **"뼈를 따지 않고 세포를 이식한다"는 축은 비어 있었다.** 본 SR은 협유지방垫 유래 줄기세포 (Buccal Fat Pad-Derived Stem Cells, BFPSCs)로 상악악안면부 (oro-maxillofacial) 골결손을 재생한 **인체 임상 연구만을** 대상으로 체계적으로 정리해, 그 공백을 메운다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA; Oxford CEBM grading, ROBINS-I) of in vivo human studies of buccal fat pad–derived stem cells (BFPSCs) for oro-maxillofacial bone defect regeneration in adults — PubMed/MEDLINE, EMBASE and Cochrane, January 2015 to September 2025.

Of 375 records screened, 6 clinical studies involving 49 patients (mean age 42.8 y, range 18–65; 29 male / 20 female) met the criteria — 30/49 (61.2%) BFPSC-treated versus 19/49 (38.8%) conventional graft controls; all 6 reported favorable clinical and radiologic outcomes at a mean 15-month follow-up (5–48 months), 4/6 added histology confirming new bone, and ROBINS-I judged 5/6 at serious risk of bias.

Clinical implication: BFPSC harvesting appears to cut postoperative pain, hospital stay and donor-site morbidity versus autologous bone harvest, but the underlying evidence is weak (Oxford CEBM 2b ×1, 3b ×3, 4 ×2) and the authors themselves call for standardized randomized trials before effectiveness can be confirmed.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR) — 성인 상악악안면부 (oro-maxillofacial) 골결손 재생에 협유지방垫 유래 줄기세포 (Buccal Fat Pad-Derived Stem Cells, BFPSCs)를 적용한 **인체 생체 내 (in vivo)** 임상 연구를 PubMed/MEDLINE·EMBASE·Cochrane에서 2015년 1월~2025년 9월까지 검색해 평가함 (PRISMA, 옥스포드 CEBM 근거등급, ROBINS-I 위험편향 도구 사용).

375건 중 최종 6편의 임상 연구, 총 **49명**(평균 나이 42.8세, 범위 18~65세, 남성 29명·여성 20명)이 포함됐고 — BFPSC군 30/49명(61.2%) 대 대조군 19/49명(38.8%); 6편 전부에서 임상적·방사학적 양호한 결과를 보고했고 평균 추적기간은 15개월(5~48개월)였으며, 4편은 조직학적 소견으로 신생골 (new bone) 형성을 확인함. ROBINS-I 평가에서는 6편 중 5편이 중대한 위험편향 (serious risk of bias)으로 판정됨.

임상적 함의 — BFPSC 채취는 자가골 (autologous bone) 채취(측방 ramus 피질판 (Lateral Ramus Cortical Plate, LRCP)·전장골능 (Anterior Iliac Crest, AIC)) 대비 수술 후 통증·입원 기간·공여부 이환율을 낮추는 것으로 보고됨. 그러나 근거등급이 낮고(옥스포드 CEBM 2b 1편, 3b 3편, 4 2편) 편향이 심해, 저자들은 효과 확정을 위해 표준화된 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)이 필요하다고 명시함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **First dedicated systematic review of BFPSC/BFP-DFAT cell therapy for in vivo human oro-maxillofacial bone defect regeneration** — the question is framed explicitly (PICOS) as "are buccal fat pad–derived stem cells effective in adults for oro-maxillofacial bone defect regeneration?"
- **Maps the entire published human evidence base**: 6 in vivo human studies, 49 patients, no more — the yield is the finding.
- **Grades each included study on the Oxford CEBM hierarchy** — 2b (1 study), 3b (3 studies), 4 (2 studies) — i.e. no study reaches level 1, and two sit at case-series level.
- **Applies ROBINS-I to the included set** and reports that 5/6 studies carry a serious risk of bias (1 moderate), so the authors explicitly warn the synthesis "should be interpreted with caution."
- **Isolates the patient-reported advantage of the cell route**: in the 2 studies that compared BFPSC-based harvesting against autologous bone harvesting (LRCP and AIC), BFPSC patients had less postoperative pain, shorter hospital stays and lower morbidity — the one signal that is consistent across the set.
- **Documents the characterization evidence** for the cells themselves: one study performed flow cytometry twice (cultured adipose-derived stem cells (ASCs) and again on a bone biopsy at 3 months post-surgery), with a consistent MSC-compatible surface-marker profile and lymphocyte-proliferation inhibition.
- **Enumerates the unresolved standardization questions** the authors themselves name: donor variability as a driver of cell quality, the need for long-term follow-up, and the need for standardized protocols.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review, narrative synthesis without meta-analysis.
- **Databases**: PubMed/MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Library. PubMed strategy combined MeSH and free-text terms: (("Stem Cells"[Mesh] OR "Mesenchymal Stem Cells"[Mesh] OR Stem Cell* OR "Mesenchymal Stem Cell*") AND ("buccal fat pad" OR "BFP" OR "BFPSCs" OR "Bichat's fat pad" OR "Buccal Fat Pad-Derived Stem Cells" OR "adipose stem cells")) AND ("Bone Regeneration"[Mesh] OR "Guided Tissue Regeneration, Periodontal"[Mesh] OR "Guided Tissue Regeneration"[Mesh] OR "Tissue Engineering"[Mesh] OR "Bone Regeneration" OR "Guided Tissue Regeneration, Periodontal"[tiab] OR "Guided Tissue Regeneration" OR "Tissue Engineering")). EMBASE and Cochrane used equivalent free-text keywords adapted to each database.
- **Search window**: January 2015 – September 2025. No automatic human/language filters at the search stage; human-only and English-only restrictions were applied at manual screening.
- **PICOS**: P = in vivo studies using BFPSCs/BFP-DFAT cells of human source for oro-maxillofacial bone defect; I = treatment of maxillofacial bone defect with BFPSCs; C = any treatment or no treatment; O = effectiveness; S = any study design.
- **Exclusions**: in vitro studies, non-human sources, cell-free BFP flap/mass applications, non-original articles (abstracts, guidelines, letters), non-English, not full text.
- **Screening**: two independent reviewers (P.D.F., I.G.) at title/abstract and full-text stages; disagreements resolved by discussion with a senior reviewer (P.V.). Abstracts with poorly defined results were deliberately carried into full-text assessment to avoid losing potential evidence.
- **Primary outcomes**: clinical (healing, absence of pain, infection, inflammation, wound dehiscence), radiologic (bone formation and graft integration on CBCT or orthopantomogram), histologic (microscopic regeneration on biopsy). **Secondary outcomes**: adverse effects, patient morbidity, hospital stay duration, patient discomfort.
- **Appraisal tools**: Oxford CEBM criteria for level of evidence; ROBINS-I for risk of bias.
- **PRISMA flow**: 375 identified → 360 after duplicate removal → 352 excluded at title/abstract → 8 full texts assessed → 2 excluded (full text not obtainable) → **6 included**.

### Background from cited literature (not the review's own data)

- **Lendeckel et al. (2004)** — first clinical use of ADSCs, reconstructing widespread calvarial defects in a 7-year-old girl; good new bone formation after 3 months.
- **Sándor GK et al.** — tissue-engineered construct of β-tricalcium phosphate (β-TCP) granules, BMP-2 and autologous ADSCs used to reconstruct a mandibular defect, restoring the chin's original anatomy.
- **Donor age effect (cited ref. 59)** — donor age negatively affects ADSC osteogenic potential; younger donors show higher osteogenic marker expression (OPN, OCL, BMP-2) and greater calcium deposition than elderly donors.
- **BFP anatomical rationale (cited refs. 28, 62, 64–66)** — the buccal fat pad is a specialized, richly vascularized adipose tissue; it is easily accessible with minimal post-harvest morbidity, its size is reportedly constant regardless of fat distribution, body weight or age, and it contains abundant neural-crest-derived stem cells with a faster proliferation rate and higher osteogenic and angiogenic surface-marker expression than stem cells from other adipose sources.

### Reporting discrepancies noted in the artifact (reproduced, not smoothed over)

1. **Title vs. scope**: the title says "for Maxillary Bone Regeneration," but the focused question, PICOS and abstract all specify **oro-maxillofacial** bone defects, and the included studies involve mandibular donor sites (LRCP, AIC). The title therefore over-narrows the actual review scope.
2. **ROBINS-I described against the wrong study type**: the risk-of-bias section reads "included diagnostic accuracy studies" — a leftover from another template. ROBINS-I is in fact the appropriate tool for non-randomized intervention studies, but the review also includes one randomized trial, to which ROBINS-I is not designed to apply (the Oxford CEBM grade 2b assignment is what identifies it).
3. **Internal inconsistency in the flow-cytology paragraph**: CD34 is listed among the "variable expression" markers and then reappears in the closing sentence among markers "exclusively expressed," alongside CD54 and CD49 — the two statements cannot both hold for the same marker.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Small, heterogeneous evidence base** — 6 studies, 49 patients, 1 randomized trial; Oxford CEBM levels 2b/3b/4 with two studies at case-series level.
- **Serious risk of bias in 5/6 studies** — the review's own ROBINS-I assessment, and its own instruction to interpret the results with caution.
- **No meta-analysis and no pooling** — heterogeneous interventions (BFPSC alone vs. BFPSC + autologous bone), heterogeneous comparators (LRCP vs. AIC autograft vs. xenograft + membrane), and heterogeneous outcome reporting prevented quantitative synthesis.
- **Small numbers per study** make the 61.2% / 38.8% treatment split descriptive only — no effect estimates, confidence intervals or p-values are reported anywhere in the review, so no comparative efficacy statement is possible from these data.
- **Short follow-up relative to regeneration biology** — mean 15 months (max 48); the authors call for long-term follow-up.
- **Donor variability is acknowledged but unquantified** — cell quality varies between donors, and the review does not report donor-level osteogenic characterisation beyond the single flow-cytometry study.
- **No standardized protocol** — the authors state that standardized trials that reduce the biases currently present are needed, and that further randomized controlled clinical trials with long-term follow-up are required to confirm the preliminary results and optimize clinical application.
- **Regulatory and manufacturing questions untouched** — cell culture, expansion, delivery timing and GMP-level processing are not addressed in the review; the six included studies are heterogeneous on these points.
- **Scope is oro-maxillofacial, not maxilla-specific** — the title's narrower framing should not be used to restrict the findings to the maxilla.

## 7. Glossary

- **BFPSC (Buccal Fat Pad-Derived Stem Cells)**: 협유지방垫 유래 줄기세포 — mesenchymal stem cells isolated from the buccal fat pad (Bichat's fat pad).
- **BFP (Buccal Fat Pad)**: 협유지방垫 — specialized adipose tissue of the cheek, richly vascularized, accessible intraorally, used clinically both as a cell source and as a cell-free pedicled flap.
- **BFP-DFAT**: Buccal Fat Pad-Derived Fat — the adipose tissue fraction from which BFPSCs are isolated.
- **ASC (Adipose-Derived Stem Cell)**: 지방유래줄기세포 — adipose-derived stromal/stem cell; the umbrella class to which BFPSCs belong.
- **MSC (Mesenchymal Stem Cell)**: 간엽줄기세포 — multipotent stromal cell able to differentiate into osteoblast, chondrocyte and adipocyte lineages.
- **ADSC**: adipose-derived stem cell — synonym used in the literature cited by this review.
- **Oxford CEBM levels of evidence**: 옥스포드 CEBM 근거등급 — hierarchy in which 2b is a small randomized trial and 4 is a case series.
- **ROBINS-I**: Risk Of Bias In Non-randomized Studies-of Interventions — 비무작위 중재 연구 위험편향 평가 도구.
- **LRCP (Lateral Ramus Cortical Plate)**: 측방 ramus 피질판 — intraoral autologous bone donor site used in combination with BFPSCs in several included studies.
- **AIC (Anterior Iliac Crest)**: 전장골능 — extraoral autologous bone donor site used as both combination partner and control comparator.
- **CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography)**: 도달광절편전산단층촬영 — the imaging modality used for radiologic bone-formation assessment in all included studies.
- **β-TCP (β-Tricalcium Phosphate)**: β-삼인산칼슘 — a resorbable calcium-phosphate scaffold used with ADSCs and BMP-2 in the earliest clinical ADSC work cited by this review.
- **CD markers / HLA**: cluster-of-differentiation surface markers (CD29, CD34, CD90, CD105, CD166 …) and human leukocyte antigen (HLA-ABC, HLA-DR) used to characterize the isolated ASC population.
- **BMP-2 (Bone Morphogenetic Protein-2)**: 골형성단백질-2 — osteoinductive growth factor, used with ADSCs in the mandibular reconstruction case cited by this review.
- **OPN / OCL**: osteopontin / osteocalcin — osteogenic differentiation markers whose expression is reported to decline with donor age.
