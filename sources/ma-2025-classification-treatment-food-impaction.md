## Why Ingested
사용자가 식편압입(food impaction) 분류 주제의 PubMed 인제스트를 시작. 기존 food-impaction 카테고리는 전부 PCL/ICL의 유병률·위험인자에 집중했고([[food-impaction/liu-2025-risk-factors-proximal-contact-loss-implant-prosthetic]]), 식편압입 자체의 **분류 체계(etiology/direction/extent/contact-relationship)와 치료 전략을 직접 정리한 페이지가 없었는데**, 본 논문은 이 카테고리에서 식편압입 분류만을 다루는 유일한 서술적 리뷰로 그 공백을 채운다.

## Three-line Summary
Narrative review (92 references, no patient data) of the classification and treatment of food impaction, organizing the field into four classification bases (etiology, direction, extent, contact relationship) and two treatment tracks (vertical vs horizontal) plus a dedicated implant section.
Cites implant-neighbor food impaction in >40% of patients (Wong et al.) and up to 66.9% after single-crown implant repairs, horizontal impaction at 9.7% of cases, Newell's occlusal-adjustment technique with 80% symptomatic improvement, and recommends 3–6-month recall after each recurrent-impaction repair.
Provides the classification-and-treatment taxonomy the PCL-focused literature lacks, but as a narrative review it reports no pooled data; its own conclusions flag insufficient personalized planning and a lack of longitudinal evidence as the field's main gaps.

## 세줄요약
식편압입 분류·치료에 관한 서술적 리뷰(참고문헌 92편, 원자료 없음) — 분류 4축(원인·방향·범위·접촉관계)과 수직형/수평형 두 치료 트랙 및 임플란트 특이 섹션으로 정리.
인용 수치: 임플란트 인접 식편압입 환자 >40%(Wong), 단일 크라운 수복 후 최대 66.9%, 수평형 식편압입 9.7%, Newell 교합조정 80% 증상 개선, 재발 식편압입 수리 후 3–6개월 재검 권고.
PCL 중심의 기존 문헌에 분류·치료 체계를 더하는 교과서형 리뷰이나, pooled data가 없고 보고된 한계로는 개인맞춤 치료 부족·장기추적 부재가 지적된다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- **Five-category historical classification synthesized**: Hirschfeld's etiology-based system (loss of proximal contact, occlusal interference, malposition of teeth, morphological abnormality, periodontal breakdown) and its clinical impracticality; simplified Chinese direction-based vertical/horizontal/mixed scheme; Zheng Dize's extent-based scheme (extensive ≥3 quadrants / partial 1–2 quadrants / limited 2–4 teeth); Fang Bisong's static/dynamic/mixed contact-relationship scheme with its two dynamic-pathogenesis mechanisms (irreversible mesial displacement misalignment; absent food spillage pathways).
- **Vertical-treatment taxonomy with 5 treatment directions** (restoring interproximal contact incl. the new MDA crown; occlusal adjustment; interproximal-zone adjustment; tooth/restoration form correction; adjacent/opposing-tooth adjustment).
- **Horizontal-treatment taxonomy with 4 directions** (periodontal treatment, gingival papilla reconstruction, tooth-loss restoration, oral/lifestyle habits).
- **Implant-specific prevention section**: screw-retention to allow porcelain addition, broad-flat abutment contact near the gingiva, immediate implantation for papilla preservation, and 3–6-month recall because recurrence accelerates after each repair.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Narrative review (no systematic search protocol, no pooled analysis; 92 cited references including epidemiologic surveys and clinical reports)
- **Databases**: n/a (non-systematic literature narrative)
- **n**: No patient data — review-level synthesis; cites external epidemiological figures (e.g. China 4th national survey: >70% of 55–74-year-olds with bone/attachment loss >4 mm; Wong et al. >40% post-implant food impaction; 66.9% after single-crown repairs; 17.5% of unsuccessful fixed-denture repairs; 45% 5-year periodontal-disease incidence in recurrent-impaction patients; 9.7% horizontal-type share)
- **Outcomes**: Classification framing (4 bases) and treatment algorithms (vertical 5 directions, horizontal 4 directions, implant prevention, iatrogenic-factor management) — no quantitative efficacy outcomes

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Epidemiology cited**: food impaction in >40% of post-implant patients (Wong ref 6); up to **66.9%** after single-crown implant repairs; **17.5%** of unsuccessful fixed-denture restorations; **45%** 5-year periodontal-disease incidence with recurrent impaction; horizontal type **9.7%** of cases (Peng 2005).
- **Newell et al. occlusal adjustment** (grinding spillways to buccal/lingual, adjusting marginal-ridge/opposing-cusp sharpness): **80%** symptomatic improvement.
- **Quantitative occlusal threshold**: when the interproximal gap exceeds **0.06 mm**, the articulator-paper contact area becomes excessive and deep-colored areas should be ground.
- **Implant monitoring**: recurrence after each repair comes faster; regular follow-up **every 3–6 months** recommended.
- **Dynamic impaction mechanisms** (contact-relationship classification): (1) lack of reversible mesial displacement of distal teeth during occlusion → long-term irreversible mesial displacement misaligned with neighbors; (2) absence of proper food spillage pathways.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Narrative review with heterogeneous, partly Chinese-language primary sources; no systematic search, no quantitative pooling of treatment efficacy, and short-term outcome focus throughout the cited literature.
- Self-flagged gaps: insufficient individualized/personalized treatment planning; "universal approach" failure; recurrent impaction in persistent cases without clear predictive assessment; lack of longitudinal studies; inconsistent multidisciplinary collaboration.
- Psychological/social burden (anxiety around eating, social interactions) largely neglected in clinical assessment.
- Future direction: digital-dentistry tools (3Shape intraoral scanning occlusal analysis, healing-abutment scanning bodies, mandibular-motion simulation, digital facebow) proposed for diagnosis and personalized planning, but validation limited to small case reports/lab studies.

## 7. Glossary
- **식편압입 (Food impaction)**: food becoming trapped in interproximal spaces or near restored teeth during mastication, leading to discomfort, periodontal disorders, and tooth wear.
- **근접접촉 소실 (Proximal Contact Loss, PCL)**: loss of contact between adjacent teeth or an implant-supported prosthesis and adjacent natural tooth — cited here as the leading cause of vertical food impaction.
- **MDA (Mesiodistal Adjustable) crown**: crown allowing mesiodistal contact re-establishment without full prosthesis replacement.
- **Dynamic food impaction**: impaction where contact appears normal at rest but separates during mastication, via mesial-displacement misalignment or absent food spillage pathways.
- **교합조정 (Occlusal adjustment)**: reshaping occlusal surfaces, marginal ridges, cusps, and food-overflow grooves to redirect food flow and relieve impaction.
