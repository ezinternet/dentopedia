## Why Ingested

This study introduces a novel thermoelectric dry cooling (DC) system based on a Peltier module as an alternative to saline irrigation for controlling heat during dental implant osteotomy — the first in vitro evaluation of this concept. It directly expands the evidence base covered in [[overviews/osteotomy-drilling-heat-determinants-irrigation-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

An in vitro RCT (n=120, bovine rib) compared a closed-loop Peltier-based dry cooling (DC) device against external saline cooling (SC) at two hand pressure levels. DC at low pressure (DC-LP) yielded the lowest temperature rise (ΔT 2.00 ± 0.84 °C, final 23.41 °C) while SC at high pressure (SC-HP) yielded the highest (ΔT 5.45 ± 0.90 °C, final 26.38 °C); all groups remained well below the 47 °C necrosis threshold. DC groups produced significantly more bone particles (28.53 vs 10.35 mg, p<0.001), as the absence of irrigation fluid preserves the autogenous bone chip yield.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **First in vitro evaluation of a Peltier-based dry cooling device** for implant osteotomy — novel closed-loop thermoelectric system with no saline irrigation.
2. DC demonstrated superior thermal control over SC at high hand pressure, particularly relevant for high-cortical-density sites.
3. DC preserved significantly more autogenous bone particles (×2.75 more than SC), benefiting autograft availability.
4. Establishes that reduced saline irrigation (dry approach) does not compromise thermal safety.
5. Identifies an inverse relationship between drilling time and temperature increase (r = −0.472).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

### Study Design
- **Model**: Fresh bovine rib blocks (2 bovines aged 24 months); standardized per Strbac et al. 2014 criteria (cortical bone 1.5–2 mm)
- **Groups** (n=30 each): A = DC-LP, B = DC-HP, C = SC-LP, D = SC-HP
- **Osteotomy**: Sequential protocol, final drill Ø4.1 mm × 10 mm depth; 800 rpm; Nucleoss burs
- **Pressures**: Low = 10 N; High = 20 N (computerized automatic test stand)
- **Randomization**: Python 3.7 random permutation; closed envelope; blinded coding

### Cooling Systems
- **DC (Dry Cooling)**: 5-component Peltier closed-loop system: power supply → Peltier cooler → miniature pump → flexible pipes → cooler insert adjacent to drill entry. Tip temperature: −11.5 ± 1 °C relative to ambient (i.e., ~9.5 °C at 21 °C ambient)
- **SC (Saline Cooling)**: External irrigation at 50 ml/min, 21 °C; replaced when temperature rose >1 °C

### Temperature Measurement
- Two K-type thermoprobes (0.4 mm diameter) placed 0.9–1.1 mm from the 3.5 mm osteotomy centerline, at 5 mm depth; positioned after 3.5 mm osteotomy, measuring final 4.1 mm drill step
- Heat transfer paste injected into probe channels; silicone sealing

### Outcome Variables
- **Primary**: ΔT = T_final − T_initial; maximum temperature at final drill
- **Secondary**: Bone particle mass (mg); drilling time (s)

### Statistics
- ANOVA + Tukey post hoc; t-test for pairwise; Pearson correlation; α = 0.05; G*Power-derived n=120

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Temperature Outcomes

| Group | Cooling | Pressure | ΔT (°C) | Final T (°C) |
|-------|---------|----------|---------|-------------|
| A (DC-LP) | Dry (Peltier) | 10 N | **2.00 ± 0.84** | **23.41 ± 1.23** |
| B (DC-HP) | Dry (Peltier) | 20 N | — | 24.00 ± 0.95 |
| C (SC-LP) | Saline | 10 N | 3.45 ± 0.88 | 24.42 ± 1.19 |
| D (SC-HP) | Saline | 20 N | **5.45 ± 0.90** | **26.38 ± 1.03** |

- All groups well below 47 °C necrosis threshold
- Initial temperature: 20.95 ± 0.57 °C (no between-group difference, p=0.987)
- Group A significantly lower ΔT than all other groups (post hoc, p<0.001)
- Group D significantly higher ΔT than Groups B and C

### Bone Particle Collection

| Group type | Mean bone particles |
|-----------|-------------------|
| DC groups (A+B) | **28.53 ± 2.46 mg** |
| SC groups (C+D) | **10.35 ± 2.64 mg** |
| p-value | <0.001 |

### Drilling Time

| Group | Mean drilling time |
|-------|--------------------|
| B (DC-HP) | **21.13 ± 0.78 s** (shortest) |
| C (SC-LP) | **25.30 ± 0.89 s** (longest) |
| Overall | 23.24 ± 2.14 s |

### Correlations
- Drilling time vs ΔT: r = −0.472 (p<0.001) — longer drilling → less ΔT
- Drilling time vs T_final: r = −0.438 (p<0.001)
- T_final vs ΔT: r = 0.927 (p<0.001)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

**Limitations**:
- Bovine rib bone may not simulate in vivo biological conditions (no vascular perfusion, healing response)
- Measurements only during the final (4.1 mm) drill; cumulative heat from earlier drills not directly captured
- Constant 800 rpm — lower speeds may diminish cooling method differences
- Anisotropic nature of bone; two thermocouples may introduce variability

**Future Work Needed**:
- Varying bur diameters and lengths
- Different drilling speeds
- Animal (in vivo) studies to assess osseointegration outcomes
- Optimization of DC tip temperature and flow rate parameters
- Investigation of DC in low-density (Type IV) bone

## 6. Related Work

- Saline irrigation remains the clinical standard; multiple studies confirm temperature reduction vs. non-irrigated conditions
- Low-speed drilling (50–300 rpm) without irrigation proposed as alternative; however, prolongs procedure time significantly
- Internal gas cooling (CO₂, N₂) showed similar ~43 °C ceiling but requires specialized drills and compressed gas supply
- Previous study by same group (referenced as prior work) first described the DC concept
- Strbac et al. 2014 bovine rib standardization methodology followed
- Li et al.: bone particle yield with 4.0 mm × 10 mm drill: 58.45 ± 7.68 mg (no irrigation) vs. 8.70 ± 4.09 mg (with irrigation) — consistent with DC vs. SC findings

## 7. Glossary

- **DC (Dry Cooling / 건식 냉각)**: Peltier-based closed-loop thermoelectric cooling applied without saline irrigation
- **SC (Saline Cooling / 식염수 냉각)**: Conventional external saline irrigation at 50 ml/min
- **Peltier effect (펠티에 효과)**: Thermoelectric phenomenon where electric current across n/p semiconductor junction causes one face to cool and other to heat
- **ΔT (온도 차이)**: T_final − T_initial; primary outcome measure
- **Thermal necrosis threshold (열괴사 역치)**: 47 °C; bone damage begins above this threshold with >1 min exposure
- **Bone thermal diffusivity (골 열확산율)**: ~0.14 mm²/s for cortical bone; low value means heat accumulates locally
- **DC-LP / DC-HP**: Dry Cooling Low Pressure / High Pressure (10 N / 20 N)
- **SC-LP / SC-HP**: Saline Cooling Low Pressure / High Pressure
