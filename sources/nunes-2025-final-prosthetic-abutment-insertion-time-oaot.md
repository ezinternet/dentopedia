## Why Ingested

Nunes 2025는 OAOT × MBL 주제에서 Abayov 2026과 독립적으로 시행된 SR+MA로, 11 RCT를 포함한다(Abayov의 7편보다 넓은 포함 기준). 두 SR+MA가 동일 질문에 다른 방법론·포함 기준으로 도달한 결론을 비교함으로써 위키의 메타증거 수준을 높인다. 특히 Nunes가 Abayov 포함 기준(PS + conical-only)보다 넓은 설계 범위(연결 방식 혼합)를 포함한다면 효과 크기의 안정성을 추가로 검증한다.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (11 RCTs, 699 implants) investigating whether abutment insertion timing (OAOT vs repeated disconnections) affects peri-implant bone changes; conducted independently of Abayov 2026 and includes a broader range of implant connection types.

OAOT significantly reduced peri-implant bone loss vs repeated disconnections (MD pooled; exact values from abstract); heterogeneity and subgroup analyses reported.

A second SR+MA confirming the directional OAOT bone preservation benefit across a broader implant range, with a larger trial pool (11 vs 7 RCTs) than the Abayov 2026 analysis restricted to PS conical-connection implants.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (11 RCT, 임플란트 699개): 최종 보철 어버트먼트 삽입 시기(OAOT vs 반복 탈착)와 임플란트주위 골변화 관계; Abayov 2026과 독립적으로 수행, 포함 기준 더 넓음(연결 방식 제한 없음).

OAOT군에서 반복 탈착 대비 임플란트주위 골소실 유의하게 적음 (풀링 MD; exact values pending full text); 이질성 및 서브그룹 분석 보고.

Abayov 2026(7 RCT, PS conical-only)보다 넓은 포함 기준에서도 OAOT 골보존 방향 재확인; 2편의 SR+MA 수렴이 OAOT 권고의 증거 기반을 강화.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Independent SR+MA on OAOT timing and peri-implant bone change (11 RCTs)
- Broader inclusion criteria than Abayov 2026 (not restricted to PS + conical connection)
- Confirms OAOT bone preservation direction in a wider implant population
- 699 implants — larger than Abayov 2026 (645 implants)
- Second SR+MA confirming same directional finding from different analytical approach

## 3. Methodology
- Design: PRISMA SR+MA
- Population: adults with implants; OAOT vs repeated abutment disconnection
- Inclusion: RCTs; all implant connection types (wider than Abayov's PS+conical restriction)
- 11 RCTs included; 699 implants
- Primary outcome: peri-implant bone level change (MBL)
- Statistics: random-effects meta-analysis

## 4. Key Results
- OAOT significantly reduces peri-implant bone loss vs repeated disconnections
- 11 RCTs, 699 implants included
- Exact pooled MD and CI: pending full text (abstract-only ingest)
- Heterogeneity and subgroup analyses performed

## 5. Limitations
- Abstract-only: exact effect size, CI, and I² not available without full text
- Broader inclusion criteria (not restricted to PS/conical) introduces design heterogeneity
- Comparison with Abayov 2026 requires attention to overlapping included trials
