## Why Ingested

박스의 surgical-axis 공백을 정확히 메우는 논문. [[wiki/overviews/implant-failure-mechanism-xray-differential-overview]]는 세 실패 기전(드릴링 과열 / 골 과압박 / 임플란트주위염)의 방사선 감별이 **불가능**하며 "가르는 유일한 근거는 수술 기록"이라 결론내렸지만, 그 근거를 실제 환자 1례에 적용해 과열을 배제하고 과압박을 지목한 사례보고가 위키에 없었다. 이 논문은 copious saline 관주(수술 기록)로 과열을 배제하고 D2 치밀골·무프리태핑·언더사이징 드릴링(수술 기록)으로 과압박을 지목하며, 재수술 시 채취한 조직에서 **무염증(aseptic) 골괴사**를 조직학적으로 확인한다 — 위키 overview가 주장한 감별 방식이 실제로 작동하는 유일한 사례.

동시에 [[wiki/overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]가 가진 16개 source paper 중 **수술적 원인(과열·과압박·토크·프리태핑) 축이 0편**이었다 — 전부 숙주·습관·부위·로딩 프로토콜 변수였다. 이 논문은 그 overview에 처음 들어가는 수술측 EIF 근거이자 예방 목록(pre-tapping, 제조사 권장 토크 상한, 1/4회전 역회전)의 출처다.

## Three-line Summary

Case report (n=1) of an early implant failure 6 weeks after placement in the mandibular left posterior of a 33-year-old non-smoking, non-diabetic female, with the bone taken at re-entry sent for histopathology.

An undersized osteotomy (3.2 mm final drill for a 3.5 mm implant) with no pre-tapping and 35–50 Ncm insertion torque in presumed D2-D3 bone produced histology-confirmed aseptic bone necrosis — bony trabeculae with absent osteoblastic riming, no viable bone anywhere in the specimen, and osteocyte-empty lacunae — after 50% bone loss to half the implant length.

Heat was excluded on the surgical record (copious saline irrigation) and infection/systemic causes on clinical and laboratory grounds (no inflammatory signs, normal CBC/ESR/viral markers), leaving compression necrosis as the presumptive cause; n=1 with no measured temperature, torque-at-placement instrument, or control makes the causal attribution a hypothesis, and the authors' own timeline claim (failure "within one month") conflicts with the case's 6-week failure.

## 세줄요약

증례보고 (n=1) — 33세 비흡연·비당뇨 여성의 하악 좌측 구치부 임플란트가 식립 6주째 조기 실패. 실패 임플란트와 함께 재수술 시 채취한 골을 조직학적으로 검사.

언더사이징 드릴링(3.5D 임플란트에 최종 3.2 mm 드릴) + 프리태핑 없음 + 35–50 Ncm 삽입토크로 D2-D3 골에 식립 → 6주 IOPAR에서 임플란트 길이의 절반까지 골소실(50%), 동요. 조직은 골성소주 + 골아세포 테두리 부재 + 생존골 전무 + 골세포 없는 소공 → **무염증(aseptic) 골괴사** 확인.

과열은 수술 기록(생리식염수 관주)으로, 감염·전신 원인은 임상(염증 소견 없음)·혈액검사(CBC/ESR/바이러스 표지 전부 정상)로 배제 → 과압박 괴사가 유력. 단 n=1이고 온도·토크 실측이 없어 인과는 가설이며, 논문 자체도 "식립 1개월 이내 실패"라고 기술한 것과 실제 6주 실패가 어긋남.

## Key Contributions

- **Human histology of aseptic peri-implant bone necrosis in the early-failure window.** Biopsy of the re-entered site yielded a single 0.3 × 0.15 × 0.05 cm non-viable bony sequestrum: bony trabeculae with absent osteoblastic riming, no viable bone throughout, lacunae devoid of osteocytes. This is the second human biopsy series in the wiki documenting overcompression-related peri-implant bone necrosis, and the first with the full surgical record attached.
- **An explicit heat-vs-compression differential worked on the surgical record.** Copious saline irrigation is stated as the reason overheating is excluded; dense crestal bone, absent pre-tapping, and the undersized osteotomy are stated as the reason compression is favoured. This is the first held paper that *operationalises* the surgical-record discriminator the wiki's differential overview asserts exists.
- **A prevention list with concrete surgical levers**, attributed to the source's review: precise surgical technique; avoid torque beyond the manufacturer's recommendation; reverse the implant a quarter turn after insertion; pre-tapping into dense bone prior to placement.
- **Failure-rate context for the bone-quality axis**: implants in type III/IV (Lekholm & Zarb) bone reportedly fail at 4.27–8.06% versus type I/II.
- **Thermal thresholds restated with an operational counter-example**: necrosis at 47 °C for ≥1 min, but Chacon et al. found twist drills designed with a relief angle stayed below 47 °C even at 15 mm depth after 25 uses — i.e. drill design, not use count alone, governs the crossing.

## Methodology

Single case report with literature narrative review; no protocol, no registration, no comparator.

- **Patient**: 33-year-old female, non-smoker, non-diabetic, no relevant medical history, written informed consent. Presented 2023-04-10 for a missing tooth in the mandibular left posterior region; a successful implant from 1 year earlier in the right posterior region served as an intra-patient control for the risk factors examined.
- **Site assessment**: pre-operative CBCT — mesiodistal 11.03 mm, buccolingual 4.2 mm, apicocoronal 12.81 mm.
- **Preparation**: two-stage plan; bone quality presumed D2-D3 → **undersized drilling**; sequential drilling at 800–1200 rpm, 35–50 Ncm, starting with a 2 mm pilot drill; osteotomy enlarged to a **3.2 mm final drill** for a **3.5D × 11.5 mm** implant; inserted manually with a ratchet torque wrench at **35–50 Ncm** until primary stability; **no pre-tapping**; passive primary closure with 3-0 silk.
- **Follow-up**: sutures out at 1 week (uneventful); recall at 3 weeks with intermittent dull pain, no clinical inflammation or infection; symptomatic return at 6 weeks with IOPAR.
- **Intervention at failure**: amoxicillin 500 mg TID + metronidazole 250 mg TID for 5 days, then re-entry on day 5 under consent; biopsy taken; implant mobile with 50% bone loss relative to implant length; removed with a fixture removal kit; specimen fixed in 10% formalin.
- **Histology**: serial sections of H&E-stained decalcified hard tissue.
- **Systemic workup**: blood examination at 4 weeks post-placement (Table 1) — Hb 13.8 g/dL, TLC 10,000/cumm, DLC (N 69 / E 3 / B 0 / M 2 / L 28 %), ESR 5 mm/h, RBC 4.8 million/µL, platelets 296,000/µL, bleeding time 1 min 30 s, clotting time 3 min 36 s, RBS 113 mg%, retroviral rapid test negative, HBsAg negative, HCV negative — **every value inside its reference interval**.

## Limitations

- **n = 1.** No denominator, no incidence, no comparison group; the causal attribution to compression is a reasoned exclusion of alternatives, not a demonstrated cause.
- **No measurement of the two competing mechanisms.** Drilling temperature was never recorded, so heat was excluded by reported practice (copious irrigation) rather than by data; insertion strain, osteotomy-to-implant misfit magnitude, and achieved peak torque were not instrumented — the "35–50 Ncm" figure is a range the operator applied by hand with a ratchet, not a recorded peak.
- **No imaging corroboration of the mechanism.** The 6-week IOPAR shows a severe angular defect, which is the generic picture of early non-integration; nothing on the film attributes it to compression.
- **Bone quality was presumed, not measured.** "D2-D3" in the case management and "D2" in the discussion differ; no periapical radiograph-based density estimate (e.g. Schwarz ratio) or intraoperative bone-quality assessment is reported.
- **Histology is descriptive, not comparative.** Absence of osteoblastic riming plus empty lacunae establishes necrosis; it does not distinguish compression necrosis from thermal necrosis histologically, and the paper does not report bacterial colonisation status of the sequestrum or implant surface, which is part of the thermal-failure fingerprint.
- **Small biopsy.** One 0.3 cm fragment from a single site cannot characterise the lesion around the whole implant.
- **Review is narrative.** 48 references, no search strategy, no risk-of-bias assessment; cited thresholds and failure rates are second-hand.
- **Internal inconsistencies in the paper itself** (see `logs/ingest-deviations.md`, `source-data-issue` row): the discussion states compression necrosis "usually occurs … (within one month after implant placement)" while this case failed at six weeks; and it names 35 Ncm as the safe ceiling for dense bone while the case was placed at 35–50 Ncm, i.e. at or above that ceiling.
- **Antibiotics before removal.** A 5-day amoxicillin + metronidazole course preceded re-entry, which is not the sequence the compression hypothesis predicts; the paper does not discuss this.

## Glossary

- **Compression necrosis (과압박 괴사)**: bone tissue death from pressure applied during implant insertion — pressure beyond physiological limits compromises the intraosseous blood supply, producing ischaemia and necrosis. Generally accepted to be limited to cortical bone.
- **Aseptic necrosis (무염증 괴사)**: bone necrosis without an infectious cause; per this paper's review, the main non-infectious trauma mechanism acting on bone at implant placement, arising from **either** over-compression **or** overheating.
- **Bone sequestrum (골격리편)**: a devitalized fragment of bone separated from living bone.
- **Empty osteocyte lacunae (빈 골세포소강)**: lacunar spaces devoid of osteocyte nuclei — the histological hallmark of osteonecrosis.
- **Osteoblastic riming (골아세포 테두리)**: the layer of osteoblasts lining viable bone surface; its absence is reported here as evidence of necrosis.
- **Undersized drilling (언더사이징 드릴링)**: preparing a narrower osteotomy than the implant so that insertion torque compresses cortical bone; can improve primary stability in low-density bone but risks microfracture and necrosis in dense bone.
- **Pre-tapping (프리태핑)**: cutting a thread into the osteotomy wall with a tap before insertion, so the implant does not have to screw itself into dense bone; the paper's prevention recommendation for dense bone.
- **Lekholm & Zarb bone quality (골질 I–IV)**: classification by the amount of cortical versus trabecular bone; type IV is a thin cortical plate around mostly trabecular bone.
- **47 °C / 1 min**: the time–temperature threshold cited for bone necrosis (see also the 47 °C/1 min regenerative-capacity threshold held as [[wiki/oral-surgery/bertollo-2011-drilling-bone-practicality-limitations-complications]]).
