## Why Ingested

Korean case report demonstrating a complete staged soft-then-hard tissue augmentation protocol for esthetic zone implant restoration, complementing the SR/MA framework on peri-implant soft tissue augmentation in [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/tavelli-2021-soft-tissue-phenotype-modification-peri-implant-nma]]. Provides a step-by-step surgical narrative (frenectomy → CTG/APF → GBR → implant → provisional contouring → customized impression coping) that illustrates the sequencing rationale missing from the overview evidence.

## Three-line Summary

Case report of a 19-year-old male with severely deficient maxillary left central incisor (#21) site (prior failed implant, thin scalloped biotype, low frenum, scarring) who underwent a staged four-phase tissue reconstruction over ~2.5 years before receiving the final implant crown.
Sequential staging: soft tissue first (frenectomy + APF + CTG), then GBR (Tutoplast allograft + titanium-reinforced Gore-tex membrane), then delayed implant (Astra 3.5×13 mm tapered), then provisional contouring at 30% reduced emergence profile for 10 months, then final screw-retained restoration via customized impression coping.
At 2.5-year follow-up peri-implant tissue and papillae were stable; main limitation was slight marginal discoloration from gold cast abutment (patient refused zirconia) and minor #11 recession from cumulative surgeries, both stable.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates soft-tissue-first staging rationale: improving thin scalloped tissue and eliminating scar/frenum before hard tissue augmentation prevents flap tension and keratinized tissue loss at GBR closure
- Describes envelope-technique CTG and APF sequence to establish adequate keratinized band prior to GBR — reducing post-GBR recession risk in thin biotype
- Documents provisional restoration emergence-profile protocol: initial 30% under-contoured provisional, then biweekly expansion to train peri-implant mucosa without papilla collapse
- Customized impression coping technique: VPS + pattern resin transfer of provisional abutment contour to preserve soft tissue topography in the final impression
- 2.5-year follow-up showing stability of staged reconstruction in a challenging esthetic zone with prior surgical failure

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Case report, single patient
- **n**: 1 patient (19-year-old male, missing #21)
- **Clinical context**: Prior block bone graft + implant failed (removed at 4 months), thin scalloped biotype, low labial frenum, scar tissue from multiple surgeries, #22 buccal recession
- **Outcomes**: Peri-implant tissue health, papilla, gingival margin stability, patient satisfaction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Residual alveolar width pre-GBR | ~1.5 mm (insufficient for implant placement) |
| GBR materials | Tutoplast allograft + titanium-reinforced non-resorbable Gore-tex membrane, 4 mm fixation screws |
| Implant (placed 8 months post-GBR) | Astra Tech 3.5 mm diameter × 13 mm length, tapered |
| Implant depth | 3 mm below CEJ; 2 mm mesio-distal clearance from adjacent teeth |
| Additional grafting at implant surgery | Bio-Oss + Ossixplus membrane for buccal volume maintenance |
| Provisional emergence profile | Started at 30% below ideal; expanded biweekly over 10 months |
| Final abutment | UCLA screw-retained, gold cast abutment |
| Follow-up | 2 years 6 months — peri-implant tissue and papillae stable, occlusion maintained |
| Limitation | Slight gingival discoloration at cervical margin from gold abutment; minor #11 recession from cumulative surgeries (both stable at 2.5 yr) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single case report — no control group, no statistical data
- Gold cast abutment selected (patient declined zirconia fracture risk) → mild subgingival discoloration at gingival margin
- Multiple prior surgeries led to #11 recession; stable at 2.5-year follow-up but aesthetic compromise
- Total treatment duration of approximately 2.5 years from first visit to final restoration (staged approach inherently long)
- No standardized photographic/radiographic protocol comparison; subjective clinical assessment only

## 7. Glossary

- **출현윤곽 (Emergence Profile, EP)**: The contour of the implant crown from the gingival margin to the contact point; critical for papilla support and cervical esthetics
- **맞춤형 인상 코핑 (Customized Impression Coping)**: Impression coping modified with VPS + pattern resin to replicate the subgingival emergence contour established by the provisional restoration
- **얇은 부채꼴 형태 (Thin Scalloped Biotype)**: Gingival phenotype with thin, delicate tissue prone to recession after surgical trauma
- **비흡수성 차단막 (Non-resorbable Barrier Membrane)**: ePTFE/titanium-reinforced membrane (Gore-tex type) providing stable space maintenance for GBR; requires second-stage removal
- **근단변위판막술 (Apically Positioned Flap, APF)**: Surgical flap displaced apically to increase the zone of keratinized gingiva
- **결합조직이식술 (Connective Tissue Graft, CTG)**: Subepithelial connective tissue harvested from palate and transplanted to augment soft tissue volume and coverage
