## Why Ingested

Ingested to provide mechanistic evidence for gingival epithelial basal stem cell biology underlying the site-specific turnover rate data in [[overviews/oral-mucosal-epithelial-turnover-overview]]. IRX1 is expressed in the gingival epithelial basal cell layer (JE + GE) and its haploinsufficiency delays re-epithelialization — directly verifying the claim that JE/gingival epithelium relies on a dedicated stem cell niche for its rapid renewal.

## Three-line Summary

In vivo mouse gingival wound model (ø0.75 mm punch biopsy, Irx1 heterozygous knockout vs WT, n=3–7/group at 1/3/7 dpi) with Krt14CreERT2 lineage tracing, RNA-Seq at 3 dpi, and two human gingival samples reveals IRX1 as a primed basal-layer transcription factor in junctional epithelium (JE) and gingival epithelium (GE) that is absent in skin epidermis.

IRX1 activates SOX9 via direct promoter binding (ChIP/luciferase assay), driving KC proliferation and migration through the EPGN/EGF signaling axis; Irx1 Het wounds were 4× larger at 1 dpi (0.31 vs 0.08 mm²), incompletely closed at 7 dpi, with ectopic KRT6A expression and absent terminal differentiation marker Loricrin at the re-epithelialization front.

Exogenous EPGN fully rescued KC migration in vitro when IRX1 was inhibited, confirming IRX1→SOX9→EPGN as a druggable cascade; results establish that JE/GE rapid renewal depends on a dedicated IRX1+ stem cell niche, with direct implications for why periodontal treatment temporarily disrupts this regenerative compartment.

## 세줄요약

생쥐 치은 창상 모델 (ø0.75mm 펀치, Irx1 헤테로 결손 (Het) vs WT, n=3–7/군, 1/3/7 dpi), Krt14CreERT2 계통추적 + 3 dpi RNA-Seq, 인간 치은 n=2 — IRX1이 접합상피 (JE)·치은상피 (GE) 기저층에 기저발현(primed)되고 표피에는 없는 전사인자임을 규명.

IRX1은 SOX9 프로모터 직접 결합 (ChIP/루시퍼라제) → EPGN/EGF 신호 → KC 증식·이동 구동; Het 창상은 1 dpi에서 4배 크고 (0.31 vs 0.08 mm²) 7 dpi에 불완전 치유 — KRT6A 이소발현 지속·재상피화 전선(RF) 최종분화 마커 Loricrin 소실.

In vitro에서 EPGN 투여로 IRX1 억제 시 이동 완전 구제 → IRX1→SOX9→EPGN 축이 치료 표적; 접합상피 (JE)/치은상피 (GE) 의 빠른 재생이 IRX1+ 줄기세포 틈새 (Niche) 에 의존함을 입증.

## One-line Summary

In vivo mouse gingival wound-healing study (n=3–7 per group, n=2 human gingiva) showing IRX1 is a primed basal-layer transcription factor in gingival epithelium that drives KC proliferation and migration via SOX9 activation and EPGN/EGF signaling; Irx1 haploinsufficiency delays wound closure by 3–7 days.

## 한줄요약

생쥐 치은 창상 모델 (n=3–7/군, 인간 치은 n=2): IRX1이 치은상피 기저줄기세포층에 기저발현(primed)되어 SOX9 활성화·EPGN/EGF 신호 유도로 KC 증식·이동을 조절하며, 반접합자(Irx1 Het) 마우스에서 재상피화가 3–7일 지연됨.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First report of IRX1 expression specifically in the **basal and immediate suprabasal layers of GE and JE** and in mesenchymal stroma niches (co-expressing STRO-1) in both murine and human gingiva.
- IRX1 is **absent from skin epidermis** but expressed in hair follicle bulge/dermal papilla — parallel oral-specific expression pattern to PITX1.
- Establishes IRX1 as a direct regulator of SOX9 (luciferase + ChIP assay in OCCM30 cells), activating the **IRX1→SOX9→KC proliferation** cascade.
- EPGN (epithelial mitogen), a KC-expressed EGF ligand, is activated by IRX1 at the wound RF; EPGN addition rescues KC migration when IRX1 is inhibited.
- *Irx1* Het mice show a 3× slower re-epithelialization (wound size 0.31 mm² vs 0.08 mm² at 1 dpi) and a psoriasis-like KC differentiation phenotype (KRT6A/SOX2 prolonged expression, absent Loricrin at RF).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Model**: Heterozygous Irx1-KO (Het) mouse vs WT; biopsy-punch gingival wound (ø0.75 mm) in front of first molar; sacrifice at 1, 3, 7 dpi; n=3–7 per time point per genotype
- **Human samples**: 2 deidentified healthy gingiva samples; IF for IRX1 expression
- **Lineage tracing**: Krt14CreERT2 × Rosa26mTmG reporter; tamoxifen 2 days before injury; GFP tracked at 0/3/7 dpi
- **RNA-Seq**: total RNA from gingival RE at 3 dpi (|logFC|>1, p<0.05)
- **In vitro**: LS-8 oral epithelial cells, overexpression/inhibition of IRX1, scratch assay, proliferation assay, EPGN rescue experiment

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Measure | WT | Irx1 Het |
|---|---|---|
| Wound area at 1 dpi | 0.08 mm² | 0.31 mm² |
| Wound area at 3 dpi | 0.01 mm² | 0.19 mm² |
| Wound area at 7 dpi | ~0 mm² (fully closed) | 0.02 mm² (incomplete) |
| Loricrin (terminal diff.) at RF 3 dpi | Present | Absent |
| KRT6A in RF at 3 dpi | RF only | All RE layers (ectopic) |
| EPGN at 3 dpi RF | Induced | Absent/delayed |
| PCNA (proliferation) in LS-8 OE-IRX1 | 2× increase vs control | — |

- **RNA-Seq**: 1,117 up / 704 down genes in WT injured vs uninjured; wound-activated KC signature (SPRR, KRT5/14, KRT6/16/17, cyclin genes) significantly upregulated; SOX9 upregulated at 3 dpi in WT
- GO terms downregulated in Het RE at 3 dpi: **KC migration, KC differentiation, wound healing, positive regulation of EGF signaling**

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small n (3–7 mice per group per time point) limits statistical power
- Human data is descriptive (n=2 gingiva samples; expression pattern only, no functional data)
- IRX1 in null mice is early neonatal lethal — only haploinsufficient model available; true loss-of-function gingival phenotype not assessable in adult
- IRX1→EPGN feedback loop mechanism (EPGN activates both SOX9 and IRX1) described but not deeply dissected
- No translation to human KC functional experiments

## 7. Glossary

- **IRX1** (Iroquois Homeobox 1): TF of the Iroquois gene cluster; expressed in oral epithelial basal cells and HF bulge
- **JE** (Junctional Epithelium, 접합 상피): epithelium attaching tooth surface to gingiva; fastest-renewing oral epithelium
- **GE** (Gingival Epithelium, 치은 상피): keratinized stratified epithelium of the gingiva
- **RF** (Regenerative Front): leading edge of migrating epithelial tongue in wound healing
- **EPGN** (Epithelial Mitogen, 상피 분열 촉진 인자): EGF family ligand expressed in wound-activated KCs
- **SPRR** (Small Proline-Rich Protein): component of the epidermal differentiation complex; upregulated in wound healing
- **TA cells** (Transient Amplifying cells): proliferative progenitors derived from stem cells in the basal layer
