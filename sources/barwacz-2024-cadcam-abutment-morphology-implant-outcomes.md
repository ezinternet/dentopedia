## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/soft-tissue/misch-2025-abutment-height-emergence-angle-peri-implant-bone-loss]]가 emergence angle과 변연골소실(MBL)의 상관을 다뤘고 [[implants/soft-tissue/siegenthaler-2022-convex-emergence-profile-mucosal-recession-rct]]가 convex vs concave 프로필의 점막퇴축 위험(OR 12.6)을 보고한 바 있어, 본 Barwacz 2024 RCT를 추가해 "linear divergent(대조) vs concave(실험)" 전이형태 비교의 직접 증거를 보강한다. 본 페이지는 이번 배치에서 함께 작성되는 [[implants/soft-tissue/bressan-2024-iao-consensus-peri-implant-soft-tissues]] (IAO 컨센서스, 지대주 재료·형태 섹션)와도 교차연결된다.

## Three-line Summary

Single-center, two-arm parallel RCT (n=60 randomized, n=54 completed at 1 year) comparing screw-retained CAD/CAM zirconia abutments with a linear divergent (control) vs concave (experimental) transmucosal emergence profile in single-tooth anterior maxillary implants, using a delayed (standard healing abutment → definitive CAD/CAM abutment) workflow.

At 1 year, neither mid-facial mucosal zenith change (p=0.853), buccal soft tissue thickness (p=0.674), keratinized mucosal width (p=0.106), nor peri-implant health indicators (MBL, PI, MI, PD, BOP; all p>0.3) differed significantly between groups; implant survival was 100% and restoration success 98.2%.

Clinically, transmucosal abutment macro-morphology (linear divergent vs concave) did not measurably influence 1-year peri-implant mucosal or health outcomes in this delayed-loading, screw-retained, directly-veneered zirconia abutment protocol — contrasting with convex-vs-concave comparisons (e.g., Siegenthaler et al.) that did show significant recession differences, suggesting convexity, not concavity vs linearity, may be the key morphologic risk factor.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 2군 병행배정 RCT (무작위배정 n=60, 1년 완료 n=54) — 전방상악 단일치아 임플란트에서 나사유지형 CAD/CAM 지르코니아 지대주의 경조직-점막 이행부 형태를 선형발산형(대조군) vs 오목형(실험군)으로 비교, 표준 치유지대주 → 최종 CAD/CAM 지대주로 이행하는 지연(delayed) 프로토콜 사용.

1년 시점 협측 점막 정점(mid-facial mucosal zenith) 변화(p=0.853), 협측 연조직 두께(p=0.674), 각화점막폭(KMW, p=0.106), 그리고 주위염 지표(MBL·PI·MI·PD·BOP, 모두 p>0.3) 모두 두 군 간 유의차 없음; 임플란트 생존율 100%, 보철 성공률 98.2%.

임상적으로 이 지연식립·나사유지·직접시멘트리스 도재 베니어 프로토콜에서는 경조직-점막 이행부 대형태(선형발산 vs 오목)가 1년 시점 주위 점막·건강 지표에 측정 가능한 영향을 주지 않았다 — 반면 convex-vs-concave 비교(예: Siegenthaler 등)는 유의한 퇴축 차이를 보였으므로, 오목 vs 선형이 아니라 볼록(convexity) 자체가 핵심 위험인자일 가능성을 시사한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First RCT (to the authors' knowledge) directly testing a **linear divergent vs concave** transmucosal abutment morphology comparison, as distinct from the convex-vs-concave comparisons more commonly reported in the literature (e.g., Koutouzis et al., Siegenthaler et al./Thoma group).
- Mirrors the **most common real-world clinical sequence**: standard healing abutment placed at implant surgery, followed 8 weeks later by master impression, then a **delayed** custom CAD/CAM zirconia abutment (no implant provisionalization) — as opposed to trial designs using immediate provisionalization.
- Introduces a **quantitative STL superimposition method** (linear length delta in mm, volumetric delta in mm³) to objectively characterize how much material was subtractively removed to create the concave profile relative to the linear divergent predicate design, and tests whether the magnitude of that morphologic change correlates with mucosal outcomes.
- Uses a **tri-axial stereotactic photography protocol** (Canfield camera + patient-specific bite fork, X/Y/Z triangulation, ImageJ pixel-to-mm calibration via UNC-15 probe) for reproducible longitudinal mid-facial mucosal zenith measurement.
- Abutments were **directly veneered with feldspathic porcelain** to within 0.5 mm of the finish line (screw-retained), eliminating the abutment-crown cement microgap that is a known driver of biological complications.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Single-center, 2-arm parallel RCT; simple randomization by an independent Clinical Research Coordinator at the master-impression visit (8 weeks post implant placement).
- **Implant system**: OsseoSpeed EV (Astra Tech Implant System, Dentsply Sirona), internal conical connection; virtually planned with Simplant Pro to a prosthetically oriented position, implant platform ≥3.0 mm apical to the planned mid-facial mucosal zenith; tooth-supported stereolithographic surgical guides (sCAIP) used for all placements by a single surgeon.
- **Abutments**: Atlantis Crown Abutment (Dentsply Sirona), custom screw-retained CAD/CAM zirconia, designed in Virtual Atlantis Design (VAD) software with two transmucosal transition morphologies — linear divergent (control, predicate design) vs concave (experimental, subtractively modified) — inserted 4 weeks after master impression, torqued to 25 Ncm, with PTFE tape and resin-composite (Filtek Supreme Ultra) access-channel obturation after etching/silanating the porcelain.
- **Sample size**: Powered on a minimum clinically relevant 1.0 mm difference in 12-month mid-facial mucosal zenith change; SD estimates 0.4-1.0 mm from prior literature; α=0.05 (12-month key comparison) / α=0.0125 (Bonferroni, 4 timepoints); target power 80-90%; 30 subjects/arm deemed adequate allowing 15% attrition.
- **Eligibility**: Adults needing single-tooth replacement, anterior maxilla (region 5-12/first premolar to first premolar), stable adjacent teeth, ≥5.5 mm buccal-lingual bone width and mesial-distal space on CBCT, ≥2 mm baseline keratinized mucosa, edentulous ≥4 months; excluded tobacco use (6 months), uncontrolled systemic disease, prior head/neck radiation, pregnancy, etc.
- **Outcomes**: Primary = 1-year change in mid-facial apico-coronal mucosal zenith. Secondary = buccal soft tissue thickness (k-file transmucosal sounding), keratinized mucosal width (KMW), MBL (periapical radiographs, standardized bite jig), plaque index (PI), mucosal index (MI, Silness & Loe adapted), probing depth (PD), bleeding on probing (BOP); plus correlation of abutment linear/volumetric morphologic change (STL superimposition, Geomagic Control X) with mucosal thickness and zenith outcomes.
- **Follow-up**: Baseline (final restoration insertion), 1, 3, 6, 12 months.
- **Statistics**: Mixed models (subject = random effect, treatment/time = fixed effects) and Pearson correlation for primary/secondary outcomes; ICC + two-sample t-test for MBL; R version 3.6.0.

| Buccal soft tissue thickness | 2.72 ± 1.13 → 2.79 ± 0.52 mm | 2.81 ± 1.13 → 2.60 ± 0.47 mm | 0.674 |
| Keratinized mucosal width (KMW) | 2.70 ± 1.14 → 3.26 ± 1.06 mm | 3.31 ± 1.84 → 3.88 ± 1.64 mm | 0.106 |
| MBL, distal | -0.50 → -0.38 mm | -0.55 → -0.51 mm | 0.771 |
| MBL, mesial | -0.53 → -0.48 mm | -0.54 → -0.51 mm | 0.773 |
| Plaque index (PI) | 0.36 → 0.45 | 0.29 → 0.57 | 0.988 |
| Mucosal index (MI) | 0.74 → 0.91 | 0.59 → 0.90 | 0.409 |
| Probing depth (PD) | 2.53 → 2.67 mm | 2.42 → 2.55 mm | 0.307 |
| BOP (0-6 sites) | 1.48 → 1.48 | 1.10 → 1.61 | 0.917 |

- Population: 60 subjects randomized (29 women/31 men, mean age 58.7y, range 23.5-79.2y), 60 implants — incisor 31 (51.6%), canine 7 (11.7%), premolar 22 (36.7%).
- Attrition: 1 concave-group subject exited at 6 months (fractured abutment neck); 5 linear-divergent-group subjects lost (1 implant failure pre-master-impression, 3 failed to appoint after 3-month visit, 1 death after 6-month visit). 54 completed at 12 months (29 concave / 25 linear divergent).
- **Implant survival 100%**, **restoration success 98.2%** (master impression → 12 months).
- Time (not group) was the significant driver of change: mid-facial mucosal zenith coronal migration was significant over time (p<0.001, greatest gain between abutment delivery and 1-month recall); KMW gain was significant over time (p<0.001); PI (p=0.044) and MI (p=0.001) changed significantly over time; PD (p=0.096) and BOP (p=0.079) showed non-significant upward trends over time.
- STL superimposition of predicate (linear) vs experimental (concave) abutment designs: mean linear-dimension delta 0.15 ± 0.14 mm (predicate 2.50 ± 0.63 mm vs experimental 2.65 ± 0.66 mm); mean volumetric delta -7.95 ± 6.10 mm³ (predicate 165.89 ± 41.74 mm³ vs experimental 157.94 ± 41.11 mm³ — i.e., material subtracted to create the concavity).
- Neither the magnitude of linear change nor volumetric change correlated with buccal soft tissue thickness change (Pearson r=-0.16, p=0.42 and r=0.06, p=0.75, respectively) or mid-facial mucosal zenith change (r=0.02, p=0.92 and r=0.05, p=0.81, respectively); mixed-model tests for time/linear/volume were likewise non-significant (all p≥0.46).
- Intra-rater MBL measurement reliability: ICC=0.96 (95% CI 0.95-0.97, p<0.001).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No professional esthetic index (e.g., Pink Esthetic Score) or patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) were collected — cannot determine whether patients/clinicians perceived any esthetic difference between groups.
- Baseline mucosal height/phenotype prior to implant placement was not assessed, precluding correlation between pre-implant phenotype and post-restoration mucosal dynamics.
- 1-year follow-up is relatively short; longer-term data needed.
- Single-center, single-surgeon, single implant system (Astra Tech/OsseoSpeed EV internal conical connection) and single abutment product line (Atlantis Crown Abutment) — generalizability to other implant/abutment systems, immediate-placement or immediately-provisionalized protocols, and convex-morphology comparisons is not established.
- The trial compares **linear divergent vs concave** only; it does not include a convex arm, so it cannot directly refute or confirm convex-specific recession risk reported elsewhere (e.g., Siegenthaler et al., OR 12.6 for convex vs concave).

## 6. Related Work

- Siegenthaler et al. 2022 (Thoma group) — convex vs concave custom zirconia CAD/CAM abutment RCT with delayed provisionalization; found convex profiles associated with significantly higher mucosal recession (64.3% vs 14.3% concave, OR 12.6) despite predominantly thick baseline phenotype. See [[implants/soft-tissue/siegenthaler-2022-convex-emergence-profile-mucosal-recession-rct]].
- Koutouzis, Adeinat, and Ali / Koutouzis, Ali, and AlRubaie — titanium CAD/CAM abutments (concave vs convex) with immediate cement-retained provisionalization at healed maxillary premolar sites; no significant between-group mid-facial mucosal change at 1 or 3 years, but both morphologies showed apical migration in the first 3 months that then stabilized.
- Patil et al. — split-mouth RCT, prefabricated titanium abutment with a 0.5 mm concave macrogroove vs linear divergent control, cement-retained, second-stage connection; no significant differences in MBL, PES, PI, PD, or BOP, nor in PROMs/professional esthetic scoring.
- Valente et al. — systematic review with meta-analysis on emergence-profile morphology, noting scarcity and methodological heterogeneity in defining concave/divergent/convex profiles across studies.
- [[implants/soft-tissue/misch-2025-abutment-height-emergence-angle-peri-implant-bone-loss]] — related evidence on abutment/emergence geometry and marginal bone loss.

## 7. Glossary

- **CAD/CAM abutment**: Computer-aided-design/computer-aided-manufacturing custom implant abutment, milled to patient-specific anatomy.
- **Transmucosal emergence profile**: The three-dimensional contour of the abutment/restoration as it transitions from the implant platform through the soft tissue to the free gingival margin; can be concave, linear (straight/divergent), or convex.
- **Mid-facial mucosal zenith**: The most apical point of the marginal buccal peri-implant mucosa, referenced to the crown incisal edge; a key esthetic/soft-tissue-stability landmark.
- **KMW (keratinized mucosal width)**: Distance from the mucogingival junction to the mucosal margin at the mid-facial aspect.
- **MBL (marginal bone level)**: Radiographic distance from a fixed implant reference point (e.g., first microthread) to the bone crest.
- **MI (mucosal index)**: Silness & Loe Gingival Index adapted for peri-implant mucosa.
- **sCAIP**: Static computer-aided implant placement — fully guided surgery using a fabricated surgical guide (as opposed to dynamic/navigation-based guidance).
- **STL superimposition**: Digital best-fit alignment of two stereolithography (3D scan) files to quantify geometric differences (here, between predicate and experimental abutment designs).
