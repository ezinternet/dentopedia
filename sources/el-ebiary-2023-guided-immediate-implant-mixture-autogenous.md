## Why Ingested
Directly tests the graft-vs-no-graft jumping-gap question at guided immediate implant placement (IIP) in the esthetic zone, using a 1:1 autogenous:xenograft mixture (dual-zone grafting) and a fully digital/guided workflow, evaluated via Pink Esthetic Score (PES) rather than the radiographic bone-thickness endpoint used by [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/sanz-2017-bone-graft-gap-immediate-implant-rct]]. Independently reinforces that trial's conclusion (grafting the gap preserves outcomes better than leaving it empty) via a soft-tissue esthetic endpoint, and sits alongside the same-year, same-subcategory [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/elsheikh-2023-prf-xenograft-alloplastic-immediate-implant]] which instead compares graft-material choice (all arms grafted).

## Three-line Summary
Parallel-group RCT (n=24, 12/arm) in the maxillary esthetic zone testing computer-guided immediate implant placement with digital immediate provisionalization, with vs. without grafting the 2–3 mm jumping gap using a 1:1 autogenous:xenograft mixture (dual-zone grafting technique).
At 6 months, median Pink Esthetic Score (PES) was significantly higher in the grafted group than the ungrafted control (13 vs. 11.5, p=0.048), rising intra-group in the grafted arm (11.58→12.42, p=0.039) and falling in the control arm (11.75→11.17, p=0.035), despite no baseline difference immediately postoperatively (p=0.746).
Grafting the jumping gap with an autogenous-xenograft mixture during guided IIP better preserves peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics than leaving it ungrafted, though the trial is small (n=12/arm), single-center, and single-surgeon.

## 세줄요약
상악 심미구역 평행군 RCT(n=24, 군당 12명) — 컴퓨터 유도 즉시식립(Guided Immediate Implant Placement)과 디지털 즉시 임시수복 시, 2~3mm 틈새(jumping gap)를 자가골(Autogenous Bone)+이종골(Xenograft) 1:1 혼합으로 이식한 군과 무이식 대조군을 비교.
6개월 후 분홍심미지수(Pink Esthetic Score, PES) 중앙값이 이식군에서 유의하게 높았고(13 vs 11.5, p=0.048), 이식군은 시간 경과에 따라 PES 상승(11.58→12.42, p=0.039), 대조군은 하강(11.75→11.17, p=0.035) — 수술 직후에는 두 군 차이 없었음(p=0.746).
유도 즉시식립 시 틈새를 자가골+이종골 혼합으로 이식(dual-zone grafting)하면 무이식 대비 연조직 심미 결과가 더 우수하나, 표본 크기(군당 n=12)가 작고 단일기관·단일술자 연구라는 한계가 있음.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: BMC Research Notes 2023;16(1):331
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s13104-023-06612-8
- **Institution**: Cairo University (Faculty of Oral and Dental Medicine — Prosthodontics & Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Depts.), Giza, Egypt; Future University in Egypt, New Cairo, Egypt

## 2. Key Contributions
- Isolates the jumping-gap graft variable (1:1 autogenous:xenograft vs. none) while holding a fully digital, guided-surgery + immediate-provisionalization workflow constant across both arms.
- Shows divergent 6-month PES trajectories: +0.84 mean in the grafted group vs. −0.58 mean in the ungrafted control, despite statistically equivalent baseline PES.
- Validates low-speed (300 rpm, no coolant) intrasocket autogenous-bone harvesting (cites the authors' own pilot histology showing viable cells vs. non-viable cells from high-speed harvest) as a graft component that avoids a second donor site/surgery.
- Supports the "dual-zone grafting" technique — graft extending from the bone crest into the soft-tissue zone beneath an immediately loaded, digitally designed provisional — as esthetic-outcome preserving.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: rct (parallel, 2-arm; block randomization with stratification, block size 4)
- **n**: 24 patients (12/arm); one non-restorable maxillary incisor/canine per patient with intact labial plate and interproximal bone, ages 20–50; sample size powered at 80% (α=0.05) from a prior study
- **Outcomes**: Pink Esthetic Score (PES, 7-parameter 0–2 scale, max 14) at implant insertion (baseline) and 6 months, scored against the contralateral tooth
- **Protocol**: CBCT + intraoral scan (STL) superimposed for prosthetically driven implant planning; 3D-printed tooth-supported surgical guide + 3D-printed digitally designed immediate provisional in both arms; study arm packed the jumping gap (2–3 mm) with 50% Bio-Oss xenograft + 50% low-speed-harvested autogenous bone up to the soft-tissue level; implants requiring <35 Ncm insertion torque were excluded from the trial; registered clinicaltrials.gov NCT04096209

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Baseline demographics: no significant age or gender difference between groups (age p=0.471; gender p=0.4)
- Immediate postoperative PES: study 11.5 (median, range 10–14) vs. control 12 (median, range 9–14); p=0.746, effect size d=0.13 (NS)
- 6-month PES: study 13 (median, range 10–14) vs. control 11.5 (median, range 9–13); p=0.048*, effect size d=0.859
- Intra-group change (baseline→6mo): study group 11.58±1.16 → 12.42±1.44, p=0.039*, r=0.596 (increase); control group 11.75±1.71 → 11.17±1.53, p=0.035*, r=0.609 (decrease)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=12/arm), single-center, single experienced surgeon — controls operator variability but limits generalizability
- Short follow-up (6 months only) — no long-term mucosal/bone stability data
- PES (a soft-tissue proxy) is the sole outcome; no parallel CBCT-measured buccal bone dimensional-change data was reported to corroborate the mechanism
- Authors themselves flag the need for further research linking mucosal stability to graft-material type and facial bone position/thickness

## 6. Related Work
- sanz-2017 ([[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/sanz-2017-bone-graft-gap-immediate-implant-rct]]): earlier RCT establishing that xenograft-filled jumping gaps (≥3mm) lose significantly less buccal bone thickness than ungrafted gaps (−0.46mm vs −0.84mm) — this paper independently reinforces that same graft-vs-no-graft direction via a soft-tissue esthetic endpoint (PES) instead.
- paknejad-2017 ([[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/paknejad-2017-flapless-immediate-implant-buccal-gap-rct]]): flapless IIP + xenograft buccal-gap fill reduced buccal bone loss vs. no graft — same directional conclusion in a different cohort/technique.
- elsheikh-2023 ([[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/elsheikh-2023-prf-xenograft-alloplastic-immediate-implant]]): same-year, same-subcategory 3-arm RCT comparing jumping-gap fill materials (PRF vs. xenograft vs. alloplastic, all arms grafted) — addresses graft-material choice rather than graft-vs-no-graft, a complementary question.

## 7. Glossary
- **PES**: Pink Esthetic Score — 7-parameter (0–2 each, max 14) index of peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics (mesial/distal papilla, soft-tissue contour/level, alveolar-process deficiency, soft-tissue texture/color), scored against a contralateral reference tooth.
- **Jumping gap**: The horizontal gap between the implant fixture surface and the inner surface of the labial/buccal bone plate created when an implant is placed immediately into an extraction socket.
- **Dual-zone grafting**: Technique filling the jumping gap with bone graft up to the bone crest and continuing with graft material into the soft-tissue zone beneath an immediately placed provisional, supporting both hard- and soft-tissue contour.
- **IIP**: Immediate Implant Placement — implant placement at the time of tooth extraction.
