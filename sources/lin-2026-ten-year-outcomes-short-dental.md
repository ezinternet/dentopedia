## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/emfietzoglou-2024-short-implants-6mm-survival-posterior-edentulism-sr]]는 ≤6mm short implant를 RCT-only로 정성 비교했으나 meta-analysis와 10년 추적은 없었다. 본 논문(Lin 2026)은 동일한 ≤6mm 정의로 8개 연구(RCT 4 + PCS 4)를 pooled meta-analysis하고, 결측(loss-to-follow-up) 데이터에 interpolation model을 적용한 sensitivity 분석까지 수행해 10년 생존율·합병증·MBL의 정량적 수치를 처음으로 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + sensitivity meta-analysis of 8 studies (4 RCTs, 4 prospective cohorts; 318 short implants ≤6 mm in 214 patients) reporting ≥10-year follow-up, comparing short (≤6 mm) vs long (≥10 mm) implants.

Pooled 10-year survival for short implants was 91.2% (patient-level) / 93.7% (implant-level) by complete-case analysis, dropping to 89.7%/92.8% after LTFU interpolation (2× failure-rate assumption); short implants had significantly lower 10-year survival than long implants (RR 0.920 PL / 0.943 IL), but no significant difference in technical complications (23.5%), biological complications (mucositis 33.6%, peri-implantitis 0.4%), or MBL (0.277 mm) vs long implants.

Short (≤6 mm) implants remain a clinically acceptable long-term option for severely atrophic posterior ridges (>89% 10-year survival) but carry a statistically lower survival than long implants, driven partly by screw-retained restorations (83.6–97.0% SR gap vs cement-retained); moderate-to-high risk of bias (RoB2/NOS) and high attrition (LTFU 2.86–37.6%) limit certainty.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + sensitivity 메타분석 — 8개 연구(RCT 4편 + 전향적 코호트 4편; short implant ≤6mm 318개, 214명) 10년 이상 추적, short(≤6mm) vs long(≥10mm) 임플란트 비교.

10년 pooled 생존율은 complete-case 기준 환자단위 91.2%/임플란트단위 93.7%였고, 결측 보정(interpolation, 2배 실패율 가정) 후 89.7%/92.8%로 낮아짐; short implant는 long implant보다 10년 생존율이 유의하게 낮았으나(RR 0.920 PL/0.943 IL), 기술적 합병증(23.5%), 생물학적 합병증(점막염 33.6%, 임플란트주위염 0.4%), 변연골소실(MBL 0.277mm)은 두 군 간 유의차 없음.

Short(≤6mm) 임플란트는 심하게 위축된 후방 치조제에서 10년 생존율 89% 이상으로 임상적으로 수용 가능한 장기 대안이지만, screw-retained(83.6~97.0% 생존율 격차) 등 요인으로 long implant보다 통계적으로 낮은 생존율을 보이며, 중등도~고위험 bias(RoB2/NOS)와 높은 추적탈락률(LTFU 2.86~37.6%)이 확실성을 제한함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First meta-analysis to isolate ≥10-year clinical outcomes of ≤6 mm short implants specifically (prior reviews mostly ≤5-year follow-up).
- Applies an interpolation (sensitivity) model (Akl et al., RI_LTFU/FU = 2) to account for loss-to-follow-up bias in 10-year survival estimates — a methodological gap in prior short-implant reviews.
- Direct pairwise RCT meta-analysis (4 RCTs) confirms short implants have significantly lower 10-year survival than ≥10 mm long implants at both patient- and implant-level, but equivalent complications and MBL.
- Subgroup analysis: screw-retained short implants have significantly lower implant-level survival (83.6–80.7%) than cement-retained (97.0–96.8%).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: PRISMA-guided systematic review with single-group meta-analyses (metaprop, Freeman-Tukey double arcsine transform) and paired-comparison meta-analyses (metabin/metacont, random-effects DerSimonian-Laird), R v4.3.1.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane Library, searched to 20 March 2026; 8,778 records → 4,173 after dedup → 56 full-text → 8 included (κ = 0.97).
- **n**: 8 studies (4 RCTs + 4 prospective cohort studies); 318 short implants (≤6 mm) in 214 patients; of these, 4 RCTs contributed 161 short implants (128 patients) vs 167 long implants (≥10 mm, 131 patients).
- **Outcomes**: primary — 10-year implant survival rate (SR); secondary — technical/biological complications, marginal bone loss (MBL).
- **Risk of bias**: RoB 2 (RCTs), Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (prospective cohorts) — overall judged moderate-to-high risk.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 10-year SR (complete case): 91.2% PL (95% CI 0.843–0.965, I²=35%), 93.7% IL (95% CI 0.878–0.980, I²=57%).
- 10-year SR (LTFU-imputed, sensitivity): 89.7% PL (95% CI 0.805–0.965, I²=63%), 92.8% IL (95% CI 0.861–0.976, I²=71%).
- Short vs long implant SR (complete case): RR 0.920 PL (95% CI 0.857–0.987), RR 0.943 IL (95% CI 0.896–0.992); imputed: RR 0.912 PL, RR 0.924 IL — all significant.
- Technical complications: 23.5% (95% CI 0.047–0.494, I²=90%); mucositis 33.6% (IL); peri-implantitis 0.4% (IL, I²=0%). No significant difference vs long implants.
- MBL: 0.277 mm PL and IL; after excluding high-risk-of-bias studies, 0.154 mm PL / 0.185 mm IL. No significant difference vs long implants.
- 85.7% of short-implant failures occurred after loading (late failures), consistent with prior evidence of accelerating failure risk beyond 3 years in function.
- No significant publication bias (Egger's test P>0.05 across all analyses; funnel plots Fig. S5–S10).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small pooled sample; nearly all short implants were exactly 6 mm (only one study included 5 mm implants) — limited generalizability to ≤5 mm implants.
- LTFU interpolation assumed a 2× failure-rate multiplier (from Akl et al. methodology) that is a conservative sensitivity assumption, not an evidence-based estimate; imputation increased heterogeneity substantially.
- High attrition across primary studies (10-year LTFU 2.86–37.6%) and moderate-to-high risk of bias (no blinding possible in RCTs; PCSs lacked independent control groups).
- Substantial clinical heterogeneity in implant systems, restoration type, retention method, and location across included studies.
- Future work: larger prospective RCTs with ≥10-year follow-up, standardized complication reporting, subgroup analyses by jaw location and parafunction (bruxism), and optimal restorative strategy (splinted vs single crown, screw- vs cement-retained).

## 6. Related Work
- Emfietzoglou & Dereka 2024 (RCT-only SR, ≤6 mm vs >6 mm, posterior partial edentulism): qualitative predecessor without meta-analysis or 10-year data — see [[implants/emfietzoglou-2024-short-implants-6mm-survival-posterior-edentulism-sr]].
- Ravida et al. 2019 (extra-short ≤6mm vs ≥10mm RCT meta-analysis) and Papaspyridakos et al. 2018 (≤6mm vs >6mm SR meta-analysis) are cited as prior shorter-follow-up (≤5y) meta-analyses this paper extends to 10 years.
- Vazouras et al. 2020 (effect of time-in-function on short-implant predictability): corroborates this paper's finding that failures accelerate beyond 3 years.

## 7. Glossary
- **SR**: Survival rate — proportion of implants remaining in function.
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss — distance change between implant shoulder and first bone-to-implant contact over time.
- **PL / IL**: Patient-level / implant-level analysis.
- **LTFU**: Lost to follow-up.
- **CIR**: Crown-to-implant ratio.
- **RI_LTFU/FU**: Relative incidence ratio of LTFU failure rate to regular follow-up failure rate, used in the interpolation/sensitivity model.
