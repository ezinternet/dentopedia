## Why Ingested

2026-09-29 surveillance on immediate implants with acute/purulent infection: laser decontamination is a distinct adjunct not yet covered in the cluster. It adds a controlled comparison to the conventional-debridement evidence summarized in [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/chrcanovic-2015-immediate-placement-implants-infected-sites]] and the meta-analytic base in [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (2014-2019, 98 patients, 149 Straumann implants: 90 immediate implants in infected sockets decontaminated with Er,Cr:YSGG laser vs 59 conventional implants in healed edentulous sites), mean follow-up 1.7 vs 1.5 years.

One failure in the laser group (1%) and none in controls; marginal bone level (MBL) changed +0.1 mm (test) vs -0.1 mm (control), a 0.2 mm difference that was not significant (P = 0.058).

Laser-treated infected sockets did not show a higher failure risk in the short term, but the comparison is non-randomized, the control is a delayed healed site, and antibiotics/chlorhexidine were co-interventions, so the laser effect itself cannot be isolated.

레이저군 실패 1건(1%), 대조군 0건; 변연골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 변화 +0.1 mm(시험) vs -0.1 mm(대조), 차이 0.2 mm로 유의하지 않음 (P = 0.058).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Described (by the authors) as the only controlled study of immediate implants in infected alveoli debrided and decontaminated with the Er,Cr:YSGG 2780 nm laser.
- Gives a fully specified laser protocol (tips, modes, air/water ratios, decontamination time).
- Reports MBL on standardized periapical radiographs with blinded measurement (intra-rater ICC 0.97).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Population**: consecutive series 2014-2019; exclusions: significant systemic disease, prior radiotherapy, steroid therapy, neurological/psychiatric disorders, immunocompromise, bruxism, smoking >15 cigarettes/day, alcohol/drug use, poor compliance. No sample size calculation (all eligible patients included).
- **Infection definition**: not operationally defined in Methods (no acute/chronic classification, no explicit purulence criterion). Baseline covariates listed "presence of abscess or fistula" and "presence of lesion"; lesions were more common in the test group. Reasons for extraction (test): caries 36%, endodontic 11%, fracture 48%, periodontal 6%.
- **Test group**: extraction, laser debridement and decontamination, implant placement in the same appointment (type 1). Antibiotic prophylaxis from the night before; 0.2% chlorhexidine gel for 2 weeks; collagen and absorbable membrane, often synthetic bone; immediate loading or loading after 3 months.
- **Laser protocol** (Waterlase iPlus, Er,Cr:YSGG 2780 nm): flap with soft-tissue mode, MC-3 tip 9 mm, 20% air/80% water, contact; bone/debridement with MZ-8 tip 6 mm, 40% air/60% water; decontamination in hard-tissue mode, 2.0 W, 20% air/80% water, 9 mm MZ-6 tip, non-contact (1-2 mm from bone), 60-90 s per alveolus, water wash 100 ml/min. Laser was the only instrument used to remove infected tissue.
- **Control group**: implants in edentulous healed sites, extraction at least 3 months earlier (type 4 placement), no laser.
- **Outcome**: MBL difference (follow-up minus baseline, mesial and distal, ImageJ, periapical parallel technique) via mixed model with patient as random effect; failure and complications secondary.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 98 patients (mean age 58.0 +/- 14.6 y; 53% female; 22 smokers); 149 implants, 90 test (60%) and 59 control (40%). All Straumann.
- Follow-up 1.7 +/- 0.6 y (test) vs 1.5 +/- 0.5 y (control), P = 0.082; minimum 1 year, up to over 4 years.
- Failure: 1 in test (1%), 0 in control. Complications: 1 mucositis in control (2%), none other in test.
- MBL: test +0.1 mm vs baseline, control -0.1 mm; difference 0.2 mm, P = 0.058.
- Baseline imbalance: lesions more common, longer implants, and more membrane/synthetic bone use in the test group.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, non-randomized; test and control differ in site condition, implant length, biomaterial use.
- Control is a healed edentulous site (delayed), not an uninfected fresh socket, so it does not test infected vs non-infected immediate placement.
- Few failures: no regression on predictors of failure was possible. Short follow-up (mean under 2 years).
- Antibiotic prophylaxis and chlorhexidine were co-interventions; the authors state the laser's causal effect cannot be established.
- Periapical radiographs only; no CBCT. Infection not classified. P = 0.058 is borderline with wide uncertainty given design.
- Authors call for prospective, preferably randomized (split-mouth) trials.

## 7. Glossary

- **Er,Cr:YSGG laser**: erbium, chromium-doped yttrium-scandium-gallium-garnet laser, 2780 nm, water-absorbed, used for soft and hard tissue.
- **Type 1 / type 4 placement**: immediate placement at extraction / late placement in a healed site (>=3 months).
- **MBL**: marginal bone level, distance from implant neck to first bone-to-implant contact.
- **Photoacoustic effect**: laser-induced pressure waves in water proposed as the bactericidal mechanism.
