## Three-line Summary

A CBCT study of 148 patients found the bony canal of the alveolar antral artery (AAA) in 57.0% of cases, with higher canal width in men and a trend toward greater AAA presence in older patients. After tooth loss the distance from the sinus floor to the AAA increases while the distance from the hard palate level stays constant. Practical recommendations include osteotomy shifting, piezo surgery for intrasinus AAA, double-window for intrabony AAA, and ligature for canals >3 mm.

## 세줄요약

148명 CBCT 환자를 분석한 연구로, 상악동 측벽의 치조동맥관(alveolar antral artery, AAA)은 57.0%에서 발견되었고 남성에서 관경이 유의하게 컸으며 고령 환자에서 더 흔한 경향을 보였다. 치아 상실 후 상악동저와 AAA 간 거리는 증가하나 경구개 수준과의 거리는 일정했다. 임상적으로 관경 >3 mm 출혈엔 결찰, 관내형엔 이중창, 동굴내형엔 피에조 수술 등 관리법을 제시한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- CBCT-based characterization of AAA bony canal presence (57.0%), bilateral presence (52.8% when visible), and canal dimensions (mean height/width 0.9 mm).
- Relationship of AAA to stable hard palate level: ~2.6 mm above hard palate in M1 region, rising cranially toward P1/P2.
- Sinus type (wide/average/narrow) and tooth loss effects on sinus floor–AAA distance; edentulous patients show larger sinus floor–AAA distance.
- Practical hemorrhage-management recommendations by AAA type (intrasinus, intrabony, extrabony) and diameter thresholds (<0.5 mm no management; >3 mm ligature).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Observational CBCT study; 251 scans evaluated, 103 excluded, 148 valid.
- Measurements: AAA presence, bilateral presence, distance to sinus floor (P1/P2/M1/M2), distance to hard palate level (P1/P2/M1), canal height/width, sinus wall width (M1), sinus type per Chan et al. 2014.
- Statistics: chi-square/Fisher, Mann-Whitney U, Shapiro-Wilk; α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- AAA bony canal present in 69/148 (57.0%); men 27/41 (65.9%), women 42/80 (52.5%).
- Age trend: <20 yrs 50.0%, 21–40 yrs 58.8%, >40 yrs 63.6%; p=0.064 (Mann-Whitney, not significant).
- Canal width significantly larger in men (p=0.015); mean canal height/width 0.9 mm.
- Sinus type: wide sinuses more likely to show AAA (narrow 6.7%, average 5.0%, wide 0.0% absent); chi-square p=0.734 (not significant).
- Distance sinus floor–AAA: P1 6.4, P2 7.3, M1 8.4, M2 8.8 mm (mean).
- Distance hard palate level–AAA: M1 2.6, P2 3.2, P1 7.7 mm (mean).
- Bilateral imaging in 72 patients; bilateral AAA in 52.8%.
- Tooth loss: distance sinus floor–AAA increases (p=0.067); hard palate distance constant.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective design; CBCT detects only intrabony form (extrabony/intrasinus forms invisible).
- Single-center university hospital; small subgroups for tooth-loss analysis.
- CBCT not angiography; small canals may be missed.

## 7. Glossary

- **Alveolar antral artery (AAA)**: anastomosis between posterior superior alveolar artery and infraorbital artery running in the lateral maxillary wall.
- **CBCT**: cone beam computed tomography.
- **Hard palate level**: stable bony reference used for AAA position prediction.
- **Sinus type (narrow/average/wide)**: Chan et al. 2014 classification by width at 2.3 mm above sinus floor.
- **Double-window technique**: two osteotomies to isolate and preserve intrabony AAA during lateral sinus lift.
