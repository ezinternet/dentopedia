## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (1499 implants/728 patients, McGill): antihypertensive drug users had dramatically lower implant failure rate (0.6% vs 4.1%; HR 0.12, 95% CI 0.03–0.49) — landmark study establishing AHT-implant survival link.

## 한줄요약
후향코호트(1499임플란트/728환자, McGill대): 항고혈압약 복용군 실패율 0.6% vs 비복용 4.1%, HR 0.12(95% CI 0.03–0.49) — 임플란트 생존과 전신약 연관성 최초 입증한 랜드마크 연구.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 임플란트 생존율에 대한 전신 약물의 연관성을 최초로 보고한 연구
- HR 0.12 — 항고혈압약 복용 시 임플란트 실패 위험 88% 감소
- 다층 혼합효과 생존분석으로 교란인자 조정
- 복용 약물 구성: RAS 억제제 54%, BB 18.9%, 티아지드 5.4%, 기타 21.6%
  (RAS 억제제 비중이 높아 약물 하위군 분석 없이 보호 효과 원인 불명확)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Study design**: Retrospective cohort
- **Population**: 728 patients, 1499 implants
  - AHT users: 142 patients, 327 implants
  - Non-users: 586 patients, 1172 implants
- **Statistics**: Multilevel mixed effects parametric survival analysis (confounders adjusted)
- **Institution**: McGill University Faculty of Dentistry, Montreal

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Failure rate: AHT 0.6% vs Non-users 4.1%
- **HR 0.12 (95% CI 0.03–0.49)** — highly significant
- Drug composition: 54% RAS inhibitors, 18.9% beta-blockers, 5.4% thiazides, 21.6% other

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No subgroup analysis by drug class (key limitation noted by Saravi 2021)
- Retrospective design
- No blood pressure data
- Wide CI (0.03–0.49) suggests small failure event count
- Authors note the landmark nature and call for further prospective studies

## 6. Related Work
- Saravi 2021: first subgroup (RAS vs BB) analysis for ISQ
- Masri 2024: confirmed AHT protection on EIF (2.29% vs 4.33%)
- Chidiac 2026: 10-year KM HR 0.23 protective effect
- Tonini 2022: null result (p=.958) in different cohort

## 7. Glossary
- HR: 위험비 (Hazard Ratio) — HR<1 = 보호 효과
- Multilevel mixed effects: 임플란트가 환자 내 중첩된 계층 구조 반영
