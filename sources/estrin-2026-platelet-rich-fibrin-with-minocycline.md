## Why Ingested

The wiki holds local-antimicrobial and PRF adjunct evidence for non-surgical periodontal therapy (NSPT) but no trial that puts autologous PRF used as a *drug carrier* against a commercial local-delivery antibiotic. This split-mouth RCT does that head-to-head (PRF + minocycline vs Arestin), extending the PRF-as-NSPT-adjunct line in [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/sherif-2025-iprf-vitamin-c-nonsurgical-periodontitis]] (I-PRF/vitamin C, null vs SRP alone) and the local-antimicrobial class summarized in [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/annisa-2023-chx-gel-chip-srp-sr-ma]]. It is also the second paper from the same research group in the wiki after [[periodontics/estrin-2026-prf-vs-emd-nspt-rct]] (PRF vs EMD), which was ingested from abstract only.

## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (private practice, Florida; 15 systemically healthy adults with Stage II/III Grade A/B periodontitis recruited, 13 analyzed) comparing, as adjuncts to full-mouth SRP plus laser, PRF mixed with liquid minocycline versus 1 mg minocycline microspheres (Arestin) in 4-6 mm interproximal pockets (one site per arm per patient) over 6 months.

Both arms improved significantly from baseline and did not differ: PD 5.08 to 3.94 mm (PRF + minocycline) vs 4.98 to 3.98 mm (Arestin), between-group p = 0.609; CAL 4.06 to 3.04 vs 4.06 to 2.98 mm, p = 0.789; GR p = 0.096; BOP 100% to 30% vs 100% to 18% (data not shown, not tested); 15.4% of sites in each arm reached residual PD of 3 mm, none below 3 mm.

PRF + minocycline looked comparable to Arestin at 6 months, but with 13 sites per arm, a design not powered for equivalence, an unequal/unclear minocycline dose, laser in both arms, and an author holding PRF intellectual property, "comparable" means "no difference detected", not shown equivalent.

## 세줄요약

분할구강 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 미국 플로리다 개인의원): 전신 건강한 성인 Stage II/III, Grade A/B 치주염 15명 모집(13명 분석); 전악 치석제거·치근활택술 (Scaling and Root Planing, SRP) + 레이저에 더해, 4-6 mm 인접면 치주낭에 PRF에 미노사이클린 (minocycline)을 섞은 군 vs 미노사이클린 1 mg 마이크로스피어 (Arestin) 군을 환자당 1부위씩 배정, 6개월 추적.

두 군 모두 기저치 대비 유의하게 개선되었고 군간 차이는 없었다: 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 5.08에서 3.94 mm (PRF+미노사이클린) vs 4.98에서 3.98 mm (Arestin), 군간 p = 0.609; 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 4.06에서 3.04 vs 4.06에서 2.98 mm, p = 0.789; 치은퇴축 (Gingival Recession, GR) p = 0.096; 탐침출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP) 100%에서 30% vs 100%에서 18% (자료 미제시, 검정 없음); 두 군 모두 15.4% 부위가 잔존 PD 3 mm에 도달했고 3 mm 미만은 없었다.

6개월 시점에서 PRF+미노사이클린은 Arestin과 유사해 보이나, 군당 13부위, 동등성 검정이 아닌 설계, 불균등하거나 불명확한 미노사이클린 용량, 양 군 공통 레이저, PRF 지식재산을 가진 저자라는 한계가 있어 "유사"는 "차이를 못 찾음"이지 동등성 입증이 아니다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Authors describe it as the first RCT comparing PRF + minocycline with commercial minocycline microspheres (Arestin) as NSPT adjuncts.
- Reports parity (no significant between-group difference) in PD, CAL and GR at 6 months, with significant within-group PD, CAL and BOP improvement in both arms.
- Positions PRF as an autologous, carrier-free local drug-delivery scaffold; the trial cannot separate the effect of PRF, minocycline, or their combination (no PRF-alone or SRP-alone arm).
- Practical framing: choice between the two may rest on workflow, cost, availability, and patient preference (PRF needs venipuncture and chairside preparation; Arestin is ready to use).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: split-mouth RCT, computer-generated randomization sequence, allocation concealed until intervention; single trained investigator measured PD, CAL, GR at baseline and 6 months.
- **Participants**: 15 adults (6 women, 9 men; mean age 46.7 +/- 10.8 y, range 30-63; 6 current smokers, 40%), Stage II/III Grade A/B periodontitis (2017 classification) with at least six interproximal sites PD > 4 mm; exclusions included periodontal treatment in the prior 6 months, antibiotics within 3 months, plaque score > 30%, uncontrolled diabetes, pregnancy.
- **Base therapy (both arms)**: full-mouth SRP (ultrasonic + hand curettes) under local anaesthesia, plus Fotona laser to remove residual inflamed granulation tissue.
- **Arm A (control)**: 1 mg minocycline microspheres (Arestin) placed subgingivally at one randomly chosen interproximal site per patient.
- **Arm B (test)**: PRF made with Bio-PRF system (two 10 ml red solid-PRF tubes + two blue liquid-PRF tubes; 8 min at 700 x g); 0.2 ml liquid minocycline (10 mg/0.5 ml, from a compounding pharmacy) mixed into 2 cc liquid PRF, clotted 10-20 min at room temperature, and 2-3 small fragments packed subgingivally with a cord packer.
- **Follow-up**: 2 weeks (oral hygiene reinforcement, soft tissue healing), 3 months supportive periodontal maintenance, 6 months re-measurement.
- **Sample size**: based on CAL gain, control SD 0.55 (from Sybil et al.), assumed effect size d = 1.0, alpha 0.05, power 80%: minimum 13, recruitment target 15 allowing 20% dropout.
- **Statistics**: GraphPad Prism 10; Shapiro-Wilk normality; paired t-test for between-group comparisons; two-tailed p < 0.05; no mixed-effects or GEE modeling of within-patient dependence.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Flow**: 15 recruited; 1 lost to follow-up and 1 excluded for inadequate plaque control (full-mouth plaque score 100% at the 6-month visit); 13 analyzed. No adverse events or complications reported.
- **PD** (mean +/- SD): PRF + minocycline 5.08 +/- 0.84 to 3.94 +/- 0.69 mm (p < 0.001; reported mean difference 1.13 +/- 0.15 mm); Arestin 4.98 +/- 0.90 to 3.98 +/- 0.60 mm (p < 0.001; difference 1.00 +/- 0.67 mm). Between groups p = 0.609.
- **CAL**: PRF + minocycline 4.06 +/- 1.09 to 3.04 +/- 1.08 mm (p < 0.001; gain 1.02 +/- 0.66 mm); Arestin 4.06 +/- 1.30 to 2.98 +/- 1.01 mm (p < 0.001; gain 1.08 +/- 0.61 mm). Between groups p = 0.789.
- **GR**: stable; within-group p = 0.081 (PRF + minocycline) and p = 0.338 (Arestin); between groups p = 0.096.
- **Residual PD at 6 months**: in each arm 2 sites (15.4%) at 3 mm and 11 sites (84.6%) above 3 mm; none below 3 mm.
- **CAL gain distribution** (identical in both arms): < 1 mm in 4 sites (30.8%); 1 to < 2 mm in 8 sites (61.5%); 2 to < 3 mm in 1 site (7.7%); none >= 3 mm.
- **BOP**: 100% of sites at baseline in both arms; 30% at 6 months with PRF + minocycline vs 18% with Arestin (stated as "data not shown"; no between-group test reported).
- Authors' interpretation: parity attributed to moderate baseline severity (mean PD about 5 mm, where NSPT alone already performs well), short follow-up, small sample, and a split-mouth design that may reduce sensitivity through carry-over.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Small n and single centre**: 13 analyzed sites per arm; the sample size was powered for superiority with an optimistic d = 1.0, not for equivalence or non-inferiority, so non-significance cannot be read as equivalence.
- **Dependence not modelled**: authors acknowledge no GEE or mixed-effects modelling of intra-subject correlation; analysis used paired t-tests.
- **Laser in both arms** is a confounder of the isolated effect of either adjunct; there was no SRP-only arm, so neither adjunct's added value can be estimated from this trial.
- **Dose inconsistency (reader-flagged)**: the Results text says PRF "combined with 1 mg minocycline", whereas the Methods describe 0.2 ml of a 10 mg/0.5 ml solution (4 mg) mixed into 2 cc PRF, of which only 2-3 fragments were placed per site; the actual dose delivered per site is not stated.
- **Reported SD oddity (reader-flagged)**: PD change SD of 0.15 mm in the PRF + minocycline arm versus 0.67 mm in the Arestin arm, despite similar baseline and 6-month SDs, is not explained.
- **Post-randomization exclusion**: one participant removed for 100% plaque at 6 months (analysis per protocol; no ITT described); BOP outcome reported without data or a statistical test.
- **No microbiological or biochemical outcomes**; 6-month follow-up only; sites were interproximal 4-6 mm pockets, so results do not extend to deeper pockets.
- **Conflict of interest**: one author holds PRF intellectual property.
- Future work (authors): larger RCTs, longer follow-up, microbiological and molecular outcomes.

## 7. Glossary
- **NSPT**: non-surgical periodontal therapy (SRP-based first-line treatment).
- **SRP**: scaling and root planing (치석제거·치근활택술).
- **PRF**: platelet-rich fibrin, an autologous leukocyte- and platelet-containing fibrin matrix from centrifuged blood; "liquid/injectable PRF" polymerizes after mixing.
- **Arestin**: commercial 1 mg minocycline microsphere subgingival local-delivery system.
- **PD / CAL / GR / BOP**: probing depth / clinical attachment level / gingival recession / bleeding on probing.
- **Split-mouth design**: each patient receives both treatments at different sites, controlling inter-individual variability but exposing results to carry-over and within-patient correlation.
