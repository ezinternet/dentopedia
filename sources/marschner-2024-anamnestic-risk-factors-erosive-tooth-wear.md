## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis of observational studies (87 publications / 71 studies; MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, Feb 2023; PROSPERO CRD4202339776) mapping 80 anamnestic risk factors for erosive tooth wear in permanent dentition.

Random-effects pooled ORs were significant for regurgitation (OR 2.27), acidic foods (OR 2.40), digestive disorders (OR 1.81), nutritional supplements (OR 1.73), carbonated drinks (OR 1.43), male gender (OR 1.30) and seasoning sauces (OR 1.28); most studies had low risk of bias.

Clinicians should prioritise GERD symptoms and dietary factors when screening for ETW risk; validated questionnaires are needed in future studies.

## 세줄요약

관찰연구 87편(연구 71건)을 대상으로 한 체계적 고찰 및 메타분석 (MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, 2023년 2월 검색)으로, 영구치열의 침식성 치아마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW) 문진 위험인자 80개를 지도화했다.

무작위효과 합산 OR은 역류 2.27, 산성 식품 2.40, 소화기 질환 1.81, 영양보충제 1.73, 탄산음료 1.43, 남성 1.30, 양념 소스 1.28에서 유의했고, 대부분의 연구가 낮은 편향위험이었다.

ETW 위험 선별 시 위식도역류질환 (Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease, GERD) 증상과 식이요인을 우선 확인해야 하며, 향후 연구는 타당화된 설문이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Maps 80 anamnestic risk factors in ten domains: socio-demographics, socio-economics, general health, oral diseases, medication, oral hygiene, food, beverages, dietary habits, leisure-related.
- Pooled ORs for seven factors (see section 4).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Observational studies on anamnestic risk factors for ETW in permanent dentition; MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science and manual searches (Feb 2023). Quality by modified Newcastle-Ottawa scale for cross-sectional studies; heatmap; random-effects meta-analysis of ORs where possible.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

87 publications / 71 studies. Significant pooled associations: male gender OR 1.30 (95% CI 1.16-1.44, p<0.001); regurgitation OR 2.27 (1.41-3.65, p=0.033); digestive disorders OR 1.81 (1.48-2.21, p<0.001); acidic foods OR 2.40 (1.44-4.00, p=0.033); seasoning sauces OR 1.28 (1.13-1.44, p=0.003); nutritional supplements OR 1.73 (1.28-2.35, p=0.019); carbonated drinks OR 1.43 (1.17-1.75, p=0.019). Most studies low risk of bias.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract-only: heterogeneity, number of studies per pooled factor, and certainty grading are not available. Observational, anamnestic (self-reported) exposures; authors call for validated questionnaires.
