## Why Ingested
The wiki holds TMD aetiology only as a general narrative ([[tmj/kapos-2020-temporomandibular-disorders-aetiology-diagnosis-review]]) and has no page on heritable/genetic susceptibility. This SR/MA (Arch Oral Biol 2024) fills that axis with a pooled polymorphism odds ratio. Source: PubMed abstract ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.archoralbio.2024.106032)).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis of 19 human studies (from 851 records) comparing genetic factors between TMD and non-TMD patients, appraised with the JBI checklist.

Genetic polymorphisms and gene differences were significantly associated with TMD signs and symptoms (myofascial pain, chronic pain, disc displacement); pooled OR for gene polymorphism and TMD development was 2.46 (95% CI 1.93-3.14, p = 0.00001).

Supports a heritable susceptibility component in TMD, but the authors call for larger, more diverse samples; only the abstract was available, so heterogeneity and per-gene results are unknown.

## 세줄요약

851편 중 19편의 인체 연구를 포함해, 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 환자와 비환자의 유전 요인을 비교한 메타분석 포함 체계적 문헌고찰 (JBI 체크리스트로 질평가).

유전자 다형성 (Gene Polymorphism)과 유전자 차이는 근막통·만성통증·관절원판 변위 같은 TMD 징후·증상과 유의하게 연관되었고, 다형성과 TMD 발생의 통합 오즈비 (Odds Ratio, OR)는 2.46 (95% CI 1.93-3.14, p = 0.00001).

TMD의 유전적 감수성 요소를 뒷받침하지만, 저자들도 더 큰 표본·다양한 집단의 검증을 요구; 초록만 확보해 이질성·유전자별 결과는 알 수 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Pooled estimate of gene polymorphism and TMD development: OR 2.46 (1.93-3.14).
- Qualitative synthesis linking genetic factors to myofascial pain, chronic pain, and disc displacement.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Databases: ScienceDirect, PubMed, Cochrane Library, Dimensions, Emerald. One reviewer selected studies using modified PICO (human TMD subjects; genetic factors compared between TMD and non-TMD; TMD signs/symptoms as outcomes). Quality: JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist for Non-randomized Experimental Investigations. 851 records, 19 included.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Significant influence of polymorphisms and gene differences on TMD signs and symptoms; pooled OR 2.46 (1.93-3.14), p = 0.00001.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
Abstract-only. Single reviewer for selection (per abstract). A JBI checklist for non-randomized experimental investigations is an unusual fit for genetic association studies. Authors advise larger, multi-setting studies. Genes studied, heterogeneity, and ancestry are not reported in the abstract.
