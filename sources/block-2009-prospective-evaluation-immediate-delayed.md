## Why Ingested
This prospective RCT compares immediate versus delayed implant placement with immediate provisionalization, directly addressing the placement-timing axis of the [[immediate-implant]] category in maxillary anterior esthetic sites. The paper's primary finding — that immediate provisionalization preserves 1 mm more facial gingival margin position than delayed placement — reinforces [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue]] outcomes and informs clinical decision-making for single-tooth esthetic cases.

## Three-line Summary
Prospective RCT, n=55 patients completing 2-year follow-up (baseline 76 recruited), comparing immediate implant placement & provisionalization vs. delayed placement (4-month healing) then immediate provisionalization; both groups showed similar crestal bone response and implant integration.
Immediate placement group preserved 1 mm more facial gingival margin position (P < .05) vs. delayed group; crestal bone levels remained stable after first 6 months in both groups.
Immediate provisionalization supports esthetic outcomes in maxillary anterior by maintaining soft tissue position, though hard tissue resorption profiles are equivalent between timing protocols.

## 세줄요약
전향적 RCT, n=55명 2년 추적 (초기 모집 76명), 즉시 임플란트 식립·즉시 가임관 vs. 지연 식립(4개월 경과) 후 즉시 가임관 비교; 양 군 모두 유사한 골정 반응 및 임플란트 통합을 보임.
즉시 식립군은 지연 식립군 대비 전정부 잇몸 변연 위치를 1mm 더 보존 (P < .05); 크레스탈 골 수준은 양 군 모두 처음 6개월 이후 안정적으로 유지.
즉시 가임관은 상악전치부 esthetic 결과를 지원하여 연조직 위치를 유지하나, 경조직 흡수 프로필은 식립 시기 프로토콜 간 유사함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Prospective evidence that **hard tissue response is similar** between immediate and delayed placement in maxillary anterior when both use immediate provisionalization
- Demonstrated clinical benefit: **immediate provisionalization preserves 1 mm more facial gingival margin position** than delayed provisionalization
- Standardized radiographic monitoring protocol for single-tooth implants using implant threads as magnification reference and pre-extraction baseline
- Soft tissue measurement methodology from standardized reference points, extending immediate-placement evidence beyond survival/integration to esthetic measures

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective randomized controlled trial
- **Cohort**: 76 patients recruited and randomized; 55 completed 2-year follow-up
- **Groups**:
  - **Group 1 (Delayed)**: Tooth extraction + immediate socket grafting, 4-month healing, then implant placement + immediate provisionalization
  - **Group 2 (Immediate)**: Immediate implant placement + provisionalization at time of extraction
- **Sites**: Maxillary anterior teeth (premolar, canine, lateral incisor, central incisor)
- **Follow-up**: Standardized digital radiographs every 6 months from baseline to 2 years restored
- **Primary Endpoint**: Crestal bone levels on standardized radiographs
- **Secondary Endpoints**: Soft tissue measures (facial gingival margin position, interdental bone movement) from standardized reference points
- **Radiographic Standardization**: Implant threads used as magnification monitor; pre-extraction baseline for dimensional reference

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Immediate Group | Delayed Group | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant integration (osseointegration) | No difference | Baseline comparison | P > .05 |
| Crestal bone movement | First 6 months: shift from baseline; stable thereafter | First 6 months: shift from baseline; stable thereafter | P > .05 |
| Adjacent-tooth crestal bone movement | No difference | Baseline comparison | P > .05 |
| Facial gingival margin position | Preserved position | Apical migration | **P < .05** |
| **Gingival margin preservation** | **+1 mm vs. delayed** | Reference (baseline) | **Significant** |

- **Attrition**: 21 patients lost to follow-up
  - Implant loss: n=5
  - Labial bone loss (treated out of protocol): n=1
  - Geographic relocation: n=11
  - Noncompliance: n=3
  - Medical problems: n=1
- Crestal bone levels did **not differ** between groups on either the implant or adjacent tooth (P > .05)
- **No differences** in group × tooth location × time interactions (three-way ANOVA)
- **Significant soft tissue finding**: Immediate group facial gingival margin position **more coronal by ~1 mm** vs. delayed group (the study's key clinical distinction)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=55 completers); subject to dropout (27% loss) due to relocation and noncompliance
- Single-tooth restoration outcome; findings may not generalize to multi-tooth cases or full-arch protocols
- Short radiographic follow-up period (2 years); long-term (5-10 year) crestal bone stability and soft tissue changes unknown
- Standardized radiographic technique mitigates but does not eliminate parallax error in two-dimensional crestal bone measurement
- Provisional restoration material and design specifications not explicitly detailed; variation in ovate pontic anatomy could influence soft tissue outcomes
- No radiographic bone loss data in the abstract; full-text measurements would clarify magnitude of first-6-month bone change

## 7. Glossary
- **Immediate implant placement (IIP)**: Implant inserted into extraction socket at time of tooth removal (Type 1 immediate placement in Hammerle's classification)
- **Immediate provisionalization**: Temporary fixed prosthesis fabricated and placed on the implant at or very soon after placement (within days) — distinct from immediate loading (which implies functional load)
- **Crestal bone level (CBL)**: Marginal bone height at the implant-abutment junction, measured radiographically; baseline set from pre-extraction tooth reference
- **Facial gingival margin**: The position (coronal-apical) of the visible soft tissue contour on the buccal/facial aspect
- **Biological width**: The soft tissue (sulcus + junctional epithelium + connective tissue) dimension at implants; ~3-4 mm at teeth, similar at implants
- **Socket grafting / alveolar ridge preservation (ARP)**: Bone substitute material placed in extraction socket to minimize post-extraction dimensional ridge resorption (Group 1 adjunct before delayed placement)
