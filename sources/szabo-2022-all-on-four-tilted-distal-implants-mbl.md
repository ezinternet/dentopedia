## Why Ingested

상악 풀아치 임플란트 계획 관련 상담 중 경사 임플란트의 변연골 소실 패턴이 핵심 임상 질문으로 부상. 기존 [[wiki/overviews/tilted-axial-implant-angled-abutment-overview]]는 FEA/SR 데이터 중심이며, 3.5년 장기 임상 추적에서 경사vs축방향 MBL 비교 실측 데이터가 부족했음. 이 회고 연구가 개인 임플란트 레벨 방사선 측정치로 그 공백을 채움.

## Three-line Summary

3.5-year single-center retrospective (36 patients, 288 All-on-Four implants: 144 maxilla + 144 mandible) with individual implant-level OPT measurements at baseline, 18, 30, and 42 months post-restoration.

Tilted distal implants lost significantly more bone than axial anterior implants at every time point; by 3.5 years, maxilla MBL averaged 0.770±0.029 mm and mandible 0.713±0.026 mm; 100% implant survival; smoking + underlying conditions amplified bone loss at specific individual implant positions (P<0.05).

Tilted implants carry a measurable MBL premium in All-on-Four — disto-approximal surface of posterior implants (tooth positions 14DA, 24DA) shows the highest cumulative resorption; clinically the absolute values remain within acceptable range if hygiene is maintained.

## 세줄요약

3.5년 단일기관 회고 연구(36명, 288개 All-on-Four 임플란트): 상·하악 각 144개, 3개월 기준시점 및 18·30·42개월 추적 OPT 방사선 측정.

경사 원심 임플란트가 전방 축방향 임플란트보다 모든 추적 시점에서 MBL(변연골소실, Marginal Bone Loss) 유의하게 큼; 3.5년 후 상악 평균 0.770 mm, 하악 0.713 mm; 생존율 100%; 흡연·전신질환이 특정 임플란트 위치의 골소실을 유의하게 악화(P<.05).

경사 임플란트는 축방향 대비 MBL 프리미엄 존재 — 상악 후방 임플란트 원심면(14DA·24DA)이 최고; 절대치는 허용 범위 내이나 구강위생 불량·흡연 시 임상적으로 유의미한 추가 소실 발생.

## 1. Document Information

- **Study type**: Single-center retrospective radiographic study
- **Follow-up**: 3.5 years post-restoration (42 months)
- **Sample**: 36 patients (24M/12F), 288 implants (Nobel Biocare), mean age 58.75 years
- **Journal**: Dentistry Journal (MDPI), 2022

## 2. Key Contributions

- Individual implant-level OPT analysis across all 4 implant positions in the maxilla and mandible
- Documents that tilted implants have significantly higher MBL than axial — contrasts with SR/MA literature that shows no significant difference (likely because SRs pool underpowered studies)
- Identifies tooth 14DA and 24DA (distal surface of tilted posterior maxillary implants) as highest-risk positions
- Shows saturation kinetics: rapid early bone loss then flattening — consistent with Hürzeler and Widmark

## 3. Methodology

- 4 implants per patient (2 axial anterior + 2 distally tilted posterior), following Nobel All-on-Four protocol
- Tilted implants placed anterior to maxillary sinus / mental foramen
- Immediate loading with provisional acrylic; definitive: milled titanium frame + heat-cured acrylic resin
- Baseline defined at 3-month post-restoration; ΔBL calculated from baseline to T1(18m), T2(30m), T3(42m)
- Calibrated OPT at 640×480px; CLINIVIEW analysis; calibrated to known implant dimensions
- Independent blinded researcher for all measurements

## 4. Key Results

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Overall survival | 100% |
| Maxilla MBL at 3.5yr | 0.770±0.029 mm |
| Mandible MBL at 3.5yr | 0.713±0.026 mm |
| Axial vs tilted | Tilted significantly higher (all time points) |
| Maxilla vs mandible | No significant difference |
| MA vs DA | No significant difference overall |
| Highest individual position | 24DA: −1.066±0.081 mm at T3 |

- Smokers/comorbidities: significantly higher bone loss at positions 14MA, 24DA, 44DA (P<.05)
- No significant linear correlation between age and bone resorption

## 5. Limitations

- Single-center, convenience sample (n=36 patients)
- 2D OPT cannot capture 3D bone changes
- Relatively short follow-up (3.5 yr post-restoration)
- Smoking group small (n=3 in maxilla, n=3 mandible subgroup) — underpowered for subgroup analyses

## 6. Related Work

- Maló et al.: 98.2% cumulative survival — comparable
- Durkan et al. SR: axial 0.34–1.14 mm, angled 0.43–1.13 mm (no difference) — contrasts with this study's finding of tilted>axial
- Hürzeler et al.: 1.5 mm in year 1 then 0.2 mm/yr thereafter — consistent kinetics

## 7. Glossary

- **ΔBL**: Change in bone level from baseline to follow-up (mm)
- **MA/DA**: Mesio-approximal / Disto-approximal surfaces
- **OPT**: Orthopantomogram (panoramic radiograph)
- **Saturation kinetics**: Rapid initial bone resorption followed by plateau
