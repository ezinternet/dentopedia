## Three-line Summary

Narrative mini-review (Frontiers in Oral Health 2022, two oral-medicine specialists) of oral chronic graft-versus-host disease (cGVHD), covering its three clinical entities — lichenoid mucositis, immune-mediated salivary dysfunction, and tissue sclerosis/trismus — in allogeneic HCT recipients.

Oral cGVHD develops in 30–50% of alloHCT recipients (>90% within 12 months); diagnosis applies NIH 2014 criteria; mucosal ulceration raises bacteremia and secondary-malignancy risk; management escalates from topical corticosteroid rinses to systemic immunosuppression.

Oral cGVHD is a leading cause of late post-HCT morbidity and constitutes an OPMD; evidence for specific topical regimens is limited, so management relies on expert consensus rather than high-quality trials.

## 세줄요약

내러티브 미니리뷰(Frontiers in Oral Health 2022, 구강내과 전문의 2명): 동종 조혈모세포이식(Allogeneic Hematopoietic Cell Transplantation, alloHCT) 수혜자에서 나타나는 구강 만성 이식편대숙주병(Chronic Graft-Versus-Host Disease, cGVHD) — 태선양 점막염·면역 매개 타액선 기능 장애·조직 경화/개구 제한 세 가지 임상 형태 정리.

alloHCT 수혜자의 30–50%에서 구강 cGVHD 발생(>90%는 12개월 이내); NIH 2014 진단 기준 적용; 점막 궤양은 균혈증·이차 악성 종양 위험 증가; 치료는 국소 스테로이드 구강세척제에서 전신 면역억제제로 단계적 상향 조정.

구강 cGVHD는 이식 후 후기 이환율의 주요 원인이자 잠재악성질환(Oral Potentially Malignant Disorder, OPMD)에 해당; 특정 국소 요법에 대한 근거는 제한적이어서 관리는 전문가 합의에 의존.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates the three discrete oral cGVHD entities: lichenoid mucositis, immune-mediated salivary dysfunction, and tissue sclerosis/trismus.
- Summarizes NIH 2014 diagnostic/staging criteria for the mouth.
- Maps topical (corticosteroid rinses) to systemic immunosuppressive therapy with adverse-effect trade-offs.
- Flags cGVHD as a leading cause of late post-HCT morbidity/mortality and a malignant-transformation risk site.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Narrative literature review by two oral-medicine specialists; no quantitative pooling.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- cGVHD diagnosed in 30-50% of alloHCT recipients, >90% within 12 months.
- Lichenoid mucositis resembles OLP; salivary dysfunction resembles Sjögren/sicca; sclerosis causes trismus analogous to scleroderma.
- Mucosal ulceration raises oral-sourced bacteremia and secondary-malignancy risk; topical corticosteroids first line, escalating to systemic immunosuppression.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative, non-systematic; pathophysiology of trismus/sclerosis poorly understood; limited high-level evidence for specific topical regimens.

## 6. Related Work

- NIH 2005/2014 Consensus on cGVHD diagnosis and staging.
- Literature on oral-mucosal cGVHD and salivary dysfunction.

## 7. Glossary

- cGVHD: chronic Graft-Versus-Host Disease.
- alloHCT: allogeneic Hematopoietic Cell Transplantation.
- Trismus: restricted mouth opening.
