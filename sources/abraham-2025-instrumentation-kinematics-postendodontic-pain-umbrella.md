## Why Ingested

[[wiki/endodontics/zamparini-2026-postop-pain-endodontic-instrumentation-irrigation-obturation-umbrella]] 등 같은 계열의 최신 umbrella review이기도 하다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42024582245) of 8 systematic reviews (7 with meta-analytic data, published 2018-2022) synthesizing the effect of root canal instrumentation kinematics — rotary vs reciprocating vs manual — on post-endodontic pain (POP), quality-appraised with AMSTAR-2 (all "high quality," 12.5-15/16) and ROBIS.

Rotary instrumentation was generally associated with lower overall POP and better moderate-to-severe pain reduction (e.g., Hou XM 2017 OR 2.24-11.71 vs manual/reciprocating; Sun C 2018 RR 0.32-0.73), attributed to reduced apical debris extrusion, while reciprocating systems showed a time-dependent advantage reported for mild pain in the immediate (~24h) phase per the abstract — though the Discussion's own pooled figures (SMD 0.25 favoring rotary at 24h; RR 1.04 favoring reciprocating at 48h) sit awkwardly against that abstract claim, an internal inconsistency reproduced as reported.

Clinical takeaway is explicitly multifactorial (no single system universally superior); major limitations include unreported/uncontrolled operator confounders across all 8 included SRs, heterogeneous VAS-based pain assessment, assessment windows capped at ≤72h, and a garbled PRISMA-flow record count in the paper's own Methods/Results (400,255 records → 102 remaining → "50 records assessed for eligibility," an unreconciled discrepancy).

## 세줄요약

우산연구(Umbrella Review, PROSPERO CRD42024582245) — 근관형성기구 운동학(Instrumentation Kinematics: 회전형 rotary vs 왕복형 reciprocating vs 수동)이 근관치료 후 통증(Post-endodontic Pain, PEP)에 미치는 영향을 다룬 SR 8편(7편 메타분석 포함, 2018-2022년 발표)을 AMSTAR-2(전부 고품질, 12.5-15/16)·ROBIS로 통합.

회전형(rotary)이 전반적 통증과 중등도~중증 통증 감소에서 더 우수(Hou XM 2017 OR 2.24-11.71, Sun C 2018 RR 0.32-0.73) — 근단 밖 잔사 압출(apical debris extrusion) 감소가 기전으로 제시되며, 왕복형(reciprocating)은 초록상 술후 24시간 이내 경도 통증에서 우위로 서술되나 본문의 실제 수치(24시간 SMD 0.25 rotary 우위, 48시간 RR 1.04 reciprocating 우위)는 초록 서술과 다소 어긋나는 내부 불일치가 있음(그대로 반영).

임상 결론은 명시적으로 다인자성(단일 기구가 모든 지표에서 우월하지 않음) — 8편 SR 전체에서 시술자 변수(operator confounder)가 사실상 미보고/미통제된 점, VAS 기반 통증평가의 이질성, ≤72시간으로 제한된 평가창, PRISMA flow 기록수치 자체의 내부 불일치(400,255건→102건 잔존→"50건 적격성 평가"로 이어지는 미해소 산술 오류)가 핵심 한계.

- **Authors**: Dax Abraham, Aakansha Puri, Alpa Gupta, Karina Singal, Adinath Walmik Talekar, Praharsha Anabathula — Department of Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics, Manav Rachna Dental College, Manav Rachna International Institute of Research and Studies, Faridabad, Haryana, India
- **Journal**: Journal of Oral Biology and Craniofacial Research, 2025, Vol 15, Issue 4, pp 888-898
- **PMID**: 40586107 | **PMCID**: PMC12206036 | **DOI**: [10.1016/j.jobcr.2025.06.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jobcr.2025.06.008)
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD42024582245
- **Study type**: Umbrella review (of SRs) with AMSTAR-2 quality stratification, ROBIS risk-of-bias assessment, and sensitivity analysis

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella-review-level synthesis focused specifically on instrumentation *kinematics* (rotary vs reciprocating vs manual) and post-endodontic pain, distinct from broader postop-pain umbrella reviews that also cover irrigation/obturation.
- Applies a formal evidence-stratification framework (statistical significance × heterogeneity × study quality) plus sensitivity analyses (subgroup by pain level/time interval, meta-regression) on top of the underlying SR data.
- Explicit four-domain confounder analysis (patient/procedure/operator/methodological) with a 0-2 scoring heat-matrix, highlighting operator-related reporting as the weakest domain across all 8 included SRs.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Search**: 6 databases (PubMed, Scopus, EBSCOhost, LILACS, Epistemonikos, Google Scholar), 2000 to March 2025, English-language SRs (with/without MA) plus RCTs comparing rotary vs reciprocating POP outcomes (mild/moderate/severe pain grading).
- **Screening**: 2 independent reviewers (title/abstract, then full text) + 3rd reviewer for disagreements; corresponding authors of included reviews contacted for missing data.
- **Quality**: AMSTAR-2 (16-item checklist, 3 independent raters + 4th arbiter) → categorized high/moderate/low/critically-low.
- **Bias**: ROBIS (3 phases: relevance, review-process concerns across 4 domains, overall bias judgment).
- **Evidence grading**: umbrella-review evidence-classification framework (per cited prior meta-analyses), with sensitivity analyses (exclusion of pre-op-pain studies, subgroup by pain nature/time interval, random-effects meta-regression) applied where the underlying SR data allowed.
- Note: the paper's own PRISMA-flow numbers contain an internal arithmetic inconsistency (400,255 records → 400,003 duplicates + 50 + 100 excluded = 102 remaining for full-text review, but the next sentence states "50 records were assessed for eligibility") — reproduced as-is in [[wiki/endodontics/shaping/abraham-2025-instrumentation-kinematics-postendodontic-pain-umbrella]] rather than silently corrected.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **8 SRs included** (7 with meta-analysis), published 2018-2022 in BMC Oral Health (n=2), Iranian Endodontic Journal (n=2), European Endodontic Journal (n=1), Oral Diseases (n=1), Journal of International Oral Health (n=1), Journal of Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics (n=1).
- **AMSTAR-2**: all 8 SRs scored 12.5-15/16 → uniformly "high quality."
- **Per-SR findings** (see full text for details): da Silveira MT 2021 rotary favored (SMD 0.27-0.37, P≤0.001); Sun C 2018 rotary favored vs manual (RR 0.32) and vs reciprocating (RR 0.73); Hou XM 2017 rotary favored for moderate/severe pain (OR 2.24-11.71); Neto JCL reciprocating favored at 48h (RR 1.04) but rotary favored at 24h (SMD 0.25); Nobar BR 2021, Antony DP 2022, and Martins CM 2019 found no significant rotary-vs-reciprocating difference; Cunha TC found continuous-rotation glide path (CR-GP) superior to manual glide path, ≈ reciprocating glide path (R-GP).
- **Pooled discussion-level statistics**: 24h — reciprocating shows elevated pain vs rotary (SMD 0.25, 95% CI 0.06-0.44); 48h — reciprocating shows a therapeutic advantage (RR 1.04, 95% CI 1.01-1.06).
- **Bottom line** (Abstract): rotary generally lower overall POP; reciprocating may reduce mild pain in the immediate (~24h) phase — this framing is not fully reconciled with the 24h SMD figure above (see Limitations).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Authors' own stated limitations: high methodological heterogeneity precluding robust meta-analytic pooling across the 8 SRs; moderate-to-high bias risk in underlying primary studies; subjective VAS-based pain assessment; assessment windows capped at ≤72h (misses longer-term recovery); pervasive, largely unreported operator confounders (none of the 8 SRs documented number of operators, experience level, magnification use, or torque/speed protocol adherence); insufficient conflict-of-interest disclosure raising publication-bias concern.
- Additional issue we flag (not resolved by the authors): apparent internal inconsistency between the Abstract's "reciprocating reduces mild pain within 24h" claim and the Discussion's own 24h-SMD figure favoring rotary; and an unreconciled PRISMA-flow record count.
- Authors call for standardized methodology, homogeneous outcome measures, and extended longitudinal follow-up in future primary/secondary research.

## 7. Glossary

- **PEP / POP**: Post-endodontic pain / post-operative pain.
- **Rotary instrumentation**: continuous clockwise NiTi rotary motion.
- **Reciprocating instrumentation**: alternating counter-clockwise/clockwise motion (e.g., WaveOne, Reciproc).
- **CR-GP / M-GP / WGP / R-GP**: Continuous-rotation glide path / manual glide path / (reciprocating) WaveOne glide path / reciprocating glide path.
- **AMSTAR-2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews (2nd version), 16-item SR quality checklist.
- **ROBIS**: Risk Of Bias In Systematic reviews tool.
- **PRIRES**: Preferred Reporting Items for Resistance Exercise Studies checklist, used here (per the paper) as the umbrella-review reporting checklist.
- **SMD / RR / OR / MD**: Standardized mean difference / risk ratio / odds ratio / mean difference.
