## Three-line Summary

In-vitro controlled study (BMC Oral Health 2025;25:753; Sun Yat-sen University; resin edentulous mandibular master model, 6 parallel implants, n=10 per group): four impression technique groups — conventional splinted open-tray (CON), IOS (TRIOS3), PG-1 (Icam4D), PG-2 (PIC); trueness and precision evaluated by reverse engineering software (3D linearity, angularity, RMS).

For trueness (RMS, µm): PG-1 10.87, PG-2 13.35, CON 40.50, IOS 91.75; for precision (RMS, µm): PG-1 3.7, PG-2 4.6, CON 36.55, IOS 82.8; angularity trueness: PG 0.12-0.14°, CON 0.35°, IOS 0.52°; all inter-group differences significant (p<0.01 or p<0.05).

Both photogrammetry systems (Icam4D and PIC) showed significantly superior trueness and precision over conventional and IOS for 6-implant full-arch; conventional outperformed IOS; IOS (TRIOS3) showed poorest accuracy for multi-implant full-arch rehabilitation.

## 세줄요약

In-vitro 대조 연구 (BMC Oral Health 2025;25:753; 쑨야트센 대학교; 수지 무치악 하악 마스터 모델, 6개 평행 임플란트, 군당 n=10): 4가지 인상법 군 — 전통 스플린트 개방형 트레이 (CON), 구강내 스캐닝 (Intraoral Scanning, IOS) (TRIOS3), 광측정술 1 (PG-1; Icam4D), 광측정술 2 (PG-2; PIC); 역공학 소프트웨어로 3D 선형·각도·균방평균제곱근 (Root Mean Square, RMS) 편차 평가.

진실도 (RMS, µm): PG-1 10.87, PG-2 13.35, CON 40.50, IOS 91.75; 정밀도 (RMS, µm): PG-1 3.7, PG-2 4.6, CON 36.55, IOS 82.8; 각도 진실도: PG 0.12-0.14°, CON 0.35°, IOS 0.52°; 군간 차이 모두 유의 (p<0.01 또는 p<0.05).

두 광측정술 시스템 (Icam4D·PIC)이 전통 인상·IOS 대비 진실도·정밀도 모두 유의하게 우위; 전통 인상이 IOS보다 우수; IOS (TRIOS3)는 6임플란트 전악에서 정확도 가장 낮음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First direct head-to-head comparison of two photogrammetry systems (Icam4D vs PIC) vs IOS (TRIOS3) vs conventional for 6-implant full-arch mandible
- Icam4D PG: RMS trueness 10.87 µm (best); IOS: 91.75 µm (worst) — 8× difference
- Precision also superior for PG: RMS 3.7 µm vs IOS 82.8 µm (22× difference)
- Conventional technique intermediate: RMS trueness 40.50 µm (better than IOS, worse than PG)
- Both PG systems showed equivalent accuracy (Icam4D vs PIC, p>0.05)
- Null hypothesis rejected: significant differences exist among all four impression techniques

## 3. Methodology
- **Model**: Resin edentulous mandibular master model, 6 parallel implants, screw-retained abutments
- **Groups** (n=10 each): CON (conventional splinted open-tray), IOS (TRIOS3), PG-1 (Icam4D), PG-2 (PIC)
- **Reference standard**: Laboratory scanner (master model) + CON group digitized with same lab scanner
- **Analysis**: STL file export → reverse engineering software → 3D linearity (X, Y, Z axes), angularity, RMS deviations
- **Statistics**: Kruskal-Wallis + post-hoc pairwise comparison; significance p<0.05

## 4. Key Results

### Trueness (vs master model reference)
| Group | Linear 3D (µm) | Angularity (°) | RMS (µm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PG-1 (Icam4D) | 25.23 | 0.12 | **10.87** |
| PG-2 (PIC) | 28.15 | 0.14 | **13.35** |
| CON | 66.05 | 0.35 | 40.50 |
| IOS (TRIOS3) | 78.58 | 0.52 | 91.75 |

### Precision (repeatability within group)
| Group | Linear (µm) | Angularity (°) | RMS (µm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PG-1 (Icam4D) | 15.80 | 0.10 | **3.7** |
| PG-2 (PIC) | 17.78 | 0.13 | **4.6** |
| CON | 39.32 | 0.24 | 36.55 |
| IOS (TRIOS3) | 45.33 | 0.38 | 82.8 |

- Significant differences in X, Y-axis, 3D linearity, angularity, RMS (p<0.01) for trueness
- Significant differences in X, Y-axis, 3D linearity, XZ-plane, RMS (p<0.05) for precision
- PG-1 vs PG-2: no significant difference (equivalent systems)

## 5. Limitations
- In-vitro only — clinical conditions (soft tissue, saliva, patient movement) not modeled
- Single master model (mandibular, 6 parallel implants) — does not cover tilted implants or maxillary arch
- Conventional group digitized by lab scanner (not by operator impression casting), which may introduce reference bias
- No passive fit measurement of final prosthesis — accuracy of impression ≠ passive fit of framework
