## Three-line Summary
Prospective study (16 patients, 70 implants, 7-year follow-up in Japan): UV-photofunctionalization (UVmediated surface activation, 15 min pre-placement) applied across three bone-quality scenarios: regular, complex/atrophic (severe ridge loss requiring GBR or sinus augmentation), and cancer-resection sites.

UV-PF achieved 100% implant success in regular cases (n=30) and in complex atrophic cases requiring GBR/sinus (n=21), but only 22.2% in cancer-resection sites (n=15); ISQ increased markedly in complex cases (+21.9 points) but declined in cancer bone (−3.5), indicating bone quality is decisive.

Clinical implication: UV-PF enhances secondary osseointegration in anatomically compromised bone, but cannot overcome pathophysiological damage from cancer resection or prior radiation; ISQ recovery reflects bone regeneration capacity, not surface chemistry alone.

## 세줄요약
전향적 임상 연구: 16명 환자, 70개 임플란트, 7년 추적 (일본 요코하마시립대 + UCLA); 세 가지 골질 시나리오 별 자외선 광기능화(UV-Photofunctionalization, UVmediated surface activation, 15분 식립 전 처리) 효과 평가.

UV-PF는 일반 케이스(n=30) 100% 성공, 복잡하고 심한 치조제 위축(골재생·상악동거상 필요, n=21) 케이스 100% 성공 달성했으나, 암 절제 부위(n=15)는 22.2% 성공만 기록; 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ) 변화는 복잡 케이스에서 +21.9로 명확한 증가, 암 부위에서 −3.5로 감소.

UV-PF는 해부학적으로 손상된 골의 재원소화를 촉진하고 ISQ 향상을 달성하나, 암 절제나 방사선 조사 같은 생리병리적 손상은 표면화학처리로 극복 불가능 — ISQ 회복은 표면만이 아닌 골재생 용량을 반영.

## 1. Document Information
J Clin Med 2020;9:2733. doi:10.3390/jcm9092733. Yokohama City University + UCLA. Single-center prospective.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Longest follow-up (7 years) for UV-photofunctionalized implants
- Demonstrates UV-PF achieves 100% success in regular AND anatomically complex (severely atrophied) cases
- Cancer-resection bone: significantly lower success (22.2%) — UV-PF cannot overcome pathophysiological compromise
- ISQ increase of 21.9 points in complex cases confirms accelerated secondary osseointegration

## 3. Methodology
16 patients, 70 implants (4 submerged). UV irradiation 15 min prior to placement. Groups: regular (n=30), complex (n=21, severely atrophied requiring GBR/sinus), cancer (n=15, resection/radiation sites). ISQ at placement and 2nd-stage surgery.

## 4. Key Results
| Group | n | 7-yr success | ISQ change |
|-------|---|-------------|------------|
| Regular | 30 | 100% | +3.2 |
| Complex (atrophied) | 21 | 100% | +21.9 |
| Cancer resection | 15 | 22.2% | −3.5 |
- Multivariate: bone quality, location, cancer resection = significant failure predictors

## 5. Limitations
Single center; small n; no randomized control (no non-UV group); cancer group heterogeneous (radiation ± resection).

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary
