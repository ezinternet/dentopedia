## Why Ingested

Provides a direct preclinical comparison of two collagen-containing bovine xenografts — the widely used Bio-Oss Collagen® (~90% HA / ~10% collagen) versus the less-characterized Extra Graft XG13® (~75% HA / ~25% collagen) — in a standardized GBR rat calvarial model with three sequential time points (7, 14, 28 days), addressing a gap in healing-dynamics evidence for XG13®. Extends the animal-model comparison tradition established by [[wiki/bone-regeneration/manfro-2013-bovine-bone-substitutes-comparative-histomorphometric]] to collagen-fraction as a potentially relevant variable in xenograft performance.

## Three-line Summary

Animal study (n=54 Wistar rats; 3 groups × 3 time points, n=6/group/time: blood clot control, Bio-Oss Collagen®, Extra Graft XG13®) using a standardized 5-mm critical-size calvarial defect under resorbable collagen membrane GBR conditions; sacrifice at 7, 14, and 28 days.

Despite comparable early inflammatory and angiogenic responses between biomaterials, Extra Graft XG13® showed significantly greater new bone formation (histomorphometry, p<0.05) and higher mineralized tissue volume/fraction (micro-CT, p<0.0001 at 14d; p<0.05 at 28d), lower porosity, and higher proportion of mature collagen fibers at 28 days (p<0.01) compared to Bio-Oss Collagen®.

Bio-Oss Collagen® showed a slower but more sustained formation pattern; micro-CT could not differentiate new bone from residual biomaterial, and the 28-day model does not capture long-term remodeling — clinical translation requires further study.

## 세줄요약

동물실험(위스터 쥐 54마리; 3군×3시점, n=6/군/시점): 혈병 대조군·Bio-Oss Collagen®·Extra Graft XG13® 세 군에 5mm 두개관 결손부를 흡수성 콜라겐막으로 차폐한 유도골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) 조건에서 7일·14일·28일 희생.

두 이종골 대체재 간 초기 염증·혈관신생 반응은 차이 없었으나, Extra Graft XG13®은 14일·28일 조직형태계측학적 신생골 형성(p<0.05), 마이크로-CT 광화 조직 부피·부피분율(14일 p<0.0001; 28일 p<0.05), 공극률 감소, 28일 성숙 콜라겐 비율(p<0.01)에서 Bio-Oss Collagen®보다 우월.

Bio-Oss Collagen®는 완만하고 지속적인 형성 패턴을 보였고, 마이크로-CT는 신생골과 잔류 이식재를 구별할 수 없으며 28일 모델은 장기 재형성을 반영하지 않아 임상 적용을 위해서는 추가 연구가 필요함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First direct in vivo comparison of Bio-Oss Collagen® (~90% HA / ~10% collagen) versus Extra Graft XG13® (~75% HA / ~25% collagen) in a rat calvarial critical-size defect GBR model with sequential 7/14/28-day time points
- Demonstrates that comparable early inflammatory and angiogenic responses do not translate to equivalent bone formation — Extra Graft XG13® produced significantly greater mineralized tissue volume and volume fraction at 14 and 28 days despite similar biological milieu
- Characterizes time-dependent collagen maturation dynamics: Extra Graft XG13® had more immature collagen at 14 days but showed greater mature collagen at 28 days (p<0.01), suggesting a shift from deposition to remodeling phase
- Identifies a potential reduction in BV from 14→28 days in Extra Graft XG13® group accompanied by increased collagen organization, raising (but not confirming) a remodeling-transition hypothesis
- Shows that two DBBM/collagen products with similar clinical indications exhibit distinct time-dependent healing dynamics, reinforcing the finding that shared category ≠ equivalent performance

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: In vivo animal experimental study (rat calvarial critical-size defect); conducted under ARRIVE guidelines; CEUA-approved (protocol 707/2024, UNESP)
- **Model**: 5-mm diameter bicortical defect in Wistar rat calvaria; all defects covered with Green Membrane® (resorbable Type I collagen; Regener, Brazil)
- **n**: 54 adult male Wistar rats (300–350 g); n=6/group/time point
- **Groups**: Blood clot (control), Bio-Oss Collagen® (Geistlich, Switzerland: ~90% HA, ~10% collagen), Extra Graft XG13® (Implacil, Brazil: ~75% HA, ~25% collagen per manufacturer)
- **Time points**: 7, 14, 28 days
- **Outcomes**:
  - Inflammatory cells and blood vessel counts (point-counting, 100× magnification, 130-point grid, 3 regions)
  - New bone formation (histomorphometry, % defect area, H&E)
  - Micro-CT (Skyscan 1172, voxel 11.87 μm): BV (mm³), BV/TV (%), Tb.N (1/mm), Tb.Th (mm), Tb.Sp (mm), total porosity (%); assessed at 14 and 28 days only
  - Collagen maturation: Picrosirius Red under polarized light (immature/green, intermediate/yellow, mature/red; %)
- **Statistics**: Two-way ANOVA (group × time), Tukey post hoc, partial eta-squared (η²p); significance p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Inflammatory response** (7d: both biomaterials vs control p<0.0001; no between-biomaterial difference; η²p group=0.96, time=0.95, interaction=0.67)

**Angiogenesis** (7d and 14d: both biomaterials vs control p≤0.002; no significant group×time interaction, η²p=0.17; no significant between-biomaterial differences)

**New bone formation (histomorphometry)**:
- Extra Graft XG13® > Bio-Oss Collagen® at 14d and 28d (p<0.05)
- Bio-Oss Collagen®: gradual, intermediate pattern; Control: lowest at all time points
- Group×time interaction η²p=0.81 (strong)

**Micro-CT (mineralized tissue — BV and BV/TV)**:
- 14d: Extra Graft XG13® vs Bio-Oss Collagen® p<0.01; vs control p<0.0001
- 28d: Extra Graft XG13® vs Bio-Oss Collagen® and control p<0.05
- BV group×time: p=0.0007, η²p=0.38; BV/TV group×time: p=0.0013, η²p=0.36
- Reduction in BV from 14→28d observed in Extra Graft XG13® group (caution: micro-CT cannot distinguish new bone from residual biomaterial)

**Micro-CT porosity**:
- Extra Graft XG13® < Bio-Oss Collagen® and control at 14d (p<0.0001) and 28d (p<0.05)

**Trabecular density (Tb.N)**:
- Extra Graft XG13® > Bio-Oss Collagen® at 14d (p<0.01); difference reduced at 28d

**Collagen maturation (Picrosirius Red)**:
- 7d: both groups predominantly immature; no significant difference
- 14d: Extra Graft XG13® more immature collagen (p<0.05); Bio-Oss Collagen® more mature (p<0.05)
- 28d: Extra Graft XG13® significantly higher mature collagen vs Bio-Oss Collagen® (p<0.01); group×time interaction: mature η²p=0.34, immature η²p=0.41
- Note: Picrosirius Red does not distinguish biomaterial-derived from newly synthesized collagen

| Extra Graft XG13® | ~75% | ~25% | Bovine, Implacil (Brazil) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No independent physicochemical characterization of biomaterials; composition data based on manufacturer information only — structure-function interpretation limited
- 28-day maximum observation period: early/intermediate healing phases captured but long-term remodeling and volume stability not evaluable
- Micro-CT cannot reliably differentiate newly formed bone from residual xenograft particles — reported parameters reflect total mineralized tissue
- Picrosirius Red cannot distinguish biomaterial-derived collagen from newly synthesized extracellular matrix
- No molecular analysis (gene expression, protein markers), no biomechanical testing
- Rat calvarial non-load-bearing model does not reproduce clinical biomechanical environment; findings require validation in larger animal models and clinical studies
- Extra Graft XG13® is a Brazilian commercial product (Implacil) — limited independent published data on this material

## 7. Glossary

- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 유도골재생)**: Technique using barrier membranes to prevent soft tissue ingrowth and allow bone formation in a protected space
- **Critical-size defect (임계크기 결손부)**: Defect that does not spontaneously heal within the observation period; rat calvarial 5-mm diameter is standard
- **BV/TV (Bone Volume / Total Volume)**: Micro-CT parameter expressing fraction of mineralized tissue relative to total defect volume; here reflects both new bone and residual graft
- **Picrosirius Red (피크로시리우스 레드)**: Histochemical stain that reveals collagen fiber organization under polarized light — immature (green), mature (red/yellow birefringence)
- **Tb.N (Trabecular Number)**: Number of trabeculae per unit length; higher values indicate denser mineralized structure
- **DBBM (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, 탈단백 우골 미네랄)**: Anorganic bovine bone substitute from which organic components have been removed; Bio-Oss is the archetype; Bio-Oss Collagen® and Extra Graft XG13® are collagen-added variants
