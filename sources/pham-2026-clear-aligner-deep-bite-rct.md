## Why Ingested

심부교합(deep bite)에 대한 투명교정의 임상 효능과 환자 만족도를 비교한 RCT로, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/charoenrat-2025-clear-aligner-anterior-open-bite-molar-intrusion-sr-ma]]가 다룬 개방교합과 대비되는 수직부조화(심부교합) 쪽 투명교정 적용 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

This RCT compared clear aligner therapy (CAT) vs conventional fixed appliances for deep bite malocclusion treatment at a Vietnamese national hospital. CAT demonstrated significantly shorter treatment duration (20.77 ± 6.43 months vs 27.47 ± 4.81 months, P<.001) with comparable overbite improvement (P=.461) and overjet reduction (P=.603). Patient satisfaction was significantly higher in the CAT group across all categories (P<.001), while fixed appliances showed greater impact on vertical facial dimensions.

## 세줄요약

심부교합 환자에서 투명교정(CAT) 대 고정교정의 임상 효능과 환자 만족도를 비교한 베트남 국립병원 RCT. CAT는 유의하게 짧은 치료 기간(20.77±6.43개월 대 27.47±4.81개월, P<.001)을 보였으며, 심부교합 개선(P=.461)과 양악 겹침 감소(P=.603)는 두 그룹 간 유의한 차이 없음. 환자 만족도는 모든 범주에서 CAT 그룹이 유의하게 높았음(P<.001). 고정교정은 수직 안면 치수에 더 큰 영향을 미쳤음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT specifically comparing CAT vs fixed appliances for deep bite malocclusion
- Demonstrates CAT offers shorter treatment duration with comparable clinical outcomes
- Patient satisfaction significantly higher with CAT across all measured categories
- Cephalometric analysis reveals different treatment mechanisms (vertical control vs incisor proclination)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Treatment duration: CAT 20.77 ± 6.43 mo vs Fixed 27.47 ± 4.81 mo (P<.001)
- Overbite improvement: NS between groups (P=.461)
- Overjet reduction: NS between groups (P=.603)
- Patient satisfaction: CAT significantly higher all categories (P<.001)
- Vertical dimensions: fixed appliances greater impact
- Upper incisor proclination: CAT associated

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single-center study
- Sample size not specified in abstract
- Vietnamese population may limit generalizability
- Long-term stability of deep bite correction unknown
- Cephalometric analysis has inherent 2D limitations

## 7. Glossary
- **Deep Bite**: 심부교합 (excessive vertical overlap of anterior teeth)
- **Overbite**: 심부 (vertical overlap of incisors)
- **Overjet**: 양악 겹침 (horizontal projection of upper incisors beyond lower)
- **MPA (Mandibular Plane Angle)**: 하악 평면각
- **CAT (Clear Aligner Therapy)**: 투명교정 치료
