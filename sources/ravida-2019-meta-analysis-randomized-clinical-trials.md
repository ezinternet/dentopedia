## Why Ingested

본 연구는 임플란트 길이 감소 시술의 근거 강도를 정량화한다. 기존 [[implants/short-narrow/monje-2019-extra-short-implants-review]] 등 단폭경(短幅径) 임플란트(임플란트·단폭경(짧은·좁은), short-narrow implants)에 대한 고찰과 비교하여, ≤6 mm 초단축(超短軸) 임플란트(extra-short implant)와 ≥10 mm 장축(長軸) 임플란트의 임상적·환자보고결과를 직접 대조한 무작위대조시험(RCT, randomized controlled trial) 기반 체계적문헌고찰(systematic review)과 메타분석(meta-analysis)을 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs comparing extra-short (≤6 mm) vs longer (≥10 mm) implants; included studies evaluated survival, marginal bone loss, complications, and patient-reported outcomes.  
Extra-short implants showed comparable implant survival in most scenarios but often required different protocols/augmentation considerations and demonstrated mixed results for marginal bone loss depending on context.  
Provides quantitative synthesis supporting cautious equivalence of ≤6 mm implants vs ≥10 mm implants in selected cases, informing reduced-dimension implant decisions.

## 세줄요약

RCT 기반 체계적문헌고찰·메타분석으로, 초단축(≤6 mm) 임플란트와 장축(≥10 mm) 임플란트의 생존율·변연골소실·합병증·환자보고결과를 비교했다.  
대부분의 시나리오에서 초단축 임플란트의 생존율은 장축 임플란트와 유사했으나, 변연골소실 및 필요 시 골증대와 관련된 결과는 문맥에 따라 상이했다.  
선택된 증례에서 ≤6 mm 임플란트는 ≥10 mm 임플란트와 동등한 결과를 보일 수 있음을 정량적으로 제시하며, 단축 임플란트 결정에 근거를 제공한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantitative comparison of extra-short (≤6 mm) vs longer (≥10 mm) implants using only RCT evidence where feasible.
- Synthesized clinical outcomes (implant survival, marginal bone loss, technical/biological complications) and patient-reported outcomes.
- Evaluated context-dependent differences (with/without bone augmentation, prosthetic factors) affecting outcomes.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Systematic review following established methodology; included randomized clinical trials comparing ≤6 mm extra-short implants vs ≥10 mm long implants.
- Meta-analyses performed for outcomes with sufficient homogenous data; heterogeneity assessed.
- Subgroup/consideration of augmentation protocols and study design features.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Implant survival:** Generally comparable between extra-short (≤6 mm) and longer (≥10 mm) implants in included RCTs under evaluated conditions.
- **Marginal bone loss (변연골소실, marginal bone loss):** Results varied; differences observed in some analyses depending on protocol/augmentation.
- **Complications/PROs:** Reported; synthesis indicates acceptable outcomes in selected indications but not universally equivalent across all scenarios.
- **Conclusion (summary):** Placement of extra-short implants (≤6 mm) can be equivalent to longer implants (≥10 mm) in carefully selected cases, though clinical decision-making must account for anatomical/prosthetic factors.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Limited number of RCTs for some outcomes and heterogeneity across protocols (augmentation strategies, prostheses).
- Patient-reported outcome data were limited relative to survival/MBL.
- Long-term data beyond study durations warrant further RCT evidence.
- Generalizability constrained by strict inclusion criteria and available trials.

## 7. Glossary

- **Extra-short implant (초단축 임플란트):** Dental implant with length ≤6 mm.
- **Long implant (장축 임플란트):** Dental implant with length ≥10 mm.
- **Marginal bone loss (변연골소실):** Bone loss at implant marginal level measured over time.
- **Meta-analysis (메타분석):** Quantitative synthesis of results across studies.
- **Systematic review (체계적문헌고찰):** Structured review with pre-defined methods to identify and synthesize evidence.
