## Three-line Summary

Prospective pilot study (Canullo L, University of Genova/Milan, Clin Implant Dent Relat Res 25(5):974-983, 2023) evaluating **OSSIX™ Bone** (hydroxyapatite/cross-linked collagen sponge, GLYMATRIX platform) as the sole graft material for transcrestal sinus floor augmentation (t-SFA) with simultaneous implant placement in 24 patients with residual bone height (RBH) >4 mm.

Twenty-two patients completed the study; **ISQ rose from 62.19 ± 8.09 at placement to 76.91 ± 4.50 at 6 months**, graft height was 7.24 mm immediately post-op, 6.57 mm at 6 months, and **5.46 mm at 12 months** — stable graft volume confirmed by time-series correlograms; buccolingual dimension significantly correlated with final graft volume.

OSSIX Bone showed "true bone formation without remnants" (no residual graft detected), confirming GLYMATRIX cross-linking allows complete ossification while maintaining adequate height for at least 12 months; 86% of patients reported no chewing interference.

## 세줄요약

파일럿 전향적 연구 (Canullo, 제노바/밀라노, CIDRR 2023) — OSSIX™ Bone (HA + 당가교 콜라겐 스펀지)을 단독 이식재로 치조정접근 상악동 거상 + 동시 임플란트. 잔존골 높이 >4mm, 24명(22명 완료), 1년 CBCT.

임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 62.19→76.91, 이식재 높이 즉시 7.24mm→1년 5.46mm (**안정적 유지**, 시계열 상관없음 확인). 협구개 폭이 이식재 부피와 유의 상관.

잔존 이식재 없음 = 완전 골화 확인; 86% 저작 불편 없음.

**Significant predictors of graft volume at 1 year**: buccolingual sinus dimension (P<0.05).

## 3. Clinical Interpretation

- **No bone substitute remnants**: OSSIX Bone undergoes complete replacement by new bone — different from deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM), which leaves 14-15% residual (cf. friedmann-2002)
- **ISQ gain +14.7 points**: Clinically excellent primary-to-secondary stability transition
- **Graft height slightly reduced (7.24→5.46 mm) but stable**: Some initial compaction expected; time-series analysis confirms no ongoing resorption
- **PIL matters**: Implant protrusion into sinus correlates with maintained height — "tenting" effect analogous to non-grafted protocols

## 4. Limitations

- Pilot study (n=22); no control group; no randomization
- RBH >4 mm only — not tested for severely atrophic maxillae
- No histologic/histomorphometric biopsy data
- 12-month CBCT follow-up only; long-term implant survival not reported
- CIDRR not OA; abstract-based ingest
