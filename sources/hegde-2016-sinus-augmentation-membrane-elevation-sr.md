## Why Ingested

SR of direct (lateral) sinus elevation without graft including human and animal studies up to 2014 — complements [[sinus-lift/lateral/riben-2012-maxillary-sinus-membrane-elevation-graftless]] and [[sinus-lift/lateral/dongo-2018-lateral-sinus-floor-graftless-meta-analysis]] with broader scope including indirect (transalveolar) results and animal evidence.

## One-line Summary

SR (PubMed/Google Scholar/Cochrane, 1997–Oct 2014): direct graftless sinus lift is a viable treatment option; average bone gain 2.37–10 mm; implant survival 79.9–100% across studies; due to heterogeneity, meta-analysis not performed.

## 한줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (1997–2014): 이식재 없는 직접 상악동거상술 생존율 79.9–100%, 평균 골증대 2.37–10mm; 이질성으로 메타분석 불가; 한계를 감안할 때 이식재 없는 직접 거상은 실행 가능한 치료 옵션.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Indian Prosthodontic Society, 2016;16:317-22
- **DOI**: 10.4103/0972-4052.191289
- **Institution**: A B Shetty Memorial Institute of Dental Sciences, Mangalore, India
- **Study design**: Systematic review
- **Search**: PubMed, Google Scholar, Cochrane CENTRAL; 1997–October 2014
- **Inclusion**: Direct sinus lift without graft during implant placement (human + animal); ≥6 months follow-up

## 2. Key Contributions

- Average bone gain across studies: 2.37–10 mm
- Implant survival rate across studies: 79.9–100%
- No evidence proving grafting material is necessary in all direct sinus lift procedures
- Previous meta-analysis showed similar survival rates regardless of graft type (auto/allo/alloplastic)
- Due to heterogeneity, data not pooled; qualitative synthesis only
- The graftless direct technique is suggested as viable keeping in mind its limitations

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Databases**: PubMed, Google Scholar, Cochrane CENTRAL
- **Period**: 1997–October 2014
- **Eligibility**: (1) Direct sinus lift without graft during implant placement; (2) human or animal studies; (3) ≥6 months follow-up
- **Authors**: Two independent reviewers; third author for consensus on controversy
- **Outcome**: Bone height gain, implant survival, implant stability, complications
- **Analysis**: No meta-analysis due to heterogeneity

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Range across studies |
|---|---|
| Average bone gain | 2.37–10 mm |
| Implant survival rate | 79.9–100% |
| Meta-analysis | Not performed (heterogeneity) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Heterogeneous study designs prevented meta-analysis
- Mix of human and animal studies limits clinical conclusions
- No AMSTAR quality assessment reported
- No formal PRISMA flow reported
- Survival rate range very wide (79.9–100%) — study quality variability

## 6. Related Work

- Riben & Thor 2012: Similar scope (lateral graftless), ≥1 year human studies only
- Dongo 2018: SR+MA 11 studies; survival 97.5%; stronger evidence with individual-data meta-analysis
- Starch-Jensen 2017: Technique selection review including graftless approaches

## 7. Glossary

- **Direct sinus lift (직접 상악동거상술)**: lateral window approach (vs indirect = transalveolar/osteotome)
- **Bone gain (골증대)**: increase in bone height after sinus augmentation
- **Implant survival (임플란트 생존율)**: proportion of implants remaining in function
