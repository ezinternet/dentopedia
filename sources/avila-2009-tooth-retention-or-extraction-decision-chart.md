## Why Ingested

Tooth retention versus extraction is the first decision in many implant-versus-save cases. The wiki has periodontal diagnosis guidance ([[periodontics/herrera-2025-consensus-report-periodontal-diagnosis]]) but no source for a structured retain-or-extract framework. This Avila 2009 chart is the framework that clinicians cite for that decision.

## Three-line Summary

Literature-based decision-process paper (Journal of Periodontology, 2009; peer-reviewed English articles plus book sources; PubMed-indexed as Meta-Analysis) — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The authors build a six-level, colour-coded chart weighing individual tooth- and patient-related factors (including smoking, furcation involvement, and restorability) for deciding whether a compromised tooth should be kept or extracted.

The abstract gives the chart's structure but no validation data; it is a reference tool, not an outcome study, and its level thresholds should not be cited as tested cut-offs.

문헌 기반 의사결정 과정 논문 (Journal of Periodontology, 2009; 영문 동료심사 논문과 교과서 검토; PubMed상 Meta-Analysis로 색인) — 초록만 수집 (전문 미확보).

## 2. Key Contributions

- A six-level colour-based chart for retain-versus-extract decisions.
- Explicit link between tooth-retention decisions and the implant alternative, as framed in the abstract.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Selection of peer-reviewed English articles across several scientific databases, plus book sources, reviewed narratively; the chart is constructed from that literature and justified factor by factor.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

The abstract reports a benchmark attributed to the implant literature: implant success rates above 90% over 15 years under ideal placement, loading and maintenance. No validation of the decision chart itself is reported.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative construction; no outcome validation of the chart in the abstract.
- Full text not retrieved; the exact factor weights are not verified here.
