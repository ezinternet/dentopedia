## Why Ingested

서울대학교 치주과 팀의 SR+MA — 감염 소켓 즉시식립 문헌을 아시아 임상가 관점에서 정리한 선구적 논문. 기존 [[immediate-implant/saijeva-2020-immediate-implant-placement-non-infected-sockets]]·[[immediate-implant/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]]의 기반이 되며, WKG(각화치은폭)를 심미적 고려 핵심 변수로 처음 명시적으로 강조한 SR.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (5 CCTs, meta-analysis 3 studies): comparison of immediate implant placement in infected vs non-infected extraction sockets up to March 2017; Seoul National University.

Risk difference for survival = -0.02 (NS); WMDs for MBL (0.32mm), PD (0.12mm), mBI (0.07), MGL (-0.06mm) all non-significant; only WKG change significantly differed between groups.

Implants can be placed in infected sockets after thorough debridement; WKG should be assessed and managed especially in aesthetic zones.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (CCT 5편, MA 3편): 감염 vs 비감염 소켓 즉시식립 비교 (서울대 치주과, 2018).

생존율 위험차 -0.02 (유의하지 않음); MBL·탐침깊이·출혈지수·치은연 모두 유의차 없음; 각화치은폭(WKG)만 두 군 간 유의한 차이.

철저한 소파 후 감염 소켓도 즉시식립 가능; 특히 심미부위에서 WKG 평가·관리 필요.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Acta Odontologica Scandinavica

- **Institution**: Department of Periodontology and Dental Research Institute, Seoul National University, Seoul, Korea
- **Year**: 2018

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the first Korean/Asian-authored SR+MA on infected socket immediate implant placement
- First to explicitly emphasize WKG management as aesthetic consideration in infected-site immediate implants
- Identified that only 5 CCTs (0 RCTs) existed on this topic as of March 2017 — highlighted evidence gap
- Provided WMD data for 6 clinical parameters (MBL, PD, mBI, MGL, WKG) from limited evidence base

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis, fixed-effects model
- **Search**: Electronic + manual, up to March 2017
- **Study types**: RCTs and nonrandomized CCTs
- **Studies included**: 5 in SR; 3 in meta-analysis
- **Primary outcome**: survival rate (risk difference)
- **Secondary outcomes**: MBL, PD, mBI, MGL, WKG (WMDs from baseline to latest follow-up)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | WMD / RD | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| Survival rate (RD) | -0.02 | NS |
| MBL change | 0.32 mm | NS |
| PD change | 0.12 mm | NS |
| mBI change | 0.07 | NS |
| MGL change | -0.06 mm | NS |
| WKG change | 0.20 mm (baseline) → 0.51 mm | **Significant** |

Only 3 CCTs included in meta-analysis — evidence base very limited.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Zero RCTs found (all CCTs) — lowest evidence quality for quantitative analysis
- Only 3 of 5 studies included in meta-analysis
- Small pooled sample
- Abstract-only access (full text not retrieved)

## 7. Glossary

- **CCT**: controlled clinical trial (non-randomized)
- **WKG**: width of keratinized gingiva — the only significant differentiator between groups
- **Risk difference**: absolute difference in event rates (survival) between two groups
