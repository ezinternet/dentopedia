## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 13 randomized trials in adults on an anticoagulant or antiplatelet who had minor oral surgery, comparing stopping the drug (control) with continuing it (test); searched PubMed-Medline, Scopus, Embase. Abstract-only — full text not retrieved.

Continuing therapy was associated with more intraoperative bleeding (reported as 157% higher; 95% CI 1.40-4.71; warfarin subgroup RR 1.79, 95% CI 1.00-3.21), but no significant difference in postoperative bleeding (RR 0.81, 95% CI 0.54-1.22, p=0.42).

Minor oral surgery can be done without altering a single-drug regimen, with local hemostatic measures strongly advised; only the abstract was available, so risk of bias, heterogeneity and antiplatelet subgroup numbers could not be checked.

## 세줄요약

항응고제 또는 항혈소판제를 복용하는 성인의 소수술(minor oral surgery)에서 약 중단군(대조)과 지속군(시험)을 비교한 무작위 임상시험 13편의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석(Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA); PubMed-Medline·Scopus·Embase 검색. 초록만 확보.

약을 계속한 군에서 술중 출혈이 더 많았고(157% 높음으로 보고, 95% CI 1.40-4.71; 와파린 하위군 RR 1.79, 95% CI 1.00-3.21), 술후 출혈은 유의한 차이가 없었다(RR 0.81, 95% CI 0.54-1.22, p=0.42).

단일 약제 요법은 변경 없이 소수술이 가능하며 국소 지혈 처치가 강력히 권고된다; 초록만 확보되어 비뚤림 위험·이질성·항혈소판 하위군 수치는 확인하지 못했다.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Clinical Oral Investigations 2021;25(6):3369-3381. PubMed PMID 33758999; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00784-021-03882-z).
- Type: systematic review with meta-analysis of RCTs (parallel or crossover).
- Note: PubMed title says "minor oral surgery"; the ingest brief paraphrased this as "dental extractions". The PubMed title is used here.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Pools RCTs only, comparing stopping vs continuing a single-drug anticoagulant or antiplatelet before minor oral surgery.
- Separates intraoperative from postoperative bleeding: higher intraoperative bleeding with continuation, no postoperative difference.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Three databases (PubMed-Medline, Scopus, Embase); included parallel or crossover RCTs; control = drug stopped before procedure, test = drug continued; pooled risk ratio; subgroups for anticoagulant and antiplatelet therapy. Thirteen studies included. Risk-of-bias tools and heterogeneity statistics not stated in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Intraoperative bleeding: continuation associated with 157% higher occurrence (95% CI 1.40-4.71).
- Warfarin subgroup: RR 1.79 (95% CI 1.00-3.21), significantly higher intraoperative bleeding.
- Postoperative bleeding: RR 0.81 (95% CI 0.54-1.22), p=0.42.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
Abstract-only; per-subgroup numbers for antiplatelets, thromboembolic outcomes, bleeding definitions and trial quality not available. Single-drug regimens only (not DAPT or combined therapy). The wording "157% higher" is reproduced as reported and not recomputed.

## 7. Glossary
- Intraoperative bleeding: bleeding during the procedure.
- Risk ratio (RR): event probability in continuation group divided by that in the stopped group.
