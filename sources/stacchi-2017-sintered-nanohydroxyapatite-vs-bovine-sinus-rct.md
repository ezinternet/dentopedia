## Why Ingested

[[sinus-lift/lateral/thomas-2026-alloplastic-biologic-grafts-sinus-sr]]는 합성골(Alloplast, BCP/β-TCP 중심)의 신생골 형성이 이종골보다 낮다는 계열(class) 수준 위계를 제시하는데, 이 위키에는 순수 나노수산화인회석(Sintered Nanohydroxyapatite, NHA)을 탈단백우골무기질(Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)과 **동일 환자 내(split-mouth)** 에서 직접 비교한 조직형태계측 근거가 없었다. 본 RCT(Stacchi 2017)는 그 공백을 채우고, 합성골 계열 내 이질성(alloplast ≠ alloplast)을 보여주는 반증 사례로 쓰인다.

## Three-line Summary

Multicenter split-mouth superiority RCT (4 Italian centers, NCT03077867, CONSORT) in 28 patients with severe bilateral posterior maxillary atrophy (residual crest 2.03 ± 0.75 mm): 52 trephine biopsies after 6 months of healing and 107 implants followed 12 months after loading, comparing pure sintered nanohydroxyapatite (NHA, test) with anorganic bovine bone (ABB/Bio-Oss, active control).

Histomorphometry showed no significant difference — vital bone 34.9 ± 15% (NHA) vs 38.5 ± 17% (ABB), p = 0.428; marrow spaces 44.5 ± 18% vs 43.5 ± 23%, p = 0.866; residual graft 20.6 ± 13% vs 22.3 ± 12%, p = 0.638 — and implant survival after 12 months of loading was 96.4% in the NHA group with no significant difference from ABB (cumulative failure 2.8%: 2/55 test vs 1/52 control, p = 0.32).

The prespecified null hypothesis was therefore **accepted**: at 6 months the osteoconductive performance of this synthetic nanohydroxyapatite is statistically indistinguishable from the bovine xenograft, so the choice between them can be driven by non-efficacy factors (xenograft refusal, prion/immune concerns, cost) rather than by a histological advantage.

## 세줄요약

다기관 스플릿마우스 우월성 무작위 대조 시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 이탈리아 4개 센터, NCT03077867, CONSORT): 중증 양측 상악 후방 무치악 위축(잔존골높이 2.03 ± 0.75 mm) 환자 28명에서 소결 나노수산화인회석 (Sintered Nanohydroxyapatite, NHA)과 무기질 우골 (Anorganic Bovine Bone, ABB; Bio-Oss)을 같은 환자의 좌우 상악동에 각각 이식하고, 6개월 후 코어 생검 52개와 임플란트 107개(부하 12개월)를 평가.

조직형태계측에서 두 군 간 유의차 없음 — 신생골 (New Bone, NB) 34.9 ± 15%(NHA) vs 38.5 ± 17%(ABB), p = 0.428; 골수강 44.5 ± 18% vs 43.5 ± 23%, p = 0.866; 잔류이식재 (Residual Graft) 20.6 ± 13% vs 22.3 ± 12%, p = 0.638 — 이며, 부하 12개월 후 임플란트 생존율은 NHA군 96.4%로 ABB군과 유의차 없음(누적 실패율 2.8%: 시험군 2/55 vs 대조군 1/52, p = 0.32).

따라서 사전 설정한 영가설 (null hypothesis)이 **채택**되었다 — 즉 6개월 시점에서 이 합성 나노수산화인회석의 골전도 성능은 우골 이종골과 통계적으로 구분되지 않으며, 두 재료의 선택은 조직학적 우위가 아니라 이종골 거부감·프리온/면역 우려·비용 같은 비-효능 요인으로 결정할 문제다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First prospective **split-mouth** direct histological comparison of *pure sintered* nanohydroxyapatite against ABB in lateral maxillary sinus augmentation — prior nanocrystalline HA evidence used HA embedded in a highly porous silica gel matrix, not pure sintered NHA.
- Provides a within-patient controlled estimate that removes inter-individual healing variability, the main confounder of between-patient graft comparisons.
- Couples the histological endpoint (6-month histomorphometry) with a clinical endpoint (implant survival after 12 months of loading) in the same cohort — a pairing most histology-only sinus trials lack.
- Documents a **null result** (34.9% vs 38.5% vital bone) with adequate power calculation (24 sinuses/group to detect effect size 0.6, α = 0.05, power 80%), which is the useful direction for the alloplast-vs-xenograft question: absence of a detectable difference, not a claim of superiority.
- Adds qualitative mechanistic observations: osteocyte density appeared higher near NHA particle surfaces, interpreted by the authors as osteoinductive surface activity (citing EGFR/AKT/ERK 1/2 pathway activation by NHA paste in Kasai et al.).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Population**: adults ≥ 18 y with severe bilateral maxillary atrophy (crestal height < 3 mm, Cawood & Howell class V–VI) requiring staged lateral sinus floor elevation for fixed rehabilitation. Exclusions included uncontrolled diabetes (HbA1c > 7.5%), head/neck radiotherapy < 24 months, IV bisphosphonates, uncontrolled periodontal disease, sinus pathology, and intraoperative Schneiderian membrane perforation.
- **Randomization**: computer-generated balanced permuted-block table (randomization.com) assigning right/left sinus of each patient to test/control; allocation in numbered sealed opaque envelopes opened only **after** membrane elevation → allocation concealed from recruiting surgeons.
- **Surgery**: piezoelectric lateral window (erosion technique), membrane elevation with ultrasonic inserts and curettes, Valsalva integrity check, graft placement, collagen membrane + 2 pins, Sentineri suturing. Contralateral sinus grafted in the same session with the other material.
- **Biopsy**: 6 months post-grafting, 3.5 mm trephine core harvested during implant bed preparation (surgical-template guided), then implants placed into the harvest sites.
- **Histology**: cold 5% glucose rinse (278 mOsm/L) → 10% buffered formalin → graded alcohol dehydration at 4 °C → resin embedding (Technovit 7200 VLC) → 50 µm diamond sectioning → grinding to 30 ± 10 µm → Toluidine Blue/Azure II with acid fuchsin or Pironine G. Brightfield and circularly polarized light microscopy.
- **Histomorphometry**: Image-Pro Plus 6.0 with per-image calibration; parameters = sample area, vital bone area/%, marrow space area/%, residual graft area/%; two readings per index and repeat if > 5% discrepancy (intraexaminer control). Single trained examiner (TT) — no reported blinding of the examiner to group.
- **Outcomes**: (1) new bone %, (2) residual graft %, (3) marrow space %; implant failure (mobility/removal); biological and mechanical complications.
- **Statistics**: SigmaStat 3.5; normality and equal-variance testing; unpaired t-test for parametric and Mann–Whitney U for nonparametric data; α = 0.05. Sample size: 24 sinuses/group for effect size 0.6 on new bone; 7 patients/center × 4 = 56 sinuses to buffer dropouts.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Follow-up is short**: 6-month histology and 12-month post-loading survival only; the planned 5-year evaluation is not reported here, so no statement about long-term graft resorption, volumetric stability or marginal bone loss is possible.
- **Statistical model vs design mismatch**: the study is split-mouth (paired data) yet analysis used unpaired t-tests and Mann–Whitney U tests rather than paired tests; this is conservative for confounding control but discards the pairing gain and is not the analysis the design implies. The reported p-values should be read with that caveat.
- Two patients (3 perforations) dropped out, and membrane perforation was an exclusion criterion — the analysed population is therefore perforation-free, limiting generalizability to routine (perforation-prone) cases.
- Histomorphometric measurement by a single examiner, with no stated blinding to allocation; intraexaminer repeat only when > 5% discrepancy.
- Perforation repaired with A-PRF in the excluded cases only; no data on how NHA behaves in perforated sinuses.
- No radiographic/volumetric (CBCT) outcome, no ISQ or insertion-torque data, no patient-reported outcomes, no cost analysis.
- Both authors' materials are commercial products; no sample-size adequacy statement for the implant-survival endpoint (powered only for new bone %).
- No deviation from the briefing values was found: all numbers above were read directly from the PMC text.

## 6. Related Work

- Thomas et al. 2026 (SR, 18 studies): class-level hierarchy autograft > allograft > alloplast (BCP/β-TCP, ~30%) > xenograft for new bone, with alloplast volumetric stability inferior — the present RCT sits against that alloplast-class generalization by testing a nano-HA alloplast head-to-head and finding equivalence.
- Trimmel et al. 2021 (Bayesian NMA, 34 RCTs): nanocrystalline HA in silica gel ranked 70% SUCRA for new bone at 5–8 months — the closest prior ranking evidence for an HA alloplast; the present trial isolates *pure sintered* NHA from the silica-gel carrier.
- Kukis et al. 2026 (SR+MA of PRF-modified grafts): PRF adjuncts to allograft/xenograft shift new bone and residual graft modestly; unaffected by the NHA-vs-ABB question but shares the 6-month histomorphometric endpoint and its non-clinical nature.
- Kasai et al. (cited by the authors): EGFR/AKT/ERK 1/2 activation by NHA paste as the proposed mechanistic basis for the osteoblastic stimulation seen near NHA particle surfaces.
- Kirkpatrick et al. and the T1/T2/T3 regeneration-phase model (cited): most bone substitutes never reach the biomaterial-free T3 phase, yet residual particles do not preclude osseointegration — consistent with 20–22% residual graft at 6 months in both arms here.

## 7. Glossary

- **NHA (Sintered Nanohydroxyapatite)**: fully synthetic hydroxyapatite with nanoscale porosity, sintered (not carrier-embedded); osteoconductive alloplast claimed to favour bone-matrix protein adhesion and osteoblast precursor differentiation.
- **ABB (Anorganic Bovine Bone)**: deproteinized bovine xenograft (Bio-Oss) — porous carbonate apatite, high osteoconductivity and dimensional stability, very slow resorption; used here as the active control.
- **Split-mouth design**: each patient receives both interventions (right vs left sinus), so every patient is their own control, removing inter-individual healing variability.
- **Vital bone %**: area of newly formed mineralized bone (including osteocytes in lacunae) divided by total biopsy section area.
- **Residual graft %**: area occupied by recognizable graft particles divided by total section area — an inverse proxy for graft resorption/turnover.
- **Marrow spaces (connective tissue) %**: non-mineralized, non-graft soft-tissue area fraction of the biopsy.
- **Cawood & Howell class V–VI**: severe posterior maxillary atrophy with minimal residual crest, requiring staged grafting before implant placement.
- **T1/T2/T3 phases (Kirkpatrick)**: time of grafting (particulate/blood-clot composite) → time of repairing (biomaterial remnants + new bone) → time of regeneration (biomaterial-free homogeneous bone), rarely reached by common substitutes.
