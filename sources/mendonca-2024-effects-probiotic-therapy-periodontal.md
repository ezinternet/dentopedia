## Why Ingested

기존 [[overviews/periodontal-adjunctive-therapy-probiotics-pdt-overview]]은 2026년 개별 RCT(Lundtorp-Olsen, Jeon)에서 프로바이오틱스가 BoP·심부포켓 수를 유의 감소시킨다는 근거를 다루지만, umbrella review 수준의 상위 근거는 아직 없었다. 본 논문(Mendonça 2024, PROSPERO 등록 umbrella review, SR 30편)은 그 근거 기반을 SR 메타수준에서 재검토해 "결과가 상충하며 확정적 결론 불가"라는 신중한 caveat을 제공하여, overview의 ~0.3mm CAL ceiling 프레이밍을 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA/PROSPERO-registered, 30 systematic reviews spanning periodontal and peri-implant probiotic adjunct therapy in adult patients with periodontal or peri-implant disease); no quantitative pooling across SRs was possible due to high clinical heterogeneity.

17 of 31 reviews reported clinically relevant benefit of probiotics as an adjunct to nonsurgical periodontal treatment (NSPT); pooled findings across SRs favored short-term (≤3 month) improvement in PPD, CAL, and BOP, with benefit not sustained at 6 months; 22/31 SRs had low risk of bias, 7 moderate, 2 high.

Evidence is conflicting and insufficient for a definitive recommendation for or against probiotic adjunct therapy; peri-implant substrate evidence is sparser and less favorable than periodontal (tooth) substrate evidence, and no SR reported data on bone loss, implant survival, or tooth loss.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(umbrella review, PRISMA/PROSPERO 등록, 성인 치주질환·임플란트주위질환 환자 대상 프로바이오틱스 보조요법을 다룬 체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review, SR) 30편); 임상적 이질성이 커서 SR 간 정량적 통합(meta-analysis)은 불가능했음.

31편 중 17편이 비수술치주치료(Non-Surgical Periodontal Treatment, NSPT) 보조요법으로서 프로바이오틱스의 임상적으로 유의미한 이득을 보고; 단기(3개월 이내) 치주낭깊이(Probing Pocket Depth, PPD)·임상부착수준(Clinical Attachment Level, CAL)·탐침시출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP) 개선에는 SR들 간 일관된 근거가 있었으나 6개월 시점에서는 이득이 유지되지 않음; 31편 중 22편 낮은 비뚤림위험(low risk of bias, RoB), 7편 중등도, 2편 높음.

근거가 상충하여 프로바이오틱스 보조요법에 대한 찬반 확정적 결론을 내릴 수 없으며, 임플란트주위(peri-implant) 기질의 근거는 치아(tooth) 기질보다 적고 덜 우호적이었고, 골소실·임플란트 생존율·발치율에 대한 데이터를 보고한 SR은 없었음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review to jointly synthesize probiotic adjunct-therapy evidence across BOTH periodontal (tooth) and peri-implant substrates, rather than treating them as separate literatures.
- Applies JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist for Systematic Reviews (not AMSTAR-2) to rate methodological quality of the 30–31 included SRs, with Cohen's κ interrater agreement.
- Produces a GRADE-style "Summary of Findings" table (Table 4) stratifying each outcome (PPD, CAL, BOP, microbiological) by substrate (tooth vs implant) and direction of effect (favors vs does not favor probiotics).
- Identifies *Lactobacillus reuteri* as the subspecies most consistently associated with clinical improvement across included SRs.
- Explicitly flags that no SR reported hard outcomes: bone loss around teeth/implants, implant survival rate, or tooth loss.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review (systematic review of systematic reviews), PRISMA-reported, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42021254469)
- **Databases**: Medline (via PubMed), LILACS, Cochrane Central Registry of Controlled Trials (CENTRAL), Google Scholar (first 300 references), DANS EASY Archive (grey literature/unpublished), searched to December 2022. Manual reference screening + author contact for unpublished data.
- **n**: 30 included systematic reviews (some risk-of-bias/quality breakdowns reported for 31 — see note below); underlying primary studies were RCTs
- **Eligibility**: Adult patients (≥18y) with periodontal disease and/or peri-implant disease; probiotic therapy (alone or combined species) as adjunct to nonsurgical treatment vs conventional treatment alone/placebo; SRs only, published in English, Portuguese, Spanish, or French, no date/country restriction
- **Quality appraisal**: JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist for Systematic Reviews, 2 independent reviewers, third reviewer for disagreements, Cohen's κ for interrater agreement
- **Outcomes**: Primary — PPD, CAL, BOP; Secondary — plaque index, gingival index, microbiological (periodontal pathogen counts), immunological markers, bone loss, implant survival, tooth loss, adverse events
- **Note on N discrepancy**: the abstract states "17 of 31 reviews" and reports 22 low/7 moderate/2 high risk of bias (22+7+2=31), while the Discussion states "this umbrella review included 30 SRs" — the paper is internally inconsistent on this count; both figures are reproduced here as stated in the source text.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PPD | 26 | 13 | 2 | 7 | 4 | Coherent short-term (≤3mo) benefit, diminishing medium/long term |
| CAL | 19 | 10 | 0 | 9 | 0 | Benefit at 1–3mo; 6mo benefit unproven |
| BOP | 23 | 10 | 3 | 7 | 3 | Benefit at 6mo unproven |
| Microbiological | 14 | 6 favor | — | 8 not favor | — | Mixed across pathogens |
| Systemic disease subgroup | 1 | — | — | 1 not favor | — | Only 1 SR stratified by systemic disease; 7 excluded systemically compromised patients as eligibility criterion |
| Bone loss (teeth/implants) | 0 | — | — | — | — | No clinical data reported by any included SR |
| Implant survival rate | 0 | — | — | — | — | No clinical data reported |
| Tooth loss | 0 | — | — | — | — | No clinical data reported |

Representative pooled effect sizes reported within included SRs (not independently pooled by the umbrella review): Hu et al. 2021 meta-analysis (24 RCTs) — CAL gain WMD 0.20 mm (95% CI 0.09–0.31), PPD reduction WMD −0.31 mm (95% CI −0.52 to −0.10), BOP reduction WMD −2.98% (95% CI −4.70 to −1.26) at 3 months, no significant difference at 6 months. Gheisary et al. 2022 (64 RCTs) reported broadly significant improvements across PPD, CAL, BOP, plaque index, gingival index, and multiple periodontopathogens/immunological markers (SMD range 0.34–0.92). Gao et al. 2020 (7 RCTs, peri-implant) found no significant PPD benefit of *Lactobacillus* over placebo in peri-implant mucositis (MD −0.05 to −0.17 mm, both non-significant).

Risk of bias across included SRs: 22/31 low, 7/31 moderate, 2/31 high; 81% of SRs adhered to PRISMA reporting.

**Conclusion (verbatim intent)**: "Based on the available evidence, the results are conflicting, and there can be no definitive conclusion for or against probiotics."

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Umbrella review inherits limitations of included SRs and their underlying primary studies (heterogeneous probiotic subspecies, regimens, vehicles, follow-up periods, and outcome definitions).
- High clinical heterogeneity across SRs precluded quantitative re-pooling (no meta-analysis of the umbrella review itself).
- 29% of included SRs had moderate-to-high risk of bias.
- Most SRs did not stratify by systemic disease status; only 1 SR reported systemic-disease-stratified results, and it found no relevant RCT in systemically compromised subjects.
- No hard clinical outcome data (bone loss, implant survival, tooth loss) reported by any included SR.
- Peri-implant substrate evidence is markedly sparser than periodontal (tooth) substrate evidence.
- Authors recommend future RCTs with ≥1 year follow-up, standardized probiotic administration route, head-to-head single- vs multi-species comparisons, and standardized periodontal outcome measurement.

## 6. Related Work

- Hu et al. (2021), Gheisary et al. (2022), Gou et al. (2020), Song and Liu (2020): individual meta-analyses favoring probiotic adjunct therapy on tooth substrate, included as primary evidence within this umbrella review.
- Gao et al. (2020), Silva et al. (2020): individual meta-analyses on peri-implant substrate, largely not favoring probiotic adjunct therapy.
- [[overviews/periodontal-adjunctive-therapy-probiotics-pdt-overview]]: existing wiki synthesis of 2026 individual RCTs (Lundtorp-Olsen/Kim, Jeon) on probiotic NSPT adjuncts — this umbrella review provides the SR-level evidence context (pre-dating those RCTs, literature search to Dec 2022) that the overview's cautious ~0.3mm CAL ceiling framing is consistent with.

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: A systematic review that synthesizes findings from multiple existing systematic reviews (SRs) on a related topic, rather than primary studies directly.
- **NSPT**: Nonsurgical periodontal treatment (scaling and root planing, SRP).
- **JBI**: Joanna Briggs Institute — provides standardized critical appraisal tools for systematic reviews.
- **PPD**: Periodontal/peri-implant probing pocket depth.
- **CAL**: Clinical attachment level.
- **BOP**: Bleeding on probing.
- **WMD/SMD**: Weighted mean difference / standardized mean difference — pooled effect size metrics used in meta-analyses.
- **PROSPERO**: International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews.
