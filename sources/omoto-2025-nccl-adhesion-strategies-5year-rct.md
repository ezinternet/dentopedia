## Why Ingested
5-year extended follow-up of the same UNESP split-mouth RCT previously held as [[resin-bonding/omoto-2025-nccl-adhesion-strategies-4year-rct]] (J Dent 2025, 4-year report). Provides longer-term evidence on survival of NCCL restorations placed with four adhesion strategies (universal adhesive ± selective enamel etching vs RMGIC ± EDTA) and adds retention risk factors from multiple logistic regression; supersedes the 4-year report.

## Three-line Summary

5-year follow-up of a split-mouth RCT: 200 NCCL restorations placed with Scotchbond Universal (SBU) without/with selective enamel etching (E-SBU) or Vitremer RMGIC without/with EDTA pretreatment (E-RMGIC); 119 restorations re-evaluated at 5 years.

No significant difference in restoration survival among the four strategies; E-SBU had more Bravo scores for marginal integrity than ionomer groups and more marginal discoloration than RMGIC, while ionomer groups had worse surface texture and RMGIC more wear than E-SBU.

Retention was significantly influenced by tooth type (premolars), degree of dentin sclerosis, and degree of gingival recession — lesion/patient factors, not the adhesion strategy, drove 5-year retention.

## 1. Document Information
- J Adhes Dent 2025;27:209-219. DOI 10.3290/j.jad.c_2318. PMID 41165258, PMC12577456. Same trial group (UNESP, Fagundes TC) as the 4-year J Dent 2025 report.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Extends the four-arm NCCL adhesion-strategy RCT from 4 to 5 years.
- Confirms equivalent survival across universal-adhesive and RMGIC strategies at 5 years.
- Identifies retention predictors (premolar, dentin sclerosis, gingival recession) via multiple logistic regression.

## 3. Methodology
- Randomized clinical trial, split-mouth; 200 restorations placed; 119 re-evaluated at 5 years.
- Groups: SBU (Scotchbond Universal + Filtek Z350XT, no etch), E-SBU (selective enamel etching), RMGIC (Vitremer), E-RMGIC (EDTA pretreatment).
- Clinical criteria with Alpha/Bravo scoring (marginal integrity, marginal discoloration, surface texture, wear, color, secondary caries, retention); multiple logistic regression for retention.

## 4. Key Results
- Survival: no significant differences among the four groups.
- Marginal integrity: E-SBU more Bravo vs both ionomer groups.
- Marginal discoloration: E-SBU more Bravo vs RMGIC.
- Surface texture: ionomer groups higher Bravo.
- Wear: RMGIC > E-SBU.
- Color, secondary caries: no significant differences.
- Retention predictors: tooth type (premolars), degree of dentin sclerosis, degree of marginal tissue (gingival) recession.

## 5. Limitations
- Abstract only; effect sizes and exact survival percentages not retrieved.
- 119/200 restorations re-evaluated (~40% loss to follow-up by 5 years).
- Material-specific (Scotchbond Universal, Vitremer).

## 7. Glossary
- SBU: Scotchbond Universal. E-SBU: SBU with selective enamel etching. RMGIC: resin-modified glass-ionomer cement (Vitremer). E-RMGIC: RMGIC after EDTA dentin conditioning. Alpha/Bravo: USPHS-type clinical ratings (Bravo = clinically acceptable deviation).
