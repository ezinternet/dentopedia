## One-line Summary
Double-blind split-mouth RCT (n=52) comparing methylprednisolone 20/30/40 mg IM vs placebo found no significant reduction in pain, trismus, or QoL after mandibular third molar surgery, with age and smoking as stronger predictors.

## 한줄요약
이중맹검 split-mouth RCT (n=52)에서 메틸프레드니솔론 20/30/40 mg 근주는 하악 사랑니 발치 후 통증·개구제한(Trismus)·삶의 질에 위약 대비 유의한 차이를 보이지 않았으며, 나이 증가와 흡연이 더 강한 예후 인자였다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Split-mouth design eliminates inter-patient confounders — highest internal validity for dose comparison
- First Danish multicenter RCT comparing 3 methylprednisolone doses vs placebo in M3 surgery
- Identified age (p=0.00) and smoking + surgery duration (p=0.00) as predictors independent of steroid dose
- Challenges dose-escalation assumption in M3 corticosteroid literature
- OHIP-14 as QoL endpoint — adds PRO dimension beyond VAS/trismus

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Double-blind, split-mouth, parallel-group RCT
- Setting: Aalborg University Hospital OMS department, March 2018 – January 2019
- n=52 (16M/36F), mean age 25.9y (range 18–39), bilateral impacted M3
- Groups: I=placebo (saline), II=20mg, III=30mg, IV=40mg methylprednisolone — IM masseter injection intraoperatively
- Follow-up: Day 1, 3, 7, 30
- Outcomes: VAS pain, interincisal mouth opening (trismus), OHIP-14 QoL
- Statistics: Descriptive + generalized estimating equation (GEE) analysis, 95% CI

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Primary outcome: No significant difference in pain (VAS), trismus (interincisal distance), or OHIP-14 between any methylprednisolone dose and placebo
- Higher prevalence of postoperative pain and worsening QoL with increased age (p=0.00)
- Smoking + increased surgery time → decreased mouth opening in early healing (p=0.00)
- No dose-response relationship detected across 20/30/40 mg groups
- No adverse effects reported

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Sample size calculated for 20mm VAS difference — may be underpowered for smaller effect sizes
- Single surgeon (MKL) limits generalizability of operative technique
- Split-mouth design requires bilateral impaction — selection bias toward younger, symmetric cases
- Long inter-session interval (mean 58.8 days, range 8–157) may introduce temporal confounders
- No direct measurement of edema (only VAS/trismus/OHIP)

## 7. Glossary
- **Split-mouth RCT**: Each patient serves as own control — left/right sides randomized to different interventions
- **GEE** (Generalized Estimating Equation): Statistical method accounting for within-patient correlation in repeated measures
- **OHIP-14**: 14-item Oral Health Impact Profile — validated PRO instrument for oral health-related QoL
- **Masseter IM injection**: Intramuscular injection into masseter muscle as local depot for corticosteroid delivery
