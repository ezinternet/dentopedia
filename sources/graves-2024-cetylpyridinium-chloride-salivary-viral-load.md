## Why Ingested

This two-trial RCT provides direct evidence that CPC mouthwash—a non-CHX, non-PVP-I option—significantly reduces salivary SARS-CoV-2 load for at least 30 minutes, expanding the preprocedural rinse toolkit for dental infection control. Its positive CPC finding is later challenged at the meta-analytic level by [[infection-control/ebrahimi-2023-mouthwash-sars-cov2-viral-load-sr-ma]], making it a key data point in the ongoing evidence debate.

## Three-line Summary

This study comprises two double-blind randomized controlled trials (RCTs) investigating the efficacy of commercially available mouthrinses in reducing salivary SARS-CoV-2 viral load in COVID-19-positive patients. In the first trial (n=128, final n=101), six rinses were tested against a saline placebo, and in the second (n=230, final n=146), ethanol (EtOH), cetylpyridinium chloride (CPC), and sterile water were compared.

Rinsing with the CPC-containing product resulted in a significant reduction in salivary SARS-CoV-2 viral load at 30 minutes post-rinse (P = .015), whereas no other rinse showed a significant effect at that time point. At 60 minutes post-rinse, no group exhibited a significant reduction relative to baseline, indicating a rebound in viral load.

The findings suggest that preprocedural oral rinsing with a CPC-based mouthwash is a feasible, inexpensive, and well-tolerated method to transiently reduce salivary viral load, potentially mitigating the spread of SARS-CoV-2 and other enveloped viruses in clinical settings.

## 세줄요약

이 연구는 COVID-19 양성 환자를 대상으로 시판되는 구강 세정액이 타액 내 SARS-CoV-2 바이러스 부하량을 감소시키는 효과를 평가하기 위해 수행된 두 개의 이중맹검 무작위 대조시험(RCT)입니다. 첫 번째 시험(n=128, 최종 n=101)에서는 6가지 세정액을 식염수 위약과 비교했고, 두 번째 시험(n=230, 최종 n=146)에서는 에탄올(EtOH), 세틸피리디늄 클로라이드(CPC), 멸균수를 비교했습니다.

CPC 함유 제품으로 세정한 후 30분 시점에서 타액 내 SARS-CoV-2 바이러스 부하량이 유의하게 감소한 반면(P = .015), 다른 세정액은 30분 시점에서 유의한 효과를 보이지 않았습니다. 세정 후 60분 시점에서는 어떤 그룹도 기준선 대비 유의한 바이러스 부하량 감소를 보이지 않았으며, 이는 1시간 이내에 바이러스 부하량이 회복됨을 시사합니다.

연구 결과는 CPC 함유 구강 세정액을 시술 전에 사용하는 것이 타액 내 바이러스 부하량을 일시적으로 감소시켜 임상 환경에서 SARS-CoV-2 및 다른 외피 바이러스의 전파를 완화할 수 있는 실현 가능하고, 저렴하며, 내약성이 좋은 방법임을 제안합니다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- A CPC-containing mouthrinse significantly reduced salivary SARS-CoV-2 viral load by 30 minutes post-rinse (P = .015).
- No other mouthrinse (EtOH, H2O2, PI, CHX) demonstrated significant viral load reduction at 30 minutes.
- The antiviral effect of the CPC rinse was transient, with viral load rebounding to baseline levels by 60 minutes.
- This study was conducted across different viral variant waves (Delta and Omicron), suggesting broad applicability.
- Patient surveys indicated a "fair to good" experience with the CPC rinse and a high willingness to use oral rinses in healthcare settings.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Two consecutive, prospective, double-blind, parallel-group, placebo-controlled randomized controlled trials.
- Trial 1: Six arms (EtOH, CPC, H2O2, PI, CHX, and Saline placebo). Final n=101.
- Trial 2: Three arms (EtOH, CPC, and Sterile Water placebo). Final n=146.
- Randomization: Block randomization schema generated by a statistician. A staff member with no patient interaction performed group assignment.
- Blinding: Participants and study coordinators were blinded to group assignment.
- Participants: Adults (18–65 years) with confirmed SARS-CoV-2 positivity (PCR or antigen) within 10 days of symptom onset or positive test.
- Procedure: Participants provided an unstimulated saliva sample at baseline, rinsed with the assigned product for 60 seconds, and provided additional saliva samples at 30 and 60 minutes post-rinse.
- Outcome: Primary outcome was the change in salivary SARS-CoV-2 viral load (N2 copy number), quantified by qPCR, from baseline to 30 and 60 minutes post-rinse.
- Statistics: An adjusted linear mixed-effects model was employed to compare viral load after rinsing relative to baseline.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome (CPC vs. Baseline at 30 min) —** Significant reduction in SARS-CoV-2 N2 copy number (P = .015).
- **Primary outcome (CPC vs. Placebo at 30 min) —** Significant reduction relative to placebo (P = .017).
- **Primary outcome (All other rinses at 30 min) —** No significant reduction in SARS-CoV-2 N2 copy number (P > .05 for all).
- **Primary outcome (All rinses at 60 min) —** No significant reduction in SARS-CoV-2 N2 copy number relative to baseline for any group.
- **Subjective outcomes —** The CPC rinse was rated as having a "fair to good" experience, and participants reported high willingness to use mouthrinses in dental and medical settings.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Viral infectivity was not assessed; only RNA copy number was measured, which is insufficient for determining the infectivity of enveloped viruses.
- The specific antiviral mechanisms of the CPC rinse (e.g., envelope disruption) were not directly investigated.
- The study cannot differentiate between the mechanical effects of rinsing and the specific antiseptic action of the mouthrinse.
- The number of active ingredients tested was limited to those commonly used in regional dental clinics at the study's initiation.
- The assessment of RNA copy number is methodologically insufficient for assessing the efficacy of antiseptic rinses on the infective potential of enveloped viral particles, an issue that has been recently reviewed.

## 7. Glossary

- **CPC (Cetylpyridinium Chloride)**: 세틸피리디늄 클로라이드. 구강 세정액에 사용되는 4차 암모늄 화합물로, 계면활성제 작용을 통해 바이러스 외피를 파괴합니다.
- **qPCR (Quantitative Polymerase Chain Reaction)**: 정량적 중합효소 연쇄 반응. 특정 DNA 또는 RNA의 양을 측정하는 데 사용되는 분자생물학적 기술입니다. 본 연구에서는 타액 내 SARS-CoV-2 바이러스 부하량을 측정하는 데 사용되었습니다.
- **RCT (Randomized Controlled Trial)**: 무작위 대조 시험. 중재법(예: 치료법)의 효과를 평가하기 위해 참가자를 무작위로 배정하는 연구 설계입니다.
- **SARS-CoV-2 (Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus 2)**: 중증급성호흡기증후군 코로나바이러스 2. COVID-19를 유발하는 바이러스입니다.
- **EtOH (Ethanol)**: 에탄올. 소독 효과를 위해 구강 세정액에 사용되는 알코올의 일종입니다.
- **CHX (Chlorhexidine Gluconate)**: 클로르헥시딘 글루코네이트. 치주 질환 치료 및 예방에 사용되는 항균 구강 세정제의 활성 성분입니다.
