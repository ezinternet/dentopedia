## Why Ingested
덱사메타손 vs 메틸프레드니솔론 직접 비교 SR+MA. [[drug/liborio-2025-methylprednisolone-third-molar-sr-ma]] 결론(덱사메타손이 trismus에 우월)의 선행 근거로 활용.

## One-line Summary
SR+MA of 7 RCTs (PROSPERO CRD42020161341) found dexamethasone and methylprednisolone equivalent for pain, swelling, and rescue analgesia, but dexamethasone significantly reduced trismus more than methylprednisolone at day 2 (SMD −0.69 mm, 95% CI −1.01 to −0.38, p<0.0001).

## 한줄요약
7개 RCT SR+MA에서 덱사메타손(Dexamethasone)과 메틸프레드니솔론(Methylprednisolone)은 통증·부종 감소에 동등했으나, 덱사메타손이 술후 2일 개구제한(Trismus) 감소에 통계적으로 유의하게 우월했다(SMD −0.69 mm, p<0.0001).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Directly compares only dexamethasone vs methylprednisolone (excludes other corticosteroids) — more specific than prior SR+MAs
- Quantifies the trismus advantage of dexamethasone with pooled SMD
- Finds no evidence that either drug causes adverse effects in single-dose perioperative use
- Evidence quality: low to moderate — acknowledges heterogeneity limitations
- PROSPERO-registered, PRISMA-compliant

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Systematic review + meta-analysis
- Databases: PubMed, CINAHL, Scopus, DOSS, Cochrane Central, Web of Science
- Inclusion: RCTs, preemptive dexamethasone vs methylprednisolone, mandibular M3 extraction, local anesthesia, healthy/asymptomatic patients
- Exclusion: Other corticosteroids, other surgical procedures
- n=7 RCTs identified
- Risk of bias: Low in 3 studies, unclear in 4
- Outcomes: Pain (VAS), rescue analgesics consumed, swelling, trismus, adverse events

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Pain: No significant pooled difference between dexamethasone and methylprednisolone
- Rescue analgesia: No significant difference
- Swelling: No significant difference
- Trismus at postoperative day 2: Dexamethasone significantly better than methylprednisolone
  - SMD = −0.69 mm (95% CI: −1.01 to −0.38), p<0.0001
- Adverse effects: None reported in any included study

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 7 RCTs — small pool for meta-analysis
- Considerable heterogeneity in methods and parameters assessed across studies
- Risk of bias unclear in 4/7 studies
- Overall evidence quality: low to moderate
- Larger RCTs with standardized protocols needed

## 7. Glossary
- **Preemptive**: Administered before or at time of surgery (not post-operatively) to blunt inflammatory cascade
- **SMD** (Standardized Mean Difference): Effect size metric for continuous outcomes across studies with different scales
- **CINAHL**: Cumulative Index to Nursing and Allied Health Literature database
- **DOSS**: Database of Open Systematic reviews and meta-analyses (less common database)
