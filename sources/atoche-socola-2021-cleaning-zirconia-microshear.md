## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (Journal of Advanced Prosthodontics, 2021; 50 CAD/CAM Ivoclar Vivadent zirconia specimens) comparing multiple cleaning/decontamination techniques on saliva-contaminated zirconia discs using dual-cure resin cement and microshear bond strength (µSBS) as the primary outcome.

All cleaning protocols significantly improved µSBS compared to the saliva-contaminated (no-clean) control; the study established that decontamination of zirconia after intraoral try-in is essential before final cementation with dual-cure resin cement.

The specific agent hierarchy was not reported in extracted data, limiting direct protocol comparison; this is a single-lab in vitro study with a limited specimen pool (50 discs), so clinical translation requires further corroboration.

## 세줄요약

체외 연구(Journal of Advanced Prosthodontics, 2021; CAD/CAM 지르코니아 시편 50개) — 타액 오염 후 다양한 세정/탈오염 프로토콜을 이중경화 레진 시멘트와 미세전단결합강도(microshear bond strength, µSBS)로 비교.

모든 세정 프로토콜이 세정 미시행 대조군(오염 상태) 대비 µSBS를 유의하게 향상시켜, 구강 내 시적 후 최종 합착 전 지르코니아 탈오염이 필수임을 확인.

세정제 간 우위 계층은 추출 데이터에 보고되지 않아 프로토콜 직접 비교가 제한적이며, 단일 실험실 50시편의 체외 연구로 임상 적용을 위한 추가 검증이 필요함.

## 1. Key Results
- CAD/CAM Ivoclar Vivadent zirconia discs, 50 specimens
- Multiple cleaning techniques compared with µSBS endpoint
- Dual-cure resin cement used
- Cleaning restored or improved µSBS over contaminated control
