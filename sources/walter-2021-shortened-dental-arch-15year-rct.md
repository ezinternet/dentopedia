## Why Ingested

RPD 선택 결정 축의 **유일한 장기 무작위 비교**라 spine으로 넣었다. [[wiki/removable-partial-denture/drummond-2024-rpd-long-term-periodontal-health-sr-ma]]는 RPD를 했을 때 무슨 일이 생기는지를 정량화하지만 비교군이 없어 "안 하는 편이 나았는가"에 답하지 못한다. 본 독일 다기관 RCT는 정확히 그 비교 — 구치를 국소의치로 채우기 vs 제2소구치에서 치열을 끝내기(단축치열, Shortened Dental Arch, SDA) — 를 15년간 추적한 유일한 자료다. 결론이 "두 군 어느 분석에서도 유의차 없음"이라는 점이 임상적으로 결정적이며, 위키의 수복 결정 사다리([[wiki/overviews/prosthetic-materials-decision-ladder]])가 아직 다루지 않은 "채우지 않는다"는 선택지를 근거와 함께 들여온다.

## Three-line Summary

Fifteen-year German multicentre randomised trial (14 dental schools) in patients >35 years with missing molars in one jaw and at least the canine plus one premolar bilaterally, comparing a precision-attachment partial removable dental prosthesis (PRDP, N = 81) against ending the arch at the second premolar (shortened dental arch, SDA, N = 71).

Event-free success for moderate-or-worse failure fell below 50% at 14.2 years in the PRDP group and 14.3 years in the SDA group, and in none of the analyses was a significant group difference found; about half of all patients kept their per-protocol prosthetic status entirely complication-free for more than ten years.

Over fifteen years the choice between replacing the molars and simply not replacing them did not measurably change prosthetic success, which moves the decision away from prosthetic longevity and onto patient-side factors — cost, invasiveness, chewing demand, and what the patient is prepared to maintain.

## 세줄요약

15년 독일 다기관 무작위 임상시험 (14개 치과대학): 35세 초과, 한쪽 악에 대구치가 없고 양측에 견치와 소구치 최소 1개가 남은 환자에서 정밀부착장치 유지 국소의치 (Partial Removable Dental Prosthesis, PRDP, N = 81) vs 제2소구치에서 치열을 끝내는 단축치열 (Shortened Dental Arch, SDA, N = 71)을 비교했다.

중등도 이상 실패에 대한 무사건 성공률이 50% 아래로 떨어진 시점은 PRDP 14.2년 · SDA 14.3년이었고 **어느 분석에서도 군간 유의차가 없었다**; 전체 환자의 약 절반은 10년 넘게 합병증 없이 최초 보철 상태를 유지했다.

15년에 걸쳐 "대구치를 채우느냐 안 채우느냐"가 보철 성공률을 바꾸지 못했다는 뜻이다. 결정의 축이 보철 수명에서 **환자 쪽 인자** — 비용·침습도·저작 요구·유지관리 감당 능력 — 으로 옮겨간다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- The **only** randomised long-term (15-year) head-to-head of replacing posterior teeth with an RPD versus deliberately not replacing them.
- Establishes that prosthetic-success curves for the two strategies are essentially superimposable (14.2 vs 14.3 years to 50% event-free success).
- Quantifies the durability ceiling honestly: change in prosthetic status is expected, affects the majority by 15 years, and rises almost linearly from soon after treatment.
- Supplies the evidence base for offering SDA as a legitimate definitive plan rather than a compromise.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Multicentre randomised trial across 14 German dental schools and universities. Eligibility: age > 35, molars missing in one jaw, at least the canine and one premolar present on both sides of that jaw. PRDP arm received a precision-attachment retained prosthesis replacing molars and any missing second premolars; SDA arm ended the arch at the second premolar, which had to be present or replaced by a cantilever fixed dental prosthesis. Follow-up examinations over 15 years. Outcome was a comprehensive composite variable comprising four failure categories, analysed by Kaplan–Meier survival (success) analysis.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | PRDP (N = 81) | SDA (N = 71) | Group difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time to <50% event-free success (moderate-or-worse failure) | 14.2 years | 14.3 years | Not significant |
| Per-protocol status complication-free >10 years | ≈ half of patients | ≈ half of patients | Not significant |
| Any analysis reaching significance | — | — | **None** |

Trajectory: the proportion with a changed prosthetic status increases almost linearly from shortly after treatment and comprises the majority by 15 years.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Ingested **abstract-only**: the composite outcome's four failure categories, per-arm event counts, attrition, and any subgroup analysis are unavailable.
- The composite endpoint mixes categories of differing clinical weight; without the full text the reader cannot see what proportion of "moderate-or-worse failure" was a repair versus a lost abutment.
- Eligibility required a canine plus at least one premolar bilaterally — a comparatively favourable residual dentition; results should not be extended to more reduced arches.
- The PRDP arm used **precision attachments**, not simple cast clasps, so the comparison is against a relatively sophisticated (and costly) removable design.
- Patient-reported outcomes are not in the abstract; the trial's own OHRQoL findings are reported in companion publications.
- German university-clinic care setting; maintenance intensity may exceed routine private practice.

## 7. Glossary

- **SDA (단축치열, Shortened Dental Arch)**: treatment concept in which the dental arch is deliberately ended at the premolars rather than restoring molar support.
- **PRDP (국소의치, Partial Removable Dental Prosthesis)**: removable prosthesis replacing some missing teeth; here retained by precision attachments.
- **Precision attachment (정밀부착장치)**: machined male/female retainer replacing a visible clasp, giving retention with better esthetics at higher cost and technical demand.
- **Cantilever FDP (캔틸레버 고정성 보철)**: fixed prosthesis with an unsupported extension pontic, used here to complete the arch to the second premolar.
- **Event-free success**: Kaplan–Meier estimate of remaining free of any predefined failure category.
