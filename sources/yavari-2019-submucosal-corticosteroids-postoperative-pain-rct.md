## Why Ingested

Addresses postoperative pain management after single-visit root canal treatment (근관치료, RCT) of irreversible pulpitis (비가역적 치수염, IP) using submucosal corticosteroid injection — directly relevant to the wiki's drug/analgesics category and endodontic pain protocol. Compared two corticosteroids (dexamethasone vs betamethasone) against placebo with patient-reported QOL outcomes. See [[yavari-2019-submucosal-corticosteroids-postoperative-pain-rct]].

## Three-line Summary

Submucosal injection of dexamethasone (덱사메타손) or long-acting betamethasone (베타메타손) significantly reduced postoperative pain compared to placebo in 197 patients undergoing single-visit RCT for irreversible pulpitis.
Dexamethasone was superior in the first 24 hours; betamethasone showed equal efficacy at 48 hours and better relief at 72 hours and 7 days due to its long-acting formulation.
Both corticosteroids improved patient quality of life (삶의 질, QOL) over the 7-day follow-up; pain was consistently higher in mandibular versus maxillary teeth and peaked at 12 hours in all groups.

## 세줄요약

비가역적 치수염 (Irreversible Pulpitis, IP) 환자 197명의 1회 방문 근관치료 (Root Canal Treatment, RCT) 후 점막하 코르티코스테로이드 (Corticosteroid) 주사가 위약 대비 통증을 유의하게 감소시켰다.
덱사메타손 (Dexamethasone)은 24시간 이내에, 지속형 베타메타손 (Betamethasone)은 72시간 이후~7일까지 더 효과적이었으며, 48시간에는 두 약제의 효과가 동등하였다.
하악 (Mandible)의 통증이 상악 (Maxilla)보다 전반적으로 높았고, 12시간 시점에 모든 군에서 통증이 정점에 달했으며, 코르티코스테로이드 투여군의 삶의 질 (Quality of Life, QOL)이 위약군보다 높았다.

## Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Endodontics, vol 45, issue 5, pp 477-482
- **Design**: Double-blind randomized clinical trial
- **N**: 242 enrolled; 197 completed (67 placebo, 66 betamethasone, 64 dexamethasone)
- **Follow-up**: 7 days (6h, 12h, 24h, 48h, 72h, 7d intervals)
- **Statistics**: Friedman and Kruskal-Wallis tests; P ≤ .05 significant

## Key Contributions

- First head-to-head RCT comparing submucosal long-acting betamethasone vs dexamethasone vs placebo after single-visit endodontic treatment
- Demonstrates time-dependent advantage: short-acting dexamethasone excels early (≤24h); long-acting betamethasone sustains relief (72h–7d)
- Quantifies QOL improvement alongside pain scores, not just VAS pain alone
- Notes sex had no significant effect on pain in either corticosteroid group

## Methodology

- **Population**: Healthy adults with irreversible pulpitis requiring single-visit root canal treatment
- **Intervention A**: Submucosal injection of long-acting betamethasone
- **Intervention B**: Submucosal injection of dexamethasone
- **Control**: Placebo submucosal injection
- **Blinding**: Double-blind (patient and assessor)
- **Outcome measures**: Self-reported pain severity and QOL questionnaire at baseline and 6h, 12h, 24h, 48h, 72h, 7d post-treatment
- **Attrition**: 45/242 lost to follow-up (18.6%)

## Key Results

| Time Point | Best Performer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 6h | Dexamethasone | Less pain than betamethasone and placebo |
| 12h | Dexamethasone | Pain peaked in all groups; dexamethasone lowest |
| 24h | Dexamethasone | Still superior to betamethasone |
| 48h | Equal (both vs placebo) | Dexamethasone = betamethasone |
| 72h | Betamethasone | Long-acting formulation sustains effect |
| 7d | Betamethasone | Efficacy persisted through full follow-up |

- Both active groups consistently outperformed placebo at all time points
- Mandibular pain > maxillary pain across all groups
- No sex difference in pain response within corticosteroid groups
- QOL higher in both corticosteroid groups vs placebo

## Limitations

- Abstract-only ingest; detailed sample size calculation, specific drug doses/formulations, and randomization method not available
- 18.6% attrition rate may affect result validity
- Healthy patients only — unclear generalizability to medically compromised patients
- Single-visit RCT only; not applicable to multi-visit protocols
- No long-term follow-up beyond 7 days

## Glossary

- **Irreversible Pulpitis (비가역적 치수염, IP)**: Pulpal inflammation that cannot resolve; requires RCT or extraction
- **Submucosal injection (점막하 주사)**: Injection into the soft tissue beneath the mucosa, used for local drug delivery
- **Betamethasone (베타메타손)**: Long-acting synthetic corticosteroid; potent anti-inflammatory
- **Dexamethasone (덱사메타손)**: Shorter-acting synthetic corticosteroid; strong early anti-inflammatory effect
- **QOL (삶의 질, Quality of Life)**: Patient-reported measure of daily function and well-being impacted by pain
