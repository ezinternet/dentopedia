## Why Ingested

Sticky bone (autologous fibrin glue / i-PRF combined with bone substitutes) is a rapidly evolving biomaterial for guided bone regeneration. This 2025 narrative review from *International Journal of Nanomedicine* synthesizes composition, preparation methods, mechanisms, and clinical applications — directly relevant to our `bone-regeneration` pages on GBR materials and platelet concentrates. Reinforces [[bone-regeneration/caponio-2023-platelet-concentrates-bone-formation-arp]] on platelet concentrates in ARP and [[bone-regeneration/assiri-2026-iprf-prf-beta-tcp-bone-regeneration-goat]] on i-PRF + β-TCP.

## Three-line Summary

A narrative review of "sticky bone" — a composite biomaterial combining autologous fibrin glue (AFG) or injectable-PRF (i-PRF) with particulate bone grafts (xenograft, allograft, alloplast). The fibrin network confers gel-like handling, defect adhesion, and sustained growth-factor release (PDGF, TGF-β, VEGF, IGF), enhancing graft stability and osteogenic cell recruitment. Clinical applications span socket preservation, sinus floor elevation, peri-implant defects, and periodontal intrabony defects, with evidence largely from case series and animal studies; high-level human RCTs remain limited.

## 세줄요약

스티키 본(Sticky Bone) — 자가 피브린 접착제(AFG) 또는 주사형 PRF(i-PRF)와 골 이식재(이종골, 동종골, 합성골)를 결합한 복합 생체재료 — 에 대한 종설 논문. 피브린 네트워크가 겔 같은 조작성, 결손부 부착력, 성장인자(PDGF, TGF-β, VEGF, IGF) 지속 방출을 제공해 이식재 안정성과 골형성 세포 모집을 향상. 치조제 보존, 상악동 거상술, 임플란트 주위 결손, 치주 골내 결손 등 다양한 적용례가 보고되었으나, 고수준 인체 RCT 증거는 아직 제한적임.

## Key Contributions

- Comprehensive classification of sticky bone preparations: AFG-based vs i-PRF-based vs self-folding i-PRF techniques
- Detailed biological property analysis: biocompatibility, biodegradability, bioactive agent delivery, scaffold mimicry, cell affinity
- Mechanistic overview: growth factor release kinetics, osteogenic/angiogenic signaling, macrophage polarization (M2), MSC recruitment/differentiation
- Clinical application mapping across oral surgery indications with representative case reports
- Future directions: 3D printing integration, peptide-functionalized matrices, personalized biomimetics

## Methodology

- **Design**: Narrative review (non-systematic)
- **Search**: Literature on sticky bone, platelet concentrates, bone substitutes, bone regeneration
- **Scope**: Composition, preparation methods, biological properties, mechanisms of action, clinical applications
- **Timeframe**: 2010 (Sohn et al. introduction) to 2025
- **Evidence base**: In vitro, animal (rabbit, goat, rat), case reports, case series; few human comparative studies

## Key Results

| Preparation Type | Fibrin Source | Key Characteristics |
|------------------|---------------|---------------------|
| AFG-based sticky bone | Autologous fibrin glue (thrombin + fibrinogen) | Requires commercial AFG kit; two-component mixing; controlled polymerization |
| i-PRF-based sticky bone | Injectable-PRF (lower centrifugation, no anticoagulant) | Simpler chairside; liquid i-PRF mixed with graft; "steak" concept (Mourão 2015) |
| Self-folding i-PRF | i-PRF membrane folding technique (Csongé 2023) | More homogeneous cellular composition; extended in vivo residence; sustained cytokine release |

**Biological properties highlighted**:
- Biocompatibility: minimal immune response, natural bone matrix components
- Biodegradability: gradual resorption synchronized with new bone formation
- Growth factor delivery: PDGF, TGF-β1, VEGF, IGF-1, BMP-2 released over 7–14 days
- Scaffold structure: 3D fibrin network mimics provisional ECM, porosity ~50–200 μm
- Cell affinity: MSC attachment, proliferation, osteogenic differentiation upregulated

**Clinical applications & evidence level**:
- Socket preservation / ridge preservation: case series, animal histomorphometry
- Sinus floor elevation (lateral/transcrestal): case reports, small case series
- Peri-implant dehiscence / GBR: case reports
- Periodontal intrabony / furcation defects: limited case series
- Alveolar cleft / large defects: case reports

## Limitations

- Narrative (non-systematic) review — selection bias possible
- Majority of clinical evidence from case reports/series (low evidence level)
- Few comparative human RCTs vs conventional GBR or particulate grafts alone
- Heterogeneous preparation protocols (centrifugation, AFG kits, i-PRF protocols) limit reproducibility
- Long-term clinical outcomes (implant survival, bone maintenance >5 yr) not established
- Cost-effectiveness vs standard GBR not analyzed
- Regulatory status of AFG/i-PRF varies by country

## Glossary

- **Sticky Bone (스티키 본, 自体フィブリン接着骨)** — AFG 또는 i-PRF와 골 이식 입자를 혼합해 만든 점착성 골 이식 복합체
- **Autologous Fibrin Glue, AFG (자가 피브린 접착제)** — 환자 혈액에서 분리한 피브리노겐과 트롬빈을 혼합해 만든 생체 접착제
- **Injectable Platelet-Rich Fibrin, i-PRF (주사형 PRF)** — 항응고제 없이 저속 원심분리해 얻은 액상 피브린 매트릭스; 성장인자 고농도 함유
- **Platelet Concentrates (혈소판 농축제)** — PRP, PRF, i-PRF, CGF 등 혈소판 및 성장인자를 농축한 혈액 유래 제제 총칭
- **Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR (유도 골 재생)** — 차폐막과 골 이식재를 이용해 골 결손부를 재생시키는 수술 기법
- **Osteoconduction (골전도)** — 골 이식재가 제공하는 골조(scaffold)를 따라 골 세포가 이동·증식해 신생골이 형성되는 현상
