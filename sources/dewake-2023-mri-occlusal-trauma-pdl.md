## Three-line Summary

T2-weighted IDEAL MRI of the periodontal ligament space was applied to 20 bruxism subjects and showed a significant correlation (ρ = 0.529) between maximum signal intensity and a 7-item clinical occlusal trauma score, suggesting MRI can quantify PDL edema caused by occlusal overload.

## 세줄요약

20명의 이갈이(bruxism) 피험자에서 T2 강조 IDEAL MRI 로 치주인대강(PDL space) 최대 신호강도를 측정한 결과, 7항목 교합외상 임상 점수와 유의한 상관(ρ = 0.529, p < 0.001)을 보여 MRI 가 교합외상을 정량화할 수 있는 새로운 방법임을 제안하였다.

## Why Ingested

First study to quantify occlusal trauma severity using MRI signal intensity of the periodontal ligament space. Demonstrates a non-ionizing, objective alternative to purely qualitative clinical scoring for occlusal trauma diagnosis. Relevant for understanding PDL edema detection and early-stage occlusal trauma assessment. Related to [[radiology/mortazavi-2016-lesions-associated-with-periodontal-ligament]] (PDL widening differential) and [[implants/survival/song-2021-implant-complications-bruxism-patients]] (bruxism biomechanics).

## Study Design

Cross-sectional observational study. 20 subjects (9M/11F, mean age 35.9 ± 14.0 years, range 22–65), including 10 periodontology patients with bruxism chief complaint + 10 bruxism-aware volunteers. All subjects: PD < 4 mm with no BOP at any site; ≥ 20 teeth (excluding third molars); normal occlusal relationship; no dental irregularities. Ethics approval: Matsumoto Dental University No. 0138.

## Methods

**Clinical occlusal trauma score (0–7):** Seven findings each scored present/absent: (1) subjective bruxism symptoms; (2) tooth mobility; (3) fremitus; (4) excessive occlusal contact area (Dental Prescale / Occluser); (5) excessive occlusal force; (6) PDL space widening on radiograph; (7) lamina dura thickening. Total score = degree of occlusal trauma.

**Intraoral radiographs:** DIGORA Optime PSP system; 70 kVp / 10 mA; bisected-angle technique; blinded reader for radiographic signs.

**MRI:** Signa HDx 1.5T (GE Healthcare); two 3-inch surface coils placed bilaterally at cheeks. Sequence: T2-weighted IDEAL water imaging (TR 2,500 ms; TE 107 ms; matrix 256×256; FOV 24 cm; section thickness 5.0 mm; gap 1.0 mm; 4 min 33 s). Evaluation plane set parallel to occlusal plane at 12 mm toward root apex from occlusal contact point. Maximum grayscale pixel value within extracted PDL-equivalent area = maximum signal intensity.

**Statistics:** Spearman's rank correlation (ρ); Kruskal–Wallis test; Mann–Whitney U post-hoc. p < 0.05 significant. SPSS v18.0.

## Key Results

**Occlusal trauma scores:** All 20 subjects had at least one clinical manifestation. Score distribution: 0 = 90 teeth (16.4%); 1 = 143 (26.2%); 2 = 113 (20.6%); 3 = 126 (23.1%). Molars showed higher scores (max 6) than anteriors (max 4).

**MRI signal intensity by score (all teeth, mean ± SD):**
- Score 0: 40.0 ± 5.9
- Score 1: 46.7 ± 8.8
- Score 2: 49.6 ± 9.9
- Score 3: 53.3 ± 11.9
- Score 4: 57.6 ± 12.0
- Score 5: 65.7 ± 15.8
- Score 6: 73.4 ± 1.5

**Spearman correlation (ρ, p < 0.001):**
- All teeth: ρ = 0.529
- Anterior teeth: ρ = 0.517
- Molar teeth: ρ = 0.396

**Signal intensity by tooth type (mean ± SD):**
- Central incisor: 44.1 ± 10.2
- Lateral incisor: 45.4 ± 10.0
- Canine: 45.9 ± 9.0
- First premolar: 49.8 ± 9.6
- Second premolar: 53.0 ± 13.2
- First molar: 55.3 ± 12.5
- Second molar: 53.9 ± 12.5
- Total: 49.6 ± 11.8

Mann–Whitney post-hoc: significant differences between most score pairs; not significant between consecutive moderate-severity scores (e.g., 1–2, 2–3 points).

## Discussion / Clinical Notes

- MRI (IDEAL T2) detects PDL edema quantitatively — consistent with edema pathophysiology of occlusal trauma (Svanberg & Lindhe 1974).
- Molars: higher absolute signal intensity but lower ρ (0.396 vs 0.517) possibly due to greater SD at high trauma scores.
- Anteriors: narrower score range (0–4), more homogeneous signal response.
- **Early detection claim:** teeth with signal intensity exceeding mean + SD within their clinical score tier may represent sub-clinical early-stage occlusal trauma — the first objective evidence for early-stage PDL edema without clinical manifestations.
- Prophylactic occlusal adjustment traditionally avoided (removal of cooperative contacts risks new premature contacts); MRI-confirmed early edema could justify prophylactic treatment on specifically flagged teeth.
- IDEAL method adapted from Ariji et al. 2010 (masseter muscle edema assessment during biting).

## Limitations

1. n = 20; small number of teeth with score ≥ 5–6.
2. Subjects selected for bruxism symptoms → asymptomatic occlusal trauma not captured.
3. Plaque control status not assessed.
4. Wide age range (22–65); no age-stratified analysis.
5. MRI contraindicated in patients with metal implants, orthodontic appliances, vascular stents, artificial joints; claustrophobia.
6. Correlation with digital occlusal force device not performed.
7. Signal-to-noise ratio not calculated.
