## Why Ingested
근관치료 성공률 향상 서베일런스. 비수술적 재근관치료(NS retreatment)의 임상·방사선적 성공률 및 예후 인자를 30개 연구(RCT + cohort)로 종합한 SR. 근관 재치료 시 CBCT 활용, 현미경·NiTi 파일·초음파 관개 등 현대 술식이 예후를 향상시킨다는 최신 근거를 종합. 기존 위키 [[endodontics/irrigation/artaza-2024-outcome-nonsurgical-root-canal-treatment]] 보완.

## One-line Summary
Systematic review (30 studies, Jan 1988–Sep 2025) of nonsurgical endodontic retreatment for persistent AP: favorable prognosis consistently linked to absent/small preoperative lesions, adequate obturation and coronal seal, specialist operators, and contemporary techniques (CBCT, microscope, NiTi rotary, ultrasonic irrigation).

## 한줄요약
체계적 문헌고찰 (30개 연구): 비수술적 재근관치료의 우호적 예후 인자는 술전 병소 없음/작은 크기, 적절한 충전 및 치관 밀폐, 전문의 시술, 현대적 술식(CBCT·현미경·NiTi·초음파 관개) 사용.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 30 studies (RCTs and cohorts, ≥10 teeth each) on nonsurgical retreatment for persistent apical periodontitis, spanning 37 years of literature (January 1988 – September 2025) across five databases plus grey literature.

Heterogeneity in outcome definitions, imaging modalities and follow-up intervals precluded meta-analysis, but four prognostic factors held consistently across studies — absence of a preoperative periapical lesion, smaller lesion size, adequate obturation and coronal seal, and a specialist operator — and CBCT-based strict criteria again produced systematically lower healing rates than 2D radiographs.

Contemporary armamentarium (CBCT, operating microscope, NiTi rotary instrumentation, ultrasonic irrigation, thermoplastic obturation) has improved retreatment predictability enough that nonsurgical retreatment should be prioritized over extraction in primary endodontic failure; the synthesis is narrative only, so no pooled effect estimate is available.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR): 지속성 치근단치주염 (Persistent Apical Periodontitis)에 대한 비외과적 재치료를 다룬 30편(무작위대조시험 및 코호트, 각 10치 이상)을 5개 데이터베이스와 회색문헌에서 37년치(1988년 1월~2025년 9월) 수집.

결과 정의·영상 방식·추적 기간의 이질성으로 메타분석은 불가했으나, 네 가지 예후인자가 연구 전반에서 일관됐다 — 술전 치근단 병소 없음, 작은 병소 크기, 적절한 근관충전과 치관부 밀폐, 전문의 술자. CBCT 엄격기준은 여기서도 2D 방사선보다 체계적으로 낮은 치유율을 보였다.

현대 장비(CBCT, 수술현미경, 니켈티타늄 (Nickel-Titanium, NiTi) 회전기구, 초음파 세척, 열가소성 근관충전)가 재치료 예측성을 충분히 끌어올렸으므로, **1차 근관치료 실패 시 발치보다 비외과적 재치료를 우선**해야 한다 — 다만 서술적 종합이라 통합 효과추정치는 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Comprehensive SR spanning 37 years of NS retreatment evidence
- Strict CBCT criteria consistently show lower healing rates than 2D radiographs
- Contemporary techniques significantly improved retreatment predictability vs historical data
- Four consistent prognostic factors identified across studies
- Narrative synthesis due to heterogeneity in outcome definitions and imaging modalities

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Systematic review (PRISMA 2020 guidelines)
- Population: Teeth with persistent AP after prior endodontic treatment
- Intervention: Nonsurgical endodontic retreatment (orthograde approach)
- Primary outcome: Periapical healing (radiograph and/or CBCT)
- Secondary outcomes: Tooth survival, prognostic factors
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2 (RCTs), ROBINS-I (cohort), Newcastle-Ottawa Scale
- Results: Narrative synthesis (quantitative meta-analysis not possible due to heterogeneity)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Success rates by imaging modality:**
- Strict criteria / CBCT-based: generally lower apparent healing rates
- Loose criteria / 2D radiograph: higher apparent healing rates
- Magnitude of difference could not be reliably quantified across studies

**Favorable prognostic factors (consistent across studies):**
1. Absence of preoperative periapical lesion
2. Smaller periapical lesion size
3. Adequate obturation quality + adequate coronal seal
4. Specialist operator

**Contemporary technique advantages documented:**
- CBCT: improved lesion detection and treatment planning
- Dental operating microscope: improved access and preparation
- NiTi rotary files: improved removal efficiency and canal shaping
- Ultrasonic irrigation: improved disinfection
- Thermoplastic obturation: improved seal

**Historical context:** Retreatment success rates have improved with contemporary techniques; NS retreatment should be prioritized over extraction in primary endodontic failure.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Heterogeneity in outcome definitions, imaging modalities, and follow-up intervals — precluded meta-analysis
- Need for standardized CBCT-specific scoring systems (PAI-based)
- Longer, uniform follow-up periods (>2 years) needed
- Consistent use of validated periapical indices recommended for future studies

## 7. Glossary
- NS retreatment / NS-ReTx: Nonsurgical endodontic retreatment (orthograde removal of prior filling + re-instrumentation + re-obturation)
- Persistent AP: Apical periodontitis persisting or recurring after prior root canal treatment
- PAI: Periapical Index — radiographic scoring scale
- CBCT: Cone-beam computed tomography
- NiTi: Nickel-titanium rotary files
- Coronal seal: Adequate coronal restoration preventing microleakage into the root canal system
