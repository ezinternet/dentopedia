## Why Ingested

수평 치조골 증대를 위한 유도골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) 프로토콜의 원전급 서술적 고찰로, 차폐막·이식재·결손 분류·수술 술식의 근거 체계를 한 페이지에 정리하기 위해 인제스트. 현재 위키에 보유한 [[bone-regeneration/buser-2023-gbr-implant-35years-basic-principle-review]]는 이 논문 이후 9년의 누적 근거를 반영한 업데이트 고찰이며, 본 논문은 그 선행 기반 고찰로서 결손 분류·재료 선택 로직의 출처 역할을 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Benic & Hämmerle, Periodontology 2000, 2014) synthesizing evidence on guided bone regeneration (GBR) for horizontal alveolar ridge augmentation, covering barrier membranes, bone substitutes, defect classification (Class 1–5), and surgical techniques for both simultaneous and staged implant placement.

Native collagen membranes + deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM) are the established standard for most GBR indications; autogenous bone blocks are preferred for large horizontal defects requiring volume stability; titanium-reinforced e-PTFE membranes are reserved for Class 3 dehiscence defects without bone wall support.

As a 2014 narrative review without systematic search protocol or meta-analysis, quantitative outcome pooling is absent; the defect classification (Class 1–5) and material selection hierarchy remain clinically influential foundations for subsequent evidence synthesis.

## 세줄요약

Benic & Hämmerle (Periodontology 2000, 2014) 서술적 고찰 — 수평 치조골 증대를 위한 유도골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)의 차폐막·이식재·결손 분류 (Class 1–5)·수술 술식 총정리; 동시 식립과 단계적 프로토콜 모두 포괄.

천연 콜라겐막 + 탈단백 우골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)이 대부분 GBR 적응증의 표준; 대형 수평 결손은 자가골 블록 권장; 골벽 지지 없는 Class 3 결손은 티타늄 강화 팽창 폴리테트라플루오로에틸렌 (expanded Polytetrafluoroethylene, e-PTFE) 차폐막 적용.

체계적 검색·메타분석 없는 서술적 고찰로 정량적 결과 통합 한계; Class 1–5 결손 분류와 재료 선택 계층구조는 이후 근거 종합의 임상적 기반으로 남아 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Membrane classification**: Nonresorbable (e-PTFE, d-PTFE, Ti-mesh, Ti foil) vs. resorbable — natural (native collagen, cross-linked collagen, freeze-dried fascia lata) vs. synthetic (PLGA, PEG, polyglactin) — with clinical pros/cons for each subtype.
2. **Bone graft taxonomy**: Four categories — autograft, allograft, xenograft (DBBM as gold standard), alloplast — with requirements for GBR graft materials (biocompatibility, osteoconductivity, mechanical membrane support, biodegradability, volume replacement).
3. **Defect classification (Class 1–5)**: Structured framework linking defect morphology to material and technique selection:
   - Class 1: Alveolar crest defect at extraction socket (simultaneous implant placement + resorbable membrane + bone substitute)
   - Class 2: Peri-implant dehiscence with bone wall support (resorbable membrane + particulate graft)
   - Class 3: Peri-implant dehiscence without bone wall support (Ti-reinforced e-PTFE + particulate graft)
   - Class 4: Combined horizontal + vertical defect
   - Class 5: Vertical ridge deficiency (autogenous bone block ± graft + resorbable membrane, staged approach)
4. **Primary ridge augmentation protocol**: Autogenous bone block (chin or retromolar ramus donor) fixed with metal screws + DBBM coverage + resorbable membrane; 4–6 months healing before implant placement.
5. **Current research directions**: Growth factors (BMP-2, PDGF, GDF-5, PTH), injectable calcium phosphate cements, and equine-derived collagenated bone substitutes as emerging alternatives.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Type**: Narrative review; no systematic search protocol, PRISMA, or pre-registration
- **Coverage**: Animal (rat, dog, monkey) and human clinical studies, RCTs, systematic reviews through approximately 2013
- **Scope**: Horizontal augmentation emphasized; vertical augmentation addressed as Class 5; simultaneous vs. staged implant placement discussed
- **Reference base**: ~242 citations spanning preclinical models through multicenter RCTs

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Membranes:**
- Native collagen membranes: current standard for majority of GBR indications; spontaneous secondary epithelialization on exposure (clinical advantage over e-PTFE); major drawback = poor collapse resistance + fast degradation
- Cross-linked collagen: prolonged degradation but higher inflammatory response and mucosal dehiscence reported; contrasting results across different crosslinking methods
- e-PTFE (Ti-reinforced): established for challenging Class 3 defects and large horizontal/vertical augmentations; premature exposure → rapid microbial colonization → infection and augmentation failure
- PEG (polyethylene glycol) hydrogel: comparable to native collagen for peri-implant dehiscences; promising for staged lateral ridge augmentation in preclinical studies
- PLGA: 81% vertical defect fill vs. 96% for Ti-reinforced e-PTFE in one multicenter RCT; inferior volume maintenance

**Bone substitutes:**
- DBBM (Bio-Oss): gold standard xenograft; osteoconductivity well-documented; slow/incomplete resorption (particles found unchanged at 11 years after sinus floor augmentation); block DBBM for horizontal defects showed firm integration but moderate new bone formation histologically
- Autograft block: highest biological potential (osteogenic + osteoinductive + osteoconductive); donor site morbidity and volume loss (up to 55% in some series) are main limitations; chin > ramus for volume but ramus > chin for neurosensory safety
- Allograft block: viable option for atrophic anterior maxilla (33 ± 18% new bone, 26 ± 17% residual allograft, 98.8% implant survival at mean 48 months); limited long-term data
- DBBM blocks + collagen membrane: 11/12 patients sufficient bone for prosthetically optimal implant placement at 9–10 months (one case series); more clinical evidence needed

**Technique outcomes:**
- Submerged vs. transmucosal healing: equivalent outcomes in multiple RCTs for survival, marginal bone level, and soft-tissue parameters; submerged mode loses ~1 mm keratinized mucosa width (flap coronal advancement effect)
- Cortical perforation of recipient bed: recommended to improve vascularization and bone repair
- Tension-free primary closure: critical for GBR success regardless of membrane type

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No systematic search or meta-analysis; evidence synthesis is expert-narrative rather than statistically pooled
- Many cited studies are small single-center series; long-term RCT data on ridge augmentation before implant placement were limited as of 2014
- Growth factor and injectable graft data were largely preclinical at time of writing
- The defect classification (Class 1–5) is descriptive and not formally validated in a prospective multicenter study

## 7. Glossary

- **유도골재생 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)**: 차폐막으로 비골성 세포를 차단하고 골형성 세포가 결손 공간을 점유하도록 유도하는 기법
- **탈단백 우골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)**: 소 유래 무기질만 남긴 이종골 이식재; Bio-Oss가 대표 제품
- **팽창 폴리테트라플루오로에틸렌 (expanded Polytetrafluoroethylene, e-PTFE)**: 비흡수성 막; 공극 구조로 세포 차단; 노출 시 오염 위험
- **치밀 폴리테트라플루오로에틸렌 (dense Polytetrafluoroethylene, d-PTFE)**: 무공극 비흡수성 막; 노출 시 e-PTFE보다 오염 저항성 양호
- **폴리에틸렌 글리콜 (Polyethylene Glycol, PEG)**: 현장 중합 합성 흡수성 막; 가수분해로 분해; 산성 부산물 없음
- **폴리락트산-폴리글리콜산 공중합체 (Polylactic acid/Polyglycolic acid copolymer, PLGA)**: 합성 흡수성 막; 큰 결손에서 e-PTFE 대비 공간유지력 열세
- **자가골 (Autograft)**: 동일 개체 유래 골; 골유도·골형성·골전도 3특성 모두 보유; 공여부 이환율이 단점
- **PASS 원칙**: 일차 폐쇄(Primary closure)·혈관신생(Angiogenesis)·공간유지(Space maintenance)·안정성(Stability) — GBR 성공의 4조건 (Buser 등이 정식화)
