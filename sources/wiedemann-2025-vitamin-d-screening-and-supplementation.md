## Why Ingested

Narrative literature review (PubMed, Google Scholar, Cochrane, SCOPUS) that links preoperative vitamin D deficiency to early dental implant failure (EDIF) and argues for routine serum screening and supplementation before implant surgery. It sits beside the systematic review [[implants/vitamin-d/miron-2025-vitamin-d-deficiency-early-implant-failure]] (same senior authors) and the clinical association study [[implants/vitamin-d/francis-2024-low-serum-vitamin-d-early-implant-failure]], and supports the modifiable-systemic-factor branch of [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]. → [[implants/vitamin-d/wiedemann-2025-vitamin-d-screening-and-supplementation]]

## Three-line Summary

Narrative literature review of vitamin D3 status and early dental implant failure (EDIF), plus the effect of systemic and topical vitamin D3 supplementation on bone-to-implant contact (BIC) and peri-implant bone level.

Vitamin D deficiency was associated with nearly a fourfold increase in overall EDIF incidence; presurgical supplementation increased osseointegration, BIC and bone-level maintenance and decreased EDIF, even in diabetic subjects.

The authors recommend preoperative serum screening and supplementation for sub-physiologic levels (30 ng/mL or below) and for smokers, diabetics, obese and immunocompromised patients; the evidence is a non-systematic review read from the abstract only.

## 세줄요약

비타민 D3 수치와 조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Dental Implant Failure, EDIF)의 관계, 그리고 전신·국소 비타민 D3 보충이 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)과 임플란트 주위 골수준에 미치는 효과를 고찰한 서술적 문헌고찰.

비타민 D 결핍은 전체 EDIF 발생률을 거의 4배 증가시켰고, 수술 전 보충은 골유착·BIC·골수준 유지를 높이고 당뇨 환자 등 고위험군에서도 EDIF를 감소시킨 것으로 보고.

저자들은 혈청 수치 30 ng/mL 이하 및 흡연자·당뇨·비만·면역저하 환자에서 수술 전 선별검사와 보충을 권고; 근거는 비체계적 고찰이며 초록만 확인한 상태.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reviews the correlation between EDIF and reduced serum vitamin D levels.
- Evaluates systemic and topical vitamin D3 supplementation in terms of BIC and peri-implant bone level.
- Proposes a hypothesis that vitamin D3 supplementation mitigates the negative effect of deficiency.
- Recommends adding preoperative serum vitamin D evaluation to the standard implant protocol.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Literature review; no number of included studies, search dates, inclusion criteria or risk-of-bias method appears in the abstract.
- **Outcomes assessed**: EDIF, biological factors (peri-implant bone level), BIC.
- **Setting, n (patients, implants), EDIF time window**: not reported in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Low vitamin D serum levels are described as increasingly accepted to harm osseointegration and immune modulation; deficiency can impair peri-implant bone formation.
- Vitamin D deficiency resulted in nearly a fourfold increase in overall EDIF incidence (no CI or pooled estimate given in the abstract).
- Presurgical vitamin D3 supplementation: increased osseointegration, increased BIC, enhanced bone level maintenance, decreased EDIF, including at-risk demographics such as diabetic subjects.
- Risk factors named as warranting supplementation: serum level 30 ng/mL or below; smokers, diabetics, obese individuals, compromised immune system.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract only; study counts, effect sizes with CIs, EDIF definition and early-failure rate are not available.
- Narrative (non-systematic) review design; the fourfold figure is not a pooled estimate in the abstract.
- The supplementation recommendation rests on a hypothesis stated by the authors and on heterogeneous underlying studies.

## 7. Glossary

- **EDIF (Early Dental Implant Failure)**: failure of an implant to osseointegrate, occurring early in healing.
- **BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact)**: proportion of implant surface in direct bone contact.
- **Vitamin D3 (cholecalciferol)**: supplement form of vitamin D; serum level 30 ng/mL or below was termed sub-physiologic in this paper.
