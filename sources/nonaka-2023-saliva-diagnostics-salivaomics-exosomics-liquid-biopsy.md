## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (JADA, Wong/UCLA group) framing three pillars of saliva-based diagnostics — salivaomics, saliva exosomics, and saliva liquid biopsy — and introducing the EFIRM electrochemical platform for noninvasive ctDNA detection of lung cancer, SARS-CoV-2, and other systemic disease from saliva.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of the American Dental Association (JADA)
- **Type**: Narrative/expert review (Types of Studies Reviewed = discussion of salivaomics, exosomics, saliva diagnostic mechanisms)
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.adaj.2023.05.006
- **PMID**: 37500232 · **PMCID**: PMC11390004
- **Corresponding group**: David T.W. Wong laboratory (UCLA), a foundational group in salivaomics.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Establishes the **three-pillar framework** of modern saliva diagnostics: salivaomics, saliva exosomics, saliva liquid biopsy.
- Frames the central unsolved problem: the **mechanism by which systemic (blood-borne) biomarkers migrate to saliva** is still poorly understood — the main barrier to bench-to-bedside translation.
- Reports that **20–30% of the salivary proteome overlaps with the plasma proteome**, supporting a vascular/lymphatic transport origin for many salivary constituents.
- Introduces **EFIRM (Electric Field–Induced Release and Measurement)** — an electrochemical sensor detecting <100 target molecules without extraction/amplification, ≈30 min, 40–50 µL sample.
- Documents **CLIA-certified EFIRM assays** for EGFR L858R / exon 19del / T790M (plasma and saliva) and SARS-CoV-2 IgG S1 (saliva).
- Introduces **ultrashort cell-free DNA (uscfDNA)**, 25–75 nt (modal 50 nt), as a novel cfDNA subtype distinct from 167 bp mononucleosomal cfDNA.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative synthesis, not a systematic review. The authors summarize evidence across omics catalogs (salivary proteome/exRNA Atlas), preclinical exosome models, and EFIRM assay development.

- **Salivaomics**: high-throughput DNA/RNA/protein/metabolite/microbiome profiling of saliva; requires protease + ribonuclease inhibitors to prevent amylase-dependent protein degradation and RNA loss; must control for food, oral bacteria, rest-vs-stimulated sampling.
- **Exosomics**: analysis of 30–100 nm exosome cargos; lipid bilayer protects contents from proteases/RNases.
- **Liquid biopsy / EFIRM**: paired capture + detector probes; capture probe electropolymerized onto a gold electrode with pyrrole; cyclic square-wave electric field drives near-field mixing so only perfectly matched sequences hybridize → single-nucleotide-variant detection in complex biofluids.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Proteome overlap**: 20–30% salivary/plasma proteome shared.
- **exRNA**: saliva exRNAs = 2.5% (256/9,892) of RNA-seq data in the exRNA Atlas.
- **Preclinical mechanism**: dominant-negative Rab11 GTPase in tumor cells abolished exosome biogenesis and removed tumor mRNA signal from saliva (pancreatic xenograft); human CD63-GFP vesicles + GAPDH recovered from saliva of lung-cancer xenograft mice — proving circulatory transport of tumor vesicles to the salivary gland.
- **Biological activity**: saliva from tumor-bearing mice suppressed NK-cell markers CD69/NKG2D in control animals; reversed by dominant-negative Rab11.
- **Clinical lung cancer**: saliva EFIRM detected recurrence-related **T790M ctDNA in 2/26 patients** that digital-droplet PCR and NGS missed.
- **EFIRM SARS-CoV-2 triplex** (antigen + neutralizing antibody + genomic RNA, 50 µL) outperformed all EUA-approved tests for all 3 biomarkers.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Core mechanism of systemic-to-saliva biomarker transport remains incompletely understood.
- Bulk-saliva noise (food, oral microbiota, rest vs stimulated) complicates biomarker isolation.
- Much mechanistic evidence is preclinical (murine xenograft); clinical validation is early (small n, e.g. 26 lung-cancer patients).
- Commercial partnerships and regulatory (FDA EUA/CLIA) steps needed for scale-up.
- Future frontiers: salivary exosome–gut microbiome interactions; combining uscfDNA biopsy with EFIRM.

## 7. Glossary

- **Salivaomics** — integrative omics (genomics/transcriptomics/proteomics/metabolomics/microbiomics) study of saliva.
- **Saliva exosomics** — analysis of molecular cargo inside 30–100 nm salivary exosomes.
- **Liquid biopsy** — detection of tumor-derived material (CTC, ctDNA, EV) in a body fluid instead of tissue biopsy.
- **ctDNA** — circulating tumor DNA carrying tumor-specific somatic mutations; amount ∝ disease burden.
- **EFIRM** — Electric Field–Induced Release and Measurement; electrochemical assay for low-abundance nucleic-acid targets in biofluids without extraction/amplification.
- **uscfDNA** — ultrashort cell-free DNA, 25–75 nt subtype distinct from 167 bp mononucleosomal cfDNA.
