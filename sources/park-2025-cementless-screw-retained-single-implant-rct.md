## Why Ingested

The wiki's cementless screw-retained axis has rested on biomechanics only — FEA evidence in [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/lee-2021-cement-vs-cementless-screw-retained-fea]] and [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/lee-2025-screwless-hook-retained-implant-fea]], synthesized in [[wiki/overviews/implant-prosthesis-retention-screw-vs-cement-overview]]. This is the first RCT specifically on a fully digital cementless screw-retained (CL-SRP) workflow for single posterior implants, closing the clinical-efficiency and early-MBL evidence gap on that axis.

## Three-line Summary

Preliminary single-center RCT (CONSORT; 40 implants / 35 patients, ~6-month follow-up) comparing a conventional combined screw-and-cement-retained prosthesis (CSCRP) workflow with a fully digital cementless screw-retained prosthesis (CL-SRP, Osstem DR screw) for posterior single-implant restorations.

The CL-SRP workflow significantly shortened total prosthetic time (588.79 ± 270.32 s vs 1363.15 ± 347.11 s, p < 0.001), impression-taking time (261.58 vs 466.10 s) and placement time (259.21 vs 918.25 s), and showed lower early marginal bone loss (0.47 ± 0.28 mm vs 0.71 ± 0.33 mm, p < 0.05); adjustment time/volume and gingival shape changes did not differ.

Digital cementless workflow improved chairside efficiency and early peri-implant bone preservation with prosthetic accuracy comparable to the conventional protocol, but small n, ~6-month follow-up, and combined test conditions (digital + cementless) limit generalizability; long-term trials are needed.

## 세줄요약

예비 단일기관 무작위대조시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; CONSORT; 40개 임플란트/35명, 약 6개월 추적) — 구치부 단일 임플란트 수복에서 재래식 혼합 나사·시멘트유지 보철(Combined Screw- and Cement-Retained Prosthesis, CSCRP)과 완전 디지털 시멘트리스 나사유지 보철(Cementless Screw-Retained Prosthesis, CL-SRP, 오스템 DR 스크류)을 비교.

CL-SRP군에서 총 보철시간(588.79±270.32초 vs 1363.15±347.11초, p<0.001)·인상채득시간(261.58 vs 466.10초)·장착시간(259.21 vs 918.25초)이 유의하게 짧았고, 조기 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)도 유의하게 낮음(0.47±0.28 mm vs 0.71±0.33 mm, p<0.05); 조정시간·조정량·치은형태 변화는 차이 없음.

디지털 시멘트리스 워크플로우는 보철 정확도를 유지하면서 체어타임 효율과 조기 임플란트 주위 골보존을 개선했으나, 표본 수가 작고(35명) 추적이 짧으며(약 6개월) 디지털화+시멘트리스가 결합된 조건이라 요인 분리가 안 됨 — 장기 연구 필요.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Functional Biomaterials 2025;16(10):378
- **DOI**: 10.3390/jfb16100378
- **Institution**: Hallym University Sacred Heart Hospital / Hallym University (Anyang, Chuncheon), Republic of Korea

## 2. Key Contributions

- First randomized clinical data on a fully digital **CL-SRP** (cementless screw-retained, titanium base + CAD/CAM zirconia crown, no cement at any stage) workflow for single posterior implants
- Quantified chairside efficiency gain: total prosthetic time −57% (588.79 vs 1363.15 s, d = 2.489), driven mainly by prosthesis placement (−72%)
- Early MBL significantly lower with the one-time base-abutment (OAOT-style) protocol (0.24 mm mean difference, p < 0.05), independent of prosthetic accuracy
- Demonstrated equivalent prosthetic accuracy (STL-quantified adjustment volume) and soft-tissue response despite workflow change

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Preliminary randomized controlled clinical trial (CONSORT); 1:1 computer-generated allocation; participants and outcome assessors blinded (clinicians not blinded); IRB HALLYM IRB 2024-05-001; KNCT KCT0009159
- **n**: 40 implants in 35 patients (20 control / 20 test); G*Power sample-size calculation (effect size 1.2, power 0.95, 20% dropout → 40 implants)
- **Control (CSCRP)**: PVS stock-tray impression → custom titanium abutment + monolithic zirconia crown cemented (RelyX U200) → extraoral excess-cement removal → 30 Ncm
- **Test (CL-SRP)**: prefabricated DR base abutment torqued 30 Ncm once → TRIOS 3 intraoral scan → CAD/CAM monolithic zirconia crown → 20 Ncm prosthetic screw, no cement
- **Implants**: Osstem TS III, Ø4.0–5.0 mm × 8.5–11.5 mm, ~1 mm subcrestal, one-stage surgery, ~12-week healing
- **Outcomes**: impression-taking (IT) / prosthesis placement (PP) / adjustment (PA) times (stopwatch); adjustment volume via STL superimposition (Geomagic Control X); gingival shape change (µm, 3D superimposition); MBL (periapical radiographs, mesial+distal, ICC 0.88)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (35 patients / 40 implants); power calculation was based on in vitro pilot data
- Short follow-up (~6 months after placement) — insufficient for long-term MBL stability, survival, or mechanical complications
- Confounded test conditions: digital workflow and cementless design were combined, so each factor's contribution cannot be isolated
- Control was CSCRP (crown intraorally cemented then removed extraorally for cement cleanup) — both arms are screw-retained variants, not a strict cemented vs screw-retained comparison
- No patient-reported outcomes (satisfaction/comfort); results limited to posterior single implants with favorable angulation

## 7. Glossary

- **CSCRP**: Combined Screw- and Cement-Retained Prosthesis — crown cemented to abutment (intraoral), assembly secured to fixture by abutment screw
- **CL-SRP**: Cementless Screw-Retained Prosthesis — titanium base + CAD/CAM crown retained by prosthetic screw, no cement
- **OAOT / one-abutment-one-time**: definitive abutment placed once and never disconnected, intended to preserve the peri-implant mucosal seal and crestal bone
- **IT/PP/PA time**: impression-taking / prosthesis-placement / prosthesis-adjustment chairside intervals
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Loss — crestal bone remodeling measured from the implant shoulder on periapical radiographs
