## Why Ingested
프라임스캔(Primescan)을 근관치료 접근와(access opening) 스캔에 처음 적용한 비교 연구. 치과용 현미경 및 CBCT 대비 정확도를 평가하여 위키의 [[digital-workflow/wildhagen-2026-accuracy-ios-post-preparations]] 및 [[digital-workflow/primescan-clinical-performance-overview]]에서 아직 다루지 못한 근관치료 적응증 근거를 추가한다.

## One-line Summary
Comparative in vitro study (24 extracted molars): Primescan accurately identified orifice number/location vs microscope and CBCT; diagnostic accuracy for access defects 87.5%, for calcified orifices 79.2% (moderate) vs microscope.

## 한줄요약
발거 대구치 24개 비교 연구: 프라임스캔은 근관구 위치·개수 판독 정확, 접근와 결함 87.5%, 석회화 근관구 79.2%(중등도) 진단 정확도.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First study evaluating Primescan for endodontic access opening imaging compared to both clinical microscope and CBCT
- Primescan accurately identifies orifice number and location (non-significant difference vs microscope and CBCT)
- High diagnostic accuracy for chamber floor defects (bur abrasion, isthmus): 87.5% vs microscope
- Moderate accuracy for calcified orifices: 79.2% vs microscope; 70.8% vs CBCT — identifies limitation for calcified cases
- Establishes feasibility of IOS for intraoperative endodontic documentation — novel application beyond restorative

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Comparative in vitro study
- Specimens: 24 extracted molars, standard endodontic access preparations performed
- Imaging modalities: (1) CEREC Primescan IOS 3D scan; (2) clinical microscope (reference); (3) CBCT
- Outcomes: Orifice number and location; chamber floor features (bur abrasion, isthmus, anatomical variations); calcified orifice detection
- Statistical analysis: Agreement statistics vs microscope and CBCT; diagnostic accuracy with 95% CI

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Orifice number and location:**
- Agreement vs microscope: non-significant difference → accurate
- Agreement vs CBCT: non-significant difference → accurate

**Access defects and anatomical features (bur abrasion, isthmus):**
- Diagnostic accuracy: **87.5%** (95% CI: 67.6–97.3) vs microscope

**Calcified orifices:**
- Diagnostic accuracy vs microscope: **79.2%** (95% CI: 57.8–92.9) — moderate
- Diagnostic accuracy vs CBCT: **70.8%** (95% CI: 48.9–87.4) — moderate

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- In vitro only — extracted teeth under ideal conditions; in vivo blood, saliva, lighting may differ
- Sample size n=24; confidence intervals wide for calcification outcomes
- No comparison to conventional photographic documentation
- Clinical threshold for acceptable diagnostic accuracy in endodontics not yet established for IOS

## 6. Related Work
- Wildhagen 2026: Primescan for post space scanning — both demonstrate feasibility of IOS for narrow intraradicular access
- Almalki 2024: IOS accuracy for post channels at different depths — complementary evidence for endodontic IOS applications

## 7. Glossary
- **Access opening**: coronal preparation to reach pulp chamber and root canal orifices
- **Orifice**: root canal entrance point at pulp chamber floor
- **Isthmus**: narrow connection between two root canals
- **Calcified orifice**: root canal entrance obliterated/narrowed by secondary dentin — most challenging to detect
