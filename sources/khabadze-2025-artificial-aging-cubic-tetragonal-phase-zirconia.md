## Why Ingested

Corroborates and extends [[dental-materials/zirconia/kengtanyakich-2020-hydrothermal-degradation-cubic-translucent-zirconia]]: uses more severe conditions (134°C/12h, 2 atm) on 3Y-TZP, 4Y/5Y-PSZ and hybrid multilayer variants, and also compares polished vs glazed surface treatments — directly relevant to the glazing debate.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro XRD study on 3Y-TZP, 4Y/5Y-PSZ, and multilayer hybrid zirconia discs (1mm × 10mm, polished and glazed, autoclave 134°C/2atm/12h): all materials maintained their dominant phase composition after aging with no notable t→m transformation.

Minor peak broadening in polished specimens suggested surface-level microstructural changes (decreased crystallite size, internal stress); glazed specimens showed no such change.

Polishing was more effective than glazing in improving resistance to microstructural degradation; cubic/tetragonal phase stability confirmed under simulated clinical aging.

## 세줄요약

3Y-TZP, 4Y/5Y-PSZ, 하이브리드 다층 지르코니아 디스크(연마 및 글레이징), 134°C/2atm/12h 오토클레이브: 전 재료에서 단사정 상전환 미발생, 주요 상 구성 유지.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Independently confirms that 4Y/5Y-PSZ zirconia shows excellent phase stability under 134°C/12h aging (more severe than Kengtanyakich's 122°C/8h)
- Extends findings to multilayer hybrid variants, clinically relevant for esthetic cases
- Demonstrates polished surfaces show minor FWHM broadening (surface stress) while glazed surfaces do not — opposite of common expectation
- Statistical analysis shows non-significant correlation between XRD peak intensity and FWHM (r=-0.253, P=0.481)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Standardized discs: 1mm thickness, 10mm diameter
- Materials: 3mol% YO₃ (3Y-TZP), 4Y/5Y-PSZ (partially stabilized), and hybrid multilayer variants
- Aging: 134°C, 2 atm, 12h autoclave
- XRD analysis before and after aging: peak position, FWHM, intensity changes
- Statistical processing of phase composition stability (P>0.05 established)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- All materials: NO notable t→m phase transformation after aging
- Polished specimens: minor peak broadening (FWHM increase) → surface-level microstructural change (decreased crystallite size, internal stress accumulation)
- Glazed specimens: stable XRD profile, no FWHM change
- Phase composition stability confirmed at P>0.05 for all material groups
- Correlation between peak intensity and FWHM: r=-0.253, P=0.481 (non-significant)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No mechanical testing (flexural strength, fracture toughness) — only XRD phase analysis
- Single time-point aging; kinetics not evaluated
- Small disc geometry (1mm×10mm) may not reflect clinical restoration dimensions
- No optical property measurements

## 7. Glossary

- **FWHM (반값전폭, Full Width at Half Maximum)**: XRD 피크의 절반 높이에서의 너비; 결정립 크기와 반비례
- **4Y/5Y-PSZ (부분안정화 지르코니아)**: 4–5mol% YO₃로 더 많은 큐빅상 형성 → 높은 반투명도
- **Multilayer hybrid zirconia**: 단면별 YO₃ 농도 구배로 강도(내부)와 미학(외면) 동시 구현
