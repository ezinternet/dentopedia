## Why Ingested

플라즈마 배치 2차(2026-05-31)에서 **유전체 장벽 방전(DBD) 방식으로 임플란트를 무균 상태로 유지하며 처리하는** 장치 공학 근거. 탄소 60%↓·초친수성·세포반응 향상을 보여 [[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/jeon-2024-vortex-direct-plasma-titanium-surface]]·[[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/zheng-2020-non-thermal-atmospheric-plasma-titanium]]의 표면 재활성화 기전을 장치 측면에서 보강.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study of a novel cylindrical dielectric barrier discharge (DBD) plasma device that uses the implant itself as the electrode and the packaging wall as the dielectric, enabling sterile chairside plasma treatment without additional contamination risk.

Plasma treatment reduced surface carbon by ~60% (XPS), rendered the SLA implant surface super-hydrophilic with increased calcium elution, and significantly improved MC3T3-E1 preosteoblast adhesion, proliferation, and differentiation.

The design addresses chairside re-activation without breaking sterility, but results are in vitro only with no in vivo osseointegration or clinical data.

## 세줄요약

임플란트를 전극, 포장재를 유전체로 활용해 무균 상태를 유지하면서 표면처리하는 원통형 유전체 장벽 방전(DBD) 플라즈마 장치의 in vitro 연구이다.

플라즈마 처리 후 SLA 임플란트 표면 탄소 약 60%↓(XPS)·초친수성·칼슘 용출↑가 확인됐고, MC3T3-E1 전골모세포의 부착·증식·분화가 유의하게 향상됐다.

포장을 개봉하지 않고 chairside에서 재활성화할 수 있는 설계이나, in vitro 결과만 있고 in vivo 골유착이나 임상 데이터가 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- DBD design treats the implant while keeping it sterile inside its packaging (chairside-ready concept).
- Quantifies ~60% carbon reduction and super-hydrophilicity with optimized discharge conditions.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Titanium implant as electrode, packaging wall as dielectric barrier → DBD plasma.
- Numerical + experimental optimization; XPS surface chemistry; in vitro cell assays on SLA.
- **Device parameters (added 2026-08-10, re-checked PDF for a user question on vacuum-plasma treatment times):** ACTILINK (Plasmapp) — sinusoidal 100 kHz, 3 kV; package pressure maintained 5–10 Torr; commercial implant (Straumann SLA, 4.1×10 mm) repackaged for processing. **Total treatment time: 60 s.** Device houses 3 identical plasma modules sharing one vacuum pump/pressure gauge (210×370×270 mm footprint).
- **Contrast cited by the authors themselves:** Diener Electronic GmbH's chamber-type vacuum plasma device requires "a long treatment time of at least 10 min" using argon gas — the authors' own stated motivation for designing a faster gas-free DBD alternative. Consistent with [[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/becker-2019-lpp-app-plasma-proinflammatory-cytokines]]'s 15-min Diener Femto PCCE LPP arm (same product line).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- XPS: ~60% reduction in surface carbon impurities.
- Super-hydrophilic surface; increased calcium elution.
- Significantly improved cell adhesion, proliferation, differentiation.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro only; no in vivo/clinical osseointegration data.

## 7. Glossary

- DBD = dielectric barrier discharge (유전체 장벽 방전). XPS = X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy.
- Super-hydrophilic = 초친수성. SLA = sand-blasted, large-grit, acid-etched.
- ACTILINK(Plasmapp) 60초 vs Diener 챔버형 ≥10분 — 논문 저자 스스로 Diener를 "장시간 소요"로 지목하며 자사 DBD 개발 동기로 제시.
