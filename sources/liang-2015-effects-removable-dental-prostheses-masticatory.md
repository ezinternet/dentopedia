## Why Ingested
단축치열 (Shortened Dental Arch, SDA) 환자에서 가철성 국소의치 (Removable Partial Denture, RPD)가 저작 기능을 얼마나 보상하는지 정량적으로 종합한 SR. Witter 연구실(Radboud) 산출물 → 기존 [[removable-partial-denture/nassani-2013-fesrpd-shortened-dental-arch-reappraisal]]의 Witter 사다리 개념에 기능 데이터를 추가하고 [[removable-partial-denture/walter-2021-shortened-dental-arch-15year-rct]] 15년 RCT의 기능 결과 해석에 맥락 제공.

## One-line Summary
SR of 8 studies (J Dent 2015) showing (E)SDA patients have 28–39% reduced masticatory performance vs complete dentition; distal-extension RDPs partially compensate (~50% recovery), with a dose-effect (more artificial teeth → better performance).

## 한줄요약
SR (8편, J Dent 2015): 단축치열·극단 단축치열 (Extreme SDA, ESDA) 환자는 완전치열 대비 저작 수행력 (Masticatory Performance, MP) 28–39% 저하; 원위 유리단 RPD는 약 50% 보상, 인공치 수가 많을수록 효과 증가 (용량-반응 관계).

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR to quantify masticatory performance deficit in (E)SDA patients: 28–39% lower comminution or mixing ability vs complete dentition
- Demonstrates RDP partial compensation: comminution improves 2–32% (vs non-prosthesis condition); mixing ability with RDP 28–83% lower than chewing on dentulous side (showing residual deficit)
- Establishes dose-effect relationship: more artificial teeth (longer occlusal platform) → better comminution and mixing ability in 4 of 5 studies
- Quantifies the gap that extreme SDA (ESDA; 0–2 posterior pairs) creates vs standard SDA (3–5 pairs)
- Provides functional evidence to complement OHRQoL data in the SDA decision framework

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Systematic review (no meta-analysis — heterogeneous outcome measures precluded pooling)
- **Search databases:** PubMed, Medline, Cochrane Library, Embase, Science Direct
- **Search period:** 2003–2014
- **Search terms:** 'masticatory'/'chewing' combined with 'performance', 'efficiency', or 'ability'
- **Inclusion:** Studies with SDA (3–5 posterior occluding pairs) or ESDA (0–2 pairs) AND masticatory performance data with RDP
- **Studies included:** 8 studies total (4 providing comminution data, 3 providing mixing ability data, 1 providing both)
- **Outcomes:** Comminution (X50 particle size), mixing ability (colour mixing ratio)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **(E)SDA masticatory deficit:** 28–39% reduction in comminution or mixing ability vs complete dentition
- **RDP comminution compensation:** 2–32% reduction in X50 (i.e., 2–32% improvement) vs no-RDP condition
- **RDP mixing ability:** 28–83% lower when chewing at RDP side vs dentulous side (partial, not full compensation)
- **Dose-effect:** Significant in 4/5 studies — longer occlusal platforms → better function
- **ESDA vs SDA:** Greater deficit in ESDA patients; RDP more beneficial in ESDA

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 8 studies; small sample sizes; no meta-analysis possible (heterogeneous methodology)
- Search restricted to 2003–2014; may miss earlier studies
- Studies predominantly from European (Dutch/German) populations
- Short-term studies only; functional adaptation and long-term compensation unclear
- RDP design variables (clasps, saddle length) not systematically explored
- Future: standardised measurement protocols to enable meta-analysis

## 7. Glossary
- **SDA:** Shortened Dental Arch — ≥3–5 posterior occluding pairs per side
- **ESDA:** Extreme Shortened Dental Arch — 0–2 posterior occluding pairs per side
- **MP:** Masticatory Performance — functional measure of food breakdown
- **X50:** Median particle size after comminution — smaller = better masticatory efficiency
- **Mixing ability:** Ratio of colour mixing in a colour-changeable chewing gum — higher = better
- **RDP:** Removable Dental Prosthesis (distal-extension RPD)
