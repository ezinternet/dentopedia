## Why Ingested

Long-term (mean 10.6 years, up to 15.4 years) prospective data on zirconia ceramic 인레이 유지형 고정성 치과 보철물 (Inlay-Retained Fixed Dental Prosthesis, IRFDP) with a modified retainer design — one of the few studies exceeding 10 years for this prosthesis type. Directly informs clinical decision-making for single posterior tooth replacement without full-coverage preparations.

→ [[inlay/chaar-2023-long-term-zirconia-ceramic-inlay-retained-fdps]]

## Three-line Summary

A prospective study of 30 three-unit zirconia ceramic IRFDPs over a mean 10.6 years demonstrated a 10-year cumulative survival rate of 89% and a success rate of 70.4%, with veneering ceramic chipping (20.1%) as the dominant complication. The modified retainer design incorporated a short bevel preparation within enamel at buccal and oral sides, bonded with phosphate monomer resin after air-abrasion. Results support zirconia IRFDPs as a viable minimally invasive alternative for posterior single-tooth replacement.

## 세줄요약

지르코니아 세라믹 인레이 유지형 FDP 30개를 평균 10.6년(최대 15.4년) 추적한 전향적 연구에서 10년 누적 생존율 89%, 성공률 70.4%를 보고하였다. 수정된 리테이너 윙 베벨 디자인과 인산염 모노머 레진 접착을 사용하였으며, 주요 합병증은 베니어 세라믹 치핑(20.1%)이었다. 결과는 지르코니아 IRFDP가 후방부 단일 결손 치료의 최소 침습적 대안으로 적합함을 지지한다.

## Key Contributions

- 10-year Kaplan-Meier survival rate of 89% for zirconia IRFDPs with modified design
- 10-year cumulative success rate of 70.4% (18/30 complication-free)
- Quantified veneering ceramic chipping as the primary complication (20.1%)
- Characterized all 4 failure modes: secondary caries/retention loss (n=2), debonding/enamel fracture (n=1), periodontitis (n=1)
- Demonstrated feasibility of extended observation beyond 15 years for selected cases

## Methodology

**Study design:** Prospective clinical study with annual recall after initial 6–12 month review.

**Population:** 30 patients receiving 30 × 3-unit fixed-fixed IRFDPs to replace:
- 7 premolars (4 maxillary, 3 mandibular)
- 23 first molars (15 maxillary, 8 mandibular)

**Prosthetic design:**
- Retainer preparation following general ceramic inlay principles
- Modified retainer-wing bevel preparation within enamel at buccal and oral sides
- Framework material: 3Y-TZP (3몰% 이트리아 안정화 정방정계 지르코니아, 3Y-TZP) ceramic — milled
- Pontic veneering: 장석 세라믹 (Feldspathic Ceramic) veneered

**Luting protocol:**
- Intaglio surface treatment: air-abrasion
- Cement: phosphate monomer-containing luting resin

**Statistical analysis:** Kaplan-Meier analysis for survival and complication rates.

## Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean observation time | 10.6 ± 1.5 years |
| 10-year cumulative survival rate | 89% |
| Total failures | 4 |
| 10-year cumulative success rate | 70.4% |
| Complication-free cases | 18/30 (up to 15.4 years) |
| Most common complication | Veneering ceramic chipping (20.1%) |

**Failure causes:**
1. Deep secondary caries with loss of retention — 2 cases
2. Repeated debonding with enamel fractures — 1 case
3. Generalized progressive periodontitis — 1 case

## Limitations

- Small sample size (n=30); single-center design limits generalizability
- Abstract only — specific details on patient demographics, preparation dimensions, and ceramic chipping management not available
- 20.1% chipping rate suggests the veneered zirconia design may not be optimal; monolithic zirconia frameworks were not evaluated
- No control group comparing to full-coverage crowns or other IRFDP materials

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| 인레이 유지형 고정성 치과 보철물 (Inlay-Retained FDP, IRFDP) | Fixed partial denture retained by inlay preparations rather than full-coverage crowns |
| 3몰% 이트리아 안정화 정방정계 지르코니아 (3Y-TZP) | 3 mol% yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal — high-strength dental ceramic |
| 장석 세라믹 (Feldspathic Ceramic) | Conventional layering ceramic used to veneer zirconia frameworks |
| 치핑 (Chipping) | Fracture or delamination of the veneering ceramic layer |
| 카플란-마이어 분석 (Kaplan-Meier Analysis) | Survival analysis method accounting for censored observations |
| 공기 연마 (Air-Abrasion) | Surface conditioning of ceramic intaglio surface with aluminum oxide particles prior to bonding |
| 인산염 모노머 레진 시멘트 (Phosphate Monomer Resin Cement) | Adhesive resin cement containing phosphate monomer for bonding to zirconia |
