## Why Ingested

Adds primary randomised evidence on the adjunctive-antibiotic question that [[implants/peri-implantitis/antonoglou-2025-do-systemic-antibiotics-offer-benefits]] pooled from only 3 RCTs: it tests a 1-week amoxicillin/metronidazole course on top of access-flap surgery and finds a BOP-only benefit, qualifying the short-term treatment-success signal in that meta-analysis and in the retrospective cohort [[implants/peri-implantitis/zhou-2026-surgical-protocols-peri-implantitis-cohort]]. It is the antibiotic-factor companion to the same Groningen group's [[implants/peri-implantitis/hakkers-2026-reconstructive-peri-implantitis-3wall-4wall-rct]].

## Three-line Summary

Single-blind, two-arm parallel RCT (1:1) at University Medical Center Groningen: 59 patients with peri-implantitis (no, one-wall or two-wall bone defects) who failed non-surgical therapy received access-flap surgery with (n=29) or without (n=30) amoxicillin/metronidazole 500/500 mg three times daily for 1 week; follow-up 12 months.

Mean peri-implant BOP was lower in the antibiotic group at T9 (-10.57%, 95% CI -20.17 to -0.97, p=0.03) and T12 (-14.47%, 95% CI -25.9 to -3.04, p=0.01); mean PPD (T12 -0.14 mm, p=0.54), SOP, MBL and composite disease resolution (39.6% vs 23.8%) showed no significant between-group differences at any timepoint.

An isolated BOP effect with no benefit on PPD, MBL or disease resolution does not support routine systemic antibiotics after surgical peri-implantitis therapy; limits are 1-year follow-up, no microbiological data, no examiner calibration and low power for the binary outcome.

## 세줄요약

단일맹검 평행군 무작위대조시험 (Randomised Controlled Trial, RCT, 1:1; 흐로닝언 대학병원): 비외과적 치료에 반응하지 않은 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 환자 59명 (골결손 없음·1벽·2벽)이 접근판막 수술 단독 (n=30) 또는 수술 + 아목시실린/메트로니다졸 (Amoxicillin/Metronidazole) 500/500 mg 1일 3회 1주 (n=29)로 배정되어 12개월 추적됐다.

항생제군의 평균 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)이 T9 (-10.57%, 95% CI -20.17 ~ -0.97, p=0.03)와 T12 (-14.47%, 95% CI -25.9 ~ -3.04, p=0.01)에서 유의하게 낮았으나, 탐침 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD; T12 -0.14 mm, p=0.54), 화농 (Suppuration on Probing, SOP), 변연골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL), 복합 질환 해소율 (39.6% vs 23.8%)은 어느 시점에도 유의차가 없었다.

BOP 단일 지표의 효과만 있고 PPD·MBL·질환 해소의 이득이 없어 수술 후 전신 항생제의 상시 사용은 지지되지 않으며, 한계는 1년 추적, 미생물 검사 부재, 검사자 보정 미시행, 이분형 결과의 낮은 검정력이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Randomised comparison of adjunctive amoxicillin/metronidazole vs no antibiotics, both on top of identical access-flap surgery (titanium curettes, air polishing, 35% phosphoric acid for 1 min, final air polishing) in no-, one- and two-wall defects.
- The only significant between-group differences in the mixed-effects models were lower BOP at T9 and T12; nothing else differed at any timepoint.
- Post hoc, exploratory subgroup analyses applying the EFP guideline thresholds (baseline PPD >= 7 mm; baseline suppuration) to the surgical setting: SOP difference at T6 only (baseline PPD >= 7 mm, p=0.02).
- Reports adverse events: the control group reported more pain, gland swelling and tooth/prosthesis discolouration.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Investigator-blinded, two-armed, parallel RCT (1:1); fixed block size of four; sealed computer-generated envelopes opened at the end of the procedure.
- **Participants**: 123 screened (January 2020 - August 2023); 59 randomised (29 test, 30 control); 49 vs 46 implants diagnosed with peri-implantitis (test vs control). All had first received non-surgical submarginal instrumentation (oral hygiene instruction, periodontal cleaning, one mechanical debridement with air polishing, erythritol powder with 0.3% chlorhexidine); no patient met disease-resolution criteria afterwards. Defects classified by Monje et al. 2019 (no, one-wall, two-wall).
- **Intervention**: Test group amoxicillin/metronidazole 500/500 mg three times daily for 1 week from immediately after surgery; all patients antiseptic mouthwash twice daily for 2 weeks. Adherence checked via empty packaging.
- **Outcomes**: Primary mean PPD and mean BOP (PPD was added as primary after study start to align with the EFP guideline; reported transparently); secondary composite disease resolution (PPD <= 5 mm, no BOP at one or more sites, no SOP), SOP, MBL, plaque, recession, keratinised mucosa, full-mouth periodontal parameters; assessed at T3, T6, T9, T12.
- **Analysis**: Linear and logistic mixed-effects models (participants, implants nested), last observation carried forward for missing data (ITT). Sample size: at least 51 patients for 80% power at a medium effect size (0.5); target 61.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Test vs control (marginal effect, 95% CI, p) |
|---|---|
| BOP T3 / T6 | -2.78% (p=0.56) / -6.67% (p=0.14) |
| BOP T9 | -10.57% (-20.17 to -0.97), p=0.03 |
| BOP T12 | -14.47% (-25.9 to -3.04), p=0.01 |
| Mean PPD T3 / T6 / T9 / T12 | 0.24 (p=0.23) / 0.11 (p=0.56) / -0.02 (p=0.94) / -0.14 mm (p=0.54) |
| MBL T3 / T6 / T9 / T12 | 0.18 (p=0.63) / 0.12 (p=0.76) / 0.05 (p=0.89) / -0.02 mm (p=0.97) |
| SOP (mean) | -2.71% (-7.46 to 2.04), p=0.26 |
| Treatment success (model) | 0.82 (-4.07 to 5.71), p=0.74 |

- Descriptive (Table 2): site-level mean BOP fell from 76.4% (test) and 74.4% (control) at baseline to 39.3% vs 53.1% at T12; mean PPD from 5.2 mm and 5.1 mm to 3.1 mm vs 3.5 mm.
- Composite disease resolution (Discussion): 39.6% of test implants vs 23.8% of control implants, not significant.
- Post hoc: SOP difference at T6 in implants with baseline PPD >= 7 mm (p=0.02); keratinised mucosa difference at T6 in baseline-suppuration implants (p=0.04); T12 mean bleeding sites per implant 2.3 (SD 1.9) vs 3.5 (SD 1.8), p<0.01; implants with 2 or more bleeding sites at T12 58.3% vs 80.0%, Fisher p=0.04.
- Adverse events: control group reported more pain (p=0.01), gland swelling (p<0.01) and discolouration (p=0.04).
- Attrition: test group one patient moved abroad (T3); control group one implant removed after T6 for disease progression and one patient discontinued for cardiovascular reasons after T6.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Dentate and edentulous patients were mixed; oral microbiota differ and may influence antibiotic effect.
- No microbiological analysis, so the mechanism and microbial shifts could not be assessed.
- Combination of two antibiotics conflicts with antibiotic-stewardship preference for monotherapy.
- Adherence checked by returned packaging, which may overestimate compliance; no patient-reported outcome measures.
- Wide confidence interval for disease resolution reflects limited power for binary outcomes; formal examiner calibration not performed.
- One-year follow-up; authors cite 3 years as recommended for long-term effects. Post hoc subgroup findings are exploratory.

## 6. Related Work

- Antonoglou 2025 (SR+MA): short-term success and BOP favoured antibiotics, no benefit at 3 years or more; the present RCT confirms only the BOP signal.
- Riben Grundstrom 2024 (RCT, as cited in the paper): amoxicillin/metronidazole improved 12-month radiographic MBL stability; not significant for BOP (55% vs 31% of implants without BOP); different decontamination and regimens.
- Carcuac 2017 (as cited): benefits of adjunctive amoxicillin not sustained over 3 years.
- Hallstrom 2017 (as cited): adjunctive azithromycin gave no benefit at 1 year.
- Herrera et al. 2023 (EFP S3 guideline): antibiotics may be considered in non-surgical therapy with PPD >= 7 mm or suppuration; not extended to surgery.

## 7. Glossary

- **BOP**: bleeding on probing; mean percentage of bleeding sites.
- **SOP**: suppuration on probing.
- **MBL**: radiographic marginal bone level.
- **NSI**: non-surgical submarginal instrumentation.
- **LOCF**: last observation carried forward, used for missing follow-up data.
- **Composite disease resolution**: residual PPD <= 5 mm, no BOP at one or more sites, and no SOP.
