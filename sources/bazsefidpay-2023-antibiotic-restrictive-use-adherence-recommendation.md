## Three-line Summary

Observational adherence audit (BMC Oral Health 2023, Sweden) evaluating compliance with restrictive dental antibiotic prescribing guidelines and confirming the safety profile of a restrictive prescribing strategy.

Restrictive antibiotic prescribing was found to be safe, with adherence variable across practitioners; targeted interventions improved adherence rates in a single-country (Swedish) setting.

A restrictive prescribing approach does not compromise patient safety — clinicians can follow stewardship guidelines without increased adverse outcomes, though adherence requires active reinforcement.

## 세줄요약

스웨덴(Karolinska) — 치과 항생제 제한적(restrictive) 처방 가이드라인 adherence와 안전성을 평가한 관찰 연구(BMC Oral Health 2023).

제한적 처방은 안전하며, 개원의 간 adherence 편차 존재 — 맞춤형 개입으로 개선 가능.

제한적 처방이 환자 안전을 저해하지 않음 — 항생제 스튜어드십 준수 권장, 단 단일 국가(스웨덴) 관찰 연구 한계.
