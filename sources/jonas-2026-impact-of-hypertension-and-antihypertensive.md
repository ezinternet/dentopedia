## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort at a tertiary referral center (2013-2021) of 425 implants in 152 patients undergoing sinus augmentation, testing whether hypertension (HTN) and antihypertensive drug classes are risk factors for early implant failure (EIF).

HTN itself was not significantly associated with EIF, but beta-blocker therapy was an independent risk factor (OR 4.05, p=0.018 patient level; OR 3.13, p=0.045 implant level), as were tobacco smoking (OR 8.52, p<0.001) and a higher number of implants per patient (OR 1.49, p=0.039); diuretics, ACE inhibitors, ARBs and CCBs were not significant.

Evidence is abstract-only and retrospective; the EIF time window, EIF rate, CIs and sinus technique are not stated in the abstract, so the beta-blocker signal needs confirmation against the full text and prospective data.

## 세줄요약

상악동 거상술 (sinus augmentation) 후 조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF)에 고혈압 (Hypertension, HTN)과 항고혈압제가 미치는 영향을 3차 의료기관에서 2013-2021년 후향적으로 분석 (환자 152명, 임플란트 425개).

HTN 자체는 EIF와 유의한 관련이 없었으나 베타차단제 (beta-blocker)는 독립 위험요인 (환자 수준 OR 4.05, p=0.018; 임플란트 수준 OR 3.13, p=0.045)이었고, 흡연 (OR 8.52, p<0.001)과 환자당 임플란트 수 증가 (OR 1.49, p=0.039)도 위험을 높였으며, 이뇨제·ACE 억제제 (ACEI)·ARB·칼슘채널차단제 (CCB)는 유의하지 않았다.

초록 기반의 후향적 근거이며 EIF 기간 정의, 실패율, 신뢰구간, 거상술 접근법은 초록에 없어 전문 확인과 전향적 자료가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Sinus-augmentation-specific test of hypertension and antihypertensive classes as risk factors for EIF.
- Patient-level and implant-level multivariable estimates: beta-blocker OR 4.05 (p=0.018) and OR 3.13 (p=0.045).
- Smoking OR 8.52 (p<0.001) and implants per patient OR 1.49 (p=0.039) were also independent risk factors.
- Diuretics, ACEIs, ARBs and CCBs were not significantly associated with EIF.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort study.
- **Setting**: Tertiary referral center, 2013-2021 (country not stated in the abstract).
- **Sample**: 425 implants in 152 patients.
- **Outcome**: Early implant failure (EIF); the time-window definition is not in the abstract.
- **Analysis**: Univariate and multivariable analyses at patient and implant levels; p < 0.05 considered significant.
- Not stated: sinus technique, graft material, simultaneous vs delayed placement, covariate list, clustering method.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only: EIF definition, event number, CIs and model structure unverifiable.
- Retrospective, single tertiary center; confounding by indication possible (not addressed in the abstract).
- Precision of the beta-blocker estimate is uncertain without event counts (inference).
- Authors' conclusion limits the finding to association; causality is not established.

## 7. Glossary

- **EIF (Early Implant Failure, 조기 임플란트 실패)**: failure of the implant before or soon after prosthetic loading; exact window not given in this abstract.
- **OR (Odds Ratio, 오즈비)**: relative odds of the outcome.
- **ACEI / ARB / CCB**: ACE inhibitor / angiotensin II receptor blocker / calcium channel blocker.
- **Beta-blocker (베타차단제)**: antihypertensive class blocking beta-adrenergic receptors.
