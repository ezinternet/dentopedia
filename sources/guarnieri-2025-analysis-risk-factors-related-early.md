## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/survival/fan-2024-smoking-early-implant-failure-sr-ma]]의 흡연-조기실패 OR 2.59와 [[implants/survival/giok-2026-factors-implant-failure-umbrella-review]]의 매몰형 vs 비매몰형 치유 위험을 실제 임상 코호트에서 한 모델로 동시에 본 사례. 흡연 효과가 치유 프로토콜에 따라 다른지 위키에 근거가 없어 확인 목적으로 인제스트했으며, 결과적으로 교호작용 검정은 없음을 확인.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-private-practice cohort (Treviso, Italy; 392 patients, 930 implants, 2000-2020): GEE analysis of patient-, surgical- and implant-related factors for early implant failure (before or at abutment connection).

Implant-level early failure rate was 5.8%; seven factors were significant (male gender OR 1.54, smoking, radiotherapy/chemotherapy history, posterior maxilla OR 2.26 [1.32-3.86], non-submerged healing, implant design, implant brand), but the retrieved text gives ORs for only two of them; smoking and non-submerged ORs are in tables not retrieved.

No smoking x healing-protocol interaction or smoker stratification was tested, so the paper cannot say whether smoking risk differs by submerged vs non-submerged healing; non-submerged is also confounded with tissue-level implant design and brand.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일 개인의원 코호트 (이탈리아 트레비소; 환자 392명, 임플란트 930개, 2000-2020): 일반화추정방정식 (Generalized Estimating Equation, GEE)으로 조기 임플란트 실패 (지대주 연결 전 또는 연결 시점)의 환자·수술·임플란트 요인을 분석.

임플란트 단위 조기 실패율 5.8%; 유의 요인 7개 (남성 OR 1.54, 흡연, 방사선·항암치료 이력, 상악 구치부 OR 2.26 [1.32-3.86], 비매몰형 치유 (non-submerged), 임플란트 디자인, 브랜드)이나 확보된 본문에는 2개의 OR만 있고 흡연·비매몰형 OR은 미확보 표에 있음.

흡연 x 치유 프로토콜 교호작용이나 흡연자 층화 분석은 시행되지 않아, 매몰형 vs 비매몰형에 따라 흡연 위험이 달라지는지는 이 논문으로 답할 수 없음; 비매몰형은 조직수준 임플란트 디자인·브랜드와도 교락.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Unselected private-practice population (no exclusion for systemic disease unless it contraindicated surgery), 20-year window.
- Multi-factor GEE model with repeated-observation correction (patients with several implants).
- Reports non-submerged (tissue-level) vs submerged (bone-level) as a significant early-failure factor, and Laser-Lok collar implants as lower-risk.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Retrospective chart review 2000-2020; 392 patients, 930 implants; sample-size calculation via G*Power (effect size 0.15, power 0.9, >=900 implants).
- Early failure = failure before or at abutment connection. Diagnosed by any of: persistent subjective complaints (pain, foreign-body sensation, dysesthesia); recurrent peri-implant infection with suppuration; mobility; continuous peri-implant radiolucency. (The lead-in sentence of the list is truncated in the retrieved text.)
- Variables: age, gender, smoking, periodontitis history, systemic disease; surgical technique, augmentation, lateral/internal sinus lift, immediate placement, immediate mucosal graft, insertion torque; implant site, diameter, length, design, level, surface, brand.
- Binary GEE (SPSS 19), "adjusted for confounding factors"; ORs reported for significant factors; alpha 0.05. No interaction terms or stratified analyses are described.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Population: mean age 51.2 +/- 15.4; 53 smokers (13.5% of patients); 87 with systemic disease (22.2%); 325 implants one-stage (34.9%) vs 605 two-stage (65.1%); 717 posterior; 125 cylindrical vs 805 conical; 316 narrow (<3.75 mm); 278 short (<10 mm).
- Implant-based early failure rate 5.8% (the number of failed implants is not stated in the retrieved text; about 54 if applied to 930).
- Significant in GEE: male gender (OR 1.54 per discussion), smoking (OR value not in retrieved text; discussion says "high odds ratios in the multivariate analysis" and cites 2-3-fold in prior studies), radiotherapy/chemotherapy history (no OR in text), maxilla/posterior maxilla (OR 2.26, 95% CI 1.32-3.86, p=0.003), non-submerged healing (tissue-level vs bone-level, higher risk; no OR in text), cylindrical design, implant brand.
- Not significant: diabetes, other systemic diseases, periodontitis history, short and narrow implants, insertion torque <25 and 25-35 Ncm (>=35 Ncm reported significant, with caution about sample size).
- Among non-submerged implants, Laser-Lok tissue-level implants had lower early failure than other tissue-level implants; BioHorizons Laser-Lok bone-level also lower.
- Smoking x protocol: not tested and no failure counts of smokers by healing protocol are reported in the retrieved text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, multiple operators over 20 years, data not collected for research, records with missing data excluded, implant designs changed over time.
- Many variables in one model; "adjusted" is asserted but covariates are not listed in the text. Non-submerged status, tissue-level design and brand are likely collinear (the paper's own Laser-Lok comparison shows this).
- Small smoker group (53 patients) limits any subgroup analysis; single practice.
- Internal inconsistency: conclusion lists "systemic diseases", "surgical technique" and "insertion torque" as significant while the results list radiotherapy/chemotherapy only and no surgical technique.
- Briefing/screening claimed smoking and non-submerged were "independent factors"; the text says only GEE-"correlated", with the OR table not retrieved.

## 7. Glossary

- **GEE**: generalized estimating equation; handles clustering of several implants in one patient.
- **Non-submerged healing**: implant/healing abutment exposed to the oral cavity during healing (one-stage; here equated with tissue-level implants).
- **Submerged healing**: implant covered by mucosa during healing (two-stage; here equated with bone-level implants).
- **Early failure**: loss before or at abutment connection.
