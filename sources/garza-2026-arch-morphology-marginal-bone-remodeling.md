## Why Ingested

No held paper examined whether arch type (maxillary vs mandibular) and mandibular morphology (U- vs V-shaped) affect early marginal bone remodeling (MBR) under rigid monolithic zirconia full-arch frameworks. This provides the first prospective data quantifying MBR differences by arch and mandibular shape, relevant for risk stratification in treatment planning.

## Three-line Summary

Exploratory prospective cohort of 40 edentulous patients (49 monolithic zirconia full-arch prostheses: 25 maxillary, 24 mandibular; 308 implants); MBR measured at baseline and 12mo; mandibular morphology (CBCT), CAD geometry (inter-implant distance, cantilever length) assessed; linear mixed-effects model for arch comparison.

Implant/prosthesis survival 99.4%/97.9%; mandibular MBR 0.86mm vs maxillary 0.73mm (β=0.13mm, 95%CI 0.10–0.16, p<0.001); V-shaped mandible MBR 0.91mm vs U-shaped 0.81mm (diff 0.10mm, p<0.001); inter-implant distance negatively correlated with MBR in mandible (r=−0.70, p<0.001) but not maxilla; 2 minor chipping events (both V-shaped mandibles).

Mandibular arch and V-shaped morphology are associated with higher early MBR under rigid zirconia frameworks; statistically robust but numerically modest absolute differences; exploratory findings warrant confirmation.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First prospective data on arch-type MBR differences under rigid monolithic zirconia frameworks
- V-shaped mandibles show higher MBR — potential morphology-based risk stratification
- Inter-implant distance negatively correlated with mandibular MBR (r=−0.70): wider spacing → less bone stress
- Survival rates excellent (99.4%/97.9%) confirm clinical reliability
- Both MBR differences are statistically significant but numerically modest (~0.1mm) — clinical significance unclear

## 3. Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |

## 5. Limitations

- Exploratory — findings require confirmation in larger independent cohorts
- Short follow-up (12 months) — early remodeling only
- Abstract only; patient-reported outcomes details, absolute MBL values, CAD geometry distributions unavailable
- Small n (49 prostheses, 40 patients) with 9 contributing both arches
