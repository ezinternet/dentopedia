## Why Ingested

Basic-science evidence for why oral wound healing outperforms skin wound healing at the cellular level — oral mucosa keratinocytes migrate faster and proliferate faster than skin keratinocytes in vitro. Complements [[wiki/periodontics/huynh-2016-saline-rinsing-gingival-fibroblast-wound-healing]] (in-vitro oral keratinocyte/fibroblast wound-healing biology) with a direct skin-vs-oral-mucosa comparison, and supports the rationale for using autologous oral mucosal keratinocyte grafts as an alternative cell source for cutaneous wound repair.

## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (Folia Biologica, Kraków 2005) comparing primary human keratinocytes isolated from skin (epidermis) and buccal (oral) mucosa biopsies from 12 healthy donors, assessing morphology, motility, and proliferation under identical culture conditions.
Oral mucosa keratinocytes migrated ~12% faster (1.73 vs 1.55 μm/min), moved in straighter trajectories (coefficient of dislocation efficiency 0.55 vs 0.36), and grew to 20% higher density by 24h than skin keratinocytes; cell size/shape parameters did not differ significantly between the two types.
Fibroblast-conditioned medium slowed oral mucosal keratinocyte migration and proliferation back toward unsupplemented-medium levels, suggesting paracrine fibroblast signaling modulates keratinocyte behavior; clinical implication is that oral mucosa keratinocytes may be a superior in-vitro-expandable cell source for skin wound/burn grafting when time or donor-skin biopsy is limited.

## 세줄요약
Folia Biologica(Kraków, 2005)의 체외(in vitro) 연구로, 건강한 공여자 12명에서 채취한 피부(표피)와 구강(협측) 점막 생검에서 분리한 1차 배양 인간 각질세포(케라티노사이트, Keratinocyte)를 동일 배양 조건에서 형태·이동성·증식능 측면에서 비교했다.
구강점막 각질세포는 피부 각질세포보다 이동 속도가 약 12% 빨랐고(1.73 vs 1.55 μm/min), 이동 경로가 더 직선적이었으며(전위효율계수, Coefficient of Dislocation Efficiency, CDE 0.55 vs 0.36), 24시간 배양 시 세포밀도가 20% 더 높게 증식했다. 세포 크기·형태 지표는 두 세포 유형 간 유의차가 없었다.
섬유아세포(Fibroblast) 조건배지가 구강점막 각질세포의 이동성·증식을 무보충 배지 수준까지 억제했는데, 이는 방사성(Paracrine) 섬유아세포 신호가 각질세포 거동을 조절함을 시사하며, 공여 피부 생검이 어렵거나 신속한 세포 확장이 필요한 화상·피부창상 이식에서 구강점막 각질세포가 우수한 대체 세포원이 될 수 있음을 뒷받침한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First direct head-to-head in vitro comparison of proliferation, morphology, and motile behavior between human skin (epidermal) and oral (buccal) mucosa keratinocytes from the same experimental protocol.
- Quantified trajectory-level motility parameters (total trajectory length, net displacement, average speed, coefficient of dislocation efficiency, CDE) via time-lapse computer-aided tracking, not just endpoint migration assays.
- Demonstrated that fibroblast-conditioned medium from oral mucosa fibroblasts suppresses oral mucosal keratinocyte motility and proliferation, implicating paracrine fibroblast-keratinocyte crosstalk.
- Provided a cellular-biology rationale for using cultured autologous oral mucosal epithelium as a skin-substitute cell source (relevant to burn/skin-defect management).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: in-vitro experimental comparative study
- **Subjects/source**: primary keratinocytes from skin biopsy (6 donors, women 35-45 yr, facial cosmetic surgery) and buccal mucosa biopsy (6 donors, 4 men/2 women 35-45 yr, sinus maxillaris surgery); Ethical Committee approval per Polish law (KBET/480/B/2003, KBET/509/B/2003)
- **n**: 100 cells/culture analyzed for morphology; 100 cell tracks/condition for motility; proliferation assessed at 24/48/72h in triplicate cultures
- **Methods**: computer-aided cell image analysis (CYTOCYCLE 2.0) for shape parameters (surface area, extension, dispersion, elongation); time-lapse tracking (3-min intervals, 120 min) for trajectories; TRITC-phalloidin staining for F-actin cytoskeleton; haemocytometer cell counts for proliferation kinetics; oral mucosa fibroblast-conditioned medium (CM) tested on oral keratinocytes
- **Outcomes**: cell morphology parameters, motility parameters (trajectory length, displacement, speed, CDE), proliferation rate, actin cytoskeleton organization, effect of fibroblast-CM

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Parameter | Skin keratinocytes | Oral mucosa keratinocytes (control) | Oral mucosa keratinocytes (+ fibroblast CM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total trajectory length (μm) | 185.56 ± 4.87 | 207.24 ± 4.81* | 148.36 ± 3.12** |
| Average speed (μm/min) | 1.55 ± 0.04 | 1.73 ± 0.04* | 1.24 ± 0.03** |
| Total final displacement (μm) | 67.99 ± 4.48 | 113.58 ± 6.23* | 36.49 ± 2.07** |
| CDE (dislocation efficiency) | 0.36 ± 0.02 | 0.55 ± 0.02* | 0.26 ± 0.01** |

*p<0.05 vs skin keratinocytes control; **p<0.05 vs oral mucosa keratinocytes control medium.
- Cell shape parameters (surface area ~1618-1639 μm², extension, dispersion, elongation) were not significantly different between the two cell types (NS).
- Oral mucosa keratinocytes achieved 20% higher cell density than skin keratinocytes by 24h, sustained through 72h.
- F-actin distribution differed: oral mucosal keratinocytes showed accumulation at surface projections/cell boundaries (fast-locomoting pattern); skin keratinocytes showed actin bundles more centrally in the lamella.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small donor sample (6 skin + 6 oral mucosa donors), single age band (35-45 yr), sex-imbalanced across the two biopsy groups (skin: all women; oral mucosa: mostly men).
- In vitro 2D monoculture model — does not capture in vivo epithelial-mesenchymal or extracellular-matrix interactions.
- No DOI/modern indexing; older methodology (manual centroid tracking, Bürker haemocytometer) versus current standards (automated live-cell imaging, scratch/transwell assays).
- Mechanism of fibroblast-CM suppression of keratinocyte motility/proliferation not molecularly characterized (no cytokine/growth-factor quantification).

## 6. Related Work
- huynh-2016-saline-rinsing-gingival-fibroblast-wound-healing: complementary in-vitro oral keratinocyte/fibroblast wound-healing study; that paper additionally found oral keratinocytes (hNOKs) show no migration response to saline/chloride manipulation, unlike gingival fibroblasts — consistent with keratinocytes and fibroblasts having distinct motility-regulation pathways in the oral environment.

## 7. Glossary
- **Keratinocyte**: the predominant epithelial cell type of skin epidermis and oral mucosa, responsible for barrier function and re-epithelialization during wound healing.
- **Coefficient of Dislocation Efficiency (CDE)**: ratio of net cell displacement to total trajectory length; 1 = perfectly straight-line movement, 0 = random non-directional movement.
- **Conditioned medium (CM)**: culture medium pre-incubated with confluent fibroblasts, then transferred to another cell culture to test paracrine (soluble factor) effects.
- **F-actin cytoskeleton**: filamentous actin network that drives cell shape and locomotion; visualized here with TRITC-phalloidin staining.
- **Buccal mucosa**: the oral mucosal lining of the cheek, used here as the oral mucosa keratinocyte source (via sinus maxillaris surgery access).
