## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42023430885, J Indian Prosthodont Soc 2024) of 4 systematic reviews (2013–2022; 86 records screened via Cochrane Central/EBSCOhost/PubMed/Google Scholar, Jan 2013–Jan 2023).

Polished monolithic zirconia wear ≤ natural enamel over time (León Velastegui: linear wear zirconia 0.2574 μm vs feldspathic 8.9149 μm vs lithium disilicate 0.0189 μm); polished < glazed zirconia; AMSTAR-2 rated 2 SRs high quality and 2 critically low; no meta-analysis possible (heterogeneous designs/methods/outcomes).

Clinical guidance: polish (do not glaze) monolithic zirconia occlusal surfaces to minimize antagonist enamel wear; evidence is a qualitative synthesis only, English-language search, no gray literature.

## 세줄요약

우산형 고찰(PROSPERO CRD42023430885, J Indian Prosthodont Soc 2024): 체계적 문헌고찰 4편(2013–2022; Cochrane Central/EBSCOhost/PubMed/Google Scholar, 2013.1–2023.1 검색, 86편 스크리닝) 종합.

연마 단일체 지르코니아 마모 ≤ 자연 에나멜(León Velastegui: 선형 마모량 지르코니아 0.2574 μm vs 장석도재 8.9149 μm vs 리튬디실리케이트 0.0189 μm); 연마 < 유약; AMSTAR-2로 2편 고품질·2편 critically low 평가; 이질성으로 메타분석 불가.

임상 지침: 대합치 에나멜 마모 최소화를 위해 단일체 지르코니아 교합면은 유약 대신 연마 마감; 근거는 정성적 종합에 그치며 영어 논문만 검색·회색문헌 미포함.

## 1. Key Results
- PROSPERO CRD42023430885; PRISMA 2020 flow: 86 records → 5 duplicates removed → 81 → title screening excluded 73 → 8 remained → abstract screening excluded 4 (1 unretrievable + 3 excluded) → **4 included**
- Databases: Cochrane Central, EBSCOhost, PubMed (incl. MEDLINE), Google Scholar; Jan 1 2013–Jan 1 2023; English-language only
- Inter-reviewer Cohen's kappa: title 0.63, abstract 0.68, full-text 0.75 (moderate agreement each stage)
- AMSTAR-2 overall confidence: **high** — Solá-Ruíz 2020, León Velastegui 2022; **critically low** — Hmaidouch & Weigl 2013, Gou et al. 2018
- 4 included SRs (all posterior full-coverage crowns, 3D-scan/image-superimposition wear quantification in μm or mm):
  - Hmaidouch & Weigl 2013 (SR, 5 studies, 6mo–3yr FU): unglazed/unpolished ceramic → higher enamel wear rate than glazed/polished; enamel loss tracks ceramic microstructure/roughness, not hardness; bite force uncorrelated with wear in yrs 1–2 but significant for contralateral antagonist wear by yr 3
  - Gou et al. 2018 (SR, 5 studies, 12–24mo FU): monolithic zirconia antagonist enamel wear similar to or greater than natural teeth but less than metal-ceramic; polished zirconia < glazed zirconia (thin glaze layers degrade within first months, exposing rough substrate)
  - Solá-Ruíz et al. 2020 (SR+MA, 4 studies, 12–24mo FU): monolithic zirconia crowns cause greater antagonist wear over time; max enamel wear against zirconia crowns = 6.13 μm/month; zirconia crown itself wears max 3.40 μm/month
  - León Velastegui et al. 2022 (SR+MA, 12 studies, 3–24mo FU): feldspathic > lithium disilicate > zirconia in linear antagonist wear (8.9149 / 0.0189 / 0.2574 μm respectively); zirconia wear decreases over time to ≤ natural wear; molars wear more than premolars (greater occlusal force); "break-in wear" — cumulative wear rises but wear RATE falls over ~2yr as occlusal loading settles; reference natural physiological wear ≈29 μm/yr molars, ≈15 μm/yr incisors
  - Background (Lambrechts, cited in Introduction, not part of the 4 SRs): natural opposing enamel wears ~20–40 μm/year on average
- 3 excluded studies (Supplementary Table 1): Passos et al. 2013 ("wear behavior of zirconia opposing enamel: a systematic review"); Aljomard et al. 2020/2022 (in vitro SR+MA, "Enamel wear against monolithic zirconia restorations" — matches wiki's [[dental-materials/zirconia/aljomard-2022-enamel-wear-monolithic-zirconia-sr-ma]], excluded here for not meeting the clinical/6–24mo follow-up inclusion criteria); Ghaffari et al. 2022 ("Natural teeth wear opposite to glazed and polished ceramic crowns: a systematic review")
- Meta-analysis not performed: heterogeneous study designs, wear-measurement techniques, and outcome variables across the 4 included SRs
- Limitations: English-language filter only; no gray literature search; no formal publication-bias assessment (insufficient studies per parameter)
