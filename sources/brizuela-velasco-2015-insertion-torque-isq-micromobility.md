## Why Ingested

Foundational in-vitro study directly linking both insertion torque (IT) and ISQ/RFA values to directly measured implant micromobility under load — providing biomechanical grounding for the IT-ISQ correlation debate captured in [[implants/isq/lages-2018-isq-insertion-torque-correlation-sr]] and subsequently quantified by Tisci 2026 SR+MA. The exponential IT-micromotion threshold (~34 N·cm) offers a mechanistic basis for the clinical loading-decision thresholds discussed throughout the ISQ literature.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (n=19 Klockner Essential Cone implants in fresh cow ribs) measuring insertion torque (IT), Osstell ISQ, and Questar-microscope implant micromotion under 100 N axial-angled load simultaneously in the same specimen set.

Perpendicular ISQ vs micromotion: inverse linear relationship (r=0.91, R²≈0.83, p<0.0001); IT vs micromotion: inverse exponential (R²=0.78, p<0.0001) with a critical threshold near 30–34 N·cm below which micromotion escalates sharply; IT vs ISQ: direct significant correlation (R²=0.73 perpendicular, 0.62 parallel).

IT and ISQ measure overlapping but non-identical stability constructs — ISQ shows cleaner linear prediction of micromotion while IT shows a threshold-dependent exponential pattern; implant micromobility above 50–150 µm risks fibrous interface formation.

## 세줄요약

체외 연구(n=19, Klockner Essential Cone 임플란트, 신선 소갈비뼈): 같은 시편에서 삽입 토크(IT)·Osstell ISQ·100 N 부하 하 임플란트 미세운동을 동시 측정.

수직 ISQ vs 미세운동: 역 선형 상관(r=0.91, R²≈0.83, p<0.0001); IT vs 미세운동: 역 지수 곡선(R²=0.78), 30–34 N·cm 임계값 미만에서 미세운동 급증; IT vs ISQ: 직접 유의 상관(R²=0.73 수직, 0.62 수평).

IT와 ISQ는 유사하지만 비동일한 안정성 구조를 측정하며, ISQ는 미세운동의 선형 예측인자, IT는 역치 의존 지수 패턴을 보임.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Simultaneous measurement of IT, ISQ, and directly measured micromotion (Questar QM100 microscope, 2 µm resolution) in the same specimens — resolves the indirect-inference gap in prior IT-ISQ correlation studies
- Identifies ~30–34 N·cm as a clinically critical IT threshold: below this, micromotion escalates steeply; above this, each 1 N·cm gain yields only ~1 µm reduction in micromotion (diminishing returns)
- Demonstrates ISQ (RFA) is a stronger linear predictor of micromotion under load than IT alone (r=0.91 vs R²=0.78 exponential), supporting ISQ's primary role in loading decisions
- Direct IT-ISQ correlation confirmed (R²=0.73 perpendicular, 0.62 parallel), but non-identical biomechanical sensitivity validates their complementary use

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: In vitro controlled laboratory study
- **Specimens**: 12 fresh cow rib fragments (50×18×15 mm, periosteum removed, stored 5d in 50% ethanol/saline per Tricio method) simulating Lekholm & Zarb bone quality type 1–2
- **Implants**: 24 Klockner Essential Cone implants (10 mm length, 4 mm diameter, internal connection, 1.5 mm polished neck), 2 per rib, 20 mm apart; 5 excluded (IT outside 10–90 N·cm meter limits) → final n=19
- **IT measurement**: Calibrated BTG90CN analog dynamometer (Tohnichi, Tokyo), N·cm
- **ISQ measurement**: Osstell ISQ machine + Smartpeg (4–6 N·cm torque), parallel and perpendicular to bone long axis; transducer replaced every 10 measurements
- **Loading**: Acrylic premolar crown on temporary abutment (10 N·cm), servohydraulic MTS BIONIX 358, 100 N at 6° to occlusal plane, 10 N/s rate
- **Micromotion measurement**: Questar QM100 long-distance traveling microscope (resolution 2 µm), image analysis via IMAGE-J v1.47 — minimum 5 points per component, 2D displacement at 0 and 100 N
- **Statistics**: Pearson correlation coefficient; p<0.05 = statistically significant

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Variable | Mean (SD) | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Peak IT (N·cm) | 32.7 (20.9) | 10–73 |
| Perpendicular ISQ | 68.2 (6.9) | 56–79 |
| Parallel ISQ | 72.0 (6.5) | 59–83 |
| Micromotion (µm) | 68.2 (44.3) | 14.6–164 |

**Correlation results:**

| Correlation pair | r / R² | p-value | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perpendicular ISQ vs micromotion | r=0.91, R²≈0.83 | <0.0001 | Inverse linear |
| Parallel ISQ vs micromotion | r=0.86, R²=0.74 | <0.0001 | Inverse linear |
| IT vs micromotion | R²=0.78 | <0.0001 | Inverse exponential |
| IT vs perpendicular ISQ | R²=0.73 | <0.0001 | Direct linear |
| IT vs parallel ISQ | R²=0.62 | <0.0001 | Direct linear |

- IT < 30 N·cm: micromotion escalates sharply
- IT > 34 N·cm: each +1 N·cm → approximately −1 µm micromotion (near-plateau effect)
- Micromotion range 50–150 µm: tolerated; above 150 µm → fibrous interface risk

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro model (cow rib) lacks periosteum, vascularization, and bone remodeling; does not replicate clinical bone quality variation
- Small n (19 implants from 10 ribs) — limited statistical power; 5 implants excluded for extreme torque values
- Single implant system (Klockner Essential Cone) — results may not generalize to other designs, connections, or surface treatments
- Single loading direction (6° axial) and single load magnitude (100 N) — clinical loading is multidirectional and cyclic
- No follow-up/osseointegration phase — measures only mechanical primary stability, not biological stability

## 6. Related Work

- Lages 2018 (lages-2018-isq-insertion-torque-correlation-sr): SR (12 studies) found pooled IT-ISQ r=0.366, p=0.079 (non-significant); Brizuela 2015 contributes to the mixed individual-study evidence that SR pooled; both superseded by Tisci 2026 (r=0.44, p<0.001, 48 studies)
- Rosas-Díaz 2024 (rosasdiaz-2024-insertion-compression-primary-stability): In-vitro compression study finding ITV-ISQ dissociation (IT 25→63 N·cm, ISQ unchanged p=0.166) — contrasts with Brizuela's direct IT-ISQ correlation, possibly reflecting compression vs standard insertion protocol differences
- Pagliani 2013: In-vitro study (Brizuela cites) showing inverse ISQ vs lateral displacement correlation in fresh bovine rib — methodologically parallel
- Trici 2011: Animal study (Brizuela cites) on high vs low torque, histology context

## 7. Glossary

- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: Dimensionless 1–100 scale derived from resonance frequency analysis (RFA) — measures lateral stiffness of implant-bone interface via SmartPeg oscillation
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis)**: Method measuring oscillation frequency of implant transducer (Hz → ISQ); higher ISQ = stiffer interface = less lateral displacement
- **IT (Insertion Torque)**: Rotational resistance during implant insertion (N·cm); reflects axial bone condensation and friction at the implant-bone interface
- **Micromotion / Micromobility**: Sub-millimeter implant displacement under load (µm); ≤50–150 µm tolerated for osseointegration; above 150 µm risks fibrous encapsulation
- **Osstell**: Commercial RFA device (Osstell AB, Gothenburg, Sweden) using magnetic resonance via SmartPeg; outputs ISQ values 1–100
- **SmartPeg**: Disposable transducer peg screwed to implant (4–6 N·cm) for Osstell ISQ measurement
- **Lekholm & Zarb classification**: Bone quality scale (type 1–4); type 1–2 = dense cortical; cow rib selected to simulate type 1–2
