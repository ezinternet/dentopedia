## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=47 patients, 60 IR-FDPs, 60 months): zirconia vs FRC cantilever IRFDP with three retainer designs — overall survival 91.7%, success 81.7%; no significant inter-group difference

Zirconia failures concentrated at connector fractures and debonding; FRC showed repairable deformation — distinct failure mode profiles despite similar survival

Inlay ring design (most conservative) performs comparably to lingual and occlusal coverage designs at 5 years

## 2. Key Contributions
- Longest follow-up (5yr) for cantilever IRFDP comparing zirconia vs FRC with multiple retainer designs
- Inlay ring (IR) — most conservative design — showed comparable performance to lingual (LC) and occlusal (OC) coverage
- Material-specific failure modes: zirconia → catastrophic (connector fractures), FRC → repairable (deformation)
- Overall survival 91.7%, success 81.7% — lower than wing-retained RBFPD but acceptable for conservative approach
- Replaces 36-month data as primary reference for this RCT series

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Prospective RCT, 47 patients, 60 IR-FDPs replacing mandibular second premolars
- 3 retainer groups (n=20 each): IR (inlay ring), LC (lingual coverage), OC (occlusal coverage)
- Each group: zirconia (n=10) vs FRC (n=10)
- Computer-generated randomization, allocation concealment (sealed opaque envelopes)
- Adhesive bonding, standardized preparation protocols
- Clinical/radiographic evaluation at 60 months: modified FDI criteria
- Statistics: Monte Carlo tests, Cochran's Q test

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Overall survival: 91.7%
- Overall success: 81.7%
- No statistically significant differences between groups (P>0.05) in clinical performance

### Failures (n=5 requiring replacement):
- 2 zirconia connector fractures
- 2 debonding-related failures
- 1 irreversible FRC framework deformation

### Complications (n=6, managed without replacement):
- Debonding
- Plastic deformation
- Veneering composite delamination
- Minor veneering chipping
- 2 cases secondary caries

### Material-specific failure patterns:
- **Zirconia**: connector fractures or loss of retention (catastrophic)
- **FRC**: predominantly repairable complications, occasional progressive deformation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small n=10 per subgroup — insufficient for subgroup statistical power
- Failure behavior differences should be interpreted as exploratory
- Single jaw (mandibular), single tooth type (second premolar)
- Single operator preparation
