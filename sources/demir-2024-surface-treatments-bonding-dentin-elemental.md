## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (Turkish dental faculties, Scientific Reports 2024) examining how multiple dentin surface treatments and bonding types (etch-and-rinse vs self-etch) affect dentin elemental composition (EDX) and bond strength.

Both surface treatment and bonding type modulated dentin elemental composition and bond strength in a treatment-dependent manner; specific outcomes varied by the material and protocol combination applied.

Clinical generalization is limited by the in-vitro design and the specific materials tested; further clinical studies are needed to confirm these surface-treatment × bonding-mode interactions.

## 세줄요약

터키 치과대학 연구진의 in-vitro 연구(Scientific Reports 2024): 여러 상아질 표면처리 방법과 본딩 방식(산부식·수세 방식 vs 자가산부식 방식)이 상아질 원소 조성(EDX) 및 결합강도에 미치는 영향을 평가함.

표면처리 방법과 본딩 방식 모두 상아질 원소 조성과 결합강도를 처리법에 따라 다르게 조절하였으며, 재료 및 프로토콜 조합에 따라 결과가 달라짐.

in-vitro 설계 및 특정 재료에 국한된 연구이므로 임상 일반화에는 한계가 있으며, 표면처리-본딩 방식 간 상호작용을 확인하기 위한 추가 임상 연구가 필요함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Couples elemental/compositional dentin analysis with bond-strength outcomes.
- Clarifies surface-treatment × bonding-mode interaction.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
In-vitro: dentin specimens, multiple surface treatments and bonding types; surface elemental composition + bond strength measured.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Surface treatment and bonding type both modulate dentin elemental composition and bond strength (treatment-dependent).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
In-vitro; specific materials/treatments; clinical generalization limited.
