## Why Ingested

사용자 요청으로 지대주 높이 (Abutment Height)와 인접면 골소실 (Interproximal Bone Loss)을 다룬 1년 무작위 대조 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)을 추가한다. 후향적 근거인 [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]]의 ≥2 mm 지대주 보호 효과를 무작위 배정으로 재확인하고, 얇은 점막 (Thin Mucosa)에서 골하방 식립 (Subcrestal Placement)을 다룬 [[implants/mbl/linkevicius-2020-submerged-healing-abutment-subcrestal-soft-tissue]]에 지대주 높이 축을 더한다.

## Three-line Summary

One-year randomized clinical trial (33 patients, 66 platform-switched implants in thin-mucosa sites) comparing 3-mm abutments placed 2 mm subcrestally with 1-mm abutments placed equicrestally.

Interproximal peri-implant bone loss (IPBL) at 3/6/12 months was 0.06 ± 0.21 / 0.07 ± 0.22 / 0.12 ± 0.33 mm in the 3-mm group versus 0.76 ± 0.79 / 0.92 ± 0.88 / 0.95 ± 0.88 mm in the 1-mm group, with significant differences at every time point.

Tall abutments combined with subcrestal placement preserved interproximal bone in thin mucosa, but height and insertion depth were changed together so the effect cannot be attributed to height alone (abstract-only).

## 세줄요약

1년 무작위 대조 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) — 얇은 점막 (Thin Mucosa) 부위 33명·플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS) 임플란트 66개; 3 mm 지대주 + 2 mm 골하방 식립 (Subcrestal Placement)군 대 1 mm 지대주 + 골정상 식립 (Equicrestal Placement)군 비교.

3·6·12개월 인접면 임플란트주위 골소실 (Interproximal Peri-Implant Bone Loss, IPBL)은 3 mm군 0.06 ± 0.21 / 0.07 ± 0.22 / 0.12 ± 0.33 mm, 1 mm군 0.76 ± 0.79 / 0.92 ± 0.88 / 0.95 ± 0.88 mm — 모든 시점에서 유의한 군간 차이.

얇은 점막에서 긴 지대주와 골하방 식립의 조합이 인접면 골을 보존했으나, 지대주 높이와 식립 깊이를 동시에 바꿔 높이 단독 효과는 분리 불가 (초록만 확보).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Randomized trial isolating interproximal (rather than overall mesial/distal) peri-implant bone as the outcome in sites with thin peri-implant mucosa.
- Reports the full 3-, 6-, and 12-month IPBL trajectory for both abutment-height groups — an unusually large absolute separation (~0.83 mm at 12 months).
- Tests abutment height as part of a combined prosthetic–surgical protocol (3 mm + 2 mm subcrestal vs 1 mm + equicrestal), directly relevant to the "tall abutment" rule under thin-tissue conditions.
- Uses repeated-measures mixed ANOVA for longitudinal bone-level changes and additionally screens patient characteristics and clinical variables for associations with IPBL.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial, 1-year radiographic follow-up.
- **Population**: 33 patients, each receiving one prosthesis supported by two implants replacing at least two adjacent missing teeth → 66 implants; thin peri-implant mucosa (no numeric thickness cutoff stated in the abstract).
- **Groups**: 3-mm abutment height with implants placed 2 mm subcrestally; 1-mm abutment height with implants placed equicrestally. Both groups used platform-switched implants.
- **Measurements**: Clinical and radiological assessment at 3, 6 and 12 months after surgery.
- **Statistics**: Repeated-measures mixed ANOVA for between-group interproximal bone-level changes; association of IPBL with categorical variables also analyzed.
- **Not available in the abstract**: implant system, randomization/concealment/blinding details, radiographic measurement method, effect sizes, confidence intervals, exact p-values, and patient-level flow.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Mean IPBL (mm), by abutment-height group:**

| Time point | 1-mm abutment (equicrestal) | 3-mm abutment (2 mm subcrestal) |
|---|---|---|
| 3 months | 0.76 ± 0.79 | 0.06 ± 0.21 |
| 6 months | 0.92 ± 0.88 | 0.07 ± 0.22 |
| 12 months | 0.95 ± 0.88 | 0.12 ± 0.33 |

- Between-group differences were significant at **every** time point (exact p-values not given in the abstract).
- The 1-mm group's loss was largely established by 3 months (0.76 → 0.95 mm by 12 months); the 3-mm group remained near zero (0.06 → 0.12 mm).
- Patient characteristics and clinical variables showed **no statistically significant** association with IPBL.
- Authors' conclusion: long abutments combined with subcrestal implant positioning in thin mucosa lead to lower IPBL than short abutments.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only**: no full-text tables, CIs, exact p-values, radiographic technique, or risk-of-bias detail available; figures above are as reported in the PubMed abstract.
- **Design confounding**: abutment height and insertion depth were deliberately co-varied (3 mm + subcrestal vs 1 mm + equicrestal), so the lower IPBL cannot be assigned to abutment height independently of placement depth — the authors themselves phrase the conclusion as the *combination*.
- **Thin mucosa not quantified** in the abstract; applicability to specific mucosal-thickness thresholds cannot be read from this source.
- Short (1-year) horizon: durability beyond 12 months is unknown here; compare the 7-year follow-up of a separate 1-mm vs 3-mm cohort in [[implants/munoz-2025-1mm-3mm-abutments-7year-rct]].
- Small sample (33 patients / 66 implants), single-cohort, two-implant prostheses only.

## 7. Glossary

- **IPBL (Interproximal Peri-Implant Bone Loss)** — radiographic bone loss at the mesial/distal aspects adjacent to neighbouring teeth/implants.
- **Abutment height** — transmucosal height of the abutment above the implant platform; here 1 mm vs 3 mm.
- **Subcrestal placement** — implant platform positioned below the crestal bone (2 mm in the 3-mm group).
- **Equicrestal placement** — implant platform flush with the crestal bone (1-mm group).
- **Platform switching (PS)** — abutment narrower than the implant platform, moving the implant–abutment junction horizontally inward.
- **Thin mucosa** — peri-implant soft tissue of limited vertical thickness; no numeric cutoff supplied in this abstract.
