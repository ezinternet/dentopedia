Practical reversal-agent mapping for dental/OR patients on antithrombotics: warfarin→vitamin K/PCC (doses/route/timing), enoxaparin→protamine (dose/ratio/timing), LMWH washout/half-life; supports the antithrombotic seminar evidence check overview.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of anticoagulant/antiplatelet reversal: vitamin K + PCC for warfarin, andexanet alfa for apixaban/rivaroxaban, idarucizumab for dabigatran, protamine sulfate for UFH/LMWH, with antiplatelet reversal via DDAVP/platelets/TXA.

## 세줄요약

항혈전제 역전에 대한 내러티브 리뷰: 와파린 역전에 비타민 K + PCC, DOAC 역전에 andexanet alfa/idarucizumab, UFH/LMWH 역전에 프로타민, 항혈소판제 역전에 DDAVP/혈소판/TXA를 정리한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidated practical reversal-agent mapping with doses/routes/timing for warfarin, UFH, LMWH, DOACs, DTIs.
- Vitamin K IV vs PO: similar INR effect at 24 h, IV more rapid; combination with PCC for immediate correction.
- Protamine 1 mg/100 units heparin; LMWH 1 mg protamine/1 mg LMWH if <8 h, 0.5 mg/1 mg if >8 h.
- Andexanet alfa reduces anti-Xa activity 94% (apixaban) / 92% (rivaroxaban) within 2–5 min; rebound at 4 h.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review synthesizing published studies, guidelines, and RCTs (ANNEXA-A/R, ANNEXA-4, RE-VERSE AD, PATCH). No new data.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Warfarin: vitamin K IV/PO similar at 24 h; IV more rapid; all INR >4 at 4 h after either route; ~50% INR <2 at 24 h with IV monotherapy → combine with PCC for immediate correction.
- PCC: INR-driven or fixed dosing; 4F-PCC 25–50 U/kg for DOAC reversal; hemostasis 93% with PCC + vitamin K.
- Protamine: UFH 1 mg/100 units IV, max 50 mg, within 2–4 h; LMWH 1 mg protamine/1 mg LMWH if <8 h, 0.5 mg/1 mg if >8 h; reverses ~50% of LMWH effect.
- Antiplatelet: platelet aggregation recovery ~4–5 days (aspirin/clopidogrel/ticagrelor), ~7 days (prasugrel).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review, no systematic search; reversal recommendations still in development.
- Emerging agents (ciraparantag/PER-977) in early-phase trials.

## 7. Glossary

- **PCC (Prothrombin Complex Concentrate)**: concentrated clotting factors (II, VII, IX, X).
- **Protamine sulfate**: heparin/LMWH reversal agent; ~1 mg per 100 units heparin.
- **Andexanet alfa**: recombinant modified factor Xa for apixaban/rivaroxaban reversal.
- **Idarucizumab**: monoclonal antibody for dabigatran reversal.
