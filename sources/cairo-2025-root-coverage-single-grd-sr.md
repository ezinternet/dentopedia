## Why Ingested

Largest systematic review to date on root coverage for single gingival recession defects (50 RCTs, 1820 subjects), explicitly incorporating both clinician-reported (RES) and patient-reported (VAS) outcomes — complementary to [[periodontics/gualtieri-2025-mcaf-gingival-recession-sr-ma]] (multiple adjacent defects) and provides the comprehensive evidence base for selecting surgical approach based on aesthetic and functional goals.

## Three-line Summary

PRISMA 2020-compliant SR of 50 RCTs (1820 subjects, 2219 single GRDs); root coverage surgery positively influenced both clinician-reported esthetic scores (RES) and patient-reported esthetic perception and satisfaction (VAS), with bilaminar techniques (CAF+sCTG) showing superior outcomes over monolaminar (CAF alone).

Bilaminar (CAF+sCTG) > monolaminar (CAF alone) for both RES and VAS; lateral flap displacement > coronal flap for RES; meta-regression shows significant positive association between mean root coverage (MRC) and RES, but not MRC and patient-reported esthetics.

Dentinal hypersensitivity was substantially reduced by surgical root coverage regardless of modality — an underrecognized benefit alongside esthetic improvement.

## 세줄요약

50편 RCT(1820명, 2219개 단일 치은퇴축)의 PRISMA 2020 체계적 문헌고찰; 근면피개술은 임상가 보고 심미 점수(RES) 및 환자 보고 심미 인식·만족도(VAS) 모두 긍정적으로 개선; 이중층 기법(CAF+sCTG)이 단층 기법 대비 우위.

CAF+sCTG(이중층) > CAF 단독(단층): RES·VAS 모두; 측방 판막 > 관상 판막: RES 우위; 메타회귀 — MRC↑ → RES↑ 유의 연관, 그러나 MRC와 환자 심미 인식 간 연관은 비유의.

수술적 근면피개는 치법에 무관하게 상아질 과민증(dentinal hypersensitivity)을 상당히 감소시킴.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Dual primary outcome framework: first SR integrating both CRO (RES) and PRO (VAS esthetic satisfaction) for single GRD surgery.
- 50 RCTs, 1820 subjects — largest pooled dataset for single GRD root coverage.
- Documents that patient-reported esthetics and clinician-reported esthetics respond differently to treatment.
- Confirms reduction of dentinal hypersensitivity as a consistent benefit of root coverage surgery.

## 3. Key Results

**Bilaminar vs monolaminar techniques**:
- CAF+sCTG > CAF alone: significant for both RES and VAS
- CAF + soft tissue substitutes: beneficial for VAS only, not RES

**Flap displacement direction**:
- Lateral flap > coronal flap: superior mean RES values

**Meta-regression findings**:
- Mean root coverage ↑ → RES ↑ (statistically significant positive association)
- Mean root coverage ↑ → patient esthetic perception: NOT statistically significant

**Dentinal hypersensitivity**: Substantially reduced regardless of modality

## 4. Limitations

- SR only (no pooled quantitative meta-analysis reported in abstract)
- Heterogeneous outcome measurement methods across included RCTs
- Abstract-only source; full methodology detail not available via PMC

## 5. Glossary

- **GRD**: Gingival recession defect
- **CRO**: Clinician-reported outcome
- **PRO**: Patient-reported outcome
- **RES**: Root coverage esthetic score (standardized clinician assessment)
- **VAS**: Visual analog scale (patient satisfaction)
- **MRC**: Mean root coverage (%)
- **CAF**: Coronally advanced flap (monolaminar technique)
- **sCTG**: Subepithelial connective tissue graft (bilaminar technique)
