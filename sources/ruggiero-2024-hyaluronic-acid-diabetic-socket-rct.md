## Why Ingested

발치와 치유 21일 타임라인 근거 보강: 기존 [[oral-surgery/al-maawi-2021-prf-extraction-socket-healing-sr]]는 PRF의 전반적 효과를 다루지만, T2DM 환자에서 발치와 폐쇄가 실제로 몇 일에 완성되는지를 3·7·14·21일 시점으로 추적한 임상 RCT 데이터가 부재했다. Ruggiero 2024는 T2DM 환자 36명에서 HA vs. 무처치 발치와의 치유 속도를 분할구강(split-mouth) 설계로 추적해, 21일에 두 군 모두 완전 폐쇄에 도달한다는 정상 치유 경계값을 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (n=36 T2DM patients, 72 extractions) comparing hyaluronic acid gel vs. no treatment for post-extraction socket healing; assessed at days 3, 7, 14, and 21 post-surgery using healing index (modified Landry) and socket closure dimensions.

HA showed significantly better healing index at D7 (p=0.01) and D14 (p=0.02), and significantly better socket closure at D3 (p=0.04), D7 (p=0.001), and D14 (p=0.001); by D21, both groups reached complete healing (healing index 4, full closure) with no statistically significant difference (p=1).

Key finding: In T2DM patients with a history of diabetes complications, all sockets achieve full epithelial closure by 21 days regardless of HA treatment — HA accelerates the timeline but the endpoint is the same; no infections or antibiotic prescriptions needed in either group.

## 세줄요약

분할구강 RCT (T2DM 환자 36명, 발치와 72개) — 히알루론산 (Hyaluronic Acid, HA) 겔 vs. 무처치 발치와 치유를 수술 후 3·7·14·21일에 치유지수(Healing Index) 및 발치와 폐쇄 크기로 평가.

HA군은 D7 (p=0.01)과 D14 (p=0.02)에서 치유지수가 유의하게 우수하고, 발치와 폐쇄(socket closure)는 D3 (p=0.04)·D7 (p=0.001)·D14 (p=0.001)에서 유의하게 더 빨랐으나, D21에는 두 군 모두 완전 치유(p=1)에 도달.

핵심: 당뇨 합병증 병력이 있는 T2DM 환자에서도 모든 발치와가 **21일 내 상피 완전 폐쇄**에 도달하고 감염은 없었으며, HA는 타임라인을 앞당길 뿐 최종 결과에 차이 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides first 3–7–14–21 day granular timeline data for post-extraction socket closure in T2DM patients with active diabetes complications — establishes that 21 days is the outer boundary for complete epithelial healing even in complicated diabetics.
- Demonstrates HA gel (sodium hyaluronate + amino acids) accelerates early soft-tissue healing without antibiotics — 97.2% of HA sites vs. 72.2% of control sites achieved excellent healing index at D14.
- 10 out of 36 control patients required 21 days to reach healing index 4 vs. only 1 in HA group — quantifying the extent of delay in untreated diabetic sockets.
- No complications or superinfections in either group — challenges routine antibiotic prescription pre-extraction in well-managed T2DM.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Single-center split-mouth randomized controlled trial (CONSORT 2010)
- **Population**: n=36 T2DM patients with prior history of diabetes complications (nephropathy, neuropathy, retinopathy, cardiopathy, peripheral vascular disease); age 67.28 ± 11.22; requiring bilateral homologous extraction
- **Exclusion**: Smokers, uncontrolled DM, flap-elevation extractions, platelet dysfunction
- **Intervention**: HA gel 3× daily for 7 days (aminogam; HA + glycine, L-proline, L-leucine, L-lysine HCl)
- **Control**: No treatment
- **Outcomes**: Healing index (modified Landry score 1–4), socket closure (OV/MD diameter, socket depth) at D0, D3, D7, D14, D21; VAS pain; patient questionnaire
- **Statistics**: Mann-Whitney test, chi-square / Fisher test; p<0.05 significant

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Healing index D7**: HA significantly better (p=0.01); D14: HA significantly better (p=0.02); D21: equal (p=1)
- **Excellent healing (index=4) at D14**: HA 97.2% vs. control 72.2% (p=0.004)
- **Socket closure (MD diameter) D3**: HA significantly better (p=0.03); similar at D7, D14, D21
- **Socket closure index D3**: HA better (p=0.04); D7: HA better (p=0.001); D14: HA better (p=0.001); D21: equal (p=1)
- **VAS pain**: HA significantly lower at D3, D7, D14; equal at D21
- **Antibiotics needed**: 0 in both groups
- **D21 complete healing**: 100% both groups — key reference point for normal epithelial closure outer boundary in T2DM

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=36); single center; retrospective trial registration.
- Only simple extractions (no flap, no bur); cannot generalize to complex surgery.
- Clinical endpoints only; no histological or bone remodeling data.
- Short follow-up (21 days maximum); bone remodeling and implant outcomes not assessed.
- HA formulation includes amino acids — unclear whether benefit is from HA alone.

## 7. Glossary

- **Healing Index (modified Landry)**: 1–4 score for post-extraction socket healing by secondary intention (4=complete epithelial coverage with healthy granulation tissue)
- **Socket closure**: Diameter of residual socket opening (OV = oral-vestibular, MD = mesio-distal)
- **Split-mouth design**: Each patient serves as own control — one side HA, contralateral side untreated
