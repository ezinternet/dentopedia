## Why Ingested

최초의 상악 무치악 재활 글로벌 컨센서스에서 Group 3의 합의 보고서로, 고급 진단 영상·골·연조직 증대술·합병증 관리에 대한 임상 권고안을 제공한다. [[implants/full-arch/schwarz-2026-global-consensus-edentulous-maxilla-guidelines]]의 전체 지침 프레임워크에 그룹 3 결과를 연결한다.

## Three-line Summary

This consensus report from Working Group 3 of the 1st Global Consensus for Clinical Guidelines (GCCG) developed four clinical recommendations on advanced diagnostic imaging, augmentation techniques, and complication management for edentulous maxilla rehabilitation. All four recommendations reached consensus (≥75% agreement) through anonymous plenary voting by 68-88 participants. The recommendations are grounded in two systematic reviews and international expert surveys, following the S2k-level guideline framework.

## 세줄요약

최초 글로벌 컨센서스 임상지침(GCCG) 그룹 3의 합의 보고서로, 상악 무치악 재활을 위한 고급 진단 영상·증대술·합병증 관리에 대한 4개 임상 권고안을 개발. S2k 수준 지침 프레임워크와 구조화된 명목그룹기법을 따라 68-88명 참가자의 익명 투표로 모든 권고안 합의 도달(≥75%). 시스테마틱 리뷰 2편과 국제 전문가 설문을 근거로 하며, PROM·장기 결과·유지 프로토콜에서의 근거 격차를 확인.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First global consensus on advanced diagnostic imaging for edentulous maxilla rehabilitation
- Evidence-based recommendations on augmentation techniques and complication management
- Identification of critical evidence gaps in PROMs and long-term outcomes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: S2k-level consensus guideline (AWMF framework)
- **Process**: Nominal group technique + anonymous plenary voting
- **Participants**: 68-88 voters per recommendation (out of 105 delegates)
- **Evidence base**: 2 systematic reviews + international expert surveys

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 4 clinical recommendations formulated (all reached consensus)
- Voting participation: 68-88 per recommendation (mean 79/105)
- Consensus threshold: ≥75% but ≤95% for consensus, >95% for strong consensus
- Covers: diagnostic imaging, soft/hard tissue augmentation, complication management

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Evidence gaps in standardized PROMs
- Limited long-term outcome data
- Need for maintenance protocol research
- Regional practice variations may affect implementation
