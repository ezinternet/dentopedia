## Why Ingested

[[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/crego-ruiz-2023-periodontal-gingival-recession-aligner-vs-fixed-sr-ma]]는 단일 SR+MA로 치주 지표를 분석했으나, 여러 SR들의 결론을 종합하는 umbrella review가 없었다. 본 논문은 4편의 SR(2,042명)을 통합해 투명교정 vs 고정식 장치의 치주 영향을 시간대별(단기·중기·장기)로 정리하는 현재 최고 합성 수준의 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (systematic review of 4 SRs with meta-analyses, 2,042 patients, clear aligners n=978 vs fixed appliances n=1,064) assessing the impact on five periodontal parameters: plaque index (PI), gingival index (GI), BOP/SBI, PPD, and gingival recession.

Clear aligners showed statistically better PI, GI, SBI, and PPD at short- and mid-term follow-up (MD −0.14 to −1.10 for indices; MD −0.26 to −0.93 for PPD); gingival recession was stable with aligners vs significant increase with fixed at 3 months; long-term differences were non-significant.

Authors concluded periodontal effects are "comparable" between the two modalities; short-term statistical advantages for clear aligners are "practically negligible in a clinical setting" (all <1 mm or <1 index point); evidence quality: 1 high, 1 moderate, 2 low quality SRs included.

## 세줄요약

Umbrella review(4편 SR+MA 통합, 2,042명; 투명교정 978명 vs 고정식 1,064명): 치태지수(PI)·치은지수(GI)·출혈지수(BOP/SBI)·치주낭깊이(PPD)·치은퇴축 5개 지표 비교.

단·중기 PI, GI, SBI, PPD에서 투명교정이 통계적으로 우수(MD −0.14~−1.10; PPD MD −0.26~−0.93); 치은퇴축은 3개월 시점 투명교정 안정 vs 고정식 유의미한 증가; 장기에서는 차이 없음.

전체 결론: 두 방법의 치주 영향은 "비교 가능(comparable)"; 단기 차이는 "임상적으로 미미(negligible, <1mm / <1점)"; 포함 SR 품질: 상 1편, 중 1편, 하 2편.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Highest synthesis level**: Umbrella review is the highest-level synthesis design; aggregates findings across 4 SRs with 2,042 patients.
- **Time-stratified analysis**: Separates short-term (2–3 months), mid-term (6–9 months), and long-term (≥12 months) effects — shows the early advantage of aligners fades over time.
- **Gingival recession signal**: The only umbrella review to highlight short-term gingival stability advantage for clear aligners vs fixed appliances.
- **Clinical vs statistical significance gap**: Explicitly distinguishes statistically significant differences from clinically negligible ones (<1 mm PPD, <1 index point).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review (SR of SRs+MA)
- **Included SRs**: 4 systematic reviews with meta-analyses
- **Patients**: 2,042 total (978 clear aligner; 1,064 fixed; ages 10–51)
- **Outcomes**: PI, GI, BOP/SBI, PPD, gingival recession
- **SR quality**: AMSTAR 2; primary study RoB: Cochrane RoB / Newcastle-Ottawa across included SRs

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Plaque Index (PI):**
- Short-term: CA better (MD −0.35 to −0.69)
- Mid-term: CA superior (MD −0.91 to −1.10)
- Long-term: NS

**Gingival Index (GI):**
- Short-term: CA better (MD −0.24 to −0.37)
- Mid-term: small CA advantage (MD −0.14)
- Long-term: NS

**PPD:**
- Short/mid-term: CA better (MD −0.26 to −0.42)
- Long-term: CA limits worsening (MD −0.45 to −0.93)

**Sulcus Bleeding Index (SBI):**
- Short-term: CA superior (SMD −0.44 to −0.49)
- Mid-term: CA favored (SMD −0.91)

**Gingival Recession:**
- CA: stable at 3 months
- Fixed: significant increase at 3 months
- Long-term: insufficient data

**Clinical interpretation**: All statistically significant differences are <1 mm (PPD) or <1 point (ordinal indices) — below thresholds of clinical relevance.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High heterogeneity across included SRs (PICO, follow-up timing, patient age)
- Overlapping primary studies in multiple SRs (double-counting risk)
- Heterogeneous follow-up timing makes time-stratification imprecise
- No CAL (clinical attachment level) data available
- Recession evidence from single study (3-month only); long-term recession data absent
- 2 of 4 included SRs rated low quality by AMSTAR 2

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: Systematic review that synthesizes multiple systematic reviews; highest evidence synthesis design
- **AMSTAR 2**: Assessment of Multiple Systematic Reviews version 2 — quality tool for SRs
- **MD**: Mean difference (continuous outcomes with same scale)
- **SMD**: Standardized mean difference (continuous outcomes with different scales)
- **PPD**: Periodontal probing depth
- **SBI**: Sulcus Bleeding Index (variant of BOP)
- **CAL**: Clinical attachment level — not assessed in included SRs
