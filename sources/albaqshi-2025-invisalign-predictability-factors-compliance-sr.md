## Why Ingested

투명교정 성공을 결정하는 **환자 순응도·치아이동 예측도·기술적 요인을 복합적으로 종합한 SR**이 위키에 없었다. 본 논문은 남성·첫 교정 경험 없는 환자의 순응도 우위, 디지털 모니터링 도구의 과대추정 편향, 이동 유형별 정확도 차이를 통합 분석한다.

## One-line Summary

SR, 6 studies: Invisalign success is multifactorial — male patients and orthodontic-naive patients show higher compliance; inadequate wear time is the leading cause of failure; bucco-lingual tipping is most accurate while rotation/intrusion/expansion are least reliable.

## 한줄요약

SR, 6개 연구: 투명교정 성공은 다요인적 — 남성·교정 경험 없는 환자가 순응도 높음, 착용 시간 부족이 주된 실패 원인; 협설 경사 이동이 가장 정확하고 회전·압하·확장은 가장 부정확.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Identifies compliance predictors: males and patients without prior orthodontic experience show higher compliance
- Establishes inadequate wear time as the primary cause of suboptimal outcomes
- Documents movement accuracy hierarchy: bucco-lingual tipping (best) > rotation/intrusion/expansion (worst)
- Highlights overestimation bias in Invisalign Progress Assessment digital monitoring
- Confirms shorter treatment duration with aligners vs fixed, but inferior smile parameters in some cases

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review
- Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library (to August 2025)
- Study designs: RCTs, cohort, case-control, cross-sectional
- Population: patients treated with Invisalign
- Studies included: 6
- Outcomes: compliance rates, wear time, tooth movement accuracy, treatment duration, smile parameters
- No meta-analysis (heterogeneity in design and outcome measures)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Compliance: males > females; no prior ortho experience = higher compliance
- Primary failure cause: inadequate wear time
- Movement accuracy ranking (most to least accurate):
  1. Bucco-lingual tipping
  2. Mesiodistal tipping
  3. Rotation (least accurate along with intrusion and expansion)
- Digital monitoring: Invisalign Progress Assessment overestimates tooth movement
- vs Fixed appliances: shorter treatment duration, better comfort/esthetics; less favorable for certain smile parameters

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 6 studies met inclusion criteria — limited evidence base
- Cureus is an open-access journal with broader peer review scope
- Heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis
- Needs prospective standardized studies with defined success criteria
- Patient behavioral factors (wear time tracking) inconsistently reported

## 7. Glossary

- Invisalign Progress Assessment: digital monitoring tool by Align Technology
- Compliance: adherence to recommended 20–22 hours/day wear schedule
- Bucco-lingual tipping: tooth movement in the labial-palatal/lingual direction
