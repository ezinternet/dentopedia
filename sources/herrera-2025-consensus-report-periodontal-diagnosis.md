## Why Ingested
20차 EFP 유럽 치주학 워크숍의 공식 컨센서스 보고서로, 치주 진단에서 전통적 탐침부터 AI·바이오마커까지 현황과 권장을 체계화한 문서. 기존 [[periodontics/caton-2018-classification-scheme-periodontal-periimplant-diseases]] 분류 이후 진단법 업데이트가 필요하다는 판단으로 인제스트 — 치주 진단 판단의 근거 축으로 활용.

## One-line Summary
Consensus report of 70 experts from 21 countries (EFP 2025) establishing that manual periodontal probing remains the reference standard while affirming emerging roles for imaging, biomarkers, and AI in periodontal diagnosis.

## 한줄요약
70명(21개국) EFP 컨센서스(2025): 수기 치주 탐침이 기준 표준으로 확립되고, 영상·바이오마커·AI 진단이 보조 역할로 점진 통합되는 방향을 권장.

## Three-line Summary

European Federation of Periodontology consensus report (J Clin Periodontol 2025;52 Suppl 29:4–33) built on 8 commissioned systematic reviews across 3 working groups with 70 participants, appraising traditional diagnostics, biomarkers and emerging technologies for periodontal diagnosis.

Manual 6-point probing was reaffirmed as the reference standard (error ±1–2 mm) with bleeding on probing carrying the best negative predictive value for stability; CBCT was judged not indicated for routine periodontal diagnosis (selective use for intra-bony defects and furcation involvement); microbial, host-derived and genetic biomarkers were classed as exploratory without validated thresholds; and AI systems, while performing at least as well as experts on image analysis, remain Class I/II diagnostic aids with no independent peer-reviewed validation.

Nothing yet displaces the periodontal probe, so new diagnostic technology should be adopted as an adjunct rather than a replacement — and note that the report was funded by Kenvue, a consumer oral-care company, a declared potential conflict for recommendations touching consumer diagnostics.

## 세줄요약

유럽치주학회 (European Federation of Periodontology, EFP) 합의보고서 (J Clin Periodontol 2025;52 Suppl 29:4–33): 3개 작업그룹·참가자 70명이 의뢰한 체계적 문헌고찰 8편을 토대로 치주 진단의 전통적 방법·바이오마커·신기술을 평가.

수동 6점 탐침 (Manual Probing)이 기준표준 (Reference Standard)으로 재확인됐고(오차 ±1~2 mm), 탐침시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)은 치주 안정성에 대한 음성예측도가 가장 높았다. CBCT는 **일상적 치주 진단에 비적응증**(골내낭·치근이개부에 선택적 사용), 미생물·숙주유래·유전 바이오마커는 검증된 역치가 없는 탐색 단계, 인공지능 (Artificial Intelligence, AI)은 영상 판독에서 전문가에 준하는 성능을 내지만 여전히 Class I/II 진단보조 기기이며 독립적 동료심사 검증이 없다.

아직 **치주탐침을 대체하는 것은 없다** — 신기술은 대체가 아니라 보조로 도입해야 한다. 다만 이 보고서는 소비자 구강관리 기업 Kenvue의 재정 지원을 받았고, 소비자용 진단에 걸리는 권고에서는 이해상충 가능성이 선언돼 있다.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Journal of Clinical Periodontology, 2025; 52(Suppl. 29):4–33
- Publication date: March 2025 (received Feb 9, accepted Mar 4)
- Funding: Kenvue Inc. (Skillman, NJ, USA); European Federation of Periodontology
- Conflicts of interest: Multi-author list; Kenvue is a consumer oral-care company (potential COI)
- 8 commissioned systematic reviews across 3 working groups; 70 participants

## 2. Key Contributions
- Reaffirms manual probing as the **reference standard** for periodontal diagnosis after comprehensive evidence review
- Establishes CBCT as **not indicated** for routine periodontal diagnosis (radiation dose/cost–benefit)
- Identifies biomarker research (microbial, host-derived, genetic) as predominantly **exploratory** — lacks diagnostic validation
- AI-assisted image interpretation **performs comparably to experts** but no method is validated for clinical diagnostic use
- Provides a research agenda for standardized datasets, multi-centre AI trials, and integrative multi-omics approaches

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Consensus workshop report
- Organization: European Federation of Periodontology (EFP), 20th European Workshop on Periodontology
- 3 working groups, each supported by systematic reviews (8 SRs total):
  - **WG1** — Traditional diagnostics: probing, imaging (2D, CBCT, MRI, ultrasound)
  - **WG2** — Biomarkers: microbial (Bostanci et al. 2025), host-derived (Rakic et al. 2025), genetic/epigenetic (Dommisch et al. 2025)
  - **WG3** — Emerging technologies: questionnaire-based assessments, AI systems
- Framework: PICOTS (Patient, Intervention, Comparator, Outcome, Time, Setting)
- Assessment tools: STARD, QUADAS-2, QUADAS-C, PRISMA-DTA, TRIPOD, TRIPOD+AI

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### WG1: Traditional Diagnostics
- Manual probing (6-point/tooth): reference standard; error ±1–2 mm (force variation, angulation, pocket anatomy)
- BOP (bleeding on probing): best negative predictive value for periodontal stability
- 2D periapical radiographs (paralleling technique): first-line radiographic method
- CBCT: **not recommended routinely**; indicated selectively for intra-bony defects and furcation involvement
- MRI, ultrasound: limited evidence, promising but not practice-ready
- No technology fulfills all "ideal" radiographic criteria simultaneously

### WG2: Biomarkers
- **Microbial**: Studies lack validated thresholds; continuous assay evolution precludes consistent cut-offs; community-level dysbiosis metrics understudied
- **Host-derived**: Primarily discovery/case-control designs, small samples, high bias, not following diagnostic test guidelines
- **Genetic/epigenetic**: Genomics useful only for rare monogenic/syndromic forms; polygenic common periodontitis not amenable to genetic diagnostics; epigenetics (DNA methylation, miRNA) shows promise but remains unvalidated

### WG3: Emerging Technologies
- Questionnaire-based assessments: utility for risk screening and monitoring; needs validation against clinical measures
- AI systems (AIS): perform at least as well as experts for radiographic and photographic image analysis
- Commercially available AI devices are Class I/II (diagnostic aids, not stand-alone diagnostics); no peer-reviewed independent validation
- Multimodal AI integration not yet superior to imaging alone
- Research agenda: standardized datasets, multi-centre studies, interpretability, Class III validation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Most biomarker studies are discovery-phase; replication and validation studies urgently needed
- AI validation relies on small proof-of-principle studies; generalizability across demographics unclear
- Kenvue funding raises potential COI for recommendations favoring consumer diagnostics
- Lack of standardized diagnostic test guidelines compliance in periodontal DAS literature
- Research priorities: longitudinal datasets, standardized annotation, integrative multi-omics, AI interpretability

## 7. Glossary
- **DAS** (Diagnostic Accuracy Study): study type evaluating test accuracy vs. reference standard
- **STARD**: STAndards for Reporting Diagnostic accuracy studies
- **QUADAS-2**: Quality assessment tool for DAS
- **PICOTS**: Patient, Intervention, Comparator, Outcome, Time, Setting framework
- **AUC** (Area Under the ROC Curve): overall diagnostic accuracy summary statistic
- **BOP** (Bleeding on Probing): clinical parameter with high negative predictive value for periodontal stability
- **CAL** (Clinical Attachment Loss): primary outcome measure for periodontitis staging
- **CBCT** (Cone Beam Computed Tomography): 3D radiographic method; higher radiation dose than 2D
- **AIS** (AI Systems): applied data science methods for diagnostic interpretation
- **TRIPOD+AI**: reporting checklist for clinical prediction models using ML methods
