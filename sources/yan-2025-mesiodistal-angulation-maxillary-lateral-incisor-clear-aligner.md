## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/muthuswamy-pandian-2025-optimized-conventional-attachment-accuracy-sr-ma]]에서 발치 증례 측절치 치근 각도 변화는 단 1편 연구만 근거로 "첨부장치 유무 유의차 없음"이라 결론됐다. 이 후향적 CBCT 연구(Yan 2025)는 발치 투명교정 치료 중 상악 측절치 근원심 경사 (Mesiodistal Angulation, MDA) 변화를 첨부장치 유무·후퇴 순서·수평 이동 거리로 직접 분석해 그 공백을 채운다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT study (27 patients, 54 maxillary lateral incisors; Angle class I/II, four-premolar extraction, clear aligner) comparing mesiodistal angulation at T0 (pretreatment) and T1 (refinement stage) under three factors: attachment presence, retraction method, and horizontal movement distance.

Lateral incisors showed significant distal inclination (p<0.001); attachments reduced distal tipping (p<0.001); en masse retraction caused more distal inclination than canine-first or frog-pattern (p<0.05); greater horizontal movement at >4mm and >7mm thresholds increased distal inclination risk (p<0.001); crown-to-root ratio had no significant effect (p>0.05).

Clear aligner extraction cases should monitor lateral incisor angulation closely; adding attachments and choosing canine-first retraction are the two actionable clinical design choices to reduce distal inclination of the lateral incisor.

## 세줄요약

후향적 CBCT 연구(27명, 상악 측절치 54개; Angle I·II급, 소구치 4개 발치, 투명교정) — 전처치(T0)와 재치료 단계(T1) 간 근원심 경사 변화를 첨부장치 유무·후퇴 순서·수평 이동 거리 3요인으로 비교.

측절치에서 원심 경사가 유의하게 발생(p<0.001); 첨부장치군이 비첨부군보다 원심 경사 적음(p<0.001); 일괄 후퇴 (En Masse Retraction)가 견치 우선 후퇴·개구리 패턴보다 원심 경사 더 크게(p<0.05); 수평 이동 거리 >4mm·>7mm 임계값에서 원심 경사 위험 증가(p<0.001); 치관-치근 비율은 유의한 영향 없음(p>0.05).

발치 투명교정 치료 시 측절치 각도 변화를 주시해야 하며, 첨부장치 추가와 견치 우선 후퇴 (Canine-First Retraction) 설계가 원심 경사 감소를 위한 두 핵심 임상 조치.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to systematically analyze mesiodistal angulation changes of maxillary lateral incisors specifically in four-premolar extraction treatment by clear aligner
- Identifies three modifiable risk factors: attachment presence, retraction sequence, and horizontal movement distance
- Quantifies movement distance thresholds (>4mm, >7mm) associated with dose-dependent increase in distal inclination
- Provides mechanistic rationale for canine-first retraction as the preferred extraction strategy for lateral incisor angulation control

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective, single-center, CBCT-based
- **Sample**: 27 patients (mean age 21.1 ± 4.9y; 18F, 9M); 54 maxillary lateral incisors (bilateral; no significant L-R difference → data merged)
- **Inclusion**: ANB 0–8°, Angle class I or II malocclusion, four premolar extraction treatment with clear aligners
- **Imaging**: I-CAT scanner (120 kVp, 5 mA, 7s, 0.25mm slice thickness, 230×170mm FOV); DICOM → Dolphin software (panoramic reconstruction); Mimics 21.0 (3D superimposition)
- **Measurement**: Angle between midpalatal suture reference line and long axis of lateral incisor (longest root canal extension) on reconstructed panoramic image
- **Timepoints**: T0 = pretreatment; T1 = refinement stage
- **Groups**: Attachment vs nonattachment; en masse vs canine-first vs frog-pattern retraction; movement distance 1–3 mm vs 4–6 mm vs 7–9 mm; crown-to-root ratio groups
- **Statistics**: Paired comparison T0 vs T1; LSD post-hoc tests for group comparisons

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Primary outcome:**
- Significant distal inclination T0→T1 (p<0.001); no significant bilateral asymmetry (p>0.05)

**Attachment effect (p<0.001):**
- Attachment group: less distal inclination; accuracy 88.4%
- Nonattachment group: greater distal inclination; accuracy 86.9%

**Retraction method (p<0.05 overall; LSD post-hoc):**
- En masse vs canine-first: significantly more distal inclination en masse (p<0.001)
- Canine-first vs frog-pattern: canine-first better (p=0.004)
- En masse vs frog-pattern: not significant (p>0.05)
- Mechanism: canine-first reaction force opposes lateral incisor distal tipping; improved aligner wrap after canine moves distally; reduced roller-coaster effect

**Horizontal movement distance (p<0.001 overall; LSD post-hoc):**
- 1–3 mm vs 4–6 mm: p=0.008
- 4–6 mm vs 7–9 mm: p=0.01
- 1–3 mm vs 7–9 mm: p<0.001
- Dose-dependent effect: risk increases meaningfully at >4mm and again at >7mm

**Crown-to-root ratio:** No significant effect (p>0.05)

**Esthetic benchmarks (cited standards):**
- Brunzel: ≤9° mesial inclination for attractive bilaterally symmetrical smile
- Pani: ≤5° tilt from central incisor axis for optimal esthetics

**ABO objective grading standard:** Roots parallel and perpendicular to occlusal plane post-treatment; apex deviation ≤1mm

**Arch circumference consequence (Jain):** 1° angulation increase = 0.021mm arch circumference consumed

**Literature comparator (AlBalaa):** Clear aligner actual precision for lateral incisor movement 51.19%; correction amount 48.81%

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, single-center design
- Small sample (n=27 patients, 54 teeth)
- Only mesiodistal angulation analyzed (torque and rotation not comprehensively assessed)
- Follow-up to refinement stage only — not final debond; long-term outcome unknown
- Ethnically homogeneous sample

## 7. Glossary

- **근원심 경사 (Mesiodistal Angulation, MDA)**: Angle formed between long axis of tooth and reference line (midpalatal suture) in the panoramic plane; "angulation" in ABO grading
- **투명교정 (Clear Aligner Therapy, CAT)**: Removable thermoplastic appliance-based orthodontic treatment
- **일괄 후퇴 (En Masse Retraction)**: Simultaneous retraction of all six anterior teeth as a unit
- **견치 우선 후퇴 (Canine-First Retraction)**: Sequential strategy — canines retracted approximately 1.5mm first before incisors follow
- **개구리 패턴 후퇴 (Frog-Pattern Retraction)**: Alternating sequential retraction pattern
- **미국 교정의 심사위원회 기준 (ABO Objective Grading System)**: Panoramic-based standard requiring root parallelism and apex deviation ≤1mm post-treatment
