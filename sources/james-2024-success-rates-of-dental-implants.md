## Why Ingested

An umbrella-style systematic review of diabetic implant outcomes that consolidates four prior SR/MAs (Schimmel 2018, Andrade 2022, Lv 2022, de Oliveira-Neto 2019) under one HbA1c-threshold framing — it independently confirms the bottom line already held on [[wiki/implants/survival/wagner-2022-diabetes-mellitus-dental-implants-sr]] ("survival holds when diabetes is controlled; peri-implant health degrades with poor control") and adds the dose-response HbA1c→MBL/PD gradient as support for the [[wiki/overviews/diabetic-patient-immediate-implant-decision]] synthesis.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 4 prior systematic reviews/meta-analyses (156 records screened, PRISMA) on dental implant success in type 2 diabetes patients, stratifying results by glycemic control (HbA1c).

Well-controlled diabetes (HbA1c <8%) does not compromise implant survival — 96.1–97.3% at 1 year and 87.3–96.1% at 5 years, with no survival difference versus non-diabetics (Andrade RR = 1.00, 95% CI 0.96–1.04); poor control (HbA1c >8%) worsens marginal bone loss and probing depth in a dose-response manner (Lv 2022).

Diabetes is not a contraindication to implants when HbA1c <8% (ideally <7%); key limitation is qualitative synthesis of heterogeneous SR/MAs without de novo statistical pooling.

## 세줄요약

당뇨 임플란트 성공률을 당화혈색소(Hemoglobin A1c, HbA1c) 조절 수준으로 층화해 본 기존 시스템틱 고찰·메타분석 4편을 종합한 PRISMA 기반 고찰(스크리닝 156건, 포함 4편).

조절 양호 제2형 당뇨병(Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus, T2DM)(HbA1c <8%)은 임플란트 생존율을 떨어뜨리지 않음 — 1년 96.1–97.3%, 5년 87.3–96.1%, 비당뇨 대비 생존 차이 없음(상대위험도(Relative Risk, RR) 1.00, 95% CI 0.96–1.04); 조절 불량(HbA1c >8%)은 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)·탐침깊이(Probing Depth, PD)를 용량-반응적으로 악화.

HbA1c <8%(가능하면 <7%)면 당뇨는 임플란트 금기가 아님; 자체 메타분석 없이 이질적인 SR/MA를 정성적으로 종합한 점이 핵심 한계.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Cureus 16(12): e76361 (2024-12-25)
- **DOI**: 10.7759/cureus.76361
- **Institution**: Abbottabad Medical College, Peshawar; Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences, Islamabad; Rehman Medical Institute, Peshawar, Pakistan
- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA) of systematic reviews and meta-analyses — qualitative narrative synthesis, no de novo statistical pooling

## 2. Key Contributions
- Consolidates four SR/MAs on diabetic implant success under a single HbA1c-threshold lens (well-controlled ≤7%, poorly controlled >8%), framing glycemic control rather than diabetes status itself as the operative variable.
- Adds the dose-response relationship between HbA1c and peri-implant health (PD and MBL worsen as HbA1c rises; threshold seen at HbA1c >8%, per Lv 2022 DRMA) as a distinct evidence thread.
- Reports peri-implant biomarker evidence (higher IL-6, TNF-α, IL-8, RANKL in diabetic/hyperglycemic patients; p = 0.01) as the mechanistic bridge between poor control and bone loss.
- Clinical guidance: maintain HbA1c <8% (ideally <7%) before/during implant therapy; interdisciplinary care (endocrinologist–dentist–hygienist) and strict protocol adherence.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA) of existing systematic reviews and meta-analyses; qualitative synthesis
- **Databases**: PubMed (41), Cochrane (31), Embase (31), Scopus (20), Web of Science (33) — 156 records total; 35 duplicates removed; 121 screened; 52 excluded; 69 sought; 28 unavailable; 41 eligibility-assessed; 37 excluded (non-diabetic 5, case reports 5, no meta-analytic component 6, ambiguous reporting 4, incomplete data 6, uncontrolled systemic diseases 11)
- **n**: 4 included studies (previous SR/MAs; 60 studies and 634 participants across the two most data-rich, follow-up up to 6 years)
- **Inclusion**: T2DM patients undergoing implant placement; systematic reviews/meta-analyses with HbA1c-based glycemic control stratification; peri-implant health outcomes
- **Quality assessment**: AMSTAR 2 (2 high, 1 moderate, 1 moderate-to-high)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Included-study synthesis (Table 1 of the paper):

| Source study | Coverage | Implant survival | Peri-implant outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schimmel 2018 (SR+MA) | Geriatric (≥75 y) & systemic conditions; 60 studies, 7 for MA | 97.3% at 1 y (95% CI 94.3–98.7); 96.1% at 5 y (95% CI 87.3–98.9) | T2DM & Parkinson high survival; cancer lower (radiotherapy) |
| Andrade 2022 (SR+MA) | T2DM immediately loaded (IL) implants; 7 studies, 5 for MA | RR 1.00 vs non-diabetic (95% CI 0.96–1.04, p = 0.91) — no difference even with poor glycemic control | MBL MD −0.08 mm (95% CI −0.25–0.08, p = 0.33), IL vs conventional loading |
| Lv 2022 (SR+MA + DRMA) | 634 participants, 10 studies, follow-up to 6 y | Not compromised at HbA1c <10% | AGEs in PICF p<0.01; ISQ p=0.04; BOP p<0.01; PD p=0.01; MBL p<0.01; PD/MBL worsen with HbA1c >8% (dose-response) |
| de Oliveira-Neto 2019 (overview of SRs) | 8 SRs (6 moderate, 2 high AMSTAR) | 31.8%–100% across reviews; 4 meta-analyses: no DM effect | MBL statistically significant but clinically irrelevant |

Abstract-level pooled framing: survival 96.1–97.3% at 1 year and 87.3–96.1% at 5 years in well-managed (HbA1c <8%) patients, comparable to non-diabetics; MBL mean difference −0.08 mm (95% CI −0.25 to 0.08).

**Quality assessment (AMSTAR 2)**: Schimmel 2018 high; Andrade 2022 high (PROSPERO + PRISMA + JBI bias tool); Lv 2022 moderate (I² >70%, small study set); de Oliveira-Neto 2019 moderate-to-high.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Narrative/qualitative synthesis only — results of the four SR/MAs were not statistically pooled, so no overall effect estimate was generated by this review itself.
- Heterogeneity among included studies (designs, populations, follow-up durations), some with high statistical heterogeneity (I² >70%).
- Small number of included studies, and variable HbA1c thresholds across sources (≤7% / <8% / <10%).
- Limited long-term data; generalized to poorly controlled diabetics or patients with comorbidities with caution; publication bias and underrepresentation of some regions acknowledged.
- Future: standardized outcome reporting, subgroup analyses at finer glycemic thresholds (<7%, 7–8%, >8%), long-term MBL and survival follow-up.

## 7. Glossary
- **HbA1c**: Hemoglobin A1c / 당화혈색소 — glycemic control marker; well-controlled ≤7%, poorly controlled >8% in this review.
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Loss / 변연골소실 — crestal peri-implant bone resorption.
- **IL**: Immediately Loaded / 즉시부하 — implants loaded at or shortly after placement.
- **PICF**: Peri-Implant Crevicular Fluid — peri-implant sulcular fluid, source of the biomarker measurements (AGEs, cytokines).
- **DRMA**: Dose-Response Meta-Analysis — tests whether outcomes worsen continuously with the exposure (here HbA1c).
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient / 임플란트 안정성 지수 — resonance-frequency-based stability measure (assessed in Lv 2022).
