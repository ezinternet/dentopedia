## Why Ingested
임플란트 1차 안정성 서베일런스 2026-09: HFT(Hybrid Funnel Technique) — 선택적 피질골 준비 + 수질골 압밀(osteocompaction)을 결합한 신규 골삭제 기법 — 의 3년 MBL 결과. [[implants/versah-protocols/tomar-2026-osseodensification-conventional-drilling-umbrella]]의 골보존 패러다임 확장; 골삭제 기법이 임플란트 표면보다 MBL에 더 중요하다는 독립적 근거.

## One-line Summary
3-year non-randomized controlled trial (CID 2026): 87 implants, 43 patients — Hybrid Funnel Technique reduced MBL to 0.46 mm vs 1.34 mm for conventional drilling at 3 years (p<0.001); site preparation technique was the only independent predictor of MBL.

## 한줄요약

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Clinical Implant Dentistry and Related Research, 2026;28(4):e70175
- DOI: 10.1111/cid.70175 | PMID: 42518174 | PMC: PMC13411644
- Setting: Rome/Genova/Foggia, Italy; University of Bern, Switzerland

## 2. Key Contributions
- HFT (selective cortical preparation + medullary osteocompaction) reduces 3-year MBL by ~0.9mm vs conventional
- Site preparation technique is the ONLY independent variable associated with 3-year MBL in multivariate analysis
- β = -1.10mm (95%CI -1.675 to -0.527, p<0.001) — not insertion torque, not surface
- Same bioactive-surface implants used in both groups — isolates osteotomy effect
- HFT group: lower BoP and PI at 3 years — better peri-implant soft tissue health too
- Challenges assumption that "surface treatment" is the primary determinant of bone maintenance

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Prospective non-randomized controlled clinical trial (CCT)
- n=87 implants, 43 patients: conventional (n=42), HFT (n=45)
- Identical bioactive-surface implants in both groups
- HFT: combines (1) selective cortical bone preparation and (2) medullary osteocompaction
- Primary outcome: radiographic MBL at 3 years
- Secondary: BoP, PI at 3 years
- Statistics: linear mixed-effects models (clustering by patient)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Non-randomized design — allocation bias possible
- Multiple centers but small per-center n
- No implant-level randomization — surgeon preference may confound
- 87 implants: adequate for primary outcome, underpowered for subgroup analyses
- Need: full RCT, longer follow-up (5+ years), diverse implant systems

## 6. Related Work
- OD (Densahbur) similarly preserves bone by compaction vs removal (Marzorati 2026, Tomar 2026)
- HFT is distinct from Versah OD — uses different instruments, same osteocompaction principle
- Cortical over-compression during conventional drilling linked to thermal injury and MBL (classic evidence)
- "Bioactive surface" effect debated — this study suggests site prep technique > surface chemistry for MBL

## 7. Glossary
- HFT: Hybrid Funnel Technique — selective cortical shaping + medullary bone osteocompaction
- Osteocompaction: lateral bone condensation during osteotomy (preserves, not removes bone)
- MBL: Marginal bone loss — crestal bone level change radiographically
- BoP: Bleeding on probing; PI: Plaque index
- CCT: Controlled clinical trial (prospective but non-randomized)
