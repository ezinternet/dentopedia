## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case-control study at Semmelweis University (Hungary, patients treated 2015-2025): 24 patients with early implant failure (implant lost before loading) versus 24 age- and sex-matched controls with implants surviving at least 5 years; preoperative mandibular trabecular bone was analysed by fractal dimension (FD) and lacunarity on panoramic radiographs (24 per group, three regions) and by volumetric 3D FD on CBCT (15 per group).

No significant difference was found between failure and control groups in any region or method (p>0.05; exact p-values not reported in the text); post hoc power ranged only from 5.1% to 42.2%, and e.g. molar-region FD (Cohen's d = 0.52) would need about 120 patients, premolar FD (d = -0.16) up to 1230.

The authors conclude that FD and lacunarity alone are not reliable predictors of early implant failure; the negative result is underpowered and the text also contains promotional statements that sit awkwardly with it, so fractal analysis should be treated as an unvalidated adjunct, not a screening tool.

## 세줄요약

헝가리 젬멜바이스 대학교(Semmelweis University)에서 2015-2025년 치료한 환자를 대상으로 한 후향적 증례-대조군 연구: 조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure, 보철 부하 전 소실) 환자 24명과 연령·성별을 맞춘 5년 이상 생존 대조군 24명의 하악 골소주 구조를 파노라마 방사선(군당 24명, 3개 부위)에서 프랙탈 차원 (fractal dimension, FD)·간극도 (lacunarity)로, CBCT(군당 15명)에서 3차원 FD로 분석했다.

모든 부위·방법에서 두 군 간 유의한 차이가 없었다 (p>0.05, 정확한 p값은 본문에 없음); 사후 검정력 (post hoc power)은 5.1-42.2%에 불과했으며, 대구치 부위 FD (Cohen's d = 0.52)는 약 120명, 소구치 FD (d = -0.16)는 최대 1230명이 필요하다고 추정했다.

저자는 FD와 간극도 단독으로는 조기 임플란트 실패의 신뢰할 만한 예측 인자가 아니라고 결론지었으나, 이 음성 결과는 검정력이 낮고 본문 결론에 상반된 기대 표현도 섞여 있으므로 프랙탈 분석은 검증되지 않은 보조 지표로만 취급해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Tests whether general (non-peri-implant) mandibular trabecular architecture, quantified by FD and lacunarity, differs between patients with early implant failure and controls.
- Applies volumetric 3D FD on CBCT (BoneJ) with two preprocessing methods (White and Rudolph; simplified Kato), which the authors describe as rare in early failure research.
- Reports a negative result with explicit post hoc power (5.1% to 42.2%) and a priori sample size estimates (about 120 to over 1230 patients depending on region).
- Shares individual-level data openly on Mendeley Data.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective case-control, STROBE-reported, single center, patients treated 2015-2025, data analysed April 2025; informed consent signed.
- **Test group**: patients with early implant failure; excluded if implants had been temporarily or permanently restored/loaded, or removed for malposition, damage to anatomical landmarks, or biomechanical complications. Early failure is defined in the Introduction as loss or removal prior to functional loading; no day/week window is stated.
- **Control group**: randomly drawn from patients with implants surviving at least 5 years and no progressive peri-implantitis (Renvert criteria), matched for age and sex distribution.
- **Overall exclusions**: uncontrolled bone-metabolism diseases, bone-active medications (antiresorptives, glucocorticoids, etc.), chemotherapy, head-neck irradiation, periapical disease, uncontrolled periodontitis, drug abuse, alcoholism or heavy smoking, and others.
- **Implants and protocol**: Callus Pro and Nobel Replace Parallel Conical Connection; placement at least 2 months after extraction; amoxicillin/clavulanic acid 7 days (clindamycin if penicillin allergic); conventional loading after a 2-month healing period without temporary prostheses.
- **n**: 24 patients per group (48 total) with panoramic radiographs; 15 per group (30 total) with mandibular CBCT. The number of implants is not reported in the retrieved text.
- **2D analysis**: 64 x 64 pixel ROIs in front, premolar, molar regions of the mandible; White and Rudolph preprocessing (Gaussian blur sigma 35, subtraction, +128, binarization, erosion/dilation, skeletonization); FD by box counting (FracLac 2.5) and lacunarity, in ImageJ 1.54p.
- **3D analysis**: 16 x 16 x 25 voxel ROIs of pure trabecular bone (CBCT, 200 um voxel, 15 x 15 cm field of view); FD via BoneJ after White and Rudolph preprocessing or simplified Kato preprocessing.
- **Statistics**: Kolmogorov-Smirnov/Shapiro-Wilk, then t-test or Mann-Whitney U; alpha 0.05; post hoc power and a priori sample size by G*Power.

- No significant group difference for any FD or lacunarity comparison (p>0.05); exact p-values not shown in the retrieved text.
- Achieved power 5.1% to 42.2%. Required total sample for 80% power: about 120 patients (60 per group) for molar FD (d = 0.52); up to 1230 for premolar FD (d = -0.16); over 400-600 for lacunarity and some CBCT comparisons.
- Numerically, 3D FD medians were lower in the failure group in most regions, but none reached significance in this small sample.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small retrospective sample, especially the CBCT subgroup (15 per group); very low power (5.1-42.2%), so non-significance may reflect lack of power rather than absence of effect (authors' own statement).
- Measures general mandibular trabecular bone, not the peri-implant site; surgical and local mechanical variables, ISQ, and Hounsfield units were not available or not analysed.
- Control group was defined by at least 5 years of survival, while the test group by pre-loading failure; implant-level data (system, length, diameter, site) are not compared in the retrieved text.
- FD is sensitive to ROI selection, voxel size, preprocessing and Gaussian sigma; 3D methodology lacks standardization.
- Internal inconsistencies in the retrieved text: the Conclusions state that fractal analysis alone is not a reliable predictor and in the next sentence that it is a valuable tool for assessing early implant outcomes; the lacunarity sentence lists "control and test group" in the reverse order from the FD sentences; the Descriptive and power tables were not included in the text.
- Future work proposed: larger prospective studies, automated ROI selection or machine-learning FD, and combination with HU, ISQ or bone area fraction.

## 7. Glossary

- **Fractal dimension (FD, 프랙탈 차원)**: measure of structural complexity of the skeletonized trabecular pattern, here by box counting.
- **Lacunarity (간극도)**: measure of gap size distribution/heterogeneity in the trabecular image.
- **Early implant failure (조기 임플란트 실패)**: loss or removal of an implant prior to functional loading, usually from failed osseointegration.
- **CBCT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, 콘빔 CT)**: 3D radiographic imaging used here for volumetric FD.
- **Post hoc power (사후 검정력)**: achieved probability of detecting the observed effect given sample size.
