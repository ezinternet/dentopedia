## Why Ingested

Surveillance hit on lateral-window sinus augmentation new bone formation. This single-arm prospective histologic study is the wiki's direct clinical evidence for a 1:1 autogenous bone + 30/70 HA/β-TCP graft in ≤5 mm residual-height sinuses — the exact material ratio and boundary condition that the BCP network meta-analysis [[sinus-lift/lateral/somngam-2025-new-bone-formation-biphasic-calcium]] can only rank indirectly. It reinforces the histomorphometric composite-graft picture of [[sinus-lift/lateral/nguyen-2026-histological-and-histomorphometric-assessment-of]] with an autograft-containing arm.

## Three-line Summary

Single-center prospective single-arm study (12 enrolled, 10 analysed at 6 months) of direct lateral-window sinus augmentation in patients with ≤5 mm subantral bone height, using a 1:1 mixture of local autogenous bone and biphasic calcium phosphate (30% HA / 70% β-TCP, Osteon II) with piezosurgical membrane elevation and a PRF membrane.
At 6 months mean subantral height rose from 4.09 ± 0.57 mm to 12.62 ± 0.99 mm (gain 8.53 ± 1.07 mm, p < 0.001); trephine histomorphometry showed 48.08% ± 2.41% new bone, 27.25% ± 3.18% soft tissue and 24.36% ± 2.91% residual graft, with no membrane perforation or major complications.
The 1:1 autograft + 30/70 BCP combination gives predictable height gain and favorable 6-month new bone, but the single-arm design (n = 10), 6-month follow-up and unrecorded implant torque limit causal claims and long-term inference.

## 세줄요약

단일센터 전향적 단일군 연구 (12명 등록, 6개월 시 10명 분석)로 잔존 상악동저골 높이 ≤5 mm 환자에서 측방창 직접 상악동 거상술을 시행 — 국소 자가골과 이상성 인산칼슘 (Biphasic Calcium Phosphate, BCP; 30% 수산화인회석/70% β-삼인산칼슘, Osteon II)을 1:1로 혼합하고 초음파골절삭 (Piezosurgery) 거상 및 혈소판풍부피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF) 막을 사용.
6개월 시 평균 상악동저골 높이가 4.09 ± 0.57 mm에서 12.62 ± 0.99 mm로 증가 (획득 8.53 ± 1.07 mm, p < 0.001)했고, 트레핀 조직형태계측에서 신생골 48.08% ± 2.41%, 연조직 27.25% ± 3.18%, 잔존 이식재 24.36% ± 2.91%였으며 막 천공·주요 합병증은 없었음.
자가골 1:1 + 30/70 BCP 조합은 예측 가능한 골고도 획득과 양호한 6개월 신생골을 주지만, 단일군 (n = 10)·6개월 추적·임플란트 토크 미기록 때문에 인과관계와 장기 결과는 제한적임.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Direct clinical, radiographic and histologic evidence for a 1:1 autogenous : 30/70 BCP graft in ≤5 mm residual-height lateral sinus augmentation — a ratio rarely isolated in the literature.
- Reports the complete 6-month tissue budget (new bone 48.08%, soft tissue 27.25%, residual graft 24.36%) alongside the radiographic height gain.
- Documents favorable cell density (osteoblasts in all sections, osteocytes embedded in newly formed bone, capillary angiogenesis) and zero membrane perforation with piezosurgery + PRF membrane sealing.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Single-center, prospective, interventional, single-arm clinical + radiographic + histologic study, 6-month follow-up.
- 45 patients screened; 12 enrolled (18–55 years, <5 mm residual bone on CBCT, non-smokers, no systemic medications); 2 lost to follow-up → 10 analysed.
- Graft: autogenous bone from local surgical site/lateral wall harvested by bone scraper, mixed 1:1 with HA + β-TCP (Osteon II, Dentium; 30/70, particle 1.0–2.0 mm).
- Approach: piezosurgical lateral window (Tatum in-fracture), 360° circumferential elevation + sinus elevators, Valsalva integrity check; PRF membrane placed against Schneiderian membrane and over the lateral window.
- Outcomes: subantral bone height on CBCT (baseline vs 6 months), 6-month trephine (4 mm outer diameter) biopsy histomorphometry (H&E, GIMP image analysis), VAS pain/swelling on days 1/3/5/7.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Subantral bone height: baseline 4.09 ± 0.57 mm → 6 months 12.62 ± 0.99 mm; mean gain 8.53 ± 1.07 mm (95% CI 7.76–9.30; t = 25.17, df = 9, p < 0.001, paired t-test).
- Histomorphometry (6 months): new bone 48.08 ± 2.41%, soft fibrous tissue 27.25 ± 3.18%, residual graft 24.36 ± 2.91%.
- Cell density: osteoblasts + in 30% / ++ in 70%; osteocytes ++ in 80% / +++ in 20%; osteoclasts + in all; minimal inflammatory cells; capillary angiogenesis observed.
- Membrane perforation: none (PRF membrane placed preventively).
- Pain/swelling: mild only (VAS 1–3), resolving by day 7; no major intra- or postoperative complications.
- Implant torque after biopsy noted as "acceptable" but not recorded as a study parameter.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-arm design (no comparator) — cannot attribute the outcome to the 1:1 ratio versus either component alone.
- Small final sample (n = 10; 2 dropouts from 12 enrolled), single center.
- 6-month follow-up only; residual graft at 6 months is not a long-term resorption estimate.
- Implant stability/torque observed but not measured or reported numerically.
- Authors call for multicentric studies with larger samples and longer follow-up.

## 7. Glossary

- **BCP (Biphasic Calcium Phosphate)** — 이상성 인산칼슘; HA (slow-resorbing, space-maintaining) + β-TCP (fast-resorbing, space-for-bone) composite; here 30/70.
- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)** — 혈소판풍부피브린; autologous fibrin matrix used here to seal the Schneiderian membrane and cover the lateral window.
- **Histomorphometry** — 조직형태계측; area-fraction quantification of new bone / soft tissue / residual graft on biopsy sections.
- **Piezosurgery** — 초음파골절삭; ultrasonic bone cutting associated with lower membrane perforation risk.
