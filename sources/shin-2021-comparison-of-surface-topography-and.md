## Why Ingested

Directly addresses a clinical decision point: can the same polishing bur kit be used across 3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, and 5Y-TZP zirconia compositions? Provides surface roughness Ra data, XRD phase-transformation analysis, and FE-SEM grain-size imaging across three yttria compositions (IPS e.max ZirCAD LT/MT/MT multi) under standardized grinding and polishing conditions. Fills a gap noted in the literature — most prior polishing studies used only 3Y-TZP.

## Three-line Summary

A laboratory study compared surface roughness, phase transformation, and topography of 3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, and 5Y-TZP dental zirconia after identical grinding and 3-step polishing using clinical bur kits. No significant differences in surface roughness (Ra) were found between yttrium oxide compositions; fine polishing (P3) achieved lower Ra than glazing in all groups. XRD confirmed no phase transformation; FE-SEM showed grain size increases with yttria content, explaining composition-independent roughness outcomes.

## 세줄요약

3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, 5Y-TZP 지르코니아 디스크를 동일 조건(2 N, 15,000 rpm, 에어 냉각)으로 연삭 후 3단계 연마 버로 순차 연마하여 표면 거칠기(Ra)를 비교한 실험실 연구. 연마 후 세 군 간 Ra에 유의한 차이 없음; 미세 연마(P3)는 모든 군에서 유약 처리(C)보다 낮은 Ra 달성. XRD에서 상변환 없음 확인, FE-SEM에서 이트리아 함량 증가에 따른 결정립 크기 증가 관찰.

## Key Contributions

1. First study to compare surface roughness of 3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, and 5Y-TZP using **clinical grinding and polishing burs** (not mirror-polishing with diamond suspension)
2. Demonstrates polishing bur kits are **composition-agnostic** — same kit applicable regardless of yttria content
3. Fine polishing (P3) achieves clinically acceptable Ra (below 0.2 µm threshold for plaque accumulation)
4. Confirms fine polishing produces lower Ra than layering-technique glazing in all compositions
5. XRD evidence that standardized clinical polishing conditions (2 N, 15,000 rpm, air-cooled) do **not** induce phase transformation

## Methodology

**Materials**: Three IPS e.max ZirCAD disks (Ivoclar Vivadent):
- LT (Low Translucency): 3Y-TZP
- MT (Medium Translucency): 4Y-TZP
- MT multi: 5Y-TZP (incisal zone used, 5Y portion)

**Specimens**: 69 bar-shaped specimens (12.0 × 6.0 × 4.0 mm), sintered at 1500°C; n = 23 per group (60 for surface roughness/XRD, 9 for FE-SEM)

**Custom polishing device**: Low-speed handpiece, 2 N pressure, 15,000 rpm, 0.1 mm/s, 10 swiping motions per step, air-cooled

**Burs**: Pre-Zirpol Dia HP (grinding) + Zirpol Dia HP 3-step kit (Neobiotech)

**Surface treatment stages**:
- C: Glazing only (control)
- G: Post-grinding
- P1, P2, P3: Sequential polishing steps

**Measurements**:
- Ra (µm): Contact surface profiler (Tencor D-300), 3.0 mm distance, 0.1 mm/s, 2.0 µm tip, 15.0 mg pressure
- XRD: Smartlab Rigaku, 40 kV, 30 mA, 10–70° 2θ, 0.02°/step
- FE-SEM: Apero S HiVac, 30,000× after thermal etching at 1,300°C

**Statistics**: Kruskal-Wallis test with Mann-Whitney-U post hoc (α = .05)

## Key Results

**Surface roughness (Ra, µm)**:
- Control (glazed): LT ~0.17, MT 0.19 ± 0.05, MM (not specified individually)
- P2 (medium polish): LT 0.18 ± 0.03, MT 0.19 ± 0.04, MM 0.13 ± 0.04; LT vs MM and MT vs MM significant (P < .001)
- P3 (fine polish): Significantly lower than control in all groups (P < .05)
- **Between-group comparison**: No significant differences among LT, MT, MM groups overall

**Phase transformation (XRD)**: No monoclinic peaks detected in any group at any stage — tetragonal phase maintained

**Grain size (FE-SEM)**: Increases with yttria content (3Y < 4Y < 5Y), consistent with cubic phase incorporation at grain boundaries

**Clinical thresholds**:
- Ra < 0.2 µm: ideal for plaque prevention (all P3 groups achieved)
- Ra < 0.5 µm: undetectable by tongue sensation (P2 meets this)

## Limitations

- Single zirconia brand (IPS e.max ZirCAD) and single bur brand (Neobiotech)
- No aging/hydrothermal degradation of specimens
- Contact profilometer (linear Ra only) — cannot capture full 3D surface topography
- MT multi specimens only from incisal zone (5Y portion); dentin zone (4Y) not tested
- Polishing conditions (pressure, RPM, cooling) may differ from actual chairside use

## Related Work

- Amarante et al.: No significant Ra difference between 3Y-TZP and 5Y-TZP with diamond polishing suspension
- Zhang et al.: No significant Ra difference among 3Y/4Y/5Y-TZP — but used mirror-polishing, not clinical burs
- Vila-Nova et al.: 3Y-TZP vs 5Y-TZP with single-step polishing
- Khayat et al.: 5Y-TZP with 5 polishing systems
- Chavali et al.: Temperature during zirconia polishing (2 N, various RPM); max 34.1°C at study conditions
- Fischer et al.: 1.0/4.5/11 N pressure — no significant Ra difference by AFM/optical profilometer
- Janavula & Mitov: Rough zirconia surfaces induce enamel wear; finely polished ≤ glazed roughness

## Glossary

- **3Y-TZP**: 3 mol% yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal (high strength, lower translucency)
- **4Y-TZP**: 4 mol% yttria composition — increased translucency, more cubic phase
- **5Y-TZP / PSZ**: 5 mol% yttria, predominantly cubic (highest translucency, lower strength)
- **Ra (Arithmetical mean roughness)**: Average absolute deviation of surface profile from mean line (µm)
- **Phase transformation / transformation toughening**: Tetragonal → monoclinic conversion, 3–5% volume expansion → crack arrest mechanism
- **LTD (Low-Temperature Degradation)**: Hydrothermal aging causing tetragonal → monoclinic transformation at the surface
- **XRD**: X-ray diffraction — identifies crystal phases by peak pattern
- **FE-SEM**: Field emission scanning electron microscope — high-resolution surface imaging
- **P1/P2/P3**: Sequential polishing steps (coarse → medium → fine)
