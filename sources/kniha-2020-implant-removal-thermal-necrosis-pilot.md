## Why Ingested

Pilot study directly translating thermal necrosis thresholds to a practical implant-removal (thermo-explantation) application. Provides time-compressed threshold data (10 s instead of 1 min) relevant to understanding how brief intense heat in surgical workflows relates to canonical 1-min animal model thresholds. Porcine jaw anatomy closer to human than rabbit tibia.

→ [[implants/osteotomy-thermal/kniha-2020-implant-removal-thermal-necrosis-pilot]]

## Three-line Summary

Porcine cadaver pilot (Clin Oral Investig 2020; 14 pig heads, 140 drill samples, 10 per temperature group): two tempering pistons (warm 49–56°C/10s; cold 5–1°C/30s) in Camlog 3.8 mm osteotomy sites; histomorphometry + SEM/EDX/TEM; no blood flow (cadaver limitation).

Significant bone matrix degeneration from **51°C for 10 s** (warm) and **5°C for 30 s** (cold); osteocyte condition: damage from 49°C rising, cold 2°C significant; 53°C→decalcification (Ca/P ratio rise) + swollen mitochondria; 56°C→ER dilation; bone-type/jaw-location correlation: weak/non-significant.

Establishes minimum temperature/time parameters for controlled thermo-explantation research; cadaver limitation (no blood flow) means true in vivo thresholds may be higher; pilot for planned rat in vivo study (confirmed published as Kniha 2023).

## 세줄요약

돼지 사체(Porcine Cadaver) 파일럿 연구 (Clin Oral Investig 2020; 돼지머리 14개, 천공 샘플 140개): 온도 그룹당 10개 샘플; 온열(49–56°C/10초) + 냉각(5–1°C/30초) 가열기; 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry) + 주사전자현미경 (Scanning Electron Microscopy, SEM)/에너지분산형X선분석 (Energy-Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy, EDX)/투과전자현미경 (Transmission Electron Microscopy, TEM); 혈류 없음(사체 한계).

**51°C/10초** (온열) 및 **5°C/30초** (냉각)부터 골 기질 변성 (Bone Matrix Degeneration) 유의하게 발생; 49°C부터 골세포 (Osteocyte) 상태 악화(유의하지 않음), 냉각 2°C부터 골세포 유의하게 사멸; 53°C→탈석회화(Ca/P 비율 상승) + 미토콘드리아 팽윤; 56°C→소포체 (Endoplasmic Reticulum) 확장.

제어된 열 임플란트 제거 (Thermo-Explantation) 연구를 위한 최소 온도/시간 파라미터 도출; 사체 한계(혈류 없음) → 실제 in vivo 임계값은 더 높을 가능성; 계획 중인 쥐 in vivo 연구(Kniha 2023으로 발표)의 선행 연구.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Clinical Oral Investigations, Springer
- Year: 2020 (online June 4, 2020)
- Design: In vitro porcine cadaver pilot
- Affiliation: University Hospital RWTH Aachen (same group as Kniha 2020 SR)

## 2. Study Design

### Animal model
- 14 porcine heads (mean age 7 months)
- Collected at slaughterhouse; study within 6 hours
- Osteotomy: Camlog Screw-Line, pilot 2 mm → 3.3 mm → 3.8 mm, depth 8 mm, cooling

### Temperature protocol
| Group | Range | Duration | Sites |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm (main) | 49, 51, 54, 55, 56 °C | 10 s each | 10/group |
| Cold (main) | 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 °C | 30 s each | 10/group |
| Warm (pilot test) | 38–58 °C | 10 s | 1 each |
| Cold (pilot test) | 1–7 °C | 30 s | 1 each |

### Outcome measures
- **Histomorphometry**: osteocyte condition (0–3 scale); matrix degeneration (0–3 scale)
- **SEM/EDX**: surface morphology; Ca/P and Ca/C elemental ratios (at 50, 53, 56°C)
- **TEM**: cellular organelle ultrastructure (mitochondria, ER)
- Tissue fraying depth (μm)

## 3. Key Results

### Warm: matrix degeneration (primary outcome)
- 49, 50°C/10s: no significant matrix degeneration
- **51°C/10s**: significant matrix degeneration (p<0.01) ← key threshold
- 54, 55, 56°C/10s: significant matrix degeneration (p<0.01)

### Warm: osteocyte condition
- 49–56°C/10s: increasing bone damage trend, but NOT statistically significant vs control
- Note: matrix degeneration is a more sensitive endpoint than osteocyte count at 10 s

### Cold: osteocyte condition
- **2°C/30s** vs 1°C/30s: significant difference (p<0.01)
- **1°C/30s** vs control: significant difference

### Cold: matrix degeneration
- **5°C/30s** and all colder (except 3°C): significant vs control
- 3°C/30s: not significant

### EDX elemental analysis
| Temperature | Ca/P ratio | Ca/C ratio |
|---|---|---|
| 50°C | 2.29 (SD 0.27) | 1.81 (SD 0.04) |
| 56°C | 3.86 (SD 2.47) | 2.13 (SD 0.39) |

Rising Ca/P from 53°C = decalcification marker

### TEM ultrastructure
- 53°C: swollen mitochondria, inner cristae structure loss
- 56°C: additionally, ER lumina pronounced dilation (ER stress response)

### Jaw location (upper vs lower)
- Weak, non-significant correlation for all endpoints — no meaningful jaw-type difference

## 4. Limitations
- Cadaver model: no blood flow → blood flow dissipates heat in vivo → true thresholds likely higher
- Short exposure time (10 s) — not directly comparable to canonical 1 min studies
- N=10 per group (pilot scale); limited histological depth of field
- 7-month pigs (adolescent) — bone density may differ from adult clinical scenario
