## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/overviews/abutment-emergence-profile-peri-implant-tissue-overview]] 에서 단기·중기 아부트먼트 높이 데이터를 다뤘으나 7년 이상 장기 RCT 근거가 부재했다. 본 7년 RCT는 1mm vs 3mm 지대주(abutment)의 장기 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 및 주위염 발생에 대한 현존 가장 긴 추적 RCT 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

7-year RCT (n=37 patients, 63 implants) comparing 1-mm vs 3-mm definitive abutments on subcrestally placed implants (1.5 mm subcrestal).

3-mm abutments showed significantly less marginal bone loss from surgery to 7 years (mesial: −0.27 vs −0.42 mm; distal: −0.33 vs −0.35 mm; p<0.001), but peri-implantitis rates (3.2% vs 18.2%) did not reach statistical significance (p=0.158).

3-mm abutments confer a bone-protective advantage primarily in the early remodelling phase; long-term peri-implant disease prevention requires adequate keratinized mucosa and PIMT compliance regardless of abutment height.

## 세줄요약

7년 RCT (37명, 63임플란트): 1.5mm 피개매식된 임플란트에 1mm vs 3mm 지대주 무작위 배정.

3mm 지대주군이 수술 후 7년간 유의하게 낮은 MBL 변화 (근심: −0.27 vs −0.42 mm, p<0.001); 주위염 발생률(3.2% vs 18.2%)은 통계적 유의 미달(p=0.158).

3mm 지대주의 골 보호 효과는 주로 초기 개조기에 집중; 장기 주위염 예방은 각화점막 폭과 PIMT 순응도가 핵심 인자.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Longest RCT on abutment height**: Provides 7-year longitudinal data on 1mm vs 3mm abutments — the longest follow-up RCT to date on this topic.
2. **Confirms short-term advantage of tall abutments extends to 7 years**: Statistically significant MBL difference (p<0.001) from surgery to year 7, though inter-group differences narrow after year 1.
3. **Peri-implantitis trend**: 3-mm group had 3.2% peri-implantitis vs 18.2% in 1-mm group — a clinically meaningful 5.7× difference, but underpowered (p=0.158) due to dropout.
4. **Keratinized mucosa as independent protective factor**: Both groups showed ~1 mm less KM in mucositis cases vs healthy implants (p=0.043–0.010).
5. **Mandibular location risk**: 1-mm abutments in the posterior mandible were 7× more likely to have peri-implant disease than those in the posterior maxilla (p=0.039).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Study design**: Randomised controlled trial (parallel group), 7-year follow-up of an original 1-year RCT cohort.

**Implant system**: Klockner Implant System (Barcelona, Spain); all placed 1.5 mm subcrestally.

**Randomisation**: Patients/implants allocated to 1-mm (control) or 3-mm (test) definitive abutments at time of surgery. Definitive screw-retained restorations connected after 16 weeks.

**Original cohort**: 65 patients, 99 implants at 1-year analysis. At 7 years: 37 patients, 63 implants remained (32 in 1-mm; 31 in 3-mm). Dropout reasons: unwilling to continue (14), death (6), lost to contact (7), moved abroad (3), discontinued clinic visits (5). One implant lost (1-mm group).

**Outcomes**:
- Primary: MBL changes (IS–fBIC) from year 1 to year 7
- Secondary: Peri-implant disease status, PPD, BoP, KM width

**Radiographic method**: Paralleling cone technique with individualised silicone bite registration keys; independent blinded examiner (ICC = 0.99).

**Statistical unit**: Patient. Parametric (Student's t, ANOVA) or non-parametric (Mann-Whitney, Kruskal-Wallis) by Shapiro-Wilk normality; Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons. Significance p<0.05.

**PIMT**: 3–6 month interval based on individual risk; 83.8% of patients were non-compliant or irregularly compliant at 7 years (partly attributed to COVID-19 disruptions).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Marginal Bone Level Changes (IS–fBIC)

| Period | Site | 1-mm group | 3-mm group | p |
|--------|------|-----------|-----------|---|
| Surgery → 7 yr | Mesial | −0.42 ± 0.50 mm | −0.27 ± 1.32 mm | <0.001 |
| Surgery → 7 yr | Distal | −0.35 ± 0.38 mm | −0.33 ± 1.39 mm | <0.001 |
| Year 1 → 7 yr | Mesial | −0.22 ± 0.48 mm | −0.24 ± 1.34 mm | 0.049 |
| Year 1 → 7 yr | Distal | −0.10 ± 0.45 mm | −0.33 ± 1.38 mm | 0.749 (NS) |

### Peri-Implant Disease Status at 7 Years

| Diagnosis | 1-mm group (n=32) | 3-mm group (n=31) |
|-----------|------------------|------------------|
| Healthy | 8 (24.2%) | 7 (22.6%) |
| Peri-implant mucositis | 18 (54.6%) | 23 (74.2%) |
| Peri-implantitis | 6 (18.2%) | 1 (3.2%) |

Overall difference: p=0.158 (NS); after Bonferroni: p=0.102 (NS).

### Other Clinical Findings

- **PPD change (1–7 yr)**: +0.68 ± 1.01 mm (1-mm) vs +0.44 ± 0.86 mm (3-mm); p=0.313 (NS)
- **BoP increase (1–7 yr)**: 1-mm abutments showed significant increase at mesio-buccal (p=0.020) and buccal sites (p=0.038); 3-mm showed significant BoP increase at disto-lingual (p=0.013)
- **KM width**: No significant between-group difference; mucositis cases had ~1.05–1.27 mm less KM than healthy implants (p=0.010–<0.001)
- **Mucosal thickness >2 mm**: Associated with peri-implant mucositis in 3-mm group (p=0.004)
- **Posterior mandible (1-mm group)**: 7× higher risk of peri-implant disease vs posterior maxilla (p=0.039)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

1. **High dropout rate (43%)**: Reduces statistical power; potential attrition bias. From 99 to 63 implants over 7 years.
2. **Underpowered for peri-implantitis**: The 15% absolute difference in peri-implantitis (18.2% vs 3.2%) likely clinically meaningful but failed to reach significance — sample too small.
3. **Radiographic standardisation**: Paralleling cone technique without digital subtraction; some imprecision acknowledged despite ICC=0.99.
4. **High PIMT non-compliance**: 83.8% non-compliant, partly COVID-19 related; confounds disease attribution.
5. **Regression analysis not performed**: Insufficient sample size and multicollinearity precluded multivariable modelling.
6. **Single implant system**: Klockner system; generalisability to other platforms uncertain.

## 6. Related Work

- Muñoz et al. (prior 1-year RCT from same group): established short-term MBL advantage of 3-mm abutments
- Tajti et al. 2024 SR+MA (8 studies): long abutments ≥2 mm → ~0.26 mm less MBL at 1 year
- Lin et al. 2025 SR+MA (93 studies): abutment height ≥2 mm among four prosthetic factors reducing MBL
- Galindo-Moreno et al.: early MBL >0.44 mm predicts progressive bone loss at 18 months
- Windael et al.: implants with early bone loss ≥0.5 mm are 5× more likely to develop peri-implantitis
- Derks et al.: peri-implantitis more likely when restorative margin ≤1.5 mm from bone crest

## 7. Glossary

- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골소실)**: Radiographic bone level change measured from implant shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact
- **IS (Implant Shoulder, 임플란트 어깨부)**: Coronal end of the implant body; reference point for radiographic measurements
- **fBIC (first Bone-to-Implant Contact, 첫 골-임플란트 접촉점)**: Most coronal point where bone contacts the implant surface
- **fBAC (first Bone-to-Abutment Contact, 첫 골-지대주 접촉점)**: When bone contact occurs on the abutment rather than implant shoulder (platform shifting scenario)
- **PIMT (Peri-Implant Maintenance Therapy, 임플란트 유지관리치료)**: Scheduled supportive periodontal/peri-implant care at 3–6 month intervals
- **KM (Keratinized Mucosa, 각화점막)**: Keratinized tissue surrounding implant; width <2 mm associated with higher disease risk
- **BoP (Bleeding on Probing, 탐침 시 출혈)**: Clinical sign of peri-implant tissue inflammation
- **PPD (Probing Pocket Depth, 탐침 깊이)**: Sulcus/pocket depth measured with periodontal probe
- **Subcrestal placement (피개매식)**: Implant placed 1.5 mm below the bone crest in this study
