## Why Ingested
구강상피 (Oral Epithelium)가 피부보다 빠르게 치유되는 분자 기전 탐색. [[periodontics/glim-2013-detrimental-dermal-wound-healing]]의 "구강점막은 섬유아세포 증식이 빠르고 재상피화율(Reepithelialization Rate)이 높다"는 결론에 대한 기전 근거를 TRPV3 채널로 제시.

## One-line Summary
In vivo/in vitro mouse study demonstrating TRPV3 (thermosensitive Ca²⁺ channel) is more highly expressed in oral epithelia than skin and drives temperature-dependent proliferation and wound closure.

## 한줄요약
마우스 생체/시험관 연구: 구강상피에서 피부보다 높게 발현되는 TRPV3 채널이 33°C 이상 온도에서 활성화되어 구강상피세포 증식 및 발치와 봉합을 촉진함.

## Three-line Summary
In vivo/in vitro mouse study (C57BL/6 WT vs. TRPV3KO) identifying TRPV3 thermosensitive Ca²⁺ channel as a driver of oral epithelial proliferation and wound closure.
TRPV3 mRNA higher in oral epithelia than skin; activation above 33°C promotes proliferation via EGFR phosphorylation; TRPV3KO mice showed reduced epithelial proliferation (BrdU) and delayed wound closure in molar extraction model.
Mouse-to-human extrapolation required; molar extraction model may not generalize to gingival/mucosal wounds; exact Ca²⁺ threshold and EGFR linkage need further study.

## 세줄요약
마우스 생체/시험관 연구(C57BL/6 WT vs. TRPV3KO): 열감수성 Ca²⁺ 채널 TRPV3가 구강상피 증식과 창상 봉합의 촉진자임을 규명.
TRPV3 mRNA 구강상피에서 피부보다 높은 발현; 33°C 이상 활성화 → EGFR 인산화 경유 증식 촉진; TRPV3KO 마우스에서 상피 증식(BrdU) 감소 및 구치 발치와 봉합 지연 확인.
인간 외삽 필요; 구치 발치 모델은 치은·점막 창상에 일반화 한계; Ca²⁺ 역치 및 EGFR 연결고리 추가 연구 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First identification of TRPV3 as a proliferation driver specifically in oral (vs. dermal) epithelia
- Quantified proliferation rate difference: TRPV3KO mice show significantly reduced oral epithelial cell proliferation vs. wild-type
- Demonstrated TRPV3 upregulation at wound sites, implicating it in repair response
- Used molar extraction model as in vivo wound healing assay
- TRPV3 activating temperatures (>33°C) physiologically match the oral cavity resting temperature

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: In vivo (C57BL/6 WT vs. TRPV3KO mice) + in vitro cultured oral epithelial cells
- Outcome measures: qRT-PCR for TRPV3 mRNA expression; proliferation rate (immunohistochemistry, BrdU labeling); wound closure (molar extraction model, time to closure); TRPV3 mRNA upregulation at wound site
- Key experiment: Temperature-dependent proliferation assay (33°C threshold activation)
- ErbB receptor (EGFR) phosphorylation measured as downstream signaling readout

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- TRPV3 mRNA higher in murine oral epithelia than skin (qRT-PCR)
- Temperatures >33°C activated TRPV3 → promoted oral epithelial cell proliferation
- TRPV3KO mice: proliferation rate significantly lower than WT (BrdU labeling)
- TRPV3KO mice: oral wound closure delayed vs. WT in molar extraction model
- TRPV3 mRNA upregulated in wounded oral tissue
- Signaling: TRPV3 activation → Ca²⁺ influx → EGFR phosphorylation (proposed pathway)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Mouse model: TRPV3 expression pattern may differ from human oral epithelia
- Molar extraction is a specific wound model; gingival/mucosal wounds may differ
- Exact Ca²⁺ concentration threshold and EGFR pathway linkage require further mechanistic study

## 6. Related Work
- Glim 2013 — oral mucosal scarless healing vs. dermal; higher reepithelialization rate
- Dawes 2003 — baseline oral mucosal surface turnover 2.7 h
- Ohshima 2002 — EGF/EGFR-mediated oral epithelial proliferation and migration (salivary EGF context)

## 7. Glossary
- **TRPV3**: Transient Receptor Potential Vanilloid 3 — thermosensitive, Ca²⁺-permeable ion channel; activated >33°C
- **ErbB/EGFR**: Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor, downstream of TRPV3 activation
- **BrdU labeling**: 5-bromo-2'-deoxyuridine incorporation assay for measuring cell proliferation
