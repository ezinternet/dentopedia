## Why Ingested

이 논문은 [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]] narrative review의 참고문헌으로 발견되었다. Bubalo 2026이 다루는 "autograft vs bone substitute" 비교 테제의 핵심 근거를 직접 검증하는 SR+MA(4 RCT, n=109)로, substitute bone graft(allograft) 단독과 substitute+autograft 병용을 수평골증대(horizontal GBR) 골획득량으로 직접 비교한다 — narrative review 수준의 서술을 정량적 메타분석 데이터로 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 4 RCTs (n=109; 55 test, 54 control) comparing allogeneic bone graft + autogenous bone graft versus allogeneic bone graft alone for horizontal guided bone regeneration (GBR) prior to implant placement.

No statistically significant difference in horizontal bone gain was found between groups, either at 0 mm from the crest (MD: -0.46 mm; 95%CI: -1.03 to 0.11) or at 4-5 mm from the crest (MD: 0.17 mm; 95%CI: -1.08 to 1.42); graft resorption, histology, and complication rates were also similar between groups.

GRADE certainty was very low for both outcomes (risk-of-bias concerns in all included studies, small samples); adding autogenous bone to allograft did not demonstrate additional benefit for horizontal bone gain, while autograft harvesting is known to add donor-site morbidity.

## 세줄요약

수평 유도골재생(GBR) 시행 전 이식재 병용 여부를 비교한 4편의 RCT(n=109; 실험군 55, 대조군 54)를 대상으로 한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 — 동종골 이식재(allogeneic bone graft) + 자가골(autogenous bone graft) 병용군 vs 동종골 단독군.

수평 골획득량에서 치조정 0mm(MD -0.46mm, 95%CI -1.03~0.11), 4-5mm(MD 0.17mm, 95%CI -1.08~1.42) 지점 모두 두 군 간 통계적으로 유의한 차이 없음; 골흡수율·조직학적 소견·합병증률도 유사.

GRADE 근거 확실성은 두 결과 모두 매우 낮음(모든 포함 연구에 비뚤림 위험 우려, 소규모 표본) — 자가골 병용이 수평 골획득에 추가 이득을 보이지 않았고, 자가골 채취는 공여부 이환율(morbidity)만 더할 수 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA specifically comparing horizontal ridge width gain between allogeneic bone graft alone vs allogeneic + autogenous bone graft combination in GBR.
- Quantifies horizontal bone gain at two clinically relevant landmarks (0 mm and 4-5 mm from the crestal alveolar ridge), each with its own meta-analysis and membrane-type subgroup analysis (collagen membrane vs acellular dermal matrix).
- Applies GRADE to rate certainty of evidence — both outcomes rated very low certainty, an important caveat for clinical extrapolation.
- Reports secondary outcomes (graft resorption, histology, complications) that are frequently omitted from narrower reviews.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PRISMA-compliant SR+MA; protocol pre-registered on INPLASY.
- PICOS: Population = patients with horizontal atrophic ridge needing GBR before implant placement; Intervention = GBR with particulate/osteoconductive materials; Comparison = substitute (allogeneic) bone graft + autogenous bone graft vs substitute bone graft alone; Outcome = horizontal bone gain (primary), graft resorption/histology/PROMs (secondary); Study design = RCTs only.
- Databases: MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, searched to June 2021, plus grey literature (System for Information on Grey Literature in Europe, NY Academy of Medicine Grey Literature Report) and hand searches of key implant/periodontal journals.
- Dual independent screening/extraction with third-reviewer arbitration; Cohen's kappa reported (≥0.85, excellent agreement).
- Risk of bias: Cochrane RoB 2 tool (five domains, "assignment to intervention"/intention-to-treat effect).
- Statistics: RevMan 5.3, random-effects model (due to methodological heterogeneity), mean difference (MD) with 95% CI, Cochran's Q (threshold p=0.1) and I² (>50% = high heterogeneity) for heterogeneity; GRADE for certainty of evidence.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 541 records identified → 220 after dedup → 5 full texts assessed → 4 RCTs included (2010-2020; USA×2, Israel×1, Iran×1); n=109 (55 test / 54 control), ages 21-73.
- All 4 studies used freeze-dried bone allograft as the "substitute" material; comparison reduces to allograft+autograft vs allograft alone.
- Autograft:allograft ratios used: 50:50 (2 studies), 30:70 (1 study).
- Horizontal bone gain at 0 mm: MD -0.46 mm (95%CI -1.03 to 0.11), I²=22% (low heterogeneity) — not significant.
- Horizontal bone gain at 4-5 mm (only 2 studies): MD 0.17 mm (95%CI -1.08 to 1.42), I²=57% (moderate/high heterogeneity) — not significant.
- Subgroup (membrane type) analyses showed no significant differences in either meta-analysis for collagen membrane or acellular dermal matrix.
- Graft resorption: combination group numerically higher in both studies reporting it (e.g., 16.91% vs 11.12%; 30% vs 25%), not statistically significant.
- Histology (2 studies): no significant between-group differences in vital bone/new bone percentages.
- No PROMs reported in any included trial.
- All studies had risk-of-bias concerns; GRADE certainty very low for both primary meta-analysis outcomes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 4 RCTs available — very small evidence base; authors explicitly flag "low number of RCTs."
- Small defect sizes only (patients missing 2-4 teeth); not generalizable to larger/more complex ridge defects.
- Majority of included studies at high risk of bias; blinding of participants inherently impossible due to donor-site awareness in the combination arm.
- Only one trial prospectively registered (and registered after study completion).
- Authors call for further well-designed RCTs to confirm the null finding.

## 6. Related Work

- Cross-referenced against a separate RCT comparing GBR with vs without autogenous block grafting: similar non-significant bone-width outcome but the autogenous block group showed significantly higher sensory disturbance (p=0.02) and hematoma (p=0.002) rates — i.e., real morbidity cost without demonstrated benefit.
- References a split-mouth study on DPBB:autogenous bone ratios (90:10 vs 60:40) showing no significant difference in gain (5.7 vs 6.2 mm).
- Referenced by [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]] as a source for the autograft-vs-substitute comparison thesis.

## 7. Glossary

- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration)**: barrier-membrane technique to regenerate bone volume prior to/at implant placement.
- **Allogeneic bone graft (allograft)**: bone graft material from a human donor (non-self), here freeze-dried bone allograft (FDBA).
- **Autogenous bone graft (autograft)**: bone harvested from the patient's own body (e.g., ramus, symphysis).
- **MD (Mean Difference)**: pooled effect-size measure used in the meta-analysis for continuous bone-gain outcomes.
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation — framework for rating certainty of evidence (very low/low/moderate/high).
- **RoB 2**: Cochrane Risk of Bias tool version 2, for assessing bias in randomized trials.
- **Acellular dermal matrix**: a resorbable, collagen-based membrane derived from processed human/animal dermis, used as a GBR barrier.
