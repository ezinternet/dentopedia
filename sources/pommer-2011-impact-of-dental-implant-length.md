## Three-line Summary

Meta-analysis of 54 prospective observational publications (19,083 implants), several with author-supplied data, testing whether short implants (≥7 mm, <10 mm) fail more than longer ones (≥10 mm) within the first year of prosthetic loading.

No length effect was found in the mandible; in the maxilla, short machined-surface implants had markedly higher failure anteriorly (OR 5.4) and posteriorly (OR 3.4), and short rough-surface implants showed elevated failure only in the anterior maxilla.

Diameter and denture type did not modify the association; short rough-surface implants remain a viable augmentation-reducing option in the posterior maxilla, where the length-survival relationship is weaker.

## 세줄요약

54편의 전향적 관찰연구(19,083개 임플란트)를 메타분석하여 단단형(7–9 mm)이 장형(≥10 mm)보다 보철 하중 첫 해에 더 많이 실패하는지 검증했으며, 일부 저자 데이터 보완이 포함됐다.

하악에서는 길이 효과가 없었으나, 상악에서는 기계 가공면 단단형이 전치부(OR 5.4)·구치부(OR 3.4) 모두 유의하게 높은 실패율을 보였고, 거친 표면 단단형은 전치부 상악에서만 실패 증가를 보였다.

직경과 보철 유형은 효과를 변경하지 않았으며, 거친 표면 단단형은 특히 상악 구치부에서 골 증대술 대안으로 유효하다고 결론지었다.

## One-line Summary

Meta-analysis of 54 prospective observational publications (19,083 implants): short implants (≥7 mm) did not fail more often than ≥10 mm implants in the mandible within the first year of loading, but short machined implants failed more in the anterior (OR 5.4) and posterior (OR 3.4) maxilla (abstract only).

## 한줄요약

전향적 관찰연구 54편(임플란트 19,083개) 메타분석에서 짧은 임플란트(7 mm 이상)는 하악에서는 부하 첫 1년 실패가 10 mm 이상과 차이가 없었지만, 상악에서는 기계가공 표면의 짧은 임플란트가 전치부 OR 5.4, 구치부 OR 3.4로 더 많이 실패했다(초록만).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Site- and surface-specific length effect: harm concentrated in the maxilla and in machined surfaces.
- Null length effect in the mandible.
- Frames short implants as an alternative to augmentation, which Krisam 2019 cites when reporting its own short-implant OR 5.8.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Systematic electronic and hand search; inclusion of prospective observational trials comparing short (≥7 mm) and longer (≥10 mm) implants; additional data from authors; 54 publications, 19,083 implants. Outcome: failure within the first year of prosthetic loading. Meta-analytic odds ratios by jaw, site and surface.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Mandible: no significant length effect. Maxilla, machined surface: anterior OR 5.4, posterior OR 3.4. Rough surface: increased failure in anterior maxilla only. No influence of diameter or denture type on short-implant failure (CIs not in abstract).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract only. Observational source studies; heterogeneous definitions; short defined as ≥7 mm, so ultra-short implants are not addressed; outcome window extends into the first loading year.
