## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251042692; MEDLINE/Embase/Scopus to May 2025; 8 studies = 3 RCTs + 5 prospective cohorts; 181 patients, 189 short implants <8 mm) on short implants supporting overdentures — abstract-only ingest.

At 1 year, implant survival was 97.2% (95% CI 93.1-98.9%; I2 = 0%); pooled MBL 0.53 mm (95% CI -0.20 to 1.27; I2 = 95.8%); 45.7% of patients had at least one maintenance event (matrix/insert replacement 24.7%, relining 14.0%), overdenture fracture 3.1%.

Short implants under overdentures show high short-term survival and manageable maintenance, but only survival is moderate-certainty (GRADE); MBL, complications and PROMs are low to very low certainty and follow-up is short.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pools survival, marginal bone level change (MBL), prosthetic complications and patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) for short (<8 mm) implants supporting overdentures.
- 1-year survival 97.2% (95% CI 93.1-98.9%; I2 = 0%).
- Quantifies prosthetic maintenance: 45.7% of patients with at least one event.
- GRADE grading: moderate certainty for survival only.

## 3. Methodology

- PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420251042692; databases MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus searched to May 2025.
- Eligibility: RCTs and prospective clinical studies of short implants (<8 mm) supporting overdentures reporting at least one of survival, MBL, prosthetic complications, PROMs.
- Risk of bias: RoB 2 (RCTs), ROBINS-I V2 (cohorts).
- Synthesis: logit transformation with Hartung-Knapp random-effects model (survival); inverse-variance random-effects (MBL); random-effects generalized linear mixed models (RE-GLMM) for complications; PROMs narrative.
- Included: 8 studies (3 RCTs, 5 prospective cohorts), 181 patients, 189 short implants.

## 4. Key Results

- **Survival (1 y)**: 97.2% (95% CI 93.1-98.9%; I2 = 0%); GRADE moderate.
- **MBL (1 y)**: pooled mean 0.53 mm (95% CI -0.20 to 1.27; I2 = 95.8%); GRADE low to very low.
- **Prosthetic complications**: predominantly minor; 45.7% of patients had at least one maintenance event; matrix/insert replacement 24.7%, relining 14.0%; overdenture fracture 3.1%.
- **PROMs**: consistently high satisfaction, heterogeneous reporting (narrative).

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract-only record: study-level data, tables, attachment and arch details are unavailable.
- Short-term evidence (1-year pooled estimates); long-term predictability not established.
- Very high MBL heterogeneity (I2 = 95.8%) with a CI crossing zero; imprecision.
- Low to very low GRADE certainty for MBL, complications and PROMs; small sample (181 patients).
- Heterogeneous PROM reporting; authors call for long-term, adequately powered trials with standardized outcomes.

## 7. Glossary

- **Short implant**: implant shorter than 8 mm (as defined in this review).
- **Implant overdenture**: removable denture retained or supported by implants via attachments.
- **MBL**: marginal bone level change around the implant.
- **RE-GLMM**: random-effects generalized linear mixed model.
- **Hartung-Knapp**: random-effects variance adjustment for small numbers of studies.
- **PROMs**: patient-reported outcome measures.
- **GRADE**: certainty-of-evidence rating system.
