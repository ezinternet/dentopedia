## Why Ingested

[[implants/isq/kastel-2019-smartpeg-torque-isq-rfa]]은 스마트팩 조임 토크가 ISQ에 영향 없다고 결론냈으나, 본 논문은 수동 조임이 정확한 토크 렌치(6 Ncm) 대비 유의하게 낮은 ISQ를 산출함(p<.001)으로써 상반된 증거를 제시 — SmartPeg 조임 표준화 논쟁의 핵심 문헌.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro polyurethane bone block study (56 implants, 7 types, D1–D4 bone) comparing four SmartPeg tightening methods: hand, plastic mount, SafeMount, and calibrated 6 Ncm torque wrench.

Hand tightening produced statistically significantly lower ISQ than the calibrated torque control (coef −2.05, p<.001); SafeMount and standard plastic mount showed no significant difference from the control.

Bone density accounted for 36% of ISQ variance — the dominant factor — while operator contributed only 6%; ISQ reliability was very poor in D4 bone regardless of tightening method.

## 세줄요약

체외 폴리우레탄 뼈 블록 연구 (임플란트 7종, 56개, D1–D4 골질): 스마트팩 조임 방법 4종 비교 — 수동, 플라스틱 마운트, SafeMount, 정확한 토크 렌치 6 Ncm.

수동 조임이 정확한 토크 렌치 대비 유의하게 낮은 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 산출 (계수 −2.05, p<.001); SafeMount와 표준 플라스틱 마운트는 대조군과 유의차 없음.

골밀도가 ISQ 변이의 36%를 차지해 가장 큰 영향 인자였고, 술자는 6%에 불과; D4 골질에서는 조임 방법에 관계없이 측정 신뢰도가 매우 낮음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First study to compare Osstell SafeMount against standard mount and calibrated torque wrench across multiple implant systems and bone densities
- Demonstrated hand tightening yields significantly lower ISQ than 6 Ncm calibrated torque (p<.001)
- Showed bone density accounts for 36% of ISQ variance — far exceeding operator (6%) or implant design (11%)
- Revealed very poor inter-operator agreement in D4 bone (ICC <0.24) regardless of tightening method
- SafeMount offered marginal ICC improvement over standard mount but not statistically significant

## 3. Methodology
- **Design**: In vitro, 8 polyurethane bone blocks (BoneModels™), D1–D2–D3–D4 densities
- **Implants**: 7 types — Straumann Standard/Standard Plus/Tapered Effect/Wide, Zimmer Biomet BNST (internal hex) and BOET (external hex), Dentsply Ankylos C/X
- **Total**: 56 implants (7 per block × 8 blocks), 1792 ISQ measurements
- **Tightening methods**: (1) hand with gauze, (2) standard plastic SmartPeg mount, (3) SafeMount (click at 4–4.5 Ncm), (4) calibrated Tohnichi torque wrench at 6 Ncm
- **RFA devices**: Osstell IDx + Osstell ISQ (both used for each measurement)
- **Operators**: 2 male operators, each performed all measurements
- **Statistics**: ICC (Koo & Li classification), linear mixed effects regression (R, lme4), Kruskal–Wallis + Dunn's post hoc

## 4. Key Results

**Mean ISQ by tightening method:**

| Method | Mean ISQ ± SD |
|---|---|
| Hand tightening (gauze) | 64.17 ± 12.99 |
| Plastic SmartPeg mount | 65.89 ± 13.57 |
| SafeMount | 66.04 ± 14.33 |
| Calibrated torque (6 Ncm) | 66.22 ± 14.12 |

**Mixed model — tightening method vs control (6 Ncm):**

| Method | Coef | 95% CI | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand tightening | −2.05 | (−2.89, −1.21) | <.001 |
| Plastic mount | −0.33 | (−1.17, 0.51) | .444 |
| SafeMount | −0.19 | (−1.03, 0.66) | .668 |

**ICC by bone density (operator agreement):**

| Bone | Hand | Plastic | SafeMount | 6 Ncm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 0.830 | 0.811 | 0.977 | 0.944 |
| D2 | 0.934 | 0.972 | 0.972 | 0.983 |
| D3 | 0.791 | 0.767 | 0.762 | 0.803 |
| D4 | 0.112 | 0.192 | 0.238 | 0.039 |

**Variance decomposition:** Bone density 36.3%, implant 11.2%, operator 6.1%, residual 36.0%.

## 5. Limitations
- Only 2 operators (both male) — broader operator pool would improve generalizability
- Statistical power did not allow meaningful comparison between individual implant designs
- In vitro polyurethane model — may not fully replicate clinical soft tissue and healing conditions
- 6 Ncm control value chosen to bridge Kästel (2–11 Ncm) and Geckili (5–8 Ncm) ranges

## 7. Glossary
- **SmartPeg**: Magnetic transducer screwed into implant for RFA measurement
- **SafeMount**: Osstell plastic mount with torque-limiting click mechanism (4–4.5 Ncm)
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient, 0–100 nonlinear scale from RFA
- **ICC**: Intraclass Correlation Coefficient — agreement measure between operators/devices
- **BoneModels™**: Standardized polyurethane foam blocks simulating D1–D4 bone densities
