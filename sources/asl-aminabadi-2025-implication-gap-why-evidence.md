## Why Ingested

This editorial names the **implication gap** — the distance between published evidence-based dentistry (EBD) guidelines and their real-world adoption — and reframes it as a system-level failure rather than clinician negligence. It is the counterweight to the appraisal-side pages in this wiki: [[wiki/evidence-appraisal/kiriakou-2014-evidence-based-dentistry-skills-interpret]] teaches *how to appraise and apply* evidence, while this piece explains *why the apply step breaks down* (economic mismatch, guideline complexity, patient variability, cognitive overload). It also feeds the decision-variability synthesis at [[wiki/overviews/treatment-planning-decision-variability-overview]], where unexplained practice variation is a recurring theme.

## Three-line Summary

Two-page editorial (J Dent Res Dent Clin Dent Prospects 2025;19(4):213-214) arguing that poor adherence to evidence-based dental guidelines stems not from clinician negligence or lack of awareness but from systemic, structural, cognitive, and patient-related barriers.

It identifies four barrier classes — economic mismatch (unreimbursed time-intensive preventive care), guideline complexity/usability (dense technical documents without decision trees), real-world patient variability (comorbidities, socioeconomic constraints vs "ideal" trial patients), and cognitive overload/guideline fatigue (decision fatigue).

Proposed fixes are system-level: simplified summaries and digital decision-support embedded in electronic dental records, reimbursement models aligned with evidence-based practices, flexible implementation pathways, and clinician feedback loops into guideline revision.

## 세줄요약

2페이지 사설(JODDD 2025;19(4):213-214): 근거기반 치의학 (Evidence-Based Dentistry, EBD) 지침 준수율이 낮은 원인가 임상의 무관심·인식 부족이 아니라 경제적 미스매치·지침 복잡성·환자 변이성·인지과부하 (Cognitive Overload) 같은 체계적·구조적 장벽에 있다고 재프레임.

네 가지 장벽: (1) 예방 처치·교육·진단이 수가로 보전되지 않는 경제 구조, (2) 의사결정 나무·체크리스트 없는 밀도 높은 기술적 문서, (3) 통제된 시험의 "이상적 환자"와 전신질환·사회경제적 제약을 가진 실제 환자의 괴리, (4) 끝없이 누적되는 지침이 유발하는 의사결정 피로 (Decision Fatigue).

해법은 개인 규율이 아닌 시스템 차원: 전자치과기록 (Electronic Dental Record) 내 의사결정지원 삽입, 근거기반 진료에 정렬한 수가 모델, 유연한 실행 경로, 임상의 피드백을 지침 개정으로 되먹임.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Reframes the guideline-adherence problem: attributes the implementation gap to systemic/structural/cognitive/patient factors rather than clinician motivation or awareness.
- Taxonomizes four barrier classes with concrete dental examples (caries risk assessment and sealants skipped under volume pressure; infection-control protocols without workflow tools; periodontal follow-up lost to transportation/financial barriers).
- Proposes a system-level intervention menu: structured summaries/flowcharts, electronic dental record decision-support prompts, reimbursement alignment, flexible pathways for patient variability, embedded continuing professional development, and clinician feedback systems.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Narrative editorial commentary — no new empirical data; synthesizes implementation-science literature (7 references spanning guideline-development strategy, implementation overviews of systematic reviews, sealant-implementation barriers, and a 2025 systematic review of decision fatigue in healthcare professionals).
- Argument structure: Introduction (misattribution critique) → four barrier sections (Economic Mismatch, Complexity of Guidelines, Patient Variability, Cognitive Overload) → system-level remedies → Conclusion.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- No empirical results — this is a position piece.
- Central claim: adherence failures are feasibility failures ("economic necessity", not "clinical negligence"); shifting from individual compliance to system-level support is the stated route to population-level oral health gains.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Editorial without data: no quantification of how much each barrier contributes to non-adherence.
- Single-country authorship (Iran); reimbursement arguments may transfer imperfectly across health systems.
- Self-declared competing interest (authors are JODDD editors) — disclosed, no other conflicts.
- Future work implied: feedback systems gathering clinician-reported guideline friction to inform revisions; digital decision-support design; payment-model experiments aligned with evidence-based care.

## 7. Glossary
- Implication gap: the distance between guideline publication and consistent clinical adoption.
- Decision fatigue: degradation of decision quality under accumulated cognitive load from excessive information/choices.
- Guideline fatigue: selective attention or inadvertent omission caused by the sheer accumulation of concurrent practice guidelines.
- EBD: Evidence-Based Dentistry — integration of best research evidence with clinical expertise and patient values.
