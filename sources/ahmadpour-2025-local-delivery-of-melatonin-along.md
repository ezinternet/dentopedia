## Why Ingested

The wiki already held melatonin twice, but on the **periodontal/soft-tissue** axis ([[periodontics/abusamak-2026-melatonin-nspt-adjunct-sr-ma]], [[periodontics/host-modulation/angelopoulou-2026-melatonin-adjunct-nspt-t2dm-sr-ma]] — both score PPD/CAL/HbA1c as an NSPT adjunct), never on **bone formation**. This review fills the missing indication — melatonin delivered locally *together with a bone graft*, scored on osteogenesis in surgical bone defects and peri-implant bone response — and extends the "local pharmacologic adjunct to bone grafting" axis that [[bone-regeneration/tale-2026-simvastatin-guided-bone-regeneration-sr]] established for simvastatin, by supplying the first systematic statement of the pathway set attributed to melatonin in this setting (Wnt, Nrf2/HO-1, PI3K/AKT, MAPK, NFkB, SIRT/SOD, BMP/Smad, PERK/ATF4).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA-guided PubMed search, January 1970–December 2023) of 24 articles covering animal and human in vivo studies plus in vitro studies on local delivery of melatonin together with bone grafts; abstract-only ingest.

91.7% of the included studies reported better outcomes than their control groups, and the proposed mechanism is activation of osteoblastic-differentiation signaling — Wnt, Nrf2/HO-1, PI3K/AKT, MAPK, NFkB, SIRT/SOD, BMP/Smad, PERK/ATF4.

The authors conclude that locally administered melatonin may stimulate bone healing in surgical bone defects and enhance peri-implant bone response, but the abstract reports no pooled effect size, no risk-of-bias appraisal and no per-study outcome data — hypothesis-supporting rather than practice-changing.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Scopes a distinct indication the wiki did not hold: **melatonin as a local agent delivered with a bone graft**, evaluated on osteogenesis rather than on periodontal indices or systemic metabolic markers.
- Deliberately spans **animal/human in vivo and in vitro studies**, so the synthesis links clinical-adjacent bone-healing outcomes to the molecular cascade thought to underlie them.
- Provides the pathway map attributed to melatonin in this setting — Wnt, Nrf2/HO-1, PI3K/AKT, MAPK, NFkB, SIRT/SOD, BMP/Smad, PERK/ATF4 — i.e. a stated mechanistic axis rather than an implicit one.
- Uses a **vote-counting synthesis** (share of included studies favouring the intervention) rather than pooled effect estimates, which is legible at abstract level but leaves effect magnitude, heterogeneity and certainty unquantified.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: systematic literature search conducted "following PRISMA instructions".
- **Database / window**: PubMed, January 1970 – December 2023.
- **Search construction**: a structured search to identify both **in vivo and in vitro** studies.
- **Screening**: articles screened on the basis of title and abstract; **24 articles** included.
- **Included evidence types**: animal in vivo studies, human in vivo studies, and in vitro studies.
- **Not reported in the abstract** (full text not retrieved): additional databases or grey literature, language restrictions, protocol registration (e.g. PROSPERO), number of screening reviewers, risk-of-bias tool, and whether any quantitative pooling was planned or performed.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingest** — every statement on this page is bounded by the published abstract; the study tables, search string, and appraisal detail could not be checked.
- **No quantitative synthesis** — 91.7% is a share of studies, not an effect magnitude; without pooled estimates, confidence intervals or a heterogeneity metric, the clinical weight of the signal cannot be graded from this source.
- **Single database** — PubMed only over a 53-year window; no Embase/Cochrane/grey-literature capture, so relevant non-indexed animal work may be missing.
- **Evidence mix** — animal and in vitro studies are included alongside human studies; the abstract does not give the split, so the share of clinically applicable human evidence is unknown.
- **No protocol standardisation reported** — melatonin dose, delivery carrier (with or without graft), graft type and healing timepoint are not stated, so the "local delivery" intervention is not reproducible from this source.
- **Safety not addressed** — the abstract reports no adverse-event data for locally delivered melatonin in bone defects or at implant sites.
- **Future work implied**: standardized, adequately powered human trials of melatonin-plus-graft protocols (dose, carrier, timepoint), with quantitative pooling and formal risk-of-bias/GRADE appraisal.

## 7. Glossary

- **Osteogenesis (골형성)**: formation of new bone tissue by osteoblasts.
- **Osteoinduction (골유도)**: induction of progenitor cells into an osteoblastic phenotype by growth-factor signalling — the property that processed xenografts/allografts largely lack and that melatonin is proposed to supply.
- **Peri-implant bone response (임플란트 주위 골 반응)**: bone formation and remodelling in the peri-implant compartment, including at bone defects around an implant.
- **Surgical bone defect (수술적 골결손)**: a surgically created intraosseous cavity managed with graft material.
- **Wnt / β-catenin**: canonical osteoblast-differentiation and bone-formation pathway.
- **Nrf2/HO-1**: antioxidant-response axis (nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2 / heme oxygenase-1).
- **PI3K/AKT, MAPK**: prosurvival and proliferative signalling cascades that support osteoblast phenotype.
- **NFkB**: inflammatory transcription factor; its modulation is the anti-inflammatory face of melatonin's proposed action.
- **SIRT/SOD**: sirtuin–superoxide dismutase axis (cellular redox homeostasis).
- **BMP/Smad**: bone morphogenetic protein signalling, the canonical osteoinductive cascade.
- **PERK/ATF4**: unfolded-protein-response axis implicated in osteoblast differentiation and collagen synthesis.
- **PRISMA**: Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses.
- **Vote counting**: synthesis method that reports the proportion of included studies favouring an intervention, without pooling effect estimates.
