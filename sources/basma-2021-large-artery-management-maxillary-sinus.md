## Why Ingested

Requested to fill the clinical gap in PSAA intraoperative hemorrhage management during lateral window SFE; this is the only PMC-indexed case report specifically documenting intraoperative displacement of a large PSA branch using piezoelectric surgery, complementing the anatomy data in [[sinus-lift/lateral/gulec-2025-psaa-cbct-maxillary-sinus-anatomy]] and [[sinus-lift/lateral/urechescu-2026-psaa-localization-lateral-window-sinus]].

## Three-line Summary

Case report of lateral window maxillary sinus floor augmentation in a healthy 58-year-old female with a large PSA branch visible on preoperative CT within the lateral sinus wall bony canal.
The artery was elevated intact along with the Schneiderian membrane using a piezoelectric device without hemorrhage or complication; postoperative healing was uneventful.
When a large-diameter PSAA branch is identified preoperatively, piezoelectric membrane elevation with intact artery displacement is a viable technique to avoid laceration and hemorrhagic complications.

## 세줄요약

CT에서 측벽 골관 내 대직경 후상치조동맥 (Posterior Superior Alveolar Artery, PSAA) 분지가 확인된 58세 여성의 측방 상악동 골이식 증례보고.
피에조전기 수술 기구 (piezoelectric device) 로 슈나이더막 (Schneiderian membrane) 과 함께 동맥을 출혈 없이 완전 거상; 술후 경과 양호.
대직경 PSAA 분지는 절제 대신 피에조 거상으로 완전 보존하는 전략이 출혈 합병증을 최소화한다.

## Key Contributions

- Clinical documentation of intact PSA branch displacement with Schneiderian membrane elevation (not laceration/hemostasis)
- Piezoelectric device as the preferred instrument when large PSAA branch is identified on preoperative CT
- Demonstrates that "elevation with membrane" strategy can convert a hemorrhage risk into a non-event
- Supports preoperative CT identification as essential for large-artery cases (management strategy shifts based on preoperative detection)
- Published management pathway: Identify on CT → plan piezo elevation → elevate artery intact → no hemorrhage management required

## Methodology

- **Design**: Case report (single patient)
- **Patient**: 58-year-old healthy female, planned for implant-supported fixed prosthesis
- **Preoperative assessment**: CT scan revealing bony canal with identifiable PSA branch on lateral wall
- **Technique**: Standard lateral window SFE using piezoelectric device system
- **Intervention**: PSA branch elevated intact with Schneiderian membrane (not cauterized/ligated)
- **Outcome**: No hemorrhagic complication, uneventful healing

## Key Results

- Preoperative CT: PSA branch clearly visible within bony canal of lateral maxillary sinus wall
- Intraoperative: PSA branch elevated en bloc with Schneiderian membrane using piezoelectric device
- Complication: None (no hemorrhage, no membrane perforation)
- Postoperative: Uneventful healing, graft stability maintained

## Limitations

- Single case report (n=1); no hemorrhage actually occurred, so actual hemostatic efficacy cannot be assessed
- No control or comparison arm
- Artery diameter not reported numerically
- Long-term implant outcomes not reported

## Glossary

- **PSA branch (후상치조동맥 분지)**: Branch of the posterior superior alveolar artery encountered in the lateral wall of the maxillary sinus
- **Piezoelectric device (피에조전기 기구)**: Ultrasonic surgical instrument with selective hard-tissue cutting capability; vibration frequency damages bone but not soft tissue/vessels, allowing intact membrane and vessel elevation
- **Elevation with membrane strategy**: Technique where a recognized large vessel is not cauterized but intentionally elevated en bloc with the Schneiderian membrane to avoid laceration
