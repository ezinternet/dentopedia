## Three-line Summary

Retrospective radiographic study (Yonsei University; 167 posterior single-tooth restorations with CAD-CAM abutments — 84 placed >1 mm subcrestally in 76 patients, 83 equicrestal in 73) comparing abutment profile angle measured from three different references against marginal bone loss (MBL).

Angle–MBL correlations were significant at 0–1 and 1–2 mm for the equicrestal platform-referenced (EI) and subcrestal bone-crest-referenced (SB) groups (P<.05) but not at 2–3 mm; in the subcrestal platform-referenced (SI) group they were significant at all three ranges, with generally smaller cut-off angles.

Subcrestal implants show a deeper angle–bone-loss coupling when the angle is referenced from the implant platform; abstract-only, so the actual cut-off values and effect sizes are not verifiable here.

## 세줄요약

후향적 방사선학 연구(연세대; CAD-CAM 지대주 후방부 단일치 보철 167개 — 골하 1 mm 초과 식립 84개/76명, 골정 식립 83개/73명): 기준점 세 가지(골정군 플랫폼, 골하군 플랫폼, 골하군 인접 골정)에서 잰 프로파일 각도(Profile Angle, PA)와 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)의 관계를 비교.

골정군(플랫폼 기준)과 골하군(골정 기준)은 0–1·1–2 mm에서 각도–MBL 상관이 유의(P<.05)했으나 2–3 mm는 미유의; 골하군을 플랫폼 기준으로 잰 경우에는 0–1·1–2·2–3 mm 모두 유의했고 역치도 전반적으로 더 작았다.

골하 식립에서는 플랫폼 기준 각도가 더 깊은 구간까지 골소실과 연관 — 초록 전용 기록이라 실제 역치값·효과크기는 확인 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Compares **three angle-reference schemes** for the same restorative-contour question: platform-referenced in equicrestal implants (EI), platform-referenced in subcrestal implants (SI), and adjacent-bone-crest-referenced in subcrestal implants (SB).
- Shows the depth range of significant angle–MBL correlation depends on the reference and on implant vertical position: **EI and SB stop at 2 mm; SI extends through 3 mm**.
- Uses **C-index analysis** to derive depth-specific cut-off angles for 0–1, 1–2 and 2–3 mm ranges (numerical cut-offs not present in the abstract).
- Isolates subcrestal placement as a modifier of how restoration contour relates to crestal bone loss.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective study of electronic medical records of patients in regular maintenance.
- **Sample**: 167 posterior single-tooth implant restorations with CAD-CAM abutments; 84 implants >1 mm subcrestal (76 patients), 83 equicrestal (73 patients).
- **Exposure**: profile angle (PA) measured with three references (EI, SI, SB), evaluated over 0–1, 1–2 and 2–3 mm distance ranges.
- **Outcome**: MBL changes from the implant shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact at different time points.
- **Statistics**: Pearson correlation and linear regression; C-index for PA cut-offs; independent t tests at those cut-offs (α=.05).
- **Note**: follow-up duration, adjustment variables and the numerical cut-offs are not given in the retrieved abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | Angle reference | 0–1 mm | 1–2 mm | 2–3 mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EI (equicrestal) | implant platform | significant (P<.05) | significant (P<.05) | not significant (P>.05) |
| SB (subcrestal) | adjacent bone crest | significant (P<.05) | significant (P<.05) | not significant (P>.05) |
| SI (subcrestal) | implant platform | significant (P<.05) | significant (P<.05) | significant (P<.05) |

- SI groups generally showed **smaller cut-off values** than EI or SB at corresponding distance ranges (exact values absent from the abstract).
- Authors' conclusion: within 2 mm of the bone crest, PA correlates with MBL in both subcrestal and equicrestal implants; with platform-referenced angles in subcrestal implants, the correlation extends across 0–3 mm.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingest**: cut-off angles, effect sizes, follow-up length, radiographic method details and confounder adjustment cannot be verified.
- Retrospective record-based design — association only; selection and residual confounding possible.
- No sample-size/power statement in the abstract; the 84 vs 83 split is by implant, with overlapping patient counts not fully described.
- The clinical meaning of smaller cut-off angles in the SI group cannot be interpreted numerically without the full text.

## 7. Glossary

- **Profile Angle (PA)**: angle of the abutment/restoration contour measured from a stated reference (implant platform or adjacent bone crest) over defined distance ranges.
- **Subcrestal / equicrestal placement**: implant platform positioned below / at the adjacent alveolar crest; the principal stratifier here.
- **Marginal bone loss (MBL)**: distance from implant shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact, the outcome.
- **C-index**: statistic used here to identify candidate PA cut-off points for each distance range.
- **CAD-CAM abutment**: computer-aided design/manufacturing abutment — the restoration type common to all implants in this sample.
