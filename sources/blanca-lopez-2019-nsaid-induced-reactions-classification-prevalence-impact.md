## Why Ingested

The wiki's `drug/analgesics` coverage was almost entirely efficacy and dosing —
[[wiki/drug/analgesics/kotowska-rodziewicz-2023-nsaids-role-dentistry-review]] and
[[wiki/drug/analgesics/becker-2010-pain-management-part-1]] give the mechanistic and
prescribing picture, but nothing supplied a framework for reading a patient's reported
"painkiller allergy." This narrative review from the Spanish RIRAAF / Málaga group (the
group that established NSAID-induced urticaria/angioedema, NIUA, as the most prevalent
NSAID-hypersensitivity entity) provides the cross-intolerant (CI) vs selective-responder
(SR) mental model, the ENDA/EAACI 5-group classification, and the epidemiology point that
NSAIDs have now overtaken beta-lactam antibiotics as the single most common cause of
hypersensitivity drug reactions — the mirror-image of the antibiotic adverse-reaction
picture in [[wiki/drug/antibiotics/thornhill-2019-adverse-reactions-oral-antibiotics-dentists]].
It also grounds the clinical caution that acetaminophen and COX-2 inhibitors are usually
— but not automatically — safe fallbacks when following an acute-pain ladder such as
[[wiki/drug/analgesics/carrasco-labra-2024-pharmacologic-management-acute-dental-pain-guideline]].

Methods note: narrative review, no systematic search, no quality appraisal — the
prevalence figures it cites (GA2LEN, the >4,000-case series) come from the group's own
and collaborators' primary work and should be read as expert-selected rather than
pooled estimates.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (J Asthma Allergy 2019;12:217-233) from the Spanish RIRAAF/Málaga group on hypersensitivity drug reactions to NSAIDs (HDR-NSAID): classification, prevalence, impact, and management.

NSAIDs are now the most common cause of HDR, surpassing beta-lactams; the ENDA/EAACI scheme splits reactions into cross-intolerant (CI: NERD, NECD, NIUA) and selective responders (SR: IgE-mediated SNIUAA, T-cell SNIDR), with a CI:SR ratio of ~3:1; in a >4,000-case series 47.2% were confirmed multiple-NSAID hypersensitivity vs 18% antibiotics.

No validated in vitro/in vivo test exists for CI — drug provocation with aspirin is the only diagnostic option — and management must actively supply a safe alternative because up to ~19% of cross-reactive patients react to paracetamol and ~10% to selective COX-2 inhibitors.

## 세줄요약

서술적 고찰 (J Asthma Allergy 2019;12:217-233), 스페인 RIRAAF/말라가 그룹: 비스테로이드 항염증제 (Nonsteroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug, NSAID)에 대한 과민성 약물반응 (Hypersensitivity Drug Reaction, HDR)의 분류·유병률·영향·관리.

NSAID는 이제 베타락탐 항생제를 제치고 HDR의 가장 흔한 원인이며, 유럽 약물알레르기 네트워크 (European Network for Drug Allergy, ENDA)/유럽 알레르기·임상면역학회 (EAACI) 분류는 교차불내성 (Cross-Intolerant, CI: NERD·NECD·NIUA)과 선택적 반응자 (Selective Responder, SR: IgE 매개 SNIUAA, T세포 매개 SNIDR)로 나뉘고 CI:SR 비는 약 3:1, 4,000건 초과 코호트에서 47.2%가 다중 NSAID 과민성으로 확진(항생제 18%).

CI에는 검증된 체외/체내 검사가 없어 아스피린 (Acetylsalicylic Acid, ASA)을 이용한 약물유발시험 (Drug Provocation Test, DPT)이 유일한 진단 수단이며, 교차반응 환자의 최대 약 19%가 파라세타몰에, 약 10%가 선택적 사이클로옥시게나제-2 (Cyclooxygenase-2, COX-2) 억제제에 반응하므로 관리는 안전한 대체약을 능동적으로 제시해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- **Epidemiological reframing**: NSAIDs are now the leading cause of hypersensitivity drug reactions worldwide, having surpassed antibiotics/beta-lactams. In a consecutive series of >4,000 drug-hypersensitivity cases evaluated over 6 years (EAACI drug expert committee protocol, confirmed by DPT), 47.2% were confirmed as hypersensitivity to multiple NSAIDs vs 18% to antibiotics.
- **Classification** (ENDA/EAACI, 5 major groups) with the CI vs SR split as the primary clinical fork:
  - **Cross-intolerant (CI)** — non-allergic, COX-1-inhibition-mediated, react to multiple chemically unrelated strong COX-1 inhibitors:
    - NERD — NSAID-exacerbated respiratory disease (formerly aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease / ASA triad)
    - NECD — NSAID-exacerbated cutaneous disease (in patients with chronic spontaneous urticaria, CSU)
    - NIUA / NIUAA — NSAID-induced urticaria/angioedema (± anaphylaxis) in otherwise healthy subjects
  - **Selective responders (SR)** — immunologically mediated, react to one drug (or one chemical group) while tolerating others including ASA:
    - SNIUAA — single/selective NSAID-induced urticaria, angioedema, anaphylaxis (immediate, IgE-mediated)
    - SNIDR — single/selective NSAID-induced delayed reactions (T-cell mediated; 24–48 h; ranges from non-immediate urticaria and fixed drug eruption / maculopapular exanthema to SJS-TEN, AGEP, DRESS). The review adds a Type IVe subgroup (NIU / accelerated reactions / serum-sickness-like).
- **NIUA is the most prevalent entity** — established by this group's work, since confirmed by others, though still under-recognized in classical textbooks.
- **CI:SR ratio ≈ 3:1** in their (and other) populations; children are more often CI than SR. High homology reported between the Spanish and a Chinese population in both culprit drugs and clinical entities.
- **Culprit drug shift**: ibuprofen is the "universal culprit" (rising aryl-propionic-acid exposure, OTC availability, ibuprofen metabolites detectable in water supply); aspirin's role has declined as its cardiovascular-prophylaxis use fell. Common culprits: ibuprofen and paracetamol, then diclofenac, more recently selective COX-2 inhibitors.
- **Management principle**: a diagnosis of HDR-NSAID is incomplete without a documented safe alternative; the safety of paracetamol or a COX-2 inhibitor "must not be taken for granted." EAACI **drug allergy passport** recommended — written in plain language, in the native language plus English.

## 3. Methodology
Narrative review. No systematic search strategy, no PRISMA flow, no risk-of-bias or GRADE appraisal. Draws on ENDA/EAACI position papers, the authors' RIRAAF primary studies, and collaborators' cohorts. Prevalence figures are cited from named primary studies (see Results) rather than pooled.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Narrative review — expert selection of evidence, no systematic search or quality appraisal.
- Classification is explicitly described as still evolving; further phenotyping/endotyping expected to change the groups.
- Mechanisms for NECD and for several CI entities rest on "limited data" and are largely inferred from NERD.
- No validated laboratory test for CI reactions; DPT (the reference standard) is resource-intensive and requires dedicated drug-allergy units with trained staff — a service-provision gap the authors flag.
- Evidence-based recommendations for choosing safe alternatives in CI are "lacking"; current advice rests on small/imperfect studies.
- Atopy–HDR-NSAID association remains unresolved (denominator problems in the group's own unpublished data).

## 7. Glossary
- **HDR / HDR-NSAID** — hypersensitivity drug reaction(s), to NSAIDs.
- **CI (cross-intolerant / cross-reactive)** — non-immunological reactions driven by COX-1 inhibition; patient reacts to multiple chemically unrelated strong COX-1 inhibitors.
- **SR (selective responder)** — immunologically mediated reaction to a single drug or chemical group, with tolerance of ASA and other NSAIDs.
- **NERD** — NSAID-exacerbated respiratory disease (rhinosinusitis/nasal polyposis/asthma; classic "ASA triad").
- **NECD** — NSAID-exacerbated cutaneous disease (NSAID aggravation of underlying chronic spontaneous urticaria).
- **NIUA / NIUAA** — NSAID-induced urticaria/angioedema (± anaphylaxis) in subjects without underlying urticarial or respiratory disease.
- **SNIUAA** — single/selective NSAID-induced urticaria, angioedema, anaphylaxis — immediate, IgE-mediated.
- **SNIDR** — single/selective NSAID-induced delayed reactions — T-cell mediated, 24–48 h.
- **DPT** — drug provocation test (controlled challenge); reference standard for CI, usually performed with ASA.
- **ENDA** — European Network for Drug Allergy; **EAACI** — European Academy of Allergy and Clinical Immunology.
- **RIRAAF** — Spanish national network for the study of adverse drug reactions.
- **Drug allergy passport** — EAACI-recommended written patient document (native language + English) stating the diagnosis and safe/unsafe drugs.
