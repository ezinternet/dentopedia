## Three-line Summary

A periodontics-focused narrative review of halitosis covering etiology, assessment methods (organoleptic, sulfide monitoring, gas chromatography), systemic associations, and management strategies including mechanical/chemical treatment.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Comprehensive etiology**: Covers both intra-oral (local) and extra-oral (ENT, gastrointestinal, respiratory, systemic) contributing factors.
2. **Assessment methods**: Describes multiple diagnostic tools — organoleptic measurements, sulfide monitoring, gas chromatography, microbial testing, and chemical test strips.
3. **Systemic disease associations**: Identifies metabolic disease and systemic conditions as cofactors in halitosis etiology.
4. **Management taxonomy**: Categorizes management into masking oral malodor, reducing VOC/VSC levels, and mechanical/chemical treatment.
5. **Multidisciplinary emphasis**: Highlights that effective management requires a team-based approach integrating dentistry and medicine.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Halitosis etiology is multifactorial; local (oral) factors dominate the majority of cases.
- Extra-oral factors include ENT conditions, gastrointestinal, respiratory, and systemic diseases.
- Assessment modalities span organoleptic scoring to gas chromatography for VOC/VSC quantification.
- Management targets both symptom masking and source reduction (VOC/VSC lowering, mechanical debridement, chemical agents).

## 7. Glossary

- **관능검사 (Organoleptic Measurement)**: 훈련된 평가자가 직접 냄새를 맡아 등급을 매기는 구취 측정법
- **황화물 모니터링 (Sulfide Monitoring)**: 구강 내 VSC 농도를 전기화학 센서로 측정하는 방법 (예: Halimeter)
- **가스크로마토그래피 (Gas Chromatography, GC)**: VSC 성분을 개별적으로 정량하는 분석법; 연구 표준
- **휘발성 유기화합물 (Volatile Organic Compounds, VOC)**: VSC 외에도 구취에 기여하는 휘발성 화합물 총칭
- **다학제적 접근 (Multidisciplinary Approach)**: 치과·내과·이비인후과 등 복수 전문과가 협력하는 진료 모델
