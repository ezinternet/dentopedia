## Why Ingested
First head-to-head comparison of virtual treatment setups (VTS) across four commercial clear aligner brands (Invisalign, ClearCorrect, 3M Clarity, Spark) for identical patient records — fills the brand-comparison evidence gap flagged in [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/yassir-2022-cat-vs-fat-overview-systematic-reviews]].

## Three-line Summary
Pilot cross-over VTS study submitting identical records of 10 patients to Invisalign, ClearCorrect, 3M Clarity, and Spark with standardized prescriptions; compared # aligners, # attachments, IPR, incisor vertical movement, canine/molar relationship, and arch widths.

Significant differences in # aligners (p=0.003; ClearCorrect fewest avg 19.7, 3M Clarity most avg 32.7), # attachments (p<0.001; ClearCorrect fewest avg 9, 3M Clarity most avg 17.8), and final canine relationship (p=0.013; Invisalign 65% bilateral Class I, ClearCorrect 15%, Spark 20%); no significant differences in IPR, incisor vertical movement, molar relationship, or arch widths.

ClearCorrect plans faster treatments with fewer attachments but at cost of suboptimal canine Class I planning; Spark performs similarly to Invisalign except for canine Class I deficiency; 3M Clarity prescribes most aligners/attachments via staged dual-thickness approach; all companies expand intercanine width ~2.2–2.7 mm on average.

## 세줄요약
동일 환자 10명 기록을 Invisalign·ClearCorrect·3M Clarity·Spark 4개 브랜드에 동시 제출, 표준화된 처방 조건으로 가상치료계획(Virtual Treatment Setup, VTS)을 비교한 파일럿 교차 연구.

교정장치 수(p=0.003; ClearCorrect 최소 평균 19.7개, 3M Clarity 최다 평균 32.7개), 어태치먼트 수(p<0.001; ClearCorrect 최소 평균 9개, 3M Clarity 최다 평균 17.8개), 최종 견치 관계(p=0.013; Invisalign 65% 양측 Class I, ClearCorrect 15%, Spark 20%)에서 유의한 차이; IPR량·상악 중절치 수직이동·구치 관계·치궁 폭경은 차이 없음.

ClearCorrect는 가장 적은 교정장치와 어태치먼트로 빠른 치료를 계획하지만 견치 Class I 달성 계획에 결함; Spark는 Invisalign과 유사하나 견치 Class I 결함; 3M Clarity는 이중두께 플라스틱 단계별 접근으로 가장 많은 교정장치/어태치먼트 처방; 전 브랜드 평균 2.2–2.7 mm 견치간 폭경 확장.

## 1. Document Information
- **Type**: Pilot comparative study (cross-over VTS submission)
- **Journal**: Dental Press Journal of Orthodontics
- **Year**: 2025 (published Oct 20, 2025)
- **Evidence level**: Comparative study / pilot
- **n**: 10 patients, 4 brands = 40 VTS submitted

## 2. Key Contributions
- First study comparing VTS across four distinct commercial clear aligner brands for the same patients with standardized prescriptions
- Quantifies significant inter-brand differences in treatment complexity (aligners, attachments) and occlusal planning (canine Class I)
- Documents that ~90% of CA research is Invisalign-only, motivating multi-brand investigation
- Provides material/design comparison table: PU (Invisalign SmartTrack), tri-layer elastomeric PU (ClearCorrect ClearQuartz), 5-layer PETG/Force blend (3M Clarity), TruGen (Spark)

## 3. Methodology
- 10 patients, 12–40 years, all permanent teeth, healthy status (no surgery/implants/deciduous teeth)
- Prescriptions standardized across brands (14-day aligner change, no written special instructions)
- Variables: # aligners, # attachments, IPR per arch, vertical movement of max central incisors, final canine & molar relationships, intercanine & intermolar widths
- Statistics: Kruskal-Wallis + pairwise Wilcoxon; chi-square for categorical; post-hoc power analysis (G*Power v3.1)
- IRB: University of Maryland #HP-00102571

## 4. Key Results
| Parameter | Invisalign | ClearCorrect | 3M Clarity | Spark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Avg # aligners | ~25 | 19.7 | 32.7 | ~22 |
| Avg # attachments | ~12 | 9 | 17.8 | ~11 |
| Bilateral canine Class I | 65% (6/10) | 15% (1/10) | 70% (7/10) | 20% (2/10) |

- **Pairwise aligners**: ClearCorrect vs Invisalign p=0.050; ClearCorrect vs 3M Clarity p=0.023
- **Pairwise attachments**: ClearCorrect vs Invisalign p<0.001; ClearCorrect vs Spark p=0.005; 3M Clarity vs Spark p=0.025
- IPR, incisor vertical, molar Class I, intercanine/intermolar widths: no significant differences
- Intercanine width expansion: 2.2–2.7 mm across brands (potentially exceeds stability limits)
- Trim design rationale: ClearCorrect's 2 mm supragingival flat trim theoretically allows fewer attachments via better force distribution; Invisalign and Spark use scalloped gingival trim (weaker force transfer per published biomechanics literature)

## 5. Limitations
- Pilot study, convenience sample (n=10), no randomization
- Heterogeneous cases limit cross-company matching precision
- VTS ≠ clinical outcome; actual tooth movement accuracy not measured
- Laboratory technician variability not fully controlled (minimized by avoiding written instructions)
- Post-hoc power analysis caveat (Lakens 2022)

## 6. Related Work
- Approximately 90% of CA publications focus only on Invisalign (Putrino et al., cited)
- SmartTrack: multi-layer aromatic thermoplastic PU, 0.75 mm, scalloped at gingival margin
- ClearQuartz: tri-layer elastomeric (PETG outer / PU inner / PETG outer), 0.75 mm, flat +2 mm supragingival
- 3M Clarity: Flex 0.625 mm / Force 0.75 mm dual-thickness for different movement types
- Spark TruGen / TruGen XR: 0.75 mm, scalloped at gingival margin

## 7. Glossary
- **VTS (Virtual Treatment Setup)**: digital 3D simulation of final tooth positions used to fabricate sequential aligners
- **IPR (Interproximal Reduction)**: selective enamel stripping between teeth to create space
- **Trim design**: gingival margin profile of the aligner; scalloped = follows gingival contour; flat = horizontal above margin
