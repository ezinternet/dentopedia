## Why Ingested

기존 [[dental-materials/zirconia/sharafeddin-2018-mdp-primers-zirconia-bond]]는 단일 in-vitro 실험으로 전용 MDP 프라이머의 우수성을 보였으나, 이 우산 리뷰(8개 SR, 233~389편 원저 종합)는 그 결론을 SR 레벨 근거로 재확인하면서 APA+10-MDP 프로토콜을 "가장 검증된 표준"으로 확정하고, 최근 대안(NAC, SIE, femtosecond laser, hot etching)의 근거 성숙도를 등급화한다. 표면처리(surface conditioning) 범주 전반의 근거지도가 zirconia 카테고리에 없었던 갭을 메운다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD420251028077) of 8 systematic reviews (5 qualitative, 3 with meta-analysis) covering 233 primary studies (1998–2025; abstract states 389 studies from the same 8 SRs — the two figures are inconsistent within the paper itself) on surface conditioning methods for resin bonding to zirconia restorations.

Airborne-particle abrasion (APA) combined with 10-MDP primers was identified as the most validated and clinically reliable protocol; inter-review overlap of primary studies was weak (corrected covered area, CCA = 0.038); 6/8 included SRs had low overall risk of bias by ROBIS.

Emerging techniques — nanostructured alumina coating (NAC), selective infiltration etching (SIE), silica nanofilm/plasma deposition, and femtosecond laser conditioning — show promising but not yet clinically mature bond-strength and durability data; substantial heterogeneity in aging protocols and bond-strength test methods across primary studies limits pooled interpretation.

## 세줄요약

우산 리뷰(PROSPERO CRD420251028077)로 지르코니아 레진 접착을 위한 표면처리(surface conditioning) 방법을 다룬 체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review, SR) 8편(정성적 5편, 메타분석 포함 3편)을 종합 — 원저 연구 233편(1998~2025년, 초록에는 389편으로 기재되어 본문과 불일치).

에어본 입자 연마(Airborne-Particle Abrasion, APA) + 10-메타크릴로일옥시데실 이수소인산염(10-MDP) 프라이머 병용이 가장 검증되고 임상적으로 신뢰할 수 있는 프로토콜로 확인; SR 간 원저 중복도는 약함(교정중복면적, Corrected Covered Area, CCA = 0.038); 포함 SR 8편 중 6편이 ROBIS 기준 전체 편향위험 낮음.

나노구조 알루미나 코팅(Nanostructured Alumina Coating, NAC), 선택적 침투 에칭(Selective Infiltration Etching, SIE), 실리카 나노필름/플라즈마 증착, 펨토초 레이저 처리는 유망하나 아직 임상 적용 성숙도가 낮음; 노화 프로토콜과 결합강도 측정법의 이질성이 커서 풀링 해석에 주의가 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First umbrella review (per the authors) synthesizing SR-level evidence specifically on surface conditioning methods for zirconia-resin bonding, rather than pooling primary in-vitro studies directly.
- Applied corrected covered area (CCA) analysis to quantify overlap of primary studies across the 8 included SRs (CCA = 0.038, weak overlap → low redundancy risk).
- Applied ROBIS to grade methodological quality of the 8 included SRs (6 low risk, 1 high risk [Habibzadeh et al. 2024], 1 moderate risk [Papia et al. 2014]).
- Consolidated a hierarchy of surface treatments (mechanical, chemical, combined, laser-based) by evidence maturity, distinguishing "clinically validated" (APA+MDP) from "promising but immature" (NAC, SIE, femtosecond laser, hot chemical etching, plasma/silica nanofilm).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Umbrella review (review of systematic reviews), following Center for Reviews and Dissemination methods + PRISMA statement.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, Scopus; PROSPERO searched for ongoing/forthcoming SRs; manual search.
- **PICO**: P = zirconia restorations (crowns/bridges); I = surface treatments; C = no surface treatment; O = bond strength, durability.
- **Inclusion**: SRs (with/without meta-analysis) in English or French, on surface conditioning for zirconia bonding, encompassing clinical/in vivo/in vitro primary studies.
- **Exclusion**: reviews pooling RCTs+SRs together, narrative reviews, studies on mechanical properties without adhesion testing, non-zirconia materials, non-crown/bridge zirconia restorations.
- **n**: 8 SRs included (of 138 records identified: 134 database + 4 other sources; 4 duplicates removed; 134 screened; 118 excluded at title/abstract; 16 full texts assessed; 8 excluded [reasons: full text unavailable ×1, orthodontic bonding ×2, disinfection protocols ×1, ceramic-to-ceramic bonding ×3, anterior-region performance ×1]); reviews span publication years 2014–2025 and cover primary studies from 1998–2025.
- **Quality appraisal**: ROBIS (risk of bias in systematic reviews); GRADE-style summary-of-findings table (Table 4) for bond-strength outcomes.
- **Overlap**: corrected covered area (CCA), interpreted 0–5 slight, 6–10 moderate, 11–15 high, >15 very high overlap.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 8 SRs included (5 qualitative-only, 3 with meta-analysis per the body text; note Table 1 as extracted lists meta-analysis = "Yes" for 4 of the 8 rows [Tehrani 2025, Li 2024, Habibzadeh 2024, Ozcan 2015], a minor internal inconsistency with the body-text count of "three").
- Primary studies: 233 (body text) vs 389 (abstract) — reported here as-is; likely an abstract-drafting error rather than two different denominators, but not reconcilable from the text provided.
- CCA = 0.038 → weak/slight overlap between reviews; three reviews found to include only primary studies already analyzed in earlier reviews (partial redundancy despite low aggregate CCA).
- ROBIS overall risk of bias: Low for Tehrani (2025), Li (2024), Rigos (2023), Russo (2019), Tzanakakis (2016), Ozcan (2015) [6/8]; High for Habibzadeh (2024); Moderate for Papia (2014).
- Most-studied/validated protocol: APA combined with 10-MDP primer — the paper's headline clinical recommendation.
- APA mechanism/parameters synthesized across SRs: particle size 25–250 µm (studies in this review ranged 25–150 µm), air pressure 0.05–0.45 MPa, application 10–20 s, standoff distance 5–20 mm; Ozcan's suggested protocol: 30–50 µm alumina, 0.5–2.5 bar, ≥20 s, 10 mm distance, continuous jet motion. Smaller particles (25–35 µm) gave higher immediate bond strength but no long-term difference vs larger particles (110–115 µm).
- Risks of APA: surface micro-cracks and tetragonal-to-monoclinic (t/m) phase transformation, potentially reducing mechanical properties; mitigations include post-APA heating (m/t reversal) or performing APA before final sintering.
- 10-MDP mechanism: phosphoric-acid group bonds to zirconia oxide layer; vinyl group co-polymerizes with resin cement.
- Nanostructured alumina coating (NAC): non-damaging alternative to APA; ~100 nm lamellar layer increases actual surface area 5–6×; in the cited Malgaj et al. RCT, NAC and APA were statistically equivalent in clinical performance despite different surface-roughening mechanisms.
- Other treatments summarized: tribochemical silica coating (TBS, alumina grit + silica, best combined with silanization), plasma-sputtered silica nanofilm + silanization, vitrification/glaze-on porcelain layer (etching duration 1–15 min), selective infiltration etching (SIE — glass-based conditioner infiltrated into grain boundaries then acid-rinsed), hot chemical etching (ethanol/37% HCl/ferric chloride at 100°C for 1 h, or 40% HF) — flagged as hazardous and not yet clinically refined, gas plasma (only treatment with bond strength similar to untreated control in Li et al.'s analysis; carbon/nitrogen plasma improved bioactivity/cytocompatibility rather than bond strength), and lasers (Er:YAG, Nd:YAG, CO₂ — risk of thermal damage/micro-cracks/monoclinic transformation; femtosecond laser gave superior bond strength with minimal collateral damage but remains costly/not widely available).
- Synthetic diamond airborne particles (Papia et al.): mean bond strength 22.7–31.0 MPa.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Internal numeric inconsistencies in the source paper: total primary studies (233 vs 389) and meta-analysis count (3 stated vs 4 apparent in Table 1) — reported here without resolution since the full data-extraction tables were not reconcilable from the available text.
- Umbrella-review-of-umbrella-review limitations: substantial heterogeneity in zirconia materials/grades, aging/thermocycling protocols and storage times, bond-strength test methods (shear vs tensile vs microtensile), and inconsistent effect-size/CI reporting across the underlying primary studies — pooled results in the constituent meta-analyses should be interpreted cautiously.
- Long-term clinical survival/failure-mode data remain scarce; most evidence is in vitro.
- Authors call for standardized bond-testing/aging protocols, longitudinal clinical trials, cost-effectiveness data for advanced techniques (e.g., femtosecond laser), and study of combinatory/sequential surface treatments.
- Two of eight included SRs had methodological flaws (Habibzadeh et al. — high ROBIS risk; Papia et al. — moderate ROBIS risk, concerns re: study identification/selection).

## 6. Related Work
- sharafeddin-2018: in-vitro SBS study showing dedicated MDP primer > universal MDP adhesive > unprimed control — consistent with this umbrella review's APA+MDP conclusion, now generalized at SR level.
- malgaj-2026 (cited in this paper's discussion as "Malgaj et al." on NAC vs APA): 8-year RCT found NAC clinically equivalent to APA for cantilever zirconia RBFDP retention — supports this review's characterization of NAC as a viable non-damaging alternative to APA.

## 7. Glossary
- **APA**: Airborne-particle abrasion — alumina-particle blasting to roughen zirconia surface.
- **10-MDP**: 10-methacryloyloxydecyl dihydrogen phosphate — bifunctional monomer that chemically bonds to zirconia's oxide layer and co-polymerizes with resin.
- **NAC**: Nanostructured alumina coating — non-invasive, calcination-fired nanoroughening alternative to APA.
- **SIE**: Selective infiltration etching — glass-conditioner infiltration into zirconia grain boundaries followed by acid rinse.
- **TBS**: Tribochemical silica coating — silica-coated alumina blasting to introduce a silica phase on zirconia for silanization.
- **CCA**: Corrected covered area — metric quantifying primary-study overlap across systematic reviews in an umbrella review.
- **ROBIS**: Risk Of Bias In Systematic reviews — tool for appraising SR methodological quality.
- **t/m transformation**: Tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation in zirconia, associated with surface stress from mechanical treatments like APA.
