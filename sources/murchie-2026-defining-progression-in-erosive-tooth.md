## Why Ingested

The held ETW papers cover aetiology, prevalence and prevention but none addresses how progression is monitored. This SR examines BEWE-type ordinal indices versus IOS quantification, qualifying the BEWE-based outcomes used in [[wiki/dental-erosion/juarez-lopez-2026-prevalence-risk-dental-erosion-carbonated]] and the consensus-based management framing of [[wiki/dental-erosion/fdi-2024-tooth-wear-policy-statement]]. → [[dental-erosion/murchie-2026-defining-progression-in-erosive-tooth]]

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA 2020, qualitative critical synthesis, no meta-analysis; MEDLINE/Embase/Scopus/WoS to January 2026, updated August 2026; 1,074 records, 5 longitudinal cohort studies, n = 24-517, follow-up 1-25 years) asking whether intraoral scanner (IOS) quantification and clinical indices (BEWE, Smith and Knight) measure the same thing as erosive tooth wear (ETW) "progression".

IOS detected measurable surface loss (about 10-150 um at roughly 12 months; about 137 um at 10 years; over 300-400 um at 25 years) in short intervals where categorical indices often showed no change; agreement with BEWE was weak to moderate (one study: volumetric loss vs BEWE-defined progression AUC 0.80, -1.22 mm3 cut-off, 79% sensitivity / 61% specificity), and progression thresholds ranged from more than 30 um to 200 um or more, or were undefined.

IOS and ordinal indices are not interchangeable progression measures, no validated link between micron-level loss and clinically meaningful progression exists, and no included study validated IOS against a high-resolution reference (e.g. laser profilometry); use indices for screening and IOS selectively as an adjunct in high-risk patients; evidence base is small (5 studies) and heterogeneous.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (PRISMA 2020, 정성적 비판적 종합·메타분석 없음; 2026년 1월까지 검색 후 8월 갱신; 1,074건 중 종단 코호트 5편, n = 24-517, 추적 1-25년) — 구강내 스캐너 (Intraoral Scanner, IOS) 정량 측정과 임상 지수 (BEWE, Smith and Knight)가 치아침식 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW) "진행"을 같은 의미로 측정하는지 평가.

IOS는 범주형 지수가 변화를 못 잡는 짧은 기간에도 표면 손실을 검출했다 (약 12개월 10-150 um, 10년 약 137 um, 25년 300-400 um 초과); BEWE와의 일치도는 약-중등도 (한 연구에서 부피 손실 vs BEWE 진행 AUC 0.80, 기준 -1.22 mm3, 민감도 79%/특이도 61%)이고 진행 기준값은 30 um 초과부터 200 um 이상까지 제각각이거나 미정의.

IOS와 서열 지수는 진행의 호환 척도가 아니며, 마이크론 단위 손실을 임상적으로 의미 있는 진행과 연결하는 검증된 기준이 없고 고해상도 기준법 (레이저 프로파일로메트리 등)에 대한 IOS 검증도 포함 연구 전무; 지수는 선별, IOS는 고위험 환자에서 선택적 보조로 쓰는 것이 현실적이나 근거는 5편·이질적.

## 1. Document Information

Murchie BD, Lynch D, Carr A, Edwards D. Clin Oral Investig 2026;30(11). Epub 2026-10-13. DOI 10.1007/s00784-026-07217-8. PMID 42867705, PMCID PMC13661733. Systematic review (qualitative synthesis), Newcastle University. Full text retrieved from PMC.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reframes the IOS-versus-index comparison as a construct mismatch (continuous dimensional loss vs ordinal severity) rather than a sensitivity contest.
- Documents absence of a validated framework tying quantified surface loss to biological or clinical relevance.
- Proposes a pragmatic workflow (indices for screening/risk stratification; IOS selectively) and a core outcome set (COS) for longitudinal ETW research.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PRISMA 2020 SR; MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science (inception to January 2026) plus grey literature (OpenGrey, Grey Literature Report, ProQuest); update August 2026; reference and forward-citation tracking. Inclusion: longitudinal ETW studies using IOS-based quantification alongside conventional clinical assessment; in vitro studies eligible but none qualified. Two independent reviewers; quality by Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (scores de-emphasised); qualitative synthesis, no meta-analysis, no publication-bias assessment. English only. Registration not stated in the text read.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 1,074 records; 186 duplicates; 888 screened; 52 full texts; 5 included (Bors 2025, Ellingsgaard 2025, Marro 2020, Maluf 2025, Garcia 2022). BEWE n = 3, modified BEWE n = 1, Smith and Knight n = 1. n = 24-517; follow-up 1-25 years.
- Surface loss: approximately 10 to over 500 um overall; about 10-150 um at about 12 months; about 137 um mean at 10 years (maxillary anterior); over 300-400 um at 25 years.
- Short intervals (up to 12 months): categorical indices often showed no significant change while IOS detected loss (mBEWE unchanged at about 12 months in one study).
- Agreement IOS vs BEWE weak to moderate; ROC for volumetric loss vs BEWE-defined progression AUC 0.80, threshold -1.22 mm3, sensitivity 79%, specificity 61% (BEWE was the reference standard, so IOS was not independently validated).
- Progression thresholds: more than 30 um in one study, 200 um or more in another, some undefined.
- Inter-examiner agreement for indices kappa 0.65-0.87. IOS precision metrics (duplicate scans, ICC, minimal detectable change) inconsistently reported.
- Physiological wear context cited: about 15 um/year premolars, 29 um/year molars.
- Only one study analysed enamel and dentine separately; greater wear on palatal anterior and occlusal molar surfaces.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Only 5 studies, heterogeneous scanners, superimposition and outcome metrics; some studies used indirect digitised casts; no validation against laser profilometry; language restriction; no publication-bias assessment; most studies enamel only. Future: report scanner/software/superimposition details, precision and minimal detectable change, enamel vs dentine, validated clinically meaningful thresholds, COS.

## 7. Glossary

- ETW: erosive tooth wear. IOS: intraoral scanner. BEWE: Basic Erosive Wear Examination (ordinal). mBEWE: study-specific modification (not BEWE 2.0). AUC: area under ROC curve. COS: core outcome set.
