## Why Ingested

The wiki holds objective masticatory-performance evidence — [[removable-partial-denture/liang-2015-effects-removable-dental-prostheses-masticatory]] quantifies a 28–39% comminution/mixing-ability deficit in shortened dental arches — and an SDA decision overview built on it ([[overviews/sda-shortened-dental-arch-evidence-overview]]), but no page appraising the *subjective* (questionnaire) side of the same construct. This COSMIN-based systematic review supplies the missing measurement axis: it grades all 19 masticatory-function PROMs and concludes only one (CFQ-Croatian/Albanian) currently clears COSMIN category A.

## Three-line Summary

COSMIN-based systematic review (5 databases to March 2021; 23 studies, 19 PROMs, 16,886 participants) critically appraising patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) of masticatory function in adults.

No PROM had high-level evidence for all psychometric properties; CFQ-Croatian/Albanian was the only instrument meeting COSMIN category A, while FIQ-Japanese, New-FIQ-Japanese, PDC-Tanzania, CFQ-Chinese and SMDOA were category B1 (potentially recommendable), and Subset-OHIP and QMFQ-Persian fell into category C (not recommended).

Measurement error was never tested in any of the 23 studies and only three studies assessed responsiveness, so instrument choice for longitudinal or treatment-change questions rests on thin evidence.

## 세줄요약

COSMIN 기반 체계적 문헌고찰(5개 데이터베이스, 2021년 3월까지; 23편 연구·19개 환자보고결과측정도구(PROM)·16,886명)로 성인의 저작기능(masticatory function) PROM을 비판적으로 평가했다.

어떤 PROM도 모든 심리측정적 특성에서 높은 수준의 근거를 확보하지 못했으며, COSMIN 범주 A에 든 것은 저작기능설문지(Chewing Function Questionnaire, CFQ)-크로아티아/알바니아판 하나뿐이었다. 식이섭취설문지(Food Intake Questionnaire, FIQ)-일본어판, 신FIQ-일본어판, 저작곤란지각(Perceived Difficulty of Chewing, PDC)-탄자니아판, CFQ-중국어판, 노인저작장애선별(Screening for Masticatory Disorders in Older Adults, SMDOA)은 범주 B1(권고 가능성 있음), 구강건강영향프로파일(Oral Health Impact Profile, OHIP) 부분척도와 저작기능질문지(Quality of Masticatory Function Questionnaire, QMFQ)-페르시아판은 범주 C(권고 안 함)로 분류됐다.

23편 모두 측정오차(measurement error)를 검정하지 않았고 반응성(responsiveness)을 평가한 연구는 3편뿐이어서, 종단·치료변화 평가 목적의 도구 선택 근거는 매우 얇다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic review of PROMs for masticatory function conducted under the COSMIN guideline — the authors state that methodological quality and psychometric properties of masticatory-function PROMs had not been reported in any earlier systematic review.
- Builds a four-way taxonomy of the instruments by content: chewing-specific **food items** (15 studies / 13 PROMs), **chewing problems** (3/3), **both food items and chewing problems** (3/2), and a single **global question** (2/1).
- Separates two appraisal axes that are usually conflated: COSMIN **risk-of-bias** rating of each study's methods (V/A/D/I, worst-score-counts) versus the **quality of the psychometric property result** (+/−/?), then grades the **level of evidence** per property (high/moderate/low/very low) with modified GRADE.
- Produces an actionable COSMIN category A/B1/B2/C allocation — only CFQ-Croatian/Albanian is category A and therefore "recommended for use".
- Documents the field's structural gaps: zero measurement-error studies, three responsiveness studies, two cross-cultural validity studies (both forward–backward translation only), and no PROM with high-level content-validity evidence.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: systematic review of studies developing or validating subjective (questionnaire/interview) measures of masticatory function in adults; any study design eligible; English full text required; PROSPERO-registered; PRISMA 2020.
- **Databases**: Medline (PubMed), Embase (Ovid), Web of Science Core Collection, CINAHL Plus (EBSCOhost), APA PsycINFO (ProQuest) — inception to March 2021. Three-part search string: (1) chewing/masticatory function/ability/mastication; (2) questionnaire*/subjective*/evaluation*/assessment*; (3) validation/validity/reliability/psychometric*. Supplemented by reference-list hand-searching and Google Scholar.
- **Exclusions**: objective-only assessment; subjective assessment used only as an outcome measure; case studies, expert opinion, animal studies, reviews.
- **Screening**: EndNote 9.3.1 deduplication; two reviewers (YPF, XS) independently screened titles/abstracts and full texts; disagreements resolved with a third expert (ECML).
- **Data extracted**: first author, year, participants, setting, design, location, and PROM characteristics + psychometric properties.
- **Methodological quality**: COSMIN risk-of-bias checklist, 10 aspects — PROM development, content validity, structural validity, internal consistency, cross-cultural validity/measurement invariance, reliability, measurement error, criterion validity, hypotheses testing for construct validity, responsiveness — each rated very good / adequate / doubtful / inadequate, worst score counts.
- **Property ratings**: each property rated sufficient (+), insufficient (−) or indeterminate (?); across studies then sufficient (+), insufficient (−), inconsistent (±) or indeterminate (?).
- **Level of evidence**: modified GRADE per property (high/moderate/low/very low) over four factors — risk of bias, inconsistency, imprecision, indirectness; publication bias deliberately not considered, per COSMIN.
- **Recommendation framework**: COSMIN category A (sufficient content validity at any level + at least low-quality evidence for sufficient internal consistency) = recommended; category C (high-quality evidence for an insufficient property) = not recommended; everything else B, subdivided by the construct-validity hypothesis-testing result into B1 (potential to recommend) and B2 (needs further research).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Included evidence base**

- 1,850 records after deduplication → 1,816 excluded at title/abstract → 34 full texts assessed, 22 excluded → 11 additional articles from the supplementary search → **23 articles / 23 studies / 19 PROMs**.
- **16,886 participants** in total; 20 cross-sectional + 3 cohort studies; sample sizes 20 to 2,244.
- Countries: Japan 8, China 4, Canada 2, and one each Spain, Iran, Brazil, Croatia, Republic of Kosovo, Korea, Sudan, Tanzania, Sweden.
- Construct scope: "most of the PROMs were unidimensional", the exception being QMFQ-Persian with five domains (problems with dentures, problems eating apple/carrot, meat products, fruits/vegetables, change needed for swallowing). Table 1's SMDOA entry is labelled both "unidimensional" and "two factors", so the unidimensionality of that instrument is ambiguous in the source.

**Methodological quality (COSMIN risk of bias)**

| Aspect | Studies assessing it | Rating pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Hypotheses testing | 21 of 23 | mostly adequate or very good (the methods paragraph calls this "hypothesis testing for structural validity"; the results paragraph calls the same column "construct validity") |
| Structural validity | 8 | mostly adequate/very good; QMFQ-Persian **inadequate** (insufficient sample size) |
| Internal consistency | 14 | mostly **doubtful** — structural validity/unidimensionality not established |
| Test–retest reliability | 8 | — |
| Content validity | 6 | 5 doubtful, 1 inadequate (CFQ-Chinese) |
| Criterion validity | 3 | 2 very good (New-FIQ-Japanese, MACE), 1 inadequate (FIAQ) |
| Responsiveness | 3 | all very good (ICA-2020, CFQ-Croatian, CFQ-Albanian) |
| Cross-cultural validity | 2 | both doubtful (QMFQ-Persian, CFQ-Albanian) |
| PROM development | 6 described | **all doubtful** |
| Measurement error | 0 | no study assessed it |

**Psychometric property results**

- Internal consistency: evaluated in 14 studies, **9 indeterminate** — they failed the criterion of "at least low evidence for sufficient structural validity".
- Test–retest: 8 studies, **7 sufficient**. Content validity: 6 studies, **4 sufficient**. Structural validity: 8 studies, **7 sufficient**. Criterion validity: 3 studies, **2 sufficient**.
- Hypotheses testing for construct validity: **12 of 21** studies sufficient.
- Responsiveness: **3 of 3** sufficient (standardized effect size higher than expected and in the hypothesized direction).
- Cross-cultural translation: **2 of 2 indeterminate** (translation only, no statistical invariance testing).

**Evidence synthesis — PROMs with a stated evidence level above "low"**

| PROM | Sufficient properties with moderate/high level of evidence |
|---|---|
| CFQ-Croatian / CFQ-Albanian | internal consistency, test–retest reliability, structural validity, hypotheses testing, responsiveness (moderate or high; all sufficient) |
| CFQ-Chinese | internal consistency (**high**); test–retest, structural validity, hypotheses testing (moderate) |
| New-FIQ-Japanese | structural validity, criterion validity, hypotheses testing (moderate or high) |
| FIQ-Japanese (1994, 1998) | hypotheses testing (moderate) |
| PDC-Tanzania | hypotheses testing (moderate); internal consistency low and indeterminate |
| SMDOA | structural validity and hypotheses testing (**high**); content validity low |

**COSMIN recommendation categories**

- **A (recommended)**: CFQ-Croatian/Albanian only.
- **B1 (potential to recommend)**: FIQ-Japanese, New-FIQ-Japanese, PDC-Tanzania, CFQ-Chinese, SMDOA — plus FIAQ-key food version and SMF, which "may be classified as B1" but need more research.
- **B2 (needs further research)**: ICA-1990, PDC-Sudan, IED, CFQ-Japanese, FIAQ, MACE, MPI.
- **C (not recommended)**: Subset-OHIP, QMFQ-Persian.
- **Unclassifiable**: FIQ-Chinese — convergent validity insufficient while discriminative validity sufficient.

**Population-matched recommendations (author-stated)**

- CFQ-Croatian/Albanian → general prosthodontic patients.
- FIQ-Japanese and New-FIQ-Japanese → complete denture wearers.
- SMDOA, CFQ-Chinese and PDC-Tanzania → community-dwelling adults in epidemiological screening.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Author-stated limitations:

- Only English-language articles were included — potentially excellent PROMs published in other languages may be omitted.
- Development/validation may have been performed rigorously but reported thinly, which can downgrade a study's methodological quality rating (relevant because all six development studies were rated doubtful).
- No PROM can be considered a "gold standard"; the positive recommendations are conditional on the evidence available.

Author-stated research agenda:

- **Measurement error** was untested everywhere; the authors argue MIC vs SDC / limits of agreement must be evaluated so score differences can be attributed to real change.
- **Content validity** — regarded by the authors as the most important validity type — was evaluated for only five of the 19 PROMs and never reached a high level of evidence.
- **Cross-cultural validity** needs CFA/regression (CTT) or DIF analysis (IRT), not mere forward–backward translation.
- **Responsiveness** was assessed in only three longitudinal studies; the authors call paired t-tests inappropriate and recommend effect size, standardized response mean, Norman's responsiveness coefficient or relative efficacy statistics.
- Feasibility and interpretability should be weighed alongside measurement properties when choosing an instrument.

Review-level caveats noted while reading the text (not author-stated):

- The statement that "the number of questions in each PROM ranged from seven to 35" is internally inconsistent with Table 1, which lists SMF as a single global question and FIAQ-key food as 5 items.
- Table 1 presents 24 instrument rows for the 19 stated PROMs; Table 4 merges four pairs (FIQ-Japanese [7,33], FIQ-Chinese [28,38], ICA [29,32], SMF [45,46]), and the exact row-to-instrument mapping is not stated. Merging ICA-1990 with ICA-2020 is questionable, since Table 1 shows different item structures and response options.
- The methods-quality narrative refers to "hypothesis testing for structural validity" (21/23 studies) where the results narrative refers to "hypothesis testing for construct validity" (21 studies) — presumably the same column, but the wording is inconsistent.
- The consensus report cited in the Background names five masticatory-function PROMs; four are included here, the fifth (a three-question ICF-based instrument) being excluded because its development/validation was never published.

## 6. Related Work

- **Objective counterpart of the same construct** — [[removable-partial-denture/liang-2015-effects-removable-dental-prostheses-masticatory]]: masticatory performance by comminution (X50) and mixing ability, 28–39% deficit in (E)SDA. Fan 2021 appraises the questionnaire side; the two together define what "masticatory function" means operationally.
- **PROM appraisal in a neighbouring field** — [[behavioral-dentistry/patient-reported-outcomes/menini-2025-proms-sinus-lift-procedures-sr-ma]]: a PROM-focused SR+MA in sinus lift; same measurement-instrument logic, different construct and population.
- **Clinical use of the construct** — [[overviews/sda-shortened-dental-arch-evidence-overview]]: decision rules on whether molar loss warrants prosthetic intervention rest on masticatory-function outcomes; this review constrains *how* that outcome should be measured.
- **Chewing function and occlusion** — [[occlusion/kwak-2024-masticatory-positioning-occlusal-stability-full-mouth]]: reconstructs occlusion around a normalized chewing position; a PROM-based masticatory-function outcome would be the patient-side readout of that intervention.
- Background consensus cited by the paper (not held in the wiki): the three-way classification of masticatory-function assessment into direct objective, indirect objective, and subjective methods.

## 7. Glossary

- **PROM (Patient-Reported Outcome Measure)**: an instrument, usually a questionnaire, that quantifies a construct from the patient's own perspective — here masticatory function.
- **COSMIN**: COnsensus-based Standards for the selection of health Measurement INstruments — the guideline (risk-of-bias checklist + property criteria + modified GRADE) used to appraise measurement instruments and to grade the certainty of their measurement properties.
- **Risk-of-bias rating (V/A/D/I)**: very good / adequate / doubtful / inadequate methodological quality of the study evidence *for one measurement property*; "worst score counts" means the lowest item rating defines the box rating.
- **Category A / B1 / B2 / C**: COSMIN recommendation tiers — A = recommended for use, C = not recommended (high-quality evidence of an insufficient property), B = not yet classifiable, with B1 (potential to recommend) and B2 (further research needed) split by the construct-validity hypothesis-testing result.
- **Measurement error**: systematic and random error in a score not attributable to true change; judged sufficient when the minimal important change (MIC) exceeds the smallest detectable change (SDC) or lies outside the limits of agreement — untested for every PROM in this review.
- **Responsiveness**: the ability of an instrument to detect clinically important change over time — a prerequisite for using a PROM as a treatment outcome.
- **CFQ (Chewing Function Questionnaire)**: the instrument family that performs best in this review; its Croatian/Albanian versions (10 items mixing food items and chewing problems) are the only category-A instruments, and the Chinese 10-item food-only version is category B1.
