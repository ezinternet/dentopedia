## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA; 798 studies screened, 5 included; 501 implants, 241 participants, 1–5-year follow-up) comparing peri-implantitis incidence and survival outcomes between tissue-level (TL) and bone-level (BL) dental implants.

Tissue-level implants showed a trend toward lower peri-implantitis (RR 0.59, 95% CI 0.14–2.48, P=0.47) and lower failure rates (RR 0.59, 95% CI 0.07–4.69, P=0.62) vs bone-level, but neither reached statistical significance; probing pocket depth and marginal bone level changes were also not significantly different at 12 or 24 months.

No definitive clinical recommendation can be made regarding implant type selection based on peri-implantitis risk; the small evidence base (5 studies), high heterogeneity, and limited trial quality constrain conclusions.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA(PRISMA; 798편 스크리닝 → 5편 포함; 임플란트 501개·241명, 1–5년 추적) — 조직수준(Tissue Level, TL) 대비 골수준(Bone Level, BL) 임플란트의 임플란트 주위염(peri-implantitis) 발생률·생존율 비교.

TL이 주위염(RR 0.59, P=0.47)·실패율(RR 0.59, P=0.62) 모두 낮은 경향이나 통계적 유의차 없음; 탐침 깊이(probing pocket depth, PPD)·변연 골 소실(marginal bone loss, MBL) 변화도 12·24개월 시 비유의.

TL vs BL 임플란트 유형 선택에 대한 명확한 임상 권고 불가; 포함 5편의 소규모 근거, 높은 이질성, 연구 질의 한계로 결론이 제한됨.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Most recent SR+MA (2025) on TL vs BL peri-implantitis outcomes
- 5 studies, 501 implants, 241 participants
- TL: peri-implantitis RR 0.59 (95% CI 0.14–2.48, p=0.47) — NS
- TL: failure RR 0.59 (95% CI 0.07–4.69, p=0.62) — NS
- No significant differences in PPD or MBL changes at 12 and 24 months
- Clinical suggestion: TL may have marginal advantage for peri-implant health

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
SR+MA following PRISMA. 798 studies identified; 5 RCTs/controlled studies included. Population: adult patients receiving TL or BL implants. Outcomes: peri-implantitis incidence, implant failure, probing pocket depth (PPD), marginal bone level changes. Meta-analysis: random-effects model; relative risk (RR) for binary outcomes.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- TL peri-implantitis: RR 0.59 (95% CI 0.14–2.48, p=0.47) — not significant
- TL failure: RR 0.59 (95% CI 0.07–4.69, p=0.62) — not significant
- PPD: no significant differences at 12 or 24 months
- MBL changes: no significant differences
- Comparable survival within 1–5 years
- Limited number of RCTs; high heterogeneity

## 7. Glossary
- **PS**: Platform switching — use of smaller-diameter abutment on larger implant collar
- **PM**: Platform matching — abutment matches implant platform diameter
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss — radiographic bone level change around implant
- **TL**: Tissue-level implant — transmucosal component integral to implant
- **BL**: Bone-level implant — implant platform at crestal bone level
