## Why Ingested
임플란트 식립 시 발생하는 협측 탈피 결손(dehiscence)에 대한 합성골(SBS)과 이종골(DBBM) 동시 GBR을 비교한 다기관 비열등성 RCT. [[bone-regeneration/buser-2023-gbr-implant-35years-basic-principle-review]]의 골재생 재료 선택 논의에 합성골 임상 데이터를 추가한다.

## Three-line Summary
Multicenter noninferiority RCT (n=49) comparing simultaneous guided bone regeneration at peri-implant dehiscences using synthetic bone substitute (biphasic calcium phosphate, SBS) versus deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM), both combined with a bioabsorbable collagen membrane, across two centers in Seoul and Madrid. Defect height reduction was 74.5% (SBS) versus 63.4% (DBBM) at 6-month re-entry (p=0.216, noninferiority confirmed); complete defect resolution reached 62.5% versus 44%, and marginal bone levels remained stable through 1 year in both groups. Biphasic calcium phosphate is a clinically valid xenograft-free alternative for GBR at dehiscences, relevant for patients with religious, ethical, or disease-transmission concerns, though the small sample (n=49) and retrospective trial registration limit confidence.

## 세줄요약
줄1: 다기관 비열등성 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)(n=49): 임플란트 식립 시 발생하는 협측 탈피결손 (Dehiscence)에서 합성골 대체재 (Synthetic Bone Substitute, SBS, 이상성 인산칼슘)와 탈단백 우골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)을 이용한 동시 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)을 서울·마드리드 2기관에서 비교.
줄2: 6개월 재진입 시 결손고 감소율 74.5%(SBS) vs 63.4%(DBBM), p=0.216으로 비열등성 확인; 완전결손 해소율 62.5% vs 44%(유의차 없음); 양군 모두 1년 변연골 (Marginal Bone) 안정.
줄3: 이상성 인산칼슘 합성골은 종교·윤리·안전 우려가 있는 환자에서 이종골을 대체할 수 있는 임상적으로 유효한 선택지이나, 소규모(n=49) 및 소급 등록이 근거 강도를 제한.

## One-line Summary
Multicenter noninferiority RCT (n=49): synthetic bone substitute (SBS, biphasic calcium phosphate) was noninferior to DBBM for GBR at peri-implant dehiscences; defect height reduction 74.5% (SBS) vs. 63.4% (DBBM), p=0.216.

## 한줄요약
다기관 비열등성 RCT(n=49): 합성골(SBS, 이상성 인산칼슘) = 이종골(DBBM)와 탈피결손 GBR 동등; 결손고 감소 74.5%(SBS) vs 63.4%(DBBM), p=0.216; 양군 변연골 안정.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Implant Dentistry and Related Research, 2024;26:1233–1244
- **Institutions**: Yonsei University, Seoul (Cha/Jung) + Universidad Complutense de Madrid (Montero/Sanz)
- **Funding**: National Research Foundation of Korea
- **Registration**: KCT0008393 (not registered prior to enrollment — limitation)
- **Follow-up**: 1 year post-implant placement

## 2. Key Contributions
- First multicenter RCT directly comparing SBS (biphasic calcium phosphate) vs. DBBM for simultaneous GBR at dehiscences
- Demonstrates noninferior defect resolution with synthetic material (avoids xenograft concerns: disease transmission, immunogenicity, religious rejection)
- Complete defect resolution rate comparable: 62.5% (SBS) vs. 44% (DBBM), p=NS
- Stable marginal bone levels in both groups up to 19 months post-surgery
- Provides data for patients unwilling to accept xenogenic material (religious/ethical reasons)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Multicenter noninferiority RCT (two-arm parallel)
- **n**: 49 patients (SBS=24, DBBM=25) with expected dehiscence defects after implant placement
- **Intervention**: SBS (biphasic calcium phosphate, highly porous) + bioabsorbable collagen membrane
- **Control**: DBBM (deproteinized bovine bone mineral) + bioabsorbable collagen membrane
- **Primary outcome**: Bone defect height (DH) change from implant insertion to re-entry at 6 months
- **Secondary outcomes**: Peri-implant health (probing depth, bleeding, plaque), implant survival, bone levels, PROMs
- **Re-entry surgery**: 6 months post-implant placement

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Defect height at insertion → re-entry**:
  - SBS: 5.1 ± 2.6 mm → 1.3 ± 2.0 mm (74.5% reduction)
  - DBBM: 4.1 ± 1.7 mm → 1.5 ± 1.9 mm (63.4% reduction)
  - Difference p=0.216 (noninferiority confirmed)
- **Complete defect resolution**:
  - SBS: 62.5% (15/24 patients)
  - DBBM: 44% (11/25 patients)
  - p=NS
- **Marginal bone levels**: Stable during 1-year follow-up in both groups
- **Implant survival**: High in both groups (specific % not in abstract)
- **PROMs**: Comparable between groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Trial NOT registered prior to enrollment (KCT0008393 — retrospective registration)
- Relatively small sample (n=49)
- 6-month re-entry is standard but histological data on bone quality not reported
- No long-term (>1 year) data on graft substitution rate
- SBS brand-specific results (highly porous biphasic calcium phosphate)

## 7. Glossary
- **SBS**: Synthetic bone substitute (biphasic calcium phosphate = hydroxyapatite + β-TCP)
- **DBBM**: Deproteinized bovine bone mineral (Bio-Oss® equivalent)
- **Dehiscence**: Exposed implant surface/threads through buccal bone plate at placement
- **Defect height (DH)**: Vertical extent of exposed implant surface
- **Re-entry surgery**: Second surgical entry to assess and finalize GBR outcomes
- **PROM**: Patient-reported outcome measure
- **Biphasic calcium phosphate (BCP)**: Synthetic scaffold combining slow-resorbing HA with faster-resorbing β-TCP
