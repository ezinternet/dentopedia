## Why Ingested

Only in vitro study comparing the interaction between preparation method (standard, underprepared, osteotome) AND cortical condition (monocortical vs bicortical) simultaneously, in a posterior maxilla analog. Establishes that bicortical fixation amplifies the benefit of underprepared osteotomy and mitigates the ISQ penalty of osteotome technique, while standard preparation in bicortical blocks shows significantly better stability than monocortical standard. Complements [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/han-2016-primary-stability-bicortical-posterior-maxilla]] (which used a single standard preparation protocol) by adding the preparation × location interaction that drives real-world outcomes.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro, 60 implants (4.0×10 mm) in polyurethane blocks; 6 groups (n=10): 2 cortical conditions (monocortical, bicortical) × 3 preparation methods (standard, underpreparation, osteotome); insertion torque, removal torque, and RFA (ISQ) measured.
Preparation method significantly affected insertion torque, ISQ, and removal torque; cortical location significantly affected only removal torque (p<0.001); standard preparation in bicortical > monocortical (ISQ, p=0.039); underprepared and osteotome showed no location effect on ISQ.
In posterior maxilla, bicortical fixation with standard preparation provides the best stability; underprepared and osteotome techniques provide similar ISQ regardless of cortical location — preparation choice matters as much as cortical engagement.

## 세줄요약

체외 60개 임플란트 (4.0×10 mm, 폴리우레탄 6군, n=10): 피질골 조건(단겹질/쌍겹질) × 준비방식(표준/과소준비/오스테오톰); 삽입토크·제거토크·ISQ 측정.
준비방식이 삽입토크·ISQ·제거토크에 유의 영향; 피질골 위치는 제거토크에만 유의(p<0.001); 표준 준비에서 쌍겹질 > 단겹질 ISQ(p=0.039); 과소준비·오스테오톰에서는 피질골 위치 효과 없음.
후방 상악에서 쌍겹질+표준 준비가 최선; 과소준비·오스테오톰은 피질골 위치와 무관하게 유사한 ISQ — 준비방식이 쌍겹질만큼 중요.

## Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Prosthetic Dentistry
- **DOI**: [10.1016/S0022-3913(12)60092-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-3913(12)60092-4)
- **Institution**: Kyung Hee University, Seoul, Korea
- **Study type**: In vitro (polyurethane bone analog)
- **n**: 60 implants, 6 groups (n=10 each)

## Key Contributions

- First study quantifying preparation method × cortical condition interaction for posterior maxilla
- Bicortical benefit (removal torque) generalizes across all three preparation methods
- ISQ bicortical advantage is preparation-dependent: significant only with standard preparation
- Confirms Han 2016 standard-preparation finding in a different model (single-piece block vs RBH-varied blocks)
- Osteotome technique reduces ISQ in both cortical conditions vs standard preparation

## Methodology

- **Implant**: 4.0 × 10 mm screw-shaped (manufacturer not specified)
- **Blocks**: Solid rigid polyurethane blocks; monocortical (no apical cortex) vs bicortical (apical cortex present)
- **Groups**:
  - M-Std, M-Under, M-Osteo (monocortical × 3 methods)
  - B-Std, B-Under, B-Osteo (bicortical × 3 methods)
- **Preparation methods**:
  - Standard: manufacturer's recommended protocol
  - Underpreparation: undersized final drill diameter
  - Osteotome: bone condensation with osteotome, no drill for final width
- **Measurements**: Insertion torque (IT), removal torque (RT), RFA/ISQ (Osstell)
- **Statistics**: 2-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD; Pearson correlation

## Key Results

| Factor | ISQ effect | p-value |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation method | Significant (standard > underprep > osteotome overall) | <0.05 |
| Cortical location | Significant only for removal torque | <0.001 |
| Interaction (location × preparation) | Significant for ISQ (but not all pairs) | p=0.045 |
| Standard prep: bicortical vs monocortical ISQ | Bicortical > monocortical | p=0.039 |
| Underpreparation: bicortical vs monocortical ISQ | No significant difference | p=1.00 |
| Osteotome: bicortical vs monocortical ISQ | No significant difference | p=1.00 |

Correlations: IT–ISQ r=0.529, IT–RT r=0.517, RT–ISQ r=0.481 (all p<0.001)

## Limitations

- Polyurethane blocks: homogeneous, no trabecular variation (unlike real bone)
- Single implant diameter and length (4.0×10 mm only)
- Abstract-only: specific numeric ISQ values per group not recoverable
- Does not include clinical validation

## Glossary

- **Underpreparation**: Osteotomy prepared to a smaller diameter than the implant — increases lateral bone compression and IT
- **Osteotome technique**: Bone condensation without drilling to final diameter — expands bone rather than removes it
- **Removal torque**: Torque required to unscrew an implant — proxy for osseointegration quality in in vitro models
