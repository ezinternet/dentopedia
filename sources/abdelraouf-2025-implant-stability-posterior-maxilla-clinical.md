## Why Ingested

Adds a double-blind RCT with 12-month post-loading follow-up on the OD-vs-conventional-drilling ISQ question in the posterior maxilla — directly relevant to the open controversy in this folder, where [[wiki/implants/osseodensification/mello-machado-2021-osseodensification-low-quality-bone-rct]] and [[wiki/implants/osseodensification/moghaddas-2025-osseodensification-standard-drilling-isq-itv]] found ISQ-equivalence while this RCT reports a significant early ISQ benefit plus a survival point-estimate gap (9/9 vs 8/10). It also supplies the longitudinal ISQ-through-loading trajectory that [[wiki/implants/osseodensification/marzorati-2026-osseodensification-standard-osteotomy-torque-isq-sr-ma]] explicitly flagged as unpoolable, and parallels the ≥ISQ-70-at-placement result of [[wiki/implants/osseodensification/ali-2026-osseodensification-techniques-implant-stability-maxilla]] (top qmd hit: [[wiki/implants/osseodensification/barbera-millan-2021-primary-stability-low-density-osseodensification]], in-vitro IT+ISQ OD win).

## Three-line Summary

Parallel-group double-blind RCT (n=20 patients/20 implants, OD n=10 vs CD n=10) in Misch D3/D4 posterior-maxilla sites; ISQ tracked from insertion through 12 months of loading, plus insertion torque, crestal bone loss and ICOI-based survival.

OD showed significantly higher ISQ post-insertion (72.7±3.1 vs 63.3±12, p=0.037) and through week 4 with no early stability dip (CD dipped during weeks 2–3), while survival (9/9 [100%] vs 8/10 [80%], p=0.474, OR 2.125), insertion torque (38.3±7.1 vs 30.6±9.4 Ncm, p=0.074) and 12-month crestal bone loss (median 1 [0.85–1.51] vs 1.018 [0.8–2.4] mm, p=0.962) showed no significant between-group difference.

OD produced earlier, more stable implant stability in the posterior maxilla without a crestal-bone penalty, which the authors interpret as suitable for early/immediate-loading candidates — but the small sample (n=20) and non-significant survival or torque differences cap certainty.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Double-blind parallel-group RCT (CONSORT-compliant, NCT04442763) comparing OD (Densah burs, CCW) vs conventional drilling (CD, clockwise) specifically in Misch D3/D4 posterior-maxilla bone, with ISQ monitored at 9 timepoints from insertion through 12 months of loading.
- OD achieved significantly higher ISQ than CD at insertion and during the first month (weeks 1–4), and showed no stability dip; CD exhibited the classic stability dip at weeks 2–3 before recovering.
- First report in this cohort of a survival point-estimate gap favoring OD (100% vs 80%, OR 2.125) — non-significant (p=0.474) but directionally favorable.
- Demonstrates that the early ISQ superiority and the OD survival trend coexist with equivalent crestal bone loss and insertion torque, narrowing OD's benefit to early stability rather than torque or bone-level outcomes.
- Adds longitudinal ISQ-through-loading data that prior SR+MA (marzorati-2026) could not pool, including a between-group convergence by weeks 6–8.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Parallel-group randomized clinical trial, 1:1 allocation, double-blind (participants, outcome assessors, statistician); surgeon unblinded by necessity. Simple computer-generated randomization (random.org), allocation concealed in opaque sealed envelopes.
- **n**: 20 patients/20 implants enrolled (OD n=10, CD n=10); 3 excluded from final analysis (1 OD dropout, 2 early CD failures) → 17 completed 12 months of loading (OD n=9, CD n=8); 4 M / 13 F, mean age 45.2 ± 7.4 y.
- **Sites**: maxillary premolar–molar, healed ≥6-month post-extraction ridges (width ≥6 mm, height ≥10 mm), D3/D4 bone by Misch classification (CBCT visual assessment). Exclusions: active periodontitis, parafunction, head/neck radiation, smoking, pregnancy/lactation.
- **Implants**: Neobiotech IS-II Active (SLA-coated, tapered, buttress threads, 0.8-mm crestal macro-thread pitch, platform-switching, conical/hex internal), diameter 3.5–4 mm, length 10–11.5 mm.
- **OD protocol**: pilot drill CW → Densah burs (Versah) CCW per densifying reference guide, 800–1200 rpm, pumping action, saline irrigation. CD: standard CW drills per manufacturer protocol.
- **Outcomes**: primary = ISQ (Osstell Mega ISQ, SmartPeg; buccal+mesial averaged, twice) at post-insertion, weeks 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 3 months, and 12 months post-loading; loading at 3 months only after ISQ ≥70. Secondary = insertion torque (N.cm, Neobiotech torque wrench), crestal bone loss (standardized parallel periapical radiographs, Digora, implant platform → first bone-to-implant contact, mesial+distal averaged) at post-insertion, 3 months, 6 and 12 months post-loading; survival by ICOI Pisa criteria.
- **Statistics**: repeated-measures ANOVA + Bonferroni (ISQ), Student's t-test (IT), Mann-Whitney U / Friedman+Dunn (CBL), Fisher's Exact (survival); significance P ≤ 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **ISQ**: OD post-insertion 72.7 ± 3.1 vs CD 63.3 ± 12 (p=0.037). OD stayed unchanged through week 3 (week-3 68.2 ± 4.7) then rose to 77.7 ± 1.5 at 12 months PL. CD dipped to 60.3 ± 6.3 (week 2) and 59.9 ± 4.6 (week 3) then recovered to 75.8 ± 3.5. Between-group significant at post-insertion, weeks 1–4 (p = 0.037 / 0.049 / 0.003 / 0.002 / 0.036); NS at weeks 6, 8, 3 months, 12 months (p = 0.145 / 0.119 / 0.237 / 0.155). Within-group change significant in both (p=0.001).
- **Insertion torque**: OD 38.3 ± 7.1 vs CD 30.6 ± 9.4 Ncm, p=0.074 (NS). IT ≥35 Ncm: 8/9 (88.9%) OD vs 4/8 (50%) CD.
- **Crestal bone loss (median [range], mm)**: post-insertion OD 0 (0,0.17) vs CD 0 (0,0.21), p=0.864; 3 months 0.72 (0.45,1.13) vs 0.6 (0.41,1.8), p=0.441; 6 months PL 0.77 (0.65,1.4) vs 0.853 (0.57,2.08), p=0.665; 12 months PL 1 (0.85,1.51) vs 1.018 (0.8,2.4), p=0.962. Intra-group increase significant in both (p<0.001). All implants <2 mm except one CD implant (2.4 mm).
- **Survival**: OD 9/9 (100%) vs CD 8/10 (80%), p=0.474, OR 2.125; 2 early osseointegration failures in CD.
- No adverse events or complications beyond the two CD failures.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=20; 17 analyzed); sample size computed for ISQ as primary outcome only.
- Eligibility restricted to medically healthy, non-smoking patients.
- Single implant type with restricted size range (3.5–4 × 10–11.5 mm); no quantitative bone-density measurement (CBCT visual grading only).
- Survival and torque differences were not statistically significant — the survival gap is a point estimate, not proof of a survival benefit.
- Female-predominant cohort (13/17) with possible influence on bone quality; authors argue uniform group representation limits this.
- Authors call for larger RCTs with quantitative bone-density assessment, varied implant designs/sizes and loading protocols.

## 7. Glossary

- **Osseodensification (OD)**: bone-preserving osteotomy technique using Densah burs rotating counterclockwise to condense/autograft bone into the osteotomy walls rather than removing it.
- **Conventional drilling (CD)**: standard subtractive osteotomy with clockwise-rotating drills per manufacturer protocol.
- **Implant Stability Quotient (ISQ)**: resonance-frequency-analysis-derived unit (0–100) of implant stability, measured here with Osstell Mega ISQ.
- **Stability dip**: the transient early-healing fall in implant stability secondary to bone remodeling around surgical trauma.
- **Insertion torque (IT)**: torque (N.cm) required to seat the implant, used as a primary-stability surrogate.
- **Crestal bone loss (CBL)**: radiographically measured loss of marginal bone from the implant platform to the first bone-to-implant contact.
