## Why Ingested

Directly addresses the clinical tradeoff in [[dental-materials/zirconia/koenig-2021-ltd-monolithic-zirconia-prospective]]: does adding cubic phase for translucency eliminate LTD risk? This in-vitro study categorizes four zirconia types by cubic content (0%, <30%, 30–50%, >50%) and tests hydrothermal aging resistance — the first study to draw the ">30% cubic = LTD-resistant" threshold.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (4 commercial Y-TZP groups by cubic content: T=0%, ST=<30%, XT=30–50%, P=>50%), autoclaved at 122°C/2 bar/8h: cubic-containing zirconia with >30% cubic (XT and P) showed NO monoclinic phase after aging and NO significant reduction in flexural strength or fracture toughness.

Groups T and ST showed significant decreases in hardness, flexural strength, and fracture toughness after aging (P<0.05), confirming LTD susceptibility at <30% cubic.

Conclusion: >30% cubic volume fraction is the threshold for high hydrothermal degradation resistance, but this comes at the cost of reduced fracture toughness and flexural strength compared to conventional 3Y-TZP.

## 세줄요약

4개 Y-TZP 군(큐빅 비율 0%, <30%, 30–50%, >50%), 122°C/2bar/8h 오토클레이브: >30% 큐빅(XT, P) → 에이징 후 단사정 미검출, 굴곡강도·파절인성 유의한 감소 없음.

<30% 큐빅(T, ST) → 에이징 후 경도·굴곡강도·파절인성 유의하게 감소(T군 가장 심함).

결론: >30% 큐빅 함량이 고내LTD성 임계값이나, 기계적 특성(굴곡강도·파절인성)이 기존 3Y-TZP 대비 감소함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Establishes **>30% cubic volume = threshold for LTD resistance** in translucent zirconia
- Demonstrates LTD does NOT occur in XT (30–50% cubic) and P (>50% cubic) under 122°C/8h autoclaving
- Shows the LTD-resistance tradeoff: cubic zirconia has significantly lower baseline FS and KIc than conventional 3Y-TZP
- Provides clinical recommendations by cubic category: >50% cubic → single anterior/premolar restorations; <30% cubic → multi-unit posterior FDPs

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 4 groups: T (conventional 3Y-TZP, 0% cubic), ST (<30% cubic), XT (30–50%), P (>50%)
- Aging: 122°C/2 bar/8h autoclave (TOMY ES-315); 1h aging ≈ 1 year clinical service (manufacturer claim)
- XRD (Philips X'Pert MPD): phase quantification before and after aging; monoclinic fraction by Garvie-Nicholson + Toraya equations
- Vickers microhardness (1 kg/10s), biaxial flexural strength (ISO 6872, piston-on-3-balls), fracture toughness (SENB method)
- SEM grain size characterization; statistics: Kruskal-Wallis + Wilcoxon (hardness) + Mann-Whitney U (FS, KIc)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | Cubic % | Monoclinic After Aging | FS Change After Aging | KIc Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T (3Y-TZP) | 0% | +16.45% | Significant decrease | Significant decrease |
| ST | <30% | +5.06% | Significant decrease | Significant decrease |
| XT | 30–50% | 0% (undetectable) | No significant change | No significant change |
| P | >50% | 0% (undetectable) | No significant change | No significant change |

- Grain sizes: T=1.42µm, ST=1.35µm, XT=1.57µm, P=2.28µm
- P group had largest grain size but still no LTD — contradicts grain-size-only hypothesis
- Cubic zirconia (>30%) had lower baseline FS (<500 MPa threshold crossed) and KIc (<3.5 MPa·m⁰·⁵)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-time-point aging (8h); long-term kinetics not assessed
- No optical/translucency measurements
- Clinical wear and fatigue not assessed
- Grain size/yttria content interaction not fully disentangled

## 7. Glossary

- **Y-TZP (이트리아 안정화 정방정 지르코니아 다결정체)**: 메타안정 정방정상 유지를 위해 YO₃ 첨가
- **Cubic phase (입방정상)**: 고농도 YO₃(>3mol%)에서 형성; 더 안정적이나 변태강화 능력 감소
- **LTD 임계값**: >30% 큐빅 → 수분에 의한 t→m 전환 억제
