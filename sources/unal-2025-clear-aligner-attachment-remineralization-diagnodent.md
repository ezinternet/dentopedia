## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (n=40, 4 groups) around clear-aligner attachments: over 6 months all remineralization protocols beat control (which worsened +4.35 DIAGNOdent), with nano-hydroxyapatite superior (−5.36) to CPP-ACP (−4.02) and quarterly fluoride varnish (−4.45);

pilot, non-randomized, huge effect size (η²=0.974).

## 1. Document Information
- BMC Oral Health 2025;26(1):96. PMC12801708. DOI 10.1186/s12903-025-07497-8. NCT07229105 (retrospectively registered).
- Bezmialem Vakif University, Istanbul, Aug 2024–Mar 2025. Prospective controlled clinical trial (exploratory pilot; non-randomized allocation by patient preference), STROBE-adapted.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First quantitative clinical assessment of demineralization specifically around clear-aligner ATTACHMENTS (a plaque-retentive site; anterior attachments are an independent WSL risk factor, OR=2.192; 35.5% WSL incidence in adolescents per Liu et al.).
- Direct 6-month head-to-head of three remineralization strategies vs standard fluoride toothpaste, monitored quantitatively with DIAGNOdent.
- Demonstrates the control group WORSENS (+42%) despite 1450 ppm F toothpaste → argues against passive monitoring in aligner therapy.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- 52 screened → 45 enrolled → 40 completed (100% post-baseline retention; 5 pre-baseline withdrawals). 4 groups, n=10 each, all using 1450 ppm F toothpaste as base.
  - A Control: fluoride toothpaste only.
  - B CPP-ACP (GC Tooth Mousse): pea-size in aligner 15 min nightly, no rinse 30 min.
  - C nHAp: professional 5% nHAp gel at T0/T1/T2 + daily home 1% nHAp gel in aligner (intensive dual protocol).
  - D Fluoride varnish: professional 5% NaF at baseline + 3 months.
- Outcome: DIAGNOdent pen, 4 margins/attachment, max of 3 passes, at T0/1/3/6 mo. Cut-offs 0–12 sound / 13–24 initial / ≥25 advanced. Intra-examiner ICC 0.85. Linear mixed-effects model (teeth nested in subjects), adjusted for baseline attachment number.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Δ DIAGNOdent (baseline→6 mo): Control +4.35 [3.77–4.93]; CPP-ACP −4.02 [−4.47,−3.56]; nHAp −5.36 [−5.99,−4.72]; Fluoride −4.45 [−4.78,−4.11]. All active vs control p<0.001.
- Group effect F=438.4, p<0.001, partial η²=0.974 (very large). Baseline attachment number NOT significant (p=0.470).
- Pairwise: nHAp > CPP-ACP (MD −1.34, p<0.001) and nHAp > fluoride (MD −0.91, p=0.019); CPP-ACP ≈ fluoride (p=0.375).
- Temporal: CPP-ACP and nHAp monotonic decline; fluoride varnish biphasic (dip → transient rise as reservoir depletes → drop after 3-mo re-application) → quarterly may be too infrequent for attachment sites.
- Subgroups: similar by age <18 vs ≥18 (p-int 0.84); posterior teeth recovered slightly more than anterior (−6.1 vs −4.6, p=0.02). No adverse events; home compliance >95%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Non-randomized allocation by preference (selection bias); small convenience sample (n=10/group, no a priori power — post-hoc power >0.99 driven by the large effect size); single center; no blinding (mitigated by objective DIAGNOdent); single examiner; Group C had a more intensive protocol (professional + home) than others; baseline attachment imbalance (statistically adjusted). Needs larger adequately-powered RCTs and an established MCID for DIAGNOdent change.

## 7. Glossary
- **Attachment**: composite resin bump bonded to enamel to grip the aligner; a plaque-retentive, high-WSL-risk site.
- **DIAGNOdent**: laser-fluorescence caries meter; 0–12 sound, 13–24 initial, ≥25 advanced demineralization on smooth surfaces.
- **nHAp**: nano-hydroxyapatite. **CPP-ACP**: casein phosphopeptide–amorphous calcium phosphate.
- **η² (partial eta-squared)**: effect-size measure; 0.974 = almost all outcome variance explained by group.
