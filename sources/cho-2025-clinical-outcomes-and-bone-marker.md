## Why Ingested

## Three-line Summary

Single-center prospective study at Yonsei University Dental Hospital (Seoul, Korea; April 2022 - May 2024) of 45 bone-level HEPES-surface implants in 36 postmenopausal women (mean age 68 years), split by lowest T-score into Group A (T-score of -2.0 or higher; 21 implants, 17 patients) and Group B (below -2.0; 24 implants, 19 patients), with one year of follow-up.

Implant survival was 100% with no failures (PubMed abstract; the full text reports no failures and ISQ never below 60), so early-versus-late failure timing cannot be analysed; mean marginal bone loss was 0.25-0.37 mm by group and jaw (no significant differences), implant stability stayed above 70 (IST), and change in T-score was -0.16 +/- 0.35 (A) versus -0.01 +/- 0.31 (B), p=0.244.

Authors call it exploratory: small sample, no multivariable adjustment, severe osteoporosis (T-score below -3) and grafted sites excluded, no antiresorptive therapy started during the year, Group B alone received vitamin D; patient nationality is not stated in the text and Korean origin is implied only by the setting.

## 세줄요약

한국 연세대학교치과병원에서 2022년 4월-2024년 5월 시행한 단일기관 전향적 연구: 폐경 후 여성 36명(평균 68세)에게 골수준형 HEPES 표면 임플란트 45개를 식립하고, 최저 T-score 기준으로 A군(-2.0 이상; 17명 21개)과 B군(-2.0 미만; 19명 24개)으로 나누어 1년간 추적했다.

임플란트 생존율은 100%로 실패가 없었으므로(PubMed 초록 기준; 본문도 실패 없음, ISQ 60 미만 없음) 조기 대 후기 실패 시점 분석은 불가하며, 평균 변연골 소실 (marginal bone loss, MBL)은 군·악골별 0.25-0.37 mm로 유의차가 없었고 임플란트 안정성 (IST)은 70 이상을 유지했으며 T-score 변화는 A군 -0.16 +/- 0.35, B군 -0.01 +/- 0.31 (p=0.244)였다.

저자들은 탐색적 연구라고 밝혔다: 소규모 표본, 다변량 보정 없음, 중증 골다공증(T-score -3 미만)과 골이식 필요 부위 제외, 1년간 골흡수억제제 (antiresorptive drug, ARD) 미시작, B군만 비타민 D 보충; 환자 국적은 본문에 명시되지 않았고 한국인이라는 점은 연구 장소로부터 추정될 뿐이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Prospective one-year study dividing postmenopausal implant patients by lowest T-score (hip, spine, femoral neck) with a threshold of -2.0 chosen to approximate the median T-score of the cohort, not the WHO osteoporosis cut-off.
- Pairs implant outcomes (MBL, ISQ/IST, probing depth, bleeding on probing) with systemic measures (BMD by DXA; CTx, P1NP, PTH, 25(OH) vitamin D).
- Reports no implant failures and stable BMD over one year when antiresorptive drugs were deliberately not started.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: single-center prospective study (STROBE), Yonsei University College of Dentistry IRB No. 2-2021-0116, written informed consent; April 2022 - May 2024; nine visits over one year.
- **Sample size**: G*Power for primary outcome MBL (alpha 0.05, power 0.8, effect size 0.8684) gave 36 with 10% dropout allowance; 40 assigned.
- **Recruitment**: 43 postmenopausal women screened; 3 with T-score below -3 and 3 needing additional bone grafts excluded; 37 enrolled (Fig.).
- **Analysed**: 45 implants in 36 patients (mean age 68, range 54-86); Group A 21 implants / 17 patients (mean 66, 54-79); Group B 24 implants / 19 patients (mean 70, 56-86). All non-smokers, all bone-level single crowns.
- **Implants**: Osstem SOI implants with HEPES surface treatment; one oral surgeon placed all implants, one prosthodontist did all prosthetics; second stage at 3 months.
- **Interventions**: Group B received preoperative vitamin D supplementation; Group A received dietary/lifestyle advice. No antiresorptive therapy initiated for one year.
- **Outcomes**: primary MBL (periapical radiographs for mesial/distal, CBCT for buccal/lingual; one calibrated examiner, measured twice, first measurement used); secondary ISQ and IST (visits 2, 4, 7, 8, 9), PPD, BOP, plaque, clinical attachment loss; success by Buser's criteria; BMD (DXA at lumbar spine, femoral neck, total hip), CTx, P1NP, PTH, 25(OH) vitamin D; whole-spine radiographs for vertebral fractures.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 28; Shapiro-Wilk/Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality; paired tests for before/after; p<0.05; ICC and Dahlberg error for reproducibility. No multivariable modelling.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample, exploratory; age and comorbidity differences prevented multivariate adjustment; residual confounding possible.
- Follow-up only one year; survival at one year says nothing about late failure.
- Could not recruit by WHO categories (T-score below -2.5 / -1 to -2.5); group threshold of -2.0 was chosen as the cohort median and is not a WHO class.
- Severe osteoporosis (T-score below -3) and sites needing bone graft excluded; findings apply to mild-to-moderate osteoporosis with sufficient bone. No antiresorptive therapy was given, so results do not extend to patients on bisphosphonates or other ARDs.
- Group B alone received vitamin D supplementation (acknowledged confounder of the vitamin D and P1NP differences).
- Drilling technique, bone quality at site, and prosthetic factors (loading timing, prosthesis, occlusion) not standardized; single examiner for MBL.
- In-paper inconsistencies: 43 screened minus 6 excluded gives 37 enrolled, but 36 patients analysed with no dropout explanation; MBL by group and jaw is 0.25-0.37 mm whereas the Discussion cites an average of 0.54 mm (the PubMed abstract gives 0.54 +/- 0.35 mm), without a stated pooling method; the Methods describe final prosthetic placement at visit 6 and crown installation at visit 7; the text says the T-score threshold "approximated the median" and is also described as dividing into osteoporosis groups; survival 100% and IST 71.4 +/- 5.52 appear only in the abstract, not in the Results body.
- Patient nationality/ethnicity not stated; generalization to other populations unknown.

## 7. Glossary

- **BMD (Bone Mineral Density, 골밀도)**: DXA-measured areal mineral density; T-score compares with young-adult mean.
- **BTM (Bone Turnover Marker, 골전환표지자)**: CTx (resorption) and P1NP (formation) in serum.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골 소실)**: bone loss below the implant thread, with gain recorded as zero.
- **ISQ / IST (Implant Stability Quotient / Implant Stability Tester, 임플란트 안정성 지수)**: resonance-frequency-based stability measures (Osstell, Anycheck).
- **ARD (Antiresorptive Drug, 골흡수억제제)**: e.g. bisphosphonates; none were started during the study.
- **HEPES surface**: hydroxyethyl piperazine ethane sulfonic acid surface treatment of the implants.
