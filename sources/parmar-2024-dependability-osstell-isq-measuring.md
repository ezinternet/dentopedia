## Why Ingested
이 논문은 Osstell ISQ 시스템의 반복 측정 신뢰도(repeatability)와 재현성(reproducibility)을 두 종류의 SmartPeg으로 교차 검증한 임상 데이터를 제공한다. 기존 위키 [[implants/isq/andersson-2019-rfa-factors-5year-neoss-survival]] 및 [[overviews/isq-loading-threshold]] 에서 ISQ 임계값 의사결정에 사용하는 측정 도구의 신뢰성 근거를 보강한다.

## One-line Summary
Cross-sectional study (n=60 implants, 18 patients) confirming excellent ICC=0.96 for Osstell ISQ with both SmartPeg Type I and II, supporting its reliability as a standardized measurement tool.

## 한줄요약
단면 연구(n=60 임플란트, 18명): SmartPeg I·II 모두 ICC=0.96, Osstell 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ) 측정의 우수한 반복성·재현성 확인.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional clinical reliability study (n=60 implants, 18 patients) assessing repeatability and reproducibility of the Osstell ISQ system using SmartPeg Types I and II, with 3 consecutive measurements per SmartPeg per implant (6 total), and ICC as the primary statistical outcome.

ICC = 0.96 for both SmartPeg Type I and II; mean ISQ stable at ~71 across sequential measurements; <3 ISQ variability found in 46.3% (Type I) and 58.6% (Type II) of cases.

A single Osstell ISQ reading is highly repeatable in a real clinical setting; both SmartPeg types are equivalent in reliability and interchangeable for routine loading-decision workflows.

## 세줄요약

임상 단면 신뢰도 연구(n=60 임플란트, 18명): SmartPeg I·II형 각 3회 연속 측정(총 6회/임플란트)으로 Osstell 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ) 반복성·재현성 평가, 급내상관계수(ICC) 산출.

SmartPeg I·II 모두 ICC=0.96; 평균 ISQ ~71로 안정; 46–59% 증례에서 측정간 차이 3 ISQ 미만.

임상 환경에서 Osstell ISQ 단회 측정은 매우 반복성이 높으며 SmartPeg 유형이 신뢰도에 영향 없음 — 부하 결정 시 SmartPeg I·II 구분 없이 사용 가능.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Validated ICC=0.96 for SmartPeg Type I and ICC=0.96 for SmartPeg Type II in clinical setting
- Mean ISQ values across 3 sequential measurements per SmartPeg were stable (~71 ISQ)
- Equivalent values or <3 ISQ variability found in 46.3% (Type I) and 58.6% (Type II) of cases
- Demonstrated that 3 consecutive measurements per SmartPeg capture full measurement precision
- Adds clinical-setting repeatability data (vs. controlled lab conditions in prior in-vitro work)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Cross-sectional
- Population: 18 patients, 60 implants total
- SmartPegs: Type I and Type II (Osstell, Integration Diagnostics)
- Protocol: 6 measurements per implant — 3 with SmartPeg I, 3 with SmartPeg II
- Statistical analysis: Intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC) for repeatability and reproducibility

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- SmartPeg I: Mean ISQ = 71.36, 71.31, 71.65 (measurements 1–3); ICC = 0.96
- SmartPeg II: Mean ISQ = 71.02, 71.58, 71.76 (measurements 1–3); ICC = 0.96
- Equivalent values or <3 ISQ difference: 46.3% (Type I), 58.6% (Type II)
- Repeatability coefficient: 0.96 (both SmartPegs)
- Overall conclusion: "nearly excellent" repeatability and reproducibility

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample: 18 patients, 60 implants; single-center
- No information on implant type, bone quality, or loading stage — confounders not controlled
- Only assessed at a single time point (no longitudinal stability tracking)
- Did not compare SmartPeg I vs II measurement equivalence quantitatively
- Bioinformation journal has modest IF (1.9); multi-institutional authorship with mixed affiliations

## 6. Related Work
- Osstell ISQ uses resonance frequency analysis (RFA) via magnetic pulse
- Measurement range: 0–100 ISQ; RF ~3000 Hz (ISQ=0) to ~8000 Hz (ISQ=100)
- Prior RFA repeatability studies reported similar ICC values in controlled settings

## 7. Glossary
- ICC (Intraclass Correlation Coefficient): statistical measure of reliability/agreement
- SmartPeg: disposable transducer attached to implant for Osstell ISQ measurement
- RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis): non-invasive implant stability measurement method
- ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient): proprietary 0–100 scale output of Osstell RFA
