## Why Ingested

Formalizes, with a symmetric two-arm random-effects meta-analysis, the direct(lateral-window)-vs-indirect(crestal/osteotome) technique comparison that [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/wallace-2003-effect-maxillary-sinus-augmentation-survival]] only informally stratified by subgroup — and its own pooled numbers (indirect 97.0% marginally exceeding direct 96.9%, difference not significant) independently echo [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/derbishi-2026-maxillary-sinus-augmentation-implant-survival-sr-ma]]'s crestal-vs-lateral pattern from a non-overlapping, four-years-earlier study set.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA, 17 studies, 1998–2020, 1,259 implants total) comparing implant survival between direct (lateral window) and indirect (osteotome/crestal, antral balloon, piezosurgery) maxillary sinus floor elevation techniques.

Pooled survival: direct 96.9% (95% CI 95.2–98.5%, 6 studies/337 implants/18 failures) vs indirect 97.0% (95% CI 95.9–98.1%, 13 studies/922 implants/35 failures) — no statistically significant difference.

Technique choice should follow clinical indication (residual bone height: <5 mm → lateral, ≥5 mm → crestal) rather than an expected survival advantage, since none was found; evidence base is limited to a single RCT among the 17 included studies.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA) — PRISMA 기반, 연구 17편 (1998–2020년), 총 임플란트 1,259개 — 직접법 (Direct, 측방창 (Lateral Window)) vs 간접법 (Indirect, 골절단기 (Osteotome)/치조정 (Crestal), 상악동막 풍선거상 (Antral Membrane Balloon Elevation, AMBE), 피에조서저리 (Piezosurgery)) 상악동거상술 (Maxillary Sinus Floor Elevation) 기법 간 임플란트 생존율 비교.

통합 생존율: 직접법 96.9% (95% 신뢰구간 (Confidence Interval, CI) 95.2–98.5%, 6개 연구/337개 임플란트/18개 실패) vs 간접법 97.0% (95% CI 95.9–98.1%, 13개 연구/922개 임플란트/35개 실패) — 통계적으로 유의한 차이 없음.

기법 선택은 기대 생존율 차이(발견되지 않음)가 아니라 임상적 적응증(잔존골높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) <5mm→측방법, ≥5mm→치조정법)을 따라야 하며, 근거기반은 포함된 17편 중 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)이 단 1편뿐이라는 한계가 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First page in this wiki to run a **formal, symmetric** random-effects binary-proportion meta-analysis on *both* arms of the direct-vs-indirect sinus-lift technique split (Wallace 2003 stratified by technique subgroup but did not formally test a direct-vs-indirect hypothesis).
- Both pooled estimates are tight and low-heterogeneity (I² = 0% for both arms), despite the underlying studies spanning 1998–2020 and multiple technique variants (osteotome, antral balloon elevation, piezosurgery within the indirect arm).
- Explicitly reconfirms the residual-bone-height (RBH) decision threshold already used elsewhere in this wiki's technique-selection literature: <5 mm → lateral (direct); ≥5 mm → crestal (indirect).
- Reports a sub-breakdown within the indirect arm: osteotome-only (9 studies) pooled 97.1% (95% CI 95.8–98.4%); piezosurgery (2 studies) 94.6% and 98.5% individually; antral membrane balloon elevation (2 studies) 95% and 100% individually — not pooled separately, but consistent with the overall indirect estimate.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis, PRISMA-guided, PICOS framework (P = edentulous posterior maxilla requiring SFE; I = maxillary SFE for implant placement; C = different SFE techniques; O = implant survival rate; S = RCT/prospective/retrospective/clinical studies).
- **Databases**: PubMed, Medline, Embase, Cochrane (search window 1997–2020, per Methods text; abstract additionally lists Google Scholar and ScienceDirect) + hand search.
- **Screening**: 895 records → 60 by title → 37 by abstract → 19 full-text → 17 final (2 excluded post-full-text-read for inadequate data/inclusion mismatch).
- **n**: 17 studies (1998–2020) — 1 RCT (Kfir et al. 2009), remainder prospective/retrospective/clinical-report designs. Total 1,259 implants (337 direct / 922 indirect).
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB tool (the 1 RCT) + Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (non-RCTs) — most rated "Good," 2 "Fair."
- **Statistics**: RevMan 5.4; binary random-effects proportion meta-analysis with forest plots, separately for direct approach, indirect-osteotome subset, and indirect-overall.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Arm | Studies | Implants | Failures | Pooled survival | 95% CI | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direct (lateral window) | 6 | 337 | 18 | **96.9%** | 95.2–98.5% | 0% |
| Indirect (all: osteotome + AMBE + piezo) | 13 | 922 | 35 | **97.0%** | 95.9–98.1% | 0% |
| Indirect — osteotome only (subset) | 9 | — | — | 97.1% | 95.8–98.4% | 0% |

- Simple (unweighted) arithmetic mean of individual study survival rates: direct 95.98% (Table 7), indirect 95.79% (Table 8) — reported separately from the pooled proportion meta-analysis, same qualitative conclusion.
- **No statistically significant difference** between direct and indirect approaches (both point estimates and CIs overlap almost entirely).
- Aside (single embedded primary study, not a Shah-level meta-analyzed moderator): Bornstein et al. 2008, within the direct/lateral arm, reported SLA-surface implants at 100% survival vs TPS-surface implants at 89%.
- Note: the abstract's printed forest-plot statistics ("P = 0.688 ... 95% CI 0.9691"; "P = 0.686 ... 95% CI 0.970") are a PDF-extraction rendering artifact — cross-checked against Tables 4–6, these are actually the **heterogeneity P-values** (0.688 direct / 0.686 indirect-overall) run together with the **pooled proportion estimates** (0.969 / 0.970) in the source PDF's text layer. The Results-section and Table 4–8 figures used throughout this page are the disambiguated, verified numbers.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 1 of 17 included studies is an RCT (Kfir et al. 2009); the rest are prospective/retrospective cohorts or clinical case-report-tier studies — authors themselves call for more RCTs.
- Follow-up limited to the inclusion window (≥6 months to ≤5 years); no long-term (>5–10 yr) survival data captured.
- Heterogeneous implant systems, surface treatments, and grafting materials pooled within each technique arm without moderator meta-analysis (contrast with Wallace 2003, which meta-regressed graft form/membrane/surface as covariates — Shah does not).
- The direct-vs-indirect comparison aggregates separately-reported cohorts rather than randomizing technique within a matched population; confounding by indication (patients selected for lateral window typically have less residual bone / worse baseline anatomy than crestal-approach patients) is plausible but not discussed by the authors — the same confound Derbishi 2026 explicitly flags for its own crestal-vs-lateral gap.
- Minor internal inconsistency in the source text's own study-count breakdown: the Introduction states "nine clinical studies, five prospective, two retrospective studies, and one RCT" (9+5+2+1=17) while the Discussion states "Nine clinical studies, six prospective, and two retrospective studies" (9+6+2=17, RCT unmentioned) — arithmetic reconciles to 17 either way but the design-label breakdown differs by one study; transcribed as-is rather than silently corrected.

## 7. Glossary

- **SFE**: Sinus Floor Elevation.
- **RBH**: Residual Bone Height — the deciding factor between lateral (direct, RBH <5 mm) and crestal (indirect, RBH ≥5 mm) approach in this paper.
- **DI / IN**: Direct / Indirect (this paper's own abbreviations for lateral-window vs osteotome-family approaches).
- **AMBE**: Antral Membrane Balloon Elevation — a closed/indirect technique variant using balloon dilation through the osteotomy site.
- **PICOS**: Population, Intervention, Comparison, Outcome, Study design — the search-strategy framework used.
- **NOS**: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale — risk-of-bias tool applied to the 16 non-RCT studies.
