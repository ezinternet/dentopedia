## Why Ingested

[[orthodontics/clear-aligner/charoenrat-2025-clear-aligner-anterior-open-bite-molar-intrusion-sr-ma]]는 전치부 개방교합과 구치 압하를 다뤘으나, **깊은교합(deep bite) 교정에서의 투명교정 효과와 정확도를 종합한 SR**이 없었다. 본 논문은 18개 연구를 통해 교합 거상 효과(0.4–3.8 mm) 및 정확도(33–48.88%)를 정량화한다.

## One-line Summary

SR, 18 studies: clear aligners open deep bite 0.4–3.8 mm with 33–48.88% accuracy; effective for dentoalveolar cases, but low-quality evidence and inferior to fixed appliances for skeletal cases.

## 한줄요약

SR, 18개 연구: 투명교정의 깊은교합 교정 효과는 0.4–3.8 mm, 정확도 33–48.88%(GRADE 저급 근거); 치조성 깊은교합에는 유효하나 골격성 증례에서 고정식 대비 우위 불확실.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Comprehensive SR distinguishing effectiveness (achieved opening) from accuracy (predicted vs achieved)
- Documents wide range: effectiveness 0.4–3.8 mm, accuracy 33–48.88%
- GRADE: low quality evidence due to high risk of bias, indirectness, imprecision
- CA recommended for mild-moderate dentoalveolar deep bites; skeletal cases remain uncertain vs FFA
- Overcorrection and refinement identified as necessary to achieve target outcomes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review (ROBIN-I for risk of bias)
- Databases: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane, grey literature (inception to May 2024)
- Population: all age groups with dental/skeletal deep bite treated with clear aligners
- Studies included: 18 (8 effectiveness, 8 accuracy, 2 both)
- Outcomes: achieved vs planned bite opening (mm), accuracy (%), comparison to FFA

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Effectiveness (bite opening range): 0.4–3.8 mm across studies
- Accuracy (deep bite correction): 33–48.88% across all studies
- GRADE evidence quality: low (effectiveness), low (accuracy)
- Primary mechanism: anterior intrusion and/or posterior extrusion
- CA vs FFA for skeletal deep bite: insufficient evidence for superiority
- Clinical implication: overcorrection or refinements necessary for target outcomes

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High risk of bias across included studies (ROBIN-I)
- Indirectness and imprecision reduced GRADE certainty
- Methodological variability (measurement methods, protocols) prevented pooling
- Recommend standardized outcome measures and RCT designs
- No long-term stability data available

## 7. Glossary

- FFA: Full-Fixed Appliance (conventional braces)
- ROBIN-I: Risk Of Bias In Non-randomised Studies of Interventions
- GRADE: Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development, and Evaluation
- Effectiveness: actual achieved correction amount
- Accuracy: ratio of achieved to planned correction (%)
