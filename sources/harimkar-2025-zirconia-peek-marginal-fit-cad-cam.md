## Why Ingested

Systematic review and meta-analysis directly comparing marginal fit of zirconia vs. PEEK copings fabricated by CAD/CAM — provides synthesized in-vitro evidence on a clinically important outcome (marginal adaptation) for two materials increasingly used in fixed prosthodontics. Fills a gap where individual studies gave conflicting results.

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis included 8 in vitro studies (2019–2025) comparing marginal fit of zirconia and PEEK copings fabricated by CAD/CAM technology. Zirconia demonstrated superior marginal adaptation in 6 of 8 studies, with reported gaps of 17–60 µm, while PEEK ranged 30–108 µm depending on processing method (milled PEEK consistently outperforming pressed/granular variants). The pooled meta-analysis of 5 studies (n=126) showed a non-significant SMD of 1.72 µm (95% CI: −0.46 to 3.90, p=0.12) with high heterogeneity (I²=95%); both materials remained within the clinically acceptable threshold of <120 µm.

## 세줄요약

지르코니아(Zirconia)와 폴리에테르에테르케톤 (Polyetheretherketone, PEEK) 코핑(coping)의 변연적합도 (marginal fit)를 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석으로, 8편의 체외 연구(2019–2025)를 포함. 지르코니아가 8편 중 6편에서 우수한 변연적합도를 나타냈으며, 밀링 PEEK는 프레스·과립형보다 일관되게 양호한 결과를 보였다. 5편 메타분석(n=126)의 표준화 평균차 (Standardized Mean Difference, SMD) = 1.72 µm (95% CI: −0.46 ~ 3.90, p=0.12)로 통계적 유의성 없었고, I²=95%의 높은 이질성이 관찰됨; 두 재료 모두 임상적 허용 기준 (<120 µm) 내.

## Key Contributions

1. First meta-analysis directly pooling zirconia vs. PEEK coping marginal fit data from multiple in vitro studies.
2. Demonstrates that CAD/CAM-milled PEEK consistently outperforms non-milled PEEK forms (pressed, granular) in marginal accuracy.
3. Confirms both materials achieve clinically acceptable marginal adaptation (<120 µm threshold) across all included studies.
4. Quantifies high methodological heterogeneity (I²=95%) as the primary obstacle to definitive material recommendations.
5. Provides a QUIN-based risk-of-bias assessment: 2 studies low risk, 6 studies moderate risk; commonest gaps were absent randomization and lack of assessor blinding.

## Methodology

- **Search databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar, Embase, EBSCOHost (2016–2025)
- **Keywords**: "Zirconia", "PEEK", "Copings", "Marginal fit" (Boolean combinations)
- **Eligibility**: In vitro studies on CAD/CAM-fabricated zirconia AND PEEK full-coverage copings; quantitative marginal gap values required; full English text
- **Exclusion**: Clinical studies, animal/case-report/review designs; non-CAD/CAM fabrication; single-material studies
- **Measurement techniques used across included studies**: FE-SEM (n=2), stereomicroscopy (n=2), digital microscopy with ImageJ (n=2), 3D triple-scan / PolyWorks (n=1), handheld digital microscope (n=1)
- **Measurement points per sample**: 4–20 points (buccal/lingual/mesial/distal)
- **Statistical tool**: RevMan (Cochrane); SMD as effect size; random-effects model (I²>50%)
- **Risk of bias**: QUIN tool (9 domains)
- **Substrates in included studies**: extracted human teeth (n=4), typodont/resin/stainless steel dies (n=4)
- **CAD software used**: Exocad, Dental Wings DWOS, CEREC, SHERA eco-scan, SHINING 3D
- **Milling units used**: Roland DWX series, VHF K5, Arum 5X-300D, SHERA eco-mill (all five-axis except one 4-axis unit)

## Key Results

**Individual study marginal gap values:**

| Study | PEEK (µm) | Zirconia (µm) | Favors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attia 2019 | Milled: 45.22±6.09; Pellets: 72.38; Granules: 78.69 | 43.98±1.3 | Zirconia |
| Emad 2020 | 49.88±7.97 | 18.39±3.1 | Zirconia |
| Makky 2020 | 69±10 | 60±10 | Zirconia |
| Amalorpavam 2021 | 30.3±5.1 | 50.26±16.02 | PEEK |
| Chouksey 2023 | 33.99±8.81 | 56.21±15.07 | PEEK |
| Emam 2023 | CAD: 108.00±20.08; Pressed: 108.00±25.10 | 48.67±11.98 | Zirconia |
| Mahajan 2024 | Palatal: 23.9; Buccal: 22.3 | Palatal: 18.5; Buccal: 17.1 | Zirconia |
| Jangade 2025 | 92.84±3.48 | 63.12±31.47 | Zirconia |

**Meta-analysis (5 studies, n=126 samples, 63 per group):**
- Pooled SMD: 1.72 µm (95% CI: −0.46 to 3.90), favoring PEEK by direction
- Statistical significance: Z=1.55, p=0.12 — NOT significant
- Heterogeneity: Tau²=5.76, Chi²=79.78, df=4, p<0.00001, I²=95%
- Funnel plot: minor asymmetry, attributed to methodological heterogeneity (not publication bias)

**Risk of bias:** 2/8 low risk (Attia 2019, Amalorpavam 2021); 6/8 moderate risk. Main methodological gaps: absent randomization (6/8 studies), no blinding of assessors (5/8 studies).

## Limitations

- All 8 studies are in vitro — no thermal cycling, mechanical loading, or salivary exposure; no aging simulation
- Half the studies used artificial substrates (typodont, resin, stainless steel dies) vs. natural teeth
- Internal fit was not assessed in any included study
- Sample size calculation inconsistently reported; randomization and blinding frequently absent
- Heterogeneity (I²=95%) prevents definitive pooled conclusions
- Geographic clustering (India + Egypt only) may limit generalizability

## Glossary

- **변연적합도 (Marginal Fit, MF)**: gap between restoration margin and tooth finish line; clinically acceptable threshold <120 µm (Holmes criterion widely cited)
- **폴리에테르에테르케톤 (Polyetheretherketone, PEEK)**: high-performance thermoplastic polymer with bone-like elastic modulus (~3–4 GPa); increasingly used in provisional and definitive restorations
- **지르코니아 (Zirconia, ZrO₂)**: high-strength ceramic; transformation-toughening mechanism; sintering shrinkage (~25%) is key dimensional challenge
- **컴퓨터 보조 설계/제조 (Computer-Aided Design/Manufacturing, CAD/CAM)**: digital workflow for restoration fabrication using scanning, design software, and CNC milling
- **표준화 평균차 (Standardized Mean Difference, SMD)**: effect size metric for meta-analysis when outcomes share the same construct but different scales
- **이질성 (Heterogeneity, I²)**: proportion of variance in meta-analysis due to between-study differences rather than chance; I²=95% indicates very high heterogeneity
- **QUIN tool**: Quality Assessment Tool for In Vitro Studies; 9-domain risk-of-bias instrument for laboratory research
