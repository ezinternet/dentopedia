## Why Ingested

Prospective cohort of transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE) with simultaneous implant placement stratified by Schneiderian membrane status (normal, primary, odontogenic thickening); reports zero early implant failure alongside higher membrane perforation and poorer bone gain in odontogenic thickening. Adds a membrane-pathology risk modifier to the early-failure and membrane-perforation literature. → [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/huang-2026-graftless-transcrestal-sinus-membrane-perforation-ma]]; see also [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort of 89 patients (123 implants) undergoing transcrestal sinus floor elevation with simultaneous implant placement, divided into normal, primary pathological thickening (PPT) and odontogenic pathological thickening (OPT) membrane groups, with CBCT at baseline, immediately post-op and 6 months.

No early implant failure or postoperative complications occurred and ISQ did not differ among groups (p = 0.116); OPT had a higher membrane perforation rate (30.4% vs 9.5% normal and 7.7% PPT, p = 0.023), lower endo-sinus bone gain (2.88 ± 1.59 vs 4.02 ± 1.63 mm normal, p = 0.015) and higher graft resorption height (2.98 ± 1.84 vs 1.37 ± 1.15 mm normal and 1.41 ± 1.26 mm PPT, p < 0.001).

PPT behaved like normal membrane; OPT raises perforation risk and reduces new bone formation without affecting early implant success; evidence is from an abstract only, with small groups and no failure events.

## 세줄요약

환자 89명 (임플란트 123개)을 정상, 원발성 병적 비후 (primary pathological thickening, PPT), 치성 병적 비후 (odontogenic pathological thickening, OPT) 군으로 나눠 임플란트 동시식립 경치조 상악동거상술 (transcrestal sinus floor elevation, TSFE)을 시행한 전향적 코호트로, CBCT를 술전, 술직후, 6개월에 촬영.

초기 임플란트 실패와 술후 합병증은 없었고 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)도 군간 차이가 없었다 (p = 0.116); OPT군은 막 천공률이 30.4%로 정상군 9.5%, PPT군 7.7%보다 높았고 (p = 0.023), 상악동 내 골 증가량은 2.88 ± 1.59 mm로 정상군 4.02 ± 1.63 mm보다 낮았으며 (p = 0.015), 이식재 흡수 높이는 2.98 ± 1.84 mm로 정상군 1.37 ± 1.15 mm, PPT군 1.41 ± 1.26 mm보다 컸다 (p < 0.001).

PPT는 정상 막과 유사했고 OPT는 천공 위험을 높이고 신생골 형성을 줄이지만 초기 임플란트 성공에는 영향이 없었다; 초록 기반이며 군 규모가 작고 실패 사례가 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Separates primary (PPT) from odontogenic (OPT) membrane thickening, rather than lumping pathological thickening together.
- Zero early implant failure across 123 simultaneously placed implants in all three groups.
- OPT associated with higher perforation rate (30.4%) and poorer endo-sinus bone gain and graft stability.
- PPT showed no significant difference from normal membrane in ESBG or GMRH (p > 0.05).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective cohort study; setting and country not stated in the abstract.
- **Population**: 89 patients, 123 implants; groups normal, PPT, OPT (group sizes not stated in the abstract).
- **Intervention**: TSFE with simultaneous implant placement in all patients.
- **Outcomes**: Schneiderian membrane perforation, early implant failure, postoperative complications, ISQ; CBCT linear measurements preoperatively, immediately post-op and at 6 months: Schneiderian membrane thickness (SMT), residual bone height (RBH), sinus floor elevation height (SFEH), endo-sinus bone gain (ESBG), graft material resorption height (GMRH).
- **Early failure definition**: the time window is not specified in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Early implant failure | None observed |
| Postoperative complications | None observed |
| ISQ among groups | No significant difference (p = 0.116) |
| Membrane perforation | OPT 30.4% vs normal 9.5% vs PPT 7.7% (p = 0.023) |
| ESBG | OPT 2.88 ± 1.59 mm vs normal 4.02 ± 1.63 mm (p = 0.015) |
| GMRH | OPT 2.98 ± 1.84 mm vs normal 1.37 ± 1.15 mm and PPT 1.41 ± 1.26 mm (p < 0.001) |
| PPT vs normal | No significant ESBG/GMRH difference (p > 0.05) |

Early-failure rate 0 of 123 implants; no risk-factor effect sizes (odds ratios) for failure can be derived since no failures occurred.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract only: group sizes, follow-up beyond 6 months, definition and window of early failure, graft material and operator details not available.
- Zero failure events make the early-success conclusion uninformative about risk-factor effects; the authors' conclusion is limited to early success.
- Only 123 implants; perforation percentages in groups are based on unstated denominators.
- Single-arm-per-group prospective cohort, not randomized.
