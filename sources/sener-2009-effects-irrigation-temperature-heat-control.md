## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/aquilanti-2023-heat-generation-initial-osteotomy]]가 "저온 saline(10°C)이 발열을 유의하게 줄인다"는 결과를 냈는데, 이 결론의 초기 근거가 된 고전 논문(2009)이 위키에 없어 추가한다. Sener 2009는 depth별(3/7/12mm) 발열 프로파일을 처음 정량화한 in vitro 연구로, [[implants/bernabeu-mira-2020-bone-heating-drilling-implant]]·[[implants/woods-2022-does-irrigating-while-drilling-decrease]]가 반복 확인한 "irrigation이 발열을 낮춘다" 원칙의 초기 실증 근거를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro bovine-mandible drilling study (Clin Oral Implants Res 2009) measuring bone temperature at 3, 7, and 12 mm depths via thermoresistors, comparing no irrigation vs external irrigation with 25°C and 10°C saline.

Without irrigation, peak temperatures were 50.9°C (3 mm), 47.4°C (7 mm), and 38.1°C (12 mm); with irrigation, all measurements stayed below body temperature except at 12 mm depth (37.4°C at 25°C saline, 36.3°C at 10°C saline), and colder saline produced lower peak temperatures at every depth.

More heat is generated in the superficial part of the osteotomy than at the bottom, so room-temperature external irrigation is generally sufficient, but colder saline is more effective and irrigation should continue between drilling steps — abstract-only (publisher landing page), full methodology/statistics not retrieved.

## 세줄요약

인비트로 소 하악골 드릴링 연구(Clin Oral Implants Res 2009) — 3/7/12mm 깊이에서 thermoresistor로 골온도 측정, 무관개 vs 25°C·10°C 생리식염수 외부관개(external irrigation) 비교.

무관개 시 최고온도는 3mm 50.9°C, 7mm 47.4°C, 12mm 38.1°C였고, 관개 시 12mm를 제외한 모든 지점에서 체온 이하로 유지되었으며(25°C 관개 시 37.4°C, 10°C 관개 시 36.3°C @ 12mm), 저온 식염수일수록 발열이 낮았다.

드릴링 초입부(superficial)가 바닥부(deep)보다 발열이 크므로 상온 외부관개로도 충분하지만, 저온 식염수가 더 효과적이며 드릴링 단계 사이에도 관개를 지속해야 한다 — 초록만 확보(출판사 랜딩페이지), 전문 방법론·통계 미확보.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Implants Research 2009;20(3):294-298
- **DOI**: 10.1111/j.1600-0501.2008.01643.x
- **Institution**: Faculty of Dentistry, Department of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, Marmara University, Istanbul, Turkey

## 2. Key Contributions
- Early depth-stratified quantification (3, 7, 12 mm) of bone drilling temperature during implant osteotomy.
- Direct comparison of two irrigation saline temperatures (25°C vs 10°C) against no-irrigation control.
- Established that superficial drilling generates more heat than deep drilling — a pattern later echoed by [[implants/bernabeu-mira-2020-bone-heating-drilling-implant]] and [[implants/aquilanti-2023-heat-generation-initial-osteotomy]].

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: in vitro experimental (ex vivo bovine bone model)
- **Specimens**: fresh frozen edentulous bovine mandible segments, sectioned into 12 × 6 cm pieces
- **Instrumentation**: thermoresistors placed 0.5 mm from drilling cavity walls at 3, 7, and 12 mm depths; signals analyzed with ORIGIN 5.0 software
- **Conditions compared**: no irrigation; external irrigation with 25°C saline; external irrigation with 10°C saline
- **Outcomes**: maximum bone temperature at each depth per condition

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- No irrigation: 50.9°C (3 mm), 47.4°C (7 mm), 38.1°C (12 mm) — the two shallower depths reach/exceed the classic 47°C/1 min necrosis threshold.
- With irrigation (25°C and 10°C saline): all depths below body temperature except 12 mm (37.4°C and 36.3°C respectively).
- Colder saline (10°C) consistently produced lower peak temperatures than 25°C saline at the deepest measurement point.
- Full statistical testing (p-values, variance) not available from the retrieved abstract-only text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only source — full Methods/Results/Discussion, sample size (number of drilling runs), and statistical analysis not retrieved (publisher paywall landing page).
- Ex vivo bovine mandible model — vascularity/perfusion cooling effect absent, unlike in vivo bone.
- Only three depths and two irrigation temperatures tested; drill design, rotational speed, and feed force not detailed in the abstract.

## 6. Related Work
- [[implants/aquilanti-2023-heat-generation-initial-osteotomy]]: independently reconfirms (2023, rotary + piezo comparison) that 10°C saline significantly reduces peak temperature vs warmer saline — direct reinforcement of Sener's 2009 finding.
- [[implants/woods-2022-does-irrigating-while-drilling-decrease]]: in vivo canine model confirms saline irrigation reduces both temperature rise and histologic osteonecrosis, generalizing Sener's in vitro irrigation-benefit finding.
- [[implants/bernabeu-mira-2020-bone-heating-drilling-implant]]: found initial/shallow drilling generates more heat than deeper stages, paralleling Sener's superficial > deep gradient.

## 7. Glossary
- **Thermoresistor**: a temperature-sensitive resistor used as a contact thermal sensor; here placed 0.5 mm from the osteotomy wall to record real-time bone temperature.
- **External irrigation**: coolant (saline) delivered onto the drill/bone surface from outside the drill body, as opposed to internal (through-the-drill) irrigation.
- **Osteotomy**: the surgical drilling procedure that creates the implant bed in bone.
