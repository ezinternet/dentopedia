## Why Ingested

Long-term retrospective (3–9 years, 105 implants) establishing optimal IPL range for non-grafting OSFE — provides the best available long-term evidence for IPL ceiling effect (<4 mm) complementing [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/suk-arj-2019-osfe-without-grafting-bone-cbct]] and [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/albash-2023-implant-protrusion-initial-bone-tsfe]].

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study (105 implants in 65 patients; mean follow-up 5.04 years, range 3–9 years; Sun Yat-sen University) examining the influence of implant protrusion length (IPL) on endo-sinus bone gain (ESBG), marginal bone loss (MBL), and bone-to-implant contact length (BICL) after non-grafting osteotome sinus floor elevation, with IPL grouped as <2 mm, 2–<4 mm, and ≥4 mm.

Cumulative survival rate was 96.4% at mean 5.04 years; mean ESBG was 1.95±0.88 mm and mean MBL was 0.58±0.68 mm; ESBG correlated positively with IPL across all groups, but bone formation efficiency (% ESBG per unit IPL) decreased significantly when IPL ≥4 mm (p=0.02), confirmed by multiple linear regression identifying IPL as the primary ESBG predictor.

Optimal IPL for non-grafting OSFE is within 4 mm; beyond this threshold, incremental bone gain per additional millimeter of protrusion diminishes significantly, making IPL <4 mm the recommended clinical target for long-term predictability.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 돌출 길이(IPL)가 무이식 골절도 상악동거상술(OSFE) 후 상악동내 골증가량(ESBG)·변연골 소실(MBL)·골-임플란트 접촉 길이(BICL)에 미치는 영향을 평가한 후향적 코호트(105개 임플란트/65명, 평균 5.04년 추적; 쑨이셴대), IPL 기준을 <2mm / 2–<4mm / ≥4mm 3군으로 분류.

누적 생존율 96.4%(평균 5.04년); 평균 ESBG 1.95±0.88mm, 평균 MBL 0.58±0.68mm; ESBG는 전 군에서 IPL과 양의 상관이었으나 IPL ≥4mm 시 골형성 효율(%ESBG/IPL)이 유의하게 감소(p=0.02); 다중선형회귀에서 IPL이 ESBG의 주 예측인자로 확인.

무이식 OSFE 최적 IPL 기준은 4mm 이내; 이 기준을 초과하면 돌출 단위당 추가 골증가 효율이 유의하게 감소하므로 IPL <4mm가 장기 예측 가능성을 위한 임상 목표치임.

## One-line Summary

Retrospective (n=105 implants, 65 patients, mean follow-up 5.04 years): optimal IPL <4mm for non-grafting OSFE; ESBG positively correlated with IPL but bone formation efficiency significantly decreases when IPL >4mm (p=0.02); cumulative survival rate 96.4%.

## 한줄요약

후향적 연구(105개 임플란트, 65명, 평균 추적 5.04년): 무이식 OSFE 최적 임플란트 돌출 길이(IPL) <4mm; ESBG는 IPL과 양의 상관관계이나 IPL>4mm 시 골형성 효율이 유의하게 감소(p=0.02); 누적 생존율 96.4%.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: International Journal of Implant Dentistry, 7:22
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s40729-021-00304-3
- **Institution**: Sun Yat-sen University, Guangzhou, China (Hospital of Stomatology)
- **Study design**: Retrospective cohort study
- **n**: 105 implants, 65 patients; 3–9 years follow-up (mean 5.04 years)
- **IPL groups**: Group 1 (<2mm), Group 2 (2–<4mm), Group 3 (≥4mm)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Cumulative survival rates: 96.4% (implant-based), 94.1% (patient-based)
- Mean ESBG at latest follow-up: 1.95 ± 0.88 mm
- Mean MBL: 0.58 ± 0.68 mm
- Mean BICL (bone to implant contact length): 5.51 ± 1.47 mm
- ESBG positively correlated with IPL in all groups
- Significant decrease in bone formation efficiency (% ESBG/IPL) when IPL ≥4mm (p=0.02)
- Recommended optimal IPL: within 4 mm for better long-term outcomes
- Multiple linear regression: IPL was the primary influencer of ESBG

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective; Sun Yat-sen University Hospital of Stomatology
- **IPL groups**: G1 (IPL<2mm), G2 (2mm≤IPL<4mm), G3 (IPL≥4mm)
- **Outcomes**: ESBG, MBL, BICL, %ESBG at latest follow-up
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier for survival; multiple linear regression (ESBG vs covariates); ANOVA (IPL groups vs ESBG/MBL/BICL/%ESBG)
- **Failure criteria**: Implant removal for mobility, intractable pain, or peri-implantitis

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Implants (patients) | 102/62 fulfilling survival criteria |
| Cumulative survival (implant-based) | 96.4% |
| Cumulative survival (patient-based) | 94.1% |
| Mean ESBG | 1.95 ± 0.88 mm |
| Mean MBL | 0.58 ± 0.68 mm |
| Mean BICL | 5.51 ± 1.47 mm |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective single-center design
- No graft control arm
- 3 implants excluded (failed) — reduced final n to 102
- No standardized implant type/diameter
- RBH range not clearly stated

## 6. Related Work

- Suk-Arj 2019: CBCT 6-month, IPL 2.02mm; positive IPL-ESBG correlation; same trend
- Albash 2023: TSFE 2-year, 34 implants; IPL critical factor; no IBH correlation
- Duan 2017: SR+MA graft-free transcrestal; synthesis of bone formation data across studies

## 7. Glossary

- **ESBG (Endo-Sinus Bone Gain, 상악동내 골 증가량)**: net new bone height gained within sinus post-SFE
- **BICL (Bone-to-Implant Contact Length, 골-임플란트 접촉 길이)**: length of implant surface in direct contact with bone
- **%ESBG**: ESBG normalized to IPL — measures bone formation efficiency per unit of implant protrusion
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골 소실)**: peri-implant crestal bone loss
