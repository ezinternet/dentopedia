## Why Ingested

[[bone-regeneration/wang-2025-porcine-bovine-xenograft-msfa-arp-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 10 studies / 452 sites) concluded porcine xenograft (PBX) is clinically comparable to bovine xenograft (BBX) in maxillary sinus floor augmentation (MSFA) and alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) — but explicitly flagged that "further well-designed RCTs" with graft-resorption data are still needed. This split-mouth randomized controlled trial is exactly that missing human randomized head-to-head, and it adds the tissue-biology layer (histomorphometry, immunohistochemistry, TEM, mRNA) that the survival-oriented review [[sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]] does not cover. It also directly addresses the porcine-as-bovine-alternative axis that the DBBM synthesis overview tracks as a material-selection question ([[overviews/dbbm-bone-substitute-overview]]).

## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth randomized controlled trial (University of Granada, Spain; 10 patients / 20 sinuses; January 2019–March 2021) comparing autologous cortical bone (ACB) mixed 20:80 with anorganic bovine bone (ABB, Bio-Oss Spongiosa 250–1000 µm) versus porcine bone mineral (PBM, Symbios Xenograft granules 0.25–1.0 mm) in bilateral staged lateral-window maxillary sinus floor augmentation, evaluated by CBCT, trephine-biopsy histology/histomorphometry, immunohistochemistry, TEM and mRNA at 6 months.

No group difference in any radiographic, histomorphometric or most immunohistochemical outcome — grafted-area mineralized tissue 29.24% (ABB) vs 32.51% (PBM) (p=.570), remnant biomaterial 31.00% vs 31.21% (p>.999), similar vertical graft resorption; the only between-group signals were more osteoid lines in ACB+ABB (5.89 vs 2.50 per mm², p=.031), higher ALP mRNA in ACB+PBM (2.13 vs 1.00, p=.011), and more osteocytes/leukocytes/vessels in ACB+ABB (CD44 p=.031, CD45 p=.047, CD34 p=.031).

Clinical implication: a porcine xenograft behaves like DBBM in sinus augmentation, so it is a defensible alternative where bovine material is undesirable — but the trial enrolled 10 patients against its own calculated requirement of 18, reported no implant-survival endpoint, and lost the registered 12- and 18-month CBCT re-measurements to COVID-19, so long-term remodeling and resorption remain unproven.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **First complete clinical dataset on Symbios® Xenograft granules.** The authors state no prior clinical study had published biologic response data for this porcine-derived product.
- **Randomized split-mouth human head-to-head** of bovine vs porcine xenograft in one procedure type (lateral window MSFA) with both materials at the same 20:80 autogenous:xenograft ratio, controlling inter-patient anatomy.
- **Layered endpoint battery**: radiographic (height, volume, resorption) + histomorphometry + immunohistochemistry (osteocytes, osteoblasts, osteoclasts, MSC, leukocytes, monocytes/macrophages, endothelial cells, osteopontin) + TEM + mRNA (osteogenic pathway) in the same grafts.
- **First head-to-head mRNA comparison in sinus augmentation**: ALP, RUNX2 and Osterix expression measured by RT-qPCR with the ABB side of each patient used as the internal control.
- **Equivalence-style conclusion for clinical decision-making**: the porcine material produced the same resorption, osteoconduction and osteogenesis behaviour as the most-studied biomaterial in the field (Bio-Oss).
- **Microvascular-density signal**: vessel density in ACB+ABB grafts was lower than the same group's previously published values, and ACB+PBM was lower still — a reminder that comparative histology is not interchangeable with absolute benchmark numbers.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Participants.** Inclusion: patients referred for maxillary posterior implants who also needed bilateral two-stage sinus floor augmentation — residual bone height <5 mm plus either full maxillary edentulism or Kennedy class I partial edentulism. Exclusion: refusal to participate, medical conditions affecting bone metabolism (e.g. bisphosphonates), heavy smoking (>10 cigarettes/day). History of periodontitis as reason for extraction was *not* an exclusion. Seven of the ten patients had partial edentulism; most had severe (6) or moderate (3) periodontitis; no major systemic disease.

**Interventions (identical for both sides of each patient).**
- Autologous cortical bone (ACB) scraped from the lateral sinus wall with a bone scraper (Safescraper®, Meta).
- One sinus: ACB + anorganic bovine bone (Bio-Oss® Spongiosa, 250–1000 µm, Geistlich) at ~20:80 volume ratio (ACB+ABB).
- Contralateral sinus: ACB + porcine bone mineral (Symbios® Xenograft granules, 0.25–1.0 mm, Dentsply Sirona) at ~20:80 volume ratio (ACB+PBM).
- Resorbable collagen membrane (Symbios® Collagen Membrane SR) over the window; 3/0 silk sutures; amoxicillin 1 g q8h for 7 days, ibuprofen 600 mg q8h for 4 days, metamizole 575 mg q8h as needed; sutures removed at 7 days. Similar graft volumes used in both sinuses.

**Outcomes and measurement.** CBCT before, immediately after and 6 months after grafting; residual alveolar crest (RAC), final height (FH), height gain (HG = FH − RAC), initial graft height (IHG), vertical resorption (IHG − HG), measured at mesial/central/distal sites; manual segmentation of a volume of interest for grafted volume; bucco-lingual sinus width at 5, 10 and 15 mm from the sinus floor. Biopsy at implant placement (6 months) with 3.5 × 22 mm trephines (AstraTech Implant System EV drills) → 10% formalin for histology/histomorphometry/IHC, Trizol for mRNA, 2.5% glutaraldehyde for TEM. Histomorphometry by H&E and Masson's trichrome with ImageJ semi-automatic quantification. IHC panel: CD44 (osteocytes), CD56 (osteoblasts), TRAP (osteoclasts), Musashi-1 (MSC), CD45 (leukocytes), CD68 (monocytes/macrophages), CD34 (endothelial cells), osteopontin (osteoconduction marker).

**Randomization, blinding, statistics.** Treatment sequence and material assignment randomized per patient via randomization.com, sequence generated and concealed by a clinic assistant not participating in the study; surgeon blind to the sequence until applying the biomaterial; sample-processing, radiographic and histopathological evaluators and the patients were blinded. Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-rank test for all between-side comparisons (split-mouth pairing); p<0.05. GraphPad Prism 7.0a.

**Sample size.** Power calculation on the primary outcome (difference in final bone height at 6 months) using data from Lee, Shin et al. 2017 (final height 15.02 (2.17) bovine vs 13.26 (1.89) porcine; effect size 0.637; α = .05; power = .80; G*Power 3.1.9) indicated a required sample of 18; the study enrolled 10 patients / 20 sinuses (10 per material).

**Protocol deviation.** The registered protocol intended CBCT re-measurement at 12 and 18 months; the COVID-19 pandemic interrupted most of those follow-ups, so 12- and 18-month data were not registered for the study.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Clinical.** Healing was uneventful in all cases with no adverse events. Graft-type distribution: ACB+PBM used in six right sinuses. Access-window height and width did not differ between groups.

**Radiographic (primary outcome).** No difference between graft types for the primary outcome or any secondary radiographic measure. Residual alveolar crest was <5 mm on average at all sites (lower centrally); graft height reached ~15 mm centrally immediately after grafting; final height and height gain were therefore greatest centrally, where resorption was also maximal. Initial and final graft volumes and the level of resorption did not differ between materials; bucco-lingual sinus width did not differ at 5, 10 or 15 mm from the sinus floor.

**Histomorphometry** (column order ACB+ABB / ACB+PBM as confirmed by the narrative text; Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-rank):

| Compartment | ACB+ABB | ACB+PBM | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pristine bone — mineralized tissue (%) | 47.85 (18.41) | 48.56 (16.72) | .945 |
| Pristine bone — non-mineralized tissue (%) | 52.15 (18.40) | 51.44 (16.72) | .945 |
| Grafted area — mineralized tissue (%) | 29.24 (10.82) | 32.51 (14.76) | .570 |
| Grafted area — non-mineralized tissue (%) | 39.76 (16.10) | 36.28 (13.43) | .652 |
| Grafted area — remnant biomaterial (%) | 31.00 (17.29) | 31.21 (19.61) | >.999 |

New trabecular bone was seen in apposition to particles of both biomaterials. Osteoid lines were higher in the ACB+ABB grafted area than in that group's own pristine bone (1.56 (2.01) vs 5.89 (4.54); p=.031).

RUNX2 and Osterix were numerically increased in ACB+PBM without significance, and the authors caution against over-reading them.

**TEM.** Firm union between newly formed trabeculae and particles of both materials; cell colonization inside material pores and neo-colonization of old osteons.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Under-powered against its own calculation.** The power analysis required 18 units; 10 patients (10 sinuses per material) were enrolled. The authors themselves list the small sample size (10 cases per group) as a reason for the null results, arguing the design and 6-month maturation are standard in split-mouth sinus studies.
- **No implant outcome.** Implant placement was the occasion for the biopsy, but no implant survival/success data are reported — so this trial cannot contribute to survival or long-term graft-stability endpoints.
- **Truncated follow-up.** Registered 12- and 18-month CBCT re-measurements were lost to the COVID-19 pandemic; long-term remodelling and resorption (the axis where bovine and porcine materials would be expected to diverge) was not measured.
- **Single centre, single surgeon** (P.G.-M.), small severely atrophic population (RBH <5 mm, largely periodontitis-driven), 6-month biopsy endpoint only.
- **Signal asymmetry that cannot be resolved**: fewer osteoid lines and more osteocytes/leukocytes/vessels in ACB+ABB, higher ALP mRNA in ACB+PBM — the authors offer a mechanistic hypothesis (ALP regulation through WNT and BMP/RUNX2/Osterix) but explicitly caution that the mRNA results "must be interpreted with caution".
- **Vessel density below the group's own historical values** for the same ABB combination, and lower still for PBM — cross-study histological benchmarking is unreliable and should not be read as a material difference.
- **Internal inconsistency in the source** (reproduced verbatim, not silently corrected): the Results text gives the pristine Musashi-1 value of the ACB+PBM side as "56.92 (84.08)", while Table 2 lists "56.82 (84.08)" for the same cell.
- **Conflict-of-interest context**: the first author is a frequent speaker for Dentsply Sirona Implants, the manufacturer of the test porcine material (Symbios® Xenograft granules), the membrane used in both arms (Symbios® Collagen Membrane SR) and the implant system used for placement (AstraTech Implant System EV); the authors declare no conflict of interest.
- **Future work**: randomized trials with larger samples, longer follow-up (≥12 months) including implant survival, and head-to-head comparison with other porcine-derived xenografts.

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| ABB — anorganic bovine bone | Inorganic bovine bone mineral, here Bio-Oss® Spongiosa (250–1000 µm), the most-studied xenograft in the field |
| PBM — porcine bone mineral | Porcine-derived xenograft, here Symbios® Xenograft granules (0.25–1.0 mm) |
| ACB — autologous cortical bone | Autogenous cortical bone scraped from the lateral sinus wall and mixed with the xenograft at 20:80 (vol) |
| RAC / FH / HG / IHG | Residual alveolar crest height / final height at 6 months / height gain (FH − RAC) / initial graft height |
| Vertical graft resorption | IHG − HG, i.e. loss of graft height between the immediate post-operative and 6-month CBCT |
| Mineralized tissue (histomorphometry) | Percentage of the biopsy area occupied by mineralized bone in the grafted area |
| Non-mineralized tissue | Percentage of the biopsy area occupied by non-mineralized (connective/marrow) tissue |
| Remnant biomaterial | Percentage of the biopsy area still occupied by unremodeled graft particles |
| Osteoid lines | Unmineralized bone seams, quantified per mm² as a marker of active osteogenesis |
| CD44 / CD56 / TRAP | Markers for osteocytes / osteoblasts / osteoclasts |
| CD45 / CD68 / CD34 | Markers for all leukocytes / monocytes-macrophages / endothelial cells (microvessel density) |
| Musashi-1 (MSI1) | Marker of mesenchymal stromal cells and osteogenic differentiation |
| Osteopontin | Matrix protein used here as a marker of osteoconduction |
| ALP | Alkaline phosphatase — osteoid mineralization; measured here at the mRNA level (RT-qPCR, 2^−ΔΔCt, GAPDH-normalized) |
| Split-mouth design | Each patient receives both treatments (one per side), so between-side comparison is paired within patient |
| CONSORT | Consolidated Standards of Reporting Trials — reporting guideline the protocol followed |
