선택 결정 축의 다른 논문들이 전부 **장착한 환자**를 추적하는 데 반해, 이 리뷰는 **장착하지 않게 된 환자**를 센다 — 5년 내 약 40%가 사용을 중단한다. ## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (J Prosthet Dent) of the current state and future needs of removable partial denture treatment for the growing partially dentate adult population, covering designs, materials, fabrication technologies, and their impact on oral health and patient compliance.

Almost 40% of patients no longer use their RPD within five years — driven by sociodemographic factors, pain, and esthetics — and design, materials, ease of repair, patient education and follow-up all significantly affect treatment success, while research on both disease-oriented and patient-centred RPD outcomes is described as lacking.

Prosthesis survival and prosthesis *use* are different endpoints, so a high technical survival rate cannot be quoted to a patient as a success rate; the modifiable levers the authors identify — education, follow-up, repairability — sit largely outside the laboratory.

## 세줄요약

서술적 문헌고찰 (Journal of Prosthetic Dentistry): 늘어나는 부분무치악 성인 인구를 대상으로 가철성 국소의치 (Removable Partial Denture, RPD) 치료의 현재 상태와 향후 과제를 설계·재료·제작기술·구강건강 영향·환자 순응도 측면에서 정리했다.

**5년 내 거의 40%의 환자가 RPD를 더 이상 사용하지 않으며** (사회인구학적 요인·통증·심미가 원인), 설계·재료·수리 용이성·환자교육·추적관찰이 모두 치료 성공에 유의한 영향을 미치는 반면, 질환 중심 결과와 환자 중심 결과 양쪽 모두에서 RPD 연구가 부족하다고 지적한다.

보철의 **생존**과 보철의 **사용**은 다른 종점이다 — 기술적 생존율이 높다고 그것을 환자에게 성공률로 말할 수 없다. 저자들이 지목한 조절 가능한 지렛대(교육·추적·수리 가능성)는 대부분 기공실 바깥에 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Puts a number on **discontinuation** (~40% within 5 years), the endpoint prosthesis-survival studies structurally cannot see.
- Names the drivers as sociodemographic, pain and esthetics — i.e. patient-side and communication-side, not primarily technical.
- Identifies the modifiable levers: design, materials, ease of repair, patient education, follow-up.
- States plainly that the RPD evidence base is thin on both disease-oriented and patient-centred outcomes.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review, not systematic. Data on treatment for partial edentulism reviewed and summarised, focused on available and emerging RPD designs, materials and means of production, and their impact on oral health; patient satisfaction and compliance data reviewed separately to assess patient-centred care.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Finding | Value / direction |
|---|---|
| Patients no longer using their RPD within 5 years | **≈ 40%** |
| Stated drivers of discontinuation | Sociodemographics · pain · esthetics |
| Factors with significant impact on success | Design · materials · ease of repair · patient education · follow-up |
| State of the evidence base | Lacking for both disease-oriented and patient-centred outcomes |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Narrative, not systematic**: no search strategy, no inclusion criteria, no risk-of-bias appraisal. The ~40% figure is a cited summary statistic, not a pooled estimate.
- Ingested **abstract-only**, so the source study/studies for the 40% cannot be traced from this page. **Treat the figure as an order-of-magnitude signal, not a precise rate**, and re-derive it from primary sources before quoting it in a consent document.
- Author group is US/UK; discontinuation is strongly shaped by health-system coverage and cost, so the rate should not be assumed to transfer to Korean practice.
- Written 2017, before current CAD/CAM and 3D-printed removable workflows matured; the "need for innovation" framing predates much of that change.
- No time-to-discontinuation curve, so it is unknown whether abandonment clusters early (fit/pain) or accumulates steadily.

## 7. Glossary

- **RPD (가철성 국소의치, Removable Partial Denture)**: removable prosthesis replacing missing teeth while retaining natural abutments.
- **Discontinuation (사용 중단)**: patient ceasing to wear a delivered prosthesis; distinct from prosthesis failure or loss.
- **Disease-oriented outcome (질환 중심 결과)**: clinician-measured endpoint such as attachment loss or caries.
- **Patient-centred outcome (환자 중심 결과)**: patient-reported endpoint such as satisfaction, comfort, OHRQoL.
