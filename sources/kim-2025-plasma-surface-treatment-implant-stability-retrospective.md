## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study (47 patients, 73 plasma-treated implants, single-arm, no control group) using the ACTILINK Reborn non-thermal atmospheric pressure plasma device applied chairside immediately before implant placement (June 2023–October 2024).

Mean ISQ increased progressively from 78.97±5.52 at placement to 83.74±4.36 at week 8 (p<0.001) with no stability dip observed; subgroup analysis showed the greatest relative gains in implants with lower initial ISQ, mandibular sites, shorter fixtures, and higher insertion torque.

The absence of a control group means the "no dip" finding and ISQ trajectory cannot be causally attributed to plasma treatment; RCT confirmation is required before clinical protocol adoption.

## 세줄요약

단일군 후향 코호트(환자 47명, 임플란트 73개, 대조군 없음): 2023년 6월~2024년 10월, 식립 직전 ACTILINK Reborn 비열 대기압 플라즈마를 chairside 적용했다.

ISQ 평균이 식립 시 78.97±5.52에서 8주 83.74±4.36으로 점진 상승(p<0.001)하고 stability dip이 관찰되지 않았으며, 초기 ISQ 낮은 군·하악·짧은 fixture·높은 식입토크 임플란트에서 상대 이득이 컸다.

대조군 없는 후향 설계로 인해 "stability dip 소실"과 ISQ 상승을 플라즈마 효과로 인과적으로 귀속할 수 없으며, 임상 프로토콜 도입 전 RCT 확인이 필요하다.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: Materials 2025, 18(19):4568
- DOI: 10.3390/ma18194568
- Study type: retrospective cohort (human), single-arm (no control)
- Device: ACTILINK Reborn; non-thermal atmospheric pressure plasma

## 2. Key Contributions

- Real-world human cohort showing ISQ trajectory and absence of stability dip after plasma.
- Subgroup gradient identifying who benefits most.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 73 plasma-treated implants in 47 patients (Jun 2023–Oct 2024). NTAP immediately before placement.
- ISQ at baseline, weekly for 4 weeks, and week 8. Subgroups: initial ISQ, jaw, length/diameter, insertion torque.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- No stability dip; uneventful healing.
- Mean ISQ 78.97±5.52 (placement) → 83.74±4.36 (week 8), p<0.001.
- Greatest relative gains in lower-initial-stability implants; very stable implants changed little.
- Mandibular sites, shorter fixtures, higher insertion torque → greater gains.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No control group; retrospective; short-term ISQ only.
- "No dip" cannot be attributed to plasma without a control — requires RCT confirmation.
