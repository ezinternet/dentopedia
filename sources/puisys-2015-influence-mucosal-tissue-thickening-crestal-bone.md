## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (97 patients, 97 bone-level 4.1 mm Straumann implants) comparing crestal bone loss in untreated thin mucosa, thin mucosa thickened with an allogenic membrane, and naturally thick mucosa at 2 months and 1 year.

At 1 year, untreated thin tissue (T1, n=33) lost 1.22 ± 0.08 mm mesially and 1.14 ± 0.07 mm distally, versus 0.24 ± 0.06 / 0.19 ± 0.06 mm after membrane thickening (T2, n=32) and 0.22 ± 0.06 / 0.20 ± 0.06 mm in thick mucosa (C, n=32); T1 vs T2 and T1 vs C were significant (P = 0.000), T2 vs C were not (P = 0.909 mesial, 0.312 distal).

Thin mucosa drives crestal bone loss around bone-level implants, and allogenic-membrane thickening reduces it to the level of naturally thick tissue — a soft-tissue route to crestal preservation.

## 세줄요약

전향적 대조 임상시험(환자 97명, 골수준(Bone-Level) 직경 4.1 mm Straumann 임플란트 97개) — 치료하지 않은 얇은 점막, 동종이식막(Allogenic Membrane)으로 두껍게 한 얇은 점막, 선천적으로 두꺼운 점막에서 2개월·1년 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss)을 비교.

1년 골소실: T1군(얇은 점막, n=33) 근심 1.22 ± 0.08 mm·원심 1.14 ± 0.07 mm, T2군(막으로 두껍게, n=32) 0.24 ± 0.06·0.19 ± 0.06 mm, C군(두꺼운 점막, n=32) 0.22 ± 0.06·0.20 ± 0.06 mm; T1-T2·T1-C 간 유의(P = 0.000), T2-C 간 비유의(근심 P = 0.909, 원심 P = 0.312).

임상 함의: 얇은 점막은 골수준 임플란트 주변 변연골 소실을 유발하며, 동종이식막으로 연조직을 두껍게 하면 자연적 두꺼운 점막 수준까지 골소실을 줄일 수 있다 — 플랫폼 스위칭(PS, Platform Switching)과 별개의 연조직 경로.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly tests whether thickening thin peri-implant mucosa with an allogenic membrane reduces crestal bone loss around **bone-level** implants (4.1 mm diameter, Institut Straumann AG, Switzerland).
- Allocates patients by vertical gingival thickness into untreated thin (T1, ≤2 mm), thin-thickened (T2), and naturally thick (C, >2 mm) groups, holding implant diameter and type constant.
- Reports a large early divergence already at 2 months (T1 0.75/0.73 mm vs T2 0.16/0.20 mm vs C 0.17/0.18 mm mesial/distal) that persists at 1 year.
- Shows membrane-thickened thin tissue (T2: 0.24/0.19 mm at 1 year) becomes statistically indistinguishable from naturally thick tissue (C: 0.22/0.20 mm), both far below untreated thin tissue (T1: 1.22/1.14 mm).
- Provides the bone-level-implant counterpart to the same group's horizontally matching-connection trial ([[wiki/implants/mbl/linkevicius-2015-crestal-bone-stability-soft-tissue-thickening-matching]]).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective controlled clinical trial; patients assigned to groups according to vertical gingival thickness (not stated as randomized).
- **Databases**: none — primary clinical trial.
- **n**: 97 patients (28 men, 69 women; mean age 47.3 ± 1.2 years) receiving 97 bone-level implants of 4.1 mm diameter (Institut Straumann AG, Switzerland); T1 thin ≤2 mm n=33, T2 thin thickened with allogenic membrane n=32, C thick >2 mm n=32.
- **Surgical approach**: implants placed in the posterior mandible in a one-stage approach; after integration restored with single screw-retained metal-ceramic restorations.
- **Radiographic assessment**: after implant placement, 2 months after healing, after prosthetic restoration, and at 1-year follow-up.
- **Outcomes**: crestal bone loss calculated mesially and distally.
- **Statistics**: Mann–Whitney U-test; significance set at 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Crestal bone loss (mm, mean ± SD), mesial / distal:

| Time point | T1 — thin, untreated (n=33) | T2 — thin + allogenic membrane (n=32) | C — thick, >2 mm (n=32) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 months after healing | 0.75 ± 0.11 / 0.73 ± 0.10 | 0.16 ± 0.06 / 0.20 ± 0.06 | 0.17 ± 0.05 / 0.18 ± 0.03 |
| 1-year follow-up | 1.22 ± 0.08 / 1.14 ± 0.07 | 0.24 ± 0.06 / 0.19 ± 0.06 | 0.22 ± 0.06 / 0.20 ± 0.06 |

Between-group comparisons (both time points, mesial and distal):

| Comparison | 2 months | 1 year |
|---|---|---|
| T1 vs T2 | significant (P = 0.000) | significant (P = 0.000) |
| T1 vs C | significant (P = 0.000) | significant (P = 0.000) |
| T2 vs C (mesial) | not significant (P = 0.861) | not significant (P = 0.909) |
| T2 vs C (distal) | not significant (P = 0.827) | not significant (P = 0.312) |

- Authors' conclusion (verbatim sense): significantly less bone loss occurs around bone-level implants placed in naturally thick mucosal tissues than in the thin biotype; augmentation of thin soft tissues with an allogenic membrane during implant placement could be a way to reduce crestal bone loss.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only**: the retrieved artifact is a PubMed abstract (no PMC record; the publisher OA PDF returned HTTP 403). No full text was obtained, so subgroup analyses, radiographic measurement details, and confounder handling are unavailable.
- Group allocation was by vertical gingival thickness rather than randomization.
- Follow-up in the abstract stops at 1 year; no longer-term crestal stability data are reported here.
- Institution/affiliation and detailed surgical protocol are not present in the retrieved abstract.

- Puisys & Linkevicius 2015 (this paper): bone-level 4.1 mm implants; allogenic-membrane thickening of thin mucosa.

## 7. Glossary

- **Bone-Level Implant**: implant whose platform sits at or below the crestal bone level, as opposed to a tissue-level (supracrestal) implant; the 4.1 mm Straumann implants used here.
- **Allogenic Membrane**: donor-derived membrane used here to thicken thin peri-implant mucosa at implant placement.
- **Vertical Gingival Thickness**: mucosal thickness measured vertically (here ≤2 mm thin vs >2 mm thick) — the grouping variable of this trial.
- **Crestal Bone Loss**: radiographic vertical reduction of the marginal bone crest around an implant, measured mesially and distally.
- **Mann–Whitney U-test**: non-parametric test used here for between-group comparisons of crestal bone loss.
- **Biotype (thin/thick)**: periodontal phenotype describing thin vs thick mucosal tissue; thin biotype is associated with greater crestal remodeling in this trial.
