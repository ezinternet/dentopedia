## One-line Summary
Split-mouth RCT (Oral Health Prev Dent 2026): 15 patients, Stage II/III periodontitis; PRF and EMD as adjuncts to NSPT produced comparable improvements in CAL, PPD, and BOP at 6 months — no significant difference between the two biologics.

## 한줄요약

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Oral Health and Preventive Dentistry, 2026. DOI: 10.3290/j.ohpd.c_2750
- PMID: 42583789; PMC: PMC13463701
- Setting: Lakewood Ranch Dental, Florida, USA (private practice RCT)
- IRB: Sterling IRB (approval ID: 12625-NEstrin)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT directly comparing PRF vs EMD as NSPT adjuncts (not vs placebo)
- Both PRF and EMD improve CAL, PPD, BOP significantly from baseline
- No significant difference between PRF and EMD — comparable efficacy as biologic NSPT adjuncts
- Split-mouth design minimizes inter-individual variability
- EMD applied flaplessly (non-surgical) — shows biologic advantage may persist even without flap access
- Clinically: PRF (autologous) and EMD (commercial) interchangeable adjuncts; selection based on clinician preference, cost, availability

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Split-mouth RCT; December 2024–April 2025
- n: 15 patients; Stage II/III, Grade A/B periodontitis (2017 World Workshop); ≥6 interproximal sites PD >4 mm
- Two treatment modalities: (i) SRP + EMD; (ii) SRP + PRF; assigned to contralateral quadrant pairs
- SRP with ultrasonic + hand curettes + adjunctive laser (Fotona)
- Follow-up: 6 months
- Outcomes: PD, gingival recession (GR), CAL, BOP
- Statistical: split-mouth paired analyses

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Both PRF and EMD: significant improvements in CAL, PPD, BOP from baseline to 6 months
- Between PRF and EMD: no statistically significant difference for any outcome
- BOP: significant reduction in both groups — anti-inflammatory effect of both biologics
- EMD advantages: established adsorption to conditioned root surface; standard under dry conditions
- PRF advantages: does not rely on root conditioning; practical when moisture control is difficult

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- n=15 — small, limits generalizability
- 6-month follow-up — full regenerative potential not captured
- Inclusion of smokers (balanced allocation) may have attenuated effects
- Flapless EMD application may underperform vs surgical access
- Future: deeper defects, furcation involvement, intrabony defects, longer follow-up

## 6. Related Work
- EMD: 30+ years of evidence for periodontal regeneration; primarily studied with surgical access
- PRF: second-generation platelet concentrate; autologous fibrin matrix with growth factors (PDGF, TGF-β1, VEGF)

## 7. Glossary
- PRF: Platelet-rich fibrin — autologous blood concentrate; fibrin matrix with growth factors
- EMD: Enamel matrix derivative (Emdogain) — promotes cementogenesis and PDL regeneration
- NSPT: Nonsurgical periodontal therapy (scaling and root planing)
- CAL: Clinical attachment level
- PPD: Probing pocket depth; BOP: bleeding on probing
