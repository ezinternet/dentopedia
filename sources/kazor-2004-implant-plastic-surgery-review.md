## Why Ingested

Peri-implant soft-tissue height and esthetics are the main reason for implant plastic surgery, and the `implants/soft-tissue` folder needs a procedure-level review. This Kazor et al. 2004 review lists the plastic-surgery options and case-selection factors, complementing the [[implants/soft-tissue/misch-2025-abutment-height-emergence-angle-peri-implant-bone-loss]] emergence-profile data.

## Three-line Summary

Review (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2004) of periodontal plastic-surgical procedures used to create and maintain peri-implant soft-tissue height for esthetics — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The article reviews the available procedures and their clinical applications, with emphasis on case selection and treatment planning.

No outcome data or success rates appear in the abstract; the procedure list should be checked against the full text before clinical use.
