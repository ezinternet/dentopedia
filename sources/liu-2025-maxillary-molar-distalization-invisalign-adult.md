## Why Ingested

Liu 2025는 iTero 구내스캐너로 얻은 3D 모델을 구개(palatal) 부위에서 중첩(superimposition)하여, T0(치료 전)·T1(U7 원심이동 후)·T2(U6 원심이동 후) 3단계에 걸쳐 상악 대구치의 원심이동량·경사각·회전·수직변화 및 상악 중절치(U1)의 순측경사·토크 변화를 정밀 추적한 12명 성인 후향적 연구다. [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/inchingolo-2023-clear-aligner-distalization-predictability-sr]]가 제기한 "distalization 예측성은 장치의 내재적 한계가 아니라 앵커리지 관리 문제"라는 테제를, TAD(temporary anchorage device) 없이 Class II 고무줄만 사용한 실측 데이터로 독립 확인한다 — 본 연구에서도 전치부 앵커리지 손실이 관찰됐다. 또한 동일한 iTero+구개 중첩 방법론을 쓴 선행 연구 [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/saif-2022-efficiency-evaluation-maxillary-molar-distalization]]에는 없던 원심경사각·회전각·수직(함입) 변화를 정량화하여 그 스코프를 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary
Retrospective single-arm study of 12 adult patients (mean age 31.8y, range 24-46), 19 U6 and 19 U7 teeth sequentially distalized with Invisalign, tracked via iTero digital models at T0/T1/T2 using palatal-area registration/superimposition.
From T0 to T2, U6 moved distally 1.71 ± 0.89 mm and U7 2.04 ± 1.16 mm, with distal tipping of U6 -5.20 ± 2.62° and U7 -5.35 ± 4.19°, molar intrusion, U6 distopalatal rotation +2.83 ± 2.45°, and anterior anchorage loss (U1 sagittal change -0.88 ± 0.99 mm, torque +3.58 ± 4.69°) despite intermaxillary Class II elastics.
Invisalign achieves maxillary molar distalization primarily through tipping rather than bodily movement, with decreased angulation control and anterior anchorage loss, indicating a need for additional auxiliaries (e.g., TADs) beyond elastics.

- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health 2025;25:1232

## 2. Key Contributions
- Uses iTero intraoral laser scanning with palatal-area registration (a relatively stable, reproducible structure) for serial 3D model superimposition, rather than 2D cephalograms or unstandardized 3D registration.
- Tracks the actual tooth movement trajectory across three discrete stages (T0/T1/T2 = before, after U7 distalization, after U6 distalization) rather than only pre/post treatment comparison.
- Quantifies not just distalization distance but also distal tipping, distopalatal rotation, vertical (intrusion) change of U6/U7, and sagittal/torque change of U1 — a fuller 3D picture of side effects than most prior single-metric studies.
- Documents a reciprocal mesial shift of U7 (-0.21 ± 0.78 mm, NS) during the T1→T2 interval, attributed to progressive loss of posterior anchor teeth as U6 begins moving.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective, single-arm (no control group), serial 3D digital-model study.
- **Population**: 12 adult patients (mean age 31.8y, range 24-46), treated July 2019-July 2022; skeletal Class I or Class II, mild upper-arch crowding (≤4 mm), Class I or end-to-end Class II molar relationship, no third molars, good periodontal condition, ≥20h/day compliance.
- **n**: 19 U6, 19 U7, and 19 U1 teeth (one side per patient, bilateral or unilateral protocol) from the 12 patients. Power analysis (PASS 11.0, referencing a prior study) indicated ≥9/10 first/second molars needed for 95% power on paired t-test — the 19/19 sample satisfies this.
- **Protocol**: Sequential distalization — U7 moved to the halfway point before U6 began moving. U6 distalization design ≤3 mm; anterior sagittal movement avoided during molar distalization. Intermaxillary Class II elastics (4.5 oz, Ormco) used throughout, canine precision cuts to lower first molar buttons. Vertical rectangular attachments on maxillary molars. Mean 27 aligners/patient (range 16-40), worn 10-14 days each.
- **Data collection**: iTero scans at T0 (before distalization), T1 (after U7 distalization), T2 (after U6 distalization); STL files analyzed in RapidForm 2006. Reference planes (occlusal, sagittal, coronal) and a 12-point crown landmark scheme established per patient; crown landmarks bound/registered via best-fit method to minimize identification error across timepoints.
- **Reliability**: Intra- and inter-rater ICCs 0.89-0.94 (excellent).
- **Statistics**: Kolmogorov-Smirnov/Shapiro-Wilk normality checks, paired t-tests for T0-T1, T1-T2, T0-T2 comparisons; Spearman rank correlation for associations. Significance p<0.05.
- **Outcomes**: Primary — distalization distance and mesiodistal angulation of U6/U7 at T0/T1/T2. Secondary (T0→T2 only) — rotation angle and vertical (intrusion) change of U6/U7; sagittal and torque change of U1.

Mean treatment duration for the distalization phase was ~10.9 months. Consistent with a systematic review cited in the discussion (Shen et al. 2024), roughly 2 mm of maxillary molar distalization is achievable with clear aligners under a sequential-distalization protocol; U6/U7 here (1.71/2.04 mm) fall within that range.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No control group (though mean treatment duration ~10.9 months makes adult craniofacial growth confounding unlikely).
- No CBCT/panoramic imaging to directly assess root position/torque — relied on non-invasive iTero digital models instead.
- Buccolingual (transverse) torque of the molars was not assessed; planned for future work.
- Small overall sample (12 patients, 19 teeth per category), though power-analysis-justified for the primary angulation outcome.

## 6. Related Work
- Saif 2022 (efficiency evaluation of maxillary molar distalization using Invisalign, palatal-rugae registration): same core iTero + palatal-registration methodology; predicted-vs-achieved distalization differed significantly and correlated with anterior anchorage loss. Liu 2025 extends this with quantified tipping/rotation/intrusion data.
- Elfouly et al. 2024 (CBCT study of tip/torque/rotation during Invisalign distalization): reported significant mesiobuccal molar rotation, consistent with Liu's U6 rotation finding, but reported no significant molar tipping — contrasts with Liu's tipping findings.
- Simon et al. 2014 and Ravera et al. 2016: reported bodily (non-tipping) molar movement with Invisalign, contrasting with Liu's tipping-dominant finding.
- Shen et al. 2024 (SR+MA on sequential clear-aligner distalization protocol): ~2 mm achievable distalization ceiling, consistent with Liu's U6/U7 T0-T2 results.

## 7. Glossary
- **U6**: Maxillary first molar.
- **U7**: Maxillary second molar.
- **U1**: Maxillary central incisor.
- **T0/T1/T2**: Study timepoints — before distalization, after U7 distalization, after U6 distalization.
- **iTero**: Intraoral laser scanner used to capture 3D digital dental models.
- **Palatal-area registration**: Superimposition method using the relatively stable/reproducible palatal rugae/vault region to align serial 3D models over time.
- **Distal tipping**: Angular (non-bodily) tooth movement where the crown moves more than the root in the distal direction.
