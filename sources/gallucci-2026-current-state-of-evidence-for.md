## Why Ingested

Updated (PROSPERO CRD42017062420), much larger successor to the same research group's earlier placement-timing/loading-timing classification work — extends [[implants/zhou-2021-placement-loading-protocols-single-implants-sr]] (site-stratified single-implant version, two shared authors Gallucci/Hamilton) from four anatomic sites to the full partially-edentulous population, and independently reinforces [[implants/aiquel-2021-timing-placement-loading-multiple-unit-sr-ma]]'s conclusion that survival is largely comparable across timing protocols (explicitly cited in this paper's own Discussion as corroborating). With 140 studies / 10,456 implants across all 9 placement×loading protocol combinations and a formal 4-tier scientific/clinical validation framework, this is currently the most comprehensive evidence map in the wiki for choosing an implant placement+loading protocol in partially edentulous patients.

## Three-line Summary

Updated PROSPERO-registered systematic review (140 studies: 42 RCTs + 98 CCTs/cohorts from 11,427 screened records; 10,456 implants; search through Jan 2024) of implant-supported fixed dental prostheses (IFDPs) in partially edentulous patients, computing follow-up- and implant-count-weighted cumulative survival across all 9 reported implant placement (Type 1 immediate / Type 2-3 early / Type 4 late) × loading (A immediate / B early / C conventional) protocol combinations.

Weighted survival ranged 91.6–100% (Type 1A 98.0%, 1B 91.6%, 1C 95.0%, 2-3A 97.8%, 2-3B 100%, 2-3C 94.0%, 4A 97.2%, 4B 97.9%, 4C 97.5%); a 4-tier validation framework (study design + sample size + ≤10% outcome-homogeneity threshold) classified 1A/1C/2-3A/2-3C/4A/4B/4C as scientifically-and-clinically validated (SCV), while 1B and 2-3B remained only "clinically documented" (CD) despite 91.6% and 100% survival respectively.

No meta-analysis was feasible (substantial clinical/methodological/reporting heterogeneity; moderate-to-high risk of bias in most included studies); the paper itself cautions that "SCV" labeling can mask clinically meaningful differences — Type 1A (98.0%) vs. Type 1B (91.6%) is roughly a four-fold difference in implant loss despite both carrying enough data to be formally classified.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Updates a PROSPERO-registered SR (CRD42017062420, originally reported 2018) with a substantially larger dataset (140 studies vs. the 2018 predecessor) and extended follow-up (up to 120–180 months), applying a formal 4-tier scientific/clinical validation framework (SCV/CWD/CD/CID) instead of raw survival percentages alone.
- Computes a follow-up-duration- and implant-count-weighted cumulative survival rate per protocol, with a priori boxplot/±3-SD outlier detection applied only to the quantitative weighting (all studies are retained in the qualitative synthesis regardless).
- Reclassifies Type 1A (immediate placement + immediate loading) from "clinically documented" in the 2018 review to "scientifically and clinically validated" now, and newly validates Type 2-3A (early placement + immediate loading), previously underreported.
- Explicitly warns that the SCV/CD terminology can mask clinically meaningful survival gaps — flags Type 1B (91.6%) as representing roughly a "four-fold" higher implant-loss rate than Type 1A (98.0%) despite both carrying enough data to be labeled.
- Reports ITT (intention-to-treat) adherence as a secondary methodological metric (calculable in 96/140 studies) to contextualize potential selection bias at allocation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review (update of PROSPERO CRD42017062420); PRISMA (Liberati et al.) + IOM trustworthy-guideline standards + Cochrane Handbook (Higgins & Green 2017); no meta-analysis performed — heterogeneity precluded pooling.
- **PICO**: Population = partially edentulous patients; Intervention = immediate/early placement+loading; Comparator = late placement/conventional loading; Outcome = implant-prosthodontic survival/success.
- **Databases**: PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL; English-language articles published up to January 1, 2024.
- **Screening**: 3 independent reviewers (JFPC, SA, KP), disagreements resolved by a 4th (GOG); Cohen's kappa for inter-reviewer agreement.
- **n**: 11,427 records screened → 140 studies included (42 RCTs, 98 CCTs/cohort studies) → 10,456 implants across 9 reported placement×loading protocol groups (the theoretical classification has 12 groups; Type 2 and Type 3 placement were merged into "2-3" empirically due to limited per-type data).
- **Risk of bias**: RoB2 for the 42 RCTs (blinding of participants/operators frequently lacking → moderate-to-high risk in performance/detection domains); Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS) for CCTs (most scored 6–7/8; only 6 of 15 assessed CCTs reached the top NOS score of 8).
- **Validation tiers**: Scientifically-and-Clinically-Validated (SCV) / Clinically-Well-Documented (CWD) / Clinically-Documented (CD) / Clinically-Insufficiently-Documented (CID) — assigned per protocol from study design + sample size + outcome homogeneity (OH+ if between-study survival variation ≤10%, OH− if >10%).
- **Weighting**: cumulative survival weighted by (follow-up duration × implant count) per study, summed per protocol; outliers (boxplot + standardized z-score ±3) excluded only from the weighted quantitative calculation, retained in qualitative synthesis.
- **Outcomes**: implant survival (primary, the only outcome consistently reported); success rate (descriptive only — definitions too heterogeneous to synthesize quantitatively); ITT adherence (secondary, descriptive).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Meta-analysis was not feasible: substantial clinical (mixed protocols/implant systems/grafting approaches even within single reports), methodological (RCT/CCT/cohort mix, implant- vs. patient-level reporting), and reporting heterogeneity (incomplete event counts/CIs/variance) precluded a common effect size.
- Predominance of moderate-to-high risk of bias: RoB2 flagged frequent lack of blinding (performance/detection bias) in RCTs; only 6/15 assessed CCTs reached the top Newcastle-Ottawa score.
- Success-rate definitions were too heterogeneous across studies to synthesize quantitatively; survival was the only consistently reported outcome, so complications, esthetic outcomes, and patient-reported measures could not be analyzed.
- ITT analysis was calculable in only 96/140 studies; highly selective patient inclusion criteria in the underlying literature limit generalizability to real-world case mixes.
- A priori outlier exclusion (boxplot/±3 SD) from the weighted quantitative calculation, and weighting by follow-up duration, both introduce minor, authors-acknowledged bias (toward longer-follow-up studies; toward non-outlier studies).
- **Two internal numerical inconsistencies were observed in the source article during this ingest** (documented here for transparency, not silently resolved): (1) the Discussion states Type 1A's current cumulative survival as "96.73%" when comparing longitudinally to the 2018 review's 98.4%, while the Results section and Abstract report 98.01% for Type 1A in this same update; (2) the Abstract's list of "scientifically and/or clinically validated" protocols omits Type 2-3A, while the Results section (Section 3, validation-criteria paragraph) and Conclusion explicitly include Type 2-3A among seven SCV-classified protocols. This page reports the Results-section/Abstract figures (98.01%; 7 SCV protocols including 2-3A) as primary, per the more granular in-body statements.
- Future direction (per the authors): well-designed prospective RCTs with standardized protocols, longer follow-up, blinding, and transparent reporting (event counts, CIs, variance) to eventually enable quantitative meta-analysis.

## 7. Glossary
- **IFDP**: Implant-supported Fixed Dental Prosthesis — the restoration type under review (single crowns and fixed partial dentures on ≥1 implant), in partially (not fully) edentulous patients.
- **Type 1/2/3/4 (placement timing)**: Type 1 = immediate (same-day post-extraction); Type 2 = early with soft-tissue healing (4–8 weeks); Type 3 = early with partial bone healing (12–16 weeks); Type 4 = late, after complete bone healing (>6 months). This review reports Type 2 and 3 merged as "Type 2-3" due to limited per-type data.
- **A/B/C (loading timing)**: A = immediate loading (within 1 week); B = early loading (1 week–2 months); C = conventional loading (after 2 months); only functional loading counted.
- **SCV / CWD / CD / CID**: Scientifically-and-Clinically-Validated / Clinically-Well-Documented / Clinically-Documented / Clinically-Insufficiently-Documented — 4-tier protocol-validation classification combining study design, sample size, and outcome homogeneity (OH).
- **OH+ / OH−**: Outcome Homogeneity positive/negative — between-study survival variation for a given protocol ≤10% (OH+) or >10% (OH−).
- **ITT**: Intention-to-Treat — analysis including all patients as originally allocated to a protocol, regardless of protocol deviations; used here descriptively as ITT% = (N treated / N intended) × 100.
- **RoB2 / NOS**: Cochrane Risk-of-Bias tool version 2 (for RCTs) / Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (for CCTs/cohort studies) — the risk-of-bias appraisal instruments used in this review.
- **PROSPERO**: International prospective register of systematic reviews; this review updates registration CRD42017062420.
