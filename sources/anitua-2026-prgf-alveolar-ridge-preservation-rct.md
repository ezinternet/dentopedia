## Why Ingested

This RCT fills a specific gap: platelet concentrate (혈소판 풍부 성장인자, PRGF) — Anitua's proprietary single-spin platelet-rich preparation — applied to alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) in the aesthetic zone had no dedicated RCT-level entry in the wiki. It provides prospective controlled evidence for a biological adjunct to socket healing, covering both soft tissue and histomorphometric bone outcomes. Related to [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/fok-2024-alveolar-socket-healing-evolving-knowledge]] (ARP biological mechanisms) and [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/alrayyes-2022-prf-socket-preservation-smokers-rct]] (PRF sibling platelet concentrate RCT).

## Three-line Summary

RCT of 46 patients receiving PRGF vs spontaneous healing after aesthetic-zone single-tooth extraction, with biopsy and CBCT-equivalent dimensional assessment at 12 weeks.

PRGF significantly improved new bone formation (48.7% median vs 36.1% control, p=0.024), reduced postoperative pain on day 3 (p=0.036), and improved soft tissue healing indices on days 3, 5, and 7.

Clinical implication: PRGF as a socket adjunct accelerates soft-tissue closure and boosts histological bone fill, though dimensional stability data are reported descriptively without CBCT millimeter values in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

미적 영역 단순 발치 환자 46명을 대상으로 PRGF 처치 vs 자연 치유를 무작위 배정, 12주 후 조직형태계측 생검 및 치조제 계측 비교.

PRGF군 신생골 형성률 중앙값 48.7% vs 대조군 36.1% (p=0.024), 3일차 통증 감소 (p=0.036), 연조직 치유지수 3·5·7일차 유의하게 개선.

임상 함의: PRGF는 연조직 조기 폐쇄와 조직학적 골충전을 가속시키나, CBCT 밀리미터 단위의 치수 변화 데이터는 초록 수준에서 구체적으로 보고되지 않는 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First dedicated RCT comparing PRGF (Anitua's single-spin platelet concentrate) vs spontaneous healing specifically for aesthetic-zone ARP
- Simultaneous soft tissue, histomorphometric, and dimensional outcomes in the same cohort
- Quantifies new bone formation (%) at 12 weeks from core biopsy — PRGF 48.7% vs control 36.1%
- Documents soft tissue healing advantage from day 3 (Landry index) with statistical significance

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized, controlled clinical trial (parallel groups)
- **n**: 46 patients; simple-tooth extraction in the aesthetic zone
- **Groups**: PRGF socket treatment vs spontaneous healing (control)
- **Follow-up**: 3, 7, 15 days (soft tissue/pain); 12 weeks (histomorphometry + dimensions)
- **Outcome measures**:
  - Pain (numeric scale, days 3/7/15)
  - Landry's soft tissue healing index (days 3/5/7)
  - Inflammation index
  - Histomorphometric bone regeneration (% new bone in biopsy at 12 weeks)
  - Dimensional alveolar ridge measurements (baseline vs 12 weeks)
- **Statistical approach**: Group comparison with p-values reported per time point
