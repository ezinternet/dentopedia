## Why Ingested

Reverse torque testing is a common pre-loading check, and the `implants` folder needs a source that questions it. This Jividen and Misch 2000 review argues that the test itself may cause early crestal bone loss, and it proposes an implant-failure nomenclature. It is the critical counterpart to the stability-based loading decisions in [[implants/isq/turkyilmaz-2008-bone-density-implant-stability-success]].

## Three-line Summary

Review (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2000) of reverse torque testing as a screen for early loading failure — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The authors argue the test's shear loading may cause early crestal bone loss and implant failure, especially in less dense bone, and they note that bone density, rotation assessment and implant size/design were not adequately evaluated.

The paper introduces a failure nomenclature; the abstract gives no quantitative failure data.
