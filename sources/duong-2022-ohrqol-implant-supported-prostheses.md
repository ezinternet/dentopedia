## Why Ingested

RPD 선택 결정에서 가장 강력한 **반대 방향 근거** — "임플란트가 삶의 질에서 우월하다"는 전제를 무너뜨리는 Periodontology 2000 리뷰라 넣었다. [[wiki/removable-partial-denture/walter-2021-shortened-dental-arch-15year-rct]]가 SDA vs RPD를 무승부로 닫았다면, 이 리뷰는 **임플란트 vs 통상 치아지지 보철**을 환자 관점에서 무승부로 닫는다. 세 번째 가지(RPD)를 고려할 때 "그래도 임플란트가 낫지 않나"라는 반문에 위키가 답을 갖게 된다.

동시에 이 리뷰는 **불편한 반증**도 들여온다 — 본문이 인용한 Kurosaki 연구의 6년 RPD 누적 생존율 33.3%는 [[wiki/removable-partial-denture/drummond-2024-rpd-long-term-periodontal-health-sr-ma]]의 5년 95.1%와 극단적으로 어긋난다. 한쪽만 들고 있으면 위키가 RPD 내구성을 크게 오독하게 되므로 짝으로 보유해야 한다. 비용 축([[wiki/removable-partial-denture/beikler-2015-eao-economic-evaluation-implant-prostheses]])에 OHIP 1점당 €80이라는 구체적 임계값도 붙인다.

## Three-line Summary

Periodontology 2000 comprehensive review (PMC full text) of oral health-related quality of life in patients rehabilitated with fixed and removable implant-supported prostheses, analysed across functional, esthetic and cost-related dimensions, with a survey of the OHRQoL instruments themselves (GOHAI, OHIP, SOHSI, DIDL, OIDP, PPIDAQ, SOOQ).

From the patient's perspective OHRQoL was **not significantly greater for dental implants than for conventional tooth-supported prostheses**, patients' and clinicians' assessments diverged (clinicians applying higher esthetic standards), soft-tissue-level versus bone-level implants and all-ceramic versus metal-ceramic restorations produced no significant differences in patient-perceived esthetics, and preoperative expectation strongly coloured perceived outcome while being a poor predictor of it.

For the removable axis specifically, the review reports that supporting a conventional removable partial denture with implants is cost-effective once payers are willing to pay more than €80 per OHIP point gained, and cites a six-year comparison in which OHRQoL improved only in the implant-supported fixed denture group and not in the fixed or removable partial denture groups.

## 세줄요약

Periodontology 2000 종합 리뷰 (PMC 전문): 고정성·가철성 임플란트 지지 보철로 수복한 환자의 구강건강관련 삶의 질 (Oral Health-Related Quality of Life, OHRQoL)을 기능·심미·비용 세 축으로 분석하고, 측정도구 자체(GOHAI·OHIP·SOHSI·DIDL·OIDP·PPIDAQ·SOOQ)도 함께 정리했다.

환자 관점에서 OHRQoL은 **임플란트가 통상 치아지지 보철보다 유의하게 낫지 않았고**, 환자 평가와 술자 평가가 갈렸으며(술자가 더 높은 심미 기준 적용), 연조직 수준 vs 골 수준 임플란트·전부도재 vs 도재용착금속관 간 환자 지각 심미에 유의차가 없었고, 수술 전 기대치가 인지 결과를 강하게 물들이면서도 정작 결과의 예측인자로는 부적절했다.

가철성 축에 한정하면, 통상 국소의치에 임플란트를 보조로 붙이는 것은 **OHIP 1점당 €80 이상을 지불할 의향이 있을 때** 비용효과적이며, 6년 비교에서 OHRQoL이 개선된 것은 임플란트 지지 고정성 의치군뿐이고 고정성·가철성 국소의치군은 개선이 없었다고 인용한다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Periodontology 2000, 2022;88(1):201–237
- **DOI**: 10.1111/prd.12419 · **PMID**: 35103325 · **PMCID**: PMC9304161
- **Type**: Comprehensive narrative review (University of Bern periodontology group)
- **Source**: **PMC full text retrieved** (JATS, ~80,000 characters). **Extraction gap**: the paper's concluding bullet list did not survive — the text runs from "the current evidence on function, esthetics, and cost-effectiveness indicates that:" straight into the CLINICAL CASES section. The summary claims below are therefore taken from the **PubMed abstract**, with the supporting detail from the retrieved body. Figures, tables and reference numerals were also stripped.

## 2. Key Contributions

- States plainly that implant rehabilitation does **not** show overall OHRQoL superiority over conventional prosthetics from the patient's perspective.
- Documents the **clinician–patient perception gap**, with clinicians applying stricter esthetic standards; degree of clinician specialisation itself affects esthetic index scoring (PES/WES, modified Implant Crown Esthetic Index).
- Shows expectation is influential but **not predictive** — a caution for consent conversations.
- Finds no patient-perceived esthetic difference between soft-tissue-level and bone-level implants, or between all-ceramic and metal-ceramic restorations.
- Supplies a concrete willingness-to-pay threshold for implant-assisted RPDs (**> €80 per OHIP point**).
- Surveys and compares the OHRQoL instruments, and concludes the lack of standardisation makes cross-study comparison unreliable.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Comprehensive electronic search of English-language publications up to 2021. A selection of standardised OHRQoL questionnaires and scales was analysed and explained. Analysis organised into three dimensions: functional evaluation, esthetic assessment, and cost-related evaluation of rehabilitation with dental implants. Narrative synthesis; no pooling, no risk-of-bias appraisal.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Comparison | OHRQoL finding |
|---|---|
| Implants vs conventional tooth-supported prostheses | **Not significantly greater** for implants |
| Soft-tissue-level vs bone-level implants | No significant esthetic difference |
| All-ceramic vs metal-ceramic | No significant difference in patient perception |
| Implant overdentures vs conventional dentures | Improved stability, function, satisfaction |
| Locator/ball attachments | High OHRQoL |
| Implant-assisted RPD **with** magnetic attachments vs without | Significantly increased OHRQoL |
| Implant-supported fixed vs FPD vs RPD (one cited study) | No statistically significant OHRQoL difference |

**Cited six-year comparison (Kurosaki et al.)** — cumulative prosthesis survival at 6 years:

| Prosthesis | 6-yr survival | OHRQoL change |
|---|---|---|
| Implant-supported fixed denture | **94.7%** | Significantly improved, sustained at 6 years |
| Fixed partial denture | **77.4%** | No improvement vs baseline |
| **Removable partial denture** | **33.3%** | No improvement vs baseline |

**Cost**: supporting a conventional RPD with implants is cost-effective when payers are willing to pay **> €80 per OHIP point gained**.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Narrative review**: no search protocol reported in the abstract, no pooling, no risk-of-bias appraisal of included work.
- The authors' own central limitation: **no consensus on definitions or standardisation** of OHRQoL instruments, so cross-study comparison is unreliable — which weakens every comparative statement in the paper, including its headline.
- **Extraction gap**: the concluding bullet list is missing from the retrieved text (see Document Information); summary claims rest on the abstract.
- **The Kurosaki RPD survival figure (33.3% at 6 years) conflicts sharply with the 95.1% five-year survival pooled in Drummond 2024.** Neither is reproduced here with its survival definition, and the discrepancy is almost certainly definitional (remake/relining counted as failure versus prosthesis still in service). **Do not cite either figure as *the* RPD survival rate** without checking the endpoint definition in the primary source.
- Author group is a periodontology/implant department; the framing is implant-centred and the removable axis appears mainly as comparator.
- Clinical cases appended to the review are single illustrative cases, not evidence.

## 7. Glossary

- **OHRQoL (구강건강관련 삶의 질, Oral Health-Related Quality of Life)**: patient-reported construct covering oral function, pain, appearance and psychosocial impact.
- **GOHAI · OHIP · SOHSI · DIDL · OIDP · PPIDAQ · SOOQ**: the principal OHRQoL instruments compared in the review; their non-equivalence is the paper's methodological warning.
- **PES/WES (핑크·화이트 심미점수, Pink/White Esthetic Score)**: clinician-scored indices of peri-implant soft tissue and crown esthetics.
- **Cost-utility analysis (비용효용분석)**: economic model expressing cost in monetary units against outcome measured as health/quality-of-life improvement.
- **Willingness to pay (지불의사)**: monetary threshold a payer accepts per unit of outcome gained; here, per OHIP point.
