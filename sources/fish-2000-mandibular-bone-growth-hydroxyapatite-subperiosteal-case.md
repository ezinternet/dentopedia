## Why Ingested

The wiki has few sources on subperiosteal implants, which are an alternative for severely atrophic jaws (see the full-arch and bone-quality pages). This Fish and Misch 2000 paper gives the design history of the tripodal mandibular subperiosteal implant and a case of apparent bone growth around a hydroxylapatite-coated version, and it is ingested for that historical and case-level record.

## Three-line Summary

Case report with design history (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2000) on a hydroxylapatite-coated mandibular subperiosteal implant — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The authors review strain-related modeling and remodeling, then describe apparent bone growth after placement, with revision needed for an acute infection at a permucosal site.

A single case report; the bone growth is an observation, not a generalizable effect, and infection at permucosal sites is a documented complication.
