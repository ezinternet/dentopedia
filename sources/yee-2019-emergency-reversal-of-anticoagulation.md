## Why Ingested

Emergency reversal reference for warfarin, LMWH, and DOACs; supports dental surgical bleeding management. → [[drug/anticoagulants/yee-2019-emergency-reversal-of-anticoagulation]]

## Three-line Summary

This narrative review summarizes physiologic hemostasis and current recommended reversal strategies for warfarin, heparin/LMWH, fondaparinux, and DOACs (dabigatran, apixaban, rivaroxaban, edoxaban, betrixaban), plus antiplatelet agents. Warfarin reversal uses IV vitamin K (onset 1–2 h, peak 4–6 h) ± 4-factor PCC (onset 10–30 min) or FFP; LMWH reversed ~60% by protamine 1 mg per 1 mg enoxaparin if given within 8 h. DOAC reversal hinges on timing (last dose >18 h → generally no reversal), with idarucizumab for dabigatran and andexanet alfa for factor Xa inhibitors.

## 세줄요약

이 내러티브 리뷰는 생리적 지혈과 와파린, 헤파린/LMWH, 폰다파리눅스, DOAC(다비가트란, 아픽사반, 리바록사반, 에독사반, 베틱사반) 및 항혈소판제의 현재 권장 가역 전략을 정리한다. 와파린 가역은 IV 비타민 K(작용 시작 1–2시간, 최대 4–6시간) ± 4인자 PCC(10–30분) 또는 FFP; LMWH는 8시간 이내 투여 시 프로타민 1 mg당 엔옥사파린 1 mg으로 약 60% 가역된다. DOAC 가역은 타이밍(마지막 복용 >18시간 → 일반적으로 가역 불필요)에 의존하며, 다비가트란은 이다루시주맙, Xa 인자는 안덱사넷 알파를 사용한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidated ED-focused reversal guidance for all common anticoagulants and antiplatelets.
- Quantified warfarin reversal pharmacokinetics: IV vitamin K onset 1–2 h, peak 4–6 h; PCC onset 10–30 min, duration 12–24 h; FFP onset 13–48 h.
- LMWH protamine dosing: 1 mg protamine neutralizes 1 mg enoxaparin if LMWH given within prior 8 h; only ~60% reversal of Xa effect.
- DOAC 18-hour rule: reversal generally not indicated if last dose >18 h prior.
- Idarucizumab 5 g IV (2×2.5 g vials) for dabigatran; RE-VERSE AD data (88–98% rapid reversal).
- Andexanet alfa ANNEXA-4: 92% median anti-Xa reduction, 82% good/excellent hemostasis at 12 h; dosing per Table 9 (not reproduced in text).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review; no original data. Synthesizes guidelines (2012 ACCP), trials (RE-VERSE AD, ANNEXA-4), volunteer studies, and meta-analyses. Tables 2–10 (dosing) referenced but not fully reproduced in extracted text.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Warfarin: IV vitamin K 10 mg for life-threatening bleeding (2012 ACCP); never SC/IM. FFP 10–15 mL/kg (4–5 units) for life-threatening hemorrhage; intrinsic INR 1.5; no benefit below INR 1.7. PCC dosing strategies: INR+weight-based, INR-based, fixed dose; immediate INR <1.5.
- Heparin half-life ~60 min. LMWH SC half-life 3–6 h. Fondaparinux half-life 17–21 h. Protamine >50 mg causes anticoagulation (factor V inhibition) — not recommended.
- Dabigatran: idarucizumab 5 g IV; RE-VERSE AD Group A median bleeding cessation ICH 11.4 h, GI 3.5 h; 30-day thrombotic events 4.8%.
- Factor Xa inhibitors: peak plasma ~2 h; steady state day 4; no reversal indication after 18 h. Andexanet ANNEXA-4: 352 patients (64% ICH, 26% GI); 30-day mortality 14%; thrombotic events 10%.
- Antiplatelets: DDAVP 0.4 mcg/kg IV one-time for ICH on antiplatelets; platelets 2–3 units/apheresis.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review; dosing tables not fully reproduced in text. Morbidity/mortality benefit of reversal not definitively demonstrated. Ciraparantag (PER977) needs further trials. Optimal andexanet infusion duration unclear.

## 7. Glossary

- PCC (Prothrombin Complex Concentrate): nonactivated factors II, VII, IX, X concentrate.
- INR (International Normalized Ratio): standardized PT.
- DOAC (Direct Oral Anticoagulant): direct factor Xa or IIa inhibitor.
- LMWH (Low-Molecular-Weight Heparin): depolymerized heparin, anti-Xa predominant.
- aPCC/FEIBA: activated PCC, factor VIII inhibitor bypassing activity.
- Idarucizumab (Praxbind): monoclonal antibody fragment binding dabigatran.
