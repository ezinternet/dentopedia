## Why Ingested
Directly compares CAP vs APA for 3Y-TZP AND 5YSZ to dentin using self-adhesive resin cement — the exact clinical scenario for zirconia inlay/onlay cementation. First study to show CAP ≈ APA for dentin bonding across both generation zirconia types, and first to explicitly show that combining treatments (APA+CAP, HF+CAP) reduces rather than improves SBS. Contextualizes and extends the single-type plasma evidence in [[dental-materials/zirconia/kamis-2023-cold-plasma-zirconia-resin-cement-bond]] and challenges "more treatment = better" assumptions in [[dental-materials/zirconia/nasri-2025-surface-conditioning-zirconia-bonding-umbrella]].

## One-line Summary
In vitro study (n=200, 3Y-TZP + 5YSZ, 10,000 thermocycles): CAP and APA produce equivalent SBS to dentin; zirconia type (3Y vs 5Y) does not affect SBS; combined treatments (APA+CAP, HF+CAP) reduce rather than increase bond strength; MDP primer is essential.

## 한줄요약
인비트로 (n=200, 3Y-TZP + 5YSZ, 10,000회 열순환): 냉온 대기압 플라즈마(CAP)와 공기분사연마(APA) 상아질 전단결합강도 동등; 지르코니아 종류(3Y vs 5Y) 차이 없음; 병용처리(APA+CAP, HF+CAP)는 오히려 결합강도 감소; 10-MDP 프라이머 필수.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First study comparing CAP vs APA for zirconia-to-DENTIN bonding (previous studies focused on composite or resin-only)
- Demonstrates 3Y-TZP and 5YSZ produce equivalent SBS with proper protocol — zirconia generation does not dictate bonding protocol
- Key finding: combinations (APA+CAP, HF+CAP) reduce SBS vs individual treatments — rough APA surface may interfere with CAP surface activation
- Primer critical: 10-MDP primer addition is the single most impactful variable (η²=0.86)
- HF acid etching confirmed ineffective even when combined with CAP — no role for HF in zirconia cementation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: In vitro study
- Population: n=200 (100 3Y-TZP + 100 5YSZ specimens); n=100 extracted molar dentin specimens
- Zirconia: ceraMotionZ HT (3Y-TZP) + ceraMotionZ Cubic (5YSZ), Dentaurum
- 5 surface treatments per zirconia type × 2 primer conditions = 10 subgroups (n=10 each)
- Surface treatments: Control / APA (50 μm Al₂O₃, 2 bar, 20 s) / CAP (dielectric barrier discharge, 20 kV, 20 kHz) / APA+CAP / HF+CAP (9.5% HF gel, 1 h)
- Dentin: phosphoric acid (37%) etch + Ambar universal adhesive
- Zirconia primer: Z-prime plus (Bisco, 50% specimens)
- Cement: Maxcem Elite Chroma (Kerr, self-adhesive resin cement)
- Aging: 10,000 thermocycles (5-55°C, 30 s dwell)
- SBS: Instron, 0.5 mm/min, bonding area = 12 mm²

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
SBS for 3Y-TZP (MPa):
| Treatment | No primer | With primer |
|---|---|---|
| Control | 4.83 | 7.56 |
| APA | 17.01 | 25.94 |
| CAP | 17.43 | 26.99 |
| APA+CAP | 15.42 | 24.40 |
| HF+CAP | 15.14 | 20.96 |

SBS for 5YSZ (MPa):
| Treatment | No primer | With primer |
|---|---|---|
| Control | 4.49 | 7.21 |
| APA | 16.75 | 26.27 |
| CAP | 17.52 | 26.89 |
| APA+CAP | 16.47 | 24.97 |
| HF+CAP | 14.94 | 22.26 |

Multiple-way ANOVA: Surface treatment p<0.0001 (η²=0.94); Primer p<0.0001 (η²=0.86); Zirconia type p=0.354 (ns)
CAP vs APA: comparable (p>0.05 within groups)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Self-adhesive cement only (no MDP-exclusive conventional cement)
- In vitro only; no dynamic loading or long-term clinical follow-up
- Dentin from extracted teeth (variable)
- No surface roughness / topography assessment
- Only 10,000 thermocycles (extended aging or fatigue testing lacking)

## 6. Related Work
- Kamis 2023: CAP for zirconia-composite bonding (single in vitro); current study extends to dentin substrate
- Nasri 2025: Surface conditioning umbrella review; current paper adds CAP comparative data
- Liu et al.: Non-thermal plasma for Y-TZP; context for CAP mechanism
- Salimi 2023 / Jiang 2024: Argon CAP + MDP primer highest SBS (consistent with current finding)

## 7. Glossary
- **CAP**: Cold Atmospheric Plasma (냉온 대기압 플라즈마)
- **APA**: Airborne Particle Abrasion (공기분사연마)
- **3Y-TZP**: 3 mol% yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal (1st/2nd generation, opaque)
- **5YSZ**: 5 mol% yttria-stabilized zirconia (high-translucency, higher cubic phase)
- **10-MDP**: 10-methacryloyloxydecyl dihydrogen phosphate (phosphate monomer, essential for zirconia bonding)
- **η²**: Partial eta squared (effect size in multiple-way ANOVA)
