## Why Ingested

This is the human-tissue counterpart to the dog models that carry most of the weight in [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/araujo-2005-dimensional-ridge-alterations-tooth-extraction]] and [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/cardaropoli-2003-bone-tissue-formation-extraction]]: it supplies the cellular timeline (granulation tissue → provisional matrix → woven bone, osteoblast peak at 6–8 wk) that later ARP syntheses such as [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/bassir-2018-alveolar-ridge-preservation-meta-analysis]] and [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/fok-2024-alveolar-socket-healing-evolving-knowledge]] use as the biological rationale. ## Three-line Summary

Prospective human histological biopsy series — 27 extraction-socket biopsies collected over a 6-month healing period and stratified into early (2–4 wk, n=10), intermediate (6–8 wk, n=6), and late (12–24 wk, n=11) phases, analysed with semi-quantitative scoring of tissue composition and cell populations.

Granulation tissue, abundant in the early phase, was replaced between the early and intermediate observation phases by provisional matrix and woven bone; vascular-structure and macrophage density declined slowly from 2–4 weeks onward, osteoblast presence peaked at 6–8 weeks and then remained almost stable, and only a small number of osteoclasts appeared in a few specimens at each interval.

Great inter-individual variability exists in hard-tissue formation: provisional connective tissue forms consistently in the first weeks, but the interval in which mineralized bone is laid down is much less predictable — which is the core argument against fixed-timing delayed implant placement and the biologic basis for ridge preservation.

## 세줄요약

전향적 인체 조직학 생검 연구 (prospective human histological biopsy series) — 6개월 치유 기간 동안 수집한 발치과 생검 (extraction-socket biopsy) 27개를 초기 (early, 2–4주 n=10), 중기 (intermediate, 6–8주 n=6), 후기 (late, 12–24주 n=11) 3시기로 나누어 조직 구성과세포군을 반정량적 점수 (semi-quantitative scoring)로 분석.

초기 시기에 다량 존재하던 육아조직 (granulation tissue) 은 초기–중기 관찰 시점 사이에 잠정 기질 (provisional matrix) 과 직조골 (woven bone) 로 대체되었고, 혈관구조 (vascular structure) 와 대식세포 (macrophage) 밀도는 2–4주부터 서서히 감소했으며, 골모세포 (osteoblast) 존재는 6–8주에 정점을 이룬 뒤 거의 안정 상태를 유지했고 파골세포 (osteoclast) 는 각 관찰 구간의 일부 검체에서 소수 관찰되었다.

인체에서 경골성 조직 형성 (hard-tissue formation) 은 개체 간 편차가 매우 크다 — 잠정 결합조직 (provisional connective tissue) 은 첫 수 주(weeks) 내 일관되게 형성되지만, 무기질화 골이 침착되는 시점은 훨씬 예측하기 어렵다. 이는 고정 기간 지연 식립 프로토콜의 한계 근거이자 능선 보존 (ridge preservation) 의 생물학적 근거이다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Human histological characterisation of extraction-socket healing monitored across a **6-month** period, with semi-quantitative analysis of both tissues and cell populations across the modeling and remodeling phases — the human counterpart to the dog work of Cardaropoli/Araujo/Lindhe.
- Establishes the tissue-and-cell sequence: abundant granulation tissue early → replacement by **provisional matrix + woven bone** between the early and intermediate observation phases → stable osteoblast presence thereafter.
- Fixes the **osteoblast peak at 6–8 weeks**, with presence remaining almost stable for the rest of the observation window, and documents only sparse osteoclasts (a few specimens per interval) — i.e. modeling and remodeling overlap rather than being cleanly separated.
- Documents **great inter-individual variability in hard-tissue formation** while showing the soft-tissue (provisional connective tissue) sequence is consistent — the central finding for implant-timing decisions.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective human histological biopsy case series (in-vivo, non-randomised, no control arm).
- **n**: 27 biopsies collected from human extraction sockets.
- **Healing-phase strata**: early 2–4 wk (n = 10); intermediate 6–8 wk (n = 6); late 12–24 wk (n = 11).
- **Aim/monitoring window**: 6-month period of socket healing.
- **Analysis**: Semi-quantitative scoring of tissues (granulation tissue, provisional matrix, woven bone) and cell populations (vascular structures, macrophages, osteoblasts, osteoclasts) at each observation interval.
- **Outcomes**: Relative amount/proportion of each tissue type; density of vascular structures and macrophages; density/presence of osteoblasts and osteoclasts; inter-individual variability in timing of mineralized bone deposition.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **n = 27 biopsies** total: 10 (2–4 wk) + 6 (6–8 wk) + 11 (12–24 wk) across a 6-month healing period.
- Granulation tissue was present "in comparatively large amounts" in the early phase and was **replaced between the early and intermediate observation phases** by provisional matrix and woven bone.
- Density of vascular structures and macrophages **slowly decreased over time from 2 to 4 weeks onward**.
- Osteoblast presence **peaked at 6–8 weeks and remained almost stable thereafter**; osteoclasts were "a small number … in a few specimens at each observation interval".
- Headline conclusion (qualitative, no statistics reported in the abstract): **great variability in man with respect to hard-tissue formation within extraction sockets** — provisional connective tissue forms consistently in the first weeks, whereas the interval of mineralized bone deposition is much less predictable.
- Reported from the abstract only; the archived PDF carries no tables, figures, p-values, or effect sizes (abstract-only capture — see note above).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Explicitly stated (abstract level)**: marked inter-individual variability in hard-tissue formation; no predictive marker for when mineralized bone will appear.
- **Stated as design scope**: semi-quantitative (not quantitative/morphometric) assessment of tissues and cell populations.
- **Inferred — small per-phase n** (10 / 6 / 11), with the intermediate phase the thinnest (n = 6), limiting group-level inference.
- **Inferred — no control/comparator**: single-arm biopsy series; cannot separate the effect of the extraction itself from patient/site factors.
- **Inferred — wide late-phase window** (12–24 wk) and heterogeneous tooth types, socket morphology, and systemic status (no stratification for smoking, diabetes, or site).
- **Inferred — biopsy itself perturbs the socket** it is meant to observe.
- **No clinical/volumetric outcomes**: histological endpoints only, so the biological timeline cannot be directly mapped to ridge dimension or implant placement timing.

## 7. Glossary
- **Modeling (bone)**: Change in bone form by net addition or removal at a surface, not by coupled resorption–formation cycles.
- **Remodeling (bone)**: Coupled osteoclast–osteoblast turnover that replaces existing bone with new bone of the same gross shape.
- **Granulation tissue**: Highly vascular loose connective tissue rich in fibroblasts, macrophages, and new capillaries, dominating the first weeks of socket repair.
- **Provisional matrix**: Immature connective-tissue matrix laid down after granulation tissue and before woven bone; the consistent early phase of socket healing.
- **Woven bone**: Immature, randomly organised bone with abundant osteocytes, the first mineralised matrix before lamellar bone replaces it.
- **Osteoblast**: Bone-forming cell responsible for matrix synthesis and mineralisation; peaks at 6–8 wk in this study.
- **Osteoclast**: Multinucleated bone-resorbing cell; sparse and inconsistent here, indicating early healing with ongoing modelling.
- **Semi-quantitative scoring**: Ordinal histology grading (e.g. absent / sparse / moderate / abundant) of tissue and cell populations, as opposed to stereometric or morphometric quantification.
