## Why Ingested

This is the wiki's first paper on **mandibular** (rather than maxillary) Invisalign distalization, and the first to quantify it specifically in **Class III** malocclusion using CBCT voxel-based superimposition rather than digital-model/iTero registration. It directly puts numbers on the aligner+TAD combination that [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/marinelli-2025-temporary-anchorage-devices-clear-aligner-sr]] found only case-report-level evidence for (their cited Lin case: "3D mandibular-molar distalization"), contrasting miniscrew anchorage against Class III elastics in a pilot cohort.

## Three-line Summary

Pilot pre/post study (prospective vs retrospective enrolment not stated), 12 Class III adults, digital models integrated with CBCT — voxel-based registration on stable cranial base structures for maxillary tooth/mandible-position assessment, and symphysis/mandibular-body registration for mandibular tooth movement — comparing the ClinCheck virtual plan against the actual outcome.

Median accuracy of mandibular first-molar cusp distal movement was 26.72-33.87% with Class III elastics (actual movement 0.62 +/- 0.82 to 0.80 +/- 0.89 mm) versus 39.28-44.66% with miniscrew anchorage (actual movement 2.02 +/- 1.71 to 2.29 +/- 1.53 mm); maxillary incisors proclined significantly more under elastic traction, and only the elastics group showed significant positional change of the condyle head.

Invisalign mandibular dentition distalisation falls well short of the ClinCheck-planned movement, especially without skeletal (miniscrew) anchorage; the exact accuracy numbers, sample composition (elastics vs miniscrews split), and any statistical tests beyond what is quoted above are not recoverable from the abstract alone.

## 세줄요약

파일럿 전후비교 연구(전향/후향 여부 초록 미기재), Class III 성인 12명, 디지털모델을 CBCT와 통합 — 상악 치아·하악 위치 평가는 두개저(cranial base) 안정 구조물 기준 voxel 기반 정합, 하악 치아이동은 하악 정중부(symphysis)·하악체 기준 정합 — 하여 ClinCheck 가상계획 대 실제결과 비교.

하악 제1대구치 교두 원심이동의 중앙값 정확도는 Class III 고무줄(elastics) 사용 시 26.72~33.87%(실제이동 0.62±0.82~0.80±0.89mm), 미니스크류(miniscrew) 고정원 사용 시 39.28~44.66%(실제이동 2.02±1.71~2.29±1.53mm); 상악 절치는 고무줄 사용군에서 유의하게 더 순측경사, 과두(condyle) 위치 변화도 고무줄군에서만 유의.

인비절라인(Invisalign) 하악 치열 원심이동은 ClinCheck 계획 대비 상당히 미달하며 특히 골격성(미니스크류) 고정원이 없을 때 두드러짐; 정확한 정확도 수치의 산출 근거, 표본 구성(고무줄/미니스크류 분할), 그 외 통계검정 세부는 초록만으로는 확인 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First quantification (in this wiki) of Invisalign accuracy for **mandibular** dentition distalisation, as opposed to the maxillary-molar-distalization literature already held.
- Applies CBCT voxel-based superimposition (cranial base for maxilla, symphysis/mandibular body for mandible) rather than surface-scan/palatal-rugae registration — a more rigorous 3D hard-tissue reference method.
- Directly contrasts Class III elastics vs miniscrew anchorage for the same movement, quantifying a roughly 13-15 percentage-point accuracy gap between the two anchorage strategies.
- Documents a skeletal side effect specific to elastics-only anchorage: significant condylar positional change, absent when miniscrews are used.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Pilot pre/post (T1/T2) digital-model + CBCT superimposition study. **Prospective vs retrospective enrolment is not stated in the retrieved abstract** — `evidence_level: retrospective` is assigned as the conservative rung, not as a verified design, consistent with how [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/saif-2022-efficiency-evaluation-maxillary-molar-distalization]] and [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/liu-2025-maxillary-molar-distalization-invisalign-adult]] were graded on the same basis.
- **Population**: 12 Class III adults, pretreatment (T1) and posttreatment (T2).
- **Data source**: Digital models integrated with maxillofacial CBCT images. T1/T2 CBCT superimposed via voxel-based registration on stable cranial base structures (maxillary tooth movement and mandibular positional change); mandibular tooth movement assessed via CBCT superimposition registered on the symphysis and mandibular body. The ClinCheck virtual planning model was superimposed on its position relative to the T1 digital model.
- **n**: 12 patients; exact per-tooth or per-anchorage-subgroup counts not stated in the abstract.
- **Outcomes**: Accuracy (achieved/planned %) of mandibular first-molar cusp distal movement; actual distal movement (mm) under Class III elastics vs miniscrew anchorage; maxillary incisor proclination; positional change of mandibular anatomical landmarks (condyle head).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Median accuracy of mandibular first-molar cusp distal movement: 26.72-33.87% with Class III elastics; 39.28-44.66% with miniscrew anchorage.
- Actual distal movement: 0.62 +/- 0.82 mm to 0.80 +/- 0.89 mm with Class III elastics; 2.02 +/- 1.71 mm to 2.29 +/- 1.53 mm with miniscrews.
- Maxillary incisors proclined significantly more with Class III elastic traction (vs miniscrew anchorage).
- No significant positional change in mandibular anatomical landmarks during treatment with miniscrew anchorage; Class III elastics led to significant positional change in the condyle head.
- Conclusion (verbatim sense): Invisalign treatment may not reach the expected efficacy in mandibular dentition distalisation, especially when skeletal anchorage is absent.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Pilot study, n=12 — small sample, likely underpowered for subgroup comparisons; no confidence intervals or p-values recoverable from the abstract.
- Abstract-only source (PMC full text empty): exact statistical tests, elastics-vs-miniscrew group sizes, and any adjustment for confounders are not verifiable beyond what is quoted here.
- Single-arm-per-condition design (no fixed-appliance comparator); generalizability beyond Class III adults with these specific anchorage protocols not addressed in the retrieved text.

## 7. Glossary
- **CBCT (cone beam computed tomography)**: 3D X-ray imaging producing volumetric hard-tissue data, used here for voxel-based superimposition rather than surface-scan digital-model registration.
- **Voxel-based registration**: superimposition method that aligns two 3D image volumes (here, T1 and T2 CBCT scans) on stable reference structures (cranial base; symphysis/mandibular body) rather than surface landmarks.
- **ClinCheck**: Align Technology's proprietary software that generates the virtual staged tooth-movement plan for Invisalign treatment, used here as the "predicted"/planned movement against which actual outcome is compared.
- **Class III elastics**: intermaxillary elastic traction (typically maxillary-anterior-to-mandibular-posterior) used in Class III camouflage/correction, here compared against miniscrew (TAD) anchorage for mandibular distalization.
