## Why Ingested

기존 위키의 alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) 근거는 개별 SR/MA 단위(예: [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/couso-queiruga-2021-post-extraction-dimensional-changes-systematic]], [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/vanderweijden-2009-alveolar-bone-dimensional-changes]])로 흩어져 있었다. Fok & Jin (2024)은 histology부터 dimensional change 근거의 overview-of-SRs, sinus pneumatisation 논쟁, 임상 인자, ARP 효능·기술변수·비용효과까지 하나의 narrative synthesis로 묶어, 기존 개별 페이지들 사이의 관계를 정리하는 상위 종합 문헌으로 유용하다.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review with an embedded overview-of-systematic-reviews (Journal of Dentistry 2024) on post-extraction alveolar socket healing — screened 459 records, included 11 systematic reviews (3 with meta-analysis, 1 network meta-analysis) on unassisted-healing dimensional change, all AMSTAR-2 "critically low" quality, plus narrative synthesis of histology, sinus pneumatisation, clinical/procedural/site factors, and alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) efficacy/technique/cost-effectiveness.

Horizontal ridge width reduction (29–63%) exceeds vertical reduction (11–22%) over 3–7 months with most modelling in the first 3 months; Couso-Queiruga (2021, 28 studies) pooled non-molar horizontal reduction 2.54 mm (95% CI 1.97–3.11) vs molar 3.61 mm (95% CI 3.24–3.98); ARP with xenograft/allograft attenuates but does not eliminate dimensional change (Cochrane: width MD 1.18 mm, height MD 1.35 mm vs extraction alone), flapless technique is not inferior to flap-and-primary-closure and may favor soft-tissue outcomes.

Clinical implication: ARP decisions should be individualised and prosthetically driven rather than routine, since evidence quality for ARP-efficacy reviews is uniformly critically low (AMSTAR-2) and dimensional change is only a surrogate outcome — the paper calls for standardised core outcome sets and explores AI as a future aid to healing prediction.

## 세줄요약

발치 후 치조와 치유(post-extraction alveolar socket healing)에 관한 서술적 리뷰(narrative review) + 삽입된 체계적 문헌고찰들의 개관(overview of SRs) (Journal of Dentistry 2024) — 459편 중 11편의 SR(3편은 메타분석 포함, 1편은 네트워크 메타분석)을 선정했고, 비보조 치유(unassisted healing)의 치수변화 근거는 AMSTAR-2 기준 전부 "critically low" 품질이었으며, 조직학·상악동 함기화(sinus pneumatisation) 논쟁·임상/시술/부위 요인·치조제보존술(Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 효능·기술변수·비용효과까지 종합했다.

수평 치조제 폭 감소(29–63%)가 수직 감소(11–22%)보다 3–7개월 동안 더 크며 대부분의 개조는 첫 3개월 내 발생; Couso-Queiruga(2021, 28편)는 비구치부(non-molar) 수평 감소 2.54mm(95% CI 1.97–3.11) vs 구치부(molar) 3.61mm(95% CI 3.24–3.98)로 보고; ARP(이종골/동종골 이식재 사용)는 치수변화를 완화하지만 완전히 막지는 못함(Cochrane: 폭 MD 1.18mm, 높이 MD 1.35mm, 발치단독 대비), 무판막(flapless) 술식이 판막거상+1차봉합 술식에 비해 열등하지 않고 연조직 결과에서 유리할 수 있음.

임상적 의미: ARP 효능 근거의 AMSTAR-2 품질이 전부 critically low이고 치수변화는 대리결과(surrogate outcome)에 불과하므로, ARP는 일률적 시행이 아닌 개별화·보철 주도(prosthetically-driven) 결정이어야 하며, 저자들은 표준화된 core outcome set 정립과 향후 인공지능(AI)의 치유예측 활용 가능성을 제언한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates histological/molecular alveolar socket healing (4 overlapping phases: haemostasis/coagulation, inflammation, proliferation, bone modelling & remodelling) into a single up-to-date narrative.
- Conducts a dedicated overview of systematic reviews (Feb 2024, 4 databases, 2004–2024) specifically on unassisted post-extraction dimensional change, appraising all included SRs with AMSTAR-2 (all rated critically low quality) — a useful quality-of-evidence cross-check for the wiki's individual ARP/dimensional-change SR pages.
- Synthesizes patient-, procedure-, and site-related factors affecting healing (facial bone plate thickness, flap vs flapless, socket integrity, systemic status) and connects them to ARP decision-making.
- Reviews ARP efficacy, technical variables (grafting material, sealing, flap design), and a rare cost-effectiveness angle (Barootchi et al. analysis), plus raises AI as a future direction for healing prediction — the first wiki source to flag this specific AI angle for ARP.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Narrative literature review with an embedded overview-of-systematic-reviews component (for the dimensional-change question only); other objectives (histology, sinus pneumatisation, clinical factors, ARP efficacy/technique/cost) analysed narratively, not systematically.
- **Databases** (overview-of-SR component): PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, searched 2004–2024, February 2024, plus manual search.
- **n**: 459 records retrieved → 11 systematic reviews included (7 SR only, 4 SR+MA including 1 network meta-analysis); inter-reviewer agreement Cohen's kappa 0.93.
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR-2 tool applied to all 11 included SRs — all rated "critically low" overall quality (each with ≥1 critical weakness across 16 domains); most commonly missing: registered protocol, funding-source reporting of primary studies, risk-of-bias consideration in interpretation.
- **Outcomes**: primary — dimensional (horizontal/vertical) ridge change after unassisted healing and after ARP; secondary — sinus pneumatisation, need for additional augmentation, cost-effectiveness, patient-reported outcomes.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Buccal alveolar plate thickness (Heimes meta-analysis, 45 studies): 0.76±0.49 mm (maxillary anterior) up to 1.42±0.74 mm (maxillary molar); Asian populations show lowest thickness values (accounts for 87% of geographic heterogeneity).
- Unassisted healing: horizontal width reduction 29–63% vs vertical reduction 11–22% over 3–7 months, most change in first 3 months.
- Tan et al. (6 re-entry studies): 6-month vertical reduction 1.24±0.11 mm (buccal), 0.84±0.62 mm (mesial), 0.80±0.71 mm (distal); horizontal reduction 3.79±0.23 mm (5 studies).
- Couso-Queiruga et al. (28 studies, quantitative): non-molar radiographic reduction — vertical mid-facial 1.65 mm (95% CI 0.42–2.88), mid-lingual 0.87 mm (95% CI 0.36–1.38), horizontal 2.54 mm (95% CI 1.97–3.11); molar sites — vertical mid-facial 1.46 mm, mid-lingual 1.20 mm, horizontal 3.61 mm (95% CI 3.24–3.98). Facial plate >1 mm associated with significantly less width/height reduction.
- ARP vs extraction alone (Cochrane, most recent): xenograft reduced width loss MD 1.18 mm and height loss MD 1.35 mm (95% CI −2.00 to −0.70; 6 studies, 184 participants, 201 sites); allograft reduced height loss MD 3.73 mm (95% CI −4.05 to −3.41; 1 study). No significant difference in additional-augmentation need or implant failure; overall certainty low–very low.
- Flapless vs flap ARP (Atieh, 149 sockets/5 RCTs): no significant difference in width/height change, but flapless favoured patient-reported outcomes and keratinized tissue width/thickness; Barootchi et al. (non-molar only) found flap elevation + primary closure associated with greater midbuccal vertical/horizontal loss.
- Cost-effectiveness (Barootchi et al.): xenograft = highest cost, followed by allograft, alloplast; xenograft/allograft-based approaches show superior performance per cost, consistent with Canullo et al.'s network meta-analysis findings.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All 11 SRs underlying the dimensional-change synthesis are AMSTAR-2 "critically low" quality — conclusions on unassisted healing magnitude and ARP efficacy carry low certainty.
- Heterogeneous primary-study methodologies (assessment method, tooth location/type, extraction reason, socket integrity) prevent robust subgroup analysis in most included reviews.
- Dimensional change is treated as the dominant outcome across ARP literature but is only a surrogate; the authors call for a standardised core outcome set (additional augmentation need, complications, cost, implant success, PROMs).
- Cost-effectiveness analyses to date (e.g., Barootchi et al.) do not account for re-grafting likelihood or total biomaterial/time investment, limiting cross-protocol comparison.
- Authors flag AI-assisted healing prediction and clinical decision-making as an unexplored future direction, without providing evidence for it (aspirational, not evaluated in this paper).

## 6. Related Work

- couso-queiruga-2021: Quantitative SR+MA (28 studies) providing the pooled molar/non-molar dimensional-change estimates this review cites as the most detailed dataset.
- vanderweijden-2009: Earlier canonical SR (12 studies) establishing baseline unassisted-healing dimensional-change magnitude; this review situates it as one of the 11 AMSTAR-2-appraised SRs.
- tan-2012: Re-entry-study SR (6 studies) cited for vertical/horizontal reduction estimates with acknowledged heterogeneity limitations.
- avila-ortiz-2014: SR+MA on ARP efficacy vs extraction alone; this review's ARP-efficacy narrative aligns with and extends its "attenuates but does not eliminate" conclusion.

## 7. Glossary

- **ARP**: Alveolar Ridge Preservation — grafting/sealing procedures performed at time of extraction to limit post-extraction dimensional change.
- **AMSTAR-2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews (version 2) — 16-domain critical-appraisal instrument for SR methodological quality.
- **APDDM**: Autogenous Partially Demineralised Dentin Matrix — a slower-resorbing autogenous graft material used in ARP.
- **dPTFE / ePTFE**: dense / expanded polytetrafluoroethylene — non-resorbable membrane materials used for socket sealing.
- **Sinus pneumatisation**: expansion of the maxillary sinus into the alveolar process following posterior tooth loss, discussed here as a controversial/debated post-extraction sequela.
