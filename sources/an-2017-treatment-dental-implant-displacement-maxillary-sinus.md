## Three-line Summary

Narrative review + illustrative case (Maxillofac Plast Reconstr Surg 2017, Chonnam National University, Korea) on removing dental implants displaced into the maxillary sinus.

Immediate surgical removal is the principle (Caldwell-Luc → now minimally invasive FESS or lateral bony-window); the authors propose an extended vertical bony window that removes the implant and does simultaneous sinus grafting + new implant placement in one stage.

Clinical implication: displaced implants should be retrieved even when asymptomatic (foreign-body chronic sinusitis risk); the single-stage extended-window approach shortens rehabilitation versus the conventional 2–3-stage protocol.

## 세줄요약

내러티브 리뷰 + 증례 (Maxillofac Plast Reconstr Surg 2017, 전남대): 상악동으로 변위된 치과 임플란트의 제거.

즉시 외과적 제거가 원칙(Caldwell-Luc → 현재는 최소침습 FESS 또는 측방 골창); 저자들은 세로로 확장한 골창으로 제거 + 상악동 골이식 + 신규 식립을 1회에 동시 시행하는 술식을 제안.

임상적 의미: 무증상이어도 변위 임플란트는 제거해야 함(이물반응성 만성 부비동염 위험); 확장 골창 단일 단계 술식이 기존 2–3단계 프로토콜보다 재활 기간을 단축.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Consolidates **etiology** of implant displacement into the sinus (pneumatization, residual bone <4 mm, simultaneous graft, poor primary stability, over-drilling, excessive removal force).
2. Reviews **retrieval approaches** — Caldwell-Luc, FESS, lateral bony-window (Tsodoulos, Nogami piezoelectric + endoscope).
3. Proposes a **simplified single-stage extended bony-window** technique combining implant removal + sinus grafting + immediate new implant placement.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review of maxillary-sinus anatomy, displacement complications, and treatment modalities, illustrated by a single case (63M, implant displaced 3 years, left maxillary sinusitis). No systematic search or pooled data.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Anatomy**: Schneiderian membrane ~1.0 mm; ostium far from sinus floor; PSA nerve ~16.9 mm from crest; septa 31.7% in premolar region (perforation risk).
- **Complications**: mucociliary clearance inhibition, mucosal thickening, ostium obstruction → sinusitis; oroantral fistula; further migration to paranasal sinuses/orbit/cranial fossa. Controversy: some displaced implants stay asymptomatic (Galindo-Moreno 4-yr follow-up), but foreign-body chronic sinusitis argues for removal even if asymptomatic (Regev, Raghoebar).
- **Pre-surgical infection control** (delayed cases): amoxicillin-clavulanate + NSAID + pseudoephedrine for 1 week.
- **Extended-window case**: vertically enlarged lateral window (upper = implant removal via horizontal incision + dental suction; lower = membrane lift), perforation covered with collagen membrane, simultaneous graft (tuberosity autograft + allograft) + new implant. CBCT clear at 4 months; 2nd stage at 6 months; no complications at 14 months.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single illustrative case; no comparative or long-term cohort data.
- Simultaneous single-stage approach contraindicated when primary stability is unattainable, membrane perforation is extensive, or severe infection is present (staged approach then required).

## 7. Glossary

- **Caldwell-Luc procedure** — classic anterior maxillary-wall antrostomy for access to the maxillary sinus.
- **FESS** — Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery: transnasal endoscopic sinus access.
- **Schneiderian membrane** — the sinus mucoperiosteal lining (~1 mm).
- **Oroantral fistula** — pathological communication between oral cavity and maxillary sinus.
- **Pneumatization** — expansion of the sinus into edentulous alveolar bone after tooth loss.
