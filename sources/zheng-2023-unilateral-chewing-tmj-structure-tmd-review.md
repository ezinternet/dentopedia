## Why Ingested

기존 TMD 위키는 진단·치료(스플린트·관절천자·BTX·운동)와 유병률·교합중재 근거에 집중돼 있는데, "편측저작 (Chewing-side Preference / Unilateral Chewing)"이라는 행동성·기능성 위험인자가 TMJ 구조(髁突·관절와·관절원판)와 TMD 발생을 어떻게 매개하는지 구조→기전 사슬을 다룬 페이지가 없었다. 본 중국어 narrative review (Zheng 2023, J Zhejiang Univ Med Sci)는 편측저작→TMJ 구조 변화→TMD의 중심 reference로, 교합인자-TMD 논쟁에 기능적 행동 변수를 보강한다. 유병률 anchor [[tmj/valesan-2021-tmd-prevalence-sr-ma]] (성인 31%)를 본문이 직접 인용하며, 교합중재 효과 회의론 [[tmj/singh-2024-occlusal-interventions-tmd-cochrane-sr]] 및 TMJOA 기전 [[tmj/song-2023-understanding-tmj-osteoarthritis-pathophysiology]] (과부하→VEGF·MMP)과 연결된다.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (J Zhejiang University Medical Sciences 2023, n=15 clinical studies tabulated) synthesizing human imaging (CBCT), clinical cross-sectional/case-control, animal (rat), and FEA studies on chewing-side preference (defined: one side >30% more cycles) as a TMD risk factor.

Preferred-side condyles show reduced posterior-superior joint space, greater articular eminence inclination and glenoid fossa depth, and higher rates of anterior disc displacement; clinical data show TMD patients have significantly higher rates of unilateral chewing (e.g., 56.5% of TMD vs healthy groups, Wu 2021 n=754), with severity correlating with chewing asymmetry (Yalçın 2017 n=519).

Three mechanistic pathways are proposed: biomechanical (BMP-2/VEGF-driven condylar cartilage remodeling), masticatory-muscle (CaN-pathway Type II→I fiber shift with EMG asymmetry), and neurogenic (substance P/CGRP upregulation in trigeminal ganglion); however, the narrative review lacks systematic search, risk-of-bias assessment, and directionality (cause vs consequence) remains uncertain.

## 세줄요약

서술적 고찰 (Journal of Zhejiang University Medical Sciences 2023, 임상연구 15편 표 집계) — 편측저작 (Chewing-side Preference, 한쪽 저작 주기수가 30% 초과)을 악관절장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)의 위험인자로 다룬 CBCT·임상·동물·유한요소분석 연구를 종합.

偏향側 髁突에서 후방·후상방 관절 간극 감소, 관절결절 경사도·관절와 깊이 증가, 비환원성 전방 관절원판 전위 비율 증가; 임상 데이터에서 TMD 환자의 편측저작 비율이 건강군보다 유의하게 높음 (Wu 2021 n=754: TMD의 56.5%), 중증도가 저작 비대칭과 비례 (Yalçın 2017 n=519).

기전 3축 제시: 생물역학 (BMP-2·VEGF 髁突 연골 재형성), 저작근 (CaN 경로 통한 Type II→I 섬유 전환·근전도 비대칭), 신경원성 (삼차신경절 P물질·CGRP 상향조절); 그러나 체계적 검색·비뚤림 평가 없는 서술적 고찰이며 인과 방향성이 미확정.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Synthesizes Chinese and international literature establishing chewing-side preference (defined: chewing cycles on one side >30% more than the other) as an important, biomechanically-plausible TMD risk factor — adding a *functional behavioral* variable to the contested occlusion–TMD debate.
2. Structures the evidence into a three-level chain: (a) effect of unilateral chewing on TMJ structure (condyle, glenoid fossa, articular disc); (b) clinical association with TMD signs/symptoms (Table 1, 15 studies); (c) candidate mechanisms (biomechanical, masticatory-muscle, neurogenic).
3. Highlights three mechanistic pathways: BMP-2/VEGF-mediated condylar cartilage remodeling under altered loading; calcineurin (CaN)-driven masticatory-muscle fiber-type transformation (Type II→Type I) with EMG asymmetry; and substance P / CGRP upregulation producing TMJ neurogenic inflammation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review (not systematic). The authors collate human imaging studies (mostly CBCT), clinical cross-sectional/case-control studies, animal (rat) experiments, and finite-element analyses. Table 1 tabulates 15 clinical studies on the chewing-side-preference↔TMD relationship (study aim, subjects, method, key result, reference). No formal search strategy, risk-of-bias, or pooled estimate is reported.

**Definition.** A chewing cycle (closing→occlusion→opening) is the basic mastication unit; when one side's cycle count exceeds the other by >30%, it is termed unilateral chewing / chewing-side preference. Etiology is multifactorial — central factors (lateralization akin to handedness) and peripheral factors (missing teeth, caries, periapical disease, joint and masticatory-muscle factors). Reported prevalence ~87% (deciduous), ~82% (mixed), ~76% (permanent dentition).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Effect on TMJ structure**
- *Condyle:* On the preferred (working) side — reduced posterior, posterior-superior, lateral and superior joint spaces; greater condylar neck width; overall smaller condyle that migrates superiorly. Non-preferred side shows compensatory anterior joint-space enlargement. (Jiang; Lyu; Li Jia.) One CBCT study (Costa 2021) found no significant condylar/coronoid volume difference — conflicting evidence.
- *Glenoid fossa / articular eminence:* Greater articular eminence inclination on preferred side (Jiang 2015); TMD-with-unilateral-chewing group has significantly higher eminence inclination than TMD-without and healthy groups, with greater fossa depth (Ma 2021). Working-side condyle sits posterosuperiorly during normal chewing; repeated unilateral loading drives adaptive remodeling.
- *Articular disc:* Increased eminence inclination + fossa depth predispose to disc displacement during translation. In anterior-disc-displacement patients with unilateral chewing, the preferred side shows significantly higher rates of non-reducing anterior disc displacement, pain and joint noise (Huang 2021).

**Clinical association (Table 1, selected)**
- Wu 2021 (754 medical students): TMD prevalence 31.7%; 56.5% of TMD patients had unilateral chewing — significantly higher than healthy; a high-risk factor.
- Yu 2015 (616 male civilian pilots): TMD prevalence 33.3%; anxiety and unilateral chewing the most significant associated factors.
- Yalçın 2017 (519 university students): unilateral chewing rate rose with TMD severity — 36.8% (no TMD), 46.9% (mild), 54.6% (moderate), 65.0% (severe).
- Other study (109 TMD vs 109 healthy): unilateral chewing 69.7% vs 54.1%.
- Symptom correlations: unilateral chewing significantly associated with mouth-opening pain, joint pain, orofacial pain, limited jaw movement, joint clicking (Chua; Diernberger; Ma; others). TMJOA-with-unilateral-chewing patients more prone to pain and to condylar surface bony destruction/flattening (Zhai 2022) → possible TMJOA-promoting factor.

**Mechanisms**
- *Biomechanical:* In unilateral-chewing rats, chewing-side condylar cartilage shows higher BMP-2, IL-1β, TNF-α, Type II collagen, IL-1, VEGF and increased cartilage thickness; non-chewing side thins. Working-side condyle rotates (compression near initial position), non-working side translates (compression on anterior fossa) → long-term anterior remodeling of non-chewing fossa and deepening of chewing-side fossa. FEA: non-chewing-side disc receives greater medial-lateral pressure → retrodiscal/muscle pain. Animal histology: trabecular osteocyte loss, empty lacunae, thinned cartilage, disc collagen disorganization, synovial proliferation/damage.
- *Masticatory muscle:* Long-term unilateral chewing raises intracellular Ca²⁺ → activates calcineurin (CaN) pathway → transcription of slow-fiber genes → Type II→Type I fiber transformation on preferred side; preferred-side masseter/temporalis EMG activity higher than non-preferred; focal inflammatory infiltrate → prostaglandin E2 → fiber rupture and muscle pain; positively correlated with asymmetry of bite force and occlusal contact area.
- *Neurogenic:* Trigeminal ganglion synthesizes substance P and CGRP, delivered to TMJ; both enhance nociceptive transmission and vascular permeability → local edema, inflammatory-cell infiltration, neurogenic inflammation. Unilateral-chewing rats show upregulated substance P / CGRP in trigeminal ganglion, with higher substance P synthesis on the chewing side.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review without systematic search, risk-of-bias appraisal, or quantitative pooling.
- Human structural studies are limited in sample size/type; age, sex, preferred side, central vs peripheral cause and duration of unilateral chewing are rarely stratified — directionality (cause vs consequence) remains uncertain.
- Conflicting CBCT volume data (Costa 2021 null).
- Mechanistic data (BMP-2, CaN, substance P/CGRP) are largely from rat models; human translation and specific signaling mechanisms need further study.
- Clinical message: clinicians should attend to unilateral chewing and its causes in both healthy and TMD patients, and correct it early to help prevent/limit TMD.

## 7. Glossary

- **Chewing-side preference / Unilateral chewing (偏侧咀嚼):** habitual chewing where one side's cycle count exceeds the other by >30%.
- **TMJ (颞下颌关节):** temporomandibular joint — glenoid fossa, articular disc, condyle, capsule, ligaments.
- **TMD (颞下颌关节紊乱):** temporomandibular disorders — group of conditions of masticatory muscles, TMJ and related structures.
- **TMJOA:** temporomandibular joint osteoarthritis.
- **Articular eminence inclination (关节结节斜度):** slope of the articular eminence governing condyle/disc translation path.
- **BMP-2:** bone morphogenetic protein 2 — anabolic factor inducing bone/cartilage formation.
- **VEGF:** vascular endothelial growth factor.
- **CaN (calcineurin):** Ca²⁺-dependent signaling molecule regulating skeletal-muscle fiber-type remodeling.
- **Substance P / CGRP:** neuropeptides from trigeminal ganglion mediating nociception and neurogenic inflammation.
