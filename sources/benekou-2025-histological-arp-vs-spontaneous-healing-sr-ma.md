## Why Ingested
Provides histomorphometric ground truth for ARP vs spontaneous healing — a dimension orthogonal to the CBCT-based dimensional SR+MAs already in the wiki (e.g., [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/atieh-2015-alveolar-ridge-preservation-cochrane-review]], [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/avila-ortiz-2014-alveolar-ridge-preservation-systematic-review]]). Prior meta-analyses addressed ridge width/height; this 2025 SR+MA of 22 RCTs is the largest to date pooling biopsy-level new bone formation (%), connective tissue (%), and residual graft content (%) — directly informing the decay candidate for [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/ridge-preservation]] open-healing dimension.

## One-line Summary
SR+MA of 22 RCTs (n=816) finds no significant overall benefit of ARP on new bone formation vs spontaneous healing (MD −5.86%, p=0.15), but PRF/PRP subgroup shows +16.28% (p<0.0001) and long-term follow-up (>3 mo, sensitivity analysis) favors ARP (MD −16.30%, p<0.00001).

## 한줄요약
22개 RCT 메타분석: ARP vs 자연치유 신생골 형성 전체 유의차 없음(MD −5.86%, p=0.15); PRF/PRP 아군에서 +16.28%(p<0.0001), 장기 추적(>3개월) 민감도 분석에서는 ARP 유리(p<0.00001).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 22 RCTs (n=816; 505 ARP / 311 spontaneous healing) evaluating biopsy-confirmed histomorphometric outcomes — new bone formation (%), connective tissue (%), and residual graft content (%) — after alveolar ridge preservation versus spontaneous healing; PRISMA 2020, random-effects model, GRADE moderate certainty.

Overall, ARP did not significantly increase new bone formation versus spontaneous healing (MD −5.86%, 95% CI −13.84 to 2.11%, p=0.15, I²=98%); however, PRF/PRP subgroup significantly favored ARP (+16.28%, p<0.0001), xenograft+collagen membrane significantly disfavored ARP (−22.47%, p=0.004), and sensitivity analysis restricted to >3-month biopsies reversed the overall finding in favor of ARP (MD −16.30%, p<0.00001, I²=51%).

Material selection — not ARP per se — determines histological benefit: PRF/PRP improves bone formation, slow-resorbing xenografts leave residual graft burden (pooled 20.49%) and do not improve net bone formation; protocols using xenograft+membrane should account for this trade-off, and longer post-extraction intervals (>3 months) before implant placement may improve bone quality.

## 세줄요약

체계적 고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review + Meta-Analysis, SR+MA) — 22개 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (816명; 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 505 / 자연치유 311) — 발치 후 생검으로 확인한 조직형태학적 결과(신생골 형성률·결합조직률·잔존이식재율) 비교; PRISMA 2020, 변량효과 모형, GRADE 중간 확실도.

전체 분석에서 ARP vs 자연치유 신생골 형성 차이는 통계적으로 유의하지 않음(MD −5.86%, p=0.15); 혈소판 농축물 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)/PRP 아군은 ARP 유리(+16.28%, p<0.0001), 이종골+교원 차폐막 아군은 ARP 불리(−22.47%, p=0.004), >3개월 추적 민감도 분석에서는 ARP 유의하게 유리(MD −16.30%, p<0.00001).

재료 선택이 조직학적 이득을 결정하는 핵심 변수: PRF/PRP는 신생골 형성을 증가시키나, 서서히 흡수되는 이종골은 효과가 없고 잔존이식재(pooled 20.49%)를 남김; 이종골+차폐막 프로토콜은 이 trade-off를 고려해야 하며, 발치 후 >3개월 대기는 골 질 개선에 유리할 수 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA pooling exclusively RCTs on histomorphometric outcomes of ARP (new bone formation %, connective tissue %, residual graft) — 22 RCTs, 816 patients
- Demonstrates the material-type paradox: xenograft+collagen membrane significantly reduces new bone formation vs spontaneous healing (−22.47%), while PRF/PRP increases it (+16.28%)
- Pooled residual graft content of 20.49% (95% CI 16.78–24.20%) highlights the "dimensional stability vs bone turnover" trade-off
- Sensitivity analysis restricted to >3-month follow-up substantially changes the conclusion: ARP then favors bone formation (MD −16.30%, p<0.00001, I²=51%)
- GRADE: moderate certainty — downgraded for risk of bias and inconsistency; no publication bias (Egger's p=0.576)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: PRISMA 2020 SR+MA; random-effects model
- Eligibility: RCTs only; ≥8 week post-extraction biopsy; ARP vs spontaneous healing; histomorphometry as outcome
- Databases: MEDLINE, SCOPUS, Cochrane CENTRAL, grey literature
- Included: 22 RCTs, 816 patients (505 ARP / 311 spontaneous healing)
- Primary outcome: new bone formation (%)
- Secondary: connective tissue (%), residual graft material (%)
- Subgroups: graft material (xenograft, allograft, alloplast, PRF/PRP, combination), membrane use
- Heterogeneity: I²=98% for primary analysis → random-effects; sensitivity: I²=51% after outlier exclusion (>3 mo follow-up)
- Risk of bias: RoB 2 tool; substantial concern noted
- Outlier analysis: DFFITS + Cook's Distance; Lim 2019 Test 2 and Zampara 2022 (allograft arm) identified

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Primary — New Bone Formation**
- Overall: MD −5.86% (95% CI −13.84 to 2.11%, p=0.15, I²=98%) — NOT significant; favors spontaneous healing numerically
- PRF/PRP subgroup: MD +16.28% (95% CI 7.53–25.03%, p<0.0001, I²=97%) — significantly favors ARP
- Xenograft + collagen membrane: MD −22.47% (95% CI −33.59 to −11.34%, p=0.004, I²=57%) — significantly disfavors ARP
- Long-term (>3 mo, sensitivity): MD −16.30% (95% CI −21.61 to −10.98%, p<0.00001, I²=51%) — significantly favors ARP
- Prediction interval for primary outcome: wide (likely −13.84 to +2.11%)

**Secondary — Connective Tissue**
- MD +1.30% (95% CI −3.55 to 6.14%, p=0.61, I²=90%) — NOT significant
- Trend: xenograft+membrane showed lower CT vs spontaneous healing; alloplasts showed higher CT

**Secondary — Residual Graft Material**
- Pooled mean: 20.49% (95% CI 16.78–24.20%, I²=88.99%)
- Meta-regression: alloplast+membrane associated with lowest residual (β=−16.34%, p=0.066); xenograft without membrane highest persistence (β=+9.31%, p=0.177)
- Omnibus meta-regression Q(4)=11.04, p=0.026 (material type + membrane use as moderators)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Extreme heterogeneity (I²=98%) limits overall conclusion reliability
- Biopsy timing, socket morphology, flap design, patient variability not standardized across studies
- Pseudo-R² for meta-regression only 5.4% — most variance unexplained
- Publication bias absence (Egger's p=0.576) but visual funnel asymmetry not excluded
- Future: standardized histological protocols, longer follow-up (>6 mo), prospective registries stratified by material

## 6. Related Work
- Adams et al. 2022: no grafting material shows superior histological bone formation vs extraction alone in high-quality trials — corroborated by overall non-significant finding
- Canullo et al. 2022: xenograft+collagen membrane improves new bone formation — present MA reports the opposite (−22.47%), contrasting finding
- Mardas et al. 2023; Machtei et al. 2019: slow-resorbing DBBM persists, reduces vital bone fraction — confirmed by 20.49% residual content
