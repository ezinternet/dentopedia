## Why Ingested

측방 상악동거상술 이식재 비교의 **역사적 기준선**이 비어 있었다 — 현재 위키에는 [[sinus-lift/lateral/trimmel-2021-relative-performance-various-biomaterials]] (베이지안 NMA, 34 RCT)와 [[sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]] (49 RCT, 생존율)만 있어 "자가골이 초기에 우세하고 9개월 이후에는 차이가 사라진다"는 2009년 조직형태계측 pooled 결론의 원출처가 없었다. 본 메타분석(30편)을 넣어 [[overviews/sinus-lift-graft-material-prf-overview]]의 이식재 축에 시간 경과별 대비 기준점을 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Histomorphometric meta-analysis of 30 human studies (53 material observations from 1995–2006 literature, weighted ANOVA with random-effects model and Tukey-Kramer post hoc adjustment) comparing Total Bone Volume (TBV) after external sinus floor elevation with autogenous bone, DFDBA, hydroxyapatite, β-TCP, anorganic deproteinized bovine bone (Bio-Oss®) and their combinations.

Autogenous bone had the highest TBV in the 4–9 month cluster (statistically significant), whereas from 9 months onward no statistically significant difference remained among materials, with Bio-Oss® + autogenous bone showing the highest (non-significant) tendency.

Autogenous bone is favoured when functional loading is expected within 9 months, otherwise Bio-Oss® combined with autogenous bone is preferable — but the pooled per-material TBV values are reported only graphically (Figs 2–3), not as numbers, and donor-site morbidity is explicitly excluded from this conclusion.

## 세줄요약

2009년 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry) 메타분석: 1995–2006년 문헌에서 선별한 인체 연구 30편(이식재 관찰 53건)을 대상으로 측방 상악동거상술 (Lateral Sinus Floor Elevation) 후 총골부피 (Total Bone Volume, TBV)를 자가골 (Autogenous Bone)·탈회동결건조골 (Demineralized Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft, DFDBA)·수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HA)·베타-삼인산칼슘 (β-Tricalcium Phosphate, β-TCP)·탈단백우골무기질 (Anorganic Deproteinized Bovine Bone, DBBM; Bio-Oss®) 및 조합별로 비교.

이식 후 4–9개월 구간에서는 자가골의 TBV가 가장 높았고(통계적 유의), 9개월 이후에는 이식재 간 유의차가 사라졌으며 DBBM+자가골 조합이 가장 높은 경향(비유의)을 보였다.

9개월 내 기능적 부하가 예상되면 자가골이 유리하고 그 외에는 DBBM+자가골 조합이 선호되나, 이식재별 pooled TBV 수치는 그림(Fig. 2–3)으로만 제시되어 수치가 없고 공여부 이환 (Donor-Site Morbidity)은 결론에서 제외되었다는 점이 핵심 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides the field's first **quantitative, time-stratified** synthesis of graft-material histology in external sinus elevation: TBV is compared not as a single pooled number but in two sampling clusters (4–9 months and ≥9 months).
- Establishes the classic "convergence" claim: autogenous bone leads in the early phase, and by 9 months all materials become statistically indistinguishable — a conclusion that later network meta-analyses had to either reproduce or overturn.
- Makes the **material-availability ceiling** explicit: only autogenous bone, Bio-Oss®, Bio-Oss® + autogenous bone and β-TCP had enough evaluable observations for pooling (DFDBA, HA, and several combinations were dropped for k ≤ 2).
- Separates osteogenesis/osteoinduction/osteoconduction conceptually and uses TBV's arithmetic dependence on residual particle volume to explain why xenograft and β-TCP groups start low and rise (particle resorption plus bone apposition).
- States its own publication-bias caveat (case reports/series preferentially report good results), a self-critique rare in this literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Search**: NLM; keywords "sinus" + "augmentation" + "bone substitute"; humans only; no animal, no in vitro, no reviews/interviews/editorials.
- **Inclusion**: external (lateral) sinus floor elevation only; materials used in several studies — autogenous bone, DFDBA, hydroxyapatite, β-TCP, anorganic deproteinized bovine bone (Bio-Oss®, Geistlich), and combinations.
- **Pooling rules**: mixtures with >80% Bio-Oss® were pooled into the Bio-Oss® group; mixtures with <80% Bio-Oss® (e.g. 50:50) were assigned to Bio-Oss® + autogenous bone. β-TCP was used without autogenous bone in almost all studies.
- **Outcome — TBV (Total Bone Volume)**: percentage of the section consisting of bone tissue; taken directly from the paper or calculated where possible. Woven + lamellar bone were summed; lateral and central biopsies were averaged.
- **Statistical model**: weighting by number of observations and inverse variance; weighted ANOVA, random-effects model, alpha-adjustment by Tukey-Kramer for post hoc tests; p < 0.05.
- **Screening flow (Table 1)**: 120 articles → 116 (English/German) → 112 (human only) → 106 (no interviews/editorials) → 81 (external SFE only) → 65 (eligible material set) → **30 with evaluable TBV**.
- **Design mix (Table 2)**: 30 studies (26 prospective, 1 retrospective, 3 case reports) yielding 53 material observations (46 prospective, 3 retrospective, 4 case reports).
- **Second exclusion step (Table 3)**: criteria I = no mean/SD reported (17 observations), criteria II = single case report (5), criteria III = material with only 1–2 reports → **41 remaining observations, 36 usable** after overlaps.

**Numeric pooled means are not reported**: the paper presents the weighted means and SDs of the two clusters only as bar charts (Figs 2–3); the text gives no numeric value for any material. Ranges above are single-study rows, not pooled estimates.

**Safety**: no allergic reaction to grafting material and no graft-related infection was described in any included study.

**Authors' explanation of the trend**: with no functional load applied before biopsy, autogenous bone resorbs (literature cited: up to 55% of augmented autogenous bone resorbs within the first 6 months), while Bio-Oss® and β-TCP are at least partially resorbed and replaced by bone, so TBV rises. β-TCP is reported elsewhere to be fully resorbed within 12–18 months.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **No numeric pooled estimates** — the material-level weighted means exist only in figures; this page therefore cannot carry a pooled TBV per material and readers should not cite one.
- **No formal risk-of-bias assessment, no PRISMA, no protocol registration, no heterogeneity statistic (I²) or funnel plot** — the analysis predates modern SR methodology; "meta-analysis" here means weighted pooling of heterogeneous case series.
- **Design base is weak**: 26 of 30 studies are prospective case series, 3 are case reports, 1 retrospective; no RCT was available, so the authors concede the evidence base is case reports/series/retrospective studies.
- **Publication bias acknowledged but unquantified**: the authors state that even these "highest grade of evidence" numbers are probably slightly optimistic.
- **Duplicate data suspected in Table 4**: the Zerbo 2004 and Zijderveld 2005 rows are numerically identical (autogenous 41.00 ± 10.000, β-TCP 17.00 ± 5.000) — likely the same patient material published twice, which would double-count those observations in the pooled analysis; this is not addressed by the authors.
- **Material pooling hides composition**: the 50:50–80:20 Bio-Oss®/autogenous mixtures are merged, and "HA" and "β-TCP" cover different products (Algipore®, Cerasorb®) with different particle geometries.
- **TBV is a purely mineralized-area metric**: it counts residual graft particles within its denominator convention inconsistently across studies, so cross-material comparison partly reflects particle resorption rather than osteogenesis; no histomorphometric differentiation of new bone vs residual particle is pooled.
- **Language restriction** (English/German) and search ending 2006; donor-site morbidity is explicitly excluded from the clinical conclusion.

## 6. Related Work

- Trimmel et al. 2021 (Bayesian NMA, 34 RCTs, crestal core biopsies at 5–8 months) — the modern successor to this question; it ranks autologous bone alone only twelfth by SUCRA, in tension with Handschel's early-phase autogenous superiority.
- Del Fabbro et al. 2025 (SR of 49 RCTs, implant survival) — shifts the outcome from histology to survival and finds all graft classes equivalent, which echoes Handschel's "no difference after 9 months" at the clinical level.
- Alkandari et al. 2025 (SR+MA, BCP vs DBBM) — a direct two-material histomorphometric comparison that Handschel's pooled design could not perform (only 1–3 observations per material for several classes).
- Boyne [1] and Tatum [2] — the original external sinus elevation technique papers cited as the procedure's origin (citation years not stated in the retrieved text).
- Kent & Block [3] — source of the ideal-graft criteria (osteoinduction, osteoconduction, volume stability) used as the analytical frame.
- Tadjoedin et al. [31] — the histological mechanism (osteogenic cell guidance from native bone surfaces through Bio-Oss® particles) that Handschel uses to explain rising TBV.

## 7. Glossary

- **TBV (Total Bone Volume)**: percentage of a histological section consisting of bone tissue; the single pooled outcome of this meta-analysis.
- **DFDBA (Demineralized Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft)**: demineralized allogeneic bone powder, claimed osteoinductive via exposed BMPs; only 1 observation here.
- **HA (Hydroxyapatite)**: synthetic calcium phosphate ceramic; slow/non-resorbing alloplast.
- **β-TCP (β-Tricalcium Phosphate)**: resorbable synthetic calcium phosphate (Cerasorb® in most included studies); reportedly fully resorbed in 12–18 months.
- **Anorganic deproteinized bovine bone (Bio-Oss®)**: deproteinized bovine xenograft, osteoconductive, resorption slow and controversial (no resorption after 6 years vs slow resorption).
- **Random-effects weighted ANOVA**: here, weighting by observation count and inverse variance with Tukey-Kramer adjustment for the post hoc material comparisons.
- **Publication bias**: selective reporting of favourable outcomes, named by the authors as a reason their pooled numbers are probably optimistic.
