## Why Ingested

기존 CA vs FA(고정식장치) 비교 페이지들([[orthodontics/clear-aligner/alhuwaizi-2026-clear-aligner-fixed-oral-hygiene-periodontal-grade-sr]], [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/crego-ruiz-2023-periodontal-gingival-recession-aligner-vs-fixed-sr-ma]])은 치주지표·구강위생 결과 중심이었고, **골개조(bone turnover)의 생물학적 기전 지표(염증성 사이토카인)** 수준에서 두 장치를 비교한 근거는 위키에 없었다. 본 Journal of Orthodontic Science 논문(Hassan 2024)은 타액 TNF-α를 4개 시점(T0/24h/7d/21d)에서 측정해 FA가 CA보다 유의하게 높은 급성 염증반응을 유발함을 보여, 기존 임상지표(치주·통증) 비교에 세포/생화학적 기전 근거를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Longitudinal clinical study (n=40, 20 fixed-appliance [FA] + 20 clear-aligner [CA] patients, mild-moderate crowding per Little's irregularity index) measuring unstimulated salivary TNF-α (ELISA) at baseline (T0) and 24 h (T1), 7 days (T2), and 21 days (T3) after appliance placement.

TNF-α rose and peaked at 24 h in both groups (FA 51.51 vs CA 43.29 pg/mL) then declined through day 21; FA levels were significantly higher than CA at T1, T2, and T3 (all p<0.05), with no significant difference at baseline.

Fixed appliances provoke a greater salivary inflammatory (bone-turnover) response than clear aligners in the initial treatment phase, consistent with the intermittent (aligner) vs continuous (fixed) force-delivery difference — small single-center sample without correlation to clinical pain or root-resorption outcomes.

## 세줄요약

경도-중등도 총생(Little's irregularity index 1–6mm) 성인 40명(고정식 장치 20명 vs 투명교정 20명)을 대상으로 장치 장착 전(T0) 및 24시간(T1)·7일(T2)·21일(T3) 후 무자극 타액 TNF-α(ELISA)를 측정한 종적 임상연구.

양 군 모두 24시간에 TNF-α가 최고치(FA 51.51 vs CA 43.29 pg/mL)를 보인 뒤 21일까지 감소했고, 24시간·7일·21일 모든 시점에서 FA가 CA보다 유의하게 높음(p<0.05); 기저치(T0)는 군간 차이 없음.

초기 치료단계에서 고정식 장치가 투명교정보다 더 큰 타액 염증(골개조) 반응을 유발하며, 이는 투명교정의 간헐적 힘 전달 vs 고정식의 지속적 힘 전달 차이와 부합한다 — 단일기관 소규모 표본, 통증·치근흡수 등 임상결과와의 상관은 미평가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of few studies assessing CA vs FA at the **tissue/cellular (biomarker) level**, rather than only clinical/periodontal indices.
- Establishes a **time-course profile** of salivary TNF-α (peak at 24 h, decline by day 21) common to both appliance types.
- Quantifies a **significant CA-vs-FA gap** at every post-placement time point (24 h, 7 d, 21 d), supporting the mechanistic hypothesis that continuous (FA) vs intermittent (CA) force delivery differentially provokes inflammatory/bone-turnover response.
- Uses a **non-invasive salivary assay** (vs serum/GCF), supporting feasibility of biomarker monitoring in routine orthodontic practice.

## 3. Methodology

- **Design**: longitudinal, two parallel groups (not randomized) — Group A: 20 patients treated with FA; Group B: 20 patients treated with CA.
- **Sample**: n=40 (22 M, 18 F), mean age 22 ± 7 years; mild-moderate crowding (1–6 mm, Little's irregularity index).
- **Inclusion**: healthy periodontal tissue, probing depth ≤3 mm, no oral surgery history, no systemic disease, no anti-inflammatory/steroid/antibiotic use in prior 3 months.
- **Sampling**: unstimulated saliva via draining method (swallow → tilt head forward → expectorate over 10 min); collected at T0 (pre-placement), T1 (24 h), T2 (7 d), T3 (21 d).
- **Processing**: centrifuged 3000 rpm × 10 min; supernatant stored at −20 °C; TNF-α quantified via ELISA (Weldon Biotech, India).
- **Statistics**: Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-rank test (intragroup, across time points); Mann-Whitney U test (intergroup, FA vs CA at each time point); SPSS v20.0; α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results

| Time point | FA mean TNF-α (pg/mL ± SD) | CA mean TNF-α (pg/mL ± SD) | FA vs CA significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 (baseline) | 43.32 ± 7.35 | 41.93 ± 6.51 | ns (p=0.6502) |
| T1 (24 h) | 51.51 ± 7.38 | 43.29 ± 6.07 | p=0.0102* |
| T2 (7 d) | 49.79 ± 7.64 | 42.97 ± 5.96 | p=0.0500* |
| T3 (21 d) | 48.68 ± 7.66 | 41.97 ± 6.03 | p=0.0490* |

- Both groups: TNF-α rose sharply by T1 (24 h), then declined progressively through T2 and T3, but remained above baseline in the FA group at T3 (CA returned close to baseline: T0-T3 ns for CA, but T0-T3 remained different in trend for FA).
- Within-group (Wilcoxon): FA showed significant change across nearly all time-point pairs; CA showed significant change for most pairs except T0-T3 (not significant — i.e., CA salivary TNF-α returned to near-baseline by day 21, unlike FA).

## 5. Limitations

- **Non-randomized** two-group comparison — appliance choice not randomized, introducing potential selection bias.
- **Single biomarker, single-timepoint-per-visit** design — no correlation with clinical outcomes (pain, mobility, root resorption, actual tooth movement rate).
- Small sample (n=20/group) from what appears to be a **single-center** Indian cohort — limited generalizability.
- No control for exact force magnitude/wire sequence in the FA group or aligner material/stage in the CA group — force levels not standardized/measured, only appliance type.
- 21-day observation window only — does not address gradual/steady-state phases of full treatment.

## 7. Glossary

- **Tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α)** — proinflammatory cytokine produced by macrophages/monocytes/osteoblasts; early modulator of osteoclastic bone resorption during orthodontic tooth movement.
- **Little's irregularity index** — a linear measurement of anterior tooth displacement used to grade mandibular/maxillary crowding severity.
- **Hyalinized zone** — cell-free necrotic area on the periodontal-ligament pressure side during orthodontic force application, cleared by osteoclasts from adjacent tissue.
- **ELISA (enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay)** — antibody-based assay used here to quantify salivary TNF-α concentration.
- **GM-CSF (granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor)** — cytokine that synergizes with TNF-α to stimulate osteoclast differentiation and bone resorption.
