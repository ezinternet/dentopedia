Foundational mechanistic study establishing the Ang II → RANKL → osteoclast pathway directly relevant to RAAS inhibitors' bone-protective effects seen in Saravi 2021 (ISQ) and Solomon 2011 (fracture HR).

## One-line Summary
Animal + in vitro study: Angiotensin II activates osteoclasts via RANKL induction in osteoblasts; olmesartan (ARB) completely blocks this pathway; AT1a receptor-deficient mice show no ovariectomy-induced bone loss.

## 한줄요약
동물+시험관 연구: Ang II가 조골세포에서 RANKL 발현 유도 → 파골세포 활성화 → 골밀도↓; olmesartan(ARB)으로 완전 차단; AT1a 수용체 결핍 마우스는 난소절제 후 골 소실 없음 — RAAS-골 보호 메커니즘의 기초 근거.

## Three-line Summary

Animal and in vitro study (OVX rats with Ang II infusion ±olmesartan; AT1a receptor knockout mice post-OVX+nephrectomy; in vitro osteoblast culture with Ang II ±olmesartan/MEK inhibitors); outcomes: TRAP activity, bone density, urinary deoxypyridinoline, RANKL mRNA expression.

Angiotensin II activated osteoclast formation by inducing RANKL in osteoblasts via MEK signalling; olmesartan (ARB) and MEK inhibitors completely blocked this pathway; AT1a−/− mice showed no bone volume decrease after OVX+nephrectomy, whereas wild-type mice had significant bone loss.

Foundational mechanistic evidence establishing the Ang II → AT1 receptor → RANKL → osteoclast cascade as the primary driver of hypertension-related bone loss; directly explains why ARB use reduces fracture risk (Solomon 2011) and improves implant stability (Saravi 2021) in antihypertensive patients.

## 세줄요약

동물·시험관 연구 (난소절제 쥐에 안지오텐신 II (Angiotensin II, Ang II) 주입 ±olmesartan 안지오텐신수용체차단제 (ARB); AT1a 수용체 결핍 마우스 난소절제+신절제 모델; 조골세포 배양 Ang II ±olmesartan/mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase (MEK) 억제제); 주석산-내성 산성 포스파타제 (Tartrate-Resistant Acid Phosphatase, TRAP)·골밀도·데옥시피리디놀린·수용체 활성화 NF-κB 리간드 (RANKL) mRNA 측정.

Ang II가 조골세포에서 MEK 신호를 통해 RANKL 발현 유도 → 파골세포 활성화; olmesartan 및 MEK 억제제로 완전 차단; AT1a 결핍 마우스는 난소절제 후 골소실 없음 (야생형 대비).

Ang II → AT1 수용체 → RANKL → 파골세포 경로가 고혈압성 골소실의 기전임을 입증한 기초 연구 — Solomon 2011의 ARB 골절 위험 감소(HR 0.76)와 Saravi 2021의 RAAS 억제제 임플란트 안정성 향상(ISQ +3.59)의 기전적 토대.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First mechanistic demonstration that Ang II acts directly on osteoblasts to induce RANKL, driving osteoclast activation
- ARB (olmesartan) and MEK inhibitors completely block Ang II's bone-resorptive effect
- AT1a receptor knockout confirms that Ang II signalling through AT1 is the required pathway
- Mechanistic foundation explaining why ARB use correlates with reduced fracture risk (Solomon 2011) and better ISQ (Saravi 2021)
- Extends RAAS-bone connection beyond blood pressure regulation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- In vitro: bone marrow-derived mononuclear cells exposed to Ang II (1×10⁻⁶ M); TRAP+ osteoclast formation quantified
- Osteoblast culture: Ang II exposure + olmesartan or MEK inhibitor; RANKL mRNA measured
- In vivo rat model: ovariectomy (OVX) + Ang II infusion (200 ng/kg/min); treated with olmesartan; outcomes: TRAP activity, bone density, urinary deoxypyridinoline
- Hypertensive rat model: olmesartan vs control; OVX effect on bone density + TRAP
- Knockout study: AT1a receptor-deficient mice + wild-type; OVX + 5/6 nephrectomy; bone volume by microCT

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- In vitro: Ang II significantly increased TRAP+ multinuclear osteoclasts; blocked by olmesartan and MEK inhibitors
- Ang II → RANKL expression in osteoblasts; completely blocked by olmesartan and MAP kinase inhibition
- OVX + Ang II infusion: ↑ TRAP activity, ↓ bone density, ↑ urinary deoxypyridinoline vs OVX alone
- Olmesartan + OVX hypertensive rats: attenuated bone density decrease and TRAP activity
- AT1a−/− mice: no bone volume decrease after OVX + nephrectomy (vs significant decrease in wild-type)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Animal model — OVX rat/mouse; not directly applicable to male or non-estrogen-deficient hypertensive patients
- No implant or dental bone outcomes measured
- Mechanism localised to AT1 receptor; AT2 receptor role not examined
- Does not address whether clinical-dose ARB achieves same bone protection in normoestrogen subjects

## 7. Glossary
- TRAP: Tartrate-Resistant Acid Phosphatase — osteoclast activity marker
- AT1/AT1a: Angiotensin II type 1 receptor subtype; mediates bone-resorptive signalling
- MEK: Mitogen-activated protein kinase kinase; downstream effector of AT1 signalling
- Deoxypyridinoline: urinary crosslink marker of bone collagen degradation (bone resorption index)
