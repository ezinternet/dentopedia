## Why Ingested

한국 건강보험공단 (National Health Insurance Service, NHIS) 전국 청구 자료로 65세 이상 환자의 임플란트 235만 개를 최대 6년 추적한 코호트로, 임플란트 표면 (SA, RBM, HA)과 지대주 유형에 따른 후기 임플란트 소실 (제거 청구) 위험을 비교한다. 한국 대학병원 단일기관 생존율 문헌과 달리 전국 실사용 데이터를 제공한다. → [[implants/survival/yum-2023-the-cumulative-survival-rate-of]]

## Three-line Summary

Nationwide Korean NHIS cohort (implants placed July 2014 to December 2019 in patients aged 65 or older, follow-up to December 2020; mean 2.84±1.25 years): 2,354,706 implants in 1,386,570 patients, failure defined by claim-based implant removal procedures and peri-implantitis treatments after prosthesis delivery.

Late implant loss (implant removal) was 12,412 of 2,354,706 implants (0.53%); relative to sandblasted, large-grit, acid-etched (SA) surfaces, adjusted HR for removal was 1.841 for resorbable blast media (RBM) and 1.424 for hydroxyapatite (HA) (p<0.0001), while abutment type was not associated with removal (p=0.653) and peri-implantitis treatment did not differ significantly by surface (p=0.0587).

All surface and abutment groups had a low incidence (highest 3.884 per 1000 implant-years, RBM with one-piece abutment); the study is observational and claim-based, lacks bone, smoking, periodontitis and brand data, and follow-up was at most about 6 years.

## 세줄요약

한국 NHIS 전국 코호트 (2014년 7월~2019년 12월 임플란트 식립, 65세 이상, 2020년 12월까지 추적, 평균 2.84±1.25년): 환자 1,386,570명의 임플란트 2,354,706개; 보철 장착 후 임플란트 제거 청구와 임플란트주위염 (peri-implantitis) 치료 청구를 실패로 정의했다.

후기 임플란트 소실 (제거)은 12,412개 (0.53%)였고, SA 표면 대비 보정 위험비 (hazard ratio, HR)는 흡수성 블라스팅 (resorbable blast media, RBM) 1.841, 수산화인회석 코팅 (hydroxyapatite, HA) 1.424 (p<0.0001)였으며, 지대주 유형은 제거 위험과 무관 (p=0.653), 임플란트주위염 치료는 표면 간 유의차가 없었다 (p=0.0587).

모든 표면·지대주 군의 발생률은 낮았고 (최고 RBM·일체형 지대주 1000 임플란트-년당 3.884), 관찰 연구이며 청구 기반이고 골 상태·흡연·치주염·브랜드 정보가 없고 추적이 최대 약 6년이라는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest implant cohort in the wiki so far (2,354,706 implants), reflecting real-world care by specialists and general dentists, not academic centers.
- Compares three NHIS-coded surfaces (SA, RBM, HA) and three stock abutment structures (one-piece straight, two-piece straight, two-piece angled).
- Separates late implant loss into simple removal (mobile implant, taken as osseointegration loss) and complex removal (fracture, nerve damage, peri-implantitis without mobility).
- Finds SA surfaces with the lowest hazard of removal, no abutment effect on overall removal, and no significant surface difference for peri-implantitis treatment.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Observational nationwide population-based cohort (STROBE); IRB exempt (approval number ERI21016) because data were de-identified.
- **Data/years**: NHIS plus linked National Health Screening database; implants placed July 2014 to December 2019 (NHIS implant coverage began 1 July 2014, ages 75 and above at launch, 70 in year two, 65 from year three); follow-up from restoration delivery to December 2020.
- **Population**: Age 65 or older. Initial 3,038,622 implants (1,757,851 patients); after exclusions (683,916 implants, 371,281 patients) final 2,354,706 implants of 1,386,570 patients. Coverage limits: partially edentulous arches only, two implants per lifetime, cement-retained non-precious metal-ceramic crowns.
- **Exclusions**: early failure before restoration delivery; custom CAD/CAM abutments; restoration not completed by December 2019; oral cancer diagnosis before restoration completion.
- **Exposure**: implant surface from NHIS product codes (RBM, SA, HA); abutment structure (one-piece, two-piece, angled).
- **Outcome definition (claim-based)**: implant complication treatments = implant removal procedures (simple: mobile, no special instrument, taken as osseointegration loss; complex: needing trephine or removal kit, for fracture, nerve damage, peri-implantitis without mobility) and/or peri-implantitis treatments (peri-implant osteoplasty or implant surface decontamination). Simple plus complex removal = "late implant loss". Early failure (before restoration) was excluded, not analysed.
- **Covariates**: sex, age, income, residence, disability, diabetes, hypertension, dyslipidemia, cancer, stroke, osteoporosis, tooth type.
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier with log-rank; Cox models 1 (unadjusted) to 4 (fully adjusted); incidence per 1000 implant-years; SAS 9.4; p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Patient level: mean age 72.11±5.23 years; 679,296 of 1,386,570 men (48.99%).
- Implant surface: SA 86.7%, RBM 9.52%, HA 3.78%; two-piece abutments 79.9% (one-piece 13.46%, angled 6.63%).
- Mean follow-up 2.84±1.25 years. Implant complication treatments: 14,440 (0.61%). Implant removal: 12,412 (0.53%), simple 8,770 (0.37%), complex 3,642 (0.15%). Peri-implantitis treatment: 2,137 (0.09%).
- Crude complications more frequent in men, age 75 or older, urban residence (p<0.0001), and in patients with disability, diabetes, hypertension or cancer (p<0.01); maxillary implants over-represented among complications (p<0.0001).
- 6-year cumulative incidence of complication treatments did not exceed 0.03 in any surface or abutment group.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Authors: bone height and quality, grafting, implant length, diameter, design and splinting not available in NHIS data; manufacturer surface-preparation differences not accounted for (no brand data); smoking and periodontitis history not considered.
- Failure is inferred from billing codes (removal and treatment claims), not clinical or radiographic examination; reviewed by the Health Insurance Review and Assessment Service. Peri-implantitis treatment captures only treated cases.
- Only patients aged 65 or older with insurance-covered implants (max two per lifetime, cement-retained non-precious metal-ceramic crowns); not generalisable to younger patients, full-arch or other restorations.
- Mean follow-up 2.84 years, maximum about 6 years; authors call for longer follow-up.
- Observational; confounding by indication and by which clinicians chose which surface is possible (not discussed in depth by the authors).
- In-paper inconsistencies: the abutment-type p-value for overall complication treatments is 0.9811 (log-rank, text) and the PubMed abstract gives 0.9542; the text gives 0.653 for abutment type and implant removal; the HA peri-implantitis HR CI (1.009-1.584) excludes 1 while the surface p is 0.0587. Per-group crude event numbers are in figures not captured in the text.

## 7. Glossary

- **NHIS (National Health Insurance Service, 국민건강보험공단)**: Korean mandatory national insurer; source of claim data.
- **SA (sandblasted, large-grit, acid-etched, 대입자 블라스팅 산부식)**: roughened titanium surface.
- **RBM (Resorbable Blast Media, 흡수성 블라스팅)**: calcium phosphate particle blasted surface.
- **HA (Hydroxyapatite coating, 수산화인회석 코팅)**: bone-like ceramic coating.
- **Simple implant removal (단순 제거)**: mobile implant removed without special instruments; taken as osseointegration loss.
- **Complex implant removal (복합 제거)**: removal needing trephine burr or kit, for fracture, nerve damage or peri-implantitis without mobility.
- **HR (Hazard Ratio, 위험비)**: relative event rate in a Cox model.
