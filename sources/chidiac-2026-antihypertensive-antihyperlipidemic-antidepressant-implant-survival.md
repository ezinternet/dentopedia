## Summary

Retrospective cohort (STROBE; n=655 patients/2,004 implants from 2,815/5,853 screened; Saint Joseph University Beirut, 2012–2022; 10-yr Kaplan-Meier) comparing mono-medicated antihypertensive (74 pts/233 implants), antihyperlipidemic (40/99), and mixed antidepressant (29/91) groups against a healthy control (512/1,581).

Antihypertensive group: failure 1.29% vs control 5.12%; HR 0.23 (95% CI 0.07–0.74, p=0.013) — strongly protective. Antihyperlipidemic: failure 1.01%; HR 0.16 (95% CI 0.02–1.15, p=0.048) — protective. Mixed antidepressants: failure 12.09%; HR 2.17 (95% CI 1.17–4.04, p=0.014) — significantly harmful. All log-rank tests p<0.05. Omnibus test p<0.05 overall.

Mechanistic basis: AHTs reduce osteoclast activity via sympathetic blockade (beta-blockers), RANKL suppression (ACEIs/ARBs), calcium homeostasis (thiazides); statins enhance osteoblast differentiation; SSRIs increase osteoclast differentiation while reducing osteoblast proliferation.

**Key caveat**: retrospective design, self-reported medications, group imbalance (29 antidepressant patients), mono-medication exclusion may over-select healthier patients. Cureus is a peer-reviewed but open-access journal with variable citation impact.
