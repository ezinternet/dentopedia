## Why Ingested

조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure, EIF) 위험인자 중 항응고제 (anticoagulants) 사용을 직접 검증한 대규모(2971개 임플란트) 후향적 코호트로, 약물 관련 EIF 위험 데이터를 early-failure 문헌에 보강하고 임플란트 수준과 환자 수준 분석 결과가 엇갈리는 점을 보여준다. → [[implants/survival/yari-2023-risk-factors-early-implant-failure]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study at the oral and maxillofacial surgery department of Rabin Medical Center (Beilinson Hospital, Israel): 687 patients and 2971 implants, of whom 173 patients (25.2%) and 708 implants (23.8%) were on anticoagulants (AC); early implant failure (EIF) was defined as implant removal within up to 12 months from loading, and 3.8% of implants (15.1% of patients) had EIF.

Crude EIF at implant level was 5.5% with AC versus 3.3% without; in the implant-level logistic model AC use had OR 2.64 (p=0.01), whereas ASA 2/3 vs ASA 1 (OR 0.30 and 0.33) and age 80 or older (OR 0.34, p=0.05) had lower odds. At patient level AC use was not significant (p=0.87) after adjustment; atrial/ventricular fibrillation was associated with higher odds (OR 2.95, p=0.01), and ASA 3 (OR 0.53) and ischemic heart disease (OR 0.40) with lower odds.

The AC association is therefore model-level dependent and confounded by cardiovascular indication (older, ASA 3, hypertension, diabetes), the patient-level model fitted poorly (Hosmer-Lemeshow p=0.22 as reported), and drug type, dose and duration were not analysed, so the result is hypothesis-generating rather than proof that anticoagulants cause implant failure.

## 세줄요약

이스라엘 Rabin Medical Center (Beilinson Hospital) 구강악안면외과의 후향적 코호트: 환자 687명, 임플란트 2971개 중 항응고제 (anticoagulants, AC) 사용 환자 173명(25.2%)·임플란트 708개(23.8%); 조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure, EIF)는 부하 후 12개월 이내 제거로 정의했고 임플란트 3.8%(환자 15.1%)에서 발생했다.

임플란트 수준 조잡 EIF율은 AC군 5.5% 대 비사용군 3.3%였고, 로지스틱 모델에서 AC 사용은 OR 2.64 (p=0.01)로 EIF 위험이 높았으며 ASA 2/3 (OR 0.30, 0.33)와 80세 이상 (OR 0.34, p=0.05)은 낮았다; 환자 수준에서는 보정 후 AC 사용이 유의하지 않았고 (p=0.87) 심방/심실세동 (atrial/ventricular fibrillation, AF/VF)이 OR 2.95 (p=0.01)로 높았다.

AC 연관성은 분석 수준에 따라 달라지고 심혈관 적응증 (고령, ASA 3, 고혈압, 당뇨)에 의해 교란되며, 환자 수준 모델의 적합도가 낮고 약물 종류·용량·기간을 분석하지 않아, 항응고제가 임플란트 실패를 일으킨다는 증거가 아니라 가설 생성 수준의 결과로 해석해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Tests the effect of anticoagulants (AC) on early implant failure (EIF) in 687 patients / 2971 implants from a single hospital department.
- Reports two parallel logistic regressions (implant level and patient level) with discordant AC results.
- Identifies AF/VF as associated with higher EIF odds and ASA 2/3, IHD and age 80 or older as associated with lower odds, and discusses likely confounding by indication.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design / setting**: retrospective cohort, department of oral and maxillofacial surgery, Rabin Medical Center, Beilinson Hospital (Israel); implants placed by specialist oral and maxillofacial surgeons. The study period is not stated in the retrieved text.
- **Sample**: 687 patients, 2971 implants; AC users 173 patients (25.2%) and 708 implants (23.8%); the remainder served as control.
- **EIF definition**: removal of the implant from the oral cavity within a period of up to 12 months from the date of its loading (the Introduction instead defines EIF as less than 1 year from abutment connection).
- **Outcome / analysis**: EIF primary outcome; chi-square and Mann-Whitney for univariate comparisons; logistic regression at implant and patient level; SPSS 25; p<0.05.
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria and variable lists are headings only in the retrieved text; AC type (VKA vs DOAC), dose and duration are not reported in the retrieved text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective design; bone densitometry not measured (stated by authors).
- Confounding by indication: AC is prescribed for cardiac arrhythmia such as AF, with older age, more hypertension and possibly smoking (authors' own caveat). The AC group differed from controls on age, ASA, comorbidities and smoking.
- Implant-level analysis treats implants in the same patient as independent (clustering is not mentioned in the retrieved text); the patient-level model, which is closer to the exposure, did not confirm the AC association, and fit poorly (Hosmer-Lemeshow p=0.22 as reported).
- AC drug class (VKA vs DOAC), dose, duration and INR are not analysed in the retrieved text; the study period, inclusion/exclusion criteria and tables are not present in the retrieved text.
- Counter-intuitive protective associations (ASA 2/3, IHD, age 80 or older) are explained speculatively by the authors (closer management); not tested. Low explained variance (about 7-9.6%).
- Internal inconsistencies in the retrieved text: EIF is defined from loading in Methods but from abutment connection in the Introduction; the Introduction/Methods hypothesis is worded as AC increasing EIF; age groups "66 and 67.9" appear to be a typo for 66-79.9; age 80 or older OR p=0.05 is borderline; text states EIF 3.8% of implants vs 15.1% at patient level. Numbers in this page are taken from the text and not from the unavailable tables.
- The authors call for replication and prospective studies of AC and osseointegration.

## 7. Glossary

- **EIF (Early Implant Failure, 조기 임플란트 실패)**: implant removal within up to 12 months from loading in this paper.
- **AC (Anticoagulants, 항응고제)**: drugs used to prevent thromboembolism (VKAs such as warfarin, DOACs such as rivaroxaban).
- **VKA (Vitamin K Antagonist, 비타민 K 길항제)**: warfarin-type anticoagulant.
- **DOAC (Direct Oral Anticoagulant, 직접경구항응고제)**: newer oral anticoagulants (e.g. rivaroxaban, dabigatran).
- **AF/VF (Atrial/Ventricular Fibrillation, 심방/심실세동)**: arrhythmia commonly treated with AC.
- **IHD (Ischemic Heart Disease, 허혈성 심질환)**; **ASA (American Society of Anesthesiologists physical status, 미국마취과학회 신체등급)**.
- **OR (Odds Ratio, 오즈비)**: relative odds of EIF in the logistic model.
