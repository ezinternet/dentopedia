## Three-line Summary

Foundational pharmacology review (Dent Clin North Am 2010;54(4):587-99) of local anesthetic agents and formulations used in dentistry, covering clinical pharmacology including onset, duration, vasoconstrictors, pH, lipid solubility, maximum tolerated doses, and lipid metabolism; covers lidocaine, mepivacaine, articaine, bupivacaine, and prilocaine.

Provides the clinical pharmacology context for understanding toxicity risks: lipid solubility correlates with toxicity potency; pH affects onset; vasoconstrictors extend duration and reduce systemic absorption; maximum tolerated dose varies by agent and patient factors.

Abstract-only; a standard reference by Moore PA and Hersh EV, frequently cited in LAST and dental anesthesia literature as the pharmacologic foundation.
