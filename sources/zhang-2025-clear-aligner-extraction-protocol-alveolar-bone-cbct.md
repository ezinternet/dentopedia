## Three-line Summary


Retrospective CBCT cohort (n=281: 186 non-extraction, 59 two-premolar-extraction, 36 one-lower-incisor-extraction) — alveolar bone height/thickness loss is common across all clear aligner extraction protocols;

two-premolar-extraction carries higher risk of lingual bone dehiscence and torque loss, one-lower-incisor-extraction carries higher risk of open gingival embrasures, and mandibular anterior teeth show more severe bone loss than maxillary anterior teeth (all p<0.05).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First large-scale (n=281) retrospective comparison of alveolar bone remodeling and root resorption across **three distinct clear aligner extraction protocols** — non-extraction (NE), two-premolar-extraction (TPE) in both arches, and the relatively rare one-lower-incisor-extraction (OLIE) — using CBCT at pre-treatment (T1) and post-treatment (T2).
- Identifies extraction-protocol-specific patterns of bone height/thickness loss, root resorption, and interproximal contact-to-alveolar-crest (ICP-AC) distance change, providing clinical reference for case selection and periodontal risk assessment before choosing an extraction strategy in CAT.
- Demonstrates that TPE carries elevated risk of lingual bone dehiscence and torque loss, while OLIE carries elevated risk of open gingival embrasures (OGE) — distinct, protocol-specific complication profiles rather than a single generic "extraction risk."
- Shows mandibular anterior teeth are more vulnerable to post-treatment bone loss than maxillary anterior teeth across protocols, attributed to the thinner anatomical structure of the mandible.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, single center (Department of Orthodontics, Stomatological Hospital of the Air Force Military Medical University), records reviewed over 3 years.
- **Population**: 281 adult patients (age ≥18) treated with Invisalign (Align Technology, Tempe, AZ, USA). Groups: 186 NE, 59 TPE (both maxilla and mandible), 36 with TPE in maxilla + OLIE in mandible. Maxillary/mandibular arches analyzed separately by protocol (NE/TPE/OLIE per arch).
- **Inclusion criteria**: no periodontal disease before/during treatment; age ≥18; clear CBCT at both T1 (pre-treatment) and T2 (post-treatment).
- **Exclusion criteria**: previously restored or root-canal-treated maxillary/mandibular anterior teeth; prior orthodontic treatment history; interproximal enamel reduction (IPR) during treatment; history of trauma or surgery in the anterior region.
- **Imaging**: CBCT (KaVo 3D eXam; 120 kV, 5 mA, 0.3 mm voxel, 9.6 s scan), reconstructed in Dolphin Imaging v11.9 with long axis of each tooth/root perpendicular to axial slices.
- **Measurements** (via reference lines at CEJ, parallel lines at alveolar crest [AC]):
  - Alveolar bone height: labial (LAAC-CEJ), lingual/palatal (LIAC-CEJ), mesial (MAC-CEJ), distal (DAC-CEJ) distances from CEJ to AC.
  - Alveolar bone thickness: labial (LAABT) and lingual/palatal (LIABT) at apex, 3 mm, 6 mm, 9 mm from CEJ.
  - Root length (sagittal view) and interproximal contact point-to-alveolar-crest distance (ICP-AC, coronal view).
  - Δ = T2 − T1 for all indices.
  - Single examiner (YZ), measured twice over 2 months; inter-rater reliability ICC = 0.91.
- **Clinical protocol detail (TPE subgroup, n=59)**: all cases used programmed incisor torque compensation (mean +9°); 59.2% used power ridges with root-lingual torque >3°; general sequence was align/level → overbite adjustment → retraction once lateral incisor–canine space appeared; no torque-specific optimized attachments were used (not available in the Invisalign system used).
- **Statistics**: Shapiro–Wilk normality test; t-test/one-way ANOVA (normal) or Mann-Whitney/Kruskal-Wallis (non-normal); post-hoc Tukey HSD (parametric) or Dunn's test with Bonferroni correction (non-parametric); SPSS v24.0; GraphPad Prism v8.0.2; two-tailed α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Maxillary anterior teeth:**
- LIAC-CEJ (lingual bone height loss) at T2 significantly greater in TPE than NE for central incisors (2.83±1.48 mm vs 1.48±1.74 mm), lateral incisors (3.08±2.44 vs 1.76±2.13 mm), and canines (2.87±1.64 vs 1.99±1.22 mm) (p<0.01); TPE group LIAC-CEJ at T2 consistently >2 mm.
- Labial alveolar bone thickness (LAABT) change smaller in NE vs TPE (p<0.05); lingual bone thickness (LIABT) change larger (more loss) in NE vs TPE for some sites — net direction: labial bone height/thickness trended to increase with TPE, lingual trended to decrease.
- ICP-AC increased more in TPE than NE (central incisors Δ 0.85±1.37 mm, T2 value 5.82±1.14 mm; lateral incisors Δ 1.15±1.02 mm; p<0.05).

**Mandibular anterior teeth:**
- ΔLIAC-CEJ significantly different across NE/TPE/OLIE for central incisors (0.26±1.38, 1.07±1.57, −0.02±1.58 mm) and lateral incisors (−0.01±1.50, 0.93±1.64, 0.99±2.09 mm) (all p<0.05).
- TPE showed greater LAABT gain and smaller LIABT (more lingual thinning) than NE at several sites (p<0.05), mirroring the maxillary pattern.
- ICP-AC at T2 lowest in NE group (5.12±1.40 mm central incisors; 5.12±0.12 mm lateral incisors; 4.58±1.06 mm canines) vs TPE/OLIE (p<0.05).
- Highest DAC-CEJ at T2 observed in OLIE group (3.04±0.71 mm central incisors; 2.20±0.85 mm canines).
- **Root resorption of canines**: NE 0.08±1.02 mm (least) vs TPE −0.51±0.65 mm vs OLIE −0.64±1.18 mm (p<0.001) — extraction protocols associated with greater canine root resorption than NE.

**Cross-protocol clinical risk patterns:**
- **TPE**: significantly higher risk of lingual bone dehiscence (LIAC-CEJ >2 mm) and torque loss/lingual crown torque during anterior retraction, particularly in mandibular incisors (p<0.05). Even with power ridges, overcorrection, and multi-week wear protocols, torque loss remained a persistent problem.
- **OLIE**: significantly higher risk of open gingival embrasures (OGE) — ICP-AC at T2 exceeded thresholds associated with OGE (≥6 mm → 44% OGE occurrence; ≥7 mm → ≥73% occurrence per cited literature); OLIE group showed the highest ICP-AC at T2 of all groups (p<0.05).
- **Mandible vs maxilla**: mandibular anterior teeth showed more severe post-treatment bone loss than maxillary anterior teeth across nearly all measured indices (p<0.05) — bone height at every site in every mandibular group exceeded 2 mm at T2; TPE mandibular incisors were especially prone to torque loss and tissue loss after distalization; ICP-AC at T2 was greater in the mandible than maxilla.
- Alveolar bone loss (height and/or thickness) was common after CAT even in the NE group, though less pronounced than in extraction groups — attributed to excessive bodily advancement/proclination pursued to avoid extraction in borderline cases.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective single-center design; no longitudinal follow-up beyond T2 to assess long-term stability of bone remodeling.
- Retrospective data collection limited availability of detailed tooth-movement data (e.g., exact retraction amount, torque expression per stage), potentially confounding interpretation of post-treatment changes.
- CBCT data were not integrated into the digital ClinCheck treatment planning/aligner setup for these cases — authors note that CBCT-integrated torque expression tends to be higher than non-CBCT-integrated planning, suggesting a path to reduce torque loss in future protocols.
- Authors call for larger multicenter studies with more rigorous statistical methods (e.g., mixed-effects models, machine learning) to control baseline heterogeneity and strengthen causal inference.
- No optimized torque-control attachments were used (unavailable in the Invisalign system at the time), limiting generalizability to aligner systems/protocols with torque-specific attachments.

## 6. Related Work

- Prior literature (cited within) established that ICP-AC ≥6 mm correlates with 44% OGE occurrence and ≥7 mm with ≥73% occurrence — used here as the clinical threshold for interpreting the OLIE group's OGE risk.
- Distinguished from prior studies (including the authors' own preliminary research) that compared fixed appliances vs clear aligners *within* the same extraction type; this study instead compares *different extraction types* within clear aligner therapy only — a comparison not previously reported at this scale, especially for the rare OLIE protocol.

## 7. Glossary

- **CAT**: Clear Aligner Therapy
- **NE**: Non-Extraction (protocol)
- **TPE**: Two-Premolar-Extraction (protocol, both arches)
- **OLIE**: One-Lower-Incisor-Extraction (protocol; maxilla TPE + mandible single incisor extraction)
- **CBCT**: Cone-Beam Computed Tomography
- **AC**: Alveolar Crest
- **CEJ**: Cementoenamel Junction
- **LAAC-CEJ / LIAC-CEJ / MAC-CEJ / DAC-CEJ**: Labial/Lingual(palatal)/Mesial/Distal Alveolar Crest-to-CEJ distance (bone height indices)
- **LAABT / LIABT**: Labial/Lingual Alveolar Bone Thickness (at apex, 3, 6, 9 mm from CEJ)
- **ICP-AC**: Interproximal Contact Point-to-Alveolar-Crest distance
- **OGE**: Open Gingival Embrasure
- **OIIRR**: Orthodontically Induced Inflammatory Root Resorption
- **T1/T2**: Pre-treatment / Post-treatment CBCT timepoints
