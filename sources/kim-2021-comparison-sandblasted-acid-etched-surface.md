## Why Ingested

Head-to-head RCT of plain SLA versus a nano-HA-coated SLA ("new hydrophilic") implant under a 3-month early-loading protocol in the posterior maxilla — the clinical arm that the nano-HA-coated SLA surface literature mostly lacks → [[implants/surface/ko-2024-hydroxyapatite-nano-coated-sla-immediate-implants]] and [[implants/surface/kim-2016-hydrophilic-tapered-implant-6week-12week-loading]].

## Three-line Summary

Prospective randomized controlled trial (J Korean Assoc Oral Maxillofac Surg 2021;47:175-182, Seoul National University Bundang Hospital; 27 patients / 31 implants) comparing SLA implants (Osstem, Group A) with new hydrophilic nano-HA-coated SLA implants (Hiossen NH, Group B) in the posterior maxilla with ≥4 mm residual bone height, loaded at 3 months.

Primary ISQ was 73.86 ± 6.40 (A) vs 71.24 ± 5.32 (B) (P = 0.222) and secondary ISQ 79.07 ± 5.21 vs 78.29 ± 4.74 (P = 0.667); NH implants gained more ISQ during healing (+7.06 vs +5.21, P = 0.265), and mean MBL was 0.045 vs 0.032 mm (P = 0.457).

Both surfaces achieved 100% success and survival at ~1 year, so with ≥65 initial ISQ a 3-month early-loading protocol is viable in the posterior maxilla regardless of which of these two surfaces is used — the hydrophilic surface's advantage shows up as a steeper stability recovery, not as a better outcome.

## 세줄요약

전향적 무작위 대조 임상시험 (대한구강악안면외과학회지 2021;47:175-182, 분당서울대; 환자 27명/임플란트 31개): 상악 구치부 잔존골높이 ≥4 mm에서 샌드블라스트·산부식 (Sandblasted and Acid-etched, SLA) 임플란트 (오스템, A군) vs 나노 수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HA) 코팅 친수성 SLA 임플란트 (Hiossen NH, B군)를 3개월 조기부하로 비교.

초기 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 73.86 vs 71.24 (P=0.222), 2차 ISQ 79.07 vs 78.29 (P=0.667); NH군의 치유기 ISQ 상승폭이 더 컸고 (+7.06 vs +5.21, P=0.265), 평균 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 0.045 vs 0.032 mm (P=0.457).

양군 모두 약 1년 성공률·생존율 100% — 초기 ISQ ≥65를 확보하면 상악 구치부 3개월 조기부하는 표면 종류와 무관하게 가능하며, 친수성 표면의 이점은 결과 개선이 아니라 안정성 회복 속도로 나타남.

## Key Contributions
- Direct RCT comparison of plain SLA (Osstem SA) against a dual SLA + nano-HA hydrophilic surface (Hiossen NH) in the same indication — posterior maxilla, ≥4 mm residual bone height.
- Confirms both surfaces support a 3-month early-loading protocol with 100% success and survival at ~1 year.
- Documents the direction of the surface effect: identical endpoint outcomes but a steeper ISQ recovery in the hydrophilic group (+7.06 vs +5.21), interpreted as accelerated secondary stability in sites that started with lower primary stability.
- Establishes a practical early-loading gate: primary ISQ ≥65 at placement.

## Methodology
Prospective randomized controlled trial, single centre (Seoul National University Bundang Hospital), enrolment June 2018-June 2019. 39 patients screened; 27 patients with 31 implants analyzed (Group A: 13 patients / 14 SA implants; Group B: 14 patients / 17 NH implants).

Inclusion: complete jaw growth; one or two consecutive missing teeth in the unilateral posterior maxilla; >4 mm residual alveolar bone height; sufficient buccopalatal and mesiodistal bone; opposing teeth present; **ISQ ≥65 immediately after placement**. Primary outcome: ISQ (primary and secondary, measured by RFA). Secondary outcomes: MBL, success/survival.

Placement: Group A 13 one-stage / 1 two-stage; Group B 12 one-stage / 5 two-stage. Adjunctive surgery at placement in 13 (A) and 14 (B) implants — sinus lift with bone graft (SLwBG), without graft (SLwoBG), and GBR in various combinations.

- Intra-group ISQ increase from primary to secondary was significant in both groups (P < 0.001, paired t-test).
- MBL did not differ by one- vs two-stage surgery (P = 0.143 A, 0.115 B) nor by adjunctive surgery type in Group A (P = 0.565). In Group B, MBL differed only between combined SLwBG+GBR and no adjunctive surgery (P = 0.024).
- No peri-implantitis, no mobility, no patient discomfort during follow-up.

## Limitations
Small sample (31 implants) and short follow-up (~12.5 months). Two different implant **systems** (different manufacturers, macrogeometry and connection) are compared, so surface chemistry is confounded with brand — the trial cannot isolate hydrophilicity. ISQ is a surrogate and correlates imperfectly with histologic osseointegration. Randomization details and allocation concealment/blinding are not described. Posterior maxilla only, with mostly short implants (8.8 mm) and frequent sinus/GBR co-interventions, which limits generalization.

## Glossary
- **SLA:** Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched titanium surface.
- **NH:** New hydrophilic implant — SLA surface with a ≤10 nm bioabsorbable nano-HA partial coating (Hiossen).
- **SA:** Sandblasted and acid-etched implant used as control (Osstem).
- **ISQ:** Implant Stability Quotient (RFA, 1-100).
- **MBL:** Marginal Bone Loss.
- **SLwBG / SLwoBG:** Sinus lift with / without bone graft.
- **GBR:** Guided Bone Regeneration.
