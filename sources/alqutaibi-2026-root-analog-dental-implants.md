## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/immediate-implant/lee-2021-immediate-implant-placement-in-fresh]]가 발치 즉시 표준 나사형 임플란트 식립의 gap-management 원칙을 다뤘다면, 본 SR(Alqutaibi 2026, PROSPERO CRD420251162616)은 발치와 형태를 그대로 복제해 소켓과의 gap 자체를 없애는 root-analog implant(RAI, 치근형태모사 임플란트) 28개 임상연구(432개 RAI)를 정리해 대안적 즉시식립 전략의 근거를 보강한다. 소켓 형태를 보존한다는 목표는 [[wiki/immediate-implant/socket-shield/ahamed-2022-partial-extraction-therapy-implant-placement]]의 partial extraction therapy(치근 일부 보존)와 개념적으로 대비되는 접근(임플란트 자체를 치근형으로 제작 vs 자연치 치근 일부를 남김)이라는 점도 흥미롭다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis; PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD420251162616) of 28 clinical studies (432 root-analog implants, RAIs) covering titanium, zirconia, and hybrid titanium-zirconia implants fabricated by CAD-CAM milling or additive manufacturing (SLM/DLMS/DLMF), placed to mimic extracted-tooth root morphology for immediate single-tooth replacement.

Most studies reported survival 71–100% and success 64.5–100%; titanium and hybrid titanium-zirconia RAIs were most predictable (survival up to 100%, MBL as low as 0.7 mm at 1 year), whereas zirconia RAIs ranged from 33.3% to 100% survival and showed higher failure/fracture risk when macroretentive design or surface treatment was inadequate.

RAIs show promising short- to medium-term esthetic and soft-tissue outcomes with reduced grafting need, but evidence is dominated by single-patient clinical reports and small case series with short follow-up (≤2 years in most), heterogeneous protocols, and only 1 RCT — well-designed multicenter RCTs with longer follow-up are needed before RAIs can be recommended over conventional immediate implants.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(메타분석 없음; PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD420251162616)로, 발거 치아의 치근 형태를 그대로 복제해 제작한 root-analog implant(RAI, 치근형태모사 임플란트) 28개 임상연구(432개 RAI, 티타늄·지르코니아·하이브리드 티타늄-지르코니아, CAD-CAM 밀링 또는 SLM/DLMS/DLMF 적층제조)를 종합.

대부분 연구에서 생존율 71–100%, 성공률 64.5–100%를 보고했으며, 티타늄·하이브리드 RAI가 가장 예측 가능(생존율 최대 100%, 1년차 MBL 최저 0.7mm)했고, 지르코니아 RAI는 생존율 33.3–100%로 편차가 크고 macroretention·표면처리가 불충분할 때 파절·실패 위험이 높았다.

RAI는 단기-중기 심미·연조직 결과가 양호하고 골이식 필요성을 줄일 수 있지만, 근거 대부분이 단일환자 임상보고·소규모 케이스시리즈(추적기간 대부분 ≤2년, RCT 단 1편)로 이질성이 커서, 기존 즉시식립 임플란트를 대체 권고하기 전에 잘 설계된 다기관 RCT와 장기 추적이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First comprehensive systematic review focused specifically on the clinical (not just technical/feasibility) evidence for root-analog implants (RAIs) — custom implants designed from CBCT/surface-scan data to replicate the exact root morphology of the tooth being extracted, intended to close the gap between socket and implant that conventional screw-type immediate implants face.
- Synthesizes 28 studies spanning 1997–2025 (16 clinical reports, 1 retrospective case series, 7 prospective case series, 2 prospective nonrandomized studies, 1 RCT, 1 retrospective comparative study), 432 RAIs total.
- Provides a material/fabrication-stratified outcomes table (titanium-milled, zirconia-milled, titanium 3D-printed [SLM/DLMF/DLMS], hybrid titanium-zirconia) with survival, success, and MBL ranges for each.
- Includes a historical timeline (Table 1) tracing RAI development from the 1997 ReImplant system through the 2022 10-year DLMS follow-up and 2025 5-year zirconia RCT data.
- Risk-of-bias assessed with study-design-appropriate tools (RoB 2 for the RCT, ROBINS-I for nonrandomized studies, JBI checklists for reports/case series, Newcastle-Ottawa Scale for the retrospective comparative study).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Search**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, searched independently by 2 reviewers in September 2025, no language restriction; PRISMA-guided; reference lists and key journals hand-searched.
- **Screening**: 8821 records identified → 2940 duplicates removed → 5881 screened → 30 full-text assessed (+1 from other sources) → 28 included (3 excluded as non-clinical/animal studies).
- **Eligibility**: Clinical trials, clinical reports, and case series reporting clinical/radiographic/prosthetic/esthetic/patient-reported outcomes of RAIs placed in maxilla or mandible, anterior or posterior, single- or multi-rooted teeth. Reviews, editorials, conference abstracts, and non-RAI prefabricated implants excluded.
- **Data extracted**: study design, sample size, patient demographics, implant material, fabrication method (subtractive CAD-CAM milling vs additive SLM/DLMS/DLMF), loading protocol, follow-up, surgical parameters, and all clinical/radiographic/esthetic/PRO outcomes.
- **Synthesis**: descriptive/narrative only — a meta-analysis was not attempted because of heterogeneity in study designs, follow-up durations, and outcome definitions.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Sample**: 28 studies, 432 RAIs, patient ages 18–82, both maxillary and mandibular sites (anterior incisors through molars), follow-up from a few months up to 10 years (longest prospective series: 5 years, Beus 2025).
- **Materials**: titanium alloy, zirconia (Y-TZP), and hybrid titanium-root/zirconia-abutment designs; fabrication mostly subtractive CAD-CAM milling, with additive DLMS/SLM/DLMF also reported.
- **Survival/success by material** (Table 3 of the paper):
  - Titanium–milled: survival 70.6–100% (up to 3 y), success 96.5–100%, MBL ~1.1–1.3 mm.
  - Zirconia–milled: survival 33.3–100% (highly surface-treatment-dependent), success 71% (5 y)–100%, MBL 0.71–1.38 mm.
  - Titanium–3D printed (SLM/DLMF/DLMS): survival 44.4–100% (up to 10 y), success 100%, MBL as low as 0.7–1.3 mm at 1 year.
  - Hybrid titanium-zirconia: survival 83.3–100%, success 64.5–100%, mean MBL 1.20 ± 0.73 mm.
- **Key comparative finding**: a 2022 prospective comparative trial (Akkoyun et al.) found CNC-milled titanium (70.6%) outperformed DLMS titanium (44.4%) and zirconia (33.3%) RAIs, with failures attributed to poor osseointegration and mechanical weakness — underscoring that fabrication method interacts with material in determining outcome, not material alone.
- **Design factor**: interdental macroretentions and surface roughening (airborne-particle abrasion, acid etching) were repeatedly identified as critical for zirconia RAI success; one prospective trial found a 0% survival group without macroretentions vs 92% with them (Pirker & Kocher 2009).
- **Esthetics/soft tissue**: consistently favorable across most reports (stable gingival contours, healthy mucosa), with occasional buccal recession/metal show-through noted for early titanium designs.
- **Benchmark context**: prior SRs report 83.7–100% survival for conventional immediate root-form implants; RAI survival (71.1–100%, one outlier at 33.3%) is broadly comparable for titanium/hybrid designs but more variable overall, especially for zirconia.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Evidence is dominated by single-patient clinical reports and small case series conducted under optimal/expert-operator conditions — limited generalizability to routine or posterior/complex cases.
- Long-term data (>5 years) are scarce (only 2 studies).
- No formal control group in most included studies; only 1 RCT and 1 retrospective comparative study in the entire body of evidence.
- Highly heterogeneous protocols (loading time 12–52 weeks, adjunctive grafting variably used) prevented meta-analysis.
- Likely reporting/publication bias; patient-reported and functional outcome data sparse.
- Authors call for large multicenter prospective studies and RCTs, standardized outcome reporting, head-to-head comparison with conventional immediate implants and alternatives (orthodontic extrusion, autotransplantation), and cost-effectiveness data.

## 6. Related Work

- Cites prior RAI feasibility/technical literature (Hodosh 1969 polymer implants; Lundgren 1992 animal model; Moin 2013 CBCT/CAD-CAM design concept) as the historical basis for the digital-fabrication RAI concept reviewed here.
- Compares RAI survival against prior SRs of conventional immediate implant placement (Soegiantho 2023; Azarias 2025; Campi 2025), all reporting 83.7–100% survival.
- Distinct from, but conceptually adjacent to, socket-shield/partial extraction therapy (PET) literature already in this wiki (see [[wiki/immediate-implant/socket-shield/lin-2022-socket-shield-technique-systematic-review-meta]]) — both aim to preserve native root-socket geometry/buccal bone, but RAI replaces the whole tooth with a root-shaped implant while PET retains a fragment of the natural root.

## 7. Glossary

- **RAI (Root-Analog Implant)**: A custom dental implant fabricated to replicate the exact external morphology of the extracted tooth root, intended to achieve intimate socket fit and primary stability without extensive osteotomy.
- **CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography)**: 3D imaging used to capture root/socket geometry for RAI design.
- **DICOM**: Digital Imaging and Communication in Medicine — the file format used to transfer CBCT scan data into CAD software.
- **CAD-CAM**: Computer-Aided Design / Computer-Aided Manufacturing.
- **SLM (Selective Laser Melting)**, **DLMS (Direct Laser Metal Sintering)**, **DLMF (Direct Laser Metal Forming)**: Additive (3D-printing) metal fabrication technologies used for titanium RAIs.
- **Y-TZP**: Yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal — the zirconia material used for zirconia RAIs.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: Radiographic measure of peri-implant crestal bone change over time.
- **PROSPERO**: International prospective register of systematic reviews.
- **ROB 2 / ROBINS-I / NOS / JBI**: Risk-of-bias assessment tools used for RCTs, non-randomized studies, retrospective cohorts, and case reports/series respectively.
