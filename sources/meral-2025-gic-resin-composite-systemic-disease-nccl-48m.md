## Why Ingested

First 4-year RCT specifically enrolling patients with systemic diseases. Compares GIC (Fuji Bulk) vs resin composite (G-ænial Posterior + universal adhesive, etch-and-rinse) in a clinically vulnerable population where technique sensitivity and cost are practical concerns. Shows GIC as a viable NCCL restoration alternative with comparable survival to composite.

## Study Design

- **Design**: Single-center randomized clinical trial, parallel-arm
- **Population**: 30 patients with systemic diseases; 134 restorations total
- **Interventions**:
  - GIC: Fuji Bulk (FB)
  - RC: G-ænial Posterior (GP) + universal adhesive in etch-and-rinse mode
- **Follow-up**: 48 months
- **Recall rate**: 60%
- **Evaluation criteria**: Modified USPHS — retention, marginal discoloration, marginal adaptation, secondary caries, surface texture, post-operative sensitivity

## Key Results

| Outcome | Fuji Bulk (GIC) | G-ænial Posterior (RC) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retention loss | 7/38 (18.4%) | 4/30 (13.3%) | 0.57 (ns) |
| 48-month survival | 82.1% | 89.3% | 0.305 (log-rank, ns) |
| Marginal adaptation change from baseline | Significant (p<0.05) | Significant (p<0.05) | — |
| Between-group difference at any period | — | — | p>0.05 (ns) |
| Secondary caries | None | None | — |

- Marginal discoloration and surface texture also showed significant change over time for both materials; no between-group difference at any time point.

## Authors' Conclusions

After 48 months, both GIC and RC demonstrated acceptable clinical performance in NCCLs of patients with systemic diseases. GIC showed no significant differences versus RC; may be more cost-effective and less technique-sensitive for patients with complex medical conditions.

## Limitations

- Small sample: 30 patients (134 restorations)
- 60% recall rate at 48 months limits statistical power
- Single clinician — may limit generalizability
- Systemic disease type(s) not specified in abstract; heterogeneous patient group likely
- abstract-only extraction; full text not retrieved

## Three-line Summary

A 4-year RCT of 134 NCCLs in systemically compromised patients found no significant difference between Fuji Bulk GIC (82.1% survival) and G-ænial Posterior RC (89.3% survival) at 48 months by any modified USPHS criterion. Both materials showed acceptable marginal changes over time without secondary caries. GIC is presented as a viable, potentially less technique-sensitive alternative for this patient population.

## 세줄요약

전신질환 환자 134개 NCCL에서 Fuji Bulk GIC(생존율 82.1%)와 G-ænial Posterior RC(89.3%)를 48개월간 비교한 RCT로, 유지율·변연 적합성·변색 등 수정 USPHS 기준 모든 항목에서 군 간 유의차 없었다. 두 재료 모두 이차 우식 없이 허용 가능한 임상 성능을 보였다. GIC는 시술 민감도가 낮고 비용 효율적이어서 전신질환 환자의 NCCL 수복 대안으로 제시된다.
