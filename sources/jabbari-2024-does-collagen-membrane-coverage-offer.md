## Why Ingested

Lateral-window 콜라겐 차폐막 (Collagen Membrane) 피개 여부의 근거는 [[sinus-lift/lateral/starch-jensen-2019-sinus-augmentation-barrier-membrane-window]] (SR+MA, 6 RCT)에서 "신생골 유의차 없음"으로 정리돼 있는데, 그 메타분석에 포함된 연구 중 동종골 (Allograft)을 이식재로 쓴 것은 없었다 — 이 RCT는 동결건조골이식재/탈회동결건조골이식재 (FDBA/DFDBA) 혼합 동종골만으로 그 축을 6개월 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry)으로 직접 검증해, 해당 SR+MA의 결론에 반대 방향의 1차 근거를 추가한다.

## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=30, 15 per arm; mean age 46.33±7.25 y) of lateral-window maxillary sinus floor augmentation with mixed mineralized/demineralized freeze-dried bone allograft (1000–2000 µm particles), randomized to 0.2–0.4 mm absorbable collagen membrane coverage of the osteotomy window versus no coverage, with 3 mm trephine core biopsy at 6 months.

Newly formed bone area was significantly greater with membrane coverage (100.47±16.37 vs 85.27±12.37; p<0.001) and connective tissue area significantly smaller (41.20±12.82 vs 54.20±14.50; p=0.015), while native bone area was significantly greater in the uncovered group (100.27±11.45 vs 79.33±13.76; p<0.001).

The authors advocate routine collagen membrane coverage of the lateral window, but the absolute-area units are reported as µm² without normalization to total core area, no post-implant follow-up was performed, and n=30 in one center limits generalization.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 30명, 군당 15명, 평균 46.33±7.25세): 동종골 (Allograft, FDBA/DFDBA 혼합 1000–2000 µm)로 측방창 상악동거상술 (Lateral Window Sinus Floor Augmentation) 후 0.2–0.4 mm 흡수성 콜라겐 차폐막 (Collagen Membrane) 피개 유무를 6개월 트레핀 (Trephine) 생검 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry)으로 비교.

차폐막 피개군에서 신생골 (Newly Formed Bone) 면적이 유의하게 컸고 (100.47±16.37 vs 85.27±12.37, p<0.001), 결합조직 (Connective Tissue) 면적은 유의하게 작았으며 (41.20±12.82 vs 54.20±14.50, p=0.015), 잔류골 (Native Bone) 면적은 비피개군에서 유의하게 컸다 (100.27±11.45 vs 79.33±13.76, p<0.001).

저자들은 측창 콜라겐 막 피개를 적극 권장하나, 면적이 총 코어 면적으로 정규화되지 않은 µm² 절대값이고 임플란트 식립 후 추적이 없으며 단일기관 30명이라는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly tests, in an allograft-only lateral-window model, whether collagen membrane coverage of the osteotomy window changes graft maturation histology at 6 months — a cell the authors state no prior meta-analysis had covered with allograft.
- Reports a bidirectional result: membrane coverage increased newly formed bone but simultaneously decreased the native bone fraction, i.e. the effect is a shift in core composition, not a uniform gain.
- Quantifies the connective-tissue effect (membrane 41.20 vs no-membrane 54.20, p=0.015), supporting the "barrier excludes non-osteogenic tissue" mechanism rather than only a new-bone effect.
- Adds correlation structure within each arm: in uncovered sites connective tissue correlated inversely with both native (r=−0.674, p=0.006) and new bone (r=−0.699, p=0.004), whereas in membrane-covered sites only native vs new bone correlated (r=−0.515, p=0.050).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Double-blind RCT; block randomization by a nurse blind to the study, sequentially numbered opaque envelopes, allocation concealed from the histologic examiner and the data analyst; CONSORT and Helsinki followed.
- **Setting / period**: Mashhad Dental School, Iran; October 2021 – October 2022.
- **Inclusion**: Healthy adults >18 y, ASA I–II, posterior edentulous maxilla needing implants for a missing second premolar and first molar, residual alveolar bone height 3–5 mm, good oral hygiene. **Exclusion**: medical contraindications, sinus pathology, sinus septa, intraoperative Schneiderian membrane perforation, loss to follow-up.
- **n**: 30 patients, 15 per arm (block randomization, equal groups).
- **Intervention**: Lateral window approach by one surgical team under local anesthesia; full-thickness mucoperiosteal flap, bony window outlined and lifted, Schneiderian membrane elevated; graft = freeze-dried bone allograft (Tehran Grafting Bank), mineralized + demineralized mixture, 1000–2000 µm particles, loosely packed. **Control = with membrane**: 0.2–0.4 mm absorbable collagen membrane (Tehran Grafting Bank) over the window. **Test = without membrane**: flap with intact periosteum repositioned directly. Silk 3-0 closure; Co-amoxiclav 625 mg TID × 7 days; sutures out at day 7.
- **Sampling**: CBCT at 6 months, then 3 mm trephine core from the augmented site immediately before implant placement; 10% formalin 24 h, decalcified in 7% nitric acid, paraffin, 5 µm sections, H&E, light microscopy (Olympus BX51); histomorphometry in ImageJ.
- **Outcomes**: area of newly formed bone, native bone and connective tissue; histologic assessment of inflammation, necrosis, connective tissue and bone quality.
- **Statistics**: SPSS v21; independent t-test, paired t-test, chi-square, Pearson correlation; α = 0.05; Shapiro-Wilk for normality (all variables normal).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | With membrane (n=15) | Without membrane (n=15) | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connective tissue area (mean ± SD; range) | 41.20 ± 12.82 (20–68) | 54.20 ± 14.50 (33–76) | T=2.59, p=0.015 |
| Native bone area | 79.33 ± 13.76 (58–108) | 100.27 ± 11.45 (84–117) | T=4.53, p<0.001 |
| Newly formed bone area | 100.47 ± 16.37 (60–123) | 85.27 ± 12.37 (64–104) | T=2.87, p<0.001 |

- Units are printed as µm² in the text and tables; no normalization to total core area is reported (see Limitations).
- Groups balanced: age 46.07±7.12 (membrane) vs 46.60±7.62 (no membrane), p=0.845; sex 7M/8F vs 8M/7F, p=0.715. No sex difference in any histomorphometric variable (independent t-test).
- **Histology**: all specimens showed newly formed trabeculae of both mature lamellar and unorganized woven bone, with larger osteocyte lacunae and higher osteocyte density; no prominent inflammation (scarce inflammatory cells at most), no necrosis, no foreign-body reaction in either arm.
- **Correlations, no-membrane arm**: connective tissue vs native bone r=−0.674 (p=0.006); connective tissue vs new bone r=−0.699 (p=0.004); native vs new bone r=0.984 (p<0.001).
- **Correlations, membrane arm**: only native vs new bone reached significance, and inversely: r=−0.515 (p=0.050); connective tissue vs native r=−0.098 (p=0.727); connective tissue vs new bone r=−0.346 (p=0.206).
- **Reported comparisons with prior work**: concordant with Wallace et al. (absorbable vs non-absorbable no difference, more connective tissue without membrane), Tarnow et al. (~2× vital bone with membrane), Choi et al. (less connective tissue with absorbable membrane) and Barone et al. (lower connective tissue with membrane, vital-bone difference not significant); discordant with Schulten et al. (membrane not beneficial, lower osteoid density).
- **Not measured**: implant survival, MBL, graft volume change (CBCT taken but not reported as an outcome), soft-tissue outcomes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No follow-up after implant placement — the histologic advantage is never linked to implant survival, marginal bone loss or prosthodontic outcome.
- Sample size 30 in a single center; the authors themselves flag limited generalizability and call for multi-center trials with longer follow-up and other graft/membrane types.
- Histomorphometric results are reported as absolute areas in µm² without stating total core area or percentages, so the three compartments cannot be checked for summing to 100% and cross-study comparison is unsafe.
- Group labels are inverted relative to convention (control = with membrane, test = without), which invites misreading of the results and discussion.
- Schneiderian membrane perforations and septated sinuses were excluded, so the population is the easy case; the authors' own "highly advocated" conclusion is therefore not tested where membrane coverage is most often argued for.

## 6. Related Work

- starch-jensen-2019: SR+MA of 6 RCTs on barrier membrane coverage of the lateral window found no significant difference in vital bone or connective tissue formation — this RCT's significant new-bone difference is the main counter-datapoint, though none of that SR's studies used allograft.
- Wallace-2003 / Tarnow et al.: earlier reports of higher vital bone fraction (up to ~2×) with membrane coverage, cited by the authors as concordant.
- Schulten et al.: membrane coverage reportedly decreased osteoid density and new trabeculae — the authors attribute the discrepancy to graft type (allograft here) and larger n.
- Choi et al.: less connective tissue with absorbable membrane but equal new bone, possibly because cores came from the central sinus — the same sampling site used here.
- Barone et al.: higher (non-significant) vital bone with membrane, significantly less connective tissue; small n and unbalanced age cited as explanations.
- Shiezadeh et al.: PRF as an adjunct to allograft in sinus floor elevation induces bone-marrow formation — adjacent adjunct strategy.
- Tawill et al.: bovine mineral ± bilayer collagen membrane; higher implant survival and shorter healing with membrane coverage.

## 7. Glossary

- **Collagen membrane (콜라겐 차폐막)**: absorbable 0.2–0.4 mm barrier placed over the lateral osteotomy window to exclude non-osteogenic soft tissue from the grafted site.
- **Lateral window / lateral antrostomy (측방창 접근법)**: bony window in the lateral wall of the maxillary sinus for membrane elevation and graft placement.
- **FDBA / DFDBA (동결건조골이식재 / 탈회동결건조골이식재)**: freeze-dried and demineralized freeze-dried bone allograft; here used as a mineralized + demineralized mixture, 1000–2000 µm.
- **Native bone (잔류골)**: pre-existing bone trabeculae in the core, as distinct from newly formed bone.
- **Histomorphometry (조직형태계측)**: ImageJ measurement of the area fraction of each tissue compartment on H&E sections.
- **Trephine core (트레핀 생검)**: 3 mm trephine biopsy of the augmented site taken before implant placement.
- **Woven vs lamellar bone (교직골 vs 층판골)**: unorganized rapid-turnover new bone versus mature organized bone; both were present in all specimens.
