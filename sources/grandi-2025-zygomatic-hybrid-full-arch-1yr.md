## Why Ingested

No held paper specifically analyzed angled abutment zygomatic implants (45°/52.5°/60°) in a hybrid full-arch protocol with 1-year outcomes including ZICL classification and mucosal health indices. Prior zygomatic papers either lack abutment angle comparison or use different protocols. This retrospective fills the gap on whether abutment angulation affects clinical outcomes in the extrasinus approach.

[[implants/full-arch/grandi-2025-zygomatic-hybrid-full-arch-1yr]]
## Three-line Summary

Retrospective 1-year analysis of hybrid full-arch maxillary rehabilitation in 35 patients using 98 straight + 81 zygomatic implants (extrasinus approach) with angled abutments at 45°, 52.5°, and 60°; biological and prosthetic outcomes plus ZICL and mucosal indices assessed.

Implant survival 100%; prosthesis survival 94.3%; ZICL0-1 in 95% of zygomatic sites; postoperative sinus opacity in 7/35 patients (20%), including 2 with unilateral sinus membrane perforation; no significant differences among abutment inclination groups.

Hybrid zygomatic-conventional full-arch rehabilitation achieves excellent 1-year implant survival regardless of abutment angulation; sinus complications (opacity, membrane perforation) remain a watchpoint.

## 세줄요약

후향적 연구, 35명, 수직 98개+접합골(Zygomatic) 81개 임플란트, 혼합형 전악수복; 경사 어버트먼트(Angled Abutment) 45°/52.5°/60° 비교; 1년 추적.

임플란트 생존율 100%; 보철 생존율 94.3%; 접합골 임플란트 분류(Zygomatic Implant Classification Level, ZICL) 0-1 95%; 부비동 불투명화(Sinus Opacity) 7명(20%), 2명 막 천공.

경사도에 따른 임상 지수 차이 없음; 1년 단기 결과 양호하나 부비동 합병증 모니터링 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Comparison of three abutment angulations (45°/52.5°/60°) on zygomatic implants — first in this wiki to stratify by abutment angle
- ZICL0-1 in 95%: good mucosal adaptation around zygomatic implants at 1yr
- Sinus opacity in 20% (7/35): highlights biological monitoring need even with extrasinus approach
- Confirms 100% implant survival at 1yr for hybrid zygomatic + conventional protocol

## 3. Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Retrospective single-center |
| N patients | 35 edentulous maxilla |
| N implants | 98 straight + 81 zygomatic = 179 total |
| Abutment angles | 45° (n=21), 52.5° (n=23), 60° (n=37) |
| Surgical approach | Extrasinus |
| Follow-up | 1 year |
| Outcomes | Implant/prosthesis survival, complications, MSEE, mPLI, mBI, ZICL |

## 4. Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Implant survival | **100%** |
| Prosthesis survival | **94.3%** |
| ZICL0-1 (zygomatic sites) | **95%** |
| Sinus opacity (postop) | 7/35 patients (20%) |
| Sinus membrane perforation | 2/35 (5.7%) — unilateral |
| Abutment angle effect on clinical indices | No significant difference |

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract only — detailed statistical comparisons, mPLI/mBI/MSEE values, and individual abutment-angle breakdown not available
- Short follow-up (1 year) — insufficient for long-term survival assessment
- Single center; small sample (n=35)
- Retrospective design
