## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 72 systematic/narrative reviews on toothpaste active ingredients, covering efficacy across six oral conditions (gum health, caries, dentine hypersensitivity, tooth surface loss, oral malodour, tooth whitening).

Nineteen active ingredients identified across six fluoride types; six ingredients showed significant benefit for their targeted condition, with efficacy varying notably among fluoride types.

Clinical implication: toothpaste selection should be tailored to patient-specific oral conditions rather than assuming all toothpastes are equivalent; abstract-only — full text not retrieved.

## 세줄요약

치약 활성성분의 유효성을 종합한 엄브렐라 리뷰(리뷰 72편) — 치은건강·우식·상아질과민증·치아표면손실·구취·미백 6개 구강상태 대상.

19개 활성성분(불소 6종 포함) 확인, 이 중 6개 성분이 목표 상태에 유의한 효과를 보였으며 불소 종류별 유효성 차이가 뚜렷함.

임상적 의미: 치약은 획일적이지 않으므로 환자별 구강상태에 맞춰 선택해야 함; 초록만 확보(전문 미확보).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Umbrella review design (systematic/narrative reviews of RCTs, observational, and in-vitro studies) covering toothpaste ingredient efficacy across 6 oral condition domains.
- Identifies 19 distinct active ingredients (including 6 fluoride types) mapped to specific clinical indications.
- Highlights that "all toothpastes are similar" is a clinician misconception — efficacy is ingredient- and condition-specific.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Umbrella review (review-of-reviews), OVID electronic search.
- 72 reviews identified and quality-assessed via PRISMA checklist.
- Focused question: efficacy of common toothpaste active ingredients for gum health, caries, dentine hypersensitivity, tooth surface loss, oral malodour, and tooth whitening/staining.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 19 active ingredients identified across the 6 oral conditions.
- 6 ingredients reported with significant benefit for their targeted condition.
- Notable variability in efficacy specifically among the 6 types of fluoride evaluated.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingestion — full-text methodology/results tables and per-ingredient effect sizes not verified beyond the abstract.
- Umbrella review of heterogeneous review types (systematic + narrative) — inherent risk of carrying forward primary-study bias.

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: A review that synthesizes findings from multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a topic.
- **Dentifrice**: Toothpaste or tooth-cleaning paste/gel product.
- **AMSTAR/PRISMA**: Methodological quality and reporting checklists for systematic reviews.
