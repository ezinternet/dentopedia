## Three-line Summary

First systematic review (21 pediatric LAST cases, PubMed + Scopus through March 2023; Medicine 2024) of lipid emulsion treatment for LAST in patients <19 years: 43% were <1 year old, bupivacaine was responsible for ~67% of cases, and lipid emulsion achieved full recovery in 20/21 (95.2%) patients.

Under general anesthesia, LAST predominantly presented as arrhythmia; in awake patients, as seizures; 20% lipid emulsion 1.5 mL/kg bolus + 0.25 mL/kg/min continuous infusion was the most common protocol; cardiovascular symptoms showed the most improvement (~71% of cases).

The one death was due to secondary carnitine deficiency from valproic acid treatment, which amplified bupivacaine toxicity by impairing fatty acid β-oxidation in cardiac mitochondria; non-anesthesiologist overdose and inadvertent intravascular injection by anesthesiologists were the two primary LAST mechanisms.

## 세줄요약

소아 LAST 리피드 에멀전 (Lipid Emulsion) 치료 첫 SR (21 증례, 2024; Medicine) — 43% <1세, 뷰피바카인 (Bupivacaine) 67%, 완전 회복 20/21 (95.2%).

전신마취 중 = 부정맥 우세; 의식 있는 상태 = 경련 우세; 20% LE 1.5 mL/kg 볼러스 + 0.25 mL/kg/min 가장 많이 사용; 심혈관 증상 개선 ~71%.

1명 사망 = 발프로산 유발 이차성 카르니틴 결핍 → 심근 β-산화 장애 → 뷰피바카인 독성 증폭; 비마취과 의사 과용량·마취과 의사 혈관내 주입이 주요 기전.
