## Three-line Summary

General healthcare narrative review (Health Science Reports 2025, not dental-specific) surveying artificial intelligence applications across diagnostics, treatment, and operational efficiency. Conducted Web of Science search spanning 2014–2024 covering machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, and robotics. Included industrial case studies (Google Health, IBM Watson Health) and mapped core AI technologies to clinical deployment domains.

AI-related healthcare publications increased 4.6-fold over the decade: 158 articles (3.54% of total) in 2014 rising to 731 articles (16.33%) by 2024. Core applications include remote patient monitoring, predictive analytics, personalized treatment planning, and diagnostic imaging. Documentation shows AI frequently outperforms human clinicians in cancer, cardiac, and neurological imaging diagnostics. Primary barriers identified: data security, budget constraints, and regulatory/ethical compliance requirements.

Review methodology is narrative (no PRISMA, no quality assessment tools) without formal risk-of-bias grading. Authors emphasize multidisciplinary cooperation among clinicians, computer scientists, and regulators required for successful implementation. Ethical, legal, and social considerations remain underdeveloped in the literature. Limited to general healthcare context; direct applicability to dentistry-specific AI applications requires separate domain analysis.

## 세줄요약

일반 의료 AI 서술적 고찰(Health Science Reports 2025, 치과 비특화) — 진단·치료·운영효율 AI 응용 조사. Web of Science 2014~2024 검색: 기계학습·딥러닝·자연언어처리·로봇공학. Google/IBM 산업 사례 포함, 임상 배포 도메인으로 AI 기술 지도.

AI 의료 출판물 10년간 4.6배 증가: 2014년 158편(3.54%) → 2024년 731편(16.33%). 원격 모니터링·예측 분석·맞춤형 치료·진단 영상 핵심 응용. AI가 암·심장·신경 영상 진단에서 인간 임상의 초과 빈도. 주요 장벽: 데이터 보안·예산 제약·규제/윤리 준수.

Narrative 방법론(PRISMA 무, 질 평가 없음), risk-of-bias 등급화 미보유. 저자들은 임상의·컴퓨터과학자·규제 담당자 간 다학제 협력 강조. 윤리·법적·사회적 고려 문헌상 미개발. 일반 의료 맥락만, 치과 AI 특화 적용은 별도 도메인 분석 필요.
