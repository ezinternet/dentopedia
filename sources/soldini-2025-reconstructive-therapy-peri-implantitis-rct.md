## Three-line Summary

RCT (36 enrolled, 34 completed; abstract-only ingest) in patients with peri-implantitis intrabony defects ≥3 mm that did not resolve after standardized non-surgical therapy, randomized to reconstructive surgery (RG) or supportive care every 3 months alone (NRG), with 12-month follow-up.

Disease resolution did not differ (NRG 8 patients, 44.4% vs RG 7 patients, 43.8%; p ≥ 0.05) and clinical variables were similar, but radiographic bone fill was about 3× greater with reconstruction (1.21 ± 0.92 mm vs 0.36 ± 0.59 mm, significant).

Reconstruction added radiographic bone gain without a measurable gain in disease resolution at 12 months, so its value depends on how much weight radiographic fill gets; limited by small size (34 completers), 12-month follow-up, and registration after recruitment (NCT05168891).

## 세줄요약

RCT(36명 등록·34명 완료, 초록만 인제스트): 표준 비수술 치료 후에도 해소되지 않은 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 골내결손 (Intrabony defect, ≥3 mm) 환자를 재건술군 (Reconstructive Group, RG)과 3개월마다 지지치료만 받는 비재건군 (Non-Reconstructive Group, NRG)으로 무작위 배정해 12개월 추적.

질환 해소율은 차이가 없었고(NRG 8명 44.4% vs RG 7명 43.8%, p ≥ 0.05) 임상 변수도 유의한 차이가 없었으나, 방사선 골충전은 재건군이 약 3배(1.21 ± 0.92 mm vs 0.36 ± 0.59 mm, 유의).

재건술은 12개월 시점에서 질환 해소율을 높이지 못하고 방사선 골 이득만 더하므로 가치는 방사선 지표를 얼마나 중시하느냐에 달렸다. 한계는 소규모(완료 34명), 12개월 추적, 모집 후 임상시험 등록(NCT05168891).

## 1. Document Information
- Clinical Implant Dentistry and Related Research 2025;27(2):e70024 (with later correction). UIC Barcelona. NCT05168891 (registered after recruitment).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Isolates the added value of reconstructive surgery over supportive care, both after standardized non-surgical therapy.
- Disease resolution did not differ; bone fill did.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- RCT. 36 enrolled, 34 completed. All received standardized non-surgical therapy; non-responders randomized to reconstructive group (RG) vs non-reconstructive/supportive (NRG, SPT q3mo).
- Intrabony defects ≥3 mm. Primary: mean radiographic bone change at 12 mo (Mann–Whitney). PROs assessed.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Disease resolution: NRG 8 patients (44.4%) vs RG 7 patients (43.8%), no significant difference (abstract). Group sizes of 18 and 16 are inferred from the percentages and the 34 completers; the abstract does not state them.
- Radiographic bone fill ~3× greater in RG: 1.21 ± 0.92 mm vs 0.36 ± 0.59 mm (significant).
- No significant difference in clinical variables.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small n; 12-month; trial registered after enrollment; resolution endpoint may undervalue radiographic gain. Longer follow-up on whether bone fill translates to survival/stability.

## 7. Glossary
- RG: reconstructive group. NRG: non-reconstructive group. SPT: supportive peri-implant therapy.
