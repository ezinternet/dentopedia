## Why Ingested

This fills a specific evidence gap: existing alveolar 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) systematic reviews are predominantly non-molar or mixed-site. Molar sites face unique challenges — larger socket dimensions, multi-rooted anatomy, greater dimensional change — yet molar-specific evidence was not synthesized in the wiki. This SR directly addresses whether ARP is warranted at molar sites and whether it reduces advanced augmentation procedures like 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR) and 상악동 거상술 (Sinus Floor Augmentation, SFA). Reference [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/fok-2024-alveolar-socket-healing-evolving-knowledge]] for foundational socket healing biology.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 14 studies (8 RCTs, 2 CCTs, 3 prospective cohort, 1 retrospective cohort; 571 molar sites) comparing ARP to spontaneous healing post-extraction for subsequent implant placement.

ARP reduced horizontal ridge width loss (ARP: −1.02 to −2.73 mm vs SH: −2.36 to −4.44 mm) and halved the need for advanced grafting procedures (20.8% ARP vs 47.7% SH), including GBR, SFA, and lateral-window SFA.

ARP at molar sites is clinically justified — it limits post-extraction bone resorption and meaningfully reduces the frequency of secondary augmentation, though heterogeneity across studies precludes meta-analytic pooling.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR focused specifically on ARP outcomes at **molar sites** (prior reviews were non-molar or mixed)
- Quantifies ridge dimension changes at molar sockets for ARP vs SH with pooled ranges
- Demonstrates ARP reduces requirement for advanced augmentation at a clinically important margin (~27 percentage points)
- Includes outcomes for maxillary posterior sites specifically (SFA via lateral window approach reduced)
- Provides decision-support data for ARP planning in cases where posterior implant placement is anticipated

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review (no meta-analysis due to study heterogeneity)
- **Databases searched**: Four electronic databases
- **Study types included**: RCTs, CCTs, prospective and retrospective cohort studies
- **Population**: Adult patients with molar extractions planned for implant placement
- **Minimum follow-up**: 3 months
- **N studies**: 14 (8 RCTs, 2 CCTs, 3 prospective cohort, 1 retrospective cohort)
- **N sites**: 571 (range per study: 20–80)
- **Outcomes**: Horizontal/vertical ridge dimension changes, need for GBR, SFA, SFA-lateral window, implant-related outcomes
- **Comparison**: ARP vs spontaneous healing (SH)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | ARP | Spontaneous Healing |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal ridge width reduction | −1.02 ± 0.88 to −2.73 ± 1.68 mm | −2.36 ± 0.91 to −4.44 ± 3.71 mm |
| Need for advanced grafting (GBR/SFA) | 20.8% | 47.7% |
| Vertical ridge height reduction | Reduced (trend) | Greater (trend) |

- ARP benefit consistent across periodontally sound sockets for horizontal dimension
- Vertical dimension benefit trend noted irrespective of socket condition
- Maxillary posterior sites: ARP reduces likelihood of requiring lateral-window SFA
- Limitation: heterogeneity precludes meta-analytic pooling; narrative synthesis only
