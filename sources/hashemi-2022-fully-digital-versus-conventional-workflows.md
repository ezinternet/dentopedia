## Why Ingested

Prospective crossover clinical trial directly comparing fully digital (구내 스캐너 (Intraoral Scanner, IoS) + 모놀리식 지르코니아 (Monolithic Zirconia)) vs. conventional (폴리비닐실록산 (Polyvinylsiloxane, PVS) 인상 + CoCr 프레임) workflows for posterior three-unit 임플란트 지지 고정 치과 보철물 (Implant-Supported Fixed Dental Prostheses, ISFDPs) — provides crossover in-vivo accuracy, passivity, occlusion, aesthetics, and time data in the same 10 patients. Extends → [[digital-workflow/sui-2026-influence-of-a-digital-versus]] and reinforces → [[digital-workflow/digital-workflow]] overview evidence on digital vs. conventional workflow clinical equivalence.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective crossover RCT-class trial (n=10 patients, 20 three-unit ISFDPs) comparing fully digital (3Shape TRIOS 3 IoS + CAD/CAM zirconia) vs. conventional (PVS open-tray + CoCr cast) workflows for posterior mandibular three-unit implant-supported reconstructions.
Impression accuracy, framework passivity and adaptation, occlusal contact quality, and patient/clinician aesthetics ratings showed no significant difference between workflows; total and laboratory fabrication time was significantly shorter with the digital workflow (p<0.001).
Within study limitations, the fully digital workflow is clinically equivalent to conventional for most outcomes while offering a substantial time advantage in the laboratory phase.

## 세줄요약

전향적 교차 임상시험(n=10, 보철물 20개)으로 완전 디지털 워크플로우(3Shape TRIOS 3 구내 스캐너 (Intraoral Scanner, IoS) + 캐드캠 지르코니아) vs. 기존 워크플로우(폴리비닐실록산 (Polyvinylsiloxane, PVS) 개구 트레이 + CoCr 주조) 하악 후방 3유닛 임플란트 지지 고정 치과 보철물 (Implant-Supported Fixed Dental Prostheses, ISFDPs)을 비교한 연구.
인상 정확도, 프레임워크 수동성·적합도, 교합 접촉, 미적 평가 모두 두 워크플로우 간 유의차 없음; 단, 디지털 워크플로우의 총 제작 시간 및 기공실 시간이 유의하게 단축(p<0.001).
연구 한계 내에서 완전 디지털 워크플로우는 기존 워크플로우와 임상 성과가 동등하면서 기공실 시간에서 유의미한 이점을 제공.

## Key Contributions

- First prospective crossover trial evaluating both digital and conventional workflows in the same patients for three-unit posterior ISFDPs
- Comparable impression accuracy: no significant difference in linear displacement, inter-implant distance, or rotational displacement between IoS and PVS splinted open-tray technique
- Framework passivity and adaptation comparable: screw resistance test angles and one-screw test results NS between workflows
- Occlusal contact quality equivalent: mean contact thickness 0.49±0.39 µm (digital) vs. 0.60±0.56 µm (conventional), p=0.487
- Patient VAS aesthetics 8.6±0.52 (digital) vs. 8.4±0.97 (conventional), NS; FDI criteria all NS
- **Significant time advantage for digital workflow**: total fabrication time and laboratory time significantly shorter (p<0.001); clinical time NS
- Crossover design controls inter-patient variability — each patient received both workflow prostheses

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective crossover clinical controlled trial (CONSORT-compliant)
- **n**: 10 patients (7 females, mean age 47.1±11 years), 20 three-unit ISFDPs
- **Setting**: University clinic, Tehran University of Medical Sciences
- **Digital group**: 3Shape TRIOS 3 IoS; Arum scan body; monolithic zirconia framework (Katana translucent, Kuraray) milled 5-axis (Amann Girrbach); Ti-base abutment
- **Conventional group**: PVS open-tray (Panasil, Kettenbach); implant analogues; type IV stone cast; CoCr cast frameworks (Cara CoCr-SLM, Kulzer); leucite-reinforced feldspathic porcelain veneering (Vintage PRO, Shofu)
- **Reference model**: Splinted impression copings + resin verification jig + industrial scanner (Atos Core 5 Mp, 2 µm accuracy, GOM)
- **Accuracy measurement**: STL superimposition with best-fit algorithm (GOM Inspect v7.5); linear/rotational displacement in x/y/z axes
- **Passivity**: One-screw test + flag technique (screw resistance test) + periapical radiographs
- **Occlusion**: Elastomeric bite record scanned; 10 smallest functional contact thicknesses measured
- **Aesthetics**: Patient VAS + FDI criteria (shade, translucency, lustre, anatomical form)
- **Statistics**: Student t-test, Mann–Whitney U, Fisher's exact, GEE model; α=0.05 (SPSS v24)

## Limitations

- Small sample size (n=10 patients)
- Only one prosthesis delivered per patient (other kept as backup) — no long-term function/complication comparison
- Clinicians not blinded during aesthetic assessments
- Single university center
- Short follow-up (6 months); longer studies needed
- Different framework materials (zirconia vs. CoCr + porcelain) — material difference may confound material-specific outcomes

## Glossary

- **ISFDP**: Implant-Supported Fixed Dental Prosthesis — 임플란트 지지 고정 치과 보철물
- **IoS**: Intraoral Scanner — 구내 스캐너
- **PVS**: Polyvinylsiloxane — 폴리비닐실록산 (addition silicone impression material)
- **CAD/CAM**: Computer-Aided Design/Computer-Aided Manufacturing — 캐드캠
- **GEE**: General Estimating Equation — 일반 추정 방정식 (statistical model for correlated data)
- **Sheffield test**: One-screw test for framework passivity assessment
- **Best-fit algorithm**: STL superimposition method for 3D accuracy measurement
