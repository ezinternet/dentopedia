## Three-line Summary

Retrospective recall study of 460 composite restorations in noncarious cervical lesions (NCCLs) in 96 patients (mean age 60.9 y, mean restoration age 38.3 months), placed by undergraduate or postgraduate students and scored with modified USPHS criteria; factors tested by binary logistic regression.
Adhesive strategy and occlusal wear facets were the most influential factors: restoration loss was associated with wear facets (p=0.041) and with 2-step etch-and-rinse (2-E&R, p=0.043) or 2-step self-etch (2-SE, p=0.050); marginal integrity was the most frequent failure (gingival 50.7%, occlusal 42.4%); age, arch, tooth position/type and composite were not associated.
Selective enamel etching before a 2-SE adhesive had the lowest failure across parameters and wear-facet teeth failed more, but the retrospective design, operator-adhesive confounding (students chose different strategies) and absent odds ratios in the extracted text limit causal and quantitative conclusions.

## 세줄요약

비우식성 치경부 병소 (Noncarious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 복합레진 수복물 460개(환자 96명, 평균 연령 60.9세, 평균 경과 38.3개월)를 학부생·대학원생 시술 건으로 후향 리콜 평가한 연구로, 수정 USPHS 기준으로 점수화하고 이분형 로지스틱 회귀분석(binary logistic regression)을 적용함.
접착 전략(adhesive strategy)과 교합 마모면(occlusal wear facet)이 가장 영향력 있는 인자였고, 수복물 탈락(restoration loss)은 마모면(p=0.041), 2단계 산부식-세척 (2-step etch-and-rinse, 2-E&R, p=0.043), 2단계 자가산부식 (2-step self-etch, 2-SE, p=0.050)과 연관되었으며, 변연 적합도 (marginal integrity) 실패가 가장 흔함(치은측 50.7%, 교합측 42.4%); 연령·악궁·치아 위치/종류·복합레진 종류는 무관.
2-SE 접착제 전 법랑질 선택적 산부식(selective enamel etching)이 전반적으로 가장 낮은 실패율을 보였으나, 후향 설계·시술자와 접착 전략의 교란(confounding)·본문에 오즈비(odds ratio) 부재로 인과·정량 해석에는 한계가 있음.

- **Journal**: Int J Dent 2022;2022:8048265
- **DOI**: 10.1155/2022/8048265 (PMID 35449763, PMCID PMC9017584)
- **Institution**: University dental clinic (IRB code COA MU-DT/PY-IRB 2018/025.3004); country not stated in the extracted text
- **Evaluation window**: recall examinations between May 2018 and October 2018

## 2. Key Contributions

- Real-world, multi-operator NCCL restoration failure analysis (undergraduate and postgraduate students), complementing single-operator RCTs.
- Identifies occlusal wear facets as an intrinsic predictor of restoration loss, partial retention loss, marginal discoloration and marginal integrity failure, especially at the gingival margin.
- Reports that adhesive strategy was the dominant extrinsic factor; selective enamel etching followed by Clearfil SE Bond had the lowest failure rates in most parameters.
- Reports marginal integrity as the most frequent failure parameter (gingival 50.7%, occlusal 42.4%).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective clinical recall study (authors call it "retrospective randomized"; patients "randomly received a clinical recall evaluation"); two calibrated examiners; Cohen's kappa 0.85 at calibration, 0.95 inter-examiner and 0.96 intra-examiner in analysis.
- **n**: 460 NCCL restorations, 96 patients (36.1% men, 63.9% women); mean age 60.9 y (37-83); mean restoration age 38.3 months (range 1-10 years); 53% upper / 47% lower teeth; 28.1% anterior / 71.9% posterior.
- **Inclusion**: lesion with occlusal enamel margin and gingival dentin margin on root surface; margin not exceeding 50% of tooth height; no proximal involvement. **Exclusion**: uncontrolled systemic disease, systemic disease affecting salivary flow, orthodontic appliances, abutment teeth, base/liner on record.
- **Clinical protocol**: pumice cleaning, cervical retraction cord, cotton rolls and saliva ejector (no rubber dam described); adhesives used per manufacturer's instructions under instructor supervision.
- **Adhesives**: 40.2% 2-E&R (Adper Single Bond 2, Optibond Solo Plus, ExciTE F), 20.2% 2-SE (Clearfil SE Bond), 39.6% selective enamel etching (37% phosphoric acid at enamel, then Clearfil SE Bond).
- **Composites**: 70.4% Filtek Z350, 26.1% Estelite Sigma Quick (both nanofilled), 3.5% Filtek Z250 (microhybrid).
- **Candidate factors**: gender, age, arch, tooth position (quadrant), tooth type, occlusal wear facet, caries risk (ADA), operator (undergraduate vs postgraduate), adhesive strategy, composite.
- **Outcomes**: modified USPHS (Alfa/Bravo/Charlie) for restoration loss, partial retention loss, caries, marginal discoloration, marginal integrity; Bravo or Charlie = failure (restoration loss and caries failure scored as Charlie only).
- **Statistics**: binary logistic regression, alpha 0.05; sample size estimated at 371 restorations (power 90%).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Restoration loss (C)**: wear facet p=0.041; 2-E&R p=0.043; 2-SE p=0.050.
- **Partial retention loss (B+C)**: occlusal margin - 2-E&R p=0.013; gingival margin - gender p=0.014, wear facet p=0.004.
- **Marginal discoloration (B+C)**: occlusal - wear facet p=0.047, 2-SE p<0.001; gingival - gender p=0.047, wear facet p=0.002, 2-SE p=0.008.
- **Marginal integrity (B+C)**: failure rate occlusal 42.4%, gingival 50.7% (worst parameter). Occlusal - 2-E&R p=0.028, 2-SE p<0.001. Gingival - gender p=0.010, wear facet p<0.001, high caries risk p=0.035, moderate caries risk p=0.001, operator p=0.001.
- **Caries**: no specific factor associated.
- **Not associated with any failure parameter**: age, arch, tooth position, tooth type, composite type.
- **Operator-strategy distribution** (Discussion): undergraduates used 2-E&R 46.9%, selective etching 30.8%, 2-SE 22.3%; postgraduates used selective etching 77%, 2-E&R 11.5%, 2-SE 11.5%.
- **Authors' interpretation**: selective enamel etching had the significantly lowest failure across parameters; it failed less than 2-E&R for restoration loss, partial retention loss and marginal integrity, and less than 2-SE for restoration loss, marginal discoloration and marginal integrity (mainly at the occlusal enamel margin).
- The extracted text refers to Tables 2 and 3 but does not contain them: odds ratios, confidence intervals, reference categories and the overall restoration-loss rate are not available in the text. Directions of association are taken from the authors' Discussion.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective: no baseline lesion depth, sensitivity or sclerotic-dentin information; the authors recommend prospective studies.
- Operator and adhesive strategy are confounded (postgraduates mostly used selective etching), so the operator and adhesive effects cannot be cleanly separated.
- Single recall window (May-October 2018) with heterogeneous restoration ages (1-10 years); time-to-failure/survival analysis was not performed.
- Three 2-E&R products were pooled; only one 2-SE product (Clearfil SE Bond) was used, so strategy effects are product-confounded.
- Composite comparison is unbalanced (96.5% nanofilled vs 3.5% microhybrid).
- Many factors tested with no multiplicity adjustment; gender explanation (oral hygiene) is speculative.
- Occlusal wear facets were recorded as present/absent only.

## 7. Glossary

- **NCCL**: noncarious cervical lesion - loss of cervical tooth structure not caused by caries (erosion, abrasion, abfraction).
- **2-E&R**: two-step etch-and-rinse adhesive (phosphoric acid etch, then primer-adhesive).
- **2-SE**: two-step self-etch adhesive (primer then bond; here Clearfil SE Bond with 10-MDP).
- **Selective enamel etching**: phosphoric acid applied to enamel margins only, followed by a self-etch adhesive.
- **Occlusal wear facet**: smooth worn surface on occlusal/incisal tooth surface indicating parafunctional or heavy occlusal loading.
- **Modified USPHS criteria**: Alfa/Bravo/Charlie clinical rating of restorations; Bravo or Charlie scored as failure in this study.
