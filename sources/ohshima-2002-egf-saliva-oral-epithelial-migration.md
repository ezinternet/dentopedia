## Why Ingested
타액 내 생리적 농도 EGF가 구강상피세포 증식과 이동을 자극하는지, 그리고 두 경로가 서로 다른 신호전달계를 쓰는지를 규명. [[periodontics/dawes-2003-oral-mucosal-epithelium-turnover-time]]에서 기술한 빠른 구강점막 교체율의 성장인자 기전 중 하나를 탐색.

## One-line Summary
In vitro study using oral buccal carcinoma cell line HO-1-N-1 showing that physiologic salivary EGF concentrations (1–10 ng/mL) stimulate proliferation via PI3-K/p38-MAPK/MEK pathways and migration via MEK/tyrosine kinase/PI3-K pathways, with the two processes using distinct signaling routes.

## 한줄요약
구강상피세포주(HO-1-N-1) 시험관 연구: 타액 생리 농도 EGF(1–10 ng/mL)가 증식(PI3-K, p38-MAPK, MEK 경로)과 이동(MEK 30%↓, TK 40%↓, PI3-K 60%↓)을 별도 신호체계로 각각 촉진함.

## Three-line Summary
In vitro study (oral buccal carcinoma cell line HO-1-N-1, Nihon University) testing effects of physiologic salivary EGF concentrations (1–10 ng/mL) on oral epithelial proliferation and migration.
EGF at 1–10 ng/mL dose-dependently stimulated DNA synthesis; proliferation inhibited by PI3-K, p38-MAPK, and MEK inhibitors; migration reduced by MEK inhibition (30%), TK inhibition (40%), and PI3-K inhibition (60%) — showing distinct signaling routes for each process.
Carcinoma-derived cell line may not represent normal oral mucosa; in vitro only; salivary EGF bioavailability in vivo may differ.

## 세줄요약
시험관 연구(구강 협점막 암종 유래 세포주 HO-1-N-1, 니혼대학교): 타액 생리 농도 EGF(1–10 ng/mL)의 구강상피 증식·이동 효과 검증.
EGF 1–10 ng/mL 농도 의존적 DNA 합성 촉진; 증식은 PI3-K·p38-MAPK·MEK 억제제로 감소; 이동은 MEK 억제(30% 감소), TK 억제(40%), PI3-K 억제(60%) — 증식과 이동이 별개 신호 경로 사용.
암종 유래 세포주는 정상 점막 대표성 한계; 체외 실험; 생체 내 타액 EGF 생물학적 이용률 불확실.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: European Journal of Oral Sciences, Vol. 110, No. 2, pp. 130–136, April 2002
- Institution: Department of Biochemistry, Nihon University School of Dentistry, Tokyo, Japan
- Funding: Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrated that physiologic salivary EGF concentrations (1–10 ng/mL) are sufficient to stimulate oral epithelial DNA synthesis dose-dependently
- Confirmed EGF-stimulated oral epithelial cell migration at the same physiologic concentration range
- Identified that proliferation and migration pathways are dissociable: different kinase inhibitors selectively block each
- Quantified pathway contributions to migration: MEK inhibition (PD98059) reduces migration to 30%; TK inhibitor (herbimycin A) to 40%; PI3-K inhibitor (wortmannin) to 60%; dexamethasone to 60%
- Suggested that salivary EGF acts as an in vivo wound repair mediator when oral mucosa is injured

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: In vitro, cell line (HO-1-N-1 — buccal mucosa squamous cell carcinoma-derived oral epithelial cell line)
- EGF concentrations tested: 1, 5, 10 ng/mL (within physiologic salivary range)
- Proliferation assay: DNA synthesis by [³H]-thymidine incorporation
- Migration assay: cell migration rate with selective kinase inhibitors
- Inhibitors used: wortmannin (PI3-K), SB203580 (p38 MAPK), PD98059 (MEK), herbimycin A (TK), dexamethasone (glucocorticoid)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- DNA synthesis stimulated dose-dependently by 1–10 ng/mL EGF
- Proliferation significantly inhibited by: PI3-K inhibitor (wortmannin), p38 MAPK inhibitor (SB203580), MEK inhibitor (PD98059)
- **Migration rate effects of inhibitors:**
  - PD98059 (MEK): migration reduced to 30%
  - Herbimycin A (TK): migration reduced to 40%
  - Wortmannin (PI3-K): migration reduced to 60%
  - Dexamethasone: migration reduced to 60%
- Conclusion: EGF-stimulated proliferation and migration use different signal transduction pathways

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Cell line is carcinoma-derived (HO-1-N-1); may not fully represent normal oral mucosal epithelium
- In vitro only; salivary EGF bioavailability and receptor access in vivo may differ
- Does not quantify actual salivary EGF concentration in the subjects

## 7. Glossary
- **EGF (Epidermal Growth Factor)**: salivary growth factor; physiologic range in saliva 1–10 ng/mL
- **PI3-K**: Phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase; blocked by wortmannin
- **p38 MAPK**: stress-activated protein kinase; blocked by SB203580
- **MEK**: MAP/ERK kinase; blocked by PD98059
- **TK (tyrosine kinase)**: upstream EGF receptor kinase; blocked by herbimycin A
- **HO-1-N-1**: human oral epithelial cell line derived from buccal mucosa squamous cell carcinoma
