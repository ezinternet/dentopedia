## Why Ingested

Holds the strongest claim in the transcrestal collection for the classic osteotome (Summers-type) technique in the hard case: simultaneous placement of **two adjacent implants** with residual bone height (RBH) < 5 mm, reporting 100% survival and zero membrane perforation over 6 months. Provides the tension-release (blunt-dissection with sinus curettes, including the inter-implant space) mechanism that likely explains the perforation avoidance — directly relevant to [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/lin-2025-schneiderian-membrane-biomechanical-transcrestal-fea]] (perforation mechanics) and to the RBH stratification in [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/albash-2023-implant-protrusion-initial-bone-tsfe]].

## Three-line Summary

Non-randomized controlled trial (IJERPH 2022;19:8019, West China Hospital of Stomatology) of a modified osteotome sinus floor elevation (OSFE) technique for two adjacent implants in multiple maxillary posterior edentulous spaces: 18 patients with RBH < 5 mm (groups 0–4 mm and 4–5 mm) vs 12 patients with RBH ≥ 5 mm (5–7 mm).

Modification: after CAS drill preparation, dome-shaped sinus curettes (Dask kit) bluntly release Schneiderian membrane tension around the cavity and — distinctively — **between the two implants**, then an osteotome elevates the membrane to the desired height before graft fill and simultaneous implant placement.

Results at 6 months: 100% implant survival, **zero membrane perforation** (literature 28% for RBH < 5 mm) and no complications; graft height apically between implants stabilized at 8.92 mm with graft resorption statistically comparable to the RBH ≥ 5 mm group.

## 세줄요약

상악 구치부 다수 결손(RBH < 5 mm)에 인접 2개의 임플란트를 동시 식립하는 수정 osteotome 상악동거상술(OSFE)의 비무작위 대조시험 (IJERPH 2022;19:8019).

변형 포인트: CAS 드릴 후 돔형 큐렛(Dask kit)으로 막의 긴장을 둔성 박리로 해소하되 **임플란트 사이 공간까지 박리**한 뒤 osteotome으로 막을 원하는 높이까지 거상 → 골이식 → 동시 식립.

6개월 결과: 생존율 100%, **막 천공 0건**(RBH<5 mm 문헌상 28%)·합병증 없음; 임플란트 사이 이식골 높이 8.92 mm로 안정, 이식골 흡수량은 RBH≥5 mm군과 유의차 없음.
