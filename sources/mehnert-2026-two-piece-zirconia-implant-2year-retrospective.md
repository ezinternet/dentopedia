## Why Ingested

First 2-year retrospective on Zeramex XT two-piece ATZ implants (currently available commercial system), providing clinical MBL, PD, BOP, and survival data for a market-relevant product with carbon-fiber PEEK (VICARBOscrew) abutment screw. Extends [[wiki/implants/surface/mohseni-2024-clinical-outcomes-zirconia-implants]] (SR/MA) with a currently available system's single-center outcomes.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center study (31 patients, 44 two-piece ATZ ZrO₂ implants Zeramex XT supporting SCs; plus 63 total implants for SC/FDP/overdentures); September 2017–October 2021; ≥2 year follow-up; private German dental office; STROBE-compliant.
100% implant survival, 0 fractures; MBL: 0.24±0.58 mm (6mo), 0.18±0.62 mm (12mo), 0.20±0.66 mm (24mo) — all NS between periods; BOP decreased significantly (9.1%→1.1% at 24mo, p=0.002); success rate 93.2% (41/44 implants); 2 minor technical complications.
Two-piece ATZ ZrO₂ implants demonstrate high 2-year survival with minimal MBL comparable to titanium; BOP trend towards improvement; carbon-fiber PEEK screw showed no failures.

## Key Contributions

- 2-year outcomes for Zeramex XT (currently available commercial two-piece ATZ ZrO₂ implant)
- 100% survival rate; 0 implant fractures — ATZ mechanical reliability confirmed
- MBL minimal at all timepoints and comparable to titanium benchmarks (<1 mm)
- BOP significantly reduced 9.1% → 1.1% at 24 months — favorable peri-implant tissue integration
- VICARBOscrew (carbon fiber-reinforced PEEK non-metallic abutment screw): 0 failures
- Bone augmentation trend: lower MBL in augmented vs non-augmented sites (NS, exploratory)
- Success rate 93.2%; 3 failing success criteria — all implants retained (100% survival)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort; STROBE-compliant; Ethics: Nordrhein Medical Association (t2L/2023)
- **Setting**: Single private practice, Germany
- **n (SC analysis)**: 31 patients, 44 implants supporting single crowns
- **n (full cohort)**: 35 patients, 63 implants (SC 44, FDP 13, overdenture 6)
- **Implant**: Zeramex XT (ATZ, Zerafil surface — sandblasted Al₂O₃ + hypophosphorous acid etch)
- **Diameters**: 4.2 mm (65.9%), 5.5 mm (34.1%); Lengths: 10 mm (61.4%), 12 mm (38.6%)
- **Screw**: VICARBOscrew (carbon fiber-reinforced high-performance polymer — non-metallic)
- **Restoration**: Monolithic ZrO₂ SCs/FDPs cemented onto ATZ abutments; screw-secured at 25 Ncm
- **Bone augmentation**: 50% of cases (lateral augmentation 34.1%, sinus lift 11.4%, peri-implant gap 4.5%)
- **Healing**: Submerged; uncovering at 4 months (6 months for sinus lift)
- **MBL baseline**: Time of implant exposure (not implant placement)
- **Follow-up**: 6, 12, 24 months; all patients completed recall

## Key Results

| Parameter | 6 months | 12 months | 24 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| MBL mean ± SD (mm) | 0.24 ± 0.58 | 0.18 ± 0.62 | 0.20 ± 0.66 |
| PD mean ± SD (mm) | 2.61 ± 0.34 | 2.72 ± 0.29 | 2.78 ± 0.26 |
| BOP mean ± SD (%) | 5.7 ± 8.8 | 2.7 ± 7.1 | 1.1 ± 4.3 |

- MBL NS between periods (p≥0.062); MBL higher in maxilla (NS)
- PD: significant increase over time (Cohen's d=1.51 at 24mo vs baseline)
- BOP: significant decrease (p=0.002 at 24mo vs baseline; p=0.003 at 24mo vs 6mo)
- Implant survival: 100% (63/63)
- Implant success (SC group): 93.2% (41/44); 3 did not meet criteria (2 MBL>1.7mm, 1 persistent BOP)
- Technical complications: 2 (1 abutment screw fracture during prosthetics; 1 crown misfit)
- 0 biological complications; 0 implant fractures; 0 VICARBOscrew failures

## Limitations

- Retrospective; single private practice; single examiner (T.M.)
- Industry-funded: CeramTec; author served as paid lecturer 2014–2024
- MBL baseline = implant exposure (not implant placement) — limits comparison with studies using placement baseline
- No standardized radiographic holders — may affect measurement reproducibility
- Underpowered for definitive comparisons; exploratory only
- No comparator group (no Ti implant arm)

## Glossary

- **ATZ (알루미나 강화 지르코니아, Alumina-Toughened Zirconia)**: Y-TZP에 알루미나를 혼합해 파절 저항성을 높인 지르코니아 조성; 2-피스 임플란트에 사용
- **VICARBOscrew**: 탄소섬유 강화 고성능 폴리머로 제작된 비금속 나사; 생체내 금속 이온 방출 없음
- **Zerafil surface (제라필 표면)**: 알루미나 샌드블라스팅 + 하이포포스포러스산 에칭으로 제조된 Zeramex XT 임플란트 표면
- **Success criteria (성공 기준)**: PD ≤4mm + BOP 없음 + 고름 없음 + MBL ≤1.7mm — 생존율(survival)과 별개
- **ICC (급내 상관계수, Intraclass Correlation Coefficient)**: 동일 측정 반복 신뢰성; ≥0.95 = 우수
