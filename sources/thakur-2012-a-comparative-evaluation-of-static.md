## Three-line Summary

Clinical comparative study (n=28 completely edentulous patients) recording centric relation (CR) and horizontal condylar guidance (HCG) by static interocclusal wax and functional extraoral Gothic arch tracing, then subjectively evaluating the resulting complete dentures.

CR recorded by the two methods coincided in only 7.14%; interocclusal-wax CR was anterior to Gothic CR in 71.42% and posterior in 21.43%, while Gothic arch gave higher mean HCG on both sides than protrusive wax but not significantly (P>0.05).

Woelfel subjective evaluation showed no significant difference in any parameter — Gothic arch records CR more posteriorly, yet this does not change complete-denture clinical performance, and HCG angle was approximately similar by both methods (abstract-only).

## 세줄요약

완전 무치악 환자 28명 임상 비교연구 — 중심위(Centric Relation, CR)와 수평과로각(Horizontal Condylar Guidance, HCG)을 교합간 왁스(정적)와 구외 고딕아치 트레이싱(기능적)으로 기록하고 Woelfel 주관 평가로 완전의치를 평가.

두 방법의 CR 일치는 7.14%뿐이며, 왁스 CR은 고딕 CR보다 전방 71.42%·후방 21.43%; 고딕아치 HCG는 양측 모두 평균적으로 더 높았으나 유의하지 않음(P>0.05).

Woelfel 주관 평가는 모든 항목에서 유의차 없음 — 고딕아치가 CR을 더 후방에 기록하지만 완전의치 임상 성과에는 영향 없고 HCG 각도는 양 방법이 대체로 유사(초록만 확보).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Direct within-patient comparison of a **static** (interocclusal wax) and a **functional** (extraoral Gothic arch tracing) record for *both* centric relation and horizontal condylar guidance in completely edentulous patients.
- Quantifies CR disagreement geometrically: the two records coincided in only **7.14%**, with wax CR **anterior** to Gothic CR in **71.42%** and **posterior** in **21.43%**.
- Compares HCG: Gothic arch tracings produced **higher mean guidance values on both sides** than protrusive wax records, but the difference was **statistically insignificant (P>0.05)**.
- Adds a patient/denture-level outcome: dentures fabricated from each record were scored with the **Woelfel subjective evaluation criteria**, and no parameter differed significantly between groups.
- Concludes that the more posterior Gothic CR does **not** produce a clinical difference in complete-denture performance, and that HCG angle is approximately similar by both methods.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: clinical comparative study (cross-sectional, 28 completely edentulous patients).
- **Records**: centric relation and horizontal condylar guidance recorded by interocclusal wax and by extraoral Gothic arch tracing.
- **Transfer**: both records transferred to an articulator and the difference between the two values recorded.
- **Grouping**: patients divided into two groups according to the CR/HCG recording method used to achieve balanced occlusion.
- **Outcome**: subjective evaluation of the resulting dentures using the "Woelfel subjective evaluation criteria"; significance judged at P>0.05.
- **Not available in the abstract**: randomization/allocation, blinding, articulator type, named statistical tests, and follow-up duration.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Result (as reported in the abstract) |
|---|---|
| CR coincidence, wax vs Gothic | 7.14% of patients |
| Interocclusal-wax CR anterior to Gothic CR | 71.42% of patients |
| Interocclusal-wax CR posterior to Gothic CR | 21.43% of patients |
| Mean HCG, Gothic arch vs protrusive wax | Gothic higher on **both sides** in all subjects; difference not significant (P>0.05) |
| Woelfel subjective evaluation | No significant difference in any parameter, both groups |
| Overall HCG angle | Approximately similar by both methods |

- The three CR percentages sum to ≈100%: coincidence 7.14%, wax anterior 71.42%, wax posterior 21.43%.
- Geometric non-coincidence did **not** translate into a detectable subjective denture difference.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only — full text not retrieved.** The PMC record (PMC3416942) exists but contains no full-text body; the JATS artifact has only the abstract (body_len=0). All content here is transcribed from that abstract at abstract level — no fabrication of tables, test names, or numeric HCG values.
- No anatomic or imaging reference standard for CR/HCG is described in the abstract; "more posterior" is relative to the static wax record, not to an anatomic gold standard.
- The outcome is **subjective** (Woelfel criteria); the abstract reports no objective occlusal, radiographic, or long-term outcome and no follow-up duration.
- Sample is small (n=28) and single-centre context is implied but not stated; generalizability cannot be assessed from the abstract.
- The 7.14% coincidence rate means the two records are rarely geometrically interchangeable; clinical equivalence on a subjective scale should not be read as geometric agreement.

## 7. Glossary

- **Centric Relation (CR)**: a reproducible mandibular position recorded here by two competing methods; agreement between them is the paper's first outcome.
- **Horizontal Condylar Guidance (HCG)**: the horizontal/condylar determinants transferred to the articulator from the record; compared between Gothic tracing and protrusive wax.
- **Interocclusal wax record (static method)**: a wax registration of the jaw relation captured in a static closure.
- **Extraoral Gothic arch tracing (functional method)**: a graphic tracing whose arrowhead apex identifies the recorded centric position from a functional excursion.
- **Woelfel subjective evaluation criteria**: the subjective rating instrument used to compare the clinical response of dentures fabricated from each record.
- **Balanced occlusion**: bilateral simultaneous contact in centric and eccentric positions — the treatment goal for which the recorded CR/HCG values were used.
