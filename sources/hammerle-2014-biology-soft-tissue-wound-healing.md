## Why Ingested
구강상피 치유·가피형성(hemostasis → fibrin clot → re-epithelialization) 4단계 메커니즘의 컨센서스 기준문서가 필요했음. 기존 [[oral-surgery/walton-2026-beyond-re-epithelialization-prolonged-remodeling]] 은 재상피화 이후 단계를 다루나, 혈병(가피) 형성 → 상피화 완료 7–14일 타임라인의 canonical 근거가 부재했음.

## Three-line Summary

Consensus review from Group 1 of the 10th European Workshop on Periodontology (2014) synthesizing the biological stages of oral soft tissue wound healing at teeth, implants, and the edentulous ridge, with systematic appraisal of pre-clinical and human histological evidence on healing phases, regenerative adjuncts, and soft tissue substitutes.

The canonical 4-phase sequence (haemostasis → inflammation → proliferation → maturation) applies to both oral mucosal and skin wounds; surgical wound re-epithelialization completes within 7–14 days at teeth, while soft tissue maturation at implants requires 6–8 weeks; human histology consistently shows long junctional epithelial attachment rather than true new attachment after most regenerative procedures.

The fibrin clot formed in Phase 1 is the structural scaffold for all subsequent cellular events; this 7–14 day epithelial healing benchmark drives clinical SOP timing for suture removal, socket management, and implant loading protocols.

## 세줄요약

치아·임플란트·무치악 치조제에서의 구강 연조직 창상 치유 생물학을 정리한 제10차 유럽치주학술대회(European Workshop on Periodontology, EWP) 컨센서스(2014); 전임상·인체 조직학적 근거를 체계적으로 검토.

지혈→염증→증식→성숙의 4단계 순서가 구강 점막과 피부 창상에 공통 적용; 치아 부위 수술 창상의 재상피화(re-epithelialization)는 7–14일 완료, 임플란트 부위 연조직 성숙은 6–8주 소요; 인체 조직학 데이터는 대부분 긴 결합상피(long junctional epithelial attachment) 형성을 보임.

1단계에서 형성된 피브린 혈병(fibrin clot)이 이후 모든 세포 반응의 구조적 기질이며, 이 7–14일 상피화 기준이 봉합사 제거·소켓 관리·임플란트 부하 시기의 임상 SOP 근거.

## One-line Summary
Consensus review (10th EWP 2014): oral soft tissue healing follows 4 sequential phases (hemostasis, inflammation, proliferation, maturation), with epithelial healing of surgical wounds completing within 7–14 days.

## 한줄요약
10차 유럽치주학술대회(EWP) 컨센서스(2014): 구강 연조직 창상 치유는 지혈→염증→증식→성숙 4단계로 진행되며, 수술 창상의 상피화는 7–14일에 완료된다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Established that oral mucosal and skin wound healing share the same 4-phase sequence: haemostasis, inflammation, proliferation, maturation/matrix remodelling
- Quantified epithelial healing timeline: surgical wounds at teeth complete within **7–14 days**
- Identified that soft tissue healing at implants requires **6–8 weeks for maturation**, resulting in scar-like tissue
- Confirmed that the **soft connective tissue determines the phenotype of the overlying oral epithelium**
- Distinguished new attachment (pre-clinical positive) vs. long junctional epithelial attachment (human histological reality)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Consensus statement: literature search + expert panel review from 10th European Workshop on Periodontology
- Scope: wound healing at teeth, implants, and edentulous ridge; regenerative adjuncts (barriers, growth factors, soft tissue substitutes)
- Outcome categories: histological evidence (pre-clinical), clinical evidence (human RCTs)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Phase 1 — Haemostasis**: platelet aggregation, fibrin clot formation (the substrate for subsequent cellular events; = 가피 전구체)
- **Phase 2 — Inflammation**: neutrophil/macrophage recruitment, cytokine release, provisional matrix
- **Phase 3 — Proliferation**: fibroblast activation, angiogenesis, keratinocyte migration beneath fibrin scaffold
- **Phase 4 — Maturation/Remodelling**: collagen crosslinking, tissue remodelling
- Epithelial healing of surgical wounds at **teeth: 7–14 days**
- Soft tissue maturation at **implants: 6–8 weeks** (scar tissue phenotype)
- Human histological data consistently show **long junctional epithelial attachment**, NOT new attachment at teeth

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Consensus statement based on literature review; heterogeneous studies
- Human histological data on implant soft tissue healing "very scarce"
- Adjunct therapies (barriers, growth factors, substitutes) at "developing stage" as of 2014

## 6. Related Work
- Walton 2026: re-epithelialization completes day 14, but homeostasis requires 5–6 months
- Yang 2022: delayed wound healing in diabetes — same 4-phase context
- Hämmerle-derived teaching underlies most clinical SOP on socket management

## 7. Glossary
- **Haemostasis**: platelet plug + fibrin clot formation; the "가피(eschar)" precursor in oral wounds
- **Re-epithelialization**: keratinocyte migration from wound margins beneath fibrin matrix
- **Long junctional epithelial attachment**: apical migration of junctional epithelium along root surface; not true new attachment
- **Maturation**: collagen remodelling phase; continues for months after visible wound closure
