## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/scocca-2026-zygomatic-implants-head-neck-cancer-sr-ma]]가 종양 환자군에서의 관골 임플란트(Zygomatic Implant, ZI) 생존율·삶의질에 초점을 맞췄다면, 본 umbrella review(D'Ambrosio 2026)는 일반 중증 상악골 위축 환자에서 OST(Original Surgical Technique) vs ZAGA(Zygomatic Anatomy-Guided Approach) 술식별 합병증 스펙트럼을 11편의 systematic review를 종합해 정리하고, 나아가 medico-legal(의료법적) 위험까지 다룬 최초의 위키 페이지다. 부비동염(sinusitis)·연조직감염·이상감각(paresthesia)·구강상악동누공(OAF)·안와손상 등 합병증 발생률의 술식 간 비교 데이터와 사전동의(informed consent)·술자 숙련도의 법적 함의를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251239499) of 11 systematic reviews (2016–2024) on complications of zygomatic implants (ZIs), comparing the original surgical technique (OST) vs zygomatic anatomy-guided approach (ZAGA/AGA); no meta-analysis was performed due to substantial study heterogeneity (AMSTAR quality tool used).

Sinusitis was the most common complication across all included reviews (range ~2.4–27.2% depending on technique and study), followed by soft-tissue infection (~2–7.5%) and paresthesia (~0.55–10.78%); implant survival was consistently high (e.g., Chrcanovic 2016: 95.21% cumulative survival at 12 years over 4556 ZIs; Brennard-Roper 2023: 96.2% at 6 years), and ZAGA used immediate loading far more often than OST (89.6% vs 22.3%, Kammerer 2023).

High ZI survival does not equate to a complication-free procedure — complication rates may be systematically underestimated due to inconsistent reporting, and the medico-legal discussion stresses that ZI should be reserved for cases with no viable alternative, with thorough informed consent and reliance on surgeon expertise, given the potential severity (e.g., orbital penetration, diplopia) of some adverse events.

## 세줄요약

관골 임플란트(Zygomatic Implant, ZI) 합병증에 관한 11편의 체계적 문헌고찰(2016–2024)을 종합한 umbrella review(PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO 등록)로, 원래 술식(Original Surgical Technique, OST)과 관골 해부학 유도 접근법(Zygomatic Anatomy-Guided Approach, ZAGA/AGA)을 비교했으며 연구 간 이질성으로 메타분석은 시행하지 않았다(AMSTAR 도구로 질 평가).

전체 연구에서 가장 흔한 합병증은 부비동염(sinusitis, 술식·연구에 따라 약 2.4–27.2%)이었고, 연조직감염(약 2–7.5%)과 이상감각(paresthesia, 약 0.55–10.78%)이 뒤를 이었으며, 임플란트 생존율은 전반적으로 높았다(Chrcanovic 2016: 12년 누적생존율 95.21%, ZI 4556개; Brennard-Roper 2023: 6년 96.2%); ZAGA는 OST보다 즉시부하(immediate loading)를 훨씬 더 많이 사용했다(89.6% vs 22.3%, Kammerer 2023).

높은 생존율이 곧 합병증 없는 시술을 의미하지는 않으며, 보고 불일치로 합병증 발생률이 과소평가되었을 가능성이 있고, 의료법적(medico-legal) 논의에서는 다른 대안이 없는 경우에만 ZI를 선택하고 충분한 사전동의(informed consent)와 술자의 숙련도가 핵심임을 강조한다(안와손상·복시 등 중증 합병증 가능성 때문).

## 2. Key Contributions
- First umbrella review to synthesize the complication spectrum of zygomatic implants (ZIs) across 11 systematic reviews (2016–2024), comparing OST vs ZAGA/AGA techniques.
- Consolidates complication incidence ranges (sinusitis, soft-tissue infection, oroantral fistula, paresthesia, ocular/orbital complications, prosthetic complications) across heterogeneous source reviews into a single reference table.
- Adds a dedicated medico-legal discussion (informed consent, risk–benefit case selection, surgeon-experience liability) that is largely absent from prior ZI clinical-outcome reviews.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Umbrella review of systematic reviews (with and without meta-analysis), PRISMA 2020-compliant, PROSPERO-registered (CRD420251239499); PEO framework (Population–Exposure–Outcome).
- **Databases**: MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, BioMed Central, Cochrane Library, PROSPERO register; English-language only; last 10 years (search up to 1 June 2025).
- **n**: 11 included systematic reviews (spanning cohorts from 14 to 101 primary studies each; thousands of ZIs in aggregate).
- **Quality tool**: AMSTAR (Ottawa Hospital Research Institute).
- **Outcomes**: implant osseointegration, implant success/survival/loss, peri-implantitis, and surgical/biological/prosthetic complications (sinusitis, soft-tissue infection, oroantral fistula, paresthesia, hematoma, ocular/orbital injury, prosthetic complications).
- No meta-analysis was performed due to substantial heterogeneity across the 11 source reviews; data were synthesized narratively.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Survival**: Kammerer 2023 — OST 90.3–100%, ZAGA 90.4–100%; Chrcanovic 2016 — 95.21% cumulative survival rate at 12 years (4556 ZIs), most failures within 6 months; Brennard-Roper 2023 — 96.2% survival at 6 years, implant failure 0.7%/year.
- **Sinusitis** (most frequent complication overall, reported in every included review): Chrcanovic 2016 2.4%; Molinero-Mourelle 2016 3.9%; Kammerer 2023 9.53% (OST) vs 4.39% (ZAGA); Lan 2021 12%; Brennard-Roper 2023 2.8–36.4% depending on follow-up; Gabriele 2023 up to 20–34% with the Brånemark technique; a comparative study cited in Discussion found 27.2% (OST) vs 3.7% (ZAGA), a statistically significant difference favoring ZAGA.
- **Soft-tissue infection**: ~2.0–7.5% (Chrcanovic 2%, Kammerer OST 7.5%/ZAGA 4.35%, Molinero-Mourelle 4%).
- **Oroantral fistula (OAF)**: 0.4–13% depending on review/technique (Kammerer OST 4.58% vs ZAGA 1.71%; oroantral communication reported in 31% of one subset).
- **Paresthesia**: 0.55–10.78% (Kammerer: OST 10.78% vs ZAGA 6.91%; direct surgical complications 0.55% OST vs 1.60% ZAGA); typically self-limiting (1 week–3 months) but occasionally permanent.
- **Immediate loading prevalence**: 22.3% (OST) vs 89.6% (ZAGA), Kammerer 2023.
- **Ocular/orbital complications** (Weber 2024, most severe category): temporary infraorbital paresthesia (27 cases), intraoperative orbital penetration (10 cases), subconjunctival hemorrhage (5 cases); orbital penetration flagged as the single most severe adverse event.
- **Prosthetic complications**: reported in 56 (OST) and 104 (ZAGA) cases in Kammerer 2023; 4.9–5% in other reviews.
- **Peri-implant recession** at the ZAGA implant–abutment interface in 52.9% (9/17) of patients in one pooled subset.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Substantial heterogeneity in study design, sample size, follow-up duration, and surgical-procedure description across the 11 included reviews precluded meta-analysis and subgroup comparison.
- Overlapping primary studies across included systematic reviews (e.g., Moraschini 2023 and Brennard-Roper 2023) create citation/double-counting bias typical of umbrella reviews.
- Many source reviews were rated at high risk of bias; formal inter-reviewer reliability metrics (e.g., kappa) were not calculated for the umbrella review itself.
- English-language-only and no grey-literature/registry search beyond PROSPERO may have excluded relevant evidence.
- 10-year search window excludes older/obsolete ZI techniques, limiting historical completeness by design.
- Authors call for standardized reporting protocols and more RCTs to enable future low-risk-of-bias systematic reviews and true meta-analysis.

## 7. Glossary
- **ZI**: Zygomatic implant — extra-long titanium fixture anchored in the zygomatic bone, bypassing atrophic maxillary alveolar bone.
- **OST**: Original Surgical Technique — Brånemark's original ZI approach, large lateral sinus osteotomy with palatal implant emergence.
- **ZAGA / AGA**: Zygomatic Anatomy-Guided Approach — anatomy-adapted ZI placement classification introduced by Aparicio (2010).
- **AMSTAR**: A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews — quality/risk-of-bias appraisal tool for systematic reviews.
- **PEO**: Population–Exposure–Outcome — modified PICO framework used to frame the review question.
- **CSR**: Cumulative survival rate.
- **OAF**: Oroantral fistula — abnormal communication between the maxillary sinus and oral cavity.
- **ORIS criteria**: Aparicio's criteria for assessing maxillary sinus function/health around zygomatic implants.
