## Why Ingested
A single-center Saudi retrospective quantifying the *distribution* of implant removal reasons (no osseointegration 36.4%, no primary stability 22.4%) extends the wiki's primary-stability/failure picture with removal-frequency data (re-removal counts after prior surgery and re-implantation) — complementing [[wiki/implants/survival/cobo-vazquez-2018-effect-lack-primary-stability-survival]] on the survival side of the same question.

## Three-line Summary
Retrospective case-control study (King Saud University, Saudi Arabia; 132 failed implants removed 2017–2022) analyzing clinical, surgical, and radiographic records of implant removals to identify underlying causes of failure.
Lack of osseointegration (36.4%) and absence of primary stability (22.4%) were the predominant removal reasons; implant type (p=0.004) and restoration type (p=0.001) significantly predicted survival (overdenture 3.44×, crown 2.42× higher failure risk), while gender (p=0.001) and medical conditions/smoking (p=0.004) shaped specific failure mechanisms.
Clinical implication: patient-specific risk assessment, meticulous surgical technique for primary stability, and prosthetic planning are vital — and only 36.4% of failure cases attended regular maintenance.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Quantifies the distribution of reasons for implant *removal* in a Saudi university hospital cohort: lack of osseointegration 36.4%, absence of primary stability 22.4%, peri-implantitis 14.0%, iatrogenic/non-functional 17.7%, infection 7.5%, prosthetic trauma 1.9%.
- Cox regression identifies implant type (p=0.004) and restoration type (p=0.001) as the only significant survival predictors — overdentures 3.44× (p=0.027) and crowns 2.42× (p<0.001) higher failure risk; regular tissue-level trended better than tapered bone-level (Exp(B)=0.373, p=0.055).
- Gender-specific failure mechanisms: men had more peri-implantitis (11.2% vs 2.8%), women more iatrogenic failures (10.3% vs 3.7%).
- Removal-frequency data: prior surgical intervention (χ²=34.797, p=0.001) and re-implantation decision (χ²=28.252, p=0.005) significantly associated with how often implants were removed/replaced in the same patient.
- Poor maintenance adherence: only 36.4% of failure patients attended regular maintenance.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective case-control, single-center chart review (STROBE-guided; IRB King Saud Univ Medical City, Project E-21-5835, approved 2024-10-09)
- **Setting**: Graduate Dental Clinic, University Dental Hospital, data 2017–2022
- **n**: 132 removed implants (G*Power sample calc: alpha 0.05, effect 0.15, power 0.9 → ≥130)
- **Inclusion**: ≥1 implant removed for failure 2017–2022; complete medical/radiographic records; pre-removal radiographs or CT
- **Exclusion**: incomplete records, unclear/distorted radiographs
- **Variables**: patient (age, gender, systemic disease, periodontal disease, smoking), implant (site, length, width, restoration status), surgical/prosthetic (prior procedures — bone graft, sinus lift, immediate placement; reason for removal; maintenance attendance; re-implantation)
- **Data collection**: 3 calibrated operators, inter-examiner agreement >90%
- **Statistics**: descriptive; Shapiro–Wilk; Cox regression survival analysis; chi-square; SPSS 27; alpha 0.05; pairwise deletion for missing data

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Reasons for removal**: no osseointegration 39 (36.4%); no primary stability 24 (22.4%); peri-implantitis 15 (14.0%); iatrogenic 15 (14.0%); infection 8 (7.5%); non-functional 4 (3.7%); prosthetic trauma 2 (1.9%)
- **Baseline**: mean age 58±11 (32–78); male 52.3%; no medical condition/smoking 59.8%, medical condition 34.1%, smokers 6.1% (HTN 15.2%, controlled DM 9.8%, hypothyroidism 4.5%, osteoporosis 3.0%)
- **Periodontal disease**: yes 44.7%, no 47.0%, edentulous 8.3%
- **Sites**: lower molar 27.3% (most common), upper molar 18.9%, upper premolar 18.2%
- **Prior surgery**: none 60.3%, bone grafting 30.2%, sinus lift 4.8%, immediate implant 4.8%
- **Implant geometry**: length 10 mm 63.4%; width regular 46.6%, narrow 44.3%, wide 9.2%
- **Implant type**: regular bone-level 78.7%, regular tissue-level 18.1%, tapered bone-level 3.1%
- **Restoration**: 81.1% failed before restoration; among restored — crown 10.6%, FDP 6.06%, OD 2.3%
- **Cox regression (survival)**: implant type p=0.004; restoration type p=0.001 (overall model χ²=57.048, df=27, p=0.001, N=164); OD Exp(B)=3.44 (p=0.027); crown Exp(B)=2.42 (p<0.001); regular tissue-level Exp(B)=0.373 (p=0.055 trend vs tapered)
- **Failure-mechanism associations (chi-square)**: gender χ²=22.850 (p=0.001); medical condition+smoking χ²=29.311 (p=0.004); restoration status p=0.005; periodontal disease χ²=17.946 (p=0.117 NS); implant area χ²=43.723 (p=0.051 borderline)
- **Removal frequency (T1–T5)**: prior surgical intervention χ²=34.797 (p=0.001); re-implantation decision χ²=28.252 (p=0.005); medical+smoking χ²=14.431 (p=0.071); implant area χ²=28.987 (p=0.088)
- **Surgeon experience**: perio consultant 37.1%, OMFS consultant 25.0%, perio PG 28.0%, OMFS PG 9.8% (not a significant survival predictor, p=0.452)
- **Maintenance**: regular 36.4%, none 63.6% (no explicit statistical test vs failure)
- **Post-removal**: re-implanted 63.6%, declined 35.7%, re-implanted elsewhere 0.8%

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design — inherent recall bias and documentation inconsistency.
- Failed-removal sample only; no control group of successful implants (despite "case-control" label), and single-center — limits generalizability/firm risk estimation.
- Missing data handled by pairwise deletion → variable subsample sizes across analyses.
- Maintenance compliance was not statistically tested against failure.
- Future: prospective multi-center studies with matched controls; deeper local-anatomical analysis; standardized medical-condition reporting.

## 7. Glossary
- **PRIMARY STABILITY (일차안정성 / 초기고정)**: mechanical anchorage of the implant in bone at placement, before biological osseointegration.
- **OSSEOINTEGRATION (골유착)**: direct functional contact between living bone and the loaded implant surface.
- **PERI-IMPLANTITIS**: inflammatory, bone-loss-associated lesion around an existing implant.
- **IMMEDIATE IMPLANT PLACEMENT (즉시식립)**: implant placed in the socket at the time of extraction.
- **FDP (Fixed Dental Prosthesis) / OD (Overdenture)**: implant-supported fixed bridge vs removable overdenture prosthetics.
- **OMFS (Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery)**: surgical specialty; here the consultant/postgraduate surgeons placing implants.
