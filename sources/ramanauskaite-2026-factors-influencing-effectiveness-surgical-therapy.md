## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Periodontology 2000, 2026; three-database literature search, 2010 to November 2025; abstract-only ingest) on factors that shape the outcome of peri-implantitis surgical therapy across surgical technique, implant-site characteristics, and patient conditions.

Reconstructive adjuncts improve radiographic defect fill, with consistent benefit confined to contained 3- and 4-wall intrabony defects and slowly resorbing substitutes favored over autogenous bone; no clear benefit in resolving clinical inflammation, and flap design, healing mode, membranes, systemic antibiotics, and biologics remain undetermined.

Outcomes depend on surgeon experience, baseline severity (extensive bone loss, suppuration, deep probing depth, rough surfaces, post-operative bleeding), keratinized mucosa, and patient adherence to supportive care; as a narrative review it gives no pooled effect estimates, so it frames rather than quantifies the effects.

## 세줄요약

내러티브 리뷰(Periodontology 2000, 2026; 3개 DB 문헌 검색, 2010–2025년 11월; 초록 기반 인제스트)로, 임플란트주위염 외과치료 결과를 좌우하는 술식·임플란트 부위·환자 요인을 정리.

재건술 보조(Reconstructive measures)는 방사선학적 결손 충전(Radiographic defect fill)을 개선하지만 임상적 염증 해소에는 명확한 이득이 없다. 일관된 이득은 3·4벽 골내결손(Intrabony defect)에 국한되며, 자가골보다 서방출 골대체재(Slowly resorbing bone substitute)가 유리하다. 판막 설계, 치유 방식, 차단막, 전신 항생제, 생물학적 제제(Biologics)의 이득은 결론이 나지 않았다.

치료 성공은 술자 경험, 초기 질환 중증도(골소실·화농·깊은 탐침깊이·거친 임플란트 표면·술후 출혈 지속), 각화점막(Keratinized mucosa, KM) 존재, 지지요법(Supportive care) 순응도에 따라 달라진다. 정량 통합 추정치가 없는 서술형 리뷰이므로 방향성 틀로만 사용하고 수치는 인용하지 않는다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Organizes peri-implantitis surgical outcome factors into three domains: surgical technique, implant-site, and patient-related.
- Identifies the contained (3- and 4-wall) intrabony defect as the consistent indication where reconstructive therapy outperforms non-reconstructive therapy.
- Flags keratinized mucosa and macrophage polarization as candidate modifiers of reconstructive outcome.
- Concludes that surgeon experience and adherence to supportive care are the most consistently reported determinants.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Narrative review (no systematic protocol or pooled analysis reported in the abstract)
- **Databases**: Three electronic databases (names not stated in the abstract)
- **Window**: January 2010 to November 2025
- **n**: Not stated in the abstract
- **Outcomes**: Radiographic defect fill, soft-tissue level, clinical inflammation resolution, probing depth, post-operative bleeding, long-term stability

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Qualitative results only (no effect sizes in the abstract):

| Factor | Reported direction |
|---|---|
| Reconstructive adjunct | Improves radiographic defect fill; soft-tissue benefit possible; no clear benefit on clinical inflammation resolution |
| Defect morphology | 3- and 4-wall contained defects consistently favor reconstruction over 1- and 2-wall |
| Graft type | Slowly resorbing substitutes may outperform autogenous bone |
| Flap design, healing mode, membranes, systemic antibiotics, biologics | No definitive conclusion |
| Surgeon experience | Associated with treatment effectiveness |
| Initial severity | Extensive bone loss, suppuration, deep probing depth, rough surface, post-op bleeding compromise success |
| Keratinized mucosa | Presence enhances reconstructive outcome |
| Macrophage polarization | Emerging association with outcome (not established) |
| Supportive care adherence | Essential for maintaining surgical results |
| Periodontitis history, smoking, gender | Inconsistent findings |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Narrative design: no formal risk-of-bias assessment or pooled estimates are reported in the abstract.
- Abstract-only ingest: full text not retrieved, so study-level detail behind each statement is unverified here.
- Several conclusions are "no definitive conclusion" or "emerging evidence" (macrophage polarization), so they should not be read as established effects.

## 7. Glossary
- **Peri-implantitis (임플란트주위염)**: inflammatory disease of the tissues around an osseointegrated implant with progressive peri-implant bone loss.
- **Intrabony defect (골내결손)**: bone defect with 1 to 4 residual walls; contained (3- or 4-wall) defects retain more support.
- **Keratinized mucosa (각화점막, KM)**: attached keratinized peri-implant mucosa, associated here with better reconstructive outcomes.
- **Macrophage polarization (대식세포 편극화)**: shift between pro-inflammatory and pro-healing macrophage phenotypes, proposed as a modifier of healing.
- **Supportive care (지지요법)**: maintenance visits after surgery to sustain results.
