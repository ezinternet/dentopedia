## Three-line Summary

Single-center retrospective cohort at Ondokuz Mayis University (January 2011 to January 2024) analysing 490 eligible patients (age 21-90, mean 53.92 ± 13.82 years) out of 3720 treated, comparing risk factors for early implant failure (EIF) and late implant failure (LIF) in one experienced surgeon's cases.

For EIF, sex, smoking and implant length of 10 mm or less were identified as risk factors; for LIF, maxilla, length under 8 mm, SLA surface modification, sinus lift, smoking and short osseointegration duration were identified (the abstract gives no effect sizes, failure rates or time-window definitions).

The distinct EIF and LIF risk profiles support patient selection, surgical technique and implant choice as levers for survival; evidence is retrospective and based on the abstract only.

## 세줄요약

Ondokuz Mayis 대학 단일기관 후향적 코호트 (2011년 1월-2024년 1월)로, 3720명 중 선정기준을 충족한 490명 (21-90세, 평균 53.92 ± 13.82세)을 대상으로 한 명의 숙련된 술자의 증례에서 조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF)와 후기 임플란트 실패 (Late Implant Failure, LIF)의 위험요인을 비교.

EIF의 위험요인은 성별, 흡연, 임플란트 길이 10 mm 이하였고, LIF의 위험요인은 상악, 길이 8 mm 미만, SLA 표면 처리, 상악동 거상술, 흡연, 짧은 골유착 기간이었다 (초록에는 효과 크기·실패율·시기 정의가 없음).

EIF와 LIF의 위험 양상이 달라 환자 선택, 수술 술식, 임플란트 선택이 생존율 개선의 수단임을 시사; 후향적 연구이며 초록 기반 분석.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Separates EIF and LIF risk profiles within a single-surgeon cohort (January 2011 to January 2024).
- EIF risk factors: sex, smoking, implant length of 10 mm or less.
- LIF risk factors: maxilla, length under 8 mm, SLA surface, sinus lift, smoking, short osseointegration duration.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective cohort, single center, one experienced surgeon.
- **Population**: 490 patients included of 3720 treated; age 21-90, mean 53.92 ± 13.82 years.
- **Variables**: patient-related, implant-specific and surgical.
- Failure time windows, implant count and statistical methods not stated in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only; definitions and magnitudes cannot be verified.
- Retrospective single-center, single-surgeon cohort; only 490 of 3720 patients analysed.
- Different length cut-offs for EIF (10 mm or less) and LIF (under 8 mm).

## 7. Glossary

- **EIF (Early Implant Failure, 조기 임플란트 실패)**: failure before prosthetic loading (window not defined in the abstract).
- **LIF (Late Implant Failure, 후기 임플란트 실패)**: failure after osseointegration/loading.
- **SLA (sandblasted, large-grit, acid-etched)**: roughened titanium surface.
