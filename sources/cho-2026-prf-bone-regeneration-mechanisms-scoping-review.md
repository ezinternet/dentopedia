## Three-line Summary

Narrative-structured scoping review (Seoul National University, Journal of the Korean Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons 2026) synthesizing PRF (platelet-rich fibrin) biological mechanisms — fibrin matrix architecture, growth-factor release kinetics, and osteoimmunological/cytokine crosstalk — and their clinical applications in oral/maxillofacial bone regeneration (GBR, ARP, sinus lift, MRONJ/ORN, TMD).

PRF's 3D fibrin scaffold entraps platelets (~97% retention) and leukocytes (>50%), releasing >9,000 ng/mL of growth factors (PDGF-AA dominant) over 10 days with peak activity in the first postoperative week; mechanistically it couples angiogenesis-osteogenesis (VEGF/ERK/Notch), drives MSC osteogenic differentiation (BMP/SMAD, ERK/MAPK), inhibits osteoclastogenesis (↓NFATc1/TRAP/cathepsin K), and shifts macrophage polarization M1→M2 (↓IL-1β/NLRP3, ↑IL-4/IL-10/arginase-1/CD206).

Positions PRF as a biologically active adjunct (not a stand-alone structural graft) across GBR/ARP/sinus-lift/MRONJ-ORN/TMD applications; explicitly flags lack of standardized centrifugation reporting (RCF vs RPM) and heterogeneous protocols as the main barrier to translating mechanistic evidence into reproducible clinical outcomes — no formal systematic search protocol (PRISMA-ScR/databases/dates) was described despite the "scoping review" title.

## 세줄요약

서술형으로 구성된 스코핑 리뷰(서울대학교, 대한구강악안면외과학회지 2026) — 혈소판풍부피브린(Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)의 생물학적 기전(섬유소 매트릭스 구조·성장인자 방출동역학·골면역학/사이토카인 크로스토크)과 구강악안면 골재생 임상 적용(골유도재생술, 발치와보존, 상악동거상술, 약물관련턱뼈괴사/방사선골괴사, 턱관절장애)을 종합.

PRF의 3차원 섬유소 지지체는 혈소판(~97% 회수) 및 백혈구(>50%)를 포집해 10일간 9,000ng/mL 이상의 성장인자(혈소판유래성장인자-AA, PDGF-AA 우세)를 방출하며 술후 첫 주에 최고 활성; 기전적으로 혈관신생-골형성 결합(VEGF/ERK/Notch), 중간엽줄기세포(MSC) 골형성분화 촉진(BMP/SMAD, ERK/MAPK), 파골세포형성 억제(NFATc1/TRAP/cathepsin K↓), 대식세포 M1→M2 극성전환(IL-1β/NLRP3↓, IL-4/IL-10/arginase-1/CD206↑)을 매개.

PRF를 독립 구조 이식재가 아닌 생물학적 보조제(bio-active adjunct)로 자리매김 — GBR/ARP/상악동거상술/MRONJ-ORN/TMD 전반에 적용; 원심분리 프로토콜 표준화 부재(RPM vs 실제 상대원심력, RCF)와 이질적 프로토콜을 기전 근거의 재현 가능한 임상 결과 전환을 막는 핵심 장벽으로 지적; "스코핑 리뷰" 제목에도 불구하고 정식 체계적 검색 프로토콜(PRISMA-ScR/데이터베이스/기간)은 기술되지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates PRF preparation-generation history (PRP → PRF → variants H-/T-/A-/i-PRF/Ly-PRF → CGF) with the biological rationale for each evolutionary step.
- Details PRF's growth-factor release kinetics quantitatively (proteomics: >9,000 ng/mL over 10 days, PDGF-AA dominant, single TGF-β1 peak ~day 7 unlike PRP's bimodal pattern).
- Organizes the mechanistic literature into six discrete pathways: angiogenesis-osteogenesis coupling, MSC osteogenic differentiation, osteoblast function/ECM formation, osteoclastogenesis inhibition, fibroblast/soft-tissue regulation, and immunomodulation (osteoimmunology).
- Surveys six clinical application domains (GBR, ARP, sinus lift including membrane-perforation management, soft-tissue healing, MRONJ/ORN, TMD) and grounds each in the mechanistic sections above.
- Flags the field's core reproducibility problem: RPM-only reporting is insufficient because actual relative centrifugal force (RCF) depends on rotor radius/design/tube angulation; proposes the AR2T3 reporting framework (Herrera-Vizcaino) as a fix.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- PRF yield: from 10 mL whole blood → ~2–3 mL PRF, retaining ~97% of platelets and >50% of leukocytes.
- Growth factor release: >9,000 ng/mL total protein over 10 days; PDGF-AA most abundant, followed by TGF-β1, PDGF-BB, VEGF, EGF, IGF-1. Growth factors/MMPs peak within 6h then decline exponentially; cytokines peak 6–24h. TGF-β1 single peak ~day 7 (vs. PRP's bimodal release). EGF peaks ~7h; VEGF/TGF-β1/PDGF-BB/MMP-9 peak ~day 7, declining after 7–10 days.
- L-PRF membrane biomechanics: elastic modulus ≈0.07 MPa, ultimate tensile strength ≈0.29 MPa, elongation at break ≈2.78.
- Sinus lift: canine model DBBM+PRF osseointegration ≈43.5%, new bone ≈41.8% at 6 months vs. tissue-glue+DBBM ≈30–31%; clinical L-PRF+DBBM new bone ≈44.6% vs. 30.0%; PRF-alone transcrestal elevation with simultaneous implants: 95.65% 1-year survival, ≈6.72 mm mean lift; other RCTs report ≈7.67 mm vertical gain at 18 months.
- MRONJ RCT: L-PRF vs. surgery alone — no statistically significant additional benefit despite numerically higher 6-month complete resolution (92% [11/12] vs. 83% [15/18]).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No formal systematic search protocol described (databases/dates/inclusion criteria absent) — limits reproducibility of the review itself.
- Highly heterogeneous PRF preparation protocols across the cited primary literature (centrifugation force/rotor/tube variability) undermine cross-study comparability; RPM-only reporting is explicitly criticized as inadequate.
- Leukocyte and erythrocyte-derived component roles remain unresolved — excessive leukocyte-associated cytokines may impair osteoblast collagen I/ALP activity; erythrocyte-derived hemoglobin-rich lysates' role in osteoclastogenesis is still experimental.
- Conventional PRF membranes have rapid degradation and limited mechanical stiffness, restricting stand-alone barrier-membrane or volume-stable-graft use; Alb-PRF and cross-linking strategies are proposed but need further validation.
- Most clinical applications (MRONJ, ORN, TMD) rest on small sample sizes and heterogeneous protocols; high-level RCT evidence remains insufficient for several domains (ORN especially).
- Future directions: standardized reporting (AR2T3 framework), controlled biofunctionalization/drug-loading strategies, and integration with stem cells, exosomes, biomimetic scaffolds, and 3D biofabrication.

## 7. Glossary

- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, 혈소판풍부피브린)**: Second-generation autologous platelet concentrate, prepared without anticoagulants, forming a 3D fibrin matrix that entraps platelets, leukocytes, cytokines, and growth factors.
- **L-PRF / A-PRF / i-PRF / H-PRF / T-PRF / Ly-PRF**: PRF variants — leukocyte-PRF, advanced-PRF, injectable-PRF, horizontal-PRF, titanium-PRF, lyophilized (freeze-dried) PRF — each optimized for different handling, injectability, or storage properties.
- **CGF (Concentrated Growth Factor, 농축성장인자)**: Most recent platelet-derivative generation, produced via alternating acceleration/deceleration centrifugation cycles, yielding denser fibrin with higher tensile strength and cytokine content.
- **RCF (Relative Centrifugal Force) vs. RPM**: The actual g-force experienced by the sample, dependent on rotor radius/design/tube angle — the paper argues RPM alone is an inadequate protocol descriptor.
- **AR2T3**: Proposed standardized reporting acronym (Herrera-Vizcaino) for PRF centrifugation protocols (vertical and horizontal).
- **Osteoimmunology (골면역학)**: The field studying crosstalk between immune cells (macrophages, T cells) and bone cells (osteoblasts/osteoclasts) during bone remodeling and regeneration.
- **M1/M2 macrophage polarization**: M1 = pro-inflammatory phenotype; M2 = anti-inflammatory/pro-regenerative phenotype (markers: arginase-1, CD206). PRF drives an M1→M2 shift.
- **BMP/SMAD, ERK/MAPK, PI3K-Akt signaling**: Intracellular signaling cascades activated by PRF-derived growth factors that drive osteogenic gene expression (RUNX2, ALP, osteocalcin) in MSCs and osteoblasts.
- **OPG/RANKL ratio**: Osteoprotegerin/receptor activator of NF-κB ligand ratio; PRF increases this ratio, indirectly inhibiting osteoclastogenesis.
- **Alb-PRF**: Albumin gel–PRF mixture (heated serum albumin + liquid PRF) developed to prolong PRF's mechanical stability and resorption time.
- **AR2T3 / RPM / RCF / GARP mechanism**: See above; GARP = glycoprotein A repetitions predominant, implicated in iPRF-induced regulatory CD4+ T cell conversion.
