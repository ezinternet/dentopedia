## Why Ingested

Adds a large two-level (individual + implant) retrospective analysis of **early implant failure (EIF)** to `implants/survival`, extending the multivariable risk-factor picture already held in [[wiki/implants/survival/yari-2023-risk-factors-early-implant-failure]] and the older [[wiki/implants/survival/chrcanovic-2016-factors-influencing-early-dental-implant]]. Its unusual signal — **absence of postoperative antibiotic therapy** (OR ≈2.7 at both levels) and **postoperative complications** (OR 28.35) — is directly relevant to the prophylaxis debate drawn by [[wiki/drug/antibiotics/momand-2024-antibiotic-prophylaxis-early-implant-failure]] (which found *preoperative* prophylaxis barely moves EIF).

## Three-line Summary

abstract-only — full text not retrieved

Retrospective chart review of 594 individuals / 2,537 implants, EIF defined as loss before and up to abutment connection, analysed by logistic regression at both the individual and implant level (α = 5%).

EIF occurred in 144 implants (5.68%) and 97 individuals (16.3%); significant individual-level factors were smoking (OR 2.54), absence of postoperative antibiotic therapy (OR 2.73) and bone augmentation (OR 1.83), while implant-level factors added postoperative complications (OR 28.35), implant length ≤8.5 mm (OR 1.79) and diameter <3.75 mm (OR 1.65).

Age, sex, alcohol abuse, diabetes, hypertension and long-term medication use were unrelated to EIF (P ≥ .12) at both levels — the authors call for cohort studies to confirm the associations before they are treated as causal.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides an EIF risk-factor model estimated **at two levels simultaneously** (individual and implant), rather than collapsing to one unit of analysis.
- Uniquely flags **absence of postoperative antibiotic therapy** and **postoperative complications** as EIF-associated — the latter with a very large OR (28.35).
- Reports that the classic systemic factors (age, sex, diabetes, hypertension, alcohol, long-term medication) were **not** associated with EIF in this cohort — a negative result against which positive systemic signals elsewhere can be compared.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective study (clinical-chart review).
- **Sample**: 594 individuals with 2,537 dental implants.
- **EIF definition**: implant loss before and up to the abutment connection.
- **Analysis**: logistic regression to test association of EIF with local, environmental, and systemic factors at the individual and implant levels; significance level 5%.
- **Databases**: not applicable (single institutional chart series).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

EIF frequency: 144 implants (5.68%); 97 individuals (16.3%).

**Individual level (significant):**

| Factor | OR | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smoking habits | 2.54 | 1.00–6.47 | .05 |
| Absence of postoperative antibiotic therapy | 2.73 | 1.22–6.13 | .02 |
| Bone augmentation | 1.83 | 1.17–2.85 | .01 |

**Implant level (significant):**

| Factor | OR | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smoking habits | 2.90 | 1.60–5.26 | <.001 |
| Absence of postoperative antibiotic therapy | 2.77 | 1.36–5.63 | .005 |
| Postoperative complications | 28.35 | 6.79–118.45 | <.001 |
| Implant length ≤8.5 mm | 1.79 | 1.07–2.99 | .03 |
| Implant diameter <3.75 mm | 1.65 | 1.08–2.52 | .02 |

**Not associated (both levels):** age, sex, alcohol abuse, diabetes, hypertension, long-term medication use (P ≥ .12).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective chart design; confounding and indication bias possible (e.g., who received postoperative antibiotics).
- Abstract-only retrieval — regression tables, model adjustment sets, and exact covariate handling are not available.
- The authors themselves call for **future cohort studies** to confirm the identified risk factors.
- The enormous postoperative-complications OR (28.35, CI 6.79–118.45) is wide and likely to be a downstream/mediating variable rather than a clean predictor.

## 6. Related Work

- yari-2023: contemporary retrospective EIF cohort with multivariable predictors (immediate provisional, posterior maxilla, type IV bone, smoking) — same design, partly overlapping signals (smoking), different local/environmental set.
- chrcanovic-2016: specialist-clinic retrospective EIF study — earlier anchor for the EIF risk-factor literature.
- manzano-2016: SR+MA of early failure risk factors (smoking, short implants, maxilla) — pooled comparator.
- momand-2024: antibiotic prophylaxis SR+MA — this paper's "absence of postoperative antibiotic therapy" signal sits at the prevention axis, complementary in timing (post- vs preoperative).

## 7. Glossary

- **EIF (early implant failure)**: loss of the implant before and up to the abutment connection (before prosthetic loading).
- **OR (odds ratio)**: ratio of odds of EIF in an exposed vs unexposed group.
- **Bone augmentation**: grafting procedure at the implant site (an indicator of a compromised/atrophic recipient bed).
- **Postoperative complications**: adverse healing events after implant placement.
