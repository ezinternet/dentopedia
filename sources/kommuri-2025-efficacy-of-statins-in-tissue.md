## Why Ingested

First systematic review aggregating animal-model evidence on local statin application (simvastatin, fluvastatin) in tooth extraction sockets; bridges the gap between statin pharmacology and the [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/ridge-preservation]] evidence base by quantifying effects on residual ridge dimensions and osteogenic growth-factor mRNA in pre-clinical models.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 9 animal studies (403 animals: 8 rat, 1 mongrel dog) assessing local statin administration (simvastatin 5 studies; fluvastatin 4 studies) in tooth extraction sockets; PROSPERO-registered, SYRCLE risk-of-bias assessment, narrative synthesis (meta-analysis precluded by heterogeneity).
Local statins enhanced residual alveolar ridge height (relative height 0.960 vs 0.922 at 12 weeks in simvastatin-PLGA group), upregulated TGF-β1 mRNA (P<0.05) and BMP-2 mRNA (P<0.01) at 1–4 weeks, increased bone density and bone volume across delivery systems (PLGA scaffolds, hydrogel microspheres, injectable fluvastatin), and reduced MRONJ-like necrotic bone exposure; single local application sufficient across all 9 studies.
All evidence is preclinical (animal-only); allocation concealment was high risk in all 9 studies and detection blinding in 8/9, limiting translational confidence pending RCT data in humans.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **First SR on statins for extraction-socket healing**: aggregates 9 animal-model studies (403 animals) with PROSPERO registration and SYRCLE bias assessment.
2. **Three therapeutic domains identified**: (a) alveolar ridge preservation, (b) osseointegration enhancement around immediate implants, (c) prevention/treatment of MRONJ.
3. **Growth-factor mechanism quantified**: Liu 2009 (in situ hybridization) — simvastatin upregulates TGF-β1 (P<0.05) and BMP-2 (P<0.01) mRNA at 1, 2, 4 weeks; VEGF significantly increased at weeks 1–2.
4. **Delivery systems catalogued**: PLGA scaffold (simvastatin 1 mg), hydrogel microspheres (simvastatin 0.01 mg), injectable PLGA microspheres (fluvastatin 20–40 µg·kg⁻¹), direct socket packing, perisocket injection.
5. **MRONJ signal**: two fluvastatin studies (Adachi 2020, Sanda 2021) show reduced necrotic bone exposure and epithelial closure — suggests statin as prophylactic adjunct at bisphosphonate/denosumab-exposed extraction sites.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

### Study Selection
- 412 initial → 39 duplicates, 2 automation-flagged, 5 other removal → 366 screened → 9 included
- Inclusion: original animal studies with statin intervention and control; English language
- Exclusion: case reports/series, commentaries, letters, reviews, in-vivo/human studies

### Animal Models
- **Rat models (8/9)**: Wistar (Wu, Liu, Yasunami, Rakhmatia, Li, Adachi, Sanda) and Sprague-Dawley (Willett); age 4 weeks–12 months
- **Mongrel dogs (1/9)**: Mansour 2014 (split-mouth design, bilateral mandibular third premolars, 18–24 months)
- Study duration: 14–84 days

### Statin Types and Doses
| Drug | Studies | Dose range | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simvastatin | 5 | 0.01–2.2 mg | PLGA scaffold, granules, hydrogel microspheres |
| Fluvastatin | 4 | 0.1–10 mg/kg (or 20–40 µg·kg⁻¹ PLGA) | PLGA microspheres, hydroxyapatite carrier, perisocket injection |

### Outcome Measures
- Residual ridge height and width (DXA, micro-CT)
- mRNA expression of TGF-β1, BMP-2, VEGF (in situ hybridization)
- Bone density, bone mineral density, bone volume/tissue volume (BV/TV)
- New bone formation, osteoblast/osteoclast ratios (histology, histomorphometry)
- Necrotic bone ratio, epithelial continuity (MRONJ endpoints)
- Bone turnover markers

### Risk of Bias (SYRCLE)
- **Allocation concealment**: HIGH in all 9 studies
- **Detection blinding**: HIGH in 8/9 studies
- **Performance blinding**: HIGH in 7/9
- **Incomplete outcome data**: LOW in all 9
- **Selective reporting**: LOW in all 9
- Overall: high risk of bias across key domains; limits internal validity

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Residual Ridge Height
- Wu 2008 (simvastatin-PLGA, rats): relative ridge height at 12 weeks — Exp: 0.960±0.026 vs Control: 0.922±0.018 (significant at weeks 2, 4, 8, 12)
- BMD significantly higher in Exp at weeks 4, 8, 12
- Willett 2017 (BMM+simvastatin 0.2 mg, Sprague-Dawley): highest interproximal bone height preservation (P<0.01), increased ridge width, higher osteoblast and lower osteoclast percentage

### Growth Factor mRNA (Liu 2009, simvastatin 1 mg PLGA)
- TGF-β1 upregulated at weeks 1, 2, 4 (P<0.05)
- BMP-2 upregulated at weeks 1, 2, 4 (P<0.01)
- VEGF significantly increased at weeks 1, 2
- Expression in fusiform stromal cells of extraction socket

### Bone Density and Volume
- Yasunami 2015 (fluvastatin-PLGA 20 or 40 µg·kg⁻¹): significantly increased BD, bone density, bone volume on day 28; extensive connective tissue formation
- Rakhmatia 2018 (fluvastatin-HA carrier): COFS group had highest BMD; increased Tb.Th and Tb.Sp vs control
- Li 2019 (simvastatin hydrogel microspheres 0.01 mg): highest socket BD at week 8 by soft X-ray; consistent new bone increase weeks 1–8

### Osseointegration (Mansour 2014, simvastatin 2.2 mg, mongrel dogs)
- Larger areas of newly formed bone at implant–bone interface at 3 months
- Enhanced neovascularization; implant serration notches almost filled with new bone
- Numerous osteocytes and dense collagen bundles in experimental group

### MRONJ Prevention/Treatment
- Adachi 2020 (fluvastatin 0.1–10 mg/kg): FS-H group — significantly shorter necrotic bone exposure (P=0.041 vs MRONJ group); larger BV/TV; soft-tissue closure only in FS-H group
- Sanda 2021 (fluvastatin 1–10 mg/kg): reduced necrotic bone ratio; complete epithelial recovery in both FS-L and FS-H groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

1. **Heterogeneity precludes meta-analysis**: varied animal models, statin types, doses, delivery systems, follow-up durations
2. **Growing rat models**: most studies used young (4–8 week) rats still in active growth — not representative of adult human extraction sockets
3. **High bias risk**: allocation concealment and detection blinding HIGH across nearly all studies
4. **No human data**: all 9 studies are animal models; clinical RCTs required
5. **Dose optimization unknown**: simvastatin 0.01–2.2 mg, fluvastatin 0.1–10 mg/kg — optimal dosing, delivery method, and frequency unestablished
6. **Long-term safety**: no data on long-term local or systemic effects in oral tissues

**Authors' recommendations**: Clinical trials assessing wound closure, bone regeneration, postoperative complications; dose-finding studies; long-term follow-up.

## 6. Related Work

- Statins' osteoinductive mechanism via HMG-CoA reductase inhibition → BMP-2 upregulation: Mundy 1999 (seminal)
- Peri-implant statin use (referenced in introduction, refs 14–16)
- MRONJ statin prophylaxis: Adachi 2020, Sanda 2021 (within this review); atorvastatin MRONJ (ref 30)
- Clinical studies suggesting statin benefit in extraction healing (refs 27–29, cited but not reviewed here)

## 7. Glossary

- **HMG-CoA reductase (스타틴 표적 효소, HMG-CoA 환원효소)**: 3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl-coenzyme A reductase; rate-limiting enzyme in cholesterol biosynthesis; inhibited by statins
- **TGF-β1 (형질전환 성장인자, TGF-β1)**: transforming growth factor-beta 1; promotes collagen synthesis and wound healing
- **BMP-2 (골형성단백질-2, BMP-2)**: bone morphogenetic protein-2; key osteoinductive growth factor upregulated by statins
- **VEGF (혈관내피세포 성장인자, VEGF)**: vascular endothelial growth factor; promotes angiogenesis; upregulated by statins
- **PLGA (폴리락트산-코-글리콜산, PLGA)**: poly(lactic-co-glycolic acid); biodegradable scaffold/microsphere carrier for sustained drug release
- **MRONJ (약물유발 악골괴사, MRONJ)**: medication-related osteonecrosis of the jaw; associated with bisphosphonates and denosumab
- **SYRCLE**: Systematic Review Centre for Laboratory Animal Experimentation; risk-of-bias tool adapted from Cochrane for animal studies
- **DXA (이중에너지 X선 흡수계측법, DXA)**: dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry; measures bone mineral density
- **BV/TV (골부피/조직부피, BV/TV)**: bone volume/tissue volume ratio; micro-CT metric of trabecular bone density
- **Tb.Th / Tb.Sp**: trabecular thickness / trabecular separation; micro-CT trabecular microarchitecture parameters
