## Why Ingested

Newest narrative review that treats progressive loading as one of four protocols (alongside immediate, early, delayed) and ties selection to bone density, insertion torque and ISQ. Complements [[implants/loading-protocol/devi-2026-immediate-vs-delayed-implant-loading]] (immediate vs delayed only) and gives the wiki a stated decision framework for where progressive loading is proposed (type III–IV bone, grafted sites). Its progressive-loading section is thin, so it is read here as a secondary framing source, not as primary evidence.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (not PRISMA, not registered) of 2019–2025 literature on immediate, early, progressive and delayed loading: 512 records screened, 72 studies analyzed (24 RCTs, 28 cohort studies, 20 SR/MA); by protocol 26 immediate, 18 early, 8 progressive, 20 delayed.

With insertion torque ≥ 35 N·cm (ISQ ≥ 70, type I–II bone) immediate and early loading showed 95–100% survival comparable to delayed loading, and the mean marginal bone loss difference between early and late loading was < 0.2 mm at 12 months; progressive loading is positioned as a bio-prudent option for low-density (type III–IV, posterior maxillary, grafted) bone, with the drawback of many prosthodontic visits.

The review's progressive-loading content is conceptual and thinly supported (no outcome numbers specific to the 8 progressive studies), so the thresholds are useful for orientation but should not be treated as pooled estimates.

## 세줄요약

2019–2025년 문헌을 대상으로 즉시·조기·점진적·지연 부하를 다룬 서술적 종설(PRISMA 아님, 등록 없음): 512건 검토 후 72편 분석(RCT 24, 코호트 28, SR/MA 20), 프로토콜별로는 즉시 26·조기 18·점진적 8·지연 20편.

삽입 토크 ≥ 35 N·cm(ISQ ≥ 70, I–II형 골)에서 즉시·조기 부하의 생존율은 95–100%로 지연 부하와 비슷했고, 조기 대 지연 부하의 평균 변연골 소실 차이는 12개월에 0.2 mm 미만; 점진적 부하는 저밀도(III–IV형, 상악 구치부, 이식골) 골에서 쓰는 보수적 선택지이며 보철 내원이 많다는 단점이 언급된다.

점진적 부하 부분은 개념 설명 위주로 근거가 얇고 8편에 대한 구체 수치가 없어, 제시된 기준은 방향 참고용이지 합산 추정치가 아니다.

## 1. Document Information

- Saudi Dental Journal 2026;38(4); PMID 41963615; PMCID PMC13069059; published 2026-04-11.
- Design: narrative review; searched PubMed, Scopus, ScienceDirect, Wiley, Google Scholar, January 2019 to October 2025.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Four-protocol classification with timing definitions: delayed (> 3 months), early (2 weeks–3 months), immediate (≤ 48 h), progressive (staged occlusal contact).
- Stability-based assignment table: immediate ≥ 35–45 N·cm / ISQ ≥ 70; early 25–35 N·cm / ISQ 60–69; delayed < 25 N·cm / ISQ < 60.
- Decision algorithm combining bone density (Lekholm–Zarb D1–D4), torque/ISQ, occlusal risk, prosthesis type and patient compliance.
- Knowledge-gap list: follow-up mostly < 5 years, no standardized success criteria, systemic conditions under-represented.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 512 records → 72 included after screening for loading as the main topic; animal-only, non-clinical and unrelated papers excluded.
- Paper states both that no inclusion/exclusion criteria were used (narrative approach) and that exclusions were applied.
- Studies grouped by protocol and interpreted by bone density, torque/ISQ, jaw, arch type and biomechanical risk factors.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Insertion torque ≥ 35 N·cm: survival 95–100% for immediate/early loading, comparable to delayed.
- Mean marginal bone loss difference early vs late loading: < 0.2 mm at 12 months.
- Cited survival: mandible about 98–99%, maxilla about 95–97% with immediate loading.
- Micromotion threshold of roughly 100–150 µm is used as the biomechanical limit.
- Failures associated with excess occlusal load, type IV bone, and absence of splinting.
- Progressive loading (section "Progressive loading – concept and protocol"): Misch-type staged system with multiple temporary prostheses; interval depends on bone type; intended for the posterior maxilla; disadvantage is a long prosthodontic phase with multiple appointments. Table: for D3–D4 bone and long cantilever (> 10 mm) risk, opt for progressive or delayed loading.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative design, no risk-of-bias assessment, no pooled analysis; selection by clinical relevance.
- Progressive-loading text contains a garbled sentence ("settings which mimic a natural environment are the best for furniture production") and a stray quoted diet paragraph, indicating editing problems; the "10% decrease in ultimate shear load" with transverse loading is cited to a secondary source and is unclear in context.
- Thresholds (torque/ISQ cut-offs by protocol) are presented as review conventions rather than outcomes tested against each other.
- Authors list as gaps: long-term (> 10-year) data, standardized success criteria, systemic disease populations, digital/AI proof of concept.

## 7. Glossary

- Progressive loading: gradual functionalization through staged occlusal contact.
- ISQ: implant stability quotient (resonance frequency analysis).
- Lekholm–Zarb D1–D4: bone density classification used for protocol selection.
