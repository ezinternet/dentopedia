## Why Ingested

GBT 근거는 대부분 기구(instrumentation) 단계에 몰려 있고, [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/cyris-2024-guided-biofilm-therapy-versus-conventional]]은 GBT와 SRP의 NSPT 동등성을 보였지만 GBT 개념을 환자 구강위생지도(OHG)·자가관리로 확장한 연구는 드물다. 본 후향 코호트(Lei 2025)는 치태 착색제를 매개로 한 GBT 기반 OHG가 치태지수·탐침시출혈·치주낭깊이와 자가효능(SESS)을 개선한다고 보고해, [[overviews/professional-biofilm-management-gbt-air-polishing-overview]]가 정리한 "공시제 기반 동기부여" 축에 환자 교육·자가관리 임상 근거를 보탠다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (as titled; the Methods section confusingly states "case-control studies of reporting guidelines have been used"), n=220 enrolled / 205 analyzed (103 control vs 102 observation) patients with chronic periodontitis at Xi'an Jiaotong University, comparing GBT-concept-based oral hygiene guidance against traditional chair-side oral health education over 3 months.

At 3 months the GBT-based OHG group had significantly lower plaque index (31.80±14.06 vs 44.27±15.74, p<0.001), bleeding on probing (23.39±18.53 vs 28.76±16.12, p<0.001), and PD≥4 mm sites (19.18±16.56 vs 26.12±14.91, p=0.002), plus higher self-care efficacy (all three SESS dimensions, p<0.001) and better self-reported oral hygiene behaviors.

Suggests plaque-disclosure-guided GBT oral-hygiene instruction improves plaque control and self-management in chronic periodontitis, but the mislabeled design, single-center retrospective nature, and inconsistent sample sizes/statistics limit causal inference.

## 세줄요약

만성 치주염 환자 220명(등록; 15명 탈락 → 최종 103명 대조군 vs 102명 관찰군)을 3개월간 전통적 구강위생교육과 가이드 바이오필름 치료(Guided Biofilm Therapy, GBT) 개념 기반 구강위생지도(oral hygiene guidance, OHG)로 나눈 후향 코호트 연구(단 논문 2장은 스스로 "case-control reporting guidelines"라 서술해 설계 표기가 불일치).

3개월 후 관찰군이 대조군보다 치태지수(Plaque Index, PI) 31.80±14.06 vs 44.27±15.74(p<0.001), 탐침시출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP) 23.39 vs 28.76(p<0.001), 치주낭깊이≥4 mm(PD≥4) 19.18 vs 26.12(p=0.002)로 유의하게 낮았고, 자가효능척도(Self-Efficacy Scale for Self-care, SESS) 3개 영역 모두 대조군보다 유의하게 높았다(p<0.001).

치태 착색제 기반 GBT 구강위생지도가 치태조절과 자가관리를 개선함을 시사하나, 설계 오표기·단일기관 후향·불일치한 표본수와 통계로 인과 해석에는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Applies the GBT concept to chair-side **oral hygiene guidance** (step 2 of GBT): plaque disclosing agent + plaque control record card + patient self-inspection in the mirror, an explicitly patient-facing extension of the GBT protocol.
- Reports that GBT-based OHG reduced PI, BOP, and PD≥4 and raised self-care efficacy (SESS) more than traditional chair-side education at 3 months.
- Documents behavioral shifts toward proactive self-care (brushing frequency, brushing duration, floss/interdental-brush use, correct brushing method) in the GBT group.
- Adds a structured continuity-of-care component (WeChat knowledge/video push every 2 weeks + monthly telephone follow-up for 3 months).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: titled a **retrospective cohort**; however, §2 states "The case-control studies of reporting guidelines have been used." (internally inconsistent — no reporting guideline is actually named).
- **Setting**: single center (Xi'an Jiaotong University), patients selected October 2020 – October 2022.
- **n**: 220 enrolled; 15 lost to follow-up; 103 control group (CG) + 102 observation group (OG) analyzed. The SESS table (Table 3) instead lists OG n=100.
- **Groups**: CG = traditional chair-side oral health education (brushing/floss interdental-brush demonstration on a model). OG = GBT-concept OHG (plaque disclosure → plaque control record card → supervised on-site brushing → proximal cleaning instruction → home plaque-staining routine → WeChat/telephone follow-up, 3 months).
- **Intervention frame**: §2.2 states "patients in both groups followed the 8-step treatment process of GBT" (probing → plaque disclosure → OHG → supragingival air polishing → subgingival air polishing → painless ultrasonic scaling → evaluation of scaling effectiveness → scheduling), with the two groups differing only in the step-3 education method — but the abstract/§2.1 describe CG as receiving "traditional interventions."
- **Inclusion**: diagnosed CP, ≥15 teeth (excluding implants/crowns/dentures), after basic periodontal treatment, willing to receive OHG and follow-up. **Exclusion**: age <18, cognitive/communication impairment, severe gingival hyperplasia, pregnancy/lactation, severe hand disease.
- **Outcomes**: PI (O'Leary Plaque Control Record), BOP%, PD≥4 mm%, SESS (Kakudate; 3 dimensions, 15 items, Likert 5-point, total 15–75), and a self-designed oral-hygiene-behavior questionnaire (Cronbach α = 0.915).
- **Statistics**: SPSS 20.0; categorical data as frequency/percentage; independent-sample t tests and χ² tests; P<0.05 considered significant. No power calculation, no confounder adjustment, no effect sizes or confidence intervals reported.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Baseline comparability.** No statistically significant differences between groups in gender, age, educational level, occupation, or income (P>.05); baseline PI, BOP, PD≥4, and all SESS dimensions were also not significantly different (P>.05).

**Oral hygiene behavior after 3 months (Table 2)** — OG superior to CG on every item:

| Item | CG (n=103) | OG (n=102) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brushing ≥3×/day | 25 (24.27%) | 41 (40.20%) | .038 |
| Brushing duration ≥3 min | 34 (45.45%) | 95 (93.14%) | <.001 |
| Daily floss / interdental brush | 49 (47.57%) | 74 (72.55%) | <.001 |
| Correct brushing method | 34 (33.01%) | 84 (86.27%) | <.001 |

**Self-care efficacy (SESS, Table 3, 3 months)** — all three dimensions significantly higher in OG (P<.001); baseline not different (P=.917 / .807 / .873):

| Dimension | CG (n=103) | OG (n=100) | t | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SE-DC (dentist consultation) | 14.47±4.17 | 23.25±14.47 | 19.670 | <.001 |
| SE-B (brushing) | 13.33±5.26 | 23.76±1.97 | 18.794 | <.001 |
| SE-DH (dietary habits) | 14.56±3.78 | 23.54±1.37 | 22.548 | <.001 |

**Clinical indicators after 3 months (Table 4)** — baseline not different (P>.05); post-intervention OG significantly lower on all three:

| Indicator | CG (n=103) | OG (n=102) | t | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PI (%) | 44.27±15.74 | 31.80±14.06 | −5.980 | <.001 |
| BOP (%) | 28.76±16.12 | 23.39±18.53 | −10.001 | <.001 |
| PD≥4 mm (%) | 26.12±14.91 | 19.18±16.56 | −3.157 | .002 |

**Within-group plaque detection.** §3.2 states biofilm removal in OG "was significantly improved (Fig. 3)" — reported only as a figure, with no numeric values or p-value in the text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Design mislabel (flagged, not corrected).** The title/abstract call this a retrospective cohort, but the Methods section states "The case-control studies of reporting guidelines have been used." A case-control design cannot support the pre/post intervention and self-efficacy conclusions reported here; the intended design (and any reporting guideline) is not clarified.
- **Control-arm ambiguity.** §2.2 says both groups followed the 8-step GBT process while §2.1/abstract describe CG as receiving "traditional interventions" — the instrumentation actually delivered to CG is described inconsistently.
- **Inconsistent sample size.** OG is n=102 in Tables 2 and 4 but n=100 in the SESS table (Table 3).
- **Missing / suspect statistics.** The within-OG plaque-detection improvement (§3.2) has no numeric values or p-value (figure only); baseline demographic comparisons are reported only as "P>.05" without exact values; no effect sizes, confidence intervals, power calculation, or multiplicity adjustment are given. Exact p-values are not reported for every comparison.
- **Possible data error.** OG SE-B post-intervention is reported as 23.25±14.47 — the SD (14.47) is identical to the CG SE-DC post value and implausibly large versus OG SE-B 23.76±1.97 in the same table.
- **Observational, single-center, 3-month follow-up.** No randomization, no blinding, no confounder adjustment; the OG's additional WeChat/telephone attention could inflate self-reported behavior and self-efficacy (attention/Hawthorne effect).
- **Scope caveat.** Because both arms are described as receiving GBT instrumentation, the contrast isolates the *education/plaque-disclosure* component, not GBT instrumentation efficacy.
- **Future work.** Prospective randomized design with allocation concealment, blinded outcome assessment, standardized SESS/PI/BOP reporting with exact statistics, longer follow-up, and multi-center samples; report per-protocol vs ITT counts.

## 6. Related Work

- **Cyris 2024** ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/cyris-2024-guided-biofilm-therapy-versus-conventional]]): split-mouth RCT, GBT vs conventional SRP clinically equivalent in NSPT with a treatment-time advantage; the present study extends the GBT question from instrumentation to oral-hygiene guidance/self-care.
- **Yein 2026** ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/yein-2026-guided-biofilm-therapy-periodontal-efficacy-sr]]): PRISMA SR (5 RCTs, n=231) concluding no consistent clinical superiority of GBT over SRD on PPD/CAL/BOP — a different (education) contrast, so not directly comparable, but relevant context.
- **Dönertaş 2026** ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/donertas-2026-gbt-subgingival-debridement-gcf-biomarkers]]): RCT finding GBT lowered PI/GI/GCF IL-1β more than curette+ultrasonic — consistent direction on plaque/inflammation.
- **Sooampon 2026** ([[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/sooampon-2026-dc-red-33-erythrosine-plaque-disclosure-rct]]): RCT on plaque-disclosing agents (erythrosine vs D&C Red No. 33); the present study uses a disclosing agent as the visual feedback/education tool.

## 7. Glossary

- **GBT (Guided Biofilm Therapy)**: a systematic, staged professional biofilm-management protocol using biofilm disclosure, air polishing, ultrasonic scaling, and patient-specific oral hygiene instruction.
- **CG / OG**: control group (traditional intervention) / observation group (GBT-based OHG).
- **PI (Plaque Index)**: O'Leary Plaque Control Record — % of tooth surfaces stained with plaque.
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing)**: % of probed sites that bleed on probing.
- **PD≥4 (PD4)**: % of sites with probing depth ≥4 mm.
- **SESS (Self-Efficacy Scale for Self-care)**: Kakudate instrument; 3 dimensions (SE-DC dentist consultation, SE-B brushing, SE-DH dietary habits), 15 items, total 15–75.
- **OHG (Oral Hygiene Guidance)**: chair-side instruction in oral hygiene methods.
- **NSPT / SRP / SRD**: non-surgical periodontal therapy / scaling and root planing / scaling and root debridement (context terms).
