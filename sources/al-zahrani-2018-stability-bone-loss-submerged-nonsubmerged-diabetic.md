## Three-line Summary

Seven-year clinical trial (n=35 patients, 118 implants) comparing submerged vs non-submerged healing protocols in type 2 diabetic (T2DM) and non-diabetic patients, measuring RFA stability and peri-implant bone loss (PIBL).

RFA-ISQ did not differ between protocols at baseline or 3 months in either group; non-submerged implants had significantly higher PIBL specifically in T2DM patients at 2, 3 and 7 years (p<0.05), while submerged implants showed a less pronounced diabetes effect.

The non-submerged healing protocol should be used with caution in T2DM patients given its association with greater longitudinal peri-implant bone loss independent of initial stability.

## 세줄요약

제2형 당뇨(T2DM)·비당뇨 환자(n=35, 118개 임플란트)에서 매몰/비매몰 치유 프로토콜의 공명주파수분석(RFA) 안정성과 치주염 관련 골 소실(PIBL)을 7년간 비교했다.

RFA-ISQ는 기저치·3개월에서 두 군 간 유의한 차이가 없었으나, 비매몰 임플란트는 T2DM 환자에서만 2·3·7년 모두 유의하게 높은 PIBL을 보였다(p<0.05).

비매몰 치유는 T2DM 환자에서 장기 치주염 관련 골 소실 증가와 관련되므로 임상적 주의가 필요하다.

## Why Ingested
7년 추적 임상시험으로 submerged vs non-submerged 비교를 당뇨 여부로 층화 — 당뇨 환자에서 비매몰형이 더 많은 변연골 소실을 보인다는 unique subgroup 근거. [[implants/mbl/aal-2025-marginal-bone-loss-single]]·[[implants/survival/troiano-2018-early-late-failure-submerged]]의 전반적 "차이 없음" 결론을 당뇨 환자에서 반증하는 중요한 예외 데이터.

## One-line Summary
7-year clinical trial (n=35 patients, 118 implants, submerged vs non-submerged) found that non-submerged implants had significantly higher peri-implant bone loss specifically in type 2 diabetic patients at 2, 3, and 7 years, while RFA (stability) did not differ between protocols at baseline or 3 months.

## 한줄요약
7년 임상시험 (n=35명/118 임플란트) — 제2형 당뇨환자에서 비매몰형 임플란트의 임플란트주위골소실 (Peri-Implant Bone Loss, PIBL)이 2·3·7년 시점에 유의하게 높았으나, 공명주파수분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) 안정성 지수(ISQ)는 두 프로토콜 간 차이 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Only paper in wiki comparing submerged vs non-submerged in a diabetic vs non-diabetic stratified cohort over 7 years
- Non-submerged significantly worse MBL in T2DM at all time points (2, 3, 7 years)
- No significant protocol effect on MBL in non-diabetic patients (inter-group NS)
- ISQ (Osstell RFA) correlated within groups over 3-month healing but not between protocols — stability trajectory similar regardless of healing mode
- T2DM itself independently associated with significantly higher PIBL vs NT2DM at all follow-ups

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Prospective clinical trial
- n: 35 T2DM and non-diabetic (NT2DM) patients; 118 implants (mean height 10–12 mm, diameter 3.3–4.1 mm)
- Groups: Submerged vs non-submerged × T2DM vs NT2DM (2×2 design implied)
- RFA: Osstell (ISQ) at fixture placement and 3 months
- Bone loss: Digital radiographs, 2/3/7-year time points
- Statistics: p < 0.05 threshold; inter-group and intra-group RFA comparisons

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- ISQ at baseline vs 3 months: significant change within T2DM group (p=0.008)
- Inter-group ISQ at baseline and 3 months: NS (p>0.05)
- PIBL: T2DM > NT2DM at each follow-up (p<0.05) — diabetes effect independent of protocol
- Non-submerged implants: significantly higher PIBL in T2DM vs NT2DM at 2, 3, and 7 years (p<0.05)
- Submerged implants: PIBL difference between T2DM/NT2DM not separately reported but overall T2DM effect confirmed

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- n=35 patients (118 implants) — small sample, possible confounding
- Abstract only; per-protocol MBL numbers not extractable
- T2DM control level (HbA1c) not clearly reported in abstract — unclear whether well-controlled or poorly-controlled T2DM
- No survival rate data reported in abstract
- Single center, Saudi Arabia — population-specific factors possible

## 6. Related Work
- Troiano 2018 SR: general early-failure +2% penalty on non-submerged, no DM stratification
- Aal 2025 RCT: no DM stratification; overall NS protocol effect
- Meza-Mauricio 2019 / Wagner 2022 / Al-Ansari 2022: diabetes-implant failure SRs (already in wiki) — provide background diabetes risk
