## Three-line Summary
Cross-sectional reliability study (85 implants, 23 patients measured by 2 operators in 4 directions): Osstell Mentor resonance frequency (RFA) device for implant stability quotient (ISQ) measurement.

Osstell Mentor demonstrated excellent intraclass correlation (ICC=0.97), indicating high reliability between repeated ISQ measurements; SmartPeg types I and II showed equivalent ISQ values (no significant difference).

Single RFA measurement is statistically sufficient to represent implant stability at placement, eliminating the need for repeated measurements or averaging — clinical implication: simplified operative workflow with maintained diagnostic accuracy.

## 세줄요약
신뢰도 및 재현성 연구: 85개 임플란트, 23명 환자, 2명 시술자가 4방향(근심·원심·협측·설측)으로 측정한 Osstell Mentor 공진주파수(RFA) 기반 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ) 장비 평가.

Osstell Mentor는 재측정 간 탁월한 급내 상관계수(ICC=0.97)를 입증; SmartPeg type I과 type II는 ISQ 값의 동등성을 나타냈고(유의하지 않음), 평균 ISQ는 72.4였으며 측정 방향에 따른 각도 변화는 임상적으로 유의하지 않음.

단일 RFA 측정이 식립 시점에서 임플란트 안정성을 통계적으로 대표할 수 있음 — Osstell ISQ는 높은 신뢰도의 표준화 도구이며, SmartPeg 타입 간 호환성으로 임상 수술실(operative) 효율성 향상.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Medicina Oral Patologia Oral y Cirugia Bucal 18(6):e877-882 (2013)
- DOI: 10.4317/medoral.19120
- Design: Cross-sectional reliability/repeatability study, 85 implants, 23 patients

## 2. Key Contributions
- High ICC (0.97) confirms Osstell ISQ is highly reliable between measurements
- Single RFA measurement is statistically sufficient (no need for repeat averaging)
- SmartPeg types I and II produce equivalent ISQ values

## 3. Methodology
- 85 implants in 23 patients, measured by 2 operators
- Measurements: mesial, distal, buccal, lingual directions
- SmartPeg type I and type II compared
- Statistical: ICC (intraclass correlation coefficient), Bland-Altman analysis

## 4. Key Results
- ICC = 0.97 (excellent reliability)
- Mean ISQ across all implants: 72.4
- SmartPeg I vs II: no significant difference (NS)
- Measurement direction: some angular variation, but clinically non-significant
- One measurement = representative of implant stability

## 5. Limitations
- Cross-sectional (single timepoint per implant — no longitudinal tracking)
- No ISQ threshold testing (survival outcome not tracked)
- Single device brand (Osstell Mentor)

## 6. Related Work
- Kästel 2019: SmartPeg tightening torque variation does not affect ISQ
