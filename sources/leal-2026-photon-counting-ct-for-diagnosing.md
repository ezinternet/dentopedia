## Why Ingested

The wiki's vertical root fracture (VRF) imaging pages are all about CBCT, and the pooled estimate that metal posts lower CBCT sensitivity ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/de-2025-exploring-diagnostic-reliability-of-cbct]]) averages over many devices and says device choice gave inconsistent results. This ex vivo study adds the first photon-counting CT (PCCT) data in the wiki and shows, within one phantom setup, that the metal-post penalty depends strongly on the scanner, which qualifies that pooled result; it also bears on the low-sensitivity picture in [[wiki/cracked-tooth/habibzadeh-2023-diagnostic-efficacy-of-cone-beam]] and the guideline stance in [[wiki/cracked-tooth/kim-2024-developing-evidence-based-clinical-imaging]].

## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo diagnostic-accuracy study: 18 endodontically treated single-rooted human teeth (8 with induced vertical root fracture, 10 intact) placed in an anthropomorphic phantom and scanned without a post, with a Ni-Cr post and with a Co-Cr post on one photon-counting CT (NAEOTOM Alpha) and four CBCT devices (270 scans), read blind by 5 oral and maxillofacial radiologists.

NewTom VGi evo and PCCT had significantly higher AUC than Veraview X800 regardless of post material (e.g. Ni-Cr AUC 0.71 NewTom, 0.64 PCCT, 0.48 Veraview); with a Ni-Cr post PCCT sensitivity was 0.65 vs 0.22 (3D Accuitomo) and 0.10 (Veraview), and sensitivity did not change significantly across post conditions for NewTom, CS9600 or PCCT; specificity did not differ among devices or conditions.

The authors conclude PCCT performs comparably to high-resolution CBCT under controlled ex vivo conditions and propose it only as an incidental, secondary read when a PCCT scan already exists, not as a replacement for CBCT (higher dose, cost, 0.2 mm voxel); AUC values were modest (0.48-0.76 with wide SDs), 8 fractured teeth, one PCCT system, no clinical predictors, so patient-level accuracy is unknown.

## 세줄요약

체외 (ex vivo) 진단정확도 연구 - 근관치료한 단근 인간 치아 18개 (인위적 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 8개, 정상 10개)를 인체모형 (anthropomorphic phantom)에 넣고 포스트 없음, 니켈-크롬 (Ni-Cr) 포스트, 코발트-크롬 (Co-Cr) 포스트 조건으로 광자계수 CT (Photon-Counting CT, PCCT; NAEOTOM Alpha) 1대와 콘빔 CT (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT) 4대에서 총 270회 촬영하고 구강악안면영상 전문의 5명이 블라인드로 판독.

NewTom VGi evo와 PCCT는 포스트 재질과 무관하게 Veraview X800보다 수신자 조작 특성 곡선하면적 (Area Under the ROC Curve, AUC)이 유의하게 높았고 (Ni-Cr 포스트에서 AUC 0.71 NewTom, 0.64 PCCT, 0.48 Veraview), Ni-Cr 포스트에서 PCCT 민감도는 0.65로 3D Accuitomo 0.22, Veraview 0.10보다 높았으며, NewTom·CS9600·PCCT는 포스트 조건 간 민감도 변화가 유의하지 않았고 특이도는 장비·조건 간 차이 없음.

저자들은 PCCT가 통제된 체외 조건에서 고해상도 CBCT와 비슷한 성능이라고 결론짓되, 다른 목적으로 이미 촬영된 PCCT가 있을 때 부수적 판독에만 쓰고 CBCT 대체로는 쓰지 말 것을 제안함 (선량·비용 높음, 복셀 0.2 mm); AUC 자체는 0.48-0.76으로 중등도이고 SD가 크며, 파절치 8개, PCCT 1종, 임상 예측 인자 없음이라 환자 수준 정확도는 불명.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First comparison of a photon-counting CT device with four endodontically suitable CBCT devices for VRF detection in teeth with metal posts (authors' claim).
- Shows the metal-post effect is device dependent: 3D Accuitomo 170 and Veraview X800 lost sensitivity with Ni-Cr posts, whereas NewTom VGi evo, CS9600 and PCCT did not change significantly.
- Positions PCCT as a complementary, incidental diagnostic option (existing hospital scan covering the dental arches) rather than a primary VRF imaging tool.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: ex vivo diagnostic-accuracy study (PRILE 2021 reporting); ethics approval protocol NH019 2019-09-03.
- **Specimens**: 18 freshly extracted single-rooted human teeth (periodontal extraction; no prior endodontic treatment, no resorption); crowns cut at the cemento-enamel junction; canals prepared with Reciproc Blue 40.06, irrigated with 2.5% NaOCl, two-thirds prepared for post space. VRF induced in 8 teeth with an Instron 8802 and a conical metal tip, confirmed by transillumination and visual inspection; 10 teeth intact (note: the paper also calls the no-post scan condition "control").
- **Phantom**: human skull plus dry dentate mandible covered with Mix D (soft-tissue equivalent); each root placed in the mandibular first premolar socket.
- **Conditions**: no post; Ni-Cr post; Co-Cr post.
- **Devices**: NAEOTOM Alpha PCCT (voxel 0.2 mm); 3D Accuitomo 170 (0.08 mm voxel, 4x4 cm FOV); Veraview X800 (0.08 mm, 4x4); NewTom VGi evo (0.125 mm, 5x5); Carestream 9600 (0.075 mm, 5x5). Highest-resolution protocol per device; 18 x 5 x 3 = 270 scans. CBCT devices were pre-selected from earlier 13-device and 10-device comparisons for acceptable metal-post artefact performance.
- **Readers**: 5 oral and maxillofacial radiologists (more than 4 years experience), blind, calibrated, generic DICOM viewer (RadiAnt) for all devices, 5-point confidence scale, max 10 scans per day; 30% (81 scans) re-read after 1 month.
- **Analysis**: AUC, sensitivity, specificity per reader; two-way ANOVA (device x post condition) with Tukey; weighted kappa; alpha 0.05; post hoc power 90%.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Device order below: 3D Accuitomo / Veraview X800 / NewTom VGi evo / CS9600 / PCCT (the PMC text export lost the table column headers; order taken from Table 1 and checked against the Results text).

| Metric (mean) | Condition | Accuitomo | Veraview | NewTom | CS9600 | PCCT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AUC | No post | 0.73 | 0.53 | 0.76 | 0.70 | 0.76 |
| AUC | Ni-Cr | 0.52 | 0.48 | 0.71 | 0.67 | 0.64 |
| AUC | Co-Cr | 0.60 | 0.51 | 0.70 | 0.65 | 0.70 |
| Sensitivity | No post | 0.67 | 0.47 | 0.75 | 0.47 | 0.75 |
| Sensitivity | Ni-Cr | 0.22 | 0.10 | 0.62 | 0.72 | 0.65 |
| Sensitivity | Co-Cr | 0.40 | 0.10 | 0.62 | 0.67 | 0.57 |
| Specificity | No post | 0.74 | 0.60 | 0.72 | 0.74 | 0.70 |
| Specificity | Ni-Cr | 0.80 | 0.86 | 0.78 | 0.62 | 0.66 |
| Specificity | Co-Cr | 0.80 | 0.92 | 0.70 | 0.60 | 0.82 |

- AUC: NewTom and PCCT significantly higher than Veraview regardless of post (p < 0.05); Accuitomo and CS9600 intermediate (no significant difference vs other CBCTs). SDs of AUC were 0.08-0.31.
- Sensitivity, no post: no significant device differences. Ni-Cr: CS9600 and PCCT significantly higher than Accuitomo and Veraview; NewTom intermediate. Co-Cr: NewTom, CS9600 and PCCT significantly higher than Veraview; Accuitomo intermediate.
- Within device: Accuitomo sensitivity higher in no-post and Co-Cr than Ni-Cr; Veraview higher in no-post than either post; NewTom, CS9600 and PCCT showed no significant change across conditions.
- Specificity: no significant differences among devices or conditions (p > 0.05).
- Agreement: inter-examiner kappa 0.34 (slight) to 0.70 (substantial); intra-examiner 0.45 (moderate) to 0.78 (substantial).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Ex vivo: no periodontal ligament thickening, bone defects or symptoms (the clinical predictors of VRF); moderate inter-examiner agreement reflects this.
- Only 8 fractured teeth; fracture width not controlled, so some fractures may be below the 0.2 mm PCCT voxel; no motion artefacts; no gutta-percha or sealer in the canals; single PCCT system, so not generalizable.
- Veraview X800 was read in a non-native viewer, which the authors say may have lowered its results.
- Abstract/Conclusion describe "high diagnostic accuracy", yet mean AUC for PCCT was 0.64-0.76 with large SDs (up to 0.31) and sensitivities 0.57-0.75; the wording is stronger than the numbers.
- PCCT dose remains higher than CBCT, cost high, availability limited, no standardized protocols; a fracture must be at least about 0.2 mm wide to be seen on PCCT.
- Future: patient-level validation, other PCCT systems, dose-optimized acquisition protocols.
- Source text inconsistency: the Results paragraph on sensitivity states NewTom was significantly higher than Veraview in the Ni-Cr group but prints "(p > 0.05)"; the table lettering and the abstract imply p < 0.05. Treated as a typo.

## 6. Related Work

- de-2025 (pooled CBCT SR+MA): metal posts lower CBCT sensitivity on average; this study shows large between-device differences.
- habibzadeh-2023 (CBCT SR): low and heterogeneous CBCT sensitivity with posts; consistent with the 0.10-0.72 range here.
- kim-2024 (imaging guideline): periapical first, small-FOV CBCT only if needed; this paper adds a radiation-justification argument against extra scans for PCCT.
- shokri-2024 and patel-2022: broader CBCT vs periapical and VRF reviews in the same folder.

## 7. Glossary

- **PCCT (photon-counting CT)**: CT with direct-conversion semiconductor detectors that count individual photons, without a scintillator; lower electronic noise, higher contrast resolution; about 200 um spatial resolution here.
- **VRF (vertical root fracture)**: longitudinal fracture through cementum, dentine and canal, usually along the root axis.
- **Beam hardening artefact**: streaks or dark bands next to high-density material such as metal posts that can hide or mimic fracture lines.
- **AUC**: area under the ROC curve; 0.5 is chance, 1.0 perfect.
- **Mix D**: material that mimics soft-tissue X-ray absorption and scatter in the phantom.
- **PRILE 2021**: Preferred Reporting Items for Laboratory studies in Endodontology.
