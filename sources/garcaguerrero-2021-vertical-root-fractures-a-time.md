## Why Ingested

The cracked-tooth cluster holds narrative accounts of vertical root fracture (VRF) in root filled teeth ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]], [[wiki/cracked-tooth/liao-2021-vertical-root-fracture-non-endodontically-treated]]) and one radiographic case-control study in Tokyo molars ([[wiki/endodontics/shaping/lee-2026-residual-pericervical-apical-dentine-vertical]]), but no clinical data on *when* after root canal treatment each risk factor acts. This nested case-control study from two Colombian specialist clinics adds that time-window dimension (retreatment early, intracanal calcium hydroxide late) and independently echoes Lee's retreatment finding.

## Three-line Summary

Nested case-control study (STROBE; 90 surgically confirmed VRF cases vs 270 time-matched controls among endodontically treated teeth, two Colombian university postgraduate endodontic clinics) analysing clinical risk factors in three post-treatment time windows (1–5, 6–10, 11–17 years).

Retreatment (versus primary treatment) raised VRF odds in the 1–5 year window (OR 12.19, 95% CI 3.64–40.81) and the 6–10 year window (OR 4.28, 95% CI 1.08–17.07); intracanal medication (calcium hydroxide) was associated with VRF only after >10 years (OR 8.55, 95% CI 2.06–35.47); intraradicular posts were protective in the first 10 years (OR 0.22 and 0.26); 53.3% of cases appeared within 5 years.

Risk factors act at different times, so surveillance of retreated teeth is most critical in the first 5 years; limitations are retrospective records from two clinics, surgically confirmed cases only (selection toward teeth that were explored), wide confidence intervals and internal inconsistencies in the reported cohort size and prevalence.

## 세줄요약

중첩 환자-대조군 연구 (Nested case-control; STROBE; 콜롬비아 2개 대학 근관치료 대학원 클리닉; 수술로 확인된 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 90례 vs 시간 매칭 대조 270개, 근관치료치) — 치료 후 3개 시간대 (1–5년, 6–10년, 11–17년)별 임상 위험요인 분석.

재근관치료 (Retreatment)는 초회 치료 대비 1–5년 구간 (OR 12.19, 95% CI 3.64–40.81)과 6–10년 구간 (OR 4.28, 95% CI 1.08–17.07)에서 VRF 위험을 높였고, 근관 내 약제 (수산화칼슘, Calcium Hydroxide)는 10년 초과 구간에서만 연관 (OR 8.55, 95% CI 2.06–35.47); 근관 내 포스트 (Post)는 처음 10년간 보호 요인 (OR 0.22, 0.26); 전체 사례의 53.3%가 5년 이내 발생.

위험요인마다 작용 시점이 달라, 재근관치료 치아는 처음 5년 추적이 가장 중요함; 한계는 후향적 기록, 수술 탐색이 이뤄진 치아만 사례로 확정한 선택 편향, 넓은 신뢰구간, 코호트 크기·유병률 보고의 내적 불일치.

- **Institution**: Universidad Nacional de Colombia (Bogotá) and Universidad de Antioquia (Medellín), Colombia

## 2. Key Contributions
- Frames VRF in endodontically treated teeth (ETT) as time-dependent: risk factors become significant in different post-treatment windows (the "etiological window", 1–17 years).
- Retreatment is proposed as a primary risk factor (first 5–10 years) and long-term intracanal calcium hydroxide as a secondary (late, >10 years) risk factor.
- Intraradicular posts showed a protective association in the first 10 years, in contrast to the usual concern that posts predispose to fracture.
- Reports an incidence distribution of cases over time: 53.3% (1–5 y), 28.8% (6–10 y), 17.7% (≥11 y).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: nested case-control from a reconstructed cohort of endodontically treated teeth (records to 2020), STROBE-reported, ethics-approved at both universities.
- **Sample**: cohort reported as 481 teeth/patients in the Methods and Results but 450 patients in the Abstract; 90 cases and 270 controls (1:3 matched on time since treatment completion). Sample-size calculation: minimum 331 teeth (83 cases, 248 controls), based on a preliminary study (OR 4.95 for direct restorations).
- **Cases**: ETT with VRF confirmed surgically (flap elevation, direct observation under operating microscope) after clinical/radiographic/tomographic signs (localized inflammation, mobility, sinus tract, angular/lateral bone loss).
- **Controls**: ETT with no clinical inflammation and radiographically normal.
- **Exclusions**: orthodontic treatment, trauma history, caries, coronal fracture; included crowned permanent teeth with complete root formation.
- **Exposures**: age, sex, tooth type/location, type of endodontic treatment (primary / retreatment / apical surgery), number of appointments, intracanal medication (calcium hydroxide), apical extension of filling, coronal restoration, role in rehabilitation, intraradicular post, adjacent implant.
- **Statistics**: descriptive, Pearson chi-square, logistic regression adjusted for confounders (R 3.1.3), alpha 0.05, stratified by time group (G1 1–5 y, G2 6–10 y, G3 11–17 y).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Case definition requires surgical confirmation, so only teeth that underwent exploratory surgery can be cases; controls were radiographically normal teeth, not teeth explored surgically (selection/verification bias).
- Retrospective records from two postgraduate clinics; operator, restoration quality and parafunction not controlled; cervical dentine wear not assessed (authors state this).
- Wide confidence intervals (e.g., OR 12.19, CI 3.64–40.81; OR 8.55, CI 2.06–35.47) and many exposures tested across three strata.
- Internal inconsistencies in the source: cohort 450 (Abstract) vs 481 (Methods/Results); "16.42% prevalence" cannot be estimated from a case-control sample matched 1:3; abstract places retreatment risk at "five to ten years" whereas the Results show the largest effect at 1–5 years; abstract mentions direct coronal restorations as protective but the Results report only posts; G2 narrative says retreated teeth probability "reduced by 12%", conflicting with the positive OR 4.28.
- Calcium hydroxide mechanism rests on cited in vitro literature (12.6% dentin fracture-resistance reduction); not tested directly.

## 6. Related Work
- Patel 2022 ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]]): narrative review stating VRF presents typically 2–5 years after root canal treatment, consistent with 53.3% of cases within 5 years here.
- Lee 2026 ([[wiki/endodontics/shaping/lee-2026-residual-pericervical-apical-dentine-vertical]]): Japanese molar case-control; repeated retreatment independent predictor, but post presence not significant in molars (here posts protective across tooth types).
- Liao 2021 ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/liao-2021-vertical-root-fracture-non-endodontically-treated]]): cited by the authors for VRF predominance in treated teeth.
- Patel 2025 ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2025-position-statement-longitudinal-cracks-fractures]]): consensus statement framing VRF in root filled teeth.

## 7. Glossary
- **Vertical root fracture (VRF)**: complete or incomplete longitudinal break along the root's vertical axis.
- **Etiological window**: interval from end of endodontic treatment to VRF diagnosis (here 1–17 years).
- **Nested case-control**: cases and matched controls sampled from within a defined cohort.
- **Retreatment**: orthograde removal of previous root filling and repeat canal treatment.
- **Intracanal medication**: interappointment dressing, here calcium hydroxide.
