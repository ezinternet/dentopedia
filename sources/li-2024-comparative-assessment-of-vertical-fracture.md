## Why Ingested

Fills the obturation-material/technique dimension of vertical root fracture (VRF) risk that the VRF cluster only covers narratively: [[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]] and [[wiki/cracked-tooth/liao-2021-vertical-root-fracture-non-endodontically-treated]] discuss obturation force qualitatively, and [[wiki/endodontics/shaping/rathke-2024-ex-vivo-minimally-invasive-endodontic]] tests sealer type in a single bench study. This NMA pools 48 in vitro trials, and it sits beside the clinical-outcome obturation pages ([[wiki/endodontics/obturation/mushtaq-2026-obturation-techniques-endodontic-outcomes-srma]]) as a bench-level, non-clinical axis.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and frequentist network meta-analysis of in vitro studies (48 trials, 2,724 extracted single-rooted human teeth; 5 databases, Jan 2000–Sep 2023; INPLASY2023100034) comparing vertical fracture resistance of roots filled with six obturating systems and four techniques against unfilled prepared (positive control) and untreated (negative control) roots.

By SUCRA, Resilon/Epiphany ranked highest among systems (80.8%), then GP/iRoot SP (55.5%), GP/MTA-Plus (47.9%), GP/AH-Plus (47.4%), GP/AH-26 (45.9%) and GP/ZOE (12.3%); Resilon/Epiphany beat GP/AH-Plus (SMD 0.77, 95% CI 0.10–1.45) and GP/ZOE (SMD 1.64, 0.47–2.80), the calcium-silicate systems did not differ significantly from the resin systems, and no technique differed significantly from another (single cone SUCRA 70.2%; Thermafil did not beat the unfilled control).

Bench-only, load-to-fracture evidence with 3/48 studies blinding the examiner, 14/48 at medium risk of bias, a loop-specific inconsistency in the technique network and small calcium-silicate arms (72 and 144 teeth); the top-ranked Resilon/Epiphany is off the market, so the data support only a weak pro-single-cone/calcium-silicate lean and cannot be extrapolated to clinical fracture rates.

## 세줄요약

체외 연구의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 빈도론적 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA) — 48개 시험, 발거된 단근 인간 치아 2,724개; 5개 데이터베이스 (2000년 1월–2023년 9월; INPLASY2023100034); 6가지 충전 시스템과 4가지 충전 술식의 수직 치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 저항성을 미충전 형성 근관 (양성 대조)·미처치 치근 (음성 대조)과 비교.

누적순위곡선하면적 (Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking Curve, SUCRA)상 시스템 순위는 Resilon/Epiphany 80.8% > GP/iRoot SP 55.5% > GP/MTA-Plus 47.9% > GP/AH-Plus 47.4% > GP/AH-26 45.9% > GP/ZOE 12.3%; Resilon/Epiphany가 GP/AH-Plus (표준화 평균차 SMD 0.77, 95% CI 0.10–1.45)와 GP/ZOE (SMD 1.64, 0.47–2.80)보다 우수, 칼슘실리케이트계와 레진계 간 유의차 없음, 술식 간 유의차 없음 (단일 콘 술식 SUCRA 70.2%; Thermafil은 미충전 대조를 넘지 못함).

체외 파절하중 실험만으로 구성되고 (검사자 눈가림 3/48편, 중간 편향위험 14/48편), 술식 네트워크에 loop 불일치가 있으며 칼슘실리케이트군 표본이 작고 (72·144개), 1위 Resilon/Epiphany는 시판 중단 — 단일 콘·칼슘실리케이트계로의 약한 경향만 시사하며 임상 파절률로 외삽할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Self-described first NMA ranking VRF resistance across six obturating systems (Resilon/Epiphany, GP/AH-26, GP/AH-Plus, GP/ZOE, GP/iRoot SP, GP/MTA-Plus) and four techniques (lateral condensation LCT, single cone SCT, warm vertical compaction VCT, Thermafil).
- Updates the earlier pairwise meta-analysis by Tan et al. (cited, not held in the wiki): adds recent trials and indirect comparisons, and now detects GP/AH-Plus > unfilled control (SMD 0.78) where Tan found it non-significant (SMD 0.59, CI −0.02 to 1.21).
- Argues that calcium-silicate sealers (iRoot SP, MTA-Plus) are the marketed alternative to the withdrawn Resilon/Epiphany, but the difference from resin sealers is not statistically supported.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR + frequentist NMA (random effects, Stata `network` package) of in vitro studies; PRISMA-NMA; protocol INPLASY2023100034.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Embase, ScienceDirect, Web of Science, Cochrane Library; Jan 2000–Sep 2023; English only; reference lists hand-searched.
- **Selection**: 5,193 records, 3,662 after de-duplication, 3,533 excluded on title/abstract; 48 studies (obturating systems) and 46 studies (techniques) included, published 2002–2023. Single-rooted human teeth with closed apices; at least two eligible arms; only the six pre-selected systems.
- **n**: 2,724 roots in the systems network = 336 negative control (unprepared, unfilled) + 580 positive control (prepared, unfilled) + 1,808 filled. Premolars in 31 studies, incisors in 10, canines in 2.
- **Outcome**: VRF load (converted to N), pooled as standardized mean difference (SMD) with 95% CI; ranking by SUCRA.
- **Quality**: modified Tan et al. 9-item risk-of-bias tool (adds reporting of loading rate); no GRADE rating was applied.
- **Inconsistency**: design-by-treatment global test and loop-specific tests.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Network sizes: systems network = 8 nodes, 28 comparisons (25 direct, 3 indirect); techniques network = 6 nodes, 15 comparisons (all direct).

| System | SUCRA |
|---|---|
| Negative control (untreated) | 99.9% |
| Resilon/Epiphany | 80.8% |
| GP/iRoot SP | 55.5% |
| GP/MTA-Plus | 47.9% |
| GP/AH-Plus | 47.4% |
| GP/AH-26 | 45.9% |
| GP/ZOE | 12.3% |
| Positive control (prepared, unfilled) | 10.4% |

Pairwise (SMD, 95% CI): Resilon/Epiphany vs positive control 1.55 (0.93–2.18); GP/AH-Plus vs positive control 0.78 (0.23–1.33); Resilon/Epiphany vs GP/AH-Plus 0.77 (0.10–1.45); Resilon/Epiphany vs GP/ZOE 1.64 (0.47–2.80). No other system-vs-system contrast was significant, including calcium-silicate vs resin sealers. All arms stayed below the untreated negative control.

| Technique | SUCRA | vs positive control, SMD (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|
| Negative control | 100% | — |
| Single cone (SCT) | 70.2% | 1.32 (0.62–2.01) |
| Warm vertical compaction (VCT) | 53.0% | 1.00 (0.01–1.99) |
| Lateral condensation (LCT) | 45.4% | 0.85 (0.32–1.38) |
| Thermafil | 28.8% | not significant |
| Positive control | 2.5% | — |

No pairwise difference among the four techniques was significant. Global inconsistency P = 0.47 (systems) and 0.86 (techniques); the SCT–Thermafil–VCT loop was locally inconsistent (P < 0.001). Risk of bias: 34 low, 14 medium, 0 high; comparison-adjusted funnel plots looked symmetric.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- 14/48 studies at medium risk of bias; only 3/48 blinded the examiner.
- Loop inconsistency in the technique network (SCT–Thermafil–VCT) which the authors say lowers the overall level of evidence.
- Only six brand systems; calcium-silicate arms small (72 and 144 teeth); authors suggest future NMAs grouped by sealer class (bioceramic, resin, ZOE).
- Tooth type, preparation technique and irrigant not modelled; bench load-to-fracture does not reproduce multidirectional oral loading; in vitro results should be extrapolated to clinical settings with care.
- Resilon/Epiphany is no longer marketed (cited long-term failure ~5.7x vs GP/AH-Plus, attributed to biodegradation), so its rank is of historical interest.

## 7. Glossary
- **VRF (Vertical Root Fracture)**: longitudinal root fracture, the most severe fracture type in root-filled teeth, usually leading to extraction.
- **NMA (Network Meta-Analysis)**: pooled direct and indirect comparisons across more than two interventions.
- **SUCRA**: surface under the cumulative ranking curve, 0–100%, probability-based ranking of interventions.
- **Positive / negative control**: prepared-but-unfilled roots / unprepared roots.
- **Monoblock**: bonded dentin–sealer–core obturation proposed for Resilon/Epiphany.
- **SCT / LCT / VCT**: single cone / lateral condensation / warm vertical compaction techniques.
