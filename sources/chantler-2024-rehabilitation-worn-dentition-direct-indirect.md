## Why Ingested

Adds a meta-analytic, survival-based answer to the direct-vs-indirect question for worn dentition that [[prosthetic-materials/hardan-2022-treatment-tooth-wear-using-direct]] could only address qualitatively (no pooling possible), and is read against the worn-dentition subgroup of [[resin/josic-2023-direct-indirect-resin-longevity-sr-ma]]. Source: PubMed abstract ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/jerd.13384)).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (MEDLINE, WoS, Scopus, CENTRAL, VHL, EMBASE; 5009 records, 14 publications: 2 RCTs, 7 prospective, 5 retrospective) of minimally invasive direct vs indirect restorations in worn dentition.

Direct vs indirect manually processed composite showed no significant survival difference (n=2; OR 1.79, 95% CI 0.64-5.05; p=0.270); prospective pooled survival was 99% (direct, n=3) vs 100% (indirect, n=5); lithium disilicate had the fewest failures (1.8%).

Authors suggest indirect ceramics (especially lithium disilicate) for long-term treatment and direct composite as a medium-term solution, but evidence is limited and of varying quality (abstract-level data only).

## 1. Document Information

- Journal of Esthetic and Restorative Dentistry 2024;37(3):690-701 (epub 2024-12-13). PubMed PMID 39670301, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/jerd.13384).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Meta-analytic comparison of survival for minimally invasive direct vs indirect restorations in worn dentition.
- Material-level failure signal: lithium disilicate 1.8% failures.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Six databases searched; PICO: worn dentition (P), indirect (I) vs direct (C), survival/success (O). No language restriction. Random-effects models for survival and prevalence.
- 14 publications: 2 RCTs, 7 prospective, 5 retrospective; 52% direct, 48% indirect restorations.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Direct vs indirect manually processed composite: n=2, OR 1.79 (95% CI 0.64-5.05), p=0.270.
- Prospective pooled survival: direct (n=3) 99% (95% CI 0.97-1.00); indirect (n=5) 100% (95% CI 0.99-1.00).
- Lithium disilicate: fewest failures, 1.8%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only the abstract was available; risk-of-bias details, heterogeneity, follow-up durations and material-specific denominators are not reported here.
- Authors note limited evidence and varying methodological quality; the prospective pooled estimates are near ceiling (99-100%), limiting discrimination.

## 7. Glossary

- Worn dentition (tooth wear): loss of tooth structure from erosion, attrition, abrasion (keywords include dental erosion).
- Indirect manually processed composite: laboratory-fabricated resin composite restoration.
- Lithium disilicate: glass-ceramic used for indirect restorations.
