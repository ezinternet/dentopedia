## One-line Summary
RCT (n=55, CVM 2-3, ~8 months): Invisalign MAA and Activator produced equivalent CBCT volumetric changes in mandible and maxilla vs. untreated controls, suggesting short-term Class II correction is predominantly positional, not skeletal.

## 한줄요약
RCT (n=55, 성장기 Class II, 약 8개월): Invisalign MAA와 Activator 모두 비처치군 대비 하악·상악 골 용적 변화에서 유의한 차이 없음 — 단기 II급 교정은 용적 성장보다 위치 변화(위치적 적응) 주도.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First 3D CBCT volumetric RCT directly comparing Invisalign MAA vs. conventional Activator in growing patients
- Demonstrates that short-term mandibular volumetric changes do not differ between MAA, Activator, and untreated controls
- ANCOVA adjustment for baseline volume imbalance and inter-scan interval strengthens null finding
- Intragroup MAA maxillary increase was significant (p=0.009) but intergroup comparison showed no difference — suggests growth artifact rather than treatment effect
- Highlights that volumetric analysis and 2D cephalometric superimposition are complementary, not interchangeable

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Single-center, parallel-arm RCT (CONSORT-reported)
- **Population**: n=55, CVM stage 2-3, skeletal Class II (ANB>4°, SNB<78°), horizontal/average growth pattern, overjet +4–+8mm, mean age 12.5y
- **Groups**: MAA n=20, Activator n=20, Control n=15
- **MAA protocol**: Invisalign MAA, 2mm advancement increments every 8 weeks, follow-up q5-6 weeks, mean duration 8.15 months
- **Activator protocol**: Projet Bite Wafer, full-time wear (except meals/sport), mean duration 7.15 months
- **Imaging**: i-CAT 3D CBCT; segmentation Mimics 15.0; HU threshold 226–3071; two time points (baseline and post-treatment)
- **Primary outcome**: mandibular and maxillary volumetric changes (mm³)
- **Secondary outcomes**: overjet correction, skeletal age progression
- **Statistics**: Kruskal-Wallis H, Wilcoxon signed-rank, ANCOVA (covariate: baseline volume, inter-scan interval); ICC for reproducibility
- **Note**: CBCT scans were clinically indicated retrospectively analyzed (not research-acquired)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Mandibular volume change** (intergroup): Activator 419.23 mm³, MAA 263.48 mm³, Control 874.50 mm³; H=0.128, p=0.938 (NS)
- **Maxillary volume change** (intergroup): Activator 809.49 mm³, MAA 996.59 mm³, Control 388.92 mm³; H=1.176, p=0.556 (NS)
- **ANCOVA adjusted mandibular**: F=0.131, p=0.877
- **ANCOVA adjusted maxillary**: F=0.049, p=0.952
- **Intragroup MAA maxillary**: z=−2.613, p=0.009 (within-group significant, not intergroup)
- **ICC**: >0.90 (excellent segmentation reproducibility)
- **Overjet**: reduced in both treatment groups; no significant intergroup difference
- **Baseline mandibular volume** differed significantly between groups (p=0.001) → necessitated ANCOVA

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=20/group); underpowered to detect small volumetric effects (type II error cannot be excluded)
- Short follow-up (6–8 months); long-term skeletal remodeling not captured
- No objective wear-time monitoring; compliance by patient/parent report only
- Absence of complementary 2D skeletal measurements (condylar growth, SNB/ANB changes)
- Retrospective CBCT design; baseline volume and scan-interval imbalances required statistical adjustment
- Future: larger samples, 12–24 month follow-up, patient-reported outcomes, complementary cephalometric analysis

## 7. Glossary
- **MAA (Mandibular Advancement Appliance)**: Invisalign system feature using precision wings to posture mandible forward
- **CVM (Cervical Vertebral Maturation)**: staging system for skeletal growth phase using lateral cephalogram
- **HU (Hounsfield Unit)**: CT density value; threshold 226–3071 used for cortical bone segmentation
- **ANCOVA (Analysis of Covariance)**: adjusted for baseline volume and inter-scan interval differences
