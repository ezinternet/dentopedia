## Why Ingested

Directly addresses whether a **non-crosslinked collagen membrane** adds value to ARP performed with a collagenated bovine xenograft (DBBM-C). Complements [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/kim-2024-collagenated-xenograft-arp-rct]] (Korean DBBM-C ARP RCT showing DBBM-C efficacy) by testing the membrane component: when buccal bone is thick, the collagenated graft alone produces equivalent hard- and soft-tissue outcomes to graft + membrane — simplifying the protocol. Extends [[bone-regeneration/wessing-2016-noncrosslinked-collagen-membrane-dehisced-implant-rct]] to the ARP indication with a different question (necessity of membrane vs quality of membrane).

## Three-line Summary

6-month parallel-arm RCT (n=20 post-extraction ARP sites, premolars/canines/incisors) at Harvard randomizing patients to collagenated bovine xenograft (BG) alone vs BG + non-crosslinked collagen membrane (MEM), with secondary-intention healing and assessment by CBCT, ultrasound, and biopsy at 6 months.
All endpoints — keratinized tissue width (4.80 vs 4.80 mm, p=0.999), horizontal bone width at 3 mm (9.02 vs 8.41 mm, p=0.464), vertical bone change (Δ-0.78 vs Δ-1.31 mm, p=0.244), histomorphometric new bone (25.8% vs 27.9%, p=0.803) — were statistically equivalent between groups.
In ARP sites with thick buccal bone, omitting the non-crosslinked collagen membrane does not compromise ridge preservation outcomes, offering a simpler and more cost-effective protocol.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT to isolate the membrane's contribution in ARP when a **collagenated** xenograft is used (previous studies used non-collagenated particulates)
- Demonstrates collagenated graft alone = graft + non-crosslinked collagen membrane in horizontal bone width, vertical bone preservation, keratinized tissue, and new bone formation
- Uses ultrasound as objective supplement to CBCT for soft tissue assessment
- Important scope limitation: results apply to sites with **thick buccal bone** (mean ~1 mm) — thin buccal wall cases excluded from inference

## Methodology

- **Design**: Parallel-arm RCT, 6-month follow-up
- **n**: 20 patients (17 premolars, 1 canine, 2 incisors)
- **Groups**: G1 = BG (collagenated bovine xenograft) alone; G2 = BG + MEM (non-crosslinked collagen membrane)
- **Healing**: Secondary intention in both groups
- **Baseline buccal bone**: G1 1.02±0.44 mm, G2 0.93±0.45 mm (NS)
- **Primary outcomes**: Horizontal ridge width (CBCT), vertical bone change
- **Secondary outcomes**: KTW, gingival thickness, histomorphometry (% new bone), ultrasound
- **Institution**: Harvard School of Dental Medicine
- **Trial registration**: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05838651

## Key Results

| Endpoint | BG alone (G1) | BG + MEM (G2) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| KTW (mm) | 4.80 ± 1.48 | 4.80 ± 1.14 | 0.999 |
| Gingival thickness (mm) | 1.22 ± 0.25 | 1.20 ± 0.35 | 0.650 |
| Horizontal bone width at 3mm (mm) | 9.02 ± 2.09 | 8.41 ± 1.45 | 0.464 |
| Vertical bone change buccal (mm) | Δ-0.78 ± 0.59 | Δ-1.31 ± 1.20 | 0.244 |
| New bone formation (%) | 25.80 ± 15.48 | 27.89 ± 18.21 | 0.803 |

All endpoints: NS. Ultrasound findings consistent with CBCT and clinical data.

## Limitations

- Small sample size (n=10/group); underpowered for small effect detection
- Abstract-only ingest — internal details (specific graft product, membrane brand, exclusion criteria) not fully recoverable
- Participants had **thick buccal bone** (~1 mm) — findings may not generalize to thin-wall or compromised sites
- 6-month follow-up only; long-term ridge stability not assessed
- Single-site selection (premolars/anterior) limits generalizability to molars
