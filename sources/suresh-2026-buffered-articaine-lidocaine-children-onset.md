## Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=100; India; 2025-2026) comparing buffered 4% articaine (1:100,000 epi, pH raised with 0.18mL 8.4% NaHCO3 in 1:10 ratio) vs non-buffered 2% lidocaine (1:100,000 epi) for dental injections in children aged 6-12 years (ASA I). Buffered articaine produced significantly faster onset (71.98 vs 115.28 seconds), lower injection pain (VAS 2.25 vs 3.93), better behavioral cooperation (Frankl 3.18 vs 2.48), and longer soft-tissue duration (159.02 vs 137.84 min) — all p<0.001. Pulpal success comparable (84% vs 82%, p=1.000).

Key distinction: This compares buffered articaine against unbuffered lidocaine (inter-agent comparison) — not the same drug buffered vs unbuffered. Effects therefore reflect both pH buffering AND superior articaine pharmacokinetics combined.

Buffering protocol: 0.18 mL of 8.4% NaHCO3 + 1.62 mL articaine cartridge = 1.8 mL final volume (1:10 ratio, bedside preparation). 30G needle, 1 mL/min injection rate, prior topical 10% lidocaine spray 60s.

## Why Ingested

Provides onset time data for 2% lidocaine in children: 115.28 ± 20.04 seconds (~1.9 min) — notably faster than adult IANB onset (~5-8 min) due to thinner cortical plates and shorter diffusion distances in pediatric mandible. Cross-ages onset comparison for the wiki.

## Key Data Points

| Parameter | Buffered Articaine 4% | Non-buffered Lidocaine 2% | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onset (s) | 71.98 ± 15.24 | 115.28 ± 20.04 | <0.001 |
| Onset (min) | ~1.20 | ~1.92 | <0.001 |
| Duration soft tissue (min) | 159.02 ± 19.10 | 137.84 ± 17.54 | <0.001 |
| VAS injection pain | 2.25 ± 0.94 | 3.93 ± 0.90 | <0.001 |
| Pulpal success | 84% | 82% | 1.000 (NS) |
| Reinjection rate | 16% | 28% | 0.227 (NS) |
| HR during injection (bpm) | 100.46 ± 8.10 | 106.44 ± 7.65 | 0.0003 |
| Frankl score | 3.18 ± 0.60 | 2.48 ± 0.74 | <0.001 |

## Limitations

- Inter-agent comparison (articaine vs lidocaine + buffering) — cannot isolate buffering effect alone
- Single-center academic India setting
- First-time dental attendees only (may not generalize to anxious experienced children)
- Maxillary vs mandibular arch not analyzed separately
- One-week follow-up only
- Categorical outcomes underpowered
