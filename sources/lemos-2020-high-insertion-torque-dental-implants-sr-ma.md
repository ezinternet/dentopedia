## Why Ingested
[[implants/isq/faot-2019-insertion-torque-mandibular-overdenture]]에서 IT ≥45 Ncm(G4)이 최고 생존율을 보였지만, 단일 소규모 전향적 연구의 한계가 있음. 이 SR+MA는 "고삽입토크(삽입 토크, IT) ≥50 Ncm vs 일반·저토크 <50 Ncm"를 임플란트 생존율·변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 관점에서 최초로 메타분석한 연구로, 과토크 임상효과의 최고 수준 근거.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (6 studies, 389 patients, 651 implants) comparing dental implants placed with high insertion torque (삽입 토크, IT ≥50 Ncm) versus regular or low IT (<50 Ncm), with outcome measures of survival rate and marginal bone loss.

Neither implant survival (RR 0.51, 95% CI 0.06–4.06, P=.52) nor MBL (MD 0.15mm, 95% CI −0.14 to 0.44, P=.30) differed significantly between groups; however, the high-IT group showed a numerically lower failure rate (1.1% vs 4.2%), most pronounced under immediate loading.

Evidence base is sparse (only 6 eligible studies), preventing definitive conclusions — further RCTs with standardized IT reporting are needed to establish a safe upper torque limit.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(6편, 389명, 임플란트 651개): 고삽입토크(삽입 토크, IT ≥50 Ncm) vs 일반·저토크(<50 Ncm)를 생존율·변연골 소실로 비교.

생존율(RR 0.51, P=.52)·MBL(MD 0.15mm, P=.30) 모두 유의차 없음; 다만 고토크군 실패율이 수치상 더 낮음(1.1% vs 4.2%), 즉시하중 하위군에서 가장 두드러짐.

근거 기반이 빈약(적격 연구 6편뿐)해 확정적 결론 불가 — 안전 상한 토크 확립을 위해 표준화된 IT 보고를 갖춘 추가 RCT 필요.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal:** The Journal of Prosthetic Dentistry, Vol. 126, Issue 4, pp. 490–496
- **Publication date:** 2020-09-08 (epub); print 2021
- **Search cutoff:** April 2019
- **Funding:** not reported; no conflicts declared

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR/MA with PICO specifically focused on IT ≥50 Ncm as the high-torque cutoff vs <50 Ncm
- Demonstrates no statistically significant between-group difference in survival or MBL — high IT does not harm implant outcomes
- Subgroup analysis reveals the survival advantage of high IT is most pronounced in immediate loading protocols (failure rate 4.2% vs 1.1%)
- Highlights the paucity of evidence: only 6 eligible studies (389 patients, 651 implants, mean age 55.28 years)
- Calls for further RCTs with standardized IT reporting to enable robust meta-analysis

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA)
- **PICO:** Patients with dental implants; Intervention = IT ≥50 Ncm; Comparison = IT <50 Ncm; Outcomes = implant survival rate + MBL
- **Search:** Electronic databases (not specified in abstract) up to April 2019; no language restriction
- **Included studies:** 6 articles; 389 patients; 651 implants (437 high IT, 214 low/regular IT)
- **Loading protocols:** Mostly delayed loading; 1 study immediate loading (n=50 each group)
- **Statistical methods:** Mantel-Haenszel (MH) method for survival, inverse variance (IV) method for MBL; α=0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Implant survival rate:**
- High IT (≥50 Ncm) failure rate: 1.1%
- Low/regular IT (<50 Ncm) failure rate: 4.2%
- Meta-analysis: RR 0.51 (95% CI 0.06–4.06), P=.52 — **not significant**

**Marginal bone loss:**
- Meta-analysis: MD 0.15mm (95% CI −0.14 to 0.44), P=.30 — **not significant**

**Subgroup — immediate loading:** Low IT showed notably higher failure rate vs high IT in the single immediate-loading study

**Clinical threshold established in this review: 50 Ncm** as the dividing line between "high" and "regular/low" IT

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 6 eligible studies — very limited evidence base for meta-analysis
- Heterogeneous IT definitions across studies; no universal upper limit for "safe" IT
- Only 1 immediate-loading study in the subgroup
- Cannot distinguish effect of IT from confounders (implant design, bone type, surgeon experience)
- Further RCTs with standardized IT reporting and longer follow-up required

## 6. Related Work
- Berardini SR 2016: No significant MBL/survival difference between high and low IT (animal + human studies)
- Faot 2019 [[implants/isq/faot-2019-insertion-torque-mandibular-overdenture]]: prospective data with IT grouping, G4 ≥45 Ncm best outcomes
- Marconcini 2018 [[implants/isq/marconcini-2018-high-insertion-torque-bone-resorption-rct]]: RCT — IT ≥50 Ncm → greater bone resorption at 3 years (contradicts this review's MBL finding for long-term)
- Khayat 2013: IT ≥70 Ncm safe (no harm to osseointegration)

## 7. Glossary
- **IT (Insertion Torque, 삽입 토크):** Rotational resistance during implant placement, Ncm
- **RR (Risk Ratio, 위험비):** Ratio of failure probability; RR <1 = high IT safer
- **MD (Mean Difference, 평균차):** Difference in MBL between groups
- **PICO:** Population-Intervention-Comparison-Outcome framework for SR
- **High IT threshold:** ≥50 Ncm (this review's definition); Faot 2019 uses ≥45 Ncm
