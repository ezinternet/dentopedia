## Three-line Summary

Critical narrative review (JDR 2018) of clinical evidence on resin bonding for long-term success of high-strength ceramics (alumina and zirconia), covering surface pretreatment, cementation protocols, and restoration-type-specific recommendations.

Resin bonding is mandatory for adhesion-dependent restorations (veneers, RBFDPs, thin ceramics) and optional for retentive crowns; Al₂O₃ air-particle abrasion and silica/silane coating (Rocatec/CoJet) are recommended for zirconia surface activation; single-retainer cantilever RBFDP showed 5-year survival of 92.3% and 10-year of 94.4%.

Long-term data on monolithic full-contour zirconia (FCZ) crowns are scarce, and the review does not address the specific bonding challenges of newer translucent 4Y/5Y zirconia compositions.

## 세줄요약

비평적 서술 리뷰 (JDR 2018): 고강도 세라믹(알루미나·지르코니아)의 레진 본딩이 장기 성공에 미치는 영향 — 표면 전처리, 시멘트 프로토콜, 수복물 유형별 지침.

접착 의존 수복물(비니어, RBFDP, 얇은 세라믹)에는 레진 본딩 필수; 지르코니아 표면 활성화에는 Al₂O₃ 공기-입자 연마 + 실리카/실란 코팅(Rocatec/CoJet) 권장; 단일 지대 캔틸레버 RBFDP 5년 생존율 92.3%, 10년 94.4%.

모놀리식 지르코니아(FCZ)의 장기 임상 데이터 부족이 주요 한계 — 신형 반투명 4Y/5Y 지르코니아의 접착 특수 문제는 다루지 않음.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Critical review in JDR discussing clinical evidence on resin bonding for long-term success of high-strength ceramic restorations (alumina and zirconia). Covers: cementation vs resin bonding protocols, surface pretreatment (sandblasting, silane, Rocatec/CoJet), clinical recommendations for different restoration types (crowns, FDPs, cantilever RBFDPs). Reviews clinical data on zirconia-based 3-unit FDPs showing 100% survival at 5 years when properly designed. Discusses differences between first- and second-generation zirconia. Notes scarcity of long-term clinical data on monolithic FCZ crowns.
