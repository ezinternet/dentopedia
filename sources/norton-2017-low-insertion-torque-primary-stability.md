## Why Ingested
저삽입 토크(≤20 Ncm) 임플란트의 생존율·변연골 소실을 전향적으로 추적 — [[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]] 핵심 명제("저토크도 성공적으로 골융합된다")를 임상 데이터로 직접 뒷받침하며, "스피너"(<5 Ncm) 3개가 전원 골융합되었다는 사례 포함.

## Three-line Summary
Prospective closed-cohort study (n=30 implants, all IT ≤20 Ncm including 3 "spinners" at <5 Ncm, 1-year follow-up): 96.7% overall survival with 100% osseointegration among followed implants — directly demonstrating that very low IT alone does not predict failure.
Mean ISQ rose from 67.2 at surgery to 80.0 at 3-month loading regardless of initial IT; no IT–ISQ correlation found (Spearman, NS); 71% showed no marginal bone loss; ISQ ≥55 immediate temporization was safe.
Argues that ISQ (axial stiffness) is a more informative stability metric than IT (rotational friction) in low-torque scenarios, challenging the clinical dogma that high IT is a prerequisite for osseointegration.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: International Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Implants, vol. 32, no. 4, pp. 849-857, 2017
- DOI: 10.11607/jomi.5889
- Affiliation: Implant & Reconstructive Dentistry, London, UK; Adjunct Clinical Professor, University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine

## 2. Key Contributions
- Prospectively demonstrates that implants with very low IT (≤20 Ncm, including <5 Ncm "spinners") can achieve osseointegration and favorable marginal bone levels
- Mean ISQ significantly improves from surgery (67 MD/65 BL) to 3 months (80 MD/79 BL) regardless of very low initial IT
- Survival 100% for 29 followed implants (96.7% including excluded case)
- Bone loss: 71.4% no loss; 21.4% 0.1-0.5mm; only 7.2% >0.5mm — all within acceptable range
- Challenges dominant belief that high IT is prerequisite for successful osseointegration
- Argues ISQ (axial stiffness) is more informative than IT (rotational friction) for stability assessment

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Closed-cohort prospective study — self-selected on IT ≤20 Ncm
- n=30 implants, 22 patients (10M/12F, age 22–79); 1-year follow-up
- ISQ measured mesiodistal (MD) and buccolingual (BL) at surgery and prosthetic loading (~3 months)
- Immediate temporization: esthetic need + minimum ISQ ≥55
- Torque groups: <5 Ncm (spinners, n=3), >5<10 Ncm (n=9), 10 Ncm (n=4), 15 Ncm (n=8), 20 Ncm (n=6)
- Outcome: ISQ change, survival, marginal bone loss at 1 year
- Stats: Spearman's rho (ISQ vs PIT), Wilcoxon signed rank (ISQ change), Mann-Whitney U and Fisher's test (bone loss)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- ISQ at surgery: 67.2 MD / 64.5 BL (mean); at 3 months: 80.0 MD / 79.1 BL
- Survival: 1 excluded (no follow-up data), 29/29 = 100%; including excluded: 96.7%
- Bone loss distribution: 71.4% no loss, 21.4% 0.1-0.5mm, 7.2% >0.5mm (max 2.82mm, 1 outlier)
- Immediate temporization (n=9): no significant difference in ISQ or bone loss vs non-temporized
- "Spinners" (n=3, IT<5 Ncm): all achieved osseointegration; ISQ at surgery ranged from 65–73 MD
- No correlation found between IT and ISQ (Spearman, p not significant)
- 3 patients diabetic (2 diet-controlled, 1 type 2 well-controlled) — all successful

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Self-selected cohort — allocation not randomized; no control group (high IT comparator)
- Small n=30; single operator; London private practice — limited generalizability
- 1-year follow-up — short for marginal bone loss assessment
- No stratification by location (maxilla 22 vs mandible 8) for ISQ analysis
- 1 implant excluded from bone loss data (no follow-up) — potential bias

## 6. Related Work
- Faot 2019: ISQ low correlation with IT in narrow implants (Kendall τ=0.252)
- Lemos 2020 SR+MA: high vs low IT — no statistical difference in survival
- Baldi 2018: IT-ISQ correlation moderate overall (ρ=0.461), not significant at extremes
- Trisi 2011: high torque (110 Ncm) — no bone necrosis at 6 weeks; "spinners" can osseointegrate argument

## 7. Glossary
- **Closed-cohort**: enrollment closed once criteria met (IT≤20 Ncm); all subsequent cases followed to completion
- **Spinner**: implant with IT<5 Ncm — freely rotates without resistance (traditionally considered high failure risk)
- **PIT (Peak Insertion Torque)**: maximum IT during insertion
- **ISQ MD/BL**: ISQ measured in mesiodistal and buccolingual directions — two perpendicular RFA measurements
- **Immediate temporization**: provisional crown placed at time of implant surgery
