## Why Ingested

Only PubMed-indexed study directly benchmarking eCligner (Seoul, Korea; Orapix brand) material against reference thermoplastic aligner materials (Duran, Essix A+, Essix ACE). Provides the sole peer-reviewed in-vitro characterization of eCligner's physical and mechanical behavior before and after thermoforming; forms the material-science foundation alongside [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/delgado-2025-thermoplastics-clear-aligners-review]] for clinical claims about aligner material properties.

## Three-line Summary

An in-vitro multiscale study tested four thermoplastic aligner materials — Duran, eCligner, Essix A+, Essix ACE — across transparency, water absorption/solubility, surface hardness, flexure modulus, and elastic modulus before and after thermoforming. eCligner was distinctive in that its water solubility decreased after thermoforming (while all others increased), surface hardness did not change significantly, and transparency remained stable, though its water absorption pre-thermoforming was the highest among tested brands. Thermoforming significantly altered mechanical properties in all four materials, rejecting the null hypothesis of property stability.

## 세줄요약

네 가지 열가소성 교정용 투명 장치 소재(Duran, eCligner, Essix A+, Essix ACE)를 열성형 전후로 다중 물성 시험한 in-vitro 연구. eCligner는 열성형 후 수분 용해도가 감소(타 소재와 반대), 표면 경도 변화 없음, 투명도 유지 등 독특한 거동을 보였으나 열성형 전 수분 흡수율이 가장 높았다. 열성형은 네 소재 모두에서 물리·기계적 특성을 유의하게 변화시켜 귀무가설이 기각됨.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic multiscale characterization of eCligner (Seoul, Korea) thermoplastic material relative to international benchmark brands
- Establishes that thermoforming significantly alters physical and mechanical properties in all tested aligner materials
- Demonstrates eCligner's unique water solubility behavior: decreases after thermoforming, unlike Duran, Essix A+, and Essix ACE
- Proposes ISO 20795-2 (Orthodontic base polymer) as a standard evaluation framework for TOA thermoplastics

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Materials tested**:
- Duran (Scheu Dental, Germany) — 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 mm
- eCligner (eCligner, Seoul, Korea) — 0.5, 0.75 mm (max available)
- Essix A+ (Dentsply Raintree Essix, USA) — 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 mm
- Essix ACE (Dentsply Raintree Essix, USA) — 0.75, 1.0 mm

**Test battery**:
1. Transparency (spectrophotometry, 400–700 nm; CM-3500D, Konica Minolta)
2. Water absorption and solubility (ISO 20795-2; 37°C ± 1°C, 14 days)
3. Surface hardness (Knoop; 9.8 N, 10 s; DMH-2, Mastsuzawa Siki)
4. Three-point bending flexure modulus (Instron 3366; 5 mm/min; 0.5–1.0 mm strain)
5. Tensile elastic modulus (Instron 5942; 12 mm/min)

**Model**: Maxillary central incisor dimensions typical of Korean adults (crown 8.5 mm height × 7 mm width); Biostar thermoforming caster (Scheu Dental).

**Statistics**: One-way ANOVA + Tukey's post-hoc; Student's t-test pre vs. post thermoforming (p < 0.01).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### eCligner-specific findings

| Property | Pre-thermoforming | Post-thermoforming | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water absorption | Highest among 4 brands | Decreased (still elevated) | ↑ then ↓ |
| Water solubility | Highest before thermoforming | Decreased (unique) | ↓ (others ↑) |
| Surface hardness | No significant inter-brand difference | Greater than Essix A+/ACE; unchanged from pre | Stable |
| Transparency | Greater decrease at 0.5/0.75 mm vs Duran/Essix A+ | No significant change (eCligner, Essix ACE stable) | Stable |
| Flexure modulus (0.5 mm) | No significant difference from Duran, Essix A+ | Increased significantly; higher than Duran | ↑ |
| Flexure modulus (0.75 mm) | Higher than Duran and Essix ACE | Increased significantly | ↑ |
| Elastic modulus (0.75 mm) | No significant difference | No significant change | Stable |

### General findings (all materials)

- Thermoforming decreases flexural and tensile forces in all materials across all thicknesses
- Thinner materials generate higher flexure modulus (inversely proportional to thickness)
- Water absorption increased for all four materials after thermoforming

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Did not simulate intraoral conditions (temperature cycles, saliva, humidity, cyclic loading)
- eCligner available only in 0.5 and 0.75 mm thicknesses — direct comparison with 1.0 mm Essix A+/Duran not possible
- No fatigue/fatigability testing under repetitive aligner insertion/removal
- No evaluation of actual tooth movement forces in a clinical or phantom model

## 7. Glossary

- **TOA** (Transparent Orthodontic Aligner, 투명 교정 장치): removable thermoplastic orthodontic device
- **PETG** (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol, 폴리에틸렌 테레프탈레이트 글리콜): most common polymer base for clear aligners
- **Thermoforming** (열성형): vacuum/pressure heat-forming of thermoplastic sheet over dental cast
- **Flexure modulus** (굴곡 탄성계수): resistance to bending deformation; key predictor of orthodontic force delivery
- **Elastic modulus** (탄성계수): stiffness under tensile loading; related to material resilience
- **Knoop hardness** (누프 경도): surface hardness measurement under microindentation load
- **Water solubility** (수분 용해도): mass lost to dissolution in water; relates to material degradation in saliva
- **eCligner** (이클라이너): Korean clear aligner brand manufactured by Orapix (Seoul, Korea), PETG-based
