## Why Ingested

The wiki's bone-quality and strain-related pages need the bioengineering rationale behind a load-tuned implant design. This Misch et al. 2001 paper combines a strain-based review with a two-implant, one-year case report, and it is the source for the claim that implant design can set the bone's remodeling response at the interface. Related wiki pages: [[overviews/implant-design-bone-quality-stress-fea]], [[overviews/bone-quality-implant-risk-modification-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Literature review plus case report (Journal of Periodontology, 2001) on a bioengineered implant designed for a predetermined strain-related bone response — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The case report describes two implants loaded for one year, with lamellar bone at the interface and a turnover rate of less than 5 µm/day, similar to bone away from the implant; the authors note the number of implants is small.

The reported histology is a two-case observation, so it should be cited as preliminary and not as proof of a design effect.
