## Why Ingested
선호 저작측 (Preferred Chewing Side, PCS) 대구치 소실이 저작 기능에 미치는 영향을 RCT 교차설계로 분리한 최초 연구. 단축치열 (Shortened Dental Arch, SDA)의 편측 영향을 직접 검증 → 기존 [[removable-partial-denture/nassani-2013-fesrpd-shortened-dental-arch-reappraisal]] SDA 개념 및 [[removable-partial-denture/removable-partial-denture]] 분야 저작 기능 근거 보강.

## One-line Summary
RCT crossover (n=28) using occlusal splints to simulate unilateral SDA: PCS molar removal significantly decreased masticatory efficiency (ME) and subjective masticatory ability (SMA) (p<0.05), while increasing occlusal load on the second premolar; non-PCS molar removal had no significant effect (J Prosthodont Res 2026).

## 한줄요약
RCT 교차설계(n=28), 교합 스플린트로 편측 단축치열 시뮬레이션: 선호 저작측 (Preferred Chewing Side, PCS) 대구치 제거 시 저작 효율 (Masticatory Efficiency, ME) 및 주관적 저작 능력 (Subjective Masticatory Ability, SMA) 유의하게 감소(p<0.05), 제2소구치 교합력 상승; 비선호측 제거는 유의 차이 없음 (J Prosthodont Res 2026).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrates that molar loss on the PCS specifically impairs masticatory function while non-PCS loss does not significantly affect overall function
- Quantifies compensatory loading: second premolar on PCS removal side shows significantly higher maximum occlusal force (Maximum Occlusal Force, MOF)
- Identifies a protective subgroup: individuals whose main occluding area (MOA) is at the second premolar are less affected by PCS molar loss (smaller MOF reduction, p<0.05)
- Suggests that PCS molar loss may necessitate prosthetic intervention, whereas non-PCS molar loss may not
- Provides experimental RCT evidence specifically addressing the asymmetric SDA scenario

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Randomized crossover trial (experimental simulation)
- **Population:** 28 participants with intact dentition
- **Intervention:** Custom occlusal splints (OSs) simulating unilateral SDA by removing molar contact on one side
- **Sequence:** Group 1: PCS removal first → non-PCS removal; Group 2: reverse sequence
- **Outcomes:** MOF (maximum occlusal force), ME (masticatory efficiency — comminution), SMA (subjective masticatory ability, questionnaire)
- **Analysis:** α=0.05; crossover analysis; subgroup by MOA location (second premolar vs first molar)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Full-arch MOF:** NS between PCS removal and intact conditions
- **Second premolar MOF on removal side:** Significantly higher after PCS removal (compensatory loading)
- **ME:** Significantly decreased after PCS removal (p<0.05)
- **SMA:** Significantly decreased after PCS removal (p<0.05)
- **Non-PCS removal:** No significant effects on ME or SMA
- **Subgroup — MOA at second premolar:** Significantly smaller reduction in full-arch MOF after PCS removal vs MOA at first molar (p<0.05) — attenuated impact

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Artificial simulation (splints ≠ actual tooth loss or actual SDA); no adaptation period
- Sample restricted to participants with intact dentition; does not include actual SDA patients
- Short-term measurement; no long-term adaptation or compensation assessed
- Masticatory efficiency measured by comminution test — does not capture all aspects of function
- Future: RCT in actual SDA patients comparing PCS vs non-PCS molar loss prosthetic intervention threshold

## 7. Glossary
- **PCS:** Preferred Chewing Side — the side an individual preferentially uses for chewing
- **MOF:** Maximum Occlusal Force — maximum force exerted during clenching
- **ME:** Masticatory Efficiency — objective measure of food comminution ability
- **SMA:** Subjective Masticatory Ability — patient-reported chewing function
- **MOA:** Main Occluding Area — principal zone of occlusal contact
- **OS:** Occlusal Splint — custom appliance used to modify occlusal contacts
