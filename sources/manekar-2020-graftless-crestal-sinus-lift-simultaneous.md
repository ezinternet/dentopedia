## Why Ingested

[[duan-2017-graft-free-maxillary-sinus-floor-elevation-sr-ma]]의 SR+MA가 무이식 상악동 거상의 집단 근거(97.9% 생존율, 평균 골증대 3.8 mm)를 확립하지만, 수압 키트(CAS kit/Hiossen)를 이용한 치조정 수압 상악동 거상술 (Crestal Hydraulic Sinus Lift, CHSL)만을 단독으로 평가한 전향적 데이터는 부족하다. 본 연구는 잔존골 높이 4–8 mm 범위에서 CHSL + 동시 식립을 평가한 전향적 단일 술자 자료로, [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/guo-2020-non-grafted-platelet-concentrations-grafted-transcrestal-sinus]]의 무이식 비교군을 기술적 맥락에서 보완한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective study evaluated graftless crestal hydraulic sinus lift (CHSL) with simultaneous implant insertion in 17 patients (26 implants) with subantral bone height 4–8 mm.

Mean bone height gain was 5.6 mm (from 6.5 mm to 11.7 mm); 100% implant survival at 1 year; mean insertion torque 32 Ncm; no membrane perforations.

Graftless CHSL with simultaneous implant insertion is a predictable, minimally invasive technique; bone formation from blood clot under tented sinus membrane.

## 세줄요약

잔존골 높이 4–8 mm인 17명 환자, 26개 임플란트에 무이식 치조정 수압 상악동 거상술 (Crestal Hydraulic Sinus Lift, CHSL)과 동시 임플란트 식립을 평가한 전향적 연구.

평균 골 높이 증가는 5.6 mm(6.5 mm→11.7 mm)였고, 1년 임플란트 생존율은 100%, 평균 식립 토크는 32 Ncm, 점막 천공 없음.

무이식 CHSL 동시 식립은 예측 가능한 최소 침습 술식으로, 텐팅된 상악동 점막 아래의 혈병 (blood clot)에서 골형성이 일어난다.

- **Institution**: Government Dental College and Hospital, Nagpur, India
- **Study type**: Prospective interventional study
- **n**: 17 patients, 26 implants (Osstem TS III)
- **Follow-up**: 1 year
- **DOI**: 10.4103/njms.NJMS_18_19

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates 100% implant survival at 1 year with graftless CHSL and simultaneous placement (RBH 4–8 mm)
- Mean bone height gain of 5.6 mm via hydraulic sinus lift without any grafting material
- No membrane perforations using CAS kit (Hiossen) hydraulic technique
- Supports blood clot alone as sufficient scaffold under tented sinus membrane

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Prospective interventional study; 17 patients, 26 implants (Osstem TS III); residual bone height (RBH) 4–8 mm; crestal hydraulic sinus lift (CAS kit, Hiossen) with saline injection under controlled hydraulic pressure; simultaneous implant placement; CBCT evaluation pre-operatively and at 6 months; clinical follow-up at 1 year; implant survival criteria per Buser et al. (2002).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Mean pre-operative bone height: 6.5 mm; post-operative: 11.7 mm → gain: **5.6 mm**
- Implant survival: **100%** at 1 year (n=26)
- Mean insertion torque: **32 Ncm**
- Membrane perforations: **0**
- Bone formation from blood clot confirmed radiographically at 6-month CBCT
- Mean patient age: 53 years

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample size (n=17, 26 implants)
- Short follow-up (1 year only)
- No control group (no grafted comparison arm)
- Single center, single surgeon (limited generalizability)
- No histologic confirmation of bone formation mechanism
- All implants placed simultaneously — staged approach not evaluated

## 7. Glossary

- **CHSL**: 치조정 수압 상악동 거상술 (Crestal Hydraulic Sinus Lift)
- **CAS kit**: 치조정접근 상악동 거상술 키트 (Crestal Approach Sinus Kit, Hiossen)
- **RBH**: 잔존골 높이 (Residual Bone Height)
- **Tenting effect**: 텐팅 효과 — 임플란트 정단부가 상악동 점막을 들어올려 혈병 형성 공간을 유지하는 효과
- **Blood clot**: 혈병 — 무이식 술식에서 골형성의 초기 scaffold
