## Why Ingested
NCCL에서 self-adhesive flowable composite(SAFC)의 명확한 실패 사례. [[resin-bonding/peumans-2023-universal-adhesive-nccl-3year-rct]]·[[resin-bonding/omoto-2025-nccl-adhesion-strategies-4year-rct]]가 보여주는 "접착 전처리가 유지력에 핵심"이라는 결론을 6개월 음성 결과로 보강. 초록 기반.

## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (abstract only; 80 noncarious cervical lesions, single center) comparing self-adhesive flowable composite (SAFC: Fusio Liquid Dentin) vs 3-step etch-and-rinse adhesive + nanohybrid composite (Optibond FL + G-aenial) at 1 week and 6 months using FDI criteria.

SAFC failed catastrophically at 6 months: 33% retention success (27/40 unacceptable retention failures) vs 100% for the 3-step ER + nanohybrid; significant group difference also in color/translucency criteria.

This is a negative RCT demonstrating that eliminating the dedicated adhesive/etching step is clinically unacceptable for NCCLs; only the 6-month horizon and a single SAFC product were tested.

## 세줄요약

비우식성 치경부 병소(Noncarious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 80개 split-mouth RCT(초록 기반): 자가접착 유동성 복합레진(Self-Adhesive Flowable Composite, SAFC — Fusio Liquid Dentin) vs 3-step 산부식·세척(Etch-and-Rinse, ER) 접착제 + 나노혼합레진(Optibond FL + G-aenial)을 FDI 기준으로 1주·6개월 비교.

6개월 유지성공률: SAFC 33%(27/40 유지실패, 비허용) vs 3-step ER+나노레진 100%; 색상·투명도 항목도 군 간 유의차.

별도 접착·산부식 단계를 생략하면 NCCL에서 임상적으로 허용 불가임을 보여주는 음성결과 RCT; 6개월 단기, 단일 SAFC 제품 한정이므로 클래스 효과로 과도 해석 주의.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrates a self-adhesive flowable composite (Fusio Liquid Dentin) is clinically unacceptable for NCCLs at 6 months.
- Strong contrast with a conventional 3-step etch-and-rinse (Optibond FL) + nanohybrid (G-aenial) at 100% success.
- Empirical support for the principle that a dedicated adhesive/etching step is critical to NCCL retention.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Randomized, controlled, single-center, split-mouth; 80 NCCLs; SAFC (Fusio Liquid Dentin) vs E&R 3-step (Optibond FL) + G-aenial. FDI criteria at 1 week and 6 months. Wilcoxon Signed Rank, Mann-Whitney U (alpha=0.05).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- SAFC success 33% (27/40 unacceptable due to retention failure) vs E&R/nanoC 100% at 6 months.
- Significant group difference also for color and translucency.
