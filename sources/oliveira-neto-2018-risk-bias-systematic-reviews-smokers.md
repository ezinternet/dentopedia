## Why Ingested

The wiki already holds a cluster of primary smoking–implant SR+MAs ([[implants/naseri-2020-smoking-levels-implant-failure-sr-ma]], [[implants/mustapha-2022-smoking-dental-implants-sr-ma]], [[implants/fan-2024-smoking-early-implant-failure-sr-ma]], [[implants/calciolari-2026-tobacco-smoking-smoke-free-products]]) synthesized in [[overviews/smoking-tobacco-periodontal-implant-overview]], but none of them critically appraise the *methodological rigor* of the SR+MA layer itself. This 2018 umbrella review fills that gap: it applies ROBIS to the 6 smoking-implant meta-analyses available through April 2017 and finds 5 of 6 carry high/unclear risk of bias — a caution flag that should temper how confidently the pooled effect sizes cited elsewhere in the wiki are read. A companion umbrella review on implant success rates in smokers (Cureus 2024) is being ingested in parallel as a sibling stem and is not yet linkable here.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella systematic review (2018, J Prosthet Dent) applying the ROBIS tool to 6 systematic reviews with meta-analysis on dental implant placement in smokers, searched through April 2017.

Only 1/6 (16.7%) of included meta-analyses had low risk of bias; 3/6 (50.0%) unclear risk; 2/6 (33.3%) high risk — the most commonly unmet ROBIS domains were study eligibility criteria and identification/selection of studies.

Most published smoking-implant meta-analyses at the time carried methodological risk of bias, so their pooled conclusions/recommendations warranted careful, not automatic, clinical adoption; abstract-only — full text not retrieved.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First (at time of publication) umbrella-level ROBIS assessment specifically of smoking-implant SR+MAs, rather than a new primary meta-analysis of implant outcomes.
- Quantifies how much of the smoking-implant SR+MA evidence base (as of April 2017) is methodologically trustworthy: only 1/6 low risk of bias.
- Identifies the two ROBIS domains most often failed — study eligibility criteria, and identification/selection of studies — giving future SR authors a concrete target for improvement.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: umbrella systematic review of SR+MAs (methodological/critical-appraisal study, not a new outcome meta-analysis).
- Eligibility: SR articles with meta-analysis on dental implant placement in smokers where implant survival/failure was the primary outcome and directly related to smoking status; duplicates excluded.
- Databases: MEDLINE (PubMed), Scopus, Web of Science, LILACS, DARE-Cochrane; grey literature via SIGLE/OpenGrey, no language restriction.
- Two independent reviewers screened titles/abstracts and full texts; a third reviewer resolved disagreements; reference lists of included articles were hand-screened for additional studies.
- Search cutoff: April 29, 2017.
- Risk-of-bias tool: ROBIS (Risk of Bias in Systematic Reviews).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 2539 initial results → 6 SR+MAs eligible for the umbrella review.
- Inter-reviewer agreement: kappa = 0.90 (very good agreement).
- All 6 included reviews were published within the preceding 11 years.
- ROBIS risk-of-bias distribution: 1/6 (16.7%) low risk; 3/6 (50.0%) unclear risk; 2/6 (33.3%) high risk.
- Most frequently unmet ROBIS domains: study eligibility criteria; identification and selection of studies.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only source — full text not retrieved (Elsevier paywall, no PMC ID); methodological detail beyond the abstract (e.g., ROBIS domain-by-domain scoring table, identity of the 6 included SRs, sensitivity analyses) could not be extracted for this page.
- Search cutoff of April 2017 means this appraisal does not cover any smoking-implant SR+MA published afterward (e.g., Naseri 2020, Mustapha 2022, Fan 2024, Calciolari 2026 in this wiki) — those newer reviews have not themselves been ROBIS-assessed here.
- Small denominator (n=6 SRs) limits how generalizable the "5/6 biased" finding is, though the two failed domains (eligibility criteria; study identification/selection) are plausibly persistent risks in later reviews too.
- Authors explicitly call for future meta-analyses to strengthen study eligibility criteria and identification/selection processes.

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella systematic review**: a review of reviews — synthesizes/appraises existing systematic reviews rather than primary studies.
- **ROBIS (Risk of Bias in Systematic Reviews)**: a structured tool for assessing the risk of bias in the conduct of a systematic review itself (as opposed to AMSTAR-2, which rates overall SR quality/reporting).
- **Kappa (κ)**: statistic measuring inter-rater agreement beyond chance; 0.90 here indicates very good agreement between the two independent reviewers.
- **Study eligibility criteria (ROBIS domain)**: whether a review's inclusion/exclusion criteria were appropriate and pre-specified.
