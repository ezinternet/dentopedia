## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/yassir-2022-cat-vs-fat-overview-systematic-reviews]]는 투명교정 (Clear Aligner Treatment, CAT)이 sagittal/transverse 이동(torque·정출·회전)에서 고정식 장치 (Fixed Appliance Treatment, FAT)보다 열등하다는 severity boundary만 제시할 뿐, 수직적 문제(anterior open bite)에 대한 상세 메커니즘은 다루지 않았다. 본 SR+MA (Charoenrat 2025)는 open bite 교정 시 CAT이 절치 정출 (incisor extrusion)과 구치 압하 (molar intrusion) 중 무엇으로 overbite를 회복시키는지 정량 분석하고, TAD 병용 고정식 장치 (Fixed Appliance with Temporary Anchorage Devices, FATADs)와 직접 비교한 최초의 SR+MA로 이 수직축 공백을 메운다. 또한 [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/marinelli-2025-temporary-anchorage-devices-clear-aligner-sr]]가 다룬 CAT+TAD 병용 요법과 대비되는, TAD를 아예 고정식 장치 쪽에 넣은 별도 비교군(FATADs) 구도라는 점에서 임상적 갈림길을 제공한다.

## One-line Summary

SR+MA (PRISMA/PROSPERO, 10 studies — 4 non-RCTs + 6 before-after) found CAT increases overbite by 2.77mm mainly via incisor extrusion (U1 +0.87mm, L1 +1.06mm) without significant molar intrusion, whereas FATADs achieve greater overbite gain (+1.64mm more than CAT) via significantly greater U6 (+1.88mm) and L6 (+0.45mm) molar intrusion; CAT shows greater L1 extrusion (+2.35mm) than FATADs; post-treatment stability was good (overbite change only 0.02mm over retention), but certainty of evidence was low to very low throughout.

## 한줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (PRISMA/PROSPERO, 10편 — non-RCT 4편 + 전후비교 6편): 투명교정 (Clear Aligner Treatment, CAT)은 주로 절치 정출로 overbite를 2.77mm 증가시키며(상악 절치 +0.87mm, 하악 절치 +1.06mm) 구치 압하는 유의하지 않은 반면, TAD 병용 고정식 장치 (FATADs)는 구치 압하(상악 구치 +1.88mm, 하악 구치 +0.45mm)를 통해 CAT보다 overbite를 1.64mm 더 크게 개선함; CAT은 하악 절치 정출이 FATADs보다 2.35mm 더 큼; 유지기간 중 재발은 미미(overbite 변화 0.02mm)했으나 전반적 근거수준은 낮음~매우낮음.

- **Study type**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA, Cochrane Handbook)

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to dissect the detailed mechanism of anterior open bite closure with CAT — quantifying the relative contribution of incisor extrusion vs. molar intrusion.
- First SR+MA to directly compare CAT vs. FATADs protocols on overbite and vertical tooth position (U1, L1, U6, L6) outcomes, rather than reviewing each modality separately.
- Provides the first pooled stability/relapse analysis of CAT-corrected open bite during retention (≥6 months post-treatment).
- Uses weighted mean difference (not standardized mean difference) for clinically interpretable millimeter values.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: SR+MA per Cochrane Handbook and PRISMA guidelines; PROSPERO CRD42024588198.
- **Search**: Medline, Scopus, Cochrane Central, Embase, Virtual Health Library (VHL), searched 2024-09-27 and updated 2025-04-17; no language/year restriction; grey literature via Google Scholar (first 100 results); manual reference screening. Deduplication/screening/extraction via Covidence.
- **Screening**: Two independent reviewers (PU, TC), third reviewer (CC) for disagreements.
- **Risk of bias**: ROBINS-I for non-RCTs; NIH quality assessment tool for before-and-after studies without control group.
- **Outcome variables**: overbite (vertical distance between U1 and L1 incisal edges), and vertical positions of U1, L1, U6 (mesiobuccal cusp), L6, measured from lateral cephalograms or CBCT at three time points — pre-treatment, post-treatment, and retention (≥6 months post-treatment).
- **Synthesis**: RevMan 5.4; weighted mean difference with 95% CI; heterogeneity via chi-square (p<0.1) and I² (25/50/75% = low/moderate/high); random-effects model when heterogeneity assumed.
- **Sensitivity analyses**: (1) excluding Suh et al. studies (possible duplicate participants — corresponding authors contacted); (2) excluding Harris et al. (manual acetate tracing without stated magnification calibration).
- **Publication bias**: funnel plots + Egger's test for outcomes with ≥10 studies.
- **Certainty**: GRADE approach.
- **Duplicate-subject handling**: authors of two overlapping Suh et al. papers contacted directly; if overlap confirmed, only one dataset retained; otherwise sensitivity analysis run both ways.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Study flow**: 1,610 records identified (294 Medline, 451 Scopus, 8 Cochrane Central, 69 Embase, 788 VHL) → 211 duplicates removed → 1,399 screened → 26 full-text reviewed → 17 excluded → +1 from manual search → **10 studies included** (6 before-and-after studies without control group, 4 non-RCTs; 3 of the non-RCTs compared CAT with FATADs).

**Population/protocol**: All CAT studies used Invisalign; 80% of data collected after 2011 (G4 protocol era), though only 6 studies explicitly stated G4 use. Treatment duration ranged 14.04±6.25 to 25.7±5.05 months. Initial SN-MP angle ranged 33.38°–44.40° (mixed normal/high-angle). FATADs protocols used miniscrews/miniplates in various configurations (TPA, lingual arch, rapid palatal expander); 3 studies compared CAT vs FATADs directly.

**CAT vs. FATADs meta-analysis (2 studies)**:
- Overbite: FATADs increased overbite significantly more than CAT, mean difference **+1.64 mm** (p<0.0001, 95% CI 0.86–2.43, I²=71%).
- U1 position: no significant difference (95% CI −0.08 to 1.29 mm favoring either direction, p=0.08, I²=72%).
- L1 position: CAT produced significantly greater extrusion than FATADs, **+2.35 mm** (p=0.04, 95% CI 0.06–4.64, I²=60%).
- U6 intrusion: FATADs produced significantly greater intrusion, **+1.88 mm** (p<0.00001, 95% CI 1.41–2.35, I²=48%).
- L6 intrusion: FATADs produced significantly greater intrusion, **+0.45 mm** (p=0.03, 95% CI 0.04–0.86, I²=0%).

**Before-and-after CAT meta-analysis (all 10 studies)**:
- Overbite: pooled increase **+2.77 mm** (p<0.00001, 95% CI 2.42–3.12, I²=83%).
- U1 extrusion: pooled **+0.87 mm** (p<0.0001, 95% CI 0.47–1.27, I²=0%).
- L1 extrusion: pooled **+1.06 mm** (p<0.00001, 95% CI 0.63–1.50, I²=0%).
- U6: no significant intrusion, mean **0.03 mm** (p=0.85, 95% CI −0.35 to +0.29, I²=0%).
- L6: no significant intrusion, mean **0.22 mm** (p=0.29, 95% CI −0.63 to +0.19, I²=0%).
- Sensitivity analyses (excluding Suh et al. and Harris et al.) left pooled effects/heterogeneity nearly unchanged.

**Stability (2 studies, retention ≥6 months to >1 year)**: no significant relapse in overbite or U1/L1/U6/L6 positions during retention (p=0.92, 0.35, 0.55, 0.31, 0.64 respectively; overbite change essentially negligible, ~0.02mm range reported in text as near-zero pooled change).

**Risk of bias**: before-and-after studies rated overall good quality despite gaps in reporting exclusions/sample-size calculations/blinding. Non-RCTs: moderate-to-serious risk of bias (confounding from mixed treatment modalities, retrospective design, unclear blinding).

**Publication bias**: symmetrical funnel plots; Egger's test p-values 0.069 (overbite), 0.774 (U1), 0.483 (L1), 0.200 (U6), 0.809 (L6) — no evidence of publication bias.

**GRADE certainty**: "low" for all before/after CAT comparisons; "very low" for stability/retention comparisons (small sample sizes, moderate heterogeneity) and for CAT vs. FATADs comparisons (confounding, measurement bias, moderate-high heterogeneity, inadequate sample size).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All included studies retrospective; only one clearly reported consecutive data collection — risk of selection/reporting bias.
- High protocol heterogeneity: myofunctional therapy (tongue-thrust management, clenching exercises), extra-thick plastic, virtual bite blocks, IPR, expansion — effects on molar intrusion unclear and not separable.
- Only 3 studies compared CAT vs. FATADs directly, and only 2 met meta-analysis inclusion criteria for stability — both at serious risk of bias.
- Retention periods short and variable (6 months to >1 year); dropout rates up to 37.5% in stability cohort.
- Baseline skeletal severity not matched between CAT and FATADs groups in included comparative studies (FATADs cohorts trended toward higher initial MP angle / more severe open bite in the source literature), confounding direct comparison.
- No RCTs included — authors explicitly call for high-quality RCTs and standardized treatment protocols in future research.
- Comparative effectiveness of CAT *combined with* TADs (as in Marinelli 2025) vs. FATADs remains completely unexplored — an open research gap the authors flag directly.

## 6. Related Work

- Contrasts with a prior meta-analysis/SR (cited in-text) that concluded CAT corrects open bite via both incisor extrusion **and** molar intrusion — this study's stricter inclusion criteria (requiring reporting of overbite + all 4 tooth positions) and inclusion of Suh et al./Harris et al. (retained via sensitivity analysis) produced the extrusion-dominant finding instead.
- Builds on but goes beyond a recent systematic review of FATADs-only open bite correction (which found FATADs increase overbite via molar intrusion + limited anterior extrusion) by adding a formal quantitative CAT-vs-FATADs comparison.
- See [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/yassir-2022-cat-vs-fat-overview-systematic-reviews]] for the general CAT vs. FAT severity/effectiveness boundary (sagittal/transverse focus).
- See [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/marinelli-2025-temporary-anchorage-devices-clear-aligner-sr]] for the alternative TAD-augmented-aligner (rather than TAD-augmented-fixed) approach to vertical control.

## 7. Glossary

- **CAT**: Clear Aligner Treatment
- **FATADs**: Fixed Appliances incorporating Temporary Anchorage Devices
- **TAD**: Temporary Anchorage Device (miniscrew/miniplate)
- **U1/L1**: maxillary/mandibular central incisor (incisal edge, vertical position)
- **U6/L6**: maxillary/mandibular first molar (mesiobuccal cusp, vertical position)
- **Overbite**: vertical distance between U1 and L1 incisal edges
- **SN-MP**: sella-nasion to mandibular plane angle (vertical skeletal pattern indicator)
- **ROBINS-I**: Risk Of Bias In Non-randomized Studies of Interventions
- **NIH quality assessment tool**: used for before-and-after (single-arm) study quality rating
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development, and Evaluation
- **WMD**: weighted mean difference
- **IPR**: interproximal reduction
