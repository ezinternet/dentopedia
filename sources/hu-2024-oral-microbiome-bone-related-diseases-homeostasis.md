## Why Ingested

Specifically maps the pathogenic routes linking oral microbial dysbiosis to systemic bone-related diseases (RA, OA, osteoporosis, osteomyelitis) through a homeostatic medicine framework — fills a focused niche within the oral dysbiosis/systemic disease space that [[oral-microbiology/rajasekaran-2024-oral-microbiome-systemic-health-review]] covers broadly; provides bone-specific mechanisms and treatment strategies (microbiome transplantation, MSCs).

## Three-line Summary

Review focused on oral microbial homeostasis, periodontal disease, and systemic bone-related diseases (rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, osteoporosis, osteomyelitis) through a "homeostatic medicine" framework; maps pathogenic routes from oral dysbiosis to bone homeostasis disruption.

Mechanisms covered: RANKL/OPG imbalance via periodontal bacteria, citrullination and anti-CCP antibody generation (RA link), gut-bone axis modulation, and systemic inflammatory mediator leakage; treatment strategies include probiotics, mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs), and oral microbiome transplantation as emerging approaches.

Diagnostic applications of microbiome profiling and therapeutic microbiome modulation are identified as future directions, positioning oral eubiosis restoration as a strategy for preventing systemic bone diseases.

## 세줄요약

구강 미생물 항상성(homeostasis), 치주질환, 전신 골 관련 질환(류마티스관절염·골관절염·골다공증·골수염)의 연계를 '항상성 의학' 프레임으로 검토; 구강 이상균총 → 골 항상성 교란 경로 정리.

기전: RANKL/OPG 불균형, 시트룰린화(citrullination)+항-CCP 항체(RA), 장-골(gut-bone) 축, 전신 염증 매개체 누출; 치료 전략 — 프로바이오틱스, 간엽줄기세포(MSC), 구강 마이크로바이옴 이식.

구강 마이크로바이옴 프로파일링 기반 진단과 미생물군 균형 회복을 전신 골 질환 예방 전략으로 제시.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Introduces "homeostatic medicine" framework for understanding oral-systemic bone relationships.
- Provides the most detailed coverage of oral microbiome → osteoporosis and osteomyelitis pathways.
- Identifies oral microbiome transplantation as a novel treatment strategy (analogous to FMT for gut).
- Covers all four major bone-related systemic diseases in one synthesis.

## 3. Key Pathogenic Routes

**Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)**:
- P. gingivalis → protein citrullination via PAD enzymes → anti-CCP antibody generation
- Aggregatibacter actinomycetemcomitans (Aa) → RANKL induction in fibroblasts
- Oral bacteria → blood → synovium → joint inflammation

**Osteoarthritis (OA)**:
- Periodontal inflammation → systemic cytokine elevation (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6) → cartilage degradation
- Bidirectional: OA medications (NSAIDs) may affect periodontal microbiome

**Osteoporosis**:
- Periodontal dysbiosis → RANKL/OPG imbalance → systemic bone resorption
- Estrogen deficiency + periodontal bacteria → synergistic bone loss
- Gut-bone axis: oral bacteria translocate to gut → microbiome disruption → calcium absorption reduction

**Osteomyelitis**:
- Oral bacteria (Streptococcus, Staphylococcus, anaerobes) → hematogenous seeding → bone infection
- Periodontal pathogens detected in osteomyelitis lesions

## 4. Treatment Strategies

**Probiotics**: Competitive exclusion of pathogens; immune modulation; RANKL/OPG normalization
**Mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs)**: Anti-inflammatory, bone regeneration potential
**Oral microbiome transplantation**: Transfer of eubiotic microbiome — experimental, promising

## 5. Limitations

- Narrative review — no systematic methodology
- Many pathogenic links are associational, not causal
- Clinical evidence for oral microbiome transplantation is absent
- MSC therapy for oral microbiome-mediated bone diseases is preclinical

## 6. Glossary

- **Homeostasis**: Dynamic equilibrium maintaining physiological function
- **RANKL/OPG**: Receptor activator of NF-κB ligand / osteoprotegerin (bone remodeling axis)
- **PAD**: Peptidylarginine deiminase (P. gingivalis enzyme causing citrullination)
- **Anti-CCP**: Anti-cyclic citrullinated peptide antibodies (RA biomarker)
- **MSC**: Mesenchymal stem cell
- **FMT**: Fecal microbiome transplantation (gut equivalent of oral microbiome transplantation)
- **Gut-bone axis**: Bidirectional signaling between gut microbiome and skeletal homeostasis
