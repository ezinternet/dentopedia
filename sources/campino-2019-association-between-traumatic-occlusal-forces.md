## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with narrative synthesis (no meta-analysis; J Int Acad Periodontol 2019) of traumatic occlusal forces and periodontitis: 30 articles retrieved, 14 analyzed (2 RCTs, 1 cohort, 4 retrospective, 7 cross-sectional).

Cross-sectional studies linked occlusal discrepancies to probing depth and clinical attachment level, but the effect magnitude was negligible; intervention studies showed minimal effect of occlusal adjustment on PD and CAL in periodontitis patients.

Evidence that traumatic occlusion is associated with periodontitis, or that occlusal adjustment meaningfully improves periodontal status, is limited (abstract-only).

## 세줄요약

서술적 종합 체계적 문헌고찰 (메타분석 없음; J Int Acad Periodontol 2019): 외상성 교합력과 치주염 — 30편 검색, 14편 분석 (RCT 2, 코호트 1, 후향 4, 단면 7).

단면 연구는 교합 부조화와 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD)·임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL)의 유의한 연관을 보고했으나 효과 크기는 무시할 수준; 중재 연구에서 교합 조정의 PD·CAL 효과는 미미.

외상성 교합과 치주염의 연관성 및 교합 조정의 치주 개선 효과는 근거가 제한적 (초록 전용).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Two focused questions: effect of traumatic occlusal forces on periodontal parameters with and without periodontitis; effect of occlusal interventions in periodontitis
- Synthesizes clinical and observational evidence in narrative form

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: systematic review of clinical and observational studies, narrative synthesis
- **n**: 30 articles retrieved, 14 full texts analyzed (2 RCTs, 1 cohort, 4 retrospective, 7 cross-sectional)
- **Outcomes**: PD, CAL

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Cross-sectional: significant association between occlusal discrepancies and PD/CAL; magnitude negligible
- Interventions: minimal PD/CAL effect of occlusal adjustment versus unadjusted teeth

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only; risk-of-bias assessment and numbers not retrieved
- Predominantly observational (11 of 14 non-randomized); cross-sectional designs cannot establish causality
- No meta-analysis (heterogeneous designs)

## 7. Glossary
- **Traumatic occlusion**: occlusal forces exceeding the adaptive capacity of the periodontium
- **Occlusal adjustment**: selective reshaping of occlusal contacts
