## Why Ingested

[[drug/anticoagulants/guardieiro-2023-chitosan-cellulose-hemostasis-dapt-rct]] established chitosan vs cellulose in DAPT; this study extends the evidence specifically to chitosan vs plain cotton gauze with a larger real-world antiplatelet cohort (single + dual therapy, n=100), adding pain and healing outcomes alongside hemostasis speed.

## One-line Summary

Prospective RCT (n=100 antiplatelet patients) demonstrating chitosan hemostatic sponge achieves hemostasis in a median 0.67 min vs 4.5 min for gauze (p<0.001), with all sockets hemostatic within 3 min vs only 11% for gauze.

## 한줄요약

항혈소판제 복용 환자 대상 RCT (n=100): 키토산 지혈 스폰지(중앙값 0.67분) vs 거즈(4.5분, p<0.001), 3분 내 지혈 100% vs 11%; 7일째 VAS 통증 및 Landry 치유지수도 키토산군 유의하게 우수.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest prospective RCT specifically in antiplatelet patients (n=100; 71% SAPT-aspirin, 29% DAPT-clopidogrel/prasugrel/ticagrelor) comparing chitosan to plain gauze
- Demonstrates dramatically faster hemostasis with chitosan across single and dual antiplatelet therapy subgroups without drug discontinuation
- Shows secondary benefits: lower VAS pain at days 3 and 7, higher Landry healing index at day 7 (both p<0.001)
- Dry socket rate similar and very low (1% chitosan vs 3% gauze; p=0.625), supporting safety
- Chitosan mechanism confirmed via TLR2 pathway: stimulates platelet activation even under antiplatelet pharmacology

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective, randomised study; split-mouth where comparable teeth available; 18 months (May 2023–Oct 2024), Indore, India
- **Population**: 100 adults on uninterrupted antiplatelet therapy; Hb >9 g/dL, platelets ≥100,000/μL; mean age 63.76±9.14 years; 75% male; 74% hypertensive, 34% diabetic
- **Exclusion**: Seafood allergy, congenital bleeding disorder, concomitant anticoagulant, alcoholism
- **Intervention**: Group A = chitosan hemostatic sponge (~1×1 cm, compressed 30–40 s then removed after saline irrigation); Group B = sterile cotton gauze under biting pressure
- **Antibiotics**: Amoxicillin 500 mg TDS × 3 days; NSAIDs avoided; paracetamol PRN
- **Primary outcome**: Time to hemostasis (digital stopwatch from tooth removal to cessation of oozing from dressing periphery)
- **Secondary**: VAS pain (10-point) at 24 h / 3 days / 7 days; Landry Healing Index at 7 days; dry socket; complications
- **Statistics**: SPSS v25; Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality; Wilcoxon signed-rank / McNemar; p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Hemostasis time**: Chitosan median 0.67 min (IQR 0.59–0.73) vs Gauze 4.5 min (IQR 4.0–5.0); p<0.001
- **3-minute milestone**: 100% chitosan sockets hemostatic vs 11% gauze; p<0.001
- **10-minute milestone**: near-100% both groups
- **DAPT subgroup**: bleeding time longer (median 5.34 vs 4.11 min, p<0.001) but chitosan still effective
- **Pain VAS day 7**: Chitosan median 1.0 (IQR 0–1) vs Gauze 1.0 (IQR 1–2); p<0.001
- **Landry index day 7**: Chitosan median 5.0 (IQR 4–5) vs Gauze 4.0 (IQR 3–5); p<0.001
- **Dry socket**: 1% vs 3%; p=0.625 (NS)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-center; operator blinding impossible; patient/statistician blinded
- 7-day follow-up only; no long-term data
- Only simple (non-surgical) extractions included
- No cost-effectiveness assessment
- Surgical extractions and complex cases excluded

## 6. Related Work

- Guardieiro 2023: chitosan vs cellulose in DAPT (smaller n; same direction)
- Radhakrishna et al.: better bleeding control with chitosan vs gauze in antithrombotic patients (96±4 s vs 797±23 s)
- Sinha et al.: average hemostatic time 1.5 min with chitosan in cardiac antiplatelet patients

## 7. Glossary

- **SAPT**: single antiplatelet therapy (e.g., aspirin 75 mg)
- **DAPT**: dual antiplatelet therapy (aspirin + clopidogrel/prasugrel/ticagrelor)
- **Landry Healing Index**: 5-point clinical score for socket healing (tissue colour, epithelialization, absence of necrosis)
- **TLR2**: Toll-like receptor 2; pathway by which chitosan activates platelets even under antiplatelet pharmacology
