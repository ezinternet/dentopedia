## Three-line Summary

In vitro study: grade 4 titanium dental implants conditioned with low-pressure plasma (LPP) vs atmospheric-pressure plasma (APP), submerged in human whole blood, IL1-β/TNF-α mRNA measured by RT-qPCR at 1/8/24 h vs untreated-implant and no-implant controls.

Untreated-implant controls showed significant increases in IL1-β and TNF-α expression, while both plasma-treated groups (LPP and APP alike) showed significant decreases in the same proinflammatory cytokines.

Both vacuum/low-pressure and atmospheric plasma modalities downregulate proinflammatory cytokine gene expression versus untreated titanium — the paper does not report a statistically compared LPP-vs-APP head-to-head magnitude, only that both differ from the untreated control in the same direction; full text (not retrieved) may contain that direct contrast.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First wiki-held paper to directly test **both** low-pressure (vacuum-type) and atmospheric-pressure plasma modalities on the same implant material and readout.
- Introduces an immune/cytokine (IL1-β, TNF-α) endpoint not otherwise present in the plasma cluster (existing papers use BIC, ISQ, contact angle, bacterial adhesion).
- Whole-blood in vitro model proposed as a screening assay for implant-surface immunocompatibility.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Grade 4 Ti dental implants: untreated (control), LPP-treated, APP-treated; plus a no-implant negative control.
- Submerged in human whole blood in vitro; sampled at 1, 8, 24 h.
- IL1-β and TNF-α mRNA quantified by RT-qPCR.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Control (untreated implant) group: significant ↑ in IL1-β and TNF-α expression over time.
- LPP-treated and APP-treated groups: significant ↓ in IL1-β and TNF-α expression vs control.
- Abstract does not give a direct LPP-vs-APP statistical comparison or effect-size numbers — both are reported as differing from control in the same (favorable) direction.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingest** — full text not retrieved (no PMC deposit found for this PMID; likely paywalled). Methodological detail (plasma device/parameters, exact LPP vs APP dose/time, statistical test, n) not available at this tier.
- In vitro ex vivo whole-blood model — not a clinical or animal osseointegration outcome; cytokine downregulation is a mechanistic/immunological signal, not a bone-contact or stability endpoint.
- No direct statistical LPP-vs-APP contrast reported in the abstract; cannot yet confirm whether one modality is superior to the other from this source alone.

## 7. Glossary

- LPP = Low-Pressure Plasma (저압/진공계 플라즈마); APP = Atmospheric-Pressure Plasma (대기압 플라즈마).
- IL1-β = Interleukin 1-beta; TNF-α = Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha — proinflammatory cytokines.
- RT-qPCR = Reverse Transcription quantitative Polymerase Chain Reaction (역전사 정량 PCR), used to measure mRNA expression.
