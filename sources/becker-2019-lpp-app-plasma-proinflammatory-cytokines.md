## Why Ingested

사용자 질문("진공/저압 플라즈마가 대기압 플라즈마 대비 의미가 있는가")에 대해 위키 [[wiki/overviews/plasma-surface-treatment-dental-implants]] 클러스터(27편)가 두 방식을 **직접 비교**한 논문을 갖고 있지 않다는 공백이 드러나 서베일런스로 발굴. 본 논문은 동일 티타늄 임플란트를 저압 플라즈마(Low-Pressure Plasma, LPP)와 대기압 플라즈마(Atmospheric-Pressure Plasma, APP) 두 방식으로 각각 처리해 사람 전혈(whole blood)에 노출시킨 head-to-head 비교로, [[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/hui-2020-cold-atmospheric-plasma-implantology-review]]의 "표면활성화·제염" 이중 근거에 **면역조절(immunomodulation)**이라는 세 번째 기전 축을 더한다. 전문 PDF 확보 후(2026-08-10) abstract-only에서 full-text로 승격 — 특히 **장치 파라미터에 처리시간이 명시**되어 있어, 원 질문의 "60초 진공 처리" 구체적 근거가 됐다.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study: 30 Camlog SCREW-LINE Promote plus (grade 4 Ti, abrasive-blasted acid-etched) implants — 10 untreated control, 10 atmospheric-pressure plasma (APP, 1-min plasma jet), 10 low-pressure plasma (LPP, 15-min vacuum chamber) — immersed in whole blood from a single healthy donor, IL1-β/TNF-α mRNA by RT-qPCR at 1/8/24 h.

Control implants showed IL1-β/TNF-α rising significantly through 8 h before falling by 24 h; both APP and LPP implants suppressed both cytokines significantly at 1 h (LPP only) and 8 h (both, P<0.01–0.05), but the pattern **reversed at 24 h** — both plasma groups showed a significant *increase* in IL1-β (P<0.05) and TNF-α (P<0.001) at that timepoint, while control had by then declined.

The paper does not report a direct LPP-vs-APP statistical contrast (each is compared to control, not to each other), and the entire study rests on a **single blood donor** (n=1 human, 10 implants/arm) — a major generalizability limit not visible in the abstract.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First wiki-held paper to directly test **both** low-pressure (vacuum-type) and atmospheric-pressure plasma modalities on the same implant material and readout, with full device parameters reported.
- Introduces an immune/cytokine (IL1-β, TNF-α) endpoint not otherwise present in the plasma cluster (existing papers use BIC, ISQ, contact angle, bacterial adhesion).
- Whole-blood in vitro model proposed as a screening assay for implant-surface immunocompatibility.
- Explicit stated aim: "two different plasma generation methods at low and atmospheric pressure were used to demonstrate the possible effects of the type of plasma used on the immune response" — the LPP-vs-APP contrast was the study's designed purpose.
- **Device/protocol asymmetry directly answers "why vacuum takes longer":** APP (atmospheric plasma jet, Plasmatreat FG 5001) treatment was fixed at **1 minute**; LPP (vacuum/low-pressure chamber, Diener Femto PCCE) treatment was **15 minutes** — a 15× difference in exposure time between the two device classes to produce comparable immunomodulatory direction.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Implants:** Camlog SCREW-LINE Promote plus, grade 4 Ti, abrasive-blasted acid-etched surface, 9 mm length × 4.3 mm diameter. 30 total: 10 control (untreated), 10 APP, 10 LPP.
- **Blood:** 65 mL venous blood from a single healthy 39-year-old Caucasian male donor; divided into 30 × 2 mL EDTA-tube portions.
- **APP device:** Plasmatreat FG 5001 plasma jet (nitrogen gas). Parameters: 300 V, 19 kHz, gas flow 30 slm, pulse cycle time 100%, power 800 W, nozzle-to-implant distance 4 mm, substrate velocity 4 m/s → **treatment duration always 1 minute**.
- **LPP device:** Diener electronics Femto PCCE Zahntechnik (argon chamber). Parameters: 100% argon saturation, power 200 W, gas pressure 50 Pa → **treatment time 15 minutes**.
- Implants immersed individually in 2 mL whole blood, agitated at 50 rpm, incubated at 37°C/5% CO₂.
- Sampling at 1, 8, 24 h; RNA extraction (Chomczynski-Sacchi method), cDNA synthesis, RT-qPCR (Rotorgene 3000) for IL1-β and TNF-α, normalized to housekeeping genes (ACTB, 18S rRNA, β2M) via ΔΔCt; ≥2-fold change threshold for significance.
- Statistics: Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality test → one-way ANOVA + Bonferroni post hoc (SPSS 22.0); P≤0.05 significant.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**IL1-β:**
- t=0: no significant change, either arm.
- 1 h: APP decreased (not significant); **LPP decreased significantly (P<0.05)**.
- 8 h: control significantly increased (P<0.01); **both APP and LPP significantly decreased (P<0.01)**.
- 24 h: control significantly decreased (P<0.001) from its 8-h peak; **both APP and LPP significantly increased (P<0.05)** — reversal relative to 8 h.

**TNF-α:**
- t=0: no significant change, either arm.
- 1 h: control significantly increased (P<0.001); APP not significant; **LPP significantly decreased (P<0.05)**.
- 8 h: control still significantly elevated (P<0.001); **APP significantly decreased (P<0.05); LPP significantly decreased (P<0.05)**.
- 24 h: control slightly declined but remained significantly elevated; **both APP and LPP significantly increased (P<0.001)** — same 24-h reversal seen with IL1-β.

**No direct LPP-vs-APP statistical test is reported** — each arm is compared only to the untreated control, not to each other. The paper's own conclusion sentence ("plasma treatment leads to downregulation... beneficial in early osseointegration") describes only the early window (1–8 h); the 24-h reversal is present in the data but not foregrounded in the abstract/conclusion.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Single blood donor (n=1 human subject)** — all 30 implant-blood interactions share one person's immune background; the authors explicitly flag this and state ≥3 donors would be needed to address inter-individual coagulation variability. This is a first-in-kind proof-of-concept, not a powered comparative study.
- Study period capped at 24 h because ex vivo whole blood loses normal immune reactivity without a nutrient supply beyond that window — no longer-term or in vivo cytokine trajectory data.
- **24-h reversal not addressed mechanistically** — the Discussion focuses on the early (1–8 h) suppression and its plausible link to super-hydrophilicity/coagulation/complement-system interactions (citing Harmankaya 2012, Milleret 2011), but does not explain why both plasma arms flip to significant cytokine *increase* by 24 h.
- Device parameters (APP 1 min vs LPP 15 min) are confounded with device type — the study cannot separate "vacuum vs atmospheric" as a pure physical-mechanism variable from "1 minute vs 15 minutes" as a dose variable; the two covary completely in this design.
- Industry-adjacent funding (Oral Reconstruction Foundation material donation, DGI grant); authors declare no conflicts of interest.

## 7. Glossary

- LPP = Low-Pressure Plasma (저압/진공계 플라즈마), 15분/50Pa/아르곤 챔버 방식; APP = Atmospheric-Pressure Plasma (대기압 플라즈마), 1분/질소 제트 방식.
- IL1-β = Interleukin 1-beta; TNF-α = Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha — proinflammatory cytokines.
- RT-qPCR = Reverse Transcription quantitative Polymerase Chain Reaction (역전사 정량 PCR), used to measure mRNA expression.
- ΔΔCt = double-delta cycle threshold, the relative-quantification method used to normalize gene expression to housekeeping genes.
