## Why Ingested
Zirconia is increasingly used as a titanium alternative but is intrinsically bioinert, so this SR maps whether calcium phosphate (CaP) coatings close that osseointegration gap — the zirconia analog of the CaP-coating-on-titanium question already held in [[implants/surface/bonfante-2012-calcium-phosphate-coatings-plasma-spray]]. It also directly informs the titanium-vs-zirconia biology axis synthesized in the wiki's overview literature.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review (27 in vitro/in vivo/preclinical studies, no meta-analysis due to heterogeneity) of calcium phosphate (CaP) coatings — HA, β-TCP, OCP, biphasic/composite, ion-doped and multifunctional variants — applied to zirconia (Y-TZP) implants/discs via sol-gel, biomimetic, RF magnetron sputtering, IBAD, laser-assisted, and other deposition methods.

CaP coatings consistently increased osteoblast proliferation and ALP activity in vitro (19/27 studies) and improved bone-to-implant contact (BIC) in vivo (15/27 studies) vs uncoated zirconia — e.g., AlFarraj: 60.3% vs 45.1% (rabbit, 8 wk); Mutsuzaki: 48.3% vs 29.7% (rabbit tibia, 6 wk); Langhoff: 35.4% vs 22.5% at 2 wk narrowing to 46.6% vs 40.5% at 4 wk — with the advantage most pronounced early and attenuating over time.

Marked heterogeneity in coating composition/thickness/roughness/adhesion reporting prevented pooling, no long-term RCT confirms superior clinical outcomes for coated vs uncoated zirconia (unlike titanium, where CaP coating is already clinically established), and the authors conclude CaP-coated zirconia remains investigational pending standardized protocols and long-term follow-up.

## 세줄요약
체계적 문헌고찰(27편, in vitro/in vivo/전임상, 이질성으로 메타분석 미시행): 지르코니아(Y-TZP) 임플란트/디스크에 적용된 인산칼슘 (Calcium Phosphate, CaP) 코팅(HA, β-TCP, OCP, 복합/이온도핑/다기능 변형) — sol-gel, 생체모방(biomimetic), RF 마그네트론 스퍼터링, 이온빔보조증착(IBAD), 레이저보조 등 다양한 증착법.

CaP 코팅은 in vitro 조골세포 증식·알칼리인산분해효소 (Alkaline Phosphatase, ALP) 활성 증가(19/27편), in vivo 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 개선(15/27편)을 비코팅 지르코니아 대비 일관되게 보임 — 예: AlFarraj 60.3% vs 45.1%(토끼, 8주), Mutsuzaki 48.3% vs 29.7%(토끼 경골, 6주), Langhoff 2주차 35.4% vs 22.5%→4주차 46.6% vs 40.5%로 격차 축소; 효과는 초기에 두드러지고 시간 경과로 감쇠.

코팅 조성·두께·거칠기·접착강도 보고의 이질성이 커 메타분석 불가했고, 코팅 지르코니아의 우월한 임상 결과를 뒷받침하는 장기 RCT는 아직 없어(티타늄은 이미 임상적으로 확립) — 저자들은 표준화된 프로토콜·장기 추적 전까지 CaP 코팅 지르코니아를 "연구 단계(investigational)"로 규정.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Materials (Basel) 2025;18(19):4501
- **DOI**: 10.3390/ma18194501
- **Institution**: Wroclaw Medical University, Poland (lead: Jacek Matys et al.)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First systematic review to synthesize CaP-coating literature specifically for zirconia implants (prior SRs focused on titanium or lumped zirconia with other ceramics).
- Maps the full landscape of CaP chemistry (HA, β-TCP, OCP, BCP, ion-substituted Mg/Sr/Zn, multifunctional composites with BMP-2, FGF-2+LMWH, silver nanoparticles, antibiotic-loaded nanoparticles) against deposition method and biological outcome.
- Quantifies that early-phase osseointegration gain is the most consistent finding across in vivo models, while long-term advantage is inconsistent/narrows.
- Flags reporting heterogeneity itself (missing roughness/thickness/adhesion data in many studies) as a barrier to comparative conclusions, not just a limitation of any one study.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA), OSF-preregistered; no meta-analysis (heterogeneity too high)
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane Library (search July 2025, no publication-year limit)
- **n**: 394 records screened → 27 studies included (367 excluded at title/abstract stage)
- **Quality appraisal**: JBI checklist for quasi-experimental (non-randomized) designs, 9 items, two independent blinded reviewers, Cohen's κ = 0.82
- **Outcomes**: coating composition/thickness/roughness/adhesion strength, in vitro cell proliferation and ALP activity, in vivo BIC / bone area fraction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Quality distribution (JBI, 9 items):** 12/27 studies scored maximum (9/9), 4 scored 8, 6 scored 7, 5 scored 6.

**Deposition methods used:** RF magnetron sputtering, wet powder spraying (WPS), aerosol/ion beam-assisted deposition (IBAD), electrophoretic deposition (EPD), sol-gel, laser-assisted melting (Nd:YAG, femtosecond, Er:YAG-PLD), biomimetic precipitation in simulated body fluid (SBF) — biomimetic precipitation was the most common single approach.

**Coating thickness:** 50–200 nm (nanometric) up to 1–5 µm (most common range), up to 100 µm in press-and-sintered composite layers (Yang, Faria).

**Roughness (Ra):** 0.08 µm (sputtered CaP) to >3.5 µm (wet-sprayed TZCP); biomimetic/laser methods intermediate (~0.5–1.0 µm).

**Adhesion strength:** highest tensile ~24.5 MPa (layered HAp/TCP, ASTM C633); sputtered CaP ~38 MPa; Stefanic reported a striking method-dependent gap — β-TCP (heat-treated) 52.3 ± 3.8 MPa vs biomimetic CaP (untreated) 2.6 ± 0.4 MPa.

**In vitro (19/27 studies):** osteoblast-like cell proliferation and ALP activity consistently increased on HA/CaP-coated vs uncoated zirconia; multifunctional coatings (BMP-2, FGF-2+LMWH) showed additive/synergistic osteogenic effects. Exceptions with no significant proliferation difference: Huang, Pae, Hirano.

**In vivo BIC (15/27 studies, rabbit/rat/goat/canine/mini-pig models):**

| Study | Model | Timepoint | BIC coated | BIC uncoated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AlFarraj | Rabbit | 8 wk | Zr+HA 60.3% (Ti+HA 59.8%) | Zr 45.1% (Ti 45.5%) |
| Cheng | Rabbit | 4 wk | 55.3% | 43.9% |
| Mutsuzaki | Rabbit tibia | 6 wk | 48.3% | 29.7% |
| Langhoff | Canine/mini-pig (split-mouth) | 2 wk / 4 wk | 35.4% / 46.6% | 22.5% / 40.5% |
| Ferguson | Goat, porous zirconia scaffold | 12 wk | higher (exact % n/r) | — |
| Cruz | Canine | 1 wk / 6 wk | greater BIC/BAF, sustained to 6 wk | — |
| Teng | Canine (CaP ± BMP-2) | 3 mo | slightly higher (n.s.) | control |

**Early-vs-long-term pattern:** the CaP-coating BIC advantage is largest at the earliest timepoint and narrows with time in every study reporting ≥2 timepoints (Langhoff, Cruz, Mutsuzaki, Teng), consistent with an early-osseointegration-acceleration effect rather than a durable BIC ceiling increase.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Substantial heterogeneity in coating composition, deposition method, and outcome reporting prevented meta-analysis and blocks head-to-head ranking of CaP types.
- Predominantly short-term follow-up (weeks) — long-term coating stability under physiologic/functional loading is unassessed.
- Many included studies omit fundamental physical parameters (roughness, thickness, adhesion strength), limiting cross-study comparison.
- No long-term randomized clinical trials exist confirming superior clinical outcomes for CaP-coated vs uncoated zirconia implants — evidence base is preclinical/in vitro-dominant.
- Authors explicitly label CaP-coated zirconia "investigational" pending standardized protocols.

## 6. Related Work
- bonfante-2012 (plasma-spray CaP coatings on Ti-6Al-4V): the titanium precedent for this same coating strategy — establishes that CaP phase/roughness differences don't always translate to BIC differences even on the already-bioactive Ti substrate, a useful contrast to this SR's zirconia (bioinert substrate) findings where coating vs no-coating differences ARE consistently seen.
- bosshardt-2017 (osseointegration of Ti, Ti-alloy, zirconia — review): the underlying biology of why zirconia underperforms titanium in osseointegration, which this SR's CaP-coating strategy is trying to correct.
- shetty-2026 (Ti vs Zr implants — umbrella review): the clinical-outcome-level Ti > Zr survival/success gap that CaP coatings aim to narrow at the biological level.

## 7. Glossary
- **Y-TZP**: Yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal — the standard dental-implant zirconia ceramic.
- **CaP (calcium phosphate)**: umbrella term for bone-mineral-mimicking coating chemistries (HA, β-TCP, OCP, BCP).
- **HA (hydroxyapatite)**: the most crystalline, least resorbable common CaP coating phase; strong osteoconductivity.
- **β-TCP (β-tricalcium phosphate)**: more resorbable CaP phase, promotes dynamic bone remodeling.
- **OCP (octacalcium phosphate)**: a biomineralization precursor phase; nucleates rapidly and bonds strongly to zirconia.
- **IBAD (ion beam-assisted deposition)**: coating method producing dense, adherent nanocrystalline CaP layers at low temperature.
- **BIC (bone-to-implant contact)**: histomorphometric % of implant surface in direct contact with bone — primary in vivo osseointegration outcome.
