## Why Ingested

감염 소켓 즉시식립 기존 SR+MA ([[wiki/immediate-implant/saijeva-2020-immediate-implant-placement-non-infected-sockets]], [[wiki/immediate-implant/almugeiren-2024-placement-immediate-dental-implants-extraction]])가 인용하는 핵심 RCT "Camara et al." 의 원본 논문. 급성 치근단 감염(농양·누공·삼출물) 50례에서 즉시식립+즉각 프로비저널을 시행한 유일한 전향적 코호트 — 1년 후 역행성 치근단 주위염 0건 확인.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (n=100: 50 acute periapical infection, 50 control), University of Murcia, Spain; flapless IIP with immediate provisionalization, Biomet 3i implants, 1-year follow-up.

100% survival both groups; all peri-implant clinical parameters (bleeding, KM, MPR, DPR, MFR, PD) NS between groups (p>0.05); radiographic bone loss 0.35 vs 0.15 mm (p=0.160 NS).

Immediate implant placement with immediate provisionalization is safe in acute periapical infection sites after meticulous debridement + amoxicillin/clavulanate protocol; zero retrograde peri-implantitis at 1 year.

## 세줄요약

전향적 코호트 (n=100: 급성 치근단 감염 50명·대조 50명), 스페인 무르시아대학교; 무피판 즉시식립+즉시 프로비저널, Biomet 3i, 1년 추적.

양 군 생존율 100%; 모든 치주 임상 지수(출혈지수·각화점막·치근단 퇴축·탐침 깊이) 통계적 유의차 없음(p>0.05); 방사선 골 소실 0.35 대 0.15 mm (p=0.160 NS).

철저한 소파+아목시실린/클라불란산 프로토콜 하 급성 감염 부위 즉시식립+즉시 프로비저널 안전; 1년간 역행성 치근단 주위염 0건.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Medicina Oral Patología Oral y Cirugía Bucal 25(6):e720-e727, 2020
- **DOI**: 10.4317/medoral.23708
- **Institution**: University of Murcia & private clinic, Spain
- **Ethics**: Murcia Ethics Committee (1547/2017)
- **Study period**: May 2017 – December 2018

## 2. Key Contributions

- First prospective cohort comparing flapless IIP + **immediate prosthetic provisionalization** in acute periapical infection vs control, with matched control group
- Confirmed 100% survival and zero retrograde peri-implantitis in 50 acutely infected sites at 1 year
- Complete STROBE-compliant clinical parameter reporting: bleeding, KM, MPR, DPR, MFR, PD, and radiographic IS-BIC
- Identified acute infection etiology breakdown: infected fracture 42%, granuloma+pus 30%, failed endodontic treatment 28%
- Protocol detail: amoxicillin/clavulanate 875/125 mg ×3/day ×4 days pre-op and ×7 days post-op

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective cohort, STROBE-compliant
- **n**: 100 patients (50 acute periapical pathology, 50 control)
- **Implants**: Biomet 3i (Zimmer Biomet); flapless transmucosal; insertion torque >35 N/cm²
- **Grafting**: bovine bone substitute (Endobone®, Zimmer Biomet) in gap between implant and vestibular cortex
- **Provisionalization**: screwed resin single crown, platform-switched, immediate (day 0, no occlusion)
- **Socket debridement (test)**: meticulous curettage + 0.12% CHX gauze × 1 min + physiological saline irrigation + 0.12% CHX irrigation
- **Antibiotics**: amoxicillin/clavulanate 875/125 mg TID ×4 days pre-op + TID ×7 days post-op (clindamycin 300 mg TID for penicillin allergy)
- **Follow-up**: 1 year (definitive restoration placed at 6 months)
- **Outcomes**: clinical (BOP, KM, MPR, DPR, MFR, PD), radiographic (IS-BIC)
- **Statistics**: Student's t-test, Pearson chi-squared

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Variable | Acute group (n=50) | Control (n=50) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survival (1 yr) | 100% | 100% | — |
| Bleeding on probing (%) | 24.99 ± 4.99 | 11.52 ± 3.04 | 0.493 NS |
| KM width (mm) | 3.76 ± 0.62 | 3.50 ± 1.44 | 0.246 NS |
| MPR (mm) | 0.29 ± 0.47 | 0.15 ± 0.34 | 0.075 NS |
| DPR (mm) | 0.38 ± 0.49 | 0.28 ± 0.48 | 0.123 NS |
| MFR (mm) | 0.35 ± 0.41 | 0.22 ± 0.32 | 0.175 NS |
| PD (mm) | 3.16 ± 0.41 | 3.31 ± 0.94 | 0.322 NS |
| IS-BIC (mm) | 0.35 ± 0.51 | 0.15 ± 0.87 | 0.160 NS |

No retrograde peri-implantitis in either group at 1 year.

Implant site distribution: upper maxilla 81%; posterior 75%; most frequent sites 1.4 (15%), 1.5 (15%); diameter 4.00 mm 66%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- 1-year follow-up only; authors note >10-year follow-up needed
- No randomization (prospective cohort, not RCT)
- Single operator
- First-of-kind for flapless + immediate provisionalization protocol — no comparator study at time of publication

## 7. Glossary

- **IS-BIC**: Implant Shoulder–Bone-to-Implant Contact; vertical distance from implant shoulder to first radiographic bone contact — standard measure of crestal bone loss
- **KM**: Keratinized Mucosa; width of keratinized peri-implant mucosa in mm
- **MPR/DPR/MFR**: Mesial/Distal Papillary Recession, Mid-Facial Recession — measured via calibrated photography (ImageJ)
- **Flapless**: No mucoperiosteal flap raised; reduced trauma, reduced bone exposure
- **Platform-switching**: Abutment diameter smaller than implant platform; reduces crestal bone stress
