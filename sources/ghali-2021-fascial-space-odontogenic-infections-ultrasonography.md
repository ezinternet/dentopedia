## Why Ingested

[[oral-surgery/gargava-2022-deep-neck-space-infection-150-cases]]는 심경부 감염의 I&D·기도관리를 다루지만 어느 공간에 어떤 병기(봉와직염/농양)가 있는지 영상으로 어떻게 가리는지는 없었다. 본 논문은 치성 근막공간 감염에서 초음파와 MRI를 같은 환자에서 직접 비교하고 초음파 병기 분류(부종→봉와직염→농양 전 단계→농양)를 제시한다. 봉와직염 감별 서베일런스 (2026-10-13, 사용자 요청).

## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-center study (India; n=20 patients, 50 odontogenic fascial-space infections) comparing grayscale ultrasonography (9 MHz linear probe) with 1.5-T MRI, confirmed by percutaneous aspiration or incision-and-drainage microbiology.

Ultrasonography identified 42/50 involved spaces (84%) versus MRI 50/50 (100%); both matched for all superficial spaces, while ultrasonography missed every deep space (sublingual, masticatory, pharyngeal); both classified 16 cases as cellulitis and 4 as abscess.

Ultrasonography is a cheap, radiation-free tool for staging superficial space infection (cellulitis vs abscess) but cannot replace MRI for deep spaces; small sample and a reference standard that is itself imaging limit certainty.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 전향 연구(인도; 환자 20명, 치성 근막공간 감염 50개): 그레이스케일 초음파(9 MHz 선형 프로브)와 1.5T MRI를 비교하고, 경피 흡인 또는 절개배농 검체의 균배양으로 확인.

초음파는 50개 중 42개(84%), MRI는 50개 전부(100%) 검출; 표재 공간은 두 방법이 일치했으나 초음파는 심부 공간(설하·저작·인두)을 전부 놓침; 봉와직염 16건·농양 4건 분류는 두 방법이 동일.

초음파는 표재 공간 감염의 병기(봉와직염 vs 농양) 판정에 저렴하고 방사선이 없는 도구지만 심부 공간은 MRI를 대체하지 못함; 표본이 작고 기준표준이 영상 자체라 확실성에 한계.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: World Journal of Clinical Cases 9(3):573–580, 2021 (published 2021-01-26)
- Design: prospective comparative imaging study, Al-Badar Dental College and Hospital, Kalaburagi, India
- Full text: PMC (PMC7829733), read in full

## 2. Key Contributions

- Direct same-patient comparison of ultrasonography and MRI for odontogenic fascial-space spread.
- Gives an echogenicity-based four-stage scheme for ultrasonography: edematous change, cellulitis, pre-abscess, abscess.
- Reports which spaces ultrasonography can and cannot see (superficial vs deep).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Patients: 20 with acute fascial swelling of odontogenic origin, clinically and radiographically diagnosed; non-odontogenic cases excluded; consent and ethics approval documented.
- Ultrasonography: 9 MHz linear array, transverse and axial sections over the swelling, on-screen calipers; echogenicity read against neighboring tissue.
- Stage definitions (from the paper): edematous change = isoechoic with more fluid; cellulitis = hyperechoic (more inflammation); pre-abscess = mixed hyper/hypoechoic; abscess = anechoic, single or multiple.
- MRI: 1.5-T Siemens, axial and coronal planes, compared with the non-infected side.
- Confirmation: microbiological testing of material from percutaneous aspiration or incision and drainage; SPSS analysis.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 50 involved spaces in 20 patients. Ultrasonography detected 42 (84%); MRI detected 50 (100%).
- Table 1 (ultrasonography = MRI): buccal 15, canine 7, submasseteric 14, submandibular 6 (superficial; 42 total). MRI only: sublingual 3, masticatory 4, pharyngeal 1 (deep; 8 total). Most frequent: buccal 30%, submasseteric 28%.
- Table 2 (ultrasonography = MRI): cellulitis 16 (80%), abscess 4 (20%).
- Authors report for ultrasonography sensitivity 84%, specificity 100%, PPV 90%, NPV 100%; MRI 100% on all four.
- Management: all four abscess cases were drained (pus verified the imaging); the 16 cellulitis patients were treated with antibiotics alone.
- Culture: aerobes 65.7% of growth; flora polymicrobial.
- Authors cite earlier work: clinical exam alone correct 68% of the time, 70% with ultrasonography added.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=20), one center; the unit of analysis is the fascial space (50), not the patient.
- Internal inconsistencies in the text: results say ultrasonography detected "all 40 superficial spaces" while Table 1 lists 42 superficial spaces; PPV 90% is reported alongside specificity 100%, which cannot both hold if there were no false positives. Treat the sensitivity/specificity/PPV figures as unreliable; the 42/50 vs 50/50 counts and the table are the checkable data.
- Only four abscesses, so the cellulitis-vs-abscess agreement rests on very few cases; MRI served as the comparator and no blinding is described.
- Ultrasonography missed every deep space; the authors conclude MRI is superior there.

## 6. Related Work

- Authors cite prior studies finding ultrasonography unreliable for deep spaces but useful for superficial-space infection and staging, and one study showing ultrasonography defines abscess borders and depth better than it characterizes cellulitis.

## 7. Glossary

- USG: ultrasonography
- MRI: magnetic resonance imaging; STIR: short tau inversion recovery
- Fascial space: anatomic compartment bounded by fascia where odontogenic infection spreads (buccal, canine, submasseteric, submandibular, sublingual, masticatory, pharyngeal)
- Echogenicity: brightness of tissue on ultrasound relative to neighbors (hyper-, iso-, hypo-, anechoic)
