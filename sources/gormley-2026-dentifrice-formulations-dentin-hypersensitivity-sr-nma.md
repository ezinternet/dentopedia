## Why Ingested

The wiki's toothpaste evidence for dentin hypersensitivity (DH) was a formulation map plus single trials; this 93-RCT network meta-analysis supplies the first comparative ranking of dentifrice actives. It turns the descriptive landscape of [[dentin-hypersensitivity/martins-2022-desensitizing-toothpastes-formulations-scoping-review]] into effect sizes and independently agrees with the stannous result of [[dentin-hypersensitivity/qiu-2026-stannous-fluoride-toothpaste-dentin-hypersensitivity-rct]]. → [[dentin-hypersensitivity/gormley-2026-dentifrice-formulations-dentin-hypersensitivity-sr-nma]]

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and frequentist network meta-analysis (93 RCTs, 9548 adults with dentin hypersensitivity; 47 studies/4636 participants in the 2-week network) comparing self-applied dentifrices against a benchmark fluoride dentifrice (RoB 2, CINeMA certainty; abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

Stannous fluoride and arginine dentifrices gave the largest reductions: Schiff MD −0.85 (95% CI −1.08 to −0.62; high certainty) and −0.78 (−1.02 to −0.54; low); Yeaple tactile MD +14.87 g (10.24–19.50; moderate) and +9.78 g (4.69–14.86; low); other formulations showed small-to-moderate effects, generally low certainty.

Stannous fluoride or arginine dentifrices are proposed as first-line self-care for dentin hypersensitivity, chosen by patient preference, tolerability and availability rather than expected superiority; only the stannous evaporative-cold result is high certainty.

## 세줄요약

성인 상아질 과민증(Dentin Hypersensitivity, DH) 대상 RCT 93편(9,548명; 2주 네트워크 메타분석 47편·4,636명)을 벤치마크 불소 치약과 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 네트워크 메타분석(Network Meta-Analysis, NMA; RoB 2, CINeMA 확실성 평가; 초록만 확인 — 전문 미확보).

불화주석(Stannous Fluoride, SnF₂)과 아르기닌 치약의 감소폭이 가장 컸다: Schiff 평균차 −0.85 (95% CI −1.08~−0.62; 높은 확실성)·−0.78 (−1.02~−0.54; 낮음), Yeaple 촉각역치 +14.87 g (10.24~19.50; 중등도)·+9.78 g (4.69~14.86; 낮음); 그 외 제형은 소~중등도 효과이며 대체로 확실성 낮음.

SnF₂ 또는 아르기닌 치약을 1차 자가관리로 권고하되 우월성 기대보다 환자 선호·내약성·구입성으로 선택; 높은 확실성은 SnF₂의 증발냉각(Schiff) 결과뿐.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Updates a previous review of self-applied dentifrices for DH (search Nov 2022–Jul 2025 plus records from the earlier review).
- Network meta-analysis ranks dentifrice actives against a benchmark fluoride dentifrice at 2 weeks across evaporative cold, tactile and thermal cold outcomes.
- Concludes stannous fluoride and arginine dentifrices are first-line self-care options.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Abstract-only — full text not retrieved. RCTs of self-applied dentifrices in adults with diagnosed DH; Medline, EMBASE, Cochrane Library (OvidSP). Random-effects frequentist NMA for outcomes at 2 weeks; risk of bias by RoB 2; certainty by CINeMA. 93 studies (9548 participants) included; 47 studies (4636 participants) in the NMAs.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome (2 wk, vs benchmark fluoride) | Stannous fluoride | Arginine |
|---|---|---|
| Schiff (evaporative cold) MD | −0.85 (−1.08, −0.62), high certainty | −0.78 (−1.02, −0.54), low certainty |
| Yeaple (tactile) MD | 14.87 (10.24, 19.50), moderate | 9.78 (4.69, 14.86), low |

Other formulations: small-to-moderate effects, generally low certainty. Per-formulation estimates for those other actives are not available from the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only: no access to per-formulation estimates, heterogeneity, risk-of-bias distribution or the number of trials per node.
- Network restricted to 2-week outcomes (47 of 93 studies); long-term durability is not addressed in the abstract.
- Arginine and most other actives rated low certainty; the authors state choice should rest on preference, tolerability and availability rather than superiority.

## 7. Glossary

- **NMA**: network meta-analysis. **CINeMA**: Confidence in Network Meta-Analysis framework. **Schiff**: 0–3 evaporative air-blast score. **Yeaple**: tactile threshold probe (grams; higher = less sensitive).
