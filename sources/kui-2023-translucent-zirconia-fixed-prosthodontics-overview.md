## Three-line Summary

Integrative review (Biomedicines 2023;11:3116) covering optical (translucency, opalescence, fluorescence), mechanical (flexural strength, fracture toughness, aging/LTD), adhesive (surface treatment protocols, cement selection), and implant-restoration aspects of 3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, and 5Y-TZP translucent zirconia in fixed prosthodontics.

3Y-TZP: highest strength (~1,200 MPa), lowest translucency, best LTD resistance; 4Y-TZP: intermediate (~800 MPa), clinically acceptable esthetics; 5Y-TZP: highest translucency but lowest strength (~500 MPa), limited to low-load anterior cases; air abrasion + modern self-adhesive resin cements achieve adequate adhesion for all types.

Monolithic translucent zirconia (3Y/4Y-TZP) now displaces veneered zirconia for posterior single crowns and implant superstructures; clinical selection requires matching yttria content to functional load and esthetic region; long-term aging data for 4Y/5Y-TZP still limited.

## 세줄요약

투명 지르코니아(3Y/4Y/5Y-TZP) 광학·기계·접착·임플란트 적용 종합 고찰(Biomedicines 2023): 3Y-TZP 최고 강도(~1,200 MPa)·최저 투명도, 5Y-TZP 최고 투명도·최저 강도(~500 MPa).

에어 어브레이션 + 현대 자가접착 레진 시멘트로 모든 투명 지르코니아 접착력 개선 가능; 모놀리식 디자인이 베니어드 지르코니아 대체 — 치핑 위험 제거.

임상 선택 원칙: 이트리아 함량 = 기능 하중 + 심미 요구 맞춤; 4Y/5Y-TZP 장기 에이징 데이터 아직 제한적 — 고응력 구치부에 5Y-TZP 사용 주의.

## One-line Summary

Integrative review (Biomedicines 2023) covering light properties, mechanical attributes, adhesive strategies, and implant-restoration use of translucent zirconia types (3Y-TZP, 4Y-TZP, 5Y-TZP), with focus on strength-esthetics trade-offs.

## 한줄요약

투명 지르코니아(3Y/4Y/5Y-TZP) 광학·기계·접착·임플란트 적용 종합 고찰: 이트리아 증가로 투명도↑·강도↓ 트레이드오프 명확, 표면처리+현대 접착제로 접착력 개선 가능.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Comprehensive characterization of 3Y-TZP vs 4Y-TZP vs 5Y-TZP strength-translucency spectrum
- Light-conducting properties: translucent zirconia achieves natural dental appearance through unique optical properties
- Fracture resistance evaluation under simulated physiological conditions (cyclic loading)
- Adhesive strategies: surface treatments + modern adhesives essential for durable bond to translucent zirconia
- Implant-supported restorations: translucent zirconia suitable for precision, reliability, aesthetic adaptability

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design:** Narrative/integrative review
- **Scope:** Fixed prosthodontics applications — single crowns, FDPs, implant-supported restorations
- **Topics:** Optical properties, mechanical properties, surface treatment, adhesion protocols, clinical performance

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Zirconia types — strength-translucency trade-off:**
| Type | Yttria content | Translucency | Flexural strength | Indication |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3Y-TZP | 3 mol% | Low | Highest (~1,000–1,200 MPa) | Posterior high-stress |
| 4Y-TZP | 4 mol% | Medium | Medium (~700–900 MPa) | Anterior single crowns |
| 5Y-TZP | 5 mol% | High | Lowest (~400–600 MPa) | Anterior esthetics, single |

- Fracture resistance: commendable under simulated physiological conditions; cyclic loading reduces 4Y/5Y more than 3Y
- Adhesion: sandblasting (Al₂O₃ 50–110 µm) + MDP-containing resin cement remains standard surface treatment protocol
- Marginal fit: CAD/CAM zirconia achieves clinically acceptable marginal adaptation (comparable to metal-ceramic)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Integrative overview without systematic search/meta-analysis
- Long-term clinical data for translucent zirconia (≥5 years) still limited compared to opaque 3Y-TZP
- Need for standardized adhesive protocols specific to translucent subtypes
