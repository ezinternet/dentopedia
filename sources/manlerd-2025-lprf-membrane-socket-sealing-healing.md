## Why Ingested

L-PRF/A-PRF의 소켓 실링 소프트티슈 효과를 다룬 기존 페이지들([[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/kollati-2019-cerabone-prf-socket-preservation-rct]], [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/alrayyes-2022-prf-socket-preservation-smokers-rct]])은 PRF를 xenograft/allograft와 병용하거나 흡연자 특수 집단에 국한했다. 본 RCT(n=45)는 L-PRF **단독**을 콜라겐 스펀지·자연치유와 직접 비교한 일반 집단 3군 RCT로, "PRF가 wound closure 자체를 가속하는가"라는 핵심 질문에 negative 답을 준다는 점에서 기존 근거를 보강하면서도 중요한 한계를 명확히 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective 3-arm RCT (n=45 extraction sockets, Mahidol University Bangkok) compared socket sealing with L-PRF membrane, collagen sponge, or spontaneous healing over 21 days.

L-PRF significantly improved soft tissue healing index at day 7 (p=0.002) and reduced pain at day 5 (p=0.026–0.036) vs both comparators, but showed no significant benefit in wound margin distance reduction or wound closure area reduction (L-PRF vs collagen sponge) at any time point.

L-PRF offers short-term symptom relief and faster early epithelial healing appearance but does not accelerate actual wound closure — clinical value is limited to first-week comfort, not enhanced soft-tissue regeneration.

## 세줄요약

전향적 3군 RCT (n=45 발치와, 마히돌대학 방콕): L-PRF막 실링 vs 콜라겐 스펀지 실링 vs 자연치유를 21일간 비교.

L-PRF는 7일째 연조직 치유지수(p=0.002)와 5일째 통증(p=0.026–0.036)에서 유의하게 우수했으나, 창상 변연 거리 감소율 및 창상 폐쇄 면적 감소율(L-PRF vs 콜라겐 스펀지)에서는 어느 시점에서도 유의한 차이가 없었다.

L-PRF는 초기 1주간의 증상 완화와 외견상 빠른 상피화에는 도움이 되지만 실제 창상 폐쇄 속도를 가속하지는 않는다 — 임상적 가치는 연조직 재생 촉진이 아니라 초기 편안함에 국한된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the few RCTs to compare L-PRF **alone** (no bone graft) directly against collagen sponge and spontaneous healing in a general (non-smoker-restricted) extraction population.
- Adds a novel secondary outcome — digital intraoral-scanner-based 3D wound closure area reduction (TRIOS3 + GOM Inspect STL superimposition) — comparing L-PRF vs collagen sponge, a more objective measure than 2D probe-based wound margin distance.
- Explicitly separates "soft tissue healing appearance / comfort" (where L-PRF wins early) from "actual wound closure kinetics" (where L-PRF shows no advantage) — a distinction often blurred in prior PRF socket-sealing literature.
- Uses the validated Landry healing index and an 11-point NRS pain diary (4x/day) for granular postoperative symptom tracking.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Design**: Prospective, randomized, controlled, parallel, 3-arm clinical trial (Dec 2022–Aug 2024).

**Sample**: 45 extraction sockets (15/group), final analysis n=48 teeth (3 collagen-sponge dropouts due to infection/loss-to-follow-up replaced under original allocation protocol; 3 excluded with no outcome data). Single blinded surgeon performed all extractions; allocation concealed via opaque envelopes assigned by an independent third party; surgeon and statistician blinded to allocation.

**Inclusion**: ≥18y, ASA I/II, tooth requiring extraction (periodontal/caries/endodontic/fracture/restorative/orthodontic reasons), intact buccal bone plate confirmed intraoperatively, adjacent teeth or implant present.

**Exclusion**: bone-metabolism-affecting drugs (bisphosphonates), malignancy/radiotherapy/chemotherapy history (5y), acute infection, uncontrolled periodontal disease, smoking >10 cigarettes/day, pregnancy/lactation.

**Groups**:
1. **L-PRF**: single (or additional for large sockets) L-PRF membrane layer over socket, crisscross suture. L-PRF prepared from 10 mL venous blood, Intra-Spin system, 2,700 rpm × 12 min, yellow middle layer compressed 5 min on light metal plate.
2. **Collagen sponge**: Collaplug (Zimmer Dental) inserted into socket, crisscross suture.
3. **Control**: crisscross suture approximating wound margins only, spontaneous healing (unsealed).

**Outcome measures**:
- Primary: wound margin distance reduction % (buccolingual + mesiodistal, UNC-15 probe, days 0/7/14/21), Landry soft tissue healing index (1–5, days 7/14/21), postoperative pain (11-point NRS, 4x/day through day 7, compared at days 1/3/5/7).
- Secondary (L-PRF vs collagen sponge only): wound closure area reduction % via intraoral scanner (TRIOS3) + STL superimposition (GOM Inspect) at days 0/7/14/21.

**Statistics**: One-way ANOVA + LSD post-hoc for primary outcomes; independent t-test for secondary outcome; Fisher's exact / Pearson's chi-square for categorical covariates; SPSS v18; α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | L-PRF | Collagen sponge | Control | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Healing index, day 7 | 3.93 ± 0.70 | 3.20 ± 0.56 | 3.20 ± 0.56 | 0.002 |
| Healing index, day 14 | 4.60 ± 0.51 | 4.33 ± 0.62 | 4.13 ± 0.52 | 0.077 (NS) |
| Healing index, day 21 | 5.00 ± 0.00 | 4.73 ± 0.80 | 4.87 ± 0.35 | 0.359 (NS) |
| Pain, day 5 | 0.07 ± 0.26 | 0.63 ± 0.85 | 0.60 ± 0.75 | 0.045 |
| Buccolingual reduction %, day 21 | 66.01 ± 13.89 | 67.16 ± 19.99 | 63.00 ± 24.92 | 0.843 (NS) |
| Mesiodistal reduction %, day 21 | 38.24 ± 20.98 | 47.78 ± 18.61 | 41.96 ± 20.97 | 0.435 (NS) |
| Wound closure area reduction %, day 21 (L-PRF vs collagen sponge only) | 80.12 ± 10.27 | 80.38 ± 13.97 | — | 0.954 (NS) |

- L-PRF significantly superior on day-7 healing index (vs both comparators, p=0.002, 95% CI [0.28,1.18]) and day-5 pain (vs collagen sponge p=0.026, vs control p=0.036).
- **No significant difference** among any group in wound margin distance reduction (buccolingual or mesiodistal) at days 7/14/21.
- **No significant difference** between L-PRF and collagen sponge in 3D wound closure area reduction at any timepoint — this is the key negative secondary-outcome finding.
- By day 21, healing-index and pain differences converge across all groups (ceiling effect).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Unbalanced tooth-position distribution across groups (p=0.001 for tooth position × treatment group), largely from collagen-sponge dropout re-recruitment — potential confounding.
- No preoperative 3D/CBCT assessment of buccal bone thickness.
- UNC-15 probe measurement without fixed landmarks/stabilized stents — precision limited despite triplicate measurement (Cohen's kappa >0.847).
- Only socket **sealing** was tested (no socket filling/GBR) — findings apply to early (21-day) soft-tissue/symptom outcomes only, not to long-term ridge dimensional preservation or bone regeneration.
- Complete-case analysis after dropouts may introduce bias and reduce power.
- Authors explicitly state: findings reflect "short-term benefits... symptom relief, rather than ridge preservation or improved tissue regeneration."

## 6. Related Work

- Cited literature agreeing with null wound-margin-distance finding: Asmael et al, Yerke et al (PRF vs spontaneous healing, no significant buccolingual difference at 21 days); Suttapreyasri & Leepong (mesiodistal).
- Contrasting finding: Alrayyes et al (2022, our [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/alrayyes-2022-prf-socket-preservation-smokers-rct]]) reported a significant mesiodistal difference between A-PRF and collagen plug at day 21 in smokers — attributed by the current authors to differing measurement tools/sites (digital caliper + plaster model vs intraoral UNC-15 probe).
- Pain-reduction concordance: Temmerman et al, Marenzi et al, De Almeida Barros Mourão et al, and a Moraschini et al systematic review, all supporting PRF's early pain-reduction effect via socket sealing/debris exclusion and leukocyte immunomodulation.
- Wound-area comparison: Aravena et al (no 3D volumetric difference, L-PRF vs spontaneous healing); Ghanaati et al (larger wound size in spontaneous healing vs PRF). Guo et al (murine): collagen sponge delayed early socket healing by reducing cell density/growth factor concentration — offered as possible explanation for collagen sponge's inferior (but not significantly different in area) performance vs L-PRF.

## 7. Glossary

- **L-PRF (Leukocyte- and Platelet-Rich Fibrin)**: second-generation autologous platelet concentrate prepared without anticoagulant, containing leukocytes, platelets, and growth factors (TGF-β1, VEGF, PDGF, IGF, BMP) within a fibrin matrix.
- **ARP (Alveolar Ridge Preservation)**: interventions applied at time of extraction to limit post-extraction ridge dimensional change.
- **Socket sealing**: an ARP sub-technique covering the socket orifice (vs socket filling with graft material or full GBR) to protect the clot and guide soft-tissue closure.
- **Landry healing index**: 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent) clinical scale scoring tissue color, bleeding on palpation, granulation tissue presence, and incision margin epithelialization.
- **NRS (Numerical Rating Scale)**: 11-point (0–10) patient-reported pain scale.
- **STL (Stereolithography) superimposition**: digital 3D scan alignment technique used here to quantify wound area reduction over time.
