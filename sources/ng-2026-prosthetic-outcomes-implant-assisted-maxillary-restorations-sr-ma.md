## Why Ingested

위키의 무치악 상악 근거는 임플란트 개수([[complete-denture/difrancesco-2021-maxillary-overdentures-four-six-implants-sr-ma]], 4 vs 6), 장기 생존([[complete-denture/lan-2025-long-term-four-implant-supported]], IOD vs IFR), 환자보고결과([[complete-denture/abou-ayash-2023-implant-supported-fixed-complete-dentures]], fixed가 PRO 우위 아님)로 나뉘어 있었으나, **보철 유형별 기술적/보철 합병증 부담(complication burden)을 pool한 계정**이 비어 있었다 — 실제 유지관리 비용·체어타임을 좌우하는 축이다. 본 SR+MA(Ng 2026)는 ID-COSM lifespan domain에 맞춰 complication-free survival과 합병증 subtype을 유형별로 정량화해 그 공백을 메운다. 파국적 실패(보철·임플란트 소실)는 드물지만(<6%), complication-free 생존은 중간(~62%)이라는 "낮은 소실 ≠ 낮은 유지부담" 대비를 보강.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 20 prospective studies / RCTs (913 patients; 542 fixed, 371 removable; 4414 standard implants; median 5-yr, up to 10-yr follow-up) pooling prosthetic and technical outcomes of full-arch implant-assisted restorations in the edentulous maxilla, framed by ID-COSM lifespan domains.

Catastrophic failure was uncommon — overall prosthesis loss 4.6% (1.9% at 3–6 yr) and pooled implant loss ~5.5% — but complication-free prosthesis survival was only 61.9% and patient-level complication-free implant survival 46.5%; fixed vs removable were similar over comparable periods, though the dominant technical complication differed (fixed: acrylic veneer fracture ~41%; overdenture: denture-base/tooth repair & reline ~91%).

Both modalities are predictable for survival but carry a high, ongoing maintenance burden, so patient counselling and a structured maintenance program matter more than the fixed-vs-removable choice itself (evidence certainty low to very low, high heterogeneity).

## 세줄요약

무치악 상악 전악 임플란트 보철의 보철·기술적 결과를 pool한 SR+MA(전향연구/RCT 20편, 913명 — 고정성 542·가철성 371, 표준 임플란트 4414개, 중앙값 5년·최장 10년), ID-COSM lifespan domain 기준.

파국적 실패는 드물었지만(전체 보철소실 4.6%, 3–6년 1.9%; 임플란트 소실 ~5.5%) complication-free 보철 생존은 61.9%, 환자수준 complication-free 임플란트 생존은 46.5%에 불과했고, 유사 관찰기간에서 고정성과 가철성은 비슷했으나 주된 기술적 합병증이 달랐다(고정성: 아크릴 비니어 파절 ~41%; 오버덴쳐: 의치상/인공치 수리·리라인 ~91%).

두 방식 모두 생존 측면에서는 예측 가능하나 지속적 유지관리 부담이 크므로, 고정성 vs 가철성 선택 자체보다 환자 교육과 체계적 유지관리 프로그램이 더 중요하다(근거 확실성 low~very low, 이질성 높음).

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Implants Research 2026; 37(6): 677–692
- **DOI**: 10.1111/clr.70116 (Open Access, CC-BY)
- **Type**: Systematic review + meta-analysis of proportions (random-effects, Freeman–Tukey double arcsine)
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD420251001443; PRISMA 2020
- **Search**: MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL; Jan 2000–Feb 2025 (final search 3 Mar 2025)
- **Design filter**: RCTs + prospective clinical studies only, ≥5 patients, ≥3-yr follow-up. Retrospective/case series/in vitro/animal/reviews excluded.
- **Risk of bias**: RoB2 (RCTs), ROBINS-I V2 (cohort/CCT); certainty by adapted GRADE.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. First comprehensive pooled account of **prosthetic + technical complication burden** in the edentulous maxilla mapped to the 2023 ID-COSM lifespan domain (prosthesis loss, implant loss, complication-free prosthesis/implant survival, technical complications, MBL, PROMs).
2. Separates **"survival" from "complication-free survival"** — showing loss is rare but complications are frequent, and quantifying the gap.
3. Provides **complication-subtype rates stratified by restoration type** (fixed one-piece full-arch vs overdenture), the axis that drives maintenance cost and chair time.
4. Shows **fixed and removable are broadly equivalent** for the headline outcomes over comparable follow-up, shifting the decision toward patient factors/maintenance rather than modality superiority.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **20 studies, 913 patients** completing ≥3 yr. Designs: **13 RCTs, 4 cohort, 3 controlled clinical trials**. Median follow-up 5 yr; longest 10 yr (Slot 2022/2023). Mean age 61.6 ± 3.6 yr.
- **Modalities**: 8 studies implant overdentures, 13 studies one-piece fixed full-arch (Ferrigno 2002 contributed both arms). 542 fixed vs 371 removable patients.
- **Implants**: 4414 standard endosseous implants; 4–8 per arch. When 4 implants supported a fixed full-arch it typically followed the **All-on-4 concept with tilted distal implants**. Nobel Biocare (6), Straumann (5), Astra Tech (5) most common.
- **Loading**: conventional 13 studies, immediate 5, early 3 (1 NR). Overdentures always conventional; immediate/early loading only in fixed full-arch.
- **Materials**: most fixed one-piece = **PMMA on metal framework** (few PFM; one PMMA-on-PEEK, Kortam 2022). Overdenture retention: Dolder/Hader **bar 336 prostheses**, locator 42, telescopic 10; all cast-metal frameworks.
- **Statistics**: random-effects meta-analysis of single proportions; within-patient clustering handled with ICC = 0.07 (sensitivity 0.05–0.30); heterogeneity via τ² (DerSimonian-Laird) and I²; Egger's test for prosthesis loss.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Primary — prosthesis loss (18 studies)**: overall **4.6% (95% CI 1.2–10.1), I²=88.9%**.
- 3–6 yr (18 studies): **1.9% (0.8–3.6)**; ≥10 yr (2 studies): **47.9% (34.5–61.5)**.
- By type (3–6 yr): overdenture **1.8% (0.2–4.9)**, fixed one-piece **2.1% (0.6–4.4)**.
- By loading: immediate 3.0% (0.0–12.1), conventional (fixed) 1.7% (0.4–4.1), overdenture conventional 2.5% (0.3–6.7). Egger's z=0.91, p=0.361 (no significant small-study effect).

**Complication-free prosthesis survival (12 studies)**: **61.9% (48.4–74.5), I²=89.5%**; overdenture 60.2%, fixed 62.2% — i.e., ~38% experienced ≥1 complication requiring intervention.

**Complication-free implant survival, patient-level (5 studies)**: **46.5% (21.1–72.9), I²=93.4%**; overdenture 51.6%, fixed 64.8%; 3–6 yr 60.5% vs ≥10 yr 26.1%. **Implant-level (3 studies): 72.2%** — the patient-vs-implant gap reflects that one complicated implant flags the whole patient.

**Technical complications (15 studies)**: overall **62.9% (42.4–81.2)**; overdenture 51.4%, fixed 56.7%.
- *Fixed one-piece*: **veneering acrylic fracture 41.4% (23.1–61.0)** = dominant; PFM porcelain fracture only 2.8%; screw issues 7.0%; abutment 2.1%; framework fracture 0.7% (Ti cast) to 3.8% (laser-welded Ti).
- *Overdenture*: **denture base/occlusal repairs or relines 91.2% (56.6–100)**; remakes 21.3%; occlusal readjustments 11.9%; bar fractures 10%; screw/abutment 1.8–3.6%.

**Implant loss (clustering-adjusted)**: fixed **5.5% (2.9–8.8)**, overdenture **5.6% (2.7–9.4)** — not correlated with prosthesis type.

**Marginal bone loss (3–6 yr)**: fixed **0.78 mm (0.34–1.21)**, overdenture **0.78 mm (0.55–1.02)**; ≥10 yr overdenture 0.64 mm (0.43–0.85). All **< 0.8 mm**, below the peri-implantitis threshold.

**PROMs (9 studies)**: VAS/OHIP-14/OHIP-49 — QoL consistently improved after treatment but assessment heterogeneity precluded pooling.

**GRADE certainty**: **low** for prosthesis loss, complication-free prosthesis survival, technical complications; **very low** for complication-free implant survival and implant loss (downgraded mainly for inconsistency/high heterogeneity).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Considerable heterogeneity (implant systems, loading, surgical/prosthetic designs); GRADE penalizes observational designs regardless of rigor.
- **Minor vs major complication** distinction (ID-COSM: one vs multiple appointments) could not be reliably applied — most pre-2023 studies lack the granularity; threshold is practice-dependent.
- Peri-implant mucositis/peri-implantitis likely underreported, biasing complication-free implant survival upward.
- Influence of **cantilevers, framework/veneer thickness, bruxism, CAD/CAM materials (monolithic zirconia, PEEK), and locator systems** could not be evaluated — most fixed = PMMA-on-metal, most overdentures = bar-retained.
- Only 2 studies reached ≥10 yr (both overdentures) — long-term fixed data sparse; ≥10 yr prosthesis-loss estimate (47.9%) rests on very few studies.
- Immediate loading of the maxilla still needs well-designed RCTs (point estimate slightly higher than conventional but CIs wide/overlapping — not evidence of inferiority).

## 6. Related Work

- Lan 2025 (maxillary 4-implant IOD vs IFR, ≥5 yr) — Ng extends this by adding the complication-burden / ID-COSM lifespan dimension.
- DiFrancesco 2021 (4 vs 6 splinted implants, maxillary OD) — Ng supports "≥4 implants sufficient" for both OD (All-on-4) and fixed.
- Abou-Ayash 2023 (fixed complete dentures, PROs) — Ng reinforces that fixed is not clearly superior; here fixed ≈ removable on headline outcomes.
- Del Fabbro 2022, Abdunabi 2019 (full-arch tilted/axial, immediately loaded maxilla) — cited for favorable survival and the immediate-loading evidence gap.

## 7. Glossary

- **ID-COSM**: Implant Dentistry Core Outcome Set and Measurement (Tonetti 2023) — standardized outcome domains; "lifespan domain" = survival/complications.
- **Complication-free survival**: time from prosthesis delivery to the first complication requiring intervention (distinct from mere survival/loss).
- **One-piece fixed full-arch**: single-unit screw-retained full-arch fixed prosthesis (vs segmented).
- **All-on-4**: 4 implants (2 axial anterior + 2 tilted distal) supporting a fixed full-arch.
- **Dolder/Hader bar**: splinted bar attachment for implant overdentures.
- **MBL**: marginal (crestal) bone loss around implants.
- **PROMs**: patient-reported outcome measures (VAS satisfaction, OHIP QoL).
