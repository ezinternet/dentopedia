## Why Ingested

[[resin-bonding/dhawan-2026-bonding-total-self-etch-fiberpost]] established that adhesive-system choice (self-etch vs total-etch vs self-adhesive) drives large push-out bond strength differences for glass fiber posts, but held post surface conditioning constant. This paper crosses the other axis: it fixes silane use and varies the **post-surface conditioning method** (none / 24% hydrogen peroxide / Nd:YVO4 laser) crossed with **cement class** (self-adhesive dual-cure vs conventional dual-cure), and additionally reports canal-third-specific (cervical/middle/apical) bond strength trends — data the Dhawan paper does not break out. It extends the fiber-post bonding evidence base with a novel, potentially safer laser-based conditioning alternative to the traditionally used hydrogen peroxide.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro push-out study: 75 glass fiber posts from 60 decoronated premolars randomized to 3×2 factorial design crossing surface conditioning (none / 24% H₂O₂-silane / Nd:YVO₄ laser-silane) with cement type (self-adhesive dual-cure vs conventional dual-cure), with push-out bond strength (PBS) measured at cervical, middle, and apical thirds after 1-week storage + 10,000 thermocycles.

Nd:YVO₄ laser conditioning produced the highest surface roughness (Ra 1.32±0.22 µm, not significantly different from H₂O₂ group 1.28±0.18 µm, p>0.05) and bond strengths comparable to hydrogen peroxide (laser+conventional cement cervical 11.26 MPa vs H₂O₂+conventional 11.47 MPa, p>0.05), with maximum PBS >11 MPa well exceeding the ~5 MPa clinical threshold.

Opposite canal-third trends by cement class: conventional dual-cure cement PBS declined cervical→apical (11.26→7.32 MPa with laser), while self-adhesive cement PBS increased cervical→apical (9.56→11.23 MPa with laser), suggesting cement chemistry drives axial stress distribution despite identical post conditioning.

## 세줄요약

In vitro push-out 연구: 소구치 60개에서 얻은 글래스 파이버 포스트 75개를 3×2 요인설계(표면처리: 무처리/24% 과산화수소-실란/Nd:YVO₄ 레이저-실란 × 시멘트: 자가접착형 이중경화 vs 전통형 이중경화)로 무작위배치, 1주 저장 후 10,000회 열주기 후 치경·중간·치근단부에서 push-out 결합강도 측정.

Nd:YVO₄ 레이저군이 최고 표면조도(Ra 1.32±0.22 µm, H₂O₂군 1.28±0.18 µm와 유의차 없음, p>0.05)를 나타냈고, 결합강도는 과산화수소군과 유사(레이저+전통시멘트 치경부 11.26 MPa vs H₂O₂+전통 11.47 MPa, p>0.05); 최대 결합강도 >11 MPa는 임상 기준 ~5 MPa를 크게 초과.

전통형 시멘트(CDCRC)는 치경부→치근단 결합강도 감소(레이저 11.26→7.32 MPa), 자가접착형 시멘트(SADCRC)는 반대로 치경부→치근단 증가(9.56→11.23 MPa)로 보였으므로, 포스트 표면처리가 동일해도 시멘트 성질이 축방향 응력 분포를 결정함을 시사.

- **Title**: Nd:YVO4 Laser versus Hydrogen Peroxide for Glass Fiber Post Conditioning: Comparative Analysis of Bond Strength and Surface Roughness with Self-Adhesive and Conventional Dual-Cure Resin Cements
- **Author**: Dania El Azzouni (Dept of Oral and Maxillofacial Prosthodontics, King Abdulaziz University, Jeddah)
- **Journal**: Pakistan Journal of Medical Sciences, 2026;42(6):1502–1507
- **DOI**: 10.12669/pjms.42.6.15983 | **PMID**: 42369919 | **PMCID**: PMC13309829
- **Study type**: In vitro laboratory study (push-out bond strength + surface roughness)
- **Ethics/setting**: King Abdulaziz University FC-741-2025 (approved June 23, 2025); study conducted July 1 – October 30, 2025

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study demonstrating Nd:YVO4 laser as a surface conditioning alternative to hydrogen peroxide for glass fiber posts, with comparable roughness and push-out bond strength outcomes and no oxidative/chemical residue risk.
- Factorial design crossing conditioning method with cement class (self-adhesive vs conventional dual-cure), broken out by canal third (cervical/middle/apical) — shows opposite bond-strength gradients for the two cement classes along the canal.
- Establishes that self-adhesive dual-cure resin cement (SADCRC) performs satisfactorily (and best in the apical third) across all conditioning groups, supporting simplified one-step cementation protocols.
- Bond strengths achieved (>11 MPa in the best groups) substantially exceed the ~5 MPa clinical threshold cited for withstanding masticatory forces.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Sample**: 60 single-rooted premolars, straight canals (curvature ≤20°), no caries/cracks/resorption/prior RCT; decoronated to 15 mm root length, embedded in acrylic resin.
- **Endodontic treatment**: ProTaper Universal rotary shaping to F3, 2.5% NaOCl + 17% EDTA final rinse, single-cone obturation (F3 gutta-percha + AH Plus sealer), Cavit access seal.
- **Post-space prep**: Gutta-percha removed to 10 mm with Peeso reamers, ~4 mm apical gutta-percha retained (radiographically confirmed), 2.5% NaOCl + 17% EDTA final disinfection.
- **Posts**: 81 White Post DC #2 (FGM) glass fiber posts split into 3 conditioning groups (n=25/initial; 75 used in final analysis per abstract):
  - **Group 1 — No conditioning (control)**: silane only.
  - **Group 2 — 24% H2O2-silane**: 60 s immersion in 24% hydrogen peroxide, rinse, air-dry, then Monobond Plus silane (60 s reaction).
  - **Group 3 — Nd:YVO4 laser-silane**: laser-irradiated (Frankfurt Laser Corporation; 1064 nm, 8 ns pulse width, 25 kHz, 500 mm/s speed, 5.3 MW/cm² power density, 200 µm vertical spacing / 150 µm depth indentation pattern per Alkhudhairy et al. protocol; 33 s exposure at 197 mm working distance), then silanized.
- **Surface roughness (Ra)**: 5 posts/group measured via profilometer (Surfcorder SE700), stylus 5 µm, scan distance 0.75 mm, 3 measurements averaged per sample.
- **Cementation**:
  - **CDCRC (conventional dual-cure)**: 37% phosphoric acid etch (15 s) → All Bond 2 adhesive (Bisco) light-cured 10 s → Duo-Link cement injected → post seated 30 s → excess removed → light-cured 40 s.
  - **SADCRC (self-adhesive dual-cure)**: RelyX Unicem (3M ESPE) mixed per manufacturer, injected into canal and applied to post, seated with firm finger pressure, excess removed, light-cured 20 s.
- **Aging**: distilled water storage at 37°C for 1 week, then 10,000 thermocycling cycles (5°C ↔ 55°C, 30 s dwell each).
- **Sectioning**: transverse slices (2.0 ± 0.2 mm) taken from cervical, middle, apical thirds (depths 1, 4, 7 mm from coronal) using a hard-tissue microtome (Isomet 1000).
- **Push-out bond strength (PBS)**: universal testing machine (EMIC), metallic plungers sized per third (coronal 1.4 mm / middle 1.2 mm / apical 0.9 mm), apical-to-coronal loading at 0.5 mm/min until failure, MPa.
- **Failure mode analysis**: stereomicroscope at 40×, classified as adhesive / cohesive / admixed, by two blinded investigators.
- **Statistics**: Shapiro-Wilk normality, one-way ANOVA + Tukey post hoc, SPSS v21.0.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Surface roughness (Ra)**:
| Group | Ra (µm, mean ± SD) |
|---|---|
| Group 1: No conditioning (control) | 0.43 ± 0.11 |
| Group 2: 24% H2O2-silane | 1.28 ± 0.18 |
| Group 3: Nd:YVO4 laser-silane | 1.32 ± 0.22 |

Overall ANOVA p<0.05; Group 2 vs Group 3 not significantly different (p>0.05); both significantly rougher than control.

**Push-out bond strength (PBS) by canal third**:
| Group | Cervical (MPa) | Middle (MPa) | Apical (MPa) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1A: No conditioning-Silane + CDCRC | 6.21±0.91 | 5.09±0.13 | 3.78±0.21 |
| 1B: No conditioning-Silane + SADCRC | 5.12±0.20 | 5.86±0.11 | 6.75±0.21 |
| 2A: H2O2-Silane + CDCRC | 11.47±0.52 | 9.67±0.43 | 7.02±0.54 |
| 2B: H2O2-Silane + SADCRC | 9.41±0.27 | 10.80±0.19 | 11.31±0.21 |
| 3A: Nd:YVO4 laser-Silane + CDCRC | 11.26±0.32(±1.17 abstract) | 9.52±0.54 | 7.32±0.43 |
| 3B: Nd:YVO4 laser-Silane + SADCRC | 9.56±0.17 | 10.76±0.23 | 11.23±0.17 |

- Highest overall bond strength: Group 3A cervical (11.26 MPa, reported as 11.26±1.17 MPa in the abstract).
- Lowest overall bond strength: Group 1A apical (3.78±0.21 MPa).
- Group 2A vs Group 3A: comparable at all three thirds (p>0.05). Group 2B vs Group 3B: comparable at all three thirds (p>0.05) — i.e., laser conditioning statistically matches H2O2 conditioning for bond strength within each cement class.
- Group 1A vs Group 1B (no-conditioning control): significant difference in PBS pattern (p<0.05).
- **Intragroup canal-third trend**: CDCRC groups (1A, 2A, 3A) show bond strength declining cervical → apical (p<0.05). SADCRC groups (1B, 2B, 3B) show bond strength increasing cervical → apical (p<0.05) — opposite gradients between cement classes.

**Failure mode**: admixed failures predominated across all thirds in H2O2+SADCRC and laser+SADCRC groups, and in the cervical third of H2O2+CDCRC/laser+CDCRC; middle/apical thirds of the CDCRC laser/H2O2 groups showed mostly adhesive failures. No-conditioning groups (both cement classes) showed predominantly adhesive failures at all thirds.

**Novelty claim (per authors)**: first study to show Nd:YVO4 laser as a viable, safer alternative to hydrogen peroxide, achieving bond strengths >11 MPa (well above the ~5 MPa clinical threshold) while avoiding H2O2-related oxidative/cytotoxicity/post-operative sensitivity concerns.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro design; does not capture the full complexity/variability of the oral environment.
- Single laser parameter set tested — outcomes may be parameter-dependent (wavelength, power density, pulse pattern held constant).
- Cement layer thickness not standardized, which may have influenced stress distribution.
- Aging limited to thermocycling only — no mechanical/cyclic loading was applied.
- Authors recommend future atomic force microscopy for deeper surface characterization, plus further in vivo and laboratory validation.

## 6. Related Work

- AlRefeai et al. — prior evidence that Nd:YVO4 laser irradiation enhances glass fiber post bond strength by creating controlled micro-irregularities; this paper's laser protocol builds on that and directly benchmarks against hydrogen peroxide.

## 7. Glossary

- **GFP (Glass Fiber Post)**: fiber-reinforced composite post used to restore endodontically treated teeth; lower modulus of elasticity than metal posts, more uniform stress distribution.
- **Nd:YVO4 laser**: Neodymium-doped Yttrium Orthovanadate laser; short pulse width, high power density; used here for photothermal/photomechanical/photochemical surface micro-ablation of the post's resin matrix.
- **H2O2 (Hydrogen Peroxide) conditioning**: conventional oxidizing chemical surface treatment that degrades the post's epoxy resin matrix via electrophilic reaction, increasing surface roughness/retention.
- **Ra (Surface Roughness, arithmetic mean)**: profilometric measure of post surface texture after conditioning.
- **PBS (Push-Out Bond Strength)**: force per unit area required to dislodge a cemented post from a root-canal dentin slice, applied apical-to-coronal; considered the most clinically relevant fiber-post bond test.
- **CDCRC (Conventional Dual-Cure Resin Cement)**: total-etch technique dual-cure luting cement (here: All Bond 2 + Duo-Link) requiring separate phosphoric-acid etch and adhesive steps.
- **SADCRC (Self-Adhesive Dual-Cure Resin Cement)**: one-step dual-cure luting cement (here: RelyX Unicem) that bonds without separate etch/prime steps, simplifying the cementation procedure.
- **Silane coupling agent**: chemical (Monobond Plus) applied to conditioned post surfaces to chemically enhance resin-cement bonding.
