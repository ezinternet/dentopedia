## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA, PROSPERO-registered) of 23 cross-sectional studies (26 sources, 16,777 children aged 2-7 years) on erosive tooth wear in the primary dentition.

Pooled prevalence was 35.6% (95% CI 24.8-48.1); acidic drinks (OR 6.90), acidic food (OR 5.14), GERD (OR 1.98), holding drinks in the mouth (OR 1.82) and regular snacking (OR 1.58) were significant risk factors, while drinking with a straw (OR 0.58) and fruit consumption (OR 0.66) were protective.

GRADE certainty is very low to low (observational design, heterogeneity, few studies per factor; acidic drinks rest on a single study), so the ORs are hypothesis-grade and support history-taking for diet and reflux rather than precise risk quantification.

## 세줄요약

- 23개 횡단연구(26개 문헌, 2-7세 소아 16,777명)를 분석한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA) — 유치열 침식성 치아마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW).

- 통합 유병률 35.6% (95% CI 24.8-48.1); 산성 음료 (OR 6.90), 산성 음식 (OR 5.14), 위식도역류질환 (Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease, GERD; OR 1.98), 음료 입에 물고 있기 (OR 1.82), 잦은 간식 (OR 1.58)이 유의한 위험인자, 빨대 사용 (OR 0.58)과 과일 섭취 (OR 0.66)는 보호 연관.

- GRADE 근거 확실성은 매우 낮음-낮음 (관찰연구·이질성·요인별 소수 연구; 산성 음료는 단일 연구) — OR은 가설 수준이며 식이·역류 문진의 근거로 활용.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled prevalence of erosive tooth wear in primary dentition (<=7 y): 35.6% (95% CI 24.8-48.1).
- Mapped 52 anamnestic risk factors across 10 domains and meta-analysed 29 as dichotomous subgroups (9 meta-analyses).
- Significant after Bonferroni-Holm: GERD, acidic food, acidic drinks, holding drinks in mouth, regular snacking (risk); straw use, fruit consumption (protective).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: SR+MA of cross-sectional studies, PRISMA, PECO framework; PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science (Feb 2023) + gray literature.
- Two independent reviewers for screening and extraction; modified Newcastle-Ottawa scale (max 10 stars); GRADE.
- Random-effects models throughout (high heterogeneity), fixed-effect sensitivity analysis, meta-regression (index, examination mode, continent, year, sample size), Egger test and funnel plots.
- 1934 records -> 64 full texts -> 26 sources / 23 studies (11 Asia, 7 Europe, 4 South America, 1 Oceania); 16,777 children.

- Prevalence meta-regression: larger studies reported lower prevalence (p<.001); Oceania differed from Europe (p=.043, one study with 0%); index, year and examination mode not significant.
- Quality: most studies good; 11 sources high, 1 satisfactory. GRADE: very low for prevalence and most domains; low for oral hygiene.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only dichotomous factors poolable; age and toothbrushing frequency not standardized; raw data unavailable; socioeconomic and oral-hygiene domains underrepresented.
- Many subgroups rest on 1-2 studies (acidic drinks: 1 study); high heterogeneity (fixed-effect juice I2 88%, water I2 97%).
- Fruit "protective" finding likely reflects inability to separate acidic from non-acidic fruit.
- Authors call for standardized questionnaires and raw-data sharing. Note: Kanzow P is a co-author of this review and of the wiki's Kanzow 2016 page.

## 7. Glossary

- Erosive tooth wear (ETW): acid-related, non-bacterial loss of tooth structure, with or without mechanical wear.
- BEWE: Basic Erosive Wear Examination index.
- GERD: gastroesophageal reflux disease.
- Anamnestic risk factor: factor identified from history/questionnaire.
- GRADE: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation.
