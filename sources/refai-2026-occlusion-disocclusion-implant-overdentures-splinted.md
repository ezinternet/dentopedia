## Why Ingested

Extends the splinted-vs-unsplinted attachment debate from biological/prosthetic outcomes into an objective functional-occlusion endpoint. [[complete-denture/leao-2018-splinted-unsplinted-overdenture-attachment-systems]] compared splinted vs unsplinted overdentures on retention/maintenance, and [[complete-denture/patel-2025-implant-overdenture-attachment-systems-sr-ma]] pooled attachment systems on peri-implant/patient outcomes, but neither quantified T-Scan occlusion/disocclusion time. This RCT adds the first digital-occlusion (T-Scan) comparison of intraoral welded bar (splinted) vs ball (unsplinted) two-implant immediately loaded mandibular overdentures, and pairs with [[occlusion/kuriakose-2024-occlusal-schemes-implant-supported-overdentures]] on how occlusal function behaves in implant overdentures.

## Three-line Summary

Parallel RCT, n=36 completely edentulous patients (12 per group): conventional complete denture (CD) control, two-implant mandibular overdenture with ball attachments (IB, unsplinted), and with intraoral welded titanium bar (IW, splinted); T-Scan occlusion time (OT) and left/right disocclusion times (DR/DL) measured at implant placement (T1) and 3 months (T2).

At T1 both implant groups had significantly shorter OT/DR/DL than CD (CD OT 0.52±0.07 s vs IB 0.35±0.06 s vs IW 0.29±0.02 s, p<0.001), with NO significant difference between IB and IW (OT Q=3.29, p=0.06); CD improved significantly over time while both implant groups stayed stable.

Splinting two implants (welded bar) gave no functional-occlusion advantage over unsplinted ball attachments, so attachment choice can rest on simplicity/maintenance/cost; limited by 3-month follow-up, insertion-torque-only stability (no ISQ), and no patient-reported outcomes.

## 세줄요약

병렬 RCT, 완전무치악 환자 36명(군당 12명): 대조군 통상 총의치(CD), 볼 어태치먼트 2-임플란트 하악 오버덴처(IB, 비연결), 구내용접 티타늄 바 오버덴처(IW, 연결); T-Scan으로 교합시간(OT)·좌우 이개시간(DR/DL)을 임플란트 식립일(T1)과 3개월(T2)에 측정.

T1에서 두 임플란트군 모두 CD보다 OT/DR/DL이 유의하게 짧았고(CD OT 0.52±0.07초 vs IB 0.35±0.06초 vs IW 0.29±0.02초, p<0.001), IB와 IW 간에는 유의차 없음(OT Q=3.29, p=0.06); CD는 시간 경과에 따라 유의하게 개선된 반면 두 임플란트군은 안정적으로 유지.

2개 임플란트를 연결(용접 바)해도 비연결 볼 어태치먼트 대비 기능적 교합 이점이 없어 어태치먼트 선택은 단순성·유지관리·비용으로 결정 가능; 3개월 단기 추적, ISQ 없이 삽입 토크로만 안정성 평가, 환자보고결과 미평가가 한계.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Evaluation of occlusion and disocclusion time in immediately loaded implant overdentures retained by two splinted versus non-splinted interforaminal implants: A randomized clinical trial
- **Authors**: Refai OM, Abdelfattah Mohamed AM, Ali El-Fiky AM
- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health, 2026; 26(1)
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s12903-026-08855-w
- **PMID**: 42332660 | **PMCID**: PMC13289338
- **Trial registration**: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06376019 (prospective, 19/04/2024)
- **Design**: Parallel three-arm randomized controlled clinical trial (CONSORT 2010)
- **Setting**: Governmental institution; completely edentulous adults (age 53–70)

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to objectively evaluate T-Scan occlusion time (OT) and disocclusion time (DT) in **immediately loaded** two-implant mandibular overdentures, directly comparing **splinted (intraoral welded bar)** vs **unsplinted (ball)** attachments, against a conventional complete denture control.
- Demonstrates that rigid implant support (either attachment) produces markedly shorter/more efficient occlusal contacts than a tissue-supported complete denture.
- Provides evidence that splinting two interforaminal implants confers **no functional-occlusion benefit** over ball attachments — supporting attachment choice on practical grounds (simplicity, maintenance, cost).
- Applies intraoral welding (Mondani technique) for chairside rigid splinting under an immediate-loading protocol.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Groups (n=12 each, 1:1:1, computer-generated randomization, opaque sealed envelopes)**: CD = conventional mandibular complete denture; IB = two-implant overdenture with ball attachments; IW = two-implant overdenture with intraoral welded titanium bar. All received conventional maxillary complete dentures.
- **Occlusal scheme**: lingualized balanced occlusion standardized across all groups.
- **Implants**: IS-III ACTIVE (Neobiotech), 4.0 mm × 10 mm, mandibular canine/interforaminal region, inter-implant distance ~20 ± 2 mm, angulation <10° (paralleling pins), open-flap after CBCT (bone density 800–1200 HU, D1–D2), insertion torque 35–40 Ncm to enable immediate loading.
- **IB**: ball abutments (3.5 mm × 11.5 mm); relief space + resilient soft liner (no definitive matrices during immediate loading phase).
- **IW**: standard titanium abutments + 2 mm rounded readymade titanium bar (Guilin Woodpecker) intraorally welded (JD Weld) at 1 pulse, 0.37% power, water-cooled; bar 2 mm off ridge crest.
- **Measurement**: T-Scan III (Tekscan), ~100 μm sensor, upright standardized head position, center-of-force occlusal correction targeting 50%±2% each side, 4 repetitions; single blinded calibrated operator. OT = first contact → maximum intercuspation; DT = onset of excursion → complete posterior separation. Timepoints T1 (placement day) and T2 (3 months).
- **Statistics**: SPSS 21; Shapiro-Wilk (normal); Mixed ANOVA (Greenhouse-Geisser) for time × treatment; paired t-test intra-group; post hoc Tukey; Bonferroni correction; p<0.05.
- **Blinding limitation**: only the T-Scan assessor blinded (visible prosthetic differences preclude participant/clinician blinding).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Mixed ANOVA**: significant main effects of group and time and significant group × time interaction for OT, DR, DL (all p<0.001). Partial eta² large for group (OT 0.791; DR 0.896; DL 0.826).
- **Post hoc Tukey**: CD differed significantly from IB and IW (p<0.001); **IB vs IW not significant** (OT Q=3.29 p=0.06; DR Q=2.8 p=0.13; DL Q=3.05 p=0.09). Pattern persisted at T2.
- **Mean ± SD (seconds):**

| Parameter | Time | CD | IB | IW | Intra-group over time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OT | T1 | 0.52±0.07 | 0.35±0.06 | 0.29±0.02 | CD ↓ significant (p=0.04) |
| OT | T2 | 0.47±0.07 | 0.33±0.03 | 0.28±0.02 | IB/IW NS |
| DR | T1 | 0.57±0.04 | 0.40±0.04 | 0.36±0.03 | CD ↓ significant (p=0.005) |
| DR | T2 | 0.51±0.05 | 0.38±0.05 | 0.33±0.04 | IB/IW NS |
| DL | T1 | 0.58±0.04 | 0.42±0.01 | 0.40±0.02 | CD ↓ significant (p=0.0008) |
| DL | T2 | 0.52±0.04 | 0.41±0.03 | 0.37±0.04 | IB/IW NS |

- **Paired t-test**: significant reduction over 3 months only in CD (settling/adaptation improves stability); IB and IW stable (already short due to rigid foundation).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Sample size powered on maximum occlusal force (Kabbua et al.), not directly on OT/DT (conceptually related but not identical outcomes).
- Incomplete blinding — only the T-Scan operator was blinded.
- Implant stability assessed by insertion torque only; no ISQ/Osstell.
- Short 3-month follow-up — inadequate for long-term functional adaptation and occlusal stability.
- No patient-reported outcomes or full masticatory-function simulation.
- Intraoral welding is technique-sensitive and adds clinical complexity; longer well-designed trials recommended.

## 7. Glossary

- **OT (Occlusion Time)**: interval from first tooth contact to maximum intercuspation during closure; shorter = more simultaneous/efficient contact.
- **DT (Disocclusion Time)**: interval from onset of excursive movement to complete posterior tooth separation; shorter = less muscle activity/TMJ stress in excursion.
- **IB / IW / CD**: ball-retained overdenture / intraoral welded bar-retained overdenture / conventional complete denture.
- **Intraoral welding**: chairside electric welding to rigidly splint titanium abutments/bar (Mondani technique).
- **Splinted vs unsplinted**: bar rigidly connecting implants vs independent ball attachments.
- **Lingualized balanced occlusion**: occlusal scheme with maxillary palatal cusps as primary functional contacts, used across all groups here.
- **Interforaminal region**: anterior mandible between mental foramina, standard site for two-implant overdenture.
