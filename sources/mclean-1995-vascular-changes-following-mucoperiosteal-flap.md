## Why Ingested
User requested an evidence base for periosteal-elevation blood-supply loss in alveolar bone; this is the classic direct in-vivo measurement showing that simply raising a full-thickness mucoperiosteal envelope flap causes significant flap-circulation reduction lasting ≥3 days (7 days interproximally) — note the measured endpoint is flap/gingival (soft-tissue) circulation rather than bone perfusion itself. It supplies the vascular rationale behind the closure-technique and trauma-minimization discussions in → [[suture-wound-closure/kim-2026-interrupted-vs-mattress-suture-gbr-rct]] and → [[suture-wound-closure/hur-2025-modified-periosteal-releasing-incision-mpri]].

## Three-line Summary
In-vivo fluorescein angiography study in 4 healthy adult mongrel dogs: buccal and lingual full-thickness envelope flaps were reflected in mandibular quadrants (intrasulcular incisions, first premolar to first molar), immediately readapted, and closed with horizontal mattress sutures in one quadrant vs direct interrupted sutures in the contralateral quadrant.
The simple act of raising the mucoperiosteal envelope flap initiated significant vascular trauma — flap-circulation reductions vs presurgical baseline were statistically significant for at least 3 days and persisted 7 days at interproximal sites, while extravascular dye diffusion recovered sooner and covered a significantly greater flap area than intracapillary circulation early in healing.
Clinical implication: even an uncomplicated envelope flap leaves a perfusion-compromised healing window over the first postoperative week; abstract-only record (truncated at 250 words — the mattress-vs-interrupted comparison results were cut off), dog model, n=4.

## 세줄요약
건강한 성인 잡종견(mongrel dog) 4마리의 하악 사분면에서 협측·설측 전층 봉투판 (Full-thickness Envelope Flap) 을 거상 후 즉시 복원하고, 수평 매트리스 봉합 (Horizontal Mattress Suture) 과 단순결절봉합 (Direct Interrupted Suture) 을 대향 사분면에 각각 적용하여 형광혈관조영술 (Fluorescein Angiography) 로 술전 및 술후 1·3·7·10·14·21일 치은 혈류를 측정한 생체내 동물실험.
점막골막판 (Mucoperiosteal Flap) 거상만으로도 유의한 혈관 손상이 시작되어 판막 혈류가 술전 기준치 대비 유의하게 감소 — 최소 3일, 치간 (interproximal) 부위에서는 7일까지 지속; 혈관외 확산 (extravascular diffusion) 은 모세관내 순환보다 빨리 회복되어 초기 치유기에 유의하게 더 넓은 판막 면적을 덮음.
임상적 의미: 합병증 없는 봉투판 수술 후에도 첫 술후 주가 관류 저하 창이므로 이 시기의 봉합·판막 취급이 중요; 초록이 250단어에서 절단되어 봉합법 간 비교 결과는 미확보, 개(dog) 모델 n=4.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct in-vivo demonstration that the simple act of raising a mucoperiosteal envelope flap initiates significant vascular trauma, quantified against each dog's presurgical baseline.
- Time-course quantification of the perfusion deficit: statistically significant flap-circulation reduction lasting at least 3 days, persisting 7 days at interproximal sites.
- Separation of two perfusion signals — intracapillary circulation vs extravascular dye diffusion — showing diffusion recovers sooner and over a significantly greater flap area during early healing.
- Methodological contribution: a simple photographic system plus computerized planimetry to quantify in-vivo fluorescein dye fluorescence at predefined flap sites over 21 days.
- Within-dog, contralateral-quadrant comparison of horizontal mattress vs direct interrupted closure after envelope flap reflection (the comparison's results are not retrievable from the truncated abstract).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In-vivo animal experimental study, contralateral-quadrant (within-dog) design
- **n**: 4 healthy adult mongrel dogs
- **Surgery**: intrasulcular incisions from mandibular first premolar to first molar; buccal and lingual full-thickness envelope flaps reflected; immediate readaptation; horizontal mattress closure in one quadrant, direct interrupted closure in the contralateral quadrant of each dog
- **Measurement**: fluorescein angiography of in-vivo gingival circulation, recorded photographically just prior to surgery and on postoperative days 1, 3, 7, 10, 14, and 21
- **Analysis sites**: each flap divided into 3 interproximal and 2 mid-buccal sites
- **Quantification**: computerized planimetry of the intracapillary and diffusion extent of dye fluorescence
- **Statistics**: paired t-test for longitudinal change vs presurgical baseline; Student t-test for cross-sectional comparisons (suture technique; interproximal vs mid-buccal at each postsurgical day)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Raising a mucoperiosteal envelope flap initiates significant vascular trauma.
- Statistically significant reductions in flap circulation relative to presurgical baseline lasted for at least 3 days, and persisted for 7 days at the interproximal sites.
- Flap diffusion (extravascular leakage) recovered sooner and extended over a significantly greater area of the flap than did intracapillary flap circulation during the early period of healing.
- No numeric values (perfusion area, % change, exact p-values) appear in the retrieved abstract — it is truncated at 250 words, and the reported differences between the two suturing techniques were cut off.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: PubMed abstract truncated at 250 words; the horizontal-mattress vs direct-interrupted comparison results and all numeric values were cut off — full text not retrieved (paywalled J Periodontol 1995, no PMC record).
- Animal model: 4 healthy adult mongrel dogs; quantitative perfusion kinetics may not transfer to human periodontal surgery.
- Small sample: n=4 (partially offset by each dog serving as its own control via contralateral quadrants and presurgical baseline).
- The abstract reports flap/gingival (soft-tissue) circulation — direct measurement of alveolar-bone blood supply after periosteal elevation is not claimed in the retrieved text.
- Flaps were reflected and immediately readapted; no prolonged reflection time, bone graft, or membrane interposition was tested.

## 6. Related Work
- Kim et al. 2026: human RCT of interrupted vs interrupted + horizontal mattress sutures for GBR primary closure (no significant dehiscence difference) — the clinical-outcome descendant of the suture-technique comparison this study set up in dogs.
- Iwanaga et al. 2019: cadaveric mapping of the facial/inferior labial artery at the mandibular buccal periosteal releasing incision — macrovascular anatomical risk at the periosteal level, alongside this study's microvascular perfusion consequence of flap reflection.
- Hur et al. 2025: modified periosteal releasing incision (MPRI) framed as a trauma-reduced variant — this study quantifies how much vascular compromise flap manipulation itself inflicts, the physiological premise of such trauma-minimization.
- Marsidi et al. 2020: bench biomechanics ranking suture techniques by pulling force (horizontal mattress highest, 8.11 N) — the mechanical counterpart to this study's perfusion view of mattress suturing.
- Jeevitha et al. 2025: SR+MA of tissue adhesives vs sutures in periodontal flap surgery with healing indices at 7/14/21 days — overlapping exactly the window in which this study shows interproximal perfusion still depressed.

## 7. Glossary
- **Mucoperiosteal flap (점막골막판)**: full-thickness soft-tissue flap that includes the periosteum, elevated off the underlying bone.
- **Fluorescein angiography (형광혈관조영술)**: intravenous fluorescein dye photographed serially to depict in-vivo tissue circulation.
- **Intracapillary circulation (모세관내 순환)**: dye fluorescence remaining within capillary lumens — the perfusion signal, slowest to recover.
- **Diffusion / extravascular leakage (혈관외 누출·확산)**: fluorescein escaping vessels into surrounding tissue — recovered sooner and over a greater flap area than intracapillary flow early in healing.
- **Computerized planimetry (전산화 면적측정)**: digital area measurement used to quantify the intracapillary and diffusion extent of dye fluorescence.
- **Horizontal mattress vs direct interrupted suture (수평 매트리스 봉합 vs 단순결절봉합)**: the two primary-closure techniques applied in contralateral quadrants of each dog.
