## Why Ingested

Largest ARP materials NMA to date (88 RCTs, 2805 patients, 3073 sockets; material rankings using multidimensional scale NMA) — supersedes [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/avila-ortiz-2019-alveolar-ridge-preservation-interventions]] (22 RCTs, "no clear winner") and partially supersedes [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/bassir-2018-alveolar-ridge-preservation-meta-analysis]] (21 studies) by providing explicit head-to-head material rankings that earlier pairwise SRs could not. Identifies xenograft (XG) and allograft (AG) as the most predictable materials for horizontal and vertical ridge dimension preservation.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + NMA (MEDLINE, Scopus, CENTRAL, EMBASE; RCTs to March 2021; 88 RCTs, 2805 patients, 3073 sockets; 1740 sockets underwent ARP, 1432 covered by membrane) evaluating dimensional and histomorphometric outcomes of biomaterials used for alveolar ridge preservation.

Pairwise MA: all grafting materials significantly reduced horizontal and vertical shrinkage vs spontaneous healing; NMA multidimensional ranking shows xenografts (XG) and allografts (AG) — alone or combined with bioactive agents (Bio+AG) — are the most predictable materials for horizontal and vertical ridge dimension preservation; platelet concentrates ranked best for percentage of new bone formation.

Xenograft and Bio+AG demonstrated significantly better performance in minimizing post-extraction dimensional changes compared with other materials, though they showed the worst histological (bone formation) outcomes — allografts and AG+alloplast combinations offered comparable dimensional results with better histomorphometry.

## 세줄요약

SR+NMA (MEDLINE·Scopus·CENTRAL·EMBASE; 2021-03까지 RCT; 88 RCT, 2805명, 3073 발치와; 이 중 1740 발치와 ARP 시행, 1432개 차폐막 피개) — 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP)에 사용된 생체재료의 치수·조직형태계측 결과 평가.

이진 메타분석: 모든 재료가 자연치유 대비 수평·수직 위축을 유의하게 감소. NMA 다차원 순위: 이종골 (Xenograft, XG)·동종골 (Allograft, AG)·생활성제+AG (Bio+AG)가 수평·수직 차원 보존에서 가장 예측 가능; 혈소판 농축물이 신생골 형성 비율 최상위 — 이는 avila-ortiz-2019의 "우월 재료 없음" 결론을 뒤집음.

XG·Bio+AG는 치수 보존 성능이 가장 뛰어나지만 조직학적(골형성) 결과는 가장 불량; 동종골·AG+alloplast는 치수·조직형태 균형에서 일관되게 좋은 결과 — 재료 선택 시 치수 보존과 신생골 형성 간 상충(trade-off) 고려 필요.

## Key Contributions

- Largest ARP NMA to date: 88 RCTs, 2805 patients, 3073 sockets (vs avila-ortiz-2019's 22 RCTs)
- First head-to-head material ranking via NMA — fills the "no clear winner" gap in earlier pairwise SRs
- Identifies XG and AG as most predictable for dimensional preservation (horizontal + vertical)
- Platelet concentrates rank highest for new bone formation percentage
- Reveals dimensional vs histomorphometric trade-off: materials ranking highest in volume preservation (XG) rank lowest in bone quality
- Bio+AG performs well in dimensional preservation — supports combination approaches

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + pairwise and network meta-analysis (NMA)
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, Scopus, CENTRAL, EMBASE (through March 2021)
- **Inclusion**: RCTs; ARP after tooth extraction; human patients; controlled for spontaneous healing
- **Outcomes (primary)**: Ridge horizontal and vertical dimension changes; new bone formation (%) into socket
- **NMA ranking**: Multidimensional scale ranking
- **Sample**: 88 RCTs, 2805 patients, 3073 sockets; 1740 sockets with ARP; 1432 with membrane coverage
- **Materials compared**: Xenograft (XG), allograft (AG), alloplast (AP), platelet concentrates (PC), bioactive agents + AG (Bio+AG), AG + alloplast (AG+AP)

## Key Results

**Pairwise MA — all materials vs spontaneous healing:**
- All materials statistically significantly reduced horizontal AND vertical shrinkage (p<.05)

**NMA multidimensional ranking — dimensional preservation:**
1. Xenograft (XG): consistently top-ranked for horizontal and vertical dimensional preservation
2. Allograft + bioactive agents (Bio+AG): comparable to XG in dimensional outcomes
3. Allograft (AG): similar to XG in clinical head-to-head comparisons, better histomorphometry
4. AG + alloplast (AG+AP): consistent performance across dimensional and bone formation outcomes
5. Alloplast (AP): also significantly reduces resorption but ranks lower than XG/AG

**NMA — new bone formation:**
- Platelet concentrates ranked best for % new bone formation
- XG and Bio+AG ranked worst for histomorphometric outcomes

**Trade-off**: High dimensional preservation (XG, Bio+AG) ↔ poor histology; good bone formation (PC, AG) ↔ intermediate dimensional preservation
