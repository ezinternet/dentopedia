## Why Ingested

측방 (Lateral) 상악동거상술 (MSFA)에서 **하악지 (Mandibular Ramus) 자가골 vs 우골광물 (Bovine Bone Mineral, BBM) + 국소 채취 자가골 25% 혼합**을 비교한 다기관 split-mouth RCT. 기존 위키의 이식재 비교 합성([[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]]: SR, 이식재 간 생존율 동등)을 무작위 배정·자기 대조(split-mouth) 설계로 검증하고, "추가 공여부(하악지) 없이 BBM+25% 자가골만으로 동등한 결과"라는 임상적으로 실행 가능한 결론을 제시한다. 하악지 채취 후 하순 감각이상(4/50)이라는 공여부 이환(morbidity) 데이터도 포함 — 임플란트 생존·성공·방사선학적 MBL·PROM까지 전 축을 다룬다.

## Three-line Summary

Multicenter split-mouth RCT (3 centers, 50 patients, 198 implants) comparing lateral-window MSFA with autogenous bone from the mandibular ramus (AB) vs BBM mixed with 25% locally harvested maxillary bone chips (BBM) — sinuses randomized 1:1, implants placed 4–6 mo post-grafting, loaded 4–6 mo later, evaluated 1 and 12 mo post-loading.

12-month implant survival 92.9% (AB) vs 93.9% (BBM) (OR 0.85; 95% CI 0.27–2.66; p=0.777); clinical success 98.8% vs 97.6% (p=0.620); radiographic success low in both (18.8% vs 25.8%, p=0.410); MBL and all clinical parameters without significant group differences (p>0.05).

Conclusion: MSFA with BBM+25% local AB yields outcomes equivalent to ramus AB without an extra donor site — reducing patient morbidity (temporary lower-lip dysesthesia after ramus harvest in 4/50 patients, median VAS>5).

## 세줄요약

다기관 split-mouth RCT (3개 센터, 50명, 198개 임플란트): 측방창 (Lateral window) 상악동거상술 (MSFA)에서 하악지 (Mandibular Ramus) 자가골 (AB) vs 우골광물 (Bovine Bone Mineral, BBM)+상악 국소 채취 자가골 25% 혼합을 비교 — 상악동 1:1 무작위, 이식 4–6개월 후 임플란트 식립·4–6개월 후 하중, 하중 1·12개월 후 평가.

12개월 하중 시 임플란트 생존율 92.9% (AB) vs 93.9% (BBM) (OR 0.85; 95% CI 0.27–2.66; p=0.777); 임상 성공률 98.8% vs 97.6% (p=0.620); 방사선학적 성공률은 양군 모두 낮음 (18.8% vs 25.8%, p=0.410); 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 및 모든 임상 지표에서 군 간 유의차 없음 (p>0.05).

결론: 추가 공여부 없이 BBM+25% 국소 자가골만으로 하악지 자가골과 동등한 결과 — 환자 이환 감소 (하악지 채취 후 일시적 하순 감각이상 4/50명, 중앙 VAS>5).

## Summary

This multicenter split-mouth RCT (Clinical Oral Implants Research 2025;36:859–870; EMC Rotterdam, Catharina Eindhoven, St Anna Geldrop, Univ. Zurich; funded by Straumann Group; registered NL59578.078.16) enrolled 50 patients (22 F/28 M, mean age 61±10 y, range 23–78; 47 fully edentulous upper jaws) requiring bilateral maxillary sinus floor augmentation (MSFA) with implant placement. Sinuses were randomized 1:1 to either MSFA with autogenous bone harvested from the mandibular ramus (AB) or MSFA with bovine bone mineral mixed with 25% locally harvested maxillary bone chips (BBM; chips from the sinus window / zygomatic region). All MSFAs used the lateral window approach (Boyne & James 1980; Tatum 1986). Implants (n=198; 99 per group; predominantly soft-tissue-level, 8/10/12 mm) were placed after 4–6 months of healing and loaded 4–6 months later; outcomes were assessed at baseline (1 month post-loading) and 12 months post-loading, including survival, clinical success, OPT-based marginal bone level (MBL), clinical parameters, and PROMs (VAS).

At 12 months post-loading, implant survival was 92.9% (91/99) in the AB group and 93.9% (92/99) in the BBM group (OR 0.85; 95% CI 0.27–2.66; p=0.777); 12 implants failed before loading (6/6) and 3 after loading (2 AB, 1 BBM). Clinical success among 166 surviving implants was 98.8% vs 97.6% (OR 0.49; CI 0.03–8.05; p=0.620) — no mobility or subjective complaints; mild (GI1) inflammation at 32 implants, moderate (GI2) at 3; no PD >5 mm; 100% prosthetic success. Radiographic success (MBL ≤1.5 mm within the first year per success criteria, n=126) was low in both groups: 18.8% (AB) vs 25.8% (BBM) (OR 0.77; CI 0.41–1.43; p=0.410); median MBL change averaged ≈2.0–2.4 mm at 12 months with no significant between-group difference at any time point. Nine of 15 failed implants (60%) were placed at sites with initial bone height below the overall median (1.8–4.3 mm). Of 100 MSFAs, 29 surgical complications occurred — 24 membrane perforations (AB=9, BBM=10, both sides=5), all managed with resorbable collagen membranes — plus 3 wound dehiscences and 4 patients needing additional GBR. Patient satisfaction was high (VAS 9.0 at 12 mo), improving significantly from screening (p=0.005); pain after MSFA and implant placement was lower than anticipated pre-surgery (p=0.021); 4 patients reported temporary lower-lip dysesthesia (median VAS >5.0) after ramus harvesting.

## Key Contributions

- Split-mouth randomized design eliminates patient-level confounders in the MSFA graft-material comparison — strongest available comparison of ramus AB vs BBM+25% local AB (1-year).
- Demonstrates equivalence (survival, clinical success, MBL, clinical parameters, PROMs): MSFA without a second donor site is non-inferior in practice.
- Quantifies donor-site morbidity: temporary lower-lip dysesthesia in 4/50 patients (median VAS >5.0) after ramus harvest — the morbidity avoided by the BBM+25% approach.
- Failure-pattern observation: 60% of implant failures occurred at sites with initial bone height < median (1.8–4.3 mm) — residual bone height remains the dominant planning variable regardless of graft material.
- Perforation rate context: 24/100 MSFAs (24%) perforated despite lateral window approach — all repaired with collagen membranes without survival consequence (consistent with [[sinus-lift/lateral/diaz-olivares-2021-schneiderian-membrane-perforation-sinus-lift]]).

## Methodology

Multicenter split-mouth RCT (3 Dutch centers + Zurich), 50 patients, bilateral MSFA; sinus-level 1:1 randomization. AB: ramus harvest; BBM: BBM + 25% local maxillary bone chips (sinus window/zygomatic). Lateral window MSFA (Boyne & James 1980; Tatum 1986). Implants after 4–6 mo, loaded 4–6 mo later; follow-ups at 1 mo and 12 mo post-loading. Outcomes: implant survival (198), clinical success (166; ICOI criteria — no mobility, no complaints), prosthetic success, radiographic success/MBL on OPTs (126; ICC 0.799 for inter-examiner reliability), clinical indices (PI, BI, GI, PPD, BOP, keratinized mucosa width), PROMs (VAS anxiety/pain/satisfaction). Statistics: OR with 95% CI, exact tests; significance p<0.05 (table-reported values used). Funded by Straumann Group.

## Clinical Takeaways

- BBM + 25% locally harvested bone chips is a viable alternative to ramus autogenous bone for lateral-window MSFA — outcomes statistically equivalent across all measured axes at 1 year.
- Avoiding the mandibular donor site spares real morbidity: 8% of patients (4/50) experienced temporary lower-lip dysesthesia after ramus harvest.
- Residual bone height still governs failure risk: failed implants clustered at sites with <4.3 mm initial height — plan graft/implant timing around this regardless of material.
- Expect early crestal bone remodeling >1.5 mm at many implant sites by 1 year (radiographic success only 18.8–25.8%) — MBL criterion-based success is strict; clinical success remained near-universal (97.6–98.8%).
- Membrane perforation (24% here, all repaired with collagen membrane) did not compromise outcomes — repair suffices (see [[sinus-lift/lateral/diaz-olivares-2021-schneiderian-membrane-perforation-sinus-lift]]).

## Limitations

- 1-year horizon only — long-term graft/bone level stability beyond loading not yet reported (trial protocol foresees longer follow-up).
- Radiographic success rates unusually low (18.8–25.8%) vs literature — authors note OPT-based MBL methodology may differ from periapical/CBCT-based measurements (e.g., Kim 2009).
- Industry funding (Straumann Group) — implants were Straumann products.
- Split-mouth randomization at sinus level within-patient; 47/50 fully edentulous upper jaws — results may not extrapolate to single-tooth situations.
- Harvest site (ramus vs local) confounds graft type comparison; BBM group's 25% chips came from the surgical window itself.
