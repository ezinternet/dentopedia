## Three-line Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=100 adults, India, 2025) comparing 4% articaine (1:100,000 epi) vs 2% lignocaine (1:80,000 epi) for IANB in patients with symptomatic irreversible pulpitis (SIP) in mandibular first molars undergoing root canal treatment.

Articaine showed significantly faster onset (4.3 vs 7.8 min), longer duration (120.8 vs 64.9 min) and better pain control (SF-MPQ 1.09 vs 1.63) — all p<0.001 — despite the lignocaine arm using higher-concentration vasoconstrictor (1:80,000 epi).

In the SIP context, IANB onset is extended approximately 50% vs healthy tissue (7.8 min vs typical ~5 min for lidocaine), providing clinically critical timing data for endodontic planning.

## Summary

Double-blind RCT (n=100; India; 2025) comparing 4% articaine (1:100,000 epi) vs 2% lignocaine (1:80,000 epi) for IANB in patients with symptomatic irreversible pulpitis (SIP) in permanent mandibular first molars undergoing root canal treatment. Articaine demonstrated significantly faster onset (4.3 vs 7.8 min), longer duration (120.8 vs 64.9 min), and better pain control (SF-MPQ 1.09 vs 1.63) — all p<0.001.

Key population: adults 18-30 years, ASA I, confirmed SIP by cold test + EPT, no periapical pathology. Notable: lignocaine arm used 1:80,000 epi (higher concentration than standard 1:100,000), which strengthens vasoconstriction — yet articaine still produced markedly superior results.

Diagnosis confirmed by electric pulp test (EPT) reading of 80 = gold standard for pulpal anesthesia confirmation. SF-MPQ used for pain assessment after caries removal and access cavity preparation.

## Why Ingested

Provides onset time data for 2% lignocaine IANB in SIP context: 7.8 min (vs ~5 min in healthy tissue). The inflamed pulpal environment extends lidocaine onset approximately 50% compared to healthy adults — clinically critical data for endodontic cases.

## Key Data Points

| Parameter | Lignocaine 2% (1:80k epi) | Articaine 4% (1:100k epi) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onset (min) | 7.8 ± 0.8 | 4.3 ± 0.4 | <0.001 |
| Duration (min) | 64.9 ± 3.0 | 120.8 ± 5.2 | <0.001 |
| SF-MPQ pain | 1.63 ± 0.640 | 1.09 ± 0.258 | <0.001 |

## Limitations

- Lignocaine arm used 1:80,000 epi (not standard 1:100,000) — slightly higher epi concentration may underestimate lignocaine duration
- Single-center India study
- Limited age range (18-30 years only)
- Mandibular first molar only
- No EPT-confirmed anesthetic failure rate reported separately
