## Why Ingested

Fills the calcium-specific gap left by [[dental-erosion/gomez-2026-anti-erosive-dentifrices-umbrella-review]], which called calcium-based technologies heterogeneous and low-certainty but covered dentifrices only; this SR+MA of in situ RCTs addresses calcium in drinks, gum and pastes, and complements the calcium/phosphate supersaturation finding in [[dental-erosion/barac-2015-erosive-effect-soft-drinks-enamel-profilometry]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42021229819) of in situ randomized controlled trials; 869 records screened, 21 eligible, testing calcium formulations (juices, chewing gum, milk, CPP-ACP pastes) against erosive tooth wear.

Calcium added to juice drinks reduced enamel loss (blackcurrant vs orange juice: 2.6 times less enamel loss, p = 0.0001, I2 = 89%); chewing gum with vs without CPP-ACP showed no significant difference in surface microhardness (p = 0.31, I2 = 71%); results for milk and CPP-ACP pastes were contradictory.

Evidence is in situ (not clinical ETW progression), with 5 low, 12 unclear and 4 high risk-of-bias studies and high heterogeneity; calcium appears useful mainly as an additive to acidic drinks. Abstract-level only.

## 세줄요약

치아 침식마모 (Erosive Tooth Wear, ETW) 예방에 대한 칼슘 제제를 평가한 in situ 무작위대조시험 체계적 고찰 및 메타분석 (PROSPERO CRD42021229819)으로, 869편 중 21편이 선정되었다.

주스에 칼슘을 첨가하면 법랑질 손실이 감소했고(블랙커런트 주스가 오렌지 주스 대비 2.6배 적은 손실, p = 0.0001, I2 = 89%), 카제인 포스포펩타이드-비정질 인산칼슘 (Casein Phosphopeptide-Amorphous Calcium Phosphate, CPP-ACP) 함유 껌은 유무 간 표면 미세경도 차이가 없었으며(p = 0.31, I2 = 71%), 우유와 CPP-ACP 페이스트의 결과는 상반되었다.

근거는 in situ 모델이라 임상적 ETW 진행 감소의 증거로 볼 수 없고, 비뚤림 위험이 낮음 5편·불확실 12편·높음 4편에 이질성이 커서, 칼슘은 주로 산성 음료 첨가제로서 유용해 보인다(초록 수준 요약).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Stated as the first systematic review of the role of calcium in preventing ETW.
- Restricts inclusion to in situ RCTs, so the evidence setting is controlled intraoral, not long-term clinical.
- Pooled calcium-enriched juice and CPP-ACP gum outcomes; milk and CPP-ACP paste results reported as contradictory.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PROSPERO-registered (CRD42021229819); electronic database search for in situ RCTs of calcium formulations.
- Outcomes: mean enamel loss, surface microhardness, surface roughness, mean erosion/softening depth, mineral loss/precipitation, remineralization.
- Cochrane RoB 2.0; random-effects meta-analysis.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 869 studies retrieved, 21 eligible; risk of bias 5 low, 12 unclear, 4 high.
- Juice: blackcurrant juice 2.6 times less enamel loss than orange juice (p = 0.0001, I2 = 89%).
- CPP-ACP chewing gum vs gum without: no significant difference in surface microhardness (p = 0.31, I2 = 71%).
- Milk and CPP-ACP pastes: contradictory results.
- Authors' conclusion: calcium formulations play an important role in ETW prevention, mainly as additives to acidic drinks.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only; per-study effect sizes, confidence intervals and GRADE certainty not available.
- In situ models only; no clinical ETW progression outcome. Most included studies had unclear or high risk of bias; I2 of 71-89% indicates substantial heterogeneity.
- The "blackcurrant vs orange juice" contrast compares different juices rather than calcium vs no calcium within one juice, as worded in the abstract; interpret with care.

## 7. Glossary

- Erosive tooth wear (ETW): non-bacterial loss of dental hard tissue from acid plus mechanical stress.
- In situ study: intraoral appliance model with enamel specimens worn by volunteers.
- CPP-ACP: casein phosphopeptide-amorphous calcium phosphate.
- Risk of bias 2.0 (RoB 2): Cochrane tool for randomized trials.
