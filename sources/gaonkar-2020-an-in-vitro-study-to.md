## Why Ingested

Directly compares two chairside polishing kits (eZr vs OptraFine) against glazed and roughened-then-glazed zirconia, providing Ra values and SEM morphology data for monolithic Ceramill Zolid zirconia. Fills gap on specific kit-level performance for clinical polishing decision-making.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/gaonkar-2020-an-in-vitro-study-to]]

## Three-line Summary

This in-vitro study compared surface roughness (Ra) of monolithic zirconia (Ceramill Zolid) after polishing with two kits (eZr, OptraFine) versus glazing using 25 discs divided into 5 groups (n=5 each). OptraFine achieved the lowest Ra (0.47 μm), slightly lower than eZr (0.49 μm) and glazed (0.53 μm), with no statistically significant difference between polishing systems. SEM analysis showed OptraFine produced smoother, more regular surfaces; both polishing systems are viable alternatives to reglazing after chairside adjustment.

## 세줄요약

단일체 지르코니아(Ceramill Zolid) 25개 디스크를 eZr·OptraFine 두 폴리싱 키트와 글레이징으로 처리 후 표면 거칠기(Ra)를 비교한 인비트로 연구. OptraFine가 Ra 0.47 μm로 가장 낮았으나 eZr(0.49 μm) 및 글레이징(0.53 μm)과 통계적 유의차 없음. 두 키트 모두 임상 조정 후 재글레이징의 대안으로 적합하며, OptraFine SEM 표면이 더 균일함.

## Key Contributions

1. Quantitative Ra comparison: OptraFine (0.47 μm) ≈ eZr (0.49 μm) < glazed control (0.53 μm) < roughened+glazed (0.59 μm) << unpolished roughened (1.24 μm)
2. No statistically significant difference between the two polishing kits (one-way ANOVA, post hoc, P < 0.05)
3. SEM showed OptraFine produces smoother, more regular surface morphology with fewer striations vs eZr
4. Both polishing systems achieve Ra values below the glazed control and significantly below the unpolished roughened baseline

## Methodology

- **Specimens**: 25 discs, 10 mm diameter × 2 mm thickness, Ceramill Zolid HT (A2 shade), CAD/CAM milled
- **Groups (n=5 each)**:
  - Group 1 (negative control): roughened, unpolished, unglazed
  - Group 2 (positive control): glazed without prior roughening
  - Group 3: roughened → eZr polishing kit (Garrison Dental Solution, Germany): coarse/medium/fine grits at 8000–10,000 rpm, 40 s each
  - Group 4: roughened → OptraFine ceramic polishing kit (Ivoclar Vivadent): light blue/dark blue silicone points + nylon brush + diamond paste at 15,000 rpm, 40 s each
  - Group 5: roughened → glazed
- **Roughening**: red band diamond finishing bur, air rotor handpiece, 15 s, constant pressure
- **Surface roughness**: Surftest SJ 210 (Mitutoyo) — Ra in μm
- **Qualitative analysis**: SEM (Zeiss Evo 18 Special Edition)
- **Statistics**: one-way ANOVA + post hoc test

## Key Results

| Group | Treatment | Ra (μm) |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | Roughened, unpolished | 1.24 |
| Group 2 | Glazed (positive control) | 0.53 |
| Group 3 | Roughened + eZr | 0.49 |
| Group 4 | Roughened + OptraFine | 0.47 |
| Group 5 | Roughened + glazed | 0.59 |

- No statistically significant difference between Groups 3, 4, and 5
- OptraFine produced smooth, regular morphology under SEM; eZr showed more striations
- Both polishing kits reduced Ra to below glazed-only control levels

## Limitations

- Small sample size (n=5 per group) — low statistical power
- Single zirconia brand (Ceramill Zolid HT) — generalizability uncertain
- No opponent wear, tribology, or antagonist abrasion testing
- Short-term in vitro only — no aging, thermocycling, or clinical simulation

## Glossary

- **Ra**: arithmetic mean surface roughness (μm) — standard profilometric parameter
- **Monolithic zirconia**: full-contour zirconia without porcelain veneer
- **OptraFine**: Ivoclar Vivadent ceramic polishing kit (silicone points + diamond paste)
- **eZr**: Garrison Dental Solution zirconia polishing kit (coarse/medium/fine grit sequence)
- **SEM**: scanning electron microscope — qualitative surface topography analysis
- **CAD/CAM**: computer-aided design/manufacturing — digital fabrication method for ceramic restorations
