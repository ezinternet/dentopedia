## One-line Summary
RCT (n=24, 36-month follow-up): socket-shield technique showed significantly less marginal bone loss (1.40 vs 1.70 mm) and superior PES (10.50 vs 9.36) compared to conventional IIP at 36 months.

## 한줄요약
RCT (n=24, 36개월): 소켓-쉴드 기법이 일반 즉시식립 대비 변연골 소실(1.40 vs 1.70 mm) 및 핑크심미지수(10.50 vs 9.36)에서 유의하게 우수 — 전 추적기간 일관된 결과.

## Three-line Summary

Parallel-arm randomized controlled trial (n=24; 12 socket-shield technique [SST] / 12 conventional immediate implant placement [IIP]) in Type I intact maxillary extraction sockets, with assessments at 8, 12, and 36 months; outcomes: marginal bone loss (MBL) on radiograph and Pink Esthetic Score (PES).

SST consistently outperformed conventional IIP at all time points: MBL at 36 months was 1.40 vs 1.70 mm (p=0.040), PES was 10.50 vs 9.36 (p=0.008); both groups achieved 100% implant survival over 36 months.

The SST advantage did not diminish over 3 years; both MBL difference (0.30 mm) and PES difference (~1.1 points) are clinically relevant, extending the longest reported SST vs conventional IIP comparison to 36 months in Type I intact sockets.

## 세줄요약

평행군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=24; 소켓 쉴드 기법 (Socket Shield Technique, SST) 12 / 일반 즉시식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP) 12) — Type I 온전 상악 발치와; 8·12·36개월 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 및 핑크심미지수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES) 평가.

SST가 전 추적 시점에서 유의하게 우수: 36개월 MBL 1.40 vs 1.70 mm (p=0.040), PES 10.50 vs 9.36 (p=0.008); 양군 100% 생존.

SST 우위는 3년간 감소하지 않았으며, MBL 차이(0.30 mm)와 PES 차이(~1.1점) 모두 임상적으로 유의미; Type I 온전 발치와에서 현재까지 가장 긴 추적(36개월)의 SST vs 일반 IIP 비교.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Long-term (36-month) RCT comparing SST vs conventional IIP in maxillary esthetic zone — one of the few with this follow-up duration
- SST consistently superior to conventional IIP across all time points (8, 12, 36 months) for both MBL and PES
- 100% implant survival in both groups at 36 months
- SST MBL advantage widened over time (0.34 mm at 8mo → 0.30 mm at 36mo — sustained, not diminishing)
- SST PES advantage consistent: ~1.0–1.1 points higher at all time points

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Parallel-arm RCT
- **n**: 24 patients (12 SST, 12 conventional); 24 implants total; Type I extraction sockets
- **Randomization**: Not detailed in abstract
- **Setting**: University hospital
- **Follow-up**: 8 months (T1), 12 months (T2), 36 months (T3)
- **Prosthetic timeline**: Temporary prosthesis at 8 months, final crown at 12 months
- **Primary outcomes**: Marginal bone loss (MBL) on radiographs, Pink Esthetic Score (PES)
- **Inclusion**: Maxillary esthetic zone, Type 1 sockets, single tooth
- **Age**: 18–55 years, mean 35.7 ± 1.5 years (12F, 12M)
- **Statistical**: Two-way repeated measures ANOVA; individual between-group comparison at each time point

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | SST (T3, 36mo) | Conventional (T3, 36mo) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| MBL (mm) | 1.40 ± 0.29 | 1.70 ± 0.36 | 0.040 |
| PES | 10.50 ± 0.90 | 9.36 ± 0.98 | 0.008 |
| Survival | 12/12 (100%) | 12/12 (100%) | — |

MBL at T1 (8mo): SST 0.66±0.37 vs conventional 1.00±0.42 (p=0.047)
MBL at T2 (12mo): SST 0.98±0.36 vs conventional 1.36±0.40 (p=0.027)
PES at T1 (8mo): SST 11.04±0.91 vs conventional 9.92±0.99 (p=0.009)
PES at T2 (12mo): SST 10.77±0.88 vs conventional 9.65±0.93 (p=0.006)

Two-way ANOVA: treatment × time interaction not significant; individual treatment and time factors each significant.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=24 total) — underpowered for subgroup analyses
- Single center, India — limited generalizability
- Only Type 1 (intact) sockets included — cannot extrapolate to compromised sockets
- Short provisional phase (8 months before final crown) — some centers use longer soft tissue maturation period
- Blinding not described
- Further studies with larger samples, longer follow-up (>5 years), and compromised socket types needed
