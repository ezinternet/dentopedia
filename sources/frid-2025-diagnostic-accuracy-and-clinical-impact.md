## Why Ingested

"봉와직염(cellulitis)과 농양(abscess)을 어떻게 감별해 절개배농(I&D) 여부를 정하는가"에 대해 위키에는 처치 원칙([[oral-surgery/gargava-2022-deep-neck-space-infection-150-cases]]의 I&D, [[drug/antibiotics/teoh-2021-oral-antibiotic-empirical-acute-dentoalveolar-infections]]의 배농 우선 원칙)만 있고 감별 방법 자체를 다룬 논문이 없었다. 본 연구는 치성감염에서 이 감별을 직접 시험한 전향 코호트다 (2026-10-13 서베일런스, 사용자 요청).

## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-center cohort (n=111 enrolled, 90 analyzed; children 1–17 y with acute maxillofacial odontogenic infection) testing handheld POCUS (Philips Lumify) read by ultrasound-naive oral-maxillofacial-surgery residents after ~10 h training, to separate abscess from cellulitis.

Sensitivity 72.97% (95% CI 57.02–84.60), specificity 73.58% (60.42–83.56), PPV 65.85%, NPV 79.59%, accuracy 73.33% (66/90); institutional admissions fell from 60.0 to 19.5/year after POCUS adoption (RR 0.33, 95% CI 0.25–0.42), an associational pre/post comparison.

Moderate accuracy only — an adjunct to the clinical exam, not a stand-alone test; asymmetric reference standard (I&D for abscess vs 7-day clinical resolution for cellulitis) risks verification bias, and deep-space infections were excluded.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 전향 코호트(등록 111명, 분석 90명; 1–17세 급성 악안면 치성감염): 초음파 경험이 없는 구강악안면외과 레지던트가 약 10시간 교육 후 휴대용 POCUS(Philips Lumify)로 농양과 봉와직염을 감별.

민감도 72.97%(95% CI 57.02–84.60), 특이도 73.58%(60.42–83.56), 양성예측도 65.85%, 음성예측도 79.59%, 정확도 73.33%(66/90); 도입 후 연간 입원 60.0→19.5건(RR 0.33, 95% CI 0.25–0.42, 연관성만 확인되는 전후 비교).

정확도는 중등도이며 임상진찰의 보조수단이지 단독 판단 도구가 아님; 농양은 I&D, 봉와직염은 7일 임상경과로 확정하는 비대칭 기준표준이라 검증편향 가능, 심부공간 감염은 제외됨.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: Children (Basel) 12(10), 2025 (published 2025-10-15)
- Design: prospective observational diagnostic-accuracy study (Mar 2021–Dec 2022) + retrospective institutional admissions comparison (2017–2021 vs Jan 2022–Apr 2025)
- Setting: Rambam Health Care Campus, tertiary academic center, Israel
- Full text: PMC (PMC12563030), read in full

## 2. Key Contributions

- First prospective test, in pediatric odontogenic infection, of handheld POCUS used by ultrasound-novice non-radiologist operators.
- Reports the full 2×2 table (TP 27, FP 14, TN 39, FN 10) so the accuracy figures can be rechecked.
- Pairs diagnostic accuracy with a system-level outcome (admission rate), explicitly labelled associational.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Population: 1–17 y with acute maxillofacial odontogenic infection (swelling, pain, erythema, warmth, trismus, fever, dental source). Excluded: non-odontogenic infection, superficial pustules, significant hematologic/immunologic disorder, prior surgery for the episode, missing consent/data.
- Operators: OMFS residents (years 1–5), no prior ultrasound experience; ~10 h training (4 h lectures, 2 h video, 2 h case review, 2 h hands-on on volunteers/phantoms); no supervised patient scanning quota.
- Device/protocol: Philips Lumify, L12-4 linear probe, B-mode, ≥2 orthogonal planes with dynamic probe compression; operator reported abscess / cellulitis / ambiguous. Operators were not blinded to the clinical exam.
- Reference standard: abscess = purulence at I&D; cellulitis = complete resolution on antibiotics alone at 7-day follow-up (clinical or structured phone interview). No CT/MRI used (radiation in children; MRI rarely available).
- Management rule: suspected cellulitis → oral antibiotics (± extraction of the source primary tooth); abscess → I&D in clinic, ED, or operating room.
- Statistics: Clopper-Pearson CIs; admissions compared with Poisson regression (log link, period-length offset), likelihood-ratio p-values.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Enrolled 111; 21 excluded (14 ambiguous POCUS with no definitive outcome, 1 missing age, 6 indeterminate outcome) → 90 analyzed. Mean age 7.8 ± 3.74 y; 58% male; sources: carious primary molars 66.7%, carious permanent molars 33.3%.
- Reference standard: 37 abscess (41.1%), 53 cellulitis (58.9%).
- 2×2: TP 27, FP 14, TN 39, FN 10 → sensitivity 72.97% (27/37), specificity 73.58% (39/53), PPV 65.85% (27/41), NPV 79.59% (39/49), accuracy 73.33% (66/90; 95% CI 63.38–81.38).
- False positives (14): mostly small hypoechoic areas with no purulence at exploration or resolution on antibiotics. False negatives (10): small or deep collections obscured by edema, or limited cooperation during probe compression; all later drained, none progressed to airway compromise or deep-space infection.
- Of the 90, 60 (66.7%) were admitted; mean LOS 1.67 ± 1.24 d, not different by POCUS result.
- Admissions: 300 in 2017–2021 (60.0/y) vs 65 in Jan 2022–Apr 2025 (19.5/y); RR 0.33 (95% CI 0.25–0.42), p < 0.001.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Verification bias: small/early abscesses that improve temporarily on antibiotics can be labelled cellulitis, which would lower apparent sensitivity/PPV and inflate specificity/NPV (authors' own discussion).
- Not blinded; does not measure incremental value over the clinical exam; single center; learning-curve effect (no supervised scanning quota).
- 14 ambiguous scans (12.6% of 111) excluded from the accuracy analysis.
- Admissions comparison is retrospective and associational (staffing, referral, triage shifts); not stratified by jaw; deep-space infections excluded and unsuitable for handheld POCUS.
- Authors call for multicenter blinded studies, composite reference standard, competency-based training, maxilla vs mandible analysis.

## 6. Related Work

- Pooled POCUS sensitivities in skin/soft-tissue infection reviews are often >90% (cited Gottlieb et al.); authors attribute the lower figure here to novice operators, handheld device, and the intricate facial anatomy.
- Wiki: [[oral-surgery/gargava-2022-deep-neck-space-infection-150-cases]] (deep-space infection, I&D in 38%) and [[drug/antibiotics/teoh-2021-oral-antibiotic-empirical-acute-dentoalveolar-infections]] (drainage/source control is the primary determinant of success).

## 7. Glossary

- POCUS: point-of-care ultrasound
- I&D: incision and drainage
- OMFS: oral and maxillofacial surgery
- PPV / NPV: positive / negative predictive value
- Verification bias: outcome ascertained differently depending on the test result
