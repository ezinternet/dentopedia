## Why Ingested

측방창 상악동거상술에서 자가골 (Autogenous Bone) 대 이종골 (Xenograft)의 조직형태계측 비교는 [[sinus-lift/lateral/trimmel-2021-relative-performance-various-biomaterials]]의 베이지안 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA)에서 "자가골이 5–8개월 시점에 우월하지 않다"는 낮은 확실성의 결론으로만 존재했고, [[sinus-lift/lateral/jue-2025-maxillary-sinus-floor-augmentation-autogenous-bone]]는 임플란트 임상 지표로만 이 축을 다뤘다. 본 논문은 동일 환자 내 좌우 비교(split-mouth) 설계로 자가골 대 돼지 이종골을 6개월 조직형태계측·방사선으로 직접 비교해 그 공백을 채운다.

## Three-line Summary

Triple-blind randomized split-mouth clinical trial (12 consecutive patients, 24 maxillary sinuses, Porto, Portugal, 2013–2017) comparing mandibular autologous bone (control) with a collagenated porcine xenograft Osteobiol MP3 (test) in two-stage lateral-window sinus lift, with 6-month CT and trephine biopsy histomorphometry.

Radiographic tissue height gain was 7.81 ± 2.34 mm (autologous) vs 8.71 ± 2.15 mm (xenograft) (p = 0.26; two-way ANOVA material effect p = 0.240, time p < 0.001, interaction p = 0.261), and total hard tissue volume was 57.31 ± 2.91% vs 56.01 ± 2.86% (p = 0.376), with no inflammation or foreign-body reaction in either group.

The porcine xenograft performed equivalently to autologous bone at 6 months while eliminating the donor site, but the trial is underpowered (n = 12), shows a significant patient effect (p = 0.029) and material × patient interaction (p < 0.001), and reports no implant-level outcomes.

## 세줄요약

삼중맹검 무작위 split-mouth 임상시험 (연속 12명, 상악동 24개, 포르투갈 포르투, 2013–2017): 2단계 측방창 상악동거상술에서 하악 자가골 (Autogenous Bone, AB; 대조군) 대 콜라겐 함유 돼지 이종골 (Xenograft) Osteobiol MP3 (시험군)를 6개월 CT·천공 생검 조직형태계측으로 비교.

방사선학적 조직높이 증가는 자가골 7.81 ± 2.34 mm vs 이종골 8.71 ± 2.15 mm (p = 0.26; 이원분산분석 재료 효과 p = 0.240, 시간 효과 p < 0.001, 상호작용 p = 0.261), 총 경조직 부피 (Total Hard Tissue, THT)는 57.31 ± 2.91% vs 56.01 ± 2.86% (p = 0.376)로 군 간 차이 없고 양군 모두 염증·이물반응 소견 없음.

6개월 시점에서 돼지 이종골은 공여부 이환 없이 자가골과 동등한 성적을 보였으나, 표본이 12명으로 검정력이 낮고 환자 효과 (p = 0.029)·재료×환자 상호작용 (p < 0.001)이 유의하며 임플란트 수준 결과 (생존율·임플란트 안정성 지수, Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)는 보고되지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides **within-patient (split-mouth) evidence** that a collagenated porcine xenograft (Osteobiol MP3, 600–1000 µm granules with 10% type I/III collagen) matches mandibular autologous bone at 6 months across four outcome domains: radiological height, histology, histomorphometry, and patient preference.
- Quantifies the equivalence with paired data rather than between-patient comparison, controlling for patient-level confounders (age, smoking, systemic status, sinus anatomy) by design.
- Separates the **material effect from the patient effect**: graft type had no significant effect on hard-tissue volume (p = 0.376), whereas patient identity did (p = 0.029), with a significant material × patient interaction (p < 0.001) — a statistical argument that graft performance is patient-dependent.
- Reports **histological absence of inflammation or foreign-body reaction** in all 240 slides for both materials, contrasting with reports of inflammatory infiltrate for other substitutes (biphasic calcium phosphate, bioactive glass, calcium sulphate) cited by the authors.
- Adds a patient-reported dimension rarely reported in graft trials: 11/12 patients (91.6%) judged both procedures equally good; 1/12 (8.3%) preferred the xenograft side.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: triple-blind (patient, surgeon allocation, and outcome assessor per the Methods), randomized, split-mouth controlled clinical trial; both materials placed in the same patient, one per side.
- **Randomization**: after Schneiderian membrane elevation, a randomized opaque sealed sterilized envelope containing the assignment code was opened — allocation was therefore **intra-operative**, which means the surgeon learned the allocation before grafting but after flap elevation; blinding of the surgeon to allocation is not fully described.
- **Participants**: 12 consecutive patients (6 male, 6 female; mean age 59.7 ± 8.7 y); smokers — 6 non, 3 light, 1 heavy, 2 former; 9/12 with up to one pathology, 11/12 on at least one medication. **Exclusions**: head/neck irradiation, immunosuppression, bisphosphonates (treated or IV), untreated periodontitis, poor oral hygiene/low motivation, uncontrolled diabetes, pregnancy/lactation, psychiatric problems, sinusitis.
- **Intervention (both sides)**: full-thickness crestal flap with two vertical releasing incisions; lateral oval osteotomy with piezoelectric instrument (NSK VarioSurg) or spherical diamond bur; Schneiderian membrane elevated with curettes; sinus filled to desired height; lateral osteotomy covered with a collagen membrane (Tecnoss); polyamide 4.0 sutures.
  - **Test**: Osteobiol MP3 (Tecnoss, Torino, Italy) — cortico-medullary porcine xenograft, 600–1000 µm, 10% type I/III collagen.
  - **Control**: intraoral autologous bone harvested from mandibular body/ramus or mental symphysis, particulated with a bone mill (ACE).
- **Medication/surgical protocol**: dental polishing 10 d before; amoxicillin 2 g 1 h pre-op; 0.2% chlorhexidine rinse; articaine 4% + epinephrine anaesthesia; post-op amoxicillin 1 g × 1 wk, 0.2% chlorhexidine gel × 2 wk, ibuprofen 400 mg PRN; soft diet 2 wk; no nose blowing/straws, sneeze with mouth open, no upper removable prosthesis; sutures removed at 10 d.
- **Second stage (6 months)**: repeat CT; patient preference interview; full-thickness flap; **trephine biopsy (2 mm internal / 3 mm total diameter, Hager & Meisinger)** transalveolar at the implant site on both sides before the first drill; samples in buffered formaldehyde; submerged implants (Osseo Speed TX, Astra Tech) placed per manufacturer protocol.
- **Radiology**: CT at baseline and 6 months; hard-tissue level measured as the distance between coronal and apical bone level in the grafted region on 10 consecutive sagittal cuts at 1 mm intervals (Blue Sky Plan software).
- **Histology/histomorphometry**: H&E; the 10 most central/representative slides per sample (240 slides total) photographed at 50× (Zeiss Axioskop 40; ImageJ 1.5.0) and measured as total area, hard tissue area (bone + graft) and soft tissue area (collagen, fibroblasts, vessels, adipocytes, voids) in pixels, converted to percentages; additional 200×/400× review by a blinded experienced dentist. **Residual graft volume was not measured separately** — the authors state the hard tissue/remnant interface was indistinguishable and residual volumes were very reduced.
- **Statistics**: Excel 16.10 + SPSS 25.0, α = 0.05, analysed by an independent blinded assessor; paired t-test, one-way and two-way ANOVA; Shapiro-Wilk used to justify parametric tests (normality not rejected); results as mean ± SD.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Radiological (CT, baseline vs 6 months)**

| Parameter | Autologous (control) | Xenograft (test) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline tissue height | 3.20 ± 0.93 mm | 3.06 ± 1.13 mm | 0.69 (NS) |
| Tissue height gain | 7.81 ± 2.34 mm | 8.71 ± 2.15 mm | 0.26 (NS) |
| Range of gain | 3.7 – 12.5 mm | 5.4 – 12.5 mm | — |
| Correlation baseline vs 6 mo | r = 0.149 | r = 0.298 | > 0.05 both |

- Two-way ANOVA: **time effect p < 0.001**; **material effect p = 0.240**; **time × material interaction p = 0.261**.
- In **75% of cases** the greater height increase occurred on the xenograft side.
- Achieved height allowed implants of **9–11 mm** length.

**Histomorphometry (6-month trephine biopsies, % of total area)**

| Parameter | Autologous | Xenograft | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total hard tissue (THT) | 57.31 ± 2.91% | 56.01 ± 2.86% | 0.376 (NS) |
| THT minimum (same patient) | 49.67% | 44.19% | — |
| THT maximum (same patient) | 64.07% | 64.19% | — |
| Between-slide SD | 1.29 – 7.03% (mean 2.91%) | not reported | — |

- Correlation of THT between the two sides: **r = 0.546, p = 0.066** (positive but not significant).
- Two-way ANOVA on THT: **graft type p = 0.376 (NS)**; **patient p = 0.029 (significant)**; **material × patient interaction p < 0.001**.
- 95% CI overlap between materials in **7 of 12 patients**; mean THT higher for autologous in **3/12** and higher for xenograft in **2/12**.
- Total soft tissue volume (TST) reported as similar between groups; exact values were in Table 2, which is absent from the retrieved text.

**Histology (240 slides)**

- Both materials showed various stages of remodelling and were integrated into newly formed bone, partially surrounded by vascularised tissue; **no inflammation, infection or foreign-body reaction** in any sample.
- Xenograft side: osteoclasts, osteoblast apposition, osteocytes, irregular lacunae in particles from osteoclastic resorption; highly vascularised active formation alternating with mature bone.
- Autologous side: graft almost completely resorbed with only a few fully integrated particles; medullary spaces filled with vessel-rich soft tissue.

**Clinical / patient-reported**

- Autologous harvest site: mandibular ramus 83.3%, chin 16.7%.
- Preference: 11/12 (91.6%) "neither / both equally good"; 1/12 (8.3%) preferred the xenograft side; **none preferred the autograft side**.
- Complications: no major intra- or post-operative events; **Schneiderian membrane perforation < 2 mm in 5/24 sinuses**, all managed with a resorbable collagen membrane.
- Implant survival, ISQ, insertion torque and marginal bone loss: **not reported**.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Small sample (n = 12 patients / 24 sinuses).** The authors themselves flag this as the main limitation given inter-patient heterogeneity; the trial is powered to detect only large differences, so "no significant difference" here is not evidence of equivalence with a defined margin (no non-inferiority margin was pre-specified).
- **No implant-level or long-term outcomes** — survival, ISQ, insertion torque, marginal bone loss and graft volume stability beyond 6 months are not reported.
- **No separate residual-graft measurement.** Because the residual bone/graft interface was indistinguishable and remnants were sparse, the histomorphometry reports only total hard tissue vs total soft tissue; the *composition* of the hard tissue (new bone vs residual xenograft) is therefore unknown. This is the single most important gap relative to reviews that report new bone % and residual graft % separately.
- **Blinding inconsistency**: Methods describe a triple-blind design, whereas the Discussion calls the study "double-blind"; the intra-operative envelope opening also means the operating surgeon was aware of allocation at the time of grafting.
- **Tables 1–2 are missing from the retrieved PMC text**, so several secondary values (TST percentages, per-patient data) could not be verified from this source; only values stated in the running prose are reported here.
- **Histomorphometric sampling** used the most central 10 slides and a 2 mm trephine at the implant site, so results reflect the implant-site core, not the whole grafted volume; the authors note biopsy location and sample size may explain part of the patient effect.
- **Generalizability**: two-step (delayed) lateral approach only, residual bone height around 3 mm, single centre, single surgeon, single xenograft product (Osteobiol MP3) — not transferable to simultaneous implant placement or to other porcine products with different granulometry/collagen content.

## 6. Related Work

- **Trimmel et al. 2021** (Bayesian NMA, 34 RCTs, 842 two-stage lateral augmentations) — autologous bone alone ranked 12th by SUCRA (57%) for new bone % at 5–8 months, i.e. not the gold standard; the present trial is consistent with that ranking, though it adds no new-bone%/residual-graft% split that could enter such a network.
- **Jue et al. 2025** (multicenter split-mouth RCT, 50 patients, 198 implants) — autogenous ramus bone vs bovine bone mineral + 25% local autogenous chips gave equivalent 12-month implant survival (92.9% vs 93.9%) and clinical success; the clinical counterpart of the equivalence seen here at the histological level, and the same argument about avoiding a donor site.
- **Del Fabbro et al. 2025** (SR of 49 RCTs) — implant survival 84.6–100% with no significant difference between DBBM, autogenous ± DBBM and synthetic HA/β-TCP; the present trial contributes histomorphometric rather than survival evidence to that question.
- **Harlos et al. 2022** (RCT, wide antral cavities) — xenogenic + autogenous 70:30 produced more new bone area than xenogenic + L-PRF or xenogenic alone at 8 months; a different comparator structure (all arms contained xenograft) and an absolute-area rather than percentage metric, so it does not directly contradict the equivalence reported here.
- **Ramírez-Fernández et al.** (cited by the authors) — substantial resorption of a similar graft material after 9 months; **Choukroun/Scarano-type histology of porcine grafts cited by the authors [29,30,33,34]** — the biological rationale (collagen-driven osteoclast adhesion and rapid substitution) that the authors use to explain faster turnover on the xenograft side.

## 7. Glossary

- **Split-mouth design**: each patient receives both interventions (one per sinus), so comparisons are within-patient and patient-level confounders cancel by design.
- **Two-stage (delayed) lateral sinus lift**: lateral antral wall osteotomy with grafting first, implant placement after a healing period (here 6 months) — used when residual bone height is too low for simultaneous implant placement.
- **Osteobiol MP3 (OMP3)**: cortico-medullary porcine bone xenograft, 600–1000 µm granules, combined with 10% type I/III collagen (Tecnoss, Italy).
- **THT / TST (Total Hard Tissue / Total Soft Tissue)**: histomorphometric percentages of hard tissue (bone + graft) and soft tissue (collagen, fibroblasts, vessels, adipocytes, voids) in the biopsy area.
- **Residual graft vs new bone**: the two components of histomorphometric hard tissue; the present study did not separate them.
- **SUCRA (Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking)**: a 0–100% score in network meta-analysis expressing the probability that a treatment is the best among all compared.
- **Schneiderian membrane**: the sinus mucoperiosteum elevated during sinus floor augmentation; perforation is the most common intra-operative complication.
- **Piezoelectric osteotomy**: ultrasonic bone cutting that spares soft tissue, used here for the lateral window.
