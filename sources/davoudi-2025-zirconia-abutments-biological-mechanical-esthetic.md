## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 14 systematic reviews/meta-analyses (AMSTAR-screened, ≥14/16 score; published through March 2023; 6,456 patients, 10,063 implants) comparing zirconia (Zr) vs. titanium (Ti) implant abutments on biological, esthetic, and mechanical/survival outcomes.

Zr abutments showed no difference or favorable results vs. Ti for marginal bone loss (4/10 reviews favored Zr, 6/10 no difference), soft tissue recession, and gingival discoloration; mechanical complication risk ratios were non-significant (0.87 and 0.52) and survival rates were statistically comparable (Zr 98.6–98.8% vs. Ti 98.62–99.4%) despite Ti's theoretically superior fracture resistance (1454 N vs. 443.6 N) and flexural strength (2000 MPa vs. 900–1200 MPa).

Clinically, Zr abutments are a viable, esthetically favorable alternative to Ti without meaningful loss of mechanical reliability, provided excessive occlusal forces/improper angulation are avoided; limitation is high inter-review heterogeneity and inconsistent/underpowered meta-analyses for esthetic indices (PES/WES/ICAI/CIS).

## 세줄요약

우산형 검토(Umbrella Review) — AMSTAR 체크리스트로 선별된(SR≥14점, MA≥16점) 14편의 체계적 문헌고찰/메타분석(2023년 3월까지 발표, 환자 6,456명·임플란트 10,063개) 대상, 지르코니아(Zirconia, Zr) vs. 티타늄(Titanium, Ti) 임플란트 어버트먼트(abutment)의 생물학적·심미적·기계적/생존 지표 비교.

변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 10편 중 4편이 Zr 우세, 6편이 무차이; 연조직 퇴축(recession)·치은 변색도 대체로 무차이 또는 Zr 우세; 기계적 합병증 위험비(Risk Ratio)는 비유의(0.87, 0.52), 생존율도 통계적으로 동등(Zr 98.6~98.8% vs. Ti 98.62~99.4%) — 이는 이론적 파절저항(Ti 1454N vs. Zr 443.6N)·굴곡강도(Ti 2000MPa vs. Zr 900~1200MPa) 상 Ti 우위와 대비되는 결과.

임상적으로 과도한 교합력·부적절한 경사만 피한다면 Zr 어버트먼트는 기계적 신뢰성 저하 없이 심미적으로 유리한 Ti의 대안이 될 수 있으나, 리뷰 간 이질성(heterogeneity)이 크고 심미지수(PES/WES/ICAI/CIS) 메타분석은 불충분·비일관적이라는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review specifically stratifying Zr-vs-Ti abutment evidence into three domains (biological, esthetic, mechanical/survival), with an explicit evidence-consistency table (Table 6) mapping which of the 14 underlying reviews favored Zr, favored Ti, or found no difference per outcome.
- Highlights the disconnect between abutment-material bench-level mechanical properties (Ti's higher fracture resistance/flexural strength) and clinically observed equivalence in survival and mechanical complication rates.
- Notes clinical mechanistic explanations for Zr's biological favorability (smoother/more corrosion-resistant surface → better epithelial/fibroblast adhesion, less bacterial colonization) and identifies angulation limits (stock Zr ≤15–20°, CAD/CAM Zr ≤30°) as practical mitigations for Zr mechanical-fracture risk.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review (SR/MA of SRs/MAs)
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, ProQuest, Cochrane (search through 2023)
- **Screening**: 457 titles after dedup → 16 full-text → 14 final after AMSTAR quality filter (SR <14/32 or MA <16/32 excluded); inter-rater kappa 0.90 (abstract) / 0.93 (full-text)
- **n**: 14 included SRs/MAs; pooled 6,456 patients, 10,063 implants
- **Outcomes**: Biological (MBL, PPD, BOP, plaque accumulation, recession, keratinized mucosa width/thickness); Esthetic (PES, WES, Copenhagen Index Score, Implant Crown Aesthetic Index, papilla index, gingival discoloration); Mechanical/survival (veneer failure, screw loosening, fracture, implant/abutment survival)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Finding |
|---|---|
| MBL | 10 reviews: 6 no difference, 4 favored Zr |
| PPD | 7 reviews: majority no difference, 1 favored Zr, 1 favored Ti |
| Recession | 7 reviews: majority no difference; some favored Zr |
| BOP / plaque accumulation | 6 reviews: 2 favored Ti, rest no difference |
| Gingival discoloration | 8 reviews: 2 favored Zr, 4 no difference, 2 no meta-analysis |
| Mechanical complication RR | 0.87 and 0.52 (both non-significant); prevalence <7.9% |
| Survival rate | No significant difference (5 reviews); reported rates Zr 98.6–98.8% vs. Ti 98.62–99.4%; Totou et al. found Ti superior specifically for abutment fracture |
| Theoretical material properties | Fracture resistance Ti 1454 N vs. Zr 443.6 N; flexural strength Ti 2000 MPa vs. Zr 900–1200 MPa |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High heterogeneity across the 14 underlying reviews (follow-up range 0.08–13 years); several meta-analyses underpowered or based on few pooled studies.
- No meta-analysis was possible for objective esthetic indices (PES/WES/CIS/ICAI/PI) — qualitative synthesis only, inconclusive.
- Radiographic MBL measurement error may exceed the reported between-group differences in several pooled reviews.
- Recommends future long-term (5–10 year) studies with life-table survival analysis and precise implant-at-risk accounting.

## 6. Related Work

- nasri-2025 (umbrella review of zirconia surface conditioning/bonding) — different scope (adhesive chemistry vs. abutment material clinical outcome).
