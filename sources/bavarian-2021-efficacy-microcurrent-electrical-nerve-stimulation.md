## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of microcurrent electrical nerve stimulation (MENS) for myofascial pain of the masticatory muscles; four independent comparisons from three studies (n = 140) were pooled, with pain on palpation as the primary outcome.

MENS reduced pain by a mean of -0.57 points (95% CI -0.91 to -0.23) versus placebo and other therapies, with high heterogeneity (I2 = 83.7%).

The authors call MENS an effective non-invasive option, but the evidence base is three small studies with marked between-study variability, so the estimate is fragile.

## 세줄요약

근막통이 있는 저작근에 대한 미세전류 전기신경자극 (Microcurrent Electrical Nerve Stimulation, MENS)의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석으로, 3개 연구의 독립 비교 4건 (n = 140)을 합쳤고 일차 결과는 촉진 시 통증 감소.

MENS는 위약 및 다른 치료 대비 평균 통증을 -0.57점 (95% CI -0.91 ~ -0.23) 줄였으나 이질성이 높았음 (I2 = 83.7%).

저자들은 MENS를 효과적인 비침습 치료로 평가하지만, 근거가 소규모 연구 3편이고 연구 간 변동이 커서 추정치는 불안정함.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Primary outcome: reduction in pain on palpation of the masticatory muscles. Four independent comparisons from three studies (n = 140) entered the meta-analysis; comparators were placebo and other therapies. Search strategy, databases, risk-of-bias tool and model type are not given in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Mean pain reduction -0.57 points (CI -0.91 to -0.23), I2 = 83.7%. The pain scale and unit are not stated in the abstract.
