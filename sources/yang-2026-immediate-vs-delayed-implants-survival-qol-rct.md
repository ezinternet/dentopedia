# Source: Yang et al. 2026 — Immediate vs Delayed Implant RCT (n=220)

## Citation

Yang Y, Zhou S, Ma Y, Wang X, Chen J, Dong Q. Impact of Immediate vs Delayed Dental Implants on Survival, Patient Satisfaction, and Quality of Life. *Oral Health Prev Dent.* 2026;24:77-88. DOI: [10.3290/j.ohpd.c_2438](https://doi.org/10.3290/j.ohpd.c_2438)

## Why Ingested

This is the largest single RCT (n=220) directly comparing immediate vs delayed implant placement at 1 year, adding patient-reported outcomes (OHIP-14, VAS satisfaction) and aesthetics (PES, WES) alongside survival — outcomes understudied in prior SRs. Provides highest-quality individual trial evidence for the immediate-implant category. Complements [[wiki/immediate-implant/garcia-sanchez-2022-immediate-vs-delayed-implant-placement-sr-ma]] with granular secondary outcome data and broader patient-centered metrics aligned with the ID-COSM consensus.

## Study Design

- Parallel-group RCT, 1:1 allocation ratio
- Setting: Single Chinese hospital
- Enrollment: January 2022 – October 2023; 256 screened → 232 eligible → 220 enrolled
- Sample: n=110 immediate (observation group), n=110 delayed (control group)
- Randomisation: Permuted blocks of size 10, sealed opaque envelopes, independent researcher
- Blinding: Outcome assessors (PES/WES) blinded to group allocation; no patient/clinician blinding (inherent to surgical timing)
- Power: 98 per arm (α=0.05, β=0.20, 10-point OHIP-14 difference); enrolled 110/arm for 10% attrition
- Follow-up: 1 year post-functional loading
- Ethics: Declaration of Helsinki; Ethics approval GN-CTC-121
- Registration: Not explicitly stated

## Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria

**Inclusion**: Age ≥18; single missing tooth; normal occlusion without bruxism; adequate CBCT bone quality (>500 HU, ≥10 mm height, ≥6 mm width); non-smokers or light smokers (<10 cigarettes/day); no periodontitis history.

**Exclusion**: Systemic/bone metabolic disease (uncontrolled hypertension, osteoporosis); severe bruxism/clenching; drug/material allergy; malignancy; pregnancy/lactation; uncontrolled diabetes or heart disease.

## Implant Protocol

Both groups: Straumann Bone Level Tapered implants; insertion torque ≥35 N·cm.

**Delayed (control)**: Minimally invasive extraction → 3-month healing → step-by-step site preparation → implant at 3 mm below ideal gingival margin, 1.5 mm from adjacent tooth; gaps >2 mm filled with Bio-Oss. Tension-free closure with 4-0 PGA sutures.

**Immediate (observation)**: Atraumatic extraction → same-session implant; palatal bone wall for primary stability; apex 3.0–5.0 mm beyond socket apex; platform 0.5 mm below alveolar crest; buccal/lingual bone ≥1.0 mm; gaps filled with Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide resorbable collagen membrane; buccal flap advanced coronally for tension-free closure.

Postoperative care identical in both groups (chlorhexidine 0.12%, ibuprofen 400 mg PRN, soft diet 2 weeks).

## Outcome Measures

**Primary**: Implant survival at 1 year (no mobility, no peri-implant radiolucency, evidence of osseointegration).

**Secondary** (assessed per ID-COSM consensus):
- Periodontal indices: Probing Depth (PD), modified Plaque Index (mPLI), modified Sulcus Bleeding Index (mSBI)
- Aesthetics: Pink Esthetic Score (PES, 7 parameters, max 14), White Esthetic Score (WES, 5 parameters, max 10)
- QoL: Chinese OHIP-14 (Cronbach's α=0.94; ICC=0.91); lower score = better
- Patient satisfaction: VAS (0–10) in 4 domains: attachment level, colour match, overall aesthetics, masticatory function

## Baseline Data

Comparable between groups (all P>0.05):
- Mean age: 40.5 (delayed) vs 41.7 (immediate) years
- Male: 56.4% vs 52.7%
- BMI: 22.6 vs 22.7 kg/m²
- Tooth location: premolar ~38%, posterior ~62% (both groups)
- Causes: caries ~58%, trauma ~23%, chronic periapical inflammation ~20%

## Results

### Primary — Implant Survival (1 year)

| Outcome | Immediate (n=110) | Delayed (n=110) | Statistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Survived | 105/110 (95.45%) | 102/110 (92.73%) | χ²=0.736, P=0.391 |
| Failed | 5 | 8 | Not significant |

95% CI: 89.7–98.5% (immediate) vs 86.2–96.8% (delayed).

Multivariate logistic regression (adjusting for age, sex, tooth region): timing not independently associated with survival (adjusted OR=1.82, 95% CI: 0.56–5.91, P=0.321).

### Secondary — Periodontal Indices (1 year post-restoration)

| Index | Immediate | Delayed | Mean difference | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Probing Depth (mm) | 3.16 ± 0.51 | 3.39 ± 0.59 | −0.23 (95% CI: −0.39 to −0.07) | 0.005 |
| mPLI | 0.95 ± 0.38 | 1.21 ± 0.54 | −0.26 (95% CI: −0.40 to −0.12) | <0.001 |
| mSBI | 0.85 ± 0.47 | 1.01 ± 0.35 | −0.16 (95% CI: −0.27 to −0.05) | 0.004 |

Both groups improved significantly from baseline; immediate group showed greater absolute improvements.

### Secondary — Aesthetics (1 year)

| Score | Immediate | Delayed | Mean difference | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PES total | 12.18 ± 1.13 | 11.34 ± 1.30 | +0.84 (95% CI: 0.51–1.17) | <0.001 |
| WES total | 7.78 ± 0.99 | 7.23 ± 1.10 | +0.55 (95% CI: 0.28–0.82) | <0.001 |

Immediate group superior in labial mucosal curvature, mucosal height, soft tissue colour and texture (PES); restoration shape and surface texture (WES).

### Secondary — Quality of Life (OHIP-14, 1 year)

| Outcome | Immediate | Delayed | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| OHIP-14 score | 3.20 ± 1.33 | 4.15 ± 1.23 | <0.001 |

Lower score = better QoL. Immediate group had significantly better oral health-related QoL.

### Secondary — Patient Satisfaction (VAS, 1 year)

| VAS Domain | Immediate | Delayed | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attachment level | 8.12 ± 1.05 | 7.45 ± 1.18 | <0.05 |
| Colour match | 8.67 ± 0.89 | 8.15 ± 1.12 | <0.05 |
| Overall aesthetics | Not separately reported | — | — |
| Masticatory function | 8.89 ± 0.94 | 8.23 ± 1.08 | <0.05 |

## Statistical Methods

GraphPad Prism 9.5.0; Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality test; independent t-test (normal) or Mann-Whitney U (non-normal) for between-group comparisons; paired t-test or Wilcoxon for within-group; chi-square or Fisher's exact for categorical; multivariate logistic/linear regression for adjusted analyses. All P-values two-sided; significance at P<0.05.

## Authors' Conclusions

No significant difference in 1-year implant survival between immediate and delayed placement. Immediate implantation yielded superior secondary outcomes: better peri-implant health (PD, mPLI, mSBI), superior aesthetics (PES, WES), better oral health-related QoL (OHIP-14), and higher patient satisfaction (VAS). The authors suggest immediate implantation may be preferable when surgical conditions permit.

## Limitations (noted by authors or inferred)

- Single-centre Chinese hospital — generalizability may be limited
- 1-year follow-up only — long-term survival and marginal bone loss not assessed
- No patient or clinician blinding (inherent to surgical study)
- Failure mode and reasons for 13 failed implants not detailed
- No marginal bone loss (MBL) data reported — key peri-implant metric absent
- VAS "overall aesthetics" domain results not clearly separated in reporting
- CONSORT flow diagram referenced but exact dropout details at each stage not provided in full text

## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=220) comparing 1-year outcomes of immediate vs delayed single-tooth implant placement; primary outcome (survival) was equivalent (95.45% vs 92.73%, P=0.391). Immediate placement yielded significantly better peri-implant health (PD, mPLI, mSBI), aesthetics (PES +0.84, WES +0.55), oral health QoL (OHIP-14 3.20 vs 4.15), and patient satisfaction across all VAS domains. Single Chinese center, 1-year only, no MBL data.

## 세줄요약

즉시식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP) vs 지연식립 (Delayed Implant Placement, DIP)을 비교한 RCT(n=220); 1년 임플란트 생존율은 동등(95.45% vs 92.73%, 유의차 없음). 즉시식립이 치주 지수(치주낭깊이 Probing Depth, PD·mPLI·mSBI), 심미 점수(핑크심미점수 Pink Esthetic Score, PES·화이트심미점수 White Esthetic Score, WES), 구강건강관련 삶의 질(OHIP-14), 환자 만족도(VAS) 모든 영역에서 유의하게 우수. 단일기관 중국 연구·1년 추적·변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 미보고는 한계.
