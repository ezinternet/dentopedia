## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA, PROSPERO-registered, AMSTAR 2 quality-appraised) synthesizing 10 systematic reviews/meta-analyses (n=4 to 98 studies each) comparing e-cigarette (ENDS) users, traditional cigarette smokers, and non-smokers on oral biofilm/plaque accumulation and oral microbiota composition.

E-cigarette users consistently showed a higher plaque index than non-smokers (though generally less pronounced than in traditional smokers) and evidence of microbial dysbiosis — increased cariogenic (S. mutans, Lactobacilli), periodontal-pathogenic (P. gingivalis, F. nucleatum), and opportunistic (Candida spp.) organisms — linking vaping to elevated risk of caries, periodontitis, peri-implantitis, and oral candidiasis.

Nine of the ten included reviews were AMSTAR 2 "moderate" quality (mainly for incomplete exclusion-list reporting) and one "high"; substantial cross-study heterogeneity and predominantly cross-sectional primary evidence mean the umbrella review should be read as a consistent-direction signal rather than a quantified dose-response verdict.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review specifically synthesizing SR/MA-level evidence on e-cigarette effects on both oral biofilm/plaque accumulation AND oral microbiota composition, side by side.
- Consolidates 10 independent SR/MAs (totaling several hundred underlying primary studies, ranging from 4 to 98 studies each) into a single risk-direction summary across caries, periodontal, peri-implant, and candidal outcomes.
- Explicitly reports AMSTAR 2 quality grading per included review and identifies the dominant systematic weakness across the field (incomplete/absent list of excluded studies; grey-literature search gaps).
- Surfaces mechanistic candidates — reduced salivary lysozyme/lactoferrin, elevated pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-10, IL-12, TNF-α), rapid (within 24h) microbial community shifts after ENDS exposure — that likely underlie the clinical plaque/dysbiosis findings.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA-guided umbrella review; PECO framework (Population: e-cigarette users/cigarette smokers/non-smokers; Exposure: e-cigarettes/ENDS; Comparison: non-smokers vs. traditional smokers; Outcome: biofilm/plaque index and oral microbiota composition).
- **Search**: PubMed/MEDLINE, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library, Scopus, up to 12 March 2025, studies published 2015–2025, no language restriction; supplemented by hand search of reference lists.
- **Screening**: 398 records retrieved → 11 duplicates removed → 387 screened → 18 excluded (not SR/MA) → 357 excluded (not e-cigarette/oral biofilm focus) → 2 excluded (commentaries) → 10 SR/MAs included, all meeting inclusion criteria.
- **Data extraction**: dual independent extraction (B.P./A.M.), dual verification (E.P.B./F.D.P.), disagreements resolved by a third blinded reviewer.
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR 2 tool (high/moderate/low/critically-low confidence ratings based on critical vs. non-critical flaw counts).
- **Statistical synthesis**: narrative/qualitative synthesis only; no formal re-meta-analysis across the 10 included reviews (heterogeneity of study designs/outcomes precluded pooling).

|---|---|---|---|
| Tattar et al. 2025 | SR+MA | 40 | Oral microbiome alterations linked to e-cig use; smokers had worst outcomes |
| Shabil et al. 2024 | SR+MA | 12 | Significantly higher plaque index in e-cig users vs. non-users |
| Vámos et al. 2024 | SR + network MA | 39 | Higher plaque index and worse peri-implant parameters vs. non-smokers (implant population) |
| Zięba et al. 2024 | SR | 65 | Increased cariogenic bacteria (S. mutans, Lactobacilli); thickened biofilm via nicotine-driven adhesion/EPS synthesis |
| Youssef et al. 2023 | SR+MA | 4 | Higher plaque index, worse peri-implant clinical/radiographic/inflammatory profile (male implant patients) |
| Camoni et al. 2023 | SR+MA | 84 | Association with self-reported gingivitis; flavored e-liquids enamel-detrimental; carcinogenic/inflammatory biomarkers detected |
| Thiem et al. 2023 | SR+MA | 16 | Plaque index highest in cigarette smokers > e-cig users ≈ non-smokers in most studies |
| Figueredo et al. 2021 | SR | 8 | Consistently increased plaque index with e-cig use (≥1y users) |
| Yang et al. 2020 | SR | 98 | Worse periodontal/peri-implant outcomes, more tooth loss, worsening caries over 6 months; only "high" AMSTAR 2 review |
| Ralho et al. 2019 | SR | 8 | No consistent plaque-index difference in most studies, but higher Candida spp. prevalence vs. non-smokers |

- **Quality distribution**: 9/10 reviews AMSTAR 2 "moderate," 1/10 (Yang 2020) "high." No review rated low/critically-low.
- **Mechanistic evidence cited**: elevated salivary pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-10, IL-12, TNF-α) in e-cig users; reduced antimicrobial proteins (lysozyme, lactoferrin); microbial community shifts detectable within 24h of ENDS exposure; enrichment of P. gingivalis and F. nucleatum.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No quantitative re-meta-analysis was performed across the 10 reviews — findings are narratively synthesized only, limiting precision of any pooled effect estimate.
- Substantial heterogeneity in study design, population (e.g., some SRs restricted to implant patients or male-only cohorts), and outcome measures across the included reviews.
- 9/10 reviews downgraded to "moderate" AMSTAR 2 quality primarily for incomplete excluded-study lists and limited grey-literature searching — a systemic methodological gap across this literature, not specific to any one review.
- Most underlying primary evidence is cross-sectional and self-report-dependent (smoking/vaping status), limiting causal inference.
- Authors explicitly call for longitudinal studies, standardized outcome measures, and mechanistic work on specific e-liquid ingredients (nicotine, flavorings) and immune-response pathways.

## 7. Glossary

- **ENDS**: Electronic Nicotine Delivery System (e-cigarette, vape device).
- **Umbrella review**: a systematic review that synthesizes findings from multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a topic, rather than primary studies directly.
- **AMSTAR 2**: A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews (2nd version) — critical-appraisal instrument rating SR/MA methodological quality as high/moderate/low/critically low.
- **PECO**: Population, Exposure, Comparison, Outcome — review question framework (the exposure-study analogue of PICO).
- **Dysbiosis**: disruption of the normal balance of the oral (or other) microbiota, favoring pathogenic/opportunistic organisms.
- **Plaque index**: clinical scoring system quantifying dental biofilm/plaque accumulation on tooth surfaces.
