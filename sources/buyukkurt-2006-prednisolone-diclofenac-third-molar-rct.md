## Why Ingested
프레드니솔론 단독 vs 프레드니솔론+디클로페낙(NSAIDs) 병용 RCT — 스테로이드-NSAID 복합 요법의 근거. [[drug/liborio-2025-methylprednisolone-third-molar-sr-ma]]의 병용 요법 공백을 보완하며, 임상에서 흔히 사용되는 스테로이드+소염진통제 조합의 원저 데이터.

## Three-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (n=45) from Turkey compared immediate postoperative IM injection of prednisolone 25 mg alone, prednisolone 25 mg combined with diclofenac, or saline control in patients undergoing lower third molar removal.
The prednisolone+diclofenac combination was significantly superior to both prednisolone alone and control for pain at 6h, trismus at days 2 and 7, and swelling at day 7 (all p<0.05); prednisolone alone reduced swelling vs control at day 2 only.
Results support combining a corticosteroid with an NSAID for comprehensive postoperative symptom control, though the small sample (n=45, 15 per group) and abstract-only access limit the study's generalizability.

## 세줄요약
줄1: 터키의 3군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=45)에서 하악 사랑니 발치 직후 프레드니솔론 (Prednisolone) 25 mg 단독, 프레드니솔론+디클로페낙 (Diclofenac) 병용, 또는 식염수 대조군을 근육 내 주사로 비교했다.
줄2: 프레드니솔론+디클로페낙 병용군이 6시간 통증·2·7일 개구제한 (Trismus)·7일 부종 (Swelling) 모두에서 단독군 및 대조군 대비 유의하게 우월했으며(p<0.05), 프레드니솔론 단독군은 2일 부종만 대조군 대비 유의했다.
줄3: 코르티코스테로이드 (Corticosteroid)와 비스테로이드성 소염진통제 (Nonsteroidal Anti-inflammatory Drug, NSAID) 병용이 술후 종합 관리에 유리하나, 군당 15명의 소규모 표본과 초록만 접근 가능한 점이 일반화 한계다.

## One-line Summary
RCT (n=45, 3 groups) found prednisolone 25 mg IM alone reduced swelling vs control at day 2, but the prednisolone+diclofenac IM combination showed superior pain suppression at 6h, trismus reduction at days 2 and 7, and swelling reduction at day 7.

## 한줄요약
3군 RCT (n=45)에서 프레드니솔론(Prednisolone) 25 mg 근주 단독은 2일 부종을 감소시켰으나, 프레드니솔론+디클로페낙(Diclofenac) 병용군이 통증(6시간), 개구제한(2·7일), 부종(7일) 모두에서 단독군 및 대조군보다 우월했다.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, 2006 Dec;64(12):1761-1766
- DOI: 10.1016/j.joms.2006.07.010 (inferred from abstract citation)
- Institution: Turkey (Department of OMS, not specified in abstract)
- Design: Three-arm RCT

## 2. Key Contributions
- One of few RCTs directly comparing prednisolone alone vs prednisolone+diclofenac combination vs control
- Demonstrates additive/synergistic effect of combining corticosteroid + NSAID
- Establishes prednisolone+diclofenac combination as superior for overall postoperative management
- Early (6h) pain data with VAS — captures immediate postoperative period often missed in longer-interval studies
- Clinical recommendation: combination useful when "extensive postoperative soft tissue swelling is anticipated"

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Three-arm RCT
- n=45 patients, surgical removal of lower third molar
- Groups:
  - I: 25 mg prednisolone IM (immediately post-op)
  - II: 25 mg prednisolone + diclofenac IM (immediately post-op)
  - III: Sterile saline (control)
- Outcomes:
  - Pain: VAS on day of surgery (hours 6 and 7)
  - Trismus: At days 2 and 7
  - Swelling: At days 2 and 7
- Statistics: ANOVA

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Pain at 6h: Prednisolone+diclofenac > control (p<0.05); prednisolone alone NS vs control
- Pain at 7h: Both prednisolone and prednisolone+diclofenac > control (p<0.05)
- Trismus at days 2 and 7: Prednisolone+diclofenac > prednisolone alone and control (p<0.05)
- Swelling at day 2: Both prednisolone groups < control (p<0.05)
- Swelling at day 7: Prednisolone+diclofenac < both prednisolone alone and control (p<0.05)
- No adverse effects reported

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small n=45 (15 per group) — underpowered for subgroup analysis
- Abstract-only access — blinding details, exact diclofenac dose, and randomization process unclear
- Single timepoint pain VAS (hours 6 and 7) — limited temporal coverage
- Post-operative administration only — no preemptive dosing comparison
- Prednisolone dose (25 mg) lower than most studies (40 mg) — limits comparison

## 7. Glossary
- **Diclofenac**: NSAID (non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug) acting via COX-1/COX-2 inhibition
- **Prednisolone**: Intermediate-acting synthetic glucocorticoid; equipotent to methylprednisolone (1:1 ratio)
- **IM (Intramuscular)**: Injection into muscle tissue for systemic absorption
- **ANOVA**: Analysis of variance — parametric test for comparing means across ≥3 groups
