## Why Ingested

This RCT validates the use of deproteinized bovine bone mineral with collagen (DBBM+C) as an adjunct to immediate implant placement and immediate provisionalization in the esthetic zone, demonstrating measurable soft and hard tissue preservation benefits. Complements [[wiki/immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/pitman-2022-immediate-implant-provisionalization-sr-ma]] by providing RCT-level evidence for a specific biomaterial strategy in the immediate-implant–esthetic workflow.

## Three-line Summary
RCT (n=22 patients, maxillary anterior immediate implants with immediate provisionalization; 6-month follow-up) comparing flapless immediate implant placement with vs. without deproteinized bovine bone–collagen (DBBM+C) socket grafting.
DBBM+C group showed significantly greater soft tissue height at mesiobuccal and distobuccal sites at 3 and 6 months (p < 0.05), and higher buccolingual ridge dimension at 6 months (p < 0.05).
DBBM+C as an adjunct to immediate implant + immediate temporization preserves soft and hard tissue dimensions better than implant placement alone, supporting its use in esthetic-zone protocols.

## 세줄요약
RCT (n=22 환자, 상악 전치 즉시식립 + 즉시 임시 보철; 6개월 추적)로 탈단백 우골 유래 콜라겐 (DBBM+C, Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral with Collagen) 소켓 이식 유무 비교.
DBBM+C군이 3개월·6개월에 근심협측·원심협측 연조직 높이가 더 높았고 (p < 0.05), 6개월에 협설측 치조제 치수가 더 컸음 (p < 0.05).
DBBM+C를 즉시식립 + 즉시 임시 보철과 함께 사용하면 단독 식립보다 연조직·경조직 치수 보존이 우수하며, 심미 영역 프로토콜에의 사용을 지지.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT demonstrating that deproteinized bovine bone mineral with collagen (DBBM+C) preserves soft tissue height dimensions when used as a socket graft in immediate implant placement with immediate temporization
- Evidence that DBBM+C improves buccolingual (buccal–lingual ridge width) hard tissue dimension at 6 months post-implant
- Prospective quantification of soft tissue contour changes (mesiobuccal, midbuccal, distobuccal) over the immediate-implant esthetic healing timeline

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Single-center, randomized controlled trial (RCT)
- **Participants**: 22 patients needing maxillary incisor extraction with anatomic feasibility for immediate implant placement
- **Randomization**: 11 test (DBBM+C group), 11 control (no biomaterial)
- **Intervention groups**:
  - **Test**: Immediate implant placement + deproteinized bovine bone mineral derived with collagen inserted into the alveolus + immediate temporization
  - **Control**: Immediate implant placement + immediate temporization (no socket graft)
- **Soft tissue measurements**: Probing at baseline, 3 months, 6 months (mesiobuccal, midbuccal, distobuccal sites)
- **Hard tissue assessment**: Cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) at baseline and 6 months post-implant to evaluate alveolar ridge buccolingual dimension
- **Follow-up**: 6 months

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Test Group (DBBM+C) | Control (No Graft) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soft tissue height at MB, DB (3 mo) | Higher | Lower | < 0.05 |
| Soft tissue height at MB, DB (6 mo) | Higher | Lower | < 0.05 |
| Buccolingual ridge dimension (6 mo) | Higher | Lower | < 0.05 |

- The test group demonstrated statistically significant preservation of soft tissue height at mesiobuccal and distobuccal sites at both 3-month and 6-month time points compared to control.
- Bone dimension measurements by CBCT confirmed greater ridge width in the buccolingual direction in the test group at 6 months.
- No perioperative complications or implant failures reported in either group during the 6-month observation period.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Relatively small sample size (n=22) and single-center design limit generalizability
- Short follow-up period (6 months); longer-term remodeling dynamics remain unclear
- Esthetic outcome measures (pink esthetic score, white esthetic score, papilla assessment) not reported, limiting clinical esthetic inference beyond dimensional metrics
- No cost-benefit analysis or patient-reported outcomes (satisfaction/comfort) documented
- Single implant system and surgical technique used; findings may not generalize to different implant designs or clinician approaches
- No histologic/radiographic assessment of graft incorporation or osseointegration kinetics

## 7. Glossary
- **DBBM**: Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral—xenograft bone substitute derived from bovine cortical bone with protein removed; widely used for alveolar ridge preservation and socket grafting
- **DBBM+C**: Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral with Collagen—DBBM combined with a type-I collagen membrane; provides moldable scaffold and enhances hemostasis
- **Socket grafting / alveolar preservation**: Placement of biomaterial into an extraction socket to reduce post-extraction dimensional bone loss (ridge resorption)
- **Flapless immediate implant**: Implant placement without elevation of a full-thickness mucoperiosteal flap; minimizes surgical trauma and preserves soft tissue blood supply
- **Immediate provisionalization / immediate temporization**: Placement of a tooth-colored temporary restoration (provisional crown) on the implant on the day of implant placement; maintains emergence profile and soft tissue anatomy
- **Buccolingual ridge dimension**: The width (thickness) of the alveolar ridge measured from buccal to lingual aspect; key predictor of soft tissue esthetic longevity and implant success
- **Pink Esthetic Score**: Semi-quantitative assessment of peri-implant soft tissue contour and color in relation to natural adjacent teeth (Fürhauser et al., standard in immediate-implant esthetics research)
