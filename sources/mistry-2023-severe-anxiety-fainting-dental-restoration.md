## Three-line Summary

Clinical vignette (Penn Medicine 2023; Dent Clin North Am 67(3):499-501) describing a patient with severe dental anxiety who experienced vasovagal syncope during a restorative appointment, with guidance on prodromal recognition and emergency response.

Key teaching: dental anxiety/phobia is itself a vasovagal trigger; providers must recognize the prodrome (pallor, diaphoresis, dizziness, nausea/vomiting) early and immediately assess airway, breathing, and cardiovascular status before deciding whether basic life support and EMS activation are required.

No original data — a brief management-focused case summary (2 pages) aimed at practitioners who may encounter syncopal patients in a general dental chair.

## 세줄요약

임상 증례 단신 (Penn Medicine, Dent Clin North Am 2023, 2쪽) — 극도의 치과 불안·공포를 가진 복원 치료 환자에서 VVS 발생 사례와 관리 지침.

핵심: 치과 공포 자체가 VVS 유발 요인; 전구증상(안면 창백, 발한, 어지러움, 오심/구토) 조기 인식 → 기도·호흡·순환(ABCs) 즉시 평가 → 필요 시 BLS + EMS 호출.

원본 데이터 없는 실무 지침형 단신으로, 불안-VVS 연결을 명시하는 것이 주요 기여.
