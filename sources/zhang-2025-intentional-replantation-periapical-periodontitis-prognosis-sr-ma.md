## Why Ingested

위키에는 근관치료 실패 시의 옵션으로 재치료([[endodontics/visit-protocol/karaoglan-2022-single-two-visit-retreatment-periapical-rct]] 등)와 치근단수술([[endodontics/gowda-2025-guided-tissue-regeneration-surgical-endodontic-outcome-umbrella]], [[endodontics/he-2026-hemostatic-agents-periapical-surgery-sr-ma]])은 있었지만, **의도적 재식(Intentional Replantation, IR)을 다룬 페이지가 전무했다** — 발치와 임플란트 직전 경계선(extraction threshold)에 정확히 놓인 옵션인데도. 이 SR+MA(39편, 2,305치아)는 그 공백을 채우면서, "발치·임플란트 대신 뭘 더 할 수 있나"라는 치료계획 질문에 대한 구체적 답 하나에 수치를 붙인다. 단, 헤드라인 수치(생존 0.89·성공 0.78)는 **39편 중 31편이 QUIPS 종합 위험도 high risk**라는 근거 위에 서 있다는 점을 반드시 같이 읽어야 한다 — low risk 연구는 단 한 편도 없었다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42023460388; PRISMA 2020) of 39 studies (49 reports, 2,215 patients, 2,305 teeth; searches to June 2024 across ten databases per abstract — nine explicitly named in-text — plus two trial registers) on intentional replantation (IR) for teeth with periapical periodontitis, assessing overall prognosis and prognostic factors via the QUIPS tool.

Pooled success rate was 0.78 (95% CI 0.70–0.84) and pooled survival rate 0.89 (95% CI 0.85–0.93) — but **31 of 39 included studies were judged high risk of overall bias and the remaining 8 moderate; not one study was low risk**, and GRADE certainty for every outcome was rated low or very low. The annual breakdown behind the pooled 89% figure shows real decay over time: 0.98 at 3 months, 0.91 at 1 year, 0.63 at 8 years.

Two prognostic factors reached statistical significance: age (survival rate ratio, SRR, 0.73, 95% CI 0.53–0.99, p=0.048 — younger patients, <25y, had higher survival; CI nearly touches 1, fragile) and combined periodontal-endodontic lesions (CPEL) (SRR 0.80, 95% CI 0.67–0.95, p=0.001 — presence of CPEL lowered survival; firmer signal, and mechanistically coherent since CPEL implies the periodontal attachment IR depends on is already compromised). The authors explicitly flag the absence of large-sample cohorts and call for standardized protocols; this review does not compare IR against extraction+implant.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(SR)+메타분석(MA)(PROSPERO CRD42023460388; PRISMA 2020) — 근단치주염(periapical periodontitis) 치아에 대한 의도적 재식(Intentional Replantation, IR)의 전체 예후 및 예후인자를 평가한 연구 39편(보고서 49편, 환자 2,215명, 치아 2,305개; 2024년 6월까지 초록 기준 10개 DB — 본문엔 9개만 명시 — + 임상시험 레지스트리 2곳 검색), 편향위험은 QUIPS 도구로 평가.

통합 성공률 0.78(95% 신뢰구간(Confidence Interval, CI) 0.70–0.84), 통합 생존율 0.89(95% CI 0.85–0.93)였으나 **39편 중 31편이 전체 편향위험 high risk, 나머지 8편도 moderate — low risk는 단 한 편도 없었다**, GRADE 근거확실성도 모든 결과에서 low 또는 very low였다. 89% 생존율의 근거가 된 연간 추이는 실제로 하락한다: 3개월 0.98 → 1년 0.91 → 8년 0.63.

통계적으로 유의한 예후인자는 두 가지: 연령(생존율비(Survival Rate Ratio, SRR) 0.73, 95% CI 0.53–0.99, p=0.048 — 25세 미만이 생존율 높음; CI가 1에 근접해 취약한 신호)과 치주-치수 복합병변(Combined Periodontal-Endodontic Lesion, CPEL)(SRR 0.80, 95% CI 0.67–0.95, p=0.001 — CPEL 존재 시 생존율 저하; 재식이 의존하는 치주 부착 자체가 이미 손상된 병변이므로 기전적으로도 타당한 더 확실한 신호). 저자들은 대규모 코호트 연구의 부재를 명시적으로 지적하며 표준화된 프로토콜을 요구했다; 본 리뷰는 IR을 발치+임플란트와 비교하지 않는다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **First page in this wiki on intentional replantation (IR)** — an option positioned exactly at the extraction threshold, between failed/unresolvable nonsurgical RCT and tooth loss.
2. **Largest synthesis of IR prognosis to date for periapical periodontitis**: 39 studies, 2,305 teeth, with formal QUIPS risk-of-bias appraisal and GRADE certainty rating (something the paper notes prior IR systematic reviews lacked or did incompletely).
3. **Separates success from survival** and reports both as pooled proportions rather than conflating them — 0.78 vs 0.89, a gap of clinical consequence (see Results).
4. **Exposes that the pooled 89% survival figure hides a real time-decay**: the paper's own annual breakdown (0.98 at 3 months → 0.91 at 1 year → 0.63 at 8 years) shows survival falling substantially with longer follow-up; the single pooled estimate aggregates across studies of very different follow-up duration.
5. **Identifies two statistically significant prognostic factors** (age, CPEL) out of eight tested, with GRADE certainty explicitly downgraded to very low/low for both — a rare case of a review being candid that its own significant findings are fragile.
6. **The severity of the risk-of-bias problem is the headline finding, not a footnote**: 31/39 studies high risk, 8/39 moderate, 0/39 low risk of overall bias.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis of prognostic studies (PICOS: Population = periapical periodontitis incl. abscess/granuloma; Intervention = IR; Comparator = any/none; Outcomes = success/survival at various times, prognostic factors, complications; Study designs = RCTs, nonrandomized cohort studies (prospective/retrospective), case-control, case series ≥10 teeth).
- **Exclusions**: in vitro/animal studies, case reports <10 teeth, deciduous teeth, teeth with vertical root fracture/developmental groove, follow-up <6 months, abstract/protocol-only reports, ongoing studies.
- **Search**: to June 5, 2024. Abstract states "ten databases"; the Methods text explicitly names nine (Ovid MEDLINE, Ovid Embase, Ovid EBM Reviews, Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, Inspec, KCI-KJD, SciELO, SinoMed-CBM) plus two trial registers (ICTRP, ClinicalTrials.gov) — a minor count discrepancy in the source article itself, not an ingestion artifact. No language/date restriction; citation tracking performed.
- **Selection**: two independent reviewers (title/abstract screen → full-text assessment → extraction), third reviewer as arbiter for disagreements.
- **Risk of bias**: QUIPS tool (Quality In Prognosis Studies), 6 domains, judged low/moderate/high per domain and overall (least-favourable domain determines overall rating).
- **Outcome definitions** (explicit, radiographic + clinical): **Survival** = asymptomatic + radiographic lesion decreased in size (PDL space unclear/discontinuous allowed). **Success** = asymptomatic + normal function + no attachment loss + radiographic lesion resolved with clear/continuous PDL. **Ankylosis** = absent/discontinuous PDL space + radiodensity fused to bone + evidence of osseous healing. **Failure** = persistent symptoms/signs, unchanged/enlarging radiolucency, mobility beyond grade I or uncontrolled pocketing, or inflammatory root resorption → extraction.
- **Synthesis**: Stata 18 for overall-prognosis pooling (random-effects, proportions: success/survival/ankylosis). RevMan 5.4 for prognostic-factor meta-analyses (random-effects, survival rate ratio (SRR) with 95% CI) where comparable outcomes existed across studies. Subgroup analysis (age cutoffs, PPD, extraoral time, root-end filling material) via chi-square (α=0.05); sensitivity analyses where feasible.
- **Heterogeneity**: Cochrane's chi-square (α=0.1) + I² statistic.
- **Certainty**: GRADE, two independent raters, observational studies starting at high certainty and downgraded for risk of bias/indirectness/inconsistency/imprecision/publication bias.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Study flow**: 1,126 records → 743 after removing 383 duplicates → 82 full-text assessed → 49 reports (39 studies) included; 33 excluded (4 no full text, 18 unrelated clinical question, 3 inappropriate design, 8 unsuitable participants).
- **Included studies**: 39 studies / 2,215 patients / 2,305 teeth. Design mix: 8 case series, 5 one-armed prospective cohorts, 24 one-armed retrospective cohorts, 2 two-armed retrospective cohorts. Geography: China 13, Korea 9, Japan 4, USA 3, Turkey 3, one each Denmark/India/Iran/Iraq/Netherlands/Switzerland/UK.
- **Risk of bias**: 31/39 high overall risk, 8/39 moderate, **0/39 low**.
- **Overall prognosis (pooled, random-effects)**: success rate 0.78 (95% CI 0.70–0.84); survival rate 0.89 (95% CI 0.85–0.93); ankylosis incidence proportion 0.38 (95% CI 0.09–0.72, very wide).
- **Survival time-course** (annual summary, underlying the pooled 0.89 figure): 0.98 at 3 months → 0.91 at 1 year → 0.63 at 8 years — a real downward trend; some intermediate time points appear non-monotonic because not all studies reported annually.
- **Prognostic factor — Age**: SRR 0.73 (95% CI 0.53–0.99, p=0.048). Patients <25y had higher survival than older patients. GRADE certainty: **very low**. CI upper bound (0.99) nearly crosses 1 — a fragile, marginal finding despite nominal significance.
- **Prognostic factor — CPEL**: SRR 0.80 (95% CI 0.67–0.95, p=0.001), from only 2 studies. GRADE certainty: **low**. Both included studies were high risk of bias, so a sensitivity analysis was infeasible; authors state it remains "strongly probable" the effect is overestimated or confounded. Two individual studies within this comparison reported IR success in simple periapical periodontitis was 1.23× and 1.64× that in teeth with CPEL.
- **Non-significant prognostic factors**: sinus tract presence/absence (SRR 0.87, 95% CI 0.76–1.00, p=0.005 — note: the source article itself groups this among "no statistically significant difference" despite reporting =0.005, an apparent internal inconsistency in the original paper, reproduced here as published, not corrected), jaw (maxillary vs mandibular, SRR 0.97, 95% CI 0.90–1.04, p=0.35), probing pocket depth (SRR 0.95, 95% CI 0.79–1.15, p=0.63), extraoral time (SRR 0.93, 95% CI 0.78–1.10, p=0.40), splint method (resin wire vs suture, SRR 1.08, 95% CI 0.66–1.78, p=0.75), root-end filling material (other vs MTA, SRR 1.03, 95% CI 0.81–1.31, p=0.81).
- **Certainty of evidence**: all outcomes graded low or very low (GRADE) due to very serious imprecision and very serious risk of bias.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **No low-risk-of-bias study exists in this literature** — every pooled estimate in this review rests on studies the authors themselves rated moderate-to-high risk.
- **Absence of large-sample prospective/retrospective cohorts**, explicitly flagged by the authors as reducing reliability of the conclusions.
- **Considerable heterogeneity (I² > 80%)** in success rate, survival rate, and ankylosis incidence, attributed to inconsistent outcome-measurement criteria across studies, not standardized protocols.
- **Confounding poorly controlled**: only 7 of 39 original studies conducted even a brief discussion of confounding factors (Asgary 2014, Chen 2022, Demiralp 2003, Hou 2016, Jang 2016, Lee 2014, Zhang 2020, per the paper's own Limitations section) — the rest have moderate-or-high confounding bias by the authors' own assessment.
- **CPEL finding limited to 2 studies**, both high risk of bias — sensitivity analysis was not feasible.
- **Root-end filling material analysis** was highly heterogeneous (control arm consistently MTA, but experimental arms used varying materials), limiting interpretability.
- **Does not compare IR to extraction + implant** — this review's scope is IR's own prognosis, not a head-to-head with the alternative that most directly competes with it in a treatment-planning decision. Do not read the ~89% survival figure as implying IR outperforms or underperforms implant therapy; that comparison is simply not made here.
- Authors call for standardized IR protocols, multicentre trials, and better confounder control in future prognostic studies.

## 6. Related Work

- Distinguished by the authors from prior IR systematic reviews: one earlier review included zero studies due to overly strict two-armed-cohort inclusion criteria; another analyzed success rate only, without prognostic factors; a third (Wang et al.) identified prognostic factors but lacked appropriate statistical analysis. This review positions itself as resolving those gaps by combining formal QUIPS risk-of-bias appraisal, GRADE certainty rating, and quantitative SRR meta-analysis of prognostic factors.
- Complements [[endodontics/gowda-2025-guided-tissue-regeneration-surgical-endodontic-outcome-umbrella]] and [[endodontics/he-2026-hemostatic-agents-periapical-surgery-sr-ma]] — both address outcomes of apical/periapical surgery, the other major surgical alternative to IR when nonsurgical retreatment fails or is contraindicated.
- Contextualized against [[endodontics/irrigation/artaza-2024-outcome-nonsurgical-root-canal-treatment]], which reports 89% (loose)/67% (rigid) favorable outcomes for nonsurgical (re)treatment of large apical periodontitis lesions — a reminder that nonsurgical retreatment is the first-line option this review's IR population has typically already exhausted or found unsuitable.
- Distinct from **avulsion replantation** (traumatic tooth avulsion + replantation, e.g. [[dental-trauma/kumari-2025-effect-splinting-outcomes-replantation-avulsed]]): that is an emergency response to trauma in an otherwise healthy tooth; IR is a planned, elective surgical procedure on a tooth already diseased with periapical periodontitis. The two share a verb ("replantation") but not an indication, timeline, or starting periodontal/pulpal status — do not conflate them.

## 7. Glossary

- **IR (Intentional Replantation)**: deliberate extraction of a tooth, extraoral treatment of the root surface/root canal, and reinsertion into its original socket — a final-line strategy to preserve a tooth affected by periapical periodontitis when nonsurgical RCT, retreatment, or apical surgery have failed or are contraindicated.
- **CPEL (Combined Periodontal-Endodontic Lesion)**: a lesion with both periodontal and endodontic (pulpal/periapical) components — i.e., the tooth's periodontal attachment is already compromised in addition to the periapical pathology.
- **QUIPS**: Quality In Prognosis Studies tool — 6-domain risk-of-bias framework for prognostic factor studies (used here, distinct from ROBIS/AMSTAR-2 used for review-level bias in other wiki pages).
- **SRR (Survival Rate Ratio)**: this review's effect measure for prognostic factors — ratio of survival between two groups, with survival (not failure/death) defined as the event of interest; SRR < 1 indicates lower relative survival in the index/exposed group (e.g., older age, CPEL presence).
- **Ankylosis**: fusion of the root directly to alveolar bone with loss of the periodontal ligament space — a recognized complication/outcome category distinct from simple failure.
