## Why Ingested

PRF/PRP Asian-journal PMC sweep의 일환으로, ARP에서 PRF의 역할을 다룬 기존 [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/ucer-2023-extraction-socket-augmentation-platelet-rich-fibrin]](PRF를 골이식재의 생물학적 증강제로 위치시킨 서술적 종설)와 [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/park-2022-prf-gbr-damaged-socket-yonsei]](PRF+DBBM sticky bone이 GBR과 비열등하다는 동물실험)에 이어, 본 RCT(Razi 2024)는 PRF를 FDBA와 head-to-head로 직접 비교한 인체 임상 데이터를 추가한다 — PRF는 연조직(각화조직 폭) 우위, FDBA는 경조직(치조제 높이·폭·골밀도) 우위라는 split-decision 결과로, 두 재료를 "PRF vs graft" 이분법이 아니라 상호보완적 선택지로 재구성할 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=60, 30 PRF vs 30 FDBA), single-tooth extraction sockets requiring alveolar ridge preservation, 6-month follow-up with clinical and CBCT measurements.

PRF group gained more keratinized tissue width at 6 months (4.0±0.7mm vs 3.5±0.6mm, P=0.036); FDBA group better preserved ridge height (−1.2±0.3mm vs −1.6±0.4mm, P=0.025) and ridge width (−1.8±0.4mm vs −2.3±0.5mm, P=0.018), and showed a 30% bone-density increase vs baseline (860 vs 700 HU).

Split-decision outcome — PRF favors soft-tissue quality/keratinized mucosa, FDBA favors hard-tissue dimensional stability and bone density; short-report-style article (~3 pages) with sparse methodological detail (no blinding/randomization method, no soft-tissue-thickness raw data despite being named in the abstract).

## 세줄요약

RCT (n=60, PRF 30명 vs FDBA 30명), 단일치아 발치와 치조제보존 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP), 6개월 추적 임상·CBCT 측정.

혈소판풍부피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF)군은 6개월 각화조직 (Keratinized Tissue) 폭 증가 우위(4.0±0.7mm vs 3.5±0.6mm, P=0.036); 동결건조골동종이식재 (Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft, FDBA)군은 치조제 높이(-1.2±0.3mm vs -1.6±0.4mm, P=0.025)·폭(-1.8±0.4mm vs -2.3±0.5mm, P=0.018) 보존 우위 및 골밀도 30% 증가(860 vs 700 HU).

연조직은 PRF, 경조직은 FDBA가 우세한 split-decision 결과로 두 재료가 상호배타적이 아닌 상호보완적 선택지임을 시사; 짧은 short-report 형식(~3페이지)이라 방법론 기술(무작위배정 방법·눈가림 여부)이 소략하고 초록에 언급된 연조직두께 raw data가 본문에 누락된 한계.

- **PMID**: 39927017 / **PMCID**: PMC11805315

- **Study type**: Randomized controlled trial (parallel-group, 2-arm), n=60

## 2. Key Contributions

- Direct head-to-head RCT comparing PRF and FDBA (rather than PRF-as-adjunct-to-graft, the more common design) for ARP after single-tooth extraction.
- Reports both a soft-tissue endpoint (keratinized tissue width) and hard-tissue endpoints (CBCT ridge height/width, bone density) in the same cohort, allowing a direct trade-off comparison between the two materials.
- Concludes with an explicit split-decision recommendation: PRF for soft-tissue quality, FDBA for hard-tissue volume/density preservation — rather than declaring one material universally superior.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized controlled trial, single-tooth extraction sockets (maxillary or mandibular), 60 patients (35 M / 25 F, age 30–65).
- **Inclusion**: single-tooth extraction needing ARP, good general health, sufficient bone volume for future implant.
- **Exclusion**: smoking, systemic disease impairing bone metabolism (diabetes, osteoporosis), drugs altering bone repair.
- **Group A (PRF, n=30)**: PRF membranes prepared per the Choukroun protocol, placed in the socket.
- **Group B (FDBA, n=30)**: FDBA particles (0.25–1 mm) filled the socket to bone level, covered with a resorbable collagen membrane.
- Both groups: atraumatic extraction under local anesthesia, mucoperiosteal flap adjusted for primary closure, sutured with 4-0 resorbable sutures.
- **Measurements**: keratinized tissue width (clinical, baseline/1/3/6 months); alveolar ridge height/width and bone density (CBCT, at mid-buccal/mid-lingual/interproximal sites, baseline and 6 months).
- **Statistics**: SPSS v23; no further detail on the statistical test used, randomization method, allocation concealment, or blinding is given in the available text — this is a short-report-style article and the methods section is brief.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Short-report format (~3 printed pages in a supplement issue): methods section lacks randomization method, allocation concealment, blinding, and calibration/reliability detail for CBCT/clinical measurements.
- Soft-tissue-thickness data (highlighted in the abstract as the headline PRF result) is not shown as a table in the retrieved full text — only keratinized tissue width and ridge dimensions are tabulated.
- No implant-stage outcome (survival, ISQ, or histology) reported; the 6-month endpoint stops before implant placement.
- Single-center, unspecified country/setting detail beyond author affiliations; no CONSORT flow diagram or trial registration number given in the retrieved text.
- No power calculation or sample-size justification described.

## 7. Glossary

- **ARP** — Alveolar Ridge Preservation
- **PRF** — Platelet-Rich Fibrin, a second-generation autologous platelet concentrate rich in growth factors (PDGF, TGF-β, VEGF, IGF)
- **FDBA** — Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft, decalcified allogenic bone used as an osteoconductive scaffold
- **CBCT** — Cone-Beam Computed Tomography
- **HU** — Hounsfield Unit (radiographic bone density measure)
- **Keratinized tissue width** — width of the band of keratinized (attached) mucosa surrounding a tooth/implant site, associated with peri-implant soft-tissue health
