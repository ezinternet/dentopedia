## Why Ingested

Comprehensive SR+MA establishing survival equivalence of Category 2/3 NDI vs SDI, and statistically significantly worse survival of Category 1 mini-implants — foundational evidence for narrow implant selection decisions. Related to [[implants/survival/park-2023-narrow-regular-diameter-mandibular-overdentures]], which later confirmed NDI equivalence in the specific overdenture context.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (76 studies qualitative, 16 quantitative) comparing narrow-diameter implants (NDI, ≤3.5 mm) classified into three categories vs standard-diameter implants (SDI) for survival rate, 1995–2017.
Category 1 (<3.0 mm) showed significantly lower survival than SDI (OR 4.54; CI 1.51–13.65); Category 2 (3.0–3.25 mm) and Category 3 (3.3–3.5 mm) showed no significant difference (OR 1.06 and 1.19, respectively).
Category 2/3 NDI are viable alternatives to bone augmentation for limited bone width; Category 1 mini-implants should be restricted to overdenture rehabilitation in severely atrophic ridges.

## Key Contributions

- First comprehensive SR+MA applying the Klein 3-category NDI classification system (<3.0, 3.0–3.25, 3.3–3.5 mm) with statistical meta-analysis
- Quantified significantly lower survival for Category 1 mini-implants vs SDI (OR 4.54) — first meta-analytic confirmation
- Demonstrated Category 2 and 3 NDI are statistically equivalent to SDI for survival — supports avoidance of bone augmentation
- Identified a high risk of bias across included literature (11/16 quantitative studies = high bias), limiting definitive conclusions
- Found 12,186 NDI total: 5,441 Cat-1, 1,133 Cat-2, 5,612 Cat-3 across 76 studies

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA-compliant)
- **Databases**: PubMed/MEDLINE, Cochrane Library; Jan 1995 – Jan 2017
- **Inclusion (qualitative)**: ≥10 NDI-treated patients, ≥12-month follow-up, clinical studies all evidence levels
- **Inclusion (quantitative)**: ≥10 patients per group (NDI + SDI comparison)
- **Exclusion**: animal studies, orthodontic mini-implants, <1-year follow-up, <10 patients
- **Quality assessment**: 6-criteria tool (Cochrane/CONSORT/MOOSE/STROBE-based): randomization, blinding, focused question, identical treatment, in/exclusion criteria, n≥20
- **Statistics**: RevMan 5.3, odds ratios, forest plots, funnel plots; significance p<0.05
- **Studies**: 76 qualitative, 16 quantitative; Category 1: 22 studies/1280 pts/5441 NDI; Cat-2: 19/823/1133; Cat-3: 35/3842/5612

## Key Results

| Category | Diameter | n Studies (qualitative) | Mean Survival | SDI Comparison (OR; 95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cat 1 (mini) | <3.0 mm | 22 | 94.7 ± 5% (80–100%) | **4.54 (1.51–13.65)** — significantly worse |
| Cat 2 | 3.0–3.25 mm | 19 | 97.3 ± 5% (80.5–100%) | 1.06 (0.31–3.61) — NS |
| Cat 3 | 3.3–3.5 mm | 35 | 97.7 ± 2.3% (91–100%) | 1.19 (0.83–1.70) — NS |

- **Risk of bias**: Low 3/16, Moderate 2/16, High 11/16 studies — caveat for all conclusions
- **MBL Cat 1**: 0.6–1.43 mm; Cat 2: 0.09–1.6 mm; Cat 3: 0.1–2.17 mm
- **Primary indications**: Cat 1 = edentulous arch/overdenture; Cat 2 = anterior single-tooth; Cat 3 = all regions including posterior
- **HRQoL**: Cat 1 only — several studies showed improved QoL with overdentures; secondary outcome not systematically addressable
- **Follow-up**: Cat 1 mean 34±20 mo; Cat 2 29±17 mo; Cat 3 39±24 mo

## Limitations

- High risk of bias in 69% of quantitative studies (11/16)
- Forest plots for several categories driven mostly by a single study
- Wide confidence intervals due to paucity of events and heterogeneity
- Heterogeneous success definitions across included studies
- Long-term data (>5 years) limited for Cat 2/3
- No HRQoL data for Cat 2/3
- Missing data on biological/technical complication rates with wide-platform crowns on NDI

## Glossary

- **NDI (Narrow-Diameter Implant)**: Dental implant with diameter ≤3.5 mm
- **SDI (Standard-Diameter Implant)**: Dental implant with diameter >3.5 mm
- **Category 1 / mini-implant**: NDI <3.0 mm; typically one-piece, primarily for overdenture retention
- **Category 2**: NDI 3.0–3.25 mm; mainly anterior single-tooth restorations
- **Category 3**: NDI 3.3–3.5 mm; broadest indications including posterior load-bearing sites
- **OR (Odds Ratio)**: Ratio of odds of failure in NDI vs SDI; OR>1 means higher failure odds for NDI
