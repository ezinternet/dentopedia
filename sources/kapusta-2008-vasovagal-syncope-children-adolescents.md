## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (MEDLINE search; Gen Dent 2008) aimed at dentists, covering the pathophysiology, incidence, treatment options, and dental implications of vasovagal syncope in children and adolescents.

The abstract contains no numerical data at all: it states only that vasovagal syncope is not uncommon in this age group but is not widely known among the dental community, and that information on its incidence in the paediatric dental setting is scarce.

The clinical message is procedural rather than quantitative — eliminate factors that predispose a patient to a vasovagal reaction, and ensure the dental team knows the emergency protocol for a vasovagal episode.

## 7. Glossary

- **VVS (Vasovagal Syncope)**: reflex-mediated transient loss of consciousness caused by neurally induced bradycardia and hypotension; the most common syncope type in children and adolescents.
- **Predisposing factor**: a patient- or procedure-related condition (anxiety, pain, fasting, prolonged upright posture, heat) that lowers the threshold for a vasovagal reaction; the abstract's actionable target.
- **MEDLINE**: the U.S. National Library of Medicine bibliographic database searched by the author.
