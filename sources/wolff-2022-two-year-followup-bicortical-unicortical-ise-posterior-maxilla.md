## Why Ingested

2-year clinical follow-up of the Hsu 2016 RCT (PMID 27004293), which originally compared three posterior maxilla techniques (bicortical fixation vs indirect sinus elevation vs unicortical) at placement only. This paper closes the short-term gap by reporting Periotest stability, intrasinus bone formation, and complications at 2 years — confirming whether the equivalence seen at stage-2 surgery (3–6 months) is durable, and adding the only comparative intrasinus bone gain data for bicortical vs ISE beyond 6 months. Directly extends [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/hsu-2016-bicortical-fixation-indirect-sinus-unicortical]] and provides the 2-year anchor for the bicortical evidence pyramid.

## Three-line Summary

Pilot 2-year follow-up RCT (32 patients, 37 implants, RBH 7–11 mm), Minnesota — bicortical fixation (BF) vs indirect sinus elevation (ISE) vs unicortical (UC); stability (PTV), intrasinus bone formation (CBCT), and complications compared.
PTV at 2 years: BF −1.69 vs UC −1.76 vs ISE −2.22 — no significant difference (p=0.76); intrasinus bone gain: ISE showed significantly more horizontal gain (1.99 vs 1.47 mm, p=0.03) and non-significantly more vertical gain (3.15 vs 2.35 mm, p=0.13) than BF.
Within limits, BF is a feasible, lower-morbidity alternative to ISE in 7–11 mm RBH; 2-year stability equivalent, though ISE generates more intrasinus bone.

## 세줄요약

2년 추적 파일럿 RCT (32명, 37개, RBH 7–11 mm) — 쌍겹질 고정 (BF) vs 간접 상악동거상 (ISE) vs 단겹질 (UC); 페리오테스트 안정성, CBCT 상악동 내 골형성, 합병증 비교.
2년 PTV: BF −1.69 vs UC −1.76 vs ISE −2.22 — 유의차 없음(p=0.76); 상악동 내 골형성: ISE > BF (수평 1.99 vs 1.47 mm, p=0.03; 수직 3.15 vs 2.35 mm, p=0.13).
2년 안정성 동등; 단 ISE가 상악동 내 골 생성량 우위 — BF는 부가 수술 없이 동등한 안정성을 제공.

## Document Information

- **Journal**: International Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Implants (JOMI)
- **DOI**: [10.11607/jomi.8302](https://doi.org/10.11607/jomi.8302)
- **Institution**: University of Minnesota, USA
- **Study type**: 2-year follow-up of a randomized prospective clinical trial (pilot)
- **n**: 32 patients, 37 implants (5 lost to follow-up from the original 38 patients/45 implants)

## Key Contributions

- Longest follow-up RCT-level data directly comparing bicortical, ISE, and unicortical techniques
- Confirms 2-year stability equivalence: no significant PTV difference across three techniques (p=0.76)
- Quantifies intrasinus bone gain: ISE > BF at 2 years (horizontal 1.99 vs 1.47 mm, p=0.03)
- No peri-implantitis or implant loss reported in follow-up period
- Supports BF as preferred technique: avoids graft, achieves equivalent long-term stability

## Methodology

- **Design**: 2-year follow-up of a prospective RCT pilot
- **Inclusion**: 7–11 mm RBH (CBCT confirmed); no more than 2 implants per patient
- **Groups**: BF (stopper-drill to bicortically engage alveolar crest + sinus floor), ISE (greenstick + graft), UC (stop 1 mm short of sinus floor)
- **Stability**: Periotest (PTV), measured at 2-year follow-up
- **Intrasinus bone**: CBCT at 2 years vs preoperative CBCT; horizontal and vertical bone gain measured
- **Complications**: Clinical exam + chart review

## Key Results

| Technique | 2-yr PTV (mean±SE) | Intrasinus horizontal bone | Intrasinus vertical bone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bicortical fixation | −1.69 ± 0.80 | 1.47 ± 0.16 mm | 2.35 ± 0.38 mm |
| Indirect sinus elevation | −2.22 ± 0.60 | **1.99 ± 0.17 mm** (p=0.03) | **3.15 ± 0.43 mm** (p=0.13) |
| Unicortical fixation | −1.76 ± 0.80 | not measured | not measured |

- PTV group comparison: p=0.76 (no significant difference)
- ISE horizontal bone gain > BF: p=0.03
- ISE vertical bone gain > BF: p=0.13 (NS, trend)
- No implant failure or peri-implantitis at 2 years

## Limitations

- Pilot study; underpowered for detecting smaller inter-group differences
- 5 patients lost to follow-up (12% dropout)
- Short-term (2-year) — no 5-year or 10-year data
- Intrasinus bone comparison only BF vs ISE (UC excluded from bone gain analysis)
- Abstract-only extraction; full complication details unavailable

## Glossary

- **PTV (Periotest Value)**: Stability metric; range −8 (rigid) to +50 (mobile); lower = more stable
- **Intrasinus horizontal bone gain**: Mediolateral bone formation within sinus cavity on CBCT
- **Intrasinus vertical bone gain**: Craniocaudal bone height gain within sinus on CBCT
