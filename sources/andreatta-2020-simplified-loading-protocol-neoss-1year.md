## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort study (48 Neoss ProActive Tapered implants, single-tooth replacements, 1-year follow-up) evaluating a simplified loading protocol based on insertion torque (IT) criteria.

IT >40 Ncm was achieved in 46/48 implants (96%); ISQ was stable throughout with no stability dip; MBL at 1 year was −0.47 ± 0.38 mm; survival rate was 100% with no complications.

IT >40 Ncm is a reliable criterion for 6–8 week simplified loading with tapered implants, but the absence of a control group and 1-year follow-up limit generalizability.

## 세줄요약

전향적 코호트 연구(48개 Neoss ProActive Tapered 임플란트, 단일치 수복, 1년 추적): 삽입 토크(Insertion Torque, IT) 기준 기반 단순화 부하 프로토콜 평가.

IT >40 Ncm은 48개 중 46개(96%)에서 달성; 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ)는 안정 유지(안정성 저하 관찰 안 됨); 1년 변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) −0.47 ± 0.38 mm; 생존율 100%.

IT >40 Ncm 기준으로 6–8주 단순화 부하가 테이퍼드 임플란트에 안전하게 적용 가능하나, 대조군 없는 단일 시스템 연구이며 추적 기간이 1년에 불과해 장기 근거로는 한계가 있다.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: International Journal of Dentistry 2020, Article ID 8832500
- DOI: 10.1155/2020/8832500
- Design: Prospective cohort, 48 implants, 1-year follow-up

## 2. Key Contributions
- IT>40 Ncm = safe loading criterion for 6–8 week simplified loading
- ISQ trajectory stable (no classic stability dip in tapered implant design)
- MBL at 1 year: −0.47 ± 0.38 mm (within acceptable range)

## 3. Methodology
- 48 Neoss ProActive Tapered implants, single-tooth replacements
- Loading criterion: IT ≥40 Ncm → provisional at 6–8 weeks
- ISQ measured at placement (T0), 6 weeks (T1), 3 months (T2), 6 months (T3), 1 year (T4)
- MBL measured on periapical radiographs

## 4. Key Results
- IT>40 Ncm achieved in 46/48 implants (96%)
- ISQ stable throughout observation period (no dip)
- MBL: −0.47 ± 0.38 mm at 1 year
- Survival rate: 100% at 1 year
- No complications, no peri-implantitis

## 5. Limitations
- 1-year follow-up only
- No control group (non-simplified loading)
- Single implant system and design
- No randomization
