## Why Ingested

사이트별 절대 조기 실패율(EIF, %)을 pooling한 SR+MA가 위키에 없었음. 기존 [[implants/survival/yari-2023-risk-factors-early-implant-failure]] 및 [[implants/survival/krisam-2019-factors-affecting-the-early-failure]]에서 상악·4형 골 위험이 OR로만 제시됐으나, 부위별 절대 실패율 비교표가 없어 보완 목적으로 인제스트.

## One-line Summary

SR+MA (21 studies, 78,230 implants): maxillary EIF 3.14% vs mandibular 1.96% (RR 1.41); anterior maxilla is the highest-risk site (6.2%) and anterior mandible the safest (2.5%).

## 한줄요약

SR+MA (21편, 임플란트 78,230개): 상악 EIF 3.14% vs 하악 1.96% (RR 1.41); 전방 상악이 최고 위험 (6.2%), 전방 하악이 최저 위험 (2.5%).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First pooled estimate of **absolute EIF rates by anatomical quadrant** (n=78,230 implants across 21 studies).
- Demonstrates that **anterior maxilla (6.2%)** and **posterior maxilla (4.8%)** drive the overall maxilla–mandible gap; posterior maxilla vs posterior mandible (1.9% vs 1.46%) is NOT significantly different.
- Anterior mandible is confirmed as the **lowest-risk site** (2.5% — lowest of all four quadrants).
- Quantifies the **3% absolute risk difference** between anterior maxilla and anterior mandible.
- Posterior mandible EIF (4.2%) is higher than posterior maxilla (1.9%) in within-mandible comparison — posterior mandible is not "safe".

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis; PRISMA guidelines
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, EMBASE, Web of Science, Cochrane, Egyptian Knowledge Bank (1998 to search date)
- **Included studies**: 21 studies (retrospective, prospective, RCTs); all implant types; all smoking statuses
- **Excluded**: cadaver/animal studies, systemic bone disease (DM, osteoporosis, bisphosphonates, corticosteroids), post-loading failures, grafted sites, sinus lift sites, immediate post-extraction sites
- **Outcome**: EIF = implant failure before prosthetic phase (pre-loading)
- **Total implants**: 78,230 (39,468 maxilla; 38,762 mandible)
- **Statistical model**: Random effects (maxilla vs mandible overall); fixed effects (subgroup comparisons where I²=0%)
- **Effect measures**: Risk Ratio (RR) + Risk Difference (RD) with 95% CI
- **Heterogeneity**: I²=58% (overall maxilla vs mandible); I²=0% (all four subgroup comparisons)
- **Publication bias**: Visual funnel plot assessment only (no Egger or Begg test)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Overall
| Jaw | Implants | Failures | EIF Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maxilla | 39,468 | 1,239 | **3.14%** |
| Mandible | 38,762 | 759 | **1.96%** |
| **Total** | **78,230** | **1,998** | **2.55%** |

- **RR maxilla vs mandible**: 1.41 (95% CI 1.19–1.67), p<0.0001, I²=58%
- **RD**: 1% absolute higher risk in maxilla

### Subgroup — Four-quadrant analysis

| Comparison | Sites | n implants | EIF rates | RR (95% CI) | p | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ant. maxilla vs Ant. mandible | 14,516 | 8,389 / 6,127 | **6.2% vs 2.5%** | 2.44 (2.04–2.91) | <0.00001 | 0% |
| Post. maxilla vs Post. mandible | 39,014 | 17,472 / 21,542 | **1.9% vs 1.46%** | 1.07 (0.92–1.25) | 0.68 | 0% → **NS** |
| Ant. maxilla vs Post. maxilla | 27,375 | 12,154 / 15,221 | **4.0% vs 2.19%** | 1.06 (0.92–1.21) | 0.44 → **NS** | 0% |
| Ant. mandible vs Post. mandible | 13,017 | 6,723 / 6,294 | **2.5% vs 4.2%** | 0.52 (0.43–0.62) | <0.00001 | 0% |
| Ant. maxilla vs Post. mandible | 29,800 | 10,598 / 19,202 | **2.8% vs 1.4%** | 0.95 (0.81–1.13) | 0.59 → **NS** | 0% |

**Key surprises:**
- Posterior maxilla vs posterior mandible: NOT significantly different
- Anterior maxilla vs posterior maxilla: NOT significantly different  
- Posterior mandible (4.2%) > posterior maxilla (1.9%) within mandible

### EIF Rate Ranking (descending risk)
1. Anterior maxilla: **6.2%** (highest)
2. Posterior maxilla: **4.8%** (intra-jaw comparison data)
3. Posterior mandible: **4.2%** (intra-jaw comparison)
4. Anterior mandible: **2.5%** (lowest)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Single author** — no independent reviewer for risk of bias assessment
- **I²=58%** (maxilla vs mandible comparison) — substantial heterogeneity; random effects applied but sources not explored
- **No multivariate adjustment**: confounders (smoking, bone quality, implant type/length) not controlled
- Grafted and sinus lift sites excluded → limits generalizability to augmented cases
- Publication bias: only visual funnel plot, no statistical test
- Studies were primarily retrospective with inherent selection bias
- Did not stratify by implant surface, length, diameter, or era

## 6. Related Work

- Esposito et al. found posterior maxillary success rates highest among all sites (conflicting with this review's pooled data)
- Alsaadi et al.: implant site not a significant factor in prospective study
- Baqain et al.: no early failure difference across bone types
- Kim & Lee: maxilla has thinner cortical plate and lower density than mandible

## 7. Glossary

- **EIF**: Early Implant Failure — implant loss before prosthetic loading (pre-prosthetic phase)
- **RR**: Risk Ratio (relative risk) — ratio of EIF rate in exposed vs unexposed group
- **RD**: Risk Difference — absolute difference in EIF rate between groups
- **I²**: Inconsistency index measuring heterogeneity across studies
