## One-line Summary
Cochrane Protocol (2026, Takahashi et al.): to assess benefits and harms of NAM therapy vs no presurgical orthopaedic treatment in children with orofacial clefts — no results yet.

## 한줄요약
코크란 프로토콜 (2026): 구순구개열 소아의 비치조 성형술 (Nasoalveolar Moulding, NAM) 치료 효과·위해 평가 예정 — 결과 없음.

## 1. Document Information

## 2. Key Contributions
- First Cochrane protocol for NAM therapy in orofacial clefts
- Establishes comparison framework: NAM vs no presurgical orthopaedic treatment
- Protocol covers both cleft lip and cleft palate populations
- Will provide high-level evidence synthesis for a technique with significant resource demands

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Population: children with orofacial clefts (cleft lip, cleft palate, or both)
- Intervention: NAM therapy (presurgical orthopaedic, intraoral appliance + nasal stent)
- Comparator: no PSO treatment
- Study type: RCTs anticipated
- Status: awaiting RCT identification and data extraction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Protocol — no results yet.
Outcomes to be assessed: surgical outcomes, nasal/lip symmetry, patient/parent burden.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Protocol stage — results entirely dependent on RCT availability
- RCTs in this population have historically been difficult to conduct (neonatal population, ethical challenges)

## 6. Related Work
- Clinical context: NAM used as presurgical preparation at specialized cleft centres; controversy about benefit vs burden

## 7. Glossary
- 비치조 성형술 (Nasoalveolar Moulding, NAM): 구순구개열 신생아에서 1차 수술 전 치조·비순 형태 개선을 목적으로 사용하는 맞춤형 구강내 장치
- 수술 전 정형치료 (Presurgical Orthopaedic, PSO): 1차 수술 전 조직 위치 최적화를 위한 치료 접근
- 구순구개열 (Orofacial Cleft): 구순열(Cleft Lip), 구개열(Cleft Palate) 또는 복합 선천성 발달 이상
