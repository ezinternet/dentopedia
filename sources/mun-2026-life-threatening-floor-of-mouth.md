## Why Ingested

Floor-of-mouth (FOM) hematoma from lingual cortical perforation during anterior mandibular implant placement — a rare but life-threatening airway emergency. This case illustrates positional deviation in mucosa-supported guided surgery in a severely resorbed edentulous mandible.

→ [[oral-surgery/mun-2026-life-threatening-floor-of-mouth]]

## Three-line Summary

A 59-year-old male with a severely resorbed edentulous mandible developed sudden intraoral bleeding and sublingual swelling causing tongue elevation and impending airway obstruction after computer-guided implant placement. Emergency neck CT and postdischarge CBCT showed lingual cortical perforation at the #31 implant site with suspected vascular injury; only the #31 implant was displaced lingually relative to the digital plan, suggesting deviation during freehand insertion after fully guided osteotomy. Emergency nasotracheal intubation with conservative management led to progressive hematoma resolution.

## 세줄요약

심한 치조골 흡수가 있는 무치악 하악에 컴퓨터 유도 임플란트 식립을 받은 59세 남성에서 구강저 혈종으로 인한 혀 상승과 임박한 기도 폐쇄가 발생했다. 응급 경부 CT와 CBCT에서 #31 임플란트 위치의 설측 피질 천공과 혈관 손상이 확인되었고, 나머지 다섯 임플란트는 계획대로였으나 #31만 설측으로 이탈해 유도 절골 후 비유도(freehand) 식립 중 편위가 의심되었다. 응급 비강 기관삽관과 보존적 치료로 혈종은 점진적으로 소실되었다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Reports a life-threatening floor-of-mouth hematoma after guided implant placement in a severely resorbed edentulous mandible.
2. Identifies mucosa-supported static guides without auxiliary fixation plus freehand implant insertion after guide removal as potential contributors to positional deviation in completely edentulous cases.
3. Emphasizes thorough CBCT assessment and preparedness for airway intervention as essential safeguards.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Single case report. A 59-year-old male underwent computer-guided implant placement using a mucosa-supported static surgical guide. Six implants were planned. Emergency neck CT and postdischarge CBCT were used to identify lingual cortical perforation; direct comparison with the original digital surgical plan (from the referring clinic) assessed positional deviation.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Lingual cortical perforation at the #31 implant site with suspected vascular injury in the floor of the mouth.
- Only the #31 implant was displaced lingually from its planned position; the remaining five implants were consistent with the plan.
- Clinical course: sudden intraoral bleeding and sublingual swelling → tongue elevation → impending airway obstruction.
- Management: emergency nasotracheal intubation + conservative management → progressive hematoma resolution.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single case report (n=1); no incidence data or comparative outcomes.
- Abstract-only available; full procedural details (guide fixation protocol, implant dimensions, drilling sequence, follow-up duration) not retrieved.
- Causal attribution (freehand insertion after guide removal) is inferred from plan comparison, not experimentally isolated.

## 7. Glossary

- **Floor of the mouth (FOM, 구강저)**: region beneath the tongue containing sublingual/submandibular glands and vessels; bleeding here can elevate the tongue and obstruct the airway.
- **Mucosa-supported static surgical guide**: tooth/mucosa-borne template used to guide osteotomy and/or implant placement without real-time navigation.
- **Lingual cortical perforation**: breach of the lingual bony plate of the mandible, risking injury to vessels in the FOM.
- **Nasotracheal intubation**: airway securement via the nasal route, used here as emergency management of impending obstruction.
- **Severely resorbed edentulous mandible**: mandible with marked alveolar bone loss after tooth loss, altering anatomy and increasing surgical risk.
