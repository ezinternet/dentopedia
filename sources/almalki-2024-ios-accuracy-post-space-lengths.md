## Why Ingested
프라임스캔(Primescan)으로 포스트 준비 공간(post space) 디지털 인상의 길이 의존성을 체계적으로 측정한 in vitro 연구. 위키의 [[digital-workflow/wildhagen-2026-accuracy-ios-post-preparations]] (µ-CT 기반 타당성 연구)와 직접 비교 가능한 선행 연구로, 같은 적응증에 대해 더 긴 준비(10 mm)와 세분화된 thirds별 분석을 추가한다.

## One-line Summary
In vitro study (n=45 typodont incisors, Primescan): RMS 58/81/101 µm for 6/8/10 mm post lengths; 6 and 8 mm clinically acceptable vs PVS; 10 mm shows significant apical accuracy decline.

## 한줄요약
타이포돈트 45개(프라임스캔): 포스트 준비 6/8/10 mm → RMS 58/81/101 µm; 6·8 mm 임상 허용 범위, 10 mm 첨단부 정확도 유의 감소.

- Journal: Journal of Esthetic and Restorative Dentistry, 36(4):673–679

## 2. Key Contributions
- Establishes length-dependent accuracy pattern for Primescan post space scanning: 6 mm > 8 mm > 10 mm
- 6 and 8 mm post lengths produce clinically acceptable accuracy (comparable to conventional PVS impressions)
- 10 mm post length shows significant apical third accuracy decline — caution warranted
- Coronal and middle thirds maintain consistent accuracy regardless of post length (NS, p>0.5)
- Apical third is the accuracy-limiting zone: 10 mm apical RMS 163 µm vs 6 mm apical 96 µm (p<0.05)
- Provides clinical guidance: direct IOS impressions viable alternative to conventional for ≤8 mm post channels

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: In vitro comparative study
- Specimens: 45 typodont maxillary central incisors; standard endodontic access and obturation simulated; post spaces at 6, 8, and 10 mm (n=15 per group)
- IOS: CEREC Primescan (Dentsply Sirona); two trained examiners
- Reference: conventional PVS impressions (light/heavy body polyvinyl siloxane) scanned with inEos X5a lab scanner
- Analysis: Geomagic Control X software; RMS deviations at coronal, middle, and apical thirds
- Statistics: Bonferroni post-hoc, one-way ANOVA; α=0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Overall RMS by post length:**
- 6 mm: **58 µm**
- 8 mm: **81 µm**
- 10 mm: **101 µm**

**Coronal and middle thirds:** NS differences among all subgroups (p>0.5)

**Apical third:**
- 6 mm: 96 µm
- 8 mm: 131 µm
- 10 mm: **163 µm** — significantly lower accuracy vs 6 mm (p<0.05) and 8 mm (p<0.05)

**Clinical interpretation:** 6 and 8 mm: comparable to PVS → acceptable alternative; 10 mm: accuracy decreases, conventional preferred at apex

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Typodont (not extracted human teeth) — enamel/dentin optical properties may differ
- Primescan only — no comparison to other IOS devices
- No µ-CT reference — conventional PVS impression as reference may underestimate true deviation
- Post diameter not investigated — depth only
- No clinical outcomes data (prosthetic fit, patient outcomes)

## 6. Related Work
- Wildhagen 2026: µ-CT-validated Primescan post space study; concludes 10 mm > 12 mm — converging finding that depth limits accuracy; Almalki extends by adding 6 mm and 8 mm benchmarks
- Grazziotin 2026: Primescan for endodontic access openings — broader endodontic IOS evidence

## 7. Glossary
- **Post space**: endodontic term for the root canal space prepared to receive a prefabricated or custom post for crown retention
- **Post and core**: restoration comprising intraradicular post + coronal core buildup for crown support
- **Typodont**: dental education model with realistic synthetic teeth — standardized but not biologically identical to extracted teeth
