## Why Ingested

즉시식립의 생존율 논의에서 놓치기 쉬운 임상 시나리오 — 임플란트 식립 *후* 발생하는 급성 감염. 기존 [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]]는 식립 *전* 소켓 상태에 집중하지만, 이 코호트는 식립 후 급성 감염 발생 시 실패 예측인자(흡연·fresh socket·골이식·항생제 타이밍)를 HR로 제시해 임상 의사결정에 직접 활용 가능.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional cohort (n=980 implant patients, Iran): 34 (3.46%) developed acute postoperative infection; 10 survived (29.4%), 24 failed (70.6%).

Significant failure predictors: smoking (p<0.05), fresh socket placement, bone graft use; without postoperative antibiotics, HR for failure increased 1.1× when infection occurred at day 4; with antibiotics, failure risk increased at day 6 (smokers) or day 9 (non-smokers).

Acute postoperative infection carries a high failure burden (70.6%); early diagnosis and postoperative antibiotic coverage are critical, especially in fresh-socket implants.

## 세줄요약

코호트 (n=980 임플란트 환자, 이란): 34례(3.46%) 급성 수술후 감염 발생; 생존 10례(29.4%), 실패 24례(70.6%).

유의미한 실패 예측인자: 흡연·fresh socket 식립·골이식 동반; 수술후 항생제 없이 4일째 감염 발생 시 HR 1.1배 상승; 항생제 사용 시 흡연자 6일, 비흡연자 9일 이후 감염 발생에서 실패위험 상승.

급성 수술후 감염은 실패율 70.6%로 높음; 조기 진단과 수술후 항생제가 critical — 특히 fresh socket 임플란트.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First cross-sectional cohort specifically focused on acute POSTOPERATIVE infection after implant placement (not pre-existing socket infection)
- Quantified high failure rate (70.6%) when acute postoperative infection develops
- Identified time-sensitive antibiotic failure thresholds (day 4 without, day 6-9 with antibiotics)
- Characterized risk factors: smoking, fresh socket, bone augmentation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Cross-sectional cohort
- **Population**: 980 implant patients at single center (Tehran, Iran)
- **Outcome**: failed (group 2, n=24) vs survived (group 1, n=10) after acute postoperative infection
- **Variables**: placement site, age/gender, bone augmentation, postoperative antibiotic therapy, smoking, time of infection diagnosis
- **Statistical**: Kaplan-Meier survival analysis; HR calculation

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Variable | Survived (n=10) | Failed (n=24) | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Infection rate | 3.46% (34/980) | — | — |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small number of infected cases (n=34)
- Single center, Iran
- Abstract-only access (full text not retrieved from PMC)
- Retrospective design — selection bias possible
- No randomization; confounders may exist

## 7. Glossary

- **Acute postoperative infection**: purulent infection occurring after implant surgery, distinct from pre-existing socket pathology
- **Fresh socket**: implant placed immediately into post-extraction site (Type 1)
- **HR (Hazard Ratio)**: 1.1× increase = 10% increased failure risk per unit of time with infection
- **Kaplan-Meier**: survival analysis method accounting for time-to-event data
