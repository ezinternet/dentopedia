## Why Ingested

The systematic review on cirrhotic bleeding gives a range (0–8.9%) but no denominators and no predictor analysis. This retrospective is the primary series inside that range: 190 visits, 333 teeth, 6.31% unusual intra-operative bleeding, and — crucially — an attempt to say *which* laboratory value predicted it. It is the page that makes the review's conclusion usable at the chair.

→ [[oral-surgery/medina-2018-bleeding-dental-extractions-liver-cirrhosis]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective observational study of 190 visits (333 teeth extracted, 1–9 per visit) in cirrhotic patients on a liver transplant waiting list, with platelet counts 16,000–216,000/mm³ and INR below 3.

Unusual intra-operative bleeding (>10 min after routine haemostatic measures) occurred in 12 cases (6.31%) and postoperative haemorrhagic complications in 12 more, all controlled with local measures; intra-operative bleeding was associated with low platelet count (P = 0.026) but that count explained only 16% of cases (adjusted R² = 0.16).

The authors conclude that a cirrhotic patient with platelets above 16,000/mm³ and INR below 3 needs no pre-operative transfusion — local measures are sufficient, and platelet *function* rather than number is the likely missing variable.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: International Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery 2018;47(12):1543–1549
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.ijom.2018.04.007
- **Design**: analytic retrospective observational study
- **Population**: cirrhotic patients on a liver transplant waiting list
- **Setting**: hospital dental service (Brazil; author group also produced the related systematic review)

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Supplies the denominators** the systematic review lacks: 190 visits, 333 teeth, 12 intra-operative and 12 post-operative bleeding events.
- **Separates two bleeding phenotypes** — unusual intra-operative bleeding (>10 min after routine haemostasis) versus postoperative haemorrhagic complications — rather than treating "bleeding" as one outcome.
- **Tests predictors and finds a weak one honestly.** Low platelet count predicted intra-operative bleeding (P = 0.026) but explained only 16% of variance, which the authors use to argue that platelet *function* matters more than number.
- **Gives a usable threshold with a caveat**: platelets >16,000/mm³ and INR <3 required no prior transfusion.

## 3. Methodology

- **Design**: retrospective analysis of records for patients on the liver transplant waiting list.
- **Outcomes**: (1) unusual intra-operative bleeding, defined as bleeding persisting >10 minutes after routine haemostatic procedures; (2) postoperative haemorrhagic complications.
- **Covariates**: clinical and laboratory variables, including platelet count and INR.
- **Volume**: 190 visits, 333 teeth extracted (1–9 teeth per visit).
- **Laboratory range**: platelets 16,000–216,000/mm³; INR < 3.
- **Analysis**: association testing between outcomes and clinical/laboratory variables, with regression modelling (adjusted R² reported).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | n | % of visits |
|---|---|---|
| Unusual intra-operative bleeding | 12 | 6.31% |
| Postoperative haemorrhagic complications | 12 | — |
| All events controlled by local measures | 24 | 100% of events |

- Intra-operative bleeding was associated with **low platelet count** (P = 0.026).
- Platelet count explained only **16%** of bleeding cases (adjusted R² = 0.16; model P = 0.44) — the authors interpret this as evidence that **platelet function changes** contribute.
- Laboratory envelope: platelets 16,000–216,000/mm³, INR < 3.
- Stated practice implication: no pre-operative blood transfusion needed above these thresholds; local measures suffice.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Single centre, retrospective, modest event count.** Twelve events per outcome limits how many predictors can be tested simultaneously; the regression is correspondingly fragile.
- **Cirrhosis aetiology is not the analysis unit.** Alcohol-related cirrhosis is present in the population but not isolated, so the study cannot attribute bleeding risk to drinking per se.
- **Platelet function was not measured** — it is inferred from the unexplained 84% of variance, not demonstrated.
- **Transplant-listing population** is a selected, closely monitored subgroup; community cirrhotic patients may differ in severity and follow-up.
- **Local measures are not standardised** in the report, so "controlled locally" covers a range of interventions.

## 7. Glossary

- **INR (international normalized ratio)**: standardised prothrombin-time ratio; higher means slower clotting.
- **Unusual intra-operative bleeding**: here defined as bleeding continuing more than 10 minutes after routine haemostatic procedures.
- **Adjusted R²**: proportion of outcome variance explained by the model after adjustment; 0.16 means 16%.
- **Thrombocytopenia**: low platelet count.
- **Liver transplant waiting list**: a selected population with advanced, medically supervised cirrhosis.
