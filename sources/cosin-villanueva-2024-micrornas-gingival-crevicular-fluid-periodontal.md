## Why Ingested

GCF는 이미 periodontal biomarker matrix로 위키에 있으나([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/donertas-2026-gbt-subgingival-debridement-gcf-biomarkers]]는 GBT 후 단백질/사이토카인 GCF biomarker를 다룸), 그 GCF 안의 epigenetic 층(miRNA)을 진단 biomarker로 정리한 페이지가 없었다. 이 PRISMA SR은 GCF miRNA 16편을 종합해 어떤 miRNA가 진단적으로 쓸 만한지(miR-146a/200b/223/23a acceptable, miR-203 not)를 명시해 GCF biomarker 근거를 분자 층위로 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

PRISMA systematic review (Int J Mol Sci 2024; 16 case-control studies, 3222 records screened) evaluating gingival crevicular fluid (GCF) miRNAs as diagnostic biomarkers for periodontal diseases; meta-analysis was not feasible due to high methodological heterogeneity.

miR-146a, miR-200b (especially miR-200b-3p), miR-223, and miR-23a demonstrated acceptable diagnostic plausibility (AUC > 0.8) for periodontitis; miR-203 did not meet this threshold and may serve as a protective therapeutic target rather than a diagnostic marker.

GCF miRNA biomarkers show molecular-layer diagnostic promise but lack standardized protocols; no single miRNA is ready for standalone clinical screening, and only 4 of 16 studies validated findings in an independent cohort.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA 체계적 고찰(Int J Mol Sci 2024; 16편 환자대조군, 3222건 스크리닝): 치은열구액(GCF) miRNA를 치주질환 진단 바이오마커로 평가; 방법론 이질성으로 메타분석 불가.

miR-146a·miR-200b(특히 miR-200b-3p)·miR-223·miR-23a는 치주염 진단 타당성(AUC > 0.8) 충족; miR-203은 해당 기준 미달 — 보호·치료 표적 역할 시사.

GCF miRNA는 분자 층위 진단 잠재력 있으나 표준 프로토콜 부재; 단독 임상 스크리닝 수준엔 미달, 16편 중 4편만 독립 코호트 검증 수행.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Focused, PRISMA-registered synthesis restricted to **GCF** miRNAs (excluding serum, saliva, gingival-tissue, in-vitro samples) as diagnostic biomarkers of periodontal disease.
- Identifies the recurrently studied GCF miRNAs and ranks their **diagnostic plausibility** by the AUC > 0.8 threshold: **miR-146a, miR-200b (miR-200b-3p), miR-223, miR-23a acceptable; miR-203 not** acceptable (but of interest as a protective/therapeutic target).
- Maps the direction of dysregulation (mostly upregulation in disease), correlation with severity, response to non-surgical therapy, and links to systemic comorbidities (T2DM, CVD, RA).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Question (PECO)**: Population = humans; Exposure = miRNA expression in GCF; Comparison = healthy individuals; Outcome = diagnosis of periodontal diseases.
- **Databases** (searched 26 Jan 2024, updated 30 May 2024): PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar. No language or time limits. Manual search of reference lists / prior SRs.
- **Inclusion**: cross-sectional, case-control, cohort, or experimental human studies using GCF samples, addressing periodontitis and/or gingivitis, analyzing miRNAs. **Exclusion**: no control group; in-vitro studies.
- **Selection**: 3222 records → 2091 duplicates removed → 1131 screened → 25 full-text → 9 excluded → **16 included**. Manual search added none.
- **Quality**: Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (NOS) for case-control studies (max 9 points), dual reviewers (M.C.-V., P.J.A.-P.; A.L.-R. as tiebreaker). 13/16 good quality, 3/16 fair.
- **Data extracted**: author/year, country, study type, sample size, age, periodontal disease type & classification, miRNAs studied, outcomes (expression level, AUC, sensitivity, specificity).
- Meta-analysis was **not possible** due to methodological heterogeneity (different miRNAs, missing numeric data, non-convertible expression units); PRISMA items 13, 14, 20, 22, 23b unfulfilled.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Study set**: all 16 included studies were **observational case-control**. Sample sizes 18–216 patients; mean ages 27.8–65.3 years. Two-thirds used the 2018 Classification of Periodontal and Peri-implant Diseases and Conditions; one-third used the Armitage classification.
- **Direction**: in most studies miRNA expression was **upregulated** in periodontal disease, though some reported contradictory results.
- **Most studied**: miR-200b-3p and miR-146a.
- **miR-200b (miR-200b-3p)**: involved in immune-cell differentiation and early inflammation; generally elevated in GCF; higher with comorbid periodontitis + T2DM or + CVD (e.g., Liu et al. 2022 periodontitis 3.06 ± 0.21, T2DM 2.74 ± 0.22, comorbid 3.51 ± 0.30, significant). Saito et al. 2017 reported lower levels (small n).
- **miR-146a**: modulates TLR signaling/cytokines; upregulated (Almiñana-Pastor 2023, Radović 2018; normalized after non-surgical therapy), but underexpressed in periodontitis in Rovas et al. 2022 — direction not conclusively settled in GCF.
- **miR-23a**: inhibits osteogenesis, destructive; increased in mild-stage periodontitis (Costantini 2023) and in GCF + PDLSC cultures (Zhang 2019, decreased after therapy) — possibly an early bone-metabolism initiator.
- **miR-203**: targets VEGFA (inhibits angiogenesis); **underexpressed** in periodontitis across all 3 studies including it; negative correlation with TNF-α — proposed protective role / therapeutic target rather than diagnostic marker.
- **miR-223**: regulator of innate immunity and osteoclast differentiation; upregulated in GCF (higher with concomitant DM); positive correlation with severity in one study, no correlation in Costantini 2023.
- **Diagnostic threshold**: AUC > 0.8 taken as acceptable. miRNAs meeting it include miR-146a, miR-23a, miR-28-5p, miR-155, miR-125b-1-3p, miR-200c-3p, miR-200b, miR-30b-3p, miR-200a-5p, miR-1226, miR-200a-3p, miR-200b-5p, miR-223, miR-223-5p, miR-200c-5p, miR-200b-3p.
- **Bottom line (Conclusions)**: miR-146a, miR-200b (miR-200b-3p), miR-223, miR-23a, miR-203 show significant differences between periodontitis and health; **all except miR-203 have acceptable diagnostic performance**.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **No quantitative synthesis/meta-analysis** — heterogeneous miRNAs across studies, missing numeric data (unretrievable from authors), non-convertible expression units.
- No grey-literature search → possible selection bias.
- Small samples in several studies (n = 18–23) risk false positives/negatives; only one study (Almiñana-Pastor 2023) used high-throughput sequencing, others targeted specific miRNAs.
- Only 3 studies included stage I (early) periodontitis; no study compared miRNA across periodontitis **grades** (progression A/B/C); only 2 measured miRNA after basic periodontal treatment.
- **Future**: homogeneous criteria (standardized miRNAs, classification, units) enabling meta-analysis; extreme-group and early-stage designs; grade-based and post-treatment longitudinal analyses.

## 6. Related Work

- Extends GCF-biomarker evidence held in the wiki ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/donertas-2026-gbt-subgingival-debridement-gcf-biomarkers]]) from proteins/cytokines to the epigenetic (miRNA) layer.
- Connects periodontitis miRNA biology to systemic low-grade-inflammation comorbidities (T2DM, CVD, RA).

## 7. Glossary

- **GCF (Gingival Crevicular Fluid)**: serum-derived exudate flowing from the gingival sulcus; volume/composition change with local inflammation — minimally invasive, site-specific biofluid.
- **miRNA (microRNA)**: short (19–24 nt) non-coding RNA that represses gene expression post-transcriptionally by binding the 3′ UTR of target mRNAs; an epigenetic regulator of inflammation.
- **AUC (Area Under the ROC Curve)**: diagnostic-accuracy summary; here AUC > 0.8 taken as the threshold for "acceptable" diagnostic plausibility.
- **NOS (Newcastle–Ottawa Scale)**: quality-appraisal tool for case-control/cohort studies (selection, comparability, exposure/outcome; max 9 points).
- **PECO**: Population–Exposure–Comparison–Outcome framing of a review question.
- **PDLSC (Periodontal Ligament Stem Cell)**: progenitor cells whose osteogenesis can be modulated by miRNAs (e.g., inhibited by miR-23a).
- **VEGFA**: vascular endothelial growth factor A; target of miR-203, linking miR-203 to angiogenesis in inflamed periodontal tissue.
