## Why Ingested

무치악 환자의 악간관계(maxillomandibular relationship) 기록을 완전 디지털 방식으로 결정하려는 시도를 보강하기 위해 인제스트. [[wiki/complete-denture/jasz-2024-centric-relation-methods-digital-mandibular-motion]]가 디지털 하악운동 분석기로 중심위(centric relation, CR) 결정 방법을 다뤘다면, 본 연구는 자체 개발 턱 추적 시스템(jaw movement tracking system)으로 수직(vertical)·수평(horizontal) 관계를 동시에 산출하는 방법을 제시하고, 3D 편차를 정량 검증한다. 동일 연구팀(Peking University)의 in-vitro 선행연구 [[wiki/complete-denture/li-2021-optical-jaw-tracking-edentulous-jaw-relations-in-vitro]]의 임상 후속작이다.

According to PubMed, the source article is "Digital Determination and Recording of Edentulous Maxillomandibular Relationship Using a Jaw Movement Tracking System" (Journal of Prosthodontics, 2022;31(8):663-672; PMID 35510309), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/jopr.13529). abstract-only — full text not retrieved (Journal of Prosthodontics, not open access).

## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical accuracy study (n=10 edentulous patients) at Peking University School and Hospital of Stomatology testing an in-house jaw movement tracking system that records habitual opening-closing trajectory and mandibular rest position (MRP), then derives a digital maxillomandibular relationship from the highest-density trajectory intersections on a plane 2 mm above MRP;

compared against conventionally fabricated complete dentures (intercuspal position) as control, the anterior reference point showed no significant displacement difference, but whole-arch 3D deviation was significant (95% CI 0.76-1.35 mm, p<0.001);

authors conclude the method is established in principle but accuracy needs further improvement before clinical use.

## 세줄요약

베이징대학 구강병원 무치악 환자 10명 대상 전향적 정확도 연구 — 자체 개발 턱 운동 추적 시스템(jaw movement tracking system)으로 습관적 개폐구 궤적과 하악안정위(Mandibular Rest Position, MRP)를 기록하고, MRP 2mm 상방 평면에서 궤적 교차점 밀도 최고점으로 디지털 악간관계(maxillomandibular relationship)를 산출.

기존 방식(통상 총의치 교합적합위)과 비교 시 전방 기준점 변위는 유의차 없었으나, 전체 악궁 3D 편차는 유의했음(95% 신뢰구간 0.76-1.35mm, p<0.001).

저자들은 방법론적 원리는 확립됐으나 정확도가 추가 개선 필요하다고 결론.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Direct digital method that determines **both axes** of the maxillomandibular relationship from a single tracked trajectory: vertical (a plane 2 mm above measured MRP, i.e. an operationalized freeway space/interocclusal rest space) and horizontal (highest-density intersection point of the habitual opening-closing trajectory through that plane).
- Registers multisource data (jaw tracking trajectory + 3D surface scans of impressions and facial structures) via a custom-made recording tool — a full digital-workflow proof of concept, not a single-device measurement.
- Provides one of the few clinical (not bench/in-vitro) accuracy quantifications for a jaw-tracking-derived edentulous relationship record, expressed as whole-arch 3D deviation rather than a single linear distance.
- Companion clinical study to the same group's in-vitro bench validation ([[wiki/complete-denture/li-2021-optical-jaw-tracking-edentulous-jaw-relations-in-vitro]]), enabling a bench-to-clinic accuracy comparison.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective clinical accuracy study, single institution.
- **Sample**: 10 edentulous patients.
- **Tracking**: novel in-house jaw movement tracking system recorded the trajectory of habitual opening-closing jaw movement and mandibular rest position (MRP).
- **Scanning**: 3D surface scanning of conventional maxillomandibular impressions and of facial structures with the patient in MRP.
- **Registration**: multisource data (trajectory + impression scans + facial scans) registered using a custom-made recording tool.
- **Vertical relationship construction**: a plane parallel to the ala-tragus and horizontal (interpupillary-type) reference lines was constructed 2 mm above MRP; its vertical position determined the vertical relationship.
- **Horizontal relationship construction**: intersections of the tracked trajectory passing through that plane were located and their density distributions analyzed; the coordinates of highest density — interpreted as the position of highest movement repeatability — were used to construct the digital maxillomandibular relationship (**test group**).
- **Control group**: the maxillomandibular relationship of the patient's new complete dentures, with artificial teeth in intercuspal position, fabricated by conventional workflow.
- **Outcome measures**: displacement of an anterior reference point (referenced to centric relation position) and 3D deviation of the entire mandibular arch, test vs control.
- **Statistics**: Wilcoxon signed-ranks test (anterior reference point displacement, test vs control); one-sample t-test (whole-arch 3D deviation).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Anterior reference point**: maximum displacement (referenced to centric relation) was in the horizontal anteroposterior direction for both test and control groups; **no significant difference** between groups.
- **Whole mandibular arch, 3D deviation**: test vs control difference **significant**, 95% CI 0.76 mm to 1.35 mm, p < 0.001.
- **Interpretive pattern**: a single anterior point agreeing between groups while the whole-arch 3D deviation is significant is consistent with the discrepancy being predominantly **rotational/angular** rather than a pure anteroposterior translation — an angular error can leave one reference point close to control while displacing the rest of the arch.
- **Author conclusion (verbatim sense)**: a digital method for determining and recording edentulous maxillomandibular relationships was established by analyzing individual trajectory features from the in-house tracking system; **however, the accuracy needs to be further improved** — i.e., not yet presented as clinically validated.
- **Bench-to-clinic contrast**: the group's own in-vitro predecessor ([[wiki/complete-denture/li-2021-optical-jaw-tracking-edentulous-jaw-relations-in-vitro]]) reported 3D deviations of 112-133 μm (0.11-0.13 mm) on bench models. The clinical whole-arch deviation here (0.76-1.35 mm) is roughly 6-10× larger — a substantial bench-to-patient accuracy degradation, plausibly from soft-tissue compliance, patient movement, and the added uncertainty of MRP/facial-scan registration that a bench setup does not have.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Small, single-centre sample**: n=10, one institution — limited generalizability.
- **Custom/in-house device**: not a commercially available or standardized system; results are not directly reproducible with off-the-shelf equipment.
- **Reference standard is not ground truth**: the "control" group is itself a conventionally fabricated complete denture's intercuspal position — i.e., the comparison is test-vs-conventional-workflow-output, not test-vs-a-verified true jaw relation. Agreement or disagreement with control does not by itself establish which is more accurate.
- **Habitual-closure premise is a substantive assumption**: using the highest-density trajectory intersection (highest repeatability of habitual closure) as the target position for maxillomandibular relationship assumes habitual closure approximates the desired reference (e.g., centric relation)-adjacent position. In long-term edentulous patients, proprioceptive loss can make habitual closure diverge from a reproducible bone-braced reference position; this is a design assumption, not a validated equivalence.
- **Clinical accuracy not yet adequate**: authors explicitly state accuracy needs further improvement — the whole-arch 3D deviation (0.76-1.35 mm) sits at or above magnitudes flagged elsewhere in this wiki as clinically perceptible (cf. [[wiki/complete-denture/singh-2026-nick-notch-intraoral-gothic-arch-jaw-relation]], where extraoral Gothic arch error of 0.74-0.86 mm is characterized as potentially clinically perceptible).
- Future work: improving tracking/registration accuracy, larger multi-centre samples, and validation against a true independent reference standard rather than conventional-denture output.

## 7. Glossary

- **Maxillomandibular relationship**: the combined vertical (occlusal vertical dimension) and horizontal (centric/eccentric) spatial relationship of the mandible to the maxilla, recorded for prosthesis fabrication.
- **Mandibular rest position (MRP)**: the postural position of the mandible when the musculature is at minimal, balanced tonic contraction; the traditional reference from which freeway space/interocclusal rest space is measured.
- **Jaw movement tracking system**: an instrumented system that records the 3D trajectory of mandibular movement relative to the maxilla over time, here custom-built in-house.
- **Habitual opening-closing trajectory**: the path traced by repeated jaw opening-closing movements performed naturally (not experimentally guided into a specific reference position).
- **3D deviation (whole-arch)**: the spatial discrepancy between two maxillomandibular relationship records measured across the entire mandibular arch, rather than at a single point — sensitive to rotational/angular error that a single-point measure can miss.
