## Why Ingested

Surveillance hit on lateral-window sinus augmentation new bone formation. This 2026 SR/MA adds meta-analytic pooling to the qualitative CGF review [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/al-aroomi-2024-cgf-maxillary-sinus-augmentation-sr]] (which could not pool), and is the first wiki-held paper to frame CGF against hydroxyapatite with an eggshell-derived nano-hydroxyapatite (EHA) translational angle — complementing the BCP-material ranking in [[sinus-lift/lateral/somngam-2025-new-bone-formation-biphasic-calcium]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251148756; PubMed/Scopus/WoS/Cochrane/ScienceDirect/Google Scholar inception–October 2025) of 6 studies (3 RCTs, 1 three-arm randomized, 2 non-randomized; 185 sinuses) in adults undergoing direct lateral sinus augmentation with concentrated growth factor (CGF).
CGF significantly increased histologic new bone formation versus control (pooled SMD 0.94, 95% CI 0.30–1.57, p = 0.004, I² = 41%) and bone density (SMD 0.89, 95% CI 0.44–1.34, p = 0.0001, I² = 0%), while bone height gain (SMD −0.29, 95% CI −0.98 to 0.41, p = 0.41, I² = 75%) and implant survival (97–100%) were unchanged.
CGF improves bone quality without compromising vertical gain or survival, but no included study tested eggshell-derived nano-hydroxyapatite + CGF directly — the EHA implication is translational inference, not pooled evidence.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic quantitative synthesis of CGF specifically in lateral/direct sinus augmentation (earlier wiki-held CGF SR was qualitative and mixed transcrestal/lateral).
- Separates the bone *quality* endpoints (histologic new bone, radiographic density) from the bone *quantity* endpoint (height gain) — CGF wins on the former, ties on the latter.
- GRADE certainty: high for bone formation, density and implant survival; moderate for height gain.
- Adds a translational EHA discussion as a hypothesis-generation bridge (not a tested intervention).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar (inception–October 2025) + manual reference checks.
- Eligibility: RCTs and comparative clinical studies, adults ≥18 years, direct sinus augmentation.
- Risk of bias: RoB 2 (randomized) and ROBINS-I (non-randomized).
- Synthesis: random-effects meta-analysis in RevMan Web; continuous outcomes as SMD/MD with 95% CIs; GRADE certainty.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 6 studies / 185 sinuses: 3 RCTs, 1 three-arm randomized trial, 2 non-randomized designs.
- New bone formation: pooled SMD 0.94 (95% CI 0.30–1.57; p = 0.004; I² = 41%), favor CGF.
- Bone density: SMD 0.89 (95% CI 0.44–1.34; p = 0.0001; I² = 0%), favor CGF.
- Bone height gain: SMD −0.29 (95% CI −0.98 to 0.41; p = 0.41; I² = 75%), no significant difference (substantial heterogeneity).
- Implant survival 97–100%; no graft-related complications reported.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only artifact**: no full text retrieved (PMC body empty) — review-level details (study-level extraction, funnel plot, pre-specified subgroup analysis) could not be verified beyond the abstract.
- Only 6 studies with mixed designs (3 RCTs + 1 three-arm randomized + 2 non-randomized); height-gain analysis has I² = 75%.
- No head-to-head EHA + CGF trial; EHA claims are inferred from translational (intraoral) evidence, not from the pooled sinus data.
- Authors call for head-to-head trials of EHA + CGF versus deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM), with or without CGF.

## 7. Glossary

- **CGF (Concentrated Growth Factor)** — 자가혈 유래 성장인자 농축물; osteoinductive biologic adjunct.
- **EHA (Eggshell-derived Nano-Hydroxyapatite)** — 달걀껍질 유래 나노 수산화인회석; osteoconductive scaffold candidate, not tested here.
- **SMD (Standardized Mean Difference)** — 표준화 평균차; effect-size metric for continuous outcomes on different scales.
