## Why Ingested

[[wiki/overviews/trigeminal-neuralgia-neuropathy-overview]]는 외상후 삼차신경병증성 통증 (Post-Traumatic Trigeminal Neuropathic Pain, PTNP)을 임상적으로 다루지만(진단, 원인별 발생률: 제3대구치 발치 48%, 임플란트 13%, 국소마취 12%, 근관치료 8%) 기전은 명시적으로 범위 밖으로 남겨둔다. 본 논문은 그 빈틈을 채우는 말초 병태생리 리뷰로, 신경 손상 후 케모카인·이온채널·위성신경교세포(SGC)·옥시토신·마이크로RNA 기전을 정리한다.

관련: [[wiki/orofacial-pain/wilke-2020-chloride-underrated-ion-nociceptors]] (말초 nociceptor 이온채널 가소성이라는 같은 틀을 다른 채널군(Cl⁻ vs Na⁺/K⁺/Ca²⁺)으로 다룸), [[wiki/oral-surgery/lee-2015-inferior-alveolar-nerve-injury-management]] (제3대구치 발치 중 하치조신경 손상 증례 — 본 논문의 염증매개 말초감작 기전이 조기 스테로이드 개입의 생물학적 근거를 제공).

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of the peripheral pathophysiology of post-traumatic trigeminal neuropathic pain (PTNP), based primarily on animal (mostly rodent, largely infraorbital-nerve-injury) models, deliberately excluding central mechanisms.

PTNP arises in only ~3% of trigeminal nerve injuries (3.3% after facial fractures, <8% after implant therapy, 3–13% after endodontic treatment) via a peripheral cascade: inflammatory-mediator release → receptor/channel phosphorylation → chemokine signaling (CCL2, CXCL13, CXCL10) → peripheral sensitization → ectopic spontaneous activity in Aβ/Aδ/C fibers → ion channel alterations (Nav1.8/1.9, K+, T-type Ca2+) → blood-nerve-barrier disruption → satellite glial cell (SGC) activation/ganglionic sensitization → phenotypic Aβ-fiber switching (allodynia); oxytocin and specific microRNAs (miR-146b-5p, miR-195, miR-21, miR-223-3p) modulate this cascade.

Central mechanisms are explicitly excluded as "outside the scope" due to complexity, and most findings are extrapolated from spinal (not trigeminal-specific) nerve injury models — a major evidence-quality limitation the authors themselves flag.

## 세줄요약

외상후 삼차신경병증성 통증(PTNP)의 말초 병태생리를 다룬 내러티브 리뷰 — 주로 설치류 안와하신경손상(ION-CCI) 동물모델 기반, 중추 기전은 의도적으로 제외.

삼차신경 손상 중 약 3%만 PTNP로 진행(안면골절 후 3.3%, 임플란트 <8%, 근관치료 3–13%); 염증매개물질 방출→수용체/채널 인산화→케모카인 신호(CCL2, CXCL13, CXCL10)→말초감작→Aβ/Aδ/C 섬유의 이소성 자발활동→이온채널 변화(Nav1.8/1.9, K+, T-type Ca2+)→혈액-신경장벽 붕괴→위성신경교세포(SGC) 활성화/신경절감작→Aβ섬유 표현형전환(이질통)의 캐스케이드; 옥시토신·특정 마이크로RNA(miR-146b-5p, miR-195, miR-21, miR-223-3p)가 이를 조절.

중추 기전은 저자들이 명시적으로 "범위 밖"이라 밝히며 제외했고, 대부분의 근거가 삼차신경 특이적이 아닌 척수신경 손상 모델에서 외삽된 것 — 저자 스스로 인정하는 핵심 근거 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Synthesizes the peripheral cascade of events (receptors/inflammatory soup → chemokines → peripheral sensitization → spontaneous activity → ion channel alterations → neurovascular/BNB disruption → microbial priming → ganglionic sensitization → phenotypic Aβ switching) specific to trigeminal nerve injury.
- First narrative synthesis to organize oxytocin's analgesic role (via V1A-R and K+ channel recovery, TRPV1/TRPV4 inhibition) and microRNA regulation (miR-146b-5p, miR-384, miR-155-5p, miR-132-3p, miR-186, miR-32-5p, miR-195, miR-21, miR-223-3p) specifically in the PTNP context.
- States clinical epidemiology by iatrogenic cause: facial fracture 3.3%, implant therapy <8%, non-surgical endodontic treatment 3–13%, surgical endodontic therapy ~5%.
- Explicitly names trigeminal-vs-spinal system differences (no DRG-like sympathetic sprouting reported in TG; distinct ganglionic gene expression) as a reason trigeminal-specific research is needed rather than extrapolating spinal findings.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative (non-systematic) literature review. No PRISMA protocol, no search strategy reported, no quantitative synthesis. Evidence synthesized predominantly from rodent (rat/mouse) infraorbital nerve chronic constriction injury (ION-CCI) and related peripheral/trigeminal ganglion (TG) models, supplemented by extrapolation from spinal (dorsal root ganglion, DRG) nerve-injury literature where trigeminal-specific data are lacking.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Prevalence**: ~3% of trigeminal nerve injuries overall develop PTNP; 3.3% after facial fractures; <8% after implant therapy; 3–13% after non-surgical endodontic treatment; ~5% after surgical endodontic therapy.
- **Clinical course**: onset typically 5th decade of life, female predominance; prognosis poor — only 10–20% report significant improvement, 30% some improvement long-term, 50% worsen or unchanged.
- **Peripheral sensitization**: driven by TNF-alpha, IL-6, IL-1beta, TRPV1, NGF, P2X/P2Y, neurokinin, CGRP, glutamatergic signaling; phosphorylation of Nav1.8/Nav1.9 sodium channels increases firing; reduction of K+ (IA, IK) currents in TG neurons increases spike frequency/duration.
- **Chemokines**: CCL2 (via CCR2, recruits macrophages, boosts Nav1.8 activity), CXCL13/CXCR5 (ERK-mediated proinflammatory cytokines, p38 MAPK), CXCL2 (mechanical allodynia), CXCL10/CXCR3 (ERK/AKT signaling) all implicated in trigeminal neuropathic pain maintenance.
- **Ganglionic sensitization**: SGC proliferation peaks ~4 days post-injury around ATF-3+ neurons; SGCs upregulate GFAP, express CD45/CD163 (macrophage/leukocyte markers); Connexin 43 and glutamate synthase silencing alters nociceptive responses.
- **Oxytocin**: intra-TG administration attenuates orofacial mechanical hypersensitivity via V1A-R interaction, K+ channel recovery, and TRPV1/TRPV4 inhibition; suppresses CGRP, IL-1B, TNFa upregulation in TG and spinal trigeminal nucleus caudalis (SpVc).
- **MicroRNAs**: miR-146b-5p/-384/-155-5p/-132-3p elevated in TN patient serum; miR-186 suppresses NLRP3 inflammasome; miR-32-5p downregulation (via Cav3.2) and miR-195 upregulation (via Sonic Hedgehog/Patched1) promote neuropathic pain; miR-21 elevation drives blood-nerve-barrier leakage (claudin-1 loss, MMP9 rise); miR-223-3p alleviates pain via MKNK2/MAPK-ERK suppression.
- **Bacterial priming**: LPS/TLR4 signaling disrupts the blood-nerve barrier and primes earlier, more severe allodynia (IL-1B, iNOS elevation); conversely, P. gingivalis exposure showed an anti-nociceptive, IL-10-mediated effect in one rat pain model — a notable non-linear finding.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Central mechanisms explicitly excluded** — the authors state "due to their complexity, this review will focus on the peripheral mechanism… central cascade of events… is outside the scope of this review." This is a major structural limitation: PTNP pathophysiology is peripheral AND central, co-dependent, and only half is covered here.
- Nearly all cited evidence derives from **rodent spinal/DRG models, not trigeminal-specific models** — the authors repeatedly caution that trigeminal and spinal systems differ (e.g., sympathetic fiber sprouting reported in DRG but not TG), so extrapolation carries uncertainty.
- No systematic search methodology, PRISMA protocol, or quality appraisal — narrative-review confidence level.
- PTNP prevalence estimates are described as unreliable ("lacking… partly due to changing diagnostic terms and criteria").
- Authors explicitly call for trigeminal-specific (not spinal-extrapolated) research to develop trigeminal-specific therapeutics.

## 7. Glossary

- **PTNP** — Post-Traumatic Trigeminal Neuropathic Pain.
- **TG** — Trigeminal Ganglion.
- **DRG** — Dorsal Root Ganglion (spinal analog of TG).
- **SGC** — Satellite Glial Cell (glial cells surrounding TG/DRG neuronal soma).
- **ION-CCI** — Infraorbital Nerve Chronic Constriction Injury (rodent trigeminal-injury model).
- **BNB** — Blood-Nerve Barrier.
- **SA** — Spontaneous (ectopic) neural Activity.
- **Nav1.8/1.9** — voltage-gated sodium channel subtypes implicated in nociceptor hyperexcitability.
- **Cav3.2** — T-type voltage-gated calcium channel subtype.
- **CGRP** — Calcitonin Gene-Related Peptide.
- **NK1 receptor** — Neurokinin 1 receptor (substance P receptor).
- **SP** — Substance P.
- **OXT / V1A-R** — Oxytocin / Vasopressin-1A Receptor.
- **miRNA** — MicroRNA.
- **Shh** — Sonic Hedgehog signaling pathway.
