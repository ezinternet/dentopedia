## Why Ingested
Requested topic: retention loss ("탈락") of cervical resin restorations. This 18-month interim report of a cluster-randomized trial (NCT02631161) compares a glass hybrid with a nanocomposite in sclerotic NCCLs placed without cavity preparation and names retention loss as the main failure mode in both arms; it parallels the GH-vs-composite NCCL trials already held → [[resin-bonding/koc-vural-2024-glass-hybrid-composite-bruxist-nccl-60m]], [[resin-bonding/meral-2025-gic-resin-composite-systemic-disease-nccl-48m]].

## Three-line Summary

Interim 18-month analysis of a 36-month cluster-randomized trial (NCT02631161): 88 patients (50-70 y) with 175 sclerotic non-carious cervical lesions (sNCCLs) restored without mechanical preparation with glass hybrid (EQUIA Forte Fil/Coat) or resin composite (OptiBond FL + Filtek Supreme XTE).
Of 160 restorations reassessed (78 patients, mean 18 months), 15 (18%) failed in GH and 11 (12%) in RC with no significant survival difference (p = 0.904, Cox); retention loss was the most common failure in both groups, and GH took less chairtime (8.6 ± 4.3 vs 11.7 ± 5.7 min, p < 0.001).
GH may be a suitable alternative to RC in sNCCLs at this interim point; this is abstract-only data from an interim analysis, so longer follow-up is needed.

## 세줄요약

36개월 군집 무작위 임상시험 (Cluster-randomized Trial, NCT02631161)의 18개월 중간 분석 — 50-70세 환자 88명의 경화성 비우식성 치경부 병소 (sclerotic Non-carious Cervical Lesion, sNCCL) 175개를 기계적 와동 형성 없이 글래스 하이브리드 (Glass Hybrid, GH)와 레진 복합체 (Resin Composite, RC)로 수복.
재평가된 160개 수복물 중 실패는 GH 15개 (18%), RC 11개 (12%)로 생존율 차이 없음 (p = 0.904, Cox); 두 군 모두 유지 상실 (Retention Loss)이 가장 흔한 실패 원인이었고, 시술 시간은 GH가 짧았음 (8.6 ± 4.3분 vs 11.7 ± 5.7분, p < 0.001).
이 중간 시점에서 GH는 sNCCL에서 RC의 대안이 될 수 있으나, 초록 수준 정보의 중간 분석이므로 장기 추적 결과 확인이 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Interim (18-month) results for GH vs RC restoration of sclerotic NCCLs without mechanical cavity preparation.
- Multi-level Cox regression identifies patient age and tooth arch as survival-associated factors.
- Treatment time recorded as a trial outcome: GH placement faster than RC.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Cluster-randomized controlled trial (NCT02631161), 36-month trial; this paper = 18-month interim analysis.
- **Participants**: 88 patients aged 50-70 y, 175 sNCCLs.
- **Interventions**: GH = EQUIA Forte Fil / EQUIA Forte Coat (GC); RC = OptiBond FL adhesive (Kerr) + Filtek Supreme XTE nanofilled composite (3M). No mechanical cavity preparation.
- **Outcomes**: restoration survival/quality by FDI criteria; treatment time.
- **Analysis**: multi-level Cox regression (survival factors); generalized linear mixed model (treatment time factors).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: no detail on FDI sub-scores, cluster handling, dropout reasons, or failure-by-retention counts.
- Interim analysis at ~18 months (range 8-25), 78/88 patients reassessed.
- Mandibular-tooth risk estimate has a confidence interval touching 1.00.
- Final 36-month results were the planned endpoint.

## 7. Glossary
- **sNCCL**: sclerotic non-carious cervical lesion — cervical lesion with hypermineralized, tubule-occluded dentin.
- **GH (glass hybrid)**: EQUIA-type high-viscosity glass-ionomer restorative with a resin coating.
- **RC**: resin composite placed with a (here 3-step etch-and-rinse) adhesive.
- **FDI criteria**: World Dental Federation clinical evaluation criteria for direct restorations.
- **Cluster-randomized**: randomization unit grouped (e.g., by patient), analyzed with multi-level models.
