## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of in-vitro studies (55 included; quality score 31–92%) across PubMed, Embase, and Scopus, assessing cold atmospheric plasma (CAP) efficacy against dental pathogens with a newly designed standardized assessment tool.

Antimicrobial reduction factors varied strongly across studies but clustered by target pathogen, working gas, and treatment time; a time-dependent increase of effect was consistent across studies.

Heterogeneity precludes any specific protocol or device recommendation; multi-species biofilm models are needed before CAP can be evaluated in randomized clinical trials.

## 세줄요약

PubMed·Embase·Scopus의 in vitro 연구 55편(품질점수 31–92%)을 포함한 체계적 문헌고찰로, 신규 표준화 평가도구를 사용해 구강 병원균 대상 저온대기압플라즈마(CAP)의 항균효과를 분석.

항균 감소폭은 연구 간 편차가 컸으나 병원균 종류·작동가스·처리시간별로 군집을 이뤘고, 처리시간이 길수록 효과가 증가하는 시간의존성이 일관됐다.

이질성 때문에 특정 프로토콜·장비 권고는 불가하며, RCT로 나아가기 전 다균종 바이오필름 모델 연구가 선행돼야 한다.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: Antibiotics 2021, 10(2):211
- DOI: 10.3390/antibiotics10020211
- Study type: systematic review of in-vitro studies (PubMed, Embase, Scopus)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Synthesizes CAP antimicrobial evidence across 55 in-vitro studies; introduces a standardized assessment tool for in-vitro comparison.
- Identifies the determinants of effect: pathogen, working gas, treatment time (time-dependent).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: PubMed, Embase, Scopus.
- Extracted device parameters, experimental setups, microbial cultivation; quality scored with a newly designed tool.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 55 studies included (quality score 31–92%).
- Reduction factors varied strongly; clusters by set pathogen, working gas, treatment time.
- Time-dependent increase of antimicrobial effect across studies.
- CAP a promising antimicrobial option within a clinically feasible application time.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In-vitro only; heterogeneous → no device/protocol recommendation.
- Multi-species biofilm models needed before randomized clinical trials.
