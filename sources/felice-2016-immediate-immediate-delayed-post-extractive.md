Part of the implant-iip-loading surveillance batch, found by manual PDF discovery (not caught by the automated scout search). Interim (4-month) report of a 3-arm RCT comparing immediate/immediate-delayed/delayed placement, later followed to 1 year in [[wiki/immediate-implant/esposito-2017-immediate-immediate-delayed-post-extractive]] — the two are companion reports of the same 210-patient trial, not independent studies.

## Three-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (n=210, 70 per arm) randomising single post-extraction implant sites to immediate placement, immediate-delayed placement (6 weeks), or delayed placement (4 months), reporting outcomes at 4 months post-loading (crown delivery).

Implant failure: 4/70 (6.0%) immediate, 4/70 (6.2%) immediate-delayed, 1/70 (1.6%) delayed (P=.369, NS); marginal bone loss significantly greater with more delay (-0.17 vs -0.20 vs -0.24mm, P=.006); Pink Esthetic Score significantly better for immediate/immediate-delayed vs delayed (12.48 vs 12.38 vs 11.71, P<.001).

At 4 months post-loading, immediate and immediate-delayed (6-week) placement show a numerically (non-significantly) higher failure rate than delayed placement, but significantly better peri-implant bone preservation and pink esthetics — an early trade-off signal the 1-year companion report re-examines.

## Key Contributions
- Large (n=210) 3-arm RCT directly comparing three placement-timing protocols (immediate/6-week/4-month) rather than the usual 2-arm immediate-vs-delayed design
- Isolates an early esthetic-vs-failure-risk trade-off: immediate/immediate-delayed placement wins on bone level and PES but numerically loses on failure rate at this early (4-month) timepoint

## Methodology
Randomised parallel-group trial, 210 patients requiring single-tooth replacement, randomised 70/70/70 to immediate, immediate-delayed (6-week), or delayed (4-month) placement. Immediate/immediate-delayed sockets grafted with bone substitute + resorbable membrane when needed; delayed sockets grafted similarly if poorly preserved or in esthetic zones. Implants with ≥25 Ncm insertion torque healed unloaded 4 months; <25 Ncm healed 6 months. Temporary then definitive crowns. Outcomes (blinded assessors): implant/crown failure, complications, marginal bone level change, PES, patient satisfaction. This report: 4-month post-loading data.

## Key Results
| Outcome | Immediate (n=70) | Immediate-delayed 6wk (n=70) | Delayed 4mo (n=70) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failures | 4 (6.0%) | 4 (6.2%) | 1 (1.6%) | .369 |
| Complications (patients) | 5 | 6 | 3 | .601 |
| Marginal bone loss (4mo) | -0.17 ± 0.11mm | -0.20 ± 0.09mm | -0.24 ± 0.12mm | .006 |
| PES at crown delivery | 12.48 | 12.38 | 11.71 | <.001 |

## Glossary
- **Immediate-delayed placement**: implant placed 6 weeks after extraction (an intermediate timing between immediate and conventional delayed placement).
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score)**: composite index of peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics.
