## Why Ingested

1st Global Consensus for Clinical Guidelines (GCCG) 2025 — single-round survey of 116 experts from 43 countries on edentulous maxilla rehabilitation with sinus lift/bone augmentation. Contains dedicated maintenance section with consensus on annual full-mouth pocket charts (85.4%) and intraoral radiographs (78.5%), against routine CBCT (77.7% disagreement). No consensus on panoramic radiographs. Directly relevant to [[wiki/overviews/supportive-peri-implant-therapy-maintenance-overview]] and [[wiki/overviews/gbr-barrier-membrane-overview]] for GBR/augmentation maintenance protocols.

## Three-line Summary

Single-round online survey (217 experts invited, 116 completed, 53.5% response) for 1st Global Consensus for Clinical Guidelines (GCCG) 2025 on edentulous maxilla rehabilitation with sinus lift/alveolar bone augmentation. 94 statements across 16 domains; 44 (46.8%) reached consensus (>75% agreement), 4 (4.3%) strong consensus (>95%). Maintenance section: consensus on annual full-mouth pocket charts (85.4%, median 6) and intraoral radiographs (78.5%, median 6); disagreement on routine CBCT (77.7%, median 2); no consensus on panoramic radiographs. Strong consensus on importance of surgical/prosthetic/biological complications and marginal bone loss as fundamental outcomes for future studies.

## 세줄요약

2025년 제1차 글로벌 임상 가이드라인 합의(GCCG) 사전 설문조사: 43개국 217명 전문가 대상, 116명 완료(응답률 53.5%). 무치악 상악 재건(상악동거상/골증대 포함) 94개 진술 중 44개(46.8%) 합의, 4개(4.3%) 강력 합의 도출. 유지관리 섹션: 연 1회 전악 포켓 차트(85.4%)와 치근단 방사선(78.5%) 합의, 루틴 CBCT는 반대(77.7%), 파노라마 방사선은 합의 불성립. 향후 연구 필수 아웃컴으로 수술/보철/생물학적 합병증과 변연골소실 강력 합의.

## Key Contributions

- First global consensus initiative (GCCG) for edentulous maxilla rehabilitation with augmentation
- 116 experts from 43 countries (largest geographic spread for implant consensus)
- Structured questionnaire: 33 items, 94 Likert-scale statements across 16 domains
- Consensus thresholds: >75% agreement = consensus; >95% = strong consensus
- **Maintenance consensus**: Annual full-mouth pocket charts (85.4%), intraoral radiographs (78.5%) justified; routine CBCT not justified (77.7% disagreement); panoramic radiographs no consensus
- **Planning consensus**: CT/CBCT essential (94.7%); photographic documentation, impressions, wax-ups, mock-ups consensus
- **Biomaterials**: Xenogeneic favored for sinus lifts; membrane fixation consensus for vertical GBR; no consensus on biologics alone; biologics+bone substitutes 60.3% agreement
- **Timing**: Delayed implant placement favored (76.7%) for terminal dentition with insufficient bone; waiting time 2–10 months (no consensus on optimal)
- **Loading**: Immediate loading opposed for non-splinted (85.4%); no consensus for splinted
- **Membrane exposure**: Resorbable membrane exposure impact no consensus (divided opinions)
- **Implant longevity**: >10 years expected for native bone (87.1%), sinus lift (76.7–78.4%); GBR (65.5%) and bone blocks (59.5%) no consensus
- **Strong consensus outcomes**: Surgical/prosthetic/biological complications + marginal bone loss = fundamental ClinROs for future studies

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-round online survey (Microsoft Forms) for GCCG 2025 preparation
- **Participants**: 217 experts invited from 43 countries (AO, EAO, ITI, Osteology Foundation members); 116 completed (53.5% response)
- **Specialties**: Periodontics 62.1%, Oral surgery 25%, OMFS 17.2%, Prosthodontics 5.2%, GP 4.3%
- **Settings**: Private clinic 71.6%, University 56.0%
- **Questionnaire**: 33 items (consent, specialization, planning, guided surgery, timing/loading, biomaterials, provisionals, antibiotics, maintenance, future trends, Schneiderian perforation, soft tissue, longevity, satisfaction, treatment selection, fundamental outcomes)
- **Likert scale**: 7-point; consensus >75% agreement/disagreement; strong consensus >95%
- **Analysis**: Descriptive statistics, median/IQR, RAND guidelines; STATA v18, GraphPad Prism v10

## Key Results

### Maintenance (Item 22) — Consensus Results
| Procedure | Agreement | Median (IQR) | Consensus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-mouth pocket charts annually | 85.4% | 6 (5–7) | **Yes** |
| Intraoral radiographs annually | 78.5% | 6 (5–7) | **Yes** |
| CBCT routinely annually | 22.3% (disagreement 77.7%) | 2 (1–3) | **No (disagreement)** |
| Panoramic radiographs annually | — | — | **No consensus** |

- **Interpretation**: Conservative, patient-centered maintenance emphasizing low-risk, high-yield diagnostics; future research needed to refine intervals/imaging by individual risk profiles

### Planning (Items 4–7) — Consensus Results
| Tool | Agreement | Median (IQR) | Consensus |
|---|---|---|---|
| CT/CBCT | 94.7% | 7 (7–7) | **Yes** |
| Photographic documentation | — | — | **Yes** |
| Impressions (conventional/digital) | — | — | **Yes** |
| Wax-ups / Mock-ups | — | — | **Yes** |
| Panoramic radiographs | 68.1% | 6 (4–7) | No consensus |
| Facial scanning | 43.1% | 4 (4–5) | No consensus |

- CT/CBCT fundamental for sinus anatomy (septae, alveolar artery, membrane) — lateral 91.3%, crestal 4%

### Biomaterials & GBR (Items 12–16, 8)
| Statement | Agreement | Consensus |
|---|---|---|
| Xenogeneic preferred for lateral/crestal sinus lift + simultaneous implant | Highest response | Pattern (no single consensus) |
| Combination biomaterials favored for sinus lift delayed + other grafting | — | Pattern |
| Membrane fixation always for vertical GBR (pins/sutures) | Median 7 (6–7) | **Yes** |
| Ti mesh/non-resorbable membranes negative impact on GBR outcomes | — | **Yes** |
| Resorbable membrane exposure detrimental impact | Divided | **No consensus** |
| Biologics alone as barrier/for socket preservation/sinus lift | Disagreement | **No consensus** |
| Biologics + bone substitutes | 60.3% agreement | Trend |

| Statement | Agreement | Consensus |
|---|---|---|

## Limitations

- Single-round survey (no Delphi iteration); consensus thresholds arbitrary
- Expert selection via organizations (AO, EAO, ITI, OF) — potential selection bias
- Anonymity prevented country-level response analysis
- Consensus ≠ evidence-based guideline; reflects expert opinion where evidence lacking
- No systematic reviews integrated at survey stage (planned for GCCG workshop)
- Maintenance section limited to frequency justification; no risk-based intervals addressed
- Panoramic radiograph role unclear (no consensus)
- GBR/augmentation-specific maintenance protocols not differentiated from general implant maintenance

## Glossary

- **GCCG (Global Consensus for Clinical Guidelines)**: 글로벌 임상 가이드라인 합의 이니셔티브
- **Edentulous maxilla (무치악 상악)**: 치아가 없는 상악
- **Sinus lift / Maxillary sinus floor augmentation (상악동거상/상악동저거상술)**: 상악동 바닥을 올려 임플란트 식립 위한 골 높이 확보
- **Alveolar bone augmentation (치조골 증대술)**: GBR, 블록 이식 등 골 결손 재건 술식
- **RBH (Residual Bone Height, 잔존 골 높이)**: 상악동 바닥부터 치조정까지의 골 높이
- **ClinRO (Clinician-Reported Outcome, 술자 보고 아웃컴)**: 임상가가 평가하는 결과 지표
- **PROM (Patient-Reported Outcome Measure, 환자 보고 결과 측정)**: 환자가 직접 보고하는 결과 지표
- **Likert scale (리커트 척도)**: 동의도 측정 7점 척도 (강하게 반대 ~ 강하게 동의)
