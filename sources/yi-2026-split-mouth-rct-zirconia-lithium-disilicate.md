## Why Ingested

Split-mouth RCT providing 3-year head-to-head comparison of monolithic ZrO₂ vs lithium disilicate (LS) single crowns on customized titanium abutments — directly addresses clinical question of whether LS can substitute ZrO₂ for posterior implant crowns. Reinforces and contextualizes [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/pjetursson-2026-metal-ceramic-all-ceramic-crowns-survival-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (n=40 patients, 80 implants) at Peking University; each patient received one ZrO₂ and one LS crown on bilateral posterior implant sites with customized Ti abutments, 3-year follow-up.
Primary outcome ΔMBL: ZrO₂ −0.62 ± 1.22 mm vs LS −0.36 ± 1.07 mm; paired difference 0.26 mm (95% CI −0.17 to 0.70), above −0.5 mm non-inferiority margin — LS non-inferior; no crown fractures, chipping, or debonding in either group.
LS monolithic crowns are a reliable alternative to ZrO₂ for single posterior implant restorations at 3 years; biological complication rate was numerically higher with LS (15% vs 5%) but not significant.

## 세줄요약

악궁분할 무작위대조시험(n=40명, 80개 임플란트), 베이징대; 양쪽 대합 후방 임플란트에 맞춤 Ti 어버트먼트로 ZrO₂(대조) vs 이규산리튬(리튬 디실리케이트, LS; 시험) 모놀리식 크라운 각각 1개씩 시멘트 고정, 3년 추적.
1차 결과 변연골수준 변화(Marginal Bone Level Change, ΔMBL): ZrO₂ −0.62 mm vs LS −0.36 mm, 쌍대 차이 0.26 mm(95% CI −0.17~0.70) — 비열등성 마진 −0.5 mm 초과 확인; 양군 모두 크라운 파절·칩핑·탈락 없음.
3년 시점에서 LS는 MBL 기준 ZrO₂에 비열등하며 대체 가능한 후방 단독 임플란트 보철 재료이나, 생물학적 합병증률(15% vs 5%, p=0.219) 차이는 향후 장기 추적 필요.

## Key Contributions

- First adequately powered split-mouth RCT directly comparing monolithic ZrO₂ vs LS for posterior implant single crowns with 3-year follow-up
- Established non-inferiority of LS using prespecified −0.5 mm MBL margin (lower 95% CI bound = −0.17 mm)
- Both materials: 0 crown fractures, 0 chipping, 0 debonding at 3 years
- ZrO₂ survival 97.5% (1 implant loss) vs LS 100%; no prosthesis loss in either group
- Biological complications numerically more common with LS (15% vs 5%, p=0.219 — not significant)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective split-mouth RCT, non-inferiority
- **Population**: Patients with bilateral single-tooth implants at homologous posterior sites
- **n**: 40 patients completing 3-year follow-up (80 implants total)
- **Intervention**: Monolithic CAD-CAM ZrO₂ crown on customized Ti abutment (Control) vs monolithic CAD-CAM LS crown on customized Ti abutment (Test); both cement-retained
- **Primary outcome**: Marginal bone level change (ΔMBL) from baseline to 3 years; non-inferiority margin −0.5 mm
- **Secondary outcomes**: Implant/prosthesis survival, biological complications, technical complications, mSBI, mPLI, FIPS (Functional Implant Prosthetic Score)
- **Setting**: Single center, Peking University

## Limitations

- Single center (Peking University); 3-year medium-term follow-up only
- Homologous site assumption may not fully control for patient-level confounders
- Biological complication rate difference (15% vs 5%) was not significant but clinically notable; longer follow-up needed
- Sample size powered for MBL, not complications

## Glossary

- **Split-mouth RCT**: 같은 환자의 양측 대응 부위에 각각 다른 처치를 배정하는 무작위대조시험 — 환자 간 변이를 제거하는 내부대조 설계
- **Non-inferiority margin**: 비열등성 시험의 허용 열등폭; 여기서는 MBL 차이 −0.5 mm 이내면 동등 인정
- **ΔMBL (Marginal Bone Level Change)**: 기준 시점 대비 변연골수준 변화; 음수값은 골소실
- **FIPS (Functional Implant Prosthetic Score)**: 임플란트 보철의 기능적 성공을 평가하는 복합 지표
- **Customized Ti abutment**: 맞춤형 티타늄 어버트먼트 — 표준화된 Ti-Base와 달리 개별 해부학에 맞게 가공
