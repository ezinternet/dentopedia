## Three-line Summary

Four-center, three-arm, 12-week Korean RCT (118 randomized, 114 patients / 114 implants analyzed, one implant per patient) of nonsurgical peri-implantitis treatment: mechanical debridement + metronidazole-minocycline ointment (MM) vs debridement + minocycline ointment (MC) vs debridement only (NST).

At 12 weeks the composite treatment-success rate was 31.6% (MM) and 20.5% (MC) vs 2.7% (NST) (p = 0.011 and 0.040 vs NST); in implants with deepest PPD ≥8 mm, MM reduced PPD more than MC at week 4 (p = 0.025) and week 12 (p = 0.047), and Tannerella forsythia detection was lower with MM than MC (p = 0.038).

Either local antibiotic ointment adds a real but small resolution gain over debridement alone, and even the best arm left about two-thirds of implants unresolved at 12 weeks; follow-up is short and one author is affiliated with MXBIO Corporation (abstract-only, conflict-of-interest details not retrieved).

## 세줄요약

4개 기관·3군·12주 한국 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 118명 무작위배정, 114명·임플란트 114개 분석, 환자당 1개) — 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 비외과적 치료에서 기계적 변연청결 (Mechanical Debridement) + 메트로니다졸-미노사이클린 연고 (Metronidazole-Minocycline, MM) vs 변연청결 + 미노사이클린 연고 (Minocycline, MC) vs 변연청결 단독 (Nonsurgical Treatment, NST) 비교.

12주 복합 치료성공률은 MM 31.6%, MC 20.5%, NST 2.7% (NST 대비 p = 0.011, 0.040); 최심 탐침깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) ≥8 mm 임플란트에서는 MM이 MC보다 PPD를 더 줄였고 (4주 p = 0.025, 12주 p = 0.047), 태너렐라 포르시시아 (Tannerella forsythia) 검출률도 MM이 MC보다 낮았다 (p = 0.038).

국소 항생제 연고 보조요법은 변연청결 단독 대비 성공률을 유의하게 높이지만, 최선 군에서도 12주에 약 2/3가 미해소로 남고; 추적이 짧으며 저자 1인이 MXBIO Corporation 소속이다 (초록만 확보 — 이해상충 상세 미확인).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Three-arm design separates the effect of adding metronidazole to minocycline (MM vs MC) from the effect of any local antibiotic (MC vs NST).
- Both local antibiotic ointments raised the 12-week composite success rate over debridement alone (31.6% and 20.5% vs 2.7%).
- MM outperformed MC for PPD reduction only in the deepest-pocket subgroup (deepest PPD ≥8 mm), and lowered T. forsythia detection vs MC.
- Explicitly targets established (not only incipient) peri-implantitis lesions, which the authors note earlier local-antibiotic trials mostly did not.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Multicenter (4 centers), three-arm, parallel-group RCT, 12 weeks
- **n**: 118 randomized; 4 excluded during the trial; 114 patients / 114 implants analyzed (one implant per patient)
- **Arms**: (a) MM — mechanical debridement + metronidazole-minocycline ointment; (b) MC — mechanical debridement + minocycline ointment; (c) NST — mechanical debridement only
- **Primary outcome**: treatment success at 12 weeks — absence of bleeding or suppuration on probing and of sites with PPD ≥5 mm (abstract wording); analyzed by multivariate logistic regression
- **Secondary outcomes**: PPD reduction (including a deepest-PPD ≥8 mm subgroup at weeks 4 and 12), microbiological detection (e.g., T. forsythia)
- Ointment dose, application schedule, blinding, allocation concealment and adverse-event data: not available from the abstract

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only: absolute PPD/BOP/marginal bone values, dosing, blinding and adverse events not retrieved.
- 12-week follow-up; no radiographic bone outcome reported in the abstract.
- Low absolute resolution even in the best arm (31.6%) — supports non-surgical local antibiotics as an adjunct, not a definitive treatment for established peri-implantitis.
- MM advantage over MC is confined to a deep-pocket subgroup (post hoc vs pre-specified status not stated in the abstract).
- One author is affiliated with MXBIO Corporation; funding and conflict-of-interest statements were not retrieved.
- The commercial brand of the minocycline ointment is not named in the abstract, so applying the MC arm to a specific product (e.g., Minoclin) is an inference from formulation, not a stated fact.

## 6. Related Work

- Lee 2026 (lee-2026-minocycline-local-delivery-peri-implantitis-multicenter-rct): later two-center Korean placebo-controlled RCT of 2% minocycline ointment; consistent short-term PD/BOP benefit.
- Grusovin 2022: SR+MA of non-surgical antibiotic adjuncts; Park 2021 contributed two comparisons ("Park 2021a" MM, "Park 2021b" MC) and was the only trial behind the local-antibiotic success RR 9.89.
- Liu 2026: 22-RCT SR+MA of adjunctive antibiotics; includes Park 2021.

## 7. Glossary

- **MM**: mechanical debridement + metronidazole-minocycline ointment arm
- **MC**: mechanical debridement + minocycline ointment arm
- **NST**: nonsurgical treatment (mechanical debridement) only arm
- **Treatment success**: composite endpoint — no bleeding/suppuration on probing and no sites with PPD ≥5 mm
- **T. forsythia**: Tannerella forsythia, a red-complex periodontal/peri-implant pathogen
