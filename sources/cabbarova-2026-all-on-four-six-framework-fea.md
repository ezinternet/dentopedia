## Why Ingested

상악 풀아치 보철 계획에서 All-on-4 vs All-on-6 결정과 프레임워크 소재 선택은 핵심 임상 의사결정. 기존 [[wiki/overviews/tilted-axial-implant-angled-abutment-overview]]에 소재별 응력 분포 비교 데이터가 없음. 이 FEA는 PEEK·PEKK·Trilor·Trinia 등 신소재까지 포함해 All-on-4/6 6가지 소재 조합 12모델을 체계적으로 비교.

## Three-line Summary

FEA study of mandibular All-on-Four vs All-on-Six configurations (12 models = 2 protocols × 6 framework materials: titanium, zirconia, PEEK, PEKK, Trilor, Trinia) with 150 N bilateral vertical occlusal loading on CBCT-based mandible model.

All-on-Six produced more homogeneous stress distribution with lower peri-implant bone and screw stress across all materials; PEEK/PEKK frameworks transmitted highest stress to implants and bone (higher deformation = more load transfer); titanium/zirconia (high E) distributed stress most favorably; FRC materials (Trilor/Trinia) showed intermediate biomechanical performance within acceptable clinical limits.

All-on-Six + rigid frameworks (Ti/Zr) is the biomechanically optimal combination; PEEK/PEKK are contraindicated for full-arch frameworks due to excessive stress transfer to peri-implant bone; FRC composites (Trilor/Trinia) represent viable alternatives if rigidity constraints exist.

## 세줄요약

하악 All-on-4 vs All-on-6, 6가지 프레임워크 소재(Ti·Zr·PEEK·PEKK·Trilor·Trinia) FEA 비교(12모델); 양측 150 N 수직 교합력, 임플란트 직경 3.7 mm × 10 mm.

All-on-6가 All-on-4보다 모든 소재에서 하중 분산 우수 — 특히 원심 임플란트·캔틸레버 응력 감소; PEEK·PEKK는 낮은 탄성계수로 임플란트·골에 응력 집중 최대; Ti·Zr는 응력 분산 가장 균등; FRC(Trilor·Trinia)는 중간 — 임상적 허용 범위.

보철 계획: All-on-6 + Ti/Zr 프레임워크 = 최적 생역학 조합; PEEK/PEKK는 풀아치 프레임워크 금기; FRC는 PEEK보다 유리하나 Ti/Zr 대비 열등; All-on-4 선택 시 강성 프레임워크 필수.

## 1. Document Information

- **Study type**: 3D Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
- **Model**: Single edentulous mandible CBCT (one patient, Necmettin Erbakan University)
- **Journal**: Clinical and Experimental Dental Research, 2026

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic FEA comparing All-on-4 vs All-on-6 across 6 framework materials including newer FRC composites (Trilor, Trinia)
- Quantifies the elastic modulus effect: low-E materials (PEEK/PEKK) paradoxically increase implant-level stress by transmitting more deformation
- Confirms All-on-6's biomechanical advantage: more implants → shorter cantilever per implant → lower distal stress concentration
- Framework-material stress ranking in All-on-Four cortical bone: PEEK > PEKK > Trilor > Trinia > Ti ≈ Zr

## 3. Methodology

- All-on-4 model: 2 axial anterior + 2 posterior at 30° distal tilt; 10 mm distal cantilever
- All-on-6 model: 2 axial anterior + 2 at 15° (premolar region) + 2 at 30° (molar region); no cantilever
- All implants: 3.7 mm × 10 mm, multi-unit abutments 2 mm gingival height
- Loading: bilateral 150 N vertical, fully fixed (encastre) mandible
- Osseointegration: "tie" constraints at implant-bone interface
- Screw preload: 25 Ncm tightening torque → 781 N preload
- Material E values: cortical bone 13,700 / Ti 110,000 / Zr 210,000 / PEEK 4,200 / PEKK 5,100 / Trinia 18,500 / Trilor 26,000 MPa

## 4. Key Results

Framework stress (vMS) in All-on-Four vs All-on-Six:
| Material | All-on-4 (MPa) | All-on-6 (MPa) | Reduction |
|----------|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| PEEK | 805.7 | 101.7 | ~87% |
| PEKK | 692.3 | 100.2 | ~86% |
| Titanium | 1117 | 206.7 | ~81% |
| Trilor | 818.2 | 145.7 | ~82% |
| Trinia | 794.5 | 134.9 | ~83% |
| Zirconia | 1372 | 227.2 | ~83% |

- All-on-Six reduces cortical bone stress across all materials
- PEEK/PEKK → highest stress in implants, abutment screws, peri-implant bone (low E → greater deformation → more load to interface)
- FRC (Trilor/Trinia): intermediate — better than PEEK, worse than Ti/Zr
- Highest abutment stress: posterior implants in All-on-4 regardless of material

## 5. Limitations

- Single patient CBCT model; no anatomical variation
- Isotropic, linear elastic material properties (biological tissues are viscoelastic, anisotropic)
- Static loading only; fatigue/cyclic effects not modeled
- No clinical validation of the 150 N bilateral loading assumption

## 7. Glossary

- **vMS**: von Mises stress (principal stress measure for ductile materials)
- **E**: Young's modulus / elastic modulus (stiffness; higher E = stiffer, better load distribution)
- **PEEK**: Polyetheretherketone (semi-crystalline thermoplastic; E≈4,200 MPa — much lower than bone)
- **PEKK**: Polyetherketoneketone (newer thermoplastic; E≈5,100 MPa)
- **Trilor/Trinia**: FRC (fiber-reinforced composite) CAD-CAM materials; intermediate stiffness
- **Cantilever effect**: Stress amplification distal to last implant — proportional to cantilever length
