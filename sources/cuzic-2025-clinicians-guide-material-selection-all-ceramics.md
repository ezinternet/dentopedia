## Why Ingested

사용자가 "치과 재료의 최근 추세·경향" 논문을 PMC 전문으로 요청. 기존 [[dental-materials/warreth-2020-all-ceramic-restorations-narrative-review]]가 all-ceramic 재료를 개괄하지만 2020년까지이며 monolithic·CAD/CAM 전환과 resin cement 선택 프레임을 최신화하지 못함. 본 리뷰(Cuzic 2025)는 2008–2025 문헌 58편으로 조성별 세라믹 분류·적용증·시멘테이션 프로토콜을 갱신해 그 페이지를 보강(reinforces)하고, [[resin-bonding/blatz-2018-resin-bonding-high-strength-ceramics-review]]의 접착 원칙을 재료-선택 의사결정 맥락으로 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative/comprehensive literature review (Materials 2025, 58 articles 2008–2025 across narrative/systematic reviews and in vitro studies) providing a clinician-facing framework for all-ceramic material selection and cementation in CAD/CAM digital dentistry.

Ceramics are split by composition into silica-based (feldspathic, leucite-reinforced, lithium disilicate) vs oxide-based (alumina, zirconia); material choice is driven by matching mechanical demand to site — translucent glass-ceramics anteriorly, high-strength zirconia / zirconia-reinforced lithium silicate for posterior/load-bearing — while etchability dictates the surface-treatment and resin-cement protocol (HF + silane for glass-matrix; air-abrasion + MDP for zirconia).

The paper is a decision-support synthesis, not new primary data: it reflects the field's shift from layered to monolithic restorations and the growing complexity of resin-cement selection, and its evidence base is heterogeneous (in vitro + reviews, no meta-analysis).

## 세줄요약

서술형/포괄적 문헌 리뷰 (Materials 2025, 2008–2025 문헌 58편 — 서술·체계적 리뷰 및 in vitro 연구 종합), CAD/CAM 디지털 치의학에서 all-ceramic 재료 선택과 시멘테이션에 대한 임상가용 프레임워크.

세라믹은 조성에 따라 규산염계(장석질·류사이트강화·리튬다이실리케이트)와 산화물계(알루미나·지르코니아)로 나뉘며, 재료 선택은 부위별 기계적 요구에 맞춤 — 전치부는 반투명 유리세라믹, 구치부·하중부는 고강도 지르코니아/지르코니아강화 리튬실리케이트 — 이고, 에칭 가능성이 표면처리·레진시멘트 프로토콜을 결정(유리기질은 HF+실란, 지르코니아는 air-abrasion+MDP)한다.

새 1차 데이터가 아닌 의사결정 지원 종합물로서 적층→모놀리식 전환과 레진시멘트 선택의 복잡성 증가를 반영하나, 근거기반이 이질적(in vitro+리뷰, 메타분석 없음)이라는 한계가 있다.

- **Aim**: Provide a practical clinician-facing overview of the composition, characteristics, applicability, and adhesive cementation of contemporary all-ceramic CAD/CAM materials.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates dental-ceramic classification by **chemical composition** (silica-based vs oxide-based) as the most clinically useful first-order scheme, over the many competing schemes (indication, etching condition, processing, microstructure, translucency, fracture resistance).
- Maps each ceramic class to **site-specific applicability** (anterior aesthetics vs posterior/load-bearing) and to its **etchability**, which determines the surface-treatment + luting-agent decision.
- Frames the field-level **shift from multilayered/veneered to monolithic restorations** (to avoid veneer-layer chipping), with the trade-offs of antagonist wear and aesthetic matching.
- Provides a two-part practical structure: (1) ceramic material classification/properties/applicability; (2) adhesive materials and cementation protocol for all-ceramic dentistry.

## 3. Methodology

- Literature review of **Google Scholar, PubMed/Medline, Web of Science**, publications **2008–2025**, English only.
- Keywords: "all-ceramic restorations", "modern dentistry", "computer-aided design", "dental ceramics", "adhesion", "resin cements".
- Included: narrative, comprehensive, and systematic reviews + in vitro studies on digital-dentistry materials. Excluded: non-English, case reports.
- **58 articles** selected (all journal categories, contingent on inclusion criteria) + supplementary references. No quantitative synthesis / meta-analysis (narrative appraisal only).

## 4. Key Results

- **Composition split**: amorphous vs crystalline; two major groups — oxide-based (alumina, zirconia) and silica/glass-matrix with fillers (feldspathic, leucite, lithium disilicate).
- **Material-to-indication mapping**:
  - Feldspathic / leucite-reinforced → anterior veneers, inlays, onlays (high translucency, lower strength).
  - Lithium disilicate → anterior + posterior crowns, short-span bridges (strength ↑, good aesthetics).
  - Zirconia-reinforced lithium silicate → wide range incl. implant-supported crowns (strength + aesthetics).
  - Zirconia → posterior multi-unit bridges, implant abutments (superior strength/fracture toughness; high-translucency & layered formulations widen aesthetic use).
  - Hybrid ceramics → elasticity + moderate strength, less abrasive, various indications incl. implant crowns.
- **Cementation**: glass-ionomer (fluoride release, prevents secondary caries) vs resin cement (higher bond strength, better aesthetics/shade-matching). Resin cements differ by polymerisation (light-, self-, dual-cured); choice depends on ceramic translucency/thickness, light-curing access, and working-time needs.
- **Surface treatment**: mechanical (air abrasion/sandblasting, diamond burs, sandpaper discs, laser) — but excessive roughening risks crack initiation → fracture; chemical (HF etching of glass-matrix ceramics → micro-retention; silanization to couple ceramic silica with resin acrylic group). MDP-based conditioning for oxide (zirconia) ceramics. Chlorhexidine after acid etching preserves collagen / inhibits MMP in etch-and-rinse dentin adhesives (not for self-etch/self-adhesive).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative/comprehensive review with **heterogeneous evidence** (in vitro + reviews), **no meta-analysis** — no pooled effect sizes or graded strength of recommendation.
- Selection and appraisal are non-systematic; risk of citation/selection bias.
- Restoration performance remains dependent on **operator skill/knowledge** and manufacturer-specific instructions — the guide is decision-support, not a survival dataset.
- Calls for continuing professional education and more standardized guidance on physical/mechanical/biological/aesthetic properties.

## 7. Glossary

- **Monolithic restoration**: single-material full-contour ceramic prosthesis (no veneer layer), avoiding veneer-chipping failure.
- **Glass-matrix (silica-based) ceramic**: feldspathic/leucite/lithium-disilicate ceramics, HF-etchable, silane-coupled to resin cement.
- **Oxide-based ceramic**: alumina/zirconia; not HF-etchable → air-abrasion + MDP-primer conditioning.
- **Silanization**: chemical coupling of ceramic silica to the acrylic group of composite resin, improving bond strength.
- **Zirconia-reinforced lithium silicate (ZLS)**: glass-ceramic combining higher strength with aesthetics.
- **Resin cement polymerisation modes**: light-cured, self-cured, dual-cured — selected by ceramic translucency/thickness and light-curing access.
