## Why Ingested

[[drug/systemic-disease/malave-2022-vasovagal-reactions-during-interventional-pain]]는 통증·불안이 미주신경성 실신 (Vasovagal Syncope, VVS)의 유발인자라고 서술하지만, 그 주장은 임상 관찰과 기전 추론에 기대고 이 위키에는 인과를 직접 시험한 근거가 없었다. 본 RCT는 정맥 천자 (Intravenous, IV) 통증을 국소마취 크림으로 조작해 기립내성 (Orthostatic Tolerance, OT)이 실제로 변하는지 crossover 설계로 측정하므로, 그 리뷰의 "통증 → VVS" 명제에 실험적 인과 사슬을 공급한다.

## Three-line Summary

Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study in N = 23 healthy young adults (14 women; age 24.2 ± 4.4 y) without needle phobia, each undergoing head-up tilt with lower body negative pressure to presyncope on three separate days (IV + EMLA, IV + Placebo, Sham + EMLA).

Orthostatic tolerance fell from Sham + EMLA (27.8 ± 2.4 min) to IV + Placebo (23.0 ± 2.8 min; p = 0.026) but not to IV + EMLA (26.2 ± 2.2 min; p = 0.185), while pain (1–5) was higher in IV + Placebo (2.8 ± 0.2) than IV + EMLA (2.0 ± 2.2; p = 0.002) and Sham + EMLA (1.1 ± 0.1; p < 0.001).

The mechanism appears to be blunted maximal sympathetic activation during orthostatic stress (lower orthostatic HR and reduced maximal forearm vascular resistance in IV + Placebo), implying that topical anaesthesia before needle procedures may reduce vasovagal susceptibility.

## 세줄요약

건강한 젊은 성인 23명(여성 14명, 평균 24.2 ± 4.4세, needle phobia 없음)을 대상으로 한 무작위·이중맹검·위약대조 crossover 연구 — 3일에 걸쳐 정맥관 삽입(EMLA 또는 위약 크림)과 sham 조건에서 head-up tilt + lower body negative pressure로 전실신 (presyncope)까지 유도.

기립내성 (Orthostatic Tolerance, OT)은 Sham + EMLA 27.8 ± 2.4분 대비 IV + Placebo에서 23.0 ± 2.8분으로 감소(p = 0.026)했으나 IV + EMLA는 26.2 ± 2.2분으로 유의한 감소가 없었고(p = 0.185), 통증 점수(1–5)는 IV + Placebo 2.8 ± 0.2로 IV + EMLA 2.0 ± 2.2(p = 0.002), Sham + EMLA 1.1 ± 0.1(p < 0.001)보다 높았다.

기전은 기립 스트레스 중 최대 교감신경 활성화 능력의 저하로 설명되며(IV + Placebo에서 기립 심박수와 최대 전완 혈관저항 (Forearm Vascular Resistance, FVR) 반응이 낮음), 시술 전 국소마취가 미주신경성 실신 위험을 낮출 가능성을 시사한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides experimental, randomised evidence that procedure-related pain itself (not only fear or the sight of blood) reduces orthostatic tolerance — the causal step that narrative reviews on vasovagal reactions assert but do not test.
- Localises the mechanism to the efferent sympathetic limb: pain was associated with lower orthostatic heart rate and a smaller maximal forearm vascular resistance increase, i.e. a reduced capacity to achieve maximal sympathetic activation during orthostatic stress.
- Separates the "needle" from the "pain" component by using a sham cannulation arm, and shows that topical anaesthesia (EMLA) attenuated the orthostatic-tolerance penalty of real cannulation.
- Suggests a preventive role for topical anaesthetics in procedures requiring needles/intravascular instrumentation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, crossover study (three study days per participant).
- **n**: N = 23 (14 women), age 24.2 ± 4.4 years, healthy young adults without needle phobia.
- **Conditions**: (1) IV cannulation with local anaesthetic cream (EMLA) — IV + EMLA; (2) IV cannulation with placebo cream — IV + Placebo; (3) sham IV cannulation with EMLA — Sham + EMLA.
- **Orthostatic challenge**: head-up tilt combined with lower body negative pressure to presyncope (the OT outcome is time to presyncope, in minutes).
- **Pain measurement**: participant-rated pain of the IV procedure on a 1–5 scale.
- **Cardiovascular measurements**: continuous, non-invasive finger plethysmography and electrocardiogram (Finometer Pro); forearm vascular resistance by brachial Doppler.
- **Setting**: single laboratory (Simon Fraser University, Canada); abstract does not report recruitment period, randomisation method, allocation concealment, washout, or sample-size calculation.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Sham + EMLA | IV + EMLA | IV + Placebo | Reported p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orthostatic tolerance (min) | 27.8 ± 2.4 | 26.2 ± 2.2 | 23.0 ± 2.8 | IV+Placebo vs Sham p = 0.026; IV+EMLA vs Sham p = 0.185 |
| Pain rating (1–5) | 1.1 ± 0.1 | 2.0 ± 2.2 | 2.8 ± 0.2 | IV+Placebo vs IV+EMLA p = 0.002; vs Sham p < 0.001 |
| Orthostatic heart rate (bpm) | 87.7 ± 3.1 | 87.3 ± 3.1 | 84.4 ± 3.1 | IV+Placebo vs IV+EMLA p = 0.007; vs Sham p = 0.001 |
| Maximal FVR response (%) | +190.6 ± 17.0 | +221.2 ± 25.9 | +140.7 ± 19.0 | IV+Placebo vs IV+EMLA p < 0.001; vs Sham p = 0.017 |

Values are mean ± SEM (as reported in the abstract). Direction of effect: higher pain → lower OT, lower orthostatic HR, and a smaller maximal FVR rise.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only ingest**: all values above come from the structured PubMed abstract; full Methods/Results (period effects, carryover, per-protocol exclusions) were not accessible.
- **Methodology reporting gaps in the abstract**: it states randomisation, double-blinding, placebo control and crossover, but does **not** report the randomisation/sequence-generation method, allocation concealment, how blinding of EMLA vs placebo cream was maintained, whether analysis was intention-to-treat, or a sample-size calculation. Risk of bias therefore cannot be fully appraised from this record.
- **Small, narrow sample**: N = 23 healthy young adults explicitly *without* needle phobia — the population with the highest real-world vasovagal risk (needle-phobic, older, comorbid, hypovolaemic patients) is excluded by design, limiting generalisability.
- **Surrogate outcome**: time to presyncope during tilt + lower body negative pressure is a laboratory analogue of clinical fainting, not syncope during dental or medical procedures.
- **Internal inconsistency in the abstract**: the IV + EMLA pain value is reported as 2.0 ± 2.2 (SD larger than the mean on a bounded 1–5 scale, and larger than the IV + Placebo dispersion of ± 0.2), which is implausible and should be checked against the full text before the number is cited quantitatively.
- **EMLA did not fully restore OT**: the IV + EMLA vs Sham comparison was not significant (p = 0.185), so the preventive claim is suggestive rather than demonstrated.

## 6. Related Work

- Malave 2022 ([[drug/systemic-disease/malave-2022-vasovagal-reactions-during-interventional-pain]]): narrative review of VVS in interventional pain procedures; lists pain/anxiety as triggers and prior VVS as the strongest risk factor, but cites no experimental pain-manipulation trial — this RCT supplies that missing causal test.
- Smereka 2019 ([[drug/systemic-disease/smereka-2019-preparedness-and-attitudes-towards-medical]]): Polish dental-office survey placing VVS first among emergencies (46.30%), i.e. the epidemiological reason this mechanism matters chairside.

## 7. Glossary

- **VVS (Vasovagal Syncope)**: neurally mediated reflex fainting with bradycardia/hypotension, often triggered by pain, fear or blood-injection-injury stimuli.
- **OT (Orthostatic Tolerance)**: time a subject tolerates an orthostatic challenge before presyncope; laboratory proxy for fainting susceptibility.
- **Head-up tilt + LBNP (Lower Body Negative Pressure)**: combined orthostatic stress used to provoke presyncope reproducibly.
- **FVR (Forearm Vascular Resistance)**: Doppler-derived index of sympathetic vasoconstrictor outflow; its maximal rise indexes maximal sympathetic activation.
- **EMLA**: eutectic mixture of local anaesthetics (lidocaine/prilocaine) topical cream.
- **Sham cannulation**: needle procedure without actual intravascular instrumentation, controlling for the expectation/setting of cannulation.
