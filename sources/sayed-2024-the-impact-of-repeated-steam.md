## Why Ingested

Addresses a direct clinical workflow question: how many times can a chairside adjustment kit (CAK) be steam-sterilized before it degrades polishing efficacy on highly translucent monolithic layered zirconia (HTMLZ)? First study to investigate steam sterilization's effect on a zirconia-specific polishing kit (EVE Diacera). Provides concrete cycle thresholds (flash ≤10, standard ≤5) referenced to the 0.25 μm Ra clinical acceptability threshold.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/sayed-2024-the-impact-of-repeated-steam]]

## Three-line Summary

This in vitro study evaluated the effect of two steam sterilization techniques — standard (121°C, 15 min) and flash/immediate (134°C, 3 min) — on the polishing efficacy of the EVE Diacera chairside adjustment kit (CAK) on highly translucent monolithic multilayered zirconia (HTMLZ, Zolid Gen-X). One hundred disk specimens were polished after each of 5 sterilization cycle milestones (1, 5, 10, 15, 20 cycles), and surface roughness (Ra, Rq) was measured by profilometry and SEM. Both sterilization techniques caused progressive roughness degradation; flash sterilization was superior, with the clinically unacceptable Ra threshold (>0.25 μm) crossed at cycle 10 for flash versus cycle 15 for standard, and the study recommends ≤10 flash sterilization cycles for the EVE Diacera kit.

## 세줄요약

이 시험관 연구는 EVE Diacera 의자옆 조정 키트(CAK)를 표준(121°C, 15분)과 즉시/플래시(134°C, 3분) 두 가지 증기 멸균 방식으로 반복 멸균했을 때 고투명도 단일체 다층 지르코니아(HTMLZ, Zolid Gen-X)의 연마 표면 거칠기에 미치는 영향을 평가하였다. 100개의 디스크 시편을 멸균 1·5·10·15·20회 시점에서 연마한 후 프로파일로미터(Ra, Rq)와 SEM으로 측정한 결과, 두 방식 모두 반복 멸균에 따라 거칠기가 증가하였으며 플래시 멸균이 표준 멸균보다 우수하였다. 임상 허용 임계값(Ra 0.25 μm)은 플래시 10회, 표준 15회에서 초과되어, EVE Diacera 키트는 플래시 멸균 최대 10회 이내 사용을 권장한다.

## Document Information

- **Study type**: In vitro experimental (comparative intervention)
- **Sample**: 100 HTMLZ disc specimens (Zolid Gen-X, 10 mm diameter × 3 mm thickness, shade A2), CAD/CAM milled, sintered per manufacturer protocol
- **Polishing kit**: EVE Diacera (Ernst Vetter, GmbH, Keltern, Germany) — 8-tool kit (2 coarse, 3 medium, 3 fine, diamond/zirconium oxide/silicon dioxide abrasives in elastomeric matrix)
- **Sterilization groups**: Gp S = standard (121°C, 15 min, 1 bar); Gp F = flash/immediate (134°C, 3 min, 2 bar); 5 cycle subgroups each (1, 5, 10, 15, 20 cycles), n=10 each
- **Measurements**: Ra (average surface roughness), Rq (root mean square roughness) by profilometer; SEM at cycles 1, 10, 20
- **Statistics**: One-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD post hoc; P ≤ 0.05
- **Ethics**: Approved by College of Dentistry, Jazan University (CODJU 22081)

## Key Contributions

1. **First study** to systematically assess steam sterilization's effect on a zirconia-specific CAK (EVE Diacera).
2. Establishes that **flash sterilization is superior** to standard for preserving polishing efficacy on HTMLZ.
3. Provides clinically actionable **cycle thresholds**: flash ≤10 cycles, standard ≤5 cycles for Ra within 0.25 μm.
4. Identifies **sterilization duration** (not temperature/pressure alone) as the dominant degradation driver — the 15-min standard cycle caused more elastomeric binder degradation than the 3-min flash cycle.
5. Validates EVE Diacera's manufacturer recommendation of flash sterilization ≤10 cycles (with caveats on the study's multi-specimen-per-cycle design).

## Methodology

- Specimens milled from pre-sintered ZGX blanks using 5-axis CNC; sintered per program P7 (manufacturer spec).
- Grinding standardized with pressure capsule gauge + surveyor-fixed handpiece at 160,000 rpm (NTI-Kahla diamond bur, 40 μm grain).
- Polishing per EVE Diacera protocol: 90 s unidirectional, motor-driven handpiece.
- Ten independent CAK sets (1 per subgroup) to prevent cross-contamination between sterilization milestones.
- Between sterilization cycles (e.g., cycles 2–4 between milestone 1 and 5), separate "wear-in" specimens polished to simulate clinical use.
- Profilometer: JEOL with Vision 32 software (Veeco), stylus scan ~0.75 mm, 3 measurements per specimen averaged, 20× (200 μm × 200 μm).
- SEM: JSM-6360 LV JEOL, 5000×, sputter-coated (Au-Pd).

## Key Results

| Group | Cycle 1 Ra (μm) | Cycle 10 Ra (μm) | Cycle 20 Ra (μm) | Crosses 0.25 μm threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gp F (flash) | 0.208 | ~0.25–0.33 | 0.413 | Cycle 10 |
| Gp S (standard) | 0.240 | ~0.33 | 0.612 | Cycle 15 |

- Rq showed **fewer significant pair differences in Gp F** than Gp S, confirming flash sterilization produces less surface height variability.
- SEM images confirmed: control surfaces smooth; cycle 10 and 20 showed progressive surface defect spread, with Gp F defects smaller/less dense than Gp S.
- One-way ANOVA significant for both Ra and Rq within each group (P ≤ 0.05).
- Clinically acceptable Ra values (≤0.25 μm) were observed in: Gp S at cycles 1, 5, 10; Gp F at cycles 1, 5.

## Limitations

- 10 specimens polished per sterilization milestone vs. 1 clinically recommended per sterilization — overestimates binder wear, inflates Ra values relative to true clinical use.
- Profilometer stylus size limits detection of sub-stylus-radius defects.
- Single zirconia brand (Zolid Gen-X) and single polishing kit (EVE Diacera); generalizability uncertain.
- Wear of polishing tips from specimen 1 to specimen 10 within each subgroup not separately quantified.
- In vitro only — no thermal cycling, oral fluid, or clinical aging simulation.

## Glossary

- **HTMLZ** (Highly Translucent Monolithic Layered Zirconia): Multi-layered CAD/CAM zirconia blending 3Y-TZP and 5Y-TZP within one block (cervical dentin = 3Y-TZP, incisal = 5Y-TZP).
- **Ra** (Average Surface Roughness): Arithmetic mean of absolute surface height deviations from mean plane; clinical threshold for biofilm/wear = 0.25 μm.
- **Rq** (Root Mean Square Roughness): Standard deviation of surface heights; more sensitive than Ra; predicts glazing thickness required.
- **CAK** (Chairside Adjustment Kit): Manufacturer-specific polishing set for in-office adjustment of ceramic restorations.
- **EVE Diacera**: Diamond-abrasive 8-piece zirconia-specific polishing kit in elastomeric (rubber) binder matrix.
- **Flash/immediate sterilization**: 134°C, 3 min, 2 bar pre-vacuum autoclave cycle (vs. standard 121°C, 15 min, 1 bar).
- **LTD** (Low-Temperature Degradation): Hydrothermal aging phenomenon in Y-TZP ceramics; not the focus here but contextually relevant.
