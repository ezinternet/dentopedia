## Why Ingested

The cracked-tooth category holds CBCT diagnostic-accuracy syntheses for vertical root fracture (VRF) that all conclude CBCT misses a substantial share of fractures, especially small and incomplete ones in root-filled teeth ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/de-2025-exploring-diagnostic-reliability-of-cbct]], [[wiki/cracked-tooth/shokri-2024-is-cone-beam-computed-tomography]], [[wiki/cracked-tooth/habibzadeh-2023-diagnostic-efficacy-of-cone-beam]]), but none holds a non-ionizing alternative modality. This head-to-head ex vivo study with a microCT reference standard is the first MRI-versus-CBCT VRF comparison in the wiki and gives the CBCT figures a directly measured, same-sample comparator (see also the dental-MRI hub [[wiki/radiology/mri/mri]]).

## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo diagnostic-accuracy study (University of Minnesota): 115 analysed extracted, root-canal-treated human tooth roots (62 with mechanically induced vertical root fracture, VRF; 53 controls) imaged with MRI (4.0 T, custom intraoral coil), CBCT (small FOV, 0.08 mm voxel) and microCT as the reference standard, read by 3 board-certified endodontists.

MRI vs CBCT: sensitivity 0.66 (95% CI 0.53-0.78) vs 0.58 (0.45-0.70; p = 0.27), specificity 0.72 (0.58-0.83) vs 0.87 (0.75-0.95; p = 0.12), AUC 0.74 vs 0.75 (p = 0.88); intra-rater kappa 0.29-0.48 (MRI) and 0.30-0.44 (CBCT), inter-rater kappa 0.37 (MRI) and 0.49 (CBCT).

No significant difference between the modalities, so the hypothesis that MRI would outperform CBCT was not supported; MRI looks comparable to CBCT for small VRF but the study is an extracted-root laboratory model with fair rater agreement, wide confidence intervals and a sample sized for precision, not for a between-modality difference.

## 세줄요약

체외 진단정확도 연구 (미네소타대): 근관치료한 발거 인간 치근 115개 분석 (인위적으로 수직치근파절 Vertical Root Fracture, VRF 유발 62개, 대조 53개)를 자기공명영상 (Magnetic Resonance Imaging, MRI; 4.0 T, 맞춤 구강내 코일), 콘빔 CT (Cone-Beam CT, CBCT; 소조사야, 복셀 0.08 mm), 기준표준인 마이크로 CT (microCT)로 촬영하고 근관치료 전문의 3인이 판독.

MRI 대 CBCT: 민감도 0.66 (95% CI 0.53-0.78) vs 0.58 (0.45-0.70; p = 0.27), 특이도 0.72 (0.58-0.83) vs 0.87 (0.75-0.95; p = 0.12), 곡선하면적 (Area Under the Curve, AUC) 0.74 vs 0.75 (p = 0.88); 판독자 내 일치도 kappa 0.29-0.48 (MRI)·0.30-0.44 (CBCT), 판독자 간 kappa 0.37 (MRI)·0.49 (CBCT).

두 방식 사이에 유의한 차이가 없어 MRI가 CBCT보다 우월하다는 가설은 지지되지 않았고, 작은 VRF에서 MRI가 CBCT와 비슷한 수준으로 보인다; 다만 발거 치근 실험실 모형이고 판독자 일치도가 낮은 편 (fair-moderate)이며 신뢰구간이 넓고 표본 수가 방식 간 차이 검정이 아니라 정밀도 확보용으로 설계됨.

- **Journal**: Journal of Endodontics 2023;49(6):703-709 (published online 2023-03-25; NIH author manuscript NIHMS1886846)
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.joen.2023.03.011
- **Institution**: Division of TMD and Orofacial Pain, School of Dentistry, University of Minnesota, USA (with the Center for Magnetic Resonance Research)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First direct MRI-versus-CBCT sensitivity/specificity comparison for VRF using microCT (rather than stain plus visual inspection) as the reference standard.
- Reports no significant difference in sensitivity, specificity or AUC between MRI and CBCT for small VRF in root-filled extracted roots.
- Adds rater confidence data: "very confident" VRF calls were correct 100% of the time for CBCT and 80% for MRI, and no consensus rating ever reached "very confident no VRF" for either modality.
- Identifies the voxel-size versus signal-to-noise trade-off as the main technical barrier for MRI (grainy images misread as tiny fractures).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: ex vivo diagnostic-accuracy study with microCT reference; paired imaging of every sample by MRI, CBCT and microCT; sample size chosen to give a narrow CI for MRI sensitivity (60 VRF + 40 controls expected, +20 spare) rather than to power a between-modality difference.
- **Samples**: 120 extracted human tooth roots decoronated, tip-amputated and root-canal-treated; optical coherence tomography screened out VRF induced during treatment; after removing catastrophic failures and identifiable anatomy, 62 roots with VRF (55 single-canal, 7 two-canal) and 53 controls (44 single-canal, 9 two-canal).
- **Imaging**: microCT (Nikon XT H 225); MRI 4.0 T whole-body scanner with custom intraoral coil, images focused with ImageJ for off-resonance blurring; CBCT Carestream CS 9000 (70 kV, 8.0 mA, 10.8 s, voxel 0.08 mm); head-mimicking phantom for MRI/CBCT.
- **Raters**: 3 board-certified endodontists (60+ years combined experience), 3 calibration sessions to reach 80% agreement, 5 rating sessions; axial stack scrolling only; yes/no VRF call plus 4-point confidence scale.
- **Outcomes/statistics**: consensus (2 of 3 raters) sensitivity/specificity (McNemar), ROC AUC from mean confidence (DeLong), Cohen's kappa intra-rater (30% re-read), Fleiss' kappa inter-rater.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Metric | MRI | CBCT | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sensitivity (95% CI) | 0.66 (0.53-0.78) | 0.58 (0.45-0.70) | 0.27 |
| Specificity (95% CI) | 0.72 (0.58-0.83) | 0.87 (0.75-0.95) | 0.12 |
| AUC (95% CI) | 0.74 (0.65-0.83) | 0.75 (0.66-0.84) | 0.88 |
| Intra-rater kappa | 0.29-0.48 | 0.30-0.44 | - |
| Inter-rater kappa | 0.37 (fair) | 0.49 (moderate) | - |
| "Very confident" VRF calls correct | 80% | 100% | - |

Confidence increased with fracture size; fractures were small (the authors cite prior MRI work detecting VRFs 26-64 um wide). No rater drift over time or rater-specific effect was found in subset analyses.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Extracted, decoronated roots in a phantom: no soft tissue, no periodontal bone-loss signs, no patient motion or intraoral coil constraints; clinical performance is unknown.
- Underpowered for a between-modality difference; wide CIs (the authors say a larger sample might reveal differences).
- Fair-to-moderate rater agreement; MRI images grainy from the high-resolution/low-SNR choice, possibly inflating false positives (specificity 0.72) in this study versus 0.83 in the group's earlier study.
- CBCT limited to small-FOV axial scrolling (no multiplanar reformatting, no magnification), which the authors say likely lowered CBCT reliability compared with other studies.
- Dental MRI itself is early-stage (custom coil, 4 T research scanner); post-processing, artifact reduction, coil and sequence work are ongoing.

## 7. Glossary
- **VRF (vertical root fracture)**: longitudinal fracture of the root, frequently in root-canal-treated teeth, usually leading to extraction.
- **MicroCT**: micro-computed tomography; very high-resolution ex vivo scan used as the reference standard.
- **SWIFT**: SWeep Imaging with Fourier Transformation, an MRI sequence tolerant of short-T2 tissues (PubMed keyword of this paper).
- **AUC**: area under the ROC curve; 0.5 = chance, 1.0 = perfect discrimination.
- **SNR**: signal-to-noise ratio; shrinking voxels to resolve tiny fractures lowers SNR and makes MRI images grainy.
