## Why Ingested

No held paper provided specific CAD ratio cut-off values (CL/CSCA and CL/SAOL) that prospectively prevent cantilever (type II) fractures in zirconia full-arch prostheses. This fills the actionable design parameter gap: zero cantilever fractures observed when CL/CSCA<0.51 and CL/SAOL<1.48 criteria were met during digital design.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective observational cohort of 151 screw-retained monolithic zirconia IFCDPs (117 patients, private practice Oct 2021–May 2023, ≥12mo follow-up); CAD parameters—cantilever length (CL), distal cross-sectional connector area (CSCA), and screw access opening length (SAOL)—measured pre-milling; cut-offs applied prospectively (CL/CSCA<0.51; CL/SAOL<1.48).

Zero type II fractures (cantilever fractures); overall prosthetic failure rate 3.97% (6/151): 1 implant failure, 5 type I fractures (mid-span, not cantilever); 4 implants failed (2 during temp, 2 after definitive).

When CL/CSCA<0.51 and CL/SAOL<1.48 cut-offs are respected, cantilever fractures do not occur; type I (mid-span) fractures remain; CSSA and SAOL ratios must complement cantilever length in digital design.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Prospective application of CAD cut-off ratios (CL/CSCA<0.51; CL/SAOL<1.48) — zero cantilever fractures when criteria met
- Introduces SAOL (screw access opening length) as a design variable in addition to CL and CSCA
- Distinguishes Type I (mid-span) from Type II (cantilever) fractures — complementary to CSSA work by Papaspyridakos 2026
- 3.97% prosthetic failure rate comes entirely from implant failure + Type I fractures (mid-span)

## 3. Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Retrospective observational cohort |
| Setting | Private practice (Cuneo, Italy) |
| Period | October 2021–May 2023 |
| N | 151 prostheses, 117 patients |
| Follow-up | ≥12 months post loading |
| CAD parameters | CL (cantilever length), CSCA (distal connector CSA), SAOL (screw access opening length) |
| Cut-offs | CL/CSCA<0.51; CL/SAOL<1.48 — applied prospectively; non-compliant designs modified |
| Primary outcome | Type II fractures (distal cantilever fractures) |

## 4. Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Type II fractures (cantilever) | **0** |
| Type I fractures (mid-span) | 5 (3.31%) |
| Implant failures | 4 (2 during temp, 2 after definitive) |
| Total prosthetic failures | 6 (3.97%) |
| Prosthesis survival | ~96% (145/151) |

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract only — exact follow-up duration, CL/CSCA/SAOL distributions, arch-specific data unavailable
- Short follow-up (≥12mo from October 2021–May 2023 cohort = likely 12–30mo)
- Single center private practice; limited generalizability
- Type I fractures (mid-span, 5 cases) not prevented by the cantilever cut-offs — separate mechanism
