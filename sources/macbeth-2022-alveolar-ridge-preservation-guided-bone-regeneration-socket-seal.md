## Why Ingested

Direct RCT comparing Socket Seal (SS) vs GBR vs unassisted healing for alveolar ridge preservation in the anterior maxilla, with CBCT-measured outcomes at 4 months. Fills a gap in [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/ridge-preservation]] where SS vs GBR head-to-head RCT evidence was sparse; reinforces and quantifies the ARP effect on buccal ridge height and implant dehiscence rates.

## Three-line Summary

RCT (n=42, 14/group) comparing GBR (Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide), Socket Seal (Bio-Oss + Mucograft collagen matrix), and unassisted healing at anterior maxillary extraction sites with 4-month CBCT follow-up.
Both GBR (+0.07±0.83 mm BARH) and SS (+0.65±1.1 mm BARH) prevented buccal ridge height loss vs control (−0.52±0.8 mm); GBR showed smaller horizontal ridge width reduction and less implant dehiscence (57% vs 85%, p=0.03).
GBR is superior for radiographic bone preservation; SS has higher complication rates (43% matrix dehiscence) but comparable vertical outcomes; both techniques reduce the need for augmentation at implant placement.

## Key Contributions

- Head-to-head RCT of GBR vs SS vs control — rare direct comparison of two ARP approaches
- Collagen matrix (Mucograft Seal) as the SS barrier — not free soft tissue graft, unlike classic Seibert technique
- Superimposed CBCT method for cross-sectional socket and alveolar process area measurements
- Quantified implant dehiscence at 4 months: GBR 57%, SS 64%, Control 85% (GBR vs Control p=0.03)
- All three groups achieved 100% prosthetically driven implant placement despite differences in bone preservation

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-centre, prospective, randomised, single-blind controlled clinical trial
- **Setting**: Specialist secondary care referral practice (military, UK)
- **Population**: n=42 (14/group); military patients 18–55 years, moderate-to-thick phenotype, anterior maxilla (15–25 position)
- **Interventions**:
  - GBR: atraumatic extraction → Bio-Oss fill → Bio-Gide membrane → cross-mattress Ethylon 6-0 sutures (no primary closure)
  - SS: Bio-Oss fill → Mucograft Seal collagen matrix → single interrupted sutures
  - Control: gauze pressure only
- **Primary outcome**: Change in mid-buccal alveolar ridge height (BARH) at 4 months via CBCT (OnDemand3D, grey-scale profile measurement)
- **Secondary outcomes**: PARH, horizontal ridge width (CARW/AARW at 5mm and 10mm), cross-sectional socket area, implant dehiscence, VAS pain, complications
- **Randomisation**: Stratified block (groups of 2), operator blinded pre-treatment; assessor blinded throughout
- **Tooth types**: 36 central incisors, 1 canine, 5 premolars
- **Antibiotics**: Amoxicillin 500 mg pre-op × 5 days

## Key Results

### Primary — Buccal Alveolar Ridge Height (BARH, 4 months)

| Group | Mid-BARH change (mm) | vs Control |
|-------|---------------------|------------|
| GBR | +0.07 ± 0.83 | p = 0.04 |
| SS | +0.65 ± 1.1 | p = 0.005 |
| Control | −0.52 ± 0.8 | — |

### Secondary — Palatal Alveolar Ridge Height (PARH)

| Group | Mid-PARH change (mm) | vs Control |
|-------|---------------------|------------|
| GBR | +0.86 ± 1.37 | p = 0.02 |
| SS | +0.65 ± 1.42 | n.s. |
| Control | −0.43 ± 0.83 | — |

### Horizontal Ridge Width (Mid-CARW, 5 mm depth)

| Group | Change (mm) | % reduction |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| GBR | −2.17 ± 0.84 | 26.4% |
| SS | −2.36 ± 2.76 | 26.7% |
| Control | −2.30 ± 1.11 | 27.5% |

No significant difference between groups (p=0.89).

### Cross-Sectional Socket Area (Mid-SA)

| Group | Area change (mm²) | % |
|-------|-----------------|---|
| GBR | −2.27 ± 11.89 | −4% |
| SS | −0.88 ± 15.48 | −1% |
| Control | −6.93 ± 8.22 | −13% |

GBR vs Control: p = 0.01.

### Implant Placement / Augmentation (4 months)

| Group | Dehiscence requiring GBR | Any augmentation |
|-------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| GBR | 4/14 (29%) | 8/14 (57%) |
| SS | 7/14 (50%) | 9/14 (64%) |
| Control | 10/14 (71%) | 12/14 (85%) |

GBR vs Control dehiscence: p = 0.03.

### Complications

- **GBR**: 49% inflammation/swelling, 42% mucosal colour change, 28% membrane sloughing, 21% graft sequestration
- **SS**: 43% collagen matrix partial breakdown (prior to suture removal), 7% complete seal loss, 21% inflammation, 28% colour change
- **Control**: 56% gingival recession, 21% sensitivity, 1 dry socket

VAS pain: no significant difference between groups at 2 weeks (p=0.62) or 8 weeks (p=0.71).

## Limitations

- Single-centre military population (predominantly male, mean 32 years); limited generalisability
- Moderate-to-thick phenotype required — excludes thin phenotype (higher-risk)
- Low power for horizontal width changes (post hoc power = 0.255) — horizontal results underpowered
- n=14/group limits subgroup analyses (tooth type, phenotype interactions)
- No soft tissue volumetric measurements; CBCT radiation exposure
- Stratified randomisation still resulted in minor imbalance in non-incisor teeth

## Glossary

- **ARP (Alveolar Ridge Preservation)**: Procedures at extraction socket to limit post-extraction dimensional bone and soft tissue changes
- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration)**: Barrier membrane technique isolating bone defect from soft tissue ingrowth to allow bone healing
- **SS (Socket Seal)**: ARP technique sealing socket aperture with a soft tissue or collagen matrix graft without primary flap closure
- **BARH (Buccal Alveolar Ridge Height)**: Vertical distance from alveolar bone crest to radiographic reference stent on buccal aspect
- **CARW/AARW**: Coronal (5 mm) / Apical (10 mm) alveolar ridge width — horizontal ridge dimension at defined depths
- **Dehiscence**: Exposure of implant threads due to insufficient bone coverage at implant placement
