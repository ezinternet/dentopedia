## Why Ingested

기존 [[overviews/tmd-management-evidence-ladder]] 관련 맥락에서 교합안정장치 (Occlusal Splint, OS)가 경추통증 (neck pain)에 추가 효과를 주는지 논란이 있었음. 이 RCT는 상담 단독 vs 상담+교합안정장치를 직접 비교해, 목통증 및 경부기능장애 (Neck Disability Index, NDI)에 두 군의 차이가 없음을 보여 conservative self-management 우선 원칙을 뒷받침함. [[overviews/occlusal-splint-tmd-value-adjunct-prediction-overview]] 축 2(스플린트 부가가치)의 근거를 보강함.

## Three-line Summary

Parallel-arm RCT (n=51 completers, Turin, Italy) in TMD pain patients with coexisting neck pain comparing counselling alone vs counselling plus Michigan-type hard occlusal splint over 3 months.

Both groups showed significant reductions in neck pain VAS (C: −7.0 mm, p=0.006; OS: −8.0 mm, p=0.02) and NDI (C: −3.3, p=0.007; OS: −4.2, p=0.001), with no between-group difference for any cervical outcome (all p>0.05; Cohen's d<0.5).

Adding an occlusal splint to counselling provides no additional benefit for neck pain or neck disability at 3 months; self-management counselling alone is sufficient for cervical comorbidities in TMD.

## 세줄요약

교합안정장치 (Occlusal Splint, OS)를 추가하는 것이 경추통증·경부기능장애 (Neck Disability Index, NDI)에 추가 효익을 주는지 평가한 병렬 RCT (n=51 완료자, 이탈리아 토리노).

양군 모두 목통증 시각아날로그척도 (Visual Analogue Scale, VAS) 및 NDI가 유의하게 감소했으나, 군간 차이는 없었음 (VAS p=0.69; NDI p=0.57; Cohen's d<0.5).

교합안정장치 추가는 경추 관련 증상에 추가적 효과 없음; 상담 단독이 TMD 동반 경추통 초기치료로 충분함.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Oral Rehabilitation
- **Year**: 2025
- **DOI**: 10.1111/joor.70040
- **PMC**: PMC12624201
- **Study design**: Parallel-arm randomised clinical trial (RCT)
- **Follow-up**: 3 months (T0 baseline → T1 at 3 months)
- **Setting**: Clinic of TMD, CIR Dental School, University of Turin, Italy
- **Ethics**: University of Turin Ethics Committee (approval no. 246/2019)

## 2. Key Contributions

1. First RCT specifically powered to detect between-group differences in cervical outcomes (NDI) in TMD patients with coexisting neck pain.
2. Demonstrates that self-management counselling alone is sufficient to improve neck pain and NDI in TMD patients — splint adds no cervical benefit.
3. Supports international consensus that conservative self-management should be the primary TMD treatment modality.
4. Highlights baseline imbalance in TMD pain (counselling group higher at baseline), requiring cautious interpretation of within-group TMD pain comparisons.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

### Participants
- Recruited March–December 2019 at University of Turin TMD Clinic
- Inclusion: adults 18–60 yrs; DC/TMD pain diagnosis (myalgia and/or arthralgia and/or headache attributed to TMD); TMD pain NRS >3; neck pain NRS >3
- Exclusion: other TMD/cervical therapy in past 3 months; NSAIDs/muscle-relaxants/TCA in past month; neurological disease; prior facial trauma/whiplash/TMJ surgery
- 116 screened → 67 randomised → 52 completers (27 counselling-only, 24 OS group; 8 dropouts per group)

### Randomisation & Blinding
- Block randomisation by independent statistician; allocation concealed
- Participants and treating clinician NOT blinded (nature of treatments precludes blinding)
- Statistician performing analysis blinded to group allocation

### Interventions
- **Group C (Counselling)**: Patient education — jaw muscle self-care, parafunctional habit modification, soft diet, mandibular rest position, home exercises
- **Group OS (Counselling + Occlusal Splint)**: Same counselling + Michigan-type hard resin stabilisation splint (full-coverage upper arch, flat occlusal surface, worn nightly); no formal adherence monitoring

### Outcomes & Instruments
- **Primary**: Neck Disability Index (NDI) — 10 items, 0–50; categories: none/mild/moderate/severe/complete
- **Secondary**: Neck pain VAS (0–100 mm); TMD pain VAS; pain-free maximum mouth opening (MMO, mm)
- Analysis: per-protocol (completers only); Mann-Whitney or t-test for between-group; Shapiro-Wilk for normality; 95% CI; Cohen's d for effect size; R software

### Sample Size
- Powered on NDI: n=64 (32/group) for 80% power to detect Δ=10 (SD=14, α=0.05 two-sided)
- Achieved n=52 completers (underpowered relative to target)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Group C (n=27) | Group OS (n=24) | Between-group p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neck pain VAS change | −7.0 mm (p=0.006) | −8.0 mm (p=0.02) | p=0.69 |
| NDI change | −3.3 (p=0.007) | −4.2 (p=0.001) | p=0.57 |
| MMO change | +2.6 mm (p=0.03) | NS | p=0.64 |
| TMD pain VAS | Higher at baseline* | — | — |

*Baseline TMD pain imbalance (C group higher, p<0.05) — limits direct between-group TMD pain comparison.

- Effect sizes: Cohen's d <0.5 for all between-group comparisons → small, not clinically meaningful
- TMD diagnosis: myalgia 100% (C) vs 96% (OS); arthralgia 44% (C) vs 38% (OS) — NS between groups
- Both groups: predominantly female (~80%), mean age ~36 years

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

1. **Underpowered**: Only 52 completers vs 64 required — type II error risk for detecting small true differences
2. **No blinding of participants/clinician**: Treatment allocation known; potential nocebo/placebo effects uncontrolled
3. **No adherence monitoring**: Splint wear compliance not measured — unknown whether poor adherence explains null splint effect
4. **Short follow-up (3 months)**: Long-term effects of splint on cervical outcomes unknown
5. **Baseline imbalance in TMD pain**: Randomisation did not fully balance this variable — results for TMD pain comparisons require caution
6. **Per-protocol analysis only**: No ITT analysis (8 dropouts per group); potential attrition bias
7. **Single centre**: Turin academic setting; generalisability limited

## 6. Related Work

- DC/TMD (Diagnostic Criteria for Temporomandibular Disorders) criteria used for diagnosis
- Graded Chronic Pain Scale (Question 2) used to assess baseline pain severity
- Italian-validated Neck Disability Index used for cervical disability measurement
- International consensus: conservative self-management as first-line TMD treatment
- Michigan-type stabilisation splint as standard hard occlusal splint reference
