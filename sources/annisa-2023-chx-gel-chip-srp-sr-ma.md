## Why Ingested

This systematic review and meta-analysis provides a head-to-head comparison of CHX delivery forms (chip vs gel) against other local antimicrobials adjunctive to SRP, revealing that CHX chip outperforms alternatives for gingival inflammation while tetracycline/metronidazole/minocycline are superior for probing depth reduction. The differentiated outcomes by antimicrobial agent and clinical parameter make this directly actionable for periodontal treatment planning alongside [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/van-swaaij-2019-chx-ads-discoloration-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated the effectiveness of chlorhexidine chips and gels compared to other local antimicrobials after scaling and root planing in periodontitis patients, including 9 studies (8 RCTs with low risk of bias, 1 non-RCT with moderate risk). Chlorhexidine chips were less effective than other antimicrobials for probing depth at 1 month (MD = 0.58mm, p < 0.00001) and 3 months (MD = 0.50mm, p = 0.001), but more effective for gingival index at 3 months (MD = -0.11mm, p = 0.02). Chlorhexidine gel was less effective for plaque index at 1 month (MD = 0.20mm, p = 0.0008).

Chlorhexidine chips showed superior gingival index improvement, while other antimicrobials (tetracycline, metronidazole, minocycline) showed better probing depth reduction.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Chlorhexidine chips less effective than other antimicrobials for probing depth at 1 month (MD = 0.58mm, p < 0.00001).
- Chlorhexidine chips less effective than other antimicrobials for probing depth at 3 months (MD = 0.50mm, p = 0.001).
- Chlorhexidine chips more effective than other antimicrobials for gingival index at 3 months (MD = -0.11mm, p = 0.02).
- No difference in plaque index between chlorhexidine chips and other antimicrobials at 3 months (MD = 0.01, p = 0.94).
- Chlorhexidine gel showed no difference in probing depth (MD = 0.40mm, p = 0.30) or gingival index (MD = -0.04mm, p = 0.83) at 1 month.
- Chlorhexidine gel less effective than other antimicrobials for plaque index at 1 month (MD = 0.20mm, p = 0.0008).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review and meta-analysis of clinical trials.
- Databases: ProQuest, PubMed, EBSCO, ScienceDirect, Scopus. Searched for publications in last 10 years.
- PRISMA compliance: Yes (PRISMA guidelines).
- Inclusion criteria: Clinical trials on chlorhexidine vs other local antimicrobials (metronidazole, tetracycline, minocycline) after SRP in periodontitis patients. Probing depth ≥5mm. Follow-up ≥30 days (1 month). Full-text English publications.
- Excluded: SRs, meta-analyses, animal studies, case series, case reports, repeated antimicrobial administration, systemic disease, smoking participants.
- Included: 9 studies (8 RCTs, 1 non-RCT) from 1253 identified records.
- Risk of bias: RCTs assessed with RoB2 (8 studies low risk). Non-RCT assessed with ROBINS-I (1 study moderate risk).
- Data synthesis: Quantitative synthesis (meta-analysis) using RevMan 5.4. Mean difference with 95% CI. p < 0.05 significant.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **CHX chip vs other antimicrobials, PD at 1 month** — MD = 0.58mm (95% CI: 0.53 to 0.64, p < 0.00001)
- **CHX chip vs other antimicrobials, PD at 3 months** — MD = 0.50mm (95% CI: 0.20 to 0.80, p = 0.001)
- **CHX chip vs other antimicrobials, PI at 3 months** — MD = 0.01 (95% CI: -0.27 to 0.29, p = 0.94)
- **CHX chip vs other antimicrobials, GI at 3 months** — MD = -0.11mm (95% CI: -0.19 to -0.02, p = 0.02) [favours CHX chip]
- **CHX gel vs other antimicrobials, PD at 1 month** — MD = 0.40mm (95% CI: -0.36 to 1.15, p = 0.30)
- **CHX gel vs other antimicrobials, PI at 1 month** — MD = 0.20mm (95% CI: 0.08 to 0.32, p = 0.0008) [favours other]
- **CHX gel vs other antimicrobials, GI at 1 month** — MD = -0.04mm (95% CI: -0.41 to 0.33, p = 0.83)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Meta-analysis of gingival and plaque indices limited by insufficient uniform data.
- Different study results made it difficult to draw consistent conclusions.
- Limited number of journals prevented subgroup research between local antimicrobial types.
- Possible adverse effects (allergy, pain, discomfort, periodontal abscess) not evaluated.
- Only 5 studies included in quantitative synthesis (meta-analysis); 4 excluded due to missing mean and SD.
- Heterogeneity high for some outcomes (I² = 81-94%).

## 7. Glossary

- **SRP (Scaling and Root Planing)**: 스케일링 및 치근 활택술 — 비외과적 치주 치료의 금본위제
- **CHX Chip**: 클로르헥시딘 칩 — 2.5mg 클로르헥시딘 글루코네이트를 함유한 서방형 치주낭 내 삽입재
- **CHX Gel**: 클로르헥시딘 젤 — 1.5% 클로르헥시딘을 잔탄검 매트릭스에 함유한 젤 형태 국소 항균제
- **PD (Probing Depth)**: 치주낭 깊이 — 치주 질환 평가의 주요 임상 지표
- **PI (Plaque Index)**: 치태지수 — 치태 축적 정도를 평가하는 지수
- **GI (Gingival Index)**: 치은지수 — 치은 염증 정도를 평가하는 지수
- **MD (Mean Difference)**: 평균 차이 — 두 군 간 평균값 차이
- **RoB2 (Risk of Bias 2)**: Cochrane의 무작위대조시험 비뚤림 위험 평가 도구
- **ROBINS-I (Risk Of Bias In Non-randomized Studies of Interventions)**: 비무작위 중재 연구의 비뚤림 위험 평가 도구
