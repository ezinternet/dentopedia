## Why Ingested

Only systematic review combining post-surgical CA stability, efficiency (appointment burden, duration), and patient PRO (OHIP-14, OQLQ-22) in a single synthesis. Includes 2 RCTs. Directly bridges PRO and stability surveillance targets in the surgical subpopulation. → [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/alghafli-2026-postsurgical-aligners-sr]]

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (10 studies, n=258, 2 RCTs) of clear aligners in post-surgical orthodontic management. Skeletal/dental stability was comparable to fixed appliances with no significant short-term relapse; RCT evidence showed significantly better periodontal health (plaque index, BOP, probing depth), OHIP-14 (p=0.004), and OQLQ-22 (p=0.002) with clear aligners. Surgery-first protocols with CA reduced treatment duration by ~4.8 months vs traditional sequencing.

- **Scope**: 10 studies (2 RCTs, 6 retrospective/case-control, 2 case series), n=258, inception to October 2025

## 2. Key Findings

Stability:
- Li 2024 RCT (n=74, Class III SFA): no significant skeletal relapse CA vs FA at 6mo; SFA+CA 18.05mo vs 22.83mo (p<0.05)
- Moon 2021, Amodeo 2020: no significant relapse; Angle Class I maintained at 1 year

Efficiency:
- Meazzini 2024: significantly fewer pre-surgical appointments (5.1 vs 10.2; p<0.05)
- Overall: fewer impressions/refinements; similar or shorter total duration

Patient Outcomes (RCT, de Leyva 2023, n=28 SFA):
- OHIP-14: CA significantly better (p=0.004)
- OQLQ-22: CA significantly better (p=0.002)
- Periodontal: plaque index, BOP, probing depth all p<0.05 favor CA
- Treatment duration: equivalent (p=0.575)

## 3. Methodology

PRISMA guidelines; 4 databases (PubMed, ScienceDirect, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library). Search inception to October 2025. RoB 2.0 for RCTs; NOS for non-RCTs; JBI for case series. No meta-analysis (heterogeneity). Narrative synthesis.

## 4. Clinical Implications

Post-surgical CA is a validated option offering measurable quality-of-life advantages (OHIP-14, OQLQ-22) and superior periodontal hygiene outcomes vs fixed appliances. Surgery-first + CA is an efficient combination. Patient motivation/compliance remains essential (45–50% full adherence in general CA population).

## 5. Limitations

10 studies total, mostly small-scale retrospective; heterogeneous protocols (aligner brands, surgical procedures, retention strategies); predominantly 6–12 month follow-up (long-term stability unknown); no formal pooled meta-analysis.

## 7. Glossary

- **SFA**: Surgery-First Approach — orthognathic surgery performed before orthodontic decompensation
- **OQLQ-22**: Orthognathic Quality of Life Questionnaire (22 items) — validated PRO for surgical orthodontic patients
- **OFA**: Orthodontics-First Approach — traditional sequencing (pre-surgical orthodontics → surgery → post-surgical)
- **BOP**: Bleeding on Probing — periodontal inflammation marker
