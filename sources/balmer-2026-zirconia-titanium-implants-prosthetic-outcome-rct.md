## Why Ingested

First multicenter RCT reporting 1-year prosthetic outcomes of screw-retained all-ceramic crowns on two-piece ZrO₂ implants vs Ti implants, filling a key gap noted in [[wiki/implants/surface/shetty-2026-titanium-vs-zirconia-implants-umbrella]]: most ZrO₂ implant data cover osseointegration/MBL, with prosthetic/technical complication data sparse for two-piece ZrO₂.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective multicenter RCT (n=117 implants; 112 at 1-year FU) at University of Zurich, University of Basel, and Medical University of Graz; screw-retained all-ceramic single crowns on two-piece ZrO₂ implants (Ti-base connection) vs Ti implants (conventional Ti-base); diameter 4.1 mm.
Restoration survival 100% in both groups; no significant differences in overall technical complication rates or peri-implant soft tissue parameters (PI, PBI, PaI); veneered ZrO₂ crowns had significantly higher complication rates than monolithic restorations regardless of implant material.
At 1 year, ZrO₂ implants with Ti-base connection support comparable prosthetic outcomes to Ti implants; crown design (monolithic vs veneered) affects technical complications more than implant substrate.

## 세줄요약

전향적 다기관 무작위대조시험(n=117개 임플란트, 1년 완료 112개), 취리히대·바젤대·그라츠 의대; 2-피스 지르코니아 임플란트(Zirconia Implant, ZI; Ti-base 연결) vs Ti 임플란트(Titanium Implant, TI; 표준 Ti-base)에 나사유지형 전도재 단관 장착, 직경 4.1 mm.
보철 생존율(Restoration Survival) 양군 100%; 전체 기술적 합병증·임플란트 주위 연조직 지표(플라크 지수, 유두 출혈 지수(PBI), 유두 지수(PaI)) 군간 유의차 없음; 베니어 지르코니아 크라운은 임플란트 재료와 무관하게 단일체보다 유의하게 높은 합병증.
1년 시점 ZI는 TI와 동등한 보철 성과; 기술적 합병증은 임플란트 소재보다 크라운 디자인(베니어 vs 단일체) 차이가 더 큰 영향.

## Key Contributions

- Multicenter RCT confirming 100% restoration survival on ZrO₂ implants at 1 year, equivalent to Ti implants
- Crown design (monolithic vs veneered) is a stronger predictor of technical complications than implant material
- Peri-implant soft tissue health (PI, PBI, PaI) comparable between ZrO₂ and Ti implants at 1 year
- Study powered for MBL differences; prosthetic outcomes are a secondary endpoint
- Primary MBL data not reported in this abstract — prosthetic-specific reporting only

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective randomized multicenter RCT
- **Setting**: University of Zurich, University of Basel, Medical University of Graz
- **n**: 117 implants restored; 112 restorations at 1-year follow-up
- **Groups**: ZrO₂ implants with Ti-base connection vs Ti implants with conventional Ti-base; diameter 4.1 mm
- **Restoration**: Screw-retained, all-ceramic single crowns (monolithic or veneered)
- **Primary outcome**: Marginal bone loss (powered); secondary: prosthetic/technical outcomes reported here
- **Baseline**: 3 weeks after crown insertion
- **Statistics**: Descriptive statistics; Chi-square for group comparisons (p<0.05)
- **Registry**: DRKS00013209

- Primary MBL outcome data not available from this abstract (prosthetic sub-report only)

## Limitations

- 1-year follow-up only; long-term data needed for ZrO₂ implant durability assessment
- Abstract-only data; detailed complication types/rates not enumerated
- Powered for MBL, not prosthetic outcomes — complication analysis is secondary/exploratory
- Diameter restricted to 4.1 mm; generalizability to other diameters unknown
- No stratification reported for restoration type (monolithic vs veneered) in group assignments

## Glossary

- **ZI (Zirconia Implant / 지르코니아 임플란트)**: 지르코니아로 제작된 임플란트 픽스처; 금속 프리
- **Ti-base connection (티타늄 베이스 연결부)**: ZI에 금속 내식성 확보를 위해 추가한 Ti 연결 요소
- **PBI (Papilla Bleeding Index / 유두 출혈 지수)**: 치간 유두 출혈 정도 측정 지표
- **PaI (Papilla Index / 유두 지수)**: 치간 유두 충전도 측정 지표
