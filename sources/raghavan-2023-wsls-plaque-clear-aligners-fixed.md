## Three-line Summary

This SR+MA analyzed 14 studies (8 suitable for meta-analysis) comparing white spot lesions (WSLs), plaque accumulation, and salivary caries-associated bacteria (SCB) between clear aligners (CA) and fixed appliances (CF). CA showed significantly lower plaque accumulation (SMD −1.58; 95% CI: −2.57, −0.58; p = 0.002) and qualitatively lower WSL incidence, severity, and SCB levels compared to CF. Moderate-quality evidence suggests CA may be preferable for oral health, though high ROB and heterogeneity warrant caution.

## 세줄요약

투명교정(CA) 대 고정교정(CF)의 백색 병소(WSL), 플라크, 충치 관련 세균(SCB)을 비교한 SR+MA로, 14편 분석(8편 메타분석). CA군에서 플라크 축적이 유의하게 적었고(표준화 평균차 −1.58, 95% CI: −2.57~−0.58, p=0.002), WSL 발생률·심도·SCB도 qualitatively 낮음. 중등도 수준 근거이나 일부 포함 연구의 높은 편향 위험과 이질성으로 신중한 해석 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA to simultaneously compare WSLs, plaque accumulation, and SCB between CA and CF
- Demonstrated statistically significant reduction in plaque accumulation with CA (SMD −1.58)
- Provides moderate-quality evidence supporting CA for oral health preservation during orthodontic treatment

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, Scopus, Embase, Google Scholar, Clinical trial registry, OpenGrey, ProQuest
- **n**: 14 studies (8 in meta-analysis)
- **Outcomes**: WSL incidence/severity, plaque accumulation, SCB levels

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Plaque accumulation: SMD −1.58 (95% CI: −2.57 to −0.58; p = 0.002) favoring CA
- Qualitative synthesis: lower WSL incidence and severity in CA group
- SCB levels: qualitatively lower in CA group
- High heterogeneity across studies (I² not specified in abstract)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- High risk of bias among some included studies
- Marked heterogeneity across studies
- Certainty of evidence rated as moderate by GRADEpro
- Limited number of studies suitable for quantitative synthesis

## 7. Glossary
- **WSL (White Spot Lesion)**: Initial enamel demineralization appearing as chalky white opacity
- **SCB (Salivary Caries-Associated Bacteria)**: Streptococcus mutans and Lactobacillus species in saliva
- **CA (Clear Aligner)**: Removable transparent orthodontic appliance
- **SMD (Standardized Mean Difference)**: Effect size measure used in meta-analysis
