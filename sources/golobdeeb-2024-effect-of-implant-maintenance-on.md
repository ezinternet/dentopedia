## Why Ingested

Early-failure batch: a retrospective cohort that ties implant maintenance (PIMT) to peri-implantitis and peri-implantitis to early implant failure. Complements [[implants/survival/yari-2023-risk-factors-early-implant-failure]] and the synthesis in [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]]. → [[implants/survival/golobdeeb-2024-effect-of-implant-maintenance-on]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 501 implants in 185 patients (1-7 years of follow-up) testing whether periodontal implant maintenance therapy (PIMT) frequency relates to peri-implantitis and early implant failure.

Peri-implantitis occurred in 6% (29) and early failure in 5% (22) of implants; peri-implantitis was 9% (n = 20) without PIMT vs 4% (n = 10) with PIMT (p = .0169), PIMT was not associated with early failure (p = .4372), but 25% of implants with peri-implantitis failed early vs 4% without (p = .0062).

Absence of PIMT was associated with peri-implantitis, which was itself associated with early failure; the authors favour 6-month maintenance intervals, but this is abstract-only retrospective evidence with unstated failure-timing definitions.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 501개 (환자 185명)를 1-7년 추적한 후향적 코호트로, 치주 임플란트 유지관리 치료 (Periodontal Implant Maintenance Therapy, PIMT) 빈도가 임플란트 주위염과 조기 임플란트 실패와 관련되는지 분석.

임플란트 주위염 6% (29개), 조기 실패 5% (22개); 임플란트 주위염은 PIMT 없음 9% (n = 20) vs 있음 4% (n = 10) (p = .0169), PIMT와 조기 실패는 유의한 연관 없음 (p = .4372), 그러나 임플란트 주위염 임플란트의 25%가 조기 실패 vs 비임플란트주위염 4% (p = .0062).

PIMT 부재는 임플란트 주위염과, 임플란트 주위염은 조기 실패와 연관; 저자들은 6개월 간격 유지관리를 권고하나 초록 기반 후향적 근거이며 실패 시점 정의는 확인 불가.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Retrospective cohort study. [ABSTRACT-ONLY — full text not retrieved]

- **Population**: 185 patients; 501 implants; 1-7 years of follow-up.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Quantifies the maintenance (PIMT) association with peri-implantitis (9% vs 4%).
2. Shows peri-implantitis, not PIMT, associated with early failure (25% vs 4%).
3. Authors conclude 6-month maintenance intervals are important.

## 3. Methodology

Retrospective analysis of implant patients with 1-7 years of follow-up. Outcomes: peri-implantitis and early implant failure. Predictors: PIMT frequency, implant characteristics, demographics. Fisher exact tests, significance level .05. (Setting, definitions and PIMT categories not in the abstract.)

## 4. Key Results

- 501 implants / 185 patients; peri-implantitis 29 (6%); early failure 22 (5%).
- Peri-implantitis: 9% (n = 20) without PIMT vs 4% (n = 10) with PIMT; p = .0169.
- PIMT and early failure: p = .4372 (not significant).
- Early failure: 25% with peri-implantitis vs 4% without; p = .0062.

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract-only; failure-timing definition, setting, adjustment and effect sizes beyond percentages are not verifiable.
- Univariate Fisher tests as reported; confounding unaddressed in the abstract.
- "Early" failure within 1-7 years follow-up may differ from the pre-prosthetic definition used elsewhere in the wiki.

Recall at about 6-month intervals is associated with less peri-implantitis; peri-implantitis marks higher failure risk. The abstract does not show that maintenance reduces failure directly.
