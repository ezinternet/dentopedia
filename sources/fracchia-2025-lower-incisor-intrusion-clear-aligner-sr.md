## One-line Summary

SR, 8 studies: lower incisor intrusion with clear aligners achieves 0.4–1.5 mm with 35–65% predictability; 7/8 studies carry serious risk of bias and overall GRADE certainty is moderate-to-low.

## 한줄요약

## 2. Key Contributions

- Isolated analysis of lower incisor intrusion specifically (separated from global deep bite correction)
- Quantified predictability range: 35–65% across 8 studies
- Identified 4 key influencing factors: planned movement magnitude, auxiliaries (attachments/elastics), refinement strategies, patient-specific factors (age, compliance)
- Documented heterogeneity: CBCT vs cephalometry vs digital models prevent meta-analysis
- ROBINS-I + GRADE applied: 7/8 serious RoB, moderate-to-low certainty

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review (PRISMA)
- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Cochrane (5 databases)
- Population: permanent dentition, lower incisor intrusion with clear aligners
- Studies included: 8 clinical studies
- Risk of bias: ROBINS-I
- Evidence certainty: GRADE
- Outcomes: achieved vs planned intrusion (mm), predictability (%), influencing factors

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Mean intrusion range: 0.4–1.5 mm across studies
- Predictability range: 35–65%
- ROBINS-I: 7/8 studies = serious risk of bias
- GRADE certainty: moderate to low
- Influencing factors:
  - Planned movement magnitude (larger plans = lower accuracy)
  - Auxiliaries (attachments + elastics improve outcomes)
  - Refinement strategies (frequency impacts final result)
  - Patient-specific factors (age, compliance)
- Long-term outcomes and patient-centered measures: not adequately assessed

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No meta-analysis possible (heterogeneity in measurement methods and protocols)
- Serious risk of bias in 7/8 included studies
- Aligner change intervals and refinement frequency varied substantially
- No long-term stability or patient-centered outcome data
- Need standardized CBCT/digital model protocols and prospective RCTs

## 7. Glossary

- ROBINS-I: Risk Of Bias In Non-randomised Studies of Interventions
- Predictability: ratio of achieved to planned intrusion (%)
- CBCT: Cone Beam Computed Tomography
- Auxiliaries: attachments, elastics, interproximal reduction
