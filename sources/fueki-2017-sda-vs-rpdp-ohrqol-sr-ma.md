## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Shortened dental arch and prosthetic effect on oral health-related quality of life: a systematic review and meta-analysis
- **Authors**: K Fueki (Tokyo Medical and Dental University), K Baba (Showa University)
- **Journal**: Journal of Oral Rehabilitation, 2017;44(7):563–572
- **PMID**: 28370239 | **DOI**: 10.1111/joor.12511
- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis; MEDLINE + Cochrane Central; search up to November 2016

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to synthesise OHRQoL evidence for SDA vs RPDP AND SDA vs IFPDP (implant-supported fixed partial dental prosthesis).
- Demonstrates no statistically significant OHRQoL difference between SDA and RPDP at 6 or 12 months.
- Identifies critical evidence gap: only 1 non-RCT comparing SDA vs IFPDP — no definitive conclusion possible.
- Provides standardised mean difference (SWMD) effect sizes for meta-analytic use.

## 3. Methodology

- **Search**: MEDLINE + Cochrane Central, 1980–November 2016; 2 independent reviewers
- **Inclusion**: RCTs and non-RCTs comparing SDA vs RPDP or IFPDP; OHRQoL as outcome via validated questionnaire
- **Outcome instrument**: All included studies used OHIP (Oral Health Impact Profile)
- **Statistics**: Random effects model; standardised weighted mean difference (SWMD)
- **Included studies**: 2 RCTs + 1 non-RCT; N = 516 participants total

## 4. Key Results

### SDA vs RPDP (2 RCTs — pooled meta-analysis)

| Timepoint | SWMD | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 6 months | 0.24 | Not significant (SDA slightly worse) |
| 12 months | 0.40 | Not significant (SDA slightly worse) |

**Conclusion: No statistically significant OHRQoL difference between SDA and RPDP at 6 or 12 months.**

### SDA vs IFPDP (1 non-RCT only — narrative only, not pooled)

| Timepoint | SWMD | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 6 months | −0.59 | Not significant (IFPDP slightly better) |
| 12 months | −0.67 | Not significant (IFPDP slightly better) |

**Conclusion: Small sample; no significant difference; IFPDP trend toward better OHRQoL but insufficient evidence.**

## 5. Limitations

- Only 2 RCTs and 1 non-RCT met inclusion criteria — small evidence base
- Follow-up limited to 12 months maximum — no long-term OHRQoL data
- Excludes Walter 2021 (published after 2016 search cutoff)
- OHRQoL only — no functional outcomes (masticatory efficiency, survival rates)
- IFPDP comparison severely underpowered (n=1 non-RCT)
