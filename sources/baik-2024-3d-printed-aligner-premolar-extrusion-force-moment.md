## Why Ingested

[[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/nucera-2022-composite-attachments-clear-aligners-sr]]는 "extrusion에 대한 attachment 효과는 연구가 아예 없다"고 명시적으로 근거 공백을 지적했다. 본 연구(Baik 2024)는 force/moment transducer + DIC(digital image correlation)로 하악 소구치 정출을 직접 계측해 그 공백을 메우는 벤치 연구다. 또한 margin 연장·협설폭 축소가 정출 효율을 좌우한다는 결과는 [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/nakornnoi-2024-aligner-trimline-biomechanics-tooth-movement-sr]]의 trimline 기전(straight/extended margin → 큰 force/moment·유지력)을 개별 실험 데이터로 뒷받침한다. 위키 clear-aligner 카테고리에 52편이 있으나 정출(extrusion)이라는 이동유형을 직접 계측한 in-vitro 연구는 이번이 첫 사례다.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro bench study using a modified force/moment transducer device (Grant et al. design) and digital image correlation (DIC) on 3D-printed clear aligners, testing 8 design combinations (4 margin designs × attachment presence/absence, n=15/group) for mandibular first premolar extrusion.

With attachment, extended margin (Group 2, median +0.96 N) and reduced buccolingual margin width (Group 4, +0.46 N) delivered adequate extrusion force at the first premolar, while thickened margin (Group 3) produced significantly lower extrusion force; without attachment, all groups paradoxically generated an intrusive force instead of the planned extrusion.

Attachment presence is a key requirement for extrusion — without it the aligner's force system is unpredictable and can act opposite to the planned direction; margin design (extension, buccolingual width reduction) independently modulates extrusion efficiency, but findings are bench-only (mechanical device, no PDL/root/biological load) and not yet clinically validated.

## 세줄요약

3D프린팅 투명교정장치(Clear Aligner, CA)에 대해 개조된 force/moment transducer 장치(Grant 등 디자인)와 디지털이미지상관법(Digital Image Correlation, DIC)을 이용, 하악 제1소구치 정출을 대상으로 8개 디자인 조합(margin 4종 × attachment 유무, 그룹당 n=15)을 비교한 in-vitro 벤치 연구.

Attachment 있는 경우 margin 연장(Group 2, 중앙값 +0.96N)·협설폭 1mm 축소(Group 4, +0.46N)가 적절한 정출력을 발현했고, margin 두께 증가(Group 3)는 유의하게 낮은 정출력을 보임; attachment 없는 경우 모든 그룹에서 계획과 반대로 함입력(intrusion force)이 발생.

Attachment 존재가 정출의 핵심 조건이며 없으면 힘 체계가 예측 불가능하고 계획과 반대 방향으로 작용할 수 있음; margin 디자인(연장·협설폭 축소)이 attachment와 독립적으로 정출 효율을 조절하나, 결과는 기계적 장치 기반 벤치 연구(치주인대·치근·생물학적 하중 미반영)로 임상 검증은 아직 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First direct force/moment measurement of mandibular premolar **extrusion** with 3D-printed clear aligners across 8 systematic design combinations (margin geometry × attachment).
- Combines a mechanical force/moment transducer system (modified from Grant et al.) with digital image correlation (DIC) to simultaneously quantify (a) the force/moment delivered to teeth and (b) the aligner's own surface displacement/strain — linking cause (aligner deformation) to effect (force transmission).
- Directly tests three under-studied design levers for extrusion: attachment presence, margin extension (subgingival 1 mm), margin thickening, and buccolingual margin width reduction.
- Identifies attachment as the dominant factor for extrusion — without it, aligners generate an intrusive (opposite-direction) force rather than extrusion, regardless of margin design.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Model**: Nissin mandibular dental model, 3D scanned and CAD-modeled (Spaceclaim); a 1 mm intruded mandibular first premolar (#34) was simulated, and a 0.5 mm extrusion force plan was applied via the aligner.
- **Aligner fabrication**: Direct 3D printing (TC-85 resin, Graphy, Seoul) at 0.5 mm thickness — not thermoformed. Two attachment conditions (dome-shaped attachment 3 mm height × 2 mm width × 1 mm prominence, vs none) × four margin designs:
  - Group 1: equi-margin, gingival scalloped
  - Group 2: extended margin, 1 mm subgingival scalloped
  - Group 3: equi-margin, 1 mm thickened at gingival margin
  - Group 4: equi-margin, 1 mm reduced buccolingual width at margin (0.5 mm buccal + 0.5 mm lingual reduction)
- **Force/moment measurement**: Multi-axis force/moment transducers (AFT20-D15, Aidin Robotics) mounted at canine, first premolar, and second premolar positions (#33/#34/#35); measured on x/y/z axes 1 hour after aligner seating, in a 37°C forced-convection incubator to simulate intraoral conditions. n=15 replicate experiments/group.
- **DIC**: Speckle-pattern-coated aligners imaged with two high-resolution VIC-3D cameras (Correlated Solutions) to map surface displacement/strain before vs after loading.
- **Statistics**: Non-normal data (Kruskal–Wallis + Bonferroni post-hoc), median/IQR reporting, SPSS v29, α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**With attachment (first premolar):**
- Extrusion force (Fz) significantly lower in Group 3 (thickened margin) than other groups; highest in Groups 2, 4, 1, 3 (descending order per text).
- Group 2 (extended margin) delivered the largest extrusion force overall (median +0.96 N); Group 4 (reduced buccolingual width) +0.46 N — both interpreted as adequate for planned extrusion.
- Canine: consistent distal force across groups; Group 4 uniquely generated a buccal force and near-zero intrusion force (median +0.05 N vs −0.68 to −0.12 N in other groups).
- Moments: buccal inclination at the first premolar in all groups; Group 4 alone showed distal angulation; rotation moments relatively larger (distal) in Groups 2 and 4.

**Without attachment (first premolar):**
- Contrary to the treatment plan, **all groups produced an intrusive force** instead of extrusion; Groups 3 and 4 showed weaker intrusive force than Group 1.
- Second premolar: distalizing force in all groups except Group 4; intrusive force in all groups.

**DIC displacement/strain:**
- With attachment, displacement concentrated around the attachment (most prominent in Groups 1 and 4); strain consistently occurred at the aligner's bottom (gingival) margin across groups.
- Without attachment, displacement was more diffuse across canine/premolars; Group 4 showed a distinctly higher proximal-area displacement pattern.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Mechanical bench system only — no periodontal ligament, root, or other biological/anatomical load-bearing structures incorporated; the authors explicitly note the system cannot fully replicate the oral environment.
- DIC captures only one (buccal) surface of a single-material viscoelastic aligner; changes elsewhere on the aligner are not directly visualized.
- No formal reliability/ICC (intraclass correlation coefficient) assessment of the force/moment sensors was performed — relied on manufacturer-provided calibration specs.
- Direct 3D-printed aligner material (TC-85) only; no comparison against conventional thermoformed aligners in the same design matrix (authors flag this as a needed follow-up).
- Single tooth position (mandibular first premolar) and single simulated malposition (1 mm intrusion) — generalizability to other movement magnitudes/teeth untested.
- In-vitro/bench evidence only; no clinical outcome or patient-level validation.

## 6. Related Work

- Grant et al. — original force/moment transducer system this study's device modifies.
- Savignano et al. (FEA) — rectangular attachments improve maxillary incisor extrusion efficiency via increased contact area; movement not achievable without attachment (echoed here for mandibular premolar).
- Rossini et al. — horizontal attachment shape proposed as most effective for extrusion (contrasts with this study's dome-shaped attachment).
- Yao et al. (FEM) — internal aligner embossment improves cervical force application for extrusion but causes uncontrolled torque/tilting; parallels this study's Group 4 (reduced buccolingual width → minimal Mx moment, interpreted as improved lingual-side support).
- [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/nucera-2022-composite-attachments-clear-aligners-sr]] — SR explicitly flags "no studies examined extrusion" for composite attachments; this paper is direct bench evidence filling that gap.
- [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/nakornnoi-2024-aligner-trimline-biomechanics-tooth-movement-sr]] — SR on trimline/margin design as a force-delivery determinant; this study's margin-extension/width-reduction results are individual-experiment support for that mechanism.
- [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/cho-2025-clear-aligner-material-force-moment-six-axis-sensor]] — same journal (KJO), analogous force/moment transducer bench methodology, different movement type (labial tipping) and different material comparison.

## 7. Glossary

- **CA (Clear Aligner)**: 투명교정장치.
- **DIC (Digital Image Correlation)**: 디지털이미지상관법 — 하중 전후 비접촉 광학 이미지 비교로 재료 표면의 변위·변형을 측정하는 기법.
- **Fx/Fy/Fz**: 근원심(mesiodistal)/협설(buccolingual)/정출-함입(extrusion-intrusion) 방향 힘.
- **Mx/My/Mz**: x/y/z축(협설경사·근원심경사·회전) 모멘트.
- **Margin (trimline)**: 아라이너 치은연 경계 디자인 — equi-margin(치은변연과 동일선), extended margin(치은연하 연장), scalloped(치은연 굴곡을 따름).
- **Attachment**: 교정력 발현을 위해 치아 표면에 부착하는 컴포지트 돌출부(본 연구는 dome형).
