## Why Ingested
User asked whether an enamel bevel is worth placing in cervical (Class V / NCCL) composite restorations. This 3-year RCT is an early randomized test of beveling and adds a time-dependent nuance to the "bevel had no influence" pooled finding in [[resin-bonding/mahn-2015-bonding-parameters-cervical-restorations-ma]] (benefit confined to the first 6 months), and it bears on the bevel step in the guideline-type recommendation of [[nccl/goodacre-2022-nccl-morphology-etiology-restoration-guidelines]] and the bevel-containing protocol of [[resin-bonding/luhrs-2020-composite-restorations-nccl-cavity-preparation]].

## Three-line Summary
Randomized clinical trial (Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina, Brazil): 105 cervical non-carious defects (buccal surfaces of canines and premolars) split into three groups of 35 - no enamel bevel + microfilled composite (Durafill VS), enamel bevel + microfilled composite, enamel bevel + flowable composite (Natural Flow) - all with a total-etch one-bottle adhesive (One-Step), followed for 3 years.
At 6 months beveled margins gave 100% retention regardless of composite versus 66% for non-beveled margins; at 2 and 3 years retention did not differ significantly among the three groups, and sensitivity, marginal discoloration and secondary caries were unaffected by beveling or composite viscosity.
Enamel beveling improved retention only transiently (first 6 months) and neither bevel nor flowable-vs-microfilled viscosity changed 3-year outcomes in these non-retentive Class V defects; abstract-only record (no group-level data beyond the abstract, no bias assessment), so the early-retention signal cannot be appraised further.

6개월 시점에 사면을 준 군은 복합레진 종류와 무관하게 유지율 (Retention Rate) 100%, 사면 없는 군은 66%; 2년·3년에는 세 군 간 유지율에 유의차 없었고 술후 지각과민·변연 변색·이차 우식도 사면과 복합레진 종류에 영향받지 않음.
법랑질 사면의 유지력 이점은 초기 6개월에 한정된 일시적 효과였고, 사면 여부와 복합레진 점도(유동성 vs 미세충전) 모두 3년 성적을 바꾸지 못함 (비유지형 Class V 결손); 초록만 확보되어 초기 유지율 신호를 더 검증할 수 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Randomized comparison of enamel beveling vs no bevel (and flowable vs microfilled composite) in Class V NCCL restorations with a 3-year follow-up.
- Shows an early (6-month) retention advantage of beveled margins that disappears by 2-3 years.
- Finds no effect of beveling or composite viscosity on sensitivity, marginal discoloration or secondary caries.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial, three parallel groups (randomization unit: defect/lesion); 3-year follow-up.
- **n**: 105 cervical non-carious defects on buccal surfaces of canines and premolars (35 per group); patient count and drop-out not reported in the abstract.
- **Groups**: (1) no enamel bevel + microfilled composite (Durafill VS); (2) enamel bevel + microfilled composite; (3) enamel bevel + flowable composite (Natural Flow). Total-etch one-bottle adhesive (One-Step) in all groups.
- **Outcomes**: retention rate, pre- and post-operative sensitivity, marginal discoloration, secondary caries; statistical analysis performed (test not named in the abstract).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: no per-group retention at 2-3 years, no confidence intervals, no dropout, no randomization/blinding details, and the statistical tests are not named.
- 35 lesions per group is small; lack of a significant difference at 2-3 years is not proof of equivalence.
- The early 66% retention in non-beveled margins is a strikingly low figure that the abstract does not explain (e.g., operator, isolation, or adhesive-related); it cannot be interpreted further without the full text.
- Single microfilled composite, single flowable and single one-bottle total-etch adhesive of that era; generalization to current universal adhesives is uncertain.

## 7. Glossary
- **Enamel bevel**: oblique finishing of the enamel cavosurface margin to enlarge the etchable enamel area and improve marginal seal/retention.
- **Class V non-retentive defect**: cervical cavity with no mechanical undercuts, relying on adhesion alone for retention.
- **Flowable composite**: low-viscosity, low-elastic-modulus resin composite, proposed as a stress-absorbing liner/restorative in cervical lesions.
- **Microfilled composite**: composite with colloidal silica fillers, lower modulus and good polish, traditionally used in cervical lesions.
- **Total-etch (etch-and-rinse) adhesive**: adhesive applied after phosphoric acid etching of enamel and dentin.
