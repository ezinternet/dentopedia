# Evaluating the advancements in a recently introduced universal adhesive compared to its predecessor

## Why Ingested

Scotchbond Universal Plus (SP) is a widely adopted universal adhesive in clinical practice; this is the first in vitro study to quantify the OIL thickness reduction between the original Scotchbond Universal (SB, OIL 35.4 µm) and the upgraded SP (OIL 12.2 µm) alongside a full physicochemical profile. Directly extends the OIL-bond-strength evidence base established by [[resin-bonding/patangia-2025-effect-oxygen-inhibited-layer-dental]].

→ [[resin-bonding/alam-2023-evaluating-advancements-recently-introduced]]

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (n=24 molars, 4 groups of 6) comparing Scotchbond Universal Plus (SP) to its predecessor (SB) in self-etch and etch-and-rinse modes on dentin microtensile bond strength, OIL, viscosity, hardness, elastic modulus, and degree of conversion.
SP showed significantly thinner OIL (12.2 ± 1.4 µm vs 35.4 ± 4.3 µm, p<0.001), lower viscosity (50.2 vs 115.5 mPa·s), higher hardness (329.6 vs 292.8 MPa), higher elastic modulus (7.7 vs 6.7 GPa), and higher degree of conversion (92.6% vs 81.4%); microtensile bond strength and survival probability were equivalent between adhesives.
Upgraded physicochemical properties of SP do not translate into improved dentin bond strength or failure pattern vs SB, suggesting OIL reduction alone does not drive bond strength in this generation of universal adhesives.

## 세줄요약

생체외 연구 (n=24 대구치, 4군, 군당 6개)로 스카치본드 유니버설 플러스(Scotchbond Universal Plus, SP)를 전임자(SB)와 자가산부식(Self-Etch, SE)·전산부식(Etch-and-Rinse, ER) 모드 하 미세인장결합강도(Microtensile Bond Strength, µTBS), 산소억제층(Oxygen-Inhibited Layer, OIL), 점도, 경도, 탄성계수, 전환율(Degree of Conversion, DC) 비교.
SP는 OIL 현저 감소(12.2 ± 1.4 µm vs 35.4 ± 4.3 µm, p<0.001), 저점도(50.2 vs 115.5 mPa·s), 높은 경도(329.6 vs 292.8 MPa), 높은 탄성계수(7.7 vs 6.7 GPa), 높은 전환율(92.6% vs 81.4%) 보임; µTBS 및 생존확률은 두 접착제 간 동등.
SP의 물리화학적 개선이 상아질 결합강도 향상으로 이어지지 않아 OIL 감소 단독으로는 이 세대 유니버설 접착제의 결합강도를 결정하지 않음을 시사.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First head-to-head physicochemical comparison of Scotchbond Universal Plus vs Scotchbond Universal under identical dentin bonding conditions
- Quantifies OIL thickness reduction: SP 12.2 ± 1.4 µm vs SB 35.4 ± 4.3 µm (65% thinner, p<0.001)
- Demonstrates that improved DC (92.6% vs 81.4%) and in-situ DC (90.1% vs 86.4%) in SP do not produce statistically superior µTBS
- Thinner adhesive layer in SP (ER: 2.9 µm vs SB 6.1 µm; SE: 6.1 µm vs SB 10.6 µm) confirmed by SEM
- Proposes that thinner adhesive layer is more susceptible to OIL-driven polymerization inhibition, partially explaining the unchanged µTBS despite higher DC

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Teeth:** 24 non-carious extracted human molars, mid-coronal dentin, stored in deionized water
**Groups (n=6/group):**
- SB-SE: Scotchbond Universal, self-etch mode
- SB-ER: Scotchbond Universal, etch-and-rinse (37% phosphoric acid 15 s)
- SP-SE: Scotchbond Universal Plus, self-etch mode
- SP-ER: Scotchbond Universal Plus, etch-and-rinse mode
**Primary outcome:** Microtensile bond strength (µTBS, MPa) — stick specimens ≈1 mm², tested at 0.5 mm/min
**Failure analysis:** SEM classification (adhesive / mixed / cohesive)
**OIL measurement:** Cross-sectional SEM after air curing (not under matrix strip)
**Viscosity:** Rotary rheometer
**Hardness / Elastic modulus:** Nanoindentation
**Degree of conversion (DC):** Raman spectroscopy (at-cure, ex situ)
**In-situ DC:** Near-infrared (NIR) spectroscopy during curing
**Survival analysis:** Kaplan-Meier (Breslow test)
**Statistics:** ANOVA / Kruskal-Wallis; p<0.05 threshold

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small n per group (n=6) — limited statistical power for µTBS differences
- No long-term water storage evaluation (no aging protocol)
- Only dentin substrate tested; no enamel comparison
- No alternative adhesive systems compared (no non-Scotchbond control)
- OIL measurement without matrix strip — simulates clinical scenario but not worst-case oxygen exposure
- In vitro only: clinical translation requires caution

## 7. Glossary

- **OIL (Oxygen-Inhibited Layer)**: Incompletely polymerized gel-like surface layer formed when atmospheric O₂ quenches free radicals during photopolymerization of adhesive resins
- **µTBS (Microtensile Bond Strength)**: Bond strength test on ~1 mm² sticks; higher spatial resolution than macro-shear tests
- **DC (Degree of Conversion)**: Fraction of C=C double bonds converted to C-C during polymerization; measured by Raman spectroscopy
- **In-situ DC**: DC measured in real time during curing with NIR spectroscopy
- **SB**: Scotchbond Universal (3M); predecessor universal adhesive
- **SP**: Scotchbond Universal Plus (3M); upgraded universal adhesive with modified monomer composition and photoinitiators
