## 7. Glossary
- **Full-Thickness Flap (Mucoperiosteal Flap)**: flap elevated including the periosteum, exposing the underlying alveolar bone.
- **Partial-Thickness Flap (Split-Thickness Flap)**: flap elevated without periosteum, leaving the periosteal layer attached to the bone.
- **Alveolar Crest Reduction**: decrease in the height and/or width of the alveolar crest bone; the outcome named in this paper's title.
- **Periosteal Blood Supply (골막 혈행)**: vascular supply carried by the periosteum to the underlying alveolar bone — the proposed mechanism linking flap thickness to crestal bone change.
