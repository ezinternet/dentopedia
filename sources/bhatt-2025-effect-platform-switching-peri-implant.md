## Why Ingested

Platform switching (PS) 관련 최신(2025) 근거 강화를 위해 PMC 서베일런스로 인제스트. [[wiki/implants/mbl/juan-montesinos-2022-platform-switching-conventional-sr-ma]] (2022 SR+MA, MD 0.255mm)가 확립한 PS의 MBL 이점을 하나의 새 RCT로 재확인·보강하는 논문.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT (60 patients, single posterior implant, 30 PS vs 30 platform-matching (PM), 12-month follow-up) comparing platform switching to platform matching on crestal bone loss (CBL) and clinical parameters.

At 12 months, mean CBL was 0.32 ± 0.15 mm in the PS group vs 0.81 ± 0.22 mm in the PM group (p < 0.001); probing depth and bleeding on probing trended lower with PS but did not reach significance.

Confirms PS as a biomechanical strategy for peri-implant bone preservation over a 12-month window; short duration and single-center design limit generalizability.

## 세줄요약

전향적 RCT(단일 구치부 임플란트 60명, PS 30 vs 일반형(PM) 30, 12개월 추적) — 골-임플란트 접합부 디자인 변경(Platform Switching, PS)이 치조정골소실(Crestal Bone Loss, CBL) 및 임상지표에 미치는 영향 비교.

12개월 시점 CBL 평균 PS군 0.32 ± 0.15mm vs PM군 0.81 ± 0.22mm(p<0.001); 탐침깊이(Probing Depth, PD)·탐침시출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP)도 PS군에서 낮은 경향이나 통계적 유의성은 없음.

PS가 12개월 시점 치조정골 보존에 유리한 보철학적 전략임을 재확인; 짧은 관찰기간·단일기관 설계는 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Prospective RCT design directly comparing PS vs PM at 12 months in single posterior implants (fills the gap the authors note: "few prospective clinical trials have directly compared PS and PM over extended periods with standardized methodologies").
- Quantifies CBL trajectory at two time points (6mo, 12mo), showing the PS-vs-PM gap widens with time (6mo: 0.19 vs 0.54mm; 12mo: 0.32 vs 0.81mm).
- Reports secondary soft-tissue parameters (PD, PI, BOP) alongside CBL, all trending in favor of PS though not statistically significant.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective, randomized controlled clinical trial, 18-month study period, Department of Periodontology and Implant Dentistry.
- **Sample size**: 60 patients (30 PS, 30 PM), power calculation α=0.05, β=0.20, minimum detectable difference 0.3mm.
- **Inclusion**: age 20–60, systemically healthy, single posterior missing tooth, adequate bone volume without grafting.
- **Exclusion**: heavy smokers (>10 cig/day), history of periodontitis, bruxism/parafunction, bisphosphonate/steroid therapy, uncontrolled systemic disease.
- **Implants**: titanium screw-type, 4.5mm diameter × 10mm length. PS group: 3.5mm abutment on 4.5mm platform. PM group: 4.5mm abutment (matched). Single surgeon, flapless approach when feasible.
- **Timeline**: healing abutments at 3 months, final restoration at 4 months post-placement.
- **Measurement**: standardized periapical radiographs (paralleling technique) at baseline, 6, 12 months; marginal bone level from implant shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact (mesial + distal), measured with ImageJ. Clinical parameters (PD, PI, BOP) via UNC-15 probe at 6 and 12 months.
- **Statistics**: SPSS v25.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Time point | PS group CBL (mm) | PM group CBL (mm) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 months | 0.19 ± 0.10 | 0.54 ± 0.16 | <0.001 |
| 12 months | 0.32 ± 0.15 | 0.81 ± 0.22 | <0.001 |

- 12-month PD: PS 2.5 ± 0.6mm vs PM 2.9 ± 0.7mm (p=0.08, NS).
- 12-month BOP: PS 10% vs PM 17% (p=0.21, NS).
- PI scores comparable across groups.
- Complications: 2 patients in the PM group showed early mucositis; no implant failures in either group.
- Discussion frames the 0.49mm 12-month CBL difference as clinically relevant (annual CBL >0.2mm after year 1 can compromise long-term prognosis), and notes consistency with the Atieh and Strietzel systematic reviews (~0.3–0.5mm reduction with PS).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small, single-center sample (n=60); short 12-month follow-up.
- PD/BOP differences did not reach significance, plausibly due to sample size/duration — authors flag this as needing longer confirmation.
- No blinding described for radiographic measurement; standard single-surgeon design limits generalizability across operators/systems.

## 6. Related Work

- [[wiki/implants/mbl/juan-montesinos-2022-platform-switching-conventional-sr-ma]] — 2022 SR+MA (9 studies): PS MD 0.255mm less MBL — this RCT's 0.49mm 12-month gap is directionally consistent and larger in magnitude.
- [[wiki/implants/mbl/messias-2019-platform-switching-mbl-5year-rct]] — 5-year equivalence RCT: PS 0.23mm lower MBL at 5 years — this trial's 12-month 0.49mm gap suggests the PS advantage may be front-loaded in year 1.
- [[wiki/implants/mbl/rocha-2016-platform-switching-crestal-bone-3year-rct]] — 3-year multicenter RCT: PS 0.28mm vs PM 0.68mm at 3yr (p=0.002) — comparable direction and similar-magnitude gap.
- [[wiki/implants/mbl/strietzel-2015-platform-switching-mbl-sr-ma]] — earlier SR+MA: PS 0.49mm vs PM 1.01mm — this trial's absolute PM-group CBL (0.81mm at 12mo) sits within that range.

## 7. Glossary

- **Platform Switching (PS)**: using an abutment narrower than the implant platform diameter, shifting the implant-abutment microgap inward and away from the crestal bone.
- **Crestal Bone Loss (CBL)**: vertical bone resorption measured from the implant shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact.
- **Probing Depth (PD)**: distance from the mucosal margin to the base of the peri-implant sulcus, measured with a periodontal probe.
- **Bleeding on Probing (BOP)**: presence of bleeding following gentle probing, a marker of peri-implant mucosal inflammation.
- **Plaque Index (PI)**: clinical index quantifying visible plaque accumulation.
