## Why Ingested
Provides a 6-year RCT comparing immediate (≤2 days) vs delayed (3 months) loading for single-tooth mandibular first molar implants — a longer follow-up than most loading-time trials. Extends [[implants/loading-protocol/esposito-2013-loading-times-dental-implants-cochrane]] (Cochrane SR+MA with 1-year data) with single-tooth, posterior-mandible, 72-month outcomes.

## Three-line Summary
Randomized prospective trial (n=20 patients, 20 implants) comparing immediate loading within 2 days vs delayed loading at 3 months for single-tooth mandibular first molar replacements, with 72-month follow-up.
Both groups showed 100% implant survival, no peri-implant radiolucency, and minimal marginal bone loss (0.1–0.15 mm at 6 months); probing depth decreased significantly in the immediate group (1.8→1.2 mm, p=0.0277) but not in the delayed group.
Immediate loading is equally effective to delayed loading for mandibular first molar single-tooth implants, offering reduced treatment time and early function with equivalent long-term clinical and radiographic outcomes.

## 세줄요약
무작위 전향적 연구 (n=20명, 20 임플란트) — 하악 제1대구치 단일 치아 결손에 즉시 부하(≤2일) vs 지연 부하(3개월) 비교, 72개월 추적관찰.
두 군 모두 100% 생존율, 주위임플란트 방사선투과도 없음, 최소 변연골 소실 (6개월째 0.1–0.15 mm); 즉시 부하군에서 탐침 깊이 유의하게 감소 (1.8→1.2 mm, p=0.0277), 지연 부하군은 변화 없음.
하악 제1대구치 단일 치아 임플란트에서 즉시 부하는 지연 부하와 동등한 장기 임상·방사선학적 결과를 보이며, 치료 기간 단축과 조기 기능 회복의 이점 제공.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 6-year follow-up for immediate vs delayed loading in single-tooth mandibular first molar implants — longer than most existing trials (typically 1–4 years)
- Randomized design with blinded outcome assessment by a periodontist
- Comprehensive evaluation: bleeding on probing, probing depth, implant mobility (Periotest), marginal bone level, peri-implant radiolucency
- Both flapless (immediate group) and flapped (delayed group) surgical approaches represented

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized prospective clinical trial (parallel-group)
- **Databases**: Not applicable (primary clinical study)
- **n**: 20 patients (14 male, 6 female), age 19–31 years (mean 23.1), 20 implants (10 per group)
- **Outcomes**: Primary — implant survival, marginal bone loss, probing depth, bleeding on probing, implant mobility (Periotest), peri-implant radiolucency; assessed at 1, 6, 12, 36, 72 months post-loading

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=10 per group) limits statistical power and generalizability
- Implant sizes varied (diameter 3.3–4.5 mm, length 10–13 mm); implant dimension effect not analyzed
- Bone quality/type (D1–D4) not considered as a variable
- Flapless vs flapped surgical technique confounded with loading protocol
- Single-center study in Indian population; multi-center long-term studies needed

## 6. Related Work
- **Esposito 2013 (Cochrane SR+MA)**: No significant difference between immediate and conventional loading at 1 year; this trial extends to 6 years with single-tooth posterior focus.
- **Salvi 2004 (RCT)**: Early loading (2/6 weeks) of SLA implants in posterior mandible — 1-year data, similar findings.
- **Heydenrijk 2002 (Prospective)**: Immediate vs delayed loading of two-part implants — probing depth decrease in immediate group, consistent with this study.

## 7. Glossary
- **즉시 부하 (Immediate Loading)**: 임플란트 식립 후 1주일 이내에 임시 또는 영구 보철물을 연결하여 교합 하중을 가하는 것
- **지연 부하 (Delayed Loading)**: 임플란트 식립 후 골유착 기간(통상 3–6개월)을 거친 후 보철물을 연결하는 것
- **변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)**: 임플란트 주위 치조골의 가장자리(마진)에서 발생하는 골 흡수, 임플란트 성공 판단의 주요 지표
- **주위임플란트 방사선투과도 (Peri-implant Radiolucency)**: 임플란트 주위 뼈에서 방사선학적으로 나타나는 투명한 영역, 골 소실 또는 염증의 징후
- **페리오테스트 (Periotest, PT)**: 임플란트 동요도를 정량적으로 측정하는 장치, 음수 값이 높을수록 안정적임을 의미
- **탐침 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD)**: 치은 마진에서 치주낭/주위임플란트낭 바닥까지의 거리, 주위임플란트 건강 평가 지표
- **출혈 탐침 (Bleeding on Probing, BoP)**: 탐침 시 출혈 여부, 주위임플란트 염증의 가장 민감한 지표
