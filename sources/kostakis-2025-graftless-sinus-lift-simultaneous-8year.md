## Why Ingested
Graftless 상악동거상술 장기 근거로 인제스트. 기존 [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/fettouh-2025-graftless-lateral-transcrestal-sinus-rct]]은 1년 RCT였는데, 이 논문은 8년 추적 전향적 코호트로 graftless의 97.76% 임플란트 성공률을 제시하며 장기 데이터의 공백을 채운다.

## One-line Summary
8-year prospective cohort (n=74 graftless + 15 grafted): graftless simultaneous sinus lift achieved 97.76% implant success rate, outperforming the grafted control group (86.6%); residual bone height was the key predictor across both groups.

## 한줄요약
8년 전향적 코호트(n=89): 골이식 없는 상악동거상술 동시 임플란트 성공률 97.76%로 골이식군(86.6%) 초과; 잔존골 높이가 두 군 모두에서 유의한 성공 예측 인자.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Longest prospective cohort (≥8 years / ≥60-month minimum follow-up) for graftless simultaneous sinus lift
- Demonstrates graftless approach superiority in implant success over grafted control (97.76% vs 86.6%)
- Establishes residual bone height as statistically significant predictor of success in both graftless and grafted groups
- Provides high-volume graftless data (n=74) making it one of the largest single-center series

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Prospective cohort
- Setting: Single center, 8-year period
- Patients: 89 total — 74 graftless (test) + 15 grafted (control)
- Indication: Posterior maxilla requiring sinus lift + simultaneous implant placement
- Follow-up: Minimum 60 months
- Key variable analyzed: Residual bone height as predictor of success
- Statistical analysis: Not detailed in abstract; significance at P<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Graftless group implant success rate: 97.76%** (74 patients)
- **Grafted control group implant success rate: 86.6%** (15 patients)
- Residual bone height: statistically significant factor influencing implant success in both groups (P<0.05)
- Graftless approach demonstrated superior success rate compared to grafted control
- No specific ISQ or bone gain values reported in abstract

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Non-randomized design: grafted control group small (n=15), limiting statistical power for comparison
- Single center (Athens, Greece) — generalizability requires caution
- Implant success vs survival criteria not fully detailed in abstract
- Absence of graft group randomization makes direct comparison subject to selection bias
- Future: RCT with larger grafted control group; stratified analysis by residual bone height subgroups

## 7. Glossary
- **Graftless sinus lift**: Schneiderian membrane elevation without bone graft material; relies on blood clot organization and tenting effect
- **Simultaneous implant placement**: Implant placed at time of sinus membrane elevation (one-stage approach)
- **Implant success rate**: Composite of survival + absence of complications/pathology (stricter than survival rate)

## Three-line Summary

- Prospective 8-year cohort study (n=89 posterior maxilla cases: 74 graftless, 15 grafted control; minimum 60-month follow-up; single center, Athens) comparing graftless vs grafted simultaneous transcrestal sinus lift with dental implant placement.
- Graftless group achieved 97.76% implant success rate vs 86.6% in the grafted control; residual bone height was a statistically significant predictor of success in both groups (p<0.05).
- Long-term evidence supports graftless simultaneous transcrestal sinus lift as first-line when adequate residual bone height criteria are met; the graftless approach yielded superior success compared to grafted controls at 8 years, challenging the necessity of bone grafting.

## 세줄요약

- 8년 전향적 코호트(n=89, 상악 구치부: graftless 74례 vs 골이식 대조군 15례; 최소 60개월 추적, 아테네 단일 기관): 골이식 없는 동시 치조정접근 상악동거상술+임플란트 식립 vs 골이식군 비교.
- Graftless군 임플란트 성공률(Implant Success Rate) 97.76% — 골이식 대조군(86.6%) 초과; 잔존골 높이(Residual Bone Height, RBH)가 두 군 모두에서 유의한 성공 예측 인자(p<0.05).
- 적절한 RBH 충족 시 골이식 없는 동시 치조정접근 상악동 거상(Transcrestal Sinus Floor Elevation, TSFE)이 8년 장기 데이터에서 첫 번째 선택지로 지지됨; 이식재 필요성에 의문을 제기하는 결과.
