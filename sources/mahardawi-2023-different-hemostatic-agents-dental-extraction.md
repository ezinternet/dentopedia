## Why Ingested

Local hemostatic pages in the wiki are mostly single-agent or two-arm trials (e.g. [[drug/anticoagulants/guardieiro-2023-chitosan-cellulose-hemostasis-dapt-rct]], [[drug/anticoagulants/kaddah-2024-tranexamic-acid-gelfoam-warfarin]]). This network meta-analysis ranks 11 agents across 23 RCTs and adds a bleeding-event outcome that qualifies the speed-based chitosan evidence. Source: PubMed / PMC, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-39023-7).

## Three-line Summary

Network meta-analysis of 23 RCTs (22 in quantitative synthesis) of hemostatic agents after dental extraction in patients on oral antithrombotic therapy, reference = conventional gauze/cotton pressure ± sutures.

Tranexamic acid (TXA) was the only agent with significantly fewer bleeding events (OR 0.27, 95% CI 0.10–0.69); cyanoacrylate ranked best but was borderline (OR 0.03, P=0.051); chitosan and collagen plug were fastest to hemostasis (SMD −9.78 and −10.13) yet ranked last for bleeding events.

Certainty was low to very low for time to hemostasis and moderate for TXA; most trials had risk-of-bias concerns and few antiplatelet-only trials were available.

## 세줄요약

구강 항혈전제 복용 환자의 발치 후 지혈제를 비교한 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA) — RCT 23편(정량 합성 22편), 기준군은 거즈·솜 압박 ± 봉합.

출혈 사건은 트라넥삼산 (Tranexamic Acid, TXA)만 유의하게 감소(OR 0.27, 95% CI 0.10–0.69); 시아노아크릴레이트 조직접착제는 순위 1위이나 경계 유의성(OR 0.03, P=0.051); 키토산·콜라겐 플러그는 지혈 시간이 가장 짧았으나(SMD −9.78, −10.13) 출혈 사건 순위는 최하위.

근거 확실성은 지혈 시간 낮음~매우 낮음, TXA는 중등도(GRADE); 다수 시험이 편향 위험 우려가 있고 항혈소판제 단독 시험이 적음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First NMA restricted to dental extraction in antithrombotic patients that also reports agents with unfavorable bleeding outcomes (the prior NMA pooled all oral surgery procedures).
- Shows a dissociation between speed of hemostasis and later bleeding: chitosan and collagen plug fastest, yet highest post-operative bleeding odds among agents.
- Identifies TXA as the only agent significantly reducing bleeding events vs conventional measures.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: PubMed/Medline, CENTRAL, Scopus plus grey literature; search ended March 20, 2023; two independent screeners.
- Included RCTs only; excluded trials in which controls stopped or modified antithrombotic therapy, or hemostasis used other methods (e.g. electrocautery).
- RoB 2; frequentist random-effects NMA (R 4.2.2, netmeta); SMD for time to hemostasis, OR for bleeding events; P-score ranking; inconsistency via net-split; certainty via GRADE/CINeMA.
- Reference group "conventional" = gauze/cotton pressure ± sutures.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Search: 1655 records → 82 full texts → 23 included (22 in MA). 13 parallel-group, 9 split-mouth, 1 mixed. Anticoagulant patients 14 studies, antiplatelet 6, unspecified 3. Simple extraction in 19 studies.
- RoB 2: 8 low, 14 some concerns, 1 high.
- Time to hemostasis (9 studies, I²=98.3%): chitosan SMD −9.78 (−12.78 to −6.78, direct); collagen plug SMD −10.13 (−15.53 to −4.73, indirect); Ankaferd longer than chitosan (SMD 7.39, 0.33–14.45).
- Bleeding events (16 studies, I²=12.5%): TXA OR 0.27 (0.10–0.69); cyanoacrylate OR 0.03 (0.0008–1.02), P=0.051. Indirect: chitosan and collagen higher odds than cyanoacrylate and TXA. Direct: bismuth subgallate and fibrin lower than collagen.
- Anticoagulated patients (11 studies, I²=32.3%): TXA OR 0.24 (0.08–0.77). Gelatin sponge performed worse than conventional measures.
- Simple extraction (12 studies, I²=34.3%): TXA OR 0.12 (0.02–0.71).
- GRADE: TXA evidence moderate; time-to-hemostasis low/very low.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Too few antiplatelet trials for an antiplatelet-only analysis; one analysis only for time to hemostasis.
- Extracted teeth (molar vs anterior) not specified; most trials with potential bias.
- Substantial heterogeneity for time outcome; cyanoacrylate estimate very imprecise (OR 0.03, CI to 1.02) and indirect.
- Authors call for more direct head-to-head trials and antiplatelet-specific evaluation.

## 7. Glossary

- Network meta-analysis (NMA): combines direct and indirect comparisons across a network of treatments.
- P-score: frequentist ranking value; higher means better.
- Cyanoacrylate tissue adhesive: topical adhesive sealing the socket/wound.
- Conventional measures: gauze/cotton pressure with or without sutures.
- CINeMA: tool for grading confidence in NMA results.
