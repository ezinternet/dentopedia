## Why Ingested

Pilot clinical audit demonstrating a fully digital occlusal recording protocol using intraoral scanning and Patient Specific Motion (PSM) technology to minimize chairside adjustments in prosthodontic restorations. Directly relevant to the `occlusion` knowledge base as a digital alternative to traditional facebow/articulator methods.

→ [[occlusion/risciotti-2024-digital-protocol-to-record-occlusal]]

## Three-line Summary

A fully digital occlusal analysis protocol combining Trios 3 intraoral scanning with 3Shape Patient Specific Motion (PSM) was evaluated in 37 patients receiving 52 metal-free restorations.
Only 17.3% required subtractive and 7.7% additive occlusal finishing, demonstrating high accuracy of the digital workflow when scan fitting is verified against articulating paper contacts.
The study highlights that PSM reduces excursive-movement errors but does not correct scan-fitting errors; clinical verification of scan fit is essential for minimal post-delivery adjustment.

## 세줄요약

구강 내 스캐너(Intraoral Scanner, IOS) Trios 3와 환자 맞춤 운동(Patient Specific Motion, PSM)을 결합한 완전 디지털 교합 분석 프로토콜을 37명 환자, 52개 보철물에 적용하여 평가하였다.
소삭 조정(Subtractive Adjustment) 17.3%, 첨가 조정(Additive Adjustment) 7.7%로 디지털 워크플로우(Digital Workflow)의 높은 정확도를 확인하였으며, 스캔 적합성을 교합지로 임상 검증한 것이 핵심이었다.
PSM은 측방 운동 중 간섭을 줄이지만 스캔 피팅 오류는 보정하지 못하므로 교합지 대조 검증이 임상 필수 단계임을 강조한다.

## Document Information

- **Study type**: Clinical audit / pilot case series
- **Setting**: Dental clinic; January–May 2023
- **Sample**: 37 patients; 52 restorations (40 single crowns, 12 multi-unit)
- **Follow-up**: Delivery appointment (not long-term)
- **Ethics**: Waived per CIOMS-2016 (non-invasive, minimal risk); no personal data collected

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a step-by-step fully digital occlusal analysis protocol using IOS + PSM for single and multi-unit metal-free restorations
- Demonstrates 77% of restorations required no occlusal adjustment; 17.3% subtractive (chairside) and 7.7% additive (lab)
- Establishes the importance of verifying digital scan fit against 21 µm articulating paper clinical contacts before CAD design
- Shows that PSM effectively controls excursive (dynamic) occlusion errors but cannot compensate for intermaxillary scan-fitting inaccuracies
- Uses 25 µm additive morphing in CAD to reinforce centric contacts and counter periodontal ligament compressibility artefacts

## Methodology

**Patients**: Single-/triple-unit metal-free (zirconia or lithium disilicate) restorations on natural teeth or implants; excluded: complex occlusal therapy (CR re-organization, VDO change).

**Digital impressions**: Trios 3 Intraoral Scanner (3Shape A/S, Copenhagen) for working arch with temporaries, abutment/scan body, antagonist, and bilateral scan bites.

**Dynamic occlusal recording**: PSM (Patient Specific Motion) tool within Trios 3 to capture individual mandibular movements.

**Clinical contact marking**: Excursive contacts — 21 µm red Accufilm II; MI contacts — 21 µm black Accufilm II; recorded during color scanning for CAD verification.

**CAD/Lab steps**: Dental System CAD software; Geometric Functional Anatomy (AFG) technique for wax-up; individual morphing tool (radius 0.48 mm, 25 µm additive) to reinforce MI contacts.

**Fabrication**: Monolithic zirconia 850 milled (0.2 mm cutting tools), sintered; lithium disilicate pressed from CAD/CAM-milled wax.

**Post-delivery verification**: 8 µm Shimstock + Accufilm II red/black; periapical radiograph when needed.

## Key Results

| Variable | n | % |
|---|---|---|
| Total restorations | 52 | 100% |
| No occlusal adjustment needed | 40 | ~77% |
| Subtractive occlusal adjustment | 9 | 17.3% |
| Additive occlusal adjustment | 4 | 7.7% |
| Subtractive performed chairside | 9 | 100% of subtractive |
| Additive performed in lab | 4 | 100% of additive |

- 33/37 patients received monolithic zirconia; 4/37 lithium disilicate
- 40 single crowns (8 on implants on T-base abutments); 12 multi-unit (3 three-unit bridges on natural teeth; 1 on implants)
- All restorations delivered in maximum intercuspidation (MI)

## Limitations

- No control group (no comparison with conventional facebow/articulator workflow)
- Small sample (37 patients, 52 restorations); sample size calculation not feasible due to novelty
- Short follow-up (delivery appointment only); no long-term outcomes reported
- Single-center clinical audit; generalizability limited
- Not evaluated for large-span bridges (>3 units)
- Dependent on operator expertise with 3Shape Trios + Dental System software

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Patient Specific Motion (PSM) | 3Shape Trios 3 feature recording individual mandibular movement kinematics for virtual articulation in CAD |
| Geometric Functional Anatomy (AFG/GFA) | CAD technique using a 3D grid with anatomical references to replace physical caliper-based wax-up |
| Accufilm II | 21 µm double-sided articulating film (red = excursive; black = MI contacts) |
| Shimstock | 8 µm foil strip for detecting minimal occlusal contacts (hold/pull test) |
| Monolithic zirconia 850 | High-strength monolithic zirconia milled without veneering porcelain, milled at 0.2 mm resolution |
| Scan fitting / compenetration | Artefact in virtual articulation where scans interpenetrate due to periodontal ligament compressibility at MI |
