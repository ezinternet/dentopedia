## Three-line Summary

In vitro (n=16/group): posterior zirconia RBFPD — multilayer 3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ gradient material outperforms 5Y-PSZ alone; WR design (1025–1617N) > IR design (684–1216N); aging had no effect

3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ centric nesting position yielded highest load-bearing capacity; top-position and 5Y-only materials showed lower safety margin

Recommendation: 3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ multilayer zirconia suitable for clinical trials; 5Y-PSZ alone should be used with caution for RBFPD indication

## 세줄요약

시험관 연구 (n=16/군): 후방부 지르코니아 레진 결합형 가공의치(RBFPD) — 다층형 3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ 그라디언트(3Y/5Y)가 5Y-PSZ 단독보다 우수; 날개형(WR, 1025–1617 N) > 인레이형(IR, 684–1216 N); 인공 노화 영향 없음

3Y/5Y 블랭크의 중심부(Centric) 네스팅 위치에서 최고 하중 지지능; 상단(Top) 위치·5Y 단독은 안전역 낮음

임상 추천: 3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ 다층형은 임상시험 권장 가능; 5Y-PSZ 단독은 RBFPD 적응증에서 신중히 사용

## 2. Key Contributions
- First in vitro comparison of multilayer zirconia grades for posterior RBFPD
- 3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ gradient material superior to 5Y-PSZ alone in load-bearing
- Vertical blank nesting position influences safety margin within same material
- Aging (thermocycling + loading) did not affect load-bearing capacity
- Provides preclinical foundation for material selection in posterior zirconia RBFPD

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- In vitro: maxillary first molar replacement RBFPD (n=16/group)
- 2 retainer designs: IR (inlay-retained), WR (wing-retained)
- 2 multilayer materials: 3Y-TZP/5Y-PSZ (3Y/5Y), 5Y-PSZ only (5Y)
- For 3Y/5Y: 2 nesting positions (center, top)
- Half of specimens: artificial aging (thermocycling + cyclic loading)
- Oblique load-bearing test (F in Newtons)
- Statistics: Welch ANOVA + Dunnett-T3 post hoc (α=0.05)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Load-bearing capacity by group (mean F range):
| Design | Material | F range (N) |
|---|---|---|
| IR | all | 684–1216 |
| WR | all | 1025–1617 |
| IR | 3Y/5Y centric | ~1216 (highest IR) |
| IR | 5Y only | ~684 (lowest) |
| WR | 3Y/5Y centric | ~1617 (highest) |

- No debonding/defects after aging
- Aging: no significant influence (P≥0.526)
- Vertical nesting in centric > top position for 3Y/5Y material
- Clear hierarchy: 3Y/5Y centric > 3Y/5Y top > 5Y only

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- In vitro only — no clinical validation
- Maxillary first molar only (may differ for mandibular or other positions)
- Opacity of monolithic zirconia limits aesthetics (acknowledged)

## 7. Glossary
- RBFPD: Resin-Bonded Fixed Partial Denture
- IR: Inlay-Retained retainer
- WR: Wing-Retained retainer
- 3Y-TZP: 3 mol% yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal (high strength)
- 5Y-PSZ: 5 mol% yttria partially stabilized zirconia (translucent, lower strength)
- Nesting position: location within the milling blank where the restoration is cut
- F: fracture/failure load in Newtons
