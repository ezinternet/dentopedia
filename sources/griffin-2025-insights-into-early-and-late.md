## Three-line Summary

Retrospective electronic-health-record cohort of US veterans (2000-2021): 132,675 veterans with 468,496 implants entered the multivariable models; early failure was defined as within 6 months of placement and late failure as after 6 months, and 13,492 failures occurred in 9,423 veterans (reported as a 7.1% failure rate over 21 years).

For early failure, age 70 years or older (+257% odds), reimplantation (+89%), Asian race and osteoporosis were significantly associated; for late failure, active periodontitis (+139%), alendronate use (+114%), age 40-60 years and African American race were associated.

The intra-class correlation coefficient (ICC) of 86% indicates strong patient-level clustering, i.e. prior implant failure or success history predicts the outcome; the evidence is retrospective and abstract-only (adjusted ORs and CIs not available).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Separates early (within 6 months of placement) from late (after 6 months) failure in a veteran population, where few studies do so.
- 132,675 veterans, 468,496 implants; 13,492 failures in 9,423 veterans.
- Early-failure factors: age 70 years or older, Asian race, osteoporosis, reimplantation. Late-failure factors: age 40-60 years, African American race, active periodontitis, alendronate use.
- ICC 86% showing strong patient-level clustering.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design/setting**: retrospective cohort; US Veterans Affairs electronic health records, January 1, 2000 to December 1, 2021; veterans aged 18 years or older with dental implants.
- **Analysis**: univariable analysis to identify significant risk factors, then multivariable mixed-effect logistic regression for odds ratios of early/late failure; ICC from a mixed-effects model for the proportion of variance attributable to patient-level clustering.
- Abstract-only: covariates, ascertainment of failure and model details unavailable.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 7.1% failure rate over 21 years as reported (13,492 failures in 9,423 unique veterans).
- Early failure: age 70 years or older approximately +257% odds; reimplantation +89%; Asian race and osteoporosis also significant (magnitudes not in abstract).
- Late failure: active periodontitis approximately +139%; alendronate approximately +114%; age 40-60 years and African American race also significant.
- ICC 86%; authors interpret outcome as strongly influenced by the patient's prior history of implant failure or success.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only; no adjusted ORs, CIs or separate early/late counts.
- 7.1% equals 9,423/132,675 (veterans), whereas 13,492/468,496 implants is about 2.9%; denominator of the stated "failure rate" is ambiguous (source-data-issue).
- Retrospective EHR data with possible misclassification and unmeasured confounders (smoking, implant system, bone quality, operator); veteran population limits generalisability.

## 7. Glossary

- **Early implant failure (조기 실패)**: failure within 6 months of placement in this study.
- **Late implant failure (후기 실패)**: failure after 6 months of placement.
- **ICC (intra-class correlation coefficient)**: proportion of outcome variance attributable to patient-level clustering.
- **Alendronate**: oral bisphosphonate used for osteoporosis.
