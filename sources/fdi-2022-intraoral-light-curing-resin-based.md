## Why Ingested

FDI official policy statement on intraoral light curing fills a clinical governance gap in the wiki: no prior page establishes the international regulatory and manufacturer standards framework for LCU selection and RBM compatibility. This document anchors all photoinitiator/LCU discussion to an authoritative professional body position.

## Three-line Summary

The FDI 2022 policy statement recognizes that narrow-spectrum LED and laser LCUs may be spectrally incompatible with newer alternative photoinitiators beyond camphorquinone, creating a clinically underrecognized polymerization risk. It calls on manufacturers of both light-curable materials and LCUs to comply with ISO standards (10650:2018 and 4049:2019) and provide full spectral and irradiance labeling. Professional education on tip position, angulation, movement, and exposure time is emphasized as essential for long-term restoration success.

## 세줄요약

FDI 2022 권고문은 협소 스펙트럼 LED·레이저 LCU가 캄포퀴논 이외의 신형 광개시제를 활성화하지 못할 수 있음을 공식 인정한다. ISO 10650:2018·4049:2019에 따라 제조사는 파장·조사도·노출 시간·재료 두께 등 완전한 스펙트럼 정보를 레이블에 표기해야 한다. 팁 위치·각도·이동·노출 시간에 관한 임상가 교육이 장기 수복 성공의 핵심으로 강조된다.

## Key Contributions

- **Spectral incompatibility problem formalized**: Narrow-spectrum LED/laser LCUs may not activate all photosensitizers present in modern RBMs using alternative photoinitiators beyond camphorquinone.
- **ISO compliance mandate**: Manufacturers must follow ISO 10650:2018 (LCUs) and 4049:2019 (resin materials), disclosing wavelength, irradiance, exposure time, maximum RBM thickness, radiant exitance, irradiance loss over distance, emission spectrum, active tip area, and beam uniformity.
- **Clinical education requirement**: Professional guidance on LCU tip position, movement, angulation, and exposure time is identified as a necessary but currently insufficient element of clinical practice.
- **Research call**: Further research on safety and efficiency of LCUs and dental materials should be encouraged.
- **Eye protection**: Safe LCU use requires appropriate eye protection for both patients and operators.

## Methodology

Consensus document developed by the FDI World Dental Federation, adopted by the FDI General Assembly at the 2021 World Dental Congress in Sydney. Policy statement format; no primary data. Based on best available scientific evidence at time of adoption.

## Key Results

**POLICY statements:**
1. Manufacturers of light-curable dental materials must provide: specific wavelengths required, irradiance needed, exposure time, and maximum RBM thickness for sufficient curing (per ISO 4049:2019).
2. Manufacturers of LCUs must provide: emitted radiant power, radiant exitance, irradiance loss over distance, emission spectrum, active tip area, light transmission, and beam uniformity specifications (per ISO 10650:2018).
3. FDI supports further research on safety and efficiency of LCUs and dental materials.

**PRINCIPLES:**
- Adequate light curing is important but commonly underestimated.
- Correct LCU selection and use is essential for patient and operator safety and for long-term success of direct restorations.

## Limitations

- Policy statement only — no primary data or systematic review methodology.
- The single explicit research recommendation ("further research should be encouraged") is broad and non-specific.
- Disclaimer notes content reflects cultural sensitivities and socio-economic constraints at time of writing.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| 광중합 개시제 (Photoinitiator, PI) | 특정 가시광 파장에 의해 활성화되어 RBM 중합을 개시하는 화학 성분 |
| 광감응제 (Photosensitizer) | 광개시제 시스템 내에서 가속제와 반응해 중합 반응종을 생성하는 성분; 캄포퀴논이 대표적 |
| 조사도 (Irradiance, mW/cm²) | 광원으로부터 표면이 받는 단위면적당 복사 파워 |
| 복사 발산도 (Radiant exitance, mW/cm²) | 표면이 방출하는 단위면적당 복사 파워 |
| 발광 스펙트럼 (Emission spectrum, nm) | 광원이 방출하는 전자기 복사의 파장 범위 |
| 광 빔 균일성 (Light beam uniformity) | 광원 빔 전면에 걸친 조사도 및 분광 복사 파워의 균일성 |
| QTH | 광범위 발광 스펙트럼을 가진 석영-텅스텐-할로겐 LCU |
| LCU | 광중합 장치 (Light-Curing Unit) |
| RBM | 레진 기반 재료 (Resin-Based Material) |
