## Why Ingested

First-ever 7-year RCT follow-up of reconstructive peri-implantitis surgery (xenograft + CGF vs collagen membrane) — long-term RCT-grade evidence is the biggest gap in the [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/monje-2025-long-term-surgical-treatment-outcomes]] literature, which was built mostly on case series. It also supplies the long-term data missing from [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/shetty-2023-platelet-concentrates-peri-implantitis-treatment-sr]], which labeled platelet-concentrate (CGF) evidence "preliminary."

## Three-line Summary

7-year follow-up of an RCT (original 57 patients/78 implants; 34 patients/49 implants completed) of reconstructive peri-implantitis surgery using xenograft covered by concentrated growth factor (CGF) vs collagen membrane (CM), all under supportive periodontal/peri-implant care (SPIC).

Treatment success fell from 46.2%/50.0% (CGF/CM, 1 year) to 23.1%/30.8% at 7 years; cumulative survival 81.25% vs 88.8%; CM held significantly better deepest PPD (p = 0.040) and radiographic vertical defect depth (p = 0.043) at implant level, while composite outcomes did not differ.

Reconstructive surgery's short-term gains relapse progressively despite regular SPIC, CGF offers no long-term advantage over standard collagen membrane, and baseline suppuration on probing is the dominant modifiable-looking failure predictor (OR = 15.45; 95% CI 1.42–168.5).

## 세줄요약

임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis) 재건수술(이종골 이식 + 익은성장인자(CGF)막 vs 콜라겐막(CM))의 7년 장기 추적 — 원 기록 57명/78개 임플란트 중 34명/49개 임플란트가 완료, 전원 임플란트 주위 지지치료(SPIC) 유지 하에서 분석.

치료 성공률은 1년 46.2%/50.0%(CGF/CM군)에서 7년 23.1%/30.8%로 하락했고 누적 생존율은 81.25% vs 88.8%; 심명 탐침깊이(Deepest PPD, p = 0.040)와 방사선학적 수직결손심도(vertical defect depth, p = 0.043)는 임플란트 수준에서 CM군이 유의하게 우세했으나 복합 성공 지표에는 차이 없음.

재건수술의 단기 개선은 SPIC 유지에도 점진적으로 재발하며, CGF는 표준 콜라겐막 대비 장기적 이점이 없고, 기저 탐침화농(Suppuration on Probing, SOP) 존재가 유일한 유의한 장기 실패 예측인자(OR = 15.45; 95% CI 1.42–168.5)였다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Implants Research 2025;36:1202–1218
- **DOI**: 10.1111/clr.14466
- **Institution**: Gazi University (Ankara, Turkey); co-authors at University of Bern, Ankara Medipol University, Yeditepe University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT to report 7-year survival/success of reconstructive peri-implantitis surgery with a barrier membrane — fills the RCT-grade long-term evidence gap.
- Demonstrates progressive relapse despite strict SPIC: treatment success halved from ~46–50% (1 year) to 23–31% (7 years), aligning with 10-year cohort declines (Roccuzzo 2020).
- CGF membrane provides no long-term advantage over Bio-Gide collagen membrane; CM held significantly better deepest PPD and radiographic defect depth at implant level.
- Baseline suppuration on probing (SOP) was the only significant patient-level predictor of treatment failure (OR = 15.45, p = 0.02); baseline deepest PPD negatively predicted success at implant level (OR = 3.166, p = 0.008).
- Confirms partial-resolution as a meaningful intermediate category (15.4–23.5% at 7 years), beyond binary success/failure.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: 7-year follow-up report of an RCT (original protocol: Isler et al. 2018; CONSORT 2010; registered NCT04769609)
- **n**: Original 57 patients (78 implants); 7-year completers: 34 patients (24 CGF implants, 25 CM implants)
- **Intervention**: Open-flap reconstructive surgery — granulation tissue removal, titanium curette debridement, Bio-Oss xenograft into 2-/3-/4-wall intrabony defects ≥3 mm, covered by CGF membranes or Bio-Gide collagen membrane; submerged healing 6 months; all patients on risk-based SPIC (3-month intervals year 1, then 3–6 months)
- **Outcomes**: Primary — CAL change (1-year, original report); secondary — treatment success (%), partial resolution, disease recurrence, PPD/BOP/PI/GI/PSTD/radiographic vertical defect depth at 1, 5, 7 years; PROMs (VAS) at 7 years
- **Statistics**: GLMM, repeated-measures ANOVA, univariate/multivariate logistic regression, Kaplan–Meier with log-rank

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Treatment success (patient level)**: 46.2% (CGF) / 50.0% (CM) at 1 year → 23.1% / 30.8% at 7 years (p > 0.05 between groups).
- **Disease recurrence (patient level)**: 34.6% / 30.8% at 7 years; implant loss 11.5% (CGF) vs 7.7% (CM).
- **Cumulative survival**: 81.25% (CGF) vs 88.8% (CM); survival + surgical-retreatment-free 66.7% vs 68.8% (log-rank p = 0.980). Median failure-free survival 4.42 vs 4.50 years.
- **PPD (patient level)**: baseline 5.83/5.44 mm → 1 yr 3.25/2.87 → 7 yrs 4.40/3.90 mm (CGF/CM); deepest PPD at implant level significantly better in CM at 7 yrs (4.48 ± 1.23 vs 4.92 ± 1.25 mm; between-group p = 0.040).
- **Radiographic vertical defect depth (implant level)**: baseline 3.81/3.23 mm → 1 yr 1.98/1.29 → 7 yrs 2.33/1.79 mm (CGF/CM; between-group p = 0.043) — partial bone fill maintained but relapse from the 1-year nadir.
- **Predictors**: baseline SOP → treatment failure OR = 15.45 (95% CI 1.42–168.5; p = 0.02, patient level) and OR = 0.125 inverse-coded (p = 0.004, implant level); baseline deepest PPD → lower success OR = 3.166 (95% CI 1.35–7.45; p = 0.008, implant level); two-wall vs four-wall defect significant at implant level (p = 0.043).
- **PROMs (7 years)**: VAS pain ~0.8–1.0, esthetics ~8.3, satisfaction ~8.3 — high satisfaction despite objective relapse.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No graft-only (no membrane) control arm — cannot isolate the membrane's incremental benefit.
- Original primary outcome (CAL) set at 1 year; 7-year analysis affected by dropout (57 → 34 patients), though in line with similar long-term reports.
- Implant-level GLMM did not model patient random effects — intra-patient clustering may overstate significance.
- PROMs collected only at 7 years; radiographic analysis 2D (non-individualized bite blocks), limiting buccal dehiscence assessment.
- Wide CI for the SOP predictor (1.42–168.5) — small sample precludes definitive conclusions.

## 6. Related Work
- monje-2025-long-term-surgical-treatment-outcomes: SR of ≥5-year surgical outcomes (mean resolution 58.6%) — this RCT provides independent RCT-grade confirmation at the lower end of its range.
- shetty-2023-platelet-concentrates-peri-implantitis-treatment-sr: included the parent Isler 2018 trial; this 7-year report refines the "promising but not conclusive" CGF verdict — no long-term benefit over collagen membrane.
- soldini-2025-reconstructive-therapy-peri-implantitis-rct, hakkers-2026-reconstructive-peri-implantitis-3wall-4wall-rct: 1-year reconstructive RCTs whose radiographic-without-clinical gains this follow-up shows are not durable at 7 years.

## 7. Glossary
- **CGF (Concentrated Growth Factor)**: Autologous platelet concentrate membrane prepared by differential centrifugation, used here as a barrier over the graft.
- **SPIC (Supportive Periodontal/Peri-Implant Care)**: Risk-profiled maintenance program (3–6-month intervals) after peri-implantitis surgery.
- **SOP (Suppuration on Probing)**: Pus on probing — baseline sign of advanced disease; strongest failure predictor here.
- **PSTD (Peri-implant Soft Tissue Deficiency)**: Distance from mucosal margin to implant abutment interface, used in CAL and success criteria.
- **Partial resolution**: PPD ≤5 mm, BOP ≤1 site, no SOP/retreatmen/loss but failing strict success criteria (e.g., residual defect ≥1 mm).
- **Vertical defect depth**: Radiographic distance from implant shoulder reference line to first bone-to-implant contact.
