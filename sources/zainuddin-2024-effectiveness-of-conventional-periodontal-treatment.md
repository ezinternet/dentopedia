## Why Ingested

기존 [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/ramanauskaite-2020-antiseptics-adjuncts-scaling-root-planing]]은 서방형(sustained-release) 국소 항균제가 SRP 단독 대비 이득이 있다고 종합했고 [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/annisa-2023-chx-gel-chip-srp-sr-ma]]는 테트라사이클린·미노사이클린 제제가 PD 감소에서 우수하다고 시사했다. 본 파일럿 RCT는 테트라사이클린 파이버 (Tetracycline fiber)와 2% 미노사이클린 겔 (Minocycline gel)을 SRP 단독 대조군과 직접 비교한 소규모 1차 데이터로, 군 간 유의차가 없었다는 점에서 위 종합 결론의 임상 적용 한계(검정력)를 보여준다. → [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/zainuddin-2024-effectiveness-of-conventional-periodontal-treatment]]

## Three-line Summary

Pilot randomized, open, single-blinded trial (n=45; 15 per arm; Universiti Sains Malaysia periodontic clinic; 3-month follow-up) comparing SRP + tetracycline fiber (Periodontal Plus AB), SRP + 2% minocycline HCl gel, and SRP alone in periodontitis patients with pockets of 5 mm or more.

All three arms improved significantly within group at 3 months (PD change 1.79 / 1.46 / 1.74 mm; BOP change 22.15 / 26.42 / 14.76 percentage points for tetracycline / minocycline / control), but repeated-measures ANOVA showed no significant between-group difference for PD, CAL, or BOP (all p>0.05), and SRP alone had the largest CAL gain (1.10 mm).

Local tetracycline or minocycline gave no demonstrated advantage over SRP alone; with n=15 per arm, 3-month follow-up, no microbiology and no sample-size justification, the trial is underpowered and cannot establish equivalence.

## 세줄요약

파일럿 무작위 개방 단일맹검 시험 (n=45, 군당 15명, 말레이시아 이슬람과학대학 치주과 클리닉, 3개월 추적): 5 mm 이상 치주낭이 있는 치주염 환자에서 SRP + 테트라사이클린 파이버 (Tetracycline fiber) vs SRP + 2% 미노사이클린 겔 (Minocycline gel) vs SRP 단독을 비교했다.

세 군 모두 군내에서는 유의하게 개선되었으나(3개월 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 감소 1.79 / 1.46 / 1.74 mm, 탐침 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP) 감소 22.15 / 26.42 / 14.76 %p; 테트라사이클린 / 미노사이클린 / 대조), 반복측정 ANOVA상 PPD·임상 부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL)·BOP 모두 군 간 유의차가 없었고(전부 p>0.05) CAL 개선은 SRP 단독군이 가장 컸다(1.10 mm).

국소 테트라사이클린·미노사이클린이 SRP 단독보다 낫다는 근거는 없으며, 군당 15명·3개월·미생물 검사 없음·표본수 산출 근거 없음으로 검정력이 부족해 동등성도 입증하지 못한다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Cureus 2024;16(2):e55167 (e-pub 2024-02-28; PubMed metadata)
- **DOI**: 10.7759/cureus.55167
- **PMID / PMCID**: 38558744 / PMC10980540
- **Institution**: School of Dental Sciences, Universiti Sains Malaysia (Kelantan), Malaysia; one author at Universiti Sains Islam Malaysia
- **Design**: Pilot randomized, open, single-blinded, three-arm parallel trial

## 2. Key Contributions

- Head-to-head comparison of two local tetracycline-class delivery forms (fiber vs 2% gel) alongside an SRP-only control in one trial.
- Greatest PD reduction in the tetracycline-fiber arm (1.44 mm at 1 month; 1.79 mm overall), greatest BOP reduction in the minocycline-gel arm (19.34 percentage points at 1 month; 26.42 overall), greatest CAL gain in the SRP-only arm (1.10 mm overall) -- none of the between-group differences significant.
- Authors' own conclusion: local agents "may improve" healing but "do not offer an advantage over the conventional method of SRP alone".

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Pilot RCT, blocked randomization with sealed envelopes; described as open, single-blinded (who was blinded is not stated).
- **Setting**: Periodontic Clinic, Hospital Universiti Sains Malaysia, Kelantan.
- **n**: 45 (15 per arm); mean age 52 +/- 10.6 years in the text; 24 male / 21 female.
- **Inclusion / exclusion**: diagnosed periodontitis, no periodontal treatment or antibiotics in the previous 3 months; exclusion for pregnancy or intended pregnancy, bone disease, immunocompromised state.
- **Interventions**: SRP (ultrasonic + hand curettes under local anesthesia) of all pockets of 5 mm or more at baseline; then (1) tetracycline fiber placed subgingivally (Periodontal Plus AB), (2) minocycline HCl 2% microsphere gel immediately after SRP, or (3) SRP only. Oral hygiene instruction and prophylaxis as needed at review visits.
- **Outcomes**: PPD, CAL, BOP% at baseline, 1 month and 3 months.
- **Calibration**: single calibrated examiner; reproducibility on 5 chronic periodontitis patients, ICC / Cronbach alpha 0.78.
- **Analysis**: SPSS 26; repeated-measures ANOVA (3 groups x 3 time points); alpha 5%; Mauchly sphericity met for PD, violated for CAL and BOP.

| BOP (%) | 52.53 (21.79) / 55.87 (14.15) / 40.63 (19.41) | 42.10 / 36.53 / 31.54 | 30.38 / 29.45 / 25.87 | 22.15 / 26.42 / 14.76 percentage points; p=0.002 / <0.001 / 0.002 |

- 1-month change: PD 1.44 / 0.99 / 1.01 mm; CAL 0.60 / 0.71 / 0.65 mm; BOP 10.43 / 19.34 / 9.09 percentage points.
- Between 1 and 3 months: PD change not significant in the SRP-only arm (p=0.1); CAL change significant only in the SRP-only arm (p=0.032).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Authors: small sample and short observation period; recommend larger sample, longer follow-up and microbiological confirmation.
- Underpowered pilot: 15 per arm, no sample-size calculation reported, no dropout/attrition reporting; non-significant between-group results are not evidence of equivalence.
- "Open, single-blinded" is not specified (examiner vs patient); no placebo carrier in the control arm.
- Baseline BOP differed numerically (control 40.6% vs 52.5-55.9%) and baseline CAL SD is large in the control arm (1.69 mm); regression-to-the-mean and baseline imbalance are not addressed.
- Sphericity was violated for CAL and BOP but no correction (e.g., Greenhouse-Geisser) is reported; no multiplicity adjustment; unit of analysis (patient vs site mean) is not stated.
- Internal inconsistencies in the text: the Discussion cites SRP+M vs SRP mean changes of 1.75 (0.93) vs 1.92 (0.84) mm for PD and 2.00 vs 2.11 mm for CAL, which do not match the trial's own Results (PD 1.46 vs 1.74; CAL 0.95 vs 1.10) and appear to come from other work without attribution; Results text gives baseline BOP 52.51% vs 52.53% in Table 4; the abstract's "CAL highest improvement in minocycline" refers only to the 1-month interval.
- Reported follow-up is 3 months; durability of any local-delivery effect is not assessed.

## 6. Related Work

- Ramanauskaite 2020 ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/ramanauskaite-2020-antiseptics-adjuncts-scaling-root-planing]]): SR+MA favouring sustained-release antiseptic adjuncts over SRP alone; this pilot did not detect an adjunct benefit.
- Annisa 2023 ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/annisa-2023-chx-gel-chip-srp-sr-ma]]): SR+MA of CHX chip/gel vs other local antimicrobials after SRP; tetracycline/minocycline-type agents showed better PD reduction than CHX.
- Smiley 2015 ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/smiley-2015-nonsurgical-periodontitis-srp-guideline]]): ADA guideline listing local minocycline among effective SRP adjuncts at PD 5 mm or more.
- Milinkovic 2025 ([[periodontics/host-modulation/milinkovic-2025-local-systemic-antibiotics-periodontal-rct]]): local vs systemic antibiotic adjuncts in NSPT (stage III).

## 7. Glossary

- **SRP**: scaling and root planing, mechanical subgingival debridement.
- **PPD / PD**: probing pocket depth.
- **CAL**: clinical attachment level (loss); here reductions are reported as improvement.
- **BOP**: bleeding on probing, percentage of sites.
- **Periodontal Plus AB**: tetracycline-impregnated fiber used as the local antibiotic in this trial.
- **Minocycline HCl 2% gel**: sustained-release minocycline microsphere formulation placed subgingivally.
