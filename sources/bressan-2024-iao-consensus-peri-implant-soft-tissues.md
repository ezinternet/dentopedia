## Three-line Summary

This is a 2024 IAO (Italian Academy of Osseointegration) consensus conference synthesizing 4 pre-registered PRISMA/PROSPERO systematic reviews on peri-implant soft tissue: keratinized mucosa (KM), collagen matrix vs. autogenous graft, abutment material, and soft-tissue thickness vs. marginal bone loss (MBL).

The assembly agreed supra-periosteal KM favorably affects peri-implant health/esthetics/cleanability/pain but not infection-unrelated crestal bone loss; free gingival graft (FGG) is best for KM gain, connective tissue graft (CTG) beats volume-stable collagen matrix (VCMX) for thickness gain though matrices cut morbidity/surgical time; titanium vs. zirconia abutment showed no consistent soft-tissue-health difference; and soft-tissue thickness vs. MBL correlation remained unresolved (review found none, but the assembly could not reach consensus).

Clinically this is a vote-based expert consensus (not new primary data) built on 4 underlying systematic reviews of variable quality (heterogeneity, few RCTs, some CCTs at high risk of bias), so it should be read as a synthesis snapshot rather than definitive proof.

## 세줄요약

2024년 IAO(Italian Academy of Osseointegration) 컨센서스 컨퍼런스로, 사전등록(PROSPERO/PRISMA) 4편의 체계적 문헌고찰을 근거로 각성점막폭(Keratinized Mucosa width, KMW)·콜라겐 매트릭스 vs 자가이식·지대주 재료·연조직 두께 대 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 4개 주제에 투표 방식 합의문을 도출했다.

합의는 골막상각화점막(supra-periosteal KM)이 주위염 예방·심미·청소용이성·통증감소에 유리하나 비감염성 골소실 예방과는 무관, 각화점막 증대는 유리치은이식술(Free Gingival Graft, FGG)이 최선, 두께 증대는 결합조직이식(Connective Tissue Graft, CTG)이 부피안정형 콜라겐 매트릭스(Volume-stable Collagen Matrix, VCMX)보다 우수하나 매트릭스가 수술시간·이환율을 줄임, 티타늄 vs 지르코니아 지대주는 연조직 건강에 뚜렷한 차이 없음, 연조직 두께-MBL 상관관계는 근거는 없다 했으나 총회는 합의에 이르지 못했다는 내용이다.

이 논문은 새 1차 데이터가 아닌 투표 기반 전문가 합의문이며 기반 리뷰들의 이질성·낮은 RCT 비중·일부 CCT 고위험 편향 한계가 있어, 확정적 증거보다는 종합 스냅샷으로 해석해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Synthesizes 4 separate, independently pre-registered systematic reviews into one voting-based consensus statement, covering the full spectrum of peri-implant soft-tissue factors in a single document.
- Introduces a refined operational split of keratinized mucosa into **supra-periosteal KM** (bone-attached, immobile against muscle traction) vs. **crestal KM** (mobile) — a terminological refinement the assembly felt was needed because prior KM literature conflated the two and produced small/inconsistent effect sizes.
- Provides a head-to-head hierarchy for soft-tissue augmentation: FGG (best for KM width gain) > collagen matrix (esthetics, lower morbidity) for width; CTG (best for thickness gain) > volume-stable collagen matrix (VCMX) for thickness, with matrices favored on surgical time/morbidity/PROMs trade-offs.
- Reports a formal abutment-material (titanium vs. zirconia) consensus: no clear superiority of either material for peri-implant soft-tissue health or recession; choice should be esthetics/phenotype-driven rather than evidence-driven.
- Documents the first (per this paper) network meta-analysis comparing multiple collagen matrix types against autogenous grafts for peri-implant soft-tissue augmentation.
- Explicitly reports a **disagreement between the underlying systematic review's finding and the expert assembly's vote** on soft-tissue thickness vs. MBL — the review found no correlation, but the panel, based on clinical experience, could not endorse that as consensus. This is a notable instance of expert judgment diverging from the literature synthesis.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Structure**: IAO active members convened for a Consensus Conference. Prior to the meeting, 4 systematic reviews were commissioned, conducted per PRISMA guidelines, and pre-registered in PROSPERO.
- **Review 1 (keratinized mucosa)**: PICO-T question — in patients with ≥1 implant-supported restoration under function ≥6 months, does presence of KM influence soft-tissue health, bone levels, esthetics, and patient-related variables? Searched MEDLINE/PubMed, CENTRAL, EMBASE; last search May 2021.
- **Review 2 (soft-tissue matrix vs. autograft)**: PICO-T — in soft-tissue deficiency around implants, does a specific matrix type outperform autogenous soft-tissue graft for buccal soft-tissue thickness and KM width? Searched MEDLINE/PubMed, EMBASE, CENTRAL, Scopus, Web of Science; search April 2021, updated April 2023. Used **network meta-analysis** methodology.
- **Review 3 (abutment material)**: PICO-T — effect of abutment material (titanium vs. zirconia vs. alumina/gold-hued titanium/PEEK terms in search string) on soft-tissue health and stability. Searched MEDLINE/PubMed, CENTRAL, Scopus; last search February 2022.
- **Review 4 (soft-tissue thickness vs. MBL)**: PICO-T — effect of soft-tissue thickness on peri-implant marginal bone loss in RCTs/CCTs assessing initial bone remodeling. Searched MEDLINE/PubMed, CENTRAL, Web of Science, Scopus; last search May 2022. Applied **trial sequential analysis (TSA)** to gauge whether accumulated evidence met the required information size (RIS).
- **Consensus process**: Results of all 4 reviews were presented and discussed at the IAO meeting (15–16 October 2021); proposed statements were put to a vote by the assembly of active members. Where review conclusions and panel judgment diverged (soft-tissue thickness/MBL topic), this was explicitly recorded rather than forced into artificial agreement.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Keratinized mucosa (Review 1)**:
- KM ≥ 2 mm associated with **less** marginal inflammation (GI, mGI, BI, msBI, BoP), plaque accumulation (PI, mPI), recession, biological complications, and less soreness/discomfort during oral hygiene.
- **No difference** for implant failure, probing pocket depth (PPD), or bone levels.
- Evidence caveats: only 1 RCT, only 2 studies at low risk of bias; implant-level data, high heterogeneity, few long-term follow-ups; effect sizes small and shrank further when a poor-compliance-patient study was excluded.
- Consensus statement: supra-periosteal KM favorably influences peri-implant health/esthetics/cleanability/patient pain; does NOT relate to prevention of infection-unrelated bone crest resorption. Both supra-periosteal and crestal KM aid cleaning and reduce pain.

**Soft-tissue augmentation matrix vs. autograft (Review 2, network meta-analysis)**:
- FGG = best for KM width increase.
- Collagen matrix (XCM) can give better esthetics/camouflage than autografts.
- Volume-stable collagen matrix (VCMX) increases soft-tissue thickness, but CTG gives better thickness outcomes than VCMX.
- Autografts (FGG/CTG) carry higher morbidity, longer surgical time, and worse camouflaging with surrounding tissue vs. matrices (PROM trade-off).
- Evidence caveats: limited number of included studies, high heterogeneity, inclusion of CCTs at higher risk of bias, variability in surgical technique/timing/measurement method.

**Abutment material (Review 3)**:
- In periodontally healthy patients, effect of titanium vs. zirconia abutment on crestal bone loss remains **controversial** — no conclusion reached.
- Peri-implant soft-tissue health and soft-tissue recession appear **not influenced** by abutment material.
- Descriptively, zirconia performed somewhat better than titanium in most outcomes in the underlying review, but the assembly still concluded clinical equivalence given study heterogeneity; material choice should be guided by esthetic demand and gingival phenotype, not by an evidence-based superiority claim.

**Soft-tissue thickness vs. MBL (Review 4)**:
- The systematic review found **no correlation** between soft-tissue thickness and MBL over a short follow-up.
- **Trial sequential analysis**: required information size (RIS) was more than 3-fold higher than the actual number of patients pooled in the meta-analysis — i.e., the evidence base is markedly underpowered to confirm a null result.
- Despite the review's null finding, **the assembly could not reach consensus** on this point, citing members' clinical experience; more RCTs excluding confounders (preparation-related, prosthetic-related bone resorption) were recommended.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All 4 underlying reviews suffer from small numbers of included studies, high between-study heterogeneity, inclusion of lower-quality CCTs alongside RCTs, and short/variable follow-up.
- KM review: very few RCTs/low-risk-of-bias studies; conflation of supra-periosteal vs. crestal KM in prior primary literature limits interpretability of pooled data.
- Matrix review: first network meta-analysis on this question, so no comparator NMA exists; variability in patient numbers, surgical technique, and thickness-measurement method across included studies.
- Abutment material review: heterogeneous study designs, outcome definitions (MBL, PI), and follow-up durations.
- Thickness/MBL review: TSA shows the pooled sample is substantially underpowered (RIS >3× actual N) — the "no correlation" finding should not be treated as definitive.
- The paper explicitly calls for future RCTs on (a) more precisely defined (supra-periosteal vs. crestal) KM outcomes, (b) standardized-methodology matrix-vs-autograft trials with larger cohorts, (c) confounder-controlled trials on soft-tissue thickness and MBL.
- As a consensus report, it reflects expert vote aggregated over existing evidence rather than new experimental data; the paper itself acknowledges the panel's inability to reach agreement on one of its four core questions (thickness–MBL).

## 7. Glossary

- **KM / KMW** — Keratinized mucosa / keratinized mucosa width; apico-coronal height of keratinized soft tissue from mucosal margin to mucogingival junction.
- **Supra-periosteal keratinized mucosa** — KM attached to underlying bone, immobile under muscular traction (this paper's proposed refinement).
- **Crestal keratinized mucosa** — KM that is mobile (not bone-attached in the same fixed sense).
- **MT** — Mucosal thickness; horizontal dimension of peri-implant soft tissue (keratinized or not); "thin" <2 mm, "thick" ≥2 mm.
- **STH** — Supra-crestal tissue height; vertical soft-tissue dimension from mucosal margin to crestal bone; "short" <3 mm, "tall" ≥3 mm.
- **PBT** — Peri-implant bone thickness; horizontal osseous dimension supporting the implant; "thin" <2 mm, "thick" ≥2 mm.
- **FGG** — Free gingival graft (autogenous).
- **CTG** — Connective tissue graft (autogenous).
- **VCMX** — Volume-stable collagen matrix.
- **XCM** — Xenogeneic collagen matrix.
- **MBL** — Marginal bone loss.
- **TSA / RIS** — Trial sequential analysis / required information size — statistical method assessing whether a cumulative meta-analysis has enough data to draw a reliable conclusion.
- **PROMs** — Patient-reported outcome measures.
- **IAO** — Italian Academy of Osseointegration.
