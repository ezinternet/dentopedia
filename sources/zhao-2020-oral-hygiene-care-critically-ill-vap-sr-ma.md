## Why Ingested

This Cochrane SR provides the strongest evidence (RR 0.67, moderate certainty) that chlorhexidine mouthwash prevents ventilator-associated pneumonia in ICU patients, demonstrating that oral hygiene care has measurable systemic critical-care outcomes. It grounds CHX mouthwash recommendation in the highest evidence tier and contextualizes the antiviral mechanism studied in [[infection-control/elzein-2021-chlorhexidine-povidone-iodine-mouthwash-salivary-sars-cov-2-rct]] within a broader antimicrobial efficacy picture.

## Three-line Summary

This Cochrane systematic review and meta-analysis included 40 randomized controlled trials (5675 participants) evaluating oral hygiene care interventions for preventing ventilator-associated pneumonia (VAP) in critically ill patients receiving mechanical ventilation for at least 48 hours in intensive care units. Searches were conducted up to February 2020 across multiple databases including CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, CINAHL, LILACS, and trial registries.

Moderate-certainty evidence from 13 studies (1206 participants) showed that chlorhexidine (CHX) mouthrinse or gel probably reduces VAP incidence (RR 0.67, 95% CI 0.47 to 0.97; I²=66%; NNTB 12, 95% CI 7 to 128) compared to placebo/usual care, with no difference in mortality (RR 1.02, 95% CI 0.84 to 1.23; I²=0%). Low-certainty evidence from 5 studies (910 participants) suggested toothbrushing may reduce VAP incidence (RR 0.61, 95% CI 0.41 to 0.91; I²=40%), with no difference in mortality (RR 0.84, 95% CI 0.67 to 1.05; I²=0%).

CHX mouthwash/gel probably prevents VAP in critically ill patients, though no mortality benefit was demonstrated. Toothbrushing may also reduce VAP, but evidence is of lower certainty. Adverse effects were poorly reported across studies, limiting safety conclusions. Future research should use standardized VAP definitions and report adverse events systematically.

## 세줄요약

본 Cochrane 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석은 중환자실에서 최소 48시간 이상 기계환기를 받는 중증 환자를 대상으로 구강위생관리(OHC) 중재의 인공호흡기 관련 폐렴(VAP) 예방 효과를 평가하기 위해 40개의 무작위대조시험(RCT, 총 5675명 참가자)을 포함하였으며, 2020년 2월까지 CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, CINAHL, LILACS 및 임상시험 등록 데이터베이스를 검색하였다.

중등도 근거 수준의 13개 연구(1206명) 메타분석 결과, 클로르헥시딘(CHX) 구강 세정액 또는 젤은 위약/일반 관리 대비 VAP 발생 위험을 유의하게 감소시켰으며(RR 0.67, 95% CI 0.47~0.97, I²=66%, NNTB 12, 95% CI 7~128), 사망률에서는 차이가 없었다(RR 1.02, 95% CI 0.84~1.23, I²=0%). 낮은 근거 수준의 5개 연구(910명)는 칫솔질이 VAP 발생 위험을 감소시킬 수 있음을 시사했으며(RR 0.61, 95% CI 0.41~0.91, I²=40%), 사망률에서는 차이가 없었다(RR 0.84, 95% CI 0.67~1.05, I²=0%).

클로르헥시딘 구강 세정액/젤은 중증 환자의 VAP 예방에 효과적이나 사망률 개선 효과는 입증되지 않았으며, 칫솔질도 VAP를 감소시킬 수 있으나 근거 수준은 더 낮다. 이상반응에 대한 보고가 미흡하여 안전성 결론 도출에 제한이 있으며, 향후 연구에서는 표준화된 VAP 진단 기준과 이상반응의 체계적 보고가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Moderate-certainty evidence from 13 RCTs (1206 participants): chlorhexidine reduces VAP incidence by 33% (RR 0.67, 95% CI 0.47 to 0.97; I²=66%; NNTB 12, 95% CI 7 to 128).
- Moderate-certainty evidence: chlorhexidine does not affect mortality (RR 1.02, 95% CI 0.84 to 1.23; I²=0%; 9 studies, 944 participants).
- Low-certainty evidence from 5 RCTs (910 participants): toothbrushing may reduce VAP incidence (RR 0.61, 95% CI 0.41 to 0.91; I²=40%).
- Low-certainty evidence: toothbrushing does not affect mortality (RR 0.84, 95% CI 0.67 to 1.05; I²=0%; 5 studies, 910 participants).
- Very low-certainty evidence: toothbrushing may reduce ICU stay duration (MD -1.89 days, 95% CI -3.52 to -0.27; 3 studies, 749 participants).
- Adverse effects were inadequately reported; only two studies reported mild reversible oral mucosal irritation with CHX.
- Updated review with new searches identified 9 new studies; 7 previously included studies excluded; new comparison added (CHX versus other oral care agents).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials (RCTs).
- Databases searched: Cochrane Oral Health's Trials Register, CENTRAL (2020 Issue 1), MEDLINE (1946 to 25 Feb 2020), Embase (1980 to 25 Feb 2020), LILACS (1982 to 25 Feb 2020), CINAHL (1937 to 25 Feb 2020), VIP Database (Jan 2012 to 8 Mar 2020). Trial registries: ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP.
- Inclusion criteria: RCTs evaluating oral hygiene care (mouthrinse, gel, swab, toothbrush, or combination) in critically ill patients receiving mechanical ventilation for ≥48 hours in hospital ICUs, without VAP or respiratory infection at baseline.
- Exclusion criteria: Quasi-randomized trials; trials of selective decontamination with topical antibiotics (covered by D'Amico 2009); probiotics (covered by Hao 2015); suction systems alone; single preoperative antiseptic rinse without postoperative OHC.
- Included studies: 40 RCTs (5675 participants) from 14 countries; 29 studies at high risk of bias in at least one domain; 2 studies at low risk of bias overall.
- Risk of bias assessment: Cochrane domain-based tool (random sequence generation, allocation concealment, blinding, incomplete outcome data, selective reporting, other bias). Two authors independently assessed.
- Primary outcomes: Incidence of VAP; Mortality (ICU mortality or 30-day all-cause mortality).
- Secondary outcomes: Duration of mechanical ventilation; Duration of ICU stay; Systemic antibiotic use; Oral health indices (plaque, gingival indices); Adverse effects; Caregiver preferences; Economic data.
- Meta-analysis: Random-effects models when ≥4 trials combined; risk ratio (RR) for dichotomous outcomes; mean difference (MD) for continuous outcomes; 95% confidence intervals. Heterogeneity assessed via Chi² and I² statistics.
- Subgroup analyses: Toothbrushing vs no toothbrushing; chlorhexidine concentration (0.12%, 0.2%, 2%); CHX application method.
- GRADE assessment: Used to rate certainty of evidence for primary and key secondary outcomes.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **CHX vs placebo/usual care (13 studies, 1206 participants) —** VAP incidence: RR 0.67 (95% CI 0.47 to 0.97; I²=66%; moderate certainty). Corresponding risk: 261 per 1000 (control) vs 175 per 1000 (intervention). NNTB 12 (95% CI 7 to 128).
- **CHX vs placebo/usual care —** Mortality: RR 1.02 (95% CI 0.84 to 1.23; I²=0%; 9 studies, 944 participants; moderate certainty).
- **CHX vs placebo/usual care —** Duration of ventilation: MD -1.10 days (95% CI -3.20 to 1.00; I²=74%; 4 studies, 594 participants; very low certainty).
- **CHX vs placebo/usual care —** Duration of ICU stay: MD -0.89 days (95% CI -3.59 to 1.82; I²=69%; 5 studies, 627 participants; low certainty).
- **Toothbrushing vs no toothbrushing (5 studies, 910 participants) —** VAP incidence: RR 0.61 (95% CI 0.41 to 0.91; I²=40%; low certainty).
- **Toothbrushing vs no toothbrushing —** Mortality: RR 0.84 (95% CI 0.67 to 1.05; I²=0%; 5 studies, 910 participants; low certainty).
- **Toothbrushing vs no toothbrushing —** Duration of ventilation: MD -1.46 days (95% CI -2.69 to -0.23; 3 studies, 749 participants; low certainty; CHX in both groups).
- **Toothbrushing vs no toothbrushing —** Duration of ICU stay: MD -1.89 days (95% CI -3.52 to -0.27; 3 studies, 749 participants; very low certainty).
- **Adverse effects —** Mild reversible oral mucosal irritation: 10% in CHX group vs 1% in control (RR 10.29, 95% CI 1.34 to 78.97; 1 study, 207 participants). Most studies did not report adverse effects.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High risk of bias in 29 of 40 included studies (73%), limiting confidence in findings.
- Substantial heterogeneity for VAP incidence (I²=66% for CHX, 74% for povidone-iodine, 84% for saline rinse), likely due to differences in intervention frequency, concentration, application method, and VAP diagnostic criteria.
- Lack of standardized VAP diagnostic criteria across studies; 17 studies used CPIS, 6 used CDC criteria, 5 used Chinese Society of Respiratory Diseases criteria, and several used different definitions.
- Most studies did not report adverse effects systematically; insufficient data to assess safety of interventions.
- No consensus on optimal chlorhexidine concentration, frequency, or application method; subgroup analysis by concentration showed no dose-response relationship.
- Toothbrushing evidence limited by high risk of bias and small number of studies; many trials combined toothbrushing with antiseptics, making it difficult to isolate toothbrushing effect.
- Future research should use CONSORT reporting standards, standardized VAP definitions (e.g., CDC VAE criteria), report adverse events, specify whether deaths are included in duration outcomes, and conduct adequately powered trials.

## 6. Related Work

- Pineda et al. 2006 — Meta-analysis (4 studies) found chlorhexidine did not reduce nosocomial pneumonia; outcome was nosocomial pneumonia rather than VAP.
- Labeau et al. 2011 — Systematic review (14 studies) found antiseptics reduced VAP incidence by approximately one-third, consistent with current review.
- Price et al. 2014 — Network meta-analysis claimed CHX may increase mortality (OR 1.25, 95% CI 1.05 to 1.50); discrepancy with current review due to differences in scope and methodology.
- Klompas et al. 2014 — Systematic review suggested higher CHX concentration associated with higher mortality, but differences were not statistically significant.
- Torres et al. 2017 — ERS/ESICM/ESCMID/ALAT guidelines noted unclear balance between VAP reduction and potential mortality risk with CHX, declined to issue recommendation until more safety data available.

## 7. Glossary

- **VAP (Ventilator-Associated Pneumonia)**: 인공호흡기 관련 폐렴. 기계환기 시작 후 최소 48시간 이후에 발생하는 폐렴으로, 중환자실 환자의 주요 합병증.
- **CHX (Chlorhexidine)**: 클로르헥시딘. 광범위 항균 효과를 가진 구강 소독제로, 구강위생관리에서 VAP 예방을 위해 사용됨.
- **OHC (Oral Hygiene Care)**: 구강위생관리. 치간 청소, 양치질, 구강 세정액 사용 등을 포함한 구강 내 플라그와 이물질 제거 관리.
- **CPIS (Clinical Pulmonary Infection Score)**: 임상 폐감염 점수. VAP 진단에 사용되는 점수 체계로, 흉부 방사선 소견, 체온, 백혈구 수, 기관지 분비물 등을 평가.
- **NNTB (Number Needed to Treat for an Additional Beneficial Outcome)**: 추가 유익 결과를 위해 필요한 치료 수. VAP 1예를 예방하기 위해 CHX로 치료해야 할 환자 수 (본 연구에서 12명).
- **CDC (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention)**: 미국 질병통제예방센터. VAP 감시 기준(VAE)을 개발한 기관.
- **GRADE (Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation)**: 근거 수준 평가 체계. 중등도(moderate), 낮음(low), 매우 낮음(very low)으로 근거 확실성을 평가.
- **ITT (Intention-to-Treat)**: 의도치료분석. 무작위 배정된 모든 참가자를 분석에 포함하는 원칙.
