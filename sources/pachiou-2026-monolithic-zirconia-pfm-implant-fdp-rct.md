## Why Ingested

First RCT with 3-year follow-up directly comparing posterior 3-unit implant-supported FDPs in monolithic zirconia (MonoZR) vs porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM); primary outcome was ceramic fracture. Extends and provides RCT-level evidence complementing [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/pjetursson-2023-implant-fdp-material-design-outcomes-sr-ma]] SR/MA.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT (n=55 patients, 45 completing 3-year follow-up) at University of Zurich; posterior 3-unit iFDPs on two implants randomized to MonoZR (n=28) or PFM (n=27); primary outcome: ceramic fracture.
Veneering ceramic chipping PFM 13% (3/23) vs MonoZR 0% (0/22, p=0.11); 2 prosthesis failures (1 per group, both from implant loss); no significant differences in peri-implant parameters or MBL changes.
MonoZR posterior iFDPs show zero chipping and comparable survival to PFM at 3 years; monolithic design eliminates the dominant technical complication of veneered frameworks.

## 세줄요약

전향적 무작위대조시험(n=55명, 3년 완료 45명), 취리히대; 후방 3-unit 임플란트 고정성 보철(Implant-Supported Fixed Dental Prosthesis, iFDP)을 모놀리식 지르코니아(MonoZR) vs 금속도재(Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal, PFM)로 무작위 배정.
베니어 세라믹 칩핑(Chipping): PFM 13%(3/23) vs MonoZR 0%(0/22, p=0.11); 보철 실패 2건(각 군 1건, 모두 임플란트 소실로 인한 이차 실패); 임플란트 주위 임상 지표 및 변연골수준(Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 변화 군간 유의차 없음.
3년 시점에서 모놀리식 ZrO₂ iFDP는 PFM 대비 칩핑 없이 동등한 생존율을 보여 후방 3-unit 보철의 유력한 대안으로 지지됨.

## Key Contributions

- RCT demonstrating 0% chipping rate with monolithic zirconia vs 13% with PFM for 3-unit posterior iFDPs
- No statistically significant difference in primary outcome (ceramic fracture) at 3 years
- Both materials high survival: MonoZR and PFM comparable overall prosthetic outcomes
- Peri-implant parameters (plaque, bleeding, probing depth) and MBL change: no group differences
- USPHS criteria: high clinical acceptability for both groups

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective RCT
- **Setting**: University of Zurich (single center)
- **n**: 55 patients at baseline; 45 completing 3-year follow-up (MonoZR n=22, PFM n=23)
- **Intervention**: 3-unit iFDP on 2 implants; MonoZR vs PFM; screw- or cement-retained per clinical need
- **Primary outcome**: Ceramic fracture
- **Secondary**: Prosthesis survival, complication-free survival, MBL changes, peri-implant clinical parameters, USPHS ratings
- **Follow-up**: 1 year and 3 years
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier, Fisher's exact test, linear mixed-effects models (α=0.05)

## Limitations

- Single center, relatively small sample size (45 completing follow-up)
- Chipping difference (0 vs 13%) did not reach significance at this sample size; larger trial may confirm
- 3-year medium-term follow-up; longer-term fracture/chipping data needed for zirconia
- Mixture of screw- and cement-retained designs may introduce variability

## Glossary

- **iFDP (Implant-Supported Fixed Dental Prosthesis)**: 임플란트 지지 고정성 치과 보철물
- **MonoZR (Monolithic Zirconia)**: 베니어 도재 없이 지르코니아 블록 전체를 연삭 가공한 단일체 지르코니아
- **PFM (Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal)**: 금속 코핑 위에 도재를 소성한 금속도재 보철
- **USPHS criteria**: 미국 공중보건서비스 보철 임상 평가 기준 (색조·변연적합·표면 등 4개 범주)
- **Complication-free survival**: 어떤 합병증도 없이 원래 상태 유지되는 비율
