## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42016043309; 30 eligible studies, search through November 2016) of 3,049 implants in 1,435 patients (mean age 46.68 years, minimum 6-month follow-up) comparing immediate implants placed in fresh extraction sockets versus delayed implants placed in healed sockets.

Delayed implant survival (98.38%) was significantly higher than immediate implant survival (95.21%, p=.001); no significant difference was found for marginal bone loss (p=.32), implant stability quotient (p=.44), or pocket probing depth (p=.94).

Immediate implants in fresh sockets should be undertaken with caution given the significantly lower survival rate versus delayed implants in healed sockets — this contrasts with later, RCT-restricted reviews reporting no significant survival difference, illustrating how inclusion criteria (observational vs RCT-only) can shift the conclusion on this exact question.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (PROSPERO CRD42016043309; 적격 연구 30편, 2016년 11월까지 검색): 발치와 신선 소켓에 즉시식립한 임플란트 3,049개(환자 1,435명, 평균 46.68세, 최소 6개월 추적)를 치유된 소켓에 지연식립한 임플란트와 비교.

지연식립 생존율(98.38%)이 즉시식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP) 생존율(95.21%)보다 유의하게 높음(p=.001); 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL, p=.32), 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ, p=.44), 치주낭 탐침깊이(p=.94)는 유의차 없음.

신선 소켓 즉시식립은 지연식립 대비 유의하게 낮은 생존율 때문에 신중히 접근해야 함 — 이는 RCT만 포함한 후속 리뷰들의 "생존율 유의차 없음" 결론과 대비되며, 포함 기준(관찰연구 포함 vs RCT 한정)이 같은 질문에 대해 결론을 얼마나 뒤흔들 수 있는지 보여준다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the larger SR+MA cohorts on this exact question at the time: 30 eligible studies, 3,049 implants, 1,435 patients.
- Reports a statistically significant survival disadvantage for immediate implants (95.21%) versus delayed implants (98.38%), p=.001.
- Finds no significant difference between the groups for marginal bone loss, implant stability quotient (ISQ), or pocket probing depth — isolating survival as the differentiating outcome rather than peri-implant tissue health metrics.
- Explicitly recommends caution when placing immediate implants in fresh extraction sockets based on the pooled survival gap.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Two reviewers searched PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, and Cochrane Library independently using different search term sets; articles through November 2016.
- PROSPERO-registered protocol (CRD42016043309).
- 30 eligible studies included; pooled dataset of 3,049 implants across 1,435 patients (mean age 46.68 years); minimum follow-up of 6 months required for inclusion.
- Outcomes analyzed: implant survival rate, marginal bone loss, implant stability quotient (ISQ), pocket probing depth.
- Note: only the PubMed structured abstract was retrieved for this source (no PMC full text available); study-design breakdown of the 30 included studies, statistical model details (fixed vs random effects), and risk-of-bias tool are not available from the abstract alone.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only retrieval**: full text not available (pypdf/PMC), so methodological detail (individual study designs, heterogeneity/I², risk-of-bias assessment, subgroup analyses by arch/tooth position/grafting) could not be extracted — treat quantitative confidence in the pooled survival estimate as unverified beyond what the abstract states.
- Includes both observational and (presumably) randomized studies — the abstract does not specify design mix, which likely explains part of the divergence from later RCT-restricted reviews (e.g., Garcia-Sanchez 2022 in this same wiki batch) that found no significant survival difference.
- No information from the abstract on follow-up duration distribution beyond "minimum 6 months" — longer-term (5-year+) comparative data not summarized here.
- Future work: full-text extraction (if PDF/PMC becomes available) to recover subgroup analyses (arch, socket wall integrity, grafting, immediate vs delayed loading) that would clarify whether the survival gap is uniform or concentrated in specific clinical scenarios.

## 7. Glossary

- **즉시식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP)**: implant placed into a fresh extraction socket at the time of, or shortly after, tooth removal.
- **지연식립 (Delayed Implant Placement)**: implant placed into a healed socket after a period of bone/soft-tissue healing following extraction.
- **변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)**: radiographic loss of crestal bone height around the implant over time.
- **임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)**: resonance frequency analysis (RFA)-derived metric of implant primary/secondary stability.
- **치주낭 탐침깊이 (Pocket Probing Depth, PPD)**: distance from the peri-implant mucosal margin to the base of the probeable sulcus/pocket, used as a peri-implant soft-tissue health indicator.
