## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (25 human third molars, 5 groups) using confocal Raman, ATR-FTIR, EDS, and SEM to quantify native coronal dentin composition and how four clinical endodontic irrigation protocols (NaOCl;

NaOCl/EDTA;

NaOCl/EDTA/CHX; NaOCl/HEDP) alter its organic (collagen/amide), inorganic (phosphate/carbonate apatite, Ca/P), elemental, and ultrastructural (tubule/smear-layer) makeup.

## 세줄요약

사람 관상 상아질의 표준 성분 구성(유기 20wt%·무기 70wt%·수분, 유기의 90%가 제1형 콜라겐)을 정량하고, 4가지 임상 근관세정 프로토콜(NaOCl·NaOCl/EDTA·NaOCl/EDTA/CHX·NaOCl/HEDP)이 콜라겐·인산/탄산 아파타이트·Ca/P·상아세관/smear layer를 어떻게 바꾸는지를 Raman·FTIR·EDS·SEM으로 특성화한 in vitro 연구(사람 제3대구치 25개, 5군).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides a concise, quantified reference for native human dentin composition (20 wt% organic / 70 wt% inorganic / ~10% water; organic = 90 wt% type I collagen + 10% NCPs) and its ultrastructure (peritubular vs intertubular dentin; coronal vs radicular differences).
- Reports baseline (distilled-water control) elemental and vibrational composition of coronal dentin (EDS atomic %, ATR-FTIR band areas, Ca/P 1.87).
- First study to use confocal Raman imaging to map organic (collagen 2875 cm⁻¹) and inorganic (phosphate 960 / carbonate 1070 cm⁻¹) distribution of coronal dentin after endodontic irrigation.
- Directly compares sequential chelation (NaOCl/EDTA, ±CHX) vs continuous/soft chelation (NaOCl/HEDP) on the same teeth (paired mixed-linear model).
- Frames coronal dentin as the primary adhesive substrate — links compositional/ultrastructural change to potential bonding consequences for the final restoration.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 25 intact human third molars; deep coronal dentin discs (finished 0.5 mm) with standardized 600-grit smear layer; each tooth contributed 5 fragments randomized across 5 groups (teeth as random effect).
- Groups: DW control; 3% NaOCl (37°C, 30 min); NaOCl + 17% EDTA (1 min); NaOCl + EDTA + 2% CHX (2 min); 3% NaOCl/9% HEDP mixture (37°C, 30 min, refreshed q20min).
- Confocal Raman microscopy (n=1/group), ATR-FTIR (n=22; normalized to amide I; amide II, phosphate, carbonate; phosphate/amide II & carbonate/phosphate ratios), EDS (n=4; C, N, O, P, Ca; Ca/P), SEM (2500×/10,000×).
- Mixed linear model, α = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Native (control) EDS atomic %: C 19.41±1.40, N 5.36±0.58, O 47.63±0.97, P 9.23±0.55, Ca 17.20±0.36, Ca/P 1.87±0.08. FTIR: amide II 20.39, phosphate 1205.87, carbonate 31.00, phosphate/amide II 60.17, carbonate/phosphate 0.026.
- NaOCl alone → "ghost mineral layer": amide II 1.67 (lowest, maximal deproteinization), phosphate 8980.99 (highest mineral), C 9.70/N 2.91 lowest, Ca 20.59/P 10.83 highest; hypermineralized, collagen-depleted; dense smear layer, obliterated tubules (SEM).
- NaOCl/EDTA → demineralizes, re-exposes collagen (amide II 15.25; C 17.42/N 6.63 rise); mineral back near control; opens tubules, peritubular erosion; Ca/P 1.99 (disturbed).
- NaOCl/EDTA/CHX → intermediate collagen (CHX binds collagen, lowers amide II vs EDTA to 10.47); Ca/P 1.99; ultrastructure similar to NaOCl/EDTA.
- NaOCl/HEDP (continuous chelation) → intermediate collagen (amide II 11.56); weaker demineralization than EDTA despite 30 min vs 1 min; Ca/P 1.87 (like control); highest surface roughness.
- Ca/P preserved (~1.87, natural proportion) in DW/NaOCl/HEDP; disturbed (↑1.99) only by EDTA-containing sequences.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro; single concentration and single contact time per irrigant; 30-min single-session simulation (multi-session could worsen effects).
- No irrigant agitation/activation or preheating variables; surface roughness not quantified; Raman only n=1/group (qualitative mapping).
- Third molars from a narrow age band (16–40 yr); does not capture aged/sclerotic dentin.
- Bonding/adhesion consequences are inferred, not measured (Part I of a series; adhesion is deferred).

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary

- **Amide I/II/III (FTIR)**: vibrational bands of type I collagen polypeptide chains; proxy for organic/protein content.
- **Phosphate (960/1010 cm⁻¹), carbonate (871/1070 cm⁻¹)**: apatite bands; proxy for inorganic/mineral content.
- **Ca/P ratio**: calcium-to-phosphorus atomic ratio (~1.87 in native dentin); index of mineral proportionality; disturbed by strong chelation.
- **Peritubular vs intertubular dentin**: highly mineralized cuff lining each tubule vs the mineralized collagen matrix between tubules.
- **Ghost mineral layer**: mineral-rich, collagen-sparse surface left by NaOCl deproteinization.
- **Soft/continuous chelation**: NaOCl mixed with a weak chelator (HEDP) used throughout instrumentation, vs sequential EDTA after NaOCl.
- **Smear layer / smear plug**: instrumentation debris film on dentin / debris extending into tubule lumen.
