## Why Ingested

[[implants/isq/herrero-climent-2013-osstell-isq-reliability-icc]]가 단일 검사자 반복 측정(ICC=0.97)으로 ISQ 재현성을 확인했지만, 임상 현장에서 실질적으로 문제가 되는 **복수 검사자 간** 신뢰도(inter-rater reliability)와 ISQ 외 다른 안정성 지표(ISV, 방사선, BIC, ITBD)와의 비교 데이터가 없었다. 본 동물 연구는 5가지 측정법을 5명의 훈련된 검사자가 동일 임플란트에 적용해 ICC를 비교함으로써 측정도구별 검사자 간 재현성의 상대적 위계를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Animal study (1 beagle, 6 implants, 5 examiners) evaluated inter-rater reliability of five implant stability measurement methods — ISQ, ISV, radiographic marginal bone level, BIC, and ITBD — using ICC with a two-way random-effects model.

Histometric parameters showed highest reliability (ITBD ICC=0.854, BIC ICC=0.806); ISQ had moderate-high reliability (ICC=0.774); radiograph moderate (ICC=0.638); ISV was the least reliable (ICC=0.447); all p<0.05.

Non-invasive clinical methods (ISQ, radiograph) provide acceptable inter-rater reproducibility; ISV (EasyCheck) is unreliable across examiners; histomorphometry is gold-standard for reproducibility but is destructive and impractical in vivo.

## 세줄요약

1마리의 비글견(임플란트 6개)에 5명의 훈련된 검사자를 배정해 ISQ, ISV, 방사선, BIC, ITBD 등 5가지 임플란트 안정성 측정법의 검사자 간 신뢰도를 ICC로 비교한 동물 연구다.

조직계측 변수가 가장 높은 신뢰도를 보였으며(ITBD ICC=0.854, BIC ICC=0.806), ISQ는 중등도-높음(ICC=0.774), 방사선은 중등도(ICC=0.638), ISV는 낮음(ICC=0.447)으로 모두 유의하였다(p<0.05).

비침습적 임상 도구 중 ISQ는 허용 가능한 검사자 간 재현성을 가지지만 ISV는 신뢰도가 낮으며, 조직계측은 재현성이 가장 우수하나 생체 내에서는 적용이 불가능하다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: J Implantol Appl Sci, 29(4):177-192
- **DOI**: 10.32542/implantology.2025019
- **Institution**: Pusan National University, Yangsan, Korea
- **Study design**: Animal experimental study (in vivo)
- **n**: 1 beagle dog, 6 implants, 5 examiners
- **Follow-up**: 8 weeks post-implantation

## 2. Key Contributions

- First multi-method ICC comparison of implant stability tools across trained examiners in a controlled in vivo model
- Demonstrates that histometric parameters (BIC, ITBD) are most reproducible but invasive
- Establishes ISQ (Osstell Mentor) as the most reliable non-invasive tool among tested clinical methods
- Identifies ISV (EasyCheck) as poorly reproducible across examiners — a clinically significant warning
- Provides reliability hierarchy: ITBD > BIC > ISQ > radiograph > ISV

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Animal model**: 1 beagle dog; 6 standard titanium implants placed in healed mandibular extraction sites
- **Examiners**: 5 junior dental clinicians trained by standardized protocol
- **Measurement methods**:
  - ISQ: Osstell Mentor (RFA-based)
  - ISV: EasyCheck (resonance frequency alternative device)
  - Radiographic MBL: standardized periapical radiographs
  - BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact, 골-임플란트 접촉률): histomorphometry
  - ITBD (Inter-Thread Bone Density, 나사산간 골밀도): histomorphometry
- **Statistical analysis**: ICC with 95% CI, two-way random-effects model; Shapiro-Wilk normality test
- **Sacrifice/histology**: 8 weeks post-implantation

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Method | ICC | 95% CI | Reliability Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| ITBD | 0.854 | — | Very high |
| BIC | 0.806 | — | High |
| ISQ (Osstell) | 0.774 | — | Moderate-high |
| Radiograph | 0.638 | — | Moderate |
| ISV (EasyCheck) | 0.447 | — | Low |

- All ICC values statistically significant (p<0.05)
- Histometric parameters (BIC, ITBD) showed highest inter-rater agreement
- ISV was the only method classified as having low reliability (ICC<0.5)
- ISQ demonstrated adequate clinical reproducibility across five trained examiners

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Very small sample (1 animal, 6 implants) — limits generalizability
- Single animal model (beagle) may not represent human bone quality or implant site variability
- Junior examiners only — experienced clinician inter-rater data absent
- No clinical (human) validation component
- Limited repetitions per examiner per method
- Cross-method correlation (e.g., ISQ vs BIC) not analyzed

## 7. Glossary

- **ISQ (임플란트 안정성 지수, Implant Stability Quotient)**: RFA-derived 1–100 scale from Osstell Mentor
- **ISV (임플란트 안정성 값, Implant Stability Value)**: Resonance frequency value from EasyCheck device
- **BIC (골-임플란트 접촉률, Bone-to-Implant Contact)**: % of implant surface in direct bone contact (histomorphometry)
- **ITBD (나사산간 골밀도, Inter-Thread Bone Density)**: % bone fill between implant threads (histomorphometry)
- **ICC (급내 상관계수, Intraclass Correlation Coefficient)**: reliability statistic; >0.75 = good, >0.90 = excellent
