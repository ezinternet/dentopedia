## Why Ingested

근관치료 치아(ETT)에 라미네이트 비니어 적용 가능성 질문에 답하는 핵심 임상 근거. 기존 위키 [[veneers/klein-2025-ceramic-laminate-veneer-survival-complications-sr-ma]] 및 [[veneers/morimoto-2016-feldspathic-glass-ceramic-veneer-survival-sr-ma]]가 ETT를 제외한 연구 모음임에 반해, 이 연구는 43개 ETT 포함 11년 전향적 시험으로 vital vs. non-vital 생존율을 직접 비교한 유일한 장기 전향적 데이터.

## One-line Summary

Prospective clinical trial (n=384 feldspathic LVs, 104 patients, 11 years) found no significant difference in survival between vital (95.6%) and endodontically treated (88.1%) teeth, but ETT showed significant color mismatch; IDS significantly improved survival when dentin exposure >50%.

## 한줄요약

전향적 임상시험 (384개 장석계 LV, 104명, 11년): 생활치(95.6%) vs. 근관치료 치아(88.1%) 생존율 유의차 없음; 상아질 50% 초과 노출 시 IDS 적용이 생존율 유의 향상(96.4% vs. 81.8%); ETT는 색조 불일치 유의 증가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First prospective long-term trial to include endodontically treated teeth (n=43) without exclusion
- Demonstrated IDS significantly improved survival in teeth with >50% dentin exposure (96.4% vs. 81.8%)
- Showed no significant survival difference between vital and non-vital teeth — not a contraindication for LV
- Identified color mismatch as the primary clinical challenge specific to ETT
- Confirmed pre-existing composite restorations do not significantly affect LV survival

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design**: Prospective clinical trial (single-center, Groningen, Netherlands)
- **Population**: 104 patients, mean age 42.1 years
- **Sample**: 384 feldspathic ceramic laminate veneers on maxillary anterior teeth
- **Subgroups**:
  - 225 teeth: no pre-existing restorations
  - 159 teeth: pre-existing resin composite (conditioned with silica coating + silanization; not removed)
  - 87 teeth: >50% dentin exposure during preparation → IDS applied
  - 43 teeth: endodontically treated (not excluded)
- **Preparation design**: Incisal overlap using mock-up technique
- **IDS protocol**: Applied when >50% dentin surface exposed during preparation
- **Follow-up**: 11 years
- **Outcome assessment**: Modified USPHS criteria at baseline and final follow-up; calibrated evaluators

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Total failures**: 19 (debonding n=3, fracture n=15, extraction due to endodontic complications n=1)
- **IDS benefit** (>50% dentin exposure): 96.4% vs. 81.8% survival (statistically significant)
- **Vital vs. non-vital (ETT)**: 95.6% vs. 88.1% (NOT statistically significant)
- **Pre-existing composite vs. none**: 84.6% vs. 95.5% (NOT statistically significant)
- **ETT color mismatch**: Significantly greater vs. vital teeth
- **Smokers**: Significantly more marginal discoloration (no intervention required)
- **Patient satisfaction (OHIP)**: Favorable across all subgroups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single material type (feldspathic ceramic); results may not generalize to lithium disilicate or zirconia
- Single-center study limits external validity
- ETT subgroup small (n=43); power to detect significant differences limited
- IDS protocol confounds dentin-exposure subgroup analysis (by design)

## 6. Related Work

- [[veneers/morimoto-2016-feldspathic-glass-ceramic-veneer-survival-sr-ma]] — SR+MA confirming ~89% 9-year feldspathic survival; this trial extends to 11 years with IDS subgroup analysis
- [[veneers/beier-2012-porcelain-veneers-nonvital-bruxism-20year]] — contradicts this study: found ETT significantly higher failure risk (P=0.0012) using older protocols (1987–2009)
- [[veneers/etienne-2025-ceramic-veneers-dentin-vitality-15year]] — 2025 retrospective (15y) also finds no significant ETT vs vital difference; aligns with this study

## 7. Glossary

- **IDS** — Immediate Dentin Sealing: adhesive applied directly after tooth preparation before impression, before cementation
- **USPHS** — United States Public Health Service criteria for clinical restoration evaluation
- **OHIP** — Oral Health Impact Profile: patient-reported quality-of-life questionnaire
- **ETT** — Endodontically Treated Teeth
