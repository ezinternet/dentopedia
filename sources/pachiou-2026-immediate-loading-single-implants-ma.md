## One-line Summary

De novo RCT-level MA (J Prosthet Dent 2026): 40 RCTs, >1200 single implants; immediate loading shows comparable survival, marginal bone loss, patient satisfaction, and complications to early or conventional loading at 1–5 years (moderate certainty for survival).

## 한줄요약

## Three-line Summary

De novo RCT-level meta-analysis, 40 unique RCTs (>1200 single implants) from 7 SRs + 2 additional RCTs, databases through October 2025 — comparing immediate vs early/conventional loading of single implants at 1–5 years with time-stratified analysis.

Immediate loading showed comparable implant survival, marginal bone loss, patient satisfaction, and complication rates to early and conventional loading at all follow-up intervals (1–5 years); GRADE certainty for survival was moderate.

Overlap among included reviews was quantified (CCA=11.5%); time-stratified analyses are a strength; moderate GRADE certainty acknowledges remaining RCT heterogeneity.

## 세줄요약

De novo RCT 수준 메타분석, 40편 RCT (>1200개 단일 임플란트), 7개 SR + 2편 추가 RCT, 2025년 10월까지 — 단일 임플란트 즉시 vs 조기/지연 로딩 1–5년 시기별 비교.

즉시 로딩이 1–5년 모든 구간에서 생존율·MBL·환자 만족도·합병증 모두 조기/지연 로딩과 동등; 생존율 GRADE 근거 수준 중등.

SR 간 중복 정량화 (CCA=11.5%); 시기별 분석이 강점; 중등 GRADE는 잔존 RCT 이질성 반영.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First de novo RCT-level MA to re-extract all eligible primary RCTs from 7 SRs + 2 additional RCTs, eliminating double-counting via Corrected Covered Area (CCA=11.5%)
- Provides time-stratified survival analysis (1, 2, 3, 5 years) for immediate vs conventional and immediate vs early loading
- Confirms GRADE moderate certainty that immediate loading has equivalent survival to conventional loading across all follow-up intervals (1–5 years)
- Demonstrates MBL differences are clinically negligible at all time points
- Patient-reported outcomes and complication rates comparable between protocols

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Study type: De novo RCT-level MA with contextual SR synthesis
- Databases: PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Epistemonikos, Cochrane (search to October 2025)
- Eligibility: SRs restricted to RCTs comparing immediate vs early/conventional loading of single implants
- 7 SRs identified → all eligible primary RCTs re-extracted to avoid double counting
- Overlap quantified: CCA = 11.5% (high overlap among reviews)
- Meta-analyses: random-effects; RR for survival/complications; MD for MBL; SMD for patient-reported outcomes
- Follow-up intervals analyzed separately: 1, 2, 3, 5 years
- Evidence quality: GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Immediate vs Conventional loading — implant survival:**
- 1 year: RR 1.00 (95% CI 0.98–1.02)
- 2 years: RR 1.01 (95% CI 0.96–1.05)
- 3 years: RR 1.01 (95% CI 0.97–1.05)
- 5 years: RR 1.00 (95% CI 0.97–1.03)
- All non-significant; **moderate certainty**

**Immediate vs Early loading — implant survival:**
- 1 year: RR 0.99 (95% CI 0.96–1.02); non-significant; moderate certainty

**Marginal bone loss:** Differences minimal and clinically negligible at all time points

**Patient satisfaction:** No significant difference between protocols

**Prosthetic/biologic complications:** Infrequent in both groups; no significant intergroup difference

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- GRADE certainty for secondary outcomes (MBL, patient satisfaction, complications) rated low to very low
- High CCA (11.5%) among systematic reviews indicates substantial trial overlap in published literature
- Few long-term (>5 year) RCTs with immediate loading
- Most RCTs focused on anterior/premolar zones under standard conditions — immediate loading in molar regions or challenging anatomy underrepresented

## 7. Glossary

- **De novo MA**: original meta-analysis re-extracting primary trial data rather than pooling SR summary statistics
- **CCA**: Corrected Covered Area — measure of trial overlap among systematic reviews; high CCA=11.5% here
- **Immediate loading**: implant loaded (provisional restoration placed) within 1 week of placement
- **Early loading**: loading 1 week to 2 months post-placement
- **Conventional loading**: loading after ≥2 months healing
