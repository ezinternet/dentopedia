## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 8 RCTs (181 patients, 86% female) testing low-level GaAlAs laser (780-940 nm) against sham, placebo or drug comparators for myofascial pain-type TMD, searched to April 2020.

Pooled VAS pain difference at end of treatment was borderline (WMD = -0.76, 95% CI -1.51 to 0.01, P = .046) and gone at 3-4 weeks (WMD = 1.24, 95% CI -0.04 to 2.51); maximum mouth opening did not change (WMD = -0.03 mm, 95% CI -4.13 to 4.06; 3-4 weeks WMD = 1.22, 95% CI -2.94 to 5.39).

The abstract concludes evidence is insufficient to show efficacy of GaAlAs laser for TMD pain or mouth opening; very high heterogeneity (I2 about 89%), small trials and allocation-concealment problems limit any firm recommendation.

## 세줄요약

8편의 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 181명, 여성 86%)을 대상으로 근막통형 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에서 저출력 갈륨알루미늄비소 (Gallium Aluminium Arsenide, GaAlAs) 레이저 (780-940 nm)를 위약·가짜·약물 대조와 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (2020년 4월까지 검색).

치료 종료 시 시각통증척도 (Visual Analogue Scale, VAS) 통합 차이는 경계 수준 (가중평균차 = -0.76, 95% CI -1.51 ~ 0.01, P = .046)이었고 3-4주 후에는 사라졌으며 (1.24, 95% CI -0.04 ~ 2.51), 최대개구량 (Maximum Mouth Opening, MMO)은 변화 없었음 (-0.03 mm, 95% CI -4.13 ~ 4.06; 3-4주 1.22, 95% CI -2.94 ~ 5.39).

초록은 GaAlAs 레이저의 TMD 통증·개구 개선 효과를 뒷받침할 근거가 불충분하다고 결론; 매우 높은 이질성 (I2 약 89%), 소규모 시험, 배정은폐 결함 때문에 강한 권고는 불가.

- Journal: Medicine (Baltimore) 2021;100(52):e28015. Published 2021-12-30.

- Affiliations: Southwest University and Southwest University Hospital, Chongqing, China.
- Type: systematic review with meta-analysis of RCTs (Cochrane ROB tool v5.1.0; PRISMA stated).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Restricts the laser question to one laser class (GaAlAs, class IIIb) and to myofascial-pain TMD diagnosed by RDC/TMD axis 1.
- Separates end-of-treatment from 3-4 week follow-up, showing the pain effect is not maintained.
- Reports no MMO benefit at either time point.
- Identifies dose heterogeneity (energy density 2.5 to 112.5 J/cm2 across trials) as a likely reason for inconsistent laser results.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: PubMed, EMBASE, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, ClinicalTrials.gov, inception to April 5, 2020; English RCTs only.
- Inclusion: RCT, GaAlAs laser as intervention, RDC/TMD axis 1. Exclusion: other lasers, fibromyalgia, osteoarthritis, injury, incomplete data.
- Comparators: sham/placebo in 6 trials; piroxicam or naproxen in 2.
- Outcomes: VAS pain (primary), MMO (secondary). RevMan 5.3 and Stata 15.1; fixed-effects if I2 < 50%, else random-effects; sensitivity analysis and funnel plot.
- Screening by two examiners with a third resolving disagreements.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 85 records retrieved, 8 RCTs (2006-2020), 181 patients, 25 (13.8%) male; per-trial n from 9 to 60.
- VAS pain, all 8 studies: I2 = 89%; after removing Khalighi 2016 and Cetiner 2006, 6 studies in a fixed-effects model gave a significant end-of-treatment difference (P = .046). Abstract estimate: WMD = -0.76, 95% CI -1.51 to 0.01.
- VAS at 3-4 weeks: 4 remaining studies, WMD = 1.24, 95% CI -0.04 to 2.51, P = .057 (not significant).
- MMO end of treatment: 2 studies, WMD = -0.03, 95% CI -4.13 to 4.06, P = .987, I2 = 0%; at 3-4 weeks WMD = 1.22, 95% CI -2.94 to 5.39, P = .565.
- Risk of bias: 5/8 adequate sequence generation, 2/8 allocation concealment, 7/8 low-risk blinding.
- Funnel plot reported symmetric.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (181), mostly female, several trials with n under 25.
- Pooled estimates rest on 2 to 6 trials after post hoc exclusion of the most heterogeneous ones; the excluded trials were dropped by sensitivity analysis, not pre-specified criteria.
- The abstract CI for the end-of-treatment VAS (-1.51 to 0.01) touches zero while P = .046 is reported; the text does not give the estimate for the 6-study fixed-effects model separately, so which model the abstract number comes from is not clear.
- The abstract says three reviewers screened; the Methods describe two plus an arbiter.
- The Conclusions paragraph says laser is superior at end of treatment with "moderate evidence", while the abstract says evidence is insufficient; the two framings should be read together, as borderline.
- Funnel-plot symmetry with 8 trials is weak evidence against publication bias.
- No GRADE certainty rating reported.
- Variation in wavelength, dose, session count and sham technique was not modeled.

## 7. Glossary

- GaAlAs laser: semiconductor diode laser emitting near-infrared light (780-940 nm in these trials).
- LLLT: low-level laser therapy.
- WMD: weighted mean difference.
- MMO: maximum mouth opening.
- RDC/TMD axis 1: physical diagnostic criteria for TMD.
- Sham laser: inactive probe applied with the same contact.
