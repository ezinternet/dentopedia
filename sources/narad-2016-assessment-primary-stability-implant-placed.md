## Three-line Summary

Case series (n=24 immediate implants) placed into debrided infected sockets (subacute periodontal infection, perio-endo infection, chronic periapical infection, periodontal cyst, traumatic infected teeth), follow-up at least 24 months.

All 24 implants were stable at 24 months with no clinical mobility or infection; radiographic crestal bone loss occurred during osseointegration and settled at the first thread.

Survival appears predictable with meticulous debridement plus pre-/post-operative antibiotics, but abstract-only data, no control group and no quantified stability values limit inference.

## 세줄요약

증례군 (n=24 즉시식립 임플란트): 소파한 감염 발치와 (아급성 치주감염, 치주-근관 병소, 만성 치근단 감염, 치주낭종, 외상성 감염치)에 즉시식립, 최소 24개월 추적.

24개월 시점에 24개 전부 동요·감염 없이 안정; 방사선상 치조정 골소실은 골유착 기간 중 발생해 첫 나사산 수준에서 안정화.

철저한 소파와 수술 전후 항생제 병용 시 생존이 예측 가능해 보이나, 초록만 확보·대조군 없음·안정성 수치 미제시로 해석에 한계.

- Reports immediate implants in sockets with subacute periodontal infection, perio-endo infection, chronic periapical infection, periodontal cyst and traumatic infected teeth after meticulous debridement.
- Reports 24/24 implants stable at 24 months without mobility or infection.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

24 implants immediately placed in prepared infected sockets; protocol emphasized meticulous debridement with pre- and post-surgical antibiotic therapy; follow-up at least 24 months (clinical mobility/infection and radiographic crestal bone). Abstract does not state prospective vs retrospective design, implant system, antibiotic regimen, or how "subacute" was defined. Full text not retrieved.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 24/24 implants stable at 24 months, no clinical mobility, no infection.
- Radiographic crestal bone loss during osseointegration, settling at the level of the first thread (no numbers in abstract).
- Despite the title ("primary stability"), the abstract reports no primary-stability measurements.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only; no numbers for bone loss or stability; no comparator; small n; design and definitions of infection types not available.
- Title-content mismatch: title refers to primary stability, abstract outcomes are survival and crestal bone.
