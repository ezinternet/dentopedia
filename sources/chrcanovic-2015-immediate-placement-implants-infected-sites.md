## Three-line Summary

SR (PubMed, 706 references → 28 included human/animal studies), Sweden/Brazil; immediate implant placement into periodontally/endodontically infected sites; protocol requirements for decontamination reviewed.

High survival rates in multiple studies support viable osseointegration in infected sites when meticulous socket cleaning, curettage/debridement, and 0.12% chlorhexidine rinse are performed.

Evidence limited by heterogeneous infection classification and lack of RCTs; further randomized trials needed.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Comprehensive SR of human + animal studies for immediate implant placement into infected sites
- 706 → 28 included studies (after excluding case reports and reviews)
- Identified minimum protocol requirements: socket curettage, debridement, CHX 0.12%
- Highlighted classification heterogeneity across studies (periodontal vs endodontic vs combined)
- Systematic gap analysis: no RCTs at the time of this review (March 2013 search)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review
- **Search**: PubMed, March 2013; 706 references initially
- **Inclusion**: Human and animal studies; implant placement into sites with periapical/endodontic/periodontal infection
- **Exclusion**: Review articles, case reports
- **Additional hand-search**: 5 additional papers from reference lists
- **Final included**: 28 studies (32 initially identified, minus 9 case reports/reviews + 5 added)
- **Outcomes**: Survival rate, osseointegration, protocol requirements

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- High survival rates in multiple studies when decontamination protocols applied
- Required protocol elements identified: (1) meticulous cleaning, (2) curettage/debridement, (3) CHX 0.12% rinse
- Infection classification heterogeneous across studies — limits pooling
- No RCTs available at time of search (March 2013)
- Animal studies generally showed favorable histomorphometric results

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No meta-analysis (insufficient homogeneity)
- Abstract-only — full protocol extraction not available
- Superseded by larger SRs (Saijeva 2020, Pranckeviciene 2024) with more studies

## 7. Glossary

- **Periapical infection**: Inflammation/infection at the apex of a tooth root, typically from pulpal necrosis
- **Perioendodontic lesion**: Combined periodontal + endodontic infection
- **Decontamination protocol**: Systematic sequence to reduce bacterial load before implant placement
