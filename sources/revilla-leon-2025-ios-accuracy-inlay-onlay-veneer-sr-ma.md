## Why Ingested
프라임스캔(Primescan)의 인레이·온레이·비니어 적응증에서의 디지털 인상 정확도를 종합한 SR+MA. 기존 단일 in vitro 연구를 가로지르는 최초 메타분석으로, 위키의 [[digital-workflow/sanioglu-2026-preparation-design-ios-trueness]] (삭제 형태 의존성) 및 [[digital-workflow/primescan-clinical-performance-overview]] 오버뷰의 인레이·온레이 근거 축을 직접 보강한다.

## One-line Summary
SR+MA (34 studies): Primescan achieved the best inlay trueness (12.29 µm) among tested IOS; impression method did not significantly affect marginal discrepancy of inlay/onlay restorations.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (34편): 인레이 진도성 프라임스캔 최우수(12.29 µm); 인레이·온레이 변연 불일치는 디지털·전통 인상 간 유의차 없음.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Journal of Esthetic and Restorative Dentistry, 37(3):727–755
- Published: March 2025 (electronic December 11, 2024)
- Funding/COI: Not reported in abstract

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA specifically quantifying IOS trueness for inlay, onlay, and veneer preparations
- Primescan demonstrates the best inlay trueness (12.29 µm) among all IOS evaluated; TRIOS 3: 38.39 µm; Omnicam: 69.34 µm
- Restoration type significantly influences virtual cast accuracy (inlay better than onlay)
- Impression method (IOS vs conventional) does not significantly affect marginal discrepancy of inlay/onlay restorations
- Insufficient data to evaluate IOS accuracy for veneer restorations — identified as research gap

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR + MA (PRISMA-compliant)
- Databases: PubMed/Medline, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane + manual search
- 34 articles included: 17 analyzing virtual cast accuracy, 17 analyzing restoration fit (marginal/internal discrepancy)
- Quality appraisal: Joanna Briggs Institute critical appraisal tool, two independent investigators + third for consensus
- Primary outcomes: (1) trueness/precision of digital casts; (2) marginal and internal discrepancy of fabricated restorations
- Subgroup analyses by IOS type and restoration type (inlay vs onlay)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Virtual cast trueness (IOS subgroup, inlays):**
- Primescan: **12.29 µm** (p<0.001)
- TRIOS 3: 38.39 µm (p<0.001)
- CS3500: 28.90 µm (p<0.001)
- TRIOS (unspecified): 52.96 µm (p<0.001)
- Omnicam: 69.34 µm (p<0.001)

**Overall virtual cast accuracy:**
- Inlay trueness: 27.47 µm (p<0.001); onlay trueness: 64.15 µm (p<0.001)
- Inlay precision: 19.88 µm (p<0.001); onlay precision: 19.69 µm (p<0.001)
- Omnicam onlay trueness: 53.00 µm (I²=99%, p<0.001)

**Restoration fit:**
- Marginal discrepancy: NS difference conventional vs IOS (p=0.06; I²=99% — high heterogeneity)
- Internal discrepancy: significant difference (p<0.001); higher in conventional method; I²=72%

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- High heterogeneity (I²=99%) in marginal discrepancy meta-analysis limits conclusions
- Insufficient studies on veneer restoration accuracy using IOS — cannot draw conclusions
- Included studies vary in preparation geometry, tooth material, operator experience
- Most studies in vitro — clinical translation requires further research

## 6. Related Work
- Sanioglu 2026: preparation design as dominant IOS accuracy factor (inter-scanner differences within acceptable range)
- Wildhagen 2026 / Almalki 2024: IOS accuracy for narrow deep preparations (post space) — analogous to proximal box depth findings

## 7. Glossary
- **Trueness**: mean deviation from reference (systematic error)
- **Precision**: inter-scan repeatability (random error)
- **Virtual cast**: digital stone model equivalent (STL output of IOS)
- **Marginal discrepancy**: gap at restoration margin after cementation simulation
- **Internal discrepancy**: gap at occlusal/axial wall internal surface
