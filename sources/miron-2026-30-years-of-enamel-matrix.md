## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (SR+MA; J Periodontal Res 2026; RCTs to April 15, 2025; RoB2) of enamel matrix derivative (EMD) across four indications: 67 RCTs (9 nonsurgical, 41 intrabony, 4 furcation, 13 recession), 2552 participants, 3521 defects.

In intrabony defects EMD added 1.00 mm PPD reduction and 1.14 mm CAL gain over open flap debridement (OFD); EMD plus bone graft added nothing significant except +0.79 CAL with xenografts; root coverage gains were non-significant, nonsurgical adjunct gave +0.30 mm PPD and no CAL benefit.

EMD is supported for intrabony regeneration; evidence for nonsurgical, root coverage and furcation use remains limited and needs higher-quality RCTs (abstract-only: no heterogeneity or bias detail retrieved).

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (SR+MA; J Periodontal Res 2026; 2025-04-15까지 RCT; RoB2): 법랑기질유도체 (Enamel Matrix Derivative, EMD)의 4개 적응증 — 67 RCT (비수술 9, 골내결손 41, 치근이개부 4, 퇴축 13), 참여자 2552명, 결손 3521개.

골내결손에서 EMD는 개방 판막 소파술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD) 대비 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 1.00 mm, 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 1.14 mm 추가 이득; 골이식 병용은 이종골 병용 시 CAL +0.79 mm 외 유의 이득 없음; 치근 피개는 비유의, 비수술 보조는 PPD +0.30 mm·CAL 이득 없음.

골내결손 재생에서만 근거가 뒷받침되며 비수술·치근 피개·치근이개부는 근거가 제한적 (초록 전용: 이질성·편향 세부 미확인).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Pools EMD across four indications in one review (nonsurgical, intrabony, furcation, recession)
- Reviews biological foundations (new cementum, PDL and alveolar bone on dry, conditioned root surfaces with minimal blood contamination)
- Tests EMD plus bone graft combinations and EMD versus non-resorbable membranes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA of RCTs, random-effects; two independent reviewers; RoB2
- **Search**: RCTs up to April 15, 2025 (databases not named in the abstract)
- **n**: 67 RCTs; 2552 participants; 3521 defects
- **Outcomes**: PPD reduction, CAL gain (abstract names these; recession outcomes not specified)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Intrabony, EMD vs OFD: +1.00 mm PPD reduction; +1.14 mm CAL gain
- EMD + bone graft vs graft alone: no significant improvement, except +0.79 CAL with xenografts
- Non-resorbable membranes: 1.08 mm greater PPD reduction than EMD
- Root coverage: modest, non-significant improvements; substantial heterogeneity
- Nonsurgical adjunct vs subgingival instrumentation: +0.30 mm PPD reduction; no CAL enhancement
- Furcation: evidence limited

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only; I², risk-of-bias counts and certainty ratings not retrieved
- Root coverage pooled results heterogeneous
- Authors call for high-quality RCTs for nonsurgical, root coverage and furcation, and for optimized protocols and novel combinations
