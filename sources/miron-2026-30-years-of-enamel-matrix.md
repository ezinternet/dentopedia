## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (SR+MA; J Periodontal Res 2026; PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO-registered; RCTs searched to April 15, 2025; RoB 2; GRADE) of enamel matrix derivative (EMD) across four indications: 67 RCTs (9 nonsurgical, 41 intrabony, 4 furcation, 13 recession) from 4406 records, 2552 participants, 3521 defects.

In intrabony defects EMD added 1.00 mm PPD reduction (95% CI 0.63-1.38) and 1.14 mm CAL gain (0.83-1.46) over open flap debridement (OFD; 20 RCTs, I2 87%); nonsurgical adjunct gave +0.30 mm PPD but a non-significant +0.21 mm CAL, root coverage and EMD-vs-graft/membrane comparisons were mostly non-significant, and GRADE certainty was very low for every intrabony comparison.

EMD is supported for intrabony regeneration versus OFD, but the review's abstract, Results and Discussion disagree on the EMD plus xenograft effect (see warning), so the graft-combination question is unresolved; furcation, nonsurgical and root coverage use need higher-quality RCTs.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (SR+MA; J Periodontal Res 2026; PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO 등록; 2025-04-15까지 RCT; RoB 2; GRADE): 법랑기질유도체 (Enamel Matrix Derivative, EMD)의 4개 적응증 — 검색 4406건에서 67 RCT (비수술 9, 골내결손 41, 치근이개부 4, 퇴축 13), 참여자 2552명, 결손 3521개.

골내결손에서 EMD는 개방 판막 소파술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD) 대비 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 1.00 mm (95% CI 0.63-1.38), 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 1.14 mm (0.83-1.46) 추가 이득 (20 RCT, I2 87%); 비수술 보조는 PPD +0.30 mm이나 CAL +0.21 mm는 비유의, 치근 피개와 EMD 대 이식재·차폐막 비교는 대부분 비유의, 골내결손 비교 전부 GRADE 근거 확실성 매우 낮음.

OFD 대비 골내결손 재생은 EMD가 뒷받침되나, 이종골 병용 효과는 초록·결과·고찰이 서로 어긋나 (경고 참조) 이식재 병용 질문은 미해결이며, 치근이개부·비수술·치근 피개는 더 높은 질의 RCT가 필요.

> [!warning] Internal discrepancy in the paper (abstract vs Results vs Discussion)
> On EMD plus bone graft versus EMD alone, three sections of the same paper give three versions. **Abstract:** no significant improvement "except greater CAL gain (0.79) when combined with xenografts". **Results 5.4.3:** pooled EMD+BG vs EMD gave PPD +0.26 mm (95% CI -0.02 to 0.53, ns) and CAL +0.33 mm (0.02-0.64, significant); no significant subgroup differences by graft type; some xenograft/alloplast studies showed *reduced* CAL gain vs EMD alone (small, ns); the figure 0.79 does not appear in Results. **Discussion 6.2.2:** states EMD vs EMD+BG "did not lead to significant gains in PPD or CAL", yet also that subgroup differences in CAL by graft type were statistically significant, with EMD + xenograft showing *reduced* CAL gain. These cannot all be true; this page reports the Results values as the primary data and does not adjudicate. Publication bias was strongly suspected for this comparison (GRADE very low).

## 2. Key Contributions
- One pooled review of EMD across nonsurgical therapy, intrabony regeneration, furcation regeneration and root coverage, with GRADE per comparison
- Brief narrative of biological foundations: enamel matrix proteins (EMPs) found on developing root surface before cementum formation; EMD = porcine-derived amelogenin-rich preparation (amelogenins >90% of protein)
- Preclinical handling data: EMD needs about 3-5 min contact to adsorb onto graft particles and blood impairs binding (premix >=5 min blood-free); on root surfaces adsorption needs a dried (and EDTA-conditioned) blood-free surface; human histology shows EMD persisting up to 4 weeks with early collagen-rich tissue by 2-6 weeks
- Intrabony benchmark vs OFD: +1.00 mm PPD, +1.14 mm CAL
- Head-to-head intrabony comparisons: EMD vs GTR, EMD +/- bone graft, EMD +/- GTR

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA of RCTs (English, any year); PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD420251088129
- **Search**: PubMed (Medline), Scopus, Web of Science to April 15, 2025, plus hand search; 4406 records, 2246 after de-duplication, 161 full texts, 67 included
- **Screening/extraction**: two reviewers independently, in duplicate
- **Time points**: nonsurgical 3 months (nearest later point if absent); intrabony, recession and furcation 12 months (closest later point if absent)
- **Outcomes**: PPD and CAL (nonsurgical, intrabony); PPD, vertical and horizontal CAL (furcation); recession depth (recession)
- **Bias**: RoB 2 (two reviewers; third resolves) on PPD or recession depth; **certainty**: GRADE by one assessor
- **Synthesis**: site as statistical unit; weighted mean differences; meta-analysis only if >=3 studies; random-effects preferentially reported when heterogeneity moderate to high; subgroups by graft and membrane type; funnel plot and Egger test when >=7 studies; change-score SD computed with assumed r = 0.7; split-mouth SD adjusted (Su and Tu); R 4.5.0

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Risk of bias (RoB 2): nonsurgical 3 high / 3 some concerns / 3 low; intrabony 34 high / 3 some concerns / 4 low; furcation 2 high / 2 low; recession 8 high / 4 some concerns / 1 low.

| Comparison | Studies | PPD (95% CI) | CAL (95% CI) | I2 | GRADE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nonsurgical: SRP vs SRP + EMD | 9 PPD / 8 CAL | +0.30 mm (0.01-0.59) | +0.21 mm (-0.12 to 0.55, ns) | 51% / 39.3% | Low |
| Intrabony: OFD vs OFD + EMD | 20 | +1.00 mm (0.63-1.38) | +1.14 mm (0.83-1.46) | 87% / 87% | Very low |
| Bone graft vs BG + EMD | 5 | +0.61 mm (0.03-1.19) | +0.84 mm (0.06-1.61) | 81.8% / 90.7% | Very low |
| EMD vs EMD + BG | 12 | +0.26 mm (-0.02 to 0.53, ns) | +0.33 mm (0.02-0.64) | 59.9% / 53% | Very low (publication bias strongly suspected) |
| EMD vs GTR (GTR higher) | 10 | +0.51 mm (-0.03 to 1.05, ns) | +0.19 mm (-0.15 to 0.52, ns) | 76.6% / 35.4% | Very low |
| EMD vs EMD + GTR | 3 | +0.32 mm (-0.22 to 0.85, ns) | +0.28 mm (-0.25 to 0.81, ns) | 0 / 0 | Very low |

- **BG vs BG + EMD**: subgroup by graft type not significant (few studies per subgroup)
- **EMD vs EMD + BG**: no significant subgroup differences by graft type; some xenograft/alloplast studies showed reduced CAL gain vs EMD alone (small, ns); funnel plot and Egger test suggested publication bias, especially for EMD vs EMD + xenograft
- **EMD vs GTR**: subgroup by membrane type: non-resorbable membranes gave 1.08 mm greater PPD reduction than EMD; CAL differences between membrane subgroups ns
- **Furcation**: 4 RCTs (Class II; Table 3), no meta-analysis because of heterogeneity in adjuncts, techniques and defect classification; Discussion cites EMD vs GTR horizontal furcation depth reduction 2.6 +/- 1.8 vs 1.9 +/- 1.4 mm at 14 months in a multicenter split-mouth trial
- **Recession (outcome: recession depth)**: CAF vs CAF + EMD (4 RCTs) small ns, moderate heterogeneity, one small study +0.91 mm with EMD; CAF + CTG vs CAF + EMD (4 RCTs) common-effect -0.43 mm (CTG better; CI as printed -0.28 to -0.59) but random-effects -0.20 mm (-0.70 to 0.31, ns), I2 87.3%; CAF + CTG vs CAF + CTG + EMD (5 RCTs) ns, high heterogeneity; GRADE very low for CAF vs CAF + EMD, low for the two CTG comparisons
- **Discussion**: EMD called the prevailing gold standard for intrabony regeneration over non-resorbable GTR on morbidity, secondary surgery and cost grounds; BG + EMD vs BG effect noted as below conventional probe measurement error; Rojas et al. (secondary citation from a separate SR) reported membrane exposure in 28.8% of defects across five studies with no flap dehiscence in EMD groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Abstract vs Results vs Discussion discrepancy (EMD + graft)**: abstract says the only CAL gain with bone graft is 0.79 mm with xenografts; Results report pooled CAL +0.33 mm (significant) and no significant subgroup differences, and 0.79 is not in Results; Discussion says no significant PPD/CAL gain overall yet significant CAL subgroup differences with xenograft reducing CAL. Logged as source-data-issue; not adjudicated here
- Majority of RCTs at high risk of bias; certainty low or very low throughout
- Very high heterogeneity in the main intrabony meta-analyses (I2 up to 90.7%); no meta-regression for defect severity, morphology, technique or patient factors
- Publication bias strongly suspected for EMD vs EMD + BG
- Secondary outcomes (adverse events, patient-reported outcomes, radiographic bone gain, pocket closure, tooth loss) not analyzed
- GRADE rated by a single assessor; site as statistical unit; change-score SDs imputed with r = 0.7
- Common-effect and random-effects results diverge for CAF + CTG vs CAF + EMD (-0.43 vs -0.20 mm)
- Authors call for high-quality RCTs on nonsurgical, furcation and root coverage use, optimized application protocols and combinations with other biomaterials

## 7. Glossary
- **EMD**: enamel matrix derivative, porcine amelogenin-based biologic mimicking root development
- **EMP**: enamel matrix protein
- **OFD**: open flap debridement, access flap without regenerative material
- **GTR**: guided tissue regeneration (barrier membrane)
- **CAF / CTG**: coronally advanced flap / connective tissue graft
- **MINST**: minimally invasive nonsurgical therapy
