## Why Ingested

Sun 2023은 동일 환자에서 치조정 식립(crestal)과 1mm 하방 식립(subcrestal)을 비교한 split-mouth RCT로, Stacchi 2023(1mm vs 2mm)과 상보적 쌍을 이룬다. 두 논문을 함께 읽으면 PS 임플란트에서 식립 깊이를 crestal → 1mm → 2mm 연속 스펙트럼으로 이해할 수 있다. 또한 split-mouth 설계는 교란변수 제어 측면에서 Stacchi의 다기관 crossover보다 강한 내적 타당도를 제공한다. → [[implants/mbl/sun-2023-clinical-radiographic-results-crestal-subcrestal]]

## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT (25 patients, 50 PS implants: 25 crestal + 25 at 1 mm subcrestal) comparing bone-level and soft-tissue outcomes over 3 years in the posterior mandible; primary outcome was marginal bone level change.

1 mm subcrestal placement showed significantly less MBL at 1 year (0.24 ± 0.11 mm) vs crestal (0.41 ± 0.14 mm; p < 0.001); at 3 years: 0.29 ± 0.15 mm vs 0.48 ± 0.17 mm (p < 0.001); soft tissue indices comparable between groups.

1 mm subcrestal placement significantly reduces MBL compared to crestal in PS implants over 3 years, without compromising soft tissue health; depth benefit is maintained long-term.

## 세줄요약

Split-mouth 무작위 대조 임상시험 (환자 25명, 플랫폼 스위칭(PS, Platform Switching) 임플란트 50개: 치조정(Crestal) vs 1mm 하방(Subcrestal), 하악 후방부, 3년 추적).

1mm 하방 식립에서 MBL 유의하게 적음: 1년 0.24±0.11mm vs 치조정 0.41±0.14mm (p<0.001); 3년: 0.29±0.15mm vs 0.48±0.17mm (p<0.001); 연조직 지표 두 군 차이 없음.

PS 임플란트에서 1mm 하방 식립이 치조정 식립 대비 3년에 걸쳐 MBL을 유의하게 감소; 연조직 건강 유지.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Split-mouth RCT enabling within-patient comparison of crestal vs 1mm subcrestal PS implants
- Significant MBL reduction with 1mm subcrestal at both 1yr (p<0.001) and 3yr (p<0.001)
- 1yr MBL: 0.24mm (subcrestal) vs 0.41mm (crestal; Δ = 0.17mm)
- 3yr MBL: 0.29mm (subcrestal) vs 0.48mm (crestal; Δ = 0.19mm)
- Split-mouth design provides strong internal validity (confounders controlled within patient)
- Complements Stacchi 2023 (1mm vs 2mm) to build a crestal→1mm→2mm placement depth continuum

## 3. Methodology
- Design: split-mouth RCT; each patient contributes one crestal and one subcrestal implant
- Population: 25 patients; posterior mandible; 2 adjacent implant sites
- Implants: bone-level PS design; matched implants in both groups
- Loading: standard protocol; 3 months
- Outcomes: MBL (primary; digital periapical radiographs), PPD, BOP, plaque score at 1yr, 3yr
- Statistics: Wilcoxon signed-rank for MBL; significance p < 0.05

## 4. Key Results
- 1yr MBL: 0.24±0.11mm (1mm subcrestal) vs 0.41±0.14mm (crestal; p<0.001)
- 3yr MBL: 0.29±0.15mm (1mm subcrestal) vs 0.48±0.17mm (crestal; p<0.001)
- Δ MBL: ~0.17–0.19mm in favor of subcrestal (consistent across timepoints)
- PPD, BOP, plaque score: comparable between groups at all timepoints
- Implant survival: 100% in both groups at 3 years

## 5. Limitations
- Posterior mandible only; generalizability to maxilla uncertain
- 25 patients — relatively small split-mouth sample
- 3-year follow-up; longer-term data needed
- Blinding not possible (placement depth known to surgeon)

## 7. Glossary
- **PS**: Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭)
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss (변연골 소실)
- **Crestal**: implant shoulder at alveolar crest level (치조정 식립)
- **Subcrestal**: implant shoulder below alveolar crest (하방 식립)
- **Split-mouth**: study design where both test and control are placed in the same patient
- **PPD**: Peri-implant probing depth (임플란트주위 탐침 깊이)
- **BOP**: Bleeding on probing (탐침 시 출혈)
