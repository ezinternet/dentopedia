## Why Ingested

Comprehensive narrative review (68 studies) systematically covering statin drug effects across all major dental domains — periodontitis, alveolar bone, osseointegration, pulp cells, orthodontic movement/relapse, wound healing, salivary glands, and oral cancer — providing the broadest single-paper reference for statin pleiotropic effects on oral health in this wiki. Relates to [[drug/systemic-disease/khalilurrahman-2026-raas-inhibitor-statin-periodontal-status-sr-ma]] on statin–periodontal interaction and [[drug/systemic-disease/chidiac-2026-antihypertensive-antihyperlipidemic-antidepressant-implant-survival]] on statin effect on implant survival.

## Three-line Summary

Statins (HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors), primarily used for cardiovascular lipid management, exert pleiotropic effects on oral health via anabolic bone metabolism (BMP-2 upregulation, osteoclast inhibition), anti-inflammatory/antioxidant properties, MMP inhibition, and antimicrobial activity. Local adjunctive use with SRP improves periodontal clinical parameters (CAL, PPD, bone fill); animal evidence supports enhanced osseointegration; benefits are also reported for pulp cells, wound healing, salivary glands, and anti-tumor effects. Most evidence remains preclinical and heterogeneous; clinical dose, route, and optimal statin type require further RCT-level confirmation.

## 세줄요약

스타틴(HMG-CoA 환원효소 억제제)은 심혈관 지질 조절 약물이지만 BMP-2 상향조절·파골세포 억제·MMP 억제·항균작용 등 다발성(pleiotropic) 효과를 통해 구강 건강 전반에 유익한 영향을 미친다. 치주치료(SRP) 보조제로 국소 투여 시 임상부착수준(CAL)·탐침깊이(PPD)·골결손 충전이 유의하게 개선되고, 동물실험에서는 골유착 향상이 일관되게 보고된다. 대부분의 근거가 전임상 수준이고 이질성이 높아, 임상 용량·투여경로·최적 스타틴 종류에 대한 대규모 무작위 대조연구가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. First comprehensive review covering all major dental domains affected by statins in a single paper
2. Confirms adjunctive local statin use improves periodontal outcomes across 6 systematic reviews/MA
3. Documents ~95% of animal implant studies show enhanced osseointegration with statin administration
4. Shows statins stimulate pulp cell mineralization and promote dentin regeneration
5. Reports statins reduce orthodontic relapse via OPG upregulation and RANKL suppression
6. Demonstrates anti-tumor effects on HNSCC and salivary adenoid cystic carcinoma cell lines
7. Identifies radioprotective effect of simvastatin on radiation-induced salivary gland dysfunction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Chronic Periodontitis
- 6 SR/MA confirm SRP + local statin significantly improves CAL, PPD, bone defect fill, sulcus bleeding index
- Local (not systemic) delivery is the effective route for clinical periodontal outcomes
- Most effective statins by meta-regression: rosuvastatin (local) and atorvastatin (by type-effect significance)
- Adjunctive statin concentration used clinically: 1.2 µg/site with biodegradable carrier (far below animal doses)

### Alveolar Bone (Post-extraction / Periodontitis)
- Animal studies: local statin in PLGA or carbonate apatite carrier → significantly greater alveolar ridge height, bone mineral density, trabecular thickness vs control
- Clinical trials: two RCTs showed non-significant higher vital bone at 2 months; PLGA/HA/statin failed at 3 months (scaffold complications) — clinical evidence insufficient
- Periodontitis animal models: atorvastatin 27 mg/kg prevents ABL and cemental resorption; simvastatin reverses ligature-induced ABL by 46% increase in bone height (micro-CT)
- Dose-dependent effect: ≥10× clinical cholesterol-lowering dose required for systemic anti-resorptive effect in rodents

### Osseointegration of Implants
- ~95% of animal studies show local/systemic statins enhance bone-to-implant contact (BIC), bone density, new bone formation around titanium implants
- Fluvastatin 75 µg topical: significantly higher BIC and mineral bone volume vs control at 2–4 weeks
- Simvastatin i.p.: BCR and BD significantly greater than control (thick trabeculae in medullary canal)
- Exception: implant surfaces directly coated with statin impaired osseointegration in one study
- No RCT data in humans; animal dose ~10× human dose; tibia model ≠ alveolar bone

### Dental Pulp Cells
- Simvastatin 1 mmol/L: suppresses DPSC proliferation but increases ALP activity and mineral nodule formation
- PLGA-lovastatin nanoparticles: comparable to MTA at 4 weeks for tubular reparative dentin/dentinal bridge
- Cohort study (n=60): 93% healing of apical periodontitis at 2–5 years in statin users vs 70% in non-users (p significant)
- Retrospective study: pulp chamber calcification (↓ height ratio) in mandibular molars of statin users — suggests increased odontoblastic activity

### Orthodontic Tooth Movement / Relapse
- Atorvastatin: significantly reduces OTM and osteoclast counts in animal models (no effect on femoral endochondral ossification)
- Simvastatin: reduces space re-opening after orthodontic space closure (RCT, n=16, p<0.001); greater GI reduction
- Mechanism: OPG upregulation, RANKL suppression → inhibited osteoclastogenesis → reduced relapse
- Inconsistent results across animal models; one simvastatin study showed no significant relapse reduction

### Wound / Bone Healing
- Rosuvastatin local: significantly more new bone at 2 weeks post-mandibular fracture vs control
- Simvastatin/chitosan gel (10 mg/mL): significant reduction in wound-healing score and VAS pain at 3 and 7 days post-FGG donor site

### Salivary Gland
- Simvastatin i.p.: radioprotective — lessened salivary secretion reduction and restored amylase activity in irradiated mice; mechanism: free radical scavenging, collagen deposition reduction, TGF-β1 delay

### Anti-cancer (HNSCC / Salivary Carcinoma)
- Simvastatin dose-dependently inhibits SACC-LM proliferation; miR-21 inhibitor reduces statin resistance
- Atorvastatin: decreases HNSCC cell invasion/migration, reduces RhoC membrane fraction, inhibits ERK1/2 and STAT3 signaling
- Lovastatin + gefitinib: >90% cell death in SCC9 cells vs monotherapy
- Statin monotherapy alone: >50% tumor cell burden reduction possible

### Adverse Effects
- Oral symptoms during statin therapy: dry mouth, itchiness, bitterness, cough (small study, n not specified); marked improvement after temporary discontinuation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Most evidence preclinical; animal doses 10× human clinical cholesterol-lowering dose
- No standardized local delivery formulation, dose, or frequency across studies
- Animal bone models (rat tibia, murine) not equivalent to human alveolar bone
- No long-term (>5 year) RCT data on implant osseointegration
- Only one study on adverse oral effects of statins (small sample, limited design)
- Ongoing clinical trials (as of 2019): 7 registered trials for periodontitis (Table 3); results pending at time of publication

## 7. Glossary

- **Statin / HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor (스타틴 / HMG-CoA 환원효소 억제제):** Drug class inhibiting cholesterol biosynthesis via mevalonate pathway; pleiotropic effects on bone, inflammation, immunity
- **BMP-2 (골형성단백질-2):** Bone morphogenetic protein-2; upregulated by statins → osteoblast differentiation
- **BIC / Bone-to-Implant Contact (골-임플란트 접촉률):** Histomorphometric measure of osseointegration
- **RANKL/OPG ratio (RANKL/OPG 비율):** Key regulator of osteoclastogenesis; statins increase OPG and decrease RANKL → anti-resorptive
- **MMP (기질금속단백분해효소):** Matrix metalloproteinase; statin inhibition reduces tissue degradation
- **SRP (치석제거 및 치근활택술):** Scaling and root planing; standard non-surgical periodontal treatment
- **CAL (임상 부착 수준):** Clinical attachment level; key periodontal outcome measure
- **PPD / PD (치주낭 깊이):** Probing pocket depth; key periodontal outcome measure
- **HNSCC (두경부편평세포암):** Head and neck squamous cell carcinoma
- **SACC (침샘 선낭암):** Salivary adenoid cystic carcinoma
- **DPSC (치수줄기세포):** Dental pulp stem cell
- **PLGA (폴리락트산-글리콜산 공중합체):** Biodegradable polymer carrier for local statin delivery
- **OTM (교정적 치아이동):** Orthodontic tooth movement
