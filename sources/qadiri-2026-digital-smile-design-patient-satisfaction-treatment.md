## Why Ingested
치과 상담 동의율 세트 인제스트: 디지털 스마일 디자인(DSD)이 심미 치과 상담에서 환자 이해도·자신감·치료 수락률에 미치는 실제 임상 효과를 n=80으로 전향적 측정. [[behavioral-dentistry/communication-relationship/touati-2021-communication-tools-patient-satisfaction-scoping]] 의 스코핑 리뷰가 예측한 디지털 도구 효과를 구체적 수치로 뒷받침하며, [[overviews/patient-consultation-communication-protocol]] 의 "정보 제공→기대 정렬→결정" 단계에 DSD를 삽입했을 때의 효과를 정량화한다.

## One-line Summary
Prospective clinical study (n=80) comparing conventional vs DSD-assisted consultation: DSD increased understanding scores (2.9→4.4, p<0.001), confidence scores, and achieved 85% treatment acceptance rate.

## 한줄요약
전향적 연구(n=80): DSD 상담 후 환자 이해도 점수 2.9→4.4(p<0.001), 치료 자신감 상승, 치료 수락률 85%로 확인.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Quantified treatment acceptance rate with DSD-assisted consultation: 85% (68/80 patients accepted proposed treatment)
- Pre- vs. post-DSD comparison on 5-point Likert scale: all domains (clarity, duration, cost, limitations) improved significantly (p<0.001)
- 95% of participants felt DSD improved communication; 97.5% found the consultation easy to understand
- 88.8% felt more involved in treatment planning process with DSD
- 98.8% stated they would recommend DSD consultation to others

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Prospective clinical study (single-arm, pre-post)
- Setting: Restorative Dentistry Department, Najran University, Saudi Arabia
- n: 80 patients seeking esthetic dental treatment (veneers, crowns, bleaching, direct composite)
- Age: 18–60 years; 67.5% female; 52.5% aged 31–50
- Intervention: conventional consultation → digital smile simulation via Smile Designer Pro software → re-assessment
- Outcomes: patient understanding (5-point Likert before/after), confidence (5-point Likert before/after), satisfaction (10-point VAS), treatment acceptance (yes/no)
- Sample size: G*Power (paired t-test, d=0.4, α=0.05, power=80%); target n=80 (65+20% for attrition)
- Statistics: paired t-test, Cohen's d effect size; p<0.05 significant

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Understanding: overall score 2.9±0.9 → 4.4±0.6 (p<0.001); clarity of procedures 2.8→4.5
- Confidence in final smile: 3.0±1.1 → 4.6±0.5 (p<0.001)
- Certainty to proceed: 3.2±1.2 → 4.4±0.7 (p<0.001)
- Ability to visualize final result: 2.5±1.0 → 4.8±0.3 (p<0.001; largest gain)
- Understanding treatment cost: 3.0±1.1 → 4.2±0.8 (p<0.001)
- Treatment acceptance: 85% (68/80); 15% declined
- Satisfaction: 96.3% overall satisfied; 98.8% would recommend DSD consultation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single-arm pre-post design (no randomized control group for acceptance rate)
- Single center (Saudi Arabia); cultural/demographic generalizability uncertain
- No long-term follow-up on whether initial acceptance translated to treatment completion and satisfaction
- Subjective self-report outcomes; no objective communication quality measure
- Smile Designer Pro only; results may not generalize to other DSD software

## 7. Glossary
- DSD: Digital Smile Design — software-based simulation projecting proposed esthetic outcome on patient photographs
- VAS: Visual Analog Scale — 0–10 cm scale for subjective satisfaction rating
- Likert scale: 1–5 ordinal scale (1=strongly disagree, 5=strongly agree)
