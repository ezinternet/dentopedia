## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 7 RCTs evaluating clindamycin (any route/regimen/dose) for infection prevention after oral surgery; only 4 trials of oral clindamycin in third molar extraction were poolable, yielding RR 0.66 (95% CI 0.38–1.16), p=0.15, NNT=29 — not statistically significant.

Clindamycin — the standard alternative for penicillin-allergic patients — cannot be shown to effectively prevent infection after third molar extraction; evidence for other oral surgery procedures was insufficient to analyze.

Prescribing clindamycin as routine antibiotic prophylaxis in third molar surgery lacks supporting evidence despite its widespread use in penicillin-allergic patients.

## 세줄요약

7편 RCT SR+MA: 구강외과 수술 후 감염 예방 목적 클린다마이신(Clindamycin) 효과 평가 (경로·용량·용법 불문); 하악 제3대구치 발치에서 경구 클린다마이신 4편 메타분석 가능 — RR 0.66(95% CI 0.38–1.16), p=0.15, NNT=29, 통계적 유의성 없음.

페니실린 알레르기 환자의 대체 항생제인 클린다마이신이 제3대구치 발치 후 감염 예방에 효과적임을 증명할 수 없음; 타 구강외과 수술에 대한 근거는 메타분석 불가.

제3대구치 수술 시 클린다마이신의 일상적 예방투여는 광범위하게 처방됨에도 불구하고 뒷받침하는 근거 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA focused exclusively on clindamycin across all oral surgery types
- Demonstrates ineffectiveness of oral clindamycin for third molar extraction infection prevention (RR 0.66, NS)
- NNT=29 (CI: 12 to −57) — wide CI includes infinity, indicating no reliable benefit
- Addresses critical prescribing gap: clindamycin used for penicillin allergy but evidence absent
- Insufficient data for other procedures (endodontic surgery, implant placement)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PRISMA; PROSPERO CRD4202122624
- Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science, Embase Ovid, Scopus
- Search to January 2021
- 540 records → 7 included in qualitative synthesis
- 4 trials of oral clindamycin in third molar extraction → quantitative MA
  - Oral clindamycin: Poeschl 2004, Kaczmarzyk 2007, Adde 2012, Kaposvári 2017
  - IV clindamycin: Halpern 2007 (15 patients, too small to pool)
  - Topical clindamycin: Hamiti-Krasniqi 2014 (dry socket outcome)
  - Endodontic surgery: Lindeboom 2006 (1 study, qualitative only)
- Effect measure: RR with Mantel–Haenszel model; NNT calculated

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Analysis | n (extractions) | RR (95% CI) | p | NNT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oral clindamycin vs placebo (3rd molar) | 486 (245 vs 241) | 0.66 (0.38–1.16) | 0.15 | 29 (CI: 12 to −57) |
| Heterogeneity | — | I²=0 | p=0.44 | — |

- **Individual topical clindamycin** (Hamiti-Krasniqi 2014): 2/60 vs 19/60 dry sockets — lower incidence in topical group (not meta-analyzable with oral)
- **IV clindamycin** (Halpern 2007, n=15): 0/15 vs 5/62 infections — small but favoring IV; not pooled
- **Endodontic surgery** (Lindeboom 2006): 2/128 vs 4/128 infections — insufficient for MA

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Very few RCTs (7 total, 4 poolable) — underpowered to detect modest effects
- Variable doses, routes, and durations across studies
- Cannot evaluate effect in penicillin-allergic patients specifically
- Other procedures (implant, orthognathic, oncologic) had no eligible RCTs with placebo control
- Need for high-quality RCTs of clindamycin vs placebo for all oral surgery types

## 6. Related Work and Context
- Contrasts with amoxicillin: NNT=5–19 for amoxicillin in third molar extraction (other SRs)
- Topical clindamycin may be effective (Hamiti-Krasniqi) — different mechanism
- Aligns with antimicrobial stewardship: prescribing clindamycin in penicillin allergy is not evidence-based

## 7. Glossary
- **Clindamycin**: Lincosamide antibiotic; bacteriostatic against anaerobes and Gram-positive organisms; used as penicillin-allergy alternative
- **NNT (Number Needed to Treat)**: 29; CI includes −57 (infinity) — statistically no benefit
- **RR 0.66**: Relative risk of infection with clindamycin vs control; 34% relative reduction not statistically significant (CI 0.38–1.16 crosses 1)

## Why Ingested

Directly addresses the evidence gap for clindamycin as antibiotic prophylaxis in oral surgery — the standard alternative in penicillin-allergic patients, yet shown here to lack supporting evidence for third molar extraction.
