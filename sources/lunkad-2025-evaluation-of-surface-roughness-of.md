## Why Ingested

다층형 단일체 지르코니아 (Multilayered Monolithic Zirconia, MMLZ) 의 표면조도 (Surface Roughness, Ra) 에 대한 반복 멸균 주기의 영향을 3종 연마 시스템 비교로 정량화한 in vitro 연구. 의장 추천 시스템(EVE Diacera, SS White)과 범용 시스템(Ultradent Jiffy) 의 임상 허용 기준 충족 여부와 멸균 주기별 효능 변화를 직접 비교한 최초 연구. 플래시 고압증기멸균 10주기 후에도 임상 허용 범위(Ra < 0.5 μm) 내 유지됨을 확인.

## Three-line Summary

This in vitro study compared the effect of up to 10 flash sterilization cycles on the surface roughness of multilayered monolithic zirconia (MMLZ) specimens polished with three commercial polishing kits: EVE Diacera, SS White, and Ultradent Jiffy. All three systems produced clinically acceptable surface roughness (Ra ≤ 0.0325 μm) after 10 autoclave cycles, with no statistically significant differences within any single polishing system across cycles. The study supports reuse of polishing kits through the manufacturer-recommended maximum of 10 flash sterilization cycles without meaningful loss of polishing efficiency.

## 세줄요약

다층형 단일체 지르코니아 (MMLZ) 시편 100개를 3종 연마 시스템(EVE Diacera, SS White, Ultradent Jiffy)으로 연마한 후 플래시 고압증기멸균 1·5·10주기에 따른 표면조도 변화를 비교한 in vitro 연구. 세 시스템 모두 10주기 멸균 후에도 임상 허용 기준(Ra < 0.5 μm) 충족(최고값 Ra 0.0325 μm). 각 시스템 내 주기별 표면조도 차이는 통계적 유의성 없음 — 제조사 권장 최대 10주기 재사용 가이드라인 지지.

## Key Contributions

1. **First study comparing sterilization-cycle effects on three commercial MMLZ polishing kits** — EVE Diacera (manufacturer-recommended), SS White (manufacturer-recommended), and Ultradent Jiffy (generic/universal).
2. **All polishing systems maintained clinically acceptable Ra after 10 flash sterilization cycles** — Ra values ranged from 0.022–0.0325 μm, well below the 0.5 μm clinical threshold.
3. **Polished surfaces smoother than glazed controls** — control glazed group had highest Ra (0.0325 μm vs. all polished groups), contradicting the assumption that glazing is superior.
4. **No cross-system significant differences post-autoclaving** — generic Ultradent Jiffy performed comparably to manufacturer-recommended systems across all sterilization cycles.
5. **EVE Diacera showed slight roughness increase at cycle 5 (P=0.0009), then partial recovery at cycle 10** — the only system with statistically significant within-group change, though still clinically acceptable.

## Methodology

**Design**: In vitro, comparative, blinded operators  
**Specimens**: 100 disc-shaped MMLZ specimens (Zolid Gen-X, shade A2; 10 mm diameter × 3 mm thickness); milled via 5-axis CAD/CAM  
**Groups**:
- Group C (n=10): glazed control, no polishing
- Group 1 (n=30): EVE Diacera, subdivided by sterilization cycles 1/5/10 (n=10 each)
- Group 2 (n=30): SS White, subdivided 1/5/10 cycles
- Group 3 (n=30): Ultradent Jiffy, subdivided 1/5/10 cycles

**Sterilization protocol**: Flash autoclave, 134°C, 2 bar, 3–5 min (15 min total per cycle)  
**Polishing**: EVE Diacera at 4,000–8,000 RPM × 30 sec; SS White and Ultradent Jiffy at 7,000–10,000 RPM × 30 sec  
**Measurement**: Profilometer (Talysurf Intra) for Ra; SEM (JSM-6360LV) at 1000× for qualitative analysis; confocal optical microscope (GT-K 3D Bruker) with Vertical Scan Interferometry  
**Statistics**: Shapiro-Wilk normality test → Kruskal-Wallis one-way ANOVA (non-parametric); Dunn post-hoc with Bonferroni correction; significance at P ≤ 0.05 (intra-group P ≤ 0.0011 after Bonferroni)

**Within-system**: No significant differences across 1/5/10 cycles within EVE Diacera, Ultradent Jiffy, or SS White (all P > 0.0011).  
**Cross-system**: Most pairwise comparisons between systems were not statistically significant.  
**SEM**: Ultradent Jiffy and SS White showed more homogeneous, smoother surfaces vs. EVE Diacera (which resembled the wavy glazed control surface).

## Limitations

1. Only flash (immediate) sterilization tested — no comparison with standard steam or dry heat sterilization
2. Small subgroup sizes (n=10 per subgroup) despite adequate total N
3. Flexural strength not evaluated — only surface roughness outcomes
4. Polishing bur wear/reuse not separately analyzed from sterilization effects
5. Only disc-shaped specimens tested; clinical crown thickness may affect results
6. Only Zolid Gen-X zirconia tested; results may not generalize to other MMLZ brands

## Related Work

- Sayed et al: polishing bur efficacy unchanged through cycle 10, decreases after cycle 15 — directly corroborates current findings
- Sabrah et al: glazed zirconia Ra 0.42 μm, polished Ra 0.49 μm — glazed smoother than polished (opposite to current study's results for MMLZ)
- Janyavula et al: polished zirconia Ra 0.17 μm (smoothest), glazed Ra 0.76 μm — polished outperforms glazed, consistent with current findings
- Preis et al: grinding increases, polishing reduces surface roughness; wear simulation has minimal impact on phase transformation
- Lacerda et al: 5 steam sterilization cycles reduce diamond bur polishing efficacy
- Papadopoulou et al: no roughness changes after 5th and 10th autoclave cycles on ceramic — consistent findings

## Glossary

- **MMLZ** (Multilayered Monolithic Zirconia): multilayer monolithic zirconia combining 3Y-TZP cervical and 5Y-TZP incisal layers
- **Ra** (Surface Roughness Average): arithmetic mean surface roughness; clinical threshold < 0.5 μm
- **Rq** (Root Mean Square Roughness): quantifies surface height variation; relevant for film thickness assessment
- **Flash sterilization**: 134°C, 2 bar, 3–5 min cycle (vs. standard 121°C, 1 bar, 15 min)
- **3Y-TZP**: 3 mol% yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal (high strength, lower translucency)
- **5Y-TZP**: 5 mol% yttria-stabilized cubic/tetragonal zirconia (high translucency, lower strength)
- **Zolid Gen-X**: CAD/CAM multilayer zirconia block (Amann Girrbach); DIN/EN/ISO 6872 Type II Class 5
- **SEM** (Scanning Electron Microscopy): qualitative surface morphology imaging at 1000×
- **Kruskal-Wallis test**: non-parametric one-way ANOVA for non-normally distributed data
