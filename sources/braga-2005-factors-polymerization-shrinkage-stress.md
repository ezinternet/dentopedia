## Summary

Polymerization shrinkage stress in bonded resin-composite restorations is a multi-factorial phenomenon governed by three primary axes: (1) volumetric shrinkage of the material, (2) the viscoelastic behavior of the resin during and after polymerization, and (3) the restrictions imposed on free shrinkage by cavity walls and adhesive bonds. Material composition—monomer type and content—determines the magnitude of volumetric shrinkage. Viscoelastic behavior, particularly the pre-gel flowable period before vitrification, determines how much stress relaxation occurs before the material rigidifies. Reaction kinetics (rate of polymerization, degree of conversion) modulate how fast the network forms and how much shrinkage occurs before gelation. Cavity confinement, quantified by the C-factor (cavity configuration factor: ratio of bonded to unbonded surfaces), amplifies residual stress when high. The authors conclude that because no single factor dominates universally, no single restorative technique reliably reduces shrinkage stress across all materials—material-specific properties must guide technique selection.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review establishing a three-factor framework for polymerization shrinkage stress in bonded resin-composite restorations: volumetric shrinkage (material-dependent), viscoelastic behavior (pre-gel flow period enabling stress relaxation), and cavity confinement quantified by C-factor.
No single factor dominates universally; high C-factor amplifies residual stress; reaction kinetics (polymerization rate, degree of conversion) determine how much shrinkage occurs before vitrification.
No universal stress-reduction technique works across all materials; material-specific properties must guide technique selection (incremental layering, soft-start curing, stress-absorbing liners show variable efficacy).

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰로 접착 레진-복합재 수복에서 중합수축응력(Polymerization Shrinkage Stress)의 3인자 프레임워크를 확립: 부피 수축(Volume Shrinkage), 점탄성 거동(Viscoelastic Behavior, 겔화 전 유동 구간), 와동 구속(C-factor, Cavity Configuration Factor).
어느 단일 인자도 보편적으로 지배적이지 않음; 높은 C-factor는 잔류 응력을 증폭; 중합 속도와 전환율이 유리화(Vitrification) 전 수축 정도를 결정함.
어떤 단일 응력 감소 기법도 모든 재료에 보편적으로 효과적이지 않음; 재료별 특성에 따른 기법 선택 필요(점진적 적층, 소프트스타트 광중합, 응력 흡수 라이너는 효과가 재료 의존적).
