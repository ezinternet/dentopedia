## Why Ingested
First SR+MA specifically estimating survival rates stratified by insertion torque threshold for immediately loaded implants — fills the gap flagged by Chen 2019 (could not analyse IT effect due to data inconsistencies). Reinforces [[wiki/implants/norton-2017-low-insertion-torque-primary-stability]] (low IT prospective data) and extends [[wiki/implants/isq/lemos-2020-high-insertion-torque-dental-implants-sr-ma]] (complements the high-IT side of the same question).

## Three-line Summary
SR+MA of 7 intervention trials (4 RCTs, 1 CCT, 2 prospective; 326 implants, 129 patients, mean follow-up 24 months) comparing low (≤35 Ncm) vs medium/high (>35 Ncm) insertion torque in immediately loaded implants.
Low IT implants had 96% survival vs 92% for higher IT (IRR 1.05, 95% CI 0.79–1.39, p=.175, I²=0.0%); no significant difference. Subgroup: splinted implants >20 Ncm had 5% higher survival than ≤20 Ncm (NS); single implants 20–35 Ncm had 8% less failure risk than >35 Ncm (NS).
Low IT (≤35 Ncm) is compatible with immediate loading; implants ≤20 Ncm can be loaded with splinted prostheses, >20 Ncm with single crowns — though evidence base remains sparse.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: J Clin Periodontol 2023;50(2):158–169
- **DOI**: 10.1111/jcpe.13733
- **Institution**: University of Santiago de Compostela (Spain), Egas Moniz — Cooperativa de Ensino Superior (Portugal), UCL Eastman Dental Institute (UK), FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg (Germany)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA to quantify pooled survival rates for low vs high insertion torque in immediately loaded implants, filling a gap identified by prior reviews (Chen 2019)
- Demonstrated no significant difference between low (≤35 Ncm) and medium/high (>35 Ncm) IT groups with absolute homogeneity (I²=0.0%)
- Provided subgroup evidence: splinted implants >20 Ncm and single implants >35 Ncm trend toward lower failure without reaching significance
- Established that ≤20 Ncm implants loaded with splinted prostheses achieve outcomes equivalent to higher-torque implants

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD42020189499)
- **Databases**: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Central (up to June 2022)
- **n**: 7 studies (4 RCTs, 1 CCT, 2 prospective); 326 implants from 129 participants
- **Inclusion**: Immediately loaded titanium implants with reported IT values; ≥12 months follow-up; ≥10 patients
- **Outcomes**: Primary — implant survival rate; Secondary — MBL, soft-tissue changes, complications
- **Analysis**: Random-effects meta-analysis (metainc/metabin, Stata 11); I² heterogeneity; RoB 2.0 (RCTs) + ROBINS-I (non-RCTs)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Pooled survival (≤35 Ncm) | 96% (SR 0.96, 95% CI 0.91–0.98, p<.001) |
| Pooled survival (>35 Ncm) | 92% (SR 0.92, 95% CI 0.86–0.96, p<.001) |
| IRR (≤35 vs >35 Ncm) | 1.05 (95% CI 0.79–1.39, p=.175, I²=0.0%) |
| Splinted >20 vs ≤20 Ncm | IRR 1.05 (95% CI 0.78–1.43, p=.956, I²=0.0%) |
| Single 20–35 vs >35 Ncm | IRR 0.92 (95% CI 0.48–1.75, p=.799, I²=0.0%) |
| Mean follow-up | 24 months (range 12–36) |
| Total implants analysed | 326 (108 ≤20 Ncm, 80 >20–≤35 Ncm, 138 >35 Ncm) |
| RoB: RCTs | All low risk (RoB 2.0) |
| RoB: non-RCTs | 1 moderate (Ottoni), 1 serious (Koutouzis) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small number of eligible studies (7) limits statistical power; all heterogeneity is sampling error (I²=0.0%)
- Only 3 studies reported single implants — underpowered subgroup analysis for single-crown loading
- Follow-up relatively short (mean 24 months); long-term (>5 year) data lacking
- No meta-analysis of secondary outcomes (MBL, complications) due to insufficient reporting across studies
- No consensus on definition of "low," "regular," and "high" insertion torque across literature

## 6. Related Work
- [[wiki/implants/norton-2017-low-insertion-torque-primary-stability]]: Prospective study of IT ≤20 Ncm including spinners (IT <5 Ncm) — 96.7% survival at 1 year; darriba-2023 reinforces this with pooled MA data
- [[wiki/implants/isq/lemos-2020-high-insertion-torque-dental-implants-sr-ma]]: Complementary SR+MA examining the high-IT side (≥50 Ncm); darriba-2023 extends the question to the low-IT boundary

## 7. Glossary
- **Insertion torque (삽입 토크, IT)**: Rotational force required to seat an implant into the osteotomy; surrogate for primary stability
- **Primary stability (초기고정)**: Mechanical fixation of an implant at placement, before biological osseointegration
- **Immediate loading (즉시부하)**: Prosthetic loading within 1 week of implant placement
- **IRR (Incidence Rate Ratio)**: Ratio of event rates between groups; IRR 1.0 = no difference
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: Non-invasive measure of implant stability via resonance frequency analysis
