## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (Jpn Dent Sci Rev 2024; PROSPERO CRD42021281155) of RCTs with 12-month follow-up comparing enamel matrix derivative (EMD) alone versus EMD plus a bone graft in periodontal intrabony defects: 15 articles from 12 studies (autogenous 2, allograft 1, xenograft 3, alloplastic 6).

Adding a bone graft gave no significant clinical benefit: PD reduction +0.27 mm (95% CI -0.02 to 0.57; I² 60%), CAL gain +0.05 mm (95% CI -0.39 to 0.50; I² 75%), recession 0.16 mm less with combination (95% CI -0.03 to 0.35; I² 44%); only radiographic defect fill improved (+0.80 mm, I² 3%; alloplastic +0.88 mm, 95% CI 0.62-1.14).

Graft addition to EMD is not supported for clinical attachment outcomes at 12 months, and xenograft trials using minimally invasive surgery (MIST) showed no added benefit; limited by heterogeneity, moderate risk of bias and few trials per graft type.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타분석 (Systematic Review + Meta-analysis, SR+MA; Jpn Dent Sci Rev 2024): 치주 골내결손에서 12개월 추적 RCT만 포함해 법랑기질유도체 (Enamel Matrix Derivative, EMD) 단독 대 EMD + 골이식재 병용을 비교 (12개 연구·15편; 자가골 2·동종골 1·이종골 3·합성골 6).

병용의 임상적 추가 이득은 없었음: 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) 감소 +0.27 mm (95% CI -0.02~0.57), 임상부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득 +0.05 mm (95% CI -0.39~0.50), 치은퇴축 차이 0.16 mm (비유의); 방사선학적 결손 충만만 +0.80 mm로 유의 (합성골 +0.88 mm, 95% CI 0.62~1.14).

12개월 CAL 기준으로 EMD에 골이식재를 더하는 것은 지지되지 않으며 최소침습수술 (Minimally Invasive Surgical Technique, MIST) 이종골 시험도 추가 이득이 없었다; 이질성·중등도 편향위험·소수 연구가 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Updates 2011 and 2014 reviews with a uniform 12-month endpoint.
- No clinical (PD, CAL, recession) benefit of adding a graft to EMD; benefit only in radiographic defect fill (+0.80 mm).
- Alloplastic grafts drove radiographic fill (+0.88 mm); xenograft effects appeared only in the conventional-flap trial, not in MIST trials.
- Discusses complications of non-resorbable alloplasts (root resorption, ankylosis, infection) and slow-resorbing materials.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA of RCTs, PRISMA; Cochrane risk-of-bias tool; RevMan 5.4.1 random-effects mean differences.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, CENTRAL to 1 Oct 2021; PubMed re-searched 18 Jan 2024.
- **n**: 15 articles / 12 studies (autogenous 2; allograft 1 three-arm; xenograft 3; alloplastic 6 projects incl. one split-mouth).
- **Outcomes**: CAL gain, PD change, recession, radiographic defect fill at 12 months; subgroups by graft type.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Pooled difference (combination vs EMD alone) | I² |
|---|---|---|
| PD reduction | +0.27 mm (95% CI -0.02 to 0.57), ns | 60% |
| CAL gain | +0.05 mm (95% CI -0.39 to 0.50), ns | 75% |
| Recession | EMD alone 0.16 mm more (95% CI -0.03 to 0.35), ns | 44% |
| Radiographic fill (6 studies) | +0.80 mm, significant | 3% |
| Radiographic fill, alloplastic | +0.88 mm (95% CI 0.62 to 1.14) | - |

Risk of bias moderate; blinding unclear in all studies; coin-toss randomization rated high risk.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Conceptual heterogeneity (defect morphology, flap design, technique); I² 44-75% for clinical outcomes.
- Moderate risk of bias; blinding and allocation concealment difficult in surgery.
- Limited defect-configuration reporting; few trials per graft subgroup (autogenous n=2 trials).
- Authors call for standardized methodology in future trials.

## 6. Related Work
- Stavropoulos 2021 NMA: combinations appeared most efficacious; this review finds no clinical gain over EMD alone at 12 months.
- Mahintach 2026: HA versus EMD, same EMD reference arm.
- Jasser 2021 (beta-TCP) and Jafari 2026 (bioactive glass): alloplast evidence.

## 7. Glossary
- **EMD**: enamel matrix derivative (amelogenin-based biologic).
- **MIST / M-MIST**: (modified) minimally invasive surgical technique.
- **DBBM**: deproteinized bovine bone mineral (xenograft).
- **FDBA / DFDBA**: freeze-dried / demineralized freeze-dried bone allograft.
