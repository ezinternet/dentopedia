## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 38 RCTs (search date September 2023) comparing conservative (physical therapy, physical agents, pharmacological, occlusal devices), minimally invasive (arthrocentesis), and surgical (arthroscopy, open joint surgery) interventions in adults with TMD disc displacement.

All seven treatment categories produced clinically meaningful pain reduction and/or improved maximum mouth opening (MMO); no single modality was universally superior, and conservative approaches generally matched or approached surgical outcomes.

The review supports the guideline recommendation to begin with conservative, reversible treatments before escalating to minimally invasive or surgical options, though heterogeneity across RCTs precluded meta-analysis.

## 세줄요약

성인 턱관절장애(Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 디스크 변위 환자를 대상으로 보존적·최소침습적·수술적 치료를 비교한 RCT 38편을 포함한 체계적 문헌고찰(검색일 2023년 9월).

7개 치료군(물리치료, 물리적 인자, 약물, 교합장치, 관절세정술, 관절경, 개방수술) 모두 통증 감소 및/또는 최대 개구량(Maximum Mouth Opening, MMO) 개선을 보였으나 단일 최우수 치료법은 도출되지 않았다.

이질성으로 메타분석이 불가했으며, 임상 지침대로 보존적·가역적 치료를 먼저 시행한 후 최소침습·수술로 단계적 접근할 것을 지지한다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Conservative, Minimally Invasive, and Surgical Treatments for TMD Disc Displacement: A Systematic Review of RCTs
- **Journal**: Dentistry Journal (MDPI)
- **Year**: 2024
- **DOI**: 10.3390/dj12080244
- **PMID**: 39195088
- **PMCID**: PMC11353764
- **Design**: Systematic review of RCTs (PRISMA)
- **PICO**: Adults with TMD disc displacement (with or without reduction) — any treatment (conservative through surgical) — pain, MMO, jaw function, QoL

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR to comprehensively compare all treatment tiers (conservative → surgical) in TMD disc displacement using RCT-only inclusion
- 38 RCTs included from 2289 initial records (kappa 0.719)
- Seven treatment categories: physical therapy (n=8), physical agents (n=3), pharmacological (n=3), occlusal devices (n=15), arthrocentesis (n=14), arthroscopic surgery (n=4), open TMJ surgery (n=5)
- No meta-analysis due to clinical and methodological heterogeneity
- Cochrane RoB 2 used for risk of bias assessment

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science (search date 17 September 2023)
- Inclusion: RCTs in adults with TMD disc displacement (DDwR or DDwoR); English language; any intervention
- Exclusion: patients < 18 years, incomplete randomization, no disc derangement diagnosis, no full text retrievable
- Screening: Rayyan tool (blind mode); 2 independent reviewers + 1 TMD specialist for consensus
- Outcomes: pain (VAS), pain pressure threshold, MMO, joint noises, jaw function scores, QoL

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Physical Therapy
- Jaw exercises + counseling: reduced pain (p=0.041), click discomfort (p<0.001), improved self-perception (p=0.002)
- Magesty et al.: reduced pain (−1.15, p=0.004), total OHIP score (−5.21, p<0.001)
- Craane et al.: no additional benefit of physical therapy over medical counseling alone
- Supervised > home-based exercise for pain and MMO (p<0.05)
- Physical exercise post-arthrocentesis: reduced pain (VAS difference 1.81, p<0.05) but no effect on MMO range

### Physical Agents
- High-intensity laser therapy (HILT): pain reduced 48%, MMO increased 24% at 12 weeks; superior to TENS (25% pain, 10% MMO)
- HILT, ultrasound (US), occlusal splints: all effective; pain reduction 0.44–0.66, MMO increase 0.19–0.25
- Low-level laser therapy (LLLT) + botulinum toxin (BTX): faster effect than anterior repositioning appliance (ARA)
- BTX intraoral vs extraoral: similar clinical results; intraoral faster (4.86 ± 1.53 vs 10.29 ± 2.69 min)
- LLLT + stabilization splint therapy (SST) combination: faster and more effective than either alone
- Pulsed electromagnetic fields (PEMF): no specific treatment effect (p>0.05)

### Intra-Articular Injections
- Hyaluronic acid vs tenoxicam vs dexamethasone: hyaluronic acid had most significant pain reduction

### Occlusal Devices (15 RCTs)
- Largest category; stabilization splints widely studied
- Anterior repositioning appliances (ARA) and stabilization splints both effective; ARA often faster for acute reduction

### Arthrocentesis (14 RCTs)
- Most studied minimally invasive procedure; effective for both DDwR and DDwoR
- Various injectates compared (hyaluronic acid, PRP, corticosteroids)

### Arthroscopic Surgery (4 RCTs)
- Effective; outcomes comparable to or better than conservative treatments in refractory cases

### Open Joint Surgery (5 RCTs)
- Arthroplasty / arthrotomy for severe or failed-conservative cases
- Generally good outcomes for pain and function; higher risk profile

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No meta-analysis due to heterogeneity in outcome measures, follow-up times, and patient populations
- Many RCTs had unclear or high risk of bias
- Short follow-up periods in most included trials
- Wide variation in outcome measurement tools
- Authors recommend standardized outcome sets for future TMD RCTs

## 7. Glossary

- **DDwR**: Disc displacement with reduction (디스크 복위성 변위)
- **DDwoR**: Disc displacement without reduction (디스크 비복위성 변위)
- **MMO**: Maximum mouth opening (최대 개구량)
- **HILT**: High-intensity laser therapy (고강도 레이저 치료)
- **LLLT**: Low-level laser therapy (저강도 레이저 치료)
- **TENS**: Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (경피적 전기 신경 자극)
- **ARA**: Anterior repositioning appliance (전방 재위치 교합 장치)
- **BTX**: Botulinum toxin (보툴리눔 독소)
- **PEMF**: Pulsed electromagnetic fields (맥동 전자기장)
