## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (Materials 2022;15:1023) testing the effect of MDP-containing cleaner (Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus) vs water or alcohol on shear bond strength (SBS) to monolithic zirconia contaminated with saliva, blood, or provisional cement.

MDP-containing cleaner significantly improved SBS to contaminated zirconia compared to water or alcohol cleaning, by combining organic contaminant displacement with chemical bonding via MDP's phosphate group to zirconia.

MDP cleaners should be preferred over water/alcohol protocols for decontaminating zirconia before cementation, though this is an in vitro finding requiring clinical validation.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 연구 (Materials 2022;15:1023) — 타액·혈액·임시접착제로 오염된 단일구조 지르코니아(Monolithic Zirconia)에 MDP 함유 세정제(Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus) vs 수세·알코올 처치 후 전단결합강도(SBS) 비교.

MDP 함유 세정제가 수세·알코올 대비 오염 지르코니아 SBS 유의하게 향상 — MDP 인산염기가 유기 오염물 제거와 지르코니아 화학 결합을 동시에 수행하는 기전.

지르코니아 합착 전 오염 세정 시 MDP 세정제 우선 사용 권고 — 단, in vitro 결과이므로 임상 검증 필요.

## 1. Key Results
- Monolithic zirconia contaminated with saliva/blood/provisional cement
- MDP-containing cleaner (Clearfil Ceramic Primer Plus used as cleaner): significantly higher SBS
- Water or alcohol cleaning: inadequate decontamination
- Mechanism: MDP's phosphate group displaces organic contaminants + chemically bonds to Zr
