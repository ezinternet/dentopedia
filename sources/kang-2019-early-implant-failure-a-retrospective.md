## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center, single-system study at Dankook University Dental Hospital (Korea) of 1,031 conical SLA bone-level implants (Luna, Shinhung) in 409 patients placed 2015-2017; early failure was defined as clinical mobility before final prosthesis or perifixtural radiolucency at the first radiograph after final prosthesis delivery, and 35 implants (26 patients) failed early (Table 1 count; the paper's text states an early-phase survival of 95.6%, i.e. 4.4%).

In the multivariate Cox model, resident surgeons (adjusted HR 2.86, 95% CI 1.37-5.94, p=0.005) and mandibular placement (adjusted HR 2.31, 95% CI 1.12-4.76, p=0.023) carried higher early-failure hazard, while sex, age, implant diameter, sinus-floor elevation and guided bone regeneration (GBR) were not significant; three individual residents had hazard ratios of 4.01 to 12.76 versus the reference faculty surgeon.

Medical history and bone quality or quantity were not analysed, causes of failure were mostly unrecorded, and the reported survival percentages do not match the table counts, so the effect sizes are best read as signals of an operator-experience effect rather than precise risks.

## 세줄요약

단국대학교치과병원에서 2015-2017년 단일 시스템(Luna, Shinhung; 원추형 SLA 골수준 임플란트)으로 식립한 409명의 임플란트 1,031개를 분석한 후향적 단일기관 연구로, 조기 실패 (early failure)는 최종 보철 전 임상적 동요 또는 최종 보철 후 첫 방사선 사진의 임플란트 주위 방사선 투과상으로 정의되었으며 35개(26명)가 실패했다(논문 본문의 조기 생존율은 95.6%).

다변량 Cox 분석에서 수련의 식립 (조정 위험비 [adjusted hazard ratio, aHR] 2.86, 95% CI 1.37-5.94, p=0.005)과 하악 식립 (aHR 2.31, 95% CI 1.12-4.76, p=0.023)이 조기 실패 위험을 높였고, 성별·연령·직경·상악동 거상술 (sinus-floor elevation, SFE)·유도골재생술 (guided bone regeneration, GBR)은 유의하지 않았다; 수련의 3명은 기준 교수 대비 위험비가 4.01-12.76이었다.

병력과 골질·골량은 분석되지 않았고 실패 원인은 대부분 기록이 없으며 본문 생존율과 표의 개수가 일치하지 않아, 효과 크기는 정확한 위험도라기보다 술자 경험 효과의 신호로 해석해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Early implant failure in a single conical SLA bone-level system (Luna, Shinhung) at one centre, avoiding multi-brand heterogeneity.
- Resident surgeon (aHR 2.86, 95% CI 1.37-5.94, p=0.005) and mandible (aHR 2.31, 95% CI 1.12-4.76, p=0.023) were independent risk factors.
- Individual-surgeon analysis: residents 11, 6 and 7 had cHR 12.76, 8.46 and 4.01 versus the faculty reference.
- Cluster behavior: 6 patients (23%) with more than one early failure accounted for 43% of early failures.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective chart review, single center, STROBE, unit of analysis the implant.
- **Population**: 1,031 implants in 409 patients placed January 2015 to December 2017 (5 implants lost to follow-up excluded); median follow-up 7.2 months (IQR 5.6-9.9); median age 54 years (IQR 47-61); 51.2% maxilla; 89.7% posterior; 62.1% placed by faculty, 37.9% by residents (5 faculty, 10 residents); 1-stage 69.1%; SFE 21.5%; simultaneous GBR 30.5%, preoperative GBR 8.8%.
- **Early failure definition**: clinical mobility before placement of the final prosthesis, or perifixtural radiolucency at the first radiograph within several weeks after final prosthesis placement; survival time from placement to removal, otherwise to most recent follow-up. Failures after final prosthesis cementation were late failures (8 implants).
- **Follow-up checks**: Periotest or RFA every 1-2 months; referral to prosthodontics if Periotest value below 0 or ISQ above 70.
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier; univariate Cox then backward stepwise multivariate Cox with Wald test (18.53, 3 df, p<0.001); faculty member with most implants as reference for individual surgeons; p<0.05; RStudio 1.2.

- Case list: 4 failures with signs of infection, 1 with pain or discomfort, 1 from occlusal contact of a healing abutment with an antagonist, 2 with suspected uncontrolled diabetes, 1 from proximity of two fixtures; the cause was unrecorded for most.
- Authors' interpretation: faculty-resident difference suggests experience or competence matters; the mandibular finding contradicts a meta-analysis favouring the mandible, and is explained by mostly partially edentulous patients and the moderately rough surface.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Medical history not fully scrutinised; bone quality and quantity not analysed (GBR or SFE only a proxy for insufficient bone).
- Primary stability not recorded; short follow-up (median 7.2 months); 42 implants in 16 patients had no completed prosthesis by June 2019.
- In-text inconsistencies: 35/1,031 is 3.4%, not 4.4%; faculty 11/640 and residents 24/391 do not match the stated CSRs of 97.5% and 92.6%; mandible is not significant univariately. Table values were used here.
- Authors call for competency-based training and further prospective studies.

## 7. Glossary

- **Early implant failure (조기 임플란트 실패)**: here, mobility before final prosthesis or perifixtural radiolucency at the first post-prosthesis radiograph.
- **CSR (Cumulative Survival Rate, 누적 생존율)**: Kaplan-Meier survival estimate.
- **cHR / aHR (crude / adjusted Hazard Ratio, 조정 전/후 위험비)**: Cox regression effect sizes.
- **SFE (Sinus-Floor Elevation, 상악동저 거상술)** and **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 유도골재생술)**.
- **RFA / ISQ (Resonance Frequency Analysis / Implant Stability Quotient, 공진주파수분석 / 임플란트 안정성 지수)**.
