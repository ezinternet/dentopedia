## Why Ingested
User requested papers on retention loss of cervical (NCCL) resin restorations. This 3-year RCT is the clinical test of whether NaOCl deproteinization after etching improves retention, and it reinforces the in vitro conclusion in [[resin-bonding/hamdi-2025-surface-pretreatments-sclerotic-dentin-bond-sr]] that NaOCl gives no benefit on NCCL dentin; its premolar finding parallels the tooth-type effect in [[resin-bonding/omoto-2025-nccl-adhesion-strategies-5year-rct]].

## Three-line Summary
3-year randomized, controlled, double-blind split-mouth trial (NCT03086720): 30 patients with at least 2 NCCLs; composite restorations placed after 37% phosphoric acid etching with either a placebo solution (control) or 10% NaOCl deproteinization (experimental), Adper Single Bond 2 + Filtek Z350; 64 restorations in 23 patients evaluated at 3 years (FDI criteria; Kaplan-Meier, log-rank).
Annual failure rate was 9% (control) vs 17.8% (10% NaOCl), a non-significant difference (p=0.077); restorations in the mandible (p=0.039) and in premolars (p=0.013) had significantly lower clinical survival, while number of teeth in the mouth was not associated (p=0.320).
10% NaOCl deproteinization after acid etching gave no additional retention benefit (the point estimate trended worse), so the extra step is not supported; lesion location and tooth type, not the adhesive pretreatment, tracked retention loss. Abstract-only record - full text not retrieved.

## 세줄요약
3년 무작위 대조 이중맹검 split-mouth RCT (NCT03086720): 비우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 2개 이상 보유 환자 30명, 37% 인산 산부식 후 위약(대조) 또는 10% 차아염소산나트륨 (Sodium Hypochlorite, NaOCl) 단백질 제거 (Deproteinization) 적용 + Adper Single Bond 2/Filtek Z350 복합레진 수복; 3년 시점 23명·64개 수복물 평가 (FDI 기준, Kaplan-Meier·log-rank).
연간 실패율 (Annual Failure Rate, AFR)은 대조 9% vs NaOCl군 17.8%로 유의차 없음 (p=0.077); 하악 (p=0.039)과 소구치 (p=0.013) 수복물의 임상 생존율이 유의하게 낮았고, 잔존 치아 수는 무관 (p=0.320).
10% NaOCl 단백질 제거는 유지력 (Retention)을 추가로 높이지 못했고 (점추정치는 오히려 불량), 불필요한 추가 단계임; 유지 소실은 접착 전처리보다 병소 위치·치아 종류와 연관. 초록만 확보 - 전문 미확보.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Clinical (not bench) test of 10% NaOCl deproteinization after 37% phosphoric acid etching for NCCL composite restorations, with retention as the primary outcome.
- No additional retention from deproteinization; annual failure rate 9% (control) vs 17.8% (NaOCl), p=0.077.
- Identifies mandibular location (p=0.039) and premolar teeth (p=0.013) as lower-survival strata.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: randomized, controlled, split-mouth, double-blinded trial; placebo solution in control.
- **n**: 30 patients with at least 2 NCCLs enrolled; 64 restorations in 23 patients evaluated at 3 years.
- **Interventions**: control = 37% phosphoric acid + placebo + Adper Single Bond 2 + Filtek Z350; experimental = 37% phosphoric acid + 10% NaOCl + Adper Single Bond 2 + Filtek Z350.
- **Outcomes**: retention (primary), FDI criteria by one calibrated examiner at baseline (1 week), 6, 12, 24, 36 months.
- **Analysis**: Kaplan-Meier, log-rank test (alpha=0.05).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Annual failure rate, control | 9% |
| Annual failure rate, 10% NaOCl | 17.8% |
| Group difference | p=0.077 (not significant) |
| Number of teeth in mouth | p=0.320 (not significant) |
| Mandible vs maxilla | lower survival, p=0.039 |
| Premolars vs other teeth | lower survival, p=0.013 |

Authors' conclusion: deproteinization with 10% NaOCl did not promote additional retention of restorations in NCCLs.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only record; the full text was not retrieved, so attrition handling, power calculation, failure-mode detail and comparator definitions beyond the abstract are not verified here.
- Attrition: 30 patients enrolled, 23 evaluated at 3 years (64 restorations).
- Single adhesive system (a two-step etch-and-rinse adhesive); results may not transfer to universal adhesives or self-etch modes.
- The 8.8-point AFR gap was not statistically significant (p=0.077), so the trial is best read as showing no benefit rather than proven harm.

## 7. Glossary
- **NCCL**: noncarious cervical lesion, loss of cervical tooth structure not caused by caries.
- **Deproteinization**: removal of the exposed collagen layer after etching, here with 10% NaOCl.
- **AFR**: annual failure rate.
- **FDI criteria**: World Dental Federation clinical evaluation criteria for restorations.
- **Split-mouth**: each patient receives both treatments in different teeth.
