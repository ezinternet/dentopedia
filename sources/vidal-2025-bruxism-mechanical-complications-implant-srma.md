## Why Ingested

This is the most quantitatively detailed 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR)+메타분석 (Meta-Analysis, MA) on 이갈이 (Bruxism) → 임플란트 지지 보철 기계적 합병증 (mechanical complications in implant-supported prosthesis). The existing wiki holds [[implants/survival/chawki-2026-particularities-of-the-implant-supported]] (survival rates) and [[implants/survival/barboza-2026-bruxism-implant-failure-umbrella-review]] (OR 4.68 overall failure), but this paper provides a 7-subgroup 교차비 (Odds Ratio, OR) breakdown — 임플란트 파절 (implant fracture) OR=16.14, 스크류 파절 (screw fracture) OR=7.07, 세라믹 파절 (ceramic fracture) OR=4.36, 어버트먼트 파절 (abutment fracture) OR=13.88 — quantifying each complication type separately. Directly boosts [[overviews/bruxism-muscle-overload-axis]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis of 20 observational studies (13 in MA) examining bruxism and mechanical complications in implant-supported prostheses; databases searched from 1984 onward.
Overall unspecific mechanical failure OR=6.10; subgroup analysis: implant fracture OR=16.14, abutment fracture OR=13.88, prosthetic screw fracture OR=7.07, prosthesis mobility OR=4.51, ceramic fracture OR=4.36, prosthesis (acrylic/teeth) failure OR=3.66, screw loosening OR=3.40.
Bruxism substantially increases risk across all 7 mechanical complication subtypes analyzed; the 7-subgroup OR breakdown is more granular than prior umbrella reviews.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides the first 7-subgroup quantitative OR analysis of bruxism impact on distinct mechanical complication types in implant-supported prostheses.
- Overall unspecific mechanical failure OR = 6.10 (bruxism vs. no bruxism).
- Subgroup ORs: implant fracture 16.14, abutment fracture 13.88, screw fracture 7.07, prosthesis mobility 4.51, ceramic fracture 4.36, prosthesis (acrylic/teeth) failure 3.66, screw loosening 3.40.
- Confirms that bruxism elevates risk in *all* subgroups analyzed — no complication type was unaffected.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Study types eligible: observational descriptive and/or analytical (cross-sectional, case-control, cohort) from 1984 onward.
- Inclusion criterion: studies correlating mechanical failure in implant-supported prosthesis with and without bruxism.
- Bias quality analysis performed on all selected studies.
- Initial retrieval: 934 studies across all databases; duplicates removed → 320 full-text reads → 20 included in SR → 13 in MA.
- Meta-analytic statistic: 교차비 (Odds Ratio, OR) for bruxism presence vs. absence.
- 7 pre-specified subgroup analyses: implant fracture, prosthesis failure (acrylic/teeth), ceramic fracture, abutment fracture, screw fracture, screw loosening, prosthesis mobility.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Complication type | OR (bruxism vs. no bruxism) |
|---|---|
| Unspecific mechanical failure | 6.10 |
| Implant fracture | 16.14 |
| Abutment fracture | 13.88 |
| Prosthetic screw fracture | 7.07 |
| Prosthesis mobility | 4.51 |
| Ceramic fracture | 4.36 |
| Prosthesis (acrylic/teeth) failure | 3.66 |

All subgroups showed increased risk with bruxism presence.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only source; confidence intervals, I², heterogeneity statistics, and GRADE ratings are not available from the abstract.
- Only observational studies included (no RCTs); no grey literature included.
- Bruxism definition and diagnostic criteria likely vary across the 20 included studies (not assessable from abstract alone).
- Subgroup sample sizes (number of studies per subgroup) not reported in abstract.

## 7. Glossary

- **이갈이 (Bruxism, BRX)**: parafunctional oral habit involving grinding or clenching of teeth, generates excessive occlusal forces on implant prostheses
- **교차비 (Odds Ratio, OR)**: measure of association between bruxism and each complication type; OR >1 = increased odds with bruxism
- **임플란트 파절 (Implant fracture)**: fracture of the implant fixture itself
- **어버트먼트 파절 (Abutment fracture)**: fracture of the prosthetic abutment component
- **스크류 파절 (Prosthetic screw fracture)**: fracture of the retention screw
- **스크류 이완 (Screw loosening)**: loosening without fracture of the retention screw
- **세라믹 파절 (Ceramic fracture)**: chipping or fracture of ceramic veneering material
- **보철 이동성 (Prosthesis mobility)**: clinically detectable movement/instability of the prosthesis
