## Why Ingested

Compares demineralization-inhibition ability of resin-modified glass ionomer (RMGI) luting cement versus three bioactive luting cements (Activa Bioactive Cement, Ceramir Crown and Bridge, TheraCem) and a self-adhesive resin cement at the crown margin (root dentin), quantifying secondary caries prevention capacity as lesion depth (µm) by polarized light microscopy.

→ [[glass-ionomer/huang-2020-inhibition-root-dentin-demineralization]]

## Three-line Summary

RMGI cement (Rely X Luting Plus, LUT) produced the largest net demineralization inhibition zone at 7700 ± 2500 µm; all three bioactive cements showed net inhibition—TheraCem (THE) at 2100 ± 2600 µm, Ceramir (CER) at 3800 ± 1900 µm, Activa (ACT) at 1400 ± 700 µm—whereas self-adhesive resin cement (Rely X Unicem 2, UNI) showed net demineralization at −2000 ± 1700 µm. This in vitro pH-cycling model at CEJ restorations with polarized light microscopy demonstrates ion-releasing cements provide meaningful demineralization resistance, though at reduced magnitude compared to RMGI. Clinically, RMGI or bioactive luting cements may be preferable for high-caries-risk patients receiving crown restorations where secondary caries is a concern.

## 세줄요약

RMGI 시멘트(Rely X Luting Plus)가 7700 ± 2500 µm로 가장 큰 탈회 억제 영역을 보였고, 바이오액티브 시멘트 3종(Ceramir 3800 µm, TheraCem 2100 µm, Activa 1400 µm)은 모두 순탈회 억제를 나타냈으며, 자기접착 레진 시멘트(Rely X Unicem 2)는 −2000 µm로 오히려 탈회가 진행됐다. CEJ(백악질-에나멜 경계)에 수복 후 pH 사이클링 30일·편광현미경 정량 평가로, 이온 방출 시멘트가 치근 상아질 탈회를 의미 있게 억제하되 RMGI 대비 27%(TheraCem) 수준임을 확인했다. 임상적으로 2차우식 고위험 환자의 크라운 합착 시 RMGI 또는 바이오액티브 시멘트가 자기접착 레진 시멘트보다 유리하다.

## Key Contributions

1. Provides direct quantitative comparison of five luting cement types for root dentin demineralization inhibition using a validated pH-cycling/polarized light microscopy model.
2. Demonstrates all three bioactive cements (Activa, Ceramir, TheraCem) inhibit root dentin demineralization net positively, distinguishing them from conventional resin cement.
3. Establishes RMGI (Rely X Luting Plus) as the reference standard for demineralization inhibition (~2× to ~5× the bioactive cement values).
4. Documents that self-adhesive resin cement (Rely X Unicem 2) provides no protection and promotes net demineralization at root dentin.

## Methodology

- **Design**: In vitro comparative study
- **Specimens**: 50 extracted molars, prepared at the cementum-enamel junction (CEJ), n=10 per group
- **Groups**: 
  - ACT: Activa Bioactive Cement (bioactive)
  - CER: Ceramir Crown and Bridge (bioactive)
  - THE: TheraCem (bioactive)
  - UNI: Rely X Unicem 2 (self-adhesive resin cement — control)
  - LUT: Rely X Luting Plus (RMGI — reference)
- **Protocol**: 30-day cycling between demineralization solution (pH 4) and remineralization solution
- **Evaluation**: Sections cut to 100 µm; polarized light microscopy; net demineralization lesion depth (µm) reported as mean ± SD
- **Outcome direction**: Positive = inhibition zone; Negative = demineralization zone

## Key Results

| Cement | Type | Net lesion depth (µm, mean ± SD) | vs. LUT |
|---|---|---|---|
| LUT (Rely X Luting Plus) | RMGI | 7700 ± 2500 | reference |
| CER (Ceramir Crown and Bridge) | Bioactive | 3800 ± 1900 | 49% |
| THE (TheraCem) | Bioactive | 2100 ± 2600 | 27% |
| ACT (Activa Bioactive Cement) | Bioactive | 1400 ± 700 | 18% |
| UNI (Rely X Unicem 2) | Self-adhesive resin | −2000 ± 1700 | net demineralization |

Statistical ranking (authors): LUT > CER, THE, ACT > UNI

## Limitations

- In vitro pH-cycling model; clinical translation uncertain
- n=10 per group; small sample size with large standard deviations (particularly THE: ±2600 µm overlapping ACT and LUT ranges)
- Polarized light microscopy at 100 µm sections; does not capture 3D mineral distribution
- No microhardness or quantitative mineral analysis (TMR/µCT)
- CEJ restoration model only; results may not extrapolate to enamel margin restorations
- No aging or fatigue component

## Glossary

- **RMGI (레진 강화형 글래스아이오노머 시멘트, Resin-Modified Glass Ionomer)**: Hybrid between GIC and resin composite; retains fluoride/calcium ion release with improved mechanical properties
- **Bioactive cement (바이오액티브 시멘트)**: Calcium silicate-based luting cement releasing Ca²⁺, OH⁻, and other ions to raise pH and promote remineralization
- **CEJ (백악질-에나멜 경계부, Cementum-Enamel Junction)**: Junction between crown enamel and root cementum; high-risk site for secondary/root caries
- **pH cycling (pH 순환모델)**: Alternating demineralization (pH 4) and remineralization solution to simulate oral caries challenge in vitro
- **Polarized light microscopy (편광 광학현미경)**: Technique to visualize lesion depth in demineralized enamel/dentin sections based on optical properties of mineral loss zones
- **Secondary caries (이차우식)**: Caries developing at the margin of an existing restoration
