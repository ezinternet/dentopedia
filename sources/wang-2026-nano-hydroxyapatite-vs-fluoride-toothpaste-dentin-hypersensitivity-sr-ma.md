## Three-line Summary

PROSPERO-registered (CRD420251272119) PRISMA 2020 systematic review and meta-analysis of 13 RCTs (609 adults with dentin hypersensitivity; n-HAP 296 vs fluoride 313) comparing nano-hydroxyapatite (n-HAP) toothpaste with fluoride toothpaste, 7 databases to December 2025.

n-HAP beat fluoride early (VAS at 2 weeks, 4 studies, n=232; tactile threshold SMD -1.31 at 2 weeks per abstract), but fluoride was better for cold air sensitivity at 8 weeks (SMD 0.35, 95% CI 0.05-0.65, I²=0%, 2 studies); 15-20% n-HAP pooled SMD -0.77 (95% CI -1.16 to -0.39, I²=11%) although the subgroup-difference test was not significant (p=0.41).

n-HAP looks like a faster-acting option for mechanical/tactile sensitivity and fluoride may suit cold-triggered, long-term maintenance, but RoB 1 raised "some concerns" in most trials, follow-up was only 1-12 weeks, several outcomes had I² 80-97%, and the 15-20% optimum is exploratory.

## 세줄요약

PROSPERO 사전등록(CRD420251272119)·PRISMA 2020 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석: 상아질 과민증(Dentin Hypersensitivity, DH) 성인 609명(나노수산화인회석 296 vs 불소 313)을 대상으로 한 RCT 13편에서 나노수산화인회석(nano-hydroxyapatite, n-HAP) 치약과 불소 치약을 비교(7개 DB, 2025년 12월까지).

n-HAP가 초기에 우세(시각통증척도 VAS 2주 4편 n=232; 초록 기준 촉각역치 2주 SMD -1.31)했으나 냉공기 민감도는 8주에 불소가 우세(SMD 0.35, 95% CI 0.05-0.65, I²=0%, 2편); 15-20% n-HAP 합산 SMD -0.77(95% CI -1.16~-0.39, I²=11%)이나 하위군 간 차이 검정은 비유의(p=0.41).

n-HAP는 기계적·촉각 자극 과민에 빠른 효과, 불소는 냉자극 과민의 장기 유지에 적합할 수 있으나, 대부분 RoB 1에서 "some concerns", 추적 1-12주, 일부 결과 I² 80-97%, 15-20% 최적 농도는 탐색적 결과.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First pooled RCT comparison of n-HAP toothpaste against fluoride toothpaste as complete products for DH.
- Time-resolved pattern: n-HAP favoured early (VAS, tactile), fluoride favoured for cold air sensitivity at 8 weeks.
- Exploratory concentration subgroup: 15-20% n-HAP most consistent.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 7 databases (PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, CNKI, Wanfang, SinoMed) to December 2025, no language restriction; trial registries and reference lists searched.
- Inclusion: parallel/crossover RCTs, systemically healthy adults ≥18 y with DH; n-HAP toothpaste (any concentration) vs fluoride toothpaste (NaF, SnF₂, AmF, Na-MFP). n-HAP combined with other actives excluded unless control matched.
- Outcomes: VAS, tactile sensitivity (Yeaple probe), cold air sensitivity (Schiff), air blast sensitivity.
- Cochrane RoB 1 (two independent reviewers); GRADE; SMD effect measure; RevMan 5.4, Stata 17, R 4.2.1; subgroups by follow-up time and n-HAP concentration; leave-one-out, fixed-effect and high-RoB exclusion sensitivity analyses; Begg/Egger when ≥10 studies. Data read from figures with GetData Graph Digitizer when needed.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Records: 2,241 identified; 13 RCTs included; 609 participants (n-HAP 296; fluoride 313); n-HAP 5-30% (mostly 10-20%); fluoride comparators varied (Na-MFP, NaF, SnF₂, AmF). No adverse effects reported.
- **VAS, 2 weeks** (4 studies, n=232): SMD 8.81 (95% CI 0.31-17.30; reported p=0.0017), favouring n-HAP; I²=80.2%. At 4 weeks (4 studies, n=284) n-HAP still favoured (p<0.00001) with I²=97.1%; at 8 and 12 weeks the difference shrank toward non-significance.
- **VAS by concentration**: 15-20% n-HAP SMD -0.77 (95% CI -1.16 to -0.39; I²=11%); lower concentration subgroup SMD -0.35 (95% CI -0.99 to 0.30; I²=58%); overall SMD -0.56 (95% CI -0.86 to -0.27; I²=28%); subgroup-difference p=0.41.
- **Tactile sensitivity**: overall SMD -1.13 (95% CI -1.66 to -0.61) favouring n-HAP; strongest at 2 weeks (p<0.00001, I²=56%; abstract gives SMD -1.31, 95% CI -1.80 to -0.82); 4 weeks p=0.02, I²=92%.
- **Cold air sensitivity**: no difference at 1, 2, 4, 6 weeks; at 8 weeks fluoride better, SMD 0.35 (95% CI 0.05-0.65; p=0.02; I²=0%; 2 studies). Overall p=0.07.
- **Air blast sensitivity**: overall SMD -0.77 (95% CI -1.38 to -0.15), I²=92%; authors draw no conclusion.
- Sensitivity analyses: VAS robust to leave-one-out; Egger p=0.198 (VAS), 0.989 (ABS).
- GRADE as stated: high certainty for VAS-concentration, CAS-time and TS-time; low for VAS-time, ABS-time, ABS-concentration.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Most trials "some concerns" for RoB, mainly blinding; follow-up only 1-12 weeks; modest total n; heterogeneous fluoride comparators and VAS scales (0-10 vs 0-100); ABS not standardized.
- Concentration subgroup differences not significant (p=0.41): 15-20% optimum is exploratory. Mechanistic explanations (tubule occlusion by n-HAP, fluorapatite maturation) are hypotheses; the trials measured symptoms only.
- Source-data inconsistencies reproduced as stated and flagged: VAS 2-week SMD 8.81 (CI 0.31-17.30) is implausibly large for an SMD and its p-value (0.0017) is not consistent with that CI; tactile 2-week SMD (-1.31) appears only in the abstract; per-study n stated as 28-105 in Results vs 20-75 in Discussion; age 18-71 vs 18-80; the "high certainty" GRADE label sits oddly with I²=92-97% on some outcomes (e.g. TS-time).
- Findings do not apply to neural-pathway agents (potassium nitrate, strontium). Sequential n-HAP-then-fluoride use is a hypothesis, not tested.
