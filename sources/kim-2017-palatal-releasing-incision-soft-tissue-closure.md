## Why Ingested

치조제 분할술 및 소량 GBR 후 협측 골막 절개 없이 구개측 감장 절개만으로 긴장 없는 일차폐쇄를 달성하는 술식의 근거가 필요해 수집. 기존 [[suture-wound-closure/moslemi-2016-periosteal-releasing-incision-diode-laser-gbr]]이 협측 골막 절개(diode laser PRI)를 다루는 반면, 본 증례는 구개측 감장 절개라는 대안 경로를 기술해 [[suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]]의 flap advancement 선택지를 보완한다.

## Three-line Summary

Case series (4 cases) describes a simple palatal releasing incision technique for tension-free primary soft tissue closure in ridge splitting and small GBR procedures.

A horizontal incision 5-7mm palatal to the crestal incision allows flap mobilization without buccal periosteal release, reducing postoperative swelling, hematoma, and pain.

This minimally invasive technique is effective for small-volume ridge augmentation and ridge splitting, particularly in patients who need to minimize postoperative discomfort; not applicable in mandible or thin palatal tissue.

## 세줄요약

치조제 분할술 및 소량 골이식 후 연조직 일차폐쇄를 위한 구개 감장 절개술을 4개 증례로 기술한 증례 보고입니다.

치조정 절개선에서 구개측으로 5-7mm 떨어진 곳에 수평 절개를 시행하고 판막을 이완시키면 협측 골막 절개 없이도 긴장 없는 봉합이 가능해 부종, 혈종, 동통을 줄일 수 있습니다.

이 최소 침습적 술식은 소량 골증대 및 치조제 분할술 후 환자의 불편감을 최소화해야 하는 경우에 유용하나, 하악 또는 구개 조직이 얇은 경우에는 적용이 어렵습니다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Describes a simple palatal releasing incision (horizontal incision 5-7mm palatal to crestal incision) as an alternative to buccal periosteal releasing incision for tension-free primary closure
- Eliminates need for buccal periosteal release, reducing postoperative swelling, hematoma, and pain
- Introduces a rotated palatal flap modification for cases adjacent to teeth
- Demonstrates application in ridge splitting with simultaneous implant placement, ridge expansion, and GBR procedures

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Case series of 4 clinical cases demonstrating the palatal releasing incision technique. Cases included: (1) ridge splitting with simultaneous implant placement, (2) ridge expansion, (3–4) guided bone regeneration (GBR). Follow-up 3–4 months with clinical photograph documentation of healing. No control group; qualitative outcome assessment.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Tension-free primary closure achieved in all 4 cases without buccal periosteal releasing incision
- Horizontal palatal incision placed 5-7mm palatal to the crestal incision
- Rotated palatal flap modification applicable for areas adjacent to teeth
- Postoperative healing uneventful with minimal swelling in all cases
- No hematoma or pain complications reported
- Effective for small-volume ridge augmentation and ridge splitting procedures

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Case series design; no controlled comparison
- Small sample (n=4)
- Subjective clinical assessment of outcomes; no standardized measurement
- Technique limited to maxillary arch with adequate palatal tissue thickness
- Not applicable in mandible or patients with thin palatal mucosa
- Longer follow-up and larger controlled studies needed

## 7. Glossary

- **Palatal releasing incision (구개 감장 절개):** Horizontal incision placed 5-7mm palatal to the crestal incision to mobilize the palatal flap for tension-free closure
- **Ridge splitting (치조제 분할술):** Surgical technique to expand a narrow alveolar ridge by splitting it longitudinally
- **GBR — Guided Bone Regeneration (골유도재생술):** Membrane-based technique to regenerate bone defects
- **Primary closure (일차폐쇄):** Tension-free wound closure by direct approximation of flap edges
- **Periosteal releasing incision (골막 이완 절개):** Incision through the periosteum to increase flap mobility; the palatal approach in this paper avoids buccal periosteal release
