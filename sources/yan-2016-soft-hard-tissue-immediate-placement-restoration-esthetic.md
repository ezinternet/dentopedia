# Source Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis comparing soft and hard tissue outcomes of immediate placement, immediate restoration, and immediate loading protocols vs conventional protocols for single-tooth implants in the esthetic zone.


## Why Ingested

심미 영역 즉시 식립 + 즉시 수복 후 연·경조직 결과 SR — [[yan-2016-soft-hard-tissue-immediate-placement-restoration-esthetic]] 페이지의 1차 근거; IIP + 즉시 임시치관의 연조직·변연골 변화 종합.

## Three-line Summary

A systematic review and meta-analysis of 13 RCTs (searched up to December 2014) compared soft and hard tissue outcomes of immediate placement, immediate restoration, and immediate loading vs conventional protocols for single-tooth implants in the esthetic zone. All meta-analyzed outcomes (bone level mesial/distal/mean, papillae mesial, midbuccal mucosa) showed SMDs with 95% CIs crossing zero — no statistically significant differences. Immediately placed, restored, or loaded single-tooth implants result in equivalent peri-implant tissue changes compared to conventional protocols.

## 세줄요약

심미구역 단일치 임플란트에서 즉시 식립(Immediate Placement, IIP)·즉시 수복(Immediate Restoration, IR)·즉시 로딩(Immediate Loading, IL) vs 기존 프로토콜을 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (13 RCT, 2014년까지). 모든 메타분석 결과(변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 근원심·치간유두·중안면 점막)에서 표준화 평균차 (Standardized Mean Difference, SMD) 95% 신뢰구간이 영점을 포함해 통계적 유의차 없음. 심미구역 즉시 프로토콜은 기존 프로토콜과 동등한 경·연조직 결과를 나타낸다.

## Study Design

- **Type**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (SR+MA)
- **Databases**: PubMed, EMBASE, Cochrane
- **Search period**: Up to December 2014
- **Inclusion**: RCTs only, single-tooth implants, esthetic zone, hard and/or soft tissue outcomes
- **N included**: 13 RCTs
- **Three comparison groups**: (1) immediate placement vs delayed, (2) immediate restoration vs conventional restoration, (3) immediate loading vs conventional/delayed loading

## Key Quantitative Findings

### Hard tissue (marginal bone level)

| Comparison | Outcome | SMD | 95% CI | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate placement | Bone level mesial | -0.04 mm | -0.25 to 0.17 | ns |
| Immediate placement | Bone level distal | -0.15 mm | -0.38 to 0.09 | ns |
| Immediate placement | Mean bone level | 0.05 mm | -0.18 to 0.27 | ns |
| Immediate loading | Bone level mesial | -0.05 mm | -0.15 to 0.06 | ns |
| Immediate loading | Bone level distal | -0.02 mm | -0.09 to 0.05 | ns |

### Soft tissue

| Outcome | SMD | 95% CI | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Papillae mesial | 0.18 mm | -0.00 to 0.37 | ns (borderline) |
| Midbuccal mucosa | -0.22 mm | -1.29 to 0.85 | ns |

## Conclusion

Immediately placed, immediately restored, or immediately loaded single-tooth implants in the esthetic zone result in similar hard and soft tissue changes compared to conventional protocols. All SMD values crossed zero; no statistically significant differences detected.

## Limitations

- Only RCTs included; no non-RCT evidence synthesized
- Search cutoff December 2014; does not capture post-2014 trials
- High heterogeneity likely for soft tissue outcomes (wide midbuccal CI)
- Short follow-up in some included RCTs
