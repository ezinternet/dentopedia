## Why Ingested

Surveillance hit (bone-graft-material: allograft/xenograft/alloplast). It adds a single-product evidence map for a resorbable carbonate apatite alloplast (CO3Ap granules, Cytrans) that the wiki's general graft-material pages do not cover, and it contrasts with the histomorphometric alloplast-vs-bovine-xenograft evidence in [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/alkandari-2025-bcp-dbbm-sinus-elevation-sr-ma]] and the material-class survey in [[wiki/bone-regeneration/zhao-2021-bone-grafts-substitutes-dentistry-review]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA/Cochrane; MEDLINE, EMBASE, CENTRAL, ClinicalTrials.gov plus hand search to Nov 2023) of 43 publications on carbonate apatite granules (CO3Ap, Cytrans): 5 in vitro, 28 animal, 7 human clinical (all small; 4 sinus-augmentation reports incl. one 3-year follow-up, 2 periodontal-defect studies, 1 three-case series), 3 reviews; a 65-year-old periodontal regeneration case is added.

In rabbit/dog models CO3Ap formed more bone than hydroxyapatite and beta-tricalcium phosphate (14.3-fold and 4.3-fold more mature bone at 4 weeks in rabbit femur) and more early bone than bovine xenograft; in humans sinus-lift height loss was 16.4-27.8% at 12-18 months, new bone 33.8% at 7 months, 100% implant survival at 3 years (n = 13), and bone resorption was 14.2% vs 24.2% for deproteinized bovine bone at 6 months (n = 6 vs 8, p < 0.001).

Evidence is promising but thin: human studies are small and short, comparisons to autogenous bone and xenograft figures are cross-study historical comparisons, no randomized trial exists, and the authors themselves call for multicenter RCTs.

## 세줄요약

체계적 고찰(PRISMA/Cochrane; MEDLINE·EMBASE·CENTRAL·ClinicalTrials.gov + 2023년 11월까지 수기검색): 탄산아파타이트 과립 (Carbonate Apatite granules, CO3Ap, Cytrans) 관련 43편 — 시험관 5·동물 28·사람 임상 7(모두 소규모)·리뷰 3 — 에 65세 환자의 치주재생 증례 1건을 추가.

토끼·개 모델에서 CO3Ap는 수산화인회석 (Hydroxyapatite, HAp)·베타-삼칼슘인산염 (beta-Tricalcium Phosphate, β-TCP)보다 많은 골을 형성(토끼 대퇴골 4주 성숙골 14.3배·4.3배)했고 초기 골형성은 우골 이종골 (Bovine xenograft)보다 우수; 사람에서는 상악동거상 후 높이 감소 16.4-27.8%(12-18개월), 7개월 신생골 33.8%, 3년 임플란트 생존율 100%(n = 13), 6개월 골흡수 14.2% vs 단백질 제거 우골 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 24.2%(n = 6 vs 8, p < 0.001).

유망하나 근거가 얇다: 사람 연구가 소규모·단기이고 자가골·이종골과의 비교는 연구 간 과거 수치 비교이며 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)이 없어, 저자 스스로 다기관 RCT를 요구.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic review dedicated to carbonate apatite (CO3Ap) granules as an intraoral alloplast; 43 publications classified by design (5 in vitro, 28 animal, 7 human, 3 reviews).
- Collates the 7 human studies: four sinus-floor-augmentation reports (first-in-human n = 8; two-stage n = 13 and its 3-year follow-up; CO3Ap vs DBBM n = 6 vs 8), two periodontal-regeneration studies (REGROTH + CO3Ap n = 10; 9-month pilot, 4 patients/7 sites) and a 3-case report series (immediate implant, lateral GBR, vertical GBR).
- Describes the proposed mechanism: CO3Ap is stable at physiologic pH but dissolves at low pH, supports osteoclast differentiation in direct contact (cathepsin K, TRAP) and upregulates osteoblast markers (ALP, osteopontin, osteocalcin), so the granules resorb while new bone replaces them.
- Adds a 65-year-old male case (tooth 8, cemental tear, 10 mm pocket) treated with FGF plus CO3Ap: probing depth 10 mm to 4 mm at 6 months with no bleeding on probing, stable at 12 months.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA and Cochrane guidance, PICO in Table 1) plus single case report; no meta-analysis, no formal risk-of-bias tool reported in the retrieved text.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed), ClinicalTrials.gov, EMBASE (OVID), CENTRAL; hand search 2012-Nov 2023 of J Periodontol, J Clin Periodontol, Clin Oral Implants Res, J Dent Res, Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants, Int J Oral Maxillofac Surg, Int J Periodontics Restorative Dent. Search string: ("carbonate apatite" or "carbonated apatite" or "Cytrans") and ("periodontal surgery" or "periodontology" or "implant" or "oral surgery"). Last search 1 Nov 2023.
- **Screening**: Two reviewers (MN, CR) independently; third (TM) for disagreements.
- **n**: 43 publications (5 in vitro, 28 animal, 7 human, 3 reviews); 10 of 28 animal studies and all human studies discussed in the text.
- **Outcomes**: Varied (new bone formation, residual graft, resorption/elevated bone height, probing depth, CAL, furcation status, implant survival).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Animal (10 highlighted studies)
- Rat tibia: CO3Ap mimicked natural remodeling (osteoblast apposition, osteoclast resorption) while sintered HAp did not form bone in the same timeframe.
- Rabbit femur vs Bio-Oss: more new bone at 4 weeks, partially replaced by 8 weeks while Bio-Oss remained unchanged; difference in new bone at 8 weeks not significant.
- Rabbit femur (Hayashi 2019): mature bone 14.3-fold (vs HAp) and 4.3-fold (vs beta-TCP) at 4 weeks, 7.5-fold and 1.4-fold at 12 weeks; HAp never resorbed.
- Beagle mandible: Mano 2019 reported 75% new bone at 12 weeks and ridge-contour restoration unlike NEOBONE or Bio-Oss; Sato 2020 vertical defects: significantly more bone than sham or bovine xenograft at 4 weeks, trend (not significant) over beta-TCP and HAp.

Human

Regulatory/market context: Japan approval Dec 2017, market from Feb 2018; FDA approval Aug 2020; US introduction Feb 2022.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Human evidence is 7 small studies (n = 3 to 13 per study), mostly single-arm and short (6-36 months); only one had a bovine-xenograft comparator.
- Comparisons with autogenous bone and bovine xenograft rely on historical/cross-study figures with different follow-up times and methods, not head-to-head data.
- No randomized controlled trial; the authors identify the lack of large multicenter RCTs against allograft, xenograft and sham controls as the single greatest drawback.
- Animal studies (28) dominate the evidence; formal risk-of-bias assessment is not described in the retrieved text; no funding/conflict-of-interest statement is present although the product is commercial.
- Authors state future trials are needed for immediate implant placement and extraction-site healing.

## 6. Related Work

- alkandari-2025: SR+MA of 10 RCTs showing biphasic calcium phosphate gives more new bone and less residual graft than DBBM in sinus lifts, a parallel finding for resorbable synthetic alloplasts.
- zhao-2021: narrative review of graft material classes; says no substitute reproduces autograft's four properties, whereas this review claims osteoinductive behaviour for CO3Ap.
- cha-2024: noninferiority RCT of a synthetic biphasic substitute vs DBBM in GBR.

## 7. Glossary

- **CO3Ap (carbonate apatite) granules**: low-crystalline, carbonate-substituted apatite alloplast (Cytrans) made by a dissolution-precipitation reaction; resorbed by osteoclasts and replaced by bone.
- **Alloplast**: fully synthetic graft material, free of human- or animal-derived tissue and thus of disease-transmission risk.
- **DBBM**: deproteinized bovine bone mineral (e.g. Bio-Oss), a slowly resorbing xenograft.
- **Elevated bone height (EBH)**: height of augmented bone in the sinus floor, used to quantify graft resorption.
- **REGROTH**: 0.3% basic fibroblast growth factor (FGF-2) preparation used with flap surgery for periodontal regeneration in Japan.
