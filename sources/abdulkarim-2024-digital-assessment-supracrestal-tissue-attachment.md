## Why Ingested

기존 [[periodontics/alam-2024-supracrestal-tissue-attachment-biologic-width]]와 [[periodontics/hamasni-2017-biologic-width-restorative-dentistry]]는 biologic width(SCTA/STA)의 정의·침범·교정을 정성적으로 다루지만, "2.04 mm는 모든 치아에 보편적인가?"라는 정량 질문에는 답을 주지 못한다. 본 in-vivo 디지털 측정 연구(203치)는 STA 치수가 치아 위치·표현형(phenotype)에 따라 크게 달라짐(하악·전치·얇은 표현형에서 더 큼)을 보여, biologic-width 페이지들의 "표준 2 mm" 전제를 정량적으로 보강·한정한다.

## One-line Summary

Retrospective in-vivo digital-superimposition study (DICOM+STL, 19 patients / 203 teeth) measuring supracrestal tissue attachment dimensions (STADs): overall mean 2.05 mm but varying by tooth position (mandibular>maxillary, anterior>posterior) and inversely with buccal bone thickness, gingival thickness, and keratinized tissue width — STA is not a universal 2.04 mm.

## 한줄요약

후향적 in-vivo 디지털 중첩 연구(DICOM+STL, 19명/203치) — 초치조상부 조직부착 치수(Supracrestal Tissue Attachment Dimension, STAD) 평균 2.05 mm이나, 치아 위치(하악>상악, 전치>구치)와 표현형에 따라 크게 다르고 협측 골두께·치은두께·각화치은폭과는 역상관 → biologic width는 모든 치아에서 2.04 mm로 고정된 값이 아님.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to combine CBCT (DICOM) + intraoral scan (STL) superimposition + clinical probe depths for **noninvasive** measurement of supracrestal tissue attachment dimensions (STADs) in vivo.
- Confirms the classic Gargiulo ~2.04 mm mean (overall STADs 2.05 mm) **but demonstrates it is not universal** — STADs range 0.02–5.85 mm and vary systematically by tooth position.
- Introduces the "phenotypic package" concept: thin buccal bone + thin gingiva + narrow keratinized tissue cluster together and predict **larger** STADs; thick phenotype predicts smaller STADs.
- Clinical rule-of-thumb: sites with larger STADs (mandibular, anterior, thin phenotype) are most easily violated by subgingival margins/crown lengthening.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Subjects**: 19 periodontally healthy implant-planning patients (14 F / 5 M; mean age 52.7 y). Exclusions: periodontal disease, SRP history, altered passive eruption (>2 mm gingival display), recession, wear, restorations, scatter.
- **Imaging**: pre-op CBCT (CS 8100) → DICOM; IOS (CEREC Omnicam) → STL; best-fit superimposition in Blue Sky Plan.
- **Measurements (mid-facial, per tooth)**: TL, FGM–CEJ, CEJ–BC, GT (at crest), TW, thickest BBT; MFPD + KTW clinically with colored probe. FGM–BC = FGM–CEJ + CEJ–BC. **STAD = FGM–BC − MFPD.**
- **Grouping**: MxA / MxC / MxP / MnA / MnC / MnP (203 teeth).
- **Stats**: one-way ANOVA + Bonferroni post hoc; Pearson correlation + linear regression; SPSS v29; p<0.05. Calibrated digital examiner ICC 0.99 (FGM–BC).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Overall STADs 2.05 mm** (SD 0.99; range 0.02–5.85).
- By position (mm): MxA 2.34, MxC 2.12, MxP 1.34; MnA 2.56, MnC 2.70, MnP 1.57. ANOVA p<0.001.
- **Mandibular > maxillary** (mean diff 0.44, p=0.001); **anterior/canine > posterior** in both arches (p<0.001).
- STADs correlations: **positive** with FGM–BC (r=0.830), CEJ–BC (r=0.574), FGM–CEJ (r=0.298), TL (r=0.159); **negative** with MFPD (r=−0.480), BBT (r=−0.419), TW (r=−0.373), GT (r=−0.283), KTW (r=−0.138, p=0.05).
- **No** association with age (p=0.272) or gender (p=0.558).
- BBT–GT r=0.679 (p<0.001); BBT/GT positively correlate with KTW, negatively with CEJ–BC. Maxillary BBT/GT/KTW > mandibular.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective; small n (19 patients); clinical examiner not formally calibrated (single operator).
- CBCT resolution loss on magnification forced exclusion of many sites → reduced sample.
- Molar measurements taken on mesiobuccal/mesial roots only; no reference marks to guarantee identical clinical vs radiographic sites.
- Mid-facial sites only — not generalizable to interproximal/lingual/palatal.
- Possible unintended inclusion of mild altered-passive-eruption cases.

## 7. Glossary

- **STA / STAD (Supracrestal Tissue Attachment / Dimension)**: 2017-World-Workshop term for "biologic width" — junctional epithelium + connective tissue attachment; measured coronal-to-crest dimension.
- **Bone sounding**: transgingival probing to the alveolar crest under anesthesia to derive the attachment dimension.
- **DICOM / STL superimposition**: registration of CBCT hard-tissue (DICOM) onto intraoral-scan soft-tissue (STL) surfaces for combined hard+soft measurement.
- **BBT / GT / KTW**: buccal bone thickness / gingival thickness / keratinized tissue width — the dentogingival phenotype components.
- **Phenotypic package**: co-clustering of thin bone + thin gingiva + narrow keratinized tissue (thin phenotype) vs the thick counterpart, here predicting STAD magnitude.
- **CEJ–BC**: cementoenamel-junction-to-bone-crest distance.
