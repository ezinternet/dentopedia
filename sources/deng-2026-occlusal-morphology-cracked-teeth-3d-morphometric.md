## Three-line Summary

Case-control study (n=83 cracked molars + 83 matched controls) using 3D intraoral scans to quantify occlusal morphology differences; steeper and taller functional cusps on the crack-initiation side independently predicted molar cracking.

Maxillary CIS cuspal inclination was 31.87° vs 26.01° in controls (P<.05); mandibular CIS functional cuspal height carried OR=8.177 in multivariate regression — the highest single predictor across both arches.

The study provides the first reference intervals linking specific cusp geometry to cracking risk, with clinical implications for morphology-guided occlusal adjustment and early risk stratification.

## 세줄요약

인트라오럴 스캔 3D 형태계측 증례-대조 연구(n=83+83): 기능교두 경사도·높이가 균열치 독립 위험인자이며, 균열 시작부(CIS) 교두가 정상 대조군보다 4~6° 가파르고 0.2~0.4 mm 더 높다.

다변량 회귀에서 하악 CIS 기능교두 높이 OR=8.177로 단일 지표 중 최고 위험; 상악 CIS 경사도 OR=1.098, 상악 CPS 높이 OR=3.407.

균열 위험 조기 탐지·교합 조정 계획에 활용 가능한 최초의 교두 형태 정량 참조 기준 제시.

## One-line Summary
Case-control study (83 cracked molars vs 83 controls) using 3D intraoral scans to quantify occlusal morphology risk factors for cracked teeth; steeper and taller functional cusps independently predicted cracking.

## 한줄요약

## 2. Key Contributions
- First 3D morphometric quantification of functional cuspal inclination/height as cracked tooth risk factors
- Identified crack initiation side (CIS) vs propagation side (CPS) morphologic differences
- Maxillary: CIS inclination OR 1.098, CPS height OR 3.407
- Mandibular: CIS functional cuspal height OR 8.177 (highest single risk factor identified)
- Provides reference intervals for early risk detection and morphology-guided occlusal adjustment

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Paired case-control comparative clinical study
- Sample: 83 cracked molars (68 patients) + 83 matched controls (69 volunteers)
- Measurement: Intraoral scans (Trios, 3Shape) + 3D digital design software
- Variables: functional/nonfunctional cuspal inclination, height, slope; CIS and CPS identified
- Statistics: Paired t-test for group comparison; multivariate logistic regression for OR

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Maxillary cracked molars: functional cuspal inclination CIS 31.87±7.72° vs controls 26.01±7.01° (P<.05); 4°-6° steeper
- Maxillary CPS inclination: 29.03±7.50° vs 25.61±7.47° (P<.05)
- Mandibular CIS functional cusp: 30.79±7.89° vs 24.53±6.97° (P<.05)
- Functional cuspal heights: 0.2-0.4 mm higher in cracked teeth (both arches, P<.05)
- Cracked molars: flatter functional cusp slopes
- Multivariate OR: maxillary CIS inclination 1.098, CPS height 3.407; mandibular CIS height 8.177

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Cross-sectional design: cannot establish temporality (steep cusp → crack, or crack → morphology change)
- Single-center study; generalizability limited
- Only molars included; premolars not analyzed
- Morphology measured post-crack identification — crack may have altered occlusal morphology

## 7. Glossary
- CIS: Crack Initiation Side — side where the crack originates
- CPS: Crack Propagation Side — side toward which crack extends
- Functional cusp: cusp bearing occlusal load (maxillary palatal, mandibular buccal)
- Cuspal inclination: angle of cusp incline relative to occlusal plane
