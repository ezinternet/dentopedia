## Why Ingested

This is the earlier of two near-identical Invisalign maxillary-molar-distalization studies ingested together — it establishes the palatal-rugae-registration measurement method and the specific finding that composite attachments did **not** enhance the molars' own achieved distal movement, a caveat that narrows the broader attachment-benefit claim in [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/nucera-2022-composite-attachments-clear-aligners-sr]]. It also sets up the direct clinical-outcome comparator for the concurrently-ingested [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/liu-2025-maxillary-molar-distalization-invisalign-adult]], which replicates the same iTero/rugae-superimposition design in a separate adult cohort.

## Three-line Summary

Single-arm pre/post clinical study (prospective vs retrospective enrolment is not stated in the retrieved abstract), 38 adult patients (mean age 25.4 y) undergoing Invisalign treatment requiring maxillary molar distalization, comparing predicted vs achieved tooth movement via iTero digital models superimposed on the palatal rugae area.

142 maxillary molars (71 first, 71 second) measured for distal movement; predicted movement differed significantly from achieved for both first and second molars (P<0.0001); distal molar movement correlated significantly with anterior anchorage loss at the central incisor (r=0.3900, P<0.008) and lateral incisor (r=0.3595, P<0.013).

Invisalign can achieve a mean prescribed distalization of 2.6 mm in adults, but clinicians should anticipate anterior anchorage loss as a countereffect (especially with large initial overjet) and plan overcorrection/auxiliaries early; the exact achieved-vs-predicted efficiency percentage is not recoverable from the abstract alone.

## 세줄요약

단일군 전후비교 임상연구(전향/후향 여부는 초록에 미기재), 상악 대구치 원심이동(distalization)이 필요한 인비절라인(Invisalign) 치료 성인 38명(평균 25.4세) — 구개주름(palatal rugae) 부위 정합(registration)으로 중첩한 iTero 디지털 모델을 이용해 예측 대 실제 치아이동 비교.

상악 대구치 142개(제1대구치 71, 제2대구치 71) 원심이동 측정; 제1·제2대구치 모두 예측값과 실제값이 유의하게 차이(P<0.0001); 대구치 원심이동량과 전치부 고정원 손실(anchorage loss)이 중절치(r=0.3900, P<0.008)·측절치(r=0.3595, P<0.013)에서 유의한 상관관계.

성인에서 평균 2.6mm 처방 시 인비절라인 상악 대구치 원심이동이 성공적으로 가능하나, 특히 초기 overjet이 큰 경우 전치부 고정원 손실이라는 역효과를 예상하고 조기에 과교정(overcorrection)·보조장치 계획이 필요; 정확한 달성/예측 효율 % 수치는 초록만으로는 확인 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Uses palatal rugae area (rather than anterior cranial base or other landmarks) as the digital-model registration/superimposition reference for measuring Invisalign molar distalization outcomes.
- Quantifies that predicted and achieved maxillary first- and second-molar distal movement differ significantly (both P<0.0001), i.e., aligners under-express the prescribed distalization.
- Establishes a statistically significant correlation between the amount of molar distal movement and anterior anchorage loss (central and lateral incisors), supporting the mechanistic link between molar distalization and reciprocal anterior proclination/anchorage loss.
- Author highlight: attachments showed no enhancement effect on the maxillary molars' own distalization movement.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Single-arm pre/post digital-model comparison (not an SR/RCT). **Prospective vs retrospective enrolment is not stated in the retrieved abstract** — `evidence_level: retrospective` is assigned as the conservative rung, not as a verified design.
- **Population**: 38 patients, mean age 25.4 years, eligible for Invisalign and requiring maxillary molar distalization.
- **Data source**: Two digital models per patient (iTero intraoral scanner, Align Technology, Santa Clara, Calif) — before treatment and after maxillary first + second molar distalization — superimposed using the palatal rugae area for registration.
- **n**: 142 maxillary molars (71 first molars, 71 second molars) measured for distal movement; 228 maxillary anterior teeth evaluated for anterior anchorage loss.
- **Outcomes**: Predicted vs achieved distal molar movement (first and second molar); correlation between molar distal movement and anterior anchorage loss (central and lateral incisors).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Predicted distal movement of the maxillary first molar differed significantly from achieved values (P<0.0001).
- Predicted distal movement of the maxillary second molar differed significantly from achieved values (P<0.0001).
- Correlation between distal molar movement and anchorage loss: central incisor r=0.3900 (P<0.008); lateral incisor r=0.3595 (P<0.013).
- Mean prescribed distalization movement in the sample: 2.6 mm.
- The exact achieved-as-percentage-of-predicted efficiency figure is **not stated in the retrieved abstract** — only that the difference was statistically significant. Do not infer a percentage.
- Author highlights (verbatim from PDF): "Invisalign can be used successfully for adult patients requiring maxillary molar distalization"; "Maxillary molar distalization using Invisalign can cause anterior anchorage loss"; "The use of attachments has no enhancement effect on the maxillary molar distalization movement."

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only source: no access to full Methods/Results/Discussion, so statistical methods, attachment-usage protocol details, and exact efficiency percentages cannot be verified beyond what is stated in the structured abstract and highlights.
- Single-arm design with no control group (e.g., no comparison against fixed appliances or against non-attachment aligner cases within this same study).
- No third-molar or eruption-stage data mentioned in the abstract; generalizability to non-Chinese/other-ethnicity populations not addressed in the available text.

## 7. Glossary
- **Palatal rugae registration**: use of the palatal rugae (ridges on the anterior hard palate, stable after early childhood) as a fixed anatomical landmark for superimposing digital models taken at different treatment timepoints.
- **iTero**: an intraoral optical scanner (Align Technology) used to acquire digital dental impressions/models, commonly used in Invisalign workflows.
- **Anterior anchorage loss**: unwanted mesial/labial movement (proclination) of anterior teeth that occurs as a reciprocal biomechanical side effect when posterior teeth (e.g., molars) are moved distally.
- **Distalization**: orthodontic movement of a tooth in the posterior (distal) direction, here applied to maxillary first and second molars.
