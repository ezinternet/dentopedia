## Why Ingested

First SR+MA to estimate worldwide peri-implant disease prevalence using the **standardized 2017 World Workshop criteria** — directly supersedes [[implants/peri-implantitis/derks-2015-peri-implant-health-disease-epidemiology]] (11 studies, mixed definitions, mucositis 43%/peri-implantitis 22%) with 20 studies and a mixed-effects model that eliminates the definition-heterogeneity limitation derks-2015 itself identified as its primary weakness.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (20 studies from 1979 screened; MEDLINE, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science; mixed-effects model; JBI critical appraisal) estimating worldwide prevalence of peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis using exclusively the 2017 World Workshop classification criteria.

Peri-implant mucositis: 63.0% (CI 57.6–68.2%) patient-level, 59.2% (CI 55.8–62.4%) implant-level; peri-implantitis: 25.0% (CI 21.1–29.3%) patient-level, 18.0% (CI 15.8–20.5%) implant-level — significantly higher mucositis than pre-2017 estimates, reflecting stricter diagnostic inclusion; subgroup analysis showed significant continental differences; non-smoker implant-level: mucositis 38.2%, peri-implantitis 5.2%.

Approximately two in three adults with implants have peri-implant mucositis and one in four have peri-implantitis — a substantial public health burden that requires uniform diagnostic protocols and targeted prevention strategies.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (1979건 스크리닝, 20편 포함; MEDLINE·Scopus·Embase·Web of Science; 혼합효과 모형) — **2017 세계 워크숍 (World Workshop, WW) 진단 기준**만 적용한 임플란트 주위 질환 유병률 추정.

임플란트 주위 점막염 (Peri-implant Mucositis): 환자 수준 63.0% (CI 57.6–68.2%), 임플란트 수준 59.2% (CI 55.8–62.4%); 임플란트 주위염 (Peri-implantitis): 환자 수준 25.0% (CI 21.1–29.3%), 임플란트 수준 18.0% (CI 15.8–20.5%) — 혼합 진단 기준 연구(derks-2015: 43%/22%)보다 유의하게 높음; 대륙 간 유의한 차이; 비흡연자 임플란트 수준: 점막염 38.2%·임플란트 주위염 5.2%.

임플란트 보유 성인의 약 2/3이 점막염, 1/4이 임플란트 주위염 — 표준화 진단 기준 적용 이전보다 유병률이 높게 추정되며, 전 세계적 구강보건 부담 강조.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA restricting inclusion to studies using 2017 World Workshop standardized criteria — eliminates the definition-heterogeneity limitation identified by derks-2015
- 20 studies vs derks-2015's 11 — larger evidence base with mixed-effects model
- Establishes significantly higher mucositis prevalence (63% vs 43%) using standardized criteria
- Continental subgroup analysis: significant geographic differences in peri-implant disease burden
- Non-smoker baseline: peri-implantitis 5.2% implant-level — key clinical reference for risk counseling
- Risk of bias: high in 50%, moderate in 45%, low in 5% — highlights ongoing data quality issues

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (mixed-effects model)
- **Criteria**: 2017 World Workshop Classification of Periodontal and Peri-Implant Diseases (cross-sectional studies only)
- **Databases**: MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science
- **Screening**: 1979 records → 20 studies included
- **Risk of bias**: JBI Critical Appraisal tool for Prevalence Studies
- **Outcomes**: Patient-level and implant-level prevalence for peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis

## Key Results

| Disease | Level | Prevalence | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peri-implant mucositis | Patient | 63.0% | 57.6–68.2% |
| Peri-implant mucositis | Implant | 59.2% | 55.8–62.4% |
| Peri-implantitis | Patient | 25.0% | 21.1–29.3% |
| Peri-implantitis | Implant | 18.0% | 15.8–20.5% |
| Mucositis (non-smoker) | Implant | 38.2% | 33.4–43.2% |
| Peri-implantitis (non-smoker) | Implant | 5.2% | 3.6–7.5% |

Subgroup analyses: significant continental differences for both diseases (p<.05). Risk of bias: 50% high, 45% moderate, 5% low.
