## Why Ingested

기존 [[oral-microbiology/ponde-2021-candida-albicans-biofilms-polymicrobial]]는 C. albicans 바이오필름 생물학과 cross-kingdom 상호작용을 서술적으로 총괄했으나, 개별 구강 연쇄상구균 종(species)이 C. albicans 개체수·형태(yeast↔hypha)에 어떻게 다르게 작용하는지 정량 데이터가 없었다. 본 in-vitro 8종 supragingival 바이오필름 연구(Lueyar 2023)는 mitis group 종별로 C. albicans에 미치는 영향을 CFU·CLSM으로 분리 측정해, ponde-2021의 "polymicrobial interaction" 명제를 종-특이적 데이터로 확장한다. 또한 [[oral-microbiology/koo-2013-exopolysaccharides-matrix-biofilm-virulence]]의 S. mutans Gtf-glucan cross-kingdom 기전을 다종 경쟁 맥락에서 재검토한다.

## Three-line Summary


In vitro eight-species supragingival biofilm model (3 experiments, triplicate) showing that adding oral streptococci progressively lowers C. albicans CFU — S.

gordonii + S. mutans gave the lowest counts — while S.

mutans plus a single mitis-group species induces C. albicans hyphae, and multiple mitis species revert it to yeast form.

## 세줄요약


In-vitro 8종 상연골상(supragingival) 바이오필름 모델(3회·삼중): 구강 연쇄상구균을 추가할수록 칸디다 알비칸스 (Candida albicans, C.

albicans) CFU가 감소하며(S.

gordonii + S. mutans 조합이 최저), 스트렙토코쿠스 뮤탄스 (S. mutans) + mitis군 단일종은 균사형(hypha)을 유도하나 mitis 다종 공존 시 다시 효모형(yeast)으로 회귀.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First to describe C. albicans interactions with *Streptococcus oralis* subsp. *tigurinus* within an eight-species biofilm model.
- Species-resolved (not just "streptococci in general") effect on C. albicans: **S. gordonii + S. mutans produced the lowest C. albicans CFU** among all mitis-group combinations.
- Demonstrated a **morphology switch driven by streptococcal community complexity**: S. mutans + one mitis species → filamentous (hyphal) C. albicans; S. mutans + several mitis species → yeast form.
- Reinforced that CFU is an unreliable candidal-load metric once filamentation occurs (biomass ≠ CFU).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Saliva-conditioned hydroxyapatite (HA) discs, anaerobic 37 °C. 16 h in mFUM + 0.3% glucose, then 48 h in mFUM + 0.15% glucose + 0.15% sucrose; dip-washed 3×/day (~64 h total).
- Nine biofilm compositions: 4-species non-streptococcal "basic" biofilm → stepwise addition of S. mutans and individual mitis-group species → full eight-species biofilm.
- Culture: CBA agar for total/MSG/S. mutans CFU; BIGGY agar for C. albicans CFU; α-hemolysis (green) distinguishes MSG, non-hemolytic C. albicans strain.
- FISH/CLSM: probes MUT590-Cy5 (S. mutans), MIT447-FAM (mitis group), Caal1249-Cy3 (C. albicans), SYTO40 total DNA; Leica SP5 ×63, Imaris 9.6.
- 3 experiments × triplicate; two-way ANOVA + Tukey, α = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Progressive C. albicans CFU reduction as streptococcal species accumulate vs basic biofilm.
- S. mutans + S. gordonii: significant C. albicans CFU reduction vs S. mutans alone (p < 0.01); eight-species biofilm: strongest (p < 0.0001). S. gordonii combination = lowest C. albicans CFU.
- S. oralis or S. oralis subsp. tigurinus + S. mutans: **no** significant change in C. albicans CFU.
- C. albicans presence: no significant change in total or MSG CFU.
- CLSM morphology: yeast (no streptococci) → hyphae (S. mutans + single MSG) → yeast again (S. mutans + multiple MSG).
- Spatial: MSG at pellicle/base, C. albicans mid/upper biofilm.
- Candidal biomass (Imaris): no significant differences (large variance).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro defined-strain model; species proportions differ from clinical saliva communities.
- CFU decouples from candidal biomass upon filamentation; biomass measure underpowered.
- Biofilm-matrix (EPS) contribution not directly measured.
- Single lab C. albicans strain; no clinical isolates; adhesion/signaling pathways left to future work.

## 7. Glossary

- **Mitis group streptococci (MSG)**: large group of commensal, primary-colonizer oral streptococci (S. oralis, S. gordonii, S. sanguinis, etc.); α-hemolytic.
- **Cross-kingdom interaction**: interaction between organisms of different biological kingdoms (here bacteria ↔ fungi) within one biofilm.
- **Filamentous / hyphal form**: elongated C. albicans growth form; its most adherent, invasive, and pathogenic morphology (vs the rounded yeast form).
- **GtfB (glucosyltransferase B)**: S. mutans exoenzyme that synthesizes α-glucans (EPS) from sucrose and binds the C. albicans surface, bridging the two species.
- **Supragingival biofilm model (Zürich)**: reproducible in vitro multispecies biofilm on saliva-coated HA discs used to study oral community dynamics.
- **BIGGY agar**: bismuth-glycine-glucose-yeast agar; selective/differential medium for Candida enumeration.
