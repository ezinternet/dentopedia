## Why Ingested

Fills the gap in caries microbiome ecology. We hold S. mutans-specific papers (bowen-2011, koo-2013, momeni-2024) and general oral microbiome reviews (baker-2024, kozak-2023), but no paper covering caries dysbiosis ecology holistically — from pioneer species succession through metagenomics and metatranscriptomics to treatment. This 2024 narrative review by Tanner lab (Forsyth-lineage) synthesizes over 100 years of caries microbiology with modern multi-omics, providing the canonical reference for caries microbiome ecology in the wiki.

## Three-line Summary

Dental caries is a polymicrobial, ecologically driven disease in which dietary carbohydrates shift the tooth biofilm from a symbiotic pioneer community toward an acidogenic, acid-tolerant dysbiosis dominated by Streptococcus mutans, Lactobacillus species, and Prevotella species at different lesion stages.
The extended ecological plaque hypothesis describes progressive biofilm succession — from dynamic stability through acidogenic and aciduric stages — with S. mutans acting as a potential keystone pathogen despite being a minority species in metatranscriptomic analysis, alongside lactobacilli comprising 42.3% of deepest dentin caries sequences.
Modern treatment has shifted from broad-spectrum antimicrobials and fluoride toward precision approaches including specifically targeted antimicrobial peptides (STAMPs, e.g., C16G2 in phase II trials), pH-responsive nanoparticles, and ecological rebalancing strategies targeting the cariogenic microbiome.

## Key Contributions

1. **Ecological succession model**: Articulates the extended ecological plaque hypothesis — pioneer species (Streptococcus gordonii, S. sanguinis) → early colonizers → secondary colonizers → mature biofilm — with explicit species assignments at each stage.
2. **Polymicrobial causation**: Systematically documents that S. mutans explains only a fraction of carious lesions; Lactobacillus, Prevotella, Actinomycetes, Bifidobacterium, and Candida are co-contributors with site- and stage-specific roles.
3. **Multi-omics synthesis**: Integrates metagenomics (Belda-Ferre, Xie data), metatranscriptomics (Simon-Soro, Peterson data), and meta-proteomics to show functional community differences beyond species composition.
4. **Treatment landscape**: Reviews fluoride (community water ≥0.7 ppm F), SDF (38% solution, arrests 66–75% primary tooth caries), STAMPs (C16G2, phase II), nanoparticles (NSF, AgNPs, CAT-NP), and ecological rebalancing strategies.

## Methodology

- Narrative review based on literature searches in caries microbiology, epidemiology, etiology, and treatment
- Covers culture-based studies (1960s–70s anaerobic culture, selective acid agar), 16S rRNA gene sequencing (clone libraries, pyrosequencing, NGS), metagenomics (whole-genome shotgun, WGS), metatranscriptomics (RNA-seq, metaModules analysis), and multi-omics integration
- Epidemiological data from NHANES 2015–2016, Global Burden of Disease, and WHO sources

## Key Results

### Epidemiology
- Global prevalence of caries in deciduous teeth: **43%** (2019); 514 million children with untreated caries
- Permanent teeth: **29.4%** age-standardized prevalence; 2.3 billion adults with untreated caries
- US expenditure on dental visits: **>$110 billion** (2016); >60% directed at caries treatment
- Caries cost globally: **>172 billion USD** and 2.7 million disability-adjusted life years

### Microbial Succession (Ecological Plaque Hypothesis)
| Stage | Key Species | Environment |
|---|---|---|
| Pioneer / Dynamic Stability | S. gordonii, S. sanguinis, S. oralis, Actinomyces | Neutral pH, aerobic |
| Early Colonizer / Acidogenic | Non-mutans streptococci, Actinomyces naeslundii | Mild acid, reducing |
| Secondary / Aciduric | S. mutans, Lactobacillus spp., Veillonella, Prevotella | Low pH, anaerobic |
| Mature / Advanced Dentin | Lactobacillus (dominant), Prevotella, Bifidobacterium, Propionibacterium | pH < 4.5 |

### S. mutans Key Data
- Genome homogeneity among isolates: **79.5–90.9%** (comparative genomics; no specific virulence loci for caries status)
- Colonizes dentition ~4 h after mealtime; pH can drop within 1 h
- Forms EPS matrix as extracellular nutrient repository and acid diffusion barrier
- Metatranscriptomics: detected as minority species in adult caries yet may be keystone pathogen remodeling community; "rotunds" concentrated over white-spot lesions

### Lactobacillus Key Data
- >100 species, >20 phylotypes in oral cavity; ~50% associated with active caries
- Deepest carious dentin (Rocas et al.): **42.3% Lactobacillus** sequences; in half of advanced lesions, lactobacilli comprise **63–96%** of sequences
- Dominant species in deep dentin: L. paracasei, L. rhamnosus, L. fermentum, L. plantarum, L. salivarius, L. gasseri
- Colonization reduced after successful restoration (loss of ecological niche)

### Candida
- C. albicans colonizes **50–70%** of caries-free individuals
- Co-infection with S. mutans leads to rampant caries in animal models; GtfB-mediated synergy
- C. dubliniensis and C. tropicalis also detected in S-ECC

### Metagenomics (Xie et al.)
- Healthy plaque: carbohydrate metabolism = **11.88%** of assigned reads; amino acids/derivatives = 7.89%; proteins = 9.34%
- ~2.8% of predicted protein-encoding genes involved in antibiotic resistance
- Caries-active subjects: enriched genes for mixed-acid fermentation, iron scavenging, oxidative/osmotic stress
- Healthy subjects: enriched bacteriocin biosynthesis, bacitracin stress-response, capsular polysaccharide genes

### Metatranscriptomics
- Each adult caries sample contains **70–400 metabolically active species** (Simon-Soro et al.)
- Carbohydrate (mono- and disaccharide) metabolism accounts for **~15%** of total dental biofilm transcriptome (twin-pair study)
- Disaccharide metabolism transcripts exceed monosaccharide by factor of 2
- Dentin caries shows higher Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium gene expression than coronal caries (metatranscriptomic)

### Treatment Outcomes
- Community water fluoridation: 30% preventive fraction for primary teeth (95% CI 11–48%), 12% for permanent teeth (US children/adolescents, 2018)
- Fluoride varnish (2–4×/year): 18–43% caries reduction in permanent teeth; 37% in primary teeth
- SDF (38%): arrests 66–75% primary tooth caries; 41–65% first permanent molars; 18–71% root caries in elders
- STAMP C16G2: pilot trial showed significant reduction of viable S. mutans in plaque and saliva, decreased lactic acid production, increased resting plaque pH; currently in phase II clinical trials
- CAT-NP + H₂O₂: effective in rat caries model with 1 min daily topical treatment

---

## Limitations

- Narrative review; no systematic search strategy or PRISMA; potential for selection bias
- Species names often redacted in source text (genus placeholders); actual species labels from context
- Metatranscriptomic datasets cited are from small sample sizes (dentin <coronal)
- Ecological models predict succession but individual-level variability is high — "no single microbiota of dental caries"
- Many treatment approaches (vaccines, phages, probiotics) have not advanced to approved clinical use

## Glossary

- **EPS**: Extracellular polysaccharide — biofilm matrix component produced by S. mutans via glucosyltransferases
- **S-ECC**: Severe early childhood caries
- **STAMP**: Specifically targeted antimicrobial peptide
- **SDF**: Silver diamine fluoride
- **WGS**: Whole-genome shotgun sequencing (metagenomics)
- **OTU**: Operational taxonomic unit (16S rRNA sequencing)
- **CAT-NP**: Catalytic nanoparticles with peroxidase-like activity
- **DMFT/DMFS**: Decayed, missing, filled teeth/surfaces index
- **ICDAS**: International Caries Detection and Assessment System
- **GtfB**: Glucosyltransferase B of S. mutans, mediates Candida synergy
