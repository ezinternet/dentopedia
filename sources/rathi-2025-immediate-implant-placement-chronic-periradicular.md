## Three-line Summary

Single case report (CARE guidelines; one 34-year-old systemically healthy man, two adjacent mandibular left molar/premolar sites #19 and #20 with a chronic periradicular abscess and a draining buccal sinus) treated with extraction, curettage, two immediate implants and a xenograft.

Both implants were placed with 35 Ncm primary stability after curettage and saline irrigation only, with amoxicillin-clavulanate plus metronidazole for 5 days; CBCT at 4 months showed the 15 x 20 mm lesion healed, and the authors report symptom-free function with cortical bone formation at 2 and 3 years (no implant survival rate, bone-level measurements or complication counts are given).

This is n=1 with no control, no quantified outcomes and a patient-driven time constraint; it illustrates one protocol in a chronic (not acute-purulent) lesion and cannot support efficacy or safety claims — the authors' general conclusion ("safe and predictable") is not supported by the case itself.

## 세줄요약

단일 증례보고 (CARE 지침; 전신질환 없는 34세 남성 1명, 하악 좌측 #19·#20 두 인접 부위의 만성 치근단 농양과 협측 누공)로, 발치·소파술 후 즉시 임플란트 2개와 이종골 이식을 시행.

소파술과 생리식염수 세척만 시행하고 amoxicillin-clavulanate + metronidazole를 5일 투여한 뒤 35 Ncm 초기 안정성으로 임플란트 2개를 식립; 4개월 CBCT에서 15 x 20 mm 병소가 치유되었고 2년·3년 추적에서 무증상 기능과 피질골 형성을 보고 (임플란트 생존율·골 수준 측정치·합병증 수는 제시되지 않음).

n=1이고 대조군·정량 결과가 없으며 환자 사정(시간 제약)이 결정 요인이었다 — 급성 화농성이 아닌 만성 병소에서 한 가지 프로토콜을 보여줄 뿐이며, 저자의 "안전하고 예측 가능" 결론은 이 증례만으로는 뒷받침되지 않는다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Documents immediate placement of two wide-diameter implants (5.0 and 6.5 mm x 13.5 mm) into adjacent mandibular sockets with a chronic periradicular lesion (15 x 20 mm on CBCT).
- Uses curettage + saline irrigation (no chemical decontamination reported), xenograft in the >1 mm jumping gap, chairside customized composite healing abutments, and delayed monolithic zirconia screw-retained restoration at 4 months.
- Classifies the case as "Complex" on the ITI SAC assessment.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Patient: 34-year-old male, systemically healthy; dull pain on chewing and persistent discharge in the left mandibular molar region; fractured prosthesis #18-20; draining sinus to the buccal vestibule at #20; probing depth 2-3 mm; keratinized tissue ~3 mm; Grade III mobility of #19 and #20.
- CBCT: periapical radiolucency around #19 and #20 ("chronic periradicular abscess"), 15 mm x 20 mm toward the lingual, lingual cortical plate <0.5 mm at #19, extensive buccal bone resorption at #20.
- Surgery: local anesthesia (4% articaine 1:100,000 epinephrine, IAN + buccal block); atraumatic extraction; curettes (CL85 Lucas, CM10 Miller) until firm bone was palpable; saline irrigation. Two Megagen AnyRidge implants; 35 Ncm; jumping gap >1 mm filled with bovine-derived xenograft (250 micron); customized nanohybrid-composite healing abutment; 5-0 polyglactin sutures.
- Medication: amoxicillin-clavulanate 1000 mg twice daily + metronidazole 500 mg three times daily + ibuprofen 400 mg three times daily for 5 days; single IM dexamethasone 8 mg; 0.2% chlorhexidine rinse twice daily for 14 days.
- Timeline: sutures out at 1 week; 2-week review; monthly checks; CBCT and monolithic zirconia screw-retained restoration at 4 months; follow-up at 2 and 3 years.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 4 months: CBCT showed lesion fully healed, "bone shield" on buccal and lingual aspects, significant bone formation.
- 1 week: patient symptom-free; soft tissue healing adequate by 2 weeks.
- 2 years: healthy bone growth, cortical bone formation at the platform; 3 years: further cortical bone near the implant-abutment junction, implants "completely functional".
- No numeric implant survival, marginal bone level, probing or complication data are reported.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single patient, no control, no quantified radiographic or clinical outcomes; follow-up findings are narrative.
- The lesion is described as chronic with a draining sinus; acute purulence, swelling or culture results are not reported, so the case does not address acute abscess sockets.
- Placement was driven by the patient's 5-month time constraint; the authors acknowledge this may have limited infection control.
- Authors' Discussion lists risks (poor bone quality, primary stability, infection recurrence) but the conclusion that the approach is "safe and predictable" rests on cited literature, not this case.
