## Why Ingested

This RCT directly evaluates the aesthetic benefit of immediate provisionalization in immediately placed single implants—a key clinical question in the esthetic zone. The study reinforces existing evidence on whether provisional restorations alter soft tissue stability compared to healing abutments, complementing [[wiki/immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/de-angelis-2021-immediate-implant-provisionalization-soft-tissue-retrospective]] and contributing to systematic reviews on IIP protocols.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized controlled trial (RCT), n=40 subjects with single immediately placed implants in maxillary anterior/premolar teeth, comparing temporary non-occluding crown (test) vs. healing abutment (control) over 12 months.

Mid-facial mucosal marginal level and papilla height showed minimal changes within both groups with no significant intergroup differences; marginal bone remodeling was modest and similar between groups, except for vertical crestal bone resorption.

Immediate provisionalization did not improve aesthetic outcomes over healing abutment restoration; both protocols achieved stable soft tissue levels at 12 months, suggesting the choice between them may depend on factors beyond pure esthetics.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First RCT with extended (12-month) follow-up directly comparing non-occluding temporary crowns vs. healing abutments for immediately placed single implants in the esthetic zone
- Documented minimal facial mucosal marginal level and papilla height changes regardless of provisionalization approach
- Demonstrated similar marginal bone remodeling patterns between immediate provisionalization and healing abutment protocols
- Clinical evidence that immediate provisionalization does not confer additional esthetic benefit in immediate implant placement

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized controlled trial
- **Sample Size**: 40 subjects
- **Inclusion**: Hopeless maxillary anterior or premolar teeth requiring extraction and single implant replacement
- **Allocation**: Random assignment to test (immediate provisional crown) or control (healing abutment) after implant placement
- **Prosthetic Phase**: Permanent restoration at 4 months
- **Follow-up**: 12 months after prosthetic delivery
- **Primary Outcomes**: Facial mucosal marginal level, papilla height, radiographic crestal bone changes
- **Secondary Outcomes**: Implant survival, clinical bone remodeling, esthetic parameters

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Test Group (Prov. Crown) | Control Group (Healing Abutment) | Significance |
|---------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|--------------|
| Implant Survival at 12 mo | 90% (9/10) | 100% (10/10) | Not significant |
| Mid-facial Mucosal Marginal Level Change | Minimal | Minimal | No difference |
| Papilla Height Change | Minimal | Minimal | No difference |
| Marginal Bone Remodeling | Modest | Modest | No difference |
| Vertical Crestal Bone Resorption | Higher | Lower | Significant difference favoring control |

**Conclusion**: Both immediate provisionalization and healing abutment protocols achieved stable soft tissue levels at 12 months. Radiographic differences (vertical crestal resorption) were minimal and did not translate to clinically meaningful esthetic divergence.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Relatively small sample size (n=40) limits statistical power for some comparisons
- Single-center study with limited generalizability to different populations/osseous morphologies
- Temporary crown design was non-occluding; results may not apply to occluding provisionalization
- Limited radiographic detail on horizontal alveolar resorption patterns
- Short follow-up (12 months); longer follow-up data would clarify mid-to-long-term esthetic durability
- Primarily anterior/premolar teeth; posterior implant esthetics not addressed

## 7. Glossary

- **Immediate Implant Placement (IIP)**: Placement of an endosseous implant into a freshly extracted tooth socket without waiting for bone healing
- **Mid-facial Mucosal Marginal Level**: The apico-coronal position of the soft tissue margin on the facial/buccal aspect of the implant crown
- **Papilla Height**: The corono-apical dimension of the interdental soft tissue (mesial or distal to the implant)
- **Marginal Bone Remodeling (MBR)**: Loss or gain of crestal bone height adjacent to the implant following placement and loading; measured radiographically
- **Provisionalization**: Placement of a temporary restoration (crown, bridge) immediately or shortly after implant placement, prior to final prosthetic delivery
- **Healing Abutment**: A temporary component placed on the implant body to facilitate soft tissue contours and protect the implant platform during osseointegration, without a restoration on top
