## Why Ingested

Provides the first head-to-head preclinical comparison of OCS-B Collagen® (NIBEC, Korea — the Korean DBBM product widely used as a Bio-Oss alternative) vs Bio-Oss® Collagen (Geistlich) in a standardized beagle bone defect + implant model. Directly complements [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/kim-2024-collagenated-xenograft-arp-rct]], the RCT showing OCS-B.C efficacy in human ARP, by providing the animal-level mechanistic and histomorphometric comparison against the gold standard. Addresses the gap that no direct in vivo comparative data existed between these two products.

## Three-line Summary

Beagle RCT (n=13 dogs, standardized 10×3×4 mm mandibular defects, 3 groups: OCS-B.C / Bio-Oss.C / no graft) comparing two collagenated DBBM products (Korean OCS-B vs Swiss Bio-Oss) by micro-CT, histomorphometry, and RFA across 8, 16, and 48 weeks (24 weeks post-implant).
Both OCS-B.C and Bio-Oss.C achieved significantly greater BV and BV/TV vs non-treatment at 8 and 16 weeks (p<0.0001), with no statistically significant difference between the two graft materials in any endpoint (BMD, BV, BIC, ISQ) at any timepoint.
OCS-B.C is non-inferior to Bio-Oss.C for GBR and peri-implant osseointegration in a beagle model, supporting its use as a clinically equivalent Korean alternative to Bio-Oss.

## 세줄요약

이종골 콜라겐 복합재료 2종(OCS-B Collagen® [NIBEC, 한국] vs Bio-Oss® Collagen [Geistlich, 스위스])을 비글견(n=13) 하악 표준화 결손(10×3×4 mm) 모델에서 미세 컴퓨터단층촬영 (Micro-CT), 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry), 공명주파수분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA)으로 8주·16주·임플란트식립 후 24주 비교 평가한 전임상 대조 연구.
양군 모두 대조군(무이식) 대비 8·16주 시점에서 골부피 (Bone Volume, BV) 및 BV/TV 유의하게 높았으며(p<0.0001), 두 이종골 이식재 간 골무기질밀도 (Bone Mineral Density, BMD)·BV·골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)·임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 차이 없음.
OCS-B Collagen®은 Bio-Oss Collagen® 대비 비열등성(non-inferiority) 확인; 한국산 대체 이종골 선택의 임상적 근거 제공.

## Key Contributions

- First controlled in vivo head-to-head comparison of OCS-B Collagen® vs Bio-Oss® Collagen
- Non-inferiority of Korean DBBM product confirmed across micro-CT, histology, and implant stability
- Collagen difference documented: Bio-Oss.C uses non-crosslinked porcine collagen; OCS-B.C uses gelation-crosslinked porcine collagen
- Defect wall height and angle preservation similar in both graft groups vs spontaneous healing

## Methodology

- **Design**: Controlled, randomized animal study (beagle dog)
- **Animals**: 13 male beagle dogs, 10–15 kg, 7–9 months, bilateral mandibular premolar extraction (P2, P3, P4)
- **Defects**: Box-shaped, 10 mm × 3 mm × 4 mm; created 4 weeks post-extraction
- **Groups**: OCS-B.C (NIBEC, Korea), Bio-Oss.C (Geistlich, Switzerland), NT (non-treatment control)
- **Membrane**: Bio-Gide® (Geistlich) covering all defects
- **Implants**: TS III SA (Osstem, ø3.0×8.5 mm) at 24 weeks post-grafting
- **Timepoints**: 8 wk, 16 wk post-graft (sacrifice); 24 wk post-implant (sacrifice)
- **Primary outcomes**: BV, BV/TV, BMD (micro-CT); BV, wall height/angle (histomorphometry); BIC, ISQ (RFA)
- **Ethics**: Seoul National University IACUC No. SNU-220914-6

## Key Results

| Endpoint | OCS-B.C | Bio-Oss.C | NT | OCS-B.C vs Bio-Oss.C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BV at 8 wk | ↑↑ vs NT (p<0.05) | ↑↑ vs NT (p<0.05) | baseline | NS |
| BV/TV at 8+16 wk | ↑↑ vs NT (p<0.0001) | ↑↑ vs NT (p<0.0001) | baseline | NS |
| BMD at 8 wk | ↑ vs NT (p<0.05) | NS vs NT | baseline | NS |
| BMD at 16 wk | NS vs NT | NS vs NT | baseline | NS |
| BIC at 24 wk post-implant | similar | similar | — | NS |
| ISQ | similar | similar | — | NS |

- ALP activity: Both graft groups significantly higher than NT at 3 days (p<0.05); no difference between graft groups
- Histology: Both grafts showed similar NB formation and seamless integration by 16 weeks; near-complete graft resorption by 16 weeks
- Wall height/angle: Both graft groups maintained dimensional stability significantly better than NT (p<0.001)

## Limitations

- Beagle animal model; species-specific metabolism may not fully translate to human
- Small sample size (5 dogs/timepoint for 8/16 wk, 3 dogs for implant group)
- Short- to mid-term follow-up; long-term graft resorption differences not assessed
- Collagen crosslinking difference (gelation vs non-crosslinked) not independently analyzed as a variable

## Glossary

- **DBBM-C**: Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral + Collagen composite graft
- **OCS-B Collagen®**: Korean DBBM-C product by NIBEC (Jincheon, Korea); 90% bovine bone mineral + 10% gelation-crosslinked porcine collagen
- **Bio-Oss® Collagen**: Swiss DBBM-C product by Geistlich; 90% bovine bone mineral + 10% non-crosslinked porcine collagen
- **Non-inferiority**: Statistical demonstration that OCS-B.C is not worse than Bio-Oss.C by a clinically meaningful margin
- **BV/TV**: Bone volume fraction (bone volume / total volume) — key micro-CT endpoint for bone fill quality
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient measured by Osstell resonance frequency analysis device
