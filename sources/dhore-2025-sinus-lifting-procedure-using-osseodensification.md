## Why Ingested

Independent Indian-group systematic review (SR without meta-analysis) covering the same osseodensification (OD) vs osteotome transcrestal sinus lift comparison as our existing SR+MA pages, drawing on a largely separate Egyptian/Alexandria RCT pool. It reinforces the OD-stability direction of [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/cobo-vazquez-2025-crestal-sinus-lift-osteotome-vs-osseodensification]] while explicitly declining pooling — evidence that the field's heterogeneity, not just the pooled effect, is a standing limitation.

## Three-line Summary

PRISMA systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42023455968) of 6 studies (5 RCTs + 1 prospective clinical study; 89 patients, 100 implants) comparing osseodensification (OD) vs osteotome for transcrestal sinus floor elevation in the atrophic posterior maxilla (mean age 41.1 y, follow-up 4–6 months).

Every included study reported statistically significant higher implant stability (ISQ) with OD (primary ISQ 65.1–74.2 vs 52.8–68.5; secondary 75.9–87.1 vs 65.1–80.2), while bone-loss differences were insignificant and residual bone height / bone gain improved with both techniques (bone gain 1.8–5.8 mm OD vs 1.4–6.9 mm osteotome); no meta-analysis was performed due to heterogeneity.

OD shows a potential primary-stability advantage, but the evidence base is heterogeneous and largely high risk of bias (2 moderate, 4 high RoB), so standardized head-to-head research is needed before definitive clinical guidance.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA 체계적 고찰(SR, PROSPERO CRD42023455968) — 경치조골 상악동거상술(Transcrestal Sinus Floor Elevation, TSFE)에서 골밀도화(Osseodensification, OD) vs 오스테오톰 비교, 6편(5 RCT + 1 전향적; 환자 89명, 임플란트 100개).

모든 연구에서 OD의 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)가 유의하게 높았고(1차 65.1–74.2 vs 52.8–68.5), 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 유의차 없음; 잔존골높이(RBH)·골획득량은 두 술식 모두 개선(골획득 1.8–5.8 mm vs 1.4–6.9 mm); 이질성으로 메타분석은 수행되지 않음.

OD의 1차 안정성 우위 가능성은 있으나 근거가 비뚤림 위험 높음(고위험 4편·중등도 2편)이고 이질적이라, 표준화된 직접 비교 연구가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- PROSPERO-registered (CRD42023455968) PRISMA systematic review comparing OD vs osteotome specifically via the transcrestal approach in posterior atrophic maxilla.
- Synthesizes 6 studies (5 RCTs + 1 prospective clinical study; 89 patients / 100 implants), a dataset dominated by Egyptian/Alexandria clinical trials.
- Documents a consistent statistically significant ISQ advantage for OD across all included studies (primary and secondary stability).
- Reports mixed/insignificant marginal-bone-loss differences and technique-neutral bone gain / residual-bone-height improvement.
- Explicitly declines meta-analysis due to heterogeneity and insufficient data for pooling — framing heterogeneity itself as the key obstacle to definitive guidance.
- Identifies moderate-to-high risk of bias (QATSDD + Cochrane RoB 2) in all six included studies.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review without meta-analysis (qualitative synthesis); PRISMA-compliant, PROSPERO CRD42023455968.
- **Databases**: PubMed (incl. MEDLINE), Cochrane Central, Google Scholar + grey literature (OpenGrey); abstract additionally lists Scopus and Embase. Hand search and reference-list screening performed.
- **Screening**: 1205 records identified → 1165 after dedup screened → 10 full-texts assessed → 4 excluded → 6 included.
- **n**: 6 studies; 89 patients; 100 implants (49 OD, 50 osteotome; 1 split-mouth study of 10 patients in both groups). Age range 23–65 y, mean 41.08 ± 15.36; follow-up 4–6 months.
- **Outcomes**: Primary/secondary implant stability (ISQ via RFA), marginal bone loss (CBCT/periapical/OPG), residual bone height, bone gain.
- **RoB tools**: QATSDD + Cochrane RoB 2 (two studies moderate, four high risk of bias); data extraction by two authors with third-author adjudication.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Meta-analysis**: not performed — insufficient data for statistical pooling + study heterogeneity.
- **Implant stability (ISQ, N/cm)** — statistically significant OD advantage in all studies:
  - Primary: OD 65.1 ± 4.3 (lowest, Arafat 2019) to 74.2 ± 3.9 (highest, Hashem 2023) vs osteotome 52.8 ± 6.2 (lowest, Arafat 2019) to 68.5 ± 2.2 (highest, Hashem 2023).
  - Secondary: OD 75.9 ± 2.9 (lowest) to 87.1 ± 3.3 (highest) vs osteotome 65.1 ± 3.06 (lowest) to 80.2 ± 1.6 (highest).
- **Marginal bone loss (3/6 studies)**: no statistically significant between-group difference; osteotome 0.43 ± 0.97 mm (lowest) to 0.93 ± 0.15 mm (highest); OD 0.2 ± 0.73 mm (lowest) to 0.98 ± 0.15 mm (highest).
- **Residual bone height & bone gain (3/6 studies)**: both techniques improved RBH; magnitude varied. Osteotome immediate postoperative RBH 4.68–7.14 mm → 6-month 8.27–11.61 mm; bone gain 1.4 ± 0.5 mm (Rostom 2021) to 6.93 ± 0.45 mm (Hashem 2023). OD immediate 4.66–8.3 mm → 6-month 10.47–10.8 mm; bone gain 1.8 ± 0.4 mm (Rostom 2021) to 5.81 ± 0.51 mm (Hashem 2023).
- **Included studies**: Arafat & Elbaz 2019; Ibrahim, Ayad & ElAshwah 2020; Hamdi & Hemd 2021; Rostom & Eiid 2021; El-Ghobashy, Shaaban & Melek 2022; Ahmed Halim Hashem et al. 2023.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No meta-analysis — no pooled effect sizes or heterogeneity statistics (I²) computed; conclusions rest on narrative synthesis.
- Small sample (6 studies, 89 patients); follow-up short (4–6 months).
- Risk of bias moderate-to-high in all included studies (4 high, 2 moderate RoB 2), limiting the strength of the OD-advantage signal.
- Outcome reporting inconsistent across studies (ISQ in 6, MBL in 3, RBH/bone gain in 3), so quantitative comparison is only partly possible.
- The authors call for well-designed, prospective, standardized research to establish robust guidelines; heterogeneity remains the main obstacle.

## 7. Glossary
- **Osseodensification (OD)**: Huwais 2012 technique using high-speed densifying burs (Densah, negative-rake, non-cutting) that compact bone laterally instead of excavating; in TSFE the autografted bone column hydraulically elevates the Schneiderian membrane.
- **Osteotome (Summers) technique**: 1994 mallet-driven crestal compaction technique that green-stick fractures the sinus floor and elevates the membrane.
- **Transcrestal sinus floor elevation (TSFE)**: sinus lift performed through the implant osteotomy (crestal approach), without a lateral window.
- **Implant stability quotient (ISQ)**: Resonance frequency analysis (RFA) measure of implant stability, N/cm scale.
- **Residual bone height (RBH)**: Vertical height of native sub-sinus bone available before sinus floor elevation.
- **PRISMA / PROSPERO**: PRISMA = reporting standard for systematic reviews; PROSPERO = international prospective register of systematic reviews (registration CRD42023455968).
