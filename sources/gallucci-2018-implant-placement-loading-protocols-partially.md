## Why Ingested

This foundational ITI Consensus-aligned systematic review (2003/2008/2013 conferences) establishes the evidence base for **all combinations** of placement timing (Types 1–4: immediate/early/late) and loading protocols (immediate/early/conventional) in partially edentulous patients. Directly cited as reference framework in [[wiki/immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/hamilton-2023-type1a-immediate-implant-immediate-loading-esthetic-sr-ma]] (Type 1A definitions) and feeds into broader placement-loading decision frameworks alongside [[wiki/implants/zhou-2021-placement-loading-protocols-single-implants-sr]] (Zhou et al. 2021 follow-up stratified by single-implant sites).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (69 publications: 23 comparative [15 RCTs, 7 CCTs], 47 noncomparative [34 prospective, 13 retrospective]; 5,248 titles → 449 full texts screened) of implant placement × loading protocol combinations (9 type combinations) in partially edentulous patients with solid-screw rough-surface implants (3–6 mm diameter).

Weighted cumulative survival rates by type: Type 1A 98.4%, Type 1B 98.2%, Type 1C 96.0%; Type 2–3B 100%, Type 2–3C 96.3%; Type 4A 97.9%, Type 4B 98.3%, Type 4C 97.7%. Evidence classification: Type 1C, Type 2–3C, Type 4B, Type 4C scientifically and clinically validated (SCV); Type 1A/1B/4A clinically documented (CD); Type 2–3A/2–3B clinically insufficiently documented (CID).

Loading protocol selection significantly influences outcome of immediate implant placement; evidence heterogeneity high, precluding meta-analysis. Primary clinical message: placement and loading decisions must be made in **combination**, not analyzed separately.

## 세줄요약

부분무치악 환자에서 임플란트 식립 시점(Type 1–4: 즉시/조기/지연) × 부하 프로토콜(즉시/조기/종래식) 조합의 임상 결과를 체계적으로 검토한 논문. 69개 발행물(23 비교연구 [RCT 15, CCT 7], 47 비교 외 연구; 5,248 제목 검색 → 449 전문 평가).

생존율: Type 1A 98.4%, 1B 98.2%, 1C 96.0%; Type 2–3B 100%, 2–3C 96.3%; Type 4A 97.9%, 4B 98.3%, 4C 97.7% (가중 누적). 근거 분류: Type 1C, 2–3C, 4B, 4C = 과학적·임상적으로 검증됨(SCV); Type 1A/1B/4A = 임상적으로 문서화됨(CD); Type 2–3A/2–3B = 임상적 근거 불충분(CID).

**핵심 임상적 함의**: 식립 시점과 부하 프로토콜은 따로가 아니라 **조합으로** 선택하고 분석해야 함. 즉시 식립의 결과는 부하 프로토콜 선택에 크게 영향받음.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Implants Research (Clin Oral Implants Res), 2018;29(Suppl. 16):106–134
- **DOI**: 10.1111/clr.13276
- **Institution**: Harvard School of Dental Medicine (Gallucci, Hamilton, Zhou); University of Bern (Buser); University of Melbourne (Chen)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Systematic quantification of **weighted cumulative survival rates** for all 9 placement × loading type combinations in partially edentulous patients (5,248-title search, 69 publications)
- Evidence classification framework distinguishing scientifically/clinically validated (SCV) vs clinically documented (CD) vs clinically insufficiently documented (CID) protocols
- Demonstration that **loading protocol selection significantly modifies the outcome** of immediate implant placement (primary stability, osseointegration dynamics)
- ITI Consensus definitions of placement types (Type 1 immediate, Types 2–3 early, Type 4 late) and loading protocols (immediate <1 week, early 1 week–2 months, conventional >2 months)
- Mechanistic insight: dimensional alveolar ridge changes post-extraction not mitigated by immediate placement; labial/buccal bone thickness and GBR influence long-term esthetic outcomes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review with cumulative analysis (no meta-analysis due to heterogeneity)
- **Databases**: Medline, Embase, Cochrane Central
- **Inclusion criteria**: Human studies, ≥10 cases, minimum 12-month follow-up, solid-screw-type implants with rough surfaces, intra-osseous diameter 3–6 mm
- **Primary outcome**: Cumulative survival rate (implant- and patient-level, weighted by follow-up duration and implant number)
- **Screening**: 5,248 titles → 2,362 abstracts → 449 full-text articles → 69 included publications (23 comparative, 47 noncomparative)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Weighted Cumulative Survival Rates by Placement × Loading Type
- **Type 1A** (immediate placement + immediate restoration/loading): **98.4%** (CD)
- **Type 1B** (immediate placement + early loading): **98.2%** (CD)
- **Type 1C** (immediate placement + conventional loading): **96.0%** (SCV)
- **Type 2–3A** (early placement [4–8 wks soft tissue or 12–16 wks partial bone] + immediate loading): Data insufficient (CID)
- **Type 2–3B** (early placement + early loading): **100%** (CID)
- **Type 2–3C** (early placement + conventional loading): **96.3%** (SCV)
- **Type 4A** (late placement [≥6 months] + immediate loading): **97.9%** (CD)
- **Type 4B** (late placement + early loading): **98.3%** (SCV)
- **Type 4C** (late placement + conventional loading): **97.7%** (SCV)

### Study Composition
- **Comparative studies** (23): 15 RCTs, 7 controlled clinical trials
- **Noncomparative studies** (47): 34 prospective cohort, 13 retrospective cohort
- **Heterogeneity**: High — precluded meta-analysis of controlled studies

### Clinical Message
- Type 1A and 1B (immediate placement with immediate/early loading) achieve high survival (98.4%, 98.2%) but classified as **CD** (clinically documented) rather than SCV because primary-stability thresholds and loading mechanics are less standardized than conventional protocols
- Type 1C, Type 2–3C, Type 4B/C (mostly conventional-loading protocols and late placement protocols) are **SCV** (scientifically and clinically validated)
- **Type 2–3B (early placement + early loading) shows 100% survival but CID** (clinically insufficiently documented) — sparse evidence base

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Study heterogeneity**: Study designs, implant types, follow-up duration, and outcome definitions highly variable; no meta-analysis possible
- **Selection bias**: Publications may not represent all placement/loading attempts (failed studies less likely to be published)
- **Implant diameter restriction**: 3–6 mm intra-osseous diameter; excludes wide-diameter implants
- **Surface type restriction**: Rough-surface implants only; machined-surface data excluded
- **Partially edentulous population only**: Does not address fully edentulous or single-tooth replacement contexts (addressed separately in Zhou 2021)
- **Long-term data gaps**: Type 2–3A and Type 2–3B lack adequate 5–10 year follow-up
- **Mechanical complications**: Review focuses on implant survival; abutment screw loosening, prosthetic failure rates not detailed

## 7. Glossary
- **Type 1 placement**: Immediate implant placement on day of extraction
- **Types 2–3 placement**: Early implant placement at 4–8 weeks (Type 2, soft tissue healing) or 12–16 weeks (Type 3, partial bone healing)
- **Type 4 placement**: Late implant placement after ≥6 months complete bone healing
- **Immediate loading**: Delivery of restoration/provisional prosthesis within <1 week of implant placement
- **Early loading**: Restoration delivered 1 week to 2 months post-implant placement
- **Conventional loading**: Restoration delivered >2 months post-implant placement
- **Primary stability**: Mechanical implant stability at time of placement (torque, ISQ)
- **Osseointegration**: Bone-to-implant contact (BIC) and bone adaptation following loading protocol
- **SCV (Scientifically and Clinically Validated)**: Protocol supported by controlled studies with consistent outcomes
- **CD (Clinically Documented)**: Protocol documented in clinical studies but lacking rigorous comparative evidence
- **CID (Clinically Insufficiently Documented)**: Protocol with sparse evidence; more studies needed
