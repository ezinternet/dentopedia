## Why Ingested

Surveillance hit (bone-graft-material): the only SR/MA in the wiki that tests a xenograft (bovine bone mineral) against spontaneous healing in a third-molar socket, i.e. periodontal protection of the adjacent second molar rather than implant-site augmentation. It adds a third-molar-specific xenograft question to [[oral-surgery/third-molar/degala-2018-evaluation-efficacy-simvastatin-bone-regeneration-third-molars]] (a different socket-regeneration adjunct) and [[oral-surgery/third-molar/pattnayak-2024-prf-prp-third-molar-extraction-sr]] (autologous platelet concentrates), and complements the xenograft-vs-allograft comparison in [[bone-regeneration/abushama-2025-xenograft-allograft-bone-graft-implant]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 3 split-mouth RCTs (81 patients, 162 mandibular third molars; non-smoking healthy patients, >=6 months follow-up) comparing bovine xenograft plus collagen membrane with spontaneous healing at the distal aspect of the second molar.
Pooled (2 trials, 73 third molars): probing depth (PPD) gain MD 2.36 mm (95% CI 0.69-4.03; P=0.005; I2=97%) and clinical attachment level (CAL) gain MD 2.52 mm (0.96-4.09; P=0.002; I2=95%) favoured xenograft; alveolar bone level (ABL) and adverse events did not differ.
Only 3 trials (1 low risk of bias) with very high heterogeneity, so the benefit is suggestive rather than robust; larger, longer, better-designed RCTs are needed.

## 세줄요약

비흡연 건강 환자의 하악 제3대구치 (Mandibular third molar, M3M) 발치 후 제2대구치 원심 치주결손 예방에 우골 이종골 (Xenograft) + 콜라겐 막을 자연치유와 비교한 split-mouth RCT 3편(81명, M3M 162개)의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (SR+MA).
2편 풀링 시 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PPD) 감소 MD 2.36 mm (95% CI 0.69-4.03, P=0.005, I2=97%), 임상 부착 수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 획득 MD 2.52 mm (95% CI 0.96-4.09, P=0.002, I2=95%)로 이종골군이 유리했으나 치조골 높이 (Alveolar Bone Level, ABL)와 부작용은 차이가 없었다.
포함 연구가 3편뿐이고 낮은 비뚤림 위험은 1편, 이질성이 매우 커서 결론은 조심스러워야 하며, 더 큰 표본·장기 추적 RCT가 필요하다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Medicina Oral, Patologia Oral y Cirugia Bucal (Med Oral Patol Oral Cir Bucal), 2021
- **DOI**: 10.4317/medoral.24260
- **Institution**: Not stated in the extracted text (Spanish authors; Gay-Escoda and Sánchez-Garcés are the senior authors)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR/MA to compare bovine xenograft alone against spontaneous healing (earlier reviews mixed materials) for preventing periodontal defects behind the mandibular second molar after third molar surgery.
- Pooled estimates favour xenograft plus collagen membrane for PPD and CAL gain, with no excess postoperative complications.
- Reports that the membrane arm did better than xenograft alone in the single trial that compared them (Sammartino et al.).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA following PRISMA; Cochrane risk-of-bias tool (v5.1.0); RevMan 5.3 random-effects model, M3M as unit of analysis in split-mouth trials; heterogeneity significant at I2 >50%.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Cochrane Library, Scopus (April 2020), plus manual search of 9 journals over the last 10 years; no language/year restriction; kappa = 1.
- **n**: 795 records, 7 full texts, 3 RCTs included (98 patients enrolled, 17 dropouts, 81 analysed, 162 M3Ms).
- **Outcomes**: PPD, CAL, ABL on distal aspect of M2M, adverse events; follow-up >=6 months (12 months in the pooled trials).
- **Eligibility**: non-smoking healthy patients; bovine xenograft +/- membrane vs spontaneous healing.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| PPD gain (2 trials, 73 M3Ms) | MD 2.36 (95% CI 0.69 to 4.03), P=0.005, I2=97% |
| CAL gain (2 trials, 73 M3Ms) | MD 2.52 (95% CI 0.96 to 4.09), P=0.002, I2=95% |
| ABL change | no significant difference |
| Adverse events | no significant difference; not pooled (insufficient data); 3 infections in grafted vs 1 in control in one trial |
| Risk of bias | one low (Sammartino), one unclear (Hassan), one high (Andrade-Munhoz) |

- Up to 50% of cases in two trials had pre-operative distal PPD >=7 mm.
- One trial (Sammartino) found xenograft plus collagen membrane better than xenograft alone for PPD and CAL (P<0.05), and histology at 6 months showed fewer residual xenogeneic particles and more mature osteoid with the membrane.
- Included patients were up to 35 years old with impacted M3Ms.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 3 RCTs, small sample, one at low risk of bias; very high heterogeneity (I2 95-97%).
- No trial allowed a meta-analysis of xenograft without membrane.
- One trial used a xenograft marketed only in Brazil, unlike the other two (well-documented material).
- All trials performed scaling and root planing in test or control sites, which may itself aid periodontal healing.
- Authors call for larger, longer, well-designed RCTs.

## 6. Related Work
- Chen et al.: leaving a gingival portion around the M2M during the M3M incision may help (cited in the text).
- Ge et al.: autologous bone showed no significant PPD/CAL benefit at 6 and 12 months (cited in the text).
- Platelet-rich plasma: poor bone regeneration but better soft-tissue healing (cited in the text).
- Allograft and alloplastic materials: no significant ABL improvement in cited trials.

## 7. Glossary
- **M3M / M2M**: mandibular third / second molar.
- **PPD**: periodontal probing depth; here, reduction (gain) at the distal site of M2M.
- **CAL**: clinical attachment level; gain indicates periodontal reattachment.
- **ABL**: alveolar bone level.
- **Xenograft**: bone substitute of animal (here bovine) origin, osteoconductive with a low resorption rate.
- **Spontaneous healing**: socket left to heal without graft or membrane.
