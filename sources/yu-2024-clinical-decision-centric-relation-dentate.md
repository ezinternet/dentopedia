## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Hua Xi Kou Qiang Yi Xue Za Zhi / West China Journal of Stomatology 2024;42(5):558-565, single group, West China Hospital of Stomatology, Sichuan University) addressing whether centric relation (CR) — originally developed for edentulous complete-denture prosthodontics — remains rational when applied to dentate jaws.

The authors frame the debate as three explicit clinical questions: should CR be the core of TMD diagnosis/treatment, the jaw position for occlusal reconstruction, and the target position in orthodontic treatment? They state that CR's clinical use is "objectively" widespread despite these doubts, and that they "preliminarily put forward" a decision tree covering the clinical situations with "little controversy."

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **Frames CR's dentate-jaw rationality as three explicit clinical questions**: (a) should CR anchor TMD diagnosis/treatment, (b) should CR be the build position for occlusal reconstruction, (c) should CR be the orthodontic target position.
2. **Proposes a decision tree** ("preliminarily put forward") restricting CR use to clinical situations judged to have "little controversy" — the wiki's first algorithmic decision-rule attempt on this topic, complementing the narrative debate already synthesized in [[overviews/cr-co-micp-reference-position-debate]].
3. **Evidence status is explicitly preliminary**: the authors' own language ("preliminarily," 初步提出) signals this is a single group's expert-opinion synthesis, not a validated or consensus algorithm — contrast with [[occlusion/kattadiyil-2021-relationship-centric-occlusion-maximal-intercuspal]], a formal Best Evidence Consensus Statement.

**NOT captured in this ingest** (full text unavailable): the decision tree's actual nodes/branch conditions, the authors' specific answers to each of the three questions, and any supporting case criteria or citations used to justify the tree.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Only the abstract-level claims could be retrieved:

- CR originated in edentulous complete-denture prosthodontics and was subsequently extended to dentate-jaw clinical practice.
- The rationality of that extension is doubted along three specific axes: TMD diagnosis/treatment core, occlusal-reconstruction build position, orthodontic target position.
- Despite the doubts, "varieties of CR clinical applications are objective" (i.e., CR is in fact widely used clinically).
- The authors summarize applicable conditions for "several main clinical situations with little controversy" and propose a decision tree for CR's clinical application as a preliminary practice reference.

## 7. Glossary

- **CR** — Centric Relation: a condyle-referenced, tooth-independent mandibular position.
- **TMD** — Temporomandibular Disorder.
- **MIP** — Maximal Intercuspal Position (tooth-referenced habitual closure position).
- **Occlusal reconstruction** — full or extensive prosthodontic rebuilding of the occlusal scheme.
