## Why Ingested
[[implants/isq/faot-2019-insertion-torque-mandibular-overdenture]]에서 G4 ≥45 Ncm이 최고 생존율을 보인 반면, 이 RCT는 IT ≥50 Ncm에서 3년 추적 시 더 큰 골흡수와 점막퇴축을 보고하여 정반대 방향의 근거를 제공함 — 과토크 임계값 논쟁의 핵심 대립 축.

## Three-line Summary

RCT of 116 single implants placed in healed sites, comparing high-IT (삽입 토크, IT ≥50 Ncm) vs regular-IT (<50 Ncm) groups using implants with the same macro-geometry but different cutting-groove designs, followed for 3 years.

High-IT implants showed significantly greater marginal bone loss and facial soft-tissue recession at 3 years, with the harm most pronounced in mandibular sites; cumulative success rate was 91.3% (high IT) vs 98.2% (regular IT).

Buccal bone thickness ≥1 mm attenuated mucosal recession even in the high-IT group, suggesting cortical bone quality and thickness modify the clinical risk of excessive insertion torque.

## 세줄요약

RCT(치유된 부위 단일 임플란트 116개): 동일 거시형태·다른 절삭홈 디자인 임플란트로 고삽입토크(삽입 토크, IT ≥50 Ncm) vs 일반토크(<50 Ncm) 군을 3년 추적.

고토크군에서 변연골 소실·협측 연조직 퇴축 유의하게 큼, 하악에서 가장 두드러짐; 누적 성공률 91.3%(고토크) vs 98.2%(일반토크).

협측 골판 두께 ≥1mm이면 고토크군에서도 점막 퇴축이 완화됨 — 피질골 질·두께가 과토크의 임상적 위험을 조절함을 시사.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT with 3-year follow-up directly comparing IT ≥50 Ncm vs <50 Ncm using same macro-geometry implants differing only in cutting-groove design
- Demonstrates that high IT is associated with significantly greater MBL and facial soft tissue recession — contradicting the "high IT = better stability = better outcomes" assumption
- Identifies mandibular sites as the location where high-IT harm is most pronounced (cortical bone density mediates damage mechanism)
- Buccal bone thickness ≥1 mm shown to protect against soft-tissue recession even with high IT
- Establishes a biologically plausible osseocompression model: high IT compresses cortical bone → ischemia → resorption → mucosal recession

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Randomized clinical study (RCT), 3-year longitudinal
- **n:** 116 implants in healed sites (not fresh sockets)
- **Implants:** Two types — same macro-geometry, different cutting groove design:
  - 90° cutting groove (self-tapping) — tended toward higher IT
  - Blossom™ cutting groove (Intra-Lock, Boca Raton, FL) — tended toward lower IT
- **IT grouping (post-allocation):**
  - High-IT: ≥50 Ncm
  - Regular-IT: <50 Ncm
- **Loading:** Delayed, 3 months post-placement
- **Outcomes:** MBL (radiographic marginal bone level), FSTL (facial soft tissue level), survival rate, CSR (Cumulative Success Rate)
- **Baseline:** Buccal bone thickness (BBT) recorded at surgery

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**Survival:** Overall 96.5% at 3 years

**Cumulative Success Rate (3 years):**
- High-IT (≥50 Ncm): 91.3%
- Regular-IT (<50 Ncm): 98.2%

**MBL:** Significantly greater in High-IT group at 3 years; mandibular sites showed the largest difference

**FSTL (Facial Soft Tissue recession):**
- High-IT group: significantly greater recession at 3 years
- Mandible > maxilla (cortical bone density effect)
- BBT ≥1 mm = protective: sites with thick buccal bone showed less recession despite high IT

**Key thresholds established:**
- ≥50 Ncm = high IT (harm threshold in this study)
- BBT ≥1 mm = protective against mucosal recession

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Cutting groove design confounds IT grouping — the two IT groups differ in implant design as well as torque achieved; cannot fully isolate the effect of torque alone
- Single-center, single-operator study; generalizability uncertain
- No stratification by bone type (D1–D4) — limits mechanistic interpretation
- 3-year follow-up; longer data needed to determine if MBL difference clinically significant over time
- ITT analysis not explicitly described

## 6. Related Work
- Lemos 2020 SR+MA [[implants/isq/lemos-2020-high-insertion-torque-dental-implants-sr-ma]]: contradicts this RCT — meta-analysis found no significant MBL difference; however Marconcini 2018 used 3-year follow-up vs shorter follow-ups in SR
- Faot 2019 [[implants/isq/faot-2019-insertion-torque-mandibular-overdenture]]: G4 ≥45 Ncm best survival 95% — contradicts this RCT's high-IT harm finding; population/implant design differ
- Khayat 2013: IT ≥70 Ncm safe (no MBL harm for tapered implants in maxilla)
- Norton 2011/2017: Low IT (≤20–25 Ncm) yields excellent outcomes

## 7. Glossary
- **FSTL (Facial Soft Tissue Level, 협측 연조직 높이):** Vertical distance from a reference point to the buccal mucosal margin; recession = apical migration
- **BBT (Buccal Bone Thickness, 협측 골판 두께):** Horizontal thickness of the facial cortical plate; ≥1 mm = adequate
- **CSR (Cumulative Success Rate, 누적 성공률):** More strict than survival — excludes implants with complications
- **Osseocompression (골압축):** High-IT insertion forces exceed bone's plastic limit → cortical ischemia → necrosis → resorption
- **High IT threshold (이 연구):** ≥50 Ncm; cf. Faot 2019 uses ≥45 Ncm as the high-IT cutoff
