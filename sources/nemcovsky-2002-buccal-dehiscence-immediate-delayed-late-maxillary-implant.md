## Why Ingested

## One-line Summary

Controlled clinical trial (n=66 patients, 102 implants) comparing buccal dehiscence-defect healing across immediate (n=23), delayed (n=39), and late (n=40) maxillary implant placement with collagen membrane + bone graft; delayed placement produced the best defect reduction (height 88.8%, area 95.6%), and single-implant placement plus absence of spontaneous membrane exposure independently predicted better healing.

## 한줄요약

대조군 임상시험(n=66명, 임플란트 102개): 즉시(n=23)·지연(n=39)·후기(n=40) 상악 임플란트 식립 시 협측 이개열 결손을 콜라겐막+골이식으로 처리한 치유 결과 비교 — 지연식립이 결손 감소율(높이 88.8%, 면적 95.6%)로 가장 우수했고, 단일 임플란트 식립과 막 노출 부재가 독립적으로 더 나은 치유를 예측.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the earliest controlled comparisons isolating **placement timing** (immediate vs. delayed vs. late) as the primary variable for buccal dehiscence-defect healing under a common protocol (collagen membrane + bone graft)
- Establishes delayed placement (extraction site closed with a full-thickness rotated palatal flap, implant placed some weeks later) as the empirically best-performing timing for buccal dehiscence resolution among the three tested
- Identifies two independently significant modifiers of healing quality: **spontaneous membrane/cover-screw exposure** (worse outcome) and **number of simultaneously placed implants** (single implant > multiple implants)
- Predates and foreshadows the later mechanistic finding (Raabe 2025, Zhou 2025 — held in this wiki) that primary-closure integrity and mechanical factors, not graft material choice, dominate GBR outcomes at this indication

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: controlled clinical trial, 3 parallel groups by placement protocol, single measurement framework
- Population: 66 patients, 102 implants total
  - Group 1 (immediate): 19 patients, 23 implants — site closed primarily with a rotated split palatal flap at extraction
  - Group 2 (delayed): 25 patients, 39 implants — site closed with a rotated full-thickness palatal flap at extraction, implant placed weeks later
  - Group 3 (late): 22 patients, 40 implants — implant placed after complete site healing
- All implants placed with bioabsorbable collagen barrier membrane + bone graft at sites with buccal dehiscence-type defects; 1–3 proximal implants placed simultaneously per site
- Outcome measurement: defect height and width measured at implant placement and again at second-stage surgery; surface area calculated as half-ellipses
- Statistics: between-group differences assessed (reported significant); no meta-analytic pooling (single trial)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | n (patients / implants) | Mean defect height reduction | Mean defect area reduction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Immediate | 19 / 23 | 77.4% ± 16.92% | 90.2% ± 9.15% |
| 2 — Delayed | 25 / 39 | **88.8% ± 15.29%** (best) | **95.6% ± 8.73%** (best) |
| 3 — Late | 22 / 40 | 75.2% ± 17.99% | 87.6% ± 11.48% |

- Between-group differences: statistically significant
- Spontaneous implant cover-screw exposure: associated with significantly smaller defect height/area reduction (worse healing) regardless of group
- Single vs. multiple simultaneous implant placement: single implant placement produced significantly better healing results

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Not randomized — group assignment follows clinical scenario (immediate/delayed/late is largely determined by case presentation, not randomization), so confounding by indication is plausible
- 2002-era protocol; collagen membrane products, surgical technique standardization, and primary-closure practice have evolved substantially since (compare Buser 2023 PASS-principle framework, Raabe 2025/Zhou 2025 ex vivo graft-displacement mechanics — all held in this wiki)
- Outcome is defect height/area reduction at second-stage surgery only — no long-term (multi-year) facial bone wall or esthetic follow-up (contrast with Chappuis 2018's 10-year data, also held in this wiki)

## 7. Glossary

- **Buccal dehiscence defect**: a bone defect exposing part of the implant's buccal (facial) surface, typically from inadequate ridge width at placement
- **Immediate placement**: implant placed at the time of tooth extraction
- **Delayed placement**: implant placed some weeks after extraction, after initial soft-tissue healing but before complete bone remodeling
- **Late placement**: implant placed after complete site healing (fully healed ridge)
- **Second-stage surgery**: the procedure exposing a submerged implant's cover screw to attach a healing abutment, used here as the defect re-measurement timepoint
