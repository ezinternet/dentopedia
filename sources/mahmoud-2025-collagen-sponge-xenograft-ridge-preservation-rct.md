## Why Ingested
This double-blind 3-arm RCT directly answers the gap flagged by [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/avila-ortiz-2019-alveolar-ridge-preservation-interventions]] — the field's reference SR+MA, which found most ARP modalities beat natural healing but "no single modality is demonstrably superior." Mahmoud et al. test a novel layered BARP-style collagen+xenograft protocol against collagen-sponge-alone and spontaneous healing, adding histomorphometric granularity (lamellar/woven bone %, residual graft %, inflammation) that the 2019 SR+MA's mostly-dimensional evidence base lacked.

## Three-line Summary
Double-blind 3-arm RCT (Al-Azhar University, Cairo, n=36 extraction sockets, 12/group, 6-month follow-up) comparing BARP-style collagen sponge + xenograft (GP I) vs. collagen sponge alone (GP II) vs. spontaneous healing (GP III control), with CBCT, soft-tissue, and histomorphometric outcomes at implant-site biopsy.
Both GP I and GP II significantly limited horizontal ridge-width loss at 1 mm vs. control (17.81 ± 3.97% and 19 ± 2.77% vs. 41.79 ± 10.3%, P < 0.001) with no difference between them, but histologically GP I had the highest lamellar bone area% (54.80 ± 6.69 vs. 27.34 ± 5.74 in GP II vs. 22.21 ± 3.66 in control, P = 0.001) and no residual inflammation, while GP II showed more inflammation and GP III the least lamellar bone.
Clinically and radiographically collagen sponge alone performed as well as collagen sponge + xenograft, but the xenograft-containing BARP protocol produced qualitatively better bone (more lamellar, less inflamed) at the histological level — the paper's headline message is that histology can dissociate outcomes that dimensional/radiographic measures show as equivalent.

## 세줄요약
이중맹검 3군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT, 알아즈하르 대학교 카이로, n=36 발치와, 군당 12개, 6개월 추적): BARP식 콜라겐 스펀지+이종골 (Xenograft, GP I) vs 콜라겐 스펀지 단독 (GP II) vs 자연치유 대조군 (GP III)을 콘빔전산화단층촬영 (Cone Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT)·연조직·조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry)으로 비교.
GP I과 GP II 모두 1mm 지점 수평 치조제 폭 손실을 대조군 대비 유의하게 억제했으나 (17.81 ± 3.97%, 19 ± 2.77% vs 41.79 ± 10.3%, P < 0.001) 두 군 간 차이는 없었음; 조직학적으로는 GP I이 층판골 (Lamellar Bone) 면적비율 최고 (54.80 ± 6.69% vs GP II 27.34 ± 5.74% vs 대조군 22.21 ± 3.66%, P = 0.001)이고 잔존 염증 없음, GP II는 염증 소견 더 많고 대조군이 층판골 최저.
임상·방사선학적으로는 콜라겐 스펀지 단독이 콜라겐+이종골과 동등했으나, 이종골 포함 BARP 프로토콜이 조직학적으로는 더 우수한 골질(층판골 多, 염증 少)을 보여 — 차원적/방사선학적 지표가 동등하다고 보여주는 결과를 조직학이 구분해낼 수 있다는 것이 이 논문의 핵심 메시지.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Scientific Reports 2025;15:43286
- **DOI**: 10.1038/s41598-025-28579-1
- **Institution**: Faculty of Dental Medicine for Girls, Al-Azhar University, Cairo, Egypt (Oral Medicine, Periodontology, Diagnosis and Radiology Department); first author affiliated with 6 October Military Forces Hospital, Ministry of Defence, Egypt

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT to compare the "Biologically-oriented Alveolar Ridge Preservation" (BARP) technique — a 3-layer collagen/xenograft/collagen sandwich restricting grafting to the coronal socket — against collagen sponge alone and spontaneous healing, with histology
- Demonstrates that clinical/radiographic equivalence between two ARP protocols can mask a real histological difference (lamellar bone %, inflammation) — a methodological point for the field, not just a materials comparison
- Adds a 6-month (longer than the typical 3–4 month) histomorphometric follow-up window, which the authors argue better captures vital bone maturation
- Reports keratinized gingiva, alveolar width (1/3/5 mm), and alveolar height outcomes together with histomorphometry in a single trial — multimodal endpoint set

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Double-blind (patient-blinded to allocation; separate blinded examiner per outcome domain), parallel 3-arm RCT. Registered NCT06896097; approved by Clinical Researches Ethics Committee, Faculty of Dentistry for Girls, Al-Azhar University (REC-ME-25-03).
- **n**: 36 extraction sockets (single-rooted maxillary anterior/premolar, ages 20–60), randomized 1:1:1 to GP I (collagen sponge + xenograft, BARP-style), GP II (collagen sponge alone), GP III (spontaneous healing control). Sample size from G*Power (effect size 0.32, power 90%, α = 0.05) referencing Guarnieri et al.
- **Outcomes**: Primary — CBCT vertical ridge dimensional change. Secondary — CBCT horizontal ridge width (1/3/5 mm), soft-tissue (keratinized gingiva width/thickness) volume change, and histomorphometric bone quality (lamellar bone %, woven bone %, residual graft %) at 6-month core biopsy taken at implant placement.
- **Materials**: GP I — deep collagen sponge layer (PARASORB® Dental cones, Resorba Medical, Germany) to 4–5 mm from the crest, topped with bovine bone xenograft (International Biotechnology Company, USA) filling the coronal socket, sealed with a superficial collagen sponge layer. GP II — collagen sponge fill only. GP III — no graft, spontaneous healing. All flapless extractions; criss-cross absorbable sutures.
- **Stats**: One-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc for between-group comparisons; chi-square for proportions; ICC for intra-rater reliability; Shapiro–Wilk for normality; SPSS v25.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Horizontal ridge width loss at 1 mm (greatest resorption point): GP I 17.81 ± 3.97%, GP II 19 ± 2.77%, GP III 41.79 ± 10.3% (overall P < 0.001; GP I vs GP II not significant)
- Vertical bone height: no significant intra-group loss in GP I (P = 0.477) or GP II (P = 0.108); GP III dropped significantly from 9.21 ± 1.02 mm to 6.91 ± 0.83 mm (P < 0.001); percent height change ≈ 8.68 ± 2.69% (GP I) and 8.61 ± 2.14% (GP II)
- Keratinized gingiva width: GP III lost the most (≈19.44% change) vs near-zero change in GP I/II (P < 0.001 vs control); thickness showed no significant difference among any group (P = 0.670)
- Histomorphometry — lamellar bone area%: GP I 54.80 ± 6.69 (highest) > GP II 27.34 ± 5.74 > GP III 22.21 ± 3.66 (GP II vs GP III not significant); overall P = 0.001
- Histomorphometry — woven bone area%: GP II highest (27.71 ± 4.85), then GP III (21.38 ± 3.43), then GP I lowest (14.69 ± 3.57); P = 0.001
- Residual graft particles area%: GP I 21.32 ± 1.86 vs GP II 4.19 ± 1.40 (P = 0.001)
- No residual inflammation seen in GP I specimens; GP II and GP III showed granulation tissue with inflammatory cells

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n = 12/group) — adequately powered for the primary dimensional endpoint per the a priori calculation, but likely underpowered for some secondary/histological subgroup comparisons
- Single-center, single-country (Egypt) population restricted to maxillary anterior/premolar sites — generalizability to posterior/molar sockets or other ethnic groups untested
- 6-month single time-point histology — no serial biopsy to track the lamellar/woven bone trajectory over time
- GP II's reported non-zero residual collagen/graft area% (4.19%) conflicts with some prior reports of complete collagen resorption; authors attribute this to brand/evaluation-method differences, not fully resolved
- No cost, chair-time, or long-term (post-implant) survival/outcome comparison between the three protocols

## 6. Related Work
- avila-ortiz-2019: SR+MA that found no ARP modality demonstrably superior — this RCT is framed explicitly as a direct empirical response, adding histology the SR+MA's evidence base lacked
- kim-2024: independent 3-arm RCT (DBBM-C vs DBBM-C+FGG vs spontaneous healing) — same directional finding that xenograft-based grafting outperforms spontaneous healing on ridge dimension
- de-angelis-2022: retrospective CBCT-volumetric ARP-vs-spontaneous-healing comparison — reinforces the magnitude-of-benefit direction seen here (roughly halved resorption with grafting)
- elizalde-mota-2024: parallel histomorphometric ARP study (lamellar bone %, residual graft % at 6 months) — comparable outcome metrics/methodology, different graft protocols (Bartee/Bio-Col)

## 7. Glossary
- **BARP (Biologically-oriented Alveolar Ridge Preservation)**: technique restricting bone-graft placement to the coronal portion of the extraction socket, layered under/over collagen sponge, introduced 2021 (Pramstraller et al.)
- **Lamellar bone area%**: histomorphometric measure of the proportion of mature, organized (lamellar) bone within a biopsy section — higher values indicate more mature bone formation
- **Woven bone area%**: proportion of immature, disorganized (woven) bone in the biopsy — an earlier-stage bone-formation marker than lamellar bone
- **Residual graft particles**: unresorbed xenograft material still visible in the biopsy at 6 months — slower resorption can delay full bone remodeling
- **Elian classification (Type I/IIa)**: extraction socket classification by buccal wall/soft-tissue integrity, used here as an inclusion criterion
- **CBCT superimposition**: registering baseline and follow-up cone-beam CT scans on fixed anatomical landmarks to quantify 3D dimensional change
