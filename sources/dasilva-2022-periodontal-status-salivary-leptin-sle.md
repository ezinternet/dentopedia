## Why Ingested

[[periodontics/dolcezza-2024-rheumatoid-arthritis-periodontal-disease-sr-ma]]로 이미 RA에서는 치주치료가 전신 질병활성도(DAS28)를 유의하게 낮춘다는 근거를 위키에 확보했는데, 같은 자가면역질환 계열인 SLE에서도 치주-전신 연결이 성립하는지 확인하려고 인제스트. 그러나 이 case-control 연구(n=38 SLE vs n=29 대조군)의 SEM 분석 결과는 대비되는 방향 — 타액 렙틴(salivary leptin)이 SLE 자체와는 유의한 역상관(표준화계수 −0.289, P=0.023)을 보이지만, 치주 상태(PPD·CAL·BOP 잠재변수)와는 유의한 직접효과가 없었다(SLE 그룹 내 상관도 비유의). 즉 "치주 개입이 전신 자가면역 바이오마커에 영향을 준다"는 RA 문헌의 일반화가 SLE·타액 렙틴이라는 이 특정 조합에는 적용되지 않는 한정 사례로, RA 근거의 무비판적 확장을 경계하는 nuance를 더한다.

## Three-line Summary

Case-control study (n=38 SLE patients, all female, mean age 39.6y vs n=29 healthy controls, mean age 46.3y; São Luís, Brazil) measuring periodontal parameters (PPD, CAL, BOP) and stimulated-saliva leptin, using a structural equation model (SEM) to test SLE status and periodontal status as independent predictors of salivary leptin.

SLE patients had significantly lower salivary leptin than controls (P=0.002); SEM showed SLE status had a significant independent inverse effect on salivary leptin (standardized estimate −0.289, P=0.023), but the periodontal-status latent variable did not; salivary leptin also correlated with serum triglycerides (Rs=−0.534), creatinine (Rs=−0.331), creatinine clearance (Rs=−0.445), leukocytes (Rs=−0.331), and total cholesterol (Rs=0.434, positive) within the SLE group.

In SLE patients, salivary leptin tracks systemic/serum metabolic-renal markers rather than local periodontal clinical parameters — a small case-control study (n=67 total) that should not be over-generalized, but it cautions against assuming a uniform periodontal-systemic biomarker link across all autoimmune conditions.

## 세줄요약

케이스-대조군 연구 (전신홍반루푸스, Systemic Lupus Erythematosus, SLE 환자 38명(전원 여성, 평균 39.6세) vs 건강대조군 29명(평균 46.3세); 브라질 상루이스) — 치주 임상지표(치주낭깊이, Periodontal Probing Depth, PPD; 임상부착소실, Clinical Attachment Level, CAL; 탐침시출혈, Bleeding on Probing, BOP)와 자극 타액 렙틴(leptin)을 측정, 구조방정식모형(Structural Equation Model, SEM)으로 SLE와 치주상태를 독립변수로 검정.

SLE군은 대조군보다 타액 렙틴이 유의하게 낮았고(P=0.002), SEM에서 SLE 여부가 타액 렙틴에 유의한 독립적 역상관 효과를 보였으나(표준화계수 −0.289, P=0.023) 치주상태 잠재변수는 유의한 직접효과가 없었음; SLE군 내에서 타액 렙틴은 혈청 중성지방(Rs=−0.534)·크레아티닌(Rs=−0.331)·크레아티닌청소율(Rs=−0.445)·백혈구(Rs=−0.331)와 역상관, 총콜레스테롤(Rs=0.434)과는 정상관.

SLE 환자에서 타액 렙틴은 국소 치주 임상지표보다 전신 대사·신장 관련 혈청 마커와 더 밀접 — 소규모(n=67) 케이스-대조군이라 과도한 일반화는 금물이나, 자가면역질환 전반에 걸쳐 치주-전신 바이오마커 연결이 균일하다고 가정해서는 안 된다는 주의를 제공.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: The influence of periodontal status and serum biomarkers on salivary leptin levels in systemic lupus erythematosus patients
- **Authors**: da Silva LA, Marques CPC, de Oliveira ICV, Franco MM, Rodrigues VP, Benatti BB
- **Journal**: The Saudi Dental Journal, 2022;34(8):708–714
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.sdentj.2022.11.001
- **PMID**: 36570575 / **PMCID**: PMC9767834
- **Study type**: Case-control study

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study (per authors) to jointly model periodontal status, clinical/serum data, and salivary leptin in SLE patients via structural equation modeling.
- Demonstrates salivary leptin is significantly reduced in SLE independent of BMI, age, and periodontal condition.
- Establishes correlations between salivary leptin and renal/lipid serum markers (creatinine, creatinine clearance, triglycerides, total cholesterol, leukocytes) specific to the SLE population.
- Directly tests (and refutes, in this cohort) the hypothesis that periodontal inflammation drives salivary leptin changes in SLE, contrasting with periodontitis-only literature showing elevated salivary leptin in local periodontal disease.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: case-control, single time point, São Luís, Maranhão, Brazil; IRB approval (Federal University of Maranhão University Hospital, No. 460.888).
- Groups: 38 SLE patients (University Hospital) vs 29 healthy controls (Dentistry School patients), ages 18–60.
- Exclusions: diabetes, smoking (or ex-smoking <10y), orthodontic appliances, periodontal treatment in prior 6 months, active infectious disease, salivary gland pathology, pregnancy/breastfeeding, antibiotics in prior 6 months.
- Periodontal exam: single calibrated examiner (intra-examiner reliability >0.8), Williams probe; PPD/CAL at 6 sites/tooth, BOP at 4 sites/tooth, full dentition.
- Saliva: stimulated (chewing latex), 7–10am, ≥1h post-meal, protease-inhibitor cocktail, −80°C storage; leptin via MAGPIX/Milliplex MAP (Merck Millipore), duplicate assay.
- Serum panel (SLE group only): triglycerides, total cholesterol, glucose, creatinine, creatinine clearance, urea, GOT, GPT, leukocytes, hemoglobin, platelets.
- Statistics: Lilliefors normality test, t-test, Mann-Whitney (by medication type), Spearman correlation; SEM in Mplus v7.0 with latent periodontal-status variable (mean PPD, mean CAL, BOP) predicting salivary leptin alongside age and SLE status as explanatory variables. Fit criteria: chi-square P>0.05, TLI/CFI>0.95, RMSEA<0.05, SRMR<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Salivary leptin: SLE group significantly lower than controls (P=0.002).
- SEM direct effects on salivary leptin: SLE status significant and inverse (standardized estimate = −0.289, P=0.023); periodontal-status latent variable NOT significant.
- Age had inverse effect on SLE status and direct effect on periodontal condition; SLE had inverse effect on periodontal status (indirect pathway), but this did not translate into a significant periodontal→leptin direct effect.
- Serum correlations (SLE group, Spearman): triglycerides Rs=−0.534 (P=0.003); creatinine Rs=−0.331 (P=0.048); creatinine clearance Rs=−0.445 (P=0.025); leukocytes Rs=−0.331 (P=0.041); total cholesterol Rs=0.434 (P=0.016, positive).
- BMI did not correlate with salivary leptin (P=0.385); no association between medication type and salivary leptin.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample size (38 SLE, 29 controls), constrained further by strict control-group screening.
- SLE disease activity (e.g., SLEDAI) not directly incorporated/adjusted for in the model per the available text.
- Cross-sectional design cannot establish causality or temporal mechanism (e.g., proposed VEGF-driven vascular clearance hypothesis for salivary-vs-serum leptin discrepancy is speculative).
- Authors call for longitudinal studies to clarify the SLE-activity ↔ periodontal ↔ biomarker relationship.

## 6. Related Work

- Contrasts with periodontitis-only literature (cited in Discussion) reporting elevated salivary leptin with periodontal inflammation (and further increased with diabetes) — here SLE status, not periodontal status, dominates the salivary leptin signal.
- Extends the periodontal-systemic-autoimmune-disease literature established for rheumatoid arthritis in this wiki (see [[periodontics/dolcezza-2024-rheumatoid-arthritis-periodontal-disease-sr-ma]], [[periodontics/qiao-2020-rheumatoid-arthritis-risk-periodontitis-sr-ma]]) to a second autoimmune condition (SLE), with a more limited finding.

## 7. Glossary

- **Salivary leptin**: leptin hormone measured in stimulated whole saliva rather than serum; proposed as a local/systemic inflammatory biomarker.
- **SLE (Systemic Lupus Erythematosus)**: chronic autoimmune disease with multi-organ inflammatory manifestations.
- **PPD (Periodontal Probing Depth)**: distance from gingival margin to base of the periodontal sulcus/pocket.
- **CAL (Clinical Attachment Level)**: distance from cemento-enamel junction to base of the periodontal sulcus/pocket.
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing)**: presence of bleeding upon periodontal probing, a marker of gingival inflammation.
- **SEM (Structural Equation Model)**: multivariate statistical technique modeling latent variables and direct/indirect effect pathways simultaneously.
