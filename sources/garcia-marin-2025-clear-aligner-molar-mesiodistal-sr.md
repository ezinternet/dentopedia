## Why Ingested
PROSPERO-registered SR on mesiodistal molar predictability with clear aligners — provides movement-specific accuracy benchmarks (distalization vs mesial movement) that complement rotational and vertical accuracy data in [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/benedetti-2025-clear-aligner-rotation-accuracy-sr]].

## Three-line Summary
PROSPERO-registered SR (CRD42022357639, PRISMA; ROBINS-I risk of bias; GRADE certainty; 6 included studies from 919 initial; search to Sept 2024) evaluating mesiodistal predictability of upper and lower molars with clear aligners without auxiliary aid.

Upper molar predictability: 61.1 ± 9.1% for movements of 0.45–3.2 mm (predominantly upper molar distalization, mostly Invisalign studies); lower molar distalization: lower predictability (specific % not stated in abstract); molar mesial movement: 85.6 ± 1.1% (high predictability); moderate-to-serious risk of bias; very low GRADE evidence.

Upper molar distalization is predictable for 1.5–3.2 mm movements; anchorage reinforcement or overcorrection recommended for any mesiodistal molar movement; standardized measurement methods needed.

## 세줄요약
PROSPERO 등록 SR(CRD42022357639, PRISMA; ROBINS-I; GRADE; 919편 검색 → 6편 포함; 검색기간 2024년 9월까지) — 보조장치 없이 투명교정으로 상·하악 구치 근원심 이동의 예측도 평가.

상악 구치 예측도: 61.1 ± 9.1% (이동 범위 0.45–3.2 mm; 주로 상악 구치 원심이동, 대부분 Invisalign 연구); 하악 구치 원심이동: 더 낮은 예측도; 구치 근심이동: 85.6 ± 1.1%(높은 예측도); 중등도~심각 비뚤림 위험; GRADE 매우 낮은 수준.

상악 구치 원심이동은 1.5–3.2 mm 범위에서 예측 가능; 모든 근원심 구치 이동에서 고정원 강화 또는 과교정 권장; 측정 방법 표준화 필요.

## 1. Document Information
- **Type**: Systematic review (no meta-analysis)
- **Journal**: Angle Orthodontist
- **Year**: 2025 (published May 15, 2025)
- **Evidence level**: SR, very low GRADE
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD42022357639
- **Included studies**: 6 (from 919 initial; predominantly Invisalign)
- **Note**: Abstract-only (PMC body empty)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR specifically focused on mesiodistal molar movements (distalization and mesial) without auxiliary aids
- Documents asymmetry: mesial movement (85.6%) much more predictable than distalization (61.1%)
- Lower molar distalization identified as less predictable than upper molar distalization

## 3. Methodology
- PRISMA guidelines; PROSPERO CRD42022357639
- Databases to September 2024
- 2 independent reviewers; ROBINS-I for risk of bias; qualitative GRADE certainty assessment
- 919 initial → 6 prospective and retrospective studies included
- Studies predominantly used Invisalign system

## 4. Key Results
| Movement | Predictability | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Upper molar distalization | 61.1 ± 9.1% | 0.45–3.2 mm |
| Molar mesial movement | 85.6 ± 1.1% | Not specified |
| Lower molar distalization | Lower than upper | Not quantified |

- Evidence quality: moderate-to-serious risk of bias; very low GRADE
- Practical implication: overcorrection or anchorage reinforcement recommended regardless of direction

## 5. Limitations
- Abstract only; full numeric breakdown of included studies not extractable
- Predominantly Invisalign studies — generalizability to other brands limited
- Heterogeneity in measurement methodology limits pooling
- Very low quality of evidence per GRADE

## 6. Related Work
- Complements existing SR on vertical (charoenrat-2025) and rotational (benedetti-2025) predictability
- Adds mesiodistal molar dimension to the overall accuracy map of CA tooth movement

## 7. Glossary
- **Distalization**: distal molar movement (posterior); used to create space for crowding or correct Class II
- **Mesial movement**: anterior molar movement; rarer clinically; used for space closure
- **ROBINS-I**: Risk Of Bias In Non-randomized Studies of Interventions tool
