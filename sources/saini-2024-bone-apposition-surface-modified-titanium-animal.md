## Why Ingested

This is the animal-model, histomorphometric (BIC%/RTV/ISQ) counterpart to [[implants/surface/czumbel-2019-sandblasting-implant-failure-rate-sr-ma]] — that SR+MA showed sandblasted (vs machined) surfaces cut human-RCT implant failure rate by ~80% at 1 year, but did not measure osseointegration mechanism directly. This paper extends the question to the preclinical/mechanistic level across a broader set of surface types (SLA, laser, acid-etched, anodized, plus additive coatings), and adds an important nuance: its own "sandblasted-alone" subgroup (not full SLA) shows no statistically significant BIC or RTV advantage over machined (p=0.65 and p=0.51 respectively), whereas SLA, laser-modified, acid-etched, and anodized subgroups do (p<0.01 for BIC). Found via `qmd search "sandblasted implant"` (top hit, 90% score).

## Three-line Summary
SR+MA (PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD42020204907) of 37 preclinical animal studies (1891 implants, 513 animals — rabbits, dogs, pigs, sheep) comparing machined (turned) vs surface-modified titanium implants in healthy healed sites, 20 of which entered meta-analysis (BIC%, RTV, ISQ) for follow-up 1–12 weeks.

Pooled BIC% was significantly higher than machined for SLA (SMD 15.62, 95% CI 10.32–20.91, I²=89%, p<0.01), laser-modified (SMD 15.64, CI 7.64–23.51, I²=89%, p<0.01), acid-etched (SMD 13.51, CI 6.24–20.78, I²=74%, p<0.01) and anodized (SMD 14.23, CI 2.98–25.49, I²=94%, p<0.01) surfaces, but NOT for sandblasted-alone (SMD 9.44, CI 6.16–12.72, I²=0%, p=0.65); for RTV only laser-modified reached significance (SMD 15.41, CI 9.31–21.51, I²=82%, p<0.01), and no subgroup reached significance for ISQ.

Clinically, subtractive and additive surface modification generally promote greater early-stage bone apposition than machined surfaces, but the effect is heterogeneous by surface type and outcome measure — plain sandblasting alone did not separate from machined on BIC or RTV in this animal-model evidence, in contrast to combined SLA/laser/acid-etch/anodized treatments.

## 세줄요약
PRISMA/PROSPERO(CRD42020204907) 기반 SR+MA — 동물실험 37편(임플란트 1891개, 동물 513마리: 토끼·개·돼지·양), 이 중 20편이 메타분석(BIC%·RTV·ISQ, 추적 1–12주)에 포함, 머신드(기계가공) 표면 vs 표면처리 티타늄 임플란트 비교.

풀링된 BIC%는 SLA(모래분사+산부식, SMD 15.62, 95% CI 10.32–20.91, p<0.01)·레이저처리(SMD 15.64, p<0.01)·산부식(SMD 13.51, p<0.01)·양극산화(Anodized, SMD 14.23, p<0.01)에서 머신드 대비 유의하게 높았으나, 단독 모래분사(Sandblasted alone)만으로는 유의차 없음(SMD 9.44, p=0.65); RTV(제거토크값)는 레이저처리만 유의(p<0.01), ISQ(임플란트 안정성 지수)는 모든 하위군에서 유의차 없음.

임상적으로 감산(subtractive)·부가(additive) 표면처리는 대체로 머신드보다 초기 골형성을 촉진하나 표면 종류·결과지표별 이질성이 큼 — 단독 모래분사만으로는 이 동물모델 근거에서 BIC·RTV 이점이 뚜렷하지 않아, SLA·레이저·산부식·양극산화 복합처리와는 대비된다.

- **Institution**: (dental college/institute, India — lead authors Saini, Sood, Chahal, Jain)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA specifically restricted to healthy-animal-model preclinical evidence (2000–2020) on surface-modified vs machined titanium implant bone apposition, pooling 37 studies (20 meta-analyzed)
- Subgroup-resolved BIC%, RTV, and ISQ effect sizes by surface-modification type (SLA, laser-modified, acid-etched, anodized, sandblasted-alone), not just a pooled "surface vs machined" estimate
- Quantifies additive-coating findings descriptively (HA, Ca-P, collagen, anodized/Ti-Sr-O, etc.) where heterogeneity precluded pooling
- Applies both SYRCLE risk-of-bias tool and modified ARRIVE guidelines to appraise animal-study quality across all 37 included studies

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: PRISMA-compliant systematic review + meta-analysis; PROSPERO CRD42020204907
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, EMBASE (2001–2020), plus manual search
- **n**: 2720 records → 2251 after dedup → 409 full-text reviewed → 37 included (qualitative synthesis) → 20 included (meta-analysis); 1891 implants in 513 animals (rabbits/guinea pigs most common, then dogs, pigs, foxhounds, sheep)
- **Outcomes**: BIC% (28 studies), RTV/N-cm (14 studies), ISQ (10 studies); secondary: torque-to-interface-failure, bone-between-threads%, bone-area%, bone-density%, pull-out test
- **Statistics**: Random-effects model, SMD with 95% CI (R Metapackage), I²/Q-statistic for heterogeneity, Begg's test for publication bias (funnel plots)
- **Quality appraisal**: SYRCLE RoB tool (10 items, high/low/unclear) + modified ARRIVE checklist (scores ranged ~13–24 across studies)

### ISQ vs machined
| Surface | Studies | SMD | 95% CI | I² | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SLA (vs turned) | 3 | 1.66 | 0.92–2.39 | 0% | 0.81 (NS) |
| Sandblasted (alone) | 2 | 0.88 | −0.26–2.02 | 0% | 0.65 (NS) |

Additive-coating findings (descriptive only, not pooled): HA-coating raised BIC% in 3 of 4 studies (e.g., Faeda 2012: 73% vs 46.3% machined; Zechner 2003: 39% vs 19.3%) but not in London 2002 (22.5% vs 31.0%, no advantage); Faeda 2009 found HA-coated RTV 64 N-cm vs 33.8 N-cm machined. Ca-P, collagen, alkali-treated, CO-ion/DLC, and calcium-metaphosphate coatings were each reported by single/paired studies to increase BIC%, RTV, or ISQ vs machined, generally without statistical pooling. Follow-up window across all studies: 1 week–12 weeks (some up to 6 months).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- High heterogeneity (I²=74–94%) in most significant BIC subgroups, driven by animal species, implantation site (intraoral vs extraoral), and follow-up interval variation
- Additive-procedure studies (coatings) could not be pooled due to heterogeneity and <2 studies per exact coating type — descriptive synthesis only
- SYRCLE/ARRIVE appraisal flagged frequent under-reporting of randomization (animal- and implant-level) and blinding of assessors; only 4/37 studies reported "3Rs" criteria
- Time-window sensitivity: SLA loses significance vs machined after >12 weeks follow-up; laser-modified loses significance at 4–8 weeks but regains it at <4 and >8 weeks — subgroup significance is not stable across healing time
- Authors call for standardized future animal-trial protocols to improve inter-study comparability

## 6. Related Work
- czumbel-2019: human RCT SR+MA on sandblasted vs machined implants — failure rate/MBL outcome, not BIC/histomorphometry; this paper's mechanistic/animal-model counterpart

## 7. Glossary
- **BIC% (Bone-to-Implant Contact)**: histomorphometric percentage of implant surface in direct contact with mineralized bone, primary osseointegration outcome
- **RTV (Removal Torque Value, N-cm)**: torque required to unscrew/dislodge an osseointegrated implant, a biomechanical proxy for interfacial bone bonding strength
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: resonance-frequency-analysis (RFA) derived noninvasive stability score; higher = more stable
- **SLA (Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched)**: combined subtractive surface treatment — distinct in this paper from "sandblasted alone"
- **SYRCLE RoB tool**: Systematic Review Center for Laboratory Animal Experimentation risk-of-bias tool, animal-study analog of Cochrane RoB
- **ARRIVE guidelines**: Animal Research: Reporting of In Vivo Experiments — reporting-quality checklist for animal studies
