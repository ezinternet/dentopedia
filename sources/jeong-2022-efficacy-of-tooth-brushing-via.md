## Why Ingested

Held pediatric toothbrushing evidence concerns the device ([[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/zini-2026-electric-vs-manual-toothbrush-children-plaque-rct]], electric vs manual brush) or the technique ([[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/rajwani-2020-manual-toothbrushing-techniques-plaque-gingivitis-sr]]), not the instruction method. This pediatric RCT tests whether a sensor-based feedback system can stand in for chairside verbal TBI, extending the digital-tool thread in [[periodontics/dannewitz-2026-digital-companion-perio-rct]] (app companion, no measurable clinical benefit in adults) and the synthesis [[overviews/oral-hygiene-instruction-overview]] with a children-specific equivalence-style result.

## Three-line Summary

Parallel-group RCT (n=42 school children aged 6–12 years with gingivitis symptoms and a baseline Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein plaque score >1.5; 21 per group; Yonsei University Dental Hospital and two public health centers, Seoul, Korea) comparing toothbrushing instruction (TBI) with a smart toothbrush and smart mirror (STM) system giving real-time voice feedback from 3D motion tracking versus conventional verbal TBI, with plaque scored at baseline, immediately after TBI (day 0), 1 week and 1 month.

Whole-mouth plaque fell 40.5% with STM versus 40.57% with conventional TBI (mean index 3.31 to 1.88 vs 3.21 to 1.72 at 1 month; time effect p<0.001, no group effect p=0.44; buccal 46.69% vs 43.83%, lingual 31.05% vs 33.89%); the maxillary first molar buccal sub-analysis favored STM numerically but did not reach significance.

STM performed comparably to verbal TBI, not better, so it is a possible alternative rather than an upgrade; limitations are short follow-up, STM used only at the study visit (not at home), small sample, retrospective trial registration, and no gingivitis, caries or socioeconomic data.

## 세줄요약

병행군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 6–12세 학령기 아동 42명, 군당 21명; 치은염 증상 및 기저 터레스키 변형 퀴글리-하인 치태지수 (Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein index) >1.5; 연세대학교 치과병원·서울 보건소 2곳): 3차원 동작 인식 실시간 음성 피드백 스마트 칫솔·스마트 거울 (Smart Toothbrush and Smart Mirror, STM) 시스템 칫솔질 교육 (Toothbrushing Instruction, TBI) 대 구두 교육 TBI 비교, 기저·교육 직후 (day 0)·1주·1개월 치태 평가.

전악 치태 감소율은 STM 40.5% 대 구두 TBI 40.57%로 동등 (1개월 지수 3.31에서 1.88 vs 3.21에서 1.72; 시간 효과 p<0.001, 군 효과 p=0.44; 협면 46.69% vs 43.83%, 설면 31.05% vs 33.89%); 상악 제1대구치 협면 하위분석은 STM이 수치상 우세했으나 유의하지 않음.

STM은 구두 TBI보다 우월하지 않고 동등한 수준이라 대체 수단 가능성만 시사; 제한점은 짧은 추적기간, STM을 연구 내원 시에만 사용 (가정 미사용), 소규모 표본, 후향적 임상시험 등록, 치은염·우식·사회경제적 자료 미수집.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health 2022;22(1):626 (published 2022-12-22)
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s12903-022-02665-6
- **Institution**: Yonsei University College of Dentistry (Advanced General Dentistry), Seoul; Konkuk University (Biomedical Engineering), Chungju, Korea; Shandong University, China
- **Registration**: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04627324, retrospectively registered 13/11/2020

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pediatric (6–12 years) RCT of a sensor-based smart toothbrush/smart mirror instruction system versus conventional verbal TBI, with plaque tracked to 1 month.
- Whole-mouth plaque reduction 40.5% (STM) vs 40.57% (conventional), no group effect (p=0.44); STM is reported as a comparable, not superior, alternative.
- Region-specific data: buccal 46.69% vs 43.83%; lingual 31.05% vs 33.89%; maxillary first molar buccal surface favored STM numerically without significance.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: parallel-group RCT (CONSORT), coin-flip randomization, blinded second hygienist as examiner; one dental hygienist formulated both TBIs.
- **n**: 55 assessed, 10 failed the baseline score, 45 enrolled, 42 analyzed (21 per group); 3 dropouts unrelated to the study. Mean age 9.64 (SD 1.96) years; 21 female, 21 male; mean 21.93 teeth (SD 4.06).
- **Eligibility**: age 6–12; at least one gingivitis symptom; baseline Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein index >1.5; excluded for rampant caries, orthodontic appliances, immunocompromise.
- **Intervention**: STM system — smart mirror on a monitor plus 3D accelerometer/magnetic sensor in a modified brush holder, 16 brushing zones, real-time voice feedback ("Good", "Very Good", "Oops"), rolling method, 10 s per zone; used only at the appointment.
- **Control**: conventional verbal TBI. Both groups received identical fluoride toothpaste and flat-trimmed nylon toothbrushes and were told to brush 3 min, 3 times daily.
- **Outcomes**: primary at least 15% reduction in Q-H plaque index (ADA benchmark); secondary whole-mouth, buccal, lingual and maxillary first molar buccal indices. Disclosing solution plus photography; examiner intra-rater kappa 0.81.
- **Sample size**: 21 per group for 90% power, two-sided alpha 0.05, to detect a 3.07% difference assuming SD 15.94% and 10% dropout.
- **Statistics**: linear mixed model for normally distributed data, Mann-Whitney U otherwise; time points baseline, day 0, 1 week (6–8 days), 1 month (28–32 days).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Mean whole-mouth baseline plaque score 3.31 (STM) and 3.21 (conventional); at 1 month 1.88 and 1.72.
- Mean percentage plaque reduction: 40.5% (STM) vs 40.57% (conventional); time effect p<0.001, group effect p=0.44.
- Buccal reduction 46.69% vs 43.83% (group p=0.78); lingual 31.05% vs 33.89% (group p=0.25).
- Maxillary first molar buccal surface: STM tended to greater reduction immediately and at 1 month; not statistically significant.
- No equipment-related physical harm reported.
- For context the authors cite a prior similar-system study (39.88% reduction) and literature values for leaflet-based (58%) and videotape-based (37%) TBI; cross-study differences may reflect baseline plaque and index system.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Short-term; STM used only during test visits, not at home.
- Correlation between STM summary scores and actual plaque control not assessed.
- No data on socioeconomic status, parental education, or caries rate/DMFS.
- Retrospective trial registration; small sample; the text states a comparability hypothesis but no explicit non-inferiority margin.
- Tables were not included in the retrieved PMC text; only text-reported values are transcribed here.

## 7. Glossary

- **STM (smart toothbrush and smart mirror) system**: toothbrush holder with 3D motion sensors linked to a mirror-display program giving real-time position and duration feedback.
- **TBI (toothbrushing instruction)**: professional teaching of brushing technique; here conventional means verbal.
- **Turesky-modified Quigley-Hein index**: 0–5 plaque score per surface (0 none to 5 covering at least two-thirds of the crown).
- **Pygmalion effect**: authors' explanation for extra lingual-side effort in controls before the 1-month evaluation.
