## Why Ingested

치태 착색제(disclosing solution)의 임상적 유용성은 [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/sooampon-2026-dc-red-33-erythrosine-plaque-disclosure-rct]]와 [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/delia-2023-dental-biofilm-indices-narrative-review]]로 이미 정리돼 있으나, 에리트로신(erythrosine) 기반 용액의 생물학적 안전성(세포독성) 기전 근거는 위키에 비어 있었다. 본 in-vitro 연구는 착색 용액이 치은 상피세포에 apoptosis를 유발하고 연조직·피질골을 광범위하게 염색함을 보여, [[overviews/oral-hygiene-instruction-overview]]가 기본값으로 권고하는 "착색제 기반 개별 교육"을 안전성 축에서 한정(refines)하는 근거로 인제스트한다.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (cultured human gingival epithelial cells plus mouse head/neck tissue and porcine skin) testing a commercial 2-Tone (Blue-Violet) erythrosine-based dental disclosing solution at 200×/100×/50× dilutions.

The solution strongly stained nuclei/cytoplasm and broadly stained soft tissue and cortical bone; MTT, flow-cytometry (Sub G1 increase) and TUNEL assays showed apoptosis-mediated cytotoxicity significant at 50× dilution (p < .001), with wound-healing failure at 50×.

Erythrosine-based disclosing solution is cytotoxic to gingival epithelium and not removable by any tested drink/oral cleanser, motivating biocompatible alternatives and cautious use in patients with oral inflammation, dryness or poor hygiene.

## 세줄요약

배양 인간 치은 상피세포와 마우스 두경부 조직·돼지 피부를 이용한 in-vitro 연구로, 에리트로신 함유 2-Tone(Blue-Violet) 치태 착색제를 200×·100×·50× 희석으로 처리했다.

착색액은 핵·세포질과 연조직·피질골을 광범위하게 염색했고, MTT·유세포분석(Sub G1 증가)·TUNEL에서 50× 희석 시 apoptosis 매개 세포독성이 유의하게 나타났으며(p < .001) 창상 치유도 실패했다.

에리트로신 기반 착색제는 치은 상피에 세포독성이 있고 시험한 어떤 음료·구강 세정액으로도 제거되지 않아, 생체적합성 대안 개발과 염증·구강건조·구강위생 불량 환자에서의 신중한 사용이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First characterization of the tissue-dyeing **range** of a dental disclosing solution: strong nuclear/chromosomal and cytoplasmic staining, plus broad staining of mouse skeletal muscle, collagenous fibers, fatty marrow, hematopoietic tissue and cortical bone — with an extent similar to H&E.
- Identifies **apoptosis** as the cytotoxic mechanism of an erythrosine-based disclosing solution via three converging readouts: cell-cycle (Sub G1) increase on flow cytometry, TUNEL DNA-fragmentation signal, and loss of intercellular adhesion with spindly/irregular morphology.
- Demonstrates that the stain is **not easily removable** — none of ten tested drinks/oral cleansers (nor absolute alcohol) decolorized porcine skin better than distilled water.
- Argues for **safer alternative disclosing agents** and for easy-decolorizing methods (e.g., activated-carbon adsorption), and for restricting use in patients with oral inflammation, oral dryness or poor oral hygiene.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: in-vitro bench study (human gingival epithelial cell culture + mouse head/neck tissue section staining + porcine-skin decolorization).
- **Cells**: human gingival epithelial cells from a patient who underwent wisdom-tooth extraction and had no oral mucosal disease; cultured in DMEM:F-12 (3:1) + 10% FBS, 37°C, 5% CO2.
- **Disclosing agent**: 2-Tone (Blue-Violet) solution (Young Dental Manufacturing); stock diluted 200× in complete culture media (v/v) for the staining experiments; wound-healing, MTT and cell-cycle experiments used 200×/100×/50× dilutions.
- **Staining**: gingival epithelial cells treated 5 min, imaged under light microscopy; deparaffinized mouse head/neck sections covered 5 min then PBS washed (×3), compared with H&E.
- **Wound-healing assay**: confluent monolayers scraped with a sterile pipette tip, recovery monitored at 24 h.
- **MTT viability**: 5 × 10³ cells/well, 24 h treatment, 5 mg/ml MTT 1 h, formazan in 200 µl DMSO, absorbance 570 nm.
- **Cell-cycle analysis**: 24 h treatment, 70% ethanol fixation, propidium iodide 40 µg/ml + RNase 200 µg/ml, FACScan + CellQuest/Modfit LT.
- **TUNEL assay**: 1 × 10³ cells/well, 24 h treatment, 4% paraformaldehyde fixation, Triton-X100 permeabilization, Alexa Fluor 647 phalloidin (4 h), In Situ Cell Death Detection Kit (Roche), DAPI (1 µg/ml) counterstain.
- **Decolorization test**: porcine skin stained 5 min; covered with drinking vinegar, orange juice, Listerine, milk, distilled water, green tea, Pocari Sweat, bottled water, toothpaste or alkaline water for 30 min with agitation; density quantified in ImageJ.
- **Statistics**: means ± SD; experiments repeated three times; repeated-measures one-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc; Mann–Whitney test for TUNEL; p < .05 significant.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Staining range**: strong chromosomal/nuclear staining and cytoplasmic staining of gingival epithelial cells; mouse head/neck skeletal muscle, collagenous fibers, fatty marrow, cellular hematopoietic tissue and cortical bone all stained — extent and degree similar to H&E.
- **Wound healing (24 h)**: control monolayers fully recovered; migration/recovery delayed in a **concentration-dependent** manner with the disclosing solution; wound-healing failure at **50× dilution** (p < .001 vs control).
- **MTT viability (24 h)**: survival similar to control at **200×** and **100×** dilution; significant reduction at **50×** dilution (p < .001 vs control).
- **Cell-cycle distribution (flow cytometry)**: similar to control at **200×**; changed at **100×**; the proportion of **Sub G1** (apoptotic) cells significantly increased at **50×** dilution.
- **TUNEL and morphology (50×)**: higher TUNEL signal than control cells, colocalized with DAPI-stained nuclei; treated cells lost intercellular adhesion and became spindly/irregular (p < .001 vs control).
- **Decolorization (porcine skin)**: no tested solution bleached the disclosing-solution stain any more than the distilled-water control (p > .05); the discussion adds that the stain was not easy to remove even with absolute alcohol.
- Note: exact percentage values for viability and the Sub G1 fraction are not reported numerically in the retrieved full text (figures only); results are carried as the qualitative/graded statements and p-values above — no numbers were invented.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No numeric concentration of the active dye (erythrosine) or absolute percentage viability/Sub G1 values are given; readouts are largely figure-based and expressed qualitatively.
- Only one commercial disclosing solution (2-Tone Blue-Violet, Young Dental) was tested; other formulations/two-tone systems were not compared.
- Findings are in-vitro (cultured cells) and animal/porcine-tissue based — no human clinical safety endpoint.
- Minimal detail on cell donor (single wisdom-tooth donor) and no replication across multiple cell lines.
- Authors call for: (a) safer/more biocompatible alternative disclosants, (b) easy-decolorizing methods such as activated-carbon adsorption, and (c) restricting use in patients with oral inflammation, oral dryness or poor oral hygiene, with expert guidance to avoid staining beyond the teeth.

## 7. Glossary

- **Disclosing solution (dental disclosant)**: dye that stains dental biofilm/plaque to make it visible for oral-hygiene instruction and plaque assessment.
- **Erythrosine (Red No. 3 / FD&C Red No. 3)**: organo-iodine xanthene dye and the most widely used plaque-disclosing dye; the U.S. FDA partially banned its use in 1990.
- **2-Tone (Blue-Violet)**: the commercial two-tone disclosing solution used in this study (Young Dental Manufacturing).
- **MTT assay**: colorimetric assay measuring cell viability via metabolic (mitochondrial) activity.
- **TUNEL**: terminal deoxynucleotidyl transferase dUTP nick end labeling — detects the DNA fragmentation characteristic of apoptosis.
- **Sub G1**: cell-cycle fraction with sub-diploid DNA content, a flow-cytometric marker of apoptotic cells.
- **H&E (hematoxylin & eosin)**: routine histological stain used here as the staining-extent reference.
