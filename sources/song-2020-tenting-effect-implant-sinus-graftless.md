## Why Ingested

이 후향적 연구는 이식재 없이 시행하는 상악동 거상술 (Graftless Sinus Lift)에서 임플란트 자체가 텐트 역할(tenting effect)을 하여 슈나이더 막 아래 신생골이 형성되는 메커니즘을 정량화한 초기 임상 근거다. [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/duan-2017-graft-free-maxillary-sinus-floor-elevation-sr-ma]]의 SR+MA가 수립한 무이식 치조정접근 상악동 거상 (TSFE) 생존율 기반 위에, 임플란트 돌출 길이와 골증가량의 직접 상관관계(r, p<0.001)를 보고해 골형성 메커니즘 측면에서 근거를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (49 implants, 26 patients) assessed bone formation patterns after lateral-approach graftless sinus floor elevation using implant tenting over 6 months.

Bone formation above the implant apex occurred in 18/46 cases (39.1%); tent-type morphology dominated (80.4%); new bone height correlated significantly with implant protrusion length (p<0.001).

Graftless sinus lift relying on the implant tenting effect is feasible, but success depends on primary stability in residual bone; the 6-month retrospective design without histology limits conclusions on bone quality.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 26명 환자의 49개 임플란트에서 이식재 없이 측방접근 상악동 거상술 후 6개월간 임플란트 텐팅 효과에 의한 골형성 양상을 평가한 후향적 연구.

줄2: 임플란트 정점 상방 골형성은 18/46례(39.1%)에서 발생했으며, 텐트형 형태가 80.4%로 우세하고, 신생골 높이는 임플란트 돌출 길이와 유의한 양의 상관관계를 보였다(p<0.001).

줄3: 텐팅 효과를 이용한 무이식 상악동 거상은 실현 가능하나 잔존골에서의 1차 안정성이 전제되어야 하며, 6개월 후향적 설계와 조직학적 검증 부재가 골질 결론을 제한한다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Tenting effect of dental implant on maxillary sinus lift without grafting
- **Authors**: Dong-Suk Song, Chul-Hoon Kim, Bok-Joo Kim, Jung-Han Kim
- **Journal**: Journal of Dental Sciences, 2020;15(3)
- **Institution**: Dong-A University Medical Center, Busan, South Korea
- **DOI**: None printed in paper (JDS 2020;15(3))
- **Study Design**: Retrospective case series
- **n**: 49 implants in 26 patients
- **Follow-up**: 6 months (CBCT at pre-op and 6 months post-op)

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Quantifies bone formation morphology after graftless sinus lift using only the implant as a tent pole — tent-type (80.4%), dehiscence-type (10.9%), and flat patterns identified
2. Establishes a statistically significant positive correlation between implant protrusion length into the sinus and new bone height gain (Pearson's r, p<0.001)
3. Documents a 93.5% implant survival rate (46/49) at 6 months without any grafting material, supporting feasibility of graftless TSFE when primary stability is achieved

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Approach**: Lateral window antrostomy; sinus membrane elevated without grafting; implants placed simultaneously
- **Imaging**: CBCT at pre-operative baseline and 6 months post-operatively
- **Measurements**: Residual bone height (RBH), sinus width, bone formation height above implant apex, bone formation morphology classification (tent / dehiscence / flat)
- **Statistics**: Pearson's correlation test for implant protrusion length vs. bone gain

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Bone formation above apex (any) | 18/46 cases (39.1%) |
| Tent-type formation | 37/46 (80.4%) |
| Dehiscence-type defect | 5/46 (10.9%) |
| Same-level bone (no elevation) | 23 implants |
| Bone gain 0–2 mm above apex | 11 implants |
| Bone gain >2 mm above apex | 7 implants |
| Implant survival at 6 months | 93.5% (46/49) |
| Implant–bone formation correlation | p<0.001 (Pearson) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective design with no control group (grafted vs. graftless)
- No histologic confirmation of new bone quality or composition
- Short follow-up (6 months) — long-term stability of graftless bone formation unknown
- Small sample (26 patients, 49 implants); single-center, single-country cohort
- No randomization or blinding; subject-level confounders not fully controlled

## 7. Glossary

- **Tenting effect**: Implant apex mechanically lifts and maintains elevated Schneiderian membrane, creating a blood-filled space that undergoes new bone formation without graft material
- **Tent-type formation**: Bone deposited in a dome/tent shape above the implant apex; most common morphology in this study
- **Dehiscence-type**: Bone remodeling defect at the sinus floor; negative outcome pattern
- **CBCT** (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, 콘빔 CT): Three-dimensional radiographic imaging used to measure pre- and post-operative bone heights and morphology
- **Residual bone height (RBH)**: Vertical alveolar bone remaining between crest and sinus floor at time of implant placement
- **Schneiderian membrane**: Periosteal lining of the maxillary sinus; elevated to create space for bone formation or graft material
