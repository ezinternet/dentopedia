## Why Ingested
This in vitro biomechanical study directly quantifies the relationship between peak insertion torque, bone density, and implant micromotion — the core variables of primary stability. It extends [[implants/isq/brizuela-velasco-2015-insertion-torque-isq-micromobility]] by testing a wider torque range (20–100 N/cm) across three bone densities and measuring actual micromotion (not ISQ/RFA surrogate), providing bench-top evidence for the torque–micromotion correlation that clinical ISQ studies infer.

## Three-line Summary
In vitro biomechanical study: 120 Ti-Bone implants (4 × 13 mm) placed in fresh bovine bone blocks of three densities (hard, normal, soft), tested at five peak insertion torque levels (20, 35, 45, 70, 100 N/cm) with lateral loads of 20, 25, and 30 N (1800 total measurements).
Spearman's rank correlation showed strong inverse dependence between peak insertion torque and micromotion (ρ = −0.54, P < 0.001); micromotion decreased significantly up to 45 N/cm in hard/normal bone, but in soft bone maximum achievable torque was only 35 N/cm before implant spinning occurred.
Increasing insertion torque reduces micromotion, but soft bone consistently exhibits high micromotion (>90 μm even at 35 N/cm), suggesting immediate functional loading in soft bone carries high failure risk.

## 세줄요약
인비트로 생체역학 연구: 120개 Ti-Bone 임플란트(4×13 mm)를 경도·일반·연골 세 밀도의 신선 우골에 식립, 5단계 삽입 토크(20, 35, 45, 70, 100 N/cm)와 3단계 측방 하중(20, 25, 30 N) 하에서 미세동요 측정(총 1,800회).
스피어만 순위 상관계수 ρ = −0.54 (P < 0.001)로 피크 삽입 토크와 미세동요 간 강한 역상관 확인; 경골·일반골에서 45 N/cm까지 미세동요 유의 감소, 연골에서는 35 N/cm 초과 시 임플란트 회전으로 더 높은 토크 달성 불가.
삽입 토크 증가가 미세동요를 낮추나, 연골에서는 일관되게 높은 미세동요(90 μm 이상) 보여 즉시 기능 부하 시 골유착 실패 위험 높음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First direct experimental correlation of peak insertion torque with measured implant micromotion (not surrogate ISQ/RFA) across controlled bone densities.
- Defined the practical torque ceiling in soft bone (35 N/cm) beyond which interfacial bone damage causes implant spinning.
- Demonstrated diminishing returns: micromotion reduction plateaus above 45 N/cm in hard/normal bone.
- Provided numeric micromotion thresholds (mean values at each torque/density/load combination) for benchmarking primary stability.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro biomechanical experiment (controlled laboratory study)
- **Databases**: N/A (primary experimental study)
- **n**: 120 implants (10 per torque × density combination); 3 bone densities × 5 torque levels × 3 lateral loads × 5 repetitions = 1,800 micromotion measurements
- **Outcomes**: Micromotion (μm) measured by digital micrometer at 10 mm above crest during lateral loading; peak insertion torque recorded at 0.5 ms intervals via strain-gauge torque wrench; statistical analysis by Kruskal-Wallis, Mann-Whitney, linear regression, Spearman's rank correlation (SPSS 11.0)

- **Spearman correlation**: ρ = −0.54, P < 0.001 (micromotion vs. insertion torque)
- **Kruskal-Wallis**: Significant difference across torque levels (P < 0.05)
- **Mann-Whitney (20 vs 35 N/cm)**: Significant reduction in micromotion at each lateral force (P < 0.05)
- **Soft bone ceiling**: >35 N/cm not achievable — implants spun due to interfacial bone damage

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Bovine bone model — not human bone; cortical/cancellous structure differs from human jaw.
- In vitro bench test — no biologic healing, remodeling, or loading history simulated.
- Single implant design (Ti-Bone, 4 × 13 mm, one-piece abutment) — geometry effects not explored.
- Only lateral loading tested; axial/off-axis loading patterns not evaluated.
- Small n per cell (10 implants) — moderate SDs, especially in soft bone.
- No histomorphometry or BIC correlation — micromotion surrogate only.

## 6. Related Work
- **Brizuela-Velasco 2015** ([[implants/isq/brizuela-velasco-2015-insertion-torque-isq-micromobility]]): Clinical/ex vivo correlation of insertion torque, ISQ, and micromobility — reinforces torque–stability link using RFA surrogate.
- **Cappare 2015** ([[implants/isq/cappare-2015-initial-bic-insertion-torque-depth-integral-in-vivo]]): In vivo dog study linking insertion torque, BIC, and depth integral — extends torque–osseointegration evidence.
- **Szmukler-Moncler 1998/2000**: Established 50–100 μm micromotion threshold for fibrous encapsulation — this paper tests whether achievable torque keeps micromotion below that threshold.

## 7. Glossary
- **피크 삽입 토크 (Peak Insertion Torque, PIT)**: 임플란트 식립 시 측정되는 최대 회전 힘(N/cm), 일차 고정력의 대리 지표로 사용됨.
- **미세동요 (Micromotion)**: 임플란트-골 계면에서의 미세한 상대적 이동(μm 단위), 50–100 μm 초과 시 섬유성 피막 형성 유발.
- **일차 고정력 (Primary Stability)**: 식립 즉시 얻는 기계적 고정력, 골밀도·삽입 토크·임플란트 디자인에 의존.
- **골밀도 분류 H/N/S (Hard/Normal/Soft)**: 시추 저항력 및 조직학적 구조로 구분한 세 골질 등급(경골: 완전 치밀골, 일반골: 2–3 mm 피질골+정상 해면골, 연골: 1 mm 피질골+저밀도 해면골).
- **응변 이론 (Strain Theory, Perren 2002)**: 골절 치유나 골유착에서 미세 간격의 변형(응변)이 임계치를 넘으면 골흡수·섬유화 유발한다는 생체역학 이론.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: 공명주파수 분석(RFA)으로 얻은 임플란트 안정성 지수(1–100), 본 연구에서는 직접 측정하지 않고 논의만 함.
- **Periotest (PTV)**: 타격식 임플란트 안정성 측정기, 본 연구에서는 미세동요와의 직접 상관관계가 없음을 지적.
