## Three-line Summary
1. RCT 기반 체계적 고찰·메타분석·TSA·GRADE로 임상 시나리오별 단경 임플란트(≤6 mm) 대 장경 임플란트(≥10 mm) 생존율/합병증을 평가했다.
2. 상악동거상 후 6 mm, 자연골/전악 수복 시 ≤6 mm 그룹은 대체로 ≥10 mm과 유사한 생존율/합병증을 보였다(고/중등도 근거).
3. 하악골 수직골증대 시나리오 및 일부 세부길이(4,5 mm)에서는 근거가 불충분하거나 낮아 결론이 제한적이다.

## 세줄요약
1. 무작위대조시험(RCT)만 포함해 임상 시나리오별로 단경(≤6 mm)과 장경(≥10 mm) 임플란트를 비교한 체계적 고찰이다.
2. 상악동거상, 자연골(상·하악), 전악 재건에서는 대체로 생존율과 합병증이 유사하며 근거 수준은 중등도~높음으로 나타났다.
3. 하악골 골증대, 특정 길이(4~5 mm)에 대해서는 RCT 근거가 불충분하여 현재로서는 확고한 결론을 내리기 어렵다.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Journal of Clinical Periodontology (2024)
- Design: Systematic review + meta-analysis + trial sequential analysis (TSA) + GRADE
- Included studies: 19 RCTs (reported across multiple publications)
- Comparison: ≤6 mm vs ≥10 mm implants, stratified by clinical scenario and follow-up

## 2. Key Contributions
- Scenario-stratified comparison (sinus lift maxilla, vertical ridge augmentation mandible, native bone either jaw, full-arch rehabilitation either jaw)
- Separate reporting by specific short lengths (4, 5, 6 mm) where possible
- Integration of TSA and GRADE to grade certainty of evidence
- Emphasizes cost/morbidity rationale for short implants when augmentation avoided

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- PRISMA-based systematic review; only RCTs
- Meta-analysis with RR for survival/complications and mean difference for MBL where appropriate
- Trial sequential analysis to assess information size
- GRADE for certainty/quality of evidence per outcome/scenario
- RoB assessment (Cochrane RoB2)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Maxillary sinus lift: 6 mm vs ≥10 mm showed similar survival/complication rates at 5 years (high certainty)
- Native bone (either jaw): ≤6 mm vs ≥10 mm similar at 5 years (moderate certainty)
- Full-arch rehabilitation (either jaw): ≤6 mm vs ≥10 mm similar at 5 years (moderate certainty)
- Vertical ridge augmentation (mandible): insufficient/highly uncertain evidence for ≤6 mm vs long implants at 5 years
- Prosthetic complications: no significant difference overall

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Heterogeneous implant systems/surfaces; limited ability to subgroup by design
- Few long-term (≥10 years) RCTs
- Scarce data for 4/5 mm individually and for mandibular augmentation scenario
- Patient-reported outcomes/cost-effectiveness underreported
- Need adherence to ID-COSM and more scenario-specific RCTs

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary
- 단경 임플란트(short implant): 일반적으로 ≤6 mm (문헌에 따라 정의 상이)
- 장경 임플란트(long implant): ≥10 mm
- GRADE(Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation): 근거 확실성 등급화 체계
- TSA(Trial Sequential Analysis): 누적 메타분석의 정보량/유의성 평가
- MBL(Marginal Bone Level): 변연골 높이
- RR(Relative Risk): 상대위험도
