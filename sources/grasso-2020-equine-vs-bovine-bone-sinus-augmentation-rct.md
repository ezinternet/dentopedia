## Why Ingested

측방창 (Lateral Window) 상악동거상술의 이식재 축은 소 기원 탈단백우골무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 대 합성골·동종골 비교에 집중되어 있었고, [[overviews/sinus-lift-graft-material-prf-overview]]의 이종골 공급원 (xenograft source) 축은 돼지골 대 우골 ([[sinus-lift/lateral/galindomoreno-2022-maxillary-sinus-floor-augmentation-comparing]])까지만 채워져 있었다. 본 RCT는 말 기원 탈단백말골무기질 (Deproteinized Equine Bone Mineral, DEBM)을 무기질우골 (Anorganic Bovine Bone, ABB)과 직접 비교한 조직형태계측 근거로, DBBM 비-우골 대안의 조직학적 등가성 축을 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized, parallel, double-blind clinical trial (single center, Rome; 16 patients / 23 lateral-window sinus augmentations, 12 DEBM vs 11 ABB; 6-month healing; 23 trephine core biopsies histology + histomorphometry) of deproteinized equine bone mineral (DEBM, Bio-Gen mix, 0.5–1 mm) versus anorganic bovine bone (ABB, Endobon, 0.5–1 mm).

Newly formed bone did not differ between groups (22.20 ± 7.18% DEBM vs 22.84 ± 7.34% ABB, p>0.05, Mann–Whitney); marrow spaces were 50.77% vs 46.20% and residual graft particles 27% vs 39.94% of total volume (no between-group test reported for these two), with no phlogistic or multinucleated giant cells at the graft interface in either group.

Both osteoconductive xenografts behaved equivalently for new bone formation at 6 months in humans, so graft-source choice between equine and bovine mineral can rest on non-efficacy considerations (prion/BSE risk perception, availability, cost) — but the trial is small, 6 months only, and reports no implant-level or long-term stability outcome.

## 세줄요약

무작위·평행·이중맹검 임상시험 (단일기관, 이탈리아 로마; 16명 / 23개 측방창 상악동거상술, 탈단백말골무기질 (Deproteinized Equine Bone Mineral, DEBM) 12 vs 무기질우골 (Anorganic Bovine Bone, ABB) 11; 치유 6개월; 23개 트레핀 생검의 조직학·조직형태계측).

신생골 (New Bone, NB) 비율은 군간 유의차 없음(DEBM 22.20 ± 7.18% vs ABB 22.84 ± 7.34%, p>0.05, 만–휘트니 검정); 골수강 (Marrow Spaces) 50.77% vs 46.20%, 잔존이식재 (Residual Graft) 27% vs 39.94%였으나 이 두 지표의 군간 통계검정은 보고되지 않음. 두 군 모두 이식재 계면에 염증세포·다핵거대세포 없음.

6개월 시점에서 말 기원과 소 기원 무기질 이종골의 신생골 형성은 등가 — 공급원 선택은 효능이 아닌 전파위험 인식·수급·비용으로 결정할 수 있다는 근거이나, 표본이 작고 6개월 단일 시점이며 임플란트 수준·장기 안정성 결과가 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides **human histology + histomorphometry** for a non-bovine xenograft (equine bone mineral) against the standard bovine comparator, 6 months after lateral-window sinus augmentation — a comparison the authors state had only a handful of prior human histological studies.
- Uses a **randomized, parallel, double-blind** design: the graft material was unknown to the patient and to the examiner performing the histological processing (G.I.), though the surgeon (G.G.) was not blinded — an honest partial blinding (outcome-assessor blinding rather than full double blinding of the operator).
- Reports that both materials are **biologically well tolerated**: no multinucleated giant cells or phlogistic cells at the bone–graft interface, only a mild inflammatory infiltrate in 1 DEBM and 2 ABB biopsies.
- Describes qualitative resorption differences while the quantitative new-bone endpoint stays flat: DEBM particles showed indented margins (resorption) and lower residual fraction (27% vs 39.94%), whereas ABB showed particles encircled by new bone and small biomaterial spots colonising marrow spaces, consistent with its classically slow resorption.
- Explicitly frames the result against a **contradicting RCT it cites** (Di Stefano et al.: equine 46.86 ± 12.81% vs bovine 25.12 ± 7.25% new bone, p<0.05) and argues the difference is not reproduced here.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: randomized, parallel-group, double-blind (patient- and histology-examiner-blinded) clinical trial; single center; 6-month healing before biopsy.
- **Eligibility**: Caucasian adults ≥25 y, ≥1 atrophic posterior maxilla with **residual crest height ≤4 mm** on CBCT, needing sinus lift for implant placement. Excluded: impaired health status, smoking >10 cigarettes/day, bisphosphonates within 3 months, prior head/neck radiotherapy, sinusitis on ENT exam, pregnancy, cancer <1 year, BMI >30, autoimmune disease, allergy to amoxicillin/clavulanic acid/ibuprofen.
- **Randomization**: STATA/12.1 randomization software. Assignment unit is described inconsistently (see §5) — the Methods text speaks of 23 consecutive *patients* assigned, the Results of 23 *procedures* in 16 patients.
- **Surgery** (all by one operator, G.G.): crestal + vertical incision, 15c blade, local anesthesia (articaine 1:100,000); bony window on buccal wall with round diamond burr (40,000 rpm, saline cooling); Schneiderian membrane elevated from the cranial part; perforations recorded by Vlassis–Fugazzotto classification and sealed with an absorbable collagen membrane when they occurred (N = 3).
- **Grafting**: DEBM or ABB per randomization, mixed with the patient's blood, placed without excessive compression; the outer window covered with a double-layer collagen membrane (OsseoGuard, Zimmer Biomet); tension-free flap closure with 5/0 monofilament.
- **Post-op**: amoxicillin 875 mg + clavulanic acid 125 mg q12h × 6 days; ibuprofen 600 mg q8h × 2 days; 0.12% chlorhexidine rinse × 21 days; review at 10 days and every 30 days (pain, epistaxis, rhinorrhoea).
- **Biopsy/processing**: at 6 months, one 10-mm core per augmentation with a 3-mm internal-diameter trephine; 10% buffered formalin fixation; glycolethacrylate resin (Technovit 7200 VLC) embedding; high-precision diamond disc sectioning and abrasion to ~30 µm; 3 slices per specimen; acid fuchsin + toluidine blue staining.
- **Histomorphometry**: light microscope + 3CCD camera + digitising pad, Image-Pro Plus; percentages of new bone, marrow spaces and residual graft particles of total sample volume; single blinded examiner (G.I.) not involved in surgery.
- **Statistics**: Mann–Whitney test for independent variables (STATA/12.1); mean ± SD; α = 0.05.

| Residual graft particles | 27% | 39.94% | not reported in text |

**Qualitative histology**

| Feature | DEBM | ABB |
|---|---|---|
| Architecture | trabecular bone, large marrow spaces; new bone bridges between particles in mid-portion | trabecular bone bridging a large quantity of residual particles; particles fully encircled near pre-existing crestal bone |
| Bone–graft contact | strict contact, osteocyte lacunae in close contact with particles | no spaces at interface; lower-density particle margins suggesting early resorption |

**Values cited by the authors as external context** (not this study's data): ABB 6-month new bone 27.5 ± 8.9% (Nevins, 14 sinuses); DEBM 23.5% (Nevins case series); DEBM 39.84 ± 2.96% (Rivara, preliminary); Di Stefano RCT equine 46.86 ± 12.81% vs bovine 25.12 ± 7.25% (p<0.05); a six-biomaterial histomorphometric study with equine 22.8% new bone / 30.1% residual vs ABB 16.1% / 37.2% (no statistical analysis).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Small sample and unit-of-analysis ambiguity**: 23 augmentations in 16 patients, with 7 bilateral cases. The Methods state 23 consecutive *patients* were randomized while the Results describe 23 procedures in 16 patients, and no method for handling within-patient clustering of bilateral sinuses is described.
- **Blinding is partial by necessity**: the surgeon performing the augmentation was not blinded; only the patient and the histology examiner were. Allocation concealment is not described.
- **No implant-level or long-term outcomes**: implant survival, insertion torque values, ISQ, marginal bone loss and graft stability beyond 6 months are not reported; the authors call for larger RCTs with longer follow-up.
- **Only one endpoint (new bone) received a statistical test.** Residual graft (27% vs 39.94%) and marrow spaces (50.77% vs 46.20%) are reported as descriptive percentages with no p-value, so the apparent numeric difference in residual material must not be read as a demonstrated difference in resorption — although it is directionally consistent with the resorption-prone biology the authors describe for DEBM.
- **No volumetric/radiographic comparison** between groups; CBCT is used only as a success criterion for the grafted height, not as an outcome.
- **Single center, single operator, single material brand per class**, so results are brand-specific rather than class-wide.
- **Text completeness**: the retrieved PMC text lacks Table 1 and the reference list; the histomorphometric SDs for marrow spaces and residual graft, and the exact p-values (reported only as p>0.05), could not be verified from the source text.
- **Literature conflict not resolved**: the authors' null result contradicts the Di Stefano RCT they cite (equine significantly superior); they attribute the discrepancy to the collagenic component of the equine xenograft and to differences between studies, but no shared-protocol head-to-head exists.

## 6. Related Work

- **Di Stefano et al. [34]** — RCT of the same two xenograft classes reporting significantly more new bone with equine (46.86 ± 12.81%) than bovine (25.12 ± 7.25%) at implant placement; the direct contradiction this trial sets itself against.
- **Nevins et al. [27,41]** — ABB in 14 sinus augmentations (27.5 ± 8.9% new bone at 6 months) and a DEBM case series (23.5% new bone); the reference values against which the present means sit.
- **Rivara et al. [30]** — preliminary DEBM histology with 39.84 ± 2.96% new bone at 6 months.
- **Six-biomaterial histomorphometric comparison [35]** — equine 22.8% new bone / 30.1% residual vs ABB 16.1% / 37.2%, no statistical analysis; the same residual-fraction direction seen here.
- **Silva et al. 2016 SR [7]** and **Chen et al. [6]** — cited for the argument that grafting may not be essential to new bone formation in the sinus (implant survival 96.00% graftless vs 99.60% grafted).
- **Burchard [18]**, regeneration triad [8], antral artery anatomy [43–45] — the framing arguments for material requirements and for vascular supply as a determinant of graft integration.

## 7. Glossary

- **DEBM (Deproteinized Equine Bone Mineral)**: heterologous equine-derived bone graft; chemical-enzymatic processing preserves the collagen component and mechanical resistance, giving a resorbable/remodelling scaffold (Bio-Gen mix in this trial).
- **ABB (Anorganic Bovine Bone)**: bovine-derived hydroxyapatite deproteinized with two-step sterilisation; non-resorbable osteoconductive scaffold with micro- and macropores (Endobon in this trial). Used interchangeably with DBBM in the wider literature.
- **Histomorphometry**: quantitative measurement of the area fractions of new bone, marrow/connective tissue and residual graft particles on undecalcified ground sections.
- **Ground section**: ~30 µm undecalcified resin-embedded slice (here acid fuchsin + toluidine blue) allowing mineralised bone and graft particles to be viewed in situ.
- **Residual graft particles**: the fraction of the biopsy volume still occupied by the graft material at the healing time point; a proxy for resorption, not for graft function.
- **Vlassis–Fugazzotto classification**: a classification of Schneiderian membrane perforation size used to decide repair.
- **Mann–Whitney U test**: non-parametric test for independent groups, used here for all comparisons.
