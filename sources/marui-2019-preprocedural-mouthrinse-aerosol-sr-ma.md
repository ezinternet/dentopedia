## Why Ingested

This SR+MA provides the quantitative foundation (64.8% CFU reduction, moderate evidence) for recommending preprocedural mouthwash as a standard infection-control step in dental practice, making it the benchmark study against which individual mouthwash trials are measured. It directly supports the broader aerosol-reduction framework established by [[infection-control/kumbargere-nagraj-2020-interventions-reduce-contaminated-aerosols-dental-procedures-preventing-infectious-diseases-sr-ma]] while providing mouthwash-specific effect sizes.

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated the efficacy of preprocedural mouthrinses in reducing the number of microorganisms in aerosol generated during dental procedures, including 13 RCTs (397 participants). Meta-analysis showed preprocedural mouthrinses significantly reduced colony-forming units by 64.8% (95% CI: 50.4% to 79.3%, I² = 37%, moderate-quality evidence). Chlorhexidine (78.9% reduction), cetylpyridinium chloride (61.2% reduction), and essential oils (61.3% reduction) were all significantly effective.

There is moderate evidence that preprocedural mouthrinses significantly reduce microorganisms in dental aerosol, potentially reducing cross-contamination risk in dental offices.

## 세줄요약

본 체계적문헌고찰 및 메타분석은 치과 시술 중 발생하는 에어로졸 내 미생물 수 감소에 대한 시술 전 구강세정제의 효과를 13개의 무작위대조시험(RCT, 397명 참가자)을 통해 평가하였다. 메타분석 결과, 시술 전 구강세정제 사용은 집락형성단위(CFU)를 64.8% 유의하게 감소시켰고(95% CI: 50.4% ~ 79.3%, I² = 37%, 중등도 근거), 클로르헥시딘(78.9% 감소), 세틸피리디늄 염화물(61.2% 감소), 정유(61.3% 감소) 모두 유의하게 효과적이었다.

중등도 근거는 시술 전 구강세정제가 치과 에어로졸 내 미생물을 유의하게 감소시켜 치과 진료실 내 교차감염 위험을 줄일 수 있음을 보여준다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Preprocedural mouthrinses reduce aerosol CFUs by 64.8% overall (95% CI: 50.4% to 79.3%).
- Chlorhexidine mouthrinse reduces CFUs by 78.9% (95% CI: 59.9% to 98.0%, I² = 0%).
- Cetylpyridinium chloride mouthrinse reduces CFUs by 61.2% (95% CI: 20.2% to 102.3%, I² = 0%).
- Essential oils mouthrinse reduces CFUs by 61.3% (95% CI: 29.9% to 92.7%, I² = 76%).
- Herbal mouthrinse showed no significant reduction (35.9%, p = 0.08).
- Moderate-quality evidence supports preprocedural mouthrinse use for infection control.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- Databases: MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Google Scholar, LILACS. Searched through May 31, 2019.
- PRISMA compliance: Yes (PRISMA, Cochrane Handbook, Check Review checklist).
- Inclusion criteria: RCTs comparing preprocedural mouthrinse vs placebo/water/no rinse in patients undergoing aerosol-generating dental procedures. Outcome: reduction in viable bacteria (CFU) in aerosol.
- Excluded: Non-RCTs, studies without control group, no randomization for mouthrinse type.
- Included: 13 RCTs (12 in meta-analysis) with 397 participants.
- Risk of bias: Assessed using Cochrane RoB tool. No study had low risk of bias (6 high risk, 7 unclear risk). None reported adequate allocation concealment.
- Data synthesis: Random-effects meta-analysis using Review Manager 5.3. Generic inverse variance method for mean difference and SE. Heterogeneity assessed with Cochran Q and I².
- GRADE: Used for evidence quality (moderate).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Overall CFU reduction (active vs control)** — Mean reduction = 64.8% (95% CI: 50.4% to 79.3%, I² = 37%, moderate-quality evidence)
- **Chlorhexidine vs control** — Mean reduction = 78.9% (95% CI: 59.9% to 98.0%, I² = 0%)
- **Cetylpyridinium chloride vs control** — Mean reduction = 61.2% (95% CI: 20.2% to 102.3%, I² = 0%)
- **Essential oils vs control** — Mean reduction = 61.3% (95% CI: 29.9% to 92.7%, I² = 76%)
- **Herbal vs control** — Mean reduction = 35.9% (95% CI: -4.1% to 75.8%, p = 0.08, not significant)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No included study had low risk of bias; none reported allocation concealment.
- Primary outcome (CFU reduction) is a surrogate outcome for clinical infection; no direct evidence of reduced infection rates.
- Dental unit waterline (DUW) contamination may confound results; impact not measured.
- Blinding of participants was adequate only in Fine and colleagues' studies.
- Six studies were industry-sponsored; funding may overestimate effects.
- Heterogeneity high for essential oils subgroup (I² = 76%).
- Publication bias could not be assessed due to <10 studies.

## 7. Glossary

- **CFU (Colony-Forming Units)**: 집락형성단위 — 미생물 생존 및 증식 능력을 측정하는 단위
- **Aerosol**: 에어로졸 — 직경 50μm 미만의 액체 또는 고체 입자, 공기 중에 장시간 부유
- **Spatter**: 스패터 — 직경 50μm 이상의 공기 중 입자, 빠르게 침강
- **CHX (Chlorhexidine)**: 클로르헥시딘 — 광범위 항균 구강세정제
- **CPC (Cetylpyridinium Chloride)**: 세틸피리디늄 염화물 — 양이온 계면활성제 항균제
- **EO (Essential Oils)**: 정유 — Listerine 등에 포함된 항균 성분 (eucalyptus, menthol, methyl salicylate, thymol)
- **DUW (Dental Unit Waterlines)**: 치과 유닛 수도관 — 미생물 오염 가능성이 있는 수원
- **GRADE (Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation)**: 근거 품질 평가 및 권고 강도 등급 체계
