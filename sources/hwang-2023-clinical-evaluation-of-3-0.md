## Why Ingested

한국 대학병원(서울대학교치과병원)에서 단일 시스템 3.0-mm 협폭 임플란트 (narrow-diameter implant, NDI) 64개(49명)를 최대 5년 관찰한 후향적 연구로, 생존율 90.6%와 6건 실패가 모두 보철 전 조기 실패(식립 후 0-4개월 제거)라는 점, 고령이 독립 위험인자(조정 HR 1.17)라는 점이 한국 NDI 및 조기 실패 문헌을 보강한다. 환자 국적/민족은 본문에 명시되지 않고 한국 병원이라는 설정으로만 암시된다. → [[implants/short-narrow/kim-2023-clinical-outcome-of-narrow-diameter]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort at Seoul National University Dental Hospital (Korea; records March 2017 to April 2022) of a single 3.0-mm two-piece SLA implant system (TS III SA 3.0, Osstem): 64 implants in 49 patients, median follow-up 30.5 months (IQR 26.75-45).

Six of 64 implants (6 of 49 patients) failed (5 osseointegration failure, 1 severe buccal thread exposure), all removed before prosthetic treatment at 0-4 months after placement, giving a Kaplan-Meier survival of 90.6%; older age was the only significant risk factor (univariate HR 1.12, p=0.03; multivariate HR 1.17, 95% CI 1.04-1.31, p=0.01), while GBR showed a non-significant trend (multivariate HR 8.12, 95% CI 0.86-76.28, p=0.07).

Mean marginal bone loss (MBL) in the 58 implants that reached prosthesis was 0.44±0.75 mm with no significant associated factor; the paper itself notes the small sample, under 5 years of follow-up, unanalysed systemic disease and smoking, and unmeasured bone quality as limits.

## 세줄요약

서울대학교치과병원(한국)에서 2017년 3월-2022년 4월 기록을 후향 분석한 연구로, 단일 3.0-mm 2-piece SLA 임플란트 (TS III SA 3.0, Osstem) 64개(환자 49명), 추적 중앙값 30.5개월 (IQR 26.75-45).

64개 중 6개(환자 49명 중 6명)가 실패했고 (골유착 실패 5, 심한 협측 나사산 노출 1) 모두 보철 시작 전 식립 후 0-4개월에 제거되어 Kaplan-Meier 생존율 90.6%였으며, 고령만 유의한 위험인자였고 (단변량 HR 1.12, p=0.03; 다변량 HR 1.17, 95% CI 1.04-1.31, p=0.01) 골유도재생술 (guided bone regeneration, GBR)은 비유의 경향 (다변량 HR 8.12, 95% CI 0.86-76.28, p=0.07)이었다.

보철까지 도달한 58개의 평균 변연골 소실 (marginal bone loss, MBL)은 0.44±0.75 mm였고 유의한 관련 요인은 없었다; 저자들은 적은 표본, 5년 미만 추적, 전신질환·흡연 미분석, 골질 미측정을 한계로 제시한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Restricts the narrow-diameter question to one implant system and one diameter (3.0 mm, Klein category 2), avoiding the mixed-system heterogeneity the authors criticise in earlier NDI cohorts.
- Implant-level Kaplan-Meier survival 90.6%, below the 93.8-100% cohort range and 97.3±5% meta-analytic mean for category 2 NDIs that the authors cite.
- All 6 failures were early (removal 0-4 months after placement, before prosthetic treatment); 5 of 6 were osseointegration failure.
- Older age was the only significant survival factor (multivariate HR 1.17 per year, 95% CI 1.04-1.31, p=0.01).
- Short-term MBL was low (0.44±0.75 mm) and not explained by any tested factor.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, STROBE reporting; IRB No. ERI22022 (SNUDH); informed consent waived (anonymous data).
- **Setting/period**: Seoul National University Dental Hospital records reviewed March 2017 to April 2022.
- **Implants**: TS III SA 3.0 (Osstem Implant Co., Seoul, Korea); two-piece internal tapered, sandblasted and acid-etched; lengths 8.5, 10, 11.5, 13 mm.
- **Population**: 64 implants in 49 patients (24 male, 25 female; median age 64, IQR 49.75-72); 28 maxilla / 36 mandible; 55 implants at maxillary lateral and mandibular incisor sites, 9 others. Exclusion: final prosthesis not completed or no post-prosthesis radiograph.
- **Variables**: age, sex, location, length, surgical stage, GBR, type of implant placement (timing classification), prosthesis type (single vs splinted), surgeon proficiency (resident vs professor). Systemic disease, smoking and bone quality were not analysed.
- **Failure definition**: implant removed from the mouth; survival irrespective of biological/technical complications. Follow-up of failed implants = placement to removal. Denominator: implants (64); patient-level counts also given (6 of 49).
- **MBL**: fixture-abutment junction to first bone-to-implant contact on most recent panoramic/periapical radiograph, mesial and distal averaged, calibrated by implant length; 58 implants measured (the 6 failed ones were removed before prosthetic treatment).
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier; univariate then multivariate backward-stepwise Cox; Mann-Whitney, Kruskal-Wallis, Spearman for MBL; SPSS 26; p<0.05.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (64 implants, 6 events); several Cox estimates have extremely wide intervals (e.g. tooth location 0.04, 0.00-744.30; placement type 4, 0.00-7.18E+199), so non-significant results are uninformative.
- Follow-up under 5 years; NDIs in use since 2017, so failures were mainly early; late failure (overload, peri-implantitis) not assessed.
- Systemic disease and smoking not analysed (verbal, retrospective records); bone quality not measured directly (stage of surgery and GBR used as indirect proxies).
- Most implants (48 of 64) placed by residents; authors suggest this may partly explain the lower survival, though surgeon proficiency was not significant.
- MBL measured from the fixture-abutment junction, so bone loss above the junction in subcrestal implants may be missed; MBL was measured in 58 implants, only those reaching prosthesis.
- Single-center, single-system, no comparison group.
- Internal inconsistencies in the retrieved text: Table 1 prosthesis type totals 58 (24 single / 34 splinted) rather than 64, consistent with the 6 pre-prosthesis failures but not stated; the Discussion cites an average follow-up of 25 months for MBL whereas Results give a median of 30.5 months (different subsets or statistics, not explained); the mean/median follow-up is called "up to 5 years" in the title while the cohort period spans 2017-2022. Nationality/ethnicity of patients is not reported.

## 7. Glossary

- **NDI (Narrow-Diameter Implant, 협폭 임플란트)**: here 3.0 mm, Klein category 2 (3.0-3.25 mm).
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연골 소실)**: distance from fixture-abutment junction to first bone-to-implant contact.
- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 골유도재생술)**: barrier-membrane bone augmentation.
- **FAJ / fBIC**: fixture-abutment junction / first bone-to-implant contact.
- **HR (Hazard Ratio, 위험비)**: relative event rate in a Cox model.
- **Early failure (조기 실패)**: here, removal before prosthetic treatment, at 0-4 months.
