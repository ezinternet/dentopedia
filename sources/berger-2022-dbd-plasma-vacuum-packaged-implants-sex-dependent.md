## Three-line Summary

In vivo rabbit femur transaxial-implant study (36 rabbits, Ti6Al4V grit-blasted+acid-etched screw implants, 6-week endpoint) comparing untreated vs 30-second DBD-plasma-treated implants stored in vacuum-sealed vials, contralateral placement, stratified by sex.

In males, DBD plasma treatment significantly increased bone-to-implant contact (micro-CT), adjacent bone volume, mechanical peak torque, and torsional energy versus untreated contralateral implants; in females, plasma did not significantly increase BIC (baseline osseointegration already high), though it did increase torsional stiffness.

DBD plasma restores surface wettability (contact angle 0° vs ~80° untreated) without altering roughness or elemental composition, and its osseointegration benefit appears to depend on baseline bone-forming capacity — most relevant in male/compromised-bone scenarios where the biological "ceiling" has not already been reached by endogenous (estrogen-related) osteogenic signaling.

## 세줄요약

토끼 대퇴골 경축 임플란트 연구(36마리, Ti6Al4V 그릿블라스팅+산부식 나사형 임플란트, 6주 종료시점) — 무처리 vs 진공 밀봉 보관 후 식립 직전 30초 DBD(Dielectric Barrier Discharge) 플라즈마 처리군을 반대측 대조 배치로 비교, 성별로 층화.

수컷에서는 DBD 플라즈마 처리가 골-임플란트 접촉률(micro-CT)·인접 골량·최대 제거토크·비틀림 에너지를 유의하게 증가시켰으나, 암컷에서는 기저 골유착이 이미 높아 BIC 유의 증가는 없었음(단 비틀림 강성은 증가).

DBD 플라즈마는 거칠기·원소조성 변화 없이 표면 습윤성만 회복(접촉각 0° vs 무처리 ~80°)하며, 그 골유착 이점은 기저 골형성능(에스트로겐 관련 내인성 골생성 신호가 이미 "천장"에 도달했는지 여부)에 의존 — 남성/골질 저하 시나리오에서 가장 유의미할 가능성.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First report of a sex-dependent osseointegration response to DBD plasma treatment of vacuum-packaged dental implants.
- Establishes that 30-second DBD plasma restores surface wettability without altering roughness (Sa ~2.9 µm unchanged) or elemental composition — mechanism is purely wettability restoration, not topographic/chemical modification.
- Companion in-vitro osteoblast work (calvarial osteoblasts from both sexes) shows male cells produce significantly higher osteocalcin/BMP2/osteoprotegerin on titanium surfaces than female cells, offering a cellular-level explanation for the in-vivo sex divergence.
- Proposes a "biological ceiling" model: plasma's local wettability effect is masked in females by an already highly osteogenic systemic (estrogen-driven) environment, but is more consequential when baseline bone-forming capacity is lower (as modeled by males here, and hypothesized for compromised-bone patients).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: animal in-vivo, rabbit femur bilateral transaxial implant model; destructive mechanical testing cohort + micro-CT/calcified-histology cohort; companion in-vitro osteoblast assays.
- n: 36 rabbits (20 male, 16 female) — cohort 1 (mechanical testing, n=18) and cohort 2 (micro-CT/histology, n=18); implants randomized per hind-limb, contralateral control.
- Plasma protocol: implants grit-blasted + acid-etched (GB+AE), vacuum-sealed in glass vials, gamma-sterilized; DBD plasma applied via tabletop applicator (Nova Plasma Ltd.) at 13 kV ignition voltage, 700 kHz, for **30 seconds** immediately before insertion.
- Endpoints: micro-CT BIC and adjacent bone volume (6 weeks), calcified histology BIC/adjacent bone area, mechanical torque-to-failure/torsional stiffness/torsional energy, surface characterization (SEM, optical profilometry, contact-angle goniometry, XPS).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Males | Females |
|---|---|---|
| BIC (micro-CT) | Significantly ↑ with plasma | No significant ↑ (already high baseline) |
| Adjacent bone volume | Significantly ↑ with plasma | Increased regardless of plasma treatment |
| Peak torque / torsional energy | Significantly ↑ with plasma | Not significantly ↑ with plasma |
| Torsional stiffness | No significant change | Significantly ↑ with plasma |
| Contact angle | 0° (plasma) vs ~80° (untreated) — both sexes, surface effect not sex-dependent | |
| Surface roughness / elemental composition | Unchanged by DBD treatment (both sexes) | |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Healthy young rabbits only — authors explicitly note the plasma effect might be more consequential in compromised-bone models (osteoporotic, aged, diabetic), which this study does not test directly.
- Rabbit pubertal timing (onset ~3 months, mid-study) proposed as a confound for the sex-divergent bone-formation baseline; mechanism (estrogen signaling) is hypothesized, not directly tested in vivo.
- No human/clinical data; 6-week single endpoint; industry-adjacent low-cost device (Nova Plasma Ltd.) not directly comparable to the vacuum-plasma devices (Plasmapp) used in the Korean cluster of papers in this category.

## 6. Related Work

- [[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/kahm-2024-vacuum-plasma-surface-treatment-osseointegration]] — different vacuum-plasma device/protocol (Plasmapp ACTILINK) and shorter follow-up (4 weeks vs 6); Kahm found no significant BIC/ISQ gain in rabbits (sex not stratified), while this paper finds a significant gain but only in males — sex-stratification may explain part of the discrepancy if Kahm's cohort was sex-mixed or predominantly one sex.
- [[wiki/implants/surface/plasma/cho-2025-rhbmp2-vacuum-plasma-titanium-implants-osseointegration]] — both use vacuum-related plasma protocols on Ti implants in vivo; Cho found plasma-alone insufficient without a bioactive coating (rhBMP-2) to reach significance, this paper found plasma-alone sufficient in males, suggesting host sex/bone-forming capacity may be as important a moderator as coating chemistry.
- [[overviews/plasma-surface-treatment-dental-implants]] — adds a host-factor (sex) moderator not previously covered by the overview's existing dose/timing/substrate-dependency caveats.

## 7. Glossary

- DBD = Dielectric Barrier Discharge (유전체 장벽 방전) — a cold, non-thermal plasma generation method using an insulating barrier between electrodes.
- BIC = bone-to-implant contact (골-임플란트 접촉률).
- GB+AE = grit-blasted + acid-etched surface treatment.
- Torsional stiffness/energy = mechanical resistance/work-to-failure metrics from torque-removal testing.
