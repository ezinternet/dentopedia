## Why Ingested

ADDwoR(환원 없는 전방 관절원판 변위, Anterior Disc Displacement without Reduction)에 대한 보존적 병용치료의 근거가 부족한 상황에서, CAD/CAM 스플린트+도수치료 병용이 스플린트 단독 및 건강교육 대조군보다 MMO·VAS·MFIQ에서 유의하게 우월함을 후향적으로 보고한 중국 임상연구. 기존 [[tmj/overview-tmj-management-conservative]] 등 TMJ 보존치료 신호 페이지를 보강하며, 병용요법 프로토콜의 임상 참고 자료로 활용.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective observational study (n=65) at Zhejiang Provincial People's Hospital comparing CAD/CAM occlusal splint alone (n=22), splint+manual therapy (n=22), and health education alone (n=21) for ADDwoR over 3 months.

The combined splint+manual therapy group showed significantly superior maximum mouth opening (MMO), pain (VAS), and mandibular function (MFIQ) at 1 and 3 months versus both other groups (p<0.05); splint alone outperformed health education on all measures.

Patient-preference (non-randomized) allocation and short 3-month follow-up limit causal inference; an RCT with longer follow-up is needed to confirm durability of benefit.

## 세줄요약

중국 저장성 인민병원에서 ADDwoR(환원 없는 전방 관절원판 변위) 환자 65명을 CAD/CAM 스플린트 단독(n=22), 스플린트+도수치료 병용(n=22), 건강교육 단독(n=21) 세 군으로 후향적 비교한 3개월 연구.

병용군은 1개월·3개월 시점 모두에서 최대개구량(MMO), 통증(VAS), 하악기능장애지수(MFIQ) 모든 지표에서 나머지 두 군보다 유의하게 우월(p<0.05); 스플린트 단독군도 건강교육군보다 우월.

환자 선호 기반 비무작위 배정과 3개월 단기 추적이 인과 추론을 제한하며, 장기 효과 확인을 위한 RCT가 필요함.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Oral & Facial Pain and Headache (JOFPH)
- **Year**: 2025
- **DOI**: 10.22514/jofph.2025.055
- **PMC**: PMC12520436
- **Institution**: Zhejiang Provincial People's Hospital, Hangzhou, China
- **Ethics**: KT2023010 (Human Research Ethics Committee, Zhejiang Provincial People's Hospital)
- **Study period**: March 2022 – March 2023
- **Design**: Retrospective observational study, patient preference-based allocation

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides clinical evidence for CAD/CAM occlusal splint combined with manual therapy (도수치료, Manual Therapy) superiority over splint alone or health education in ADDwoR
- Describes the Kovacs Digital Occlusal Splint (KDOS) protocol in detail (iTero scanning → Exocad design → SELECT five-axis milling)
- Uses three validated outcome measures: MMO (최대개구량), VAS (시각통증척도), MFIQ (하악기능장애지수)
- Confirms 81–87% statistical power for the sample size

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Participants**: 65 adult patients with ADDwoR confirmed by DC/TMD criteria and MRI; persistent symptoms ≥3 months; VAS ≥3.0; MMO ≤30 mm.

**Exclusion**: Prior TMJ surgery, systemic conditions (RA, OA), concurrent TMD pharmacotherapy or orthodontics, severe psychological disorders, substance abuse, acute TMJ infection/inflammation.

**Groups**:
1. Occlusal Splint Group (n=22, mean age 34.5±7.2 years): KDOS protocol — intraoral scan (iTero), digital balancer fabrication, jaw position determination, Exocad design, SELECT five-axis milling; worn ≥12 h/day mainly during sleep; monthly follow-up
2. Combined Treatment Group (n=22, mean age 33.8±6.9 years): KDOS splint + weekly manual therapy (15–20 min/session, 3 months) — soft tissue mobilization (trigger points: temporalis, masseter, SCM) + joint mobilization (traction, protrusion, capsular stretching)
3. Health Education Group (n=21, mean age 35.2±7.5 years): dietary and behavioral counseling only; monthly follow-up

**Outcome measures**: MMO (mm), VAS (0–10 pain), MFIQ (mandibular functional impairment questionnaire) at baseline, 1 month, 3 months

**Statistics**: ANOVA / appropriate tests; α=0.05; power 81–87%

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- All groups showed improvement from baseline across all outcomes
- **Combined group** significantly outperformed both splint-only and health-education groups at 1 month and 3 months on MMO, VAS, and MFIQ (p<0.05)
- **Splint-only group** outperformed health-education group on all measures
- Baseline characteristics (age, sex, VAS, MMO) were comparable across all three groups (p>0.05)
- Manual therapy administered once weekly for 15–20 minutes over 3 months by licensed physical therapists specialized in TMJ rehabilitation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Non-randomized design**: patient preference-based allocation may introduce selection bias and expectation (placebo) effects
- **Short follow-up**: only 3 months; long-term durability of combined therapy benefit unknown
- **Single institution**: Zhejiang Provincial People's Hospital; external generalizability uncertain
- **No blinding**: patients knew their treatment; expectancy bias possible (though theoretically symmetrical across groups)
- **No MRI re-evaluation**: disc position changes not confirmed post-treatment
- **Future**: RCT with longer follow-up, larger multicenter sample, MRI outcome assessment recommended

## 6. Related Work

- Al-Moraissi network meta-analysis (742 patients, 16 RCTs): no single intervention consistently optimal for ADDwoR pain/MMO
- DC/TMD (Diagnostic Criteria for Temporomandibular Disorders) used for diagnosis
- KDOS protocol referencing Kovacs et al.; Exocad/SELECT milling workflow

## 7. Glossary

- **ADDwoR**: Anterior Disc Displacement without Reduction — 환원 없는 전방 관절원판 변위; disc remains anteriorly displaced throughout jaw movement
- **MMO**: Maximum Mouth Opening — 최대개구량 (mm)
- **VAS**: Visual Analog Scale — 시각통증척도 (0–10)
- **MFIQ**: Mandibular Functional Impairment Questionnaire — 하악기능장애지수
- **KDOS**: Kovacs Digital Occlusal Splint — CAD/CAM 기반 디지털 교합 스플린트 제작 프로토콜
- **CAD/CAM**: Computer-Aided Design/Computer-Aided Manufacturing — 컴퓨터 지원 설계/제조
- **DC/TMD**: Diagnostic Criteria for Temporomandibular Disorders — 측두하악장애 진단기준
- **Manual Therapy**: 도수치료 — soft tissue mobilization + joint mobilization for TMJ
