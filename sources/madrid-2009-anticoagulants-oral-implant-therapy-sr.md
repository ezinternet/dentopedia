## Why Ingested
항응고제 복용 환자의 구강 임플란트 치료 가능성과 관리 프로토콜을 다룬 초기 SR 기준 문헌. [[drug/anticoagulants/chahine-2019-anticoagulation-dental-procedures-sr]] 및 [[drug/anticoagulants/lee-2024-postop-bleeding-anticoagulants-dentoalveolar-cohort]]의 선행 근거로 기능하며, 임플란트 수술의 항응고 관련 금기 여부에 답한다.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review of 19 studies (5 RCT, 11 CCT, 3 prospective case series) from Medline, Cochrane, and EMBASE (1966–2008) evaluating oral surgery—predominantly extraction—in patients on oral anticoagulation therapy with INR maintained at 2–4.
Patients continuing OAT did not show significantly higher post-operative bleeding; INR value within the therapeutic range did not correlate with bleeding risk; and no thromboembolic events were reported across all 19 studies.
OAT discontinuation is not recommended for minor procedures including single tooth extraction and implant placement; this pre-DOAC landmark provided the foundational safety evidence extended by subsequent NOAC studies.

## 세줄요약
줄1: 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR) 19편 (Medline·Cochrane·EMBASE, 1966–2008): 경구항응고요법 (Oral Anticoagulation Therapy, OAT) 환자의 구강외과술 (주로 발치·임플란트 수술)에서 유지·중단·헤파린 브리징 (Heparin Bridging) 전략 비교.
줄2: 치료적 국제정상화비율 (International Normalized Ratio, INR) 2–4 범위 내 항응고제 유지 시 술후 출혈 유의하게 증가하지 않음; INR 수치 자체는 출혈 예측인자 아님; 19편 전체에서 혈전색전증 (Thromboembolism) 사례 없음.
줄3: 단순 발치·임플란트 수술 (자가골 이식·광범위 피판 없는 경우)에서 경구항응고제 중단 불필요; 직접구강항응고제 (Direct Oral Anticoagulant, DOAC) 이전 시대 선도적 SR로 이후 NOAC 문헌의 기준선 역할.

## One-line Summary
SR (19 studies, 1980–2008): OAT (INR 2–4) patients do not have significantly higher post-op bleeding than non-OAT patients; implant placement under OAT is not contraindicated; local hemostats are sufficient.

## 한줄요약
SR(19편): INR 2–4 항응고요법 환자는 비중단 시 출혈 위험이 유의하게 높지 않으며, 임플란트 수술이 금기가 아님; 국소 지혈제로 관리 가능.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR specifically addressing implant therapy (not just extraction) in OAT patients
- Concluded that dental implant placement under OAT is NOT contraindicated
- Evidence: no significant difference in post-op bleeding between OAT-continued vs. OAT-discontinued groups
- Post-op bleeding did NOT correlate with INR value (within therapeutic range 2–4)
- No thromboembolic events reported in any included study
- Provides management protocol: continue OAT + use local hemostats

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review
- **n studies**: 19 (5 RCT, 11 CCT, 3 prospective case series)
- **Search period**: 1966–2008
- **Procedures covered**: Oral surgery (predominantly extractions); limited implant-specific data
- **Management strategies compared**: OAT continuation vs. discontinuation vs. heparin bridging
- **Local hemostats evaluated**: Tranexamic acid mouthwash, gelatin sponge, cellulose gauze

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- OAT patients (INR 2–4) continued: no significant increase in post-op bleeding
- Topical hemostatic agents effective in preventing post-op bleeding when OAT continued
- OAT discontinuation NOT recommended for: single tooth extraction, implant placement (without autogenous bone graft, extensive flap, or osteotomy outside bony envelope)
- Post-op bleeding DID NOT correlate with INR within 2–4 range
- No thromboembolic events across all 19 studies
- Heterogeneity prevented data pooling/meta-analysis

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Most data from extraction studies; implant-specific evidence very limited
- Pre-DOAC era — no direct oral anticoagulant data
- Heterogeneous management protocols; no meta-analysis
- Short follow-up in most studies

## 6. Related Work
- Chahine 2019 SR: updated with NOAC data, same conclusion
- Lee 2024 cohort: large-scale confirmation + risk stratification
- Moldovan 2023: contemporary update
