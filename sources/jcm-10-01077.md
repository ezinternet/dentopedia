## Why Ingested
This 5-year RCT provides high-level evidence comparing immediate vs delayed loading of single-tooth implants in the maxillary aesthetic zone, directly extending [[wiki/implants/loading-protocol/gjelvold-2017-immediate-delayed-loading-single-tooth]] (the 1-year results from the same trial) with longer-term survival, marginal bone loss, and aesthetic outcomes.

## Three-line Summary
Prospective RCT with 50 patients (25 immediate loading, 25 delayed loading) receiving single-tooth implants in healed maxillary aesthetic zone sites (teeth 15–25), evaluated at 5-year follow-up.
At 5 years, implant survival was 100% (IL) vs 95.8% (DL); success rates 91.7% vs 83.3%; mean marginal bone loss −0.50 mm (IL) vs −0.54 mm (DL) (p=0.782); no significant differences in papilla index, PES, or WES.
Immediate and delayed loading protocols yield comparable 5-year clinical and aesthetic outcomes for single-tooth implants in the maxillary aesthetic zone; smoking significantly increased marginal bone loss (p=0.021).

## 세줄요약
전향적 RCT로 50명 환자(즉시부하 25명, 지연부하 25명)가 상악 심미부위(15-25번) 치유된 골에서 단일치아 임플란트 식립 후 5년간 추적관찰.
5년째 생존율 100% vs 95.8%, 성공률 91.7% vs 83.3%, 평균 변연골소실 −0.50mm vs −0.54mm (p=0.782), 유두지수·PES·WES 모두 군간 유의차 없음.
즉시부하와 지연부하 프로토콜이 상악 심미부위 단일치아 임플란트에서 5년간 유사한 임상·심미적 결과를 보임; 흡연은 유의한 골소실 증가와 연관 (p=0.021).

## 2. Key Contributions
- 5-year follow-up of a prospective RCT comparing immediate vs delayed loading for single-tooth implants in the maxillary aesthetic zone
- Comprehensive assessment including survival, success, marginal bone loss, papilla index, PES, and WES
- Demonstrates comparable long-term outcomes between loading protocols in healed sites

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective randomized controlled trial (RCT), parallel-group, single-center (Malmö, Sweden)
- **n**: 50 patients (24 IL, 24 DL evaluated at 5 years; 2 lost to follow-up)
- **Outcomes**: Implant survival, implant success (Albrektsson/Zarb criteria), marginal bone level (MBL), papilla index (Jemt), Pink Esthetic Score (PES), White Esthetic Score (WES)
- **Implants**: Tapered Internal implants (BioHorizons), screw-retained temporary crowns (IL: 2 months; DL: after 4-month healing), definitive zirconia-veneered titanium-base crowns

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Immediate Loading (n=24) | Delayed Loading (n=24) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5-year implant survival | 100% (24/24) | 95.8% (23/24) | — |
| 5-year implant success | 91.7% (22/24) | 83.3% (20/24) | — |
| 5-year MBL (mean ± SD) | −0.50 ± 0.73 mm | −0.54 ± 0.65 mm | 0.782 |
| 5-year papilla index (mesial) | 2.54 ± 0.51 | 2.57 ± 0.73 | 0.518 |
| 5-year papilla index (distal) | 2.33 ± 0.67 | 2.39 ± 0.84 | 0.527 |
| 5-year PES | 11.00 ± 2.08 | 11.17 ± 2.39 | 0.501 |
| 5-year WES | 7.63 ± 1.53 | 7.91 ± 1.53 | 0.365 |
| Almost perfect aesthetic (PES≥12 & WES≥9) | 20.8% (5/24) | 30.4% (7/23) | — |

Subgroup: Smokers had significantly more MBL (−1.90 ± 0.90 mm) than non-smokers (−0.40 ± 0.58 mm) (p=0.021). No differences by sex or retention type (cemented vs screw-retained).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=24/group at 5 years) limits statistical power for subgroup analyses
- Single-center study at a specialist clinic; generalizability to general practice settings unclear
- Only healed sites (Type 2/3 placement) studied; results may not apply to immediate implant placement scenarios
- Blinding of surgeon to loading protocol only until surgery; prosthetic examiner blinded but surgical examiner not

## 7. Glossary
- **IL**: Immediate Loading — provisional restoration placed at time of implant surgery or within 48 hours
- **DL**: Delayed Loading — conventional two-stage protocol with healing period before loading
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Loss — radiographic bone level change from baseline (implant-abutment junction)
- **PES**: Pink Esthetic Score — 7-item soft tissue aesthetic score (max 14)
- **WES**: White Esthetic Score — 5-item crown aesthetic score (max 10)
- **Papilla Index (Jemt)**: 0–3 scale for mesial/distal papilla fill (3 = complete fill)
