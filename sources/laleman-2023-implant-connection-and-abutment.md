## Why Ingested

Narrative review that assembles the **prosthetic-side risk factors** for peri-implant diseases — connection type, transmucosal configuration, abutment material/design and handling — into one chairside decision framework, and explicitly concedes that direct disease-outcome evidence is scarce. It is the conceptual counterpart to [[implants/peri-implantitis/lin-2025-influence-of-prosthetic-designs]] and the interpretive frame for [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]]: the review's recommendations are mostly inferred from early bone loss, which the newer OAOT meta-analyses now test directly.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review evaluating whether implant connection and abutment characteristics (material, design, transmucosal configuration, handling) act as predisposing or precipitating factors for peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis.

Direct evidence that these features cause peri-implant disease is limited; the authors favour internal conical connections with inherent platform switching over flat-to-flat or external connections, early coronal placement of the prosthetic interface (tissue-level implant or one-abutment-one-time) to reduce disconnections and cement remnants, titanium or zirconia in the transmucosal portion, and higher implants (≥2 mm) with an emergence angle below 30°.

Recommendations are largely extrapolated from early bone-loss endpoints rather than disease incidence — useful as a hypothesis-generating component-selection framework, not as firm evidence-based clinical mandates.

## 세줄요약

서술적 리뷰: 임플란트 연결부와 지대주 특성(재료·형태·점막통과부 구성·조작 횟수)이 임플란트주위점막염(Peri-implant Mucositis)·임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis)의 선행·유발 인자인지를 검토.

직접 근거는 제한적이나, 골수준 임플란트에서는 내원뿔 연결(Internal Conical Connection, 플랫폼 스위칭 내재)이 평면-평면·외부 연결보다 유리하고, 보철 계면을 가능한 한 치은정 수준으로 빨리 옮겨 탈착 횟수와 시멘트 잔여물을 줄이며(조직수준 임플란트 또는 원-어버트먼트-원-타임), 점막통과부는 티타늄·지르코니아가 바람직하고, 높이 ≥2 mm + 출현각도(Emergence Angle, EA) <30°가 유리하다고 제안.

권고 대부분이 초기 골소실이라는 간접 지표에서 외삽된 것이라, 가설 생성용 부품 선택 틀로 읽어야지 확정적 근거 기반 지침으로 읽어서는 안 됨.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Frames implant connection and abutment choice as **predisposing/precipitating** (not sole causative) factors for peri-implant mucositis/peri-implantitis.
- Conical internal connection with inherent platform switching preferred over internal flat-to-flat and external connections for bone-level implants (early bone-loss endpoint).
- Recommends moving the prosthetic interface coronally to juxta-mucosal level as soon as possible — tissue-level implant or transmucosal (one-abutment-one-time) abutment — to reduce disconnections and cement remnants and optimise the soft-tissue seal.
- Biocompatibility caveat: absent evidence for several restorative materials, prefer titanium or zirconia in the transmucosal portion.
- Prosthetic geometry signal: higher implants (≥2 mm) with emergence angle <30° appear more favourable.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Narrative (critical) review of implant connection, abutment material, abutment design and abutment handling literature.
- **Search**: Not reported as a systematic PRISMA search — a targeted review of clinical and preclinical evidence.
- **Endpoint hierarchy**: Direct peri-implant disease outcomes are sparse; most supporting data are early crestal bone loss, histologic soft-tissue seal or biomechanical microgap studies.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Domain | Review's directional conclusion | Evidence basis |
|---|---|---|
| Connection type (bone-level) | Internal conical (inherent PS) > flat-flat / external | Early bone loss, mostly indirect |
| Prosthetic interface position | Move coronally to juxta-mucosal ASAP | Disconnection count, cement remnants, soft-tissue seal |
| Abutment protocol | Tissue-level or transmucosal OAOT | Reduced manipulation |
| Transmucosal material | Titanium or zirconia preferred | Biocompatibility evidence gap for alternatives |
| Abutment height / emergence angle | ≥2 mm higher, EA <30° | Radiographic/histologic association |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No systematic search or formal evidence grading stated; conclusions are expert synthesis.
- Explicitly acknowledges that most information is indirect — derived from early bone loss rather than mucositis/peri-implantitis incidence.
- Conflicts of interest: France Lambert reports research grants/lecturing/consulting fees from Straumann, Nobel Biocare and ITI.
- A correction was issued after first online publication ("longer implants" → "higher implants"), so citing the ≥2 mm claim requires the corrected version.

## 7. Glossary
- **PS (Platform Switching)**: abutment diameter reduced relative to implant platform, moving the microgap medially.
- **OAOT (One-Abutment-One-Time)**: definitive abutment connected once and never removed.
- **EA (Emergence Angle)**: angle between the restoration contour and the mucosal surface at the implant interface.
- **Transmucosal portion**: the abutment/implant segment traversing the peri-implant soft tissue.
