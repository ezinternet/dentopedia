## Why Ingested

기존 [[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/angermair-2024-microgap-abutment-displacement-implant-connection]]가 정하중 하 임플란트-지대주 연결부의 마이크로갭·지대주 변위를 in-vitro로 규명했으나, 그 마이크로갭 위치(implant-level vs abutment-level)가 실제 임상 결과(변연골소실·탐침깊이·생존율)에 미치는 영향은 다루지 않았다. 본 SR+MA(3 RCT, 8편, 126명·341 implant)는 그 임상적 공백을 직접 메우며, intermediate abutment(AL) 사용이 IL 대비 36개월 시점 MBL·탐침깊이를 통계적으로 유의하게 감소시키나(각각 -0.13mm, -0.66mm) 임상적으로는 미미함을 정량화한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs (3 parallel-arm RCTs, 8 publications, 126 patients, 341 implants) comparing abutment-level (AL, intermediate abutment) versus implant-level (IL) connections in screw-retained implant-supported fixed partial prostheses.

Statistically significant but small reduction in marginal bone loss (MBL) favoring AL at 36 months (MD -0.13 mm; 95% CI -0.23 to -0.03; p=0.01; moderate CoE), and significant probing depth (PD) reduction at 36 months (MD -0.66 mm; p=0.01) and 60 months (MD -0.60 mm; p<0.001); no differences in MBL at 12/60 months, or in bleeding on probing, implant survival, mechanical complications, or patient satisfaction at any time point.

Intermediate abutments improve soft-tissue health (reduced PD) without added complications or failure risk, but the MBL benefit is not clinically relevant — both AL and IL remain viable protocols for multi-unit screw-retained FDPs; evidence base is limited to 3 trials from 2 countries with low-to-moderate certainty (GRADE).

## 세줄요약

나사 유지형 임플란트 지지 고정성 부분보철물에서 중간 지대주(abutment-level, AL) 연결 대 임플란트-레벨(IL) 직접연결을 비교한 RCT 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (병렬군 RCT 3편, 8개 출판물, 환자 126명, 임플란트 341개).

36개월 시점 변연골소실(MBL)에서 AL이 통계적으로 유의하게 낮았고(MD -0.13mm; 95% CI -0.23~-0.03; p=0.01; 중등도 근거등급), 탐침깊이(PD)도 36개월(MD -0.66mm; p=0.01)과 60개월(MD -0.60mm; p<0.001)에 AL에서 유의하게 감소; 12·60개월 MBL, 출혈지수(BoP), 임플란트 생존율, 기계적 합병증, 환자만족도는 모든 시점에서 군간 차이 없음.

중간 지대주는 연조직 건강(탐침깊이 감소)을 개선하되 합병증·실패 위험 증가는 없으나, MBL 개선 효과는 임상적으로 유의미하지 않아 AL·IL 모두 다분할 나사유지형 보철에서 실행 가능한 선택지이며, 근거는 2개국 3개 RCT에 국한되어 근거등급이 중등도-낮음(GRADE)이라는 한계가 있다.

- **Study type**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (SR+MA) of RCTs, PRISMA 2020 + Cochrane Handbook methodology

## 2. Key Contributions

1. First SR+MA to directly quantify the clinical impact of intermediate (abutment-level, AL) versus implant-level (IL) connection protocols specifically for **screw-retained multi-unit fixed partial prostheses** (not single-unit restorations), pooling MBL, probing depth (PD), bleeding on probing (BoP), implant survival, mechanical complications, and patient-reported satisfaction across follow-up intervals (12, 36, 60 months).
2. Demonstrates that all included evidence traces back to only 3 independent parallel-arm RCTs (Göthberg et al. Sweden 2014/2016/2018; Maceiras et al. Spain 2023/2024; Toia et al. Sweden 2019/2022/2024) reported across 8 publications as longitudinal extensions — the review correctly treats these as single trials with multiple time points rather than 8 independent studies, avoiding double-counting.
3. Applies GRADE certainty-of-evidence rating per outcome/time point, showing moderate certainty for implant survival and mechanical complications but low certainty for MBL, PD, BoP, and patient satisfaction — flagging the imprecision inherent in a 126-patient, 3-trial evidence base.
4. RoB2 assessment found all 8 included reports had low risk of bias across all 5 domains — a notably clean risk-of-bias profile for implant-prosthodontic RCTs.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA 2020-compliant systematic review + random-effects meta-analysis; Cochrane Handbook methodology; PROSPERO-registered (CRD42023475778).
- **Search**: MEDLINE (PubMed), Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane CENTRAL, EMBASE searched 2025-05-22; supplemented by manual reference screening and Google Scholar; no date/language restrictions.
- **PICOS**: Population = partially edentulous adults; Intervention = screw-retained FDPs with intermediate (AL) abutments; Comparison = direct IL connection; Outcome = MBL (primary), PD/BoP/survival/mechanical complications/PROMs (secondary); Study design = RCTs with ≥6 months follow-up.
- **Screening**: Two independent reviewers (P.M-M., S.A.M.) with 10% calibration set before full screening; disagreements resolved by third reviewer (A.R.); Cohen's κ = 0.89 (title/abstract stage), κ = 1.00 (full-text stage).
- **Data extraction**: Two independent reviewers (P.M-M., G.P.S.), standardized spreadsheet, third-reviewer arbitration.
- **Statistics**: Weighted mean differences (MD) for continuous outcomes, risk differences (RD) for dichotomous outcomes; random- or fixed-effects models per heterogeneity; DerSimonian-Laird or inverse-variance for between-study variance; heterogeneity via Cochran's Q and I²; analyses conducted at both patient level (PL) and implant level (IL as unit-of-analysis, distinct from IL=implant-level connection group) where possible; STATA v18 and R v4.3.1.
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB 2 tool (5 domains), visualized with the robvis tool.
- **Certainty of evidence**: GRADE approach with Summary of Findings tables at 12, 36, 60 months.
- **Sensitivity analysis**: Leave-one-out analysis on the primary outcome (MBL); publication bias not formally assessed (too few studies per outcome for funnel plots/Egger's test).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Study selection**: 757 records retrieved → 341 duplicates removed → 416 screened → 403 excluded → 13 full-text assessed → 5 excluded (1 non-RCT, 1 short implants, 2 fully edentulous, 1 single-unit) → **8 reports from 3 parallel-arm RCTs** included (Sweden: Göthberg 2014/2016/2018, Toia 2019/2022/2024; Spain: Maceiras 2023/2024).
- **Sample**: 136 patients enrolled, 126 analyzed at final follow-up; 341 implants total (195 AL, 146 IL); implant systems: Brånemark Mark III (external connection), Ticare Inhex and OsseoSpeed EV (internal conical hexagonal); all metal-ceramic screw-retained prostheses.
- **MBL (primary outcome)**: 12 months — no significant difference (implant-level MD -0.31mm, 95% CI -0.69 to 0.08, p=0.12; patient-level MD -0.08mm, p=0.24). 36 months — significant favoring AL at implant level (MD -0.13mm, 95% CI -0.23 to -0.03, p=0.01, I²=42.1%, moderate CoE); not significant at patient level (MD -0.07mm, p=0.35). 60 months — not significant (MD -0.40mm, 95% CI -1.05 to 0.26, p=0.24, I²=97.15% high heterogeneity).
- **PD (secondary)**: 12 months — trend not significant (implant-level MD -0.49mm, p=0.09). 36 months — significant favoring AL (MD -0.66mm, 95% CI -1.13 to -0.19, p=0.01, low CoE). 60 months — significant favoring AL (MD -0.60mm, 95% CI -0.91 to -0.29, p<0.001, low CoE).
- **BoP**: no significant differences at any time point (12mo implant-level MD -8.19%, 95% CI -16.96 to 0.59, p=0.07, trend toward IL benefit but NS).
- **Implant survival**: RD = 0.00 at all time points (12, 36, 60 months); no heterogeneity (I²=0%).
- **Mechanical complications**: RD -0.02 (36mo) and 0.00 (60mo), both NS, I²=0%.
- **Patient satisfaction**: MD 0.03 (95% CI -0.62 to 0.68, p=0.93) at 12 months — no difference.
- **Risk of bias**: all 8 reports rated low risk of bias across all 5 RoB2 domains.
- **GRADE certainty**: moderate for implant survival and mechanical complications; low for MBL, PD, BoP, and patient satisfaction (imprecision/inconsistency-driven downgrades).
- **Sensitivity**: leave-one-out analysis confirmed robustness of primary MBL estimates — all pooled results remained within original 95% CIs.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Evidence base limited to only 3 independent RCTs from 2 countries (Sweden, Spain), raising generalizability concerns.
- High heterogeneity (I² up to 97%) at 12 and 60-month MBL analyses limits confidence in pooled estimates at those time points.
- Publication bias could not be formally assessed (insufficient studies per outcome for funnel plot/Egger's test).
- All included implants were bone-level systems with external or internal conical hexagonal connections and metal-ceramic screw-retained prostheses only — findings may not generalize to tissue-level implants, other connection geometries (e.g., flat-to-flat), or all-ceramic/zirconia-framework prostheses.
- The authors note biologic parameters (PD, BoP) have not been shown to correlate directly with MBL regardless of follow-up duration, complicating mechanistic interpretation of the PD benefit.
- Authors call for further well-designed RCTs, particularly with longer follow-up and diverse implant/connection systems, to confirm findings and clarify the biological mechanism (coronal repositioning of the implant-abutment micro-gap away from the alveolar crest).

## 6. Related Work

- Cites the "one abutment, one time" concept (minimizing abutment disconnection to preserve biological width) as a plausible mechanism for the PD benefit.
- Discusses biomechanical rationale: AL abutments may act as a mechanical buffer redistributing occlusal loads before reaching the implant platform, versus IL connections exposing the implant-abutment interface directly to masticatory forces — consistent with in-vitro microgap/displacement findings in [[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/angermair-2024-microgap-abutment-displacement-implant-connection]].
- Notes differing torque protocols between AL (secondary screw, 15-20 Ncm) and IL (single long screw, 30-35 Ncm) restorations as a possible explanation for equivalent mechanical-complication rates despite theoretical differences in preload/clamping force.

## 7. Glossary

- **AL (Abutment-Level connection)**: Prosthetic workflow using an intermediate/transmucosal abutment screwed to the implant platform, onto which the prosthesis is subsequently retained by a shorter screw.
- **IL (Implant-Level connection)**: Prosthetic workflow where the prosthesis framework is retained directly to the implant platform without an intermediate abutment.
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss — radiographic measure of peri-implant crestal bone level change over time.
- **PD**: Probing depth — clinical measure of peri-implant sulcus/pocket depth.
- **BoP**: Bleeding on probing — clinical sign of peri-implant soft-tissue inflammation.
- **RoB 2**: Cochrane Risk of Bias 2.0 tool for RCTs.
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development and Evaluation — framework for rating certainty of evidence.
- **PROSPERO**: International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews.
- **"One abutment, one time"**: Clinical concept of placing a definitive abutment once and avoiding repeated dis/reconnection to preserve peri-implant biological width.
