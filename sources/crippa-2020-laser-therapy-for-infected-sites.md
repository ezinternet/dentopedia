2026-09-29 감염 발치와 즉시식립(급성·화농성 포함) 서베일런스의 일환으로 인제스트. 기존 [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/munoz-camara-2020-immediate-implants-acute-periapical-infected]] 및 [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]]에 없는 소독 수단(Er,Cr:YSGG 레이저)과 심미부위 단일 증례 5년 추적을 추가하는 저근거 보완 자료.

## Three-line Summary

Single case report (n=1, 40-year-old woman, maxillary left lateral incisor 2.2 with periapical periodontitis after failed apicectomy) plus a small non-systematic literature search (PubMed, 1980-2019; 4 clinical laser-plus-immediate-implant articles found).

The socket was decontaminated with an ErCr:YSGG 2780 nm laser (60-90 s), a 3.3 x 10 mm Straumann Roxolid SLA implant was placed at >=35 N with Bio-Oss and a membrane, loaded at 4 months; no implant loss, peri-implantitis or peri-implant bone loss was reported at 5 years (no numerical bone data given).

One uncontrolled case cannot show that laser decontamination changes the outcome of immediate placement in infected sockets; it is hypothesis-level illustration only, and the lesion is not classified as acute or chronic.

발치와를 ErCr:YSGG 2780 nm 레이저로 60-90초 소독한 뒤 Straumann Roxolid SLA 3.3 x 10 mm 임플란트를 35 N 이상으로 식립하고 Bio-Oss와 차단막을 적용, 4개월 후 부하; 5년째 임플란트 상실·임플란트 주위염·주위골 소실이 없었다고 기술 (골 수치 데이터는 없음).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Presents one esthetic-zone immediate implant placed into a socket with periapical periodontitis after Er,Cr:YSGG laser debridement/decontamination, with a stated 5-year follow-up.
- Gives explicit laser settings (hard-tissue mode 2.0 W, 15 Hz, 40% air, 60% water, 100 mL H2O/min, MZ-6 tip; 60-90 s per socket, no tip contact).
- Brief narrative literature search finding only four clinical laser-plus-immediate-implant articles; calls for an RCT.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: single case report + PICO-guided narrative literature search (PubMed/Medline, 1 Jan 1980 - 30 Jun 2019; keywords "immediate implant placement" AND "laser", "dental implants" AND "laser" AND "postextraction"; all article types in dental journals).
- **Case**: 40-year-old healthy woman, good oral hygiene; tooth 2.2 previously failed apicectomy; "clinical and radiological signs of an infection, particularly periapical periodontitis". Acute vs chronic status, purulence, fistula, socket wall integrity and probing data are not reported.
- **Protocol**: atraumatic extraction; full-thickness flap (crestal incision + vertical releases); laser debridement and decontamination; Straumann Roxolid SLA RN implant 3.3 x 10 mm, >=35 N, 1 mm below the most apical bone peak; Bio-Oss + GUIDOR matrix barrier (the abstract says "resorbable membrane"); primary closure with PTFE 3/0; amoxicillin 1 g x2/day for 6 days; chlorhexidine 0.20% twice daily for 15-20 days; Maryland bridge as provisional; loading at 4 months.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- No implant loss, peri-implantitis or peri-implant bone loss reported; primary stability >35 N.
- "Satisfactory esthetic outcomes" at a 2-year clinical check; radiographs scheduled at 1, 4, 8, 12 months in year 1; 5-year success asserted in the Discussion (figure content not in the retrieved text; no numeric bone level, PES or probing values).
- Cited literature: Kusek 10 cases (laser-disinfected immediate implants; claimed 3x faster healing); Montoya-Salazar 36 implants in chronic periapical lesions with laser protocol; Crippa et al. series of 94 implants, 6 months-4 years, success 94.6% (89/94); Waasdorp SR did not cover laser.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- n=1, no control/comparison, no comparison of laser vs conventional debridement; the laser contribution cannot be isolated from curettage, antibiotics, chlorhexidine, graft and membrane.
- Lesion not classified; no quantitative outcomes; retrieved text lacks figures/tables.
- Literature search is non-systematic, single database, no quality appraisal. Authors themselves state an RCT is needed.

## 7. Glossary

- **Er,Cr:YSGG laser**: erbium, chromium-doped yttrium-scandium-gallium-garnet laser, 2780 nm, used in hard/soft tissue modes.
- **Periapical periodontitis**: inflammatory lesion around the root apex; acuity not specified in this paper.
- **Apicectomy**: surgical root-end resection.
- **Maryland bridge**: resin-bonded provisional fixed prosthesis.
