## Why Ingested

Bone-density-specific implant design is a recurring question in the wiki's bone-quality and primary-stability pages. This Misch et al. 1998 report is the early bone-quality-based implant system (four density categories, compression-oriented thread), and its clinical survival figure is the one the wiki needs to see with its limits. Related wiki pages: [[overviews/bone-quality-implant-risk-modification-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Preliminary clinical report (Implant Dentistry, 1998) on a bone-quality-based implant system with four density-specific designs — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The authors report 364 consecutive implants placed in five centres with 98.9% overall surgical survival, and state that all bone densities may have similar initial survival rates.

This is a preliminary, multi-centre report without a comparator arm or a long follow-up in the abstract; the survival figure should not be treated as a long-term benchmark.

## 세줄요약

예비 임상 보고 (Implant Dentistry, 1998) — 4단계 골밀도에 맞춘 설계를 갖는 골질 기반 임플란트 시스템 — 초록만 수집 (전문 미확보).

저자들은 5개 임상 센터에서 연속 식립한 364개 임플란트의 수술 생존율이 전체 98.9%였으며, 모든 골밀도에서 초기 생존율이 비슷할 수 있다고 보고.

예비, 다기관 보고이며 초록에 비교군과 장기 추적이 없음; 생존율은 장기 기준치로 취급하지 말 것.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Density-categorized implant system with compression-oriented thread.
- Early multicentre survival report (364 implants, 98.9%).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

FEA and animal studies preceded the multicentre clinical placement series (five centres).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Surgical survival 98.9% overall (364 implants); authors report similar initial survival across bone densities.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Preliminary; no comparator or long-term follow-up in the abstract.
- Surgical survival is not the same as prosthetic success.
- Full text not retrieved.
