## Why Ingested
This experimental dog study evaluates a novel extraction socket model with thin buccal wall and the effect of Bio-Oss collagen for ridge preservation, comparing dimensional and histological outcomes to untreated sockets.

## Three-line Summary
Line 1: Experimental study in dogs using a novel extraction socket model (very thin buccal wall) comparing Bio-Oss collagen (BC) vs untreated control (CT).
Line 2: BC group showed greater alveolar ridge width preservation and different tissue composition vs control at 12 weeks.
Line 3: Bio-Oss collagen demonstrated ridge preservation effect even in challenging sockets with thin buccal bone wall.

## 세줄요약
줄1: 개 실험 연구 — 협측골 벽이 매우 얇은 새로운 발치와 모델을 이용해 Bio-Oss collagen (BC)과 미처치 대조군(CT) 비교.
줄2: 12주 후 BC군에서 치조제 폭 보존이 더 우수했으며, 조직학적 분석상 조성 차이를 보임.
줄3: Bio-Oss collagen은 얇은 협측골 벽을 가진 발치와에서도 치조제 보존 효과를 나타냈다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Journal of Dental Sciences 2021;16:831–839
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.jds.2021.03.005
- **Institution**: Wuhan University, Wuhan, People's Republic of China

## 2. Key Contributions
- Introduced novel extraction socket model with very thin buccal wall
- Evaluated Bio-Oss collagen for ridge preservation in this challenging model
- Reported CBCT and histological outcomes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Experimental animal study (dogs)
- **Model**: Novel extraction socket with thin buccal wall
- **Groups**: Bio-Oss collagen (BC) vs untreated control (CT)
- **Outcomes**: Alveolar ridge width (WD), CBCT volume/structure, histomorphometry (mineralized bone, bone marrow, other tissues)
- **Time point**: 12 weeks

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- BC group showed greater bone volume/ridge width preservation than CT on CBCT
- At 12 weeks, original buccal bone wall lost in both groups
- Histological differences in tissue proportions observed between groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Animal model; translation to humans requires caution
- Single time point (12 weeks)
- Novel socket model specificity
