## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised clinical trials (RCTs) comparing enamel bevelling with no bevel for composite restorations in non-carious cervical lesions (NCCLs) of adults with at least 1-year follow-up: 1356 records screened, 14 abstracts retained, 4 trials after full-text review, of which only 2 had low risk of bias.

The pooled risk difference for retention was 0.0 (95% CI -0.04 to 0.04; p = 0.91) and for marginal discolouration 0.05 (95% CI -0.02 to 0.13; p = 0.17), i.e. no superiority of bevelled restorations at the 1-year short-term follow-up.

There is not enough evidence to support bevelling over a non-bevelled technique in NCCLs, especially for longer periods; the conclusion rests on only two low-risk-of-bias RCTs, so better standardised and longer-term RCTs are needed.

## 세줄요약

비치아우식성 치경부 병소 (Non-Carious Cervical Lesion, NCCL) 복합레진 수복에서 법랑질 사면 형성 (Enamel Bevel) 대 비형성을 비교한 1년 이상 추적 무작위 임상시험 (Randomised Clinical Trial, RCT)의 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis): 1356건 검색, 초록 선별 후 14편, 전문 검토 후 4편, 이 중 비뚤림 위험 (Risk of Bias) 낮음은 2편뿐.

유지율 (Retention)의 통합 위험차 (Risk Difference)는 0.0 (95% CI -0.04~0.04; p = 0.91), 변연 변색 (Marginal Discolouration)은 0.05 (95% CI -0.02~0.13; p = 0.17)로, 1년 단기 추적에서 사면 형성군의 우월성은 관찰되지 않았다.

사면 형성이 비형성보다 낫다고 뒷받침할 근거가 부족하며 (특히 장기 추적), 결론이 비뚤림 위험 낮은 RCT 2편에 근거하므로 표준화된 장기 RCT가 더 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First systematic review and meta-analysis restricted to RCTs that isolate the enamel-bevel variable in NCCL composite restorations.
- Pooled retention risk difference of 0.0 (95% CI -0.04 to 0.04) shows no 1-year benefit of bevelling; marginal discolouration also not significantly different (0.05; 95% CI -0.02 to 0.13).
- Explicitly flags the evidence base as thin (2 low-risk-of-bias RCTs) and calls for standardised, longer-term trials.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs; Cochrane risk of bias tool.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, LILACS, BBO Library, Cochrane Library, SIGLE, IADR conference abstracts, trial registries, ProQuest Dissertations and Periodicos Capes Theses; no restrictions.
- **n**: 1356 records after duplicate removal; 14 after abstract screening; 4 after full-text examination; 2 with low risk of bias.
- **Outcomes**: Retention rate; marginal discolouration; at least 1-year follow-up in adult NCCLs.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Risk difference | 95% CI | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retention | 0.0 | -0.04 to 0.04 | 0.91 |

Abstract-only record: per-trial data, follow-up durations beyond the 1-year short-term and heterogeneity statistics were not available.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Conclusion based on only two low-risk-of-bias RCTs and short (1-year) follow-up.
- Authors call for better standardisation and reporting of enamel-bevelling RCTs with longer follow-up.
- Full text not retrieved; trial-level details unverified.

## 7. Glossary
- **NCCL**: non-carious cervical lesion; loss of cervical tooth structure without caries.
- **Enamel bevel**: oblique preparation of the enamel margin intended to enlarge the etched enamel bonding area.
- **Risk difference**: absolute difference in event proportions between groups; 0.0 means no difference.
- **Marginal discolouration**: staining at the restoration margin, a clinical criterion of marginal quality.
