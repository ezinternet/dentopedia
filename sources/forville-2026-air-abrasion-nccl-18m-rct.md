## Three-line Summary

A split-mouth RCT (n=63, 126 restorations, 18 months) evaluated airborne-particle abrasion (APA) with 50 μm aluminum oxide as dentin pretreatment before universal adhesive + bulk-fill composite in NCCLs. Retention at 18 months was 96.7% (control) vs 90.3% (APA); clustered Cox regression showed HR=3.03 for APA failure (95% CI 0.74–12.37), not statistically significant (p=0.122). APA did not improve marginal staining, marginal adaptation, postoperative sensitivity, or secondary caries outcomes.

## 세줄요약

에어본 입자 마모(APA, 50 μm 산화알루미늄)를 비우식성 치경부 병소(NCCL) 수복 전 상아질 전처치로 추가하는 효과를 18개월 분구법 RCT(n=63)로 평가했다. 유지율은 대조군 96.7% vs APA군 90.3%로 APA군에서 오히려 낮았으며, 군집보정 콕스 회귀분석에서 실패 위험비 HR=3.03이었으나 통계적으로 유의하지 않았다(p=0.122). APA는 변연착색·변연적합·술후과민·이차우식에서도 유의한 차이를 보이지 않았다.

## Why Ingested

First split-mouth RCT evaluating APA (air abrasion, 50 μm Al₂O₃) as dentin pretreatment in NCCLs. Clinically relevant because air abrasion was proposed to remove the sclerotic dentin layer and improve adhesion; result: no benefit over universal adhesive alone at 18 months. Part of an active research program by Loguercio/Reis group on NCCL adhesion strategies.

## Study Design

- **Design**: Prospective randomized split-mouth double-blind controlled trial
- **Participants**: 63 participants, 126 restorations (≥2 similar NCCLs per patient)
- **Follow-up**: Baseline, 6 months, 18 months
- **Evaluation criteria**: FDI criteria and modified USPHS
- **Primary outcome**: Retention/fracture

## Interventions

- **Control**: Universal adhesive (selective enamel etching) + bulk-fill composite resin
- **Test (APA)**: Same adhesive + airborne-particle abrasion with 50 μm aluminum oxide, 10 s at 5 bar
- **Isolation**: Rubber dam

## Results

| Outcome | Control | APA |
|---|---|---|
| Retention at 18m | 96.7% (92.4–100.0) | 90.3% (83.3–98.0) |
| Restorations lost | 1 | 5 |
| HR for failure (Cox) | ref | 3.03 (0.74–12.37), p=0.122 |

- No significant differences in marginal staining, marginal adaptation, postoperative sensitivity, or secondary caries

## Conclusion

Under the tested parameters, dentin APA did not improve clinical performance of composite restorations in NCCLs over 18 months. The APA group showed a numerically higher failure rate, though the difference was not statistically significant, likely due to limited power at this follow-up.

## Limitations

- Short follow-up (18 months); longer observation may reveal differences
- Likely underpowered to detect the observed HR (3.03) as significant
- Single APA protocol (50 μm Al₂O₃, 10 s, 5 bar); different parameters untested
