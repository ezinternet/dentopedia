## Why Ingested

Prospective clinical data on keratinized gingiva gain using full-thickness apically positioned flap at implant placement, stratified by whether ridge augmentation was performed. Extends the evidence base for [[implants/soft-tissue/bassetti-2016-soft-tissue-augmentation-second-stage-sr]] by providing 1st-stage fAPF-specific outcomes and directly addressing whether RA status modifies KG gain.

## Three-line Summary

Han 2021 prospectively followed 79 patients (203 implants) undergoing full-thickness apically positioned flap (fAPF) at implant placement, comparing KG gain between a one-stage group without ridge augmentation (Group A) and a two-stage group with ridge augmentation (Group B). Despite significantly lower baseline KG in Group B (0.82 mm vs 1.68 mm), multivariable GEE analysis found no significant difference in KG gain, shrinkage amount, or shrinkage ratio between groups. Total KG shrinkage ratio was 42.43%, and the study concludes fAPF is a reliable technique for KG augmentation regardless of ridge augmentation status.

KG 전체 수축률은 42.43%이며, RA 여부와 무관하게 fAPF는 KG 증대에 신뢰할 수 있는 술식이다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Prospective comparative study
- **Journal**: Clinical Implant Dentistry and Related Research 2021;23(6):920–926
- **Institution**: Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Section of Dentistry, Seoul Metropolitan Government–Seoul National University Boramae Medical Center, Seoul, South Korea
- **Study period**: April 2017 – April 2019
- **Sample**: 79 participants, 203 implants

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates fAPF at implant placement reliably gains KG regardless of concurrent or prior ridge augmentation
- Provides quantitative KG shrinkage data (42.43% total shrinkage ratio) over 6-month follow-up
- Group B baseline KG significantly lower (0.82 mm) than Group A (1.68 mm), making the equal-gain finding clinically meaningful — RA does not compromise fAPF effectiveness even when starting tissue width is reduced
- Multivariable GEE controls for patient- and implant-level clustering, strengthening internal validity

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Single-center prospective comparative study
- **Groups**:
  - Group A: one-stage surgical protocol, no ridge augmentation (n not broken out per abstract)
  - Group B: two-stage surgical protocol with ridge augmentation
- **Procedure**: Full-thickness apically positioned flap (fAPF) at time of implant placement
- **Measurement tool**: Paper ruler
- **Timepoints**: Baseline, 1 week, 3 weeks, 3 months, 6 months post-surgery
- **Statistical model**: Multivariable generalized estimating equation (GEE) — accounts for intra-patient clustering (multiple implants per patient)
- **Outcomes**: KG gain, KG shrinkage amount, KG shrinkage ratio

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Group A (no RA) | Group B (RA) | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline KG width | 1.68 mm | 0.82 mm | p < 0.001 |
| KG gain | 1.92 ± 1.67 mm | 1.48 ± 1.36 mm | p > 0.05 (NS) |
| Shrinkage amount | — | — | p > 0.05 (NS) |
| Shrinkage ratio | — | — | p > 0.05 (NS) |
| **Total shrinkage ratio** | **42.43%** | | |

- No significant difference in KG gain between groups despite meaningfully different baselines
- fAPF concluded reliable for KG augmentation regardless of RA status

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-center study limits generalizability
- Abstract does not report per-group shrinkage ratio breakdown
- No randomization — group allocation tied to clinical indication (RA vs no RA), introducing selection bias potential
- 6-month follow-up; longer-term stability of KG gain not assessed
- Blinding of measurements not described

## 7. Glossary

- **fAPF (Full-thickness Apically Positioned Flap)**: A surgical flap design where the full thickness of mucosa is reflected and repositioned apically to expose and maintain keratinized tissue coronally around the implant
- **KG (Keratinized Gingiva)**: Band of attached, non-mobile gingival tissue; adequate width is associated with improved peri-implant tissue stability
- **RA (Ridge Augmentation)**: Bone and/or soft tissue procedures to increase ridge dimensions prior to or concurrent with implant placement
- **GEE (Generalized Estimating Equation)**: Statistical model for correlated outcome data (e.g., multiple implants per patient); accounts for intra-cluster correlation
- **Shrinkage ratio**: Proportion of initial post-surgical KG gain lost over time due to tissue maturation and contraction
