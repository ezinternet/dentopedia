## Why Ingested

한국 대학병원(이화여자대학교 목동병원, 서울)의 1:4 짝지은 환자-대조군 연구로, 조기 실패가 아닌 후기 기계적 실패 (late mechanical failure: 고정체·나사·지대주 파절)의 위험인자로 교근 (masseter) 3차원 부피와 단면적을 제시한다. 조기 실패 문헌과 대비되는 후기 실패 축을 보강한다. → [[implants/survival/barboza-2026-bruxism-implant-failure-umbrella-review]]

## Three-line Summary

1:4 matched case-control study at Ewha Womans University Mokdong Hospital (Seoul, Korea) comparing 23 late mechanical failures (fixture, screw or abutment fracture) with 92 successful controls, matched for gender, age (+/-10 years), anatomical location and maintenance period (+/-2 years); masseter volume and cross-sectional area (CSA) were measured on 3D reconstructions of CBCT data.

In a multivariable generalized estimating equation model, larger masseter bulk was associated with mechanical failure (p < 0.001): odds rose 1.24-fold per 1000 mm3 of volume and 1.11-fold per 10 mm2 of CSA; AUCs were 0.846 (volume) and 0.871 (CSA), with Youden cut-offs of 31,272.56 mm3 and 691.30 mm2.

The authors call the cut-offs preliminary and exploratory, requiring prospective validation; this record is abstract-only, and nationality or ethnicity of participants is not stated (Korean setting only implied by the institution).

## 세줄요약

이화여자대학교 목동병원(서울, 한국)에서 후기 기계적 실패 (고정체·나사·지대주 파절) 23건과 성공 대조군 92건을 성별, 연령 (±10년), 해부학적 위치, 유지기간 (±2년)으로 1:4 짝지어 비교하고 CBCT 3차원 재구성으로 교근 부피와 단면적 (cross-sectional area, CSA)을 측정했다.

다변량 일반화추정방정식 (generalized estimating equation, GEE) 모델에서 교근이 클수록 기계적 실패와 유의하게 연관되었고 (p < 0.001), 부피 1000 mm3 증가당 오즈 1.24배, 단면적 10 mm2 증가당 1.11배였다; 곡선하면적 (AUC)은 부피 0.846, 단면적 0.871, Youden 절단값은 31,272.56 mm3와 691.30 mm2였다.

저자들은 절단값을 예비적·탐색적이라 하며 전향적 검증이 필요하다고 밝혔다; 초록 기반 기록이며 참여자의 국적·인종은 명시되지 않았고 한국이라는 점은 기관에서 유추된 것뿐이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Introduces 3D masseter morphology (volume, CSA) from CBCT as a patient-level risk marker for late mechanical failure (fixture, screw or abutment fracture) rather than biological or early failure.
- Gives preliminary cut-offs for risk stratification: 31,272.56 mm3 (volume; sensitivity 86.96%, specificity 77.17%) and 691.30 mm2 (CSA; sensitivity 82.61%, specificity 79.35%).
- Bootstrap-validated AUC 95% CIs: 0.751-0.931 (volume) and 0.767-0.951 (CSA).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: 1:4 matched case-control study; single Korean university hospital (Ewha Womans University Mokdong Hospital).
- **Sample**: 23 cases of late mechanical failure and 92 successful controls (115 participants/implants in total as described; the abstract does not state whether the unit is implants or patients).
- **Matching**: gender, age (+/-10 years), anatomical location, maintenance period (+/-2 years).
- **Exposure**: masseter volume and CSA from 3D reconstruction of CBCT data.
- **Statistics**: multivariable generalized estimating equation model adjusting for potential confounders; ROC analysis with maximum-Youden thresholds; internal bootstrap validation.
- **Failure definition**: late mechanical failure = fixture fracture, screw fracture or abutment fracture. The exact time window defining "late" is not given in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Increased masseter bulk significantly associated with mechanical failure (p < 0.001).
- Odds of failure: 1.24-fold per 1000 mm3 volume; 1.11-fold per 10 mm2 CSA (confidence intervals not given in the abstract).
- AUC 0.846 (volume), 0.871 (CSA).
- Youden thresholds: 31,272.56 mm3 and 691.30 mm2; sensitivity 86.96% and 82.61%; specificity 77.17% and 79.35%.
- Early-vs-late timing: only late mechanical failure is studied; no early-failure rate or overall implant failure rate is reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only: adjusted covariates, the definition of "late", implant systems, and unit of analysis (patients versus implants) are not verifiable.
- Small case group (23); thresholds derived and validated internally (bootstrap) in the same sample, so likely optimistic.
- Authors state findings are exploratory and need prospective validation in independent cohorts before routine clinical use.
- Nationality/ethnicity not stated; generalization beyond this Korean single center is unknown.

## 7. Glossary

- 교근 (masseter muscle): main jaw-closing muscle; bulk reflects bite-force capacity.
- 단면적 (cross-sectional area, CSA): muscle area on an axial CBCT slice.
- 후기 기계적 실패 (late mechanical failure): fixture, screw or abutment fracture after a period of function.
- 일반화추정방정식 (generalized estimating equation, GEE): regression handling correlated observations.
- 곡선하면적 (area under the curve, AUC): ROC discrimination measure.
