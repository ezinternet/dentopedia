## Why Ingested

This RCT provides direct clinical evidence that 0.2% chlorhexidine and 1% povidone-iodine mouthwashes significantly reduce SARS-CoV-2 salivary viral load within 5 minutes of rinsing, directly supporting pre-procedural mouthwash protocols in dental infection control. It is the primary individual-trial evidence base that higher-level reviews such as [[infection-control/ebrahimi-2023-mouthwash-sars-cov2-viral-load-sr-ma]] synthesize and, for CHX, challenge.

## Three-line Summary

This was a parallel-group, blinded, randomized, placebo-controlled trial evaluating the efficacy of 0.2% chlorhexidine and 1% povidone-iodine mouthwashes against salivary SARS-CoV-2 in 61 COVID-19 positive patients (mean age 45.3 ± 16.7 years) conducted between June and September 2020 at Rafik Hariri University Hospital, Lebanon. Saliva was collected before and 5 minutes after a 30-second mouthwash/gargle, and viral load was measured via rRT-PCR by assessing change in cycle threshold (delta Ct).

Both 0.2% chlorhexidine and 1% povidone-iodine significantly reduced salivary SARS-CoV-2 viral load compared to distilled water (P = .0024 and P = .012, respectively). Mean Ct increases were 5.69 for chlorhexidine and 4.45 for povidone-iodine (both P < .0001 for paired before-after comparisons), with no significant difference between the two active solutions (P = .24).

Preprocedural rinsing with 0.2% chlorhexidine or 1% povidone-iodine appears effective as a preventive strategy to reduce SARS-CoV-2 transmission during dental procedures, though further studies are needed to determine the duration of their antiviral effect.

## 세줄요약

본 연구는 COVID-19 양성 환자 61명(평균 연령 45.3 ± 16.7세)을 대상으로 0.2% 클로르헥시딘과 1% 포비돈-아이오딘 구강 세정액의 타액 내 SARS-CoV-2 불활성화 효과를 평가한 평행군, 이중맹검, 무작위배정, 위약대조 임상시험으로, 2020년 6월부터 9월까지 레바논 라피크 하리리 대학병원에서 수행되었다. 시술 전후(세정 30초, 5분 후) 타액을 채취하여 rRT-PCR로 주기 역치(CT) 변화를 측정하였다.

0.2% 클로르헥시딘과 1% 포비돈-아이오딘 모두 증류수 대비 타액 내 SARS-CoV-2 바이러스 농도를 유의하게 감소시켰으며(P = .0024 및 P = .012), 평균 Ct 증가값은 각각 5.69와 4.45로 두 군 간 유의한 차이는 없었다(P = .24).

치과 시술 전 0.2% 클로르헥시딘 또는 1% 포비돈-아이오딘 구강 세정은 COVID-19 전파 위험을 줄이는 예방 전략으로 효과적이나, 항바이러스 효과의 지속 시간에 대한 추가 연구가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First large-scale controlled clinical trial evaluating 0.2% chlorhexidine and 1% povidone-iodine against salivary SARS-CoV-2 in vivo.
- Both mouthwashes significantly increased Ct values (reduced viral load) compared to distilled water placebo within 5 minutes post-rinse.
- Chlorhexidine 0.2% showed a mean Ct increase of 5.69 (P < .0001); povidone-iodine 1% showed a mean Ct increase of 4.45 (P < .0001).
- No significant difference in efficacy was found between the two active solutions (P = .24).
- SARS-CoV-2 was consistently detected in saliva (61/66 patients), supporting saliva as a valid diagnostic specimen.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Parallel-group, blinded (patients, intervention supervisor, lab technicians, outcome assessor), randomized, placebo-controlled clinical trial.
- Randomization: Simple random sampling using Excel software; allocation concealment via SNOSE technique.
- Blinding: Groups labelled A (distilled water), B (0.2% chlorhexidine), C (1% povidone-iodine); codes revealed only at study end.
- Sample size: 77 eligible, 16 excluded, 61 compliant participants analyzed (distilled water n=9, chlorhexidine n=25, povidone-iodine n=27). Unequal group sizes due to ethical preference for more subjects in active test groups in "very ill" COVID-19 patients.
- Participants: Laboratory-confirmed COVID-19 positive via nasopharyngeal swab, diagnosis-to-inclusion 0–2 days. Excluded: intubation/mechanical ventilation or declined consent.
- Intervention: 15 mL of solution, 30-second mouthwash/gargle, saliva collected pre- and 5-minutes post-rinse.
- Outcome: Primary outcome was change in cycle threshold (delta Ct) of salivary SARS-CoV-2 (RdRp gene) normalized to human RNase P internal control.
- Statistics: Kruskal-Wallis test with Dunn's post-hoc for between-group delta Ct comparisons; paired t-test for pre-post within-group comparisons. Normality assessed via Kolmogorov-Smirnov test. Significance set at P < .05. Software: GraphPad Prism 5.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome (delta Ct) —** Chlorhexidine 0.2%: 6.37 ± 1.08 vs distilled water: 0.519 ± 0.519 (P = .0024); Povidone-iodine 1%: 4.72 ± 0.89 vs distilled water (P = .012). No significant difference between chlorhexidine and povidone-iodine (P = .24).
- **Pre-post Ct change —** Chlorhexidine group: mean Ct increased from 27.69 ± 7.16 to 33.9 ± 7.08 (mean difference 5.69, P < .0001). Povidone-iodine group: mean Ct increased from 29.88 ± 6.2 to 34.36 ± 6.3 (mean difference 4.45, P < .0001). Control (distilled water): no significant difference (P = .566).
- **Patient characteristics —** 61 participants (36 female, 25 male), mean age 45.3 ± 16.7 years (range 17–85).
- **RNase P normalization —** Mean Ct of human RNase P pre-wash: 25.41 ± 2.5; post-wash: 26 ± 2.72 (P = .332, no significant difference).
- **Adverse events —** None reported.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Short follow-up period: only 5 minutes post-rinse; duration of antiviral effect not assessed.
- Unequal group sizes: control group was smaller (n=9) than active groups (n=25, n=27), potentially reducing statistical power for between-group comparisons.
- Self-collected saliva samples may introduce variability in collection technique, though this minimized staff exposure risk.
- PCR detects viral RNA, not necessarily infectious virus; correlation with infectivity reduction not directly established.
- Single-center study in hospitalized patients; results may not generalize to outpatient or asymptomatic populations.
- No assessment of clinical outcomes (e.g., infection rates in dental staff) — only surrogate virological endpoint.
- Authors note further studies are required to evaluate the length of effectiveness over time for accurate prescription against SARS-CoV-2.

## 6. Related Work

- To et al. 2020 — Consistent detection of SARS-CoV-2 in saliva, supporting its use as a diagnostic specimen.
- Yoon et al. 2020 — Chlorhexidine mouthwash was effective in reducing SARS-CoV-2 viral load in saliva in a small clinical trial (2 patients).
- Martinez Lamas et al. 2020 — First in vivo test showing povidone-iodine mouthwash reduced salivary SARS-CoV-2 viral load (4 patients).
- Meister et al. 2020 — In vitro study showing various oral rinses including chlorhexidine inactivated SARS-CoV-2 strains on Vero E6 cells.
- Bidra et al. 2020 — In vitro demonstration of rapid virucidal activity of povidone-iodine oral rinse against SARS-CoV-2 at 0.5%–1.5% concentration and 15-second contact time.

## 7. Glossary

- **Ct (Cycle Threshold)**: 주기 역치. 실시간 PCR에서 형광 신호가 검출 가능한 수준을 넘는 증폭 사이클 수로, Ct값이 낮을수록 초기 바이러스 농도가 높음을 의미 (inversely related to viral load).
- **SARS-CoV-2 (Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus 2)**: 중증급성호흡기증후군 코로나바이러스 2. COVID-19의 원인 병원체.
- **rRT-PCR (Real-Time Reverse Transcriptase Polymerase Chain Reaction)**: 실시간 역전사 중합효소연쇄반응. RNA 바이러스를 검출 및 정량화하는 분자진단 기법.
- **RNase P**: 리보핵산분해효소 P. 인간 세포에서 발현되는 내인성 대조 유전자로, 검체의 품질과 RNA 추출 및 PCR 증폭 효율을 정규화하는 데 사용됨.
- **Delta Ct (ΔCt)**: 세정 후 정규화된 Ct값에서 세정 전 정규화된 Ct값을 뺀 값. 양수일 경우 바이러스 부하 감소를 의미.
