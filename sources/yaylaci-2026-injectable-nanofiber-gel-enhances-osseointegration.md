## Why Ingested

Preclinical test of a flowable osteotomy-applied bioactive interface as an alternative to pre-applied implant coatings (which the authors say can delaminate at high insertion torque), benchmarked against the SLA surface; complements the coating literature in [[implants/surface/beckwith-2024-implant-coatings-affect-healing]] and [[implants/surface/damerau-2021-inorganic-surface-coatings-tcp-ha-periimplant]].

## Three-line Summary

Rabbit implant study: custom titanium implants placed with an injectable self-assembling peptide-amphiphile nanofiber gel (designed to be osteoinductive, adhesive and antimicrobial) applied in the osteotomy vs a standard SLA surface; evaluated at 3 and 5 wk by micro-CT (BIC, BV/TV) and reverse torque (group sizes not stated in the abstract).

At 5 wk the nanofiber group had higher BIC (58.7% vs 50.5%, P < 0.001), BV/TV (51.8% vs 42.6%, P < 0.001) and reverse torque (53.2 vs 41.8 N·cm, P < 0.01) than SLA.

Single preclinical rabbit study, abstract-level only; the claims that the gel prevents or repairs drilling-induced microdamage and fills microcracks are the authors' design intent, not measured outcomes. Not evidence of clinical benefit in humans.

## 세줄요약

토끼 임플란트 연구: 자가조립 펩타이드 양친매성 (Peptide Amphiphile) 나노섬유 주입형 젤 (골유도·접착·항균 목적 설계)을 골절개부에 적용한 맞춤 티타늄 임플란트 vs 표준 SLA (Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched) 표면. 3주·5주에 미세CT (micro-CT)로 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC)과 골체적/전체체적 (BV/TV), 역회전력 (Reverse Torque) 평가 (군별 n은 초록에 없음).

5주에 나노섬유군이 SLA보다 BIC (58.7% vs 50.5%, P < 0.001), BV/TV (51.8% vs 42.6%, P < 0.001), 역회전력 (53.2 vs 41.8 N·cm, P < 0.01) 모두 높았다.

단일 전임상 토끼 연구이며 초록 수준이다. 드릴링 유발 미세손상 예방·복구와 미세균열 충전은 저자의 설계 의도이지 측정된 결과가 아니다. 사람에서의 임상적 이득 근거는 아니다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Proposes an injectable, self-assembling peptide-amphiphile nanofiber interface applied in the osteotomy rather than a pre-applied implant coating.
- Reports higher 5-wk BIC, BV/TV and reverse torque than SLA in rabbits.
- Authors state the gel is designed to prevent or repair drilling-induced microdamage and occupy peri-implant microcracks; this is design intent, not a measured outcome in the abstract.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Rabbit model, custom titanium implants, nanofiber interface vs SLA surface
- **Time points**: 3 and 5 wk
- **n**: not stated in the abstract
- **Outcomes**: micro-CT BIC and BV/TV; biomechanical reverse torque

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome (5 wk) | Nanofiber | SLA | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| BIC | 58.7% | 50.5% | < 0.001 |
| BV/TV | 51.8% | 42.6% | < 0.001 |
| Reverse torque | 53.2 N·cm | 41.8 N·cm | < 0.01 |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only ingest; sample sizes, 3-wk values, gel composition details and histology not captured.
- Microdamage prevention/repair and microcrack filling were not reported as measured outcomes in the abstract.
- Single rabbit study; no human data. Whether the nanofiber group also differed in surface (SLA control vs gel-treated custom implant) cannot be judged from the abstract.

## 7. Glossary
- **Peptide amphiphile**: self-assembling molecule forming nanofibers, here an injectable gel.
- **BIC**: bone-to-implant contact. **BV/TV**: bone volume/total volume.
- **Reverse torque**: removal torque as a biomechanical measure of osseointegration.
