## Why Ingested

Surveillance hit on lateral-window sinus augmentation new bone formation — the only wiki-held paper that treats sinus *anatomy* (sinus width, inner contour length) as an effect-modifier of histomorphometric new bone formation, and shows the modifier differs by xenograft source. It extends the residual-height-only framing in [[sinus-lift/lateral/khijmatgar-2023-residual-bone-height-new-bone-sinus-nma]] to two new anatomical variables and complements the material-comparison histology of [[sinus-lift/lateral/grasso-2020-equine-vs-bovine-bone-sinus-augmentation-rct]].

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (lateral-window sinus augmentation, 23 grafted sinuses) comparing deproteinized porcine bone mineral (DPBM, n = 10) versus deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM, n = 13), testing whether sinus width (SW), inner maxillary sinus contour length (IMSCL) and residual ridge height (RRH) predict histomorphometric new bone formation (%NBF).
%NBF correlated negatively with SW (DPBM r = −0.660, p = 0.038; DBBM r = −0.614, p = 0.026) and IMSCL (DPBM r = −0.737, p = 0.015; DBBM r = −0.573, p = 0.041), but not RRH; multivariate R² was 0.591 for DPBM versus 0.314 for DBBM.
Anatomy drives new bone formation in lateral sinus augmentation, but the effect is material-dependent — anatomical variables explain far more of the %NBF variance with porcine than bovine xenograft.

## 세줄요약

후향적 연구 (측방창 상악동 거상술, 이식 23개 상악동)로 탈단백 돼지골 무기질 (Deproteinized Porcine Bone Mineral, DPBM; n = 10)과 탈단백 소골 무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM; n = 13)을 비교하며 상악동 폭 (Sinus Width, SW), 상악동 내측 윤곽 길이 (Inner Maxillary Sinus Contour Length, IMSCL), 잔존 치조골 높이 (Residual Ridge Height, RRH)가 조직형태계측 신생골 형성률 (%New Bone Formation, %NBF)을 예측하는지 검정.
%NBF는 SW (DPBM r = −0.660, p = 0.038; DBBM r = −0.614, p = 0.026) 및 IMSCL (DPBM r = −0.737, p = 0.015; DBBM r = −0.573, p = 0.041)과 음의 상관을 보였으나 RRH와는 유의한 상관이 없었고, 다변수 R²는 DPBM 0.591, DBBM 0.314였음.
상악동 해부학적 구조는 측방 상악동 거상술에서 신생골 형성에 영향을 주지만 그 정도는 이식재에 따라 달라, 돼지 유래 이식재에서 해부학적 변수가 설명하는 분산이 소 유래보다 훨씬 큼.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First analysis in the wiki to isolate SW and IMSCL (not just residual height) as predictors of %NBF in lateral-window augmentation.
- Shows the anatomy→new-bone relationship is stronger with porcine than bovine xenograft (multivariate R² 0.591 vs 0.314).
- Separates %NBF from RRH — RRH was not a significant predictor in either graft group, contrasting with the RBH-conditioned ranking framing of other syntheses.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Retrospective, radiographic assessment of anatomical variables against histomorphometrically evaluated %NBF.
- Two lateral-window groups: DPBM (n = 10) and DBBM (n = 13); no group differences in patient epidemiology, anatomical variables, or surgical/patient risk factors, enabling comparison.
- Linear regression (correlations, R²) and multivariate regression, computed separately per xenograft material.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Predictor | DPBM (n = 10) | DBBM (n = 13) |
|---|---|---|
| SW vs %NBF | r = −0.660, p = 0.038 (R² 0.435) | r = −0.614, p = 0.026 (R² 0.377) |
| IMSCL vs %NBF | r = −0.737, p = 0.015 (R² 0.543) | r = −0.573, p = 0.041 (R² 0.328) |
| RRH vs %NBF | not significant (R² 0.258) | not significant (R² 0.053) |
| Multivariate R² | 0.591 (59.1%) | 0.314 (31.4%) |

- All %NBF correlations with SW and IMSCL were negative (wider/longer sinus → less new bone).
- Anatomical influences were "evidently higher" when porcine graft material was applied.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only artifact** — paywalled; no full text, so regression tables, %NBF absolute values, healing time and follow-up details could not be verified.

- Confounding by unmeasured technique/healing-time variables cannot be excluded.

## 7. Glossary

- **SW (Sinus Width)** — 상악동 폭; bucco-palatal width of the sinus cavity.
- **IMSCL (Inner Maxillary Sinus Contour Length)** — 상악동 내측 윤곽 길이; length of the inner sinus wall contour.
- **RRH (Residual Ridge Height)** — 잔존 치조골 높이; subantral bone height available for implant anchorage.
- **DPBM / DBBM** — 탈단백 돼지골 / 소골 무기질; xenograft bone substitutes of porcine / bovine origin.
