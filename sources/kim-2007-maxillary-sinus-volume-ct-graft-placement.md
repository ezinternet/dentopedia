## Why Ingested

Preoperative CT volumetric analysis of the maxillary sinus is underrepresented in the wiki's lateral sinus-lift pages, which focus on outcomes but rarely on graft material *quantity* planning. This 2007 Korean case-control study provides numeric benchmarks (volume at 5/10/15 mm augmentation heights for edentulous vs dentulous patients) that are directly relevant to [[sinus-lift/lateral/mordenfeld-2010-msfa-dpbb-biopsies-11year]] and the graft-material decisions discussed in [[bone-regeneration/zhao-2021-bone-grafts-substitutes-dentistry-review]].

## Three-line Summary

Line 1: Case-control CT volumetric study (n=19 patients, 20 sinuses) comparing graft volume requirements at 5, 10, and 15 mm augmentation heights between edentulous and dentulous patients at a Korean university hospital.
Line 2: Edentulous sinuses required significantly more graft material at all heights: 0.56 vs 0.41 cm³ (5 mm, p=0.027), 2.35 vs 1.76 cm³ (10 mm, p=0.018), and 4.85 vs 3.80 cm³ (15 mm, p=0.044).
Line 3: Preoperative CT volumetric analysis enables rational graft material estimation and donor-site selection, particularly for autogenous bone where harvest volume is limited.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 한국 대학병원의 환자-대조군 CT 부피 연구(n=19명, 20개 상악동)로 무치악 vs 유치악 환자에서 5, 10, 15 mm 거상 높이별 필요 이식재 부피를 비교했습니다.
줄2: 무치악군이 모든 높이에서 유의하게 더 많은 이식재가 필요: 5mm(0.56 vs 0.41cm³, p=0.027), 10mm(2.35 vs 1.76cm³, p=0.018), 15mm(4.85 vs 3.80cm³, p=0.044).
줄3: 술 전 CT 부피 분석으로 이식재 양과 자가골 공여부를 합리적으로 계획할 수 있습니다.

- **Institution**: Hallym University, Korea
- **DOI**: None (Korean regional journal, pre-DOI indexing)
- **Study Design**: Case-control study
- **Sample**: 19 patients (20 sinuses) — 8 edentulous, 11 dentulous; all male, 60s age group
- **Follow-up**: N/A (cross-sectional measurement only)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides CT-based reference volumes for sinus graft material at three clinically relevant augmentation heights (5, 10, 15 mm)
- Demonstrates that edentulous sinuses require significantly more graft volume than dentulous sinuses at each height
- Addresses the gap in preoperative graft quantity estimation, especially for autogenous bone harvest planning
- Reports resorption rates for common graft materials: autograft iliac bone ~50%, mandibular bone less; deproteinized bovine bone (xenograft) ~20%

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- CT imaging of 19 patients scheduled for lateral maxillary sinus floor elevation
- Automated CT volume analyzer (IDL 6.0 software) measured sinus volume from sinus floor to 5, 10, and 15 mm above floor
- Compared edentulous (n=8 sinuses) vs dentulous (n=12 sinuses) patients
- Statistical analysis: Mann-Whitney U test (non-parametric, appropriate for small sample)
- Assumed linear graft surface cross-section; did not model actual curved clinical contour

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Augmentation Height | Edentulous (cm³) | Dentulous (cm³) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 mm | 0.56 | 0.41 | 0.027 |
| 10 mm | 2.35 | 1.76 | 0.018 |
| 15 mm | 4.85 | 3.80 | 0.044 |

- All between-group differences were statistically significant (p < 0.05)
- Graft resorption rates reported: iliac autograft ~50%, mandibular autograft lower, bovine xenograft (deproteinized) ~20%
- Larger sinus volumes in edentulous patients attributed to post-extraction sinus pneumatization

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Very small sample size (8 edentulous, 11 dentulous), all male patients in their 60s
- Limited generalizability across sex, age group, and ethnicity
- Assumed linear graft surface, not actual clinically placed contour
- No accounting for graft resorption in the volume calculation protocol
- Single-institution, single-ethnic cohort (Korean); may not represent other populations

## 7. Glossary

- CT (Computed Tomography, 컴퓨터 단층 촬영): cross-sectional imaging used for volumetric sinus measurement
- Autograft (자가골): bone from the patient's own body (iliac crest, mandible)
- Xenograft (이종골): bone substitute from animal source (deproteinized bovine bone)
- Resorption rate (흡수율): percentage of graft volume lost over time
- Repneumatization (재함기화): re-expansion of sinus air space after tooth loss
- IDL 6.0: Interactive Data Language — software used for CT volume analysis
