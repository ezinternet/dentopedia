## Why Ingested

기존 [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/hamilton-2023-type1a-immediate-implant-immediate-loading-esthetic-sr-ma]]와 [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chen-2014-esthetic-outcomes-immediate-early-implant]]는 심미부위 즉시식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP)의 위험·성공요인을 다루지만, 대조군은 주로 지연식립 (Delayed Implant Placement, DIP)이거나 self-referential(IIP 내부 selection criteria)이다. 본 SR+MA (Asghar 2023)는 RCT만 6편을 모아 IIP를 조기식립 (Early Implant Placement, EIP)과 직접 대조한다는 점에서 다르다 — 수직 골레벨과 치은열구탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD)는 IIP·EIP 간 유의차가 없었으나(각각 MD 0.10·-0.00, 둘 다 P>0.05), 핑크심미점수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES)는 EIP가 IIP보다 유의하게 우수했다(P<0.05). 이는 [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/chen-2014-esthetic-outcomes-immediate-early-implant]]가 보고한 즉시식립군의 협측 변연 점막 퇴축 경향을 RCT 근거로 재확인하면서, "골·치주 지표는 동등하지만 연조직 심미는 조기식립이 근소 우위"라는 더 정밀한 결론으로 nuance를 더한다.

같은 배치로 들어오는 [[immediate-implant/mello-2017-immediate-fresh-extraction-vs-delayed-healed-socket-sr-ma]], [[immediate-implant/garcia-sanchez-2022-immediate-vs-delayed-implant-placement-sr-ma]], [[immediate-implant/pommer-2021-maxillary-single-tooth-timing-protocols-sr-ma]]는 대부분 즉시 vs 지연(delayed) 비교에 집중하는 반면, 본 논문은 즉시 vs "조기(early)"라는 세 번째 타이밍 축을 별도로 격리해 비교한다는 점에서 이 배치를 상호보완한다.

**주의**: 이 논문은 Evidence-Based Dentistry(Springer Nature)의 압축형 근거요약(condensed evidence-summary) 포맷 논문이다 — PubMed 상 자체로 Systematic Review + Meta-Analysis로 분류되지만, 페이지 수가 짧고 포함 RCT가 6편에 불과해 결론은 지시적(indicative)으로만 취급해야 하며, 더 방대한 primary SR이 필요하다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 6 randomised controlled trials comparing immediate implant placement (IIP) versus early implant placement (EIP) protocols in healthy adults requiring single-tooth replacement in the aesthetic zone, with Cochrane ROB-2 risk-of-bias assessment.

No statistically significant difference between IIP and EIP in vertical bone levels (4 studies, 148 patients, MD 0.10, 95% CI -0.29 to 0.09/1.32, P>0.05) or probing depth (2 studies, 100 patients, MD -0.00, 95% CI -0.23 to 0.23, P>0.05); Pink Aesthetic Score (PES) was significantly better for EIP versus IIP (P<0.05); implant failure ranged 0-9.3% across the six studies.

IIP shows clinically comparable bone-level and periodontal outcomes to EIP but a measurable esthetic disadvantage in PES, and this is a condensed 6-RCT evidence-summary-journal analysis rather than a full-length primary SR, so treat conclusions as indicative pending longer-follow-up confirmatory trials.

## 세줄요약

건강한 성인의 심미부위 단일치 수복에서 즉시식립 (Immediate Implant Placement, IIP)과 조기식립 (Early Implant Placement, EIP)을 비교한 무작위대조시험(Randomised Controlled Trial, RCT) 6편의 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (Systematic Review + Meta-Analysis, SR+MA), Cochrane ROB-2 도구로 비뚤림 위험 평가.

수직 골레벨(4개 연구, 148명, MD 0.10, P>0.05)과 치은열구탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD)(2개 연구, 100명, MD -0.00, P>0.05)는 IIP·EIP 간 유의차 없음; 핑크심미점수 (Pink Aesthetic Score, PES)는 EIP가 IIP보다 유의하게 우수(P<0.05); 6개 연구 전반 임플란트 실패율 0–9.3%.

골·치주 지표는 IIP와 EIP가 동등하지만 연조직 심미(PES)는 EIP가 근소 우위 — 단 6편 RCT만 포함된 압축형 근거요약(condensed evidence-summary) 저널 논문이므로 결론은 지시적 수준으로만 해석하고 장기추적 확증 RCT가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Isolates a comparison axis (IIP vs EIP) that is frequently conflated with IIP vs delayed placement in the broader immediate-implant literature.
- Applies Cochrane ROB-2 to the 6 included RCTs, giving a formal risk-of-bias layer that many narrative comparisons of timing protocols lack.
- Quantifies the esthetic tradeoff (PES) separately from hard/soft tissue clinical parameters (vertical bone level, probing depth), showing these do not move together.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis restricted to randomised controlled trials only (a stricter inclusion filter than most SRs in this domain, which often mix RCTs with retrospective/prospective cohorts).
- **Databases searched**: MEDLINE (via OVID), EMBASE (via OVID), ISI Web of Science core collection, Cochrane, SCOPUS, Google Scholar.
- **Population**: Healthy adult patients requiring single-tooth replacement in the aesthetic zone.
- **Comparison**: Immediate implant placement (IIP) protocol vs early implant placement (EIP) protocol.
- **Risk-of-bias tool**: Cochrane Risk of Bias 2 (ROB-2).
- **Outcomes pooled**: vertical bone levels (4 studies), probing depth (2 studies), pink aesthetic score (PES), implant failure rate (narratively reported per study, not pooled).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **6 RCTs** included in total.
- **Implant failure**: 3.84%, 9.3%, and 4.45% in three of the studies; zero failures reported in the remaining studies.
- **Vertical bone levels** (4 studies, 148 patients): mean difference (MD) 0.10 [95% CI reported as -0.29 to 0.09, 1.32 in the abstract — likely a truncated/atypical CI notation in the source]; P > 0.05 (not significant).
- **Probing depth** (2 studies, 100 patients): MD -0.00 [95% CI -0.23 to 0.23]; P > 0.05 (not significant).
- **Pink Aesthetic Score (PES)**: significantly improved in EIP compared to IIP, P < 0.05.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 6 RCTs included; meta-analyses of vertical bone level and probing depth pool as few as 2–4 studies (100–148 patients), so pooled estimates carry wide uncertainty.
- Published as a compact "Evidence-Based Dentistry" evidence-summary article (Springer Nature) rather than a full-length primary systematic review — page count and methodological detail are limited relative to a standard SR+MA.
- The 95% CI reported for vertical bone level in the abstract ("-0.29 to 0.09, 1.32") is not a standard CI format, suggesting either a typographical/transcription issue in the published abstract or an unclear reporting of a secondary statistic; the primary full-text (not retrieved here) would be needed to resolve this.
- Implant failure rates are reported narratively per study rather than pooled, so no meta-analytic failure-rate effect size is available.
- No long-term (>a few years) follow-up data are synthesized; authors explicitly call for future research with long-term follow-up.
- Retrieved as abstract-only (no PMC full text); Methods/Results detail beyond the structured abstract (e.g., individual study characteristics, full ROB-2 domain breakdown) could not be verified.

## 7. Glossary

- **Immediate Implant Placement (IIP)**: implant placement at the time of tooth extraction, into the fresh extraction socket.
- **Early Implant Placement (EIP)**: implant placement after a period of soft-tissue (and partial bone) healing following extraction, typically several weeks to a few months post-extraction, but before full bone healing (which would be classified as delayed placement).
- **Pink Aesthetic Score (PES)**: a validated clinical index scoring peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics (e.g., mesial/distal papilla, soft-tissue level, contour, color, texture) around an implant restoration.
- **Probing Depth (PD)**: the depth of the peri-implant/periodontal sulcus measured with a periodontal probe, used as a proxy for soft-tissue/peri-implant health.
- **Cochrane Risk of Bias tool (ROB-2)**: the Cochrane Collaboration's standardized instrument for assessing risk of bias in randomised controlled trials across defined domains (randomization process, deviations from intended interventions, missing outcome data, measurement of the outcome, selection of the reported result).
- **Mean Difference (MD)**: a meta-analytic pooled effect-size statistic expressing the average difference in a continuous outcome between two groups across combined studies.
