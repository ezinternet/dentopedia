## Why Ingested
SDA 개념을 임플란트 영역으로 확장한 직접 근거. M1만 임플란트로 수복하는 전략(SDA 개념 적용)이 M1+M2 동시 수복 대비 비용·합병증 면에서 유리한지 검토 → 기존 [[removable-partial-denture/mclister-2018-prosthodontic-interventions-survival-shortened]] 및 [[removable-partial-denture/walter-2021-shortened-dental-arch-15year-rct]]의 임플란트 확장 근거로 활용.

## One-line Summary
Retrospective study (n=247 patients, 283 regions) comparing M1-only vs M1+M2 implant restoration in patients missing both first and second molars: equivalent implant survival (HR 1.66, p=0.567), but M1-only had 1.7x lower cost, 0% vs 16.2% peri-implantitis, and protective against MBL >0.5 mm (Clin Oral Implants Res 2025).

## 한줄요약
제1·2대구치 결손 환자에서 제1대구치만 임플란트 수복(SDA 개념) vs 양측 동시 수복 비교: 임플란트 생존율 동등(HR 1.66, p=0.567), M1만 수복군은 비용 1.7배 절감, 치주염 (Peri-implantitis, PI) 0% vs 16.2% (p=0.043), 골흡수 0.5 mm 초과 위험도 보호인자 (Clin Oral Implants Res 2025).

## 2. Key Contributions
- M1-only implant strategy (SDA concept) produces equivalent implant survival to full-arch M1+M2 restoration (HR 1.66, 95% CI 0.29–9.50, p=0.567)
- M1-only group: 1.7x lower cumulative treatment costs (β=−14.40, p<0.001)
- Peri-implantitis (Peri-implantitis, PI) incidence: 0% (M1-only) vs 16.2% (M1+M2) over 3 years (p=0.043)
- M1-only was a protective factor against marginal bone loss (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) >0.5 mm (β=0.34, p=0.012)
- Patient-reported outcomes (Patient-Reported Outcome Measures, PROMs): no significant difference in chewing ability for soft or hard foods; M1-only had lower post-surgery pain

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Retrospective cohort study
- **Population:** 247 patients, 283 partially edentulous posterior regions; both M1 and M2 missing
- **Group 1:** M1+M2 simultaneous implant restoration (n=195 regions)
- **Group 2:** M1-only implant restoration (n=88 regions)
- **Outcomes:** Implant survival, complication-free survival (Cox regression), cumulative cost, peri-implant conditions, MBL (radiological), PROMs (chewing ability, pain, discomfort, food impaction)
- **Follow-up:** ≥3 years (exact duration not specified in abstract)
- **Statistical models:** Cox proportional hazards for survival; β coefficients for cost and MBL outcomes

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Implant survival:** HR 1.66 (95% CI 0.29–9.50, p=0.567) — no significant difference
- **Complication-free survival:** HR 1.21 (95% CI 0.49–3.04, p=0.678) — no significant difference
- **Cumulative cost:** Group 2 (M1-only) 1.7x lower (β=−14.40, p<0.001)
- **Peri-implantitis:** 0% (Group 2) vs 16.2% (Group 1) at 3 years (p=0.043)
- **MBL >0.5 mm:** M1-only was protective (β=0.34, p=0.012)
- **PROMs — chewing:** NS between groups for soft and hard foods
- **Post-surgery pain:** M1-only group reported lower scores

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design; allocation non-randomised (selection bias risk)
- Follow-up duration not explicitly stated; 3-year horizon for peri-implantitis may underestimate long-term rates
- Single-centre design (West China + Zhejiang); generalisability to other populations unclear
- Confounders (bone quality, opposing arch, parafunctional habits) not fully controlled
- Need for long-term RCT comparing M1-only vs M1+M2 implant strategies

## 7. Glossary
- **SDA:** Shortened Dental Arch — dentition ending at first molars or premolars
- **MBL:** Marginal Bone Loss — radiological peri-implant bone resorption
- **PI:** Peri-implantitis — inflammatory condition around implants causing bone loss
- **PROMs:** Patient-Reported Outcome Measures
- **MOA:** Main Occluding Area — region where primary occlusal contacts occur
