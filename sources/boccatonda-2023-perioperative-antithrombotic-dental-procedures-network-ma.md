## Why Ingested

Adds the only network meta-analysis (NMA) in the anticoagulants folder, restricted to RCTs of coumarin management, to test the "continue VKA" message of [[drug/anticoagulants/chahine-2019-anticoagulation-dental-procedures-sr]]. Its antiplatelet discussion also sits beside [[drug/anticoagulants/shah-2022-perioperative-antiplatelet-therapy-sr-ma]] and [[drug/anticoagulants/yong-2022-dapt-dental-extraction-umbrella-review]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with network meta-analysis of 8 randomized trials (PubMed/Embase/Cochrane, search to 29 Oct 2022; PROSPERO 399590) on vitamin K antagonist (coumarin) management before dental surgery; DOAC trials were not included.

In the network analysis a 2-day coumarin suspension had lower bleeding risk than continuous full-dose therapy (OR 0.30, 95% CI 0.09-0.97), while low-dose coumarin (OR 0.58, 0.21-1.62) and LMWH (OR 0.79, 0.28-2.21) did not differ; no thromboembolic events in any trial and gauze plus tranexamic acid was the most effective local agent.

Bleeding was mostly mild, early and locally controllable, but the trials were heterogeneous with mostly unclear bias and few low-risk studies, and the authors' conclusion that holiday causes no extra bleeding sits awkwardly with their own 2-day suspension result.

## 세줄요약

줄1: 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 8편을 대상으로 한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA); 치과 수술 전 비타민K길항제 (Vitamin K Antagonist, VKA, 쿠마린) 관리 전략 비교이며 직접경구항응고제 (Direct Oral Anticoagulant, DOAC) 시험은 포함되지 않음.

줄2: 2일 쿠마린 중단군은 지속 full-dose군보다 출혈 위험이 낮았고 (OR 0.30, 95% CI 0.09-0.97), 저용량 쿠마린 (OR 0.58)·저분자량 헤파린 (Low-Molecular-Weight Heparin, LMWH; OR 0.79)은 차이 없음; 전 연구에서 혈전색전증 (Thromboembolism) 없음; 거즈+트라넥삼산 (Tranexamic Acid) 조합이 국소 지혈에 가장 효과적.

줄3: 출혈은 대부분 경미하고 술후 첫날 발생해 국소 지혈로 조절되었으나, 이질적 프로토콜·불명확한 편향위험·소수의 저위험 연구가 한계이며 저자의 "중단해도 출혈 증가 없음" 결론은 자체 결과와 긴장 관계.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: Int J Environ Res Public Health 2023;20(7), published 2023-03-28 (source: PubMed, PMC10093975)
- DOI: [10.3390/ijerph20075293](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph20075293)
- Institutions: Univ. of Chieti-Pescara; "G. d'Annunzio" University; Bentivoglio Hospital, Bologna (Italy)
- Type: systematic review + network meta-analysis; PROSPERO 399590

## 2. Key Contributions

- First network meta-analysis on anticoagulant management for dental surgery (authors' claim).
- Compares full-dose, reduced-dose and 2-day-suspended coumarin and LMWH; and gauze, gauze + tranexamic acid, fibrin sponge.
- Narrative update on antiplatelets, warfarin, heparin and DOAC perioperative handling from cited literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Databases PubMed/Medline, EMBASE, Cochrane; search updated to 29 Oct 2022. Title/abstract screening allowed RCTs and prospective/retrospective studies; SRs, editorials, non-English, case reports/series and in vitro excluded. Two reviewers. Risk of bias: OHAT tool in RevMan 5.5, 7 criteria, low risk = at least 5/7. Network models (Owen et al. method): haemostatic agents (gauze, gauze/tranexamic acid, fibrin sponge) and anticoagulant regimen (full-CU, low-CU, 2-DSu, Hep). Only low-risk studies entered the network analysis.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 2051 records; 6 duplicates; 1963 excluded; 82 full texts; 74 excluded (36 off topic, 23 non-English, 3 case reports/series, 12 reviews); 8 included.
- All 8 were RCTs; one double-blind, one split-mouth. All tested coumarin full therapy; one reduced dose; two 2-day suspension; one LMWH.
- Bleeding mostly immediate (<24 h); delayed bleeding in 4 studies; preop INR all <3.0; no thromboembolic events.
- Risk of bias: over 75% unclear for performance/detection; only Lourenco-Queiroz scored 7/7.

| Comparison vs continuous full coumarin | OR (95% CI) | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| 2-day suspension | 0.30 (0.09-0.97) | p<0.05 |
| Low-dose coumarin | 0.58 (0.21-1.62) | p>0.05 |
| LMWH | 0.79 (0.28-2.21) | p>0.05 |

Gauze + tranexamic acid was reported most effective for early bleeding (p<0.05); numeric effect sizes were not given in the retrieved text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Non-homogeneous protocols and few low-risk RCTs limit power; wide CI on the key OR (upper bound 0.97).
- Internal inconsistency: the conclusion says anticoagulant holiday does not increase bleeding and that withdrawal or bridging carries more thrombotic than bleeding risk, but the trials reported no thromboembolic events and the 2-day suspension was associated with less, not equal, bleeding. The thrombotic claim rests on cited background literature, not the pooled data.
- Discussion covers antiplatelets and DOACs although the pooled trials were coumarin-only; those statements come from cited literature.
- Title/abstract screening listed prospective/retrospective studies as eligible while the abstract says RCT only.
- Future: DOAC safety in renal insufficiency; anti-factor XIa drugs.

## 7. Glossary

- Network meta-analysis: indirect-plus-direct comparison of several interventions in one model.
- Coumarin / VKA: warfarin-type oral anticoagulants.
- LMWH: low-molecular-weight heparin used for bridging.
- OHAT: Office of Health Assessment and Translation risk-of-bias tool.
- INR: International Normalized Ratio.
