## Why Ingested
Classic split-mouth RCT directly comparing one-stage (ITI nonsubmerged) vs two-stage (Brånemark submerged) implant protocols in the partially edentulous maxilla — a frequent clinical decision point. Extends [[implants/survival/astrand-2000-nonsubmerged-implants-edentulous-upper-jaw]] (edentulous maxilla ITI-only 1-year data) and provides a head-to-head survival/MBL comparison missing from prior single-system cohorts.

## Three-line Summary
Split-mouth RCT (28 patients, 56 implant sites) comparing ITI nonsubmerged (one-stage) vs Brånemark submerged (two-stage) implants supporting fixed partial bridges in the partially edentulous maxilla, with 1-year loading follow-up.
No significant difference in implant survival (2/28 Brånemark lost pre-loading, 1/28 ITI lost at 1 year) or marginal bone change (0.2 ± 0.08 mm vs 0.1 ± 0.11 mm at 1 year). Both systems showed bone loss before baseline (1.9 mm vs 1.5 mm from reference point).
Crater-form bone destruction suggesting peri-implantitis observed at 18% of ITI implants, though clinical signs present at only 2 implants — highlights importance of radiographic monitoring for nonsubmerged systems.

## 세줄요약
분할구강 RCT (28명, 56개 부위)로 ITI 비침수형(1단계) 대 Brånemark 침수형(2단계) 임플란트를 부분 무치악 상악고정가교로 비교, 1년 부하 후 추적.
생존율(브로네마크 2실패/ITI 1실패) 및 변연골 변화량(0.2±0.08mm 대 0.1±0.11mm) 모두 유의차 없음. 단, 기준선 전 골소실 확인됨(1.9mm 대 1.5mm).
ITI 임플란트 18%에서 분화구형 골파괴(주위염 의심) 관찰되었으나 임상 증례는 2례 — 비침수형 시스템의 방사선적 모니터링 중요성 시사.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First prospective split-mouth RCT directly comparing ITI one-stage vs Brånemark two-stage protocols in the same patient
- 1-year prospective data on marginal bone level (MBL) change with standardized radiographic methodology
- Identification of crater-form bone loss pattern specific to nonsubmerged (ITI) implants (18% prevalence)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective randomized split-mouth clinical trial
- **Databases**: N/A (primary clinical study)
- **n**: 28 patients (56 implant sites — 28 ITI, 28 Brånemark); each patient received both systems contralaterally
- **Outcomes**: Implant survival, marginal bone level change (radiographic, 1-year post-loading), peri-implant clinical parameters, crater-form bone destruction incidence

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=28) limits statistical power for survival differences
- Short follow-up (1 year post-loading) — late failures and progressive bone loss not captured
- All patients received 6-month healing for both systems (standardized to Brånemark protocol), which may not reflect optimal ITI protocol
- No patient-reported outcomes or soft tissue assessments reported
- Single-center, single-surgeon context limits generalizability

## 7. Glossary
- **Nonsubmerged implant (비침수형 임플란트)**: One-stage implant placed at bone level with transmucosal healing abutment; no second-stage surgery (ITI system).
- **Submerged implant (침수형 임플란트)**: Two-stage implant placed subcrestally, covered by mucosa during healing; requires second-stage uncovering (Brånemark system).
- **Split-mouth design (분할구강 설계)**: Each patient receives both test and control interventions on contralateral sides; controls for inter-patient variability.
- **Marginal bone level (MBL, 변연골 레벨)**: Distance from implant reference point (e.g., abutment-implant junction) to first bone-to-implant contact; primary radiographic outcome.
- **Crater-form bone destruction (분화구형 골파괴)**: Saucer-shaped peri-implant bone loss pattern suggestive of peri-implantitis, often seen with nonsubmerged implants.
- **Peri-implantitis (임플란트 주위염)**: Inflammatory lesion with progressive bone loss around an osseointegrated implant.
