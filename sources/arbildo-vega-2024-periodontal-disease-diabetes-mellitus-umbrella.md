## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (18 systematic reviews, 2009–2023, AMSTAR-2 appraised; 296 primary studies, 17.26% overlap) of the association between periodontal disease (PD) and diabetes mellitus (T1DM/T2DM/GDM).

11 of the included SRs reported a PD–DM association vs 2 reporting none; meta-analytic pooled estimates ranged RR 1.26 (1.12–1.41) to 1.86 (1.25–2.77) and OR 1.69 (0.68–4.21) to 2.59 (2.12–3.15), with DM patients showing worse plaque index, gingival index, CAL, probing depth, and BOP.

High-confidence evidence supports a (likely bidirectional) PD–DM association across all diabetes types, but poor SR reporting quality (only 13/18 high confidence) and high primary-study overlap temper certainty; hypertension is not addressed.

## 세줄요약

치주질환(PD)과 당뇨병(1형·2형·임신성) 연관을 다룬 18편 체계적 고찰의 엄브렐라 리뷰(2009–2023, AMSTAR-2 평가, 원저 296편·중복 17.26%).

포함된 SR 중 11편이 PD–DM 연관을, 2편은 무연관을 보고; 메타분석 통합치는 RR 1.26(1.12–1.41)~1.86(1.25–2.77), OR 1.69(0.68–4.21)~2.59(2.12–3.15) 범위로, 당뇨 환자에서 치태지수·치은지수·임상부착소실·탐침깊이·탐침시출혈이 모두 악화.

모든 당뇨 유형에서 PD–DM 연관을 high-confidence로 지지하나 SR 보고 품질 편차(18편 중 13편만 high)와 높은 원저 중복이 확실성을 제한; 고혈압은 다루지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates **18 systematic reviews** (with/without meta-analysis) on the PD–DM association into a single AMSTAR-2-graded synthesis — larger and more current than the three prior umbrella reviews, which had diluted their focus by also including periodontal-therapy-efficacy studies.
- Stratifies the association by **periodontal clinical parameter** (plaque index, gingival index, CAL, probing depth, BOP, number of teeth, prevalence, general association) rather than a single pooled effect.
- Separates evidence by **diabetes type**: T1DM (4 SRs), GDM (3 SRs, all high confidence), and DM in general (10 SRs, 8 high confidence).
- Provides a **methodological-quality map**: 13/18 SRs high confidence, 1 low, 4 critically low — surfacing where the perio-diabetes literature is weakest (AMSTAR-2 critical domains 2, 4, 7, 9, 13, 15).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Databases**: PubMed, Cochrane, SciELO, Scopus + gray literature (OpenGrey, Google Scholar), searched to 20 August 2023.
- **Inclusion**: systematic reviews assessing the PD–DM association, no time/language limit. **Exclusion**: narrative/rapid reviews, intervention/observational/preclinical studies, and non-research item types.
- **Screening**: 572 → 519 after de-duplication → 12 full-text → +2 (from other umbrella reviews) +4 (other reasons) → **18 SRs** for synthesis. Rayyan software; dual independent reviewers with a third-reviewer tiebreak at every stage.
- **Quality**: AMSTAR-2 (Kappa 0.85), dual duplicate assessment; confidence graded high/moderate/low/critically-low per Shea et al.
- **Overlap**: 296 primary studies; 17.26% appeared in >1 SR (35 twice, 8 three times, 7 four times).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **General association**: 11/13 reporting SRs positive, 2 negative. Meta-analysis in 6 SRs → RR 1.26 (1.12–1.41) to 1.86 (1.25–2.77); OR 1.69 (0.68–4.21) to 2.59 (2.12–3.15).
- **Plaque index**: MD 0.20 (0.18–0.23); SMD 0.54–0.71.
- **Gingival index**: SMD 0.46 (0.08–0.84) to 0.63 (0.39–0.87).
- **Clinical attachment level (CAL)**: MD 0.26 (0.00–0.53) to 1.00 (0.15–1.84); SMD 0.47–0.82. One SR: association with T2DM but not T1DM.
- **Number of teeth**: MD −2.14 (−2.87 to −1.40) (fewer teeth in DM).
- **Prevalence**: OR 0.19 (0.08–0.37) to 1.85 (1.61–2.11).
- **Probing depth**: MD 0.11 (−0.03–0.25) to 0.46 (0.01–0.91); SMD 0.36–0.67. One SR: T2DM but not T1DM.
- **Bleeding on probing**: MD 7.90 (4.24–11.56); SMD 0.32–0.65.
- **Background risk anchor** (Introduction): poorly controlled diabetes carries an **86% higher risk** of periodontitis vs non-diabetic / well-controlled.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Effect estimates reported as **ranges across constituent SRs**, not a single re-pooled figure — heterogeneous diagnostic criteria, populations (children/adolescents/adults), and diabetes types (T1DM/T2DM/GDM).
- **High primary-study overlap** (17.26%) means the same primary data are counted multiple times across SRs, inflating apparent consistency; corrected covered area (CCA) not reported.
- Only 13/18 SRs high confidence; 4 critically low — the evidence base has real methodological gaps (registration, exhaustive search, RoB handling, publication bias).
- **Bidirectionality asserted but not quantified** here — the umbrella confirms association, not causal direction; well-conducted long-term RCTs are the stated priority.
- **Scope**: periodontal disease ↔ diabetes only. Hypertension / broader cardiometabolic disease are out of scope.

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review** — an overview of systematic reviews; the top of the evidence pyramid, synthesizing SRs (not primary studies) and grading their methodological quality.
- **AMSTAR-2** — A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews (v2), a 16-item checklist grading SR confidence as high/moderate/low/critically-low based on critical + non-critical domains.
- **CAL (Clinical Attachment Level)** — distance from the cemento-enamel junction to the base of the periodontal pocket; the reference measure of cumulative periodontal destruction.
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing)** — bleeding elicited by gentle periodontal probing; a marker of active gingival inflammation.
- **GDM (Gestational Diabetes Mellitus)** — glucose intolerance first recognized during pregnancy; one of the three diabetes types analyzed.
- **CCA (Corrected Covered Area)** — a metric quantifying primary-study overlap across the SRs in an umbrella review; not reported by this study.
