## Why Ingested
항고혈압약 복용 환자에서 임플란트 생존율이 아닌 치주염 주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 유병률이 현저히 높다는 반전 결과를 보고. [[drug/systemic-disease/masri-2024-early-implant-failure-antihypertensive-medications]] 및 [[implants/survival/hamade-2024-hypertension-dental-implants-sr-ma]]의 "항고혈압약 = 보호"라는 결론에 counterpoint 근거 제공.
→ [[drug/systemic-disease/seki-2020-antihypertensive-medications-clinical-parameters-anodized]]

## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=77 implants/35 patients, 7-year follow-up): antihypertensive medication users had significantly higher peri-implantitis rates (26.5% vs 4.7%, OR 7.38) despite zero implant loss in both groups.

## 한줄요약
후향코호트(77 임플란트/35환자, 7년 추적): 항고혈압약 복용군에서 치주염 주위염 유병률 26.5% vs 4.7%(OR 7.38), 임플란트 손실은 양군 모두 없음 — "생존 = 성공"이 아님을 시사.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=77 TiUnite anodized implants/35 patients, mean 84.8 months follow-up, Nihon University); antihypertensive users (13 patients/34 implants; CCB, ARB, thiazide) vs normotensives (22 patients/43 implants); peri-implantitis defined as PPD ≥6 mm + BoP/suppuration + bone resorption ≥25% implant length; logistic regression performed.

Peri-implantitis significantly higher in AHT group (26.5% vs 4.7% implant-level, OR 7.38; multivariate OR 12.90; p significant); mean probing pocket depth (PPD) higher (3.8 vs 3.0 mm); no implant loss in either group at 7 years.

Antihypertensive medications appear to protect implant survival while simultaneously elevating peri-implantitis risk — likely via CCB-induced gingival hyperplasia or immune modulation; demonstrates that survival and success are independent endpoints and cannot be conflated in AHT patients.

## 세줄요약

후향코호트(TiUnite 양극산화 임플란트 77개/35환자, 평균 84.8개월 추적, 일본대학); 항고혈압약(칼슘채널차단제(CCB)·안지오텐신수용체차단제(ARB)·치아자이드) 복용 13환자/34임플란트 vs 정상혈압 22환자/43임플란트; 치주임플란트염 (Peri-implantitis) = 탐침 포켓 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) ≥6 mm + 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BoP)/화농 + 골소실 ≥25%.

치주임플란트염 임플란트 수준 26.5% vs 4.7% (OR 7.38; 다변량 OR 12.90), 평균 PPD 3.8 vs 3.0 mm; 양군 모두 7년간 임플란트 손실 없음.

항고혈압약이 생존은 보호하면서 치주임플란트염 위험은 높이는 역설적 분리 현상 — CCB 유발 치은비대 또는 면역조절 가능성; 고혈압 환자 임플란트 평가에서 생존율·성공률을 구분해야 함을 시사.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 임플란트 생존 아닌 치주염 주위염을 주요 결과로 평가 — 성공률 관점에서 항고혈압약의 부정적 영향 최초 기술
- 7년 장기 추적으로 생존은 100%지만 성공은 저하됨을 논증
- 다변량 분석에서 항고혈압약 복용력만 유의한 독립 위험인자(OR=12.90)
- CCB 유발 치은비대 → 임플란트 주위 적용 가능성 제기
- 항고혈압약 = 생존 보호 + 치주건강 악화라는 역설적 분리 신호

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Study design**: Retrospective cohort, single periodontist (K.S.)
- **Implants**: 77 TiUnite anodized Nobel Biocare implants (35 maxilla, 42 mandible)
- **Groups**: AH (13 patients/34 implants) vs H (22 patients/43 implants)
- **Medications**: CCB, ARB, thiazide diuretics (mono or combination)
- **Outcomes**: PPD (6-point/implant), BoP, modified PI, marginal bone loss (radiographic)
- **Peri-implantitis definition**: PPD ≥6 mm + suppuration/BoP + bone resorption ≥25% implant length
- **Inclusion**: Age ≥50, two-stage, anodized implants, ≥6-month maintenance; EXCLUDED: smokers, periodontitis history, bone grafting, systemic diseases other than HTN
- **Statistics**: Mann-Whitney U, chi-squared, logistic regression

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Parameter | AH group | H group | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| PPD (mean) | 3.8 ± 1.3 mm | 3.0 ± 0.7 mm | Significant |
| Peri-implantitis (implant) | 26.5% (9/34) | 4.7% (2/43) | Significant |
| Peri-implantitis (patient) | 30.8% (4/13) | 9.1% (2/22) | Not significant |
| BoP | 0.3 | 0.2 | NS |
| Marginal bone loss | 0.66 mm | 0.34 mm | NS |
| Implant loss | 0 | 0 | — |

- OR peri-implantitis: **7.38** (95% CI 1.474–36.954)
- Multivariate OR: **12.90** for AHT history (other variables NS)
- Mean follow-up: 84.8 months

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Very small sample (n=35 patients)
- All same implant type (cannot generalize to other surfaces)
- No differentiation by drug class for peri-implantitis analysis
- Single-center, single periodontist
- 14 patients lost to follow-up (from initial 49)
- Only 50+ age group

## 6. Related Work
- Wu 2016: higher survival with AHT (HR 0.12) — contrasting result
- Masri 2024: lower EIF with AHT medication — different endpoint
- Chidiac 2026: 10-year protective effect on survival

## 7. Glossary
- TiUnite: Nobel Biocare's anodized (oxidized) implant surface
- PPD: 탐침 포켓 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth)
- BoP: 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing)
- mPI: modified Plaque Index
- CCB: 칼슘채널차단제 (Calcium Channel Blocker)
- ARB: 안지오텐신 수용체 차단제 (Angiotensin Receptor Blocker)
