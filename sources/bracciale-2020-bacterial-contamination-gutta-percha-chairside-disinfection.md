## Why Ingested

Part of a 4-paper gutta-percha (GP) cone-decontamination cluster. This is the primary empirical anchor documenting that in-use GP cones harbour bacteria (22.9%) and that a chairside 5.25% NaOCl protocol disinfects them — grounding the aseptic-chain concern that root-canal microbiology work such as [[endodontics/neelakantan-2018-endodontic-microbiology-editorial]] frames, and pairing with the cluster SR [[endodontics/aucinaite-2025-naocl-chx-gutta-percha-cone-decontamination-sr]] on NaOCl vs CHX cone decontamination.

## One-line Summary

In-vitro contamination survey (240 GP points, 4 brands, in-use packages): 22.9% harboured bacteria, no brand difference, and a chairside 5.25% NaOCl (1 min) + detergent (5 min) protocol disinfected 76.4% (P<0.001).

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Quantifies bacterial contamination of GP points taken from **in-use** commercial boxes (open 4–8 weeks, ~8 appointments/week) rather than sealed unused packages — a more realistic clinical-cross-infection scenario.
2. First reported contamination data for the **R&S®** brand (n=104), alongside Dentsply®, Proclinic®, ProTaper®.
3. Shows contamination is **not brand-dependent** but is associated with **less-used / oversized GP point sizes** (K30, D size) that stay in open boxes longer.
4. Validates a simple chairside disinfection protocol (CDP): 5.25% NaOCl 1 min → detergent (3% Tween 80 + 5% sodium thiosulfate) 5 min → sterile-water rinse, disinfecting 76.4% of contaminated cones.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Sampling**: 240 GP points across ISO sizes A, B, C, D, K15, K20, K25, K30, K35, K40, F1, F2, F3 from 4 brands (Dentsply® 104 / R&S® 104 / Proclinic® 8 / ProTaper®), collected by one operator with sterile gloves from **already-open in-use boxes** during the filling phase. Operators performing NSRCT were blinded to study goals to avoid altering handling behaviour.
- **Contamination assay**: each point placed individually (1 point per tube, sterile tweezers) into sterile **fluid thioglycolate medium** (Merck), incubated at 37°C, checked every 72 h for turbidity up to 21 days. Method per Pereira and Siqueira.
- **Chairside disinfection protocol (CDP)** applied to contaminated points: immersion in 10 mL 5.25% NaOCl (1 min), then active circular wash in 10 mL detergent (3% Tween 80 + 5% sodium thiosulfate, 5 min) to strip NaOCl crystals, then 10 mL sterile distilled water rinse; dried with sterile gauze, re-cultured in fresh thioglycolate medium.
- **Statistics**: SPSS v25; chi-square test for distribution comparison, binomial test for dichotomous variables; significance P<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Overall contamination**: 22.9% positive vs 77.1% negative (P<0.001).
- **By brand**: Dentsply® and R&S® highest at **47.3% each**; no significant relation between contamination and brand (chi-square P=0.273; P=0.188 with Proclinic® excluded for small n).
- **By size**: **K30 most contaminated at 16.4% (9/55)** — 8/9 R&S®, 1/9 Proclinic®. All six Dentsply® D-size points contaminated (10.9% of collected points) — the only size with significantly higher positive rate.
- **Chairside disinfection**: effective in **76.4% (42/55)** of contaminated points (P<0.001).
- **Interpretation**: oversized/rarely-used cones (D taper ~6%, ISO 30 cut up to ISO 35) stay in open boxes longer → more handling exposure → higher contamination.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Culture-based turbidity only — **no bacterial quantification or species identification** (budget/time). Secondary-infection potential of contaminants not assessed.
- **Unequal sampling** across brands (Dentsply®/R&S® n=104 each vs Proclinic® n=8) limits between-brand statistical comparison.
- Only 2 points per slot sampled — the true per-box contamination rate could differ; not all points from a contaminated slot were positive (attributed to GP's zinc-oxide antibacterial content and anti-adhesion coating).
- CHX not tested (incompatible with NaOCl — forms precipitate); no surface-topography or sealer-adhesion assessment after CDP.
- Single university-clinic setting (student operators under supervision).

## 7. Glossary

- **GP (Gutta-Percha)**: thermolabile root-canal filling material; cannot be heat-sterilized, so it must be chairside-disinfected before use.
- **NSRCT**: Non-Surgical Root Canal Treatment.
- **RCS**: Root Canal System.
- **CDP**: Chairside Disinfection Protocol.
- **Fluid thioglycolate medium**: broad-range culture medium supporting aerobic and anaerobic growth from low bacterial numbers.
- **NaOCl**: Sodium hypochlorite — endodontic irrigant/disinfectant; 5.25% inhibits growth faster but can deposit crystals and degrade GP surface.
- **Tween 80 / sodium thiosulfate**: detergent/neutralizer solution used to strip residual NaOCl crystals from the cone surface.
- **CHX**: Chlorhexidine — alternative disinfectant, avoided here due to NaOCl incompatibility (precipitate formation).
