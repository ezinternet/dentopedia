## Why Ingested

The `post-and-core` category already holds several primary SR+MA on individual variables (ferrule, fiber-vs-metal post, no-post) but lacked an umbrella review tying the whole restoration-choice landscape together at the highest evidence tier. This 2022 umbrella review pools 14 SR+MA (118 underlying studies, >10,971 samples) across success/survival/failure rate for single crowns, posts, and endocrowns, giving [[overviews/post-and-core-fiber-post-decision-overview]] an AMSTAR2-graded meta-synthesis layer above its constituent papers such as [[post-and-core/tsintsadze-2022-survival-rates-glass-fiber-metal-posts]] and [[post-and-core/al-dabbagh-2024-circumferential-ferrule-fiber-posts-survival]].

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (AMSTAR2-appraised; 14 SR/MA pooling 118 studies, >10,971 samples) of prosthetic restoration success/survival/failure on endodontically treated (ET) teeth.

Single crowns and endocrowns showed the most reliable outcomes (endocrown 5-yr success ~94% in some analyses vs conventional crown 5-yr survival ~98.3%); fiber posts had a higher success rate than metal posts in low-risk-of-bias reviews, while root-fracture rates between fiber and metal posts were similar; fixed/removable prostheses success (78%/66%) trailed single crowns.

Only 9 of 14 included S/M-R were low risk of bias (41.5%), and marked heterogeneity across pooled studies means the umbrella review's conclusions (favoring single crowns/endocrowns and fiber posts) should be treated as moderate-confidence, hypothesis-supporting evidence rather than definitive practice guidance.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(umbrella review, AMSTAR2 평가; SR/MA 14편, 원저 118편·표본 10,971명 초과 통합) — 근관치료치아(Endodontically Treated Teeth, ET teeth) 보철 수복의 성공률·생존률·실패율 종합.

단일크라운(single crown)과 엔도크라운(endocrown)이 가장 안정적 결과를 보였고(엔도크라운 5년 성공률 약 94% vs 통상 크라운 5년 생존율 약 98.3%), 저위험편향(low risk of bias) 리뷰에서는 섬유포스트(fiber post)가 금속포스트(metal post)보다 성공률이 높았으며 치근 파절률(root fracture rate)은 두 재료 간 유사, 고정성/가철성 보철물의 성공률(78%/66%)은 단일크라운에 못 미쳤다.

포함된 SR/MA 14편 중 저위험편향은 9편(41.5%)에 불과하고 연구 간 이질성(heterogeneity)이 커, 단일크라운·엔도크라운·섬유포스트를 선호하는 결론은 확정적 임상지침이 아니라 중등도 신뢰도의 가설 지지 근거로 받아들여야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella-level synthesis specifically on prosthetic (not merely endodontic) restoration success/survival/failure for endodontically treated (ET) teeth, pooling 14 SR/MA published up to November 2020.
- Applies AMSTAR2 (16-item) formal risk-of-bias grading to the included SR/MA layer itself, separating low-risk (9/14, 41.5%) from moderate-risk (5/14) evidence — a meta-level quality filter absent from any single constituent SR/MA.
- Organizes findings into three clinically actionable buckets: (a) success rate of prosthetic restorations generally, (b) success/survival of single crowns with vs without posts (and post material/type), and (c) failure-mode analysis across post/crown/prosthesis types.
- Cross-cutting synthesis of the ferrule effect, fiber-vs-metal post performance, and endocrown-vs-conventional-crown comparisons drawn from independently conducted SR/MA (Ploumaki, Sarkis-Onofre, Wang, Figueiredo, Heydecke & Peters, Naumann, Sedrez-Porto, Al-Dabbagh, among others).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA-guided umbrella review. Electronic search of MEDLINE/PubMed, Cochrane, and Google Scholar through November 2020, no language restriction.
- **PICO**: Population = teeth having received root canal treatment; Intervention = prosthetic restoration of ET teeth; Comparison = none (no controlled comparator specified); Outcome = success rate, survival rate, and post-endodontic failure rate.
- **Screening**: 43 candidate S/M-R identified (36 PubMed/MEDLINE, 5 Cochrane, 2 manual) → 17 after title/abstract/eligibility screening → 14 after full-text review. Exclusions: duplicate reviews, comments, editorials.
- **Data extraction**: Three independent trained reviewers (inter-rater kappa = 1.0); disagreements resolved by discussion, escalated to a fourth reviewer if unresolved.
- **Risk-of-bias tool**: AMSTAR2, 16 questions; two independent assessors (kappa = 0.9), third reviewer as tiebreaker. Scoring: 8–11 positive = low risk; 4–7 = moderate risk; ≤3 = high risk.
- **Included evidence base**: 14 SR/MA comprising 118 underlying primary studies (mix of RCTs, in vitro studies, clinical trials, prospective and retrospective cohorts) and >10,971 pooled samples.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Bias distribution**: 9/14 SR/MA (41.5% of a broader survey pool) low risk of bias; 5/14 moderate risk; none rated high risk after full-text screening.
- **Success rate, general prosthetic restorations** (3 SR/MA, 22 studies): endocrown 5-yr success ~77% vs conventional crown ~94% (one study); single crown 92%, fixed prosthesis 79%, removable prosthesis 66% (Sedrez-Porto / Ploumaki).
- **Success rate, single crown with/without post** (3 SR/MA, 34 studies): no-post 94%, with-post 92%, cast post-and-core 93%, prefabricated post 94% (Ploumaki); elastic/fiber post success range 71.8–100% (Sarkis-Onofre); prefabricated posts used more often than metal (45.8% vs 16.7%, Girotto).
- **Survival rate, single crowns** (8 SR/MA, 73 studies): endocrown 5-yr survival 91.4% vs conventional crown 98.3% (Al-Dabbagh); crown 94% vs composite resin 91% survival (Suksaphar); crown 10-yr survival 81% vs direct restoration 63% (Stavropoulou & Koidis); fiber posts significantly outperform metal posts in survival, especially with >2 missing coronal walls (Wang); metal post 90% vs fiber post 83.9% survival (Figueiredo); cast post-and-core survival 87.2–88.1% (Heydecke & Peters); ferrule increases survival by preserving cavity walls (Naumann); teeth without ferrule show wider survival variance (0–97%) than with ferrule (Sarkis-Onofre).
- **Failure rate** (6 SR/MA, 48 studies): fiber vs metal posts show similar root-fracture and post-debonding rates (Wang, Figueiredo); prefabricated metal + carbon-fiber posts show double the root-fracture rate of metal + fiberglass posts (Figueiredo); post-debonding is the most common failure mode across single crowns and fixed prostheses (Ploumaki, Sorrentino); endocrown failure rate higher than conventional restoration methods (Sedrez-Porto).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No comparator arm by design (umbrella review with descriptive PICO, no formal meta-analytic pooling of effect sizes across the 14 SR/MA) — findings are narratively synthesized, not statistically re-pooled.
- Marked heterogeneity in study designs, populations, and outcome definitions within and across the 14 included SR/MA limits direct comparability of the percentage figures quoted.
- AMSTAR2 assessment flagged that the Funding Sources item was frequently unreported across included SR/MA, reducing transparency about potential sponsor bias.
- Search restricted to three databases (PubMed/MEDLINE, Cochrane, Google Scholar) through November 2020 only — evidence published after this date (e.g., subsequent ferrule-specific and RCT-level fiber-vs-metal comparisons already in this wiki) is not captured.
- Authors explicitly call for more high-quality, low-heterogeneity primary studies (RCTs) to strengthen confidence in the single-crown/endocrown and fiber-post preference conclusions.

## 7. Glossary

- **ET teeth**: Endodontically treated teeth (root canal treated).
- **S/M-R**: Systematic and/or meta-analytic review(s) — the paper's shorthand for the constituent studies pooled in this umbrella review.
- **Umbrella review**: A review that systematically collects and appraises multiple systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a related set of questions, sitting one evidence tier above individual SR/MA.
- **AMSTAR2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews, version 2 — 16-item critical appraisal instrument for systematic reviews (used here to grade bias risk of the *included* SR/MA, not of primary studies).
- **Ferrule (circumferential ferrule)**: A band of sound coronal tooth structure (typically ≥1.5–2 mm, 360°) surrounding the restoration margin, associated with improved fracture resistance and survival.
- **Endocrown**: A monolithic restoration bonded directly into the pulp chamber and over the occlusal surface of an ET tooth, used as an alternative to a post-and-core-plus-crown.
- **Post-debonding**: Loss of adhesive retention of an intracanal post, cited here as the most common failure mode.
