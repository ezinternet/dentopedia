## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study, 65 patients (34 mild crowding, 31 moderate-severe crowding) treated with clear aligner therapy (CAT) after four first-premolar extractions; posterior functional cusp distances (FCDs) measured pre/post-treatment on 3D digital models.

Post-treatment FCDs increased significantly in the mild-crowding group at all posterior sites (greatest at first molar: MB 1.64±1.39 mm, DB 1.48±1.55 mm, both p<0.001), while the moderate-severe crowding group increased only at the first molar MB cusp (p<0.001); smaller ANB angle and greater upper incisor proclination (U1-NA, U1-SN) were positively associated with FCD increase.

Posterior FCD increase (the "roller coaster effect," a posterior open-bite tendency) is most pronounced at the first molar and in mild-crowding cases, and can be used as a simple clinical indicator; case selection should weigh sagittal skeletal pattern (ANB) and incisor proclination before extraction-based CAT.

## 세줄요약

회고적 연구, 4개 제1소구치 발치 후 투명교정(CAT)을 받은 65명 (경도 총생 34명, 중등도-중증 총생 31명); 3D 디지털 모델에서 치료 전후 구치부 기능교두거리(FCD) 측정.

경도 총생군에서는 모든 구치부 부위에서 치료 후 FCD가 유의하게 증가(제1대구치에서 가장 큼: 협측근심 1.64±1.39mm, 협측원심 1.48±1.55mm, 모두 p<0.001)했고, 중등도-중증 총생군은 제1대구치 협측근심 교두에서만 증가(p<0.001); 작은 ANB각과 큰 상악전치 순측경사(U1-NA, U1-SN)가 FCD 증가와 양의 상관관계.

구치부 FCD 증가("롤러코스터 효과", 구치부 개교합 경향)는 제1대구치와 경도 총생 환자에서 가장 두드러지며 간단한 임상 지표로 활용 가능; 발치 기반 CAT 적용 전 골격성 전후관계(ANB)와 전치 순측경사를 고려한 증례 선택이 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to quantify the "roller coaster effect" (RCE) — posterior open bite tendency after premolar-extraction CAT — using posterior functional cusp distance (FCD) as a simple, clinically applicable 3D metric.
- Directly compares RCE severity between mild (Mi) and moderate-severe (Mo-Se) crowding groups within the same extraction-CAT cohort, showing crowding severity modulates RCE risk.
- Identifies the first molar (both mesiobuccal and distobuccal cusps) as the site of greatest FCD increase, pointing to molar extrusion/incomplete anterior intrusion as the dominant mechanism (since mesial molar movement was minimal in this cohort).
- Establishes cephalometric risk factors — smaller ANB angle (more Class III-tending or less Class II sagittal relationship) and greater upper incisor proclination (U1-NA, U1-SN) — as correlates/predictors of RCE, giving clinicians a pre-treatment screening angle.
- Extends the "roller coaster effect" concept (coined for CAT vs. straight-wire comparison in prior literature, e.g., Qian et al. 2024) into a single-modality (CAT-only) risk-stratified analysis with regression-based risk factor identification.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective analysis of consecutively treated CAT patients.
- **Population**: 65 patients treated with CAT following four first-premolar extractions, split into:
  - Mild crowding (Mi) group: n=34
  - Moderate-to-severe crowding (Mo-Se) group: n=31
- **Outcome measure**: Posterior functional cusp distance (FCD), measured at:
  - Second premolar
  - Mandibular first molar — mesiobuccal (MB) cusp
  - Mandibular first molar — distobuccal (DB) cusp
  - Second molar
- **Measurement method**: Pre- and post-treatment digital models analyzed using a standardized 3D coordinate system (superimposition-based cusp-tip distance measurement consistent with prior CoS/FCD literature from the same research lineage, e.g., Tang et al. 2025).
- **Statistical approach**: Within-group pre/post comparison (paired testing) for FCD change at each posterior site; between-group (Mi vs. Mo-Se) comparison of FCD increase magnitude; correlation analysis between cephalometric variables (ANB, U1-NA, U1-SN) and posterior FCD increase; regression analysis to identify independent risk factors for RCE.
- Full statistical details (exact test names, regression coefficients/CIs, adjustment covariates) are not present in the retrieved abstract-level artifact.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Mild crowding (Mi) group**: FCD increased significantly post-treatment at *all* posterior sites measured.
  - First molar MB: +1.64 ± 1.39 mm (p<0.001) — largest increase observed.
  - First molar DB: +1.48 ± 1.55 mm (p<0.001).
- **Moderate-severe crowding (Mo-Se) group**: FCD increased significantly *only* at the first molar MB cusp (p<0.001); other posterior sites did not show significant increase.
- **Between-group comparison**: Mi group showed significantly greater FCD increases than Mo-Se group at all compared sites (all p<0.05).
- **Mechanism note**: Because mesial molar movement was minimal in this cohort, the FCD increase is attributed primarily to incomplete/insufficient posterior control (relative extrusion or lack of intrusion) rather than horizontal molar drift.
- **Correlation findings**: Greater upper incisor proclination (U1-NA angle, U1-SN angle) positively correlated with greater posterior FCD increase.
- **Regression finding**: Smaller ANB angle was identified as an independent risk factor for RCE (i.e., patients with a more Class III-tending or reduced Class II sagittal skeletal pattern showed greater posterior FCD increase/RCE susceptibility).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Retrospective, non-registered design**: No prospective registration; inherent selection and information bias risks of retrospective chart/model review.
- **Abstract-only artifact for this ingest**: The source PDF used here contains only the abstract and reference list, not the full methods/results tables, discussion, or supplementary data — some quantitative nuance (exact regression coefficients, subgroup breakdowns beyond first molar, adjustment for confounders such as age/sex/anchorage design) could not be extracted and should be revisited if the full-text PDF becomes available.
- **Single ethnic/regional cohort**: Population characteristics (ethnicity, treating clinician/practice, aligner brand) are not specified in the abstract; generalizability to other populations or aligner systems (e.g., non-Invisalign systems) is unclear.
- **FCD as proxy measure**: FCD increase is used as a proxy for "posterior open bite tendency"/RCE, but does not directly measure occlusal contact loss, bite force, or functional occlusal outcome — clinical significance of a given FCD increase magnitude (e.g., is +1.64mm clinically meaningful for occlusal function?) is not established by this metric alone.
- **No control/non-extraction comparison arm**: Unlike the related Tang 2025 CoS study (extraction vs non-extraction), this study did not include a non-extraction comparator, so the added risk directly attributable to extraction (vs. CAT mechanics generally) cannot be isolated from these data alone.
- **Future work**: Full-text retrieval to extract regression model detail; prospective validation of ANB/U1 proclination as pre-treatment screening thresholds; correlation with actual occlusal-contact loss (T-scan or occlusal registration) rather than FCD alone; comparison with straight-wire fixed appliance extraction cases (cf. Qian et al. 2024 EJO cjae072) to determine if RCE is CAT-specific or extraction-general.

## 7. Glossary

- **RCE (Roller Coaster Effect)**: Colloquial/descriptive term for the vertical undulation of the posterior occlusal plane — specifically a posterior open-bite tendency — that can occur during extraction-based clear aligner therapy, first named in Qian et al. 2024.
- **CAT**: Clear Aligner Therapy.
- **FCD (Functional Cusp Distance)**: The linear distance between the functional (centric-holding) cusp tip of a posterior tooth and a reference plane/opposing structure, measured pre- and post-treatment on superimposed 3D digital models; used here as the primary quantitative indicator of posterior open-bite tendency.
- **MB / DB**: Mesiobuccal / distobuccal (cusp locations on a mandibular molar).
- **Mi / Mo-Se**: Mild crowding / Moderate-to-severe crowding (study subgroups).
- **ANB angle**: Cephalometric angle representing the anteroposterior (sagittal) relationship between the maxilla (A point) and mandible (B point) relative to nasion; smaller ANB indicates a relatively more Class III-tending sagittal skeletal pattern.
- **U1-NA / U1-SN**: Cephalometric angles quantifying upper (maxillary) central incisor inclination/proclination relative to the NA line and SN (sella-nasion) plane, respectively.
