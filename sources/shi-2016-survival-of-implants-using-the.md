## One-line Summary

Systematic review of 34 studies (1,977 patients, 3,119 implants) on osteotome sinus floor elevation: weighted cumulative survival was higher without grafting (97.30%) than with grafting (95.89%), 84 of 102 failures occurred within the first year of loading, and implants <8 mm and grafted sites with RBH <5 mm did worse (abstract only).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Early failures dominate in osteotome sinus cases (84/102 failures within 1 year of loading).
- Nongrafted osteotome elevation performs at least as well as grafted.
- RBH <5 mm lowers survival in grafted cases; <8 mm implants underperform.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Systematic review; electronic search for prospective and retrospective osteotome sinus floor elevation studies published 2000-01-01 to 2015-10-30; inclusion: osteotome with or without graft, survival/RBH/graft data, mean follow-up ≥1 year after loading, ≥10 patients. Mean weighted cumulative survival compared between grafted and nongrafted; RBH and implant length effects examined.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

34 studies, 1,977 patients, 3,119 implants; 102 failures, 84 within the first year of function. Weighted cumulative survival: graft 95.89% vs nongraft 97.30% (P=.05). Grafted group: lower survival at RBH <5 mm. Nongrafted group: no significant RBH effect (threshold detail truncated in the PubMed abstract). Implants <8 mm: significantly lower survival.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract only. Weighted pooling of mixed prospective/retrospective studies without stated risk-of-bias assessment in the abstract; survival rather than early-failure endpoint.
