## Why Ingested

4Y-TZP와 6Y-TZP 고투명 지르코니아에 대해 표면처리(연마, 폴리싱, 글레이징), LTD 에이징, pH 변화를 복합 평가한 in vitro 연구. 단순 표면처리 비교를 넘어 LTD 후 pH 환경 노출(산성 vs 알칼리성)의 상호작용을 체계적으로 분석하여 임상적 내구성 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Grinding followed by polishing produced the highest biaxial flexural strength and lowest surface roughness in both 4Y-TZP and 6Y-TZP, while grinding alone caused the greatest mechanical deterioration. LTD aging combined with pH 3 exposure accelerated tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation to 22% and reduced flexural strength by up to 30%, versus 15% transformation and 18% strength loss at pH 9. 6Y-TZP exhibited lower absolute strength than 4Y-TZP but greater resistance to LTD-induced phase transformation due to its higher cubic phase content.

## 세줄요약

연마(그라인딩)+폴리싱이 4Y-TZP 및 6Y-TZP 모두에서 최고 이축굴곡강도와 최저 표면조도를 나타냈으며, 연마만 단독 시행 시 기계적 성능이 가장 크게 저하되었다. LTD 에이징+pH 3 노출 조합은 단사정 변태를 22%까지 가속하고 굴곡강도를 최대 30% 감소시킨 반면, pH 9에서는 단사정 변태 15%, 강도 18% 손실로 비교적 양호했다. 6Y-TZP는 4Y-TZP보다 절대 강도는 낮지만 고큐빅상 함량 덕분에 LTD 저항성이 우수하여, 고부하 후방부 적용 시 표면처리 선택이 중요하다.

## Document Information

- **Study type**: In vitro laboratory study
- **Sample size**: 336 disc-shaped Y-TZP specimens (12 mm diameter, 1.2 mm thickness)
- **Materials**: 4Y-TZP (Nacera DD cube ML HT, Germany); 6Y-TZP (Nacera Pearl Q3 ML-HT, Germany)
- **Groups**: 2 zirconia types × 4 surface treatments × 3 conditions (control/LTD+pH3/LTD+pH9); n=14 per subgroup

## Key Contributions

1. First study to evaluate combined effects of LTD aging and prolonged pH exposure (14 days) on high-translucent zirconia surface treatments
2. Demonstrates that post-grinding polishing is the most effective surface treatment for preserving mechanical properties after LTD
3. Quantifies differential degradation under acidic (pH 3) vs alkaline (pH 9) conditions after LTD
4. Provides Weibull reliability analysis for all 24 experimental groups
5. Characterizes 4Y-TZP vs 6Y-TZP trade-off: strength vs LTD resistance

## Methodology

### Sample Preparation
- Pre-sintered zirconia plates cut to ~14.6×14.6×3.5 mm (accounting for 20% sintering shrinkage)
- Sintered at 1450°C, 120-min holding, 10°C/min rate
- Final dimensions verified by digital caliper

### Surface Treatments
- **Glazed**: IPS e.max Ceram Glaze (Ivoclar Vivadent) at 800°C, 1 min, single-cycle
- **Grinding**: High-speed handpiece 350,000 rpm, 46-μm diamond bur, 2 N force, 30 s, water cooling
- **Grinding + Polishing**: Three-step protocol (Meisinger ZR 10,000 rpm → Optrafine 12,000 rpm → Signum HP Diamond 15,000 rpm)
- **Grinding + Glazing**: 46-μm diamond bur at 170,000 rpm + IPS e.max Ceram Glaze at 800°C

### Aging Protocol
- **Control**: Distilled water at 37°C for 30 days
- **LTD**: Autoclave at 134°C, 0.2 MPa (2 bar), 30 h (≈10 years of clinical aging)
- **Post-LTD pH exposure**: 37°C for 14 days in pH 3 (citric acid buffer) or pH 9 (ammonium buffer)

### Testing
- Biaxial flexural strength (BFS): Piston-on-three-balls method (ISO 6872), Instron 5965, 1 mm/min
- Weibull analysis: Characteristic strength (σ₀) and Weibull modulus (m)
- XRD (Empyrean, Malvern Panalytical): Garvie-Nicholson/Toraya method for monoclinic fraction
- SEM (Quanta 250 FEG): 20 kV, 1000×/2500×
- Profilometry (Talysurf CCI HD): Ra, Rq; 2.5×2.5 mm scan area

## Key Results

### Biaxial Flexural Strength (selected groups)

| Group | Mean ± SD (MPa) | Weibull m | σ₀ (MPa) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4GC (glazed control) | 1171.4 ± 113.3 | 12 | 1220.25 |
| 4GrC (grinding control) | 839.3 ± 72.9 | 8 | 871.12 |
| 4GPC (grind+polish control) | 1111.6 ± 118.3 | 10.9 | 1163.07 |
| 4GGC (grind+glaze control) | 1137.5 ± 98.9 | 13.2 | 1181.24 |
| 4G3 (glazed + LTD + pH3) | 1217.5 ± 81.5 | 20.0 | 1252.71 |
| 4Gr3 (grinding + LTD + pH3) | 760.5 ± 79.5 | 11.4 | 794.46 |
| 4Gr9 (grinding + LTD + pH9) | 631.2 ± 65.7 | 10.1 | 660.46 |

### Surface Roughness
- Glazed control (GC): Ra 0.71 ± 0.12 μm (lowest)
- Grinding + LTD + pH9 (Gr9): Ra 2.57 ± 0.31 μm (highest)
- Post-grinding polishing significantly reduced Ra vs grinding alone (p < 0.05)

### Phase Transformation (XRD)
- Control group: ~3% monoclinic phase
- Post-LTD average: ~18% monoclinic
- Grinding alone: up to 25% monoclinic after LTD
- Grinding + polishing: 12% monoclinic after LTD (lowest)
- pH 3 environment: 22% monoclinic transformation, 30% flexural strength reduction
- pH 9 environment: 15% monoclinic transformation, 18% flexural strength reduction

### 4Y-TZP vs 6Y-TZP
- 4Y-TZP: higher BFS (~78% tetragonal / ~22% cubic in control)
- 6Y-TZP: lower BFS but greater LTD resistance (~66% tetragonal / ~34% cubic in control)
- 6Y-TZP showed less monoclinic phase increase after LTD across all surface treatment groups

## Limitations

1. Excludes dynamic intraoral conditions (masticatory forces, thermal cycling, oral biofilm)
2. No combined surface treatment groups (e.g., polishing + glazing)
3. 5Y-TZP excluded; direct comparison limited to two extremes of translucency-strength spectrum
4. No direct optical measurements; "high-translucent" based on manufacturer classification
5. Short-term pH exposure (14 days) may not fully simulate multi-year clinical exposure

## Glossary

- **LTD (Low-Temperature Degradation)**: 저온열화 — 수분+온도로 인한 지르코니아의 정방정→단사정 상전이
- **BFS (Biaxial Flexural Strength)**: 이축굴곡강도 — 복수 방향 굽힘 하중에 대한 파절 저항성
- **4Y-TZP**: 4몰% 이트리아 안정화 정방정 지르코니아 다결정체 — 고투명 중강도
- **6Y-TZP**: 6몰% 이트리아 — 최고투명 저강도, LTD 저항성 우수
- **Weibull modulus (m)**: 와이불 계수 — 높을수록 강도 분포 균질성·신뢰성 ↑
- **Ra**: 산술평균 거칠기 (Arithmetic mean roughness)
- **Rq**: 제곱평균제곱근 거칠기 (Root mean square roughness)
- **Monoclinic phase**: 단사정상 — LTD 진행 지표, 미세균열 유발
