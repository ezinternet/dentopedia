## Three-line Summary

Evidence summary (emergency medicine, 2024) answering whether point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) can separate soft-tissue abscess from cellulitis, built from four systematic reviews and meta-analyses of POCUS for diagnosing abscess.

The abstract reports no pooled numbers; it concludes that POCUS "can reliably differentiate abscess and cellulitis and assist with management" of skin and soft-tissue infections.

Non-dental skin/soft-tissue evidence from an abstract only; the figures behind the conclusion were not retrieved, so it cannot be used to quote accuracy values.

응급의학 근거 요약(2024): 연부조직 농양과 봉와직염을 POCUS로 구분할 수 있는지를, POCUS 농양 진단을 다룬 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 4편으로 정리.
