## Why Ingested

Muscular-origin TMD (myalgia/myofascial pain) is the subtype where splints are most commonly prescribed, yet the held splint SRs ([[tmj/zhang-2020-occlusal-splint-tmd-rct-sr]], [[tmj/singh-2024-occlusal-interventions-tmd-cochrane-sr]]) cover all TMD subtypes. This SR isolates the muscular subtype and compares splints against other active treatments, complementing [[tmj/salloum-2024-stabilization-splint-ultrasound-myofascial-pain-rct]] and [[tmj/cimino-2025-counselling-vs-splint-tmd-neck-pain-rct]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis reported in the abstract) of studies on stabilization splints for muscular-origin TMD versus other treatments; six databases plus gray literature searched; 10 studies included.

Splints (n = 160) were reported as effective as other treatments (n = 209) for pressure pain threshold, chewing pain, mouth opening, spontaneous pain and palpation pain; 5 studies low and 5 some-concerns risk of bias; certainty very low for all outcomes.

A positive splint effect on muscular TMD could be neither confirmed nor refuted; splints are not shown to be superior to alternatives.

## 세줄요약

근원성 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에서 안정화 스플린트 (Stabilization Splint)를 다른 치료와 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR); 6개 데이터베이스와 회색문헌 검색, 10편 포함 (초록에 메타분석 언급 없음).

스플린트군 (n = 160)은 압통역치·저작 시 통증·개구량·자발통·촉진통에서 다른 치료군 (n = 209)과 동등하다고 보고됨; 비뚤림위험 낮음 5편·일부 우려 5편; 모든 결과에서 근거 확실성 매우 낮음 (GRADE).

스플린트의 긍정적 효과는 확인도 반박도 불가하며, 다른 치료 대비 우월성은 입증되지 않음.

## 1. Document Information

Honnef et al., Cranio 2022;42(6):718-729 (online 2022-03-21), Brazilian Center for Evidence Based Research (Federal University of Santa Catarina) with Michelotti and Flores-Mir. PubMed PMID 35311479; DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/08869634.2022.2047510. Source: PubMed abstract only.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Restricts the splint question to TMD of muscular origin.
- Compares stabilization splints with other active treatments, not only with no treatment or placebo splints.
- Applies Cochrane risk-of-bias assessment and GRADE.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Six electronic databases and gray literature searched. Risk of bias: Cochrane Collaboration tool. Certainty: GRADE. Ten articles included. Details (eligibility, registration, diagnostic criteria) not available from the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Splints (n = 160) vs other treatments (n = 209): reported as equally effective on pressure pain threshold, pain during chewing, mouth opening, spontaneous pain intensity and pain by palpation.
- Risk of bias: 5 low, 5 some concerns.
- Certainty of evidence: very low for all outcomes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract-only; comparator types, effect sizes and follow-up durations not retrieved. Whether a meta-analysis was performed is not stated in the abstract, so it is labelled `sr`. Very low certainty precludes firm conclusions.
