## Why Ingested

첫 번째 이중맹검 위약대조 RCT로 부데소니드를 대용량 저압 생리식염수 비강 세척에 추가했을 때 만성부비동염(CRS) 환자의 증상(SNOT-22) 및 내시경 소견 개선에 미치는 증분 효과를 검증. 기존 비대조 코호트 연구들(Snidvongs 2011, Sachanandani 2012, Steinke 2014)의 한계를 극복. [[oral-medicine/odontogenic-sinusitis/jaiswal-2024-sinus-irrigation-as-an-adjunctive]]의 상악동 세척 보조요법 근거를 비치성 CRS까지 확장하는 핵심 RCT.

## Three-line Summary

Double-blind, placebo-controlled RCT (n=80 randomized, 61 completed) evaluating budesonide (1 mg/day) added to large-volume, low-pressure saline irrigation for 30 days in chronic rhinosinusitis patients. Mean SNOT-22 improvement was 20.7 points (budesonide) vs 13.6 points (saline alone), mean difference 7 points (95% CI −2 to 16, Cohen d=0.39). 79% achieved MCID (≥9 points) with budesonide vs 59% with saline (difference 20%, 95% CI −2.5% to 42.5%); endoscopic scores improved 3.4 vs 2.7 points.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 위약대조 RCT (무작위 80명, 완료 61명)로 만성부비동염 환자에게 30일간 대용량 저압 생리식염수 세척에 부데소니드 1mg/일을 추가한 효과 평가. SNOT-22 평균 개선도 부데소니드군 20.7점 vs 식염수군 13.6점, 평균 차이 7점 (95% CI −2∼16, Cohen d=0.39). 임상적 의미 있는 개선(MCID ≥9점) 달성률 부데소니드 79% vs 식염수 59% (차이 20%, 95% CI −2.5%∼42.5%), 내시경 점수도 3.4 vs 2.7점 개선.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **First RCT with placebo control** for budesonide in large-volume saline irrigation for CRS — prior evidence limited to 3 small prospective cohorts without controls (n=8-31).
2. **Dual outcome assessment**: Patient-reported (SNOT-22, CGI) + objective (Lund-Kennedy endoscopic grading).
3. **Clinically meaningful effect size**: Mean SNOT-22 improvement difference 7 points (Cohen d=0.39, medium); MCID achievement 79% vs 59% (OR 2.6).
4. **Subgroup insights**: Greater benefit in patients *without* prior sinus surgery (mean diff 10.1 points) vs with surgery (−0.1 points); nasal polyps associated with reduced benefit (−4.1 points).
5. **Safety**: No related adverse events; budesonide well tolerated over 30 days.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

### Study Design
- **Type**: Single-center, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group RCT
- **Randomization**: Block randomization generated by study statistician; allocation concealed via numbered bottles (1-80)
- **Blinding**: Participants, clinicians, outcome assessors blinded; only statistician unblinded
- **Duration**: 30-day intervention; assessments at baseline, 2 weeks, 4 weeks (post-intervention)
- **Sample size**: 80 enrolled (40/group) powered for 9-point SNOT-22 MCID, α=0.05, 80% power, 20% dropout anticipated

### Participants
- **Inclusion**: Age ≥18, CRS ≥12 weeks per EPOS criteria, ≥2 cardinal symptoms (mucopurulent drainage, nasal obstruction, facial pain/pressure, hyposmia), endoscopic/CT evidence of inflammation
- **Exclusion**: Mucociliary disorders, antibiotics ≤2 weeks, sinus surgery ≤6 weeks, CSF leak, steroid allergy, TB, ocular herpes, pregnancy/lactation, chronic systemic steroid dependence
- **Baseline (n=74)**: Mean age 51±14.7 yr, 68% female, 90% White; 53% no comorbidity (ACE-27); 25% nasal polyps (imbalanced: 34% budesonide vs 16% saline); 28% prior sinus surgery; mean SNOT-22 44.1±18.5, Lund-Kennedy 5.3±2.2

### Interventions
- **Device**: 8-oz NeilMed Sinus Rinse bottle (large-volume, low-pressure)
- **Saline**: USP-grade NaCl/NaHCO₃ with distilled/boiled water
- **Budesonide arm**: 2 × 0.5 mg budesonide capsules dissolved per irrigation (1 mg/day total)
- **Placebo arm**: 2 × lactose capsules (identical appearance)
- **Protocol**: Once daily, half volume per nasal cavity, 30 days; verbal + written instructions

### Outcomes
- **Primary**: Intraparticipant change in SNOT-22 (0-110) baseline to post-treatment
- **Secondary**: 
  - CGI 7-point Likert scale (minimally/much/very much improved)
  - Lund-Kennedy endoscopic score (0-20)
- **MCID**: ≥9-point SNOT-22 reduction (established threshold)
- **Analysis**: ITT (mixed general linear model), per-protocol sensitivity (excluding 1 noncompliant), subgroup by polyps/surgery/comorbidity

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Budesonide (n=29) | Saline (n=32) | Difference (95% CI) | p / Effect Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Δ SNOT-22 (mean±SD)** | 20.7±17.9 | 13.6±18.8 | 7.0 (−2 to 16) | d=0.39 (medium) |
| **MCID ≥9 pts achieved** | 23/29 (79%) | 19/32 (59%) | 20% (−2.5% to 42.5%) | OR 2.6 (0.84-8.2) |
| **Δ Lund-Kennedy (mean±SD)** | 3.4±2.3 | 2.7±1.9 | 0.7 (−0.6 to 2.0) | NS |
| **CGI "improved" any degree** | 24/29 (83%) | 20/32 (63%) | 16% (−6% to 38%) | — |

### Subgroup Analyses (SNOT-22 change difference)
- **No prior surgery** (n=20 vs 23): +10.1 points (95% CI −1.1 to 21.3)
- **Prior surgery** (n=9 vs 9): −0.1 points (95% CI −19.6 to 19.4)
- **No polyps** (n=18 vs 26): +10.2 points (95% CI −1.6 to 22.1)
- **Polyps present** (n=10 vs 6): −4.1 points (95% CI −20.4 to 12.2)
- Comorbidity (ACE-27): Not a confounder

### Per-protocol (excluding 1 noncompliant budesonide)
- Δ SNOT-22: 22.1 vs 13.6, difference 8.5 (95% CI −0.6 to 18.0)
- MCID: 82% vs 59%, difference 23% (95% CI 1% to 45%), OR 3.15 (0.95-10.42)

### Safety
- No treatment-related adverse events in either group
- No adrenal suppression assessed (short duration)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

1. **Short duration (4 weeks)**: CRS is chronic; longer treatment may show greater/lesser effect. Adrenal axis not assessed.
2. **Imprecise estimates**: Wide 95% CIs include null for primary outcome (−2 to 16) and most subgroups — underpowered for definitive conclusions.
3. **Subgroup imbalances**: Polyps unevenly distributed (34% vs 16%); small subgroup sizes (n=6-10 per arm) prohibit firm conclusions.
4. **Compliance by self-report only**: No objective adherence measure (bottle weights, capsule counts).
5. **Single center, predominantly White (90%)**: Generalizability limited.
6. **No active comparator**: Did not compare budesonide irrigation vs intranasal steroid spray (standard care).
7. **Post-hoc subgroup findings**: Surgery and polyp interactions exploratory; need prospective validation.

**Future research needed**: Larger RCTs with longer follow-up, active comparator (steroid spray), stratification by polyp status/surgery history, adrenal safety monitoring, cost-effectiveness.

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **CRS** | Chronic rhinosinusitis — inflammation of paranasal sinuses ≥12 weeks |
| **SNOT-22** | Sino-Nasal Outcome Test 22-item — validated PRO for rhinosinusitis (0-110) |
| **MCID** | Minimal clinically important difference — ≥9 points for SNOT-22 |
| **Lund-Kennedy** | Endoscopic grading system for sinonasal inflammation (0-20) |
| **CGI** | Clinical Global Impressions — 7-point patient-rated improvement scale |
| **ACE-27** | Adult Comorbidity Evaluation-27 — comorbidity severity instrument |
| **Large-volume, low-pressure** | Irrigation delivering ~240 mL at low pressure (e.g., squeeze bottle) |
| **Budesonide** | Glucocorticoid steroid; topical anti-inflammatory for rhinitis/polyps/COPD |
