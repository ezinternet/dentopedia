Extends the HaeNaem design line [[implants/osteotomy-thermal/kim-2019-double-spiral-condensing-screw-implant]] and the undersized-drilling stability literature [[immediate-implant/primary-stability/sierra-rebolledo-2021-undersized-drilling-immediate-tapered-implants-maxilla]].

## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT (n=32 implants, healed posterior maxilla, single-tooth replacement, immediate loading within 48–72 h) randomizing undersized drilling (축경 드릴링, Group I, n=16) vs single drilling technique using the HaeNaem Co. One Drill System Kit (단일 드릴링, Group II, n=16), Neobiotech IS II active implants, 12-month follow-up.

No significant between-group difference on any outcome (all P > 0.05): insertion torque 35.0±2.58 vs 35.62±2.50 Ncm (P=0.49), ISQ 77.3 vs 76.3 down to 76.1 vs 75.5 at 12 months (P=0.54–0.74), buccal MBL 0.47 vs 0.70 mm at 12 months (P=0.06); every implant successful with no complications (100%).

Both techniques are clinically equivalent in poor-density posterior maxilla under an immediate-loading protocol; the single-drilling merits claimed (less heat/mechanical damage/friction, shorter operative time) are author claims, not measured outcomes, and the small sample plus short follow-up limit generalization.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First head-to-head RCT comparing single drilling technique (one drill at implant diameter after a guiding drill — no sequential widening) vs undersized drilling technique for **immediately loaded** implants in the posterior maxilla.
- Provides a non-retracted clinical outcome dataset for the HaeNaem Co. One Drill System Kit (guiding drill 3.5 mm at 800 rpm, then a single drill at implant diameter, 800 rpm).
- Both techniques delivered high insertion torque (≥ 35 Ncm) and primary stability (> 70 ISQ) with stable 12-month ISQ trajectories and no between-group differences on any clinical or radiographic measure.
- Claims single drilling merits — avoiding excessive heat generation, mechanical damage, high frictional forces, and shortening operative time / surgical-site exposure — presented as author claims, not as measured study outcomes.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective randomized clinical trial (CONSORT flow followed); computer-generated randomization list (SPSS v27.0), allocation by a senior resident not involved in the trial.
- **n**: 32 patients / 32 implants — Group I (undersized drilling) 16, Group II (single drilling) 16. Sample size via G*Power 3.1.9.7, effect size 1.20 (from Elsheikh et al., 2022), 2-tailed, α=0.05, power 90%.
- **Selection**: Inclusion — missing maxillary posterior tooth/teeth, age ≥18 y, bone height ≥10 mm, proper oral hygiene + follow-up willingness, adequate inter-arch space and mesio-distal width, free of parafunctional habits; Exclusion — root apex at planned site, drug or alcohol abuse, uncontrolled systemic disease, pregnancy, smokers.
- **Surgery**: articaine HCl 4% + 1:100,000 adrenaline; full-thickness paracrestal mucoperiosteal flap. Group I — manufacturer sequence with final drill skipped, 800–1200 rpm per bone density. Group II — One Drill System Kit (HaeNaem Co., Ltd, Korea): guiding drill 3.5 mm at 800 rpm, then one drill of the implant diameter. Neobiotech IS II active (Seoul, Korea) implants placed 1 mm subcrestal; manual ratchet for torque; healing abutments; 4/0 prolene sutures.
- **Prosthetics**: immediate loading within 48–72 h; open-tray impression; metal-ceramic restoration cemented with temporary cement.
- **Outcomes**: insertion torque; implant stability (ISQ) at insertion and 3/6/12 mo; modified sulcus bleeding index (mBI) at 3/6/12 mo; peri-implant probing depth at loading and 3/6/12 mo; buccal and palatal bone density (preop, immediate postop, 6/12 mo); buccal and palatal marginal bone loss at 6/12 mo.
- **Statistics**: SPSS v27.0; independent t-test (between groups), paired t-test and repeated-measures ANOVA (within-group over time), Fisher exact test (categorical); normality by Kolmogorov–Smirnov and Shapiro–Wilk; two-sided, P ≤ 0.05 significant.
- **Preop imaging**: CBCT (OnDemand 3D, Cybermed Co., Seoul, Korea) for buccolingual width and residual bone height.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Between-group comparisons were non-significant (P > 0.05) on every outcome at every time point.

- **Insertion torque**: Group I 35.00 ± 2.58 vs Group II 35.62 ± 2.50 Ncm (P=0.49). Both ≥ 35 Ncm.
- **ISQ (immediate / 3 / 6 / 12 mo)**: G1 77.31±3.92 / 74.37±3.27 / 75.81±4.06 / 76.12±5.92; G2 76.25±5.76 / 73.18±3.10 / 74.93±4.07 / 75.50±4.53; P = 0.54 / 0.29 / 0.54 / 0.74. All > 70.
- **mBI (3 / 6 / 12 mo)**: G1 0.15±0.11 / 0.22±0.34 / 0.10±0.11; G2 0.20±0.18 / 0.08±0.11 / 0.18±0.33; P = 0.35 / 0.12 / 0.36.
- **Probing depth (loading / 3 / 6 / 12 mo)**: G1 3.67±0.33 / 2.97±0.36 / 2.48±0.36 / 2.35±0.36 mm; G2 3.47±0.42 / 2.64±0.58 / 2.39±0.37 / 2.20±0.28 mm; P = 0.14 / 0.06 / 0.49 / 0.19. Within-group decrease over time significant in both (repeated ANOVA P < 0.001).
- **Buccal bone density (preop / immediate / 6 / 12 mo)**: G1 300.95±136.5 / 850.98±255.47 / 1095.54±206.58 / 1107.26±209.87; G2 323.72±224.26 / 815.71±254.09 / 1106.97±230.72 / 1114.66±243.32; P = 0.73 / 0.69 / 0.88 / 0.92; within-group increase significant (P < 0.001).
- **Palatal bone density (preop / immediate / 6 / 12 mo)**: G1 284.77±122.43 / 580.82±213.56 / 802.97±361.59 / 868.68±559.54; G2 317.46±169 / 720.11±230.99 / 780.51±282.99 / 864.12±309.29; P = 0.53 / 0.08 / 0.84 / 0.97; within-group increase significant (P < 0.001).
- **Buccal MBL (6 / 12 mo)**: G1 0.34±0.34 / 0.47±0.45 mm; G2 0.54±0.25 / 0.70±0.16 mm; P = 0.06 / 0.06. Within-group: G1 NS (P=0.23), G2 significant (P < 0.001).
- **Palatal MBL (6 / 12 mo)**: G1 0.53±0.83 / 0.62±0.75 mm; G2 0.54±0.42 / 0.80±0.40 mm; P = 0.96 / 0.40. Within-group: G1 P=0.055, G2 P=0.005.
- **Survival**: every implant successful, no complications over the 12-month follow-up (100%).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (powered on implant stability only — n=16/group — with a 1.20 effect size drawn from a prior study), and short 12-month follow-up; the authors state larger samples and longer follow-up would give more reliable results.
- Single center, healed posterior-maxilla single-tooth sites only — results may not transfer to immediate-extraction or anterior sites.
- The claimed single-drilling advantages (less heat, less mechanical damage, shorter operative time) were asserted, not measured — no intraosseous temperature, drill-friction, or operative-time data were collected.
- Group II shows a borderline higher buccal MBL at both 6 and 12 months (P=0.06) and significant within-group MBL progression; whether short single-drill protocols trade stability for slightly greater crestal loss needs larger studies.

## 6. Related Work
- Sierra-Rebolledo et al. 2021 (RCT, immediate tapered implants, anterior maxilla): undersized drilling did **not** improve ISQ/insertion torque vs conventional drilling — this paper extends the same theme (no UD advantage) to healed posterior maxilla under immediate loading, but with a different comparator (single drilling instead of conventional sequential drilling).
- Kim 2019 (Korean patent KR 10-2304707, (주)해냄/Haenaem Co.): double-spiral condensing-screw design — the company's design background; this study is a separate HaeNaem product line (One Drill System Kit, single-drilling drills).
- El-Kholey et al. 2019 (SR, 15 papers): UD, osteotome, piezosurgery, and osseodensification all raise primary stability in low-density bone, but long-term survival superiority is weakly supported — consistent with this RCT's finding of equivalence rather than superiority.
- Gehrke et al. 2021 (in-vitro, polyurethane): UD benefit was density-dependent, absent at the lowest simulated density — matching this clinical equivalence in soft posterior-maxilla bone.
- Changrani et al. 2024 (HaeNaem Zero Bone Loss Kit, Cureus) — **(retracted/철회됨 — 인용 금지)**: a different HaeNaem product's indirect sinus-lift paper; mentioned only for manufacturer context and must not be cited for any claim.

## 7. Glossary
- **축경 드릴링 (undersized drilling, UD)**: osteotomy prepared with the manufacturer sequence minus the final drill, leaving the bed narrower than the implant diameter to increase primary stability.
- **단일 드릴링 (single drilling technique, SD)**: simplified osteotomy using one drill at the implant diameter (after a guiding drill) without sequential widening — here the HaeNaem Co. One Drill System Kit.
- **삽입 토크 (insertion torque, IT)**: torque recorded during implant seating (manual ratchet here); ≥ 35 Ncm in both groups.
- **임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)**: RFA-derived stability scale; > 70 in both groups across all time points.
- **변형 치은열구 출혈지수 (modified sulcus bleeding index, mBI)**: peri-implant soft-tissue bleeding index.
- **임플란트 주위 탐침 깊이 (peri-implant probing depth, PPD)**: sulcus depth around the implant; decreased significantly over time within both groups.
- **변연골 소실 (marginal bone loss, MBL)**: crestal bone height change on radiographs; largest at 12 months on the buccal side (G2 0.70 mm), between-group differences NS.
