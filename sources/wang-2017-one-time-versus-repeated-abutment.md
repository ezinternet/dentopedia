## Why Ingested

The **earliest pooled estimate** on the one-time vs repeated abutment question in platform-switched implants, and the historical anchor for how much the effect estimate has shrunk: its "superior" conclusion contrasts with the modest MD −0.23 mm of [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]] and the null RCTs it did not yet have. The near-title match to the 2026 SR+MA is a genuine sibling-document relationship, not a duplicate (DOI and study set differ).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of eight studies (seven RCTs, one controlled clinical study) comparing one-time abutment connection with repeated abutment connection in platform-switched implants; 197 versus 214 implants, with bone, soft-tissue, probing-depth and complication outcomes.

One-time abutment favoured vertical bone change at 6 months (SMD 0.41 fixed / 3.23 random), 12 months (1.51 / 14.81) and 3 years (2.47 / 2.47), and soft-tissue change (0.21 / 0.23); probing depth and post-surgical complications showed no significant difference.

The authors conclude one-time abutment is superior, but explicitly flag small samples and limited original-study quality — the implausibly large random-effects SMDs are a statistical fragility signal, so the direction is more trustworthy than the magnitude.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석(무작위대조시험 7편 + 대조임상시험 1편, 총 8편): 플랫폼 스위칭 임플란트에서 1회 지대주 연결군(197개) vs 반복 연결군(214개) 비교; 골·연조직 변화, 탐침 깊이, 합병증 결과.

1회 연결군이 수직 골변화에서 유리(6개월 SMD 0.41 고정/3.23 무작위, 12개월 1.51/14.81, 3년 2.47/2.47), 연조직 변화도 0.21/0.23으로 유리; 탐침 깊이(Probing Depth, PD)와 술 후 합병증은 유의차 없음.

저자는 1회 연결의 우위를 결론내리면서도 표본이 작고 원연구의 질이 제한적이라고 명시 — 비현실적으로 큰 무작위효과 SMD는 통계적 취약성 신호이므로 방향성만 신뢰하는 것이 타당.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: PLoS ONE. 2017;12(10):e0186385
- **DOI**: 10.1371/journal.pone.0186385
- **Institution**: College & Hospital of Stomatology, Anhui Medical University, Hefei, China
- **Study type**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (no PROSPERO registration reported)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First meta-analysis dedicated to one-time versus repeated abutment connection in platform-switched implants.
- Pools bone change at three time horizons (6 months, 12 months, 3 years) plus soft tissue, probing depth and complications.
- Formalises the terminology mess — "repeated abutment", "removable abutment", "abutment dis/reconnections" — under one intervention label.
- Concludes in favour of one-time abutment while openly calling for better RCTs.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis; structured search of PubMed, Embase and Web of Science.
- **Inclusion**: RCTs and controlled clinical studies comparing one-time vs repeated abutment in platform-switched implants.
- **Studies pooled**: bone 6 studies, soft tissue 3, probing depth 2, complications 4.
- **Implant systems**: Global, Ankylos, JDEvolution (JdentalCare), Straumann Bone Level, Conelog Screw-Line.
- **Statistics**: Fixed- and random-effects standardised mean differences (SMD) with 95% CI.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Fixed-effect SMD | Random-effects SMD | Studies pooled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical bone change, 6 months | 0.41 | 3.23 | 6 (bone total) |
| Vertical bone change, 12 months | 1.51 | 14.81 | 6 (bone total) |
| Vertical bone change, 3 years | 2.47 | 2.47 | 6 (bone total) |
| Soft-tissue change | 0.21 | 0.23 | 3 |
| Probing depth | NS | NS | 2 |
| Post-surgical complications | NS | NS | 4 |

- Implant counts: 197 (one-time) vs 214 (repeated).
- The fixed/random divergence at 6 and 12 months (0.41→3.23; 1.51→14.81) is internally implausible for a bone-change outcome; treat the numeric magnitude as unreliable, the direction as tentative.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Eight small studies with heterogeneous implant systems and follow-up windows; no study-quality grading reported beyond narrative caution.
- SMD rather than mm pooled for bone outcomes, and the reported random-effects values are extremely large — likely model/integer-scale artefact.
- Authors themselves state that future RCTs are needed because of small samples and limited research quality.
- Published 2017; all included studies predate the newer RCTs pooled in 2026 reviews, so this is a historical rather than current best estimate.

## 7. Glossary
- **One-time abutment**: definitive abutment connected once at surgery or stage-two and retained throughout prosthetic fabrication.
- **Repeated abutment**: healing/provisional abutment disconnected and reconnected for impressions, try-in and delivery.
- **SMD (Standardised Mean Difference)**: effect size in pooled standard-deviation units; scale instability here limits magnitude interpretation.
- **PS (Platform Switching)**: diameter-reduced abutment on a wider implant platform.
