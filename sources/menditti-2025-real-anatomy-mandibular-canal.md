## Why Ingested

기존 위키는 하악관(Mandibular Canal, MC) 변이를 nutrient canal 계열([[radiology/kaur-2019-evaluation-mandibular-anterior-nutrient]])과 bifid mandibular canal 계열([[radiology/wamasing-2018-imaging-bifid-mandibular-canal]])로 분리해서 다뤄왔다. 이 2025년 narrative review는 두 흐름을 하나의 해부학적 프레임워크로 통합 제안한다 — bifid MC, trifid MC, intramandibular accessory canal, dental canal의 4분류 + extramandibular buccal/lingual "nutrient function" canal을 별도 카테고리로 명확히 구분해야 한다는 주장. 다만 저널(*In Vivo*, Athens)은 상대적으로 낮은 임팩트의 학술지이며, 이 review는 formal SR 방법론(PRISMA, 위험비뚤림 평가 등)을 따르지 않은 narrative-review 수준이므로 권위 있는 결론이라기보다 "통합 분류 제안"으로만 취급해야 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (literature search 1931-2023, PubMed + Web of Science, ~50 full-text articles included) synthesizing anatomical variants of the mandibular canal (MC) and inferior alveolar neurovascular bundle (IANVB).

Proposes MC variants be classified into four categories — bifid MC, trifid MC, intramandibular accessory canals, and dental canals — while extramandibular buccal/lingual "nutrient function" canals (independent of the IAN, prevalence up to ~95% per Aps study) should be treated as a distinct, non-connected category requiring separate clinical consideration.

Clinical implication: CBCT (not panoramic radiography) is essential for pre-surgical/implant planning in the mandible to detect these variants and avoid hemorrhage, anesthesia failure (IANB), or nerve injury; key limitation is that this is a narrative (not systematic) review from a lower-tier journal with no formal quality appraisal of included studies.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰(1931-2023년, PubMed·Web of Science, 약 50편 전문 포함)을 통해 하악관(Mandibular Canal, MC) 및 하치조 신경혈관다발(Inferior Alveolar Neurovascular Bundle, IANVB)의 해부학적 변이를 종합한 서술적 review.

MC 변이를 이분지 하악관(Bifid Mandibular Canal, BMC), 삼분지 하악관(Trifid Mandibular Canal, TMC), 하악골내 부속관(intramandibular accessory canal), 치성관(dental canal) 4가지로 분류하고, 하악골외 협측/설측 "영양공급 기능(nutrient function)" 관(하치조신경과 독립적, Aps 연구 기준 유병률 최대 ~95%)은 별도 범주로 명확히 구분해야 한다고 제안.

임상적 의미: 파노라마(OPG)가 아닌 CBCT가 하악골 수술·임플란트 사전계획에서 이러한 변이를 발견해 출혈·하치조신경차단(IANB) 마취 실패·신경 손상을 예방하는 데 필수적이며, 핵심 한계는 이 논문이 formal 체계적 문헌고찰이 아닌 서술적 review이고 낮은 임팩트 저널(In Vivo)에서 발표되어 포함 연구에 대한 공식적 질 평가가 없다는 점.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates embryology, gross anatomy, and radiological literature on the mandibular canal (MC) and its variants into one integrated discussion.
- Proposes a working classification of MC variants into four types: **bifid MC**, **trifid MC**, **intramandibular accessory canals**, and **dental canals**.
- Argues that **extramandibular buccal/lingual canals** (nutrient-function canals independent of the inferior alveolar nerve, IAN) should be treated as a distinct category from intramandibular MC variants because they have no connection to the MC and different clinical implications.
- Critiques the widely used Naitoh (2009) and Langlais classifications, arguing that folding the retromolar canal (RMC) into "bifid MC" statistics inflates reported BMC prevalence (RMC alone reported 40.4-70% in some series).
- Recommends CBCT over panoramic radiography (OPG) as the appropriate imaging modality for detecting these variants pre-operatively.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Literature search of PubMed and Web of Science databases spanning 1931-2023, using keywords: "mandibular canal; retromolar canal; anatomic mandibular canal variations; bifid and trifid mandibular canal; accessory bucco-lingual branches." Abstracts were screened for relevance; articles without sufficient abstract information were excluded. An additional 17 articles (including etiopathogenesis papers) were manually added outside the database search. Of 50 studies deemed eligible, full texts were retrieved and evaluated for final inclusion. No formal PRISMA methodology, dual-reviewer screening, or risk-of-bias/quality appraisal tool is reported — this is a narrative synthesis, not a systematic review.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- MC anatomy: divided into posterior (retromolar triangle, RMT), middle (contains mental foramen, MF), and anterior (contains incisal canal) segments; average length 65-70 mm; diameter tapers from ~2.5 mm (third molar region) to ~2 mm (premolar region).
- Reported BMC prevalence varies drastically by imaging modality: 0.08-1.2% on OPG in some studies, up to 38% (range 10-44%) in others, and >65% on CBCT.
- Retromolar canal (RMC) reported bifidity as high as 40.4-70% in some series when misclassified as BMC Type I under Naitoh's system — the authors argue this should be considered a normal anatomical variant of the IAN rather than a true "duplication."
- Aps study cited: extramandibular buccal/lingual nutrient canals found in 95% of specimens, with no patient having zero foramina/accessory canals; no significant sex-based difference in number or diameter.
- Naitoh's (2009) CBCT-based classification: Type 1 = RMC, Type 2 = subdental plexuses, Type 3 = forward canals in mandibular body, Type 4 = buccolingual canals (~2% prevalence, described as infrequent).
- No significant geographic, gender, or age-based differences reported for MC/BMC diameter across the literature reviewed.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative (not systematic) review published in a lower-impact journal (*In Vivo*) — no PRISMA diagram, no dual independent screening reported, no formal risk-of-bias/quality appraisal of the ~50 included studies. Conclusions should be read as an expert synthesis/classification proposal, not as pooled quantitative evidence.
- The proposed 4-type + extramandibular-canal classification is presented as a recommendation for future research, not yet validated against a large CBCT cohort by these authors.
- Authors explicitly call for new studies using modern CBCT on large mandible samples to establish accurate prevalence data, particularly for RMC frequency and its true clinical/surgical implications.
- MRI is mentioned as a possible complementary modality but noted as potentially unable to resolve the contents of small bony canals even at high resolution.
