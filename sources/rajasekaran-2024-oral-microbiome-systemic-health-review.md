## Why Ingested

Comprehensive review of the oral microbiome's role in oral and systemic health, covering novel associations with GI, cardiovascular, endocrinal, neurological, autoimmune diseases, and cancer — provides the broadest systemic disease coverage for the oral dysbiosis category, complementing [[oral-microbiology/hu-2024-oral-microbiome-bone-related-diseases-homeostasis]] (bone diseases) and [[oral-microbiology/baker-2024-oral-microbiome-diversity-biogeography-health]].

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of the oral microbiome ecosystem (bacteria, fungi, archaea, viruses), its dysbiosis-driven oral diseases (caries, gingivitis, periodontitis, candidiasis, halitosis), and novel systemic associations including cardiovascular, gastrointestinal, endocrinal, neurological, autoimmune diseases, and cancer.

Reviews efficacy of microbiome-modulating interventions: dental probiotics (competitive exclusion, antimicrobial production), xylitol (mutacin inhibition), oral rinses (CHX, fluoride, essential oils), oil pulling, and peptide-based antimicrobials.

Emphasizes personalized oral care strategies based on microbiome profiling, positioning the oral cavity as a critical window into systemic health and a therapeutic target for both local and systemic disease prevention.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Maps the full spectrum of oral microbiome–systemic disease associations in a single review.
- Novel associations: oral microbiome → neurological conditions (Alzheimer's — P. gingivalis), autoimmune diseases (RA, lupus), and cancer (oral, colorectal, pancreatic).
- Reviews 7 categories of microbiome-modulating interventions with mechanism-based discussion.
- Historical perspective: caries/periodontal disease ancient history vs modern prevalence.

## 3. Key Content Areas

**Oral disease mechanisms:**
- Caries: S. mutans virulence (glucosyltransferases, acid production, biofilm)
- Periodontitis: keystone pathogen P. gingivalis, red complex dysbiosis
- Candidiasis: C. albicans hyphae + Streptococcal cross-kingdom interactions
- Halitosis: VSC-producing anaerobes (Fusobacterium, Prevotella, Treponema)

**Systemic associations:**
- Cardiovascular: periodontal bacteria → atherosclerosis, endocarditis
- GI: H. pylori oral reservoir, esophageal/colorectal cancer links
- Endocrine: bidirectional diabetes ↔ dysbiosis
- Neurological: P. gingivalis in Alzheimer's brain (gingipains), dementia links
- Autoimmune: periodontal bacteria → RA (citrullination), lupus
- Cancer: OSCC, pancreatic cancer, CRC (F. nucleatum)

**Interventions:**
- Probiotics: L. reuteri, L. rhamnosus — competitive exclusion, immune modulation
- Xylitol: non-fermentable sugar, mutacin inhibition, S. mutans suppression
- CHX, fluoride, essential oils: broad antimicrobial but microbiome-disruptive
- Oil pulling: sesame/coconut oil — limited evidence, mechanism unclear
- Peptides: bacteriocins, defensins — targeted antimicrobials

## 4. Limitations

- Narrative review — no systematic search or PRISMA protocol
- Industry-affiliated authors (Vibrant Sciences) — potential bias
- Variable evidence quality across systemic associations (observational mostly)
- Causal vs. associational links often conflated

## 5. Glossary

- **VSC**: Volatile sulfur compounds (halitosis markers)
- **P. gingivalis**: Porphyromonas gingivalis (keystone periodontal pathogen)
- **CHX**: Chlorhexidine
- **RA**: Rheumatoid arthritis
- **OSCC**: Oral squamous cell carcinoma
- **F. nucleatum**: Fusobacterium nucleatum (links oral to CRC)
- **CRC**: Colorectal cancer
