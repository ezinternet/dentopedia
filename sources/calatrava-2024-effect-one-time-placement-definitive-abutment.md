## Why Ingested

Calatrava 2024는 Abayov 2026 SR+MA의 포함 논문 7편 중 통계적으로 가장 강한 단일 신호(p=0.008, MBL gap 0.52mm)를 제공한 RCT다. [[implants/mbl/abayov-2026-one-abutment-one-time-versus]]의 데이터 테이블에서 이미 수치로 인용되어 있으며, 독립 페이지로 보유함으로써 OAOT × PS × conical 패러다임 아래 인용 추적 가능성을 확보한다.

## Three-line Summary

Single-center RCT (80 implants, 40 patients) comparing one-abutment-one-time (OAOT) vs 3 abutment disconnections over 12 months; bone-level platform-switched conical-connection implants (ConelogScrew-Line, Camlog) in the posterior jaw; primary outcomes were MBL and soft tissue changes.

OAOT group: MBL −0.13 mm at 6 months vs −0.61 mm in the disconnection group (p = 0.008); at 12 months: −0.53 mm (OAOT) vs −0.01 mm (control); keratinized tissue width, probing depth, and bleeding on probing also significantly better in OAOT group.

OAOT protocol provides clinically meaningful bone preservation (0.52 mm at 6 months; 0.54 mm advantage at 12 months) in PS conical-connection implants, with improvements across multiple soft-tissue parameters; the largest individual signal in the Abayov 2026 SR+MA.

## 세줄요약

## 2. Key Contributions
- Strongest individual OAOT signal in Abayov 2026 SR+MA: p = 0.008 at 6 months
- MBL advantage: −0.52 mm at 6 months; −0.54 mm at 12 months (OAOT vs repeated disconnections)
- Soft tissue outcomes also significantly better: keratinized tissue width, probing depth, bleeding on probing
- Bone-level PS conical-connection implants (Camlog ConelogScrew-Line) with immediate loading
- 80 implants, 40 patients; 12-month follow-up

## 3. Methodology
- Design: single-center parallel RCT
- Population: 40 patients needing 2 implants; posterior jaw
- Implants: Camlog ConelogScrew-Line (bone-level PS conical connection)
- Control: 3 abutment disconnections during healing/prosthetic phases
- OAOT: definitive abutment placed at implant surgery; never removed
- Outcomes: MBL (primary), KT width, probing depth, BOP at 6 and 12 months

## 4. Key Results
- 6-month MBL: OAOT −0.13 mm vs Control −0.61 mm (p = 0.008; Δ = 0.48 mm)
- 12-month MBL: OAOT −0.53 mm vs Control −0.01 mm (Δ = 0.54 mm; Abayov SR+MA uses these values)
- KT width: significantly greater in OAOT at all timepoints
- Probing depth: lower in OAOT group
- Bleeding on probing: lower in OAOT group
- Survival: 100% in both groups at 12 months

## 5. Limitations
- Single center; 12-month follow-up only
- Relatively small sample (n=40 patients / 80 implants)
- Camlog system only; generalizability to other conical-connection systems uncertain
- Abutment geometry and height may have confounded soft tissue outcomes

## 7. Glossary
- **OAOT**: One-Abutment-One-Time — definitive abutment placed at implant surgery, never removed
- **PS**: Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭)
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss (변연골 소실)
- **KT**: Keratinized tissue (각화치은) — keratinized gingiva/mucosa width around implant
- **BOP**: Bleeding on probing (탐침 시 출혈)
