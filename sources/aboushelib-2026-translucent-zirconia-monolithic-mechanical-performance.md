## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (n=160 bar specimens; 3Y-PSZ vs 4Y-PSZ; 4 surface treatments: polishing/glass bead abrasion/alumina abrasion/grinding; conventional vs speed sintering; ±2M cycle fatigue; 4-point bending + Weibull statistics) examining sintering protocol × surface treatment interactions on flexural strength and reliability.

Glass bead treatment significantly improves 3Y-PSZ strength (p<0.001); grinding significantly reduces both types (p<0.001); speed sintering does not affect 3Y-PSZ (p=0.10) but significantly decreases 4Y-PSZ strength (p<0.001); after fatigue, 3Y-PSZ stable while 4Y-PSZ significantly reduced (p<0.001).

3Y-PSZ is more suitable for high-stress applications and tolerates speed sintering; 4Y-PSZ requires careful sintering protocol and surface treatment selection; grinding (chairside occlusal adjustment) must be minimized and followed by polishing to prevent significant strength reduction.

## 세줄요약

3Y-PSZ vs 4Y-PSZ 굴곡강도 실험(n=160 막대 시편; 4가지 표면처리; 일반/속성 소결; 200만 회 피로): 유리구슬 처리 → 3Y-PSZ 강도 유의 증가(p<0.001); 연삭 → 양쪽 모두 유의 감소(p<0.001).

속성 소결은 3Y-PSZ에 무영향(p=0.10)이나 4Y-PSZ 강도 유의 감소(p<0.001); 피로 하중 후 3Y-PSZ 안정, 4Y-PSZ 유의 감소(p<0.001); Weibull 계수: 연마된 4Y-PSZ(12.8), 유리구슬 3Y-PSZ(12.9) 최고.

3Y-PSZ는 고응력 임상 부위 및 속성 소결에 적합; 4Y-PSZ는 소결·표면처리 프로토콜 엄격 선택 필요; 의자 옆 연삭(chairside grinding) 후 반드시 연마 추가.

## One-line Summary

In vitro (n=160 specimens, 3Y-PSZ vs 4Y-PSZ, 4 surface treatments, conventional vs speed sintering) showing glass bead treatment improves 3Y-PSZ strength, speed sintering reduces 4Y-PSZ strength, and 3Y-PSZ is more reliable for high-stress applications.

3Y-PSZ vs 4Y-PSZ 지르코니아 굴곡강도 실험(n=160, 4가지 표면처리, 일반/속성 소결): 유리구슬 처리 시 3Y-PSZ 강도↑, 속성 소결 시 4Y-PSZ 강도↓; 반복하중 후 3Y-PSZ 안정 — 고응력 부위는 3Y-PSZ 권장.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that 3Y-PSZ is more tolerant to speed sintering (no significant strength reduction) while 4Y-PSZ is not
- Glass bead treatment (50 µm) significantly improves 3Y-PSZ flexural strength (p<0.001)
- Grinding significantly reduces flexural strength in BOTH zirconia types (p<0.001) — clinical grinding during adjustments is hazardous
- After 2 million cyclic loading: 3Y-PSZ maintains stable strength; 4Y-PSZ exhibits significant reduction (p<0.001)
- Weibull modulus: highest in polished 4Y-PSZ (12.8) and glass-bead treated 3Y-PSZ (12.9) — most reliable options

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design:** In vitro experimental
- **Specimens:** 160 bar-shaped specimens (3Y-PSZ + 4Y-PSZ, n=20 per subgroup)
- **Surface treatments:** (1) Polishing (control), (2) airborne-particle abrasion with 50 µm glass beads, (3) alumina abrasion, (4) grinding
- **Sintering protocols:** Conventional vs speed sintering
- **Fatigue protocol:** Half specimens tested initial; half after 2 million cyclic loading
- **Tests:** 4-point bending test; Weibull statistics; SEM microstructure; ANOVA + Bonferroni post-hoc (α=0.05)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Condition | 3Y-PSZ | 4Y-PSZ |
|---|---|---|
| Glass bead treatment | Significantly improved (p<0.001) | No significant change |
| Grinding | Significantly reduced (p<0.001) | Significantly reduced (p<0.001) |
| Speed sintering | No significant effect (p=0.10) | Significantly decreased (p<0.001) |
| After cyclic loading | Stable (p=0.082) | Significantly reduced (p<0.001) |
| Weibull modulus (highest) | Glass bead: 12.9 | Polished: 12.8 |

**Clinical implications:**
- Speed sintering compatible with 3Y-PSZ (time-efficient, no strength penalty)
- Speed sintering for 4Y-PSZ requires careful protocol selection
- Avoid clinical grinding; prefer polishing for surface finishing

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro — does not replicate full oral environment (saliva, thermal cycling)
- Bar specimens vs actual crown geometry — stress distribution may differ clinically
- Limited to 3Y and 4Y subtypes; 5Y data not included
