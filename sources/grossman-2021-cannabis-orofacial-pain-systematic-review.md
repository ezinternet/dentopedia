## Why Ingested

Held TMD pages cover manual therapy, counselling, splints and botulinum toxin for myofascial pain but no cannabinoid option; this SR fills that pharmacologic gap and adds a negative acute-pain signal for oral cannabinoid agonists after third molar surgery. It extends [[wiki/tmj/chan-2022-myogenous-tmd-diagnosis-treatment-review]] (pharmacotherapy of myogenous TMD) and sits beside [[wiki/tmj/demelo-2020-manual-therapy-myofascial-pain-tmd-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis stated in the abstract; Br J Oral Maxillofac Surg) of cannabis-based products for medicinal use (CBPM) on orofacial pain and inflammation.

Topical dermal cannabidiol (CBD) showed positive findings (reduced pain, improved muscle function) in myofascial pain, whereas two oral synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists (AZD1940, GW842166) showed no significant analgesia after surgical third molar removal.

Orofacial-specific literature is sparse; high-quality evidence for cannabinoids exists only for chronic nociceptive/neuropathic pain elsewhere, so orofacial use is not yet evidence-supported.

## 세줄요약

칸나비노이드 기반 의료용 제품 (Cannabis-Based Products for Medicinal Use, CBPM)이 구강안면통 (orofacial pain)과 염증에 미치는 영향을 본 체계적 문헌고찰 (초록 기준 메타분석 언급 없음).

근막통증 (myofascial pain) 환자에서 국소 칸나비디올 (Cannabidiol, CBD) 제형은 통증 감소와 근기능 개선을 보였으나, 경구 합성 칸나비노이드 수용체 작용제 (AZD1940, GW842166)는 제3대구치 발치 후 유의한 진통효과를 보이지 않았다.

구강안면 영역 문헌은 매우 적고 고수준 근거는 타 부위 만성 침해수용성·신경병증성 통증에 한정되어, 구강안면통 적용은 아직 근거가 불충분하다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Reviews natural and synthetic CBPM for orofacial pain and inflammation.
- Topical dermal CBD: positive findings in myofascial pain.
- AZD1940 and GW842166 (oral synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists): no significant analgesia after surgical third molar removal.
