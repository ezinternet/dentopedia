## Why Ingested
Pairs a head-to-head RCT of bone-level vs tissue-level same-manufacturer implants with ISQ/ITV stability trajectories — the ISQ literature here is dense with design comparisons (tapered vs cylindrical, e.g. [[wiki/implants/isq/de-elio-2023-insertion-torque-resonance-frequency-tapered-parallel-implants]]) but thin on the BL-vs-TL platform axis; this pilot fills that gap and independently re-tests the ITV–ISQ correlation probed by [[wiki/implants/isq/do-vale-souza-2021-relation-insertion-torque-implant-stability]].

## Three-line Summary
Pilot parallel-arm RCT (21 patients, 90 implants: 44 bone-level [BL], 46 tissue-level [TL]; BioHorizons, Univ. of Barcelona) comparing insertion torque value (ITV) and implant stability quotient (ISQ, Penguin RFA, horizontal and vertical) at placement and after 90 days.
No significant ITV difference (BL 36.0 ± 11.5 vs TL 38.0 ± 11.5 Ncm, p = 0.381) or baseline ISQ; at 90 days horizontal ISQ was marginally higher for TL (66.5 ± 20.9 vs 57.1 ± 18.9, p = 0.029); ITV correlated with baseline ISQ (r = 0.493, p < 0.001) but not with 90-day ISQ.
Implant design (BL vs TL) does not govern primary stability, and secondary stability depends on biological healing rather than initial mechanical engagement — a feasibility pilot with no a priori power calculation.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Same-manufacturer direct comparison of BL vs TL implant designs (identical intraosseous macrogeometry, thread, surface, and osteotomy protocol — design axis isolated to collar/platform and microgap location).
- Reports no significant ITV or baseline ISQ difference between BL and TL, but a marginal 90-day horizontal ISQ advantage for TL.
- Confirms a moderate ITV–baseline ISQ correlation (r = 0.493) that dissociates from 90-day ISQ — secondary stability is not predicted by initial torque.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Parallel-arm pilot RCT (randomized via Randomizer Numbers app v1.4.0)
- **n**: 21 patients (13 M, 8 F; age 19–84) / 90 implants (44 BL, 46 TL); no implants lost
- **Setting**: University of Barcelona Dental Hospital (HOUB)
- **Implants**: BioHorizons Camlog; tapered internal hexagon, aggressive buttress threads, laser-lock neck, resorbable blast-textured body; BL 1.5 mm internal conical connection (placed flush with crest), TL 45° conical hexagon + 2.3 mm transmucosal neck (placed supracrustal); diameters 3.0–4.6 mm, lengths 7.5–12 mm
- **RFA**: Penguin device, MulTi-peg ~5 Ncm; ISQ horizontal and vertical at baseline (placement) and 90 days (flap re-entry)
- **Outcomes**: ITV (Ncm) at placement; ISQ-H and ISQ-V at baseline and 90 days; ITV–ISQ correlation
- **Statistics**: Shapiro–Wilk normality (non-normal), Mann–Whitney U, Spearman correlation, Holm correction, linear mixed-effects models adjusted for patient-level clustering; α = 0.05 two-tailed; no a priori sample size calculation (feasibility trial)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- ITV: BL 36.0 ± 11.5 vs TL 38.0 ± 11.5 Ncm, p = 0.381 (not significant; no difference)
- Mandible torque > maxilla: 40.8 ± 9.3 vs 34.5 ± 12.5 Ncm, p = 0.001
- Baseline ISQ-H: p = 0.928; baseline ISQ-V: p = 0.874 (no difference)
- 90-day ISQ-H: TL 66.5 ± 20.9 vs BL 57.1 ± 18.9, p = 0.029 (marginal; not significant at 90-day ISQ-V, p = 0.677; overall 90-day ISQ p = 0.073)
- Jaws: BL maxilla/mandible 20/24; TL maxilla/mandible 16/30
- ITV–baseline ISQ: r = 0.493, p < 0.001; ITV–90-day ISQ: not significant
- Complications: none; low incidence of inflammation; uneventful postoperative course

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Pilot/feasibility study — no a priori power calculation; 90 implants across 21 patients (patient-level clustering)
- Marginal single significant difference (90-day ISQ-H, p = 0.029) after multiple comparison handling; internal tables report an uncorrected Mann–Whitney torque p of 0.533 vs 0.381 elsewhere
- Only early stability (90 days) measured; no radiographic crestal-bone/soft-tissue outcomes; mixed jaw/site distributions, implant lengths/diameters varied
- Single-center, one manufacturer/geometry — no microgap-position biologic outcome directly assessed

## 7. Glossary
- **ITV (Insertion Torque Value, 삽입 토크 값)**: Frictional resistance measured in Ncm during implant seating; index of primary stability
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient, 임플란트 안정성 지수)**: 1–100 scale from resonance frequency analysis (RFA); 57–82 indicates optimal anchorage
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis, 공진주파수분석)**: Non-invasive stability measurement using transducer/peg resonance (here Penguin device)
- **BL (Bone-Level, 골-레벨)**: Implant whose platform sits at the bone crest; microgap intraosseous/supracrustal at connection
- **TL (Tissue-Level, 티슈-레벨)**: Implant with a transmucosal collar (here 2.3 mm); microgap supragingival
- **Primary vs secondary stability**: Initial mechanical interlock (torque-derived) vs biological osseointegration-modulated stability during healing
