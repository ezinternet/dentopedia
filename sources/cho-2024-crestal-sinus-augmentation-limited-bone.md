## Why Ingested

Existing transcrestal sinus augmentation literature typically requires residual bone height (RBH) ≥4–5 mm; this paper directly addresses the clinical gap of RBH <3 mm with a crestal kit approach, extending the conventional indication threshold. Ingested to expand the evidence base alongside [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/decker-2024-clinical-radiographic-transcrestal-sinus-augmentation]] and [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/kadkhodazadeh-2024-transcrestal-maxillary-sinus-elevation-sr-ma]], which collectively benchmark crestal-approach outcomes across residual bone categories.

## Three-line Summary

Observational retrospective study evaluating crestal approach sinus augmentation in 11 patients with residual alveolar bone height <3 mm using a specialized crestal approach kit (Shinhung/Osstem) with xenograft (Bio-Oss) at Seoul St. Mary's Hospital, Korea.

Vertical bone height increased from 2.1 ± 0.5 mm to 8.8 ± 0.9 mm (Δ6.7 mm, p<0.001); bucco-palatal width increased from 10.6 ± 2.9 mm to 12.2 ± 2.2 mm (p<0.01); no significant membrane perforations or complications reported.

Crestal sinus augmentation can effectively achieve adequate bone height even in patients with <3 mm RBH, suggesting expansion of the traditional 5–8 mm indication range, though small sample size (n=11) and short follow-up (~7 months, no implant survival data) limit generalizability.

## 세줄요약

수직 골높이 2.1 ± 0.5 mm → 8.8 ± 0.9 mm (Δ6.7 mm, p<0.001), 협-구개 폭경 (Bucco-Palatal Width) 10.6 ± 2.9 mm → 12.2 ± 2.2 mm (p<0.01); 슈나이더막 (Schneiderian membrane) 천공 및 심각한 합병증 없음.

잔존골 <3 mm에서도 치조정 접근 상악동 거상으로 충분한 골 높이 확보 가능하나 소규모 (n=11), 단기 추적 (~7개월), 대조군 없음, 임플란트 생존율 데이터 부재로 일반화 제한적.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Medicine (Baltimore), 103(45): e40331
- **DOI**: 10.1097/MD.0000000000040331
- **Institution**: Department of Periodontics, Seoul St. Mary's Hospital, College of Medicine, The Catholic University of Korea
- **Study design**: Retrospective observational study
- **n**: 11 patients (9 male, 2 female; mean age 63.6 ± 10.9 years)
- **Follow-up**: 202.3 ± 37.7 days post-augmentation (before implant placement)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates clinical feasibility of transcrestal sinus augmentation (TSFE) with RBH <3 mm — traditionally considered a contraindication for crestal approach and referred to lateral window technique
- Reports statistically significant gains in vertical bone height (Δ6.7 mm, p<0.001) and bucco-palatal width (Δ1.6 mm, p<0.01)
- Uses a staged protocol: sinus augmentation first, then implant placement after ~202 days healing — no simultaneous implant data reported
- Introduces the concept that the crestal kit (Shinhung/Osstem) allows membrane elevation under hydraulic or mechanical pressure even with minimal residual bone

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Population**: 11 patients requiring posterior maxillary implants with RBH <3 mm confirmed by CBCT (콘빔 CT, Cone-Beam Computed Tomography)
- **Intervention**: Crestal approach sinus augmentation using a specialized kit (Shinhung/Osstem); xenograft (Bio-Oss) with collagen membrane placed via osteotomy
- **Measurements**: CBCT analysis pre-operatively and post-augmentation (mean 202 days); vertical bone height, bucco-palatal width, mesio-distal width
- **Statistical analysis**: Wilcoxon signed-rank test (appropriate for small non-parametric paired samples)
- **No control group**: all patients received crestal approach regardless of surgeon preference for lateral window

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Dimension | Pre-op | Post-op | Change | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical bone height | 2.1 ± 0.5 mm | 8.8 ± 0.9 mm | +6.7 mm | <0.001 |
| Bucco-palatal width | 10.6 ± 2.9 mm | 12.2 ± 2.2 mm | +1.6 mm | <0.01 |
| Mesio-distal width | 9.9 ± 1.2 mm | 10.2 ± 1.2 mm | +0.3 mm | >0.05 (NS) |

- Membrane perforation: none reported
- Postoperative complications: none reported
- Final vertical bone height 8.8 mm adequate for implant placement per authors

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample size (n=11) — insufficient statistical power for subgroup analysis or complication rate estimation
- Short-term follow-up (~6–7 months); no implant placement or survival data reported
- No control group (no comparison to lateral window approach with same RBH)
- Single center, single surgeon — generalizability limited
- No histologic confirmation of graft integration or bone quality
- Retrospective design subject to selection bias

## 7. Glossary

- **TSFE** (Transcrestal Sinus Floor Elevation): 치조정 접근 상악동 거상술 — crestal approach variant of sinus floor elevation
- **RBH** (Residual Bone Height): 잔존골 높이 — alveolar bone height from crestal ridge to sinus floor
- **CBCT** (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography): 콘빔 CT — 3D dental imaging used for bone measurement
- **Xenograft**: 이종골 — bone graft material from another species (Bio-Oss = deproteinized bovine bone mineral)
- **Bucco-palatal width**: 협-구개 폭경 — horizontal dimension of the augmented sinus
- **Staged approach**: 2단계 접근법 — sinus augmentation first, implant placement at a later date
