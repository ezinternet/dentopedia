## Why Ingested

Part of the implant-iip-loading surveillance batch, found by manual PDF discovery. Provides a molar-region, diameter-selection-strategy contrast to the anterior-esthetic-zone timing trials in this wiki (Puisys 2022, Block 2009, Chan 2019) — notably, this trial's conclusion (delayed placement wins on esthetics) runs opposite to the anterior-zone pattern, worth surfacing as population-specific rather than a general contradiction; see [[wiki/immediate-implant/puisys-2022-immediate-implant-placement-vs-early]].

## Three-line Summary
RCT (n=100, molar extraction sites, one center) comparing immediate placement of 6.0–8.0mm wide-diameter implants against 4-month ridge preservation (porcine bone substitute + collagen barrier) followed by delayed placement of conventional 4.0–5.0mm diameter implants, followed to 1 year post-loading.

Implant failure 5/47 (10.6%) immediate vs 2/44 (4.6%) delayed (NS, P=.436); Pink Esthetic Score significantly favored delayed at both 4 months (9.65 vs 10.44, P=.03) and 1 year (9.71 vs 10.86, P=.02); 1-year marginal bone loss significantly greater for immediate (1.06mm vs 0.63mm, P<.0001); immediate placement required significantly fewer visits (7.48 vs 10.30, P<.001) and surgical interventions (2.14 vs 3.08, P<.001).

Authors' own conclusion: immediate placement of wide-diameter implants in molar sockets yielded inferior esthetic outcomes compared to ridge preservation + delayed conventional-diameter placement — an efficiency-vs-esthetics trade-off opposite in direction to some anterior-esthetic-zone timing trials, reflecting the different clinical scenario (molar site, wide-diameter implant selection) rather than a general contradiction of the immediate-placement literature.

## 세줄요약
RCT(n=100, 대구치 발치부위, 단일기관) — 즉시 6.0–8.0mm 광경implant 식립 vs 4개월 치조제보존(돈유래골이식+콜라겐차단막) 후 지연 4.0–5.0mm 통상직경 식립 비교, 부하 후 1년 추적.

임플란트 실패 5/47(10.6%) 즉시 vs 2/44(4.6%) 지연(무의미, P=.436); 핑크심미지수(PES)는 4개월(9.65 vs 10.44, P=.03)·1년(9.71 vs 10.86, P=.02) 모두 지연군 유의 우위; 1년 변연골소실은 즉시군이 유의하게 큼(1.06 vs 0.63mm, P<.0001); 즉시군은 내원횟수(7.48 vs 10.30, P<.001)·수술개입(2.14 vs 3.08, P<.001) 유의하게 적음.

저자 결론: 대구치 소켓의 광경즉시임플란트는 치조제보존+지연 통상직경 식립 대비 심미 결과가 열등 — 전치 심미부 타이밍 시험 일부와 반대 방향의 효율-심미 트레이드오프이며, 즉시식립 문헌 전반에 대한 일반 반박이라기보다 특정 임상 시나리오(구치부·광경직경 선택)의 소견으로 해석해야 함.

## Key Contributions
- RCT-level evidence (rather than case series) directly comparing a wide-diameter-immediate vs conventional-diameter-delayed strategy specifically in the molar region
- Reports a clear efficiency trade-off (fewer visits/surgeries for immediate) against an esthetic/bone-level trade-off (worse for immediate) — quantifying both sides of the decision rather than only survival
- Explicit authors' conclusion favoring delayed placement in this specific scenario, a useful counterweight to anterior-zone literature favoring immediate timing

## Methodology
RCT, single center. 100 patients with 1–2 molar teeth requiring extraction (no vertical buccal bone loss vs palatal wall), randomised to immediate placement of 6.0–8.0mm-diameter implants (n=50) or ridge preservation (porcine bone substitute + resorbable collagen barrier) followed 4 months later by delayed placement of 4.0–5.0mm-diameter implants (n=50). Bone-to-implant gaps in the immediate group filled with autogenous bone from trephine drilling. Loading 4 months after placement (either protocol) with fixed provisional, then definitive metal-ceramic restoration 4 months later. Outcomes: implant failure, complications, PES, marginal bone level change, patient satisfaction, visits, surgical interventions. Followed to 1 year post-loading.

## Key Results
| Outcome | Immediate wide-diameter | Delayed conventional-diameter | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failures | 5/47 (10.6%) | 2/44 (4.6%) | .436 (NS) |
| Complications (patients) | 10 | 4 | .084 (NS) |
| PES at 4 months | 9.65 ± 1.62 | 10.44 ± 1.47 | .03 |
| PES at 1 year | 9.71 ± 2.71 | 10.86 ± 1.37 | .02 |

## Limitations
- Abstract-only (PubMed capture); randomization/allocation-concealment method not retrieved.
- Single-center trial; molar-region/wide-diameter-specific — results should not be generalized to anterior single-tooth or standard-diameter scenarios.
- Authors describe this as "preliminary" 1-year data.

## Glossary
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score)**: composite index of peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics.
- **Ridge preservation**: socket grafting at extraction to limit post-extraction ridge resorption before delayed implant placement.
