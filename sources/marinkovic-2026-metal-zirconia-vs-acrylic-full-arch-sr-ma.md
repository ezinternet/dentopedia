## Why Ingested

No held SR+MA directly compared metal-zirconia (metallic substructure + monolithic zirconia superstructure) vs metal-acrylic full-arch screw-retained prostheses with quantitative pooled estimates for wear, peri-implantitis, and marginal bone loss. This fills the material-comparison gap with Level I evidence.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 12 studies (11 in MA) comparing metal-zirconia vs metal-acrylic full-arch implant-supported screw-retained prostheses; ROBINS-I bias assessment; GRADE certainty evaluation; search 2000–2025 across CENTRAL, PubMed, Embase, Epistemonikos.

Metal-zirconia showed significantly lower prosthetic wear (RR=0.29; 95% CI 0.14–0.59), reduced peri-implantitis risk (RR=0.67; 95% CI 0.47–0.96), and favored MBL (SMD=0.36); prosthesis survival trend RR=1.05, loss RR=0.09; no significant differences in implant survival, framework fractures, esthetics, or speech; most studies had moderate-to-critical RoB.

Metal-zirconia demonstrates superior biological and prosthetic wear profile over metal-acrylic; RCTs with long-term follow-up needed to confirm survival advantages given high RoB in current evidence base.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled RR estimates for wear, peri-implantitis, and survival: metal-zirconia significantly better on wear (RR=0.29) and peri-implantitis (RR=0.67)
- MBL difference SMD=0.36 favoring metal-zirconia
- No significant difference in implant survival, framework fractures — material switch does not compromise implant integrity
- Highlights persistent high RoB in comparative studies: findings informative but not conclusive for survival
