## Source Summary

Cross-sectional study comparing 3D-measured crown angulations of maxillary second molars in untreated adolescents (UMGS, n=41, mean age 14.9 y) against Andrews' historical manual measurements from untreated Class I subjects with normal occlusion.

**Key measurements (UMGS vs Andrews):**

| Parameter | UMGS | Andrews | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mesiodistal crown angulation (tip) | −18.9° (distal) | +0.4° (mesial) | <0.0001 |
| Transversal crown inclination (torque) | −10.6° (lingual) | −8.0° (lingual) | 0.0118 |

**Methods:** Digital dental casts (3D Slicer software) from UMGS archive; mixed effect models with group, arch side, and group×side interaction as fixed effects.

**Conclusion:** Fully erupted upper second molars in normal occlusion show significantly more distal crown tip and lingual inclination than Andrews' values, suggesting that tip prescription for preadjusted appliances may require revision.
