## Why Ingested

상악 제2대구치의 치관 경사(팁·토크)가 Andrews 처방값과 실측값 사이에 큰 괴리가 있음을 3D 디지털 계측으로 확인한 논문. 기성 브래킷 처방(Preadjusted Appliance Prescription)의 제2대구치 팁 값 재검토 근거로서, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner-indications-limitations]] 및 교정 치료 계획 섹션에 기초 데이터를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

This cross-sectional study (n=41 untreated adolescents, UMGS archive) measured maxillary second molar crown angulations digitally with 3D Slicer and compared them to Andrews' historical manual measurements. Fully erupted upper second molars showed significantly more distal crown tip (−18.9° vs +0.4°, P<0.0001) and more lingual inclination (−10.6° vs −8.0°, P=0.0118) than Andrews' values. These findings challenge Andrews' mesial-tip observation and suggest the tip prescription for preadjusted brackets at the maxillary second molar may require revision.

## 세줄요약

디지털 치과 모형(3D Slicer)으로 정상 교합 청소년 41명(UMGS)의 상악 제2대구치 치관 경사를 측정하고 Andrews 표본의 수작업 계측값과 비교하였다. 근원심 경사(팁): UMGS −18.9°(원심 경사) vs Andrews +0.4°(근심 경사)(P<0.0001), 협설 경사(토크): UMGS −10.6° vs Andrews −8.0°(P=0.0118)로 유의한 차이가 확인되었다. Andrews의 관찰과 반대되는 원심 경사가 존재함을 근거로, 기성 브래킷 처방 (Preadjusted Appliance Prescription)의 제2대구치 팁 값 재검토를 제안하였다.

## Source Summary

Cross-sectional study comparing 3D-measured crown angulations of maxillary second molars in untreated adolescents (UMGS, n=41, mean age 14.9 y) against Andrews' historical manual measurements from untreated Class I subjects with normal occlusion.

**Key measurements (UMGS vs Andrews):**

| Parameter | UMGS | Andrews | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mesiodistal crown angulation (tip) | −18.9° (distal) | +0.4° (mesial) | <0.0001 |
| Transversal crown inclination (torque) | −10.6° (lingual) | −8.0° (lingual) | 0.0118 |

**Methods:** Digital dental casts (3D Slicer software) from UMGS archive; mixed effect models with group, arch side, and group×side interaction as fixed effects.

**Conclusion:** Fully erupted upper second molars in normal occlusion show significantly more distal crown tip and lingual inclination than Andrews' values, suggesting that tip prescription for preadjusted appliances may require revision.
