## Why Ingested

2026-09-29 감염 발치와 즉시식립 서베일런스(급성·화농성 감염 포함)에서 발굴. 기존 [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/colak-2023-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-retrospective]]와 [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]]는 만성 병소 즉시식립의 생존율을 다루지만 PRGF 보조 프로토콜(1회 vs 2회)은 다루지 않아, 보조요법 변수를 직접 비교한 소규모 임상 근거로 보완.

## Three-line Summary

Non-randomized (allocation method not described) two-arm clinical comparison, n=100 patients (50/50), immediate implants in chronically infected sockets (chronic periapical infection of endodontic/endoperiodontal origin) with PRGF applied once (at placement) versus twice (at placement and 1 month later).

Two-time PRGF gave higher ISQ stability at 2 and 3 months and less marginal bone loss at 3 and 6 months (numbers not in retrieved text; Table 1 missing); 14 of the implants failed to osseointegrate (group split not reported); pain and swelling resolved by week 12 in both groups.

Weak evidence: no randomization/blinding described, no failure breakdown, follow-up only 3-6 months, and no acute/purulent sites; the twice-PRGF benefit is hypothesis-generating only.

## 세줄요약

비무작위(배정 방법 미기술) 2군 임상 비교, 환자 100명(50/50), 만성 치근단 감염(치내·치내-치주 기원) 발치와에 즉시 임플란트 식립, 혈소판 유래 성장인자 (Platelet-Rich Growth Factor, PRGF)를 1회(식립 시) vs 2회(식립 시 + 1개월 후) 적용.

PRGF 2회군에서 2·3개월 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)가 더 높고 3·6개월 변연골 소실이 적었으나(수치는 확보된 본문에 없음, 표 1 누락), 14개 임플란트가 골유착 실패(군별 분포 미보고); 통증·부종은 양군 모두 12주에 소실.

근거 약함: 무작위·눈가림 미기술, 실패 내역 없음, 추적 3-6개월, 급성·화농성 부위 미포함이라 PRGF 2회 이점은 가설 수준.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Compares PRGF once vs twice (second application 1 month after implant placement) as an adjunct to immediate implants in infected sockets.
- Reports ISQ (Ostell) at baseline, 2 and 3 months, plus pain/swelling and marginal bone loss.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 100 patients, Group A (n=50) PRGF at placement; Group B (n=50) PRGF at placement and 1 month later. Group A is labelled "placebo comparator" and Group B "active comparator", but no randomization, concealment or blinding is described.
- Infected site definition: extraction of teeth with chronic periapical infection of endodontic or endoperiodontal origin, determined by clinical and radiographic evaluation. Acute infection and pus are not mentioned.
- Protocol: atraumatic extraction, curettage, socket inspection, osteotomy 2 mm beyond apex, implant dipped in PRGF, gap filled with PRGF +/- bone graft, passive suturing; antibiotics and analgesics; 3-month healing then second-stage surgery (two-stage implants). Antibiotic agent/regimen not specified.
- PRGF: 8 ml blood, 3.2% sodium citrate, 270 g centrifugation, middle layer collected, no leukocytes.
- Outcomes: pain (VAS 0-10), swelling, ISQ by RFA, marginal bone loss (3 and 6 months).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 63% male, 37% female; 18-35 years 46% (A) vs 54% (B).
- 14 implants failed to osseointegrate (group distribution not reported).
- Pain and swelling decreased from day 1 to week 1 and were nil by week 12 in both groups.
- ISQ difference between groups "clinically significant" at 2 and 3 months, higher in Group B; marginal bone loss lower in Group B at 3 and 6 months. No numeric values or p-values in retrieved text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No allocation method, no blinding, no sample-size justification, no numeric ISQ/MBL, no per-group failure counts; follow-up 3-6 months; ISQ compared as "clinically significant" without stated test.
- Group A being labelled "placebo comparator" is inconsistent with it receiving PRGF.
- Authors call for longer follow-up and larger samples.

## 7. Glossary

- **PRGF**: platelet-rich growth factor, autologous plasma preparation (here 270 g, single spin, no leukocytes).
- **ISQ**: implant stability quotient measured by resonance frequency analysis (Ostell).
- **RFA**: resonance/radiofrequency analysis.
- **Endoperiodontal lesion**: combined endodontic and periodontal origin lesion.
