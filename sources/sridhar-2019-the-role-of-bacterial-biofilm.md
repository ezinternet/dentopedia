## Why Ingested

The held occlusal-overload cluster is entirely clinical/observational or review-level — [[wiki/occlusion/mojaver-2025-occlusal-overload-peri-implant-health-sr]] proposes a dual mechanical × biofilm pathway but explicitly notes it needs direct experimental confirmation, and [[wiki/occlusion/sadowsky-2019-occlusal-overload-dental-implants-review]] reviews overload as a failure mechanism without surface-level data. This bench fatigue study is the missing mechanistic link: it directly tests biofilm × cyclic loading on titanium implant surfaces and shows oxide-layer damage, independently reinforcing the dual-pathway model with in-vitro evidence.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro dynamic fatigue bench study: four dental implants per group were immersed in PBS (0.01 M, negative control), lactic acid (pH 5, positive control), or bacterial polyculture (test) and loaded 45 N → 450 N at 4 Hz for 2 million cycles, then evaluated by optical microscopy, X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), and electrochemical corrosion testing.

Surface discoloration appeared in all three groups, but only the bacterial-polyculture specimens showed TiO2 thinning on XPS and a lower corrosion potential (Ecorr) than both controls — direct bench evidence that bacterial biofilm and mechanical forces act synergistically on the titanium surface; linear polarization resistance (LPR) and corrosion rate (CR) remained comparable among groups.

Mechanistically supports the dual mechanical × biofilm pathway of implant failure, but this is abstract-only evidence with n = 4 per group, no living tissue, and no direct measurement of the hypothesized metallic particle release — hypothesis-generating for surface damage, not for clinical failure rates.

## 세줄요약

시험관 내 (in-vitro) 동적 피로시험 (dynamic fatigue test) — 임플란트 각 4개를 PBS(음성대조군)·젖산(pH 5, 양성대조군)·세균 폴리컬처(bacterial polyculture, 시험군)에 침지한 채 45 N→450 N·4 Hz·200만 회 반복 하중을 가하고 광학현미경·X선광전자분광학 (X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy, XPS)·전기화학 부식시험으로 평가.

세 군 모두 표면 변색이 관찰됐지만, 시험군(세균 폴리컬처)에서만 산화막 (TiO2) 박층(thinning)과 낮은 부식전위 (Corrosion Potential, Ecorr)이 확인돼 기계적 하중과 세균 biofilm의 시너지 효과를 벤치 수준에서 직접 입증 — 선형분극저항 (LPR)·부식률 (CR)은 군 간 유사.

임플란트 실패의 기계적 × biofilm 이중 경로 (dual-pathway) 모델에 기전적 근거를 제공하나, 초록 한정·그룹당 n=4·living tissue 부재·금속 입자 방출 미측정 한계로 임상 실패율 근거가 아닌 표면 손상 가설 수준의 근거다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Develops a dynamic fatigue test method for dental implants under wet (immersed) environments — a bench model that couples mechanical loading with chemically/biologically aggressive media, beyond standard dry fatigue testing.
- Provides direct experimental evidence that bacterial biofilm and mechanical forces act **synergistically** (not independently) to damage titanium implant surfaces — XPS-confirmed TiO2 thinning specific to the bacterial-polyculture group.
- Challenges the prevailing assumption that bacteria alone are the primary etiological factor in titanium implant failure, framing failure as a mechanical × biological interplay that may release metallic particles in vivo.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In-vitro dynamic fatigue bench study with negative (PBS) and positive (lactic acid) controls.
- **n**: 4 dental implants per immersion group (45 N → 450 N, 4 Hz, 2,000,000 cycles).
- **Groups**: (i) 0.01 M phosphate-buffered saline (PBS, negative control); (ii) lactic acid, pH 5 (positive control); (iii) bacterial polyculture (test).
- **Outcomes/analyses**: surface morphology (optical microscopy), surface chemistry (XPS), electrochemical corrosion behavior (Ecorr, LPR, CR).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Loading protocol: 45 N → 450 N at 4 Hz for 2 million cycles, per group.
- Surface discoloration: evident in **all three** groups post-testing.
- XPS: TiO2 layer thinning in **test (bacterial polyculture) specimens only** — attributed to the interplay of biofilm and mechanical forces.
- Corrosion potential (Ecorr): **lower** in test specimens than in both positive (lactic acid) and negative (PBS) controls — consistent with oxide-layer damage.
- Linear polarization resistance (LPR) and corrosion rate (CR): **comparable among groups** — overall corrosion resistance after testing was preserved despite the localized oxide damage.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only record — full text not retrieved; sample sizes per analysis, polyculture composition, and statistical detail cannot be verified here.
- Small n (4 implants per group), no living tissue/bone interface, and the hypothesized in-vivo release of metallic particles was not directly measured in the reported results.

## 7. Glossary
- **XPS**: X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy — surface-chemistry analysis quantifying oxide-layer composition/thickness.
- **Ecorr**: corrosion (open-circuit) potential — lower values indicate greater thermodynamic tendency to corrode / oxide damage.
- **LPR**: linear polarization resistance — electrochemical measure of resistance to corrosion (higher = more resistant).
- **CR**: corrosion rate — kinetic measure of material loss per unit time.
- **Bacterial polyculture**: mixed-species bacterial community grown together, approximating biofilm conditions better than a single-species culture.
