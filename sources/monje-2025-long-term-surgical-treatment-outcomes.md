## Why Ingested
Long-term (≥5 years) surgical outcome data is the missing evidence layer behind the EFP S3 guideline's peri-implantitis surgical pathway and our short-term RCT pages (e.g., [[implants/peri-implantitis/monje-2025-surface-decontamination-reconstructive-peri-implantitis]] reports 12-month outcomes only). This SR quantifies what actually happens at 5+ years — ~60% disease resolution, ~27% retreatment — refining [[implants/peri-implantitis/herrera-2023-prevention-treatment-peri-implant-efp]] with numbers the guideline could not provide.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review (qualitative synthesis, no meta-analysis) of 17 long-term studies (≥5 years; 23 treatment arms, 839 patients, 1326 implants, mean follow-up 75.4 months) on surgical treatment of peri-implantitis under supportive peri-implant care (~3×/year).

Mean disease resolution was 58.6% (range 30.4–97.7%), progressive bone loss arrestment 69.6%, BOP resolution 59.9%, retreatment need 27.2%, and implant survival 88.6% (75–100%); residual pockets ≥6 mm carried ~8× higher odds of recurrence (OR 7.3–9.2).

Surgical therapy is effective but not curative in the long term — advanced baseline bone loss, residual pockets ≥6 mm, keratinized mucosa <2 mm, and modified implant surfaces predict recurrence; high heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis.

## 세줄요약
임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis) 외과적 치료의 장기(≥5년) 결과를 검토한 체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review, 정성적 종합·메타분석 불가) — 17개 연구(23개 치료군, 환자 839명, 임플란트 1326개, 평균 추적 75.4개월), 임플란트 주위 지지치료(Supportive Peri-implant Care, 약 3회/년) 하에서 분석.

평균 질환 해소율(disease resolution)은 58.6%(범위 30.4–97.7%), 진행성 골소실 억제율 69.6%, 출혈탐침(Bleeding on Probing, BOP) 소실률 59.9%, 재치료 필요률 27.2%, 임플란트 생존율 88.6%(75–100%); 술 후 잔여 주머니(residual pocket) ≥6 mm면 재발 오즈비(Odds Ratio, OR)가 약 8배(OR 7.3–9.2).

외과 치료는 장기적으로 유효하지만 근치(cure)는 아님 — 기저 골소실의 진행 정도, 잔여 주머니 ≥6 mm, 각화점막(Keratinized Mucosa, KM) 폭 <2 mm, 변형된 임플란트 표면이 재발 예측인자; 연구 간 이질성이 커 메타분석은 불가했다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First systematic synthesis dedicated exclusively to long-term (≥5 years) outcomes of **surgical** peri-implantitis therapy: 17 studies, 839 patients, 1326 implants.
- Quantifies the long-term ceiling of surgical treatment: ~60% disease resolution, ~70% bone-loss arrestment, ~90% survival under supportive care — retreatment in ~27% of cases.
- Identifies residual pockets ≥6 mm post-surgery as the single strongest recurrence predictor (~8× higher odds; OR 7.3–9.2), plus extent of bone loss (OR 1.3), KM width <2 mm, and modified implant surfaces.
- Documents outcome-reporting gaps: mucosal recession and patient-reported outcomes were reported in **zero** of the 17 studies; disease resolution after retreatment was seldom evaluated.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review, qualitative (narrative) synthesis only — heterogeneity in disease-resolution definitions precluded meta-analysis.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed) + EMBASE, search through June 2025; no language/year restriction; reference lists of included studies and related reviews screened.
- **PICOS**: Adults with ≥1 functioning implant diagnosed with peri-implantitis (2017 AAP/EFP case definition or clear composite criteria); any intervention aiming at inflammation resolution; human clinical trials (RCT, prospective/retrospective cohort, case-control) with ≥5 years follow-up post-treatment and ≥10 patients.
- **n**: 17 studies → 23 treatment arms; 839 patients (15–149 per study), 1326 implants; mean follow-up 75.4 months (54–120; one 4.5-year study included by editorial weight); supportive care ~3×/year (1–4×/year).
- **Outcomes**: Composite disease resolution (clinical + radiographic), PPD/BOP/suppuration, marginal bone level change, implant survival, PROs; prognostic factors for recurrence.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result (mean, range) | Arms reported |
|---|---|---|
| Disease resolution (composite) | 58.6% (30.4–97.7%) | 14 arms / 13 studies |
| — reconstructive | 62.02% (36.3–97.7%) | — |
| — non-reconstructive | 53.98% (30.4–89%) | — |
| Progressive bone loss arrestment (≥1 mm) | 69.6% (36.3–97.7%); reconstructive 71.86% vs non-reconstructive 63.7% | 14 arms |
| BOP elimination at latest follow-up | 59.9% (18.7–93.4%) | 16 arms |
| Need for retreatment | 27.2% (7.8–54%) | 7 arms |
| Implant survival | 88.6% (75–100%); no reconstructive vs non-reconstructive difference | 19 arms |

**Prognostic factors for disease recurrence** (4 studies): residual pockets ≥6 mm post-surgery OR 7.3–9.2 (~8× odds); extent of bone loss OR 1.3; KM width <2 mm after surgery; implant surface (modified).

Screening: 5840 (PubMed, deduplicated) + 455 (EMBASE) records → 41 full texts → 17 included (10 retrospective cohorts, 5 prospective cohorts, 1 RCT, 1 case series).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No meta-analysis was possible — high heterogeneity in disease-resolution definitions, surgical protocols, and surface decontamination methods; means/ranges are simple averages across arms.
- Evidence base dominated by retrospective cohorts (10/17); only one RCT reached 5 years.
- Mucosal recession and patient-reported outcomes were reported in no included study; disease resolution after retreatment was seldom evaluated.
- Reconstructive vs non-reconstructive comparison is confounded by differing definitions and outliers; one study included despite 4.5-year mean follow-up.

## 6. Related Work
- herrera-2023: EFP S3 guideline — this SR supplies the long-term surgical-outcome numbers the guideline lacked (refines).
- mahardawi-2023: KM insufficiency as peri-implantitis risk factor — extended here to post-surgical recurrence prognosis (KM <2 mm).
- mojaver-2025: SPiT efficacy SR — all outcomes here were achieved within supportive care (~3×/year), contextualizing surgical results as maintenance-dependent.
- monje-2025-surface-decontamination-reconstructive-peri-implantitis: 12-month multicenter RCT by the same group — short-term counterpart to this long-term synthesis.
- baima-2022 / gardelis-2025: surface decontamination and surface-modification evidence — consistent with the surface-as-recurrence-predictor finding.

## 7. Glossary
- **Disease resolution**: Composite endpoint (pocket depth, BOP, suppuration, radiographic bone stability) defining peri-implantitis "success" — definitions varied across included studies.
- **Progressive bone loss arrestment**: No continued marginal bone loss (>1 mm) at latest follow-up after treatment.
- **Supportive peri-implant care (SPC/SPiT)**: Maintenance recall program (~3×/year here) required to sustain surgical results.
- **Residual pocket (≥6 mm)**: Post-surgical probing depth ≥6 mm — strongest recurrence predictor (OR 7.3–9.2).
- **Keratinized mucosa (KM) width**: Band of keratinized tissue around the implant; <2 mm associated with recurrence.
- **Retreatment**: Repeat surgical/non-surgical intervention after initial therapy; needed in 27.2% at long term.
