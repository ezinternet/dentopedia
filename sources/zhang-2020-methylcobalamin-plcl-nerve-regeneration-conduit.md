## Why Ingested

Local sustained delivery of methylcobalamin (MeCbl, 메틸코발라민) via biodegradable PLCL nerve guidance conduit improves peripheral nerve regeneration — provides translational bridge between vitamin B12 pharmacology and guided nerve repair relevant to inferior alveolar nerve (하치조신경, IAN) injury management. Extends [[oral-surgery/lee-2015-inferior-alveolar-nerve-injury-management]] with a biomaterial-delivery mechanism for neurotrophic agents.

## Three-line Summary

Animal study (rat sciatic nerve, 10 mm defect model) testing PLCL nerve guidance conduits loaded with 5%, 15%, and 25% methylcobalamin (MeCbl).

15% MeCbl-PLCL conduit achieved the most satisfactory sciatic nerve recovery: largest axon diameter, thickest myelinated fibers, sustained MeCbl release up to 21 days, and enhanced Schwann cell proliferation/elongation.

Biodegradable PLCL conduits with local MeCbl delivery offer a promising alternative to autograft for peripheral nerve gaps, with potential relevance to IAN reconstruction.

## 세줄요약

쥐 좌골신경 10 mm 절손 모델에서 PLCL 신경유도도관에 메틸코발라민(MeCbl)을 5·15·25% 농도로 담지해 신경재생을 평가한 동물실험이다.

15% MeCbl-PLCL 도관이 가장 우수한 결과를 보임: 최대 축삭 직경, 가장 두꺼운 수초섬유, MeCbl 21일 지속방출, 슈반세포 증식 및 신장 촉진.

생분해성 PLCL 도관을 통한 국소 메틸코발라민 전달은 말초신경 결손(하치조신경 손상 포함)에서 자가신경이식의 대안으로 유망하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrated fabrication of rolled-and-sutured PLCL nerve guidance conduits (NGCs) loaded with methylcobalamin (MeCbl) at three concentrations (5%, 15%, 25% wt).
- Showed sustained in vitro MeCbl release from PLCL films for up to 21 days.
- Established dose-dependent effects: 15% MeCbl loading was optimal for Schwann cell proliferation/elongation and Neuro2a neuron cell proliferation in vitro.
- Confirmed in vivo peripheral nerve regeneration across a 10 mm sciatic nerve gap in rats, with motor and sensory function recovery and improved myelination at the 15% dose.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Material**: Poly(D,L-lactide-co-caprolactone) (PLCL) films loaded with MeCbl at 5%, 15%, and 25% wt%; rolled and sutured to form tubular NGCs.
- **In vitro**: Schwann cell and Neuro2a cell culture on MeCbl-loaded films; proliferation (CCK-8 or equivalent) and morphology assessed.
- **In vivo**: Rat sciatic nerve 10 mm amputation defect model; NGC implantation; functional recovery (motor and sensory), histology, and myelination (fiber diameter, myelin thickness) assessed.
- **Controls**: Comparison across MeCbl concentrations; likely compared to empty PLCL conduit and/or autograft (details from abstract only).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- MeCbl released sustainably from PLCL films up to 21 days.
- MeCbl-loaded films enhanced Schwann cell proliferation and elongation (dose-dependent, optimal at 15%).
- Enhanced Neuro2a cell proliferation on MeCbl films.
- In vivo: 15% MeCbl-PLCL conduit demonstrated largest axon diameter and thickest myelinated fibers among groups.
- Recovery of both motor and sensory function observed with MeCbl-loaded conduits.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Animal study only (rat sciatic nerve); no clinical human data.
- Web-capture PDF limits access to full statistical data, sample sizes, and detailed histomorphometry values.
- Long-term durability of PLCL conduit and nerve regeneration quality not established beyond acute healing period.
- Comparison with autograft (gold standard) details not available from abstract alone.
- Mechanism of MeCbl action (SNAP-25 modulation, axon elongation, remyelination) cited from prior literature but not mechanistically dissected in this study.

## 7. Glossary

- **MeCbl / 메틸코발라민 (Methylcobalamin)**: Active coenzyme form of vitamin B12; promotes Schwann cell activity and axonal regeneration.
- **PLCL / 폴리(D,L-락티드-코-카프로락톤) (Poly(D,L-lactide-co-caprolactone))**: Biodegradable copolymer used for flexible nerve conduit fabrication.
- **NGC / 신경유도도관 (Nerve Guidance Conduit)**: Tubular scaffold bridging a peripheral nerve gap to guide axonal regeneration.
- **Schwann cell / 슈반세포**: Primary myelinating cells of the peripheral nervous system; key to nerve regeneration.
- **Myelination / 수초화**: Formation of myelin sheath around axons; thicker myelin = better conduction.
- **Sciatic nerve / 좌골신경**: Large peripheral nerve in rats used as a standard model for peripheral nerve injury research.
