## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (Iranian Endodontic Journal 2024) pooling 31 systematic reviews/meta-analyses (12 SR, 19 SR/MA) comparing calcium hydroxide (CH) apexification, MTA apexification, and regenerative endodontic treatment (RET) for immature permanent teeth with pulp necrosis, quality-appraised with AMSTAR-2.

MTA apexification achieved faster apical barrier formation than CH with similar overall success rates; RET generally outperformed apexification in root development (root length, dentinal wall thickness) while showing comparable survival/success rates; methodological quality of included reviews was critically low to low except one rated high.

Clinically, MTA and RET are both preferable to CH apexification for reduced treatment time, but RET's superiority in structural root development does not translate into higher survival — and the field still lacks standardized RET protocols, limiting definitive clinical guideline-setting.

## 세줄요약

이란 근관치료 저널(Iranian Endodontic Journal, 2024) umbrella review, 미성숙 영구치 치수괴사(pulp necrosis)의 관리전략을 다룬 31편 체계적문헌고찰/메타분석(SR/MA)(SR 12편+SR/MA 19편)을 AMSTAR-2로 품질평가하며 통합.

수산화칼슘(Calcium Hydroxide, CH) 근관충전(Apexification) 대비 미네랄삼산화물응집체(Mineral Trioxide Aggregate, MTA)가 근단 장벽 형성 속도에서 우세했고 전체 성공률은 유사했으며, 재생근관치료(Regenerative Endodontic Treatment, RET)는 치근 발육(치근 길이·상아질 벽 두께)에서 근관충전보다 우수했으나 생존·성공률은 대등했다.

포함된 리뷰의 방법론적 질은 대부분 "critically low~low"(1편만 high)로 낮아, MTA·RET가 CH보다 유리하다는 결론은 시술시간 단축 측면에서는 확실하나 표준화된 RET 프로토콜 부재로 임상 가이드라인 확정에는 추가 고품질 연구가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review (as of the search date, up to July 2024) to synthesize the full landscape of SR/MA evidence (31 reviews) comparing three treatment modalities for immature necrotic permanent teeth: CH apexification, MTA apexification, and RET.
- Provides a systematic AMSTAR-2 quality appraisal across all 31 included reviews, revealing that the underlying evidence base is largely low-quality despite consistent directional findings.
- Separately tabulates (Table 1) apexification-material comparisons (CH vs MTA vs other CSCs), (Table 2) RET scaffold comparisons (blood clot vs platelet concentrates and variants), and (Table 3) RET-vs-apexification head-to-head reviews — giving a structured map of where evidence agrees and where it conflicts.
- Identifies platelet concentrates (PRP, PRF) as generally superior scaffolds to blood clot alone for periapical healing, while noting inconsistent effects on pulp vitality, apical closure, and dentinal wall thickness across studies.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Protocol**: Conducted per Cochrane Handbook methodology principles (not a registered PROSPERO umbrella-review protocol per se, but methodologically modeled on high-quality prior umbrella reviews).
- **Search**: MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Cochrane Library, Scopus, plus grey literature and manual reference-list screening, inception to July 2024, no language restriction.
- **Eligibility**: SR/MA assessing apexification and/or RET outcomes in immature permanent teeth with pulp necrosis; excluded case reports/series, observational/experimental primary studies, opinion pieces, and SR/MA without sufficient outcome data.
- **Selection/extraction**: Two independent reviewers (S.A., S.S.); discrepancies resolved by discussion.
- **Extracted data**: Study type, detailed intervention/material/protocol description, and clinical/radiographic outcomes — survival/success rate, periapical healing, apical barrier formation time, apical foramen width, apical closure, root development, dentinal wall thickness, pulp vitality, adverse effects.
- **Quality tool**: AMSTAR-2 (16 items), overall confidence rated critically low / low / moderate / high per standard AMSTAR-2 decision rules.
- **Heterogeneity**: Study design and outcome-measure heterogeneity across the 31 reviews precluded a formal re-meta-analysis at the umbrella level (narrative/qualitative synthesis only).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Search yield**: 1100 records → 250 full-text reviewed → 31 SR/MA included (12 SR, 19 SR/MA); publication years 2011-2024; reported patient age ranges across reviews spanned roughly 6-58 years.
- **Apexification materials (5 reviews)**: MTA/CSC achieved faster apical barrier formation than CH in 2 SR/MAs; overall success rates similar across 3 SR/MAs; apical closure comparable in 2 SR/MAs.
- **RET scaffolds (19 reviews)**: Platelet concentrates (PRP/PRF/PP) generally showed superior periapical healing vs blood clot alone in some analyses, but overall success rates were similar between PC and BC in most studies; pulp vitality, apical closure, and dentinal wall thickness findings were inconsistent across studies (roughly split between "no significant difference" and "PC superior").
- **RET vs apexification (11 reviews)**: No significant difference in survival/overall success rates in most comparisons; RET showed superior root development (root length, dentinal wall thickness) in the majority of reviews (e.g., Panda 2022, Stefanidou 2024); periapical healing outcomes were generally comparable; one review (Kahler) uniquely favored apexification for clinical success/healing, but without meta-analysis (low confidence).
- **AMSTAR-2 quality**: Methodological quality rated "critically low to low" for nearly all 31 reviews, with only one rated "high" (the review comparing RET/apexification, ref. [43] in the source numbering, 998 patients, 10 RCTs).
- **Adverse effects**: CH apexification — cervical root fracture risk, prolonged treatment. MTA — tooth discoloration (aesthetic concern). RET — variable outcomes, insufficient long-term complication data.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All findings are derived from an umbrella review of predominantly low-to-critically-low-quality SR/MAs — the umbrella review cannot exceed the evidentiary ceiling of its included reviews.
- High heterogeneity in study designs, outcome definitions/measurement, and scaffold/material protocols across included reviews precluded a formal quantitative re-synthesis (no pooled effect estimate was calculated by the umbrella authors themselves).
- Potential publication bias toward positive findings noted as a limitation across the underlying evidence base.
- The authors explicitly flag that the true clinical benefit of RET-induced root development (i.e., whether it improves long-term tooth survival/function beyond apexification) remains unestablished and needs dedicated long-term follow-up studies.
- Lack of standardized RET protocols (scaffold choice, disinfection regimen, barrier material) is identified as the primary barrier to definitive clinical guideline-setting.

## 7. Glossary

- **CH (Calcium Hydroxide) apexification**: 근관 내 수산화칼슘을 장기간 적용해 치근단에 석회화 장벽을 유도하는 전통적 근관충전술.
- **MTA (Mineral Trioxide Aggregate) apexification / apical plug**: 칼슘실리케이트 시멘트를 치근단에 위치시켜 인공 장벽을 형성하는 술식.
- **CSC (Calcium Silicate-based Cement)**: MTA를 포함한 칼슘실리케이트계 시멘트 총칭.
- **RET (Regenerative Endodontic Treatment) / revitalization**: 치수-상아질 복합체 재생을 목표로 하는 생물학적 근관치료 접근법.
- **PC (Platelet Concentrate)**: 혈소판농축물 총칭(PRP, PRF, PP 등 스캐폴드로 사용).
- **PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) / PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)**: 각각 혈소판풍부혈장/혈소판풍부피브린 — RET 스캐폴드로 사용되는 자가혈액유래 생체재료.
- **BC (Blood Clot)**: RET의 표준 스캐폴드인 혈병.
- **AMSTAR-2**: 체계적문헌고찰의 방법론적 질을 critically low/low/moderate/high 4단계로 평가하는 16문항 도구.
- **Apical barrier formation time**: 근단 장벽(석회화 또는 인공)이 형성되는 데 걸리는 시간.
- **Dentinal wall thickness / root length**: 재생근관치료 성공의 구조적 지표 — 치근 발육 정도를 나타냄.
