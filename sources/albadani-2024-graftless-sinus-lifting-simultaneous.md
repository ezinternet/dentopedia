## Why Ingested

Prospective study comparing graftless transalveolar SFE in freshly extracted vs healed sockets — adds evidence for graftless approach extension and socket-type effect; extends [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/fettouh-2025-graftless-lateral-transcrestal-sinus-rct]] and graftless transcrestal cluster.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study comparing graftless transalveolar sinus floor elevation with simultaneous implant placement in 60 patients (30 freshly extracted sockets vs 30 healed sockets) with residual bone height ≥5 mm, using a single B&B 4.5×10 mm implant per site and CBCT follow-up at ≥12 months.

Both groups achieved 100% implant survival with no complications; ISQ was satisfactory and not significantly different between groups (p=0.38); intrasinus bone formation was numerically higher in fresh sockets (0.22±0.33 mm) than healed (0.11±0.64 mm) but not statistically significant (p=0.26).

Graftless transalveolar sinus floor elevation with simultaneous implant placement is safe and predictable in both fresh and healed sockets when residual bone height is ≥5 mm; socket type at the time of surgery does not substantially alter outcomes.

## 세줄요약

잔존골 높이(Residual Bone Height, RBH) ≥5mm 환자 60명(신선 발치와 30명 vs 치유 소켓 30명)을 대상으로 무이식 경구개 상악동거상술(transalveolar sinus floor elevation) + 동시 임플란트(B&B 4.5×10mm) 식립을 시행하고 CBCT로 ≥12개월 추적한 전향적 임상 연구.

양군 임플란트 생존율 100%, 합병증 없음; ISQ는 양군 간 유의차 없음(p=0.38); 상악동 내 신생골 형성량은 신선 발치와군(0.22±0.33mm)이 수치상 높았으나 통계적으로 유의하지 않음(p=0.26).

RBH ≥5mm 조건에서 무이식 경구개 상악동거상 + 동시 임플란트는 신선·치유 소켓 모두 안전하고 예측 가능하며, 수술 시 소켓 상태가 결과에 실질적 차이를 미치지 않음.

## One-line Summary

Prospective RBH ≥5 mm study (n=60, 30 fresh vs 30 healed): graftless transalveolar SFE with simultaneous implant achieves 100% survival and satisfactory ISQ in both groups; no significant inter-group difference in bone formation.

## 한줄요약

잔존골 높이 ≥5mm 환자 60명(신선 발치와 30 vs 치유 소켓 30) 전향 연구: 무이식 경구강 상악동거상 + 동시 임플란트 식립 시 양군 모두 생존율 100%, 초기 안정성(ISQ) 만족; 집단 간 신생골 형성 유의차 없음(p=0.26).

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health, 24:227
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s12903-024-03949-9
- **Institution**: Jiblah University for Medical and Health Sciences (JUMHS), Yemen; B&B dental implants (4.5 mm × 10 mm)
- **Study design**: Prospective clinical study
- **n**: 60 patients (30 fresh extraction sockets [Group A], 30 healed sockets [Group B])
- **Follow-up**: 12–36 months (mean ≥12 months post-SFE); CBCT at baseline and ≥1 year post-op

## 2. Key Contributions

- 100% survival rate in both fresh and healed socket groups; no biological or mechanical complications
- ISQ: no significant inter-group difference (p=0.38)
- Intrasinus bone formation: Group A (fresh) showed slightly higher bone formation (0.22±0.33 mm above implant) vs Group B (0.11±0.64 mm), but NS (p=0.26)
- Group B: 0.11±0.64 mm of intrasinus implant height without bone formation (some negative values possible, ie no measurable gain)
- Graftless simultaneous implant tenting works in both socket conditions with RBH ≥5 mm

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Inclusion**: RBH ≥5 mm, posterior maxilla, no systemic disease, no sinus pathology, nonsmokers
- **Implant**: B&B dental (Italy), 4.5 mm diameter × 10 mm length, uniform across both groups
- **Protocol**: Atraumatic extraction (Group A) or mid-crestal incision (Group B); drill sequence to 1 mm below sinus floor; manual then gentle wrench insertion; bicortical anchorage; PEEK healing abutment 5 mm; prosthetics at 6 months
- **Jumping zone**: Xenograft used only to fill buccal gap in fresh sockets (not for sinus)
- **CBCT**: Pre-op and ≥12 months post-op; intrasinus bone formation measured
- **Statistics**: Independent t-test; G*Power sample size (n=25/group minimum, n=30 enrolled with 85% power)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Group A (fresh) | Group B (healed) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISQ | Satisfactory (NS diff) | Satisfactory | 0.38 |
| Intrasinus bone formation | 0.22±0.33 mm | 0.11±0.64 mm | 0.26 |
| Survival rate | 100% | 100% | — |
| Complications | None | None | — |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single implant diameter/length (4.5×10 mm) — no macrodesign variation
- Short RBH range (≥5 mm) — no <5 mm cases
- Small n per group; single-center Yemen
- CBCT measurement resolution limits detection of small bone changes
- No control group (lateral SFE or graftless lateral comparison)

## 6. Related Work

- Fettouh 2025: RCT graftless lateral vs transcrestal — comprehensive outcome comparison
- Duan 2017: SR+MA graft-free SFE — 91.8% survival, bone gain 3.5–5.8 mm
- Guo 2020: Non-grafted vs platelet concentrates + grafted transcrestal — no significant difference in outcomes

## 7. Glossary

- **Graftless SFE (무이식 상악동거상술)**: sinus membrane elevation without bone substitute; blood clot provides scaffold
- **Tenting technique (텐팅 기법)**: implant protrusion into sinus maintains membrane elevation space
- **PEEK (폴리에테르에테르케톤)**: healing abutment material; prevents implant sinking
