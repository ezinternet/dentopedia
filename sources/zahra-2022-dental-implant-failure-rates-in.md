## Three-line Summary

Retrospective chart review at New York University College of Dentistry of patients with at least 1 year of follow-up after receiving one or more dental implants, testing whether self-reported penicillin allergy and the antibiotic given are linked to implant failure.

Overall implant failure was 12.9%; failure was 8.4% in non-allergic patients taking amoxicillin versus 17.1% with reported allergy (adjusted OR 2.22, 95% CI 1.44-3.44), and 19.9% in allergy-reporting patients taking clindamycin (adjusted OR 2.9, 95% CI 1.77-4.47 versus amoxicillin).

The authors report a significant increase in early implant failure in allergy-reporting patients; evidence is retrospective and abstract-only, so sample size, early-failure definition and early-failure rate could not be verified.

## 세줄요약

뉴욕대학교 치과대학에서 임플란트를 1개 이상 식립하고 최소 1년 추적된 환자의 차트를 검토한 후향적 연구로, 환자가 보고한 페니실린 알레르기와 투여 항생제가 임플란트 실패와 관련되는지 평가.

전체 임플란트 실패율 12.9%; 알레르기 없이 아목시실린을 복용한 군 8.4% 대비 알레르기 보고군 17.1% (보정 OR 2.22, 95% CI 1.44-3.44), 클린다마이신 복용 알레르기 보고군 19.9% (아목시실린 대비 보정 OR 2.9, 95% CI 1.77-4.47).

저자들은 알레르기 보고 환자에서 조기 임플란트 실패 (early implant failure)가 유의하게 증가했다고 보고; 후향적·초록 기반이라 표본 크기, 조기 실패 정의와 비율은 확인 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Compares failure by reported penicillin allergy and by antibiotic given (amoxicillin, clindamycin, other).
- Allergy-reporting patients had higher failure (17.1% vs 8.4%; adjusted OR 2.22, 95% CI 1.44-3.44).
- Reports increased early implant failure in allergy-reporting patients.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective chart review, single dental school.
- **Eligibility**: one or more implants placed at the College; at least 1 year of follow-up.
- **Not stated in abstract**: n of patients and implants, covariates in the adjusted model, early-failure definition.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | Failure rate | Adjusted OR (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|
| Overall | 12.9% | - |
| No allergy, amoxicillin | 8.4% | reference |
| Allergy reported | 17.1% | 2.22 (1.44-3.44) |
| Allergy, clindamycin | 19.9% | 2.9 (1.77-4.47) vs amoxicillin |
| Allergy, non-amoxicillin antibiotic | 20.9% | 2.77 (1.77-4.32) vs amoxicillin |

- Lower failure with amoxicillin than other antibiotics; early failure significantly increased in allergy-reporting patients (no number given).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective; antibiotic choice depends on allergy status (confounding by indication).
- Abstract-only: sample size, early-failure definition and rate, and model covariates not verifiable.
- Single institution.

## 7. Glossary

- **자가보고 페니실린 알레르기 (Self-reported Penicillin Allergy, SRPA)**: patient-stated allergy, often unverified by testing.
- **조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF)**: failure before prosthetic loading; time window not given in this abstract.
- **보정 OR (adjusted Odds Ratio)**: odds ratio after covariate adjustment.
