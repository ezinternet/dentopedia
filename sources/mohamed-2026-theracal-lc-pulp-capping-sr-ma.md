## One-line Summary
SR+MA (BMC Oral Health 2026): 11 RCTs (2015–2025), 6–36 month follow-up; TheraCal LC shows comparable success to MTA, Biodentine, and calcium hydroxide for IPC and DPC in permanent teeth.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA specifically evaluating TheraCal LC across both IPC and DPC in permanent teeth
- 11 RCTs from 2015–2025 provide medium-term (6–36 month) comparative data
- Comparable success rates to established materials (MTA, Biodentine, CH)
- Consistently low pain scores with satisfactory dentin bridge formation
- ROB assessment: most studies "some concerns" — overall acceptable methodological quality

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR+MA; PRISMA 2020
- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, WoS, CENTRAL (up to August 2025)
- Inclusion: RCTs comparing TheraCal LC vs calcium hydroxide, Biodentine, or MTA in IPC or DPC of permanent teeth
- n: 606 records → 11 eligible RCTs
- Follow-up: 6–36 months
- Risk of bias: ROB-2 (independently by multiple reviewers)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- TheraCal LC success rate: comparable to MTA, CH, and Biodentine across included studies
- Pain scores: consistently low in TheraCal LC group
- Dentin bridge formation: satisfactory at follow-up periods
- No significant adverse outcomes reported

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Most studies "some concerns" for ROB (randomization, selective reporting)
- Relatively short maximum follow-up (36 months) — long-term TheraCal durability unclear
- Heterogeneity in success criteria definitions across studies

## 6. Related Work
- MTA: established gold standard for DPC; Biodentine: strong competitor
- TheraCal LC: light-cured property improves handling vs MTA; this SR confirms clinical viability

## 7. Glossary
- TheraCal LC: light-cured, resin-modified calcium silicate pulp-capping material (Bisco)
- IPC: Indirect Pulp Capping
- DPC: Direct Pulp Capping
- Dentin bridge: calcified barrier formed at pulp exposure site after successful capping
