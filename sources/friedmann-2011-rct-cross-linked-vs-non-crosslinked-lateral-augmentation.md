## Three-line Summary

Randomized pilot RCT (University of Witten, J Clin Periodontol 38(7):677-685, 2011) comparing ribose cross-linked collagen membrane (RCLM, Ossix Plus, n=17) vs non-cross-linked collagen membrane (n=20) for one-stage lateral bone augmentation at peri-implant dehiscence defects using biphasic calcium phosphate, with morphometric assessment at 6 months via customized casts.

The RCLM group achieved significantly greater lateral mineralized tissue gain at crestal level (**1.8 vs 0.7 mm, p=0.046**) and vertical gain (**1.1 vs 0.2 mm, p=0.035**); the broader measurement at the flap margin showed no significant difference (3.0 vs 2.1 mm, p=0.57), suggesting the cross-linked membrane's advantage is most pronounced in the critical crestal zone where barrier durability matters most.

Soft tissue dehiscence was more frequent in the RCLM group (70.5% vs 55%), but unlike non-cross-linked membranes, the RCLM maintained its barrier function through exposure events — a critical clinical advantage for cross-linked membranes.

## 세줄요약

파일럿 RCT (독일 비텐 의대, JCP 2011) — 리보스 가교 콜라겐 멤브레인(RCLM = Ossix Plus, n=17) vs 비가교 (n=20), β-TCP 이식재와 함께 임플란트 주위 골열개 측방 증대. 6개월 맞춤 캐스트 형태계측.

RCLM 그룹: 치조정 수준 측방 골량 **1.8 vs 0.7 mm (p=0.046)**, 수직 **1.1 vs 0.2 mm (p=0.035)** 유의하게 우세.

핵심 장점: RCLM은 노출이 발생해도(70.5%) 장벽 기능 유지 — 가교 덕분에 노출에 강함.

## 3. Clinical Interpretation

- **Cross-linked advantage is localized to the crestal zone** (most critical for implant outcomes) — broader measurements converge, suggesting the extra space maintenance matters most at the tight crestal margin
- **Dehiscence resilience** is a key RCLM benefit: Bio-Gide deteriorates when exposed; Ossix Plus maintains barrier function through exposure — explaining why higher dehiscence rate did not negate the bone gain advantage
- **Pilot size caveat**: n=17/20 — underpowered for definitive conclusions; nonetheless, this is the best available human RCT for RCLM vs NCL in lateral augmentation

## 4. Limitations

- Pilot study (underpowered, single-blind)
- One-stage protocol only (no delayed augmentation comparison)
- No CBCT volumetric measurement; cast-based morphometry
- JCP not OA; no PMC full text; abstract-based ingest
