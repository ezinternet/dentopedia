## Why Ingested
삭제량 부족(under-milling) 프로토콜 0.2/0.5/0.8mm에 따른 ITV vs ISQ 반응을 120개 임플란트에서 비교한 in-vitro 연구. ISQ는 under-milling 정도에 따라 유의한 차이 없음(p=0.166)이지만 ITV는 크게 달라짐을 실증했다. [[overviews/isq-loading-threshold]] 의 "ISQ vs ITV dissociation" 논의와 [[implants/versah-protocols]] 의 압축 프로토콜 근거를 보강한다.

## One-line Summary
In vitro study (n=120 implants, polyurethane type II-A bone blocks) showing that under-milling degree (0.2/0.5/0.8mm) significantly increases ITV but does NOT significantly alter ISQ, challenging the assumption that higher ITV always predicts higher ISQ-defined stability.

## 한줄요약
In-vitro 연구(n=120): under-milling 0.2/0.5/0.8mm별 ITV는 유의하게 달라지나(25→63 Ncm, p<0.001) ISQ는 무의한 차이(67.6/65.8/65.7, p=0.166) — ITV와 ISQ의 해리(dissociation) 실증.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrated dissociation between ITV and ISQ under different compression protocols in type II-A bone
- Under-milling of 0.8mm maximized ITV (63.2±14.9 Ncm) but did not elevate ISQ vs 0.2mm (65.7 vs 67.6)
- MANOVA: compression protocol explains 63.9% of ITV+ISQ combined variability (ηp²=0.639)
- Implant diameter explains 27.0% of variability; length only 12.1%
- No interaction effects between compression protocol, diameter, and length on ITV or ISQ

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: In-vitro (polyurethane blocks)
- n: 120 dental implants
- Bone model: polyurethane equivalent to type II-A bone (Lekholm & Zarb modified by Rosas et al.)
- Implant diameters: 3.5, 3.8, 4.5, 5.0 mm
- Implant lengths: 8.5, 10.0, 11.5, 13.0, 15.0 mm
- Under-milling protocols: 0.2 mm, 0.5 mm, 0.8 mm undersized preparation
- Outcomes: ITV (Ncm) and RFA/ISQ measured immediately after placement
- Statistical: MANOVA, multivariate effect size (ηp²); significance p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**ITV by protocol (p<0.001):**
- 0.8mm under-milling: 63.2 ± 14.9 Ncm
- 0.5mm under-milling: not specified in extracted text (intermediate)
- 0.2mm under-milling: 25.1 ± 8.3 Ncm

**ISQ by protocol (p=0.166 — NOT significant):**
- 0.2mm: 67.6 ± 5.4 ISQ
- 0.5mm: 65.8 ± 3.4 ISQ
- 0.8mm: 65.7 ± 4.0 ISQ

**Multivariate variance explained (combined ITV+ISQ):**
- Compression protocol: ηp²=0.639 (63.9%)
- Implant diameter: ηp²=0.270 (27.0%)
- Implant length: ηp²=0.121 (12.1%)
- Interactions: not significant (p>0.05)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Polyurethane blocks do not replicate biological bone healing response
- Single bone quality (type II-A) — results may differ in D3/D4 bone
- ISQ measured immediately post-placement only — no healing or remodeling phase
- No histological or biomechanical failure endpoint
- Latin American institutional authorship; single study without external validation

## 6. Related Work
- Brånemark original: 0.75mm undersized protocol; now modified to 0–1mm range
- Compressive protocols studied for immediate loading to improve primary stability
- High ITV ≠ high ISQ: this study provides direct in-vitro evidence for this dissociation

## 7. Glossary
- Under-milling (subpreparation): bone osteotomy prepared smaller than final implant diameter to create compression
- ITV (Insertion Torque Value): resistance during implant placement in Ncm
- ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient): Osstell RFA output 0–100
- MANOVA: multivariate analysis of variance
- ηp² (partial eta squared): multivariate effect size measure
- Type II-A bone: Lekholm & Zarb modified classification — medium to dense cancellous + thin cortical
