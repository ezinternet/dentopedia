## Why Ingested

[[infection-control/eswaramurthy-2021-contemporary-sterilization-protocols-healing-abutments]]의 핵심 결론("어떤 재멸균 프로토콜도 표면을 100% virgin 상태로 되돌리지 못한다")을 프로토콜-대-프로토콜 head-to-head in vitro로 정량 검증하기 위해 인제스트. 이 논문은 오토클레이브+초음파 baseline 위에 4개 화학·기계 보조법을 얹어 직접 비교하고, 어떤 군에서도 오염이 완전 제거되지 않음을 재확인하면서 동시에 3% NaOCl과 air polishing(glycine)을 body-surface 오염 저감에 가장 효과적인 보조법으로 지목한다 — "reuse decontamination 후보 순위"를 만든다는 점에서 anchor SR을 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary


In vitro study of 50 used healing abutments (DIO UFII) across 5 groups (all ultrasonic + autoclave;

+3% NaOCl / +12% CHX / +air polishing(glycine) / +3% H2O2): occlusal-surface residual protein did not differ between groups (p=0.26), but on the body/shank surface air polishing (1.7±1.1) and NaOCl (2.4±1.1) left significantly less contamination than control (6.1), H2O2 (4.6), or CHX (5.4) (p<0.05) — yet contamination was never fully eliminated in any group.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Direct head-to-head of 4 adjunct decontamination methods** on a common ultrasonic + autoclave baseline, on genuinely used clinical healing abutments (not artificially soiled specimens).
- **Quantifies the "no virgin surface" SR finding**: contamination was reduced but **never completely eliminated** in any of the 5 groups, confirming Eswaramurthy 2021 (SR) and Wadhwani (99% of HAs retain proteins after standard cleaning).
- **Ranks the adjuncts on body/shank surface**: 3% NaOCl and glycine air polishing are the two effective adjuncts; 12% CHX and 3% H2O2 were no better than the ultrasonic+autoclave control.
- **Surface-region dependence**: the occlusal (top) surface — with deep recesses that limit mechanical/chemical access — showed no method advantage, so residual contamination is region-specific.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Specimens**: 50 used healing abutments — DIO UFII (DIO Implant, Korea), Ø4.5 × 3 mm, each used once for 4–6 weeks in 50 patients.
- **Groups (n=10 each), all preceded by ultrasonic (Eurosonic 4D, 60°C, 10 min) then autoclave (Melag, 121°C)**:
  - G1 **Control**: ultrasonic + autoclave (15 min) only.
  - G2 **NaOCl**: + 3% sodium hypochlorite, 20 min, saline rinse 1 min, autoclave.
  - G3 **CHX**: + 12% chlorhexidine, 5 min, saline rinse, autoclave.
  - G4 **Air polishing**: + Perio-mate (NSK) glycine powder, 15 s, 5 mm, 45–60°, autoclave (10 min).
  - G5 **H2O2**: + 3% hydrogen peroxide, 1 min, saline rinse, autoclave.
- **Contamination measurement**: Phloxine B staining (fluorescein derivative binding proteins/peptides, incl. prion fragments); stereomicroscope ×15; 5 images per HA (4 body/shank + 1 occlusal/top); Cool PHP tools software; contamination expressed as % of surface area stained.
- **Statistics**: sample size 50 (α=0.05, power 0.80); one-way ANOVA, p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Occlusal (top) surface — % area contaminated (mean ± SD):**

| Group | Contamination (%) |
|---|---|
| Control | 5.5 ± 2.8 |
| NaOCl (3%) | 4.9 ± 2.5 |
| CHX (12%) | 5.3 ± 2.5 |
| Air polishing (glycine) | 3.1 ± 1.8 |
| H2O2 (3%) | 4.8 ± 3.1 |

→ **No significant difference among groups (p=0.26)** — attributed to HA geometry limiting access to deep occlusal recesses.

**Body / shank surface — % area contaminated (mean ± SD):**

| Group | Contamination (%) | vs control |
|---|---|---|
| Air polishing (glycine) | **1.7 ± 1.1** | significantly lower |
| NaOCl (3%) | **2.4 ± 1.1** | significantly lower |
| H2O2 (3%) | 4.6 ± 0.7 | ns |
| CHX (12%) | 5.4 ± 2.4 | ns |
| Control | 6.1 ± 2.3 | — |

→ **Significant group difference (p<0.001)**. Air polishing and NaOCl were each significantly lower than control / H2O2 / CHX; no difference air-polishing vs NaOCl; no difference among control / H2O2 / CHX. Null hypothesis (equal contamination) rejected.

- **Bottom line**: contamination **not completely eliminated in any group**; NaOCl + glycine air polishing (with autoclave + ultrasonic) most effective for the body surface; occlusal surface not improved by any adjunct.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=10 per group); in vitro (no clinical inflammation/failure outcome).
- **Bottom surface not evaluated**; **non-protein residue not identified** (Phloxine B detects proteins only).
- Contamination varies per patient (diet, oral microflora, hygiene) — a confounder across specimens.
- Air polishing (glycine/erythritol), though effective, **may alter surface roughness / wettability / surface energy** — needs further study before endorsing for repeated reuse.
- NaOCl + electrochemical decontamination reported more effective than NaOCl alone (enables multiple reuses without altering topography) — a suggested future direction.

## 6. Related Work

- Wadhwani et al: 99% of HAs retained proteins/peptides after standard cleaning + sterilization.
- Eswaramurthy 2021 SR: no protocol restores a 100% virgin surface (this study's anchor).
- Browne et al: reused impression copings + HAs reached sterility equal to new; crevicular IL-1β / TNF-α, bleeding/plaque indices not significantly different between unused vs reused HAs in patients.
- Kyaw 2023 RCT: NaOCl + electrochemical (combined) decontamination enables acceptable reuse.

## 7. Glossary

- **Healing abutment (HA)**: interim titanium/Ti-alloy element placed at 2nd-stage implant surgery; contacts soft tissue, forms a mucosal seal, shapes emergence profile. Manufacturer-labeled single-use but commonly reused.
- **Phloxine B**: fluorescein-derivative stain binding proteins/peptides (incl. prion fragments); used to visualize residual organic contamination.
- **Air polishing (glycine)**: low-abrasive glycine-powder air abrasion (e.g., Perio-mate/NSK) used to mechanically remove biofilm/residue.
- **Virgin surface**: the pristine, contaminant-free state of a new (never-used) abutment — the reference "no protocol achieves 100% virgin surface" benchmark.
