## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study (26 patients, 64 implants) comparing 1-year crestal bone loss around implants placed about 2 mm supracrestally (test, n=32) or level with the bone (control, n=32), stratified by mucosal thickness (thin, medium, thick).

Test implants lost 1.35 mm +/- 0.33 SD (thin, up to 2 mm), 0.32 mm +/- 0.44 SD (medium), and 0.12 mm +/- 0.16 SD (thick, 3.1 mm and more), with a significant thickness effect (F[2,29]=37.3; P=.000); control implants lost 1.8 mm +/- 0.52 SD, 1.62 mm +/- 0.63 SD, and 1.55 mm +/- 0.47 SD with no thickness effect (F[2,29]=0.73; P=.503).

Initial tissue thickness influenced crestal bone changes around supracrestally placed implants but not around implants placed at bone level — an abstract-only report whose subgroup definitions and measurement details are not retrievable.

시험군 골소실은 얇은 점막(최대 2 mm) 1.35 mm +/- 0.33 SD, 보통 0.32 mm +/- 0.44 SD, 두꺼운 점막(3.1 mm 이상) 0.12 mm +/- 0.16 SD로 두께 효과가 유의(F[2,29]=37.3; P=.000)했고, 대조군은 1.8 mm +/- 0.52 SD, 1.62 mm +/- 0.63 SD, 1.55 mm +/- 0.47 SD로 두께 효과가 없었다(F[2,29]=0.73; P=.503).

초기 점막 두께는 상방 식립 임플란트 주변의 치조정 골 변화에는 영향을 주었으나 골수준 식립에서는 그렇지 않았다 — 초록만 확보되어 하위군 정의·측정 세부는 확인 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Defines three mucosal-thickness bands at implant placement — thin (up to 2 mm), medium, and thick (3.1 mm and more) — and asks which can be called thin, medium, or thick.
- Crosses mucosal thickness with implant placement depth: 32 implants placed about 2 mm supracrestally (test) versus 32 positioned equal to the bone level (control).
- Shows a strong thickness-dependent crestal bone loss gradient in the supracrestal group (1.35 / 0.32 / 0.12 mm) that is absent in the bone-level group (1.8 / 1.62 / 1.55 mm).
- Provides early clinical support for the concept that initial soft-tissue thickness modifies crestal remodeling, later developed in this group's platform-switching and soft-tissue-thickening trials.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: 1-year prospective clinical study (stated in the title and abstract; no randomization stated in the available text)
- **Population**: 26 patients, 64 implants evaluated
- **Groups by placement**: test n=32, placed about 2 mm supracrestally; control n=32, positioned equal to the bone level
- **Groups by mucosa** (at time of implant placement): thin, medium, thick — thin defined as "up to 2 mm" and thick as "3.1 mm and more"
- **Outcome**: crestal bone changes measured at implant placement and after a 1-year follow-up
- **Statistics**: ANOVA across the three thickness groups, reported separately for test and control implants

## 6. Related Work

- Linkevicius 2009 (this paper): mucosal thickness × placement depth, 1-year crestal bone loss.
- Linkevicius 2015 (vertical soft tissue thickness, platform switching): same group's follow-up that quantifies the thin-mucosa penalty around platform-switched implants and reframes the ~2 mm thickness cut-off.
- Linkevicius 2015 (soft-tissue thickening, matching connection): same group's later test of thickening thin mucosa to reduce crestal bone loss.
- Zukauskas 2021 (placement depth × soft tissue thickness): later comparative trial bundling placement depth, connection geometry, and tissue thickness — the multidimensional extension of the two factors crossed here.

## 7. Glossary

- **Crestal Bone Loss**: radiographic vertical reduction of the marginal bone crest around an implant; the outcome measured at placement and at 1 year here.
- **Mucosal Tissue Thickness**: vertical thickness of the peri-implant soft tissue at the crest at implant placement; grouped here as thin (up to 2 mm), medium, and thick (3.1 mm and more).
- **Supracrestal Placement**: positioning the implant platform above the alveolar bone crest; about 2 mm in the test group of this study, versus equal to the bone level in controls.
- **ANOVA**: analysis of variance; used here to test whether crestal bone loss differed across the three tissue-thickness groups.
- **SD**: standard deviation, reported alongside each mean bone-loss value.
