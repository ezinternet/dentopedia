## Why Ingested

Large retrospective cohort (n=406 implants) from the Schwarz group (Frankfurt) comparing all four major surgical peri-implantitis protocols (OFD, implantoplasty, reconstructive, combined) under identical surface decontamination (titanium brush) and a single academic centre — enabling surgeons and non-surgical variables to be examined as confounders. Complements Park 2025 [[implants/peri-implantitis/park-2025-implantoplasty-vs-titanium-brushes-peri-implantitis-rct]] (RCT, implantoplasty vs titanium brush, resective) and Monje 2026 [[implants/peri-implantitis/monje-2026-flip-vs-plip-implantoplasty-extent-peri-implantitis-rct]] (quasi-RCT, FLIP vs PLIP, reconstructive), offering a real-world multi-protocol cohort to contextualise RCT findings.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (n=406 implants / 223 patients; single academic centre, Frankfurt; April 2018–June 2023) comparing four surgical peri-implantitis protocols — OFD (n=37), implantoplasty (n=39), reconstructive (n=241), combined (n=89) — all using titanium brush decontamination, with mean follow-up 30.80±19.62 months.

Treatment success at 1 year (max PD ≤5 mm + BOP ≤1 site + no suppuration) was 54.7% overall with no significant group differences: OFD 62.5%, implantoplasty 58.3%, reconstructive 55.4%, combined 48.3%; 40 implants (9.9%) lost and 38 (9.4%) required surgical retreatment over follow-up.

In multivariable analysis, systemic antibiotics (pre-op OR=3.54; pre+post-op OR=4.49) and surgeon experience (OR=0.12, more experience = better) predicted treatment success; molar site, antibiotics, and baseline suppuration predicted implant loss; overdenture and baseline max PD predicted surgical retreatment.

## 세줄요약

후향적 코호트연구 (Retrospective Cohort Study) (n=406개 임플란트/223명; 프랑크푸르트 단일 대학병원; 2018.4–2023.6): 4가지 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 외과 술식 — 개방형 피판술 (Open Flap Debridement, OFD, n=37), 임플란토플라스티 (Implantoplasty, n=39), 재생 수술 (Reconstructive, n=241), 병합술식 (Combined, n=89) — 전 군 티타늄 브러시 (Titanium Brush) 표면 처리, 평균 추적 30.80±19.62개월.

1년 치료 성공률 (최대 탐침깊이 (Probing Depth, PD) ≤5 mm + 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP) ≤1 부위 + 무화농) 전체 54.7%; 군간 유의한 차이 없음 (OFD 62.5%, 임플란토플라스티 58.3%, 재생 55.4%, 병합 48.3%); 추적 기간 중 임플란트 소실 40개 (9.9%), 외과적 재치료 38개 (9.4%).

다변수 분석에서 전신 항생제 (술전: OR=3.54, p=0.04; 술전+술후: OR=4.49, p=0.02) 및 술자 경험 (OR=0.12, p=0.003)이 치료 성공과 관련; 구치부·항생제·기저 화농이 임플란트 소실 예측, 완전 피개의치·기저 PD가 외과적 재치료 예측.

## Key Contributions

- Largest single-centre retrospective comparing all four major surgical peri-implantitis protocols under identical surface decontamination (titanium brush)
- No significant treatment success difference between OFD, implantoplasty, reconstructive, and combined at 1 year — protocol choice matters less than operator and adjuvants
- Systemic antibiotics (pre-op or pre+post-op) independently associated with treatment success — strongest modifiable predictor; also associated with higher implant loss (paradox likely reflects prescription in more severe cases)
- Surgeon experience strongly associated with success (OR=0.12; more experience = better) — underscores training and setting quality
- Combined approach carried numerically highest implant loss rate (16.9%) though not significant (p=0.13, underpowered)
- Post-hoc power 27% — severely underpowered for between-group protocol comparisons; "no difference" cannot be taken as equivalence

## Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Retrospective cohort (STROBE); chart review |
| Setting | Dept. Oral Surgery & Implantology, Goethe University Frankfurt, Germany |
| Period | April 2018 – June 2023 |
| n | 406 implants / 223 patients; 4 trained oral surgeons |
| Surface decontamination | Titanium brush (Hans Korea Co. Ltd.) — ALL groups |
| OFD | Open-flap debridement only (n=37) |
| Implantoplasty | Implantoplasty adjunct to OFD (n=39) |
| Reconstructive | Bone graft ± collagen membrane (n=241) |
| Combined | Implantoplasty + reconstructive (n=89) |
| Primary outcome | Treatment success at 1 year: max PD ≤5 mm + BOP ≤1 site + no suppuration (Criterion 1) |
| Follow-up timepoints | T0 (baseline), T1 (6 months), T2 (1 year), T3 (2 years), T4 (3 years), T5 (4 years), T6 (5 years) |
| Mean follow-up | 30.80 ± 19.62 months |
| Statistics | Mixed-effects logistic/quantile regression; FDR-adjusted pairwise; Firth logistic for small-n sensitivity |
| Post-hoc power | 27% (effect size Cramér's V=0.1035, n=274 implants at 1 year) |

## Key Results

| Outcome | OFD | Implantoplasty | Reconstructive | Combined | Overall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Success at 1 yr (Criterion 1) | 62.5% | 58.3% | 55.4% | 48.3% | **54.7%** |
| Implant loss (entire follow-up) | — | — | — | 16.9% (highest) | 9.9% (40/406) |
| Surgical retreatment | — | — | — | — | 9.4% (38/406) |
| Non-surgical retreatment | — | — | — | — | 32.8% (133/406) |

Between-group success rates: **no significant differences** (FDR-adjusted).

**Multivariable predictors of treatment success (1 year):**

| Predictor | OR | p |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-operative antibiotics | 3.54 | 0.04 |
| Pre- + post-operative antibiotics | 4.49 | 0.02 |
| Surgeon experience | 0.12 | 0.003 |

**Multivariable predictors of implant loss:**

| Predictor | OR | p |
|---|---|---|
| Molar site | 3.21 | 0.03 |
| Pre- + post-operative antibiotics | 3.13 | 0.02 |
| Baseline suppuration | 4.51 | 0.002 |

**Multivariable predictors of surgical retreatment:**

| Predictor | OR | p |
|---|---|---|
| Overdenture | 3.59 | 0.003 |
| Max PD at baseline | 1.30 | 0.001 |

**Longitudinal trend**: success rates declined over time; at 5 years (T6), overall success rates across criteria ranged approximately 27–42%.

## Limitations

- Retrospective design: allocation to protocol not randomised; selection bias unavoidable
- Post-hoc power 27% — substantially underpowered; null group differences cannot be interpreted as equivalence
- 4 surgeons with different experience levels; experience itself was a significant predictor
- Antibiotic regimens varied across surgeons/protocols (not standardised)
- Long follow-up but heavily unbalanced: large dropout by T4/T5/T6
- PMCID available but not the source of primary retrieval (chart review study)
- Defect morphology, baseline MBL, and implant surface type not systematically reported as predictors

## Glossary

- **OFD (Open Flap Debridement)**: Surgical access for mechanical debridement without adjunctive procedures; baseline surgical comparator
- **Implantoplasty**: Mechanical surface modification (grinding) to remove threads and create smooth implant surface supracrestally
- **Reconstructive**: Addition of bone substitute graft ± barrier membrane to regenerate intrabony defect
- **Combined**: Implantoplasty (supracrestal) + reconstructive (intrabony compartment)
- **Titanium brush**: Powered rotating brush for implant surface decontamination (here: Hans Korea Co. Ltd.); used as standard decontamination in all groups
- **Treatment success Criterion 1**: Max PD ≤5 mm + BOP ≤1 site + no suppuration
- **Firth logistic regression**: Penalised likelihood method for small-sample logistic models; used as sensitivity analysis for sparse outcome events
