## Why Ingested

Single case report documenting life-threatening airway compromise from a floor-of-mouth (FOM) hematoma caused by lingual mandibular cortical plate perforation during anterior mandible implant placement in an edentulous patient. Illustrates the awake fiberoptic intubation strategy and subsequent implant removal. → [[oral-surgery/vehmeijer-2016-airway-management-of-a-patient]]

## Three-line Summary

Abstract-only case report of a 62-year-old man presenting to the emergency department with airway compromise from a floor-of-mouth hematoma after implant surgery in the edentulous anterior mandible, caused by two malpositioned implants perforating the lingual cortical plate. Computed tomography angiography showed floor-of-mouth elevation with airway occlusion. Awake fiberoptic intubation was performed immediately, the two malpositioned implants were removed, and extubation occurred after 3 days.

## 세줄요약

62세 남성이 무치악 전방 하악 임플란트 수술 중 발생한 구강저 (Floor of the Mouth, FOM) 혈종으로 응급실에 내원하여 기도 폐쇄를 보인 단일 증례 보고이다. 전산화단층촬영 혈관조영술 (Computed Tomography Angiography, CTA)에서 구강저 상승과 기도 폐쇄, 두 개의 부정위치 임플란트로 인한 설측 피질골 천공이 확인되었다. 즉시 각성 굴곡기관지경 삽관 (Awake Fiberoptic Intubation)을 시행하고, 부정위치 임플란트 두 개를 제거한 뒤 3일 후 발관하였다.

## 7. Glossary

- **Floor of the Mouth (FOM)**: the soft-tissue region beneath the tongue; hematoma here can elevate the tongue and occlude the airway.
- **Lingual Mandibular Cortical Plate**: the inner (tongue-side) bony wall of the mandible; perforation during implant drilling can injure vessels supplying the floor of the mouth.
- **Awake Fiberoptic Intubation**: endotracheal intubation performed via a flexible bronchoscope while the patient remains conscious, preserving spontaneous ventilation in anticipated difficult airways.
- **Computed Tomography Angiography (CTA)**: cross-sectional imaging with intravenous contrast to evaluate vascular bleeding and airway anatomy.
