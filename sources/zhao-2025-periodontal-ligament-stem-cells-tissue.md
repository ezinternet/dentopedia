## Three-line Summary

치주인대 줄기세포(PDLSCs)가 기계적 힘(교정력)과 염증 신호에 어떻게 반응하고 치조골 리모델링을 조절하는지 종합 리뷰 (Stem Cell Res Ther 2025).

압박측: RANKL/M-CSF 분비 → 파골세포생성·골흡수; 장력측: BMP·OIF → 조골세포분화·골형성; 기계수용체(TRPV4, Piezo1, TRPC6, integrins)가 신호전달 매개.

염증환경에서 PDLSCs는 T세포·대식세포·호중구를 면역조절하고(PGE2·exosome·miR-155-5p 경로), 반대로 LPS·TNF-α는 PDLSCs 골형성능을 저해한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Comprehensive review of PDLSCs as the key regenerative cellular component of the periodontal ligament (PDL)
- PDLSCs first isolated by Seo et al. 2004; >100 population doublings self-renewal; higher embryonic stem cell marker expression (SSEA-4) than BMMSCs or DPSCs
- Multipotent: osteogenic, adipogenic, chondrogenic, cardiomyocyte, islet-like cell, neural, cementoblast differentiation capacity
- Mechanical force context: orthodontic tooth movement (OTM) in vivo models + in vitro (Flexcell, compression, tension, LMHF vibration)
- Inflammatory context: LPS, TNF-α, Porphyromonas gingivalis OMVs, Fusobacterium nucleatum

## 3. PDLSCs Under Mechanical Forces

### Microenvironment Changes
- Rapid upregulation of TNF-α, IL-1β, IFN-γ, PGE2, IL-6, MMPs → sterile inflammatory cascade
- M1-polarized macrophages drive osteoclastogenesis; M2 macrophages support bone repair on tension side
- T cells required for OTM: γδT cells initiate IL-17A → recruit monocytes/neutrophils at pressure sites; activated T cells produce RANKL
- B cells secrete RANKL and IL-6 (implicated but less characterized in OTM)

### PDLSC Biological Responses
- **Proliferation**: Nestin⁺ and PDGFRα⁺ populations increase within 3 days of force; CD146⁺Ki67⁺ PDLSC populations elevated at days 3 and 7
- **Compressive side**: PDLSCs secrete M-CSF and RANKL → osteoclast precursor maturation via RANK-RANKL-OPG axis → multinucleated osteoclasts → bone resorption (MMP-9, TRAP, cathepsin K secretion)
- **Tension side**: vascular perfusion enhanced; BMP/OIF/cytokines → mesenchymal precursor → osteoblast differentiation; COL-I, OCN, ALP synthesis
- **Apoptosis**: biphasic force-magnitude-dependent; heavy forces upregulate Bax, downregulate BCL-2; peak at day 7 post-force
- **Autophagy**: LC3-II and Beclin-1 elevated on compressive side; force-induced autophagy in PDLSCs modulates M1 macrophage polarization via AKT signaling
- **Pyroptosis**: caspase-1-dependent pyroptosis regulates OTM (Chen et al. 2024)

### Mechanotransduction Mechanisms
- **Cytoskeleton**: F-actin, microtubules, intermediate filaments reorganize under force; LMHF vibration produces thicker F-actin stress fibers
- **Ion channels**: TRPV4 modulates PDLSC stemness/bone remodeling; Piezo1 in Lepr⁺ PDLSCs converts mechanical stimuli to biological responses; TRPC6 (non-selective cation channel) regulates osteogenic differentiation
- **Integrins**: αVβ5 integrin mediates TGF-β1 upregulation under compressive force; LRP6 enhances F-actin dynamics and osteogenic differentiation under tension
- **GPCRs**: seven-transmembrane domain mechanosensors; α7 nAChR activation promotes Wnt signaling and osteoclastogenesis under cyclic compression
- **Non-coding RNAs**: 53 differentially expressed miRNAs under stretch; miR-146a and miR-34a downregulated by cyclic stretch → suppress osteogenic potential; circRNAs regulate tension-induced osteogenesis; lncRNAs regulate ECM gene expression via RNA-seq-identified lncRNA/mRNA networks
- **ECM remodeling**: MMPs/TIMPs balance critical for ECM degradation-regeneration during OTM; MMP-1 targets native collagen; mechanical tension upregulates ECM-related genes (collagen fibril assembly, hypoxia response)

## 4. PDLSCs Under Inflammatory Conditions

### Immunomodulatory Functions
- **T cells**: PDLSCs suppress T cell proliferation via PGE2-dependent mechanisms; allogeneic PDLSC transplantation upregulates PGE2; STRO-1⁺CD146⁺ PDLSCs inhibit DC-mediated lipid antigen presentation; exosomal miR-155-5p targets SIRT1 in CD4⁺ T cells → regulates Th17/Treg differentiation; PDLSCs modulate IL-6/IDO1 to expand Tregs and suppress Th17; Treg cells reciprocally enhance PDLSC osteogenic potential via Jagged1-Notch2 signaling
- **B cells**: PDLSCs inhibit B cell proliferation, chemotaxis, differentiation, IgM/IgG/IgA production; PD-L1/PD-1 binding suppresses B cell activation; PDLSCs can enhance B cell survival via IL-6 (context-dependent)
- **Macrophages**: allogeneic PDLSC transplantation → initial M1↑ then M1↓; CD163+ M0 from day 7; CD206+ M2 by day 21; PDLSC-conditioned medium enhances IL-10 production and TGF-β expression in M0 macrophages; LPS-pretreated PDLSCs release exosomal miR-143-3p → M1 polarization; LPS-stimulated PDLSCs EVs activate NLRP3 inflammasome in macrophages
- **Neutrophils**: PDLSCs secrete IL-6 → promotes neutrophil proliferation/survival; PDLSCs recognize P. gingivalis and facilitate neutrophil recruitment via paracrine signaling

### PDLSC Biological Changes Under Inflammation
- **Proliferation**: low-concentration TNF-α no effect; high concentration suppresses; low-dose LPS enhances, high-dose inhibits; F. nucleatum suppresses + triggers apoptosis/ferroptosis; P. gingivalis OMVs inhibit proliferation
- **Apoptosis**: LPS → Bax↑/BCL-2↓/caspase-3↑; F. nucleatum time-dependent; P. gingivalis OMVs dose-dependent; NF-κB pathway mediates H2O2-induced apoptosis; Nrf2 and lncRNA JHDM1D-AS1 are protective
- **Osteogenesis**: periodontitis-derived PDLSCs show diminished osteogenic potential; LPS downregulates BMAL1 → PANoptosis → inhibits osteogenic differentiation; healthy PDLSC-derived exosomes restore osteogenesis by suppressing excessive Wnt signaling; P. gingivalis OMVs inhibit osteogenesis via ER stress/PERK pathway
