## Why Ingested

CSPS (NovaMin, bioactive glass) 치약의 상아질 과민증 (Dentin Hypersensitivity, DH) 근거를 개별 SR이 아니라 "SR들의 SR" 수준에서 재점검하기 위해 인제스트. 위키가 보유한 [[dentin-hypersensitivity/defreitas-2021-bioactive-toothpastes-dentin-hypersensitivity-sr]]이 이 우산 리뷰의 9편 중 하나이며, 우산 리뷰는 그 결론의 확실성에 단서(저품질·높은 중복)를 단다. 후속 RCT [[dentin-hypersensitivity/creeth-2026-calcium-sodium-phosphosilicate-dentin-hypersensitivity-rct]]·메커니즘 [[dentin-hypersensitivity/joshi-2013-novamin-gluma-dentinal-tubule-occlusion-sem]]·최신 NMA [[dentin-hypersensitivity/gormley-2026-dentifrice-formulations-dentin-hypersensitivity-sr-nma]]와 함께 읽는다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42024617640; PRISMA; AMSTAR 2 + ROBIS; searched to Feb 2025) of 9 systematic reviews (5 with meta-analysis; published 2014–2021) on calcium sodium phosphosilicate (CSPS) toothpastes vs placebo/negative control for dentin hypersensitivity, covering 29 unique primary RCTs (13 placebo/negative-control).

Reviews generally favored CSPS over placebo/negative control, but no new pooling was done; overlap was very high (CCA 21.55% all trials, 31.73% placebo-controlled trials), only 1 of 9 reviews was AMSTAR 2 high quality (8 low/critically low), and all 9 had heterogeneity.

CSPS toothpaste is probably effective for DH, but the "9 reviews agree" picture is largely the same ~13 placebo-controlled trials counted repeatedly, so certainty is lower than the review count suggests.

## 세줄요약

우산 리뷰(PROSPERO CRD42024617640; PRISMA; AMSTAR 2 + ROBIS; 2025년 2월까지 검색): 2014–2021년 발표된 체계적 문헌고찰 9편(메타분석 5편)을 대상으로, 칼슘소듐포스포실리케이트(Calcium Sodium Phosphosilicate, CSPS) 치약 vs 위약·음성대조의 상아질 과민증(Dentin Hypersensitivity, DH) 효과를 평가. 고유 1차 RCT 29편(위약/음성대조 13편).

개별 리뷰들은 대체로 CSPS가 우월하다고 보고했으나 새로운 통합분석은 없었고, 중복도가 매우 높았으며(CCA 전체 21.55%, 위약대조 31.73%), AMSTAR 2 고품질은 9편 중 1편뿐(8편 저/극저품질), 9편 모두 이질성이 있었다.

CSPS 치약은 DH에 효과적일 가능성이 높으나, "9편이 일치"는 사실상 같은 위약대조 RCT 약 13편의 반복 집계이므로 리뷰 편수가 시사하는 것보다 확실성이 낮다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Claims to be the first umbrella review of CSPS toothpastes for DH.
- Quantifies primary-trial overlap across CSPS reviews (percentage overlap, CA, CCA) in two phases.
- Appraises each included review with AMSTAR 2 and ROBIS.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane; to February 2025; English only. 5681 records, 2803 duplicates removed, 85 full texts, 76 excluded, 9 SRs included.
- Eligible: SRs/MAs of clinical studies of CSPS toothpaste vs placebo (inert) or negative control (regular low-fluoride, e.g. 1450 ppm F) paste; positive-control comparisons were out of scope for the question.
- Included reviews: Talioti 2014, West 2015, Bae 2015, Zhu 2015, Hu 2018, Marto 2019, Hu 2019 (NMA), Martins 2020 (NMA), de Freitas 2021. CSPS RCTs per review 2–15 (CSPS 2.5–15%); follow-up immediate to 6 months.
- Overlap: Pieper CCA; 0–5 slight, 6–10 moderate, 11–15 high, >15 very high.
- Two reviewers independently, third resolved disagreements. No GRADE of the umbrella evidence, no new meta-analysis (heterogeneity precluded subgroup analysis).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Overlap Phase I (all CSPS trials): N=79 instances, r=29 unique trials, c=9 reviews; 65.51% overlap, CA 30.26%, CCA 21.55%. Phase II (placebo/negative control only): N=46, r=13 trials; 61.53% overlap, CA 39.31%, CCA 31.73%. Both "very high".
- AMSTAR 2: 1 high (Zhu 2015), 8 low/critically low. Typical flaws: no registered protocol/synthesis plan, incomplete searches (no grey literature), no list of excluded studies, funding of included trials not reported, RoB not integrated into interpretation.
- ROBIS: relevance OK for all; domain 4 (synthesis/findings) unclear/high for 7 of 9 — only Zhu 2015 and Hu 2018 low across domains (text: "nine reviews had unclear or high RoB" for domain 4 conflicts with "only two low" in the Discussion; read as 7 of 9).
- Review-level findings as tabulated: Bae 2015 SMD −2.36 (95% CI −3.72 to −1.00; I²=92%) vs placebo; Zhu 2015 5% CSPS better than negative control (moderate evidence); Talioti 2014 7.5% best, 2.5% ≈ placebo at 8 weeks; Hu 2019 CSPS ≈ potassium ≈ strontium, better than fluoride/placebo at 4 weeks; Martins 2020 CSPS among best vs tactile, air, cold stimuli; de Freitas 2021 5% NovaMin better than controls at all follow-ups.
- Authors' conclusion: CSPS effective vs placebo/negative control; cautious interpretation; Discussion nonetheless says findings "support the routine use" of CSPS toothpastes.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No pooled estimate and no GRADE rating; conclusions are a narrative tally of review conclusions.
- Overlap very high, so agreement among reviews is not independent confirmation. Authors note Phase II (placebo-controlled) overlap is higher than Phase I.
- "Negative control" pastes contain fluoride, which itself reduces DH, so effects against it may be underestimated and "placebo" is not inert (authors' own caveat).
- Included reviews end in 2021 although the search ran to Feb 2025; later CSPS evidence (e.g. new RCTs) is not captured. English only; 5 of 9 are not CSPS-specific (broad DH reviews with 2–13 CSPS trials), so CSPS data are a slice of each.
- Dose conclusions conflict (5% vs 7.5% vs 2.5%); too few 7.5% trials; authors call for pre-registered, standardized trials.
- Text inconsistency flagged: "routine use" clinical-implication sentence is stronger than the authors' own quality and heterogeneity findings.

## 7. Glossary
- CSPS (NovaMin, 45S5 bioactive glass): releases Ca/Na/phosphate/silicate, forming hydroxycarbonate apatite that occludes dentinal tubules.
- Umbrella review: systematic review of systematic reviews.
- CCA (corrected covered area): overlap index of primary studies across reviews; >15 = very high.
- AMSTAR 2: 16-item SR quality appraisal (high/moderate/low/critically low). ROBIS: risk-of-bias tool for SRs.
