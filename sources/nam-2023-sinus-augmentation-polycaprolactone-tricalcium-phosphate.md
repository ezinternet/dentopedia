## Why Ingested

위키의 상악동거상술 측방접근 폴더에는 혈소판 농축물 adjunct가 신생골을 늘리지만 임플란트 안정성·생존율은 바꾸지 못한다는 SR+MA([[sinus-lift/lateral/zhang-2025-platelet-bone-enhancers-dbbm-sinus]])와, 세포·생물학적 adjunct가 상악동거상술에서는 체적 이득이 없다는 SR+MA([[bone-regeneration/monje-2026-use-of-cells-and-or]])가 있으나, **입자형 이식재를 튜브형 3D 프린팅 스캐폴드로 치환해 동시(1단계) 식립**을 가능하게 하려는 전임상 근거는 없었다 — 본 논문이 그 빈칸(스캐폴드 + hUCMSC + PRP, minipig 20 임플란트, 조직형태계측 BIC)을 채운다.

## Three-line Summary

Animal study (5 minipigs, 10 maxillary sinuses, 20 simultaneously placed 10×4.0 mm implants; 12-week healing) comparing a tube-shaped 80:20 PCL-TCP honeycomb scaffold loaded with hUCMSCs plus PRP (n=5), with PRP alone (n=5), or scaffold alone (n=6) in one-stage lateral sinus augmentation.

Grayscale density (102.48±68.38 vs 58.10±29.69, P=0.045) and bone-implant contact (BIC 0.34±0.21 vs 0.09±0.19, P=0.016) were significantly higher with hUCMSCs+PRP than scaffold alone, but micro-CT bone volume fraction (4.42% vs 6.73%, P=0.366) and new bone height (1.26 vs 0.73 mm, P=0.056) were not, and 4 of 20 implants (2 each in the two cell/PRP groups, 0 in scaffold-only) were lost.

The scaffold can be friction-fitted onto the fixture to tent the sinus membrane and permit single-stage placement, but the added cell/PRP handling bought only interface-level (BIC) gains at the cost of more failures in an n=5, no-negative-control, 12-week pilot.

## 세줄요약

동물실험(미니피그 5마리, 상악동 10개, 동시식립 10×4.0 mm 임플란트 20개, 12주 치유): 튜브형 80:20 폴리카프로락톤-β삼인산칼슘(Poly(ε-caprolactone)-β-tricalcium phosphate, PCL-TCP) 허니컴 스캐폴드에 인간 제대유래 중간엽줄기세포(Human Umbilical Cord-derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells, hUCMSCs)와 혈소판풍부혈장(Platelet-Rich Plasma, PRP)을 적재한 군(n=5), PRP 단독군(n=5), 스캐폴드 단독군(n=6)을 측방 상악동거상술 1단계 식립에서 비교.

회색도(Grayscale) 102.48±68.38 vs 58.10±29.69(P=0.045), 골-임플란트 접촉률(Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 0.34±0.21 vs 0.09±0.19(P=0.016)는 hUCMSC+PRP군이 스캐폴드 단독군보다 유의하게 높았으나, 마이크로전산화단층촬영(Micro-CT) 골부피분율(Bone Volume/Tissue Volume, BV/TV) 4.42% vs 6.73%(P=0.366)·신생골높이(New Bone Height, NBH) 1.26 vs 0.73 mm(P=0.056)는 유의차 없었고, 임플란트 20개 중 4개(세포·PRP 두 군에서 각 2개, 스캐폴드 단독군 0개)가 소실됨.

스캐폴드를 픽스처에 마찰결합시켜 상악동 점막을 텐팅(tenting)하고 1단계 식립을 가능하게 하는 설계 이점은 확인되나, 세포·PRP 추가 처치는 계면(BIC) 수준 이득에 그쳤고 실패율은 오히려 높았음 — n=5, 음성대조군 부재, 12주 단기 예비실험이라는 한계.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a **geometry-first** solution to the particulate-graft problem in sinus augmentation: a tube-shaped, fused-deposition-modelled 80% PCL / 20% β-TCP scaffold whose 4.0 mm inner diameter friction-fits the 4.0 mm implant fixture and whose 8.0 mm outer diameter × 4.0 mm height sits on the elevated sinus floor, mechanically tenting the Schneiderian membrane instead of relying on particle packing.
- Reports the first in-vivo sinus dataset for this scaffold, with a three-arm design that isolates the scaffold (PCL-TCP), the growth-factor component (PCL-TCP+PRP) and the cell component (PCL-TCP+hUCMSCs+PRP).
- Provides xenogeneic evidence that human umbilical cord MSCs can be transplanted into non-immunosuppressed minipig sinuses without overt inflammatory reaction — the hUCMSC+PRP group showed new bone and scaffold degradation "without inflammatory reactions", whereas the scaffold-only group showed central fibrosis and inflammatory change within the scaffold.
- Quantifies the interface gain that matters for loading: BIC rose roughly 3.8-fold with hUCMSCs+PRP versus scaffold alone (0.34 vs 0.09), while bulk micro-CT bone volume did not differ.
- Documents the cost side of the same protocol: 4/20 implants floated into the sinus (2 in each cell/PRP arm, none in scaffold-only), i.e. the more complex the handling, the worse the primary stability in this model.

## Methodology

**Scaffold fabrication**
- PCL-TCP 80:20 composite (Osteopore International, Singapore), fused deposition modelling; lay-down pattern 0°/60°/120°, porosity 70%, mean pore size 0.515 mm by micro-CT; honeycomb array of interconnected equilateral triangles (3D interwoven bioresorbable filaments).
- Dimensions: inner diameter 4.0 mm, outer diameter 8.0 mm, height 4.0 mm, thickness 2.0 mm; male-female screw design on the outer surface; inner lumen sized to the implant fixture (non-threaded inner surface — manufacturing limitation acknowledged).
- Sterilized with low-temperature (36.5 °C) ethylene oxide.

**Cells and PRP**
- hUCMSCs (Dongguk University): FACS-positive for CD73/CD90/CD105/CD166, negative for CD14/CD31/CD34/CD45; tri-lineage differentiation (osteocyte/chondrocyte/adipocyte) confirmed; expanded to passage 5.
- Seeding: 4.0×10⁷ cells per scaffold by drop-down and soak with a micropipette at the time of surgery.
- PRP: 200 mL whole blood per animal from the internal jugular vein immediately pre-op, EDTA anticoagulation, 3,200 rpm × 5 min then 5,200 rpm × 5 min; 1.0–1.5 mL PRP per 50 mL blood; activated with thrombin 1,000 U + calcium gluconate (1:1) then mixed with PRP at 1:6 volume ratio; loaded 0.2–0.5 mL per scaffold.

**Animals and surgery**
- 5 male minipigs (Medi Kinetics Micropigs), 12.5–17.5 months, 32.8–41.0 kg; 2 implants per maxillary sinus → 20 implants in 10 sinuses.
- Lateral (infraorbital, inverted-L incision below the malar prominence) access, Schneiderian membrane elevation, 2 submerged implants per side (Osstem GSII, 10.0 mm length × 4.0 mm width) at the zygomatico-maxillary buttress.
- Groups: PCL-TCP+hUCMSCs+PRP (n=5), PCL-TCP+PRP (n=5), PCL-TCP only (n=6).
- Post-op oxytetracycline 3 mL IM and flunixin meglumine 2 mL IM for 1–3 days.

**Assessment (12-week sacrifice)**
- Fluorescent labelling chronology: tetracycline HCl at 3 and 11 weeks, calcein at 6 weeks, alizarin red at 9 weeks.
- Paranasal CT (Somatom Sensation 10; 1.5 mm slices) for stability/complications.
- Soft X-ray densitometry (Softex CMB-2, 30 kVp, 2 mA, 90 s; OPTIMAS 6.51): mean grayscale of 5 random points in regenerated vs host bone, plus % new bone area.
- Micro-CT (Skyscan 1072/1172; ×14, 80 kV/100 mA, 180° rotation, 0.9° step, 3.4 s exposure): ROI 4.0–5.0 mm high × 8.0–8.5 mm diameter, threshold 30–40 HU (chosen because bare PCL-TCP scaffolds read 0–15 HU), reporting BV, BV/TV, BS, BS/TV, BS/BV, i.S.
- Histomorphometry: non-decalcified 30 μm ground sections (Exakt), HE and Masson's trichrome, Kappa ImageBase; NBH (mm), NBA (mm²), BIC ratio in the sinus-exposed segment, RSA (residual scaffold area, mm²).
- Statistics: one-way ANOVA + Fisher PLSD, mean ± SD, P<0.05 (Statview).

## Key Results

**Implant survival / gross findings**
- 20 sites in 10 sinuses; healing uneventful per animal, but 4 implants "floated into the sinuses": 2 in PCL-TCP+hUCMSCs+PRP (analyzed n=5) and 2 in PCL-TCP+PRP (analyzed n=5); PCL-TCP-only had no failures (n=6). The discussion attributes this to poor primary stability of graft+implant in the more complex procedures.

**Histology / fluoromicroscopy**
- hUCMSCs+PRP: new bone and scaffold degradation from the sinus-floor interface into the scaffold interior without inflammatory reaction; new bone around the *outer* scaffold surface (tenting effect) and at the scaffold-implant interface; woven-bone-like tissue in the micropores.
- PCL-TCP only: immature bone at the sinus-floor/scaffold interface plus outer-surface bone (tenting also present), but the central region was fibrous with a few vessels and inflammatory changes.
- Fluorochrome chronology: new-bone activity at the BIC area in all groups, but "rare chronological ingrowth patterns" — i.e. the labels did not trace an orderly front of bone advancing through the scaffold.

## Limitations

- No negative control arm (empty sinus / no scaffold / particulate graft), so scaffold superiority over conventional grafting is not tested — only the incremental value of cells and PRP on the same scaffold.
- n=5 animals; 3-arm histomorphometry with 5/5/6 specimens is drastically underpowered — the study's own conclusion leans on non-significant trends (NBH P=0.056) alongside the two significant outcomes (grayscale P=0.045, BIC P=0.016), with no multiplicity adjustment.
- 4/20 implant failures (20%), all confined to the two cell/PRP arms, is far above clinical norms and plausibly reflects the added handling time/complexity rather than the biology — an unconfounded read of efficacy is impossible.
- 12-week healing only; no loaded implants, no long-term survival/MBL, and no measurement of the scaffold's mechanical contribution (tenting) versus its biological one.
- Scaffold inner surface non-threaded (manufacturing limitation), so the friction fit — the design's central mechanical claim — was not optimized; the authors propose a bolt-nut threaded inner surface as future work.
- Xenogeneic human cells in a porcine host: immunomodulation is argued from literature, not measured; cell viability/distribution after seeding was not quantified (no bioreactor, no homogeneous-seeding verification).
- Micro-CT thresholding (30–40 HU) to exclude PCL-TCP radiopacity and metal artifact may truncate low-density new bone and biases the volumetric comparison.

## Glossary

- **PCL-TCP scaffold** — Poly(ε-caprolactone)–β-tricalcium phosphate composite (80:20) printed as a honeycomb tube; PCL gives slow degradation and load-bearing, β-TCP adds bioactivity and cell-loading capacity.
- **hUCMSC** — Human umbilical cord-derived mesenchymal stem cell; xenogeneic, low-immunogenicity MSC source used here as the osteogenic cell component.
- **PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma)** — Autologous platelet concentrate releasing PDGF, TGF-β and IGF-I; used as the angiogenic/mitogenic gel phase.
- **BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact)** — Percentage of implant surface in direct contact with bone; here measured only in the sinus-exposed segment of the fixture.
- **Tenting effect** — Mechanical maintenance of the elevated Schneiderian membrane by a rigid body (scaffold), creating a protected space for bone formation instead of relying on particulate packing.
- **RSA (Residual Scaffold Area)** — Area of non-degraded scaffold remaining on the ground section; an inverse proxy for scaffold resorption.
