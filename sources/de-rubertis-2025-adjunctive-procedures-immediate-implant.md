## One-line Summary

SR+MA of 12 RCTs (484 implants, 6–36 months): adjunctive procedures (xenograft, CTG, SST) may modestly improve peri-implant tissue stability after IIP but significantly increase short-term postoperative pain (WMD +19.45 mm VAS, p=0.04), while implant survival remains high (96–100%) regardless of adjunctive use.

## 한줄요약

즉시식립(IIP) 보조술식(이종골이식·결합조직이식·소켓쉴드) 12 RCT SR+MA(484개 임플란트): 보조재는 협측골·연조직 보존에 미미하게 유리하지만 술후 통증을 유의하게 증가시키며(WMD +19.45mm VAS, p=0.04), 보조재 종류와 무관하게 생존율 96–100% 유지.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA restricted exclusively to RCTs comparing IIP + adjunctive procedure vs IIP alone (no historical controls)
- Implant survival 96–100% across all 12 RCTs regardless of adjunctive approach — baseline high success confirmed
- Meta-analysis of postoperative pain (4 RCTs): adjunctive procedures significantly higher pain (WMD 19.45mm VAS, 95% CI 0.55–38.36, p=0.04; I²=96.9%)
- Xenograft/CTG: modest horizontal ridge loss reduction (~1mm); GRADE low-to-moderate certainty
- SST (Venkatraman 2023): significantly less soft tissue change (−0.15 vs −0.64mm); higher PES (12.9 vs 11.2)
- CTG (Guglielmi 2022): significantly better horizontal ridge preservation (−1.16 vs −2.09mm at 1mm, p=0.0003); no vertical bone advantage
- Key gap identified: PES reported in only 4/12 RCTs; patient-reported esthetic outcomes (PROMs) rarely measured

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: SR+MA per PRISMA 2020; Cochrane Handbook
- PICO: Adults receiving IIP in esthetic zone; adjunctive material (xenograft, alloplast, CTG, SST); vs IIP alone; implant survival, esthetic outcomes (PES/WES), peri-implant hard/soft tissue dimensions, postoperative morbidity
- Eligibility: RCTs only; IIP in esthetic area; ≥6 months follow-up; single implants
- RoB: Cochrane RoB 2 (2 reviewers, κ=0.91)
- GRADE: for each outcome domain
- Statistics: random-effects REML; WMD with 95% CI for continuous data (pain); I² for heterogeneity; Egger's test deferred (n<10 for quantitative synthesis)
- Pain score harmonization: 0–10 VAS → 0–100 scale; NRS treated as equivalent to VAS

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Included studies (12 RCTs, 484 participants, 1 implant each):**
- Xenograft (7 RCTs): Cardaropoli 2014, Bittner 2020, Mastrangelo 2018, Jacobs 2020, Girlanda 2019, Grassi 2019, Fettouh 2023
- Alloplastic (2 RCTs): Daif 2013 (β-TCP), Naji 2021 (nanocrystalline calcium sulphate)
- Mixed graft (1 RCT): El Ebiary 2023 (DBBM + autogenous bone)
- CTG (1 RCT): Guglielmi 2022
- SST (1 RCT): Venkatraman 2023

**Implant survival:** 96.0–100% in all groups; no group differences attributable to adjunctive approach

**Postoperative pain (meta-analysis, 4 RCTs):**
- WMD +19.45mm (95% CI 0.55–38.36; p=0.04) — adjunctive > control
- I²=96.9% (high heterogeneity; meta-analysis considered exploratory)

**Hard tissue (xenograft):**
- Cardaropoli 2014: ridge width Δ 0.69±0.68 (graft) vs 1.92±1.02mm (control) — significantly less loss
- Girlanda 2019: BW at 1mm apical to crest 6.57±0.45 vs 6.07±0.24mm — significantly greater with graft
- Fettouh 2023: horizontal collapse 0.5±0.4 vs 0.8±0.4mm — significantly less with DBBM
- Jacobs 2020, Mastrangelo 2018: no significant inter-group bone difference

**Soft tissue (CTG):**
- Guglielmi 2022: soft tissue contour ΔT0–T6 −0.32 to −0.04mm (CTG) vs −1.94 to −1.08mm (control); p<0.05
- KTW gain: 0.60±1.71mm (CTG) vs 0.14±1.28mm (control)

**SST:**
- Venkatraman 2023: soft tissue change −0.15±0.86mm (SST) vs −0.64±0.35mm (control); p<0.05
- PES T12: 12.9±0.8 (SST) vs 11.2±1.4 (control)

**GRADE certainty:**
- Implant survival: moderate (downgraded for indirectness — short FU, small n)
- Esthetic outcomes (PES): low (heterogeneity, non-standardized indices)
- Hard/soft tissue dimensions: low (heterogeneous interventions, small n)
- KTW: moderate
- Postoperative morbidity: moderate (downgraded for inconsistency I²≈97%)

**RoB:** 2/12 low risk; 4/12 high risk (Domains 2, 4); 10/12 moderate-to-high

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Quantitative synthesis only feasible for postoperative pain (4 RCTs); all other outcomes qualitative
- High heterogeneity in pain meta-analysis (I²=96.9%): mixed graft types (xenograft, alloplast, CTG) and surgical approaches (flapless vs flap)
- PES assessed in only 4/12 RCTs; PROMs inconsistently reported
- No standardized hard/soft tissue measurement methods across trials
- Follow-up range 6–36 months — most studies short-term only
- One trial per comparator type for CTG and SST — no pooling possible
- No provisionalization-only arm; CTG comparison unavoidable confounds
- Future: standardized PES/WES reporting; PROMs integration; provisionalization-specific RCTs

## 6. Related Work

- Zaki et al. 2021 — SR: xenografts in IIP reduce ridge resorption and improve esthetics
- Seyssens et al. 2021 — CTG in IIP: −0.41mm midfacial improvement; 12× lower recession risk
- Lambert et al. 2025 — soft tissue augmentation in IIP: reduces recession, no PES advantage

## 7. Glossary

- **IIP (Immediate Implant Placement, 즉시식립)**: implant placed at time of tooth extraction
- **DBBM (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, 탈단백 소이종골)**: xenograft bone substitute; most commonly used in included trials
- **CTG (Connective Tissue Graft, 결합조직이식)**: subepithelial soft tissue graft to augment buccal volume
- **SST (Socket Shield Technique, 소켓쉴드술식)**: retention of buccal root fragment to preserve bundle bone
- **WMD (Weighted Mean Difference)**: pooled mean difference adjusted for study weight
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score, 핑크심미점수)**: 7-item peri-implant soft tissue esthetic index (0–14 max)
- **GRADE**: evidence certainty framework (high/moderate/low/very low)
- **β-TCP (Beta-tricalcium phosphate)**: alloplastic resorbable bone substitute
