## Why Ingested

[[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/donertas-2026-gbt-subgingival-debridement-gcf-biomarkers]] measures individual GCF inflammatory biomarkers (IL-1β, MMP-8) after subgingival debridement; this broad narrative review supplies the upstream framework — which oral/salivary/blood/GCF biomarkers matter, why (IL-1β/TNF-α/RANKL-OPG/microbial DNA), and which emerging point-of-care platforms (lateral flow, biosensors, microfluidics) could turn those single-marker measurements into multiplex chairside risk stratification. It also anchors the pathogen mechanisms already held in [[oral-microbiology/hajishengallis-2014-porphyromonas-gingivalis-host-manipulation]] and [[oral-microbiology/lamont-2014-polymicrobial-synergy-dysbiosis-inflammatory]] into the diagnostics/biomarker thread.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of periodontal–systemic health linkages across seven organ systems (CVD, diabetes, adverse pregnancy, Alzheimer's, cancer, RA, respiratory) and their shared inflammatory pathways, synthesizing saliva/blood/GCF biomarker panels (IL-1β, TNF-α, CRP/IL-6, MMP-8/-9, RANKL-OPG, microbial DNA) as diagnostic targets.

Severe periodontitis (~1.1B cases globally; 47% US adults ≥30y) associated with 12% higher CHD risk (meta-analysis, n=86k), 3× diabetes/respiratory infection risk, and elevated cancer risk (esophageal +39%, gastric +13%, colorectal +21%, pancreatic +35%); GCF RANKL:OPG ratio and salivary IL-1β/TNF-α emerging as early detection biomarkers.

AI-integrated multiplex point-of-care platforms (lateral flow, microfluidics, biosensors) proposed to enable rapid chairside multiplex detection and personalized systemic risk stratification, but translation blocked by biomarker variability, lack of standardized cut-offs, and need for large multicenter validation studies across populations.

중증 치주염(~11억 명 유병률; 미국 성인 ≥30세 47%) — 관상동맥질환 위험 12% 증가(메타분석, n=86k), 당뇨병/호흡기감염 3배 위험, 암 위험도 상승(식도 +39%, 위 +13%, 대장 +21%, 췌장 +35%); GCF RANKL:OPG 비율과 타액 IL-1β/TNF-α가 조기 검출 바이오마커로 부상.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Consolidates the **oral–systemic axis** across seven organ systems into one framework, framed around shared inflammatory pathways (IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α, CRP) and microbial dissemination via bacteremia.
- Organizes **diagnostic biomarkers by compartment** — saliva (IL-1β, TNF-α, MMPs, microbial DNA), blood (CRP, IL-6, AGEs), GCF (RANKL, OPG, MMP-8/-9; RANKL:OPG ratio as bone-turnover marker).
- Contrasts **labside (ELISA, PCR)** — high accuracy but slow, costly, centralized, single-parameter — with **chairside/point-of-care (lateral flow assays, microfluidics/lab-on-a-chip, biosensors)** — rapid, portable, multiplex-capable.
- Argues for **AI-integrated multiplex platforms** (smartphone-linked) as the path to comprehensive early detection and personalized systemic risk stratification.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Selected quantitative claims reproduced from cited sources (the review does not generate new data):

**Epidemiology / systemic associations**
- Severe periodontitis: ~1.1 billion prevalent cases (2019); age-standardized prevalence +8.44% 1990–2019. US adults ≥30 y: ~47.2% any periodontitis, ~70% at ≥65 y.
- CVD: meta-analysis of 5 cohorts (n=86,092) → **12% higher CHD risk**; case–control (n=1,423) → **OR 2.22**. Pg/Aa/Tf DNA found in atheromatous plaques.
- Diabetes: diabetics **3× more likely** to develop periodontitis; type 1 diabetes periodontitis prevalence 58% vs 15% in non-diabetics; T1D children gingivitis 21% / periodontitis 6%.
- Cancer: oral-cancer meta-analysis (n=3,183) higher risk with periodontal disease; GI cancer risk increases — esophageal +39%, gastric +13%, colorectal +21%, pancreatic +35%, liver +9%.
- Respiratory: periodontitis patients **3× more likely** to develop nosocomial pneumonia.

**Biomarkers**
- Saliva: IL-1β and TNF-α — high sensitivity for active periodontal inflammation, useful for early detection; specificity drops under systemic inflammation.
- GCF: RANKL:OPG ratio — reliable specificity for bone resorption / progression; MMP-8, MMP-9 elevated.
- Blood: CRP + IL-6 combined — significant CVD predictor; AGEs elevated in severe periodontitis; periodontal treatment lowers systemic markers.

**Diagnostic platforms**
- Labside: ELISA (antibody–antigen, colorimetric/fluorescent/chemiluminescent; high specificity/reproducibility, slow, centralized), PCR (high sensitivity for pathogen DNA — Pg, Td, Fn; contamination-sensitive, costly).
- Chairside: LFAs (immunochromatographic, minutes, cheap, lower specificity than ELISA/PCR; commercial exemplars OraQuick, BinaxNOW), microfluidics/lab-on-a-chip (multiplex, low sample volume; GeneXpert etc.), biosensors (bio-recognition + transducer, real-time, miniaturizable/wireless; Simoa, i-STAT).

**Treatment → systemic markers**
- SRP (gold-standard non-surgical) reduces CRP and IL-6. Adjuncts: chlorhexidine chips, minocycline microspheres, systemic amoxicillin+metronidazole for aggressive/refractory cases; surgery (flap/GTR/graft) for advanced; LANAP and sub-antimicrobial-dose doxycycline (host modulation) as adjuncts.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Narrative review** — selective citation, no systematic search or quantitative pooling; associations are largely observational, causation unproven (confounding by smoking/socioeconomic status, reverse causation, need for longitudinal interventional trials).
- Biomarker translation blocked by inter-individual variability (genetics, lifestyle, comorbidity), lack of standardized cut-offs, sample-collection differences (saliva vs blood vs GCF), and validation confined to research settings. Large multicenter studies needed for robust thresholds.
- Chairside platforms need cost reduction, manufacturing simplification, reliability, and regulatory clearance (FDA/EMA, post-market surveillance).
- Future: device development, biomarker validation across populations, AI-driven multiplex analysis, personalized/precision periodontics, smartphone/cloud remote monitoring.

## 7. Glossary

- **GCF** — gingival crevicular fluid; local exudate at the gingival sulcus carrying inflammatory mediators and microbial products.
- **RANKL / OPG** — receptor activator of NF-κB ligand / osteoprotegerin; RANKL drives osteoclast bone resorption, OPG is its decoy receptor; RANKL:OPG ratio in GCF indexes bone turnover.
- **MMP-8 / MMP-9** — matrix metalloproteinases; collagen-degrading enzymes elevated in active periodontitis.
- **LFA** — lateral flow assay; immunochromatographic point-of-care strip test.
- **Lab-on-a-chip / microfluidics** — miniaturized multiplex platforms handling microliter fluid volumes for concurrent biomarker detection.
- **Biosensor** — biological recognition element coupled to a physical transducer for real-time analyte detection.
- **Gingipains** — *P. gingivalis* cysteine proteases; degrade host proteins/complement, modulate immunity.
- **AGE / RAGE** — advanced glycation end products and their receptor; AGE–RAGE signaling amplifies inflammation in diabetes.
