## Why Ingested

First quantitative study measuring leadership → patient safety culture correlation in a dental setting using validated instruments (TLS + DMOSOPS). Adds a missing dimension to [[wiki/overviews/patient-safety-culture-dentistry-overview]] which relied on Schmutz 2019 (general healthcare SR+MA) for the quantitative pillar — this is dental-specific quantification of the leadership↔safety relationship.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional study at Riphah Dental Hospital, Islamabad (n=104 dentists, response rate 86.7%): assessed transformational leadership style (Transformational Leadership Scale, TLS, 15 items) and patient safety culture (dental-adapted DMOSOPS, 44 items/10 dimensions).

Overall leadership (Total Leadership Score) was moderately correlated with Total Patient Safety Score (r=0.455, p<0.001); inspirational communication (r=0.504) and supportive leadership (r=0.373) drove most of the association; vision dimension had no significant correlation with safety.

High positive responses in teamwork (82.91%), organizational learning (78.41%), patient care tracking (77.05%); lowest in work pressure and pace (32%) and personal recognition (57.14%); staff felt errors were held against them (positive response 17.2%) and afraid to ask questions (41.67%).

## 세줄요약

파키스탄 Riphah Dental Hospital 단면조사(n=104 치과의사, 응답률 86.7%): 변혁적 리더십(TLS 15항목)과 환자안전문화(DMOSOPS 44항목)를 측정.

전체 리더십(TLS)과 전체 환자안전점수(TPSS) 간 중간 상관관계 r=0.455(p<0.001); 영감적 소통(r=0.504)·지지적 리더십(r=0.373)이 주 구동인자; 비전 차원은 유의 상관 없음.

팀워크 양성응답 82.91%·조직학습 78.41%로 높은 반면, 업무 압박 32%·"오류가 나를 불리하게 이용된다" 양성응답 17.2%·"질문하기 두렵다" 41.67% — 비처벌 문화 구축 필요성 시사.

## Key Contributions

- **First dental-specific quantification of leadership → safety culture**: r=0.455 (p<0.001) — moderate positive correlation
- **DMOSOPS application**: dental-adapted MOSPSC (10 safety dimensions + 2 supplementary); high teamwork (82.91%), high organizational learning (78.41%)
- **Vision ≠ safety**: 'vision' leadership dimension had no/very weak correlation with patient safety dimensions — having a long-term vision does not itself improve day-to-day safety
- **Recognition gap**: 'acknowledges improvement in my quality of work' positive response only 19% — leadership fails at recognition despite otherwise supportive stance
- **Work pressure crisis**: 'we often feel rushed' (19.23%), 'too many patients for providers' (21.57%) — high-volume settings undermine safety regardless of leadership
- **Blame culture persists**: errors held against staff (17.2% positive = only 17.2% feel safe), afraid to ask questions (41.67%), difficult to voice disagreement (33.33%)
- **Co-Lead recommendation**: Collective Leadership for Safety Cultures programme — leadership as team responsibility, applicable to dental multidisciplinary settings

## Methodology

- **Design**: Hospital-based cross-sectional study
- **Setting**: Riphah Dental Hospital (RDH), Islamabad, Pakistan
- **n**: 104 dentists (out of 120 eligible; 10 on leave, 6 refused); response rate 86.7%
- **Breakdown**: house officer residents 57.7%, postgraduate residents 24%, demonstrators 6.7%, consultants 11.6%
- **Instruments**: Transformational Leadership Scale (TLS; 5 subdimensions: vision, inspirational communication, intellectual stimulation, supportive leadership, personal recognition) + DMOSOPS (44 items, 10 dimensions)
- **Analysis**: Pearson's correlation coefficient; responses dichotomised into positive/negative

## Limitations

- Cross-sectional design; cannot determine causality or leadership style changes over time
- Single-centre (RDH Islamabad); limited generalizability
- Self-reported instruments; social desirability bias possible
- Dentists only; excluded dental nurses, technicians, hygienists, administrative staff

## Glossary

- **TLS (Transformational Leadership Scale)**: 15-item validated instrument with 5 subdimensions: vision, inspirational communication, intellectual stimulation, supportive leadership, personal recognition
- **DMOSOPS (Dental Medical Office Survey on Patient Safety Culture)**: dental-adapted MOSPSC; 44 items across 10 safety culture dimensions
- **Transformational leadership**: leadership style inspiring followers to exceed expected performance through vision, motivation, intellectual stimulation
- **Co-Lead**: Collective Leadership for Safety Cultures programme; treats leadership as team responsibility rather than single individual's role
