## Three-line Summary

Prospective pilot study (N=22 patients, 22 posterior 3-unit FDPs from 5Y-PSZ Lava Esthetic, Amann Girrbach milling, RelyX Universal cement; LMU Munich; follow-up 3 years; modified FDI criteria) reporting 100% survival with zero mechanical complications and 3 biological complications (14.3%) at 3 years.

No chipping, fractures, or loss of retention occurred; 3 biological complications (endodontic treatment, occlusal sensitivity, caries) were all successfully managed; FDI criteria rated clinically acceptable or better; PPD stable at 2.2–2.8 mm; wear of glaze layer visible in 16 restorations after 2 years (expected).

First published clinical data on 5Y-PSZ cubic zirconia multi-unit FDPs; results support monolithic high-translucent zirconia as viable posterior FDP option, eliminating chipping risk while providing aesthetics comparable to LD; worst-case survival (counting dropout as failure) = 95.5%; longer follow-up and larger samples needed.

## 세줄요약

전향연구 파일럿(N=22, 구치부 3유닛 FDP, 5Y-PSZ Lava Esthetic, Amann Girrbach 밀링, RelyX Universal, LMU 뮌헨, 3년 추적): 생존율 100%, 기계적 합병증 0건, 생물학적 합병증 3건(14.3%) — 모두 완전 해결.

치핑·파절·유지력 소실 없음; FDI 기준 전항목 임상적 허용 이상; PPD 안정(2.2–2.8 mm); 2년 후 16개 보철물에서 광택층 마모 가시적(예상 범위).

5Y-PSZ 입방형 지르코니아 다유닛 FDP 최초 임상 데이터 — 치핑 위험 없이 LD 수준 심미성 제공; 최악의 경우(탈락=실패) 3년 생존율 95.5%; 장기·대규모 추적 필요.

## Why Ingested

5Y-PSZ(고투명 입방형 지르코니아)의 다유닛 FDP 임상 성능 최초 데이터 — [[prosthetic-materials/kui-2023-translucent-zirconia-fixed-prosthodontics-overview]]에서 임상 근거 부족이 지적된 갭을 직접 채우며, [[prosthetic-materials/ban-2021-classification-properties-dental-zirconia-implant]]의 5Y-TZP 적응증(저하중 전치부) 주장에 반박하는 첫 다유닛 구치부 임상 근거.

## One-line Summary

Prospective pilot (N=22, 3-unit posterior FDPs from 5Y-PSZ, 3yr) showing 100% survival with zero mechanical complications, all FDI criteria acceptable — first clinical evidence supporting monolithic high-translucent zirconia for posterior multi-unit FDPs.

## 한줄요약

전향 파일럿(N=22, 구치부 3유닛 5Y-PSZ FDP, 3년): 생존율 100%, 기계적 합병증 0건, FDI 전항목 허용 이상 — 5Y-PSZ 고투명 지르코니아 다유닛 FDP 최초 임상 데이터.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First prospective clinical data on 5Y-PSZ (cubic zirconia) in multi-unit posterior FDPs
- 100% survival at 3 years with zero mechanical complications
- Elimination of chipping risk through monolithic design confirmed clinically
- Material characterization: Lava Esthetic — 800 MPa flexural strength, 1,200 HV, 216 GPa elastic modulus
- Air abrasion (50 μm alumina) + RelyX Universal cement protocol validated for 5Y-PSZ FDPs

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design:** Prospective single-arm pilot study
- **Ethics:** Ethics committee approval UE No 19–0243
- **Setting:** University Hospital LMU Munich
- **Sample:** 22 patients, 22 posterior 3-unit FDPs (44 abutment teeth); 18 maxillary, 26 mandibular
- **Material:** Lava Esthetic 5Y-PSZ (Solventum Dental Solutions); 5-axis milling (Ceramill Motion 2, Amann Girrbach)
- **Cementation:** Air abrasion 50 μm Al₂O₃ at 0.1 MPa; RelyX Universal dual-cure self-adhesive resin cement
- **Follow-up:** Baseline (4 wks), 1yr, 2yr, 3yr
- **Evaluation:** Modified FDI criteria (esthetic/functional/biological); PPD; radiographs at 3yr; patient satisfaction

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| 3-year survival | 100% (21/21) |
| Worst-case survival (1 dropout) | 95.5% |
| Mechanical complications | 0 (zero chipping, fractures, loss of retention) |
| Biological complications | 3 (14.3%; ~4.76%/yr) |
| FDI criteria | All acceptable or better |
| PPD | 2.2–2.8 mm (stable) |

**Biological complications:**
1. Endodontic treatment (preparation trauma) — FDP remained in service
2. Occlusal sensitivity → resolved with adjustment
3. Cervical caries → GIC filling; asymptomatic

**Material:** Lava Esthetic — 800 MPa, 1,200 HV, 216 GPa; shade-graded; inherent fluorescence

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Pilot study: small sample (n=22); single-arm; no comparator
- Short observation period (3 years)
- COVID-19 impact on follow-up windows
- Generalizability limited — need multicenter RCT
- Authors note: wear of glaze layer visible at 2yr (expected; not classified as failure)
