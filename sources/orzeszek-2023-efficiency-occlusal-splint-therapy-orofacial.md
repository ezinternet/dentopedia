## Why Ingested

Adds an RCT-only systematic review restricted to painful masticatory-muscle disorders (myalgia/myofascial pain) to the held splint evidence, where [[wiki/tmj/zhang-2020-occlusal-splint-tmd-rct-sr]] and [[wiki/tmj/singh-2024-occlusal-interventions-tmd-cochrane-sr]] cover TMD broadly; it also supplies head-to-head splint vs acupuncture/laser/physiotherapy/education comparisons that complement [[wiki/tmj/honnef-2022-stabilization-splints-signs-symptoms-muscular-tmd]]. Identified by TMD surveillance sweep (PMID 36978070).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA 2020; PubMed, CINAHL, Scopus; searched to June 2022) of 13 RCTs (589 patients) comparing occlusal splints with no treatment, education/counselling, acupuncture, low-level laser, LED, physical therapy, Kinesio Taping, myofunctional therapy and sensorimotor training in orofacial myalgia and myofascial pain.

All 13 trials were at high risk of bias (participants cannot be blinded; allocation concealment unclear in all 13); no meta-analysis was possible, and in most head-to-head comparisons splints performed similarly to the alternatives, with counselling/education in one RCT giving better spontaneous pain relief at 3 months (p=0.034).

The authors conclude evidence is insufficient to show that splints offer an advantage over other interventions or no treatment for muscle pain; high-quality, blinded, larger, long-term trials are needed.

## 세줄요약

근육통 (myalgia) 및 근막통 (Myofascial Pain, MP)에서 교합장치 (Occlusal Splint, OS)를 무치료·교육/상담·침·저출력 레이저 (Low-Level Laser Therapy, LLLT)·발광다이오드 (LED)·물리치료 등과 비교한 RCT 13편 (589명)의 체계적 문헌고찰 (PRISMA 2020; 2022년 6월까지 검색).

13편 모두 비뚤림 위험 높음 (참여자 눈가림 불가, 배정은폐 전부 불명확)이며 메타분석은 불가능했고, 대부분의 직접 비교에서 교합장치는 대안 치료와 비슷했으며 한 RCT에서는 교육군이 3개월 자발통 감소가 더 컸음 (p=0.034).

교합장치가 무치료나 다른 중재보다 우월하다고 볼 근거는 불충분하며, 눈가림을 갖춘 대규모 장기 시험이 필요함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR restricted to painful masticatory-muscle disorders (earlier reviews mixed myalgia with joint and disc disorders, per the authors).
- Groups trials by comparator: acupuncture, laser, other devices, LED, physical therapy/Kinesio Taping/myofunctional therapy, education/counselling.
- Concludes evidence on splint superiority is insufficient.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PRISMA 2020; PubMed, Scopus, CINAHL; English RCTs Jan 2010 to Jun 2022; last search 4 June 2022.
- Adults >=18 with RDC/TMD or DC/TMD muscle pain; groups >=20 patients.
- Two independent reviewers, third for disputes; Cochrane RoB tool; narrative synthesis.
- Flow: 474 records, 107 after duplicates, 37 removed at screening, 13 included.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 589 participants: 276 OS, 108 conservative/counselling, 30 acupuncture, 30 laser, 23 sensorimotor training, 16 Kinesio Taping, 10 myofunctional therapy, 37 manual therapy, 59 LED. Splints: stabilization 207, reflex 59, repositioning 10. Follow-up 4 weeks to 6 months.
- Risk of bias: all 13 high overall; allocation concealment unclear in 13; randomization unclear in 8.
- Splint vs acupuncture (Grillo, Barrero): similar short-term effects.
- Splint vs laser: Oz, no between-group difference; Manfredini, at 3 weeks VAS fell only with laser (p=0.018), at 6 months all groups improved (laser p=0.001, appliance p=0.002, counselling p=0.048).
- Sensorimotor training vs splint (Giannakopoulos, n=45): pain reduction 53% vs 40%, no between-group difference.
- Physical therapy vs splint (Grootel, n=72): long-term success 51-60% in both; physiotherapy 10.4 weeks shorter.
- Education vs splint (Michelotti, n=44): education better for spontaneous pain at 3 months (p=0.034; effect size 0.33).
- NTI-tss: no added benefit over counselling (Erbasar p=0.922; Conti).
- LED + splint superior to isolated treatments (Costa, p<0.05).
- Kinesio Taping at least as effective as splint in sleep bruxism (Keskinruzgar).
- Prefabricated SOLUBrux vs laboratory stabilization splint (Ficnar, n=63): mouth-opening gain appeared faster with the prefabricated device.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All trials at high risk of bias; heterogeneous pain reporting and statistics precluded meta-analysis; English-only; baseline pain not characterized; no long-term or large balanced-sex studies.
- Source inconsistency: the Results text gives the inclusion window end as 1 June 2021 while Methods/Abstract say 1 June 2022.
- No GRADE certainty rating reported.

## 7. Glossary

- Myofascial pain (MP): regional muscle pain with tender points, RDC/TMD group I.
- Occlusal splint (OS): removable occlusal-surface appliance (stabilization, reflex, repositioning).
- NTI-tss: nociceptive trigeminal inhibition tension suppression system, an anterior bite device.
- Low-level laser therapy (LLLT): photobiomodulation.
