## Why Ingested

측방접근(lateral window) 상악동 거상술 "술식" 자체 — window 골절제·membrane elevation·이식재 배치라는 수술 변수들이 임플란트 생존율에 미치는 영향 — 를 비교한 논문을 찾던 중 선정. 위키의 기존 lateral 카테고리(85편)는 막천공 위험요인·이식재 성분·해부학 위주였고, "술식 변수(block vs particulate 이식, membrane 피개 유무, 동시/지연 식립) 비교"를 정면으로 다루는 고전 SR이 없었다. [[sinus-lift/lateral/starch-jensen-2019-sinus-augmentation-barrier-membrane-window]]의 "membrane 피개 무의미" 결론과 본 논문의 "membrane 피개 시 생존율 ↑" 결론이 정면으로 배치되어 relations: contradicts 엣지 대상이 됨(§ Related Work).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-regression of 43 studies (34 lateral window, 5 osteotome, 2 localized sinus floor, 2 crestal core) on implant survival after maxillary sinus augmentation, published through April 2003.

Lateral window implant survival averaged 91.8% (range 61.7–100%); meta-regression found higher survival with rough-surfaced implants, particulate (vs block) grafts, and membrane coverage of the lateral window, with no difference by autogenous-bone content, timing (simultaneous vs delayed), or publication year.

Landmark early synthesis but abstract-only in this ingest (no PMC full text) and methodologically dated — pre-CBCT, pre-GRADE, mostly retrospective/case-series evidence (only 3 of 43 studies were RCTs); its membrane-benefit finding is directly contradicted by a later, RCT-only SR+MA.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 메타회귀 (43개 연구: lateral window 34편, osteotome 5편, 국소 상악동저 관리 2편, crestal core 2편) — 2003년 4월까지 발표된, 상악동 거상술 후 임플란트 생존율.

Lateral window 임플란트 생존율 평균 91.8%(범위 61.7–100%); 거친 표면 임플란트, 블록형보다 입자형 (Particulate) 이식재, 측창 (Lateral Window) 막 (Membrane) 피개 시 생존율이 유의하게 높았고, 자가골 함유량·동시/지연 식립 시기·발표 연도에 따른 차이는 없었음.

초기의 대표적 종합 연구이지만 이번 인제스트는 초록만 확보(PMC 전문 없음)했고 방법론적으로 오래됨 — CBCT·GRADE 이전 시대, 43편 중 RCT는 3편뿐(대부분 후향적·증례군); 특히 막 피개 효과 결론은 이후 RCT만으로 구성된 SR+MA에서 정면으로 반박됨.

- **PMID**: 14971260
- **Institution**: Department of Implant Dentistry, New York University, New York, USA

## 2. Key Contributions
- One of the earliest systematic reviews to meta-regress *surgical technique variables* (not just overall efficacy) against implant survival after maxillary sinus floor augmentation.
- Establishes lateral-window average implant survival (91.8%) as a widely-cited historical benchmark.
- Identifies four technique variables with a measurable survival association: implant surface roughness, graft particulate vs block form, lateral-window membrane coverage, and (null result) autogenous bone content.
- Explicitly separates lateral window (34 studies) from osteotome (5), localized sinus floor management (2), and crestal core (2) technique subgroups — an early acknowledgment that "sinus lift" is not one technique.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review with meta-regression (a form of meta-analysis)
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, Cochrane Oral Health Group Specialized Trials Register, Database of Abstracts and Reviews of Effectiveness; hand search of 3 major implant journals; contact with researchers/editors/industry for unpublished accepted data
- **n**: 43 studies (3 RCT, 5 CT, 12 case series, 23 retrospective analyses); technique breakdown 34 lateral window / 5 osteotome / 2 localized sinus floor / 2 crestal core
- **Inclusion**: ≥20 interventions, ≥1-year loaded follow-up, implant survival as outcome, English language
- **Exclusion**: studies with simultaneous non-sinus interventions (e.g., simultaneous ridge augmentation); studies with unrecoverable missing data
- **Outcomes**: primary — implant survival; covariates meta-regressed — block vs particulate graft, implant surface, graft material, membrane use over lateral window, simultaneous vs delayed placement, follow-up length, publication year, evidence level

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Lateral window technique implant survival: 61.7%–100% range, **91.8% average**
- Rough-surfaced implants > machine-surfaced implants (survival)
- Particulate graft > block graft (survival)
- Membrane over lateral window → higher survival than no membrane
- 100% autogenous bone vs composite graft: **no difference** in survival
- No significant difference: simultaneous vs delayed placement, follow-up duration, publication year, study evidence level
- Sinus-augmented implant survival compares favorably to non-grafted posterior maxilla historical rates

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Abstract-only in this ingest** — no PMC full text available (not open access); per-study data, full meta-regression statistics, and figures not verified beyond the structured abstract.
- Predominantly low-to-moderate evidence base: only 3/43 studies were RCTs; 23/43 were retrospective analyses.
- Search cutoff April 2003 — predates CBCT-based planning, current bone substitutes/PRF adjuncts, piezoelectric window osteotomy, and GRADE-based certainty rating; several specific findings (esp. membrane benefit) have since been re-tested with more rigorous, narrower designs and did not replicate (see Related Work).
- Insufficient data to evaluate smoking, residual bone height, screw vs press-fit design, or implant micromorphology beyond machined/rough; insufficient data to recommend PRP.
- Authors note this as a consensus-conference systematic review (Ann Periodontol 2003 sinus/ridge consensus issue), not an independent PRISMA-era SR.

## 6. Related Work
- [[sinus-lift/lateral/starch-jensen-2019-sinus-augmentation-barrier-membrane-window]]: **contradicts** this paper's membrane finding — a later, RCT-only (6 RCTs) SR+MA specifically isolating lateral-window membrane coverage found **no significant difference** in implant survival with vs without membrane, against Wallace's meta-regressed "membrane → higher survival."
- [[sinus-lift/lateral/derbishi-2026-maxillary-sinus-augmentation-implant-survival-sr-ma]]: much larger, more recent (2026) SR+MA (22 studies, 6,860 implants, GRADE-assessed) reporting a 97% pooled long-term survival — updates Wallace's 91.8% average with a tighter, higher estimate under modern technique/material conditions.
- [[sinus-lift/lateral/starch-jensen-2017-sinus-floor-augmentation-treatment-modalities]]: broader treatment-modality review touching similar technique-comparison ground.

## 7. Glossary
- **Lateral window technique**: Classic Tatum/Boyne-derived sinus augmentation approach — a bony window is opened in the lateral (buccal) maxillary sinus wall, the Schneiderian membrane is elevated, and graft material is placed beneath it before or with implant placement.
- **Osteotome technique**: Crestal (transalveolar) approach using osteotomes to in-fracture the sinus floor from the ridge crest, without a lateral window.
- **Meta-regression**: A meta-analytic technique that models the relationship between a study-level covariate (e.g., graft type, membrane use) and an effect estimate (here, implant survival) across included studies.
- **Particulate vs block graft**: Particulate = morselized/granular graft material packed into the sinus cavity; block graft = a solid, pre-shaped graft block.
- **Barrier membrane (lateral window)**: A resorbable/non-resorbable membrane placed over the osteotomy window after graft placement, distinct from membrane use for Schneiderian membrane perforation repair.
