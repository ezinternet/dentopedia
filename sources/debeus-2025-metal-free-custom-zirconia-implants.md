## Why Ingested

Reference-mined as one of the 28 included studies in [[wiki/immediate-implant/alqutaibi-2026-root-analog-dental-implants]], the umbrella SR on root-analog/custom-made implants. This paper is the SR's **longest prospective zirconia-RAI follow-up (5 years)** and the source of the **lowest-bound zirconia survival figure (75.8%)** the SR flags as a cautionary outlier against the higher pooled zirconia-implant survival estimates reported elsewhere in the literature (~95.6% at 1 year in other reviews). Ingesting the primary source lets the wiki show *why* the number is low (5/30 mechanical/horizontal fractures, palatal off-axis placement, lateral occlusal overload) rather than just citing a bare percentage.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-arm clinical trial, n=30 patients receiving custom-made two-piece ZrO2 (ZV3) implants replacing a single missing maxillary premolar, followed up to 5 years (median 63.0 months) at University Medical Center Groningen.

Implant survival at 5 years was 75.8% (95% CI 60.0-91.0%) and success 71.0% (95% CI 54.0-88.0%); 9/30 implants were lost — 4 early osseointegration failures and 5 horizontal mechanical fractures — with no significant survival difference by implant manipulation (81.3% vs 68.6%) or occlusal scheme (canine guidance 80.0% vs group function 73.8%).

Survival was lower than the literature-reported benchmark for zirconia implants (~95.6% at 1 year) and the authors explicitly concluded ZV3 implant placement as applied "cannot be recommended for clinical practice," attributing the high fracture rate to off-axis (palatal) placement and lateral occlusal overload rather than to zirconia as a material per se.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the few mid-to-long-term (5-year) prospective clinical trials on custom-made, two-piece, metal-free zirconia (ZrO2) dental implants (ZV3 system), a scarce category in the literature per the authors.
- Directly evaluates two hypothesized influencing factors on survival: (a) intraoral manipulation (buccal recontouring with a diamond burr) of the implant prior to impression-taking, and (b) occlusal scheme (canine guidance vs group function) — neither reached statistical significance, though the manipulated group trended toward *higher* survival (81.3% vs 68.6%), arguing against manipulation-induced fracture risk as commonly assumed in the ZrO2 literature.
- Reports an unusually high mechanical (horizontal) fracture rate (5/30, ~17%) concentrated after prosthetic loading, contrasting with a same-institution earlier cohort study (Becker/Brunello, ZV3 standard implants) that saw fiberglass-post fractures but zero implant fractures over up to 9 years — highlighting that implant *design*/*placement axis* (custom two-piece, palatally off-axis in some cases) rather than the ZrO2 material class may drive fracture risk.
- Patient-reported outcomes were strongly positive despite the poor survival/success rates: satisfaction significantly exceeded pre-treatment expectations (self-confidence, chewing ability, implant visibility, pain during function all improved beyond expectation).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective, single-arm (uncontrolled) clinical trial, up to 6 years planned follow-up.
- **Population**: 31 patients enrolled (consecutive, missing single maxillary premolar); 30 received the allocated custom-made ZrO2 (ZV3, Zircon Vision GmbH) implant (1 excluded — protocol violation, different implant type placed). Mean age 52.5y (range 23-75).
- **Inclusion**: age >=18, missing maxillary 1st/2nd premolar, bone height >=10 mm beneath maxillary sinus, informed consent.
- **Exclusion**: adjacent missing teeth, need for bone augmentation, active orthodontics, severe bruxism, acute periodontitis, prior implant loss, ASA >=3, prior head-neck radiation, chronic orofacial pain, reduced mental capacity.
- **Implant**: ZV3 individually designed two-piece ZrO2 implants milled from isostatically pressed, yttria-stabilized, cerium co-stabilized zirconia; CBCT + intraoral scan-based custom design; minimum 1 mm surrounding bone volume; air particle abrasion pre-sintering.
- **Surgical/restorative protocol**: placement >=3 months post-extraction, native bone (no augmentation) where palatal bone allowed primary stability, antibiotic prophylaxis + chlorhexidine rinse; 12-week healing; optional intraoral diamond-burr buccal recontouring; polyether impression; lithium disilicate crown (IPS e.max Press) on original ZV3 fiberglass post, adhesively cemented (RelyX Ultimate) ~15 weeks post-placement; light occlusal contacts with wide freedom in centric relation.
- **Follow-up time points**: T0 (pre-op), T1 (2 weeks post-crown, baseline), T12 (1 year), T60 (~5 years).
- **Outcomes measured**: implant survival (functional in situ), implant success (Albrektsson-type criteria: no mobility, no radiolucency, MBL loss <=0.2 mm/year after year 1, no pain/inflammation/nerve damage), MBL via calibrated intraoral radiographs (DicomWorks, two independent raters), clinical periodontal parameters (plaque, calculus, bleeding, probing depth), FDI World Dental Federation crown criteria, and a 16-item PROM questionnaire (VAS, 0-100%).
- **Stratification factors tested**: implant manipulation (yes/no) and occlusal scheme (canine guidance vs group function), determined retrospectively from cast models.
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier survival/success with 95% CI, log-rank tests for stratified comparisons, repeated-measures ANOVA + Bonferroni post hoc for MBL over time, Wilcoxon signed-rank for expectation-vs-satisfaction comparison. SPSS v28 / MedCalc v20.0.4.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Implant survival**: 83.3% (95% CI 70-97%) at 1 year; **75.8% (95% CI 60.0-91.0%) at 5 years (60 months)**; 59.6% (95% CI 49.4-69.9%) at study end (77 months). 9/30 implants lost total: 4 early (failed osseointegration), 5 mechanical (horizontal fracture). 3 additional subjects lost to follow-up (2 deaths, 1 unreachable).
- **Manipulation subgroup**: 81.3% (manipulated) vs 68.6% (not manipulated) survival at T60; log-rank chi2(1)=0.290, p=0.590 (NS).
- **Occlusal scheme subgroup**: 80.0% (canine guidance) vs 73.8% (group function) survival at T60; log-rank chi2(1)=0.106, p=0.745 (NS).
- **Implant success**: 71.0% (95% CI 54.0-88.0%) at 5 years; 10/30 not successful, 6 lost to follow-up for success determination.
- **MBL**: -0.26 mm (SD 0.45) baseline-to-1y; -0.25 mm (SD 0.54) baseline-to-5y; no significant change between time points (p=1.00), no annual bone loss >0.2 mm detected after year 1.
- **Crown performance (n=14 evaluated at T60)**: no failures, no interventions required; FDI aesthetic scores (surface lustre, colour match, translucency) deteriorated over time but stayed clinically acceptable; 9/30 crowns (30%) lost a strong approximal contact point over time — lower than comparator studies reporting 43% and 58% contact-point loss.
- **PROMs**: pre-op expected satisfaction median 93.9% (IQR 86.9-96.3%); 5-year actual satisfaction median 95.7% (IQR 91.5-97.6%); satisfaction significantly exceeded expectations for self-confidence (p=0.035), implant-loading confidence (p=0.004), chewing ability (p=0.005), implant visibility (p=0.006), pain during function (p=0.005).
- **Author's explicit clinical conclusion**: "ZV3 implant placement as applied in this study cannot be recommended for the clinical practice" — survival "lower than expected and clinically not acceptable."

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=30, further reduced by high no-show rate at 5-year recall — only 14/19 eligible patients physically re-examined), producing wide 95% CIs on survival/success and limiting power to detect subgroup differences (manipulation, occlusal scheme).
- Single-arm, uncontrolled design — no titanium comparator arm, so relative performance vs titanium in the same cohort/setting cannot be directly established; cross-study comparison is confounded by differing measurement protocols and populations.
- High mechanical (horizontal) fracture rate (5/30) is not explained by any single identified variable despite explicit discussion with authors of a related same-institution cohort (Becker/Brunello) that saw zero implant fractures; palatal/off-axis placement and lateral occlusal overload are proposed but not statistically confirmed mechanisms.
- Authors note several published risk-mitigation recommendations from the implant manufacturer's therapy guide (vitamin D monitoring, bone ozonizing, PRP, Periotest osseointegration testing) were *not* followed in this protocol, introducing an uncontrolled deviation from "ideal" protocol that limits generalizability.
- Explicitly call for a larger, prospective, randomized clinical trial before recommending custom-made ZrO2 implants more broadly, and for further research specifically into the mechanisms of mechanical (fracture) failure in ZrO2 implants.

## 6. Related Work

- Pieralli et al. (systematic review cited in-text) — reports 1-year ZrO2 implant survival ~95.6%, substantially higher than this study's 5-year figure of 75.8%, used as the primary literature benchmark this paper compares itself against unfavorably.
- Hashim et al. (systematic review cited in-text) — broader indications/types of ZrO2 implants, concludes no clear indications yet established and highlights knowledge gaps on long-term outcomes/failure modes, consistent with this paper's cautionary framing.
- Becker et al. / Brunello et al. (same UMCG cohort, ZV3 *standard* — not custom — implants, up to 9-year follow-up) — zero implant fractures but fiberglass-post complications, contrasted directly against this paper's high implant fracture rate to argue design/placement (not material) drives failure.
- Brüll et al. (retrospective evaluation, 121 ZV3 implants incl. 66 of the same two-piece design) — reports 96.5% survival at 3 years, comparable to broader literature and much higher than this paper's cohort.
- [[wiki/immediate-implant/alqutaibi-2026-root-analog-dental-implants]] — the umbrella SR that includes this trial as one of its 28 root-analog/custom-implant studies; this paper anchors the SR's cautionary lower-bound zirconia survival citation.

## 7. Glossary

- **ZrO2 / zirconia**: yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal (Y-TZP), a high-strength ceramic used as a metal-free alternative to titanium for dental implants.
- **ZV3 implant**: a custom-made, two-piece, metal-free zirconia implant system (Zircon Vision GmbH) individually milled per patient from CBCT + intraoral scan data.
- **MBL**: marginal bone level (change), radiographically measured distance from the implant shoulder to the first bone-to-implant contact, tracked longitudinally as a proxy for peri-implant bone health.
- **Kaplan-Meier survival/success analysis**: time-to-event statistical method used here to estimate implant survival/success probabilities with 95% confidence intervals across the follow-up period, accounting for variable follow-up duration and losses to follow-up.
- **FDI World Dental Federation criteria**: a validated clinical scoring system for restorations across aesthetic, functional, and biological domains, used here to grade the implant-supported crowns.
- **Canine guidance vs group function**: two occlusal schemes for lateral (dynamic) mandibular movement — canine guidance concentrates lateral contact on the canines; group function distributes it across multiple posterior teeth — tested here as a possible implant-survival risk factor via retrospective cast-model analysis.
