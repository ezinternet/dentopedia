## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/arbildo-vega-2025-short-vs-standard-implants-edentulous-umbrella-review]] (umbrella review, 60 SR)이 "short 임플란트 생존·MBL 동등 또는 우위" 명제를 최고 근거수준에서 지지하지만, 실제 6년 이상 장기 추적 원저자료는 상대적으로 드물다. 본 retrospective cohort (평균 추적 6.52년)는 그 명제를 장기 임상자료로 재확인하는 동시에, implant length 자체가 아니라 insertion depth가 MBL의 실제 결정 인자임을 제시해 [[implants/emfietzoglou-2024-short-implants-6mm-survival-posterior-edentulism-sr]]의 결론을 한정·정교화한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study of 84 implants (39 short 6 mm, 45 standard 8.5 mm) in 73 partially edentulous patients (premolar-molar region), mean follow-up 6.52 ± 2.81 years (range 5.3–12.5 years).

MBL did not differ significantly between standard (0.12 ± 0.07 mm) and short (0.05 ± 0.08 mm) implants (p > 0.05); CIR was significantly higher in short implants (1.96 ± 0.05 vs. 1.41 ± 0.05, p < 0.001) but was not associated with MBL, while insertion depth was positively correlated with MBL (r = 0.372, p < 0.001; linear mixed model β = 0.1929, p = 0.017).

Short (6 mm) implants achieved comparable long-term marginal bone stability to standard implants when appropriate surgical/prosthetic protocols were applied (100% survival both groups); implant length alone was not the primary determinant of MBL — insertion depth (likely reflecting subcrestal biological-width remodeling rather than pathological loss) was the key associated factor, but the retrospective, single-operator, unmatched-baseline design limits causal inference.

## 세줄요약

후향적 코호트 연구 (n=84 임플란트: short 6mm 39개, standard 8.5mm 45개; 환자 73명, 구치부 부분무치악), 평균 추적기간 6.52 ± 2.81년 (범위 5.3–12.5년).

변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 standard (0.12 ± 0.07mm)와 short (0.05 ± 0.08mm) 군 간 유의차 없음 (p > 0.05); 관관-임플란트 비율 (Crown-Implant Ratio, CIR)은 short군에서 유의하게 높았으나 (1.96 vs 1.41, p < 0.001) MBL과 무관, 삽입 깊이 (insertion depth)만 MBL과 유의한 상관 (r = 0.372, p < 0.001; 선형혼합모형 β = 0.1929, p = 0.017).

Short(6mm) 임플란트는 적절한 외과·보철 프로토콜 적용 시 standard 임플란트와 동등한 장기 변연골 안정성을 보임 (양 군 100% 생존율); 임플란트 길이 자체보다 삽입 깊이(생물학적 폭경 재형성 반영 가능)가 MBL의 핵심 관련 인자였으나, 후향적·단일 술자·비매칭 기저설계로 인과추론에는 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Long-term (mean 6.52y, up to 12.5y) direct clinical comparison of 6 mm short vs 8.5 mm standard bone-level implants in posterior partially edentulous sites, showing statistically comparable MBL and 100% survival in both groups.
- Identifies insertion depth — not implant length or CIR — as the parameter significantly associated with MBL (r = 0.372, p < 0.001; linear mixed model p = 0.017).
- Proposes that the insertion-depth–MBL association reflects physiological biological-width remodeling after subcrestal placement rather than pathological bone loss, since magnitude remained clinically minimal in both groups.
- Uses linear mixed-effects modeling with patient as random effect to account for within-patient clustering (multiple implants per patient).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective cohort study, STROBE-guided, single-center (Namsang Dental Clinic), single experienced prosthodontic specialist (>30 years' experience), IRB-approved (KC24RASI0215).
- **n**: 90 patients screened → 84 implants in 73 patients analyzed (39 short 6 mm, 45 standard 8.5 mm; all 5.0 mm diameter, Osstem TS III bone-level, internal hexagon).
- **Outcomes**: Marginal bone loss (MBL), crown–implant ratio (CIR), abutment height, insertion depth, emergence angle/profile, implant survival/success (Albrektsson criteria), biologic/mechanical complications.
- **Statistics**: t-tests, Mann–Whitney U, Shapiro–Wilk normality, chi-squared/Fisher's exact, Spearman correlation, linear mixed-effects models (patient as random effect), ICC for intra-examiner reliability (18 implants), α = 0.05. A priori power calculation: 36 implants/group for 90% power to detect 0.38 mm MBL difference.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- MBL: standard 0.12 ± 0.07 mm vs. short 0.05 ± 0.08 mm (p > 0.05, not significant).
- CIR: short 1.96 ± 0.05 vs. standard 1.41 ± 0.05 (p < 0.001); no significant CIR–MBL correlation (r = 0.079, p = 0.477).
- Insertion depth: short 0.95 ± 0.15 mm vs. standard 1.52 ± 0.14 mm (p = 0.019); positively correlated with MBL (r = 0.372, p < 0.001); linear mixed model β = 0.1929, SE = 0.0543, p = 0.017.
- Survival: 100% in both groups over follow-up; success 100% (short) and 97.78% (standard, 1 implant exceeded 0.2 mm/year Albrektsson MBL threshold).
- Mechanical complications in 10 implants (8 patients): decementation (5), loss of interproximal contact (2), prosthesis fracture (1), abutment/screw fracture (1), screw loosening (1); no significant difference between groups (p = 0.595). No major biologic complications (e.g., peri-implantitis requiring surgery) recorded.
- Intra-examiner ICC: excellent for CIR (0.980), good for emergence angle (0.807) and abutment height (0.874), moderate for baseline/final bone level and MBL (0.595–0.700).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design with complete-follow-up-only inclusion → possible selection bias, overestimation of survival/success.
- Implant length assigned by available vertical bone height (routine clinical decision) → baseline group imbalance (e.g., bone graft used only in standard group, 28.9%; abutment type distribution differed), limiting causal comparability.
- Small sample, especially short-implant group (39 implants), though above the a priori minimum.
- Radiographic MBL measurement has inherent error; magnitude of bone change was small, so statistical significance should not be over-interpreted as clinically meaningful.
- Single experienced operator (>30 years) for all surgery/prosthetics → strong internal validity but limited external generalizability.
- Associational, not causal, design; groups not formally matched for bone quality, ridge width, occlusal load, or reason for tooth loss.
- Authors call for prospective, randomized studies with standardized protocols and larger samples to clarify causal roles of insertion depth and prosthetic parameters.

## 7. Glossary
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: Radiographic loss of peri-implant crestal bone measured from a baseline (prosthesis-delivery) reference.
- **CIR (Crown-Implant Ratio)**: Ratio of crown height (highest point of crown to implant platform) to implant length (platform to apex).
- **Insertion depth**: Distance from the implant platform to the bone crest at placement (subcrestal positioning).
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: Resonance frequency analysis measure of implant stability.
- **SCRP (Screw-Cement Retained Prosthesis)**: Hybrid retention combining screw access with cement luting.
- **Emergence angle/profile**: Angle/contour of the transmucosal restoration relative to the implant long axis, classified as concave, straight, or convex.
