## Three-line Summary

5-year multicenter RCT (n=47 patients, 233 implants) comparing maxillary implant-supported fixed complete dentures (FCDs) supported by four (4-I) versus six (6-I) implants, evaluating marginal bone level (MBL) change, complications, cost, and satisfaction.

Implant survival was 100% (4-I) vs 99.3% (6-I, one early failure); no significant MBL difference between groups at 5 years; 4-I showed significantly higher technical complications (16.6% vs 0%) but lower initial and total treatment cost; no peri-implantitis in either group.

The 4-I protocol is non-inferior to 6-I in radiographic MBL after 5 years, with comparable survival and biological/prosthetic complications, higher technical complications, but lower cost — supporting 4-I as a viable maxillary full-arch option; full-text Methods/Results detail was not recoverable from the retrieved PDF (Wiley landing-page printout — abstract-only artifact).

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the few RCTs with 5-year follow-up directly comparing 4 vs 6 implants supporting a maxillary fixed complete denture (FCD), extending the same multicenter cohort previously reported at 3 years (Toia et al. 2021, companion paper).
- Provides prospective, randomized (not just retrospective/registry) evidence on the long-debated "how many implants for full-arch maxillary fixed rehabilitation" question.
- Reports MBL, biological/prosthetic/technical complications, treatment cost, and both clinician and patient satisfaction in the same trial — a multidimensional outcome set rarely combined in a single full-arch implant-number RCT.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Multicenter randomized controlled trial; individuals randomly assigned to 4-I or 6-I groups receiving maxillary implant-supported FCDs.
- **Follow-up**: 1, 3, and 5 years post-loading.
- **Assessments per visit**: FCDs unscrewed; clinical parameters recorded with a periodontal probe (plaque index, bleeding on probing, pocket depth, keratinized mucosa width); periapical radiographs obtained; maintenance care performed.
- **Outcomes evaluated**: Radiographic MBL changes (primary), incidence of technical/prosthetic/biological complications, treatment costs, clinician and patient satisfaction.
- **Sample**: 47 patients rehabilitated with a total of 233 implants across the two groups.
- Full randomization method, allocation concealment, blinding, and statistical model details were not present in the retrieved abstract-only PDF.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Survival**: 6-I group 99.3% (one early implant failure); 4-I group 100%.
- **MBL**: No significant differences at 5 years, either between groups (4-I vs 6-I) or within groups over time.
- **Soft tissue**: Both groups showed a decrease in keratinized mucosa width over follow-up.
- **Complications**: Both groups experienced prosthetic and biological complications; no peri-implantitis was observed in either group. The 4-I group had a significantly higher incidence of technical complications: 16.6% vs 0% (6-I).
- **Cost**: Cost analysis favored the 4-I group for both initial and total treatment costs.
- **Satisfaction**: Clinician and patient satisfaction differed by group — 4-I was preferred aesthetically, 6-I was preferred functionally (particularly for speaking ability at earlier follow-ups).
- **Conclusion (authors')**: FCDs supported by four implants are non-inferior to six implants in terms of 5-year radiographic MBL change; both groups showed comparable survival and biological/prosthetic complication rates; 4-I was associated with higher technical complications but lower overall treatment cost.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrieved artifact is abstract-only (Wiley landing-page PDF print); precise statistics (CIs, p-values for MBL/complication comparisons), detailed baseline demographics, and per-implant-position (anterior/posterior, axial/tilted) subgroup data were not extractable and should be pulled from full text if a deeper dive is needed.
- Relatively small sample (n=47 patients) for a 5-year multicenter trial — likely limits power for secondary/complication-rate comparisons despite adequate power for the primary MBL non-inferiority endpoint.
- Industry funding (Dentsply Sirona Implants) and a speaker-honoraria disclosure for the first author are potential sources of bias to weigh when interpreting favorable outcomes for either implant-number protocol.
- This is the 5-year update of the same multicenter cohort reported at 3 years by Toia et al. (2021); a full synthesis of 3-year vs 5-year trends within the same cohort awaits ingestion of the companion 2021 paper into the wiki.

## 7. Glossary

- **FCD**: Fixed Complete Denture — implant-supported, screw-retained full-arch fixed prosthesis.
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Level (change measured radiographically around implants over time).
- **4-I / 6-I**: Four-implant / six-implant supported prosthesis group.
- **Keratinized mucosa width**: Band of firmly attached keratinized peri-implant soft tissue, measured with a periodontal probe.
- **Non-inferiority trial**: RCT design testing whether a simpler/cheaper intervention (4-I) is not unacceptably worse than a reference standard (6-I) on a pre-specified margin.
