## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/endodontics/vpt/aapd-2024-vital-pulp-therapy-primary-teeth-guideline]]는 2024년 컨센서스 가이드라인 관점에서 MTA 1차 선택을 권고하지만, 그 권고의 근거가 된 개별 systematic review들을 AMSTAR2로 메타평가한 umbrella 수준 문헌은 없었다. 본 umbrella review(Lopes 2021)는 33편의 SR(IPT/DPC/pulpotomy/pulpectomy 전체)을 방법론적 질로 등급화하여, 가이드라인의 근거 기반(evidence base)이 실제로 얼마나 견고한지(대부분 moderate quality)를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review of 33 systematic reviews (from 272 screened records, AMSTAR2-rated) on vital pulp therapy (VPT: indirect pulp treatment, direct pulp capping, pulpotomy) and non-vital pulp therapy (NPT: pulpectomy) in primary teeth.

Methodological quality of included SRs was mostly moderate (17/33) to low (9/33), with 6 high and 3 critically low; MTA emerged as the best-supported pulpotomy medicament (superior to formocresol, ferric sulfate, calcium hydroxide in multiple moderate-to-high quality SRs), while direct pulp capping remained controversial due to complication rates.

Overall quality of evidence for both VPT and NPT was judged moderate — future high-standard SRs and well-designed RCTs with longer follow-up are needed to firm up clinical protocols and material choices.

## 세줄요약

원심(primary dentition) 치수생활치료(vital pulp therapy, VPT: 간접치수복조 IPT·직접치수복조 DPC·치수절단술 pulpotomy)와 치수실활치료(non-vital pulp therapy, NPT: 근관치료 pulpectomy)에 대한 33편 체계적 문헌고찰(SR)을 AMSTAR2로 평가한 우산리뷰(umbrella review).

포함된 SR의 방법론적 질은 대부분 중등도(17/33)~낮음(9/33)이었고(높음 6, 매우낮음 3), MTA(Mineral Trioxide Aggregate)가 치수절단술 재료 중 가장 근거가 탄탄한 선택(포름크레졸·황산제이철·수산화칼슘보다 우수)으로 확인됐으며, 직접치수복조(DPC)는 합병증 발생률 때문에 여전히 논란의 여지가 있었다.

VPT와 NPT 모두 전반적 근거의 질은 중등도로 평가됐으며, 임상 프로토콜과 재료 선택을 확립하려면 추적기간이 긴 고품질 SR과 잘 설계된 RCT가 더 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review (SR-of-SRs) systematically appraising both VPT (IPT, DPC, pulpotomy) and NPT (pulpectomy) evidence in primary teeth, using AMSTAR2 for methodological rating of the 33 included SRs.
- Consolidates fragmented material comparisons (MTA vs. formocresol vs. ferric sulfate vs. calcium hydroxide vs. Biodentine vs. laser vs. herbal medicaments) across 33 SRs into a single confidence-graded synthesis.
- Quantifies systemic weaknesses across the pediatric-pulp-therapy SR literature: 93.9% failed to report funding sources of included primary studies, 27.3% did not adequately discuss heterogeneity, 27.3% did not report conflicts of interest, 20.0% lacked a satisfactory search strategy explanation.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review (systematic review of systematic reviews), following PRISMA 2020 and established SR-of-SR guidance.
- **PECO**: Population = primary-dentition patients with deep caries; Exposure = clinical management; Comparison = VPT (IPT/DPC/pulpotomy) vs. NPT (pulpectomy); Outcome = diagnosis and treatment-type efficacy.
- **Search**: Seven databases (PubMed/Medline, Scopus, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, Scielo, Embase, LILACS, TRIP) plus grey literature, up to June 2021; no year or language restriction.
- **Screening**: Two independent reviewers (title/abstract + full-text), third reviewer for disagreements; inter-examiner kappa = 1.00 at full-text stage.
- **Risk of bias**: AMSTAR2 (16-item tool) — rated High / Moderate / Low / Critically low per the standard AMSTAR2 rubric.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 272 records screened → 60 full-texts assessed → 33 SRs included (13 excluded at full-text with stated reasons).
- AMSTAR2 quality distribution: 3 critically low, 9 low, 17 moderate, 6 high; none fully complied with the AMSTAR2 checklist.
- **IPT**: liner choice (CH vs. GIC/adhesive) made no difference to success (high/moderate-quality SRs); IPT outperformed pulpotomy on success rate (low confidence); Hall technique 78% success vs. pulpectomy 76% (moderate confidence).
- **DPC**: 88.8% overall success regardless of material (CH, bonding agents, MTA, FC) per a high-quality SR, corroborated by a moderate-quality SR and a high-quality Cochrane SR; DPC underperformed pulpotomy (moderate confidence).
- **Pulpotomy materials**: MTA was consistently the best-supported medicament — superior to FC and CH in several moderate/high-quality SRs and meta-analyses; MTA vs. Biodentine showed no significant difference (moderate/high-quality reviews); FC vs. FS showed no significant difference in one moderate-quality meta-analysis; herbal medicaments (allium sativum, Ankaferd Blood Stopper, elaeagnus angustifolia, propolis) showed comparable success but inadequate evidence base.
- **Laser pulpotomy**: mixed/inconsistent results across 7 SRs — ranging from no difference to inferior to superior vs. conventional pulpotomy, generally lower-quality evidence.
- **FC safety concern**: multiple (mostly moderate-quality) SRs flagged formocresol's formaldehyde content as a carcinogenicity/mutagenicity/cytotoxicity concern.
- **NPT/pulpectomy**: ZOE performed similarly to Vitapex/Sealapex (moderate confidence); Cochrane high-quality SR found no medicament/technique conclusively superior; Ca(OH)2/iodoform best for teeth nearing exfoliation (moderate risk of bias); LSTR vs. pulpectomy showed no significant difference (moderate/high-quality meta-analyses); rotary vs. manual instrumentation showed similar clinical/radiographic success with faster treatment time in most SRs, though one SR found insufficient evidence either way.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Umbrella-review design inherently does not re-examine individual primary studies within each included SR — conclusions rest on the interpretation given by each SR's authors, so any errors or biases in the underlying SRs propagate upward.
- Overall evidence quality across the field is only moderate; the authors explicitly call for more well-designed RCTs with longer follow-up and higher-quality SRs, especially to resolve open questions on laser pulpotomy and herbal medicaments.
- No SR fully complied with AMSTAR2, and funding/COI reporting was poor across the field (93.9% failed to report included-study funding sources) — a systemic transparency gap in pediatric pulp-therapy SR literature.

## 7. Glossary

- **VPT (Vital Pulp Therapy)**: 치수생활치료 — treatments preserving pulp vitality (IPT, DPC, pulpotomy).
- **NPT/NVT (Non-Vital Pulp Therapy)**: 치수실활치료 — treatment of an irreversibly inflamed/necrotic pulp (pulpectomy).
- **IPT (Indirect Pulp Treatment)**: 간접치수복조 — leaving deep affected dentin covered with a biocompatible liner to avoid pulp exposure.
- **DPC (Direct Pulp Capping)**: 직접치수복조 — covering an exposed pulp directly with a biocompatible material.
- **Pulpotomy**: 치수절단술 — removal of the coronal pulp with medicament application to the remaining radicular pulp.
- **Pulpectomy**: 발수(근관)치료 — complete removal and filling of the root canal system of a primary tooth.
- **MTA (Mineral Trioxide Aggregate)**: 미네랄삼산화물응집체 — calcium-silicate-based pulpotomy/capping medicament.
- **FC (Formocresol)**: 포름크레졸 — traditional pulpotomy medicament, flagged for formaldehyde-related toxicity concerns.
- **FS (Ferric Sulfate)**: 황산제이철 — hemostatic pulpotomy medicament alternative to formocresol.
- **CH (Calcium Hydroxide)**: 수산화칼슘 — capping/lining/pulpotomy medicament, generally lower success rate in this review.
- **AMSTAR2**: 체계적 문헌고찰 방법론적 질 평가도구(A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews, version 2) — 16-item tool rating SR confidence as High/Moderate/Low/Critically low.
- **LSTR (Lesion Sterilization and Tissue Repair)**: 병소살균조직재생술 — non-instrumentation endodontic treatment technique using antibiotic paste.
