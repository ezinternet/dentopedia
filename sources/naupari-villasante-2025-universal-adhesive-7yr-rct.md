## Three-line Summary

Double-blind split-mouth multicenter RCT (2 centers, 50 participants, 200 NCCLs, 4 bonding strategies: SEE, SET, ERDry, ERWet) evaluating Futurabond U universal adhesive over 7.5 years using FDI and USPHS criteria; 135 restorations analyzed at final recall (84% retention).

At 7.5 years, retention rates were 72.7% (SEE), 76.5% (SET and ERDry), and 79.4% (ERWet), with no statistically significant differences between strategies (p>0.05); 32 restorations lost retention total, 16 showed minor marginal staining, 28 had minimal marginal adaptation defects.

The clinical performance of a universal adhesive in NCCL restorations was satisfactory and equivalent across all four bonding techniques at 7.5 years, supporting technique simplification (SEE) without compromising longevity.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 분할구강 다기관 무작위대조시험(2기관, 50명, 비우식성 치경부 병소(NCCL) 200개, 4가지 술식: 자가산부식(SEE)·선택적 법랑질 산부식+자가산부식(SET)·산부식-세척 건조 상아질(ERDry)·산부식-세척 습윤 상아질(ERWet)) — 7.5년간 Futurabond U 범용 접착제(단일 도포, 이중중합) 평가; FDI·USPHS 기준, 135개 수복물 최종 분석(재방문율 84%).

7.5년 유지율: SEE 72.7%, SET·ERDry 76.5%, ERWet 79.4% — 술식 간 유의한 차이 없음(p>0.05); 총 32개 유지 실패, 16개 경미한 변연 착색, 28개 최소 변연 적합성 결함.

7.5년 시점에서 모든 접착 술식에서 임상적으로 동등하고 만족스러운 성능 → 가장 단순한 술식(SEE) 선택이 장기 성능을 희생하지 않음을 시사.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Dental Materials, 2025; 42(4):567–577
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.dental.2025.11.015
- **PMID**: 41318227
- **Design**: Double-blind split-mouth two-center RCT
- **Follow-up**: 7.5 years (evaluations at baseline, 6m, 1yr, 3yr, 5yr, 7.5yr)

## 2. Why Ingested
Longest follow-up (7.5yr) for a universal adhesive (Futurabond U) in NCCLs using four bonding strategies in a rigorous split-mouth multicenter double-blind design; provides key longevity data showing no significant retention difference between SEE, SET, ERDry, and ERWet techniques — directly informs clinical protocol simplification decisions.

## 3. Population & Methods
- **Participants**: 50 adults with ≥4 NCCLs; split-mouth design (each participant received all 4 strategies)
- **Centers**: 2 clinical centers
- **Total restorations**: 200 (n=50 per group); 135 analyzed at 7.5yr (84% recall rate)
- **Adhesive**: Futurabond U (Voco) — single-dose, dual-cure universal adhesive
- **Groups**:
  - SEE: self-etch only
  - SET: selective enamel etching + self-etch
  - ERDry: etch-and-rinse with dry dentin
  - ERWet: etch-and-rinse with wet dentin
- **Evaluation criteria**: FDI criteria and USPHS criteria

## 4. Results
| Strategy | Retention rate (7.5yr) | Restorations lost |
|---|---|---|
| SEE | 72.7% | 9 |
| SET | 76.5% | 8 |
| ERDry | 76.5% | 8 |
| ERWet | 79.4% | 7 |

- No statistically significant differences between groups (p>0.05)
- 16 restorations with minor marginal staining (no group difference)
- 28 restorations with minimal marginal adaptation defects (no group difference)
- 1 secondary caries (ERWet group)

## 5. Key Findings
- Universal adhesive Futurabond U performs equivalently across all four bonding techniques at 7.5 years in NCCL restorations
- SEE (simplest protocol) retention (72.7%) was not significantly different from ERWet (79.4%)
- Overall satisfactory clinical performance regardless of bonding technique
- Split-mouth double-blind design controls for inter-patient variability

## 6. Limitations
- Single adhesive brand (Futurabond U) — generalizability to other universal adhesives uncertain
- abstract-only source — detailed statistical analyses, hazard ratios, and subgroup data not available
- 84% recall rate at 7.5yr (135/200 restorations analyzed)
