## Why Ingested

Fills the RCT-only evidence gap on abutment shape vs MBL in the wiki — complements Atieh 2023 (observational studies, peri-implantitis focus) and Prpic 2026 (scoping review). This MA isolated abutment shape from emergence angle as predictors of early MBL and crestal bone remodeling (CBR) — the CBR distinction is novel. See [[wiki/implants/mbl/atieh-2023-emergence-angle-contour-marginal-bone-loss-sr-ma]] for the observational-study arm.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of RCTs (7 RCTs; 283 implants) comparing convex/wide vs concave/flat abutments on early peri-implant MBL and crestal bone remodeling (CBR).

Abutment shape alone (convex vs concave) did not significantly differ in early MBL (MD 0.083 mm; 95% CI −0.011 to 0.176; p = 0.082); however, emergence angle (EA) >30° subgroup showed a significant MBL increase (MD 0.190 mm; p < 0.001), and convex/wide abutments produced significantly more CBR than concave/flat (MD 0.560 mm; 95% CI 0.240–0.880; p = 0.001).

The critical threshold is not convexity per se but EA >30°; convex abutments drive crestal bone remodeling even without crossing that threshold, making abutment shape a secondary but independent factor in early peri-implant bone response.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA of RCT (7편; 임플란트 283개): 볼록(Convex)/넓은 vs 오목(Concave)/평평한 어버트먼트가 초기 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss)과 치조정골 재형성(CBR, Crestal Bone Remodeling)에 미치는 영향.

어버트먼트 형태 단독 MBL 차이 통계 미달 (MD 0.083mm; p=0.082); 출현각도(EA, Emergence Angle) >30° 서브그룹에서 MBL 유의하게 증가 (MD 0.190mm; p<0.001); 볼록/넓은 어버트먼트 → 치조정골 재형성 유의하게 더 많음 (MD 0.560mm; p=0.001).

임상적 핵심: EA >30°가 MBL의 주 임계치; 볼록 어버트먼트는 EA와 독립적으로 CBR을 촉진 → 두 인자 모두 관리 필요.

## Key Contributions

- RCT-only meta-analysis: highest level of evidence for this question
- Separates abutment SHAPE effect from EA threshold effect
- Novel: CBR (bone above implant platform) as distinct outcome from MBL
- EA >30° threshold confirmed as significant MBL predictor (p < 0.001)
- Convex/wide abutments drive more CBR even when overall MBL is similar
- Small sample (283 implants, 7 RCTs) but high internal validity

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA of RCTs; PRISMA 2020
- Search: Electronic databases; up to April 2024
- Inclusion: RCTs comparing convex/wide vs concave/flat bone-level implant abutments; MBL/CBR outcomes
- n: 7 RCTs; 283 implants
- Outcomes: MBL (bone level from implant platform to initial BIC), CBR (bone level above implant platform)
- Statistics: Random-effects model; α = 0.05

## Limitations

- Small total sample (7 RCTs, 283 implants)
- "Early" MBL only — no long-term follow-up data
- High heterogeneity anticipated given different implant systems and follow-up durations
- Blinding issues inherent in abutment shape comparison

## Glossary

- **Abutment shape**: The 3D contour of the prosthetic abutment at the implant-prosthesis junction — convex (bulging outward), concave (narrowing inward), or flat
- **Crestal bone remodeling (CBR)**: Bone level change above the implant platform (supra-platform) — distinct from MBL which measures below-platform change
- **Emergence angle (EA)**: Angle between the restoration profile and the implant axis at the junction; >30° is the established threshold
- **Random-effects model**: Statistical model that accounts for between-study variability; appropriate when heterogeneity expected
