## Why Ingested

The wiki holds VRF diagnostic-accuracy and review evidence ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]], [[wiki/cracked-tooth/habibzadeh-2023-diagnostic-efficacy-of-cone-beam]]) and one international cracked-tooth practice survey ([[wiki/cracked-tooth/gioti-2026-symptomatic-cracked-tooth-management-survey]]) but no VRF-specific data on what clinicians actually do; this Brazilian survey of 517 dentists supplies that practice-pattern layer and extends the survey evidence from European cracked teeth to VRF in a different health-care setting.

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional online questionnaire survey (7 questions, content-validated by 8 experts, Brazil): 517 dentists (72.3% endodontists, 17.41% general practitioners, 10.25% other specialists) reported how they diagnose and manage suspected vertical root fracture (VRF).

The most-cited clinical sign was a narrow, deep periodontal pocket (71.8%), followed by pain on vertical percussion (54.7%) and mobility (42.7%); a J-/halo-shaped radiolucency was the leading radiographic sign (59.3%); 85.7% (443/517) request CBCT, 23.6% combine four exams, and 91.9% review both CBCT images and report; after CBCT, extraction was the most cited management (59.6%; 308 respondents) versus surgical exposure (17.2-17.4%; about 90), with endodontists more likely than other groups to consider surgical exploration or endodontic (re)treatment before extraction.

Self-reported practice from a convenience online sample (no outcome data, no ground-truth diagnosis): it shows that VRF work-up varies with professional training and that extraction is the default, but it provides no evidence on diagnostic accuracy or treatment success.

## 세줄요약

횡단 온라인 설문 연구 (7문항, 전문가 8인 내용타당도 검토, 브라질): 치과의사 517명 (근관치료 전문의 72.3%, 일반의 17.41%, 기타 전문의 10.25%)이 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 의심 증례의 진단·관리 방식을 자기보고.

가장 많이 언급된 임상 징후는 좁고 깊은 치주낭 (71.8%), 이어 수직 타진통 (54.7%)과 동요도 (42.7%); 방사선 소견은 J형/후광형 (halo) 투과상이 최다 (59.3%); 85.7% (443/517)가 콘빔CT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT)를 의뢰, 23.6%는 4가지 검사 조합, 91.9%는 CBCT 영상과 판독문을 함께 확인; CBCT 후 관리는 발치가 최다 (59.6%, 308명), 외과적 노출 (Surgical Exposure) 17.2–17.4% (약 90명); 근관치료 전문의는 발치 전 외과적 탐색·근관치료/재치료를 더 고려.

편의표본 온라인 자기보고라 결과 (outcome)·실제 진단 확정 자료가 없음 — 수련 배경에 따라 VRF 진단 경로가 달라지고 발치가 기본값임을 보여줄 뿐, 진단 정확도나 치료 성공률에 대한 근거는 아님.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: J Clin Exp Dent 2025;17(8):e980-e988
- **DOI**: 10.4317/jced.63012
- **Institution**: Department of Restorative Dentistry, School of Dentistry, University of Sao Paulo, Brazil

## 2. Key Contributions
- First Brazilian-wide questionnaire dataset on diagnosis and management of suspected VRF across endodontists, general practitioners and other specialists.
- Shows the VRF work-up is multi-modal: the commonest individual pattern was three clinical signs (26.1%; narrow deep pocket + single fistula + pain on vertical percussion) and four auxiliary exams (23.6%; periapical radiograph + periodontal probing + fistulography + CBCT).
- Documents that professional qualification is associated with the number of signs and exams reported and with the chosen management.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Cross-sectional online survey (Google Forms), anonymous, distributed by e-mail and social media; ethics committee approved (n. 10503519.2.0000.0075).
- **Instrument**: 7 questions (qualification, clinical signs, radiographic signs, auxiliary exams, CBCT evaluation method, clinical management); content validity reviewed by 8 experts (4 endodontists, 2 general practitioners, 2 other specialists). No reliability testing reported.
- **n**: 517 respondents; excluded students and non-practising dentists.
- **Analysis**: descriptive; Fisher's exact test (qualification vs CBCT evaluation and management); Kruskal-Wallis with DSCF post hoc for counts of signs/exams (Shapiro-Wilk non-normal); Jamovi 2.5.4; p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Narrow, deep periodontal pocket | 371 (71.8%) |
| Pain on vertical percussion / mobility | 54.7% / 42.7% |
| Most common number of clinical signs per respondent | 3 (26.1%) |
| J-/halo-shaped radiolucency | 307 dentists (59.3% abstract; 59.4% discussion) |
| CBCT requested | 443 (85.7%) |
| Four auxiliary exams combined | 23.6% |
| Median number of exams: endodontists / GPs / other specialists | 4 / 3 / 2 (p<0.001) |
| CBCT evaluated with images + report / images only / report only | 475 (91.9%) / 39 (7.5%) / 3 (0.6%); no association with qualification (p=0.664) |
| Management after CBCT: extraction | 59.6% (308) |
| Management after CBCT: surgical exposure for direct visualization | 17.2% (about 90; 79 of them endodontists) |
| Qualification vs management | significant association (p<0.05) |
| Qualification vs number of radiographic signs | p=0.037; pairwise post hoc not significant |

Endodontists reported more signs than other specialists (p<0.05) and were more likely to consider surgical exploration or endodontic (re)treatment before extraction; general practitioners and other specialists more often went straight to extraction. 

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Self-reported convenience sample recruited online (selection and response bias acknowledged); 72.3% endodontists, so group sizes are imbalanced and may explain non-significant post hoc comparisons.
- No clinical outcomes, no confirmed-VRF cases, and no comparison of respondents' choices with a reference standard; seven fixed questions limit nuance (e.g., CBCT FOV/voxel size not captured).
- Brazil only; generalizability to other health-care systems is uncertain.
- Source reporting inconsistencies (see wiki deviation log): abstract writes p-values as "<0.05" including for the CBCT-evaluation comparison that the body gives as p=0.664 (not significant); 59.3% vs 59.4%; surgical exposure 17.2% (text) vs 90/517 = 17.4%.

## 7. Glossary
- **VRF**: vertical root fracture, a longitudinal root fracture with incomplete separation of the segments (AAE definition cited by the authors).
- **Halo / J-shaped radiolucency**: periapical plus lateral root-surface radiolucency characteristic of VRF.
- **Fistulography**: radiographic tracing of a sinus tract with a radiopaque material (gutta-percha cone) to locate its source.
- **Surgical exposure**: flap elevation to directly visualize the root surface for a fracture line.
- **DSCF**: Dwass-Steel-Critchlow-Fligner pairwise post hoc test after Kruskal-Wallis.
