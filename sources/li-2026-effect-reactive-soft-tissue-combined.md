## Why Ingested

The socket-seal question in the wiki has so far been answered in **esthetic-zone, mostly single-rooted** sockets: [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/lopez-pacheco-2021-socket-seal-surgery-esthetic-zone-sr]] synthesized the SSS technique literature, and [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/macbeth-2022-alveolar-ridge-preservation-guided-bone-regeneration-socket-seal]] supplied the direct GBR-vs-socket-seal RCT in the anterior maxilla. The incumbent in a **type IV (severe defect) molar socket** is a different beast: GBR with a collagen membrane plus a collagen sponge over a large multi-rooted defect.

This 2026 retrospective cohort is the held paper that tests a challenger to that incumbent in that specific socket class — **reactive soft tissue (RST)** used as the seal, combined with **injectable platelet-rich fibrin (iPRF)** and DBBM. It matters now because it is a *simplification* claim (membrane and sponge removed, lower cost) made against the full GBR stack, with CBCT outcomes at 6 months rather than histology or soft-tissue-only endpoints.

It lands alongside two companion RST papers: [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/shehabeldin-2023-intrasocket-reactive-tissue-state-current]] reframed intrasocket reactive tissue as a **preserve-or-remove decision** and called the preservation strategy a hypothesis needing testing, and [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/jiang-2024-efficacy-reactive-soft-tissue-alveolar]] supplied the first prospective cohort (RST vs collagen sponge, both over DBBM). This page is the arm with a GBR comparator. The iPRF component also extends the fibrin lineage already in the wiki from L-PRF membrane sealing ([[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/manlerd-2025-lprf-membrane-socket-sealing-healing]]) to an injectable formulation used as a graft adjunct, and it parallels the posterior "simplified GBR" idea in [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/wang-2024-simplified-gbr-biocollagen-prf-posterior-ridge]]. Molar-specific ARP justification comes from [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/vora-2025-arp-molar-sites-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (40 molar sites with type IV severe defects) comparing socket sealing with reactive soft tissue (RST) + injectable platelet-rich fibrin (iPRF) + DBBM against guided bone regeneration (GBR) with collagen membrane + collagen sponge, assessed on CBCT at T0 (pre-op), T1 (immediately post-op) and T2 (6 months) — abstract-only ingest.

Both arms gained horizontal width at 1, 3 and 5 mm below the crest with no between-group difference; RST was superior at the crest centre for vertical change from T2 to T1 (+0.34 mm gain vs −0.88 mm loss, p=0.023) and for graft material retention (92.84% vs 68.04%, p=0.003), while secondary grafting rate did not differ and short-term implant survival was 100% in both arms — abstract-only ingest.

RST + DBBM reduced ridge resorption in severe defect molar sites and showed a better tendency for vertical height preservation and graft retention than GBR, with the authors framing it as comparable bone preservation at lower surgical complexity and cost — abstract-only ingest.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **New indication for RST socket sealing**: tests reactive soft tissue as a seal in **type IV / severe defect molar sockets**, a socket class where the wiki's socket-seal evidence (esthetic zone, anterior maxilla) does not directly apply.
2. **Injectable PRF (iPRF) as an ARP adjunct**: pairs a liquid fibrin matrix with DBBM rather than using a solid L-PRF membrane, extending the fibrin-formulation line already held for socket sealing.
3. **Vertical compartment separated from horizontal**: reports horizontal width at three depths as non-inferior while locating the between-group advantage at the crest centre and in graft retention — a split that matters because most ARP trials pool dimensional change.
4. **Graft retention as a reported endpoint**: quantifies how much of the placed particulate graft remains at 6 months (92.84% vs 68.04%), a variable rarely reported in ARP trials.
5. **Procedure-simplification argument**: claims comparable bone preservation with a simpler, cheaper protocol than collagen membrane + collagen sponge GBR.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort study (single report; group allocation rationale not stated in the abstract).
- **Sample**: 40 sites, all **type IV molar sockets** (severe defect).
- **Test arm**: reactive soft tissue (RST) used for socket sealing + injectable platelet-rich fibrin (iPRF) + DBBM (deproteinized bovine bone mineral).
- **Control arm**: GBR with collagen membrane + collagen sponge.
- **Imaging**: CBCT at three timepoints — **T0** preoperatively, **T1** immediately postoperatively, **T2** at 6-month follow-up.
- **Primary/dimensional outcomes**: change in horizontal ridge width (measured 1, 3 and 5 mm below the crest), change in vertical ridge height (including the crest centre), graft material retention rate.
- **Secondary outcomes**: post-operative surgical complications, incidence of secondary bone grafting at implant placement, short-term implant survival rate.
- **Follow-up horizon**: 6 months to T2 CBCT (implant-related outcomes covered as "short-term" only).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | RST + iPRF + DBBM | GBR (collagen membrane + sponge) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizontal width increase at 1, 3, 5 mm below crest | Significant increase | Significant increase | No between-group difference (exact P not reported in abstract) |
| Vertical change at crest centre (T2 − T1) | **+0.34 mm gain** | **−0.88 mm loss** | **0.023** |
| Graft material retention rate | **92.84%** | **68.04%** | **0.003** |
| Secondary bone grafting rate at implant placement | — | — | No significant difference |
| Short-term implant survival rate | **100%** | **100%** | — |

- Both arms improved horizontal width significantly at all three depths; the RST advantage is therefore **vertical and graft-retention specific**, not horizontal.
- Complication rates are named as an outcome in the abstract but no counts or proportions are reported — not recoverable here.
- Authors' conclusion: RST + DBBM reduced ridge resorption in severe defect molar sites and showed a better tendency in preserving vertical ridge height and graft retention versus GBR; clinically framed as comparable bone preservation with a simpler, cheaper procedure.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Retrospective design**: allocation to RST vs GBR was not randomized; the abstract does not state the rationale for assigning a site to either arm, so selection bias (defect severity, site position, operator preference) cannot be assessed.
- **Small sample**: 40 sites total across two arms; the abstract does not give the per-arm n.
- **Single centre, single report**: no external validity check; no mention of blinding or calibration for CBCT measurement.
- **6-month horizon**: T2 is the only follow-up timepoint reported. Longer-term ridge stability, graft resorption kinetics beyond 6 months, and prosthodontic outcomes are unaddressed; "short-term implant survival" is the only implant endpoint, and 100% in both arms at this n is uninformative about differences.
- **Abstract-only ingest**: complication incidence, secondary grafting percentages, per-arm sample sizes, defect subclassification and statistical methods are not in the record, so the numbers above cannot be stress-tested (no confidence intervals, no adjustment analysis).
- **Future work implied**: a randomized comparison in type IV molar sockets with reported per-arm n, complication counts, and follow-up beyond 6 months — plus histology or histomorphometry to separate "graft retention" from "vital bone formation".

## 6. Related Work

- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/macbeth-2022-alveolar-ridge-preservation-guided-bone-regeneration-socket-seal]] — the direct GBR vs socket-seal RCT, but in the **anterior maxilla**: GBR was superior for radiographic preservation and dehiscence reduction, while socket seal was comparable for vertical height with higher complication rates (collagen matrix breakdown). This paper's molar, severe-defect result runs in the opposite direction on vertical height, which is the interesting tension.
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/shehabeldin-2023-intrasocket-reactive-tissue-state-current]] — narrative review of intrasocket reactive tissue; its central claim is that preserving the tissue as a seal is a *hypothesis* awaiting clinical testing. This cohort is that test on the bone axis, with a GBR comparator.
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/jiang-2024-efficacy-reactive-soft-tissue-alveolar]] — prospective cohort (40 completions) of RST vs collagen sponge over DBBM in sockets with bone deficiency: dimensional outcomes comparable, only early soft-tissue healing differed. Same test arm concept, different control and a cleaner design; together the two papers bracket the comparator question (sponge vs full GBR).
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/lopez-pacheco-2021-socket-seal-surgery-esthetic-zone-sr]] — SR+MA+TSA of SSS techniques in the esthetic zone; found no technique proven superior and evidence insufficient. Supplies the baseline claim this molar cohort qualifies by socket class.
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/manlerd-2025-lprf-membrane-socket-sealing-healing]] — L-PRF membrane vs collagen sponge vs spontaneous healing over 21 days: L-PRF helped early soft-tissue healing and pain but not wound closure. Contrasts with the injectable formulation used here.

## 7. Glossary

- **Reactive soft tissue (RST)** — soft tissue used as a biological seal over the socket orifice in place of a collagen membrane; the abstract does not describe the tissue's origin, the harvesting technique, or how the seal is secured.
- **Injectable platelet-rich fibrin (iPRF)** — liquid/serum-phase PRF preparation that can be injected into a particulate graft bed, as opposed to a pressed L-PRF membrane used as a cover.
- **DBBM (deproteinized bovine bone mineral)** — the xenograft particulate carrier used in the test arm.
- **Type IV socket** — severe-defect extraction socket class; here the molar sites in which both arms were treated.
- **Guided bone regeneration (GBR)** — control protocol: particulate graft covered by a collagen membrane plus a collagen sponge.
- **Alveolar ridge preservation (ARP)** — post-extraction grafting intended to limit dimensional change before implant placement.
- **Graft material retention rate** — percentage of the placed graft volume still detectable at a follow-up CBCT.
- **T0 / T1 / T2** — pre-operative / immediately post-operative / 6-month CBCT timepoints; the vertical result is reported as **T2 − T1**, i.e. change *after* surgery rather than total change from the pre-operative ridge.
