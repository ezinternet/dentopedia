## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/implants/almeida-2017-dental-implants-sjogren-syndrome-sr]]는 6편 관찰연구(대부분 후향적, RCT 없음, 평균 추적 3.97년)를 pooling한 SR이라 근거가 잠정적이었다. 본 논문은 pSS 환자군과 나이·성별·부위-matched 대조군을 둔 **5년 전향적 controlled study**로, 기존 SR의 결론(생존율 높음, MBL 낮음, QoL 개선)을 더 강한 연구설계로 재확인하면서 DMFT·타액분비량·OHIP 등 세부 수치를 추가한다. 타액분비저하(hyposalivation) 환자의 치과 치료(임플란트) 관련 논문 조사 중 PMC 풀텍스트로 확보.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective 5-year controlled clinical study (23 primary Sjögren's syndrome (pSS) patients vs 24 matched controls, all women, mean age 57.1y) evaluating single implant-supported crown outcomes.

All implants survived in both groups; marginal bone loss, probing depth, prosthetic complications, and clinician-rated aesthetics did not differ between pSS and control, despite pSS having significantly higher DMFT (p=0.008), lower saliva flow (p<0.001), and worse OHIP scores (p<0.001) throughout.

Dental implants are a safe, reliable rehabilitation option in pSS despite severe hyposalivation; quality-of-life burden in pSS is likely driven by hyposalivation symptoms themselves rather than implant-treatment outcomes — small sample size (n=47) limits generalizability.

## 세줄요약

전향적 5년 대조군 임상연구 (원발성 쇼그렌증후군(primary Sjögren's syndrome, pSS) 환자 23명 vs matched 대조군 24명, 전원 여성, 평균 57.1세) — 단일 임플란트 보철 예후 평가.

양 군 모두 임플란트 생존율 100%; 변연골소실(marginal bone loss, MBL)·탐침깊이·보철 합병증·심미 평가는 군 간 차이 없었으나, pSS군은 DMFT 유의하게 높고(p=0.008) 타액분비량 유의하게 낮으며(p<0.001) OHIP 삶의 질 점수도 전 기간 유의하게 나빴음(p<0.001).

중증 타액분비저하에도 불구하고 임플란트는 pSS 환자에서 안전하고 신뢰할 수 있는 재활 옵션; pSS 환자의 삶의 질 부담은 임플란트 치료 결과보다 타액분비저하 증상 자체에서 기인하는 것으로 보임 — 표본크기(n=47)가 작아 일반화에 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First mid/long-term (5-year) prospective study of single-tooth implant outcomes in primary Sjögren's syndrome (pSS) with a matched, non-SS control group — prior evidence was mostly retrospective SRs or short-term (≤18 month) prospective cohorts pooling pSS and secondary SS (sSS).
- Restricting to pSS only (excluding sSS, often confounded by immunosuppressant/biologic use for the concomitant connective-tissue disease) isolates the "pure" hyposalivation effect on implant prognosis.
- Reports granular longitudinal data: DMFT, VAS dry-mouth/eye scores, unstimulated and chewing-stimulated whole saliva flow rates, marginal bone loss, probing pocket depth, Copenhagen Index Score aesthetic outcomes, and OHIP-49 quality-of-life scores at baseline, 1, 3, and 5 years.
- Demonstrates a dissociation between clinician-assessed biological/technical/aesthetic implant outcomes (no difference vs. control) and patient-reported quality of life (significantly worse in pSS throughout) — implying QoL burden in pSS stems from hyposalivation itself, not implant performance.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective clinical study with 5-year follow-up; matched (age, gender, edentulism region) non-SS control group. Ethics approval H-3-2014-032 (Capital Region of Denmark); STROBE-consistent reporting.
- **Setting**: Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery + Department of Odontology, Copenhagen University Hospital / University of Copenhagen; recruitment June 2016-March 2020, followed to October 2023.
- **Population**: 293 pSS patients screened → 50 eligible for initial exam → final cohort 23 pSS + 24 matched controls (1 pSS patient excluded for requiring complex treatment). pSS diagnosis per 2002 AECG criteria (retrospectively also met 2016 ACR-EULAR criteria).
- **Intervention**: bone-level implants (Straumann Bone Level Roxolid) placed by 2 maxillofacial surgeons; healing abutment, 3-month healing; screw-retained (n=42) or cemented (n=5) single crowns on customized titanium abutments (Straumann Variobase) by 2 prosthodontists.
- **Follow-up timepoints**: T0 (baseline, 2 months post-restoration), T1 (1 year), T3 (3 years), T5 (5 years).
- **Outcome measures**: whole saliva flow rates (unstimulated/chewing-stimulated, draining method); implant survival/success (Albrektsson criteria: MBL <1.5 mm year 1, <0.2 mm/year thereafter); marginal bone loss (periapical radiographs); modified plaque index, sulcus bleeding index, probing pocket depth; peri-implantitis/mucositis (Berglundh criteria); technical complications; Copenhagen Index Score (clinician-rated aesthetics, 1=best–4=poorest); OHIP-49 (patient-reported oral health-related quality of life, Danish version).
- **Statistics**: nonparametric Brunner-Langer model for longitudinal main effects/interaction; Mann-Whitney with Bonferroni correction for pairwise comparisons; α=0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Cohort**: 47 participants, all women, mean age 57.1±12.6y (range 23-74). Retention: T1 22/22, T3 18 pSS/22 control (1 pSS patient died), T5 21 pSS/19 control.
- **Comorbidity/medication burden**: significantly higher in pSS (comorbidities 57% vs 16%, p=0.004; medication use 78% vs 33%, p=0.002; allergies 57% vs 25%, p=0.028).
- **DMFT**: significantly higher in pSS throughout follow-up (p=0.008), stable over time (p=0.100).
- **Saliva flow**: unstimulated 0.04-0.05 mL/min (pSS, hyposalivation range) vs 0.22-0.40 mL/min (control, normal range); chewing-stimulated 0.49-0.71 mL/min (pSS) vs 1.50-2.02 mL/min (control) — pSS significantly lower at every timepoint (p<0.001).
- **Implant survival/success**: 100% in both groups at 5 years (Albrektsson MBL criteria). Mean MBL (T0→T5): -0.04±0.36 mm (pSS) vs -0.23±0.60 mm (control), p=0.685 between groups.
- **PPD**: no significant between-group difference at any timepoint (T5: 2.93±0.62 mm pSS vs 2.62±0.49 mm control, p=0.446).
- **Gingival index**: significantly higher (worse) in pSS at implant sites (p=0.003) and overall (p<0.001), despite comparable plaque scores (p=0.301-0.362).
- **Biological complications**: only 1 case — a control-group implant developed peri-implantitis at T1 (attributed to herpes zoster infection), successfully treated, resolved by T3/T5. No complications in the pSS group.
- **Technical complications**: no significant between-group difference (p=0.241) — 4 abutment loosenings (2 each group) and 2 crown loosenings (control only) at T3; 3 ceramic fractures (1 pSS, 2 control) at T5.
- **Aesthetics (CIS)**: harmony/symmetry scores worse in pSS (p<0.001, attributed to higher pre-existing DMFT/extractions, not implant quality); crown morphology, color match, mucosal discoloration comparable (p=0.053-0.976).
- **OHIP-49**: significantly worse overall score in pSS throughout (p<0.001) but improved over time in both groups (p=0.026); overall OHIP correlated with dry-mouth VAS (r=0.63, p<0.001).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample size (n=47 total, 23 pSS) — recruitment was harder than anticipated since few pSS patients needed single-tooth replacement.
- Single-tooth implants only; variation in implant regions (anterior/premolar/molar, maxilla/mandible) not stratified in analysis.
- All participants women — reflects pSS's strong female predominance (~90%) but limits generalizability to male patients.
- No sSS (secondary Sjögren's) patients included — deliberately excludes potential immunosuppressant/biologic confounding, but findings may not extrapolate to sSS.
- Authors note interpretation should be cautious given the limited number of participants.

## 7. Glossary

- **pSS (primary Sjögren's syndrome)**: autoimmune exocrinopathy occurring alone (vs. secondary SS, which occurs with another autoimmune disease e.g. rheumatoid arthritis); ~90% female, mean onset ~55y.
- **DMFT**: Decayed, Missing, Filled Teeth index (max 28 in this study, excluding third molars).
- **UWS/SWS**: Unstimulated / chewing-Stimulated Whole saliva flow rate; hyposalivation = UWS ≤0.1 mL/min or SWS ≤0.7 mL/min.
- **CIS (Copenhagen Index Score)**: clinician-rated implant-crown aesthetic outcome scale (1=best to 4=poorest), covering harmony/symmetry, crown morphology, color match, mucosal discoloration, papilla index.
- **OHIP-49**: Oral Health Impact Profile, 49-item patient-reported oral health-related quality-of-life questionnaire.
- **Albrektsson success criteria**: implant success defined as MBL <1.5 mm in year 1 and <0.2 mm/year thereafter.
