## Why Ingested
QoL (OHIP-14) 엔드포인트 + 투여 경로(경구 vs 점막하 주사) 비교 RCT. [[drug/larsen-2021-methylprednisolone-doses-split-mouth-rct]]의 OHIP-14 결과(null)와 대조적으로 프레드니솔론의 QoL 개선 효과를 보고해 약물·경로 차이 논의에 직접 데이터를 제공.

## Three-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (n=186, 62 per group) from a Nigerian tertiary hospital compared oral prednisolone 40 mg, submucosal injection prednisolone 40 mg, and no prednisolone in patients undergoing third molar surgery, using OHIP-14 as the primary outcome assessed at days 1, 3, and 7.
Both prednisolone groups showed significantly better oral health-related quality of life than controls (p<0.05); submucosal injection produced significantly less QoL deterioration than oral administration (p=0.001).
This is the largest RCT establishing OHIP-14 as primary endpoint for M3 corticosteroid research, and the positive QoL signal for prednisolone directly contrasts with Larsen 2021's null result for methylprednisolone, suggesting drug- or route-level differences warrant further investigation.

## 세줄요약
줄1: 나이지리아 3차 병원의 3군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=186, 군당 62명)에서 경구 프레드니솔론 (Prednisolone) 40 mg, 점막하 주사 (Submucosal Injection) 40 mg, 무투여 대조군을 비교하여 구강건강관련 삶의 질 (Oral Health-Related Quality of Life, OHRQoL, OHIP-14)을 주요 결과 지표로 평가했다.
줄2: 프레드니솔론 투여군(경구·점막하 모두)은 미투여 대조군 대비 삶의 질이 유의하게 좋았으며(p<0.05), 점막하 주사군이 경구 투여군보다 삶의 질 저하가 유의하게 적었다(p=0.001).
줄3: OHIP-14를 주요 평가 지표로 삼은 최대 규모 RCT로, 프레드니솔론의 긍정적 삶의 질 신호는 라르센(Larsen) 2021의 메틸프레드니솔론 (Methylprednisolone) null 결과와 대비되어 약물·경로별 차이에 대한 추가 연구가 필요함을 시사한다.

## One-line Summary
RCT (n=186, 3 groups) found prednisolone 40 mg (oral or submucosal) significantly improved OHRQoL after third molar surgery vs no prednisolone, with submucosal injection superior to oral route (OHIP-14, p=0.001).

## 한줄요약
3군 RCT (n=186)에서 프레드니솔론 40 mg 투여(경구 또는 점막하 주사)는 사랑니 수술 후 구강건강관련 삶의 질(OHRQoL, OHIP-14)을 비투여군 대비 유의하게 개선했으며, 점막하 주사군이 경구 투여군보다 우월했다(p=0.001).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Largest single prednisolone RCT for M3 QoL with equal-size groups (n=62 per group)
- Demonstrates submucosal injection route superiority over oral route for QoL outcomes
- OHIP-14 as primary endpoint — one of few studies to use validated PRO as primary outcome
- Provides positive QoL signal that contrasts with Larsen 2021 null result (different drug: prednisolone vs methylprednisolone; different route emphasis)
- Submucosal prednisolone described as "simple, safe, painless, cost-effective"

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Three-arm RCT
- n=186 (62 per group):
  - Group A: 40 mg oral prednisolone
  - Group B: 40 mg submucosal injection prednisolone
  - Group C: No prednisolone (control)
- Assessment: Preoperative + postoperative days 1, 3, 7
- Primary outcome: OHIP-14 total score + subscale scores
- Classification: Affected (OHIP ≤28) vs Not affected (OHIP >28)
- Statistics: Comparison of mean OHIP scores between periods and between groups

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- All groups: Significant increase in OHIP scores postoperatively (worse QoL) — most marked at day 1
- Groups A+B vs C: Prednisolone significantly associated with better QoL and earlier recovery (p<0.05)
- Group B vs A: Submucosal injection significantly less QoL deterioration than oral prednisolone (p=0.001)
- Gradual improvement throughout postoperative period in all groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only access — exact statistical method, randomization process, and blinding details not fully available
- Nigerian tertiary hospital — generalizability to other settings unclear
- No pain/trismus/edema data reported separately — QoL-only endpoint
- Comparison drug is prednisolone (not methylprednisolone or dexamethasone) — limits direct comparison with most literature
- No long-term follow-up beyond day 7

## 6. Related Work
- Larsen 2021 (methylprednisolone split-mouth RCT — OHIP-14 null result)
- Libório 2025 (methylprednisolone SR+MA — QoL as secondary endpoint)
- Markiewicz 2008 (corticosteroids SR+MA)

## 7. Glossary
- **OHIP-14**: Oral Health Impact Profile (14 items) — validated QoL instrument covering pain, functional limitation, disability, handicap
- **Submucosal injection**: Injection into the oral submucosal tissue — local depot with rapid local bioavailability
- **OHRQoL**: Oral Health-Related Quality of Life
