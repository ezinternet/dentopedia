## Why Ingested

위축 상악 구치부에서 "short implant(graftless) vs long implant + sinus graft" 의사결정 근거를 보강하기 위해 인제스트. 기존 [[sinus-lift/lateral/toledano-2022-short-versus-standard-implants-sinus]] 및 [[implants/zhang-2024-short-vs-long-implants-sinus]] 와 짝을 이뤄, short implant가 생존율·변연골흡수에서 비열등함을 더 큰 RCT 풀(22 RCT)로 재확인.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 22 RCTs (667 patients, 1595 implants: 767 short/graftless vs 828 long+sinus graft) comparing short implants placed without sinus augmentation to conventional long implants with sinus grafting in the posterior atrophic maxilla.

No significant difference in implant survival at patient level (RR 1.01, 95% CI 0.52–2.0, p=0.87, I²=0%) or implant level (RR 1.09, 95% CI 0.6–2.0, p=0.7, I²=0%); marginal bone resorption slightly lower with short implants (MD 0.16 mm, p=0.00, I²=74.83%); biological complications marginally higher with long implants, prosthetic complications marginally higher with short implants (both non-significant).

Short implants are a valid graftless alternative to sinus augmentation in the posterior atrophic maxilla; however, high heterogeneity in marginal bone resorption results (I²≈75%) and variable follow-up durations across included RCTs limit direct cross-study comparisons.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA(RCT 22편, 환자 667명, 임플란트 1595개: short 767개/long+이식 828개): 위축 상악 구치부에서 short 임플란트(무이식)와 long 임플란트+상악동이식 간 비교.

환자 수준 생존율 유의차 없음(RR 1.01, 95% CI 0.52–2.0, p=0.87, I²=0%); 임플란트 수준 생존율도 유의차 없음(RR 1.09, p=0.7); 변연골흡수는 short 군에서 약간 적음(MD 0.16 mm, p=0.00, I²=74.83%); 생물학적·보철 합병증 방향 반대 경향이나 모두 비유의.

Short 임플란트는 상악동 이식 없이 위축 상악 구치부에 사용 가능한 유효 대안; 단, 변연골흡수 이질성 높음(I²≈75%) 및 포함 RCT 간 추적 기간 불균일로 직접 비교 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Large RCT pool (22 trials) for the graftless-short vs grafted-long question.
- Patient-level and implant-level survival both reported.
- Quantifies complication trade-off direction (biological vs prosthetic).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- SR+MA restricted to RCTs; 667 patients, 1595 implants (short:767, long:835).
- Outcomes: implant survival rate (patient + implant level), marginal bone resorption, biological and prosthetic complications.
- Random-effects meta-analysis; heterogeneity reported (I²).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Survival, patient level: RR 1.01 (95% CI 0.52–2.0), P=0.87, I²=0%.
- Survival, implant level: RR 1.09 (95% CI 0.6–2.0), P=0.7, I²=0%.
- Marginal bone resorption: MD 0.16 (95% CI −0.23 to −0.08), P=0.00, I²=74.83%.
- Biological complications: marginally higher for long implants (RR 0.48, 95% CI 0.23–0.8, P=0.13).
- Prosthetic complications: marginally higher for short implants (RR 1.56, 95% CI 0.85–3.15, P=0.43).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- High heterogeneity for marginal bone resorption (I²≈75%).
- Complication comparisons not statistically significant (wide CIs).
- Follow-up durations vary across included RCTs.

## 7. Glossary
