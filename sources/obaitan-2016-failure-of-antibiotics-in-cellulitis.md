## Why Ingested

봉와직염 진단이 유사질환과 혼동되기 쉽다는 점을 정량으로 보여 준다. [[oral-surgery/rrapi-2021-cellulitis-a-review-of-pathogenesis]]가 금표준 검사가 없다고 서술한 것의 실측 결과로, 항생제 임상시험의 치료 실패율이 6–37%로 흩어진다 (병리 기전 서베일런스, 2026-10-13, 사용자 요청). 일반 봉와직염 RCT 대상이며 치성 감염은 포함되지 않는다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (Am J Emerg Med, 2016) of antibiotic treatment failure in randomized trials of cellulitis: 19 articles, 20 studies, 3,935 patients, trials from developed countries only.

Treatment failure ranged 6–37% in the 9 trials reporting intent-to-treat results, pooled 18% (95% CI 15–21%); among clinically evaluable participants 3–42%, pooled 12% (95% CI 10–14%).

The authors attribute the wide variation to confusion of cellulitis with mimics and to doubtful construct validity of the diagnosis, and call for objective diagnostic tools; abstract only, so trial-level data are not available.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: American Journal of Emergency Medicine 34(8):1645–1652 (published 2016-05-26)
- Type: systematic review and meta-analysis; full text not retrieved (abstract only)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies trial-to-trial variability in antibiotic failure in cellulitis trials.
- Links the variability to misdiagnosis of mimics rather than to drug differences.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PubMed search to August 2015, English only; studies conducted wholly outside developed countries excluded because cellulitis pathophysiology is likely different there. Two reviewers screened and extracted; Freeman-Tukey transformation, random-effects pooling.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 19 articles / 20 studies / 3,935 patients (5% of identified studies met criteria).
- ITT (9 trials): 6–37%, pooled 18% (95% CI 15–21%). Clinically evaluable (15 articles): 3–42%, pooled 12% (95% CI 10–14%).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract only. The explanation (mimics, construct validity) is the authors' interpretation, not a tested result.
- General cellulitis trials; no odontogenic or facial infection.

## 7. Glossary

- ITT: intention-to-treat
- Construct validity: whether a diagnostic label measures one coherent disease
- Mimic (pseudocellulitis): non-infectious condition that looks like cellulitis
