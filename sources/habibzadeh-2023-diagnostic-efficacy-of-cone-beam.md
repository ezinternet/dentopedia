## Why Ingested

The wiki's VRF pages ([[cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]], [[cracked-tooth/liao-2021-vertical-root-fracture-non-endodontically-treated]]) state that CBCT is limited for fracture-line detection in root-filled teeth, but only as narrative-review assertions; this SR tabulates the underlying per-study CBCT sensitivity/specificity/accuracy (20 studies) with and without intracanal posts, giving those claims numbers and a study-design breakdown. It also sits beside [[cracked-tooth/alaugaily-2022-cbct-patterns-bone-loss-clinical]] (CBCT detects the bone-loss pattern rather than the line) and [[cracked-tooth/hernando-2026-diagnostic-methods-dental-cracks-systematic-review]] (all-modality crack diagnosis).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (5 databases 2000–2022; 1493 records identified, 20 studies included — 17 no-post experiments and 8 post experiments, mostly in-vitro, only 2 in-vivo and 1 clinical study) of CBCT diagnostic performance for vertical root fracture (VRF) in endodontically treated teeth.

Unweighted mean ± SD across studies without a post: sensitivity 71.50 ± 22.19% (range 32–100), specificity 75.64 ± 19.41% (36.7–100), accuracy 78.47 ± 17.19%; with a post: sensitivity 72.76 ± 18.73%, specificity 75.44 ± 18.26%, accuracy 74.02 ± 10.64%. Sensitivity was 58.5% in-vivo and 66.4% ex-vivo (specificity 46.8% ex-vivo) versus 75.1% in-vitro, and 100% sensitivity occurred only in-vitro.

The authors could not confirm optimal CBCT efficacy because of low sensitivity, strong heterogeneity (no pooled meta-analysis, no formal risk-of-bias tool reported) and scarce clinical data, and advise combining radiographic and clinical examination.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review) — 5개 데이터베이스(2000–2022)에서 1493편을 확인해 20편 포함(무포스트 17개·포스트 8개 실험; 대부분 체외 연구, 생체 내 2편·임상 1편), 근관치료치 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)에 대한 콘빔 CT (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT) 진단 성능을 평가.

포스트 없는 경우 연구 간 단순 평균 ± SD: 민감도 71.50 ± 22.19% (32–100), 특이도 75.64 ± 19.41% (36.7–100), 정확도 78.47 ± 17.19%; 포스트 있는 경우 민감도 72.76 ± 18.73%, 특이도 75.44 ± 18.26%, 정확도 74.02 ± 10.64%. 민감도는 생체 내 (in-vivo) 58.5%·발거치 (ex-vivo) 66.4% (특이도 46.8%)로 체외 (in-vitro) 75.1%보다 낮았고, 민감도 100%는 체외 연구에서만 나옴.

낮은 민감도·연구 간 이질성·임상 근거 부족으로 CBCT의 최적 효능을 확증하지 못하며(메타분석 통합·편향위험 평가 없음), VRF는 방사선 소견과 임상 검사를 병용해 진단해야 함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Per-study extraction tables (sensitivity/specificity/accuracy, study design, sample, post type, observers, calibration, blinding, inter-observer agreement, CBCT machine and exposure parameters) for 20 CBCT-VRF diagnostic studies.
- Stratifies performance by study design and by presence of intracanal posts, showing performance is worst in the more clinically realistic designs.
- Narrative discussion of artifact sources (gutta-percha, metal posts, bioceramic fillers, atomic number of post alloys) and acquisition parameters (voxel size, FOV, kVp, mA).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review with descriptive synthesis; reporting based on "a modification of the guidelines of the Cochrane Handbook"; PRISMA flowchart; PICO table.
- **Databases**: Web of Science, PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane Library, Embase (2000–2022), plus Google Scholar and grey literature; English full text only.
- **Selection**: 1493 records, 1371 removed as irrelevant, 93 after de-duplication, 73 excluded on eligibility, 20 analysed. Two reviewers screened with a third arbitrating. Included in-vitro, ex-vivo, in-vivo, cross-sectional, clinical, prospective and retrospective studies; excluded reviews, animal and histological studies, case reports, studies without a reference standard, and studies of horizontal fractures or VRF in sound teeth.
- **Outcomes**: Sensitivity, specificity, accuracy (area under the curve was among the variables extracted). No quality-assessment instrument or statistical pooling is reported; "overall" values are unweighted means and SDs of study-level estimates.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Root-filled teeth, no intracanal post (17 experiments), mean (SD) %:

| Study design | Sensitivity | Specificity | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-vitro | 75.11 (23.53) | 81.93 (16.90) | 88.98 (8.60) |
| In-vivo (2 studies) | 58.50 (36.06) | 79.00 (2.83) | 81.00 |
| Ex-vivo (2 studies) | 66.37 (12.04) | 46.80 (17.49) | 54.87 (15.90) |
| Clinical (Edlund 2011, n = 32) | 88.0 | 75.0 | 84.0 |
| Cross-sectional (Wanderley 2021) | 53.1 | 80.5 | 67.7 |
| **Overall** | **71.50 (22.19)** | **75.64 (19.41)** | **78.47 (17.19)** |

Root-filled teeth with intracanal post (8 studies):

| Study design | Sensitivity | Specificity | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| In-vitro | 74.09 (18.46) | 78.57 (17.81) | 76.04 (10.07) |
| Ex-vivo (Oliveira 2021, metallic post) | 83 | 53 | 68 |
| Retrospective (Wanderley 2021, cast metal post) | 46.6 | 60.4 | 57.8 |
| **Overall** | **72.76 (18.73)** | **75.44 (18.26)** | **74.02 (10.64)** |

- Reported ranges: sensitivity 32–100% and specificity 36.7–100% (no post); sensitivity 30–92%, specificity 45–100% (post).
- Voxel size: Junqueira 2013 found 0.125 mm voxels gave sensitivity 100% / accuracy 99% versus 78% / 79% at 0.25 mm.
- Fiber vs metal posts: the authors argue fiberglass posts cause fewer artifacts, yet one included study (Chiguti 2021) found fiberglass-post sensitivity of only 30–50% versus 70–80% for metallic posts, attributed to a less conspicuous fracture line.
- Two in-vitro studies reported 100% specificity with metal posts present; the authors attribute this to wide, artificially induced fractures that do not mimic clinical VRF.
- Inter-observer agreement ranged from 32% to 99.4%; observer calibration is listed as performed in 9 of the 20 studies in the observer table.
- The authors note that average values sit near the 75% sensitivity / 85% specificity benchmark proposed for caries detection, but judge CBCT sensitivity for VRF "questionable".

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Predominantly in-vitro evidence (induced fractures, no soft tissue or supporting-bone effects, standardised positioning), which overstates performance; 100% sensitivity occurred only in-vitro.
- No pooled meta-analysis and no risk-of-bias/QUADAS assessment; unweighted means across heterogeneous designs, CBCT machines, voxel sizes, post/filler types and observer panels; English-only, full-text-only inclusion.
- The discussion cites a review reporting 84–100% sensitivity in clinical studies (Chang et al.) and a meta-analysis reporting low pooled values (sensitivity 0.752, specificity 0.652; Talwar et al.), i.e. the wider literature disagrees.
- Calls for larger, methodologically homogeneous clinical studies, and for testing metal-artifact-reduction tools, exomass and contrast agents.

## 7. Glossary
- **VRF (Vertical Root Fracture)**: complete or partial fracture running along the long axis of the root, usually buccolingual.
- **Beam hardening**: preferential absorption of low-energy photons by dense materials (posts, gutta-percha), producing dark bands and streaks that can mimic fracture lines.
- **Voxel size / FOV**: CBCT spatial-resolution unit / field of view; smaller voxels and narrow FOV favour VRF detection at higher dose.
- **Intracanal post**: metal or fiber post cemented in the root canal; a major source of CBCT artifact.
- **In-vitro / ex-vivo / in-vivo**: bench model with induced fractures / extracted human teeth under more clinical-like conditions / living patients; in this review ex-vivo and in-vivo estimates are lower than in-vitro.
