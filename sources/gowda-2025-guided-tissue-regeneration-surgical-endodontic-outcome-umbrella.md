## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/endodontics/he-2026-hemostatic-agents-periapical-surgery-sr-ma]]가 periapical surgery의 지혈제 보조 요소를 다뤘다면, 본 umbrella review + network meta-analysis(NMA)는 같은 수술의 골재생 보조 요소(GTR barrier membrane/graft)를 14개 SR·48개 원 연구 수준까지 파고들어 16개 개입을 직접 서열화했다는 점에서 periapical surgery adjunct 지식을 보강한다. 일반 GBR 기전을 다룬 [[wiki/bone-regeneration/elgali-2017-guided-bone-regeneration-materials-mechanisms]]의 barrier-membrane 원리가 근관 수술 맥락에서 어떻게 실제 순위로 이어지는지 확인하는 근거로 활용.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review + embedded frequentist network meta-analysis (NMA) of 14 systematic reviews (48 unique deduplicated primary studies, CCA overlap = 13.46%) evaluating guided tissue regeneration (GTR) adjuncts in surgical endodontic treatment (apical/periapical surgery).

The combination of expanded polytetrafluoroethylene (e-PTFE) membrane + hydroxyapatite ranked first (SUCRA = 84.4%; OR vs. control = 0.13, 95% CI 0.02-0.98; estimated NNT ≈ 5), followed by concentrated growth factor (CGF) + xenogenic Bio-Oss collagen graft (SUCRA = 73.2%) and collagen membrane + bovine-derived hydroxyapatite (SUCRA = 70.8%); combination therapies consistently outranked single-component interventions, and the control (no GTR) group ranked lowest (SUCRA = 12.2%).

Despite the clear ranking hierarchy, credibility grading of the 27 underlying effect estimates found none reached Class I/II (convincing/highly suggestive); only 5 reached Class III (suggestive) and 22 were Class IV (weak), so CINeMA certainty was mostly moderate-to-low — clinical guidance remains provisional pending head-to-head RCTs of the top-ranked combinations.

## 세줄요약

14개 체계적 문헌고찰(중복제거 후 원 연구 48편, CCA 중복도 13.46%)을 재추출해 시행한 우산 리뷰(umbrella review) + 빈도주의(frequentist) 네트워크 메타분석(network meta-analysis, NMA)으로, 근관수술(surgical endodontic treatment, 치근단수술) 시 유도조직재생(guided tissue regeneration, GTR) 보조재료의 효과를 비교했다.

확장 폴리테트라플루오로에틸렌(expanded polytetrafluoroethylene, e-PTFE) 막 + 수산화인회석(hydroxyapatite, HA) 병용이 1위(SUCRA=84.4%; 대조군 대비 교차비(odds ratio, OR)=0.13, 95% 신뢰구간(confidence interval, CI) 0.02-0.98; 치료필요수(number needed to treat, NNT)≈5)를 기록했고, 농축성장인자(concentrated growth factor, CGF)+이종골 Bio-Oss 콜라겐 이식재(SUCRA=73.2%), 콜라겐막+우골유래 수산화인회석(SUCRA=70.8%)이 뒤를 이었으며, 복합요법이 단일요법보다 일관되게 우수했고 무처치 대조군이 최하위(SUCRA=12.2%)였다.

다만 27개 효과추정치 중 Class I·II(확실·매우 시사적) 근거는 전무했고 Class III(시사적) 5개, Class IV(약함) 22개로 CINeMA 확실성이 대체로 중등도-저등도였으므로, 서열은 참고용이며 상위 조합 간 직접비교 무작위대조시험(RCT)이 확정에 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review to quantitatively re-extract primary-study-level data across all eligible GTR/apical-surgery systematic reviews and run a unified NMA, avoiding double-counting from the high overlap (CCA = 13.46%) among the 14 source reviews.
- Ranks 16 distinct regenerative interventions (membranes, grafts, autologous platelet concentrates, and their combinations) head-to-head via SUCRA, rather than relying on the pairwise comparisons reported piecemeal across the source reviews.
- Applies a formal 7-domain credibility framework (Class I-IV) to each of the 27 underlying effect estimates, and CINeMA (GRADE-based) certainty rating to each NMA comparison — explicitly separating "which intervention ranks highest" from "how much should we trust that ranking."
- Provides an actionable clinical hierarchy: membrane + graft combinations (e-PTFE + HA; collagen + bovine HA; CGF + xenogenic collagen graft) outperform any single-component strategy, and no-GTR control performs worst of all 16 nodes.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Search & eligibility**: MEDLINE (PubMed), Scopus, Embase, and Web of Science searched to July 2025 for systematic reviews/meta-analyses evaluating GTR in surgical endodontic treatment (human subjects). 61 records → 47 duplicates removed → 14 unique records, all 14 met eligibility (none excluded at full-text stage). PICO: Population = patients undergoing surgical endodontic treatment; Intervention = GTR barrier membranes/regenerative biomaterials; Comparator = conventional apical surgery without GTR or alternative GTR materials; Outcome = clinical + radiographic success (absence of symptoms + radiographic periapical healing per Rud and Molven, Gutmann and Harrison, or modified PENN CBCT criteria); only trials with ≥12-month follow-up entered the primary NMA dataset.

**Overlap quantification**: Corrected Covered Area (CCA) computed via the GROOVE tool across the 14 reviews and 48 unique primary studies → CCA = 13.46%, graded "high" overlap, justifying re-extraction at the primary-study level rather than pooling review-level estimates.

**Credibility assessment (umbrella-review layer)**: Each of 27 effect estimates graded across 7 domains (statistical significance of summary estimate; significance of largest study; pooled N > 1000; I² > 50% heterogeneity; Deeks' test for small-study effects, P < 0.10; 95% prediction interval excluding null; excess significance bias test P < 0.10) → classified Convincing (I), Highly suggestive (II), Suggestive (III), or Weak (IV).

**Risk of bias (review-level)**: ROBIS tool across 4 domains (eligibility criteria; study identification/selection; data collection/appraisal; synthesis/findings) — independently by two reviewers, third-reviewer arbitration.

**Quantitative synthesis (NMA layer)**: Stata 17, frequentist random-effects NMA on the deduplicated primary-study dataset. Outcomes as ORs with 95% CIs; network geometry, forest plots, loop-specific inconsistency factors, net heat plots; SUCRA rankograms + radial SUCRA plots for treatment ranking; network league table for pairwise comparisons; comparison-adjusted funnel plots for publication bias; leverage-vs-residual-deviance plots for model fit.

**Certainty of NMA estimates**: CINeMA (Confidence in Network Meta-Analysis) framework across 6 domains (within-study bias via Cochrane RoB 2.0, across-study bias, indirectness, imprecision, heterogeneity via τ²/I², incoherence via node-splitting) — rated high/moderate/low/very low, independently verified by two reviewers.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Study base**: 14 systematic reviews → 48 unique primary studies after deduplication (CCA = 13.46%, "high" overlap). 7/14 reviews rated high overall ROBIS risk of bias (Sumangali, Sánchez-Torres, di Lauro, Rosen, Jabbari, Flynn); 7/14 rated low risk (Zubizarreta-Macho, Tsesis, Sabeti, Muthanna, Montero-Miralles 2021, Liu, Deng).
- **Credibility grading (27 effect estimates)**: 0 Class I (convincing), 0 Class II (highly suggestive), 5 Class III (suggestive — Sumangali's collagen membrane + bovine HA, and 4 Deng 2016 estimates on bone-replacement analogs/combination therapy in through-and-through and large lesions), 22 Class IV (weak).
- **NMA network — 16 nodes**: autologous platelet concentrates (APCs); bio-resorbable polylactic acid membrane; bovine bone hydroxyapatite; CGF + hydroxyapatite; CGF + xenogenic Bio-Oss collagen graft; calcium sulphate graft; collagen-based bone graft; collagen membranes; collagen membranes + bovine-derived hydroxyapatite; control (no GTR); hydroxyapatite alone; PRP; xenogenic Bio-Oss collagen graft; e-PTFE membranes; e-PTFE + hydroxyapatite; (plus control).
- **SUCRA rankings (top to bottom)**: 1st e-PTFE + hydroxyapatite (SUCRA 84.4%; OR vs. control 0.13, 95% CI 0.02-0.98; estimated absolute risk difference (ARD) +21%, NNT ≈ 5); 2nd CGF + xenogenic Bio-Oss collagen graft (SUCRA 73.2%; OR vs. control 0.26, 95% CI 0.07-0.97; ARD ≈ +18%, NNT ≈ 6); 3rd collagen membrane + bovine-derived hydroxyapatite (SUCRA 70.8%; OR vs. control 0.33, 95% CI 0.22-0.50; ARD ≈ +15%, NNT ≈ 7); hydroxyapatite alone (SUCRA 67.8%) and CGF + hydroxyapatite (SUCRA 67.4%) close behind. Lowest: control/no-GTR (SUCRA 12.2%, rank 16), followed by collagen membrane (Ossix-plus) and bio-resorbable polylactic acid membrane.
- **Consistency/bias checks**: all loop-specific inconsistency factors < 0.15 (one loop with heterogeneity 0.081 but inconsistency factor only 0.025); comparison-adjusted (net) funnel plot symmetrical, suggesting minimal small-study/publication bias.
- **CINeMA certainty**: "High confidence" only for collagen membrane + bovine-derived hydroxyapatite vs. control, and collagen membranes vs. e-PTFE membranes. Most other comparisons (including several involving the top-ranked e-PTFE + HA node) were "moderate confidence" due to imprecision/heterogeneity; comparisons involving APCs or CGF vs. collagen/platelet-based interventions scored lower due to imprecision.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Despite a clean SUCRA hierarchy, the underlying credibility grading is weak: 22/27 (81%) of effect estimates were Class IV, none reached Class I/II, and most NMA comparisons were only moderate CINeMA certainty — the authors themselves caution that "specific material rankings remain tentative" pending confirmatory RCTs.
- Several top-ranked ORs have wide 95% CIs crossing or nearly crossing 1 (e.g., e-PTFE + HA vs. control 95% CI 0.02-0.98; CGF + HA vs. control 95% CI 0.01-4.02), reflecting sparse direct trial data for these specific combinations.
- Protocol was not prospectively registered (no PROSPERO/OSF registration stated).
- Authors explicitly call for head-to-head RCTs directly comparing top-performing interventions (especially e-PTFE + HA vs. leading collagen-based combinations) with standardized biomaterial protocols, standardized lesion classification, and longer follow-up plus patient-reported outcomes.

## 6. Related Work

- Positioned as extending/refining earlier reviews: Zubizarreta-Macho et al. ranked "membrane + graft" highest overall but did not isolate e-PTFE + HA as uniquely superior — this NMA "refines rather than contradicts" that prior finding.
- Concordant with Muthanna et al. (collagen membrane + bovine HA superior on 2D/3D healing) and Sabeti et al. 2025 (CGF + graft best at ~12 months, albeit short follow-up/small samples).
- Complements [[wiki/endodontics/he-2026-hemostatic-agents-periapical-surgery-sr-ma]] (same procedural context — periapical/apical surgery — but focused on hemostatic agents rather than regenerative adjuncts) and generalizes principles from [[wiki/bone-regeneration/elgali-2017-guided-bone-regeneration-materials-mechanisms]] (GBR barrier-membrane biology) into the endodontic-surgery-specific comparative hierarchy.

## 7. Glossary

- **GTR (guided tissue regeneration)**: placement of a biocompatible barrier membrane, with or without bone graft, to exclude epithelial/connective-tissue in-growth and favor periodontal-ligament/osteoblast repopulation of a healing defect.
- **e-PTFE**: expanded polytetrafluoroethylene, a non-resorbable barrier membrane.
- **CGF**: concentrated growth factor, an autologous platelet concentrate providing growth factors and a fibrin scaffold.
- **APC**: autologous platelet concentrate (PRF, PRP, injectable PRF, CGF, etc.).
- **SUCRA**: Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking curve — 0-100% probability metric that a treatment is among the most effective in a network meta-analysis.
- **CCA**: Corrected Covered Area — overlap metric for primary studies shared across multiple systematic reviews (via the GROOVE tool).
- **CINeMA**: Confidence in Network Meta-Analysis, a GRADE-based framework rating certainty of NMA effect estimates across 6 domains.
- **ROBIS**: Risk Of Bias In Systematic reviews — tool assessing bias introduced during the systematic review process itself (not primary-study quality).
