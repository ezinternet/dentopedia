## Why Ingested

기존 위키의 항혈소판제 발치 근거는 엄브렐라 리뷰 [[wiki/drug/anticoagulants/yong-2022-dapt-dental-extraction-umbrella-review]]("DAPT 출혈 증가는 임상적으로 유의하지 않을 수 있음")와 일반 수술 대상 SR+MA [[wiki/drug/anticoagulants/shah-2022-perioperative-antiplatelet-therapy-sr-ma]]에 의존했다. 본 SR+MA는 발치 전용으로 요법별(아스피린 단독·비아스피린 단독·DAPT)·대조군별 상대위험도(RR)를 정량화해 "단독 아스피린 안전, DAPT는 고위험"의 경계를 수치로 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PubMed and Google Scholar; 35 papers in the SR, 23 in the meta-analysis) of bleeding after minor dental extraction in patients on different antiplatelet therapy (APT) regimens, with healthy, interrupted-APT, and no-control subgroups.

Aspirin vs healthy controls showed no significant immediate or delayed bleeding risk (RR 1.26, P = .5; RR 2.17, P = .09), whereas dual APT vs healthy controls had RR 10.3 (immediate, P < .0001) and RR 7.72 (delayed, P = .001); continued vs interrupted dual APT was RR 2.13 for immediate bleeding (P = .07, not significant).

Extraction is safe on aspirin monotherapy, but dual APT patients should be regarded as at risk for immediate and continued bleeding; abstract-only retrieval, so study-level detail and bias assessment are not available.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-analysis, SR+MA; PubMed·Google Scholar 검색, SR 35편 중 메타분석 23편) — 항혈소판요법 (Antiplatelet Therapy, APT) 요법별 소발치 후 출혈 위험을 건강 대조군·APT 중단 대조군·무대조군으로 나누어 비교.

아스피린 대 건강 대조군은 즉시·지연 출혈 모두 유의차 없음(RR 1.26, P = .5; RR 2.17, P = .09), 이중항혈소판요법 (Dual Antiplatelet Therapy, DAPT)은 건강 대조군 대비 즉시 RR 10.3(P < .0001), 지연 RR 7.72(P = .001); DAPT 유지 대 중단은 즉시 출혈 RR 2.13이나 유의하지 않음(P = .07).

아스피린 단독 복용자는 안전하게 발치 가능하나 DAPT 환자는 즉시·지속 출혈 고위험군으로 간주해야 함; 초록만 확보되어 개별 연구 상세와 편향 평가는 확인 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Dental-extraction-specific quantification of bleeding risk by APT regimen (aspirin, single non-aspirin, dual).
- Separates immediate from delayed bleeding and stratifies by control type (healthy, interrupted APT, none).
- Provides a directly comparable DAPT-continuation vs interruption estimate (RR 2.13, not significant).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design:** SR+MA; databases PubMed and Google Scholar.
- **Studies:** 35 in the SR, 23 with data usable for meta-analysis.
- **Subgroups:** no control, healthy control, interrupted-APT control; meta-analysis split into immediate and delayed bleeding.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Aspirin vs healthy: immediate RR 1.26 (P = .5); delayed RR 2.17 (P = .09) — no significant risk.
- Single non-aspirin APT vs healthy: immediate RR 3.72 (P = .0009).
- Dual APT vs healthy: immediate RR 10.3 (P < .0001); delayed RR 7.72 (P = .001).
- Dual APT continued vs interrupted: immediate RR 2.13 (P = .07, not significant).
- Authors' conclusion: extraction is safe on aspirin monotherapy; dual APT patients are at risk for immediate and continued bleeding.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingestion: confidence intervals, heterogeneity (I²), risk-of-bias tools, definition of "bleeding", and local hemostatic measures used are not available.
- Only two databases were searched (PubMed, Google Scholar).
- The delayed-bleeding aspirin estimate (RR 2.17, P = .09) is imprecise and not distinguishable from a modest increase on the abstract alone.
- The DAPT continued-vs-interrupted comparison (P = .07) is borderline and does not address thromboembolic harm of interruption.

- **APT / DAPT:** antiplatelet therapy / dual antiplatelet therapy.
- **RR:** relative risk.
- **Immediate vs delayed bleeding:** bleeding at/soon after extraction vs later recurrence (as categorized by the authors).
