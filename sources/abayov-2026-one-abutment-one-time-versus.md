## Why Ingested

2026년 최신 SR+MA — 플랫폼 스위칭(PS) + 원뿔 연결(conical connection) 임플란트에 한정하여 1어버트먼트-1회(OAOT) 프로토콜이 반복 탈착 대비 변연골 소실(MBL)에 미치는 효과를 정량화한 첫 전용 메타분석. 기존 [[implants/mbl/juan-montesinos-2022-platform-switching-conventional-sr-ma]]가 PS 설계를 전반적으로 다룬 것과 달리, 이 논문은 PS × conical connection × OAOT 삼중 교차 효과를 목표로 한다.

## Three-line Summary

PRISMA SR+MA of 7 RCTs (395 patients, 645 implants, 2020–2024) comparing one-abutment-one-time (OAOT) vs repeated abutment disconnections in bone-level, platform-switched, conical-connection implants; marginal bone loss (MBL) was the primary outcome.

Pooled random-effects meta-analysis (6 parallel-group RCTs, 603 implants) showed statistically significant MBL reduction favouring OAOT (MD −0.23 mm; 95% CI −0.44 to −0.02); no significant difference in probing depth; implant survival >94% in both groups; I² = 77%.

OAOT provides a modest but consistent crestal bone preservation benefit in PS conical-connection implants, without clear advantages for other peri-implant outcomes; clinical relevance of the 0.23 mm difference should be interpreted cautiously.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA SR+MA, 7 RCT (환자 395명, 임플란트 645개, 2020–2024): 골수준 PS 원뿔 연결 임플란트에서 1어버트먼트-1회(OAOT, One-Abutment-One-Time) vs 반복 탈착 어버트먼트; 주요 결과: 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss).

6 parallel-group RCT 풀링(임플란트 603개): OAOT에서 MBL 유의하게 적음(MD −0.23 mm; 95%CI −0.44~−0.02); 탐침 깊이(PD, Probing Depth) 차이 없음; 생존율 양군 >94%; 이질성 I² = 77%.

PS + 원뿔 연결 임플란트에서 OAOT는 MBL 보존에 소폭이나 일관된 이점; 임상 유의성은 제한적 — 비심미 부위에서 0.23 mm 차이의 실질적 의미는 주의해서 해석해야.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA specifically restricted to PS + internal **conical connection** implants comparing OAOT vs repeated abutment disconnections
- 7 RCTs included (2020–2024); 395 patients; 645 implants; follow-up 1–5 years
- Pooled MBL: OAOT −0.23 mm (MD; 95% CI −0.44 to −0.02) statistically significant
- Individual RCTs: 3 of 7 reached statistical significance; 4 showed numerical trend only
- Probing depth pooled: MD +0.07 mm (NS; 95% CI −0.12 to +0.26)
- Survival rates uniformly >94% in both arms; no clinically relevant intergroup difference
- I² = 77% (substantial heterogeneity); between-study variance τ² = 0.0304

## 3. Methodology
- Design: PRISMA SR+MA; PROSPERO CRD420251151458
- Search: MEDLINE/PubMed; November 2018–November 2025
- Population: adults ≥18, bone-level PS implants with **internal conical** connection
- Inclusion: RCTs; OAOT (definitive abutment placed once, never removed) vs ≥1 disconnection/reconnection
- Exclusion: flat-to-flat (external hex), tissue-level implants, mixed connection types
- Primary outcome: MBL (mm); Secondary: PD, implant survival, biological/prosthetic complications
- Statistical: random-effects REML; continuous outcomes as mean differences (MD) with 95% CI; forest plots; Egger regression for funnel asymmetry
- Risk of bias: JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist — 6/7 low risk; 1/7 moderate

## 4. Key Results
| Study | Follow-up | OAOT MBL | Control MBL | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Praça 2020 | 2yr | −0.18 mm | −0.13 mm | 0.792 |
| D'Avenia 2020 | 5yr | 0.11 mm | 0.48 mm | 0.012 |
| Ríos-Santos 2020 | 1yr | 0.36 mm | 0.48 mm | >0.05 |
| Hamudi 2021 (split-mouth) | 3yr | 0.35 mm | 0.57 mm | 0.005 |
| Moreira 2021 | 1yr | 0.14 mm | 0.21 mm | 0.33 |
| Calatrava 2024 | 1yr | −0.53 mm | −0.01 mm | 0.008 |
| Sanz-Sánchez 2024 | 5yr | 0.53 mm | 0.97 mm | 0.063 |

- **Pooled (6 parallel-group RCTs)**: MD −0.23 mm (95%CI −0.44 to −0.02; p<0.05)
- Probing depth (3 studies, 164 implants): MD +0.07 mm (95%CI −0.12 to +0.26; NS)
- Implant survival: all studies >94%; no statistically significant differences
- Prosthetic complications: only D'Avenia reported — more in control (9 vs 1)
- Implant systems used: Morse Taper M+N (Zimmer Biomet), Ankylos C/X (Dentsply), KlocknerVEGA (Soadco), MISC1 (MIS), ConelogScrew-Line (Camlog)

## 5. Limitations
- Only MEDLINE/PubMed searched (no EMBASE/Cochrane)
- Search window restricted to 2018–2025 (may miss relevant 2015–2018 RCTs)
- Substantial heterogeneity (I² = 77%)
- Abutment height inconsistently reported across included studies
- Clinical relevance of 0.23 mm MBL difference uncertain, especially in non-aesthetic areas
- Prosthetic complication data too limited for quantitative synthesis

## 7. Glossary
- **OAOT**: One-Abutment-One-Time — definitive abutment placed at implant surgery, never removed thereafter
- **PS**: Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭) — narrower abutment on wider implant platform
- **Conical connection**: internal tapered implant-abutment interface (원뿔 연결)
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss (변연골 소실) — radiographic crestal bone change
- **PD**: Probing depth (탐침 깊이) — peri-implant sulcus depth
- **I²**: Heterogeneity statistic — 77% = substantial between-study variability
