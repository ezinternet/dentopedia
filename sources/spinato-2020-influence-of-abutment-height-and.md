## Why Ingested

지대주 높이 (Abutment Height)와 점막 두께 (Mucosal Thickness)를 무작위 배정으로 교차한 18개월 RCT를 추가한다. 후향적 근거인 [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]]의 짧은 지대주 불리 방향을 높이 단독 변수로 재확인하고, 얇은 점막에서 골소실이 크다는 [[implants/mbl/puisys-2015-influence-mucosal-tissue-thickening-crestal-bone]]의 결론을 조건부로 한정하는 상충 신호를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Randomised clinical trial (80 patients, 80 internal hex platform-switched implants placed equicrestally in the posterior mandible with a two-stage protocol) crossing vertical mucosal thickness (thin <= 2.0 mm vs thick > 2.0 mm) with randomly assigned 1-mm or 3-mm abutments for single screw-retained crowns, followed to 18 months of loading.

At 6 months of loading (T2) marginal bone loss (mean +/- SE) was 0.61 +/- 0.09 mm (thin) and 0.64 +/- 0.07 mm (thick) with 1-mm abutments versus 0.32 +/- 0.07 mm (thin) and 0.26 +/- 0.04 mm (thick) with 3-mm abutments; the greatest loss occurred in the first 6 months, and 74 of 77 implants were functioning at 18 months (96% survival).

Short (1-mm) abutments lost more bone than 3-mm abutments in both mucosal-thickness strata, and vertical mucosal thickness had no significant influence — abstract-only, so exact p-values, CIs and later-time-point values are not available.

## 세줄요약

무작위 대조 임상시험 (Randomized Clinical Trial, RCT) — 하악 구치부에 내부 육각 연결 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching, PS) 임플란트 80개(환자 80명)를 골정상 (Equicrestal) 이단계 (Two-Stage) 방식으로 식립, 수직 점막 두께 (Vertical Mucosal Thickness; 얇음 ≤ 2.0 mm / 두꺼움 > 2.0 mm)와 무작위 배정된 1 mm·3 mm 지대주 (Abutment)를 교차해 기능 부하 18개월까지 추적.

기능 부하 6개월(T2) 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL; 평균 ± SE)은 1 mm 지대주군 얇은 점막 0.61 ± 0.09 mm·두꺼운 점막 0.64 ± 0.07 mm, 3 mm 지대주군 얇은 점막 0.32 ± 0.07 mm·두꺼운 점막 0.26 ± 0.04 mm; 골소실은 첫 6개월에 가장 컸고 18개월에 77개 중 74개 기능 (생존율 96%).

1 mm 지대주가 두 점막 두께 층 모두에서 3 mm 지대주보다 골소실이 컸고 수직 점막 두께는 유의한 영향이 없었다 — 초록만 확보되어 정확한 p값·신뢰구간·이후 시점 수치는 확인 불가.

## Key Contributions

- Randomised comparison of 1-mm versus 3-mm elevated abutments with implants placed at the same (equicrestal) depth, so abutment height is varied without co-varying insertion depth.
- Stratifies patients by vertical mucosal thickness (thin <= 2.0 mm vs thick > 2.0 mm) and reports that thickness had no significant influence on marginal bone loss.
- Describes the time course to 18 months of loading: the greatest marginal bone loss occurred during the first 6 months of function.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Randomised clinical trial; 18-month post-loading clinical and radiographic evaluation.
- **Population**: 80 implants in 80 patients, posterior mandible; two groups by vertical mucosal thickness, thin (<= 2.0 mm) and thick (> 2.0 mm).
- **Implants**: internal hex platform-switched implants, placed equicrestally, two-stage protocol.
- **Intervention**: elevated prosthetic abutments of 1 mm or 3 mm randomly assigned for single screw-retained crowns within both groups.
- **Measurements**: mesial and distal marginal bone loss at implant placement (T0), crown delivery (4 months, T1), and 6 (T2), 12 (T3) and 18 months (T4) of functional loading.
- **Not available in the abstract**: implant system, randomization/concealment/blinding details, radiographic method, sample size per cell, exact p-values, confidence intervals.

## Key Results

- Three patients dropped out at T2; at T4, 74 of 77 implants were functioning (96% survival rate).
- Marginal bone loss (mean +/- SE) at T2:

| Group | Thin mucosa (<= 2.0 mm) | Thick mucosa (> 2.0 mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 1-mm abutment | 0.61 +/- 0.09 mm | 0.64 +/- 0.07 mm |
| 3-mm abutment | 0.32 +/- 0.07 mm | 0.26 +/- 0.04 mm |

- The abstract states that T2 loss was significantly greater in the 1-mm abutment groups than in the 3-mm groups; exact p-values are not given.
- The marginal bone loss pattern over 18 months showed the greatest loss during the first 6 months of function; T3/T4 values are not reported in the abstract.
- Authors' conclusion: 1-mm abutments showed greater marginal bone loss than 3-mm abutments on identical implants, with vertical mucosal thickness having no significant influence.

## Limitations

- **Abstract-only**: no full-text tables, CIs, exact p-values, or risk-of-bias detail.
- Only T2 values are reported in the abstract, so the 12- and 18-month magnitude of the abutment-height difference cannot be read here.
- Single configuration tested (internal hex, platform-switched, equicrestal, two-stage, posterior mandible); findings may not transfer to other connections, depths or one-stage protocols.
- Mucosal-thickness strata are subgroups of a trial whose randomised factor was abutment height; the null thickness finding is a stratified comparison and the per-cell n is not stated.

## Glossary

- **Abutment height** — transmucosal height of the elevated abutment above the implant platform; here 1 mm vs 3 mm.
- **Vertical mucosal thickness** — thickness of peri-implant soft tissue above the crest; thin <= 2.0 mm, thick > 2.0 mm.
- **Equicrestal placement** — implant platform flush with the crestal bone.
- **Platform switching (PS)** — abutment narrower than the implant platform.
- **Two-stage protocol** — implant submerged at placement, uncovered at a second surgery.
- **Marginal bone loss (MBL)** — radiographic crestal bone change from placement (T0), reported as mean +/- SE here.
