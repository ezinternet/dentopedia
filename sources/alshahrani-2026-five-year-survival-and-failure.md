## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cross-sectional study (J Prosthodont 2026, n=3300 monolithic zirconia complete arch fixed implant-supported prostheses, 5-year life table) from a single Dubai commercial dental laboratory. Five-year cumulative survival was 91.67%, with escalating annual failures (2→14 events/year); framework fractures comprised 90.5% of all failures, with 89.5% associated with vertical prosthetic space deficiency (CSSA <80 mm² or posterior space <10 mm). Ti cylinder debonding and fracture were zero, and ceramic chipping occurred only with occlusal veneering.

## Abstract

This retrospective cross-sectional study analyzed 3300 screw-retained 3Y-TZP monolithic zirconia complete arch fixed implant-supported prostheses (CAFIPs) from a Dubai commercial dental laboratory (August 2019–August 2024). Maxillary n=1900, mandibular n=1400. Porcelain veneering limited to gingival zone. Life table survival analysis excluding clinician/patient-driven remakes. 5-year cumulative survival 91.67%. Escalating failure trend: Year 1: 2 events → Year 5: 14 events. Framework fractures n=38 (90.5% of failures), 89.5% associated with insufficient vertical space (<10mm posterior, CSSA <80mm²). Ceramic chipping n=4 (mandibular, excessive veneering). Zero Ti cylinder debonding/fracture. Screw loosening n=12, fracture n=2 (maintenance events).

## Discussion Highlights

- 91.67% 5-yr survival aligns with/exceeds prior full-arch zirconia reports (89–95%)
- **Framework fracture = dominant failure (90.5%)** — consistent with literature identifying this as Achilles' heel of full-arch zirconia
- **Vertical space deficiency = primary modifiable risk factor** (89.5% of fractures) — corroborates Papaspyridakos 2024 CSSA ≥80mm² / >10mm posterior space thresholds
- **Escalating annual failures (2→14)** suggests 5-yr survival underestimates long-term risk; mechanisms: cyclic fatigue, microcrack propagation, cumulative occlusal overload
- **Zero Ti cylinder failures** — screw-retained Ti base interface mechanically reliable in monolithic design (contrast: bilayer veneered Zr where Ti debonding occurs)
- **Ceramic chipping rare (n=4)** and only with occlusal veneering — supports gingival-only veneering strategy
- Limitations: retrospective lab-based; single lab (Dubai); implant system heterogeneity; vertical space from digital casts not intraoral; no clinical/patient outcomes; no parafunction/maintenance data

## Clinical Implications

| Finding | Clinical Action |
|---|---|
| **Vertical space <10mm posterior / CSSA <80mm² → 89.5% of fractures** | **Mandatory pre-design CBCT space analysis**; enforce Papaspyridakos thresholds; consider alternative materials/designs if space inadequate |
| **Escalating failure trend (2→14/yr)** | Counsel patients: 5-yr survival ≠ long-term guarantee; schedule extended surveillance |
| **Ti cylinder 0% failure** | Screw-retained monolithic zirconia = mechanically robust connection; prefer over cement-retained |
| **Gingival-only veneering → 0 chipping** | Adopt gingival-only porcelain protocol; avoid occlusal veneering especially in mandible |
| **Maxillary fracture rate 4× mandibular** | Maxilla more vulnerable — stricter space verification; consider metal-reinforced frameworks for high-risk maxillary cases |
