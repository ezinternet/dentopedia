## Why Ingested

This systematic review and meta-analysis establishes cetylpyridinium chloride (CPC) as a clinically viable substitute for chlorhexidine when used adjunctively with toothbrushing, offering a stain-free option without sacrificing plaque or gingivitis control. The finding is directly relevant to long-term mouthwash recommendations and refines the CHX framework established in [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/van-swaaij-2019-chx-ads-discoloration-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis evaluated the effectiveness of cetylpyridinium chloride (CPC) mouthwash versus chlorhexidine (CHX) mouthwash on plaque and gingivitis scores in 14 RCTs (18 comparisons) from both brushing and non-brushing situations. In non-brushing studies, CHX-MW showed a small statistically significant favourable effect over CPC-MW for plaque index (WMD = 0.55, p = 0.003, 95% CI: 0.19 to 0.91). In brushing studies, no significant differences were found between CPC-MW and CHX-MW for plaque (WMD = 0.04, p = 0.11) or gingival index (WMD = 0.02, p = 0.20).

There is moderate-certainty evidence that CPC-MW can serve as a substitute for CHX-MW in brushing situations, but CHX-MW remains superior when plaque control is the main focus without brushing.

## 세줄요약

본 체계적문헌고찰 및 메타분석은 세틸피리디늄 염화물(CPC) 구강세정제와 클로르헥시딘(CHX) 구강세정제의 치태 및 치은염 점수 효과를 14개의 무작위대조시험(RCT, 18개 비교군)에서 양치 및 비양치 상황 모두에서 평가하였다. 비양치 연구에서는 CHX-MW가 CPC-MW보다 치태지수에서 작지만 통계적으로 유의한 우월성을 보였고(WMD = 0.55, p = 0.003, 95% CI: 0.19 ~ 0.91), 양치 연구에서는 치태(WMD = 0.04, p = 0.11) 및 치은지수(WMD = 0.02, p = 0.20)에서 CPC-MW와 CHX-MW 간에 유의한 차이가 없었다.

중등도 확실성의 근거는 CPC-MW가 양치 상황에서 CHX-MW의 대안이 될 수 있음을 보여주며, CHX-MW는 비양치 상황에서 치태 조절이 주 목적일 때 여전히 우선 선택이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- CHX mouthwash shows small statistically significant superiority over CPC in non-brushing plaque control (WMD = 0.55, p = 0.003).
- No significant difference between CPC and CHX for plaque control in brushing situations (WMD = 0.04, p = 0.11).
- No significant difference between CPC and CHX for gingival index in brushing situations (WMD = 0.02, p = 0.20).
- CPC mouthwash causes less tooth staining than CHX mouthwash.
- CPC-MW can serve as a reliable long-term alternative to CHX-MW for plaque and gingivitis prevention when used with brushing.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and controlled clinical trials (CCTs).
- Databases: MEDLINE-PubMed and Cochrane-CENTRAL searched from inception to May 2023. Reference lists hand-searched.
- PRISMA compliance: Yes, followed PRISMA and Cochrane Handbook guidelines. Protocol registered.
- Inclusion criteria: RCTs/CCTs in humans ≥18 years, good general health, ≥20 teeth. CPC-MW vs CHX-MW in brushing or non-brushing models. Plaque index (Q&HPI/Turesky, S&LPI, O'Leary) and gingivitis index (S&LGI, Saxton & van der Ouderaa BOP). Staining as secondary outcome (Lobene or modified Lobene).
- Excluded: Hospitalized patients, prosthodontics, orthodontics, implants, studies not reporting plaque/gingivitis outcomes.
- Included: 14 publications providing 18 comparisons (12 non-brushing, 6 brushing).
- Risk of bias: Assessed using Cochrane RoB2 tool. 1 study low risk, 3 moderate risk, 10 high risk.
- Data synthesis: Descriptive analysis + meta-analysis (Review Manager 5.4). Random-effects models used; fixed-effects for <3 studies.
- Heterogeneity: Assessed via I² statistic. Subgroup and sensitivity analyses performed.
- GRADE: Used for evidence quality rating.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Plaque index (non-brushing, end scores)** — WMD = 0.55 (95% CI: 0.19 to 0.91, p = 0.003, I² = 93%) [favours CHX]
- **Plaque index (brushing, end scores)** — WMD = 0.04 (95% CI: -0.01 to 0.10, p = 0.11, I² = 74%)
- **Gingival index (brushing, end scores)** — WMD = 0.02 (95% CI: -0.01 to 0.05, p = 0.20, I² = 55%)
- **Plaque index (brushing, change scores)** — WMD = 0.03 (95% CI: -0.06 to 0.13, p = 0.51, I² = 12%)
- **Gingival index (brushing, change scores)** — WMD = 0.01 (95% CI: -0.03 to 0.04, p = 0.70, I² = 0%)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Considerable statistical heterogeneity in non-brushing meta-analysis (I² = 93%).
- Only one study was rated low risk of bias; 10 studies high risk, 3 moderate.
- Most studies did not report allocation concealment or adequate blinding.
- Publication bias could not be assessed due to <10 studies per meta-analysis.
- Different concentrations of CPC (0.05-0.1%) and CHX (0.05-0.2%) used across studies.
- Lack of reporting on side effects and patient-reported outcomes in most studies.
- Short-term studies (4 days to 5 weeks); long-term safety and efficacy data needed.

## 7. Glossary

- **CPC (Cetylpyridinium Chloride)**: 세틸피리디늄 염화물 — 양이온 계면활성제, 광범위 항균 스펙트럼, 특히 그람양성균 및 효모에 효과적
- **CHX (Chlorhexidine)**: 클로르헥시딘 — 양이온성 비스-비구아나이드, 광범위 항균 효과, 구강세정제의 금본위제(gold standard)
- **WMD (Weighted Mean Difference)**: 가중 평균 차이 — 메타분석에서 동일 단위의 결과를 결합할 때 사용
- **Substantivity**: 지속성 — 구강 내에서 항균제가 장시간 잔류하는 특성 (CHX: ~7시간, CPC: ~90분)
- **SRP (Scaling and Root Planing)**: 스케일링 및 치근 활택술 — 비외과적 치주 치료의 금본위제
- **Q&HPI (Quigley-Hein Plaque Index)**: Quigley-Hein 치태지수 — 치태 평가에 사용되는 지수 중 하나
- **S&LPI (Silness-Loe Plaque Index)**: Silness-Loe 치태지수 — 치태 평가에 널리 사용되는 지수
- **RoB2 (Risk of Bias 2)**: Cochrane의 무작위대조시험 비뚤림 위험 평가 도구
