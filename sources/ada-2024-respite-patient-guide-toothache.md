## Why Ingested

The wiki held the **clinician** renderings of ADA 2024 acute dental pain guidance — [[wiki/drug/analgesics/carrasco-labra-2024-pharmacologic-management-acute-dental-pain-guideline]] and the chairside guides — but not the **patient-facing** companion from the same FDA-funded RESPITE project. Ingested to give patient-explanation work a citable source for the *communication* layer (0–10 pain-scale anchoring, recovery-day timeline, duration-of-relief framing, side-effect iconography), which is what these sheets contribute over the clinician versions. Direct source for the Korean patient tool built in `agenda/2026-08-25_patient-analgesic-explanation-tool.md`.

## Three-line Summary

Single-sheet patient-education handout (2 pages) from the FDA-supported RESPITE project (ADA / University of Pittsburgh / University of Pennsylvania), presenting the ADA 2024 acute dental pain medication ladder for toothache to patients across three age bands — adolescents 12–<17 y, adults 17–<65 y, older adults 65+.

Content mirrors the clinician guideline exactly: first line is over-the-counter non-opioid — ibuprofen 400 mg (max 2,400 mg/d) or naproxen sodium 440 mg (max 1,100 mg/d), each optionally combined with acetaminophen 500 mg (max 4,000 mg/d); acetaminophen 1,000 mg (max 4,000 mg/d) alone when NSAIDs are contraindicated; opioid-containing steps are prescription-only, split-tablet (acetaminophen 325 mg + oxycodone 5 mg or hydrocodone 5–7.5 mg, plus a second acetaminophen 325 mg tablet), and carry an explicit "lowest effective dose, fewest tablets, shortest duration" constraint. For short-term temporary relief the sheet adds 10% or 20% topical benzocaine, up to four pea-size applications, marked DO NOT SWALLOW; for extended relief, a one-time long-acting local anesthetic.

What the sheet adds over the clinician version is entirely presentational and is the reason to hold it: a 0–10 pain scale for patient self-report, an **UNTIL TREATED** banner that frames every option as a bridge to definitive treatment rather than a cure, a per-option "hours of pain relief" bar so patients can compare duration rather than only strength, and pictographic side-effect warnings (dizziness, drowsiness, nausea/vomiting, constipation, numbness, needle) with a severe-liver-damage flag on acetaminophen and an adolescent misuse-risk flag on opioids.

## 세줄요약

미국 식품의약국 (Food and Drug Administration, FDA) 지원 RESPITE 프로젝트(미국치과의사협회 (American Dental Association, ADA)·피츠버그대·펜실베이니아대)의 **환자용 한 장짜리 유인물**(2 pages): 치통 잠정관리 상황의 ADA 2024 급성 치통 약물 사다리를 청소년(12–17세 미만)·성인(17–65세 미만)·고령자(65세 이상) 세 연령대에 걸쳐 환자에게 제시한다.

내용은 술자용 지침과 동일하다 — 1차는 일반의약품 비오피오이드: 이부프로펜 (Ibuprofen) 400mg(1일 최대 2,400mg) 또는 나프록센 나트륨 (Naproxen Sodium) 440mg(1일 최대 1,100mg), 각각 아세트아미노펜 (Acetaminophen) 500mg(1일 최대 4,000mg) 병용 선택 가능. 비스테로이드성 소염진통제 (Nonsteroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug, NSAID) 금기 시 아세트아미노펜 1,000mg 단독. 오피오이드 (Opioid) 포함 단계는 처방의약품이며 분할정 방식(아세트아미노펜 325mg + 옥시코돈 5mg 또는 하이드로코돈 5–7.5mg, 별도 아세트아미노펜 325mg 1정)에 "최소 유효용량·최소 정수·최단 기간" 제약이 명시된다. 단기 잠정 진통으로 10% 또는 20% 벤조카인 (Benzocaine) 도포마취를 완두콩 크기로 최대 4회까지 추가하며 **삼키지 말 것**이 명시되고, 진통 연장에는 장시간작용 국소마취제 1회 투여가 제시된다.

**술자용 대비 이 유인물의 고유 기여는 전적으로 표현 방식이며 그것이 보유 이유다** — 환자 자가보고용 0–10 통증척도, **"확정치료 전까지(UNTIL TREATED)"** 배너로 모든 선택지가 치료가 아니라 확정치료까지의 다리임을 못박고, 선택지별 "진통 지속시간" 막대(세기가 아니라 지속시간으로 비교하게 함), 그림 부작용 경고(어지러움·졸음·오심구토·변비·감각마비·주사), 아세트아미노펜의 중증 간손상 경고와 청소년 오남용 위험 경고.

## Key Contributions

- Translates the ADA 2024 medication ladder into a single patient-readable sheet without altering any dose or ceiling
- Introduces a **0–10 pain scale** as the patient-side input to the ladder — the clinician guides have no equivalent patient self-report anchor
- Presents each option by **duration of relief**, not only by potency — reframing the choice in the term patients actually ask about
- Uses pictographic side-effect encoding so the risk column is readable without pharmacological vocabulary
- Carries the same age-banding as the clinician guides (adolescent / adult / older adult) so the patient and clinician sheets stay aligned in a shared-decision conversation

## Key Results

Not a study — no outcome data. Dose ceilings reproduced from the sheet:

| Option | Unit dose | Maximum daily |
|---|---|---|
| Ibuprofen | 400 mg | 2,400 mg |
| Naproxen sodium | 440 mg | 1,100 mg |
| Ibuprofen + acetaminophen | 400 mg + 500 mg | 2,400 mg / 4,000 mg |
| Naproxen sodium + acetaminophen | 440 mg + 500 mg | 1,100 mg / 4,000 mg |
| Acetaminophen alone (NSAID contraindicated) | 1,000 mg | 4,000 mg |
| Acetaminophen + oxycodone / hydrocodone (Rx) | 325 mg + 5 mg / 5–7.5 mg | acetaminophen 4,000 mg |

## Limitations

- **Not evidence** — a communication artifact derived from the guideline; every clinical claim traces to [[wiki/drug/analgesics/carrasco-labra-2024-pharmacologic-management-acute-dental-pain-guideline]], not to this sheet. Cite the guideline for facts, this sheet for how to say them.
- **US regulatory and market context.** Over-the-counter availability, unit strengths, and the opioid options shown do not transfer to the Korean setting unchanged. The wiki's own clinician reference (`interactives/2026-07-22_prescription-master-reference.html`) records that oxycodone, codeine, and tramadol were equivalent to placebo in network meta-analysis and are therefore hard to justify prescribing.
- Text extraction loses the layout that carries much of the meaning (scale, timeline, icons) — read the PDF, not the extracted text, when reusing the design.
- No Korean-language version exists; any Korean patient material is a translation-plus-localization, not a reproduction.

## Glossary

- **RESPITE** — the FDA-supported project (grant U01FD007151) that produced the ADA 2024 acute dental pain guideline package, including these patient sheets.
- **0–10 pain scale (Numeric Rating Scale)** — patient self-report of pain intensity; the patient-side entry point to the medication ladder.
- **Duration of relief** — hours a given option is expected to control pain, shown as a bar; distinct from potency.
- **Split-tablet opioid regimen** — acetaminophen dosed partly in the combination tablet and partly as a separate tablet, so the acetaminophen ceiling can be respected while titrating the opioid component.
