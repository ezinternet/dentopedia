## Why Ingested
소결 온도가 다른 두 종류의 탈단백우골미네랄(DBBM) 비교 근거로 인제스트. 기존 [[sinus-lift/lateral/li-2023-dbbm-particle-size-sinus-floor-elevation]]이 입자 크기를 비교했다면, 이 논문은 소결 온도(저온 300°C Bio-Oss® vs 고온 400–1200°C Gegreen®)의 임상적 차이를 RCT로 탐색 — 두 재료 간 차이 없음을 보고.

## One-line Summary
Pilot multicenter RCT (n=36, 6-month): LT-DBBM (Bio-Oss®, 300°C) vs HT-DBBM (Gegreen®, two-step high-temperature calcination) in LSFE — no significant differences in bone height gain, bone volume, resorption rate, or Micro-CT bone microarchitecture at 6 months.

## 한줄요약
파일럿 다기관 RCT(n=36, 6개월): 저온소결 DBBM(Bio-Oss®)과 고온소결 DBBM(Gegreen®) 비교 — 골높이 획득, 골부피, 흡수율, Micro-CT 골 미세구조 모두 유의차 없음; 소결 온도가 임상 결과에 영향 미치지 않음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT directly comparing low-temperature sintered DBBM (Bio-Oss®) vs high-temperature sintered DBBM (Gegreen®) in lateral window sinus lift
- Demonstrates clinical equivalence (no significant differences in all 10+ measured outcomes) despite different manufacturing processes
- Provides comprehensive multi-modal outcome data: CBCT-based 3D volumetry (MIMICS software) + Micro-CT + histology
- Supports "clinical interchangeability" of Bio-Oss®-class vs HT-DBBM for LSFE under 6-month evaluation horizon
- Caution: interim analysis (n=36 vs target n=53 per group); limited statistical power

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Pilot, multicenter, randomized, controlled, single-blind clinical trial (interim analysis)
- n=36 total: LT-DBBM n=20 (12M/8F, age 53.29), HT-DBBM n=16 (6M/10F, age 52.00)
- Particle size: 0.25–1.0mm (comparable between groups)
- LT-DBBM: Bio-Oss® (Geistlich Pharma, Switzerland), sintered at 300°C
- HT-DBBM: Gegreen® (Ruishen Biotechnology, China), two-step high-temperature calcination (400–800°C + 800–1200°C)
- Technique: Lateral window sinus floor elevation (LSFE); collagen membrane coverage
- Implant placement: 6 months postop (first-stage surgery with bone core biopsy)
- Imaging: CBCT (T0, T1, T2); DICOM → MIMICS 21.0 for 3D STL reconstruction and volumetric analysis
- Micro-CT: BV/TV, Tb.Th, Tb.N, Tb.Sp from bone column samples (n=11 LT, n=12 HT)
- Histology: EDTA decalcification, 4μm paraffin sections, H&E staining
- Statistics: GraphPad Prism 8; t-test; significant at p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Bone height (CBCT):**
| Measure | LT-DBBM (Bio-Oss®) | HT-DBBM (Gegreen®) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| ΔH1 (immediate gain, mm) | 12.55 ± 0.42 | 12.46 ± 0.59 | NS |
| H2 (6-month total height, mm) | 14.35 ± 0.44 | 14.89 ± 0.65 | NS |
| ΔH2 (6-month gain, mm) | 11.28 ± 0.46 | 11.41 ± 0.55 | NS |
| ΔH (resorption T1→T2, mm) | -0.96 ± 0.21 | -1.05 ± 0.28 | NS |

**Bone volume (CBCT):**
| Measure | LT-DBBM | HT-DBBM | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| ΔV1 (immediate gain, mm³) | 2165 ± 285.6 | 2477 ± 298.4 | NS |
| ΔV2 (6-month gain, mm³) | 2025 ± 301.9 | 2248 ± 299.8 | NS |
| Resorption rate ΔV/ΔV1 | 8.32 ± 3.02% | 10.44 ± 4.63% | NS |

**Micro-CT bone microarchitecture (6-month biopsy):**
| Parameter | LT-DBBM | HT-DBBM | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| BV/TV | 13.21 ± 2.75% | 13.92 ± 3.07% | NS |
| Tb.Th (mm) | 0.044 ± 0.008 | 0.061 ± 0.011 | NS |
| Tb.N | 4.76 ± 1.52 | 3.52 ± 1.31 | NS |
| Tb.Sp | 0.41 ± 0.10 | 0.40 ± 0.08 | NS |

**Histology:** Both groups showed newly formed mature bone, blood vessels around bone particles, no adverse inflammation in either group.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Interim analysis: n=36 vs planned n=53 per group — underpowered for definitive conclusions
- Single 6-month time point; DBBM healing may continue 9–12 months
- Pilot study design limits generalizability; complete 4-group RCT (with PRF arms) pending
- Sintering temperature mechanistic differences (cell adhesion, crystallinity) not directly tested in vivo
- Future: complete 4-group RCT (Bio-Oss + PRF, Gegreen + PRF); longer follow-up to 12 months; implant survival as primary endpoint

## 7. Glossary
- **LT-DBBM**: Low-temperature sintered Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral — Bio-Oss® equivalent (300°C)
- **HT-DBBM**: High-temperature sintered DBBM — Gegreen® (400–800°C + 800–1200°C two-step)
- **BV/TV**: Bone Volume fraction / Total Volume — Micro-CT metric for bone density
- **Tb.Th**: Trabecular Thickness (mm) — bone trabecula average thickness
- **Tb.N**: Trabecular Number — trabecula density
- **Tb.Sp**: Trabecular Separation — medullary cavity average width between trabeculae
- **ΔH**: Height resorption from T1 (immediate) to T2 (6 months)
- **MIMICS**: Medical imaging software for 3D CBCT reconstruction and volumetric analysis

## Three-line Summary

- Pilot multicenter RCT (n=36, LT-DBBM Bio-Oss® n=20 vs HT-DBBM Gegreen® n=16, 6-month follow-up with CBCT, Micro-CT, and histology) comparing low-temperature (300°C) vs high-temperature (400–1200°C) sintered deproteinized bovine bone mineral in lateral window sinus floor elevation.
- No significant differences between groups in any measured outcome: immediate bone height gain (12.55 vs 12.46mm), 6-month total height (14.35 vs 14.89mm), volume resorption rate (8.32% vs 10.44%), or Micro-CT bone microarchitecture (BV/TV ~13.2–13.9%).
- Sintering temperature does not affect 6-month clinical outcomes in lateral sinus augmentation; LT- and HT-DBBM are clinically interchangeable at this time horizon, though the study remains underpowered as an interim analysis.

## 세줄요약

- 파일럿 다기관 무작위 대조 시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)(n=36: 저온소결 탈단백 우골광물(LT-DBBM, Low-Temperature Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral) Bio-Oss® n=20 vs 고온소결(HT-DBBM, High-Temperature DBBM) Gegreen® n=16): 측방창 상악동저 거상술(Lateral Sinus Floor Elevation, LSFE) 6개월 추적, CBCT·Micro-CT·조직학 평가.
- 두 군 간 모든 측정 지표에서 유의차 없음: 즉시 골높이 획득(12.55 vs 12.46mm), 6개월 총 골높이(14.35 vs 14.89mm), 부피 흡수율(8.32% vs 10.44%), 골 미세구조 골부피비율(BV/TV, Bone Volume/Total Volume) ~13.2–13.9%.
- 소결 온도는 측방 상악동거상술의 6개월 임상 결과에 영향을 미치지 않음; 다만 중간 분석(interim analysis)으로 검정력 부족 — 완전 4군 RCT 결과를 기다려야.
