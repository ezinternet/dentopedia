## Why Ingested

Masticatory function is almost never an outcome in the zirconia-vs-metal-ceramic crown literature we hold — our pages compare survival, chipping and marginal fit ([[wiki/prosthetic-materials/pjetursson-2026-metal-ceramic-all-ceramic-crowns-survival-sr-ma]], [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/gseibat-2025-zirconia-metal-ceramic-posterior-5year-rct]]) but not chewing or inflammation. This propensity-matched cohort reports masticatory efficiency, chewing VAS, serum IL-6/TNF-α/CRP and a mediation analysis through gingival health, so it is the wiki's only data point on that axis — and because it is observational with heavy confounding, it is filed as a hypothesis-generating signal rather than as material-selection evidence. It also extends the local periodontal-response thread of [[wiki/overviews/restorative-margin-periodontal-interface-overview]] toward systemic inflammatory markers.

## Three-line Summary

Single-center propensity-score-matched retrospective cohort (196 patients treated Jan 2023–Jan 2024, matched to 79 zirconia all-ceramic vs 79 porcelain-fused-to-metal full crowns; 3-month follow-up) measuring serum IL-6/TNF-α/CRP, masticatory efficiency, chewing VAS, gingival status and clinical success.

All three inflammatory markers fell more in the zirconia group at 3 months (IL-6 7.2 ± 2.0 vs 9.8 ± 2.5 pg/mL; TNF-α 11.5 ± 2.7 vs 14.3 ± 3.0 pg/mL; CRP 3.5 ± 1.0 vs 4.8 ± 1.3 mg/L; all P < .001), masticatory efficiency reached 78.6 ± 9.2% vs 70.2 ± 8.9% (P < .001 for time×group), and an indirect effect of −0.37 (95% CI −0.58 to −0.19) on ΔCRP ran through gingival improvement.

Treatment effects of this size at 3 months are implausible for a single crown and the design cannot separate material from digital-versus-conventional workflow, cement and unmeasured systemic inflammation — treat the material-inflammation pathway as an unconfirmed hypothesis.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 성향점수매칭 후향적 코호트 (Propensity-Score-Matched Retrospective Cohort, 2023년 1월–2024년 1월 196명 → 지르코니아 전세라믹관 79명 vs 금속도재관(Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal, PFM) 79명, 3개월 추적) — 혈청 인터루킨-6(Interleukin-6, IL-6)·종양괴사인자-α(Tumor Necrosis Factor-α, TNF-α)·C-반응성단백(C-Reactive Protein, CRP), 저작효율, 저작 기능 시각아날로그척도(Visual Analogue Scale, VAS), 치은 상태, 임상 성공률 평가.

3개월째 세 염증지표 모두 지르코니아군에서 더 크게 감소 (IL-6 7.2 ± 2.0 vs 9.8 ± 2.5 pg/mL; TNF-α 11.5 ± 2.7 vs 14.3 ± 3.0 pg/mL; CRP 3.5 ± 1.0 vs 4.8 ± 1.3 mg/L; 모두 P < .001), 저작효율 78.6 ± 9.2% vs 70.2 ± 8.9%, ΔCRP에 대한 치은 개선 경유 간접효과 −0.37 (95% CI −0.58 to −0.19).

크라운 1개로 3개월에 이 정도 효과크기는 생물학적으로 납득하기 어렵고 재료 효과가 디지털 vs 전통 워크플로우·시멘트·미측정 전신 염증과 교락되어 있어, 재료-염증 경로는 미확인 가설로만 취급해야 함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Puts **masticatory function** (objective masticatory efficiency **and** patient-reported chewing VAS) into a zirconia-vs-metal-ceramic crown comparison — an outcome the survival/chipping literature does not measure.
- Reports **serum** inflammatory markers (IL-6, TNF-α, CRP) at three timepoints (baseline, 6 weeks, 3 months) rather than local gingival indices only, proposing a material → gingival health → systemic inflammation pathway.
- Runs a **mediation analysis** (bias-corrected bootstrap, 5000 resamples) with change in gingival index as mediator of the material effect on ΔCRP: indirect effect −0.37 (95% CI −0.58 to −0.19).
- Reports **responder analyses** with pre-specified thresholds (IL-6 ↓≥30%, CRP ↓≥1.0 mg/L, VAS ↑≥2 points) and effect estimates with 95% CIs, not just mean differences.
- Reports a 3-month composite "clinical success" of 93.7% (zirconia) vs 83.5% (PFM; OR 2.78, 95% CI 1.05–7.39, P = .039).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: observational cohort with 1:1 propensity score matching; no randomization, no allocation concealment.
- **Sample**: 196 consecutive patients treated 2023-01 to 2024-01 → zirconia n = 102, PFM n = 94 → after propensity score matching **79 vs 79** (n = 158 analyzed).
- **Inclusion**: age ≥ 18; indication for full-crown restoration (caries, fracture, tooth structure loss, esthetic demand); preserved root and stable periodontal condition; suitable occlusion; complete data and able to attend follow-up.
- **Exclusion**: uncontrolled diabetes, hematologic/severe cardiovascular disease, immune disorders, radio/chemotherapy; severe periodontitis, mobility III, severe bone resorption, periapical lesions, severe occlusal disorder, TMD; long-term heavy smoking, alcohol abuse, severe bruxism/nocturnal grinding; pregnancy/lactation; oral mucosal disease or maxillofacial tumors; material allergy; psychiatric/intellectual impairment or lost follow-up.
- **Zirconia arm (observation)**: intraoral scan → CAD/CAM milled zirconia coping → veneering and sintering → intraoral try-in with marginal/occlusal check → cemented with **resin-modified glass ionomer**.
- **PFM arm (control)**: silicone rubber impression → plaster model → wax pattern, casting, polishing → porcelain layering and sintering → try-in → cemented (cement type not specified).
- → Note: the two arms differ in **material, impression method (digital vs conventional), fabrication route and cement**, not in material alone.
- **Inflammatory outcomes**: venous blood at baseline, 6 weeks and 3 months; serum IL-6, TNF-α, CRP by ELISA (kit manufacturer not reported).
- **Masticatory outcomes**: (a) objective **masticatory efficiency** by the **color-mixing / sieving method**, expressed as the percentage distribution of chewed sample passing through sieves (test food, chewing strokes and sieve mesh sizes not reported); (b) subjective **chewing VAS 0–10** (0 = cannot chew at all, 10 = completely normal chewing). Both at baseline, 6 weeks, 3 months.
- **Gingival / mediation**: gingival index and gingival symptoms collected for mediation analysis; exact index scale not reported.
- **Clinical success**: composite of good marginal adaptation, stable occlusion, no dislodgement or significant complication, satisfactory patient-reported outcome.
- **Bias control**: masticatory efficiency and VAS extracted from records by 2 independent investigators blinded to crown material, standardized extraction, inter-rater ICC = 0.89. Treating clinicians and patients were not blinded.
- **Statistics**: SPSS 26.0 + R 4.2.0; independent-samples t test; χ²/Fisher; repeated-measures ANOVA (time within, group between, time×group interaction, Greenhouse–Geisser if sphericity violated) with linear mixed-effects models (random intercept per individual) as robustness check; responder proportions with RR/OR + 95% CI; mediation by bias-corrected bootstrap (5000 resamples); two-sided α = 0.05; **no adjustment for multiple comparisons** (stated by the authors).
- **Sample size**: determined by eligible patients in the window; **post hoc** power calculation based on the 3-month CRP difference (MD 1.3 mg/L, SD 1.2 mg/L) gave 1−β = 0.93 for n = 158.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

**Author-stated:** 3-month follow-up only (no long-term stability or late complications); inflammatory markers influenced by baseline inflammatory status, oral hygiene, lifestyle, and residual confounding persists despite PSM; single-center, limited sample, generalizability needs multicenter/long-term validation; retrospective design limits prospective control of confounders; residual bias from unmeasured variables; **no adjustment for multiple comparisons**.

**Critical appraisal (added at ingest — the paper is observational, not an RCT):**
- **No randomization.** Material was chosen per patient; propensity score matching balances only measured covariates, and the matching implementation (algorithm, caliper, ratio, whether exact matching on categorical variables was used) is not reported. 79/79 retained from 102/94 means ~20% of each arm fell outside the matched set — selection on the matched subset is itself a bias source.
- **Material is confounded with procedure.** Zirconia crowns were scanned digitally, CAD/CAM milled and cemented with resin-modified glass ionomer; PFM crowns were made from silicone impressions with conventional casting and an unspecified cement. Any "material effect" bundles material + workflow + cement + possibly preparation design.
- **Outcome plausibility.** A single full crown (28/79 zirconia patients had an anterior restoration, which contributes little to chewing) lowering serum IL-6 by 5.4 pg/mL and CRP by 3.3 mg/L within 3 months, and raising masticatory efficiency from ~52% to ~79%, is far larger than the intervention can plausibly explain. Baseline CRP 6.8 mg/L and IL-6 12.6 pg/mL indicate a mildly inflamed population whose markers may regress with any successful treatment (regression to the mean, healing of the treated tooth/gingiva, seasonal or assay variation). Neither group's absolute values are adjusted for BMI, periodontal disease elsewhere, intercurrent infection, or medication.
- **Temporality of the mediation.** Mediator (gingival index change) and outcome (ΔCRP) are measured at the same visits; the mediation model assumes no unmeasured mediator–outcome confounding and correct temporal ordering — neither is demonstrable in a retrospective single-center cohort. The indirect effect (−0.37) is therefore an association decomposition, not an established mechanism.
- **Multiplicity.** Three biomarkers × three timepoints + two masticatory indices + responder analyses + four adverse-event comparisons + mediation were tested without correction. The P = .039 (clinical success) and P = .047 (gingival bleeding) results would not survive even a Bonferroni-style correction across the reported comparisons; the ANOVA main effects are more robust than the individual contrasts.
- **Post hoc power.** The power calculation uses the observed CRP difference — a circular, observed-power computation that cannot validate the study's ability to detect a clinically meaningful difference.
- **"Clinical success" at 3 months is not survival.** 93.7% vs 83.5% is a 3-month composite of fit, occlusion and patient satisfaction; it must not be read against 5-year survival/complication data for these materials.
- **Blinding is partial.** Only the two data extractors for masticatory efficiency/VAS were blinded (ICC 0.89); clinicians and patients knew the material, and VAS is a patient-reported outcome in an unblinded comparison, open to expectancy and cost/appearance effects.
- **Reporting gaps.** No PSM method/caliper, no PFM cement, no crown-per-patient breakdown beyond single/multiple, no gingival index scale, no ELISA kit/manufacturer, no test food or sieving protocol for masticatory efficiency, no ethics approval number, no definition of "long-term heavy smoking".
- **Text/data defect in the PMC version.** Table 1's periodontal-condition row for the zirconia group renders as "Nov-68" (control 66/13), so baseline periodontal balance cannot be verified from the available text despite χ² = 0.24, P = .63. Logged as `source-data-issue`.
- **Registry/transparency.** No trial or study registration, no STROBE checklist, no data-sharing beyond "available on reasonable request".

**Future work (author-stated):** multicenter, larger-sample, long-term studies.

## 7. Glossary

- **Masticatory efficiency (%)**: objective chewing performance, here measured by the color-mixing/sieving method — the share of a comminuted test sample passing successive sieves.
- **Chewing VAS**: 0–10 patient-rated chewing ability (0 = cannot chew, 10 = normal).
- **Propensity score matching (PSM)**: post-hoc balancing that pairs treated and untreated subjects with similar predicted probability of receiving the treatment, addressing measured confounders only.
- **IL-6 / TNF-α / CRP**: serum inflammatory markers; CRP is the acute-phase reactant most often used as a systemic inflammation readout.
- **Mediation analysis (indirect effect)**: decomposition of a total effect into a direct path and a path running through an intermediate variable (here Δ gingival index), estimated by bootstrap.
- **Responder analysis**: proportion of patients crossing a pre-specified clinically meaningful change threshold, reported as RR/OR with CI.
