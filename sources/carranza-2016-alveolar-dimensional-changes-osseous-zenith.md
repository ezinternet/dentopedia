## Three-line Summary

Prospective CT study of 20 patients after minimally traumatic single-rooted extraction with preserved walls and primary closure; CT within 24 h and at 6 months with a radiopaque-marker guide tracked the "osseous zenith" (bone crest immediately apical to the gingival zenith).

The osseous zenith displaced significantly (p<0.001): vertical resorption 2.1 ± 1.7 mm (median 1.9, range 0.2–7.5) and horizontal 1.8 ± 0.8 mm (median 1.7, range 0.6–4.4); crestal facial width did not correlate with displacement, but facial crest width <0.7 mm at the apicocoronal midpoint predicted >3 mm displacement.

Mid-level (not crestal) facial bone width may be the better CT predictor of zenith-supporting bone loss before implant planning — small sample, abstract-only.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Introduces "osseous zenith" as the bony landmark beneath the gingival zenith.
- Quantifies 6-month vertical and horizontal displacement of the osseous zenith after atraumatic extraction with primary closure.
- Identifies mid-level facial bone width (<0.7 mm) as a predictor of large (>3 mm) displacement; crestal width was not predictive.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 20 subjects; single-rooted teeth with preserved alveolar walls; minimally traumatic extraction; primary closure.
- CT at TC1 (<24 h) and TC2 (6 months); radiographic guide with radiopaque marker for reproducible reference.
- Correlation of displacement with alveolar anatomical variables.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Displacement (6 mo) | Mean ± SD | Median | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical | 2.1 ± 1.7 mm | 1.9 mm | 0.2–7.5 mm |
| Horizontal | 1.8 ± 0.8 mm | 1.7 mm | 0.6–4.4 mm |

- Both p<0.001.
- Facial crestal width: no correlation with displacement.
- Facial width at apicocoronal midpoint <0.7 mm → >3 mm displacement expected.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only; tooth types, correlation coefficients, and threshold derivation method not retrieved.
- n=20; wide ranges; threshold not externally validated.
- Primary closure (flap advancement) may itself affect remodeling; no ridge-preservation comparison arm.

## 7. Glossary

- **Osseous zenith**: the bone crest immediately apical to the gingival zenith (authors' term).
- **TC1/TC2**: baseline (<24 h) and 6-month CT scans.
