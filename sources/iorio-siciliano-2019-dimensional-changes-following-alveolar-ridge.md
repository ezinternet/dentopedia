## Why Ingested

Three-arm posterior-site RCT restricted to sockets with **intact alveolar bone walls**, directly testing whether alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) still helps once the usual indication (a compromised/dehisced wall) is absent. It found **no significant benefit** of either xenograft protocol over spontaneous healing — a finding in tension with [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/de-angelis-2022-ridge-preservation-spontaneous-healing-volumetric]] (retrospective, ARP significantly reduced resorption vs. spontaneous healing) and [[wiki/bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/kim-2024-collagenated-xenograft-arp-rct]] (RCT, similar collagen-enriched xenograft, found a late-phase volume benefit over spontaneous healing). This paper's intact-wall inclusion criterion is the plausible reason for the divergence, but it is flagged here as a genuine tension worth a second look rather than silently filed as routine reinforcement.

## Three-line Summary
Three-arm RCT (Naples, n=40 subjects/posterior teeth or roots candidate for extraction, sockets with intact alveolar bone walls) comparing bovine-derived xenograft with 10% collagen + collagen membrane (test) vs. bovine-derived xenograft particles + collagen membrane (control 1) vs. spontaneous healing / no grafting (control 2), 6-month follow-up.

All three groups showed statistically significant vertical and horizontal bone resorption from baseline to 6 months (p<0.001), except vertical resorption in the spontaneous-healing group (control 2), which was not significant; at 6 months, intergroup comparison showed no statistically significant difference among the three groups (p>0.05).

In posterior sites with intact bone walls and adequate buccal bone thickness, ridge preservation with either xenograft protocol failed to show a clinically relevant dimensional advantage over spontaneous healing — suggesting ARP's benefit may be conditional on wall/socket compromise rather than a universal posterior-site default.

## 세줄요약
3군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (나폴리, n=40명/구치부 발치 예정 치아 또는 치근, 치조골벽 (Alveolar Bone Wall) 완전한 발치와만 포함) — 소 유래 이종골 (Bovine-derived Xenograft) +10% 콜라겐+콜라겐막 (시험군) vs 소 유래 이종골 입자+콜라겐막 (대조군1) vs 자연치유/무이식 (대조군2) 비교, 6개월 추적.

전 군에서 기저치 대비 6개월 수직·수평 골흡수 (Bone Resorption)가 통계적으로 유의 (p<0.001), 단 대조군2(자연치유)의 수직 흡수만 유의하지 않음; 6개월 시점 군간 비교는 통계적으로 유의한 차이 없음 (p>0.05).

치조골벽이 온전하고 협측골 두께가 충분한 구치부에서는 두 이종골 프로토콜 모두 자연치유 대비 임상적으로 유의미한 차원 보존 이점을 보이지 못함 — 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP)의 이점이 구치부 전반의 기본값이 아니라 골벽 손상 여부에 조건부일 수 있음을 시사.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Isolates the ARP-vs-spontaneous-healing question specifically in posterior sockets with **intact alveolar bone walls**, a population usually pooled with compromised/dehisced sockets in prior ARP literature.
- Head-to-head comparison of two xenograft formulations (10%-collagen-enriched vs. plain particulate) against no grafting, rather than the more common single-graft-vs-control design.
- Reports the (unusual) within-group null result for vertical resorption in the no-graft control — most 6-month spontaneous-healing data elsewhere shows significant vertical loss.
- Provides clinical-relevance framing explicitly cautioning against routine ARP in posterior sites when wall integrity and buccal bone thickness are adequate.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: 3-arm parallel RCT.
- **n**: 40 subjects, 40 posterior teeth/roots candidate for extraction; per-arm breakdown not available in the retrieved abstract.
- **Inclusion**: posterior sites with integrity of alveolar bone walls (non-dehisced sockets).
- **Groups**: (1) test — bovine-derived xenograft with 10% collagen + collagen membrane; (2) control 1 — bovine-derived xenograft particles + collagen membrane; (3) control 2 — no grafting (spontaneous healing).
- **Outcomes**: vertical and horizontal bone dimensional change, baseline vs. 6 months.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Baseline→6-month vertical **and** horizontal resorption: significant in all 3 groups (p<0.001), **except** vertical resorption in control group 2 (spontaneous healing), which was not significant.
- 6-month intergroup comparison (test vs. control 1 vs. control 2): **not** statistically significant for alveolar bone dimensional change (p>0.05).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Abstract-only ingestion**: full methods, exact dimensional values (mm), per-arm sample sizes, tables, and the authors' own stated limitations/discussion were not retrieved (paywalled Springer article).
- Explicitly restricted to intact-wall sockets by design — findings should not be extrapolated to dehisced/compromised posterior sockets, which is the population where ARP is more conventionally indicated.
- Single-center (Naples) study; 6-month endpoint only, no implant-placement or long-term follow-up data in the retrieved content.

## 6. Related Work
- de-angelis-2022 (retrospective, spontaneous healing vs. ARP): found ARP significantly reduced horizontal/volumetric resorption — opposite direction from this RCT's null result; the intact-wall inclusion criterion here is the likely explanation, but flagged as a tension, not silently reconciled.
- kim-2024 (RCT, collagenated xenograft ARP vs. spontaneous healing): found a late-phase (84–180 day) volume-maintenance benefit for a similarly collagen-enriched xenograft — same evidence tier (RCT) as this paper, yet different conclusion; population wasn't restricted to fully intact walls (only >2-wall bone required).
- scheyer-2016 (RCT, posterior **dehisced** sockets, graft-vs-graft not vs. spontaneous healing): complementary population — this paper's intact-wall cohort and Scheyer's dehisced-wall cohort together bracket when posterior ARP/grafting does and doesn't add value.

## 7. Glossary
- **Alveolar Ridge Preservation (ARP)**: grafting an extraction socket at the time of tooth removal to limit post-extraction ridge resorption, typically to preserve future implant-site bone volume.
- **Bovine-derived xenograft**: deproteinized bovine bone mineral (DBBM) used as a slowly-resorbing osteoconductive scaffold; here tested both plain and as a 10%-collagen-enriched formulation.
- **Integrity of alveolar bone walls**: inclusion criterion requiring an intact (non-dehisced, adequately thick) socket wall at baseline — the opposite of the compromised-wall sockets more commonly studied in ARP trials.
- **Spontaneous healing**: extraction with no graft material or membrane placed; the socket heals by unassisted blood-clot organization (control group 2 here).
- **Vertical / horizontal bone resorption**: postextraction dimensional loss measured as ridge height (vertical) or width (horizontal) change between baseline and follow-up.
