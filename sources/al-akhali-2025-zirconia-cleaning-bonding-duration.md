## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (140 zirconia discs, 5 cleaning groups) evaluating surface free energy and tensile bond strength after a 72-hour delay between airborne-particle abrasion and bonding, simulating clinical delay in cementation.

Ivoclean produced the highest surface free energy (68.4 mN/m) followed by alcohol (61.8 mN/m); Katana Cleaner reduced SFE; no significant TBS difference at 3 days, but at 150 days/37,500 thermocycles, cleaning method significantly affected bonding durability.

Katana Cleaner is contra-indicated after a 72-hour delay; Ivoclean or alcohol are the superior cleaning choices to maintain durable zirconia bonding after delayed cementation.

## 세줄요약

체외실험 (지르코니아 디스크 140개, 청소법 5군) — 샌드블라스팅(Air-Particle Abrasion, APA) 후 72시간 지연 시 세정 방법이 표면자유에너지(Surface Free Energy, SFE)와 인장결합강도(Tensile Bond Strength, TBS)에 미치는 영향 평가.

Ivoclean SFE 68.4 mN/m으로 최고, 알코올 61.8 mN/m 순; Katana Cleaner는 SFE 감소; 3일 TBS는 군 간 유의차 없으나, 150일/37,500 열순환 후에는 세정법이 내구성에 유의한 영향.

72시간 지연 후 Katana Cleaner는 금기; 지르코니아 지연 접착 시 내구성 있는 결합을 위해 Ivoclean 또는 알코올 세정을 선택해야 함.

## 1. Key Results
- 140 zirconia discs; 72h delay between APA and bonding (simulates clinical delay)
- 5 cleaning groups: alcohol, Ivoclean, Katana Cleaner, Clearfil directly after APA, Clearfil after 72h delay
- SFE: Ivoclean (68.4) > alcohol (61.8) >> Katana Cleaner (33.1) (P≤0.05)
- TBS 3 days: 60.7 MPa (alcohol) to 51.6 MPa (Clearfil P2) — no significant difference
- 150 days / 37,500 thermocycles: cleaning method significantly affects durability
- Katana Cleaner: reduces SFE — contra-indicated for this application
