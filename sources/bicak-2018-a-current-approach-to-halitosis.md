## Why Ingested

Comprehensive mini-review synthesizing classification, aetiology, detection methods, and management of halitosis for a dental primary-care audience — broadens the detection-method and classification coverage started in [[halitosis/silva-2017-estimated-prevalence-halitosis-sr]] and complements [[halitosis/memon-2022-aetiology-associations-halitosis-systematic]].

## Three-line Summary

Narrative mini-review (54 articles) covering halitosis classification, VSC-based aetiology, direct and indirect detection techniques, and conventional vs novel treatment approaches, concluding that conventional methods remain more effective.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Provides a structured two-tier classification: **delusional** (pseudohalitosis, halitophobia) vs **genuine** (physiologic, pathologic-oral, pathologic-extraoral).
2. Enumerates all major VSC species and local oral factors driving their production (saliva flow, O₂ concentration, bacterial metabolism).
3. Catalogues nine distinct halitosis detection/measurement techniques from organoleptic scoring to PCR, with practical notes on each.
4. Summarises both conventional and novel (Melaleuca oil, champignon extract, probiotics, green tea) treatment strategies.
5. Delineates the dentist's referral pathway when oral causes have been excluded.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Expanded literature search: PubMed/Medline, Scopus, Google Scholar, TÜBİTAK ULAKBİM databases.
- Hand search of references performed in addition to database searching.
- Inclusion criteria: peer-reviewed full-text articles in Turkish or English on halitosis/oral malodor/VSC.
- Exclusion: abstracts, editorial letters, non-dental articles, duplicates, non-English/Turkish language articles.
- Final corpus: 54 full-text articles reviewed narratively; no meta-analytic pooling.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Domain | Finding |
|---|---|
| Prevalence | Up to 50% (varies by assessment method) |
| Oral origin | 85% of patients with malodor have oral-cavity bacterial source |
| Main VSCs | Hydrogen sulfide (H₂S), methyl mercaptan (CH₃SH), dimethyl sulfide ((CH₃)₂S) |
| Tongue biofilm | Posterior dorsum is primary malodor source in periodontally healthy individuals |
| Periodontal pocket | Ideal anaerobic environment for VSC production; VSCs also accelerate tissue destruction |
| H. pylori | Mixed evidence — BANA-positive rates higher in H. pylori oral carriers; eradication may help |
| SRP + GPAP | Glycine powder air-polishing adds no benefit over SRP alone for VSC reduction (Caygur et al.) |
| Conventional methods | Judged more effective and widely used than novel alternatives |

**Detection techniques ranked by complexity**: organoleptic (0–5 scale) → Halimeter (portable sulfide monitor) → gas chromatography (OralChroma portable variant) → BANA test → chemical sensor probes → electronic nose → beta-galactosidase paper disc → salivary incubation test → ammonia monitoring → ninhydrin colorimetry → PCR (quantitative microbiome).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-author literature review; no PRISMA flow chart or quality-of-evidence rating.
- Turkish + English language restriction introduces selection bias.
- No pooled quantitative analysis; treatment comparisons are narrative only.
- Novel agents (Melaleuca oil, champignon extract, green tea) reviewed from limited studies — effect sizes not synthesised.
- Authors note conventional methods outperform novel ones, but do not provide head-to-head data.

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| VSC (Volatile Sulfur Compound) | H₂S, CH₃SH, (CH₃)₂S — primary odorant gases in halitosis |
| Pseudohalitosis | Patient perceives malodor not objectively detectable by others |
| Halitophobia | Persistent belief of malodor despite treatment; psychological origin |
| Physiologic halitosis | Transient morning breath from nocturnal hyposalivation; no pathology |
| BANA test | Detects T. denticola, P. gingivalis, T. forsythia via trypsin-substrate colour change |
| Halimeter | Commercial portable sulfide monitor (Interscan Corp.) measuring VSC in ppb |
| OralChroma | Portable gas chromatography device distinguishing individual VSC species |
| Organoleptic measurement | Clinician's direct olfactory assessment; scored 0–5 |
| SRP | Scaling and Root Planing — first-line periodontal treatment |
| GPAP | Glycine Powder Air-Polishing — adjunct to SRP; no additional VSC benefit shown |
| Trimethylaminuria | Metabolic disorder causing fish-odour halitosis via trimethylamine accumulation |
