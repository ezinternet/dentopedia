## Why Ingested

기존 위키에 개별 SR/SR+MA로 보유 중인 [[implants/vertical-ridge-augmentation/sabri-2024-titanium-mesh-bone-augmentation-sr-ma]] (pooled vertical 3.36mm) 및 [[implants/vertical-ridge-augmentation/abu-mostafa-2022-titanium-mesh-vertical-augmentation-sr]]를 포함한 티타늄 메시(Titanium Mesh, TM) 계열 SR 8편을 AMSTAR 2 + GRADE로 메타평가하는 우산 리뷰(umbrella review)라 근거 위계의 최상위 층을 채운다. 개별 SR들이 종종 "critically low"/"low" 품질임을 정량적으로 드러내고, 리뷰 간 1차 연구 중복(overlap)으로 풀링 정밀도가 제한된다는 방법론적 경고를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (8 systematic reviews, incl. 2 with meta-analysis; 51 index studies, 20 RCTs) appraising titanium mesh (Ti mesh) for alveolar ridge augmentation using AMSTAR 2 and GRADE.

Weighted mean vertical bone gain 4.05 mm and horizontal bone gain 3.96 mm across reviews; weighted mean mesh exposure rate 24.7% (range 16%–35%); GRADE certainty moderate for bone gain, low–moderate for mesh exposure, low for implant survival/success; the single high-quality meta-analysis (Sabri 2024) reported lower bone gain than the cross-review weighted mean.

Most included SRs were AMSTAR 2 "critically low" or "low" quality and shared overlapping primary studies, which limits the precision of pooled estimates; clinically, TM remains effective for 3D bone reconstruction but exposure risk (~1 in 4) demands advanced surgical skill, and PROMs remain an evidence gap.

## 세줄요약

우산 리뷰(umbrella review, 체계적 문헌고찰 8편 — 그중 메타분석 2편 포함; 색인 연구 51편, RCT 20편)로 AMSTAR 2 및 GRADE를 이용해 티타늄 메시(Titanium Mesh, TM)의 치조제 골증대 근거를 평가.

리뷰 간 가중평균 수직 골증대 4.05mm, 수평 골증대 3.96mm; 가중평균 메시 노출률 24.7%(범위 16–35%); GRADE 확실성은 골증대에서 moderate, 메시 노출은 low-moderate, 임플란트 생존·성공은 low — 유일한 고품질 메타분석(Sabri 2024)은 리뷰 간 가중평균보다 낮은 골증대치를 보고.

포함된 SR 대부분이 AMSTAR 2 "critically low" 또는 "low" 등급이었고 1차 연구가 리뷰 간 중복되어 풀링 정밀도가 제한됨; 임상적으로 TM은 3차원 골재건에 효과적이나 노출 위험(~4명 중 1명)이 높아 숙련된 술기가 요구되며, 환자보고결과(Patient-Reported Outcome Measures, PROMs)는 여전히 근거 공백.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First umbrella review to formally appraise the quality of individual systematic reviews (AMSTAR 2) and the certainty of evidence (GRADE) specifically for titanium mesh in oral bone reconstruction.
- Quantifies weighted mean vertical (4.05 mm) and horizontal (3.96 mm) bone gain, and mesh exposure (24.7%) across 8 pooled/overlapping SRs, with an explicit citation-matrix overlap analysis (51 index studies).
- Documents that customized (CAD/CAM) TM does not clearly reduce exposure risk versus conventional TM — exposure rates for customized meshes reported elsewhere (25%–31%) are comparable to conventional TM in this review.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Umbrella review (overview of systematic reviews), Cochrane overview-of-reviews structure, PRISMA-adjacent reporting.
- **Databases**: PubMed/Medline, Ovid (Embase), Cochrane Library, Scopus, Web of Science; search Oct 26 2024, updated Jun 30 2025.
- **n**: 8 included systematic reviews (2 with meta-analysis) covering 51 unique index/primary studies (20 RCTs).
- **Quality tools**: AMSTAR 2 (SR methodological quality), GRADE (certainty of evidence), citation-matrix/node-link graph for primary-study overlap between reviews.
- **Outcomes**: Primary — vertical and/or horizontal absolute bone gain. Secondary — implant survival/success, mesh exposure, premature mesh removal, implant placement feasibility, PROMs, peri-implant disease incidence, histology.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result | Supporting reviews | GRADE |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical bone gain | Weighted mean 4.05 mm (unweighted 4.2 mm) | 4 reviews (2 SR + 2 MA) | Moderate |
| Horizontal bone gain | Weighted/unweighted mean ~4.0 mm | 3 reviews (2 SR + 1 MA) | Moderate |
| Mesh exposure | Weighted mean 24.7% (range 16%–35%; unweighted 24.3%) | 7 reviews | Low–moderate |
| Premature mesh removal | 20%–23% (2–3 primary studies) | 2 reviews | — |
| Implant placement feasibility | Usually preserved despite exposure | 6 reviews | Low |
| Implant survival | 90%–100% (6–96 months) | 5 reviews | Low |
| Implant success (Albrektsson) | 85%–100% (12–96 months) | 3 reviews | Low |
| Histology | No significant difference vs d-PTFE/resorbable membranes | 2 reviews | Low–moderate |

AMSTAR 2 ratings for included SRs skewed "critically low"/"low"; more recent SRs (last 5 years) showed better methodological rigor (protocol registration, PRISMA adherence). No data were available for PROMs, peri-implant disease incidence, need for soft-tissue augmentation, or aesthetic parameters across any included review.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Validity is bounded by the quality of the underlying primary SRs — most were AMSTAR 2 critically low/low, and umbrella-level flaws compound primary-level bias.
- Substantial overlap of primary studies across the 8 SRs (citation-matrix analysis) limits the precision/independence of pooled weighted means.
- Heterogeneous outcome reporting (anatomical site, defect severity often not specified) precluded reliable indirect comparisons between TM sub-techniques or against alternative GBR approaches.
- GRADE assessment at the overview-of-reviews level required ad hoc adaptation since key primary-level data needed for standard GRADE were often missing/underreported.
- No PROMs data available in any included review — flagged as the priority gap for future primary research.

## 6. Related Work
- Sabri 2024 (SR+MA, included in this umbrella): highest-quality meta-analysis of the 8; reported vertical bone gain (3.36 mm pooled) lower than this review's cross-review weighted mean (4.05 mm), consistent with the paper's own observation that its single high-quality review underestimates relative to the aggregate.
- Abu-Mostafa 2022 (SR, included in this umbrella): vertical-augmentation-focused SR also synthesized here.
- Tay 2022 (SR+MA, cited but not itself an included review): reported mesh/membrane exposure associated with 35% decrease in vertical bone gain, cited as corroborating evidence for the exposure–bone-gain relationship discussed here.

## 7. Glossary
- **Umbrella review**: A systematic review of systematic reviews ("overview of reviews"), synthesizing evidence at one level above individual SRs.
- **AMSTAR 2**: A critical appraisal tool rating the methodological quality of systematic reviews (rating categories: high, moderate, low, critically low).
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation — framework rating certainty of evidence (high/moderate/low/very low).
- **TM (titanium mesh)**: A non-resorbable, form-stable barrier device used in guided bone regeneration (GBR) to maintain space for bone regrowth.
- **Citation-matrix/overlap analysis**: A method for quantifying how many primary studies are shared between multiple systematic reviews, used to assess double-counting risk in umbrella reviews.
