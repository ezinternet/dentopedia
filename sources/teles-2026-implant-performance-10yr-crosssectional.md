## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional study of 35 patients (133 implants, mean 13.2 yr in function) in a private clinic in Brazil assessing prevalence of peri-implant diseases and risk/protective factors.

Implant survival was 95.5%; peri-implant mucositis 31.4% and peri-implantitis 31.4% (patient level); bruxism, osteoporosis, smoking, high plaque index, and insufficient keratinized mucosa were associated with peri-implant diseases; external hexagon connection, history of periodontitis, and immediate loading were linked to early mechanical complications.

Abstract-only study; single private clinic limits generalizability; no comparison group; cross-sectional design cannot establish causality.

## 세줄요약

브라질 단일 클리닉에서 35명(133개 임플란트, 평균 13.2년 기능)을 대상으로 한 단면 연구로 주위 조직 질환 유병률과 위험·보호 인자를 평가함.

임플란트 생존율 95.5%; 환자 수준 임플란트 주위 점막염(Peri-implant Mucositis, PIM) 31.4%·임플란트 주위염(Peri-implantitis, PI) 31.4%; 브루시즘(Bruxism), 골다공증(Osteoporosis), 흡연, 높은 플라크 지수, 각화 점막 부족이 주위 조직 질환과 연관됨; 외부 육각 연결(External Hexagon Connection), 치주 질환 과거력, 즉시 부하(Immediate Loading, IL)는 초기 기계적 합병증과 연관됨.

단일 클리닉 초록 전용 연구로 인과관계 확립 불가; 일반화에 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Long-term (≥10 year) real-world implant data from a private clinic setting
- Bruxism associated with both peri-implant disease AND early mechanical complications — dual-axis risk
- Osteoporosis linked to greater marginal bone loss (MBL), especially in patients with prosthetic complications
- Keratinized mucosa (KM) insufficiency identified as a modifiable biological risk factor
- External hexagon connection associated with early mechanical complications (versus internal connection)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Cross-sectional, single private clinic, Brazil
- **Inclusion**: Patients with implant-supported prostheses functioning ≥10 years with complete clinical and radiographic records
- **Sample**: 35 patients, 133 implants; mean functional time 13.2 years
- **Clinical parameters**: Plaque index (modified plaque index, mpI), bleeding on probing (BOP), suppuration (SUPP), probing depth (PD), keratinized mucosa (KM), peri-implant soft tissue dehiscence (PISTD), radiographic marginal bone loss (MBL)
- **Covariates**: Systemic health (including osteoporosis, diabetes), implant features (connection type, loading protocol), prosthetic features, patient habits (smoking, bruxism)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

*(abstract-only — numbers derived from abstract text only)*

- Implant survival: **95.5%**
- Peri-implant mucositis (patient level): **31.4%**; implant level: **32.3%**
- Peri-implantitis (patient level): **31.4%**; implant level: **12.1%**
- Risk factors for peri-implant diseases: insufficient KM, high mpI, smoking, bruxism, osteoporosis
- Risk factors for early mechanical complications: bruxism, external hexagon connection, history of periodontitis, immediate loading
- Prosthetic complications: mainly mild, did not compromise survival; linked to greater bone loss especially with osteoporosis

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only — full quantitative data (ORs, CIs, p-values) not available
- Single private clinic; highly selected patient population with complete records
- Cross-sectional design: cannot determine temporal sequence or causality
- Small sample (n=35 patients); insufficient power for multivariable modeling of rare outcomes
- No control group; no standardized diagnosis threshold stated in abstract

## 7. Glossary

- **BOP** (Bleeding on Probing, 탐침 출혈): peri-implant inflammation indicator
- **KM** (Keratinized Mucosa, 각화 점막): protective band around implant
- **MBL** (Marginal Bone Loss, 변연 골소실): radiographic bone level change
- **mpI** (Modified Plaque Index, 수정 플라크 지수): clinical hygiene measure
- **PISTD** (Peri-Implant Soft Tissue Dehiscence, 임플란트 주위 연조직 열개)
- **PD** (Probing Depth, 탐침 깊이)
