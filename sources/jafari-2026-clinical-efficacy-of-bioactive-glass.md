## Why Ingested

The wiki held bioactive glass (BG) only as a *material class* inside narrative reviews — [[wiki/bone-regeneration/haugen-2026-bone-grafts-everything-you-need]] lists it among alloplasts on the osteogenesis/osteoinduction/osteoconduction triad and [[wiki/bone-regeneration/zhao-2021-bone-grafts-substitutes-dentistry-review]] files it in the synthetic/bioceramic family — with no page quantifying its clinical effect in a periodontal indication. This SR+MA supplies the first pooled clinical-efficacy estimate for BG as a standalone periodontal-regeneration intervention held in this wiki, and it fills the "alloplast × indication" cell next to [[wiki/periodontics/jasser-2021-effectiveness-beta-tricalcium-phosphate-comparison]] (β-TCP in periodontal infra-bony defects) and the modality-level ranking in [[wiki/periodontics/host-modulation/stavropoulos-2021-periodontal-regeneration-intrabony-nma]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (SR+MA; Odontology 2026; Embase/PubMed/Cochrane Library searched to April 2025) of randomized controlled trials (RCTs) assessing bioactive glass (BG) as a standalone intervention for periodontal regeneration; 12 RCTs, 234 patients, 406 periodontal defects, Cochrane standards, random-effects pooling in RevMan v5.4.

BG significantly improved probing depth (PD) reduction (MD = 1.27 mm; p = 0.0006) and clinical attachment level (CAL) gain (MD = 1.49 mm; p = 0.0005), with moderate-to-high heterogeneity between studies and no significant publication bias.

BG is a credible osteoconductive regenerative adjunct for periodontal defects, but the pooled estimates rest on 12 small RCTs with moderate-to-high heterogeneity, and this abstract-only ingest cannot verify risk of bias, comparator arms, follow-up timepoints or subgroup structure.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Pooled clinical-efficacy estimate for bioactive glass (BG) used as a **standalone** periodontal-regeneration intervention — the material-specific counterpart to the wiki's modality-level NMA and to the β-TCP alloplast review.
- Reports both standard periodontal regeneration endpoints (PD reduction, CAL gain) as absolute mean differences in millimetres, so the effect can be placed directly against the grafting-vs-debridement range in [[wiki/periodontics/host-modulation/stavropoulos-2021-periodontal-regeneration-intrabony-nma]].
- Reports heterogeneity and publication-bias testing together with the pooled estimates, allowing the reader to calibrate the pooled numbers.
- Frames the material as *bioactive and osteoconductive* — consistent with the alloplast classification in [[wiki/bone-regeneration/haugen-2026-bone-grafts-everything-you-need]] (osteoconductive ✅, osteoinduction only via surface ion release, no osteogenesis).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: systematic review + meta-analysis, following Cochrane Collaboration standards.
- **Databases / date**: Embase, PubMed and Cochrane Library; search up to April 2025. (No language or date restriction reported in the abstract.)
- **Eligibility**: randomized controlled trials (RCTs) assessing BG as a standalone intervention for periodontal regeneration; studies reporting PD and/or CAL outcomes.
- **Review process**: two reviewers independently extracted data and assessed risk of bias.
- **Analysis**: random-effects meta-analysis in RevMan (Review Manager) v5.4, chosen to address between-study heterogeneity.
- **n**: 12 RCTs; 234 patients; 406 periodontal defects.
- **Outcomes**: probing depth (PD) reduction and clinical attachment level (CAL) gain.

**Authors' conclusion (as reported):** BG significantly enhances clinical outcomes in periodontal regeneration; despite variability among studies, it demonstrates strong potential as an effective regenerative material, warranting further high-quality trials.

**Background framing from the abstract:** periodontal disease causes progressive destruction of tooth-supporting tissues; conventional therapy such as open flap debridement (OFD) controls inflammation but provides limited regeneration; BG is a bioactive, osteoconductive material that has emerged as a promising regenerative agent in periodontal therapy.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Abstract-only ingest** — risk-of-bias judgements, forest plots, heterogeneity values, subgroup structure, comparator definitions, follow-up times and adverse events are unavailable; any such claim must be read from the full text before clinical use.
- **Moderate-to-high heterogeneity** across the 12 pooled RCTs means the MD values are averages over materially different protocols and defect morphologies, not a single reproducible effect.
- **Small evidence base** — 12 RCTs, 234 patients, 406 defects; individual trials are correspondingly small.
- **Authors' own call**: further high-quality RCTs are needed before BG can be treated as an established regenerative material.
- No radiographic bone-level outcome, tooth-survival outcome, cost or morbidity endpoint is mentioned in the abstract; their absence there is not evidence of their absence in the full text.
- **Ingest-level caveat**: because the full text was not retrievable (closed access), this page cannot support any statement about subgroup behaviour, long-term stability, or how BG compares head-to-head with EMD, PRF or other graft materials.

## 7. Glossary
- **Bioactive glass (BG)** — synthetic silicate/borate/phosphate glass used as a bone-graft substitute (alloplast); the abstract describes it as a bioactive, osteoconductive material ([[wiki/bone-regeneration/haugen-2026-bone-grafts-everything-you-need]]).
- **Osteoconductive (골전도성)** — the material acts as a scaffold that host bone grows onto; no osteogenic cells and no intrinsic osteoinductive signal.
- **PD (probing depth, 치주낭 깊이)** — distance from the gingival margin to the base of the periodontal pocket; the outcome here is *PD reduction* after treatment.
- **CAL (clinical attachment level, 임상 부착 수준)** — distance from a fixed CEJ reference to the base of the pocket; the outcome here is *CAL gain* after treatment.
- **MD (mean difference, 평균차)** — pooled absolute difference between study arms, expressed in millimetres in this review.
- **Heterogeneity (이질성)** — variation in effect size between studies; reported here as moderate-to-high (no I² value in the abstract).
- **Publication bias (발간 편향)** — small-study/selective-reporting bias; reported here as not significant.
- **Random-effects model** — pooling model that weights studies by a between-study variance term; used here "to address study heterogeneity".
- **RevMan (Review Manager) v5.4** — Cochrane's meta-analysis software, the pooling tool used in this review.
- **Open flap debridement (OFD, 변연절제술)** — conventional periodontal surgery named in the abstract as controlling inflammation but providing limited regeneration.
