## Why Ingested

Speed sintering is clinically critical for same-day zirconia delivery. This in-vitro study tests three zirconia materials (3Y-TZP, 4Y-PSZ, 5Y-PSZ) across speed vs. conventional sintering × glazed vs. polished, measuring flexural strength, translucency, XRD phase fraction, SEM grain size, and AFM surface roughness before and after hydrothermal aging.

## Three-line Summary

Speed sintering did not significantly affect flexural strength (p = 0.245) or translucency (p = 0.478) of monolithic zirconia with varying yttria content across three commercially available materials (Katana UTML 5Y-PSZ, Katana STML 4Y-PSZ, DD Cube Bio ZX2 3Y-TZP). Speed sintering reduced average grain size in all materials and produced a smoother surface topography in 3Y-TZP, while glazed groups showed smoother surfaces than polished groups in 4Y-PSZ and 5Y-PSZ after aging. Higher yttria content increased translucency, cubic phase, and grain size, while reducing flexural strength (DD Cube Bio ZX2: ~891–1081 MPa vs. Katana UTML: ~508–568 MPa) and monoclinic phase formation after aging.

## 세줄요약

소결 속도는 단일체 지르코니아의 굴곡강도(p=0.245)나 반투명도(p=0.478)에 유의한 영향을 미치지 않았다. 고속 소결은 모든 재료에서 평균 입자 크기를 감소시켰으며, 3Y-TZP에서는 더 균일한 표면을 형성했다. 이트리아 함량이 높을수록 반투명도와 큐빅상 비율이 증가하고 굴곡강도와 단사정상 형성은 감소하였다.

## Document Information

- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health (2025)
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s12903-025-07225-2
- **PMID**: 41402817 | **PMCID**: PMC12709688
- **Study type**: In vitro laboratory study
- **Materials tested**: Katana UTML (5Y-PSZ), Katana STML (4Y-PSZ), DD Cube Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP)
- **Sample size**: 180 specimens (12×12×1.2 mm square prisms, n=15 per group)
- **Sintering protocols**: Speed sintering furnace (In Fire HTC speed; Dentsply Sirona) vs. conventional
- **Surface treatments**: Grinding + polishing (EVE Diacera RA 322) vs. glazing (Ivoclar Vivadent)
- **Aging**: Hydrothermal aging at 134°C, 0.2 MPa, 5h

## Key Contributions

1. Confirms clinical viability of speed sintering for all three zirconia types: no significant effect on flexural strength or translucency
2. Documents speed sintering effect on grain size reduction across all materials (SEM-confirmed)
3. Reveals material-dependent monoclinic phase response: 5Y-PSZ fully stable, 4Y-PSZ develops monoclinic only post-aging in speed+polished group (8.9% vs. 4% conventional), 3Y-TZP shows monoclinic before and after aging
4. AFM analysis quantifies surface quality differences: glazed groups smoother than polished in 4Y-PSZ and 5Y-PSZ; speed sintered 3Y-TZP produces more homogeneous surface than conventional
5. Validates that polishing kits designed for zirconia can be used after speed sintering

## Methodology

- 180 specimens distributed across 12 groups (3 materials × 2 sintering protocols × 2 surface treatments)
- Translucency parameter (TP): digital spectrophotometer (Vita Easyshade V) on black and white backgrounds, formula: TP = [(L_B−L_W)² + (a_B−a_W)² + (b_B−b_W)²]^½
- Flexural strength: piston-on-3-ball (P3B) test per ISO 6872 (Instron 4467, 1 mm/min)
- Phase analysis: XRD (Empyrean, CuKα, 2θ 5–90°), Rietveld refinement (TOPAS V3.0)
- Microstructure: FE-SEM (Merlin, Carl Zeiss) ×10000 magnification with thermal etching at 1200°C, 20 min
- Surface topography: AFM (Hitachi 5100N, tapping mode, 20 µm² area)
- Statistics: 3-way ANOVA, 1-way ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, Bonferroni/Tukey post-hoc

## Key Results

**Flexural strength (MPa, after aging)**:
- DD Cube Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP): 891–1081 MPa (highest)
- Katana STML (4Y-PSZ): 660–707 MPa
- Katana UTML (5Y-PSZ): 508–568 MPa
- Speed sintering vs. conventional: no significant difference within any material (p > 0.05)
- Surface treatment: no significant effect (p = 0.189)

**Translucency**: Only material type significantly affected ΔTP (p < 0.05); sintering speed, surface treatment, and aging did not (p > 0.05). Ranking: Katana UTML > Katana STML > DD Cube Bio ZX2

**Phase transformation (XRD)**:
- Katana UTML (5Y-PSZ): 0% monoclinic before and after aging regardless of sintering/finishing
- Katana STML (4Y-PSZ): 0% monoclinic before aging; 8.9% monoclinic in speed+polished vs. 4% in conventional+polished after aging
- DD Cube Bio ZX2 (3Y-TZP): 5–15.8% monoclinic before aging; 16–19.8% monoclinic after aging

**Grain size (SEM, after aging)**:
- Katana UTML: 1.6 µm (speed) vs. 1.95 µm (conventional)
- Katana STML: 1.04 µm (speed) vs. 1.15 µm (conventional)
- DD Cube Bio ZX2: 0.5 µm (speed) vs. 0.78 µm (conventional)

## Limitations

- Only three materials tested; single color shade (A2)
- Square prism specimens do not replicate complex crown geometry
- Short autoclave aging (5h, 134°C) cannot fully simulate intraoral conditions
- Single polishing system used for all specimens
- Multilayer zirconia not evaluated

## Glossary

- **Speed sintering**: Accelerated sintering using induction-heated furnace; completes in ~30–60 min vs. 6–8h conventional
- **LTD (Low-Temperature Degradation)**: Hydrothermal aging causing t→m phase transformation; reduces flexural strength
- **t→m transformation**: Tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation; indicates LTD susceptibility
- **TP (Translucency Parameter)**: CIE L*a*b* colorimetric index of light transmission
- **XRD**: X-ray diffraction for phase composition quantification
- **AFM**: Atomic force microscopy for surface roughness/topography
- **FE-SEM**: Field emission scanning electron microscopy for grain size measurement
- **Rietveld refinement**: Quantitative phase analysis method from XRD patterns
