## Three-line Summary

A within-patient clinical study compared trueness and chairside adjustment of DLP-printed zirconia crowns at three build angles (0°, 45°, 90°) versus conventionally milled crowns across 10 patients (40 crowns total). Crowns printed at 0° showed the best trueness (RMS 49.0 µm) and required the least occlusal (25.7 µm) and intaglio (20.6 µm) adjustment; milled crowns required the most adjustment, particularly at the intaglio surface (56.5 µm). Although all deviations fell within accepted clinical tolerances, build angle is a critical process parameter for optimizing DLP zirconia restoration fit and minimizing chairside adjustment burden.

## 세줄요약

DLP 프린팅 지르코니아 크라운을 0°·45°·90° 빌드 앵글과 밀링 방식으로 제작해 환자 내 비교(10명, 40크라운)한 임상연구. 0° 빌드 앵글이 적합도 (RMS 49.0 µm)와 교합면(25.7 µm)·인타글리오(20.6 µm) 조정량 모두 최소였으며, 밀링 크라운이 인타글리오 조정량 가장 큼(56.5 µm). 모든 편차가 임상 허용범위 내였으나, DLP 지르코니아에서 빌드 앵글 최적화(0°)가 의장 조정 부담 감소와 워크플로 효율 향상에 핵심 인자.

## Key Contributions

1. **Build angle is the dominant process variable for DLP zirconia trueness**: 0° orientation yielded the best trueness (RMS 49.0 µm), significantly better than 45° (63.3 µm), 90° (70.5 µm), and milled (66.2 µm); differences among 45°, 90°, and milled groups were non-significant.
2. **DLP universally outperforms milling for intaglio adaptation**: All DLP groups (regardless of angle) required significantly less intaglio adjustment (20.6–25.4 µm) than milled crowns (56.5 µm), reflecting geometric limitations of subtractive manufacturing (bur diameter, tool-path constraints).
3. **Milled crowns require the most occlusal adjustment**: Milled group needed 63.8 µm occlusal RMS adjustment vs 25.7 µm for 0° DLP; clinically meaningful difference (mean Δ 38.1 µm, p=0.011).
4. **0° and 45° are clinically equivalent by equivalence testing**: Two one-sided tests confirmed equivalence within ±30 µm and ±50 µm (both p<0.001), supporting 45° as an acceptable alternative when anatomical constraints or support structure placement require it.
5. **Within-patient design improves precision**: Linear mixed-effects models with patient as random intercept controlled for inter-individual variation; ICC 0.96–0.99 confirmed high scanning repeatability.

## Methodology

- **Design**: Prospective, within-patient (crossover-type); each patient = 1 milled + 3 DLP crowns (0°/45°/90°) → 40 total
- **Fabrication**: DLP-printed pre-sintered zirconia green bodies at three build angles; conventionally CAD/CAM-milled zirconia (control)
- **Trueness measurement**: RMS surface deviation of intraoral scan of cemented crown vs original CAD design file (3D superimposition)
- **Adjustment measurement**: RMS deviation between pre-adjustment and post-adjustment intraoral scans (quantifies material removed chairside)
- **Statistics**: Linear mixed-effects models (fixed effect: manufacturing method; random intercept: patient); Tukey-adjusted pairwise post-hoc contrasts; sensitivity with one-way repeated-measures ANOVA; prespecified equivalence testing (±30 µm and ±50 µm margins)
- **Repeatability**: ICC [2,1] 0.96–0.99 for all outcome measures

## Key Results

| Group | Trueness RMS (µm) | Occlusal Adj RMS (µm) | Intaglio Adj RMS (µm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| DLP 0° | 49.0 ± 11.6 | 25.7 ± 7.8 | 20.6 ± 9.8 |
| DLP 45° | 63.3 ± 11.4 | — | 25.4 ± 10.7 |
| DLP 90° | 70.5 ± 11.5 | — | 21.2 ± 7.0 |
| Milled | 66.2 ± 13.4 | 63.8 ± 15.0 | 56.5 ± 26.2 |

- Overall method effect: trueness χ²=20.689 p<0.001; occlusal χ²=13.904 p=0.003; intaglio χ²=28.624 p<0.001
- 0° significantly better trueness than 45° (Δ14.2 µm, p=0.033), 90° (Δ21.5 µm, p=0.002), milled (Δ17.2 µm, p=0.012)
- Milled significantly worse intaglio than all DLP groups (Δ31.1–35.9 µm, all p≤0.004)
- All deviations within accepted clinical tolerance range for single-unit fixed restorations

## Limitations

- Small sample (n=10; restricted to single-unit molar crowns)
- No long-term clinical follow-up (delivery outcomes only)
- Mechanical properties, fracture behavior, and anisotropic microstructure effects not evaluated
- Grinding-induced phase transformation and antagonist enamel wear not assessed
- Single DLP system/material — findings may not generalize to other systems

## Related Work

- Previous studies on nanoparticle jetting and vat-polymerization techniques also reported superior accuracy at 0° orientation
- Mou et al. reported marginal accuracy deterioration at steeper build angles in DLP-printed zirconia crowns
- Revilla-León et al. demonstrated higher trueness/precision for 0°-oriented crowns using tilting stereolithography

## Glossary

- **Trueness (적합도, 정확도)**: Agreement between fabricated object and original CAD design; measured as RMS surface deviation
- **DLP (디지털 광조형, Digital Light Processing)**: Additive manufacturing using UV projection to photopolymerize layers of ceramic-resin slurry
- **RMS (평균제곱근, Root Mean Square)**: Statistical measure of overall surface deviation magnitude
- **Intaglio surface (인타글리오 면)**: Inner (tissue-facing) surface of a crown restoration
- **Build angle (빌드 앵글)**: Orientation of the restoration relative to the print platform during additive manufacturing
- **ICC (급내상관계수, Intraclass Correlation Coefficient)**: Measure of measurement reliability/repeatability
