## Why Ingested

PRISMA-compliant SR (13 human clinical studies) evaluating Ti vs ZrO₂ vs PEEK abutments specifically on peri-implant tissue health parameters (MBL, soft tissue, inflammation, survival), complementing [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/tiskratok-2026-tooth-colored-abutment-peri-implant-soft-tissue-sr]] (136 in-vitro/in-vivo studies) with a stricter clinical-only synthesis.

## Three-line Summary

PRISMA SR (PubMed + Scopus, 2019–2026; 5,362→13 studies), qualitative synthesis of human clinical studies on titanium, zirconia, and PEEK abutment effects on peri-implant tissue health; ROBINS-I risk of bias assessment; PROSPERO CRD420251024493.
Titanium: most reliable — consistent minimal MBL, low inflammation, high long-term predictability; Zirconia: comparable biology with superior soft tissue response and aesthetics, particularly in anterior regions; PEEK: promising reduced inflammation and favorable biocompatibility but insufficient long-term evidence for routine use.
Titanium remains the clinical gold standard for mechanical stability and MBL; ZrO₂ is the preferred alternative for esthetic zones; PEEK requires further longitudinal studies before routine adoption.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA 체계적 문헌고찰(PubMed+Scopus, 2019–2026; 5,362→13편), Ti·ZrO₂·PEEK 어버트먼트의 임플란트 주위 조직 건강(MBL, 연조직, 염증, 생존율)에 대한 임상 연구 정성 합성; ROBINS-I 비뚤림 평가; PROSPERO CRD420251024493.
Ti: 최소 변연골흡수(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)·낮은 염증·높은 장기 예측성 — 임상 표준; ZrO₂: 생물학적 성과 동등 + 연조직 반응·심미성 우수(특히 전치부); PEEK: 염증 감소 잠재력·생체적합성 양호 — 장기 데이터 부족으로 일상 사용 시기상조.
Ti는 기계적 안정성·MBL 면 임상 표준 유지; 심미 구역엔 ZrO₂ 우선; PEEK는 장기 추적 연구 충분해질 때까지 신중히 접근.

## Key Contributions

- PRISMA-compliant clinical-only SR: only human studies; excludes in-vitro/animal
- 13 included studies; ROBINS-I bias assessment; mostly low-moderate risk
- ZrO₂ associated with reduced probing depth, lower BOP, better soft tissue vs Ti
- Ti maintains superior mechanical stability and long-term MBL predictability
- PEEK shows reduced inflammation in short-term but no long-term data available
- Evidence base concentrated on Ti and ZrO₂; PEEK evidence sparse (1 study included)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA); qualitative synthesis (meta-analysis not feasible)
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus (Embase/Cochrane excluded — noted as limitation)
- **Period**: 2019–2026
- **Initial hits**: 5,362 → 158 after dedup → 13 included
- **Inclusion**: Human clinical studies (RCT, prospective, retrospective, case series); English
- **Exclusion**: Animal/in-vitro, case reports, reviews, abstracts, pre-2019
- **Risk of bias**: ROBINS-I — most studies low to moderate

## Key Results

| Material | MBL | Soft Tissue | Inflammation | Aesthetics | Evidence Strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Titanium | Minimal; stable | Stable | Low | Standard | Strong/long-term |
| Zirconia | Comparable to Ti | Superior; reduced PD/BOP | Reduced vs Ti | Superior | Moderate/long-term |
| PEEK | Limited data | Promising | Reduced | N/A | Weak/short-term |

**Included studies (n=13): key examples**
- Savitha 2024 (RCT, n=90, 1yr): ZrO₂ significantly lower PD, BOP, plaque vs Ti and gold alloy
- Fonseca 2021 (prospective, n=40, 4.5–8yr): ZrO₂ high survival with minimal soft tissue complications
- Milinkovic 2022 (RCT pilot, n=20, 3mo): PEEK fewer inflammatory markers than Ti
- Kumar 2024 (prospective, n=40, 6mo): ZrO₂ associated with reduced MBL and improved soft tissue

## Limitations

- Meta-analysis not feasible due to heterogeneity in design, outcomes, follow-up
- Only PubMed + Scopus searched (Embase/Cochrane not included)
- PEEK evidence limited to 1–2 studies; no definitive conclusions
- No standardized outcome definitions across included studies
- Mostly short to medium follow-up; few long-term studies

## Glossary

- **ROBINS-I**: Risk of Bias in Non-Randomized Studies of Interventions; 7-domain tool
- **PEEK (폴리에테르에테르케톤, Polyetheretherketone)**: 고성능 폴리머 어버트먼트 소재; 생물학적 잠재력 있으나 장기 데이터 부족
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing / 탐침 출혈)**: 탐침 시 출혈 — 임플란트 주위 염증 지표
- **PD (Probing Depth / 탐침 깊이)**: 치주낭 깊이 측정값; 임플란트 주위 건강 지표
