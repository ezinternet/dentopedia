## Why Ingested

지금까지 위키에서 관골(협골) 임플란트 (Zygomatic Implant, ZI)는 [[sinus-lift/lateral/rocha-2023-sinusitis-rate-sinus-lift-zygomatic-ma]]에서 sinusitis 위험 비교군(sinus lift 1.11% vs ZI 3.76%)으로만 등장했다. 본 논문(Scocca 2026, J Dent)은 **종양절제 후 상악 재건에서 ZI 생존·실패·조사(RT)·QoL을 다룬 첫 SR+MA**로, 위축상악 재건 결정 트리에서 조사된/종양성 상악(irradiated/oncologic maxilla) 분기의 첫 결과-수준 근거 페이지가 된다. 이는 short implant·경사식립 전략을 다룬 [[implants/del-fabbro-2022-full-arch-tilted-axial-implants-sr-ma]] 및 [[overviews/short-implant-vs-sinus-augmentation-decision]]가 다루지 않는 "골이식·통상 임플란트가 불가능한 극단적 결손" 시나리오를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 17 studies (294 head-and-neck oncology patients, 679 zygomatic implants) evaluating survival, timing-dependent failure, radiotherapy effect, and QoL of zygomatic implants (ZIs) for maxillary rehabilitation after tumor resection.

Pooled overall survival was 97% (666/679; 95%CI 94–99; I²=42.4%); failure was 2% for ZIs placed at primary surgery vs 9% at secondary surgery (p=0.4073, NS), and radiotherapy raised failure odds non-significantly (OR 3.42; 95%CI 0.97–12.06; p=0.0546), while PROMs (UW-QOL, EORTC QLQ-H&N35) showed satisfactory function even in irradiated patients.

ZIs are a predictable, minimally invasive option for extensive maxillary defects — placement at primary resection may be modestly favorable and rehabilitation remains feasible after radiotherapy, but high survival does not eliminate a substantial prosthodontic/maintenance burden.

## 세줄요약

종양절제 후 상악 재건을 위한 관골 임플란트(ZI)의 생존·타이밍별 실패·방사선치료(RT) 영향·삶의질을 다룬 17편 SR+MA (두경부암 환자 294명, ZI 679개).

통합 전체 생존율 97%(666/679; 95%CI 94–99; I²=42.4%)였고, 실패율은 1차 수술 동시 식립 2% vs 2차 식립 9%(p=0.4073, 무의미), RT는 실패 오즈를 비유의하게 높임(OR 3.42; 95%CI 0.97–12.06; p=0.0546); PROM(UW-QOL, EORTC QLQ-H&N35)은 조사 환자에서도 만족스러운 기능을 보였다.

ZI는 광범위 상악 결손의 예측가능·저침습 선택지로 1차 절제 시 동시 식립이 다소 유리할 수 있고 RT 후에도 재건이 가능하나, 높은 생존율이 보철적·유지관리 부담을 없애주지는 않는다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (single-arm/proportion meta-analyses), PRISMA-compliant, PROSPERO ID 1,245,372.
- **Journal**: Journal of Dentistry 170 (2026) 106709. Open access (CC BY).
- **Authors**: Veronica Scocca, Giovanni Dell'Aversana Orabona — Maxillofacial Surgery Unit, University Federico II of Naples, Italy.
- **Search**: PubMed/MEDLINE, Ovid MEDLINE, Cochrane Library, Scopus, EMBASE, Google Scholar; years 1950–2025 (last search 1 Dec 2025); snowballing.
- **PICOTS**: P = head and neck oncology patients; I = zygomatic implant (ZI) placement; C = failure by timing (primary vs secondary surgery), failure in irradiated vs non-irradiated; O = ZI survival, timing failure, RT failure, complications, QoL.
- **Inclusion**: retrospective/prospective cohort, case–control, cross-sectional, RCT reporting ZI outcomes in oncology maxillary/midface rehabilitation; ≥4 oncology patients. **Excluded**: case reports, conference abstracts, letters, book chapters, technical notes, non-full-text, non-oncology ZI indications. Mixed cohorts included only if oncology-specific data extractable.
- **Quality**: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS); leave-one-out sensitivity; funnel plot + Egger's test.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **First SR+MA** specifically evaluating zygomatic implants for oncologic maxillary/midface rehabilitation (prior literature: only one non-systematic review).
- Pooled quantitative estimates for (a) overall ZI survival, (b) failure by surgical timing (primary vs secondary), and (c) failure risk with adjuvant radiotherapy.
- Qualitative synthesis of patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) across validated head-and-neck/oral-rehabilitation instruments.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 521 records → 437 duplicates removed → 46 off-topic excluded → 38 full-text assessed → **17 studies** included (excluded at full text: 1 review, 1 no full text, 13 case reports, 1 non-English, 3 technical notes).
- 17 studies: 13 retrospective (RSP), 4 prospective (PSP: Pellegrino 2025, Vosselman 2023, Butterworth 2021, Butterworth 2018). Countries span Italy, UK, Netherlands, Australia, China, USA, Germany, South Africa, Switzerland, Sweden, Turkey, Armenia. 15/17 maxilla, 2 midface.
- Single-arm proportion meta-analyses; **Freeman–Tukey double arcsine transformation** to stabilize variance; **random-effects model**; Cochran's Q and I² for heterogeneity; ORs for RT failure. R software (meta, dmeta packages), v4.4.2.
- Implant **failure** defined strictly as complete loss of the implant; **complications** = any biological/technical issue on a retained implant.
- Publication bias by funnel plot + Egger's test (≥4 studies); leave-one-out sensitivity (>4 studies per outcome).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Sample**: 294 oncology patients (per-study n 5–49), 679 ZIs. Mean 2.36 ZIs/patient (95%CI 2.04–2.74). Mean follow-up 37.3 months (95%CI 11.1–63.6).
- **Overall survival (OS)**: 97% (666/679; 95%CI 94–99); low-moderate heterogeneity I²=42.4% (Q=0.0047, p=0.0337).
- **Failure by timing**: primary surgery 2% (20/413; 95%CI 0–11) vs secondary surgery 9% (17/172; 95%CI 3–19); **p=0.4073 (not significant)**.
- **Radiotherapy subgroup**: OR 3.42 (95%CI 0.97–12.06; **p=0.0546, not significant**); negligible heterogeneity I²=0.2% (consistent across studies).
- **PROMs (QoL)**: most-used instruments UW-QOL and EORTC QLQ-H&N35; also MDADI, SHI, FACE-Q, LORQv3, VAS, OFS. Consistently satisfactory oral function, mastication, speech, psychosocial well-being — including irradiated patients (Butterworth 2021: 72% rated overall QoL good or better despite RT-group dysfunction). Prosthetic design mattered: hybrid prostheses scored higher for chewing/stability/phonetics; removable preferred for cleaning ease (Atalay 2017).
- **Illustrative studies**: Butterworth 2018 largest sample (131 ZIs, follow-up to 10y; Kaplan–Meier 5-yr survival ≥90%, 12-mo 94%, 60-mo 92%; secondary placement HR 2.25 vs primary, NS). Schmidt 2004 lowest survival (77%; 2/3 failures had received RT).
- **Risk of bias**: NOS mostly low (scores 6–8). Leave-one-out: no single study drove pooled estimates (robust). Publication bias — survival funnel asymmetric (Egger t=2.59, p=0.0213); timing asymmetric (t=4.46, p=0.0006); RT symmetric (t=0.0177, p=0.9859).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Most included studies retrospective → selection bias, limited generalizability.
- Substantial clinical heterogeneity (populations, timing, surgical protocols, prosthetic designs, follow-up).
- Inconsistent PROM reporting (different instruments, variable timing, small samples) → no quantitative QoL synthesis possible.
- Funnel-plot asymmetry for survival and timing outcomes suggests possible small-study/publication bias.
- Authors stress: high survival ≠ uncomplicated rehabilitation — altered post-maxillectomy anatomy, flap reconstruction, scar contracture, and RT amplify prosthodontic complexity, hygiene access, phonetics, and maintenance burden.

## 6. Related Work

- Rocha 2023 SR+MA (sinusitis after sinus lift vs zygomatic implants) — situates ZI-associated sinusitis in the broader complication landscape.
- Del Fabbro 2022 (tilted vs axial full-arch implants) and short-implant vs sinus-augmentation decision syntheses — adjacent atrophic-maxilla strategies for less extreme (non-oncologic) resorption.
- Abdunabi 2019 (immediately loaded maxillary full-arch) — loading-strategy context for full-arch maxillary rehabilitation.

## 7. Glossary

- **ZI (Zygomatic Implant, 관골/협골 임플란트)**: long implant anchored in the zygomatic body, bypassing atrophic/resected maxillary alveolus; introduced by Brånemark (1998).
- **Primary vs secondary placement**: ZI inserted at the time of tumor resection (primary) vs at a later, separate surgery (secondary).
- **PROM (Patient-Reported Outcome Measure)**: UW-QOL, EORTC QLQ-H&N35, MDADI (dysphagia), SHI (speech), FACE-Q, LORQv3 (oral rehabilitation), OFS (oral function scale).
- **Freeman–Tukey double arcsine transformation**: variance-stabilizing transform for meta-analysis of proportions (esp. near 0/1).
- **NOS (Newcastle-Ottawa Scale)**: quality-assessment tool for non-randomized studies.
