## Why Ingested

This systematic review and meta-analysis of 16 RCTs quantifies the additive benefit of combining antibacterial agents with topical fluoride for caries prevention in children (SMD=-0.12, p=0.004), strengthening the evidence base for combination prophylaxis protocols. Its pediatric focus and direct comparison to fluoride monotherapy make it immediately applicable to preventive dentistry clinical decisions alongside [[caries/zheng-2025-silver-diamine-fluoride-vs-varnish-caries-prevention-rct]].

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis compared topical fluoride-antibacterial agent combined therapy versus fluoride monotherapy for preventing dental caries in children (1-16 years), including 16 RCTs from 3475 screened records. Combined therapy showed superior caries-preventive effectiveness (SMD = -0.12, 95% CI: -0.2 to -0.04, p = 0.004, I² = 20%). No significant difference was found for post-intervention salivary S. mutans count (SMD = -0.11, 95% CI: -0.33 to 0.10, p = 0.30, I² = 0%).

Fluoride-antibacterial agent combined therapy offers added benefit over fluoride monotherapy for caries prevention in children, though evidence is low certainty.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Combined fluoride-antibacterial therapy superior to fluoride monotherapy for caries prevention in children (SMD = -0.12, p = 0.004).
- No significant difference in salivary S. mutans counts between combined therapy and fluoride monotherapy (SMD = -0.11, p = 0.30).
- Low certainty evidence generated; driven primarily by two studies on xylitol.
- Antibacterial agents evaluated: povidone-iodine, chlorhexidine, xylitol, triclosan, cetylpyridinium chloride.
- Combined therapy may be beneficial for high-risk children.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- Databases: PubMed, EbscoHost, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials.
- PRISMA compliance: Yes.
- Inclusion criteria: RCTs in children 1-16 years comparing topical fluoride-antibacterial agent combined therapy vs fluoride monotherapy. Outcomes: caries increment (DMFS/dmfs) or salivary S. mutans counts.
- Excluded: Non-RCTs, studies without relevant outcomes, studies not in children.
- Included: 16 trials in qualitative synthesis; 9 trials in quantitative synthesis (meta-analysis).
- Risk of bias: Assessed using Cochrane RoB tool.
- Data synthesis: Meta-analysis using Review Manager 5.4 (Inverse Variance test). SMD with 95% CI. I² for heterogeneity. p < 0.05 significant.
- GRADE: Used for evidence certainty (low certainty).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Caries increment (combined vs monotherapy)** — SMD = -0.12 (95% CI: -0.20 to -0.04, p = 0.004, I² = 20%, p for heterogeneity = 0.29)
- **Salivary S. mutans count (post-intervention)** — SMD = -0.11 (95% CI: -0.33 to 0.10, p = 0.30, I² = 0%, p for heterogeneity = 0.77)
- Key antibacterial agents: Xylitol (2 studies drove the result), Povidone-iodine, Chlorhexidine, Triclosan, Cetylpyridinium chloride.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Low certainty evidence (GRADE downgraded for risk of bias and indirectness).
- The result is driven primarily by two studies on xylitol.
- Heterogeneity across studies in antibacterial agents, fluoride types, and delivery methods.
- Limited number of studies for each antibacterial agent subgroup.
- Publication bias not assessed due to limited studies.
- Salivary S. mutans may not be the most relevant outcome for caries prevention (plaque levels may be more relevant).
- Need for more high-quality trials with consistent outcome measures.

## 7. Glossary

- **SMD (Standardized Mean Difference)**: 표준화 평균 차이 — 서로 다른 단위의 결과를 결합할 때 사용
- **DMFS (Decayed, Missing, Filled Surfaces)**: 우식·상실·수복 치면 — 영구치 우식 경험 지표
- **dmfs (decayed, missing, filled surfaces)**: 우식·상실·수복 치면 — 유치 우식 경험 지표
- **S. mutans (Streptococcus mutans)**: 스트렙토코쿠스 뮤탄스 — 주요 우식 원인균
- **Xylitol**: 자일리톨 — 항우식 효과가 있는 천연 감미료
- **CHX (Chlorhexidine)**: 클로르헥시딘 — 광범위 항균 구강세정제
- **PVP-I (Povidone-Iodine)**: 포비돈-아이오딘 — 요오드포르 항균제
- **CPC (Cetylpyridinium Chloride)**: 세틸피리디늄 염화물 — 양이온 계면활성제 항균제
