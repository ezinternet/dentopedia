## Three-line Summary

Single-institution retrospective radiographic study of 1,692 Straumann tissue-level implants placed in 881 patients (Yonsei University Dental Hospital, 2003–2009) to estimate 10-year cumulative survival and describe patterns of implant loss.

The 10-year cumulative survival rates were 98.23% at the implant level and 95.70% at the patient level; 13 implants (10 patients) were removed before prosthesis (early stage) and 8 implants (7 patients) after prosthesis (late stage), with early failures mostly from osseointegration failure and some late failures occurring without apparent marginal bone loss.

Survival was related to implant diameter, length, site and insertion torque, so tissue-level implants can be considered a reliable long-term option, but only the abstract was available and effect sizes for the risk factors are not reported there.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Large single-institution 10-year denominator (1,692 implants, 881 patients) for one tissue-level implant system.
- Survival at implant level (98.23%) and patient level (95.70%).
- Early (pre-prosthesis, 13 implants/10 patients) vs late (post-prosthesis, 8 implants/7 patients) failure split.
- Late failures occurred without apparent marginal bone loss in several cases.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Retrospective chart and radiograph review; implants placed January 2003 to December 2009; failure = complete removal. Life-table and Kaplan-Meier survival; log-rank test and Cox regression with shared frailty for risk factors and failure types.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

10-year cumulative survival 98.23% (implant) and 95.70% (patient). Survival related to diameter, length, site and insertion torque; most failures within 1 year due to osseointegration failure. Effect sizes not in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract only. Retrospective, single institution, single implant system; failure defined as complete removal only. Part II of the series is presumably the companion analysis (not held).

## 6. Related Work

Antoun 2017 referral-clinic series; Moy 2005 risk-factor cohort; Grisar 2017 retrospective survival analysis.

## 7. Glossary

Early stage: before prosthesis installation; late stage: after prosthesis completion; shared frailty: Cox model term accounting for clustering of implants within patients.
