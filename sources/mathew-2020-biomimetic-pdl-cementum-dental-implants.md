## Why Ingested

골유착 임플란트의 PDL 부재 한계를 해소하려는 생체모방(Biomimetic) 임플란트 연구를 체계적으로 정리한 스코핑 리뷰로, 1989–2018년 동물·인체 연구 12편을 포함한다. [[implants/bajaj-2023-ligaplants-pdl-tissue-engineering-implant]]의 서술적 고찰보다 엄격한 스코핑 방법론으로 같은 분야를 커버하며, 바이오하이브리드 임플란트의 구조적·기능적 특성 데이터를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Scoping review (MEDLINE 1989–2018, n=1395 screened → 12 included animal studies + 1 human trial) of in vivo studies reporting structural features and functional efficiency of artificial PDL or cementum developed around titanium dental implants.

Evidence progression: retained/adjacent roots → exogenous scaffolds (PLG, ePTFE, Dacron) → cultured PDL cells from extracted teeth → bio-hybrid implants with dental follicle stem cells and immortalized human cells (most advanced generation).

One human trial (Gault 2010 ligaplant) confirmed mobility equivalent to natural teeth; two bio-hybrid implant studies demonstrated orthodontic-force-mediated bone remodeling and nociceptive response via nerve fiber innervation in newly formed PDL.

## 세줄요약

골유착 임플란트 주위 인공 PDL·백악질 형성의 구조적·기능적 특성을 보고한 생체 내(in vivo) 연구를 스코핑 고찰(MEDLINE 1989–2018, 1395편 선별 → 동물 12편 + 인체 1편).

증거 진화: 잔존/인접 치근 → 외래 스캐폴드(PLG, ePTFE, Dacron) → 발거치 PDL 배양 세포 → 배아성 치낭 줄기세포·불멸화 인체세포 기반 바이오하이브리드 임플란트(최신 세대).

인체 1편(Gault 2010 리가플란트)에서 자연치와 동등한 이동성 확인; 바이오하이브리드 임플란트 2편에서 교정력 매개 골 리모델링과 신경섬유 분포(통증 감지)를 통한 유해 자극 반응 입증.

## Key Contributions

- Structured taxonomy of 4 artificial PDL source strategies: (1) retained natural roots, (2) exogenous scaffold biomaterials, (3) cultured PDL cells, (4) bio-hybrid implants with stem cell-derived tissues
- Confirmed: HA-coated implants form better-organized fibrous tissue with perpendicularly oriented collagen than titanium-alloy implants
- Gault 2010 ligaplant: tight integration without bone contact, new bone induction, mobility = natural teeth
- Oshima 2014 and Nakajima 2016 bio-hybrids: PDL fiber structure (transverse collagen + longitudinal elastin), Sharpey's fiber insertion into new cementum, orthodontic tooth movement through appropriate osteoclast/osteoblast response confirmed
- Two bio-hybrid studies: antineurofilament-immunoreactive nerve fibers detected in new PDL; C-Fos-immunoreactive neurons increased 2 h post-orthodontic force (nociceptive response confirmed)
- Critical gap: complete functional recovery with absolute sensory perception not yet demonstrated — autologous cell sources needed

## Methodology

- **Design**: Scoping review; PRISMA flow
- **Search**: MEDLINE via Ovid (1989–2018, Jan 2, 2019)
- **Initial hits**: 1,395; duplicates removed → 1,305 rejected (periodontal tissue regeneration/bone defect focus) → 44 full-text → 12 included
- **Inclusion criteria**: in vivo studies; artificial PDL or cementum developed around titanium dental implants; commercial pure titanium implants; microscopically characterized
- **Excluded**: review articles, hypothetical studies, extra-oral implants, bone implants, periodontal defect regeneration

## Key Results

**12 included studies summary by source type:**

| Author (Year) | Source type | Duration | Key finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buser 1990 | Retained roots | 12 months | Root cementum on implant surface + PDL-like space with perpendicular fibers |
| Warrer 1996 | Retained roots (canine) | 3 months | Sharpey's fibers perpendicular where PDL invasion allowed |
| Urabe 1999 | Adjacent PDL cells | 3 months | HA > TA: uniform fibrous tissue + calcified cementum-like tissue |
| Choi 2000 | Cultured PDL cells | 3 months | Cementum-like tissue + PDL-like layer on 37% of implant surfaces |
| Marei 2009 | PLG scaffold | 1 month | Sharpey's-like fibers, cementum tightly adherent, bone remodeling |
| Gault 2010 | Ligaplant (human) | 18 days→3 mo | Firm integration, new bone, mobility = natural teeth |
| Lin 2011 | Rat PDL progenitors + Matrigel | 18 weeks | Osseointegration + thin cementum layer + poorly vascularized PDL |
| Oshima 2014 | Bio-hybrid (ED18.5-DF) | 1 month | Orthodontic movement, nerve innervation, osteoclast/osteoblast confirmed |
| Nakajima 2016 | Bone-attached + rat PDL (HA+Pt) | 40 days | Identical to Oshima; orthodontic movement + nociception |
| Lee 2017 | Immortalized human cells (4 types) | 56 days | PDL markers positive; aligned fibrous tissue; PDL-specific marker PLAP-1 expressed |

**Functional evaluations:**
- Shock absorption: confirmed (Gault elastic test, Caiazza shear stress stability)
- Orthodontic movement: confirmed in bio-hybrid (Oshima, Nakajima) via bone remodeling markers
- Nociception: confirmed in bio-hybrid studies via NF-immunoreactive nerve fibers

## Limitations

- 12 included studies is a small evidence base spanning ~30 years
- Animal-model-dominant; only one human trial (ligaplant, basic mobility endpoint)
- Lack of standardized search vocabulary — possible missed articles
- No complete functional recovery documented in any included study

## Glossary

- **PDL (치주인대)**: Periodontal Ligament — fibrous connective tissue anchoring tooth root to alveolar bone via Sharpey's fibers
- **Cementum (백악질)**: Calcified tissue covering tooth root surface; anchor point for Sharpey's fibers
- **Bio-hybrid implant**: Implant wrapped in embryonic dental follicle tissue (e.g., ED18.5-DF) to regenerate PDL-like attachment
- **Sharpey's fibers**: Collagen fiber bundles inserting perpendicularly into cementum and bone — signature of functional PDL attachment
- **HA coating (수산화인회석 코팅)**: Hydroxyapatite surface coat that promotes more organized PDL and uniform cementum deposition vs uncoated titanium
- **Osseoperception (골유착감각)**: Tactile sensibility via osseointegrated implants — functionally inferior to PDL-mediated proprioception
