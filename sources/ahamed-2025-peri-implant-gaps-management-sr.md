## Why Ingested

Mined as a reference from [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]], a narrative review on bone-substitute material selection for alveolar ridge augmentation. This SR (Ahamed 2025) parallels that review's material-selection framework but applies it specifically to the peri-implant "jumping gap" after immediate implant placement (11 RCTs comparing xenograft/alloplast/PRF/allograft), giving a focused evidence base for that particular clinical decision point.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA-P, PROSPERO CRD42024508852) of 11 RCTs (2013-2024 search window) comparing grafting materials/techniques for managing the peri-implant "jumping gap" after immediate implant placement.

Xenografts and alloplastic grafts consistently outperformed platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) and autogenous bone in preserving buccal bone volume and marginal bone levels; socket-shield technique and immediate provisionalization (Prosthetic Socket Preservation) also showed promise for soft-tissue/esthetic preservation; implant stability (ISQ) generally did not differ significantly by graft material across most studies.

High between-study heterogeneity (populations, outcome measures, follow-up 6-18 months) precluded meta-analysis/GRADE and pooled quantitative conclusions; allogenic grafts were understudied (only 1 of 11 trials).

## 세줄요약

PRISMA-P 체계적 문헌고찰(PROSPERO CRD42024508852)로, 즉시 임플란트 식립 후 발생하는 peri-implant gap(jumping gap) 관리 기법을 비교한 RCT 11편(2013-2024)을 분석.

이종골(xenograft)과 동종/합성골(alloplastic graft)이 혈소판풍부섬유소(PRF) 및 자가골보다 협측 골량·변연골 유지에서 일관되게 우수했고, 소켓실드 기법과 즉시임시수복(Prosthetic Socket Preservation)도 연조직·심미 유지에 유망했으며, 임플란트 안정성(ISQ)은 대부분의 연구에서 이식재 종류 간 유의차가 없었다.

연구 간 이질성(대상군·평가지표·추적기간 6-18개월)이 커서 메타분석/GRADE 적용이 부적절했고, 동종골(allograft)은 11편 중 1편만 다뤄 근거가 부족하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic synthesis (per the authors) focused specifically on the peri-implant/jumping gap after immediate implant placement, rather than immediate implant placement broadly.
- Direct head-to-head evidence table across 11 RCTs comparing xenograft, alloplast, PRF, allograft, mineralized plasmatic matrix (MPM), beta-TCP, hyaluronic acid/melatonin gel, socket shield, and flap vs flapless techniques.
- RoB 2 risk-of-bias assessment applied uniformly (all 11 included studies were RCTs).
- Identifies two practical management levers: (1) grafting material choice and (2) immediate provisional prosthesis / Prosthetic Socket Preservation (PSP) surgical protocols (magnetic mallet, bone condensation, piezo-surgery).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PRISMA-P 2015-compliant protocol, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42024508852).
- Databases: MEDLINE (PubMed), Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, 2013-present; Google Scholar for grey literature; manual reference-list screening. English-language only.
- PICOS: human participants requiring immediate implant placement with a jumping gap; any gap-management intervention (grafting, GBR, soft-tissue augmentation, immediate provisionalization) vs comparator/control; primary outcomes = soft tissue contour, buccal bone level, esthetics; secondary = implant stability, survival.
- Study designs eligible: RCTs, cohort, case-control (2013-2024). Case reports/series <10 patients, reviews, editorials excluded.
- Screening: Rayyan software, 2 independent reviewers (title/abstract then full text), third-reviewer arbitration for disagreements.
- Risk of bias: RoB 2 (randomized trials) — all 11 included studies were RCTs.
- PRISMA flow: 102 records → 49 after dedup/eligibility → 10 excluded (no "peri-implant gap"/"jumping gap" in title/abstract) → 39 sought full text (1 inaccessible) → 27 excluded (irrelevant/design) → **11 included**.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Study-level findings (all RCTs, follow-up 6-18 months, maxillary arch, mostly anterior esthetic zone or premolar sites):

| Study (year) | Comparison | Key finding |
|---|---|---|
| El Ebiary (2023) | xenograft+autogenous vs no graft | higher PES at 6mo (p=0.048) |
| Samy (2024) | MPM vs beta-TCP | MPM: less MBL, better bone density; ISQ ns |
| Elsheikh (2023) | xenograft/alloplast vs PRF | better buccal bone preservation (p<0.001); ISQ/pocket depth ns |
| Amer (2022) | hyaluronic acid+melatonin gel vs none | better gingival index, less probing depth (p<0.05 / p=0.001) |
| Elbrashy (2022) | bovine cancellous xenograft vs PRF | better crestal bone maintenance (p=0.002) |
| Hammad (2021) | mixed allograft-xenograft vs xenograft alone | minimal MBL diff, esthetic scores ns |
| Shaaban Metwally (2021) | PRF vs non-PRF | PRF: higher bone density at 9mo (p=0.039) |
| Atef (2021) | socket shield vs bovine xenograft | better midfacial mucosal preservation (p=0.017), esthetics maintained |
| Adam (2020) | NanoBone vs autogenous chin graft | NanoBone: higher marginal bone level, similar ISQ |
| Naji (2019) | flap+graft vs flapless-no-graft | similar horizontal bone healing (premolar sites) |
| Sanz (2016) | DBBM-C vs no graft | less horizontal crest resorption (p=0.018-0.029) |

Cross-cutting patterns: xenograft/alloplast > PRF/autogenous bone for buccal bone/MBL preservation in most head-to-heads; implant stability (ISQ) largely material-independent; PRF results mixed (benefit for bone density in some trials, no advantage in others); socket shield and immediate provisionalization/PSP protocols favorable for soft-tissue and esthetic preservation; allogenic grafts (allograft alone) essentially untested (only 1/11 studies, and only as a mixture).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High clinical/methodological heterogeneity (populations, outcome definitions, follow-up length) precluded meta-analysis; GRADE framework explicitly deemed inappropriate by the authors.
- Small number of included trials (n=11) despite a broad multi-database search — reflects a narrow, specific research question.
- Risk-of-bias concerns in specific studies: Shaaban Metwally (2021) lacked randomization/allocation-concealment/blinding detail; Adam (2020) had significant baseline group differences.
- Allogenic bone grafts essentially unstudied in isolation.
- English-language restriction and a single inaccessible full-text article (1 of 39) are additional selection constraints.
- Authors call for standardized outcome measures, larger high-quality multicenter RCTs, and longer-term follow-up.

## 6. Related Work

- Builds on and narrows the broader immediate-implant-placement literature (gap management specifically, not immediate placement generally).
- Complements narrative reviews of bone-substitute selection more broadly, e.g. [[wiki/bone-regeneration/bubalo-2026-bone-substitutes-alveolar-ridge-augmentation]], by providing RCT-level comparative data for one specific application (peri-implant gap after immediate placement) rather than general material properties.
- Relevant to socket-shield technique literature (`immediate-implant/socket-shield` category) via the Atef (2021) comparison.

## 7. Glossary

- **Peri-implant gap / jumping gap / jumping distance**: the horizontal space between an implant surface and the buccal bone plate after immediate implant placement into an extraction socket.
- **PRF**: platelet-rich fibrin, an autologous blood-derived biomaterial.
- **MPM**: mineralized plasmatic matrix.
- **beta-TCP**: beta-tricalcium phosphate, a synthetic alloplastic bone substitute.
- **DBBM(-C)**: deproteinized bovine bone mineral (with collagen).
- **PES**: pink esthetic score, a soft-tissue esthetic outcome measure.
- **ISQ**: implant stability quotient (resonance frequency analysis).
- **Socket shield technique**: retaining a thin buccal root fragment during extraction/immediate implant placement to preserve buccal bone/soft tissue.
- **PSP**: Prosthetic Socket Preservation — immediate provisional prosthesis/customized abutment protocols intended to preserve gingival contour and bone volume.
- **RoB 2**: Cochrane Risk of Bias 2.0 tool for randomized trials.
