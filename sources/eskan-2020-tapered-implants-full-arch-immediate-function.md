## Why Ingested

상악 풀아치 즉시 부하 프로토콜 중 골질 불량 부위에서 초기 안정성 확보를 위한 테이퍼드 임플란트(tapered implant) 디자인의 임상 결과가 필요. Straumann BLT(Bone Level Tapered) + NDI 조합의 4.5년 MBL·생존율 데이터가 [[wiki/coskunses-2021-narrow-diameter-implants-full-arch-fixed]] 보다 긴 추적기간을 제공.

## Three-line Summary

Multicenter retrospective (42 patients, 43 fixed full-arch prostheses, 171 Straumann BLT SLA Roxolid implants — 134×4.1mm + 37×3.3mm; majority 4 implants per jaw with 2 tilted posteriors ≤30°; immediate loading; follow-up 32–55 months).

Overall implant survival 97.7% (3 maxilla + 1 mandible failures); mean interproximal MBL at 24 months 0.15 mm (0.16 mm maxilla / 0.14 mm mandible); no significant difference in MBL between tilted and axial implants; bone loss between 12–24 months averaged 0.09 mm.

The apically tapered design with aggressive threading enables primary stability in soft bone and fresh sockets, making immediate loading predictable; NDI fractures (0/37) were absent at 55 months with Ti-Zr alloy; bruxer night guards critical for provisional screw loosening prevention.

## 세줄요약

다기관 회고 연구(42명, 43개 풀아치 보철, Straumann BLT 171개 — 4.1 mm 134개 + 3.3 mm 37개; 대부분 악궁당 4개 경사 원심 2개; 즉시 부하; 추적 32–55개월).

전체 생존율 97.7%; 2년 평균 근원심 MBL 0.15 mm(상악 0.16 / 하악 0.14); 경사 vs 축방향 임플란트 MBL 차이 없음; 12–24개월 구간 골소실 0.09 mm로 안정.

테이퍼드 디자인은 연조골·발치와에서 초기 안정성 확보 → 즉시 부하 예측성 향상; Ti-Zr NDI는 55개월 추적에서 파절 0건; 브럭시즘 환자 야간 마우스가드 준수가 임시 보철 나사 풀림 예방에 결정적.

## 1. Document Information

- **Study type**: Multicenter retrospective clinical study
- **Follow-up**: 32–55 months (mean ~43 months)
- **Sample**: 42 consecutive patients, 171 implants (98 maxilla + 73 mandible)
- **Journal**: International Journal of Implant Dentistry, 2020

## 2. Key Contributions

- Validates BLT tapered implants for full-arch immediate loading up to 4.5 years with excellent MBL
- NDI (3.3 mm Ti-Zr) in posterior positions: 0 fractures at 55 months — confirms fatigue safety
- Occlusal precautions described: tilted implants ≤30°, flat fossa/shallow occlusal anatomy, canine-protected occlusion, night guard for all patients
- Provisional fracture rate ~15% concentrated in unidentified bruxers not wearing night guards

## 3. Methodology

- Pro Arch Guide (Straumann) for precise tilting ≤30°; 30° MUA for posterior tilted implants, 0° or 17° for anterior
- Loading criteria: IT ≥30 Ncm (lower than Coskunses 2021's ≥35 Ncm)
- Definitive prosthesis at 4 months: CAD-CAM milled or laser-sintered metal framework + ceramic or acrylic
- Night guard provided to all patients on day of surgery
- OPG marginal bone level measured by calibrated assistants using Romexis software at 0, 4, 12, 24 months

## 4. Key Results

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Implant survival (overall) | 97.7% (4/171 failures) |
| Survival — maxilla | 96.9% |
| Survival — mandible | 98.7% |
| MBL 0–24 months | 0.15 mm |
| MBL 12–24 months | 0.09 mm |
| Maxilla MBL 24m | 0.16 mm |
| Mandible MBL 24m | 0.14 mm |
| Tilted vs axial MBL | No significant difference |
| NDI fractures (55m) | 0/37 |
| Provisional prosthesis fracture | ~15% (all in unidentified bruxers not using night guard) |
| Definitive prosthesis fracture | 0% |

## 5. Limitations

- Retrospective multicenter; no control group
- OPG only (2D); periapical not used
- Survival failures all in provisional period — may underestimate late losses
- Short MBL measurement window (only 24 months of bone data)

## 6. Related Work

- Malo et al. 2005: first All-on-Four data; 98.9% survival at 1yr — consistent
- Coskunses 2021: same NDI Ti-Zr concept, similar 2yr survival and MBL — reinforces
- Patzelt et al. SR: tilted=axial MBL — consistent

## 7. Glossary

- **BLT**: Bone Level Tapered (Straumann implant with apically tapered body + aggressive threads)
- **IT**: Insertion Torque (Ncm)
- **Pro Arch Guide**: Straumann surgical guide for tilting angulation control
- **Osteotomy under-preparation**: Drilling slightly smaller than implant diameter to increase primary stability in soft bone
