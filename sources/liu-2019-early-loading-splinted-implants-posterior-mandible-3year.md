## Why Ingested
하악 구치부 스플린팅 임플란트의 조기 부하(6–8주) 3년 전향적 다기관 연구. [[sources/zhou-2016-early-loading-splinted-implants-posterior-mandible-1year]]의 동일 코호트 3년 추적 데이터로, 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ) 변화와 변연골 패턴을 장기 확인한다.

## Three-line Summary
Prospective multicenter case series from three Chinese centers followed 40 subjects (92 implants; OsseoSpeed TX, fluoride-modified nanostructure surface) with early loading (6–8 weeks) of splinted screw-retained fixed prostheses in the posterior mandible over 36 months.
Implant survival was 100% at 3 years, and mean marginal bone level changed by +0.23 ± 0.48 mm (gain, p=0.00061 vs. loading baseline), with statistically significant bone gain confirmed at 12, 24, and 36 months.
Early loading of splinted fluoride-modified implants in posterior mandible appears durable at 3 years, but the absence of a control group and single-brand restriction limit generalizability beyond this specific system.

줄3: 불화물 개질 표면의 스플린팅 조기부하 임플란트는 3년간 안전하고 안정적이나, 대조군 없음 및 단일 브랜드 제한으로 타 시스템 일반화에는 주의가 필요하다.

## One-line Summary
Prospective multicenter (n=40 subjects, 92 implants, 3-year follow-up): 100% survival; mean marginal bone gain of 0.23 mm at 36 months after early loading of fluoride-modified splinted implants in posterior mandible.

## 한줄요약
전향적 다기관(n=40, 임플란트 92개, 3년): 생존율 100%; 조기부하 후 36개월 변연골 0.23mm 증가(통계적 유의); 불화물 개질 임플란트 스플린팅의 하악 구치부 안전성 확인.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Clinical Oral Implants Research, 2019;30(10):1049–1058
- **Institutions**: Three centers in China (Shanghai institutions)
- **Funding**: None (no conflicts)
- **Follow-up**: 36 months after loading

## 2. Key Contributions
- 3-year data on 107 implants placed (92 evaluated) with fluoride-modified nanostructure surface (OsseoSpeed TX)
- Demonstrates bone GAIN (not loss) at 36 months — may relate to favorable surface chemistry + adequate occlusal load
- Confirms early loading (6–8 weeks) as safe and effective for splinted posterior mandible implants
- Multicenter design (3 centers China) reduces single-center bias

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective multicenter case series
- **n**: 45 subjects enrolled, 40 completed 3-year follow-up (92 implants)
- **Implant system**: OsseoSpeed TX (fluoride-modified nanostructure surface, tapered apex, Dentsply)
- **Loading protocol**: Screw-retained splinted fixed permanent prosthesis 6–8 weeks post-surgery
- **Placement**: One-stage, 2–3 implants per edentulous region, posterior mandible
- **Outcome timepoints**: 6, 12, 24, 36 months
- **Primary outcomes**: MBL change from time of loading; implant survival; soft tissue health

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Implant survival**: 100% (92/92 at 36 months)
- **Marginal bone level change (subject level)**:
  - 36 months: mean gain of +0.23 ± 0.48 mm (p=0.00061 vs. loading time)
- **MBL at implant level**: statistically significant changes at 12 (p=0.039), 24 (p=0.015), 36 months (p<0.001)
- **Soft tissue health**: maintained throughout follow-up
- Pattern: slight initial bone change then stabilization/gain — consistent with favorable osseointegration

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No control group (single-arm design); no comparison with delayed loading
- All centers in China — may not generalize to other populations
- 5 subjects lost to follow-up (potential dropout bias)
- OsseoSpeed TX is a specific brand; results may not extend to other surface types

## 6. Related Work
- Zhou 2016: 12-month baseline data from same cohort (0.08mm gain)
- Anitua 2019 (short implants immediate loading): different length focus but loading timing context
- Slot 2022 (bar overdentures): splinting in different prosthetic context

## 7. Glossary
- **OsseoSpeed TX**: Dentsply Sirona implant with fluoride-modified nanostructure surface
- **Early loading**: Prosthetic loading 6–8 weeks post-implant placement (between immediate and conventional)
- **MBL**: Marginal bone level (measured radiographically from implant shoulder)
- **Nanostructure surface**: Implant surface with nano-scale topography to enhance bone attachment
