## Why Ingested

[[sinus-lift/lateral/diaz-olivares-2021-schneiderian-membrane-perforation-sinus-lift]] remains the highest-inbound page on this topic (ib=27); this 2024 SR+MA independently confirms its conclusion with broader inclusion criteria (lateral window only, 10 qualitative studies, 7 in meta-analysis) and provides an updated odds-ratio analysis, reinforcing clinical confidence that repaired perforations do not compromise implant survival.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (PubMed/Scopus/Web of Science, May 2024; 10 qualitative, 7 meta-analysis; lateral window approach only; ≥6-month follow-up after prosthesis placement) comparing implant survival rates in perforated vs non-perforated Schneiderian membranes after lateral sinus lift.

Implant survival rate was 97.1% in 480 perforated sinuses (1,044 implants, 30 failures) vs 97.7% in 1,224 non-perforated sinuses (2,725 implants, 62 failures); OR = 0.78 (95% CI 0.49–2.23; p = 0.28); I² = 0% — no significant difference.

Schneiderian membrane perforation, when repaired, does not negatively influence implant survival and should not be considered a reason to abort the procedure; one-stage vs two-stage implant placement also showed no significant survival difference in perforated sinuses (RR = 1.00; CI 0.94–1.07; p = 0.95).

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (PubMed/Scopus/WoS, 2024년 5월 검색; 정성분석 10편, 메타분석 7편; 측방(lateral window) 접근법만 포함; 보철 장착 후 ≥6개월 추적) — 슈나이데리안막 천공 여부에 따른 임플란트 생존율 비교.

천공군 480증례 1,044개 임플란트(실패 30개) 생존율 97.1% vs 비천공군 1,224증례 2,725개 임플란트(실패 62개) 생존율 97.7%; 교차비(Odds Ratio, OR) = 0.78 (95% CI 0.49–2.23; p = 0.28); 이질성(I²) = 0% — 유의한 차이 없음.

슈나이데리안막(Schneiderian membrane) 천공은 적절히 수복되면 임플란트 생존에 부정적 영향을 미치지 않으며, 수술 중단의 이유가 되지 않는다.

## Key Contributions

- Updated meta-analysis with broader inclusion criteria than diaz-olivares-2021 (10 qualitative vs 8, lateral window only)
- OR analysis (vs rate ratio in earlier reviews): OR = 0.78, 95% CI 0.49–2.23, p = 0.28
- Zero heterogeneity (I² = 0%) confirms consistent findings across studies
- Subgroup analysis: one-stage vs two-stage placement in perforated sinuses — no difference (RR = 1.00)
- Mean perforation incidence: 29.42% (range 10–237 per study)
- Collagen membrane repair is the most-used technique (7/10 studies); alternatives (suture, PRF, Bichat fat pad, oxidized cellulose, block graft) also effective

## Methodology

- **Design**: SR+MA (PRISMA guidelines)
- **Databases**: Medline/PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science (searched May 2024)
- **Inclusion**: In vivo human studies; lateral window sinus lift with graft material; membrane perforation occurred + repair technique described; ≥6 months follow-up post-prosthesis; RCTs, non-RCTs, cohort, case-control, case series
- **Exclusion**: Reviews, animal studies, case reports, transcrestal approach, no graft material, no implant count specified
- **n**: 10 qualitative, 7 meta-analysis; 1,666 patients; 2,229 sinus lifts; 5,052 implants total
- **Statistical method**: Binary random effects; odds ratio (OR); Review Manager 5.4
- **Bias assessment**: Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (NOS); 6 studies ≥6 stars (low-high risk)

## Key Results

| Group | Sinuses | Implants | Failures | Survival Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Non-perforated | 1,224 | 2,725 | 62 | **97.7%** |
| Perforated | 480 | 1,044 | 30 | **97.1%** |

- OR = 0.78 (95% CI 0.49–2.23); p = 0.28; I² = 0% (no heterogeneity)
- One-stage vs two-stage in perforated sinuses: RR = 1.00 (CI 0.94–1.07; p = 0.95)
- One-stage vs two-stage in non-perforated sinuses: RR = 0.99 (CI 0.96–1.02; p = 0.60)
- Repair materials: collagen membrane (7/10), suture (2), PRF (mentioned in several), Bichat fat ball, oxidized cellulose, block graft, connective tissue graft

## Limitations

- Study heterogeneity in sample sizes (18–1,588 implants per study)
- No stratification by perforation size (unlike Hernández-Alfaro et al. who showed larger perforations had lower ISR: <5mm 97.14%, 5-10mm 91.89%, >10mm 74.14%)
- Variables not controlled: implant surface design, graft type, surgeon experience, patient habits
- Only lateral window approach included — cannot extrapolate to transcrestal
- Mean follow-up 25.15 months — limited long-term data

## Glossary

- **슈나이데리안막 (Schneiderian membrane)**: 상악동의 내측을 덮는 점막; 상악동 거상술 중 천공 시 수복이 필요
- **교차비 (Odds Ratio, OR)**: 두 군 간의 사건 발생 교차 비율; OR<1 = 천공군에서 낮은 생존 경향
- **이질성 (Heterogeneity, I²)**: 메타분석 포함 연구 간 결과의 분산도; I²=0%는 완전 일관성
- **뉴캐슬-오타와 척도 (Newcastle–Ottawa Scale, NOS)**: 비무작위 연구의 비뚤림 위험 평가 도구
- **측방창 접근법 (Lateral Window Approach)**: 상악동 측벽에 창을 내어 거상하는 표준 기법
