## Why Ingested

→ [[implants/mbl/linkevicius-2009-reaction-crestal-bone-around-implants]] reports the same group's three-band mucosal-thickness classification in a larger 2009 cohort (26 patients, 64 implants); this earlier companion trial (19 patients, 46 implants) supplies the separate mesial/distal bone-loss values and the ≤2 mm / 1.45 mm conclusion that the later work relies on. It is the primary source behind the thickness boundary taken as given in [[wiki/implants/mbl/linkevicius-2015-vertical-soft-tissue-thickness-crestal-bone-platform-switching]] and [[wiki/overviews/peri-implant-biologic-width-overview]].

## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial (abstract-only) of 46 implants (23 test placed about 2 mm supracrestally, 23 control at bone level) in 19 patients; test sites were split by probe-measured gingival thickness into thin (A) and thick (B) groups, with intraoral radiographs at placement and 1 year.

Thin-mucosa test implants lost 1.61 +/- 0.24 mm (SE; range, 0.9 to 3.3 mm) mesially and 1.28 +/- 0.167 mm (range, 0.8 to 2.1 mm) distally versus 0.26 +/- 0.08 mm and 0.09 +/- 0.05 mm in thick mucosa, while bone-level controls lost 1.8 +/- 0.164 mm and 1.87 +/- 0.166 mm; ANOVA found a significant thin-vs-thick difference on both aspects.

Initial gingival tissue thickness at the crest significantly influences marginal bone stability — with 2.0 mm or less tissue, crestal bone loss up to 1.45 mm may occur despite a supracrestal implant–abutment interface.

## 세줄요약

전향적 대조 임상시험(초록만 확보) — 환자 19명·임플란트 46개(시험군 23개는 약 2 mm 치조정 상방, 대조군 23개는 골수준 식립); 시험군은 탐침으로 측정한 점막 두께에 따라 얇은 군(A)·두꺼운 군(B)으로 나누고 식립 시와 1년 후 구내 방사선촬영으로 치조정골 변화를 측정.

얇은 점막 시험군 골소실은 근심 1.61 +/- 0.24 mm(SE; 범위 0.9~3.3 mm)·원심 1.28 +/- 0.167 mm(범위 0.8~2.1 mm), 두꺼운 점막군은 근심 0.26 +/- 0.08 mm(범위 0.2~0.9 mm)·원심 0.09 +/- 0.05 mm(범위 0.2~0.6 mm), 골수준 대조군은 근심 1.8 +/- 0.164 mm·원심 1.87 +/- 0.166 mm였고 분산분석(ANOVA, Analysis of Variance)에서 시험군의 얇은·두꺼운 군 간 차이는 근심·원심 모두 유의.

식립 시 치조정 연조직 두께는 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 안정성에 유의한 영향을 준다 — 점막이 2.0 mm 이하면 임플란트-지대주 연결부(Implant–Abutment Interface)를 상방에 두어도 최대 1.45 mm의 골소실이 생길 수 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Crosses **placement depth** (about 2 mm supracrestal vs bone level) with **initial gingival thickness** (thin vs thick) in the same 19-patient cohort, with 23 test and 23 control implants.
- Reports crestal bone loss **separately for mesial and distal aspects** at both placement and 1 year, unlike the companion paper's pooled thickness bands.
- Quantifies the thin-mucosa penalty under supracrestal placement: 1.61 +/- 0.24 mm mesial and 1.28 +/- 0.167 mm distal, versus 0.26 +/- 0.08 mm and 0.09 +/- 0.05 mm in thick mucosa (significant by ANOVA on both aspects).
- States a clinical threshold directly: if tissue thickness is 2.0 mm or less, crestal bone loss up to 1.45 mm may occur even when the implant–abutment interface is positioned supracrestally.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: 1-year prospective controlled clinical trial; randomization or allocation concealment is not described in the available abstract.
- **Population**: 19 patients, 46 implants — 23 test and 23 control.
- **Placement groups**: Test implants placed about 2 mm supracrestally; control implants positioned at the bone level.
- **Thickness assessment**: Tissue thickness at implant sites measured with a periodontal probe before implant placement; test implants then divided into group A (thin) and group B (thick) according to tissue thickness.
- **Restoration**: Metal-ceramic cement-retained prostheses constructed after healing.
- **Outcomes**: Crestal bone changes measured on intraoral radiographs at implant placement and after 1 year, reported mesially and distally.
- **Statistics**: Analysis of variance (ANOVA) compared test A (thin) vs test B (thick) on both aspects; no F statistics or p values are given in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | Mesial bone loss at 1 year | Distal bone loss at 1 year |
|---|---|---|
| Test A (thin mucosa) | 1.61 +/- 0.24 mm (SE; range, 0.9 to 3.3 mm) | 1.28 +/- 0.167 mm (range, 0.8 to 2.1 mm) |
| Test B (thick mucosa) | 0.26 +/- 0.08 mm (range, 0.2 to 0.9 mm) | 0.09 +/- 0.05 mm (range, 0.2 to 0.6 mm) |
| Control (at bone level) | 1.8 +/- 0.164 mm (range, 0.6 to 4.0 mm) | 1.87 +/- 0.166 mm (range, 0.0 to 4.1 mm) |

- Analysis of variance revealed a significant difference in bone loss between test A (thin) and B (thick) groups on both the mesial and the distal aspect; the abstract reports no test statistics.
- Bone-level control implants lost roughly 1.8–1.87 mm regardless of aspect, higher than either supracrestal test subgroup in mean terms.
- The authors' conclusion sets a 2.0 mm tissue-thickness boundary and an upper estimate of 1.45 mm crestal bone loss despite supracrestal placement of the implant–abutment interface.
- Values are transcribed verbatim from the PubMed abstract; the thick-mucosa distal range (0.2 to 0.6 mm) is reproduced as printed even though its lower bound exceeds the printed mean of 0.09 mm — the inconsistency is flagged in §5 rather than silently corrected.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only source**: no PMC record and no PDF were obtained; all numbers above come from the structured abstract alone, with no full Methods/Results body available.
- Randomization is not described; test allocation to thin vs thick groups follows measured tissue thickness, leaving patient- and site-level confounding unaddressed in the available text.
- The abstract gives no numeric definition of "thin" and "thick" beyond the conclusion's 2.0 mm boundary, and no F values or p values for the reported significant ANOVA.
- Two internal tensions are left unresolved by the abstract: the thick-mucosa distal range (0.2 to 0.6 mm) sits above its reported mean (0.09 +/- 0.05 mm), and the conclusion's "up to 1.45 mm" estimate is lower than the reported thin-group mesial mean (1.61 +/- 0.24 mm). Both are carried forward verbatim, not reconciled.
- No implant system, survival data, dropout accounting, or follow-up beyond 1 year is present in the retrieved text.

## 7. Glossary

- **Crestal / marginal bone loss (MBL)**: Radiographic reduction in peri-implant alveolar bone height adjacent to the implant platform; the primary outcome here.
- **Supracrestal placement**: Positioning the implant platform (and implant–abutment interface) about 2 mm above the alveolar crest; the test condition in this trial.
- **Implant–abutment interface (IAJ)**: The implant–abutment junction whose position relative to the crest is central to the authors' conclusion.
- **Thin / thick mucosa**: Test subgroups defined by periodontal-probe measurement of gingival tissue thickness before placement; the conclusion identifies 2.0 mm or less as the at-risk boundary.
- **Analysis of variance (ANOVA)**: Statistical test used to compare mean bone loss across the thin and thick test subgroups on each aspect.
- **Metal-ceramic cement-retained prosthesis**: The restoration type placed after healing in all patients in this trial.
