## Why Ingested

This network meta-analysis of 36 RCTs provides a comprehensive SUCRA-ranked hierarchy of 13 mouthwash types for oral mucositis prevention, directly challenging CHX-centric clinical practice by ranking chlorhexidine last. It complements and extends [[oral-medicine/mucositis/wang-2023-mouthwash-oral-mucositis-sr-ma]], which focused on treatment rather than prevention, together forming a near-complete mouthwash evidence base for OM management.

## Three-line Summary

This network meta-analysis evaluated the effects of 13 different mouthwashes on preventing oral mucositis in patients undergoing radiotherapy and chemotherapy, including 36 RCTs (3457 patients). According to SUCRA rankings, aloe vera juice was most effective for OM prevention, followed by Chinese herbal medicine, Bing Peng San, honey, Lactobacillus brevis, chamomile, riboflavin, Kangfuxin Ye, benzydamine, turmeric, sucralfate, povidone-iodine, and chlorhexidine.

Aloe vera juice, Chinese herbal medicine, and honey are significantly more effective than povidone-iodine, chlorhexidine, and placebo for preventing oral mucositis.

## 세줄요약

본 네트워크 메타분석은 방사선 및 항암화학요법을 받는 환자의 구강점막염(OM) 예방에 대한 13가지 구강세정제의 효과를 36개의 무작위대조시험(RCT, 3457명 환자)을 통해 평가하였다. SUCRA 순위 결과, 알로에 베라 주스가 OM 예방에 가장 효과적이었으며, 그 다음으로 한약재, 빙붕산, 꿀, 락토바실러스 브레비스, 카모마일, 리보플라빈, 강복신액, 벤지다민, 심황, 수크랄페이트, 포비돈-아이오딘, 클로르헥시딘 순이었다.

알로에 베라 주스, 한약재, 꿀은 포비돈-아이오딘, 클로르헥시딘, 위약보다 구강점막염 예방에 유의하게 더 효과적이었다.

- Chinese herbal medicine ranked second for OM prevention.
- Bing Peng San ranked third for OM prevention.
- Honey ranked fourth for OM prevention.
- Aloe vera juice, Chinese herbal medicine, and honey significantly more effective than PVP-I, CHX, and placebo (p < 0.05).
- 13 mouthwashes evaluated across 36 RCTs (3457 patients).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Network meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
- Databases: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, CNKI (China National Knowledge Infrastructure). Searched up to April 2023.
- PRISMA compliance: Yes.
- Inclusion criteria: RCTs on mouthwash for OM prevention in patients undergoing chemoradiotherapy. No pre-existing OM. Outcome: incidence of OM. Published in Chinese or English.
- Excluded: Patients with concomitant oral medications for OM, incomplete data, duplicates, conference abstracts, inconsistent data types.
- Included: 36 RCTs (3457 patients), 13 mouthwashes.
- Risk of bias: Assessed using Cochrane RoB tool (RevMan 5.3).
- Data synthesis: Network meta-analysis using Stata 16.2. SUCRA probability ranking. Consistency model used (p = 0.945 for inconsistency). Direct meta-analysis with RevMan 5.3 (random-effects model).
- Heterogeneity: Assessed via I² and closed-loop heterogeneity tests.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **SUCRA ranking (best to worst)** — 1. Aloe vera juice, 2. Chinese herbal medicine, 3. Bing Peng San, 4. Honey, 5. Lactobacillus brevis, 6. Chamomile, 7. Riboflavin, 8. Kangfuxin Ye, 9. Benzydamine, 10. Turmeric, 11. Sucralfate, 12. Povidone-iodine, 13. Chlorhexidine
- **Aloe vera juice vs Placebo** — OR = 0.52 (95% CI: 0.46 to 0.59, p < 0.001, I² = 100%)
- **Honey vs Placebo** — OR = 0.76 (95% CI: 0.69 to 0.83, p < 0.001, I² = 97%)
- **Benzydamine vs Placebo** — OR = 0.58 (95% CI: 0.44 to 0.77, p < 0.001, I² = 0%)
- **Chlorhexidine vs Placebo** — OR = 1.19 (95% CI: 0.92 to 1.53, p = 0.18, I² = 90%)
- **Total (all comparisons)** — OR = 0.75 (95% CI: 0.72 to 0.79, p < 0.001, I² = 88.3%)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High heterogeneity (I² = 88.3%) overall; heterogeneity remained after subgroup analysis.
- Most studies (34 of 36) were two-arm trials; limited direct comparisons.
- 12 studies had missing results (high attrition bias risk in some).
- Only 18 studies mentioned randomization method; 5 did not describe randomization.
- Only 6 studies mentioned blinding methods.
- Publication bias possible (funnel plot asymmetry with small studies).
- Need for more high-quality RCTs with larger sample sizes to confirm findings.

## 7. Glossary

- **OM (Oral Mucositis)**: 구강점막염 — 방사선 및 항암화학요법으로 인한 구강 점막의 염증 및 궤양성 질환
- **SUCRA (Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking)**: 누적 순위 곡선하면적 — 네트워크 메타분석에서 여러 중재법의 효과 순위를 평가하는 지표
- **NMA (Network Meta-Analysis)**: 네트워크 메타분석 — 직접 및 간접 비교를 통해 여러 중재법을 동시에 평가하는 방법
- **PVP-I (Povidone-Iodine)**: 포비돈-아이오딘 — 요오드포르 항균제
- **CHX (Chlorhexidine)**: 클로르헥시딘 — 광범위 항균 구강세정제
- **OR (Odds Ratio)**: 교차비 — 사건 발생 오즈의 비율
- **Kangfuxin Ye**: 강복신액 — 중국 전통 의약 성분의 구강세정제
