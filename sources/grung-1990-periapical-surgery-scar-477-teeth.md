## Why Ingested

Classic retrospective reference establishing the 9% post-surgical periapical scar tissue incidence from a single-surgeon series of 477 teeth. Provides foundational epidemiology for the periapical fibrous scar (반흔 치유) literature and directly informs clinical counseling on expected healing outcomes after periapical surgery. Establishes that large preoperative lesions and retrograde fills are key risk factors for scar tissue healing rather than complete bone regeneration.

→ [[endodontics/diagnosis/grung-1990-periapical-surgery-scar-477-teeth]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective follow-up study (Norwegian county hospital, single surgeon, n=477 teeth): periapical surgery outcomes classified into 4 radiographic groups — complete healing 78%, scar tissue 9%, uncertain 4%, unsatisfactory 9%.

Periapical fibrous scar (incomplete healing, Group 2) incidence was strongly associated with large preoperative lesion size; retrograde fill technique showed markedly higher failure rate (28%) versus orthograde (4%).

Established the canonical ~9% post-surgical scar tissue rate and validated scar tissue as a "success" outcome (combined success rate 87%), distinguishing it from true endodontic failure.

## 세줄요약

노르웨이 지방병원 단일 술자 후향적 추적(n=477치): 치근단 수술 (Periapical Surgery) 결과를 방사선 4군 분류 — 완전 치유 (Complete Healing) 78%, 반흔 치유 (Scar Tissue Healing) 9%, 불확실 4%, 불만족 9%.

치근단 반흔 (Periapical Fibrous Scar) 발생률은 수술 전 병소 크기와 강한 양의 연관; 역방향 충전 (Retrograde Fill) 실패율 28% vs 순방향 (Orthograde) 4%.

반흔 치유를 성공으로 편입할 경우 전체 성공률 87% — 반흔을 근관 치료 실패가 아닌 대안적 치유 경로로 개념화한 선구적 근거.

## Key Data

| Outcome Group | Description | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 | Complete healing | 78% |
| Group 2 | Incomplete healing (scar tissue) | 9% |
| Group 3 | Uncertain healing | ~4% |
| Group 4 | Unsatisfactory healing | ~9% |
| **Success total** | Groups 1+2 | **87%** |
| **Failure total** | Groups 3+4 | **13%** |

Risk factors for scar tissue: large preoperative periapical lesion; retrograde obturation technique (28% failure vs 4% orthograde).
