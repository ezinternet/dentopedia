## Why Ingested

Strongest quantitative evidence for graftless lateral SFE with individual-patient-data meta-analysis — pooled survival 97.5%, 5-year individual survival 93.1%; extends [[sinus-lift/lateral/riben-2012-maxillary-sinus-membrane-elevation-graftless]] and [[sinus-lift/lateral/hegde-2016-sinus-augmentation-membrane-elevation-sr]] with rigorous statistical analysis.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review combined with both individual-patient-data (IPD) and aggregate-data meta-analysis (11 observational studies, 660 implants, 17 failures) of graftless lateral sinus floor elevation with simultaneous implant placement, using Freeman-Tukey double-arcsine transformation for survival pooling and Cox proportional hazards regression for the IPD component.

Pooled survival rate was 97.5% (95% CI 95.8–98.9%); mean bone height gain was 5.7 mm (95% CI 4.78–6.56); membrane perforation rate was 20.0%; 5-year individual-data survival was 93.1%; implants longer than 13 mm showed a protective hazard ratio of 0.64 (95% CI 0.41–0.99).

Graftless lateral SFE achieves high survival rates comparable to grafted approaches, with 5.7 mm mean bone gain driven by the blood-clot scaffold mechanism; using implants >13 mm in length further reduces failure risk, making implant length selection an important clinical decision in graftless cases.

## 세줄요약

이식재 없는 측방 상악동저 거상술 + 동시 임플란트에 대한 체계적 문헌고찰 + 개별환자 데이터(IPD) 및 집계 데이터 이중 메타분석(관찰연구 11편, 임플란트 660개, 실패 17개); Freeman-Tukey 이중호변환 적용, IPD 파트는 Cox 비례위험 회귀 분석.

통합 생존율 97.5%(95% CI 95.8–98.9%), 평균 골 증가 5.7mm(95% CI 4.78–6.56), 막 천공률 20.0%; 5년 개별환자 생존율 93.1%; 13mm 초과 임플란트의 위험비(Hazard Ratio, HR) 0.64(95% CI 0.41–0.99)로 보호 효과 확인.

무이식 측방 상악동거상술은 이식재 사용 술식에 상응하는 생존율과 평균 5.7mm 골증가를 달성하며, 13mm 초과 임플란트 선택이 실패 위험을 추가로 낮추는 임상적 의사결정 인자임.

## One-line Summary

SR + individual- and aggregate-data MA (11 studies, 660 implants, 17 failures): pooled survival 97.5%; mean bone height gain 5.7mm; 5-year individual-data survival 93.1%; longer implants (>13mm) associated with lower hazard ratio; membrane perforation rate 20.0%.

## 한줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 개별환자·집계 이중 메타분석 (11편, 660개 임플란트): 통합 생존율 97.5%; 평균 골 증가 5.7mm; 5년 개별환자 생존율 93.1%; 긴 임플란트(>13mm) 위험비 감소; 막 천공률 20.0%.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pooled survival rate: 97.5% (95% CI 95.8–98.9%)
- Mean bone height increase: 5.7 mm (95% CI 4.78–6.56)
- Mean MBL: 1.2 mm (95% CI 0.84–1.51)
- ISQ at follow-up: 70.5 (95% CI 64.2–76.9)
- Membrane perforation rate: 20.0% (95% CI 10.9–30.6)
- Sinus infection rate: 3.0% (95% CI 0.5–6.8)
- Individual-data 5-year survival: 93.1% (SE 2.67%), mean survival time 6.7 years
- Longer implants (>13mm length) had hazard ratio 0.64 (95% CI 0.41–0.99) — protective
- Conclusion: Graftless lateral SFE is safe and effective with high survival rates, especially for implants >13mm

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study type**: Observational studies (prospective + retrospective) with graftless lateral SFE and simultaneous implant
- **Survival**: Freeman-Tukey double-arcsine transformation for pooling
- **Bone gain/MBL**: Changes from baseline meta-analysis with sampling variance
- **ISQ**: Analyzed at follow-up
- **IPD analysis**: Kaplan-Meier + Cox proportional hazards regression; one-step approach
- **Secondary outcomes**: Bone height gain, MBL, ISQ, membrane perforation, sinus infection

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only observational studies eligible (no RCTs for graftless lateral at time of search)
- Significant heterogeneity in study designs, follow-up, implant types
- IPD analysis limited to studies sharing individual data
- No subgroup by RBH
- Abstract-only accessible — full results from abstract text extraction

## 6. Related Work

- Riben 2012: Qualitative SR predecessor; describes mechanism; all studies showed high survival
- Hegde 2016: SR 1997–2014; survival 79.9–100%; heterogeneity precluded pooling
- Del Fabbro 2025: SR of 49 RCTs for grafted lateral SFE (comparison)

## 7. Glossary

- **IPD meta-analysis (개별환자 메타분석)**: uses raw patient-level data rather than published study-level aggregates; more precise
- **Freeman-Tukey transformation (프리먼-튜키 변환)**: variance-stabilizing method for proportions in meta-analysis
- **HR (Hazard Ratio, 위험비)**: Cox regression-derived; HR<1 = protective (lower failure risk)
