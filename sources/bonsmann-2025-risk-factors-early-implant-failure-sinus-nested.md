## Three-line Summary

Nested case-control study (8 German centers, 2016–2021; n=129 early-failure cases + 273 controls) of risk factors for implant failure within 12 months following sinus augmentation, analyzed by multivariable logistic regression.

Smoking, history of periodontitis, simultaneous vs delayed implant placement, and sinus floor anatomy emerged as significant risk factors with adjusted odds ratios reported in the paper.

This first sinus-augmentation-specific multicenter nested case-control design quantifies confounder-adjusted risk, though generalizability is limited by single-country sampling and the 12-month early-failure endpoint only.

## 세줄요약

다기관 nested case-control 연구 (독일 8개 센터, 2016–2021; 조기 실패 129례 + 대조 273례): 상악동 거상술 후 12개월 내 임플란트 조기 실패 위험인자를 다변수 로지스틱 회귀로 분석.

흡연, 치주염 기왕력, 동시 vs 지연 식립, 상악동 바닥 해부학적 형태가 유의한 위험인자로 보정 교차비(adjusted OR) 보고됨.

최초의 상악동 특이적 다기관 nested case-control 디자인으로 교란인자 보정 위험을 정량화하나, 독일 단일국가 표본과 12개월 이내 조기 실패만 평가한 한계가 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 첫 sinus augmentation-specific 다기관 nested case-control 디자인.
- 12개월 내 조기 실패 risk factors 정량.
- Multivariable confounder-adjusted OR.

## 3. Methodology
Nested case-control across 8 German centers (2016-2021); cases n=129 (early implant failure within 12 months); controls n=273; multivariable logistic regression.

## 4. Key Results
[근거강함] Specific OR for risk factors (smoking, periodontitis history, simultaneous vs delayed placement, sinus floor anatomy) reported in paper.

## 5. Limitations
- Single-country (Germany) 8 centers
- Early failure only (no long-term)
- Nested case-control selection bias risk
