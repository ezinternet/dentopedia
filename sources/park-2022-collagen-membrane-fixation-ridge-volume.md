## Why Ingested

Membrane fixation is a debated technical variable in 유도골재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR); [[wiki/bone-regeneration/wessing-2018-gbr-collagen-membranes-particulate-graft-sr-ma]] included fixation as one of four SR+MA moderators but lacked dedicated animal experimental data isolating the fixation variable across different membrane types. This Park 2022 beagle dog study directly fills that gap with controlled fixation vs. non-fixation comparison across two commercially distinct non-cross-linked collagen membranes.

## Three-line Summary

Animal study (8 beagle dogs) comparing collagen membrane fixation (CM1-F/CM1-UF, CM2-F/CM2-UF) on ridge volume stability and new bone formation in contained GBR defects, with micro-CT and histomorphometric analysis at 8 weeks.
Membrane fixation showed no significant effect on total augmented volume for either membrane type (p > .05); however, CM2-F had significantly greater augmented tissue width at the ridge crest than CM2-UF (2.3 ± 0.1 mm vs. 1.57 ± 0.27 mm, p < .05), whereas CM1 showed no such difference.
Fixation does not universally enhance ridge volume stability in contained defects; its benefit on coronal ridge width is membrane-type dependent, suggesting the structural properties of the membrane modulate whether fixation matters.

함입된 결손부(contained defect)에서 차폐막 고정은 전체 치조제 용적 안정성을 보편적으로 개선하지 않으며, 치관부 폭 증가 효과는 차폐막 종류에 따라 달라짐 — 차폐막의 구조적 특성이 고정 효과의 크기를 조절하는 핵심 변수임을 시사.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to directly compare fixation vs. non-fixation of two distinct non-cross-linked collagen membranes in the same animal model with contained defects
- Demonstrates that total ridge volume stability is not improved by membrane fixation regardless of membrane type
- Identifies a membrane-type-dependent effect of fixation specifically at the coronal (crest) level of the augmented ridge
- Establishes that the structural properties of the membrane itself modulate whether pin/tack fixation provides additional benefit

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: In vivo animal experiment (randomized controlled, bilateral split-mouth model)
- **Subjects**: 8 beagle dogs
- **Defect**: Box-shaped defect bilaterally after maxillary pre-molar extraction; all defects augmented with particulate bone substitute material
- **Groups**: CM1-F (collagen membrane 1, fixed), CM1-UF (CM1, unfixed), CM2-F (collagen membrane 2, fixed), CM2-UF (CM2, unfixed); 두 종류 모두 비교차결합 콜라겐 차폐막 (non-cross-linked collagen membrane)
- **Observation period**: 8 weeks
- **Outcomes**: Total augmented volume (micro-CT), augmented tissue width at ridge crest (histomorphometry)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only: full quantitative data on bone formation (BIC, BA%) and micro-CT volume metrics not available from retrieved text
- Animal model (beagle dog) — does not directly translate to human clinical conditions
- Only 8 weeks follow-up — long-term stability after membrane resorption not assessed
- Only contained defects studied — results may not apply to non-contained/vertical defects where fixation may be more critical
- Membrane identity (CM1, CM2) not disclosed in abstract — limits direct clinical extrapolation to specific commercial products

## 7. Glossary

- **Guided bone regeneration (GBR) / 유도골재생술**: Technique using a barrier membrane to exclude fast-growing soft tissue from a bone defect, allowing slower-growing osteogenic cells to fill the space
- **Non-cross-linked collagen membrane / 비교차결합 콜라겐 차폐막**: Resorbable collagen barrier without chemical cross-linking — resorbs faster, more biocompatible, but potentially less volume-stable under mechanical load
- **Membrane fixation / 차폐막 고정**: Securing the barrier membrane to the bone surface using pins or tacks to prevent membrane displacement during healing
- **Contained defect / 함입 결손부**: Bone defect surrounded by bone walls on all or most sides, providing natural scaffolding; mechanical stability of the graft is inherently supported
- **Histomorphometry / 조직형태계측**: Quantitative microscopic measurement of tissue dimensions and composition from histological sections
- **Micro-CT / 마이크로 전산화 단층촬영**: High-resolution X-ray imaging technique for quantifying bone volume and architecture in small specimens
