## Three-line Summary

Synthesis article integrating outcomes from a 3-year prospective cohort and retrospective registry analysis of cracked tooth syndrome (CTS) conducted by the National Dental Practice-Based Research Network (PBRN), led by Oregon Health & Science University. Consolidated findings from 8 published articles spanning 2017–2022 examining clinical presentation, treatment patterns, and outcomes of posterior cracked teeth. Largest multi-site real-world cohort of CTS in North American dental practice.

Clinical registry addressed three core questions relevant to patient management: Which cracked teeth progress and worsen? When should practitioners intervene with definitive treatment? What is the most effective treatment approach? Findings identified deep cracks, symptomatic presentation, pulp involvement, and vital pulp status as predictive factors for disease progression. Data supported cuspal coverage restorations as preferred treatment, showing superior outcomes to monitoring alone or nonsurgical management in most cases.

Observational design without randomization limits causal inference about treatment effectiveness. Registry practice participant population may differ systematically from general dentistry, introducing selection bias. Lessons learned synthesis format prioritizes clinical relevance over formal meta-analysis; readers must consult original 8 articles for detailed methodology and statistical analysis. Despite limitations, represents largest contemporary real-world evidence for CTS natural history and treatment outcomes in managed-care dental networks.

## 세줄요약

National Dental PBRN(Oregon Health & Science University 주도)의 3년 다기관 전향적 cohort + 후향적 레지스트리: 후방 균열치 임상 특성·치료·결과. 2017~2022 8편 발표 논문 통합. 북미 최대 규모 균열치 실제 cohort.

3가지 핵심 임상 질문: (1) 어떤 균열치가 악화? (2) 언제 개입? (3) 최선 치료? → 심한 crack·증상·치수 관여·활성 치수는 진행 예측인자. Cuspal coverage 수복 우선, 경과관찰/비수술 대비 우수.

관찰 무작위화 미보유로 인과성 제약. PBRN 참여 임상→ selection bias. Lessons learned 형식은 임상성 우선, 원문 8편 상세 방법론 참조 필요. 제한점에도 managed-care network의 현재 균열치 자연사·치료 결과 최대 근거.

## 3. Methodology
- Multi-site observational study (National Dental PBRN)
- 8 published articles (2017-2022)
- 후방 cracked teeth 한정
- 3-year follow-up

## 4. Key Lessons (3 questions)
- **Which get worse**: deep crack, symptomatic, pulp involvement, vital pulp
- **When to intervene**: spontaneous pain, periapical change, deep crack with pulp risk
- **Best treatment**: PBRN 데이터는 cuspal coverage 강력 권장 (Kaur 2026 umbrella와 일관)

## 5. Limitations
- Observational — RCT 아님
- PBRN 참여 임상 → selection bias
- Lessons learned는 narrative — 본문 8 article 직접 참조 필요
