# Katanec 2026 — Simvastatin + Cerabone® Socket Preservation: Negative Histomorphometric RCT

## Why Ingested

Katanec 2026은 스타틴(statin) × 치조와 보존(socket preservation) 교차점의 유일한 split-mouth RCT로, cerabone® 이종이식재에 심바스타틴(simvastatin) 1mg을 단순 혼합했을 때 조직형태계측(histomorphometry) 지표에 유의차가 없었음을 보여준다. 이 부정적 결과는 [[overviews/statin-bone-healing-implant-osseointegration-overview]]의 "서방형 전달 플랫폼 없는 국소 스타틴 적용은 효과 없음" 결론을 직접 뒷받침하며, 스타틴 전달 방식이 결과를 좌우한다는 핵심 메커니즘 분기를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

**EN:** n=9 split-mouth RCT; cerabone® plus ± simvastatin 1 mg/0.2 mL saline, 3-month trephine biopsy histomorphometry. No significant differences: NB/TV 31.0% vs 26.2% (p=0.45), CT/TV 59.1% vs 64.3% (p=0.31), BSM/TV 9.9% vs 9.5% (p=0.91). Negative result — simple admixture of simvastatin with a slowly resorbing xenograft does not enhance early mineralized tissue formation; controlled-release platforms needed for future translation.

**KO:** n=9 분할구강 RCT; cerabone® plus ± 심바스타틴 1mg, 3개월 골 생검 조직형태계측. NB/TV(31.0% vs 26.2%, p=0.45), CT/TV, BSM/TV 모두 유의차 없음. 부정적 결과 — 이종이식재에 심바스타틴을 단순 혼합하는 방식으로는 초기 무기질화 조직 형성이 향상되지 않음; 서방형 전달 시스템 필요.

## 세줄요약

n=9 분할구강 RCT; cerabone® plus ± 심바스타틴 (simvastatin) 1 mg/0.2 mL 식염수, 3개월 골 코어 생검으로 조직형태계측 (histomorphometry) 시행.
신생골 (Newly Formed Bone, NB) /총조직용적 (Total Volume, TV) 31.0% vs 26.2% (p=0.45), 결합조직 (Connective Tissue, CT) /TV 59.1% vs 64.3% (p=0.31), 잔류 이식재 (Bone Substitute Material, BSM) /TV 9.9% vs 9.5% (p=0.91) — 모두 유의차 없음.
부정적 결과 (negative result): 이종이식재 (xenograft) 에 스타틴 단순 혼합으로는 초기 골형성 향상 없음; 서방형 전달 시스템과 더 큰 표본의 RCT 필요.

## Study Design

- **Type:** Prospective split-mouth clinical pilot RCT
- **Sample:** n=9 adults (5F/4M; mean 56 years, range 29–79); bilateral extraction requiring contralateral-quadrant socket preservation
- **Randomization:** Computer-generated sequence (randomizer.org), allocation concealed
- **Ethics:** University of Zagreb School of Dental Medicine Ethics Committee (05-PA-30-III-12/2021), Declaration of Helsinki 2013
- **Exclusion:** Metabolic bone disease, bisphosphonate/corticosteroid/systemic statin use; untreated periodontitis, active infection

## Interventions

| Site | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Control | cerabone® plus (0.5–1 mm granules, Botiss) hydrated in sterile saline |
| Experimental | cerabone® plus + simvastatin 1 mg/0.2 mL saline (Calbiochem, USA) immediately before placement |

Both sites covered with Jason® resorbable collagen membrane (Botiss); primary closure with 4-0 monofilament. Postoperative: amoxicillin 500 mg × 5 days, 0.12% chlorhexidine rinses × 1 week.

## Biopsy & Histomorphometry

- 3-month trephine biopsy (Ø 2 mm, depth 8 mm) from socket center during implant site preparation
- Embedding in methyl methacrylate, ~50 µm sections; stains: toluidine blue + Masson's trichrome
- Blinded analysis; ZEN Core v3.0 + ImageJ v1.53; 3 high-magnification fields/section, ICC > 0.90 inter-observer
- Quantified: NB/TV (newly formed bone), BSM/TV (residual bone substitute), CT/TV (connective tissue)

## Results

| Parameter | cerabone® Plus | cerabone® + Statin | p-value | Cohen's d |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NB/TV (%) | 31.0 ± 15.5 | 26.2 ± 11.6 | 0.45 | 0.27 |
| CT/TV (%) | 59.1 ± 12.0 | 64.3 ± 9.4 | 0.31 | −0.36 |
| BSM/TV (%) | 9.9 ± 8.5 | 9.5 ± 9.7 | 0.91 | 0.03 |

No statistically significant differences on paired t-test or Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Modest non-significant CT/TV trend (~+5%) in statin group may reflect early fibrovascular/angiogenic activity rather than impaired osteogenesis. No adverse events, no graft exposure, no systemic/local statin-related complications.

## Discussion Points

1. Lack of effect consistent with dose/carrier dependency — simple admixture does not provide controlled release; dense xenograft may restrict drug diffusion
2. BMP-2-mediated osteoanabolic effect requires sustained low-micromolar concentrations; single-injection bolus likely clears or sequesters rapidly
3. Small CT/TV shift plausibly reflects early VEGF/eNOS fibrovascular phase that precedes mineralisation — not definitively negative for bone long-term
4. Contrasts with animal calvarial/mandibular models and some periodontal RCTs; confirms translational gap noted in recent SRs
5. No pharmacokinetic data for drug release from cerabone® — critical limitation for mechanistic interpretation

## Limitations

- n=9; exploratory pilot, no a priori power calculation; wide CIs; underpowered for small effects
- Split-mouth cannot fully eliminate site-specific anatomy/vascular confounders
- No molecular markers (BMP-2, VEGF, osteocalcin)
- Statin release kinetics from cerabone® not characterized
- Single 2-mm trephine may not capture spatial heterogeneity within socket
- 3-month endpoint only; no sequential assessment to distinguish transient from durable effects

## Conclusion

Within this pilot, 1 mg local simvastatin + cerabone® plus = histologically neutral at 3 months vs cerabone® alone. Biologically safe. Future research priority: controlled-release delivery platforms (hydrogels, microspheres, electrospun membranes) + pharmacokinetic characterization + molecular endpoints + larger powered RCTs.
