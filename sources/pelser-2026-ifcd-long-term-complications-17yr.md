## Why Ingested

No held paper provided ≥15yr follow-up for IFCDs with Kaplan-Meier complication-free survival, Andersen-Gill recurrent event analysis, and material-specific HR comparisons (Ti-resin vs ceramic veneered). This fills the long-term complication profile gap with the longest follow-up available (up to 17yr) and quantified material HR differences.

[[implants/full-arch/pelser-2026-ifcd-long-term-complications-17yr]]
## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study of 91 IFCDs (498 implants, 72 patients, 2003–2023), mean follow-up 6.8yr (range 0.5–17yr); Kaplan-Meier for time-to-first-complication; Andersen-Gill Cox regression for recurrent complications; material comparison (RV vs CV, base-metal-ceramic vs Ti-resin).

IFCD survival 92.3%; 4.2% implant explantation; 49.5% complication-free; 169 total complications (recurrent included); RV vs CV prosthesis survival NS (p=0.85) but CV better veneer fracture-free survival (p=0.0094); Ti-resin HR=4.25 (p=0.0015) overall complication risk and HR=7.11 (p=0.0029) veneer fracture risk vs base-metal-alloy-ceramic.

Technical complications dominate long-term; veneer fractures most frequent; material choice (Ti-resin vs ceramic-veneered metal) is the strongest modifiable predictor of complication risk.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Longest IFCD follow-up study with Kaplan-Meier and Andersen-Gill recurrent event Cox regression
- Ti-resin IFCD: HR=4.25 total complications vs base-metal-ceramic — a 4x greater complication burden
- Ti-resin veneer fracture HR=7.11 — specific complication type quantified
- Veneer fracture-free survival significantly better for ceramic veneered (p=0.0094), even though overall prosthesis survival was equivalent
- 49.5% complication-free over follow-up — implying most long-term IFCDs will experience ≥1 complication

## 4. Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| IFCD survival | **92.3%** (7 failed) |
| Implant explantation | **4.2%** (21/498) |
| Complication-free prostheses | **49.5%** |
| Total complications | 169 (including recurrent) |
| Dominant complication type | Technical (veneer fractures) |
| RV vs CV prosthesis survival | NS (p=0.85) |
| RV vs CV veneer fracture-free survival | CV better (p=0.0094) |
| Ti-resin vs base-metal-ceramic HR (total) | **4.25** (p=0.0015) |
| Ti-resin vs base-metal-ceramic HR (veneer fracture) | **7.11** (p=0.0029) |

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract only — Kaplan-Meier curves, subgroup details, cumulative complication rates not available
- Single center (Mainz); case mix may differ from private practice
- Heterogeneous material types over 20-year study period
