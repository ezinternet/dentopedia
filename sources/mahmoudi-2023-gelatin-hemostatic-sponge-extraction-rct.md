## Why Ingested
신규 젤라틴 기반 생분해성 지혈 스펀지 vs 상업용 Gelfoam을 split-mouth 이중맹검 RCT로 비교한 연구. [[oral-surgery/dinkova-2025-local-hemostasis-oral-surgery-review]] 지혈제 비교 리뷰의 젤라틴 스펀지 카테고리 직접 임상 데이터.

## One-line Summary
Split-mouth double-blind RCT (n=26) showing a novel cross-linked gelatin sponge significantly outperformed commercial Gelfoam in bleeding control at 1h (p=0.003), 1–4h (p=0.002), and 4h+ (p=0.042), and significantly reduced postextraction pain (VAS, p<0.05) with only one dry socket in control group.

## 한줄요약
분구강 이중맹검 RCT(n=26): 신규 가교 젤라틴 스펀지가 상업용 Gelfoam 대비 출혈 조절(1h p=0.003), 통증 감소(p<0.05) 유의미하게 우수; dry socket은 대조군 1건만 발생.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Novel cross-linked gelatin sponge superior to Gelfoam across all bleeding time points
- Absorbed blood gauze weight: 3.97±1.1 g (test) vs 6.32±1.06 g (control) — 37% reduction
- Split-mouth double-blind design eliminates patient-level confounders
- Dry socket: 1 case in control, 0 in test group
- Pain reduction greater for novel sponge (p<0.05) across 12, 24, 48h VAS

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: split-mouth randomized double-blind clinical trial
- N: 26 patients; 2 bilateral mandibular molar extractions per patient
- Intervention: novel cross-linked gelatin sponge (test) vs commercial gelatin sponge/Gelfoam (control)
- Hemostasis measurement: sterile gauze weight (absorbed blood) at 1h and 1–4h post-extraction
- Bleeding recorded at 1h, 1–4h, and >4h post-extraction
- Pain: VAS at 12, 24, 48h
- Dry socket assessment: clinical exam at day 4

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Gauze blood absorption: 3.97±1.1 g (test) vs 6.32±1.06 g (control), p<0.05
- Bleeding reduced in test group: 1h (p=0.003), 1–4h (p=0.002), >4h (p=0.042)
- Pain reduction significantly higher in test group (p<0.05) at 12, 24, 48h
- Dry socket: 1 case (control), 0 cases (test)
- Average pain decreased over time in both groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=26)
- Mandibular molars only; extrapolation to other teeth limited
- No long-term follow-up beyond day 4
- Novel sponge preparation not commercially available (experimental material)
- Blinding of operator not described

## 6. Related Work
- Gelfoam: widely used commercial porcine gelatin sponge hemostatic agent
- Cross-linked gelatin: improved mechanical strength and hydrolysis resistance vs uncrosslinked
- Mahmoudi A et al. prior study: gelatin porous sponge reducing dry socket prevalence

## 7. Glossary
- Gelfoam: commercial absorbable gelatin sponge hemostatic material (porcine skin gelatin)
- Post-extraction bleeding (PEB): abnormal bleeding persisting 8–12h post-extraction
- Dry socket (alveolar osteitis): failure of blood clot formation/retention post-extraction
- Cross-linking: physical, chemical, or enzymatic modification improving gelatin mechanical stability
- Split-mouth design: bilateral paired treatment allows within-patient control eliminating systemic confounders
