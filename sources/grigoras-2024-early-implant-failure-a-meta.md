## Why Ingested

보철 수복 **전** 초기 실패(early implant failure)의 국소·전신 위험인자 코호트 근거를 보강한다. 기존 [[implants/survival/manzano-2016-risk-factors-in-early-implant]]·[[implants/survival/borba-2017-risk-factors-for-implant-failure]]·[[implants/survival/yari-2023-risk-factors-early-implant-failure]]가 강조하는 상악 우세·남성 우세·당뇨 취약성과 **정면으로 어긋나는 결과**(상·하악 동일, 여성 우세, 당뇨 무관)를 보고해 — 초기 실패 위험인자가 여전히 합의되지 않았음을 보여주는 반증 데이터 포인트다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective 7-year single-practice cohort (213 patients, 810 implants; 2016–2023, Târgu Mureș, Romania) analyzing early implant failure (non-osseointegration before prosthetic loading) against age, sex, insertion region, implant type, and general health.

Non-osseointegration rate 2.71% (22/810); 77.3% (17/22) of failed implants occurred in patients with affected general health, and failed implants were most common at 10 mm length (40.9%); sex difference in failed-implant diameters was significant (p < 0.001).

The study found *no* significant association with diabetes, cardiovascular disease, or hypertension, and equal maxilla/mandible loss with female predominance — both contradicting the mainstream literature (Manzano: maxilla predominance; male predominance), so it should be read as a dissenting single-operator dataset, not a risk-factor confirmation.

## 세줄요약

7년 단일 개인병원 후향 코호트(환자 213명·임플란트 810개, 2016–2023, 루마니아 트르구무레슈) — 나이·성별·식립부위·임플란트 종류·전신건강 상태에 따른 보철 전 초기 실패(골유착 실패) 분석.

골유착 실패율 2.71%(22/810), 실패 임플란트의 77.3%(17/22)가 전신질환 환자에서 발생, 실패 최다 길이는 10 mm(40.9%), 성별 간 실패 임플란트 직경 차이 유의(p < 0.001).

당뇨·심혈관질환·고혈압과 유의 연관 **없음**, 상·하악 실패 동일 및 여성 우세 — 주류 문헌(Manzano: 상악 우세, 남성 우세)과 배치되므로 **확증이 아닌 반증 단일 술자 데이터**로 읽어야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Reports a 2.71% early non-osseointegration rate over 810 implants placed across 7 years in a single private practice — close to rates cited by Derks, van Steenberghe, Tolstunov, Quirynen, Chrcanovic, Roos-Jansåker.
- Finds 77.3% of failures concentrated in patients with compromised general health, supporting a host-susceptibility axis for pre-prosthetic failure.
- Explicitly reports results that *contradict* the received literature (no diabetes/CVD/hypertension association; equal maxilla/mandible distribution; female predominance), making it a useful counterweight to consensus risk-factor claims.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective observational cohort ("meta-analysis of 7 years of experience" in the title, but the study design is a single-center retrospective series), 2016–2023.
- **n**: 213 patients (78 male, 135 female; age 21–78) → 810 implants.
- **Implants**: screw-type, conical connection, platform-switching and non-platform-switching; diameters narrow <3.5 mm / standard 3.6–4.5 mm; lengths 8, 10, 11.5, 13, 16 mm.
- **Adjuncts**: vestibular bone addition in 103 upper (12.7%) and 63 lower (7.8%) implants; external sinus lift with Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide membrane in lateral regions.
- **Protocol**: 0.20% chlorhexidine rinse 45 s; articaine 4% + 1:100,000 adrenaline; amoxicillin-clavulanate 1000 mg BID from the day before surgery (clindamycin 300 mg TID if penicillin-allergic); follow-up at 1, 3, 6 months; failure defined by peri-implant infection, radiolucency, >1 mm bone resorption in year one, persistent pain, suppuration, or foreign-body sensation.
- **Outcomes**: non-osseointegration (implant removed before final prosthesis) vs age, sex, region, implant type/diameter/length, general health.

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Total implants inserted | 810 (women 421 / 52.0%, men 389 / 48.0%) |
| Early non-osseointegration | 22 implants (2.71%) |
| Failures in patients with compromised general health | 17 (77.3%) |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single private practice, single operator → results entangled with operator skill and local protocol; not generalizable.
- Titled "meta-analysis" but contains no pooled synthesis or between-study statistics — it is a retrospective series analyzed with SPSS descriptive/T-tests. Treat the label as misleading.
- Very few failure events (n=22) → low statistical power for every subgroup comparison; the null findings for diabetes/CVD/hypertension may be type-II error, not true absence of effect.
- No control for implant brand/surface beyond platform-switching status; systemic-condition groups are small and heterogeneity is unquantified.
- No adjustment for smoking, which the wiki's own SR evidence ([[implants/survival/fan-2024-smoking-early-implant-failure-sr-ma]]) ranks as a major early-failure risk factor.

## 7. Glossary
- **Early implant failure**: implant lost due to failure to achieve osseointegration, before placement/loading of the final prosthesis.
- **Non-osseointegration**: absence of direct bone-to-implant contact; measured here as clinical mobility + radiographic radiolucency before prosthetic loading.
- **Platform switching (PS)**: abutment narrower than the implant platform, shifting the microgap inward; also non-PS (platform-matched) designs were used.
- **Vestibular bone addition (VBA)**: lateral/ridge augmentation to gain buccal bone support at placement.
- **Proton pump inhibitor (PPI) link**: PPI-mediated osteoclast V-ATPase inhibition proposed as a bone-turnover modifier; no direct association found in this cohort.
