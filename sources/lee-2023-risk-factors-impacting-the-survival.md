## Three-line Summary

Retrospective chart review (2005-2021) at Seoul National University Dental Hospital, Korea: 464 replaced implants in 370 patients, of which 429 survived and 35 were removed a second time.

Five-year survival of replaced implants was 90.2% with vs 97.0% without a periodontitis history (p = 0.008); independent risk factors for second removal were periodontitis history (aHR 3.417; 95% CI 1.161-10.055), GBR at first placement (aHR 2.152; 95% CI 1.052-4.397), while non-GBR at replacement was protective (aHR 0.262; 95% CI 0.088-0.778).

Abstract only: periodontitis history and GBR at the first placement mark replaced implants at higher risk of a second removal; patient nationality is not stated (Korean setting only implied by institution).

## 세줄요약

2005-2021년 서울대학교치과병원 차트를 후향적으로 검토: 1차 제거 후 재식립된 임플란트 464개(환자 370명) 중 429개 생존, 35개 2차 제거.

재식립 임플란트 5년 생존율은 치주염 병력 있음 90.2%, 없음 97.0% (p = 0.008); 2차 제거의 독립 위험인자는 치주염 병력 (aHR 3.417; 95% CI 1.161-10.055), 1차 식립 시 골유도재생술 (guided bone regeneration, GBR) (aHR 2.152; 95% CI 1.052-4.397)이며 재식립 시 GBR 미시행은 보호 요인 (aHR 0.262; 95% CI 0.088-0.778).

초록 기반: 치주염 병력과 1차 식립 시 GBR이 재식립 임플란트의 2차 제거 위험을 높임; 환자 국적은 명시되지 않고 한국 병원이라는 점에서만 시사됨.

## 2. Research Question

Which factors influence the survival of dental implants replaced after a first removal?

## 3. Methods

- Design: retrospective chart review, 2005-2021.
- Identification: replaced implants after the first removal; split into a surviving group and a second-removal group.
- Analysis: risk factors evaluated accounting for clustering of multiple implants within patients; multivariable model adjusted for clinical variables (adjusted hazard ratio, aHR). Specific covariates, surgical protocols and implant systems are not given in the abstract.
- Population: patient nationality/ethnicity is not stated in the text; Korean setting is implied only by the institution.

## 4. Results

- 464 replaced implants in 370 patients (denominator: implants and patients); 429 surviving, 35 second removal (7.5% of implants, computed from these counts).
- Timing of second removal (early vs late, interval from replacement) is not given in the abstract; only 5-year survival is reported.
- 5-year survival: periodontitis-history sites 90.2 +/- 0.18% vs 97.0 +/- 0.15% without (p = 0.008).
- 5-year survival: 89.1 +/- 0.27% with GBR at first placement vs 93.9 +/- 0.14% without GBR (p = 0.032).
- 5-year survival: 97.6 +/- 0.13% vs 90.3 +/- 0.17% for GBR vs non-GBR at replacement (p = 0.026); group labels are ambiguous in the abstract (see Limitations).
- Independent risk factors for second removal: periodontitis history aHR 3.417 (95% CI 1.161-10.055); GBR at first placement aHR 2.152 (95% CI 1.052-4.397); non-GBR at primary replacement aHR 0.262 (95% CI 0.088-0.778).

## 5. Conclusions

Periodontitis history, GBR at first implant placement and non-GBR at primary implant replacement were identified as risk factors for the survival of replaced implants.

## 6. Limitations

- Abstract only; methods, definitions of removal and covariate lists not verifiable.
- Retrospective single-center design.
- Wide CI for periodontitis history (1.161-10.055) indicates imprecision; only 35 events.
- In-paper inconsistency: the abstract lists 97.6% for "replaced implants with GBR" and 90.3% "without GBR", yet reports non-GBR at replacement as protective (aHR 0.262); the labels appear swapped or the wording is unclear. The reported "+/-" values (e.g. 0.18%) are unlabeled (SD vs SE).

## 7. Glossary

- Replaced implant: implant placed after removal of a failed implant at the same site
- 골유도재생술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)
- 조정 위험비 (adjusted Hazard Ratio, aHR)
- 치주염 (periodontitis)
