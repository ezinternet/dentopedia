## Why Ingested

기존 [[resin-bonding/li-2025-curing-modes-dual-cure-dentin]]는 광중합 여부(light- vs self-curing)가 dentin 접착 강도·내구성에 미치는 영향을 다루지만, 큐어링 팁-표면 거리라는 별개의 device-parameter가 세포 생존율이라는 생체적합성 종말점에 직접 연결되는지는 다루지 않는다. 본 논문(Hashemian 2025)은 큐어링 팁 거리(0/2/4/6/8mm)를 DC%와 치은섬유아세포 생존율에 동시에 매핑하여, "불충분 광조사 → 낮은 DC% → 잔류 단량체 방출 → 세포독성"이라는 임상 안전성 경로를 정량화한다 — 근관/치은 인접 근원심면 와동에서 실제로 발생하는 임상 시나리오(팁이 와동 바닥에 닿지 않는 상황)에 대한 직접적 근거.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (J Oral Biol Craniofac Res 2025) curing a nanocomposite (Filtek Z350 XT) at 0/2/4/6/8 mm light-curing tip distances (n=4/group) and measuring degree of conversion (ATR-FTIR) and human gingival fibroblast viability (MTT): DC% and cell viability both declined significantly with increasing distance, cell viability fell below the ISO 70% biocompatibility threshold at ≥6 mm, and DC% correlated positively with cell viability (top surface R=0.597, bottom R=0.490).

## 2. Key Contributions
- First direct linkage (in this wiki) between light-curing tip distance and a cell-viability biocompatibility endpoint (MTT), rather than only mechanical/conversion endpoints
- Quantifies the DC% decline with distance for a specific LED unit (O-Star Cordless, Woodpecker, 15.8° emission angle, 1200 mW/cm² nominal output) at clinically relevant proximal-cavity depths (0–8 mm)
- Establishes an ISO 10993 biocompatibility failure threshold (70% viability) is crossed specifically at ≥6 mm tip distance for this composite/unit combination
- Demonstrates a statistically significant positive correlation between DC% and cell viability at both top and bottom sample surfaces, with the correlation stronger at the top (directly irradiated) surface

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Material: 3M ESPE Filtek Z350 XT nanocomposite, Dentin shade A2 (Bis-GMA/Bis-EMA/UDMA/PEGDMA/TEGDMA monomer system)
- Sample geometry: disk-shaped, 2 × 6 mm, cured in a polycarbonate mold
- Groups: G0, G2, G4, G6, G8 — light-curing tip positioned at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 mm from the composite surface using 2-mm metal spacers; n = 4 per group
- Light-curing unit: LED, O-Star Cordless (Woodpecker), 20 s exposure at 1200 mW/cm² (radiometer-verified at each distance)
- Post-cure: samples incubated 37°C for 24 h prior to DC and MTT assays
- DC measurement: ATR-FTIR (Nicolet iS50); peak heights of aliphatic C=C (1638 cm⁻¹) vs carbonyl reference (1720 cm⁻¹) on top and bottom surfaces, plus uncured composite reference; DC calculated via standard formula (Omnic software)
- Biocompatibility: ISO 10993-compliant MTT assay using human gingival fibroblasts (DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% antibiotics); composite samples eluted in culture medium 24 h, extracts applied to cells in 96-well plates for 24 h, MTT formazan dissolved in DMSO, absorbance at 570 nm; viability expressed relative to untreated control
- Statistics: Shapiro-Wilk normality test; one-way ANOVA for group comparisons; Pearson correlation for DC (top/bottom) vs cell viability vs tip distance

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- DC%: top surface significantly higher than bottom at all distances (p < 0.001)
- DC% decreased with increasing distance on both surfaces: 44.57% (0 mm) → 32.99% (8 mm); statistically significant only for G0 vs G6 and G0 vs G8 at the bottom surface (p < 0.05) — the overall downward trend across all groups did not reach significance everywhere
- Cell viability decreased significantly with distance in all group comparisons except G0 vs G2 (p < 0.001)
- Cell viability remained above the ISO 70% threshold through 4 mm; fell to 65.5% at 6 mm and 58% at 8 mm — both below the ISO 10993 biocompatibility threshold
- Pearson correlation: DC% vs cell viability positive and significant at both surfaces (p < 0.05) — top surface R = 0.597, bottom surface R = 0.490
- Tip distance correlated negatively and significantly with both DC% and cell viability at both surfaces (p < 0.05)
- At 8 mm, measured light intensity fell to ~313 mW/cm² (near the ~300 mW/cm² minimum reported as required for adequate cure) due to the unit's 15.8° emission angle and inverse-square intensity falloff with distance

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small per-group sample size (n = 4)
- Single composite tested (Filtek Z350 XT) and single LED curing unit (O-Star Cordless) — generalizability to other composite/monomer systems and curing units (different emission angles, output power, wavelength spectra) is unverified
- MTT/extract-elution cytotoxicity assay is a screening-level biocompatibility test; authors themselves recommend more clinically simulating assays (dentin barrier test, agar diffusion test) as follow-up
- **Ethical clearance not documented**: the authors state in the manuscript that no ethical clearance form could be located for this study and explicitly request guidance ("I couldn't find any Ethical clearance form. I would greatly appreciate any guidance"); patient/guardian consent is stated as not applicable since the study is in vitro (no human subjects), but the absence of a located ethics approval record for the cell-culture work is a genuine, self-disclosed procedural limitation of this specific paper and should be weighed when assessing its evidentiary rigor
- DC% differences did not reach statistical significance across the full 0–8 mm range at every pairwise comparison (only some pairs reached p < 0.05), despite a clear descriptive downward trend — likely underpowered given n = 4/group

## 6. Related Work
- Price et al. (cited in Discussion): reported 50–77% light intensity reduction at 6 mm tip distance depending on curing-unit type, motivating this study's design
- General literature on TEGDMA/UDMA cytotoxicity and genotoxicity in dental pulp cells and gingival tissue (mitochondrial dysfunction, oxidative stress, IL-6/TNF-α inflammatory activation) is used to mechanistically interpret the viability decline
- Nanoparticle (silica, zirconia) release from nanocomposites and cellular uptake via endocytosis/phagocytosis as an additional proposed cytotoxicity mechanism at incomplete cure

## 7. Glossary
- **DC (Degree of Conversion)**: percentage of C=C double bonds converted to C-C single bonds during composite polymerization; higher DC% correlates with better mechanical properties, color stability, and biocompatibility
- **ATR-FTIR**: Attenuated Total Reflectance-Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy; used here to quantify DC via characteristic absorption peak height ratios
- **MTT assay**: colorimetric assay measuring metabolically active (viable) cell mitochondrial reductase activity via formazan formation; standard ISO 10993 cytotoxicity screening method
- **ISO 10993**: international standard for biological evaluation of medical devices; commonly cited 70% relative cell viability threshold for acceptable biocompatibility
- **TEGDMA / UDMA**: triethylene glycol dimethacrylate / urethane dimethacrylate — diluent/base monomers in the Filtek Z350 XT resin matrix implicated in cytotoxic/genotoxic effects when released unreacted
