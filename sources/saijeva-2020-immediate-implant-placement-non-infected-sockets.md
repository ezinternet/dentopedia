## Why Ingested

감염 소켓 즉시식립의 생존율 근거를 체계적으로 확인하기 위해 인제스트. 기존 [[immediate-implant/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]]가 2024년 더 넓은 SR+MA를 제공하지만 Saijeva 2020은 MBL·MGL·PD·mBI·WKG 등 이차 임상 파라미터 별도 메타분석(1·2·3+년 추적)을 포함해 보완 근거로 활용.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (9 cohort studies, n=2281 sockets): infected vs non-infected extraction sockets in immediate implant placement, follow-up up to 5 years.

No significant difference in implant survival rates (RR=0.99; 97.64% vs 98.57%); MBL, MGL, PD, mBI all comparable; only WKG showed slight but significant reduction favoring non-infected group.

Infected sockets are not a contraindication to immediate implantation after thorough debridement and antibiotic prophylaxis, but keratinized tissue width should be monitored.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (9편 코호트, n=2281 소켓): 감염 vs 비감염 발치와 즉시식립 (최대 5년 추적).

임플란트 생존율 유의한 차이 없음 (RR=0.99; 97.64% vs 98.57%); 변연골 소실·치은연·탐침 깊이·수정출혈지수 모두 두 군 동등; 각화치은폭(WKG)만 비감염 군에 유리한 미세 차이.

철저한 소파와 항생제 병용 시 감염 소켓도 즉시식립 금기 아님; 단 각화치은폭 관리 유의.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to analyze MBL, MGL, PD, mBI, WKG separately for infected vs non-infected immediate implant sockets (not just survival)
- Confirmed equal survival rates (RR=0.99) across 9 studies with 2281 sockets
- Identified WKG as the only parameter significantly different between groups — clinical implication for aesthetics
- Comprehensive risk of bias assessment using Joanna Briggs Institute Checklist for Cohort Studies

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis, PRISMA-compliant
- **Search**: PubMed, ScienceDirect, ISI Web of Knowledge, Google Scholar; Jan 2010–Feb 2020
- **Included**: 9 cohort studies (3 retrospective, 6 prospective); 1346 patients, 2281 sockets; 933 infected, 1348 non-infected
- **Exclusions**: no RCTs found; animal studies, non-English, studies without control group excluded
- **Statistical model**: Mantel-Haenszel fixed-effects (dichotomous); inverse variance (continuous); random-effects if I²>75%
- **Follow-up**: minimum 6 months; range 12–93 months

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | IS vs NIS | Key value |
|---|---|---|
| Survival rate | 97.64% vs 98.57% | RR=0.99 (P=0.08) NS |
| Failure rate | — | RR=1.8 (P=0.06) NS |
| MBL (3yr+) | — | MD=-0.03 (P=0.41) NS |
| MGL (2yr) | — | MD=-0.07 (P=0.23) NS |
| PD (2yr) | — | MD=0.06 (P=0.70) NS |
| mBI (3yr+) | — | MD=-0.002 (P=0.97) NS |
| WKG (3yr+) | NIS favored | MD=0.25 (P=0.38) slight↓ IS |

No RCTs found; all evidence from retrospective/prospective cohorts.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No RCTs available on this topic
- Classification of socket infection pathology varied widely across studies (chronic periapical, periodontal, acute/chronic)
- Only English studies included — selection bias possible
- Heterogeneity in antibiotic protocols, flap techniques, grafting materials
- Synonymous use of "survival" and "success" across studies creates inconsistency

## 7. Glossary

- **IS**: infected socket — extraction site with periapical/periodontal pathology
- **NIS**: non-infected socket — healthy extraction site
- **WKG**: width of keratinized gingiva — clinically important for peri-implant health and aesthetics
- **mBI**: modified bleeding index — peri-implant inflammation surrogate
- **MBL**: marginal bone level — key radiographic success parameter
