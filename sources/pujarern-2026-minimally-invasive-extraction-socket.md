## Why Ingested

기존 위키에 치조와 치유 기전 관련 페이지(예: [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/fok-2024-alveolar-socket-healing-evolving-knowledge]])는 ARP 효능에 집중하며 **발치 기법별 치유 기전·생물학적 시퀀스**는 상세히 다루지 않는다. Pujarern 2026은 Amler 시퀀스·Araújo & Lindhe 조직학 데이터·4단계 치유 기전을 종합하고, 최소침습 발치 기법(10종) 각각의 작동 원리·비교 근거를 정리한다. 기존 [[overviews/bone-regeneration-socket-biology-and-arp-critique]] 보강용으로 인제스트.

## One-line Summary

Narrative review (Front Oral Health 2026): post-extraction socket healing biology (4 phases, Amler sequence, Araújo & Lindhe histology) + 10 minimally invasive extraction techniques (periotome → enzymatic ColG) + socket preservation strategies for implant-ready sites.

## 한줄요약

## 2. Key Contributions
- 발치와 치유 4단계(hemostasis/coagulation 0–24h, inflammation 24–72h, proliferation 2–3w, remodeling 4w+)와 세포·사이토카인 축 정리
- Amler(1960) 시퀀스: 상피증식 4일, 골형성 시작 7일, 혈병 조직화 7–21일, 상피융합 22일, 골 합병 35일
- Araújo & Lindhe 핵심 데이터: 협측 치조정이 1주째 설측 대비 0.3mm 상방 → 8주째 1.9mm 하방; 총 2.2±0.2mm 차이(~45μm/일)
- 최소침습 기법 10종 각각의 작동원리·장단점·비교 임상 근거 체계화
- 압전수술기(piezotome) = 협측 골 손실 최소, 그러나 발열(+18°C)·조작 시간↑; 치주인대 분리기 (Periotome) = 빠른 조작·낮은 VAS, 두 기법 6개월 MBL 유사

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Socket healing timeline (Amler 1960)**
| Event | Timepoint |
|---|---|
| Epithelial proliferation | Day 4 |
| Bone formation begins | Day 7 |
| Clot organization | Days 7–21 |
| Epithelial fusion | Day 22 |
| Bone fill (radiographic) | Day 35 (clinical: 3–6 months) |

**Araújo & Lindhe (beagle dog) buccal–lingual crest position**
| Interval | Buccal vs lingual crest |
|---|---|
| 1 week | Buccal +0.3 ± 0.2 mm (coronally) |
| 2 weeks | Buccal −0.3 ± 0.1 mm (apically) |
| 4 weeks | Buccal −0.9 ± 0.3 mm |
| 8 weeks | Buccal −1.9 ± 0.2 mm |
| Net reduction (1–8w) | 2.2 ± 0.2 mm (~45 μm/day) |

**Ridge resorption pattern**
- Buccal > lingual/palatal (bundle bone PDL-dependency)
- Molar > premolar ≥ incisor (larger alveoli, complex morphology, higher biomechanical forces)
- ~50% bone volume loss within 12 weeks
- Soft tissue closure: 10–20 weeks; radiographic bone fill: 3–6 months

**Minimally invasive technique comparison**
| Technique | Mechanism | Key evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Periotome | Syndesmotomy (PDL fiber cutting) | Op time ↓ (5.8 vs 12.8 min), VAS↓, gingival laceration↓ vs conventional |
| Flexible periotome | Syndesmotomy | Op time 4.43 vs 7.2 min; superior wound healing index |
| Piezotome (29–32 kHz) | Ultrasonic hard-tissue cutting | Least buccal bone loss; heat +18°C even with saline; op time longest |
| Magnetic mallet | Axial magnetic pulse, minimal hand pressure | 427 ankylosed teeth: zero cortical bone loss; fewer buccal plate fractures vs conventional |
| SIBS (sonic) | High-freq air-pressure vibration | Heat <3°C (vs conventional bur <3°C; piezotome +18°C) |
| Physics forceps | Single first-class lever, 8× mechanical advantage | Less crown/root fracture; VAS↓ day 1, 3 |
| Vertical extraction (Benex) | Axial pull via screw + cable | 83–85% overall success; single-root 87.2%, multi-root 69.7% |
| VRE | Vestibular access + coronal push | Pink esthetic score +1.27 (95% CI 0.15–2.39) vs incisal extraction |
| Enzymatic (ColG) | Collagenase G: PDL collagen degradation | Extraction force ↓50% (ex vivo porcine) |
| SST / Root sectioning | Buccal root fragment retention | Preserves buccal bundle bone; favorable histology |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Narrative review — no systematic search, no GRADE assessment; heterogeneous study quality
- Most minimally invasive techniques supported only by small RCTs, case series, or ex vivo models
- Long-term RCTs comparing techniques head-to-head with implant outcomes lacking
- Enzymatic extraction (ColG) is ex vivo only; no in vivo clinical data

## 6. Related Work
- Araújo & Lindhe 2005: landmark beagle dog histology study of post-extraction remodeling
- Amler 1960: foundational human socket healing time sequence
- Schropp 2003: clinical/radiographic 12-month prospective (2/3 resorption in first 3 months)
- Physics forceps SR+MA: superior extraction duration, pain, soft/hard tissue trauma

## 7. Glossary
- **Bundle bone (다발골)**: Alveolar bone immediately adjacent to PDL; highly PDL-vascular-dependent; lost rapidly after extraction
- **SST (Socket-shield technique, 치아방패법)**: Intentional retention of 2mm buccal root fragment to preserve buccal periodontium
- **SIBS**: Sonic instrument for bone surgery — air-pressure-driven high-frequency vibration
- **ColG (Collagenase G)**: Bacterial enzyme from *Clostridium histolyticum* used for enzymatically assisted tooth extraction
- **VRE (Vestibular root extraction)**: Vestibular access incision + coronal push from apex to minimize labial plate trauma
