## Why Ingested

i-PRF·PRF의 골재생 보조효과를 β-TCP 이식재와 직접 head-to-head로 비교한 대형동물(goat) critical-size defect micro-CT 연구로, 기존 [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/park-2022-prf-gbr-damaged-socket-yonsei]](비글견 2벽성 결손에서 sticky bone vs GBR 비열등성)과 대비되는 결과를 보인다 — 본 연구는 i-PRF+β-TCP가 β-TCP 단독·PRF+β-TCP보다 8주 시점 신생골량·골밀도에서 유의하게 우수함을 보고해, i-PRF의 부가 이득이 결손 유형(장골 metacarpal critical-size defect vs 치조 2벽성 결손)에 따라 달라질 수 있음을 시사한다.

## Three-line Summary

Animal experimental study (18 male Najdi goats, 72 critical-size metacarpal bone defects, 4 groups/goat: spontaneous healing, β-TCP alone, β-TCP+PRF, β-TCP+i-PRF; micro-CT at 2/5/8 weeks).

By week 8, the β-TCP+i-PRF group had significantly higher new bone volume (BV-NFB 80.083 mm3) and mineral density (BMD-NFB 0.822 g/mm3) than β-TCP+PRF (62.665 mm3 / 0.802 g/mm3), β-TCP alone (58.995 mm3 / 0.799 g/mm3), and spontaneous healing (39.455 mm3 / 0.425 g/mm3); β-TCP+i-PRF also showed the fastest residual graft resorption (TCPV 15.319 mm3) and best trabecular microarchitecture.

i-PRF's fibrin network appears to synergize with β-TCP's osteoconductive scaffold to accelerate graft turnover and bone quality in a large-animal critical-size defect model, but this is animal (goat) micro-CT evidence only — no histology was performed, and clinical extrapolation to human GBR/socket defects is not yet established.

## 세줄요약

동물 실험 연구 (수컷 Najdi종 염소 18마리, 좌측 중수골에 72개 임계크기결손, 마리당 4군: 자연치유, β-삼인산칼슘(β-Tricalcium Phosphate, β-TCP) 단독, β-TCP+혈소판풍부피브린(Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF), β-TCP+주사형혈소판풍부피브린(injectable Platelet-Rich Fibrin, i-PRF); 2/5/8주 마이크로 전산화단층촬영(micro-CT) 평가).

8주 시점 β-TCP+i-PRF군이 신생골량(BV-NFB 80.083 mm3)·신생골 골밀도(BMD-NFB 0.822 g/mm3)에서 β-TCP+PRF군(62.665 mm3/0.802 g/mm3), β-TCP 단독군(58.995 mm3/0.799 g/mm3), 자연치유군(39.455 mm3/0.425 g/mm3)보다 유의하게 우수했고, 잔존이식재 흡수(TCPV 15.319 mm3)도 가장 빨랐으며 소주골 미세구조도 가장 우수했다.

i-PRF의 피브린 네트워크가 β-TCP의 골전도성 지지체와 시너지를 이뤄 대형동물 임계크기결손에서 이식재 대사·골질을 촉진하는 것으로 보이나, 조직학적 평가는 수행되지 않았고 동물(염소) micro-CT 근거에 국한되므로 인체 GBR/발치와 결손에 대한 임상 외삽은 아직 확립되지 않았다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First reported direct head-to-head comparison of i-PRF+β-TCP vs PRF+β-TCP vs β-TCP alone vs spontaneous healing, all within the same critical-size defect model (4 defects/goat, standardized proximal-to-distal order) in a large-animal (goat) metacarpal bone.
- Uses a full micro-CT parameter panel — new bone volume/density (BV-NFB, BMD-NFB), residual graft volume/density (TCPV, TCPMD), and trabecular microarchitecture (Tb-Th, Tb-Sp, Tb-No) — across three time points (2, 5, 8 weeks) spanning inflammatory, matrix-maturation, and remodeling phases.
- Demonstrates that i-PRF's advantage over PRF is not just in new bone quantity/quality but also in accelerated resorption of the β-TCP carrier itself, consistent with a "bioactive milieu accelerating scaffold turnover" mechanism.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Animals**: 18 male Najdi goats (mean 30 ± 2 kg, 8 ± 1.5 months), housed under veterinary supervision, 5-day acclimatization.
- **Defects**: 4 critical-size defects (6.1 mm outer / 5.25 mm inner trephine bur diameter, 8 mm length, 10 mm spacing) per left metacarpal bone, standardized proximal→distal order:
  1. Negative control (spontaneous healing)
  2. β-TCP alone (IngeniOs β-TCP Bioactive, Zimmer Dental, 0.25–1 mm particles)
  3. β-TCP + PRF (Choukroun/Dohan 2006 protocol)
  4. β-TCP + i-PRF (Mourão protocol, 700 rpm × 3 min)
- All defects covered with resorbable collagen membrane (Bio-Gide).
- **Sacrifice timing**: 6 goats each at 2, 5, and 8 weeks (representing early/inflammatory, intermediate/matrix-maturation, and late/mineralization-remodeling phases).
- **Imaging**: High-resolution micro-CT (SkyScan 1173, 100 kV/343 mA, 12.39 μm pixel resolution), 3D reconstruction (N Recon/Bruker SkyScan), ROI-based quantification (CTAn software) by a blinded examiner.
- **Statistics**: Two-way then one-way ANOVA with Tukey (or Games-Howell) post-hoc; Shapiro-Wilk/Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality and Levene homogeneity confirmed (p>0.05).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **BV-NFB (new bone volume)** at week 8: negative control 39.455 mm3 < β-TCP 58.995 mm3 ≈ β-TCP+PRF 62.665 mm3 < β-TCP+i-PRF 80.083 mm3 (p=0.000 across groups at 2/5/8 wk; β-TCP vs β-TCP+PRF not significantly different).
- **BMD-NFB (new bone mineral density)** at week 8: 0.425 / 0.799 / 0.802 / 0.822 g/mm3 respectively (i-PRF highest at 2 and 5 wk; no significant difference among the three β-TCP-containing groups by week 8).
- **TCPV (residual graft volume)** at week 8: β-TCP 24.972 mm3, β-TCP+PRF 22.013 mm3, β-TCP+i-PRF 15.319 mm3 — i-PRF group resorbed fastest (significantly lowest), consistent with accelerated scaffold turnover. TCPMD showed no significant intergroup difference at any time point.
- **Trabecular microarchitecture**: β-TCP+i-PRF had significantly higher Tb-Th and Tb-No, and lower Tb-Sp, than β-TCP+PRF at most time points — closest overall to normal bone architecture by week 8.
- **Bottom line**: β-TCP+i-PRF > β-TCP+PRF ≈ β-TCP alone > spontaneous healing for both quantity (BV-NFB) and quality (BMD-NFB, trabecular architecture) of new bone at 8 weeks.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No histology/histomorphometry was performed — micro-CT alone cannot assess cellularity or dynamic remodeling indices; authors explicitly flag this as a limitation and call for combined micro-CT + histomorphometric follow-up studies.
- Animal model (goat metacarpal long-bone critical-size defect) is mechanically and anatomically distinct from human alveolar/jaw defects — direct clinical extrapolation to dental GBR, sinus lift, or socket preservation is not established.
- Single defect per treatment arm per animal at fixed proximal-to-distal anatomical order (rather than randomized position) could introduce a positional confound, though standardized across all animals.
- Small sacrifice cohort per time point (n=6 goats × 1 defect/group = 6 defects/group/time point).

## 6. Related Work

- Park 2022 (Yonsei, beagle dog 2-wall alveolar defect): PRF-based sticky bone (i-PRF + DPBM) was non-inferior — not superior — to conventional GBR; this Assiri 2026 goat study instead finds i-PRF+β-TCP outperforming both PRF+β-TCP and β-TCP alone, highlighting how defect anatomy/species/graft carrier can shift PRF's incremental benefit from "non-inferior" to "superior."
- Miron & Zhang (i-PRF mechanistic literature cited throughout): sustained PDGF/TGF-β/collagen-1 release and enhanced fibroblast/osteoprogenitor migration underlie the proposed mechanism for i-PRF's advantage over classic PRF and PRP.
- β-TCP socket preservation, alveolar bone grafting, and periodontal GBR clinical literature (Brkovic, Weijs, Katuri, Kaushick — cited in Discussion) — establishes β-TCP as a clinically validated alloplastic carrier that this study pairs with platelet concentrates.

## 7. Glossary

- **i-PRF (Injectable Platelet-Rich Fibrin)**: Liquid autologous platelet/leukocyte concentrate obtained by low-speed centrifugation (~700 rpm, 3 min) without anticoagulant; releases growth factors more rapidly than solid PRF and can be mixed with graft particulate to form "sticky bone."
- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)**: Solid/semi-solid autologous fibrin clot concentrate (Choukroun/Dohan protocol) used as a membrane or graft additive; releases growth factors more gradually than PRP.
- **β-TCP (Beta-Tricalcium Phosphate)**: Synthetic, fully resorbable alloplastic bone graft substitute; osteoconductive scaffold that is progressively replaced by host bone.
- **BV-NFB / BMD-NFB**: Micro-CT-derived Bone Volume and Bone Mineral Density of Newly Formed Bone within the region of interest — quantity and quality metrics for new bone.
- **TCPV / TCPMD**: Residual (unresorbed) β-TCP graft particle Volume and Mineral Density — a proxy for scaffold resorption/turnover rate.
- **Tb-Th / Tb-Sp / Tb-No**: Trabecular Thickness, Separation, and Number — micro-CT microarchitecture parameters describing how bone-like the newly formed trabecular bone is.
- **Critical-size defect**: A surgically created bone defect large enough that it will not heal spontaneously within the animal's lifetime without intervention — the standard preclinical model for testing graft materials.
