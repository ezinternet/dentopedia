## Why Ingested

위키의 [[overviews/clear-aligner-indications-limitations]]는 축 1("Roller Coaster Effect")과 축 6(발치 프로토콜·치조골)에서 투명교정(Clear Aligner Therapy, CAT) 발치 증례의 근거가 대부분 후향적/증례 수준 crown-only 관찰(zhuo-2026-the-roller-coaster-effect, tang-2025-evaluating-the-effectiveness-of-clear, zhang-2025-clear-aligner-extraction-protocol-alveolar-bone-cbct)에 의존하고 있었다. 본 논문(Wang 2025)은 CBCT 3D superimposition으로 크라운뿐 아니라 치근(root) 이동까지 정량화한 최초급 연구로, 4소구치 발치 증례에서 설계-실제 치아이동 불일치의 근본 기전(torque loss, anchorage loss, root control 실패)을 직접 규명해 이 갭을 메운다. [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/tang-2025-evaluating-the-effectiveness-of-clear]]가 지적한 "현재 부착 설계로 극복 불가능한 생역학적 한계"에 구체적 root-level 수치 근거를 제공하고, [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/zhang-2025-clear-aligner-extraction-protocol-alveolar-bone-cbct]]의 torque-loss 시그니처(설측 골개열)에 대한 이동학적 원인을 보완한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study, 47 patients (542 teeth) treated with four-premolar-extraction clear aligner therapy (CAT); pre/post-treatment CBCT superimposition measured crown AND root movement in three dimensions and compared to ClinCheck-designed movement.

Root movement was consistently underachieved versus crown movement — maximum discrepancy at U1 root (5.49±2.30 mm) in the anteroposterior direction; anterior teeth extruded despite designed intrusion, and U3 crowns achieved only 71.5% of designed distal movement; age, extraction site (U4 vs U5), anchorage method, staging pattern, and attachment design significantly influenced discrepancies.

Clinical implication: ClinCheck for premolar-extraction CAT needs systematic root-movement/torque overcorrection (incisor and canine root retraction, anterior intrusion), and choice of anchorage/attachment/staging materially changes anchorage-loss and root-control risk — 47-patient single-center retrospective, small subgroup n's limit factor analysis.

## 세줄요약

후향적 연구, 47명(542개 치아) 4소구치 발치 투명교정(CAT) 증례; 치료 전후 CBCT 중첩으로 크라운뿐 아니라 치근의 3차원 이동을 측정하여 ClinCheck 설계값과 비교.

치근 이동은 크라운 대비 일관되게 미달성 — 전후방(APD)에서 U1 치근이 최대 불일치(5.49±2.30 mm); 전치는 함입 설계에도 불구하고 오히려 정출되었고, U3 크라운은 설계된 원심이동의 71.5%만 달성; 연령, 발치부위(U4 vs U5), 고정원 방법, 단계화 방식, 부착물 설계가 불일치에 유의한 영향.

임상적 의미: 소구치 발치 투명교정의 ClinCheck 설계에는 치근이동·토크 과보정(절치·견치 치근 후방견인, 전치부 함입)이 체계적으로 필요하며, 고정원·부착물·단계화 선택이 anchorage loss와 root control 위험을 좌우함 — 단일기관 후향적 47명 연구로 일부 서브그룹 표본수가 작아 요인분석 해석에 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study (per authors) to comprehensively measure **root** movement — not just crown — in 3D for premolar-extraction CAT cases, using CBCT superimposition rather than intraoral-scan/CBCT registration (avoiding segmentation/registration-accuracy confounds).
- Quantifies designed-vs-achieved discrepancy separately for crown and root in three axes (anteroposterior/APD, lateral/LD, occlusal-gingival/OGD) across six maxillary tooth types (U1, U2, U3, U5, U6, U7).
- Linear mixed-effects model identifies which clinical factors (age, extraction site U4 vs U5, anchorage type, space-closing pattern, attachment design) significantly modulate discrepancy — giving clinicians actionable levers rather than only describing the problem.
- Reframes canine "lingual inclination" assumption from prior crown-only literature: this study found U3 as a whole moved **buccally** (crown more than root) rather than lingually, because prior conclusions were crown-only.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective, single-center, Jan 2020–May 2024.
- **Inclusion**: 4 first/second premolar extractions; no other maxillary tooth loss (except 3rd molars); skeletal Class I/II; no fixed/auxiliary appliance combination; no orthognathic surgery combination; high-quality pre/post CBCT.
- **Imaging**: KaKo Kerr CBCT, FOV 23×17 cm, 37.1 mAs, 17.8 s at 120 kV, voxel 0.15 mm, 768×768 resolution.
- **Superimposition**: Dolphin Imaging v11.95, 3D coordinate system per Feng et al. method (horizontal plane = maxillary functional occlusal plane; sagittal plane = midpalatal suture/nasal septum; coronal plane perpendicular through U7 distal point). Anterior-cranial-base-anchored registration with a finely aligned framed area (sella turcica upper edge to root apex, nasion to posterior maxilla, bilateral condyles).
- **Measurement points**: incisal-edge midpoint (incisors), cusp tip (canine), buccal cusp (premolar), mesiobuccal cusp (molar) as crown reference; corresponding root apex as root reference. APD/LD/OGD computed from z/x/y coordinate differences.
- **Sample size**: G*Power calculation using smallest effect size (0.37, U1 OGD, from Dai et al reference) → minimum n=60 teeth; actual sample 81–94 teeth per tooth type across 47 patients, meeting requirement.
- **Statistics**: Pearson correlation for intra-operator consistency; Shapiro-Wilk normality; paired t-test or Wilcoxon signed-rank for designed-vs-achieved comparison; linear mixed-effects model (patient as random effect) for influencing-factor analysis.
- **Cohort composition**: 3 patients received skeletal anchorage (miniscrews between U5–U6); 28 patients used auxiliary anchorage devices overall; extraction sites split between U4 (n=13, excluded from primary tooth-count analysis due to low count) and U5.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Anteroposterior (APD)**:
- Incisor crown retraction fully achieved; incisor **root** retraction poorly achieved — maximum discrepancy at U1 root: 5.49±2.30 mm.
- U3 (canine) crown achieved only 71.5% of designed distal movement; root even less.
- Posterior crowns/roots exceeded designed mesial displacement (except U7 mesiobuccal root) — indicating **mesial tipping** beyond plan (anchorage loss).

**Lateral (LD)**:
- Incisors failed to move distally as designed; U2 root showed the greatest discrepancy (p<.001), causing distal tipping of U2.
- U3 exhibited **buccal** displacement overall (crown > root) — contradicts prior crown-only literature's "lingual inclination" assumption.
- U5–U6 showed lingual crown movement with buccal root inclination; U7 showed lingual inclination.

**Occlusal-gingival (OGD)**:
- Anterior teeth (especially U1) extruded despite being designed for intrusion.
- U5 over-intruded, U7 over-extruded; no significant U6 discrepancy (p=.28) — argues against a clinically meaningful "bite-block effect" for molars in this cohort.

**Influencing factors** (linear mixed-effects model):
- Designed movement magnitude significantly affected nearly all discrepancies except U1 crown APD/LD and U6 OGD.
- Adolescents vs adults: greater U1 crown APD/OGD discrepancy in adolescents; for U6 root LD, adults moved more lingually than designed while adolescents moved more buccally than designed (p<.001), attributed to adolescent maxillary growth (forward/downward/widening).
- U5-extraction (vs U4) group had greater discrepancies in U1 root APD, U6 APD, U3 crown APD/OGD, U3 root LD — but smaller U3 crown LD discrepancy — overall interpreted as U5 extraction carrying **greater anchorage-loss risk**.
- Anchorage: Class II elastics and miniscrews both reduced U1/U3 crown APD discrepancy vs no-extra-anchorage (protective), but miniscrews **increased** U1 root APD discrepancy (uncontrolled labial root tipping) — attributed to elastic-force vector from miniscrew to U3 precision-cut increasing U1 clockwise torque.
- Staging: sequential retraction increased U1 crown APD discrepancy but preserved root control (no root difference vs en-masse); en-masse retraction reduced U6 crown LD discrepancy (better anchorage) — trade-off between anterior torque control and posterior anchorage.
- Attachments: U3 vertical attachments caused greater U1/U3 root APD discrepancy (more uncontrolled tipping) vs optimized attachments; U6 double-rectangular attachments reduced U6 root APD discrepancy vs optimized; U6 horizontal-attachment/no-attachment groups had greater U6 crown LD discrepancy (higher anchorage-loss risk).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, single-center design; some subgroup n's very small (e.g., only 6 U6 teeth without attachments, 3 patients with miniscrews) — risk of missing true influential factors or over-attributing to small subgroups.
- Only 3D **linear** movement analyzed; angulation/inclination (rotational) changes not directly measured — authors recommend future work compare designed vs achieved angulation/inclination.
- All patients from one institution/one clear aligner system context; generalizability to other aligner brands/protocols not established.
- Miniscrew subgroup (n=3 patients) too small for firm anchorage-loss-prevention conclusions despite directionally consistent findings.

## 6. Related Work

- Cites Nada et al and Feng et al for CBCT superimposition methodology (anterior-cranial-base referencing).
- Cites Palone et al (32.8% overcorrection recommendation for maxillary incisor buccolingual inclination) and Liu et al (finite-element study on premolar-extraction overcorrection producing palatal root torque/incisor intrusion) as prior overcorrection literature this study's findings support extending.
- Cites Dai et al as source of the smallest-effect-size estimate used for sample-size calculation, and as a comparator finding (U6 mesial movement of 2.8 mm despite "stable" design).
- Cites Yan et al (U2 distal tipping in premolar-extraction CAT, alleviated by attachments + sequential retraction) — consistent with this study's U2 findings.
- Cites Harris et al on the "bite-block effect" threshold (3–4 mm posterior block thickness) — used to explain why U6 showed no significant intrusion discrepancy in this cohort.
- Relates to wiki pages: [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/zhuo-2026-the-roller-coaster-effect]] (posterior open-bite/FCD consequence of extraction CAT), [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/tang-2025-evaluating-the-effectiveness-of-clear]] (curve-of-Spee undercorrection in extraction CAT), [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/zhang-2025-clear-aligner-extraction-protocol-alveolar-bone-cbct]] (alveolar bone/torque-loss signature by extraction protocol).

## 7. Glossary

- **CAT / CA**: Clear Aligner Therapy / Clear Aligner
- **CBCT**: Cone-Beam Computed Tomography
- **APD**: Anteroposterior Displacement
- **LD**: Lateral Displacement
- **OGD**: Occlusal-Gingival Displacement
- **U1–U7**: Maxillary tooth notation (U1 central incisor, U2 lateral incisor, U3 canine, U4 first premolar, U5 second premolar, U6 first molar, U7 second molar)
- **ClinCheck**: Invisalign's digital treatment-planning/simulation software
- **FCD**: Functional Cusp Distance (used in related roller-coaster-effect literature, not measured in this paper)
