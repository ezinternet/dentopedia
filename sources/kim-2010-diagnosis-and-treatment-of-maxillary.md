## Why Ingested

Adds a two-patient early-literature case report in which implant-associated maxillary sinusitis was managed by a direct lateral-wall approach (curettage), antibiotics and regular sinus irrigation. It sits beside [[sinus-lift/lateral/nam-2014-fess-intraoral-implant-related-sinusitis]] (FESS plus intraoral approach for implant-related sinusitis), contrasts with the endoscopic/ostium-centred framing of [[sinus-lift/lateral/kim-2019-maxillary-implants-otolaryngologist-perspective]], and supplies an older surgical-approach data point for [[overviews/odontogenic-maxillary-sinusitis-overview]]. ## Three-line Summary
Case report (n=2) of maxillary sinusitis that developed after dental implant placement, describing oral and radiographic clinical features and management.
Symptoms improved with curettage through the lateral wall of the maxillary sinus, antibiotics and sinus irrigation (abstract states improvement "after 2 weeks of the operation").
The authors conclude acute maxillary sinusitis should be treated by a direct lateral-wall opening, regular in-hospital irrigation and antibiotics; n=2 and abstract-only limit the evidence.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Describes the prognosis and treatment of maxillary sinus infection associated with dental implants in 2 patients.
- Reports symptom improvement with lateral-wall curettage, antibiotics and sinus irrigation.
- Proposes a direct lateral-wall approach with regular in-hospital irrigation and antibiotics for acute maxillary sinusitis after implant placement.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Case report (2 patients)
- **n**: 2 patients
- **Outcomes**: Oral and radiographic clinical features; symptom improvement
- **Interventions**: Curettage through the lateral wall of the maxillary sinus, antibiotics, sinus irrigation
- Further procedural detail (implant site, timing, imaging findings, follow-up length) is not available in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Symptoms of maxillary sinusitis improved with lateral-wall curettage, antibiotics and sinus irrigation; the abstract places this "after 2 weeks of the operation".
- No quantitative outcomes are reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- n=2 case report; no comparator and no quantitative outcome data.
- Abstract-only: methods, causes (e.g., implant protrusion, graft material, ostium status) and follow-up cannot be assessed.
- The recommendation for a direct approach rests on two cases and is not compared with endoscopic or conservative alternatives.

## 7. Glossary
- **측방벽 소파술 (Curettage through the lateral wall)**: removal of diseased tissue via a window in the lateral maxillary sinus wall.
- **상악동 세척 (Sinus irrigation)**: repeated flushing of the sinus cavity, here performed regularly in hospital.
