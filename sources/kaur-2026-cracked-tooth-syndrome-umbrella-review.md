## Why Ingested

균열치 증후군 (Cracked Tooth Syndrome, CTS) 치료법의 **umbrella review (SR of SRs)** — 분야 최고 evidence-level. [[cracked-tooth/jkda-2024-62-1-003·004·005]] 진단·치료 review와 결합해 cracked-tooth cluster를 완성 (한국 review + 국제 SR).

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (Manipal College of Dental Sciences, JCDE 29(1):11–19, 2026; PROSPERO CRD420250648720): 5개 DB(PubMed·Cochrane·Scopus·EMBASE·Web of Science) 검색 433건 → 4개 SR (30 primary studies, 45 occurrences) 통합.

AMSTAR-2 confidence는 SR마다 상이 — Olivieri 2020 High, Leong Low, Zhang 2024·Mathew 2024 Critically low; CCA 0.17 (17%, "extremely high overlap" 기준선 15% 초과).

**Endodontic therapy + full cuspal coverage**가 tooth survival·treatment success 최고 (crown 없을 시 발치 위험 11.3배, RCT 진행 위험 3배 증가); periodontal probing depth >4mm는 불량 예후 인자.

AMSTAR-2 confidence는 SR별 상이 — Olivieri 2020 High, Leong Low, Zhang 2024·Mathew 2024 Critically low; CCA 0.17(17%, "extremely high overlap" 기준선 15% 초과).

## 1. Document Information
저자: Manipal College of Dental Sciences (인도, 만갈로르). PROSPERO CRD420250648720. Protocol: PRISMA-P 2015 준수. Review 자체는 PRISMA 2020 + PRIOR checklist (umbrella review 전용) + Cochrane Handbook + Joanna Briggs Institute 지침 준수. JCDE = Journal of Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics, 29(1):11–19 (2026).

Research question (PICO): P=CTS 환자(치아 위치·crack 깊이·치수 상태 무관), I=full-coverage crown/partial coverage(onlay·overlay)/direct·indirect adhesive restoration/RCT/occlusal adjustment/extraction, C=observation·placebo·no treatment, O(primary)=tooth survival·treatment success·pain/sensitivity 감소·crack progression·restoration failure·retreatment/extraction, O(secondary)=complications·cost·patient satisfaction.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 첫 CTS umbrella review — SR-level evidence 종합 (PRISMA-P 2015 protocol + PROSPERO 사전등록 + PRIOR checklist)
- 4개 포함 SR 각각에 AMSTAR-2 16-domain (critical domain 7개) 평가 → confidence rating 개별 산출 (Olivieri High / Leong Low / Zhang·Mathew Critically low)
- CCA (Corrected Covered Area) overlap 분석 → 0.17 (17%, threshold 15% 초과로 "extremely high overlap")
- **Endo + full cuspal coverage**가 최고 prognosis 결론; 정량적 위험비(발치 11.3배, RCT 진행 3배)까지 제시
- Periodontal probing depth >4mm를 독립적 불량 예후 인자로 명시

## 3. Methodology
- Umbrella review (SR of SRs), PRISMA-P 2015 protocol → PROSPERO CRD420250648720 등록, 수행은 PRISMA 2020 + PRIOR checklist + Cochrane Handbook + JBI 준수
- DB (5개, 2025-06-15까지): PubMed 59건, Scopus 156건, EMBASE 141건, Cochrane 0건, Web of Science 77건 = 총 433건
- 274건 중복 제거 → 159건 title/abstract 스크리닝 (2 reviewer 독립, 3rd reviewer 중재) → 149건 제외 → 10건 full-text 평가 → 6건 제외(비영어 전문 2건, 치료적 중재 미평가 3건, integrative review 1건) → **최종 4개 SR 포함**
- 2 reviewer 독립 data extraction (piloted extraction form: author/year/study type/sample/intervention/comparator/outcome/effect estimate)
- AMSTAR-2 (16 domain, critical domain 7개)로 방법론적 질 평가, 이견은 3rd reviewer 중재
- Citation matrix + Pieper 공식 기반 CCA로 primary study overlap 정량화

## 4. Key Results

**PRISMA flow**: 433 records → 274 duplicates 제거 → 159 screened → 149 excluded → 10 full-text reviewed → 6 excluded → **4 SR included** (30 distinct primary studies, 45 occurrences, CCA 0.17/17%)

**포함 SR 4편 요약**

| SR (year) | Included primary studies (n) | Sample (n) | Population | Key finding | AMSTAR-2 confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zhang (2024) | 27 | 5279 | Vital posterior teeth with cracks (CTS–RVDP) | Crown 배치가 생존율 향상; 무증상 치아는 monitoring 안전 | Critically low |
| Mathew (2024) | 6 | 566 | Endodontically treated cracked teeth | Full crown이 대체 수복물보다 생존율 우수 | Critically low |
| Olivieri (2020) | 7 | 674 | Cracked posterior teeth post-RCT | 1-year survival 88%; deep probing·pain가 불량 예후 연관 | High |
| Leong (2019/2020, 원문 내 연도 표기 불일치) | 4 | 585 | RCT 필요 성인(≥18세) cracked teeth | 5-year survival 84.1%(60개월); crack 깊이·증상이 예후 저하 연관 | Low |

- AMSTAR-2 domain 세부: Zhang·Mathew는 excluded-study list 부재 + publication bias 미평가로 critically low; Olivieri는 모든 critical domain 충족(funding source 보고만 누락)으로 high; Leong은 protocol 미등록 + excluded list 부재 + single-extractor(부분 검증)로 low
- **Tooth survival**: Zhang 92.8–97.8%(vital)/90.5–91.1%(RCT 후 crown); Mathew 75.8–100%; Olivieri 88%; Leong 84.1%(60개월)
- **Treatment success**: Mathew 75.8–100%; Olivieri 83.7%; Leong ~82–84%
- **Pulp vitality preservation** (Zhang만 보고): 조기 보존적 adhesive restoration 후 1–3년 vitality 유지율 85.6–90.4%
- **Progression to RCT** (Zhang): cuspal coverage 없으면 RCT 진행 위험 3배
- **Extraction risk** (Olivieri): crown 없으면 발치 위험 11.3배; Leong도 유사 경향
- **Periodontal status** (Olivieri, Leong): probing depth >4mm에서 생존율 저하·crack progression 위험 증가 일관 관찰

## 5. Limitations
- 4 SR — 작은 evidence base (원저자 명시)
- CCA 0.17(17%) — primary study 중복 사용, threshold(15%) 초과로 "extremely high overlap"
- AMSTAR-2 quality가 SR마다 low~critically low (Olivieri만 high) — protocol 미등록·excluded list 부재·publication bias 미평가·single-extractor 등 원인
- Follow-up 기간 비일관, outcome 정의 비일관, retrospective observational study 다수 → comparability·causal inference 제한
- Gray literature 제외(방법론적 일관성 위해) → publication bias 가능성 원저자 스스로 인정
- Primary RCT 부족 (분야 일반의 구조적 한계 — umbrella review도 결국 동일 primary study base에 의존)

## 7. Glossary
- **CTS**: Cracked Tooth Syndrome
- **RVDP**: Reversible pulpitis with dentinal pain (Zhang 2024 review가 사용한 vital-crack subclassification)
- **AMSTAR-2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews 2 — 16 domain(critical 7개)로 SR 방법론적 질 평가
- **CCA**: Corrected Covered Area — Pieper 공식 기반 SR 간 primary study overlap 정량 지표. 임계값 15% 초과 시 "extremely high overlap"
- **PRIOR checklist**: Preferred Reporting Items for Overviews of Reviews — umbrella review 전용 보고 지침
- **Cuspal coverage**: 교두 덮음 (onlay, crown)
