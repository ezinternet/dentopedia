PDRN 골/연조직 재생 페이지들([[pdrn/lee-2023-impact-polydeoxyribonucleotide-lateral-sinus-elevation]])의 기전 토대.

## Three-line Summary

Animal study (rat experimental periodontitis model, ligature-induced lower first molar) comparing PDRN gel vs vehicle ± the A2A adenosine receptor antagonist DMPX to establish the mechanism of PDRN's anti-inflammatory action.

PDRN gel restored normal periodontal histology and significantly reduced inflammatory markers (p-JNK, p-ERK, TNF-α, IL-6, HMGB-1) and apoptotic BAX while preserving Bcl-2; DMPX (A2A antagonist) completely abolished all PDRN benefits, confirming A2A receptor mediation.

PDRN's anti-inflammatory and anti-apoptotic effects in periodontal tissues are mechanistically dependent on A2A adenosine receptor stimulation — the foundational receptor-mechanism evidence underpinning all later dental PDRN applications.

## 세줄요약

동물 실험(쥐 치주염 모델, 하악 제1대구치 결찰로 유발) — PDRN 겔 vs 부형제 ± A2A 아데노신 수용체 길항제(DMPX)로 항염 기전 검증.

PDRN 겔이 정상 치주 조직학 회복 및 염증(p-JNK, p-ERK, TNF-α, IL-6, HMGB-1)·세포사멸(BAX) 마커 유의 감소, Bcl-2 보존; DMPX 투여 시 모든 PDRN 효과 완전 소실 → A2A 수용체 매개 기전 확인.

PDRN의 치주조직 항염·항세포사멸 효과는 A2A 아데노신 수용체 자극에 의존 — 이후 모든 치과 PDRN 응용의 기전적 토대.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal of Clinical Periodontology 2013;40(1):26–32 (e-pub 2012-08-29). doi 10.1111/jcpe.12010. Animal experiment.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Causally links PDRN's anti-inflammatory action to A2A adenosine receptor stimulation (receptor-blockade abolishes effect) — mechanistic foundation reused across later PDRN dental literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Experimental periodontitis (EPD) induced by ligating the cervix of the lower-left first molar in rats; sham had no ligature. After 7 days, PDRN gel vs vehicle ± DMPX (A2A antagonist). Histology + inflammatory/apoptotic protein expression.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Vehicle EPD: severe gingival/PDL infiltrate, ↑ p-JNK, p-ERK, TNF-α, IL-6, HMGB-1, BAX; ↓ Bcl-2.
- PDRN gel: restored histology, blunted inflammatory/apoptotic proteins, preserved Bcl-2.
- DMPX (A2A antagonist) abrogated PDRN's positive effects → A2A-receptor mediated.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Animal (rat) model; short term (7 d); periodontitis model not directly an implant/bone-graft endpoint — mechanism generalization to peri-implant/sinus contexts is inferential.

- PDRN: polydeoxyribonucleotide. A2A: adenosine A2A receptor. DMPX: 3,7-dimethyl-1-propargylxanthine (A2A antagonist). EPD: experimental periodontitis.
