## Why Ingested

In vitro study directly relevant to titanium base abutment cementation protocols in implant-supported prosthetics. Tests five ceramic/hybrid materials × three cement types commonly used in hybrid abutment crown workflows. Clinically actionable: identifies zirconia + TheraCem as recommended combination.

→ [[prosthetic-materials/dhesi-2021-titanium-base-abutment-adhesion-protocol]]

## Three-line Summary

Shear bond strength (SBS) testing of titanium disks cemented to five restorative materials (Zenostar T zirconia, Lava Ultimate, IPS e.max CAD, Vita Enamic, G-ceram) using three cements (Panavia 21, TheraCem, Multilink hybrid abutment). Both ceramic type (p<0.0001) and cement type (p<0.0001) significantly affected SBS; thermocycling (5000 cycles) did not (p>0.05). Zirconia (Zenostar T) + TheraCem produced the highest SBS and is recommended for clinical use.

## 세줄요약

티타늄 디스크(Tritan)를 5종 세라믹/하이브리드 재료에 3종 시멘트로 접착, 매크로 전단결합강도(SBS) 측정. 세라믹 종류(p<0.0001)·시멘트 종류(p<0.0001) 모두 유의한 영향; 열순환(5000회, 5–55°C)은 무유의(p>0.05). 지르코니아(Zenostar T)+TheraCem 조합이 가장 높은 SBS → 임상 권장.

## Key Contributions

1. Provides head-to-head comparison of three luting cements across five CAD/CAM restorative materials bonded to titanium bases
2. Demonstrates thermocycling (5000 cycles) does not significantly degrade bond to titanium bases — clinically reassuring for long-term cementation durability
3. Identifies TheraCem (self-adhesive resin cement) as the top performer with zirconia substrates
4. Reports ~equal incidence of cohesive (50.34%) and adhesive (49.66%) failure modes — no dominant failure pattern
5. Provides practical material-cement selection guidance for hybrid abutment crown workflows

## Methodology

- **Specimen**: Tritan titanium disks (N=450, n=15 per group)
- **Materials tested** (cemented to titanium):
  1. Zenostar T (zirconia)
  2. Lava Ultimate (hybrid resin-ceramic)
  3. IPS e.max CAD (lithium disilicate)
  4. Vita Enamic multicolor (hybrid ceramic)
  5. G-ceram (feldspathic ceramic)
- **Cements tested**:
  - Panavia 21 (MDP-containing self-cure resin cement)
  - TheraCem (self-adhesive resin cement, BISCO)
  - Multilink hybrid abutment (adhesive resin cement)
- **Aging**: Half thermocycled 5000 cycles (5–55°C), half kept dry (no aging)
- **Bond testing**: Macro shear bond testing via universal testing machine
- **Failure classification**: Cohesive vs adhesive
- **Statistics**: Three-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD post-hoc

## Key Results

- Ceramic/material type: significant effect on SBS (p<0.0001)
- Cement type: significant effect on SBS (p<0.0001)
- Thermocycling: no significant effect on SBS (p>0.05)
- Failure mode: cohesive 50.34% vs adhesive 49.66% (not significantly different)
- **Best combination**: Zenostar T (zirconia) + TheraCem → recommended for clinical use
- Zirconia showed highest overall SBS across cement groups
- Hybrid materials (Lava Ultimate, Vita Enamic) showed lower SBS compared to crystalline ceramics

## Limitations

1. In vitro study — clinical masticatory loading and biological environment not replicated
2. Only 5000 thermocycles — some protocols use 10,000+ for long-term aging simulation
3. No sandblasting or tribochemical silica coating (Rocatec) conditioning step reported — conditioning protocol details limited in abstract
4. Abstract-only source; exact SBS values per group not available for quantitative comparison with other studies
5. Single titanium alloy brand (Tritan) tested — results may not generalize to other titanium base systems
