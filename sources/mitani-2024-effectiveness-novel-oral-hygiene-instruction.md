## Why Ingested

Adds a periodontitis-targeted refinement of instruction built on the O'Leary plaque control record (PCR), which the wiki's index review describes as the standard dichotomous chairside-feedback index ([[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/delia-2023-dental-biofilm-indices-narrative-review]]). Extends the wiki's OHI literature ([[overviews/oral-hygiene-instruction-overview]]) from generic instruction to site-directed instruction.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized clinical trial (Japan; sample size not reported in the abstract) comparing novel OHI, guided by a plaque control record restricted to sites with probing depth (PD) >=4 mm (agPCR, calculated by a dedicated app), against conventional OHI guided by O'Leary's PCR; PD, bleeding on probing (BOP) and periodontal inflamed surface area (PISA) were recorded at baseline and 5-6 months.

PISA converged to a lower value after instruction in the novel OHI group, and the improvement rate of PISA correlated positively with agPCR in the novel OHI group but showed no correlation in the conventional OHI group; the abstract reports no numeric effect sizes, p-values or n.

Directing instruction to deep-pocket plaque sites may improve inflammation control over whole-mouth PCR-based OHI, but the abstract-only evidence (no numbers, no sample size, no blinding details) is insufficient to quantify the benefit.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Proposes agPCR: a plaque control record counting only plaque-stained surfaces at sites with PD >=4 mm, to avoid 'inattentional blindness' (instruction drifting to stained areas that do not coincide with deep pockets).
- Provides a free application that calculates the agPCR automatically (per the abstract's keywords).
- Reports that the improvement rate of PISA correlated positively with agPCR only in the novel OHI group.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Randomized clinical trial, two parallel groups: novel OHI (agPCR-based) vs conventional OHI (O'Leary PCR-based).
- Outcomes: PD, BOP and PISA at baseline and 5-6 months later.
- Sample size, randomization/blinding details, inclusion criteria and adjunct treatment: not reported in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- PISA converged to a lower value after instruction in the novel OHI group (approximation-curve analysis); no numeric values in the abstract.
- Improvement rate of PISA vs agPCR: positive correlation in the novel OHI group, no correlation in the conventional OHI group.
- Authors conclude that control of inflammation was more effective in the novel OHI group.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: no n, effect sizes, confidence intervals or p-values retrievable.
- Authors' conclusion rests on approximation curves and a correlation; between-group numeric differences are not reported in the abstract.
- Single-country (Japan) trial; short follow-up (5-6 months); generalizability not assessed.

## 7. Glossary
- **PCR (plaque control record)**: O'Leary's dichotomous whole-mouth index: percentage of tooth surfaces with disclosed plaque.
- **agPCR**: 'aggressive target' PCR: counts only plaque-stained surfaces at sites with PD >=4 mm.
- **PISA**: periodontal inflamed surface area: combined measure of BOP-positive periodontal pocket surface area.
- **Inattentional blindness**: failure to notice a relevant target when attention is directed elsewhere; here, instruction directed at stained areas that miss the deep pockets.
