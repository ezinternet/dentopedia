## Why Ingested

위키에는 이갈이(Bruxism)–임플란트 합병증 축에 소규모 코호트·우산리뷰 데이터가 있으나, 2–6유닛 고정성 국소의치(Implant-Supported Fixed Partial Denture, ISFPD) 특이적으로 bruxism이 나사 파절·임플란트 파절·세라믹 칩핑(ceramic chipping)에 미치는 영향을 대규모 회향 코호트(n=642명·876개 보철물·2,241개 임플란트)로 정량화한 데이터가 없었다. 본 Chrcanovic 2020은 30년 누적 생존율 추정치(72.7%)와 bruxism의 Cox 회귀 위험비를 제공해 [[implants/survival/chawki-2026-particularities-of-the-implant-supported]]의 방향성 결론에 환자 수준 ISFPD 코호트 층위를 더하고, [[implants/survival/song-2021-implant-complications-bruxism-patients]]의 late-failure 프레임을 FPD 합병증 수준에서 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study of 642 patients receiving 876 ISFPDs (2,241 implants, 2–6 prosthetic units) at one specialist clinic in Sweden, followed up for a mean 108.0 ± 76.2 months (approximately 9 years).

88 prostheses and 112 implants failed (CSR at 30 years 72.7%); 299 ISFPDs (33.2%) had technical complications; bruxism was associated with higher risk of screw fracture, implant fracture, and ceramic chipping, while cantilever design increased risk of prosthesis failure and screw loosening/fracture.

Bruxism and cantilever presence are clinically significant risk factors for mechanical complications and failure in multi-unit implant FPDs; smoking was also independently associated with lower implant survival.

## 세줄요약

스웨덴 전문 클리닉 1곳에서 2–6유닛 고정성 국소의치(Implant-Supported Fixed Partial Denture, ISFPD) 876개(임플란트 2,241개)를 장착한 642명을 평균 108.0 ± 76.2개월(약 9년) 추적한 회향 코호트 연구.

보철 실패 88개·임플란트 실패 112개(30년 누적생존율(Cumulative Survival Rate, CSR) 72.7%); 기술 합병증 299개(33.2%); 이갈이(Bruxism)는 나사 파절·임플란트 파절·세라믹 칩핑(Ceramic Chipping) 위험 증가와 연관, 캔틸레버(Cantilever) 설계는 보철 실패·나사 이완/파절 위험 증가와 연관.

이갈이와 캔틸레버 존재는 다유닛 임플란트 고정성 보철의 기계적 합병증·실패에 대한 임상적으로 유의한 위험인자; 흡연도 임플란트 생존율 저하와 독립적으로 연관.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Large-scale retrospective dataset (642 patients, 876 ISFPDs, 2,241 implants) with long mean follow-up (~9 years) quantifying ISFPD-specific survival and complication rates
- Cox regression identifying bruxism as a risk factor for screw fracture, implant fracture, and ceramic chipping — providing patient-level hazard data absent from held SR-level pages
- 30-year CSR estimate of 72.7% for ISFPDs as a long-term prognostic benchmark
- Cantilever ISFPDs associated with higher failure risk and screw loosening/fracture — actionable design guidance
- Internal abutment connection and two-pontic prostheses associated with higher ceramic chipping risk
- Prosthesis span (number of units) did not significantly influence failure or complications — a clinically useful null finding
- Implant failure was the primary driver of ISFPD failure

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective cohort — single specialist clinic, all patients treated with ISFPDs of 2–6 units included
- **n**: 642 patients; 876 ISFPDs; 2,241 implants (26 implant failures before prosthesis installation, 86 after)
- **Follow-up**: Mean 108.0 ± 76.2 months (~9 years); range not reported in abstract
- **Outcomes**: Implant/prosthesis failure (primary) and technical complications (screw fracture, screw loosening, ceramic chipping, implant fracture)
- **Analysis**: Cumulative survival rate (Kaplan-Meier or life table methodology implied by CSR at 30 years); Cox regression for risk factors
- **Key exposures**: Bruxism, smoking, cantilever, abutment connection type, number of pontics, prosthesis span

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Implant failure**: 112 total (26 before prosthesis installation, 86 after)
- **Prosthesis failure**: 88 ISFPDs
- **CSR at 30 years**: 72.7% (ISFPDs)
- **Technical complications**: 299/876 ISFPDs (33.2%)
- **Bruxism associations**: Higher risk of screw fracture, implant fracture, ceramic chipping
- **Cantilever associations**: Higher risk of prosthesis failure and screw loosening/fracture
- **Abutment connection**: Internal connection associated with higher ceramic chipping risk
- **Two-pontic prostheses**: Higher ceramic chipping risk
- **Prosthesis span**: No significant influence on failure or complications
- **Smoking**: Lower implant survival vs non-smokers

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only — full text not retrieved; specific Cox regression hazard ratios and confidence intervals, Kaplan-Meier curves, and detailed statistical results are not available
- Single-centre retrospective design limits generalizability
- Mean follow-up of ~9 years is moderate; the 30-year CSR is an extrapolated estimate
- Bruxism classification method not stated in abstract (likely clinical self-report or chart-based — a recognized limitation given heterogeneous diagnosis across the held literature)

## 7. Glossary
- **ISFPD (Implant-Supported Fixed Partial Denture)**: 고정성 임플란트 지지 국소의치(Fixed Partial Denture, FPD); 2개 이상 임플란트로 지지되는 복수 유닛 고정성 보철물
- **CSR (Cumulative Survival Rate)**: 누적 생존율; Kaplan-Meier 또는 생명표 분석으로 산출
- **Cantilever**: 캔틸레버; 한쪽 끝이 지지되지 않는 보철 연장부
- **Ceramic chipping**: 세라믹 칩핑; 도재 수복물의 표면 파절 합병증
- **Bruxism**: 이갈이; 수면 또는 각성 시 비기능적 이갈기·이악물기 행동
