## One-line Summary
SR+MA (Medicine 2026; 26 RCTs, n=1266): curcumin vs chlorhexidine — PI/GI/BI comparable (p>0.05); CHX superior in PD reduction (SMD 0.883, p<0.001) and AL (SMD 0.539, p=0.042) — curcumin is a plaque/gingival adjunct alternative, not a periodontal depth treatment.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (Medicine 2026; 26편, n=1266): 커큐민 vs CHX — PI·GI·출혈지수 동등(p>0.05); CHX가 치주낭 깊이(SMD 0.883)·부착소실(SMD 0.539)에서 우월 — 커큐민은 치태·치은염 보조제로 적합, 치주낭 치료 대체 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Largest SR+MA comparing curcumin vs chlorhexidine: 26 RCTs, 1266 subjects
- Plaque Index: SMD −0.041 (95%CI −0.245 to 0.163, p=0.692) — statistically equivalent
- Gingival Index: SMD 0.033 (95%CI −0.216 to 0.281, p=0.797) — statistically equivalent
- Bleeding Index: SMD −0.044 (95%CI −0.208 to 0.295, p=0.735) — statistically equivalent
- Probing Depth: SMD 0.883 (p<0.001) — CHX significantly superior
- Attachment Loss: SMD 0.539 (p=0.042) — CHX significantly superior
- Long-term (>1 month) PD/AL: subgroup shows similar efficacy — CHX advantage mainly short-term
- Clinical interpretation: curcumin is a viable alternative for surface plaque/gingival inflammation; NOT for pocket management

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR+MA; RCTs only
- Search strategy: multiple databases; no date specified in abstract
- Statistical: random-effects model; SMD with 95%CI; I² for heterogeneity
- Subgroup analyses: follow-up duration (≤1 month vs >1 month); primary vs secondary outcomes
- Risk of bias: 24/26 studies high RoB; 2 with some concerns

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | SMD (95%CI) | p-value | Favors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plaque Index | −0.041 (−0.245 to 0.163) | 0.692 | Neither (equivalent) |
| Gingival Index | 0.033 (−0.216 to 0.281) | 0.797 | Neither (equivalent) |
| Bleeding Index | −0.044 (−0.208 to 0.295) | 0.735 | Neither (equivalent) |
| Probing Depth | 0.883 (0.419 to 1.347) | <0.001 | CHX |
| Attachment Loss | 0.539 (0.019 to 1.058) | 0.042 | CHX |

- Subgroup: long-term (>1 month) PD/AL subgroup: CHX short-term advantage diminishes
- Heterogeneity: I²=83–94% (very high across all outcomes)
- GRADE: not reported in abstract; 24/26 RCTs high RoB limits confidence

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- 24/26 studies high risk of bias
- Extreme heterogeneity across all outcomes (I²=83–94%)
- Curcumin formulations, dosages, vehicles not standardized across studies
- CHX concentration varied
- Subgroup finding (long-term equivalence) should be interpreted cautiously

## 7. Glossary

- CHX: Chlorhexidine — gold-standard antiseptic mouthrinse; broad-spectrum antimicrobial
- SMD: Standardized Mean Difference
- PD: Probing Depth (치주낭 깊이)
- AL: Attachment Loss (부착소실)
