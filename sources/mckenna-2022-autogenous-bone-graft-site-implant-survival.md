## Why Ingested
This systematic review and meta-analysis directly quantifies how autograft donor site selection (iliac crest vs. intraoral) affects long-term implant survival, providing the highest level of evidence to complement [[bone-regeneration/benic-2014-horizontal-bone-augmentation-guided-regeneration]], which classifies autograft sources but does not meta-analyze their comparative survival outcomes.

## Three-line Summary
This systematic review and meta-analysis (23 studies, 14 for meta-analysis) compared implant survival rates and donor site complications between iliac crest bone grafts (ICG) and intraoral bone grafts (IOG) for dental implant placement, following PRISMA guidelines with PROSPERO registration.
Intraoral grafts consistently outperformed iliac crest grafts at all timepoints: 6-month (98.4% vs. 95.8%), 12-month (98.4% vs. 97.0%), 24-month (98.2% vs. 85.9%), 60-month (91.5% vs. 90.0%), and 120-month (95.2% vs. 88.8%), all p<0.001; iliac crest grafts also carried higher rates of donor site complications including pain, gait disturbance, and sensory disturbance.
Intraoral autografts are superior to iliac crest grafts for implant survival and morbidity, though evidence robustness is limited by a small number of high-quality studies and heterogeneous complication reporting.

## 세줄요약
본 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석(23개 연구, 14개 메타분석)은 PRISMA 지침과 PROSPERO 등록을 따르며 임플란트 식립을 위한 장골능 골이식 (Iliac Crest Graft, ICG)과 구강내 골이식 (Intraoral Bone Graft, IOG) 간의 임플란트 생존율과 공여부 합병증을 비교했다.
구강내 골이식은 모든 추적 기간에서 일관되게 우수했다: 6개월(98.4% vs 95.8%), 12개월(98.4% vs 97.0%), 24개월(98.2% vs 85.9%), 60개월(91.5% vs 90.0%), 120개월(95.2% vs 88.8%), 모두 p<0.001; 장골능 이식에서 통증, 보행 장애, 감각 장애 등 공여부 합병증이 더 빈번히 보고됐다.
구강내 자가골이식이 임플란트 생존율과 이환율에서 장골능 이식보다 우수하나, 고품질 연구 수 부족과 합병증 보고의 이질성으로 근거 견고성이 제한된다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- At 6 months: ICG 95.8% (95% CI: 93.1–97.4) vs. IOG 98.4% (95% CI: 96.0–99.4), p<0.001
- At 12 months: ICG 97.0% (95% CI: 90.1–99.2) vs. IOG 98.4% (95% CI: 95.8–99.4), p<0.001
- At 24 months: ICG 85.9% (95% CI: 80.7–89.9) vs. IOG 98.2% (95% CI: 94.6–99.4) after sensitivity analysis, p<0.001
- At 60 months: ICG 90.0% (95% CI: 81.3–94.9) vs. IOG 91.5% (95% CI: 31.8–99.6), p<0.001
- At 120 months: ICG 88.8% (95% CI: 79.8–94.0) vs. IOG 95.2% (95% CI: 84.6–98.6), p<0.001
- Iliac crest grafts associated with donor site complications: pain/discomfort, gait disturbance, sensory disturbance
- Mental region grafts associated with high rate of paresthesia of the lower lip and anterior mandibular teeth
- Donor site complication data too heterogeneous for meta-analysis

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA guidelines)
- **Registration**: PROSPERO (CRD42021283738)
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed), EMBASE, Cochrane CENTRAL (January 1, 1980 – March 7, 2022)
- **Eligibility**: Prospective human studies, 2-piece micro-rough implants in autogenous bone grafts, ≥6 months follow-up
- **n**: 23 studies total; 14 included in meta-analysis
- **Statistical method**: Random effects model; I² for heterogeneity; funnel plots + trim-and-fill for publication bias
- **Quality assessment**: Cochrane collaboration tool (RCTs); Newcastle-Ottawa scale (non-randomized studies)
- **Outcomes**: Implant survival rate at 6/12/24/60/120 months; donor site complications

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small number of high-quality studies limits robustness of findings
- Donor site complication data too heterogeneous for meta-analysis; qualitative synthesis only
- Significant variation in reporting terms and methods across included studies
- Many studies lacked timelines for follow-up or complication development
- No standardized core outcome set for implant dentistry research
- Wide confidence intervals at some timepoints (e.g., IOG at 60 months: 31.8–99.6%)

## 7. Glossary
- **ICG**: Iliac Crest Graft (장골능 골이식) — Autogenous bone harvested from the iliac crest (pelvis); used for large bone defects requiring high volume
- **IOG**: Intraoral Graft (구강내 골이식) — Autogenous bone from intraoral sites such as mandibular symphysis or ramus
- **PRISMA**: Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (체계적 문헌고찰 보고 가이드라인)
- **PROSPERO**: International prospective register of systematic reviews (국제 체계적 문헌고찰 사전 등록 데이터베이스)
- **Random effects model**: Statistical model accounting for heterogeneity between studies (연구간 이질성을 고려한 통계 모델)
