## Why Ingested
한국 임상 데이터로 임플란트 표면 유형(SLA vs RBM)과 식립 부위(전악·상하악·직경)별 ISQ 차이를 206개 임플란트에서 비교한 후향적 연구. 기존 [[implants/isq/andersson-2019-rfa-factors-5year-neoss-survival]] 등의 서양 데이터를 보완하며, Straumann SLA와 Osstem SSǎ(RBM) 간의 직접 ISQ 비교 국내 근거를 제공한다.

## One-line Summary
Retrospective study (n=206 implants, 131 patients, Korea) comparing ISQ at 3 months: mandible > maxilla, wider diameter > narrower, and SLA surface (Straumann) > RBM surface (Osstem) especially at posterior mandible and premolar maxilla.

## 한줄요약
후향적 연구 (206개 임플란트, 131명, 한국): 하악 > 상악, 직경 4.8 > 4.1 (SLA™ 한정), SLA™(Straumann) > SSǎ™(Osstem RBM) — 특히 하악 구치부(p=0.045)와 상악 소구치부(p=0.032)에서.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study from Korea (206 implants, 131 patients) comparing ISQ at 3 months by jaw, diameter and surface (Straumann SLA vs Osstem RBM).

ISQ was higher in the mandible than the maxilla (p<0.001), higher with 4.8 mm than 4.1 mm SLA implants (p<0.001), and higher with SLA than RBM at the posterior mandible (p=0.045) and premolar maxilla (p=0.032).

Site and diameter matter for early stability, but surface cannot be isolated because the two systems also differ in macro-geometry; single time point, retrospective, and detailed tables were only partly extractable from the Korean-language text.

## 세줄요약

악골 부위, 직경, 표면 (Straumann SLA 대 Osstem RBM)에 따른 3개월 ISQ를 비교한 한국 후향적 연구 (임플란트 206개, 131명)다.

ISQ는 하악이 상악보다 (p<0.001), SLA 4.8 mm가 4.1 mm보다 (p<0.001) 높았고, SLA가 RBM보다 하악 구치부 (p=0.045)와 상악 소구치부 (p=0.032)에서 높았다.

부위와 직경은 초기 안정성에 영향을 주지만 두 시스템은 매크로 형태도 달라 표면 효과만 분리할 수 없다; 단일 시점의 후향적 연구이며 한국어 본문이라 상세 표는 일부만 추출되었다.

- Funding/COI: Not reported

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct ISQ comparison between Straumann SLA and Osstem RBM surfaces in a clinical Korean population
- Confirms mandibular superiority over maxillary ISQ — consistent with higher cortical bone density
- Shows diameter effect within SLA™ group: 4.8mm > 4.1mm implants
- Identifies specific regions where surface difference is significant (posterior mandible, premolar maxilla)
- Provides ISQ data at 3-month healing timepoint (abutment connection stage) rather than immediate post-placement

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective cross-sectional analysis of clinical records
- **Population**: 131 patients, 206 implants; Apple Tree Dental Clinic, Bucheon, Korea
- **Implant placement**: Protocol per supplier recommendation by a single experienced surgeon (10 years experience)
- **ISQ measurement**: 3 months post-fixture installation (before abutment connection); buccal and lingual directions, average of two readings
- **Factors analyzed**: Implant type (SLA vs SSǎ), surgery area (mandible vs maxilla, anterior vs posterior), implant diameter (4.1 vs 4.8mm for SLA group), systemic disease, smoking
- **Statistical methods**: SPSS; significance threshold p<0.05; specific tests not clearly stated in extracted text

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Mandible vs maxilla**: Mandibular implants significantly higher ISQ (p<0.001)
- **Diameter effect (SLA only)**: 4.8mm diameter > 4.1mm diameter (p<0.001)
- **Surface type (SLA vs SSǎ)**:
  - Posterior mandible: SLA > SSǎ (p=0.045)
  - Premolar maxilla: SLA > SSǎ (p=0.032)
  - Other regions: no statistically significant difference reported

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design — no randomisation or controlled allocation
- KCI journal (Korean Citation Index), limited English text; detailed statistical tables partially inaccessible due to Korean-only body text and PDF OCR issues
- No longitudinal ISQ trajectory — single time point (3 months)
- Implant systems differ in both surface treatment AND macro-geometry (Straumann vs Osstem); cannot isolate surface effect alone
- Systemic disease and smoking effects not fully analyzed in extracted text
- Small subgroup sizes when stratified by area + diameter + surface

## 7. Glossary
- **SLA**: Sandblasted Large-grit Acid-etched (Straumann proprietary hydrophobic surface)
- **SSǎ (RBM)**: Resorbable Blast Media surface — Osstem's hydroxyapatite-blasted surface (smoother than SLA)
- **Osstell Mentor**: Resonance frequency analysis device; predecessor to Osstell IDx; measures ISQ 1–100
- **ISQ at 3 months**: Secondary stability phase; value reflects bone-implant contact maturity, not primary mechanical stability
