## Why Ingested
New 2026 pilot RCT testing two physical decontamination adjuncts (erythritol air-polishing, Er:YAG laser) on top of titanium-curette submarginal instrumentation for peri-implantitis, a question the network meta-analysis [[implants/peri-implantitis/bai-2024-seven-nonsurgical-methods-peri-implantitis-network]] ranked indirectly; it also adds a direct head-to-head data point beside [[implants/peri-implantitis/eraydin-tufek-2026-nonsurgical-peri-implantitis-multiarm-rct]] and a keratinized-mucosa moderator relevant to [[overviews/keratinized-mucosa-peri-implant-health-overview]].

## Three-line Summary
Single-masked, three-arm, 6-month pilot RCT (47 patients, 84 implants) of non-surgical submarginal instrumentation with titanium curettes alone (control) versus control plus erythritol air-polishing (test 1) or control plus Er:YAG laser (test 2) for peri-implantitis.
PPD fell by a mean of 1-1.4 mm at 1 month in all groups, stable at 3 months with slight relapse at 6 months; BoP fell 24%-36% at 1 month with no intergroup differences; only 12%-18% of implants reached the therapeutic endpoint.
Adjunct choice did not clearly change outcomes and endpoint achievement was low; multilevel analysis suggested associations with >=2 mm vestibular keratinized mucosa and with Er:YAG use, but these need confirmation in larger studies (abstract-only data).

## 세줄요약
단일맹검 3군 6개월 파일럿 RCT(환자 47명, 임플란트 84개): 임플란트주위염에서 티타늄 큐렛 비외과적 치은연하 기구 처치 단독(대조군) 대비 에리스리톨 에어폴리싱 추가(시험군 1) 또는 Er:YAG 레이저 추가(시험군 2)를 비교했다.
모든 군에서 탐침 깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD)가 1개월에 평균 1-1.4 mm 감소해 3개월까지 유지되고 6개월에 약간 재발했으며, 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BoP)은 1개월에 24%-36% 감소했고 군간 차이는 없었다; 치료 종결점 도달률은 12%-18%에 그쳤다.
보조요법 종류에 따른 뚜렷한 차이가 없고 종결점 도달이 제한적이었으며, 전정측 각화점막 2 mm 이상 및 Er:YAG 사용과의 연관성이 시사됐으나 더 큰 연구의 확인이 필요하다(초록 수준 정보).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct three-arm comparison of erythritol air-polishing and Er:YAG laser as adjuncts to titanium-curette submarginal instrumentation in peri-implantitis.
- Reports a low therapeutic endpoint rate (12%-18%; PPD <= 5 mm, BoP <= 1 site, no suppuration) across all arms.
- Multilevel logistic model of implant- and patient-level predictors, suggesting associations with >= 2 mm vestibular keratinized mucosa and Er:YAG use.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: 6-month single-masked randomized clinical trial, three parallel groups (pilot).
- **Groups**: control = non-surgical submarginal instrumentation with titanium curettes; test 1 = control + erythritol air-polishing; test 2 = control + Er:YAG laser.
- **n**: 47 patients, 84 implants.
- **Follow-up**: baseline, 1, 3 and 6 months.
- **Outcomes**: primary = PPD reduction; secondary = BoP reduction and endpoint of therapy (PPD <= 5 mm, BoP <= 1 site, no suppuration). Multilevel logistic model for predictors.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- PPD reductions in all groups (mean 1-1.4 mm at 1 month), stable at 3 months, slight relapse at 6 months.
- BoP reduction 24%-36% at 1 month in all groups, no further significant change later and no intergroup differences.
- Therapeutic endpoint rates 12%-18%.
- Multilevel analysis: association of >= 2 mm vestibular keratinized mucosa and of Er:YAG use with higher odds of treatment success.
- Safety: healing uneventful in all groups except one case of surgical emphysema associated with erythritol air-polishing.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Pilot sample (47 patients, 84 implants across three arms); authors state findings require confirmation in larger studies.
- Abstract-only: detailed intergroup PPD statistics, confidence intervals, odds ratios and methods were not available to this page.

## 6. Related Work
- bai-2024: network meta-analysis ranking seven non-surgical adjuncts; this trial adds direct comparative data.
- eraydin-2026: multi-arm RCT of non-surgical adjuncts also without clear between-group differences.

## 7. Glossary
- **PPD**: probing pocket depth (탐침 깊이).
- **BoP**: bleeding on probing (탐침 시 출혈).
- **Er:YAG**: erbium-doped yttrium aluminium garnet laser.
- **Erythritol air-polishing**: low-abrasive powder air-polishing used for implant surface decontamination.
