## Three-line Summary

PRISMA systematic review and meta-analysis of cross-sectional studies in healthy adults with intact maxillary anterior sextants, assessing gingival zenith (GZ) mesiodistal position, apicocoronal level, and contralateral symmetry (JBI appraisal, GRADE).

GZ was distal / on-axis / mesial in 96% / 3% / 1% of central incisors, 84% / 14% / 1% of lateral incisors, and 43% / 44% / 5% of canines; the lateral-incisor GZ was coronal to the central–canine zenith line in 82%; contralateral GZ level and position were symmetrical.

Distal GZ displacement increases the more anterior the tooth, giving reference targets for restorative/implant emergence-profile design — but GRADE certainty was very low for every comparison.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to pool GZ position (distal / axial / mesial) and level data for maxillary incisors and canines.
- Quantifies a gradient: distal GZ displacement is most frequent and largest in central incisors, then lateral incisors, then canines.
- Confirms the lateral-incisor GZ usually sits coronal to the central–canine zenith line (82%).
- Shows contralateral symmetry of GZ level and position.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PVO framework (population, variable, outcome) and PRISMA checklist.
- Population: healthy adults with complete maxillary anterior sextant; cross-sectional studies only.
- Outcomes: prevalence of GZ position categories; quantitative GZ level/position measurements.
- Risk of bias: Joanna Briggs Institute checklist for analytical cross-sectional studies.
- Certainty: GRADE.
- Number of included studies and pooled sample size are not given in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Tooth | GZ distal | GZ on axis | GZ mesial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Central incisor | 96% | 3% | 1% |
| Lateral incisor | 84% | 14% | 1% |
| Canine | 43% | 44% | 5% |

- Lateral incisor GZ levelled coronally (relative to the zenith line) in 82%.
- Contralateral symmetry for GZ levelling and positioning.
- GRADE certainty: very low for all comparisons.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest: number of studies, sample sizes, pooled millimetre values, and heterogeneity statistics were not retrieved.
- Very-low certainty across all outcomes (observational cross-sectional base, measurement heterogeneity).
- Canine percentages do not sum to 100% (43+44+5=92%) — the remainder is not explained in the abstract.
- Healthy-dentition reference values; transfer to implant sites is an inference, not tested.

## 6. Related Work

- Chu 2009 (J Esthet Restor Dent) — original GZP/GZL caliper study (central ~1 mm distal, lateral ~0.4 mm, canine centred; lateral GZL ~1 mm coronal).
- Emergence-profile zone concepts (critical/subcritical contour; EBC) that use the zenith as a design target.

## 7. Glossary

- **Gingival zenith (GZ)**: most apical point of the free gingival margin of a tooth.
- **Zenith line**: line joining the GZs of the central incisor and canine; reference for lateral-incisor GZ level.
- **Vertical bisected midline (VBM)**: long-axis bisector of the clinical crown used to locate GZ mesiodistally.
