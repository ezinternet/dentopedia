## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cross-sectional comparative cohort (45 anterior maxillary extraction sockets in 44 patients; flapless immediate implant placement with palatally biased osteotomy) comparing four groups defined by bone graft and provisional restoration: no BGPR, provisional only (PR), bone graft only (BG), and both (BGPR), with soft-tissue dimensions measured on gypsum casts.

Vertical peri-implant soft-tissue height was greater in grafted (BG+BGPR) than nongrafted sites (2.72 vs 2.29 mm, P < .06), facial labial-palatal thickness at the gingival third was greater in grafted (2.90 vs 2.28 mm, P < .008) and in provisional-restoration sites (2.81 vs 2.37 mm, P < .06), and the BGPR-vs-none contrast showed the largest mean difference (3.09 vs 2.03 mm) but was not statistically significant (P < .79); net gain was ~1 mm, with most gains in the gingival and middle zones above the 2.0 mm color-masking threshold.

Clinical implication: combining labial-gap bone grafting with a screw-retained provisional restoration at flapless immediate placement was associated with thicker, taller peri-implant mucosa (about 0.5–1.0 mm), but the retrospective cast-based design, absent baseline soft-tissue measurement, and internally inconsistent significance reporting limit causal inference.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단면 비교 코호트(Chu 등 2015, Int J Periodontics Restorative Dent; 상악 전치부 발치와 45개/44명, 무판막 즉시식립·구개측 편향 osteotomy) — 골이식과 임시수복 유무로 4군(무처치, 임시수복만, 골이식만, 둘 다)을 비교하고 석고모형에서 연조직 높이·두께를 측정했다.

연조직 수직 높이는 이식군이 비이식군보다 컸고(2.72 vs 2.29 mm, P < .06), 치은 1/3의 협설 두께는 이식군(2.90 vs 2.28 mm, P < .008)과 임시수복군(2.81 vs 2.37 mm, P < .06)에서 컸으며, 골이식+임시수복 대 무처치는 평균차가 가장 컸지만(3.09 vs 2.03 mm) 통계적으로 유의하지 않았다(P < .79); 순증가는 약 1 mm, 주로 치은·중간 구역에서 2.0 mm 색차폐 역치를 넘었다.

임상적 의미: 무판막 즉시식립 시 협측 gap 골이식과 나사고정 임시수복의 병용은 연조직 두께·높이 약 0.5–1.0 mm 증가와 연관되었으나, 후향적·석고모형 기반 설계, 기저 연조직 두께 미측정, 유의성 보고의 내적 불일치로 인과 해석에는 주의가 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Isolates the **soft-tissue** effect of two independent variables at flapless immediate placement — labial-gap bone grafting and immediate provisional restoration — in a 4-arm (2×2) clinical comparison.
- Reports vertical soft-tissue height and labial-palatal thickness by three corono-apical zones (gingival G, middle M, incisal I) measured on gypsum casts.
- Frames the result around a **2.0 mm thickness threshold** for abutment color masking and crestal bone stability, rather than around bone fill alone.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Retrospective cross-sectional comparative cohort; 45 anterior maxillary sockets in 44 patients (70% maxillary central incisors), age 22–75 y (mean 48.5).
- **Groups (2×2)**: No BGPR (no graft, no provisional); PR (provisional only); BG (bone graft only); BGPR (graft + provisional).
- **Inclusion**: systemically healthy; maxillary anterior (first premolar to first premolar); no periodontal disease/gingival recession; unrestored contralateral tooth; no endodontic lesion with labial plate perforation.
- **Exclusion**: medical/psychiatric contraindications, pregnancy, healing limitations, type II/III sockets (Elian), bruxism/parafunction, compromised soft tissue, poor compliance.
- **Surgery**: flapless atraumatic extraction (sharp dissection of supracrestal fibers, 15c blade), socket debridement, palatally biased osteotomy; tapered non-platform-switched internal-connection implants placed 3–4 mm apical to FGM; primary stability 25–35 Ncm from apical macro-threads.
- **Gap management**: labial gap left with blood clot (No BGPR, PR) or filled with small-particle bone allograft (BG, BGPR).
- **Restoration**: screw-retained autopolymerizing acrylic provisional in infraocclusion for PR/BGPR (subgingival contour supporting soft tissue and protecting clot/graft); straight or stock contoured healing abutment for No BGPR/BG with a resin-bonded pontic. No BGPR/BG: first disconnection ≥4 months, then PEEK abutment + nonsurgical soft-tissue sculpting ≥3 weeks. PR/BGPR: first disconnection ≥5 months.
- **Measurement**: implant-level impression with pattern resin to capture subgingival profile; gypsum casts measured with spring-loaded caliper (0.1 mm sensitivity) under 2.5× loupes at the midpoint of three ~1 mm zones (G, M, I) created by a default 3.0 mm implant depth; vertical height = FGM to implant shoulder.
- **Statistics**: regression analysis and ANCOVA (JMP v10); contralateral tooth used to estimate postextraction site stability; α = .05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Overall means**: vertical soft-tissue height (FGM to implant-abutment junction) 2.5 mm; labial-palatal thickness at G/M/I = 2.7 / 2.1 / 1.3 mm.
- **Recession**: average 0.5 mm vertical collapse of peri-implant soft tissue in all groups from placement to measurement.
- **Thickness gradient**: thickest at the apical extent (implant-abutment interface, always > 2 mm in all groups) and thinning toward the FGM.
- **Grafted vs nongrafted**: vertical height 2.72 vs 2.29 mm (P < .06); facial thickness at gingival level 2.90 vs 2.28 mm (P < .008) as reported in the abstract.
- **Provisional vs none**: facial thickness 2.81 vs 2.37 mm (P < .06).
- **BGPR vs none**: 3.09 vs 2.03 mm at gingival level, P < .79 (not significant).
- **Incisal and middle zones**: no statistically significant association between graft and/or provisional restoration on vertical or horizontal dimension.
- **Authors' conclusion**: graft + provisional at flapless immediate placement increases vertical and labial-palatal soft-tissue dimensions by 0.5–1.0 mm versus neither, most often in gingival and middle zones and above the 2.0 mm threshold.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Retrospective** design with no long-term follow-up; treatment allocation not randomized and groups may differ by phenotype, implant position, and adjacent restorations (authors acknowledge these confounders).
- **No baseline (pre-implant) labial soft-tissue thickness** measurement, so change-from-baseline cannot be computed; all dimensions are post-healing cast measurements.
- **Cast/caliper measurement only** (no CBCT or histology); 0.8 mm caliper tip limited measurement intervals to 0.5 mm, and measurements may not reflect final abutment/restoration contours.
- **Internal inconsistency in significance reporting** (read directly from the PDF): the abstract attributes P < .008 to "grafted vs nongrafted" gingival thickness (2.90 vs 2.28 mm), whereas the Fig 13 layout places P = .008 on the PR-vs-no-PR pair (2.9 vs 2.8 mm); the body text also reverses the order of the 2.28/2.90 comparison. The largest mean difference (BGPR vs none, 3.09 vs 2.03 mm) carries P < .79. These should be treated as reported values, not as a verified effect estimate.
- **P < .06 used to support superiority** in two comparisons — does not meet the stated α = .05.
- **Clustering**: 45 sockets in 44 patients (one patient contributed two sites) with no clustered analysis reported.
- **Not tested**: soft-tissue grafting (SCTG/dermis allograft) alone or combined with bone grafting + provisional restoration; the authors call for future research.
- **Series dependency**: Part 1 (ridge dimensional change) is not held in this wiki; bone-level conclusions must not be imported from Part 2.

## 7. Glossary
- **BGPR**: bone graft + provisional restoration group.
- **PR / BG**: provisional restoration only / bone graft only groups.
- **FGM**: free gingival margin.
- **Gap (labial gap)**: the facial distance between the implant surface and the buccal socket wall after palatally biased placement.
- **Color masking threshold (2.0 mm)**: peri-implant mucosal thickness above which underlying abutment/metal color is considered adequately masked.
- **PEEK abutment**: polyether-ether-ketone abutment used for soft-tissue sculpting before definitive restoration.
