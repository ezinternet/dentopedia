## Why Ingested
수돗물 불소화 (Community Water Fluoridation, CWF)에 대한 Cochrane SR pub3 (2024 갱신). 기존 [[caries/worthington-2024-sdf-preventing-managing-dental-caries]] SDF 개인 도포 근거와 함께 집단 대 개인 불소 중재 비교 근거 완성. 불소화 프로그램의 공중보건 의사결정에 활용.

## One-line Summary
Cochrane SR (157 non-randomised studies, pub3): contemporary evidence for water fluoridation's caries prevention is very limited and very low certainty; older pre-1975 evidence suggests benefit but may not apply today; association with fluorosis established.

## 한줄요약
코크란 SR (관찰연구 157편, pub3): 수돗물 불소화 (Community Water Fluoridation, CWF)의 현대적 우식 예방 근거는 매우 제한적·낮은 확신도; 불소증과의 연관성은 확립.

- Journal: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2024

- Evidence current to: 16 August 2023
- Previous versions: pub1 (2015), pub2 (2015 extended)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Most comprehensive Cochrane SR on water fluoridation: 157 studies
- Stratifies evidence by pre/post-1975 (pre/post widespread fluoride toothpaste era)
- Establishes that contemporary evidence is very limited — only 21 studies on CWF initiation
- Confirms fluorosis association with water fluoridation (mostly non-aesthetic)
- Key policy context: CWF effects now confounded by background fluoride from toothpaste

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- 157 studies — ALL non-randomised designs (RCT not feasible)
- Objectives: (1) CWF initiation/cessation effects on caries; (2) water fluoridation association with fluorosis
- Pre-1975 vs post-1975 stratification
- Databases: CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase + 4 others + 2 trial registries; search August 2023
- All evidence downgraded for: confounding, non-random design, blinding, old evidence (indirectness)
- No new fluorosis evidence sought in this update

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Water fluoridation INITIATION:
- Pre-1975 studies: large reductions in caries (dmft/DMFT) — but LOW certainty at best (old evidence, not contemporary)
- Post-1975 contemporary evidence: VERY LIMITED, VERY LOW certainty
- Conclusion: cannot confidently determine magnitude of caries prevention in contemporary settings

Water fluoridation CESSATION:
- Very limited evidence; possible increase in caries after cessation; VERY LOW certainty

DENTAL FLUOROSIS:
- Association between water fluoridation and fluorosis established (mostly non-aesthetic)
- This objective not updated with new studies in pub3

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- RCTs not feasible (ethical/logistical reasons) → non-randomised evidence only
- Contemporary evidence very limited — high background fluoride from toothpaste makes CWF effect difficult to isolate
- Older evidence (pre-1975) may not apply to modern populations with widespread fluoride toothpaste
- Confounding by socioeconomic factors, diet, other fluoride sources
- Fluorosis section not updated in this version

## 7. Glossary
- 수돗물 불소화 (Community Water Fluoridation, CWF): 공중보건 차원의 최적 불소 농도(0.7ppm) 조정
- 치아 불소증 (Dental Fluorosis): 치아 발달기 과잉 불소 섭취로 인한 에나멜 외관 변화(백반~갈색 반점)
- dmft/DMFT: 유치/영구치의 우식(decayed)·발거(missing)·충전(filled) 치아 수 지표
