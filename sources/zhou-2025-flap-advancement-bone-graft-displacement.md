## Why Ingested

이 전임상 연구는 [[suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]]가 제시한 flap-advancement 전략 프레임워크(minor/moderate/major, PRI가 최대 +5.5mm 기여)에 대해, "전진량/장력이 실제로 GBR 이식재 안정성에 영향을 주는가"라는 하위 mechanism을 최초로 정량 검증한다 — 기존 프레임워크가 답하지 못했던 downstream outcome(graft displacement)을 채운다. 같은 연구팀(Frankfurt Goethe University Carolinum, Raabe 그룹)이 동일 돼지 반측하악골·CBCT ΔGMT 프로토콜로 결손 형태·4핀 고정을 다룬 동반논문([[bone-regeneration/raabe-2025-defect-morphology-membrane-fixation-graft]], DOI 10.1111/clr.70072)과 짝을 이루는 연구.

## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo preclinical study, 60 horizontal bone augmentation (HBA) procedures on 30 fresh pig hemimandibles with uncontained (concavity) defects, testing moderate (FA1, single periosteal releasing incision) vs. major (FA2, additional periosteal scoring) flap advancement, each with vs. without two-pin membrane fixation.

FA2 achieved greater advancement (9.20±0.55mm) at lower flap tension (0.02±0.01N) than FA1 (4.42±0.67mm; 0.09±0.02N), and crestal graft displacement (ΔGMT at L0) was significantly smaller for FA2 (−35.66±30.68%) than FA1 (−44.99±20.80%, p<0.001); two-pin membrane fixation showed no significant effect on ΔGMT at any level (p≥0.19), independent of flap advancement (interaction p=0.56).

Clinically, achieving greater flap advancement at lower tension — not adding two fixation pins — is the modifiable factor that best preserves crestal graft volume in uncontained defects during primary closure; limitation: ex vivo pig-mandible model, no healing period, single defect morphology, only two-pin fixation tested.

## 세줄요약

돼지 반측하악골(hemimandible) 30개, 미함입형(uncontained) 결손 60건을 대상으로 한 사체(ex vivo) 전임상 연구로, 중등도 피판전진(FA1, 골막이완절개 1회) vs 대량 피판전진(FA2, 추가 골막 스코어링)을 각각 2-핀 막 고정 유무와 조합해 비교했다.

FA2는 FA1보다 더 큰 전진량(9.20±0.55mm vs 4.42±0.67mm)을 더 낮은 장력(0.02±0.01N vs 0.09±0.02N)으로 달성했고, 치은변연부 이식재 변위(ΔGMT, L0)는 FA2에서 유의하게 작았다(−35.66±30.68% vs −44.99±20.80%, p<0.001); 2-핀 막 고정은 어느 레벨에서도 ΔGMT에 유의한 영향이 없었다(p≥0.19).

임상적으로 미함입형 결손에서는 2-핀 막 고정 추가보다 낮은 장력으로 더 큰 피판전진을 확보하는 것이 치은변연부 이식재 부피 보존에 더 중요한 조절 가능 인자다; 한계는 동적 술후 치유 과정이 없는 돼지 사체 모델, 단일 결손 형태, 2-핀 고정만 검증했다는 점.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to quantify how much of GBR graft-material displacement is attributable to the **magnitude of flap advancement / associated flap tension itself**, isolated from defect morphology and fixation, in an uncontained (concavity) defect model.
- Directly measured flap tension intraoperatively via a spring dynamometer attached to a central interrupted suture, linking a mechanically quantified variable (N) to a radiographic outcome (ΔGMT via CBCT).
- Tested two-pin membrane fixation specifically in an uncontained defect morphology (most prior fixation-benefit studies used contained/box-shaped defects), finding it insufficient — a defect-morphology-dependent boundary condition for when pin fixation helps.
- Ruled out soft-tissue phenotype (keratinized mucosa width, mucosal thickness) as a significant independent predictor of ΔGMT once flap tension is accounted for.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Ex vivo preclinical (2×2 factorial), pig hemimandible model, single-blind (closure surgeon blinded to design/objectives).
- **Setting**: 30 fresh hemimandibles from 30-week-old pigs (local butcher), Frankfurt am Main, Germany, June–October 2024; no ethical approval required (non-live animal tissue).
- **n**: 60 HBA procedures (30 mandibles × 2 sequential procedures per mandible: FA1 then FA2), 15 per group across 4 groups (FA1−Pins, FA1+Pins, FA2−Pins, FA2+Pins).
- **Intervention**: Standardized trapezoidal full-thickness mucoperiosteal flap over a bone concavity (diastema region, uncontained defect) augmented with particulate DBBM (Bio-Oss) + resorbable collagen membrane (Bio-Gide). FA1 = single ≈0.5mm perpendicular periosteal releasing incision (target 4–5mm advancement). FA2 = additional periosteal scoring/stretching (target 9–10mm advancement). Membrane fixation randomized: two non-threaded press-fit pins (Frios Plus) vs. none.
- **Outcome measurement**: Flap tension via spring dynamometer at a central interrupted suture; flap advancement via periodontal probe; graft material thickness (GMT, bone substitute + membrane) via CBCT at 9 levels (L0 = platform/crest to L8 = 8mm apical, 1mm increments), before (GMT_1) and after (GMT_2) primary wound closure. Primary outcome = ΔGMT (%) at L0. Intra-examiner ICC = 0.85.
- **Statistics**: Paired t-tests for ΔGMT; repeated-measures ANOVA (Greenhouse-Geisser corrected, Mauchly's sphericity rejected) for FA × Fixation × Level; GEE-based multiple linear regression (beta, 95% CI) for FA/fixation/soft-tissue-phenotype predictors of ΔGMT. Sample size (n=15/group) powered for 80% power, mean difference 0.6, SD 0.5, alpha 0.05 (ANOVA), based on a prior preclinical study.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Flap advancement and tension achieved:**

| Group | Flap advancement (mm) | Flap tension (N) |
|---|---|---|
| FA1 (moderate, single PRI) | 4.42 ± 0.67 | 0.09 ± 0.02 |
| FA2 (major, additional scoring) | 9.20 ± 0.55 | 0.02 ± 0.01 |

**ΔGMT (%) at L0 (crestal/primary outcome) and L4 (only other significant level), by FA:**

| Level | FA1 | FA2 | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 | −44.99 ± 20.80 | −35.66 ± 30.68 | <0.001 |
| L4 | −7.20 ± 8.61 | −1.68 ± 13.20 | 0.02 |
| L1–L3, L5–L8 | — | — | all >0.05 (ns) |

**ΔGMT (%) at L0, full 2×2 breakdown:**

| Group | ΔGMT (%) at L0 |
|---|---|
| FA1 − Pins | −42.3 ± 16.5 |
| FA1 + Pins | −47.6 ± 24.6 |
| FA2 − Pins | −30.6 ± 29.4 |
| FA2 + Pins | −40.7 ± 32.0 |

- Overall pins vs. no-pins: no significant difference in ΔGMT at any level (p≥0.19); within-FA comparisons also non-significant (FA1+Pins vs FA1−Pins p≥0.22; FA2+Pins vs FA2−Pins p≥0.22).
- No-pins subgroup: FA1 vs FA2 at L0 significant (−42.3% vs −30.6%, p=0.02); pins subgroup: FA1 vs FA2 at L0 not significant (−47.6% vs −40.7%, p=0.25).
- Repeated-measures ANOVA (Greenhouse-Geisser): FA main effect F=9.41, p=0.008; Fixation F=0.32, p=0.58; Level F=53.4, p<0.001; FA×Fixation p=0.56; FA×Level p=0.99; Fixation×Level p=0.91; three-way interaction p=0.56.
- GEE multiple regression: FA2 (vs. FA1 reference) beta=0.46 (95% CI 0.15–0.77), p=0.004 — the only significant independent predictor of ΔGMT. Mucosal thickness at 3mm (MT3) beta=0.51, p=0.06; MT9 beta=0.31, p=0.06 (both only marginal, and non-significant once tension-adjusted). KMW p=0.77, MT6 p=0.15 — not significant.
- Phenotypic baseline: KMW 1.93±0.47mm; MT3 1.85±0.49mm; MT6 3.60±0.99mm; MT9 6.15±0.87mm.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Ex vivo design: no postoperative healing period, inflammation, swelling, or functional loading (mastication/speech) — displacement measured only immediately after primary closure.
- Single defect morphology (uncontained bone concavity) and single fixation modality (two pins) tested; results may not generalize to contained (box-shaped) defects or alternative stabilization (periosteal suturing, fibrin sealant, more pins).
- Sequential FA1-then-FA2 procedures on the same hemimandible may have introduced soft-tissue fatigue/reduced elasticity (mitigated by continuous saline hydration; same-site design also controlled for inter-specimen phenotype variability).
- Single calibrated examiner for all CBCT measurements (no second blinded examiner) — high intra-rater ICC (0.85) but no inter-rater check.
- Authors call for in vivo animal or human clinical trials to confirm findings under biologically active healing conditions, and for biomechanical/digital simulation of real-time graft deformation.

## 6. Related Work

- Raabe C, Cafferata EA, Zhou W, et al. 2025 (same group, DOI 10.1111/clr.70072) — companion ex vivo study on the same pig-hemimandible/CBCT-ΔGMT protocol, varying defect morphology (partially contained vs. contained) and **four**-pin membrane fixation instead of flap-advancement magnitude; found four-pin fixation significantly halves crestal GMD (−15.8% vs −38.1%, p<0.001) — contrasts with this study's null result for **two**-pin fixation, suggesting fixation efficacy may be pin-count/defect-morphology dependent. See [[bone-regeneration/raabe-2025-defect-morphology-membrane-fixation-graft]].
- Plonka AB, Sheridan RA, Wang HL. 2017 — SR establishing the minor/moderate/major flap-advancement taxonomy and quantifying technique contributions (PRI +5.5mm); this study supplies the mechanistic graft-stability outcome that taxonomy did not test. See [[suture-wound-closure/plonka-2017-flap-designs-flap-advancement-implant-therapy]].
- Greenstein G, et al. — origin of the minor(<3mm)/moderate(3–6mm)/major(≥7mm) flap-advancement classification used to define this study's FA1/FA2 groups.

## 7. Glossary

- **HBA (Horizontal Bone Augmentation)**: GBR procedure to widen a deficient alveolar ridge using particulate graft + barrier membrane.
- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration)**: barrier-membrane-based bone augmentation technique.
- **ΔGMT (Change in Graft Material Thickness)**: primary outcome; % change in graft+membrane thickness (CBCT) between immediately after grafting and after primary wound closure.
- **FA1 / FA2**: moderate (single periosteal releasing incision, target 4–5mm) vs. major (additional periosteal scoring, target 9–10mm) flap advancement groups.
- **PRI (Periosteal Releasing Incision)**: incision through the periosteum at the flap base to release tension and permit coronal advancement.
- **L0–L8**: CBCT measurement levels from the implant-platform/crest (L0) to 8mm apically (L8), 1mm increments.
- **ICC (Intra-class Correlation Coefficient)**: measure of intra-examiner measurement reliability (0.85 here).
- **GEE (Generalized Estimating Equations)**: regression method used here to model correlated repeated ΔGMT measurements.
- **KMW / MT (Keratinized Mucosa Width / Mucosal Thickness)**: soft-tissue phenotypic covariates measured pre-op.
