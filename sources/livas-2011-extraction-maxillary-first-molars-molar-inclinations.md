## Why Ingested

Examines how orthodontic extraction of maxillary first molars (Begg technique, Class II Div 1) changes the spatial inclination of adjacent second and third maxillary molars — directly relevant to the extraction-treatment protocol context explored in [[wiki/occlusion/livas-2016-fixed-retention-unopposed-molar-overeruption]] by the same research group. Adds cephalometric evidence that the extraction protocol substantially uprights M2 and M3 and facilitates third molar eruption.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cephalometric study of 37 Class II Division 1 patients (mean age 13.2 years) treated with maxillary first molar extraction and Begg technique, compared against 54 archival untreated Class I/II controls (Nittedal Growth Material, Oslo) followed for ≥3.6–3.7 years.

Extraction resulted in dramatic M2 uprighting (M2-PP from 26.7° → 6.9°) and near-fourfold greater M3 uprighting (19.9° vs 5.6° in controls), with M2 shifting from distal to mesial inclination relative to the functional occlusal plane.

Extraction of maxillary first molars in Class II Division 1 patients significantly uprights M2 and M3 and facilitates normal third molar eruption, supporting this extraction protocol as an orthodontically beneficial strategy.

## 세줄요약

Class II Division 1 부정교합 환자 37명(평균 연령 13.2세)을 상악 제1대구치 발치 + Begg 기법으로 치료하고, 노르웨이 Nittedal 성장자료에서 추출한 비치료 대조군 54명과 측방두부계측방사선(Lateral Cephalometric Radiograph) 지표를 비교한 후향적 연구.

발치군에서 제2대구치 (Second Molar, M2)의 구개평면 (Palatal Plane, PP) 대비 기울기가 26.7°→6.9°로 현저히 개선됐고, 제3대구치 (Third Molar, M3)의 기능교합평면 (Functional Occlusal Plane, FOP) 대비 직립화가 대조군의 약 4배(19.9° vs 5.6°)였으며, M2는 원심경사에서 근심경사로 전환됐다.

상악 제1대구치 발치 교정은 Class II Division 1 증례에서 M2·M3를 현저히 직립화하고 제3대구치의 정상 맹출을 촉진하므로, 이 발치 프로토콜의 교정적 유익성을 지지하는 측방두부계측 근거다.

## Key Contributions

- First study to quantify both M2 and M3 inclination changes relative to PP and FOP after maxillary first molar extraction in a Class II Div 1 orthodontic sample with an archival growth control
- Demonstrated that extraction-group M2 shifts from distal inclination (14.2° at T1) to mesial inclination (−6.2° at T2) relative to FOP — a direction reversal not seen in controls
- Showed M3 uprighting in extraction group was ~4 times that of untreated controls (19.9° vs 5.6°)
- Established that 43% of controls never achieved the M3 distal inclination attained by every extraction patient (maximum distal inclination 9.4° in extraction group)
- Provided cephalometric evidence supporting maxillary first molar extraction as a beneficial orthodontic strategy for facilitating M3 eruption

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective case-control (treated vs archival untreated)
- **Experimental group**: n = 37 (18 boys, 19 girls); mean age 13.2 ± 1.62 years; Class II Div 1 malocclusion, overjet ≥ 4 mm, no missing teeth, M3 present; all treated with maxillary first molar extraction + Begg technique
- **Control group**: n = 54 (18 boys, 36 girls); mean age 13.4 ± 1.99 years; Class I and II, untreated; drawn from Nittedal Growth Material, Oslo University
- **Imaging**: Standardized lateral cephalometric radiographs at T1 (start of active treatment) and T2 (≥3.7 years post-treatment for experimental; ≥3.6 years for control)
- **Measurements**: M2 and M3 inclinations relative to (1) palatal plane (PP) and (2) functional occlusal plane (FOP)
- **Statistics**: Paired comparisons within groups; between-group comparisons at T1 and T2

## Key Results

| Measurement | Control T1 | Control T2 | Extraction T1 | Extraction T2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M2-PP (°) | 17.7 ± 5.81 | 11.9 ± 4.61 | 26.7 ± 5.75 | 6.9 ± 6.76 |
| M3-PP (°) | 30.1 ± 8.54 | 19.6 ± 9.01 | 32.2 ± 7.90 | 12.8 ± 7.36 |
| M2-FOP (°) | no sig. change | — | 14.2 ± 4.62 | −6.2 ± 6.10 (P < 0.0001) |
| M3-FOP (ΔT2–T1) | 5.6° uprighting | — | 19.9° uprighting | — |

- Both groups improved M2-PP and M3-PP significantly (within-group)
- Extraction group: M2-FOP reversal from distal to mesial (P < 0.0001); no change in controls
- M3 uprighting relative to FOP was ~4× greater in extraction group (19.9° vs 5.6°)
- Maximum distal inclination of M3 in extraction group at T2: 9.4° — attained by only 43% of controls

## Limitations

- Archival control from a different geographic/ethnic population (Norwegian vs extraction group's origin not fully described as white)
- Begg technique is not the predominant modern technique; results may differ with current fixed appliance systems
- No mandibular molar inclination data
- Radiographic limitations of 2D cephalometrics for 3D molar position changes
- No reporting of M3 eruption rates (clinical eruption), only inclination changes

## Glossary

- **Palatal plane (PP)**: Reference line from ANS (anterior nasal spine) to PNS (posterior nasal spine); used as angular reference for maxillary molar inclination in lateral cephalometrics
- **Functional occlusal plane (FOP)**: Bisector of the overlap of posterior teeth; more directly related to clinical occlusal function than PP
- **M2 / M3**: Abbreviations for second molar and third molar, respectively
- **Begg technique**: Light-wire differential-force orthodontic technique using round archwires and Class II elastics; involves first-stage tipping then uprighting
- **Distal inclination**: Crown tipped distally (toward the throat) relative to the reference plane; in M2/M3 context, correlates with impaction risk
- **Uprighting**: Movement of the crown mesially to bring the long axis more vertical; reduces impaction risk for M3
