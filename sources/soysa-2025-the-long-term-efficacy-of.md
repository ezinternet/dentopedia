## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of RCTs (52 articles = 43 independent studies; 33 articles in the meta-analysis; ≥6-month follow-up; PROSPERO CRD42021247300) of locally delivered tetracycline-class sustained-release adjuncts (tetracycline fiber, doxycycline gel, minocycline microspheres/ointment) added to scaling and root planing (SRP) in chronic periodontitis, including a smokers vs non-smokers subgroup.

Adjunct benefit was modest but significant: PPD WMD 0.516 mm at 6–9 months and 0.371 mm at ≥12 months; CAL WMD 0.336 mm and 0.310 mm; split-mouth designs inflated effects (meta-regression β=0.422 for PPD). Egger's test showed significant publication bias for PPD and CAL, and most RCTs were at unclear/high risk of bias.

The abstract's claim that non-smokers gained more than smokers long-term (PPD 0.630 vs 0.112 mm) is not supported by the paper's own meta-regression (smoking moderator P=0.0513 for long-term PPD, P=0.566 for CAL; both P>0.05 at 6–9 months), so smoking-specific conclusions are exploratory.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA(RCT 52편 = 독립 연구 43개, 메타분석 33편, 추적 ≥6개월, PROSPERO CRD42021247300): 만성 치주염에서 치석제거·치근활택(SRP)에 국소 서방형 테트라사이클린계(테트라사이클린 파이버·독시사이클린 겔·미노사이클린 마이크로스피어/연고)를 추가한 효과를 평가하고 흡연자 대 비흡연자 하위분석 포함.

추가 효과는 유의하나 작음: 치주낭 깊이(PPD) WMD 6–9개월 0.516 mm·≥12개월 0.371 mm, 임상 부착 수준(CAL) 0.336 mm·0.310 mm; 분할구강(split-mouth) 설계가 효과를 키움(메타회귀 PPD β=0.422); Egger 검정에서 PPD·CAL 출판편향 유의, 대부분 RCT가 비뚤림 위험 불명확/높음.

초록의 "비흡연자가 장기적으로 더 큰 이득(PPD 0.630 vs 0.112 mm)"은 논문 자체 메타회귀(흡연 조절변수 장기 PPD P=0.0513, CAL P=0.566)로 뒷받침되지 않으므로 흡연 관련 결론은 탐색적 수준이다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First tetracycline-class-only local-delivery SR+MA with a ≥12-month stratum, product-level (Actisite, Atridox, Ligosan, Arestin, Dentomycin, minocycline ointment) and drug-level subgroups, and secondary outcomes (BOP, PI, GI).
- Pooled adjunct effect is small (~0.3–0.5 mm); minocycline (especially minocycline ointment) is the most consistent across outcomes and time frames; doxycycline PPD benefit is not significant at ≥12 months.
- Meta-regression identifies study design (split-mouth) as the only significant moderator.
- Authors conclude local tetracycline-class agents are broadly usable, with smokers benefiting but possibly losing long-term PPD gain (exploratory).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR+MA, PRISMA 2020; PROSPERO CRD42021247300.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Cochrane CENTRAL, Scopus, Embase (to Dec 2024) + reference lists; English-language RCTs only.
- **Inclusion**: adults with chronic/adult periodontitis; SRP + locally delivered tetracycline/doxycycline/minocycline vs SRP alone or SRP + vehicle/placebo; parallel or split-mouth; ≥6-month follow-up. Excluded: systemic antimicrobials, local antimicrobial monotherapy, non–sustained-release vehicles, diabetic-only populations.
- **n**: 52 articles → 43 independent studies (17 split-mouth, 26 parallel; 31 single-center, 12 multicenter); 33 articles in the meta-analysis; 2,525 control / 2,575 test patients (Discussion). 27 studies initial-therapy, 14 maintenance, 2 both. 6 non-smokers-only, 16 mixed, 4 smokers-only, 17 not reported.
- **Outcomes**: PPD, CAL (primary); BOP, PI, GI (secondary); adverse effects.
- **Statistics**: WMD of baseline-to-endpoint change (correlation r=0.5 assumed), random-effects (DerSimonian–Laird), I², subgroup analysis and meta-regression, Egger + trim-and-fill, leave-one-out sensitivity; R 4.4.2; Cochrane RoB tool.
- **Time frames**: medium-term 6–9 months; long-term ≥12 months.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | 6–9 months | ≥12 months |
|---|---|---|
| PPD WMD (mm) | 0.516 (95% CI 0.413–0.620), 42 comparisons, I² 72.4% | 0.371 (0.181–0.560), 21 comparisons, I² 74.4% |
| CAL WMD (mm) | 0.336 (0.204–0.467), 35 comparisons, I² 79.2% | 0.310 (0.240–0.381), 18 comparisons, I² 25.1% |
| BOP WMD (index) | 0.261 (0.085–0.437), 5 comparisons | 0.302 (0.180–0.425), 5 comparisons |
| PI WMD (index) | 0.089 (0.034–0.144), 13 comparisons | 0.124 (0.042–0.207), 7 comparisons |
| GI WMD (index) | 0.155 (0.064–0.247), 12 comparisons | 0.155 (0.075–0.235), 7 comparisons |

BOP/PI/GI values are index-score differences; the paper's text sometimes labels them "mm".

**By drug (PPD / CAL WMD, mm)**
- Tetracycline: 6–9 mo PPD 0.740 (n=8) / CAL 0.460; ≥12 mo PPD 0.427 (n=3, P=0.0538, n.s.) / CAL 0.605 (n=3, significant).
- Doxycycline: 6–9 mo PPD 0.454 (n=7) / CAL 0.464; ≥12 mo PPD 0.143 (n.s.) / CAL 0.282 (n=7, significant).
- Minocycline: 6–9 mo PPD 0.460 (n=27) / CAL 0.241; ≥12 mo PPD 0.580 (n=10) / CAL 0.307 (n=8).

**By product**: Actisite 6–9 mo PPD 0.705 (n=5), ≥12 mo PPD 0.554 (n=2) / CAL 0.795 (n=2); minocycline ointment ≥12 mo PPD 0.745 (n=4) / CAL 0.329 (n=4); Arestin 6–9 mo PPD 0.406 (n=17); Atridox 6–9 mo PPD 0.446, CAL 0.495, ≥12 mo CAL 0.281 (PPD n.s.); Ligosan 6–9 mo PPD 0.518, CAL 0.408; Dentomycin CAL n.s. at both times.

**Moderators**
- Study design: split-mouth vs parallel PPD 0.819 (n=13) vs 0.391 (n=29) at 6–9 mo; meta-regression β=0.422 (P=0.0001) PPD, β=0.339 (P=0.0094) CAL (medium-term).
- Smoking: 6–9 mo PPD smokers 0.709 (n=2) vs non-smokers 0.694 (n=7); CAL 0.710 (n=2) vs 0.734 (n=4). ≥12 mo PPD smokers 0.112 (n=4, n.s.) vs non-smokers 0.630 (n=4, P=0.0109); CAL smokers 0.464 (n=4, P=0.0048) vs non-smokers 0.715 (n=2, n.s., P=0.144). Meta-regression smoking/non-smoking: PPD ≥12 mo P=0.0513, CAL ≥12 mo P=0.566, 6–9 mo PPD P=0.753, CAL P=0.910. The text states smoking status did not significantly moderate outcomes.
- Phase: initial vs maintenance ≥12 mo PPD 0.498 vs 0.179 (n.s.); CAL 0.340 vs 0.206 (n.s.); not a significant moderator.
- Assessment: partial-mouth PPD ≥12 mo 0.444 vs full-mouth 0.034 (n.s.).

**Bias and quality**: Egger significant for PPD (6–9 mo intercept 0.486, 9 imputed → adjusted 0.397; ≥12 mo intercept 0.419, 7 imputed → adjusted 0.587) and CAL (6–9 mo 9 imputed → adjusted 0.189; ≥12 mo no adjustment); BOP/PI/GI no bias. Allocation concealment low risk in only ~5–6 studies; only three studies rated moderate risk overall; remaining judged high/unclear.

**Adverse effects**: rarely reported (gingival redness, tingling, headache, abscess, root sensitivity, taste change, tongue pigmentation).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Substantial heterogeneity (I² up to 79%); most trials at high/unclear risk of bias; significant publication bias for PPD/CAL; English-only, no grey literature.
- Internal inconsistency: abstract says 52 RCTs; the body counts 43 independent studies (33 meta-analyzed) and elsewhere "42 RCTs"; abstract claims smokers vs non-smokers differ at P<0.05, but meta-regression does not support it, and the non-smoker long-term CAL gain was non-significant.
- Smoker subgroups are tiny (n=2–4 comparisons); 17 studies did not report smoking status; definitions varied.
- Trials use pre-2017 "chronic periodontitis"/SRP terminology; no patient-reported outcomes or cost-effectiveness; furcation lesions understudied (only 3 furcation trials).
- Authors call for larger long-term RCTs, standardized smoking definitions, head-to-head and cost-effectiveness studies, and furcation-specific trials.

## 6. Related Work
- Ramanauskaite 2020 (held): sustained-release antiseptics beat irrigation; Soysa narrows to the tetracycline class with longer follow-up.
- Annisa 2023 (held): CHX chips inferior to other local antimicrobials (tetracycline, minocycline, metronidazole) for PPD; consistent with tetracycline-class PPD benefit.
- John 2017 NMA (held): adjuncts add only ~0.3 mm CAL with ~20% publication-bias inflation; Soysa's ≥12-month CAL 0.310 mm matches.
- Smiley 2015 ADA guideline (held, partially superseded) and Sanz 2020 EFP guideline (held): local delivery effective only at selected (PPD ≥5 mm / high-risk) sites; Soysa supports a small but real tetracycline-class effect.
- Milinkovic 2025 RCT (held): single-session local antibiotic ≈ 7-day systemic AMX/MET at 6 months.
- Authors' external comparators (not held): Herrera et al. (PPD 0.365 mm at 6–9 mo), Matesanz-Pérez et al., Nadig & Shah, Chambrone et al. (smokers).

## 7. Glossary
- **SRP (Scaling and Root Planing)**: Non-surgical subgingival debridement; the control/baseline therapy here.
- **WMD (Weighted Mean Difference)**: Pooled adjunct-minus-control difference in change from baseline.
- **Split-mouth design**: Within-patient comparison of treated vs control sites; reduces between-patient variance but here associated with larger effects.
- **Sustained-release local delivery**: Fibers (Actisite), gels (Atridox, Ligosan), microspheres (Arestin), ointment/gel (Dentomycin, minocycline ointment) that keep drug in the pocket for days.
- **Trim-and-fill**: Publication-bias adjustment that imputes missing studies.
- **PPD / CAL / BOP / PI / GI**: Probing pocket depth, clinical attachment level, bleeding on probing, plaque index, gingival index.
