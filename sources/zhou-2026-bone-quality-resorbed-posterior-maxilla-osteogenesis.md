## Why Ingested

측방 상악동거상술 문헌은 이식재 종류·입자크기·PRF 병용이 신생골 (New Bone, NB) 비율을 얼마나 올리는지에 집중돼 있고([[sinus-lift/lateral/somngam-2025-new-bone-formation-biphasic-calcium]], [[sinus-lift/lateral/harlos-2022-histomorphometric-evaluation-of-different-graft]]), **수혜부 자체의 골질**이 골형성 결과를 좌우하는지는 비어 있었다. 본 후향적 연구(n=176명/190동)는 흡수된 후방 상악의 잔존 골소주 비율과 술후 신생골 비율의 상관(r=0.23)을 조직형태계측으로 제시해, 이식재 선택 축과 별개인 "수혜부 골질" 축을 위키에 추가한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (Shanghai Ninth People's Hospital, 2015–2021; 176 patients / 190 sinuses, 168 patients / 182 sinuses analysed) of lateral-window maxillary sinus floor augmentation with DBBM in advanced posterior maxillary atrophy (RBH ≤ 4 mm), combining preoperative CBCT cortical-bone classification with histomorphometry of trephine biopsies taken at second-stage implant placement.

Native bone trabeculae averaged 40.01% ± 14.85% and newly formed bone in the grafted area 18.77% ± 5.69%; native trabecular percentage correlated positively with new bone formation (r = 0.23, P = .04), while age, sex, healing time (P = .06) and radiographic alveolar type (P = .82) showed no significant association.

Denser residual trabecular bone may favour osteogenesis after sinus floor augmentation, but the correlation is weak (r = 0.23, ~5% of variance), the design is retrospective and single-centre with one surgeon and one graft material, and no implant-level outcomes were reported.

## 세줄요약

후향적 코호트 (중국 상하이 제9인민병원, 2015–2021년; 환자 176명·상악동 190개 접수, 최종 분석 168명·182동): 잔존골높이 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) ≤ 4 mm의 심한 후방 상악 위축에서 탈단백우골무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)로 측방접근 상악동저거상술 (Maxillary Sinus Floor Augmentation, MSFA) 후 2차 임플란트 식립 시 채취한 골핵 생검을 조직형태계측하고, 술전 콘빔전산화단층촬영 (Cone-Beam Computed Tomography, CBCT) 피질골 분류와 대조.

원래 골소주 (Native Bone Trabecula) 비율 40.01% ± 14.85%, 이식부 신생골 (New Bone, NB) 비율 18.77% ± 5.69%; 원래 골소주 비율과 신생골 비율은 유의한 양의 상관 (r = 0.23, P = .04)이었으나 나이·성별·치유기간 (P = .06)·방사선학적 치조형 (P = .82)은 유의하지 않았다.

잔존 골소주가 치밀할수록 골형성이 촉진될 가능성을 시사하나 상관강도가 약하고(r = 0.23, 설명력 약 5%) 후향적·단일기관·단일 술자·단일 이식재 연구이며 임플란트 수준 결과가 없어 예후 예측 도구로 쓰기엔 근거가 부족하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Characterises the **recipient site** in the severely resorbed posterior maxilla on two axes at once: a CBCT cortical-bone classification (modified from Choucroun et al.) and histomorphometric native trabecular fraction from the same sinuses.
- Reports the distribution of residual cortical-bone configurations in RBH ≤ 4 mm ridges: bicortical 42.21%, unicortical 25.97%, no cortical bone 19.48%, sinus floor–crestal bone fusion 12.34%.
- Tests — and largely rejects — the assumption that the radiographic cortical pattern is a proxy for histological bone density: native trabecular % did not differ across the four radiographic types (P = .65).
- Quantifies the host-quality → osteogenesis link in a large biopsy series: native trabecular % vs newly formed bone % r = 0.23 (P = .04), independent of age, sex, healing time and radiographic type.
- Adds a histological reference range for the resorbed posterior maxilla (native trabeculae 40.01% ± 14.85%), positioned against Todisco & Trisi (~30–34%) and Lindhe (~55% in fully healed edentulous maxillae).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective cohort / chart-and-biopsy review, STROBE-reported, single centre, single surgeon.
- **Inclusion**: adults ≥18 y, maxillary molar/premolar loss ≥3 months, RBH ≤ 4 mm with adequate ridge width, signed informed consent.
- **Exclusion**: active sinus infection; uncontrolled inflammatory disease (incl. uncontrolled periodontal disease, uncontrolled diabetes); heavy smoking (>10 cigarettes/day); head/neck radiotherapy >60 Gy; osteoporosis or bisphosphonate/steroid therapy; incomplete records. (Four additional patients excluded for simultaneous ridge augmentation at MSFA.)
- **Surgery**: lateral-window MSFA with piezoelectric osteotomy, Schneiderian membrane elevation, grafting with DBBM granules (Bio-Oss), native collagen membrane (Bio-Gide) over the window, primary closure.
- **Staged placement + biopsy**: 4–12 months later, trephine core (outer Ø 4.0 mm, inner Ø 3.0 mm, length 10 mm) at the grafted site, then 10–12 mm tissue-level implants (Straumann). One specimen per patient, taken from the site with the lowest RBH when multiple implants were planned. Fixation in 10% neutral buffered formalin.
- **Radiographic assessment**: CBCT (Planmeca Promax; 5 mA, 96 kV; voxel 0.2 mm; FOV 13 × 9 cm), DICOM reconstructed in coDiagnostiX, within 1 month pre-op. RBH measured on sagittal sections along the virtual implant axis. Bone quality classified by cortical configuration into type a (bicortical: both sinus floor and crestal bone cortical), b (unicortical: one of the two cortical), c (neither cortical), d (no bone coronal to the sinus floor — fusion of sinus floor and crestal bone).
- **Histology**: decalcified, paraffin-embedded, 6 µm sections along the long axis, H&E; three sections per specimen (#10, #20, #30); ×4 overview for native bone area, ×10 image from a randomly selected region of the grafted area; Image Pro-Plus 6.0, colour-contrast segmentation with manual tuning; area % = selected pixels / total pixels; mean of 3 sections per specimen.
- **Reliability**: two independent examiners (>5 y experience, not involved in treatment); Kappa for the categorical radiographic type, ICC for continuous histomorphometric outcomes; all measurements analysed at sinus and biopsy level.
- **Statistics**: one-way ANOVA (native trabecular % by radiographic type); multiple linear regression with r as correlation coefficient (native trabecular %, new bone %, healing time, radiographic type); GEE with age (continuous) and sex (binary) as fixed effects and exchangeable correlation structure to adjust for bilateral sinuses in the same patient; α = .05; PASW Statistics 18.0.

**Authors' interpretation**: denser native bone — read as a larger osteoblast reservoir and stronger structural support — is associated with more new bone in the grafted area, whereas porous, matrix-poor recipient bone may compromise graft integration and maturation. They contrast the native trabecular mean with Todisco & Trisi (33.83% ± 10.92% premolar, 30.62% ± 14.08% molar) and with Lindhe (~55.2% mineralised bone in fully healed posterior maxilla), attributing the lower values to the inverse relationship between RBH and trabecular bone quantity/quality.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Retrospective design** with unmeasured confounding (graft volume, sinus anatomy, membrane handling, smoking history within the ≤10/day allowance), acknowledged by the authors as the primary limitation.
- **Weak correlation**: r = 0.23 (≈5% of variance explained) with a wide confidence interval; the reported 95% CI 0.01–0.19 is narrower than the point estimate and appears to be the coefficient's CI rather than a variance-explained interval — reported verbatim here without reinterpretation.
- **Bone quality is a structural surrogate** (mineralised trabecular area %), not bone mineral density; vascularity — a key biological component — was not assessed.
- **2D assessments only**: CBCT slices and histological sections cannot capture 3D sinus-floor complexity; no micro-CT.
- **Heterogeneous healing time** (4.0–12.4 months) in a staged protocol; the authors defend this with their own prospective study showing no difference in new bone at 5, 7 and 11 months, and with the non-significant regression coefficient (P = .06).
- **Single centre, single surgeon, single graft material (DBBM + collagen membrane)** — generalisability to other grafts, membrane strategies or transcrestal approaches is untested.
- **No implant-level outcomes** (survival, ISQ, insertion torque, marginal bone loss) and no radiographic graft-volume/3D stability data; the clinical claim that preoperative bone-quality assessment "can help predict—and potentially improve—osteogenic outcomes" remains a hypothesis.
- **Attrition of the histology subsample**: 95 grafted-area specimens out of 182 sinuses (52%) — the smallest analysed subset, and the one carrying the primary correlation.

## 6. Related Work

- Todisco & Trisi [20]: histomorphometric bone volume 33.83% ± 10.92% (premolar) and 30.62% ± 14.08% (molar) in the maxilla — the closest reference values, though from a much smaller sample without stated alveolar-bone status.
- Lindhe et al. [13,14]: ~55.2% mineralised bone in fully healed edentulous posterior maxilla — the higher benchmark the authors explain via RBH-dependent bone loss.
- Reich et al. [24]: positive correlation between native and grafted bone quality in sinus biopsies — the direct precedent for this study's finding.
- Choucroun et al. [17]: the cortical-configuration classification the authors modified; Lekholm & Zarb [9] and Misch [10]: the traditional 2D/tactile classifications critiqued here.
- Zhou et al. [16] (same group): digital wax-up/surgical planning protocol and the finding that sinus floor width, not RBH, correlates with new bone formation — cited as the anatomical confounder set.
- Graft-material and adjunct literature: DBBM is the reference xenograft; the study does not test particle size, PRF or BCP variants, so its native-bone axis is orthogonal to the material-selection literature held elsewhere in this wiki.

## 7. Glossary

- **MSFA (Maxillary Sinus Floor Augmentation)**: elevation of the sinus floor with graft placement to gain vertical bone height in the atrophic posterior maxilla.
- **RBH (Residual Bone Height)**: crest-to-sinus-floor vertical distance measured along the planned implant axis; ≤4 mm here defines advanced atrophy.
- **DBBM (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral)**: porous carbonate apatite xenograft (Bio-Oss) with osteoconductive but limited osteogenic capacity.
- **Native bone trabecula %**: mineralised native trabecular area divided by total area in the non-grafted biopsy region — a structural/histomorphometric surrogate for recipient-site bone quality.
- **Newly formed bone %**: area of deeply stained, osteocyte-rich bone covering/bridging DBBM particles within the grafted region.
- **Bicortical / unicortical / no cortical bone / floor–crestal fusion**: the four CBCT types (a–d) based on whether the sinus floor and the alveolar crest each present a cortical layer.
- **GEE (Generalised Estimating Equations)**: regression for clustered observations (bilateral sinuses in one patient) with an exchangeable correlation structure.
- **ICC / Kappa**: intraclass correlation coefficient (continuous agreement) / Cohen's kappa (categorical agreement).
