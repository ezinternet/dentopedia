## Why Ingested

Growing-patient case combining facemask maxillary protraction + clear aligner (Anglebutton attachment) + miniscrew posterior anchorage/intrusion for a rare unilateral scissor bite — extends the clear aligner literature into interceptive/growth-modification territory alongside [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/fonseca-planells-2026-clear-aligner-maxillary-expansion-growing]] (CAT in growing patients) and [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/marinelli-2025-temporary-anchorage-devices-clear-aligner-sr]] (TAD-assisted CAT biomechanics).

## Three-line Summary

Case report, n=1 (11yr 3mo girl), Angle Class III malocclusion with severe unilateral posterior scissor bite treated with combined facemask + clear aligner (Anglebutton attachment) + posterior miniscrew anchorage/intrusion + occlusal pads on the healthy side.

Active treatment lasted 17 months and achieved maxillary protraction, correction of the scissor bite, Class I canine/molar relationships, and correction of the occlusal cant via intrusion of the extruded upper-left posterior segment.

Single case report — no comparator or long-term follow-up data reported in the retrieved text; illustrates feasibility of using clear aligners as dental anchorage during orthopedic (facemask) correction in a growing Class III patient.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates a triple-combination protocol (facemask + clear aligner with Anglebutton attachments + miniscrew anchorage) for a growing patient with skeletal Class III and severe unilateral scissor bite — a malocclusion combination with limited case-report precedent.
- Shows clear aligners can serve as dental anchorage during orthopedic maxillary protraction (facemask), broadening the described scope of clear aligner application beyond purely dentoalveolar movement.
- Illustrates use of miniscrews for simultaneous posterior anchorage and intrusion to correct an occlusal cant caused by extrusion of the upper-left buccal segment.
- Reports a relatively short active treatment time (17 months) for a complex 3D skeletal and dental discrepancy in a growing patient.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: single case report (n=1).
- Patient: female, 11 years 3 months at presentation; chief complaint of difficulty occluding posterior teeth and protruding lower anterior teeth; described preference for chewing on the right side only; no additional medical conditions noted.
- Diagnosis: Angle Class III malocclusion with severe unilateral (right-sided, per figures referenced) posterior scissor bite; slightly asymmetric mandible/chin; mesoprosopic facial pattern; extrusion of the upper-left buccal segment causing occlusal cant; incisor crowding.
- Treatment objectives: improve soft-tissue profile/smile esthetics; correct unilateral posterior scissor bite; eliminate occlusal cant from the extruded upper-left buccal segment; establish ideal overjet/overbite; correct incisor crowding.
- Treatment alternatives considered: (1) delay treatment until growth completion, then decide camouflage vs. surgery-orthodontics combination depending on severity; (2) proceed now with crossbite/scissor-bite correction given maxillary insufficiency and growth potential — the option selected.
- Protocol: gingivectomy of the upper posterior segment performed first; intraoral scan 2 months later for virtual aligner design in iOrtho® software (v5.6, Angelalign Technology, Shanghai); two AngelButton™ attachments placed on the aligner around the upper canines to bond facemask elastics; an additional AngelButton™ placed buccally (detail truncated in source); facemask (Tiantian, Changsha, China) used for maxillary protraction; miniscrews inserted in the posterior region for anchorage and intrusion of extruded segment; occlusal pads used on the healthy (non-scissor-bite) side.
- Active treatment duration: 17 months.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Maxillary protraction achieved with proclination of the upper incisor.
- Improved facial profile with a more symmetrical, harmonious smile; adequate gingival display without black corridors (per source text).
- Occlusal plane corrected via intrusion of the upper-left posterior segment.
- Scissor bite unlocked and corrected, attributed to occlusal-surface attachments plus the bite-plane effect of the aligners.
- Ideal intercuspation with Class I canine and molar relationships achieved.
- No quantitative cephalometric or model-based measurements (e.g., ANB, overjet/overbite mm changes) were present in the retrieved (truncated) text.

## 7. Glossary

- **Scissor bite (Brodie bite)**: a malocclusion where the buccal cusps of the maxillary posterior teeth occlude buccal to the mandibular posterior teeth, often from excessive maxillary width and/or mandibular deficiency.
- **Facemask (FM)**: extraoral orthopedic appliance used to protract the maxilla in growing Class III patients, typically anchored via elastics to intraoral hooks/attachments.
- **AngelButton™**: a proprietary clear-aligner attachment (Angelalign Technology) used here to bond elastics from the aligner to the facemask and to create an occlusal bite-plane/intrusive effect.
- **Miniscrew (TAD)**: temporary skeletal anchorage device used for absolute anchorage during tooth movement (here, posterior anchorage and intrusion).
- **Occlusal cant**: a tilt/asymmetry of the occlusal plane, here caused by extrusion of the upper-left buccal segment.
- **iOrtho®**: clear aligner treatment-planning software (Angelalign Technology) used for virtual setup.
