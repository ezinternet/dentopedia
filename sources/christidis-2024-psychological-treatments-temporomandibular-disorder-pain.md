## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42022320106) of psychological interventions for painful temporomandibular disorders (TMD): 18 RCTs in the narrative synthesis, 6 in the meta-analysis.

Narrative synthesis: psychological treatments appear equivalent to standard treatment for pain; meta-analysis: psychological + standard + manual treatment was significantly better for pain reduction than counselling and standard treatment alone (very-low-quality evidence).

Psychological treatment is a promising add-on rather than a replacement; the meta-analytic signal rests on very-low-certainty evidence from only six RCTs.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Dedicated systematic synthesis of psychological treatments for painful TMD, short- and long-term perspective.
- Pairs a narrative synthesis (18 RCTs) with a smaller meta-analysis (6 RCTs), risk of bias by Cochrane tool and certainty by GRADE.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Searched MEDLINE, CINAHL, EMBASE, Cochrane CENTRAL and Web of Science for RCTs of psychological interventions in TMD; preregistered in PROSPERO (CRD42022320106). Risk of bias: Cochrane tool; certainty: GRADE.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Abstract-level only (no numeric pooled estimates available in the abstract): 18 RCTs included; 6 pooled. Psychological options seem equivalent to standard treatment; the combination of psychological treatment with standard and manual treatment was significantly better in pain reduction than counselling plus standard treatments alone (very-low-quality evidence).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Full text not retrieved: effect sizes, CIs, heterogeneity, specific psychological modalities and risk-of-bias ratings are not available here. Abstract itself flags very-low-quality evidence for the meta-analytic finding and only 6 of 18 RCTs being poolable.
