## Why Ingested
Adds a controlled, randomized split-mouth in-vitro comparator to the wiki's osseodensification (OD) ridge-expansion cluster, which so far rests on uncontrolled clinical data ([[wiki/implants/osseodensification/koutouzis-2019-alveolar-ridge-expansion-osseodensification-multicenter-retrospective]]) and animal histology ([[wiki/implants/osseodensification/tian-2019-alveolar-ridge-expansion-osseodensification-osteotome]]). Its new outcome — measured buccal/lingual peri-implant defect extent after OD vs conventional drilling — is a dimension the cluster lacks, and it quantifies the buccal-ridge-thickness gain digitally (3D STL) rather than by caliper.

## Three-line Summary
Randomized split-mouth in-vitro pilot on 10 fresh pig mandibles with limited ridge thickness (20 implants, 4.5 × 10 mm): conventional cutting-bur drilling (CTL, n = 10) vs osseodensification with Densah® burs (OD, n = 10).

OD gave significantly higher insertion torque (49.9 ± 11.45 vs 40.4 ± 8.07 N·cm), drastically smaller buccal defects (height 0.37 ± 0.72 vs 2.5 ± 2.14 mm; only 1 buccal defect > 0.5 mm vs 8), and greater buccal ridge expansion at the crest and 1 mm level after preparation (0.66 ± 0.64 vs 0.18 ± 0.2 mm).

OD expands the ridge and prevents buccal peri-implant defects versus conventional drilling, but as an ex-vivo pilot with no healing phase and no sample-size calculation, clinical benefit needs confirmation in human studies.

무작위 분할구 (Randomized split-mouth) in vitro 예비연구 — 제한된 치조제 두께의 신선 돼지 하악골 10개, 4.5 × 10 mm 임플란트 20개(군당 n=10): 기존 절삭버 드릴링 (Conventional Drilling, CTL) vs 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD) Densah® 버.

결과 — 삽입 토크 (Insertion Torque): OD 49.9 ± 11.45 vs CTL 40.4 ± 8.07 N·cm (p<0.05); 순측 결손 높이 0.37 ± 0.72 vs 2.5 ± 2.14 mm (0.5 mm 초과 순측 결손 1개 vs 8개); 순측 능선 확장 (치조정부): 0.66 ± 0.64 vs 0.18 ± 0.2 mm — 모두 OD 우세.

OD는 치조골 능선을 확장하고 임플란트 주위 순측 결손을 예방하나, 치유 기간 없는 사체조직 in vitro 예비연구 + 표본수 계산 부재로 사람 대상 임상확증 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First randomized split-mouth in-vitro comparison of OD vs conventional drilling with peri-implant **bone defect extent** (height, width, area) as an outcome — measured on both buccal and lingual walls, a dimension rarely addressed in the OD literature.
- Quantifies the OD-mediated **increase in buccal ridge thickness** using digital 3D STL analysis (intraoral scan, blinded evaluator, duplicate measurements) at the crest and 1, 2, 4, 6 mm apical — at two time points (post-preparation, post-placement).
- Shows OD significantly **raises insertion torque** vs conventional drilling in limited-thickness fresh pig mandibular sites (49.9 ± 11.45 vs 40.4 ± 8.07 N·cm).
- Proposes that OD's expansion + defect prevention may allow simultaneous implant placement and bone grafting, reducing the need for more invasive onlay grafting in ridges with compromised thickness.
- Highlights the lingual wall's limited expandability (thicker, less plastic lingual cortex) — the expansion benefit concentrates on the buccal side.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized split-mouth in-vitro pilot (ex-vivo porcine). Randomization (Randomizer for clinical trials software) assigned first operated side and which side received CTL vs OD. All surgical procedures by a single operator.
- **Sample**: 10 fresh pig mandibles (~1 yr, ~100 kg), edentulous space ≥ 10 mm between lower canine and first lower premolar; full-thickness flap exposed bone.
- **n**: 20 sites / 20 implants (n = 10 per group); cone-morse implants 4.5 × 10 mm (CM SW Plus, S.I.N. Implant System).
- **CTL protocol**: guide + twist 2.0 + tapered burs 3.0/3.3/4.0 mm, clockwise 800 rpm, 20 N·cm.
- **OD protocol**: Densah® burs (pilot + 2.0/2.3/3.0/3.3/4.3), counterclockwise 800 rpm, 20 N·cm, copious irrigation; perforation depth 11 mm (implant seated 1 mm subcrestal). Implants seated at 20 rpm to 35 N·cm or crestal level, finished with manual torque wrench.
- **Outcomes**: primary = buccal bone defect height after placement; secondary = defect width/area (buccal + lingual, ImageJ on standardized photographs, periodontal-probe scale reference), insertion torque, linear buccal ridge increase from 3D STL (T1 baseline → T2 post-preparation → T3 post-placement; ExoCAD overlap, first lower premolar reference; blinded evaluator, 2 measurements 7 days apart). Mann-Whitney test, α = 5% (GraphPad Prism 8).
- **Ethics**: Animal Ethics Committee, Araraquara Dental School FOAr–UNESP, protocol 20/2019.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Insertion torque**: OD 49.9 ± 11.45 vs CTL 40.4 ± 8.07 N·cm, p < 0.05 (paper prints the erroneous unit "N/cm²"; torque in N·cm).
- **Baseline ridge thickness**: no intergroup difference (crest, 1, 2, 4, 6 mm); analog vs digital measurement agreed at baseline.
- **Buccal defects**: CTL 8 vs OD 1 defect > 0.5 mm height.
  - Height: CTL 2.5 ± 2.14 vs OD 0.37 ± 0.72 mm
  - Width: CTL 2.46 ± 1.38 vs OD 0.73 ± 0.82 mm
  - Area: CTL 5.97 ± 6.57 vs OD 0.36 ± 0.47 mm² (buccal p ≤ 0.01)
- **Lingual defects**: width CTL 3.17 ± 1.54 vs OD 1.98 ± 1.10 mm; area CTL 4.65 ± 3.24 vs OD 1.72 ± 1.06 mm² (p ≤ 0.05); height not significantly different.
- **Buccal ridge increase after site preparation (T1→T2)**: crestal CTL 0.18 ± 0.2 vs OD 0.66 ± 0.64 mm; 1 mm CTL 0.09 ± 0.15 vs OD 0.39 ± 0.37 mm.
- **Buccal ridge increase after implant placement (T2→T3)**: crestal CTL 0.03 ± 0.11 vs OD 0.28 ± 0.35 mm; 1 mm CTL 0.07 ± 0.13 vs OD 0.25 ± 0.35 mm (all significant at crest and 1 mm only; 2/4/6 mm NS).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No sample-size calculation; small pilot (n = 10/group) in ex-vivo bone.
- Fresh pig jaws carry anatomical variation in alveolar ridge form (partly controlled by split-mouth).
- Ex-vivo model: no healing/osseointegration phase, so only immediate defect geometry and torque are captured.
- Tapered implant design and operator handling likely contributed to the outcome (tapered, non-cutting-thread implants known to favor torque and expansion) — the OD effect is not isolated from implant geometry.
- Authors call for clinical studies with adequate sample size; CTL group's defect-prone outcome supports caution in limited-thickness ridges with conventional drilling even in this model.

## 7. Glossary
- **Osseodensification (OD)**: counterclockwise, non-cutting (negative-rake) bur drilling that compacts bone laterally instead of removing it.
- **Densah®**: Versah's osseodensification bur system used for the OD osteotomy.
- **Insertion torque (IT)**: rotational resistance (N·cm) met while seating the implant; a proxy for primary stability.
- **STL (STereoLithography)**: 3D mesh file format from intraoral scanning, used here for overlapped ridge-thickness measurements.
- **Split-mouth design**: within-subject pairing where each jaw contributes both treatment and control sites, reducing between-subject variability.
- **Mann-Whitney test**: non-parametric test for two independent groups used for all intergroup comparisons.
