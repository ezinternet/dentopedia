## Why Ingested
This SR fills a gap not addressed by [[implants/spinelli-2023-tissue-level-laser-lok-flapless-4year]] (single-cohort Laser-Lok data) or [[implants/zhang-2021-tissue-level-bone-level-fresh-extraction-sockets-dogs]] (tissue-level vs bone-level neck geometry, no significant MBL difference): it synthesizes 15 studies specifically isolating implant-**neck micro-topography** (rough micro-threaded vs laser micro-textured vs smooth machined) as the intervention against MBL and peri-implant soft-tissue outcomes — a crestal-collar-specific angle distinct from body/thread-surface roughness literature already held (mesa/micro/nano scale, SLA/CA/UV).

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review (PRISMA) of 15 controlled human clinical studies (6 RCT, 5 prospective, 4 retrospective; n participants 15–400 per study; 2013–2022) comparing rough micro-threaded or laser micro-textured (LMS) implant-neck designs against smooth machined-neck implants for marginal bone loss (MBL) and peri-implant soft tissue outcomes; meta-analysis was not feasible due to study heterogeneity.
Rough micro-threaded necks showed markedly lower post-loading MBL (0.12±0.17mm to 3.25±0.4mm) than smooth machined necks (0.38±0.51mm to 3.75±0.4mm); LMS-neck implants showed lower 3-year probing depth (PD) than machined-neck implants (2.3±0.7mm vs 3.8±0.8mm), with similar gingival recession between groups (1.08±0.4mm vs 2.46±0.3mm).
Roughened micro-threaded/laser-microtextured neck designs significantly reduce functional-loading MBL and PD versus machined necks, but all included RCTs had short follow-up (mostly 12–24 months, one at 60 months) and four studies found no significant between-group MBL difference — long-term (>5yr) well-designed RCTs are still needed.

## 세줄요약
PRISMA 체계적 문헌고찰(SR) — 15편의 대조 임상연구(RCT 6편, 전향적 5편, 후향적 4편; 연구당 참여자 15–400명; 2013–2022년), 거친 미세나사산(micro-threaded) 또는 레이저 미세조직화(Laser Micro-textured Surface, LMS) 임플란트 넥 디자인을 매끄러운 기계가공(machined) 넥과 비교해 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 및 임플란트주위 연조직 결과를 평가; 연구 간 이질성으로 메타분석은 시행하지 못함.
거친 미세나사산 넥은 부하 후 MBL이 매끄러운 넥(0.38±0.51mm~3.75±0.4mm)보다 뚜렷이 낮음(0.12±0.17mm~3.25±0.4mm); LMS 넥은 3년 시점 탐침깊이(Probing Depth, PD)가 기계가공 넥보다 낮음(2.3±0.7mm vs 3.8±0.8mm), 치은퇴축은 두 군 간 유사(1.08±0.4mm vs 2.46±0.3mm).
거친 미세나사산·레이저 미세조직화 넥 디자인은 기능부하 하 MBL·PD를 유의하게 감소시키지만, 포함된 모든 RCT의 추적기간이 짧고(대부분 12–24개월, 1편만 60개월) 4편은 군 간 유의차가 없어 5년 이상 장기 RCT가 추가로 필요함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR to specifically isolate implant-**neck** micro-topography (micro-threads vs laser micro-texture vs machined) as the intervention variable for MBL and peri-implant soft tissue, separate from body/thread surface roughness literature.
- Quantifies the MBL range difference between micro-threaded (0.12–3.25mm) and machined-neck (0.38–3.75mm) implants across heterogeneous follow-up durations (12–60 months).
- Identifies LMS-neck implants (Laser-Lok microgrooves, 8–12µm) as producing both lower MBL and lower PD than machined necks, attributed to perpendicular (rather than parallel) collagen fiber attachment at the LMS collar limiting epithelial downgrowth.
- Notes conflicting within-review findings: 4 of 15 studies found no significant MBL difference between micro-threaded/LMS and machined necks, and one study found smooth necks had *lower* peri-implantitis frequency (2.92% vs 14.41%, p<0.001) despite higher MBL literature trend — highlighting outcome-dependent contradictions.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: PRISMA-guided systematic review, PICOS framework, no meta-analysis (heterogeneity precluded pooling)
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, EMBASE via EBSCO (Jan 2013–Aug 2022), English-language only; 205 electronic + 42 manual hits → 189 after dedup → 97 full-text reviewed → 15 included
- **n**: 15 studies (6 RCT, 5 prospective cohort, 4 retrospective cohort); participants 15–400 per study, ages 22–80; 3 studies on LMS-neck implants, 12 on rough micro-threaded-neck implants
- **Outcomes**: Primary = marginal/crestal bone loss (MBL, radiographic — periapical in 10 studies, CT in 2, panoramic in 3). Secondary = bleeding on probing (BoP), plaque index, width of attached gingiva, gingival recession, clinical attachment loss (CAL), probing depth (PD)
- **Quality appraisal**: Cochrane RoB tool for RCTs (2 reviewers); Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS) for cohort studies (score ≥6/9 = high quality); disagreements resolved by third reviewer

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- 10-year MBL: LMS-neck implants 1.23±0.21mm vs machined-neck 2.8±0.9mm.
- 2-year MBL: non-Laser-Lok 1.07±0.30mm vs Laser-Lok microtextured 0.49±0.34mm.
- Post-loading MBL range: micro-threaded neck 0.12±0.17mm to 3.25±0.4mm; machined (no micro-thread) 0.38±0.51mm to 3.75±0.4mm.
- 18-month MBL by neck type: scalloped 2.01±0.77mm, smooth 1.19±0.82mm, rough 0.90±0.57mm.
- 12-month MBL: rough micro-threaded + internal connection + platform switching 0.12±0.17mm vs machined/external connection/platform switching 0.38±0.51mm.
- Overall MBL (one study): smooth 1.26±0.90mm, rough 1.20±1.1mm, scalloped 2.28±0.97mm (p<0.05).
- 3-year early-loading PD: LMS group 2.3±0.7mm vs machined-neck 3.8±0.8mm; gingival recession 1.08±0.4mm vs 2.46±0.3mm.
- 5–9 year PD: rough micro-threaded neck (platform switching) 4.24±0.49mm vs machined-neck (butt-joint) 4.29±1.13mm; CAL 3.95±1.04mm vs 3.42±1.67mm.
- Smooth-neck implants had significantly lower peri-implantitis frequency than non-smooth-neck implants (2.92% vs 14.41%, p<0.001) — a soft-tissue-infection-outcome finding that runs counter to the MBL-driven advantage attributed to rough/micro-threaded necks elsewhere in the same review.
- Quality: 4/6 RCTs (67%) had high/unclear RoB for allocation concealment; 3/6 (43%) had performance bias; all cohort studies rated good quality on NOS; RoB for reporting bias was low across all RCTs.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Meta-analysis not feasible — substantial heterogeneity in loading-to-placement interval, implant site (maxilla vs mandible), follow-up duration, and outcome variables across the 15 studies.
- All RCTs had short follow-up (12–24 months for 5 of 6; only one reached 60 months) — long-term (>5yr) neck-design MBL trajectory remains under-documented.
- Four included studies found no statistically significant MBL difference between intervention and control necks, tempering the review's headline conclusion.
- Soft-tissue clinical indices (BoP, plaque index, PD) used across included studies were not purpose-built to assess peri-implant soft tissue specifically, limiting outcome specificity.
- CBCT-based MBL measurement (2 studies) is noted to underestimate facial bone dimension due to metal artifact, a measurement-method caveat affecting cross-study comparability.

## 6. Related Work
- spinelli-2023 (tissue-level-laser-lok-flapless-4year): single prospective cohort on Laser-Lok tissue-level implants — provides individual-study granularity behind this SR's pooled LMS observations.
- zhang-2021 (tissue-level-bone-level-fresh-extraction-sockets-dogs): dog histomorphometric study comparing tissue-level (smooth machined collar) vs bone-level (micro-threaded, platform-switched) neck designs — found no significant vertical bone resorption difference, an animal-model counterpoint to this SR's human-data MBL advantage for micro-threaded necks.

## 7. Glossary
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: radiographically measured crestal/marginal bone height change around an implant over time, a primary implant-success indicator.
- **LMS (Laser Micro-textured Surface)**: implant-neck surface treated by computer-controlled laser ablation to create 8–12µm microgrooves (e.g., Laser-Lok), intended to promote perpendicular connective-tissue fiber attachment.
- **Micro-thread**: small-pitch threads (roughness ~1–10µm) machined into the implant neck/collar to improve mechanical interlocking with crestal bone.
- **PD (Probing Depth)**: sulcular/peri-implant probing depth, a soft-tissue health indicator.
- **CAL (Clinical Attachment Loss)**: measured loss of periodontal/peri-implant attachment level, combining probing depth and gingival margin position.
- **Platform switching (PS)**: use of a narrower-diameter abutment relative to the implant platform, associated with reduced peri-implant crestal bone remodeling.
