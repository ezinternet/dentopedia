## Why Ingested

위키에 개별 전신질환(당뇨, 비타민D, 고혈압, 부정교합 등)별 임플란트 골유착 영향 논문은 다수 있지만, 여러 전신질환·약물을 하나의 umbrella review로 묶어 "임플란트 식립 전 전신 병력을 게이트로 삼아야 하는가"를 종합적으로 묻는 상위 근거가 없었다. 이 논문은 그 공백을 메운다. 특히 이 umbrella review에 포함된 8편의 SR 중 하나가 이미 위키에 있는 [[implants/aghaloo-2019-systemic-diseases-medications-implant-osseointegration-sr]](ITI 합의 SR)이어서, 그 SR의 개별 결론이 다른 7개 SR(항흡수제, 당뇨, SSRI, PPI, 심혈관질환 등 다룬 리뷰)과 함께 얼마나 재현되는지 교차검증하는 상위 문서 역할을 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (8 systematic reviews, English, March 2017–July 2022) of systemic diseases/medications and dental implant osseointegration, success/survival rate, peri-implantitis, and implant loss; AMSTAR 2 quality appraisal; PROSPERO CRD42023397955.

Narrative synthesis only (no pooled meta-analysis): antiresorptive drugs (bisphosphonates) show no significant effect on osseointegration/survival (e.g., de Medeiros SR-MA: 4.70% vs 3.57% failure, osteoporotic vs healthy, n.s.); SSRIs and PPIs show a consistent negative signal (implant failure-rate differences of ~7.5% and ~4.5% respectively across multiple included reviews); diabetes results are conflicting and mostly short-term; neurologic disease, HIV, hypothyroidism, cardiovascular disease, and antihypertensives/beta-blockers/diuretics show no reduced osseointegration.

Clinical implication: no single systemic condition among those reviewed is an absolute osseointegration contraindication except prolonged glucocorticoid use; SSRI/PPI users and poorly-controlled/long-term diabetics warrant closer monitoring; overall evidence quality is low-to-moderate (most of the 8 included SRs rated low/moderate on AMSTAR 2, one critically low) so conclusions should be treated as provisional.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(umbrella review, SR 8편, 2017년 3월~2022년 7월 영어 논문)로, 전신질환·약물이 임플란트 골유착, 성공/생존율, 임플란트주위염, 임플란트 상실에 미치는 영향을 종합; AMSTAR 2로 품질 평가, PROSPERO CRD42023397955 등록.

정량적 메타분석 없이 서술적 종합(narrative synthesis)만 수행: 항흡수제(비스포스포네이트)는 골유착·생존율에 유의한 영향 없음(예: de Medeiros SR-MA에서 골다공증군 4.70% vs 건강군 3.57% 실패율, 비유의); SSRI·PPI는 여러 포함 리뷰에서 일관되게 부정적 신호(실패율 차이 각각 약 7.5%, 약 4.5%); 당뇨는 결과가 상충하며 대부분 단기 연구; 신경계질환·HIV·갑상선기능저하증·심혈관질환·항고혈압제/베타차단제/이뇨제는 골유착 저하를 보이지 않음.

임상적 의미: 검토된 전신질환 중 절대적 골유착 금기는 장기 고용량 글루코코르티코이드 사용뿐이며, SSRI/PPI 복용자 및 조절 불량·장기 당뇨 환자는 더 주의 깊은 모니터링이 필요; 다만 근거 질이 전반적으로 낮음~중등도(포함 SR 8편 중 대다수가 AMSTAR 2 low/moderate, 1편은 critically low)이므로 잠정적 결론으로 다뤄야 함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First (at time of publication) umbrella-level synthesis pooling multiple systematic reviews across a broad range of systemic diseases and medications (not limited to a single condition) against implant osseointegration/success/survival/peri-implantitis/loss outcomes.
- Applies AMSTAR 2 to grade the methodological quality of all 8 included SRs, exposing that most evidence in this space is low-to-moderate quality.
- Surfaces two medication classes — SSRIs and PPIs — as showing a consistent (if still preliminary) negative signal for osseointegration/implant failure across multiple independently-conducted SRs, a signal less prominent in single-disease-focused SRs.
- Explicitly contextualizes conflicting diabetes findings by noting most underlying studies are short-term / well-controlled-disease cohorts, while long-term and poorly-controlled diabetes data (per the 2017 World Workshop) trend worse.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PRISMA-compliant umbrella review; PICO reframed as PEO (Population–Exposure–Outcome).
- Databases: PROSPERO, MEDLINE/PubMed, BioMed Central, Scopus, Cochrane Library; searched to 1 February 2023; English-language only; last-10-years filter to capture newer medications.
- Screening: two independent reviewers for title/abstract and full-text; third reviewer for disagreements; reference-list hand-search of included reviews.
- Data extraction: two reviewers, third for disputes; outcomes = osseointegration, implant success rate, implant survival, implant loss, peri-implantitis.
- Synthesis: tabulated study characteristics + narrative synthesis in Microsoft Excel 2019 — explicitly **no quantitative pooling/meta-analysis** across the 8 included SRs.
- Quality/risk-of-bias: AMSTAR 2 tool applied to each included systematic review.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Search yielded 5488 records (137 BioMed Central, 2538 MEDLINE/PubMed, 2713 Scopus, 21 Cochrane, 79 PROSPERO) → 1250 duplicates + 40 non-English excluded → 4198 titles → 18 abstracts selected → 10 excluded at full-text (3 not relevant, 7 failed inclusion criteria) → **8 systematic reviews included** (published March 2017–July 2022).
- Antiresorptive drugs (bisphosphonates): no significant effect on osseointegration/survival across most included reviews; de Medeiros SR-MA: 4.70% failure (702 implants, osteoporotic) vs 3.57% (4114 implants, healthy) — n.s.; Stavropoulos meta-analysis: no significant implant-loss difference, success rate 85.7–100% (test) vs 84.6–100% (control); conflicting individual-study ORs for implant loss (Yip 2012 OR 2.7 favoring worse outcomes in bisphosphonate users; Al-Sabbagh & Robinson 2015 OR 9.2 favoring worse outcomes in controls).
- Diabetes: Aghaloo/Javed/Naujokat — no short-term osseointegration difference vs healthy; Ferreira OR 1.9 for peri-implantitis; Tawil: HbA1c ≤7% → 0 peri-implant disease vs HbA1c 7–9% → 6/141 implants affected; Costa et al. — no difference.
- SSRIs: Chappuis +7.5% failure rate; Aghaloo 89.4–94.4% (SSRI) vs 95.4–98.15% (non-SSRI) survival; Cheng confirms 7.5% difference; Wu retrospective cohort (916 implants/490 patients) — increased failure risk.
- PPIs: Chappuis +4.5% failure rate; Aghaloo 12–6.8% (PPI) vs 4.5–3.2% (non-PPI) failure; Cheng confirms 4.5% difference.
- Cardiovascular disease/antihypertensives: Aghaloo — no adverse effect from cardiac disease/hypertension; Wu — 0.6% failure (antihypertensive users) vs 4.1% (non-users), i.e., protective signal.
- HIV: Ata-Ali SR — implant failure comparable HIV+ vs healthy.
- AMSTAR 2: most of the 8 included SRs rated low/moderate quality; 1 critically low.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Heterogeneity across the 8 included reviews (different diseases/drugs, designs, outcome definitions) precludes any pooled statistical analysis — this is an explicitly narrative, not quantitative, umbrella review.
- Overall AMSTAR 2 quality of included SRs is low-to-moderate (one critically low), limiting confidence in every downstream conclusion.
- Antiresorptive drugs were covered by 4 of the 8 SRs; other topics (SSRI, PPI, HIV, neurologic, cardiovascular) each rest on far fewer/thinner underlying reviews.
- Smoking/alcohol confounding in the underlying primary studies is flagged but not adjustable at the umbrella level.
- Chemotherapeutics, immunosuppressants (e.g., cyclosporine A), and statins are noted as plausible additional modifiers not covered by any included SR.
- Authors call for further long-term RCTs and molecular/genetic mechanistic research on drug–bone-formation interactions.

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: a systematic review of systematic reviews — synthesizes conclusions across multiple existing SRs/meta-analyses rather than primary studies directly.
- **AMSTAR 2**: "A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews," version 2 — critical appraisal tool for methodological quality of SRs of randomized and/or non-randomized studies.
- **PEO**: Population–Exposure–Outcome, a modified PICO framework used for non-interventional/etiologic review questions.
- **MRONJ/ONJ**: Medication-Related Osteonecrosis of the Jaw — necrosis of jaw bone associated with antiresorptive (e.g., bisphosphonate) or antiangiogenic drug therapy.
- **MIBL**: Marginal (peri-implant) bone loss.
- **SERMs**: Selective Estrogen Receptor Modulators — a drug class used in osteoporosis management with anti-catabolic bone effects.
