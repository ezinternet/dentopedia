## Why Ingested

The wiki's splint evidence was general-TMD ([[tmj/zhang-2020-occlusal-splint-tmd-rct-sr]], [[tmj/singh-2024-occlusal-interventions-tmd-cochrane-sr]]) or single-trial ([[tmj/cimino-2025-counselling-vs-splint-tmd-neck-pain-rct]]); this SR restricts to the myogenous subtype, the subtype most relevant to masticatory muscle pain decisions alongside [[tmj/chan-2022-myogenous-tmd-diagnosis-treatment-review]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA; MEDLINE via PubMed and Web of Science, final search May 2022) of 10 RCTs (539 participants, mean follow-up 6 months) on stabilization splints in myogenous TMD.

Most trials reported symptom improvement versus baseline across pain (10 studies), mouth opening (5), headache (2) and quality of life (4); no pooled estimate is reported in the abstract, and overall evidence quality was low (moderate to high risk of bias, small samples).

Splints appeared effective for signs and symptoms, but low-certainty, baseline-referenced evidence cannot separate splint-specific effect from natural course or placebo; larger prospective studies are needed.

## 세줄요약

근육성 측두하악장애 (Myogenous Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD)에서 교합안정장치 (Stabilization Splint) 효과를 본 체계적 문헌고찰 (PRISMA; MEDLINE·Web of Science, 최종 검색 2022년 5월), RCT 10편·539명·평균 추적 6개월.

대부분의 시험이 통증(10편)·개구량(5편)·두통(2편)·삶의 질(4편)에서 기저치 대비 호전을 보고했으나, 초록에는 통합 효과추정치가 없고 근거 질은 낮음 (중등도-높은 비뚤림 위험, 소표본).

장치가 효과적으로 보였으나 근거 확실성이 낮고 기저치 대비 호전이라 장치 고유 효과와 자연경과·위약효과를 구분할 수 없음; 대규모 전향 연구 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Subtype-specific (myogenous TMD) SR of stabilization splints restricted to RCTs.
- Reports four outcome domains: pain, mouth opening, headache, quality of life.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PRISMA-guided; MEDLINE via PubMed and Web of Science; final search May 2022; 10 RCTs, n=539, mean follow-up 6 months. Risk-of-bias tool, comparators and synthesis method are not stated in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Pain: 10 studies; mouth opening: 5; headache: 2; QoL: 4.
- Most trials improved versus baseline; overall quality of evidence low.
- No meta-analysis or pooled effect size in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract-only. Search ended May 2022 although published 2026, so it misses 2022-2026 trials. Moderate to high risk of bias and small samples; authors call for large prospective studies of long-term effectiveness.
