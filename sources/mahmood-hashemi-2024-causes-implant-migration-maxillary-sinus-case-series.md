## Three-line Summary

Retrospective case series (J Dent Shiraz 2024, Tehran University of Medical Sciences), 39 patients over 25 years (1996–2021) whose dental implants migrated into the maxillary sinus, all retrieved via the Caldwell-Luc approach under local anesthesia by a single surgeon.

Displacement occurred during placement in 22/39 (inadequate bone height 11, over-drilling 11, closed sinus lift 6, immediate placement 3) and postoperatively in the rest (healing-abutment stage 5, prosthetic stage 3 — both from failed osseointegration); no complications over 2–10-year follow-up.

Clinical implication: thin residual bone <5 mm should be sinus-lifted before placement to prevent migration, and Caldwell-Luc is a reliable low-complication retrieval technique — but displacement makes 31/39 patients reluctant to re-treat.

## 세줄요약

후향 케이스 시리즈 (J Dent Shiraz 2024, 테헤란 의대), 25년간(1996–2021) 상악동으로 변위된 임플란트 39명을 단일 술자가 전부 국소마취 하 Caldwell-Luc로 제거.

변위는 식립 중 22/39(잔존골 부족 11, 과드릴링 11, 폐쇄형 sinus lift 6, 즉시식립 3), 나머지는 술후(힐링어버트먼트 단계 5, 보철 단계 3 — 모두 골유착 실패); 2–10년 추적에서 합병증 없음.

임상적 의미: 잔존골<5mm는 식립 전 sinus lift로 변위 예방, Caldwell-Luc는 합병증 적은 신뢰할 만한 제거법 — 다만 변위 후 31/39 환자가 재치료를 꺼림.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Large single-center **stage-by-stage cause distribution** of implant migration into the sinus (intraoperative vs postoperative sub-causes).
2. **All-Caldwell-Luc** real-world evidence (n=39) with 2–10-year uneventful follow-up, arguing Caldwell-Luc as a reliable low-complication retrieval technique.
3. Documents the **patient-reluctance** downstream cost (31/39 declined re-treatment).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Retrospective case series; 39 patients (21F/18M) referred over 25 years for implant displaced into the maxillary antrum. Panoramic + CBCT localization. Preop Co-amoxiclav 625 mg TID (48 h) + 0.2% CHX rinse. Caldwell-Luc under local anesthesia: carbide bur #5 osteotomy with saline irrigation → lateral-wall bony window → membrane incision → retrieval by suction/rongeur → 4-0 Vicryl closure. Postop antibiotics (1 wk), decongestant, analgesic; nose-blowing avoidance 2 wks; CHX BID ×7 d. Descriptive analysis only.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Causes of migration (n=39):**

| Stage | Cause | M | F | n |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intraoperative | Inadequate bone height | 6 | 5 | 11 |
| Intraoperative | Over-drilling / wide socket | 4 | 7 | 11 |
| Intraoperative | Closed (crestal) sinus lift | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| Intraoperative | Immediate placement (fresh socket) | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Postoperative | Healing-abutment placement (no osseointegration) | 3 | 2 | 5 |
| Postoperative | Prosthetic procedure (inadequate osseointegration) | 1 | 2 | 3 |

- Total displacement **during placement = 22/39**.
- **31/39 patients (13M, 18F) reluctant** toward further implant placement afterward.
- **No complications** over 2–10-year follow-up.
- Comparators cited: Manor (55 cases, Caldwell-Luc in 52), Sgaramella (21 cases/24 implants; bone <5 mm at 19/24, sinus lift omitted in 15), Hamdoon (11; Caldwell-Luc least challenging), Mumtaz, Maciel.
- **Prevention threshold**: residual bone height <5 mm → perform sinus lift before placement.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, single surgeon, single technique (no endoscopic comparison arm); descriptive only, no statistics.
- No systematic radiographic outcome grading (contrast with Lee 2024).
- Selection/referral bias inherent to a private-practice referral series.

## 7. Glossary

- **Caldwell-Luc antrostomy** — intraoral approach to the maxillary sinus through a bony window in the anterolateral wall.
- **Closed sinus lift (crestal/osteotome)** — transcrestal sinus floor elevation; a migration risk when membrane/bone gives way.
- **Rongeur** — a strong forceps for gripping/removing bone or foreign bodies.
- **Oroantral fistula** — pathological oral–sinus communication.
