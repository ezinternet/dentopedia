## Why Ingested
Requested as part of a literature sweep around a 2% minocycline HCl periodontal ointment product (Minoclin, Dongkook). The wiki's held minocycline evidence ([[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/soysa-2025-the-long-term-efficacy-of]], [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/arnett-2023-effect-of-scaling-and-root]]) is all non-surgical (SRP adjunct); this paper is a Korean RCT of minocycline ointment as an adjunct to flap surgery, a different clinical setting. → [[periodontics/jung-2012-the-clinical-effect-of-locally]]

## Three-line Summary
Split-mouth RCT (Yonsei University, Korea; n=20 patients with chronic severe periodontitis) comparing flap surgery plus locally delivered minocycline ointment (FM, re-applied at 3 months) against flap surgery alone (FO), with PD, BOP, CAL, recession and plaque index assessed at baseline, 3 and 6 months.
At 6 months FM sites showed greater PD reduction (3.34 ± 0.03 vs 2.62 ± 0.06 mm), BOP reduction (78.01 ± 11.42% vs 50.33 ± 15.01%) and CAL gain (1.88 ± 0.21 vs 1.55 ± 0.13 mm), all p < 0.05.
Authors conclude adjunctive minocycline may benefit surgical protocols; only the abstract was available, so randomization/blinding details, the unusually small PD dispersion, and the 6-month-only follow-up could not be checked.

## 세줄요약
분할구강 무작위대조시험(연세대, 한국; 만성 중증 치주염 환자 20명) — 판막수술 + 미노사이클린 연고 국소도포(FM, 3개월째 재도포) vs 판막수술 단독(FO); 기저·3·6개월에 치주낭 깊이 (Probing Depth, PD), 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP), 임상 부착수준 (Clinical Attachment Level, CAL), 치은퇴축, 치태지수 평가.
6개월에 FM 부위가 PD 감소(3.34 ± 0.03 vs 2.62 ± 0.06 mm), BOP 감소(78.01 ± 11.42% vs 50.33 ± 15.01%), CAL 획득(1.88 ± 0.21 vs 1.55 ± 0.13 mm) 모두 더 컸다 (p < 0.05).
저자는 미노사이클린이 수술 프로토콜에 이로울 수 있다고 결론 — 초록만 확인 가능해 무작위화·맹검 세부, PD 산포가 매우 작은 점, 6개월 추적 한계는 검증하지 못했다.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Journal of Clinical Periodontology 2012;39(8):753-9
- **DOI**: 10.1111/j.1600-051X.2012.01908.x
- **Institution**: Department of Periodontology, Research Institute for Periodontal Regeneration, College of Dentistry, Yonsei University, Seoul, Korea

## 2. Key Contributions
- Tests minocycline ointment as an adjunct to flap surgery (not SRP alone) in chronic severe periodontitis.
- Within-patient (split-mouth) comparison, FM vs FO, with a second minocycline application at 3 months.
- Reports a 6-month advantage for the minocycline side in PD, BOP and CAL.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Split-mouth comparative trial (PubMed publication type: Randomized Controlled Trial)
- **n**: 20 patients, chronic severe periodontitis
- **Intervention**: flap surgery + locally delivered minocycline ointment (FM) vs flap surgery only (FO); minocycline re-applied 3 months post-operatively
- **Outcomes**: plaque index, PD, BOP, gingival recession, CAL at baseline, 3 and 6 months

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome (6 months) | FM (flap + minocycline) | FO (flap only) |
|---|---|---|
| PD reduction | 3.34 ± 0.03 mm | 2.62 ± 0.06 mm |
| BOP reduction | 78.01 ± 11.42% | 50.33 ± 15.01% |
| CAL gain | 1.88 ± 0.21 mm | 1.55 ± 0.13 mm |

Both sites improved; FM > FO at 6 months (p < 0.05). Values as reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: randomization method, allocation concealment, blinding and the statistical test used are not stated in the abstract.
- n = 20, single center, 6-month follow-up.
- Split-mouth designs can inflate adjunct effects (see [[periodontics/non-surgical-instrumentation/soysa-2025-the-long-term-efficacy-of]], meta-regression on design); possible carry-over between sides is not addressed in the abstract.
- The minocycline arm also received a second application at 3 months, so benefit is not separable from re-application.

## 6. Related Work
- Soysa 2025: SR+MA of tetracycline-class local adjuncts to SRP — non-surgical setting, modest effects.
- Arnett 2023: minocycline microspheres with SRP — benefit confined to deep sites, small.

## 7. Glossary
- **FM**: flap surgery + minocycline ointment site.
- **FO**: flap surgery only site.
- **Split-mouth**: each patient serves as own control, test and control treatments on different sides.
