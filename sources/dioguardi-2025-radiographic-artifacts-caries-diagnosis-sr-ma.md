## Why Ingested

사용자 요청으로 PA(periapical/intraoral) artifact 근거 보강 — 특히 "실제 병변으로 오인되는 해석적(interpretive) artifact" 축. 기존 위키에 파노라마 ghost artifact 페이지는 있으나, 구내 bitewing/PA에서 **우식으로 오인되는 광학 artifact**(Mach band, cervical burnout, TSR)를 정량화한 근거가 없었다. 본 SR+MA는 이 optical effect의 유병률(상악 대구치 TSR ~26%)과 위양성률(~13%)을 메타분석해, [[radiology/elkhateeb-2022-psp-image-plate-artifacts-prevalence]](기술적 acquisition artifact)와 짝을 이루는 **해석 단계 artifact** 근거가 된다. 우식 검출 정확도([[caries/foros-2021-early-caries-detection-methods-sr-ma]])를 위양성원(false-positive source) 관점에서 한정(refine)함.

## One-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (Oral Radiol 2025, 5 studies, PROSPERO-registered) of radiographic optical artifacts on intraoral bitewing/periapical images: non-carious triangular-shaped radiolucencies (TSR) on maxillary molars had ~26% raw prevalence (pooled 0.308, I²≈98%) — markedly higher in primary (~51%) than permanent (~27%) molars — and optical effects (TSR, Mach band, cervical burnout) were misread as caries/fracture in ~13% of observations (pooled 0.133); GRADE certainty low to very low.

## 한줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(Oral Radiol 2025, 5편, PROSPERO 등록): 구내 bitewing/치근단 영상의 광학 artifact — 상악 대구치 비우식성 삼각형 방사선투과상(Triangular-Shaped Radiolucency, TSR) 유병률 ~26%(pooled 0.308, I²≈98%), 유치(~51%)가 영구치(~27%)보다 훨씬 높음. 광학효과(TSR·Mach band·치경부 소각 cervical burnout)가 우식/파절로 오인된 위양성 ~13%(pooled 0.133); GRADE 근거등급 낮음~매우 낮음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to **quantify interpretive/optical PA-bitewing artifacts** (Mach band, cervical burnout, TSR) and their diagnostic cost.
- **Prevalence** of non-carious TSR on maxillary molars ≈ **26%** (pooled 0.308), with a strong **pediatric skew** (primary ~51% vs permanent ~27%).
- **False-positive rate** of ~**13%** (caries or fracture) attributable to these optical effects.
- Catalogs **mitigation**: masking test, digital contrast/grey-level analysis, beam angle, AI.
- Grades evidence honestly: **low to very low** certainty (I²>90%, few studies).

## 3. Methodology

- PRISMA + Cochrane Handbook; PROSPERO CRD420251083823. Cochrane/PubMed/Scopus + grey literature; search "triangular-shaped radiolucencies" OR "Mach band".
- 640 records → 5 included (2 TSR-prevalence, 3 false-positive). Two reviewers, third adjudicator.
- Risk of bias: **AXIS** (prevalence), **QUADAS-2** (diagnostic). **GRADE** certainty. Meta-analysis OpenMeta[Analyst], DerSimonian–Laird random effects; Egger's test + funnel plot for publication bias.

## 4. Key Results

**The three optical effects**
- **Cervical burnout**: tangential beam across curved proximal surface near CEJ → localized radiolucency mimicking caries (worse in multirooted teeth).
- **Mach band**: perceptual contrast illusion at density boundaries (EDJ) → apparent radiolucency; positive (light) / negative (dark) bands; masking test distinguishes.
- **TSR**: non-carious triangular radiolucency on mesial maxillary molars (Carabelli-cusp superimposition), mistaken for caries.

**Meta-analysis**
- **TSR prevalence** (maxillary molars): raw 270/1021 = **26.44%**; pooled **0.308** (95% CI 0.129–0.487), I²≈98%.
  - Permanent 0.199 (158/802); Primary **0.477** (112/223). Second primary molar >60%; permanent second molar 5.9%. Pooled primary 51.1% vs permanent 27.4%.
- **False positives**: 60/464 = **12.93%**; pooled 0.133 (95% CI 0.041–0.225), I²=89%. Range 4.6% (Tabari, TSR) to 26% (Nielsen, Mach band as fracture).
- **Publication bias**: Egger p=0.57 (no clear asymmetry, low power). **GRADE**: primary LOW, secondary VERY LOW.

**Mitigation**: masking test (opaque card over enamel — radiolucency vanishes → Mach band); grey-level/contrast software; beam angle/centering; AI/post-processing; always correlate with clinical exam.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 5 observational studies; **very high heterogeneity** (I²>90%); GRADE low–very low.
- Several studies used extracted teeth / lab / student observers, radiographic (not histological) reference — limited generalizability.
- No standardized TSR/Mach-band diagnostic criteria; small samples.
- Needs prospective multicenter studies, observer training, and AI-assisted differentiation.

## 7. Glossary

- **Mach band effect**: visual-system contrast illusion exaggerating brightness/darkness at a density boundary; can mimic (or mask) a lesion.
- **Cervical burnout**: apparent radiolucency near the CEJ from tangential beam passage through thinner proximal structure; mimics root/cervical caries.
- **TSR (triangular-shaped radiolucency)**: non-carious triangular lucency on mesial maxillary molars from Carabelli-cusp superimposition; mistaken for proximal caries.
- **Masking test**: covering the radiopaque enamel margin — if the radiolucency disappears it is a Mach-band artifact, if it persists it may be true caries.
- **False positive (FP)**: an artifact interpreted as disease (caries/fracture), risking overtreatment.
- **AXIS / QUADAS-2 / GRADE**: risk-of-bias tools for cross-sectional (AXIS) and diagnostic-accuracy (QUADAS-2) studies, and an overall certainty-of-evidence framework (GRADE).
