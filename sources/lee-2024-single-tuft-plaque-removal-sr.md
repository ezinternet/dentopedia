## Three-line Summary

This scoping review examined the efficacy of single-tuft toothbrushes (STBs) in removing dental plaque from challenging oral surfaces. The review identified 403 articles across 7 databases, with 9 studies (8 RCTs, 1 in-vitro) meeting inclusion criteria, evaluating STB effectiveness on interproximal molars, lingual surfaces, and orthodontic appliance areas.

STBs demonstrated superior plaque removal in hard-to-reach areas compared to conventional toothbrushes alone, particularly on buccal/lingual interproximal surfaces of molars and around orthodontic brackets. When combined with conventional brushes, STBs showed significant improvement (p<0.05), though they did not consistently outperform floss or interdental brushes across all metrics. User satisfaction was high among orthodontic patients, but awareness remained low (5.6% usage rate).

STBs are effective adjuncts for managing difficult-to-access oral regions but cannot fully replace conventional toothbrushes or other interdental devices. Clinical application should focus on specific patient populations (orthodontic, implant, partial edentulism) with targeted education programs to address the awareness-accessibility gap.

## 세줄요약

이 주제범위 문헌고찰은 첨단칫솔 (Single-Tuft Toothbrush, STB)의 접근이 어려운 구강 부위 치면세균막 제거 효과를 평가했다. 7개 데이터베이스에서 403개 문헌을 검색하여 9개 연구(무작위대조시험 8개, 체외실험 1개)를 선정했으며, 대구치 인접면, 설측면, 교정장치 주변 부위에서 STB의 효과를 분석했다.

첨단칫솔은 대구치 협·설측 인접면과 교정 브라켓 주변에서 일반칫솔 단독 사용보다 우수한 치면세균막 제거 효과를 보였으며, 일반칫솔과 병행使用时 통계적으로 유의한 개선이 있었다(p<0.05). 그러나 치실이나 치간칫솔에 비해 모든 지표에서 일관되게 우월하지는 않았다. 교정환자에서 사용 만족도는 높았으나, 인지도 부족으로 사용률은 5.6%에 불과했다.

첨단칫솔은 접근이 어려운 부위 관리에 효과적인 보조 도구이나, 일반칫솔이나 타 보조용품을 완전히 대체할 수는 없다. 교정·임플란트·부분 무치악 환자 등 특정 집단에 초점을 맞추고, 인지도 및 접근성 개선을 위한 교육 프로그램이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First scoping review specifically synthesizing single-tuft toothbrush (STB) plaque removal efficacy from 9 studies (8 RCTs, 1 in-vitro) spanning 1976–2024
- STBs showed superior plaque removal on molar interproximal surfaces (buccal and lingual) and around orthodontic brackets compared to conventional brushes alone (p<0.05)
- STB + conventional brush combination significantly outperformed conventional brush monotherapy in orthodontic patients across anterior and posterior regions (Cunha et al., 2018)
- No statistically significant difference was found between STB, floss, and interdental brushes in O'Leary and PHP indices (Im, 2019)
- User satisfaction with STB was highest among interdental devices, but usage rate was only 5.6% due to low awareness and limited accessibility
- Chlorhexidine (0.2%) alone did not prevent plaque accumulation over 72 hours, contradicting earlier reports (Davies et al., 1970), highlighting biofilm resilience
- Gingival bleeding index (GBI) improvements were inconsistent across studies due to the 72-hour intervention window being insufficient to reflect long-term inflammatory changes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Scoping review following Arksey & O'Malley (2005) framework and Peters et al. (2015) guidelines
- Databases & search window: PubMed, Scopus, Google Scholar, KCI, DBpia, KMBASE, National Assembly Library, RISS; searched October 13 – November 13, 2024
- Search strategy: keywords including "single-tuft toothbrush," "end-tuft toothbrush," "unituft toothbrush," "sulcus brush," "tapered-end toothbrush"; Korean term "첨단칫솔"; advanced search with phrase matching and broad screening to minimize miss
- Inclusion criteria: primary research; plaque removal experiments included; no age or comparator restrictions; all publication types (journals, theses, conference proceedings)
- Exclusion criteria: no full text; manufacturer comparison studies; non-English/Korean; not primary research
- Study selection: Two reviewers independently screened titles/abstracts; 403 initial records → 16 after exclusion → 13 full-text reviewed → 9 final (duplicates removed, 4 excluded for not being primary research/not addressing STB plaque efficacy)
- Included studies: 8 RCTs (4 crossover with ≥3-day washout), 1 in-vitro experiment on zirconia crowns with artificial plaque
- Intervention duration: 4 studies set plaque accumulation period at 72 hours to capture early biofilm before host immune response activation (based on Waerhaug 1952 and Maliska 2006 PLA/Plague Free Area findings)
- Outcomes assessed: Plaque indices (O'Leary, PHP, Quigley & Hein), gingival bleeding index (GBI, Loe & Silness), probing pocket depth (PPD), user satisfaction/preference
- Visualization: Erythrosine disclosing agent used to distinguish visible plaque index (VPI) from disclosed plaque index (DPI)
- Data charting: descriptive-analytical method with standardized extraction framework in Excel; Zotero for reference management

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome — STB vs conventional brush on molars** — STB significantly reduced plaque on maxillary buccal and mandibular lingual interproximal surfaces (Lee & Moon, 2011: p<0.05)
- **STB + conventional brush combination —** In orthodontic patients, combined use significantly reduced plaque on both anterior and posterior teeth vs conventional brush alone (Cunha et al., 2018: p<0.05)
- **STB vs floss/interdental brush —** No statistically significant difference in O'Leary or PHP indices (Im, 2019: p>0.05); STB more effective at line angles, interdental brush better at interproximal contacts (Kim et al., 2021)
- **Chlorhexidine 0.2% —** Significant plaque increase at 72 hours despite daily rinsing (Hasegawa et al., 2017), contradicting earlier finding of complete inhibition (Davies et al., 1970)
- **GBI improvement —** Significant improvement in 2 studies (Aeran et al., 2019; Cunha et al., 2018), but not in Hasegawa et al. (2017); attributed to 72-hour window insufficient to detect inflammatory changes
- **User satisfaction —** Highest satisfaction among interdental devices (Im, 2019); willingness-to-recommend score highest for STB; usage rate only 5.6% (4/75) vs 56% for interdental brush and 48% for mouthwash
- **In-vitro comparison on zirconia —** STB, floss, and interdental brushes showed different removal patterns; ranking by area removed was possible but clinically meaningless due to complementary strengths (Kim et al., 2021)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Limited number of included studies (n=9) precludes generalizability of STB efficacy across all clinical scenarios
- Heterogeneity in study designs (RCT vs in-vitro, crossover vs parallel) and outcome measures (multiple plaque indices) limits direct comparison
- Most clinical studies had short intervention windows (72 hours), insufficient to assess long-term effects on gingival inflammation, pocket depth, or attachment loss
- STB safety concerns (gingival trauma, dentin hypersensitivity, tooth wear) from stiff bristles were not systematically evaluated in the included studies
- Manufacturer-specific brushing techniques recommended by vendors lack independent verification in the literature
- Patient populations were not stratified by baseline oral hygiene status, which may influence relative effectiveness
- Lack of standardized protocol for STB use (indications, technique, frequency) hinders clinical translation

## 6. Related Work

- Davies RM et al. 1970 — Chlorhexidine topical application completely inhibited bacterial colonization and plaque formation without mechanical cleaning
- Loe H 1979 — Long-term chlorhexidine use causes staining and taste alteration, raising safety concerns
- Waerhaug J 1952 — Described the Plaque Free Area (PLA/Plague Free Zone) between gingival margin and plaque edge; foundational for 72-hour biofilm studies
- Maliska AN et al. 2006 — PLA appears at 24 hours and persists up to 72 hours; disappears at 96 hours with host immune-inflammatory response
- So MH et al. 2010 — Oral hygiene product usage survey showing low STB awareness compared to floss and interdental brushes

## 7. Glossary

- **STB (Single-Tuft Toothbrush)**: 첨단칫솔 — small-headed brush (one or few tufts, 3-6 mm diameter) with 90° angled shank; used for hard-to-reach oral surfaces
- **DPI (Disclosed Plaque Index)**: 착색된 치면세균막 지수 — plaque visualized with erythrosine dye; detects small plaque deposits
- **VPI (Visible Plaque Index)**: 육안적 치면세균막 지수 — gross plaque accumulation assessed without disclosing agent
- **PLA (Plaque Free Area / Zone)**: 치면세균막 비존재 영역 — zone between gingival margin and plaque border observed during early biofilm formation
- **Quorum sensing**: 정족수 감지 — bacterial cell-density-dependent gene regulation mechanism in biofilms
- **PHP index (Patient Hygiene Performance Index)**: 구강환경관리능력지수 — measure of patient's oral hygiene effectiveness
- **GBI (Gingival Bleeding Index)**: 치은출혈지수 — Loe & Silness index assessing gingival inflammation via bleeding on probing
- **Crossover study**: 교차연구 — each participant sequentially receives all interventions with washout period to minimize carryover effect
