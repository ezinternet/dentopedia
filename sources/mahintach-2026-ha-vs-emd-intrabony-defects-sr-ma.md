## One-line Summary
SR+MA (Oral Health Prev Dent 2026): 3 RCTs; HA clinically non-inferior to EMD for CAL gain in intrabony defects at 12 months (WMD 0.51 mm); PPD non-inferiority not confirmed.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA directly comparing HA vs EMD for intrabony defects (not vs placebo)
- HA non-inferior to EMD for CAL gain at 12 months (WMD 0.51 mm, CI −0.08 to 1.11)
- HA non-inferior for gingival recession at 12 months (WMD −0.4 mm, CI −0.79 to −0.01)
- PPD non-inferiority not established (WMD 0.71 mm, I²=72.5%)
- Only 3 RCTs eligible from 117 articles — evidence base thin; preliminary

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR+MA; PRISMA-compliant
- Databases: PubMed, ScienceDirect, Scopus, Google Scholar, LILACS
- Inclusion: RCTs comparing HA vs EMD for intrabony defect surgery
- Outcome: CAL gain (primary), PPD reduction, gingival recession
- Non-inferiority margin: 0.5 mm for CAL
- Statistical model: random-effects; weighted mean differences (WMD) + 95% CI
- n: 3 RCTs included from 117 screened

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 3 eligible RCTs — underpowered for definitive conclusions
- High heterogeneity for PPD (I²=72.5%) limits pooled interpretation
- Further RCTs needed with standardized protocols

## 6. Related Work
- HA has established anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties used in surgical perio
- EMD (Emdogain) is the established regenerative gold standard for intrabony defects

## 7. Glossary
- HA: Hyaluronic acid — naturally occurring glycosaminoglycan, anti-inflammatory, wound healing
- EMD: Enamel matrix derivative (Emdogain) — enamel matrix proteins promoting regeneration
- CAL: Clinical attachment level
- PPD: Probing pocket depth
- WMD: Weighted mean difference
