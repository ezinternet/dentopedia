## Why Ingested

This SR+MA provides the first pooled quantitative comparison of 변연적합도 (marginal fit) and 내부적합도 (internal adaptation) between milling and 3D printing for zirconia 고정성 보철물 (fixed dental prostheses, FDP). It directly informs digital fabrication workflow selection decisions with clinically actionable effect sizes.

→ [[prosthetic-materials/sengottaiyan-2026-zirconia-milling-vs-3d-printing-marginal-fit]]

## Three-line Summary

A systematic review and meta-analysis of 9 in vitro studies found that milled zirconia prostheses achieve statistically better marginal fit than 3D-printed restorations (MD = −13.12 µm; P < .001), while internal adaptation did not differ significantly between techniques (MD = −3.26 µm; P = .30). Both fabrication methods produced clinically acceptable marginal fit and internal adaptation. High heterogeneity (I² ≈ 92%) limits the strength of conclusions, and the evidence base is restricted to predominantly single-crown, in vitro designs.

## 세줄요약

밀링으로 제작한 지르코니아 보철물이 3D 프린팅보다 변연적합도가 통계적으로 유의하게 우수했다(MD = −13.12 µm; P < .001). 내부적합도는 두 기법 간 유의한 차이가 없었다(P = .30). 두 기법 모두 임상적으로 허용 가능한 수준이었으나, 높은 이질성(I² ≈ 92%)이 결론의 적용 범위를 제한한다.

## Key Contributions

1. First meta-analysis to quantitatively pool milling vs 3D printing for zirconia FDP 변연·내부적합도.
2. Demonstrated statistically significant marginal fit advantage for milling (MD = −13.12 µm) with high heterogeneity.
3. Confirmed no significant difference in internal adaptation between fabrication techniques.
4. Both techniques confirmed clinically acceptable by combined evidence.

## Methodology

- **Databases**: PubMed, Medline/Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar + manual search
- **Records screened**: 2,205 → 9 included in meta-analysis
- **Inclusion**: In vitro studies comparing 밀링 (milling) vs 3D 프린팅 (3D printing) for zirconia complete coverage FDP marginal and/or internal adaptation
- **Study designs**: Majority single crowns; 1 study assessed a 4-unit FDP
- **Data extraction**: Independent extraction + meta-analysis
- **Risk of bias**: QUIN tool

## Key Results

| Outcome | Mean Difference | 95% CI | P-value | I² |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 변연적합도 (Marginal fit) | −13.12 µm (milling better) | −20.21 to −6.03 | < .001 | 91.98% |
| 내부적합도 (Internal adaptation) | −3.26 µm | −9.41 to 2.88 | .30 | 85.93% |

- Milled zirconia: statistically significantly smaller marginal gaps
- Internal adaptation: no significant difference between techniques
- Both techniques: clinically acceptable marginal fit and internal adaptation

## Limitations

- High heterogeneity (I² = 91–92%) for both outcomes limits pooled interpretation
- Only 9 studies included; most focused on single crowns; only 1 assessed multi-unit FDP
- In vitro designs only — clinical performance may differ
- 3D printing technology is rapidly evolving; included studies may not reflect current printer/material capabilities

## Glossary

- **변연적합도 (Marginal fit, MF)**: Gap between restoration margin and prepared tooth finish line; clinically acceptable threshold commonly cited as < 120 µm
- **내부적합도 (Internal adaptation, IA)**: Fit between internal surface of restoration and prepared tooth axial/occlusal walls
- **절삭 가공 (Milling, subtractive manufacturing)**: CAD/CAM 기반 블록 절삭; 현재 디지털 보철의 표준
- **3D 프린팅 (3D printing, additive manufacturing)**: Layer-by-layer deposition; emerging for zirconia via binder jetting / DLP / SLA
- **Mean Difference (MD)**: Pooled difference in means across studies; negative MD here = milling produces smaller (better) gap
- **I²**: Heterogeneity statistic; > 75% = high heterogeneity
- **QUIN tool**: Quality assessment tool for in vitro studies used for risk-of-bias evaluation
