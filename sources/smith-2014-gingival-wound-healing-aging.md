## Why Ingested
치은 창상 치유의 세포·분자 기전(상피 이동, 과립조직 형성, 조직 리모델링) 을 정리한 리뷰 필요. 기존 [[oral-surgery/walton-2026-beyond-re-epithelialization-prolonged-remodeling]] 이 재상피화 이후를 다루는 반면, 본 논문은 치은 상피화 단계의 분자적 결손(노화 모델)을 통해 정상 치유 과정의 핵심 요소를 역으로 조명함.

## Three-line Summary

Literature review (Journal of Dental Research, 2014; Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile and Universidad de Chile) examining the cellular and molecular mechanisms by which aging impairs gingival wound healing, using the aging model to reveal the essential requirements of normal healing across epithelial migration, granulation tissue formation, and tissue remodeling phases.

Aging disrupts all three healing phases: exaggerated and prolonged inflammation (Phase 1–2); decreased integrin α5β1/αvβ6 signaling, reduced EGF/TGF-β growth factor activity, diminished cell proliferation, and impaired angiogenesis (Phase 3); excess MMP-1/MMP-8-mediated collagen breakdown, reduced collagen synthesis, and deteriorated stem cell proliferative and differentiation potential (Phase 4).

By identifying aging-induced molecular deficits across every healing phase, this review maps the essential machinery of normal gingival wound closure; integrin-dependent keratinocyte migration and stem cell renewal are the most clinically relevant targets, as their deterioration mirrors the effects of diabetes and other systemic impairments on socket healing.

## 세줄요약

노화가 치은 창상 치유를 손상시키는 세포·분자 기전을 규명한 문헌 리뷰(J Dent Res 2014; 칠레 카톨리카 대학·칠레 대학); 노화 모델을 통해 상피 이동·육아조직 형성·조직 리모델링 3단계에서의 정상 치유 필수 요소를 역으로 조명.

노화는 모든 치유 단계를 손상: 과다·지속 염증반응(1–2단계); 인테그린 α5β1/αvβ6 신호 감소, EGF/TGF-β 성장인자 활성 저하, 세포 증식·혈관신생 감소(3단계); MMP-1/MMP-8 과활성으로 콜라겐 과분해, 콜라겐 합성 감소, 줄기세포 증식·분화능 저하(4단계).

전 단계에 걸친 노화 유발 분자 결손을 규명함으로써 정상 치은 창상 폐쇄의 필수 기전 지도를 제공; 인테그린 의존성 각질세포 이동과 줄기세포 재생능력이 가장 임상적으로 중요한 표적으로, 당뇨 및 전신 질환에서의 소켓 치유 손상과 동일한 기전을 공유.

## One-line Summary
Review (J Dent Res 2014): aging impairs gingival wound healing through multiple mechanisms — increased inflammation, altered integrin signaling, reduced growth factor activity, decreased proliferation, diminished angiogenesis, and stem cell deterioration.

## 한줄요약
리뷰(J Dent Res 2014): 노화는 치은 창상 치유를 다중 기전으로 손상시킴 — 증가된 염증반응, 변형된 인테그린 신호, 성장인자 감소, 세포증식·혈관신생 저하, 줄기세포 분화능 저하.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Identified that gingival wound healing involves the same sequential phases as general wound healing: **epithelial migration → granulation tissue formation → tissue remodeling**
- Characterised aging as a systemic impairment model revealing the essential cellular requirements of normal gingival healing
- Highlighted **integrin signaling** as a critical control mechanism for keratinocyte migration
- Identified **stem cell proliferative and differentiation potential** as a late-phase determinant of wound quality
- Demonstrated **augmented MMP-mediated collagen remodeling** as a paradoxical aging feature (excessive breakdown, not formation)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Literature review (no systematic methodology stated)
- Focus: cellular and molecular basis of aging-induced defects in gingival wound healing
- Model: experimental animal data + in vitro studies + clinical observations

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Normal gingival healing phases: **epithelial migration, granulation tissue formation, tissue remodeling**
- Aging defects span all phases:
  - **Phase 1–2 (inflammation)**: exaggerated, prolonged inflammatory response
  - **Phase 3 (proliferation)**: ↓ integrin signaling, ↓ growth factor activity (EGF, TGF-β), ↓ cell proliferation, ↓ angiogenesis
  - **Phase 4 (remodeling)**: ↓ collagen synthesis, ↑ MMP-mediated collagen breakdown; ↓ stem cell differentiation potential
- Aging-induced stem cell deterioration impairs long-term tissue homeostasis (consistent with Walton 2026 Wnt-responsive stem cell depletion finding)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Literature review; heterogeneous experimental models
- Most cellular/molecular data from animal or in vitro models — limited direct human gingival tissue data
- Clinical implications not quantified (no RCT data)

## 6. Related Work
- Walton 2026: stem cell (Axin2+/Wnt-responsive) depletion persists 5 months post-wound
- Hämmerle 2014 (EWP): 4-phase consensus; 7–14 day epithelial healing

## 7. Glossary
- **Integrin signaling**: cell-ECM adhesion receptor pathway controlling keratinocyte migration across fibrin/fibronectin scaffold during re-epithelialization
- **Granulation tissue**: provisional vascularized connective tissue replacing fibrin clot; precursor to definitive connective tissue
- **MMPs (matrix metalloproteinases)**: collagen-cleaving enzymes; normally balanced with TIMPs; dysregulated in aging
- **Epithelial migration**: 가피 하부 fibrin 기질을 따라 상처 가장자리에서 keratinocyte가 이동하는 재상피화 과정
