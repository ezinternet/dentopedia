## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (IJOMI 2026) of in vivo studies on platform-switched implants published January 2014–June 2024 (MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science + hand search); 91 records, 80 full texts, 24 articles included.

PS implants were reported to improve pink esthetic score, mesial and distal papilla height, buccal peri-implant mucosal level, peri-implant mucosal zenith change, and VAS esthetics, with superior anterior-region esthetic integration.

Concludes PS improves esthetics by preserving soft tissue — but without meta-analysis, risk-of-bias grading in the abstract, or design-restricted inclusion, the claim is narrative-strength.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: MEDLINE (PubMed), Scopus, Web of Science; hand search; English; 2014-01 to 2024-06.
- Two independent reviewers.
- Inclusion: in vivo PS studies reporting peri-implant soft-tissue values.
- 91 → 80 full-text → 56 excluded → 24 included.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Direction-only reporting in the abstract: PS associated with improved PES, papilla height, buccal mucosal level, zenith change, and VAS esthetics; anterior region esthetics superior.
- No pooled effect sizes in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only.
- No meta-analysis; risk-of-bias and certainty grading not mentioned in abstract.
- "In vivo studies" includes non-randomized designs; the search yielded only 91 records, which is narrow.
- Authors call for long-term studies.

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary

- **Platform switching (PS)**: abutment narrower than the implant platform, medializing the implant–abutment junction.
- **PES**: pink esthetic score.
