## Why Ingested

[[drug/anticoagulants/bergo-2026-tranexamic-acid-anticoagulants-oral-surgery]] covers TXA mouthwash formulations in anticoagulated patients broadly; this study adds a critical surgical-socket delivery format — TXA pre-soaked into Gelfoam placed directly in the socket — tested in a triple-blind split-mouth design specifically in warfarin patients (INR 2.0–3.5), providing head-to-head socket-level pain and healing data absent from rinse-protocol studies.

## One-line Summary

Triple-blind split-mouth RCT (n=30 warfarin patients, INR 2.0–3.5) showing TXA-soaked Gelfoam significantly reduces post-extraction VAS pain from day 3 onward and accelerates socket healing versus saline-soaked Gelfoam, with no difference in hemostasis time.

## 한줄요약

와파린 복용 환자(INR 2.0–3.5) triple-blind split-mouth RCT (n=30): TXA 적신 Gelfoam이 3일째부터 VAS 통증 유의하게 낮추고(p<0.001) 치유 가속; 지혈 시간은 양 군 차이 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First triple-blind RCT specifically evaluating TXA-soaked Gelfoam (socket placement) vs saline-Gelfoam in warfarin patients at therapeutic INR (2.0–3.5)
- Anticoagulants continued without dose modification throughout; no bridging required
- Demonstrates TXA benefit beyond hemostasis: significant pain reduction from day 3 (VAS), accelerated socket healing at days 7 and 14
- Hemostasis time similar between groups — TXA in Gelfoam format may not confer additional hemostatic benefit over a gelatin sponge scaffold alone
- Triple-blind design (patient, operator, assessor) minimises detection bias; split-mouth eliminates inter-patient INR variability

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Triple-blind split-mouth RCT; Faculty of Dentistry, Alexandria University
- **Population**: 30 patients on continuous warfarin (INR 2.0–3.5) requiring bilateral single-rooted extractions; mean age ~50 years; conditions: rheumatic heart disease, DVT, AF; anticoagulant not stopped
- **Exclusion**: INR >3.5, complex extractions, allergies, pregnancy, recent NSAID use
- **Intervention (test)**: Gelfoam pre-soaked in TXA 4.8% solution, placed in socket after extraction, suture with 3-0 silk
- **Control**: Gelfoam pre-soaked in saline, otherwise identical protocol
- **Crossover**: contralateral sites at ≥2-week interval; side allocation randomly assigned
- **Outcomes**: VAS pain (10 cm) at 1, 3, 7, 14 days; Landry Healing Index at 7 and 14 days; hemostasis time (min)
- **Statistics**: SPSS v26; Mann-Whitney U / Wilcoxon; Friedman test; p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**VAS Pain (median ± IQR):**
| Day | TXA-Gelfoam | Saline-Gelfoam | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5.53 ± 0.93 | 5.47 ± 0.87 | NS |
| 3 | 2.67 ± 0.85 | 3.13 ± 0.43 | 0.005 |
| 7 | 0.73 ± 0.78 | 1.80 ± 1.00 | <0.001 |
| 14 | 0.13 ± 0.35 | 0.80 ± 0.61 | <0.001 |

- **Hemostasis time**: no statistically significant difference between groups
- **Landry Healing Index day 7**: TXA group significantly higher (p<0.001)
- **Landry Healing Index day 14**: TXA group significantly higher (p<0.001)
- No postoperative complications (rebleeding, dry socket, infection) in either group

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (n=30); single-center
- Only simple single-rooted extractions — complex or surgical cases not covered
- Therapeutic INR range limited to 2.0–3.5; results may not apply at higher INR
- Short follow-up (14 days); bone healing not assessed
- Gelfoam used as scaffold in both arms; pure TXA vs pure gauze comparison not included

## 7. Glossary

- **INR**: International Normalized Ratio; warfarin therapeutic target 2.0–3.5 for cardiac indications
- **TXA (Tranexamic Acid, 트라넥삼산)**: synthetic lysine analogue; blocks fibrinolysis by inhibiting plasminogen-to-plasmin conversion; 4.8% solution used topically
- **Gelfoam**: porcine gelatin sponge; acts as physical scaffold for clot formation; resorbs over 3–5 weeks
- **Landry Healing Index**: 5-point clinical score for post-extraction socket healing (colour, epithelialization, necrosis absence)
