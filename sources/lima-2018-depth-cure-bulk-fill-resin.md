## Why Ingested

Classic SR establishing minimum parameters for successful bulk-fill curing (≥1000 mW/cm², ≥20s) — provides the evidence base for LCU selection in bulk-fill restorations.

→ [[resin/lima-2018-depth-cure-bulk-fill-resin]]

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PubMed/Medline, Embase, Scopus, ISI Web of Science, n=33 studies) evaluating depth of cure of bulk-fill resin composites using microhardness testing and degree of conversion measurements.
Favorable depth of cure requires LCU irradiance ≥1000 mW/cm² AND exposure time ≥20 seconds; acceptable thresholds are bottom/top hardness ratio ≥0.8 and degree of conversion ≥50%.
No single bulk-fill product universally achieved acceptable depth of cure; LCU parameters are more critical than material choice; polywave LCUs are useful but not essential.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (PubMed·Embase·Scopus·ISI, n=33 연구)로 벌크필 복합레진(Bulk-Fill Resin Composite)의 중합 깊이를 미세경도와 전환율(Degree of Conversion, DC)로 평가함.
적절한 중합 깊이 달성에 광강도(Irradiance) ≥1000 mW/cm² AND 조사시간 ≥20초 필요; 허용 기준: 하부/상부 경도비(Bottom/Top Ratio) ≥0.8, DC ≥50%.
어떤 단일 벌크필 제품도 모든 조건에서 보편적으로 허용 기준을 달성하지 못함; 광중합기(LCU) 파라미터가 재료 선택보다 중요; 폴리웨이브(Polywave) LCU는 유용하나 필수 아님.

## Abstract Summary

Systematic review evaluating scientific evidence on depth of cure of bulk-fill resin composites (BFRCs) and related factors. Databases searched: PubMed/Medline, Embase, Scopus, ISI Web of Science (Oct 2016–May 2017). Included studies assessed depth of cure by microhardness test and/or degree of conversion (DC). 742 studies found; 33 included.

## Key Results

**Study counts:**
- 21 studies evaluated microhardness
- 19 studies assessed degree of conversion (DC)
- n=33 total included studies

**Microhardness (bottom/top ratio, threshold ≥0.8):**
- 10 studies: acceptable ratio (≥0.8) for ALL materials
- 9 studies: material-dependent results
- 2 studies: non-satisfactory results (<0.8)

**Degree of Conversion (threshold ≥50%):**
- 11 studies: acceptable DC (≥50%) for ALL BFRCs
- 8 studies: material-dependent results

**LCU parameters across studies:**
- Irradiance range: 650–1330 mW/cm²
- Exposure time range: 5–60 seconds

**Key finding:** Favorable depth of cure requires LCU irradiance **≥1000 mW/cm²** AND exposure time **≥20 seconds**.

**Polywave LCUs:** useful but NOT essential for polymerizing alternative photoinitiator-containing BFRCs.

**Clinical imperative:** LED devices displaying ≥1000 mW/cm² and 20 seconds are imperative for successful polymerization of MOST BFRCs.
