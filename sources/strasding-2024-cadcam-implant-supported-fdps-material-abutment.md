## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Clin Oral Implants Res 2024;35:984–999) synthesizing evidence on CAD/CAM abutment and restoration material selection for implant-supported FDPs, covering titanium/zirconia abutments, monolithic/veneered restorations, and digital workflow considerations for single crowns and multi-unit cases.

Ti-base abutments recommended as standard for single-implant restorations (superior fracture resistance vs. zirconia); monolithic zirconia or lithium disilicate for posterior single crowns; micro-veneered CAD/CAM ceramics for esthetic anterior cases; zirconia frameworks for multi-unit implant FDPs with high load demands.

CAD/CAM material selection must consider restoration type (crown vs. FDP), region (anterior vs. posterior), implant connection geometry, and esthetic demands; milling precision, surface treatment, and cementation protocol are equally critical to long-term success.

## One-line Summary

Narrative review (Clin Oral Implants Res 2024) providing updated guidance on CAD/CAM abutment and material selection for implant-supported single crowns and multi-unit FDPs, recommending Ti-base abutments and monolithic zirconia/lithium disilicate by region.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Systematic guidance on Ti-base abutments as preferred alternative to stock/customized metal or zirconia abutments for single crowns (combines metallic connection stability + ceramic esthetic potential)
- For multi-unit restorations: conical Ti-base bridges recommended to compensate for divergent implant axes
- Posterior zone: monolithic zirconia (3–5Y mol%) or lithium disilicate as first choice; anterior: micro-veneered ceramic for optimal esthetics
- LD limited to single crowns; zirconia (3–5Y mol%) extends to multi-unit and cantilever FDPs
- Warning: mechanical properties vary significantly by zirconia type — not all zirconia suitable for all regions/spans

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Study design:** Narrative review
- **Scope:** Provisional and definitive CAD/CAM restorations for implant-supported FDPs in partially edentulous patients
- **Topics:** Abutment types (stock, customized, Ti-base, hybrid), provisional materials (PMMA, bis-acrylic), definitive materials (metal-ceramic, zirconia, lithium disilicate, hybrid ceramic, full contour)

## 4. Key Guidance Summary

**Abutment selection — single crowns:**
- Ti-base abutments: preferred for esthetic zones; various geometries and heights available
- Customized titanium abutments: still indicated for specific clinical scenarios (deep margin placement, complex emergence profile)

**Abutment selection — multi-unit FDPs:**
- Conical Ti-base bridges: recommended for divergent implant axes
- One-piece customized titanium frameworks: option for complex multi-unit cases

**Restorative material — posterior (non-esthetic zone):**
- Monolithic zirconia 3–5Y mol% or LD: recommended
- Avoid veneered zirconia in posterior to minimize chipping risk

**Restorative material — anterior (esthetic zone):**
- Micro-veneered ceramic for optimal appearance
- LD: single crowns only
- Zirconia 3–5Y mol%: single + multi-unit and cantilever

**Key material caution:** High-translucency zirconia (4–5Y, more yttria) = lower mechanical strength — not indicated for all regions or span lengths.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review methodology — no systematic search, no meta-analysis
- Rapid material evolution: recommendations may require frequent updating
- Long-term RCT data for newer Ti-base designs still accumulating
