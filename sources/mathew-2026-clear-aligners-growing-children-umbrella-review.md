## Why Ingested

Extends the growing-patient CA evidence base already anchored by [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/fonseca-planells-2026-clear-aligner-maxillary-expansion-growing]]; provides the first umbrella review specifically synthesising SR-level evidence on CA clinical outcomes in children undergoing active growth, covering dentoalveolar, skeletal, periodontal, and patient-reported outcome domains.

## One-line Summary

Umbrella review (JBI methodology, 6 SRs from 1,609 records): CA in growing patients achieves predictable dentoalveolar correction but skeletal evidence is limited and mixed, with overall very low methodological quality (5/6 SRs critically low AMSTAR-2).

## 한줄요약

우산고찰 (JBI 방법론, 6편 SR / 1,609건): 성장기 소아에서 투명교정(CA)은 치조성 개선(정렬·overjet·arch 발달)에 일관되지만 골격 효과는 불확실하고, 포함 SR 5/6편이 AMSTAR-2 critically low — 근거 질 매우 낮음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review applying JBI methodology and PRISMA for Overviews checklist to synthesise SR-level CA evidence in growing children
- All 6 included SRs consistently reported dentoalveolar improvements (tooth alignment, overjet reduction, arch development)
- Skeletal outcomes heterogeneous: 2 SRs noted possible mandibular changes; 3 reported minimal/no skeletal effects; 1 found insufficient evidence
- Periodontal and patient-reported outcomes (PROs) each addressed by only 2 SRs — flagged as underreported domains
- Meta-analytic results showed low heterogeneity for sagittal skeletal measures (ANB, SNA, SNB) but variable heterogeneity for dental outcomes (overjet, overbite)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Study design: Umbrella review (synthesis of systematic reviews); JBI umbrella review methodology; PRISMA for Overviews checklist
- Search: 1,609 records → 6 systematic reviews included
- Inclusion criteria: SRs on CA in growing/mixed-dentition children
- Quality appraisal: AMSTAR-2 — 5 critically low, 1 low quality; high degree of corrected covered area (CCA) reported, indicating substantial overlap among primary studies across included SRs
- Outcome domains: dentoalveolar (primary), skeletal, periodontal, patient-reported outcomes
- Statistical methods: meta-analyses performed for ANB, SNA, SNB, overjet, overbite

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Dentoalveolar outcomes**: all 6 SRs consistently positive — tooth alignment, overjet reduction, arch development
- **Skeletal outcomes** (6 SRs, discordant):
  - 2 SRs: possible mandibular changes observed
  - 3 SRs: minimal or no skeletal effects
  - 1 SR: insufficient evidence to conclude
- **Sagittal skeletal MA**: low heterogeneity for ANB, SNA, SNB pooled estimates (specific values not provided in abstract)
- **Dental MA**: variable heterogeneity for overjet and overbite
- **Periodontal outcomes**: reported in 2 SRs — insufficient to draw conclusions
- **Patient-reported outcomes (PROs)**: reported in 2 SRs — insufficient to draw conclusions
- Overall conclusion: CA therapy in growing patients → predictable dentoalveolar correction; skeletal, periodontal, and PRO evidence remains limited

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- AMSTAR-2 critically low for 5/6 included SRs — conclusions rest on methodologically weak evidence base
- High degree of overlap (CCA) across SRs means many primary studies contribute to multiple included reviews, inflating apparent evidence volume
- Abstract-only source: full quantitative pooling details (effect sizes, CIs, heterogeneity I²) not extractable
- Periodontal and PRO domains severely underrepresented; future SRs should mandate standardised periodontal and QoL reporting in CA/growing-patient trials
- No long-term follow-up data reported; post-pubertal stability of dentoalveolar gains unaddressed
- Mix of CA systems (Invisalign First, generic thermoplastic) likely included — system-specific conclusions not possible

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: synthesis of systematic reviews, sits above SR+MA in the evidence hierarchy
- **AMSTAR-2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews (version 2); rates SR methodological quality as critically low / low / moderate / high
- **CCA (Corrected Covered Area)**: measure of overlap among primary studies across included SRs; high CCA signals redundancy
- **PRISMA for Overviews**: reporting checklist adapted from PRISMA for umbrella reviews
- **JBI**: Joanna Briggs Institute; developer of umbrella review methodology used in this paper
- **Dentoalveolar**: pertaining to alveolar bone and teeth (as distinct from basal/skeletal bone)
- **PROs (Patient-Reported Outcomes)**: outcomes measured directly from patient self-report (e.g., pain, QoL, satisfaction)

## Three-line Summary

- Umbrella review (JBI methodology, PRISMA for Overviews, PROSPERO CRD420251054252) synthesizing 6 systematic reviews from 1,609 records on clinical outcomes of clear aligners in growing children with mixed/transitional dentition.
- All 6 SRs consistently reported dentoalveolar improvements (tooth alignment, overjet reduction, arch development); skeletal outcomes were discordant — 2 SRs noted possible mandibular changes, 3 reported minimal or no skeletal effects, 1 found insufficient evidence; periodontal and patient-reported outcomes (PROs) insufficient; AMSTAR-2 rated 5/6 SRs critically low.
- Clear aligners predictably correct dentoalveolar problems in growing patients but lack evidence for meaningful skeletal modification; the current evidence base rests on very low methodological quality across all included reviews.
