## Why Ingested

Ingested to document clinical experience with a Korean domestically available porcine-derived collagen matrix (Collagen Graft x1D, Genoss) for three distinct soft tissue applications. Existing SR/MA evidence in the wiki (e.g., [[implants/soft-tissue/rios-osorio-2025-xcm-vs-ctg-fgg-implant-soft-tissue-sr-ma]] and [[implants/soft-tissue/montero-2022-soft-tissue-substitutes-vs-autogenous-keratinized-mucosa-sr]]) characterizes xenogeneic collagen matrices (XCM) vs. autografts in aggregate; this case series adds product-specific feasibility data (vestibuloplasty, soft tissue augmentation, alveolar ridge preservation) for a cross-linked porcine matrix not yet represented in the wiki.

## Three-line Summary

Case series (3 patients) evaluated a porcine-derived collagen matrix (Collagen Graft x1D) for vestibuloplasty, soft tissue augmentation, and alveolar ridge preservation.

The matrix showed biocompatibility, rapid epithelialization, and favorable integration without notable complications; vestibuloplasty achieved functional attached mucosa and reduced probing depth from 8 to 5 mm at 14 months.

Porcine collagen matrix is a feasible, less invasive alternative to autogenous grafts, minimizing donor site morbidity while achieving acceptable clinical outcomes across three soft tissue applications.

## 세줄요약

돼지 유래 콜라겐 매트릭스(Collagen Graft x1D)를 전정성형술, 연조직 증대술, 치조제 보존술에 적용한 3개 증례의 증례 시리즈.

매트릭스는 생체적합성, 빠른 상피화, 합병증 없는 통합을 보였으며, 전정성형술에서 기능적 부착 점막을 얻고 탐침 깊이가 8 mm에서 5 mm로 감소했다(14개월 추적).

돼지 콜라겐 매트릭스는 공여부 이환율을 최소화하면서 세 가지 연조직 적응증에서 수용 가능한 임상 결과를 달성할 수 있는 자가 이식편의 대안이다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: J Implantol Appl Sci (대한임플란트학회지), 30(2):123–130
- **Institution**: National Health Insurance Service Ilsan Hospital, Goyang, Korea
- **Study design**: Case series
- **n**: 3 cases
- **Follow-up**: 14 months (Cases 1 & 2); 2 months (Case 3 / ARP)
- **Product**: Collagen Graft x1D (Genoss, Suwon, Korea) — cross-linked porcine type I collagen, bilayer structure

## 2. Key Contributions

- Clinical documentation of a bilayer cross-linked porcine collagen matrix across three application paradigms: vestibuloplasty (open healing), submerged soft tissue augmentation (peri-implant), and alveolar ridge preservation (ARP) after extraction
- Demonstrates rapid epithelialization (observed at 2 weeks) under open healing conditions
- Vestibuloplasty case: functional attached mucosa formed; probing depth (PD) reduced from 8 mm to 5 mm at 14 months
- Soft tissue augmentation case: Pink Esthetic Score (PES) of 11/14 maintained at 14 months
- ARP case: vertical ridge height maintained at 73.3% of baseline at 2 months
- No donor site morbidity (absent by design: no autograft harvested)
- No notable complications reported across all 3 cases

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Study design**: Descriptive case series; no control group; no randomization.

**Product**: Collagen Graft x1D (Genoss, Suwon, Korea) — cross-linked type I porcine collagen with bilayer structure (porous side facing wound bed; dense side facing oral environment). Cross-linking aimed at extending resorption time and structural support.

**Surgical approaches**:
- Case 1 (Vestibuloplasty): Partial-thickness flap with apical positioning; collagen matrix sutured in place; open healing.
- Case 2 (Soft tissue augmentation, peri-implant): Submerged technique; matrix placed beneath a coronally advanced flap at second-stage surgery; primary closure achieved.
- Case 3 (ARP): Socket-shield/open healing protocol; matrix placed to cover extraction socket; no primary closure.

**Assessment**: Clinical photographs, periapical radiographs, probing depths, PES score. No volumetric or histologic analysis.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Case series design (n=3): no control group, no randomization; highest risk of bias
- Small sample size prevents generalizability
- Qualitative/clinical assessment only — no volumetric (CBCT-based) or histologic analysis
- Short follow-up for ARP case (2 months); 14 months adequate for soft tissue stability but not long-term bone/implant outcomes
- Single product (Collagen Graft x1D); results may not transfer to non-cross-linked porcine matrices (e.g., Geistlich Mucograft)
- No comparison arm to autogenous grafts (FGG/CTG); superiority/non-inferiority cannot be claimed

## 7. Glossary

- **ARP (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, 치조제 보존술)**: Procedure to maintain alveolar ridge dimensions following tooth extraction, often using graft materials placed in the socket.
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score, 분홍 심미 점수)**: A 7-item clinical index (0–14) scoring peri-implant soft tissue esthetics (mesial/distal papilla, soft tissue contour, color, texture, alveolar process, root convexity).
- **PD (Probing Depth, 탐침 깊이)**: Distance from gingival margin to the base of the sulcus/pocket on probing.
- **Vestibuloplasty (전정성형술)**: Surgical procedure to deepen the oral vestibule and/or increase the width of attached/keratinized mucosa.
- **XCM (Xenogeneic Collagen Matrix, 이종 콜라겐 매트릭스)**: Animal-derived (porcine or bovine) collagen scaffold used as a substitute for autogenous soft tissue grafts.
- **Cross-linked collagen (가교 콜라겐)**: Collagen whose fibers are chemically bonded to extend resorption time and improve mechanical stability; contrasts with non-cross-linked matrices (e.g., Mucograft).
- **CTG (Connective Tissue Graft, 결합 조직 이식편)**: Autogenous subepithelial connective tissue harvested from the palate; gold standard for soft tissue augmentation.
- **FGG (Free Gingival Graft, 유리 치은 이식편)**: Autogenous keratinized gingival tissue harvested from the palate; gold standard for increasing keratinized mucosa width.
