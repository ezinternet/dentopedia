## Why Ingested

Fills the e-cigarette gap that [[implants/calciolari-2026-tobacco-smoking-smoke-free-products]] explicitly left open (vaping evidence judged insufficient for pooling) by approaching the same exposure from the microbial side, giving [[overviews/smoking-tobacco-periodontal-implant-overview]] a mechanistic candidate for why vaping might sit between smoking and never-smoking. Read against the dysbiosis framework of [[overviews/oral-microbiome-biofilm-dysbiosis-synthesis]], though at GRADE very-low certainty.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (12 studies, human molecular-method comparisons of saliva/subgingival plaque/mucosal-swab microbiota) examining whether e-cigarette users' oral microbiota differs from never-smokers and current (combustible) smokers.

Beta diversity consistently showed distinct microbial communities in e-cigarette users vs never-smokers, with sample-specific enrichment of genera such as Veillonella, Leptotrichia and Fusobacterium; alpha-diversity findings were inconsistent, and overall the vaping microbial profile partially overlapped with but did not fully resemble either smokers or never-smokers.

Certainty of evidence is very low (GRADE) — predominantly cross-sectional studies with heterogeneous methods — so this should be read as a hypothesis-generating dysbiosis signal rather than a settled harm/safety verdict for vaping relative to smoking; longitudinal, standardized-protocol studies are needed.

## 세줄요약

체계적 고찰(인체 연구 12편, 타액·치은연하치태·구강점막에서 분자생물학적 방법으로 구강 미생물총 비교) — 전자담배(e-cigarette) 사용자의 구강 미생물총이 비흡연자·궐련 흡연자와 다른지 검증.

베타 다양성(군집 구조)은 전자담배 사용자와 비흡연자 간 뚜렷한 차이를 일관되게 보였고 Veillonella, Leptotrichia, Fusobacterium 등 속(genus)이 증가하는 경향; 알파 다양성(종 풍부도)은 연구 간 결과가 일관되지 않았으며, 전반적으로 전자담배군의 미생물 프로파일은 흡연자·비흡연자 어느 쪽과도 완전히 일치하지 않고 부분적으로 겹침.

근거 수준은 GRADE 기준 매우 낮음(대부분 단면연구·방법 이질성)이므로, 이는 확정된 결론이 아니라 가설 생성 수준의 미생물 불균형(dysbiosis) 신호로 해석해야 하며, 장기추적·표준화 프로토콜 연구가 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First systematic synthesis specifically isolating e-cigarette (vaping) exposure's effect on oral microbiota, separate from combustible smoking.
- Highlights beta-diversity (community structure) as the more consistent signal than alpha-diversity (richness) across heterogeneous study designs.
- Surfaces methodological gaps (DNA extraction variability, unreported batch effects, inconsistent negative controls) as an agenda for future oral-microbiome tobacco research.

## 3. Methodology
PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science searched 27 August 2025. Human studies using molecular methods (16S rRNA sequencing etc.) comparing oral microbiota among e-cigarette users, never-smokers, and current smokers across saliva, subgingival plaque, and oral mucosal swabs. Risk of bias: JBI tools; certainty of evidence: GRADE. 12 studies included, mostly cross-sectional.

## 4. Key Results
[근거매우약함, GRADE very low] Beta diversity: consistently distinct microbial communities in e-cigarette users vs never-smokers. Alpha diversity: inconsistent across samples/studies. Taxonomic differences heterogeneous and sample-dependent; some enrichment of Veillonella, Leptotrichia, Fusobacterium vs never-smokers. Overall profile partly overlaps with, but differs from, both smokers and never-smokers.

## 5. Limitations
- GRADE certainty very low for all outcomes — serious risk of bias from exposure measurement, confounder control, and (for cohort studies) incomplete follow-up/attrition.
- Predominantly cross-sectional design, inadequate for a dynamic ecosystem like the oral microbiota (misses temporal/exposure-duration effects).
- Small samples, incomplete group matching, device heterogeneity (e-cigarette type/nicotine content not standardized), limited confounder control.
- Methodological reporting gaps: DNA extraction method, 16S variable region, sequencing platform/pipeline, batch-effect control, and negative/blank controls inconsistently reported across studies.
- **Funding/COI note for critical appraisal**: one included primary study was funded by a research foundation affiliated with the state-owned China National Tobacco Corporation (Wang et al.). Separately, this review's senior author discloses extensive consulting/funding ties to tobacco-harm-reduction and e-cigarette-adjacent organizations (Global Action to End Smoking [formerly Foundation for Smoke-Free World], and multiple pharma/consumer-health consultancies) — disclosed transparently in the paper, but worth flagging as a source-quality caveat when citing this SR's framing of vaping risk.
