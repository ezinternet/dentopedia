## Why Ingested
심미부 단일치 발치 후 조기 식립 + 동시 GBR(2-layer composite graft) 프로토콜의 10년 장기 추적 데이터. [[bone-regeneration/buser-2023-gbr-implant-35years-basic-principle-review]] 근거 체계의 핵심 기초 임상 데이터이며, [[immediate-implant/buser-2017-implant-placement-timing-post-extraction-esthetic]] "조기 식립 권고"의 10년 결과 검증 논문이다.

## One-line Summary
Prospective case series (n=20, 10-year): early implant placement + simultaneous GBR (2-layer composite graft) in esthetic zone achieved 95% success, median facial bone gain to 1.67 mm, stable peri-implant bone (0.35 mm loss at 1–10 y), and high PES (8).

## 한줄요약
전향적 증례(n=20, 10년): 심미부 조기식립+GBR(2층 복합이식) — 성공률 95%, 안면골벽 1.67mm 증가, 변연골 소실 0.35mm(1~10년), PES 8(고). 협측골벽이 0에서 재건되는 장기 안정성 확인.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Journal of Dental Research, 2018;97(2):138–145 (doi: 10.1177/0022034517737755)
- **Institution**: University of Bern, Switzerland (Buser group)
- **Registration**: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03252106
- **Follow-up**: 10 years
- **n**: 20 patients, single-tooth post-extraction sites in anterior maxilla

## 2. Key Contributions
- 10-year data on facial bone wall thickness: 0 mm at surgery → 1.67 mm at 10 years (significant increase)
- Facial bone wall peak (DIC) located 0.16 mm coronal to implant shoulder — favorable position
- Minimal facial bone loss from 6 to 10 years (0.02 mm/year) — confirming long-term stability
- Pink Esthetic Score (PES) median = 8 — high esthetic outcome maintained
- Modulating factors: preoperative proximal crest width and soft tissue thickness predict outcomes
- Establishes equivalence of stable bone conditions (null hypothesis: difference >0.2 mm/year rejected)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Prospective case series (non-randomized)
- **Population**: 20 patients with single-tooth loss in esthetic zone (anterior maxilla)
- **Protocol**: Early implant placement (Type 2, 6–8 weeks post-extraction) + simultaneous contour augmentation with 2-layer composite graft
  - Inner layer: autogenous bone chips (osteogenic)
  - Outer layer: low-substitution bone filler (volume maintenance)
  - Covered with biodegradable collagen membrane
- **Outcomes**: Peri-implant bone loss, facial bone wall thickness (CBCT), PES, mucosal recession
- **Imaging**: CBCT at preoperative, 6 years, 10 years; periapical radiographs for MBL
- **Statistical**: Nonparametric analysis + equivalence testing

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Success rate**: 95% (19/20)
- **Peri-implant bone loss (1–10 years)**: median 0.35 mm
- **Facial bone wall thickness**:
  - At surgery: 0 mm (no wall)
  - At 10 years: 1.67 mm (significant increase; p<0.001)
- **Facial vertical bone loss (6–10 years)**: 0.02 mm — clinically negligible
- **PES (10 years)**: median 8 (scale 0–14)
- **Facial recession (IC₁–IC₁₀)**: median 0.17 mm — minimal
- **Equivalence test**: Bone loss <0.2 mm/year confirmed between timepoints (stable)
- **Modulating factors**: Narrow proximal crest, thin soft tissue = worse outcomes

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=20), single center, no control group
- All performed by master surgeon (Buser) — may overestimate outcomes in general practice
- Selection bias: only patients with adequate anatomy included
- 30% of cases had complications (not further specified in abstract)

## 6. Related Work
- Buser 2023 GBR review: places these results in 35-year context
- Buser 2017 (immediate vs. early vs. late): provides timing rationale for early placement
- Chappuis 2013 (7-year early implant contour augmentation): precursor paper

## 7. Glossary
- **Early implant placement (Type 2)**: Implant placed 6–8 weeks post-extraction with soft tissue healing but no bone fill
- **2-layer composite graft**: Autogenous bone chips (inner) + low-substitution xenograft (outer) + collagen membrane
- **DIC**: Distance from implant crown margin to facial bone wall peak
- **PES**: Pink Esthetic Score (evaluates peri-implant soft tissue esthetics, 0–14)
- **CBCT**: Cone beam computed tomography
