## Why Ingested

Ingested as part of a deliberate topical sweep on mandibular/alveolar "nutrient canals" (NC) as a radiographic anatomical structure — a topic the wiki previously had zero coverage of. This is a large cross-sectional comparison (n=2989) specifically testing the NC–hypertension association; it is being ingested alongside a companion multi-disease paper in the same sweep, [[radiology/hasan-2022-prevalence-nutrient-canals-mandibular]] (nutrient-canal prevalence across diabetes/hypertension/periodontitis).

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional radiographic comparison of 2989 patients (1440 known hypertensives vs 1549 nonhypertensive controls), using intraoral periapical radiographs (IOPAR) of the lower anterior region to score presence/number of nutrient canals (NC) against periodontal bone-loss status, hypertension severity, and duration.

NC incidence was significantly higher in hypertensive patients (55.2%) than controls (36.2%, p=0.001, ~2.17x); incidence rose with age up to 60 years and with increasing alveolar bone loss (22.1% no bone loss → 75.8% with >2/3 root bone loss), and was higher in edentulous vs dentulous patients (55.5% vs 44.1%), but showed no correlation with hypertension severity and an unexpected drop in patients with hypertension >10 years (17.6%).

Clinically, NC presence on a routine periapical radiograph is not diagnostic of hypertension by itself but should raise suspicion and prompt blood-pressure screening, especially given how many hypertensive patients are undiagnosed at dental visits; key limitations are the purely descriptive/observational design (no adjustment for confounders such as age distribution imbalance between groups) and reliance on visual interpretation via magnifying lens rather than digital/CBCT quantification.

## 세줄요약

2989명(고혈압군 1440명 vs 비고혈압 대조군 1549명)을 대상으로 하악 전치부 치근단방사선사진(IOPAR)에서 영양관(Nutrient Canal, NC) 유무를 치주 골소실 상태·고혈압 중증도·이환기간과 함께 평가한 단면연구다.

NC 발생률은 고혈압군(55.2%)이 대조군(36.2%)보다 유의하게 높았고(p=0.001, 약 2.17배), 60세까지는 연령이 증가할수록, 그리고 치조골 소실이 심할수록(골소실 없음 22.1% → 2/3 이상 소실 75.8%) NC 발생률이 증가했으며 무치악 환자가 유치악 환자보다 높았으나(55.5% vs 44.1%), 고혈압 중증도와는 상관관계가 없었고 이환기간 10년 초과 군에서는 오히려 발생률이 급감했다(17.6%).

임상적으로 치근단방사선사진에서 NC 소견은 그 자체로 고혈압을 진단하지 못하지만 미진단 고혈압 환자가 많은 치과 내원 상황에서 혈압 재검사를 유도하는 보조 단서로 활용할 수 있으며, 순수 관찰연구 설계(군간 연령분포 불균형 등 교란변수 미보정)와 확대경을 이용한 육안 판독(디지털/CBCT 정량화 미적용)이 핵심 한계다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- One of the larger single-center cross-sectional studies (n=2989) specifically testing whether the radiographic presence of nutrient canals (NC) on lower-anterior periapical films correlates with a diagnosis of hypertension.
- Stratifies NC incidence across multiple independent variables in the same cohort: hypertension status, age decade, periodontal bone-loss extent, hypertension severity (BHS categories: mild/moderate/severe), hypertension duration, and dentulous vs edentulous status — giving a multi-axis picture rather than a single comparison.
- Reports a counterintuitive finding (NC incidence drops sharply after >10 years of hypertension) and offers a plausible pathophysiologic explanation (terminal-stage arteriosclerotic vessel calcification causing NC disappearance), distinguishing it from studies that only report a simple monotonic trend.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Cross-sectional, case-control-style comparison (not randomized, not longitudinal).
- **Setting**: Department of Oral Medicine and Radiology, single institution; ethical committee clearance obtained.
- **Population**: 2989 individuals aged 10–70 years. Exclusion criteria: diabetes, tuberculosis, coarctation of aorta, rickets, other bone disorders, smokers, and any patients with periapical lesions.
- **Groups**:
  - Study group (n=1440, known hypertensive): Subgroup 1 no bone loss (n=574), Subgroup 2 with bone loss (n=655), Subgroup 3 edentulous (n=211).
  - Control group (n=1549, nonhypertensive): Subgroup 1 no bone loss (n=813), Subgroup 2 with bone loss (n=632), Subgroup 3 edentulous (n=104).
- **Imaging protocol**: IOPAR of the lower anterior region, bisecting-angle technique, New Life Radiology S.R.L. intraoral X-ray unit at 70 kVp / 10 mAs; exposure time 0.6 s (dentulous) / 0.5 s (edentulous). Films processed in an automatic film processor (Periomat) and read on a viewer with a magnifying lens (no digital/CBCT quantification).
- **Outcome variables recorded**: presence/absence of NC, number and location of NC, periodontal bone-loss category (none / up to 1/3 / up to 2/3 / >2/3 root length), hypertension duration and severity (auscultatory BP method, sitting posture, British Hypertension Society severity bands).
- **Statistics**: Descriptive statistics (mean ± SD, %); chi-square test for study-vs-control comparison; binomial probability distribution for subgroup significance; 95% CI computed.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Comparison | Finding |
|---|---|
| Overall NC incidence (n=2989) | 45.3% |
| Study group (hypertensive) vs control | 55.2% vs 36.2% (≈2.17x, p=0.001) |
| Age 41–50y | Study 63.9% vs control 39.9% |
| Age 51–60y | Study 63.7% vs control 52.4% |
| No bone loss vs >2/3 root bone loss | 22.1% vs 75.8% NC incidence |
| Bone loss, study group vs control (with bone loss) | 75.4% vs 59.8% |
| Bone loss, study group vs control (no bone loss) | 31.4% vs 15.6% |
| Hypertension severity (mild/moderate/severe) | 55.7% / 57.9% / 58.3% NC — no clear severity gradient |
| Hypertension duration 1–3y vs >10y | 61.6% vs 17.6% (sharp drop) |
| Edentulous vs dentulous (overall) | 55.5% vs 44.1% |
| Edentulous: study vs control | 56.8% vs 52.8% — not significant |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Cross-sectional/observational design — cannot establish causality between hypertension and NC formation, only association.
- Age distribution was imbalanced between study and control groups in some strata (e.g., very few study-group patients <30 years), limiting the reliability of age-stratified comparisons.
- NC detection relied on 2D periapical film read with a magnifying lens rather than digital radiography or CBCT, which the authors themselves note limits sensitivity/precision compared with 3D imaging.
- No adjustment for potential confounders such as smoking history beyond exclusion, medication effects on vasculature, or inter-observer reliability of NC scoring.
- The paradoxical decline in NC incidence beyond 10 years of hypertension duration is explained post hoc (terminal arteriosclerotic calcification) rather than confirmed by direct vascular imaging.

## 7. Glossary

- **Nutrient canal (NC)**: A radiolucent line in alveolar bone representing the path of a blood vessel/nerve bundle, most often seen in the mandibular anterior region on periapical radiographs; ends in a nutrient foramen.
- **IOPAR**: Intraoral periapical radiograph.
- **Bisecting-angle technique**: An intraoral radiographic projection technique that bisects the angle between the film plane and the long axis of the tooth to minimize image distortion.
- **British Hypertension Society (BHS) severity categories**: Classification of hypertension into mild/moderate/severe bands based on systolic/diastolic blood pressure.
- **Arteriosclerosis**: Thickening and loss of elasticity of arterial walls, associated with luminal narrowing; proposed mechanistic link to increased collateral vessel/nutrient canal formation in this paper.
