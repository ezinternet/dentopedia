## Why Ingested

This RCT directly compares patient-reported outcomes (OHIP-14 quality of life, satisfaction) between zygomatic implant-supported and All-on-4-supported full-arch fixed prostheses — the first head-to-head trial on this question. Critical for all-on-x decision-making in atrophic maxillae. Complements existing wiki pages on zygomatic implants such as [[implants/full-arch/malo-2008-extramaxillary-zygomatic-implants]] and [[implants/full-arch/aparicio-2014-zygomatic-implants-indications-techniques]] and the systematic review [[implants/full-arch/gracher-2021-full-arch-rehabilitation-in-patients]].

## Abstract

Purpose: No published research has compared patients' quality of life and satisfaction with fixed prostheses supported by zygomatic implants with those supported by all-on-four prostheses. The aim of this study was to evaluate patients' quality of life and satisfaction with fixed prostheses on zygomatic implants compared with the all-on-four concept.

Materials and Methods: A total of 80 patients with atrophic edentulous maxillae were randomized into two groups: Group 1 (rehabilitated with fixed prostheses supported by 2–4 zygomatic and 2–4 conventional implants in the anterior region) and Group 2 (fixed prostheses on four implants in the anterior region following an all-on-four concept). One year after placement of the definitive prostheses, patients completed OHIP-14 and satisfaction questionnaires.

Results: In all seven domains of the OHIP-14 and in the overall scores, a worse quality of life was found in Group 2 patients, with statistically significant differences between the two groups (p ≤ 0.05). Patients with zygomatic implants were more satisfied with their prostheses, with a statistically significant difference (p < 0.001).

Conclusions: According to the results of this study, rehabilitation of patients with edentulous atrophic maxillae with prostheses supported by zygomatic implants combined with anterior implants provided better patient quality of life and satisfaction than prostheses supported by four implants.

## Key Points

- RCT, 80 patients (40 per group), atrophic edentulous maxillae
- Group 1: 2–4 zygomatic + 2–4 anterior conventional implants (236 total: 139 ZI, 97 CI)
- Group 2: All-on-4 concept (4 conventional implants, 2 axial + 2 tilted 35–45°)
- 100% implant survival in both groups at ~19–20 months follow-up
- OHIP-14 total: ZI group 18.48 ± 3.42 vs All-on-4 30.43 ± 4.37 (p < 0.001) — lower = better
- All 7 OHIP-14 domains significantly favored ZI group (p ≤ 0.05)
- Satisfaction: 77.5% "extremely satisfied" in ZI group vs 0% in All-on-4 (p < 0.001)
- Peri-implantitis: ZI 12.2% (17/139) vs All-on-4 11.9% (19/160), p=0.113 (NS)
- Prosthetic complications: 0 in ZI group vs 3 fractures + 5 food traps in All-on-4 group
- ZAGA classification: 82.7% ZAGA 2, 17.3% ZAGA 1 (no ZAGA 0, 3, 4)
- Complications: 1 sinusitis (2.5%), 1 orbital cellulitis (2.5%) in ZI group; 0 in All-on-4
