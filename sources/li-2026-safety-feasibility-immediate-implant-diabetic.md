## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/drug/systemic-disease/wagner-2022-diabetes-mellitus-dental-implants-sr]] 및 [[wiki/drug/systemic-disease/al-ansari-2022-diabetes-mellitus-dental-implants-sr-ma]]는 임플란트 일반(지연식립 포함)과 당뇨의 관계를 다루지만, 즉시식립(IIP) 프로토콜에 특화된 당뇨 환자 비교 메타분석은 없었다. 본 SR+MA(10편, 1,350명, 1,623개 임플란트)는 그 공백을 메우며, 혈당조절 수준(양호/불량)별로 생존율·변연골소실(MBL)·탐침깊이(PD)·탐침출혈(BOP)을 층화 비교한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 10 studies (1,350 patients, 1,623 implants) comparing immediate implant placement (IIP) outcomes between diabetic (well- and poorly-controlled) and healthy patients, follow-up ≥6 months.

Implant survival rate showed no significant difference regardless of glycemic control (well-controlled RR=1.00, 95% CI 0.97–1.02, P=0.79; poorly-controlled RR=0.96, 95% CI 0.88–1.06, P=0.47), but marginal bone loss and bleeding-on-probing were significantly higher in diabetics, worsening with poorer glycemic control (poorly-controlled MBL MD=0.39mm, P<0.00001; BOP MD=0.24, P<0.00001).

IIP is clinically feasible in diabetic patients with survival comparable to healthy individuals, but poor glycemic control carries a measurably higher risk of peri-implant bone loss and inflammation, so strict perioperative glycemic management is required — notable heterogeneity among the 10 pooled studies limits certainty.

## 세줄요약

당뇨(양호·불량 혈당조절) 환자와 건강 대조군의 즉시식립(Immediate Implant Placement, IIP) 결과를 비교한 10편 체계적고찰+메타분석 (환자 1,350명, 임플란트 1,623개, 추적 ≥6개월).

임플란트 생존율은 혈당조절 수준과 무관하게 유의차 없음(양호 조절군 RR=1.00, P=0.79; 불량 조절군 RR=0.96, P=0.47)이나, 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)과 탐침출혈(Bleeding On Probing, BOP)은 당뇨군에서 유의하게 높고 혈당조절이 나쁠수록 악화(불량군 MBL MD=0.39mm, BOP MD=0.24, 모두 P<0.00001).

당뇨 환자에서도 IIP는 임상적으로 시행 가능하며 생존율은 건강군과 대등하지만, 혈당조절이 불량하면 골소실·염증 위험이 유의하게 높아 술 전후 엄격한 혈당관리가 필수 — 포함된 10편 간 이질성이 커 확실성에는 제한이 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First meta-analysis, to the authors' knowledge, focused specifically on IIP (not implants generally) comparing diabetic vs healthy patients.
- Stratifies by glycemic control level (well- vs poorly-controlled DM), showing SR is preserved but MBL/BOP risk scales with control quality.
- Pools implant survival, MBL, PD, and BOP as four distinct outcome axes rather than survival alone, giving a fuller peri-implant-health picture.
- Subgroup analysis by follow-up duration (≤6mo / 12mo / ≥24mo).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: SR+MA per PICO framework; NOS quality assessment; RevMan 5.4 for pooling.
- **Included studies**: 10 studies (from 1,337 initially retrieved → 900 after dedup → 84 full-text reviewed → 10 included); sample sizes 23–655 patients (168–922 implants); follow-up 6 months to 15 years.
- **Population**: diabetic (well-/poorly-controlled) vs healthy patients undergoing IIP for single/multiple teeth, age ≥18.
- **Exclusions**: case reports, reviews/meta-analyses, animal studies, n<10, incomplete data.
- **Outcomes**: implant survival rate (RR), MBL/PD/BOP (MD), each with 95% CI; random-effects model when I²≥50%.
- **Sensitivity/bias**: leave-one-out sensitivity analysis; funnel plot for publication bias.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Survival rate**: well-controlled DM vs healthy — RR=1.00 (95% CI 0.97–1.02, P=0.79); poorly-controlled DM vs healthy — RR=0.96 (95% CI 0.88–1.06, P=0.47). No significant difference in either stratum.
- **MBL**: well-controlled DM MD=0.08mm (95% CI 0.03–0.14, P=0.004) vs healthy; poorly-controlled DM MD=0.39mm (95% CI 0.25–0.53, P<0.00001) — both significantly worse than healthy, magnitude scaling with glycemic control quality.
- **PD**: well-controlled DM no significant difference (MD=0.17mm, P=0.09); poorly-controlled DM significantly higher (MD=0.62mm, 95% CI 0.38–0.86, P<0.00001).
- **BOP**: well-controlled DM significantly higher (MD=0.12, P<0.00001); poorly-controlled DM even higher (MD=0.24, 95% CI 0.23–0.24, P<0.00001).
- Notable heterogeneity across pooled studies flagged by authors as limiting the robustness of these estimates.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Substantial between-study heterogeneity (study design, implant systems, diabetes definitions/control thresholds not standardized).
- Only 10 studies met inclusion criteria; several outcome subgroups had few contributing studies.
- Follow-up durations vary widely (6 months to 15 years), pooled without full stratification in all analyses.
- Authors call for larger prospective studies standardizing glycemic-control thresholds and IIP protocols.

## 7. Glossary

- **IIP**: Immediate Implant Placement — implant inserted at the time of tooth extraction.
- **DM**: Diabetes Mellitus.
- **SR**: implant Survival Rate.
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Loss.
- **PD**: Probing Depth.
- **BOP**: Bleeding On Probing.
- **NOS**: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (quality assessment tool for non-randomized studies).
