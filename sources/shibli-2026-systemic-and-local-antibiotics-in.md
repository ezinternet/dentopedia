## Why Ingested
Ingested on request as part of a batch on evidence relevant to a local 2% minocycline HCl ointment product (Minoclin), because this network meta-analysis ranks local minocycline (as microspheres) among the best local antibiotic adjuncts while concluding that local antibiotics are less effective than systemic regimens. It bears directly on the conflict already recorded between [[implants/peri-implantitis/liu-2026-efficacy-of-adjunctive-antibiotics-compared]] (no bone-level benefit; local ≈ systemic for PPD) and [[implants/peri-implantitis/bai-2024-seven-nonsurgical-methods-peri-implantitis-network]] (systemic antibiotics rank first for marginal bone loss), and sits beside the local-minocycline RCT [[implants/peri-implantitis/lee-2026-minocycline-local-delivery-peri-implantitis-multicenter-rct]] and the earlier route comparison in [[implants/peri-implantitis/grusovin-2022-antibiotics-non-surgical-peri-implantitis-ma]].

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review with pairwise and network meta-analysis (five databases plus gray literature to March 2026; 21 studies, 1,718 implants; random-effects) of systemic and local antibiotics added to mechanical debridement in surgical and non-surgical peri-implantitis treatment, on PPD, CAL, BoP, PLI and MBL.

Systemic metronidazole + amoxicillin was most effective for PPD and MBL reduction, especially with surgical treatment; metronidazole alone was most effective with non-surgical treatment (notably CAL and MBL); among local agents, minocycline microspheres and local amoxicillin + metronidazole ranked best, and antibiotics overall improved clinical parameters versus debridement alone.

Authors conclude local antibiotics help but appear less effective than systemic regimens, with substantial microbiological variability by delivery method; abstract-only data, no reported effect sizes, certainty grading or harms, and the conclusion sits uneasily with guideline caution against routine systemic antibiotic use.

저자들은 국소 항생제도 지표를 개선하지만 전신 요법보다 덜 효과적으로 보인다고 결론지었고, 전달 방식에 따른 미생물학적 변이가 컸다; 초록만 확보되어 효과크기·근거확실성 등급·이상반응이 없으며, 상시 전신 항생제 사용을 경계하는 지침과는 결이 다르다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First network meta-analysis held in the wiki that ranks individual antibiotic agents and regimens (systemic metronidazole, systemic amoxicillin + metronidazole, local minocycline microspheres, local amoxicillin + metronidazole) rather than pooling "antibiotics" as one class.
- Separates the surgical and non-surgical treatment settings, which Liu 2026 did not stratify.
- Reports that systemic regimens outrank local regimens, and that local minocycline microspheres are among the best-performing local agents.
- Flags substantial microbiological variability by delivery route and intervention type.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review with pairwise and network meta-analyses, random-effects models.
- **Databases**: Five databases plus gray literature, searched through March 2026 (database names not given in the abstract).
- **n**: 21 studies, 1,718 implants (patient count and the split of RCTs vs non-randomized designs not given in the abstract).
- **Interventions**: Systemic and local antibiotics combined with mechanical debridement, in surgical and non-surgical peri-implantitis treatment.
- **Outcomes**: PPD, CAL, BoP, PLI, MBL; microbiological outcomes discussed qualitatively.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: no effect sizes, SUCRA values, confidence intervals, heterogeneity, network-consistency checks, risk of bias or GRADE certainty can be verified.
- "21 studies" may include non-randomized designs; the abstract does not say.
- No adverse-event or antimicrobial-resistance data are reported in the abstract, which matters for any systemic-antibiotic recommendation.
- Minocycline in this review is described as microspheres; a 2% minocycline HCl ointment is a different formulation, so the ranking cannot be transferred to an ointment product without caution.
- The conclusion (systemic regimens most effective) conflicts with Liu 2026's finding of no bone-level benefit and similar PPD gains by route, and with a surgical RCT (Hakkers 2026) that found no MBL benefit of amoxicillin + metronidazole.
- Authors call for well-designed trials with microbiological outcomes and standardized protocols.

## 6. Related Work
- Liu 2026 (SR+MA, 22 RCTs): no bone-level benefit and local ≈ systemic for PPD — conflicts with this NMA on bone outcome and route.
- Bai 2024 (NMA, 33 RCTs, non-surgical): systemic antibiotics ranked first for MBL — consistent with this NMA.
- Grusovin 2022 (SR+MA, 6 RCTs, non-surgical): systemic PPD gain larger than local — consistent on route.
- Antonoglou 2025 (SR+MA, surgical): short-term success and 0.37 mm bone gain with systemic antibiotics — consistent with the surgical-setting signal here.
- Hakkers 2026 (RCT, surgical): amoxicillin + metronidazole reduced only BoP; no MBL or PPD benefit.
- Lee 2026 (RCT): 2% minocycline HCl ointment reduced PD and BoP at 12 weeks versus placebo.
- Herrera 2023 (EFP S3 guideline): recommends against routine systemic antibiotics.

## 7. Glossary
- **Network meta-analysis (NMA)**: Meta-analysis combining direct and indirect comparisons to rank multiple treatments in one network.
- **Mechanical debridement (MD)**: Instrument-based removal of biofilm and deposits from the implant surface.
- **Open flap debridement (OFD)**: Surgical access flap for direct decontamination of the implant surface.
- **Minocycline microspheres**: Locally delivered, resorbable sustained-release minocycline particles placed subgingivally.
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss, measured radiographically around the implant.
