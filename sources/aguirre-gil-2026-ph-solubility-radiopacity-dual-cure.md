## Why Ingested

기존 [[resin-bonding/li-2025-curing-modes-dual-cure-dentin]]는 dual-cure resin cement의 curing mode(광중합 vs 자가중합)가 dentin 접착강도·내구성에 미치는 영향을 다뤘다. 본 논문(Aguirre-Gil 2026)은 같은 dual-cure resin cement군(NX3 Nexus는 Kerr의 Nexus 계열로 li-2025의 N3/Nexus 3와 동일 제품 라인)에 대해 접착강도가 아닌 물리화학적 성질(pH·solubility·radiopacity)을 비교해, 시멘트 선택 근거를 "접착 성능" 축에서 "임상 검출성·안전성" 축으로 보강·확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro experimental study (BMC Oral Health 2026, n=30 discs, 15 per cement) comparing NX3 Nexus (Kerr) vs Variolink N (Ivoclar Vivadent) dual-cure resin cements on three physicochemical properties: pH, solubility, and radiopacity.

Variolink N showed significantly higher radiopacity (2.00 vs 1.59 mmAl, p=0.026) attributable to ytterbium trifluoride (Z=70) vs NX3's barium-glass fillers (Z=56); NX3 Nexus showed significantly higher pH (5.84 vs 5.32, p=0.008); solubility was not significantly different (p=0.841) and both cements met ISO 4049:2019 thresholds.

Variolink N provides better radiographic detectability; NX3 Nexus has a slightly more neutral pH (lower sensitivity/pulpal-inflammation risk); both are clinically safe for solubility — in-vitro disc model limits extrapolation to dynamic intraoral conditions.

## 세줄요약

시험관내 실험 연구 (BMC Oral Health 2026, n=30 디스크, 시멘트당 15개): NX3 Nexus(Kerr) vs Variolink N(Ivoclar Vivadent) dual-cure 레진시멘트의 pH·용해도·방사선불투과도 비교.

Variolink N이 방사선불투과도 유의하게 높음(2.00 vs 1.59 mmAl, p=0.026) — ytterbium trifluoride(Z=70) vs 바륨유리 충전재(Z=56) 차이; NX3 Nexus가 pH 유의하게 높음(5.84 vs 5.32, p=0.008); 용해도는 유의차 없음(p=0.841), 두 시멘트 모두 ISO 4049:2019 충족.

Variolink N은 방사선 검출성 우수; NX3 Nexus는 pH가 더 중성(치수 자극 위험 감소); 용해도는 두 제품 모두 안전 — 생체내 동적 조건에서의 적용은 추가 연구 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct head-to-head in-vitro comparison of two widely used dual-cure resin cements (NX3 Nexus, Variolink N) across three physicochemical properties simultaneously: pH, solubility, and radiopacity.
- Provides a mechanistic explanation for radiopacity differences tied to radiopacifier chemistry (ytterbium trifluoride in Variolink N vs barium-glass fillers in NX3 Nexus) and atomic number (Z) of the incorporated element.
- Confirms both cements remain within ISO 4049:2019 solubility/sorption thresholds despite compositional differences.
- Frames pH findings clinically: both cements remain above the pH=3 threshold associated with pulpal inflammation/postoperative sensitivity, and low pH may inhibit chemical-cure kinetics.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Sample size**: Pilot study (n=5/cement) informed a G*Power 3.1.9.7 calculation (α=0.05, power=0.80, effect size=10.47) → total n=30 discs, 15 per cement, then subdivided into 3 subgroups of n=5 per test (pH, solubility, radiopacity) per cement.

**Materials**:
- NX3 Nexus (Kerr Corporation, Orange, USA) — dual-cure; composition: HEMA, PTU, CHPO, uncured methacrylate ester monomers, titanium dioxide, pigments. Filler loading reported up to ~70 wt% (~55 vol%), barium-glass radiopaque fillers.
- Variolink N (Ivoclar Vivadent AG, Schaan, Liechtenstein) — dual-cure; base: barium glass filler + mixed oxide, dimethacrylates (BisGMA, UDMA, TEGDMA), ytterbium trifluoride, initiators/stabilizers, pigments; catalyst paste similarly formulated.

**Sample preparation**: 8 mm diameter × 1 mm thick metal-mold discs, manufacturer protocol, top surface pressed with slide plate to remove excess. Light-cured with Bluephase N LED unit (Ivoclar Vivadent), 10-mm tip, 1200 mW/cm² irradiance, 20 s at ~1 mm distance, tip perpendicular. Stored 37°C for 24 h post-cure.

**Radiopacity (ISO 4049)**: PSPIX dental imaging plate (27×54 mm) + PSPIX scanner (Acteon Imaging); 10-step aluminum wedge (1–10 mm) as reference. Exposure: 70 kVp, 8 mA (X-MindAC, Acteon Group), 0.125 s, object-to-focus distance 120 mm. Greyscale analyzed in Adobe Photoshop CC at 5 locations/sample, averaged, converted to mmAl.

**pH**: Cements mixed 20 s on acetate paper block, spread, measured with PH55 digital pH meter (Milwaukee Electronics Kft).

**Solubility**: Discs desiccated then incubated at 37°C; initial mass (M1) via analytical balance (Mettler Toledo, 0.001 g precision); immersed individually in 25 mL distilled water, incubated 37°C × 7 days; dried and re-weighed (M2); solubility = (M1−M2)/V (µg/mm³).

**Statistics**: SPSS v28.0. Shapiro-Wilk normality + Levene homoscedasticity checked. Radiopacity: independent-samples Student's t-test (normal, homogeneous variance). pH and solubility: Mann-Whitney U test (non-normal distributions). Significance threshold p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Test | Cement | n | Mean | SD | Median | IQR | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Radiopacity (mmAl) | NX3 Nexus | 5 | 1.59 | 0.30 | 1.70 | 0.48 | 0.026* |
| Radiopacity (mmAl) | Variolink N | 5 | 2.00 | 0.15 | 2.00 | 0.21 | |
| Solubility (µg/mm³) | NX3 Nexus | 5 | 0.46 | 1.66 | -1.60 | 2.51 | 0.841 |
| Solubility (µg/mm³) | Variolink N | 5 | -0.46 | 0.84 | -1.51 | 0.58 | |
| pH | NX3 Nexus | 5 | 5.84 | 0.09 | 5.73 | 5.95 | 0.008* |
| pH | Variolink N | 5 | 5.32 | 0.04 | 5.26 | 5.38 | |

(*significant, p<0.05)

- Radiopacity: Variolink N significantly higher than NX3 Nexus (p=0.026), attributed to ytterbium trifluoride (Yb, Z=70) vs NX3's barium-glass fillers (Ba, Z=56); higher atomic number → higher X-ray attenuation. Both values below several prior literature reports, attributed to composition, X-ray exposure parameters, sensor type, and equipment differences.
- Solubility: no significant difference (p=0.841); average reported as 1.88 µg/mm³ (NX3 Nexus) and 4.06 µg/mm³ (Variolink N) in the discussion (sorption framing) vs the disc-level means in Table 2. Both cements remained below ISO 4049:2019 thresholds (solubility ≤7.5 µg/mm³, sorption ≤40 µg/mm³). Negative solubility values (Variolink N) attributed to net water sorption exceeding leachable-component mass loss.
- pH: NX3 Nexus significantly higher (more neutral) than Variolink N (p=0.008); both remained above pH=3, the threshold associated with pulpal inflammation and postoperative sensitivity risk.
- Null hypothesis (no differences among the three properties) was partially rejected — differences found for pH and radiopacity, not solubility.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Stated by authors:
- Could not simulate dynamic pH changes driven by diet/salivary composition and flow in vivo.
- In-vitro solubility may be lower than in-vivo values; high salivary enzymatic activity can accelerate polymer-matrix degradation.
- Radiographic radiopacity assessment in vivo may be influenced by oral fluids, overlying soft tissue, and adjacent dental structures — not captured by the in-vitro disc model.
- Only initial (post-cure) pH was measured; longitudinal pH change after acid neutralization and network maturation (expected to trend toward neutral/mildly alkaline, reducing water uptake/hydrolytic susceptibility) was not evaluated.
- Degree of conversion and mechanical properties (bond strength, compressive strength) were not assessed — recommended for future work.
- Small subgroup size (n=5 per test per cement), though powered via G*Power a priori.

## 6. Related Work

Findings on radiopacity consistent with Gomes et al. and Hosney et al. (Variolink-family cements with ytterbium trifluoride showing higher radiopacity). Solubility results contrast with Patroi et al. and Mese et al., attributed to methodological differences (sample-size determination, repeated testing on same disc, surface wear from handling). Builds on ISO 4049 standard methodology for radiopacity/solubility testing.

## 7. Glossary

- **Dual-cure resin cement**: Luting material combining light-initiated and self/chemical-cure polymerization mechanisms, used when light cannot fully penetrate to the adhesive interface (e.g., under crowns, deep margins).
- **Radiopacity (mmAl)**: X-ray attenuation of a material expressed as equivalent thickness of aluminum; higher mmAl = greater radiographic contrast against tooth structure.
- **Solubility (µg/mm³)**: Mass loss per unit volume after water immersion, reflecting leaching of unreacted/soluble monomer constituents.
- **Ytterbium trifluoride (YbF₃)**: High-atomic-number (Z=70) radiopacifying filler used in some resin cements (e.g., Variolink family), contributing to elevated radiopacity.
- **ISO 4049**: International standard for polymer-based restorative dental materials, specifying acceptable solubility/sorption/radiopacity test methods and thresholds.
- **CRIS Guidelines**: Checklist for Reporting In-vitro Studies, a reporting-quality framework for dental in-vitro research.
- **mmAl**: Millimeters of aluminum equivalent — the standard unit for expressing dental material radiopacity via greyscale-to-aluminum-wedge calibration.
