## 2. Key Contributions

- Synthesizes multiple pathomechanisms of COVID-19 taste disorder in a single framework
- Highlights ACE2/TMPRSS2 high expression in taste buds as a direct viral entry route
- Proposes cytokine storm → apoptosis → impaired taste bud stem cell renewal as a secondary damage pathway
- Identifies Angiotensin II accumulation from local RAS imbalance as an amplifying factor
- Describes SARS-CoV-2 neurotropism via olfactory bulb as an additional pathway affecting taste
- Reviews adjunct factors: host zinc deficiency, genetic susceptibility, sialic acid, neurotransmitters
- Discusses stem cell-based regeneration and tongue organoids as future therapeutic directions

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Narrative (non-systematic) review
- Literature scope: Studies on COVID-19 taste pathogenesis and treatments up to publication date (2023-09-20)
- Full text not retrieved; summary based on abstract

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

*Abstract-only*

- Taste dysfunction identified as a common early-stage COVID-19 symptom, persisting weeks or permanently in some cases
- Entry receptors (ACE2/TMPRSS2) highly expressed in taste buds — cytocidal effect intensified
- Cytokine storm causes secondary damage; interferons and proinflammatory cytokines trigger apoptosis and disrupt taste bud stem cell renewal
- Ang II accumulation from unbalanced RAS amplifies immune response
- SARS-CoV-2 neurotropic: invades brain via olfactory bulb
- Zinc deficiency, genetic susceptibility, sialic acid, neurotransmitters also implicated
- Stem cell differentiation stimulated by epithelial- and neural-derived exogenous factors as regeneration strategy; tongue organoids emerging as functional taste bud models

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review design — no systematic search, no GRADE assessment
- Mechanism of taste disorder described as "poorly studied" due to subjectivity and complexity
- Future directions: stem cell-based regeneration, tongue organoids, clinical trials for post-infectious dysgeusia treatments

## 7. Glossary

- **ACE2 (Angiotensin-Converting Enzyme 2)**: Primary SARS-CoV-2 entry receptor, highly expressed in taste bud cells
- **TMPRSS2 (Transmembrane Serine Protease 2)**: Co-receptor facilitating SARS-CoV-2 spike protein priming
- **RAS (Renin-Angiotensin System)**: Hormonal cascade regulating blood pressure; COVID-19 disrupts local RAS balance, increasing Ang II
- **Dysgeusia (미각이상, Dysgeusia)**: Distorted or abnormal taste perception
- **Ageusia (미각소실, Ageusia)**: Complete loss of taste
- **Hypogeusia (미각감소, Hypogeusia)**: Reduced taste sensitivity
- **Neurotropism (신경친화성, Neurotropism)**: Tendency of a pathogen to infect and damage neural tissue
- **Tongue organoids (혀 오가노이드, Tongue organoids)**: Lab-grown 3D miniature tongue structures used for taste bud research
