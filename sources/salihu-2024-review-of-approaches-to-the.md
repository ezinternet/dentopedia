## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (no stated search strategy, no formal appraisal; Med Sci Monit 2024) of management of vertical root fracture (VRF) with a tooth-preservation focus: root amputation/resection/hemisection, laser-mediated fusion, calcium hydroxide dressing, fracture-line bonding with or without intentional replantation, and extraction.

Cited figures are VRF incidence of 4-32% in root-canal-treated teeth, 80% of VRFs detected within 2 years of first symptoms, and dentin weakening of 23-43.9% after calcium hydroxide filling (Rosenberg 2006); preservation evidence consists of case reports (e.g., 6 teeth bonded with 4-META/MMA-TBB resin and replanted; a 12-month incisor case) and the review gives no pooled success rates.

Treatment is reasonable for incomplete fractures with intact periodontium in a cooperative patient, whereas extraction suits split-tooth-like fractures, severe bone loss, chronic infection or medically compromised patients; the authors themselves call for standardized protocols and long-term efficacy studies.

## 세줄요약

내러티브 리뷰 (검색 전략·비판적 평가 미기재; Med Sci Monit 2024) — 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)의 치아 보존 중심 관리: 치근 절단·절제·반절 (Root Amputation/Resection/Hemisection), 레이저 융합, 수산화칼슘 (Calcium Hydroxide) 드레싱, 파절선 접착 ± 의도적 재식립 (Intentional Replantation), 발치.

인용 수치는 근관치료치 VRF 발생률 4-32%, VRF의 80%가 초기 증상 후 2년 내 발견, 수산화칼슘 근관충전 후 상아질 약화 23-43.9% (Rosenberg 2006); 보존 근거는 증례 수준 (예: 4-META/MMA-TBB 레진으로 접착 후 재식립한 6개 치아, 12개월 추적 전치부 1례)이며 통합 성공률은 제시하지 않음.

불완전 파절·치주 건강·협조적 환자에서 치료를 고려하고, 분할치 (Split Tooth) 양상·심한 골소실·만성 감염·전신질환 환자에서는 발치가 적합; 저자 스스로 표준 프로토콜과 장기 효능 연구가 필요하다고 명시.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Tooth-preservation-oriented walk-through of five VRF management options with advantages/disadvantages (Table 1 in the paper).
- Frames the two treatment goals: eliminating microbial invasion at the fracture line and preventing periodontal tissue loss.
- Treat/extract decision criteria in the Conclusions: intact periodontal status + incomplete fracture + cooperative patient favours treatment; split-tooth-like fracture, bone destruction, severe chronic infection, or systemic compromise favours extraction.
- Argues intentional replantation should be tried first because it is more economical than an implant and an implant remains possible if replantation fails.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Topic | Statement in the paper |
|---|---|
| VRF incidence in root-canal-treated teeth | 4% to 32% |
| Timing of detection | 80% of VRFs detected within 2 years after onset of initial symptoms |
| Signs | sinus tract, increased probing depth, swelling/abscess, percussion tenderness |
| Imaging | CBCT described as more accurate than periapical radiography for VRF detection (3D, less noise) |
| Resection/hemisection | Taschieri protocol: resect fractured apical segment, bur-prepare residual groove, seal with MTA; hemisection case (lower right first molar mesial root) successful at 3 years; resected root without root-end filling may fracture under load |
| CO2 laser | fusion works best with thin, well-approximated fractures, full-coverage cast crown, perpendicular access; pulp heat acceptable if remaining dentin >= 1 mm; 1996 study on 81 single-rooted teeth: Nd:YAG ineffective for fusion (surface ablation), CO2 more effective |
| Calcium hydroxide | Stewart 1988: successful VRF treatment over 9-12 months; Rosenberg 2006: dentin weakened 23% to 43.9%; more recent studies: no increased fracture predisposition |
| Fracture-line bonding | Materials: 4-META/MMA-TBB resin, dual-cure resin, MTA, Biodentine; intracanal bonding cannot reach the whole fracture line (residual bacteria); flap-only external repair needs bone removal; extraction + extraoral bonding + replantation gives 3D access |
| Replantation reports | 6 teeth (1 incisor, 1 canine, 1 premolar, 3 molars) bonded with 4-META/MMA-TBB and replanted ("substantial success"); upper left central incisor (Moradi Majd): 18 min procedure, 10-day semirigid splint, asymptomatic at 12 months with reduced radiolucency; root resorption remains a persistent problem |
| Extraction | Treatment of choice for complete fractures and intraosseous fractures with periodontal pain; deep dehiscence -> extraction + implant |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No search strategy, selection criteria, or quality appraisal; selective narrative citation.
- Success claims rest on single case reports and small series with short follow-up (12-36 months); no pooled survival data.
- Laser and calcium hydroxide sections draw on 1980s-1990s and in vitro studies.
- Authors state standardized protocols and long-term efficacy research are still needed.

## 7. Glossary

- **VRF (Vertical Root Fracture)**: fracture extending along the long axis of the root; may be partial or complete.
- **4-META/MMA-TBB resin**: 4-methacryloxyethyl trimellitate anhydride in methyl methacrylate initiated by tri-n-butyl borane; adhesive resin cement used to bond fracture fragments.
- **Intentional replantation**: deliberate extraction, extraoral treatment and re-insertion of the tooth into its socket.
- **Hemisection / root resection**: surgical division or removal of one root of a multi-rooted tooth while retaining the rest.
- **MTA (Mineral Trioxide Aggregate)**: bioceramic sealing material used for root-end/fracture-groove sealing.
