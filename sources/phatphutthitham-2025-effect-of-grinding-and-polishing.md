## Why Ingested

Clinically important in-vitro study evaluating two chairside grinding and polishing protocols on 5Y-PSZ (high-translucency zirconia), comparing EVE diamond-stone burs (Protocol I) versus Jota superfine diamond bur + polishing kit (Protocol II). Directly relevant to clinical occlusal adjustment decisions for high-translucency zirconia restorations.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/phatphutthitham-2025-effect-of-grinding-and-polishing]]

## Three-line Summary

Grinding and polishing of 5Y-PSZ with either protocol significantly reduced surface roughness below the 0.20 µm clinical threshold after high-gloss finishing. Protocol II (Jota superfine diamond bur + sequential diamond-impregnated silicone burs) produced significantly smoother final surfaces than Protocol I (EVE DIASYNT stone bur) in Lava Esthetic, while Cercon xt showed comparable results between protocols. Biaxial flexural strength was significantly reduced only in Lava Esthetic with Protocol I (EVE); no phase transformation (monoclinic increase) was detected in any group.

## 세줄요약

5Y-PSZ(고투명 지르코니아) 두 제품(Lava Esthetic, Cercon xt)에 두 가지 연삭·연마 프로토콜을 적용한 결과, 최종 고광택 연마 후 표면조도(Ra)는 임상 허용 기준(0.20 µm) 이하로 감소하였다. 프로토콜 II(Jota 초미세 다이아몬드 버 + 순차적 실리콘 버)는 Lava Esthetic에서 프로토콜 I(EVE DIASYNT 석재 버)보다 유의하게 낮은 최종 표면조도를 나타냈다. 굴곡강도는 Lava Esthetic에서 프로토콜 I 적용 시에만 유의하게 감소하였으며(508→424 MPa), 모든 군에서 단사정상(monoclinic) 증가에 의한 상전환은 관찰되지 않았다.

## Document Information

- **Study type**: In vitro laboratory study
- **Evidence level**: in-vitro
- **Materials**: Lava Esthetic 5Y-PSZ (3M ESPE), Cercon xt 5Y-PSZ (DeguDent)
- **Protocols**:
  - Protocol I: EVE DIASYNT PLUS HP (stone bur, 150–200 µm diamond) → EVE DIACERA RA medium (15–20 µm) → EVE DIACERA RA fine (4–8 µm), low-speed 10,000 rpm, no coolant
  - Protocol II: Jota Z850.FG superfine diamond bur (38–45 µm, high-speed 160,000 rpm, with coolant) → Jota ZIR Gloss medium (15–20 µm) → Jota ZIR Gloss fine (4–8 µm), 10,000 rpm
- **Sample size**: 66 specimens (11 per group), disk-shaped 15 mm diameter × 1 mm thick, ISO 6872:2015
- **Measurements**: Contact profilometry (Ra), biaxial flexural strength (MPa), SEM, XRD (Rietveld refinement)
- **Statistical analysis**: One-way ANOVA + Bonferroni post-hoc, repeated measures ANOVA

## Key Contributions

1. First study to use a custom polishing machine to standardize direction, speed, and applied force (0.98 N for grinding, 2 N for polishing) for 5Y-PSZ grinding and polishing protocols
2. Demonstrated that Protocol II (high-speed superfine diamond bur with coolant) produces smoother surfaces than Protocol I (stone bur without coolant) for Lava Esthetic
3. Showed that biaxial flexural strength reduction after polishing is material-dependent: Lava Esthetic (no hafnium oxide) is more susceptible than Cercon xt (contains HfO₂, Al₂O₃, SiO₂)
4. Confirmed absence of significant monoclinic phase transformation after polishing in 5Y-PSZ, though cubic→tetragonal reversal was noted on XRD
5. Final Ra values after high-gloss polishing were within clinically acceptable range (< 0.20 µm) for all groups except LE (0.18 µm, borderline)

## Methodology

**Specimen fabrication**: CAD/CAM milling (inLab MC X5) of partially sintered blanks; sintering at 1,500°C/2h, 20°C/min ramp; baseline surface preparation with 320–2,000 grit SiC papers on polishing machine.

**Simulated clinical scenario**: Protruding 3 mm × 0.5 mm platform on disk center to simulate high-contact occlusal area requiring chairside adjustment.

**Custom polishing machine**: Controlled sweeping motion (5 mm distance, 1 mm/s), standardized applied forces (0.98 N grinding, 2 N polishing).

**Surface roughness measurement**: Talyscan 150 contact profilometer, 5-µm diamond stylus, 2×2 mm² area, 5 parallel lines; recorded at Ra0 (baseline), Ra1 (after grinding), Ra2 (after polishing), Ra3 (after high-gloss).

**Biaxial flexural strength**: Universal testing machine, 3-ball support (12 mm diameter), 1 mm loading tip, 0.5 mm/min crosshead speed.

**Phase analysis**: XRD (Bruker D8, Cu-Kα, 20–40° 2θ), Rietveld refinement (TOPAS V3.0).

## Key Results

### Surface Roughness (µm)

| Group | Ra0 (baseline) | Ra1 (after grind) | Ra2 (after polish) | Ra3 (high-gloss) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LC (Lava control) | 0.12 | — | — | — |
| LE (Lava + EVE) | 0.12 | 1.62 | 0.21 | 0.18 |
| LJ (Lava + Jota) | 0.12 | 0.74 | 0.13 | 0.14 |
| CC (Cercon control) | 0.12 | — | — | — |
| CE (Cercon + EVE) | 0.12 | 0.38 | 0.21 | 0.18 |
| CJ (Cercon + Jota) | 0.12 | 0.38 | 0.18 | 0.13 |

- Protocol I (EVE) produced significantly higher Ra1 than Protocol II (Jota) in Lava Esthetic (1.62 vs 0.74 µm, p<0.05)
- Both protocols returned to < 0.20 µm after high-gloss polishing
- CJ was the only group with Ra3 comparable to baseline (p > 0.05)

### Biaxial Flexural Strength (MPa)

| Group | Mean ± SD |
|---|---|
| LC (control) | 508.16 ± 34.01 |
| LE (EVE) | 423.77 ± 77.96* |
| LJ (Jota) | 486.69 ± 70.58 |
| CC (control) | 532.37 ± 15.73 |
| CE (EVE) | 513.82 ± 21.14 |
| CJ (Jota) | 516.40 ± 37.18 |

*Significantly lower than LC (p < 0.05).

### XRD Phase Composition (%)

| Group | Tetragonal | Monoclinic | Cubic |
|---|---|---|---|
| LC | 69.82 | 0.75 | 29.43 |
| LE | 92.74 | 1.13 | 6.13 |
| LJ | 89.79 | 1.00 | 9.21 |
| CC | 64.92 | 0.73 | 34.35 |
| CE | 91.32 | 1.08 | 7.60 |
| CJ | 93.31 | 0.78 | 5.91 |

- Grinding/polishing caused cubic→tetragonal shift in both materials
- No significant monoclinic increase (phase transformation toughening absent, as expected for 5Y-PSZ)

## Limitations

- Only two commercial 5Y-PSZ products evaluated; multilayer zirconia not included
- Small specimen numbers insufficient for Weibull statistical analysis (ISO 6872:2015 requirement)
- In vitro custom polishing machine conditions may not fully replicate clinical handpiece ergonomics
- Phase analysis limited to XRD; rhombohedral phase detection may require higher-resolution methods
- Translucency change after cubic→tetragonal shift was noted qualitatively but not quantified

## Glossary

- **5Y-PSZ**: 5 mol% yttria-partially stabilized zirconia; high-translucency generation with mixed cubic/tetragonal structure
- **Ra**: Arithmetic mean surface roughness (µm)
- **Biaxial flexural strength**: Piston-on-3-balls test measuring fracture resistance of disk specimens (MPa)
- **Phase transformation toughening**: Stress-induced tetragonal→monoclinic transformation that arrests crack propagation; reduced in 5Y-PSZ due to lower tetragonal fraction
- **Rietveld refinement**: Quantitative XRD phase analysis method fitting diffraction pattern profiles
- **EVE DIASYNT PLUS HP**: Organic synthetic stone bur with 150–200 µm diamond particle size for slow-speed grinding
