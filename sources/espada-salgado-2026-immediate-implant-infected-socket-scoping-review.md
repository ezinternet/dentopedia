## Why Ingested

기존 [[immediate-implant/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, 6개 대조군 연구만 최종 포함)와 [[immediate-implant/munoz-camara-2020-immediate-implants-acute-periapical-infected]]가 "철저한 소파+항생제 동반 시 감염 부위 즉시식립이 안전"이라는 낙관적 결론을 내린 데 비해, 본 스코핑 리뷰는 같은 저널(Medicina Oral)에서 2015–2025년 24편(케이스 리포트~RCT 혼재)을 매핑해 "표준화된 프로토콜을 권고할 근거는 아직 없다"는 더 신중한 결론을 낸다 — 기존 두 페이지의 낙관적 톤에 대한 근거 수준 관점의 보정추가.

## Three-line Summary

Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR, JBI methodology; 72 records screened, 24 clinical studies 2015–2025 included) mapping evidence on immediate implant placement (IIP) in infected extraction sockets.

Most included studies were case reports/series or non-comparative observational designs; atraumatic extraction, meticulous debridement, and primary stability were the consistently reported procedural keys, with favorable survival/MBL/esthetics reported overall.

Evidence is feasible-suggestive but heterogeneous and largely non-comparative — no standardized clinical protocol for infected-socket IIP can currently be recommended; well-designed comparative studies are still needed.

## 세줄요약

스코핑 리뷰 (PRISMA-ScR/JBI 방법론; 72편 스크리닝, 2015–2025년 24편 임상연구 포함): 감염 발치와 즉시식립(IIP) 근거를 매핑.

포함 연구 대부분이 케이스 리포트/시리즈 또는 비교군 없는 관찰연구; 무외상 발치·철저한 소파·초기 안정성 확보가 공통 핵심 술식으로 보고되었고, 전반적으로 생존율·변연골·심미 결과는 양호.

근거는 "가능성 시사" 수준이나 이질적이고 비교군이 부족 — 감염 소켓 IIP의 표준화된 임상 프로토콜을 현재로서는 권고할 수 없음; 잘 설계된 비교 연구가 여전히 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First scoping review (JBI/PRISMA-ScR) specifically mapping the infected-extraction-socket IIP literature, rather than pooling effect sizes
- Documents the adjunctive-intervention landscape: systemic antibiotics, CHX decontamination, bone grafts, barrier membranes, platelet concentrates (PRF/CGF), laser therapy, vestibular socket techniques, immediate provisionalization
- Explicitly separates endodontic/periapical-lesion sockets (most-studied) from periodontal and mixed-etiology infected sockets (under-studied)
- Notes study-design skew toward case reports/series and non-comparative designs — a methodological-quality caveat that pooled SR+MA papers (e.g., Pranckeviciene 2024) do not surface as prominently

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- JBI scoping-review methodology, PRISMA-ScR reporting
- Databases: Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, PubMed/MEDLINE, LILACS (via BVS), SciELO, ClinicalTrials.gov, searched through 2026-03-17
- 72 records after de-duplication → independent dual-reviewer screening → 24 clinical studies (2015–2025) met inclusion criteria
- Included designs: case reports, case series, retrospective studies, prospective cohorts, one split-mouth study, one RCT

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Consistently reported procedural keys across studies: atraumatic extraction, meticulous socket debridement, achievement of adequate primary stability
- Adjunctive measures catalogued (non-pooled, descriptive only — no meta-analysis performed): systemic antibiotics, CHX irrigation, bone grafts, barrier membranes, PRF/platelet concentrates, laser decontamination, vestibular socket technique, immediate provisionalization
- Outcomes generally favorable (implant survival, marginal bone stability, esthetics) but not quantitatively pooled due to heterogeneity
- No standardized infection-severity classification or decontamination protocol identified across the 24 studies

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Scoping review by design does not perform meta-analysis or formal risk-of-bias grading of pooled effect — cannot resolve whether infected-socket IIP survival is statistically equivalent to non-infected (that question is Pranckeviciene 2024's domain)
- Evidence base dominated by case reports/series and non-comparative studies — high risk of publication bias toward favorable outcomes
- Heterogeneous infection definitions (acute vs chronic, endodontic vs periodontal vs mixed) across included studies limit cross-study comparison
- Authors explicitly call for well-designed comparative studies with standardized reporting
