## Why Ingested

The wiki holds long-term peri-implantitis surgery evidence only as a pooled review ([[implants/peri-implantitis/monje-2025-long-term-surgical-treatment-outcomes]], 58.6% mean disease resolution at ≥5 years) and a 7-year RCT with low success ([[implants/peri-implantitis/isler-2025-long-term-treatment-outcomes-peri-implantitis]], 23-31%). This 10-year single-arm prospective cohort adds a primary long-term data set for a specific protocol (EDTA decontamination + DBBMC/EMD/doxycycline, crater-like defects, 4-6-monthly supportive care) that lands at 58% composite success, matching the pooled figure while using a much stricter patient selection. Full text came from PMC (pubmed-text).

## Three-line Summary

Prospective single-arm cohort (30 patients, one peri-implantitis implant each, 29 completed 10 years; single specialist practice, Sydney) treated with surgical reconstruction (24% EDTA decontamination + deproteinised bovine bone mineral with 10% collagen (DBBMC) + enamel matrix derivative (EMD) + doxycycline, coronally advanced flap) and individualised 4-6-monthly supportive peri-implant therapy (SPIT).

Implant survival was 29/30 (96.6%); probing depth fell from 8.90 ± 1.90 mm to 3.06 ± 0.98 mm and radiographic bone loss from 57% to 12.86% (stable from year 2); 12 implants needed azithromycin for recurrent suppuration; only 58.6% (17/29) met the composite successful-treatment-outcome criterion (STOC) at 10 years.

Survival is high but "success" is about 60% even in healthy non-smokers with crater-like defects and strict maintenance; no control arm, one operator, and a small cohort mean the contribution of each component (EMD, doxycycline, DBBMC, SPIT) cannot be separated.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 10-year follow-up (3-year results published earlier by the same group) of a uniform surgical protocol for peri-implantitis with explicit crater-like/circumferential defect selection.
- Reports survival (96.6%) separately from composite success (58.6%), showing how far apart the two endpoints sit.
- Documents that recurrent suppuration was handled non-surgically (debridement + azithromycin) and that no re-surgery was needed during 10 years.
- Provides a small, non-randomised CTG subgroup (8 implants) with higher keratinised tissue and less recession.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: prospective single-arm cohort, 30 consecutive patients referred 2009-2012; no concurrent control.
- **Inclusion**: systemically healthy non-smokers, full-mouth plaque and bleeding scores < 20%, no residual bleeding pockets > 4 mm after pre-surgical periodontal therapy, one affected implant with crater-like or circumferential defect, PD ≥ 6 mm with BOP/suppuration, ≥ 20% radiographic bone loss relative to implant length, in function ≥ 2 years. Excluded: uncontrolled diabetes, bisphosphonates, pregnancy/lactation, mobile implants, malpositioned implants.
- **Surgery**: flap, granulation tissue removal, ultrasonic debridement, 24% EDTA 2 min, DBBMC (Bio-Oss Collagen) mixed with 0.35 mL EMD plus one 100 mg doxycycline capsule, coronally advanced flap closure; connective tissue graft (CTG) added when keratinised tissue < 1 mm or anterior maxilla (6 maxillary anterior + 2 mandibular posterior implants). Resorbable sutures only.
- **SPIT**: mostly every 4-6 months; ultrasonic debridement of whole mouth; curettage if BOP; azithromycin 500 mg x 3 days if suppuration.
- **Measures**: PD, recession, keratinised tissue, periapical radiographs at baseline, 2, 3, 5, 10 years by a calibrated oral health therapist not involved in surgery (x3.5 magnification; calibration within 0.5 mm on 5 patients).
- **STOC at 10 y**: PD < 5 mm, no further bone loss > 10% vs baseline, no BOP/suppuration, recession < 0.5 mm (anterior) / < 1.5 mm (posterior).
- **Statistics**: paired t-tests between time points, p < 0.01 as significance threshold; no multivariable or survival modelling.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Cohort**: mean age 44.9 ± 11 y, 64% female; sites: mandibular posterior 40%, maxillary anterior 33.3%, maxillary posterior 16.6%, mandibular anterior 10%; implants Branemark TiUnite 46.7%, Astra Tech 26.7%, Straumann 10%, others 16.7%.
- **Survival**: 29/30 (96.66%); one mandibular posterior implant lost osseointegration by year 8 and was removed.
- **Table 2 (baseline / 2 y / 3 y / 5 y / 10 y)**: bone loss 6.92 ± 1.26 / 2.62 ± 0.80 / 2.60 ± 0.73 / 2.73 ± 0.67 / 2.78 ± 0.56 mm (57 / 14.8 / 14.5 / 12.3 / 12.86%); PD 8.90 ± 1.90 / 3.50 / 3.5 / 3.20 / 3.06 ± 0.98 mm; mid-buccal recession 0.16 / 0.15 / 0.22 / 0.20 / 0.48 ± 0.96 mm (NS); keratinised tissue 1.79 / 1.90 / 1.93 / 1.50 / 1.48 mm. Bone loss and PD differed from baseline (p < 0.01); 2/3/5/10-year values did not differ from each other.
- **Inflammation**: 100% BOP and suppuration at baseline; FMBS 70% to 15-18% across follow-up; suppuration recurred in 12 implants (6 maxillary, 6 mandibular posterior), resolving after debridement + azithromycin (2-week reassessment).
- **STOC at 10 y**: success 58.62% (17/29), failure 41.37% (12/29): persistent BOP 5, further bone loss 4, anterior recession 2, posterior recession 1, PD > 5 mm 0. Derived (not in paper): counting the removed implant as a failure gives 17/30 = 56.7%.
- **CTG subgroup (non-randomised, 8 vs 21)**: keratinised tissue 1.93 ± 0.17 vs 1.34 ± 0.39 mm (p = 0.0003); recession 0.00 vs 0.56 ± 0.50 mm (p = 0.0028); PD and bone loss similar; all CTG implants met STOC (per authors).
- No patient required re-surgery in 10 years.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No concurrent control; EMD, doxycycline, DBBMC, EDTA and SPIT cannot be separated (authors).
- Small, single-institution cohort; selection restricted to systemically healthy non-smokers with crater-like defects, so generalisation to smokers, diabetics or non-contained defects is not supported (authors; selection also acknowledged in Discussion as a driver of the good results).
- Surgeon is also first author and analyst; examiner was a separate therapist but masking is not stated.
- Paired t-tests on repeated measures with p < 0.01 only; no adjustment for implant surface, defect configuration or compliance, and no time-to-event analysis.
- The 29-implant denominator for STOC excludes the lost implant (see ITT recalculation above).
- CTG comparison is by clinical indication (thin tissue/anterior maxilla), so confounded; p-values are post hoc.
- Internal inconsistencies noted on reading: the Discussion quotes "negligible soft-tissue recession (0.16 ± 0.14)" for the 5-year comparison although that is the baseline value (5-year value is 0.20 ± 0.42); recession threshold is "<" in Methods but "≤" in Results; Results text says 40% failed STOC versus 41.37% in Table 3; non-CTG recession 0.56 (text) vs 0.59 (table). None changes the conclusions.
- The abstract states inclusion PD "> 6 mm" while Methods say "≥ 6 mm". The registry number format suggests registration in 2019, after enrolment (inference). Authors call for multicentre RCTs with long follow-up.

## 7. Glossary
- **DBBMC**: deproteinised bovine bone mineral with 10% collagen (Bio-Oss Collagen).
- **EMD**: enamel matrix derivative (Emdogain).
- **SPIT**: supportive peri-implant (and periodontal) therapy.
- **STOC**: successful treatment outcome criterion - composite of PD, bone loss, BOP/suppuration and recession thresholds.
- **CTG**: connective tissue graft.
- **FMBS / FMPS**: full-mouth bleeding / plaque score.
