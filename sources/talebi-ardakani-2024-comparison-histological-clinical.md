This RCT evaluates allogenic bone graft with/without injectable platelet-rich fibrin (I-PRF) for alveolar ridge preservation (ARP), providing histomorphometric evidence (new bone/residual graft/nonmineralized tissue) complementary to existing PRF/allograft ridge preservation literature.

## Three-line Summary
In a double-blinded RCT of 20 extraction sockets, allograft + I-PRF significantly reduced radiographic/clinical ridge width loss and yielded more new bone with less residual graft than allograft alone.  
I-PRF did not significantly reduce ridge height loss or affect nonmineralized tissue percentage at 3 months.  
Histologically, both approaches were successful; adjunctive I-PRF improved bone formation and graft remodeling compared to allograft alone.

## 세줄요약
발치와 보존술에서 동종골 이식재 단독 대비 동종골 + 주사형 혈소판풍부피브린(I-PRF) 병용 시 방사선학적/임상적 치조제 폭 감소가 유의하게 적었고, 신생골 비율은 높고 잔여 이식재 비율은 낮았다.  
치조제 높이 감소와 비광화조직 비율은 유의한 차이가 없었다.  
3개월 조직학적 분석상 I-PRF 병용이 골형성 및 이식재 리모델링에 유리한 결과를 보였다.

## 1. Document Information
- Study design: Double-blinded randomized controlled clinical trial (RCT)
- Setting: Shahid Beheshti University of Medical Sciences, Tehran, Iran (Oct 2022–Mar 2023)
- Sample: 20 single-rooted extraction sockets in 12 patients (control n=10, intervention n=10)
- Intervention: Socket preservation with allograft (500–1000 μm) + collagen type 1 ± I-PRF
- Follow-up: 3 months to implant placement with CBCT + biopsy (2×4 mm trephine)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrates histomorphometric benefit of I-PRF adjunct to allograft for ARP (more new bone, less residual graft)
- Shows clinical/radiographic benefit in ridge width preservation (less width loss)
- Provides human biopsy data comparing allograft±I-PRF

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Randomization via Random Allocation Software; dark coded envelopes
- Blinding: pathologist and statistician blinded
- I-PRF: 10 mL venous blood, no anticoagulant, 700 rpm × 3 min; upper 1 mL collected with blunt needle, used immediately
- Measurements: radiographic/clinical bone width at 3 mm below crest, radiographic height; histomorphometry of new bone/residual graft/nonmineralized tissue (% of area)
- Statistics: Shapiro–Wilk, Student’s t-test (α=0.05)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Radiographic bone width reduction: control 1.07±0.40 mm vs intervention 0.67±0.39 mm (P=0.038)
- Clinical bone width reduction: control 1.31±0.55 mm vs intervention 0.82±0.39 mm (P=0.033)
- Radiographic bone height reduction: control 1.20±0.46 mm vs intervention 0.96±0.34 mm (P=0.213, NS)
- New bone: control 44.15%±13.09% vs intervention 60.72%±19.32% (P=0.038)
- Residual graft: control 30.75%±10.24% vs intervention 20.28%±8.14% (P=0.021)
- Nonmineralized tissue: control 25.76%±10.77% vs intervention 19.73%±9.82% (P=0.208, NS)
- No inflammation/foreign body reaction; uneventful healing

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=20), short follow-up (3 months)
- Single-rooted teeth only
- Animal-model comparisons common in literature; human biopsy is strength but limited duration
- Suggest larger studies with longer follow-up and possibly split-mouth design

## 7. Glossary
- 치조제 보존술 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP): 발치 후 치조제 흡수를 최소화하기 위한 술식
- 동종골 이식재 (Allogenic Bone Graft): 타인 유래 골이식재
- 주사형 혈소판풍부피브린 (Injectable Platelet-Rich Fibrin, I-PRF): 저속 원심분리로 제조한 액상 PRF
- 혈소판풍부피브린 (Platelet-Rich Fibrin, PRF): 혈소판 농축 피브린 기질
- 조직형태측정 (Histomorphometry): 조직 절편 상 면적 비율 측정
- 잔여 이식재 입자 (Residual Graft Particles): 미흡수된 이식재
- 신생골 (Newly Formed Bone): 재생된 골조직
- 비광화조직 (Nonmineralized Tissue): 연조직/미광화 기질
