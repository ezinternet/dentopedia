## Why Ingested

This systematic review directly addresses a major barrier to CHX adherence — tooth staining — by demonstrating that anti-discoloration system (ADS) formulations reduce staining (SMD=-3.19) without sacrificing anti-plaque or anti-gingivitis efficacy. It challenges the clinical dogma that staining is an inevitable trade-off for CHX effectiveness, complementing [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/windhorst-2025-cpc-chx-plaque-gingivitis-sr-ma]] which evaluates CPC as a stain-free CHX alternative.

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review and meta-analysis included 13 RCTs (16 comparisons) evaluating chlorhexidine mouthwash with anti-discoloration system (CHX-MW+ADS) versus CHX-MW without ADS on tooth staining, plaque, and gingivitis in non-brushing and brushing models. In non-brushing studies, CHX-MW+ADS significantly reduced tooth staining (SMD = -3.19, p = 0.0005, 95% CI: -3.98 to -1.41) while showing no significant differences in plaque (DiffM = 0.10, p = 0.45) or gingival index (DiffM = 0.04, p = 0.15). In brushing studies, no significant differences were found for staining, plaque, or gingivitis between CHX-MW+ADS and CHX-MW.

There is moderate-quality evidence that ADS reduces tooth staining without compromising the anti-plaque and anti-gingivitis efficacy of CHX mouthwash in both non-brushing and brushing studies.

## 세줄요약

본 체계적문헌고찰 및 메타분석은 항변색 시스템(ADS)이 포함된 클로르헥시딘 구강세정제(CHX-MW+ADS)와 포함되지 않은 클로르헥시딘 구강세정제(CHX-MW)를 치아 착색, 치태, 치은염에 대해 평가한 13개의 무작위대조시험(RCT, 16개 비교군)을 포함하였다. 비양치 모델에서 CHX-MW+ADS는 치아 착색을 유의하게 감소시켰으나(SMD = -3.19, p = 0.0005, 95% CI: -3.98 ~ -1.41), 치태(DiffM = 0.10, p = 0.45)나 치은지수(DiffM = 0.04, p = 0.15)에서는 유의한 차이가 없었다. 양치 모델에서는 착색, 치태, 치은염 모두에서 CHX-MW+ADS와 CHX-MW 간에 유의한 차이가 없었다.

중등도의 근거는 ADS가 CHX 구강세정제의 항치태 및 항치은염 효과를 저하시키지 않으면서 치아 착색을 감소시킨다는 것을 보여준다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- ADS addition to CHX mouthwash significantly reduces tooth surface discoloration in non-brushing studies (SMD = -3.19, p = 0.0005).
- No significant difference in plaque index between CHX-MW+ADS and CHX-MW in non-brushing studies (DiffM = 0.10, p = 0.45).
- No significant difference in gingival index between groups in non-brushing studies (DiffM = 0.04, p = 0.15).
- In brushing studies, ADS does not affect anti-plaque (DiffM = 0.01, p = 0.29) or anti-gingivitis efficacy (DiffM = 0.00, p = 0.87).
- Moderate-quality evidence supports ADS use to reduce CHX staining without compromising clinical efficacy.
- The "if it does not stain, it does not work" paradigm is challenged by the findings.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials (RCTs) and controlled clinical trials (CCTs).
- Databases: MEDLINE-PubMed and Cochrane-CENTRAL searched up to October 2018 (updated to February 2019). Reference lists hand-searched. Grey literature searched (OpenGrey, EFP, IADR).
- PRISMA compliance: Yes, followed PRISMA and Cochrane Handbook guidelines.
- Inclusion criteria: RCTs/CCTs in humans ≥18 years, good general health, no dentures/orthodontics/implants/periodontal surgery. CHX-MW+ADS vs CHX-MW with identical CHX concentration (or omitted if unavailable). Daily rinsing ≥2×/day. Outcomes: discoloration, plaque, bleeding/gingivitis.
- Excluded: Non-RCTs, studies without control group, studies not reporting relevant outcomes.
- Included: 13 publications (16 comparisons): 6 non-brushing, 10 brushing studies.
- Risk of bias: Assessed using customized checklist (7 criteria). Studies classified as low/moderate/high risk of bias. Most studies had low-moderate risk of bias.
- Data synthesis: Descriptive analysis + meta-analysis (Review Manager 5.3). Random-effects model used when heterogeneity present; fixed-effects for <4 comparisons.
- Heterogeneity: Assessed via Chi², I², Tau².
- GRADE: Used to rate quality of evidence (moderate for most outcomes).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Staining score (non-brushing, end scores)** — SMD = -3.19 (95% CI: -3.98 to -1.41, p = 0.0005, I² = 97%)
- **Staining score (non-brushing, incremental)** — SMD = -3.03 (95% CI: -4.76 to -1.30, p = 0.0006, I² = 97%)
- **Staining score (brushing, end scores)** — DiffM = 0.12 (95% CI: -3.32 to 3.55, p = 0.95)
- **Plaque index (non-brushing, end scores)** — DiffM = 0.10 (95% CI: -0.15 to 0.34, p = 0.45)
- **Plaque index (brushing, end scores)** — DiffM = 0.01 (95% CI: -0.01 to 0.02, p = 0.29)
- **Gingival index (non-brushing, end scores)** — DiffM = 0.04 (95% CI: -0.02 to 0.11, p = 0.15)
- **Gingival index (brushing, end scores)** — DiffM = 0.00 (95% CI: -0.05 to 0.06, p = 0.87)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Considerable statistical heterogeneity was observed in non-brushing studies for staining outcomes (I² = 87-97%).
- Sensitivity analysis showed heterogeneity reduced when outlying study (X) was excluded for baseline and incremental scores, but remained high for end scores.
- Publication bias could not be assessed due to <10 studies in meta-analyses.
- Industry involvement in some studies (Curaden, ICPA, GlaxoSmithKline, Johnson & Johnson) may introduce bias.
- Different ADS formulations, CHX concentrations, and study protocols (crossover vs parallel) contribute to heterogeneity.
- Most studies were short-term (4-35 days); long-term effects remain unclear.
- Only moderate-quality evidence; high-quality RCTs with standardized methods are needed.

## 7. Glossary

- **CHX (Chlorhexidine)**: 클로르헥시딘 — 광범위 항균 구강세정제, 치태 및 치은염 감소에 가장 효과적인 항균제
- **ADS (Anti-Discoloration System)**: 항변색 시스템 — CHX에 의한 치아 외인성 착색을 감소시키기 위해 첨가된 성분(예: 메타중아황산염, 아스코르브산)
- **DiffM (Difference of Means)**: 평균 차이 — 두 군 간 평균값 차이
- **SMD (Standardized Mean Difference)**: 표준화 평균 차이 — 서로 다른 단위의 결과를 결합할 때 사용
- **Maillard Reaction**: 마이야르 반응 — 당과 단백질 간 반응으로 멜라노이딘(색소) 형성
- **Melanoidins**: 멜라노이딘 — CHX에 의해 촉매되는 중합 반응으로 형성된 착색 색소
- **Meta-analysis**: 메타분석 — 여러 연구 결과를 통계적으로 결합하는 방법
- **GRADE (Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation)**: 근거 품질 평가 및 권고 강도 등급 체계
