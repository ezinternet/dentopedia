## Why Ingested

This narrative review directly addresses color stability and optical durability of extrinsically stained monolithic zirconia — a topic central to clinical decisions about surface finishing and characterization in zirconia restorations. It synthesizes 9 in vitro studies (2016–2026) under mechanical, chemical, thermal, and hydrothermal aging, providing a clinically actionable summary of which aging factors matter most and what thresholds matter. It also identifies glazing vs polishing as the key modifiable variable for preserving color stability.

## Three-line Summary (EN)

This narrative review synthesized 9 in vitro studies evaluating color stability of extrinsically stained monolithic zirconia under simulated aging. Mechanical surface reduction (>100 μm occlusal adjustment) was the most critical factor, causing ΔE00 values to exceed the clinical acceptability threshold (>1.8), while chemical and thermal aging produced generally acceptable color changes. Glazing consistently outperformed polishing as a surface finish for preserving extrinsic stain integrity over time.

## Document Information

- **Study type**: Narrative review
- **Search period**: 2016–February 2026
- **Databases**: PubMed, ScienceDirect, Wiley Online Library
- **Initial results**: 1,354 articles
- **Final inclusion**: 9 in vitro studies
- **Main metrics**: ΔE (CIELAB), ΔE00 (CIEDE2000), 상대 투명도 파라미터 (Relative Translucency Parameter, RTP), 표면 거칠기 (Surface Roughness, Ra)

## Key Contributions

1. **Aging factor hierarchy**: Mechanical aging > chemical aging > thermal aging in impact on color stability of extrinsically stained zirconia
2. **Critical mechanical threshold**: 교합면 조정 (Occlusal Adjustment) >100 μm → ΔE00 exceeds 50:50% acceptability threshold (>1.8)
3. **Glazing is protective**: Glazed surfaces consistently showed better color stability than polished surfaces across chemical, mechanical, and combined aging protocols
4. **Translucency vs. color**: Thermal aging affects translucency (RTP) more than color stability (ΔE); color and translucency are not equivalent endpoints
5. **Composition matters**: 고투명 지르코니아 (High-Translucency Zirconia) 6Y-PSZ showed lower color stability than 3Y-TZP under combined mechanical/thermal aging
6. **Clinical recommendation**: Prefer precolored (intrinsically pigmented) zirconia when long-term mechanical adjustments or stability is a priority

## Methodology

**Study selection**:
- Two independent reviewers; disagreements resolved by third reviewer
- Inclusion: extrinsically stained zirconia, colorimetric evaluation after simulated aging, 2016–2026, English, in vitro
- Exclusion: exclusively intrinsically pigmented zirconia, no aging evaluation, non-peer-reviewed

**Aging protocols covered**:
- Thermal aging (thermocycling): 5 studies — most common protocol
- Mechanical aging (cyclic loading, occlusal adjustment, toothbrushing): 3 studies
- Chemical aging (coffee, tea immersion): 3 studies
- Hydrothermal aging (autoclave): 1 study

**Color measurement**:
- ΔE (CIELAB): perceptibility threshold ~3.3–3.7
- ΔE00 (CIEDE2000): acceptability threshold ≤1.8 (50:50% threshold)
- Note: ΔE and ΔE00 are not interchangeable; review intentionally avoided numerical comparison between metrics

## Key Results

### Mechanical Aging
- Most impactful factor on color stability
- Herpel et al. (2021): ΔE00 exceeded acceptability threshold in linear fashion when occlusal adjustment >100 μm
- Spyropoulou et al. (2016): Cyclic loading (500,000 cycles) affected L*, a*, b* but remained within ΔE <3.7
- Glazed > polished for protecting extrinsic stain layer from mechanical disruption

### Chemical Aging
- Coffee/tea induced moderate but generally clinically acceptable discoloration
- Glazing maintained ΔE within acceptable limits; polishing less protective
- Externally stained zirconia > preshaded zirconia in discoloration after coffee thermocycling, but both clinically acceptable
- Chemical exposure tends to affect translucency (RTP) more than color (ΔE)
- Zirconia more resistant to staining beverages than hybrid ceramics or lithium disilicate

### Thermal Aging
- Minimal impact on color stability; ΔE/ΔE00 generally below thresholds
- Thermocycling significantly affected RTP (translucency), particularly in thinner/externally shaded specimens
- Combined thermal + mechanical or chemical aging showed synergistic, greater color changes

### Hydrothermal Aging
- Rafael et al. (2018): Autoclave significantly increased discoloration, especially with fluorescent characterization liquids
- Fluorescent surface additives more susceptible to degradation under combined heat/moisture

### Included Studies Summary (9 papers)
| Author | Year | Aging | Key Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spyropoulou | 2016 | Cyclic mechanical loading | ΔE <3.7, clinically acceptable |
| Papageorgiou-Kyrana | 2018 | Thermocycling | ΔE <3.7, liquid-shaded slightly more stable |
| Subaşı | 2018 | Coffee thermocycling | Externally stained > preshaded discoloration; affects RTP |
| Alp | 2018 | Coffee thermocycling | ΔE00 similar; RTP affected; translucency decreases with thickness |
| Rafael | 2018 | Hydrothermal (autoclave) | Significant discoloration, especially fluorescent-treated |
| Kanat-Ertürk | 2020 | Coffee/tea immersion | Glazing > polishing for color stability |
| Herpel | 2021 | Mechanical reduction 20–500 μm | ΔE00 >1.8 when >100 μm removed |
| Bayestehtarat | 2023 | Thermocycling + toothbrushing | Changes within perceptibility/acceptability thresholds |
| Abounassif | 2026 | Thermocycling + toothbrushing | 6Y-PSZ < 3Y-TZP stability; glazing > polishing for external stain |

## Limitations

1. All 9 included studies are in vitro — no clinical trials available
2. Substantial heterogeneity in aging protocols (thermocycling 5,000–10,000 cycles; immersion hours to weeks)
3. Variability in zirconia types (3Y, 4Y, 5Y yttria content), translucency grades, staining protocols
4. Non-standardized ΔE00 thresholds and measurement conditions across studies
5. Few studies evaluated simultaneous combined chemical + thermal + mechanical aging

## Glossary

- **ΔE (CIELAB)**: CIELAB 색차 (Color Difference) — 임상 지각 한계 ~3.3–3.7
- **ΔE00 (CIEDE2000)**: 개선된 색차 지수 — 임상 허용 한계 ≤1.8 (50:50% 기준)
- **RTP (Relative Translucency Parameter)**: 상대 투명도 파라미터 — 투명도 변화를 정량화
- **Ra (Surface Roughness)**: 표면 거칠기 (산술 평균)
- **3Y-TZP**: 3몰% 이트리아 첨가 정방정계 다결정 지르코니아 — 고강도 표준 조성
- **6Y-PSZ**: 6몰% 이트리아 첨가 부분안정화 지르코니아 — 고투명, 낮은 강도
- **Extrinsic Staining**: 외재성 착색 — 소결 후 표면에 착색제 도포 후 유약 처리
- **Glazing**: 유약 처리 — 소결 후 표면 보호층 형성
- **Thermocycling**: 열순환 — 온도 반복 변화로 구강 열적 스트레스 모사
