## Why Ingested

치근단 병소 부위의 즉시식립 실험별 결과가 상이하여 종합 근거가 필요했음. 기존 [[immediate-implant/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]]와 달리 이 MA는 플라크 지수와 출혈지수를 주요 지표로 하여 임플란트 생존율의 OR을 2.08로 제시 — 감염 부위에서도 즉시식립 성공 예측의 수치적 근거로 활용.

## Three-line Summary

Meta-analysis (10 RCTs, n=849 patients) comparing immediate implant placement in sites with vs without apical pathosis, 2000–2022.

Infected sites showed lower plaque index (RR=0.59) and bleeding index (RR=0.77), and implant survival OR=2.08 — all statistically significant (p<0.05); 8 individual RCTs showed 92–100% survival rates.

Immediate implant placement in apical pathosis sites is clinically viable when antiseptic protocols and proper debridement are applied.

## 세줄요약

MA (RCT 10편, n=849): 치근단 병소 유무별 즉시식립 결과 비교 (2000–2022).

감염 부위: 플라크지수 RR=0.59, 출혈지수 RR=0.77, 임플란트 생존 OR=2.08 — 모두 p<0.05; 개별 RCT 생존율 92–100%.

소독 프로토콜·철저한 소파 적용 시 치근단 병소 부위 즉시식립 임상적으로 실현 가능함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Comprehensive quantitative meta-analysis of 10 RCTs on immediate implant in apical pathosis sites
- Quantified benefit via OR=2.08 for implant survival (vs 1.0 null)
- Demonstrated low plaque index (RR=0.59) and low bleeding index (RR=0.77) in infected sites post-implant
- Included Camara et al. (100 patients, acutely infected sockets with periapical disease) showing no retrograde peri-implantitis at 1 year

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis, PRISMA-compliant
- **Search**: MEDLINE/PubMed, Embase, Scopus, Cochrane, Web of Sciences; 2000–2023
- **n**: 10 studies, 849 patients
- **PICO**: P=apical pathosis; I=immediate implantation; C=delayed implantation; O=plaque index, bleeding index, implant survival rate
- **Statistical model**: Mantel-Haenszel with random bivariate effects; RevMan software
- **Publication bias**: Begg's test p=0.45 (not significant); symmetric funnel plot

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Pooled estimate | 95% CI | I² | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plaque index (RR) | 0.59 | 0.36–0.96 | 90% | <0.05 |
| Bleeding index (RR) | 0.77 | 0.60–0.98 | 89% | <0.05 |
| Implant survival (OR) | 2.08 | 1.56–1.79 | 83% | <0.05 |

Key individual studies:
- Crespi (4-yr): 98.9% infected vs 100% non-infected; no MBL differences
- Camara (1-yr, acutely infected): no retrograde peri-implantitis; IS-BIC 0.35±0.51 vs 0.15±0.87 mm (NS)
- Kakar (laser decontamination): 95.45% survival (105/110)
- Jung (5-yr): 100% survival both groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High heterogeneity (I²=83–90%) — diverse protocols and patient populations
- English-only publication restriction
- Large proportion of studies lacked detailed socket morphology classification
- Varied selection criteria across included RCTs — difficult to standardize
- Limited data on aesthetic outcomes (soft tissue aesthetics, pink esthetic score)

## 7. Glossary

- **Apical pathosis**: periapical inflammatory/infectious lesion around root apex
- **OR (Odds Ratio)**: 2.08 here indicates immediate implant more likely to succeed in infected vs control conditions
- **Retrograde peri-implantitis**: peri-implant bone loss originating from a pre-existing periapical lesion
- **IS-BIC**: implant shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact — radiographic bone level measure
