## Why Ingested

치유 기간이 치조정보존 (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, ARP) 결과에 미치는 영향을 체계적으로 평가한 최초의 RCT. 탈단백우골미네랄-콜라겐 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral with Collagen, DBBM-C)로 ARP 시행 후 3·6·9개월 치유 시점별 조직계측·체적·임플란트 관련 결과를 동시에 보고하며, 협측골두께 (Buccal Bone Thickness, BBT)가 골 및 연조직 소실의 강력한 예측인자임을 최초로 volumetric 차원에서 입증.

## Three-line Summary

A three-arm RCT (n=42, non-molar sites, University of Iowa) randomized patients to 3, 6, or 9 months of healing after ARP with DBBM-C (Bio-Oss Collagen) sealed with a porcine collagen matrix, measuring histomorphometric, volumetric, linear, implant-related, and patient-reported outcomes at the time of implant placement.

Mineralized tissue proportion increased over time (3 mo: 13.53%; 6 mo: 33.33%; 9 mo: 37.05%), while remaining xenograft material decreased (16.94% → 10.69% → 9.46%), with minimal differences between 6 and 9 months confirming DBBM's low biodegradability; buccal bone thickness ≤1 mm at baseline was strongly and inversely associated with total and facial alveolar bone volume reduction and soft tissue contour loss.

Implant placement was feasible in all 42 sites with primary stability achieved (median insertion torque 35 Ncm); however, 16.7% required ancillary bone augmentation (GBR), of which 85.7% had thin facial bone (≤1 mm) at baseline, supporting a recommendation for 6-month re-entry in most non-molar ARP sites.

## 세줄요약

아이오와대학교 치과대학에서 비구치부 발치와에 DBBM-C + 돼지 콜라겐 차단막으로 치조정보존 시행 후 3·6·9개월 치유 집단(각 n=14)으로 무작위 배분하여 조직계측·체적·선형·임플란트 관련·환자보고 결과를 임플란트 식립 시점에 종합 평가하였다.

치유 기간이 길수록 광화조직 (Mineralized Tissue) 비율이 증가(13.53% → 33.33% → 37.05%)하고 잔존 이종골 (Remaining Xenograft Material) 비율이 감소(16.94% → 10.69% → 9.46%)하였으나, 6개월과 9개월 사이의 차이는 미미하여 DBBM의 낮은 생분해성을 재확인하였다.

42개 부위 모두 임플란트 1차 안정성 확보에 성공(중앙값 삽입 토크 35 Ncm)하였으나 16.7%에서 추가 골재생유도술 (Guided Bone Regeneration, GBR)이 필요하였고 그 중 85.7%는 발치 당시 협측골두께 ≤1 mm 부위로, 협측골두께가 골·연조직 위축의 가장 강력한 예측인자임을 체적 차원에서 처음으로 확인하였다.

## Key Contributions

1. **First study with histomorphometric data beyond 6-month healing** after ARP with DBBM-C in humans.
2. **Healing time effect**: Mineralized tissue proportion increases substantially from 3 to 6 months, with marginal gain from 6 to 9 months; xenograft proportion stabilizes between 6 and 9 months.
3. **Buccal bone thickness as predictor**: First volumetric demonstration that BBT ≤1 mm at extraction is inversely associated with total and facial bone volume loss and soft tissue contour reduction.
4. **Ancillary GBR rate**: 16.7% of sites required augmentation at implant placement; 85.7% of those had thin facial bone at baseline.
5. **Clinical recommendation**: 6-month re-entry is appropriate for most non-molar ARP sites treated with DBBM-C; 3-month placement feasible but higher risk of suboptimal bone density.

## Methodology

- **Design**: 3-arm single-centre RCT; CONSORT-compliant
- **Setting**: University of Iowa College of Dentistry, Iowa City, IA, USA
- **Randomization**: Computer-generated list by non-involved team member; 14 patients per group
- **Sample size**: Power calculation based on Heberer et al. (effect size 0.5, α=0.05, power 80%) → 14/group, 42 total
- **Inclusion**: Adults ≥18 y, non-molar tooth extraction (excluding mandibular incisors), ASA I/II, ≤1 bony wall dehiscence ≤50% height, implant-supported FDP planned
- **Exclusion**: Smokers/recent quitters, uncontrolled DM (HbA1c >7%), immunosuppression, active infection, pregnancy
- **ARP protocol**: Flapless extraction → socket curettage → fill with DBBM-C (Bio-Oss Collagen, Geistlich) → seal with porcine collagen matrix (Mucograft Seal, Geistlich) → 4–6 simple interrupted sutures (Resolon 6-0)
- **Groups**: A = 3 months (10 weeks); B = 6 months (20 weeks); C = 9 months (32 weeks)
- **Implant systems**: Straumann BL or Astra Tech; trephine biopsy (2.5 mm ID) for histology
- **Histomorphometry**: H&E-stained 5-µm decalcified sections; blinded examiner; ImageJ; intra-examiner ICC = 0.99
- **Digital imaging**: Intraoral scan (Planmeca Emerald S) + CBCT (i-CAT Next Gen, 0.3 mm voxel); DICOM-STL superimposition via Romexis v5.2.1 and Geomagic Control X
- **Statistics**: Kruskal-Wallis; Spearman/Pearson correlations; Mann-Whitney-Wilcoxon; linear regression

## Key Results

### Histomorphometric outcomes (41 biopsies — 1 discarded in Group A)

| Tissue type | Group A (3 mo) | Group B (6 mo) | Group C (9 mo) | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mineralized tissue | 13.53% (0–59.62) | 33.33% (0.49–56.21) | 37.05% (9.53–68.85) | .094 |
| Non-mineralized tissue | 63.32% (38.5–85.93) | 55.55% (36.01–81.98) | 52.48% (29.84–62.2) | .150 |
| Remaining xenograft | 16.94% (0.12–32.29) | 10.69% (0.5–31.2) | 9.46% (0.57–31.23) | .428 |

### Implant-related outcomes

- Implant placement feasible and primary stability achieved in **all 42 sites**
- Median insertion torque: **35 Ncm** (range 25–45 Ncm); no significant between-group difference
- Implant diameter: 3.6–4.2 mm; length: 8–11 mm
- Ancillary bone augmentation required: **16.7% (7/42)** sites (A:2, B:1, C:4; buccal dehiscence 3–7 mm depth)
  - **85.7% (6/7)** of augmented sites had BBT ≤1 mm at baseline
- No ancillary soft tissue augmentation required in any site

### Volumetric bone outcomes (median %)

| | Group A | Group B | Group C | p-value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total bone volume | −6.36% | −7.94% | −12.45% | .001 |
| Facial bone volume | −8.55% | −10.87% | −14.92% | .002 |
| Lingual bone volume | −4.35% | −5.76% | −8.99% | .070 |
| Total ridge contour | −11.35% | −12.92% | −20% | .005 |
| Facial ridge contour | −13.29% | −14.51% | −28.62% | .006 |

### Linear bone outcomes (median, mm; no significant between-group differences)

- Horizontal ridge width at 1 mm: A −1.2, B −1.35, C −2.4
- Facial bone height: A −0.6, B −0.5, C −0.4
- Lingual bone height: A −0.4, B −0.45, C −0.3

### Predictors

- BBT at extraction inversely associated with ridge width reduction at 1 mm (p=.02) and 3 mm (p=.008)
- BBT inversely associated with total and facial bone volume reduction (p=.001) and total/facial soft tissue contour reduction (p<.001; p=.02)

### Patient-reported outcomes

- No serious adverse events
- Discomfort decreased progressively over time; no between-group difference
- Overall satisfaction median: **99/100** (range 77–100); similar across groups

## Limitations

1. Non-molar sites only (mostly maxillary) — cannot generalize to molar, mandibular anterior, or extensively damaged sockets
2. No unassisted socket healing control group (by design — study aimed at healing-time comparison)
3. No post-implant provisionalization or long-term prosthetic follow-up data reported
4. Single centre; predominantly maxillary teeth (n=39/42)
5. Funded by graft manufacturer (Geistlich) — potential for conflict of interest

## Related Work

- **Lindhe et al.** — DBBM-C histology at 6 mo: ~19% residual xenograft; speculated higher MT with longer healing (confirmed here)
- **Heberer et al.** — 6-week healing: 54% NMT, 28% MT, 11% xenograft (all higher NMT than groups B/C here)
- **Avila-Ortiz et al.** — ARP overview/SR; wound healing scoring index used in this study
- **Chappuis et al.** — BBT <1 mm linked to greater resorption after unassisted healing (consistent with this study)
- **Thoma et al.** — 90.9% of ARP sites required augmentation at implant placement (much higher than this study's 16.7%)
- **Jonker et al.** — 32% augmentation rate at implant placement post-ARP (higher than this study)

## Glossary

- **ARP (Alveolar Ridge Preservation, 치조정보존)**: socket-filling therapy at tooth extraction to attenuate ridge atrophy
- **DBBM-C (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral with Collagen, 탈단백우골미네랄-콜라겐)**: Bio-Oss Collagen; slow-resorbing xenograft
- **CM (Collagen Matrix, 콜라겐 차단막)**: porcine Mucograft Seal used to seal socket orifice
- **BBT (Buccal Bone Thickness, 협측골두께)**: thickness of facial alveolar bone at extraction; key phenotypic predictor
- **MT (Mineralized Tissue, 광화조직)**: newly formed bone in biopsy
- **NMT (Non-Mineralized Tissue, 비광화조직)**: connective tissue/marrow in biopsy
- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 골재생유도술)**: ancillary augmentation at implant placement
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient — resonance frequency analysis of primary stability
- **STL/DICOM superimposition**: digital method combining intraoral scan (surface) and CBCT (bone) for 3D dimensional change assessment
