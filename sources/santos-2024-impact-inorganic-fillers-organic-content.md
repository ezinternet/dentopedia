## Summary

Scoping review (PubMed, 2011–2024, 15 studies selected from 140) examining how inorganic filler composition, organic matrix content, and polymerization mode affect the degree of conversion (DC) of monomers in resin-matrix cements used for indirect restorations.

**Key findings:**
- 8/15 studies (53%) reported high DC (70–90%); 7/15 (46%) reported lower DC values
- Dual-cured resin-matrix cements showed highest DC, flexural strength, and hardness vs. light- and self-polymerized cements
- Ceramic veneer thickness strongly affects DC: 0.4 mm → ~83% DC vs. 1.5 mm → ~77% DC
- Filler content, size, refractive index, and monomer composition (Bis-GMA/UDMA/TEGDMA ratio) all modulate DC
- DC >55% is the recommended threshold for occlusal restorations

## Why Ingested

Scoping review quantifying how ceramic thickness and cure mode affect degree of conversion (DC) of resin cements — directly relevant to indirect restoration cementation decisions. Provides evidence basis for choosing dual-cure cements with thick ceramics and understanding filler/monomer composition effects on polymerization adequacy.

## Three-line Summary

Scoping review (PubMed, 2011–2024, n=15 studies from 140 screened) examining how inorganic filler composition, organic matrix, and polymerization mode affect degree of conversion (DC) of resin-matrix cements for indirect restorations.
Dual-cured cements achieved highest DC (70–90%); ceramic veneer thickness significantly reduces DC (0.4 mm → ~83% vs. 1.5 mm → ~77%); DC >55% recommended as threshold for occlusal restorations.
Filler size, content, refractive index, and monomer ratio (Bis-GMA/UDMA/TEGDMA) all modulate DC; dual-cure mode is optimal for adequate polymerization under thick ceramic restorations.

## 세줄요약

스코핑 리뷰 (PubMed, 2011–2024, n=15 연구)로 레진-매트릭스 시멘트의 무기 충전재 조성, 유기 기질, 중합 모드가 전환율(Degree of Conversion, DC)에 미치는 영향을 검토함.
이중중합(Dual-Cure) 시멘트가 가장 높은 DC (70–90%) 달성; 세라믹 두께가 DC에 유의한 영향 (0.4mm→83%, 1.5mm→77%); 교합면 수복에 DC >55% 권고.
충전재 크기·함량·굴절률·단량체 비율(Bis-GMA/UDMA/TEGDMA)이 모두 DC에 영향; 두꺼운 세라믹 하방에서 적절한 중합을 위해 이중중합 모드 선택이 유리함.
