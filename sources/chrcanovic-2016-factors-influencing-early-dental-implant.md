## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study of 10,096 implants from one prosthodontic clinic (Malmö, 1980–2014) focusing on early failure before second-stage surgery to isolate predictors free of prosthetic confounders.

Pre-abutment failure was 1.74% (overall failure 6.36%); patient-level logistic regression and implant-level GEE models identified smoking and antidepressant use as the only independent multivariate predictors, with bone quantity/quality, surface, grafting and jaw significant only univariately.

The authors propose serotonin-mediated inhibition of bone metabolism as the mechanism for antidepressant-associated failure — a clinically relevant signal for patient medication review before implant placement.

## 세줄요약

단일 보철과 클리닉(말뫼, 1980–2014)의 10,096개 임플란트를 대상으로 2차 수술(지대주 연결) 이전 조기 실패를 보철 교란 없이 분석했다.

지대주 연결 전 실패율 1.74%(전체 6.36%)였으며, 환자 수준 로지스틱 회귀와 임플란트 수준 GEE 모두에서 흡연과 항우울제 복용만이 독립 다변량 예측인자였고, 골 양·질·표면·이식·악골은 단변량에서만 유의했다.

항우울제 기전으로 세로토닌 매개 골 대사 억제가 제시되며, 임플란트 식립 전 환자 복용 약물 검토 시 임상적 경고 신호로 활용된다.

## One-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of all implants placed at one Swedish specialist clinic 1980–2014 (10,096 implants, 2,670 patients): 176 implants (1.74%; 5.21% of patients) failed before abutment connection, and smoking (patient-level OR 3.27; implant-level GEE OR 2.12) and antidepressant intake (patient-level OR 2.44) were the multivariate predictors.

## 한줄요약

스웨덴 전문 클리닉 1980–2014년 전체 식립(임플란트 10,096개, 환자 2,670명) 후향적 코호트에서 2차 수술 전 조기 실패는 176개(1.74%, 환자 단위 5.21%)였고, 흡연(환자 단위 OR 3.27, 임플란트 단위 GEE OR 2.12)과 항우울제 복용(환자 단위 OR 2.44)이 다변량 예측인자였다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Very large single-clinic denominator (10,096 implants, 34 years) for early failure up to abutment connection: 1.74% implant level, 5.21% patient level.
- Separates patient-level (logistic) and implant-level (GEE, exchangeable correlation) models to handle clustering.
- Smoking survives multivariable adjustment at both levels (patient OR 3.265, 1.486–7.173; implant OR 2.120, 1.113–4.037).
- First large clinical signal for antidepressants and early failure (patient-level OR 2.438, 1.251–4.751; implant-level OR 1.742, NS).
- Bone quantity/quality distribution was similar for implants lost before and after abutment connection, suggesting poor bone was not a dominant early-failure driver in this population.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Retrospective cohort, STROBE-concordant; Regional Ethical Review Board Lund (Dnr 2014/598, 2015/72); NCT02369562. Population: all 2,670 consecutive patients (10,096 cylindrical/conical endosseous implants; zygomatic excluded) at the Centre of Dental Specialist Care, Malmö, 1980–2014. Failure = implant removal; early failure = loss up to second-stage surgery. Variables: implant surface (turned vs enlarged), system, length, diameter, design, antibiotic prophylaxis, grafting, extraction reason, jaw, anterior/posterior, sex, age, Lekholm–Zarb bone quantity/quality, systemic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, hypercholesterolemia, hypothyroidism, asthma, psoriasis, chemotherapy, head–neck irradiation), medications (antidepressants, immunosuppressants, bisphosphonates, antithrombotics, HRT, acid suppressants), smoking, snuff, bruxism. Statistics: univariate then multivariate logistic regression (patient level); univariate then multivariate GEE (binomial, logit, exchangeable) at implant level with P<0.1 entry and VIF/tolerance collinearity checks; SPSS 22.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Overall failure 642/10,096 (6.36%); early failure 176 implants in 139 patients (1.74% implant level, 5.21% patient level; mean age of these patients 54.1 years). Sites of early failures: anterior maxilla 73, anterior mandible 36, posterior maxilla 44, posterior mandible 23; only 4 occurred in non-submerged implants. Patient-level multivariate: smoking OR 3.265 (1.486–7.173, P=0.003); antidepressants OR 2.438 (1.251–4.751, P=0.009); cigarettes/day and age NS. Implant-level multivariate GEE: smoking OR 2.120 (1.113–4.037, P=0.022); antidepressants OR 1.742 (0.856–3.542, P=0.126); jaw, bone quantity (D: OR 3.621, 0.804–16.308, P=0.094), bone quality, surface, system and grafting NS after adjustment. Univariate implant-level smoking OR 2.709 (1.737–4.226).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Author-stated: retrospective chart data with gaps; no medication dosage or detailed systemic status; antidepressant classes pooled. Additional: 34-year span mixes implant generations (turned vs modern surfaces); early-failure endpoint (abutment connection) differs from "before final prosthesis" definitions; specialist referral population.

## 6. Related Work

Chrcanovic 2015 smoking meta-analysis (failure RR ~2.23); Alsaadi 2007 (local and systemic factors in early failure); Lekholm & Zarb 1985 bone classification; serotonin–bone literature (Bliziotes 2001, Warden 2005/2008).

## 7. Glossary

GEE: generalized estimating equations; SSRI: selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor; Lekholm–Zarb A–E/1–4: bone quantity/quality classes; abutment connection: second-stage surgery.
