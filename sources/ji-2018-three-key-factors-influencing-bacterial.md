## Why Ingested

쓰리웨이(air/water) 시린지가 왜 오염되는지 — 그 기전과 통제 인자를 규명한 대규모 조사. [[infection-control/dang-2022-assessment-microbiota-diversity-dental-unit]]가 시린지 물에 "무엇이" 있는지 정량했다면, 본 6년(318 유니트, 64개 병원) 조사는 오염을 좌우하는 세 인자(소독 주기·급수원·역류방지밸브)를 규명해 임상 통제 레버를 제공한다. abstract-only(PMC 본문 공란).

## Three-line Summary

6-year survey (2012–2017) of 318 dental chair units across 64 hospitals in Tianjin, China, sampling handpiece and air/water syringe output water.

Only 56.14% of samples met the DUWL guideline; 78.93% of units used NO disinfection; max 1.8×10⁵ CFU/mL; three key factors: disinfection frequency, water source, and a valid anti-retraction valve.

Air/water syringe output-water contamination is controllable through periodic disinfection, cleaner feed water, and functioning anti-retraction valves.

## 세줄요약

6년(2012–2017) 조사: 중국 톈진 64개 병원의 318개 치과 유니트에서 핸드피스·air/water 시린지 출수를 채취.

기준 만족은 56.14%뿐, 78.93%가 소독 미시행, 최고 1.8×10⁵ CFU/mL; 오염 좌우 3대 인자 = 소독 주기·급수원·유효한 역류방지밸브.

쓰리웨이 시린지 출수 오염은 주기적 소독·깨끗한 급수·작동하는 역류방지밸브로 통제 가능.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. Large 6-year real-world survey of DUWL contamination including **air/water syringe** output water.
2. Custom sampling connector and a retraction-measurement device to quantify anti-retraction valve function.
3. Identified three controllable factors: disinfection frequency, feed-water source, and valid anti-retraction valve.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 318 DCUs randomly selected from 64 hospitals, 2012–2017.
- Output water from handpieces and air/water syringes tested against China's national standard.
- Retraction (suck-back) volume measured with a purpose-built device.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 56.14% (343) of samples complied with the DUWL output-water guideline.
- 78.93% of DCUs used NO disinfection.
- Highest bacterial concentration 1.8×10⁵ CFU/mL.
- Three key factors: (1) daily/weekly disinfection; (2) feed source = hospital-purified or bottled purified water; (3) valid anti-retraction valve.
- Isolated potential pathogens: *Bacillus cereus*, *Burkholderia cepacia*, *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*.

## 7. Glossary

- **Anti-retraction valve**: one-way valve distal to handpiece/syringe preventing suck-back of oral fluids.
- **Retraction (suck-back)**: negative-pressure backflow of oral fluids into the waterline on instrument release.
- **CFU/mL**: colony-forming units per mL; DUWL output-water guidelines commonly ≤100–500 CFU/mL.
