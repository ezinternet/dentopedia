## Why Ingested

GBR umbrella review(엄브렐라 리뷰) PMC 풀텍스트 인제스트 파이프라인 요청으로 광범위 PubMed 서치를 수행했으나, PMC 풀텍스트가 있는 신규 GBR umbrella review는 발견하지 못함(발견된 후보는 모두 기존 위키에 이미 존재: ng-2025 titanium mesh umbrella, gowda-2025 GTR endodontic umbrella 등). 대신 GBR에 생물학적 보조제(biologic adjunct)를 결합하는 최신 근거로 이 SR을 채택 — [[bone-regeneration/mizraji-2023-barrier-membranes-gbr-overview]]의 barrier membrane/graft 중심 GBR 개관에 simvastatin이라는 약리학적 보조제 축을 추가하고, [[bone-regeneration/jiang-2020-bmp2-calcium-phosphate-alveolar-orthodontic]]와 함께 "BMP-2 경로를 표적으로 하는 골형성 촉진 보조제" 계열을 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (no meta-analysis; 5 studies — 3 human clinical, 2 animal) of local simvastatin as an adjunct to guided bone regeneration (GBR) or related implant regenerative procedures.

Simvastatin reduced peri-implant crestal bone loss (0.975±0.044mm vs 1.356±0.038mm, P<0.0001), increased bone width gain (5.55±0.80mm vs 3.36±0.19mm) and bone mineral density (640.02±262.22 vs 297.38±82.94) vs controls; animal data showed higher bone-to-implant contact (84.3% vs 59.5% at 4 weeks, grafted).

Simvastatin appears to be a low-cost, biologically plausible adjunct for hard-tissue regeneration around implants, but evidence is limited to 5 small/heterogeneous studies with no meta-analysis possible, and implant stability (ISQ) showed no consistent benefit.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(메타분석 없음; 5편 — 인체 임상 3편, 동물실험 2편)로, 국소 심바스타틴을 유도골재생(GBR) 또는 관련 임플란트 재생술식의 보조제로 사용한 근거를 종합.

심바스타틴군은 치조정골소실 감소(0.975±0.044mm vs 1.356±0.038mm, P<0.0001), 골폭 증가(5.55±0.80mm vs 3.36±0.19mm), 골밀도 증가(640.02±262.22 vs 297.38±82.94)를 보였고, 동물실험에서는 골-임플란트 접촉률(BIC)이 대조군 대비 유의하게 높음(이식 시 4주째 84.3% vs 59.5%).

심바스타틴은 저비용·생물학적으로 타당한 경조직 재생 보조제로 보이나, 근거는 5편의 소규모·이질적 연구에 국한되어 메타분석이 불가능했고 임플란트 안정성(ISQ) 개선은 일관되지 않았다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic synthesis specifically focused on **localized simvastatin as an adjunct to GBR/implant-associated regenerative procedures** (prior reviews addressed simvastatin in bone regeneration/periodontal defects generally, not implant-specific regenerative contexts).
- Separately tabulates human clinical (n=3) vs preclinical animal (n=2) evidence, distinguishing clinically-relevant outcomes from mechanistic/histological findings.
- Applies PRISMA 2020 + PROSPERO registration, with domain-appropriate risk-of-bias tools (RoB-2 for RCTs, ROBINS-I for non-randomized, SYRCLE for animal studies).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PICOS-guided eligibility: local simvastatin (gel/scaffold/granules/implant coating) as adjunct to GBR or related peri-implant regenerative procedures, vs same protocol without simvastatin; RCTs, controlled clinical trials, prospective studies, and animal studies eligible; in vitro, case reports, and non-comparative studies excluded.
- Searches: PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane CENTRAL, Google Scholar, ClinicalTrials.gov, to April 30, 2025, no language restriction.
- Meta-analysis was deemed inappropriate given the small number (5) and heterogeneity of studies (concentration, carrier, comparator protocol, follow-up); narrative synthesis grouped by clinical vs animal evidence and outcome domain.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Human clinical (n=3):**
| Study | Design | n | Intervention | Key result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| El Shafei 2022 | Non-RCT split-mouth | 8pt/16 implants | 1.2mg SMV + PRF vs PRF alone, IIP | Bone loss 0.975±0.044mm vs 1.356±0.038mm (P<0.0001, 12mo); ISQ NS |
| Betha 2024 | RCT | 50 | 1% SMV gel at socket, IIP | Probing depth 5.7→3.2mm (SMV) vs 5.8→4.1mm (control), 12wk |
| Issa 2024 | RCT | 22pt/49 implants | 1.2% SMV gel + Tutobone/Tutopatch, ridge splitting + simultaneous implant | Bone width 5.55±0.80mm vs 3.36±0.19mm; BMD 640.02±262.22 vs 297.38±82.94 (9mo) |

**Animal (n=2):**
| Study | Model | Intervention | Key result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mansour 2014 | Dog (n=10) | 2.2mg SMV granules in HPMC, IIP | More new bone, higher osteocyte count, more vascularity vs control |
| Jun 2021 | Rabbit (n=12/48 implants) | 0.5mM SMV-coated implants ± UV, Bio-Oss + collagen membrane | Grafted BIC 4wk: 84.3% (SMV) vs 59.5% (control); BA 75.9% vs 51.4% |

- Consistent benefit: hard-tissue regeneration (crestal bone preservation, bone width, BMD, BIC). Inconsistent benefit: implant stability (ISQ).
- Risk of bias: both RCTs low risk (all domains); non-RCT moderate (confounding); animal studies moderate (Mansour) to low (Jun), mainly due to unclear randomization/blinding reporting typical of preclinical work.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 5 studies met inclusion criteria — small evidence base, no meta-analysis possible.
- Substantial heterogeneity: simvastatin concentration (1%, 1.2%, 0.5mM), delivery vehicle (gel, PRF, granules, implant coating), comparator protocol, and follow-up (2 weeks–12 months) — direct head-to-head comparison not possible, no optimal protocol identifiable.
- Study populations restricted to healthy, non-smoking adults/animals — generalizability to medically compromised or older patients (who may benefit most) unestablished.
- Authors call for larger, standardized RCTs with longer follow-up and core outcome sets (MBL, bone width, BMD, esthetics, ISQ).

## 7. Glossary

- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 유도골재생)**: barrier-membrane technique excluding non-osteogenic soft tissue to allow bone regeneration in a secluded space.
- **HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor**: statin drug class; simvastatin is repurposed here for local osteoinductive effect via BMP-2 upregulation, not its systemic lipid-lowering action.
- **BIC (Bone-to-Implant Contact, 골-임플란트 접촉률)**: histomorphometric measure of osseointegration.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient, 임플란트 안정성 지수)**: RFA-based implant stability measure.
- **SYRCLE**: risk-of-bias tool specific to animal intervention studies.
