## Why Ingested

[[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/muthuswamy-pandian-2025-optimized-conventional-attachment-accuracy-sr-ma]]는 부착물(attachment) 설계가 회전 정확도에 미치는 효과를 분석했으나, **치아 부위별 회전 정확도 수치**가 부재했다. 본 논문은 절치·견치·소구치·대구치 각 부위별 정확도 범위를 제시하고, 평균 65%(범위 36–85%)라는 현재 투명교정 회전 운동의 한계를 명확히 수량화한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (12 studies, 1 RCT + 11 non-randomized; PRISMA; July 2025 search) of clear aligner rotational accuracy in adult patients, measuring predicted vs achieved tooth movement via 3D digital superimposition before refinement.

Overall rotational accuracy averaged ~65% (range 36–85%); accuracy varied significantly by tooth type: molars most predictable (74–85%), followed by premolars (54–83%) and incisors (35.1–90%), with canines least predictable (~36–58%, ~4.5° residual error); attachment effectiveness and IPR benefit remain inconsistent across studies.

Evidence quality is low to moderate (1 RCT: moderate; 11 non-randomized: low); well-designed RCTs with standardized protocols are needed before reliable clinical guidelines can be established.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(12편, 1 RCT+11 비무작위; PRISMA; 2025년 7월 검색): 성인 투명교정 회전 정확도를 3D 디지털 중첩으로 평가(리파인먼트 전).

전체 회전 정확도 평균 약 65%(범위 36–85%); 부위별: 대구치 74–85%로 가장 높고, 소구치 54–83%, 절치 35.1–90%, 견치 36–58%(잔여 오차 약 4.5°)로 최저; 부착물·IPR의 효과는 연구간 불일치.

근거 수준 낮음–보통(RCT 1편: 보통, 비무작위 11편: 낮음); 표준화된 RCT 설계 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Tooth-type breakdown**: First SR to stratify rotational accuracy by all four tooth types with quantified ranges — canines are the problem tooth (36–58%), molars are the most reliable (74–85%).
- **Overall accuracy benchmark**: Average ~65%, range 36–85% — a clinically important number for patient counseling.
- **Attachment controversy**: 11/12 studies used attachments, yet evidence for their benefit remains inconsistent; controlled comparisons do not confirm superiority of optimized over conventional designs for rotation.
- **IPR effect size**: The only study isolating IPR (Kravitz) found 43% accuracy with IPR vs lower without, but not statistically significant — IPR may help but is unproven.
- **Staging implication**: Most studies used 2°/aligner; reducing to <1.5°/aligner is suggested by literature to improve accuracy.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA 2020)
- **Studies**: 12 (1 RCT, 11 non-randomized prospective/retrospective)
- **Population**: Adults ≥18 years, full permanent dentition, clear aligner only (no fixed appliances)
- **Measurement**: 3D digital model superimposition (predicted vs achieved); pre-refinement outcomes only
- **Outcome**: Rotational accuracy (%) + lack of correction (LC°) + mean absolute error (MAE°)
- **Risk of bias**: RoB 2 for RCT (some concerns); ROBINS-I for non-randomized (no low-risk studies)
- **GRADE**: RCT → moderate; non-randomized → low
- **Meta-analysis**: Not performed (heterogeneity precluded pooling)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Overall accuracy**: ~65% (range 36–85%)

**By tooth type:**

| Tooth | Accuracy Range | Residual Error |
|---|---|---|
| Molars (1st) | 74–85% | ~0.4–2.3° |
| Premolars | 54–83% | ~2.2–4.6° |
| Incisors (central) | up to 90% | — |
| Canines | 36–58% | ~4.5° |

- Maxillary canines especially unpredictable (~50%)
- Incisor range wide (35.1–90%): maxillary centrals outperform laterals
- Second molars less predictable than first molars

**Secondary:**
- Attachments: used in 11/12 studies; effectiveness "inconsistent"
- IPR: highest accuracy 43% (Kravitz, with IPR) but not statistically significant
- Staging: 2°/aligner predominant; <1.5° suggested for improvement
- Wear time: predominantly 14 days per aligner

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 1 RCT; 11 non-randomized retrospective designs dominate
- Heterogeneity (aligner systems, staging protocols, attachment types, measurement methods) precludes meta-analysis
- No standardized compliance assessment (patient wear time unverified)
- Adults only — no adolescent data
- High risk of bias across nearly all non-randomized studies
- Search through July 2025 — most recent Invisalign generations may not be represented

## 7. Glossary

- **Rotational accuracy (%)**: (Achieved rotation / Planned rotation) × 100
- **Lack of correction (LC)**: Planned − achieved rotation in degrees
- **MAE**: Mean absolute error between planned and achieved rotation
- **IPR**: Interproximal reduction — selective enamel removal between teeth to create space
- **SmartTrack**: Invisalign's multilayer polyurethane material (introduced ~2013); superior force maintenance vs earlier single-layer polymers
