## Why Ingested

위키의 [[overviews/clear-aligner-indications-limitations]] axis 3(Class III camouflage/growth-modification)는 현재 [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/zhang-2025-clear-aligner-based-multidisciplinary]](n=1, 성인 비수술 camouflage)와 [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/ye-2025-combined-use-of-miniscrews]](n=1, 성장기 scissor bite) 두 편의 단일 증례보고에만 의존한다고 명시적으로 flag되어 있다. 본 논문은 골격성 Class III 환자에서 Surgery-First Approach(SFA) 병용 시 투명교정(CA, n=20) vs 고정식장치(FA, n=14)의 OHRQoL을 직접 비교한 **controlled clinical trial**로, 해당 축을 case-report tier에서 controlled-study tier로 격상시키는 첫 근거다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective controlled clinical trial, n=34 (CA 20, FA 14), skeletal Class III patients treated with Surgery-First Approach (bimaxillary: LeFort I + BSSO) using clear aligners (Invisalign) vs conventional fixed appliances, OHRQoL measured via OQLQ, OHIP-14, SF-36 at T0 (pre-tx)/T1 (1wk post-appliance)/T2 (4wk post-surgery)/T3 (6mo post-surgery).

Both groups showed significant progressive intra-group OHRQoL improvement over time (most domains significant by T2–T3), but no statistically significant between-group difference in OQLQ, OHIP-14, or SF-36 scores at any timepoint (all p>0.05); OHIP-14 intra-group improvement over time reached significance only in the CA group (p=0.02 vs FA p=0.1).

Clinical implication: appliance choice (aligner vs fixed) does not appear to differentially affect OHRQoL trajectory in SFA-treated skeletal Class III patients — both are viable — though small unequal group sizes (20 vs 14, non-randomized consecutive allocation) and single-center Brazilian-Portuguese-questionnaire design limit generalizability.

## 세줄요약

전향적 대조임상연구, n=34 (CA 20명, FA 14명), 골격성 Class III 환자를 Surgery-First Approach(SFA, 양악: LeFort I + BSSO)로 치료하며 투명교정(Invisalign) vs 고정식장치를 비교, OHRQoL을 OQLQ·OHIP-14·SF-36으로 T0(치료전)/T1(장치장착 1주)/T2(수술후 4주)/T3(수술후 6개월) 측정.

두 군 모두 시간에 따라 유의한 군내 OHRQoL 개선을 보였으나(대부분 도메인이 T2~T3에 유의) 군간 비교에서는 OQLQ·OHIP-14·SF-36 어떤 시점에서도 유의차 없음(모두 p>0.05); OHIP-14 군내 시간경과 개선은 CA군만 유의(p=0.02, FA군 p=0.1).

임상적 의미: SFA로 치료받는 골격성 Class III 환자에서 장치 종류(투명교정 vs 고정식)가 OHRQoL 궤적에 유의한 차이를 만들지 않음 — 둘 다 실행 가능한 선택지 — 다만 불균등 표본크기(20 vs 14), 비무작위 순차배정, 단일기관·브라질 포르투갈어 설문 등 한계로 일반화에 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study identified in this wiki to directly compare CA vs FA OHRQoL specifically within a **Surgery-First Approach** skeletal Class III cohort using three validated questionnaires simultaneously (OQLQ, OHIP-14, SF-36 — Brazilian Portuguese validated versions).
- Reports the first known use of SF-36 alongside orthognathic-specific instruments in aligner-treated orthognathic-surgery patients (per the authors' claim).
- Provides a controlled (non-randomized, consecutive-allocation) comparator, upgrading the wiki's Class III aligner evidence tier from single case reports to a group-comparison prospective trial.
- Documents a CA-specific SFA protocol detail: cutouts planned in the first 10 aligners for buttons (molar-to-canine, both arches) to maximize trans-/post-surgical stability, plus post-surgical accelerated aligner-change interval (every 5 days for first 3 months, then standard 10-day cycle).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective controlled clinical trial (registered REBEC + ClinicalTrials.gov), consecutive (non-randomized) allocation — CA-eligible patients enrolled first until minimum sample reached, then subsequent eligible patients allocated to FA.
- **Sample**: CA group n=20 (11F/9M, mean age 23.3y); FA group n=14 (5F/9M, mean age 19.9y); 3 women initially in CA excluded (relocation, poor cooperation, lost interest). Sample size calculated via Harvard calculator: MCID 12 points, SD 10.5, α=0.05, power 80% → 14/group minimum.
- **Inclusion**: no growth potential (CS6), skeletal Class III by negative Wits, bimaxillary surgery indicated (maxillary deficiency/mandibular excess/combination).
- **Exclusion**: craniofacial anomaly/prior facial fracture, TMJ issues, periodontal disease affecting compensations, extraction need (except 3rd molars), curve of Spee >6 mm, mandibular asymmetry >6 mm, contraindicating medication.
- **Surgical protocol**: LeFort I osteotomy + bilateral sagittal split osteotomy (BSSO); genioplasty in 6 CA and 5 FA patients; no maxillary segmentation; all surgeries by one surgeon (H.M.S).
- **Appliance protocols**: CA = Invisalign (ClinCheck), buttons cut out for elastics in first 10 aligners, attachments (Grandioso resin, VOCO) placed post-surgical-guide confirmation, 5-day change cycle × 3 months → standard 10-day cycle. FA = 0.022"/0.028" Roth pre-adjusted brackets (American Orthodontics), 0.014" annealed NiTi presurgical (no dental movement, no surgical guide use pre-op). Both: appliances installed 1–2 weeks pre-surgery; intermaxillary fixation screws + post-surgical guide with elastics for 2–3 weeks.
- **Outcome instruments**: OQLQ-22 (Brazilian Portuguese validated, B-OQLQ), OHIP-14 (Brazilian Portuguese validated), SF-36 (Brazilian Portuguese validated); also IOTN-AC (professional [AC-E] and self-perceived [AC-A] aesthetic component) and Wits/IGC for group-comparability check.
- **Timepoints**: T0 (pre-treatment), T1 (1wk post-appliance install), T2 (4wk post-surgery), T3 (6mo post-surgery).
- **Statistics**: JAMOVI v2.3.9; Friedman test (within-group, non-parametric paired) + Durbin-Conover post hoc; Mann-Whitney (between-group, independent); Shapiro-Wilk normality check; significance α=0.05. Examiner reliability: weighted Kappa (IGC, excellent 0.943 both groups) and Wilcoxon signed-rank (IOTN AC-E reassessment).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Baseline comparability**: no significant between-group differences in Wits (−9.5 CA vs −8.2 FA, p=0.45), IGC (37.1 vs 35.6, p=0.83), or IOTN AC-E (6.1 vs 5.2, p=0.25) — groups were matched in case severity despite non-randomized allocation.
- **OQLQ**: both groups improved significantly over time (Friedman p<0.001 each); no between-group difference at any timepoint (all Mann-Whitney p>0.05). Domain-level: facial aesthetics improved significantly by T2 in both groups; oral function improved only by T3 in both; aesthetic awareness improved at T2 in FA and at T2+T3 in CA; social aspects improved at T2+T3 in both.
- **OHIP-14**: both groups improved over time descriptively, but intra-group Friedman significance reached only in CA (p=0.02) not FA (p=0.1); no between-group difference at any timepoint (all p>0.05). Domain-level mixed pattern across functional limitation, physical pain, physical/psychological disability, social disability, handicap (see source table for per-domain p-values).
- **SF-36**: no between-group differences at any timepoint (all p>0.05); physical functioning and role-physical improved significantly by T3 in both groups; role-physical notably dipped at T2 (post-surgical low point) then recovered by T3 in both groups; bodily pain increased at T2 then decreased at T3 in CA only; general health improved at T2 in both.
- **IOTN-AC**: both professional (AC-E) and self-perceived (AC-A) aesthetic scores improved significantly over time in both groups (p<0.001) with no between-group difference at any timepoint.
- Overall pattern: **appliance type (CA vs FA) did not differentiate OHRQoL trajectory** — both converge on similar improvement magnitude and timing (most gains consolidating by 6-month post-surgical mark), consistent with prior literature (Alfawal, Leyva) cited in Discussion.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Non-randomized, consecutive (not simultaneous-random) allocation — introduces potential selection bias (patients choosing/eligible for CA enrolled first).
- Unequal, moderate group sizes (20 vs 14) — meets calculated minimum but limited power for subgroup/domain-level comparisons.
- Single-center design (UERJ/HUPE, Brazil), questionnaires validated only in Brazilian Portuguese — cross-cultural/health-system generalizability unclear.
- No comparator against non-surgical CAT-only or FA-only camouflage cases; strictly SFA context.
- 6-month follow-up endpoint — long-term (≥1–2y) OHRQoL trajectory not captured (contrast with FA-only 2-year studies cited, e.g. Cordeiro et al.).
- Retrieved full text truncated at Discussion section (~32,700 characters) — Conclusions/full Discussion text beyond that point not captured in this source note; Abstract conclusions were used to confirm the bottom line.

## 6. Related Work

Cites and contrasts with: Alfawal et al. and Leyva et al. (both reporting improved QoL in aligner-treated groups, Leyva without domain-level breakdown), Pelo et al. and Gao et al. (pre-surgical QoL decline, post-surgical recovery pattern), Cordeiro et al. and Johanson et al. (FA-only long-term OQLQ improvement over 2y/24mo), Antonio-Zancajo et al. (aligner users report less chewing discomfort, lower psychological impact vs FA). Positions itself as filling a gap: "no studies have directly compared both modalities specifically within the context of orthognathic surgery" prior to this trial.

## 7. Glossary

- **SFA (Surgery-First Approach)**: orthognathic protocol performing surgery before/without presurgical orthodontic decompensation.
- **CA / FA**: clear aligner / fixed appliance.
- **OQLQ (Orthognathic Quality of Life Questionnaire)**: 22-item instrument specific to orthognathic-surgery patients, covering facial aesthetics, oral function, aesthetic awareness, social aspects.
- **OHIP-14 (Oral Health Impact Profile-14)**: 14-item general oral-health-related quality-of-life instrument, 7 domains (functional limitation, physical pain, psychological discomfort, physical/psychological disability, social disability, handicap).
- **SF-36**: 36-item general health-related quality-of-life instrument, 8 domains (physical functioning, role-physical, bodily pain, general health, vitality, social functioning, role-emotional, mental health).
- **IOTN-AC**: Index of Orthodontic Treatment Need, Aesthetic Component (AC-E = professional/examiner rating, AC-A = patient self-rating).
- **IGC**: Index of Orthodontic Treatment Complexity/Grading (severity index used for group-comparability check).
- **BSSO**: bilateral sagittal split osteotomy (mandibular ramus).
- **LeFort I osteotomy**: maxillary osteotomy for repositioning.
