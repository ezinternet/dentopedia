## Why Ingested

This paper is not dentistry-specific, but it directly informs [[overviews/drug-analgesics-postop-pain-overview]] — the wiki's synthesis of postoperative analgesic strategy — by extending the evidence to the IV route (oral-surgery sedation/GA settings where oral NSAID dosing is not an option) and to non-dental multimodal-analgesia guidelines the wiki did not previously hold. It reinforces the wiki's existing NNT-anchored first-line recommendation (ibuprofen + acetaminophen) with an independent line of evidence: 20–60% postoperative opioid-sparing from IV NSAIDs, and preemptive dosing benefit consistent with [[drug/analgesics/costa-2015-preemptive-nsaids-third-molar-pain-meta]] and [[drug/analgesics/gousias-2025-preemptive-analgesia-periodontal-implant-sr-ma]].

## Three-line Summary

This narrative review synthesized literature from the past 10 years (up to May 2024) on intravenous (IV) NSAIDs for perioperative pain management in adults and children, focusing on ibuprofen, ketorolac, ketoprofen, naproxen, paracetamol, and acetylsalicylic acid, with emphasis on European approvals and practice.

IV NSAIDs reduce postoperative opioid consumption by approximately 20–60%, with IV ibuprofen demonstrating a favorable profile for opioid-sparing and pain relief; head-to-head trials remain limited but available data suggest IV ibuprofen had comparable or superior efficacy to IV paracetamol and ketorolac in several surgical settings.

The review supports IV NSAIDs as a cornerstone of multimodal analgesia; however, the lack of robust head-to-head comparative RCTs and limited pediatric data (particularly for infants under 6 years) represent significant evidence gaps that future research should address.

## 세줄요약

본 내러티브 리뷰는 성인과 소아에서 수술 전후 통증 관리에 사용되는 정맥 내 비스테로이드성 소염진통제(IV NSAIDs)에 관한 최근 10년간(2024년 5월까지)의 문헌을 종합하였으며, ibuprofen, ketorolac, ketoprofen, naproxen, paracetamol, acetylsalicylic acid를 중심으로 유럽의 승인 현황과 임상 관행을 검토하였다.

IV NSAIDs는 수술 후 아편유사제(opioid) 사용을 약 20–60% 감소시키며, IV ibuprofen은 아편유사제 절감 효과와 통증 완화 측면에서 유리한 프로파일을 보였다; 직접 비교 임상시험은 제한적이나, IV ibuprofen은 여러 수술 환경에서 IV paracetamol이나 ketorolac과 비교하여 동등하거나 우수한 효능을 나타내는 자료가 보고되었다.

본 리뷰는 IV NSAIDs를 다중양식 진통(multimodal analgesia)의 핵심으로 지지하지만, 강력한 직접 비교 무작위대조시험(RCT)의 부족 및 6세 미만 영유아에 대한 제한된 소아 데이터는 향후 연구가 해결해야 할 주요 근거 공백으로 지적되었다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- IV NSAIDs reduce postoperative opioid consumption by approximately 20-60% based on systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
- IV ibuprofen demonstrated a more favorable profile for peri- and postoperative opioid sparing and pain relief compared to other IV NSAIDs.
- IV ibuprofen and IV paracetamol show similar frequencies of significant adverse events (13.7% vs 14.5%, respectively, from Moore et al.'s PAIN study).
- IV ibuprofen is the preferred IV NSAID for pediatric patients, approved in France for children >6 years or >20 kg, with off-label use extending to younger children.
- Pre-emptive single-dose IV ibuprofen reduced 24-hour opioid consumption by up to 45% in laparoscopic cholecystectomy.
- Fixed-dose combination of IV ibuprofen and paracetamol showed superior pain relief (SPID48: 23.4 mm) compared to either drug alone (9.5-10.4 mm) in bunionectomy.
- Continuous IV infusion of NSAIDs may offer opioid-sparing benefits, with morphine PCA use reduced by 25% with continuous ketorolac vs bolus administration.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Narrative review, non-systematic
- Databases: MEDLINE (via PubMed)
- Search window: 10 years prior to 1 May 2024 (with consideration of relevant French-language publications)
- Inclusion criteria: Clinical studies on IV NSAIDs for perioperative pain; full-text available
- Exclusion criteria: Case reports, conference abstracts, oral abstracts, in vitro studies, unavailable full-text
- Studies screened: Titles and abstracts screened by one investigator (A.M.-S.); full-texts assessed by all investigators
- Data extraction: Not formalized; narrative synthesis of efficacy, safety, and pharmacokinetic data
- Quality assessment: Not performed (narrative review)
- Primary focus: Ibuprofen, ketorolac, ketoprofen, naproxen, paracetamol, acetylsalicylic acid (European-approved IV formulations)
- Pediatric considerations: Reviewed separately due to age-specific dosing, limited approvals, and off-label use

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Postoperative opioid reduction (IV NSAIDs vs placebo) —** 20–60% reduction (systematic reviews/meta-analyses). IV ibuprofen specifically: 24-hour morphine consumption 17.6 ± 3.2 mg vs 19.7 ± 3.0 mg in cervical cancer surgery (p = 0.04); total morphine consumption 11.14–11.29 mg vs 14.51 ± 9.19 mg in mixed surgery (p = 0.0011–0.0014).
- **Pain scores (IV ibuprofen vs placebo) —** VAS significantly lower at 24 hours (1 ± 1.05 vs 2 ± 2.25 for placebo in total hip replacement; p = 0.006); SPID48 9.5 mm vs −1.3 mm for placebo in bunionectomy (p < 0.001).
- **IV ibuprofen vs IV paracetamol —** Pain scores lower with ibuprofen (at 24h: 1.16 ± 0.79 vs 0.26 ± 0.44 in laparoscopic cholecystectomy; p < 0.001); opioid use reduced (215.66 ± 97.26 vs 342.33 ± 65.59; p < 0.001). Network meta-analysis: IV ibuprofen mean difference −1.54 at 0 min and −0.36 at 24h vs IV paracetamol.
- **Adverse events —** Similar frequency for ibuprofen and paracetamol (13.7% vs 14.5%); higher for aspirin (18.7%). Nausea/vomiting incidence lower with ibuprofen vs paracetamol (p < 0.05).
- **Pre-emptive IV ibuprofen —** 24-hour opioid consumption: 303.33 ± 132.08 mg vs 553.00 ± 257.04 mg for placebo in laparoscopic cholecystectomy (p < 0.001); 45% reduction.
- **Continuous infusion —** Morphine PCA use 25% lower with continuous ketorolac vs bolus at similar cumulative doses.
- **Pediatric data —** Rescue fentanyl 18% lower with IV ibuprofen vs placebo (p = 0.043) in tonsillectomy; proportion requiring fentanyl 42% vs 62% (p = 0.021).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative, non-systematic review; not a formal systematic review or meta-analysis.
- Centered on practice in France and Europe; may not generalize to all healthcare systems.
- Five included studies were conducted during or after the COVID-19 pandemic, with potential confounding related to NSAID use and COVID-19 outcomes.
- Scarcity of head-to-head RCTs comparing different IV NSAIDs limits direct comparisons.
- Pediatric data, particularly for infants under 6 years, are limited; most studies involve older children.
- Short observation periods in many studies limit detection of medium- and long-term adverse events.
- Heterogeneity in study outcomes and lack of standardized pain and function measures.

## 6. Related Work

- Martinez et al. 2017 — Network meta-analysis of 135 trials: paracetamol + NSAID/nefopam superior to single agents for opioid reduction.
- Koh et al. 2015 — Review: IV ibuprofen and IV paracetamol both associated with significant opioid sparing and good tolerability.
- Southworth et al. 2020 — Narrative summary of 9 studies (1062 adults): IV ibuprofen reduces pain, opioid use, fatigue, and surgical stress response.
- Zhou et al. 2023 — Meta-analysis of 32 studies (3716 participants): IV ibuprofen superior to placebo and paracetamol for postoperative pain.
- Pessano et al. 2024 — Cochrane review: moderate-certainty evidence for ibuprofen reducing pain <2h post-surgery in children (SMD -1.12, 95% CI -1.39 to -0.86).

## 7. Glossary

- **NSAID (Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug)**: 비스테로이드성 소염진통제 — 염증을 억제하고 통증을 완화하는 약물로, COX 효소를 억제하여 작용.
- **COX (Cyclooxygenase)**: 시클로옥시게나제 — 아라키돈산을 프로스타글란딘으로 전환하는 효소. COX-1은 구성적 발현, COX-2는 염증 시 유도됨.
- **IV (Intravenous)**: 정맥 내 — 약물을 정맥을 통해 직접 투여하는 경로.
- **Multimodal analgesia**: 다중양식 진통 — 다양한 수용체와 경로를 표적으로 하는 여러 진통제를 병용하는 전략.
- **SmPC (Summary of Product Characteristics)**: 제품 특성 요약 — 유럽에서 승인된 의약품의 공식 정보 문서.
- **Opioid-sparing effect**: 아편유사제 절감 효과 — 다른 진통제 사용으로 아편유사제 필요량이 감소하는 현상.
- **PCA (Patient-Controlled Analgesia)**: 환자 자가조절 진통 — 환자가 스스로 진통제를 투여할 수 있는 시스템.
- **SPID48 (Sum of Pain Intensity Difference over 48 hours)**: 48시간 통증강도 차이 합계 — 진통 효과를 평가하는 복합 지표.
