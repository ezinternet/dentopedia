## Why Ingested

기존 위키는 트라넥삼산 (Tranexamic Acid, TXA)을 **양치액(mouthwash)** 형태로만 보유한다 — [[drug/anticoagulants/inchingolo-2024-anticoagulation-therapy-dental-practice-sr]]는 TXA 양치액을 1차 국소지혈제로 규정하고, [[oral-surgery/dinkova-2025-local-hemostasis-oral-surgery-review]]는 양치액의 출혈 감소 효과를 보고한다. 본 scoping review는 그 아래 층위인 **정제를 분쇄한 분말/페이스트를 술중 발치와에 직접 적용**하는 제형을 처음으로 종합하여, 상용 5% TXA 양치액을 구할 수 없는 진료 환경(국내 포함)에서 실행 가능한 프로토콜(250–500 mg 정제 + 생리식염수 페이스트 + 흡수성 스폰지)을 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

PRISMA-ScR scoping review (search October 2024, six databases, 1,127 records screened) of topical tranexamic acid (TXA) powder prepared from crushed tablets — not commercial mouthwash — applied at the surgical site during minor oral surgery.

Ten studies were included (3 retrospective, 2 clinical trials, 2 prospective cohorts, 1 case-control, 1 case report, 1 letter to the editor); TXA doses ranged 250–500 mg per application (six studies used 250-mg tablets, three used 500-mg), most commonly crushed and mixed with saline into a paste used to soak a resorbable sponge placed in the alveolar socket, and all ten studies reported successful bleeding control in patients on anticoagulant/antiplatelet therapy or with hemophilia, Glanzmann thrombasthenia, or liver disease.

The clinically actionable protocol — a crushed 250-mg TXA tablet in saline soaking a resorbable sponge intraoperatively, plus one tablet in 100 mL cold saline as a rinse four times daily for seven days — is cheap and available where 5% commercial mouthwash is not, but TXA was almost always combined with sutures, sponges, and compression, so its isolated effect remains unquantified.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA-ScR scoping review(2024년 10월 검색, 6개 데이터베이스, 1,127건 스크리닝) — 상용 양치액이 아닌 **정제를 분쇄한 트라넥삼산 (Tranexamic Acid, TXA) 분말**을 소수술 술부에 직접 적용한 근거를 처음으로 종합.

10편 포함(후향 3, 임상시험 2, 전향 코호트 2, 환자-대조 1, 증례보고 1, 편집자 서한 1); 용량은 회당 250–500 mg(250 mg 정제 6편, 500 mg 정제 3편), 가장 흔한 술식은 **분쇄 정제 + 생리식염수 페이스트로 흡수성 스폰지를 적셔 발치와에 충전**하는 방법이며, 항응고·항혈소판 치료 중이거나 혈우병·글란츠만 혈소판무력증·간질환이 있는 고위험 환자 전 10편에서 지혈 성공을 보고.

임상 적용 프로토콜은 **술중: 250 mg 정제 분쇄 + 생리식염수 페이스트로 흡수성 스폰지 적심 / 술후: 정제 1알을 냉각 생리식염수 100 mL에 녹여 1일 4회 7일간 양치** — 상용 5% 양치액을 구할 수 없는 환경에서 저비용 대안이 되지만, 거의 모든 연구가 봉합·스폰지·압박과 병용했으므로 **TXA 단독 효과는 아직 정량되지 않았다**(scoping review이므로 통합분석 없음).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First synthesis focused specifically on **locally applied TXA powder prepared from crushed tablets** — a formulation deliberately separated from the well-studied ready-to-use 5% TXA mouthwash, which was an explicit exclusion criterion.
- Assembles the actual application protocols (dose, vehicle, carrier, timing) from ten heterogeneous reports into one comparable table, making the technique reproducible chairside.
- Frames powder TXA as a cost and access solution: tablets are substantially cheaper than intravenous preparations and widely available where commercial 4.8–5% mouthwash is not.
- Documents the pharmacokinetic rationale for topical use — plasma TXA after topical application is **less than one tenth** of intravenous levels, and oral 1-g dosing yields no detectable salivary TXA while a 10 mL rinse of 5% solution inhibits fibrinolysis in saliva for hours.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: scoping review reported per PRISMA-ScR; protocol pre-registered on OSF (https://osf.io/n5mbp).
- **Search**: October 2024, four independent calibrated reviewers; MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, Embase, BVSalud, Web of Science, Periódicos CAPES; reference lists hand-screened; no language, date, or geographic restriction. Screening in Rayyan; deduplication in EndNote.
- **Focused question**: efficacy of topical TXA powder in controlling bleeding and preventing hemorrhagic complications following dental surgery.
- **Inclusion**: clinical studies, case reports, and letters to the editor in oral surgery patients reporting topical use of **crushed TXA tablets**.
- **Exclusion**: reviews, conference abstracts, editorials, in vitro and animal studies; **ready-to-use mouthwash solutions, injectable, and systemic TXA**; major surgery (cardiovascular, orthopedic).
- **Selection**: two independent blinded reviewers, third reviewer for disagreements.
- **Extracted fields**: author, year, country, design, sample size/groups, procedure type, TXA reference, application method, bleeding outcome.
- **Risk of bias**: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale for cohort/case-control/prospective/retrospective (7–9 stars = low, 4–6 = moderate, <4 = high); JBI Critical Appraisal Checklists for clinical trials and case reports (≥70% criteria = low risk, 40–69% = moderate, <40% = high).
- **No quantitative synthesis** — no pooling, no effect estimate, no heterogeneity assessment (by design, scoping review).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Study flow**: 1,127 records identified (Scopus 496, Web of Science 245, BVSalud 205, PubMed 143, Periódicos CAPES 25, Embase 13) → 22 duplicates removed → 1,105 screened → 1,057 excluded on title/abstract → 48 eligible → 2 excluded → 46 full texts assessed → 36 excluded → **10 studies included**.

**Designs included**: 3 retrospective, 2 clinical trials, 2 prospective cohorts, 1 case-control, 1 case report, 1 letter to the editor.

**Dose**: six studies used 250-mg TXA tablets; three used 500-mg tablets; one did not specify concentration. Range reported as **250–500 mg per application**.

**Application techniques** (the clinically actionable part):

- **Paste into the socket** (most common): crushed 250-mg tablet mixed with saline, used to soak a resorbable sponge (RS) filling the alveolar socket — Rocha 2017, Rocha 2019, Rocha 2020, Souza 2022.
- **Paste directly into the alveolus without a sponge**: crushed 250-mg tablet + saline — Buhatem Medeiros 2017.
- **Gelfoam impregnated with TXA powder**, socket repacked — Lu 2018 (Taiwan).
- **Crushed 500-mg tablets on wet cotton wool** over the extraction site, patient bites for **30 minutes** — Coetzee 2007, hemophilia (n=30), resource-limited setting.
- **Gauze soaked in 500 mg TXA in saline** for local pressure, plus RS in socket — Blinder 1999.
- **Powdered 500-mg tablet + saline paste into socket**, delivered on a soft plate/splint — Bhavyaa 2021, 7-year-old with Glanzmann thrombasthenia.

**Postoperative rinse regimens described** (adjunct, not the review's index intervention):

- One tablet in **100 mL cold saline, 4×/day for 7 days** (3 studies — Rocha 2017/2019/2020, Souza 2022).
- **500 mg, 2 minutes, 4×/day for 4 days** (Blinder 1999).
- **3 minutes every 6 hours for 7 days** (Bernardoni-Socorro 1998).
- **500 mg in 10 mL water (5%), 6×/day for 2 days preoperatively** (Bhavyaa 2021).

**Populations**: every participant had a coagulation disorder — antiplatelet therapy, vitamin K antagonists (VKA), or direct oral anticoagulants (DOACs); plus hemophilia (n=30), Glanzmann thrombasthenia (n=1), and liver disease (n=47).

**Procedures**: 8 of 10 studies exclusively dental extraction; 1 also periodontal therapy (tartar removal/curettage); 1 also oral biopsy.

**Outcome**: **all ten studies reported successful bleeding control** with topical TXA combined with adjunctive local measures, and none required interruption of anticoagulant or antiplatelet therapy. Souza 2022 additionally reported reduced hospitalization and transfusion requirements in liver-disease patients. Blinder 1999 noted severe periodontitis as an independent bleeding-risk amplifier.

**Contextual figures cited in discussion** (not this review's own data): systemic TXA reduced transfusion likelihood by ~one third across 129 RCTs; a separate topical-TXA review pooled 28 trials across orthopedics, cardiology, otolaryngology, and dentistry; an SR of 430 anticoagulated patients found local TXA significantly reduced postoperative bleeding risk. Tablet-derived rinse solutions remain stable up to **31 days** at 23°C or 5°C.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **No isolated effect of TXA can be extracted** — the review's own stated central limitation. In nearly every included study TXA powder was co-administered with resorbable sponges, Gelfoam, sutures, and mechanical compression, so the counterfactual is a bundle, not TXA alone.
- **No pooled estimate, no comparative outcome numbers.** As a scoping review it maps protocols; it does not report bleeding incidence percentages, relative risk, or number needed to treat for powder TXA.
- **Weak evidence base**: 10 studies including a case report and a letter to the editor; only 2 clinical trials, none apparently a modern blinded RCT of powder TXA vs placebo. Risk of bias was appraised (NOS, JBI) but the per-study ratings are reported only in a supplementary table not present in the retrieved text.
- **One study did not report TXA concentration**, and vehicle volumes are inconsistent (10 mL saline vs 100 mL, saline vs water), so "250–500 mg" is a dose per application, not a standardized concentration.
- **Heterogeneous populations** (drug-induced vs hereditary vs hepatic coagulopathy) pooled descriptively; whether the protocol transfers across these mechanisms is untested.
- **Publication span 1998–2022** with much of the protocol data from a single Brazilian group (Rocha et al., 4 of 10 studies), raising a concentration-of-evidence concern.
- Authors call for well-designed studies evaluating the **isolated** efficacy of locally applied TXA powder.

## 6. Related Work

- Bernardoni-Socorro et al., 1998 (Venezuela) — clinical trial, TXA vs no additional hemostatic measure in anticoagulated patients undergoing scaling/curettage/extraction.
- Blinder et al., 1999 (Israel) — three-arm trial (RS+sutures vs RS+sutures+TXA vs fibrin glue+RS+sutures), n=150.
- Coetzee, 2007 (South Africa) — letter to the editor, crushed 500-mg tablets on cotton wool in 30 hemophilia patients; cost-effectiveness argument.
- Buhatem Medeiros et al., 2017 (Brazil) — case-control, dual antiplatelet therapy maintained during extraction.
- Rocha et al., 2017 / 2019 / 2020 (Brazil) — retrospective and two prospective cohorts covering antiplatelet, VKA, and DOAC patients; source of the 250-mg-paste-on-sponge protocol.
- Lu et al., 2018 (Taiwan) — retrospective, antiplatelet (n=183) and VKA (n=60); Gelfoam impregnated with TXA powder; warfarin interruption did not reduce bleeding risk. **This is the paper held in the wiki as [[drug/anticoagulants/lu-2018-warfarin-antiplatelet-dental-extraction-management]].**
- Bhavyaa, 2021 (India) — case report, Glanzmann thrombasthenia in a 7-year-old.
- Souza et al., 2022 (Brazil) — retrospective, 47 liver-disease patients undergoing extraction and biopsy.

## 7. Glossary

- **TXA (Tranexamic Acid, 트라넥삼산)**: synthetic lysine derivative; antifibrinolytic that competitively blocks lysine-binding sites on plasminogen, preventing plasmin formation and thereby stabilizing formed fibrin clots.
- **Topical/local TXA powder (국소 트라넥삼산 분말)**: TXA tablets crushed to powder and applied at the surgical site, alone or as a saline paste — the index intervention of this review, distinct from mouthwash.
- **RS (Resorbable Sponge, 흡수성 스폰지)**: gelatin or collagen sponge placed in the alveolar socket; here used as the carrier soaked with TXA paste.
- **Gelfoam**: absorbable gelatin sponge brand; in Lu 2018 impregnated with TXA powder before socket repacking.
- **PRISMA-ScR**: Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses Extension for Scoping Reviews — reporting standard for scoping (mapping) reviews, which do not pool data.
- **NOS (Newcastle-Ottawa Scale, 뉴캐슬-오타와 척도)**: risk-of-bias tool for non-randomized studies scoring selection, comparability, outcome; 7–9 stars = low risk.
- **JBI (Joanna Briggs Institute) Critical Appraisal Checklist**: appraisal instrument for trials and case reports; ≥70% of criteria met = low risk of bias.
- **VKA (Vitamin K Antagonist, 비타민 K 길항제)**: e.g., warfarin; monitored by INR.
- **DOAC (Direct Oral Anticoagulant, 직접경구항응고제)**: dabigatran, rivaroxaban, apixaban, edoxaban.
- **GT (Glanzmann Thrombasthenia, 글란츠만 혈소판무력증)**: hereditary platelet glycoprotein IIb/IIIa defect causing impaired aggregation and mucocutaneous bleeding.
