## Why Ingested

Second human RCT on progressive loading after [[implants/loading-protocol/appleton-2005-a-radiographic-assessment-of-progressive]], and the only one in the wiki that uses a within-patient (split-mouth) design with micro-thread implants. Fills the human-evidence gap next to the dog studies [[occlusion/podaropoulos-2016-bone-reactions-progressive-static-load-dogs]] and the Cochrane statement in [[implants/loading-protocol/esposito-2013-loading-times-dental-implants-cochrane]] that no progressive-loading trials were identified.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized split-mouth trial, 10 patients (20 Astra Tech micro-thread implants, posterior maxilla, D3/D4 bone): each patient received one progressively loaded and one conventionally loaded single implant crown; follow-up 12 months.

Crestal bone loss at 12 months was 0.11 (SD 0.19) mm progressive vs 0.36 (SD 0.36) mm conventional (between-group P = 0.021); peri-implant density rose in all zones over time and trended higher with progressive loading but the between-group difference was not significant (P = 0.433).

Progressive loading reduced crestal bone loss in this small trial, but n = 10, 12-month 2D radiographic endpoints and no survival data limit any clinical claim.

## 세줄요약

상악 구치부(D3/D4 골질) 단일 임플란트에서 점진적 부하와 일반 부하를 비교한 무작위 분할구강 시험: 환자 10명, Astra Tech 마이크로스레드 임플란트 20개, 한 환자에서 양 방식을 하나씩 적용, 12개월 추적.

12개월 치조정 골소실은 점진적 부하 0.11 (SD 0.19) mm, 일반 부하 0.36 (SD 0.36) mm로 군간 유의 (P = 0.021); 골밀도는 점진적 부하에서 높은 경향이었으나 군간 차이는 유의하지 않았다 (P = 0.433).

점진적 부하가 치조정 골소실을 줄이는 방향이지만, 표본 10명·12개월·2차원 방사선 지표·생존율 부재로 임상적 결론은 제한적이다.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal of Dentistry (Tehran, Iran) 2013;10(2):155-63; PMID 23724215; PMCID PMC3666076. DOI not listed in PubMed/PMC record.
- Tehran University of Medical Sciences, Department of Implantology; ethics board approved.
- Registered type: prospective randomized controlled trial (split-mouth).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Within-patient comparison removes between-patient differences in occlusal force, diet and habits (the authors' stated rationale).
- Quantifies staged loading for micro-thread implants with a stent-standardized radiographic method.
- Reports bone density in nine ROIs normalized to a reference ROI.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Inclusion: bilateral single edentulous sites (or unilateral with pier abutment) in posterior maxilla, canine guidance, D3/D4 bone, good hygiene, natural or tooth-borne opposing occlusion. Exclusion: diabetes, osteoporosis, radiotherapy, periodontitis, bruxism/clenching, smoking.
- 11 enrolled, 1 excluded (pregnancy) → 10 patients (8 F, 2 M, mean age 40.5); 20 implants (Astra Tech OsseoSpeed MicroThread 4.5 × 11 mm, insertion torque ≥ 30 N/cm [as written in paper]).
- Both implants uncovered at 4 weeks; abutments at 6 weeks.
- Conventional: metal-ceramic crown at 8 weeks after surgery.
- Progressive: three heat-cured acrylic temporary crowns — 2 months (2 mm infraocclusion, soft diet), 4 months (occlusal contact adjusted with 40 µm foil, firmer diet), 6 months (full occlusion, 12 µm foil, regular diet); metal-ceramic crown at 8 months.
- Radiographs: 100 periapical RVG images at 2, 4, 6, 8, 12 months with occlusal stent and XCP aiming device; normalized crestal bone loss (NCBL = 11 × MCBL / MIH); density = normalized average intensity in 9 ROIs.
- Statistics: Friedman (within group), Wilcoxon signed-rank (between groups), repeated-measures ANOVA (density); α = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Progressive | Conventional | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crestal bone loss at 12 months, mean (SD) | 0.11 (0.19) mm | 0.36 (0.36) mm | 0.021 (between groups) |
| Change over time within group | bone loss stopped after 6 months, slight fill at 12 months | significant loss | 0.791 (progressive, NS); 0.012 (conventional) |
| Peri-implant bone density (crestal, middle, apical) | higher at all areas | lower | 0.433 (NS) |
| Density change over time | significant vs month 2 in all areas, both groups | same | < 0.001 |

Comparison cited by the authors: Appleton 2005 reported 0.2 vs 0.59 mm.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- n = 10 patients, 12-month follow-up; no implant survival, prosthetic or patient-reported outcomes.
- Randomization/allocation concealment/blinding of radiographic readers not described in the text read. [미검증 — not found in the extracted text]
- Progressive arm also differs in crown material (acrylic provisional for 8 months) and diet instruction, so staging, material and diet are confounded.
- Conventional crowns were loaded at 8 weeks, i.e., early loading, not a delayed-loading control.
- 2D periapical measurements only; selection limited to D3/D4 posterior maxilla, non-smokers, no bruxism.
- The paper writes torque in N/cm² and cites Misch (1980) for the concept; no independent verification here.
- Authors recommend larger, longer, compromised-bone and long-span studies.

## 7. Glossary

- NCBL: normalized crestal bone loss, scaled to the known 11 mm implant length.
- NAI: normalized average intensity of an ROI relative to the reference ROI; proxy for bone density.
- Split-mouth (within-patient) design: each patient receives both interventions at different sites.
