## Why Ingested
Graftless 상악동거상술의 공간 유지 문제를 해결하는 신규 RCT로 인제스트. 기존 [[sinus-lift/lateral/fettouh-2025-graftless-lateral-transcrestal-sinus-rct]]는 implant tenting에 의존했는데, 이 논문은 생분해성 스크류를 공간 유지 장치로 사용해 이식재 없이 골형성을 달성 — 조직학적으로 xenograft군과 동등한 신생골 형성률 달성(37.3% vs 35.2%, p=0.324).

## One-line Summary
RCT (n=14, RBH ≤4mm): graftless lateral sinus lift with bioabsorbable screws (Inion FreedomScrews™) achieved equivalent histomorphometric NBF (37.3% vs 35.2%, p=0.324) compared to xenograft control, despite significantly lower radiographic bone height gain (8.43 vs 12.07mm, p<0.001).

## 한줄요약
RCT(n=14, 잔존골 ≤4mm): 생분해성 스크류 공간유지 graftless 상악동거상술 — 신생골 형성률 37.3%(이식재군 35.2%와 통계적 동등, p=0.324)이나 방사선학적 골높이는 이식재군이 유의하게 높음(12.07 vs 8.43mm, p<0.001).

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT using bioabsorbable screws (Inion FreedomScrews™) as exclusive space-maintaining device in lateral window graftless sinus lift
- Demonstrates histomorphometric equivalence (NBF 37.3% vs 35.2%, p=0.324) between graftless+screw approach and xenograft — bone quality comparable
- Identifies critical limitation: lower radiographic bone height (8.43 vs 12.07mm) despite equivalent histology — space maintenance remains the bottleneck
- Provides mechanistic insight: graftless sites show no residual foreign particles (unlike xenograft with DBBM remnants), potentially favoring remodeling
- Eliminates second surgery for hardware removal (vs titanium non-resorbable devices)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Double-blinded, parallel-group RCT (CONSORT 2010)
- n=14 total: 7 xenograft (control), 7 graftless with bioabsorbable screws (intervention)
- Demographics: 12F/2M, mean age 42.4±7.4 years (range 35–60)
- Inclusion: RBH ≤4mm, no sinus pathology, no systemic disease
- Surgical approach: Lateral window (both groups)
- Control: DBBM xenograft (UBGEN S.r.l., Italy) + collagen membrane
- Intervention: Two bioabsorbable screws (Inion FreedomScrews™, 2.0mm diameter × 9–11mm) fixed lateral-medially above osteotomy border to tent Schneiderian membrane + collagen membrane
- Imaging: CBCT (Papaya 3D Plus) preop and 8 months postop; fused with OnDemand3D software
- Histomorphometry: Trephine core biopsy at 8 months (3.0mm OD, 10mm); H&E staining; ImageJ analysis at ×10 magnification
- Implant placement: At 8 months, simultaneous with biopsy; all 14 sites achieved primary stability
- Statistics: Shapiro-Wilk, Paired T-test (intragroup), Unpaired T-test (intergroup)

- Xenograft group: residual DBBM particles visible in histological sections
- Graftless group: clean mature bone, no foreign material retention

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample size (n=7 per group) — limited statistical power
- 8-month follow-up only — no implant survival data reported
- Bioabsorbable screw resorption timing may not be optimal for all patients (premature collapse risk)
- Non-inferior radiographic bone height despite equivalent histology suggests space maintenance remains the bottleneck
- Future: larger cohorts; longer follow-up; alternative adjunctive stabilization (e.g., PRF + bioabsorbable screws); comparison with implant-tenting graftless

## 7. Glossary
- **Inion FreedomScrews™**: Bioabsorbable (PLGA-based) screws for orthopedic/dental applications
- **DBBM**: Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral (xenograft; UBGEN brand in this study)
- **NBF (%)**: Percentage area of newly formed bone in histomorphometric ROI
- **ICC**: Intra-class correlation coefficient (0.92 — high radiographic measurement reliability)
- **Tenting**: Mechanical elevation and support of Schneiderian membrane using implants or devices

## Three-line Summary

- Double-blind RCT (n=14, 7 per group, RBH ≤4mm) comparing graftless lateral window sinus augmentation using bioabsorbable screws (Inion FreedomScrews™) as space maintainers against DBBM xenograft control; 8-month follow-up with CBCT and histomorphometry.
- Histomorphometric new bone formation was equivalent between graftless and xenograft groups (37.3% vs 35.2%, p=0.324), but radiographic bone height was significantly lower in the graftless group (8.43 vs 12.07mm, p<0.001); all 14 implants achieved primary stability.
- Schneiderian membrane has equivalent osteogenic potential to xenograft scaffolding; the limiting factor for graftless lateral sinus lift is space maintenance quantity, not bone-forming capacity.

## 세줄요약

- 이중맹검 무작위 대조 시험(Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)(n=14, 잔존골 높이(Residual Bone Height, RBH) ≤4mm): 생분해성 스크류(Inion FreedomScrews™)를 공간유지 장치로 사용한 graftless 측방창 상악동거상술 vs 탈단백 우골광물(Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM) 이종골 대조군, 8개월 CBCT·조직형태계측 추적.
- 신생골 형성률(New Bone Formation, NBF)은 두 군 동등(37.3% vs 35.2%, p=0.324)이나 방사선학적 골높이는 graftless군이 유의하게 낮음(8.43 vs 12.07mm, p<0.001); 전체 14개 임플란트 1차 안정성 달성.
- 슈나이데리안 막(Schneiderian Membrane)은 이종골이식재 버금가는 골형성 잠재력 보유; graftless 측방 상악동거상술의 제한 인자는 골형성 능력이 아닌 공간유지 정도.
