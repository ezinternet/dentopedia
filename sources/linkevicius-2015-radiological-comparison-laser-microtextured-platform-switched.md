## Why Ingested

The wiki holds the thickness-qualifier paper [[wiki/implants/mbl/linkevicius-2015-vertical-soft-tissue-thickness-crestal-bone-platform-switching]] (thin mucosa negates platform switching's bone benefit) but not its active-comparator companion in the same thin-mucosa (≤2 mm) setting. This study compares a laser-microtextured collar (Laser-Lok) head-to-head with platform switching and shows the laser advantage exists only before loading, vanishing by 1 year — an important qualifier for both [[wiki/overviews/platform-switching-marginal-bone-preservation-synthesis]] and the laser-microtextured-neck synthesis [[wiki/implants/surface/huraib-2023-micro-threaded-laser-textured-implant-neck-peri-implant]].

## Three-line Summary

Comparative clinical study (PubMed abstract only) of 30 laser-microtextured 4.6 mm implants versus 30 platform-switched 5/4 mm implants in 30 patients with thin mucosa (≤2 mm), followed radiographically to 1 year.

Laser-microtextured sites lost less crestal bone before loading — 0.71 ± 0.25 mm vs 1.02 ± 0.25 mm at 2 months (p = 0.001) and 1.10 ± 0.30 mm vs 1.37 ± 0.27 mm at restoration delivery (p = 0.001) — but the difference disappeared at 1 year (1.41 ± 0.42 mm vs 1.43 ± 0.23 mm, p = 0.976).

Neither a laser-microtextured collar nor a platform-switched connection eliminated crestal bone loss when vertical soft-tissue thickness was ≤2 mm at placement; the laser benefit is confined to the pre-loading period.

## 세줄요약

비교 임상연구(초록만 확보) — 얇은 점막(≤2 mm) 환자 30명에게 레이저 미세조직화(Laser-Microtextured) 4.6 mm 임플란트 30개와 플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching, PS) 5/4 mm 임플란트 30개를 식립하고 1년까지 방사선 추적.

부하 전에는 레이저 미세조직화군의 치조정골 소실이 유의하게 적었다 — 2개월 0.71 ± 0.25 mm vs 1.02 ± 0.25 mm(p = 0.001), 보철물 장착 시 1.10 ± 0.30 mm vs 1.37 ± 0.27 mm(p = 0.001) — 그러나 1년에는 차이가 사라졌다(1.41 ± 0.42 mm vs 1.43 ± 0.23 mm, p = 0.976).

식립 시 수직 연조직 두께가 ≤2 mm이면 레이저 미세조직화 칼라나 플랫폼 스위칭(PS) 어느 쪽도 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)을 막지 못한다 — 레이저의 이득은 부하 전 기간에 한정된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Directly compares two crestal-bone preservation strategies — laser-microtextured collar (Laser-Lok) versus platform-switched connection — **within the same thin-mucosa (≤2 mm) constraint**, where both were expected to underperform.
- Localizes the laser-microtextured advantage to the **pre-loading** healing period: significant bone sparing at 2 months and at restoration delivery, but complete convergence by 1 year.
- Confirms that a ≤2 mm vertical soft-tissue thickness at placement overrides the bone-preserving intent of both design strategies — 1-year crestal bone loss ~1.4 mm in both groups.
- Reports crestal bone loss at four radiographic time points (placement, 2 months, restoration delivery, 1 year), letting the trajectory rather than a single endpoint carry the argument.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Comparative clinical study; randomization is not stated in the abstract (evidence level `prospective`).
- **Databases**: Not applicable (clinical study, not a review).
- **n**: 30 patients (12 males, 18 females; mean age 42.3 ± 2.4 years); 30 laser-microtextured implants (Group 1) and 30 platform-switched implants (Group 2).
- **Population**: Thin mucosal tissues (≤2 mm) at implant placement.
- **Setting**: Posterior mandible, one-stage placement; after integration restored with screw-retained metal-ceramic restorations.
- **Outcomes**: Mean crestal bone loss at four radiographic examinations — after implant placement, 2 months after healing, at prosthetic restoration delivery, and after 1-year follow-up.
- **Statistics**: Mann–Whitney U-test; significance set at 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Time point | Group 1 — laser-microtextured 4.6 mm (n = 30) | Group 2 — platform-switched 5/4 mm (n = 30) | P value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 months after healing | 0.71 ± 0.25 mm SD (range, 0.25–1.6 mm) | 1.02 ± 0.25 mm SD (range, 0.6–1.55 mm) | 0.001 |
| At restorations' delivery | 1.10 ± 0.30 mm SD (range, 0.65–1.85 mm) | 1.37 ± 0.27 mm SD (range, 0.90–1.80 mm) | 0.001 |
| After 1-year follow-up | 1.41 ± 0.42 mm SD (range, +0.1–2.30 mm) | 1.43 ± 0.23 mm SD (range, 1–1.80 mm) | 0.976 |

- The between-group difference is significant at both pre-loading time points (p = 0.001 each) and favors the laser-microtextured group by 0.31 mm at 2 months and 0.27 mm at delivery.
- By 1 year the groups are statistically indistinguishable (difference 0.02 mm, p = 0.976); the Group 1 range begins at +0.1 mm, i.e. some sites gained bone, while group-level means still converge.
- Authors' conclusion, verbatim in substance: neither laser-microtexturing of the implant collar nor a platform-switched implant/abutment connection eliminated crestal bone loss when vertical soft tissue thickness was ≤2 mm at placement; laser-microtextured implants may present less proximal bone loss than platform-switching implants in the period before implant loading.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only source**: the retrieved artifact is a PubMed abstract (no PMC record, no PDF obtained) — full Methods/Results body, radiographic measurement method, and dropout accounting are not recoverable; all numbers above are from the abstract verbatim.
- Randomization, allocation concealment, and blinding are not stated in the abstract; allocation to the two implant systems may be non-random.
- Single anatomic site (posterior mandible), single thin-mucosa threshold (≤2 mm), and two specific implant systems — generalizability to other sites, thickness strata, and connections is untested.
- One-year follow-up only; whether the pre-loading laser advantage re-emerges or diverges beyond 1 year is unknown.
- The abstract reports no implant-survival, peri-implant-disease, or patient-reported outcomes — crestal bone loss is the sole reported endpoint.

## 7. Glossary

- **Laser microtexturing (Laser-Microtextured Surface, LMS)**: Laser-created microchannels (e.g. Laser-Lok, ~8 µm) on the implant collar intended to orient connective-tissue fibers and limit epithelial downgrowth.
- **Platform Switching (PS)**: Restoring a bone-level implant with an abutment narrower than the implant platform, moving the implant–abutment junction inward away from the crestal bone.
- **Crestal / marginal bone loss (MBL)**: Radiographic reduction in peri-implant alveolar bone height adjacent to the implant platform — the sole reported outcome here.
- **Thin mucosal biotype**: Vertical soft-tissue thickness ≤2 mm at the implant site, the inclusion criterion defining both groups in this study.
- **Proximal bone loss**: Bone loss at the mesial/distal aspects adjacent to the implant — the authors specify the pre-loading laser benefit in these terms.
- **Mann–Whitney U-test**: Non-parametric test for between-group differences, applied here to the two independent implant groups.
