## Why Ingested

Four-arm RCT directly comparing allograft (FDBA) vs xenograft (DBBM) and collagen sponge (CS) vs free gingival graft (FGG) socket sealing — the head-to-head graft/sealing-material data point [[wiki/overviews/socket-preservation-arp-overview]] synthesizes across. Reinforces [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/majzoub-2019-influence-different-grafting-materials]]'s "minimal inter-material difference" finding with independent RCT-level allograft-vs-xenograft data, and its CS≈FGG null result sits in tension with [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/del-fabbro-2022-sealing-materials-post-extraction]]'s SUCRA ranking placing autologous soft tissue above collagen-based sealing (flagged for review — see Related Papers).

## Three-line Summary
Four-arm RCT (n=41 patients, single-center, Athens) comparing FDBA+collagen sponge (A), FDBA+free gingival graft (B), DBBM+FGG (C), and FGG alone (D) for alveolar ridge preservation, with clinical vertical/horizontal measurements at extraction and 4 months.

Groups A, B, C had significantly less bone resorption than FGG-alone group D; FDBA vs DBBM showed no practical difference except a marginal buccal-central vertical advantage for xenograft (adjusted β=1.07mm, 95%CI 0.01–2.10, p=0.05); CS and FGG performed equally as sealing materials over FDBA.

Any bone graft (allograft or xenograft) under a soft-tissue seal outperforms soft-tissue sealing alone; allograft and xenograft are practically interchangeable, and collagen sponge is a viable, less-morbid substitute for FGG as a sealing material over FDBA — but this is a 41-patient, abstract-only source with no retrieved effect sizes beyond the one reported comparison.

## 세줄요약
4군 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=41명, 아테네 단일기관) — 동결건조골 동종골 (Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft, FDBA)+콜라겐 스펀지 (Collagen Sponge, CS)(A군), FDBA+유리치은이식 (Free Gingival Graft, FGG)(B군), 탈단백우골 이종골 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)+FGG(C군), FGG 단독(D군) 비교 — 발치 직후·4개월 후 임상 계측.

A·B·C군이 D군(FGG 단독) 대비 유의하게 적은 골흡수를 보였고, FDBA와 DBBM은 실질적 차이 없음(협측 중앙부 수직 골흡수만 이종골이 근소하게 우세: 보정 β=1.07mm, 95%신뢰구간 0.01–2.10, p=0.05), FDBA 위에서 CS와 FGG는 실링재로서 동등한 효과.

동종골이든 이종골이든 이식재를 사용하는 것이 연조직 실링만 하는 것보다 우수하며, 동종골과 이종골은 실질적으로 상호대체 가능하고 콜라겐 스펀지는 FDBA 위 실링재로서 FGG의 이환율 낮은 대안이 될 수 있음 — 단 41명 소규모, abstract만 확보된 자료로 보고된 비교 외 효과크기는 확인 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions
- One of few RCTs directly comparing allograft (FDBA) vs xenograft (DBBM) head-to-head within the same trial design (most SRs pool across heterogeneous studies rather than a single RCT contrast).
- Directly tests collagen sponge (CS) as an FGG substitute over the same graft material (FDBA) — a comparison the field's largest sealing-material NMA (Del Fabbro 2022) does not isolate.
- Four-arm parallel design isolates both the graft-material axis (allograft vs xenograft) and the sealing-material axis (CS vs FGG) against a no-graft (FGG-alone) control.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized controlled trial (RCT), single-center, 4 parallel arms.
- **n**: 41 patients randomized to (A) FDBA+CS, (B) FDBA+FGG, (C) DBBM+FGG, (D) FGG alone.
- **Outcomes**: Vertical and horizontal clinical measurements of bone/ridge resorption, taken immediately after extraction and again at 4 months.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Groups A, B, and C (all graft-containing) had significantly less vertical and horizontal bone resorption than group D (FGG alone).
- No significant difference between allograft (FDBA) and xenograft (DBBM) overall, **except**: vertical bone resorption at the buccal central site — group C (DBBM) vs group B (FDBA+FGG): adjusted β coefficient 1.07 mm; 95% CI 0.01–2.10; p = 0.05 (xenograft marginally better).
- No significant differences in hard tissue dimensions between CS and FGG when both applied over FDBA (group A vs group B).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Abstract-only source** — full Methods/Results/Discussion, tables, and per-group descriptive statistics were not retrievable from this PDF (Springer paywalled preview page); only the structured abstract's numbers are captured here.
- Single-center, n=41 across 4 arms (~10/arm) — likely underpowered for anything but large between-group effects; the one "significant" finding (p=0.05) sits exactly at the conventional threshold.
- 4-month follow-up only — no data on longer-term stability or eventual implant outcomes.
- Authors themselves note more RCTs are needed to compare FDBA vs DBBM histologically and to assess CS/FGG effects on soft-tissue dimensional change (i.e., only hard-tissue outcomes were evaluated here).

## 6. Related Work
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/majzoub-2019-influence-different-grafting-materials]] — SR (40 RCTs) found allograft (1.52mm) and xenograft (1.47mm) horizontal resorption minimally different; this RCT's FDBA≈DBBM null result reinforces that with direct within-trial data.
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/avila-ortiz-2019-alveolar-ridge-preservation-interventions]] — EFP/AO SR+MA found no single ARP modality demonstrates head-to-head superiority; this trial's A/B/C-vs-D pattern (any graft > no graft) and FDBA≈DBBM result are consistent with that.
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/del-fabbro-2022-sealing-materials-post-extraction]] — NMA (12 RCTs, 312 sites) ranked autologous soft tissue (FGG) above collagen-based sealing materials by SUCRA. **Flag**: this RCT's direct CS-vs-FGG comparison (over FDBA) found no significant difference — a narrower, RCT-level data point that sits in tension with Del Fabbro's ranking. Written here as `refines` rather than `contradicts` since El-Sioufi's CS is a sponge/plug, not clearly the same material class as Del Fabbro's "collagen membrane" or "collagen matrix xenograft" categories, and the trial is likely underpowered relative to Del Fabbro's 312-site network — but this deserves a second look (see also wiki page relations note).
- [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/kim-2024-collagenated-xenograft-arp-rct]] — later (2024) three-arm RCT on collagenated DBBM ± FGG sealing; same technique-comparison paradigm, not typed-edged here since it postdates this paper (edge direction belongs on the Kim page, pointing back at this one, in a future ingest/update).

## 7. Glossary
- **ARP**: Alveolar Ridge Preservation.
- **FDBA**: Freeze-Dried Bone Allograft.
- **DBBM**: Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral (xenograft).
- **FGG**: Free Gingival Graft.
- **CS**: Collagen Sponge (used here as a socket-sealing material over FDBA).
- **SS**: Socket Sealing material — whatever covers the graft (membrane, soft tissue, sponge, etc.).
