## Why Ingested

EFP S3 지침 (Herrera 2023) 은 외과적 임플란트주위염 치료에서 전신 항생제의 상용을 "제한적·불일치 근거"로 반대하고, 2026년 서술형 리뷰는 그 이득을 "미결"로 남겼으며, 406임플란트 후향 코호트의 항생제 신호 (OR 3.54/4.49) 는 적응증 교란 (Confounding by Indication) 가능성 때문에 확정 근거가 아니었다 — 이 2025 SR+MA는 Carcuac 2016/2017, Carrillo de Albornoz 2024, Riben Grundström 2024, Ramanauskaite 2025 등 신규 시험을 통합해 그 공백을 수치로 채운다. [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/ramanauskaite-2026-factors-influencing-effectiveness-surgical-therapy]]의 "미결" 판정을 단기 이득·장기 무이득·표면 의존성으로 정량화하고, [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/zhou-2026-surgical-protocols-peri-implantitis-cohort]]의 교란된 항생제 연관성에 시험 수준 근거를 붙인다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review with random-effects meta-analyses (PROSPERO CRD42019135753) of 7 studies (3 RCTs + 4 non-RCTs; 595 patients, 1388 implants) comparing surgical peri-implantitis treatment with vs. without adjunct systemic antibiotics, six databases searched to December 2024.

Short-term (1–2 years) treatment success favoured antibiotics (5 studies; OR 2.33, 95% CI 1.29–4.21, p=0.004), with more radiographic bone gain (MD 0.37 mm), better bone-level stability (OR 2.73), less BoP (OR 0.49) and less SoP (OR 0.33), but no long-term (≥3 years) success benefit (OR 1.84, p=0.43) and benefit confined to modified-surface implants (OR 4.10) versus turned surfaces (OR 0.79).

GRADE certainty is mostly low, harms data come from a single trial (diarrhoea 21.2% vs 0%), so the evidence supports at most case-by-case short-term prescribing for modified-surface implants, not routine use.

## 세줄요약

외과적 임플란트주위염 (Surgical Peri-Implantitis) 치료에서 전신 항생제 (Systemic Antibiotics) 보조의 이득을 평가한 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석 (SR+MA) — 7편 (무작위 3 + 비무작위 4), 환자 595명, 임플란트 1,388개, 6개 데이터베이스 검색 (~2024년 12월), PROSPERO CRD42019135753.

단기 (1–2년) 치료 성공 (Treatment Success) 은 항생제군이 우세했고 (OR 2.33, 95% CI 1.29–4.21, p=0.004), 방사선학적 골수준 (Radiographic Bone Level, RBL) 변화 +0.37 mm, 골소실 <0.5 mm 유지 OR 2.73, 탐침출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BoP) OR 0.49, 화농 (Suppuration on Probing, SoP) OR 0.33 모두 유의 — 반면 장기 (≥3년) 성공은 유의하지 않았고 (OR 1.84, p=0.43), 이득은 표면처리 (Modified) 임플란트에만 나타났다 (OR 4.10 vs 무처리 0.79).

GRADE 근거 확실성은 대부분 '낮음 (Low)'이고 이상반응 자료도 단일 시험에 그쳤으므로 (설사 21.2% vs 0%), 상시 처방이 아니라 표면처리 임플란트의 사례별 단기 처방만 정당화된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Pools trial-level evidence on systemic antibiotics **specifically as an adjunct to surgical** peri-implantitis therapy (previous reviews were either not meta-analytic or outdated), and separates short-term (1–2 years) from long-term (≥3 years) endpoints.
- Quantifies a short-term treatment-success benefit (OR 2.33; 455 → 660 per 1000, i.e. 206 more successes per 1000 implants) that is statistically significant but only **low-certainty** in GRADE.
- Reports an implant-surface interaction: benefit is confined to **modified-surface** implants (OR 4.10) and absent for **turned** implants (OR 0.79); control-group success was 76.2% for turned vs 36.9% for modified surfaces (p=0.001).
- Applies GRADE to harms: antibiotics **probably** increase diarrhoea (21.2% vs 0%; moderate certainty), and phenoxymethylpenicillin+metronidazole caused more diarrhoea than amoxicillin+metronidazole (34.6% vs 7.7%, p=0.03).
- Shows no substantial long-term advantage, which is the key argument against routine prescription under antimicrobial-stewardship considerations.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review with random-effects meta-analyses (Cochrane Handbook; PRISMA), PROSPERO-registered, a priori protocol; treatment effects pooled as ORs (binary) or MDs (continuous) with 95% CIs, restricted maximum likelihood variance estimator; τ²/I² and 95% prediction intervals reported.
- **Databases**: six electronic databases, inception to December 2024, plus grey literature (DOAJ, Digital Dissertations, metaRegister of Controlled Trials, WHO, Google Scholar) and reference/citation lists of included studies and reviews.
- **Eligibility**: RCTs and non-randomised comparative clinical studies in adults with peri-implantitis receiving any surgical treatment ± adjunct systemic antibiotics, ≥12 months follow-up, any language/year.
- **n**: 7 studies (3 RCTs — Carcuac 2016/2017, Riben Grundström 2024; 4 non-RCTs — Charalampakis 2011, Hallström 2017, Berglundh 2018, Carrillo de Albornoz 2024, Ramanauskaite 2025), 595 patients, 1388 implants. RCTs at low risk of bias overall; non-RCTs mostly moderate/serious, one critical.
- **Outcomes**: primary = treatment success (disease resolution, however defined); secondary = PPD change and residual PPD ≥5 mm, RBL change and RBL loss ≤0.5 mm, BoP, SoP, gingival recession; harms. Subgroups: RCT vs non-RCT, implant surface (turned vs modified).
- **Certainty**: GRADE summary-of-findings tables; individual patient data were requested but only one author group supplied a complete dataset.

- Control-group success differed by surface: turned 76.2% (95% CI 3.1–99.7%) vs modified 36.9% (13.7–68.2%), p=0.001 — interpreted as greater decontamination difficulty and higher recurrence risk of modified surfaces.
- Antibiotic regimens varied widely (compound, single vs combined, 1–3×/day, 3–7 days); exploratory re-analysis of Carrillo de Albornoz 2024 favoured azithromycin (OR 3.55) over metronidazole (1.82) or amoxicillin (1.79). Carcuac 2017 (included) had reported fewer implant removals over 3 years with antibiotics (OR 0.10, 0.02–0.41).
- Sensitivity analysis excluding non-RCTs at serious/critical risk of bias gave similar findings.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Few RCTs, so non-randomised studies were included; GRADE therefore starts at low for most pooled estimates, and RCT/non-RCT subgroups sometimes disagreed (long-term success p=0.02; residual PPD p=0.02; SoP p=0.04).
- Non-RCT methodological weaknesses (no pre-registration, unclear patient selection, no blinding, no a priori sample-size calculation, no open data) and mostly per-protocol rather than intention-to-treat analysis.
- Heterogeneous peri-implantitis case definitions and treatment-success criteria across studies; the authors could not re-derive the EFP S3 composite endpoint because only one group shared full IPD.
- Most meta-analyses rest on small studies (<100 implants/group); incomplete reporting, unavailable datasets and confounders blocked several pre-planned subgroup/meta-regression and reporting-bias analyses.
- Harms evidence is limited to one RCT; the ≥3-year endpoint may be better framed as disease recurrence than as treatment success.
- Future work: uniform S3 outcome definition, longer randomised follow-up, head-to-head antibiotic regimens, and antibiotic-free strategies (e.g. implantoplasty) in modified-surface cases.

## 6. Related Work

- **Grusovin 2022** ([[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/grusovin-2022-antibiotics-non-surgical-peri-implantitis-ma]], SR+MA of 6 RCTs): antibiotic adjuncts to **non-surgical** debridement — local antibiotics improved success (RR 9.89), systemic antibiotics only PPD/PAL; the present review extends the question to the **surgical** setting, where a short-term success benefit does emerge.
- **Herrera 2023** ([[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/herrera-2023-prevention-treatment-peri-implant-efp]], EFP S3 guideline): restrictive on routine systemic antibiotics for peri-implant disease; this review supplies the quantified basis (short-term gain, no ≥3-year gain, scarce harms data) and its surface-specific caveat.
- **Zhou 2026** ([[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/zhou-2026-surgical-protocols-peri-implantitis-cohort]]): retrospective 406-implant cohort in which pre-/post-operative antibiotics predicted 1-year success (OR 3.54/4.49) amid likely confounding by indication — the present meta-analysis is the trial-level evidence that qualifies that signal.
- **Ramanauskaite 2026** ([[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/ramanauskaite-2026-factors-influencing-effectiveness-surgical-therapy]]): narrative review that leaves the systemic-antibiotic benefit "undetermined"; this SR+MA resolves part of that by time horizon and implant surface.
- **Bai 2024** ([[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/bai-2024-seven-nonsurgical-methods-peri-implantitis-network]]): network MA ranking systemic antibiotics+debridement top for CAL/MBL in **non-surgical** peri-implantitis, while noting the EFP S3 tension.
- **Ramanauskaite 2025 / Carrillo de Albornoz 2024 / Carcuac 2016, 2017** (included primary studies; not separately ingested).

## 7. Glossary

- **Peri-implantitis**: plaque-associated inflammatory disease of peri-implant tissues with bleeding/suppuration on probing and progressive crestal bone loss.
- **Treatment success (EFP S3 endpoint)**: residual PPD ≤5 mm with no BoP at more than one site, no suppuration and no progressive bone loss.
- **RBL (Radiographic Bone Level)**: distance from the implant reference point (usually the shoulder) to the first bone-to-implant contact on radiographs; change post–pre estimates bone gain/loss.
- **BoP / SoP (Bleeding / Suppuration on Probing)**: dichotomous chairside inflammation markers at a standardised probing force.
- **Turned vs modified surface**: machined (turned) titanium versus roughness-modified surfaces (SLA, anodised, blasted, etc.); modified surfaces are harder to decontaminate and recur more often.
- **GRADE**: system rating certainty of a body of evidence (high/moderate/low/very low) across risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias.
- **Random-effects (REML) meta-analysis**: pooling that allows true effect sizes to vary between studies, estimating the between-study variance (τ²) by restricted maximum likelihood; prediction intervals show where a future study's effect may fall.
