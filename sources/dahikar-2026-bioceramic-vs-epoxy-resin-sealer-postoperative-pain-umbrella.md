## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/endodontics/zamparini-2026-postop-pain-endodontic-instrumentation-irrigation-obturation-umbrella]]는 세정·기구조작·충전 3개 영역을 아우르는 넓은 우산리뷰(umbrella review)로 충전(obturation) 영역은 서술적 종합(narrative synthesis)에 그쳤다. 본 Dahikar 2026 우산리뷰는 실러 종류(바이오세라믹 vs 에폭시레진) 한 가지에 집중해 7편의 체계적 문헌고찰(SR)에서 추출한 RCT 수준 데이터로 실제 정량적 메타분석(pooled SMD/RR)을 수행 — 좁지만 더 정량적인 보강 근거로 ingest.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42023461029, PRISMA 2020) of 7 systematic reviews (2020–2024, 63 unique RCTs, 5 with meta-analysis) comparing bioceramic vs. epoxy resin-based sealers for postoperative pain after primary non-surgical root canal treatment, with de novo random-effects pooling of RCT-level data extracted from the included reviews.

Pooled analysis found a small but statistically significant reduction in pain favoring bioceramic sealers at 24h (SMD = −0.15, 95% CI −0.28 to −0.03, p<0.05) and 48h (SMD = −0.29, 95% CI −0.42 to −0.16, p<0.001), with no differences beyond 48h, in analgesic consumption (RR = 0.91–0.92), or flare-up incidence (RR = 0.97); evidence overlap across reviews was moderate (CCA ≈ 12%) and AMSTAR-2 quality ranged from moderate to low.

Clinically, the pain reduction with bioceramic sealers is real but too small to be clinically meaningful, so sealer selection should rest on material handling/biocompatibility/retrievability properties rather than expected postoperative discomfort — reinforcing (with actual pooled numbers) the null clinical-significance conclusion that [[wiki/endodontics/zamparini-2026-postop-pain-endodontic-instrumentation-irrigation-obturation-umbrella]] reached narratively for the obturation domain.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(SR) 7편(2020–2024년, 고유 RCT 63편, 그중 5편 메타분석 포함)을 대상으로 원발성 비외과적 근관치료(NSRCT) 후 바이오세라믹(bioceramic) 실러와 에폭시레진(epoxy resin) 실러 간 술후통증(postoperative pain)을 비교한 우산리뷰(umbrella review, PROSPERO CRD42023461029, PRISMA 2020)로, 포함된 SR에서 추출한 RCT 수준 자료로 자체 랜덤효과모델 메타분석(random-effects meta-analysis)을 새로 수행했다.

풀링 분석 결과 바이오세라믹 실러가 24시간(표준화평균차, Standardized Mean Difference, SMD = −0.15, 95% 신뢰구간 −0.28~−0.03, p<0.05)과 48시간(SMD = −0.29, 95% CI −0.42~−0.16, p<0.001)에 통계적으로 유의하게 작은 통증 감소를 보였으나, 48시간 이후·진통제 사용(위험비, Risk Ratio, RR = 0.91–0.92)·급성발작(flare-up) 발생률(RR = 0.97)에서는 차이가 없었고, 연구 간 근거 중복도(Corrected Covered Area, CCA)는 중간 수준(약 12%), AMSTAR-2 방법론 질은 중간~낮음이었다.

임상적으로 바이오세라믹 실러의 통증 감소는 통계적으로는 실재하나 임상적 의미를 갖기엔 너무 작아, 실러 선택은 술후통증 예상보다는 재료의 조작성·생체적합성·재처치 가능성 등에 근거해야 하며, 이는 [[wiki/endodontics/zamparini-2026-postop-pain-endodontic-instrumentation-irrigation-obturation-umbrella]]가 충전 영역에서 서술적으로 내린 "임상적으로 무의미하다"는 결론을 실제 풀링 수치로 재확인·보강한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review to focus specifically and exclusively on bioceramic-vs-epoxy-resin sealer postoperative pain (as opposed to Zamparini 2026's broader 3-domain instrumentation/irrigation/obturation umbrella review, where sealer comparison was one of several sub-questions handled narratively).
- Performs de novo random-effects pooling (SMD/RR) of RCT-level data drawn from the 5 quantitative SRs, rather than relying solely on each SR's own reported pooled estimate — a genuine second-order meta-analysis, not just narrative aggregation.
- Quantifies evidence overlap across the 7 included SRs via Corrected Covered Area (CCA ≈ 12%, moderate), and appraises each with AMSTAR-2 + GRADE.
- Runs meta-regression testing visit protocol, pulp status, and pain-scale type as moderators — none reached significance, supporting the interpretation that sealer-type differences are robust to these study-level factors (or, equivalently, that no subgroup showed a materially larger effect).
- Robustness checks (REML vs. Paule–Mandel estimators, Knapp–Hartung adjustment, Peto sensitivity for the binary endpoint) did not change conclusions.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PRISMA 2020 + Cochrane Handbook + JBI umbrella-review manual methodology, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42023461029). PICOS: adults undergoing primary single-visit NSRCT; bioceramic (calcium silicate-based, e.g. EndoSequence BC, iRoot SP, TotalFill BC, Endoseal MTA) vs. epoxy resin-based (predominantly AH Plus) sealers; postoperative pain (VAS/NRS or dichotomous pain/analgesic/flare-up) at 24h/48h primary, >48h/analgesic/flare-up secondary; SRs and meta-analyses of RCTs/quasi-RCTs/CCTs. Searched PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Embase, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, LILACS, Google Scholar plus grey literature (OpenGrey, SIGLE, OpenThesis), January 2000–November 2024, English only. Two independent reviewers screened title/abstract/full-text with consensus/third-party adjudication. AMSTAR-2 for review quality; risk-of-bias tools extracted from constituent reviews (Cochrane RoB-2, ROBINS-I, JBI). Random-effects (DerSimonian–Laird) meta-analysis of RCT-level SMDs/RRs pooled from constituent reviews, each primary trial counted once per outcome/time point; heterogeneity via I² (low <25%, moderate 25–75%, high >75%); GRADE certainty per outcome; CCA for evidence overlap; sensitivity analyses (REML, Paule–Mandel, Knapp–Hartung, Peto) and meta-regression (visit protocol, pulp status, pain scale) for robustness. Software: RevMan 5.4.1, Stata v16, R (meta package).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Search**: 569 records → 424 after dedup → 75 full-text assessed → 7 SRs included (5 with meta-analysis, 1 qualitative only), covering 63 unique RCTs, published 2020–2024 (Iran, Italy, India, Brazil, Turkey).
- **Pain at 24h**: pooled SMD = −0.15 (95% CI −0.28 to −0.03, p<0.05), I² = 0% (5 RCTs pooled: Fonseca 2019, Ates 2019, Ates 2019 Vital, Yu 2020, Graunaite 2018, Supare 2024).
- **Pain at 48h**: pooled SMD = −0.29 (95% CI −0.42 to −0.16, p<0.001), I² = 0% (4 RCTs: Fonseca 2019, Ates 2019, Ates 2019 Vital, Supare 2024).
- **Pain at 6h**: SMD = −0.12 (95% CI −0.38 to 0.14) — slightly favors bioceramic, low certainty.
- **Pain at 72h/7 days**: SMD = −0.03 (95% CI −0.16 to 0.10) — no difference, moderate certainty.
- **Analgesic consumption (24h)**: RR = 0.91 (95% CI 0.78–1.06) — no difference, low certainty.
- **Flare-up incidence**: RR = 0.97 (95% CI 0.74–1.26) — no difference, low certainty.
- **Evidence overlap**: CCA ≈ 11.7% average across 7 reviews (moderate).
- **AMSTAR-2**: 1 high quality, 4 moderate quality, 1 high risk of bias.
- **Meta-regression**: no significant moderator effects for visit protocol, pulp status, or pain scale (all p>0.3) at 24h, 48h, or composite.
- **GRADE certainty**: moderate for 24h/48h/>72h pain estimates, low for 6h pain, analgesic use, and flare-up incidence; no outcome reached high certainty.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

English-language restriction (possible language bias); umbrella-level conclusions inherit any bias present in the 7 constituent reviews; heterogeneous pain-assessment scales/timing/analgesic-reporting across primary studies complicate direct comparison; retreatment and multi-visit cases underrepresented; most primary RCTs had ≤7-day follow-up, precluding assessment of long-term outcomes (periapical healing, persistent inflammation); k<10 per endpoint precluded formal small-study-bias (Egger) testing. Authors call for standardized time-restricted pain assessment (100mm VAS at 6h/24h/48h/7d), adequately powered multicenter RCTs with uniform protocols, PROSPERO pre-registration and PRISMA 2020 compliance in future SRs, patient-reported outcome measures, and mechanistic studies of inflammatory biomarkers (IL-6, TNF-α, substance P).

## 7. Glossary

- **NSRCT**: Non-Surgical Root Canal Treatment
- **BC / ER sealer**: Bioceramic (calcium silicate-based) / Epoxy Resin-based sealer
- **SMD**: Standardized Mean Difference — effect size standardizing pain scores measured on different scales (VAS/NRS) to a common metric; negative values here favor bioceramic sealers (less pain)
- **RR**: Risk Ratio
- **CCA**: Corrected Covered Area — quantifies primary-study overlap between systematic reviews in an umbrella review
- **AMSTAR-2**: A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews 2 — methodological-quality appraisal instrument for systematic reviews
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development and Evaluations — framework for rating certainty of evidence
- **VAS / NRS**: Visual Analogue Scale / Numeric Rating Scale (pain measurement tools)
- **PROSPERO**: International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews
