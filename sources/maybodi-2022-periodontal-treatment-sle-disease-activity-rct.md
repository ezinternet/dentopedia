## Why Ingested

전신 자가면역질환에서 비외과적 치주치료(SRP)가 전신 염증 지표를 낮추는지는 류마티스관절염(RA)에서만 주로 검증되어 있었다 — [[periodontics/dolcezza-2024-rheumatoid-arthritis-periodontal-disease-sr-ma]] (SR+MA, RCT 6편, DAS28 −0.56·CAL −0.41mm 유의 개선). 본 RCT(Maybodi 2022)는 같은 "SRP → 전신염증 감소" 패턴이 전신홍반루푸스(SLE)에서도 재현되는지를 직접 검증한 첫 RCT급 근거로, RA에서 확립된 치주-전신염증 연결고리를 SLE로 확장(extends)하는 자료다.

## Three-line Summary

Double-blinded RCT, n=90 active-SLE patients with periodontitis (45 SRP+OHI vs 45 OHI-only), 3-month follow-up.

SRP group showed clinically meaningful periodontal improvement (PD −2.10mm, CAL −2.02mm) and a significant between-group CRP reduction (P=0.032), but SLEDAI change did not differ between groups (P=0.894).

Nonsurgical periodontal treatment reduces acute-phase inflammatory biomarkers (CRP, ESR trend) in SLE patients but does not demonstrably alter overall lupus disease activity index at 3 months — supports periodontal care as an adjunct, not a disease-activity-modifying intervention.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 RCT, 활동성 SLE + 치주염 환자 90명(SRP+구강위생교육군 45명 vs 구강위생교육단독군 45명), 3개월 추적.

SRP군은 치주지표가 임상적으로 유의하게 개선(PD −2.10mm, CAL −2.02mm)되었고 CRP는 군간 유의한 차이(P=0.032)를 보였으나, SLEDAI 변화는 군간 유의한 차이가 없었음(P=0.894).

비외과적 치주치료는 SLE 환자의 급성기 염증표지자(CRP, ESR 경향)는 낮추지만 3개월 시점 전신 루푸스 질병활동도 자체를 유의하게 바꾸지는 못함 — 치주치료는 질병활동 조절제가 아닌 보조적 항염 수단으로 자리매김.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First adequately-powered RCT (n=90) directly comparing SRP+OHI vs OHI-only in active SLE patients with periodontitis, roughly triple the sample size of the only prior comparable trial (Fabbri et al., n=32/17).
- Demonstrates a statistically significant between-group reduction in CRP (P=0.032) attributable to periodontal treatment, contradicting Fabbri et al.'s finding of no CRP/ESR difference between groups.
- Shows SLEDAI decreased in BOTH groups over 3 months, with no significant between-group difference — i.e., periodontal treatment's effect on acute-phase reactants does not translate into a measurable SLEDAI benefit at this timepoint.
- Reinforces the "Hawthorne effect" as a plausible confound: the OHI-only control group's periodontal indices improved statistically (through better home care from instruction and observation) but not to a clinically meaningful degree, unlike the calculus-removal effect in the SRP group.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: single-center, double-blinded RCT registered in the Iranian Registry of Clinical Trials; approved by institutional ethics committee; conducted Jan 2019–Feb 2020 at a rheumatology clinic.
- Population: 90 patients with ACR-diagnosed active SLE (SLEDAI >2), periodontitis stages 2–4/grade B–C (2017 World Workshop classification), ≥20 teeth. Excluded: overlapping autoimmune/systemic disease, smoking, pregnancy, recent radiotherapy/chemotherapy, recent periodontal treatment or antibiotics, anticoagulant/cytotoxic drug use, medication changes during study.
- Intervention: case group received oral hygiene instructions + full-mouth scaling and root planing (ultrasonic instrument, Woodpecker); control group received oral hygiene instructions only.
- Blinding: periodontal re-examiner and SLEDAI assessor were both blinded to group assignment.
- Outcomes measured at baseline and 3 months: periodontal parameters (PD, CAL, bleeding index, O'Leary plaque index), serum CRP, ESR, and SLEDAI score.
- Statistics: SPSS23; Chi-square, independent t-test, Mann-Whitney U, Wilcoxon signed-rank; significance threshold P<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Case (baseline→3mo) | Control (baseline→3mo) | Between-group P (3mo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| SLEDAI | 3.31→2.37 | 3.22→2.46 | 0.499 (diff-in-diff P=0.894) |
| CRP | 2.87→1.64 (within P=0.001) | 4.23→3.66 (within P=0.588, ns) | 0.032 |
| ESR | 20.64→14.77 (within P=0.001) | 20.82→18.6 (within P=0.109, ns) | 0.180 |
| PD (mm) | 4.28→2.17 | 4.15→4.08 | 0.001 |
| CAL (mm) | 3.74→1.71 | 3.97→3.8 | 0.001 |
| Bleeding index | 42.02→14.11 | 41.95→40.55 | 0.001 |
| Plaque index | 63.33→17.44 | 63.68→62.04 | 0.001 |

- No baseline differences between groups in age, sex, or any periodontal/serologic parameter.
- Periodontal parameters improved in both groups but only the SRP group reached clinically meaningful magnitude (≥1mm range changes); control group changes were statistically significant but clinically negligible.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Short follow-up (3 months only) — insufficient to capture longer-term SLEDAI trajectory or relapse/flare patterns.
- Only nonsurgical periodontal treatment was feasible given patients' systemic/immunosuppressed status; surgical periodontal therapy was not tested.
- Single-center Iranian cohort; generalizability to other populations/ethnicities not established.
- Control group's own statistically-significant (if not clinical) periodontal improvement suggests an observation/Hawthorne effect that may partly attenuate the measured case-control CRP/ESR contrast.

## 6. Related Work

- Fabbri et al. (prior RCT, n=32/17) — found nonsurgical periodontal treatment significantly improved SLEDAI, but no significant CRP/ESR between-group difference; this study's findings are essentially inverted (CRP/ESR positive, SLEDAI null), an open contradiction worth flagging for future SR/MA synthesis.
- Zhang et al. — Chinese SLE cohort found periodontal disease associated with higher SLEDAI and plaque index (observational, not interventional).
- Sales, Souza, Calderaro et al. — multiple descriptive/observational studies found no significant association between periodontal status and SLEDAI/CRP/ESR, partially consistent with this trial's null SLEDAI result.
- [[periodontics/dolcezza-2024-rheumatoid-arthritis-periodontal-disease-sr-ma]] — analogous SRP-reduces-systemic-inflammation finding established for RA (DAS28, CAL); this SLE trial tests whether the same causal pathway generalizes to a second autoimmune disease.
- [[periodontics/qiao-2020-rheumatoid-arthritis-risk-periodontitis-sr-ma]] — periodontitis as an epidemiologic risk factor for RA, part of the broader periodontal-systemic-autoimmune literature this paper extends.

## 7. Glossary

- **SLE (Systemic Lupus Erythematosus)**: multi-organ autoimmune disease with fluctuating disease activity.
- **SLEDAI (SLE Disease Activity Index)**: composite clinical/serologic score (range 0–105) quantifying current lupus activity; >2 indicates active disease.
- **CRP (C-Reactive Protein) / ESR (Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate)**: acute-phase inflammatory biomarkers, used here as infection/inflammation proxies distinguishable from lupus-specific activity.
- **SRP (Scaling and Root Planing)**: nonsurgical mechanical periodontal debridement, the "case" intervention in this trial.
- **PD/CAL (Probing Depth / Clinical Attachment Loss)**: standard periodontal severity measurements.
- **Hawthorne effect**: behavior-change artifact from being observed/instructed, invoked here to explain the control group's statistically-but-not-clinically significant periodontal improvement.
