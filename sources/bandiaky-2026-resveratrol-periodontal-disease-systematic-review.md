## Why Ingested

Extends the wiki's nutraceutical/host-modulation adjunct evidence for non-surgical periodontal therapy beyond the antioxidant CoQ10 line in [[periodontics/host-modulation/fernandez-2025-coenzyme-q10-nonsurgical-periodontal-sr]] and the omega-3 line in [[periodontics/host-modulation/heo-2022-omega-3-fatty-acids-periodontitis-ma]]. Resveratrol is a polyphenol antioxidant/anti-inflammatory adjunct; this SR quantifies a strong preclinical bone-protection signal (ABL reduction 7.09-60.60%) but exposes the same clinical gap (small heterogeneous RCTs, no radiographic bone-loss data) that limits every dietary-antioxidant adjunct — reinforcing that these agents are supportive, not standalone, additions to SRP in [[periodontics/kwon-2021-current-concepts-management-periodontitis]].

## Three-line Summary

PRISMA-2020 systematic review of resveratrol (stilbene polyphenol) as an adjunct in periodontal therapy — 22 studies (16 preclinical animal, 6 double-blind RCTs); no meta-analysis possible due to heterogeneity.

In animal ligature models resveratrol reduced alveolar bone loss by 7.09-60.60% (12 of 13 studies with ABL data) and lowered IL-1beta/IL-6/TNF-alpha and NADPH oxidase; the 6 RCTs (adjunct to SRP or standalone) were variable but generally positive for PPD, BI, PI and CAL, with greatest effect in aggressive periodontitis (Stage III-IV grade C).

Resveratrol may be considered an adjunct to non-surgical periodontal therapy, but clinical evidence is limited by small samples, heterogeneous dosing/routes, short follow-up, and zero human bone-loss data — no standardised protocol can yet be defined.

## 세줄요약

레스베라트롤(stilbene 계열 폴리페놀)의 치주치료 보조 효과를 다룬 PRISMA-2020 체계적 문헌고찰 — 22편(전임상 동물 16편, 이중맹검 RCT 6편); 이질성으로 메타분석은 불가.

레스베라트롤은 비외과적 치주치료의 보조제로 고려될 수 있으나, 소규모·이질적 용량/경로·짧은 추적·사람 골소실 데이터 부재로 근거가 제한적이라 표준 프로토콜은 아직 설정 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Updates the resveratrol-periodontitis evidence base to October 2025, pooling **22 studies (16 preclinical animal + 6 clinical RCTs)** under a PICOS framework with ABL as the primary outcome.
- Quantifies the preclinical bone-protection signal: **ABL reduced 7.09-60.60%** across studies, driven by osteoclast inhibition and RANKL downregulation.
- Maps the mechanistic triad — anti-inflammatory (down IL-1beta/IL-6/IL-8/TNF-alpha, NF-kB), antioxidant (down NADPH oxidase/NOX, up SOD/SIRT1/Nrf2), and anti-osteoclastic (down RANKL, down osteoclast formation).
- Consolidates the 6 human RCTs and flags the decisive clinical gap: **no clinical study measured radiographic or clinical bone loss**, so human ABL efficacy remains unproven.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA-2020 systematic review; PROSPERO CRD42022247114.
- **Databases**: Medline, Cochrane Library, Lilacs, Wiley, ScienceDirect to October 2025 + hand-search of periodontal journals. English only, no date limits. Terms: ("resveratrol" OR "3,4,5 trihydroxystilbene") AND ("periodontal disease" OR "periodontitis" OR "gingivitis").
- **PICOS**: P = mice/rats (preclinical) or periodontitis patients (clinical); I = resveratrol alone or adjunct; C = placebo; O(primary) = ABL reduction; O(secondary) = plaque, cytokines, TAC, BI, CAL, PPD, adverse effects. Excluded: derivatives/red wine, systemic-effect-only, no-control, reviews, in vitro.
- **Screening**: 3 investigators (ONB, MVB, SS). 1,290 records → 1,172 duplicates removed → 118 screened → 27 eligibility → 22 included (11 primary + 11 from a prior SR+MA).
- **Risk of bias**: SYRCLE tool (animal), Cochrane RoB tool (RCT).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Preclinical (16 studies)**
- ABL evaluated in 13/16; **12 showed reduction (7.09-60.60%)**; in 8 comparable-dose studies reductions were 7.09-28.58%.
- Two null studies: Wagner 2019 (low 0.05 mg/ml dose) and Chin 2016 (only 7-day treatment, 25 mg/kg) → efficacy dose/duration dependent.
- Models: 10 Wistar, 3 Sprague-Dawley, 3 mice (C57BL/6J, C57BLKS/J); all ligature-induced (silk/cotton/elastic), some + LPS or P. gingivalis. Doses 5-25 mg/kg, treatment 7-140 days, mostly gavage.
- Mechanisms: down IL-1beta/IL-6/IL-8/TNF-alpha/IL-17, down NF-kB p65/p38MAPK/STAT3, down NADPH oxidase, down RANKL/osteoclasts; up SOD/SIRT1/Nrf2/HO-1/IL-4/OPG.
- Cirano 2016: no effect on Aa/Pg/Tf ligature load → effect is host-modulatory, not antimicrobial.

**Clinical (6 double-blind RCTs; 143 placebo, 215 resveratrol participants)**
- Mohammed 2025 (n=57, mouthwash 4wk + NSPT): improved PI, BI, PPD, not CAL; down salivary IL-6, not RANKL.
- Lucchesi 2025 (n=38 smokers, 500 mg/d x180d + NSPT): improved PPD, CAL, PGM; down IL-1beta/IL-6 in deep sites.
- Nikniaz 2023 (n=40, 480 mg/d x4wk + NSPT): improved PI only.
- Zhang 2022 (n=160 aggressive PD, 125/200/500 mg/d x8wk, no periodontal Tx): improved CAL, BI, OHI-S, PPD; down all inflammatory markers at all doses.
- Javid 2019 (n=50 T2DM, 480 mg/d x28d): down serum IL-6; no change TNF-alpha/TAC/CAL.
- Javid 2017 (n=50 T2DM, 480 mg/d x28d): down insulin/insulin resistance/PPD.
- **Greatest clinical effect in aggressive periodontitis (Stage III-IV grade C).** No RCT measured bone loss.

**Risk of bias**: Preclinical predominantly unclear-to-high (performance 60% high). RCTs lower risk; none high, but performance/attrition unclear in 67%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **No meta-analysis** — heterogeneity in models, protocols, doses, routes, and outcomes.
- Clinical evidence limited by small samples, methodological heterogeneity, variable dosing/routes, short follow-up (4wk-6mo).
- **No human bone-loss data** (radiographic or clinical) — human ABL efficacy unproven.
- Oral bioavailability of resveratrol in humans is low and inconsistently reported; topical/local delivery untested.
- No evidence for surgical/regenerative periodontal contexts.
- Future: well-designed RCTs to define optimal dose, duration, delivery route, and to assess bone-loss outcomes and aggressive-periodontitis subgroups.

## 7. Glossary

- **ABL** — Alveolar Bone Loss; primary outcome, distance from cementoenamel junction to bone crest.
- **Resveratrol** — 3,5,4'-trihydroxystilbene, a stilbene-class polyphenol antioxidant.
- **NSPT** — Non-Surgical Periodontal Therapy (scaling and root planing).
- **PPD / CAL / BI / PI** — Probing Pocket Depth / Clinical Attachment Level / Bleeding Index / Plaque Index.
- **RANKL** — Receptor Activator of Nuclear factor-kappa-B Ligand; activates osteoclasts.
- **NADPH oxidase / NOX** — enzyme complex generating reactive oxygen species.
- **SIRT1 / Nrf2 / NF-kB** — sirtuin-1 / nuclear factor-E2 related factor 2 / nuclear factor-kappa-B; redox and inflammatory transcription regulators.
- **SYRCLE** — risk-of-bias tool for animal intervention studies.
