## Why Ingested

Held cracked-tooth outcome papers stratify prognosis mainly by probing depth ([[cracked-tooth/chan-2025-survival-endodontically-treated-cracked-teeth]]) or pool outcomes across restorations ([[cracked-tooth/zhang-2024-cracked-teeth-treatment-outcomes-sr-ma]]). This cohort tests the Iowa Index with an added crack-extension axis and reports multivariable failure predictors, giving a stage-based triage that extends both. → [[cracked-tooth/wongkornchaowalit-2026-modified-iowa-index-root-canal-treated-cracked-teeth]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort of 263 root canal-treated cracked posterior teeth (1–5 years, mean 40.3 months) using a modified Iowa Index that subcategorizes each stage by crack extension. Overall success 82.9% and survival 89.7%; estimated survival 99.2% at 2 years and 78.6% at 5 years; stage IV-R (radicular crack plus pocket ≥5 mm) had success 33.3% and survival 63.0% versus 86.5–100% survival in stages I–III. Abstract-only — full text not retrieved; seven independent failure predictors were identified, but effect sizes could not be extracted.

## 세줄요약

근관치료를 받은 상·하악 구치부 균열치아 263개를 1~5년(평균 40.3개월) 추적한 후향적 코호트로, Iowa 지수를 균열 범위별로 세분화한 수정 Iowa 지수를 적용하였다. 전체 성공률 82.9%, 생존율 89.7%였고 추정 생존율은 2년 99.2%, 5년 78.6%였으며, 근단 방향 연장과 치주낭 ≥5 mm가 겹친 IV-R 단계는 성공률 33.3%, 생존율 63.0%로 I~III단계(생존율 86.5~100%)보다 현저히 나빴다. 초록만 확보하여(전문 미확보) 7개 독립 실패 예측인자의 효과크기는 확인하지 못했다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Modified Iowa Index: each Iowa stage subdivided by crack extension (including radicular extension).
- Seven independent predictors of failure from Cox regression.
- Identifies radicular extension plus pocket ≥5 mm (Stage IV-R) as a high-risk profile.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Retrospective cohort, 263 cracked posterior teeth, 1–5 year follow-up; strict clinical and radiographic outcome criteria; Cox regression for failure predictors. Details of inclusion criteria, operator, restorative protocol and censoring were not available (abstract-only).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Success 82.9%, survival 89.7%; mean follow-up 40.3 months.
- Estimated survival 99.2% (2 y), 78.6% (5 y).
- Independent failure predictors: increasing age; pocket depth ≥5 mm along the crack line; radicular extension ≥3 mm; preoperative periapical lesion; multiple crack lines; no occlusal splint in patients with parafunctional habits; restoration with onlay or composite.
- Stages I–III (PD <5 mm): success 78.9–98.3%, survival 86.5–100%.
- Stage IV-R: success 33.3%, survival 63.0%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

abstract-only — full text not retrieved: hazard ratios, CIs, per-stage n, and the comparison group for restoration type were not seen. Retrospective design; follow-up of 1–5 years is much shorter than the 10–15 years of Chan 2025, so 5-year estimates rest on fewer at-risk teeth. The restoration finding (onlay or composite vs presumably full coverage) is confounded by indication unless adjusted, which could not be verified.

## 7. Glossary

- Iowa Index: stage-based prognostic classification of cracked teeth (Krell and colleagues) using periradicular status, crack location and probing depth.
- Stage IV-R: radicular crack extension combined with periodontal pocket ≥5 mm (this paper's modified stage).
