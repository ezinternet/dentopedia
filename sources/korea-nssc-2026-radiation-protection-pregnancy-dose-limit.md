## Three-line Summary

Korean NSSC administrative notice (2026-07-09) implementing Annex 1 note 2 of the Nuclear Safety Act Enforcement Decree, which delegates the dose limit for workers with a confirmed pregnancy to the Commission.

From the time a pregnancy is reported to the employer and confirmed until delivery, the equivalent-dose limit at the lower-abdomen surface is 2 mSv, and radionuclide intake is limited to 1/20 of the annual limit on intake (ALI).

It binds radiation workers under the Nuclear Safety Act, not diagnostic X-ray staff under the Medical Service Act regime; for dental clinics it is the closest Korean benchmark that can be adopted as an internal target, not a directly applicable legal limit (interpretation — confirm with the local public health centre).

임신 사실을 사업자에게 보고해 확인된 시점부터 출산 시까지 하복부 표면 등가선량한도 2 mSv, 방사성핵종 섭취는 연간섭취한도(ALI)의 1/20.

## 2. Key Contributions

- The only numeric pregnancy-specific radiation dose limit in Korean law: 2 mSv lower-abdomen surface equivalent dose, from confirmation to delivery.
- The trigger is the worker reporting the pregnancy to the employer — the limit applies only after notification.
- It is a surface (abdomen) dose, not a fetal dose — different quantity from the EU "fetus <1 mSv" in Horner 2004.
- Scope is the Nuclear Safety Act regime; dental diagnostic X-ray staff are regulated separately (MOHW ordinance) with no pregnancy clause.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group (Decree Annex 1) | Effective dose | Lens | Hands/feet/skin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Radiation workers | 50 mSv/y within 100 mSv/5 y | 150 mSv/y | 500 mSv/y |
| Occasional entrants, transport workers, approved <18 y | 6 mSv/y | 15 mSv/y | 50 mSv/y |
| Others (public) | 1 mSv/y | 15 mSv/y | 50 mSv/y |
| **Confirmed pregnancy (NSSC notice §4-1)** | **2 mSv lower-abdomen surface, confirmation → delivery; intake ≤ ALI/20** | | |
