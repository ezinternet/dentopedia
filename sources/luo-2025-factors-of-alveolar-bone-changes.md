## Why Ingested

투명교정 발치 증례에서 상악 중절치 주위 치조골 변화의 "원인"(tooth displacement pattern)을 다룬 논문으로, 기존 [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/zhang-2025-clear-aligner-extraction-protocol-alveolar-bone-cbct]]가 발치 프로토콜(TPE vs OLIE vs 무발치) 단위로 골변화를 비교한 것과 상보적으로, 치아 이동 양상(retraction/intrusion/root distal movement/lingual inclination) 단위로 골변화 기전을 세분화한다. 같은 Invisalign-CBCT 방법론 계열 문헌을 심화하는 역할.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective CBCT cohort (n=94 patients), extraction orthodontic cases treated with clear aligner therapy — pre-treatment (T0) and post-treatment (T1) CBCT used to quantify labial/palatal bone thickness (LBT/PBT) and height (LBH/PBH) changes around maxillary central incisors, related to 3D tooth displacement pattern.

Palatal bone defect was significant while labial bone was largely maintained; multiple linear regression identified movement-pattern-specific risk factors — tooth retraction ↓PBT/↑LBT, crown retraction (uncontrolled tipping) ↓PBH, incisor intrusion ↑PBT/↓LBT + defect of both LBH and PBH, root distal movement → LBT/LBH resorption, lingual inclination → LBT loss.

Movement pattern (not just retraction magnitude) determines the site and type of alveolar bone risk, so treatment planning should anticipate the predicted 3D tooth displacement to avoid alveolar bone defect; this source is abstract-level only — full-text body (detailed regression tables, exact n per movement subgroup, effect sizes) was not retrievable (paywalled Wiley Early View, no PMC full text).

## 세줄요약

후향적 CBCT 코호트 (n=94명), 투명교정(CAT) 발치 증례 — 치료 전(T0)·후(T1) CBCT로 상악 중절치 주위 순측/구개측 골두께(LBT/PBT) 및 골높이(LBH/PBH) 변화를 3차원 치아 이동 양상과 연관지어 분석.

구개측 골결손이 유의했고 순측골은 대체로 유지됨; 다중선형회귀 결과 이동 양상별 위험 요인이 상이 — 치아후방이동(retraction) → PBT 감소/LBT 증가, 치관후방이동(uncontrolled tipping) → PBH 감소, 절치 압하(intrusion) → PBT 증가/LBT 감소 + LBH·PBH 모두 결손, 치근원심이동 → LBT/LBH 흡수, 설측경사 → LBT 소실.

단순 후방이동량이 아니라 이동 "양상"이 골위험의 부위·유형을 결정하므로 치료계획 시 예측 치아이동을 고려해 치조골 결손을 예방해야 함; 본 소스는 초록 수준(abstract-only)이며, 페이월(Wiley Early View, PMC 전문 없음)로 인해 본문 상세 회귀분석 표·부분군별 정확한 n·효과크기는 확보하지 못함.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Isolates **tooth movement pattern** (not just extraction status or net retraction distance) as the key explanatory variable for alveolar bone dimensional change around maxillary central incisors in CAT extraction cases.
- Distinguishes labial vs palatal compartments: labial bone is comparatively preserved, while palatal bone bears the dimensional loss — a compartment-specific risk signature relevant to clear aligner biomechanics (which tend to produce more lingual/palatal crown movement/tipping than fixed appliances in some retraction mechanics).
- Differentiates **uncontrolled tipping vs controlled tipping** as producing distinct bone-dimensional change patterns, reinforcing that biomechanical control of the movement type (not simply achieving the target position) modulates periodontal/osseous risk.
- Uses multiple linear regression with backward elimination to attribute specific bone-change outcomes (PBT, LBT, PBH, LBH) to specific movement components (retraction, crown retraction, intrusion, root distal movement, lingual inclination) rather than reporting only pre/post aggregate change.
- Provides a movement-pattern-based framework that treatment planners can use prospectively (given a predicted tooth displacement vector from aligner software) to flag high-risk cases before finishing the setup.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective cohort, single institution (Wuhan University School & Hospital of Stomatology).
- **Population**: 94 patients, extraction orthodontic cases treated with clear aligner therapy (CAT); focus tooth = maxillary central incisor.
- **Imaging**: paired CBCT at pre-treatment (T0) and post-treatment (T1).
- **Bone measures**: labial bone thickness (LBT), palatal bone thickness (PBT), labial bone height (LBH), palatal bone height (PBH) — measured at different root levels (exact levels, e.g., apex/mid-root/cervical increments, not specified in the retrievable abstract text).
- **Tooth-movement measures**: 3D tooth displacement (retraction, intrusion, root distal movement, crown/lingual inclination) derived presumably from CBCT superimposition (T0 vs T1); classification into movement-pattern subgroups including uncontrolled vs controlled tipping.
- **Statistics**:
  - Paired t-test and Wilcoxon signed-rank test (per normality) for T0 vs T1 within-group comparison of tooth movement and bone change.
  - Two-sided Welch's t-test and Mann–Whitney U test for between-movement-pattern-group comparisons.
  - Multiple linear regression with backward elimination to identify independent factors associated with each bone-change outcome (PBT/LBT/PBH/LBH deltas).
- Methodological citations for the regression/statistical approach (per reference list): Lilliefors test for normality (ref 26), Davidson & MacKinnon econometrics text (ref 27), Cohen et al. applied multiple regression (ref 28), Kutner et al. applied linear statistical models (ref 32) — consistent with a standard OLS backward-elimination regression workflow with normality pre-checks.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Retrievable at abstract-level only (no full numeric tables in the accessible PDF):

- **Palatal bone**: defect (loss) was **significant** after CAT extraction/retraction treatment.
- **Labial bone**: **basically maintained** (i.e., not significantly reduced), contrasting with the palatal-side loss.
- **Tipping type**: uncontrolled tipping and controlled tipping produced **different bone-dimensional change patterns** (direction/magnitude not quantified in abstract).
- **Tooth retraction** → associated with **↓PBT** and **↑LBT** (opposite-direction change between palatal and labial thickness).
- **Crown retraction** (interpreted as an uncontrolled-tipping-type movement) → **dominant factor for ↓PBH** (palatal bone height reduction).
- **Incisor intrusion** → **↑PBT**, **↓LBT**, and **bone defect of both LBH and PBH** (i.e., intrusion is the most bone-height-adverse movement pattern of those studied, affecting both aspects).
- **Root distal movement** → associated with **resorption of LBT and LBH** (labial-side thickness and height loss).
- **Lingual inclination** → **evidently related to LBT loss** (labial thickness loss).
- No CI/p-value magnitudes, effect sizes, or regression coefficients were present in the retrievable abstract/reference text; these exist in the full-text body and supplementary Data S1 (`ocr70044-sup-0001-DataS1.docx`, 1.1 MB), which were not accessible to this ingest.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

**Author-stated (from abstract/conclusion):**
- Conclusion frames 3D tooth displacement/movement pattern as risk factors and recommends treatment planning account for predicted tooth movement to prevent alveolar bone defect — implying the authors view pre-treatment prediction/simulation as an actionable mitigation, though no specific threshold or cutoff values are given in the abstract.

**Identified by this source (methodological, from what's inferable):**
- Retrospective design — no control arm (e.g., non-extraction CAT or fixed-appliance extraction comparator); associations reported are correlational (regression), not causal.
- Single center, single population (Wuhan, China) — generalizability to other ethnicities/skeletal patterns/orthodontist protocols unconfirmed.
- Focus restricted to **maxillary central incisors only** — findings may not generalize to lateral incisors, canines, or mandibular incisors (contrast with [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/zhang-2025-clear-aligner-extraction-protocol-alveolar-bone-cbct]], which found mandibular anterior teeth had more severe bone loss than maxillary anterior teeth across protocols — root-level, tooth-position-specific comparison across studies is warranted).
- **This ingest is abstract-only.** No access to the full-text body, tables, exact subgroup sample sizes, regression coefficients/95% CI, or the supplementary Data S1 file. Numeric effect sizes and precise statistical significance levels for each factor (beyond directionality) could not be extracted or verified. Any quantitative synthesis referencing this paper must flag this limitation and should not be over-interpreted beyond the qualitative directional findings listed in Section 4.

## 7. Glossary

- **CAT**: Clear Aligner Therapy
- **CBCT**: Cone-Beam Computed Tomography
- **T0 / T1**: pre-treatment / post-treatment timepoints
- **LBT**: Labial Bone Thickness
- **PBT**: Palatal Bone Thickness
- **LBH**: Labial Bone Height
- **PBH**: Palatal Bone Height
- **Uncontrolled tipping**: tooth movement where the crown moves more than the root (net angular change), as opposed to bodily/translational movement
- **Controlled tipping**: tipping movement in which root apex position is relatively preserved while crown moves, contrasted here with "uncontrolled tipping"
- **Root distal movement**: distal (posterior) displacement of the root apex, as distinct from crown-only movement
- **Lingual inclination**: palatal/lingual tipping of the tooth's long axis
- **Backward elimination**: a stepwise regression variable-selection method removing the least significant predictor iteratively until only significant predictors remain
