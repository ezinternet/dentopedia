## Three-line Summary

Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR; PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane) of clinical studies in adults reporting implant loss within 6 months and before prosthetic loading: 1,838 records → 962 after dedup → 28 full-text assessed → 8 studies included.

The 8 included studies showed substantial heterogeneity in how "early implant failure" itself was defined and assessed, and the authors synthesized three interacting aetiological domains — local (impaired bone healing, infection), systemic (host susceptibility, genetic predisposition), and procedural (surgical trauma, inadequate primary stability).

The main clinical message is preventative and mechanical: optimize primary stability, minimize micromovements during healing, and improve wound closure/infection control — but the tiny evidence base (8 studies) and inconsistent outcome definitions mean this is a framework proposal, not a quantified risk profile.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Quantifies the *definitional* problem in the early-failure literature: because studies set their failure cut-off differently (at abutment connection, at loading, weeks after the final prosthesis, or at 1 year post-loading), their results cannot be pooled — this is the reason the field has no agreed risk profile.
- Proposes a three-domain aetiological framework (local / systemic / procedural) for early implant failure, with early loss framed as multifactorial interaction rather than a single dominant cause.
- Restates primary stability and micromovement control as the most actionable prevention targets, plus wound closure and infection control.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Scoping review following PRISMA-ScR.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane Library.
- **Eligibility**: clinical studies in adult humans reporting implant loss within 6 months and before prosthetic loading.
- **Screening flow**: 1,838 articles identified → 962 after deduplication → 28 assessed for eligibility → 8 included.
- **Data extracted**: study design, population characteristics, failure criteria, reported aetiological factors.
- **Outcome**: synthesis of aetiological factors + proposed structured classification.

## 7. Glossary
- **Early implant failure**: implant loss before prosthetic loading, typically within 6 months of placement (definition varies by study — the central problem this review raises).
- **PRISMA-ScR**: PRISMA extension for Scoping Reviews — reporting guideline for mapping evidence rather than pooling it.
- **Primary stability**: mechanical anchorage of the implant immediately after placement (bone-implant contact + friction), the main procedural determinant of early survival.
- **Micromovement**: healing-phase implant displacement beyond the tolerated threshold; a proposed key mechanism of fibrous encapsulation instead of osseointegration.
- **Host susceptibility**: patient-level biological predisposition (genetic/immunological) to impaired osseointegration.
