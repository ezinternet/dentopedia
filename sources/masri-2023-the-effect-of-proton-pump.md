## Why Ingested

Early-implant-failure batch: an Israeli single-center cohort (687 patients, 2971 implants) testing whether chronic proton pump inhibitor (PPI) use predicts early failure defined as removal up to 12 months after loading. It gives an implant-level adjusted OR (1.91) that is not confirmed at patient level, and complements the nested case-control study that flagged PPI purchase only in univariate analysis. → [[implants/survival/basson-2023-correlates-of-early-dental]]

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center cohort (Rabin Medical Center, Israel; implants placed 2011-2019; 687 patients, 2971 implants, 119 patients/555 implants with at least 1 year of continuous PPI use) testing PPI use as a risk factor for early implant failure (EIF), defined as removal within 12 months of loading.

Early failure was more frequent in PPI users at implant level (5.4% vs 3.5%); implant-level logistic regression gave PPI OR 1.91 (p=0.01) and anterior mandible location OR 2.28 (p<0.001), but the model explained only about 3% of variance and the patient-level model showed no PPI effect (p=0.11) and fitted poorly.

PPI users were older, had worse ASA status and more systemic disease and received shorter, narrower implants, so confounding is likely; the authors conclude PPI use "may" increase EIF and call for prospective trials, and the text contains an internal inconsistency about which predictors were significant.

## 세줄요약

이스라엘 Rabin Medical Center 단일기관 후향적 코호트 (2011-2019 식립, 환자 687명, 임플란트 2971개; 1년 이상 지속 복용한 양성자펌프억제제 (Proton Pump Inhibitor, PPI) 사용자 환자 119명/임플란트 555개)로, PPI 사용이 보철 부하 후 12개월 이내 제거로 정의한 조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF)의 위험인자인지 평가했다.

임플란트 수준에서 EIF는 PPI 사용자에서 더 흔했고 (5.4% vs 3.5%), 로지스틱 회귀에서 PPI OR 1.91 (p=0.01), 하악 전치부 위치 OR 2.28 (p<0.001)이었으나 모델 설명력은 약 3%였고, 환자 수준 모델에서는 PPI 효과가 없었으며 (p=0.11) 모델 적합도도 불량했다.

PPI 사용자는 고령이고 ASA 등급이 나쁘며 전신질환이 많고 더 짧고 좁은 임플란트를 받아 교란 가능성이 크며, 저자들은 PPI가 EIF를 증가시킬 "수 있다"고만 결론짓고 전향적 시험을 요청했다; 본문에는 어떤 예측인자가 유의했는지에 대한 내부 불일치가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Tests chronic PPI use (ATC A02BC; at least 1 continuous year) against early implant failure in a consecutive-patient cohort with exclusions for bisphosphonates, immunosuppressants, chemotherapy, head-and-neck radiation and maxillofacial trauma.
- Implant-level multivariate result: PPI users had 1.91 times the odds of EIF (p=0.01); anterior mandible OR 2.28 (p<0.001).
- Patient-level multivariate model did not show a PPI effect (p=0.11), so the association is not robust to the unit of analysis.
- Documents that PPI users differ systematically from non-users (age, ASA, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, diabetes, osteoporosis, implant size), which frames the finding as an association with a medication-using, sicker population.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort, STROBE reporting, ethics committee approval (0674-19rmc); single center; all consecutive patients with implant insertion 2011-2019.
- **Population**: 687 patients and 2971 implants. PPI group: 119 patients (17.3%) and 555 implants (18.7%); control 568 patients (82.7%) and 2416 implants (81.3%).
- **PPI exposure**: continuous use of a PPI (omeprazole, pantoprazole, lansoprazole, dexlansoprazole, esomeprazole, rabeprazole, dexrabeprazole or combinations) for at least 1 year. Specific agent and duration were not analysed.
- **Inclusion**: implant placed at the center; at least 12 months of follow-up from loading; available radiographs (panoramic, CT), clinical examination and follow-up. Exclusion: follow-up under 1 year from loading, bisphosphonates, pregnancy, immunosuppressants, maxillofacial trauma, chemotherapy, head-and-neck radiation.
- **Implants**: two-piece, internal hex, rough-surface titanium (Zimmer TaperedScrew-Vent, MIS Lance, Ditron MPI); experienced surgeons and prosthodontists.
- **Data collected**: age, gender, physical status (ASA), systemic diseases, HbA1c before/after prosthesis delivery in diabetes, smoking, implant location, implants per person, bone augmentation, brand, length, width, EIF.
- **EIF definition**: implant removal within 12 months from loading (so the window extends beyond the pre-prosthetic period).
- **Statistics**: SPSS 25; chi-square for categorical, Mann-Whitney for continuous; univariate tests at implant and patient level; logistic regression at both levels; significance at 5%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective, single center, no randomization; PPI users were older, sicker (ASA 3, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, diabetes, osteoporosis) and received different implants, so confounding by indication is likely. Variance explained by the models was only about 3-4%.
- The patient-level model, which treats clustering correctly at person level, found no PPI effect and fitted poorly; the implant-level analysis does not obviously account for multiple implants in one patient (not stated in the text).
- PPI agent, dose and duration were not analysed (authors acknowledge); exposure defined only as at least 1 year of continuous use.
- Early failure window was up to 12 months after loading, and inclusion required at least 12 months of post-loading follow-up, so this is not a pre-prosthetic-only failure definition.
- Source-data issues in the retrieved text: (a) the Discussion says logistic regression showed a significant contribution only for anterior mandible (OR 2.28), while Results and Conclusions report PPI OR 1.91 (p=0.01); (b) the Introduction states the null hypothesis as PPI use "increases the chance of EIF"; (c) the patient-level age paragraph ends with a statement about physical status, and the ASA 1 percentages for patient level (47.6% vs 7.7%) are listed in an order that conflicts with the surrounding text; (d) most test statistics and all tables were stripped from the extraction. ASA 3 showing lower failure odds (OR 0.34) is unexplained.
- The conclusion is hedged ("may increase EIF"); authors call for prospective randomized trials.

## 7. Glossary

- **PPI (Proton Pump Inhibitor, 양성자펌프억제제)**: gastric acid suppressant (ATC A02BC) such as omeprazole or pantoprazole.
- **EIF (Early Implant Failure, 조기 임플란트 실패)**: here, implant removal within 12 months of loading.
- **ASA (American Society of Anesthesiologists physical status, 신체 등급)**: 1 healthy to 3 severe systemic disease.
- **OR (Odds Ratio, 오즈비)**: relative odds of the outcome in exposed vs unexposed.
