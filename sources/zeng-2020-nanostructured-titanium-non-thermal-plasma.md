## Three-line Summary

In vitro and in vivo (rat femur) study testing a handheld piezoelectric direct-discharge non-thermal plasma on alkali-treated titanate nanonetwork (TNS) titanium surfaces, assessing both decontamination (S. aureus biofilm) and osteogenic response (rat BMSCs, ALP, ECM mineralization, gene expression, bone formation).

Plasma effectively eliminated S. aureus biofilm without significantly altering the nanostructure or roughness of the TNS surface; rat BMSC osteogenic activity (ALP, ECM mineralization) and in vivo bone formation were improved by the TNS + plasma combination.

This combined nanostructure + plasma strategy is promising for treating implant surfaces both before placement and as a peri-implantitis decontamination approach, though the individual plasma vs nanostructure contributions are inseparable and no human/clinical data exist.

## 세줄요약

TNS(알칼리처리 티타네이트 나노네트워크) 티타늄에 휴대형 압전직접방전(PDD) 비열 플라즈마 적용 — in vitro(S. aureus 바이오필름 제거, 쥐 BMSC 골형성) 및 in vivo(쥐 대퇴골 골형성) 동시 평가.

플라즈마는 나노구조·거칠기를 손상시키지 않고 S. aureus 바이오필름을 효과적으로 제거했으며, TNS+플라즈마 조합은 BMSC 골모세포 분화(ALP, ECM 광화) 및 in vivo 골형성을 개선.

나노구조+플라즈마 이중 전략은 식립 직전 표면 활성화 및 임플란트주위염 제염 모두에 유망하나, 두 개입의 독립 기여도 분리 불가 및 임상 데이터 부재가 한계.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: International Journal of Molecular Sciences 2020, 21(10):3533
- DOI: 10.3390/ijms21103533
- Study type: in vitro (cell + antibacterial) + in vivo rat femur

## 2. Key Contributions

- Combines surface nanostructuring (TNS) with handheld plasma; achieves decontamination AND bone response on one surface.
- Plasma cleans biofilm without destroying the nanostructure — key for re-using/rescuing a nanotextured surface.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- TNS specimens treated with piezoelectric direct discharge (PDD) plasma.
- Decontamination tested vs Staphylococcus aureus.
- Rat BMSCs: attachment, ALP, ECM mineralization, osteogenic gene expression; in vivo rat femur for bone response.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Nanotopography and surface roughness not significantly changed by plasma.
- Cell and bone-formation activity improved by TNS + plasma.
- Plasma effectively eliminated biofilm contamination.
- Conclusion: promising for treating nanomaterials immediately before implantation and as a peri-implantitis strategy.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Combined intervention (nanostructure + plasma) makes plasma-specific contribution harder to isolate.
- Animal (rat) + in vitro; no human/clinical data.
