## Why Ingested

기존 위키의 타액 진단 축([[oral-medicine/li-2024-salivary-diagnostics-opportunities-challenges]], [[oral-medicine/nonaka-2023-saliva-diagnostics-salivaomics-exosomics-liquid-biopsy]])과 GCF 바이오마커 축([[overviews/gingival-crevicular-fluid-biomarker-diagnostics-overview]], [[periodontics/cosin-villanueva-2024-micrornas-gingival-crevicular-fluid-periodontal]])은 있으나, **타액 프로테오믹스로 치주염을 진단하는 어느 단백질이 실제로 재현되는가**를 정면으로 다룬 체계적 고찰이 없었다. 본 SR (13편, untargeted proteomics)은 여러 연구에서 일관되게 재현된 판별 단백질(S100A8·C3·profilin-1·fibrinogen 상승, cystatin-SN·leukocyte elastase inhibitor 감소)을 추려 [[overviews/saliva-diagnostics-and-salivary-gland-dysfunction-overview]]에 치주 진단 분기를 채우는 근거로 활용.

## One-line Summary

Systematic review of 13 untargeted salivary-proteomics case–control/longitudinal studies; complement C3, profilin-1, S100A8, and fibrinogen were consistently over-expressed and cystatin-SN and leukocyte elastase inhibitor under-expressed in periodontitis, but only S100A8 had a poolable diagnostic accuracy (meta-analytic AUC 0.71, 95% CI 0.66–0.75).

## 한줄요약

타액 프로테오믹스 논문 13편의 체계적 고찰 — 치주염에서 C3·profilin-1·S100A8·fibrinogen은 일관 상승, cystatin-SN·leukocyte elastase inhibitor는 일관 감소했으나, 진단정확도를 메타분석할 수 있었던 마커는 S100A8 하나뿐(AUC 0.71)으로 아직 단독 스크리닝 검사로 쓰기엔 근거가 미성숙.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic review to synthesize **untargeted** (discovery, hypothesis-free) salivary proteomics specifically across periodontitis vs. health/gingivitis, screening 461 records → 13 included studies.
- Applies a **consistency analysis**: only proteins differentially expressed in ≥3 independent studies are called "consistent," filtering discovery noise (17 of 52 twice-reported proteins showed conflicting direction).
- Identifies a reproducible signature — **up in periodontitis**: complement C3, profilin-1, S100A8, fibrinogen; **up in health**: cystatin-SN, leukocyte elastase inhibitor.
- Performs a **targeted add-on search + meta-analysis** of diagnostic accuracy, finding only S100A8 reported in ≥2 studies (pooled AUC 0.71, low heterogeneity I²=2.4%).
- Quality-appraises all 13 studies with a **QUADOMICS-modified NIH tool** (2 high, 9 moderate, 1 low, 1 very-low), exposing that only 4/13 validated findings in an independent cohort and only 2/13 reported sample-size justification.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA systematic review; PECO — P: systemically healthy adults; E: clinical periodontitis; C: periodontal health or gingivitis; O: salivary protein-expression differences and diagnostic accuracy.
- **Search**: Medline/PubMed, Scopus, Embase, Cochrane through Oct 2023; MeSH/EMTREE + keyword string ("periodontitis…" AND MS/proteomics terms AND "saliva/salivary"), duplicate screening by two calibrated reviewers (κ = 0.86 abstracts, 0.91 full text).
- **Eligibility**: human observational (cross-sectional/case–control/cohort) with proteomics-derived salivary protein profiles; excluded <10 subjects/group, systemic disease, pregnancy, reviews/case reports/animal/in-vitro, non-English.
- **Included studies**: 13 (11 cross-sectional, 2 longitudinal treatment studies); n per study 20–141; conducted in Europe (6), Asia (4), South America (3). Saliva: 9 unstimulated whole, 4 stimulated whole. Detection: LC–MS/MS (8) or MALDI-TOF MS (5).
- **Synthesis**: qualitative consistency analysis (broad meta-analysis **not feasible** due to methodological/outcome heterogeneity — proteins counted 2–4161 per study, units ranged from Da peaks to μg/μL to fold-change). Targeted secondary search + fixed-effect (inverse-variance) meta-analysis only where ≥2 studies reported AUC/sensitivity/specificity for the same marker. Pathway enrichment via Flame (GO-BP, GO-CC). Quality via modified QUADOMICS (15 items, 0–15).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Consistent signature (≥3 studies)**: over-expressed in periodontitis — complement C3, profilin-1, S100A8, fibrinogen (also keratin I/II, alpha-2-macroglobulin reported ≥3× but with mixed direction); under-expressed (i.e., higher in health) — cystatin-SN, leukocyte elastase inhibitor.
- **Diagnostic accuracy within included studies**: Antezack (6 unidentified peaks → decision tree) sensitivity 70.3% / specificity 77.8%; Bostanci ML 5-protein panel AUC >0.97; Grant best panel (α-1-acid glycoprotein + MMP-9 + pyruvate kinase + S100A8 + age) AUC 0.970 for health/gingivitis vs. periodontitis, and 0.789 for mild-moderate vs. advanced; Tang peptide-peak AUCs 0.688–0.860.
- **Pooled marker**: only **S100A8** appeared in ≥2 studies with extractable accuracy → meta-analytic **AUC 0.71 (95% CI 0.66–0.75), I²=2.4%** (low heterogeneity) — modest as a standalone test.
- **Pathway enrichment**: consistent proteins map to negative regulation of endopeptidase activity, regulation of response to external stimulus, humoral immune response, and regulation of complement activation; localized to extracellular space/cell periphery and secretory granules/vesicles.
- **Quality**: 2 high (Bostanci 12.0, Grant 12.75), 1 very-low (Chaiyarit 3.25), 1 low (Mertens 6.5), 9 moderate. Recurrent weaknesses: no sample-size justification (11/13), unclear index-test↔reference-standard interval (most), external independent-cohort validation in only 4/13.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Author-stated**: high methodological/reporting heterogeneity prevented broad meta-analysis; few markers had basic diagnostic-accuracy metrics; single-marker discrimination between periodontitis and gingivitis largely untested; findings need larger comparative/longitudinal validation and standardized protocols (standardized saliva collection, ≥3 studies threshold arbitrary but conservative).
- **Additional**: dominance of cross-sectional case–control designs (cases often younger/older than controls — confounded by age, e.g., several studies had control mean age 24–27 vs. case 45–60) limits causal/temporal inference; smoking excluded in only 8/13; a single pooled marker (S100A8, AUC 0.71) is well below a deployable screening threshold; no cost/point-of-care feasibility analysis; classification systems (1999 vs. 2018) mixed across studies.

## 7. Glossary

- **Untargeted (discovery) proteomics**: hypothesis-free screening of the whole detectable proteome, vs. targeted assays (e.g., ELISA, MRM) measuring pre-specified proteins.
- **LC–MS/MS**: liquid chromatography–tandem mass spectrometry; high-accuracy protein identification/quantification.
- **MALDI-TOF MS**: matrix-assisted laser desorption/ionization time-of-flight mass spectrometry.
- **QUADOMICS**: QUADAS-derived quality-assessment tool adapted for omics diagnostic-accuracy studies (15 items here).
- **Consistency analysis**: counting a protein as reproducible only when ≥3 independent studies report it differentially expressed in the same direction.
- **S100A8 / S100A9 (calprotectin)**: calcium-binding neutrophil alarmins; inflammation markers repeatedly elevated in periodontitis saliva.
- **Cystatin-SN (CST1)**: salivary cysteine-protease inhibitor, protective; markedly reduced/absent in periodontitis saliva.
- **AUC**: area under the ROC curve; 0.5 = chance, 1.0 = perfect discrimination.
