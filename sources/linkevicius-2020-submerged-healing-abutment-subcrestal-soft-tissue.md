## Why Ingested

The wiki's subcrestal-placement cluster already holds the depth-comparison endpoints: [[wiki/implants/mbl/stacchi-2023-marginal-bone-changes-platform-switched-conical]] (1 vs 2 mm subcrestal, no difference), [[wiki/implants/mbl/sun-2023-clinical-radiographic-results-crestal-subcrestal]] (1 mm subcrestal reduced marginal bone loss), and the mechanistic [[wiki/implants/mbl/terzioglu-2024-effect-supracrestal-tissue-height-marginal]] (supracrestal tissue height × depth). This 2-year RCT adds the missing **active comparator** — vertical soft-tissue thickening by tenting over a 2 mm healing abutment at an epicrestal implant — and directly tests it against 1.5 mm subcrestal placement in thin-tissue patients. It extends the earlier author-group finding in [[wiki/implants/mbl/linkevicius-2015-vertical-soft-tissue-thickness-crestal-bone-platform-switching]] that thin mucosa (≤2 mm) undermines platform-switched bone stability, and belongs with the depth axis synthesized in [[wiki/overviews/platform-switching-marginal-bone-preservation-synthesis]].

## Three-line Summary

2-year randomized clinical trial (32 patients, 40 submerged bone-level platform-switched implants) comparing 1.5 mm subcrestal placement with epicrestal placement plus soft-tissue tenting over 2 mm healing abutments in patients with vertical soft tissue ≤2 mm.

At 2 years, subcrestal Group 1 lost 0.18 ± 0.32 mm of crestal bone versus 0.51 ± 0.4 mm in epicrestal/tented Group 2 (P = .001); subcrestal bone remodeling was 1.17 ± 0.51 mm.

Epicrestal tenting raised vertical tissue thickness from 1.85 ± 0.26 to 3.65 ± 0.41 mm (P = .005) yet preserved bone less well; subcrestal placement is the more effective bone-preserving strategy in thin-tissue patients (abstract-only).

## 세줄요약

2년 무작위 임상시험(Randomized Clinical Trial, RCT) — 수직적 연조직 두께 ≤2 mm인 32명에게 골수준 플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching, PS) 임플란트 40개를 식립해 1.5 mm 골하방 식립(Subcrestal Placement) 군과 2 mm 치유 지대주(Healing Abutment)로 연조직 텐팅(Soft Tissue Tenting)한 골정상 식립 군을 비교.

2년 후 골하방 군의 골능 소실은 0.18 ± 0.32 mm, 텐팅 골정상 군은 0.51 ± 0.4 mm로 유의한 차이(P = .001)가 있었고, 골하방 군의 골 리모델링은 1.17 ± 0.51 mm였다.

텐팅은 연조직 두께를 1.85 ± 0.26 mm에서 3.65 ± 0.41 mm로 유의하게 증가시켰으나(P = .005) 골 보존은 골하방 식립이 우위 — 얇은 연조직 환자에서 골하방 식립이 더 효과적(초록만 확보).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Direct randomized head-to-head of two thin-tissue strategies — 1.5 mm subcrestal placement versus epicrestal placement with 2 mm healing-abutment soft-tissue tenting — around submerged bone-level platform-switched implants.
- Quantifies the 2-year crestal bone advantage of subcrestal placement (0.18 ± 0.32 mm) over epicrestal tenting (0.51 ± 0.4 mm, P = .001) in patients with ≤2 mm vertical tissue.
- Separates the two outcomes: tenting significantly increased vertical soft-tissue thickness (P = .005) but did not match subcrestal placement for crestal bone preservation.
- Reports subcrestal bone remodeling (distance from bone crest to implant neck) of 1.17 ± 0.51 mm at 2 years.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Randomized clinical trial, 2-year follow-up (published as an original article).
- **Population**: 32 patients with vertically thin soft tissue of 2 mm or less.
- **Implants**: 40 submerged bone-level platform-switched implants, allocated to two groups — Group 1: 1.5 mm subcrestal placement; Group 2: epicrestal placement with soft-tissue tenting over 2 mm healing abutments.
- **Second-stage / restoration**: At second-stage surgery implants received 4 mm healing abutments; soft-tissue thickness was measured in the epicrestal group; implants were then restored with zirconia-based screw-retained single restorations.
- **Radiographic timing**: Second-stage surgery, restoration delivery, and 2 years of follow-up.
- **Outcome definitions**: Bone loss = distance from implant neck to first radiographically visible bone-to-implant contact; bone remodeling = distance from bone crest to implant neck.
- **Statistics**: Mann–Whitney U test; significance set at 0.05.
- **Full-text status**: Abstract-only — the retrieved PDF is a publisher landing page; no methods tables, CONSORT flow, allocation-concealment detail, or per-group radiographic tables are available, so only abstract-level detail is reported here.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Group 1 — 1.5 mm subcrestal | Group 2 — epicrestal + 2 mm tenting | Comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crestal bone loss at 2 years | 0.18 ± 0.32 mm | 0.51 ± 0.4 mm | P = .001 (significant) |
| Bone remodeling (bone crest → implant neck) | 1.17 ± 0.51 mm | not reported in abstract | — |
| Vertical tissue thickness, epicrestal group | — | 1.85 ± 0.26 mm before → 3.65 ± 0.41 mm after 2 mm healing abutment | P = .005 (significant) |

- Authors' conclusion: subcrestal implant placement significantly reduces crestal bone loss compared with vertical soft-tissue thickening by tenting of epicrestally placed implants, although soft-tissue tenting significantly increases soft-tissue thickness.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract-only artifact**: all figures above are transcribed verbatim from the publisher abstract; no full-text tables, confidence intervals, effect sizes, or flow of participants are available for verification.
- Group-level implant counts (how the 40 implants split between the two groups) and patient-level allocation details are not given in the abstract.
- No implant survival, peri-implantitis, marginal soft-tissue health, or patient-reported outcomes are reported in the abstract.
- Bone remodeling is reported for the subcrestal group only; the epicrestal comparator value is unavailable.
- Follow-up is 2 years; longer-term stability of the tenting-derived soft-tissue gain is not addressed in the abstract.

## 7. Glossary

- **Subcrestal placement (골하방 식립)**: Implant platform positioned apical to the alveolar bone crest (here 1.5 mm below).
- **Epicrestal placement (골정상 식립)**: Implant platform positioned at the level of the alveolar bone crest.
- **Soft-tissue tenting (연조직 텐팅)**: Using an oversized (here 2 mm) healing abutment to push and thicken the peri-implant soft tissue vertically.
- **Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭, PS)**: A narrower abutment seated on a wider implant platform, shifting the implant–abutment interface horizontally inward.
- **Bone remodeling (골 리모델링)**: As defined by the authors, the distance from the bone crest to the implant neck.
- **Mann–Whitney U test (만-휘트니 U 검정)**: Nonparametric rank-sum test used for between-group comparisons here.
