## Why Ingested
Independent external validation of [[complaint-management/bmjqs-2015-004596]] (HCAT) outside its UK development setting -- evidence that the classification axis transfers to other systems and to compensation-claim data, supporting reuse of HCAT-style coding in a Korean dental context.

## Three-line Summary

Reliability study (BMJ Open 2019) applying the full HCAT (Healthcare Complaints Analysis Tool) to a random sample of 140 Danish emergency-care patient compensation claims, with multiple trained raters, to test inter-assessor reliability and cross-setting validity outside the UK development context.

Multiple trained raters achieved high inter-assessor reliability across HCAT domains; however, the emergency-care compensation-claim data source produced skewed domain coverage (clinical/safety-heavy, under-sampling relational and management categories).

HCAT can be applied to compensation-claim data and non-UK settings, but a single clinical-setting sample cannot fully validate the instrument's complete domain structure; replication across diverse settings is needed.

## 세줄요약

신뢰도 연구 (BMJ Open 2019): HCAT(의료 민원 분석 도구)를 덴마크 응급 의료 환자 보상청구 무작위 표본 140건에 다수 훈련된 평가자가 적용하여 평가자 간 신뢰도 및 UK 외 설정 타당성 검증.

복수 훈련 평가자 간 높은 신뢰도 달성; 단, 응급 보상청구 데이터 출처로 인해 임상/안전 도메인 쏠림 — 관계적·관리 범주 과소 표본.

HCAT는 보상청구 데이터 및 비UK 환경에 적용 가능하나, 단일 임상 환경 표본으로는 전체 도메인 구조 검증 불충분 — 다양한 환경에서 반복 연구 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First test of the *entire* HCAT instrument (domains, categories, subcategories) outside its development setting.
- Demonstrates HCAT can be applied to patient *compensation claims*, not only complaint letters.
- Reports high inter-assessor reliability with multiple trained raters.
- Flags that domain/subcategory coverage can be skewed by case source (emergency compensation claims).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Random sample of 140 completed compensation cases from the Danish Patient Compensation Association (emergency hospital care). Multiple trained raters applied the full HCAT. Intra- and inter-assessor reliability assessed.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Multiple trained raters achieved high inter-assessor reliability. Coverage of HCAT domains and subcategory severity levels was skewed (clinical-heavy in a compensation-claim sample), leading the authors to caution that the study cannot stand alone and needs replication across settings.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
Single data source (emergency compensation claims) skews toward clinical/safety problems and under-samples relational/management categories, limiting full-instrument evaluation. Modest sample (n=140). Findings setting-specific.

## 6. Related Work
Validation study of Gillespie & Reader (2016). Complements UK applications by the LSE/Imperial group.

## 7. Glossary
- **Inter-assessor reliability**: agreement between different raters coding the same case.
- **Compensation claim**: a formal injury-compensation case, distinct from a complaint letter.
