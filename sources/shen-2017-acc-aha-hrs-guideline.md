## Three-line Summary

Multi-society clinical practice guideline (ACC/AHA/HRS, 16-member writing committee) for the evaluation and management of patients with syncope, published in Circulation 2017;136(5):e60–e122 (epub 2017-03-09), with an erratum in Circulation 2017;136(16):e271–e272.
At the record level the guideline establishes the scope only: a structured approach to syncope that separates reflex (neurally mediated) syncope from cardiac syncope, and grades risk as short-term versus long-term — the framing, not the recommendations, is what this artifact supports.
No recommendation class (I/IIa/IIb/III), level of evidence (A/B/C), or specific diagnostic/therapeutic statement was retrieved, so none is reproduced here; the full recommendation set remains unread.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Scope anchor for the differential**: the guideline's remit is *evaluation and management* of syncope as a symptom, not a single disease — i.e. it addresses the work-up pathway rather than one therapy.
- **Reflex vs cardiac distinction as the organizing axis**: the title/scope position the document around separating neurally mediated (reflex) syncope from cardiac syncope, which is precisely the assumption dental and procedural-sedation literature makes when it treats a vasovagal episode as benign.
- **Risk stratification framing**: the guideline is structured around stratifying patients by short-term and long-term risk rather than by event severity at presentation — the framing that decides who needs cardiac work-up versus reassurance.
- **Multi-society consensus**: ACC + AHA + HRS authorship makes this the reference standard for the topic in North America, which is why the dental literature cites it without re-deriving the cardiology.
- **Explicitly NOT a contribution of this page**: no individual recommendation, class, or LOE is reported, because none was retrieved.

## 7. Glossary

- **Syncope (실신)**: transient loss of consciousness due to cerebral hypoperfusion, with rapid onset, short duration, and spontaneous complete recovery.
- **Reflex (neurally mediated) syncope (반사성 실신)**: syncope from a reflex causing vasodilation and/or bradycardia — vasovagal syncope is the prototype; generally benign.
- **Cardiac syncope (심장성 실신)**: syncope from an arrhythmic or structural cardiac cause; associated with higher risk and the reason a work-up is triggered.
- **Risk stratification (위험층화)**: sorting patients by probability of short-term serious events (e.g. in the ED) versus long-term events, to decide admission, testing, or outpatient reassurance.
- **Class of recommendation / Level of evidence (권고등급 / 근거수준)**: the ACC/AHA grading axes (Class I–III; LOE A/B/C) — **not retrieved for this guideline in this artifact**.
- **ACC / AHA / HRS**: American College of Cardiology / American Heart Association / Heart Rhythm Society.
