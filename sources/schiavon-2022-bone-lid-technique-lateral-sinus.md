## Why Ingested
Introduces and evidence-grades the bone lid technique — repositioning the osteotomized lateral bone window as an autologous barrier instead of discarding it or covering the antrostomy with a membrane — extending the window-closure comparison already established in [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/starch-jensen-2019-sinus-augmentation-barrier-membrane-window]] (membrane vs. no membrane) to a third technique option, backed by dedicated animal meta-analysis and two small clinical studies.

## Three-line Summary
SR+MA (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42020184317) of 5 animal studies (4 rabbit, 1 sheep) and 2 clinical studies (1 RCT, 1 case–control; 15 bone lids, 122 implants) evaluating the repositioned bone lid technique in lateral sinus lift, searched through April 2022.
Random-effects meta-analysis of 3 animal studies found significantly higher new bone formation with the bone lid vs. resorbable membrane at 2 weeks (P=0.04) and 8 weeks (P=0.03, not significant at 4 weeks P=0.08); clinical studies found no significant difference in bone-to-implant contact (92–93.5%), bone regeneration, or implant survival (99–100%) between bone lid, membrane, and autogenous-graft closure.
Moderate evidence supports a bone-formation benefit for the bone lid over resorbable membranes in animals, with a low complication rate (1/15 clinical perforation, no lasting dislocation), but only 2 small, moderate-to-high-risk-of-bias clinical studies (<15-month follow-up) currently exist to confirm this in humans.

## 세줄요약
체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42020184317) — 동물실험 5편(토끼 4, 양 1) + 임상연구 2편(RCT 1, 환자-대조군 1연구; 골개(Bone Lid) 15례, 임플란트 122개), 2022년 4월까지 검색, 측방 상악동거상술(Lateral Sinus Lift) 시 절제한 골창을 원위치로 되돌려 덮는 골개(Bone Lid) 술식을 평가.
무작위효과모형 메타분석(동물실험 3편)에서 골개군이 흡수성 차폐막(Resorbable Membrane)군보다 신생골 형성이 2주(P=0.04)·8주(P=0.03)에 유의하게 높았음(4주는 P=0.08로 비유의); 임상연구에서는 골-임플란트 접촉률(Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC, 92–93.5%)·골재생·임플란트 생존율(99–100%) 모두 골개·차폐막·자가골 이식 간 유의차 없음.
동물실험에서는 골개가 흡수성 차폐막보다 신생골 형성에 유리하다는 중등도 근거가 있고 합병증률도 낮으나(임상 천공 1/15, 지속적 골개 탈락 없음), 인체 적용을 뒷받침할 임상연구는 편향위험 중~고도의 소규모 연구 2편(추적 15개월 미만)뿐이라 추가 확인이 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA to isolate the repositioned bone lid as the specific intervention variable in lateral sinus lift (PROSPERO CRD42020184317, PRISMA-compliant).
- Random-effects meta-analysis (3 rabbit studies) quantifies a statistically significant new-bone-formation advantage for the bone lid over a resorbable membrane at 2 and 8 weeks.
- Reports a low overall complication rate — 1 membrane perforation among 15 clinical bone lid cases (6.7%), no lasting bone lid dislocation in any pre-clinical study — and high short-term implant survival (99–100%) regardless of closure method.
- Applies dual risk-of-bias frameworks (ARRIVE 2.0 + SYRCLE for animal studies, RoB 2 + ROBINS-I for clinical studies), finding only 2/5 animal studies at low risk and the RCT at "some concerns."

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA), PROSPERO-registered (CRD42020184317).
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed), Web of Science, Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials, plus hand search of major oral/maxillofacial surgery and implant dentistry journals and reference lists. English-language studies only, through 5 April 2022.
- **n**: 5 animal studies (4 rabbit — Moon et al., Sohn et al. ×2, Omori et al.; 1 sheep — Perini et al.) + 2 clinical studies (1 RCT — Johansson et al., n=24 patients/101 implants; 1 case–control — Sohn et al., n=10 patients/21 implants). Clinical total: 15 bone lids, 122 implants.
- **Outcomes**: Primary — percentage of new bone formation (histomorphometric). Secondary — bone window/lid healing, dental implant survival rate.
- **Risk of bias**: ARRIVE 2.0 (21 items) + SYRCLE (10 items) for animal studies; RoB 2 (5 domains) for the RCT; ROBINS-I (7 domains) for the case–control study.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Meta-analysis (random-effects, 3 rabbit studies)**: new bone formation significantly higher in the bone lid group at 2 weeks (P=0.04) and 8 weeks (P=0.03); not significant at 4 weeks (P=0.08). Random-effects model used due to significant heterogeneity.
- **Perini et al. (sheep)**: bone regeneration 22.1±12.6% (bone lid) vs 7.5±4.5% (β-TCP + resorbable membrane), P=0.028; bone interpenetration 66.1±14.7% vs 44.2±15.1%, P=0.046.
- **Moon et al. (rabbit)**: new bone at 8 weeks 31.28±3.51% (bone lid) vs 21.74±2.40% (β-TCP + collagen membrane); each timepoint significant vs. the previous (P<0.05).
- **Sohn et al. (rabbit, DBBM+collagen control)**: new bone at 8 weeks 65.96±2.99% (bone lid, no graft) vs 23.26±2.07% (control); significantly faster/denser regeneration in the bone lid group.
- **RCT (Johansson et al., n=24/101 implants)**: mean residual bone height 4.3 mm (bone lid) vs 3.5 mm (collagen membrane) vs 4.3 mm (autogenous bone graft), no significant difference; micro-CT BIC on retrieved experimental implants 93.5% (bone lid) vs 92.0% (CM) vs 93.5% (ABG), no significant difference; 1/101 implant failure (in the membrane group).
- **Case–control (Sohn et al., n=10/21 implants)**: no significant difference in bone regeneration between bone lid and non-resorbable membrane; 100% implant survival at 6–12 months; 1 membrane perforation, managed with resorbable membrane + fibrin adhesive.
- **Perforation rate**: ≈6.7% (1/15) across the clinical bone lid cases, within the literature's reported 3.6–44% range for lateral sinus lift generally.
- **Risk of bias**: only 2/5 animal studies (Perini, Omori) rated low risk (SYRCLE); RCT overall "some concerns" (RoB 2); case–control 4 low-risk / 3 moderate-risk domains (ROBINS-I).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small number of included studies (5 animal + 2 clinical), several at moderate-to-high risk of bias.
- No uniform standard control treatment — comparators varied across studies (resorbable membrane, non-resorbable membrane, autogenous bone graft, or no covering), limiting pooled comparison.
- Animal-to-human transferability concerns: different sinus anatomy, different assessment methods, and implant survival cannot be assessed in small-animal models.
- Clinical evidence limited to short follow-up (7–14.8 months); implant-related outcomes were secondary, not primary, aims in both clinical studies.
- Only 1 RCT among the 2 included clinical studies; authors call for future clinical trials using high-resolution 3D imaging to confirm the animal-derived bone-formation benefit in humans.

## 6. Related Work
- Starch-Jensen et al. 2019 ([[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/starch-jensen-2019-sinus-augmentation-barrier-membrane-window]]): SR+MA (6 RCTs) found no significant difference in implant survival, bone formation, or complications between barrier-membrane and no-membrane closure of the lateral window. Schiavon 2022 extends this window-closure comparison to a third option — the repositioned bone lid — with dedicated animal meta-analytic evidence of a bone-formation advantage, while its own clinical data remain consistent with Starch-Jensen's "closure method doesn't significantly change human outcomes" finding. Schiavon et al. cite this paper directly in their own reference list (ref. 12).
- Del Fabbro et al. 2025 ([[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/del-fabbro-2025-sinus-augmentation-materials-lateral-approach]]): SR (49 RCTs) found graft material choice (DBBM/autogenous/synthetic) does not affect short-term implant survival (84.6–100%). Several of Schiavon 2022's included studies used no graft material at all — the bone lid technique was tested as a graftless closure method — a contrasting axis to Del Fabbro's materials-focused comparison.
- Díaz-Olivares et al. 2021 ([[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/diaz-olivares-2021-schneiderian-membrane-perforation-sinus-lift]]): SR+MA reporting a general lateral-window Schneiderian membrane perforation rate of 30.6%, with repaired-perforation implant survival not significantly different from non-perforated sites. Schiavon 2022's clinical bone lid series reported a lower 6.7% (1/15) perforation rate, successfully managed with resorbable membrane + fibrin adhesive — consistent with, not contradicting, Díaz-Olivares' size-based repair logic.
- Wallace & Froum 2003 ([[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/wallace-2003-effect-maxillary-sinus-augmentation-survival]]): early SR + meta-regression benchmark putting average lateral-window implant survival at 91.8% (range 61.7–100%). Schiavon 2022's clinical implant survival (99–100%) sits at the high end of this historical range, though drawn from only 2 small, short-follow-up studies rather than Wallace's 34-study lateral-window pool.

## 7. Glossary
- **Bone lid (bony lid)**: The osteotomized lateral bone window, removed during antrostomy and repositioned back into its original site at the end of surgery, acting as an autogenous, osteoconductive barrier instead of a membrane.
- **β-TCP**: β-tricalcium phosphate, a synthetic resorbable bone graft substitute.
- **DBBM**: Deproteinized bovine bone mineral, a bovine-derived xenograft material.
- **BIC**: Bone-to-implant contact — the percentage of an implant's surface in direct histological/micro-CT contact with bone.
- **ARRIVE guidelines**: Animals in Research: Reporting In Vivo Experiments — a 21-item checklist for animal study reporting quality.
- **SYRCLE**: A 10-item risk-of-bias assessment tool designed specifically for animal intervention studies.
