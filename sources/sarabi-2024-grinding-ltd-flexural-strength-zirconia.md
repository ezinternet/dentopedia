## Why Ingested

Addresses a gap in [[dental-materials/zirconia/koenig-2021-ltd-monolithic-zirconia-prospective]]: does chairside grinding-then-reglaze actually protect against LTD, or is polishing better? This study directly compares 8 surface-treatment × aging combinations with XRD monoclinic quantification and 3-point flexural strength.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro RCT-equivalent (n=64 bar specimens, Y-TZP, 8 groups), LTD = 134°C/40h steam + pH-cycling + toothbrushing: grinding and polishing increased flexural strength vs standard polish or glaze.

Aging did not significantly decrease flexural strength in any group (P=0.109–0.970); however, monoclinic content rose sharply in all aged groups (Gr-Ag: 51.2%, Gl-Ag: 41.8%, Po-Ag: 38.8%, Sp-Ag: 15.4%).

The paradox — high monoclinic content yet unchanged strength — reflects transformation toughening: surface compressive stress from t→m offset crack propagation until a clinical threshold is reached.

## 세줄요약

Y-TZP 64개 막대 시편, 8군(표면처리 × 에이징), LTD=134°C/40h 증기+pH 사이클+칫솔질: 연마·폴리싱이 표준연마·글레이징보다 굴곡강도 유의하게 높음.

에이징이 굴곡강도를 유의하게 감소시키지 않음(P=0.109–0.970). 그러나 Gr-Ag군 단사정 비율 51.2%로 급증.

강도 유지 이유는 변태강화(transformation toughening): t→m 전환 시 체적팽창이 표면 압축응력을 생성해 균열 진행 억제.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates grinding and polishing significantly increase baseline flexural strength vs standard polish/glaze in Y-TZP
- Shows that combined LTD (steam + pH cycling + toothbrushing) does not reduce flexural strength despite causing substantial t→m transformation
- Confirms glazing restores surface smoothness but does NOT restore mechanical strength lost to grinding — and does NOT protect against LTD monoclinic content rise
- Grinding group showed highest monoclinic before aging (12.6%) due to transformation toughening activation; highest after aging (51.2%)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 64 bar-shaped specimens (20×4×2 mm), milled from pre-sintered Y-TZP, sintered at 1450°C
- Groups: Sp (standard polish), Gr (grinding, blue-yellow band instrument), Gl (grinding + overglaze, 820°C), Po (grinding + 2-step intraoral polish)
- Each × aged: LTD 40h at 134°C/2 bar (ISO 13356) → pH cycling 15 cycles (pH 4.3 demineralizing / pH 7 remineralizing) → toothbrushing 10,000 cycles
- XRD (PANalytical X'Pert PRO) for monoclinic quantification (Gravies & Nicholson equation); 3-point flexural test (ISO 6872)
- Two-way ANOVA + Tukey's post-hoc; Pearson's correlation FS vs monoclinic

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | Flexural Strength (MPa) | Monoclinic Before Aging | Monoclinic After Aging |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sp | 729.7 ± 38 | 0% | 15.4% |
| Gr | 900.4 ± 71.8 | 12.6% | 51.2% |
| Gl | 752.6 ± 32.3 | 0% | 41.8% |
| Po | 933.7 ± 35.4 | 10.2% | 38.8% |

- Gr and Po significantly higher FS than Sp and Gl (all P<0.0001)
- Aging did NOT significantly reduce FS in any comparison (P=0.109–0.970)
- Pearson correlation: FS increased with monoclinic% in Gr and Po (transformation toughening); in Gr-Ag, Po-Ag, Gl-Ag, monoclinic increase did not change FS

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single zirconia brand (Zirconzahn); results may not generalize to 4Y/5Y-PSZ formulations with different cubic content
- Lab aging protocol accelerated (40h steam ≈ months of clinical service); clinical significance of 51% monoclinic unclear
- No long-term cyclic fatigue assessment
- No clinical follow-up

## 7. Glossary

- **LTD (저온열화, Low-Temperature Degradation)**: 수분 존재 시 가속되는 t→m 상전환
- **Transformation toughening (변태강화)**: t→m 전환 시 체적팽창으로 균열 선단에 압축응력 발생 → 균열 억제
- **Overglaze (과유약)**: 연삭 후 820°C에서 소성하는 표면 코팅
