## Why Ingested

[[orthodontics/clear-aligner/benedetti-2025-clear-aligner-rotation-accuracy-sr]] 등 정확도 SR은 각 치아이동 유형별 예측도를 정량화했으나, **1차 시리즈 완료 후 리파인먼트를 유발하는 임상·생체역학·디지털 요인을 종합적으로 분석한 SR**이 없었다. 본 논문은 리파인먼트를 "치료 결과"로 재정의하고 20개 연구의 근거를 종합한다.

## One-line Summary

SR (SWiM), 20 studies: refinement after the first aligner series is multifactorial, driven by case complexity, extraction, IPR, movement-type limitations (rotation, intrusion, vertical, transverse); remote monitoring reduces appointments but not refinement need.

## 한줄요약

SR(SWiM), 20개 연구: 1차 투명교정 시리즈 후 리파인먼트는 증례 복잡도·발치·IPR·이동 유형 한계(회전·압하·수직·수평 확장)에 의한 다요인 결과이며, 원격 모니터링은 방문 빈도를 줄이지만 리파인먼트 필요성은 감소시키지 못한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reframes refinement as a multifactorial clinical outcome, not a uniform finishing procedure
- Identifies 6 key drivers: malocclusion complexity, extraction treatment, IPR strategies, severe spacing, larger correction sequences, movement-specific limitations
- Demonstrates remote monitoring reduces appointment burden without reducing refinement need
- Highlights preliminary status of AI/ML-based refinement prediction evidence
- Calls for standardized definitions of "refinement" across studies

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review following PRISMA 2020 + Synthesis Without Meta-analysis (SWiM)
- Databases: PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar
- Population: Patients treated with clear aligners, any age
- Inclusion: Studies reporting refinement, additional aligners, finishing aligners, midcourse correction, planned-vs-achieved discrepancies
- Studies included: 20
- Risk of bias: design-specific tools by study type
- Analysis: structured narrative synthesis (heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Refinement-associated factors: malocclusion complexity, extraction cases, IPR strategies, severe spacing, larger correction sequences
- Movement types with reduced predictability: rotations, intrusion, vertical correction, transverse expansion, selected anterior movements
- Remote monitoring: reduces appointment burden; does NOT consistently reduce refinement need
- AI/ML prediction: evidence remains preliminary
- Outcome heterogeneity: refinement defined as refinement aligners, finishing phases, midcourse correction, first-refinement scans — inconsistently across studies

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- High heterogeneity in refinement definitions precluded meta-analysis
- Predominantly retrospective study designs
- No standardized patient/tooth/movement-level predictors established
- Need for prospective studies with standardized refinement definitions
- ML/digital monitoring tools need further validation

## 6. Related Work

- Individual movement accuracy SRs (rotation, intrusion, expansion)
- Remote monitoring literature
- Treatment planning complexity indices

## 7. Glossary

- SWiM: Synthesis Without Meta-analysis
- IPR: Interproximal Reduction
- Refinement: additional aligner series ordered after the first planned sequence
- Midcourse correction: adjustment to ClinCheck plan before completion
