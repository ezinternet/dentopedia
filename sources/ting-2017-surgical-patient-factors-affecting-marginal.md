## Three-line Summary

Comprehensive overview of systematic reviews (umbrella/AMSTAR-rated synthesis; 41 SRs/MAs selected from 116 screened, published up to November 2015; 11 on implant factors, 10 on patient factors, 19 on surgical protocol, 1 on all three) on surgical and patient factors affecting marginal bone loss (MBL) around osseointegrated dental implants.

Statistically significant factors: periodontitis (esp. generalized aggressive vs chronic), male sex, and smoking (incl. greater MBL in maxilla than mandible in smokers) all increased MBL; alveolar socket/ridge preservation with grafts reduced MBL, while ridge augmentation sites had more MBL than sites treated with shorter implants; implant length/type and immediate vs delayed placement showed conflicting results across included SRs, while implant surface, implant position (tilted vs axial), flapless vs flapped surgery, soft-tissue augmentation, insertion torque, and 1-stage vs 2-stage surgery showed no significant effect.

As a narrative overview of pre-existing SRs (no new pooled meta-analysis, AMSTAR quality-graded only), it is best used as a clinical risk-factor triage map rather than a source of pooled effect sizes; inconsistent MBL reference points and follow-up durations across the 41 underlying reviews limit precision.

## 세줄요약

체계적문헌고찰들의 종합적 개관(umbrella/AMSTAR 평가 종합, 2015년 11월까지 발표된 SR/MA 116편 중 41편 선정 — 임플란트 요인 11편, 환자 요인 10편, 수술 프로토콜 19편, 3요인 모두 1편), 골유착 임플란트 주위 변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)에 영향을 미치는 수술적·환자적 요인 평가.

통계적으로 유의한 요인: 치주염(특히 전신형 공격성 치주염 > 만성 치주염), 남성, 흡연(흡연자는 하악보다 상악에서 MBL이 더 큼)이 MBL을 증가시켰고; 발치와 보존술(socket/ridge preservation with grafts)은 MBL을 감소, 반면 치조제 증대(ridge augmentation) 부위는 짧은 임플란트 대체 부위보다 MBL이 더 많았음; 임플란트 길이·종류·즉시 vs 지연 식립은 결과 상충, 임플란트 표면·경사(tilted vs axial) 식립·flapless vs flapped 수술·연조직 이식술·삽입 토크·1-stage vs 2-stage는 유의한 영향 없음.

기존 SR들을 AMSTAR 등급 평가와 함께 서술적으로 종합한 개관(자체 pooled meta-analysis 없음)이므로 pooled 수치보다는 임상 위험요인 분류 지도로 활용하는 것이 적합하며, 41편 개별 SR 간 MBL 측정 기준점·추적기간의 불일치가 정밀도를 제한한다.

- **Journal**: Implant Dentistry 2017;26(2):303–315
- **DOI**: 10.1097/ID.0000000000000565
- **Institution**: Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry, Philadelphia, PA, USA

## 2. Key Contributions
- First AMSTAR-graded comprehensive overview (umbrella synthesis) of SRs/MAs on non-prosthodontic (surgical + patient) factors affecting MBL around dental implants, covering 41 reviews (25 high, 16 moderate AMSTAR quality).
- Consolidated conflicting individual-SR conclusions on implant length, implant type, and immediate vs delayed placement into a single "conflicting evidence" bucket for clinicians.
- Identified periodontitis, smoking, and male sex as the three patient factors with the most consistent statistically significant association with increased MBL across independent SRs.
- Distinguished surgical technique choices with no proven MBL effect (flapless vs flapped, 1- vs 2-stage, insertion torque, soft-tissue grafting, tilted vs axial position) from those with a proven effect (ridge/socket preservation grafting vs ridge augmentation with standard-length implants).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Comprehensive overview of systematic reviews and meta-analyses (umbrella-style narrative synthesis with AMSTAR quality appraisal; no new pooled quantitative meta-analysis performed by the authors).
- **Databases**: EMBASE, PubMed, Web of Science, Cochrane Library, Google Scholar, plus hand-searching of reference lists.
- **n**: 41 SRs/MAs selected from 116 full-text-screened (591+86+233+26+20 initial hits across databases); AMSTAR-rated moderate-to-high quality only (score ≥4/11); studies reviewing fewer than 5 primary studies were excluded.
- **Outcomes**: Marginal/crestal bone loss (MBL) around osseointegrated implants attributable to implant characteristics/position, patient factors (periodontitis, smoking, gender), and surgical protocol factors (flap design, immediate placement, grafting, soft-tissue augmentation, insertion torque, staging).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Implant factors** (12 SRs): implant length and implant type showed conflicting results across reviews (e.g., short implants variably reported as increasing or not affecting MBL); implant surface (rough vs machined), bone-level vs tissue-level design (pooled MBL difference 0.05 mm, NS), and tilted vs axial position (all 4 SRs, including Del Fabbro & Ceresoli 2014, reported NS) showed no significant MBL effect.
- **Patient factors** (11 SRs): periodontally compromised patients had significantly more MBL than periodontally healthy patients (6 SRs concordant); generalized aggressive periodontitis > chronic periodontitis in long-term studies (weighted mean difference 0.28–0.43 mm, of debated clinical significance); smokers had significantly more MBL than nonsmokers in 4/5 SRs (Clementini 2014: +0.164 mm/year); men had significantly more MBL than women (2 SRs).
- **Surgical factors** (20 SRs): flapless vs flapped surgery showed no significant MBL difference (5 SRs, including Chrcanovic 2014 flapless meta-analysis); immediate vs delayed placement was conflicting (3 SRs NS, 2 SRs reported MBL gain favoring immediate, 3 SRs reported magnitude only); alveolar ridge/socket preservation grafting significantly reduced MBL (Mardas 2015: SMD −0.039 mm, significant) while ridge augmentation sites had significantly more MBL than sites restored with shorter implants (Yang/Emami 2011); soft-tissue augmentation, insertion torque, and 1- vs 2-stage protocols showed no significant MBL effect (except in short implants, where 1- vs 2-stage was significant per Mezzomo 2014).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No new pooled meta-analysis — the paper is a narrative synthesis of conclusions already reached by 41 underlying SRs, so it inherits each SR's own risk of bias and cannot resolve conflicting findings quantitatively.
- MBL reference points (implant platform/abutment junction vs machined/rough surface junction) and radiographic protocols were inconsistently defined across the underlying studies, limiting cross-study comparability.
- Follow-up durations varied widely (1 year to >5 years) and most included studies were not exclusively prospective, introducing heterogeneity the overview could not statistically adjust for.
- Search cutoff was November 2015 — does not capture SRs/MAs published since (e.g., del-fabbro-2022, malak-2024 on tilted vs axial MBL specifically).
- Authors explicitly deferred prosthodontic (post-loading) factors affecting MBL to "another overview" (not covered here).

## 6. Related Work
- del-fabbro-2014 (Del Fabbro & Ceresoli, tilted vs axial MBL SR): one of the 4 implant-position SRs pooled in this overview; concordant NS finding.
- chrcanovic-2014 (flapless vs conventional flapped MA): one of the 5 flap-design SRs pooled in this overview; concordant NS finding on MBL specifically.
- malak-2024 (axial vs tilted implant failure/MBL umbrella review): a much later, narrower-scope umbrella review on the same implant-position question this overview also addressed as NS.

## 7. Glossary
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: radiographically measured vertical loss of crestal/marginal bone around an implant, typically referenced to the implant platform or implant-abutment junction.
- **AMSTAR**: A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews — 11-item checklist used here to grade included SR quality as high (8–11), moderate (4–7), or low (0–3, excluded).
- **Umbrella review / overview of systematic reviews**: a synthesis whose unit of inclusion is other systematic reviews/meta-analyses rather than primary studies.
- **Alveolar ridge/socket preservation**: grafting technique applied at the time of tooth extraction to limit post-extraction dimensional bone loss before/at implant placement.
- **1-stage vs 2-stage implant surgery**: single-surgery (transmucosal healing) vs two-surgery (submerged healing, second-stage uncovering) implant placement protocols.
