## Why Ingested

위키는 골다공증을 임플란트 *위험 인자*(장기 생존율 82.6–94.1%로 하락, [[wiki/implants/kim-2026-dental-implant-osteoporosis-osteosclerosis]]), 항흡수제(bisphosphonate/denosumab)를 *위해 인자*(MRONJ 위험, [[wiki/overviews/drug-mronj-antiresorptive-overview]])로 다루고, 비타민 D를 조절 가능한 보조 인자([[wiki/overviews/vitamin-d-osseointegration-implant-overview]])로 다룬다. 그러나 **골형성 촉진(osteoanabolic) 약물로 골유착을 오히려 밀어올릴 수 있는가**라는 반대 방향의 가지는 위키에 없었다. Kindaro 2026은 부갑상선호르몬(Parathyroid Hormone, PTH; teriparatide, PTH 1–34) — 항흡수제의 거울상인 골형성 촉진제 — 의 임플란트 골유착 효과를 정리한 첫 페이지를 연다. 단, 포함된 12편이 모두 골다공증 유발 **동물모델**(사람 연구 0편)이라 인체 번역은 미검증임을 명시. [[wiki/implants/aghaloo-2019-systemic-diseases-medications-implant-osseointegration-sr]]의 약물-골유착 프레임을 골형성 촉진 축으로 확장.

## Three-line Summary

Updated PRISMA systematic review (INPLASY-registered) of 12 preclinical studies (2015–Aug 2025) testing PTH/teriparatide supplementation on dental implant osseointegration in osteoporotic animal models (OVX/ORX rats & rabbits; tibia/femur/maxilla); no human studies existed. SYRCLE risk-of-bias tool used; no meta-analysis (heterogeneity).

Intermittent PTH consistently raised bone-to-implant contact (BIC), bone volume fraction (BV/TV), and removal torque vs osteoporotic controls; combination regimens (PTH + vitamin D, PTH + raloxifene, or PTH-loaded local scaffolds) outperformed PTH monotherapy, with local PTH-functionalised bioglass restoring stability to healthy-animal levels.

Evidence is animal-only with substantial heterogeneity, small samples (n=10–80), short follow-up (2–12 wk), 7/12 studies high risk of investigator-blinding bias, and heavy same-group author overlap — PTH remains an experimental adjunct with strong biological plausibility but NO human trial support; translation to patients is unproven.

## 세줄요약

골다공증 유발 동물모델(난소/고환 절제 쥐·토끼, 경골/대퇴/상악)에서 PTH/테리파라타이드의 임플란트 골유착 효과를 다룬 12편 전임상 연구의 업데이트 체계적 문헌고찰(PRISMA, INPLASY 등록, 2015–2025.8); 사람 연구는 0편, 메타분석은 이질성으로 불가.

간헐적 PTH 투여는 골-임플란트 접촉률(BIC)·골부피율(BV/TV)·제거토크를 대조군 대비 일관되게 증가시켰고, 병용요법(PTH+비타민 D, PTH+랄록시펜, PTH-담지 국소 스캐폴드)이 단독요법을 능가 — 국소 PTH-기능화 바이오글라스는 건강 동물 수준까지 회복.

동물 전용 근거로 이질성 큼, 소표본(n=10–80)·짧은 추적(2–12주)·7/12편 연구자 맹검 고위험·동일 저자군 중복이 한계 — PTH는 생물학적 개연성은 강하나 인체 임상시험 근거가 전무한 실험적 보조제이며, 환자 적용 번역은 미검증.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Systematic review (updated) of preclinical animal studies; PRISMA 2020, INPLASY202550047.
- **Journal**: Journal of Oral Biosciences 68 (2026) 100778; open access (CC BY).
- **Institution**: College of Medicine and Dentistry, James Cook University, Queensland, Australia.
- **Scope**: Updates Javed et al. (2015). Search Jan 2015 – Aug 2025 across PubMed, Ovid MEDLINE, Cochrane, Scopus, Web of Science.
- **PICO**: Population = osteoporotic animal models; Intervention = PTH supplementation (teriparatide / PTH analogues); Comparison = no supplementation; Outcome = osseointegration (BIC via histomorphometry, BV/TV & architecture via micro-CT, biomechanical stability via removal/push-out torque or insertion torque).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First wiki-relevant synthesis of an **osteoanabolic** pharmacologic strategy for implant osseointegration — the mirror image of the antiresorptive (bisphosphonate/denosumab) agents that dominate the wiki's MRONJ/osteoporosis pages.
- Stratifies preclinical evidence along three clinically meaningful axes: **pre- vs post-implantation** timing, **systemic vs local** delivery, and **monotherapy vs combination**.
- Extends the mixed-outcome 2015 Javed review; the larger 2015–2025 dataset shows a more reproducible anabolic signal than previously recognised.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 177 records retrieved (Scopus 58, WoS 42, Ovid MEDLINE 30, PubMed 35, Cochrane 12); 86 duplicates removed; 91 title/abstract screened; 19 full texts; 12 final included. Cohen's kappa = 1.0 (full inter-reviewer agreement).
- All 12 included studies were **animal interventional** → risk of bias assessed with SYRCLE's RoB tool (10 signalling questions, 6 bias domains); no overall summary score per SYRCLE guidance.
- Models: rats (Sprague-Dawley, Wistar) and rabbits (New Zealand); osteoporosis induced by ovariectomy (OVX) or orchiectomy (ORX), sometimes + glucocorticoids. Implants placed in tibia, femur, or maxilla.
- Sample sizes 10–80 animals; follow-up 2–12 weeks; PTH dosing 0.5–80 μg/kg/day, typically 3–5×/week subcutaneous; therapy duration usually 4–8 weeks (Demirbaş extended to 12 wk).
- Outcomes: micro-CT (BV/TV, trabecular architecture, BMD), histomorphometry (BIC), biomechanics (push-out/pull-out/removal torque, insertion torque), plus immunohistochemistry/SEM in a subset.
- **Narrative synthesis only — no meta-analysis** (heterogeneity in species, dosing, timing, outcomes).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Across nearly all studies, intermittent PTH significantly improved peri-implant BIC, BV/TV, and removal torque vs osteoporotic controls, with thicker trabeculae, reduced trabecular separation, lower porosity.
- **Timing**: Cui et al. — teriparatide 4 wk pre-operatively gave mechanical/architectural outcomes similar to 8 wk post-operative therapy; pre-treatment accelerates osseointegration and shortens the compromised-stability window. Al-Omari (2025) 1-week post-op course improved soft-tissue healing but only marginally increased bone → short courses insufficient.
- **Delivery**: Systemic PTH improves peri-implant density but carries transient hypercalcaemia/nausea and cortical-porosity risk (dose/frequency-dependent). Local PTH-functionalised bioglass (Biogran; Gomes-Ferreira 2022) restored biomechanical stability and BIC in osteoporotic rats to healthy-animal levels. Teriparatide-loaded octacalcium-phosphate/collagen scaffolds produced 29.2–38.1% bone formation vs 17.5% scaffold-only (cited GBR SR).
- **Combination**: PTH + vitamin D (Gomes-Ferreira 2023) → synergistic ↑BV/TV, trabecular thickness, ↓porosity. PTH + raloxifene (Demirbaş) → high BIC and removal torque (anabolic PTH + antiresorptive raloxifene = complementary). PTH + low-magnitude high-frequency loading (Shibamoto) > either alone. Combination consistently beat monotherapy.
- **Negative/null signals**: Park et al. — trabecular thickness ↑ significant (p=0.023) but BIC and BV/TV improvements not statistically significant. (Introduction also notes Kucher — no BIC gain under hyperglycaemia; Ohkawa — no peri-implant bone volume/shear strength difference.)
- **Risk of bias**: all studies low risk on baseline characteristics and selective reporting; **all unclear on allocation concealment**; **7/12 high risk on investigator blinding**. Cui and Al-Omari (2024) strongest (low risk in 8/10 domains). Heavy same-group author overlap (Oki, Gomes-Ferreira, Al-Omari) → potential confirmation/publication bias.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small-animal models (rat/rabbit) differ from humans in bone metabolism and marrow composition; OVX/ORX ± glucocorticoid does not capture multifactorial human osteoporosis.
- Small samples, short follow-up (4–10 wk) — cannot assess long-term implant survival.
- Wide dosing heterogeneity, inconsistent randomisation/blinding reporting, incompletely described implant surface topography (confounds attribution of effect to PTH).
- Local intermittent-release carriers are manufacturing-complex with poorly understood pharmacokinetics.
- **No human studies exist.** Conclusion: PTH should be regarded as an **experimental adjunct** with strong biological plausibility but insufficient evidence for routine clinical use; rigorous human trials required.

## 7. Glossary

- **PTH / teriparatide / PTH(1–34)**: recombinant parathyroid hormone; **intermittent** low-dose = osteoanabolic (↓sclerostin, ↑osteoblast differentiation), **continuous** = catabolic (↑RANKL, osteoclastogenesis).
- **Osteoanabolic vs antiresorptive**: PTH *builds* bone; bisphosphonates/denosumab *suppress resorption*. Opposite mechanisms.
- **BIC**: bone-to-implant contact (histomorphometry). **BV/TV**: bone volume fraction (micro-CT). **RT**: removal torque (biomechanical stability).
- **OVX / ORX**: ovariectomy / orchiectomy — surgical induction of osteoporosis in animals.
- **SYRCLE RoB**: risk-of-bias tool for animal intervention studies.
- **Raloxifene**: selective estrogen receptor modulator (antiresorptive).
