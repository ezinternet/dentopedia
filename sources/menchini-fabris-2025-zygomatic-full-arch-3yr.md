## Why Ingested

No held paper specifically reported >3yr middle-term outcomes for extra-maxillary zygomatic + standard implant hybrid full-arch configurations with ZICL and MSEE indices, from the same Italian research group (Menchini-Fabris, Grandi, Toti, Covani) that conducted the 1yr companion study (Grandi 2025). This provides middle-term follow-up data filling the gap between short-term (1yr) and long-term (>5yr) zygomatic hybrid outcomes.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective middle-term study (>3yr) of 31 severely atrophic maxilla patients with hybrid full-arch rehabilitation using 62 zygomatic + 90 standard implants (extramaxillary technique); survival, success, complications, mPLI, mBI, MSEE, and ZICL assessed.

100% implant survival and 100% prosthesis survival; implant success 98.4% and prosthesis success 87% (patient as unit); 1 mucositis case; 7 patients with mechanical/biological complications (all resolved); ZICL1 in >80%; MSEE <4mm in 81%.

Bilateral zygomatic prosthetic configurations demonstrate excellent middle-term survival for severely resorbed maxillae; mucosal health and ZICL outcomes confirm biocompatibility of the extramaxillary approach at >3 years.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Middle-term (>3yr) ZICL and MSEE data for zygomatic + standard hybrid full-arch configuration
- 100% implant and prosthesis survival with no losses — strong survival signal at >3yr
- Prosthesis success 87% (patient unit) vs 100% implant survival — highlights prosthetic complication burden distinguishable from implant loss
- ZICL1 in >80%: peri-zygomatic mucosal health well maintained at middle term
- Companion to Grandi 2025 (same group, 1yr data): collectively builds a 1yr → 3yr+ outcome profile

## 4. Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Implant survival | **100%** (0/152 lost) |
| Prosthesis survival | **100%** (0 lost) |
| Implant success (>3yr) | **98.4%** |
| Prosthesis success (patient unit) | **87%** |
| Mucositis | 1 patient (zygomatic site) |
| Complications (mech/bio) | 7 patients; all resolved |

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract only — individual complication types, mPLI/mBI numerical values, radiographic MBL data not available
- No control group
- Single center; small sample (n=31)
- No specification of exact follow-up duration range (only ">3yr")
