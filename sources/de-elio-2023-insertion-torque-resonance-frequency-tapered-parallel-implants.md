## Three-line Summary

Split-mouth RCT-style preliminary study, 60 implants (30 tapered / 30 parallel) in 17 patients, comparing primary implant stability (PIS) via insertion torque (IT) and resonance frequency analysis (RFA, both Osstell and Penguin systems).

IT was significantly higher with parallel implants (P<.05) despite similar bone density/alveolar ridge width (ARW) between groups; between-group ISQ differences were not significant (P>.05); in tapered implants IT was inversely correlated with ARW (P<.001); Osstell and Penguin ISQ values differed significantly from each other (P<.001).

Final drill bit diameter (3.25 mm for parallel vs 3.4 mm for tapered, per manufacturer protocol) likely confounds the IT comparison more than implant macrogeometry itself, and RFA values are not interchangeable across devices — abstract-only, full methodology/statistics not retrieved.

## 세줄요약

split-mouth 예비 RCT, 임플란트 60개(테이퍼형 30 / 평행형 30), 환자 17명 대상으로 삽입 토크 (Insertion Torque, IT)와 공진주파수분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA — Osstell·Penguin 두 기종)으로 1차 임플란트 안정성 (Primary Implant Stability, PIS) 비교.

골밀도·치조제 폭 (Alveolar Ridge Width, ARW)이 두 군 간 유사함에도 평행형 임플란트에서 IT가 유의하게 높았고(P<.05), 군 간 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 차이는 유의하지 않았으며(P>.05), 테이퍼형에서는 IT와 ARW가 역상관(P<.001), Osstell·Penguin 간 ISQ 값도 유의하게 달랐다(P<.001).

최종 드릴 직경(평행형 3.25mm vs 테이퍼형 3.4mm, 제조사 프로토콜)이 임플란트 형태 자체보다 IT 비교를 교란했을 가능성이 크고 RFA 기기 간 값은 상호 호환되지 않는다 — 초록만 확보(전문 미확보), 상세 방법론·통계는 미포함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct split-mouth comparison of tapered vs parallel implants of the **same coronal diameter (3.75 mm)**, isolating macrogeometry effect on IT/ISQ while controlling per-patient variability
- Dual RFA device measurement (Osstell + Penguin) on the same implants, showing device values are not interchangeable (P<.001)
- ARW–IT correlation specifically within the tapered subgroup (inverse, P<.001), flagging a design-specific vulnerability in wide (thick) ridges

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Preliminary, split-mouth, randomized-placement clinical study
- **n**: 17 patients, 60 implants (30 tapered, 30 parallel)
- **Implants**: Same coronal diameter (3.75 mm) both types; final drill bit 3.25 mm (parallel) vs 3.4 mm (tapered), per manufacturer recommendation
- **Imaging**: CBCT with radiological-surgical templates for bone density and ARW estimation
- **Outcomes**: Insertion torque (IT, dental implant motor), ISQ via Osstell and Penguin RFA systems, ARW correlation

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Bone density and ARW: similar between groups (P>.05)
- IT: higher with parallel implants (P<.05)
- ISQ (Osstell/Penguin, between implant type): not significantly different (P>.05)
- Tapered implants: IT inversely correlated with ARW (P<.001)
- Osstell vs Penguin ISQ values: significantly different from each other (P<.001)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only ingest — full statistical detail (exact ISQ/IT means, CI, regression coefficients) not available; only p-value directionality captured
- Small preliminary sample (60 implants, 17 patients), single-center, unclear blinding of RFA operators
- Final drill bit diameter differs by implant type per manufacturer protocol — a structural confound (as also flagged by [[implants/mourao-2025-tapered-vs-cylindrical-implants-early-healing-rct]] for a different tapered-vs-cylindrical RCT) that makes the IT comparison not a pure macrogeometry test
- Authors explicitly flag future need to disentangle drill-bit-size effects from implant-design effects, and to investigate tapered-implant stability limits in thick, low-density bone

## 7. Glossary
- **PIS**: Primary Implant Stability — mechanical stability at placement, before osseointegration
- **IT**: Insertion Torque — rotational resistance recorded during implant placement (N·cm)
- **RFA**: Resonance Frequency Analysis — non-invasive lateral/bending stability measurement (Osstell, Penguin)
- **ISQ**: Implant Stability Quotient — 1–100 scale output of RFA
- **ARW**: Alveolar Ridge Width — buccolingual bone width at the implant site, estimated from CBCT
