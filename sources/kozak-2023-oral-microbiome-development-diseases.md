## Why Ingested

Found while mining the reference list of Wen et al. 2026 ("The effect of dental floss sequence on plaque removal efficacy and periodontal health"), which opens by describing dental plaque as a complex oral-microbiome biofilm. This broad narrative review supplies the oral-microbiome background context (dysbiosis model, red-complex/keystone pathogens, oral-systemic disease links) that grounds why plaque-biofilm disruption matters clinically. It reinforces the existing [[wiki/oral-microbiology/sedghi-2021-oral-microbiome-key-organisms-networks]] page's network-dysbiosis framework and extends it toward systemic-disease associations (COPD, cardiovascular disease, Alzheimer's disease, rheumatoid arthritis, cancer) covered more mechanistically in [[wiki/oral-microbiology/lamont-2018-oral-microbiota-dynamic-communities-host]].

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Int J Mol Sci, 2023) synthesizing the oral microbiome's role in periodontal disease (PD) pathogenesis and its downstream links to systemic disease.

Frames PD as network-level dysbiosis (not single-pathogen) of a >700-species oral biofilm, and traces mechanistic links from oral dysbiosis to COPD/cystic fibrosis, COVID-19 severity, Alzheimer's disease (P. gingivalis/gingipains-tau, BBB permeability via SCFAs/LPS/amyloid), cardiovascular disease (TMAO, LPS-TLR4, foam cell formation), diabetes (SCFA-mediated insulin resistance), rheumatoid arthritis (P. gingivalis/A. actinomycetemcomitans-driven anti-CCP/citrullination), and pancreatic/colorectal cancer.

Narrative-review level evidence (no systematic search/quality appraisal) — useful as an orientation map of oral-systemic mechanisms and key organisms, but individual disease-association claims should be verified against the primary/SR literature cited within.

## 세줄요약

국제분자과학저널(Int J Mol Sci, 2023) 게재 서술적 리뷰로, 구강미생물총(oral microbiome)이 치주질환(PD) 발병기전에 미치는 역할과 전신질환과의 연관성을 종합했다.

PD를 단일 병원균이 아닌 700종 이상 구강 바이오필름의 네트워크 수준 미생물 불균형(dysbiosis)으로 규정하며, 만성폐쇄성폐질환(COPD)·낭포성섬유증, COVID-19 중증도, 알츠하이머병(P. gingivalis/gingipain-tau 경로, BBB 투과성), 심혈관질환(TMAO, LPS-TLR4 경로), 당뇨병(단쇄지방산 매개 인슐린저항성), 류마티스관절염(P. gingivalis/A. actinomycetemcomitans의 항-CCP항체·시트룰린화 유발), 췌장암·대장암까지 구강-전신 연결 기전을 정리했다.

서술적 리뷰 수준 근거(체계적 검색·질 평가 없음)로, 구강-전신 기전의 오리엔테이션 지도로는 유용하나 개별 질환-연관성 주장은 원문 인용 문헌으로 재확인이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Frames periodontal disease (PD) pathogenesis around the modern polymicrobial-synergy-and-dysbiosis (PSD) model rather than a "red complex" single-pathogen model, consistent with the wiki's existing [[wiki/oral-microbiology/hajishengallis-2012-psd-model-periodontal-disease]] and [[wiki/oral-microbiology/lamont-2014-polymicrobial-synergy-dysbiosis-inflammatory]] pages.
- Summarizes the oral microbiome's composition (>700 species; bacteria, fungi including Candida spp., viruses, archaea, protozoa) and its role as both a local (periodontal) and systemic disease determinant.
- Surveys probiotic/synbiotic evidence for oral health (Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium, S. salivarius) across periodontal disease, caries, Candida infection, and halitosis.
- Consolidates oral-systemic disease mechanisms across six systemic domains: respiratory disease (COPD, cystic fibrosis), COVID-19, neurodegeneration (Alzheimer's disease), cardiovascular disease, diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis, and cancer (pancreatic, colorectal).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative literature review (not a registered systematic review/meta-analysis); no PRISMA flow, explicit search strategy, or study-quality appraisal described in the retrieved full text. Organized into five sections: (1) Introduction/PD epidemiology and risk factors, (2) Microbiome in oral health, (3) Oral microbiome in periodontal disease, (4) Oral microbiome in systemic diseases, (5) Conclusions.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Periodontitis prevalence: 10-50% of the global population depending on severity; genetic contribution to periodontitis heritability estimated at OR 0.38 (95% CI 0.34-0.43) in twin studies and OR 0.15 (95% CI 0.06-0.24) in family studies (Nibali et al., cited).
- ~90% of PD cases present with concurrent halitosis, driven by the same species implicated in PD (sulfur-compound-producing anaerobes).
- Mechanistic pathway summaries (not quantitative meta-analytic effect sizes): TMAO-mediated atherogenesis in cardiovascular disease; SCFA-mediated beta-cell/insulin pathway in diabetes; P. gingivalis gingipain-tau interaction and C5aR-dependent colonization/bone loss in Alzheimer's disease and periodontitis, respectively; P. gingivalis/A. actinomycetemcomitans-driven anti-CCP antibody and citrullination pathways in rheumatoid arthritis.
- Salivary microbiome shifts reported in pancreatic cancer (reduced/elevated genera vs. healthy controls, per Farrell et al., cited) and proposed gut-barrier mechanism for oral-microbiome-associated colorectal cancer.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative (non-systematic) review: no stated inclusion/exclusion criteria, no risk-of-bias/quality appraisal of cited primary studies, and no meta-analytic pooling — findings should be treated as a mechanistic/associative map rather than graded evidence.
- Several disease-association claims (Alzheimer's, RA, cancers) rest on single or few primary studies cited within the review; the review itself does not adjudicate conflicting evidence in these areas.
- Full text extracted via PMC has some fragmentary phrasing artifacts (species names occasionally dropped by the source XML-to-text conversion) — cross-check specific pathogen attributions against the primary literature before using for clinical decision-making.

## 7. Glossary

- **PD (Periodontal Disease)**: infectious/inflammatory disease of the tooth-supporting tissues, spanning reversible gingivitis to irreversible periodontitis.
- **Dysbiosis**: imbalance in the relative abundance or composition of a microbial community that contributes to disease.
- **Red complex**: historical triad of P. gingivalis, T. forsythia, T. denticola considered the core periodontitis pathogens; superseded by polymicrobial-synergy models.
- **TMAO (Trimethylamine N-oxide)**: microbiome-derived metabolite implicated in atherosclerosis via foam-cell formation and VCAM-1 upregulation.
- **SCFA (Short-Chain Fatty Acid)**: microbial fermentation product (acetate, butyrate, propionate) affecting insulin secretion, gut-barrier integrity, and CNS signaling.
- **Anti-CCP antibody**: anti-citrullinated protein antibody characteristic of rheumatoid arthritis; linked here to P. gingivalis/A. actinomycetemcomitans-induced citrullination.
- **BBB (Blood-Brain Barrier)**: barrier whose permeability is implicated in oral-microbiome-linked neuroinflammation/neurodegeneration pathways described in this review.
