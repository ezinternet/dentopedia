## Why Ingested

This is the earliest (2021) of three regenerative endodontic procedure (REP) umbrella/synthesis reviews landing in `wiki/endodontics/regenerative/` in this batch, and it establishes the foundational quality-of-evidence baseline (29 SRs, AMSTAR2) that the later two papers (2022 immature-teeth-management umbrella review and the REP success-factors umbrella review) build on or update. It reinforces [[wiki/overviews/regenerative-endodontics-rep-overview]] by supplying the earliest systematic AMSTAR2 grading of the REP SR literature (only 2/29 SRs "high quality"), and extends the case-report/protocol-heterogeneity concerns already captured in [[wiki/endodontics/regenerative/zhou-2022-regenerative-endodontic-immature-teeth-trauma]].

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA, 5 databases searched to December 2020) of 29 systematic reviews (20 without meta-analysis, 9 with meta-analysis) on regenerative endodontic procedures (REP) for necrotic immature permanent teeth, appraised with AMSTAR2.

Methodological quality of the 29 SRs was unfavorable — only 2 rated high quality, 14 moderate, 3 low, and 10 critically low; clinical-outcome and stem-cell-research evidence was low quality on average, and REP vs apexification success rates were broadly comparable (e.g., 87.9–100% REP survival vs 90.6–97.1% apexification) but protocols for irrigation, intracanal medication, scaffold, and coronal barrier remain unstandardized.

Clinicians should treat REP protocol specifics (irrigant concentration, TAP/DAP choice, scaffold type, coronal barrier material) as still evidence-immature; histological evidence in the included SRs shows REP produces repair-like (cementum-/bone-like) tissue rather than a true regenerated pulp-dentin complex.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(umbrella review, PRISMA, 2020년 12월까지 5개 데이터베이스 검색)로 괴사된 미성숙 영구치 재생근관치료(Regenerative Endodontic Procedure, REP)에 관한 체계적 문헌고찰(Systematic Review, SR) 29편(메타분석 없는 SR 20편 + 메타분석 SR 9편)을 AMSTAR2로 평가.

29편 SR의 방법론적 질은 우호적이지 않아 고품질 2편, 중등도 14편, 저품질 3편, 매우 저품질 10편에 불과했으며, 임상결과 및 줄기세포 연구 근거 수준은 평균적으로 낮았고, REP 대 근단폐쇄술(apexification) 성공률은 대체로 유사했음(예: REP 생존율 87.9–100% vs apexification 90.6–97.1%)에도 세정·근관 내 투약·스캐폴드·치관측 배리어 프로토콜은 여전히 표준화되지 않음.

임상의는 REP 세부 프로토콜(세정액 농도, TAP/DAP 선택, 스캐폴드 종류, 치관측 배리어 재료)을 아직 근거가 미성숙한 것으로 다뤄야 하며, 포함된 SR들의 조직학적 근거는 REP가 진성 재생 치수-상아질 복합체가 아닌 복구양(백악질·골 유사) 조직을 만든다는 점을 보여줌.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella-level synthesis of the REP systematic-review literature, aggregating 29 SRs (20 narrative-synthesis SRs + 9 with meta-analysis) drawn from 403 initial search hits.
- Applies AMSTAR2 uniformly across all 29 SRs, producing a comparative quality ranking (2 high / 14 moderate / 3 low / 10 critically low) that had not previously existed for this literature.
- Organizes findings into three outcome domains — clinical protocols (irrigation, intracanal medication, scaffolds, coronal barrier), clinical outcomes (survival, apical closure, root development), and stem cell research — giving structure to a fragmented evidence base.
- Explicitly names methodological weaknesses common across the field: case-report inclusion in SRs, sparse a priori protocol registration (only 3/29 SRs registered), inconsistent risk-of-bias tool application, and small pooled-study counts undermining meta-analytic confidence intervals.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review per PRISMA guidelines, protocol defined a priori by all authors.
- **Search**: 5 databases (PubMed-Medline, Cochrane CENTRAL, Scielo, Web of Science, LILACS) searched to December 2020, plus OpenGrey for grey literature and manual reference-list screening. Boolean query: (endodontic OR pulp OR tooth) AND (revitalization OR revascularization OR regenerative OR pulpal regeneration) AND (systematic review OR meta-analysis). No year or language restriction.
- **Selection**: Two independent reviewers (L.L., J.N.); disagreements resolved by a third reviewer (V.M.). Inclusion criteria: SRs with or without meta-analysis, human and/or animal teeth, assessing REP/revitalization/revascularization/regeneration clinical characteristics.
- **Data extraction**: Predefined table capturing first author, year, databases searched, number/type of included studies, interventions, quality-assessment tool used, main results, main conclusion. Outcomes classified into protocols/materials, survival outcomes, and stem cells.
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR2 (Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews, 2nd edition) applied by two calibrated reviewers (L.L., J.N.); inter-examiner reliability kappa = 0.91 (95% CI 0.89–0.92).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Study flow**: 403 entries → 174 duplicates removed → 35 full-text screened → 6 excluded → 29 SRs included (full-text screening kappa = 1.00).
- **AMSTAR2 grading**: 2 high quality, 14 moderate quality, 3 low quality, 10 critically low quality. No SR fully satisfied all AMSTAR2 criteria.
- **Protocols**: Irrigation predominantly NaOCl (0.5–8.0%) ± EDTA (5.0–17.0%) ± CHX (0.12–2.0%); intracanal medication predominantly triple antibiotic paste (metronidazole/ciprofloxacin/minocycline) or modified variants, calcium hydroxide ± CHX, or formocresol; scaffolds predominantly blood clot, with PRP/PRF/PLGA variants reported but no meta-analytic superiority signal; coronal barrier predominantly MTA (with Biodentine, glass ionomer variants, Portland cement also reported); MTA associated with more discoloration than Biodentine in one comparative SR.
- **Clinical outcomes**: REP successful in 94.5% overall across etiologies (trauma 64.94%, dens evaginatus 22.92%, caries 5.61%, unknown/broken cusp 6.51%). REP vs apexification success/survival broadly comparable across SRs (e.g., 87.9% vs 90.6%; 100% vs 95%; 97.8% vs 97.1% in different meta-analyses), with REP showing superior lateral dentinal wall thickening and continued root development versus MTA apical-plug apexification in several SRs. Reinfection/persistent infection was the most common REP failure mode; crown discoloration was the leading complication in the REP-survival meta-analysis.
- **Stem cell research**: 8/29 SRs focused on stem cells/histology (2 with meta-analysis); five main dental stem cell (DSC) populations identified (DPSC, SHED, SCAP, PDLSC, DFPC). No regeneration protocol produced a predictable true pulp-dentin complex — regenerated tissue was predominantly cementum-/bone-like repair tissue.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Umbrella-review design means individual primary studies within each included SR were not independently re-examined — conclusions rest on each SR author's own interpretation, a limitation the authors explicitly flag.
- Only 3/29 included SRs had a priori protocol registration; broader lack of registration in the underlying literature limits reproducibility assessment.
- Several included SRs mixed human and animal-model data or misapplied risk-of-bias tools designed for RCTs to case reports/non-randomized studies, risking spurious quality conclusions.
- Meta-analytic pooled estimates in the underlying SRs often rest on fewer than five studies, producing artificially narrow confidence intervals.
- Authors explicitly call for future high-standard SRs and well-designed RCTs to clarify REP clinical protocols and outcomes — this remains an open gap as of the review's December 2020 search cutoff.

## 7. Glossary

- **REP (Regenerative Endodontic Procedure)**: biologically based treatment aiming to replace a damaged pulp-dentin complex with new vital tissue in necrotic immature permanent teeth; also called revitalization or pulp revascularization.
- **AMSTAR2**: A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews, 2nd edition — critical-appraisal instrument for systematic reviews, rating quality as high/moderate/low/critically low.
- **Umbrella review**: a systematic review of systematic reviews, synthesizing conclusions across multiple SRs rather than primary studies.
- **TAP / DAP**: triple/double antibiotic paste — intracanal medications used in REP (commonly metronidazole, ciprofloxacin, ± minocycline for TAP).
- **MTA**: mineral trioxide aggregate — most common coronal/cervical barrier material in REP.
- **Apexification**: procedure achieving apical closure/barrier formation without resuming root wall thickening or root elongation, contrasted with REP's regenerative goal.
- **DSC populations**: DPSC (dental pulp stem cells), SHED (stem cells from exfoliated deciduous teeth), SCAP (stem cells from apical papilla), PDLSC (periodontal ligament stem cells), DFPC (dental follicle precursor cells).
