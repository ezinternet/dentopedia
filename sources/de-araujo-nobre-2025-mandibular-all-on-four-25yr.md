## 2. Key Contributions

- Longest published follow-up (20–25 years) for mandibular All-on-4 immediate function — currently unmatched in the literature.
- Establishes implant CSurR 90% and prosthesis CSurR 92% at 25 years for mandibular All-on-4.
- Documents MBL trajectory: 1.71 mm @20yr → 2.28 mm @25yr — continued slow progression.
- Identifies sex (male), restorative material type, smoking, and prior contiguous implant failure as arch-specific risk factors.
- Provides companion mandibular data to complement the maxillary 20yr cohort from the same group (PMID 41598384).

## 3. Methodology

- **Population**: 181 patients (108F, 73M; mean age 59.2 ± 11.9yr); 724 implants; 181 fixed full-arch mandibular prostheses
- **Protocol**: All-on-4 concept — 2 axial anterior + 2 tilted posterior implants; immediate function
- **Mean follow-up**: 264 months (range 240–321); 77 patients at 20yr, 3 patients at 25yr
- **Lost to follow-up**: 100/181 patients
- **Primary outcomes**: implant survival/success, prosthesis survival
- **Secondary outcomes**: MBL @20yr and @25yr, mechanical/biological complications, risk indicators
- **Statistics**: Cox regression (implant failure), binary logistic regression (MBL>3mm, biological/mechanical complications)

## 4. Key Results

| Outcome | Value |
|---|---|
| Implant CSurR | **90% @25yr** |
| Prosthesis CSurR | **92% @25yr** |
| MBL @20yr | 1.71 ± 1.98 mm |
| MBL @25yr | **2.28 ± 2.07 mm** |
| Mechanical complications (patient) | 70.7% |
| Biological complications (patient) | 24.9% |
| Biological complications (implant) | 11.6% |

**Risk indicators:**
- Implant failure: biological complications + restorative material type
- Mechanical complications: male sex
- Biological complications: smoking + previous failure of contiguous implant

## 5. Limitations

- Only 77 patients available at 20yr, 3 patients at 25yr — extremely small 25yr cohort; 25yr figures require caution
- 100/181 patients (55%) lost to follow-up; single-center retrospective
- Abstract-only ingest; specific HR/OR values not available
