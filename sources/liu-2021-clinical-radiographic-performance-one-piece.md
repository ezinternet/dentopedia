## Why Ingested

원-피스(One-piece) vs 투-피스(Two-piece) 임플란트 설계 비교의 **pooled 근거가 위키에 없었다** — [[implants/pirc-2026-one-piece-two-piece-implants-15year-rct]]는 동일 비교의 단일 RCT(15–17년, n=60)일 뿐이라 추적기간·플랫폼 스위칭 여부로 해석이 갈리는 지점이 그대로 남아 있었다. 이 SR+MA는 실패 위험·합병증·임플란트주위염·변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)을 한 번에 풀링해 Pirc의 단일 시험 결과를 "pooled 단기-중기 근거"와 "15년 단일 시험 꼬리"로 분리해 읽게 해 준다(→ `refines` 성격). 동시에 골소실 방향은 정면으로 엇갈린다: 이 논문은 플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching) 하에서 **투-피스가 MBL을 0.21 mm 더 적게** 소실(WMD 0.21 mm, 95% CI 0.07–0.36, P=0.004)했다고 보고하는 반면 Pirc는 원-피스가 0.08 mm, 투-피스가 1.53 mm로 **원-피스가 압도적으로 우위**였다. 게다가 이 논문의 실패 위험 우열 없음(RR 0.49, P=0.22)은 Pirc의 원-피스 기술적 합병증 6배 열세와 다른 축의 긴장이다 — `contradicts`로 남겨 논쟁 레이더에 띄운다. 플랫폼 스위칭 → MBL 감소라는 결론은 [[implants/mbl/mortazavi-2021-bone-loss-tissue-bone-level-implants]]가 독립적으로 도달한 결론과 일치하며(`reinforces`), 반면 2013년 Camlog Foundation 합의문이 제시한 "플랫폼 스위칭은 무조건적으로 골을 보존한다"는 광범위한 권고를 "플랫폼 매칭 설계끼리의 비교에서는 차이가 없다(P=0.67)"로 조건부화한다(`refines`).

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 11 articles reporting 10 studies (MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane CENTRAL, OpenGrey, to May 2019) comparing one-piece (OPI) vs two-piece (TPI) implants, with failure and complication rates as primary clinical outcomes and peri-implant bone loss from baseline to last follow-up as the radiographic outcome.

No significant difference in failure risk (RR 0.49, 95% CI 0.16–1.53, P = 0.22) or in peri-implantitis at 1 year (RR 1.15, 95% CI 0.37–3.53, P = 0.81) or at 2–3 years (RR 1.95, 95% CI 0.23–16.63, P = 0.54); peri-implantitis accounted for most failures and complications, and in the platform-switching subgroup two-piece implants showed significantly less marginal bone loss (WMD 0.21 mm, 95% CI 0.07–0.36, P = 0.004) while the platform-matching subgroup showed none (P = 0.67).

Implant configuration per se does not change short-term survival or complication incidence; crestal bone is protected by the platform-switching connection rather than by the one-piece/two-piece label — but confidence is limited by only 10 studies, very wide peri-implantitis CIs, and a search cut-off of May 2019 that predates modern internal-connection/titanium-base systems.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (11편 논문 = 10개 연구, MEDLINE·EMBACE·Cochrane CENTRAL·OpenGrey, 2019년 5월까지 검색) — 원-피스(One-piece Implant, OPI) vs 투-피스(Two-piece Implant, TPI) 임플란트의 임상·방사선학적 성적 비교, 일차 임상 결과는 실패·합병증률이고 방사선학적 결과는 기저점부터 최종 추적까지의 임플란트주위 골소실이다.

실패 위험 차이는 유의하지 않았고(RR 0.49, 95% CI 0.16–1.53, P=0.22), 임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis)도 1년(RR 1.15, 95% CI 0.37–3.53, P=0.81)·2–3년(RR 1.95, 95% CI 0.23–16.63, P=0.54) 모두 차이 없었으며, 실패·합병증의 대부분을 임플란트주위염이 차지했다. 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 플랫폼 스위칭(Platform Switching) 아군에서 투-피스가 유의하게 적게 소실(WMD 0.21 mm, 95% CI 0.07–0.36, P=0.004)했고, 플랫폼 매칭(Platform Matching) 아군에서는 차이가 없었다(P=0.67).

임플란트 설계 자체는 단기 생존·합병증을 바꾸지 않고, 치조정골 보존은 원-피스/투-피스 라벨이 아니라 플랫폼 스위칭 연결이 좌우한다 — 다만 10개 연구라는 작은 풀(pool)와 매우 넓은 임플란트주위염 신뢰구간, 그리고 현대적 내부연결·티타늄 베이스(Ti-base) 시스템 이전인 2019년 5월 검색 종료를 고려해 근거 확신은 제한된다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA to pool **clinical and radiographic outcomes of one-piece vs two-piece implant systems head-to-head** rather than treating them as separate implant categories.
- Shows that **implant configuration is not a survival or peri-implantitis decision**: no significant difference in failure risk and in peri-implantitis at either 1 year or 2–3 years.
- Identifies **peri-implantitis as the dominant failure/complication mode** in both configurations — the mechanism that matters is biological, not the presence of an implant-abutment interface per se.
- Subgroup meta-analysis by **platform switching vs platform matching** relocates the bone-preservation benefit from the one-piece/two-piece axis to the **connection geometry** axis: platform-switched two-piece implants lose less marginal bone (WMD 0.21 mm), whereas platform-matching comparisons show no difference.
- Provides the pooled short-to-mid-term counterweight to the 15–17-year single-trial result in [[implants/pirc-2026-one-piece-two-piece-implants-15year-rct]].

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review with meta-analysis
- **Databases**: MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials (CENTRAL), OpenGrey — searched to **May 2019** (OpenGrey = grey-literature arm, unusual for implant SRs)
- **Screening**: Two authors independently screened the retrieved literature against inclusion/exclusion criteria
- **n**: 11 articles reporting **10 different studies** (multiple articles per study)
- **Primary clinical outcomes**: failure and complication rates
- **Radiographic outcome**: peri-implant bone loss between baseline and last available follow-up
- **Analysis**: pooled risk ratios (RR) with 95% CI for failure and peri-implantitis; **subgroup meta-analysis by platform switching vs platform matching** for marginal bone loss (WMD, 95% CI)
- *Not reported in the abstract (full text not retrieved)*: PROSPERO registration, risk-of-bias tool, heterogeneity statistics (I²), GRADE certainty, publication-bias assessment, implant systems included, follow-up durations of individual studies

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | OPI vs TPI | Effect | 95% CI | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failure (risk) | no significant difference | RR 0.49 | 0.16–1.53 | 0.22 |
| Peri-implantitis @ 1 year | no significant difference | RR 1.15 | 0.37–3.53 | 0.81 |
| Peri-implantitis @ 2–3 years | no significant difference | RR 1.95 | 0.23–16.63 | 0.54 |
| Marginal bone loss — platform-switching subgroup | **TPI less loss** | WMD 0.21 mm | 0.07–0.36 | **0.004** |
| Marginal bone loss — platform-matching subgroup | no significant difference | — | — | 0.67 |

- Studies included: 11 articles / 10 studies.
- Failure and complication events were dominated by **peri-implantitis** in both configurations.
- Conclusion as stated by the authors: OPI and TPI show **similar short-term survival rates and incidences of complications**; nevertheless **TPI with platform switching may be a better option to reduce peri-implant bone loss**.
- Reading the CIs: the failure and peri-implantitis intervals are wide enough (and the 2–3-year peri-implantitis interval, 0.23–16.63, wide enough) that "no significant difference" cannot be read as "equivalent designs".

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- *Stated/inferable from the abstract:* only **10 studies** pooled; the peri-implantitis CI at 2–3 years (0.23–16.63) shows the evidence base is heavily **underpowered** for this outcome — absence of significance is not evidence of equivalence.
- *Inferred (full text not retrieved):* no risk-of-bias, heterogeneity, or GRADE certainty is reported in the abstract, so the credibility of the pooled estimates cannot be graded from this record alone.
- *Inferred:* search cut-off **May 2019** excludes the modern wave of internal-connection/titanium-base (Ti-base) two-piece systems, so the "platform-switched TPI is superior" subgroup finding may not transfer to current-generation implant systems.
- *Inferred:* the pooled follow-up is short-to-mid term (peri-implantitis stratified at 1 y and 2–3 y), while the bone-loss comparison is baseline-to-last-follow-up across heterogeneous intervals — late/very-late bone-loss divergence is not captured.
- *Inferred:* mixing 10 studies reported in 11 articles means several cohorts contribute multiple effect estimates; whether the analysis handled this clustering is not stated in the abstract.
- *Inferred:* heterogeneity of implant systems, connection designs, loading protocols and outcome definitions across a small study set is a plausible source of the very wide peri-implantitis intervals.
- *Future work:* adequately powered RCTs comparing one-piece vs **platform-switched two-piece** systems with ≥5-year radiographic follow-up, with peri-implantitis as a pre-specified outcome.

## 7. Glossary

- **One-piece implant (OPI)**: single-piece implant whose coronal part and implant body are machined as one unit, so there is no implant-abutment interface (microgap) at bone level; functionally tissue-level in design.
- **Two-piece implant (TPI)**: separate fixture and abutment joined by a screw; the implant-abutment junction (IAJ) creates a microgap whose position relative to the bone crest drives crestal remodeling.
- **Platform switching**: two-piece configuration whose abutment diameter is smaller than the implant platform diameter, shifting the microgap inward and laterally away from the crestal bone.
- **Platform matching**: two-piece configuration in which abutment and implant platform diameters coincide, leaving the microgap at the crestal level.
- **Marginal bone loss (MBL)**: radiographic change in crestal bone height from baseline to the last available follow-up, measured against a fixed reference point; reported here as weighted mean difference (WMD) in millimetres.
- **Risk ratio (RR)**: ratio of the risk of an event (failure, peri-implantitis) between two groups; RR of 1 = equal risk, and a confidence interval spanning 1 = no significant difference.
- **Peri-implantitis**: inflammatory peri-implant disease with loss of supporting bone; here the dominant failure/complication mode for both implant configurations.
