## Why Ingested

The wiki already holds socket-seal evidence built on **substitute** materials — collagen sponge and L-PRF membrane as sealing arms ([[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/manlerd-2025-lprf-membrane-socket-sealing-healing]]), sealing-material rankings from a network meta-analysis ([[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/del-fabbro-2022-sealing-materials-post-extraction]]), and socket-seal surgery in the esthetic zone ([[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/lopez-pacheco-2021-socket-seal-surgery-esthetic-zone-sr]]) — but no page where **autologous reactive soft tissue (RST) is the sealing arm in bone-deficient sockets, paired with DBBM and with histology**. That gap matters clinically: the esthetic-zone synthesis ranks autologous soft tissue highest for horizontal preservation while flagging donor-site morbidity, so a prospective head-to-head against a ready-made collagen sponge in compromised sockets (plus a biopsy at implant placement) is the missing middle piece.

→ [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/jiang-2024-efficacy-reactive-soft-tissue-alveolar]]

A second, closer neighbour is the same-group's RST-vs-collagen-sponge comparison in **immediate implants** ([[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/deng-2024-posterior-open-wound-healing-immediate-implant]]) — same two materials, different clinical problem (open-wound closure vs graft containment in ARP), different outcome battery (soft-tissue score/MBL vs ridge dimensions + histology). Read together they define what RST actually buys: soft-tissue healing speed, not extra bone.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (44 patients recruited, 40 completed; single protocol, RST vs collagen sponge both over DBBM in sockets with bone deficiency) measuring horizontal/vertical ridge dimensions at extraction (T0) and 6 months after ARP (T2), modified Masse healing index at 14 days (T1) and T2, KMW, and biopsy histology at implant placement — abstract-only ingest.

Horizontal ridge gains at 1/3/5 mm below the crest were RST 4.37/5.57/4.16 mm vs CS 3.57/4.66/2.97 mm (reported as comparable, no p-value given), vertical gains were similar buccal and lingual, and the only significant between-group difference was early healing — modified Masse HI 6 (RST) vs 7 (CS) at T1, p=0.003 — with no significant difference in KMW change or new-bone proportion.

Both sealants plus DBBM limited ridge resorption in bone-deficient sockets, so RST's advantage is confined to first-two-week soft-tissue healing rather than graft performance; the abstract omits allocation method, randomization, group sizes and any p-values for the dimensional outcomes.

## 세줄요약

전향적 코호트 (Prospective Cohort, 환자 44명 모집·40명 완료): 골결손이 있는 발치와에서 반응성 연조직 (Reactive Soft Tissue, RST) 대 콜라겐 스펀지 (Collagen Sponge, CS)로 발치와를 밀봉하되 두 군 모두 탈단백 우골무기질 (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral, DBBM)을 채움 — 초록만 인제스트.

수평 치조제 증가량은 치조정 하방 1/3/5 mm에서 RST 4.37/5.57/4.16 mm vs CS 3.57/4.66/2.97 mm로 "비슷함"(p값 미제시), 수직 증가량도 협·설측 모두 유사; 유의차는 조기 치유 하나뿐 — 치유지수 (Healing Index, HI) 14일째 6 (RST) vs 7 (CS), p=0.003 — 이며 각화점막폭 (Keratinized Mucosa Width, KMW) 변화와 신생골 비율은 차이 없음.

두 재료 모두 DBBM과 함께 골결손 발치와의 골흡수를 줄였으므로, RST의 이점은 이식재 성능이 아니라 **초기 2주 연조직 치유 속도**에 국한된다; 초록에는 배정 방법·무작위화·군별 표본 수·차원 결과의 p값이 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Direct prospective comparison of **autologous reactive soft tissue** vs **collagen sponge** as the *sealing* layer over DBBM in sockets **with bone deficiency** — the compromised-socket setting, not the intact-wall esthetic-zone socket that dominates the socket-seal literature.
- Pairs **dimensional outcomes** (horizontal at 1/3/5 mm below crest, vertical buccal and lingual) with **histology from biopsies taken at implant placement**, so the comparison is not purely radiographic.
- Separates the material's effect into two domains and finds them divergent: **soft-tissue healing speed** (RST better at 14 days) versus **hard-tissue/histologic performance** (comparable), which is a more useful distinction for material selection than a single "which sealant wins" ranking.
- Reports KMW change as a secondary soft-tissue endpoint — relevant because sealing materials differ in whether they add keratinized tissue or merely cover the wound.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective cohort study (registered ChiCTR2200061459); the abstract does not describe randomization, so this is not an RCT.
- **Sample**: 44 patients recruited, 40 completed the study.
- **Clinical problem**: extraction sockets **with bone deficiency** receiving alveolar ridge preservation (ARP).
- **Intervention / comparator**: RST (autologous reactive soft tissue) vs CS (collagen sponge) used as the **socket-sealing** layer; **both groups received DBBM** as the graft fill.
- **Timepoints**: T0 = before extraction; T1 = 14 days after ARP; T2 = 6 months after ARP.
- **Outcomes**:
  - Horizontal bone dimension at 1, 3 and 5 mm below the crest (T0 → T2).
  - Vertical bone dimension, buccal and lingual (T0 → T2).
  - Modified Masse healing index (HI) for soft-tissue healing (T1, T2).
  - Keratinized mucosa width (KMW) (T0, T2).
  - Histology: biopsies collected **during implant placement** — new bone proportion reported.
- **Analysis**: not described in the abstract (no test names, no group sizes).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Endpoint | RST | Collagen sponge | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horizontal ridge gain, 1 mm below crest | 4.37 mm | 3.57 mm | not reported (stated comparable) |
| Horizontal ridge gain, 3 mm below crest | 5.57 mm | 4.66 mm | not reported (stated comparable) |
| Horizontal ridge gain, 5 mm below crest | 4.16 mm | 2.97 mm | not reported (stated comparable) |
| Vertical bone gain, buccal / lingual | similar across groups | similar across groups | not reported |
| Modified Masse HI, T1 (14 d) | 6 | 7 | **p = 0.003** (favours RST) |
| Modified Masse HI, T2 (6 mo) | not reported | not reported | not reported |
| KMW change (T0 → T2) | no significant difference | no significant difference | not significant |
| New bone proportion (biopsy) | no significant difference | no significant difference | not significant |

- **Authors' conclusion**: RST and CS, both combined with DBBM, "effectively reduced ridge resorption in sockets with bone deficiency"; RST had "a more favorable effect on the early healing of sockets" and is described as "a promising socket sealing material in ARP."
- **Reading the directional gap**: numerically the RST arm is higher at all three horizontal levels, but the abstract labels the comparison "comparable" and supplies no p-value — treat the numerical ordering as descriptive, not as demonstrated superiority.
- **Direction of the HI scale**: the authors report 6 as *better* than 7, i.e. in their modified Masse index a **lower** score indicates better healing at these values.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Non-randomized** design (prospective cohort) — allocation method and any matching/adjustment are unstated in the abstract, so confounding by socket morphology, tooth type, or site cannot be excluded.
- **Single centre inferred, not stated** — the abstract names no institution; the registration is a Chinese registry, which is consistent with but does not prove a single-centre design.
- **No untreated / spontaneous-healing control** is reported in the abstract, so "reduced ridge resorption" is relative to expected post-extraction change, not to an internal control arm.
- **Group sizes missing** — 44 recruited / 40 analysed, but the split per arm and the reasons for the 4 dropouts are not given.
- **No p-values for the primary dimensional outcomes**; "comparable" and "similar" are the authors' summaries. Effect sizes cannot be judged statistically from the abstract.
- **Short horizon** — 6 months to T2; no prosthetic or long-term implant outcome is reported.
- **Histology limited to one timepoint** (implant placement) and reported only as "new bone proportion"; no graft resorption, particle encapsulation, or bone-to-graft contact data.
- **Abstract truncated** — the published conclusion is partially recoverable; details on RST harvesting (donor site, technique) and on how deficient sockets were defined are absent.
- **Follow-up work implied**: an RCT with pre-specified allocation, an untreated control, and longer follow-up is needed before RST sealing can be positioned above collagen sponge on anything other than early healing.

## 7. Glossary

- **ARP (Alveolar Ridge Preservation)**: post-extraction grafting/sealing intended to limit dimensional ridge change before implant placement.
- **RST (Reactive Soft Tissue)**: autologous soft tissue harvested adjacent to the extraction site and used as a sealing layer over the graft; the "reactive" label refers to its wound-reactive healing behaviour rather than to a processed biomaterial.
- **CS (Collagen Sponge)**: absorbable collagen haemostatic sponge used as a ready-made socket seal.
- **DBBM (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Mineral)**: slow-resorbing xenograft used here as the graft fill in both arms.
- **HI (Healing Index), modified Masse**: ordinal soft-tissue healing score recorded at T1/T2; in this study a lower score (6 vs 7) denoted better healing.
- **KMW (Keratinized Mucosa Width)**: band of keratinized attached gingiva/mucosa measured buccally; tracked from T0 to T2.
- **T0 / T1 / T2**: before extraction / 14 days after ARP / 6 months after ARP.
