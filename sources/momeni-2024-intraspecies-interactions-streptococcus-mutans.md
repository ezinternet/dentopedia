## Why Ingested

기존 S. mutans 페이지들([[oral-microbiology/bowen-2011-streptococcus-mutans-glucosyltransferases]], [[oral-microbiology/klein-2012-mutans-protein-synthesis-mixed-species-biofilm]])은 단일 균주 또는 종간(interspecies) 상호작용에 집중했으나, 같은 종 내 여러 유전형(genotype) 간 상호작용이 우식원성에 미치는 영향은 공백이었다. 본 논문(Momeni 2024)은 임상 분리주 G09·G18의 co-culture가 biofilm 산도·구조·집락화를 상승시킴을 in vitro/in vivo로 보여, "다수 S. mutans 유전형 = ECC 위험인자"라는 역학 관찰의 기전을 보강한다. [[oral-microbiology/lueyar-2023-dynamic-interactions-between-candida-albicans]]의 cross-kingdom 상호작용과 대비되는 intraspecies 축이다.

- Design: laboratory (in vitro biofilm) + in vivo Drosophila colonization + retrospective cross-sectional association analysis (n=78 children) nested within a prior 8-year longitudinal cohort.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First focused CLSM + time-lapse dissection of **intraspecies** (genotype × genotype) S. mutans biofilm interactions in a clinical-isolate, patient-based model — most prior work is single-strain or interspecies.
- Demonstrates co-culture of two genotypes is **mutualistic**: lower biofilm pH, ~doubled cell density/thickness, and greater colonization vs either mono-culture.
- Shows a **division of labor**: G18 drives biofilm acidity (higher IPS/aciduricity, lower biomass), G09 drives biofilm thickness/architecture (tall aggregates → "volcano" structures).
- Strains occupy **distinct, non-overlapping spatial domains** within the mixed biofilm (CLSM + time-lapse), a spatial-organization finding for intraspecies communities.
- Links a clinical epidemiological signal (multiple genotypes within first year of detection → ECC, p=0.019) to a mechanistic in vitro/in vivo phenotype.
- Methodological caveat surfaced: mCherry tagging can artifactually induce an aggregate phenotype in some clinical S. mutans strains — control with an alternate fluorophore.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Association analysis**: n=78 African-American children (~1 y baseline → 6 y), high-caries-risk, non-fluoridated population; Fisher's exact test on multiple-genotype presence vs caries (DMFS >0).
- **Isolates**: >14,000 isolates reduced to 34 rep-PCR/WGS-confirmed genotypes; Child C-232 (G09 + G18, the two most prevalent) selected for depth study; UA159 reference control.
- **Biofilm assays** (THB + 1% sucrose, 16 h): biomass (crystal violet), IPS (iodine), pH (pHrodo red), glucan (cascade blue), cell density (Syto9); ImageJ quantification.
- **CLSM**: G09-mCherry / G18-GFP, Zeiss LSM 880/980, Imaris; fluorescence intensity, thickness, volume/area, spatial architecture. Differential antibiotic-selective CFU plating confirmed 50/50 inoculum.
- **Time-lapse**: Zeiss CD7, hourly 24-30 h, brightfield (oblique illumination) + GFP + pHrodo.
- **In vivo**: Drosophila melanogaster capillary-feeding colonization model, differential CFU recovery.
- **Statistics**: Student's t-test / one-way ANOVA + Tukey; p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Multiple genotypes **within first year of detection** significantly associated with ECC: caries 52.6% (>1 genotype) vs 29.5% (1 genotype), **p=0.019**; at initial detection NOT significant (p=0.327). Mean 2.7 genotypes (range 2-6) in multi-genotype children.
- 86% of caries-free children failed to persistently colonize S. mutans.
- Co-culture biofilm pH significantly lower than mono-culture in 9/10 children and in pooled population (increased pHrodo; increased Syto9 cell density). No overall biomass/IPS population difference.
- G18: lower biomass than G09 and UA159, but higher IPS than UA159; drives acidity independent of glucan (G18 glucan lower).
- CLSM: total S. mutans fluorescence ~**doubled** in co-culture despite equal inoculum; G09 thickness significantly greater in mix; volume/area significantly doubled vs mono/UA159.
- Time-lapse: biofilm architecture distinguishable by 3-4 h (G09 microcolony aggregates vs G18 "fish-net" chains); **bi-phasic** pH acidification (~10-11 h and ~24-26 h).
- Drosophila: G09 colonization consistently greater when G18 present (mutualistic).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Deep mechanistic analysis rests on a **single child's two genotypes** (G09, G18); generalizability to other genotype pairs and inhibitory (mutacin-producing) strains untested.
- 10-children screen used only 1 biological replicate (trend-level, not powered).
- Drosophila is a simplified colonization surrogate, not a mammalian caries model; no rat-caries or human validation of the co-culture effect.
- Fluorescent-protein tagging artifact (mCherry-induced aggregation) complicates architecture interpretation.
- Intraspecies effects were studied in isolation; polymicrobial/cross-kingdom context (e.g., with Candida) left to future work.

## 7. Glossary

- **ECC / SECC**: Early Childhood Caries / Severe ECC (decayed-missing-filled surfaces in children <6 y).
- **Genotype (S. mutans)**: genetically distinct strain identified by rep-PCR / whole genome sequencing.
- **Intraspecies interaction**: interaction between different strains of the same species (vs interspecies between different species).
- **IPS**: Intracellular iodophilic Polysaccharide — glycogen storage enabling continued acid production after carbohydrate depletion.
- **Aciduricity**: ability to survive/function in acidic environments.
- **CLSM**: Confocal Laser Scanning Microscopy.
- **pHrodo / cascade blue / Syto9**: fluorescent probes for pH (acidity), extracellular glucan, and cell density/nucleic acid respectively.
- **rep-PCR**: repetitive extragenic palindromic PCR (genotyping).
- **UA159**: standard S. mutans reference/control strain.
