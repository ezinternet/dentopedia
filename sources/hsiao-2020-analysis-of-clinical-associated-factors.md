## Why Ingested

The wiki holds narrative and consensus accounts of vertical root fracture (VRF) epidemiology ([[cracked-tooth/liao-2021-vertical-root-fracture-non-endodontically-treated]], [[cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]], [[cracked-tooth/patel-2025-position-statement-longitudinal-cracks-fractures]]) that cite age >40-50 years and premolar/molar predilection, plus one case-control study ([[endodontics/shaping/lee-2026-residual-pericervical-apical-dentine-vertical]]) limited to molars. This paper adds primary, microscope-confirmed (methylene-blue) cohort data with adjusted odds ratios for age and tooth type across all tooth types, and a null result for post, restoration type and retreatment history that can be read against Lee 2026 and the reviews' caution on posts.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective chart-based cohort (Taipei Medical University Hospital, 2012-2018; 359 endodontically treated, symptomatic teeth from 275 patients that underwent microscope-assisted endodontic microsurgery with methylene-blue staining; VRF found in 67 teeth, 18.7% on a tooth basis, 22.5% on a patient basis).

In multivariable logistic regression, age >50 years (adjusted OR 3.20, 95% CI 1.81-5.64) and molars (adjusted OR 4.31, 2.24-8.27) or premolars (adjusted OR 2.61, 1.16-5.86) versus incisors were significant; gender, root canal treatment history, restoration type and post presence/type/extension were not; fractured molar roots were mainly maxillary mesiobuccal (15/16) and mandibular mesial (9/13).

Age and tooth type flag teeth in which VRF should be suspected, but the sample contains only surgery-referred failing teeth with no non-endodontically treated or extracted comparators, so prevalence and null findings (post, restoration) are not generalisable.

## 세줄요약

후향적 의무기록 코호트(대만 타이베이의대병원 2012-2018; 증상이 지속되는 근관치료치 중 현미경 하 치근단 미세수술을 받은 359개 치아, 275명, 메틸렌블루 염색으로 확인) — 수직치근파절(Vertical Root Fracture, VRF)은 67개 치아로 치아 기준 18.7%, 환자 기준 22.5%.

다변량 로지스틱 회귀에서 50세 초과(보정 OR 3.20, 95% CI 1.81-5.64), 대구치(보정 OR 4.31, 2.24-8.27)와 소구치(보정 OR 2.61, 1.16-5.86; 절치 대비)가 유의했고, 성별·근관치료 이력·수복물 종류·포스트(Post) 유무/종류/연장은 유의하지 않았으며, 대구치 파절 치근은 상악 근심협측근(15/16)과 하악 근심근(9/13)에 집중됨.

연령과 치아 종류는 VRF를 의심할 단서이나, 대상이 수술 의뢰된 실패 치아뿐이고 비근관치료치·발치된 치아 대조가 없어 유병률과 포스트·수복물의 무효 결과는 일반화하기 어려움.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Quantifies VRF prevalence (67/359 teeth, 18.7%; 62/275 patients, 22.5%) in a microsurgically explored, symptomatic root-filled cohort.
- Gives adjusted odds ratios for age >50 years and for molars/premolars versus incisors.
- Reports no significant association with gender, tooth location, history of root canal treatment (non-surgical only vs non-surgical plus surgical), restoration type, or post presence/type/apical extension.
- Maps fractured molar root sites: maxillary mesiobuccal root 15/16 (93.7%), mandibular mesial root 9/13 (69.2%).
- Builds on the authors' earlier diagnostic-factor study (J-shaped/halo radiolucency, probing depth >=5 mm, sinus tract, swelling/abscess), which is cited but not held in the wiki.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective observational study of charts and digital radiographs; one department, Jan 2012-Jul 2018.
- **Population**: endodontically treated teeth with persistent signs/symptoms that underwent endodontic microsurgery; teeth with incomplete clinical or radiographic data excluded. 359 teeth, 275 patients; 215 female (59.9%), 144 male; mean age 44.8 +/- 13.2 years (20-84).
- **Reference standard**: root surface dyed with methylene blue and inspected under the microscope during surgery.
- **Variables**: age, gender, tooth type and location, root canal treatment history, restoration (direct vs cuspal coverage; abutment role), post (presence, type, apical extension).
- **Analysis**: Pearson chi-square; binary logistic regression (univariate, then multivariable with variables significant on univariate), SPSS 22.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single-centre retrospective chart review; sample restricted to failing, symptomatic, surgery-referred teeth, so the 18.7% prevalence overstates population risk (authors note exclusion of non-restorable and extracted teeth).
- Teeth extracted without surgical exploration and non-endodontically treated teeth are absent; sample size is small for sub-comparisons (e.g. 23 teeth with combined non-surgical and surgical history, 13 fibre posts).
- Timing of fracture relative to retreatment cannot be established; most teeth were retreated shortly before microsurgery.
- Only variables significant on univariate analysis entered the multivariable model; restoration and post effects were not adjusted jointly with age and tooth type.
- Radiographic dentine thickness, canal shape and occlusal load were not measured; the authors state other associated factors remain undetermined.

## 6. Related Work

- [[cracked-tooth/liao-2021-vertical-root-fracture-non-endodontically-treated]]: review reporting onset mostly after age 40 and clustering in maxillary premolars and mesial roots of mandibular molars; consistent with this cohort.
- [[cracked-tooth/patel-2022-present-status-future-directions-vertical]]: review naming over-preparation, root anatomy and occlusal load as drivers; this cohort supports anatomy (tooth type, root site) over post presence.
- [[cracked-tooth/patel-2025-position-statement-longitudinal-cracks-fractures]]: consensus statement; places VRF at 4-32% of root-filled-tooth extractions and describes the older-age, first-molar root-site pattern for non-root-filled teeth; this cohort shows a similar pattern in root-filled teeth.
- [[endodontics/shaping/lee-2026-residual-pericervical-apical-dentine-vertical]]: molar case-control study also finding post non-significant; found retreatment history a strong predictor whereas this cohort's coarse treatment-history variable was non-significant.

## 7. Glossary

- **Vertical root fracture (VRF)**: longitudinal fracture initiated at any root level and propagating along the root axis, usually buccolingually.
- **Endodontic microsurgery**: apical surgery under the dental operating microscope; here also used for exploratory visualisation of the fracture line.
- **Methylene blue staining**: dye applied to the resected root surface to reveal fracture lines under magnification.
- **Cuspal coverage prosthesis**: crown or bridge restoration covering the cusps, as opposed to direct amalgam/composite restorations.
- **Adjusted OR**: odds ratio from the multivariable logistic model controlling for the other retained variables (here age and tooth type).
