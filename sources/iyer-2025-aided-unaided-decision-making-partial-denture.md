## Why Ingested
치과 상담 동의율 세트 인제스트: 환자 의사결정 보조도구(PDA)가 부분의치 선택에서 결정 갈등(decisional conflict)을 얼마나 줄이는지 RCT 설계로 정량 측정한 드문 치과 연구. [[behavioral-dentistry/communication-relationship/asaad-2019-shared-decision-making-sdm-dentistry]] 가 지목한 "치과 PDA 부족" 문제를 실험적으로 검증하고, DCS 점수로 상담 원조 효과를 수치화한 근거를 [[overviews/patient-consultation-communication-protocol]] 에 추가한다.

## One-line Summary
RCT (n=76) of patient decision aid (PDA) vs unaided consultation for partial denture selection: aided group DCS 8.0±6.4 vs unaided 20.6±15.9 (p<0.001); aided patients more frequently chose fixed partial denture (71.1% vs 34.2%).

## 한줄요약
RCT(n=76): 부분의치 선택 시 환자 의사결정 보조도구(PDA) 사용군 결정갈등 점수 8.0 vs 비사용군 20.6(p<0.001); 보조군은 고정성 선택 71.1% vs 비보조군 34.2%.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Cureus, Vol 17, Issue 1, e77803, January 2025
- Institution: College of Dentistry, King Saud Bin Abdulaziz University for Health Sciences, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
- IRB: KAIMRC IRB/0445/23
- Data collection: May–November 2024 (6 months)
- Funding/COI: not disclosed

## 2. Key Contributions
- Translated and validated the Decisional Conflict Scale (DCS-16) into Arabic for dental use (Cronbach's α=0.84)
- Developed an evidence-based PDA for partial dentures (removable vs. fixed) in collaboration with a board-certified prosthodontist
- Demonstrated significantly lower decisional conflict in aided vs unaided group across all 5 DCS subscales
- Showed PDA shifted treatment preference: aided group 71.1% chose fixed partial denture (FPD) vs unaided 65.8% chose removable (RPD)
- Age significantly associated with uncertainty subscale: younger patients (20–40) less certain than older (41–60) even with aid

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Randomized controlled trial (single-center, parallel-group, blinded investigator for questionnaire administration)
- Setting: Outpatient College of Dentistry, King Saud Bin Abdulaziz University, Riyadh
- n: 76 total (38 aided, 38 unaided); randomization via concealed envelopes
- Inclusion: patients indicated for partial denture service
- Aided group: PDA provided + used by senior undergraduate clinicians to explain RPD vs FPD options (advantages, disadvantages, evidence)
- Unaided group: standard clinical consultation (usual care)
- Outcome: DCS-16 score (0–100; higher = more conflict) per subscale and total
- Statistics: independent sample t-test; Chi-square; multinomial analysis for exploratory variables

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Overall DCS: aided 8.0±6.4 vs unaided 20.6±15.9 (significant difference)
- FPD-seeking aided DCS: 6.3±4.4 vs FPD-seeking unaided: 27.2±13.3 (p<0.001)
- Treatment choice: aided → 71.1% FPD, 28.9% RPD; unaided → 34.2% FPD, 65.8% RPD
- DCS subscales: all 4 subscales (Informed, Value Clarity, Support, Uncertainty) showed high internal correlation (Cronbach's α maintained at 0.84 across item-deletion)
- Age effect: younger (20–40 yrs) less confident in decision even in aided group (OR: −17.8, CI 1.47–2.28, p=0.00 for Q14 effective decision subscale)
- Marital status and prior dental visits also significantly associated with responses in aided group

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Single-center, single-country; cultural context (Saudi Arabia) limits generalizability
- Blinding: clinicians administering PDA not blinded; patients knew group allocation
- No follow-up: does lower decisional conflict translate to treatment completion or satisfaction?
- Student clinicians administered PDA; experienced clinician effect unknown
- Exploratory variable associations (age, income) are hypothesis-generating only

## 7. Glossary
- DCS: Decisional Conflict Scale — O'Connor's validated 16-item tool measuring patient uncertainty during healthcare decision-making (0=no conflict, 100=maximum conflict)
- PDA: Patient Decision Aid — evidence-based tool informing patients of treatment options, pros/cons, enabling values clarification
- FPD: Fixed Partial Denture — 고정성 부분의치 (브릿지 등)
- RPD: Removable Partial Denture — 가철성 부분의치
- Effective decision subscale: DCS subscale measuring whether patient feels the decision reflects their values and can implement it
