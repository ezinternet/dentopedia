## Why Ingested

`clear-aligner` 카테고리 52편 중 상악 절치(upper incisor) TORQUE(축이동) 정확도를 특정해 정량화한 논문이 드묾 — [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/ubuzima-2025-clear-aligner-fixed-anterior-teeth-movement-adults-sr]]가 "FA가 torque·rotation에서 CA보다 우위"라는 study-tally를 제공하지만 수치는 아니었고, [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/cao-2025-clear-aligner-biomechanics-finite-element-analysis-sr]]는 FEA로 그 기전(치관-치근 불균등 응력)을 설명한다. 본 SR(Gonçalves 2023)은 상악 절치에 국한해 torque 39–51.5%, tip 45.5–155.7%, rotation 41.8–66.2%, intrusion 0–44.6%, extrusion 45–142.4% 등 이동유형별 achieved/predicted 정확도 백분율 범위를 직접 제시해 그 weak-point를 수치로 뒷받침한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42020190272) of 5 studies (4 retrospective + 1 prospective, non-randomized, 20–38 patients each, 612 total upper-incisor movements) on the accuracy of Invisalign clear aligner tooth movement in the maxillary central and lateral incisors, published 2010 onward (post-SmartTrack era).

Achieved-vs-predicted accuracy ranged from 0% (intrusion moving opposite direction, i.e. extruding) to 155.7% (lingual tip overshooting); torque accuracy was consistently among the lowest (49.1–51.5% when reported as %), while extrusion showed some of the highest values (up to 142.4%) and horizontal translations were comparably accurate (67.6–78.9%).

No RCTs exist on the topic post-2010; overall aligner efficiency on upper incisors is low and inconsistent across movement types, meaning refinements/overcorrection are frequently required, with attachments and Power Ridge features improving but not resolving accuracy for torque and intrusion.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42020190272)로 5편(후향 4 + 전향 1, 모두 비무작위, 환자 20–38명, 총 상악절치 이동 612건)의 Invisalign 상악 중절치·측절치 이동 정확도 연구를 종합 — 2010년 이후(SmartTrack 도입 이후) 발표 논문만 포함.

달성/예측 정확도는 0%(함입이 반대방향인 정출로 나타난 경우)부터 155.7%(설측경사 과달성)까지 분포; torque 정확도는 보고될 때마다 가장 낮은 축(49.1–51.5%)에 속했고, 정출(extrusion)은 최대 142.4%로 가장 높은 축에 속했으며, 수평이동(translation)은 67.6–78.9%로 비교적 정확했다.

2010년 이후 이 주제로 시행된 RCT는 전무; 전체적으로 상악절치에서 aligner 효율은 낮고 이동유형별 편차가 커 재조정(refinement)·과교정이 자주 필요하며, attachment·Power Ridge가 정확도를 개선하나 torque·intrusion 문제를 완전히 해결하지 못한다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Systematic review (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42020190272; ROBINS-I risk-of-bias assessment; no meta-analysis — 5 heterogeneous studies)
- **Journal**: Turkish Journal of Orthodontics, published 2023-06-22
- **PMID**: 37346374 | **PMCID**: PMC10318852 | **DOI**: 10.4274/TurkJOrthod.2022.2021.0254

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR to isolate accuracy data specifically for **upper incisors** (rather than movement type across all teeth, as prior SRs did), enabling tooth-group-specific clinical interpretation.
- Uniformizes heterogeneous accuracy reporting (percentage ratios vs. mean predicted-achieved differences in mm/degrees) into comparable accuracy percentages across studies.
- Documents that torque terminology is inconsistently used across the underlying studies (clinical torque vs. labiolingual tip conflated), flagging a measurement-definition problem in the field.
- Confirms intrusion as the least accurate movement (sometimes reversing direction entirely) and extrusion as the most accurate/overachieved movement — the opposite of common clinical assumption that vertical movement is uniformly hard for aligners.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PRISMA-guided SR; protocol registered PROSPERO CRD42020190272. Databases: PubMed, LILACS, Scopus, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, plus manual search of orthodontic journals; English/French/Spanish/Portuguese; studies published 2010+ only (post-Invisalign SmartTrack/SmartForce/SmartStage introduction). Search yielded 291 records → 238 after dedup → 12 full-text reviewed → 4 eligible + 1 added from reference search = **5 included studies**. Three independent reviewers for selection (disagreements resolved by 2 additional authors); two independent reviewers for ROBINS-I bias assessment (non-randomized studies tool, 7 domains). Study profile: 4 retrospective non-randomized + 1 prospective non-randomized, sample sizes 20–38 patients per study, 612 total incisor movements analyzed. Bias: 1 low RoB, 3 moderate RoB, 1 serious RoB (missing-data/dropout reporting was the critical bias driver in 3 studies; 3 studies also had insufficient accuracy metrics reported, hampering cross-study comparison).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

By movement type (CI = central incisor, LI = lateral incisor; accuracy = achieved/predicted ratio, >100% = overshoot, 0% = reversed direction):

- **Torque**: 49.1–51.5% (CI, only 2/5 studies reported clean percentages); one study instead reported mean difference 1.75°±2.86° (CI) and 0.08°±2.93° (LI, near-perfect). Torque terminology inconsistently defined across studies (clinical torque vs. labiolingual tip conflated in some).
- **Labiolingual tip**: lingual tip 57.4–155.7% (CI), 54.4–57.4% (LI); labial tip 52.8–54.2% (CI), 61.4–69.9% (LI). Highest single value (155.7%) occurred in a bicuspid-extraction protocol study, complicating interpretation.
- **Mesiodistal tip**: 45.5–57.5% (CI), 38.5–51.5% (LI).
- **Intrusion**: 33.4–33.9% (CI), 36.7–44.6% (LI) in one study; in another study intrusion was **0% accuracy** — predicted intrusion instead produced extrusion (opposite direction) in both CI and LI, attributed to a bite-block effect from posterior tooth superimposition.
- **Extrusion**: mean differences -0.50±1.17 mm (accuracy 142.4%) to 0.30±0.28 mm (CI); 0.03±0.26 mm to 1.36±0.63 mm (LI). Two studies reported extrusion accuracy >100% (achieved exceeded predicted) — extrusion was the single most accurate/overachieved movement category in the review.
- **Rotation**: 48.7–61.1% (CI), 41.8–66.2% (LI); mean differences 0.33°±2.80° to 2.33°±1.21° (CI, ~57.2% accuracy), 0.70°±3.23° to 3.10°±1.48° (LI, ~66.1% accuracy).
- **Labial/lingual translation**: 67.6% (CI labial translation, one study); LI lingual translation 0.01±0.66 mm (near-perfect).
- **Mesiodistal translation**: 78.9% (CI), 77.2% (LI) — highest accuracy among horizontal movements.
- **Overall accuracy range across all movements**: 0% to 155.7%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 5 eligible studies found despite a broad multi-database search; sparse and heterogeneous evidence base.
- **No RCTs exist** on this topic since the 2010 SmartTrack-era cutoff — all included evidence is non-randomized (retrospective/prospective cohort).
- Small, variable sample sizes (20–38 patients/study); inconsistent reporting formats (some report % accuracy, some report raw mm/degree differences, some report only min/max) hamper direct cross-study comparison.
- Confounding by attachment/Power Ridge use not consistently isolated — 3/5 studies used attachments, 1 used Power Ridge, 1 used neither, but the review cannot fully separate device-feature effect from study-level accuracy.
- Torque definition inconsistency across included studies is itself a methodological limitation of the underlying literature, not just of this review.
- Authors call for future primary studies to report complete predicted-vs-achieved raw data with standardized accuracy-percentage conversion.

## 7. Glossary

- **CI / LI**: central incisor / lateral incisor.
- **Accuracy (%)**: ratio of achieved to predicted tooth movement/position; >100% = overachieved (opposite-overshoot), 0% = movement in opposite direction to predicted.
- **ROBINS-I**: Risk Of Bias In Non-randomized Studies of Interventions — 7-domain bias assessment tool.
- **SmartTrack / SmartForce / Power Ridge**: Invisalign proprietary material/feature technologies introduced from 2009 onward, intended to improve tooth-movement accuracy (SmartForce includes the Invisalign Power Ridge, an attachment-like feature to improve torque control).
- **Torque**: third-order (buccolingual root apex) movement; **tip**: crown angulation (labiolingual or mesiodistal) without necessarily moving the root apex equivalently; **translation**: bodily (parallel crown+root) movement.
