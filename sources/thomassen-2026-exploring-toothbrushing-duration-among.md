## Why Ingested
Quantifies actual (uninstructed) brushing duration, the behavioral side of the 2-minute guideline that the wiki's efficacy pages assume; extends the powered-vs-manual comparison in [[wiki/periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/luo-2026-electric-toothbrush-technologies-plaque-gingivitis-nma]] and the technique review [[wiki/periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/rajwani-2020-manual-toothbrushing-techniques-plaque-gingivitis-sr]] with duration data.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42024565180; MEDLINE-PubMed + Cochrane-CENTRAL to Jan 2025) of timed single-brushing exercises in uninstructed healthy adults: 12 observational studies, 16 evaluations, 1246 participants (14 MTB evaluations, 2 PTB evaluations).

Weighted mean brushing time was 96.8 s overall (95% CI 77.10-116.50); manual toothbrush (MTB) 87.3 s (CI 68.50-106.10) vs powered toothbrush (PTB) 151.1 s (CI 142.37-159.83; non-overlapping CIs); MTB duration rose with publication year (R2 = 0.522); GRADE certainty moderate for MTB, limited for PTB.

Uninstructed adults brush manually for ~1.5 min, short of the 2-min guideline, while PTB users exceed 2 min; limits: observational lab-timed studies (Hawthorne effect), mostly European, 1978-2018 data, only 2 PTB evaluations, no formal meta-analysis or plaque outcome.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (PROSPERO CRD42024565180; MEDLINE-PubMed·Cochrane-CENTRAL, 2025년 1월까지)로, 지도받지 않은 건강한 성인의 1회 칫솔질 시간을 측정한 관찰연구 12편·16개 평가·1246명 (수동칫솔 14건, 전동칫솔 2건)을 분석함.

가중평균 칫솔질 시간은 전체 96.8초 (95% 신뢰구간 77.10-116.50), 수동칫솔 (Manual Toothbrush, MTB) 87.3초 vs 전동칫솔 (Powered Toothbrush, PTB) 151.1초 (신뢰구간 비중첩)였고, 수동칫솔 시간은 출판연도에 따라 증가 (R2 = 0.522); GRADE 확실성은 수동칫솔 중등도, 전동칫솔 제한적.

지도받지 않은 성인은 수동칫솔로 약 1.5분 닦아 2분 권고에 못 미치고 전동칫솔 사용자는 2분을 넘김; 한계: 실험실 타이머 측정 관찰연구 (호손 효과), 대부분 유럽, 1978-2018년 자료, 전동칫솔 평가 2건뿐, 정식 메타분석·플라크 결과 없음.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Int J Dent Hyg 2026;24(4):691-701 (published 2026-04-04)
- **DOI**: 10.1111/idh.70048
- **Institution**: Department of Periodontology, Academic Centre for Dentistry Amsterdam (ACTA), Netherlands

## 2. Key Contributions
- First systematic review of measured (not self-reported) single-session toothbrushing duration in uninstructed adults, separated a priori into MTB and PTB.
- Reports a positive relation between publication year and MTB brushing time (R2 = 0.522).
- GRADE profile: moderate certainty for ~1.5 min MTB, limited body of evidence for PTB (> 2 min).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: SR following Cochrane Handbook, MOOSE and SWiM; protocol a priori; ROBINS-I risk of bias; GRADE. No formal meta-analysis: weighted mean (by n) with 95% CI, descriptive linear regression on publication year (SPSS 30.0).
- **Databases**: MEDLINE-PubMed, Cochrane-CENTRAL (search Oct 15, 2024, updated Jan 22, 2025).
- **n**: 3287 unique records, 52 full texts, 40 excluded, 12 studies (16 evaluations, 1246 participants; 14-300 per study).
- **Outcomes**: mean brushing time (s) by toothbrush type; correlation with publication year. Self-reported questionnaires excluded; dental professionals/students excluded.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Range of study means 33 s to 158 s.
- Overall 96.8 s (SD 40.2; 95% CI 77.10-116.50; 16 evaluations, n = 1246).
- MTB 87.3 s (SD 35.9; CI 68.50-106.10; 14 evaluations, n = 1060).
- PTB 151.1 s (SD 6.3; CI 142.37-159.83; 2 evaluations, n = 186; individual means 157.5 s and 145 s). The abstract states 143 s for PTB, which differs from the body/Table 3 value (151.1 s); the body value is used here.
- MTB means rose with publication year (R2 = 0.522); PTB regression not feasible (2 points).
- All studies observational (cross-sectional, cross-over, one cohort), all included studies moderate risk of bias; GRADE: MTB moderate, PTB limited; consistency rather inconsistent; publication bias possible.
- Discussion cites (external, not re-verified here) that 2 min vs 1 min improves plaque removal and 2 to 3 min adds no benefit; FDI notes effective cleaning may matter more than a fixed duration.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Timed, often filmed sessions may induce a Hawthorne effect; naturalistic home brushing likely shorter.
- Substantial clinical heterogeneity (mirror, own vs provided brush, dentifrice use); all but one study European; oldest data from 1978.
- Number of teeth, age and dexterity unreported; only 2 PTB evaluations (both oscillating-rotating).
- Children and dental professionals not covered; authors' group has industry ties (declared).

## 7. Glossary
- **MTB**: manual toothbrush.
- **PTB**: powered (electric) toothbrush; here oscillating-rotating heads.
- **SWiM**: Synthesis Without Meta-analysis reporting guideline.
- **ROBINS-I**: risk-of-bias tool for non-randomized studies.
- **Hawthorne effect**: behavior change because participants know they are observed.
