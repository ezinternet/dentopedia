## Why Ingested

The wiki holds a 2026 nonpharmacological NMA for myogenic TMD ([[tmj/zhang-2026-nonpharmacological-myogenic-tmd-nma]]) but no earlier NMA that also ranks injection, botulinum toxin, ozone and hypnosis options. This 2021 NMA (52 RCTs, search to August 2018) adds those modalities and certainty grades, and independently points to manual therapy as a top option, as in [[tmj/demelo-2020-manual-therapy-myofascial-pain-tmd-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + frequentist network meta-analysis of 52 RCTs in adults with myogenous TMD (M-TMD), comparing counseling, occlusal appliances, manual therapy, laser, dry needling, LA or BTX-A injection, muscle relaxants, hypnosis/relaxation, ozone, and placebo/no treatment (search to August 2018).

Most treatments beat placebo for pain at most follow-ups; short-term (<=5 months) top ranks were manual therapy (83.5%, low certainty), ozone (75.7%, very low), counseling (71.2%, moderate) and occlusal appliances (71.7%, moderate); intermediate-term (>=6 months) were BTX-A (85.8%, very low), counseling (80%, low), occlusal appliances (62.8%, low), hypnosis (50.6%, very low).

Manual therapy is presented as the most effective option, followed by counseling, intramuscular LA and occlusal appliances, but certainty is low to very low for many rankings and the authors urge cautious interpretation.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 + 빈도론적 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA) 52개 RCT, 성인 근육성 측두하악장애 (Myogenous Temporomandibular Disorders, M-TMD) — 상담치료, 교합장치, 도수치료, 레이저, 건침, 국소마취제 (Local Anesthesia, LA)·보툴리눔독소 A (Botulinum Toxin-A, BTX-A) 주사, 근이완제, 최면·이완, 오존, 위약/무치료 비교 (검색 2018년 8월까지).

대부분의 치료가 위약보다 통증을 유의하게 줄였고, 단기(5개월 이하) 순위는 도수치료 83.5%(낮은 확실성), 오존 75.7%(매우 낮음), 상담치료 71.2%(중간), 교합장치 71.7%(중간); 중기(6개월 이상)는 BTX-A 85.8%(매우 낮음), 상담치료 80%(낮음), 교합장치 62.8%(낮음), 최면 50.6%(매우 낮음).

도수치료가 가장 효과적인 것으로 제시되고 상담치료, LA 근육 내 주사, 교합장치가 뒤따르나, 다수 순위의 근거 확실성이 낮거나 매우 낮아 저자들은 신중한 해석을 권고.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Network ranking of ten modality groups (including injections, ozone, hypnosis) for adult M-TMD, with short-term and intermediate-term horizons.
- Certainty ratings attached to each top-ranked option.
- Conclusion that manual therapy is the most effective treatment for M-TMD, followed by counseling, intramuscular LA and occlusal appliances.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Adults with M-TMD; RCTs comparing two or more of the listed modalities.
- Primary outcomes: pain reduction and mechanical sensitivity; secondary: maximal mouth opening (MMO).
- Standardized mean difference, frequentist NMA in STATA; risk of bias by Cochrane tool. Details beyond the abstract (databases, registration, inconsistency tests) were not retrieved.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 52 RCTs included.
- Versus placebo, at most follow-ups manual therapy, counseling, occlusal splints, BTX-A or LA needling, and dry needling significantly decreased post-treatment pain.
- Short-term (<=5 months) ranking probabilities: manual therapy 83.5% (low), ozone 75.7% (very low), counseling 71.2% (moderate), occlusal appliances 71.7% (moderate).
- Intermediate-term (>=6 months): BTX-A 85.8% (very low), counseling 80% (low), occlusal appliances 62.8% (low), hypnosis 50.6% (very low).
- MMO results are not reported in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest: no effect sizes, confidence intervals, or MMO data available.
- Authors note limitations of included studies and scarce strong evidence; many top ranks are low or very low certainty.
- Search ended August 2018, so later trials (e.g. recent photobiomodulation RCTs) are not represented.
- The abstract lists occlusal appliances at 71.7% after counseling at 71.2%, which is not in descending order; the ordering is reproduced as printed.
- The concluding sentence names LA injection among the top options, although LA is not among the four listed short-term or intermediate-term top ranks.

## 7. Glossary

- **M-TMD**: myogenous (muscle-origin) temporomandibular disorder.
- **NMA**: network meta-analysis; combines direct and indirect comparisons.
- **Ranking probability (SUCRA-like %)**: probability-based ranking score; higher means more likely to be among the best.
- **BTX-A**: botulinum toxin type A.
- **LA**: local anesthesia (intramuscular injection).
