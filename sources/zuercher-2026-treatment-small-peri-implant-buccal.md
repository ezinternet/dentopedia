## Why Ingested

소형 협측 골결손부(buccal bone dehiscence, ≤3 mm)를 임플란트 식립 시점에 연조직 대 경조직 증대술로 처리했을 때의 3년 안정성 데이터가 부재했다. 이 파일럿 RCT는 SCTG와 GBR 각각의 중기 buccal contour 안정성과 임플란트 주위 건강 결과를 직접 비교한 최장 추적 데이터를 제공한다.
→ [[implants/soft-tissue/nart-2025-soft-tissue-augmentation-pstd-sr-ma]]

## Three-line Summary

Pilot RCT (n=16 at 3 years, SCTG=5, GBR=6, control=5) comparing soft vs hard tissue augmentation at single-implant placement in sites with small buccal bone dehiscences (≤3 mm).

Both SCTG and GBR maintained stable buccal contours from year 1 to year 3: contour change at 1 mm below margin was median 0.19 mm (SCTG) vs 0.11 mm (GBR); at 3 mm, 0.05 mm vs −0.05 mm; peri-implant parameters, MBL, and OHIP-14 were favorable and comparable between groups.

Small sample limits inferential analysis; adequately powered RCTs needed; EFP S3 guideline notes no current evidence that soft tissue augmentation alone reduces peri-implant disease in sites with thin tissues (<2 mm).

## 세줄요약

파일럿 RCT(3년 추적, n=16): 협측 골결손부(≤3 mm) 가진 단일 임플란트 식립 부위에서 연결조직이식(SCTG)과 유도골재생(GBR)을 무작위 배정.

양군 모두 1년→3년 사이 buccal contour 변화 미미(1 mm 하방: SCTG 중앙값 +0.19 mm, GBR +0.11 mm; 3 mm 하방: +0.05 mm vs −0.05 mm); 치주낭깊이·BOP·변연골소실·OHIP-14 양호하고 군 간 유사.

소규모(파일럿), 기술통계만 적용 → 충분한 검정력의 RCT 필요; EFP S3 가이드라인은 연조직 단독증대가 임플란트 주위염 발생을 줄인다는 근거가 현재 없다고 결론.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Longest published follow-up (3 years) of a randomized comparison of SCTG vs GBR for small peri-implant buccal bone dehiscences (≤3 mm) at implant placement time
- Demonstrates buccal contour stability of both approaches beyond the first year — most remodeling occurs in year 0–1
- SCTG achieved greater KM width (3.0 mm) vs GBR (2.1 mm) at 3 years
- BBT at implant shoulder numerically lower in SCTG (median 0.05 mm) vs GBR (0.55 mm) — SCTG does not regenerate bone; GBR partially does but with variability
- MBL stable in both groups: SCTG median 0.0 mm (Q1: −0.1; Q3: 0.2), GBR 0.3 mm (Q1: 0.0; Q3: 2.1)
- One peri-implantitis case in SCTG group (excluded from 3-year analysis)
- EFP S3 Clinical Practice Guideline cited: no evidence that soft tissue augmentation alone reduces peri-implant disease in thin-tissue sites (<2 mm)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Randomization**: Computer-generated allocation sequence, sealed opaque envelopes opened intraoperatively after confirming dehiscence. Coordinator not involved in outcome assessment.

**Inclusion**: Age ≥18, single-tooth posterior maxilla/mandible, full-mouth plaque + bleeding <25%, PD <4 mm. Buccal bone dehiscence ≤3 mm confirmed intraoperatively by calibrated probe (PCPUNC 15 Hu-Friedy).

**Implant**: OsseoSpeed EV (Dentsply Sirona), 3.6 mm diameter (premolar) or 4.2 mm (molar). Transmucosal healing. Healing period 3–6 months. Monolithic multilayered zirconia crown, screw-retained.

**Follow-up**: Baseline (crown delivery + 7–10 days), 1 year, 3 years.

**Primary outcome — profilometric contour change**:
- STL superimposition of optical impressions (SMOP software, best-fit to adjacent tooth surfaces)
- ROI at mid-facial aspect: mesial/distal 1 mm from adjacent teeth, coronal 1 mm apical to mucosal margin, apical at mucogingival junction
- Linear measurements at 1 mm (ROI-1/TW-1) and 3 mm (ROI-3/TW-3) below mid-facial mucosal margin

**Secondary outcomes**: PD, BOP, PCR, KM width (6 sites/implant); BBT via CBCT+STL superimposition at implant shoulder, −1 mm, −3 mm; MBL via standardized periapical radiographs (ImageJ, thread-pitch calibration); OHIP-14 questionnaire.

**Statistics**: Descriptive only (mean, SD, median, Q1, Q3); GraphPad Prism v9, SPSS v27.0. No inferential testing given small sample.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Buccal contour (1 year → 3 year change)

| Measurement | SCTG (n=5) | GBR (n=6) | Control (n=5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ROI-1 median | +0.19 mm | +0.11 mm | +0.27 mm |
| ROI-3 median | +0.05 mm | −0.05 mm | +0.11 mm |
| TW-1 median | −0.32 mm | −0.17 mm | +0.10 mm |
| TW-3 median | −0.14 mm | −0.03 mm | −0.18 mm |

Positive = volume gain; negative = volume loss. All changes minimal — both groups stable.

### Peri-implant parameters at 3 years

| Parameter | SCTG | GBR | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| PD mean (mm) | 3.4 (SD 1.1) | 3.1 (SD 0.7) | 3.1 (SD 0.7) |
| BOP mean | 0.3 (SD 0.2) | 0.3 (SD 0.2) | 0.1 (SD 0.2) |
| PCR mean | 0.3 (SD 0.2) | 0.2 (SD 0.1) | 0.1 (SD 0.2) |
| KM width mean (mm) | 3.0 (SD 2.0) | 2.1 (SD 1.2) | 3.0 (SD 1.4) |

### Buccal bone thickness at 3 years (medians)

| Level | SCTG | GBR | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| BBT-0 (shoulder) | 0.05 mm | 0.55 mm | 1.34 mm |
| BBT-1 (−1 mm) | 1.53 mm | 0.82 mm | 1.74 mm |
| BBT-3 (−3 mm) | 2.26 mm | 0.98 mm | 2.07 mm |

ICC for BBT: 0.96 (95% CI: 0.79–0.99).

### Marginal bone loss at 3 years

| Group | MBL median | Range (Q1, Q3) |
|---|---|---|
| SCTG | 0.0 mm | (−0.1, 0.2) |
| GBR | 0.3 mm | (0.0, 2.1) |
| Control | 0.2 mm | (−0.1, 0.9) |

ICC for MBL: 0.99 (95% CI: 0.96–0.99).

OHIP-14 medians: SCTG 0.0, GBR 0.0, control 3.0 — all favorable.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Small sample (pilot)**: n=16 at 3 years; purely descriptive, no inferential statistics, no power calculation
- **Per-protocol attrition**: 2 dropouts from 1-year cohort; potential attrition bias
- **CBCT limitations**: Registration inaccuracies; BBT values represent radiographically detectable bone, not histological bone
- **Non-randomized control**: Control group descriptive reference only
- **Missing baseline variables**: Soft-tissue thickness, defect morphology (contained vs non-contained) not systematically recorded
- **Single protocol per arm**: Only one SCTG and one GBR protocol evaluated
- **Mid-term (3 years)**: Insufficient to determine long-term peri-implant disease risk
- **Generalizability**: Carefully selected, well-maintained patients; standard-diameter implants (3.6–4.2 mm)
- EFP S3: No current evidence that soft tissue augmentation alone reduces peri-implant disease in thin-tissue (<2 mm) sites

## 7. Glossary

- **SCTG (Subepithelial Connective Tissue Graft, 연결조직이식)**: Autogenous connective tissue harvested from palate; primary goal is soft tissue volume increase
- **GBR (Guided Bone Regeneration, 유도골재생)**: Barrier membrane + graft to regenerate missing bone; primary goal is buccal bone volume restoration
- **Buccal bone dehiscence (협측 골결손부)**: Absence or deficiency of buccal alveolar bone from implant shoulder apically; ≤3 mm = small
- **Profilometric contour change (프로파일측정 윤곽 변화)**: STL-based measurement of soft tissue surface dimension change over time
- **BBT (Buccal Bone Thickness, 협측 골두께)**: CBCT-measured bone width buccal to implant at specified levels
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level/Loss, 변연골소실)**: Radiographic distance from implant shoulder to first bone-to-implant contact
- **OHIP-14 (Oral Health Impact Profile-14)**: 14-item patient-reported quality of life questionnaire; lower score = better
- **KM (Keratinized Mucosa, 각화점막)**: Width of keratinized tissue measured midfacially
- **ROI (Region of Interest)**: Defined 3D zone for profilometric measurement; ROI-1 at 1 mm, ROI-3 at 3 mm below mucosal margin
