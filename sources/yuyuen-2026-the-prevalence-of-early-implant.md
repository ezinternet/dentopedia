## Three-line Summary

Retrospective nested case-control study at a single Thai university dental hospital: 7,368 implants placed 2016-2023, 114 implants (96 patients) failed before final restoration, compared with 192 randomly selected control patients (1:2).

Early failure prevalence was 1.55%; in multivariable logistic regression, implant diameter under 3.5 mm (OR 6.21, 95% CI 1.88-20.55, p=0.003) and length under 10 mm (OR 2.06, 95% CI 1.12-3.82, p=0.022) were the only significant factors, while untreated periodontitis (OR 2.12, 95% CI 1.07-4.24, p=0.033) was significant only in univariate analysis.

Patient factors (sex, age, diabetes, smoking) were not significant, but missing HbA1c and incomplete smoking data limit these null findings; the single-center retrospective design and small number of cases limit power.

## 세줄요약

태국 단일 대학병원의 후향적 중첩 환자-대조군 연구 (nested case-control): 2016-2023년 식립한 임플란트 7,368개 중 최종 보철 전 114개 (환자 96명)가 실패했고, 무작위 선정한 대조군 환자 192명 (1:2)과 비교했다.

조기 실패 유병률은 1.55%였고, 다변량 로지스틱 회귀에서 직경 3.5 mm 미만 (OR 6.21, 95% CI 1.88-20.55, p=0.003)과 길이 10 mm 미만 (OR 2.06, 95% CI 1.12-3.82, p=0.022)만 유의했으며, 치료받지 않은 치주염 (OR 2.12, 95% CI 1.07-4.24, p=0.033)은 단변량에서만 유의했다.

환자 요인 (성별, 연령, 당뇨, 흡연)은 유의하지 않았으나 당화혈색소 (HbA1c) 기록 누락과 불완전한 흡연력으로 이 음성 결과의 해석에는 한계가 있고, 단일기관 후향 설계와 적은 사례 수로 검정력도 제한적이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First assessment of early implant failure prevalence and associated factors in a Thai hospital population, per the authors: 114 of 7,368 implants (1.55%) failed before final restoration.
- Nested case-control design with all cases included and 1:2 random controls; one failed implant randomly chosen per patient with multiple failures.
- Multivariable model: diameter under 3.5 mm OR 6.21 (95% CI 1.88-20.55, p=0.003); length under 10 mm OR 2.06 (95% CI 1.12-3.82, p=0.022).
- Untreated periodontitis raised the odds 2.12-fold versus no periodontitis history (p=0.033; 95% CI 1.07-4.24), but not after multivariable adjustment; treated periodontitis was not associated with early failure (p=0.909).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective nested case-control study from hospital records; chart codes concealed patient identities.
- **Setting/period**: Maha Chakri Sirindhorn Dental Hospital, Mahidol University, Thailand; implants placed 2016-2023; operators were specialists or residents in training.
- **Population**: 7,368 implants placed; 114 early failures in 96 patients (cases); 192 control patients (1:2 ratio).
- **Early failure definition**: implant removed before the final restoration (failure categorized as early or late by timing of final restoration); no fixed day window was given. Excluded: implants explanted at insertion for lack of primary stability, and implants removed after osseointegration for improper positioning.
- **Variables**: patient (sex, age, DM, osteoporosis, antiresorptive drugs, smoking); tooth (periodontal status: history of periodontitis by PSR 3 or 4 or radiographic bone loss, treated vs untreated); implant (brand, type, design, diameter, length, surface, location, platform level crestal vs subcrestal, covering material, placement timing, cause of tooth loss, GBR or sinus lift history, antibiotic regimen, operator experience).
- **Statistics**: t-test or Mann-Whitney U for continuous, chi-square or Fisher's exact for categorical; variables with univariable p<0.10 entered into multiple binary logistic regression; adjusted OR with 95% CI; p<0.05 significant; SPSS 28.
- **Multivariable model variables**: DM, history of periodontitis, implant diameter, length, location, platform level, covering material, placement timing.

- Univariate significant implant factors: diameter, location, platform level (from radiographs), covering material (cover screw vs healing abutment); anterior vs posterior mandible associated in univariate analysis only.
- Reasons for tooth loss at the implant site in the failure group: fracture 34.7%, caries 28.6%, periodontitis 12.2%.
- Early placement tended toward higher failure than immediate placement, not significant in multivariable analysis.
- Smoking, osteoporosis, vitamin D: not testable (smoking data incomplete; too few osteoporosis cases; vitamin D not charted).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective single-center chart review with missing HbA1c in diabetic patients, incomplete smoking history (self-report, no quantity or duration, current vs former not distinguished), illegible or incomplete records, and missing radiographs preventing reliable platform-level assessment.
- Small number of cases limited power (authors note too few completely edentulous and osteoporosis patients); wide CI for diameter (1.88-20.55) shows imprecision.
- Failure defined by timing relative to final restoration with no fixed time window; antibiotic use and operator effects are listed as variables but results are not reported in the text.
- In-paper inconsistencies: the sample size paragraph states a prior prevalence of 1.5% and requires 6,307 implants yet gives a "2% +/- 0.3%" CI wording; Methods say chart codes were used while the Discussion says implant numbers were identifiers; Discussion says controls were year-matched while Methods say simple random selection at 1:2; the percentages for tooth-loss causes (34.7%, 28.6%, 12.2%) imply a denominator of 49 rather than 96 patients/114 implants. Tables 1-4 are absent from the retrieved text, so figures other than those in the running text could not be checked.
- Authors recommend longer data collection, HbA1c and cigarette consumption in charts, and treatment planning (e.g. GBR) to allow adequate implant length and diameter.

## 7. Glossary

- **조기 임플란트 실패 (Early Implant Failure, EIF)**: implant removal before the final restoration (prosthesis).
- **환자-대조군 연구 (Nested Case-Control Study)**: cases and sampled controls drawn from a defined cohort.
- **교차비 (Odds Ratio, OR)**: odds of failure in the exposed relative to the unexposed.
- **치주 선별 검사 점수 (Periodontal Screening and Recording, PSR)**: screening index; scores 3 or 4 defined periodontitis history here.
- **당화혈색소 (Glycated Hemoglobin, HbA1c)**: glycemic control marker; under 7% counted as controlled DM in this paper's definition.
