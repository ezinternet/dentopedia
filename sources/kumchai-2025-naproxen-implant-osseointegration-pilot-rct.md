## Why Ingested

This is the only randomized controlled trial that directly measures dental implant osseointegration outcomes (ISQ and marginal bone loss) under NSAID exposure in humans, filling the human-evidence gap flagged by the narrative review [[wiki/drug/analgesics/etikala-2019-nsaids-periodontal-implant-therapy-review]]. It supplies the effect sizes and sample-size estimates that were previously missing from the NSAID-and-osseointegration literature.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized, placebo-controlled pilot trial (n=12; maxillary implant patients) comparing naproxen 220 mg q8h for 7 days (n=7) against placebo (n=5), both with adjunctive acetaminophen, tracking implant stability (ISQ by resonance frequency analysis) and marginal bone loss over 16 weeks.

At 4 weeks ISQ rose +1% (naproxen) vs +41% (placebo) and at 16 weeks +34% vs +67%; the naproxen group also showed ~55% more marginal bone loss at 4 weeks and ~52% more at 16 weeks — none of these differences reached statistical significance given the small sample, though point estimates consistently favored impairment.

As an underpowered pilot the study cannot confirm a clinical effect, but it provides effect sizes showing an ISQ-based definitive trial needs >=64/group while a marginal-bone-loss endpoint needs only 7-10/group, recommending MBL as the efficient endpoint for future trials.

## 세줄요약

무작위 위약대조 파일럿 시험 (n=12; 상악 임플란트 환자)으로, 나프록센 (Naproxen) 220 mg 8시간마다 7일 투여군 (n=7)과 위약군 (n=5)을 아세트아미노펜 (Acetaminophen) 병용 하에 비교하며, 임플란트 안정성 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ; 공명주파수분석, Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA)과 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)을 16주간 추적했다.

4주 ISQ 증가는 나프록센 +1% 대 위약 +41%, 16주는 +34% 대 +67%였고, 나프록센군은 4주에 MBL이 약 55% 더 많고 16주에 약 52% 더 많았으나 — 소표본 때문에 어느 것도 통계적 유의성에 도달하지 못했고, 점추정치는 일관되게 골유착 저해 방향을 가리켰다.

과소검정된 파일럿이라 임상적 효과를 확정할 수는 없으나, ISQ 기반 확정 시험은 군당 64명 이상이 필요한 반면 MBL 지표는 군당 7-10명만 필요함을 보여, 향후 시험의 효율적 지표로 MBL을 권고한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First human randomized controlled trial to directly measure osseointegration outcomes (implant stability and marginal bone loss) under NSAID exposure, rather than inferring risk from general bone-healing studies.
- Provides quantitative effect sizes for both an ISQ (implant stability) endpoint and a marginal-bone-loss (MBL) endpoint under naproxen versus placebo.
- Derives sample-size estimates for a future definitive trial and identifies MBL as the far more efficient primary endpoint.
- Confirms naproxen's analgesic efficacy in this setting: only 14.3% of the naproxen group required rescue medication versus 80% of placebo.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: prospective, randomized, placebo-controlled pilot trial in patients receiving maxillary dental implants.
- Groups: naproxen 220 mg every 8 hours for 7 days (n=7) vs matching placebo (n=5); both groups additionally received acetaminophen 500 mg every 6 hours, with acetaminophen-codeine as rescue analgesia.
- Sample: 12 patients analyzed; one naproxen subject removed after implant failure at 1 week.
- Exclusions: smokers, uncontrolled diabetes, chronic NSAID users.
- Outcomes: implant stability by ISQ (resonance frequency analysis, Osstell) at baseline and 1, 4, and 16 weeks; marginal bone loss by periapical radiograph.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- ISQ increase at 4 weeks: +1% (naproxen) vs +41% (placebo); at 16 weeks: +34% (naproxen) vs +67% (placebo) — not statistically significant between groups.
- Marginal bone loss: ~55% more at 4 weeks and ~52% more at 16 weeks in the naproxen group vs placebo — not statistically significant (p >= 0.05).
- All implants had ISQ > 40 by study end; one implant failure occurred at 1 week (naproxen group).
- Rescue medication required by 80% of placebo group vs 14.3% of naproxen group (naproxen relieved pain).
- Power analysis: an ISQ-based definitive trial needs >=64 per group at 4 weeks (up to hundreds); an MBL-based trial needs only ~7-10 per group.
- Supplementary questionnaire of 18 early-implant-failure patients: 67.7% reported post-operative NSAID use vs ~26% in the general population (NHANES); 32.3% did not use NSAIDs.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Underpowered pilot (n=12) — no result reached statistical significance, so no definitive clinical conclusion can be drawn.
- Despite non-significance, point estimates consistently favored impairment (slower ISQ gain, greater MBL in the naproxen group), suggesting NSAIDs may impair early osseointegration.
- Future adequately powered trials should adopt marginal bone loss as the primary endpoint (7-10/group) rather than ISQ (>=64/group) for efficiency.
- Confined to maxillary implants; the questionnaire arm is observational and subject to recall bias.

## 7. Glossary

- ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient): a 1-100 scale quantifying implant stability, derived from resonance frequency analysis; higher values indicate greater stability.
- RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis): non-invasive technique (e.g., Osstell) that measures implant stability by exciting a transducer attached to the implant.
- MBL (Marginal Bone Loss): loss of crestal bone around an implant, measured radiographically; an indicator of peri-implant bone health.
- PGE2 (Prostaglandin E2): a lipid mediator produced via the cyclooxygenase pathway; important for bone remodeling and repair.
- COX-2 (Cyclooxygenase-2): inducible enzyme in prostaglandin synthesis; critical for craniofacial bone repair (COX-2 knockout mice show calvarial non-union) and a target inhibited by NSAIDs.
