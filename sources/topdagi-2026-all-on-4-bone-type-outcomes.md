## Why Ingested

뼈 유형(Bone Type, BT)이 All-on-4 생존율에 독립적으로 영향을 미치는지 정량화한 대규모 후향적 다기관 코호트 연구. 기존 [[wiki/implants/full-arch/figueiredo-2025-all-on-four-overview-sr]]가 CSR 94.8–99.3% 범위를 보고했으나 골질(Bone Quality) 계층별 분리 분석은 없었음. 이 연구는 Lekholm & Zarb 분류 Type 1–4를 층화 변수로 삼아 Type 4 골에서 유의한 생존율 감소를 확인하고, 단일체 지르코니아(Monolithic Zirconia) 보철의 기계적 합병증 우위도 함께 보고했다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective multicenter cohort study of 250 edentulous patients (125 maxillary, 125 mandibular; 1,000 implants) receiving All-on-4 prostheses (September 2018–2021), stratified by Lekholm & Zarb bone types 1–4; mean follow-up 3.6 ± 0.4 years.

Overall implant survival 94.8%; Type 1 bone 98.3% vs Type 4 bone 89.1% (p < 0.05); peri-implant complications significantly more frequent in lower-density bone; prosthetic complications metal-acrylic 26.9% vs monolithic zirconia 8.9% (p < 0.001); patient satisfaction high across all bone types (VAS 8.7 ± 1.2).

Bone quality assessment and prosthetic material selection are independent determinants of All-on-4 medium-term outcomes; Type 4 bone and metal-acrylic prostheses carry measurably higher complication burdens.

## 세줄요약

후향적 다기관 코호트, 완전 무치악 환자 250명(상악 125·하악 125, 임플란트 1,000개), All-on-4 보철, September 2018–2021 시술; Lekholm & Zarb 골 유형(Bone Type) 1–4 층화; 평균 추적 3.6 ± 0.4년.

전체 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 및 생존율 94.8%; Type 1 골 98.3% vs Type 4 골 89.1% (p < 0.05); 치주위 합병증(Peri-implant Complications) 저밀도 골에서 유의하게 증가; 보철 합병증(Prosthetic Complications) 금속-아크릴 26.9% vs 단일체 지르코니아(Monolithic Zirconia) 8.9% (p < 0.001); 환자 만족도 전 골 유형 높음(Visual Analogue Scale, VAS 8.7 ± 1.2).

골질 평가와 보철 재료 선택이 All-on-4 중기 결과의 독립 예측인자; Type 4 골과 금속-아크릴 보철은 합병증 부담 유의하게 높음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Bone type (Lekholm & Zarb Type 1–4) stratified survival data for All-on-4: Type 4 bone 89.1% vs Type 1 bone 98.3% at mean 3.6 years
- First large-cohort direct comparison of metal-acrylic vs monolithic zirconia prosthetic complication rates in All-on-4 (26.9% vs 8.9%)
- Confirms ISQ and insertion torque as markers of primary stability variation across bone types
- Demonstrates that patient satisfaction (VAS 8.7 ± 1.2) remains high even in challenging bone types despite higher complication rates

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Implant survival by bone type:**

| Bone Type (Lekholm & Zarb) | Implant Survival Rate |
|---|---|
| Type 1 (dense cortical) | 98.3% |
| Type 2 | Not specified separately |
| Type 3 | Not specified separately |
| Type 4 (low-density cancellous) | 89.1% |
| **Overall** | **94.8%** |

- Type 4 survival significantly lower than Type 1 (p < 0.05)
- Peri-implant complications (peri-implantitis and screw loosening) significantly more frequent in lower-density bone types
- Prosthetic complications: metal-acrylic 26.9% vs monolithic zirconia 8.9% (p < 0.001)
- Patient satisfaction: Mean VAS 8.7 ± 1.2 across all bone types

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only source; intermediate survival rates for Types 2 and 3 not extractable
- Retrospective design inherits selection bias and confounding limitations
- Multicenter design introduces operator variability
- Mean follow-up 3.6 years is medium-term; long-term survival beyond 5–10 years not addressed
- Bone type classification (Lekholm & Zarb) is subjective; no CT-based objective bone density data (e.g., Hounsfield units) reported

## 7. Glossary

- **ISQ**: 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient) — RFA(공명진동수분석, Resonance Frequency Analysis) 측정값
- **VAS**: 시각 아날로그 척도 (Visual Analogue Scale)
- **Lekholm & Zarb classification**: 골 유형 분류 (Bone Type Classification) — Type 1(치밀 피질골)~Type 4(저밀도 해면골)
- **Monolithic zirconia**: 단일체 지르코니아 (Monolithic Zirconia Prosthesis) — 베니어 없는 완전 지르코니아 보철
