## Why Ingested
A-PRF(Advanced Platelet-Rich Fibrin)의 구강·악안면외과 적용 전반을 PRISMA 기반 SR로 정리한 연구. [[pdrn/marenzi-2015-lprf-postextraction-socket-healing-splitmout]] L-PRF 직접 임상 데이터의 상위 종합 근거이며, A-PRF vs L-PRF 비교 임상 근거를 체계화함.

## One-line Summary
Systematic review (38 articles, PRISMA/PROSPERO CRD42024584161) summarizing A-PRF applications in oral and maxillofacial surgery, confirming lower postoperative pain, improved graft integration, enhanced epithelialization, and wound healing across indications, though high-quality RCT evidence remains limited.

## 한줄요약
체계적 고찰(38편, PRISMA): A-PRF가 수술 후 통증 감소·이식재 통합·상피화 촉진·창상 치유에 유의한 효과; 그러나 대규모 맹검 RCT 부족으로 결론 강도 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First comprehensive SR specifically on A-PRF (not L-PRF/PRP) across oral & maxillofacial indications
- 38 studies included (largest group: 102 patients; smallest: 10 patients)
- A-PRF associated with lower postoperative pain vs L-PRF or blood clot controls
- A-PRF positive effect on grafting material integration confirmed
- A-PRF superior to PRF for epithelialization of free gingival graft donor sites
- A-PRF centrifugation protocol: 1500 rpm × 14 min in vacuum tubes (slower/longer than PRF)
- A-PRF releases growth factors (PDGF, VEGF, FGF, TGF-α/β, BMPs, MMPs) for up to 10 days

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: systematic review following PRISMA guidelines
- Database: not specified explicitly; articles through 11 November 2024
- N included: 38 articles
- Comparison: A-PRF vs L-PRF, blood clot, or conventional healing
- Study sizes: 10–102 patients
- Indications covered: tooth extractions, free gingival grafts, ridge augmentation, implant surgery, maxillofacial procedures
- A-PRF protocol: patient venous blood → vacuum tubes → 1500 rpm × 14 min (no anticoagulants)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Pain: A-PRF correlated with lower postoperative pain vs comparators
- Graft integration: positive effect on grafting material integration confirmed
- Free gingival graft donor sites: A-PRF protected well; promoted more efficient epithelialization
- Wound healing: enhanced overall; soft tissue management and bone preservation noted
- Growth factor release duration: up to 10 days (superior to PRF's shorter release window)
- MMPs (e.g., MMP-9): released more from A-PRF than PRF due to modified centrifugal processing

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Included studies have small sample sizes (max n=102)
- Most studies lack blinding of personnel
- Short follow-up in majority of included studies
- Need for large-sample, blinded RCTs with long follow-up in each application
- Authors acknowledge A-PRF evidence base is preliminary for most indications

## 7. Glossary
- A-PRF (Advanced Platelet-Rich Fibrin): 3rd-generation PRF; 1500 rpm × 14 min centrifugation; looser fibrin matrix; more interfibrous space; sustained growth factor release up to 10 days
- A-PRF+ : higher centrifugation variant
- I-PRF (Injectable PRF): liquid form of A-PRF family
- CGF (Concentrated Growth Factor): alternative 3rd-generation preparation
- PDGF, VEGF, FGF, TGF-α/β, BMPs, MMP-9: key growth factors and proteases in A-PRF
- PROSPERO: international register for systematic review protocols
