## Why Ingested

기존 [[occlusion/didier-2026-occlusal-contacts-ios-vs-carbon-paper-concordance]]는 임상 환경(교정 환자)에서 IOS occlusogram vs 카본지의 일치도가 낮다는 것을 보였으나, MIP 하나만 다뤘고 지표도 카파(kappa) 하나였다. 본 in-vitro 연구(Morsy 2024)는 진단정확도 4지표(민감도·특이도·PPV·NPV)로 MIP뿐 아니라 전방운동(protrusive)·측방운동(lateral) 하악 경계위치까지 확장해 측정한다 — 그 결과 **측방에서만 민감도 28%로 임상 허용선 아래로 급락**한다는, didier의 "IOS 단독 신뢰 불가"라는 결론을 다른 각도(동적 교합)에서 강화하는 동시에, MIP·전방운동은 오히려 우수하다는 대비를 추가한다. Acquisition software의 "multi-occlusion function"이라는 구체적 메커니즘을 직접 시험대에 올린 최초 연구.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro accuracy study: full-dentate 3D-printed master casts mounted on a semi-adjustable articulator, marked with 12 µm articulating paper as the reference standard, versus a Medit i700 intraoral scanner (IOS) using the acquisition software's multi-occlusion function to capture nine virtual interocclusal records (VIRs) per position for maximal intercuspation (MIP), protrusive, and left/right lateral mandibular border positions.

Split verdict by diagnostic accuracy (sensitivity / specificity / PPV / NPV): MIP was excellent (100% / 99% / 99% / 100%), protrusive was acceptable (100% / 83% / 86% / 100%), but lateral positions fell below the clinically acceptable minimum (sensitivity ≥70%, specificity ≥90%) — sensitivity only 28% and NPV only 56% (specificity 93%, PPV 79%) — meaning the scanner missed roughly three of every four true occlusal contacts identified by articulating paper in lateral excursion.

Mesh perforations (virtual overlap artifacts) were far larger in lateral VIRs (up to 455 µm) than protrusive (42 µm) or MIP (148 µm), consistent with a posterior tilting effect during virtual articulation when only 3 reference occlusal stops are available — the mechanistic explanation for why the software's best-fit algorithm fails specifically in lateral excursion.

## 세줄요약

시험관내(in-vitro) 정확도 연구: 완전 치열 3D 프린트 모형을 반조절성 교합기(semi-adjustable articulator)에 장착하고 12 µm 교합지(articulating paper)를 기준(reference)으로 삼아, Medit i700 구강스캐너(Intraoral Scanner, IOS)의 다중교합(multi-occlusion) 기능으로 최대교두감합위(Maximal Intercuspation, MIP)·전방운동위(protrusive)·좌우측방운동위(lateral)마다 가상교합기록(Virtual Interocclusal Record, VIR) 9회씩을 채득해 비교했다.

진단정확도(민감도·특이도·양성예측도 PPV·음성예측도 NPV)는 위치별로 **엇갈린 결과**를 보였다 — MIP는 우수(100%/99%/99%/100%), 전방운동은 허용 가능(100%/83%/86%/100%)했으나, **측방운동은 임상 허용 최소기준(민감도 ≥70%, 특이도 ≥90%)에 못 미쳐 민감도 28%·NPV 56%(특이도 93%, PPV 79%)에 그쳤다** — 즉 교합지가 찾아낸 실제 교합접촉의 약 4분의 3을 스캐너가 놓친다는 뜻이다.

메쉬 관통(mesh perforation, 가상 모형 간 겹침 오류)은 측방 VIR에서 최대 455 µm로, 전방운동(42 µm)·MIP(148 µm)보다 훨씬 컸다 — 측방에서는 교합 기준점(SOC)이 3개뿐이라 소프트웨어의 최적맞춤(best-fit) 알고리즘이 후방으로 기울어지는 효과(tilting effect)가 발생하며, 이것이 측방 민감도가 급락하는 기전적 설명이다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. **First formal accuracy quantification of the IOS "multi-occlusion function" across border positions.** Sensitivity, specificity, PPV, and NPV calculated separately for MIP, protrusive, and left/right lateral virtual interocclusal records (VIRs) — not just MIP, which prior literature had already addressed.
2. **Clean split verdict, not a blanket pass.** MIP (100%/99%/99%/100%) and protrusive (100%/83%/86%/100%) meet the clinically acceptable minimums cited from prior literature (sensitivity ≥70%, specificity ≥90%); lateral positions do not (sensitivity 28%, NPV 56%), despite acceptable specificity (93%) and PPV (79%).
3. **Mechanistic explanation for the lateral failure.** Mesh perforation (virtual cast overlap) reached up to 455 µm in lateral VIRs vs 42 µm (protrusive) and 148 µm (MIP), attributed to a posterior tilting effect during best-fit virtual articulation when only 3 interocclusal reference stops (vs 6 anterior for protrusive, 9 bilateral posterior for MIP) are available to the software's alignment algorithm.
4. **Direction of the lateral error matters clinically.** The IOS tends to *miss* true contacts in lateral excursion (low sensitivity) rather than fabricate false ones (specificity/PPV still acceptable) — the opposite failure mode from didier-2026's MIP occlusogram finding (which over- *and* under-counted depending on color threshold).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: In-vitro, fully controlled, repeated-measures (9 VIRs per position) diagnostic-accuracy study; no patients.
- **Master casts**: Full-dentate maxillary/mandibular 3D-printed casts (Savoy dental model skin, photopolymer, ANSI/ADA-validated material for scanner-accuracy testing), hand-articulated in MIP, mounted on a semi-adjustable articulator (Bio-Art A7-Plus) programmed with average values (condylar inclination 30°, Bennett angle 15°).
- **Reference standard**: 12 µm articulating paper (Bausch Arti-Fol) marking sites of occlusal contact (SOC) directly on the master casts, for each of 4 positions:
  - **MIP**: condylar elements locked; adjustments to give 9 bilateral, evenly distributed SOCs.
  - **Protrusive**: condyles unlocked; a custom DuraLay-resin incisal table cast against the articulator's programmed protrusive condylar path, then stabilized with an elastic band 48 h; SOCs confined to the 6 anterior teeth.
  - **Left/right lateral**: same custom-incisal-table technique along the programmed lateral condylar path; SOCs confined to canine + first/second premolar on the working side (3 SOCs).
- **Test method**: Medit i700 IOS, multi-occlusion acquisition function, single operator (7 years' IOS experience). Full-arch scans, then 9 independently captured VIRs per position under a standardized 50 N static load on the articulator, each producing a separately re-articulated virtual cast pair saved as STL.
- **Comparison method**: Reference SOCs/sites of clearance (SCs) marked on screenshots via Photoshop; virtual SOCs/SCs computed in CloudCompare v2.7 as signed nearest-neighbor inter-arch distance, color-coded (green 0–100 µm = contact; blue <0 µm = mesh perforation; gray >100 µm = clearance). Each site scored (0/+/−) against the reference to build TP/TN/FP/FN, then:
  - Sensitivity = TP/(TP+FN); NPV = TN/(TN+FN); PPV = TP/(TP+FP).
  - **Note (source-data-issue, reproduced verbatim, not corrected)**: the Methods section states "specificity = TP/(TP+FP)" — textually identical to the PPV formula given in the same paragraph. The standard definition is specificity = TN/(TN+FP). This appears to be a typographical/copy-paste error in the published Methods; the Results/Discussion values are internally consistent with the standard definitions (e.g., lateral specificity 93% is distinct from lateral PPV 79%), so the error looks confined to the formula listing itself, not the reported numbers.
- **Clinically-acceptable threshold** (cited from prior literature, not derived in this study): sensitivity ≥70%, specificity ≥90%; PPV/NPV as close to 100% as possible.
- **Sample size**: G*Power, α=0.05, power=80%, effect size 0.92 (based on Arslan & Nemli) → minimum n=9 per group, met exactly (9 VIRs per position).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Position | Sensitivity | Specificity | PPV | NPV | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MIP | 100% | 99% | 99% | 100% | Excellent — exceeds acceptable minimums |
| Protrusive | 100% | 83% | 86% | 100% | Acceptable — meets minimums |
| Lateral (L & R) | **28%** | 93% | 79% | **56%** | **Below acceptable** — misses ~72% of true contacts |

- **Mesh perforation (virtual overlap, µm)**: MIP up to 148; protrusive up to 42; **lateral up to 455** — largest at the last molars, consistent with posterior tilting during best-fit articulation.
- Findings agree with Abdulateef (IOS VIRs acceptable but can miss contacts) and Arslan (fewer interocclusal reference points reduce IOS virtual-articulation accuracy) — no prior literature specifically on *dynamic* (border-position) IOS articulation accuracy was available for direct comparison.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **In vitro only**: 3D-printed casts on a mechanical articulator with locked/programmed condylar paths — no patient, no mandibular flexure, no muscle-driven closure, no saliva, no body posture/time-of-day variability (all cited by the authors as clinical accuracy confounders not simulated here).
- **Reference standard is itself imperfect**: articulating paper is liable to false positives/negatives from variable biting force and saliva in vivo; a static 50 N load was used here specifically to reduce (not eliminate) that limitation, and the paper marking was performed only once per relation (see [[occlusion/rovira-lastra-2026-articulating-paper-accuracy-occlusal-points]] for articulating paper's own ~81% sensitivity / 15% false-positive rate against a scanned-registration reference).
- **Single IOS system** (Medit i700) — accuracy of other scanners' multi-occlusion functions not established.
- **Single operator** for all scans/VIRs — inter-operator variability not assessed.
- Authors recommend further research before recommending IOS multi-occlusion for dynamic (border-position) virtual articulation generally, and comparison across IOS systems.

## 6. Related Work

- **Abdulateef** — VIRs with IOS have acceptable accuracy overall, though the IOS can miss occlusal contacts (matches this study's lateral finding).
- **Arslan** — decreased interocclusal reference points reduce VIR accuracy with IOS (mechanistic parallel to the 3-SOC lateral condition here).
- **Reich et al. / Wei et al. / Rovira-Lastra et al.** (cited in [[occlusion/didier-2026-occlusal-contacts-ios-vs-carbon-paper-concordance]]'s introduction) — broader digital-occlusion-registration reliability literature.

## 7. Glossary

- **IOS** — Intraoral scanner (Medit i700 here).
- **VIR** — Virtual interocclusal record; a scan of the interocclusal relationship used to best-fit-align maxillary and mandibular digital arches.
- **Multi-occlusion function** — the specific IOS acquisition-software feature under test: it captures MIP and mandibular border-position VIRs to enable virtual articulation without conventional mechanical mounting.
- **SOC / SC** — Site of occlusal contact / site of clearance.
- **Mesh perforation** — a virtual-articulation artifact where opposing digital arches overlap (signed distance <0 µm), caused by tilting during best-fit alignment; not a real anatomical event.
- **Sensitivity / Specificity / PPV / NPV** — here: sensitivity = fraction of true (articulating-paper) contacts the scanner also detected; specificity = fraction of true clearance sites the scanner correctly left as clearance; PPV = fraction of scanner-flagged contacts that were true contacts; NPV = fraction of scanner-flagged clearance sites that were true clearance.
