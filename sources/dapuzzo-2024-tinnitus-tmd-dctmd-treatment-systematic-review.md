## Why Ingested

Batch ingest of dental-tinnitus PMC literature to build `tmj` category coverage of the tinnitus-TMD relationship (mechanism, association, and treatment); the wiki previously held only one narrow cross-sectional paper, [[wiki/tmj/ma-2025-tmd-tinnitus-chewing-side-preference-correlation]], with no treatment-outcome evidence. This SR+MA is the highest-value addition — the only systematic review specifically evaluating RCT **treatment** outcomes (not just association) for tinnitus-TMD using DC/TMD diagnostic criteria.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42022314972) of RCTs on treatment approaches for tinnitus associated with TMD diagnosed via DC/TMD; from 635 initial records, 5 RCTs (329 participants, 4 European + 1 Turkish, published 2017–2020) were included.

Pooled meta-analysis found a significant positive treatment effect on tinnitus symptoms (odds ratio 0.26, 95% CI [0.19, 0.37]; risk ratio 0.49 [0.40, 0.59]; risk difference −0.31 [−0.38, −0.24]), with a multidisciplinary non-invasive approach (manual therapy + exercise + education) showing the strongest individual-study results.

Baseline tinnitus severity, gender, quality of life, age, and tinnitus duration emerged as predictive factors of treatment outcome, but the evidence base is limited by only 5 small RCTs, risk-of-bias concerns in more than half, and substantial heterogeneity in treatment modalities (I² up to 78% for the risk-difference pooled estimate).

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Treatment approaches, outcomes and prognostic indicators in patients with tinnitus and temporomandibular disorders evaluated with DC/TMD: A systematic review and Meta-analysis
- **Authors**: Fabrizia d'Apuzzo, Giuseppe Minervini, Martina Cerbone, Patricia Rotolo Rossana, Vincenzo Grassia, Ludovica Nucci
- **Journal**: Journal of Oral Rehabilitation, 2024
- **DOI**: 10.1111/joor.13796 | **PMID**: 39020483 | **PMCID**: PMC11740268
- **Study type**: Systematic review + meta-analysis of RCTs (PROSPERO CRD42022314972, registered April 2022; PRISMA 2020 flow)

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic review to specifically evaluate treatment effects and predictive factors for patients with tinnitus and TMD diagnosed using the (research) Diagnostic Criteria for TMD (DC/TMD or RDC/TMD).
- Pools 5 heterogeneous-but-comparable RCTs across three treatment paradigms — manual therapy + exercise + education, orofacial physical therapy (delayed-start design), and low-level laser therapy (LLLT) — into a single meta-analysis (OR, RR, RD).
- Consolidates cross-study predictive factors (baseline tinnitus severity, gender, quality of life, age, tinnitus duration) that clinicians can use to counsel patients on likely treatment response.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Protocol**: A priori protocol per Cochrane Handbook 5.1.0; PRISMA-compliant; PROSPERO CRD42022314972 (registered April 2022).
- **PICO**: Participants — patients with tinnitus and DC/TMD-diagnosed TMD; excluded animals, tinnitus of medical origin (infections, otosclerosis, Ménière's disease), other correlated syndromes, otologic/craniofacial cancers, prior maxillofacial surgery, incomplete records. Excluded study designs: cross-sectional surveys, case reports/series, reviews/meta-analyses, opinion pieces, editorials, protocols.
- **Databases**: Medline, Web of Science, LILACS, searched without date/language/type restriction up to 14 March 2023, plus manual reference search of earlier SRs.
- **Selection**: Two independent reviewers screened title/abstract then full text; disagreements settled by consensus.
- **Data extraction**: Two independent researchers via Excel sheet — authors, year, country, sample, gender, mean age, TMD diagnosis method, tinnitus diagnosis, intervention, control, outcomes/predictive factors.
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB 2 tool for randomised trials, by two reviewers with third-reviewer arbitration.
- **Statistics**: R 3.65; mean difference (MD) and SD computed; meta-analysis on measurable data across RCTs vs controls using odds ratio (OR), risk ratio (RR), and risk difference (RD), displayed as forest plots.
- **The 5 included RCTs**:
  1. **Delgado de la Serna et al. 2020** (Spain, n=61): manual therapy + exercise + education (n=31) vs exercise + education alone (n=30).
  2. **Plaza-Manzano et al. 2021** (Spain, n=56, same cohort design as #1): predictive-factor analysis of manual therapy + exercise + education vs exercise + education.
  3. **Van der Wal, Michiels et al. 2020** (Belgium, n=80): early-start vs delayed-start orofacial physical therapy (counselling, mouth-habit reversal, massage, stretching, relaxation, biofeedback) for somatosensory temporomandibular tinnitus (TFI 25–90).
  4. **Van der Wal, Van de Heyning et al. 2020** (Belgium, n=101): predictive-indicator analysis of the same early/delayed-start orofacial therapy design.
  5. **Demirkol et al. 2017** (Turkey, n=31): low-level laser therapy (LLLT) — Nd:YAG laser (n=15) vs 810 nm diode laser (n=16) applied to the external auditory meatus.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Study flow**: 635 records → 472 excluded pre-screening + 63 duplicates → 100 full texts assessed → 5 RCTs included, 329 total participants (175 intervention, 154 control), mean age 43.3 y. 4 RCTs from Europe (2 Spain, 2 Belgium), 1 from Turkey; all published 2017–2020.
- **Delgado de la Serna 2020**: manual therapy + exercise + education outperformed exercise + education alone on TMD pain (NPRS, F=10.639, p<.001, η²=0.153), tinnitus severity (VAS, F=17.878, p<.001, η²=0.233), THI (F=39.291, p<.001, η²=0.501), CF-PDI (F=18.096, p<.001, η²=0.395), BDI-II (F=14.234, p<.001, η²=0.194), mouth opening (F=17.683, p<.001), lateral excursions (F=18.594, p<.001), and PPTs at masseter/temporalis/TMJ (all p<.001); SF-12 quality of life showed no group difference (F=0.590, p=.622).
- **Plaza-Manzano 2021**: higher baseline tinnitus severity and tinnitus-related handicap predicted better outcomes at 3 and 6 months (explaining 12–42% of variance); higher baseline PPT hypersensitivity over temporalis predicted poorer outcomes (10–41% of variance).
- **Van der Wal, Michiels 2020**: 34% of patients showed clinically relevant TQ improvement (cutoff ≥8.72 points) immediately post-treatment, rising to 46% at follow-up; TFI clinically relevant improvement (cutoff ≥13 points) was 41% immediately and 61% at follow-up.
- **Van der Wal, Van de Heyning 2020**: predictive model for TQ change (duration of tinnitus + higher initial TQ somatic subscale score) correctly predicted outcome in 68.5% of patients; predictive model for TFI change (age, female gender, duration of tinnitus) predicted outcome in 68.1%. Individual predictors: female gender OR 2.70, higher TQ somatic subscale score OR 1.52, painful TMJ palpation OR 2.46, better speech-in-noise test score OR 0.88.
- **Demirkol 2017**: both laser types improved tinnitus severity from baseline to 1 month (Nd:YAG p=.001; diode p=.005); median percentage improvement in VAS tinnitus severity was 100% for Nd:YAG vs 30% for the 810 nm diode laser.
- **Meta-analysis (pooled across all 5 RCTs)**:
  - Odds ratio (OR) 0.26, 95% CI [0.19, 0.37]; χ²=6.24, df=4, p=.18; I²=36% (moderate heterogeneity); overall effect z=7.75, p<.00001.
  - Risk ratio (RR) 0.49, 95% CI [0.40, 0.59]; χ²=5.12, df=4, p=.28; I²=22% (low heterogeneity); overall effect z=7.53, p<.00001.
  - Risk difference (RD) −0.31, 95% CI [−0.38, −0.24]; χ²=18.34, df=4, p=.001; I²=78% (substantial heterogeneity); overall effect z=8.53, p<.00001.
- **Risk of bias**: only Demirkol et al. showed "some concerns" across all RoB domains; high risk of performance bias (blinding) in 2 RCTs; 80% of studies had low risk of bias from randomisation; only 40% had minimal risk of performance bias; 100% had complete outcome data reporting.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 5 RCTs met inclusion criteria — small evidence base limits the meta-analysis.
- Risk-of-bias concerns present in more than half of included studies (chiefly performance/blinding domain).
- Substantial heterogeneity in treatment modalities (manual therapy, orofacial physical therapy, LLLT) contributes to a high I² (78%) for the risk-difference pooled estimate, warranting cautious interpretation.
- BMI was not investigated as a predictive factor (prior literature shows only marginal correlation with tinnitus, and higher BMI is associated with less TMD pain).
- Authors call for larger multicentric prospective RCTs with longer follow-up, and future exploration of additional mediators/comorbidities and cost-effectiveness of interventions.

## 6. Related Work

- First SR+MA to investigate treatment effects specifically in patients diagnosed with tinnitus and TMD per the recent DC/TMD criteria.
- Discussion situates findings against prior literature on somatic/somatosensory tinnitus, cervical spine–TMD comorbidity, and non-TMD tinnitus risk factors (protective mask use, post-COVID-19 syndrome) with unclear evidence.
- Companion wiki page: [[wiki/tmj/ma-2025-tmd-tinnitus-chewing-side-preference-correlation]] (cross-sectional association study, chewing-side preference and tinnitus laterality).

## 7. Glossary

- **DC/TMD**: Diagnostic Criteria for Temporomandibular Disorders — standardised, internationally accepted diagnostic template for TMD.
- **TMD**: Temporomandibular Disorders — conditions affecting the TMJ and/or masticatory muscles.
- **THI**: Tinnitus Handicap Inventory — self-report questionnaire assessing tinnitus-related disability.
- **TQ / TFI**: Tinnitus Questionnaire / Tinnitus Functional Index — outcome measures of tinnitus severity and functional impact.
- **VAS**: Visual Analogue Scale.
- **NPRS**: Numeric Pain Rating Scale.
- **CF-PDI**: Craniofacial Pain and Disability Inventory.
- **PPT**: Pressure Pain Threshold.
- **LLLT**: Low-Level Laser Therapy.
- **Somatosensory/somatic tinnitus**: tinnitus modulated by somatic movements (e.g., jaw clenching, neck muscle tension), thought to arise via convergence of trigeminal/cervical and auditory pathways.
- **RoB 2**: Cochrane Risk of Bias tool version 2, for randomised trials.
