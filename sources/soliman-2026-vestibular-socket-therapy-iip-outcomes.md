## One-line Summary

First SR+MA (7 RCTs, 186 participants) of VST for IIP in compromised sockets: 99.5% implant survival, favorable soft tissue outcomes, but no significant buccal bone plate thickness advantage over comparators (GRADE: very low certainty for all bone outcomes).

## 한줄요약

VST(전정접근 소켓 치료)를 이용한 즉시식립에 대한 첫 SR+MA(RCT 7편, 186명): 임플란트 생존율 99.5%·연조직 양호하나, 비교군 대비 협측골판 두께 유의차 없음(GRADE 근거수준 매우 낮음).

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR+MA exclusively evaluating VST with IIP
- 7 RCTs, 186 participants; implant survival 99.5% (185/186); no shield exposure, peri-implantitis, or dehiscence
- Meta-analysis: VST vs comparators shows no significant difference in buccal bone plate thickness at any level (crestal +0.24mm, mid-root +0.32mm, apical +0.19mm; all NS; I²=45–82%)
- GRADE: very low certainty for all three bone-thickness outcomes (downgraded for risk of bias, imprecision, publication bias, inconsistency)
- All 7 trials from one investigator network in Egypt — geographic and operator generalizability unconfirmed
- VST demonstrates minimal mid-facial recession and stable papillae (PES 10.29–13.17)
- Crestal cortical membrane barrier (not graft volume) identified as likely critical determinant of crestal bone preservation

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: SR+MA per PRISMA 2020
- PICO: Adults with compromised fresh extraction sockets; VST with IIP; vs established techniques (early placement + contour augmentation, PET/socket shield, dual-zone); buccal bone plate thickness (primary), implant survival, MML, papilla, PES
- Eligible: RCTs, 2020–January 2026 (VST first described 2020)
- Exclusion: non-RCTs, VST not per original Elaskary protocol
- RoB: Cochrane RoB 2; certainty: GRADE
- Statistics: random-effects (DerSimonian–Laird); MD with 95% CI; meta-analysis only for VST vs distinct alternative (3 trials); 4 trials comparing within-VST variations reported narratively only

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Screening: 312 records → 7 RCTs included
- RoB: 6/7 "some concerns"; 1/7 high risk
- **Buccal bone plate thickness (meta-analysis of 3 VST-vs-alternative trials):**
  - Crestal: MD +0.24mm (95% CI −0.13 to 0.61; I²=45%) — NS
  - Mid-root: MD +0.32mm (95% CI −0.45 to 1.09; I²=82%) — NS
  - Apical: MD +0.19mm (95% CI −0.48 to 0.87; I²=67%) — NS
- **Implant survival**: 99.5% (185/186 across all 7 trials)
- **MML**: Less mid-facial recession with VST vs early placement + contour augmentation (−0.53mm vs −1.87mm, Δ1.34mm, p<0.001 in one trial)
- **PES**: 10.29–13.17 across all arms; 91.6% achieved PES >10 (Hamed 2023)
- **VST vs PET**: equivalent buccal bone, PES, MML at 6 months (one trial)
- **Bone shielding vs dual-zone**: significantly greater crestal and mid-root bone thickness with bone shielding (Elaskary 2024)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- All 7 RCTs from one closely related investigator network (Egypt) — Elaskary VST originator co-authored this review
- One trial per comparator type — subgroup meta-analysis not feasible
- Follow-up max 12 months; longer-term stability unconfirmed
- GRADE: very low for all bone-thickness outcomes
- Only 2 databases searched (PubMed + EMBASE); no sensitivity analysis
- Baseline anatomical variables (KMW, gingival phenotype, jumping-gap dimension) inconsistently reported
- 186 participants total — underpowered for mid-root and apical level analyses

## 6. Related Work

- Elaskary et al. 2020 — VST original description
- PET/socket shield SR+MA (Wu 2022) — PES ~12.27 comparable
- IIP in compromised sockets SR (Amid 2023) — 99.5% survival consistent
- Buccal plate preservation: dual-zone, GBR, socket shield literature

## 7. Glossary

- **VST (Vestibular Socket Therapy)**: minimally invasive IIP technique using vestibular tunnel access + flexible cortical xenogeneic membrane to reconstruct deficient buccal plate without crestal incision
- **Type II socket**: partial/complete loss of facial bone plate beneath intact overlying soft tissue
- **PET (Partial Extraction Therapy / Socket Shield)**: retention of buccal root fragment to preserve bundle bone and PDL
- **Dual-zone concept**: simultaneous grafting of implant gap (zone 1) and subgingival emergence profile (zone 2)
- **PEEK**: polyether ether ketone (customized healing abutments used in VST trials)
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score)**: peri-implant soft tissue esthetic index (0–14 max)
