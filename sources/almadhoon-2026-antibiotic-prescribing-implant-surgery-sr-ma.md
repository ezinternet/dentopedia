## One-line Summary
SR+MA (n=39 studies, 7266 dentists, 137,207 patients); pooled routine antibiotic prescribing prevalence 61.3% (95%CI 53.4–68.8%); guideline concordance 1–28%; post-operative regimens predominate despite no benefit over single pre-operative dose.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (39편, 치과의사 7266명, 환자 137,207명); 임플란트 수술 일상 항생제 처방 유병률 61.3%; 가이드라인 준수율 1–28%; 술후 요법이 주류이나 단회 술전 투여 대비 이득 없음.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA, 39 studies (7266 dentists, 137,207 patients), MEDLINE/Scopus/WoS January 2010–October 2025 — global antibiotic prescribing patterns and guideline adherence in dental implant surgery.

Pooled routine antibiotic prescribing prevalence was 61.3% (I²=96.8%); guideline concordance ranged only 1–28%; post-operative 5–7-day courses predominated (52.6%) despite no superiority over single pre-operative dose; amoxicillin was most prescribed (50.9%).

Declining guideline concordance and extreme heterogeneity highlight a global stewardship failure; GRADE certainty was very low, and no RCT evidence exists for procedure-specific stewardship interventions.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA, 39편 (치과의사 7,266명, 환자 137,207명), 2010–2025 MEDLINE/Scopus/WoS — 임플란트 수술 항생제 처방 패턴 및 가이드라인 준수도 글로벌 분석.

일상적 항생제 처방 유병률 61.3%; 가이드라인 준수율 단 1–28%; 술후 5–7일 처방이 주류(52.6%)이나 단회 술전 투여 대비 이득 없음; 아목시실린 가장 많이 처방(50.9%).

가이드라인 준수율이 시간이 갈수록 감소하는 추세로 스튜어드십 실패를 시사; GRADE 근거 수준 매우 낮음; 수술 특이적 중재에 대한 RCT 부재.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First global SR+MA on antibiotic prescribing specifically in dental implant surgery
- Pooled prescribing prevalence 61.3% — wide range 24.9–88.8% (extreme heterogeneity I²=96.8%)
- Guideline concordance shockingly low: only 1–28% across studies
- Post-operative 5–7-day courses are predominant (52.6% combined pre+post) despite no superiority over single pre-op dose
- Amoxicillin is most prescribed (50.9%)
- Prescribing escalates with case complexity — 97.3% in "compromised cases"
- Temporal trend: guideline concordance is DECLINING — stewardship efforts not translating

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: SR + meta-analysis
- Inclusion: dentist surveys, patient-record studies, register-based studies
- Search: January 2010 – October 2025; MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science
- n=39 studies; 7266 dentists; 137,207 patients
- Pooling: Freeman-Tukey transformation + random-effects meta-analysis
- Regimen and appropriateness: narrative synthesis
- Bias: JBI tool; certainty: GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Pooled antibiotic prescribing prevalence: 61.3% (95%CI 53.4–68.8%; I²=96.8%)
  - Range: 24.9% (lowest) to 88.8% (highest)
- Most common regimen: combined pre+post-operative (52.6%)
  - Post-op duration: typically 5–7 days
- Most prescribed agent: amoxicillin (50.9%)
- Guideline concordance: 1–28% across studies (very low; declining trend)
- Compromised cases: up to 97.3% prescribing
- Evidence certainty (GRADE): very low overall

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Extreme heterogeneity (I²=96.8%) limits pooled estimate interpretation
- GRADE certainty: very low — data quality insufficient for definitive recommendations
- No RCT evidence on procedure-specific stewardship interventions
- Authors call for procedure-specific international guidelines and targeted stewardship programs
