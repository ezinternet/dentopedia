## Why Ingested

This is a high-quality Bayesian network meta-analysis comparing the therapeutic efficacy of 10 different mouthwashes for chemoradiotherapy-induced oral mucositis. It provides the first comprehensive hierarchy of treatment options, with robust statistical ranking that can inform clinical guidelines. Its systematic methodology and direct relevance to oral medicine and oncology supportive care make it a high-value addition.

## Three-line Summary

(1) This was a systematic review and Bayesian network meta-analysis of 13 randomized controlled trials (570 patients) comparing 10 mouthwash types for treating established oral mucositis in cancer patients receiving radiotherapy or chemotherapy.

(2) After one week, GM-CSF mouthwash was ranked most effective (54% probability), with povidone-iodine being superior to chlorhexidine (WMD, 2.63; 95% CI, 0.20-5.01). After three weeks, natural drugs (43% probability) and vitamin E (24%) were ranked highest; vitamin E (WMD, -0.94; 95% CI, -1.03 to -0.85), natural drugs (WMD, -0.93; 95% CI, -1.46 to -0.40), and phenytoin (WMD, -0.38; 95% CI, -0.59 to -0.17) all outperformed placebo.

(3) The authors conclude that GM-CSF is the most effective overall, but povidone-iodine and sodium bicarbonate offer better cost-effectiveness, while natural drugs provide a safe, well-tolerated alternative with delayed but sustained benefit, suitable for long-course chemoradiotherapy.

(2) 1주차 치료에서는 과립구-대식세포 집락자극인자 (GM-CSF) 가글액이 가장 효과적이었고(54% 확률), 포비돈-요오드는 클로르헥시딘보다 우수했다 (WMD, 2.63; 95% CI, 0.20-5.01). 3주차에서는 천연물 가글액(43%)과 비타민 E(24%)가 가장 높은 순위를 보였으며, 비타민 E (WMD, -0.94; 95% CI, -1.03 to -0.85), 천연물 (WMD, -0.93; 95% CI, -1.46 to -0.40), 페니토인 (WMD, -0.38; 95% CI, -0.59 to -0.17)이 위약보다 우수했다.

(3) 저자는 GM-CSF가 가장 효과적이지만, 비용 대비 효과를 고려하면 포비돈-요오드와 중탄산나트륨이 유리하며, 천연물 가글액은 안전성과 내약성이 뛰어나 장기 항암방사선치료 환자에서 유용한 대안이 될 수 있다고 결론지었다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First Bayesian network meta-analysis to rank 10 mouthwashes for treating established (not preventing) chemoradiotherapy-induced oral mucositis.
- GM-CSF mouthwash ranked best at 1 week (54% probability), with superior efficacy over chlorhexidine (WMD, 2.63; 95% CI, 0.20-5.01).
- Povidone-iodine was significantly better than chlorhexidine after 1 week (WMD, -2.64; 95% CI, -2.72 to -2.56), but inferior to GM-CSF (WMD, 0.20; 95% CI, 0.06-0.34).
- Natural drugs showed time-dependent efficacy, ranked best at 3 weeks (43% probability), outperforming placebo (WMD, -0.93; 95% CI, -1.46 to -0.40).
- Gabapentin mouthwash performed no better than placebo and had the lowest rank probability, likely due to limited local effect and potential cytotoxicity.
- Sodium bicarbonate ranked highest at 2 weeks, possibly due to its mucolytic and pH-neutralizing properties.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design:** Systematic review and Bayesian network meta-analysis. PRISMA guidelines followed.
- **Search:** PubMed, Embase, Cochrane Library, Web of Science from inception to April 21, 2022. Language restricted to English.
- **Inclusion criteria:** RCTs; patients with radiotherapy/chemotherapy-induced OM; comparison of ≥2 mouthwash types; OM severity reported via WHO grades; follow-up 7–28 days.
- **Exclusion criteria:** Prophylactic studies; insufficient data; retrospective studies; conference abstracts; reviews; letters.
- **Risk of bias:** Assessed via Cochrane Handbook ROB tool (random sequence generation, allocation concealment, blinding, incomplete data, selective reporting, other biases). Results presented in Figure 4.
- **Data synthesis:** Pairwise meta-analyses with random-effects model (WMD, 95% CI, I²). Bayesian network meta-analysis using Markov chain Monte Carlo (ADDIS v1.15), 100,000 iterations, 20,000 tuning, thinning interval 10.
- **Ranking:** Rank probabilities calculated from the proportion of Markov chain iterations where each intervention had the highest WMD vs. a common control.
- **Inconsistency:** Evaluated by comparing consistency and inconsistency models in ADDIS; no significant inconsistency was found.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Primary outcome —** OM score improvement (WHO grade reduction): pairwise and network meta-analysis WMDs.
- **1 week — GM-CSF vs. povidone-iodine:** WMD, 0.20; 95% CI, 0.06-0.34 (favors GM-CSF).
- **1 week — Povidone-iodine vs. chlorhexidine:** WMD, -2.64; 95% CI, -2.72 to -2.56 (favors povidone-iodine).
- **1 week — Vitamin E vs. placebo:** WMD, -0.76; 95% CI, -0.92 to -0.60 (favors vitamin E).
- **3 weeks — Vitamin E vs. placebo:** WMD, -0.94; 95% CI, -1.03 to -0.85.
- **3 weeks — Natural drugs vs. placebo:** WMD, -0.93; 95% CI, -1.46 to -0.40.
- **3 weeks — Phenytoin vs. placebo:** WMD, -0.38; 95% CI, -0.59 to -0.17.
- **Network meta-analysis 1 week — Povidone-iodine vs. chlorhexidine:** WMD, 2.63; 95% CI, 0.20-5.01 (favors povidone-iodine).
- **Rank probability (1 week):** GM-CSF 54%, Povidone-iodine 24%, Vitamin E 14%.
- **Rank probability (2 weeks):** Sodium bicarbonate 24%, Natural drugs 21%.
- **Rank probability (3 weeks):** Natural drugs 43%, Vitamin E 24%.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Sample sizes** were relatively small across the 13 included RCTs, and study quality varied, increasing potential bias.
- **Heterogeneity** from different cancer types and chemoradiotherapy protocols could not be explored due to insufficient studies for subgroup analyses.
- **Incomplete network connectivity:** Not all nodes (mouthwash types) were directly compared, limiting the comprehensiveness of indirect estimates.
- **Follow-up periods** varied across studies and were categorized into 1-, 2-, and 3-week subgroups, which may have introduced misclassification bias.
- **Limited generalizability** of GM-CSF recommendation for leukemia patients due to its hematopoietic effects.
- Future studies should conduct large-scale, head-to-head RCTs, especially comparing sodium bicarbonate, GM-CSF, and povidone-iodine at 2 weeks, and should evaluate long-term safety and cost-effectiveness.

## 7. Glossary

- **OM (Oral Mucositis)**: 구강점막염; 방사선·항암치료 후 발생하는 구강 점막의 염증성 궤양.
- **GM-CSF (Granulocyte-Macrophage Colony-Stimulating Factor)**: 과립구-대식세포 집락자극인자; 골수 조혈을 자극하고 염증 조절에 관여하는 사이토카인.
- **WHO Grade (WHO grading system)**: 세계보건기구 구강점막염 중증도 분류 체계(1~4단계).
- **WMD (Weighted Mean Difference)**: 가중평균차이; 치료 효과 비교에 사용된 연속형 변수의 효과 크기.
- **Bayesian Network Meta-Analysis**: 베이지안 네트워크 메타분석; 직접 및 간접 비교를 통합하여 다중 중재법을 동시에 비교하는 통계 기법.
- **Natural drugs (in this study)**: 천연물 가글액; Achillea, Althaea, Plantago major, Curcumin, Royal jelly 추출물을 포함.
- **Povidone-iodine**: 포비돈-요오드; 광범위 외용 소독제로 구강점막염 치료에 사용.
