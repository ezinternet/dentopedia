## Why Ingested

The wiki's smoking–implant evidence rested on single-outcome SR+MAs — [[implants/naseri-2020-smoking-levels-implant-failure-sr-ma]] (dose-response failure) and [[implants/fan-2024-smoking-early-implant-failure-sr-ma]] (early failure) — with no pooled estimate carrying survival, crestal bone loss, and peri-implantitis together. This 45-article SR+MA supplies that combined anchor for [[overviews/smoking-tobacco-periodontal-implant-overview]], and is the first to scope smoke-free tobacco and e-cigarettes as separate implant exposures (finding that evidence still insufficient).

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42024628116, 45 articles / 44 studies, ≥1 year post-loading follow-up) quantifying implant survival, crestal bone loss (CBL) and peri-implantitis incidence in tobacco smokers versus non-smokers, and separately scoping evidence on smoke-free (smokeless) tobacco and e-cigarette users.

Cigarette smoking significantly reduces implant survival at both implant level (OR 0.40, 95% CI 0.27-0.61) and patient level (OR 0.43, 95% CI 0.20-0.90), and increases CBL by 0.64 mm (95% CI 0.29-0.99); most studies also report higher peri-implantitis incidence in smokers. Evidence for smoke-free tobacco products and vaping is insufficient to draw firm conclusions.

Clinically, smoking is not an absolute contraindication but is a well-quantified modifiable risk factor; behavioural smoking-cessation counselling should be integrated into every implant treatment plan, and this SR+MA is now the largest, most recent quantitative anchor for informed consent (updates/supersedes single-outcome estimates in Naseri 2020 and Fan 2024 with a broader survival + CBL + peri-implantitis picture).

## 세줄요약

체계적 고찰+메타분석(PROSPERO 등록, 논문 45편/연구 44건, 부하 후 최소 1년 추적) — 흡연자 대 비흡연자의 임플란트 생존율, 변연골 소실(CBL), 임플란트주위염 발생률을 정량화하고, 무연 담배·전자담배 근거는 별도로 정리.

흡연은 임플란트 단위(OR 0.40, 95% CI 0.27–0.61)와 환자 단위(OR 0.43, 95% CI 0.20–0.90) 모두에서 생존율을 유의하게 낮추고, CBL을 0.64mm 증가시키며(95% CI 0.29–0.99), 대부분 연구에서 임플란트주위염 발생률도 높음. 무연 담배·전자담배 근거는 아직 불충분.

흡연이 임플란트의 절대적 금기는 아니지만 정량화된 조정 가능 위험요인이므로, 모든 임플란트 치료계획에 금연 상담을 포함해야 함 — 현재까지 가장 크고 최신의 생존율+CBL+임플란트주위염 종합 근거.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Largest and most recent SR+MA (45 articles/44 studies) combining implant survival, CBL, and peri-implantitis incidence for smoking, rather than a single outcome.
- First systematic attempt to scope smoke-free tobacco products and e-cigarettes as separate implant risk exposures (though data remain insufficient).
- PROSPERO-registered, Cochrane Handbook-aligned protocol with a March 2025 search update.

## 3. Methodology
Three databases searched (MEDLINE/OVID, EMBASE, Cochrane CENTRAL) to May 2024, updated March 2025. Studies reporting implant failure/survival in tobacco smokers or smoke-free product users vs non-smokers with ≥1 year post-loading follow-up (1 to 17 years across studies) were included; CBL and biological complications also extracted. Random-effects (DerSimonian-Laird) meta-analysis of OR for survival, α = 0.01.

## 4. Key Results
[근거강함] 41 of 44 studies addressed cigarette smoking specifically. Implant-level survival OR 0.40 (95% CI 0.27-0.61, p<0.001); patient-level survival OR 0.43 (95% CI 0.20-0.90, p=0.02); CBL +0.64 mm (95% CI 0.29-0.99, p<0.001) in smokers vs non-smokers. Majority of studies report higher peri-implantitis incidence in smokers. GRADE quality of evidence: low. Data on smoke-free tobacco and e-cigarette users insufficient for pooled analysis.

## 5. Limitations
- All included studies observational; GRADE-rated low quality of evidence.
- High heterogeneity in population, implant surface, loading protocol, and definition of "smoker" (from ≥1 cigarette/day to severe smokers only).
- Smoking status self-reported in all studies (no biomarker validation); literature suggests ~1 in 10 smokers under-report status.
- Comparator groups inconsistently defined (never-smokers vs currently-non-smoking vs unclear), and former smokers often pooled with never-smokers, which can dilute effect estimates given dose/duration-dependent recovery.
- Most studies used implant-based rather than patient-based denominators, risking non-independence of implants within patients.
- Smoke-free product and e-cigarette evidence base too sparse to meta-analyze — a gap, not a null finding.
