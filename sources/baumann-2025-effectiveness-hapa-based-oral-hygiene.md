## Why Ingested
Adds the first RCT in this wiki testing a health-behavior-theory (HAPA) oral hygiene instruction against standard instruction specifically in patients aged 65 and older, with interdental-brush use as the target behavior. It extends [[wiki/behavioral-dentistry/motivational-interviewing/vilar-doceda-2023-behavioral-interventions-periodontitis-patients-improve]] (behavioral interventions for oral hygiene in periodontitis patients) to an older population, and complements [[wiki/interdental-cleaning/yilmaz-2025-interdental-cleaning-tool-sustainability-rct]] (adherence to interdental cleaning tools) and [[wiki/periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/choi-2017-dental-plaque-control-oral-hygiene-education-korea-sr]] (oral hygiene education effect).

## Three-line Summary
RCT in 62 analyzed community-dwelling adults aged 65-87 (30 HAPA-based vs 32 standard instruction; 67 randomized, 7.5% drop-out) at one German university dental clinic, testing stage-tailored (Preintender/Intender/Actor) interdental-brush instruction under the Health Action Process Approach (HAPA), with a 12-week follow-up.
Gingival Bleeding Index (GBI) fell in both groups with no between-group difference (F(1,57)=0.15, p=.70); Plaque Control Record (PCR) fell only with HAPA (62.27 to 57.00%; control 59.47 to 60.69%; between-group p=.026, eta2=0.083), and self-reported interdental brush use rose from 4.30 to 9.93 times/week (eta2=0.61; control not measured).
Adding stage-tailored planning to standard interdental-brush instruction may help older adults' plaque control and interdental habits, but limits are a small sample, self-reported behavior in one arm only, no participant blinding, 12-week follow-up, and a motivated university-clinic sample.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health 2025;25(1):1209 (published 2025-07-19)
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s12903-025-06615-w
- **Institution**: Department of Operative Dentistry, Carolinum Dental University-Institute gGmbH, J.W. Goethe University, Frankfurt/Main, Germany (with Furtwangen University and University of Konstanz, health psychology)
- **Registration**: DRKS00030040 (registered 22.03.2023, i.e. after recruitment in Sept-Oct 2022)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Tests HAPA-based, stage-tailored oral hygiene instruction (questionnaire-classified Preintender / Intender / Actor) against standardized instruction in adults aged 65 and older.
- Reports a PCR advantage for HAPA at 12 weeks (between-group F(1,57)=5.19, p=.026, eta2=0.083) but no GBI advantage; both groups improved GBI.
- Documents behavior-theory mediators in the HAPA arm: action self-efficacy, behavioral control, outcome expectancy, and planning rose; intention and risk perception did not.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Two-arm parallel RCT, 1:1 simple randomization in R, sealed envelopes; follow-up examiners blinded, participants and baseline instructors not blinded.
- **Population**: Age 65+, at least 10 remaining interproximal spaces; excluded if professional cleaning in prior 6 months or prior periodontal-department treatment. 67 randomized (33/34), 62 analyzed (30 HAPA / 32 control); age 65-87, mean 71.3 (SD 5.3).
- **Intervention**: About 65-min session for both groups incl. individually fitted interdental brushes (ISO size record) and professional cleaning. HAPA group additionally completed a 15-min questionnaire (score 0-69: Preintender 0-22, Intender 23-46, Actor 47-69), received stage-tailored emphasis, and wrote a personal goal after 15 min of instruction.
- **Outcomes**: PCR and GBI (six sites per tooth) at baseline and 12 weeks (mean interval 12.29 weeks); HAPA-arm questionnaire measures incl. self-reported interdental brush use per week.
- **Sample size**: 64 planned (52 needed to detect 20% difference, SD 25%, power 80%, plus 20% drop-out).
- **Analysis**: ANCOVA on follow-up controlling for baseline and age; repeated-measures ANOVA and MANCOVA within HAPA arm; Bonferroni correction.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | HAPA group (n=30) | Control (n=32) | Between-group |
|---|---|---|---|
| GBI % baseline to 12 wk | 9.5 to 5.70 (d=0.71, p<.001) | 11.34 to 5.56 (d=0.65, p<.001) | F(1,57)=0.15, p=.70, eta2=0.003 |
| PCR % baseline to 12 wk | 62.27 to 57.00 (d=0.42, p=.030) | 59.47 to 60.69 (d=-0.08, p=.319) | F(1,57)=5.19, p=.026, eta2=0.083 |

- Interdental brush use per week (HAPA arm, self-reported): 4.30 to 9.93 (change 5.63, F(1,29)=44.46, p<.001, eta2=0.61). Control-arm brush use and brushing frequency were not collected.
- Brushing frequency per week: 12.8 to 13.1 (p=.482).
- HAPA measures: action self-efficacy 2.73 to 3.51 (eta2=0.43); behavioral control 2.51 to 3.57 (eta2=0.58); outcome expectancy 2.67 to 3.14 (eta2=0.37); action planning eta2=0.32; coping self-efficacy eta2=0.28; coping planning eta2=0.17; intention (p=.340) and risk perception (p=.168) not significant.
- Stage shift: Preintender 3 to 0, Intender 14 to 10, Actor 13 to 20 (+53.8%), chi2(2)=7.10, p=.019; seven of 14 Intenders became Actors.
- Across stages at follow-up (MANCOVA): no difference in GBI, PCR or brushing frequency; Actors' interdental brush use tendency p=.055.
- Baseline GBI 9.5-11.3% and PCR 59-62% were similar between groups (all p>.30). No adverse events.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Modest sample; stage subgroups tiny (Preintender n=3), so stage-level analyses are underpowered.
- Only 12-week follow-up; sustainability of habit change unknown.
- Behavior outcomes self-reported and collected only in the HAPA arm, so the interdental-brush increase has no control comparison and may include recall/expectation bias.
- Participants and baseline instructors not blinded; registration date (22.03.2023) is after recruitment (Sept-Oct 2022).
- Between-group PCR difference is modest (57.00 vs 60.69% at follow-up); the control group's PCR rose slightly while the HAPA group's fell.
- No socioeconomic or cognitive assessment; motivated university-clinic sample (mean 22.5 interproximal spaces, few Preintenders) limits generalizability.
- Authors propose objective usage tracking, constant-force probing, longer follow-up and simplified routine-practice protocols.

## 7. Glossary
- **HAPA (Health Action Process Approach)**: Schwarzer's model separating a motivational phase (risk perception, outcome expectancy, self-efficacy to intention) from a volitional phase (action/coping planning, recovery self-efficacy).
- **Preintender / Intender / Actor**: HAPA stages: no intention yet / intends but has not acted / already acting.
- **PCR (Plaque Control Record)**: dichotomous percentage of plaque-positive surfaces after disclosing.
- **GBI (Gingival Bleeding Index)**: percentage of sites bleeding on probing after 10 s.
- **Intention-behavior gap**: intentions do not reliably translate into action; planning is proposed to bridge it.
