## Summary

ASRA Third Practice Advisory on LAST (interim update from 2010; Reg Anesth Pain Med 2018) summarizing updated mechanisms of lipid resuscitation, epidemiology, and clinical recommendations for prevention, recognition, and treatment of LAST.

New mechanisms identified: rapid partitioning (lipid sink), direct inotropy, post-conditioning. Trend toward delayed LAST presentation reflecting increased ultrasound guidance use, local infiltration techniques, and continuous LA infusions. Small patient size and sarcopenia identified as additional risk factors. Increasing frequency of LAST outside traditional hospital settings and in non-anesthesiologists.

## Key Updates from 2010 Advisory

- **ILE mechanisms (updated)**: rapid partitioning + direct inotropy + post-conditioning
- **Epidemiology**: lower reported frequency at single institutions and registries — but still considerable in general community
- **Delayed presentation trend**: mirrors ultrasound guidance adoption (fewer intravascular injections), local infiltration (slower uptake), continuous infusions
- **New risk factors**: small patient size, sarcopenia
- **Setting shift**: increasing LAST outside hospitals; increasing involvement of non-anesthesiologists
- **Liposomal bupivacaine**: limited data at time of advisory
