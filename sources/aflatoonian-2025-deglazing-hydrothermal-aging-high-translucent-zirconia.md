## Why Ingested

Addresses clinical scenario in [[dental-materials/zirconia/koenig-2021-ltd-monolithic-zirconia-prospective]]: what happens when a glazed monolithic zirconia crown is abraded (deglazed) intraorally? This RCT-protocol in-vitro study directly tests glazed vs deglazed vs never-glazed HT 3Y-PSZ after combined aging.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (40 HT zirconia discs, 3Y-PSZ Upcera, 4 groups: G, G-A, DG-A, NG-A), combined aging (hydrothermal 134°C/x→pH cycling→load cycling): deglazed (1338.22 MPa) and non-glazed (1320.44 MPa) groups had significantly higher flexural strength than glazed (1092.78 MPa) and glazed-aged (1141.11 MPa).

Glazing did NOT significantly change flexural strength after aging (G=1092.78 vs G-A=1141.11 MPa, P=0.872); aging also did NOT significantly reduce FS in glazed specimens.

XRD revealed monoclinic phase in DG-A (4.23%) and NG-A (7.63%), absent in G and G-A — confirming glaze layer blocks surface LTD but glaze removal does not weaken the ceramic.

## 세줄요약

HT 3Y-PSZ (Upcera) 40개 디스크, 4군: G(글레이징), G-A(글레이징+에이징), DG-A(탈글레이징+에이징), NG-A(비글레이징+에이징), 복합 에이징(수열+pH+부하): 탈글레이징군(1338.22 MPa) 및 비글레이징군(1320.44 MPa)이 글레이징군(1092.78 MPa)보다 굴곡강도 유의하게 높음(P<0.005).

글레이징 후 에이징해도 굴곡강도 유의한 변화 없음(P=0.872); 글레이징이 표면 LTD는 차단하나(단사정 0%) 기계적 강도를 향상시키지 않음.

XRD: DG-A 4.23%, NG-A 7.63% 단사정 — 글레이즈 제거 후 소량의 t→m 발생하나 굴곡강도에 영향 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that **deglazed (worn) zirconia does NOT weaken** compared to glazed counterparts — clinical implication: intraoral glaze wear is not a strength concern for HT 3Y-PSZ
- Confirms glazing reduces baseline flexural strength relative to polished/non-glazed surfaces (consistent with Sarabi 2024)
- Shows glaze layer blocks surface LTD (DG-A: 4.23% monoclinic; G-A: 0%) but glaze removal reveals transformation toughening
- Multi-modal aging protocol (hydrothermal + pH + load cycling) more clinically representative than single-step aging

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- 40 discs (12mm diameter, 1.2mm), milled, sintered, all initially glazed
- Groups: G (glaze only), G-A (glaze + aging), DG-A (remove glaze + aging), NG-A (never glazed + aging)
- Combined aging sequence: hydrothermal (134°C) → pH cycling → load cycling
- Biaxial flexural strength (ISO 6872, piston-on-3-balls setup)
- XRD for t→m phase quantification
- 1-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD (α=0.05); Kolmogorov-Smirnov normality check

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | Flexural Strength (MPa) | Monoclinic Phase |
|---|---|---|
| G (glaze) | 1092.78 ± 175.40 | ~0% |
| G-A (glaze + aged) | 1141.11 ± 117.43 | ~0% |
| DG-A (deglaze + aged) | 1338.22 ± 175.13 | 4.23% |
| NG-A (no glaze + aged) | 1320.44 ± 77.44 | 7.63% |

- DG-A and NG-A significantly higher FS than G and G-A (P<0.005)
- G vs G-A: P=0.872 (non-significant) — aging does not reduce glazed FS
- Monoclinic in DG-A and NG-A ≥4% confirms surface transformation but insufficient to reduce strength

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single brand of 3Y-PSZ (Upcera HT); results may differ for other compositions
- No clinical follow-up
- Deglazing method standardization could be improved

## 7. Glossary

- **Deglazing (탈글레이징)**: 소성된 글레이즈 레이어가 구강 내 마모로 제거되는 현상
- **HT zirconia (고투명 지르코니아)**: 3Y-PSZ 계열, 높은 반투명도 위해 큐빅상 증가
- **Combined aging protocol**: 수열+pH+기계적 부하를 순차 적용해 구강 내 복합 열화 모사
