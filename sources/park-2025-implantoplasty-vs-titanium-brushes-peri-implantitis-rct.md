## Why Ingested

First head-to-head RCT directly comparing rotating titanium brush vs implantoplasty for implant surface decontamination in surgical peri-implantitis therapy — fills an explicit gap flagged in the EFP 2023 guideline [[implants/peri-implantitis/herrera-2023-prevention-treatment-peri-implant-efp]] that evidence for implantoplasty was still insufficient. Korean single-center trial with 12-month follow-up providing marginal bone level data unavailable in prior observational series.

## Three-line Summary

Non-inferiority RCT (n=30 patients; Yonsei University Dental Hospital, Seoul; Aug 2021–Jan 2023) comparing rotating titanium brush (test) vs implantoplasty (control) for implant surface decontamination in surgical peri-implantitis therapy with 12-month follow-up.

Titanium brush was non-inferior for PPD reduction (−3.6 vs −3.3 mm); marginal bone level remained stable with titanium brush (0.0±0.6 mm) whereas implantoplasty group showed additional bone loss (0.7±1.2 mm; p=0.03); 2 implants lost in the implantoplasty group vs 0.

Titanium brush cut surface decontamination time by ~45% (3 min 1 s vs 5 min 27 s; p=0.006); baseline PPD and adjacent peri-implantitis implant predicted pocket closure failure; OHIP-14 scores were similar between groups.

## 세줄요약

비열등성 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=30명; 연세대학교 치과대학병원, 서울; 2021.8–2023.1): 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 수술 시 회전 티타늄 브러시 (Rotating Titanium Brush; 시험군) vs 임플란토플라스티 (Implantoplasty; 대조군) 비교, 12개월 추적관찰.

티타늄 브러시는 탐침깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD) 감소에서 비열등 (−3.6 vs −3.3 mm); 치조골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 변화: 티타늄 브러시 0.0 mm (안정) vs 임플란토플라스티 +0.7 mm 추가 소실 (p=0.03); 임플란트 소실 임플란토플라스티 군 2개, 티타늄 브러시 군 0개.

오염 제거 시간 ~45% 단축 (3분 1초 vs 5분 27초; p=0.006); 포켓 폐쇄 실패 예측 인자: 기저 탐침깊이 (p=0.044), 인접 임플란트주위염 (p=0.033); 구강건강영향지수 (Oral Health Impact Profile, OHIP-14)는 양군 차이 없음.

## Key Contributions

- First RCT directly comparing rotating titanium brush vs implantoplasty for peri-implantitis surgical decontamination
- Titanium brush non-inferior to implantoplasty for PPD reduction; superior for MBL preservation (no additional bone loss vs 0.7 mm; p=0.03)
- Decontamination time significantly shorter with titanium brush (3:01 vs 5:27 min; p=0.006)
- Adjacent implant with peri-implantitis identified as novel predictor of pocket closure failure (p=0.033)
- OHIP-14 improved similarly; ease of oral hygiene unchanged, indicating no perceived detriment from thread exposure

## Methodology

- **Design**: Single-center, non-inferiority RCT; 2-arm parallel; 1:1 allocation; concealed by sealed envelopes
- **Setting**: Dept. of Periodontology, Yonsei University Dental Hospital, Seoul (Aug 2021–Jan 2023)
- **Sample**: 30 patients / 30 implants (15 per arm); n calculated based on PPD SD 1.0 mm, non-inferiority margin 1.2 mm, α=2.5%, power 80%
- **Inclusion**: Bone loss ≥3 mm + PPD ≥6 mm + BOP/suppuration persisting after non-surgical therapy + pronounced supracrestal defect
- **Test (titanium brush)**: TN brush (Dentium) straight rotating; R-brush (Neobiotech) angled — when prosthesis removed
- **Control (implantoplasty)**: Oval tungsten carbide burs + Arkansas stone burs → polished with silica burs (Komet Dental)
- **Additional**: All implants received osseous recontouring for severe crater defects; flaps repositioned at bone level
- **Outcome**: Composite (implant not lost + BOP ≤1 site + PPD ≤5 mm + no additional bone loss ≥0.5 mm); PPD, BOP, PI, GI, MR at 6 sites; MBL by periapical radiograph at BL/6/12 months; OHIP-14 at BL/2wk/3/6/9/12 months
- **Surgery performed by**: Single experienced periodontist (J.‐K.C.)
- **Post-op**: Acetaminophen 650 mg; 0.12% CHX rinse ×2/day ×2 weeks; recalls at 3, 6, 9, 12 months

## Key Results

| Parameter | Titanium Brush | Implantoplasty | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composite outcome 12 mo | 6/15 (40.0%) | 4/15 (26.7%) | 0.70 |
| Pocket closure (PPD ≤5mm) 12 mo | 12/15 (80.0%) | 12/15 (80.0%) | 1.00 |
| PPD reduction (mean, mm) | −3.6 ± 1.5 | −3.3 ± 1.2 | 0.29 (non-inferior) |
| MBL change 12 mo (mm) | 0.0 ± 0.6 | +0.7 ± 1.2 | 0.03* |
| Implant loss | 0 | 2 | — |
| Surface decontamination time | 3 min 1 s | 5 min 27 s | 0.006* |

**Pocket closure failure predictors** (logistic regression):
- Baseline PPD: coeff −1.520, p=0.044
- Adjacent implant with peri-implantitis: coeff −13.805, p=0.033

**OHIP-14**: Both groups showed slight improvement; satisfaction at 12 months: 7.33±3.27 (test) vs 7.85±3.26 (control); p=0.67.

## Limitations

- Small sample (n=15 per arm); sample size calculated for PPD, not composite primary outcome → underpowered for primary endpoint
- Single operator not blinded to allocation
- Osseous recontouring not standardized
- OHIP-14 not validated in Korean population
- No CBCT for bone level assessment
- No tissue sampling for titanium particle analysis
- Implantoplasty not restricted by defect morphology (per protocol allocation)

## Glossary

- **Implantoplasty**: Mechanical modification of exposed implant thread geometry (tungsten carbide burs + Arkansas stone) to create smooth surface; purpose is biofilm removal but raises overheating/fracture concerns
- **Rotating titanium brush**: Powered instrument (TN brush / R-brush) that instructs exposed surfaces without removing thread geometry; reaches thread valleys with minor macroscopic alteration
- **Composite outcome**: Simultaneous fulfillment of: implant survival + BOP ≤1 site + PPD ≤5 mm + no additional bone loss ≥0.5 mm (EFP 2023 endpoint)
- **Non-inferiority RCT**: Design where the test arm must prove it is not meaningfully worse (>1.2 mm PPD margin) than the control; does not claim superiority
- **Pocket closure**: PPD ≤5 mm at all 6 peri-implant sites at 12 months (80% in both groups)
- **OHIP-14**: Oral Health Impact Profile-14 — validated QoL questionnaire; 14 items, higher score = worse OHRQoL
