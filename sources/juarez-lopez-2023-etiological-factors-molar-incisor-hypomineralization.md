## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 40 studies (25 included in meta-analysis; 44,037 participants from Asia, Europe, America; 2004–2022 literature); PRISMA/PROSPERO-registered; random-effects model due to significant heterogeneity (I²=54–94%).
Maternal illness during pregnancy (OR 4.03, 95% CI 1.33–12.16), low birth weight (OR 1.23, 95% CI 1.10–1.38), childhood illness (OR 4.06, 95% CI 2.03–8.11), antibiotic use (OR 1.76, 95% CI 1.31–2.37), and high fever (OR 1.48, 95% CI 1.18–1.84) were significantly associated with MIH; prematurity, cesarean, and respiratory disorders were not.
MIH etiology is multifactorial; a simultaneous combination of ≥2 risk factors with genetic susceptibility is likely required, underscoring the need for early dental surveillance in at-risk children.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA 등록 SR+MA (40편 정성 · 25편 정량; 44,037명) — 임신 중 산모 질환 (OR 4.03), 저체중 출생 (OR 1.23), 영아기 전신질환 (OR 4.06), 항생제 복용 (OR 1.76), 고열 (OR 1.48)이 구치-절치 법랑질 저석회화증 (Molar-Incisor Hypomineralization, MIH)과 유의하게 관련.
미숙아 출산, 제왕절개, 호흡기 질환은 유의한 연관 없음.
MIH는 다인자성 — 유전적 감수성과 복합 위험 인자의 공동 노출이 필요하며, 위험군 아동에 대한 조기 치과 추적이 핵심 예방 전략.

## Key Contributions

- First PROSPERO-registered SR+MA to pool pre/peri/postnatal risk factors for MIH with quantified OR
- Confirms **maternal illness during last trimester** as strongest prenatal predictor (OR 4.03)
- Quantifies postnatal antibiotic use (amoxicillin) and high fever as independent risk factors
- Refutes prematurity, cesarean birth, and respiratory disorders as significant MIH predictors
- Documents high heterogeneity (I²=54–94%) — evidence calls for cautious interpretation pending higher-quality cohort studies

## Methodology

- Design: Systematic review + Meta-analysis (PRISMA; PROSPERO CRD42022348603)
- Databases: PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, LILACS, SciELO (2004–December 2022)
- Study types included: cross-sectional (n=28), cohort (n=5), case-control (n=7)
- Total participants: 44,037
- Quality assessment: Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS ≥5 for inclusion)
- Statistical analysis: RevMan v5.4; random-effects model; OR with 95% CI
- Languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese
- Exclusions: case reports, reviews, in vitro, genetic-factor studies, patients >18 years

## Limitations

- Language restriction (English/Spanish/Portuguese only) — may miss literature
- 75% of studies cross-sectional with questionnaire-based medical history — recall bias
- Maternal illness and childhood disease categories heterogeneous across studies
- High I² values (54–94%) reduce precision of pooled estimates
- Number of studies per subgroup relatively small
- Genetic factors excluded — may underestimate gene-environment interaction
- Need for well-designed cohort studies with documented medical records

## Glossary

- **MIH (Molar-Incisor Hypomineralization, 구치-절치 법랑질 저석회화증)**: Qualitative enamel developmental defect affecting ≥1 permanent first molars and possibly incisors; characterized by demarcated opacities varying from white to yellow/brown
- **Amelogenesis (법랑질 형성)**: Process of enamel matrix secretion and mineralization by ameloblasts; disruption during apposition/maturation phase causes MIH
- **Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (NOS)**: Quality assessment tool for observational studies; scores selection, comparability, and outcome/exposure on a 9-star system
- **OR (Odds Ratio, 오즈비)**: Measure of association between exposure and outcome in case-control or cross-sectional studies
- **I² (이질성 지수)**: Percentage of variability in meta-analysis due to between-study heterogeneity; ≤25%=none, 25–50%=low, 50–75%=moderate, >75%=significant
- **CPP-ACFP (Casein Phosphopeptide-Amorphous Calcium Fluoride Phosphate)**: Remineralizing agent used in MIH management to promote enamel remineralization
