## Why Ingested

Acetaminophen은 임신 중 치성 통증의 1차 진통제로 [[drug/antibiotics/aliabadi-2022-antibiotic-use-endodontic-treatment-pregnancy]]에서 권고된다. 본 SR은 그 전제에 신경발달장애(NDD) 연관이라는 단서를 달아, [[overviews/drug-analgesics-postop-pain-overview]]의 임신부 진통제 선택에 균형 근거 제공. (단 연관≠인과; 본문 최종 권고는 광범위한 제한이 아니라 최소 유효용량·최단 기간 사용.)

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (Navigation Guide methodology, 46 studies through Feb 2025, qualitative synthesis): 27 studies reported positive associations between prenatal acetaminophen exposure and offspring neurodevelopmental disorders (ADHD, ASD, other NDDs), 9 null and 4 negative; higher-quality studies were more often positive.

The abstract advises pregnant women to limit acetaminophen, while the discussion recommends judicious use — lowest effective dose, shortest duration, under medical guidance — rather than a broad limitation, citing the risks of untreated maternal fever and pain (neural tube defects, preterm birth).

Association, not causation: confounding by indication cannot be excluded, and in the largest Swedish cohort (Ahlqvist 2024) modest associations attenuated to the null in sibling-controlled analysis (ADHD HR 1.07 → 0.98), which the authors question on exposure-ascertainment grounds.

## 세줄요약

Navigation Guide 방법론 체계적 고찰(46편, 2025년 2월까지, 정성 종합): 산전 아세트아미노펜 노출과 자녀 신경발달장애(ADHD·ASD·기타 NDD)의 연관 — 27편 양성, 9편 null, 4편 음성; 고품질 연구일수록 양성 경향.

초록은 임신부 사용 제한을 권고하나, 본문 최종 권고는 광범위한 제한이 아니라 최소 유효용량·최단 기간·의료진 지도하 신중한 사용 — 치료하지 않은 산모 발열·통증도 신경관 결손·조산 위험이 있기 때문.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Applies the Navigation Guide (environmental-health evidence-grading framework) to the prenatal acetaminophen–NDD literature.
- Stratifies 46 studies by direction of association and study quality.
- Concludes the body of evidence is consistent with an association, and recommends precautionary limitation of acetaminophen in pregnancy.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Systematic PubMed search through Feb 25, 2025, predefined inclusion criteria.
- Studies rated for risk of bias and strength of evidence (Navigation Guide).
- Qualitative synthesis chosen over meta-analysis due to substantial heterogeneity.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **46 studies** included.
- **27 positive** (significant link to NDD), **9 null**, **4 negative** (protective).
- Higher-quality studies more likely to show positive associations.
- Outcomes: ADHD, ASD, broader NDDs and related symptomatology.
- By outcome (PDF re-read 2026-09-13): ADHD 20 studies (14 positive, 3 null, 1 inverse, 2 mixed); ASD 8 (5 positive, 2 null, 1 mixed); other NDDs 18 (6 positive, 2 null, 4 inverse, 6 mixed).
- **Conclusion (abstract)**: evidence consistent with an association between prenatal acetaminophen exposure and increased NDD incidence → advise pregnant women to limit acetaminophen.
- **Recommendation (discussion)**: judicious acetaminophen use — lowest effective dose, shortest duration, under medical guidance, tailored to individual risk–benefit — rather than a broad limitation, because untreated maternal fever and pain pose risks such as neural tube defects and preterm birth.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Association, not causation** — observational evidence; confounding, including confounding by indication (fever/infection), was rated within the Navigation Guide risk-of-bias assessment but cannot be excluded.
- Qualitative (no pooled effect size); vote-counting by direction can overweight numerous small studies; authors note possible time-lag bias (~80% of studies published after 2013).
- Sibling-controlled analyses: in the largest cohort (Ahlqvist 2024, Sweden, N=2,480,797) modest full-cohort associations attenuated to the null in the sibling analysis (ADHD HR 1.07 → 0.98); the authors question its exposure ascertainment (midwife interviews without specific acetaminophen inquiry).
- This SR is a precautionary signal, not a contraindication; its own recommendation is dose- and duration-minimization.

## 7. Glossary

- **NDD (neurodevelopmental disorder)**: conditions from atypical brain development (e.g., ASD, ADHD).
- **Navigation Guide**: a systematic, transparent framework for grading environmental-health evidence, adapted from clinical SR methods.
- **Confounding by indication**: bias where the reason a drug is taken (here fever/pain/infection) is itself associated with the outcome.
- **Acetaminophen (paracetamol)**: first-line analgesic/antipyretic in pregnancy per ACOG.
