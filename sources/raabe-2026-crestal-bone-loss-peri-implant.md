## Why Ingested

This is the longest-follow-up paper (25 years) in a batch of papers on implant prosthetic design/misfit/connection ↔ marginal bone loss, and it reinforces [[overviews/implant-prosthesis-misfit-connection-mbl-overview]]'s framework that patient/site biology outweighs prosthetic micromechanics for long-term bone stability. In this 233-implant tissue-level cohort, prosthesis type (single crown / splinted crown / bridge), prosthetic retention, marginal misfit, and transmucosal emergence geometry were ALL non-significant for 25-year ΔCBL (p≥0.17), while incisor-region location, probing depth, and suppuration on probing were the significant drivers (all p<0.001) — directly echoing the overview's Couso-Queiruga 2025 finding that smoking/periodontitis history beat gap dimension in multivariate models. It stands in explicit CONTRAST to companion literature that finds positive prosthetic-design associations with MBL/peri-implantitis (e.g., a recent SR+MA identifying splinting as an MBL risk factor, and a study finding a 48° emergence-angle threshold for disease) — see [[implants/peri-implantitis/lin-2025-influence-of-prosthetic-designs]] for the contrasting positive-association evidence.

## Three-line Summary

Single-center observational study, 147 patients / 233 tissue-level implants restored with single crowns (SC), splinted crowns (SP), or bridges (BR), evaluated at 10 and 25 years post-placement.

Peri-implantitis prevalence rose from 10→25 years (SC 2.3%→7.8%, SP 1.6%→6.3%, BR 0%→4.9%) with no significant difference between prosthesis types at either timepoint (p≥0.95); 25-year ΔCBL (SC −0.37±0.91mm, SP −0.25±0.85mm, BR −0.70±1.0mm) also did not differ significantly by prosthesis type (p=0.37), nor did prosthetic retention, marginal misfit, or emergence geometry.

Multivariate analysis found only incisor-region location, increased probing depth, and suppuration on probing significantly associated with greater bone loss (all p<0.001) — over a 25-year horizon in this tissue-level cohort, patient/site biology outweighed prosthetic-design factors.

## 1. Document Information

- **Title**: Crestal Bone Loss and Peri-Implant Conditions at Tissue-Level Implants: Influence of Prosthesis Type After 25 Years
- **Authors**: Clemens Raabe, Manrique Fonseca, Andrea Roccuzzo, Martin Schimmel, Giovanni E. Salvi, Vivianne Chappuis, Emilio Couso-Queiruga
- **Journal**: Clinical Implant Dentistry and Related Research, 2026;28(3):e70158
- **DOI**: 10.1111/cid.70158 | **PMID**: 42132365 | **PMCID**: PMC13174906
- **Study type**: Single-center observational (long-term prospective re-examination cohort)
- **Setting**: Department of Oral Surgery and Stomatology, School of Dental Medicine, University of Bern, Switzerland; funded by ITI (Grant No. 1574-2021)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Rare 25-YEAR outcome dataset for implant-supported fixed dental prostheses (IFDPs) — one of the longest follow-ups in the implant prosthodontic literature.
- Directly tests whether IFDP type (SC/SP/BR) influences long-term crestal bone loss (ΔCBL) and peri-implant disease prevalence at tissue-level (TL) implants — finds NO significant effect, contrasting some shorter-follow-up literature.
- Identifies incisor-region location, probing depth, and suppuration on probing as the dominant long-term drivers of bone loss, not prosthetic design variables (retention type, marginal misfit, emergence geometry).
- Suggests TL implant design (2.8 mm platform height, machined supracrestal collar) may buffer against prosthetic-design imperfections (misfit, steep emergence) that are more consequential in bone-level implant literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: STROBE-guided single-center observational study; ethics approval KEK-BE-No. 2023-02279 (Cantonal Ethics Committee of Bern).
- **Cohort**: Adult patients rehabilitated with tissue-level SLA titanium implants between May 1997–January 2001; re-examined at 10-year (Jan 2010–Jan 2011) and 25-year (Sep 2024–May 2025) follow-ups. Required both clinical AND radiographic data at both timepoints for inclusion.
- **Final sample**: 147 patients, 233 implants (SC n=129, SP n=63 [incl. cantilever], BR n=41 [incl. cantilever]); maxilla n=95, mandible n=138.
- **Clinical measures**: keratinized mucosa width (KMW), facial mucosal thickness (FMT), probing depth (PD), bleeding on probing (BOP), suppuration on probing (SOP) — 6 sites/implant, UNC-15 probe, 2 calibrated periodontists.
- **Radiographic measures**: periapical (parallel technique, XCP holders) and panoramic radiographs; CBL and emergence geometry measured on panoramic radiographs (chosen to minimize angulation effects) using ImageJ by 2 calibrated examiners (ICC 0.91–0.96). Emergence angle = angle between implant axis and restoration tangent at platform level; emergence profile classified concave/convex/straight. Misfit assessed dichotomously on periapical radiographs.
- **Statistics**: R v4.4.2; robust linear mixed regression + multinomial-Poisson GLMM (patient ID random effect); univariate screening (p≤0.10) → multivariate ΔCBL model with location as nuisance random effect.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Peri-implantitis prevalence**: 10y→25y — SC 2.3%→7.8%, SP 1.6%→6.3%, BR 0%→4.9% (p≥0.95, no group difference at either timepoint).
- **Peri-implant mucositis at 25y**: SC 74.4%, SP 87.3% (trend higher, not significant), BR 80.5%.
- **Mean ΔCBL (10y→25y)**: SC −0.37±0.91mm, SP −0.25±0.85mm, BR −0.70±1.0mm (p=0.37, no significant difference by prosthesis type).
- **Multivariate significant predictors of greater ΔCBL**: incisor location (vs canines −0.74mm, premolars −0.78mm, molars −0.86mm; p<0.001); probing depth (−0.16mm per additional mm, p<0.001); suppuration on probing (−0.97mm, p<0.001).
- **Multivariate NON-significant for ΔCBL**: IFDP type (p=0.17), platform height (p=0.17), transmucosal emergence geometry, marginal misfit, KMW, FMT, BOP.
- **Radiographic misfit prevalence**: SC 10.9%, SP 23.8%, BR 14.6% — not associated with peri-implant health.
- **Peri-implant sheathing/corticalization** (radiographic finding without necessarily clinical inflammation): present in 10.1–14.6% of implants across groups, not influenced by IFDP type; etiology unclear (proposed: aseptic titanium-particle immune response or postloading bone densification/occlusal trauma).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Observational, single-center academic setting — limits generalizability, no causal inference.
- Selection bias: only patients retained/consenting for BOTH 10y and 25y follow-up included (survivorship-style bias), likely underestimating true CBL change and PM/PI prevalence.
- Heterogeneity within predefined IFDP categories (splinted vs nonsplinted, cantilever presence, retention type) may dilute true associations.
- CBL/emergence geometry measured on panoramic (not periapical) radiographs to minimize angulation — but panoramic imaging has lower spatial resolution and 2D limitation (misses buccal/lingual changes).
- Systemic risk factors captured only at the 25-year timepoint, may not reflect baseline/earlier status.
- Authors call for prospective multicenter cohorts/RCTs to confirm the limited IFDP-type effect across implant systems and populations.

## 6. Related Work

- Contrasts with a long-term retrospective study (17–23y follow-up) reporting higher PM/PI for fixed partial dentures vs single crowns, and with other literature linking splinting/BR to higher PI vs SC (attributed to reduced hygiene access in multi-unit reconstructions).
- Contrasts with a recent SR+MA identifying splinting as a risk factor for crestal bone loss, though other SRs found no significant splinted-vs-nonsplinted difference (context-dependent on prosthesis design/cleansability).
- Contrasts with a study identifying a 48° emergence-angle threshold for PM/PI risk in tissue-level implant-supported prostheses; this study found no emergence-geometry association.
- Consistent with a systematic review that failed to identify a clinical misfit threshold for peri-implant bone loss.
- Consistent with a prior retrospective study reporting 3.7-fold higher bone-loss probability at anterior vs posterior sites, and meta-analyses showing pooled RR 1.34–1.76 for anterior-region peri-implantitis risk.
- See [[overviews/implant-prosthesis-misfit-connection-mbl-overview]] for the broader synthesis this paper's null prosthetic-design finding feeds into.
- See [[implants/peri-implantitis/lin-2025-influence-of-prosthetic-designs]] for a paper with positive prosthetic-design associations (contrast).

## 7. Glossary

- **IFDP**: Implant-supported fixed dental prosthesis.
- **SC / SP / BR**: Single crown / splinted crown / bridge.
- **CBL / ΔCBL**: Crestal bone level / change in crestal bone level over time.
- **PM / PI**: Peri-implant mucositis / peri-implantitis.
- **PD / BOP / SOP**: Probing depth / bleeding on probing / suppuration on probing.
- **KMW / FMT**: Keratinized mucosa width / facial mucosal thickness.
- **TL implant**: Tissue-level implant (transmucosal collar above the bone-implant interface, as opposed to bone-level implants).
- **Emergence angle/profile**: Geometric description of how the prosthetic crown contour transitions from the implant platform through the soft tissue.
