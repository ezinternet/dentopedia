## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251029633) of 18 RCTs (n = 1038; 11 muscular, 4 articular, 3 mixed TMD) comparing photobiomodulation (PBM) effects across TMD subtypes.

Pooled effects favored PBM for pain (SMD = -0.84; 95% CI -1.18 to -0.51) and mandibular function (SMD = 0.72; 95% CI 0.38 to 1.05); articular TMD trials showed the most robust and homogeneous effects.

PBM is effective in both muscular and articular TMD, supporting subtype-specific protocols; articular superiority rests on only 4 trials and an abstract-level read (no per-subtype estimates or certainty grades retrieved).

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO CRD420251029633): 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 18편 (n = 1038; 근육성 11편, 관절성 4편, 혼합 3편)으로 측두하악장애 (Temporomandibular Disorders, TMD) 아형별 광생체조절치료 (Photobiomodulation, PBM) 효과를 비교.

통증 (SMD = -0.84; 95% CI -1.18 ~ -0.51)과 하악 기능 (SMD = 0.72; 95% CI 0.38 ~ 1.05) 모두 PBM이 유의하게 우수했고, 관절성 TMD 시험에서 효과가 가장 강하고 균질했음.

근육성·관절성 모두 PBM이 유효해 아형별 프로토콜을 뒷받침하나, 관절성 우위는 4편 기반이며 초록 수준이라 아형별 추정치·근거 확실성 등급은 확인 불가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Subtype-specific comparison of PBM in muscular vs articular TMD.
- Pooled pain SMD = -0.84 (95% CI -1.18 to -0.51); function SMD = 0.72 (95% CI 0.38 to 1.05).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

PubMed, MEDLINE, LILACS, Cochrane Library to 2025-03-31; RCTs of PBM in muscular/articular TMD with VAS pain and/or MMO; Cochrane RoB 2.0; random-effects with subtype subgroups. 18 RCTs, n = 1038 (11 muscular, 4 articular, 3 mixed).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

PBM significantly improved pain and mandibular function in all subtypes; articular trials showed the most robust and homogeneous effects (abstract-level statement; per-subtype numbers not available).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Abstract-only: no per-subtype estimates, I2, RoB distribution, laser parameters or certainty grades. Articular group is only 4 trials. Mechanism (anti-inflammatory) is the authors' suggestion.

## 7. Glossary

- Photobiomodulation (PBM): low-level light therapy using red/near-infrared light for analgesic and anti-inflammatory effect.
- SMD: standardized mean difference.
- MMO: maximum mouth opening.
- VAS: visual analogue scale for pain.
