## Why Ingested

Korean study (Yonsei University) directly comparing UV irradiation vs. DBD plasma treatment (the mechanism underlying ACTILINK/Plasmapp) on titanium — first published head-to-head comparison of UV and DBD plasma surface properties + biological activity with follow-up storage assessment; directly answers the mechanistic question at the intersection of [[implants/surface/houshmand-2023-uv-photofunctionalization-sla-titanium-disks]] and [[implants/surface/plasma/kahm-2024-vacuum-plasma-surface-treatment-osseointegration]].

## One-line Summary

In vitro (Yonsei, Korea): UV-treated Ti stored in distilled water maintained hydrophilicity and osteoblast activity comparable to freshly UV- or DBD plasma-treated Ti for 28 days; vacuum desiccator and ambient air storage did not.

## 한줄요약

In vitro (연세대학교): UV 처리 후 증류수 보관한 티타늄은 28일 후에도 직후 UV 또는 DBD 플라즈마 처리 직후와 동등한 친수성·골아세포 활성을 유지했으나, 진공 건식 및 대기 보관은 그렇지 않았다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First head-to-head comparison of UV vs. DBD plasma treatment on Grade IV machined Ti at equivalent 15-min exposure times
- Shows UV and DBD plasma achieve equivalent surface hydrophilicity immediately after treatment — confirming that DBD plasma restores wettability similarly to UV
- Storage method determines whether UV-gained biological activity is preserved: wet storage (distilled water) ≈ fresh treatment; vacuum or ambient fails
- XPS analysis: UV + wet storage = highest Ti and O surface content, lowest C; prevents hydrocarbon recontamination
- Biological: protein adsorption (albumin) + MC3T3-E1 cell adhesion + cytoskeleton development all superior in UV+wet vs. all dry storage conditions and comparable to fresh UV/DBD treatments

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Material: Grade IV commercially pure titanium discs, machined surfaces
- Groups: (1) control (no treatment), (2) UV treatment (15 min), stored in: (a) distilled water 28 days, (b) vacuum desiccator 28 days (31.325 kPa), (c) sealed container ambient air 28 days; (3) DBD plasma treatment (15 min), tested immediately
- Surface characterization: AFM, XRD, contact angle, XPS
- Biological: albumin adsorption; MC3T3-E1 osteoblast adhesion and cytoskeleton development
- Comparisons: immediate UV vs. immediate DBD plasma vs. 28-day stored UV (3 storage conditions)
- Statistics: not specified in abstract

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Contact angle**: UV+dH2O storage ≈ immediate UV ≈ immediate DBD plasma (superhydrophilic); UV+vacuum > UV+ambient but both < immediate treatments (p<0.001)
- **XPS (surface chemistry)**: UV+dH2O maintained elevated Ti, O; lowest C — prevented hydrocarbon recontamination; vacuum and ambient storage showed C accumulation
- **Albumin adsorption**: UV+dH2O > UV+vacuum, UV+ambient; comparable to immediate UV and DBD plasma
- **MC3T3-E1 cell adhesion**: UV+dH2O significantly enhanced vs. vacuum/ambient; comparable to immediate UV/DBD
- **Cytoskeletal development**: UV+dH2O ≈ immediate UV ≈ immediate DBD (well-spread); vacuum/ambient showed reduced spreading
- **Key comparison**: immediate DBD plasma ≈ immediate UV for all endpoints at 15 min treatment time

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Machined surface only (not SLA/SLA-active) — clinical implants use roughened surfaces
- 15-min UV and 15-min DBD exposure — may not reflect clinical device protocols (ACTILINK: 60s)
- MC3T3-E1 is murine preosteoblast — different from primary human osteoblasts
- No in vivo data
- DBD plasma tested only immediately — no storage study for plasma-treated Ti
- Authors note wet storage requires maintaining sterility — clinical logistics not addressed

## 7. Glossary

- **DBD plasma**: Dielectric Barrier Discharge plasma — same technology as ACTILINK/Plasmapp; generates RONS at atmospheric or low pressure
- **XPS**: X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy — measures surface elemental composition
- **AFM**: Atomic Force Microscopy — measures surface topography
- **MC3T3-E1**: murine preosteoblast cell line used as osteoblast model
- **dH2O**: distilled water storage medium
