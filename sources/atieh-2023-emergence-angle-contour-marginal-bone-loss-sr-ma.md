## Why Ingested

This SR+MA directly addresses the emergence angle (EA) threshold of 30° as a driver of peri-implant MBL, a gap in the existing wiki. Complements [[wiki/implants/mbl/liu-2025-abutment-shape-marginal-bone-loss-crestal-bone-remodeling]] (RCT-only MA) and [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/prpic-2026-emergence-angle-profile-implant-restorations-scoping]] (scoping review). Provides the first pooled MBL and peri-implantitis risk comparison across observational studies (9 years max follow-up).

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (4 studies; 912 implants; 397 participants; 3.8–10.9yr follow-up) comparing peri-implant outcomes of implant restorations with emergence angle (EA) >30° vs ≤30°.

Overall MBL difference between EA >30° and ≤30° was not statistically significant (MD 0.80mm; 95% CI −0.13 to 1.72; p = 0.09); in platform-matched implants the ≤30° group had significantly less MBL; convex emergence profile had a significantly higher peri-implantitis rate (57.8%) vs concave/straight profile (21.3%; RR 2.32; 95% CI 1.12–4.82; p = 0.02).

Emergence angle alone does not reliably predict MBL, but a convex profile markedly increases peri-implantitis risk (~2.3×); clinically, concave or straight profiles should be preferred over convex regardless of exact angle.

## 세줄요약

SR+MA (4편; 임플란트 912개; 397명; 최대 10.9년 추적): 출현각도(EA, Emergence Angle) >30° vs ≤30° 비교.

전체 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss) 차이 통계 미달 (MD 0.80mm; p=0.09); 플랫폼 매칭(PM) 임플란트에서 ≤30°군 유의하게 MBL 적음; 볼록 출현 윤곽(Convex Profile) → 임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis) 발생률 57.8% vs 오목/직선형 21.3% (RR 2.32, p=0.02).

출현각도 단독으로는 MBL을 예측하기 어렵지만, 볼록 윤곽은 임플란트주위염 위험 ~2.3배; 임상적으로 오목·직선형 출현 윤곽 선호.

## Key Contributions

- First SR+MA specifically comparing EA >30° vs ≤30° on MBL and peri-implantitis risk
- Overall MBL difference NS (MD 0.80 mm), but subgroup analysis in PM implants shows significant benefit of ≤30°
- Convex profile: significantly higher peri-implantitis prevalence (57.8% vs 21.3%; RR 2.32)
- Follow-up of 3.8–10.9 years — longest observation period in this literature

## Methodology

- Design: SR+MA; PRISMA guidelines
- Databases: Electronic database search (databases not specified in abstract)
- Inclusion: Observational studies; EA >30° vs ≤30° comparison; ≥3.8yr follow-up
- n: 4 studies; 912 implants; 397 participants
- EA >30° group: 455 implants; ≤30° group: 457 implants
- Outcomes: MBL (primary), peri-implantitis prevalence, periodontal parameters
- Statistics: Random-effects meta-analysis; risk ratio for peri-implantitis

## Key Results

| Outcome | EA >30° | EA ≤30° | MD/RR | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MBL (overall) | Higher | Lower | MD 0.80 mm (CI −0.13 to 1.72) | 0.09 (NS) |
| MBL (PM implants subgroup) | Higher | Lower | Significant | <0.05 |
| Peri-implantitis (convex) | 57.8% | 21.3% (concave/straight) | RR 2.32 (CI 1.12–4.82) | 0.02 |

- Quality of evidence: Low to moderate certainty (observational studies, few included)
- Publication years of included studies: not specified in abstract

## Limitations

- Only 4 eligible studies — very limited evidence base
- Heterogeneity of designs, implant systems, and follow-up durations
- Low to moderate certainty of evidence
- No distinction between anteriorly and posteriorly placed implants in analysis
- Confounding by operator skill and implant system not fully accounted for

## Glossary

- **Emergence angle (EA)**: The angle formed between the implant-prosthesis profile and the implant axis at the implant-restoration junction; >30° considered risk threshold
- **Emergence profile**: The 3D contour of the restoration from the implant platform to the clinical crown margin — convex, straight, or concave
- **Platform-matched (PM)**: Implant with abutment of same diameter as implant platform (no platform switching)
- **Peri-implantitis**: Inflammatory condition around an implant with progressive bone loss
- **Risk ratio (RR)**: Ratio of event probabilities in two groups; RR >1 = higher risk in the first group
