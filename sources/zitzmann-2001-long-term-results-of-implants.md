## Why Ingested

→ [[bone-regeneration/zitzmann-2001-long-term-results-of-implants]]

To document long-term (5-year) outcomes of endosteal implants placed with guided bone regeneration (GBR) using Bio-Oss/Bio-Gide or Bio-Oss/Gore-Tex compared to implants entirely surrounded by bone.

## Three-line Summary

1. In 75 patients, 112 Brånemark implants with peri-implant defects were treated with GBR (Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide); in a split-mouth subgroup 41 defects received Bio-Oss + Gore-Tex, with 112 control implants entirely surrounded by bone.
2. After 5 years, cumulative implant survival ranged 93–97% across groups; mean marginal bone loss was higher in GBR-treated sites (Bio-Oss/Bio-Gide 1.83 mm, Bio-Oss/Gore-Tex 2.21 mm) vs controls (1.73 mm), increasing over time.
3. Keratinized mucosa remained stable (~3.0–3.16 mm); plaque/inflammation/recession correlated more with restoration type than treatment type, but initial defects >2 mm vertically were considered indication for GBR.

## 세줄요약

1. 75명의 환자에서 Brånemark 임플란트 112개(결손부)에 Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide로 유도골재생(GBR)을 시행하고, split-mouth 하위군 41개 결손부에는 Bio-Oss + Gore-Tex를 사용했으며, 112개 대조군은 골로 완전 둘러싸인 부위였다.
2. 5년 후 누적 생존율은 그룹 간 93–97%였고, 변연골소실은 GBR군(Bio-Oss/Bio-Gide 1.83 mm, Bio-Oss/Gore-Tex 2.21 mm)이 대조군(1.73 mm)보다 더 컸으며 시간 경과에 따라 유의하게 증가했다.
3. 각화조직은 ~3.0–3.16 mm로 안정적이었고, 플라크/염증/퇴축은 치료 방식보단 보철물 형태와 더 밀접했으며, 수직 결손 2 mm 이상 시 GBR이 적응증으로 판단되었다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Int J Oral Maxillofac Implants
- **Year/Volume/Pages**: 2001 May-Jun;16(3):355-66
- **PMID**: 11432655
- **Study type**: Prospective 5-year longitudinal study (split-mouth subgroup)
- **Sample**: 75 patients, 112 test implants (GBR) with Bio-Oss+Bio-Gide, 41 in split-mouth with Bio-Oss+Gore-Tex, 112 control implants (entirely surrounded by bone)
- **Full text status**: abstract-only from PubMed page (full_text: false) — no attempt to fabricate numbers

## 2. Key Contributions

- Long-term (5-year) prospective evaluation of GBR-treated implants vs bone-encased controls.
- Comparison of resorbable (Bio-Gide) vs non-resorbable (Gore-Tex) membranes in a split-mouth design.
- Assessment linking peri-implant conditions to restoration type vs treatment.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Implants**: Brånemark System (endosteal)
- **GBR materials**: Bio-Oss (deproteinized bovine bone mineral) + Bio-Gide (resorbable collagen membrane); split-mouth Bio-Oss + Gore-Tex (expanded PTFE)
- **Controls**: implants entirely surrounded by bone
- **Follow-up**: after definitive prosthesis placement at 6 months, then annually for 5 years
- **Outcomes**: implant survival, marginal bone level (MBL), plaque, peri-implant mucosal conditions, keratinized mucosa height (KM), marginal soft tissue level (MSTL)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome (5 years) | Bio-Oss + Bio-Gide | Bio-Oss + Gore-Tex | Control (bone-encased) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cumulative implant survival | 93–97% (range across groups) | 93–97% | 93–97% |
| Mean marginal bone level (MBL) | 1.83 mm | 2.21 mm | 1.73 mm |
| Keratinized mucosa (KM) | ~3.02–3.16 mm (stable) | ~3.02–3.16 mm | ~3.02–3.16 mm |
| Plaque | ~15% of sites (with inflammation symptoms) | ~15% | ~15% |

- MBL increased significantly over time and differed significantly among groups.
- Plaque/inflammation/recession correlated more strongly with restoration type than treatment type.
- Indication suggested if initial defect size >2 mm vertically.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only record (full text not retrieved); some details may be missing.
- Heterogeneous prostheses (single-tooth, fixed, removable) confounded soft tissue outcomes.
- Split-mouth limited to subset (41 implants) with Gore-Tex vs larger Bio-Gide group.

## 7. Glossary

- **유도골재생 (Guided bone regeneration, GBR)**: 차단막을 이용해 연조직 세포의 침입을 방지하고 골세포/골전구세포가 결손부를 재생하도록 유도하는 기법
- **변연골수준 (Marginal bone level, MBL)**: 임플란트 경부 수준과 인접 변연골의 거리
- **각화점막 (Keratinized mucosa, KM)**: 임플란트 주위 각화된 점막의 높이
- **변연연조직수준 (Marginal soft tissue level, MSTL)**: 변연 연조직의 위치
- **브레넥마크시스템 (Brånemark System)**: 골유착형 임플란트 시스템
