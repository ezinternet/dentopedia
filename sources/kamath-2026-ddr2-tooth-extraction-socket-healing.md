## Why Ingested

This paper fills the basic science gap in extraction socket healing by establishing DDR2 (Discoidin Domain Receptor 2, DDR2) — a collagen-activated receptor tyrosine kinase — as a required mediator of alveolar bone repair. The wiki held no molecular mechanism paper on collagen receptor signaling in socket healing. The findings bridge skeletal progenitor cell (SPC) biology to ridge preservation by identifying DDR2 in GLI1+ progenitor cells as critical for cell proliferation, collagen matrix organization, and new bone formation. Relates to [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/fok-2024-alveolar-socket-healing-evolving-knowledge]] (extraction socket healing biology framework) and [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/cardaropoli-2003-bone-tissue-formation-extraction]] (early bone tissue formation sequence).

## Three-line Summary

Mouse knockout study (global and GLI1-conditional Ddr2 deletion; 4-week-old mice, n=6–10 per group per time point) examining DDR2 collagen receptor in maxillary first molar extraction socket healing over 4 weeks via μCT, histology, lineage tracing, and polarized light collagen analysis.

Global Ddr2 knockout reduced socket bone volume (BV/TV) to 57% of wildtype at 1 week, 71% at 2 weeks, and 81% at 4 weeks, with reduced bone mineral density at all times; cell proliferation (EdU) fell ~30% while apoptosis was unchanged; collagen fibril organization (degree of linear polarization) was significantly disrupted at 1 week; DDR2-lineage cells comprised up to 60% of socket cells at 1 week, with ~80% co-localizing with the preosteoblast marker OSX.

DDR2 functions substantially through GLI1+ skeletal progenitor cells (conditional knockout produced 33% BV/TV inhibition vs. 43% for global knockout), and its GVMGFO-binding activation motif on fibrillar collagen may be exploitable for scaffold-based alveolar bone regeneration strategies.

## 세줄요약

생쥐 전신 및 조건부(GLI1 특이적) Ddr2 결손 모델(4주령, 그룹당 n=6–10)에서 상악 제1대구치 발거 후 4주간 μCT·조직학·계통추적·편광 콜라겐 분석으로 DDR2 콜라겐 수용체의 발치와 치유 역할을 규명한 동물연구.

전신 Ddr2 결손은 발치 1주차 골부피율(BV/TV)을 야생형의 57%로 감소시켰으며(2주 71%, 4주 81%), 세포 증식(EdU)은 약 30% 감소, 세포자멸은 변화 없었고, 1주차 콜라겐 섬유 정렬도(DoLP)가 유의하게 저하되었으며 DDR2 계통 세포는 1주차 발치와 세포의 최대 60%를 구성하고 이 중 ~80%가 전골아세포 표지자 OSX와 공존.

DDR2는 GLI1+ 골격전구세포를 통해 주로 기능하며(조건부 결손 BV/TV 억제 33% vs. 전신 결손 43%), 섬유형 콜라겐의 GVMGFO 결합 모티프를 활용한 PLLA 나노섬유 지지체가 치조골 재생에 응용 가능할 것으로 제안.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First report demonstrating DDR2 collagen receptor is required for alveolar bone repair in tooth extraction sockets
- Establishes DDR2+ cells as major skeletal progenitor contributors to socket healing (up to 60% of socket cells at 1 week post-extraction)
- Shows DDR2 and GLI1 lineage cells substantially overlap in the socket and both serve as osteoblast precursors
- Demonstrates DDR2 operates through GLI1+ skeletal progenitor cells (conditional knockout = 33% BV/TV inhibition vs. 43% for global knockout)
- Mechanistically links DDR2 to cell proliferation and collagen fibril organization during socket repair
- Proposes therapeutic application: GVMGFO/GFOGER peptide-decorated PLLA scaffolds for alveolar bone regeneration

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Species/model**: Mus musculus; Ddr2LacZ/LacZ (global null) and Gli1-CreERT2;Ddr2fl/fl (conditional) knockout lines; lineage tracing via Ddr2-CreERT2;Rosa26-tdTomato and Gli1-CreERT2;Rosa26-tdTomato

**Tooth extraction**: Upper first molars (M1) extracted from 4-week-old mice; tamoxifen (80 µg/g body weight × 3 days i.p.) administered 48 h before extraction for Cre-dependent models

**Sample sizes**: n=6–10 mice per experimental group for μCT; ≥3 mice for histology and immunofluorescence; approximately equal sexes per group

**Time points**: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 4 weeks post-extraction

**Outcomes measured**:
- μCT: BV/TV, BMD, trabecular parameters (thickness, spacing, number) in standardized distal buccal root socket volumes
- Histology: H&E for gross bone fill; Picrosirius Red with polarized light (degree of linear polarization, DoLP; standard deviation of angle of polarization, SD AoP) for collagen organization
- Proliferation: EdU incorporation (4 h before harvest at 3 days post-extraction)
- Apoptosis: TUNEL assay
- Immunofluorescence: Osterix (OSX/SP7, preosteoblast), bone sialoprotein (BSP/IBSP, osteoblast), DDR2
- Lineage tracing: β-galactosidase staining (Ddr2-LacZ) and tdTomato fluorescence

**Statistics**: GraphPad Prism 10; one- or two-way ANOVA with uncorrected Fisher's LSD or two-tailed unpaired t-test; P<0.05 threshold

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Global Ddr2 knockout — μCT socket healing (BV/TV)**:
- 1 week: Ddr2−/− = 57% of WT
- 2 weeks: Ddr2−/− = 71% of WT
- 4 weeks: Ddr2−/− = 81% of WT
- BMD: reduced at all time points in mutants
- Trabecular parameters (thickness, spacing, number): no significant difference between WT and mutant

**Cell proliferation and apoptosis (3 days post-extraction)**:
- EdU+ cells: ~30% reduction in Ddr2−/− vs. WT
- TUNEL+ cells: no significant difference

**Collagen organization (Picrosirius Red, polarized light)**:
- DoLP at 1 week: significantly lower in Ddr2−/− vs. WT
- WT DoLP: stable from 1–4 weeks
- Ddr2−/− DoLP: gradually increased over 4 weeks (converging toward WT)
- SD AoP: decreased in both genotypes from 1–2 weeks (improved alignment); additional significant decrease 1–4 weeks only in WT

**Lineage tracing — Ddr2-CreERT2 cells in socket**:
- 1 day post-extraction: tdTomato+ cells at bone surface adjacent to socket
- 1 week post-extraction: up to 60% of total socket cells (DAPI+) are tdTomato+
- At 3 days: ~50% of tdTomato+ cells co-localize with OSX (preosteoblast)
- At 1 week: ~80% of tdTomato+ cells co-localize with OSX
- BSP co-localization: ~20% at 3 days → ~35% at 7 days

**GLI1 lineage tracing**: similar time-dependent socket infiltration and OSX co-localization as DDR2 lineage; Gli1-lineage cells also serve as preosteoblast progenitors

**Conditional knockout (Gli1-CreERT2;Ddr2fl/fl) at 1 week**:
- BV/TV inhibition: ~33% (vs. ~43% for global knockout)
- BMD inhibition: ~24% (vs. ~39% for global knockout)
- Reduced DDR2 immunofluorescence confirmed in socket sections
