## Why Ingested
메틸프레드니솔론 투여 경로(Masseter vs Gluteal vs Control)를 직접 비교한 유일한 RCT. [[drug/liborio-2025-methylprednisolone-third-molar-sr-ma]]에서 투여 경로 이질성이 한계로 지목된 부분을 보완하는 원저 데이터.

## Three-line Summary
Single-blind placebo-controlled RCT (n=60) from Iran compared 40 mg methylprednisolone IM injected into the masseter muscle vs gluteal muscle vs no intervention, administered 30 minutes before impacted lower third molar surgery.
No significant differences were found across the three groups for pain (p=0.285), ultrasound-measured edema (p=0.250), or trismus (p=0.337) at any assessment point.
The null result for both injection routes challenges the local-depot hypothesis and, taken together with Larsen 2021, questions the routine use of methylprednisolone for third molar postoperative morbidity regardless of injection site.

## 세줄요약
줄1: 이란의 단일맹검 대조 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (n=60)에서 하악 매복 사랑니 수술 30분 전 메틸프레드니솔론 (Methylprednisolone) 40 mg을 교근 (Masseter) 또는 둔근 (Gluteal Muscle)에 주사하거나 무처치한 3군을 비교했다.
줄2: 통증(p=0.285), 초음파 측정 부종 (Edema)(p=0.250), 개구제한 (Trismus)(p=0.337) 모두 3군 간 유의한 차이가 없었다.
줄3: 두 주사 경로 모두 대조군 대비 우월하지 않아 국소 저장소 가설을 지지하지 않으며, 주사 부위와 무관하게 메틸프레드니솔론의 일상적 사용에 의문을 제기한다.

## One-line Summary
Single-blind RCT (n=60) comparing 40 mg methylprednisolone injected into masseter vs gluteal muscle vs no intervention found no significant differences in pain, edema, or trismus after impacted lower third molar surgery.

## 한줄요약
단일맹검 RCT (n=60)에서 40 mg 메틸프레드니솔론을 교근(Masseter) 또는 둔근(Gluteal Muscle)에 주사한 군과 대조군 간 통증·부종·개구제한 차이가 유의하지 않았다.

## 1. Document Information
- Journal: Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, 2021 Sep;79(9):1829-1836
- DOI: 10.1016/j.joms.2021.03.009 (inferred from journal citation; abstract-only PDF)
- Design: Single-blind placebo-controlled RCT
- Setting: Iran (institution not specified in abstract)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT directly comparing masseter vs gluteal IM routes for methylprednisolone in M3 surgery
- Edema quantified by ultrasound imaging — objective measurement vs facial tape measure
- Null result challenges assumption that local (masseter) route is superior to systemic (gluteal)
- Consistent with Larsen 2021 null result and Gholami's own prior literature (Taheri Talesh 2009)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Single-blind, placebo-controlled RCT
- n=60 (31F/29M), age 19–35 years, impacted lower third molar
- Groups: I=40mg methylprednisolone masseter IM (30 min pre-op); II=40mg methylprednisolone gluteal IM (30 min pre-op); III=no intervention (control)
- Outcomes:
  - Pain: VAS at day 1, 5, 7
  - Edema: Ultrasound imaging at day 5, 7
  - Trismus: Interincisal distance at day 5, 7
- Statistics: Friedman test (within-group, repeated measures); Kruskal-Wallis (between-group); α=0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Pain: No significant difference among 3 groups (p=0.285, CI=−2.91±0.40)
- Edema: No significant difference (p=0.250, CI=0.22±0.42)
- Trismus: No significant difference (p=0.337, CI=−5.93±2.22)
- Neither masseter nor gluteal route demonstrated superiority over control

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only data available — full methodology, dropout rates, and exact ultrasound protocol not verifiable
- Single-blind design: raters knew allocation in some assessments
- 30-minute pre-operative window — timing relative to surgery may affect peak plasma levels
- No OHIP/QoL assessment
- Iran-based study — population-specific factors (diet, activity level) may influence edema resolution

## 6. Related Work
- Larsen 2021 (dose comparison split-mouth RCT — also null result)
- Libório 2025 (methylprednisolone SR+MA)
- Buyukkurt 2006 (prednisolone IM + diclofenac — positive result)
- Singh 2023 (dexamethasone vs methylprednisolone SR+MA)

## 7. Glossary
- **Masseter IM**: Injection into masseter muscle — local proximity to surgical site
- **Gluteal IM**: Standard systemic intramuscular route via gluteus maximus
- **Friedman test**: Non-parametric equivalent of repeated-measures ANOVA
- **Kruskal-Wallis test**: Non-parametric one-way ANOVA equivalent for independent groups
