## Why Ingested

The wiki holds individual long-term studies on the All-on-Four concept (de-araujo-nobre series) and an umbrella review (figueiredo-2025), but lacked a recent PRISMA-2020/PROSPERO-registered SR that (a) explicitly compares All-on-Four versus FDP4+ across all outcomes (implant survival, prosthesis survival, MBL, biological and technical complications) and (b) covers prosthetic material choice (metal-ceramic vs metal-acrylic fracture rates). This 2026 SR fills that gap with 20 primary studies and up to 20 years of follow-up.

## Three-line Summary

PRISMA-2020 systematic review (PROSPERO CRD420261352454) comparing All-on-Four with FDP4+ (>4 implants) in edentulous adults; 20 primary studies included (11 PIO + 9 PCO); Newcastle–Ottawa Scale quality assessment; narrative synthesis after heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis.

Prosthesis survival comparable (All-on-Four 85.0–100% vs FDP4+ 88.2–100%); implant survival 89.7–97.9% (All-on-Four, 10yr) vs 98.9–99.0% (FDP4+); MBL at 5yr up to 1.9 mm (All-on-Four) vs 2.9 mm (FDP4+ comparators); metal-ceramic prostheses showed lower fracture (7.27–18.2%) than metal-acrylic (7.3–36.7%).

Conclusion: All-on-Four is a predictable option for edentulous patients avoiding grafting; no significant differences in survival vs FDP4+; evidence limited by predominantly retrospective heterogeneous designs.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA-2020·PROSPERO 등록 체계적 문헌고찰(20편, 최대 20년 추적); All-on-Four 대 4개 초과 임플란트 고정성 전악보철(FDP4+)을 비교; 뉴캐슬-오타와 척도(Newcastle–Ottawa Scale, NOS) 질 평가; 이질성으로 메타분석 불가 → 서술적 종합.

보철 생존율 유사(All-on-Four 85.0–100% vs FDP4+ 88.2–100%); 임플란트 생존율 All-on-Four 89.7–97.9%(10년) vs FDP4+ 98.9–99.0%; 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL) 5년 시점 All-on-Four 최대 1.9 mm vs FDP4+ 최대 2.9 mm; 금속-도재(metal-ceramic) 보철 파절률(7.27–18.2%)이 금속-아크릴(metal-acrylic)보다 낮음(7.3–36.7%).

결론: All-on-Four는 골증대술 거부·의치 거부 환자에게 예측 가능한 옵션; FDP4+ 대비 생존율 유의차 없음; 대부분 후향적 설계로 근거 수준 제한.

## 2. Key Contributions

- PRISMA-2020-compliant SR with PROSPERO registration comparing All-on-Four vs FDP4+ across four outcome domains: implant survival, prosthesis survival, MBL, and complications.
- Provides explicit prosthetic material comparison: metal-ceramic (7.27–18.2% fracture) vs metal-acrylic (7.3–36.7% fracture) — favoring metal-ceramic.
- Covers up to 20-year follow-up from primary studies, making it the longest-horizon SR comparison in the wiki.
- Confirms no statistically significant differences in survival between All-on-Four and FDP4+, while acknowledging FDP4+ implant survival is numerically higher at long-term (98.9–99.0% vs 89.7–97.9%).
- Clarifies tilted vs axial implants: no significant MBL or survival differences (0.07 mm absolute difference reported by Lopes et al. — clinically irrelevant).

## 3. Methodology

- **Study design**: Systematic review, narrative synthesis
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD420261352454 (registered 31 October 2025)
- **Search**: MEDLINE/PubMed + EMBASE; January 2000 – January 2026 (last searched 17 March 2026)
- **Languages**: English, German
- **Population**: Adults ≥18 years with edentulous jaws
- **Inclusion**: Clinical studies, minimum 4-year follow-up, reporting implant/prosthesis survival, MBL, or complications
- **Exclusion**: Partial prostheses, removable/hybrid prostheses, pediatric, in vitro, abstracts, pilot studies, systematic reviews, regenerative procedures
- **Quality assessment**: Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (NOS) — 7–9 stars = low risk; 4–6 = moderate; 0–3 = high
- **Overall quality rating**: Low-moderate (predominantly retrospective designs)
- **Meta-analysis**: Planned but abandoned due to heterogeneity in prosthetic materials, loading protocols, follow-up, outcome definitions

### 4.5 Prosthesis Material Fracture Rates

| Material | Fracture Rate | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Metal–PMMA (acrylic) | 7.3–36.7% | 7–11 years |
| Metal–ceramic | 7.27–18.20% | variable |
| Metal–zirconia | 5.36–29.7% | variable |

Metal-ceramic preferred over metal-acrylic for lower fracture rates. Zirconia frameworks: one study, no technical complications reported.

### 4.6 Biological Complications

- Peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis: prevalent, inconsistently reported across studies
- Highest infection burden: Maló et al. (2019) — 203 cases / 1,072 patients
- Gingival inflammation: highest Hopp et al. (2017) — 189 cases
- Considerable variability in diagnostic criteria limits direct comparability

## 5. Patient Selection Criteria

**Indications**: Edentulous mandible/maxilla or terminal dentition; patient preference for fixed restoration; insufficient posterior bone requiring augmentation otherwise; good general health; oral hygiene compliance.

**Bone requirements**: Horizontal width >4 mm, vertical height >8 mm in anterior region to first/second premolar.

**Contraindications**: Heavy smoking (>15 cigarettes/day), pregnancy/lactation, poor compliance, insufficient bone volume, metabolic bone diseases, bisphosphonate therapy, head/neck radiotherapy/chemotherapy.

**Risk factors for failure**: Uncontrolled diabetes (OR 1.65), smoking (OR 1.65–3.03), bruxism (OR 2.71–8.70 for technical complications), periodontitis.

## 6. Clinical Recommendations

- Minimum insertion torque 30–50 Ncm required before immediate loading (AWMF S3 guideline).
- Metal-ceramic preferred over metal-acrylic for definitive prosthesis (lower fracture rate).
- Staged approach: PMMA provisional → zirconia-ceramic definitive.
- Bruxism: protective occlusal splint recommended.
- Opposing arch with metal-ceramic implant prosthesis: increased risk of complications (OR 2.04); prefer zirconia crowns.
- Recall: individualized, 3–12 months intervals (AWMF S3).
- Tilted implants: no clinically meaningful MBL or survival difference vs axial placement.
