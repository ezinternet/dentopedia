## Why Ingested

인제스트 당시 [[wiki/overviews/osteotomy-drilling-heat-determinants-irrigation-overview]]는 peck drilling을 "하드본·가이드수술에서 권고"라고만 기술했으나, 구체적인 mm 간격을 수량화한 데이터가 없었다. 본 논문은 peck 간격을 0→3mm / 0→5mm / 0→8mm로 명시하고, 드릴 형태(원통형 vs 원추형)에 따라 간헐 기법의 효과가 달라진다는 사실을 in vivo로 검증한 첫 데이터다.

## Three-line Summary

In vivo animal study (New Zealand rabbit, n=48 osteotomies, 4 groups) comparing cylindrical vs conical drills × continuous vs intermittent (peck) movement on bone temperature and 30-day histological healing outcomes.

Intermittent movement (0→3 mm, 0→5 mm, 0→8 mm passes) significantly reduced temperature for cylindrical drills only (ΔT 6.91→4.30°C, −37%, p=0.001); conical drills showed equivalent thermal performance regardless of technique (ΔT 2.78 vs 2.77°C, p=0.977); conical drills outperformed cylindrical drills on temperature, new bone formation, and PMN cell count in all group comparisons.

The drill geometry–technique interaction reframes the peck drilling recommendation: for conical drills, technique choice matters far less than the drill itself; for cylindrical drills, intermittent movement is a meaningful mitigation strategy.

## 세줄요약

In vivo 동물실험(뉴질랜드 토끼, n=48 골절제, 4군): 원통형 vs 원추형 드릴 × 연속 vs 간헐(peck) 동작이 골 온도와 30일 조직학적 치유에 미치는 영향 비교.

간헐 동작(0→3mm / 0→5mm / 0→8mm 3단계 삽입)은 원통형 드릴에서만 유의미한 온도 감소(ΔT 6.91→4.30°C, −37%, p=0.001); 원추형은 기법 무관 동등(ΔT 2.78 vs 2.77°C, p=0.977); 원추형은 온도·신생골 형성·PMN 세포수 모든 지표에서 원통형보다 우수.

Peck drilling 권고를 드릴 형태 맥락으로 재구성: 원추형 드릴 사용 시 기법보다 드릴 자체가 결정인자이며, 원통형 드릴 시에는 간헐 동작이 의미 있는 발열 완화책임.

## 2. Key Contributions

- **Peck interval quantification**: Defines intermittent movement as three staged passes (0→3 mm, 0→5 mm, 0→8 mm) with drill markings at 3 mm and 5 mm — the first in vivo study to specify these increments for dental implant osteotomy.
- **Drill-geometry × technique interaction**: Intermittent movement significantly helps cylindrical drills (p=0.001, −2.6°C) but provides no additional benefit for conical drills (p=0.977, −0.008°C).
- **Histological endpoint**: Intermittent technique improves 30-day new bone formation area (cylindrical: 1.00→1.48 mm², p=0.003) and reduces PMN cell count regardless of drill type — suggesting biological benefit beyond temperature alone.
- **Conical superiority**: Conical drills at 1500 rpm outperform cylindrical drills at lower staged RPMs across all metrics, raising the question of whether drill geometry contributes more to heat control than drilling technique.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective in vivo animal study
- **Model**: 12 female New Zealand rabbits (4.0 ± 0.5 kg), bilateral tibiae
- **N**: 48 osteotomies total (n=12 per group × 4 groups)
- **Groups**:
  - G1: Cylindrical drills (2.2/2.8/3.5 mm) at 800/600/500 rpm — continuous
  - G2: Cylindrical drills (same) — intermittent (0→3→5→8 mm)
  - G3: Conical drills (same diameters) at 1500 rpm — continuous
  - G4: Conical drills — intermittent (same passes)
- **Osteotomy depth**: 8 mm
- **Irrigation**: External saline at room temperature (23 ± 1°C), continuous
- **Temperature sensor**: Type K thermocouple, 1 mm from osteotomy wall, 3 mm depth
- **Histology at 30 days**: Picrosirius hematoxylin staining; new bone formation (mm²) measured in cortical portions bilateral to cut; PMN cells counted in six 500×500 µm quadrants per specimen (ImageJ)
- **Statistics**: One-way ANOVA + Bonferroni post-hoc; GraphPad Prism 5.01; p<0.05

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Temperature variation (ΔT from baseline 33.5°C):**

| Group | ΔT (°C) | SD |
|---|---|---|
| G1 — cylindrical + continuous | 6.91 | ±1.4 |
| G2 — cylindrical + intermittent | 4.30 | ±1.3 |
| G3 — conical + continuous | 2.78 | ±0.6 |
| G4 — conical + intermittent | 2.77 | ±0.7 |

Key pairwise comparisons: G1 vs G2 p=0.001 (Δ2.61°C); G3 vs G4 p=0.977 (NS); all cylindrical vs all conical p<0.0001.

**New bone formation at 30 days:**
G1: 1.00 ± 0.3 mm² → G2: 1.48 ± 0.3 mm² (p=0.003) → G3: 2.20 ± 0.4 mm² → G4: 2.43 ± 0.4 mm² (G1 vs G4 p<0.0001)

**PMN cell count:**
G1: 62.4 ± 5.9 → G2: 50.7 ± 4.2 (p<0.0001) → G3: 44.4 ± 3.7 → G4: 42.4 ± 3.7 (all conical vs G1 p<0.0001)

**Recovery to baseline**: G1–G2 ~20 s; G3–G4 ~10 s

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Rabbit tibia has extreme cortical density + soft medullary bone — poor analogue for human jaw anatomy (D1–D4 range)
- Room-temperature saline only — no chilled irrigation arm; cannot isolate technique vs irrigation interaction
- Thermocouple placed at 3 mm depth only; no cortical-entrance vs apical stratification
- Single time point (30 days); no ISQ, no implant survival, no BIC
- No force or feed rate control between operators

## 6. Related Work

- Sener 2009 ([[implants/sener-2009-effects-irrigation-temperature-heat-control]]): showed depth-stratified temperature — highest at 3 mm cortical level; rationale for why Gehrke's measurement at 3 mm captures the peak zone
- Kapse 2022 ([[implants/kapse-2022-thermal-effect-operatory-room-temperature-surgical]]): irrigation temperature the sole significant variable — Gehrke does not test chilled irrigation; the two studies together suggest that drill geometry + technique matter, but irrigation temperature likely dominates both

## 7. Glossary

- **Intermittent movement**: Drilling in staged depth increments (0→3→5→8 mm) with drill withdrawal between passes to clear chips and allow irrigation fluid access
- **ΔT**: Temperature elevation above baseline (baseline 33.5 ± 0.5°C in this study)
- **PMN**: Polymorphonuclear leukocytes (neutrophils) — inflammatory cell marker in peri-implant tissue at 30 days
- **New bone formation (nBF)**: Area of newly formed bone in cortical section measured by color differentiation from native bone in picrosirius staining
