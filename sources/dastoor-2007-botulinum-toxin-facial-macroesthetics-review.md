## Why Ingested

The wiki's botulinum-toxin folder covers clinical uses of botulinum toxin, and this Dastoor, Misch and Wang 2007 review is the implant-practice source on facial macroesthetic indications. It is ingested as the esthetic-adjunct counterpart to dental-implant esthetics, scoped to the abstract.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative literature review (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2007) on botulinum toxin type A for dynamic facial wrinkles in perioral, glabellar and forehead regions — abstract-only ingest, full text not retrieved.

The review covers the pathogenesis of hyperfunctional wrinkles, the history and techniques of treatment, and clinical controversies, and stresses that wrinkles not caused by muscle hyperfunction will not respond.

Proper case selection and an understanding of dosing and complications are presented as the prerequisites for a satisfactory result; no numeric outcome data are given in the abstract.

## 세줄요약

문헌 고찰 (Journal of Oral Implantology, 2007) — 입주위·미간·이마의 동적 주름에 대한 A형 보툴리눔 독소 (botulinum toxin type A) 사용 검토 — 초록만 수집 (전문 미확보).

고찰은 과기능 근육에 의한 주름의 발생 기전, 치료의 역사·기법, 임상 논쟁을 다루며, 근육 과기능이 원인이 아닌 주름(노화·중력·광손상·외상·반흔)은 반응하지 않는다고 강조.
