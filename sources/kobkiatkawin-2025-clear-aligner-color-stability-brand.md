## Why Ingested
Multi-brand in vitro study comparing color stability of Invisalign, ClearCorrect, Beforedent, and Käse Aligner with FTIR polymer composition analysis — provides direct brand-level material evidence for why Invisalign (polyurethane) discolors more than PETG-based competitors; fills brand-specific material evidence gap in [[wiki/orthodontics/clear-aligner/delgado-2025-thermoplastics-clear-aligners-review]].

## Three-line Summary
In vitro color stability comparison of 4 CA brands (Invisalign, ClearCorrect, Beforedent, Käse Aligner; n=704 total samples, 176/brand) exposed to coffee, tea, cola, and artificial saliva at 6 h and 12 h; FTIR polymer composition and 3D laser surface microscopy performed.

Invisalign showed the most significant color changes (ΔE* >3.3 clinically unacceptable in coffee at 6 h and 12 h; unique aromatic TPU with highest inner surface roughness Sa=1.97 μm via wavy parallel grooves); ClearCorrect exceeded ΔE* 3.3 only in coffee at 12 h; Beforedent and Käse Aligner (both PETG) showed clinically acceptable ΔE* in all beverages; coffee > tea > cola > artificial saliva staining order for all brands.

FTIR confirms Invisalign = polyurethane (SmartTrack TPU with PETG inner/PU outer), ClearCorrect = polyester outer (ClearQuartz tri-layer with PU core), Beforedent and Käse = PETG; carbamate group polarity of PU drives higher water/pigment absorption than ester group in PETG, explaining systematic color instability of PU-based aligners.

## 세줄요약
4개 브랜드(Invisalign·ClearCorrect·Beforedent·Käse Aligner) 총 704개 샘플을 커피·차·콜라·인공타액에 6시간·12시간 침지 후 색안정성 비교; FTIR 고분자 분석 및 3D 레이저 표면현미경 검사 병행.

Invisalign이 가장 큰 색변화(커피 6시간·12시간 모두 임상적 허용치 ΔE*>3.3 초과; 내면 표면거칠기 Sa=1.97 μm — 파상 평행 홈으로 최고 높음); ClearCorrect는 커피 12시간에만 ΔE*>3.3; Beforedent·Käse Aligner(모두 PETG)는 전 음료에서 허용 범위; 착색 순서 커피>차>콜라>인공타액.

FTIR 분석으로 확인: Invisalign=폴리우레탄(SmartTrack — 방향족 TPU, PU 외층/PETG 내층), ClearCorrect=폴리에스터 외층(ClearQuartz 3층 — PETG 외/PU 코어/PETG 외), Beforedent·Käse=PETG 단일; 카바메이트기(-NHCOO-)의 높은 극성이 PU 기반 교정장치의 수분·색소 흡수를 PETG보다 높게 만드는 원인.

## 1. Document Information
- **Type**: In vitro comparative study
- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health
- **Year**: 2025 (published July 1, 2025)
- **Institution**: Mahidol University Faculty of Dentistry, Bangkok, Thailand
- **Sample**: n=704 (176 per brand), 0.5–0.55 mm thickness flat samples
- **Evidence level**: In vitro (not clinical)

## 2. Key Contributions
- First brand-comparison color study with ≥4 brands including Beforedent and Käse Aligner (Asian market brands)
- FTIR chemical identification of each brand's polymer at the molecular level
- Surface roughness (Sa) quantification linking roughness to staining susceptibility
- 6-hour immersion protocol designed to simulate 1 week of in-vivo beverage contact time

## 3. Methodology
- Samples cut from labial surface of each aligner at central incisor position; 704 round samples (7 mm diameter)
- Beverages: coffee (Nescafe 3g/180ml), original Coca-Cola, ChaTraMue red tea (1 bag/300ml), artificial saliva pH 7.36 (control)
- Immersion: 37±1°C, 6h (=1 week clinical exposure) and 12h (=2 weeks)
- Color: CIE L*a*b* via Ultrascan PRO spectrophotometer; ΔE* calculated; NBS unit = ΔE* × 0.92
- Clinically unacceptable threshold: ΔE* >3.3
- FTIR: Spectrum One, ATR technique, 400–4000 cm⁻¹, 4 cm⁻¹ resolution; only samples with ΔE* >3.3 analyzed
- Surface: 3D laser scanning microscope (LEXT OLS5100, 10x) before and after immersion
- Statistics: Shapiro-Wilks normality → 2-way ANOVA + Bonferroni corrections; 1-way ANOVA + Tukey for surface roughness

## 4. Key Results

**Color change ΔE* (clinically unacceptable >3.3):**

| Brand | Coffee 6h | Coffee 12h | Tea 6h | Tea 12h | Cola | Saliva |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invisalign | **>3.3 ✗** | **>3.3 ✗** | Noticeable | Noticeable | Trace-slight | Trace |
| ClearCorrect | Noticeable | **>3.3 ✗** | Noticeable | Noticeable | Trace-slight | Trace |
| Beforedent | Noticeable | Noticeable | Slight | Slight | Trace | Trace |
| Käse Aligner | Noticeable | Noticeable | Slight | Slight | Trace | Trace |

**Surface roughness inner surface (Sa, μm):**
- Invisalign: **1.97 μm** (significantly higher than all others, p<0.001) — parallel wavy grooves
- Käse Aligner: 0.54 μm
- ClearCorrect: 0.40 μm
- Beforedent: 0.39 μm

**FTIR polymer identification:**
- Invisalign: Aromatic thermoplastic polyurethane (PU) — PETG inner / PU outer (SmartTrack LD30)
- ClearCorrect: Polyester outer (PETG outer / PU core / PETG outer — ClearQuartz tri-layer)
- Beforedent: PETG
- Käse Aligner: PETG

## 5. Limitations
- In vitro; does not account for plaque, temperature cycling, cleaning behaviors, or saliva composition variability
- 6h/12h immersion may not fully simulate continuous intraoral exposure patterns
- Transparency makes surface scanning require gold coating

## 6. Related Work
- Confirms and extends prior studies (Liu & Sun, Bernard & Rompré, Šimunović, Salvatori): Invisalign discolors more than ClearCorrect, which discolors more than PETG-based aligners
- ClearCorrect material update noted: older studies (<2020) reported ClearCorrect as PU; FTIR now confirms polyester outer layer (ClearQuartz tri-layer with PETG outer barrier)
- PU carbamate group (-NHCOO-) more polar than PETG ester group (-COO-) → higher water absorption and hydrogen bonding with pigments

## 7. Glossary
- **ΔE***: total color difference in CIE L*a*b* space; <1.0 clinically imperceptible, 1–3.3 acceptable, >3.3 clinically unacceptable
- **NBS unit**: ΔE* × 0.92; rates changes from "Trace" (0–0.5) to "Very much" (>12.0)
- **Sa**: arithmetical mean surface roughness (μm); higher = more pits/grooves = more stain retention
- **FTIR-ATR**: Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy with Attenuated Total Reflectance; identifies polymer chemistry by vibration peaks
