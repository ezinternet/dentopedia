## Why Ingested

Retrospective clinical study comparing digital vs. conventional workflow outcomes in posterior monolithic zirconia implant crowns — directly quantifies occlusal accuracy improvement with intraoral scanning + auxiliary occlusal device using T-Scan III. Extends → [[digital-workflow/zhou-2021-digital-occlusal-analysis-posterior-implant]] on occlusal force distribution over time and reinforces → [[digital-workflow/digital-workflow]] overview evidence on digital impression accuracy.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study (n=239) at Peking University First Hospital comparing digital workflow (DWG, intraoral scan + CAD/CAM, n=101) vs. conventional workflow (CWG, polyether impression + stone cast, n=138) for posterior monolithic zirconia implant crowns.
DWG showed significantly lower max occlusal deviation, mean occlusal deviation, occlusal adjustment area, occlusion time ratio (all P<0.001), lower implant crown ROF (P=0.004), fewer crowns requiring adjustment (P=0.012), and shorter laboratory time (P<0.001).
Digital workflow incorporating intraoral scanning with auxiliary occlusal device yields superior occlusal accuracy, optimized force distribution, and reduced chairside adjustment burden.

베이징대학교 제1병원에서 후방부 단일 임플란트 지지 모놀리식 지르코니아 크라운 239례(디지털 워크플로우 (Digital Workflow, DWG) n=101, 기존 워크플로우 (Conventional Workflow, CWG) n=138)를 비교한 후향적 연구.

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates statistically significant improvement in occlusal deviation parameters with digital workflow vs. conventional workflow in a large clinical sample (n=239)
- Quantifies occlusal force distribution advantage: lower implant crown ROF, higher adjacent natural tooth ROF (more physiologic loading pattern) with DWG
- Auxiliary occlusal device (2.0 mm PMMA wafer) during intraoral scanning identified as key precision element
- Reduced laboratory time: significantly shorter total lab time and impression/scan time with DWG
- Fewer crowns requiring any occlusal adjustment at insertion (DWG: fewer, CWG: more, P=0.012)
- 6-month follow-up data confirms stability of occlusal outcomes

## Methodology

- **Design**: Retrospective cohort study
- **n**: 239 patients (239 crowns); DWG n=101, CWG n=138
- **Setting**: Peking University First Hospital (single center)
- **Inclusion**: Single osseointegrated implant ≥3.75 mm diameter, monolithic zirconia crown, posterior region (premolar/molar), natural opposing and adjacent teeth, complete records at baseline + 6 months
- **Exclusion**: Parafunctional habits, FPD same arch, active periodontitis (CAL ≥3 mm + BOP ≥30%), TMD, missing data, non-zirconia materials, recent orthodontics (< 2 years)
- **DWG protocol**: Intraoral scan with auxiliary PMMA occlusal device (2.0 mm wafer, maximal intercuspal position), Medit i500 scanner, Medit Link CAD, CAD/CAM milling (Katana zirconia, Kuraray Noritake), self-adhesive resin cement (RelyX Unicem, 3M ESPE)
- **CWG protocol**: Polyether impression (Impregum Penta, 3M ESPE), Type IV stone cast, semi-adjustable articulator, same zirconia/cement protocol
- **Occlusal analysis**: T-Scan III system (Tekscan) — relative occlusal force (ROF), occlusal time ratio, digital occlusal deviation mapping
- **Outcomes**: Max/mean occlusal deviation, occlusal adjustment area, occlusion time ratio, ROF (implant crown vs. adjacent natural tooth), laboratory time, crowns requiring adjustment, articulating paper adjustments
- **Follow-up**: 6 months post-insertion

## Key Results

| Outcome | DWG | CWG | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max occlusal deviation | Lower | Higher | <0.001 |
| Mean occlusal deviation | Lower | Higher | <0.001 |
| Occlusal adjustment area | Lower | Higher | <0.001 |
| Occlusion time ratio | Lower | Higher | <0.001 |
| ROF — implant crown | Lower | Higher | 0.004 |
| ROF — adjacent natural tooth | Higher | Lower | 0.002 |
| Total laboratory time | Shorter | Longer | <0.001 |
| Impression/scan time | Shorter | Longer | <0.001 |
| Crowns requiring adjustment | Fewer | More | 0.012 |

- 6-month follow-up: occlusal outcomes remained stable in both groups; DWG maintained superiority
- DWG: improved force balance between implant and adjacent natural teeth (more physiologic)

## Limitations

- Retrospective design: group allocation by clinical preference/equipment availability (not randomized) → selection bias possible
- Single center, single ethnic population (Beijing) — generalizability uncertain
- No blinding for occlusal adjustments
- Conventional workflow did not use digital occlusal analysis at design stage (limits direct comparison)
- Only monolithic zirconia — cannot generalize to other crown materials
- Short follow-up (6 months) — longer-term occlusal stability unknown

## Glossary

- **디지털 워크플로우 (Digital Workflow, DWG)**: 구내 스캐너로 직접 디지털 인상을 채득하고 CAD/CAM으로 보철물을 제작하는 완전 디지털 과정 (석고 모형 불필요)
- **보조 교합 장치 (Auxiliary Occlusal Device)**: 최대교두감합위를 등록하기 위해 구내 스캔 시 사용하는 맞춤형 아크릴 레진 웨이퍼 (두께 2.0 mm)
- **상대 교합력 (Relative Occlusal Force, ROF)**: T-Scan 시스템으로 측정한 특정 치아의 전체 교합력 대비 비율 (%)
- **T-Scan III**: 디지털 교합 분석 시스템; 힘 분포·교합 시간 순서·중심점 등을 실시간으로 기록
- **교합시간비 (Occlusion Time Ratio, OTR)**: 전체 교합 접촉 중 임플란트 보철물이 교합 접촉하는 시간 비율
- **모놀리식 지르코니아 크라운 (Monolithic Zirconia Crown)**: 베니어 도재 없이 지르코니아 블록 전체로 제작된 단일 재료 크라운
