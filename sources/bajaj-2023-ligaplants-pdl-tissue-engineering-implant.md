## Why Ingested

임플란트 골유착(Osseointegration)의 근본적 한계 — PDL 부재로 인한 충격흡수·고유감각·교합력 분산 결여 — 를 해소하는 조직공학적 접근인 리가플란트(Ligaplant)는 위키에 아직 전용 페이지가 없다. 이 리뷰는 PDL 세포+임플란트 생체재료 복합체의 개념, 임상 고려사항, 현재 증거 범위를 체계적으로 정리하여 [[implants/mathew-2020-biomimetic-pdl-cementum-dental-implants]]의 스코핑 리뷰와 함께 PDL 바이오엔지니어링 evidence base를 구성한다.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of ligaplants (PDL cells + implant biomaterial) searched PubMed/Google Scholar/CENTRAL from Oct 2022; 328 articles relevant to PDL tissue engineering for implants reviewed after screening 450 excluded.

Ligaplants promise shock absorption, proprioception, orthodontic tooth movement potential, and bone remodeling capacity absent in conventional osseointegrated implants; one human trial (Gault 2010) confirmed firm integration without direct bone contact and tooth-equivalent mobility scores.

Clinical translation remains distant — complex culture requirements, nonperiodontal cell contamination risk, unpredictable host acceptance, and high cost are the current barriers; most evidence is from animal models.

## 세줄요약

리가플란트(PDL 세포+임플란트 생체재료) 개념·임상·증거를 정리한 서술적 고찰; PubMed·구글학술·CENTRAL에서 Oct 2022까지 검색, 관련 328편 분석(450편 제외 후).

리가플란트의 장점: 기존 골유착 임플란트가 결여한 충격흡수·고유감각·교정 치아이동·골 리모델링 지원. 첫 인체 시험(Gault 2010): 직접 골접촉 없이 견고 통합, 자연치와 동등한 이동성 점수 확인.

임상 이전(Translation)은 요원 — 세포 배양 복잡성, 비-PDL 세포 오염 위험, 숙주 반응 예측 불가, 고비용이 장벽. 대부분의 증거는 동물 모델에 국한.

## Key Contributions

- Consolidated conceptual framework for ligaplants — PDL cells + biomaterial implant yielding fibrous periodontal-like attachment
- Documented Gault 2010 human trial: ligaplants show mobility scores similar to natural teeth (vs osseointegrated implants = zero mobility)
- Tabulated advantages (shock absorption, proprioception, bone maintenance, bone-graft elimination potential) vs disadvantages (culture complexity, nonperiodontal cell risk, unpredictable host acceptance, cost)
- Described the tissue-engineering pipeline: temperature-responsive culture dishes → cell isolation from mid-root PDL → bioreactor culture on HAP-coated titanium pins → transplantation

## Methodology

- **Design**: Narrative review (literature review with PRISMA flow)
- **Search**: PubMed, Google Scholar, CENTRAL; keywords: ligaplants, PDL, osseointegration, tissue-engineered ligament, implants, tissue engineering, biomaterial; from Oct 2022
- **Included**: methodology studies, case reports on ligaplant concept, success, advantages/disadvantages, properties
- **Excluded**: tissue engineering in oral dentistry broadly (n=450), non-PDL implant tissue engineering

## Key Results

- **Ligaplant definition**: PDL cells combined with implant biomaterial; inserted into extraction socket; initial loose fit to protect PDL cell cushion; achieves firm integration without direct bone contact
- **Gault 2010** (human trial): Human PDL cells from extracted tooth cultured on HAP-coated titanium pin bioreactor → ligaplant transplanted into human jaw; result: firm integration, induced new bone formation, mobility scores equivalent to natural teeth
- **Animal evidence**: Studies from Choi (2000), Lin (2011), Oshima (2014), Nakajima (2016) demonstrated PDL-like tissue, cementum-like layers, Sharpey's fiber insertion, and orthodontic movement response in animal models
- **Advantages**: Shock absorption via micro-movements, proprioception, bone remodeling support, bone grafting elimination potential, natural dentition-like anchoring for orthodontic movement
- **Disadvantages**: Complex and sensitive culture conditions (temperature, timing, cell purity), high cost, PDL-growth unpredictability, nonperiodontal cell growth risk during extended culture, unpredictable host acceptance

## Limitations

- Narrative review without systematic synthesis or quality appraisal
- Most evidence from animal models (rodent, canine, primate, rabbit, swine)
- Single human trial (Gault 2010) with basic mobility-test endpoint only
- No survival/MBL/ISQ clinical data exist for ligaplants in humans

## Glossary

- **Ligaplant**: Combination of PDL cells and implant biomaterial creating a fibrous periodontal-like connection around a dental implant
- **Osseointegration (골유착)**: Direct structural/functional bone-implant connection without intervening fibrous tissue
- **PDL (치주인대)**: Periodontal ligament — fibrous connective tissue between root cementum and alveolar bone; provides shock absorption, proprioception, and sensory function absent in osseointegrated implants
- **HAP (수산화인회석)**: Hydroxyapatite — calcium phosphate ceramic used as implant coating to enhance cell adhesion
- **Osseoperception (골유착감각)**: Residual mechanosensory function transmitted through osseointegrated implants via bone and jaw periosteum, inferior to PDL-mediated proprioception
