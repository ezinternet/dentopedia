## One-line Summary
RCT (n=80 DAPT patients, flapless single-tooth extraction): piezosurgery (PI) achieved normal clotting (≤30 min) in 70% vs 40% with turbine handpiece (TH) (p=0.007), with fewer intraoperative complications (25% vs 47.5%, p=0.036).

## 2. Key Contributions
- First RCT examining piezosurgery vs conventional turbine for flapless extraction in DAPT patients
- Quantifies 30-percentage-point advantage in achieving immediate hemostasis with piezosurgery
- Establishes instrument type as independent predictor of prolonged bleeding (OR 3.10)
- Piezosurgery preferred for high-hemorrhagic-risk patients on dual antiplatelet therapy

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Randomized controlled trial
- Population: 80 patients on DAPT (aspirin + P2Y12 inhibitor, e.g., clopidogrel); single-tooth extraction; equally divided PI (n=40) vs TH (n=40)
- All patients: Low platelet aggregation confirmed, similar coagulation function between groups
- Intervention PI: Piezosurgery for flapless extraction — ultrasonic blade for sulcular incision and PDL severance without flap elevation
- Control TH: Conventional high-speed turbine handpiece with bur for coronal reduction/extraction
- Primary outcome: Immediate intra-alveolar clot formation status (normal ≤30 min vs prolonged >30 min)
- Secondary outcomes: Intraoperative complications, surgical duration, postoperative complications (7 days)
- Statistics: Logistic regression for independent predictors; Chi-square for categorical outcomes

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Normal immediate clotting (≤30 min): PI 70% vs TH 40% (p=0.007)
- Intraoperative complications: PI 25% vs TH 47.5% (p=0.036)
- OR for prolonged bleeding with TH vs PI: 3.10 (95% CI: 1.20–8.00, p=0.019)
- Surgical duration: PI group longer (not specified numerically in abstract)
- Postoperative complications (7 days): No significant difference between groups

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-level data only (full text not PMC-available); exact bleed volumes and clot scoring details not accessible
- Single center; DAPT regimens may vary (aspirin dose, P2Y12 agent type)
- Longer surgical time with PI may be a tradeoff in emergency settings
- Does not address anticoagulant-only patients (warfarin, DOACs)

## 7. Glossary
- **DAPT (Dual Antiplatelet Therapy)**: Concurrent use of two antiplatelet agents (usually aspirin + P2Y12 inhibitor); standard post-ACS/stent protocol
- **Flapless extraction**: Tooth removal without surgical flap elevation; preserves periosteal blood supply
- **PI (Piezosurgery / Piezoelectric instrument)**: Ultrasonic device cutting hard tissue selectively; minimal thermal/mechanical soft-tissue trauma
- **TH (Turbine Handpiece)**: High-speed rotary instrument; conventional bur-based extraction technique
- **Intra-alveolar clotting**: Primary hemostatic plug formation in extraction socket; impaired in DAPT patients due to platelet inhibition
