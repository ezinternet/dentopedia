## Three-line Summary

Comprehensive review (narrative, not systematic) evaluating the clinical significance of apical patency in root canal therapy, drawing on recent meta-analyses and clinical studies to address the longstanding debate over whether patency maneuvers improve or worsen postoperative outcomes.

Evidence from recent meta-analyses suggests patency may actually alleviate postoperative pain intensity rather than exacerbate it, contrary to longstanding clinical anxiety; however, the evidence remains conflicting across individual studies. Preoperative CBCT is identified as essential for identifying complex anatomy (e.g., MB2 canals) that impede patency, and specialized NiTi files with reciprocating motor kinematics enhance the predictability of glide path establishment.

The paper introduces the conceptual distinction between "Anatomical Patency" (respecting original canal morphology) and "Operative Patency" (forceful negotiation beyond the apex), arguing clinicians should prioritize the former. Failure to achieve patency does not always dictate a negative outcome, but maintaining it is associated with improved long-term healing.

## 세줄요약

종합 리뷰(Comprehensive Review, 비체계적) — 근단 개통성(Apical Patency)의 임상적 의의, 술후 통증(Postoperative Pain)과의 상관관계, 해부학적 고려사항, 협상(Negotiation) 기구·모터 키네마틱스(Motor Kinematics)를 기존 메타분석·임상연구를 근거로 종합 검토.

최신 메타분석에 따르면 근단 개통성은 술후 통증을 오히려 완화할 수 있으나(Apical Patency가 통증 악화가 아닌 감소를 유발), 개별 연구 간 증거는 여전히 상충 — 사전 CBCT(Deep Understanding Cone-beam CT)는 MB2 근관 등 복잡 해부학 확인에 필수, 특수 NiTi 파일과 왕복형(Reciprocating) 모터 키네마틱스가 활주로(Glide Path) 형성의 예측가능성을 높인다.

"해부학적 개통성(Anatomical Patency: 원래 형태 존중)"과 "시술적 개통성(Operative Patency: 근단 너머 강압적 협상)"의 개념적 구분을 제시하며, 전자를 우선시할 것을 권고 — 개통성 미달이 항상 나쁜 결과를 의미하지 않으나, 개통성 유지 장기 치유율 향상과 관련.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First comprehensive review dedicated specifically to apical patency — a concept that has been debated for decades but lacked a focused synthesis.
- Introduces the conceptual distinction between "Anatomical Patency" (respecting original morphology) and "Operative Patency" (forceful negotiation beyond the apex), providing a framework for clinical decision-making.
- Synthesizes conflicting evidence on patency and postoperative pain from recent meta-analyses, showing the relationship is more nuanced than the traditional fear that patency increases pain.
- Highlights the role of preoperative CBCT imaging in identifying complex anatomy that impedes patency, and evaluates specialized negotiation instruments and motor kinematics.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Literature review of clinical studies and recent meta-analyses on apical patency.
- Focus areas: (1) correlation between patency maneuvers and postoperative pain, (2) role of preoperative CBCT imaging, (3) efficacy of specialized negotiation instruments and motor kinematics.
- Narrative synthesis approach — not a systematic review with formal inclusion/exclusion criteria or meta-analytic pooling.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Patency and pain**: Recent meta-analyses suggest patency may alleviate postoperative discomfort intensity, contrary to traditional clinical anxiety — though evidence remains conflicting across individual studies.
- **CBCT**: Preoperative CBCT identified as essential for detecting complex anatomy (e.g., MB2 canal) that would otherwise impede patency negotiation.
- **Specialized files**: Use of specialized NiTi files and reciprocating motor modes enhances predictability of glide path establishment.
- **Long-term outcomes**: Failure to achieve patency does not always dictate a negative outcome, but maintaining patency is associated with improved long-term healing.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review (not systematic) — subject to selection bias in cited literature.
- Evidence on patency–pain relationship remains conflicting; further RCTs with standardized protocols needed.
- The Anatomical vs Operative Patency distinction is conceptual and requires validation through prospective clinical studies comparing outcomes of morphology-respecting versus aggressive patency techniques.

## 7. Glossary

- **Apical Patency (근단 개통성)**: The clinical practice of maintaining a small file at or near the apical foramen during root canal preparation to ensure the canal remains open and unobstructed.
- **Anatomical Patency (해부학적 개통성)**: Maintaining patency while respecting the original root canal morphology — the recommended approach.
- **Operative Patency (시술적 개통성)**: Forceful negotiation of instruments beyond the apical foramen — the discouraged approach.
- **Glide Path (활주로)**: A smooth, reproducible path established from the coronal access to the apical foramen before final shaping.
- **MB2 Canal (근심설측 제2근관)**: The second mesiobuccal canal in maxillary molars, often difficult to locate without CBCT.
- **CBCT (Deep Understanding Cone-beam CT)**: Cone-beam computed tomography — three-dimensional imaging essential for identifying complex root canal anatomy.
