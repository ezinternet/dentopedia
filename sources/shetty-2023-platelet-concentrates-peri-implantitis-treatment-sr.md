## Why Ingested

임플란트 주위염(Peri-implantitis) 치료에 혈소판농축제제(Platelet Concentrates, PC — PRP/PRF/CGF)를 보조적으로 쓰는 근거가 위키에 없어 갭을 메우기 위해 인제스트. [[implants/peri-implantitis/hong-2024-peri-implantitis-treatment-protocol-flowchart]]의 단계적 수술 치료 프로토콜에 PC가 어떻게 끼워질 수 있는지 보강하는 자료이며, 재생 전략을 다룬 [[implants/peri-implantitis/su-2026-peri-implantitis-bone-defects-regenerative-strategies]]와도 연결된다. 다만 12편 중 OFD 직접비교는 1편뿐이고 이질성이 커 "promising하지만 결정적이지 않다(preliminary/promising, not conclusive)"는 저자 결론을 그대로 반영해야 한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (Shetty & Menaka 2023, JISP) of platelet concentrates (PRF/CGF/L-PRF) for treating peri-implantitis, searching 5 databases (441 hits, PROSPERO CRD42020202363); only 4 studies met inclusion (2 RCTs, 1 prospective trial, 1 case series; 130 implants total), and only 1 directly compared PC vs. open flap debridement (OFD).

The single PC-vs-OFD RCT (Hamzacebi 2015) found PRF membrane significantly improved BOP, PD, CAL gain, keratinized mucosa gain, and recession vs. OFD alone at 6 months; the other three studies combined PC with graft materials (Bio-Oss, PRF/collagen membranes, xenograft) against varied comparators and also showed clinical improvement, with Isler 2018 reporting the largest BOP reduction (24.32% ± 11.22%) using Bio-Oss + concentrated growth factor (CGF).

High heterogeneity in study design, sample size, surgical technique, and PC preparation protocol precluded meta-analysis; the authors explicitly conclude the evidence is "preliminary" and "promising" but not conclusive due to the lack of comparative RCTs, so PC use in peri-implantitis should not yet be treated as an established adjunct.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(Shetty & Menaka 2023, JISP)로 혈소판농축제제(Platelet Concentrates, PC — PRF/CGF/L-PRF)를 임플란트 주위염(Peri-implantitis) 치료에 사용한 근거를 5개 데이터베이스에서 검색(441편 중 12편 최종선정, PROSPERO CRD42020202363); 최종 포함 4편(RCT 2, 전향적 임상시험 1, 증례군 1, 총 임플란트 130개) 중 개방판막소파술(Open Flap Debridement, OFD)과 직접비교한 연구는 1편뿐.

유일한 PC-vs-OFD RCT(Hamzacebi 2015)는 PRF막이 6개월 시점 탐침시출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP)·탐침깊이(Probing Depth, PD)·임상부착수준(Clinical Attachment Level, CAL) 증가·각화점막(Keratinized Mucosa, KM) 증가·치은퇴축 감소에서 OFD 단독 대비 유의한 개선을 보였고, 나머지 3편은 PC를 골이식재(Bio-Oss 등)와 병용해 다양한 대조군과 비교했으며 역시 임상적 개선을 보였음(Isler 2018 — Bio-Oss+농축성장인자(Concentrated Growth Factor, CGF) 병용군에서 BOP 감소 24.32%±11.22%로 최대).

연구설계·표본크기·수술기법·PC 제조법의 높은 이질성으로 메타분석은 불가했으며, 저자들은 근거를 "예비적(preliminary)"·"유망(promising)"하나 비교 RCT 부족으로 "결정적이지 않다(not conclusive)"고 명시 — 임플란트 주위염 치료의 확립된 보조요법으로 아직 간주할 수 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First systematic review to focus **exclusively** on platelet concentrates (PRP/PRF/L-PRF/CGF) for peri-implantitis treatment outcomes (prior SRs covered PC in general implant therapy, not peri-implantitis specifically).
- Establishes a PICO framework (PC vs. OFD/conventional flap therapy) and searches 5 databases + handsearch (441 records → 12 candidate articles → 4 final inclusions).
- Synthesizes primary outcomes (BOP, PD, MBL) and secondary outcomes (CAL, MR, KM) across heterogeneous study designs, with formal Cochrane risk-of-bias assessment (RevMan 5.4.1).
- Transparently flags the core evidence gap: only 1 of 4 included studies is a direct PC-vs-OFD comparison; the field lacks standardized PC preparation protocols and comparison arms.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: PRISMA-guided systematic review, no meta-analysis (heterogeneity too high).
- **Databases**: PubMed, Google Scholar, ProQuest, Cochrane, Ovid MEDLINE, plus handsearch of bibliographies; English-language articles through Dec 31, 2020.
- **Inclusion**: RCTs, controlled clinical trials, case series, case-control studies; ≥5 patients; ≥6-month follow-up; treatment of peri-implantitis combined with platelet concentrates; human studies only (in-vitro/animal excluded).
- **Screening**: Two independent reviewers, third-reviewer arbitration for discrepancies.
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane Collaboration tool via RevMan 5.4.1, categorized high/unclear/low per domain, synthesized as a risk-of-bias graph.
- **Analysis**: Weighted mean difference (WMD) with 95% CI planned per outcome; I² heterogeneity statistic assessed; meta-analysis abandoned due to high heterogeneity and paucity of well-designed RCTs.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Search yield**: 441 records → 12 articles after title screening → 3 duplicates removed → 9 for abstract screening → 5 excluded (4 animal studies, 1 no-PC-used study) → **4 final included studies**, 130 implants total, follow-up 3–12 months (mean 6 months).
- **Included studies**:
  1. Hamzacebi 2015 (RCT, n=38): PRF membrane vs. OFD — only head-to-head PC-vs-OFD comparison in the review.
  2. Guler 2017 (prospective clinical trial, n=35): PTG+PRF membrane vs. xenograft+collagen membrane+PRF membrane.
  3. Isler 2018 (RCT, n=52): Bio-Oss+CGF vs. Bio-Oss+collagen membrane (Bio-Gide), follow-up to 12 months.
  4. Martins 2019 (case series, n=5): L-PRF + xenograft, no comparison arm.
- **Hamzacebi 2015 (PRF vs. OFD)**: PRF group showed significantly greater improvement in BOP, PD reduction, CAL gain, KM gain, and reduced mucosal recession at 6 months vs. OFD alone.
- **Isler 2018**: Largest BOP reduction (24.32% ± 11.22%) at 6 months using Bio-Oss + CGF; significant MBL improvement with CGF vs. collagen-membrane-only control from baseline to 12 months.
- **Mucosal recession (MR)**: 0.62 ± 0.49 mm baseline → 0.14 ± 0.28 mm at 6 months (statistically significant, p<0.001) in the pooled PRF-treated cohorts — less recession than the ~1 mm typically expected after peri-implant surgery (Heitz-Mayfield).
- **Risk of bias**: Studies rated high-to-moderate quality; randomization explicitly reported only by Isler 2018; blinding of participants/personnel unclear across all studies; outcome-assessor blinding done in only 1 study; 2 studies showed attrition bias.
- **No meta-analysis performed** — heterogeneity in study design, sample size, surgical/decontamination technique, and PC centrifugation protocol was too high.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only **1 of 4** included studies directly answers the review's PICO question (PC vs. OFD); the other 3 use PC combined with grafts against varied comparators, diluting the strength of any pooled inference.
- Small overall evidence base: 4 studies, 130 implants, heavy heterogeneity in PC type (PRP/PRF/L-PRF/CGF), centrifugation protocol, decontamination method, and follow-up duration.
- No meta-analysis was possible; conclusions are narrative/qualitative only.
- Blinding was largely unclear or absent, elevating performance/detection bias risk across the included studies.
- Authors explicitly call the evidence "preliminary" and state a "definitive conclusion is not possible" — future work needs large-scale RCTs with homogeneous comparison groups and standardized PC preparation protocols.

## 6. Related Work

- Cited prior SRs on PC for postextraction socket healing, alveolar socket preservation, periodontal surgery, and intrabony defects — all reporting positive PC effects, motivating this peri-implantitis-specific review.
- Two prior SRs on PRF/PRP for general implant therapy outcomes (not peri-implantitis-specific) are cited as the closest precedent this review updates/narrows.
- Cross-references periodontal PRF literature (Sharma & Pradeep; Lekovic; Aroca; Thamaraiselvan) on intrabony defects and gingival recession to contextualize peri-implant findings.

## 7. Glossary

- **PC (Platelet Concentrate)**: Blood-derived product concentrated in platelets and growth factors; umbrella term covering PRP and PRF.
- **PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma)**: Anticoagulated, centrifuged plasma fraction enriched in platelets.
- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)**: Non-anticoagulated centrifuged fibrin clot enriched in platelets and leukocytes; can be pure (P-PRF) or leukocyte-rich (L-PRF).
- **CGF (Concentrated Growth Factor)**: A PRF variant produced with a specific variable-speed centrifugation protocol, yielding a denser fibrin matrix.
- **OFD (Open Flap Debridement)**: Conventional surgical access flap for mechanical debridement of peri-implant/periodontal defects, used here as the comparator.
- **BOP (Bleeding on Probing)**: Clinical marker of peri-implant/periodontal inflammation.
- **PD (Probing Depth)**: Depth of the peri-implant/periodontal sulcus/pocket on probing.
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level)**: Radiographic measure of crestal bone position relative to a fixed reference (e.g., implant shoulder).
- **CAL (Clinical Attachment Level)**: Distance from a fixed reference point to the base of the probeable sulcus/pocket.
- **MR (Mucosal Recession)**: Apical shift of the peri-implant mucosal margin.
- **KM (Keratinized Mucosa)**: Width of keratinized peri-implant soft tissue.
- **PTG (Porous Titanium Granules)**: A bone graft substitute material used in one included study.
