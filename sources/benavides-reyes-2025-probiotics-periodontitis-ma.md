## Why Ingested

Complements [[periodontics/host-modulation/mendoca-2024-probiotics-nspt-nma]] with a parallel MA specifically quantifying plaque index (PI) and BOP responses to probiotics in both gingivitis and periodontitis — includes 24 RCTs (951 participants) and reveals time-dependency of probiotic benefits, providing complementary evidence to the NMA.

## Three-line Summary

Meta-analysis of 24 RCTs (951 participants; 10 gingivitis, 14 periodontitis) evaluating probiotics as adjunct to periodontal therapy; in periodontitis, PI and BOP were significantly reduced, but PPD effect was significant only in shorter follow-up studies.

Periodontitis: PI (SMD=−0.35; 95% CI [−0.54;−0.15], p=0.001) and BOP (SMD=−0.32; 95% CI [−0.58;−0.05], p=0.021) significantly lower in probiotic group; PPD overall non-significant (SMD=−0.25, p=0.077) but significant in ≤12-week studies (SMD=−0.42, p=0.019).

In gingivitis, PI and BOP showed non-significant trends favoring probiotics, with high heterogeneity (I²=94–96%) — evidence quality is low and clinical relevance of effect sizes remains uncertain.

## 세줄요약

24편 RCT(951명; 치은염 10, 치주염 14)의 메타분석; 치주염에서 프로바이오틱스 보조 사용은 PI와 BOP를 유의하게 감소시켰으나 PPD 개선은 단기 추적(≤12주) 연구에서만 유의했다.

치주염: PI(SMD=−0.35, p=0.001), BOP(SMD=−0.32, p=0.021) 유의 감소; PPD는 전체 비유의(SMD=−0.25, p=0.077), 단기 연구에서만 유의(SMD=−0.42, p=0.019).

치은염에서는 PI·BOP 모두 비유의 경향(높은 이질성 I²=94–96%) — 근거 질은 낮고, 임상적 유의성은 불확실.

- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health. 2025;25(1):490
- **PMID**: 40186219 | **PMCID**: PMC11971800 | **DOI**: 10.1186/s12903-025-05888-5
- **Protocol**: PROSPERO CRD42024509910
- **Study type**: Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA; GRADE evidence quality)
- **Full text**: PMC open access

## 2. Key Contributions

- First MA to stratify probiotic effects by disease type (gingivitis vs. periodontitis) AND by follow-up time.
- Reveals time-dependency: PI improves at longer follow-up (>8 weeks); BOP improves at shorter follow-up (≤12 weeks).
- Demonstrates gingivitis is less responsive to probiotic therapy than periodontitis.
- Identifies L. reuteri, L. rhamnosus, and combinations as most frequently studied.

## 3. Methodology

- **Search**: PubMed (Scopus, Cochrane, EMBASE), March 2024; English only; 10 years maximum
- **Inclusion**: RCTs, gingivitis/periodontitis patients, probiotic vs control/placebo, ≥9 participants/group, CONSORT-compliant
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane RoB-2 tool; GRADE evidence quality
- **Outcomes**: PI (%), BOP (%), PPD (mm)
- **Statistics**: Hedges formula (SMD), random-effects model (REML); subgroup by follow-up time

## 4. Key Results

**Periodontitis (14 studies):**
- **PI**: SMD=−0.35 (95% CI [−0.54;−0.15], p=0.001) — significant; larger in studies >8-week follow-up
- **BOP**: SMD=−0.32 (95% CI [−0.58;−0.05], p=0.021) — significant; only in ≤12-week studies
- **PPD**: SMD=−0.25 (95% CI [−0.53;+0.03], p=0.077) — overall non-significant; significant in ≤12-week studies (SMD=−0.42; p=0.019)

**Gingivitis (10 studies):**
- PI: SMD=−0.51 (95% CI [−1.37;+0.35], p=0.248) — non-significant; high heterogeneity I²=94.83%
- BOP: SMD=−0.49 (95% CI [−1.66;+0.68], p=0.409) — non-significant; I²=95.70%

**Evidence quality**: Low (GRADE) — risk of bias, imprecision, and potential publication bias

## 5. Limitations

- English-only search may miss relevant studies
- Low quality of evidence (GRADE low to very low)
- Heterogeneous dosing/strains/routes/follow-up periods
- Modest effect sizes (SMD 0.3–0.4) — clinical relevance uncertain
- Only 24 studies total; PI/BOP outcomes, not CAL (key periodontitis endpoint)

## 6. Glossary

- **SMD**: Standardized mean difference (Hedges formula)
- **PI**: Plaque index
- **BOP**: Bleeding on probing
- **PPD**: Probing pocket depth
- **REML**: Restricted maximum likelihood (random-effects estimation)
- **GRADE**: Evidence quality grading system
