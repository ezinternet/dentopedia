## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/implants/isq/lee-2024-primary-implant-stability-isq-devices-invitro]]은 체외(골밀도·길이) 조건에서 Osstell(ISQ)과 Anycheck(IST)을 비교했고 [[wiki/implants/isq/hong-2025-comparative-analysis-of-novel-damping]]은 구강 내 DCA 기기 간 일치도만 다뤘다. 본 연구는 즉시 구치부 식립이라는 임상적으로 어려운 상황에서 두 기기를 식립 시와 인상 채득 시의 두 시점에 걸쳐 직접 비교해, 골유착 진행에 따른 ISQ–IST 일치도 변화(69.2%→92.3%)와 임상적 상호교환성 한계를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-center study (45 patients, 52 implants) of immediate flapless single-molar placement comparing Osstell ISQ (RFA) versus AnyCheck (IST/DCA) stability at implant placement and prosthetic impression.
Both values rose significantly (ISQ 76.0±6.8→81.8±5.2, IST 77.6±6.0→82.5±3.7; both p<.001; Cohen's d 1.09 and 0.75); categorical agreement at the ≥75 cutoff improved 69.2%→92.3% and Bland–Altman bias was −1.54/−0.65 (n.s.).
Devices were clinically comparable once osseointegration was established but not fully interchangeable (LoA still ≈±10 at impression); the authors advise using the same device for longitudinal monitoring of an individual implant.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단일기관 연구(45명, 52개 임플란트): 발치 즉시 플랩리스 단일 구치부 식립에서 공명주파수분석 (Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA) 기반 Osstell 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)와 감쇠능 분석 (Damping Capacity Analysis, DCA) 기반 AnyCheck 임플란트 안정성 검사 (Implant Stability Test, IST)를 식립 시와 인상 채득 시 비교했다.
두 지표 모두 유의하게 증가(ISQ 76.0±6.8→81.8±5.2, IST 77.6±6.0→82.5±3.7; 모두 p<.001; Cohen's d 1.09/0.75)했고, 범주 일치도(역치 ≥75)는 69.2%→92.3%로 개선되었으며 블랜드-알트만 (Bland–Altman) 편향은 −1.54/−0.65(유의하지 않음)였다.
골유착이 진행된 뒤 두 기기는 임상적으로 비교 가능했으나 완전히 상호교환 가능하진 않았다(인상 시 일치한계 ≈±10); 개별 임플란트 종단 추적에는 동일 기기 사용을 권고했다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First clinical (intraoral) head-to-head of RFA (Osstell ISQ) and DCA (AnyCheck IST) specifically in immediately placed single molar implants using a flapless technique, measured at both placement and prosthetic impression.
- Quantifies that both devices track the same upward stability trajectory with large effect sizes (Cohen's d 1.09 for ISQ, 0.75 for IST) and that inter-device agreement improves as osseointegration progresses (categorical agreement 69.2%→92.3%; 95% limits of agreement narrowing from ≈28 to ≈16 units).
- Shows the devices are clinically comparable once osseointegration is established but explicitly NOT fully interchangeable, and recommends consistent use of the same instrument for longitudinal monitoring of an individual implant.
- Highlights a practical advantage of AnyCheck in flapless immediate placement: percussion-based measurement with the healing abutment in place (no SmartPeg removal/re-attachment), reducing chair time and potential soft-tissue/bone–implant-interface microtrauma.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective, single-center chart review (Inje University Sanggye Paik Hospital), 2020–2022; IRB-approved with waived informed consent.
- **n**: 45 patients (18 male, 27 female; mean age 69.1 years), 52 implants (24 maxillary, 28 mandibular); extraction indications: periodontal disease 18, caries 14, residual roots 9, fracture 11. Positions #16(7), #17(5), #26(6), #27(6), #36(10), #37(5), #46(11), #47(2).
- **Implants**: bone/tissue-level SLA or SLActive-type surfaces (TS III SA n=31, SS III SA n=3, TS III SOI n=2, SimpleLine n=13, SQ n=3); diameters 4.5–6.0 mm (5.0 mm in 41/52); lengths 7.0–10.0 mm. Bone grafting at 33/52 sites.
- **Timing**: stability measured on the day of placement and on the day of prosthetic impression (mean interval 84.3±29.4 days ≈ 2.8 months); impression timing set by surgeon judgment (bone condition + insertion torque), not by ISQ/IST values. Mean post-restoration follow-up 42.0±17.7 months.
- **Measurements**: Osstell ISQ — SmartPeg, 4 directions (mesial/buccal/distal/lingual) averaged; AnyCheck — single buccal percussion of the healing abutment, 6 automated taps → one IST value; ISQ measured first, then IST, by a single experienced surgeon.
- **Outcomes/statistics**: Shapiro–Wilk normality; paired t-test with Cohen's d (95% CI) for change; Passing–Bablok regression and Pearson correlation; Bland–Altman bias and 95% LoA; categorical agreement at stability cutoffs ≥75 (primary) and ≥70 (sensitivity) with percent agreement and Cohen's κ (Landis & Koch); SPSS 25.0 and R 4.5.2.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **All 52 implants clinically successful**; no failures or complications over mean 42.0±17.7 months after prosthetic restoration. No missing stability data.
- **Stability increased** (Table 1): ISQ 76.0±6.8 → 81.8±5.2 (p<.001; Cohen's d 1.09, 95% CI 0.74–1.43); IST 77.6±6.0 → 82.5±3.7 (p<.001; d 0.75, 95% CI 0.44–1.06). SDs decreased over time (ISQ −24%, IST −38%), indicating convergence toward a uniformly high stability level.
- **Correlation**: ISQ–IST positive at both time points — r=0.379 (p=.006) at placement; r=0.601 (p<.001) at impression. Passing–Bablok: IST = 13.00 + 0.857×ISQ (placement); IST = 27.67 + 0.667×ISQ (impression). The flatter slope reflects the restricted stability range (~70–90) at impression, not disagreement.
- **Bland–Altman**: mean bias −1.54 at placement (p=.126) and −0.65 at impression (p=.268), neither significant; 95% LoA width narrowed from ≈28 units to ≈16 units. At impression the LoA still spanned ≈±10 units, so the devices are not fully interchangeable.
- **Categorical agreement, ≥75 cutoff** (Table 2): at placement — both stable 25, both unstable 11, ISQ-only stable 6, IST-only stable 10 → 69.2% (95% CI 55.7–80.1), κ 0.341 (0.074–0.607, fair). At impression — both stable 46, both unstable 2, ISQ-only stable 0, IST-only stable 4 → 92.3% (81.8–97.0), κ 0.469 (0.019–0.920, moderate). More implants were IST-only stable than ISQ-only at placement (10 vs 6), suggesting different early sensitivity profiles.
- **Sensitivity, ≥70 cutoff** (Table 3): agreement 75.0% at placement and 98.1% at impression; κ −0.134 and 0.000 (kappa paradox — nearly all implants classified stable at impression, CI not reliably estimable).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Small sample (52 implants) → wide 95% CIs for agreement indices; at impression only 2 implants were unstable, giving κ 0.469 a very wide CI (0.019–0.920).
- Measurement procedures differ: Osstell averages 4 directions whereas AnyCheck measures only the buccal aspect; the effect on agreement cannot be excluded.
- No direct biomechanical/histologic validation (removal torque, histology), so true osseointegration could not be independently established.
- No stratified analyses by implant diameter/length, insertion site, or healing interval; insertion torque and bone quality were not consistently documented, and the retrospective single-center design lacked power for subgroups.
- Two-time-point retrospective design precluded trajectory-based analysis; prospective multi-time-point studies are needed to compare temporal dynamics directly.

## 7. Glossary

- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: Osstell resonance-frequency-analysis stability value on a 1–100 scale.
- **IST (Implant Stability Test value)**: AnyCheck damping-capacity-analysis stability value.
- **RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis)**: non-contact stability measurement via the bending resonance of an attached SmartPeg (Osstell).
- **DCA (Damping Capacity Analysis)**: percussion-based stability measurement from the implant's vibration decay (AnyCheck, Periotest).
- **Bland–Altman analysis / LoA (limits of agreement)**: method-comparison approach quantifying mean bias and the range within which differences between two devices lie.
- **Cohen's κ**: chance-corrected agreement statistic for categorical classifications (here, stable vs unstable).
