## Three-line Summary

Clinical review (Gen Dent 2015;63(1):48-52) on preventing LAST in pediatric dental patients through proper maximum recommended dose calculations, covering Clark's Rule, Rule of 25, and mg/lb body-weight methodology; notes that LAST occurs more frequently in children than adults, particularly with 3% mepivacaine.

Reviews mechanisms and signs/symptoms of LA toxicity; emphasizes that physiologic and anatomic differences (lower body weight, immature metabolism) make children especially vulnerable; provides practical dosing calculation tools to prevent overdose in dental practice.

Abstract-only; a practical clinical reference for pediatric dental LA dosing by Moore PA and Hersh EV — two of the most-cited authors in dental anesthesiology.

## 세줄요약

소아 치과 국소마취제 최대권장용량 계산 임상 리뷰 (Gen Dent 2015, 48-52) — Clark's Rule, Rule of 25, mg/lb 체중 기반 계산법; 소아 LAST 발생 빈도 성인보다 높고 3% 메피바카인 (Mepivacaine) 특히 위험.

소아의 생리적·해부학적 차이(저체중, 미성숙 대사)로 취약; 과용량 예방을 위한 실무 계산 도구 제공.

abstract-only; 치과 마취학 최다 인용 저자(Moore PA, Hersh EV)의 소아 치과 LA 용량 표준 참고.
