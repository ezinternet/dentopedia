## Three-line Summary

Narrative review article describing biological events in the periodontal ligament and alveolar bone in response to application of orthodontic forces published in The Scientific World Journal (2015). Authors synthesized literature on cellular and molecular mechanisms underlying bone remodeling during orthodontic tooth movement. Examined vascular, inflammatory, and cellular responses to mechanical loading that orchestrate osteoclast and osteoblast activity in compression and tension zones.

Orthodontic force application causes differential vascular and cellular responses: pressure-side exhibits vasoconstriction and ischemia triggering osteoclast recruitment, while tension-side shows increased vascularity and osteoblast activation. Cytokines (interleukin-1, tumor necrosis factor-alpha, prostaglandin E₂) and neuropeptides mediate these spatial-temporal responses. Optimal orthodontic forces maintain PDL viability and microvascular function without causing hyalinization; excessive forces induce PDL hyalinization and delayed tooth movement.

Review is limited to basic science literature without direct clinical application. In vitro and animal studies do not fully recapitulate the complex multifactorial loading conditions, individual biological variability, and long-term effects seen in clinical orthodontics. Force magnitude thresholds are not standardized across individuals or tooth types, limiting practical clinical guidance for force optimization in heterogeneous populations.

## 세줄요약

교정력 적용 시 치주인대(PDL)·치조골의 생물학적 사건 서술적 고찰 (Scientific World Journal 2015). PDL 혈관·세포·분자 메커니즘 종합. 압축·장력 부위 osteoclast/osteoblast 활성화 조절 설명.

압박측: 혈관 수축·허혈→ osteoclast 모집; 장력측: 혈관↑→ osteoblast 활성화. 사이토카인(IL-1, TNF-α, PGE₂)·신경펩타이드 매개. 최적 교정력은 PDL 생활성·미세혈관 유지, 과도한 힘은 유리화(hyalinization)→ 이동 지연.

기초과학 중심, 임상 적용 부족. In vitro/동물 다변수 부하 조건·개인 생물학 변이·장기 효과 미재현. 힘 임계값 개인/치아별 미표준화로 임상 인도 제한.
