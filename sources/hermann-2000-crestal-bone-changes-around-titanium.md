## Why Ingested

Foundational canine histometry showing that the implant–abutment interface (microgap) position, not the surgical approach, sets the level of crestal bone resorption in 2-piece implants — the mechanistic premise behind platform switching, cited alongside the clinical MBL evidence → [[implants/mbl/lazzara-2006-platform-switching-new-concept]].

## Three-line Summary

Randomized canine study (59 SLA titanium implants, 5 foxhounds, 6 subgroups, non-submerged vs submerged, 1- vs 2-piece, unloaded, 6 months, non-decalcified histology) comparing crestal bone levels by rough/smooth border and interface (microgap) position.

In 1-piece implants the first bone-to-implant contact (fBIC) sat within 0.20 mm of the rough/smooth border; in 2-piece implants with the interface at or below the crest, the microgap set the resorption (interface-to-crestal bone 1.25–1.68 mm), while an interface 1 mm above the crest gave 2.64 mm with the fBIC at the r/s border; submerged vs non-submerged technique made no difference.

Animal, unloaded data: microgap position and surface design determine early crestal bone change, supporting a coronally placed or inwardly shifted interface — but results do not transfer directly to loaded human implants.

## 세줄요약

무작위 개 실험 (SLA 티타늄 임플란트 59개, 폭스하운드 5마리, 6개 하위군; 비매몰 vs 매몰, 1-piece vs 2-piece; 비하중, 6개월; 비탈회 조직계측): 거친면/매끈면 경계 (rough/smooth border)와 임플란트-지대주 접합부 (interface, 미세간극, microgap) 위치에 따른 치조정 골수준 비교.

1-piece 임플란트에서는 첫 골-임플란트 접촉 (first Bone-to-Implant Contact, fBIC)이 r/s 경계 0.20 mm 이내였고, 접합부가 치조정 이하인 2-piece 임플란트에서는 microgap이 골흡수량을 결정 (접합부-치조정 골 거리 1.25–1.68 mm); 접합부가 치조정 1 mm 상방이면 2.64 mm에 fBIC은 r/s 경계; 매몰 여부는 영향 없음.

동물·비하중 자료: microgap 위치와 표면 디자인이 초기 치조정 골변화를 결정 — 접합부를 상방 또는 내측으로 이동시키는 설계를 뒷받침하나, 하중이 걸린 사람 임플란트로 직접 외삽은 어려움.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: J Periodontol 2000;71(9):1412-24

- **Institution**: Department of Periodontics, University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio, USA

## 2. Key Contributions

- Side-by-side histometric comparison of 1- vs 2-piece and non-submerged vs submerged implants in one model
- 1-piece: fBIC governed by the rough/smooth border (within 0.20 mm)
- 2-piece: interface at/below crest determines resorption; interface 1 mm above crest puts fBIC at the r/s border
- Crestal bone change is independent of submerged vs non-submerged technique

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: randomized animal (canine mandible), split into 6 subgroups (A–F)
- **n**: 59 implants, 5 foxhounds; smooth machined coronal part + SLA apical part
- **Follow-up**: 6 months (abutment connection at 3 months in submerged groups); unloaded
- **Outcome**: non-decalcified histology of peri-implant crestal bone level

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Type | Configuration | Interface–crestal bone (mm) | r/s border → fBIC (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, B | 1-piece, r/s at crest / 1 mm below | fBIC within 0.20 mm of r/s | — |
| C | 2-piece, non-submerged, interface at crest | 1.68 ± 0.19 | 0.39 ± 0.23 |
| D | submerged, interface at crest | 1.57 ± 0.22 | 0.28 ± 0.21 |
| E | submerged, interface 1 mm above | 2.64 ± 0.24 | 0.06 ± 0.27 |
| F | submerged, interface 1 mm below | 1.25 ± 0.40 | 0.89 ± 0.41 |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest; full methods not reviewed
- Canine model, unloaded, 6 months — load effects not tested
- Small n (5 animals); SLA-coated design only
- The companion study ([[implants/mbl/hermann-2001-influence-size-microgap-crestal]]) addressed microgap size and component movement
