## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study (Int J Implant Dent 2026, n=91 IFCDs in 72 patients, mean follow-up 6.8 years, up to 17 years) evaluating technical and biological complications of implant-supported fixed complete dentures (IFCDs). Overall IFCD survival was 92.3%; veneer fracture–free survival was significantly higher for ceramic-veneered (CV) versus resin-veneered (RV) IFCDs (91.3% vs 38.1% at 10 years, p=0.0094). Titanium–resin IFCDs exhibited a 4.25-fold overall complication risk (HR=4.25, p=0.0015) and 7.11-fold veneer fracture risk (HR=7.11, p=0.0029) compared to base metal alloy–ceramic, establishing material selection as the dominant modifiable factor for long-term maintenance burden.

## 세줄요약

후향적 코호트(Int J Implant Dent 2026, n=91개 IFCD·72명, 평균 추적 6.8년, 최대 17년) — 임플란트 지지 완전 고정성 보철물(IFCD) 기술적·생물학적 합병증 및 소재 비교. 전체 IFCD 생존율 92.3%; 비니어 파절 없는 생존율 10년 CV 91.3% vs RV 38.1%로 유의차(p=0.0094). 티타늄-레진 IFCD는 금속-세라믹 대비 전체 합병증 4.25배(p=0.0015)·비니어 파절 7.11배(p=0.0029) 위험 — 소재 선택이 장기 유지 관리 부담의 핵심 수정 가능 인자.
