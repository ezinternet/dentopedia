## Why Ingested

[[oral-surgery/al-maawi-2021-prf-extraction-socket-healing-sr]]에서 단일 핵심 인용 논문으로 언급된 T1DM 발치와 PRGF 임상 연구. 기존 위키에는 당뇨 발치와에서 실제 상피화(epithelialization) 타임라인과 치유지수 수치를 제시하는 **임상 데이터 페이지가 없었다**. Mozzati 2013은 T1DM 환자에서 21일 추적 4시점(3·7·14·21일) 치유지수를 정량화해 발치와 폐쇄 속도 기준값을 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective split-mouth study (34 T1DM patients undergoing bilateral homologous extraction) comparing PRGF-treated vs. naturally-healing sockets at 4 postoperative checkups over 21 days; outcomes: Healing Index, residual socket volume, VAS, complications.

PRGF resulted in significantly smaller residual socket volumes and better Healing Indices from days 3 to 14 (p not specified per timepoint, but group-level significance reported); patients with glycemia ≥240 mg/dL showed worse Healing Index and smaller socket volume decrease.

Conclusion: PRGF accelerates socket epithelialization and tissue maturation in T1DM patients; glycemic control (HbA1c, fasting glucose) significantly modulates healing capacity; poorly controlled DM (≥240 mg/dL) is distinct from well-controlled DM in healing trajectory.

## 세줄요약

후향적 분할구강 연구 (T1DM 환자 34명, 동측 양쪽 발치) — PRGF 처치 vs. 자연치유 발치와를 21일간 4시점(3·7·14·21일)에 치유지수(Healing Index)·잔류 발치와 용적·VAS·합병증으로 평가.

PRGF군에서 잔류 발치와 용적 유의하게 감소, 치유지수는 3–14일에 유의하게 우수; 혈당 ≥240 mg/dL 환자 소집단에서는 치유지수 악화 및 발치와 용적 감소 미미.

결론: PRGF는 T1DM 발치와에서 상피화(epithelialization)와 조직 성숙을 가속하고; 혈당 조절(HbA1c, 공복혈당)이 치유 속도를 크게 결정하며; 불량 혈당 조절(≥240 mg/dL)은 잘 조절된 당뇨와 다른 치유 궤적을 보임.

## 1. Document Information

- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.joms.2013.10.010 (published online Oct 30, 2013)
- **Design**: Retrospective split-mouth study
- **Institution**: SIOM Oral Surgery and Implantology Center, Turin; University of Turin
- **Funding**: Not stated
- **COI**: Not stated

## 2. Key Contributions

- First (cited as of Al-Maawi 2021) clinical study to report PRGF efficacy specifically in T1DM post-extraction sockets with 21-day granular follow-up at 4 time points.
- Establishes that poorly controlled DM (glycemia ≥240 mg/dL) is a distinct subgroup with worse healing — important threshold for clinical risk stratification.
- End Organ Disease Score incorporated as a covariate — multi-organ complication burden modulates outcomes.
- Documents 21-day follow-up as the observation window for epithelial socket closure in T1DM patients.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Population**: 34 T1DM patients (insulin-dependent), January–December 2012; required contemporary bilateral homologous extractions
- **Intervention**: PRGF (Anitua protocol) placed in one socket; contralateral socket healed naturally (each patient = own control)
- **Outcome measures**: Healing Index (soft tissue closure score), residual socket volume (indirect measure of epithelialization), VAS pain, postsurgical complications, patient questionnaire
- **Covariates**: Hyperglycemia level, HbA1c, End Organ Disease Score, smoking habits
- **Follow-up**: 4 checkups over 21 days (days 3, 7, 14, 21)
- **Statistics**: Wilcoxon test (paired continuous), Mann-Whitney test (independent), chi-square / Fisher (categorical)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Healing Index days 3–14**: PRGF group significantly better than control (specific p-values per timepoint not reproduced in available abstract; group significance stated)
- **Residual socket volume days 3–14**: PRGF group significantly smaller (faster closure)
- **Hyperglycemia ≥240 mg/dL subgroup**: Worse Healing Index AND smaller socket volume decrease — distinct healing impairment threshold
- **Patient questionnaire**: Unanimously in favor of PRGF treatment
- **Complications**: Not separately quantified in available data; no major complications implied

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective design — allocation not prospectively randomized (split-mouth but retrospective assignment)
- Small sample (n=34); single center, single operator team
- Only T1DM (insulin-dependent); not generalizable to T2DM directly
- No data beyond 21 days — bone remodeling outcomes not captured
- PRGF (Anitua) protocol specifically — not directly comparable to L-PRF or A-PRF

## 7. Glossary

- **PRGF**: Plasma Rich in Growth Factor — Anitua protocol first-generation platelet concentrate; uses anticoagulant; distinct from PRF/L-PRF (no anticoagulant, second-gen)
- **T1DM**: Type 1 diabetes mellitus (insulin-dependent); autoimmune beta-cell destruction
- **End Organ Disease Score**: Complication burden index for diabetic patients (nephropathy, retinopathy, neuropathy, cardiovascular)
- **Healing Index**: Ordinal scale for socket soft-tissue healing (similar to Landry index)
- **Residual socket volume**: Indirect surrogate for epithelialization rate — smaller volume = faster closure
