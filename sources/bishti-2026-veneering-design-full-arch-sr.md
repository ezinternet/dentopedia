## Why Ingested

No held systematic review specifically compared veneering design (monolithic vs micro-veneered vs fully veneered) outcomes for all-ceramic full-arch implant-supported prostheses, with quantitative chipping incidence and success/survival estimates. This provides the critical 3-year benchmark for full-arch all-ceramic complications stratified by ceramic design type.

[[implants/full-arch/bishti-2026-veneering-design-full-arch-sr]]
## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 12 studies on all-ceramic implant-supported FDPs (3-4-unit and full-arch), comparing monolithic, micro-veneered, and fully veneered designs; prospective clinical studies, ≥1yr, no language/date restrictions.

3-year success rate 89.36% (3-4-unit) and 75.56% (full-arch); 3-year survival 98.64% and 96.45% respectively; monolithic/micro-veneered 3-4-unit had significantly lower chipping than fully veneered (p<0.0001); annual chipping rate 2.1% (3-4-unit) and 6.9% (full-arch).

Monolithic/micro-veneered preferred for short-span; full-arch all-ceramic requires caution — high complication rates and limited evidence; conclusions restricted to 3Y-TZP zirconia >1000 MPa.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First SR to quantify veneering design influence on all-ceramic full-arch implant prostheses
- 3-year full-arch success rate 75.56% — notably lower than 3-4-unit 89.36%: full-arch ceramic is a high-complication context
- Annual chipping rate 6.9% for full-arch (vs 2.1% for 3-4-unit): span length dramatically increases complication risk
- Monolithic/micro-veneered significantly better for chipping in 3-4-unit spans; full-arch design comparison not feasible (insufficient data)
- Scope limited to 3Y-TZP >1000 MPa: multilayer zirconia and other ceramics not covered

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract only — individual study list, heterogeneity, follow-up durations not available
- Veneering design comparison feasible only for 3-4-unit; full-arch veneering comparison not calculable
- Restricted to 3Y-TZP >1000 MPa; no multilayer or non-zirconia ceramics
- Prospective studies only; may exclude large retrospective datasets
