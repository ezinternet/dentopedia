## Three-line Summary

Retrospective single-centre study (Seoul National University Dental Hospital, implants placed 2014-2019, ≥5-year follow-up): 316 tapered-straight-tapered bone-level SLA implants (Evertis, formerly Luna) in 186 patients; MBL analysed on 171 implants with panoramic radiographs.

Cumulative survival was 98.1% (6 late failures, 3.4-6.7 years after placement, mean follow-up 7.0±1.36 years); no variable was a significant survival risk factor in the mixed-effects Cox model (DM HR 4.670, p=0.059; survival 94.3% with DM vs 98.9% without). Mean MBL was 0.59±1.36 mm mesial and 0.68±1.36 mm distal; DM (coeff 0.614, p=0.026), 2-staged surgery and shallower implant placement depth were associated with more mesial MBL.

Survival was high but the study was underpowered for risk factors (wide CIs, 6 events), early failures were excluded, and MBL came from panoramic radiographs read by one examiner; DM patients warrant closer MBL monitoring.

## 세줄요약

서울대학교치과병원 단일기관 후향 연구(2014-2019 식립, 추적 ≥5년): 테이퍼-직선-테이퍼 bone-level SLA 임플란트(Evertis, 구 Luna) 316개(환자 186명); 변연골 소실(MBL)은 파노라마 방사선 사진으로 171개 분석.

누적 생존율 98.1% (후기 실패 6개, 식립 후 3.4-6.7년, 평균 추적 7.0±1.36년); 혼합효과 Cox 모델에서 유의한 위험인자 없음 (당뇨 HR 4.670, p=0.059; 생존율 당뇨 94.3% vs 비당뇨 98.9%). 평균 MBL 근심 0.59±1.36 mm, 원심 0.68±1.36 mm; 당뇨(계수 0.614, p=0.026), 2회법 수술, 얕은 식립 깊이가 근심 MBL 증가와 연관.

생존율은 높으나 사건 수(6개)가 적어 위험인자 검정력이 부족하고(넓은 CI), 조기 실패는 제외되었으며 MBL은 한 명이 파노라마로 측정; 당뇨 환자는 MBL 면밀 관찰 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions
1. Long-term (≥5 years) survival and MBL data for a Korean-manufactured tapered-straight-tapered implant; a prior report on the same implant (cumulative survival 97.0%) included many <5-year follow-ups and had no MBL data (per the paper).
2. Mixed-effects Cox analysis of 12 candidate factors for late failure, handling within-patient clustering.
3. Linear mixed-effects model (backward stepwise) for mesial and distal MBL including implant placement depth (IPD).

## 3. Methodology
- Design: retrospective cross-sectional (self-described), observational reporting guideline; Dental Implant Center, Seoul National University Dental Hospital; placement January 2014 to December 2019; IRB approved, consent waived.
- Implant: bone-level, internal, SLA surface, tapered-straight-tapered macro-design (Evertis; Shinhung, Seoul).
- Inclusion: ≥5 years follow-up after placement. Excluded: incomplete records, early implant failure (removal before final prosthesis). MBL analysis additionally required ≥5-year digital panoramic radiographs and excluded implants failed within 5 years or not assessable.
- Survival definition: implant and fixed prosthesis present in the mouth regardless of biological or mechanical complications; failure = implant removal (clinical record or panoramic radiograph).
- Variables: sex, age (<40, 40-60, >60), DM, smoking, placement region, jaw, diameter (narrow 3.5; standard 4.0/4.5; wide 5.0/6.0 mm), length (short 7.0/8.5; standard 10.0/11.5 mm), 1- vs 2-staged surgery, immediate placement, bone augmentation, prosthetic splinting; IPD added for MBL.
- MBL: baseline panoramic radiograph (immediately or within 2 weeks after placement) vs latest panoramic at ≥5 years, measured from implant coronal end to first bone-to-implant contact, calibrated to fixture length; single examiner, one session.
- Statistics: Kaplan-Meier with 95% CI; mixed-effects Cox model; Mann-Whitney / Kruskal-Wallis; linear mixed-effects with backward stepwise selection; p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results
- Sample: 316 implants / 186 patients; 53 implants (16.8%) in DM patients, 22 (7.0%) in smokers; 218 (69.0%) in patients >60 years.
- Survival: 98.1% overall (6 failures; failure at 3.4-6.7 years); mean follow-up 7.0±1.36 years. Causes: fixture fracture (2) and peri-implantitis (4).
- Survival by subgroup: DM 94.3% vs non-DM 98.9%; mandible 96.8% vs maxilla 99.4%; mandibular molar 96.2%; 1-staged 100.0% (0/111) vs 2-staged 97.1% (6/205); smokers 100.0% (0/22).
- Cox HRs (all non-significant): DM 4.670 (95% CI 0.942-23.149, p=0.059); mandible 5.107 (0.597-43.719, p=0.137); molar 3.484 (0.407-29.816, p=0.254); 2-staged 1.661 (0.867-3.183, p=0.126); female 0.506 (p=0.432); age >60 2.348 (p=0.436). Smoking and ≥3-unit splinting HRs were not estimable (0 events).
- MBL (171 implants): mesial 0.59±1.36 mm, distal 0.68±1.36 mm. DM: mesial 1.43±2.63 vs 0.37±0.60 mm (p=0.067); distal 1.31±2.33 vs 0.52±0.92 mm (p=0.064).
- Univariate: mesial MBL 2-staged 0.72±1.54 vs 1-staged 0.19±0.32 mm (p=0.019); distal MBL standard length 1.02±1.85 vs short 0.43±0.75 mm (p=0.016).
- Mesial MBL mixed model: DM coeff 0.614 (p=0.026); 2-staged surgery coeff 0.410 (p=0.002 as printed); mesial IPD coeff -0.143 (p=0.046). Distal MBL: only distal IPD significant (coeff -0.327, 95% CI -0.467 to -0.187, p<0.001). Deeper placement (larger IPD) was associated with less MBL.

## 5. Limitations
- Retrospective, single-centre; selection bias and uncontrolled confounding acknowledged by the authors.
- Only 6 failure events; very wide HR CIs; no variable reached significance, so absence of association is not evidence of no effect.
- Only implants with ≥5-year follow-up were included and early failures excluded, so survival refers to late failure among implants that survived to that point (survivorship/censoring).
- Survival rate is a crude cumulative figure from Kaplan-Meier; DM and smoker subgroups small (53 and 22 implants).
- MBL on 2D panoramic radiographs, single examiner, one session; no reliability statistics reported in the text; the printed 95% CIs for mesial staged-surgery and IPD in the mesial model are inconsistent with the stated coefficients (transcription/typographic issue in the paper).
- Manufacturer-specific implant; no control group of another design, so no design-comparison claim is possible.
