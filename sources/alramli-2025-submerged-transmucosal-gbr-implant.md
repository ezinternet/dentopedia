## One-line Summary
RCT (n=28, CBCT): submerged vs transmucosal healing after simultaneous GBR with implant placement showed no significant difference in vertical bone gain, defect resolution, or buccal bone thickness at 4 months.

## 한줄요약
RCT (n=28, CBCT): 임플란트 동시 GBR 후 서브머지드 vs 치통 점막 치유의 수직골획득·결손해소·협측골두께 차이 없음 (4개월).

## 2. Key Contributions
- First CBCT RCT directly comparing submerged (SBM) vs transmucosal (TSM) healing for simultaneous GBR
- No significant difference in vertical bone gain (VGAIN), defect resolution, or radiographic bone thickness (RBT) between approaches
- Significant reduction in RBT found within both groups from T0→T1 (p<0.05), regardless of healing mode
- Simplifies clinical decision-making: transmucosal approach does not compromise GBR outcomes

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Randomized controlled clinical trial
- Population: 28 patients; single implant at restorative-driven position with ≥2 mm coronal exposure requiring GBR
- Intervention: SBM group (submerged, cover screw + flap closure) vs TSM group (transmucosal, healing abutment exposed)
- Simultaneous GBR performed at implant placement
- CBCT taken at T0 (immediately post-surgery) and T1 (4 months post)
- Primary outcome: Vertical bone gain (VGAIN)
- Secondary: Defect resolution, radiographic vertical defect height (RVDH), radiographic bone thickness (RBT)
- Statistics: group comparisons for primary/secondary outcomes; within-group T0→T1 for RBT

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- VGAIN: no significant difference SBM vs TSM (p>0.05)
- Defect resolution: no significant difference (p>0.05)
- RVDH: no significant difference (p>0.05)
- RBT: no significant difference between groups (p>0.05); significant reduction T0→T1 within both groups (p<0.05)
- Study conclusion: healing approach (submerged vs transmucosal) does not affect GBR bone outcomes

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Small sample (n=28); reduced statistical power for subgroup analyses
- 4-month follow-up; long-term bone stability data not reported
- No report of patient-reported outcomes or complications
- Lacks soft tissue/mucosal assessment endpoints
- Funding and COI transparency incomplete in abstract

## 7. Glossary
- **SBM**: Submerged healing — cover screw, flap closure over implant
- **TSM**: Transmucosal healing — healing abutment, mucosa not closed over implant
- **VGAIN**: Vertical bone gain measured on CBCT
- **RVDH**: Radiographic vertical defect height
- **RBT**: Radiographic bone thickness at buccal aspect
- **GBR**: Guided bone regeneration — membrane + graft to regenerate bone at implant dehiscence
