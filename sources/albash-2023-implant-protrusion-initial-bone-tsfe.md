## Why Ingested

First retrospective cohort quantifying the independent effects of implant protrusion length (IPL) vs initial bone height (IBH) on initial bone gain (IBG) after graftless transcrestal SFE — shows IPL is the critical factor while IBH is not an influencing factor; complements [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/yu-2021-implant-protrusion-length-nongrafting-osfe]] which analyzed similar IPL-outcome relationships.

## One-line Summary

Retrospective cohort (34 implants, 29 patients; motorized threaded bone expanders; CBCT at 6 months): mean IBG 1.69 mm; strong positive IPL–IBG correlation; no IBH–IBG correlation; 8.82% membrane perforations; 100% survival rate — IPL is the critical factor, IBH is not influencing.

## 한줄요약

후향적 코호트 (34개 임플란트, 29명; 전동 나사형 골확장기; 술전·6개월 후 CBCT): 평균 초기 골증가 (Initial Bone Gain, IBG) 1.69mm; 임플란트 돌출 길이 (Implant Protrusion Length, IPL)–IBG 강한 양의 상관; 초기 잔존 골 높이 (Initial Bone Height, IBH)–IBG 상관 없음; 막 천공 8.82%; 생존율 100% — IPL이 결정 인자, IBH는 영향 인자 아님.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Annals of Medicine & Surgery (2023) 85:3291–3297; published online June 5, 2023
- **DOI**: 10.1097/MS9.0000000000000870
- **Institution**: Tishreen University, Lattakia, Syria
- **Authors**: Ziad Albash, Ali Khalil, Nadim Sleman
- **Study design**: Retrospective cohort
- **Period**: January 2020 – September 2022
- **n**: 34 implants, 29 patients
- **Technique**: Motorized threaded bone expanders (graftless TSFE); simultaneous implant placement

## 2. Key Contributions

- Mean initial bone height (IBH): 6.37 ± 0.85 mm
- Mean implant protrusion length (IPL): 2.01 ± 0.55 mm
- Mean initial bone gain (IBG): 1.69 ± 0.44 mm
- Membrane perforations: 3/34 (8.82%)
- Implant survival rate at 6 months: 100%
- Strong positive correlation IPL–IBG (r = high): IPL is the critical determinant
- No significant correlation IBH–IBG: initial bone height does not independently influence bone gain
- Conclusion: The length of implant protrusion into the sinus is the critical factor for initial bone gain in graftless transcrestal SFE

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Technique**: Motorized threaded bone expanders for crestal osteotomy and sinus membrane elevation; graftless (blood clot only)
- **Imaging**: CBCT at pre-op and 6 months post-op; bone height measured at implant apex level
- **Measured variables**: IBH (pre-op residual bone height), IPL (post-op implant protrusion beyond sinus floor), IBG (6-month bone height gain around implant apex)
- **Statistics**: Pearson/Spearman correlation for IPL–IBG and IBH–IBG relationships
- **Complication tracking**: Membrane perforations recorded during surgery; managed without aborting procedure

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective design; single center (Syria); small n (34 implants)
- Short follow-up (6 months) — long-term survival and bone stability not addressed
- No control group (grafted vs graftless)
- Motorized expanders only — may not generalize to osteotome or piezo techniques
- Syria setting may introduce case-mix differences (patient selection, equipment)
- Larger prospective studies needed with longer follow-up and technique comparison

## 7. Glossary

- **IBH (Initial Bone Height, 초기 잔존 골 높이)**: pre-operative residual alveolar bone height below sinus floor
- **IPL (Implant Protrusion Length, 임플란트 돌출 길이)**: length of implant apex protruding beyond the native sinus floor into the sinus cavity
- **IBG (Initial Bone Gain, 초기 골증가)**: new bone formed around the implant apex in the sinus at 6-month CBCT
- **Motorized threaded bone expander (전동 나사형 골확장기)**: rotary instrument that condenses and elevates sinus floor via crestal approach
- **Graftless TSFE (무이식 치조정접근 상악동 거상, TSFE)**: transcrestal sinus floor elevation relying solely on blood clot for bone formation
