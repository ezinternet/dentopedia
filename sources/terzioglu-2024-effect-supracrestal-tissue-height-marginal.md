## Why Ingested

Terzioglu 2024는 임플란트주위 생물학적 폭경(STH, Supracrestal Tissue Height)이 PS 임플란트의 MBL에 미치는 직접 효과를 식립 깊이와 교차하여 측정한 최초의 전향적 RCT 중 하나다. STH 개념은 위키의 [[implants/mbl/stacchi-2023-marginal-bone-changes-platform-switched-conical]] (1mm vs 2mm 하방 식립)와 [[implants/mbl/terzioglu-2024-effect-supracrestal-tissue-height-marginal]] 를 연결하는 기전적 고리이며, "왜 2mm 하방에서 노출이 없는가"의 설명을 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective RCT (n=60 implants, 3 groups: crestal/equicrestal + STH categorized by thick/thin tissue) evaluating whether supracrestal tissue height modifies MBL outcomes around platform-switched bone-level implants at 1 year.

STH below 3 mm was associated with significantly greater MBL; implants in sites with thin supracrestal tissue (≤3 mm) placed equicrestally showed the worst bone preservation; thick supracrestal tissue or deeper subcrestal placement compensated for STH deficit.

STH is an independent predictor of MBL in PS bone-level implants; clinically, sites with STH ≤ 3 mm may benefit from subcrestal placement to establish adequate biological space and prevent bone remodeling.

## 세줄요약

전향적 무작위 대조 임상시험 (임플란트 60개, 3군: 치조정·각화조직 두께별): 초치조정 조직 높이(STH, Supracrestal Tissue Height)가 플랫폼 스위칭(PS) 골수준 임플란트의 MBL에 미치는 영향, 1년 추적.

STH < 3mm 부위에서 변연골 소실(MBL, Marginal Bone Loss) 유의하게 증가; 치조정 식립 + 얇은 STH 조합이 가장 불량; 두꺼운 STH 또는 하방 식립이 STH 부족을 보상.

STH는 PS 임플란트 MBL의 독립 예측인자; 임상적으로 STH ≤ 3mm 부위는 하방 식립으로 생물학적 공간 확보가 필요할 수 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Prospective RCT establishing STH as independent predictor of MBL in PS implants
- First RCT to directly test STH × placement depth interaction in PS bone-level implants
- STH ≤ 3 mm: significantly greater MBL, especially with equicrestal placement
- Clinical implication: subcrestal placement recommended when STH is thin
- Links biological width theory to radiographic bone loss outcomes

## 3. Methodology
- Design: prospective parallel RCT; 3 groups based on STH + placement depth
- Population: 60 patients/implants; posterior region; partially edentulous
- Implants: bone-level PS implants; platform-switched design
- Groups: crestal vs subcrestal placement × thick vs thin STH
- Follow-up: 1 year post-loading
- Outcomes: MBL (primary; radiographic); STH measured clinically at placement

## 4. Key Results
- Thin STH (≤ 3 mm) + equicrestal placement: most MBL
- Thick STH (> 3 mm) or subcrestal placement: compensatory bone preservation
- STH independently predicts MBL (multivariate analysis)
- Exact MBL values by group: pending full text (abstract-only ingest)

## 5. Limitations
- 1-year follow-up
- STH measurement clinical (probe-based) — some measurement error
- Single center; n=60 implants
- Exact STH threshold may need validation in larger samples

## 7. Glossary
- **STH**: Supracrestal tissue height (초치조정 조직 높이) — vertical extent of peri-implant soft tissue from bone crest to mucosal margin; analogous to biological width
- **PS**: Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭)
- **MBL**: Marginal bone loss (변연골 소실)
- **Equicrestal**: implant shoulder level with alveolar crest (치조정 식립)
- **Subcrestal**: implant shoulder below alveolar crest (하방 식립)
