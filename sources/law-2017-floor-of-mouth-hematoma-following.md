## Three-line Summary

Structured review (1990–2016) of 25 reported cases of floor-of-mouth hematoma after dental implant placement plus one case presentation. Lingual cortical perforation caused hemorrhage in 84% of cases; 68% required emergency airway intervention (intubation or tracheostomy); 72% required surgical management in hospital. Only 1 of 17 cases used preoperative 3D imaging, supporting its role in reducing risk.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Systematic review of 25 reported floor-of-mouth hematoma cases after dental implant placement (1990–2016).
- Quantified etiology: lingual cortical perforation in 84% (n=21).
- Airway morbidity: emergency intubation or tracheostomy in 68% (n=17).
- Hospital surgical management in 72% (n=18).
- Offending implant management reported inconsistently: of 17 cases, 5 removed, 9 retained in situ, 3 abandoned.
- Only 1 case involved preoperative 3D imaging; authors advocate 3D imaging to visualize individual mandibular shape.
- Additional case: serious FOM hematoma requiring immediate surgical evacuation and hemostasis.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Online literature search 1990–2016 with terms: floor of mouth hematoma, sublingual hematoma, dental implant hematoma, implant in mandible, complication of dental implant.
- Abstracts screened for relevance; English-language reports included.
- Data extracted: demographics, implant location, coagulopathy, pre/postoperative imaging, airway management, hematoma treatment, offending implant management.

## 7. Glossary

- **Floor of the Mouth (FOM)**: 구강저 — space beneath tongue containing sublingual/submandibular glands and vessels.
- **Lingual cortical perforation**: 설측 피질골 천공 — breach of lingual mandibular cortex risking vessel injury.
- **Sublingual hematoma**: 설하혈종 — blood collection in sublingual space.
- **Emergency airway management**: 응급 기도 확보 — intubation or tracheostomy for obstruction.
- **3D imaging (CBCT)**: 3차원 영상 — preoperative visualization of mandibular shape.
