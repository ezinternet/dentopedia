## Why Ingested

초기(보철 전) 임플란트 실패 주제 배치의 일부로, 비타민 D 결핍이 조기 임플란트 실패 (EDIF)의 수정 가능한 위험 인자인지에 대한 최신(2026) 범위 문헌고찰이며 EDIF 5편 통합 RR 1.87 (95% CI 1.01-3.45)과 ISQ 통합 결과를 제공한다. 기존 비타민 D 개별 코호트·SR을 묶어 보강하고 비타민 D 외 영양소 근거가 거의 없다는 공백을 명시한다. → [[implants/vitamin-d/miron-2025-vitamin-d-deficiency-early-implant-failure]] · [[overviews/early-implant-failure-risk-prevention-overview]] · [[overviews/vitamin-d-osseointegration-implant-overview]]

## Three-line Summary

Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR; PROSPERO CRD420251172055) of human clinical studies up to November 2025 on dietary factors and nutrients in dental implant therapy: 27 studies included (13 cohort, 8 RCT, 3 cross-sectional, 2 case-control, 1 uncontrolled interventional), of which 24 examined vitamin D, 1 vitamin B and 1 vitamin C; 9 entered quantitative synthesis.

For early dental implant failure (EDIF, studies with outcomes assessed within 12 months, EDIF follow-up roughly 3-6 months), pooling of 5 studies gave a risk ratio of 1.87 (95% CI 1.01-3.45, p=0.05) for low versus sufficient vitamin D; ISQ at 3 months was higher in vitamin D-sufficient patients (2 studies, mean difference 4.37, 95% CI 0.41-8.33, p=0.03, I2=75%).

Evidence is dominated by observational designs with serious risk of bias in many studies and heterogeneous vitamin D thresholds, so the pooled associations are fragile (the EDIF interval touches 1.0); the authors advise vitamin D screening in at-risk patients, not a standard dosing regimen, and caution that excess levels may also be unfavorable (one 19-year study).

## 세줄요약

PRISMA-ScR 기반 범위 문헌고찰 (scoping review; PROSPERO CRD420251172055)로 2025년 11월까지의 영양·식이 인자와 임플란트 치료 결과에 대한 인체 임상연구 27편(코호트 13, 무작위대조시험 8, 단면 3, 증례-대조 2, 비대조 중재 1)을 포함했고, 24편이 비타민 D, 1편이 비타민 B, 1편이 비타민 C를 다뤘으며 9편이 정량 종합에 들어갔다.

조기 임플란트 실패 (early dental implant failure, EDIF) 5편 통합에서 비타민 D 저하군의 위험비 (risk ratio, RR)는 1.87 (95% CI 1.01-3.45, p=0.05)이었고, 3개월 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)는 비타민 D 충분군에서 평균 4.37 높았다 (2편, 95% CI 0.41-8.33, p=0.03, I2=75%).

근거는 관찰연구 위주이고 상당수가 비뚤림 위험이 심각하며 비타민 D 기준도 이질적이라 통합 결과는 취약하다 (EDIF 신뢰구간이 1.0에 걸침); 저자들은 고위험 환자에서 비타민 D 선별을 고려하되 표준 용량은 제시하지 못하며, 과잉 수치도 불리할 수 있다고 주의한다 (19년 추적 1편).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Claims to be the first scoping review to synthesize human clinical evidence on nutrients and osseointegration.
- 27 studies: 24 vitamin D, 1 vitamin B (pain/paresthesia after inferior alveolar nerve damage), 1 vitamin C (early soft-tissue healing).
- Small quantitative subsets: EDIF pooled RR 1.87 (5 studies), ISQ at 3 months MD 4.37 (2 studies), early MBL pooled in a forest plot (values not stated in text).
- Notes that evidence for other micronutrients (boron, selenium, copper, iron, zinc, magnesium) is essentially absent from clinical implant studies.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Scoping review following PRISMA-ScR; PICO on patients receiving implants with defined nutritional status/intake/supplementation.
- **Search**: PubMed (364 hits), Web of Science (86), Cochrane Library (142) = 592; 522 after de-duplication; 37 full texts; 10 excluded; 27 included (qualitative), 9 in meta-analysis. Language English; up to November 2025; hand-search of four implant journals.
- **Exclusions**: case reports, technical reports, animal, in vitro, reviews; topical/surface-applied micronutrients (e.g. vitamin D-coated implants).
- **Risk of bias**: RoB 2 for RCTs (2 low, 3 some concerns, 3 high); ROBINS-I for non-randomized studies (mostly moderate to serious; 1 critical).
- **Statistics**: RevMan Web; continuous outcomes inverse-variance random-effects MD; EDIF Mantel-Haenszel fixed-effect RR; I2 for heterogeneity.
- **Early vs late**: early outcomes defined as assessed at 12 months or less; late > 12 months.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Included studies | 27 (13 cohort [8 prospective, 5 retrospective], 8 RCT, 3 cross-sectional, 2 case-control, 1 uncontrolled interventional) |
| EDIF studies | 5; one significant association with vitamin D deficiency, four showed higher EDIF with low vitamin D without significance |
| EDIF pooled (low vs sufficient vitamin D) | RR 1.87, 95% CI 1.01-3.45, p=0.05 |
| ISQ 3 months pooled (2 studies) | MD 4.37, 95% CI 0.41-8.33, p=0.03, I2=75% |
| ISQ studies | 7; 5 (71%) positive association with vitamin D, 2 not significant |
| Single-study ISQ slope (Bhandage) | +0.48 ISQ per 1 ng/mL at 3 months (p=0.01); +0.62 at 6 months (p=0.002) |
| Short-term MBL | 7 studies, most report more MBL with low vitamin D; pooled forest plot (2 studies) significant, numbers not given in text |
| Long-term | Diachkova RCT: all peri-implantitis cases in vitamin D deficient/severely deficient patients at 10 years (p=0.04); Cheng case-control (19 years): vitamin D excess linked to more bone loss and failure, esp. maxilla |
| Strength of evidence (Table 4) | ISQ ++, EDIF +, short-term MBL ++, bone density +, soft tissue +/++, long-term + |

Component EDIF studies (Table 2): Grigoras (166 patients/556 implants, up to 12 months, no significant correlation); Al-Quisi (108/128, 4 months, severe deficiency associated with early failure); Francis (109/174, 3-6 months, no correlation); Mohsen (53/143, 12 weeks, higher EDIF with lower vitamin D); Singh retrospective (90 patients, 5 months, increased EDIF with lower vitamin D); Mangano (885 patients/1,740 implants, 4 months, no significant link); a second Mangano entry (822 patients/1,625 implants, 4 months, no link).

Vitamin D thresholds used: <30 ng/mL = deficiency/insufficiency; 20-29 insufficiency; <20 deficiency; <10 severe deficiency. Supplementation regimens (7 studies): 60,000 IU monthly for at least 3 months; 5,000-8,000 IU/day for about 12 weeks; 50,000 IU/week for 8 weeks; 6,000 IU/day for 6 weeks; individualized correction then 1,000-2,000 IU/day maintenance.

## 5. Strengths

- Pre-registered protocol, three databases plus hand search, dual independent risk-of-bias rating with RoB 2 and ROBINS-I.
- Separates early (12 months or less) from late outcomes and reports a strength-of-evidence table.
- Candid about heterogeneity and limits of pooling.

## 6. Limitations and Critical Appraisal

- Scoping design with limited-subset meta-analysis; pooled EDIF RR has a lower CI bound of 1.01 (p=0.05) and the ISQ pool rests on 2 studies with I2=75%.
- Evidence mostly observational; 3 of 8 RCTs high risk; EDIF studies rated moderate (2) or serious (3) risk of bias; confounders (smoking, comorbidity, bone quality, implant factors) inconsistently adjusted.
- Studies defined EDIF with different follow-up windows (about 3 to 12 months) and different vitamin D cut-offs.
- Source-data issues in the text: vitamin D (24) + B (1) + C (1) = 26 not 27; the abstract counts 15 positive + 5 no association + 2 = 22 of 27; the EDIF section says 5 studies while Table 2 lists more EDIF-type entries (including two Mangano cohorts of overlapping size); the Table 2 Ghasemi RCT (pain/paresthesia) is the vitamin B study; Dulinska-Litewka is described as retrospective 72 patients; forest plot numbers for MBL are not stated in text.
- Includes only English language studies; no non-vitamin D micronutrient data.

## 7. Glossary

- EDIF (early dental implant failure, 조기 임플란트 실패): failure within the early healing phase; windows vary by study.
- ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient, 임플란트 안정성 지수): resonance frequency analysis value.
- MBL (marginal bone loss, 변연골 소실).
- 25-hydroxyvitamin D (25(OH)D): serum marker of vitamin D status.
- PRISMA-ScR: reporting guideline for scoping reviews.
