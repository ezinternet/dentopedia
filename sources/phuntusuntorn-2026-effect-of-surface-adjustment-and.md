## Why Ingested

This in vitro study directly addresses clinically important questions about how surface adjustment (grinding + polishing) and hydrothermal aging affect the mechanical performance and microstructural stability of three major zirconia generations (3Y-TZP, 5Y-PSZ, 6Y-PSZ). It provides strength values across all four experimental conditions and characterizes phase stability with XRD and SEM.

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/phuntusuntorn-2026-effect-of-surface-adjustment-and]]

## Three-line Summary

Finishing and polishing (FP) substantially increased biaxial flexural strength in 3Y-TZP (1213.7 → 1704.3 MPa) and moderately in 5Y-PSZ (694.2 → 993.7 MPa), while 6Y-PSZ showed minimal response. Simulated hydrothermal aging (134°C, 0.2 MPa, 5 h autoclave) did not significantly affect strength, hardness, or surface roughness in any material, and no monoclinic phase was detected by qualitative XRD. SEM revealed localized grain-boundary microcracking in aged 3Y-TZP, indicating early low-temperature degradation (LTD) signs without measurable mechanical consequences under the applied conditions.

## 세줄요약

마무리·연마(FP)는 3Y-TZP의 굴곡강도를 1213.7→1704.3 MPa로 크게 향상시키고 5Y-PSZ에는 중등도(694.2→993.7 MPa) 개선 효과를 보였으며, 6Y-PSZ는 변화가 미미했다. 모의 열수 노화(134°C·0.2 MPa·5시간)는 세 재료 모두에서 강도·경도·표면 거칠기에 유의한 영향을 주지 않았고, 정성적 X선 회절에서 단사정상(monoclinic)도 검출되지 않았다. SEM에서 노화 후 3Y-TZP에 국소적 결정립 경계 미세균열이 관찰되어 초기 저온열화(LTD) 징후가 나타났으나 기계적 성능 저하로는 이어지지 않았다.

## Key Contributions

1. **FP strengthens 3Y-TZP most**: grinding with 100 µm diamond bur + 3-step polishing (ZiLMaster) raised 3Y-TZP strength by ~40% (1213.7 → 1704.3 MPa); transformation-toughening compressive residual stresses are the proposed mechanism.
2. **5Y-PSZ moderate response**: FP raised 5Y-PSZ from 694.2 → 993.7 MPa (~43% increase), but remained well below 3Y-TZP levels.
3. **6Y-PSZ strength insensitive to FP**: mean change 1015.6 → 1065 MPa, not statistically significant; reduced transformable tetragonal fraction limits stress-induced toughening.
4. **Hydrothermal aging benign under these conditions**: neither strength, hardness, nor roughness changed significantly in any material after ISO aging; qualitative XRD found no monoclinic peaks.
5. **SEM detects early LTD in 3Y-TZP**: grain-boundary microcracking and grain pull-out observed in aged 3Y-TZP despite intact macromechanical properties, suggesting early-stage degradation below detection threshold of bulk mechanical tests.
6. **Phase stability of high-translucency zirconia**: 5Y-PSZ and 6Y-PSZ surface morphology largely unchanged after aging, indicating good LTD resistance due to cubic phase dominance.

## Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Specimens | Disc 12 mm × 1.2 mm; 3Y-TZP sintered 1500°C/120 min; 6Y-PSZ 1450°C/90 min |
| Groups | 2 surface conditions × 2 aging × 3 materials = 12 groups, n=10 each |
| Surface adjustment | 100 µm diamond bur at 200,000 rpm/20 s → 3-step ZiLMaster polishing |
| Hydrothermal aging | 134°C, 0.2 MPa, 5 h (ISO 13356:2015 protocol) |
| Strength test | Piston-on-3-ball biaxial flexural test (ISO 6872:2015), 0.5 mm/min |
| Surface roughness | Contact profilometer, arithmetic mean Ra (µm) |
| Hardness | Nanoindentation (Berkovich, 2940 mN max load), Shimadzu DUH-211 |
| Phase analysis | XRD (Bruker D8 ADVANCE, Cu-Kα, 20–80° 2θ, qualitative) |
| Grain size | SEM after thermal etching at 1400°C/30 min; ≥300 grains/specimen via ImageJ |
| Statistics | 3-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD, α=0.05 |

## Key Results

### Biaxial Flexural Strength (MPa)

| Material | Control | Control + Aging | FP | FP + Aging |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3Y-TZP | 1213.7 | 1153.5 | **1704.3** | 1674.6 |
| 5Y-PSZ | 694.2 | 651.3 | **993.7** | 871.5 |
| 6Y-PSZ | 1015.6 | 986.3 | 1065.0 | 1019.0 |

- Zirconia type (ηp²=0.69) and FP (ηp²=0.37) significant; aging (ηp²=0.02) not significant
- Significant interaction: zirconia type × FP (ηp²=0.23)

### Surface Roughness (Ra, µm)

- FP significantly reduced Ra in all materials (p<0.05, ηp²=0.94)
- 3Y-TZP control: 0.21 µm; after FP: 0.13 µm
- Aging did not significantly change Ra after polishing

### Surface Hardness

- Only zirconia type significant (ηp²=0.25); FP and aging had no significant effect
- Gradual increase with yttria content (3Y-TZP ~13.0 GPa → 6Y-PSZ ~14.1 GPa) but not statistically significant

### XRD / Phase Analysis

- No monoclinic peaks detected in any condition (qualitative analysis)
- Slight peak broadening after grinding may indicate residual lattice strain
- 3Y-TZP shows tetragonal peaks; 5Y-PSZ/6Y-PSZ show broader peaks consistent with tetragonal-cubic mixture

### SEM Microstructure

- 3Y-TZP: fine homogeneous grains; aging → grain-boundary microcracking, early LTD signs
- 5Y-PSZ/6Y-PSZ: bimodal grain-size distribution; aging → no significant surface degradation

## Limitations

1. **Qualitative XRD only**: cannot quantify monoclinic phase fraction; small phase transformation may have been missed
2. **Accelerated aging model**: 5 h at 134°C ≈ 15–20 years in vivo, but does not replicate full oral environment (pH cycling, mechanical stress)
3. **Single grinding protocol**: 100 µm diamond bur with fixed pressure — clinical variability in adjustment not captured
4. **Monolayer specimens**: multilayer zirconia clinical behavior may differ
5. **Short aging duration**: longer aging may reveal progressive LTD in 3Y-TZP given the microcracking already visible at 5 h

## Glossary

- **3Y-TZP**: 3 mol% yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia polycrystal — conventional high-strength zirconia
- **5Y-PSZ / 6Y-PSZ**: partially stabilized zirconia with 5 or 6 mol% yttria — high-translucency grades with more cubic phase
- **LTD (Low-Temperature Degradation)**: progressive tetragonal-to-monoclinic phase transformation in humid environments, also called hydrothermal aging or aging
- **Transformation toughening**: stress-induced t→m phase transformation at crack tips generating compressive residual stresses that resist crack propagation
- **FP (Finishing and Polishing)**: clinical surface adjustment combining grinding (diamond bur) followed by sequential polishing
- **Biaxial flexural strength**: piston-on-3-ball test per ISO 6872 measuring fracture resistance of ceramic discs
- **Ra**: arithmetic mean surface roughness (µm)
- **XRD (X-ray Diffraction)**: crystallographic phase analysis technique
