## Why Ingested

Directly quantifies heat reduction by irrigation route in guided-surgery osteotomy — a protocol gap not covered by existing static-guide thermal studies such as [[implants/osteotomy-thermal/chauhan-2018-biomechanical-factors-heat-generation-osteotomy]]. The internal-irrigation channel guide design provides a novel data point for clinical protocol selection.

## Three-line Summary

Internal irrigation via a 3D-printed guide with dedicated channels produced the lowest heat generation during guided implant osteotomy, reducing mean ΔT to 3.50°C (solid model) and 0.84°C (bilayered model) versus 31.41°C and 13.31°C with no irrigation. External irrigation was intermediate (9.48°C and 2.45°C), confirming that cooling route, not just presence of irrigation, matters. Bilayered resin models (simulating cortical + trabecular bone) showed lower ΔT than homogeneous solid models under infrared thermography (p = 0.009), suggesting that model choice affects measured thermal risk.

## 세줄요약

3D 프린팅 가이드 내부 채널을 통한 내부 관류는 골삭제 중 열 발생을 가장 효과적으로 줄였으며, 이중층 모델 기준 평균 온도 상승을 0.84°C로 억제했다 (관류 없음 13.31°C 대비). 외부 관류는 중간 수준(2.45°C)으로, 관류의 유무뿐 아니라 관류 경로가 열 관리에 결정적임을 보여준다. 피질골·해면골을 모사한 이중층 모델은 단일 고형 모델보다 적외선 열화상 기준 낮은 온도 상승을 나타내어(p = 0.009), 실험 모델 선택이 열 위험도 측정에 영향을 줌을 시사한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

1. First direct comparison of **no irrigation vs. external irrigation vs. internal (guide-channel) irrigation** during fully guided implant osteotomy in a standardized in vitro setup.
2. Demonstrates that **internal irrigation reduces ΔT by ~90% vs. no irrigation** (solid model: 31.41°C → 3.50°C; bilayered: 13.31°C → 0.84°C).
3. Compares two measurement modalities — **infrared thermography** (surface) vs. **internal thermistor** — showing methodological differences matter: bilayered advantage was significant by IR (p = 0.009) but less consistent by thermistor.
4. Bilayered resin models may partially approximate cortical/trabecular thermal behavior; provides a more realistic substrate than fully solid models.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

**Specimens:**
- Standardized resin mandibular models in two configurations:
  - **Solid**: homogeneous resin throughout
  - **Bilayered**: outer cortical-density layer + inner trabecular-density layer

**Drilling Protocol:**
- Sequential drill sizes: 2.0 → 4.2 mm diameter
- Depth: 18 mm
- Speed: 1200 rpm
- Guided protocol with a static surgical guide

**Irrigation Groups (3):**
1. No irrigation
2. External irrigation (conventional handpiece water supply)
3. Internal irrigation: 3D-printed guide incorporating dedicated internal irrigation channels delivering coolant directly to the drill tip

**Temperature Measurement:**
- Infrared thermography (surface thermal imaging)
- Internal thermistor sensor (subsurface temperature)
- Primary outcome: ΔT (temperature change from baseline)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Condition | Solid Model ΔT (°C) | Bilayered Model ΔT (°C) |
|---|---|---|
| No irrigation | 31.41 | 13.31 |
| External irrigation | 9.48 | 2.45 |
| Internal irrigation | 3.50 | 0.84 |

- Bilayered < Solid for ΔT by infrared thermography (p = 0.009)
- Thermistor measurements: less consistent difference between model types
- Internal irrigation consistently produced the lowest ΔT across both models and both measurement methods
- All internal irrigation values well below the 47°C / 10-second thermal osteonecrosis threshold (assuming baseline ~37°C body temperature, ΔT of 10°C would reach 47°C)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **In vitro only**: resin models do not fully replicate bone's thermal conductivity, vascularity, or periosteum
- Thermistor data showed less consistent model-type differences, suggesting measurement method affects conclusions
- Single drill speed (1200 rpm) — clinical practice uses variable speeds
- Guide design specific to study; internal irrigation channel geometry may vary across manufacturers
- No histological outcome (osseointegration, necrosis) assessed — temperature data only
- Future work: in vivo animal or cadaveric studies; variable rpm protocols; comparison across commercial guided-surgery systems with internal irrigation

## 7. Glossary

- **ΔT (Delta T)**: Temperature change from baseline (°C); primary thermal outcome metric
- **Infrared thermography**: Non-contact surface thermal imaging; measures heat at specimen surface
- **Thermistor**: Contact-based internal temperature sensor; measures heat within specimen
- **Internal irrigation**: Cooling fluid delivered through channels built into the surgical guide, directing coolant to the drill tip
- **External irrigation**: Conventional cooling via handpiece water spray directed at exterior of drill site
- **Bilayered model**: Resin specimen with outer high-density layer (cortical analog) and inner lower-density layer (trabecular analog)
- **Thermal osteonecrosis**: Bone cell death from excessive heat; threshold commonly cited as 47°C for 1 minute or 47°C for 10 seconds
- **Guided osteotomy**: Implant site drilling performed through a static surgical guide (template) for position/angulation control
