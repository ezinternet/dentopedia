## Why Ingested

[[wiki/immediate-implant/munoz-camara-2020-immediate-implants-acute-periapical-infected]]의 토론부(ref 27)에서 직접 인용된 원본 RCT. 급성 감염 소켓(36개)·만성 감염(47개)·비감염(85개) 3군 비교; 초음파(piezo) 골절개 + 30초 초음파 소독 프로토콜이 핵심 차별점. Saijeva 2020 SR+MA 포함 9개 연구 중 하나.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective cohort (n=86 patients, 168 immediate implants), Italy; 3 groups — non-infected (85), acute infection (36), chronic infection (47); atraumatic ultrasonic extraction + 30 s ultrasonication at 72 W in infected sites; 1-year follow-up.

1-year survival: non-infected 98.8%, chronic 100%, acute 94.4%; differences NS; no implant lost after loading.

Immediate implant survival is comparable in infected and non-infected sites when ultrasonic debridement and standard antibiotic/surgical protocol are applied.

## 세줄요약

전향적 코호트 (n=86명, 168 임플란트), 이탈리아; 3군 — 비감염(85)·급성 감염(36)·만성 감염(47); 초음파(piezo) 무외상 발치 + 감염 부위 72W 30초 초음파 소독; 1년 추적.

1년 생존율: 비감염 98.8%·만성 100%·급성 94.4%; 군 간 유의차 없음; 최종 보철 후 탈락 0건.

초음파 소독+표준 외과 프로토콜 적용 시 감염·비감염 소켓 즉시식립 생존율 동등.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to compare acute vs chronic infection vs non-infected sites using ultrasonic (piezo) surgery for debridement
- Demonstrated 100% survival in chronically infected sites; 94.4% in acutely infected (vs 98.8% control); NS
- Ultrasonication at 72 W for 30 seconds as infection abatement protocol
- No implant loss after loading — all failures occurred before loading
- Included in Saijeva 2020 SR+MA (one of 9 cohort studies)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective cohort
- **n**: 86 patients, 168 immediate implants
  - Non-infected: 85 implants
  - Acute infection: 36 implants
  - Chronic infection: 47 implants
- **Protocol**: Piezo/ultrasonic atraumatic extraction + implant osteotomy (no flap); infected sites ultrasonicated 30 s at 72 W
- **Follow-up**: 1 year
- **Statistics**: Kaplan-Meier survival rates

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Group | n implants | 1-year survival |
|---|---|---|
| Non-infected | 85 | 98.8% |
| Chronic infection | 47 | 100% |
| Acute infection | 36 | 94.4% |

Differences NS. No implant lost after loading. Ultrasonic extraction allowed root removal in one piece; drilling uncomplicated without skidding.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract only — detailed protocol/clinical parameters not available from this source
- Short follow-up (1 year)
- No randomization; no control for confounders

## 7. Glossary

- **Piezo/ultrasonic surgery**: Piezoelectric vibration device (e.g. Piezosurgery®) for atraumatic bone cutting; preserves soft tissue; allows precision at thin cortical walls
- **Ultrasonication**: Application of ultrasonic vibration directly to the socket for bacterial abatement
