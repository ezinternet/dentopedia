## Why Ingested

No prior wiki page examines whether CBCT-based 연골하골 변화 (Subchondral Bone Alteration, SBA) severity grade predicts functional splint outcomes in TMJ 관절통 (arthralgia) patients; this fills a direct prognostic gap adjacent to [[tmj/zhang-2020-occlusal-splint-tmd-rct-sr]], which reviews splint efficacy broadly but does not address imaging-based patient stratification.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study (n=109, 89.9% female, mean age 44.3 years) of DC/TMD-diagnosed unilateral TMJ arthralgia patients receiving 3 mm resilient splint therapy, with CBCT-graded condylar SBA severity (mild/moderate/severe) as predictor and JFLS-20 scores at 6 months as outcome.

All SBA severity groups showed significant JFLS improvement (p<0.001), but patients with severe condylar SBAs had significantly worse masticatory outcomes (JFLS-M, p=0.027) and overall jaw function sum (p=0.002) compared with mild SBA patients after ANCOVA controlling for age, sex, pain intensity, and pain duration.

CBCT-based SBA grading at baseline may stratify arthralgia patients for whom 6-month splint outcomes will be limited, prompting earlier consideration of arthrocentesis/lavage, though the study lacked psychosocial covariates and a control group.

## 세줄요약

DC/TMD 기준 단측 TMJ 관절통 환자 109명(여성 89.9%, 평균 44.3세)을 대상으로 CBCT 기반 과두 연골하골 변화(Subchondral Bone Alteration, SBA) 중증도(경도/중등도/중증)가 3 mm 탄성 스플린트 6개월 기능 결과(JFLS-20)를 예측하는지 후향적 코호트 연구.

모든 SBA 그룹에서 JFLS 개선이 유의미했으나(p<0.001), 중증 SBA 환자는 연령·성별·통증·기간 보정 후에도 경도 SBA 대비 저작 제한 개선이 유의하게 불량(JFLS-M p=0.027, JFLS-sum p=0.002).

CBCT SBA 등급이 스플린트 치료 반응 예측에 활용될 수 있으나, 심리사회적 변수 미보정·대조군 없음이 주요 한계이며 향후 전향적 연구가 필요함.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Scientific Reports (Nature Publishing Group)
- **PMC ID**: PMC13106724
- **DOI**: 10.1038/s41598-026-43226-z
- **Study type**: Retrospective cohort (exploratory)
- **n**: 109 (98 female, 11 male)
- **Design**: Single-arm, no control group; pre-post at T0 (baseline) and T1 (6-month follow-up)
- **Setting**: Clinical TMJ specialty setting; DC/TMD-diagnosed unilateral arthralgia

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to apply a CBCT-based SBA grading system (mild/moderate/severe grades 1–4) to predict functional outcomes of occlusal splint therapy in TMJ arthralgia
- Demonstrates that severe condylar SBAs are associated with significantly worse masticatory functional improvement after 6-month splint therapy even after controlling for baseline covariates
- Supports CBCT as a potential prognostic tool for TMJ arthralgia treatment planning beyond diagnosis

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Inclusion**: DC/TMD unilateral arthralgia (single-joint); CBCT available at baseline; 3 mm resilient splint therapy for 6 months
- **SBA grading**: 4-category CBCT scoring system collapsed to three groups: mild (grade 1, n=36), moderate (grade 2, n=42), severe (grades 3–4, n=31)
- **SBA subtypes recorded**: erosion (88.1%), flattening (55.1%), sclerosis (50.5%), osteophyte (44%), resorption (28.4%), cyst (14.7%)
- **Outcome measure**: 턱기능제한척도 (Jaw Functional Limitation Scale, JFLS-20) — three subscales: Mastication (JFLS-M), Vertical Jaw Mobility (JFLS-JM), Verbal/Emotional Expression (JFLS-VEE)
- **Pain**: VAS (100 mm scale)
- **Statistics**: Paired sample t-test (intragroup pre-post); ANCOVA for between-group comparison controlling for age, sex, pain intensity, pain duration

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Baseline characteristics** (no significant difference among SBA groups, p>0.05):
- Mean age: 44.3 ± 16.6 years; 89.9% female
- Baseline pain VAS: 57.2 ± 22.7 mm
- Pain duration: 107.6 ± 263.7 weeks

**Pain reduction** (all groups, 6-month VAS):
- Mild SBA: 27.4 mm reduction (p<0.001)
- Moderate SBA: 25.1 mm reduction (p<0.001)
- Severe SBA: 23.3 mm reduction (p<0.001)

**JFLS improvement** (all groups, T0→T1, p<0.001):
- Total JFLS-M change: −12.6; JFLS-JM: −7.3; JFLS-VEE: −7.5; JFLS-sum: −9.2

**Between-group (ANCOVA)**:
- Severe vs mild SBA: JFLS-M significantly worse (p=0.027)
- JFLS-sum: mild vs moderate (p=0.011), mild vs severe (p=0.002); moderate vs severe NS (p>0.05)

**T0/T1 JFLS-M by group**:
- Mild: 25.3→10.6 (Δ14.7); Moderate: 25.6→11.8 (Δ13.8); Severe: 16.7→8.0 (Δ8.6)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No control group; placebo, regression-to-mean, and non-specific effects not separated
- Exploratory design; no psychosocial covariate control (depression, anxiety, catastrophizing)
- SBA grade 3 and 4 collapsed into single 'severe' category — may lose discriminatory precision
- Results limited to unilateral DC/TMD arthralgia; not generalizable to bilateral or other subtypes
- CBCT-only; no contrast-enhanced MRI to distinguish inflammatory arthritis from age-related osteoarthrosis
- Future: longitudinal SBA progression studies, multivariable prognostic models, validation in independent datasets

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary

- **SBA (Subchondral Bone Alteration, 연골하골 변화)**: OA-related changes in condylar cortical/subcortical bone visible on CBCT — erosion, flattening, sclerosis, osteophyte, resorption, cyst
- **JFLS-20 (Jaw Functional Limitation Scale, 턱기능제한척도)**: 20-item PRO measuring masticatory, mobility, and verbal/emotional jaw function limitation
- **DC/TMD (Diagnostic Criteria for Temporomandibular Disorders, 턱관절장애 진단기준)**: 2014 standardized diagnostic system for TMD
- **VAS (Visual Analogue Scale, 시각통증척도)**: 100 mm pain intensity scale
- **ANCOVA (Analysis of Covariance, 공분산분석)**: Statistical method adjusting outcome for baseline covariates
- **Arthralgia (관절통)**: Pain of joint origin confirmed by provocation; key DC/TMD pain-related diagnosis
