## Why Ingested

이 2026년 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석(SR+MA)은 최대교두감합위(Maximum Intercuspation Position, MIP)를 공통 기준으로 5가지 중심위(Centric Relation, CR) 기록법의 과두 변위를 정량 풀링한 최신 근거로, [[wiki/complete-denture/jasz-2024-centric-relation-methods-digital-mandibular-motion]]의 단일 코호트 3차원 편차 결과를 독립적으로 재확인하는 동시에 '우월한 술식'이라는 결론을 높은 이질성으로 한정한다. 특히 [[wiki/overviews/gothic-arch-jaw-relation-recording-overview]]의 "재현성 ≠ 정확도(기준 의존)" 논지를 MIP 기준 풀링과 CR-영역 개념으로 정교화하므로, 고딕아치 악간관계 개요 페이지에 직접 연결되는 2026년 근거 레이어로 인제스트한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and single-arm random-effects meta-analysis (12 cross-sectional studies published 1994–2024; 1,693 records screened) compared condylar displacement from maximum intercuspation position (MIP) across five centric relation (CR) recording techniques: chin-point guidance (CPG), bimanual manipulation (BM), power-centric registration (PCR), Gothic arch tracer (GA), and active movement (AM).

Pooled results: CPG shifted only the Y-axis (SMD = 1.17, 95% CI 0.19–2.16, P = 0.02, I² = 98%) and PCR shifted all three axes (X SMD = 0.35, P = 0.0001; Y SMD = 0.76, P < 0.00001; Z SMD = 0.66, P < 0.00001), whereas BM, GA, and AM showed no significant displacement on any axis — with I² mostly above 90%.

All techniques showed a certain degree of reproducibility within a narrow range; the authors conclude CR is a reproducible condylar *region* rather than a single technique-specific point, so the clinical equivalence or superiority of any single method cannot be definitively determined.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰·단일군 메타분석(1994~2024년 횡단면 연구 12편, 1693편 스크리닝)이 최대교두감합위(Maximum Intercuspation Position, MIP)를 공통 기준으로 턱끝유도(Chin-Point Guidance, CPG)·양수조작(Bimanual Manipulation, BM)·로스 파워 중심위 기록(Roth's Power-Centric bite registration, PCR)·고딕아치 추적자(Gothic Arch tracer, GA)·능동운동(Active Movement, AM) 5가지 중심위(Centric Relation, CR) 기록법의 과두 변위를 비교했다.

풀링 결과 턱끝유도는 Y축만 유의하게 이동(표준화평균차(Standardized Mean Difference, SMD) = 1.17, 95% 신뢰구간(Confidence Interval, CI) 0.19~2.16, P = 0.02, I² = 98%), 파워 중심위는 세 축 모두 유의한 변위(X SMD 0.35, P = 0.0001; Y 0.76, P < 0.00001; Z 0.66, P < 0.00001)를 보였고, 양수조작·고딕아치·능동운동은 어느 축에서도 유의차가 없었다(대부분 I² > 90%).

모든 술식은 좁은 범위에서 일정한 재현성을 보였고 중심위는 특정 술식으로만 얻는 단일 점이 아니라 재현 가능한 과두 영역이라는 결론 — 따라서 단일 술식의 임상적 동등성·우월성은 확정할 수 없다.

**Abstract Results (verbatim from artifact):**

> This study included 12 articles published between 1994 and 2024. Five different types of CR recording techniques were analyzed. Most techniques showed slight differences in CR and MIP with moderate to high heterogeneity. The chin-point guidance technique demonstrated a significant difference in the Y-axis (SMD = 1.17, P = 0.02), but no statistically significant differences were observed in the X and Z axes between CR and MIP. Roth's power-centric relation recording method was the only technique to show significant differences relative to MIP in all three axes. Dawson's bimanual method and Gothic arch tracer method for CR recording did not reveal any significant differences in condylar position on any axis.

**Abstract Conclusion (verbatim from artifact):**

> While the various methods for obtaining CR records in this study have their own advantages and disadvantages, and the CR positions obtained differed among the techniques, they all demonstrated a certain degree of reproducibility.

## Key Contributions

- **MIP as a common zero point, not an anatomic CR gold standard.** The review deliberately anchors every technique to the patient's maximum intercuspal position (habitual occlusion) in a three-dimensional coordinate system (X = sagittal, +anterior; Y = vertical, +superior; Z = transverse), so technique-to-technique condylar offsets become quantitatively comparable.
- **Five-technique quantitative synthesis.** Single-arm pooling of CPG, BM, PCR, GA, and AM displaced condylar position along all three axes; the included-study counts per technique were CPG 3, BM 5, PCR 6, GA 3, AM 3, with 5 studies reporting bilateral condylar positions.
- **Technique-specific signal.** Only PCR shifted all three axes significantly; CPG shifted only vertically (Y); BM, GA, and AM were non-significant on every axis — but with high heterogeneity (I² up to 99%), so the paper declines to declare any technique superior.
- **CR-as-region reframing.** The discussion explicitly endorses the notion that CR is "a reproducible region of condylar position rather than a single point obtainable only through a specific occlusal method" (ref. 32), allowing technique choice by patient habit, intraoral condition, and operator proficiency.
- **Direction-of-error and OVD cautions carried forward.** The GA discussion cites Jász et al. (ref. 16) that the GA vertex lay anterior to MIP through condylar translation, and warns against recording at extreme jaw positions (>6 mm from center); CPG force should be light (≈1–2 pounds / 4.4–8.8 N) to avoid a forced posterior seat.

## Methodology

- **Question (PICO)**: P = individuals receiving CR recording; I = condylar position from CR records via different techniques; C = MIP condylar location as benchmark; O = CR–MIP condylar-head difference and consistency.
- **Search**: PubMed, EMBASE, Google Scholar, Cochrane Library (plus PubMed "related articles"), English-language only, no publication-year restriction, up to January 2026. Keywords/MeSH: CR technique, MIP, chin-point guidance, bimanual manipulation, gothic arch, CR registration.
- **Inclusion**: clearly defined patient criteria; complete/near-complete dentition; a CR-positioning method; ≥5 subjects; quantitative condylar data (mean, SD, n) permitting SMD; English original research. **Exclusion**: edentulous or removable-denture patients, reviews/case reports/duplicates/SRs, studies not quantifying CR–MIP, and CR–CR-only comparisons.
- **Screening flow (as stated)**: 1,693 studies identified → 97 full texts reviewed → 45 trials relevant to CR and MIP → after exclusions (11 no MIP reference, 2 questionnaires, 7 edentulous, 3 TMD, 2 CBCT, 2 MRI, 1 T-scan, 2 CR-material accuracy, 3 percentage-only conclusions) → **12 articles included** (≈1994–2024).
- **Measurement & analysis**: two reviewers (L.S.H., Y.C.H.) extracted data, discrepancies resolved by a third (I.T.W.); condylar positions by CPI recording method, MIP-calibrated 3D coordinates; SMD with 95% CI, DerSimonian–Laird random-effects model, I² heterogeneity, five technique subgroups, three axes. Single-arm meta-analysis reported descriptively.
- **Bias**: ROBINS-I; the paper states "three trials exhibited a moderate risk of bias, while ten trials showed a low risk" — see Limitations for the internal count discrepancy.

## Limitations

- **All included studies were cross-sectional**, not randomized controlled trials: "All included studies employed cross-sectional designs rather than randomized controlled clinical trials." The paper notes studies lacking significant heterogeneity also lacked RCT designs.
- **Moderate-to-high heterogeneity** (I² up to 99%) across most axes — attributed to population anatomy, technique characteristics, measurement methods, and operator experience/learning curves — limits the strength of pooled findings (especially CPG's Y-axis result).
- **Small per-group samples**; the authors raise concerns about generalizability. Subjects were healthy, dentate, TMD-negative, no bruxism/orthodontics; edentulous and TMD patients were excluded by design, so the conclusion does not transfer to complete-denture patients directly.
- **No standardized comparison equipment**: the paper states CPI was used as the primary outcome measure "due to the lack of standardized equipment for comparing CR techniques"; CBCT/MRI were excluded from the analysis, though the authors suggest they may be incorporated in future work. Dentulous vs edentulous measurement reference points differ, further limiting eligible studies.
- **Internal count discrepancy reproduced verbatim**: for 12 included articles, the artifact states "three trials exhibited a moderate risk of bias, while ten trials showed a low risk" (3 + 10 = 13). This is left as written in the source; no silent correction.
- **Terminology typos in source** reproduced as-is: "Risfdfgk of Bias in Non-Randomized Studies of Interventions" (ROBINS-I), "frost plot" (forest plot), "DeSimonian and Laird" (DerSimonian–Laird).

## Glossary

- **CR (Centric Relation, 중심위)** — condyle–glenoid-fossa spatial relationship independent of tooth contact; here treated as a reproducible *region*, not a single point.
- **MIP (Maximum Intercuspation Position, 최대교두감합위)** — habitual full-tooth contact with no external force; used as the 0 point / comparison benchmark (also ICP/HO).
- **CPG (Chin-Point Guidance, 턱끝유도)** — operator applies light pressure to the chin depression to guide the mandible posterior-superiorly.
- **BM (Bimanual Manipulation, 양수조작)** — Dawson's two-handed passive technique seating the condyle superiorly in the fossa.
- **PCR (Power-Centric Recording, 파워 중심위 기록)** — Roth's two-piece wax technique combining an anterior stop with mandibular manipulation.
- **GA (Gothic Arch Tracer, 고딕아치 추적자)** — central-bearing-point / arrow-point tracing; vertices the CR position.
- **AM (Active Movement, 능동운동)** — patient-guided methods including swallowing/deglutition ("swallow or free closure").
- **SMD (Standardized Mean Difference, 표준화평균차)** — pooled effect size for continuous outcomes; 95% CI reported.
- **I²** — proportion of effect-size variance due to heterogeneity rather than chance.
- **ROBINS-I** — risk-of-bias tool for non-randomized studies of interventions.
- **CPI recording method** — the condylar-position indication method used to derive 3D coordinates in the included reports.
