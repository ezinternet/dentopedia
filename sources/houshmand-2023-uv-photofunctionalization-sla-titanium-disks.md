## Why Ingested

플라즈마/UV 표면 재활성화 오버뷰 작업 중 "탄화수소 오염이 정확히 무엇인가"라는 질문에 답하려 PubMed에서 찾은 논문. [[implants/surface/hayashi-2014-hydrocarbon-attenuates-osteoblast-titanium]]와 [[implants/surface/minamikawa-2016-long-term-degradation-titanium-biological]]은 탄화수소 축적이 골아세포 활성을 억제한다는 것만 보였는데, 이 논문이 인용하는 Roy et al 모델은 처음으로 **오염의 화학적 기전**(대기 노출 시 산화된 티타늄 표면이 탄화수소 오염물과 카르복실기·아민기 반응으로 결합)을 명시한다. 또한 EDS 기반으로 UVB와 UVC를 같은 SLA 디스크에서 직접 head-to-head 비교한 최초 국지적 근거.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (J Adv Periodontol Implant Dent 2023): 24 commercial SLA titanium disks (>4 weeks old, biologically aged) in 3 groups (n=8) — control, UVC (210 nm, 48 h), UVB (310 nm, 48 h) — assessed by EDS/SEM for surface C/N/O atomic concentration and Sessile-Drop contact angle.

UVC significantly reduced surface carbon (0.75 vs 0.95% control, p<0.001) and nitrogen (3.92 vs 4.17%, p<0.001) and produced the lowest contact angle (16.40° vs 129.88° control, p<0.001); UVB reduced contact angle (58.92°) but did NOT significantly reduce carbon (p=0.171) or nitrogen (p=0.785) vs control — oxygen was unchanged in all groups.

Cites the Roy et al mechanistic model: atmospheric exposure lets the oxidized titanium surface bind hydrocarbon contaminants via carboxyl/amine group reactions, and UV photons dissociate this hydrocarbon–titanium bond — UVC did this effectively, UVB achieved hydrophilicity without actually removing the underlying carbon/nitrogen contamination.

## 세줄요약

In vitro 연구(J Adv Periodontol Implant Dent 2023): 상업용 SLA 티타늄 디스크 24개(4주 이상 경과, 생물학적 노화됨) 3군(n=8) — 대조군·UVC(210nm, 48h)·UVB(310nm, 48h) — EDS/SEM으로 표면 탄소/질소/산소 원자농도, Sessile-Drop법으로 접촉각 측정.

UVC는 표면 탄소(0.75% vs 대조군 0.95%, p<0.001)·질소(3.92% vs 4.17%, p<0.001)를 유의하게 감소시키고 접촉각도 최저(16.40° vs 대조군 129.88°, p<0.001); UVB는 접촉각은 낮췄으나(58.92°) 탄소(p=0.171)·질소(p=0.785)는 대조군과 유의차 없음 — 산소는 3군 모두 변화 없음.

Roy et al 기전 모델 인용: 대기 노출 시 산화된 티타늄 표면이 카르복실기·아민기 반응으로 탄화수소 오염물과 결합하며, 자외선 광자가 이 탄화수소-티타늄 결합을 해리시킴 — UVC는 이를 실제로 수행했으나 UVB는 친수성만 만들고 탄소/질소 오염 자체는 못 없앰(친수성↔탄소제거가 분리될 수 있음을 보여준 사례).

## 1. Document Information

J Adv Periodontol Implant Dent 2023;15(2):117-122. DOI: 10.34172/japid.2023.015. PMID: 38357335, PMCID: PMC10862043. Shahid Beheshti University of Medical Sciences (Tehran) + Qazvin University of Medical Sciences.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Direct head-to-head EDS comparison of UVB vs UVC on the same commercial SLA disk batch (most prior work tests one wavelength or uses XPS on different disk sets).
- Demonstrates hydrophilicity and hydrocarbon removal can dissociate: UVB achieved a meaningfully lower contact angle than control (58.92° vs 129.88°) without significantly reducing surface carbon or nitrogen — challenges the assumption that wettability change is a reliable proxy for hydrocarbon removal.
- Cites and is consistent with the Roy et al carboxyl/amine binding model for how hydrocarbon contamination chemically attaches to the oxidized titanium surface — the clearest mechanistic statement of *how* the contamination forms that this wiki cluster holds to date.

## 3. Methodology

24 sandblasted, large-grit, acid-etched (SLA) commercial titanium disks (Biotem, Korea; 8 mm diameter × 2 mm thick), synthesized >4 weeks prior (biologically aged). 3 groups (n=8): control (intact), UVC (~210 nm, 20 W source, 6 W measured at 1 cm), UVB (~310 nm, 20 W source, 3.7 W measured at 1 cm), each irradiated 48 h at 1 cm distance. EDS via SEM (TeScan-Mira III) at 20 kV, ~200 µm scan area, 3 points/disk, weight % of C/N/O. Wettability via Sessile Drop (SCAM-S1, MNT), 4 µL deionized water, contact angle read at first surface contact. One-way ANOVA + Tukey/Games-Howell post hoc, SPSS 26.

## 4. Key Results

| Element/measure | UVC | UVB | Control | p (overall) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon (%) | 0.75±0.02 | 0.90±0.07 | 0.95±0.07 | <0.001 |
| Nitrogen (%) | 3.92±0.25 | 4.12±0.76 | 4.17±0.25 | <0.001 |
| Oxygen (%) | 4.62±0.32 | 4.82±0.29 | 4.97±0.52 | 0.064 (NS) |
| Contact angle (°) | 16.40±0.54 | 58.92±0.43 | 129.88±0.70 | <0.001 |

Pairwise: UVC carbon/nitrogen significantly lower than both UVB and control (p<0.001 carbon; nitrogen UVC vs UVB p=0.047, UVC vs control p<0.001). UVB carbon NOT significantly different from control (p=0.171); UVB nitrogen NOT significantly different from control (p=0.785). Contact angle: all three pairwise comparisons significant (p<0.001).

## 5. Limitations

In vitro (no cell/animal/human outcome); no XPS available (EDS is less surface-sensitive/quantitative than XPS, which the authors explicitly note as a limitation); single 48 h exposure duration only (no dose-response across durations); single commercial disk source (Biotem, Korea); no biological/osteoblast readout to confirm the carbon reduction translates to activity gain (relies on citing Hori et al and others for that link).

## 7. Glossary

EDS: Energy Dispersive Spectroscopy — SEM-based elemental surface analysis (less sensitive than XPS but does not require vacuum-only sample prep); SLA: Sandblasted, Large-grit, Acid-etched titanium surface; UVB/UVC: ultraviolet B (290–320 nm) / C (100–290 nm) wavebands; Sessile Drop method: contact-angle measurement by depositing a liquid droplet and imaging its profile.
