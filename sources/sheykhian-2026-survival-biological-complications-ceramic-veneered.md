## Why Ingested

[[wiki/prosthetic-materials/pjetursson-2023-implant-fdp-material-design-outcomes-sr-ma]]는 posterior implant-supported multi-unit FDP를 material별(PFM/veneered Zr/monolithic Zr/LDS)로 비교한 SR+MA로, veneered zirconia FDP가 chipping/fracture 발생률이 가장 높다는 결론을 냈다. 본 Sheykhian 2026 SR+MA는 그 하위 서브셋인 **screw-retained ceramic-veneered ISFDP만을 isolate**하여 survival 97%·success 82%·complication 13%라는 focused pooled estimate를 제공, retention type(screw)까지 통제한 더 좁은 근거를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (7 cohort/prospective studies, PRISMA 2020, 694 patients / 663 screw-retained ceramic-veneered implant-supported fixed dental prostheses [ISFDPs] on 1246 implants, follow-up 3–12 years) evaluating survival, success, and biological complication rates.

Pooled survival rate was 97% (ES 0.97, 95% CI 0.92–1.09, fixed-effects, I²=0.0%), pooled success rate was 82% (ES 0.82, 95% CI 0.74–0.90, random-effects DerSimonian–Laird, I²=55.3%), and pooled complication rate was 13% (ES 0.13, 95% CI 0.08–0.18, fixed-effects, I²=5.7%); the most common complications were ceramic chipping, screw loosening, and screw/prosthesis fracture.

Screw-retained ceramic-veneered ISFDPs are a predictable long-term option (high survival, acceptable complication burden), but the moderate heterogeneity in success-rate pooling (differing "success" definitions across studies), predominance of retrospective designs, and small number of included studies (n=7) limit precision; no PROSPERO registration.

## 세줄요약

체계적 고찰 + 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA) (코호트/전향 연구 7편, PRISMA 2020 기반, 환자 694명 / 나사고정 세라믹-비니어드 임플란트 지지 고정성 보철물[ISFDP] 663개, 임플란트 1246개, 추적 3–12년) — 생존율·성공율·생물학적 합병증률 평가.

풀링된 생존율(survival rate) 97% (ES 0.97, 95% CI 0.92–1.09, 고정효과모형, I²=0.0%), 성공율(success rate) 82% (ES 0.82, 95% CI 0.74–0.90, 랜덤효과모형 DerSimonian–Laird, I²=55.3%), 합병증률(complication rate) 13% (ES 0.13, 95% CI 0.08–0.18, 고정효과모형, I²=5.7%); 가장 흔한 합병증은 세라믹 치핑(chipping), 나사 풀림(screw loosening), 나사·보철물 파절.

나사고정 세라믹-비니어드 ISFDP는 예측 가능한 장기 치료옵션(높은 생존율·수용 가능한 합병증)이나, 성공율 풀링에서 중등도 이질성(연구간 "성공" 정의 차이), 후향적 연구 위주 설계, 포함 연구 수 적음(n=7)이 정밀도를 제한; PROSPERO 사전등록 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides a focused (retention-type-controlled) pooled estimate isolating **screw-retained** ceramic-veneered ISFDPs specifically, unlike prior SR+MAs that pooled heterogeneous retention/material combinations (contrasted explicitly against Pozzi et al. and Pjetursson et al. in the Discussion).
- Reports three separate pooled outcomes with different statistical models chosen by heterogeneity level: survival (fixed-effects, I²=0.0%), success (random-effects DerSimonian–Laird, I²=55.3%), complication (fixed-effects, I²=5.7%).
- Sensitivity analysis excluding the two moderate-risk-of-bias studies (Diéguez-Pereira 2020, Wittneben 2014) showed stable pooled survival/complication estimates, supporting robustness.
- Discussion contextualizes complication mechanism: ceramic chipping in veneered zirconia is attributed to CTE (coefficient of thermal expansion) mismatch between zirconia core and veneering ceramic plus residual cooling-phase tensile stress, exacerbated by bruxism/occlusal overload; contrasts explicitly with monolithic zirconia's digital-workflow reproducibility advantage.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Databases**: Cochrane, Embase, MEDLINE (PubMed and Ovid), searched to January 2025; supplemented by reference checking and Google Scholar; English-language only.
- **PICO**: Population = patients missing >2 teeth (upper/lower/both jaws); Intervention = screw-retained ceramic-veneered ISFDPs; Comparison = not applicable; Outcome = survival rate, success rate, complication rate.
- **Exclusion**: non-RCT/non-cohort designs.
- **Screening**: two independent blinded reviewers, third reviewer for consensus.
- **Risk of bias**: Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (NOS), 0–9 scale (high risk 0–3, moderate 4–6, low 7–9). 5 studies rated low risk, 2 moderate risk, 0 high risk.
- **Statistics**: effect size (ES) with 95% CI; fixed-effects inverse-variance model for survival and complication rate (low/no heterogeneity); random-effects DerSimonian–Laird model for success rate (moderate/significant heterogeneity, I²=55.3%, p=0.04); heterogeneity assessed via chi-square/I² (thresholds: ≤25% low, 25–50% medium, 50–75% high, >75% very high); Stata 17; significance threshold p<0.05; sensitivity analysis excluding moderate-risk-of-bias studies.
- **PRISMA flow**: 474 records identified → 61 duplicates + 22 automation-excluded + 18 other removed before screening → 373 screened → 316 excluded → 57 full-text assessed → 50 excluded (8 incomplete data, 5 not screw-retained ceramic-veneered ISFDP, 34 no extractable survival/complication data, 3 without separable data) → 7 included.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Study pool**: 694 patients (369 female, 325 male), mean age 56.58 years; 663 ISFDPs supported by 1246 implants; follow-up 3–12 years across included studies (Pontevedra 2024, Zhang 2024, Solá-Ruiz 2022, Pontevedra 2022, De Angelis 2021, Diéguez-Pereira 2020, Wittneben 2014).
- **Survival rate**: 97% (ES 0.97; 95% CI 0.92–1.09; I²=0.0%, Q=3.41, p=0.7565) — fixed-effects.
- **Success rate**: 82% (ES 0.82; 95% CI 0.74–0.90; τ²=0.0058, I²=55.29%, Q=13.42, p=0.04) — random-effects.
- **Complication rate**: 13% (ES 0.13; 95% CI 0.08–0.18; I²=5.7%, Q=6.36, p=0.3839) — fixed-effects.
- **Complication types**: ceramic chipping, screw loosening, screw fracture, prosthesis fracture, proximal contact loss, loss of implant access hole sealing material.
- **Sensitivity analysis**: excluding the 2 moderate-risk-of-bias studies (Diéguez-Pereira 2020, Wittneben 2014) — pooled survival/complication rates remained stable.
- Discussion cites external context: chipping can reduce ISFDP survival by up to 50% in some literature [ref 21]; PFM ISFDPs historically ~96.4% at 5y / 93.9% at 10y [refs 7,8].

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 7 studies included, mostly retrospective (5 of 7 are "ReS"), limiting causal inference and generalizability.
- Moderate/significant heterogeneity in success-rate pooling (I²=55.3%) attributed to inconsistent definitions of "success" across studies (some counted any complication as failure, others allowed minor manageable events).
- Incomplete reporting of patient-level risk factors (bruxism, smoking, systemic disease, medication, periodontal history) across included studies — could not be adjusted for.
- Planned subgroup/sensitivity analyses by prosthesis design or complication type were not statistically feasible due to small numbers per subgroup and inconsistent variable reporting.
- No PROSPERO registration.
- Authors call for prospective studies and RCTs with standardized success definitions and longer follow-up.

## 7. Glossary

- **ISFDP**: Implant-Supported Fixed Dental Prosthesis
- **NOS**: Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (risk-of-bias tool for cohort studies)
- **ES**: Effect Size (here, pooled proportion)
- **I²**: heterogeneity index (percentage of variance across studies attributable to heterogeneity rather than chance)
- **CTE**: Coefficient of Thermal Expansion
- **PFM**: Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal
- **PICO**: Population, Intervention, Comparison, Outcome (systematic review framework)
