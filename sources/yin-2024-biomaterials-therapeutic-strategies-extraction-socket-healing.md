## Why Ingested

This review fills a materials-science gap in the wiki's extraction socket healing knowledge. While existing pages such as [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/fok-2024-alveolar-socket-healing-evolving-knowledge]] address socket healing biology and alveolar ridge preservation outcomes, none systematically map the biomaterials landscape — scaffold types, bioactive agents, drug-delivery carriers, and their molecular targets — to the healing phases.

Yin et al. (2024) provide a mechanistic bridge: they ground biomaterial selection in the cellular-molecular events of socket healing (hemostasis, inflammation, proliferation, remodeling) and then survey emerging strategies including growth-factor delivery, immunomodulatory materials, and anti-infective biomaterials. This makes it a high-utility reference for understanding why specific materials are chosen and what therapeutic gaps remain.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review of biomaterials and therapeutic strategies for tooth extraction socket healing, synthesizing cellular-molecular healing biology with advances in materials science and bioengineering.

Reviews the current landscape of biomaterials for socket healing — including growth-factor carriers, immunomodulatory scaffolds, and anti-infective materials — and identifies emerging therapeutic targets.

Provides a conceptual framework linking healing-phase biology to material design, but as a narrative review lacks head-to-head efficacy data; clinical translation of the highlighted strategies remains early-stage.

## 세줄요약

발치와 치유를 위한 생체재료 및 치료 전략을 다루는 서사적 고찰로, 세포·분자 수준의 치유 생물학과 재료과학 발전을 종합한다.

발치와 치유에 사용되는 생체재료의 현황(성장인자 전달체, 면역조절 지지체, 항감염 재료 등)을 검토하고 새로운 치료 표적을 제시한다.

치유 단계별 생물학을 재료 설계와 연결하는 개념적 틀을 제공하지만, 서사적 고찰 특성상 직접 비교 효능 데이터가 없으며 임상 적용은 아직 초기 단계다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Synthesizes the fundamental biology of extraction socket healing — hemostasis, inflammation, proliferation, and remodeling — as a rational basis for biomaterial design
- Maps the current biomaterials landscape for socket healing, categorizing strategies by mechanism (growth factor delivery, immunomodulation, antimicrobial activity)
- Identifies emerging therapeutic targets and molecular pathways relevant to post-extraction complications (delayed healing, dry socket, ridge resorption)
- Highlights advances in drug-delivery systems and bioinspired scaffolds applicable to ridge preservation and implant site development
- Outlines clinical translation challenges and future research directions for biomaterial-enhanced socket healing

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Narrative review (Journal Article; Review). Scope covers: (1) fundamental healing processes and cellular-molecular interactions in extraction sockets; (2) current biomaterials used for socket healing; (3) emerging strategies and potential therapeutic targets; (4) future directions and clinical translation challenges. No systematic search protocol or PRISMA flow reported; breadth-oriented synthesis drawing on materials science, cell biology, and oral surgery literature.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- The oral microenvironment (salivary flow, oral movements, bacterial biofilms) is framed as a principal driver of delayed socket healing and post-extraction complications
- Biomaterials reviewed span multiple classes: scaffolds, growth-factor delivery carriers, immunomodulatory agents, and anti-infective materials
- Emerging strategies include targeting specific cellular-molecular pathways to modulate the inflammatory phase and promote angiogenesis and osteogenesis
- Clinical application of advanced biomaterials faces challenges including biocompatibility, controlled release kinetics, infection risk, and regulatory translation
- Review positions biomaterial-enhanced socket management as integral to aesthetic and functional rehabilitation, particularly for implant site preparation
