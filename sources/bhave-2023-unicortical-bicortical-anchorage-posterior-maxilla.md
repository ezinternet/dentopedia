## Why Ingested

First split-mouth prospective clinical study directly comparing OSFE-based bicortical anchorage (implant engages sinus floor) vs unicortical conventional placement in atrophic posterior maxilla. Fills a critical evidence gap: [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/albadani-2024-graftless-sinus-lifting-simultaneous]] and [[wiki/sinus-lift/transcrestal/albash-2023-implant-protrusion-initial-bone-tsfe]] cover graftless TSFE outcomes but neither isolates bicortical engagement vs unicortical as the experimental variable with RFA and bone loss endpoints.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective split-mouth RCT, 15 patients / 42 implants, posterior maxilla; one side OSFE + bicortical anchorage (1 mm past sinus floor), opposite side conventional unicortical (0.5–1 mm short of floor).
Bicortical group: higher baseline ISQ (63.6 vs 57.9), 1-month ISQ (67.0 vs 62.0), 3-month ISQ (71.1 vs 65.7), all p<0.001; crestal bone loss equivalent at 3 months (0.09 mm mesial, 0.08 mm distal, p>0.05).
Bicortical anchorage via OSFE achieves statistically superior implant stability (임플란트 안정성 지수, ISQ) without increased crestal bone loss vs unicortical, supporting its use when RBH ≥10 mm.

## 세줄요약

전향적 분리구강 시험, 15명/42개 임플란트, 후방 상악; 한쪽 OSFE + 쌍겹질 고정(상악동 바닥 1 mm 관통), 반대쪽 단겹질 일반 식립(바닥 0.5–1 mm 여유).
쌍겹질군 기저 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ) 63.6 vs 57.9, 3개월 71.1 vs 65.7 (p<0.001); 치조정 골소실 (Crestal Bone Loss, CBL) 3개월 차 양군 동등(p>0.05).
잔존골고 (Residual Bone Height, RBH) ≥10 mm에서 OSFE 기반 쌍겹질 고정은 골소실 없이 우월한 안정성 달성 — 단겹질 회피 임상 대안으로 지지.

## Document Information

- **Journal**: National Journal of Maxillofacial Surgery (India)
- **DOI**: [10.4103/njms.njms_7_22](https://doi.org/10.4103/njms.njms_7_22)
- **Institution**: Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery & Prosthodontics, India
- **Study type**: Split-mouth prospective clinical study
- **n**: 15 patients, 22 bicortical implants (Group I), 20 unicortical implants (Group II)

## Key Contributions

- Demonstrates bicortical (sinus floor engagement via OSFE) yields significantly higher ISQ at all time points vs unicortical
- Crestal bone loss equivalent between techniques through 3-month follow-up
- No sinus membrane perforations or nasal complications in either group
- Uses HC2 (Switzerland) roughened surface implants; bicortical group received longer implants (mean 12.11 mm vs 10.78 mm)
- Mean RBH both groups ~11 mm (no significant difference p=0.306)

## Methodology

- **Design**: Split-mouth prospective, June 2018–July 2019
- **Patients**: 15 (9M:6F), mean age 39.9 years, bilateral posterior maxilla edentulous (Kennedy Class I)
- **Group I (bicortical)**: OSFE + implant 1 mm longer than available bone, engaging sinus floor; n=22 implants
- **Group II (unicortical)**: Conventional drilling, implant 0.5–1 mm short of floor; n=20 implants
- **Implant**: HC2 roughened SLA-type, mostly 4.2 mm width; Group I predominantly 13 mm length, Group II 11.5 mm
- **Outcomes**: ISQ (Osstell) at baseline, 1, 3 months; crestal bone loss (OPG, ImageJ) at 1, 3 months; pain VAS and swelling (Laskin) at 1, 7, 28, 84 days

## Key Results

| Timepoint | Bicortical ISQ | Unicortical ISQ | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 63.64 ± 2.57 | 57.90 ± 3.65 | <0.001 |
| 1 month | 66.95 ± 2.48 | 61.95 ± 3.41 | <0.001 |
| 3 months | 71.14 ± 3.37 | 65.70 ± 3.13 | <0.001 |

- Mesial CBL at 3 months: 0.09 mm (bicortical) vs 0.08 mm (unicortical), p=0.789 (NS)
- Distal CBL at 3 months: 0.07 mm (bicortical) vs 0.08 mm (unicortical), p=0.903 (NS)
- Pain scores: identical reduction patterns in both groups (no significant inter-group difference)
- Swelling: grade 1 initially → nil in both groups; no difference

## Limitations

- Short follow-up (3 months); no long-term survival or MBL data
- Small sample (n=15 patients)
- Indian tertiary center single-site study
- Implant length systematically longer in bicortical group (confounds ISQ advantage)
- No blinding; assessor not independent

## Glossary

- **Bicortical anchorage**: Implant engages two cortical plates — crestal cortex AND sinus floor cortex — for dual-cortex fixation
- **OSFE (Osteotomized Sinus Floor Elevation)**: Summers osteotome technique elevating sinus membrane crestal access
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: RFA-derived 0–100 score; <60 low, 60–70 moderate, >70 high stability
- **Unicortical**: Implant engages only crestal cortex; tip stops short of sinus floor
- **RBH (Residual Bone Height)**: Available bone height between crest and sinus floor on CBCT/OPG
