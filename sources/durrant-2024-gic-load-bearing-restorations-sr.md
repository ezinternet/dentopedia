## Why Ingested
RCT-level evidence on the long-contested load-bearing use of GIC; strengthens [[glass-ionomer/durrant-2024-gic-load-bearing-restorations-sr]] and the GIC overview's decision points.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review (PRISMA; EBSCO/PubMed/Embase/Cochrane; 12 RCTs included) evaluating the clinical performance of glass ionomer cement (GIC) in Class I and II load-bearing posterior restorations.

GIC showed comparable clinical performance to traditional restorative materials across multiple USPHS-type parameters including surface margin, retention, fracture, marginal adaptation, wear, occlusal contour, and recurrent caries; postoperative sensitivity was not significantly different.

GIC can be considered a viable alternative to conventional materials for load-bearing Class I/II restorations, though standardized long-term RCTs are needed given GIC's known lower fracture toughness and wear resistance.

## 세줄요약
체계적 문헌고찰(PRISMA; EBSCO/PubMed/Embase/Cochrane; RCT 12편 포함) — Class I·II 하중부 구치부 수복에서 글래스아이오노머(GIC)의 임상 성능을 평가.

GIC는 표면 변연·유지력·파절·변연 적합도·마모·교합 윤곽·이차 우식 등 다수 USPHS 기준 지표에서 기존 재료(복합레진 등)와 비교 가능한 결과를 보였으며, 술 후 민감도도 유의한 차이 없음.

GIC는 하중부 Class I·II 수복에서 대안 재료로 고려 가능하나, 파절 인성·마모 저항 한계와 이질적 결과 보고로 인해 장기 표준화 RCT가 필요함.

## 2. Key Contributions
- RCT-only synthesis scoped to load-bearing (Class I/II) restorations.
- Concludes overall performance comparable to traditional materials.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
PRISMA-style SR. Databases: EBSCO, PubMed, Embase, Cochrane. Inclusion: RCTs only; 12 trials. USPHS-type clinical outcome parameters.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
GIC suitable/comparable: surface margin, esthetic anatomical form, retention, fracture, marginal adaptation, occlusal contour, wear, approximal form. No significant difference: postoperative sensitivity, recurrent caries, tooth integrity.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
Heterogeneous outcome reporting; GIC's known lower fracture toughness/wear resistance; need standardized long-term RCTs.

## 6. Related Work
Black's classification (Class I/II); Minamata/EU amalgam restrictions; resin composite as alternative.

## 7. Glossary
- Load-bearing restoration: Class I (occlusal) or Class II (proximal ≥2 surfaces).
- USPHS criteria: clinical evaluation parameters for restorations.
