## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (9 systematic reviews of osseodensification [OD] vs conventional drilling; PROSPERO CRD420251116804; PubMed/Scopus/Web of Science/LILACS/Cochrane; AMSTAR-2 quality appraisal; abstract-only — full text not retrieved).

Most included SRs reported higher primary implant stability (insertion torque, ISQ) with OD, particularly in low-density bone; marginal bone level (MBL) change and implant survival were generally comparable between techniques, with survival >95% in both groups.

OD's clinical benefit appears limited to primary-stability gain in low-density bone, with no demonstrable long-term advantage in MBL preservation or survival; higher-quality RCTs are needed — echoes the same "stability-yes, MBL/survival-no" pattern already seen across individual OD SR+MAs in this wiki.

## 세줄요약

우산형 리뷰(umbrella review, 골밀도화(Osseodensification, OD) vs 통상 드릴링을 다룬 체계적 문헌고찰 9편 대상; PROSPERO CRD420251116804 등록; PubMed/Scopus/Web of Science/LILACS/Cochrane 검색; AMSTAR-2 방법론적 질 평가; 초록만 확보 — 본문 전문 미확보).

포함된 SR 대부분이 저밀도골에서 특히 OD가 삽입 토크(insertion torque, IT) 및 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)로 측정한 1차 안정성이 더 높다고 보고했으며, 변연골 소실(Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 변화와 임플란트 생존율은 두 기법 간 대체로 유사(양군 모두 생존율 95% 초과)했다.

OD의 임상적 이점은 저밀도골에서의 1차 안정성 향상에 국한되며 MBL 보존이나 장기 생존율에서는 뚜렷한 우위가 없음 — 고품질 RCT 추가 필요; 위키 내 개별 OD SR+MA들에서 이미 반복 확인된 "안정성 향상-예, MBL/생존율 개선-아니오" 패턴을 상위 근거 수준에서 재확인한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review (SR-of-SRs) synthesizing existing OD vs conventional-drilling systematic reviews, rather than primary studies.
- PROSPERO-registered protocol (CRD420251116804) with formal AMSTAR-2 methodological-quality appraisal of the 9 included SRs.
- Descriptive synthesis distinguishing three outcome domains: primary stability (IT/ISQ), marginal bone level, and implant survival — clarifying which OD claims are robust across the SR literature and which are not.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review of systematic reviews and meta-analyses (PROSPERO CRD420251116804).
- **Databases**: PubMed (MEDLINE), Scopus, Web of Science, LILACS, Cochrane Library.
- **n**: 9 systematic reviews included (primary-study/patient-level n not extractable from abstract).
- **Quality tool**: AMSTAR-2.
- **Outcomes**: primary implant stability (insertion torque, ISQ), marginal bone level change, implant survival.
- **Synthesis**: descriptive (no re-pooled quantitative meta-analysis of the 9 SRs reported in the abstract).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Most of the 9 included SRs reported **higher primary implant stability** (insertion torque and ISQ) with OD vs conventional drilling, **particularly in low-density bone**.
- **Marginal bone level (MBL) change**: generally comparable between OD and conventional drilling.
- **Implant survival**: comparable between techniques, exceeding **95%** in both groups.
- Full AMSTAR-2 scores per included SR, and the identity/overlap of the 9 SRs beyond the reference list, were not recoverable from the abstract-only extraction (paywalled full text).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only source — full-text Results/Discussion tables, per-SR AMSTAR-2 scoring, and citation-overlap (corrected covered area) analysis not captured in this ingest.
- Umbrella review inherits the limitations of its included SRs (small primary-study n, heterogeneous OD protocols/bur systems, mixed animal/cadaveric/clinical evidence pooled within some source SRs).
- Authors explicitly call for higher-quality RCTs to confirm the low-density-bone stability advantage.

## 6. Related Work

- inchingolo-2021: individual SR+MA included among the 9 umbrella-review sources; reports BIC/torque advantage — reinforced at umbrella level.
- gaspar-2022: individual SR included among the 9; implant-stability-only SR — reinforced at umbrella level.
- kalra-2025: individual SR+MA included among the 9 (also published in J Prosthet Dent); first to pool crestal bone level, found null CBL result — matches this umbrella review's "no MBL advantage" conclusion.

## 7. Glossary

- **Umbrella review**: a systematic review whose unit of inclusion is other systematic reviews/meta-analyses, synthesizing evidence-of-evidence.
- **AMSTAR-2**: A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews, version 2 — critical-appraisal instrument for SRs of randomized/non-randomized intervention studies.
- **Osseodensification (OD)**: bone-preserving, non-subtractive osteotomy drilling technique (e.g., Densah burs) intended to compact/densify bone along the osteotomy wall to increase primary implant stability, particularly in low-density bone.
- **Insertion torque (IT) / Implant Stability Quotient (ISQ)**: surrogate measures of primary (mechanical) implant stability at placement.
