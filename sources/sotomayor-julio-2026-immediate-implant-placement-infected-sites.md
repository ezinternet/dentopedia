## Why Ingested

2026-09-29 서베일런스 (급성/화농성 감염 소켓 즉시식립) 중 발견. 감염 소켓 + 협측골벽 결손 상황에서 플랩·차단막 없이 rhBMP-2/콜라겐/BCP 롤로 협측골을 재건한 단일 증례라, 기존 [[immediate-implant/infected-socket/pranckeviciene-2024-immediate-implant-periapical-pathology-sr-ma]]의 만성 병변 즉시식립 근거에 임상적 술식 사례를 보태고, [[immediate-implant/gap-grafting/ebrahim-2026-vestibular-socket-therapy-immediate-implant-rct]] 등 협측 결손 관리 술식과 비교 맥락을 제공한다. 주의: 감염은 만성(chronic)이며 급성 농양/화농은 기술되지 않음.

## Three-line Summary

Single case report (n=1, 56-year-old woman, tooth 2.1, chronic infection with vertical root fracture and absent buccal plate), 36-month follow-up, plus an exploratory in silico bioinformatic analysis.

Flapless, membrane-free immediate implant with a collagen-sponge/BCP "Roll" and 0.1 mg rhBMP-2 gave a Pink Esthetic Score of 13 (stable M12-M36) and CBCT-visible buccal cortical reconstruction by M18, sustained to M36; bioinformatics flagged BMP2, RUNX2, SPP1, BGLAP as hub genes.

n=1 with no control, no histology, and no PROM; the infection was chronic (no pus or acute abscess documented), so it cannot support claims about acute purulent sites.

## 세줄요약

증례보고 (n=1, 56세 여성, 2.1번, 수직 치근파절+만성 감염+협측골판 소실), 36개월 추적, 탐색적 생물정보학 분석 병행.

무판막·무차단막 즉시식립 + 콜라겐스펀지/BCP 롤 + rhBMP-2 0.1 mg로 핑크 심미 점수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES) 13 (12~36개월 유지), 18개월 CBCT에서 협측 피질골 재건이 관찰되어 36개월까지 유지; BMP2·RUNX2·SPP1·BGLAP가 핵심 유전자로 제시됨.

n=1, 대조군·조직학·환자보고결과 (PROM) 없음; 감염은 만성이며 급성 농양·화농은 기술되지 않아 급성 화농 부위 근거로는 쓸 수 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Introduces the "Roll BMP" technique: BCP granules layered on hydrated absorbable collagen sponge (ACS), rolled into a 7-10 mm cylinder, packed in the buccal gap, then injected with hydrated rhBMP-2 at three levels (coronal, middle, apical).
- Flapless, membrane-free; soft-tissue closure by secondary intention or 5/0 resorbable sutures if needed.
- Adds an in silico analysis (GENIE, STRING, Cytoscape/cytoHubba) proposing BMP2, RUNX2, SPP1, BGLAP as hub genes; authors call it exploratory.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Patient: healthy 56-year-old woman; tooth 2.1 with 5-year metal-ceramic crown on cast post-and-core, mobility from partial dislodgment, gingival inflammation, secondary caries, vertical root fracture; periapical radiolucency and suggestive root perforation; CBCT: buccal plate absent, "slight periapical lesion".
- Surgery (D1): atraumatic extraction and local debridement; 3.5 x 12 mm INNO implant placed subcrestally (4-mm marginal gingival reference); Roll BMP with 0.1 g BCP, 0.1 mg rhBMP-2, Atelocare ACS; Oraid resorbable pad; composite-bonded provisional (Ribbond).
- Medication: amoxicillin 1 g q12h x 7 days; paracetamol 1 g q12h x 3 days; ketoprofen 100 mg q12h x 3 days.
- Follow-up: periapical D1, M3, M5, M12, M36; CBCT M18, M24, M36; PES at M3, M5, M12-M36.
- Definitive zirconia abutment and lithium disilicate crown at M5.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- PES: 9 (M3), 11 (M5, after definitive prosthesis), 13/14 stable M12-M36 (minor mesial papilla deficiency).
- Radiographic: granules lost and initial consolidation M3; trabecular maturation M5; stable density M12.
- CBCT: new buccal cortical plate thicker/denser than contralateral tooth at M18 (17.4 mm vertical measurement from implant apex to coronal level per legend); corticalization to crestal level sustained M24-M36.
- Patient reported satisfaction and no pain or procedure-related adverse events; no formal PROM.
- Bioinformatics: 75 genes; GO-BP ossification/biomineralization; hub genes BMP2, RUNX2, SPP1, BGLAP; three clusters (inflammatory/angiogenic, osteogenic, mineral regulation).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single case, no control or comparison to GBR; buccal bone gain measured qualitatively on CBCT; no histology.
- Infection was chronic (root fracture, periapical lesion); no pus, abscess, sinus tract, or culture reported; infection severity not graded.
- Antibiotic given post-operatively only as described; no pre-operative course, no separate decontamination agent, no wait period reported.
- Off-label/regulatory and cost limits for rhBMP-2 acknowledged; bioinformatics is hypothesis-generating (p-values, FDR from a MEDLINE text-mining tool, not patient data).
- Authors call for controlled trials with histomorphometry and dose optimization.

## 7. Glossary

- Roll BMP: collagen sponge rolled around BCP granules, loaded with rhBMP-2, placed in the buccal gap.
- rhBMP-2: recombinant human bone morphogenetic protein-2, osteoinductive growth factor.
- BCP: biphasic calcium phosphate (HA + beta-TCP) graft granules.
- Type 2 socket: socket with partial buccal plate loss/soft tissue present (as used by the authors).
- PES: Pink Esthetic Score (max 14).
