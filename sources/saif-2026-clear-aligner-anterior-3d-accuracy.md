## Why Ingested
Focused 3D analysis of anterior tooth movement accuracy (Invisalign, first set only, no refinement) — provides movement-specific benchmarks across 4 movement types for the anterior region; complements [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/garcia-marin-2025-clear-aligner-molar-mesiodistal-sr]] (molar mesiodistal SR) to cover the full arch accuracy picture; unique in isolating first-phase accuracy before any refinement.

## Three-line Summary
Retrospective study (40 adults, avg 27.8 years, Invisalign, non-extraction, first aligner set without refinement; 480 anterior teeth; 10-day aligner change; avg 27 aligners) comparing initial intraoral scans + ClinCheck predictions with actual final scans for 4 movement types (vertical, mesiodistal rotation, mesiodistal tipping, buccolingual tipping) in upper and lower anterior teeth.

Overall mean accuracy 50.3% (upper 47.6%, lower 53%); worst: upper incisor intrusion 23.1%; best upper: distal tipping 64.85%, mesial rotation 63.32%; best lower: lingual tipping 75.52%, labial tipping 66.51%; all 4 movement types showed significant differences between predicted and achieved (p<0.05 for all).

ClinCheck overestimates achievable anterior tooth movement in all four planes; intrusion is the least predictable anterior movement; refinements are recommended for almost all patients with initial aligner sets; 50% accuracy baseline should inform treatment planning for anterior cases.

## 세줄요약
후향적 연구(40명 성인, 평균 27.8세, Invisalign, 비발치, 초기 교정장치 세트만·재치료 없음; 전치부 480개; 10일 교체; 평균 27개 교정장치) — ClinCheck 예측치 vs 실제 결과 비교; 수직이동·근원심 회전·근원심 경사·협설 경사 4가지 이동 유형.

전체 평균 정확도 50.3% (상악 47.6%, 하악 53%); 최악: 상악 절치 압하(수직이동) 23.1%; 상악 최고: 원심 경사 64.85%·근심 회전 63.32%; 하악 최고: 설측 경사 75.52%·순측 경사 66.51%; 4가지 이동 유형 모두 예측치-실제값 간 유의한 차이(p<0.05).

ClinCheck는 모든 방향에서 전치부 이동량을 과대평가; 압하가 가장 예측하기 어려운 전치 이동; 초기 교정장치 세트 후 거의 모든 환자에서 재치료 권장; 50% 정확도 기준으로 전치부 치료계획 수립 필요.

## 1. Document Information
- **Type**: Retrospective observational study
- **Journal**: Scientific Reports
- **Year**: 2026 (published March 4, 2026)
- **Institution**: Xi'an Jiaotong University (First Affiliated Hospital) + West China Hospital (Sichuan University)
- **Sample**: 40 adults (18M/22F), avg 27.8 years
- **Teeth**: 480 anterior teeth (upper + lower)
- **IRB**: XJTU1AF2022LSK-186

## 2. Key Contributions
- Isolated first-set accuracy without any refinement — unique design that establishes true baseline CA performance before corrective interventions
- Simultaneous quantification of 4 movement types in same population
- Confirms intrusion as least predictable anterior movement (23.1%)
- Establishes buccolingual tipping as most predictable (lingual 75.52% lower)
- Sample size calculation (G*Power): 38 patients required at α=0.05, 85% power, d=0.5

## 3. Methodology
- **Design**: Retrospective; Declaration of Helsinki; XJTU1AF2022LSK-186
- **Inclusion**: Adults, non-extraction (except 3rd molars), finished first aligner set without midcourse correction or refinement, no active periodontal disease
- **System**: Invisalign (Align Technology, California) — all treated by Invisalign-certified orthodontists
- **Protocol**: 10-day wear per aligner; max 0.25 mm linear movement, 2° rotation, 1° tipping per aligner
- **Evaluation**: Initial and final intraoral scans + ClinCheck predicted final digital models
- **Movements**: Vertical (intrusion/extrusion as linear mm), mesiodistal rotation (°), mesiodistal crown tipping (°), buccolingual crown tipping (°)
- **Treatment period**: Jan 2019 – Feb 2024; avg 27 aligners, avg 9 months duration
- **Statistics**: Paired t-tests or Wilcoxon signed-rank; effect sizes (Cohen's d); ICC for intraexaminer reliability

## 4. Key Results

**Overall accuracy:**

| Group | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| All anterior teeth | 50.3% |
| Upper anterior | 47.6% |
| Lower anterior | 53.0% |

**By movement type (upper dentition):**

| Movement | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| Distal tipping | **64.85%** (best upper) |
| Mesial rotation | 63.32% |
| Intrusion (vertical) | **23.1%** (worst) |
| Labial/lingual tipping | Intermediate |

**By movement type (lower dentition):**

| Movement | Accuracy |
|---|---|
| Lingual tipping | **75.52%** (best lower) |
| Labial tipping | 66.51% |

- All 4 movement types: significant predicted vs achieved differences (p<0.05 for all)
- Upper incisors: greatest discrepancy observed for maxillary central incisors
- Intrusion movements: systematically over-prescribed in ClinCheck
- No refinements performed in this study — results represent raw first-set efficacy

## 5. Limitations
- All Invisalign — not generalizable to other CA brands
- Single-institution, single operator type (Invisalign-certified)
- Retrospective; selection bias possible
- No refinement — may not reflect final clinical outcomes after complete treatment

## 6. Related Work
- Haouili et al.: overall 50% Invisalign accuracy for all tooth types (aligns with this study)
- Sachdev: 56.18% overall anterior accuracy (slightly higher, may include refinements)
- Murphy et al.: aligners achieve significantly less tooth movement vs fixed in all directions (especially torque/tipping/rotation of maxillary canines)
- Systematic reviews cited: buccolingual tipping most predictable; extrusion/intrusion/rotation least predictable

## 7. Glossary
- **Accuracy**: % of planned movement achieved = [(planned - |planned-achieved|) / |planned|] × 100; 100% = perfect
- **Intrusion**: apical movement (tooth pressed into bone); historically worst CA movement
- **ClinCheck**: Align Technology's proprietary VTS software; prediction models consistently overestimate clinically achievable movement
