## Why Ingested

Provides direct comparative ex vivo data on BLX vs SP/TE/BL/BLT primary stability in a 5 mm residual bone model simulating sinus floor elevation — extends [[sinus-lift/transcrestal/cho-2024-crestal-sinus-augmentation-limited-bone]] and [[sinus-lift/lateral/allurkar-2025-implant-stability-residual-bone-macrodesign]] with Straumann-specific macrodesign comparison in low-bone conditions.

## Three-line Summary

Ex vivo comparative study using 5 mm porcine iliac crest blocks simulating a sinus floor elevation model with 42 blocks divided into 7 groups of Straumann implants (SP, TE, BL, BLT, BLX, and two short variants), measuring maximum insertion torque (MIT) and implant stability quotient (ISQ) by resonance frequency analysis.

Straumann BLX 10 mm showed significantly higher MIT than SP, BL (p<0.01) and TE (p<0.05), and significantly higher ISQ than all other implant types (p<0.01); BLX short 6 mm outperformed SP 10 mm in ISQ, indicating macrodesign can partially compensate for reduced implant length.

In atrophic maxilla conditions simulating residual bone height of 5 mm, selecting a compression-favoring variable-thread macrodesign (BLX) provides measurably superior primary stability — a controllable clinical factor before secondary osseointegration biology begins.

## 세줄요약

돼지 장골두 5mm 블록(상악동거상 시뮬레이션)에 42블록/7군 Straumann 임플란트(SP·TE·BL·BLT·BLX·단형 2종)를 식립하고 최대삽입토크(Maximum Insertion Torque, MIT)와 임플란트 안정성 지수(Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)를 측정한 생체 외(ex vivo) 비교 연구.

BLX 10mm는 MIT(SP·BL 대비 p<0.01, TE 대비 p<0.05) 및 ISQ(전 군 대비 p<0.01) 모두 유의하게 높았으며, BLX 단형 6mm가 SP 10mm보다 높은 ISQ를 기록해 매크로디자인이 길이 부족을 부분 보완함을 확인.

잔존골 높이(Residual Bone Height, RBH) 5mm의 상악 구치부 상황에서, 가압형 가변 나사산 매크로디자인(BLX) 선택은 2차 골유착 전 단계에서 통제 가능한 1차 안정성 향상 인자임.

## One-line Summary

Ex vivo porcine model (5 mm blocks): Straumann BLX showed significantly higher MIT and ISQ than SP, TE, BL and BLT in a sinus floor elevation simulation.

## 한줄요약

돼지 장골두 5mm 블록(상악동거상 시뮬레이션) 모델에서 Straumann BLX 임플란트의 최대삽입토크(MIT)와 임플란트 안정성 지수(ISQ)가 SP·TE·BL·BLT 대비 유의하게 높음.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: BMC Oral Health, 22:332
- **DOI**: 10.1186/s12903-022-02345-5
- **Institution**: Kyushu University, Faculty of Dental Science
- **Study design**: Ex vivo comparative study
- **n**: 42 porcine iliac crest bone blocks; 7 groups (6 implants each)

## 2. Key Contributions

- BLX showed significantly higher MIT than SP, BL (p<0.01) and TE (p<0.05)
- BLX showed significantly higher ISQ than all other groups (p<0.01)
- BLT showed higher MIT than BL (p<0.05) and SP (p<0.01)
- BLX 6mm short implant still outperformed SP 10mm in ISQ — length partially compensated by macrodesign
- Suggests implant macrodesign selection is a controllable factor for primary stability in SFE

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Model**: 5 mm thick porcine iliac crest blocks (simulating atrophic maxilla RBH)
- **Implants**: 5 types × 10 mm (SP, TE, BL, BLT, BLX) + SP short and BLX short (6 mm); all SLActive surface
- **MIT**: Digital torque driver (CEDAR, Sugisaki Keiki)
- **ISQ**: Osstell Mentor RFA transducer, 4-direction measurement
- **Statistics**: One-way ANOVA + Tukey post-hoc; p<0.05 threshold
- Thread pitch: SP=1.25mm, TE/BL/BLT=0.8mm, BLX=1.125mm, BLX short=0.9mm

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Ex vivo only — porcine iliac crest ≠ human atrophic posterior maxilla
- Single bone thickness (5 mm); no variable RBH testing
- No in vivo osseointegration or survival follow-up
- Single manufacturer (Straumann) — cannot generalize to other systems

## 6. Related Work

- Allurkar 2025: In vitro bovine rib ISQ vs RBH — Tapered Effect macrodesign highest ISQ at 6–9mm RBH
- Jelusic 2014: ISQ at 4 months equal between sinus-augmented and non-augmented (prospective)
- Cho 2024: Transcrestal SFE in RBH <3mm without macrodesign comparison

## 7. Glossary

- **MIT (Maximum Insertion Torque, 최대삽입토크)**: peak torque during implant placement; primary stability proxy
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient, 임플란트 안정성 지수)**: RFA-derived 1–100 scale; higher = more stable
- **BLX**: Straumann Bone Level Cross-fit — tapered body with variable thread pitch for compression
- **SLActive**: Straumann's hydrophilic surface; accelerated osseointegration compared to SLA
