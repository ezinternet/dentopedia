## Why Ingested

Implant osseointegration과 peri-implant 골 대사는 결국 bone remodeling의 생리(파골·조골 순환, RANKL/OPG 축, 기계적 부하 적응) 위에서 일어난다 — 기존 bone-biology 페이지들은 기계적 부하([[bone-biology/xu-2016-sclerostin-wnt-occlusal-hypofunction-alveolar-bone]])·병리적 흡수([[bone-biology/kondo-2022-current-perspectives-residual-ridge]]) 축에 있고, **정상 생리의 단일 교과서 레퍼런스**가 없었다. 이 StatPearls 장이 그 토대 역할을 하며, 임플란트 골유착 생물학([[implants/surface/bosshardt-2017-osseointegration-titanium-zirconia-review]]) 및 골다공증-임플란트 상호작용(implant 생존 위험)을 이해하는 체계적 출발점이다. DOI·abstract 없는 narrative 교과서 장이라 evidence_level = `narrative-review`.

## Three-line Summary

StatPearls narrative textbook chapter (NCBI Bookshelf, last update 2023-03-17) — basic physiology of bone remodeling: two cell lineages (osteoclast/osteoblast) plus osteocytes (mechanotransduction), governed by Wolff's Law of load adaptation, regulated hormonally (PTH, estrogen, calcitonin, GH, glucocorticoids, thyroid).

Key principles: osteoclasts resorb then osteoblasts deposit in a coupled cycle maintaining plasma calcium homeostasis; PTH/glucocorticoids raise RANKL and lower OPG (OPG = decoy receptor), estrogen deficiency accelerates turnover with resorption outpacing formation; drugs act on the RANK-RANKL axis (denosumab), farnesyl pyrophosphate synthase (bisphosphonates), SERM (raloxifene), and calcitonin.

Clinical relevance for dentistry: this is the biology that underpins osseointegration and peri-implant bone turnover; disease states (osteoporosis, hyperparathyroidism, Paget, osteopetrosis) and antiresorptive drugs explain implant survival risk and MRONJ-adjacent pharmacology.

## 세줄요약

StatPearls 교과서 장(NCBI Bookshelf, 2023-03-17 마지막 개정) — 골개조(Remodeling)의 기본 생리: 두 세포 계통(파골세포·조골세포)과 골세포(기계적 감지·신호 전달), Wolff 법칙에 따른 하중 적응, 호르몬(PTH·에스트로겐·칼시토닌·성장호르몬·글루코코르티코이드·갑상선호르몬)에 의한 조절.

핵심 원리: 파골세포 흡수→조골세포 형성의 결합 순환으로 혈장 칼슘 항상성을 유지; PTH·글루코코르티코이드는 RANKL↑·OPG(decoy 수용체)↓, 에스트로겐 결핍은 재건 대비 흡수 우위의 고전환을 유발; 약물은 RANK-RANKL 축(데노수맙)·farnesyl pyrophosphate synthase(비스포스포네이트)·SERM(랄록시펜)·칼시토닌에 작용.

치과적 의미: 이 생리가 골유착(Osseointegration)·임플란트 주위 골대사(Peri-implant Bone Turnover)의 기반이며, 골다공증·부갑상선항진증·Paget·대리석골병과 항흡수 약물이 임플란트 생존 위험·항흡수약 관련 약물학을 설명한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Single consolidated physiology reference for bone remodeling — resorption (osteoclast) → reversal (macrophage-lineage) → formation (osteoblast) cycle, with osteocytes as the most abundant cell in mature bone and the mechanotransduction conductor (Wolff's Law).
- Complete endocrine regulation map: PTH (via RANKL, CaSR negative feedback), estrogen (IL-6 blockade), calcitonin, growth hormone/IGF, glucocorticoids (RANKL↑, OPG↓), thyroid hormone (T3/T4/TSH) — each with mechanism of action.
- RANKL/OPG axis framed as the central molecular switch of osteoclast activity, linking physiology directly to pharmacotherapy (denosumab = anti-RANKL mAb; OPG = RANKL decoy receptor).
- Clinically relevant disease states (osteoporosis, hyperparathyroidism primary/secondary/tertiary, Paget, osteopetrosis) plus bone-turnover serum markers (formation: ALP/B-ALP/osteocalcin/PICP/PINP; resorption: OHP/PYD/DPD/BSP/TR-ACP) and imaging (scintigraphy Tc-99m-MDP, DEXA).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Narrative review / textbook chapter (StatPearls). Not a clinical study — no cohort, no n, no abstract. Structured as an educational physiology synopsis.
- **Scope**: cellular biology → hormonal mechanism → laboratory/imaging testing → pathophysiology → clinical pharmacology.
- **Evidence base**: cited primary reviews (Frost 1994 Wolff's Law; Siddiqui & Partridge 2016 bone remodeling; Wein & Kronenberg 2018 PTH; Khosla 2012 estrogen; etc.). Non-systematic.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Two bone-remodeling cell lineages**: osteoclasts (hematopoietic origin; multinucleated; lysosomal enzymes + H⁺; form Howship lacunae) and osteoblasts (mesenchymal origin; deposit collagen + mineral; 3 fates — flatten to lining cell, become osteocyte, or apoptosis); reversal phase by mononuclear macrophage-lineage cells.
- **Osteocytes**: most abundant cell in mature bone; occupy lacunae; mechanotransduction (mechanical force → biochemical signal); regulate bone fluid flow.
- **Wolff's Law**: increased loading strengthens spongy then cortical architecture; decreased loading weakens. Duration·magnitude·rate of tendon-pulling force dictate integrity change.
- **Function**: adjust architecture to body needs, repair microdamage (prevents old-bone accumulation), maintain plasma calcium homeostasis.
- **Hormone endpoints**: %-level quantitative claims are not given (textbook); qualitative: PTH raises plasma Ca (RANKL-mediated osteoclast resorption); estrogen deficiency → turnover where resorption outpaces formation (osteoblast dysfunction/lost template); glucocorticoids → RANKL↑/OPG↓; hyperthyroidism = high turnover, hypothyroidism = low.
- **Disease spectrum**: osteoporosis (most common metabolic skeletal disorder; trabecular bone; wrist/hip/spine; menopause/estrogen deficit, aging ↓osteoblast number); hyperparathyroidism (adenoma/hyperplasia; secondary hypocalcemia from vitamin D deficiency/CKD; tertiary hypertrophy → hypercalcemia); Paget (disorganized resorption, woven-bone osteoblastic response, pathologic fracture); osteopetrosis (osteoclast failure to resorb → ↑mass, weak integrity).
- **Drug mechanism map**: bisphosphonates (alendronate; inhibit farnesyl pyrophosphate synthase → osteoclast detaches → resorption halted; non-nitrogenous types abandoned — mineralization inhibition/osteomalacia), calcitonin (inhibits osteoclast activity, ↑renal Ca excretion), raloxifene (SERM, bone agonism → ↓osteoclast survival, hinders IL-6), denosumab (anti-RANKL mAb → inhibits RANK-RANKL → limits resorption).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative textbook level — non-systematic literature selection, no quantitative synthesis, no risk-of-bias appraisal; claims are qualitative physiology rather than measured outcomes.
- Presents baseline normal physiology and classical endocrinology; does not address peri-implant-specific bone turnover, implant osseointegration dynamics, or modern mechanistic work (osteocyte sclerostin/Wnt signaling is not covered here).
- Pharmacology section is a mechanism catalog, not comparative efficacy data; antiresorptive management (e.g., MRONJ risk) is outside scope.

## 7. Glossary

- **Osteoclast**: multinucleated bone-resorbing cell of hematopoietic lineage; attaches to bone, uses lysosomal enzymes + H⁺ to digest matrix; creates Howship lacunae.
- **Osteoblast**: bone-forming cell of mesenchymal origin; deposits collagen and minerals; fate = lining cell, osteocyte, or apoptosis.
- **Osteocyte**: most abundant bone cell; resides in lacunae; conducts mechanotransduction (mechanical stress → biochemical signal) and regulates bone fluid flow.
- **RANKL / RANK / OPG axis**: RANKL (raised by PTH, glucocorticoids) activates osteoclasts via RANK; OPG is a decoy-receptor (TNF superfamily) that blocks RANKL-RANK; denosumab is an anti-RANKL antibody.
- **Remodeling cycle**: coupled resorption (osteoclast) → reversal (macrophage-lineage) → formation (osteoblast) sequence that repairs microdamage and maintains calcium homeostasis.
- **Wolff's Law**: bone architecture adapts to mechanical loading — increased load strengthens, decreased load weakens.
