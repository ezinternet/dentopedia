## Why Ingested

기존 측방창 (Lateral Window) 상악동거상술 (Sinus Elevation) 이식재 근거는 PRF 보조를 DBBM에 더한 계열, BCP, 동종골, 그리고 네트워크 메타분석 (Network Meta-Analysis, NMA) 순위에 치우쳐 있고, **넓은 안강 (Wide Antral Cavity)** 에서 자가골 (Autogenous Bone) · L-PRF · 이종골 (Xenogenic Bone) 을 직접 무작위 비교한 3군 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry) RCT는 wiki에 없었다. 본 논문은 [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/almutairi-2025-lprf-dbbm-sinus-augmentation-sr-ma]] 의 pooled L-PRF 효과(신생골 MD +7.07%)를 그 MA에 **포함되지 않은** 독립 RCT로 재검토하고, [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/jue-2025-maxillary-sinus-floor-augmentation-autogenous-bone]] 가 임플란트 임상 지표로 다루던 자가골 대 이종골 혼합 축을 조직계측 수준으로 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

Randomized controlled trial (36 patients, 12 per group) of lateral-window maxillary sinus elevation in wide antral cavities (residual bone remnants <3 mm, 8–10 mm augmentation height), comparing xenogenic bone + autogenous bone (70:30) versus xenogenic bone + L-PRF versus xenogenic bone alone.

At 8 months new bone area was 2678.37 (1116.40) μm² for xenogenic + autogenous bone, 984.87 (784.27) μm² for xenogenic + L-PRF and 480.66 (384.76) μm² for xenogenic bone alone (p < 0.05); the L-PRF arm also showed fewer xenogenic bone particles and a large amount of connective tissue.

The authors conclude that in large antral cavities autogenous bone mixed with xenogenic bone remodels best and that L-PRF produces few advantages — a conclusion drawn from histomorphometry alone (no implant outcomes), reported as absolute areas rather than percentages, and available here as an abstract only.

## 세줄요약

넓은 안강 (Wide Antral Cavity) — 잔존골 (Residual Bone) <3 mm, 치조능선 (Alveolar Ridge) 기준 8–10 mm 거상 높이 조건에서 측방창 (Lateral Window) 상악동거상술 (Sinus Elevation) 을 시행하고 이종골 (Xenogenic Bone) + 자가골 (Autogenous Bone) 70:30, 이종골 + 백혈구혈소판풍부피브린 (Leukocyte-Platelet-Rich Fibrin, L-PRF), 이종골 단독을 무작위 배정한 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) (36명, 군당 12명).

8개월 생검 조직형태계측 (Histomorphometry) — 신생골 (New Bone) 면적: 이종골+자가골 2678.37 (1116.40) μm², 이종골+L-PRF 984.87 (784.27) μm², 이종골 단독 480.66 (384.76) μm² (p < 0.05). L-PRF 군에서는 잔존 이종골 입자 (Residual Xenogenic Particles) 가 적고 연조직 (Connective Tissue) 이 많이 관찰되었다.

저자들은 넓은 안강에서는 자가골+이종골 혼합이 가장 우수하고 L-PRF의 이점은 크지 않다고 결론짓는다 — 단, 임플란트 결과는 없고 면적 절대값(%)이 아닌 μm²로 보고된 초록 전용 연구다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Three-arm randomized histology trial in a population the lateral-window literature held here barely covers: **wide antral cavities** needing 8–10 mm of augmentation on residual bone remnants <3 mm.
- Puts autogenous bone and PRF on one histological axis against a common xenogenic carrier (G1 vs G2 vs G3), so the adjunct choice — not just the carrier — is randomized.
- Reports residual xenogenic particle counts and the connective-tissue fraction qualitatively, which is where the L-PRF arm looked least favourable.
- Independent of the Almutairi 2025 meta-analysis pool: that SR+MA's five RCTs are Pichotano 2019, de Almeida Malzoni 2023, Nizam 2018, Zhang 2012 and Tatullo 2012 — Harlos 2022 is **not** among them, so it is an out-of-pool RCT on the same clinical question.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: randomized controlled clinical trial, three parallel arms
- **n**: 36 patients; 12 per group (the abstract reports 36 patients and random filling of sinuses; the unit of allocation — patient or sinus — is not stated)
- **Procedure**: sinus elevation through the lateral window
- **Population**: wide antral cavities; residual bone remnants <3 mm; augmentation height 8–10 mm from the alveolar ridge
- **Arms**: G1 xenogenic bone + autogenous bone (70:30); G2 xenogenic bone + L-PRF; G3 xenogenic bone alone
- **Outcome**: 8-month bone biopsy of the grafted site, analysed histomorphometrically to quantify newly formed bone tissue
- **Not reported in the abstract**: allocation concealment, blinding of the pathologist, biopsy site and sampling method, implant placement protocol and timing, adverse events, funding and conflicts of interest

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Arm | Graft association | New bone area at 8 months, μm² (SD) |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Xenogenic bone + autogenous bone (70:30) | 2678.37 (1116.40) |
| G2 | Xenogenic bone + L-PRF | 984.87 (784.27) |
| G3 | Xenogenic bone alone | 480.66 (384.76) |

- Between-group difference reported as p < 0.05; the abstract does not specify which pairwise comparisons were tested.
- G2 (xenogenic bone + L-PRF): "fewer xenogenic bone particles and a large amount of connective tissue" — the abstract does not state the comparator for this observation.
- No implant survival, ISQ, or morbidity data in the abstract.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Abstract only.** No full text was retrievable, so per-pair p-values, the numeric connective-tissue and residual-particle fractions, biopsy method, implant protocol, adverse events and funding are all unknown here.
- **Outcome unit mismatch.** Histomorphometry is reported as absolute area (μm²), not as a percentage of the biopsy — not directly poolable with the percentage outcomes in [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/almutairi-2025-lprf-dbbm-sinus-augmentation-sr-ma]] or [[wiki/sinus-lift/lateral/motimath-2026-xenograft-prf-sinus-histomorphometric]].
- **Internal tension worth re-checking against the full text**: the numeric ordering (G2 > G3 for new bone area) sits alongside the stated clinical relevance that "L-PRF produces few advantages regarding new bone formation in the wide maxillary sinuses". The abstract gives no pairwise test for G2 vs G3, so the two statements cannot be reconciled from the abstract alone.
- Single centre, single country (Brazil); 12 per group with wide standard deviations relative to the means.
- No implant-level outcomes, so this trial speaks to graft histology only.

## 7. Glossary

- **Wide antral cavity**: pneumatized maxillary sinus large enough to require 8–10 mm of augmentation on residual bone remnants <3 mm.
- **Xenogenic bone**: graft material of non-human origin (bovine-derived here; the abstract does not name the product).
- **Autogenous bone**: patient's own bone, used as a 70:30 admixture with xenogenic bone in G1.
- **L-PRF**: leukocyte-platelet-rich fibrin, a autologous platelet concentrate used as the adjunct in G2.
- **Histomorphometry**: quantitative histology of bone biopsies — here, the area of newly formed bone tissue.
- **REBEC**: Brazilian Clinical Trials Registry (RBR-2pbbrvg).
