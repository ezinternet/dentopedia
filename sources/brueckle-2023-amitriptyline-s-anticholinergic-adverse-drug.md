## Why Ingested

Not a dental paper; ingested because it quantifies dry mouth as the single most frequent anticholinergic adverse drug reaction of a tricyclic antidepressant in placebo-controlled RCTs, giving an effect-size anchor for the drug-induced xerostomia mechanism described in [[wiki/geriatric-dentistry/halasabalu-kalgeri-2026-polypharmacy-oral-health-elderly]] (which lists tricyclic antidepressants among xerogenic classes) and complementing the class-level SR [[wiki/oral-medicine/salivary-chemosensory/ramirez-martinez-acitores-2020-antihypertensive-xerostomia-salivary-flow-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 23 placebo-controlled RCTs (4217 adults, mean age 40.3 y; depression n=13, pain n=9, dyspepsia n=1) comparing oral amitriptyline vs placebo for adverse drug reactions (ADRs).

Anticholinergic ADRs were far more frequent with amitriptyline (OR 7.41, 95% CI 4.54-12.12; RD 0.35; NNH 2.89); dry mouth was the most frequently reported, with OR 11.10 (6.46-19.06) for the aggregated dry-mouth category and 11.60 (6.42-20.98) for individual dry mouth (15 studies), while non-anticholinergic ADRs did not differ from placebo (OR 1.18, 0.74-1.89).

Clinically, dry mouth is a highly probable, non-dose-dependent effect of amitriptyline, but 17/23 trials were high risk of bias with heterogeneity of 83% and mean age ~40, so estimates may understate risk in older adults.

## 세줄요약

플라시보 대조 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) 23편(성인 4217명, 평균 40.3세; 우울증 13편·통증 9편·소화불량 1편)을 대상으로 아미트립틸린 (Amitriptyline)과 위약의 약물이상반응 (Adverse Drug Reaction, ADR)을 비교한 체계적 문헌고찰 및 메타분석 (Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis, SR+MA).

항콜린성 ADR이 아미트립틸린군에서 크게 많았고 (OR 7.41, 95% CI 4.54-12.12; 위험차 0.35; 유해수 (Number Needed to Harm, NNH) 2.89), 구강건조 (dry mouth)가 가장 흔히 보고된 항목으로 통합 구강건조 범주 OR 11.10 (6.46-19.06), 개별 구강건조 OR 11.60 (6.42-20.98; 15편)였으며 비항콜린성 ADR은 위약과 차이가 없었다 (OR 1.18, 0.74-1.89).

임상적으로 구강건조는 용량과 무관하게 나타날 가능성이 높은 아미트립틸린 효과이나, 23편 중 17편이 높은 비뚤림 위험 (Risk of Bias)이고 이질성 83%이며 평균 연령이 약 40세여서 고령자 위험은 과소평가일 수 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First multiple-indication SR comparing amitriptyline ADRs against placebo, restricted to RCTs to avoid disease-symptom and nocebo bias of observational data.
- Separates anticholinergic (ACH), non-anticholinergic (NACH) and general (G) ADRs, with OR, risk difference and NNH.
- Reports dry mouth as the largest single-symptom signal (OR 11.60).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: MEDLINE, Embase, PsycINFO, PsycLIT, Psyndex, CENTRAL from inception to Sept 2022; grey literature, FDA/EMA and trial registries.
- Included: randomized double-blind placebo-controlled RCTs, oral amitriptyline, any indication/dose, >=100 adults, group-wise ADR data.
- 1,898 records screened, 471 full texts, 23 studies (published 1970-2018); flexible dosing in 20/23, 5-300 mg/day, median titration period 8 weeks.
- RoB 2; random-effects (DerSimonian-Laird); meta-regression on dose; subgroups by sex, dose, form, indication.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Studies | OR (95% CI) | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACH-ADRs (primary) | 20 (n=3,510) | 7.41 (4.54-12.12) | RD 0.35 (0.26-0.46); NNH 2.89; I2 83% |
| NACH-ADRs | 14 (n=2,442) | 1.18 (0.74-1.89) | NNH 34.27; RD 0.03 (-0.02 to 0.07) |
| G-ADRs | 21 (n=3,437) | 3.74 (2.69-5.20) | NNH 6.23; RD 0.16 (0.10-0.22) |
| Dry mouth-related (aggregated) | 15 (1294 vs 1247) | 11.10 (6.46-19.06) | I2 69% |
| Dry mouth (individual) | 15 (1100 vs 1050) | 11.60 (6.42-20.98) | I2 70% |
| Somnolence | 8 | 5.06 (4.01-6.39) | I2 0% |
| Constipation | 13 | 3.06 (2.16-4.34) | I2 14% |
| Discontinued due to ADRs | 13 | 3.57 (2.26-5.65) | I2 13% |
| Insomnia | 11 | 0.58 (0.39-0.86) | favors amitriptyline |
| Headache | 9 | 0.73 (0.55-0.97) | favors amitriptyline |

- Dry mouth was the most frequently reported ACH-ADR (9 studies as the primary per-study outcome).
- Anticholinergic ADRs were not dose-dependent (meta-regression p = .40); the authors note that dose at ADR onset was rarely reported.
- Leucht et al. (depression), as cited by the authors: dry mouth OR 13.50 vs 11.60 here.
- A dry-mouth-specific NNH is not reported in the text.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- 17/23 studies high RoB (15 of them with attrition >=20%); ACH-ADR heterogeneity I2 = 83%.
- Funnel plot asymmetry for NACH-ADRs (p = .01).
- The >=100 participant criterion excluded 269 small studies.
- Mean age ~40 years; no trials in older or multimorbid patients; falls and cognitive decline not reported.
- Dry mouth was a self-reported/clinician-reported ADR; salivary flow was not measured (fewer than five studies used objective measures).

## 7. Glossary

- **ACH-ADR**: adverse drug reaction indicative of anticholinergic (muscarinic-blocking) activity.
- **NACH-ADR**: ADR not indicative of anticholinergic activity.
- **G-ADR**: general unspecific ADR (overall ADRs, discontinuation due to ADRs).
- **NNH**: number needed to harm; here 1/RD (2.89 for ACH-ADRs).
- **RoB 2**: Cochrane revised risk-of-bias tool for randomized trials.
