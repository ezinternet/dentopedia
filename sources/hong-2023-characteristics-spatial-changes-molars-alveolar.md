## Why Ingested

하악 제1대구치 상실 후 상악 대합 제1대구치의 정출량을 CBCT로 직접 정량화한 인간 단면 연구. 기존 정출 클러스터([[occlusion/wang-2022-unopposed-molar-overeruption-implant-cbct]])가 발치~보철 ~9개월의 단기 CBCT 측정(0.43 mm)인 반면, 이 연구는 평균 62.6개월(~5.2년)의 장기 무치악 상태를 CBCT로 측정해 근심교두 정출 1.37 mm·원심교두 0.85 mm를 확인한다. 인접 하악 제2대구치의 근심·설측 경사 및 치조골 흡수도 동시에 계측하고, 근심이동(Molar Protraction) 가능 거리 < 필요 거리임을 3D 시뮬레이션으로 증명한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cross-sectional CBCT study (42 CBCT scans with missing mandibular first molars, 36 patients, mean 62.6 months post-extraction; 42 matched controls; West China Hospital of Stomatology, Sichuan University) measuring 3D spatial changes in molars and alveolar bone after mandibular first molar loss.

Maxillary first molars extruded 1.37 ± 0.83 mm (mesial cusp) and 0.85 ± 0.70 mm (distal cusp) in the missing group only; mandibular second molars tipped significantly mesially (57.47 ± 10.34° vs 82.75 ± 5.14°, P<0.001) and lingually (71.75 ± 8.34° vs 73.23 ± 7.60°, P<0.05); all changes correlated with duration of tooth loss (R = −0.334 to −0.726).

Available mesialization distance was consistently less than required at all levels (CEJ P<0.001, mid-root P<0.001, apex P<0.05), with buccal bone defect at CEJ (3.15 ± 0.87 mm) as the primary limiting factor — supporting early restoration and mandating bone augmentation + lingual root torque for molar protraction.

## 세줄요약

후향적 단면 CBCT 연구(하악 제1대구치 결손 평균 62.6개월 환자 42명 vs 대조군 42명; 사천대학 화서 구강병원): 하악 제1대구치 상실 후 대합치·인접치·치조골의 3차원 변화를 동시 계측.

상악 제1대구치 정출 (Supraeruption): 근심교두 1.37 ± 0.83 mm, 원심교두 0.85 ± 0.70 mm (결손군에서만 관찰); 하악 제2대구치 근심 경사 (Mesial Tipping): 57.47 ± 10.34° (대조군 82.75°, P<0.001), 설측 경사 (Lingual Tipping): 71.75 ± 8.34° (대조군 73.23°, P<0.05); 법랑-백악질 경계부 (CEJ) 협측 치조골 결손 3.15 ± 0.87 mm; 결손 기간이 길수록 모든 변화 유의하게 악화(R = −0.334 ~ −0.726).

하악 제2대구치 근심이동 (Molar Protraction) 시뮬레이션: 가능 이동 거리 < 필요 거리 (모든 레벨에서 유의); 협측 CEJ 골 결손이 주된 제한 인자 → 치조골 이식 (Bone Augmentation) + 설측 치근 토크 (Lingual Root Torque) + 상악 대구치 함입 (Intrusion) 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- CBCT-confirmed 3D quantification of asymmetric maxillary first molar supraeruption after mandibular first molar loss: mesial cusp (1.37 mm) > distal cusp (0.85 mm) (P<0.01)
- Simultaneous documentation of mandibular second molar mesial tipping (57.47°) and lingual tipping, both worsening with duration of tooth loss (R = −0.726 and −0.528 respectively)
- 3D simulation revealing that buccal bone defect at CEJ level (3.15 mm) is the primary limiting factor for molar protraction — exceeds available bone at all cross-section levels
- Establishes that longer tooth-loss duration predicts greater secondary deformity (all three changes correlated, R = −0.334 to −0.726), justifying early implant or orthodontic restoration

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective cross-sectional CBCT morphometric study
- **n**: 42 CBCT scans missing mandibular first molars (36 patients, 28.7 ± 6.4 years, 33 female) vs 42 CBCT controls (36 patients, 26.1 ± 5.8 years, 27 female); 13 missing right, 17 missing left, 6 missing bilateral first molars
- **Inclusion**: Age 18–40; all remaining posterior teeth present (excluding missing mandibular first molar in study group); no orthodontic/orthognathic history; no generalized periodontitis
- **Duration**: Mean 62.6 ± 13.3 months post-extraction
- **Reference**: Mandibular posterior tooth plane, standardized in Invivo Dental v5.1.6
- **Measurements**: (1) Mesio-distal angulation of mandibular second molar (sagittal); (2) Bucco-lingual angulation (coronal); (3) Maxillary first molar mesial and distal cusp extrusion from posterior occlusal plane; (4) Vertical alveolar bone height loss from CEJ (buccal/middle/lingual); (5) Horizontal alveolar bone width reduction (buccal and lingual, at CEJ/mid-root/apex); (6) 3D simulation of available vs required molar mesialization distance and bone defect widths
- **Statistics**: Student t-test, ANOVA, Pearson correlation; α = 0.05; SPSS v22

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective cross-sectional — no longitudinal data; causality inferred only; selection bias possible
- Female-predominant sample (33/36 missing group; 27/36 controls) — may not generalize to males
- Young adult cohort only (18–40 years) — cannot generalize to older or periodontally compromised patients
- Mean duration 62.6 months (range not specified) — direct comparison with Wang 2022 (9 months) limited
- CBCT radiation exposure limits broader screening

## 7. Glossary

- **Mesio-distal angulation (근원심 경사)**: Angle between tooth long axis and posterior occlusal plane in sagittal view; smaller value = greater mesial tipping
- **Bucco-lingual angulation (협설측 경사)**: Angle between tooth long axis and posterior occlusal plane in coronal view; smaller value = greater lingual tipping
- **Supraeruption / overeruption (정출, 과맹출)**: Vertical eruption of a tooth beyond the occlusal plane in the absence of an opposing tooth
- **CEJ (Cemento-enamel Junction, 법랑-백악질 경계부)**: Junction between enamel and cementum; used as reference for alveolar bone height measurement
- **Molar protraction (대구치 근심이동)**: Orthodontic mesial movement of a posterior tooth to close an edentulous space
- **Bone dehiscence / fenestration**: Alveolar bone defect exposing root surface, limiting available mesialization distance during molar protraction
