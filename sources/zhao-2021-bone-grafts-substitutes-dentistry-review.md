## Three-line Summary

Narrative literature review (no formal systematic search strategy or meta-analysis) surveying dental bone graft and substitute materials — natural (autograft, allograft/DBM, xenograft, phytogenic), synthetic (HA, β-TCP, biphasic CP ceramics, bioactive glass, calcium phosphate cements, calcium sulfates, polymers, metals), composite, growth-factor-based, and living-cell-infused categories — for craniofacial/dental bone defect repair including implant site preparation.

Autografts remain the only material possessing all four fundamental biological properties (osseointegration, osteogenesis, osteoconduction, osteoinduction) and the gold standard for complex augmentation, but donor-site morbidity and limited supply drive use of alternatives; deproteinized bovine xenograft achieved 39% new bone formation at 6 months (vs 40% for autograft) in maxillary sinus grafting, with 95% implant survival reported at 14 years in one longitudinal cohort.

All currently available synthetic/allogeneic/xenogeneic substitutes satisfy only the osteoconductivity criterion (none reproduces autograft's full four-property profile), and evidence for newer materials (growth-factor-based, MSC-infused, nano-HA) is drawn largely from case reports/animal studies rather than standardized clinical trials — a key limitation for extrapolating to routine practice.

## 세줄요약

정식 체계적 검색전략이나 메타분석 없는 narrative review로, 치과용 골이식재/골대체재를 천연(자가골·동종골/DBM·이종골·식물유래)·합성(HA·β-TCP·이상성 CP세라믹·생체활성유리·인산칼슘시멘트·황산칼슘·고분자·금속)·복합재·성장인자기반·생세포주입형 범주로 나누어 임플란트 부위 준비를 포함한 두개안면/치과 골결손 수복 전반을 조망.

자가골(autograft)만이 4대 생물학적 특성(골유착·골형성·골전도·골유도)을 모두 갖춘 유일한 재료로 복잡 골증대의 gold standard이지만 공여부 이환율·제한된 공급 때문에 대체재 사용이 증가하며, 탈단백우골 이종골(deproteinized bovine xenograft)은 상악동거상술에서 6개월 시점 39% 신생골형성(자가골 40%와 비교)을 보였고 한 장기추적 코호트에서 14년 95% 임플란트 생존율이 보고됨.

현재 시판되는 합성/동종/이종 대체재는 모두 골전도성(osteoconductivity) 조건만 충족하며(자가골의 4대 특성 전체를 재현하는 재료 없음), 성장인자기반·MSC 주입형·나노 HA 등 신소재의 근거는 대부분 증례보고나 동물실험 수준이라 표준화된 임상시험 부재가 실제 진료 외삽의 핵심 한계.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Comprehensive dentistry-wide (not defect-type-limited) taxonomy of bone graft/substitute materials, organized by tissue source/material group into five broad categories: natural, synthetic, composite, growth-factor-based, and cell-infused.
- Quantifies market context: ~2.2 million bone graft procedures/year globally (~US$664M by 2021, ~13%/year growth); dental bone substitute market ~US$493M (2018) projected to ~US$931M by 2025 (9.5% CAGR); ~50% of dental implant procedures involve bone grafting.
- Explicit USFDA device classification: bone grafts filling bony voids/defects = Class II; bone graft containing drugs = Class III.
- Details specific commercial product mechanisms (deproteinized bovine bone/Bio-Oss-type xenograft processing, DBM putty, chitosan/silk fibroin/Gusuibu/coral-HA/marine-algae-HA phytogenic and natural alternatives, biphasic CP ceramics, bioactive glass ion-doping, Mg-Sr metal alloys, sticky bone, MSC-infused products) with mechanism-of-action and reported outcome figures.
- Frames the central unmet need: no non-autograft material yet reproduces all four fundamental biological properties (osseointegration, osteogenesis, osteoconduction, osteoinduction); current substitutes are osteoconductive only.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Narrative literature review; no stated database search strategy, inclusion/exclusion criteria, or PRISMA flow — synthesizes primary studies, animal models, and commercial product data narratively by material category.
- Organizing structure: (1) ideal bone-grafting-material characteristics, (2) five-way classification (natural / synthetic / composite / growth-factor-based / cell-infused), (3) future directions, (4) conclusions.
- Natural materials subdivided: autografts, allografts (incl. DBM, collagen-based), xenografts (bovine-derived HA, chitosan, silk fibroin), phytogenic (Gusuibu, coral-based HA, marine-algae HA).
- Synthetic materials subdivided: hydroxyapatite (HA), β-tricalcium phosphate (β-TCP), biphasic calcium phosphate ceramics, bioactive glass (BAG), calcium phosphate cements (CPCs), calcium sulfates, polymers, metals (nickel-titanium, Mg-Sr alloys).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Xenograft vs autograft (maxillary sinus)**: bovine xenograft achieved 39% new bone formation at 6 months vs 40% for autograft; 31% of xenograft material remained at the graft site vs 18% of autograft — suggesting comparable-or-better efficacy for volume retention.
- **Long-term survival**: 14-year longitudinal study (Ewers) reported 95% implant survival in atrophic maxillae following sinus grafting with marine-algae-derived HA.
- **Gusuibu + collagen carrier**: new bone formation increased 24% vs Gusuibu alone, and 90% vs absorbable collagen sponge alone (Wong and Rabie).
- **Cell-sheet MSC therapy (pig model)**: dental-pulp-derived heterologous MSCs (cell sheet or injection) increased alveolar bone height regeneration by 52.7mm and 32.4mm respectively (Cao et al., Hu et al.) — periodontal ligament-derived MSCs outperformed dental-pulp-derived MSCs in a dog model (Park et al.).
- **Silk fibroin membrane**: ~4mm new bone gain at 6 months after mandibular third-molar extraction site grafting (2016 clinical trials).
- **GFBS safety signal**: one of the two major USFDA-approved BMP-based commercial products has been discontinued; the other has been associated with "a large number of life-threatening complications."
- **Allograft disease-transmission risk**: ~8% prevalence of unknown transmissible disease reported in osteoarthritic femoral heads used for allograft harvesting.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative (non-systematic) review — no reproducible search strategy, no risk-of-bias/quality appraisal of included studies, no quantitative synthesis; conclusions carry selection-bias risk.
- Much of the evidence for newer materials (growth-factor-based substitutes, MSC-infused products, nanocrystalline HA) is drawn from case reports or animal models rather than standardized clinical trials — authors explicitly flag "the reliability of this information may be questionable."
- No currently available synthetic/allogeneic/xenogeneic material reproduces autograft's full osteogenic + osteoinductive + osteoconductive + osseointegrative profile; synthetic substitutes remain osteoconductive-only, restricting regeneration largely to the outer surface layer.
- Authors call for more standardized preclinical/clinical studies, better structural design (pore size/density/morphology/interconnectivity/resorbability), and controlled time-release delivery systems for growth factors to advance clinical translation.
- Cost of newer hybrid/growth-factor/cell-based technologies vs benefit relative to existing osteoconductive-only substitutes is flagged as an unresolved practical consideration.

## 7. Glossary

- **Osseointegration**: chemical bonding of a grafting material to bone surface without an intervening fibrous tissue layer.
- **Osteogenesis**: new bone formation via osteoblasts/progenitor cells present within the grafting material itself.
- **Osteoconduction**: ability of a material to act as a passive scaffold supporting ingrowth of host vessels/osteoblasts/progenitor cells.
- **Osteoinduction**: recruitment and differentiation of host stem cells into osteoblasts, mediated by local growth factors (BMPs, PDGFs, TGF-β, FGFs) within/released by the graft.
- **DBM (Demineralized Bone Matrix)**: acid-treated allograft derivative exposing an osteoinductive-molecule-rich matrix after removal of the mineral phase.
- **β-TCP (beta-Tricalcium Phosphate)**: resorbable synthetic calcium phosphate ceramic, faster-resorbing than HA.
- **BAG (Bioactive Glass)**: silicate-based ceramic that forms a surface HA layer in vivo, promoting osteoconduction and exhibiting antimicrobial activity when ion-doped (Zn, Ag).
- **CPC (Calcium Phosphate Cement)**: self-setting paste (aqueous + powder components) that hardens in situ into HA nanocrystals.
- **GFBS (Growth Factor-Based bone Substitute)**: bone substitute bioactivated with growth factors (e.g., BMP-2, BMP-7, rhPDGF-BB) to add osteoinductive capacity to an osteoconductive scaffold.
- **Sticky bone**: bone graft matrix enriched with growth factors using autologous fibrin glue, improving moldability and cell-selective healing.
- **MSC (Mesenchymal Stem Cell)**: non-hematopoietic multipotent progenitor cell (e.g., bone-marrow, dental-pulp, or periodontal-ligament-derived) capable of osteoblastic differentiation.
- **BRONJ**: bisphosphonate-related osteonecrosis of the jaw.
