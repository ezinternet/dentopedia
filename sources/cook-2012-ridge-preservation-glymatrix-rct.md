## Three-line Summary

RCT (University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio, J Periodontol 84(5):585-594, 2012) comparing two ridge preservation protocols in 44 patients following non-molar tooth extraction: **Protocol 1** (bovine xenograft 90% anorganic bone + 10% porcine collagen fibers / non-cross-linked bilayer collagen membrane) vs **Protocol 2** (cross-linked bovine collagen sponge 70% + non-sintered hydroxyapatite / ribose-glycation cross-linked porcine type I collagen membrane = GLYMATRIX platform), with core biopsy histology at 21 weeks.

**Protocol 2 achieved significantly greater vital bone (47.03 ± 9.09% vs 32.83 ± 14.72%, P<0.001)** with zero residual graft vs 13.44% in Protocol 1; connective tissue/other content was similar between groups (53% vs 54%).

Clinical ridge dimension changes were not significantly different between protocols, suggesting the histologic advantage of GLYMATRIX does not automatically translate to macroscopic volume difference at 21 weeks — consistent with the complete ossification (no remnant graft) mechanism of cross-linked collagen systems.

## 3. Clinical Interpretation

- **Zero residual graft in Protocol 2**: Cross-linked collagen sponge undergoes complete ossification, replicating the finding of canullo-2023 for OSSIX Bone (no remnants)
- **47% vital bone at 21 weeks**: Substantially higher than Protocol 1 (32%) — important for implant timing and long-term osseointegration quality
- **No clinical dimension difference**: Histologic superiority without macroscopic volume advantage at 21 weeks suggests measurement timing; ridge dimensions may diverge at longer follow-up
- **Platform coherence**: Protocol 2 uses GLYMATRIX-platform materials for both graft AND membrane — consistent with the single-platform strategy seen in OSSIX Bone (sinus lift)

## 4. Limitations

- 21-week endpoint; long-term implant outcomes not reported
- Clinical dimension measurement (not CBCT) — may lack sensitivity
