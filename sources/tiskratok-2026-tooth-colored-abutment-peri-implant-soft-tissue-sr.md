## Why Ingested

Most comprehensive SR (136 articles) comparing peri-implant soft-tissue responses to tooth-colored abutment materials (ZrO₂, PEEK) vs titanium, directly supporting clinical abutment material selection decisions. Extends [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/canullo-2020-titanium-abutment-surface-peri-implant-tissue-ma]] and relevant to [[wiki/prosthetic-materials/waltenberger-2025-zirconia-implant-dentistry-guideline]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review (136 articles; MEDLINE/PubMed, Google Scholar, Scopus) comparing peri-implant soft-tissue responses to tooth-colored abutments — primarily zirconia and PEEK — versus titanium, across in-vitro and in-vivo evidence.
Zirconia: excellent biocompatibility, enhanced cell viability/attachment, reduced inflammation and biofilm formation vs titanium; PEEK: favorable mechanical properties but limited cell attachment due to hydrophobicity requiring surface modification; titanium remains clinical standard but shows greater inflammation and biofilm.
Zirconia is the preferred tooth-colored abutment for optimal peri-implant soft-tissue biology; PEEK requires surface treatment to match zirconia's biologic profile; neither exceeds zirconia's combined soft-tissue performance.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰(136편; MEDLINE·Google Scholar·Scopus), 치색 어버트먼트 소재 — 주로 지르코니아·폴리에테르에테르케톤(Polyetheretherketone, PEEK) — 의 임플란트 주위 연조직 반응을 in-vitro/in-vivo 근거로 Ti와 비교.
지르코니아: 우수한 생체적합성(Biocompatibility), 향상된 세포 생존율·부착, Ti 대비 낮은 염증반응·바이오필름 형성; PEEK: 기계적 물성 양호하나 소수성(Hydrophobicity)으로 세포 부착 제한 — 표면개질 필요; Ti는 임상 표준이나 치색 소재보다 염증·바이오필름 ↑.
지르코니아가 연조직 생물학적 관점 최적 치색 어버트먼트; PEEK는 표면처리 없이 생물학적 성능 한계; 두 소재 모두 장기 임상데이터 추가 필요.

## Key Contributions

- SR of 136 articles comparing tooth-colored abutment materials on peri-implant soft tissue
- Zirconia vs Ti: lower inflammation, reduced biofilm formation, improved cell attachment — superior biologic profile
- PEEK vs Ti: mechanically favorable but poor cell attachment without surface modification — limits clinical adoption
- Zirconia identified as strongest tooth-colored abutment option for soft-tissue health maintenance
- Titanium remains clinical standard but biologically inferior to zirconia for soft-tissue interface

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review
- **Databases**: MEDLINE/PubMed, Google Scholar, Scopus
- **n included**: 136 articles
- **Focus**: Tooth-colored abutment materials (ZrO₂, PEEK) peri-implant soft-tissue responses
- **Criteria**: Original research in English; effects of abutment materials on peri-implant soft tissue (in-vitro and in-vivo)

## Key Results

| Material | Biocompatibility | Cell Attachment | Inflammation | Biofilm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zirconia | Excellent | Enhanced | Reduced vs Ti | Reduced vs Ti |
| PEEK | Favorable | Limited (hydrophobicity) | Favorable | Not specified |
| Titanium | Standard | Standard | Higher than ZrO₂ | Higher than ZrO₂ |

- Zirconia: improved soft-tissue integration, reduced biofilm-related risks, stable soft-tissue barrier
- PEEK: surface modification required to improve cell proliferation and matrix production
- Ti: remains gold standard for osseointegration but biologically inferior at soft-tissue interface

## Limitations

- Heterogeneous study types (in-vitro + in-vivo); clinical applicability variable
- Abstract-only data — detailed meta-analysis statistics not available from PubMed
- No quantitative synthesis (meta-analysis) reported — qualitative/narrative conclusion
- PEEK surface modification types and outcomes not differentiated in abstract

## Glossary

- **치색 어버트먼트 (Tooth-colored Abutment)**: 지르코니아·PEEK 등 치아색 소재로 제작된 임플란트 어버트먼트; 금속 노출 없이 심미성 제공
- **PEEK (폴리에테르에테르케톤, Polyetheretherketone)**: 고강도 반결정질 열가소성 폴리머; 소수성으로 세포 부착 제한, 표면개질 필요
- **Biofilm (바이오필름)**: 치아/보철물 표면에 형성되는 미생물 군집; 임플란트 주위염 위험 인자
- **Hydrophobicity (소수성)**: 물을 밀어내는 성질; PEEK 표면의 세포 부착 제한 원인
