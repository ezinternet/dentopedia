## Why Ingested
구강작열감증후군(BMS) 치료에 대한 2016년 코크란 리뷰(Cochrane Oral Health Group) 업데이트판 — BMS 관리 근거를 체계적으로 정리한 최신 SR로, [[oral-medicine/burning-mouth-syndrome]] 관련 임상 결정과 환자 설명 근거로 인제스트.

## Three-line Summary
Cochrane systematic review of 23 RCTs (1,121 participants, 83% female) comparing any intervention vs placebo for burning mouth syndrome (BMS) symptom relief and quality of life (QoL).
Very low-quality evidence (GRADE ⊕⊝⊝⊝) of short-term benefit from electromagnetic radiation (1 RCT, 58 pts), topical benzodiazepines/clonazepam (2 RCTs, 111 pts), physical barriers (1 RCT, 50 pts), and anticonvulsants/gabapentin (1 RCT, 100 pts); long-term benefit from psychological therapy (1 RCT, 30 pts), capsaicin oral rinse (1 RCT, 18 pts), and topical clonazepam (1 RCT, 66 pts).
Overall insufficient evidence to support or refute any intervention for BMS; all outcomes graded very low quality due to high risk of bias in 18/23 trials and small sample sizes.

## 세줄요약
코크란 체계적 문헌고찰(SR), 23개 RCT(1,121명, 여성 83%)를 대상으로 구강작열감증후군(BMS) 치료 중재 vs 위약 비교 — 증상 완화 및 삶의 질(QoL) 평가.
전자기파(1 RCT, 58명), 국소 벤조디아제핀/클로나제팜(2 RCT, 111명), 물리적 차단(1 RCT, 50명), 항경련제/가바펜틴(1 RCT, 100명)에서 단기 증상 완화 '매우 낮은 질' 근거 확인; 심리치료(1 RCT, 30명), 캡사이신 구강세척(1 RCT, 18명), 국소 클로나제팜(1 RCT, 66명)에서 장기 증상 완화 매우 낮은 질 근거.
전체적으로 **모든 중재에 대해 매우 낮은 질 근거**(GRADE ⊕⊝⊝⊝)만 존재, 23개 중 18개 연구가 높은 비뚤림 위험 — 현재 어떤 BMS 치료도 지지하거나 반박할 충분한 근거 **없음**.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Cochrane systematic review (Intervention Review)
- **Databases**: Cochrane Oral Health's Trials Register, CENTRAL, MEDLINE Ovid, Embase Ovid, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP (to 31 Dec 2015)
- **n**: 23 RCTs, 1,121 analysed participants (83% female)
- **Outcomes**: Primary — symptom relief (pain/burning), change in QoL; Secondary — change in taste, dryness sensation, adverse events
- **Analysis**: Short-term (≤3 months) vs long-term (>3 to ≤6 months); standard Cochrane methods, GRADE assessment

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Short-term symptom relief — electromagnetic radiation | Very low quality evidence of benefit (1 RCT, 58 pts) |
| Short-term symptom relief — topical benzodiazepines (clonazepam) | Very low quality evidence of benefit (2 RCTs, 111 pts) |
| Short-term symptom relief — physical barriers (thin plastic tongue covers) | Very low quality evidence of benefit (1 RCT, 50 pts) |
| Short-term symptom relief — anticonvulsants (gabapentin) | Very low quality evidence of benefit (1 RCT, 100 pts) |
| Long-term symptom relief — psychological therapy | Very low quality evidence of benefit (1 RCT, 30 pts) |
| Long-term symptom relief — capsaicin oral rinse | Very low quality evidence of benefit (1 RCT, 18 pts) |
| Long-term symptom relief — topical clonazepam | Very low quality evidence of benefit (1 RCT, 66 pts) |
| Antidepressants (trazodone) vs placebo | No difference (1 RCT, 37 pts, very low quality) |
| Dietary supplements (alpha-lipoic acid) | No difference; adverse events: headache (2 RCTs, 118 pts), GI complaints (3 RCTs, 138 pts) |
| Adverse events — antidepressants | Increased dizziness/drowsiness (1 RCT, 37 pts, very low quality) |
| Adverse events — alpha-lipoic acid | Increased headache (2 RCTs, 118 pts), GI complaints (3 RCTs, 138 pts) |
| Risk of bias | 1 RCT low risk, 4 unclear, 18 high risk |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- 23개 포함 연구 중 **18개가 높은 비뚤림 위험**(high risk of bias) — 전체 증거 질 매우 낮음
- 표본 크기 작음(대부분 100명 미만), 단일 연구 결과가 대다수
- 표준화된 결과 측정 도구 부재, 추적 기간 짧음(최대 6개월)
- 심리치료, 국소 치료 등 유망 중재도 단일 소규모 RCT 근거만 존재
- 향후: 개선된 방법론·표준화된 결과 세트의 대규모 RCT 필요

## 7. Glossary
- **BMS (Burning Mouth Syndrome, 구강작열감증후군)**: 뚜렷한 원인 없이 구강 점막 작열감/통증이 지속되는 만성 질환
- **GRADE**: 증거 등급 체계(Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation) — ⊕⊝⊝⊝ = 매우 낮은 질
- **QoL (Quality of Life, 삶의 질)**: 건강 관련 삶의 질 척도(예: Beck Depression Inventory 등)
- **Clonazepam (클로나제팜)**: 벤조디아제핀 계열 항경련제/항불안제, 국소 적용 시 BMS 증상 완화 가능성
- **Gabapentin (가바펜틴)**: 항경련제, 신경병성 통증에 사용, BMS 단기 증상 완화 매우 낮은 질 근거
- **Alpha-lipoic acid (알파리포산, ALA)**: 항산화 영양보충제, BMS에 두통·위장장애 부작용 증가 보고
