## Why Ingested

Directly examines subgingival microbiome community-level changes after NSPT ± intensive antiplaque, using healthy-plane modeling — distinct from existing [[oral-microbiology/krajewski-2025-nspt-subgingival-microbiota-sr-ma]] (SR+MA) by providing individual-level microbiome trajectory analysis and the key finding that baseline microbiome predicts treatment response better than treatment type.

## Three-line Summary

Secondary analysis of 86 Stage II/III periodontitis subjects randomized to SRP alone vs. SRP + antiplaque regimen (0.12% CHX ×3 months + interdental cleaners ×6 months), with 30 healthy references; evaluated subgingival microbiome trajectories relative to a modeled healthy plane (HPL) by 16S rRNA sequencing.

Only the SRP+antiplaque group showed a statistically significant reduction in distance to the HPL at 6 months; however, individual responses were highly variable in both groups, and random forest analysis identified baseline microbiome composition as a stronger predictor of microbiome treatment response than treatment type.

The finding that pre-treatment dysbiosis pattern outperforms treatment modality in predicting eubiosis recovery supports the concept of microbiome-guided personalized periodontal therapy.

- **Full text**: Not available via PMC (abstract only)

## 2. Key Contributions

- Introduces healthy plane (HPL) modeling from reference cohort as a community-level benchmark for dysbiosis assessment.
- Demonstrates that adjunct antiplaque moves microbiome toward eubiosis more effectively than SRP alone.
- Establishes baseline microbiome > treatment type as predictor — supporting personalized microbiome-guided care.
- Provides an individual-level trajectory framework (vs. population-level averages).

## 3. Methodology

- **Design**: Secondary analysis of RCT (SRP alone vs SRP+antiplaque)
- **Sample**: 86 stages II/III periodontitis; 30 healthy references
- **Intervention antiplaque**: 0.12% chlorhexidine × 3 months + interdental cleaners × 6 months
- **Sequencing**: 16S rRNA gene; healthy plane (HPL) modeled from reference group
- **Analysis**: Random forest (predictors of microbiome response)

## 4. Key Results

- SRP+P+S: significant reduction in distance to HPL at 6 months
- SRP alone: NOT statistically significant reduction in HPL distance
- Both groups: high individual variability — only fraction achieved health-direction change
- Random forest: baseline microbiome composition = stronger predictor than treatment type

## 5. Limitations

- Abstract-only source
- Secondary analysis (not primary design for microbiome endpoints)
- 16S rRNA — genus-level resolution; limited for species-specific conclusions
- Short 6-month follow-up

## 6. Glossary

- **HPL**: Healthy plane — mathematical model of subgingival microbiome structure in health
- **CHX**: Chlorhexidine
- **SRP**: Scaling and root planing
- **16S rRNA**: Bacterial ribosomal RNA gene used for microbiome profiling
- **Eubiosis**: Balanced microbial community associated with health
- **Dysbiosis**: Pathological microbial imbalance
