## Why Ingested

[[drug/anticoagulants/agrawal-2025-efficacy-chitosan-dressing-antiplatelet]] focuses on antiplatelet patients with otherwise-intact coagulation; this study extends chitosan evidence to patients with genuinely deranged coagulation profiles (INR 1.5–4, liver cirrhosis, thrombocytopenic purpura) where the clinical stakes are higher and gauze is most likely to fail.

## One-line Summary

Prospective split-mouth RCT (n=102 patients with deranged coagulation; 204 sockets) showing chitosan dressing achieves hemostasis in 83.1% of sockets within 10 min vs 18.8% for gauze, with mean times 15.10 vs 45.20 min (p<0.001).

응고이상 환자(항응고 요법, 간경변, 혈소판감소 포함, n=102/204개 소켓) split-mouth RCT: 키토산 드레싱 10분 내 지혈 83.1% vs 거즈 18.8%, 평균 지혈시간 15.10±12.88분 vs 45.20±20.62분 (p<0.001); 부종 발생률도 유의하게 낮음(3.9% vs 15.7%).

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates chitosan efficacy in genuinely compromised hemostasis (INR 1.5–4, liver cirrhosis, thrombocytopenic purpura) — not just antiplatelet patients
- Largest chitosan-vs-gauze dataset in a mixed coagulopathy population (n=102 patients, 204 extraction sites)
- Within-subject split-mouth design eliminates inter-individual variability in coagulation status
- Antibiotic use or dose modification not required; medications continued without alteration throughout
- Additional benefit: significantly reduced swelling (3.9% vs 15.7%) in the chitosan group

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective split-mouth RCT; PGIMER Chandigarh, India (ethics 2018, publication 2024/25)
- **Population**: 102 patients with deranged coagulation (INR 1.5–4, altered PT/aPTT/platelets) requiring ≥2 extractions: 86 on anticoagulant therapy (cardiac), 15 liver cirrhosis, 1 thrombocytopenic purpura
- **Medications**: continued unchanged throughout; no bridging or dose alteration
- **Test site**: chitosan dental dressing placed in socket, pressed with finger + standard gauze pressure; removed next day per manufacturer guidelines
- **Control site**: standard gauze pressure pack only; contralateral or same-quadrant
- **Matching**: single-rooted vs single-rooted; multi-rooted vs multi-rooted; midline = control, lateral = test
- **Primary outcome**: time to hemostasis assessed at 10, 30, 60 min post-extraction
- **Secondary**: dry socket, infection, swelling at days 1, 3, 7

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Hemostasis timeline:**
| Time point | Chitosan (test) | Gauze (control) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 min | 58.8% (60/102) | — |
| 10 min cumulative | 83.1% | 18.8% |
| 30 min cumulative | 93.9% | 35.5% |
| 60 min cumulative | 99.8% | 99.5% |

- Mean hemostasis time: **15.10 ± 12.88 min** (test) vs **45.20 ± 20.62 min** (control); p<0.001
- Dry socket: 6 patients (5.9%) test vs 1 (1%) control; p>0.05 (NS — slight excess not significant)
- Infection: 0 in either group
- Swelling: 4 (3.92%) test vs 16 (15.68%) control; p<0.05 (significant)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Ethics approval 2018 but publication 2025 — long gap suggests extended recruitment; no explicit trial registration cited
- Dry socket numerically higher in chitosan group (5.9% vs 1%), though not significant; larger trials needed to clarify
- Single-center; operator blinding impossible in split-mouth design
- No standardized wound-closure (suture not mentioned as uniform)
- Limited to extractions requiring ≥2 teeth; single-tooth extraction data absent

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary

- **Deranged coagulation profile**: INR 1.5–4, abnormal PT/aPTT, or thrombocytopenia
- **Split-mouth design**: each patient serves as own control; left/right or same-quadrant paired sockets
- **INR**: International Normalized Ratio; >1.5 indicates impaired clotting pathway (therapeutic anticoagulation range typically 2–3)
