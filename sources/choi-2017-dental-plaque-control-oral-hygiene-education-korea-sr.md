## Why Ingested

Updated, broader-scope successor to [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/choi-2013-dental-plaque-control-ohi-domestic-literature-sr]] (same first author) — widens the domestic literature base from 16 to 22 studies and pins down a concrete optimal PMPR regimen (4–5 visits at 1–2 week intervals) that the wiki lacked a Korean-specific citation for.

## Three-line Summary

This systematic review of 22 Korean studies (1990–2015) evaluated the effectiveness of professional mechanical plaque removal (PMPR) combined with oral hygiene education (OHE) or oral hygiene instruction (OHI) across children, adults, and elderly populations. The optimal regimen was 4–5 visits at 1–2 week intervals, with disclosing agent-based instruction showing greater efficacy than verbal or model-based education alone.

Plaque index reductions ranged from 38% to 9.4-fold decreases depending on intervention frequency and method; periodontal probing depth decreased by 0.1–2.25 mm in 3-year maintenance programs (Shin et al., 2001), and bleeding on probing improved significantly with repeated education (Jo and Min, 2010: 42.52% improvement).

Regular OHE combined with PMPR reduces tooth loss and improves periodontal health, but the lack of randomized controlled trials (RCTs) in Korean literature limits the strength of evidence; future research should adopt rigorous RCT designs and develop standardized outcome measures.

## 세줄요약

이 체계적 문헌고찰은 1990~2015년 한국 연구 22편을 분석하여 전문가 기계적 치면세마 (PMPR)와 구강보건교육 (OHE) 또는 구강위생교육 (OHI)의 효과를 아동, 성인, 노인에서 평가하였다. 최적 중재는 1~2주 간격 4~5회 방문이었으며, 착색제를 활용한 교육이 구두교육이나 모형교육보다 효과적이었다.

치면세균막 지수는 중재 방법과 횟수에 따라 38%에서 9.4배까지 감소하였고, 3년 유지관리에서 치주낭 깊이는 0.1~2.25 mm 감소하였으며(Shin 등, 2001), 반복교육 시 탐침 출혈이 유의하게 개선되었다(Jo와 Min, 2010: 42.52% 개선).

정기적 OHE와 PMPR은 치아상실을 줄이고 치주건강을 개선하나, 한국 문헌에 무작위 대조군 임상시험 (RCT)이 없어 근거 수준이 제한적이며 향후 엄격한 RCT 설계와 표준화된 결과 지표 개발이 필요하다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Systematic review of 22 Korean studies on PMPR and OHE/OHI from 1990–2015
- Optimal PMPR regimen identified: 4–5 visits at 1–2 week intervals using disclosing agent with repeated instruction
- Professional mechanical plaque removal combined with OHE reduced plaque index by 38% (Kim, 2011) to 9.4-fold (Kang et al., 2008)
- Maintenance care over 3 years reduced probing depth: 4–6 mm category decreased by 1.13 mm, ≥7 mm decreased by 2.25 mm (Shin et al., 2001)
- Disclosing agent-based instruction showed 24.1-point improvement in Patient Hygiene Performance (PHP) index versus model or verbal instruction (An et al., 2012)
- Repeated education (3–4 sessions at weekly intervals) was more effective than single instruction for plaque control
- Highlighted critical gap: no randomized controlled trials (RCTs) in Korean literature on this topic
- Routine OHE/OHI with PMPR reduces tooth loss, bleeding on probing, probing depth, and halitosis

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Systematic review (non-intervention, literature synthesis)
- Databases & search window: KoreaMed (46), KMbase (42), KISS (102), NDSL (56), KISTI (31) — 277 core database results; additional manual search of 5 Korean journals (Journal of Korean Academy of Oral Health, Journal of Dental Hygiene Science, Journal of Korean Society of Dental Hygiene, Journal of Korean Academy of Periodontology, Journal of the Korean Academy of Pediatric Dentistry) yielded 30 more records; total 307 records identified
- PRISMA compliance: Not explicitly stated (flow chart provided, Fig. 1)
- Inclusion criteria: Korean articles (1990–2015) on plaque control including OHE/OHI interventions; outcomes measured plaque index, periodontal pockets, attached gingiva, tooth loss, bleeding, probing depth
- Exclusion criteria: Duplicate publications, abstracts only, simple surveys, toothbrush testing studies, preliminary studies, interventions not matching core question
- Studies included: 22 final studies after screening (232 duplicates removed, 45 screened, 19 excluded for content mismatch, 4 excluded for unclear OHI)
- Risk of bias assessment: Not performed (narrative synthesis)
- Study types: Experimental-control group (5), within-subject comparison (10), comparison by management frequency (7)
- Age distribution: children/youth 4 studies (18.2%), adult 15 (68.2%), elderly 2 (9.1%), other 1 (4.5%)
- Outcome measures: O'Leary plaque index (12 studies), Patient Hygiene Performance (PHP) index (5), Simplified Oral Hygiene Index (S-OHI) (4), others (1)
- Statistics: Narrative synthesis; no meta-analysis performed due to heterogeneity in populations, interventions, and outcome measures

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Optimal PMPR regimen —** 4–5 visits at 1–2 week intervals; 1-week interval with 3–5 sessions most common (7 studies), 2-week interval with 3 sessions (3 studies)
- **Plaque index reduction —** Kim (2011): O'Leary plaque index 59.3% → 21.2% (38% reduction) after 3 sessions at 2-week intervals; Kang et al. (2008): Quigley Hein Plaque Index 9.4-fold decrease in preschool children; Eom et al. (2009): 3–4 repeated sessions showed 30→69% plaque control score improvement
- **Probing depth reduction (3-year maintenance) —** Shin et al. (2001): ≤3 mm category: 2.17→2.14 (0.03 mm decrease), 4–6 mm: 4.5→3.14 (1.36 mm decrease), ≥7 mm: 7.6→5.38 (2.25 mm decrease); Chae et al. (2009): ≤3 mm: 86.6%→93.88% (increase), 4–6 mm: 12.23%→5.56% (decrease), ≥7 mm: 1.17%→0.56% (decrease)
- **Bleeding on probing —** Jo and Min (2010): 42.52% improvement after 4 weekly sessions (31.55→74.07% plaque-free); Kim (2011): Interdental bleeding index 3.76→1.9 (1.86% decrease) after 6 weeks; Shin et al. (2001): 1-year maintenance: 15%→15.9% (0.8% increase), 3-year: 12.8%→12.4% (0.3% decrease)
- **Disclosing agent effectiveness —** An et al. (2012): PHP index improvement: model group 67.9→70.7 (2.8 points), direct practice group 57.7→69.3 (11.6 points), disclosing agent group 51.7→75.8 (24.1 points) — disclosing agent most effective
- **S-OHI improvement —** Son and Choi (2015): 2.28→1.44 at 6 months, 1.41 at 12 months (1.61-fold decrease); Lee et al. (2009): 2.87→1.13 (1.74 decrease) after 4 sessions
- **Halitosis reduction —** Chae et al. (2005): H₂S: 398.42→178.67 ppb, CH₄S: 133.04→60.77 ppb, C₂H₆S: 54.79→44.71 ppb after 3 sessions at 2-week intervals
- **Tooth loss reduction —** Axelsson et al. (2004) cited: PMPR reduced tooth loss from 30% to 5% over 30 years (external reference)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No randomized controlled trials (RCTs) identified in Korean literature; all included studies were quasi-experimental designs
- High heterogeneity across studies in populations, interventions, and outcome measures prevented meta-analysis
- Limited to Korean databases and Korean-language publications; potential publication bias and language bias
- No risk of bias assessment or quality scoring performed for included studies
- Core databases had overlapping records and lacked integrated search functionality; some databases did not support search query design
- Lack of standardized outcome measures across studies; multiple plaque indices used (O'Leary, PHP, S-OHI, Quigley Hein) with different scoring systems
- Economic evaluation and patient-reported outcomes not assessed
- Long-term data (>3 years) limited; most studies had short follow-up (4 weeks to 6 months)
- Elderly and special population data limited (only 2 studies on elderly, 1 on other populations)
- Need for developing Korean healthcare literature database comparable to MEDLINE/Cochrane for efficient systematic reviews

## 6. Related Work

- Van der Weijden and Timmerman (2002) — Systematic review on subgingival debridement clinical efficacy in chronic periodontitis; reported OHE with PMPR reduced plaque index, bleeding index, and gingivitis after ≥6 months
- Needleman et al. (2005) — Systematic review of professional mechanical plaque removal for prevention of periodontal diseases; PMPR with OHE, scaling, and root planing improved plaque, periodontal pockets, gingival recession and quality of life in adults ≥18 years
- Nicholls (2005) — 12-year retrospective analysis in general dental practice; regular dental treatment, periodontal care, and OHE slowed tooth loss rate
- Axelsson et al. (2004) — 30-year maintenance study (1972–2002) in 51–65-year-olds; PMPR reduced tooth loss from 30% to 5%; CPITN scores improved 0%→90%; attachment loss ≥3 mm reduced from 50% to 1 mm in 95% of sites
- Kim and Seong (1993) — First Korean study on toothbrushing instruction frequency and oral microorganism changes; 4 sessions at 4-week intervals significantly reduced streptococcus mutans and improved PHP

## 7. Glossary

- **PMPR (Professional Mechanical Plaque Removal)**: 전문가 기계적 치면세균막 제거; professional scaling, root planing, and toothbrushing performed by dental personnel
- **OHE (Oral Health Education)**: 구강보건교육; education provided to patients on oral hygiene practices
- **OHI (Oral Hygiene Instruction)**: 구강위생교육; instruction on toothbrushing and plaque control techniques
- **PHP Index (Patient Hygiene Performance Index)**: 구강환경관리능력지수; measures patient's ability to maintain oral hygiene
- **S-OHI (Simplified Oral Hygiene Index)**: 간이구강위생지수; simplified measure of debris and calculus
- **O'Leary Plaque Index**: 치면세균막 지수; percentage of tooth surfaces with plaque
- **SRP (Scaling and Root Planing)**: 스켈링과 치근활택술; non-surgical periodontal treatment
- **CPITN (Community Periodontal Index of Treatment Needs)**: 지역사회 치주치료필요지수; community-based periodontal disease screening index
