## Why Ingested

우리가 보유한 직접경구항응고제 (Direct Oral Anticoagulant, DOAC) 근거는 "중단하지 말라"까지는 도달했지만 ([[drug/anticoagulants/kim-2024-post-extraction-bleeding-direct-oral-anticoagulants]], [[drug/anticoagulants/xiang-2026-continuous-interrupted-doac-minimal-bleeding-sr-ma]]), *하루 중 언제 발치할 것인가*라는 실행 수준의 공백이 남아 있었다. 이 논문은 약동학 최저 혈중농도 (trough) 시점을 실제 프로토콜로 명시하고 와파린 대조군과 비교한 첫 전향 시험이라, 비중단 원칙을 예약 스케줄로 번역하는 고리를 채운다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective blinded three-arm feasibility trial at a Brazilian cardiology institute (InCor, University of São Paulo) in atrial fibrillation patients: 20 on rivaroxaban once daily, 18 on dabigatran/apixaban twice daily, 20 on warfarin as control — 58 patients, 84 extractions, DOACs never discontinued.

Extractions were timed to trough plasma concentration (14 h after last rivaroxaban dose; 8 h after last dabigatran/apixaban dose; warfarin arm at INR 2.0–3.0), and 24-hour bleeding was 0/20 (0%) rivaroxaban, 2/18 (11.1%) dabigatran/apixaban, 2/20 (10%) warfarin — no significant difference (P=.324) and zero major bleeding events.

Trough-timed scheduling makes uninterrupted DOAC extraction operationally concrete, but this is a small preliminary feasibility trial with no discontinuation comparator, so it cannot establish that timing itself confers the safety.

## 세줄요약

브라질 상파울루대 심장연구소 (InCor) 전향적 맹검 3군 실행가능성 시험: 심방세동 환자 58명·발치 84건을 리바록사반 1일1회 20명, 다비가트란/아픽사반 1일2회 18명, 와파린 대조 20명으로 나누고 직접경구항응고제 (DOAC)는 전혀 중단하지 않았다.

발치를 최저 혈중농도 (trough) 시점에 맞춰 시행 — 리바록사반 최종 복용 14시간 후, 다비가트란/아픽사반 8시간 후, 와파린군은 국제표준화비율 (International Normalized Ratio, INR) 2.0–3.0 — 24시간 출혈은 리바록사반 0/20(0%), 다비가트란/아픽사반 2/18(11.1%), 와파린 2/20(10%)로 군간 유의차 없었고(P=.324) 대량출혈은 0건이었다.

"중단하지 않는다"는 원칙을 *몇 시에 예약을 잡을 것인가*로 번역해준다는 점이 실무적 가치이나, 소규모 예비 시험이고 중단군 대조가 없어 타이밍 자체의 기여는 입증되지 않았다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Proposes and pilots a **pharmacokinetically timed** extraction protocol: rather than only asking *whether* to interrupt DOACs, it specifies *when in the dosing interval* to operate, keying the appointment to the trough plasma concentration.
- Supplies an **active warfarin comparator** managed at therapeutic INR 2.0–3.0, so uninterrupted DOAC extraction is benchmarked against the long-accepted uninterrupted-VKA standard rather than against a no-anticoagulant baseline.
- Separates DOACs by dosing frequency (once-daily rivaroxaban vs twice-daily dabigatran/apixaban) instead of pooling them, since the two schedules imply different trough intervals (14 h vs 8 h).
- Cardiology-institute setting with atrial fibrillation patients — the population in which the interrupt-or-not decision carries genuine thromboembolic cost.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: prospective blind clinical trial (feasibility); conducted at the Dentistry Department of the Heart Institute (InCor), Clinics Hospital, University of São Paulo.
- **Population**: anticoagulated patients with atrial fibrillation requiring tooth extraction.
- **Arms** (allocated by anticoagulant pharmacokinetics, not by randomization as described in the abstract):
  - Rivaroxaban, once daily — 20 patients; extraction **14 hours** after the last dose.
  - Dabigatran or apixaban, twice daily — 18 patients; extraction **8 hours** after the last dose.
  - Warfarin (control) — 20 patients; extraction with **INR 2.0–3.0**.
- **Anticoagulant handling**: DOACs not discontinued at any point.
- **Outcome assessment**: patients returned at **24 hours** post-extraction for bleeding evaluation; bleeding classified as minor vs major. Study described as "blind" — which party was blinded is not specified in the abstract.
- **Totals**: 58 patients, 84 tooth extractions.
- **Statistics**: between-group comparison of bleeding rates (P reported).
- **Not recoverable from the abstract**: local hemostatic measures used, extraction difficulty/surgical vs simple breakdown, arch distribution, CHA2DS2-VASc and HAS-BLED distributions, renal function, concomitant antiplatelet use, exclusion criteria, IRB/registration numbers, blinding detail.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Rivaroxaban (14 h trough)**: 0/20 — no minor and no major bleeding.
- **Dabigatran/apixaban (8 h trough)**: 2/18 (**11.1%**) minor bleeding.
- **Warfarin control (INR 2.0–3.0)**: 2/20 (**10%**) minor bleeding.
- **Between-group difference**: not statistically significant, **P = .324**.
- **Major bleeding**: **zero events across all 58 patients / 84 extractions**.
- **Interpretation by authors**: preliminary results suggest a non-discontinuation DOAC protocol is feasible in clinical practice.
- Note the direction of the numbers: uninterrupted DOAC at trough was, if anything, numerically at or below the uninterrupted-warfarin rate — but with 4 total events the study is powered only for feasibility, not for equivalence.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Very small and underpowered**: 58 patients, 4 total bleeding events; P=.324 across three arms establishes no difference *was detected*, not that none exists. Authors themselves label the findings preliminary.
- **No discontinuation arm**: every patient continued therapy, so the trial cannot isolate the contribution of the trough-timing rule from the general safety of continuation. A patient extracted at peak concentration was never observed.
- **Allocation by drug, not randomized**: arms differ in anticoagulant *and* in dosing schedule *and* potentially in baseline risk; the warfarin "control" is a different-drug comparison, not a randomized control.
- **24-hour follow-up window only** — delayed post-extraction bleeding beyond day 1, a recognized DOAC pattern, would be missed.
- **Extraction burden unreported at arm level**: 84 extractions across 58 patients means multiple extractions per visit for some, but the per-arm surgical difficulty is not recoverable from the abstract.
- **Abstract-only ingest**: local hemostasis protocol — the factor most of our other holdings identify as decisive — is not available here, which materially limits reproduction of the protocol.
- **Future work**: larger, adequately powered and ideally randomized trials; explicit peak-vs-trough comparison; longer bleeding surveillance.

## 7. Glossary

- **DOAC (Direct Oral Anticoagulant)**: anticoagulant directly inhibiting a single coagulation factor — dabigatran (thrombin/factor IIa), rivaroxaban/apixaban/edoxaban (factor Xa).
- **Trough (최저 혈중농도)**: the lowest plasma drug concentration in a dosing interval, immediately before the next dose. For once-daily rivaroxaban the trough window used here is ~14 h post-dose; for twice-daily dabigatran/apixaban, ~8 h.
- **Peak vs trough**: DOACs have short half-lives and pronounced peak-trough swing, which is what makes appointment timing a plausible lever — unlike warfarin, whose effect is near-constant across a day.
- **INR (International Normalized Ratio)**: standardized prothrombin time measure; therapeutic range 2.0–3.0 for atrial fibrillation on warfarin. Reliable for VKAs, **not** a valid monitor for DOACs.
- **Minor vs major bleeding**: minor = manageable with local measures; major = requiring transfusion, hospitalization, or intervention. No major events occurred in this trial.
- **Atrial fibrillation (심방세동)**: the indication in all three arms; carries stroke risk that makes anticoagulant interruption non-trivial.
