## Why Ingested

2026년 코크란 업데이트(pub5, 2013년 이후 검색 연장)로 [[drug/antibiotics/esposito-2013-antibiotics-dental-implant-placement-cochrane]] pub4를 완전 대체. 포함 RCT가 6편→15편, 참여자 1162명→2874명으로 확대. NNT=19(vs 25)로 항생제 예방 효과가 더 선명히 확인. 토르프-2023 및 모만드-2024의 분석들이 의존하는 근거 사슬의 최신 기점.

## One-line Summary

Cochrane SR+MA pub5 (search to Nov 2025): 15 RCTs, 2874 participants; prophylactic amoxicillin 2 g preop probably reduces early implant failure (RR 0.34; NNT=19), with single dose equivalent to multiple doses.

## 한줄요약

코크란 SR+MA pub5 (2025년 11월까지 검색): 15편 RCT, 2874명; 술전 아목시실린 2g 단일 투여가 조기 임플란트 실패를 아마 감소(RR 0.34; NNT=19)시키며, 단일 투여와 다중 투여 간 차이 없음.

## Three-line Summary

Cochrane SR+MA update (pub5), 15 RCTs (2874 participants), CENTRAL/MEDLINE/Embase January 2013–November 2025 — prophylactic antibiotics for dental implant placement without bone augmentation, updating the 2013 pub4 review.

Prophylactic amoxicillin 2 g pre-operatively probably reduces early implant failure (RR 0.34; NNT=19); single dose is equivalent to multiple doses; absolute risk reduction was from 80 to 27 failures per 1000 participants.

8 of 15 RCTs were at high risk of bias; one severe allergic reaction was documented in the multiple-dose group; GRADE certainty was moderate for the primary comparison.

## 세줄요약

코크란 SR+MA pub5, 15편 RCT (2874명), CENTRAL/MEDLINE/Embase 2013년 1월–2025년 11월 — 골증대술 없는 임플란트 식립 시 예방적 항생제 효과 (2013년 pub4 업데이트).

술전 아목시실린 2 g 단회 투여가 조기 임플란트 실패를 아마 감소 (RR 0.34; NNT=19); 단일 투여와 다중 투여 간 유의차 없음.

15편 중 8편 고위험 비뚤림; 다중 투여군에서 중증 알러지 반응 1건; 1차 비교 GRADE 근거 수준 중등.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest meta-analysis of antibiotic prophylaxis at implant placement to date (15 RCTs vs 6 in pub4)
- Confirms single preoperative dose equivalent to multiple doses (low certainty; no significant difference)
- Adds direct amoxicillin vs clindamycin comparison: no significant difference (1 RCT, 81 participants)
- Documents one severe allergic reaction (hospitalisation) in multiple-dose group — argues for parsimonious use
- Absolute risk reduction: from 80 to 27 failures per 1000 participants (NNT=19)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Study type: Cochrane SR+MA update
- Databases: CENTRAL, MEDLINE, Embase, WHO ICTRP, ClinicalTrials.gov (Jan 2013–Nov 2025)
- Eligibility: RCTs ≥3-month follow-up, ≥10 participants/arm, implant placement without concomitant bone augmentation
- Comparisons evaluated: (1) Antibiotics vs placebo/no antibiotics; (2) Single vs multiple doses; (3) Preop vs postop; (4) Amoxicillin vs clindamycin
- Risk of bias: RoB 1 tool; 8/15 low risk, 7/15 high risk
- Statistical: random-effects MA; RR with 95% CI; GRADE

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Comparison 1: Antibiotics vs placebo/no antibiotics**
- Implant failures: RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.22–0.53); I²=0%; 10 studies, 1919 participants; **moderate certainty**
- Prosthesis failures: RR 0.38 (95% CI 0.23–0.60); I²=0%; 9 studies, 1864 participants; **moderate certainty**
- Postoperative infections: RR 0.55 (95% CI 0.33–0.93); I²=0%; 10 studies, 1919 participants; **moderate certainty**
- Adverse events: RR 0.63 (95% CI 0.08–5.09); 9 studies, 1900 participants; **very low certainty**

**Comparison 2: Single vs multiple dose**
- Implant failures: RR 0.55 (95% CI 0.20–1.55); I²=0%; 5 studies, 760 participants; **low certainty**
- Postoperative infections: RR 0.57 (95% CI 0.21–1.50); **low certainty** — no meaningful difference

**Comparison 3: Preop vs postop**
- All outcomes: very low certainty; RR 2.12 (0.19–23.06); 2 studies, 293 participants — uninformative

**Comparison 4: Amoxicillin vs clindamycin**
- No significant differences; low certainty; 1 study, 81 participants

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Studies published 2008–2024, mostly European + North American settings
- Most regimens tested single preop amoxicillin 2g; other antibiotics or routes underrepresented
- Post-immediate-extraction implant scenarios not separately analysed
- Adverse events rare and inconsistently reported; cannot exclude rare serious harms
- Authors call for RCTs comparing different regimens and timing in post-extraction contexts

## 7. Glossary

- **NNT**: Number needed to treat; NNT=19 means 19 patients must receive prophylaxis to prevent 1 failure
- **RoB 1**: Cochrane Risk of Bias version 1 tool (5 domains)
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations, Assessment, Development and Evaluations — certainty of evidence scale
- **CCA**: Corrected Covered Area — quantifies trial overlap among reviews
