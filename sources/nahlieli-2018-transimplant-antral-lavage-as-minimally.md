## Why Ingested

The odontogenic-sinusitis category holds diagnostic reviews and consensus on dental-ENT management but nothing on treating sinus disease *through* an implant channel. This preliminary report describes a transimplant antral lavage (puncture, drainage, saline irrigation, steroid injection) via an implant with an internal port, which bears on the question of irrigation in sinus disease that [[oral-medicine/odontogenic-sinusitis/craig-2022-odontogenic-sinusitis-state-art]] and [[oral-medicine/odontogenic-sinusitis/albu-2025-treatment-landscape-odontogenic-sinusitis]] frame as a conventional ENT/dental-source-removal problem. Added as part of a PubMed sweep on implant-port sinus treatment; it is a small uncontrolled series and should be read as hypothesis-generating.

## Three-line Summary

Preliminary uncontrolled series of 11 consecutive patients (mean age 68.1 years) with chronic rhinosinusitis (CRS) treated by transimplant antral lavage through an implant with an internal port (dental implant valve approach, DIVA): 3 via previously installed implants and 8 with implant placement combined with the CRS treatment.

Symptomatic improvement began from day 1; 9 of 11 patients had complete relief of most symptoms (nasal obstruction/discharge, anosmia/hyposmia) at day 30, follow-up nasal endoscopy showed no active sinus disease, and all implants were osseointegrated and in prosthetic use with no recurrence in the follow-up period.

Suggests an implant with an internal central port and sealing screw can serve as an oroantral port for irrigation, observation and further treatment of the maxillary sinus, but this abstract-only record reports no control group, no follow-up duration and no separation of odontogenic from non-odontogenic CRS.

## 세줄요약

만성 비부비동염 (Chronic Rhinosinusitis, CRS) 환자 11명(평균 연령 68.1세)을 연속 모집한 예비 비대조 증례군 — 내부 포트가 있는 임플란트 (dental implant valve approach, DIVA)를 통한 임플란트 경유 상악동 세척 (transimplant antral lavage); 기존 식립 임플란트 3명, 식립과 동시 시행 8명.

치료 첫날부터 증상 호전이 시작되었고 30일째 11명 중 9명에서 대부분의 증상(비폐색·콧물, 후각상실/저하)이 완전히 해소되었으며, 추적 비내시경에서 활동성 부비동 질환 소견이 없었고 모든 임플란트가 골유착 상태로 보철에 사용 중이며 추적 기간 재발이 없었다.

내부 중앙 포트와 밀폐 스크류를 갖춘 임플란트가 상악동 세척·관찰·추가 치료용 구강-상악동 통로가 될 수 있음을 시사하나, 초록만 확인한 이 기록에는 대조군·추적 기간·치성/비치성 CRS 구분이 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reports use of an implant with an internal port (dental implant valve approach, DIVA) as an oroantral port to treat chronic rhinosinusitis in a minimally invasive manner.
- Describes the transimplant lavage sequence: sinus membrane puncture via the implant channel once the implant is fully installed, pus drainage, saline irrigation, then injection of a steroid solution (100 mg hydrocortisone).
- Applies the technique both through previously installed implants (3 patients) and combined with implant placement (8 patients).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Preliminary report of 11 consecutive cases (uncontrolled; prospective vs retrospective not stated in the abstract).
- **n**: 11 patients, mean age 68.1 years.
- **Intervention**: Transimplant lavage via DIVA implant channel; saline irrigation followed by 100 mg hydrocortisone injection.
- **Outcomes**: Symptoms (nasal obstruction/discharge, anosmia/hyposmia), follow-up nasal endoscopy, implant osseointegration/prosthetic use, clinical and radiologic stability/recurrence.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Symptomatic improvement reported from the first day after the first-session procedure.
- Nine of 11 patients had complete relief of most symptoms at day 30.
- Follow-up nasal endoscopy showed no evidence of active sinus disease.
- All implants were well-osseointegrated and still in use for prosthetic purposes.
- Clinical and radiologic results showed stability and no recurrence in the follow-up period (duration not stated in the abstract).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest: full-text details (inclusion criteria, CRS etiology, follow-up length, number of sessions, outcome scoring) were not retrieved.
- Uncontrolled series of 11 patients with no comparator; "preliminary report" per the title.
- The abstract does not state whether CRS was odontogenic; relevance to odontogenic sinusitis is by dental-ENT management setting, not by a confirmed dental source.
- Two of 11 patients did not reach complete relief of most symptoms at day 30 (outcome not described in the abstract).

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary

- **DIVA (dental implant valve approach)**: implant with an internal port and integral dedicated sealing screw used as a channel into the maxillary sinus.
- **CRS (chronic rhinosinusitis)**: chronic inflammation of the nasal and paranasal sinus mucosa.
- **Transimplant antral lavage**: sinus membrane puncture, drainage and irrigation performed through the implant channel.
