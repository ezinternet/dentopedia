## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis addressing two focused questions on immediate versus delayed dental implant placement (search through November 2019): Q1 pooled 6 head-to-head RCTs comparing immediate vs delayed placement; Q2 pooled 53 studies (22 RCTs, 11 CCTs, 20 case series) of immediate placement alone.

Q1 meta-analyses found no significant difference in implant survival between immediate and delayed placement, but immediate placement showed significantly better marginal bone levels and Pink Esthetic Score (PES) at 1-year post-loading; Q2 found immediate implants had a 97% survival rate and high PES scores (10.36-11.25).

Despite similar survival, immediate implants carried threefold early and twofold delayed complication rates versus delayed placement — success/complication reporting criteria need to be more consistent across future studies, and marginal bone loss / gingival-papillary recession data varied widely among included studies.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Splits the immediate-vs-delayed timing question into two distinct focused questions with different eligible study designs — Q1 (RCT-only comparative) vs Q2 (all-design pooled single-arm) — rather than pooling heterogeneous designs together, isolating design-driven effect-size differences.
- Meta-analyzes eight parameters spanning survival, success, radiographic marginal bone levels, buccal wall height/thickness, peri-implant mucosal margin position, esthetics, and patient-reported outcomes (PROMs).
- Quantifies immediate-implant complication burden relative to delayed placement (threefold early, twofold delayed), a dimension often omitted from survival-only comparisons.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Electronic search of MEDLINE, EMBASE, Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials, and OpenGray, plus hand search, conducted up to November 2019.
- Q1 (comparative): RCTs with delayed implant placement as the control arm.
- Q2 (single-arm pooled): immediate-implant arms of RCTs, controlled clinical trials (CCTs), and prospective case series.
- Outcomes: survival rate, success rate, radiographic marginal bone levels, buccal wall height/thickness, peri-implant mucosal margin position, aesthetic outcomes, patient-reported outcomes.
- Abstract-only retrieval — full-text methodology detail (risk-of-bias tool, statistical model, heterogeneity handling) not extracted from this source; see original article for full detail.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Q1 (6 RCTs): no significant difference in implant survival between immediate and delayed placement.
- Q1: immediate placement significantly favored for marginal bone levels and PES at 1-year post-loading.
- Q2 (53 studies): immediate implants pooled survival rate ~97%; PES range 10.36-11.25.
- Marginal bone loss and gingival/papillary recession data varied substantially across included studies (not pooled into a single estimate per the abstract).
- Complications: immediate implants ~3x early complications and ~2x delayed complications versus delayed placement.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingestion (`full_text: false`) — risk-of-bias assessment details, heterogeneity (I²) statistics, and funnel-plot/publication-bias analysis are not captured here; re-extract from full text if deeper appraisal is needed.
- Q1's comparative pool is small (6 RCTs), limiting precision of the survival non-difference finding.
- Q2 pools heterogeneous designs (RCT arms, CCTs, case series) without a delayed-placement comparator, so its 97% survival/PES figures describe immediate implants in isolation, not a head-to-head effect size.
- Search cutoff (November 2019) predates several years of subsequent RCTs on immediate placement.
- Authors explicitly call for more consistent success-criteria reporting and further exploration of complication incidence/type.

## 7. Glossary

- **IIP (Immediate Implant Placement)**: implant placement into a fresh extraction socket at the time of tooth removal.
- **DIP (Delayed Implant Placement)**: implant placement after a period of socket/ridge healing following extraction.
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score)**: composite index scoring peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics (papilla, mucosal contour, level, color, texture, alveolar process deficiency).
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level)**: radiographic measurement of crestal bone height around an implant, tracked over time as a proxy for peri-implant bone stability.
- **CCT (Controlled Clinical Trial)**: a comparative trial without randomization.
- **RCT (Randomised Controlled Trial)**: a comparative trial with randomized allocation to intervention/control arms.
