## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis addressing two focused questions on immediate versus delayed dental implant placement (search through November 2019; MEDLINE, EMBASE, CENTRAL, OpenGray + hand search, 6457 records → 138 full-text): Q1 pooled 6 head-to-head RCTs comparing immediate vs delayed placement (495 patients, 480 implants); Q2 pooled 53 studies (22 RCTs, 11 CCTs, 20 case series) of immediate placement alone.

Q1 meta-analyses found no significant difference in implant survival (RR 0.98, 95% CI 0.95–1.01; I²=0%), but immediate placement showed significantly better marginal bone levels (2 studies) and Pink Esthetic Score (PES) at 1-year post-loading; Q2 found immediate implants had a 97.0% pooled 1-year survival (95% CI 95–99%) and high PES scores (10.36–11.25 at 5–10 years); all five meta-regression covariates (flap, restoration timing, grafting, position, follow-up) were non-significant.

Despite similar survival, immediate implants carried threefold early (mainly surgical) and twofold delayed (mainly aesthetic) complication rates versus delayed placement — 11% of immediate implants experienced complications, which would have counted as failures under strict success criteria; success criteria reporting needs to be more consistent and complication incidence/type further explored.

## 세줄요약

즉시(Immediate) vs 지연(Delayed) 임플란트 식립 비교 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(2019년 11월까지 검색, MEDLINE·EMBASE·CENTRAL·OpenGray+수기검색 6457건→전문 138편): Q1은 즉시-지연 head-to-head RCT 6편(환자 495명, 임플란트 480개), Q2는 즉시식립 단독 53편(RCT 22+CCT 11+증례군 20)을 각각 풀링.

Q1 메타분석에서 임플란트 생존율 차이는 유의하지 않았으나(상대위험도 RR 0.98, 95% CI 0.95–1.01), 1년 부하 후 변연골수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL)·핑크심미점수 (Pink Esthetic Score, PES)는 즉시군이 유의하게 우수; Q2에서 즉시식립 1년 생존율 97.0%(95% CI 95–99%), PES 10.36–11.25(5–10년)로 높게 보고.

생존율은 유사했으나 즉시식립은 조기 합병증 3배(주로 수술적)·지연 합병증 2배(주로 심미적)였고, 즉시식립 임플란트의 11%가 합병증 발생 — 엄격한 성공기준 하에선 실패로 간주될 수 있음. 성공기준·합병증 보고의 일관성 강화가 필요.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Splits the immediate-vs-delayed timing question into two distinct focused questions with different eligible study designs — Q1 (RCT-only comparative) vs Q2 (all-design pooled single-arm) — rather than pooling heterogeneous designs together, isolating design-driven effect-size differences.
- Meta-analyzes eight parameters spanning survival, success, radiographic marginal bone levels, buccal wall height/thickness, peri-implant mucosal margin position, esthetics, and patient-reported outcomes (PROMs).
- Quantifies immediate-implant complication burden relative to delayed placement (threefold early, twofold delayed; 11% of immediate implants overall), a dimension often omitted from survival-only comparisons; these complications would have counted as failures under stricter success definitions.
- Runs meta-regression on five clinical covariates (flap design, immediate non-occluding restoration, grafting, anterior/posterior position, follow-up duration) — all non-significant for survival, a first-order clinical calibration for immediate-placement decision-making.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: SR+MA per Cochrane guidelines, PRISMA (Moher 2010), PROSPERO CRD42020186059.
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (In-Process & Non-Indexed + 1950–present via Ovid), EMBASE Classic + EMBASE (1947–present via Ovid), Cochrane CENTRAL, OpenGray; extensive hand/search of 11 journals 2001–Nov 2019. No language restriction.
- **Screening**: Initial 6457 records (6 from hand search) → 138 full-text assessed; duplicate screening by 2 reviewers (inter-rater kappa FQ1 = 0.762, FQ2 = 0.782), third reviewer arbitrated.
- **Eligibility**: single-tooth immediate DI placement, ≥10 patients/group (RCTs) or ≥20 (single arms/case series), ≥12 months post-loading; retrospective, medically-compromised, immediate full-occluding loading, adjacent-implant studies excluded. Q1 = RCTs with delayed/late (types 2–4) control; Q2 = immediate arms of RCTs + CCTs + prospective case series.
- **Risk of bias**: modified Cochrane tool (Higgins & Green 2011), 7 domains (randomization, allocation concealment, blinding participants/personnel, blinding outcome assessment, incomplete data, selective reporting, other); 2 independent assessors.
- **Statistical model**: random-effects meta-analysis throughout; dichotomous data as risk ratio (RR) with 95% CI; FQ2 as proportion estimates (ES); meta-regression on flap (flap vs flapless), GBR/grafting, restoration timing, membrane presence, and implant position. Stata 13.
- **n**: Q1 — 6 RCTs, 495 patients, 480 implants (243 immediate, 237 delayed); Q2 — 53 papers (22 RCT arms, 11 CCT arms, 20 case series).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Q1 survival**: 5-study MA (I²=0%, p=.563): RR 0.98 (95% CI 0.95–1.01), no significant difference, trend favoring delayed; survival range 84.6–100% (immediate) vs 96.5–100% (delayed).
- **Q1 success**: reported in only 3 studies with different criteria — meta-analysis not possible.
- **Q1 complications** (4 RCTs): immediate — 24 early (3 mechanical, 4 technical, 17 post-surgical) + 28 late (2 biological, 26 aesthetic); delayed — 7 early (3 mechanical, 4 surgical) + 12 late (12 aesthetic). ~3x early, ~2x delayed.
- **Q1 MBL**: 2-study MA at 1 year (Esposito 2015, Felice 2015): significantly more bone loss in delayed/late implants.
- **Q1 PES**: 2-study MA at 1 year (I²=74.3%, p=.048): significantly higher PES in immediate group. WES (1 study, Tonetti 2017): no difference.
- **Q1 PROMs**: 4 studies; 3 no difference; Raes 2016 favored delayed at 5 years.
- **Q2 survival**: 1-yr 97.0% (95% CI 95–99%, I²=55.5%); 3-yr 98.0% (97–100%, n=11); 5-yr 97.0% (95–99%, n=10); 8–10-yr 97.0% (86–100%, n=3). Survival range across studies 66.7–100%.
- **Q2 subgroups**: immediate-restored (not loaded) 98/100/98/94% at 2/3/4–5/8yr; delayed-restored 96/97/96/97%; anterior 98/99/98/93%; posterior 86% at 2yr (72–96) → 98% at 4–5yr; flapless 99/100/97%; flap 95/99/94/92%; grafted 97/99/98%; no-graft 97/100/96/94%.
- **Q2 meta-regression**: flap p=.382; immediate non-occluding restoration p=.688; bone graft p=.591; implant position p=.804; follow-up duration p=.713 — none significant.
- **Q2 complications**: 31 studies, 2426 implants / 2077 patients; 11% of implants experienced complications (6% early, 5% late). Early: surgical 2.8%, biological 2.3% dominant; late: biological 2.3%, aesthetic 2.0% dominant. Raw counts 147 early (4 technical, 7 mechanical, 126 post-surgical, 10 aesthetic) + 119 late (3 technical, 8 mechanical, 58 biological, 49 aesthetic).
- **Q2 MBL**: 23 studies; ranges — 1yr (n=13): +0.08 mm gain to −0.88 mm loss; 2yr (n=5): +0.1 to −1.0 mm; 3yr (n=7): +0.17 to −1.1 mm; 5yr (n=5): +0.2 to −1.08 mm.
- **Q2 gingival margin**: 0.23 mm gain to 0.71 mm loss at 1–5 years; papilla recession <0.5 mm at ≥5 years.
- **Q2 PES**: mean 10.36–11.25 at 5–10 years (abstract); range 6.8–13 across timepoints (text).
- **Q2 PROMs**: only 11/53 studies reported PROMs; only 4 used a validated tool (OHIP-14).
- **Risk of bias**: FQ1 — 2 low (Felice 2015, Tonetti 2017), 2 unclear (Esposito 2015, Raes 2016), 2 high (Block 2009, Tsirlis 2005). FQ2 — 6 low, 5 with 1 unclear domain, 6 unclear, 27/53 high in ≥1 domain; allocation concealment and blinding most common bias sources.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Follow-up mostly short (75% of included studies ≤3 years post-loading).
- Success criteria rarely reported and non-uniform across studies — success meta-analysis impossible in both FQ1 and FQ2; no consideration of aesthetic/PROM components in success definitions.
- MBL and PES Q1 meta-analyses rest on very few studies (2 each); Q1 MBL comparison may be confounded by delayed arms placed in grafted/ridge-preserved sites (Esposito 2015, Felice 2015).
- Buccal plate remodelling (height/thickness) under-reported; only one CBCT study with baseline data (Raes 2018); authors call for baseline-CBCT longitudinal studies.
- Meta-regression underpowered (small per-subgroup study counts; e.g., posterior 2-yr group n=7 with 86% CI 72–96%).
- Only 1 RCT showed more recession in delayed group (Block 2009, high RoB) — mucosal margin position long-term remains unresolved.
- Search cutoff November 2019 predates several subsequent RCTs; authors contacted for missing data but no replies within 4 weeks.
- Smokers were included in most studies (no medical-compromise exclusion besides uncontrolled diabetes/cancer), and confounding factors (socket morphology, grafting materials, phenotype, abutment length, implant orientation) may explain heterogeneity.

## 7. Glossary

- **IIP (Immediate Implant Placement)**: implant placement into a fresh extraction socket at the time of tooth removal (ITI type 1).
- **DIP (Delayed Implant Placement)**: implant placement after a socket/ridge healing period (ITI types 2–4, ≥6 weeks post-extraction in this review's comparison arm).
- **PES (Pink Esthetic Score)**: composite index scoring peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics (papilla, mucosal contour/level/color/texture, alveolar-process deficiency).
- **WES (White Esthetic Score)**: composite index scoring the esthetics of the prosthetic crown (tooth form, outline, color, surface texture, translucency).
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Level)**: radiographic measurement of crestal bone height around an implant, tracked over time as a proxy for peri-implant bone stability.
- **CCT (Controlled Clinical Trial)**: a comparative trial without randomization.
- **RCT (Randomised Controlled Trial)**: a comparative trial with randomized allocation to intervention/control arms.
- **PROM (Patient-Reported Outcome Measure)**: outcomes reported directly by the patient (e.g., OHIP-14, VAS), without clinician interpretation.
- **RoB (Risk of Bias)**: appraisal of systematic error in a study; here the modified Cochrane 7-domain tool.
