## 7. Glossary

- **Local Anesthesia (LA)**: 국소마취 — here, dental injection anesthesia during routine treatment.
- **Anaphylaxis**: 아나필락시스 — immediate systemic hypersensitivity reaction; typically with skin/mucosal involvement, which is exactly what is missing in this paper's scenario.
- **Vasovagal Reaction (VVS)**: 미주신경성 실신/반응 — reflex bradycardia and hypotension mediated by vagal overactivity (Bezold-Jarisch pathway), the principal alternative explanation raised in the title.
- **Hypotension**: 저혈압 — the single objective sign the title anchors on.
- **Skin symptoms**: 피부증상 — urticaria, flushing, angioedema; their absence is the discriminator the paper's title turns on.
