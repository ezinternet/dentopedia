## Why Ingested
Primescan이 포스트 준비 공간(post preparation)을 디지털로 취득할 수 있는지 처음 타당성을 검증한 연구 — [[post-and-core/]] 카테고리와 [[digital-workflow/]] 모두에 연결되는 새 임상 시나리오이며, 기존 단순 왕관 준비 정확도 근거를 근관 치료 후 치아로 확장한다.

## One-line Summary
In vitro µ-CT study (n=20 premolars): Primescan achieved higher trueness and precision than TRIOS 4 for post space scanning, though accuracy was scanner- and depth-dependent.

## 한줄요약
소구치 20개 µ-CT 기준 in vitro 연구: 포스트 준비 공간 스캔에서 프라임스캔이 TRIOS 4보다 진도성·정밀도 모두 우수, 특히 깊은 준비(12 mm)에서 차이 현저.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First µ-CT-based feasibility assessment of IOS for post space digitization
- Primescan demonstrated superior trueness and precision vs TRIOS 4 at both depths (10 mm, 12 mm)
- Increasing preparation depth (10→12 mm) significantly worsened accuracy for both scanners (p < 0.001)
- Positive correlation between trueness and precision (r = 0.838)
- Preparation diameter (ISO 090 vs ISO 110) had no significant effect on trueness

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro comparative (extracted teeth)
- **Specimens**: 20 extracted mandibular premolars; coronal substance loss simulated; endodontic treatment performed; post spaces: depths 10 mm and 12 mm, diameters ISO 090 and ISO 110 (n=5 per group)
- **IOS**: Primescan (PR), TRIOS 4 (TR)
- **Reference**: µ-CT (micro-computed tomography) — higher accuracy than conventional laboratory scanners for narrow preparations
- **Analysis**: Intraoral scan STL aligned to µ-CT models using best-fit at three cross-sectional levels
- **Statistics**: Three-way ANOVA, post-hoc, independent t-tests, Pearson correlation (α = 0.05)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Trueness**: PR > TR (p < 0.001); scanner × depth interaction significant (p < 0.001)
- **Precision**: PR > TR (p < 0.001); particularly pronounced at 12 mm depth
- Depth effect: 12 mm worse than 10 mm for both scanners (p < 0.001)
- Diameter (ISO 090 vs 110): no significant effect on trueness; minor but significant effect on precision (p = 0.041)
- Trueness–precision correlation: r = 0.838 (high)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Feasibility study only; sample sizes small (n=5 per group)
- In vitro — saliva, soft tissue, clinical isolation not modeled
- Only two IOS tested; no conventional post impression comparison
- Clinical threshold for "acceptable" post-space scanning accuracy not yet established
- Future work: clinical study; threshold validation against crown/post fit outcomes

## 7. Glossary
- **Post space / post preparation**: root canal preparation to receive a post-and-core restoration
- **µ-CT (micro-CT)**: micro-computed tomography; resolution < 10 µm, superior reference for narrow preparations
- **ISO 090 / ISO 110**: post diameter classification (0.9 mm and 1.1 mm respectively)
- **Best-fit alignment**: software-based optimal superimposition of two 3D surfaces
