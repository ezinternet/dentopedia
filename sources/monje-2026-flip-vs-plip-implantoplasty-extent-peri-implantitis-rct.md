## Why Ingested

First controlled trial comparing the extent of implantoplasty in combined (resective + reconstructive) peri-implantitis surgery: extending implantoplasty into the intrabony compartment (FLIP) vs restricting it to the supracrestal component only (PLIP). Addresses a practical intraoperative decision-point not previously tested in an RCT; adds to [[implants/peri-implantitis/park-2025-implantoplasty-vs-titanium-brushes-peri-implantitis-rct]] which covers titanium brush vs implantoplasty (resective only), creating a paired reference for implantoplasty depth decisions.

## Three-line Summary

Quasi-randomized controlled trial (n=33 patients / 40 implants; single-center, Badajoz, Spain; Oct 2021–Sep 2025) comparing full-length implantoplasty (FLIP: supracrestal + intrabony) vs partial-length implantoplasty (PLIP: supracrestal only) in combined surgical peri-implantitis therapy with 1-year follow-up.

FLIP achieved higher unadjusted disease resolution (90.4% vs 64.6%; unadjusted OR=11.1, p=0.04) and better MBL gain (2.42 vs 1.62 mm; p=0.009 adjusted) and mSBI reduction (p=0.003), but at the cost of significantly greater mucosal recession (1.84 vs 1.33 mm; p=0.006); adjusted disease resolution model did not reach significance (OR=14, p=0.13).

Overall disease resolution 77.5%; smoking was the only variable independently associated with failure (OR=0.01; p=0.013); study is underpowered for disease resolution and limited by quasi-randomization and author conflict of interest.

## 세줄요약

준무작위대조시험 (Quasi-Randomized Controlled Trial) (n=33명/40개 임플란트; 스페인 바다호스; 2021.10–2025.9): 임플란트주위염 (Peri-implantitis) 병합 수술 치료 시 전장 임플란토플라스티 (Full-Length Implantoplasty, FLIP: 치조정상부 + 골내 성분) vs 부분 임플란토플라스티 (Partial-Length Implantoplasty, PLIP: 치조정상부만) 비교, 12개월 추적.

FLIP의 비보정 질환 해소율 90.4% vs PLIP 64.6% (비보정 OR=11.1, p=0.04); 치조골 수준 (Marginal Bone Level, MBL) 획득 FLIP 2.42 mm vs PLIP 1.62 mm (보정 p=0.009) 유의한 차이; 단 치은퇴축 (Mucosal Recession, MR) 은 FLIP에서 유의하게 더 큼 (1.84 vs 1.33 mm; p=0.006).

전체 질환 해소율 77.5%; 흡연만이 독립 실패 예측 인자 (OR=0.01; p=0.013); 질환 해소를 위한 검정력 부족, 준무작위 설계, 주저자 이해충돌 (사용 버 특허권 소유, 골이식재 회사 후원)로 해석 주의 요망.

## Key Contributions

- First controlled comparison of implantoplasty extent (full vs partial) in combined peri-implantitis surgery
- FLIP showed higher disease resolution in unadjusted analysis (90.4% vs 64.6%) and superior MBL gain; however adjusted model lost significance — interpretation should be cautious
- Trade-off: FLIP provides better bone and bleeding outcomes at the cost of ~50% more mucosal recession than PLIP
- Smoking independently associated with failure regardless of implantoplasty extent (OR 0.01)
- No implant fractures despite implantoplasty into intrabony compartment (all standard/wide-diameter implants)
- Ti-6Al-4V implants not included — vanadium toxicity concern is mitigated in modern implant systems

## Methodology

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Quasi-randomized (allocation by last digit of record number — not true RCT) |
| Setting | Single center, Badajoz, Spain |
| Period | Oct 2021 – Sep 2025 |
| n | 33 patients / 40 implants (FLIP: 18 pts/19 implants; PLIP: 15 pts/21 implants) |
| Inclusion | Combined defect (IB, IIIB, IIIC), intrabony ≥3 mm, implant ≥3.5 mm, KM ≥2 mm |
| FLIP | Tungsten carbide + Arkansas + silicon burs through full implant length (supracrestal + intrabony) |
| PLIP | Same burs but implantoplasty restricted to supracrestal component only |
| Both groups | After implantoplasty: NiTi brushes + H₂O₂ 3% 2 min + tetracycline chlorhydrate 2 min + saline; then autogenous bone 1:1 xenograft ± PRF ± collagen membrane |
| Post-op | CHX 0.2% + chitosan 0.5% gel ×3/day ×2 weeks; amoxicillin 750 mg ×2/day ×7 days; ibuprofen 600 mg |
| Recall | Structured 4-month peri-implant maintenance; air polisher (erythritol) + ultrasonics |
| Primary outcome | Disease resolution (BOP ≤1 site + no suppuration + PPD ≤5 mm + no bone loss ≥1 mm beyond SE) at 12 months |

## Key Results

| Parameter | PLIP | FLIP | p (intergroup) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PPD reduction (mm) | 3.53 ± 2.02 | 3.83 ± 1.45 | 0.42 (NS) |
| mSBI reduction | 1.73 → 0.14 | 2.29 → 0.10 | **0.003** (FLIP better) |
| Mucosal recession increase (mm) | 0.62 → 1.33 (+0.71) | 0.11 → 1.84 (+1.73) | **0.006** (FLIP worse) |
| MBL gain (mm) | 1.62 ± 1.27 | 2.42 ± 1.20 | **0.009** (adjusted, FLIP better) |
| Intrabony defect reduction (mm) | 2.02 ± 0.85 | 2.47 ± 1.36 | 0.40 (NS) |
| Disease resolution | 64.6% | 90.4% | — |
| Unadjusted OR (disease resolution) | ref | 11.1 | 0.04 |
| Adjusted OR (disease resolution) | ref | 14 | 0.13 (NS) |

- **Smoking** independently predicted failure: OR=0.01, p=0.013
- **Early complications** (mucosal dehiscence): 4.8% (PLIP) vs 21.1% (FLIP)
- No implant fractures; no biomechanical complications requiring intervention

## Limitations

- Quasi-randomized design (last digit of record number) — allocation bias possible
- Sample size calculated for PPD, not disease resolution — underpowered for primary endpoint
- Only 1-year follow-up; longer-term needed to validate MBL benefit
- Limited implant surface heterogeneity (all rough bone-level)
- Conflict of interest: primary author's royalties and industry funding
- Incomplete blinding of radiographic examiner possible (surface changes from implantoplasty visible)
- Small n; barrier membrane use not standardized

## Glossary

- **FLIP (Full-Length Implantoplasty)**: Implantoplasty extending through both the supracrestal component and the intrabony compartment — smooths the entire exposed implant surface
- **PLIP (Partial-Length Implantoplasty)**: Implantoplasty limited to the supracrestal component only; the intrabony surface is decontaminated by other means (brushes, chemical agents)
- **Combined defect**: Peri-implantitis bone loss with both a supracrestal component (implant outside bony housing) and an intrabony component
- **Defect types IB/IIIB/IIIC**: Classification of peri-implantitis bone defects; IB = circumferential, IIIB/IIIC = combined with missing buccal plate
- **mSBI (Modified Sulcular Bleeding Index)**: 0–3 scale for bleeding severity/extensiveness on probing
- **Disease resolution**: Composite: BOP ≤1 site + no suppuration + PPD ≤5 mm + no progressive bone loss ≥1 mm
- **GEE (Generalized Estimating Equations)**: Statistical method accounting for within-patient correlation when multiple implants per patient
