## Three-line Summary

PRISMA 2020 SR+MA of RCTs (PROSPERO CRD420251034218; search to 29 March 2026; 10 RCTs 2011–2024, 3–60 months, 11–49 patients per group) comparing transmucosal abutment profiles recategorized as narrowed (concave/curved), straight, or widened; meta-analyses only for narrowed vs straight.

Full-text results: MBL 5 RCTs/282 implants SMD −0.25 (95% CI −0.55 to 0.06; p=0.11; I²=38%) — not the abstract's SMD 0.08, p=0.66; PD 2 RCTs/112 implants SMD −0.05 (−0.42 to 0.32; p=0.80; I²=0%); PES 2 RCTs/119 implants SMD −0.04 (−0.33 to 0.26; p=0.80; I²=0%) — not the abstract's −0.12, p=0.51; secondary outcomes (MMZ, KMW, MT) narrative only; concave arms carried the few failures/fractures (single events).

The "no difference" conclusion rests on small, imprecise, partly overlapping data with internal inconsistencies (abstract ≠ results, category definitions, citations, GRADE) — it shows absence of evidence, not evidence of equivalence; no direct convex-vs-concave pooling was done.

## 세줄요약

RCT 한정 SR+MA (PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO 등록, 2026.3.29까지 검색; RCT 10편 2011–2024, 3–60개월, 군당 11–49명): 경점막 지대주 형태를 좁은(오목·곡선)·직선·넓은 군으로 재분류, 메타분석은 좁은 vs 직선만.

전문 결과: 변연골 소실 (MBL) 5편·282개 SMD −0.25 (95% CI −0.55~0.06, p=0.11, I²=38%) — 초록의 SMD 0.08·p=0.66과 다름; 탐침깊이 (PD) 2편·112개 SMD −0.05 (p=0.80, I²=0%); 핑크 심미 지수 (PES) 2편·119개 SMD −0.04 (p=0.80) — 초록의 −0.12·p=0.51과 다름; 정중 협측 점막 정점 (MMZ)·각화점막 폭 (KMW)·점막 두께는 서술만; 실패·파절은 오목군에 단발 사건.

"차이 없음" 결론은 소규모·부정확·일부 중복 가능 자료와 내부 불일치(초록≠본문, 범주 정의, 인용, GRADE) 위에 있음 — 동등성의 근거가 아니라 근거 부족; 볼록 vs 오목 직접 합성은 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- RCT-only synthesis of abutment transmucosal profile on MBL, PD, PES.
- Narrative tables of biological (Table 4), technical (Table 5), and esthetic/PROM (Table 6) outcomes for all 10 RCTs.
- Histology signal from one included RCT (Camacho-Alonso 2025) favoring concave over cylindrical abutments.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PICO: adults with dental implants; different abutment geometries (linear divergent, concave, cylindrical, wide, curved, convex) vs conventional designs.
- Databases to 29 Mar 2026: MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, ReBEC, Trials, Virtual Health Library, ProQuest; reference lists.
- Inclusion: RCTs, any language, adults, **single implants**, ≥1 of MBL/PD/PES.
- Screening: two reviewers (κ 0.83 title/abstract; κ 0.88 full text); third reviewer for disputes.
- Flow: 10,556 records (abstract says 10,566) → 5,270 after duplicates → 18 full texts → 8 excluded (4 full text unavailable; 4 compared connection types) → 10 RCTs.
- Categorization (Table 1, text): narrowed = tapered with concavity (incl. curved); straight = straight or slightly outward-bulging (text: "including linear and cylindrical"; abstract: "linear, convex, cylindrical"); widened = increased cervical diameter.
- Rule: when several reports share a population, only the most recent/longest is pooled.
- Statistics: RevMan 5.3, random effects; SMD with 95% CI (methods also say "mean difference"); I²; RoB 2; GRADE (GRADEpro).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Meta-analyses (narrowed vs straight; full-text Results section)

| Outcome | Studies / implants | SMD (95% CI) | p | I² | GRADE (text) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MBL | 5 / 282 (143 vs 139) | −0.25 (−0.55 to 0.06) | 0.11 | 38% | moderate |
| PD | 2 / 112 (58 vs 54) | −0.05 (−0.42 to 0.32) | 0.80 | 0% | low |
| PES | 2 / 119 (59 vs 60) | −0.04 (−0.33 to 0.26) | 0.80 | 0% | moderate |

Abstract reports MBL SMD 0.08 (p=0.66) and PES SMD −0.12 (p=0.51) — these do not match the Results text.

### Narrative findings (Tables 4–6)

- Bernabeu-Mira 2023: wide > cylindrical MBL and marginal bone remodeling (p<0.001).
- Camacho-Alonso 2025: concave > cylindrical soft-tissue height and barrier epithelium extension (p<0.001), more transverse/perpendicular collagen, less inflammation and vascular proliferation (p<0.001).
- Koutouzis 2019/2023 (convex vs concave): PI, BoP, PD, MBL no difference; 2023 soft-tissue profile differed at several time points (p≤0.02) and mesial interproximal mucosa (p=0.04).
- Barwacz 2025: MMZ no between-group difference (p=0.853), increased over 1 year in both (p<0.001); buccal thickness p=0.674; KMW p=0.106.
- Esposito 2018, Patil 2014/2017: PES no difference (p=0.443; p=0.41; NS).
- Complications: 1 implant failure in the concave group in each Koutouzis trial; 1 concave abutment-neck fracture (Barwacz); implant failure 2.6% and concave abutment fracture 0.8% (Pérez-Sayans).

### Risk of bias

- High: Barwacz 2025 and Koutouzis 2023 (Results/Figure 2 citations 6, 38); Discussion instead names refs 6 and 35 (Patil 2011).
- Some concerns: Esposito 2018, Camacho-Alonso 2025, Patil 2011, Patil 2014, Pérez-Sayans 2022.
- Low: Patil 2017, Bernabeu-Mira 2023, Koutouzis 2019.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

Authors' limitations: few small RCTs, low power, heterogeneity in design/implant/abutment/loading/follow-up, baseline mucosal height and phenotype rarely assessed, bias in blinding and selective reporting.

Re-appraisal (this wiki, 2026-11-21, from the full text):

- **Abstract–results mismatch**: MBL (abstract SMD 0.08, p=0.66 vs results SMD −0.25, CI −0.55 to 0.06, p=0.11) and PES (abstract −0.12, p=0.51 vs results −0.04, p=0.80). Record count also differs (10,566 vs 10,556).
- **MBL direction unclear**: text says "a slight tendency favoring straight abutments"; whether negative SMD favors straight depends on the unshown sign convention (forest plot not legible in extracted text).
- **Category definition inconsistent**: abstract places convex in "straight"; methods text lists only linear and cylindrical; Table 1 says "straight or slightly outward-bulging". Which arm the convex-vs-concave trials (Koutouzis) entered — if any — is not stated; no convex-vs-concave meta-analysis exists.
- **Unclear study set for MBL**: the citation for the 5 MBL trials is "2,5,35–37", but ref 5 is Avila-Ortiz 2020 (a narrative paper). The GRADE text ("one of the five included RCTs at high risk") suggests the fifth is Barwacz 2025 (ref 6, high risk, linear divergent vs concave) — an inference. Patil 2011 (ref 35) reported marginal recession, not MBL, in Table 4, yet is cited for the MBL pooling.
- **Possible double counting**: the three Patil reports (2011, 2014, 2017) share a sponsor group, abutment comparison, ages (17–56) and similar n (26–29), and two are anterior mandible; Patil 2011 and 2014 appear together in the MBL citation despite the stated "one report per population" rule. Overlap is inferred, not confirmed.
- **PD/PES evidence = 2 trials**: cited as refs 2,3 in Results but 6,35 in Discussion.
- **Table 4 copy error**: Pérez-Sayans 2022 row repeats the Patil 2014 text verbatim (MBL p=0.25; PD p=0.85), while the Discussion says Pérez-Sayans reported significantly greater bone preservation with narrow abutments — the two statements conflict.
- **GRADE inconsistency**: PES rated "moderate" but described as a "two-level downgrade" (high minus two = low), and its two trials were judged some concerns + low risk; PD with one high-risk + one some-concerns trial was rated low — the high-risk trial among refs 2,3 does not match the RoB figure (both are some concerns/low).
- **Eligibility vs data**: inclusion required single implants, yet Esposito 2018 included fixed partial dentures.
- **Imprecision**: MBL CI spans −0.55 to +0.06 SMD; a null p-value with this CI does not establish equivalence.
- **Scope**: abutment profile only; crown emergence angle/profile and provisional contour (e.g., Siegenthaler 2022) are outside its question; Seijas Naya 2025 (concave vs straight, Galimplant) was not among the included trials.
- Technical complications in concave arms are single events in small trials — a signal to watch, not a rate.

## 7. Glossary

- **Narrowed / straight / widened**: authors' recategorization of transmucosal abutment profile.
- **SMD**: standardized mean difference (unitless; not directly mm).
- **MMZ**: mid-facial mucosal zenith.
- **PM-AM / PM-aJE / aJE-AM**: soft-tissue height / barrier epithelium extension / supracrestal connective tissue (histology).
