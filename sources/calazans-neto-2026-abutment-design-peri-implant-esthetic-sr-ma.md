## Three-line Summary

PRISMA 2020 SR+MA restricted to RCTs (10 RCTs, 3–60 months; 6 pooled) comparing narrowed (concave/curved), straight (linear/convex/cylindrical), and widened abutment profiles; RoB 2.0 (high risk 2, some concerns 5, low 3) and GRADE (overall moderate).

Narrowed vs straight showed no significant difference in marginal bone loss (SMD 0.08, p=0.66), probing depth (SMD −0.05, p=0.80), or Pink Esthetic Score (SMD −0.12, p=0.51); no differences in biological or esthetic secondary outcomes (including mid-facial mucosal zenith); technical complications were rare, mostly in concave abutments (early failures, occasional fractures).

Within current RCTs, abutment shape does not appear to significantly change peri-implant tissue or esthetic outcomes — the "straight" category lumps convex with linear, which may dilute shape effects.

## 세줄요약

RCT 한정 SR+MA (PRISMA 2020; RCT 10편, 3–60개월, 6편 정량 합성): 좁은(오목·곡선) vs 직선(선형·볼록·원통) vs 넓은 지대주 프로파일 비교; RoB 2.0 (고위험 2·일부 우려 5·저위험 3), GRADE 전체 중등도.

좁은 vs 직선: 변연골 소실 (MBL) SMD 0.08 (p=0.66), 탐침깊이 (PD) SMD −0.05 (p=0.80), 핑크 심미 지수 (PES) SMD −0.12 (p=0.51) 모두 차이 없음; 정중 협측 점막 정점 (Mid-facial Mucosal Zenith) 등 2차 결과도 차이 없음; 기술적 합병증은 드물며 주로 오목 지대주 (초기 실패·간헐적 파절).

현 RCT 근거로는 지대주 형태가 조직·심미 결과를 유의하게 바꾸지 않음 — 단 "직선" 범주에 볼록을 합쳐 형태 효과가 희석됐을 가능성.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: MEDLINE/PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, ReBEC, Trials, Virtual Health Library, ProQuest (incl. grey literature).
- 10,566 records → 5,270 after duplicates → 10 RCTs included; 6 in meta-analysis.
- Primary outcomes: MBL, PD, PES. Secondary: abutment survival, biological/technical complications, mid-facial mucosal zenith (MMZ), keratinized mucosa width, mucosal thickness, mucosa position, soft-tissue profile.
- RoB 2.0; GRADE.

- Secondary biological and esthetic outcomes: no differences.
- Technical complications rare; mostly concave abutments.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only: study list, heterogeneity, and pooled n not retrieved.
- Only 6 RCTs pooled; SMD units obscure clinical magnitude.
- "Straight" pools convex with linear and cylindrical profiles — a convex-vs-concave contrast (as in Siegenthaler 2022) is not isolated.
- Outcomes are mostly abutment-level shape; crown emergence profile and emergence angle are not the comparison axis.
