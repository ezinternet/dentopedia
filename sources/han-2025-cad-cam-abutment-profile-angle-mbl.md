## Why Ingested

Requested as the Han et al. JPD abutment paper: it supplies the missing depth-resolved angle data that [[wiki/implants/mbl/atieh-2023-emergence-angle-contour-marginal-bone-loss-sr-ma]] could not give (its overall angle effect on MBL was non-significant), showing that the angle–bone-loss relationship exists only within 0–2 mm of the implant-abutment junction. It also refines the generic >30° framing used by [[wiki/implants/soft-tissue/prpic-2026-emergence-angle-profile-implant-restorations-scoping]] with mesial/distal- and depth-specific cut-offs (34°/28° and 33°/20°).

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective clinical study (Yonsei University; 206 patients / 260 posterior single-tooth implant restorations, internal conical connection with platform switching) measuring CAD-CAM abutment profile angle at 0–1, 1–2, and 2–3 mm below the implant-abutment junction against marginal bone loss (MBL) from delivery to ≥7 years.

Convex emergence profile had greater MBL than concave/straight at every follow-up (P<.001); profile angle correlated with MBL only in the 0–1 and 1–2 mm ranges (not 2–3 mm, P>.05), with cut-offs 34° mesial / 28° distal (0–1 mm) and 33° / 20° (1–2 mm).

The angle threshold is depth- and side-dependent near the implant-abutment junction, refining the generic >30° rule — but this is an abstract-only record, so effect sizes and adjusted estimates cannot be checked.

## 세줄요약

후향적 임상연구(연세대; 환자 206명·후방부 단일치 임플란트 보철 260개, 내부 원추형 연결 + 플랫폼 스위칭): 임플란트-지대주 접합부에서 0–1·1–2·2–3 mm 하방의 CAD-CAM 지대주 프로파일 각도를 장착 시부터 7년 이상의 변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)과 분석.

볼록 출현윤곽(Convex Emergence Profile)이 오목·직선형보다 모든 추적 시점에서 MBL이 컸고(P<.001), 각도와 MBL의 상관은 0–1·1–2 mm 구간에서만 유의(2–3 mm는 P>.05); 역치는 0–1 mm에서 근심 34°·원심 28°, 1–2 mm에서 33°·20°.

접합부 인접 각도 역치는 깊이와 근원심에 따라 달라 일반화된 30° 기준을 정교화하나, 초록 전용 기록이라 효과크기·보정 추정값은 확인할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Measures CAD-CAM abutment profile angle at three distinct subgingival depths (0–1, 1–2, 2–3 mm from the implant-abutment junction) instead of a single emergence angle.
- Convex emergence profile → significantly greater MBL at all follow-up visits vs concave/straight (P<.001).
- Angle–MBL correlation is significant only close to the junction (0–1 and 1–2 mm), not at 2–3 mm (P>.05).
- Reports c-index-derived cut-offs that differ by mesial vs distal side and by depth: 34° / 28° (0–1 mm), 33° / 20° (1–2 mm).
- Population restricted to platform-switched, internal conical-seal implants — a design-specific answer rather than a generic angle rule.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective clinical study (PubMed MeSH: Retrospective Studies); 206 patients, 260 posterior single-tooth implant-supported restorations.
- **Implants**: Internal conical seal connection, platform-switched design; CAD-CAM abutments.
- **Imaging**: Digital periapical radiographs; emergence profile + profile angle at 0–1, 1–2, 2–3 mm from implant-abutment junction; MBL from restoration delivery to ≥7 years.
- **Statistics**: Linear mixed model for profile × time on MBL with Bonferroni post hoc; Pearson correlation at each distance range; c-index cut-offs followed by independent t tests (α=.05).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Result as reported in the PubMed abstract |
|---|---|
| Convex vs concave/straight EP | Greater MBL at each follow-up visit (P<.001) |
| Angle ↔ MBL, 0–1 mm | Significant correlation; cut-off 34° mesial / 28° distal |
| Angle ↔ MBL, 1–2 mm | Significant correlation; cut-off 33° mesial / 20° distal |
| Angle ↔ MBL, 2–3 mm | No significant correlation (P>.05) |
| Follow-up | From delivery to 7 years or more |
| n | 206 patients / 260 restorations |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only — no full text, so MBL effect magnitudes, confidence intervals, and adjusted estimates cannot be verified.
- Retrospective, single design family (platform-switched internal conical seal); generalisation to other connections/platform designs is untested.
- Cut-offs derived by c-index within this sample; external validation not reported in the abstract.
- Retraction check: PubMed lists no "Retracted Publication" article type and no correction/comment records (checked 2026-11-25).

## 7. Glossary

- **CAD-CAM**: computer-aided design and computer-aided manufacturing of the abutment.
- **Profile angle**: abutment contour angle measured at defined distances below the implant-abutment junction (here 0–1, 1–2, 2–3 mm).
- **Emergence profile (EP)**: shape of the restoration as it emerges through soft tissue (convex, concave, straight).
- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)**: peri-implant crestal bone loss measured on radiographs.
- **c-index**: cut-off optimisation statistic used here to derive angle thresholds.
