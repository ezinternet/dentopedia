## Why Ingested
근관치료 성공률 서베일런스. 2D 방사선 기반 기존 SR들과 달리 CBCT로 평가한 NSRCT 성공률을 최초로 체계적으로 종합한 SR+MA로, CBCT strict criteria 성공률이 2D 방사선 기반 수치(~79%)보다 현저히 낮다는 중요한 판독 격차를 정량화한다. 기존 위키 [[endodontics/irrigation/artaza-2024-outcome-nonsurgical-root-canal-treatment]] 대비 CBCT 특이 데이터 제공.

## One-line Summary
SR+MA (19 studies, 1,365 teeth) of CBCT-assessed NSRCT outcomes: 41.03% strict success (complete lesion resolution) vs 85.01% loose success (lesion reduction), underscoring the large gap between 2D-radiograph and CBCT-based outcome reporting.

## 한줄요약
SR+MA (19개 연구, 1,365개 치아): CBCT 기반 NSRCT strict 성공률 41.03% vs loose 기준 85.01% — CBCT는 2D 방사선보다 완전 치유를 더 엄격하게 판정하며 임상적 기준 선택이 성공률 해석에 결정적임.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA to specifically pool CBCT-assessed NSRCT and NS retreatment outcomes
- Quantifies the strict vs. loose outcome criteria gap: 41% vs 85% success for NSRCT
- Confirms CBCT identifies far more residual lesions than 2D radiography (known to show 79% favorable outcomes)
- Moderate overall quality of evidence (GRADE)
- Prognostic factors identified: preoperative lesion presence, lesion size, obturation quality, coronal seal

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA-P protocol)
- Population: Permanent teeth with NSRCT or NS retreatment, CBCT-assessed outcomes
- Outcome definitions:
  - Strict: complete resolution of periapical radiolucency (CBCT)
  - Loose: reduction in size of existing periapical lesion
- Databases: EMBASE, Web of Science, PubMed/MEDLINE, Cochrane CENTRAL + grey literature
- Studies: 19 articles (4 RCTs, 1 non-RCT, 7 ambispective cohorts, 7 prospective cohorts)
- Sample sizes: 26–176 teeth; median recall rate 80%
- Risk of bias: GRADE approach; 2 high quality (RCTs), 10 moderate, 7 low

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**NSRCT outcomes (CBCT-based):**
- Strict criteria (complete resolution): **41.03%** (95% CI: 28.68%–53.95%; I²=94.76%)
- Loose criteria (lesion reduction): **85.01%** (95% CI: 80.85%–88.75%; I²=68.36%)

**NS Retreatment outcomes (CBCT-based):**
- Reported separately; strict criteria generally lower than NSRCT

**Prognostic factors (from meta-regression):**
- Absence of preoperative periapical lesion: favorable
- Smaller lesion size: favorable
- Adequate obturation and coronal seal: favorable
- Specialist operator: favorable

**Quality of evidence:** Moderate overall (GRADE); heterogeneity high for strict criteria (I²=94.76%).

**Historical comparison:** 2D periapical radiographs report ~79% favorable outcomes; CBCT reveals only ~35–41% complete resolution (Vandenberghe et al. reference).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective and heterogeneous study designs dominate — limits causal inference
- Considerable heterogeneity in strict criteria outcomes (I²=94.76%)
- No start date restriction may include older studies with non-contemporary techniques
- Future studies need standardized CBCT scoring systems and longer follow-up (>4 years)
- Validated frameworks (PAI-based CBCT criteria) needed for quantitative synthesis

## 7. Glossary
- NSRCT: Nonsurgical root canal treatment (primary treatment)
- NS Retreatment: Nonsurgical endodontic retreatment (orthograde approach)
- Strict criteria: Complete resolution of periapical radiolucency (Rud/Molven classification)
- Loose criteria: Reduction in size of existing periapical radiolucency
- PAI: Periapical Index — radiographic scoring system for periapical status
- CBCT: Cone-beam computed tomography
