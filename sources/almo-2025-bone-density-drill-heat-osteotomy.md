## Why Ingested

This paper adds the critical missing variable — bone density — to the single-vs-sequential drill heat literature. Prior [[wiki/implants/osteotomy-thermal/rugova-2024-thermal-evaluation-bone-drilling-sequential|rugova-2024-thermal-evaluation-bone-drilling-sequential]] tested sequential drilling under no irrigation; this study tests both protocols across D1–D4 with irrigation, revealing a crossover interaction: single drills are cooler in dense bone but hotter in soft bone. The interaction effect (F = 69.03, p < 0.001) is the first quantitative evidence that bone density moderates drill-type thermal response.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study (240 osteotomies in polyurethane D1–D4 blocks, two-way ANOVA) comparing heat generation of a single 3.5 mm drill versus conventional sequential drilling (2.2→2.8→3.5 mm) at 800 rpm with water irrigation.

Single drills produced significantly lower temperatures than conventional drills in D1 (25.2 vs 26.3 °C, p < 0.001, d = −1.69) and D2 (22.9 vs 24.2 °C, p < 0.001, d = −2.09), but significantly higher temperatures in D3 (24.7 vs 23.5 °C, p < 0.001, d = 1.93) and D4 (24.5 vs 23.4 °C, p < 0.001, d = 2.09); overall drill-type main effect was non-significant (p = 0.776) while the density×drill interaction was highly significant (F = 69.03, p < 0.001, η² = 0.25).

Clinically, the single-drill protocol may be preferable in dense mandibular bone (D1–D2) for shorter surgical time and lower heat, but conventional sequential drilling is advisable in softer maxillary bone (D3–D4) where debris clogging with a single drill elevates frictional heating.

## 세줄요약

인비트로 연구 (240개 골절개, 폴리우레탄 D1~D4 블록, 2-way ANOVA) — 단일 드릴(3.5 mm) vs 순차 드릴(2.2→2.8→3.5 mm)의 열 생성 비교. 800 rpm, 수냉 조건.

단일 드릴이 치밀골 D1(25.2 vs 26.3 °C, p < 0.001, d = −1.69)과 D2(22.9 vs 24.2 °C, p < 0.001, d = −2.09)에서 유의하게 낮은 온도 생성; 반면 저밀도골 D3(24.7 vs 23.5 °C, p < 0.001, d = 1.93)와 D4(24.5 vs 23.4 °C, p < 0.001, d = 2.09)에서는 유의하게 높은 온도. 드릴 유형 단독 효과는 불显著 (p = 0.776)이나 골밀도×드릴 상호작용은 유의 (F = 69.03, p < 0.001, η² = 0.25).

임상 시사: 치밀 하악골(D1~D2)에서는 단일 드릴이 수술 시간 단축 및 발열 감소에 유리; 저밀도 상악골(D3~D4)에서는 단일 드릴의 골屑 쓸림(clogging)으로 마찰 열 증가 → 순차 드릴이 열 보존에 유리.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrates a significant density×drill-type interaction (F = 69.03, p < 0.001) on heat generation — bone density moderates whether single or sequential drilling produces less heat
- Provides quantitative evidence that single-drill protocols are superior in dense bone (D1–D2) but inferior in soft bone (D3–D4), with large effect sizes (Cohen's d > |1.6|) at all density levels
- Challenges the blanket claim that single drilling is universally equivalent to or better than sequential drilling by showing the thermal crossover depends on bone quality

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro experimental (2×4 factorial: drill type × bone density)
- **Specimens**: 240 osteotomies in polyurethane (SAWBONES) blocks — D1 (40 PCF), D2 (30 PCF), D3 (20 PCF), D4 (10 PCF)
- **Drill protocols**: Single drill (3.5 mm, Blue Sky Bio) vs conventional sequential (2.2→2.8→3.5 mm, Straumann cassette); 800 rpm, 2 kg load, 50 mL/min water irrigation
- **Temperature measurement**: K-type thermocouple (HT617B, HABOTEST), 1 mm from osteotomy wall, immediate post-drilling reading
- **Statistical analysis**: Two-way ANOVA (Shapiro–Wilk normality, Levene homogeneity), Tukey's HSD post-hoc, partial η², Cohen's d

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Post-drilling thermocouple readings may miss instantaneous peak temperatures that dissipated before measurement
- Polyurethane blocks lack vascularisation and differ in thermal conductivity from living bone — in vivo peaks may be higher or lower
- Artificial bone does not replicate biological variability (systemic diseases, cortical vs cancellous heterogeneity)
- Only one drill speed (800 rpm) and one irrigation protocol tested — generalisability to other parameters unknown
- Future work should include in vivo and clinical validation, different drill geometries, and real-time thermography

## 7. Glossary
- **Polyurethane bone analogue**: SAWBONES synthetic foam blocks calibrated to mimic D1–D4 human bone density; standardised substitute for in vitro osteotomy studies
- **K-type thermocouple**: Wide-range temperature sensor (−200 to +1372 °C) used for point-measurement of bone temperature at the osteotomy wall
- **Partial eta-squared (η²)**: Proportion of variance explained by a factor in ANOVA; η² > 0.14 is considered large
- **Cohen's d**: Standardised effect size; |d| > 0.8 is large
- **Osteonecrosis threshold**: 47 °C sustained for ≥1 minute causes irreversible bone cell death (Eriksson & Albrektsson, 1984)
