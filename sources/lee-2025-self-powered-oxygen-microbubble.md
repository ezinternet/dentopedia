## Why Ingested

Conventional decontaminants (0.2% chlorhexidine, 3% H2O2) fail to eradicate anaerobic biofilm from implant microgaps — the same gap-access limitation documented in [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/baima-2022-surface-decontamination-protocols-surgical-periimplantitis]]. This paper proposes MnO2-doped diatom microbubblers as a novel catalase-mimetic solution that can physically penetrate the microgap, extending the decontamination problem-space addressed by [[wiki/implants/peri-implantitis/pujarern-2024-biofilm-removal-implant-airflow-erythritol]].

## Three-line Summary

(Line 1: In vitro/ex vivo study fabricating self-powered microbubblers (SM) — MnO2-nanosheet-doped diatom particles — to decontaminate anaerobic biofilm within the fixture-abutment interface (FAI) microgap of dental implants.)
(Line 2: SM + H2O2 eliminated Porphyromonas gingivalis biofilm from microgap-mimicking gelatin hydrogels and patient-derived peri-implantitis implants, outperforming 0.2% chlorhexidine and 3% H2O2 alone; mechanical stability at abutment-fixture connection was restored and bacterial leakage was reduced.)
(Line 3: A promising nanotechnology proof-of-concept with no in vivo safety or clinical efficacy data; H2O2 cytotoxicity at effective concentrations and scale-up feasibility remain unresolved.)

## 세줄요약

(줄1: MnO2 나노시트를 규조토(diatom)에 도핑한 자가추진 마이크로버블러(SM)로 임플란트 임시기둥-고정체 접합부(fixture-abutment interface, FAI) 마이크로갭 내 혐기성 바이오필름을 제거하는 in vitro/ex vivo 연구.)
(줄2: SM + H2O2 조합은 젤라틴 하이드로겔 모델과 환자 유래 치주염 임플란트 모두에서 0.2% 클로르헥시딘·3% H2O2 단독보다 우수한 바이오필름 제거 효과, 기계적 안정성 회복, 세균 누출 감소 확인.)
(줄3: 임상 RCT·생체 내 안전성 데이터 전무; 유효 농도에서의 세포독성 및 대량 생산 가능성은 미해결 과제.)

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: ACS Biomaterials Science & Engineering 2025;11(5):3019–3030
- **DOI**: 10.1021/acsbiomaterials.5c00303
- **Institution**: Seoul National University, Seoul National University Dental Hospital, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Gachon University (Korea/USA)

## 2. Key Contributions

- First demonstration of self-powered microbubblers (SM) — cylindrical porous diatom particles doped with MnO2 nanosheets via APTES/KMnO4 reduction — capable of penetrating anaerobic microgap biofilm through catalase-mimetic O2 bubble generation in H2O2
- Dual decontamination mechanism: mechanical disruption (bubble-driven propulsion of rigid diatom shells) + oxygenation (anaerobic-niche conversion), not achievable by free MnO2 alone
- Ex vivo validation using patient-derived peri-implantitis implant fixtures (IRB136-10-19, Seoul National University Dental Hospital): SM + H2O2 outperformed 0.2% CHX and 3% H2O2 on multispecies biofilm decontamination
- Restoration of abutment-fixture mechanical stability and reduction of bacterial leakage from microgap following SM treatment

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: In vitro + ex vivo experimental study (no RCT, no human clinical endpoint)
- **Models**:
  - *In vitro*: MnO2-doped diatom synthesis (APTES surface amine modification → KMnO4 reduction), physicochemical characterization (SEM, TEM, XPS, BET, zeta potential), biofilm disruption in gelatin hydrogel microgap models, P. gingivalis biofilm assay
  - *Ex vivo*: Patient-derived implant fixtures explanted for peri-implantitis (>6 mm pocket depth or periapical radiolucency or severe mobility); internal/external microbiome sampling via 16S rRNA V3-V4 sequencing (Illumina MiSeq, DADA2/QIIME2/SILVA pipeline), SM treatment comparison against antiseptics
- **n**: Multiple patient-derived implant fixtures (exact n not specified in text extract); in vitro experiments replicated across triplicate conditions
- **Outcomes**: Biofilm disruption (confocal microscopy, CFU), bacterial leakage from microgap, abutment-fixture torque (mechanical stability), 16S rRNA microbiome composition (ANCOM, Shannon diversity)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- SM (H2O2 activated) penetrated and disrupted gelatin hydrogel biofilm models mimicking microgap dimensions; free MnO2 without diatom scaffold failed to achieve equivalent penetration
- SM + H2O2 decontaminated P. gingivalis biofilm in FAI microgap more effectively than 0.2% CHX and 3% H2O2 in both in vitro and ex vivo patient-implant settings
- Biofilm prevalence context: peri-implantitis affects 4.7–45% of patients; dental implants carry the highest biofilm infection rate among biomedical devices (5.9–56% vs prosthetic joints 1–15%, urinary catheters 9.1–26.6%, cardiac devices 0.13–38.6%)
- Multispecies biofilm from patient-derived explants similarly decontaminated; 16S rRNA sequencing confirmed compositional shift
- Restored mechanical stability at the abutment-fixture connection after SM-based microgap decontamination; bacterial leakage reduced

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro/ex vivo only — no animal study, no clinical RCT, no in vivo biocompatibility data
- H2O2 at biofilm-effective concentrations has known cytotoxicity; tissue safety at clinical delivery dose not established
- Exact SM concentration and H2O2 dose required for clinical use not optimized
- Long-term durability of decontamination effect and risk of recolonization not evaluated
- Industrial-scale synthesis of MnO2-diatom SM (APTES/KMnO4 reduction) cost and regulatory path not addressed

## 6. Related Work

- Baima 2022: surface decontamination protocols for surgical peri-implantitis — conventional antiseptic benchmarks this paper supersedes at microgap level
- Pujarern 2024: biofilm removal from implant using airflow/erythritol — complementary non-surgical decontamination approach in accessible surfaces
- Monje 2025: surface decontamination in reconstructive peri-implantitis surgery — surgical context where SM may eventually be adjunctive

## 7. Glossary

- **SM (Self-powered Microbubbler)**: MnO2-nanosheet-doped diatom particle that generates O2 bubbles via catalase-mimetic H2O2 decomposition, propelling itself through biofilm
- **Microgap (마이크로갭)**: Confined space at the fixture-abutment interface of two-piece bone-level internal implants; anaerobic niche inaccessible to conventional antiseptics/mechanical debridement
- **FAI (Fixture-Abutment Interface, 임시기둥-고정체 접합부)**: The internal joint between the implant body and the prosthetic abutment where microgaps form
- **MnO2 nanozyme**: Manganese dioxide nanosheet with catalase-mimetic enzymatic activity; catalyzes H2O2 → O2 + H2O without being consumed
- **Diatom (규조토)**: Silica-based microalgae shell used as a cylindrical porous scaffold for MnO2 doping; structural rigidity enables mechanical abrasion during bubble-driven propulsion
- **EPS (Extracellular Polymeric Substance, 세포외 고분자 물질)**: Protective biofilm matrix that confers resistance to conventional antiseptics and host immune responses
- **P. gingivalis (Porphyromonas gingivalis)**: Key anaerobic gram-negative pathogen implicated in peri-implantitis and inflammatory bone loss
- **16S rRNA sequencing**: Culture-independent molecular method for microbial community profiling; V3-V4 regions amplified; analyzed via DADA2/QIIME2
