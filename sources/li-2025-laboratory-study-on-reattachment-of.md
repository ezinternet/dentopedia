## Why Ingested

[[resin-bonding/corbella-2025-surgical-techniques-vertical-root-fractures-sr]]의 수직치근파절(VRF) 파절편 접착 근거는 전부 저품질 증례군(case series)이어서 4-META/MMA-TBB 레진 자체의 생물학적 적합성·기계적 성능·밀봉 데이터가 비어 있었다. 이 in vitro 연구가 그 재료-수준 공백(세포독성, 파절저항, 미세누출)을 벤치 데이터로 메우며, [[cracked-tooth/salihu-2024-review-of-approaches-to-the]]가 "유망"이라고 서술한 파절선 접착의 재료 근거를 보강한다.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro laboratory study comparing 4-META/MMA-TBB resin (Super-Bond C&B), iRoot BP Plus, Fuji IX glass ionomer cement (GIC) and Clearfil SA Luting for vertical root fracture (VRF) reattachment: L929 fibroblast biocompatibility (CCK-8, live/dead staining, flow cytometry), fracture resistance (n = 20/group, bovine incisor roots) and methylene-blue microleakage (n = 10/group).

4-META/MMA-TBB resin was as biocompatible as iRoot BP Plus on flow cytometry (P > 0.05), restored fracture resistance to 220.9 +/- 45.0 N (79.3% of sound roots, 278.5 N) and had the shortest dye penetration (0.63 mm vs Clearfil SA Luting 1.44 mm and Fuji IX GIC 2.08 mm); Fuji IX GIC was weakest (53.2 N), and Clearfil SA Luting was the most cytotoxic.

Bench-only evidence on bovine roots with a gutta-percha wedge load, no aging or periodontal healing: it supports material choice (resin over GIC) for fragment reattachment but cannot establish clinical success, and the bonding interface remained the weak point (mostly mixed Type-III failures).

## 세줄요약

in vitro 실험실 연구 — 수직치근파절 (Vertical Root Fracture, VRF) 재접착에 쓰이는 4-META/MMA-TBB 레진 (Super-Bond C&B)을 iRoot BP Plus, Fuji IX 글래스아이오노머 시멘트 (Glass Ionomer Cement, GIC), Clearfil SA Luting과 비교: L929 섬유모세포 생체적합성 (CCK-8, 생/사 염색, 유세포분석), 소 절치 치근 파절저항 (군당 n = 20), 메틸렌블루 미세누출 (군당 n = 10).

4-META/MMA-TBB 레진은 유세포분석에서 iRoot BP Plus와 생존세포 비율이 유사했고 (P > 0.05), 파절저항 220.9 +/- 45.0 N (건전 치근 278.5 N의 79.3%), 염료 침투 깊이 0.63 mm로 가장 짧았다 (Clearfil SA Luting 1.44 mm, Fuji IX GIC 2.08 mm); Fuji IX GIC는 파절저항 53.2 N으로 가장 약했고 Clearfil SA Luting은 세포독성이 가장 컸다.

소 치근, 거즈-가타퍼처 쐐기 하중, 노화·치주 치유 없는 벤치 근거라 재료 선택 (GIC보다 레진)의 근거는 되나 임상 성공을 입증하지 못하며, 접착 계면이 여전히 약한 지점이었다 (주로 혼합형 Type-III 파절).

## 2. Key Contributions
- First laboratory data on 4-META/MMA-TBB resin as a root-repair (VRF reattachment) material in three domains at once: cell biocompatibility, fracture resistance and microleakage, against a calcium silicate cement (iRoot BP Plus), a GIC and a self-adhesive resin cement.
- Positions the pure (filler-free) 4-META/MMA-TBB resin as comparable in biocompatibility to iRoot BP Plus, while the self-adhesive resin cement Clearfil SA Luting was clearly cytotoxic (RGR Grade 2-3; lowest viable-cell fraction).
- Shows reattached roots recover only part of sound-root strength (79.3% resin, 66.1% Clearfil SA Luting, ~19% GIC), with failures concentrated at the bonding interface.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro laboratory study; 4 materials (Super-Bond C&B = 4-META/MMA-TBB resin, iRoot BP Plus, Fuji IX GIC, Clearfil SA Luting).
- **Biocompatibility**: extracts per ISO 10993-12 (3 cm2/ml, 24 h) on mouse L929 fibroblasts; CCK-8 at 1/2/3/5 days (RGR graded 0-5), Calcein AM/PI live/dead staining at 1/2/3 days, Annexin V-FITC/PI flow cytometry at 2 days; 5 replicates per group.
- **Fracture/microleakage specimens**: extracted bovine incisors from 7-year-old cattle, 15 mm roots, canal enlarged to 3 mm; VRF created by 3 mm notch + vertical splitting through the canal; bonding surfaces cleaned ultrasonically; 24 h at 37 C. iRoot BP Plus excluded from mechanical tests "due to poor bonding".
- **Fracture test**: 4 groups (control = sound root filled with gutta-percha; 3 reattachment groups), n = 20 each; root wrapped in 0.2 mm silicone (PDL simulation), 1.5 mm conical steel pin on the gutta-percha, 1 mm/min vertical load; failure mode Type-I cohesive / Type-II adhesive / Type-III mixed.
- **Microleakage**: n = 10 per group, 2% methylene blue for 7 days, sections at 1, 6.5 and 13 mm, penetration measured at 32x.
- **Statistics**: Shapiro-Wilk, Brown-Forsythe, one-way ANOVA + Tukey, alpha = 0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Measure | Control (sound) | 4-META/MMA-TBB | Clearfil SA Luting | Fuji IX GIC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fracture resistance (N, mean +/- SD) | 278.50 +/- 45.19 (a) | 220.90 +/- 45.01 (b) | 184.00 +/- 45.45 (b) | 53.16 +/- 15.76 (c) |
| Fraction of sound-root strength | 100% | 79.3% | 66.1% | ~19% (derived) |
| Failure mode (Type-II / Type-III counts) | n/a | 2 / 18 | 1 / 19 | 0 / 20 |
| Dye penetration depth (mm) | n/a | 0.63 +/- 0.15 | 1.44 +/- 0.19 | 2.08 +/- 0.17 |

- CCK-8: 4-META/MMA-TBB resin, iRoot BP Plus and Fuji IX GIC stayed Grade 0-1 (RGR > 75%) throughout; Clearfil SA Luting was Grade 2 on day 1 and Grade 3 on days 2, 3 and 5. iRoot BP Plus RGR was higher on day 1 but fell and was not different from the resin or GIC by day 5.
- Flow cytometry (day 2): viable-cell proportion similar for iRoot BP Plus and 4-META/MMA-TBB resin (P > 0.05; both below untreated control, P < 0.05); lowest for Clearfil SA Luting; GIC intermediate.
- Resin vs resin fracture resistance difference not significant (P > 0.05); both > Fuji IX GIC (P < 0.05) and < control (P < 0.05). Control roots split mainly buccolingually; reattached roots mostly Type-III with more interfacial debonding in the two resin groups and more cohesive failure inside the GIC.
- 4-META/MMA-TBB resin had the shortest penetration (P < 0.05 vs both others); GIC the longest.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Bench-only, bovine incisor roots (7-year-old cattle) instead of human roots; no thermocycling, water-aging, cyclic loading or bacterial challenge.
- Load applied through a gutta-percha-wedged steel pin down the canal, which is a splitting-type load rather than masticatory load; fracture resistance after reattachment of an already-split root in a PDL-simulated socket is only a surrogate.
- Cytotoxicity tested on mouse L929 fibroblast extracts of set materials, not on periodontal ligament cells or in the periodontal environment; Clearfil SA Luting was light-cured per manufacturer, and its in-vivo exposure may differ.
- iRoot BP Plus was dropped from fracture and microleakage tests because of weak bonding (authors' premise, not tested here), so no head-to-head mechanical data for it.
- The 4-META/MMA-TBB vs Clearfil SA Luting fracture resistance difference was not significant; the claimed advantage in blood-contaminated dentin and in-socket polymerization is cited from other work, not measured here.
- Authors state in vivo periodontal healing after reattachment still needs study.

## 7. Glossary
- **4-META/MMA-TBB resin**: 4-methacryloxyethyl trimellitate anhydride / methyl methacrylate - tri-n-butyl borane; filler-free PMMA-powder/MMA-liquid adhesive resin (Super-Bond C&B), polymerization is initiated by TBB and tolerates moisture.
- **VRF reattachment**: bonding of the split root fragments with adhesive, intraorally via the canal/flap or extraorally followed by intentional replantation.
- **RGR (relative growth ratio)**: CCK-8 OD of the test extract divided by control OD x 100%; Grade 0 >= 100%, Grade 1 75-99%, Grade 2 50-74%, Grade 3 25-49%.
- **Type-I/II/III failure**: cohesive failure in the material / adhesive failure at the interface / mixed failure.
- **iRoot BP Plus**: premixed calcium silicate-based bioceramic repair cement.
