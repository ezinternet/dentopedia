## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA + PROSPERO CRD42024599304) of 7 systematic reviews (out of 243 screened) comparing single-implant (SIMO) vs two-implant (TIMO) mandibular overdentures on implant failure, peri-implant marginal bone loss (MBL), prosthetic complications/maintenance, patient satisfaction, and OHRQoL, appraised with AMSTAR 2.

One SR favoured SIMO over TIMO for both implant failure and MBL; another SR found TIMO had significantly more implant failures at 1 and 5 years, more prosthetic failures at 1 year, and more O-ring replacements at 5 years versus SIMO. AMSTAR 2 quality distribution across the 7 SRs: 2 critically low, 2 low, 3 moderate — no SR rated high quality.

SIMO performs comparably to conventional TIMO as a treatment for the edentulous mandible, but the underlying SR evidence base is of variable (mostly low-to-moderate) methodological quality, so the authors call for more robust patient-level evidence before refining clinical guidelines.

## 세줄요약

PRISMA 방법론을 따른 umbrella review(우산형 문헌고찰, PROSPERO CRD42024599304 등록) — 243편 스크리닝 중 선정된 7편의 체계적 문헌고찰(SR)을 대상으로 단일임플란트 하악 오버덴쳐(Single-Implant Mandibular Overdenture, SIMO)와 두 임플란트 하악 오버덴쳐(Two-Implant Mandibular Overdenture, TIMO)의 임플란트 실패·변연골소실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)·보철 합병증/유지관리·환자 만족도·구강건강 관련 삶의 질(Oral Health-Related Quality of Life, OHRQoL)을 AMSTAR 2로 품질 평가하며 비교.

한 SR은 임플란트 실패와 MBL 모두에서 SIMO가 TIMO보다 우수했다고 보고한 반면, 다른 SR은 TIMO가 1년·5년 시점 임플란트 실패가 유의하게 많고 1년 보철 실패, 5년 O-ring 교체도 유의하게 많다고 보고 — 상반된 결과. 포함된 7편 SR의 AMSTAR 2 품질은 critically low 2편, low 2편, moderate 3편으로 high 등급은 없음.

SIMO는 기존 표준인 TIMO와 대등한 하악 무치악 재활 치료법으로 자리매김하지만, 근거로 쓰인 SR들의 방법론적 질이 대체로 낮아 임상 가이드라인 확정 전 더 견고한 환자 수준 근거가 필요하다는 한계가 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review specifically appraising the SIMO-vs-TIMO SR evidence base with AMSTAR 2, giving an "evidence about the evidence" quality layer on top of the primary SR/MA literature.
- Synthesizes 7 SRs across five outcome domains simultaneously: implant failure, peri-implant MBL, prosthetic complications/maintenance, patient satisfaction, and OHRQoL — broader outcome scope than any single included SR.
- Documents that the 7 included SRs disagree directionally on implant failure and MBL (one favours SIMO, another favours TIMO at certain timepoints), highlighting inconsistency in the primary evidence rather than a uniform conclusion.
- Flags that no included SR achieved AMSTAR 2 "high" quality (distribution: 2 critically low, 2 low, 3 moderate), tempering confidence in any single directional claim.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review, PRISMA-reported, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42024599304).
- **Databases**: Web of Science, ProQuest, SCIELO, KCI, Grants Index, Scopus, PubMed/MEDLINE, Cochrane Library; no language restriction.
- **Screening**: 243 papers suitable for title/abstract screening → 7 SRs included.
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR 2 (A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews, version 2).
- **Outcomes**: implant failure, peri-implant MBL, overall prosthetic complications and maintenance, patient satisfaction, OHRQoL.
- **Population**: edentulous patients rehabilitated with SIMO vs TIMO.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

Abstract-level results only (body tables/AMSTAR 2 item scores not retrieved):

- One included SR: SIMO favoured over TIMO for both implant failure and peri-implant MBL.
- Another included SR: TIMO showed significantly more implant failures at 1 and 5 years, significantly more prosthetic failures at 1 year, and significantly more O-ring replacements at 5 years, compared to SIMO.
- AMSTAR 2 quality of the 7 included SRs: 2 critically low, 2 low, 3 moderate (none high).
- Overall conclusion: SIMO corresponds with conventional TIMO as a potentially effective treatment for edentulous mandible rehabilitation.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only ingest — this summary cannot report per-SR outcome tables, AMSTAR 2 item-by-item findings, or narrative-synthesis nuance beyond what the abstract states.
- Underlying evidence base is heterogeneous and mostly low/critically-low AMSTAR 2 quality, per the authors' own appraisal — an inherent limitation of the primary literature, not just this review.
- Authors explicitly call for additional studies to obtain more robust patient evidence and refine clinical guidelines.

## 7. Glossary

- **SIMO**: single-implant mandibular overdenture.
- **TIMO**: two-implant mandibular overdenture.
- **AMSTAR 2**: A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews, version 2 — critical appraisal tool for SR methodological quality.
- **MBL**: peri-implant marginal bone loss.
- **OHRQoL**: oral health-related quality of life.
- **Umbrella review**: a review that synthesizes multiple existing systematic reviews on a topic, rather than primary studies directly.
