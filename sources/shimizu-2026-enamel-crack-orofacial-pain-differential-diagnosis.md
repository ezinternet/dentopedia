## Three-line Summary

Prospective clinical study with 3-year follow-up investigating the relationship between enamel cracks and orofacial pain, focusing on differential diagnosis between crack-related pain and other orofacial pain conditions.

Enamel cracks were associated with specific orofacial pain patterns, and accurate differential diagnosis between crack-related pain and non-crack orofacial pain (TMD, neuropathic pain, dentinal hypersensitivity) required integration of multiple clinical criteria over the follow-up period.

The 3-year longitudinal data provide evidence for crack-specific orofacial pain progression patterns and support incorporation of enamel crack evaluation into standard orofacial pain assessment protocols.

## 세줄요약

전향적 임상 연구(3년 추적): 법랑질 균열과 구강안면통증(Orofacial Pain, OFP)의 관계를 조사하며, 균열 관련 통증과 비균열 구강안면통증(악관절장애·신경병증성 통증·상아질 과민증) 간 감별진단에 초점.

법랑질 균열은 특이적 구강안면통증 패턴과 연관되며, 3년 추적기간 동안 다중 임상기준의 통합적 적용이 정확한 감별진단에 필수.

3년 종단 데이터는 균열 특이적 구강안면통증 진행 패턴의 근거를 제공하며, 표준 구강안면통증 평가 프로토콜에 법랑질 균열 평가 포함을 지지.

## Why Ingested
법랑질 균열과 구강안면통증의 장기(3년) 감별진단 관계를 처음 전향적으로 추적한 연구. 기존 위키의 균열 진단 논문들([[cracked-tooth/raj-2025-cracked-tooth-syndrome-diagnostic-dilemma]], [[cracked-tooth/hernando-2026-diagnostic-methods-dental-cracks-systematic-review]])은 주로 crack detection accuracy에 초점을 두었으나, OFP 감별진단 맥락에서의 균열 역할은 다루지 않았다.

## One-line Summary
Prospective 3-year follow-up study: enamel cracks produce specific orofacial pain patterns requiring multimodal criteria for differential diagnosis from TMD and neuropathic pain.

## 2. Key Contributions
- 3-year prospective data on enamel crack-associated orofacial pain progression (longitudinal follow-up rare in this field)
- Establishes crack-specific orofacial pain patterns distinguishable from TMD and neuropathic pain
- Provides evidence for including enamel crack evaluation in standard orofacial pain workup
- Multimodal differential diagnosis criteria validated over 3-year observation period
- Addresses clinical overlap between CTS pain and non-dental orofacial pain — a common diagnostic challenge

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Design: Prospective clinical study with 3-year follow-up
- Population: Patients with orofacial pain and/or enamel cracks
- Comparisons: Crack-related orofacial pain vs TMD, neuropathic pain, dentinal hypersensitivity
- Outcome measures: Pain patterns, diagnostic criteria performance, clinical progression
- Follow-up: 3 years

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Enamel cracks: associated with specific/characteristic orofacial pain patterns
- Differential diagnosis: requires integration of multiple clinical criteria (not single-test diagnosis)
- 3-year longitudinal data: crack-related pain progression patterns documented
- Specific effect sizes and diagnostic accuracy metrics not extractable from abstract

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only: specific criteria, effect sizes, and cohort characteristics not available
- Single-center; generalizability to general practice needs confirmation
- Healthcare (MDPI) journal — review quality variable; MDPI open-access model
- 3 years may not capture all crack progression patterns (some studies show changes beyond 5 years)

## 7. Glossary
- Orofacial pain (OFP): pain in the mouth, face, and jaw — broader than dental pain; includes TMD, neuropathic pain, odontogenic pain
- Enamel crack: superficial crack confined to enamel (below craze line severity threshold — or early cracked tooth)
- Differential diagnosis: systematic process of distinguishing between conditions with overlapping symptoms
