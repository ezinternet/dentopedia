## Three-line Summary

Evidence-Based Dentistry commentary summarising the ten-year periodontal outcomes of the German multicentre randomised trial (14 centres, from October 2000) that allocated 215 adults aged ≥35 with bilateral missing molars to a removable partial denture (n = 109) or a shortened dental arch approach (n = 106).

By intention-to-treat, ten-year vertical clinical attachment loss differed significantly in favour of the SDA group in both the study jaw (0.79 mm) and across all teeth (0.69 mm), and the SDA group was also more favourable on bleeding on probing and plaque index.

The commentators conclude there is evidence of reduced periodontal impact from the SDA approach, **while explicitly judging the difference unlikely to be clinically significant** — a sub-millimetre attachment difference over a decade is real but small next to the cost, invasiveness and maintenance differences between the two strategies.

## 세줄요약

근거중심치의학 (Evidence-Based Dentistry) 논평: 독일 다기관 무작위 임상시험 (14개 기관, 2000년 10월 개시)의 10년 **치주** 결과를 요약한다. 35세 이상·양측 대구치 결손 성인 215명을 가철성 국소의치 (Removable Partial Denture, RPD, n = 109) 또는 단축치열 (Shortened Dental Arch, SDA, n = 106)로 무작위 배정했다.

배정치료분석 (Intention-To-Treat, ITT) 기준 10년 수직적 임상부착소실 (Clinical Attachment Loss, CAL)은 연구대상 악 (0.79 mm)과 전체 치아 (0.69 mm) 모두에서 **SDA 군이 유의하게 유리**했고, 탐침시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)과 치태지수 (Plaque Index)에서도 SDA가 우수했다.

논평자들은 SDA가 치주에 미치는 영향이 더 적다는 근거가 있다고 결론지으면서도 **"그 차이가 임상적으로 유의할 가능성은 낮다"** 고 명시한다 — 10년에 걸친 1 mm 미만의 부착 차이는 실재하지만, 두 전략 간 비용·침습도·유지관리 차이에 비하면 작다.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

Primary design (as summarised): multicentre RCT across 14 German dental schools and universities from October 2000. Eligibility: adults ≥35 years with bilateral missing molars, both canines and at least one premolar present on each side in one jaw. Central randomisation using randomly permuted blocks stratified by age. Allocation to posterior restoration with an RPD, or a fixed prosthesis following SDA principles (no replacement posterior to the second premolar). Data at baseline (after pre-prosthetic treatment), six months, annually to five years, then eight and ten years. Analysis: ITT and modified per-protocol; two-sided α = 0.05, 95% CI.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Secondary source.** This is a commentary on a trial, not the trial report. Any citation of these numbers should ideally be traced to the primary publication.
- Severe attrition down the analysis ladder: 109/106 randomised → 79/71 ITT-analysed → **25/22 per-protocol**. A per-protocol population under a quarter of the randomised sample invites selection effects, and the abstract does not report why.
- The commentators' own conclusion contains the counterweight: the difference is **unlikely to be clinically significant**. A 0.69–0.79 mm CAL difference over ten years should not be presented to a patient as a reason to choose SDA on its own.
- Plaque index and BOP differences are consistent with the appliance simply making hygiene harder — a mechanism that is modifiable by maintenance, not intrinsic to the strategy.
- Ingested **abstract-only**, so the commentators' critical appraisal — the distinctive content of an EBD piece — is unavailable.

## 7. Glossary

- **SDA (단축치열, Shortened Dental Arch)**: deliberately ending the dental arch at the premolars rather than restoring molar support.
- **CAL (임상부착소실, Clinical Attachment Loss)**: distance from cementoenamel junction to pocket base; the standard measure of periodontal destruction.
- **ITT (배정치료분석, Intention-To-Treat)**: analysis by allocated group regardless of treatment actually received; preserves randomisation.
- **Per-protocol (프로토콜 순응 분석)**: analysis restricted to participants who completed treatment as allocated; breaks randomisation and can introduce selection bias.
- **BOP (탐침시 출혈, Bleeding on Probing)** / **Plaque Index (치태지수)**: standard clinical measures of gingival inflammation and plaque accumulation.
- **EBD commentary (근거중심치의학 논평)**: structured critical summary of a primary study published in Evidence-Based Dentistry.
