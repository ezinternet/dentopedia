## Why Ingested

무기질 우골 (Anorganic Bovine Bone, ABB) 기질이 조골세포 증식에 미치는 영향을 시험관에서 검증한 초기 연구로, ABB에 혈소판 유래 성장인자-BB (Platelet-Derived Growth Factor-BB, PDGF-BB)와 인슐린 유사 성장인자-I (Insulin-like Growth Factor-I, IGF-I)를 흡착시켜 생물학적 활성을 유지할 수 있음을 입증한다. 동일 재료(ABB)의 세포 부착·증식 지지 특성을 확인한 [[bone-regeneration/stephan-1999-anorganic-bovine-bone-osteoblastic-attachment]]를 성장인자 변형 차원에서 확장하며, 골이식재-성장인자 복합체의 임상 적용 가능성에 대한 초기 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

In vitro study examined adsorption of PDGF-BB and IGF-I to anorganic bovine bone mineral (ABB) matrix and their effect on osteoblastic cell proliferation.
Both PDGF-BB and IGF-I adsorbed in a concentration-dependent manner; IGF-I affinity was 10-fold higher; PDGF-BB significantly enhanced osteoblast proliferation (p<0.05), while IGF-I showed no significant effect.
PDGF-BB can be adsorbed to ABB and retains biological activity, suggesting potential clinical application of growth factor-modified bone grafts.

## 세줄요약

무기질 우골(ABB) 기질에 PDGF-BB와 IGF-I의 흡착과 조골세포 증식 효과를 평가한 시험관 연구입니다.
PDGF-BB와 IGF-I 모두 농도 의존적으로 흡착되었고, IGF-I의 친화도는 10배 더 높았으며, PDGF-BB는 조골세포 증식을 유의하게 증가시킨 반면(p<0.05), IGF-I는 유의한 효과가 없었습니다.
PDGF-BB는 ABB에 흡착되어 생물학적 활성을 유지하므로, 성장인자-변형 골이식재의 임상 적용 가능성을 시사합니다.

## 1. Document Information

- **Journal**: Journal of Periodontology, 1999;70(8):834–839
- **DOI**: 10.1902/jop.1999.70.8.834
- **Institution**: State University of New York at Buffalo (SUNY Buffalo), USA
- **Study type**: In vitro experimental study
- **n**: Cell culture (neonatal rat osteoblasts); not patient-based

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates that both PDGF-BB and IGF-I adsorb to ABB matrix in a concentration-dependent manner
- IGF-I shows 10-fold higher affinity for ABB than PDGF-BB
- After 10 days: ~50% of PDGF-BB and ~10% of IGF-I released from ABB
- PDGF-BB adsorbed to ABB significantly enhances osteoblastic cell proliferation (p<0.05); IGF-I does not
- Establishes that growth factor biological activity is retained after adsorption to ABB — foundational evidence for growth factor-modified bone graft concept

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: In vitro experimental study
- **Materials**: Anorganic bovine bone (ABB) mineral matrix; ¹²⁵I radiolabeled PDGF-BB and IGF-I for adsorption/release measurements
- **Cell model**: Primary neonatal rat osteoblastic cells cultured on ABB loaded with growth factors
- **Proliferation assay**: ³H-thymidine incorporation
- **Morphology**: Scanning electron microscopy (SEM)
- **Key measurements**: Dose-response adsorption curves; release kinetics over 10 days; cell proliferation index

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- PDGF-BB and IGF-I both adsorb to ABB in concentration-dependent manner
- IGF-I affinity for ABB: ~10-fold higher than PDGF-BB (quantitative binding data)
- Release at Day 10: ~50% PDGF-BB released; ~10% IGF-I released (higher retention for IGF-I)
- PDGF-BB significantly enhanced osteoblast proliferation vs. control (p<0.05)
- IGF-I: no significant effect on cell proliferation despite higher adsorption affinity
- SEM confirmed osteoblastic cell attachment and spreading on ABB surface

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vitro only — no in vivo or clinical validation
- Rat (animal) cells, not human osteoblasts
- Short-term observation; no long-term release kinetics beyond 10 days
- No comparison with other carrier materials (e.g., collagen, HA, TCP)
- IGF-I's lack of proliferative effect despite higher binding not mechanistically explained
- Growth factor concentrations used may not reflect clinically achievable levels

## 6. Related Work

- Stephan 1999 (sibling paper): ABB supports osteoblastic cell attachment and proliferation — establishes baseline ABB biocompatibility that this paper builds upon with growth factor modification
- Chakar 2014: DBBM modified with platelet lysate (APL) in rabbit model — conceptually related approach of combining biological factors with xenogenic bone substitute

## 7. Glossary

- **PDGF-BB**: 혈소판 유래 성장인자-BB (Platelet-Derived Growth Factor-BB) — potent mitogen for mesenchymal cells/osteoblasts
- **IGF-I**: 인슐린 유사 성장인자-I (Insulin-like Growth Factor-I) — anabolic factor for bone metabolism
- **ABB**: 무기질 우골 (Anorganic Bovine Bone) — deproteinized, defatted bovine bone mineral (equivalent to DBBM/Bio-Oss)
- **Osteoblast**: 조골세포 — bone-forming cell
- **¹²⁵I radiolabeling**: radioiodine labeling technique to quantify protein binding and release
- **³H-thymidine incorporation**: radioactive thymidine uptake assay measuring DNA synthesis / cell proliferation
- **SEM**: Scanning Electron Microscopy (주사전자현미경)
