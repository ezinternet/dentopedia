## Why Ingested
Contemporary narrative review (2026) synthesizing post-2015 RCT and SR+MA evidence comparing immediate vs delayed loading across single-tooth, posterior partial, overdenture, and full-arch indications. Reinforces and contextualizes [[implants/loading-protocol/esposito-2013-loading-times-dental-implants-cochrane]] and [[implants/loading-protocol/gallucci-2018-implant-placement-loading-protocols-partially]] with newer trial data and a clinical decision framework.

## Three-line Summary
Narrative review prioritizing post-2015 randomized clinical trials and high-quality systematic reviews/meta-analyses comparing immediate loading (IL) vs delayed loading (DL) across single-tooth, posterior partial edentulism, mandibular overdenture, and full-arch maxillary rehabilitation indications.
Across indications, IL shows comparable implant survival to DL when primary stability is high, occlusal control is appropriate, and patient risk is low; mandibular overdenture RCTs show similar survival with less radiographic bone loss for IL at 1–5 years; single-tooth esthetic zone shows comparable outcomes at 5 years; short implants in posterior maxilla show higher bone loss with IL; full-arch fixed prostheses show comparable survival with some protocols reporting lower bone loss for IL.
IL is predictable in selected cases but more technique- and case-sensitive; DL remains preferable when primary stability is uncertain, parafunction exists, bone quality is poor, systemic/local risk is elevated, or occlusal control cannot be ensured.

IL은 적절히 선정된 증례에서 예측 가능하고 DL과 유사하나 기술·증례 의존도가 더 높음; 일차 고정 불확실·이갈이·골질 불량·전신/국소 위험 증가·교합 조절 불가 시 DL이 여전히 권장됨.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Indication | Key Finding |
|---|---|
| Single-tooth, esthetic zone (healed sites) | Gjelvold et al. 5-yr RCT: Survival 100% (IL) vs 95.8% (DL); MBL −0.50±0.73 vs −0.54±0.65 mm (NS); PES/WES comparable; smokers had more MBL |
| Single-tooth (general) | Comparable clinical and radiographic outcomes between IL and DL |
| Posterior mandible split-mouth | >10-yr follow-up: IL showed no significant disadvantage vs DL in crestal bone loss |
| Mandibular Locator-retained overdenture | IL demonstrated similar survival; some trials show less radiographic bone level change vs DL at 1–5 years |
| Short implants (posterior maxilla) | IL may be associated with higher bone loss and bleeding indices than DL, despite generally acceptable outcomes |
| Maxillary full-arch fixed prostheses | Long-term data suggest comparable survival; some reports indicate lower mean bone loss in IL groups under specific protocols |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Not a formal PRISMA-compliant systematic review — risk of selection bias and non-exhaustive literature coverage acknowledged
- Heterogeneity in loading protocol definitions and implant systems limits direct comparison
- Studies frequently fail to differentiate between functional and nonfunctional immediate loading, complicating interpretation
- Limited reporting of patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) across included studies
- Operator experience and patient selection criteria variability not fully accounted for

## 7. Glossary
- **즉시 부하 (Immediate Loading, IL)**: 임플란트 식립 후 48-72시간 이내에 임시 보철물을 장착하는 프로토콜 (기능적/비기능적 구분)
- **지연 부하 (Delayed Loading, DL)**: 임플란트 식립 후 3개월 이상 경과 후 보철물을 장착하는 기존 표준 프로토콜
- **기능적 즉시 부하 (Immediate Functional Loading, IFL)**: 중심 교합 및 측방 운동에서 교합 접촉이 있는 즉시 부하
- **비기능적 즉시 부하 (Immediate Nonfunctional Loading, INFL)**: 교합 접촉 없이 심미 영역 연조직 보존을 목적으로 하는 즉시 부하
- **변연골레벨 변화 (Marginal Bone Level change, MBL)**: 임플란트 주변 변연골의 방사선적 소실 정도
- **일차 고정 (Primary Stability)**: 식립 즉시 얻는 기계적 안정성 (보통 ≥30-35 Ncm 또는 ISQ ≥60-70 기준)
- **Locator 어태치먼트**: 하악 오버덴처에서 널리 사용되는 탄력성 유지장치
