## Three-line Summary

SR+MA (J Prosthet Dent 2026, 136:e79-e101; AAFP Committee; PubMed/Medline, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane; up to August 2025): 29 studies included — 3 reverse impression, 26 extraoral photogrammetry (PG); JBI critical appraisal tool for quality assessment.

Meta-analysis of extraoral PG: mean linear discrepancy 27 µm, angular discrepancy 0.21°, linear trueness 69 µm, linear precision 43 µm, angular trueness 0.23°, angular precision 0.15°, RMS trueness 48 µm; reverse impression accuracy variable but generally within clinically acceptable limits.

Conclusions: Extraoral PG techniques are clinically acceptable for complete-arch implant position capture; reverse impression is promising but limited evidence; both methods address the accuracy degradation of IOS over long spans.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First SR+MA pooling extraoral photogrammetry accuracy data for complete-arch implant scanning
- 26 extraoral PG studies, 3 reverse impression studies included
- Establishes quantitative benchmark: extraoral PG mean linear 27 µm, RMS trueness 48 µm
- Extraoral PG: "clinically acceptable for capturing implant positions"
- Reverse impression: promising accuracy but insufficient evidence for clinical endorsement
- Both techniques introduced to overcome the long-span accuracy limitation of IOS

## 3. Methodology
- **Design**: SR+MA (PRISMA)
- **Databases**: PubMed/Medline, Scopus, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane + manual search
- **Date range**: Inception to August 2025
- **Inclusion**: Studies evaluating trueness and/or precision of complete-arch implant scans via reverse impression or extraoral PG
- **Quality assessment**: JBI critical appraisal tool, dual review, third reviewer for disagreements
- **Meta-analysis**: Studies reporting mean ± SD linear and angular discrepancies from extraoral PG systems
- **Publication bias**: Funnel plot + Egger test

## 4. Key Results
- 29 studies met inclusion criteria (3 reverse impression, 26 extraoral PG)
- **Extraoral PG pooled estimates:**
  - Mean linear discrepancy: 27 µm
  - Mean angular discrepancy: 0.21°
  - Mean linear trueness: 69 µm
  - Mean linear precision: 43 µm
  - Mean angular trueness: 0.23°
  - Mean angular precision: 0.15°
  - Mean RMS trueness: 48 µm
  - Mean linear precision (second reporting): 16 µm
- **Reverse impression**: Variable results depending on scanning technology/pattern; accuracy generally within clinically acceptable limits
- Publication bias assessed but specific results available in full text only

## 5. Limitations
- Abstract-only: full quantitative subgroup analyses (by PG system, by number of implants, etc.) not available
- Included studies are predominantly in-vitro — clinical relevance extrapolation needed
- PG system heterogeneity across 26 studies (Icam4D, PIC, others)
- Reverse impression evidence very sparse (only 3 studies)
