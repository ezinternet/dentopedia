## Why Ingested

치주수술 후 2주간의 초기 창상 치유를 미세순환(레이저 도플러)·치은열구액/창상액(GCF/WF) 사이토카인·플랩 폐쇄로 동시에 측정한 드문 인체 데이터 — [[periodontics/glim-2013-detrimental-dermal-wound-healing]]의 구강점막 창상 치유 생물학을 임상 수준의 염증 지표로 확장하고, [[periodontics/hasuike-2024-enamel-matrix-derivative-monotherapy-versus]]가 시사한 "골이식재보다 플랩 안정성이 관건"이라는 해석에 초기 치유 단계의 직접 근거를 제공한다.

## Three-line Summary

Prospective descriptive split-mouth study (Int J Mol Sci 2017; 30 patients: 18 enamel matrix derivative/biphasic calcium phosphate (EMD/BCP) regenerative surgery, 12 surgical crown lengthening (SCL), each with an untreated healthy control site) characterizing 14-day early periodontal wound healing by laser Doppler flowmetry (LDF), gingival crevicular fluid/wound fluid (GCF/WF) cytokines, and flap closure.
Both surgical groups showed an immediate LDF perfusion drop with recovery by D14 and an early pro-inflammatory surge — IL-1β concentration 7.5-fold (EMD/BCP) vs 6-fold (SCL) at D1, IL-1β amount 39-fold/19-fold and IL-6 amount 39-fold/22-fold at D1 (p<0.001) — with IL-10 suppressed to 0.25–0.4-fold; SCL healed better (Early Healing Index, EHI; p=0.032) with fewer dehiscences (3/12, 25%) than EMD/BCP (13/18, 72%; p=0.024).
Dehiscent sites carried 1.5-fold more GCF/WF, 4-fold more IL-1β and 2.5-fold more TNF-α than closed sites: primary flap closure, rather than the graft, appears decisive for uncomplicated early healing; the 14-day window and non-randomized procedure allocation limit causal inference.

## 세줄요약

전향적 기술적 분할구 연구(치주수술 환자 30명: 법랑기질유도체/이상인산칼슘(Enamel Matrix Derivative/Biphasic Calcium Phosphate, EMD/BCP) 골내결손 재생 18명, 외과적 치관연장술(Surgical Crown Lengthening, SCL) 12명, 각 미치료 건강 대조부위 포함) — 레이저 도플러 혈류계(Laser Doppler Flowmetry, LDF), 치은열구액/창상액(Gingival Crevicular Fluid/Wound Fluid, GCF/WF) 사이토카인, 플랩 폐쇄로 14일 초기 치주 창상 치유 평가.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First human description of the first two post-surgical weeks after periodontal surgery using three parallel readouts — flap microcirculation (LDF), GCF/WF volume and cytokine levels, and soft-tissue closure (EHI/dehiscence) — against untreated intra-patient control sites.
- Demonstrates a stereotyped early inflammatory response to periodontal surgery: immediate pro-inflammatory cytokine surge (IL-1β, TNF-α, IL-6) with reciprocal IL-10 suppression, largely self-limited when the flap stays closed.
- Identifies maintenance of primary flap closure as the decisive variable: dehiscence (16/30 pooled sites) prolonged inflammatory exudation regardless of EMD/BCP application.
- Reports an unexpected, clinically important adverse signal — EMD/BCP sites had 72% interproximal dehiscence vs 25% after SCL — attributed by the authors to supracrestal BCP overfilling without coronal flap advancement rather than to impaired perfusion.
- Shows that EMD's well-documented anti-inflammatory effects in vitro were not reproducible in clinical GCF/WF samples, despite EMD/BCP application.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Prospective, descriptive, split-mouth clinical study; two non-randomized treatment groups defined by clinical indication (EMD/BCP for intrabony defects; SCL for prosthetic reasons), each patient contributing one surgical and one clinically healthy untreated control site. Ethics approval Witten/Herdecke University no. 39/2011 (24 April 2011); CONSORT-style GCP-ICH/Helsinki compliance; recruitment 17 May 2011–27 September 2012.
- **Databases**: Not applicable (clinical study, not a secondary review).
- **n**: 30 patients analysed — EMD/BCP n=18, SCL n=12; one surgical + one control site per patient. All surgeries by a single experienced surgeon (GG).
- **Interventions**: EMD/BCP — simplified/modified papilla preservation flap, 24% EDTA root conditioning, Emdogain + Bone Ceramic BCP filling the intrabony defect and supracrestally beyond the residual crest to restore convex interproximal contour, 6-0 modified vertical mattress sutures. SCL — bevelled internal/sulcular incisions, mucoperiosteal flap, ostectomy/osteoplasty to ~3 mm from restoration margin to new crest, 6-0 vertical mattress sutures with periosteal anchorage. Both groups: 0.2% chlorhexidine rinse twice daily until suture removal at 2 weeks.
- **Outcomes**: Primary readouts — LDF perfusion (Periflux 5010, perfusion units relative to baseline) at D0a (pre-op), D0b (post-op), D1, D3, D7, D14; GCF/WF volume (Periotron 8000, µL) and cytokine concentration (pg/mL) / total amount per sample (pg/sample) for IL-1β, TNF-α, IL-6, IL-10 by multiplex immunoassay (Magpix/Luminex) at D0a, D1, D3, D7, D14; soft-tissue healing at D14 by EHI (1–5) and dichotomous dehiscence (yes/no), assessed by two blinded calibrated examiners.
- **Statistics**: Patient as unit of analysis; non-parametric tests (Wilcoxon signed-rank for longitudinal, Mann–Whitney U for cross-sectional, Pearson χ² for categorical); SPSS 22; significance p<0.05.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Patients**: EMD/BCP median age 58 y (IQ 47–71), 9 male/9 female; SCL 51 y (IQ 48–58), 7/5; smokers 3/18 vs 1/12 (all p>0.05). Baseline radiographic defect depth 6 mm (IQ 5–7) in EMD/BCP vs 0 in SCL (p<0.001).
- **Microcirculation**: Control sites unchanged over 14 days. Both surgery groups had significantly reduced papillary-base perfusion immediately post-op (D0b) vs baseline, recovering significantly by D1 but still below controls at D0b and D1; recovery to D14 was similar in both groups, with no between-group difference in blood-flow reduction.
- **GCF/WF volume**: At baseline higher in EMD/BCP than SCL/controls; at D1 and D3 both surgical groups exceeded controls; EMD/BCP exceeded SCL at D1 and controls at D3 and D7.
- **IL-1β**: Concentration at D1 elevated 7.5-fold (EMD/BCP) and 6-fold (SCL) over controls (p<0.05), declining significantly from D3 to D7. Total amount/sample at D1 and D3 was 39-fold (EMD/BCP) and 19-fold (SCL) vs controls (p<0.001); declined from D1 to D14, but EMD/BCP remained 6-fold (vs SCL) and 12-fold (vs controls) elevated at D14 (p<0.05).
- **TNF-α**: Concentration higher in controls than EMD/BCP at baseline; total amount/sample rose in both surgical groups at D1/D3 above controls. EMD/BCP stayed high, exceeding SCL 2-fold and healthy sites 2.5-fold at D14 (p<0.05).
- **IL-6**: Concentration rose significantly post-op in both groups and exceeded controls at D1, D3, D7; total amount/sample at D1 increased 39-fold (EMD/BCP) and 22-fold (SCL) (p<0.001), declined from D3 to D14, yet both surgical groups remained above controls continuously D1–D14 (p<0.05).
- **IL-10**: Concentration fell significantly from baseline to D1 in both surgical groups, reaching 0.25–0.4-fold of healthy sites at D1, D3, D7 (p<0.05); total amount/sample did not change significantly over time or differ between groups.
- **Soft tissue healing (D14)**: SCL EHI distribution 0/5/4/1/2 across EHI 1–5 (0%/42%/33%/8%/17%) vs EMD/BCP 2/2/1/4/9 (11%/11%/6%/22%/50%), favouring SCL (p=0.032, Pearson χ²). Dehiscence: EMD/BCP 13/18 (72%) vs SCL 3/12 (25%), p=0.024. Factors favouring SCL also shown as 75% of SCL sites at EHI 2–3 vs 25% at EHI 4–5.
- **Dehiscence analysis (pooled, 16/30 dehiscent at D14)**: Dehiscent sites had 1.5-fold higher GCF/WF volume (p=0.049), 4-fold higher IL-1β total amount (p=0.033) and 2.5-fold higher TNF-α total amount (p=0.021) than closed sites; other cytokine parameters unaffected (p>0.05).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Descriptive, non-randomized design: procedure allocation followed clinical indication (intrabony defect vs crown-lengthening need), so between-group differences (notably the dehiscence rate) cannot be attributed to EMD/BCP independently of defect anatomy, flap design, or supracrestal graft volume.
- Small sample (n=30; 18/12) and short 14-day follow-up capture only the early inflammatory phase — no attachment-level, bone-fill, or long-term regeneration outcomes were measured.
- The authors' own interpretation of the unexpectedly high EMD/BCP dehiscence rate is post hoc (supracrestal BCP overfilling beyond the defect margins without coronal flap advancement); the ideal granular graft volume is unknown.
- GCF/WF is a mixture of cells and exudates from multiple sources, which may dilute or obscure cell-type-specific effects of biologics — offered as the reason EMD's in vitro anti-inflammatory effects were not reproduced clinically.
- Industry support (Institut Straumann AG, manufacturer of Emdogain and Bone Ceramic) is disclosed with a no-role statement; a single surgeon performed all cases.
- No direct comparison of flap-design or suture modifications, and no microbiological assessment despite the authors invoking bacterial colonization of dehiscent sites as the mechanism for chronic inflammation.

## 6. Related Work

- glim-2013: oral mucosa heals faster and with less scarring than skin — this study supplies the in-vivo cytokine/perfusion counterpart to that comparative wound-healing biology.
- huynh-2016: in-vitro gingival fibroblast migration/wound closure; this study frames the inflammatory milieu (IL-1β/TNF-α) those cells face in a real periodontal wound.
- jasser-2021 / hasuike-2024: bone-graft substitutes (β-TCP/alloplast) in periodontal intrabony defects and EMD±graft SR/MA — same procedure family, different outcome axis (long-term defect fill/attachment vs 2-week soft-tissue healing).
- Villa et al. 2017 (cited as ref. 40 in the paper): similarly found a discrepancy between in vitro EMD cytokine effects and clinical wound-fluid measurements; cited in the Discussion, not held as a separate wiki page.

## 7. Glossary

- **EMD/BCP**: Enamel matrix derivative (Emdogain) combined with granular biphasic calcium phosphate (Bone Ceramic) — regenerative treatment of periodontal intrabony defects.
- **SCL**: Surgical crown lengthening — resective periodontal surgery to increase retention/avoid subgingival restoration margins.
- **LDF**: Laser Doppler flowmetry — non-invasive measurement of tissue microcirculation in perfusion units (PU).
- **GCF/WF**: Gingival crevicular fluid / wound fluid — crevicular fluid at healthy sites and exudate at surgical sites, collected with paper strips.
- **EHI**: Early Healing Index (1–5) — 1 = complete closure without fibrin line, 2–3 = complete closure with fibrin line/clot, 4–5 = incomplete closure with partial/complete interproximal necrosis.
- **Dehiscence**: Breakdown of interproximal flap closure after initially achieved primary closure, healing by second intention.
- **IL-1β / TNF-α / IL-6**: Pro-inflammatory cytokines driving the early inflammatory phase of wound healing; persistent elevation impairs collagen synthesis.
- **IL-10**: Anti-inflammatory/regulatory cytokine; its early suppression here mirrors the first inflammatory phase.
- **Multiplex immunoassay**: Luminex Magpix bead-based assay measuring multiple cytokines simultaneously in GCF/WF samples.
