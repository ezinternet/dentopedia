## One-line Summary
Prospective cohort (n=33 posterior immediate implants): using reactive soft tissue from the socket as primary wound closure reduced buccal bone height loss and maintained keratinized mucosa width, with 100% survival at 1 year.

## 한줄요약
전향 코호트 (n=33 후방부 즉시식립): 소켓 내 반응성 연조직을 1차 봉합재로 사용하면 협측골 소실 줄고 KMW 유지, 1년 생존율 100%.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Demonstrates "reactive soft tissue" (granulation tissue + long junctional epithelium) in compromised sockets can serve as primary wound closure, replacing or supplementing conventional flap advancement
- 100% implant survival at 1-year follow-up
- EG showed less buccal bone height loss and less keratinized mucosa (KM) width reduction vs CG at 6 months
- Challenges the dogma that all reactive/granulation tissue must be debrided before immediate implant placement
- Provides CBCT-based bone dimensional data at T0 (pre-surgery/immediately post) and T2 (6 months)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Prospective cohort
- Population: 33 posterior immediate implants (posterior maxillary and mandibular); compromised extraction sockets (caries, periapical pathology)
- Groups:
  - EG (experimental): reactive soft tissue raised and sutured as primary closure over implant
  - CG (control): reactive soft tissue elevated but failed/insufficient to achieve primary coverage
- Timing: implants inserted immediately after extraction
- CBCT: T1 (immediately post-surgery) and T2 (6 months)
- Soft tissue: measured at T0 (pre-extraction), prosthesis delivery, and T3 (1-year follow-up)
- Outcome: Marginal bone level (MBL), buccal bone height/width, KM width, implant survival

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Implant survival: 100% during study period
- Buccal bone height: EG showed less reduction vs CG (T1→T2)
- KM width: less decrease in EG vs CG
- MBL at 1 year: no significant difference between groups
- Soft tissue changes at T3: no significant difference between groups
- Interpretation: reactive tissue closure contributes to hard-soft tissue augmentation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Non-randomized (cohort design); group assignment by whether reactive tissue "succeeded" in covering the site — possible selection bias
- Small sample (n=33)
- 1-year follow-up only
- No standardized infection/compromised socket severity grading
- CG defined by failure of closure rather than a true comparator — confounds interpretation

## 7. Glossary
- **Reactive soft tissue**: granulation tissue + long junctional epithelium present in compromised (infected/carious) extraction sockets
- **EG**: Experimental group — reactive tissue used as primary wound closure
- **CG**: Control group — reactive tissue insufficient to achieve coverage
- **KMW**: Keratinized mucosa width
- **MBL**: Marginal bone level
