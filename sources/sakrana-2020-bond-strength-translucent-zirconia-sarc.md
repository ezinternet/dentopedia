## Why Ingested

→ [[dental-materials/zirconia/sakrana-2020-bond-strength-translucent-zirconia-sarc]]

Translucent (4Y/5Y) zirconia surface conditioning strategies directly affect clinical bonding reliability. This in-vitro study compares three surface treatments (airborne particle abrasion, Piranha acid, hot acid etching) combined with Silano-Pen and three self-adhesive resin cements across thermal aging — covering a clinically important gap in the bonding workflow for translucent zirconia restorations.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro microtensile bond strength study (360 bars, 3 surface treatments × Silano-Pen × 3 cements × aging) testing how airborne particle abrasion, Piranha acid, and hot acid etching influence bonding of self-adhesive resin cements to translucent zirconia after 10,000 thermocycles.

Hot acid etching produced the highest mean bond strength and a homogeneous granular texture with a wide porous network on SEM; Piranha acid gave the lowest values; Panavia SA significantly outperformed TheraCem and Panavia F2.0; Silano-Pen + hot acid yielded the best combination.

Pre-treatment method and cement selection both significantly affect zirconia bonding durability, while the cement × Silano-Pen interaction was non-significant (p = .067).

## 세줄요약

체외실험 (마이크로인장결합강도 시험편 360개, 3가지 표면처리 × Silano-Pen × 3가지 시멘트 × 열순환 10,000회): 공기마모(Airborne Particle Abrasion, APA), 피라냐산(Piranha Acid), 열산처리(Hot Acid Etching)가 반투명 지르코니아(Translucent Zirconia)와 자가접착 레진시멘트(Self-Adhesive Resin Cement) 결합강도에 미치는 영향 비교.

열산처리에서 가장 높은 평균 결합강도 및 주사전자현미경(Scanning Electron Microscopy, SEM)상 균질한 과립 질감·넓은 다공성 네트워크; 피라냐산이 최저; Panavia SA > TheraCem > Panavia F2.0; Silano-Pen + 열산처리 조합 최고.

표면처리법·시멘트 종류 모두 지르코니아 결합 내구성에 유의한 영향; 시멘트 × Silano-Pen 상호작용은 비유의(p = .067).

## Key Contributions

- Comparative microtensile bond strength (μTBS) data for three surface treatments on translucent zirconia
- Hot acid etching creates wider porous network (SEM confirmed) → highest bond strength
- Piranha acid yields lowest bond strength despite strong chemical action
- Panavia SA (MDP-based self-adhesive) significantly superior to TheraCem and Panavia F2.0
- Silano-Pen effect is surface-treatment-dependent — combination with hot acid maximises bonding
- 10,000 thermocycles as aging proxy for clinical durability

## Methodology

- **Specimen:** 36 translucent zirconia blocks, cut and sintered
- **Surface treatment groups (n = 3):**
  1. Airborne particle abrasion (APA)
  2. Piranha acid etching
  3. Hot acid etching
- **Silano-Pen:** applied after each surface treatment condition
- **Resin cements (n = 3):** Panavia SA, TheraCem, Panavia F2.0
- **Total microtensile bars:** 360 (36 blocks × 10 bars each)
- **Aging:** half subjected to 10,000 thermal cycling
- **Outcome:** microtensile bond strength (μTBS, MPa)
- **SEM:** surface morphology characterisation

## Key Results

- **Surface treatment ranking (μTBS):** Hot acid > Airborne particle abrasion > Piranha acid
- **Cement ranking (μTBS):** Panavia SA > TheraCem > Panavia F2.0 (statistically significant)
- **Silano-Pen × cement interaction:** p = .067 (non-significant)
- **SEM:** hot acid group — homogeneous granular texture, wide distribution of porous network
- **Aging effect:** all groups showed bond strength reduction after 10,000 thermocycles; relative ranking maintained

## Limitations

- Abstract-only source — exact μTBS values (MPa) not available; statistical sub-group data limited
- Single zirconia brand/translucency level (specific product not extractable from abstract)
- In-vitro design; no simulation of oral environment beyond thermal cycling
- Silano-Pen is a specific product; generalisability to other silane/primer systems is extrapolation

## Glossary

- **μTBS (Microtensile Bond Strength, 마이크로인장결합강도):** Bond strength measured on small cross-section specimens (~1 mm²); sensitive to surface micro-topology
- **APA (Airborne Particle Abrasion, 공기입자마모, 샌드블라스팅):** Mechanical surface roughening with alumina particles under pressure
- **Piranha Acid (피라냐산):** H₂SO₄ + H₂O₂ mixture; strong oxidiser for surface hydroxylation
- **Hot Acid Etching (열산처리):** Elevated-temperature acid treatment creating porous surface topography
- **Silano-Pen:** Chairside silane coupling agent in pen-applicator form; promotes chemical bonding between zirconia and resin
- **Panavia SA (자가접착 레진시멘트):** MDP-containing self-adhesive resin cement (Kuraray)
- **TheraCem:** Calcium- and fluoride-releasing self-adhesive resin cement (BISCO)
- **Panavia F2.0:** MDP-phosphate monomer resin cement requiring separate primer (Kuraray)
- **Translucent Zirconia (반투명 지르코니아):** 4Y-TZP / 5Y-TZP formulations with higher yttria content for improved translucency but reduced strength vs 3Y-TZP
