## Why Ingested

No held SR+MA directly compared 4-implant-supported overdentures (IODs) vs full-arch fixed restorations (IFRs) specifically in the maxilla with ≥5yr follow-up and quantified implant survival, prosthesis survival, MBL, and complication profiles separately. This provides the key IOD vs IFR decision-making evidence base for maxillary edentulous treatment planning.

## Three-line Summary

SR+MA of 16 studies (5,568 implants) comparing 4-implant-supported overdentures (IODs, n=9 studies) vs full-arch fixed restorations (IFRs, n=7 studies) in maxillary edentulous patients with ≥5yr follow-up; PubMed, Embase, Cochrane; RoB2 and NOS; primary outcome implant survival rate.

Weighted ISR: IODs 94.5% (CI 92.1–96.9%, I²=84%) vs IFRs 98.5% (CI 97.4–99.5%, I²=78%); prosthesis survival 85.0% (IOD) vs 99.9% (IFR); MBL after 5yr −0.27mm (IOD) vs −1.20mm (IFR); most common complications: retention loss 0.34/patient (IOD), acrylic tooth fracture 0.09/patient (IFR).

IFRs show higher implant and prosthesis survival; IODs show less marginal bone loss at 5yr but higher complication rates; both have predictable medium-term results.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Large MA: 16 studies, 5,568 implants comparing IOD vs IFR in maxilla ≥5yr
- Key trade-off: IFR > ISR and prosthesis survival; IOD < MBL
- Prosthesis survival gap is large: 99.9% (IFR) vs 85.0% (IOD) — IOD prostheses are significantly less durable
- Complication profiles differ: retention loss (IOD) vs mechanical/ceramic complications (IFR)
- High I² (84%, 78%) indicating study heterogeneity

## 3. Methodology

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Design | Systematic review + meta-analysis |
| Databases | PubMed, Embase, Cochrane + manual |
| Inclusion | ≥10 maxillary edentulous patients, IOD or IFR, ≥5yr |
| N studies | 16 (9 IOD, 7 IFR) |
| N implants | 5,568 |
| Bias tools | RoB2 (RCTs), Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (observational) |
| Primary outcome | Implant survival rate |
| Secondary outcomes | Prosthesis survival, MBL, complications |

## 4. Key Results

| Outcome | IOD (overdenture) | IFR (fixed) |
|---|---|---|
| Weighted ISR | **94.5%** (CI 92.1–96.9%, I²=84%) | **98.5%** (CI 97.4–99.5%, I²=78%) |
| Prosthesis survival | **85.0%** | **99.9%** |
| MBL at 5yr | **−0.27 ± 1.31 mm** | **−1.20 ± 0.76 mm** |
| Most common complication | Retention loss (0.34/patient) | Acrylic tooth dislodgment/fracture (0.09/patient) |

## 5. Limitations

- High heterogeneity (I²=84%, 78%) — pooled estimates must be interpreted cautiously
- Abstract only — attachment system subgroup results, individual study data, forest plots unavailable
- Maxilla only; not applicable to mandibular treatment planning
- Baseline variance acknowledged in abstract
