## Why Ingested

Directly quantifies how five irrigation activation techniques (ultrasonic, sonic, NaviTip FX, conventional needle, KTP laser) affect dentinal tubule penetration of three key intracanal medicaments (CH, DAP, TAP) using CLSM — fills a gap in [[wiki/endodontics/irrigation/cheung-2021-irrigant-activation-methods-clinical-review]] which covers clinical outcomes but not medicament penetration depth by activation mode. Complements [[wiki/endodontics/irrigation/ordinola-zapata-2022-intracanal-medicaments-status-directions]] by providing lab-measured penetration data for TAP and DAP specifically.

## Three-line Summary

Controlled lab study (n=180 extracted mandibular premolars, 5 activation groups × 3 medicament subgroups) measuring CLSM fluorescence penetration area at 2, 5, and 8 mm from apex.
TAP achieved the highest dentinal tubule penetration across all activation methods (P<0.05); ultrasonic activation (UI) yielded significantly greater penetration than sonic, NaviTip FX, conventional, and KTP laser in all medicament groups (P<0.05); coronal > middle > apical penetration for all groups.
Ultrasonic activation combined with TAP is recommended for cases requiring deep tubule disinfection (e.g., regenerative endodontics for immature necrotic teeth); CH remains appropriate for routine inter-appointment antimicrobial use.

## 세줄요약

대조 실험실 연구 (180개 발치 하악 소구치, 5개 세정 활성화 방식 × 3개 약제 하위군), 근단부에서 2·5·8 mm 위치에서 공초점 레이저 주사 현미경 (Confocal Laser Scanning Microscopy, CLSM)으로 형광 침투 면적 측정.
삼중 항생제 페이스트 (Triple Antibiotic Paste, TAP)가 모든 활성화 방식에서 가장 높은 상아세관 (Dentinal Tubule) 침투율 기록 (P<0.05); 초음파 활성화 (Ultrasonic Irrigation, UI)가 음파·NaviTip FX·기존 주사바늘·KTP 레이저보다 유의하게 우수 (P<0.05); 치관부 > 중간부 > 근단부 순.
초음파 활성화 후 TAP 적용이 심부 상아질 소독이 필요한 케이스(미성숙 괴사치 재생 근관치료 등)에 권장; 수산화칼슘 (Calcium Hydroxide, CH)은 일상적 내원 간 항균 약제로 여전히 적합.

## Key Contributions

- First direct CLSM comparison of KTP laser vs ultrasonic/sonic/NaviTip FX/conventional for medicament (not irrigant) penetration
- Demonstrated TAP penetrates up to 200 μm into dentinal tubules — superior to DAP and CH at all root levels and all activation techniques
- Quantified the additive benefit of activation technique on medicament penetration: ultrasonic activation yielded highest values regardless of medicament choice
- KTP laser (532 nm) performed unexpectedly poorly — attributed to poor water absorption at this wavelength compared to Er:YAG
- Confirmed coronal-to-apical gradient of penetration (8 mm > 5 mm > 2 mm); apical dentin has fewer, smaller tubules with tubular sclerosis

## Methodology

- **Design**: Controlled in vitro lab study
- **Sample**: 180 extracted single-rooted single-canal mandibular premolars (no calcification, fractures, or caries); standardized to 19 mm length
- **Preparation**: ProTaper Next (X1–X4), 5.25% NaOCl irrigation, 17% EDTA + NaOCl + distilled water for smear layer removal; ethylene oxide sterilization
- **Groups (n=36 per activation group)**:
  - Conventional needle (2 mm short of WL, up-down motion, 3 min)
  - NaviTip FX (Ultradent; same insertion/motion, 3 min)
  - KTP laser (SMARTLITE D, DEKA; 532 nm, 2.0 W, 100 mJ, 20 Hz, 200 μm fiber, 3 min)
  - Sonic activation (EndoActivator, Dentsply, 3 min)
  - Ultrasonic activation (IrriSafe #20/.00, Satelec Acteon piezo unit, 3 min)
- **Medicament subgroups (n=12 per subgroup)**:
  - CH: calcium hydroxide powder + distilled water (2:1)
  - DAP: metronidazole + ciprofloxacin (equal parts) + distilled water
  - TAP: metronidazole + ciprofloxacin + minocycline (equal parts, 1 g/mL) + distilled water 0.50 mL
- **Fluorescent tracer**: 0.1% Rhodamine B mixed into medicaments
- **Sections**: Precision saw (IsoMet 1000) at 2 mm (apical), 5 mm (middle), 8 mm (coronal); ~1 mm thick
- **Imaging**: Leica TCS-SPE CLSM; penetration area measured with ImageJ (μm → mm²)
- **Statistics**: Three-way ANOVA + Tukey post hoc (P=0.05); SPSS v22

## Key Results

| Activation \ Medicament | CH (Apical/Middle/Coronal) | DAP (A/M/C) | TAP (A/M/C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conventional | 0.04 / 0.18 / 0.54 | 0.11 / 0.36 / 0.58 | 0.24 / 0.61 / 0.78 |
| NaviTip FX | 0.10 / 0.32 / 0.68 | 0.19 / 0.52 / 0.74 | 0.32 / 0.70 / 0.92 |
| KTP Laser | 0.18 / 0.48 / 0.76 | 0.26 / 0.65 / 0.83 | 0.44 / 0.81 / 1.08 |
| Sonic | 0.26 / 0.62 / 0.96 | 0.38 / 0.86 / 0.91 | 0.46 / 0.98 / 1.24 |
| Ultrasonic | 0.38 / 0.82 / 1.06 | 0.48 / 0.92 / 1.12 | 0.72 / 1.18 / 1.46 |

(All values in mm²; statistically significant differences indicated by superscript notation in original)

- TAP > DAP > CH at every level and every activation method (P<0.05)
- UI > Sonic > KTP Laser > NaviTip FX > Conventional for all medicaments (P<0.05 for UI vs all others)
- Coronal > Middle > Apical at all conditions (P<0.05)
- Null hypothesis rejected: activation technique significantly affects medicament penetration

## Limitations

- In vitro only — non-infected extracted teeth, no biofilm model
- Single tooth type (mandibular premolar, single-rooted straight canals); curved canals excluded
- 24-hour incubation only — no long-term exposure assessment
- Rhodamine B may affect the viscosity/surface tension of medicaments slightly
- No antimicrobial efficacy testing — penetration depth ≠ antibacterial effect demonstrated

## Glossary

- **CLSM (Confocal Laser Scanning Microscopy)**: Optical microscopy technique using point illumination and spatial pinhole to eliminate out-of-focus light; enables fluorescence-based quantification of material penetration depth in dentin
- **TAP (Triple Antibiotic Paste)**: Mixture of metronidazole + ciprofloxacin + minocycline; broad-spectrum endodontic medicament; minocycline causes tooth discoloration
- **DAP (Double Antibiotic Paste)**: Mixture of metronidazole + ciprofloxacin; developed to avoid minocycline-related crown discoloration of TAP
- **Ultrasonic activation (UI)**: Piezoelectric irrigation agitation (~25,000 vibrations/s); creates acoustic streaming and cavitation enhancing irrigant/medicament delivery
- **KTP laser**: Potassium Titanyl Phosphate laser, 532 nm wavelength (frequency-doubled Nd:YAG); poorly absorbed by water, limiting cavitation effect compared to Er:YAG
- **Rhodamine B**: Fluorescent dye (0.1%) mixed with medicaments to enable CLSM visualization of dentinal tubule penetration depth
