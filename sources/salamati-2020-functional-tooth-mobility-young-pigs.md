## Why Ingested

The only in-vivo measurement of tooth displacement, alveolar bending, and PDL fluid pressure simultaneously during natural mastication in the collection — the physiological anchor for the biomechanical PDL pages [[wiki/orthodontics/papadopoulou-2013-biomechanical-time-dependency-periodontal-ligament]] (FEM material parameters) and [[wiki/orthodontics/keilig-2016-in-vivo-measurements-pdl]] (time-dependent modulus in humans). Also provides the mobility range-of-motion baseline against which the dynamic stiffness measurements in [[wiki/orthodontics/jepsen-2023-biomechanical-properties-periodontal-tissues-non-periodontitis]] should be interpreted.

## Three-line Summary

In-vivo pig study (J Biomech 2020;104:109716, NIHMS 1630169) instrumenting 12 three-month-old farm pigs with ultrasonic crystals (pulp chamber + buccal/palatal alveolar plates), rosette strain gauges, and an intraosseous pressure transducer to record maxillary molar mobility, alveolar bending, and PDL fluid pressure simultaneously during unrestrained feeding and masseter muscle stimulation.

During the power stroke of mastication the molar displaced buccally and apically 192 ± 95 μm regardless of chewing side; compressive bone strain was greater on the buccal than on the palatal plate; PDL pressure rose to 3.63 ± 0.80 kPa; masseter contraction reproduced the pattern at generally lower values (20–302 μm, average 113 ± 89 μm).

PDL space widths measured by micro-CT ranged 250–400 μm (buccal/mesial > palatal/distal) — consistently larger than the mobility range, with no correlation between PDL width and mobility; in-vivo mobility (63–322 μm) sits within the published pig range (0–800 μm) and above rat/monkey/human values.

## 세줄요약

돼지(3개월령 12마리)에 초음파 크리스털·로제트 스트레인 게이지·골내 압력변환기를 장착하고 자유 섭식 중 상악 구치의 동요도·치조골 휨·PDL 액압을 동시 계측한 생체 실험 (J Biomech 2020;104:109716).

교합강타(power stroke) 중 구치는 씹는 쪽과 무관하게 협측·치근단 방향으로 192±95 μm 변위, 골 압축 변형률은 협측판 > 구개판, PDL 압력은 3.63±0.80 kPa로 상승; 깨물근 자극은 같은 패턴을 더 낮은 값(20–302 μm, 평균 113±89 μm)으로 재현.

micro-CT PDL 폭 250–400 μm (협측/근심 > 구개측/원심) — 동요도 범위보다 항상 크고 상관 없음; 생체 동요도 63–322 μm는 문헌상 돼지 범위(0–800 μm) 내, 사람(100–400 μm)·원숭이·쥐보다 큼.
