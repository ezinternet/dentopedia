즉시식립 관련 SR/MA는 대부분 식립 타이밍(immediate/early/delayed placement)만 다루는데, 본 논문(Pommer 2021, COIR Suppl 21)은 상악 심미부위 single-tooth implant에서 식립 타이밍(IP/EP/DP)과 부하 타이밍(IL/EL/DL)을 **교차(cross-tabulate)**해 ≥3년 장기 결과(생존율·변연골흡수)를 비교한 드문 연구다. 기존 [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/buser-2017-implant-placement-timing-post-extraction-esthetic]]가 Type 1–4 식립 타이밍 분류체계를 제시했고 [[immediate-implant/lang-2012-immediate-implant-survival-success-sr]]가 즉시식립 생존·성공률을 SR로 정리했다면, 본 논문은 그 타이밍 분류에 부하 타이밍 축을 더해 7편 대조군 연구 메타분석(IPIL vs IPDL p=.306, DPIL vs DPDL p=1.000, EPDL vs DPDL p=.600) + 29편/965 임플란트 pooled 분석 모두에서 **어떤 조합도 장기적으로 유의한 차이를 보이지 않음**을 보고한다. 이 null-result는 동일 배치로 인제스트되는 [[immediate-implant/mello-2017-immediate-fresh-extraction-vs-delayed-healed-socket-sr-ma]], [[immediate-implant/garcia-sanchez-2022-immediate-vs-delayed-implant-placement-sr-ma]], [[immediate-implant/asghar-2023-immediate-vs-early-implant-esthetic-zone-sr-ma]] 등 일부 유의차를 보고하는 SR들과 대비되는 counterweight로, "장기 결과는 타이밍 프로토콜보다 case selection·술기가 더 중요할 수 있다"는 해석의 근거가 된다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis of maxillary single-tooth implants in the aesthetic zone with ≥3-year follow-up, cross-tabulating implant placement timing (immediate/early/delayed placement) against prosthetic loading timing (immediate/early/delayed loading); 7 controlled trials meta-analyzed plus pooled single-arm data from 29 studies total (965 implants).

No statistically significant differences in long-term survival rate or marginal bone remodeling were found across any placement-times-loading timing combination tested (e.g. IPIL vs. IPDL p=.306; DPIL vs. DPDL p=1.000; EPDL vs. DPDL p=.600); data were insufficient to meta-analyze the full combination matrix, and no impact of one abutment-one time, flap design, or simultaneous augmentation could be established.

Available long-term evidence does not support preferring one placement/loading timing protocol over another for maxillary single-tooth implants — but the evidence base itself remains too sparse (few controlled trials per combination) for definitive combination-level conclusions.

## 세줄요약

상악 심미부위 단일치아 임플란트를 대상으로 ≥3년 추적한 체계적고찰+메타분석; 식립 타이밍(즉시식립 Immediate Placement, IP / 조기식립 Early Placement, EP / 지연식립 Delayed Placement, DP)과 부하 타이밍(즉시부하 Immediate Loading, IL / 조기부하 Early Loading, EL / 지연부하 Delayed Loading, DL)을 교차 비교 — 대조군 연구 7편 메타분석 + 단일군 연구 포함 총 29편(임플란트 965개) pooled 분석.

식립×부하 타이밍 조합 어디에서도 장기 생존율·변연골개조(Marginal Bone Remodeling)에 유의한 차이 없음(IPIL vs IPDL p=.306, DPIL vs DPDL p=1.000, EPDL vs DPDL p=.600); one abutment-one time 개념·판막(flap) 설계·동시 골/연조직 증대술의 영향도 입증되지 않음.

현재 근거로는 상악 단일치아 임플란트에서 특정 식립/부하 타이밍 프로토콜을 우선할 근거가 부족하다 — 다만 조합별 대조군 연구 수 자체가 적어 결론의 확실성에는 한계가 있음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Uniquely cross-tabulates BOTH implant placement timing (IP/EP/DP) AND prosthetic loading timing (IL/EL/DL) for maxillary single-tooth implants, rather than analyzing placement timing alone as most prior SRs do.
- Restricts scope to the aesthetic zone with ≥3-year follow-up, targeting long-term rather than short/medium-term outcomes.
- Combines controlled-trial meta-analysis (7 studies) with pooled single-arm data (29 studies, 965 implants) to maximize the evidence synthesized despite sparse head-to-head comparisons.
- Explicitly tests secondary technique variables (one abutment-one time, flap design, simultaneous augmentation) for confounding effect on the timing comparisons.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Systematic review with meta-analysis (electronic + manual search); comparative trials meta-analyzed, single-arm study data pooled separately.
- **Population**: Maxillary single-tooth implants in the aesthetic zone.
- **Timing protocols compared**: Placement — immediate (IP) / early (EP) / delayed (DP); Loading — immediate (IL) / early (EL) / delayed (DL).
- **Outcome window**: ≥3 years (long-term survival rate and/or marginal bone resorption).
- **Meta-analyzed comparisons**: IPIL vs. IPDL (3 studies), DPIL vs. DPDL (2 studies), EPDL vs. DPDL (2 studies) — total 7 controlled trials.
- **Pooled analysis**: 29 studies, 965 implants, evaluating differences between timing protocols and marginal bone remodeling.
- Abstract does not report specific databases searched, date range, or risk-of-bias tool — not retrievable without full text.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- IPIL vs. IPDL: no significant difference, p=.306 (3 studies).
- DPIL vs. DPDL: no significant difference, p=1.000 (2 studies).
- EPDL vs. DPDL: no significant difference, p=.600 (2 studies).
- Pooled analysis (29 studies, 965 implants): no differences detected between placement timing or loading timing groups, nor in marginal bone remodeling.
- No demonstrable impact of the one abutment-one time concept, flap design, or simultaneous bone/soft-tissue augmentation on long-term outcomes.
- Explicitly stated limitation: insufficient data for meta-analytic comparison of all placement×loading combinations (only 3 of the possible 9 combinations had enough controlled-trial data to meta-analyze).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Abstract-only retrieval — full text (search strategy, risk-of-bias assessment, funnel plots, I² heterogeneity, individual study characteristics) not available; confidence in methodological rigor cannot be fully verified from the abstract alone.
- Only 7 controlled trials existed across all timing combinations, and only 3 of the theoretically possible 3×3 placement×loading combinations had ≥2 controlled trials to meta-analyze — most combinations (e.g. IPIL vs. EPDL, IPIL vs. DPDL) remain uncompared.
- Pooled single-arm analysis (29 studies) cannot establish causal timing effects the way controlled comparisons can — it is descriptive/associative only.
- Restricted to maxillary single-tooth implants in the aesthetic zone; findings do not generalize to multi-unit, mandibular, or posterior implants.
- Null results across all tested comparisons may reflect genuine equivalence, but could also reflect underpowered comparisons (small study counts per contrast).

## 7. Glossary

- **IP / EP / DP**: Immediate / Early / Delayed Placement (implant placement relative to tooth extraction).
- **IL / EL / DL**: Immediate / Early / Delayed Loading (prosthetic loading relative to implant placement).
- **One abutment-one time**: Placing the definitive abutment at implant surgery and never removing it, avoiding repeated disconnection of the peri-implant soft-tissue seal.
- **Marginal bone remodeling/resorption**: Radiographic change in crestal bone level around the implant over time, a standard long-term implant-outcome metric.
- **Aesthetic zone**: Maxillary anterior region where soft-tissue and emergence-profile esthetics are clinically critical outcome dimensions.
