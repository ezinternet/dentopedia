## Why Ingested
Added to document whether osseodensification (OD) causes bone microfracture or microdamage around the osteotomy in dense bone, extending the sheep trabecular-bone work in [[implants/osseodensification/stauber-2024-trabecular-bone-osseodensification-sheep]] and qualifying the cadaveric torque gain in [[implants/osseodensification/mercier-2022-osseodensification-primary-stability-cadavers]] with in vivo cortical-bone histology.

## Three-line Summary
Sheep in vivo study (16 adult sheep, 64 titanium implants with bone chambers, mandibular cortical bone, harvest at 3/6/12/24 weeks) comparing osseodensification (OD, Densah burs counterclockwise) against conventional subtractive drilling (Reg), crossed with machined (Mach) vs acid-etched (AE) surfaces, in 3.8 mm osteotomies for 4 mm implants.
Insertion torque (AE: 87.00 vs 80.95 N·cm; Mach: 74.04 vs 74.00 N·cm; p = 0.689 and 0.997 for OD vs Reg), bone-to-implant contact (BIC) and bone area fractional occupancy (BAFO) did not differ between OD and Reg at any time point (all p > 0.05); surface treatment also made no difference, and no necrosis, inflammation or microfractures were seen with OD.
In dense cortical bone either technique is acceptable when the osteotomy is not undersized relative to the implant major diameter; OD benefits seen in trabecular bone should not be assumed here (modest group size; histomorphometry limited to one section per implant; undersizing not tested).

## 세줄요약
양 전임상 연구(성체 양 16마리, 하악 피질골 티타늄 임플란트 64개, 3/6/12/24주 평가): 골밀도화 (Osseodensification, OD)와 기존 절삭 드릴링 (Reg)을 기계가공 (Machined) 대 산부식 (Acid-Etched, AE) 표면과 교차 비교.
식립 토크 (AE 87.00 vs 80.95 N·cm, Mach 74.04 vs 74.00 N·cm; p = 0.689, 0.997), 골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC), 골면적 점유율 (Bone Area Fractional Occupancy, BAFO) 모두 전 시점에서 OD와 Reg 간 유의차 없음; OD 군에서 괴사·염증·미세골절 (Microfracture) 소견 없음.
치밀 피질골에서는 골형성 와동이 임플란트와 거의 같은 직경일 때 두 술식 모두 적합; 소표본(군당 n = 16)이며 임플란트 1개당 조직절편 1개로 분석한 한계가 있음.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Biomimetics 2025;10(10):662
- **DOI**: 10.3390/biomimetics10100662
- **Institution**: Not stated in the retrieved text (animal work approved by the IACUC of École Nationale Vétérinaire d'Alfort, France)

## 2. Key Contributions
- In vivo comparison of OD vs subtractive drilling in dense mandibular cortical bone, crossed with implant surface (machined vs acid-etched).
- Reports no necrosis, inflammation, microfractures or impaired osseointegration with OD when the osteotomy was not considerably undersized.
- Reports no surface-treatment effect on torque, BIC or BAFO in cortical bone.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Preclinical in vivo (sheep), randomized and interpolated within each animal; mixed models with drilling technique, surface and time as fixed effects.
- **n**: 16 adult sheep (~55 kg), 4 implants each, 64 implants (n = 16 per group); 4 sheep per time point (3, 6, 12, 24 weeks).
- **Implants**: Custom conical Ti-6Al-4V (Versah), 4 mm x 10 mm, deep threads with designated bone chambers; Mach or AE (37% HCl) surface; placed in 3.8 mm osteotomies (not undersized).
- **Osteotomies**: Reg = 2.0 mm pilot then 3.2 and 3.8 mm twist drills; OD = 2.0 mm pilot then VS2228 (2.8 mm) and VS3238 (3.8 mm) Densah burs counterclockwise; all at 1100 rpm with continuous saline irrigation.
- **Outcomes**: Insertion torque (digital torque meter), BIC and BAFO within healing chambers (one non-decalcified section per implant, Stevenel's Blue / Van Gieson), qualitative histology.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Insertion torque: AE 87.00 (OD) vs 80.95 N·cm (Reg), p = 0.689; Mach 74.04 vs 74.00 N·cm, p = 0.997 (reported spread +/- 21.4 N·cm).
- BIC: OD vs Reg not different at 3 wk (AE p = 0.883; Mach p = 0.158), 6 wk (0.822; 0.285), 12 wk (0.135; 0.755), 24 wk (0.713; 0.157).
- BAFO: not different at 3 wk (AE 0.654; Mach 0.060), 6 wk (0.796; 0.210), 12 wk (0.157; 0.866), 24 wk (0.944; 0.457).
- Histology: osseointegration in all groups; woven bone at 3 wk, more bone in chambers at 6 wk, lamellar maturation at 12 wk, dense lamellar bone at 24 wk. In OD sites, remaining bone chips served as nucleation sites at 6 wk. No samples excluded; no infection, inflammation or adverse reactions on gross examination.
- OD tended to give numerically higher BIC and BAFO early (3-6 weeks) but not significantly.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Dense cortical bone with minimal trabecular space; the authors note findings may not transfer to lower-density regions or aging/osteopenic bone.
- Osteotomy was matched to the implant major diameter; the authors stress undersizing should be avoided with OD, so microfracture safety under undersizing was not tested.
- Single histological section per implant; modest group size.
- Future work suggested on combining macro- and micro-roughness (e.g., grit blasting plus acid etching) with OD.

## 6. Related Work
- Mercier et al.: OD increased insertion torque and bone density in human cadaveric mandibles (cited by authors as contrasting result).
- Studies in dense bovine femur and a recent RCT in high-density bone: little or no OD advantage (cited by authors).
- Vaddamanu et al.: CT-measured peri-implant density increase after OD (cited by authors).

## 7. Glossary
- **OD**: osseodensification, non-cutting compaction burs (negative rake) run counterclockwise.
- **Reg**: conventional subtractive drilling.
- **BIC**: bone-to-implant contact, percent of chamber length in direct bone contact.
- **BAFO**: bone area fractional occupancy, percent of chamber area filled by bone.
- **AE / Mach**: acid-etched / as-machined implant surface.
