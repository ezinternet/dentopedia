## Why Ingested

Early narrative review (2011) establishing the foundational biologic rationale for platform switching (PLS) — inflammatory-infiltrate displacement and FEA stress redistribution — that later higher-evidence trials and SR+MA built upon. Provides a baseline crestal bone loss range (0.05–1.4 mm vs 1.67 mm controls) that contextualises the quantitative findings in [[implants/mbl/di-girolamo-2016-platform-switching-matching-sr-ma]] and [[implants/mbl/rocha-2016-platform-switching-crestal-bone-3year-rct]].

## Three-line Summary

This narrative review summarizes clinical and biomechanical evidence for platform switching (PLS), in which a smaller-diameter abutment is seated on a wider implant platform to reduce crestal bone loss.

PLS reduces marginal bone loss to 0.05–1.4 mm compared to ~1.67 mm in matched-platform controls, attributed to coronal displacement of the implant-abutment junction inflammatory infiltrate and more favourable stress distribution (FEA evidence).

PLS is recommended particularly in esthetic zones, narrow ridges (<3 mm), and anatomic-limitation sites; evidence for immediate-loading PLS remained inconclusive at the time of writing.

## 세줄요약

임플란트 플랫폼보다 좁은 직경의 지대주를 사용하는 플랫폼 스위칭(PLS)의 임상적·생체역학적 근거를 종합한 서술 리뷰.

PLS는 변연골 소실을 0.05–1.4 mm로 줄여주며(대조군 ~1.67 mm 대비), 이는 임플란트-지대주 접합부(IAJ) 염증 침윤의 치관 방향 이동 및 유한요소법(FEA) 분석상 응력 분산 개선으로 설명된다.

심미 구역, 협착 치조제(<3 mm), 해부학적 제한 부위에서 특히 권고되며, 즉시부하 시 PLS 효용은 당시 기준 불명확.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Synthesises the biologic rationale for PLS: coronal displacement of biologic width and the inflammatory connective tissue (ICT) zone away from crestal bone.
- Compiles crestal bone loss data across multiple clinical studies: PLS range 0.05–1.4 mm vs platform-matched (PM) ~1.67 mm.
- Summarises FEA evidence showing stress concentration shifting from bone crest to implant screw centre with PLS.
- Identifies clinical indications: short implants, limited bone height, narrow ridges, esthetic zones.
- Highlights controversy in immediate loading and mixed evidence in animal studies.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Narrative literature review; not systematic, no meta-analysis.
- Electronic search of PubMed (1990–2011).
- Included clinical trials, case series, radiographic studies, and FEA computational studies.
- Summarised crestal bone loss measurements and biologic/biomechanical explanations.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Platform Switching | Platform Matching (control) |
|---|---|---|
| Crestal bone loss range | 0.05–1.4 mm | ~1.67 mm |
| Follow-up range across studies | 4–169 months | — |
| Biologic width position | Coronally displaced | At bone crest |
| Peak stress location (FEA) | Implant screw centre | Bone-implant interface |

- PLS benefits most evident in: short implants, limited crestal bone height, narrow ridges (<3 mm buccal-lingual width), esthetic zones.
- Immediate loading with PLS: evidence was controversial; standard loading preferred when primary stability allows choice.
- Animal studies: generally supported PLS for MBL reduction, though some variation noted.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review methodology; selection and publication bias not controlled.
- No meta-analysis or pooled quantitative synthesis.
- Heterogeneous study designs, follow-up periods (4–169 months), and implant systems across cited studies.
- Some cited studies lack control groups.
- Long-term data beyond 5 years were sparse at the time of writing.

## 7. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| PLS / PS | 플랫폼 스위칭 (Platform Switching) — placing a narrower abutment on a wider implant collar |
| IAJ | 임플란트-지대주 접합부 (Implant-Abutment Junction) |
| Biologic width | 생물학적 폭경 — junctional epithelium + connective tissue attachment dimension |
| FEA | 유한요소법 (Finite Element Analysis) — computational stress simulation |
| ICT | 염증성 결체조직 (Inflammatory Connective Tissue) — tissue zone at IAJ |
| MBL | 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss) |
| PM | Platform Matching — abutment diameter equals implant platform diameter |
