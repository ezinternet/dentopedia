## Why Ingested

Part of the implant-iip-loading surveillance batch, found by manual PDF discovery. Systematic review synthesizing the clinical-parameter determinants (biotype, flap status, jump-space, torque/ISQ threshold) of hard/soft-tissue outcomes after immediate implant placement + restoration (IIPR) in the anterior maxilla — complements [[wiki/immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/kinaia-2017-soft-tissue-changes-around]]'s biotype-stratified meta-analytic findings with a broader determinant-mapping synthesis.

## Three-line Summary
Systematic review (17 studies: 4 RCT, 6 prospective, 2 comparative prospective, 3 controlled cohort, 2 retrospective; 626 immediately placed and immediately restored [IIPR] implants in 609 patients) evaluating which clinical parameters (implant type, primary stability, gingival biotype, flap status, socket grafting, jump-space, loading protocol) influence hard/soft-tissue outcomes in the anterior maxilla.

65.6% of implants placed flapless; jump space (gap between implant and alveolar wall) averaged 1.38–2.25mm across studies reporting it; 405/626 sockets grafted; 419 implants reached ≥32 Ncm torque or ISQ≥60; overall success 97.96%, survival 98.25% at mean 31.2-month follow-up; mean crestal bone and interproximal mucosa change <1mm; midfacial mucosal change <0.95mm regardless of gingival biotype (thick in 60.5% of sites) except in one study.

Immediately placed and immediately restored implants in the anterior maxilla show consistently favorable hard/soft-tissue outcomes across a heterogeneous evidence base, with flapless surgery and grafting-free protocols feasible in appropriately selected cases, but strict patient selection was a common feature across all included trials.

## Key Contributions
- Maps which specific clinical parameters (not just placement timing) drive hard/soft-tissue outcomes in immediate placement + immediate restoration (IIPR)
- Quantifies flapless-surgery prevalence (65.6%) and jump-space dimensions (1.38–2.25mm) across the pooled evidence, both underreported elsewhere
- Reports a high pooled success (97.96%) and survival (98.25%) rate specific to the IIPR protocol, at mean 31.2-month follow-up

## Methodology
Systematic review, PubMed search Jan 2000–Sep 2015 plus hand search, English abstracts only. Included: human clinical trials, ≥10 participants, hard/soft-tissue outcomes reported. Study types: RCT, prospective, prospective comparative, retrospective. 17 studies (4 RCT, 6 prospective, 2 comparative prospective, 3 controlled cohort, 2 retrospective) with 626 IIPR implants in 609 patients.

## Key Results
| Parameter | Finding |
|---|---|
| Flapless surgery | 411/626 implants (65.56%) |
| Jump space (gap) | 1.38–2.25mm (170 implants reporting) |
| Socket grafting | 405/626 grafted, 201 not grafted, 20 unknown |
| Primary stability threshold | 419 implants ≥32 Ncm or ISQ≥60; 53 implants at 25 Ncm |
| Success / survival | 97.96% / 98.25% (mean follow-up 31.2 months) |
| Thick gingival biotype | 379/626 sites (60.5%) |
| Crestal bone / interproximal mucosa change | <1mm |
| Midfacial mucosal change | <0.95mm regardless of biotype (1 study exception: thin biotype more recession) |

## Limitations
- Abstract-only (PubMed capture); full risk-of-bias assessment and per-study breakdown not retrieved.
- Heterogeneous study designs pooled (only 4 of 17 are RCTs); English-language abstracts only.
- Strict patient selection across all included trials limits generalizability to unselected populations.

## Glossary
- **IIPR**: Immediate Implant Placement and Restoration — implant placed and provisionally/definitively restored at the time of extraction.
- **Jump space**: the residual gap between the implant surface and the alveolar socket wall after immediate placement.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)**: resonance-frequency-analysis measure of implant stability.
