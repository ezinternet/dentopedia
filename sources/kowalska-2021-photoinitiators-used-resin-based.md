## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (64 articles, 1985–2020) of commercial and alternative photoinitiator systems in dental resin composites. Compared camphorquinone (CQ), PPD, PQ, benzophenone (BP), TPO, BAPO, and novel germanium compounds on degree of conversion (DC), hardness, color stability, and biocompatibility. No single alternative fully replaces CQ; germanium compounds (Ivocerin) and acylphosphine oxides used synergistically with CQ are the most promising alternatives.

## 세줄요약

치과용 복합레진의 상업·대안 광개시제 64편 서사적 리뷰. 캄퍼퀴논(CQ)·PPD·PQ·벤조페논·TPO·BAPO·게르마늄 화합물을 전환율·경도·색상안정성·생체적합성 측면에서 비교. CQ를 완전히 대체하는 광개시제는 없으나 Ivocerin(게르마늄 계열)과 CQ 병용 아실포스핀 산화물이 가장 유망하다.

## Why Ingested

Provides systematic comparison of all major photoinitiator types used in dental composites — CQ (with yellowing and biocompatibility concerns), PPD (hybrid type I/II), TPO and BAPO (type-1 acylphosphines), and novel germanium compounds (Ivocerin). Foundational reference for resin photoinitiator selection, understanding DC optimization, and aesthetic vs. mechanical property tradeoffs.

## Key Data Points

- CQ absorption: 360–510 nm, max ~468 nm; concentration 0.17–1.03 wt%
- CQ optimal amine co-initiator ratios: CQ:EDMAB 1.44:0.42 or 1.05:1.65 mol% for max hardness; 2.40:0.83 mol% for max DC
- DMAEMA (co-initiator): at 0.5 wt% CQ → DC 45% (10 s) / 62% (120 s); at 1.5 wt% CQ → DC 76–79%
- EDMAB (co-initiator): at 0.5 wt% CQ → DC 65–68% regardless of exposure time
- TPO: DC ~10% higher than CQ (0.86 wt% → 74%; 0.22 wt% → 68%); Knoop top hardness 33.5 vs CQ 26.3
- BAPO: Knoop top hardness 32.9; molar extinction coeff. 870 L/mol·cm vs CQ 33 L/mol·cm; quantum yield 5× CQ
- Ivocerin in luting cement: DC ~87% vs CQ ~44%; Vickers hardness 47 VHN vs CQ 33 VHN
- PPD absorption max: 393–410 nm; less yellow than CQ; PPD+CQ ratios 1:1 and 1:4 optimal
- BTMGe/DBDEGe setting time 3–5 s vs CQ 8 s

## Notes

- Study searched PubMed, Google Scholar, Elsevier, etc.; 6000+ articles found, 1000 checked, 64 selected
- Review period: 1985–2020
- Critical limitation: in vitro only; no clinical comparison data
- Ivocerin patented exclusively by Ivoclar Vivadent; not available for generic formulations as of review date
