## Why Ingested

투명교정의 부작용에 대한 체계적 리뷰 및 단일군 메타분석으로, 통증·치근 흡수·플라크·구강위생 등 광범위한 부작용을 종합한다. [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/butsabul-2024-clear-aligner-root-resorption-cbct-sr-ma]]의 치근흡수 MA를 더 넓은 안전성 지평으로 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

This SR with single-arm meta-analysis synthesized 34 studies (10 RCTs, 6 non-randomized, 18 cohort) on adverse effects of removable orthodontic aligners. Pain peaked within 24h, decreased by day 3, and was minimal by 1 week. Apical root resorption showed mean linear loss of −0.33 mm (95% CI −0.55 to −0.11) and volumetric loss of −4.37 mm³ (95% CI −5.51 to −3.24). Other reported outcomes included periodontal changes, enamel demineralization, WSLs, speech alterations, halitosis, open gingival embrasures, TMD symptoms, and awake bruxism.

## 세줄요약

가역적 투명교정의 부작용에 대한 34편(RCT 10, 비 무작위 6, 코호트 18)의 SR 및 단일군 메타분석. 통증은 24시간 내 정점, 3일 후 감소, 1주 후 최소화; 유치단 흡수는 선형 손실 −0.33mm(95% CI −0.55~−0.11), 체적 손실 −4.37mm³(95% CI −5.51~−3.24). 치주 변화·ENAMEL 탈회·백색 병소·음성 변화·구취·개방 치간공·TMJ 증상·각성 시 악간마찰 등도 보고됨.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Largest SR to date on aligner adverse effects (34 studies)
- First meta-analysis of aligner-related apical root resorption (linear and volumetric)
- Comprehensive mapping of all reported adverse effect categories
- Risk of bias assessment using RoB 2 and ROBINS-I

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Systematic review with single-arm meta-analysis
- **Databases**: Cochrane, Embase, LILACS, Livivo, MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science + gray literature
- **n**: 34 studies (10 RCTs, 6 non-randomized, 18 cohort)
- **Outcomes**: Pain/discomfort, root resorption, plaque, periodontal changes, WSLs, speech, TMD, bruxism

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Pain: peaked 24h, minimal by 1 week
- Root resorption: linear −0.33 mm (95% CI −0.55 to −0.11)
- Root resorption: volumetric −4.37 mm³ (95% CI −5.51 to −3.24)
- Plaque at 3 months: inconclusive
- RCTs predominantly low ROB; non-randomized/cohort mostly moderate-high ROB

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Clinical and methodological heterogeneity limited meta-analysis to 3 outcomes
- Most studies at moderate-high ROB (non-RCTs)
- Long-term adverse effect data scarce
- Need for standardized outcome reporting in aligner studies

- **Single-arm Meta-analysis**: 단일군 메타분석 ( pooling effect sizes from single-group studies)

- **ROBINS-I**: Risk Of Bias In Non-randomised Studies of Interventions
