## Why Ingested
[[implants/isq/marconcini-2018-high-insertion-torque-bone-resorption-rct]]이 ≥50 Ncm를 "고토크 위험 임계값"으로 설정했으나, >55 Ncm 구간에서 실제 변연골 손실(Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)이 어느 정도인지를 다른 임플란트 시스템(NobelActive)과 후향적 설계로 검증한 연구가 없었음. Aldahlawi 2018은 113개 NobelActive 임플란트에서 IT >55 Ncm vs ≤55 Ncm의 정량적 MBL 차이를 보고하며, 하악 피질골 취약성을 별도 분석한 추가 근거.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective cohort study (113 NobelActive implants, 66 patients) grouping achieved insertion torque (삽입 토크, IT) into high (>55 Ncm, mean 67.35 Ncm) vs control (≤55 Ncm, mean 37.9 Ncm) to measure crestal bone loss (변연골 소실, MBL).

High-IT group showed significantly greater MBL (0.95±1.60 mm vs 0.18±0.68 mm, P<0.05); mandibular sites were the highest-risk subgroup with 2.12±1.99 mm vs maxillary 0.25±0.65 mm; all 6 implant failures occurred in the high-IT group (overall survival 94.6%).

This study corroborates Marconcini 2018 in a different implant system (NobelActive vs Blossom™ groove design) and refines the harm threshold to >55 Ncm, with the mandible-specific effect pointing to cortical bone density as the key mediator.

고토크군 MBL 유의하게 큼(0.95mm vs 0.18mm, P<0.05); 하악 고토크군 2.12mm vs 상악 0.25mm; 임플란트 실패 6개 전부 고토크군(전체 생존율 94.6%).

Marconcini 2018을 다른 임플란트 시스템(NobelActive)으로 보강하며, >55 Ncm에서 하악 피질골 취약성이 주요 매개 인자임을 시사.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Establishes IT >55 Ncm as a clinically meaningful harm threshold using NobelActive implants — extends Marconcini 2018's ≥50 Ncm RCT evidence to a different implant system and design
- Quantifies crestal bone loss: Group A (>55 Ncm) mean 0.95±1.60 mm vs Group B (≤55 Ncm) mean 0.18±0.68 mm — statistically significant (P<0.05)
- Identifies the **mandible as the highest-risk site**: Group A mandibular MBL 2.12±1.99 mm vs maxillary 0.25±0.65 mm (P<0.05) — 8× greater harm in dense cortical bone
- Provides survival data: 94.6% overall survival at follow-up; 6 implants failed (all in Group A)
- Confirms that mean IT in high-torque clinical practice (Group A mean 67.35±4.0 Ncm) produces measurable crestal bone remodeling harm beyond the ≥50 Ncm threshold

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design:** Retrospective cohort with post-hoc grouping by achieved IT
- **n:** 113 NobelActive implants, 66 patients
- **Groups:**
  - Group A (High IT): IT >55 Ncm; mean 67.35±4.0 Ncm
  - Group B (Control): IT ≤55 Ncm; mean 37.9±12.62 Ncm
- **Implant system:** NobelActive (tapered, self-tapping; Nobel Biocare, Zurich, Switzerland) — same macro-geometry for all
- **Outcomes:**
  - Crestal bone loss (MBL, mm; radiographic)
  - Implant survival
  - Site-specific analysis (mandible vs maxilla)
- **Statistical method:** P<0.05 significance threshold; test not specified

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design: IT grouping post-hoc, not pre-randomized; confounders (bone density, site selection, operator skill) not fully controlled
- NobelActive-specific: tapered self-tapping design has high inherent insertion resistance; findings may not generalize to cylindrical or other tapered implant systems
- No standardized follow-up duration reported; MBL measurement timepoint unclear
- Small and unequal group sizes not reported (n per group not given)
- No ISQ or secondary stability data
- No stratification by bone density type (D1–D4), which would clarify the mandibular vulnerability mechanism
- No COI disclosure limits industry-bias assessment

## 6. Related Work
- Marconcini 2018 [[implants/isq/marconcini-2018-high-insertion-torque-bone-resorption-rct]]: RCT with ≥50 Ncm threshold; Aldahlawi corroborates and extends with >55 Ncm in a different implant system
- Faot 2019 [[implants/isq/faot-2019-insertion-torque-mandibular-overdenture]]: contradicts — G4 ≥45 Ncm best survival in narrow implants; different population (edentulous atrophic mandible) and implant design
- Lemos 2020 [[implants/isq/lemos-2020-high-insertion-torque-dental-implants-sr-ma]]: SR+MA showing no significant MBL difference at ≥50 Ncm; Aldahlawi's >55 Ncm finding suggests the harm threshold may lie between 50–55 Ncm
- Trisi 2011 [[implants/isq/trisi-2011-high-low-implant-torque-histology-sheep]]: no necrosis at 110 Ncm in animal model; not incompatible if Aldahlawi's MBL reflects remodeling, not necrosis

## 7. Glossary
- **NobelActive (노벨액티브):** Tapered, tri-fluted self-tapping implant (Nobel Biocare); the fluted body design increases bone compression during seating, making high IT easier to achieve — and potentially amplifying overtorque harm
- **Crestal bone loss / MBL (변연골 소실):** Radiographic measurement of bone height change at the implant-abutment interface over time; primary surrogate for peri-implant tissue health
- **Group A mean IT 67.35 Ncm:** The real-world "overshoot" torque achieved when clinicians drive NobelActive implants past the 55 Ncm clinical threshold in dense mandibular bone
