## Summary

Prospective pharmacokinetic study (n=14, 41 procedures; Anesth Analg 2016) establishing that the 6 μg/mL threshold for mild lidocaine toxicity can be reliably avoided at doses up to 28 mg/kg (without liposuction) or 45 mg/kg (with liposuction) of tumescent lidocaine anesthesia.

Strong linear relationship between mg/kg dose and peak serum concentration Cmax (r²=0.85 without liposuction). Delayed absorption kinetics — Tmax ~13h — fundamentally explain why large tumescent doses are safe despite far exceeding the FDA's 7 mg/kg historical limit.

Toxicity threshold definition: 6 μg/mL = mild toxicity onset (lightheadedness, perioral numbness, tinnitus, blurred vision, ataxia); higher concentrations = seizures, cardiac arrest.

## Key Data

- **Toxicity threshold**: 6 μg/mL (mild lidocaine toxicity onset)
- **FDA 7 mg/kg limit**: established 1948 for epidural, no supporting PK data for infiltration
- **Cmax vs dose**: linear, r²=0.85 (without liposuction)
- **Tmax**: ~13.1h without liposuction; ~12.5h with liposuction
- **Safe dose without liposuction**: 28 mg/kg (risk of Cmax ≥6 μg/mL: <1/2000)
- **Safe dose with liposuction**: 45 mg/kg (risk: <1/2000)
- **AUC without liposuction** (at 45 mg/kg): 56.2 μg·h/mL
- **AUC with liposuction** (at 45 mg/kg): 40.7 μg·h/mL (28% lower)
- Liposuction removes ~28% lidocaine before systemic absorption
- No subject exceeded Cmax 4.4 μg/mL (all below 6 μg/mL threshold)
