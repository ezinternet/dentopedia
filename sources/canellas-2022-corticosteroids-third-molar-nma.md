## Why Ingested

Supersedes [[drug/analgesics/markiewicz-2008-corticosteroids-reduce-postoperative-morbidity-after-third-molar-surgery]]: this NMA (61 RCTs, 3561 subjects) compares 5 corticosteroids by drug, dose, and route — identifying dexamethasone 8mg as the optimal agent — while markiewicz-2008 only pooled "CS vs no-CS" across 12 RCTs without comparing agents. Also contradicts markiewicz-2008's "no pain benefit" finding for corticosteroids.

## Three-line Summary

Frequentist NMA (61 RCTs, 3561 subjects; Embase/PubMed/Cochrane; 5 corticosteroids: dexamethasone, betamethasone, methylprednisolone, prednisolone, triamcinolone) assessing comparative effectiveness for reducing inflammatory complications (edema, pain, trismus) after mandibular third molar surgery.

Dexamethasone 8mg via submucosal injection (oedema: −3.58 [−6.98; −0.17]) and pterygomandibular injection (−3.56 [−6.30; −0.82]) significantly outperformed placebo; dexamethasone 8mg submucosal also significantly reduced pain on post-op days 1 and 2 (−30.95 and −15.25 VAS units); ranking analysis confirmed dexamethasone 8mg submucosal and oral tablets as highest probability best options.

Dexamethasone 8mg is the preferred perioperative corticosteroid for third molar surgery — submucosal injection for maximum edema and pain control; no serious adverse effects across all corticosteroids reviewed.

## 세줄요약

빈도주의 네트워크 메타분석 (NMA) (61 RCT, 3561명; 5종 코르티코스테로이드 비교) — 하악 제3대구치 발치 후 염증성 합병증(부종·통증·개구제한) 감소를 위한 코르티코스테로이드 비교효과 평가.

덱사메타손 (Dexamethasone) 8mg 점막하주사 (−3.58 [−6.98; −0.17]) 및 익돌하악주사 (−3.56 [−6.30; −0.82]) 가 위약 대비 부종 유의 감소; 점막하 덱사메타손 8mg은 술 후 1·2일차 통증도 유의 감소(각 −30.95 및 −15.25 VAS); 순위분석에서 덱사메타손 8mg 점막하·경구가 최선.

임상 결론: 제3대구치 수술 주위수술기 코르티코스테로이드는 덱사메타손 (Dexamethasone) 8mg 점막하주사 또는 경구투여가 최적; 전체 코르티코스테로이드에서 심각한 부작용 없음.

## Key Contributions

- Largest NMA comparing 5 corticosteroids by agent, dose, and route for third molar surgery (61 RCTs, 3561 subjects)
- First NMA to identify dexamethasone 8mg as the agent with highest ranking probability for both edema and pain
- Contradicts prior CS-binary syntheses (including markiewicz-2008): submucosal dexamethasone 8mg also significantly reduces pain on post-op days 1 and 2
- Confirms submucosal and oral tablets as most effective routes for dexamethasone

## Methodology

- **Design**: Frequentist network meta-analysis
- **Databases**: Embase, PubMed, Cochrane Library (no language restrictions)
- **Inclusion**: RCTs only
- **Corticosteroids compared**: Dexamethasone, betamethasone, methylprednisolone, prednisolone, triamcinolone (multiple doses + routes)
- **Outcomes**: Oedema, pain (VAS), trismus
- **Sample**: 61 studies, 3561 subjects
- **Ranking**: P-scores for best treatment probability

## Key Results

**Oedema reduction:**
- Dexamethasone 8mg submucosal: −3.58 (95% CI −6.98; −0.17) vs placebo — significant
- Dexamethasone 8mg pterygomandibular: −3.56 (95% CI −6.30; −0.82) — significant
- Ranking: Dexamethasone 8mg submucosal and oral highest p-scores (0.71/0.75)

**Pain reduction:**
- Dexamethasone 8mg submucosal: −30.95 VAS on day 1 (95% CI −43.41; −18.49) — significant
- Dexamethasone 8mg submucosal: −15.25 VAS on day 2 (95% CI −23.27; −7.22) — significant
- No other corticosteroid showed significant pain reduction vs placebo

**Adverse events:** No serious adverse effects across all corticosteroid types or routes
