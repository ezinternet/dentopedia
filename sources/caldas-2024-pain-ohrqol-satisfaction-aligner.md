## Why Ingested

Fills PRO/satisfaction gap: first Brazilian cross-sectional study quantifying OHIP-14 in ClearCorrect adult patients with validated pain scoring. Directly addresses surveillance target "patient-reported outcomes / OHRQoL 2024–2026." → [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/caldas-2024-pain-ohrqol-satisfaction-aligner]]

## Three-line Summary

Cross-sectional study of 94 Brazilian adults using ClearCorrect clear aligners measuring OHIP-14, pain (VAS), and treatment satisfaction simultaneously. Moderate pain was the only significant predictor of higher OHIP-14 impairment (p=0.036); overall OHIP-14 mean was low (3.36/56). High satisfaction was maintained regardless of pain level.

## 세줄요약

94명 브라질 성인(ClearCorrect) 단면연구에서 OHIP-14 평균 3.36(56점 만점)으로 삶의 질 영향 매우 낮음. 중간 강도 통증만 OHIP-14 유의하게 상승(p=0.036); 저강도 통증은 유의하지 않음. 통증 수준에 관계없이 전반적 치료만족도는 높게 유지됨.

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Cross-sectional observational study
- **Journal**: Brazilian Oral Research (2024), DOI 10.1186/s40510-024-00514-6
- **PMID/PMCID**: 38679672 / PMC11056347
- **Sample**: n=94 adults (mean age not specified in abstract), ClearCorrect aligners, clinic in Brazil
- **Instrument**: OHIP-14 (14-item Oral Health Impact Profile, 0–56 scale); VAS pain; Global Satisfaction rating

## 2. Key Findings

- OHIP-14 mean: 3.36 (SD not extracted) — low overall OHRQoL impact
- Pain predictor: moderate pain → significantly higher OHIP-14 (p=0.036); mild pain → NS
- Functional limitation domain: most affected OHIP-14 subscale
- Treatment satisfaction: high across all pain groups — pain severity did not reduce satisfaction
- Social well-being and confidence domains: minimal impairment

## 3. Methodology

Cross-sectional; non-probabilistic convenience sample; in-clinic administration of validated questionnaires. No control arm. OHIP-14 Brazilian Portuguese validated version. Pain scored by VAS, categorized mild/moderate/severe. Descriptive statistics; Kruskal-Wallis with post-hoc for OHIP-14 by pain category; Fisher's exact test for categorical outcomes.

## 4. Clinical Implications

Moderate pain during clear aligner treatment translates into measurable OHRQoL impairment; clinicians should proactively manage pain at aligner changes to protect quality of life. Satisfaction remains high — patient communication about expected pain may improve experience further.

## 5. Limitations

Cross-sectional design (no longitudinal trajectory); single treatment system (ClearCorrect only); convenience sample; treatment phase at survey not standardized (could be initial or final).

## 7. Glossary

- **OHIP-14**: 14-item Oral Health Impact Profile — validated PRO measuring oral-health-related QoL on 7 dimensions; range 0–56, higher = greater impact
- **VAS**: Visual Analogue Scale for pain intensity (0–100 mm)
- **ClearCorrect**: clear aligner brand (Straumann Group)
