## One-line Summary
Retrospective cohort (n=32 posterior immediate implants, 2-year): reactive soft tissue primary closure maintained bone/soft tissue levels; KM reduced 0.56 mm in intact-soft-tissue group A vs gained 0.67 mm in both-defect group B (p=0.009).

## 한줄요약
후향 코호트 (n=32 후방부 즉시식립, 2년): 반응성 연조직 1차 봉합 시 골·연조직 유지; KMW는 Group A(연조직 온전) −0.56mm vs Group B(연조직+골 결손) +0.67mm (p=0.009).

## 2. Key Contributions
- Extends reactive soft tissue primary closure technique (Liu 2019) to sockets with BOTH buccal bone defect AND mucogingival recession (Group B) — more challenging clinical scenario
- Demonstrates KM gain (+0.67 mm) in Group B (soft+hard defect) vs KM loss in Group A (bone defect only, intact soft tissue)
- 2-year bone and soft tissue maintenance in both groups
- Buccal bone dimensional changes at 6 months comparable between groups
- Supports utility of reactive tissue as an augmentation material even in dual-defect sites

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Study design: Retrospective cohort
- Population: 32 posterior immediate implants
  - Group A (n=17): intact soft tissue + buccal bone dehiscence only
  - Group B (n=15): both soft tissue recession + buccal bone dehiscence
- Both groups: reactive soft tissue from extraction socket used for primary wound closure
- Follow-up: 6 months (CBCT for buccal bone) and 2 years (clinical soft tissue assessment)
- Outcome: buccal bone dimensions (6 months), KM width and bone/soft tissue levels (2 years)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Buccal bone dimensional changes at 6 months: generally comparable between Group A and B
- KM at 6 months: Group A −0.56 mm, Group B +0.67 mm (p=0.009)
- 2-year follow-up: bone and soft tissue levels well maintained in both groups
- No reported implant failures during study period

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Retrospective design; no comparator group without reactive tissue closure
- Small sample sizes (n=17 and n=15)
- Group B definition relies on pre-op mucosal assessment; staging subjective
- No standardized infection severity reporting
- Long-term data beyond 2 years not available

## 7. Glossary
- **Group A**: Intact buccal soft tissue + buccal bone dehiscence only
- **Group B**: Both buccal bone dehiscence AND mucogingival recession (dual defect)
- **KM**: Keratinized mucosa
- **Reactive soft tissue**: granulation tissue + long junctional epithelium in compromised socket — used as primary closure flap material
