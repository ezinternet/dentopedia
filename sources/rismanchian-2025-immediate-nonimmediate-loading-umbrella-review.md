## Why Ingested

기존 [[implants/benic-2014-loading-protocols-single-implant-crowns-sr-ma]]는 11개 RCT SR+MA(2012년까지)로 단일임플란트 즉시 vs 통상 로딩의 동등성을 정립했지만, 이후 10여 년간 발표된 근거를 반영하지 못한다. 본 umbrella review(24개 SR, 8,063명·18,373 임플란트)는 최신 SR/MA들을 정성적으로 종합해 동일 결론(생존율 동등, 초기 MBL만 IL이 약간 높음)이 장기적으로도 유지됨을 재확인하는 보강 근거로 활용.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review synthesizing 24 systematic reviews/meta-analyses (cumulative ~8,063 patients, 18,373 implants) comparing immediate loading (IL) vs nonimmediate/delayed loading (NIL) of single-tooth implants, follow-up ≥6 months (search through August 2024).

Both protocols showed high long-term survival (IL 92–97.8% vs NIL 95–99%, not statistically different); IL showed slightly greater marginal bone loss (0.2–1 mm vs 0.1–0.8 mm, NS) especially in year 1; peri-implant soft-tissue health and prosthetic-complication rates were comparable between groups.

IL is a viable option when high primary implant stability is achieved, but NIL remains preferable for patients with compromised bone quality or high-risk conditions; qualitative (not pooled quantitative) synthesis across heterogeneous SRs limits precision of the umbrella-level estimate.

## 세줄요약

24개 체계적 문헌고찰/메타분석(누적 환자 약 8,063명, 임플란트 18,373개)을 종합한 우산리뷰 (umbrella review): 단일치아 임플란트의 즉시로딩 (Immediate Loading, IL) vs 비즉시(지연)로딩 (Nonimmediate/Delayed Loading, NIL) 비교, 추적 ≥6개월 (2024년 8월까지 검색).

두 프로토콜 모두 높은 장기 생존율을 보였으나(IL 92–97.8% vs NIL 95–99%, 통계적 유의차 없음), IL은 특히 1년차에 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)이 약간 더 컸다(0.2–1 mm vs 0.1–0.8 mm, 유의차 없음); 임플란트주위 연조직 건강·보철 합병증도 양군 간 유사.

IL은 높은 초기안정성 (Primary Stability)이 확보될 때 유효한 선택지이나, 골질이 불량하거나 고위험 조건의 환자에서는 NIL이 여전히 우선 고려된다; 이질적 SR들을 정량적 메타분석이 아닌 정성적으로 종합했다는 방법론적 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review specifically restricted to single-implant (not multiple/full-arch) restorations comparing IL vs NIL, pooling evidence from 24 prior SRs/MAs published over the past two decades.
- Consolidates survival-rate data across many prior meta-analyses (mostly non-significant differences, several ORs/RRs near 1.0), giving a qualitative "meta-summary" of the field's overall direction of effect.
- Reports MBL, prosthetic-complication, and soft-tissue outcomes side-by-side across included SRs to identify where evidence converges vs conflicts.
- Notes one included analysis (ref [4]) found significantly higher IL failure risk (RR 4.76, 95% CI 1.74–13.02) even with nonocclusal provisional crowns — flagged as an outlier/case-selection-dependent finding rather than the consensus.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review (SR of SRs/MAs) following PRISMA.
- **Databases**: PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, Science Direct, ProQuest, Cochrane Library, Magiran, MOH Articles + grey literature (Open Grey, WorldCat, ProQuest dissertations, MOH Thesis); search through end of August 2024.
- **Inclusion**: SRs/MAs directly comparing IL vs NIL in single-tooth implants, English or Farsi, follow-up ≥6 months.
- **Exclusion**: multiple-implant/full-arch studies, case reports/series, expert opinion, observational studies without SR/MA design.
- **n**: PRISMA flow reports 768 register + 12 database records → 371 screened → 68 sought → 54 assessed → 48 included studies of included reviews; Results text states 24 systematic reviews were the primary umbrella-review unit, cumulative sample size 8,063 patients / 18,373 implants (note: the paper's own PRISMA diagram (n=48) and Results narrative (n=24) are not fully reconciled — reported as-is).
- **Quality assessment**: 3 independent reviewers scored each included SR on randomization, recency (10–20y), ≥6-month follow-up, geographic diversity, and MA rigor (effect size, heterogeneity, sensitivity); disagreements resolved by a 4th reviewer. Risk-of-bias tiers: 1–3 criteria met = high risk, 4–5 = moderate, 6–7 = low.
- **Outcomes**: implant survival rate, marginal bone loss, peri-implant soft-tissue health, prosthetic complications, patient satisfaction.
- **Analysis**: qualitative narrative synthesis (no new quantitative pooling across the 24 SRs).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Survival**: IL 92%–97.8%, NIL 95%–99% over follow-up; difference not statistically significant (P > 0.05) in the large majority of pooled comparisons cited (e.g., OR 0.75, 95% CI 0.32–1.76 at 1 year; OR 0.75, 95% CI 0.33–4.80 at 2 years; RR 1.00, 95% CI 0.97–1.03, P = 1.00 in one large MA). One outlier analysis found significantly higher IL failure (RR 4.76, 95% CI 1.74–13.02, P = 0.002) and another found significantly lower IL survival (RR 0.974, 95% CI 0.954–0.994, P = 0.012).
- **Placement/loading-timing subgroup data** (from one included MA, ref [21]): IPIL 99.6%, IPDL 97.9%, EPEL 100%, EPDL 95.8%, DPIL 99.1%, DPEL 92.4%, DPDL 100% (all with wide/overlapping 95% CIs).
- **Marginal bone loss**: IL 0.2–1 mm vs NIL 0.1–0.8 mm; difference not statistically significant (P > 0.05).
- **Prosthetic complications** (crown loosening, fracture): similar rates, no significant difference (P > 0.05).
- **Soft tissue / patient satisfaction**: reported as similar between protocols across included SRs.
- Illustrative primary study cited: Gjelvold et al. 5-year RCT — IL 100% vs NIL 95.8% survival, no significant MBL/esthetic difference (matches [[immediate-implant/loading-protocol/gjelvold-2021-immediate-delayed-loading-single-tooth-5year-rct]] already in this wiki).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Umbrella review of SRs performs only qualitative (narrative) synthesis — no new pooled quantitative estimate across the 24 SRs, so heterogeneity across the underlying meta-analyses (different populations, follow-up durations, IL definitions) is not statistically reconciled.
- Internal inconsistency between the PRISMA flow diagram (48 included studies) and the Results narrative (24 systematic reviews as the analytic unit) is not clarified in the text.
- Individual outlier findings (e.g., RR 4.76 IL failure risk) are mentioned but not deeply reconciled against the overall "no significant difference" narrative — risk of cherry-picking bias in a qualitative summary.
- Authors call for further comparative research on economic implications, standardized IL stability thresholds, and evidence-based patient-selection guidelines.

## 6. Related Work

- Benic 2014 SR+MA (11 RCTs, single-implant crowns): established IL≈NIL equivalence for survival/MBL under adequate primary stability — this umbrella review reinforces that conclusion with more recent, broader evidence.
- Gjelvold 2021 5-year RCT: cited directly in this review's Literature Review section as a key long-term comparative trial.
- Zhao et al. 2022 SR+MA (10 RCTs, cited as ref [33]): also found no significant IL vs NIL difference in implant failure or MBL — an included/cited study rather than distinct wiki page currently.
- Pigozzo et al. 2018 SR+MA on immediate vs early loading (cited ref [31]): complementary comparison (IL vs early loading rather than IL vs delayed).

## 7. Glossary

- **IL**: Immediate Loading — prosthesis placed within 48 hours of implant surgery.
- **NIL**: Nonimmediate (Delayed) Loading — prosthesis placed after a 3–6 month osseointegration period.
- **MBL**: Marginal Bone Loss — radiographic peri-implant crestal bone loss, key long-term outcome marker.
- **IPIL/IPDL/EPEL/EPDL/DPIL/DPEL/DPDL**: Placement-Loading timing combinations (Immediate/Early/Delayed Placement × Immediate/Early/Delayed Loading) used in one cited sub-analysis.
- **PRISMA**: Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses — reporting guideline followed for the umbrella review flow.
