## Why Ingested

Surveillance sweep for peri-implantitis reconstructive surgery. This RCT tests the same adjunct (electrolytic cleaning, EC) as [[implants/peri-implantitis/regidor-2026-electrolytic-decontamination-peri-implantitis-rct]] but with autogenous tuberosity bone rather than xenograft, so it adds a second independent trial on whether EC helps reconstructive therapy.

## Three-line Summary

RCT (n = 31 patients with peri-implantitis): surgical reconstruction with autogenous tuberosity bone graft and mechanical debridement, with (test) or without (control) adjunctive electrolytic cleaning (EC); 12-month follow-up.

Both groups improved significantly in PPD, GI and BOP with no between-group differences; radiographic mesial defect fill was greater with EC (4.6 +/- 1.10 mm vs 2.5 +/- 1.77 mm; p = 0.002), while intraoperative distal fill at 6 months did not differ (4.7 +/- 1.41 vs 3.8 +/- 1.72 mm; p = 0.069).

Autogenous reconstruction gave clinical and radiographic improvement in both arms and EC added no clinical benefit; no histology, so re-osseointegration is unconfirmed (abstract-level reading).

## 세줄요약

RCT (peri-implantitis 환자 31명): 자가 상악결절 골이식 + 기계적 디브라이드먼트에 전해질 세정 (Electrolytic Cleaning, EC) 추가 여부(시험군/대조군) 비교, 12개월 추적.

양군 모두 탐침깊이 (Probing Pocket Depth, PPD), 치은지수 (Gingival Index, GI), 탐침 시 출혈 (Bleeding on Probing, BOP)이 유의하게 개선되었고 군간 차이는 없었다. 방사선 근심측 결손 충전은 EC군이 더 컸으나 (4.6 +/- 1.10 mm vs 2.5 +/- 1.77 mm; p = 0.002), 6개월 술중 원심측 충전은 차이가 없었다 (4.7 vs 3.8 mm; p = 0.069).

두 군 모두 임상·방사선학적 개선을 보였고 EC의 추가 임상 이득은 없었다. 조직학 자료가 없어 재골유착 (re-osseointegration)은 확인되지 않았다 (초록 수준 해석).

## 2. Key Contributions
- RCT of autogenous tuberosity bone reconstruction for peri-implantitis with or without electrolytic cleaning (EC).
- No clinical benefit of EC (PPD, GI, BOP, suppuration) at 12 months.
- Greater radiographic mesial defect fill with EC (p = 0.002), but not distal or intraoperative fill.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Randomized controlled clinical trial (abstract-level).
- **n**: 31 patients with peri-implantitis.
- **Groups**: control = mechanical debridement + bone grafting; test = same + EC.
- **Outcomes**: PPD at mesial, buccal, distal, lingual sites; GI; BOP (baseline and 12 months); radiographic defect fill at mesial and distal sites (baseline and 1 year); intraoperative bone fill at four sites on the day of surgery and 6 months post-surgery.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- PPD reduction significant at all sites in both groups; GI and BOP improved versus baseline in both; no between-group differences for these parameters.
- Radiographic mesial defect fill: test 4.6 mm (+/- 1.10) vs control 2.5 mm (+/- 1.77); p = 0.002.
- Intraoperative distal fill at 6 months: test 4.7 mm (+/- 1.41) vs control 3.8 mm (+/- 1.72); p = 0.069 (not significant).
- Suppuration resolved completely in both groups at 1 year (p < 0.001), no group difference.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No histological data (could not be obtained in humans), so re-osseointegration is unconfirmed.
- Abstract-only ingest: sample-size justification, randomization, blinding, implant/defect characteristics, and complications are not available.
