## Why Ingested

Repeated abutment-screw tightening during provisional and try-in phases is routine, but the wiki's screw pages mostly address settling and retightening timing, not what repeated cycles do to the screw itself. This in vitro study adds reverse-torque trajectories across 10-20 cycles plus micro-CT deformation. It qualifies the "settling plateaus after the second tightening" finding of [[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/saleh-saber-2017-repeated-torque-tightening-abutment-lengths]] and confirms the universal preload loss catalogued in [[prosthetic-materials/abutment-screw/nithyapriya-2018-factors-loss-preload-dental-implants]].

## Three-line Summary

In vitro bench study of two ISO 14801-certified titanium abutment screws (gold-plated hexalobular Gold-Tite/ZIMVIE vs anodized star-shaped Vega/Klockner), 6 groups of n = 5 screws, tightened 10 or 20 cycles at 20-40 N·cm into a new dummy implant per screw, with reverse torque value (RTV) recorded every cycle and micro-CT (n = 3 per group) of screw geometry.

RTV was below the applied torque in 30/30 screws (73-89%; median 87%); RTV fell significantly per cycle in the Vega/Klockner screws (-0.17 N·cm/cycle, p = 0.006) but not in the Gold-Tite screws (-0.11, p = 0.302); micro-CT showed head-localized area change with the mean cross-sectional area essentially unchanged, and in Gold-Tite only after 20 cycles (max head area about +8%).

Preload loss is intrinsic to screw tightening and RTV behaviour under cycling is system-dependent, so abutment screws should be treated as limited-use components; between-protocol contrasts were underpowered (n = 5) and micro-CT was descriptive only (n = 3), so no numeric replacement threshold is supported.

## 세줄요약

체외 실험(ISO 14801 인증 티타늄 지대주 나사 2종: 금도금 육각형 헤드 Gold-Tite/ZIMVIE vs 양극산화 별모양 헤드 Vega/Klockner; 6군 각 n = 5; 나사마다 새 더미 임플란트; 20-40 N·cm로 10 또는 20회 조임), 매 회 역회전 토크값 (Reverse Torque Value, RTV) 측정 및 마이크로 CT (micro-CT, n = 군당 3) 분석.

RTV는 30/30개 나사에서 적용 토크보다 낮았고(73-89%, 중앙값 87%), Vega/Klockner에서는 회당 -0.17 N·cm로 유의하게 감소(p = 0.006)했으나 Gold-Tite에서는 유의하지 않았다(-0.11, p = 0.302); micro-CT에서는 평균 단면적은 거의 불변이고 헤드 부위에 국한된 변화가 관찰되었으며, Gold-Tite는 20회 과사용 조건에서만 헤드 최대 단면적 약 +8% 증가했다.

프리로드 손실은 나사 조임에 본질적이고 반복 조임에 따른 RTV 거동은 시스템 의존적이므로 지대주 나사는 제한 사용 부품으로 다뤄야 하나, 군간 비교는 검정력 부족(n = 5)이고 micro-CT는 기술적 분석(n = 3)에 그쳐 구체적 교체 기준 수치는 제시할 수 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Models RTV as a trajectory across repeated cycles (linear mixed-effects model, screw as random effect) rather than a single endpoint.
- Combines torque magnitude (20/30/40 N·cm) and cycle count (10 vs 20) in a factorial design within each of two screw systems.
- Adds micro-CT morphometry (Otsu segmentation, section-by-section deformation vs unloaded reference) that localizes screw change to the head region.
- Uses a new dummy implant for every screw, removing the implant-thread wear confound identified in the authors' earlier work.
- Frames abutment screws as limited-use (consumable) components, without proposing numeric thresholds.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: In vitro, single experienced operator, calibrated cordless electronic torque wrench (NSK iSD900), RTV by calibrated digital torque screwdriver, implants embedded in acrylic at 90 degrees. Repeat tightening at the same torque was applied 10 min after the final cycle.
- **Groups (n = 5 screws each)**: Gold-Tite/ZIMVIE (recommended 20 N·cm): D1 10 x 20, D2 20 x 20 (over-use), D3 10 x 30 (over-torque). Vega/Klockner (recommended 30 N·cm): V1 10 x 30, V2 20 x 30 (over-use), V3 10 x 40 (over-torque).
- **Hypotheses**: H01 RTV equals applied torque; H02 RTV unaffected by torque or cycles; H03 no screw-type effect; H04 no plastic deformation (descriptive).
- **Statistics**: screw is the experimental unit; mixed-effects model per system; Mann-Whitney U on per-screw mean RTV (final-cycle RTV as sensitivity check); Wilcoxon signed-rank for RTV vs insertion torque.
- **Micro-CT**: GE Phoenix v|tome|x m, 19.9 um voxel; 2 unused reference screws per system plus 3 screws per group; the two most coronal (screwdriver-engagement) sections excluded a priori; deformation = % change in cross-sectional area vs reference section by section; descriptive with Cohen's d.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **RTV vs insertion torque**: 30/30 screws lower (median 87%; group medians 80-90%); Wilcoxon p = 0.062 because all screws fell on one side (reported as directionally uniform, not a significance test).
- **Per-screw mean RTV, group median (N·cm)**: Gold-Tite 10 x 20 = 16.40, 20 x 20 = 17.55, 10 x 30 = 26.80; Vega 10 x 30 = 26.65, 20 x 30 = 26.56, 10 x 40 = 32.77.
- **RTV trend across cycles**: Vega/Klockner -0.17 N·cm/cycle (95% CI -0.29 to -0.05; p = 0.006); Gold-Tite -0.11 (95% CI -0.31 to +0.10; p = 0.302), with greater between-screw variability.
- **Between-protocol contrasts**: Vega 10 x 30 vs 20 x 30 p = 0.84; Vega vs Gold-Tite at 10 x 30 p = 1.00; Vega 10 x 30 vs 10 x 40 and Gold-Tite 10 x 20 vs 20 x 20 both p = 0.008 but fragile (complete separation of 5 vs 5; p = 0.29 on final-cycle RTV) and labelled exploratory.
- **Micro-CT, Vega/Klockner (n = 3/group)**: mean-area change +3.64% (10 x 30), +1.66% (20 x 30), +4.55% (10 x 40); Cohen's d 1.9 (20 vs 30 N·cm), 2.9 (20 vs 40), 0.9 (30 vs 40); non-monotonic cycle effect; about 22 screws per group would be needed to test adjacent-torque differences.
- **Micro-CT, Gold-Tite (n = 3/group)**: mean area essentially unchanged (-0.4 to -0.8%); maximum head area per Table 5: -0.74% (10 x 20), -1.04% (10 x 30), +8.42% (20 x 20; specimen range +4.3 to +13.1%); Cohen's d 0.08 (10 x 20 vs 10 x 30), 2.38 and 2.46 for contrasts involving 20 x 20.
- **Source-text inconsistency**: the narrative of Section 3.3 gives recommended/over-torque head change as +0.3% and +0.1% and over-use as +8.2%, whereas Table 5 and Section 3.4 give -0.74/-1.04/+8.42 (and -0.7/-1.0/+8.4). The Table 5 values are used here.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- n = 5 screws per group (between-group contrasts underpowered) and n = 3 per group for micro-CT (descriptive only).
- Dummy implants (same titanium alloy, no external surface treatment) and a single shared abutment per group, so abutment interface wear cannot be fully excluded.
- No functional cyclic loading, so fatigue behaviour is not captured; no tribometry or SEM to support the surface-roughness explanation for the anodized-screw result (speculative).
- Electronic torque wrench only; findings do not transfer directly to manual drivers or to operator-dependent torque delivery.
- Future work: cyclic loading, dedicated assemblies per specimen, tribometry, larger samples, multi-operator designs, Morse-taper/conical connections, and relation of repeated-tightening behaviour to certification testing.

## 6. Related Work
- Batista et al. (earlier stereoscopic microscopy and micro-CT study): this paper extends the qualitative deformation findings and fixes the reused-implant confound.
- Guzaitis et al.: more than 10 insertion cycles reduced RTV and altered thread morphology; not held in the wiki.
- Alnasser et al.: no difference between double tightening with or without 10-min interval; argues for protocol standardization; not held in the wiki.
- Winkler et al. and Alfaifi et al.: settling effect and comparable RTV/insertion-torque ratios (cited by the authors).

## 7. Glossary
- **RTV (Reverse Torque Value, 역회전 토크값)**: torque needed to initiate reverse rotation of the abutment screw; used as a proxy for retained preload.
- **Preload (프리로드)**: clamping force generated at the implant-abutment interface by elastic deformation of the screw joint.
- **Settling effect (안착 효과)**: microplastic flattening of surface asperities that dissipates part of the applied torque.
- **Over-use / over-torque**: 20 cycles at the recommended torque / 10 cycles above the recommended torque.
- **Micro-CT (마이크로 CT)**: micro-computed tomography, here with Otsu thresholding for cross-sectional area.
