## Why Ingested

Landmark study demonstrating catastrophic LTD failure of a commercially available zirconia (Prettau) under accelerated aging — provides the most dramatic evidence for why LTD is a clinically relevant failure mechanism. Essential evidence base for [[dental-materials/zirconia/chopra-2024-mechanical-characteristics-zirconia-dentistry]] claims about brand-dependent LTD susceptibility.

## Three-line Summary

In-vitro study (3 Y-TZP types: Prettau, Zirprime, ZirTough; thin bar specimens 22×3×0.2mm, n=55/brand), aged at 134°C/0.2MPa (200h) and 180°C/1.0MPa (48h): Prettau fractured completely at 200h (134°C) and all specimens fractured during aging at 16h (180°C), while ZirTough showed lowest monoclinic content (31.4% vs 78.8% for Prettau at 200h).

Flexural strength correlated inversely with monoclinic phase fraction (Spearman ρ=-0.71 to -1.0 across all groups) — confirming monoclinic content as a valid LTD failure predictor.

ZirTough (a newer formulation) outperformed both commercial materials: 1243±101 MPa at 200h vs Prettau (all fractured) and Zirprime (779±137 MPa), suggesting formulation differences critically determine LTD resistance.

## 세줄요약

Y-TZP 3종(Prettau, Zirprime, ZirTough), 초박막 시편(22×3×0.2mm), 134°C/200h + 180°C/48h 수열 에이징: Prettau는 134°C/200h에서 전 시편 파절, 180°C에서 16h만에 전 시편 파절.

굴곡강도와 단사정 분율 간 역상관(Spearman ρ=-0.71~-1.0) — 단사정 함량이 LTD 파절 예측 지표로 유효.

ZirTough가 LTD 최저 저항: 200h 후 단사정 31.4%(Prettau 78.8% 대비) — 처방 차이가 LTD 저항을 결정적으로 좌우.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Demonstrates catastrophic LTD failure (complete specimen fracture) of Prettau (a commercially used dental zirconia) under accelerated aging
- Provides thin-bar protocol (0.2mm thickness) relevant to thin monolithic restorations
- Establishes strong inverse correlation between monoclinic content and flexural strength
- Shows two-temperature aging protocol reveals differential LTD susceptibility

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Thin bar specimens: 22×3×0.2mm, cut from sintered blocks per manufacturer specs
- Chemical composition verified by EDS
- Aging Protocol 1: 134°C/0.2MPa steam autoclave at 5, 50, 100, 150, 200h (n=5/group/brand)
- Aging Protocol 2: 180°C/1.0MPa at 8, 16, 24, 48h (n=5/group/brand)
- XRD monoclinic quantification; 4-point bend flexural strength (ASTM 1161-B); SEM fracture surface analysis
- 1-way ANOVA with heteroscedasticity-consistent SE; Spearman rank correlation FS vs monoclinic

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**134°C aging (Flexural Strength, MPa):**

| Brand | Baseline | 200h | Monoclinic at 200h |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prettau | 1328 ± 89.9 | All fractured | 78.8 ± 2.0% |
| Zirprime | 1041 ± 130 | 779 ± 137 | 74.8 ± 0.52% |
| ZirTough | 1436 ± 136 | 1243 ± 101 | 31.4 ± 4.4% |

**180°C aging:**
- Prettau: all fractured at 16h; monoclinic 79.0 ± 0.13%
- Zirprime: 595 ± 88.4 MPa at 48h; monoclinic 68.1 ± 4.4%
- ZirTough: 1068 ± 76.8 MPa at 48h; monoclinic 39.5 ± 5.56%
- SEM: transformed surface layer visible on fracture surfaces

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Thin bar (0.2mm) may not reflect clinical restoration dimensions
- No clinical follow-up
- Three zirconia brands only; not generalizable to 4Y/5Y-PSZ formulations (not yet commercially available in 2014)
- Fracture toughness not measured
