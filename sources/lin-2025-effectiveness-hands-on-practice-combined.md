## Why Ingested
Adds an adolescent fixed-appliance RCT on *how* oral hygiene is taught (hands-on typodont practice + app/video) rather than which brush is used, extending the device-focused orthodontic pages [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/aykol-sahin-2024-toothbrush-types-orthodontic-gingivitis]] and [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/liu-2026-powered-manual-toothbrushes-orthodontic-sr-ma]], and the education-format evidence in [[periodontics/oral-hygiene-instruction/choi-2017-dental-plaque-control-oral-hygiene-education-korea-sr]].

## Three-line Summary

Single-centre RCT (Xiamen, China; n=120 randomized, 113 analysed [57 vs 56]; adolescents 12-18 y in fixed orthodontic treatment): three ~60-min sessions over 12 weeks combining a custom mobile app/videos with typodont hands-on practice (plaque-disclosing feedback, Modified Bass) versus one 30-min verbal session plus pamphlet.

At 3-month follow-up the intervention group had lower plaque index (1.0 vs 1.7; difference -0.7, 95% CI -0.9 to -0.5) and gingival index (0.9 vs 1.5; difference -0.6), higher knowledge scores (17.9 vs 13.8) and lower OHIP-14 (10.8 vs 16.8), all P<0.001, with baseline plaque/gingival indices balanced (2.1 vs 2.0; 1.8 vs 1.7).

The bundled package is confounded with roughly six times more contact time (about 180 vs 30 min) and is unblinded for participants, follow-up is only 3 months, and several reporting features (see Limitations) warrant cautious weighting; treat as supportive, low-certainty evidence that structured practice-based education beats a single verbal session.

## 세줄요약

단일기관 무작위대조시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT; 중국 샤먼, 무작위 배정 120명·분석 113명 [57 vs 56], 고정식 교정 중인 12–18세 청소년): 맞춤 모바일 앱·동영상과 치열모형 (typodont) 실습 (치태염색 피드백, 수정 바스법)을 결합한 약 60분×3회 (12주) 교육 vs 30분 1회 구두설명 + 팸플릿.

3개월 추적에서 중재군이 치태지수 (Plaque Index, PI) 1.0 vs 1.7 (차이 −0.7, 95% CI −0.9 ~ −0.5), 치은지수 (Gingival Index, GI) 0.9 vs 1.5, 지식점수 17.9 vs 13.8, 구강건강영향지수 (OHIP-14) 10.8 vs 16.8로 모두 유리 (P<0.001); 기저 PI/GI는 균형 (2.1 vs 2.0; 1.8 vs 1.7).

패키지 효과가 접촉시간 (약 180분 vs 30분)과 뒤섞여 있고 참가자 눈가림이 불가하며 추적이 3개월뿐이고 보고상 의문점이 있어, 구조화된 실습형 교육이 1회 구두설명보다 낫다는 낮은 확실성의 지지 근거로만 가중할 것.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Oral Health Prev Dent 2025;23:711-720 (published 2025-11-26)
- **DOI**: 10.3290/j.ohpd.c_2325
- **Institution**: Hospital of Xiamen Medical College (Stomatology Hospital), China

## 2. Key Contributions
- Tests a three-session package (custom app with 3D appliance models, instructional videos, typodont practice with plaque-disclosing feedback, peer problem-solving) against standard single-session verbal instruction in adolescents with fixed appliances.
- Reports clinical (PI, GI), knowledge, OHIP-14 and self-reported behaviour outcomes to 3 months.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: parallel-group RCT, computer-generated sequence stratified by age (12–15, 16–18) and gender, sealed opaque envelopes; participants/educators unblinded, hygienist assessors and statistician blinded.
- **n**: 120 randomized (60/60); 113 analysed (57/56); retention 94.2%.
- **Intervention**: 3 × ~60 min at baseline, week 4, week 8; control 1 × 30 min verbal + pamphlet; both received the same hygiene kit.
- **Outcomes**: knowledge (20-item questionnaire), OHIP-14, Silness-Löe PI, Löe-Silness GI at baseline, 12 weeks, 3-month follow-up; satisfaction, self-reported practice frequency, app usage.
- **Analysis**: mixed-effects model (group × time), sample size 51/group for d=0.5, +15% inflation.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome (3-month) | Intervention (n=57) | Control (n=56) | Difference (95% CI) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | 17.9 ± 1.8 | 13.8 ± 2.5 | 4.1 (3.3 to 4.9) |
| OHIP-14 | 10.8 ± 3.9 | 16.8 ± 5.0 | −6.0 (−7.6 to −4.4) |
| PI | 1.0 ± 0.3 | 1.7 ± 0.5 | −0.7 (−0.9 to −0.5) |
| GI | 0.9 ± 0.3 | 1.5 ± 0.4 | −0.6 (−0.7 to −0.5) |

- Baseline PI 2.1 ± 0.5 vs 2.0 ± 0.6; GI 1.8 ± 0.4 vs 1.7 ± 0.5. Cohen's d at 3 months: knowledge 1.86, OHIP-14 1.39, PI 1.71, GI 1.63.
- Brushing at least twice daily 89.5% vs 62.5%; daily interdental aid use 77.2% vs 42.9% (both P<0.001, self-reported).
- Satisfaction 4.6 ± 0.5/5 (93% satisfied or very satisfied); app access 5.3 ± 1.8 times/week. No age or gender interaction; no adverse events.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Authors: 3-month follow-up only; single centre; no cost-effectiveness analysis; participants/educators unblinded; subgroup power limited.
- Contact time differs about sixfold between arms (about 180 vs 30 min), so the effect of *hands-on plus multimedia* cannot be separated from *more instruction*; no arm isolates hands-on alone or multimedia alone.
- Behaviour outcomes are self-reported; OHIP-14 is labelled an "attitude" measure although it captures oral-health-related quality of life.
- Reporting concerns noted from the text: the registry is named without a registration number; the ethics approval number (KS20250507001) appears to encode a May 2025 date, while enrolment is stated as January 2023–June 2024; the data-availability statement refers to "simulation experiment data"; the SDs are small and effect sizes very large (d 1.4–1.9) for an education trial. These do not prove error but lower confidence.

## 7. Glossary
- **Typodont**: dental training model used here with fixed appliances for brushing practice.
- **Plaque Index (PI, Silness-Löe)**: 0–3 score of plaque thickness at the gingival margin.
- **Gingival Index (GI, Löe-Silness)**: 0–3 score of gingival inflammation.
- **OHIP-14**: 14-item oral health impact questionnaire; lower is better.
- **Modified Bass technique**: sulcular brushing method taught in the hands-on session.
