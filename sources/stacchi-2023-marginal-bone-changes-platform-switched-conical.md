## Why Ingested

PS + 원뿔 연결 임플란트에서 하방 식립 깊이(1mm vs 2mm)의 변연골 영향을 직접 비교한 최초의 다기관 crossover RCT. 기존 위키의 [[implants/mbl/juan-montesinos-2022-platform-switching-conventional-sr-ma]]와 [[implants/mbl/messias-2019-platform-switching-mbl-5year-rct]]가 PS 설계 자체를 다루는 것과 달리, 이 논문은 PS 전제 하에 식립 깊이가 실제 임상 결과에 미치는 미세 조절 효과를 정량화한다.

## Three-line Summary

Multicenter crossover RCT (51 patients, 102 platform-switched conical-connection implants placed 1 mm vs 2 mm subcrestally; definitive abutments immediately connected; ISQUAL loading after 4 months; radiographic follow-up at placement, prosthetic delivery, and 1 year).

Mean peri-implant bone level (PBL) reduction from baseline to 1 year: 0.49 ± 0.32 mm (2 mm subcrestal) vs 0.46 ± 0.35 mm (1 mm subcrestal; p = 0.66); history of periodontitis independently associated with greater PBL reduction; at 1 year, 0% of 2 mm implants had PBL below implant platform vs 11.8% of 1 mm implants.

No significant MBL difference between 1 mm and 2 mm subcrestal depth in PS conical-connection implants; however, 2 mm subcrestal placement maintains implant surface coverage with bone at 1 year and may offer preventive benefit against subsequent peri-implant pathology.

## 세줄요약

다기관 crossover RCT (환자 51명, PS 원뿔 연결 임플란트 102개, 1mm vs 2mm 하방 식립, 즉시 최종 어버트먼트 연결, 4개월 후 최종 보철, 식립 시·보철 시·1년 방사선 추적).

1년 후 골수준(PBL) 감소: 2mm 하방 0.49 ± 0.32 mm vs 1mm 하방 0.46 ± 0.35 mm (p=0.66, 차이 없음); 치주염 과거력이 PBL 감소와 독립적 연관; 1년 시점에서 임플란트면 노출: 2mm 하방 0% vs 1mm 하방 11.8%.

1mm vs 2mm 하방 식립 간 MBL 통계적 차이 없음; 다만 2mm 하방에서 임플란트 표면이 골로 덮인 상태 유지 → 향후 임플란트주위 병변 예방에 잠재적 이점.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First multicenter crossover RCT comparing 1 mm vs 2 mm subcrestal placement depth in PS conical-connection implants
- 51 patients, 102 implants (crossover: each patient received both 1 mm and 2 mm subcrestally placed implants)
- No statistically significant difference in PBL change between 1 mm and 2 mm subcrestal depths
- 2 mm subcrestal: 0% implants with PBL below implant platform at 1 year vs 11.8% at 1 mm subcrestal
- History of periodontitis independently associated with greater PBL reduction (multivariate regression)
- Immediate definitive abutment connection in both arms (OAOT principle applied)

## 3. Methodology
- Design: multicenter parallel-randomized crossover RCT; 4 centers
- Population: partial edentulous, needing 2 implants in posterior maxilla or mandible
- Implants: bone-level PS conical connection; definitive abutments immediately connected
- Loading: unsubmerged healing 4 months; screw-retained metal-ceramic crowns
- Outcomes: radiographic PBL at T0 (placement), T1 (prosthesis delivery), T2 (1 year post-loading)
- Statistics: peri-implant bone level change T0→T2; multivariate linear regression for predictors

## 4. Key Results
- Mean PBL reduction T0–T2: 2 mm group 0.49 ± 0.32 mm vs 1 mm group 0.46 ± 0.35 mm (p = 0.66; NS)
- Multivariate regression: history of periodontitis significant predictor of greater PBL reduction
- At T2: 6 Control (1 mm) implants showed PBL below implant platform (11.8%); 0 Test (2 mm) implants
- No implant failures reported
- Both groups: all implants maintained viable at 1 year

## 5. Limitations
- Follow-up limited to 1 year post-loading
- Relatively small sample (51 patients; 102 implants)
- Crossover design: position effects possible if adjacent implants are placed at different depths
- Long-term implications of deeper placement (e.g., probing access, retrievability) not assessed

## 7. Glossary
- **PBL**: Peri-implant bone level (임플란트주위 골수준) — radiographic distance from implant-abutment junction to first bone contact
- **PS**: Platform switching (플랫폼 스위칭)
- **Subcrestal placement**: implant shoulder positioned below alveolar crest (하방 식립)
- **Conical connection**: internal tapered implant-abutment connection (원뿔 연결)
- **OAOT**: One-abutment-one-time — definitive abutment placed once, not removed
