## Why Ingested

Houshmand 2023([[implants/surface/houshmand-2023-uv-photofunctionalization-sla-titanium-disks]])이 "UVC만 탄소 제거 실효, UVB는 접촉각만 개선"을 보였는데, 이 논문은 UVC + UVA **복합 파장(UV-AC)**이 단일 파장보다 우월한지를 in vitro + in vivo로 직접 검증한다. → [[implants/surface/houshmand-2023-uv-photofunctionalization-sla-titanium-disks]]의 단일 파장 결론을 확장·보완.

## One-line Summary

In vitro + in vivo animal study (3D-printed porous Ti6Al4V, rabbit condyle model): dual-wavelength UV-AC (270 + 365 nm) produced higher ALP activity, hydrophilicity, and BIC than either UVC (270 nm) or UVA (365 nm) alone, with the advantage operating via adhesion–cytoskeleton–nuclear mechanotransduction.

## 한줄요약

In vitro + in vivo 동물 연구 (3D 프린팅 다공성 Ti6Al4V, 토끼 과두부 결손): 복합 파장 자외선 (UV-AC, 270+365 nm)이 단일 UVC(270 nm) 또는 UVA(365 nm)보다 골아세포 인산분해효소 (Alkaline Phosphatase, ALP) 활성·친수성·골-임플란트 접촉률 (Bone-to-Implant Contact, BIC) 모두 우수하였으며, 접착-세포골격-핵 기계적신호전달 경로를 통해 작용함을 확인.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First study to directly compare UVC, UVA, and UV-AC (dual-wavelength) on the **same 3D-printed porous Ti6Al4V implant** using both in vitro (BMSCs) and in vivo (rabbit condyle) endpoints.
- Demonstrates that UV-AC produces synergistic osteogenic effects exceeding either wavelength alone — challenges the implicit assumption that UVC alone is sufficient.
- Mechanistic arm: UV-AC upregulated focal adhesion proteins (integrin, vinculin, talin) and cytoskeletal structures (actin, myosin), coupling to YAP/TAZ nuclear signaling.
- UV photofunctionalization leaves mechanical properties (compressive strength, elastic modulus) of the 3D-printed scaffold statistically unchanged.
- In vivo fluorescent labeling (calcein + tetracycline) + micro-CT showed higher intraporous and peri-implant bone volume fraction for UV-AC vs. single-wavelength groups.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Implants**: 3D-printed porous Ti6Al4V (5 mm × 6 mm, 400 µm pore, 73% porosity; electron-beam melting)
- **UV groups**: GC (no UV control), G270 (UVC 270 nm, ≥2 mW/cm², ≥1.8 J/cm²), G365 (UVA 365 nm, ≥30 mW/cm², ≥27 J/cm²), GU (UV-AC 270+365 nm combined); 15 min exposure
- **In vitro**: rat bone marrow mesenchymal stem cells (BMSCs); CCK-8 viability (days 3/7/14), ALP activity (days 7/14), F-actin/SEM, calcein-AM/PI live-dead
- **Surface characterization**: contact angle (JC2000C1), FE-SEM, EDS, XPS, XRD
- **Mechanical**: universal testing machine (MTS), compressive strength before/after UV
- **In vivo**: rabbit bilateral femoral condyle defect; micro-CT, Goldner trichrome histology, fluorescence microscopy; n not specified in abstract
- **Statistics**: one-way ANOVA + Tukey post hoc

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Wettability**: UV-AC produced the lowest contact angle across all groups (exact values in figures, not in abstract; qualitatively near-superhydrophilic)
- **ALP (day 14)**: UV-AC > G270 > G365 > GC (significant, p not quoted in abstract)
- **BIC (in vivo)**: UV-AC significantly higher than G270, G365, GC
- **Osteoid bone ratio**: UV-AC significantly higher than other groups by micro-CT and Goldner staining
- **Mechanical**: no significant difference in compressive strength or elastic modulus across UV groups
- **Mechanotransduction**: UV-AC showed highest expression of integrin β1, vinculin, talin; most organized actin/myosin cytoskeleton; highest nuclear YAP translocation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- In vivo arm uses rabbit condyle (cancellous bone) only — dental implant site (cortical + cancellous) not tested
- Exact surface carbon quantification (XPS/EDS C%) not reported for all UV conditions — the mechanistic link between dual-wavelength and hydrocarbon removal is inferred, not directly measured
- No clinical study; 3D-printed porous implants differ substantially from conventional commercial SLA implants
- Optimal energy dose ratio between 270 nm and 365 nm arms not characterized

## 6. Related Work

- Houshmand 2023 — showed UVC removes carbon but UVB does not (EDS); this paper extends to UV-AC combination and in vivo outcomes

## 7. Glossary

- UV-AC: dual-wavelength UV combining 270 nm (UVC) + 365 nm (UVA) in one exposure
- GU: group label for UV-AC in this paper
- YAP/TAZ: Yes-associated protein / transcriptional co-activator with PDZ-binding motif — mechanosensitive transcription cofactors
- BIC: Bone-to-implant contact (%)
- ALP: Alkaline phosphatase (osteogenic differentiation marker)
