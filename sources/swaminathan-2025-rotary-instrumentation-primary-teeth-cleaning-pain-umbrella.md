## Why Ingested

기존 [[wiki/endodontics/najjar-2019-caoh2-iodoform-vitapex-vs-zoe-pulpectomy-sr-ma]]는 유치 발수(pulpectomy) 충전재(ZOE vs Ca(OH)2/iodoform)에 초점을 맞췄지만 기구조작(instrumentation) 방식(rotary vs manual)의 세정효율·술후통증 근거는 다루지 않는다. 본 우산리뷰(umbrella review, Swaminathan 2025)는 소아 발수에서 rotary vs manual instrumentation을 세정효율(cleaning effectiveness)·술후통증(postoperative pain) 두 임상 종점으로 직접 비교한 2편의 SR+MA(Khadmi 2025, Faghihian 2022)를 AMSTAR-2·GRADE로 종합해, [[wiki/endodontics/shaping/singh-2026-rotary-reciprocating-kinematics-postoperative-pain-retreatment-sr]]가 다룬 rotary kinematics·술후통증 논의를 유치(primary teeth) 영역으로 확장하는 근거로 활용한다.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PRISMA, PROSPERO CRD42024590194) of two SRMAs (Khadmi 2025, n=2936 teeth across 47 trials; Faghihian 2022, n=341 across 7 trials) comparing rotary versus manual instrumentation for primary-tooth pulpectomy, focused specifically on cleaning effectiveness and postoperative pain.

Rotary instrumentation showed better obturation quality (root OR=1.66, teeth OR=2.62, voids OR=4.29) and significantly lower postoperative pain at 6h/24h/48h (OR=3.37/2.23/2.27) versus manual files in the higher-quality included review; GRADE certainty was Low for cleaning effectiveness and Moderate for postoperative pain.

Only 2 eligible SRMAs existed (low review overlap, CCA=0.15) and obturation-material heterogeneity (ZOE vs Metapex/Ca(OH)2) was not stratified, so the clinical advantage of rotary systems should be interpreted cautiously pending standardized pediatric endodontic outcome sets.

## 세줄요약

우산리뷰(umbrella review, PRISMA·PROSPERO CRD42024590194)로, 유치 발수(pulpectomy)에서 회전형(rotary) 대 수동(manual) 기구조작을 비교한 2편의 체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(SR+MA)(Khadmi 2025, 47개 임상시험·치아 2,936개; Faghihian 2022, 7개 임상시험·341개)을 세정효율(cleaning effectiveness)·술후통증(postoperative pain) 두 종점 중심으로 종합했다.

회전형 기구가 충전(obturation) 질(근관 단위 OR=1.66, 치아 단위 OR=2.62, 공극(voids) OR=4.29)과 6·24·48시간 술후통증(OR=3.37/2.23/2.27, 고품질 리뷰 기준)에서 우수했으며, 근거등급(GRADE)은 세정효율 Low, 술후통증 Moderate로 평가되었다.

포함 가능한 SRMA가 단 2편(리뷰 간 중복도 CCA=0.15로 낮음)이었고 충전재(ZOE vs Metapex/Ca(OH)2) 이질성이 층화분석되지 않아, 표준화된 소아 근관치료 결과지표 세트가 확립되기 전까지는 회전형 기구의 임상적 우위를 신중히 해석해야 한다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review to narrow specifically to two clinically relevant, patient-centered outcomes — cleaning effectiveness and postoperative pain — for rotary vs manual instrumentation in primary tooth pulpectomy, distinguishing it from the broader Patnana et al. umbrella review that covered clinical outcomes generally.
- Applies both AMSTAR-2 (methodological quality of included SRMAs) and GRADE (certainty of evidence per outcome) frameworks together, plus a quantitative corrected covered area (CCA) analysis of primary-study overlap between the two included reviews.
- Surfaces obturation-material heterogeneity (ZOE vs Metapex/iodoform-Ca(OH)2) as an unstratified confound potentially affecting both cleaning-quality and pain outcomes across the underlying trials.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PRISMA-guided umbrella review; protocol registered PROSPERO CRD42024590194.
- Search: PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane Library, Web of Science, Google Scholar, OpenGrey; final search 2025-05-26; English language, human studies, SR/MA study type filter.
- PICOS: population = children undergoing primary-tooth pulpectomy; intervention = rotary instrumentation; comparator = manual/hand instrumentation; outcomes = cleaning effectiveness (via obturation quality/void presence) and postoperative pain (at 6h/12h/24h/48h); study design = SRMAs including RCTs on primary teeth only (in vitro, narrative reviews, permanent-tooth studies excluded).
- Screening in Rayyan by two independent reviewers (title/abstract then full text), disagreements resolved by a third reviewer; standardized Excel extraction.
- Quality appraisal: AMSTAR-2 (16 domains, 7 critical) rated each included SRMA high/moderate/low/critically-low confidence.
- Certainty of evidence: GRADE across 5 domains (risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias) per outcome.
- Quantitative meta-analysis of the umbrella review itself was planned but abandoned due to clinical/statistical heterogeneity across the 2 included SRMAs; a CCA heatmap was used instead to quantify primary-study overlap between the two reviews.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Study flow**: 287 records (6 databases + 1 register) → 22 duplicates removed, 51 auto-excluded → 214 screened → 42 full-text assessed → 2 SRMAs included (Khadmi et al. 2025; Faghihian et al. 2022).
- **Khadmi et al. 2025** (European Archives of Paediatric Dentistry; 47 trials, n=2,936 teeth, age 3–12y): AMSTAR-2 = High confidence. Reported both outcomes.
  - Cleaning effectiveness: Root-unit OR=1.66 (95% CI 1.25–2.21, p=0.0005, I²=49%); Teeth-unit OR=2.62 (95% CI 2.00–3.44, p<0.00001, I²=0%); Voids OR=4.29 (95% CI 2.34–7.87, p<0.00001, I²=22%) — all favoring rotary.
  - Postoperative pain (pain-absence OR, favoring rotary): 6h OR=3.37 (95% CI 2.40–4.74, p<0.00001, I²=11%); 24h OR=2.23 (95% CI 1.40–3.54, p=0.0007, I²=38%); 48h OR=2.27 (95% CI 1.29–3.99, p=0.004, I²=0%).
  - No significant difference in clinical success at 3 and 6 months between rotary and manual.
- **Faghihian et al. 2022** (Contemporary Clinical Dentistry; 7 trials, n=341, age 3–9y): AMSTAR-2 = Low confidence (no registered protocol, no funding disclosure, partial RoB integration). Cleaning effectiveness only: Optimum obturation OR=3.53 (95% CI 1.79–6.97) favoring rotary; Overfilled OR=0.58 (95% CI 0.27–1.26); Underfilled OR=0.45 (95% CI 0.19–1.11) — not significant, i.e., rotary did not significantly reduce over/underfilling risk. Rotary reduced instrumentation time by 1.79 minutes vs manual. All included trials at high risk of bias (lack of allocation concealment).
- **Review overlap**: CCA = 0.15 (low overlap); all 7 Faghihian et al. primary studies were also included in Khadmi et al.
- **GRADE certainty**: Cleaning effectiveness = Low (2 studies, serious inconsistency + serious imprecision). Postoperative pain = Moderate (1 study/Khadmi et al. only, no serious limitations on the assessed domains).
- Obturation materials across underlying trials were predominantly ZOE, with Metapex (iodoform-Ca(OH)2) used in several Khadmi-included trials and some ZOE+Ca(OH)2 combinations — not stratified in either SRMA's analysis.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 2 eligible SRMAs met inclusion criteria — an inherently thin evidence base for an umbrella review, limiting generalizability and precluding the umbrella review's own meta-analysis.
- High primary-study overlap between the two included reviews (all of Faghihian's 7 studies also in Khadmi's 47) despite the low CCA metric nominally suggesting otherwise — Khadmi et al. is a substantial superset, so the two reviews are not fully independent confirmations.
- Obturation material (ZOE vs Metapex vs combinations) varied across underlying trials and was not stratified, confounding interpretation of both cleaning-quality and pain effect sizes.
- Postoperative pain measurement scales and follow-up time-points were inconsistent across underlying primary trials (Wong-Baker FACES, Modified Wong-Baker, four-point scales).
- Faghihian et al. (the lower-confidence review) is the only one reporting overfilling/underfilling outcomes, where rotary showed no significant advantage — a caveat against overgeneralizing rotary's cleaning superiority.
- Authors call for core outcome sets for pediatric endodontic trials, standardized pain scales (Wong-Baker Faces or FLACC), consistent obturation-quality evaluation protocols, and well-designed multicenter RCTs to enable higher-certainty future meta-analyses; also flag operator experience and resource availability (especially low-resource settings) as real-world implementation factors not captured in the pooled effect sizes.

## 7. Glossary

- **SRMA**: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
- **Umbrella review**: A review that synthesizes findings from multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a topic, rather than primary studies directly.
- **AMSTAR-2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews (version 2) — 16-domain instrument rating methodological quality/confidence (high/moderate/low/critically low) of included systematic reviews.
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation — framework rating certainty of evidence (high/moderate/low/very low) per outcome across 5 domains.
- **CCA (Corrected Covered Area)**: A quantitative metric of primary-study overlap between multiple systematic reviews included in an umbrella review; lower values indicate less overlap (more independent evidence).
- **ZOE**: Zinc Oxide Eugenol, a common pulpectomy obturation material for primary teeth.
- **Metapex**: An iodoform-based calcium hydroxide paste used as a resorbable pulpectomy obturation material.
- **PICOS**: Population, Intervention, Comparator, Outcome, Study design — framework for structuring systematic review eligibility criteria.
