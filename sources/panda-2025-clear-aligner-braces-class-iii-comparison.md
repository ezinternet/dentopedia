## Why Ingested

[[overviews/clear-aligner-indications-limitations]]의 Class III축(axis 3)은 현재 대조군 없는 단일 증례보고 2편(Zhang 2025 성인 camouflage, Ye 2025 성장기 facemask 병용)에만 근거하고 있어, SR+MA 수준 근거가 있는 Class II축과 대비해 "case-report tier"로 명시적으로 플래그되어 있다. 본 논문(Panda 2025)은 Class III 부정교합 환자를 대상으로 투명교정(Clear Aligner, CA)과 고정식 장치(Fixed Braces, FA)를 직접 비교한 후향적 임상연구(n=50, 각 군 25명)로, 이 축의 근거 공백을 case-report에서 비교군 있는 코호트 수준으로 한 단계 끌어올린다. 경중등도 비발치 증례의 CAT=FA 동등성을 뒷받침하는 핵심 근거인 [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/baneshi-2024-clear-aligners-malocclusion-sr-ma]]는 Class III·중증 증례를 명시적으로 배제하는데, 본 논문은 바로 그 배제된 영역(Class III)에서 동일한 질문(CA vs FA 임상결과)을 직접 검증한다는 점에서 대비된다. [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/zhang-2025-clear-aligner-based-multidisciplinary]], [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/ye-2025-combined-use-of-miniscrews]]와 함께 axis 3을 구성한다.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective clinical study, n=50 (25 clear aligners vs 25 fixed braces), mild-to-moderate Angle's Class III malocclusion (ANB -4° to 0°), no prior orthodontic treatment or craniofacial syndrome.

Aligners showed shorter treatment duration (18±3 vs 22±4 months, p<0.05) and higher compliance (92% vs 75%, p<0.05); overjet correction was comparable (3.2±0.5 vs 3.8±0.4 mm, p>0.05); but braces achieved greater skeletal (ANB) improvement (1.5±0.5° vs 0.8±0.3°, p<0.05) and lower refinement need (5% vs 10%).

Clear aligners are a viable dental-camouflage option for mild-to-moderate Class III cases favoring esthetics/compliance/speed, but fixed braces remain the preferred choice when skeletal correction is the priority; study is retrospective, single-center, small (n=25/arm), and excludes surgical-severity Class III.

## 세줄요약

후향적 임상연구, n=50 (투명교정 25명 vs 고정식 장치 25명), 경중등도 Angle's Class III 부정교합(ANB -4°~0°), 이전 교정치료·두개안면 증후군 없음.

투명교정이 치료기간 단축(18±3 vs 22±4개월, p<0.05)과 순응도 우위(92% vs 75%, p<0.05)를 보였고, 전치돌출(overjet) 개선은 유사(3.2±0.5 vs 3.8±0.4mm, p>0.05)했으나, 고정식 장치가 골격적(ANB) 개선(1.5±0.5° vs 0.8±0.3°, p<0.05)이 더 크고 재조정 필요율도 낮았다(5% vs 10%).

투명교정은 심미·순응도·속도를 중시하는 경중등도 Class III 치성 보정에 실행 가능한 대안이나, 골격 교정이 우선인 경우 고정식 장치가 여전히 선호되며, 후향적·단일기관·소규모(군당 n=25) 연구로 수술 대상 중증 Class III는 배제됐다는 한계가 있다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Provides direct head-to-head clinical comparison of clear aligners (CA) vs fixed braces (FA) specifically in Angle's Class III malocclusion — an area where the wiki previously held only single case reports without comparators.
- Quantifies the trade-off pattern for Class III: CA wins on treatment duration and compliance, ties on dental (overjet) correction, but loses on skeletal (ANB) correction and post-treatment refinement rate.
- Reinforces (in a Class III-specific population) the broader CAT literature pattern that aligners excel at dental/tipping-type correction but underperform on skeletal-type change, consistent with mechanistic explanations (continuous force of brackets/wires vs intermittent force of removable aligners).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Retrospective study, single comparison cohort split into two treatment arms.
- **Population**: 50 patients with mild-to-moderate skeletal Class III (ANB angle between -4° and 0°), no prior orthodontic treatment, good periodontal health, no craniofacial syndrome. Severe skeletal Class III requiring orthognathic surgery and active periodontal disease/missing permanent teeth were excluded.
- **Groups**: Group 1 (n=25) clear aligners; Group 2 (n=25) fixed braces (multibracket).
- **Outcome measures**: Treatment duration, overjet/overbite change (dental models), ANB angle change (lateral cephalograms, pre/post), patient compliance (self-reported wear-time for aligners), post-treatment stability assessed over 12 months (need for refinement/adjustment).
- **Statistics**: t-tests comparing group outcomes.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Parameter | Aligners (n=25) | Braces (n=25) | P |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treatment duration (months) | 18±3 | 22±4 | <0.05 |
| Compliance rate (%) | 92% | 75% | <0.05 |

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Retrospective design (no randomization), single-center, small sample (n=25/arm).
- Restricted to mild-to-moderate skeletal Class III (ANB -4° to 0°); explicitly excludes surgical-severity cases, so findings do not generalize to severe skeletal Class III.
- Compliance for aligners was self-reported (recall/social-desirability bias risk); no objective wear-time sensor data.
- Post-treatment stability follow-up limited to 12 months — authors explicitly call for longitudinal studies on long-term stability.
- No blinding of cephalometric/model assessors reported.

## 7. Glossary

- **ANB angle**: Cephalometric angle indicating the anteroposterior relationship of maxilla to mandible; used to classify/quantify skeletal Class I/II/III discrepancy.
- **Overjet**: Horizontal overlap between maxillary and mandibular incisors.
- **Refinement (aligners)**: Additional aligner set(s) issued after the initial series to correct residual discrepancies.
- **Camouflage treatment**: Orthodontic-only correction of a skeletal discrepancy via dental compensation, without orthognathic surgery.
