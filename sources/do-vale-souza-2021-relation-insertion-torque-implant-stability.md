## Why Ingested
Single-system prospective primary study (n=25, uniform implant) offering a controlled-system data point on the IT–ISQ correlation question that has oscillated between [[wiki/implants/isq/lages-2018-isq-insertion-torque-correlation-sr]] (NS, r=0.366) and [[wiki/implants/isq/tisci-2026-isq-it-mbl-survival-sr-ma]] (pooled r=0.44): IT correlates with initial ISQ (r=0.457) but not with 6-month ISQ. It also answers the "persistently low ISQ months after placement" question — initial ISQ<60 in Type III bone osseointegrated in nearly all cases, so ISQ did not predict osseointegration failure.

## Three-line Summary
Prospective single-arm clinical study (n=25 patients, 25 implants, one uniform 11.5×3.75 mm Hexagonal Morse cone system, DSP Biomedical) correlating insertion torque (IT, Ncm) with ISQ (Osstell Mentor RFA) at placement and at 6 months.

IT positively correlated with initial ISQ (r=0.457, p=0.022) but not with final 6-month ISQ (p=0.308); mean ISQ rose from 48.24±19.28 to 65.96±8.47 (p<0.001); only 1/25 implants (Type III bone) failed osseointegration.

The IT–ISQ correlation is confined to primary stability; low initial ISQ (<60) in Type III bone mostly still osseointegrated (IT was the more consistent predictor), supporting ISQ as a complement to IT rather than a standalone predictor of osseointegration.

## 세줄요약
단일군 전향 임상연구 (n=25명, 임플란트 25개, 단일 규격 11.5×3.75mm 헥사고날 모스 콘 (Hexagonal Morse cone) 시스템): 삽입토크 (insertion torque, IT)와 임플란트 안정성 지수 (Implant Stability Quotient, ISQ)의 상관을 식립 직후와 6개월 후에 평가.

IT는 초기 ISQ와 양의 상관 (r=0.457, p=0.022)이나 6개월 후 ISQ와는 무상관 (p=0.308); 평균 ISQ 48.24±19.28 → 65.96±8.47 (p<0.001); 1개 임플란트(Type III 골)만 골유착 실패.

IT–ISQ 상관은 일차안정성에만 국한; 초기 ISQ<60이어도 Type III 골에서 대부분 골유착 성공 (IT가 더 일관된 예측인자) — ISQ는 IT의 보조 지표이지 골유착 실패의 단독 예측인자가 아님.

## 2. Key Contributions
- IT positively correlates with initial (placement-day) ISQ in a uniform single-system cohort: r=0.457, p=0.022.
- The IT–ISQ correlation does not extend to secondary stability: IT vs 6-month ISQ showed no correlation (p=0.308).
- Initial ISQ did not predict osseointegration failure: 14/15 Type III implants with low initial ISQ (mean 36.00) still osseointegrated; the single failure was associated with neither IT (p=0.394) nor initial (p=0.164) nor final ISQ (p=0.091).
- Bone type drove both measures: Type I > Type III for IT (36.50 vs 31.20 Ncm, p=0.018) and for initial and final ISQ (66.60→70.10 vs 36.00→63.20).

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: prospective single-arm clinical study
- **n**: 25 patients (68% female, 32% male; mean age 50±9 years), one implant per patient
- **Implant**: 11.5×3.75 mm Hexagonal Morse cone (DSP Biomedical, Brazil); single experienced operator; surgical protocol with fresh extraction socket
- **Bone type**: Lekholm–Zarb classification — Type I n=10 (40%), Type III n=15 (60%)
- **Outcomes**: IT maximum value (calibrated torque wrench, Ncm) at placement; ISQ (Osstell Mentor RFA; mean of mesial/distal/buccal/lingual SmartPeg readings) at placement and at 6 months; osseointegration per Albrektsson–Zarb survival criteria
- **Stats**: Pearson correlation, dependent Student's t-test, two-way repeated-measures ANOVA + Tukey, independent Student's t-test, Kendall's correlation; significance level 5%

- **Survival**: 24/25 (96%) osseointegrated at 6 months; the 1 failure was in Type III bone and was not predicted by IT or ISQ.
- Loading inference drawn by authors: type I implants could get delayed/early loading (ISQ ≥65), type III only delayed loading; mean IT (31–37 Ncm) below the 45 Ncm threshold cited from Trist et al. for immediate/early loading — so delayed loading regardless of bone type.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only bone types I and III were evaluated — Type II and IV (notably posterior maxilla Type IV) absent; authors call for similar studies in Types II/IV.
- Findings generated on a single implant system/size (11.5×3.75 mm Morse cone) — generalizability to other macro-designs/sizes unclear.
- Modest n=25, single center, single operator, mostly females (68%).
- Osseointegration assessed by clinical survival criteria (Albrektsson–Zarb), not histomorphometry.
- Bone-type classification is operator-subjective (radiographic + tactile + Misch density).

## 6. Related Work
- Lages 2018 (SR, 12 studies): pooled IT–ISQ r=0.366, NS; this study's significant r=0.457 in a controlled single system contributes primary-study evidence against that NS result (consistent with the superseding Tisci 2026 SR+MA).
- Tisci 2026 (SR+MA, 48 studies): pooled r=0.44, p<0.001 — the r=0.457 magnitude replicates independently; this paper also matches Tisci's finding that ISQ is not an independent predictor of survival.
- Baldi 2018: IT–ISQ correlation in tapered knife-edge implants is torque-dependent (ρ=0.481 in moderate-torque group) — complements this uniform-system finding.
- Sarfaraz et al. (cited in text): IT 30–60 Ncm considered a good primary-stability indicator and favorable for osseointegration — the mean values here (31–37 Ncm) fall in that window.
- Trist et al. (cited in text): immediate loading valid in low-density bone if IT ≥45 Ncm — not reached by mean values in this study, guiding the delayed-loading only conclusion.

## 7. Glossary
- **Insertion torque (IT)**: The frictional resistance the implant encounters during apical rotatory placement, recorded in Ncm; reflects site bone quality and primary stability.
- **Implant stability quotient (ISQ)**: 0–100 scale from resonance frequency analysis (RFA) quantifying the implant's resistance to vibration/bending; 100 = maximum stability.
- **Resonance frequency analysis (RFA)**: Noninvasive piezoelectric-transducer method that vibrates the implant (via SmartPeg) and converts the resonant frequency to ISQ.
- **Primary stability**: Mechanical engagement of the implant with bone immediately after placement.
- **Secondary stability**: Stability achieved after bone production and maturation (remodeling) around the implant, e.g. at 6 months.
- **Hexagonal Morse cone**: Taper-lock (cone-in-cone) implant-abutment connection design (DSP Biomedical, Brazil).
