## Why Ingested

기존 위키는 UV-PF의 임상·항균·연조직 근거를 다루지만 "왜 거친 표면에서 골아세포 증식이 억제되며 UV-PF가 이를 어떻게 해소하는가"라는 기전 설명이 빠져 있었다. 이 리뷰가 정확히 그 갭을 메운다. [[implants/surface/ogawa-2025-3d-theory-osseointegration-material-topography]]의 Dimension 2(표면 형태) 한계 논의와 직접 연결.

## One-line Summary

Narrative review (UCLA Ogawa group): three non-coating surface strategies — nanonodular texturing, meso-scale texturing, and UV photofunctionalization — overcome the "biological dilemma" of osteoblast kinetics on microrough titanium surfaces by simultaneously enhancing proliferation without sacrificing differentiation.

## 한줄요약

서사 리뷰 (UCLA Ogawa 그룹): 나노결절 텍스처링·메소 텍스처링·자외선 광기능화 (Ultraviolet Photofunctionalization, UV-PF) 3가지 비코팅 전략이 거친 티타늄 표면의 골아세포 "생물학적 딜레마"(증식↓분화↑의 역상관)를 해소해 증식·분화 동시 향상을 달성함을 종합.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Frames the "biological dilemma of osteoblast kinetics" (Runx2-mediated inverse proliferation–differentiation correlation) as the fundamental unsolved problem of microrough surfaces.
- Reviews nanonodular texturing (TiO₂ sputtered soft-edge surfaces): 3–5× more osteoblast attachment at 6 h, 2× proliferation, without impaired differentiation; 3-fold higher biomechanical osseointegration in rat femur.
- Reviews meso-scale texturing: scales between micro- and macro-roughness; overcomes the size-mismatch constraint of pure microroughness.
- Reviews UV-PF's specific contribution: removes the hydrocarbon pellicle (biological aging) that suppresses initial cell recruitment — UV-PF shifts the starting conditions so both kinetics operate from a clean baseline.
- UV-PF as "physicochemical surface reset": compatible with any topography (surface-agnostic), provides superhydrophilic starting state that amplifies the topographic strategies.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Narrative review; no systematic search, no PRISMA
- Covers in vitro cell studies (rat osteoblast, human MG63, BMSCs), in vivo animal (rat femur, rabbit), select clinical reports
- Primary focus: surface science ↔ osteoblast kinetics relationship

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

**Nanonodular texturing (vs. knife-edge microrough):**
- Osteoblast attachment at 6 h: 3–5× higher
- Proliferation (days 2–6): ~2× higher
- Differentiation (osteogenic gene expression, calcium deposition): maintained or amplified
- Biomechanical osseointegration (rat femur pushout): 3-fold greater

**UV photofunctionalization:**
- Removes hydrocarbon pellicle (40–75% surface carbon on aged Ti)
- Restores superhydrophilicity (contact angle near 0°)
- Enhances osteoblast recruitment, attachment, spreading, and proliferation on microrough surfaces
- Maintains or amplifies differentiation advantage of microrough surfaces
- Effect operates on top of any topography (nanonodular, meso, or standard SLA)

**Meso-scale texturing:** modulates cell spreading and migration at scale between micro (0.5–5 µm) and macro (>10 µm); details in referenced Ogawa group papers.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Narrative review; no meta-analytic pooling, risk of selection bias toward Ogawa group's own work
- Clinical evidence for nanonodular and meso texturing is limited (mostly in vitro/animal)
- UV-PF + nanonodular combination is proposed but not tested in clinical RCT
- Long-term clinical outcomes for novel textures absent

## 7. Glossary

- Biological dilemma: inverse correlation between osteoblast proliferation and differentiation on microrough surfaces (Runx2-mediated)
- Nanonodular texturing: TiO₂ sputter deposition creating nano-nodules on microrough Ti, converting knife-edges to soft-edges
- Meso-scale texturing: surface texturing at length scales between micro (0.5–5 µm) and macro (>10 µm)
- Hydrocarbon pellicle: carbon-containing contamination layer on stored titanium (biological aging)
- BIC plateau: observed clinical BIC of 45–65%; below the theoretical 100%
