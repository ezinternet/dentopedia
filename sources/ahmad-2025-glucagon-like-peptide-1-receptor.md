## Why Ingested
GLP-1 수용체 작용제(글루카곤양 펩타이드-1 수용체 작용제, GLP-1 Receptor Agonist, GLP-1RA)(세마글루타이드·엑세나타이드·리라글루타이드)가 임플란트 주위 골손실과 치주 염증에 미치는 기전을 처음으로 종합한 리뷰로, [[drug/systemic-disease/enteghad-2024-diabetes-mellitus-periodontal-periimplant-disease-review]] 및 [[implants/survival/shahi-2026-implant-outcomes-diabetes-mellitus-sr]]의 약물 관리 축을 GLP-1RA 계열로 확장함.

## One-line Summary
Narrative review (10 in vitro, 9 animal, 1 clinical study) showing GLP-1RAs promote peri-implant osseointegration and reduce periodontal bone loss in T2DM, acting via Wnt/β-catenin and MAPK pathways independently of glycemic control.

## 한줄요약
GLP-1RA(엑세나타이드·리라글루타이드)가 혈당 강하와 독립적으로 임플란트 주위 골형성과 치주 염증을 개선함을 10개 in vitro·9개 동물·1개 임상 연구로 종합한 내러티브 리뷰.

## 2. Key Contributions
- First narrative review specifically on GLP-1RAs × dental implant/periodontal outcomes
- Establishes glycemic-independent mechanism: Wnt/β-catenin + MAPK → PDLSC osteogenic differentiation
- Animal evidence: GLP-1RAs reduce alveolar bone resorption and oxidative stress independent of glucose control
- Advanced delivery (exenatide-PLGA microspheres) shows enhanced peri-implant osseointegration in diabetic rodent models
- Sole clinical study: GLP-1RA group had significantly less peri-implant marginal bone loss vs insulin and metformin (p < 0.01)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Narrative review
- **Scope**: 10 in vitro, 9 preclinical animal, 1 clinical study
- **Clinical study type**: Retrospective cohort; compared GLP-1RA vs insulin vs metformin groups for peri-implant marginal bone loss in peri-implantitis patients
- **Search**: Multiple databases; no formal PRISMA protocol (narrative)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
**In vitro:**
- Exenatide + liraglutide → ↑ osteogenic differentiation of PDLSCs via Wnt/β-catenin and MAPK pathways
- SDF-1 synergy → ↑ PDLSC proliferation + bone regeneration under high-glucose conditions

**Animal:**
- GLP-1RAs ↓ periodontal bone resorption, oxidative stress, inflammatory mediators
- Exenatide-PLGA microspheres → significantly enhanced peri-implant osseointegration vs control (diabetic rat model)
- Effects observed even without improvement in blood glucose

**Clinical (1 study):**
- GLP-1RA group: significantly less clinical attachment loss and radiographic bone loss vs insulin and metformin groups (p < 0.01)
- Interpretation limited by retrospective design and absence of randomization

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Only 1 clinical study; no RCT exists
- Narrative (not systematic) review — risk of selection bias
- Clinical study retrospective, uncontrolled confounders
- Unknown optimal GLP-1RA agent, dose, timing relative to implant placement
- No long-term clinical data (>2 years)

## 7. Glossary
- **GLP-1RA**: Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist (글루카곤양 펩타이드-1 수용체 작용제) — drug class: exenatide, liraglutide, semaglutide
- **PDLSC**: Periodontal ligament stem cell
- **Wnt/β-catenin pathway**: Osteogenic signaling cascade
- **PLGA microspheres**: Poly(lactic-co-glycolic acid) biodegradable drug delivery vehicle
