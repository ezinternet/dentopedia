## Why Ingested
User-requested. The wiki already has a dedicated conflict synthesis on this question ([[overviews/high-insertion-torque-primary-stability-crestal-bone-overview]]) built on a higher-weight SR+MA ([[implants/isq/lemos-2020-high-insertion-torque-dental-implants-sr-ma]]), so this 2025 scoping review's marginal contribution is modest and should not be overstated: (1) **currency** — the most recent synthesis on IT >50 Ncm, and its bottom line ("high IT → more MBL, recession, failure") runs *opposite* to Lemos 2020's null, which is worth recording; (2) it **reports effect sizes** for MBL and facial soft-tissue recession (mandible MBL 1.53 vs 1.03 mm; mandible FSTL −1.40 vs −0.26 mm at 36 months) from a single 3-year study whose design matches [[implants/isq/marconcini-2018-high-insertion-torque-bone-resorption-rct]] — our Marconcini page is abstract-only and lacks those numbers (attribution is inferred: PMC returned no reference list); (3) it articulates the strain → ischemia → necrosis → MBL pathway that [[implants/coyac-2019-preclinical-model-links-osseo-densification-misfit]] demonstrates histologically, but only as narrative assertion, not new evidence. The soft-tissue-recession finding is **not new** to the wiki — it is Marconcini's finding restated. It does not supersede Lemos 2020 or the overview.

## Three-line Summary
Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR, JBI 3-stage search; PubMed + Scopus, 2011–2024; 11 human clinical studies — controlled intervention + observational cohorts) on the clinical consequences of high insertion torque (IT >50 Ncm) on marginal bone loss (MBL), facial soft tissue level (FSTL), and implant failure.
Narrative (no meta-analysis) conclusion: high IT gives adequate primary stability but is associated with greater MBL (mandible 1.53±0.29 vs 1.03±0.12 mm; maxilla 1.16±0.61 vs 0.96±0.46 mm, 3 years) and greater facial recession (mandible −1.40 vs −0.26 mm; maxilla −0.54 vs 0.03 mm, 36 months), modified by bone density and buccal bone thickness <1 mm; failures clustered in D1 and D4 bone, with D2–D3 a "safer zone".
Weak-to-moderate synthesis: the headline numbers trace to one 3-year study, there is no pooled estimate, reporting has internal inconsistencies, and it does not engage the post-hoc torque-classification confound (IT is a marker of dense cortical bone) — it does not overturn the null of the Lemos 2020 SR+MA.

## 세줄요약
주제범위 문헌고찰 (Scoping Review; PRISMA-ScR, PubMed·Scopus 2011–2024, 사람 임상연구 11편): 고삽입토크 (High Insertion Torque, IT >50 Ncm)가 변연골 소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)·협측 연조직 수준 (Facial Soft Tissue Level, FSTL)·임플란트 실패에 미치는 임상적 영향을 정리.
메타분석 없이 서술적 결론: 고토크는 1차 안정성 (Primary Stability, PS)은 확보하나 MBL 증가(하악 1.53 vs 1.03 mm, 상악 1.16 vs 0.96 mm, 3년)·협측 퇴축 증가(하악 −1.40 vs −0.26 mm, 상악 −0.54 vs 0.03 mm, 36개월)와 연관; 골밀도·협측골 두께 (Buccal Bone Thickness, BBT) <1 mm가 조절인자, 실패는 D1·D4 골에 집중(D2–D3가 "안전지대").
핵심 수치는 사실상 단일 3년 연구(Marconcini 2018로 추정) 유래이고, 정량 합성·교란(토크 사후 분류) 논의가 없어 Lemos 2020 체계적 문헌고찰·메타분석 (SR+MA)의 무의 결론을 뒤집지 못한다 — 대체(supersession) 아님.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Most recent (2025) synthesis specifically on IT >50 Ncm and clinical outcomes; bottom line is **harm-leaning** (more MBL, facial recession, failure, "diminished osseointegration"), in contrast to the Lemos 2020 SR+MA null
- Reports concrete effect sizes by jaw for MBL and FSTL over 3 years (see Results) — the only source in the wiki currently carrying these numbers
- Frames failure as **bimodal by bone density**: failures "primarily restricted" to D1 (dense) and D4 (soft) bone, D2–D3 as a "safer zone" — consistent with the overview's thesis that IT reads the bone bed rather than acting as an independent dose
- Identifies buccal bone thickness (<1 mm vs >1 mm) and gingival biotype (thin vs thick) as modifiers of recession and MBL
- States the mechanistic chain (IT beyond elastic limit → microcracks → microcirculation/angiogenesis impairment → ischemia → necrosis → resorption) and cites histology (Campos et al.) for compression necrosis — but as narrative, not as new data
- Acknowledges dissenting studies reporting no MBL or survival penalty from high IT

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR; JBI 3-stage search; PCC framework — Population: implant patients; Concept: high IT; Context: any setting)
- **Databases**: PubMed (MEDLINE), Scopus; MeSH terms "torque", "dental implants", "bone resorption", "peri-implantitis", "complications"
- **Window**: studies published 2011–2024; final search stated as 16 September 2023; data extraction Feb–May 2024
- **Eligibility**: interventional + observational human studies with high-IT placement and clinical outcomes; excluded non-English, animal/ex vivo, studies without ethics approval, case reports/series, and all reviews (so Lemos 2020 was not engaged)
- **Flow**: 823 records → 454 duplicates, 11 non-English, 39 in vitro/animal, 282 irrelevant, 4 no ethics approval, 13 case reports/series removed → 20 full texts → 9 excluded → **11 included** (4 controlled intervention studies, 7 observational cohort studies per in-text citation counts)
- **Screening**: two independent reviewers, disagreements resolved by consensus, senior reviewers for complex cases
- **Quality/RoB**: Methods state NHLBI tools; Results additionally state Cochrane RoB (RCTs) and ROBINS-I (non-randomized); overall judgment "low risk of bias" (no per-study detail in our copy)
- **Synthesis**: narrative/tabular only — no meta-analysis, no certainty-of-evidence grading
- **Threshold**: high IT >50 Ncm vs regular <50 Ncm in most included studies (one recession passage refers to a "low <35 Ncm" group)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Outcome | Regular IT (<50 Ncm) | High IT (>50 Ncm) | Follow-up | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MBL, mandible | 1.03 ± 0.12 mm | 1.53 ± 0.29 mm | 3 years | <0.001 |
| MBL, maxilla | 0.96 ± 0.46 mm | 1.16 ± 0.61 mm | 3 years | <0.0001 (as reported) |
| FSTL change, maxilla | +0.03 mm (minimal) | −0.54 mm | 36 months | <0.001 |
| FSTL change, mandible | −0.26 mm | −1.40 mm | 36 months | <0.001 |

- MBL and FSTL rows are each cited to a single study (bracket citations empty in our copy); design features (jaw split, FSTL, BBT <1 mm modifier, 36 months) match Marconcini 2018 — so the mandible-vs-maxilla contrast (Δ 0.50 vs 0.20 mm MBL; Δ 1.14 vs 0.57 mm recession) is one trial, not a pooled pattern
- Mandibular harm is ~2–2.5× the maxillary harm on both outcomes — consistent with the dense-cortical-bed mechanism in the wiki overview, even though the review itself says the harm holds "regardless of the jaw region"
- **Biotype study** (n=37 patients; 31 thick, 6 thin): MBL 0.9 ± 0.18 mm at crown delivery → 1.21 ± 0.19 mm at 1 year; thin biotype significantly greater MBL at both time points (p<0.05)
- **BBT**: sites with BBT <1 mm showed greater soft-tissue loss; BBT >1 mm significantly less FSTL reduction
- **Failure**: high IT associated with more peri-implantitis and failure; failures concentrated in D1 and D4 bone; D2–D3 more favorable
- **Dissent acknowledged**: some included studies found no MBL, survival, or success penalty from high IT (not quantified)
- Authors' conclusion: most included articles showed greater MBL, facial recession, failure, and diminished osseointegration with IT >50 Ncm

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Scoping review ≠ systematic review**: no meta-analysis, no pooled effect, no GRADE; conclusions are vote-count/narrative and cannot be weighed against Lemos 2020's pooled RR/MD
- **Headline numbers come from one study** (effectively Marconcini 2018); the review's "consistently associated … regardless of the jaw region" generalizes from it
- **Does not address the central confound**: in all human high-IT studies torque is classified post hoc, so "high IT" is inseparable from dense cortical bone / under-preparation — the review notes bone density matters but still treats IT as the cause
- **Internal inconsistencies in reporting**: RoB tools named differently in Methods (NHLBI) vs Results (Cochrane RoB + ROBINS-I); final search date (Sept 2023) precedes the stated eligibility window end (2024) and extraction period; "prospective" OSF registration cited with a 2025 access date; "three other reviewers" lists five; a stray sentence refers to "suture materials used in third molar surgeries" (template carry-over); the first conclusion sentence ("correlation between an increased number of implants and a rising incidence of complications") does not follow from the data
- **Self-contradictory framing**: abstract says high IT gives "better osseointegration", conclusions say it "diminished osseointegration"
- **Arbitrary threshold**: >50 Ncm cut-off inherited from included studies; no dose-response analysis
- Animal/histology excluded by criteria yet histology (Campos et al.) cited in Discussion to support necrosis; Trisi-type null histology not discussed
- Search limited to 2 databases, English only; authors concede the search may have been "overly specific"
- Authors call for RCTs with longer follow-up and additional variables

## 6. Related Work
- Lemos 2020 (SR+MA, 6 studies, 651 implants): no significant survival or MBL difference for IT ≥50 Ncm — this review's bottom line conflicts with it but carries lower evidence weight and likely overlapping primary studies
- Marconcini 2018 (RCT, 116 implants, 3 years): probable source of this review's MBL/FSTL numbers — not independent confirmation
- Aldahlawi 2018 (retrospective, NobelActive): >55 Ncm → more MBL, mandible ~8× maxilla — same direction
- Faot 2019 (prospective, narrow implants, atrophic mandible): ≥45 Ncm best survival, no MBL effect — opposite direction
- Trisi 2011 (sheep histology): no compression necrosis at 110 Ncm — conflicts with the review's mechanistic assertion
- Coyac 2019 (rat preclinical): misfit-induced osteocyte death and resorption — histologic counterpart to the pathway this review asserts
- Campos et al. (histology, cited in-text): compression necrosis with high IT, healing chambers with low IT — not held in the wiki

## 7. Glossary
- **Insertion torque (IT)**: rotational resistance at implant seating, in Ncm; a proxy for primary stability
- **High IT**: here >50 Ncm (vs regular <50 Ncm)
- **Marginal bone loss (MBL)**: radiographic crestal bone change around the implant
- **Facial soft tissue level (FSTL)**: vertical position of the facial mucosal margin relative to the adjacent tooth; negative change = recession
- **Buccal bone thickness (BBT)**: thickness of the buccal plate at the implant site; <1 mm associated with more recession
- **Scoping review**: maps the extent and nature of evidence (PRISMA-ScR/JBI) without formal pooled effect estimation
- **PCC**: Population–Concept–Context framework for scoping-review questions
- **Compression necrosis**: osteocyte death from strain exceeding bone's elastic/physiological threshold
