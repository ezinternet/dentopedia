## Why Ingested

Largest prospective PRO comparative study (n=120, 12-month, 4 timepoints) with multi-instrument assessment (PIDAQ, TSQ, VAS, OHIP-14) and objective oral-health outcomes (plaque index, white spot lesions). Addresses surveillance target "PRO/satisfaction 2024–2026." → [[orthodontics/clear-aligner/patil-2026-perception-satisfaction-ca-vs-fa]]

## Three-line Summary

Prospective 12-month cohort (n=120, n=60/group, 4 timepoints) comparing CA vs FA using PIDAQ, TSQ, VAS, and OHIP-14. Clear aligners showed superior aesthetics satisfaction (78.3% vs 41.7% "excellent"), comfort (VAS 7.8 vs 5.9), and PIDAQ psychosocial impact at all timepoints, but OHIP-14 converged by 12 months (p=0.156). White spot lesions significantly less common with CA (11.7% vs 31.7%, p=0.008).

## 1. Document Information

- **Type**: Prospective parallel-group cohort (self-selected, matched)
- **Journal**: Bioinformation 22(2):770-775 (2026), DOI 10.6026/973206300220770
- **PMID/PMCID**: 42109316 / PMC13150237
- **Sample**: n=120 (60 CA/Invisalign, 60 FA/MBT 0.022"), adults 18–45y, 12 months follow-up, university orthodontic clinic

## 2. Key Findings

- PIDAQ at 12mo: CA 18.4±6.2 vs FA 29.7±7.8 (p<0.001) — CA far superior psychosocial impact
- Aesthetics "excellent" (VAS ≥80) at 12mo: CA 78.3% vs FA 41.7% (p<0.001)
- Comfort VAS at 12mo: CA 7.8±1.4 vs FA 5.9±1.6 (p<0.001)
- Pain VAS at T1 (3mo): CA 3.2±1.1 vs FA 5.8±1.4 (p<0.001); converges by T3 (12mo)
- OHIP-14 at 3mo: CA 21.4±5.8 vs FA 26.8±6.2 (p<0.001); at 12mo: NS (p=0.156) — converged
- White spot lesions: CA 11.7% vs FA 31.7% (p=0.008)
- Plaque index at 6mo: CA 1.2±0.5 vs FA 1.8±0.6 (p<0.001)
- TSQ at 12mo: CA 82.4±8.7 vs FA 71.3±9.8 (p<0.001)
- Self-reported wear: 21.8±1.2 h/day (above population 36% compliance rate)

## 3. Methodology

Prospective, parallel-group cohort. Self-selected treatment, matched on age/sex/PAR/aesthetic concern. Inclusion: Class I/II, non-extraction, good perio. Assessments at T0, T1 (3mo), T2 (6mo), T3 (12mo). Blind clinical examiner. SPSS 26, repeated-measures ANOVA Bonferroni correction.

## 4. Clinical Implications

For adult non-extraction cases where aesthetics/comfort are primary drivers, CA is clearly superior throughout treatment. The convergence of OHIP-14 at 12 months confirms that functional outcomes are similar at treatment end. White spot lesion prevention is a strong argument for CA in caries-susceptible patients.

## 5. Limitations

Self-selected (not randomized); single institution; non-extraction cases only; university setting (compliance may be higher); no blinding of patients; 23.3% of CA patients frustrated with maintenance.

## 7. Glossary

- **PIDAQ**: Psychosocial Impact of Dental Aesthetics Questionnaire (23 items, 0–92; lower = less impact)
- **TSQ**: Treatment Satisfaction Questionnaire (15 items; higher = more satisfied)
- **White spot lesion**: early enamel demineralization (caries precursor) around brackets
- **MBT**: McLaughlin-Bennett-Trevisi preadjusted edgewise bracket system (0.022" slot)
