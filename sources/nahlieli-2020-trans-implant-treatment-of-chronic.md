## Why Ingested

The wiki's chronic-sinusitis-with-implant evidence was a 2-patient case report of drainage during lateral-window sinus augmentation ([[sinus-lift/lateral/park-2024-surgical-drainage-simultaneous-sinus-floor]]) plus ENT-sequence reviews such as [[oral-medicine/odontogenic-sinusitis/kim-2019-definition-management-odontogenic-maxillary]]. This is a larger implant-based sinusitis-treatment series (n=31) than the wiki's prior holdings, adding a repeatable lavage/follow-up protocol that uses the implant itself as the access port; ingested from the abstract during a PubMed-text surveillance batch.

## Three-line Summary

Retrospective study of 31 patients with chronic recurrent maxillary sinusitis (CRS) treated by trans-implant lavage through implants with an internal removable sealing screw — 28 had simultaneous implant placement plus sinusitis treatment (referred for sinus elevation), 3 were treated via an existing implant.

28/31 (90%) had complete relief of most symptoms (nasal obstruction/discharge, anosmia/hyposmia) by day 30; follow-up nasal endoscopy showed no active sinus disease; 27 implants were well-osseointegrated and in prosthetic use, 1 implant failed from poor bone quality, and no CRS recurrence was reported during follow-up.

The proposed protocol (pus drainage via the punctured sinus floor, 3 lavage sessions, 4 CBCT scans, 4 transnasal endoscopies, with implant and sinus assessment at days 30 and 60) is promising, but this is an abstract-level, uncontrolled retrospective series.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Tests a protocol for trans-implant treatment of CRS using implants with an internal removable sealing screw (internal central port).
- Assesses appropriate indications, the optimal number of treatment sessions, and the overall treatment and follow-up schedule.
- Proposes a combined protocol evaluating implant status and sinusitis-treatment results together at days 30 and 60 after surgery.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: retrospective study.
- **n**: 31 patients with CRS; 28 referred for sinus elevation underwent simultaneous implantation and sinusitis treatment; 3 were treated through an already existing implant.
- **Intervention**: trans-implant lavage — drainage of pus through the punctured sinus floor, then antral irrigation/lavage.
- **Suggested protocol**: 3 lavage sessions, 4 CBCT scans, 4 transnasal endoscopic observations; combined implant-plus-sinus evaluation at days 30 and 60.
- Further methods (inclusion criteria, irrigant, antibiotic use, follow-up length, implant system) are not in the abstract.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Symptom relief: 28/31 (90%) had complete relief of most symptoms (nasal obstruction/discharge, anosmia/hyposmia) up to day 30 postoperative.
- Follow-up nasal endoscopy: no evidence of active sinus disease.
- Implants: 27 well-osseointegrated and in prosthetic use; 1 implantation failed because of poor bone quality.
- Remaining 30 cases: clinical and radiological results showed implant stability and no CRS recurrence over the whole follow-up period.
- Presenting symptoms (publisher-page results snippet): nasal discharge 31/31 (100%), nasal obstruction 28/31 (90%), post-nasal drip 15/31 (48%), hyposmia/anosmia 13/31 (42%), facial pain (maxillary/orbital/frontal) 14/31 (45%). Patients reported improvement almost immediately after the first procedure.
- Conclusion (abstract): an implant with an internal central port and integral sealing screw may be used for drainage, irrigation, observation, and further treatment of the maxillary sinus in CRS.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Disclosure (from the landing page):** the senior author (Nahlieli) is a clinical advisor to Upheal Dental Ltd (Israel); no funding reported. Ethics approval from two Israeli medical centers (Barzilai, Galil), retrospective.

## 7. Glossary

- **CRS (Chronic Recurrent Sinusitis)**: chronic recurrent maxillary sinusitis as used in this paper.
- **Trans-implant lavage**: irrigation of the maxillary sinus through the central port of an implant placed into the sinus floor.
- **Internal removable sealing screw**: screw closing the implant's central port between treatment sessions.
- **CBCT**: cone beam computed tomography.
