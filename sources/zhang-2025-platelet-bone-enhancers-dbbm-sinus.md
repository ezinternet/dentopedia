## Why Ingested

기존 [[sinus-lift/lateral/almutairi-2025-lprf-dbbm-sinus-augmentation-sr-ma]]가 L-PRF만 한정(5 RCTs, n=140)해 DBBM 대비 신생골·잔류이식재 유의 개선을 보고했는데, 본 SR+MA(Zhang 2025, 16 studies, n=372)는 PRP·PRF·PRGF 전체 혈소판 농축물을 포괄해 그 결론을 더 큰 표본으로 재확인하면서도 중요한 세분화를 추가한다: 잔류이식재 감소는 PRF/PRGF 하위군에서만 유의하고 PRP 하위군에서는 유의하지 않다. 임플란트 안정성/생존율에 유의차 없다는 점도 일치한다.

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review + meta-analysis (PRISMA; 16 studies, 372 patients, 455 procedures) of platelet-derived bone enhancers (PRP, PRF, PRGF) as adjuncts to deproteinized bovine bone matrix (DBBM) in two-stage maxillary sinus floor elevation.

Bone enhancers significantly increased new bone formation (MD=5.92, 95%CI 2.17–9.67, p=0.002) and reduced residual graft volume (MD=−1.93, 95%CI −2.25 to −1.61, p<0.001), but had no significant effect on immediate implant stability (RFA) or implant survival rate (RR=1.03, p=0.252).

Subgroup analysis shows PRF/PRGF (not PRP) drive the residual-graft reduction, and only PRF significantly improves new bone formation on its own — clinical benefit is enhancer-type specific, not a class effect, and long-term implant survival data remain limited.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰+메타분석(PRISMA; 16개 연구, 372명, 455건 시술): 2단계 상악동거상술에서 탈단백우골기질(Deproteinized Bovine Bone Matrix, DBBM)에 혈소판 유래 골증강제(PRP·PRF·PRGF)를 병용한 효과 평가.

혈소판 증강제 병용 시 신생골 형성 유의 증가(MD=5.92, p=0.002), 잔류이식재량 유의 감소(MD=−1.93, p<0.001); 그러나 즉시 임플란트 안정성(공진주파수분석, Resonance Frequency Analysis, RFA)·임플란트 생존율(RR=1.03, p=0.252)에는 유의차 없음.

하위군 분석상 잔류이식재 감소는 PRF·PRGF에서만 유의(PRP는 유의하지 않음)하고 신생골 형성 단독 유의 효과도 PRF에서만 확인됨 — 효과는 증강제 종류별로 다르며 계열 전체 효과가 아님; 장기 임플란트 생존 자료는 부족.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Largest pooled synthesis to date specifically restricted to two-stage sinus floor elevation with DBBM ± platelet-derived enhancer (16 studies, 372 patients, 455 procedures), spanning PRP, PRF, and PRGF as distinct subgroups.
- Demonstrates a genuine class-level bone-neoformation benefit (MD=5.92%) that is not sensitive to omission of the case-control study, with non-significant publication bias (Egger p=0.189, Begg p=0.102).
- Shows residual graft volume reduction is driven by PRF (MD=−4.32) and PRGF (MD=−1.89) subgroups, not PRP — directly clarifies that "platelet concentrate" is not a uniform intervention.
- Confirms implant stability (RFA) and survival rate are unaffected by adjunct type, healing interval, or DBBM particle size in every subgroup tested — decouples histomorphometric gain from functional/clinical implant outcomes.
- Subgroup analysis by healing interval (4/5/6/8 months) and DBBM particle size (0.25–1 mm vs 1–2 mm) identifies 4-month and 6-month intervals, and 0.25–1 mm particle size, as showing the clearest new-bone-formation benefit.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Databases: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane CENTRAL, Web of Science, Scopus, Google Scholar (inception–Feb 2025), plus reference-list hand-search.
- Inclusion: two-stage (delayed) implant placement after maxillary sinus augmentation with a defined healing interval; DBBM ± PRP/PRF/PRGF adjunct; RCT, case-control, CCT, or cohort design.
- Exclusion: one-stage (immediate) protocols without a separable two-stage subgroup; pre-clinical/animal studies; case series, reviews, SR/MA, comments/letters; duplicates.
- Screening: 387 records → 232 after dedup (155 removed) → 31 full-text assessed → 16 included (14 RCTs, 2 case-control studies; two-, three-, or four-arm designs).
- N = 372 patients / 455 procedures; mean age 43.5–56.9 years; male % 20–80%; alveolar ridge height inclusion thresholds of <3, 4, 5, or 7 mm across studies.
- Quality assessment: Cochrane risk-of-bias tool (RCTs) and Newcastle-Ottawa Scale (case-control studies; both scored 8/9).
- Statistics: mean difference (MD) for continuous outcomes, risk ratio (RR) for binary outcomes, random-effects model if I²>50% else fixed-effects; sensitivity analysis (leave-one-out) when heterogeneity present and ≥3 studies; funnel plot + Egger's/Begg's tests when ≥5 studies, with trim-and-fill adjustment if bias detected; subgroup analyses by enhancer type (PRP/PRF/PRGF), healing interval, and DBBM particle size; R v4.3.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | MD/RR | 95% CI | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| New bone formation | MD +5.92 | 2.17 to 9.67 | 0.002 |
| Residual graft volume | MD −1.93 | −2.25 to −1.61 | <0.001 |
| Graft resorption rate | MD +0.24 | −4.75 to 5.23 | 0.925 (NS) |
| Fibrous tissue % | MD −0.97 | −7.33 to 5.40 | 0.765 (NS) |
| Immediate implant stability (RFA) | MD −4.62 (excl. outlier: −1.64) | −11.02 to 1.78 | 0.157 (NS) |
| Implant survival rate | RR 1.03 | 0.98 to 1.07 | 0.252 (NS) |

Subgroup detail: new bone formation benefit reaches significance only in the PRF subgroup (MD=7.54) and in 4-month (MD=13.51) or 6-month (MD=5.40) healing-interval subgroups and 0.25–1 mm particle-size subgroup (MD=7.68). Residual graft reduction is significant in PRF (MD=−4.32) and PRGF (MD=−1.89) subgroups, and across 4/6/8-month intervals and both particle-size strata; PRP subgroup alone shows no significant residual-graft reduction. Begg's test flagged significant publication bias for implant survival (p=0.011), but trim-and-fill adjustment left the null result unchanged (RR=1.02, p=0.390).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- No standardized PRP/PRF/PRGF preparation protocol across included studies (blood volume 10–90 mL, centrifugal force 300–580G) — a likely heterogeneity source despite subgroup stratification.
- DBBM dosage/particle size inconsistently reported (only 7 and 9 studies respectively), limiting some subgroup analyses.
- Small per-study sample sizes overall; authors call for multi-center, large-sample, prospective RCTs.
- Long-term implant survival/function data are sparse — the review's implant-outcome conclusions rest mainly on short-term follow-up.
- 4/14 RCTs had high risk of bias in allocation concealment; 3/14 in blinding of outcome assessment.

## 6. Related Work

- Contrasts with Raghoebar et al. (2005), cited in the discussion, which found no benefit of PRP on autogenous-bone sinus grafts.
- Directly comparable/complementary to [[sinus-lift/lateral/almutairi-2025-lprf-dbbm-sinus-augmentation-sr-ma]] (L-PRF-only SR+MA, 5 RCTs) and [[bone-regeneration/idiri-2023-prf-xenograft-sinus-ridge-augmentation-sr]] (all-PRF SR without meta-analysis, 7 RCTs, no significant benefit found).

## 7. Glossary

- DBBM (Deproteinized Bovine Bone Matrix): decalcified/deproteinized bovine-derived bone graft substitute (e.g., Bio-Oss).
- PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma): autologous liquid platelet concentrate obtained by centrifugation, releases growth factors rapidly.
- PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin): second-generation autologous fibrin matrix (no anticoagulant), releases growth factors gradually.
- PRGF (Plasma Rich in Growth Factors): platelet concentrate with high fibrin content and controlled growth-factor release, prepared differently from PRP/PRF.
- RFA (Resonance Frequency Analysis): method for measuring implant stability (ISQ) via resonance frequency.
- MD / RR (Mean Difference / Risk Ratio): pooled effect-size measures for continuous vs. binary outcomes in meta-analysis.
