## Three-line Summary

EFP/AAP Workshop SR+MA (Journal of Clinical Periodontology 2023; up to August 2022; interventional and observational studies, ≥6-month follow-up) distinguishing primordial prevention (risk-factor control before implant placement) from primary prevention (maintaining peri-implant health after placement).

Risk-factor control — smoking cessation, treatment of periodontal disease before implant placement, and oral hygiene reinforcement — reduces the incidence of peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis; evidence is strongest for periodontal history and smoking as modifiable risk determinants.

This supporting SR+MA underpins the EFP S3 clinical practice guideline for prevention and treatment of peri-implant diseases; however, heterogeneity in risk-factor definitions and mixed study designs limit the strength of individual pooled estimates.

## 세줄요약

EFP/AAP 워크숍 SR+MA(J Clin Periodontol 2023; 검색 2022년 8월; 중재·관찰 연구, ≥6개월 추적): 임플란트 식립 전 위험인자 통제(Primordial Prevention)와 식립 후 건강 유지(Primary Prevention)를 구분해 분석.

금연·임플란트 식립 전 치주염 치료·구강위생 강화 등 위험인자 통제는 임플란트주위 점막염(Peri-implant Mucositis) 및 임플란트주위염(Peri-implantitis) 발생률을 감소시키며, 치주염 병력과 흡연이 가장 강력한 수정 가능 위험인자로 확인.

본 SR+MA는 EFP S3 임플란트주위질환 예방·치료 임상 가이드라인의 근거 자료; 단, 위험인자 정의 이질성과 혼합 연구 설계로 개별 풀링 추정치의 근거 강도가 제한됨.

## 2. Key Contributions
- EFP S3 가이드라인 supporting SR+MA.
- Primordial (pre-placement) + primary (post-placement) prevention 구분.
- Modifiable risk factors quantified.

## 3. Methodology
SR+MA up to Aug 2022; interventional + observational studies; ≥6 mo follow-up; outcomes peri-implant mucositis and/or peri-implantitis.

## 4. Key Results
[근거강함] Risk factor control (smoking cessation, periodontal management before implant, oral hygiene reinforcement) reduces peri-implant mucositis and peri-implantitis incidence.

## 5. Limitations
- Mixed observational/interventional
- Heterogeneity in risk factor definitions
