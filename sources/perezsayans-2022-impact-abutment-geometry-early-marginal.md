## Why Ingested

사용자 요청으로 지대주 (Abutment) 형태가 초기 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)에 미치는 영향을 직접 비교한 이중맹검 무작위 대조 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT)을 추가한다. 동일 연구팀의 24개월 [[implants/seijas-naya-2025-concave-straight-abutment-bone-rct]]의 6개월 선행 근거이며, 후향적으로 지대주 높이의 보호 효과를 제시한 [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]]에 대해 "높이 효과는 형태에 따라 달라진다"는 상호작용 근거를 더한다.

## Three-line Summary

Double-blind randomized clinical trial (25 patients, 77 implants) comparing concave (Slim) versus cylindrical (Straight) abutments inserted with a one-abutment-one-time protocol and followed for 6 months.

Early global marginal bone loss (MBL) at 6 months was −0.6 ± 0.7 mm with cylindrical abutments versus −0.4 ± 0.6 mm with concave abutments (p = 0.030); the mixed-model concave coefficient was 2.8231 (95% CI 1.4379 to 4.2083, p = 0.0002).

Concave geometry reduced early MBL, but follow-up stopped at 6 months and the height-by-shape interaction was negative (−1.3340, p = 0.0001), so the height rule cannot be transferred across shapes.

## 세줄요약

이중맹검 무작위 대조 임상시험 (Randomized Controlled Trial, RCT) — 환자 25명·임플란트 77개; 일회 지대주 일회 장착 (One-Abutment-One-Time, OAOT) 프로토콜로 오목형 (Concave, Slim) 대 원통형 (Cylindrical, Straight) 지대주를 비교하고 6개월 추적.

6개월 조기 전체 변연골소실 (Marginal Bone Loss, MBL)은 원통형 −0.6 ± 0.7 mm, 오목형 −0.4 ± 0.6 mm(p = 0.030); 혼합선형회귀에서 오목형 계수는 2.8231(95% 신뢰구간 1.4379~4.2083, p = 0.0002).

오목형 형태가 조기 골소실을 줄였으나 추적 기간이 6개월로 제한되고 높이×형태 상호작용이 음수(−1.3340, p = 0.0001)여서 높이 규칙을 형태 간에 그대로 적용할 수 없음.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Randomized, double-blind comparison of abutment *shape* at constant implant diameter (4 × 10 mm) and with a single abutment placed at surgery — isolating the horizontal geometry variable from the height variable used in earlier trials.
- Reports the mixed-model intercept and shape coefficients separately for mesial, distal and average MBL, plus a significant shape × height interaction.
- Supplies a 6-month predecessor to the same group's 24-month shape × height RCT ([[implants/seijas-naya-2025-concave-straight-abutment-bone-rct]]).
- Provides an independently randomized test of the "taller abutment preserves bone" claim from [[implants/mbl/galindomoreno-2014-prosthetic-abutment-height-is-a]], showing that the direction reverses with concave geometry.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Balanced, randomized, double-blind clinical trial with two parallel arms and no control group; CONSORT-based reporting; independent data-monitoring board and one formal interim analysis at 50% of subjects/follow-up (no trial correction).
- **Population**: 25 patients, 80 implants placed; 3 excluded (2 osseointegration failures, 1 concave abutment fracture) → 77 implants analysed (38 cylindrical, 39 concave). Mean patient age 56.7 ± 10.9 years (36.3–77.5); 10 men, 15 women.
- **Implants**: 80 hexagonal internal-connection implants (IPX, Nueva Galimplant), 4 × 10 mm, Ti-IV, 11° conical connection, Nanoblastplus surface (Ra 1.7 µm, 99.9% TiO₂), placed in mature bone ≥6 months post-extraction, subcrestally (mean 0.9 ± 0.8 mm below crest).
- **Abutments**: 40 cylindrical (Straight™) and 40 concave (Slim™) antirotational screw-retained abutments, 2 or 3 mm high; all placed at surgery (one-abutment-one-time) at ≥25 Ncm (35 Ncm when primary stability allowed).
- **Blinding**: patients and the data analyst were unaware of allocation; randomization by implant location and abutment type using an SPSS 24.0 macro.
- **Outcomes**: primary — MBL by digital intraoral radiography at 8 weeks (preloading) and 6 months (post-loading, 8 months after implantation); secondary — RFA/ISQ, periodontal indices, smoking/bruxism, tooth position, biotype, abutment type.
- **Measurement reliability**: two independent observers; kappa 0.82 for probing depth, 0.93 for MBL.
- **Statistics**: implant as unit of analysis; mixed linear regression with fixed/random effects weighted for implants per patient (R 4.1.1); alpha 0.05. A priori sample size assumed a 0.5 mm effect size, alpha 0.05, power 90% → 40 implants per arm (80 total, 15% loss allowance).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Cylindrical (n = 38) | Concave (n = 39) | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| MBL at 8 weeks, average (mm) | −0.50 (0.51) | −0.18 (0.35) | 0.020 |
| MBL at 6 months, average (mm) | −0.11 (0.46) | 0.002 (0.32) | 0.100 |
| Global MBL, average (mm) — Table 2 | −0.62 (0.72) | −0.17 (0.32) | 0.001 |
| Global MBL, average (mm) — Abstract/Results text | −0.6 ± 0.7 | −0.4 ± 0.6 | 0.030 |

- Global MBL was significantly greater in the cylindrical group at mesial (p = 0.029) and distal (p = 0.004) sites as well as on average.
- Mixed linear regression, average MBL: intercept (cylindrical) ES −1.3730 (t = −2.4893, p = 0.0139); concave (Slim) coefficient ES 2.8231 (95% CI 1.4379 to 4.2083, t = 4.0957, p = 0.0002); abutment height ES 1.0633 (p = 0.0001); Slim × height interaction ES −1.3340 (95% CI −1.9685 to −0.6994, t = −4.2246, p = 0.0001).
- At 8 weeks the between-group difference was already present (cylindrical −0.5 ± 0.5 mm vs concave −0.2 ± 0.4 mm, p = 0.010); after loading the average difference narrowed and was not significant (p = 0.100).
- Periodontal indices: bleeding 16.9 ± 11.5% → 8.4 ± 8.8%, plaque 12.3 ± 5.2% → 29.5 ± 25.4%, probing depth 3.9 ± 2.2 → 3.4 ± 1.2 mm; no significant between-group differences.
- Mean ISQ 69.1 ± 10.9 at implant placement (range 23–82.3), 71.3 ± 7 after abutment insertion; no differences by abutment type.
- Adverse events: 2 implant failures (2.6%), 1 concave abutment fracture (0.8%); no mucositis, crown loosening/mobility or ceramic chipping.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- **Internal inconsistency (source verbatim retained)**: the Abstract and Results text give the concave global MBL as −0.4 ± 0.6 mm, whereas Table 2 reports −0.17 (0.32) mm for the same group; both are reproduced above without correction. The abstract's reported CI for the cylindrical intercept (−2.5919 to −0.1327) also differs from the table's average-model CI (−2.4627 to −0.2833) and matches the distal model instead.
- Follow-up ends at 6 months, so the result concerns *early* MBL only; the authors explicitly call for longer confirmation (partly answered by the 24-month RCT in [[implants/seijas-naya-2025-concave-straight-abutment-bone-rct]]).
- Anterior esthetic-zone teeth (13–23, 33–43) were excluded; posterior healed sites only.
- Prosthesis shape and crown emergence profile were not controlled, although the Discussion names them as possible MBL modifiers.
- The height effect is not a simple main effect: the significant negative Slim × height coefficient means increasing height reduced mean bone level in the concave group while increasing it in the cylindrical group. The authors call for dedicated height studies.
- Small patient sample (25 patients / 77 implants) with more implants than patients; mixed modelling weighted for clustering, but all arms came from a single Spanish centre.

## 7. Glossary

- **MBL (Marginal Bone Loss)** — radiographic bone-level change from the reference point at the implant neck to the most coronal peri-implant bone crest; negative values denote loss in this paper.
- **Concave / Slim abutment** — antirotational abutment whose transmucosal portion narrows along its whole height, leaving more room for peri-implant soft tissue.
- **Cylindrical / Straight abutment** — conventional abutment with a constant (straight) transmucosal diameter.
- **One-abutment-one-time** — definitive abutment inserted at implant surgery and never disconnected.
- **ISQ (Implant Stability Quotient)** — resonance frequency analysis measure of implant stability.
- **Mixed linear regression** — model with fixed effects (time, abutment type, height, interaction) and random effects accounting for multiple implants per patient.
