## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (Odovtos, Online First 2026) appraising evidence on supracrestal tissue attachment (STA, historically "biologic width") management through the OCEBM framework, synthesizing comparative and evidence-grade tables across conventional, laser-assisted, and digitally guided techniques.

Conventional surgery (gingivectomy, access flap with osteotomy/osteoplasty) remains reliable at Level 2/Grade B when guided by biologic and prosthetic principles; digitally guided workflows (CBCT + intraoral scanning + CAD/CAM guides) add precision and reproducibility (Level 2/Grade B), laser-assisted procedures improve soft-tissue comfort but lack bone-cutting validation (Level 3/Grade C).

Key anchors: physiological STA vertical dimension 2.73–3.0 mm, pre-surgical periodontal stabilization Level 1/Grade A, deep margin elevation Level 2/Grade B, CBCT soft-tissue retraction only Level 3/Grade C, with AI-augmented CBCT/scanner planning flagged as the emerging frontier.

## 세줄요약

임플란트·보철 경계의 생물학적 기준인 초치조상부 조직부착(STA, 구 biologic width) 관리를 재래식·레이저·디지털 3축으로 옥스퍼드 근거등급(OCEBM)으로 평가한 내러티브 리뷰(Odovtos 2026 Online First).

재래식 술식(치은절제술·판막+골절제/골성형)은 생물학적·보철 원칙 하에 여전히 신뢰 가능(Level 2/Grade B), CBCT+구강스캔+CAD/CAM 가이드 기반 디지털 워크플로는 정밀·재현성 강화(Level 2/Grade B), 레이저(다이오드·Er:YAG)는 연조직 정밀·지혈·이환율 감소에 유리하나 골절제 근거는 제한적(Level 3/Grade C).

핵심 기준값: STA 생리적 수직폭 2.73–3.0 mm; 술전 치주안정화 Level 1/Grade A 필수; deep margin elevation Level 2/Grade B; CBCT의 연조직 묘출은 Level 3/Grade C로 제한적 검증; AI+CBCT+스캔 통합 개인맞춤 계획이 향후 방향.
