## Why Ingested

Adds the treatment/remineralization side of the dental-erosion category, where [[wiki/dental-erosion/gomez-2026-anti-erosive-dentifrices-umbrella-review]] ranks anti-erosive dentifrice chemistries; this small in vitro SR gives a discordant fluoride-toothpaste reading and the only CPP-ACP (tooth mousse) arm in the category. Note: study design (in vitro) is inferred — the PMC text dropped the italic words "in vitro" throughout.

## Three-line Summary

Qualitative systematic review (PRISMA, OSF-registered; 6 databases + grey literature; no meta-analysis) of in vitro studies on human permanent teeth treated with topical anti-erosion agents vs an artificial-saliva comparator; 522 records screened, only 4 studies included (n = 35, 150, 40, 36 specimens), 3 at low and 1 at high risk of bias.

Two fluoride-toothpaste studies showed no clear anti-erosion benefit over artificial saliva (in the Brazilian study, Sensodyne Pronamel and Elmex Erosion Protection had the lowest surface loss but did not differ from the control, p > 0.05), whereas the two CPP-ACP (Tooth Mousse) studies reported benefit: microhardness +13.27% vs +2.53% with artificial saliva, and significantly reduced enamel wear (p < 0.001).

Evidence is four heterogeneous laboratory studies without a water-only negative control and without acquired pellicle, so the CPP-ACP signal is hypothesis-level and the 'fluoride does not work' reading should not be generalized; it does not override the higher-level umbrella review favoring stannous fluoride dentifrices.

## 세줄요약

체계적 문헌고찰 (Systematic Review, SR; 메타분석 없음): 인간 영구치에 국소 항침식제를 적용하고 인공타액 (artificial saliva) 대조군과 비교한 실험실 연구 (in vitro) 4편만 포함 (표본 n = 35, 150, 40, 36; 522편 검색), 비뚤림 위험 (risk of bias) 3편 낮음·1편 높음.

불소 치약 2편은 인공타액 대비 뚜렷한 항침식 효과가 없었고 (브라질 연구에서 Sensodyne Pronamel과 Elmex Erosion Protection이 표면 손실 최저였으나 대조군과 차이 없음, p > 0.05), 카제인 포스포펩타이드-비정질 인산칼슘 (Casein Phosphopeptide-Amorphous Calcium Phosphate, CPP-ACP) 2편은 미세경도 +13.27% (인공타액 +2.53%), 법랑질 마모 유의 감소 (p < 0.001)를 보고.

포함 연구가 4편이고 방법이 이질적이며 물만 쓴 음성대조군과 획득피막 (acquired pellicle)이 없어 CPP-ACP 신호는 가설 수준이고, '불소는 효과 없음'으로 일반화하면 안 된다. 스태너스불화물 치약을 우위로 본 상위 수준 우산 리뷰 (umbrella review)를 뒤집지 못한다.

## 1. Document Information

- Journal: PeerJ 2022;10:e13864 (published 2022-11-08). Source: PubMed PMID 36389398 / PMC9651041; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.13864).
- Design: systematic review, qualitative synthesis, PRISMA; OSF registration 10.17605/OSF.IO/XMFNZ. Authors from Federal University of Pará (Brazil) and University of Alberta (Canada).
- Study mix: all 4 included studies are laboratory (in vitro) studies on extracted human teeth; no clinical trials were included.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First qualitative synthesis restricted to human-enamel in vitro studies comparing topical anti-erosion agents with artificial saliva.
- Flags CPP-ACP (Tooth Mousse) as the only agent with a significant microhardness gain and wear reduction among included products.
- Shows that commercial anti-erosion toothpastes differ by abrasivity and deposit formation, not simply by fluoride content.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- PICO: eroded human permanent teeth; any topical agent; comparator artificial saliva; outcome enamel change.
- Databases: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, Cochrane, Scopus, Lilacs; grey literature Google Scholar and OpenGrey. Two independent reviewers, third for disagreement.
- Excluded: bovine teeth, dentine-only, primary teeth, no intervention group, reviews, cases, opinion pieces.
- Risk of bias: Cochrane tool adapted; key domains allocation concealment, participant blinding, outcome-assessment blinding; high RoB = severe problems in two key domains.
- Flow: 522 records, 305 duplicates removed, 217 screened, 13 full-text, 4 included.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Study (country) | n | Teeth | Outcome | Agents | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Czech Republic | 35 | third molars | microhardness | Pronamel, Elmex Erosion Protection, BioRepair Plus, SensiShield, Enamel Care vs artificial saliva / Elmex Erosion rinse | Enamel Care formed compact deposit layer; Pronamel and BioRepair no protective deposits; Elmex Erosion and SensiShield highly abrasive, low anti-erosion action |
| Brazil | 150 | premolars | microhardness | 8 toothpastes vs artificial saliva, Colgate Caries Protection | All groups progressive surface loss; Pronamel and Elmex Erosion lowest loss, not different from control or each other (p > 0.05); Regenerate and Blend-a-Med Pro Expert highest |
| Thailand | 40 | incisors | microhardness | CPP-ACP vs artificial saliva | Microhardness +13.27% vs +2.53%; no significant interaction among CPP-ACP, artificial saliva, deionized water (p = 0.548) |
| United Kingdom | 36 | third molars | profilometry | Tooth Mousse (CPP-ACP) vs mousse without CPP-ACP vs artificial saliva | Tooth Mousse significantly reduced enamel wear (p < 0.001) |

Risk of bias: 3 of 4 low, 1 high (lack of quantitative data). All four had blinding issues for the test-machine operator. Authors conclude CPP-ACP therapies can be beneficial and fluoride toothpastes showed no anti-erosion effect, both with caution.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Only 4 studies, heterogeneous substrate, erosion stages, evaluation times and erosive protocols (citric acid in 3, Coca-Cola in 1); no meta-analysis possible.
- Absent water-only negative control can distort between-group differences; acquired pellicle cannot be reproduced in vitro.
- One of the two fluoride-toothpaste studies was at high risk of bias. The text itself says other recent studies found fluoride after acid challenge reduced enamel loss.
- Internal inconsistency in the source: the abstract says CPP-ACP was the only product that significantly increased microhardness and reduced wear, while the Results text lists APF gel, Elmex Erosion and Pronamel as best for enamel protection; APF gel is not in the Table 1 agent list.
- Authors call for standardized, well-designed studies to reach high-certainty evidence.

## 7. Glossary

- Dental erosion: chemical loss of mineralized dental tissue by nonbacterial acids.
- CPP-ACP: casein phosphopeptide-amorphous calcium phosphate, milk-derived agent stabilizing calcium phosphate (Tooth Mousse).
- Surface microhardness: hardness of enamel surface; loss indicates demineralization.
- Profilometry: surface-loss measurement by stylus/optical profile.
- Artificial saliva: in vitro storage medium used as the no-treatment comparator.
