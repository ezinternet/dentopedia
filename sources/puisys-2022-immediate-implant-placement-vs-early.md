## Why Ingested

This 1-year RCT directly compares immediate implant placement with immediate provisioning and socket preservation against early placement with GBR and delayed loading in the esthetic maxillary anterior zone. It contributes evidence on placement-timing outcomes (primary difference: 0.2 mm mid-buccal mucosal level gain vs no change; comparable esthetics via PES >12 both groups) and operational efficiency (test group: half the chair time of control group). Reinforces [[immediate-implant/buser-2017-implant-placement-timing-post-extraction-esthetic]] on immediate placement outcomes and relates to [[immediate-implant/esthetic-soft-tissue/peitsinis-2025-implant-placement-timing-loading-protocols]].

## Three-line Summary
(Line 1: study type, n, context — what was studied)
Randomized controlled trial, n=50 patients (25 immediate, 25 early), 1-year follow-up comparing immediate placement with immediate provisioning/socket preservation vs. early placement with GBR/delayed loading in maxillary anterior esthetic zone (intact bone walls, interproximal bone intact).

(Line 2: primary result / key finding with numbers)
No implant failures in either group; Pink Esthetic Score (PES) comparable at 1 year: immediate group 12.8 ± 1.19 vs. early group 12.5 ± 1.36 (p = .362); mid-buccal mucosal level change: immediate group +0.2 ± 1.02 mm (p = .047) vs. early group 0 mm; crestal bone changes minimal in both (p > .05).

(Line 3: clinical implication or key limitation)
Both protocols achieved excellent esthetic outcomes within 1 year; immediate placement reduced chair time by ~50% but esthetic gain was modest; patient-selection criteria (intact walls, no infection) critical to success; limited to 1-year follow-up.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Direct RCT comparison of immediate vs. early placement timing in esthetic zone with placement-timing as primary variable (not confounded by loading protocol difference)
- Quantified soft-tissue response (MBML) favoring immediate placement (+0.2 mm gain, p=.047) as a measurable esthetic advantage
- Demonstrated operational efficiency gain (50% chair-time reduction with immediate protocol)
- Confirmed comparable long-term PES (>12) at 1 year for both placement timings when patient-selection criteria met
- Isolated immediate provisionalization as a variable controllable independent of placement-timing axis

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Parallel-group RCT (1:1 randomization)
- **Sample**: 50 patients (recruited: n unclear from abstract, ITT analysis); 25 assigned immediate, 25 assigned early
- **Inclusion**: Single-tooth gap in maxillary anterior (central incisor to first premolar); intact alveolar bone walls; interproximal bone intact; ≥18 years old
- **Exclusion**: Active infection; medical contraindications; smokers; heavy alcohol use; pregnancy/lactation; systemic disease affecting healing
- **Intervention — Immediate group** (test): Type 1 immediate implant placement (same extraction visit); socket preservation with bone graft + barrier membrane; immediate provisional crown (same day delivery)
- **Intervention — Early group** (control): Immediate socket grafting (extraction visit); 4-month osseous healing period; then implant placement (Type 2 early placement); guided bone regeneration (GBR) with barrier; delayed final crown (after osseointegration, conventional timeline)
- **Follow-up**: Baseline (extraction), 1-year final follow-up; measurements at both timepoints
- **Primary outcomes**: Pink Esthetic Score (PES), mid-buccal mucosal level (MBML), crestal bone changes (CBC), patient chair time
- **Secondary outcomes**: Peri-implant soft-tissue parameters (not detailed in abstract)
- **Blinding**: Not stated in abstract (likely open-label, placement timing cannot be blinded)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

| Outcome | Immediate Group (n=25) | Early Group (n=25) | P-value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Implant failures at 1 year | 0 | 0 | — |
| PES at 1 year (≥12 = excellent) | 12.8 ± 1.19 | 12.5 ± 1.36 | 0.362 |
| MBML change, baseline to 1 year (mm) | +0.2 ± 1.02 | 0 (no change) | 0.047 |
| Crestal bone change — mesial (mm) | 0.1 ± 0.21 | 0.2 ± 0.25 | 0.540 |
| Crestal bone change — distal (mm) | 0.2 ± 0.22 | 0.3 ± 0.19 | 0.462 |
| Patient chair time (minutes) | 127 ± 13 | 259 ± 15 | <0.001 |

Key findings:
- **No implant failures** in either arm across 1-year follow-up (100% survival in both groups)
- **Pink Esthetic Score** (PES) comparable and excellent in both: 12.8 vs 12.5 (p=.362), indicating no significant esthetic outcome difference
- **Mid-buccal mucosal level** showed marginal gain in immediate group (+0.2 mm) vs. no change in early group (p=.047); this was the only significant difference in soft-tissue response
- **Crestal bone levels** remained stable after first 6 months in both groups; mesial and distal CBC minimal and statistically equivalent (p >.05)
- **Chair time reduction** in immediate protocol: 127 min vs. 259 min (p < .001); immediate protocol saves ~132 minutes per patient (50% reduction)
- Conclusion statement (from abstract): "Both treatment protocols resulted in excellent esthetic outcomes with PES >12 after 1-year follow-up"

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Short follow-up**: Only 1-year data presented; long-term (3–5 year) outcomes not yet available from this trial
- **Small sample size**: n=50 limits power for detecting subtle esthetic or bone-level differences; confidence intervals not provided
- **No subgroup analysis by patient characteristics**: Biotype, initial ridge anatomy, or systemic factors not stratified
- **Operator/center**: Single Lithuanian center; generalizability to other populations/operators unclear
- **Early group protocol variation**: Early placement timing "Type 2 vs Type 3" not clearly delineated (4-month timing places it on borderline)
- **Operative time vs. chair time**: Study reports total chair time (127 vs 259 min) but does not break down operative/restorative phases separately
- **Limited outcome domains**: No complications/adverse events explicitly tallied; no patient satisfaction/quality-of-life scores reported
- **Confounding by treatment intensity**: Early group received GBR while immediate group did not; GBR represents a distinct surgical intensity variable beyond timing alone

## 7. Glossary
- **Immediate implant (Type 1)**: Dental implant placed into extraction socket at time of tooth removal (same surgical visit)
- **Early implant (Type 2/3)**: Implant placed 4–16 weeks post-extraction (healed socket, partial bone fill)
- **Socket preservation**: Grafting and/or barrier-membrane management of extraction socket to minimize post-extraction dimensional change
- **Immediate provisional restoration**: Temporary crown/bridge placed same day as implant placement (before osseointegration complete)
- **Guided bone regeneration (GBR)**: Surgical technique using bone graft material and barrier membrane to regenerate bone in vertical/horizontal defects
- **Pink Esthetic Score (PES)**: Validated 10-point clinical scale assessing peri-implant soft-tissue esthetics (papilla, contour, color, texture, mucogingival margins)
- **Mid-buccal mucosal level (MBML)**: Vertical position of buccal soft tissue margin relative to incisal edge or reference point; change = tissue resorption (negative) or coronal gain (positive)
- **Crestal bone changes (CBC)**: Radiographic bone-level change measured at mesial and distal interproximal sites; zero or minimal change indicates good bone remodeling control
