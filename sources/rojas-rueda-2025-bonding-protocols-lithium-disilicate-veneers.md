사용자가 인제스트를 위해 repo 루트에 둔 논문. 기존 [[wiki/veneers/hajeer-2026-lithium-disilicate-zirconia-veneer-retention-sr-ma]]가 LDS 비니어의 시험관 접착강도(광중합 시멘트)를 정량 비교한 데 비해, 본 논문은 임상 술식 단계(HF 에칭·실란·프라이머/접착제·러버댐 격리)를 증례보고 기반으로 정리하고 실제 증례로 보여 주어 이를 보완한다. 세라믹 접착 일반론은 [[wiki/dental-materials/ceramic/blatz-2018-resin-bonding-high-strength-ceramics-review]]와 연결.

## Three-line Summary

Narrative review (case reports 1980-2024, single database) plus one clinical case: 27-year-old woman, 10 hand-crafted lithium disilicate (LDS) veneers (second premolar to second premolar) bonded under rubber dam, 2-year follow-up.

The 8 case reports reviewed (Table 2) all use hydrofluoric acid (5-10%, 20 s to 1 min) then silane on the ceramic and phosphoric-acid etch plus adhesive on the tooth; case protocol: HF 20 s, silane 60 s, adhesive, 29-micron Al2O3 sandblast, 37% phosphoric acid 15 s, light-cure cement (Variolink Esthetic LC), 20 s per surface.

Evidence is qualitative only (no meta-analysis, PRISMA not followed, satisfaction self-reported, n=1, 2-year follow-up); the practical message is to follow manufacturer times, minimize dentin exposure, and bond under total (preferably non-latex) rubber-dam isolation.

## 세줄요약

서술적 문헌고찰(1980-2024 증례보고, 단일 데이터베이스) + 임상 증례 1건: 27세 여성, 이차소구치~이차소구치 리튬 디실리케이트 (Lithium Disilicate, LDS) 비니어 10개를 러버댐 하에서 접착, 2년 추적.

고찰한 증례보고 8편(Table 2)은 모두 세라믹에 불산 (Hydrofluoric Acid, HF) 5-10%(20초~1분) 후 실란, 치아에 인산 에칭 후 접착제를 사용; 본 증례 프로토콜은 HF 20초, 실란 60초, 접착제, 29 마이크론 알루미나 샌드블라스트, 37% 인산 15초, 광중합 시멘트(Variolink Esthetic LC), 면당 20초 광조사.

근거는 정성적 수준(메타분석 없음, PRISMA 미준수, 만족도 자가보고, n=1, 추적 2년)이며, 실용적 메시지는 제조사 시간 준수, 상아질 노출 최소화, 전체(가급적 비라텍스) 러버댐 격리 하 접착이다.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Search December 2024, MEDLINE (PubMed) and Google, January 1980 to December 2024, English only.
- Included: clinical case reports of labial LDS veneers describing all bonding steps. Excluded: in vitro studies, retrospective studies, reviews, veneers on implant abutments/crowns, cases lacking bonding-step description.
- Selection by two reviewers (C.A.J., H.N.), disagreements resolved by a third. No quantitative analysis.
- Case: 27-year-old female; stained composite veneers on both central incisors, incisal wear canine to canine, diastemata on the right; declined bleaching and composite veneers. Diagnostic wax-up (Wax GEO Classic) and intra-oral mock-up (Integrity); 10 veneers from right to left second premolar; butt-joint minimally invasive preparation through the mock-up (Solution Laminate Veneer Preparation System), cord #00/#0, PVS impression; hand-crafted IPS e.max Ceram veneers on type IV stone die.
- Bonding: HF (Porcelain Etch) 20 s; silane (Monobond Plus) 60 s; adhesive (OptiBond FL) air-thinned; rubber dam first molar to first molar; teeth sandblasted with water and 29-micron Al2O3; 37% phosphoric acid 15 s; OptiBond FL; Variolink Esthetic LC light-shade cement; cure 20 s facial, 20 s interproximal, 20 s mesial, 20 s distal. Night guard provided.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- Table 2 case reports (year; protocol summary): Schmitter 2012 (0.4 mm veneer; 35% phosphoric acid 20 s, silane 1 min; intact at 1 year); Soares 2014 (9.5% HF 20 s, silane 60 s; light-cure cement); Zandinejad 2015 (10% HF 25 s; RelyX Veneer); Martins 2019 (5% HF, silane with hot-air heat treatment, 4 min ultrasonic bath); Souza 2020 (Monobond Etch and Prime 20 s + 40 s, 2-year follow-up); Tyagi 2022 (9.5% HF 1 min, silane 60 s; dual-cure RelyX U200); Das 2023 (5% HF 20 s, silane 1 min, 2% chlorhexidine on exposed dentin); Hu 2024 (5% HF 20 s; Panavia V5 self-etch adhesive).
- Table 3 survival (as tabulated): Klein 2021 SR+MA 96.8% at 10.4 years; Malchiodi 2019 98.7% (79 veneers, ~3 years); Imburgia 2021 99.83% (1075 veneers, 105 patients, 30.8 months); Nejatidanesh 2018 97.8% at 5 years (197 veneers, 71 patients); Sen 2024 89.1% (197 veneers, up to 10 years).
- Table 4 HF protocols: feldspathic 9.5% HF 2-2.5 min; leucite 60 s; lithium disilicate 20 s.
- Case outcome: patient satisfied with contour, shape and shade; satisfied at 2 years (subjective).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Authors: one database, focused search, not PRISMA-aligned; single case, satisfaction self-reported, 2-year follow-up; future work should compare adhesive brands and lengthen follow-up.
- Internal inconsistencies noted on reading: Sen 2024 survival is 89.1% in Table 3 but 92.7% (197 veneers in 32 patients, 10 years) in Section 4.2; Malchiodi 2019 is cited as [28] in Table 3 but [29] in Section 4.2; Section 4.7 describes 15 s sandblasting while the case description gives no sandblast time; the Section 4.7 tooth-side sequence omits phosphoric acid. Nejatidanesh 2018 and Sen 2024 both list 197 veneers.
- Table 2 reports only outcomes of individual case reports; no comparison of protocols is possible.
