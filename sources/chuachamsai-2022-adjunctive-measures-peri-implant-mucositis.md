AMSTAR 2 confidence가 critically low~low로 낮다는 메타적 한계도 명시해 향후 개별 SR 인용 시 주의할 지점을 제공.

## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42021254350) of 12 systematic reviews (2015-2020) covering 17 primary clinical trials (2014-2020) on adjunctive treatments (antiseptics, probiotics, air-polishing, laser/photodynamic therapy, antibiotics) added to non-surgical mechanical debridement (NSMD) for peri-implant mucositis (PIM).

Six SRs performing meta-analysis found no significant difference in probing depth, bleeding on probing, clinical attachment level, or plaque index between NSMD alone and NSMD + adjunctive treatment; vote-counting confirmed no additional benefit for antiseptics or probiotics specifically.

Overall AMSTAR 2 confidence of included SRs ranged from critically low to low, and about two-thirds of primary studies had high risk of bias — no adjunctive therapy has emerged as clearly superior to NSMD alone for managing PIM.

## 세줄요약

PROSPERO 등록(CRD42021254350) umbrella review — 임플란트주위 점막염(Peri-Implant Mucositis, PIM) 관리에서 비외과적 기계적 소파(Non-Surgical Mechanical Debridement, NSMD)에 보조요법(항균제, 프로바이오틱스, 에어폴리싱, 레이저/광역동요법, 항생제)을 추가한 효과를 다룬 SR 12편(2015-2020)·1차연구 17편(2014-2020) 종합.

메타분석을 수행한 SR 6편 모두 탐침깊이(Probing Depth, PD), 탐침출혈(Bleeding on Probing, BOP), 임상부착수준(Clinical Attachment Level, CAL), 치태지수(Plaque Index, PI)에서 NSMD 단독 대비 유의한 차이를 보이지 않았으며, vote-counting에서도 항균제·프로바이오틱스의 추가 이득이 확인되지 않았다.

포함된 SR들의 AMSTAR 2 종합신뢰도는 critically low~low였고 1차연구의 약 2/3가 high risk of bias였다 — 현재까지 NSMD 대비 명확히 우월한 보조요법은 없다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- First umbrella review to synthesize systematic reviews of adjunctive treatments for peri-implant mucositis (PIM) specifically (as opposed to peri-implantitis or mixed populations).
- Applies AMSTAR 2 to grade the methodological quality of 12 included SRs, finding all rated low or critically low.
- Uses a vote-counting method (≥3 primary studies required, >2/3 significant-positive threshold) to synthesize conclusions when meta-analysis was not feasible or not performed by the original SR.
- Maps citation overlap of the 17 primary clinical trials across the 12 SRs, revealing substantial double-counting of the same underlying evidence base.
- Identifies unstudied adjuncts (ozone therapy, desiccant application, electrolytic cleaning, herbal medications) for which no SR evidence currently exists.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- **Design**: Umbrella review (overview of systematic reviews) per a pre-registered PROSPERO protocol (CRD42021254350).
- **Search**: Seven databases (Cochrane Library, Embase via Ovid, MEDLINE via PubMed, ProQuest, PROSPERO, Scopus, Web of Science) searched to September 15, 2021, English language only, dual search strategy (filtered + unfiltered with "systematic review" term) to maximize sensitivity.
- **Selection**: Two independent reviewers (title/abstract kappa 0.97; full-text kappa 0.89), third reviewer for consensus. 701 records → 22 full-text screened → 12 SRs included, 10 excluded.
- **Eligibility**: SRs of adult PIM patients comparing NSMD ± adjunctive treatment; SRs exclusively on peri-implantitis excluded; each included SR required ≥1 eligible primary study.
- **Quality appraisal**: AMSTAR 2 (7 critical + 9 non-critical domains) applied independently by two reviewers to grade each SR's overall confidence (high/moderate/low/critically low).
- **Synthesis**: Where SRs did not draw definitive quantitative conclusions, a vote-counting method was applied (minimum 3 primary studies per adjunct; benefit concluded if >2/3 of primary studies showed a significant positive result). Data from primary studies were cross-checked against original publications; missing risk-of-bias judgments were derived via the Cochrane Handbook algorithm.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- 12 SRs included (published 2015-2020, all outside Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews), covering 17 primary clinical trials (16 RCTs + 1 CCT, 2014-2020).
- AMSTAR 2 overall confidence: 7 SRs "low," 5 SRs "critically low." Most common critical flaw: no interpretation of risk of bias in discussing results (9 SRs); second: no publication-bias assessment (5 SRs).
- Adjunctive modalities reviewed: antiseptics (5 RCTs, chlorhexidine 0.12-0.5%), probiotics (6 RCTs, lozenges ± sulcus application), air-polishing (1 RCT + 1 CCT, glycine powder), laser/photodynamic therapy (2 RCTs, diode laser), antibiotics (2 RCTs — systemic azithromycin, local tetracycline HCl fibers).
- 6 SRs performed meta-analysis: no significant difference vs. NSMD alone in probing depth, bleeding on probing, clinical attachment level, or plaque index.
- Vote-counting: no additional benefit confirmed for antiseptics (probing depth, BOP) or probiotics (probing depth, BOP, plaque index); other adjuncts had <3 primary studies and could not be vote-counted.
- Only 2 primary studies (antiseptic adjuncts) showed a significant short-term (≤3 month) advantage, without longer follow-up to confirm durability.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Included SRs and primary trials were few in number and low quality; ~2/3 of primary studies had high risk of bias.
- Substantial overlap of the same 17 primary studies across the 12 SRs likely inflates apparent consistency of the "no benefit" finding.
- Vote-counting does not weight by effect size or estimate precision — a methodological limitation acknowledged by the authors.
- No SR evidence exists yet for several adjuncts already in clinical use or reported in case-level literature (ozone therapy, desiccants, electrolytic cleaning, herbal agents).
- Authors call for standardized, adequately powered RCTs with unified case definitions (e.g., 2017 World Workshop classification, used by only 1 of 12 SRs) and longer follow-up, particularly in higher-risk subgroups (periodontitis history, diabetes, smoking, deep mucosal tunnel implants, over-contoured prosthetic emergence profiles).

## 7. Glossary

- **PIM (Peri-Implant Mucositis)**: Inflammation of peri-implant mucosa without continuing marginal bone loss after initial remodeling.
- **NSMD (Non-Surgical Mechanical Debridement)**: Professional mechanical plaque/biofilm removal without surgical access, the standard baseline treatment for PIM.
- **AMSTAR 2**: A Measurement Tool to Assess Systematic Reviews (2nd version) — critical-appraisal instrument for systematic reviews of healthcare interventions.
- **Umbrella review**: A systematic overview that synthesizes multiple systematic reviews (rather than primary studies) on a topic.
- **Vote-counting**: A non-statistical synthesis method that tallies the proportion of primary studies showing a significant effect, used here when meta-analysis was infeasible.
- **PROSPERO**: International prospective register of systematic review protocols.
