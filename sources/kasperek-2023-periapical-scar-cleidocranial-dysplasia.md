## Why Ingested

Demonstrates how a systemic condition (cleidocranial dysplasia) combined with extensive childhood surgical history creates the ideal substrate for periapical scar — which was subsequently misdiagnosed as endodontic failure. Provides prevalence figure (2.5–12%), risk factors, and long-term monitoring framework for periapical fibrous scar. Clinically actionable: when a patient has CCD or significant prior periapical surgery, persistent radiolucency ≠ failure by default.

## Three-line Summary

Case report (University of Liverpool, J Endod 2023): 18-year-old female with cleidocranial dysplasia (CCD) presented with persistent periapical radiolucency at tooth #9; asymptomatic apical periodontitis and endodontic failure were initially suspected.

Her CCD had caused multiple supernumerary teeth requiring extensive childhood periapical surgery, which left fibrous scar tissue — correctly diagnosed as periapical scar after clinical and radiographic re-evaluation, avoiding unnecessary retreatment.

Periapical fibrous scars carry a prevalence of 2.5–12%; this case illustrates that prior surgical history and systemic conditions affecting bone healing are key risk factors, and long-term clinical/radiographic monitoring is the appropriate management when scar is diagnosed.

## 세줄요약

증례보고 (리버풀대, J Endod 2023): 쇄골두개이형성증 (Cleidocranial Dysplasia, CCD)을 가진 18세 여성이 #9 치아 치근단의 지속적 방사선 투과상으로 내원; 초기에 무증상 치근단 치주염·근관치료 실패로 의심되었음.

CCD로 인한 다수 과잉치를 소아기에 수술적 제거한 결과 치근단 반흔 (Periapical Scar, PS)이 형성되었으며, 임상·방사선 재평가 후 PS로 진단되어 불필요한 재치료를 피함.

치근단 섬유성 반흔의 유병률은 2.5–12%; 이전 수술 이력·골 치유 장애 소인이 주요 위험인자이며, 진단 후 장기 추적관찰이 적절한 관리 전략임.
