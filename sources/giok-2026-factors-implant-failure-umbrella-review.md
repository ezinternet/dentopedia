## Three-line Summary

Umbrella review (PROSPERO CRD42025634487) of 25 meta-analyses (9 of RCTs, 26 of cohort/case-control observational studies, 35 total associations) examining factors associated with dental implant failure, searched Scopus/PubMed/Cochrane/Epistemonikos through June 2024.

Among RCT meta-analyses, turned-vs-anodized implants, submerged-vs-nonsubmerged healing, and bone augmentation with long-vs-short implants showed high-certainty evidence of higher failure risk; among observational meta-analyses, smoking showed highly suggestive evidence and 9 other factors (periodontal compromise, PPI therapy, Crohn's disease, bone quality II/III vs IV, immediate loading protocols, short implants, SSRI use, turned-vs-anodized) showed suggestive evidence — none reached "convincing" grade.

Clinical implication: smoking is the best-supported modifiable observational risk factor for implant failure, and anodized-surface implants are supported by both trial and observational evidence; most other associations remain at suggestive-or-lower credibility and the paper is abstract-only in this wiki (no access to full results tables/forest plots).

우산리뷰(Umbrella Review, PROSPERO CRD42025634487) — 메타분석 25편(RCT 메타분석 9편, 코호트·환자대조군 관찰연구 메타분석 26편, 총 35개 연관성)을 종합, Scopus/PubMed/Cochrane/Epistemonikos에서 2024년 6월까지 검색.

RCT 메타분석 중 turned vs anodized 임플란트, submerged vs nonsubmerged 치유, 장골증대+장임플란트 vs 단임플란트는 고신뢰도(high certainty)로 실패 위험 증가와 연관; 관찰연구 메타분석 중 흡연(Smoking)은 highly suggestive 등급, 나머지 9개 인자(치주염 이환·양성자펌프억제제(PPI)·크론병·II/III형 골질 vs IV형·즉시로딩·단임플란트·SSRI·turned vs anodized)는 suggestive 등급 — "convincing"(확증적) 등급은 없음.

임상적 의미: 흡연이 관찰연구 근거에서 가장 강력하게 뒷받침되는 교정 가능한 위험인자이며, 산화(anodized) 표면 임플란트는 시험·관찰 근거 모두에서 지지됨; 그 외 대부분의 연관성은 suggestive 이하 등급에 머물고, 본 위키 페이지는 초록만 확보한 상태(본문 결과표·서브그룹 데이터 미확보)라는 핵심 한계가 있다.

- **Journal**: The Journal of Prosthetic Dentistry 2026;135(3):518-527 (epub 2025-05-27)
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.prosdent.2025.04.041
- **PMID**: 40425441 (According to PubMed)
- **Institution**: School of Dentistry, IMU University, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (lead); co-authors at University of Michigan and Ajman University, UAE

## 2. Key Contributions
- First umbrella review to apply full credibility grading (heterogeneity, 95% prediction interval, small-study effects, excess significance, credibility ceilings) across implant-failure meta-analyses spanning both RCTs and observational studies in one framework.
- Identifies smoking as the single "highly suggestive" evidence-grade risk factor among observational associations — the strongest tier below "convincing."
- Cross-validates trial-level (GRADE) and observational-level (credibility-ceiling) evidence for anodized vs turned implant surfaces, finding concordant direction across both designs.
- Documents that none of the 35 examined associations reached "convincing" credibility, underscoring the overall evidentiary uncertainty in implant-failure risk-factor literature.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **Design**: Umbrella review of meta-analyses (RCTs + observational studies)
- **Registration**: PROSPERO CRD42025634487
- **Databases**: Scopus, PubMed, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, Epistemonikos (inception–June 2024)
- **n**: 2922 publications identified → 224 full-texts evaluated → 25 articles describing 35 associations included (9 RCT meta-analyses, 26 observational meta-analyses)
- **Statistical approach**: effect sizes recalculated with random-effects model per meta-analysis; evaluated between-study heterogeneity, 95% prediction interval, small-study effects, excess significance, credibility ceilings
- **Grading**: GRADE (RCT meta-analyses); nonsignificant/weak/suggestive/highly suggestive/convincing credibility tiers (observational meta-analyses)

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

RCT meta-analyses (n=9): 6/9 (67%) statistically significant associations (P<.05).
- High-certainty evidence (higher failure risk): turned vs anodized implants; submerged vs nonsubmerged implant healing; bone augmentation with long implants vs short implants.
- Moderate-certainty evidence (higher failure risk): short implants (<10 mm) vs long implants (>10 mm); immediate vs delayed implant placement.

Observational meta-analyses (n=26): 18/26 (69%) statistically significant associations (P<.05); none graded "convincing."
- Highly suggestive: smoking.
- Suggestive: periodontally compromised vs healthy patients; proton pump inhibitor (PPI) therapy; Crohn's disease; bone quality type II vs IV; bone quality type III vs IV; nonsubmerged immediately loaded vs submerged delayed loaded implants; short (<10 mm) vs long (>10 mm) implants; SSRI therapy; turned vs anodized implants; immediately loaded vs conventionally loaded implants.
- Findings remained robust after sensitivity analyses.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Abstract-only ingest in this wiki — full text not accessible (paywalled); no individual OR/CI values, forest plots, or complete 35-association table were retrievable from the source PDF (a print of the abstract landing page).
- No association reached "convincing" credibility per the authors' own grading — most conclusions remain provisional pending higher-quality primary studies.
- Umbrella-review methodology inherits the limitations (heterogeneity, publication bias, differing definitions of "failure") of the underlying meta-analyses it synthesizes.
- Authors note remaining factors require additional high-quality studies before firm clinical recommendations can be made.

## 6. Related Work

## 7. Glossary
- **Umbrella review**: A systematic review that synthesizes multiple existing systematic reviews/meta-analyses on a topic, rather than primary studies directly.
- **GRADE**: Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation — framework for rating certainty of evidence from RCTs (high/moderate/low/very low).
- **Credibility ceiling (observational evidence grading)**: A tiered classification (nonsignificant → weak → suggestive → highly suggestive → convincing) used to grade the credibility of meta-analyses of observational (non-randomized) studies, accounting for heterogeneity, small-study effects, and excess significance bias.
- **PROSPERO**: International Prospective Register of Systematic Reviews, a registry for pre-specifying SR/MA protocols.
- **Turned vs anodized implant surface**: Comparison between a machined (turned/smooth) titanium implant surface and an anodically oxidized (roughened) surface, both established implant surface-treatment technologies.
