## Three-line Summary
Narrative review (Korean Dental Association journal, 2024) synthesizing ARP techniques (socket sealing, grafting materials, flap/flapless), available graft materials, and clinical implications for subsequent implant placement.

Ideal ARP protocol involves granulation tissue removal, suitable graft material, and socket sealing; ARP minimizes invasiveness at subsequent implant surgery, but consensus on optimal graft type and implant-placement timing is still lacking.

ARP is an established approach to reduce post-extraction ridge resorption, but definitive clinical guidelines on material selection and placement timing await higher-quality evidence.

## 세줄요약
소켓 밀폐·이식재·플랩 여부를 포함한 ARP(치조제보존술) 기술과 재료, 이후 임플란트 식립에 대한 임상적 함의를 정리한 narrative review (대한치과의사협회지 2024).

이상적 ARP = 육아조직 제거 + 적절한 이식재 + 소켓 밀폐; ARP는 이후 임플란트 식립 시 침습성을 최소화하나, 최적 이식재 유형과 식립 시기에 대한 합의는 아직 부재.

치조제 흡수 감소를 위한 ARP는 확립된 접근법이나, 재료 선택·식립 시기에 관한 결정적 가이드라인은 고질 근거 축적을 기다리는 중.

## 2. Key Contributions
Compact ARP procedural and material overview suitable as a reference primer for clinicians considering ARP indications before implant placement.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Literature review. ARP techniques, available graft materials, and socket-sealing options synthesized. Implications for subsequent implant placement discussed.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Ideal ARP = granulation tissue removal → suitable graft → socket sealing. Demonstrated to minimize invasiveness at subsequent implant placement. Lacks consensus on optimal graft type and implant-placement timing.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
Narrative review (not systematic); no quantitative pooling; published guidelines still missing.
