## Why Ingested

2년간 지속된 임플란트 유발 하치조신경 이상감각이 임플란트 제거만으로 완전 회복된 극단적 지연 사례 — [[oral-surgery/nerve-injury/khawaja-2009-ian-implant-removal-nerve-injury-cases]]가 제시한 조기(≤96시간) 제거 원칙의 경계를, 단순 접촉·비침습성 손상이라는 조건 하에 크게 확장한다.

## Three-line Summary

This case report describes a 65-year-old male patient with a two-year history of right lower lip paresthesia and occasional drooling following implant placement in the right mandibular first molar region (tooth 46). Clinical and radiological examination with cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) confirmed the implant apex resting on the inferior alveolar nerve canal. Surgical retrieval of the well-osseointegrated implant resulted in complete neurosensory recovery within six weeks, after which the edentulous site was restored with a provisional three-unit fixed partial denture.

## 세줄요약

본 증례보고는 우측 하악 제1대구치(46번) 부위에 임플란트 식립 후 2년간 우측 하순 지속성 감각저하와 가끔씩 침 흘림을 호소한 65세 남성 환자를 기술한다. 콘빔 컴퓨터단층촬영(CBCT)을 포함한 임상 및 방사선 검사에서 임플란트 첨단이 하치조신경관에 접촉되어 있음이 확인되었다. 골유착이 잘 된 임플란트를 외과적으로 제거한 결과 6주 내에 신경감각이 완전히 회복되었으며, 이후 결손 부위는 임시 3유닛 고정성 국소의치로 수복되었다.

## 2. Key Contributions

- Reported a case of inferior alveolar nerve paresthesia persisting for two years after implant placement that completely resolved within six weeks of implant retrieval.
- Demonstrated that even delayed implant removal (two years post-placement) can lead to complete neurosensory recovery when the implant apex merely contacts the mandibular canal surface without intraneural impingement.
- Identified peri-implantitis with circumferential bone loss around the implant that facilitated uncomplicated surgical retrieval despite the extended duration since placement.
- Confirmed the diagnostic utility of cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) for accurately assessing implant relationship to the inferior alveolar nerve canal.
- Showed complete resolution of associated symptoms including paresthesia, burning sensation, intermittent pain, and salivary drooling following implant removal.
- Highlighted that surgical exploration provides the optimal visualization of inferior alveolar nerve injury and should be considered when imaging suggests nerve involvement.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

- Design: Case report (single patient)
- Patient: 65-year-old male with history of myocardial infarction (20 years prior), currently medicated
- Presentation: Two-year history of right lower lip and mental region numbness, occasional drooling from right mouth corner, intermittent dull throbbing pain, episodic burning sensation in right inner cheek
- Clinical examination: Prosthetic crown on implant at tooth 46; edematous gingiva with bleeding on probing (probing depth 5 mm); inflammation at tooth 45; complete loss of tactile and thermal sensation in right lower lip, mental region, and chin skin
- Radiological evaluation: Orthopantomogram (OPG) and cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) confirming implant apex resting on inferior alveolar nerve canal
- Intervention: Surgical implant retrieval under local anesthesia after obtaining patient and physician consent; flap elevation, implant extraction with forceps, granulation tissue debridement, osseous surgery, and flap approximation with sutures
- Postoperative: Edentulous site restored with three-unit fixed partial denture at week 4
- Neurosensory assessment: Objective (light touch, sharp/blunt discrimination, two-point discrimination, moving points) and subjective (10-point scale) evaluations performed preoperatively and at weeks 3, 4, and 6 post-surgery
- Follow-up: Patient monitored for three months to confirm complete recovery

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

- **Preoperative paresthesia —** Complete loss of tactile and thermal sensation in right lower lip, mental region, and chin skin; subjective score 9/10 for neuropathic region
- **Week 3 post-retrieval —** Area affected by neurosensory disturbance reduced to approximately 50% of preoperative neuropathy area
- **Week 4 post-retrieval —** Significant improvement with 30% of affected area still showing persistent neurosensory deficit; sensitivity to cold and mechanical stimuli present
- **Week 6 post-retrieval —** Complete resolution of neurosensory deficit (100% recovery) (NR for p, CI, I²)
- **Burning sensation —** Visual Analogue Scale score 6/10 preoperatively; reduced to 0/10 approximately two weeks post-surgery (NR for p, CI)
- **Intermittent pain —** Completely resolved within two days post-surgery (NR for p, CI)
- **Salivary drooling —** Gradually reduced and resolved completely by week 6 (NR for p, CI)
- **Probing depth —** 5 mm at tooth 46 preoperatively, suggesting peri-implantitis with circumferential bone loss

## 5. Limitations and Future Work

- Single case report with limited generalizability; findings may not apply to all patients with implant-related nerve injuries.
- Lack of quantitative neurosensory testing (e.g., Semmes-Weinstein monofilaments, current perception threshold) for objective assessment of recovery.
- No intraoperative nerve visualization or direct nerve evaluation was performed beyond implant removal; the exact nature of nerve-implant interaction remains speculative.
- Two-year delay between initial injury and treatment may confound the interpretation of recovery timeline.
- Patient's history of cardiovascular disease and medications may have influenced healing and recovery, but these factors were not systematically evaluated.
- Absence of pre-implant placement neurosensory baseline assessment makes it difficult to confirm the absence of pre-existing sensory deficits.
- No systematic comparison with conservative management or alternative treatment approaches provided.

## 7. Glossary

- **IAN (Inferior Alveolar Nerve)**: 하치조신경. 하악골 내 하치조관을 주행하며 하악 치아, 하순, 턱 부위의 감각을 담당하는 삼차신경의 하악분지.
- **CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography)**: 콘빔 컴퓨터단층촬영. 치과 및 구강악안면 영역에서 3차원적 해부학적 평가를 위해 사용되는 방사선 검사법.
- **Paresthesia**: 이상감각. 자극 없이 발생하는 비정상적 감각(저림, 화끈거림, 따끔거림 등)으로, 본 증례에서는 하순 및 정신부위의 무감각을 의미.
- **OPG (Orthopantomogram)**: 파노라마 방사선사진. 상하악 전체의 치아와 악골을 한 장의 영상으로 보여주는 검사.
- **Peri-implantitis**: 임플란트 주위염. 임플란트 주변 조직의 염증성 질환으로, 골소실과 치주낭 형성을 동반.
- **Osseointegration**: 골유착. 임플란트 표면과 주변 골조직 사이의 직접적 구조적·기능적 연결.
- **Neuropraxia**: 신경실조증. Seddon 분류 중 가장 경미한 신경손상 형태로, 축삭의 연속성은 유지되나 일시적 신경차단이 발생하며 완전 회복이 기대됨.
