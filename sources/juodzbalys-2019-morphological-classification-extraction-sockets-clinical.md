## Why Ingested

This systematic review synthesizes available extraction socket classification systems (6 systems, 2004–2016) and proposes a novel clinical decision tree for socket preservation/augmentation in aesthetic vs. non-aesthetic zones — directly supporting case planning for ridge preservation indications in the clinic. Complements existing socket healing and preservation pages such as → [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/fok-2024-alveolar-socket-healing-evolving-knowledge]] and → [[bone-regeneration/ridge-preservation/fan-2021-ridge-preservation-novel-extraction-socket]].

## Three-line Summary

Systematic review of 10 human studies (MEDLINE/EMBASE, Jan 2009–May 2019) reviewing extraction socket classification systems and indications for socket preservation/augmentation.
Six classification systems were identified (Caplanis 2005, Elian 2007, Juodzbalys 2008, Smith & Tarnow 2013, Chu 2015, El Chaar 2016); none fully captures all morphological parameters of the alveolar ridge.
A novel decision tree for socket preservation/augmentation differentiating aesthetic from non-aesthetic zones was proposed, extending indications beyond socket morphology to include surrounding tissue anatomy and implant timing.

## 세줄요약

MEDLINE/EMBASE 검색(2009–2019) 10편 체계적 고찰로 발치와 분류체계(Extraction Socket Classification)와 소켓 보존술(Socket Preservation) 적응증을 정리.
6가지 분류체계(Caplanis·Elian·Juodzbalys·Smith&Tarnow·Chu·El Chaar) 중 치조제(Alveolar Ridge)의 모든 형태 변수를 포괄하는 단일 체계는 없음.
심미 영역·비심미 영역을 구분한 신규 임상 의사결정 트리(Clinical Decision Tree)를 제안 — 소켓 형태뿐 아니라 주변 조직 해부학 및 임플란트 식립 시기(Implant Placement Timing)를 포함.

## Key Contributions

- First systematic review to compare all published extraction socket classification systems head-to-head against PRISMA criteria
- Demonstrates that no single existing classification captures the full morphological picture (hard tissue + soft tissue + radiologic parameters together)
- Proposes a practical 2-branch decision tree: aesthetic zone vs. non-aesthetic zone, each with preservation/augmentation vs. observation paths
- Indications for socket preservation extend beyond socket morphology to include: gingival biotype, keratinized tissue width, anticipated implant placement timing, and patient risk profile
- Highlights that post-extraction bone loss begins within 90 days; horizontal 29–63%, vertical 11–22% at 6 months

## Methodology

- **Design**: Systematic review (PRISMA)
- **Databases**: MEDLINE (PubMed) + EMBASE
- **Search period**: January 2009 – May 2019
- **Initial hits**: 553; included: 10 studies
- **Inclusion**: Human prospective/follow-up/cohort/RCT/SR studies in English evaluating extraction socket morphology or classification; classified with indications for preservation/augmentation
- **Exclusion**: Animal studies, case reports, letters, editorials, in vitro
- **PROSPERO registration**: CRD42019136314
- **Risk of bias**: Cochrane collaboration tool, 7 domains; most studies moderate/unclear risk

## Key Results

### Extraction Socket Classification Systems Identified

| Study | Year | Hard Tissue Parameters | Soft Tissue Parameters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caplanis et al. | 2005 | Socket wall status, bone loss, DEJ distance | Periodontal biotype |
| Elian et al. | 2007 | Buccal bone level | Facial soft tissue level |
| Juodzbalys et al. | 2008 | 8 hard tissue parameters incl. alveolar height, buccal thickness, labial bone position | 6 soft tissue parameters incl. biotype, papillae, KG width |
| Smith & Tarnow | 2013 | Septal bone coverage of immediately placed implant | N/R |
| Chu et al. | 2015 | Buccal bone plate level + defect | Buccal soft tissue deficiency |
| El Chaar et al. | 2016 | Buccal plate loss, periapical topography, interproximal bone | Soft tissue biotype |

### Novel Decision Tree (Key Branches)

- **Aesthetic zone**: assess gingival biotype + buccal bone plate integrity → socket augmentation if thin biotype or buccal plate loss; connective tissue graft if STD (soft tissue deficiency)
- **Non-aesthetic zone**: assess residual bone volume → preservation if adequate bone; augmentation if compromised socket walls
- Indications broader than morphology: patient systemic risk, parafunctional habits, anticipated implant timing all modulate decision

### Post-Extraction Bone Changes Referenced

- Horizontal bone loss: 29–63% within 6 months
- Vertical bone loss: 11–22% within 6 months
- ~50% width loss within first year
- Majority of height loss within first 90 days

## Limitations

- 10 included studies represents a small pool; heterogeneity precluded meta-analysis
- Most included studies carry moderate/unclear risk of bias (Cochrane tool)
- Classification systems reviewed predate several contemporary socket-shield and vascularized soft-tissue protocols (post-2019 evidence not captured)
- Decision tree is consensus/expert-derived, not prospectively validated in an RCT

## Glossary

- **Extraction Socket Classification**: systematic scoring of post-extraction alveolar hard and soft tissue morphology to guide treatment decisions
- **Socket Preservation (Ridge Preservation)**: procedures placed at extraction to limit dimensional changes of the alveolar ridge
- **Socket Augmentation**: restoration of damaged socket walls before/concurrent with ridge maintenance
- **Aesthetic Zone**: anterior maxilla and premolar region where gingival contour and tissue volume are visible and critical to prosthetic outcomes
- **Gingival Biotype**: thickness and morphology of the periodontal soft tissues (thin vs. thick) influencing healing after extraction
- **Decision Tree (Clinical)**: branching algorithm using clinical/radiographic parameters to route patients to preservation, augmentation, or observation protocols
